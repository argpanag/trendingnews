About Us

What is trends-online.com?

What We Do

trends-online.com automatically aggregates trending news from Google Trends across 47+ countries. We collect headlines and articles that are currently trending and present them in a clean, easy-to-read format.

How It Works

Our system runs every hour to:

  • Fetch the latest trending topics from Google Trends
  • Collect news articles related to those topics
  • Organize them by country and date
  • Present them on this website for easy browsing

Countries Covered

We cover 47+ countries including the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Japan, Australia, and many more. Each country has its own dedicated page showing trending news from that region.

Purpose

Our goal is to provide a simple, fast-loading website where you can quickly see what's trending around the world. All content is sourced from publicly available news feeds and Google Trends data.
