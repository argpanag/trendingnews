Investigation after man found dead on Cork golf course

TheTools · 23.10.2026 10:54 · source · permalink

An investigation is under way into the death of a man whose body was found in Macroom, Co Cork, this morning.

The man has been named locally as Jack O'Donovan, aged in his 20s, of Dan Corkery Place in Macroom.

Mr O'Donovan's body was found on the course at Macroom Golf Club, just outside the town, at 9.30am.

The scene was preserved for a technical examination.

A post-mortem examination is due to be carried out by Assistant State Pathologist Dr Margaret Bolster.

Gardaí have said that the examination will determine the course of the investigation but that it has been allocated the resources of a murder investigation.

A senior investigating officer has been appointed and an incident room has been established at Macroom Garda Station.

A family liaison officer has been appointed to provide support to the family.

Gardaí are interested in speaking with anyone who may have been in the area of the golf club at Castle Grounds in Macroom between 2pm on Thursday and 10am Friday morning.

Any road users or pedestrians who were in the area, and who may have camera footage (including mobile phone and dash-cam), are asked to make it available to gardaí.

Anyone with information is asked to contact Macroom Garda Station on (026) 20590, the Garda Confidential Line on 1800 666 111, or any garda station.
