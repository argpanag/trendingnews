Teenager dies in ‘tragic accident’ involving vehicle at Tuam property

TheTools · 08.10.2026 21:13 · source · permalink

A teenager has died following a “tragic accident” involving a vehicle at a private property in Tuam, Co Galway, on Wednesday.

A Garda spokesperson said the boy, aged in his late teens, was pronounced dead at the scene.

His body has been removed to University Hospital Galway and a postmortem will take place.

Gardaí are treating the death as a “tragic accident”.

“No further information is being made available,” the spokesperson said.
