‘I’m just a grandma’: Woman alleges security left her injured in Spark Arena gig removal

TheTools · 30.09.2026 06:29 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

A spokesperson from Spark Arena said they were aware of the incident. Photo / Anna Heath

Police are making inquiries after a grandmother said she suffered a sprained wrist and extensive bruising during a scuffle with security at Spark Arena.

The Thames 50-year-old was enjoying a metal gig on the weekend when she said she was dragged off her feet, thrown to the ground and had her wrist twisted by two Red Badge security guards.

She said she suffered extensive bruising on her leg, breast, wrist and both arms and was so distressed by the incident she bit one of the guards.

“I’m 50 years old and weigh 70kg, I’m just a grandma,” she told the Herald, tearing up.

“The plan was to have a night out with girlfriends to have a few drinks, have a dance and then go home to my daughter and grandkid. So it wasn’t a big night or anything.”

Police said the woman had been ejected from a venue because of “behaviour and intoxication”.

They said after she attempted to regain entry, she allegedly bit a security guard while being removed.

Red Badge events and venues operations manager David Leupolu said Spark Arena had initiated an investigation and referred the complaint to police.

“As this matter is currently under Police investigation, we are unable to provide any further information. Please direct any inquiries regarding the matter to Police.”

The woman, who did not want to be named, told the Herald she travelled to Auckland to spend time with her daughter and 2-week-old grandchild, and to attend the Rock 2000 Live event.

On Friday evening she caught the train from South Auckland into the CBD to meet her friends. The group headed to the arena about 5.30pm.

“I went to the bathroom a couple of hours in and was accidentally separated from friends.

“So I went near the front of the mosh and just carried on dancing.”

About 8.30pm the woman took out a 40ml bottle of Malibu liquor she had managed to sneak inside the venue, which was then confiscated by a Red Badge security guard.

“I own up to this. Fair enough, if you get caught you’ve got to own up to it and so it’s fine he took it, those are the rules.”

Less than a minute later she said two security guards, a man and woman, picked her up and “dragged her off her feet” as she screamed “stop, stop that’s really hurting me”.

“They started gripping my arms really tight, it really hurt.

“I was yelling at them to ‘not touch me’ and to ‘get off me’.

“I don’t know how it happened, whether they pushed me or I tripped, but I was on the ground outside and they were standing over me yelling at me to leave the premises.”

She alleged the female security guard then picked her up and twisted her wrist, leaving her with pain she believed was caused by a sprain.

She now planned to get an X-ray done to check whether she had any broken bones.

“I was in so much pain and she wasn’t stopping so I bit her finger.

“I’ve never done anything like that before, but it was just a human reaction,” she sobbed.

Security called police, who arrested the woman and later released her to travel home.

She took a train back to her daughter’s home in Papakura.

A police spokesperson said they were making further inquiries into “the actions of a female” as well as the security provider.

“The female was arrested and upon Police inquiries she was subsequently released to travel home.

“Police are making further inquiries into the actions of the female, as well as those of the security provider responsible for ensuring the venue was a safe environment.”

A spokesperson from Spark Arena said they were aware of the incident.

“The safety and wellbeing of everyone at Spark Arena, including our patrons and the people who work at our events, is our priority, and we take any concerns raised about incidents at our venue seriously.”

The venue said it would be inappropriate to comment further but would continue to work with police.
