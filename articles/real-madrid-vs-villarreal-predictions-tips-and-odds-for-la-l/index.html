Real Madrid vs Villarreal predictions: Tips and odds for La Liga match

TheTools · 10.10.2026 18:19 · source · permalink

Liam Flin offers his three best tips for tonight’s La Liga match between Real Madrid and Villarreal, including a 15/4 player bet

Having had an extended international break to reflect on last month’s frustrating La Liga defeat by rivals Atletico, Real Madrid will be hoping they can return to winning ways when they host Villarreal at the Bernabeu tonight (kick-off, 8pm BST).

With only seven games played in La Liga, Real are already six points off runaway leaders Barcelona but they are 3/10 favourites to win against Villarreal, who are 7/1 outsiders with the draw 21/4.

All three of my selections for this match were odds-against at time of tipping and I have even identified a value pick in the ‘last goalscorer’ market at 15/4.

Victory but no clean sheet for Real 

There is naturally cause for concern for Real Madrid when comparing their start to the season with that of Barcelona but it has not been a bad return for Jose Mourinho by any means.

Real have won five of their opening seven league games, losing away to Real Betis and Atletico, but they have won all three of their home fixtures, scoring 12 goals in the process.

They also won their first game in the Champions League, also at the Bernabeu, capitalising on two errors to beat Inter.

Going forward, there have been few issues and they will be relieved that star man Kylian Mbappe is available, having overcome a knee injury he sustained during the international break.

At the back, though, it has been a different story. Real have kept just one clean sheet in eight competitive matches under Mourinho this term and they head into this game weakened in defence by the absence through suspension of Dean Huijsen.

Both teams to score is priced at 8/15 by itself but combined with a home victory it looks like a smart bet at 6/5.

Real have won but conceded on five occasions in all competitions this season and visitors Villarreal are not the kind of team who sit back under former Rayo Vallecano boss Inigo Perez.

It was a slow start to the season for Villarreal but they won back-to-back fixtures against Malaga and Levante – both 3-1 – before the international break and have drawn a blank just once in their seven top-flight games this term, so they can at least bag a consolation goal in defeat.

Espi warming to the role of finisher

You are unlikely ever to find much value in backing Kylian Mbappe to score in La Liga and this game is no different – the Frenchman is 2/5 to score or 1/5 to score or assist.

But there could be some value in backing the player who could replace him during the game, Carlos Espi.

Mourinho will surely want to manage Mbappe’s minutes to ensure the knee problem he picked up is not aggravated and Espi is the prime candidate to step in for Mbappe later in the game.

Espi, who is only 21 and joined from Levante in the summer, has come off the bench to make all four of his La Liga appearances this season.

But in just 25 minutes of league football, he has already bagged two goals for Real, scoring late winning goals in the club’s season opener against Espanyol and in last month’s 3-2 win away to Elche.

He is just 8/11 to get on the scoresheet but given he is unlikely to start and could again be introduced late on, those impressed by the youngster should instead look at him to score the last goal at juicier odds of 15/4.

Make Moleiro your main shot selection

Another way for punters to capitalise on Real Madrid’s defensive permeability going into this game is to get behind Villarreal players in the shots markets.

Madrid have allowed an average of 14 shots per game in their home fixtures in La Liga, which is the sixth-highest average in the division, while only Real and Barca have averaged more than Villarreal’s 6.6 shots on target per match.

Villarreal will be especially keen to get shots away after restoring confidence with successive La Liga wins and the player who stands out in the betting is winger Alberto Moleiro at 6/5 to have a shot on target.

Moleiro was a livewire in victory over Levante before the break, directly contributing to all three of his side’s goals with one strike and two assists.

He also scored against Malaga and could find space to exploit down the left flank given the two right-backs he could face – either Denzel Dumfries or Trent Alexander-Arnold – are encouraged to get forward.

Moleiro has managed 10 shots in La Liga this season, six of them on target, so the 6/5 on offer is worth taking, especially as the same wager is odds-on elsewhere at time of writing.
