Alexandra Eala ‘super happy’ to be back, aims to finish on high at Hong Kong Open

TheTools · 03.11.2026 14:55 · source · permalink

Filipino star tells passionate crowd after first-round win there are ‘few places in the world that would be better’ for her to end season

Philippines sensation Alexandra Eala said there were few better places to wrap up a tennis season after battling past Emerson Jones at the Prudential Hong Kong Tennis Open on Tuesday.

Playing in Hong Kong for the second time, after her debut last year, Eala also said she was “super happy” to come back and start her quest to end the season on a high with a win.

The Filipino was set to renew her rivalry with American Alycia Parks in the first round, but the world No 59 from Florida withdrew about an hour before the evening match, with the 18-year-old Jones, who lost in the qualifying draw, replacing her as a lucky loser.
