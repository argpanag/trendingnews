West Ham boss Nuno hints at changes for Fulham Carabao Cup clash

TheTools · 15.09.2026 10:18 · source · permalink

West Ham United manager Nuno Espirito Santo has suggested he will make full use of his squad for Tuesday's Carabao Cup match against Fulham. The Hammers have won their last four league matches to climb to the top of the Championship table but will now get a chance to pit their wits against a team from the division above.

Nuno hasn't been afraid to mix things up in the league. He made five changes on Friday as his team hosted Wrexham and was rewarded with a decisive 6-0 win.

"As many games as we have, it’s always positive for us to keep playing," Nuno said ahead of the Fulham meeting. "As long as we use the squad, I think it’s a good chance for the players who have been working very hard every day.

"We can’t always play them, but now the cup is a good chance for the boys to go and enjoy it, and grow and improve. Throughout the season, we’re going to need all the players, so we want everyone to grow and improve as soon as possible."

West Ham beat Portsmouth in the opening round of this year's Carabao Cup before overcoming Southampton at St Mary's in round two. On both occasions, his team came from behind to secure handsome victories over their south coast opponents.

Last season, the two matches between West Ham and Fulham in the Premier League were decided by a single goal. Raul Jimenez broke Hammers hearts late on at the London Stadium before Crysencio Summerville grabbed the winner at Craven Cottage.

That win in West London came just days before Nuno's side won another derby against Brentford to reach the quarter-finals of the FA Cup. Leeds ended their run after a penalty shoot-out but the Portuguese manager recognised the value of a cup run when it comes to building momentum for the league.

"It was disappointing with how it ended (last year’s FA Cup run), but I truly believe that the cups are so important," he added. "They’re fantastic competitions for us, and they can have a really positive impact on everything, so we’ll be pushing for another big result on Tuesday."

Fulham could also make changes of their own on Tuesday. Backup goalkeeper Benjamin Lecomte played in the previous round and has spoken about the opportunities the competition can provide.

"For me, it's always a good opportunity to have the Carabao Cup, and also to have more minutes to play," said the Frenchman. "So I hope, if I play, it will be a good game for me and also for the team."
