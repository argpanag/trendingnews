Fulham v Man United LIVE: Michael Carrick under pressure ahead of crucial Premier League clash

TheTools · 20.09.2026 14:43 · source · permalink

Fulham host Manchester United in the Premier League with Michael Carrick under pressure for the first time since taking charge.

A dismal week for United went from bad to worse as they crashed out of the Carabao Cup at home to Brighton, just days their controversial Manchester derby defeat at Old Trafford.

With a three-week international break around the corner, Carrick could do with a win at Craven Cottage to lift the mood, but another defeat could also increase the scrutiny around his position.

Fulham earned their first point of the season under Alvaro Arbeloa at Anfield last weekend and then knocked West Ham out of the Carabao Cup in midweek. But the Cottagers come into this afternoon’s fixture bottom of the table and with Arbeloa also desperate for a win to change the narrative ahead of the international window.
