Former Oilers Defenseman Hilariously Announces His Retirement

TheTools · 26.11.2026 16:23 · source · permalink

A casual podcast appearance turned into an impromptu celebration as veteran blueliner Jeff Petry caught his own wife off guard when he announced the end of his 1,048-game career.

Former Edmonton Oilers defenseman Jeff Petry has announced his retirement from the NHL and he did it in the most hilarious way possible. 

During the "Never Offside With Julie & Cat" podcast, Tyler Toffoli's wife asked how he wanted to be introduced, he responded, "Retired..." Petry's wife started laughing and said, "I guess that's how we're doing it." He then added, "Coach of an under-10 team."

Everyone raised a glass and toasted his NHL career. 

Petry spent parts of five seasons with the Oilers, from 2010 - 2015. He was traded and then spent eight seasons in Montreal, recording 70 goals and 248 points in 508 games while playing a key role in the Canadiens' memorable run to the Stanley Cup Final in 2021.

He moved onto Pittsburgh, Detroit, Florida, and Minnesota before calling it a career.

He finishes his NHL career with 96 goals and 394 points in 1,048 regular-season games.
