Transfer: Bayer Leverkusen chief addresses Boniface's transfer speculation

TheTools · 25.10.2026 09:52 · source · permalink

Bayer Leverkusen managing director, Simon Rolfes has reacted to speculation linking Victor Boniface with a move away from the club.

Boniface has already expressed his desire to leave the former Bundesliga champions when the transfer window reopens in January, according to Sky Sports.

The Nigeria international has struggled for regular time at the club this season.

Rolfes, however, stated that the striker will get his opportunity at the right time.

"That doesn't change Boni's situation. He needs to stay fit, keep at it and improve, then his opportunity will arise. I'm not worried," Rolfes said.

Boniface spent last season on loan with another Bundesliga club, Werder Bremen.
