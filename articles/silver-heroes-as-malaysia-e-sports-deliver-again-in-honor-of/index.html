Silver heroes as Malaysia e-sports deliver again in Honor of Kings

TheTools · 28.09.2026 15:39 · source · permalink
    bookmark

PETALING JAYA: Malaysia's e-sports team added another silver medal to their haul at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games in the Honor of Kings event  at the Aichi Sky Expo on Monday (Sept 29). 

The squad of Nicholas Ng, Chen Tat Yann, Lim Chee Hong, Chong Han Hui, Ryan Ng, and Lee Han Siang fell 0-4 to China in the fina. 

They had earlier entered the final after beating Hong Kong 2-0 in the semi-finals. 

It was Malaysia’s second silver medal from e-sports after they also finished second in the e-football event. 

The e-sports camp have now delivered Malaysia three medals in Japan, including a gold medal from simulation racer Taj Izrin Aiman Taj Madira in the Gran Turismo 7 event.
