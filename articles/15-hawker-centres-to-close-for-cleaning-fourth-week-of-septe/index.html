15 hawker centres to close for cleaning fourth week of September

TheTools · 20.09.2026 23:00 · source · permalink

A total of 15 hawker centres across Singapore will close for one to three days from Sept 21 to Sept 27 for cleaning.

According to the National Environmental Agency, such cleaning is conducted regularly so that Singaporeans can enjoy affordable food in a clean and hygienic environment.

The hawker centres undergoing cleaning include Tekka Centre, Holland Village Market and Food Centre, and Jurong West Hawker Centre, among others.

In addition, two hawker centres — Kim Keat Palm Market and Food Centre at Toa Payoh Lorong 7 and Blk 726 Clementi West Street 2 — are currently undergoing repairs and redecoration works.

The works are slated to be completed on Dec 13 and Dec 27 respectively.

There are a total of 123 markets and hawker centres across Singapore.
