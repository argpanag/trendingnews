Ysabella Thomas, centre, who represented Port-of-Spain, poses with runner-up Anjali Maharaj, left, and second runner-up Dr Anna-Lee Cadogan after being crowned Miss World Trinidad and Tobago at Queen’s Hall, St Ann’s, on Sunday.

MISSWORLD.COM

Law grad­u­ate Ys­abel­la Thomas, rep­re­sent­ing Port-of-Spain, has been crowned Miss World Trinidad and To­ba­go 2026.

Thomas won the ti­tle dur­ing the na­tion­al com­pe­ti­tion at Queen’s Hall, St Ann’s, on Sun­day.

She al­so won the Miss Pho­to­genic, Peo­ple’s Choice and Mul­ti­me­dia Fast Track awards.

An­jali Ma­haraj, rep­re­sent­ing San Juan, was named first run­ner-up, while Dr Ann-Lee Cado­gan placed sec­ond run­ner-up.

Sheyan Wade, of Phillip­ine, and Nevaeh Maxwell, of San Fer­nan­do ,com­plet­ed the top five.

Thomas holds a Bach­e­lor of Laws and a Mas­ter of Laws in In­tel­lec­tu­al Prop­er­ty. She has com­plet­ed her Le­gal Ed­u­ca­tion Cer­tifi­cate and is await­ing her call to the Bar lat­er this year.

She sees law as a way to ad­vo­cate for oth­ers and serve her com­mu­ni­ty.

That in­ter­est in ad­vo­ca­cy ex­tends to her work through Beau­ty With a Pur­pose.

Thomas sup­ports a non-prof­it or­gan­i­sa­tion work­ing to ad­dress pe­ri­od pover­ty and men­stru­al in­equal­i­ty in Trinidad and To­ba­go and across the re­gion.

Ed­u­ca­tion and en­vi­ron­men­tal ad­vo­ca­cy are al­so among the caus­es she cham­pi­ons. Thomas has found­ed Trav­el Trin­ba­go, a lifestyle brand that show­cas­es Trinidad and To­ba­go’s cul­ture through lo­cal­ly in­spired de­signs.

She will rep­re­sent Trinidad and To­ba­go at Miss World 2027 in Tan­za­nia.
