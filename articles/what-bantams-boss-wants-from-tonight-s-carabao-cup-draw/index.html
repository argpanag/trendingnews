What Bantams boss wants from tonight's Carabao Cup draw

TheTools · 16.09.2026 19:58 · source · permalink

GRAHAM Alexander is hoping for another Carabao Cup cracker at Mbanq Valley Parade.

The fourth-round draw takes place around 10.15 tonight after the Manchester United and Brighton televised tie.

It is the first time the Bantams have reached the last 16 of the competition since that unforgettable run all the way to the final in 2013.

Alexander has no particular opponent in mind – but would love another home clash under the lights like the Burnley game.

“I really enjoyed the night of Burnley,” said the City boss. “I loved the games we had last year, Blackburn and Stoke away, brilliant victories in big stadiums.

“Even the Newcastle one when it was obviously a sore result but the actual build-up to the game, a packed St James’ Park. It was an amazing experience for the players to be out there.

“But I thought Burnley at a packed house at Valley Parade was really good. I’d love to get a big one at home.

“But there are a lot of big clubs in there and all the ones from our level still in will all want the same thing.”

A crowd of 19,686, attracted by the club’s decision to slash ticket prices, saw City edge the Championship club on penalties in the second round.

Stephen Humphrys heads home as City beat Leyton Orient (Image: Thomas Gadd)

Their reward was a trip to Leyton Orient where a much-changed side beat their League One rivals in front of just over 3,000 fans in east London.

Alexander added: “I’ve got an open mind about the draw, just like I had after the Burnley game when we got Leyton Orient.

“I know some people saw it as a bit of a dampener but, for me, it was just a game we wanted to win.

“We went down there and had the right attitude and it was a great result for us. It gives us another opportunity in the cup.

“I’m just looking forward to being in the draw. It’s exciting.

“It’s hard to get to this stage when you’ve played three other rounds.

“Everyone wants a good cup run for football and financial reasons at this level. The further you go, the more (chance of facing) big clubs.

“I’m sure there are players with certain aspirations of playing against the team they support.

“I just love Valley Parade when it’s rammed and the atmosphere’s great. But we’ll see what we get.”
