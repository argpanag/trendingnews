Oasis to headline Gillette Stadium on 2027 tour - The Boston Globe

TheTools · 15.09.2026 22:06 · source · permalink

After skipping Massachusetts on last year’s reunion tour, Oasis will perform at Gillette Stadium on their just-announced “Live ‘27″ tour.

Taking to social media yesterday, brothers and band members Liam and Noel Gallagher announced the 2027 shows, which will include three dates in Foxborough - Aug. 10, 13, and 14- as well as three appearances in Las Vegas and multiple dates in mainland Europe, Ireland, and the United Kingdom.

The “Live ‘27″ shows will mark the first time the band has played in the Boston area in 22 years, the last time being in 2005 at the Xfinity Center (then named the Tweeter Center) in Mansfield.

Oasis, who are known for Britpop hits like “Wonderwall,” “Don’t Look Back in Anger,” and “Champagne Supernova,” reunited last year after a 16-year hiatus. The “Live ‘25” tour spanned 41 sold-out shows and included multiple dates in Chicago, East Rutherford, N.J., and Pasadena, Calif.

There will be no general sale on Ticketmaster, according to a post from the band’s social media that explained a new effort to “reduce queuing and give genuine fans the best possible chance of getting tickets.” Fans will instead partake in a registration process that will qualify them to receive a unique access code that will give them the chance to purchase tickets. Applicants will be selected at random, the post stated.

Just days ago, the pair dropped some hints on social media that a visit to Gillette was in the works. A video from last week, posted without a caption, displayed a man getting a shave at a barbershop with a Gillette poster on the wall in the background; a clock in the shop also reads “March 11,″ a nod to the date of the band’s first Boston show, which took place in 1995.

Other tours slated for Gillette’s 2027 season are Luke Combs’s “My Kinda Saturday Night” tour, which comes to Foxborough May 1, and Kenny Chesney’s “The Original Vibe Room” tour, which is scheduled for Aug. 28.
