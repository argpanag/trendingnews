Team 18 clarifies position on out-of-contract Reynolds

TheTools · 07.10.2026 23:21 · source · permalink

Reynolds told reporters on Wednesday at Mount Panorama that he didn’t know whether he would race for Team 18 beyond 2026.

On Thursday, Burgess revealed Team 18 had recently met with Reynolds and his manager John Ruggiero to discuss their position.

“David knows fully well what’s going on,” said Burgess.

“We’ve sat and had a very good chat with him and his management before this event.

“We both understand where we’re both at.”

It’s understood Team 18 expressed a desire to see more focus and commitment from the veteran father of two amid a quest for improved results.

Team 18 has Supercheap Auto-backed wildcard Bayley Hall in its stable as well as Porsche Carrera Cup champion Harri Jones.

“It’s our job to be looking at the driver market,” said Burgess.

“We’re no different to an AFL team, NRL team or whatever.

“We’ve got to put our best foot forward. We’ve got to make sure we’ve got the two best drivers we can put in our car at any one time. That’s our job – and David understands that.

“He’s closer to the end of his career than he is to the start of his career.

“There’s always gonna be that point where [the question is] is it the right time? Is it not the right time? But that’s a matter for us and David to sort out.

“We love Dave, we’ve got a great relationship with him, and I’m sure whatever happens, we’ll be doing something with him in the future, but who knows what that is at the moment, and we don’t need to decide today or this week.

“We need to focus on this event, and those things will naturally take care of themselves.”

Reynolds has been soundly beaten by his teammate Anton De Pasquale, who sits sixth in the drivers’ championship.

Asked whether the results of the final few events would have any bearing on Reynolds’ future, Burgess downplayed suggestions the veteran needed to prove himself.

“We don’t employ a driver – apart from the co-drivers – for one weekend or two weekends,” said Burgess.

“It’s a long season and you want the results to add up in Adelaide. That’s where we’re going for the championship.

“Yes, here we’re going for the Peter Brock Trophy is very special, but it’s the championship we want to win as well, and that takes all year long to achieve that, so.

“No, it won’t matter what he does this weekend, we hope he’ll have a strong weekend like he always does here, but that won’t sway one way or another what decision we finally take.”
