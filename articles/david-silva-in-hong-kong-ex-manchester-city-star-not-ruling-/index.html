David Silva in Hong Kong: ex-Manchester City star not ruling out Sha Tin return

TheTools · 06.11.2026 10:01 · source · permalink

Spanish World Cup winner poised to line-up for Supreme against BC Rangers at sold-out Kai Tak Youth Sports Ground on Saturday

David Silva said he would “not close the door” on the possibility of repeating his headline-grabbing guest appearance for Supreme Sha Tin this weekend, as the club’s ambitious owner acknowledged he would gladly keep doling out bumper pay cheques to keep the World Cup winner on board.

Still, Jim Wong Chi-chuen, who has granted Silva a “seven-figure” Hong Kong dollar payday to play against BC Rangers at a sold-out Kai Tak Youth Sports Ground on Saturday, said that for the 40-year-old to commit long term “the fans have to show him the love, the player has to play to the standard, and his condition needs to be good”.

Acknowledging that it would be “difficult to leave a really strong legacy” from his flying visit, Silva told the South China Morning Post that “for now, one match is enough … but I never close any doors”.
