Disney+ adds ads to all subscription tiers

TheTools · 19.09.2026 22:49 · source · permalink

Thinking of watching your favorite Disney movie tonight, but don’t feel like dealing with pesky ads?

You better own the DVD.

Starting today, Disney+ can now include advertising and promotional material for every subscription tier. If you have the highest, which used to be commercial free, you now could see commercials.

It doesn’t mean you’ll be loaded up with ads, but if you’re seeing them pop up, it’s not a mistake.

An email sent to subscribers announced the changes.

"Changes to your Disney+ Subscriber Agreement, include, in summary, the following (though we do encourage you to read the new Subscriber Agreement in full): We've clarified that all Service Plans may include: (i) promotional content, (ii) sponsorships, and (iii) advertisements before/after playback of Content and in channels, live/as-live, special events, and any third-party services content," the announcment said.

If you have the cheaper tier with ads, nothing changes. You’ll still enjoy your shows the way you always have.

If you pay for commercial free, that’s not the case anymore, although you won’t see as many commercials as the other tiers. You’ll instead be given offers on Disney promotions, see trailers, branded content and possibly see ads before or after shows.
