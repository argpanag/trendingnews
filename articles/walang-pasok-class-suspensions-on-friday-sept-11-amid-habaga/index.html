WALANG PASOK: Class suspensions on Friday, Sept. 11 amid habagat rains

TheTools · 10.09.2026 18:13 · source · permalink

[Updated 5:21 a.m., Sept. 11, 2026]

MANILA, Philippines — Several areas in the country have suspended in-person classes for Friday, Sept. 11, amid flooding and heavy rains brought by the persistence of the southwest monsoon or habagat.

READ: Pagasa: Habagat rains to affect Luzon, Visayas

The areas that declared suspensions include:

Pampanga

  • San Simon – no face-to-face classes; all levels; public and private (except Concepcion Integrated School)
  • Sto. Tomas – no face-to-face classes; all levels; public and private (until Saturday, Sept. 12)
  • Minalin –  no face-to-face classes; all levels; public and private

Metro Manila

  • Valenzuela – no face-to-face classes; all levels; public and private
  • Quezon City – no face-to-face classes; all levels; public and private; shift to alternative delivery modes
  • Caloocan City – no face-to-face classes; all levels; public and private; shift to alternative delivery modes
  • Navotas City – no face-to-face classes; all levels; public and private; shift to alternative delivery modes

Pangasinan

  • Bayambang – no face-to-face classes; all levels; public and private
  • Calasiao – no face-to-face classes; all levels; public and private
  • Dagupan City – no face-to-face classes; all levels; public and private; including government work
  • Mangatarem – no face-to-face classes; all levels; public and private

Bulacan

  • Calumpit – no face-to-face classes; all levels; public and private
