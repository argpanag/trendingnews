Here's a quick rundown of the offers on the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max before we go into more detail on the phone plans from Optus, Vodafone and Telstra.

You can get a $300 digital Visa gift card when you sign up to any Optus iPhone 18 Pro or Pro Max plan through Finder, using the tables below.

Optus also has a few offers when you sign up for its 12-, 24- or 36-month phone repayment plans.

We've paired the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max with Optus's $79 Launch Plus Promo Plan on a 36-month repayment period.

However, when you hit 'GO TO SITE' on any of the plans in the table, you'll be able to choose whichever contract length and model you prefer.

Vodafone is giving $200 in bonus trade-in credit when you pre-order the iPhone 18 Pro or Pro Max on a 12-, 24- or 36-month phone repayment plan.

This offer is available until 15 October.

Its SIM-only plans are also discounted for the first year.

If you pair your phone repayments over 24 or 36 months with Vodafone's Medium or Large plans, you'll get Free Upgrade and Protect Plus included, worth $9.99 a month.

Telstra is giving $300 bonus trade-in credit when you pre-order the iPhone 18 Pro or Pro Max on a 12-, 24- or 36-month phone repayment plan.

It will also match a higher trade-in value found elsewhere.

Both offers are available until 9 October.

Similar to the other telcos, you'll need to pair your phone repayment plan with one of Telstra's SIM-only plans.

Right now, its Essential and Premium plans are discounted for the first 12 months.
