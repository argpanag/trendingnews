Multiple people hurt in hay wagon crash

TheTools · 05.10.2026 18:25 · source · permalink

WELLSBURG, N.Y. (WENY) - A hayride crash in New York on Sunday left multiple people injured, authorities said.

The crash happened in Wellsburg, a village near the Pennsylvania border.

Authorities said a tractor-trailer was hauling a hay wagon when the vehicle went too fast down a road. When the driver turned the wheel, the tractor rolled over and the wagon separated from the tractor, authorities said.

The wagon then crashed, according to authorities.

Emergency responders treated 14 people for injuries at the scene. Up to five other people were taken to a hospital, and some of them suffered critical injuries, authorities said.

It remains unclear how many people were on the hayride when the crash happened.
