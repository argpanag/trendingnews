12 Illinois researchers named to Highly Cited Researchers list

TheTools · 21.11.2026 22:48 · source · permalink

CHAMPAIGN, Ill. (WAND) – Twelve University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign researchers were named to the 2026 Clarivate Analytics Highly Cited Researchers list.

The list recognizes leading scientists and social scientists whose publications have demonstrated exceptional influence within their fields and across the global research community, according to the Clarivate website.

The honorees include:

  • Lisa Ainsworth, professor of crop sciences and plant biology
  • Kaiyu Guan, professor of natural resources and environmental sciences
  • Axel Hoffmann, professor of materials science and engineering
  • Atul Jain, professor of climate, meteorology and atmospheric sciences
  • Tracy Lawson, professor of plant biology
  • Andrew Leakey, professor of plant biology and crop sciences
  • Donald Ort, professor emeritus of plant biology and crop sciences
  • Brent Roberts, professor of psychology
  • Jimeng Sun, professor at the Siebel School of Computing and Data Science
  • Boxuan Zhao, professor of cell and developmental biology
  • Ed Diener, professor emeritus of psychology
  • Stephen Long, professor of crop sciences

For more information about the honorees, visit the University of Illinois website.
