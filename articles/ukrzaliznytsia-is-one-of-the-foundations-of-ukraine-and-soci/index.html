Photo source: Office of the President of Ukraine

The Cabinet of Ministers approved monthly additional payments for railway workers who are clearing up the aftermath of Russian strikes. Railway workers are also receiving state awards.

Russia is effectively “hunting” the railway almost every day: its infrastructure, locomotives, stations and, worst of all, people. Despite this, the state and Ukrzaliznytsia are working to recover faster and maintain connections between regions, while finding new ways to keep services running where the situation is especially difficult.

This is reported by Володимир Зеленський.

Above all, it is necessary to protect people’s safety and support railway workers directly involved in the recovery effort. Monthly additional payments for those helping to address the aftermath of Russian strikes were described as an important and fair decision.

Many railway workers are people Ukraine is rightfully proud of. These are the workers being honored today with state awards of Ukraine.
