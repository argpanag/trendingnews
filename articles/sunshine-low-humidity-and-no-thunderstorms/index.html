Sunshine, Low Humidity, and No Thunderstorms

TheTools · 26.09.2026 13:05 · source · permalink

SARASOTA, Fla. (WWSB) - Tropical Storms Fay and Gonzalo make the 6th and 7th storms in the 2026 Atlantic Hurricane Season. But there is nothing anywhere near the Suncoast. And still no hurricanes at all in the 2026 Atlantic Hurricane Season with 65 days to go.

September has been wet for some, dry for others. At SRQ, the rain total so far is 10.27”. Meanwhile, Osprey only reported 2.42”. On the southeast coast, Pompano Beach picked up 24.06”, so far.

Saturday and Sunday are shaping up to be absolutely beautiful days on the Suncoast. Expect plenty of sunshine with highs in the mid-80s—just slightly below what we typically see this time of year. The real story, though, is how much more comfortable it’ll feel. Lower humidity levels mean mornings will be crisp, with temperatures dipping into the 60s. Dew points could dip into the 50s. That’s down from the humid dew points around 75° last week.

Tropical moisture moves back over the Suncoast after the weekend. Rain chances will increase by mid-week. Dew points are also moving back into the 70s by the end of the week, so humid Florida returns. Temperatures will start to climb back toward normal by Monday and Tuesday, with highs near 90 degrees. By Wednesday afternoon, we’ll see our first chance of scattered showers and thunderstorms.

TROPICAL OUTLOOK - NEXT 7 DAYS

Tropical Storms Fay and Gonzalo continue to spin in the Atlantic, while Hurricane Nolo is bringing more flooding rains to the big island of Hawaii.

Tropical Storm Fay continues to weaken as it moves over the open Atlantic Ocean, roughly 1,090 miles southwest of the Azores. Maximum winds have dropped to around 40 mph, and gradual weakening is expected to continue over the next couple of days. Fay is moving west-southwest and will eventually turn toward the south-southwest, but it will remain well away from Florida.

Tropical Storm Gonzalo is also weakening as it moves northward near the Cabo Verde Islands. With maximum winds near 45 mph, Gonzalo could become a post-tropical remnant low or dissipate later today. It poses no threat to our area.

🎾 Saturday Sports Forecast – Sunscreen and Sunshine!

Saturday is an excellent day to hit the courts or the links. Sunny skies and comfortable temperatures in the mid-80s create ideal conditions for outdoor sports. Northeast winds around 10 to 15 mph will provide a slight breeze that’ll help keep you cool while you’re active. The low humidity means you won’t feel as fatigued, making it a perfect day for extended play. No rain in the forecast, so you can plan your matches and rounds without worry.

Beach & Boating Forecast Saturday

Beaches are sunny in the morning, and sunny in the afternoon. No thunderstorms today!

🏖️ Saturday Beach Forecast

Saturday at the beach is going to be fantastic. Sunny skies and comfortable temperatures in the mid-80s make it a perfect day to spend time by the water. Winds will be light, coming from the north around 10 to 15 mph, and there’s no thunderstorm threat at all. Surf will be minimal—just 1 foot or less—and the rip current risk is low. The water conditions are calm and inviting, making it a great day for swimming, paddleboarding, or just relaxing on the sand.

⛵ Suncoast Boating Forecast

The general trend is for a Light to Moderate Chop each day. No thunderstorms means a busy weekend on the bay and the gulf today!

SaturdayNortheast winds will start around 10 to 15 knots in the morning, shifting to north in the afternoon. Seas will run around 2 feet with moderate chop in the bays and inland waters. It’s a decent day to be on the water, but conditions won’t be glass-smooth.

SundayMuch calmer conditions move in. East winds will be light at 5 to 10 knots, becoming northwest in the afternoon. Seas will drop to 1 foot or less, and the chop in bays and inland waters will be light. This is a much more pleasant day for boating if you prefer calmer conditions.

MondayLight and variable winds around 5 knots will make for smooth, peaceful conditions on the water. Seas will remain at 1 foot or less, and bays and inland waters will be smooth. This is an ideal day for boating if you’re looking for calm waters.

TuesdaySoutheast winds will be light at 5 to 10 knots, becoming south in the afternoon. Seas stay at 1 foot or less with light chop in bays and inland waters. Keep in mind that scattered showers and isolated thunderstorms are possible in the afternoon, so monitor conditions and be prepared to head back to shore if storms develop.
