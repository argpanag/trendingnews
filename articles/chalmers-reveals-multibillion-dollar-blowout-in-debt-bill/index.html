Chalmers reveals multibillion-dollar blowout in debt bill

TheTools · 23.09.2026 01:58 · source · permalink

Treasurer Jim Chalmers has flagged that the coming mid-year budget update will show that surging global interest rates have punched a multibillion-dollar hole in the government’s finances, adding pressure on Labor to rein in the deficit.

Chalmers said on Wednesday that a key feature of the budget update, due by December, was the “damaging impact of higher bond yields on everyone’s budget, including ours”.
