Malaysia Day 2026 Celebration To Showcase Unity, Harmony Across Regions - Abdul Halim

TheTools · 09.11.2026 15:06 · source · permalink

KUALA LUMPUR, Sept 11 (Bernama) -- The Malaysia Day 2026 celebration, scheduled to be held at the Borneo Convention Centre Kuching (BCCK), Sarawak on Sept 16, will reflect the harmony and togetherness of Malaysians across the regions through the diversity of cultures and shared beliefs.

Communications Ministry secretary-general Datuk Abdul Halim Hamzah said the uniqueness of this year’s celebration would highlight the spirit of Segulai Sejalai (togetherness) through the unity of various races in a national-level official event.

He said this manifestation of harmony would be showcased through the Majlis Kesyukuran involving various faiths organised by the Unit for Other Religions (UNIFOR) on Sept 14, which would demonstrate unity among followers of various religions.

“This majlis kesyukuran is being held together with people of different faiths, who are also contributing according to their respective beliefs to ensure that the ceremony is conducted smoothly,” he said when he appeared as a guest on the Apa Khabar Malaysia programme produced by Bernama TV here today.

Abdul Halim said this would be followed by a majlis kesyukuran by Muslims at the State Mosque on Sept 15, which would be graced by Sarawak Yang Dipertua Negeri Tun Dr Wan Junaidi Tuanku Jaafar.

Abdul Halim said the highlight of the Malaysia Day celebration would also feature local traditions through a symbolic cutting of pulut kuning instead of the traditional cake-cutting ceremony, as well as the signing of the Malaysia Proclamation Book by Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim, Sarawak Premier Tan Sri Abang Johari Tun Openg and Sabah Chief Minister Datuk Seri Hajiji Noor to commemorate the physical and spiritual unity of all Malaysians.

He said the event would also be enlivened by a combination of silat, hadrah, gendang pampat drumming, lion dance, a theatre performance depicting the formation of Malaysia, as well as a concert featuring local artistes, in addition to a fireworks display at the Kuching Waterfront.

He also invited all Malaysians to watch the live broadcast of the celebration on RTM TV1 and TVS, as well as special coverage by Bernama.

“I am confident that Malaysians are eagerly looking forward to the 2026 celebration becoming a reality because although the weather may pose some challenges, we have made every effort to ensure its success.

“May, with Allah’s permission, everything proceed smoothly, and we can celebrate in a peaceful and harmonious atmosphere, with everything successfully carried out,” he said.

This year’s celebration retains the National Day theme ‘Malaysia MADANI: Kesejahteraan Dinikmati’, paired with Sarawak’s unity slogan Segulai Sejalai.
