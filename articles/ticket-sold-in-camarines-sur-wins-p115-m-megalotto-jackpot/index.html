Ticket sold in Camarines Sur wins P115-M MegaLotto jackpot

METRO

Oct 19, 2026

2:56 PM GMT+8

A lone bettor from Camarines Sur got lucky after hitting the PHP115 million jackpot of the MegaLotto 6/45 draw on Friday.

MANILA – A lone bettor from Camarines Sur got lucky after hitting the PHP115 million jackpot of the MegaLotto 6/45 draw on Friday.


The Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO), in an advisory on Saturday, said the winning ticket, purchased in Roxas Avenue, Triangulo, Naga City, correctly guessed the winning combination: 08-22-19-10-35-27.


The winner has one year to claim the PHP115,989,634.14 prize at the PCSO main office in Mandaluyong City.


Claimants must present the winning ticket and two valid identification cards.


Under the Tax Reform for Acceleration and Inclusion law, winnings above PHP10,000 are subject to a 20 percent tax.


Aside from the jackpot winner, 65 players won PHP18,461.53 each for correctly picking five of the six winning numbers; 3,068 bettors will receive PHP358.53 for matching four numbers; and 42,611 bettors will receive PHP30. 


The 6/45 Mega Lotto is drawn every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.


The PCSO urges the public to support its gaming products to raise more revenues for health initiatives, medical services, and other national charities. (PNA Christopher Lloyd Caliwan)
