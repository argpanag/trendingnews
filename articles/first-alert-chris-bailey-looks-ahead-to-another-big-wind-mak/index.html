FIRST ALERT | Chris Bailey looks ahead to another big wind maker

TheTools · 27.11.2026 20:16 · source · permalink

LEXINGTON, Ky. (WKYT)- It’s a rather wintry day out there with a mix of snow and rain showing up as our powerful holiday storm system slowly pulls away. As it exits, the next system is set to roll through here with big wind and rain on Sunday.

Our Thanksgiving was every bit as nasty as expected with record rains, gusty winds and a little slushy snowfall to end it. Some of the slushy snows continue out there this morning across central and eastern Kentucky. Light accumulations will be noted on grassy and elevated surfaces.

Highs today are generally in the 30s for the central and east with gusty winds making it feel the part of the 20s.

Saturday is a windy day and feels better as temps rebound a bit with partly sunny skies.

Sunday finds a fast-moving system rolling through here, bringing widespread rain and wind. Watch how quickly overspreads the state from west to east on Sunday. Notice how as it tapers off Monday morning; there’s the low-end risk for a flake or two showing up.

Winds on Sunday may cause issues with gusts of 40mph or greater a good bet in some areas.

Seasonable temps are with us Monday into Tuesday as we flip the calendar to December. Another potent storm system looks to sweep in here with wind and rain by the end of next week as temps come up a bit.
