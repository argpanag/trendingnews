WALANG PASOK: Mga suspendidong klase sa Miyerkoles, September 9, 2026

TheTools · 09.09.2026 13:40 · source · permalink

Suspendido ang klase sa mga paaralan sa ilang bahagi ng bansa sa Miyerkoles, September 9, 2026 dahil sa nararanasang pagbaha at masamang panahon dulot ng Habagat.

Bulacan

  • Calumpit-- All levels public and private shift to Alternative Learning Modes (ALMs)

Zambales

  • Botolan -- All levels public and private shift to Alternative Learning Modes (ALMs)

Pampanga

  • Angeles City-- All levels public and private, shift to Alternative Learning Modalities (ALMs)
  • Mexico -- All levels public and private, shift to Alternative Learning Modalities (ALMs)
  • Masantol— All levels public and private
  • San Simon-- All levels, public and private (maliban sa Concepcion Integrated School ay magsasagawa ng face-to-face classes) Sept. 7-11.
  • Sto. Tomas-- All levels, public and private, shift to Alternative Learning Modes
  • Candaba— Modular class sa mga sumusunod na paaralan:
  1. Lanang Elementary School
  2. Pasig Elementary School
  3. San Agustin Elementary School
  4. Lanang High School
  5. Pasig High School
  6. Paralaya High School
  7. Pampanga State University (PamSU)
  • San Fernando— No classes sa mga sumusunod na paaralan:
  1. Camp Olivas Elementary School (San Nicolas)
  2. Pandaras Integrated School
  3. San Nicolas Integrated School

May face-to-face classes sa lahat ng antas sa mga pampublikong paaralan na hindi nabanggit. Ipinapaubaya naman sa pamunuan ng pribadong paaralan na magsuspinde ng klase.

Benguet

  • Kibungan -- Day Care Only

Pangasinan

  • Mangaldan-- All levels, public schools only, shift to Alternative Learning Modes; private schools (discretion of the school management)
  • Calasiao-- All levels, public and private, shift to Alternative Learning Modes is encourage.

I-refresh ang page para sa update.-- FRJ GMA News
