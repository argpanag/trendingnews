Metro 2039 Preorders Could Begin October 13, It's Claimed

TheTools · 10.10.2026 21:42 · source · permalink

Metro 2039 preorders could open on October 13, 2026, according to a new report. The report also includes new information on edition prices, contents, and physical format details.

Metro 2039 is an apocalyptic first-person shooter from 4A Games, published by Deep Silver. The title is slated for release on February 4th for PlayStation 5, PC, and Xbox Series X|S. According to new reports, preordering the highly anticipated game is just days away.

  • RELATED: Metro 2039 Extended Preview—The Most Authentic Metro Experience Imaginable

Metro 2039 Deluxe Edition Details Surface in Report

Billbil-kun of Dealabs details three Metro 2039 editions: Day One, Deluxe, and Collector. Metro 2039 Day One reportedly costs €69.99 ($78.41), Deluxe €109.99 ($123.22), and Collector €299.99 ($336.08). The Collector’s Edition adds a wearable replica of the Spartan helmet to the Deluxe Edition’s contents.

All three editions are expected to come with physical releases. The report suggests the PC physical release will, of course, be a code-in-a-box. The report offers no clarification on the physical release for the other platforms.

The official Metro social media account already said pre-orders would go live this week, but Dealabs says they won’t open later than October 14, with the likely opening day being Wednesday, October 13.

Well before its release, the game had already racked up several award nominations. In fact, the game received the most nominations at the 2026 Gamescom Awards. These nominations included Best PC Game, Best PlayStation Game, Best XBOX Game, and Best Visuals. Also, Metro 2039 has already secured almost two million wishlists globally.
