  8. Ingebrigtsen 'extremely happy'published at 21:06 BST
    21:06 BST

    Men's 5,000m final

    Jakob Ingebrigtsen speaking to BBC Sport after winning the men's 5,000m.

    "It's amazing. I haven't had that big of a season. Not too many weeks ago, I agreed with my team that 2026 was far gone, but then it started to get a bit better, and there was a lot of hope.

    "I just tried to do as good as I could each day, eventualy I got momentum going and I think I've had a lot to gain from the races I've had.

    "I felt strong today, but still the 5,000m level this year has been through the roof. I'm extremely happy that I managed to get sub-13, I think I've only done it once before, so that's amazing.

    "For me the physical ability to be able to run that fast is a great thing for me going into the next races and through the winter. You fear the worst, the worst case scenario am I able to run again at all at this level, so it's truly amazing."
