Live Updates: Panthers vs. Eagles

TheTools · 18.10.2026 17:57 · source · permalink

Tanner McKee starts at quarterback as the Eagles welcome the defending NFC South Champions to Lincoln Financial Field for the Crucial Catch game.

Follow along for live updates from Lincoln Financial Field as the Eagles (2-3) host the Panthers (2-2). Carolina enters fresh off a bye week, while Philadelphia looks to bounce back from a close loss in London to the Jaguars.

With Jalen Hurts on Injured Reserve, Tanner McKee starts his first game of the season with DeVonta Smith active. Meanwhile, Bryce Young's arsenal of receivers increases as Jalen Coker returns to the field for the first time since Week 3.

First Quarter

Kickoff at CAR 35 (15:00)

  • Ryan Fitzgerald kicks off in Philadelphia! Darius Cooper picks the ball up off a bounce and returns to the PHI 26.

1st-and-10 at PHI 26 (14:55)

  • Bobby Okereke sacks Tanner McKee on a blitz for a loss of 11 yards to the PHI 15.

4th-and-10 at PHI 26 (13:40)

  • Tre'von Moehrig makes the stop on third down after a gain of 11 by Dameon Pierce. Braden Mann punts to the 15. Trevor Etienne breaks right to the CAR 39, stopped by Smael Mondon Jr.

1st-and-10 at CAR 50 (12:57)

  • Bryce Young finds Tetairoa McMillan in the middle of the field on a curl route for a gain of 15 to the PHI 35. Andrew Mukuba in coverage.

4th-and-4 at PHI 29 (10:55)

  • Panthers score on a Field Goal. Nolan Smith takes down Chuba Hubbard on third down for no gain. Ryan Fitzgerald's field goal is good from 47 yards out. (Panthers 3 - Eagles 0).

Kickoff at CAR 35 (10:50)

  • Britain Covey muffs Ryan Fitzgerald's kick and is tackled at the PHI 22 by Isaiah Simmons for four yards on the return.

4th-and-13 at PHI 19 (9:53)

  • Eagles go three-and-out for a second consecutive time. Trevor Etienne returns Braden Mann's 59-yard punt to the CAR 39, ran out of bounds by Carson Steele. Thomas Incoom commits a holding penalty, pushing Carolina back to the 16.

2nd-and-6 at CAR 20 (9:01)

  • Bryce Young finds Tetairoa McMillan for 21 yards near the right sideline to CAR 41.

1st-and-10 at CAR 41 (8:39)

  • Sack! Nolan Smith brings down Bryce Young for a loss of eight yards to the CAR 33. Initially blocked by Tommy Tremble, the tight end then ran an out route, leaving Smith unblocked.

4th-and-12 at CAR 39 (7:05)

  • Riq Woolen breaks up a deep pass downfield on third down by Bryce Young, intended for Darren Waller. Panthers bring out their punter, Sam Martin, who kicks to the PHI 11. Britain Covey calls for a fair catch.

1st-and-15 at PHI 6 (6:57)

  • After a false start on Jordan Mailata, Tanner McKee fires deep left for DeVonta Smith. The ball falls incomplete but Will Lee is called for pass interference, moving Philadelphia to the PHI 29.

Carson Steele exits the game with an injury.

3rd-and-6 at PHI 33 (6:34)

  • Conversion! Tanner McKee is forced out of the pocket right, keeps his eyes downfield, and finds Dontayvion Wicks on the sideline for seven yards at the PHI 40.

Jaelan Phillips exits the game with an injury.

2nd-and-1 at PHI 50 (5:29)

  • Dameon Pierce follows Markel Bell's block right, rushes for nine yards to the CAR 42.

4th-and-10 at PHI 41 (4:00)

  • Bobby Okereke deflects Tanner McKee's pass on third down at the line of scrimmage. Braden Mann punts to the CAR 14. Davis Moore calls for a fair catch on the right side of the field.

1st-and-10 at CAR 14 (3:53)

  • Bryce Young finds Jalen Coker open on a slant route, brought down by Kenny Moore II at the CAR 33 for a 19-yard gain.

4th-and-1 at CAR 37 (1:48)

  • The Eagles defense get their third stop on third down, with Jalen Carter batting down Bryce Young's pass. Keyshawn James-Newby is called for running into the punter on 4th-and-6. Sam Martin's second punt bounces into the end zone for a touchback.

3rd-and-4 at PHI 26 (0:23)

  • Conversion! McKee finds Dallas Goedert short right for the tight end's second catch of the drive. The five-yard gain to the PHI 31 is enough for the first down.
