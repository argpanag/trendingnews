The Supercars co-drivers already signed for 2027

TheTools · 05.10.2026 03:46 · source · permalink

AS the primary Supercars silly season prepares to go into overdrive, it’s worth setting the scene for another market that will become a big focus in the coming weeks and months.

That is, the all-important co-driver field.

Typically, teams start to pounce on enduro specialists for the following season around Great Race time, such is the value of having a speedy second set of hands for The Bend 500 and Bathurst 1000.

So, which drivers and which seats are already sorted and out of play for 2027?

Quite a few, as it turns out: at least nine, by V8 Sleuth’s count.

Grove Racing and Triple Eight both signed its current co-drivers on two-year deals covering 2026 and ’27.

So, that’s Will Davison, Tim Slade, Nick Percat and Scott Pye all under lock and key.

Walkinshaw TWG Racing and Team 18 similarly announced the recruitments of Jaxon Evans and James Courtney, respectively, on multi-year agreements that ought to run into 2027.

Craig Lowndes is also a lock at Team 18 for next season, having inked a two-year contract to remain as spearhead of the Supercheap Auto wildcard program following its departure from the Triple Eight fold.

Dick Johnson Racing has made no secret that Todd Hazelwood’s future is secure, in order to keep intact his and Brodie Kostecki’s dream team combination, while Tickford Racing only recently announced Mark Winterbottom has been extended for another couple of years.

It’s not beyond the realms of possibility that someone from outside that nine could also be low-key under contract, but for now, assume all others are impending free agents.

Keep in mind too that there may be some current full-timers who end up resorting to co-driver status, with Declan Fraser expected to be among them – and as a safe set of hands with an abundance of recent Gen3 mileage, he should command serious interest.

A potentially big fish to watch out for is David Reynolds, should his full-time days come to an end. An absolute guru around Mount Panorama, Reynolds is yet to be guaranteed another stay of execution in the full-time Team 18 seat next to Anton De Pasquale.
