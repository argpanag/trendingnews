De la Fuente keeps Lamine plans under wraps, minutes cap set at 270

Luis de la Fuente has rotated heavily in Spain’s four-in-10-days window, yet Lamine Yamal is the only player to start all three. He declined to say if the winger will rest against Croatia in Split on Tuesday.

According to El Periódico Mediterráneo, Lamine has played over 90 at Wembley, 68 in Sevilla against England and 45 in Oviedo versus Czechia, around 205-210 overall.

OneFootball Videos

That leaves a maximum of 60-65 for the finale under de la Fuente’s plan that no player will exceed 270 minutes across the four games. Whether those minutes come from the start or after the break remains open.

The coach praised him again, calling him in outstanding form and a born competitor who always wants to play. Lamine has four in three, opening the scoring each time inside six minutes.

A draw in Croatia secures a quarter-final spot. Victory brings first place if England do not beat Czechia. Otherwise top spot would likely be decided on 15 November at the Metropolitano, while defeat in Split would leave Spain needing to win that match or on 12 November in Prague.

Spain are 42 competitive matches unbeaten, extending the longest run in international football. De la Fuente said fortunes can change quickly and he tends to downplay defeats despite the streak. Croatia come off a 0-7 loss to England. De la Fuente called it a footballing accident, not the true gap, and warned it could spur them, citing Germany’s 7-1 over Brazil in 2014 and Spain’s 6-0 against Germany.
