    16:52 GMT

    It is a winning start to life under Mario Frick for Aberdeen after a blistering first half and a four-goal performance means the Dons jump up to eighth place in the table.

  10. FULL-TIME Hibernian 0-4 Dundee Unitedpublished at 16:50 GMT
    16:50 GMT

    Dundee United make it eight games unbeaten as they hammer Hibernian at Easter Road to end Marink Reedijk's resurgence in humiliating fashion.

    Zac Sapsford got a first-half double before Lyall Cameron and Jesse Randall scored in the second half to kill off any home hopes of a comeback.

    The result was all the more disappointing given Hibs' previous two games - their first ever win over Hearts at Hampden and a 4-0 thumping of Aberdeen, which cost Stephen Robinson his job with the Dons.

    Jim Goodwin is enjoying his finest spell in the top-flight as United boss. Not since 2012 under Peter Houston have they had such form and they've managed to separate themselves from the clump of sides in mid-table.
