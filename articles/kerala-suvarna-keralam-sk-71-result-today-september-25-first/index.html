The Kerala State Lottery Department is set to conduct the Suvarna Keralam SK-71 draw today, Friday, September 25, 2026, with a top prize of ₹1 crore. The weekly lottery draw is scheduled for 3 p.m. at Gorky Bhavan near Bakery Junction in Thiruvananthapuram, while the official results are expected to be published after the draw.

Suvarna Keralam tickets are priced at ₹50 and are sold through authorised lottery dealers across Kerala. Along with the ₹1 crore first prize, the SK-71 draw offers ₹30 lakh for the second prize and ₹5 lakh for the third prize. A consolation prize of ₹5,000 is also available for tickets in other series matching the first-prize number.

Participants can check the winning numbers after the draw through the Kerala State Lotteries Department's official results and gazette. The SK-71 result will include the winning numbers across the different prize categories, allowing ticket holders to verify whether they have won.

The Suvarna Keralam lottery is conducted every Friday. For SK-71, the draw is scheduled for 3 p.m. on September 25 at Gorky Bhavan in Thiruvananthapuram. The result is expected to be made available after the draw, with official result information generally published later in the afternoon.

The prize structure means that winning opportunities extend beyond the three main prize categories. Several lower-tier prizes are awarded based on the last four digits of the ticket number, while the consolation prize is given to matching numbers in the remaining series for the first prize.

Once the SK-71 draw is completed, ticket holders should compare their ticket numbers with the winning numbers published by the Kerala State Lotteries Department. Results can also be checked through the official lottery gazette and authorised result channels.

Ticket holders should retain the original ticket if their number appears in the winning list. The ticket must be verified against the official result before a prize claim is made. The department's rules also require winners to submit claims within the prescribed period.

Winners should first verify their ticket against the official result and then follow the applicable claim procedure based on the prize amount. Claims are generally required to be submitted within 30 days of the draw.

For prizes above ₹1 lakh, the claim is to be submitted to the Directorate of State Lotteries in Thiruvananthapuram, along with the required documents. These can include a self-attested copy of the ticket, valid identification, passport-sized photographs and the prescribed claim receipt. Additional documents may be required in cases involving minors or joint winners.

Lottery winners should verify the latest claim requirements with the Kerala State Lotteries Department before submitting their documents. The SK-71 result should also be checked against the official government result rather than relying solely on unofficial websites or social media posts.

The Kerala State Lotteries programme was launched by the Kerala government in 1967. The department now conducts multiple weekly lotteries as well as monthly and bumper draws, making the lottery system a regular feature of the state's public lottery programme.
