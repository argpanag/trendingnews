Louisiana State Police veteran promoted to deputy superintendent

TheTools · 12.10.2026 21:00 · source · permalink

BATON ROUGE, La. (WAFB) - Captain Mike Nicolini, a longtime member of Louisiana State Police, has been promoted to lieutenant colonel and named the agency’s Deputy Superintendent of Special Operations.

Nicolini will succeed Lt. Col. John Porter, who will move to Deputy Superintendent of Patrol following the retirement of Lt. Col. Jody Hasselbeck at the end of the month.

In his new role, Nicolini will oversee Transportation Safety Services, the DPS Capitol Detail and State Facilities, Physical Security, Crisis Response, Emergency Services, Air Support, and Protective Services.

A Marine Corps veteran, Nicolini has more than 25 years of law enforcement experience.

He joined LSP as a Trooper with Troop L in 2007 and most recently served as commander of the Protective Services Unit.

He also served on former Gov. Bobby Jindal’s security detail for five years and has been part of Gov. Jeff Landry’s detail since Landry’s election.
