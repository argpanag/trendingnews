Al-Hilal Saudi FC - In impressive winning form

Here’s a closer look at Al-Hilal Saudi FC’s current situation. We will analyse their impressive run of form and recent results. This section will also cover their tactical approach and the expected Al-Hilal Saudi FC vs. Al-Ittihad FC line-ups.

Recent form

Al-Hilal Saudi FC currently tops the Saudi Pro having won eight of their last ten matches across all competitions. In the league alone, they have amassed an impressive 18 points out of a possible 21, suffering only one surprising 2-0 home defeat at the hands of Al Suqoor. This strong run of form highlights their dominance.

Their attacking prowess is undeniable, as the team has scored 23 times in just seven league fixtures. Midfield maestros Sergej Milinković-Savić and Rúben Neves have played a key role, contributing a combined seven goals and four assists between them.

Crucially, Al-Hilal have no reported injuries, enabling them to field a full-strength squad. Recent emphatic victories, such as a 6-0 thrashing of Al Taawon in the league and a 2-1 triumph over Al-Gharafa in the AFC Champions League, have boosted their morale and provide a clear psychological advantage ahead of the Al-Hilal Saudi FC vs. Al-Ittihad FC match.

Al-Ittihad FC - Enjoying an unbeaten run

Let’s look at Al-Ittihad FC’s current situation. We will analyse their momentum, recent results, and standout players for the upcoming match in Riyadh.

Recent form

Al-Ittihad FC are enjoying a fantastic start to their domestic campaign and are currently sitting second in the Saudi Pro League table. They remain unbeaten after seven matches, having secured five wins and two draws to accumulate 17 points. This consistent run of results demonstrates their determination to compete for the title against Al-Hilal this season.

Their momentum extends across all competitions, although a recent 1-1 draw against Al Shamal in the AFC Champions League brought and end to their three-game winning streak in the league. In the domestic championship, they have been particularly solid away from home, conceding just a single goal in three games. This defensive resilience on the road will be a key factor.

The team’s attack is firing on all cylinders, with forwards S. Bergwijn and Y. En-Nesyri both having scored four goals so far. However, you should be aware that defender Hassan Kadesh is being evaluated for an injury and may not be available. Otherwise, they are at full strength for this encounter. Their impressive form and potent attack mean they pose a significant threat and are capable of challenging the tournment leaders.
