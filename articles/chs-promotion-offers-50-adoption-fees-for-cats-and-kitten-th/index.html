CHS promotion offers 50% adoption fees for cats and kitten through November

TheTools · 11.11.2026 01:13 · source · permalink

LINCOLN, Neb. (KLKN) — The Capital Humane Society (CHS) is having the Mary Jo Livingston Adoption Promotion in memory of Gary Reiners.

From today until Nov. 29, people can adopt cats or kittens over five months old for 50% off.

CHS said rabies and license fees may apply.

CHS’ cat capacity is at 100% at the Pieloch Pet Adoption Center and 80% at the Admissions and Assessment Center, leaving little room for new pets that need a place to stay.
