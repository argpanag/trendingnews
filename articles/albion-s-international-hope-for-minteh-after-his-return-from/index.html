Albion’s international hope for Minteh after his return from ankle surgery

TheTools · 12.11.2026 23:30 · source · permalink

Albion hope to see Yankuba Minteh enjoy playing time as they plan a productive international window for their squad.

Minteh has joined up with Gambia following severely limited playing time with his club.

The winger underwent minor ankle surgery after being fouled in the pre-season friendly with Roma three months ago.

He returned as a substitute away to Manchester City in the Carabao Cup and again in the home win over Brentford.

Minteh has been named in the Gambia squad to face home and away AFCON qualifiers against Malick Yalcouye’s Ivory Coast.

Albion boss Fabian Hurzeler said: “We have few different players who have different journeys now in their international duty.

“Some players have time to rest, some players will train, and we try to lift their standards, and their energy, and their condition in their body.

“Then there are some players who will go away with the international teams, and players like Minteh, of course, they need to get back on the pitch.

“I want to wish every player who's having international games all the best, and I know it's a big honour to play for their country.

“So hopefully everyone does well.”

Minteh limped out of the Roma game after an awkward challenge from Bryan Cristante but has worked hard to return.

Hurzeler said: “He needed to have a small surgery, nothing big.

“We made the decision to have surgery because it increases the probability of coming back as you were before.
