It is with deep sadness that we announce the unexpected passing of Clarence Farrell, The Cairns, Crossakiel, Kells, Co. Meath.

Predeceased by his father Vincent.

Sadly missed by his heartbroken family, his wife Catherine, children Jack, Emily and Rocco, his mother Kathleen, sisters Felicity (Pat) and Karen (George), brother Terry (Anna), nephews Caleb and Ryan, extended family and wide circle of friends.

 

May Clarence Rest In Peace.

 

Reposing in Mullen's Funeral Home O'Growney St. Athboy (C15 KW58) on Wednesday at 4pm until 7pm. Removal on Thursday morning leaving Mullen's Funeral Home arriving for Funeral Mass at 10:30am in St. James' Church, Athboy, followed by cremation service at 1pm in Lakelands Crematorium Cavan.

 

 

HOUSE PRIVATE PLEASE

 

Family Flowers Only, donations, if desired, to a charity of your choice.

 

The Farrell family would like to the extend their heartfelt gratitude to the National Ambulance Service Paramedics and An Garda Síochána who attended to Clarence on Sunday morning.
