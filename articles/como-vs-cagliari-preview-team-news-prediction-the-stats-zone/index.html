One of the stories of European football this season, Como get back in action with the visit of Cagliari to Stadio Giuseppe Sinigaglia on Saturday.

Kick-off date/time: 21 November 2026 at 2:00 pm

How to watch on TV: UK – Not televised / USA – Paramount+ & DAZN

How to watch online: UK – Cannot be streamed / USA – Paramount+ & DAZN

Como preview: Impressive Como

Cesc Fabregas’ Como have been extremely impressive in the UEFA Champions League and they have been strong in Serie A but profligacy in front of goal has perhaps undermined them a touch as they sit outside of the top four ahead of the weekend.

Como were toothless as they went into the November international break with a 2-0 defeat to defending champions Internazionale at San Siro a couple of weeks ago due to early strikes scored by Francesco Pio Esposito and Marcus Thuram.

Cagliari preview: Rediscovering their form

Cagliari won four and drew one of their opening six matches of the campaign before they then endured a four-match losing streak after the international break to drift down into the bottom half of the table.

Ahead of the break, though, the Sardinians got themselves back in the top half of the table with Cagliari edging past Frosinone by a goal to nil a couple of weeks ago, courtesy of a first half winner scored by Michel Adopo.

Como team news: Some returning

Como have players returning for this one with England international Trevoh Chalobah working himself back to full fitness alongside Assane Diao and Jesus Rodriguez.

Predicted XI (4-2-3-1): Butez; Couto, Jacobo, Kempf, Alex Valle; Maximo Perrone, Luis Milla; Nico Paz, Da Cunha, Baturina; Douvikas

Unavailable: Kean (Injured)

Questionable: Chalobah (Fitness), Addai (Injured), Diao (Fitness), Jesus Rodriguez (Injured)

Cagliari team news: Several doubts

Cagliari have plenty of doubts for this one with Yerry Mina potentially sidelined alongside fellow key men such as Harry Winks and M’Bala Nzola.

Predicted XI (3-4-2-1): Caprile; Deiola, Kofler, Javi Rodriguez; Ze Pedro, Adopo, Romano, Grandu; Kevin Carlos, Maldini; Mendy

Unavailable: Felici (Injured)

Questionable: Yerry Mina (Injured), Aurelio (Injured), Winks (Injured), Idrissi (Injured), Trepy (Injured), Nzola (Injured), Fadera (Injured)

Key stats

  • Como are unbeaten in all four Serie A meetings with Cagliari since 1994/95 (W2 D2), all of which have come since 2024/25. 
  • Como won the most recent meeting 2-1 in Cagliari on 7 March 2026; at home they won 3-1 on 10 May 2025 and drew 0-0 on 8 November 2025. 
  • The four Serie A meetings since 1994/95 have produced nine goals, an average of 2.25 per game. 
  • Como are fifth with 23 points coming into Matchweek 12 – already their best total after 11 games in four Serie A seasons since 1994/95 (previous best 18 in 2025/26). 
  • Como have won seven of their 11 games this season, four of them away (2-1 at Napoli, 4-1 at Genoa, 3-1 at Fiorentina and 3-1 at Torino). 
  • Como have conceded only one goal in four home games this season (W3 D1) and have kept three successive home clean sheets (0-0 vs Roma, 2-0 vs Sassuolo and 1-0 vs Venezia) – equalling their longest run of home clean sheets in four Serie A seasons since 1994/95 (also three, November 2025 to January 2026). 
  • Como's 2-0 defeat at Inter on 8 November ended a four-game winning run. 
  • Cagliari are ninth with 16 points, seven behind Como – their joint-second-best total after 11 games in 26 Serie A seasons since 1994/95 (best 21 in 2019/20). 
  • Cagliari's ten goals is the third-fewest in Serie A this season (only Venezia and Parma have scored fewer), yet four of their five wins have been 1-0. 
  • Cagliari beat Frosinone 1-0 on 7 November to end a run of four successive defeats. 
  • Cagliari won their first three away games this season (1-0 at Parma, 2-1 at Atalanta and 1-0 at Udinese) but have lost the last three (0-4 at Monza, 2-4 at Roma and 1-2 at Lazio), conceding ten goals. 
  • Cagliari have won only 11 of their 95 Serie A away games on a Saturday since 1994/95 (D21 L63). 
  • Andrea Calzavara has refereed only four Serie A games since 1994/95, all since December 2025 – his only previous Cagliari game was their 4-0 defeat at Monza on 19 October 2026, the only game he has taken this season. 
  • Como have never had Calzavara in charge in Serie A; home sides are W2 D1 L1 in his four games. 

‘Key stats’ are Serie A only (not all competitions)

Como vs Cagliari prediction

Como’s profligacy in front of goal in Serie A is perhaps shown by the fact that they have scored more in their four UEFA Champions League games than they have in 11 Serie A matches thus far this season. They will be on the front foot from the off as ever and should get themselves another victory here but Cagliari are defensively sound so, with the combination of Como’s wastefulness, it may not be a thriller for the neutral by Lake Como.

Como vs Cagliari tip: Como to win & under 3.5 goals
