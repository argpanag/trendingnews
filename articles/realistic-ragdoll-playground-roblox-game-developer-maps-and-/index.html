Realistic Ragdoll Playground puts reactive dummies at the center of a Simulation / Physics Sim game. Its developer is the Roblox group Chakn Studios.

The physics playground comes with paid extras and a calendar of scheduled activities. This guide covers the game’s creation date, all five game-pass prices, and both event schedules.

Who developed Realistic Ragdoll Playground, and how does it work?

Chakn Studios describes active ragdoll physics without canned animations, with every hit producing a physics-based reaction. You can shoot, shove, blow up, and run over dummies, which the developer says will stumble, clutch their wounds, fall, and get back up.

The developer also describes dummies mowing lawns, dancing, working, and chatting. Their responses to you vary: brawlers swing at you, gunmen draw their weapons, and cowards run away.

Explosive red barrels can trigger chain reactions, while drivable cars can send dummies flying. The advertised features also include grabbing and throwing dummies, then launching them with trampolines and ramps. Chakn Studios advertises badges for headshots, strikes, high launches, and other activities.

The seven advertised maps are Playground, Neighbourhood, Construction, Rooftops, Office, Mountain, and Stunt Park. The game supports PC, mobile, and console, and its listed server capacity is one player.

On 29 October 2026 at 10:20 AM IST, the game had 4,412 people playing, 1,078,950 visits, and 7,546 favorites. Likes accounted for 76% of 1,988 votes at that time. Realistic Ragdoll Playground also appeared on Roblox’s Up-and-Coming list on 29 October 2026.

Realistic Ragdoll Playground release date

Roblox lists Realistic Ragdoll Playground as created on 17 October 2026, but publishes no separate release date. Its most recent listed update is 29 October 2026.
