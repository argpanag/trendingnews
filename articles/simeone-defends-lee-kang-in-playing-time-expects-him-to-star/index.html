Simeone defends Lee Kang-in playing time, expects him to start vs Barcelona

TheTools · 08.11.2026 03:50 · source · permalink

Diego Simeone, manager of Atlético Madrid, reacted uncomfortably to questions about Lee Kang-in (25)'s playing time. He emphasized his judgment, saying there are reasons for players' playing time.

Spanish outlet Mundo Deportivo reported on the 8th (Korea time) that Simeone was asked about the playing time of Lee Kang-in and Jonathan David at a press conference ahead of the match against Barcelona.

Simeone responded to criticism that the two players' playing time has recently decreased by asking, "How little are they playing? What, exactly, is the ideal amount of playing time in minutes?"

He went on to say, "I think players are playing as much as they can have a positive impact on the team. It is only natural to help players perform at their best in their respective positions."

While disagreeing with the assessment that playing time is low, he stressed that the choices are aimed at raising the players' performance.

Lee Kang-in left Paris Saint-Germain (PSG) last summer and joined Atlético Madrid. Having started the season with his new team, he has so far recorded 2 goals and 1 assist in La Liga.

However, he has not recently been given starting opportunities. He also began on the bench in Atlético's fourth group-stage match of the 2026-2027 UEFA Champions League (UCL) against Bayern Munich on the 4th. He was brought on as a second-half substitute and spent about 30 minutes on the field.

Simeone said it is more important whether a player can contribute to the team than scrutinizing whether each player's playing time is appropriate.

The match against Barcelona is expected to be a new opportunity for Lee Kang-in. Atlético will play Barcelona at the Riyadh Air Metropolitano in Madrid, Spain, at 5 a.m. on the 9th in a 2026-2027 La Liga round 12 home match.

Atlético currently sits second in the league with 28 points. The gap with leader Barcelona (30 points) is just two points. If they win this match, they can overtake Barcelona and move to first place.

Because it is an important matchup, attention is also focused on Simeone's starting selections.

Mundo Deportivo expected Atlético to make changes to the starting lineup compared with the Bayern match. The outlet predicted, "There will be several changes compared with the Bayern match. Koke and Lee Kang-in are likely to start instead of Romain Le Normand and Alexander Sørloth."

If the prediction is correct, Lee Kang-in, who came on as a substitute in the Bayern match, will again get a starting opportunity against Barcelona.

Simeone also expressed caution about the match against Barcelona.

He emphasized, "Matches against Barcelona are always tough and difficult fights. We must play the game in a way that can threaten the opponent."

The match has the league lead at stake. If Atlético wins, they will reach 31 points and move ahead of Barcelona. Lee Kang-in can also seize the chance to prove his worth in an important match.

After the Barcelona match, Lee Kang-in is expected to join the South Korea national soccer team.

The South Korea national team, led by interim manager Robert Moreno, will play two friendly matches in Qatar during the November international window. Ranked 32nd by the International Federation of Association Football (FIFA), South Korea will face Saudi Arabia (55th) at 11:30 p.m. on the 13th and Qatar (60th) at 11 p.m. on the 17th.

With the year's final international matches approaching, it is important for Lee Kang-in to show good performances with his club.

When asked about playing time, Simeone said there was no problem with his choices. Attention is focused on what Lee Kang-in, who could start against Barcelona, will show in the important matchup.
