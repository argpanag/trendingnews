H&M Just Dropped a $25 Version of a Viral Candlestick (Instagram Is Obsessed!)

TheTools · 21.09.2026 20:59 · source · permalink

Although summer may be coming to a close, you don’t need to push floral centerpieces to the back burner until spring. H&M Home just released a new set of candlesticks that will help keep a head-turning floral detail alive all throughout both fall and winter. They’re shaped like blooming calla lilies, and their highly trendy chrome finish will make them sparkle on every table setting you incorporate them into.

“The viral calla lily candle hack made it to H&M Home,” Instagram user Et Bord wrote in a recent caption.

H&M’s New Candlesticks Totally Validate This Gorgeous DIY

As mentioned by the creator who first spotted them in local stores, these candlesticks are likely a callback of a viral calla lily candlestick DIY that seemingly originated with Toronto-based florist MayBloom in August. The hack involves inserting the stem of a detachable candlestick into the literal stem of a fresh calla lily flower. You can then use some florist wire to attach an LED taper candle to the bloom or replace the top candle cup, as shown in another Instagram video (as well as on TikTok!), to create a gorgeous centerpiece display.

“For now, I consider this an editorial experiment rather than a practical event design,” the caption of the MayBloom post reads. Even so, it seems that calla lily lighting is officially rocketing into the trendsphere this season.

You Can Shop the Look in Chrome (and Matte Black!)

If you love this DIY and want it to become an evergreen part of your decor collection, H&M’s calla lily candlesticks are the perfect solution. They are made from recycled aluminum, come in two sizes (short and tall), and feature a gorgeous petal-shaped cup. On H&M’s website, you can also pick up the candlesticks in matte black for a more rustic look. The shorter version starts at just under $25, and the tall candlestick will set you back just under $30.

“A little surreal seeing an experiment I shared on my page turn into a whole calla lily candle trend — and now H&M Home,” MayBloom wrote on Et Bord’s post. Another commenter added, “I fear I need these.” I fear exactly the same! 

But Hurry — They’re Already Selling Out

While these finds are indeed new, H&M is already completely sold out of chrome candlesticks online — and its unclear if they’ll restock soon. You’ll need to check your local store if you want to snag a pair in this trendy finish (at least for now!). However, online shoppers can still purchase a matte black option — or check out a matching chrome wall sconce that is also available online, too.

The calla lily candlestick trend will continue to live on even after calla lily season has come to a close. Try the DIY, then grab a pair of these candlesticks from H&M before they’re gone. The hardest choice you’ll have to make is whether to go with chrome or black. You might just have to get both!
