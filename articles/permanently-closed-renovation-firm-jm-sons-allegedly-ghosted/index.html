Permanently closed? Renovation firm JM & Sons allegedly 'ghosted' clients; at least one homeowner left stranded after paying $40k

TheTools · 26.11.2026 00:00 · source · permalink

Customers of renovation company JM & Sons General Contractor have taken to social media to allege that the firm has gone silent, reportedly leaving ongoing projects unfinished and homeowners scrambling for answers.

Multiple customers have alleged online that the company "ghosted" them after collecting payments, with some claiming renovation works were left incomplete.

The complaints have surfaced across Google Reviews and social media platforms, where some customers have also warned others against engaging the company.

According to its website, JM & Sons General Contractor was incorporated in 2018 when Mr Jamaluddin Bin Masood's two sons joined his interior business.

Customers claiming to have paid in full have also alleged that the firm cut off contact with them after collecting the full payment.

One of those affected is Muhammad Azly, 41, who said his family's renovation project was left in limbo after repeated attempts to contact the company went unanswered.

Family left stranded after $40k paid to firm

The 41-year-old told AsiaOne that his family of five can no longer stay in their home, which remains unfinished after they paid about $40,000 for renovation works.  

Azly shared that his family had first engaged JM & Sons General Contractor in early July for full renovation works for his home.

Azly said communication issues surfaced shortly after works began, with project managers and contractors frequently failing to respond to questions raised in a group chat.

He also alleged that although he had paid in full for renovation tiles, only part of the order was eventually placed.

Said Azly, "The project coordinator is not always contactable."

Azly and his wife realised they were being left fully unanswered by the company about two weeks ago and have not been able to contact anyone from the company since then.

His family's home has since been left with no doors to their rooms, incomplete carpentry and in an uninhabitable state.

Company listed as 'Permanently closed'

JM & Sons General Contractor, which is rated 4.5 on Google with 100 reviews, has since been listed as "Permanently Closed."

AsiaOne understands that the company was still receiving 5-star reviews on Google, in the past month.

An employee from the firm who declined to be named told AsiaOne that the company was shut down officially yesterday (Nov 24) and all staff were terminated. The claims could not be independently verified amid reports of the company's vanishing presence online.

According to netizens, visits to the renovation firm's office at North Link Building in Admiralty were unfruitful after finding the units were shuttered.

Calls made by AsiaOne to the company's listed contact number have also gone unanswered, and an email sent to the firm bounced.

The company's social media accounts, previously operating under @jmsons_sg, appear to have been deactivated amid the online complaints.

Its Google business profile is now marked "Permanently Closed".
