JPMorgan shares enter technical correction after peak decline

TheTools · 09.10.2026 16:06 · source · permalink

JPMorgan Chase JPM has entered a technical correction after falling 10% from its all-time high, Barchart said in a post on X, with the stock extending its decline on Wednesday.

The shares were down 0.51% at $329.58 at the time of writing, leaving the stock up 2.3% year to date.

The chart covered JPMorgan's daily price movement from July through Oct. 7 and showed the shares rising to the $366.50 all-time high in August before trending lower through September and early October.

The company is set to report its third-quarter earnings on Tuesday, October 13.
