Alex Eala at the Pan Pacific Open: Schedule and draw

TheTools · 26.10.2026 10:00 · source · permalink

Alexandra “Alex” Eala returns to WTA action this week as she competes in the Pan Pacific Open.

Eala will begin her singles campaign in the WTA 500 tournament against Mika Stojsavljevic in the Round of 32.

She will also compete in the doubles event, partnering with American player Desirae Krawczyk, who is of Filipino descent.

Here is Eala’s schedule and draw at the tournament:

Singles

  • Round of 32: Alex Eala vs. Mika Stojsavljevic — October 26, 4:00 p.m. (Philippine time)

Doubles

  • Round of 16: Alex Eala/Desirae Krawczyk vs. Hao-Ching Chan/Miyu Kato (schedule to be determined)
