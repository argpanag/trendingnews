She is the only Russian player to win the WTA Finals as Mirra Andreeva attempts to add name to list

TheTools · 15.11.2026 21:37 · source · permalink

Mirra Andreeva has set her sights on the 2026 WTA Finals trophy.

The Russian has been flawless at the event so far, winning all four of her matches in straight sets.

Andreeva secured her place in the final after beating Jessica Pegula 6-3, 6-4 on Saturday.

The 19-year-old will play Elina Svitolina in Sunday’s final after the Ukrainian defeated Linda Noskova in the other semi-final.

If Andreeva triumphs on Sunday, she will become only the second Russian player to capture the WTA Finals singles event.

The 17-year-old Russian first stunned Serena in the Wimbledon final, winning the first of her five Grand Slam titles.

A few months later, Sharapova again defeated the future 23-time major singles champion.

Sharapova set up a final with Serena at the 2004 WTA Finals after defeating compatriots Svetlana Kuznetsova, Vera Zvonareva and Anastasia Myskina.

Sharapova ended Serena’s dream of winning a second WTA Finals title in four years, beating the American 4–6, 6–2, 6–4 in the final.

“Oh my gosh, I’m still shaking, I can’t believe it,” Sharapova told ESPN after the final, as reported by tennis.com.

“So many things are going through my mind right now—you know, the way I just pulled it out and stuck in there. I mean, oh, it’s crazy!”

Serena Williams dominated her rivalry with Maria Sharapova after losing the final of the 2004 WTA Finals

After suffering a second consecutive defeat to the Russian, many would have expected Serena and Sharapova to contest a close-fought rivalry throughout the rest of their respective careers.

That potential scenario never played out in reality.

Between 2005 and 2019, Serena won 19 consecutive matches against Sharapova. The Russian won only four sets across those 19 contests.

“Obviously, I’m going against a player who has won five Grand Slams and reached the final of even more so I knew it would be tough,” Serena said after beating Sharapova in their last-ever match at the 2019 US Open.

“I was super intense and super focused because it was an incredibly tough draw.

“Whenever I come up against her I play my best tennis.”
