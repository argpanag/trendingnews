Trump's Letter To North Korean Leader Delivers Embarrassing Result: Report

TheTools · 23.11.2026 03:42 · source · permalink

President Donald Trump is being snubbed by North Korean leader Kim Jong Un, according to a Sunday report from the Wall Street Journal.

Though Trump has gushed about his friendship with Kim, his administration has made multiple attempts to get in touch with Pyongyang since returning to the White House, to no avail.

Trump was reportedly left hanging when he first reached out via established phone lines and communication channels shortly after his January 2025 inauguration.

Delivering a letter to Kim proved even more difficult. White House officials basically staked out North Korea’s United Nations office in New York City waiting for an opportunity.

They were reportedly able to foist a letter onto a North Korean diplomat, who later “tossed the unopened letter in front of a U.S. representative” during a U.N. meeting.

Trump basically issued an open call to Kim while on his way to Asia last October, telling reporters, “Put out the word. They have a lot of nuclear weapons but not a lot of telephone service.”

Persuading Pyongyang to abandon its atomic weapons was said to be the focus of Trump’s first-term sit-downs with Kim and now seems to be a topic the supreme leader is refusing to revisit.

Kim has publicly pushed for the U.S. to recognize North Korea as a nuclear power, telling Washington it was looking at a choice between “peaceful coexistence or eternal confrontation” in a February speech.

There was reportedly a “last-minute scramble” for the leaders to meet during Trump’s trip to China last week but Kim “set conditions the U.S. couldn’t accept,” according to those familiar.

Trump has described his relationship with Kim in almost intimate terms over the years. During a 2018 campaign rally, the U.S. president said he and Kim “fell in love” when they first met several months prior.

In a 2020 interview with journalist Bob Woodward, Trump likened his dynamic with Kim to something romantic.

“You meet somebody and you have a good chemistry,” he said. “You meet a woman. In one second, you know whether or not it’s all going to happen.”
