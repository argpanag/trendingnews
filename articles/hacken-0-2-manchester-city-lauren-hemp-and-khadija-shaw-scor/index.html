Highlights: Man City stay unbeaten in Champions League after win at Hacken

Substitutes Lauren Hemp and Khadija Shaw scored to help Manchester City claim a vital Champions League win at Swedish side Hacken.

After a goalless first half, the pair were introduced for the start of the second period in a match played in light snow on an artificial pitch.

England winger Hemp scored a fortunate opener in the 55th minute when Emma Ostlund's clearance bounced off her and found the bottom corner.

City pushed for a second and Mary Fowler put the ball in the net, only for it to be ruled out as Yui Hasegawa was in an offside position in front of home goalkeeper Jennifer Falk.

Hemp then hit the post before playing a crucial role in City's second.

Her fine pass from inside her own half released Shaw and the Jamaica striker held off a challenge and finished well past Falk to make it 2-0 after 77 minutes.

City had a chance to make it 3-0 when they were awarded a stoppage-time penalty after Aivi Luik was ruled to have fouled Shaw, but Fowler's spot-kick was saved.

The win took City up to third in the Champions League table, one point ahead of Bayern Munich, Arsenal and Chelsea who all have a game in hand.

There is one more match for City in the league phase - at home to bottom side Austria Vienna on 16 December.

Andree Jeglertz's side will be pushing for a win to claim a top-four finish, which would see them avoid the play-offs and move straight into the quarter-finals.

City's squad strength proves crucial

City came into this match after a disappointing 1-0 loss at Chelsea, who leapfrogged Jeglertz's side to go top of the Women's Super League.

And the City boss opted to make five changes from that game as he looked to give his key players a rest.

Falk, who had a spell on loan at Liverpool last season, did well to keep it goalless in the first half.

She twice denied Aoba Fujino, got down quickly to keep out Grace Clinton's header and parried a powerful attempt from Rebecca Knaak.

Hacken had frustrated Chelsea with a 0-0 draw in London in their most recent Champions League game, but City's strength in depth proved crucial as their substitutes changed the game.

Hemp made the first and created the second, with another goal for Shaw, who is the top scorer in the WSL this season with 12 goals in 10 games.

Swedish champions Hacken did not register a shot on target until the 76th minute, with City's centre-half pairing of Knaak and Gracie Prior impressing.

A failure to win would have left City facing the play-offs, but this victory keeps alive their hopes of advancing straight to the quarter-finals - although they will need to beat Austria Vienna and hope other results go their way.
