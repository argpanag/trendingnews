Sheriff's office asks everyone to shelter in place - The Pagosa Springs Sun

TheTools · 23.10.2026 18:58 · source · permalink

The Archuleta County Sheriff's Office has requested that everyone shelter in place.

The agency notes water levels are beginning to recede, but many roads are impassable and unsafe for travel.

 If you have an immediate medical emergency, please contact 911.

The evacuation check-in center is not currently accessible, but if you need an evacuation location, the PLPOA Clubhouse at 230 Port Ave, is open and available.
