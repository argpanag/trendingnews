Cool mornings, warm afternoons and little hope for rain

TheTools · 23.10.2026 23:32 · source · permalink

Pleasant weather will continue through the weekend and into next week, but persistent dryness and low humidity will keep the fire danger elevated across the region.

Temperatures in the Jackson metro once again climbed from the middle 40s Friday morning to the middle 80s in the afternoon. These significant daily temperature swings will continue as a weak ridge settles over the Deep South and keeps conditions slightly warmer than average.

Overnight lows will range from the middle 40s to lower 50s before afternoon temperatures return to the lower and middle 80s. Skies will remain mainly clear.

A similar pattern is expected over the next several days, with morning temperatures in the 40s and lower 50s followed by afternoon highs in the upper 70s and lower 80s.

Conditions will remain largely unchanged during the final week of October. A light southerly breeze could make the mornings slightly warmer, with lows in the upper 40s and lower 50s. Afternoon highs near the lower 80s will remain possible each day.

There is a chance for rain next weekend, but current trends do not indicate a significant amount. Any rainfall would likely provide little relief from the ongoing dry conditions.

The region has not recorded more than an inch of rain in a single day since September 3, highlighting the need for a widespread, soaking rainfall.
