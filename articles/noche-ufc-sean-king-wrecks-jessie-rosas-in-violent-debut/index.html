Noche UFC: Sean King Wrecks Jessie Rosas in Violent Debut

TheTools · 12.09.2026 18:53 · source · permalink
  • Events
  • Results & Recaps
  • UFC

After a cut took him out of a fight with Miles Johns last month, Jessie Rosas instead made his promotional debut at Noche UFC on Saturday, taking on fellow newcomer Sean King III.

Rosas, the brother of UFC bantamweight star Raul Rosas Jr., was getting the lion’s share of the attention ahead of the fight, though King was coming into it undefeated.

Turns out, pundits should have been paying more attention to King. He was the entire story on Saturday, debuting in violent, explosive fashion.

The fight started with King throwing a lot of kicks. As the longer fighter, he looked to establish range right away. King then shot in, getting the bodylock then lifting, and slamming Rosas on the side of his head. The slam put Rosas out cold, and the extra shots were just the nail in the coffin. Exactly three landed before the ref could dive in to make the save. Rosas remained face down for a minute, but appeared okay afterwards.

An impressive debut for Sean King III, who improved to 7-0 with the win, while taking exactly zero damage in the fight. Given the recent trend of quick turnarounds in the UFC, we could be seeing him again soon.

Official Result: Sean King def. Jessie Rosas by KO (slam and punches), Round 1, 0:36
