World Rally Championship: Elfyn Evans v Oliver Solberg in final stage shootout for title

Summary

  • Final stage of World Rally Championship season in Italy

  • Elfyn Evans wins title after Oliver Solberg crashes in final stage

  • The Welshman becomes only the third British world champion and the first in 25 years

  • Solberg's dad, Petter, won title in 2003

    Elfyn Evans wins WRC title

    And breathe, everyone.

    Elfyn Evans has become the UK's first World Rally Champion in 25 years as he joins Colin McRae and Richard Burns in the history books.

    He's also Wales' first drivers' champion.

    You can read the report from the dramatic conclusion here, and stay tuned across BBC Sport for more reaction from a famous day in Welsh sport.

    A huge congratulations to Elfyn and co-driver Scott Martin. Two deserving champions.

    We'll see you next year for Rally Monte Carlo - and you'll be able to follow it across BBC Sport.

    Donna Evans, Elfyn's wife, on Rally TV: "It was awful. Normally I stay at home and stay out of this. I'm so proud and it's been a long time coming. I thought it was over and we'd be on the first plane out of here tonight but we're so happy."

    Caroline Martin, Scott's wife, on Rally TV: "This has been 20 years in the making. It's been intense and a long time coming. They have been so close. Oliver's time will come, like with Scott and Elfyn."

  3. 'I tried my very best'published at 15:00 BST 4 October
    15:00 BST 4 October

    Elfyn Evans wins WRC title

    An emotional Oliver Solberg after his heartbreaking finish on Rally TV: "Two corners, two corners. First congratulations to Elfyn. He has been very consistent all season. That I even had a shot after my rollercoaster of a season is incredible.

    "I tried everything. I knew I was pushing but didn't know if it was enough. I pushed even more and I couldn't save the car. I tried my very best.

    "When you are so close, I maybe didn't believe it at the beginning of the weekend but you believe more and more. I tried. Hopefully I have a few more years to try again."
