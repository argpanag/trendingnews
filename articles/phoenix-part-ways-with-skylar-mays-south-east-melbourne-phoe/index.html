Phoenix part ways with Skylar Mays | South East Melbourne Phoenix

TheTools · 13.11.2026 14:17 · source · permalink

By

Phoenix Media

12 Nov

1

min read

The South East Melbourne Phoenix can confirm the club has parted ways with American point guard Skylar Mays, effective immediately.

The 29-year-old played 11 games for the Phoenix during NBL27, averaging 11.7 points and four assists.

Phoenix General Manager of Basketball Operations Simon Mitchell thanked Mays for his contribution to the club.

“As a club we thank Skylar for his professionalism and contributions during his time with the Phoenix, and we wish him success in the future.” Mitchell said.

“This was a tough decision as Skylar is immensely talented, however, we have goals for this group, and it’s been decided a change is required to realise those.”

The Phoenix will begin their search for a replacement immediately.
