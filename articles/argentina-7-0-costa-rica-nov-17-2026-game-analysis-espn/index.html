Argentina 7-0 Costa Rica (Nov 17, 2026) Game Analysis - ESPN

TheTools · 18.11.2026 03:27 · source · permalink

Argentina bounced back from a loss to Uruguay on Saturday with a seven-goal explosion against Costa Rica on Tuesday, spurred on by rising star Nico Paz and fellow striker Julián Álvarez.

Argentina's first game since the retirement of national team legend Lionel Messi ended poorly with a 2-1 defeat to CONMEBOL rival Uruguay, but the mood and tone of Tuesday's game was much different from the outset in a 7-0 win over an overmatched Costa Rica.

Paz and Álvarez traded classy first-half goals over the course of the opening 45 minutes of the game in Córdoba to stake Argentina to a 4-0 lead at the break.

It was more of the same in the second half, as Franco Mastantuono, on loan at Fiorentina from Real Madrid, scored either side of a goal from Alexis Mac Allister to put Argentina seven goals clear by the time the referee blew the full-time whistle.

Como striker Paz has now scored in four of his last five games for Argentina, while Álvarez has struck in each of his last four appearances after finding the net only twice in his previous 15 matches.

The game puts a bow on an eventful year for Lionel Scaloni and Argentina, who lost to Spain the World Cup final and saw the country's greatest player retire after a farewell match in October.
