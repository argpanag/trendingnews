Cam Skattebo's puke-and-rally, TD flips traditions continue in NFL

TheTools · 13.11.2026 14:18 · source · permalink

All-time great Arizona State football player Cam Skattebo's legend continues in the NFL, on Week 10's Thursday Night Football on Nov. 12.

That legend is including his history of vomiting during big games. After his New York Giants' final drive of the first quarter, Skattebo was reported to have puked at his feet on the sideline. He reportedly covered up the mess with a towel and got ready to rally.

The Giants trail their NFC East rival Washington Commanders (2-5-1) 10-7 at the half.

Skattebo once puked-and-rallied his way to Peach Bowl Offensive Most Valuable Player honors when with the Arizona State Sun Devils during the 2024-25 season. Skattebo ran the rock 30 times for 143 rush yards with two rushing touchdowns, caught eight passes for 99 receiving yards and threw one pass for 42 pass yards and a passing touchdown despite the throwing up during the game.

The vomiting has not stopped him. Skattebo scored the Giants' lone touchdown in the first half, completing the celebration with his signature flip.

The second-year pro also enters the third quarter with eight carries for 35 yards and two receptions for 37 yards. Skattebo, New York's top running back option, came into the game with 494 all-purpose yards on the season.

The Giants are 5-2-1 on the year as they look to build a four game lead on them in the division standings.
