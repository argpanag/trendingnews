Prince Harry 'takes aim' at Meghan Markle with 'disrespectful' comment

TheTools · 24.11.2026 05:15 · source · permalink

Prince Harry has reportedly "taken aim" at Meghan Markle with a "disrespectful" comment.

The Duke of Sussex sparked criticism after joking about trying to change the American accents of the couple's children, Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet, during an appearance on ITV's This Morning.

The Daily Express described Harry as having "taken aim" at Meghan in its headline after some viewers argued his comment could also be seen as referring to his American wife's accent.

Harry appeared on the programme alongside Alison Hammond and Dermot O'Leary while promoting the 2027 Invictus Games in Birmingham.

During the interview, the Duke spoke about his family's move back to Britain after six years living in California and discussed how Archie, seven, and Lilibet, five, had adapted.

He described the move, which has seen the royals spotted in Oxfordshire, as a "big culture shift" for the children, including having to wear school uniforms, but said their confidence had "gone through the roof".

Harry then joked that he was attempting to "squeeze" the American accents out of his children, while saying Lilibet could already do an impression of saying "Mummy."

The remark prompted criticism from some people online, with the Express reporting that some viewers regarded it as disrespectful towards Meghan, who was born and raised in the United States.

One person wrote: "It's a bit of an odd thing to joke about given their own mother has an American accent."

His security arrangements changed after Harry and Meghan stepped back from their roles as working members of the Royal Family in 2020 and moved overseas.
