Sudbury Wolves drop OHL pre-season finale against Kingston

TheTools · 13.09.2026 11:28 · source · permalink

Regular season opens when Peterborough Petes visit Sudbury this coming Friday night

Article content

A Sudbury Wolves squad with a lengthy list of scratches managed to keep pace for most of its OHL pre-season finale against the Kingston Frontenacs, but eventually fell by a 4-3 score at Ray Twinney Recreation Complex in Newmarket on Saturday afternoon.

Kingston Harris scored his second goal in exhibition play and fellow rookie forward Parker Smith added his third before NHL draft-eligible defenceman Genc Ula potted his second for Sudbury, which slipped to 3-3 to finish the pre-season after also losing 3-0 to the Soo Greyhounds on home ice Friday night.

Ula, Hudson Martin and Colton Colby registered assists for the Wolves. Paolo Frasca, with 13 saves on 16 shots, and Owen Leonard, with seven on eight, split the netminding duties for the locals, who will open the OHL regular season agains the Peterborough Petes this coming Friday.

Start time at Sudbury Community Arena is 7:05 p.m.

Brody Cook, Aleks Kulemin, Alex Misiak and Tanner Nettleton scored for Kingston. Royden Smith and Mason Raycroft, the son of former Wolves and Fronts netminder Andrew Raycroft, combined to make 18 saves for Kingston.

Like most of its league rivals, Sudbury was without several players who left for NHL rookie camps this past week, including Jan Chovan to Los Angeles, Rowan Henderson to Chicago, JC Lemieux to Buffalo, Adam Nemec to Ottawa, Vladimir Provorov to Columbus, Artem Gonchar to the New York Rangers and Finn Kearns to Seattle.

Ivan Belikov, Luca Blonda, Bjorn Bronas, Hudson Chitaroni, Blake Clayton, Kash Kwajah, Liam Ladds and Patrik Ula also sat out Saturday’s game. Sudbury called up prospects Cole Krottner, Ronan Quinn, Connor Hollebek and Brody Schulz and defenceman Fischer Ishwaran, all of whom attended training camp last month.
