Hansi Flick Raves About the New Raphinha

Raphinha is enjoying a start to the season that has completely changed the conversation around him. After being moved into a central striker role, the Brazilian has found a new dimension to his game and quickly become one of the biggest stars at Barcelona. For Hansi Flick, what the forward is showing is already on another level.

Turning him into the team’s attacking focal point could have seemed like a risky decision, but the early results are fully backing Flick’s approach. The Brazilian is no longer simply a winger tasked with creating danger from the outside; he has also become a constant threat between the center-backs.



Flick Ranks Raphinha Among the Best in the World

“Raphinha is playing fantastic, on another level. It’s unbelievable. When he plays like this, for me, he is one of the best players in the world,” Hansi Flick said during his press conference ahead of the clash with Levante.

The Brazilian has scored eight goals in five matches at the start of the campaign and has found the net in every game Barcelona have played so far.



Raphinha Leads a Barcelona Attack That Has Fans Dreaming

The Brazilian’s impact is part of an almost perfect start for Barcelona. The team won its first four LaLiga matches before making a statement in the UEFA Champions League, securing a dominant 5-1 victory over Feyenoord.

For the German coach, Barcelona also have several alternatives capable of filling the central role. Flick highlighted that Anthony Gordon, Karim Adeyemi, Dani Olmo and Fermín López can also operate through the middle, while pointing to other options within the squad.



Barcelona Look to Keep Raphinha Flying

The next challenge will be Levante, with Barcelona looking to maintain their perfect start to the league campaign.

The pressure will increase as the season progresses and tougher matches arrive, particularly with the European schedule developing alongside domestic competition. That is when the Brazilian’s ability to sustain this level from a completely different position will truly be tested.
