Knicks open NBA season with a chance to end the league’s longest drought without a repeat champion

The 2026-27 NBA season tips off today with a high-powered three-game slate featuring the five consensus title favorites heading into the campaign.

Opening-day action begins in an innovative early time slot at 3:00 PM ET as the Boston Celtics, holding the fifth-best championship odds, visit the Detroit Pistons, who earned the top seed in the Eastern Conference last year, in a game designed to capture European primetime audiences.

Following that matchup, the New York Knicks will raise their championship banner and receive their rings at Madison Square Garden before hosting a revamped Philadelphia 76ers squad boasting the superstar tandem of LeBron James and Jaylen Brown.

The opening slate concludes in the West with a marquee Western Conference Finals rematch between the Oklahoma City Thunder and the San Antonio Spurs, two powerhouses currently sitting as the overall betting favorites to hoist the Larry O'Brien Trophy ahead of both Eastern contenders.

Knicks Seek to End Historic Era of NBA Parity

By capturing their first title in 53 seasons, New York extended a remarkable stretch that has seen eight different champions across the last eight years (Raptors, Lakers, Bucks, Warriors, Nuggets, Celtics, Thunder, and Knicks), the longest streak of non-repeating champions in NBA history.

Across this historic era of parity, only the Golden State Warriors, Boston Celtics, and Miami Heat have managed to reach multiple Finals appearances, with Miami dropping both of their title trips.

Entering this campaign with largely the same core roster intact, the Knicks possess the continuity needed to halt this championship carousel and become the league's first back-to-back champions since the Warriors (2017–2018).

However, across an grueling 82-game regular season where injuries and roster fluctuations dictate momentum, the true title hierarchy usually crystallizes after the 2027 All-Star Game in Phoenix.

Standing in New York's path are formidable obstacles across both conferences. In the East, the rival Philadelphia 76ers join the Knicks as co-favorites to reach the Eastern Conference Finals. Meanwhile, the Western Conference features the ultimate title favorites: the 2024-25 champion Oklahoma City Thunder and the runner-up San Antonio Spurs.

Despite displaying early postseason growing pains last spring, San Antonio enters the year with immense expectations as reigning Defensive Player of the Year Victor Wembanyama enters his fourth NBA season with valuable playoff mileage under his belt.

As opening night unfolds, the league embarks on another chapter expected to deliver iconic individual milestones, much like Bam Adebayo's historic 83-point explosion last season, and high-stakes postseason drama.
