Here's when you can watch a comedy based in Newport on the BBC

TheTools · 30.10.2026 20:32 · source · permalink

A new comedy drama set in Newport is going to debut on BBC iPlayer this week before its BBC airing in November.

Zooport, created by Newport writer and filmmaker Darragh Mortell, will premiere on October 30 as one of four taster films being released by BBC Cymru Wales to showcase new work from Welsh creatives.

The comedy drama follows disgraced filmmaker Dav, played by Jim Caesar (Big Boys), who returns to his hometown of Newport in an attempt to rebuild his life after a scandal in London.

Mr Mortell said: "Newport is such a distinctive city, full of character, humour and brilliant people, and I wanted to portray it with authenticity and affection.

"While the story is rooted in South Wales, I hope the themes will resonate with anyone who's ever left home and wondered where they really belong."

Dav’s homecoming quickly becomes complicated when he discovers his mother has rented out his old bedroom to a Ukrainian refugee, and he is forced to confront past mistakes, family tensions, and an ex-girlfriend, all while navigating a cast of eccentric Newport characters.

The cast includes Carly-Sophia Davies, Darren Evans, Sam C. Wilson, Arthur Aliiev, Charlie Brooks, Kimberley Nixon, and Rakie Ayola.

Mr Mortell said he hopes the project could lead to a full series.

He said: "I'm incredibly grateful to BBC Wales for backing the project and to the cast and crew who helped bring it to life.

"I'd love this taster episode to be the start of a full series, enabling us to feature as much authentic Newport talent, behind and in front of the camera, as possible."

Darragh Mortell and David P Davies (Image: Supplied)

Producer David P. Davis, known for his work on Industry, is joined by crew members whose work includes Doctor Who, The White Lotus, and House of the Dragon.

Nick Andrew, head of commissioning for BBC Cymru Wales, said: "We’re delighted to be supporting world-class Welsh talent and creatives through these scripted pilots.

"We have a strong track record in taking creative risks and piloting distinctive content for Wales."

Zooport will be broadcast on BBC Wales on November 20.
