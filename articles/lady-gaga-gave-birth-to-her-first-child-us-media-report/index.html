Lady Gaga gave birth to her first child, US media report

TheTools · 12.09.2026 07:15 · source · permalink

US media reported that pop superstar Lady Gaga gave birth to her first child with her fiancé, businessman Michael Polansky. Pakistan’s Dawn wrote about this on Friday.

Media confirmation

A source confirmed the information about the child’s birth to Page Six and People. At the same time, the baby’s name, sex, and date of birth have not been disclosed.

The 40-year-old performer, whose real name is Stefani Germanotta, began a relationship with Polansky in 2020. In 2024, she became engaged to the businessman.

More current news is available on the UA.News Telegram channel Telegram.

Plans for motherhood

The singer, whom fans call Mother Monster, has spoken in recent years about her desire to become a mother. In an interview with The New York Times in March last year, she said she was excited about the prospect of motherhood, although she had previously had concerns about it.

Lady Gaga also noted that she had been thinking about combining motherhood with a life and career that had been centered on her for many years. According to her, it is important for her to give children space so that they can determine their own path in life.

In April, the singer completed the Mayhem Ball world tour. Her album Mayhem received several Grammy nominations and earlier this year won the award for Best Pop Vocal Album.
