Elena Rybakina pulls out of 2026 WTA Finals at Indian Wells due to medical issue and is replaced by Marta Kostyuk

TheTools · 08.11.2026 07:07 · source · permalink

The world No 1 has revealed she is "not physically ready to compete" on Sunday and has been replaced in draw by Marta Kostyuk; You can watch the WTA Finals live on Sky Sports from 8-15 November

Saturday 7 November 2026 21:36, UK

Defending champion Elena Rybakina has withdrawal from the 2026 WTA Finals on the eve of the season-ending tournament citing a medical issue.

The world No 1 was set to face Linda Noskova in the tournament's opening match on Sunday. Her place in Group 1 in the season-ending tournament will now be taken by Marta Kostyuk.

In a statement, the three-time Grand Slam winner said: "I am really sad to inform all my fans and supporters that I unfortunately have to withdraw from the WTA Finals this year.

"I've had an incredible season but my body is just not physically ready to compete. I'm going to spend some time resting and recovering at home and getting myself ready for the 2027 season.

"The WTA Finals is a very special tournament and I wish everyone the best of luck this week. Can't wait to be back in Indian Wells in March!"

Rybakina was going to defend her title that she won going undefeated in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia last year.

The 2025 edition saw her arrive as the last qualifier, but she was the first to earn her spot this year after winning the US Open.

The 27-year-old will still end the season as world No 1 despite the withdrawal, finishing with a 54-14 record and four titles - the Australian Open, Stuttgart, the US Open and Ningbo Open.

Noskova, who became Wimbledon's youngest women's singles champion since Petra Kvitova this summer at the age of 21, will now face world No 10 Kostyuk instead.
