PRC Applauds the Top Performing Schools of the September 2026 GECBLE! | Professional Regulation Commission

TheTools · 02.10.2026 15:39 · source · permalink

The Professional Regulation Commission (PRC / Commission) extends its commendation and salute to the Top Performing Schools in the September 2026 Geodetic Engineers Computer-Based Licensure Examination (GECBLE) for their outstanding performance and commitment to excellence in Geodetic Engineer education.

The top four (4) recognized schools, having distinguished themselves through the formidable and committed performance of their examinees, each with 10 or more examinees and a passing rate of at least 80%. Their achievement reaffirms the quality of education, training, and preparation provided to future Registered Geodetic Engineers (RGEs).

The Commission likewise commends the administrators, faculty members, mentors, and successful examinees whose dedication and collective efforts contributed to this achievement. Their success highlights the important role of higher education institutions in developing competent, ethical, and globally competitive registered Geodetic Engineers who adhere to legal and professional frameworks, precise measurement, scientific mapping, and sustainable management of the Earth’s surface and quality living and well-being of the Filipino people.

The results of the September 2026 GECBLE were released two (2) working days after the last day of examination, reflecting the Commission’s continuing efforts to provide timely, efficient, and responsive licensure examination services.

The PRC honors this milestone with the top-performing schools and the newest members of the Geodetic Engineer profession. Congratulations on this well-earned achievement!
