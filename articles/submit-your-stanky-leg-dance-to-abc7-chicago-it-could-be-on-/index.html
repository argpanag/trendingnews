Submit your 'Stanky Leg' dance to ABC7 Chicago; it could be on TV after Bears wins

TheTools · 03.11.2026 19:10 · source · permalink

CHICAGO (WLS) -- Chicago Bears Coach Ben Johnson has been seen doing a dance some have called the "Stanky Leg" after Bears wins.

ABC7 Chicago wants viewers to send us their version of Coach Johnson's dance or the "Stanky Leg."

Send in video of you, your children, your parents or your coworkers doing the dance.

After every Bears victory, ABC7 will try to show fans doing the "Stanky Leg."

ABC7 hopes to be showing videos until Super Bowl Sunday.
