Here's what's open and closed in Ottawa this Thanksgiving | CBC News

TheTools · 10.10.2026 18:20 · source · permalink

Many changes Monday, Oct. 12, including waste pickup and transit

Autumn is well underway and it's Thanksgiving time again across Canada.

The harvest celebration falls on the second Monday of October, Oct. 12 in 2026.

Here's a partial list of holiday opening hours and closures in Ottawa. It's best to check directly with any businesses or services ahead of time.

Shopping and attractions

Stores in certain areas of the city including the Glebe and ByWard Market can open on holidays under the province's Retail Business Holidays Act.

Grocery stores

Most grocery stores in the city will be closed Monday, but some in those designated areas will remain open.

Some stores may have different hours on Sunday and Monday.

Alcohol

Beer Store and LCBO locations will be closed.

Alcohol may be available at rural LCBO convenience outlets and at participating grocery stores that are open Monday.

Malls

Bayshore, Place d'Orléans and St. Laurent malls will be closed. The Rideau Centre will be open, but individual store hours may vary.

People cross at the intersection of Orléans and Jeanne d'Arc boulevards in Orléans last November. (Gabrielle Huston/CBC)

Museums and galleries

The National Gallery of Canada and the agriculture, aviation, history, nature, science and war museums will be open.

The Ottawa Art Gallery and Diefenbunker will be closed.

Services

Waste

  • There will be no curbside green bin, recycling or garbage collection, nor will there be multi-residential recycling or green bin collection.
  • Pickup will resume on Tuesday with collection delayed by one day for the rest of the week.
  • Multi-residential garbage containers will be collected as normal.

Libraries

All Ottawa Public Library branches will be closed.

Municipal child care

All municipal child-care centres will be closed.

Recreation

Many recreation facilities are closed, though some will be open on modified schedules.

A view from a drone of the Rideau Canal in late September 2026. (Michel Aspirot/CBC)

Ottawa Public Health

  • The Site mobile van will operate from 5 p.m. to 11:30 p.m.
  • The Sexual Health Clinic and satellite clinics will be closed.
  • Dental clinics will be closed.

Pharmacies

Some locations will be open. Check individual stores for holiday hours.

Banks

Monday is a bank holiday and many branches are closed. There may be access to some ATMs.

Other services

  • City hall and client service centres at Ben Franklin Place and 255 Centrum Blvd. will be closed.
  • The provincial court at 100 Constellation Dr. will also be closed.
  • The city's 311 contact centre will be open for urgent matters.
  • ServiceOntario, Service Canada and similar other government services are closed.

Getting around

Parking

All City of Ottawa parking regulations and restrictions apply.

Transit

  • OC Transpo will operate on a Sunday schedule.
  • OC Transpo's Rideau Centre customer service centre will be open 10 a.m. to 6 p.m.
  • Para Transpo regular bookings will be automatically cancelled. Riders can book trips through My Para Transpo or by calling 613-560-5000.
  • The taxi coupon line will remain open, as will the 57272 Para Transpo text service.
