More S’pore families opting for 1-day funerals due to cost factors, small social & family circle

TheTools · 28.11.2026 10:02 · source · permalink

Roughly 10 per cent of funerals in Singapore are now completed within 24 hours.

November 28, 2026, 10:04 AM

Photos via Shin Min Daily News.

More families in Singapore are opting for “one-day funerals”, a streamlined arrangement that completes the entire process—from collecting the body to cremation—within 24 hours, Shin Min Daily News reported on Nov. 25, 2026.

About 10 per cent of cases

According to some funeral directors interviewed by the Chinese daily, such arrangements now account for nearly 10 per cent of their cases.

Ang Ziqian, vice-president of the Association of Funeral Directors Singapore and executive director of Ang Chin Moh Funeral Directors, told Shin Min that he has observed a clear upward trend in one-day funerals since the Covid-19 pandemic.

He noted that such cases make up close to 10 per cent of his company’s total volume in 2026.

Similarly, Jenny Tay, managing director of Direct Funeral Services, shared that her company has handled over 300 one-day funerals annually since 2023.

"We handled 399 cases in 2023, 419 in 2024, 385 last year, and 361 up to October this year," she told Shin Min.

Quek Soo Chek, owner of Union Casket, told Shin Min that he has similarly observed an increase in one-day funerals over the past two years.

While his company used to handle fewer than one such case a month, they now handle four to five monthly, making up about 10 per cent of their business.

Lower costs and changing mindsets

The increase in one-day funerals is driven by financial considerations, smaller family circles, and a desire to respect the deceased’s wish not to trouble relatives, according to the funeral directors.

Quek said a traditional wake and funeral typically costs more than S$10,000, while a one-day funeral costs just over S$1,000.

"The price difference is quite substantial," he said. "Some families may feel there is no need to hold an overly elaborate ceremony after the deceased has passed on."

Tay noted that some cash-strapped families choose the option to save money.

"Generally, three- to five-day wakes are still more common. Seven-day wakes are relatively rare, and are mostly held only when families need to wait for relatives to fly back from overseas to pay their respects," Tay told Shin Min.

Ang shared that some families opt for a one-day affair and consider it sufficient if the deceased was single, had no children, or had a small social circle.

In other instances, the deceased had explicitly stated before passing that they wanted a quiet departure without troubling their loved ones.

"We have encountered patients who did not want to trouble their families with funeral preparations after they pass away," Ang said.

Beyond finances, Quek noted that choosing a simpler send-off does not mean families are unfilial, pointing out that some may have already spent substantial sums on medical care for their loved one.

He also observed that mindsets are evolving, noting that sea burials and garden burials have also grown in popularity in recent years.

Still important to grieve properly

Despite the growing convenience of one-day funerals, Ang said the length of a funeral is not the most important thing.

Ang also stressed that a funeral is not just for the deceased, as living relatives and friends also need sufficient time to accept the loss and say a proper goodbye.

"In today’s society, everything is about efficiency, but families shouldn’t just focus on the length of the funeral," Ang said. “They should leave enough time to properly remember the deceased.”

How a one-day funeral works

Unlike traditional wakes that typically last between three and five days, a one-day funeral skips the wake setup altogether.

Once notified, the funeral director collects the body, prepares and dresses the deceased, and transports them straight to the crematorium, Shin Min wrote.

Because the timeline is tight, family members have to work around available time slots at the crematorium.

The final farewell ceremony prior to cremation lasts around 20 minutes.
