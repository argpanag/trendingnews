Ashton Kutcher’s Anthropic Investment Pushes Him Toward Billionaire Status  - Newsweek

TheTools · 20.11.2026 11:10 · source · permalink

Senior Reporter

Ashton Kutcher might become a billionaire. You're not being Punk'd – his venture capital firm's $435 million investment in Anthropic is now worth about $7 billion, making him one of the biggest winners from the most anticipated IPO moves of the year. After years as an actor in projects like That 70s Show, No Strings Attached and Two and a Half Men, Kutcher began investing in tech startups.

In 2010, he co-founded the venture capital firm A-Grade with Guy Oseary, his investment partner and longtime manager of stars such as Madonna. The firm had a stacked portfolio that included Spotify, Uber and Airbnb, reportedly growing $30 million into about $250 million. A-Grade's successor was built in 2015 when Kutcher and Oseary announced Sound Ventures.

Bloomberg reported that Sound Ventures owns about 0.5 percent of AI giant Anthropic – which has a proposed $1.4 trillion IPO valuation. The firm is estimated to make a roughly 1,500 percent increase in its investment when Anthropic is expected to go public on November 18.

How Ashton Kutcher's Net Worth Will Be Transformed by Anthropic's IPO

Sound Ventures first invested in Anthropic in 2023 during a meeting between Kutcher, Oseary and CEO Dario Amodei at Oseary's Beverly Hills home. At the time, Anthropic was estimated to be valued at roughly $5 billion.

It has a slew of other major investments in companies like Hugging Face Inc., which NVIDIA just bought for $13 billion, and World Labs, which was bought by Advanced Micro Devices for $8.2 billion.

A recent leak revealed that Sound Ventures was also a major investor in OpenAI. The venture capital firm reportedly holds a 0.15 percent stake in the AI giant. At the time, that stock was worth over $1 billion. Since then, OpenAI stock is worth much more. As of November, the company is valued at $1.25 trillion.

Ashton Kutcher's Relationship With Sam Altman

Bloomberg reports that Sound Ventures has invested more than $400 million in the ChatGPT developer to date and that, since OpenAI and Anthropic are direct competitors, Kutcher got permission from OpenAI CEO Sam Altman before moving forward with the Anthropic investment.

Years before ChatGPT was raking in over one billion global monthly users, Altman founded Loopt, a company that allowed smartphone users to selectively share their locations with other users. Kutcher evaluated the company for a potential angel investment but decided to pass. He did maintain the relationship, which paid off over a decade later after Loopt shut down and Altman moved on to OpenAI.

Why Ashton Kutcher Left Sound Ventures

Kutcher left his role as partner at the firm in July but is still serving as a senior adviser at Sound Ventures. He launched a new firm with VC executive Morgan Beller and even tapped Sound Ventures partner Effie Epstein as an adviser to his new firm.

According to the Wall Street Journal, Kutcher’s departure was partly due to different views on which startup stages to target for investments. Sound Ventures reportedly wanted to back more established companies, rather than betting on early-stage startups.

Where Kutcher Ranks Among Hollywood’s Richest Self-Made Investors

When Anthropic goes public, Kutcher will not instantly receive a $7 billion deposit in his accounts or on a giant check. Sound Ventures manages money for outside investors so those profits would be split among several parties. According to Celebrity Net Worth, Kutcher has a net worth of $550 million. He typically ranks high on the list of celebrity investors.

Irish musician Bono co-founded Elevation Partners in 2004, named after the U2 song Elevation. The firm has $1.9 billion of assets under its management, which were acquired by other companies between 2012 and 2015. Bono himself is currently worth $700 million.

Tennis star Serena Williams' net worth is estimated to be $300 million. She recently revealed her plans to make $200 million for her new firm with business partner Beth Ferreira, Starfire Ventures. Her first investment company, Serena Ventures, was launched in 2014 and has since backed over 100 companies, including some that have reached billion-dollar valuations.

Rapper Nas co-founded QueensBridge Venture Partners in 2014, which has an estimated portfolio and returns of between $140 and $200 million. The firm has backed companies like Coinbase, Ring, Pluto TV, Lyft and Dropbox. Nas has an estimated net worth of $200 million.

Actress and writer Mindy Kaling has become a prominent angel investor, building a portfolio of over 20 companies across health, beauty and consumer goods, including Maven Clinics and Impossible Foods. She even appeared as a guest shark on Shark Tank and has a net worth of $35 million.
