Juventus-Napoli is Sunday's second big match as the 10th round of Serie A 2026/2027. draws to a close. It is already a crucial game for both Luciano Spalletti's Juventus and Massimiliano Allegri's Napoli. Both have 14 points, 9 behind Como, who occupy fourth place and a Champions League spot.

After a thrilling 3-3 draw with Roma and a convincing 4-1 away win over Monza, De Bruyne and his team-mates are improving. Juventus, meanwhile, suffered their second defeat in their last 3 league games in Genoa. Their performance fell well short of what Spalletti expects from his players.

  • Goals and highlights

    58' What a mistake! - Lucumì falls under pressure from Politano and, as he slides, plays a short ball back towards Vicario. The Napoli winger pounces, but Vicario races out to beat him to it at the last moment and takes the ball cleanly.

    45+2' Chance - Meret produces a superb save this time. Alajbegovic takes matters into his own hands just inside the edge of the area and shoots across goal, but the alert Napoli goalkeeper dives to his left to keep it out.

    31' Chance - At the other end, the move follows the same pattern: Kolo Muani reaches the byline and sends a high cut-back towards Alajbegovic. Unchallenged, he shapes to shoot but sends his right-footed effort narrowly over.

    30' Chance - Anguissa wins the ball and sends Politano through. His cross finds Lang in the middle, but Lang fires over with the goal at his mercy.

    28' Off the post - A cross from the right ricochets off Cabal's body and strikes the post to Meret's right. As play continues, Cabal goes down in the area and appeals for a penalty after contact with Olivera.

    16' Chance - Koopmeiners takes the corner, and Lucumì meets it with his knee in the middle of the area, sending the ball narrowly over.

    11' Koopmeiners risks a red card - Trying to win the ball from Anguissa, Koopmeiners catches him first on the calf and then on the ankle with a bad challenge. Sozza doesn't even show a yellow card, while Napoli call for a red (read the refereeing analysis here)

    7' Chance - Napoli's first real chance comes after sustained pressure down the right leads to a cross from Politano. Lucca gets into a good position against Bremer in the middle of the area and heads the ball, but Vicario makes a superb save to turn it behind for a corner and keep the score level.

    0' Kick-off is scheduled for 20:45 at the Allianz Stadium in Turin.
