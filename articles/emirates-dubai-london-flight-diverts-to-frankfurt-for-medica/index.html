Emirates Dubai-London flight diverts to Frankfurt for medical emergency

TheTools · 01.10.2026 19:48 · source · permalink

An Emirates flight from Dubai to London Heathrow was diverted to Frankfurt, in western Germany, on Thursday due to a medical emergency involving a passenger, according to a statement by the Dubai Media Office.

Emirates said the cabin crew provided assistance to the passenger during the flight, while medical support was arranged upon arrival in Frankfurt.

Flight EK31 landed safely at Frankfurt Airport, where the passenger disembarked to receive further medical attention, according to the statement.

The flight was expected to continue its journey to London Heathrow at around 6 pm local time (1400GMT), the media office added.

It comes one day after Flight FZ1073, carrying 174 passengers from Dubai to Tel Aviv, made an emergency landing at Prince Sultan bin Abdulaziz International Airport in the northwestern Saudi Arabian city of Tabuk after an altercation was reported in the cockpit.

The airport said the passengers later departed Tabuk aboard an alternative aircraft provided by Flydubai.
