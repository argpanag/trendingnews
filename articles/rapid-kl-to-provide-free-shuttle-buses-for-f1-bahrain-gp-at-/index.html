Rapid KL to provide free shuttle buses for F1 Bahrain GP at SIC

TheTools · 24.09.2026 17:36 · source · permalink

PUTRAJAYA: Rapid KL, in collaboration with Sepang International Circuit (SIC), will provide free shuttle bus services in conjunction with the Formula 1 (F1) Gulf Air Bahrain Grand Prix (GP) at the SIC from Oct 2-4.

Ninety-five buses will be deployed for the free shuttle service on a loop basis, thereby ensuring convenient and comfortable movement of visitors from key locations to the event venue.

Rapid Bus Sdn Bhd chief executive officer Syahrul Azmir Ahmad Zaki said the special shuttle bus service to SIC would operate from three locations, namely the bus hub at Level 1 of the KL International Airport (KLIA) Terminal 2, the Mitsui KLIA bus hub and De-Village, Persiaran Millenia 2, Bandar Baru Enstek.

"The service will operate from 7am to midnight on all three days, with a frequency of between 10 and 15 minutes. To complement the service, Rapid KL will also provide shuttle buses within the circuit to facilitate visitors' movement to key locations.

"The provision of these services reflects Rapid KL's commitment to supporting the hosting of international-standard events and encouraging the use of public transport," he said in a statement Thursday (Sept 24).

To ensure smooth and safe operations, all buses will undergo safety inspections and be monitored continuously throughout the service period.

Rapid KL personnel will be also stationed at strategic locations around the event venue to assist visitors and manage passenger movements.

He also advised visitors to plan their journeys in advance, follow instructions from personnel and queue when using the shuttle bus service.

Further information regarding the routes and operating schedules of the shuttle bus services can be obtained through Rapid KL's official website and social media channels.

Malaysia will host the F1 Gulf Air Bahrain GP at SIC, marking the first time in F1 history that a race has been relocated to another country following its cancellation.

F1 said in a earlier statement that the event, which is subject to final agreement and official approval, including from the FIA World Motor Sport Council, would be known as Formula 1 Gulf Air Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia. - Bernama
