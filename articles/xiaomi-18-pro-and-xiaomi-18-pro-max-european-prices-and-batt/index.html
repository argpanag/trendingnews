Xiaomi 18 Pro and Xiaomi 18 Pro Max European prices and battery capacities leak

TheTools · 23.10.2026 09:22 · source · permalink

Login

Vlad 19 October 2026

Xiaomi Android Rumors

If you thought the leaked European prices for vivo's X500 Pro and X500 Pro Max, or Oppo's Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max were high, then you probably won't be ready for this. Today a new report claims to bring us the European prices for the Xiaomi 18 Pro and Xiaomi 18 Pro Max, and they are positively eye-watering.

The Xiaomi 18 Pro with 256GB of storage is said to go for €1,449, while the 512GB version will set you back a whopping €1,649. It will allegedly be available in black, Cosmic Blue, Velvet Red, and white.

The Xiaomi 18 Pro Max, on the other hand, will apparently only be sold with 512GB of storage for €1,849. That is right on par with the iPhone 18 Pro Max's recommended retail price in Europe for the same amount of storage, by the way.

The Xiaomi 18 Pro in Europe will have a battery rated at 6,000 mAh, which may end up being advertised as a typical capacity of 6,100 mAh, 6,150 mAh, or 6,200 mAh, down from the 7,000 mAh cell in the Chinese model. The Pro Max in Europe will have a battery rated at 7,050 mAh, which will most likely be advertised as a typical capacity of 7,200 mAh, down from the 8,500 mAh in the Chinese variant.

The Xiaomi 18 Pro in Europe will have support for 90W wired charging, while the Pro Max will go to 100W like both Chinese versions. The 50W wireless charging support will remain unchanged in Europe. Since the Xiaomi 18 Pro and Xiaomi 18 Pro Max have had their European Energy Labels published, their European launch is definitely imminent.

Those labels also reveal that Xiaomi expects the devices' batteries to last for at least 1,000 charging cycles before the capacity drops below 80% of the original value.
