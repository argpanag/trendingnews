It is with great sadness that we announce the death of Naoise Jordan, Ballykeelaun, Parteen, Co. Clare and late of Whitegate. Peacefully in his sleep at home on the 16th September 2026.

Predeceased by his adored wife, Patsy, 6 sisters and 5 brothers.

Deeply loved father of Ruth (Macken), Tony and Raymond, wonderful grandfather of Rachel, Jack, Harry, Paul, Sadhbh, Josh, Paddy, Lia, Naoise and Abbie.

A true gentleman who will be deeply missed by his family, son-in-law Dominic, daughters-in-law Niamh and Sarah, sisters-in-law Sr. Catherine, Helen and Pam. Nieces, nephews, grandnieces, grandnephews, extended family, his GAA family, wide circle of friends, neighbours, and his wonderful carer Fiona.

Former Clare, Whitegate and Parteen hurler.

Former President Clare GAA.

 

May Naoise's gentle soul Rest in Peace.

 

Naoise will repose in Mc Mahon Funeral Home, Blackwater on Saturday 19th September from 4pm to 5:30pm. Arriving for funeral Mass in St. Patrick's Church, Parteen on Sunday 20th September at 2pm followed by burial afterwards in Ballycannon Cemetery, Meelick.
