Tennis | ATP Vienna 2026 | Fery advances after Fearnley retirement, takes on in-form Musetti

TheTools · 28.10.2026 19:57 · source · permalink

By Tony Fairbairn

  • Arthur Fery def Jacob Fearnley [Q] 5-1 RET
  • Fery v Lorenzo Musetti | H2H: Musetti leads 1-0

 

 

H2H & Match Fundamentals

It was an unfortunate ending to the match as Jacob Fearnley retired at 5-1 down to Arthur Fery and now Fery will meet an in-form Lorenzo Musetti in the second round of Vienna.

 

Tale of the Tape – Key Stats (Last 52 Weeks)

Fery will need to be on the top of his game especially on return against an aggressive and in-form Musetti.

 

 

Playing styles and prediction

Musetti has had an injury prone season but has sprung into life at just the right time. The Italian claimed the title in Lyon, defeating two ATP Finals contenders along the way in the form of Taylor Fritz and Flavio Cobolli. Now, Musetti has beaten Frances Tiafoe to reach the second round in Vienna and is hoping to continue that momentum to carry a wave of momentum into next season.

Fery will need to be aggressive and smart on return while using the cross-court slice to open up the court. Meanwhile, Musetti will continue to be aggressive on the forehand as he seeks a high first serve percentage. This should be a big test for Fery and the Brit will need a good start in order to reach the quarter-finals.

Prediction: Fery in three sets.
