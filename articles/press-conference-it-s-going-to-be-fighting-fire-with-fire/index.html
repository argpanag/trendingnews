Press Conference | “It’s going to be fighting fire with fire”

TheTools · 12.10.2026 17:45 · source · permalink

PSV will face RB Leipzig in the second match of the group stage of the UEFA Champions League. Mauro Júnior knows that his team must give it their all tomorrow to defeat the German club.

Mauro Júnior

What are your expectations for tomorrow’s match?
“I think it’s going to be a very tough match. They have a very good team. They’re very good at transitioning on the attack. So we have to prevent that. Of course, they have a lot of strengths, but I’m confident enough. I think we can get a good result here.”

You played as a midfielder on Friday, even though you normally play left back. How difficult is it to switch between those positions?
“In my opinion, it’s never easy to switch from left back to midfield, but in recent years I’ve played both positions. I prefer playing left back. If the coach and the team need me as a midfielder, then I have to step up.”

PSV has many experienced players on its roster. What can the fans expect from the team in Europe this season?
“The goal is to advance in the UEFA Champions League. Tomorrow’s match is an important one for us to earn points.”

Peter Bosz

Have any players returned to the roster since last Friday?
“I don’t think any of the injured players have returned. We were, however, able to make one change to the roster because Bro Hansen is now available.”

Should you warn the players about the atmosphere in the stadium?
“For most players, playing in large stadiums in front of big crowds is nothing new. They’re used to matches in the [UEFA] Champions League, to big games in our own league, and to international matches. We also have a number of very young players on our team, for whom this could be an amazing experience. We can expect a great atmosphere tomorrow. That’s part of the UEFA Champions League.”

Both coaches love attacking football. Will it be fire against fire?
“It’ll be fire with fire. We have our own style of play and our own approach to the game. We’ll stick to that.”

How do you view the team’s playing style?
“I’ve watched several of Leipzig’s matches by now and have gotten a sense of how they play. However, I don’t want to just say that I know the coach and know exactly how he thinks. What I’m seeing now is that Leipzig is playing a little differently than before. They’re trying to build up more from the back and create plays from there. It’s less direct than it used to be.”
