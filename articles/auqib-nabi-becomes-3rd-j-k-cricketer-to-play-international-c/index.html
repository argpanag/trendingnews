Auqib Nabi becomes 3rd J&K cricketer to play international cricket for India

Auqib Nabi becomes 3rd J&amp;K cricketer to play international cricket for Indiashort by Anmol Sharma / 09:29 am on Wednesday, 30 September, 2026Jammu and Kashmir pacer Auqib Nabi made his international debut for India in the second ODI against West Indies in Guwahati on Wednesday. The 29-year-old has become the third male cricketer from J&amp;K to represent India at international level, after Parvez Rasool and Umran Malik.
