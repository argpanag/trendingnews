Co-Op Superstores Premier Senior Hurling Championship Final |

Two of Cork city’s best-known hurling clubs meet at SuperValu Páirc Uí Chaoimh on Sunday, October 18, as Glen Rovers face St Finbarrs in the Co-Op Superstores Premier Senior Hurling Championship Final.

Both clubs have come through demanding knockout ties to reach the decider. Glen Rovers defeated Midleton in the semi-final, while St Finbarrs overcame Kanturk, setting up a meeting between two sides with a proud history in Cork club hurling.

 

 

Road to the Final

Glen Rovers secured their final place with a 2-21 to 1-18 victory over Midleton in the semi-final. They had also defeated Charleville in the quarter-final, continuing a strong run through the knockout stages.

St Finbarrs advanced after a 1-27 to 1-22 win over Kanturk. Earlier in the knockout stages, they recorded a convincing quarter-final victory over Imokilly.

The final brings Glen Rovers and St Finbarrs together at SuperValu Páirc Uí Chaoimh, with the Premier Senior Hurling Championship title and county honours on the line.

Match Details

🏆 Co-Op Superstores Premier Senior Hurling Championship Final
📅 Sunday, October 18
⏰ 4.00pm
