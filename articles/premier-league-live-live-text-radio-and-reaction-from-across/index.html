  3. 'First time we have lacked intensity in a game'published at 20:18 GMT 7 November
    20:18 GMT 7 November

    FT: Nottm Forest 3-1 Man City

    Manchester City

    Manchester City boss Enzo Maresca speaking to BBC MOTD:

    "It's quite clear - first half we didn't match intensity or win duels or second balls. First thing required in these games is intensity. We tried to react at 2-0 but it's too late. We scored one, probably could have scored more but in transition we concede one more."

    Why was intensity low? "I need to analyse that. Today's game is probably the first that we lack intensity in the first 45 minutes. Second half better. Never happened before - we need to understand the reason why and try not to let them happen again.

    "First one is a consequence of the way we want to play. We want to play from the back so this can happen. Second and third goals quite similar - we lost the duel then concede on transition.

    "We are conceding too many goals unfortunately and we need to improve that."

    How to change that: "[We need to] Use the international break to understand why and try to avoid in the future.

    "Always concerned [about defeats] but we need balance - seven wins in a row not happened then three in a row. Need the balance.

    "No panic. We need to understand but no panic."

  6. 'When you lose all duels it's difficult'published at 20:13 GMT 7 November
    20:13 GMT 7 November

    FT: Nottm Forest 3-1 Man City

    Manchester City

    Manchester City boss Enzo Maresca speaking to Sky Sports:

    "First half when you don't match intensity and lose all the duels it's difficult. Then when we want to react, it's too late.

    "In this kind of match, you need to match intensity and win duels, otherwise it's always complicated. First half we were not good enough in terms of duels, second balls - we lost all of them. Then when we went to react we were 2-0 down and in football that is always complicated.

    "The first goal we are trying to play, build. The second one we lost duels in the middle of the pitch then the third one we lost the duel at the edge of their box. We need to match intensity in these kind of games."

    On substitutions at half time: "[Marc] Guehi asked for a change because of injury - he had a problem in the first half. [Josko] Gvardiol also during the week had a small issue. First half we were not good enough, we did not match what this kind of game requires.

    "Second half was better but we don't need to wait to concede one or two and then react. We need to start from the beginning.

    "It's football. We won the first seven in a row and that's never happened and now we've lost three in a row - it's football. We have to try to be better game after game.

    On whether players and manager are still learning about each other: "Absolutely - I didn't know them before and now it's three or four months that we are together. I think they need to know me and I need to know them. It's a learning journey.

    "We know what we do well but we need to understand what we are not doing well and try to improve it."

  7. 'Amazing goals and great team effort'published at 20:11 GMT 7 November
    20:11 GMT 7 November

    FT: Nottm Forest 3-1 Man City

    Nottingham Forest

    Nottingham Forest boss Oliver Glasner, speaking to BBC Match of the Day: "You can never plan to win 3-1 against City because are so good.

    "But today, especially the players and our fans deserve this win because they were so passionate from the first minute, scoring nice goals.

    "We had moments to suffer but then we could rely on our fans pushing us, supporting us. Amazing goals and such a great team effort today so really proud.

    "I think we've had better performances in possession because today we rarely had the ball. Regarding the team spirit, the energy, I think it was our best game.

    "The way we play football I think we've had many better games but how we defended, how passionately we defended, how tactically smart we defended, it was one of our better games."

    On outrunning City: "I think it is the only way you can beat Man City. They're so good, such great players, such a good structure and they move the ball so quickly. They can play through the middle, they have incredible dribblers on the wing and they have such pace. Then, of course, they have Haaland in the middle.

    "You should control everything and if you want to control it, you have to run because you have to be so quickly aware.

    "Of, course you can just defend but you also need to go forward and this is what we did today in the right moments, we scored the goals in the right moments and credit again to the players."

    On Liam Delap: "When you come in with a price tag on your back everybody believes 'if you pay so much, he has to score all the goals'.

    "I would say it's Liam's second regular Premier League season because he didn't play a lot at Chelsea. He had one great season at Ipswich then lost a lot of confidence at Chelsea, so now he's getting better and better into rhythm.

    "These goals are his biggest strengths with his right and his left, also using his power and his strength. We knew that he would score goals for us and today, they were nice ones."

  12. 'Would have been tough for every team to play us today'published at 20:00 GMT 7 November
    20:00 GMT 7 November

    FT: Nottm Forest 3-1 Man City

    Nottingham Forest

    Nottingham Forest boss Oliver Glasner, speaking to Sky Sports: "It was a very good day to play Manchester City but, to be honest, today, the energy in the team and at the City Ground was so incredible that it would have been tough for every team in the world to play against us today.

    "So all the credit to the players and our fans for this amazing Saturday evening."

    On his principles and standards: "It's not about me or my standards, it's about our standards. I could see a team today that was fighting for each other. Every single player left his heart on the pitch and, of course, we could see after the goal early in the second half, the quality and the pressure.

    "When it was tough we had our fans, who backed up and supported us. Also the new players. We knew there would be times to suffer and bring in the new players to continue this hard work.

    "A fantastic night and, I think, a deserved win."

    On outrunning City by 6km: "Today, of course. But if you want to win against City, you need to do this. You could see how good they are, how good the structure is and how quick there are.

    "You can't control everything. If you want to control the pocket, they have the great dribblers on the wing. I think we did very well first half and second half we struggled with their quick wingers.

    "But then we started to control it and credit to Liam [Delap], this strike with his left foot is something you have or you don't have. Today we showed it and helped us get the win over the goal line."

    On Liam Delap's performance: "We knew exactly his strengths when he can use his power and his finish with right and left is just amazing. A lot of things to improve, he's still a young man in, I would say, his second regular Premier League season.

    "Maybe it is his third but he didn't play so often last year. Today no single player deserves to be mentioned because I thought it was such a team effort. We waited for our situations and how passionately we defended our goal in the box, I could mention everyone."

    On importance of the win: "I think everybody felt huge happiness but also relief, to be honest. Today, it was a bit the opposite [of the rest of the season], we were very efficient. The first two shots on target were goals. The players just deserve it today.

    "It can be a turning point but we have to prove it. Many players will go to their national teams, hopefully everyone comes back well and then we go to Bournemouth. The way we performed today should give us confidence."

  15. A rapid downturn for Man Citypublished at 19:49 GMT 7 November
    19:49 GMT 7 November

    FT: Nottm Forest 3-1 Man City

    Manchester City have lost three consecutive Premier League games for the first time since a run of four in November-December 2024.

    They have become the second team in English top-flight history – after Sheffield Wednesday in 1904-05 – to win their first seven games of a season and then lose their next three.

    And this was their first 2+ goal Premier League defeat against a side starting the day in the relegation zone since November 2008 - a 2-0 loss to Bolton Wanderers.

Nottingham Forest boss Oliver Glasner, speaking to Sky Sports: "It was a very good day to play Manchester City but, to be honest, today, the energy in the team and at the City Ground was so incredible that it would have been tough for every team in the world to play against us today.

"So all the credit to the players and our fans for this amazing Saturday evening."

On his principles and standards: "It's not about me or my standards, it's about our standards. I could see a team today that was fighting for each other. Every single player left his heart on the pitch and, of course, we could see after the goal early in the second half, the quality and the pressure.

"When it was tough we had our fans, who backed up and supported us. Also the new players. We knew there would be times to suffer and bring in the new players to continue this hard work.

"A fantastic night and, I think, a deserved win."

On outrunning City by 6km: "Today, of course. But if you want to win against City, you need to do this. You could see how good they are, how good the structure is and how quick there are.

"You can't control everything. If you want to control the pocket, they have the great dribblers on the wing. I think we did very well first half and second half we struggled with their quick wingers.

"But then we started to control it and credit to Liam [Delap], this strike with his left foot is something you have or you don't have. Today we showed it and helped us get the win over the goal line."

On Liam Delap's performance: "We knew exactly his strengths when he can use his power and his finish with right and left is just amazing. A lot of things to improve, he's still a young man in, I would say, his second regular Premier League season.

"Maybe it is his third but he didn't play so often last year. Today no single player deserves to be mentioned because I thought it was such a team effort. We waited for our situations and how passionately we defended our goal in the box, I could mention everyone."

On importance of the win: "I think everybody felt huge happiness but also relief, to be honest. Today, it was a bit the opposite [of the rest of the season], we were very efficient. The first two shots on target were goals. The players just deserve it today.

"It can be a turning point but we have to prove it. Many players will go to their national teams, hopefully everyone comes back well and then we go to Bournemouth. The way we performed today should give us confidence."

FT: Nottm Forest 3-1 Man City

Manchester City have lost three consecutive Premier League games for the first time since a run of four in November-December 2024.

They have become the second team in English top-flight history – after Sheffield Wednesday in 1904-05 – to win their first seven games of a season and then lose their next three.

And this was their first 2+ goal Premier League defeat against a side starting the day in the relegation zone since November 2008 - a 2-0 loss to Bolton Wanderers.
