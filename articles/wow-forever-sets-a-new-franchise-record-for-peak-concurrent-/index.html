WoW: Forever Sets a New Franchise Record for Peak Concurrent Players

TheTools · 10.11.2026 00:03 · source · permalink

Sunday, November 8th 2026 WoW: Forever Sets a New Franchise Record for Peak Concurrent Players World of Warcraft: Forever is now the biggest launch in WoW history by peak concurrent players, according to Blizzard. The official Warcraft account also says the game brought back the highest number of returning players the franchise has ever seen. Forever went live on November 4 for Windows and macOS, and it is included with a regular WoW subscription. Blizzard didn't share any actual numbers, so it's hard to say how big the lead is. Going by the claim, Forever beat every expansion launch, including Wrath of the Lich King, as well as WoW Classic in 2019. However, Blizzard never published peak concurrent figures for those launches either, so a direct comparison isn't possible. The launch itself went far more smoothly than the beta, which had its population caps raised several times. Forever runs on large megaservers that span multiple regions instead of dozens of smaller realms, and many players reported short login queues or none at all. Blizzard's Holly Longdale summed it up on X as "so far so goodish." Developer Josh Greenfield admitted to a few problems, mainly with the boat and zeppelin service, and Blizzard added temporary portals while it prepares a proper fix. On Twitch, the WoW category reportedly peaked at around 1.12 million viewers during launch, per Streams Charts. Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella had already called the Forever beta the largest in franchise history, and it looks like the launch has kept that momentum going. Source: World of Warcraft on X
