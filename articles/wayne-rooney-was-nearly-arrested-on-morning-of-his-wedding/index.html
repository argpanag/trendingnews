Wayne Rooney was 'nearly arrested' on morning of his wedding

TheTools · 22.11.2026 10:14 · source · permalink

Wayne Rooney has revealed that he was nearly arrested on the morning of his wedding to Coleen after a wild night with his friends.

The Manchester United legend has been married to Coleen for over 18 years now, but their marriage nearly got off to a shocking start.

Today's top videos

The couple tied the knot in Italy in 2008, with a lavish ceremony that was reported to have cost nearly €6million.

However, Rooney has now admitted on I’m a Celebrity that he was close to being behind bars in Portofino instead.

The former footballer told fellow contestant Chloe Burrows about the wedding drama, explaining that he had very little involvement in the planning.

Rooney said that he did not even see the wedding venue until the day of the ceremony, though he brought some big musical guests with him.

He said that Westlife performed during the couple’s first dance, while Stereophonics star Kelly Jones sang the following day.

The night before, however, was nearly disastrous for Rooney after Coleen had left to stay on a boat with her bridesmaids.

The Premier League icon explained that she warned him: ‘Don’t get too p****d. You’re getting married in the morning.’

While he promised his bride-to-be that he would keep things in check, Rooney quickly found himself in trouble.

He explained: ‘Seven o’clock in the morning, had the police at the door.

‘A few of the lads ended up in my room and we carried big, massive speakers up. And then a few bottles got thrown over the balcony and the police turned up. So, I nearly got arrested!’

Campmate Ade Adepitan then quipped: ‘Coleen wouldn’t have been too pleased with that’, while Claire Sweeney added: ‘That’s rock and roll.’

Jordan Banjo later joked on Unpacked, the I’m A Celebrity spin-off show, that Rooney’s public relations team must be nervous every time he starts to tell a story.

He laughed: ‘All I’m saying is that I’m sure they’ve paid him a lot of money. But his PR is sat at home biting their fingernails every single time Wayne opens his mouth because he’s such an open book in a good way, which is really nice.’
