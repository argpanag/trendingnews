Estrela Amadora and Braga Put Their Unbeaten Starts on the Line

Estrela Amadora and Braga face off on Thursday, September 10 at Estádio José Gomes in Amadora in a rescheduled Matchday 3 fixture of the 2026-27 Liga Portugal season. Both teams enter the matchup still unbeaten in league play.

The hosts have built their unbeaten start largely through draws, while Braga have managed to turn more of their performances into victories and remain within striking distance of the teams near the top despite having games in hand.

Recent history also suggests a much tighter contest than the difference between the two squads might indicate. The last two meetings between Estrela Amadora and Braga have ended in draws, including a dramatic 2-2 result in May.


How Estrela Amadora Enter the Match

Estrela Amadora remain unbeaten in Liga Portugal after recording one win and three draws, a run that has kept them in the middle of the standings during the opening weeks of the season.

Draws have become a recurring theme for the team. Including the end of last season, Estrela have drawn five of their last six league matches, although they recently earned an important 3-2 away victory over Nacional Madeira.

Leandro Antonetti and Abraham Marcus have emerged as two of their main attacking threats. The biggest challenge will be improving their results at Estádio José Gomes, where wins have been difficult to come by, although Estrela have already shown they can compete against strong opposition at home by earning a draw against Sporting this season.


How Braga Enter the Match

Braga also remain unbeaten and travel to Amadora with two wins and one draw from their opening league fixtures. The visitors have shown a greater ability to dictate matches through possession, regularly controlling more than 55% of the ball.

Their start included a 2-2 away draw against Moreirense, before Braga began putting victories together, including a narrow 1-0 win over Vitória thanks to a late goal from Diego Rodrigues.

Their biggest question mark remains their form away from home. Braga have drawn six of their last seven away league matches, a trend that could give Estrela an opportunity in a matchup between two sides that have proven particularly difficult to beat.


Recent Meetings Point Toward Another Tight Contest

Estrela Amadora and Braga have drawn their last two meetings, with both matches producing goals and late drama.

Their most recent encounter in May finished 2-2 after the score remained 1-1 until the 89th minute. Pau Víctor put Braga ahead from the penalty spot in the 90th minute, only for Stefan Lekovic to rescue Estrela with an equalizer in the 90+6 minute.

The previous meeting ended 3-3 in January 2026, while Braga’s most recent victory in the matchup came in January 2025, when they earned a 1-0 win. Estrela have also recorded five draws in their last 10 head-to-head meetings with Braga, further suggesting another closely contested game.

Final Verdict

Braga enter the match as favorites, both because of the quality of their squad and their ability to control possession and dictate the tempo. Their unbeaten start also confirms that they will present a difficult challenge for Estrela Amadora.

However, Braga’s tendency to draw away from home makes this far from a straightforward assignment. Six draws in their last seven away league matches show that they have struggled to turn control into victories on the road.

Estrela Amadora have also made draws a habit and have already held Braga in their last two meetings. The visitors have enough quality to take all three points, but recent history suggests they may have to work extremely hard to break down the hosts.
