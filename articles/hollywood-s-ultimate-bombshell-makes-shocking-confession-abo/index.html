Hollywood’s ultimate bombshell makes shocking confession about her sex life

TheTools · 28.09.2026 00:28 · source · permalink

One of the world’s sexiest women has opened up about what's really going on between her sheets

She’s long been regarded as one of Hollywood’s most alluring women, famous for portraying smouldering femme fatales and appearing in some of the most provocative scenes on cinema screens. Yet in an unexpected revelation, Sharon Stone has confessed she hasn’t slept with anyone ‘for quite a while’.

The Basic Instinct actress opened up about her romantic life on a recent podcast, explaining that she is single at the moment, with no partner to share either her bed or her day-to-day life.

Sharon, 68, said, ‘I’ve been celibate for quite a while now because I’m at the point where I really only want to be with someone that I care for. And now that my kids are grown, I want someone that’s my companion, not just someone to have sex with. I’m kind of bored with that.’

The actress has been married twice and is the mother of three adopted sons – Roan, 26, Laird, 21, and Quinn, 20. Her first marriage was to TV and film producer Michael Greenburg in 1984, before they divorced in 1990. She later married journalist Phil Bronstein in 1998, but the couple separated in 2004. Since then, Sharon has remained publicly unattached, though she has been linked to various men, including Italian real estate investor and entrepreneur Angelo Boffa, Argentine model Martin Mica, and actor David DeLuise. She also shared that she once dated rapper Nelly.

‘I’m a really good girlfriend, but I haven’t been a girlfriend in a long time,’ she told The Big Bang Theory star Mayim Bialik on her Breakdown podcast. ‘And I’ve been a girlfriend to bad boyfriends. And I’ve been a girlfriend to good boyfriends. I’ve had good luck, and bad luck and no luck at all. And for quite a long time, I’ve had no luck at all.’

Sharon also conceded that, even after two marriages, she has yet to find ‘the one’. ‘I have had loves in my life, but I don’t think I’ve had the great love of my life, no.’

However, she isn’t overly concerned, saying psychics have suggested she’ll meet her soulmate later in life, in her late 60s. Sharon remembered one psychic predicting, ‘You’re not going to meet your person until you’re quite old, quite a bit older. And then they’re going to be like you. In a way, you’re going to identify with them. But it won’t be until you’re really quite a bit older, almost 70.’ Another psychic reportedly offered a similar message as she nears 70.

In recent years, Sharon has said she has stepped away from the dating scene entirely. ‘I’m done dating, I’ve had it with dating,’ she said in 2020. ‘I just find people to be insincere and not worth my time. I enjoy my alone time and time with my kids and my friends more. I don’t want any insincerity and bologna and game playing.’

She went on to explain, ‘I have really good men friends, but I just feel when it comes to emotional maturity in relationships that men and women are seemingly in different spots. They aren’t at the same place right now.’

The star has also spoken about being content on her own, with her three sons as the main men in her world. ‘This is a very good period of my life right now – my life is happy,’ she said. ‘I think somewhere in the back of your mind you think maybe one day you won’t be a single parent. Then, eventually, you realise, I think it’s better. I’m no longer hoping for someone.’

Sharon, who spent years recovering after a brain aneurysm in 2001, later endured a highly public custody fight over her eldest son, Roan, in 2008 with newspaper executive Phil Bronstein, after their relationship deteriorated following a cancer scare. She has said she decided to have a double mastectomy when tumours were discovered in her breasts, a choice he did not support.

Although the tumours were later found to be benign and she did not go through with the operation, she has described his reaction as a turning point that effectively ended their marriage.

‘He thought I was ridiculous. He thought it was foolish. He thought I was making too many decisions myself,’ she said. ‘That was the end of the marriage. That was it. He was done with me then. It was over. It was just over in the room. You could just tell. It was over. It was just over.’

She added, ‘I learned so much from having been so ill and regaining the gratitude for the simple things – for being able to see, for being able to talk, for being able to walk. I was ashamed that it took something so big. I was really ashamed of myself. I lost everything – my marriage, my career, my kids, everything.’

Despite having kissed some of Hollywood’s biggest stars on screen – including Robert De Niro, Leonardo DiCaprio, Michael Douglas and Sylvester Stallone – she said her Casino co-star Robert De Niro still stands out as a favourite.

‘He’s the best kisser in the business. He kissed me right out of my shoes,’ she said. ‘He really just knocked me out, right? And Martin Scorsese cut, and he looks at us, and he goes, “I think we got it, but would you guys like another one?” We both looked at him and went, “Yeah, I think maybe we need one more”.’

Not every co-star relationship was positive, though. She didn’t see eye to eye with William Baldwin, whom she worked alongside in the 1993 erotic thriller Sliver. He later said he would ‘never, ever’ work with her again after tension arose around Sharon’s claim that the late producer Robert Evans urged her to sleep with Baldwin to strengthen their on-screen chemistry. ‘If I could sleep with Billy, then we’d have chemistry on screen and save the movie,’ she said.

The film’s director Phillip Noyce recalled, ‘The two were not at all happy opposite each other. There was such a great deal of animosity on set. Sharon’s open disdain for Billy resulted in him feeling extremely insecure.’
