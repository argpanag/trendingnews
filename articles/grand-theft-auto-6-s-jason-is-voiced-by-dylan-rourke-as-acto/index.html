Grand Theft Auto 6’s Jason is Voiced by Dylan Rourke, as Actor Finally Confirms Role

TheTools · 21.11.2026 01:14 · source · permalink

The man behind Jason in GTA 6 has taken to Instagram to post a heartfelt note of thanks to all of the people who worked hard to make what felt like a distant dream at one point a very enjoyable reality.

Dylan Rourke has appeared in shows like Grey’s Anatomy and Modern Family, alongside several films. We’re certainly behind his take on Jason, as he brings a certain introspective quality to the man that makes him feel like a very human half of a dynamic duo. That message of thanks echoes a sentiment that seems to be going around a lot among the game’s makers, especially after that lovely outpouring of gratitude from the developers once they saw all the reactions the game was receiving.

It’s well-deserved, considering that Leonida’s long-term value is already apparent, and potentially on its way to becoming something else we keep talking about as the months go by. We’ll certainly be a part of that conversation, since we found Grand Theft Auto 6 to be a very fine addition to modern gaming.
