Amanda Holden channels Sharon Stone as she recreates iconic scene

TheTools · 29.10.2026 17:14 · source · permalink

Amanda Holden is gearing up for Halloween, and the Heart FM presenter wowed her followers when she recreated Sharon Stone's infamous Basic Instinct scene

Amanda Holden is always known for her killer fashion looks, so as Halloween approaches, she decided to recreate one of cinema's most infamous fashion moments.

The presenter uploaded a short clip of herself in a white mini-dress, unfolding her legs in a nearly identical dress just as Sharon Stone did in Basic Instinct. After recreating the scene, Amanda showed off the rest of her outfit, including her matching white shoes.

Amanda twirled around the Heart FM offices as she showed off her stunning ensemble in full, with her bleach-blonde hair cascading down behind her. The presenter also flaunted her manicured nails, which had been painted a striking purple.

She also revealed that she had gone for the look as she was due to interview Michael Douglas, who starred in the 1992 film alongside Sharon and George Dzundza.

In her caption, she joked: "Some call it Basic instinct, I call it outfit of the day," and she also revealed that her flawless dress, which boasts long sleeves, came from Odd Muse London.

As ever, her followers were impressed with the display, as one called her a "legend" and a second enthused: "Gorgeous dress. You look stunning," and a third added: "Your excitement shows."

However, a fourth joked about the plummeting temperatures in the UK, as they teased: "All I can say is the heating in the Heart Office must be fantastic. I had my opaque tights on yesterday to stay warm in our office."

Amanda Holden's stunning fashion ensembles

The 55-year-old is never shy when it comes to her fashion looks, she stunned when she shared a series of snaps wearing a butter yellow bikini set complete with a matching linen shirt by Melissa Odabash.

"Apparently Britain has gone with the wind!! I've gone with the sunshine [sunshine emoji]. Love Abu Dhabi. Just me and Chris!" she joked in the caption.

One person who was more than impressed was the designer herself, Melissa Odabash, who commented: "Omggggg [flame emojis] my fav bikini girl looking stunning." A second added: "Stunning [love heart emojis]." Meanwhile, a third penned: "Mega babe!"

Amanda celebrated her 55th birthday in February poolside in Dubai alongside her family and friends. The Britain's Got Talent judge opened up about being in her 50s in an exclusive chat with HELLO! in 2024, telling us, "I just don't think age is a thing anymore."
