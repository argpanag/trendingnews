Kate Middleton Is Against Meghan Markle ‘Eclipsing’ Her Hard Work — Source - AOL

TheTools · 16.11.2026 09:16 · source · permalink

Kate Middleton is reportedly hoping this year’s annual Christmas carol concert remains the focus of the festive season, but a new report claims she is concerned that Prince Harry and Meghan Markle could unintentionally dominate headlines. Sources suggest the Sussexes might share a major announcement or make a charity appearance around the same time. 

Kate Middleton doesn’t want Prince Harry and Meghan Markle stealing holiday spotlight, per source

According to RadarOnline, the Princess of Wales has been preparing for her sixth annual Together at Christmas service at Westminster Abbey. The annual event honors individuals who make a meaningful difference in their communities. 

A royal insider claimed Kate Middleton has invested years into building the televised concert into a meaningful holiday tradition. “She has poured her heart and soul into this Christmas concert,” the source alleged. They added that it has become “one of the most personal and important occasions in her calendar.”

The insider further claimed Kate does not want the event overshadowed by fresh headlines involving Meghan Markle and Prince Harry. “There’s a genuine concern that the Sussexes could suddenly announce a major charity initiative or make a high-profile appearance around the same time,” the source said. According to the insider, Kate worries media attention could quickly shift away from the concert.

The report also claims the Princess of Wales is not opposed to the Sussexes supporting charitable causes. Instead, the source alleged she hopes there is “a degree of courtesy and consideration” regarding the timing of major announcements. Kate reportedly wants the evening to remain focused on recognizing volunteers and community heroes rather than fueling speculation about the royal family’s ongoing tensions.

The Together at Christmas concert has become one of Kate’s signature royal engagements since it debuted in 2021. Each year, it highlights individuals whose compassion and service have positively impacted others while celebrating the spirit of the holiday season.

For now, the reported concerns remain based on unnamed sources. Currently, no evidence indicates that Prince Harry and Meghan Markle plan to schedule competing engagements. Furthermore, the Sussexes have not announced any official plans for the upcoming Christmas period.
