Death mourned of much-loved Donegal priest Fr Dermot McShane - Donegal Daily

TheTools · 14.09.2026 15:13 · source · permalink

The late Fr Dermot McShane

There is deep sadness in south Donegal following the death of Very Reverend Fr Dermot McShane.

A native of Cappagh, Teelin, he passed away at Letterkenny University Hospital on Sunday at the age of 92.

Until his passing, Fr McShane was Pastor Emeritus of the Killaghtee (Bruckless) parish.

He served as the Kilaghtee parish priest for 21 years, from 1991 to 2012.

Fr McShane passed away peacefully following a long illness and he had been in the care recently of both LUH and the Killybegs Community Hospital.

On March 22, 1958, Fr McShane was ordained to the priesthood in the Basilica of St John Lateran in Rome for the Raphoe Diocese.

Previously, he was educated in Teelin and in Summerhill College, Co Sligo and well as St Eunan’s College in Letterkenny before studying for the priesthood at St Patrick’s College, Maynooth and the Pontifical Irish College in Rome.

His first placement was a short spell in Raphoe before he had stints in St Eunan’s College, Drimarone, Colaiste Bride and Ardara Scoil Mhuire, Derrybeg.

He served as a curate in Ardara (1966-69), Lettermacaward (1969-75) and Fintown (1975-83).

He was then appointed as the parish priest of Kilcar, a role he served in until being transferred to Kilaghtee in 1991.

Predeceased by his parents Dorby and Ellen, his brothers Pádraig, Tomás and Conall, Fr McShane will be sadly missed by his cousins, by Bishop Niall Coll, Bishop Philip Boyce, his fellow priests, his former housekeeper Bríd McCallig, his parishioners and a large circle of friends.

Fr McShane’s remains will be received at the Church of St Joseph and St Conal, Bruckless at 5pm on Tuesday, September 15. 

He will repose until Rosary at 8pm and Requiem Mass will be celebrated at the Church of St Joseph and St Conal, Bruckless at 12 noon on Wednesday, September 16 with removal afterwards for interment in the grounds of St. Columba’s Church, Carrick.
