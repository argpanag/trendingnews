Three taken to hospital for smoke inhalation after kitchen fire in Jalan Bukit Merah flat

TheTools · 20.11.2026 11:08 · source · permalink

AI-translated article with transliterated names. Read original Chinese article here.

A fire broke out in the kitchen of an HDB flat in Jalan Bukit Merah on Thursday night but died out before civil defence personnel arrived. Three people were later taken to hospital for smoke inhalation.

The incident occurred at about 8.15pm on Thursday (19 November) in the kitchen of a third-floor flat at Block 105 Jalan Bukit Merah.

In response to queries from Lianhe Zaobao, the Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF) confirmed that the fire involved the contents of two cooking pots in the kitchen and had burned itself out before its personnel arrived.

Three residents of the flat were taken to Singapore General Hospital for smoke inhalation. As a precaution, the police and SCDF also evacuated about 30 people from the affected block.

Preliminary investigations indicate that the fire was likely caused by unattended cooking.

Emergency vehicles at foot of block draw onlookers and livestreamers

Footage livestreamed on TikTok showed at least two fire engines and a police car parked at the foot of the block. The incident drew a crowd of onlookers, while others believed to be affected residents gathered in a safe area downstairs to wait.

The kitchen where the fire broke out was not visible in the livestream because of the camera angle. About an hour after the incident, the fire engines began leaving the scene, and the livestream ended soon after.

SCDF reminded residents never to leave cooking unattended and to keep stoves, cooker hoods and ovens clean to prevent grease from building up. Residents should also avoid overloading electrical sockets or leaving appliances to charge overnight unattended. Incense sticks and cigarette butts must be fully extinguished before disposal.

As previously reported, a fatal fire broke out in August last year at neighbouring Block 106, killing a couple in their 30s. The pair had reached out of a window to call for help, but the flames were too intense. They suffered severe burns before being rescued and later died of their injuries. The fire was believed to have been caused by the battery pack of a personal mobility device (PMD).
