Breel Embolo out of Switzerland's Nations League matches for purchasing forged COVID certificates in 2021

TheTools · 25.09.2026 16:58 · source · permalink
  • Atlanta United
  • International Duty

Breel Embolo was originally set to join Switzerland for its UEFA Nations League matches after Atlanta United’s contest on Saturday against New York City FC.

That was, however, until the Swiss Football Association announced on Friday that it had removed Embolo from the squad. According to Swiss outlet 20 Minuten, he confessed to purchasing forged COVID-19 travel certificates from a member of the Hell’s Angels motorcycle club in 2021. He was ordered in 2025 to pay fines but now will face further punishment, being held out of matches against Scotland, Slovenia, and North Macedonia.

“Breel Embolo has informed the Swiss Football Association (SFV) about the matter concerning his private life that became public in the media today,” the SFV said in a statement. “As a result, the association’s management has decided that the player will not join the team as planned and that the national team will complete its current training camp without him.”

Embolo won’t be the only one out of the squad: captain Granit Xhaka was involved in a similar situation in 2022 and confessed to his decision as well.

After this window, Switzerland’s next Nations League action is scheduled for November 13 (Slovenia; away) and 16 (Scotland; home).
