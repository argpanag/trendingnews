‘Big Brother UK’ 2026 Season 23 Cast Photos: Meet All The Housemates From ITV2 Competition — Live Updates

TheTools · 13.09.2026 20:00 · source · permalink

AJ Odudu and Will Best are back as co-hosts of Big Brother UK 2026 (Season 23) on ITV2 and ITVX.

The live reality competition returned to the UK with a new set of housemates ready to take on the challenge for a chance to win a cash prize.

This season has already teased surprises that will keep the housemates on their toes as unexpected twists take over the game.

The first twist of the season includes a housemate who’s not really a housemate but a mole set to cause chaos in the house. Entering the house for a week is Bov Boys member Tays (Michael Taylor).

RELATED: ‘Big Brother UK’ 2026 House Photos For Season 23 Of ITV2 Reality Competition: Diary Room Chair, Bedroom, Kitchen & More

The Big Brother UK 2026 cast includes: Yasmin (29, Manchester), Harry (30, Croydon), Chelsey (29, Manchester), Philip (53, Liverpool), Lynx (24, London), Charlotte (18, Oxford), Pauline (31, Essex), Rochelle (57, Essex), Millie (19, Paisley), Frankie (23, East London), Samuel (21, Slough), Rob (25, Bideford), Rhianna (21, Chelsea), Omar (21, North West London), and Johanne (44, Los Angeles).

Across the extended run, viewers can expect ambitious tasks, high-stakes nominations and dramatic live evictions as the Housemates face challenges at every turn in their bid to outlast their rivals. Who will be crowned this year’s winner and take home a life-changing cash prize? Who wins? You decide.

Accompanying the main series this season is Big Brother: The Group Chat, a companion show hosted by Kate Lawler and GK Barry. The show will feature exclusive interviews with evicted Housemates, celebrity fans and special guests, alongside strong opinions, plenty of personality and the inside scoop from those who know the house best.

RELATED: ‘The Celebrity Traitors’ Season 2 Cast Photos: ‘Love Island’s Maya Jama, Bella Ramsey, Myha’la & More Join BBC Competition

Big Brother: The Group Chat will air Sunday through Thursday nights straight after Big Brother UK, giving fans even more action and bringing them closer to the personalities, dynamics and relationships at the heart of the series. The show will air on ITV2, ITVX and YouTube.

The Big Brother brand has received over 157 million streams on ITVX. Big Brother is produced by Initial (part of Banijay UK) for ITV2 and ITVX.
