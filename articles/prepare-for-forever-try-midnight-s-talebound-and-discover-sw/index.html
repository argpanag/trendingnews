Satisfy Your Sweet Tooth in November’s Trading Post

Bring your appetite for adventure—this month is all about indulging your sweet side! November’s Trading Post is serving up a sugar rush with the Sweet Tooth theme, ready to add a little sweetness to your collection. Complete activities to earn Trader’s Tender, and fill your Traveler’s Log to claim this month's bonus reward: the Hot Motocross Bunmover 4600 mount.

"When dessert manufacturing meets speed optimization. Intact bun delivery not guaranteed."

Visit T&W (Tawney and Wilder) just outside the Mage District in Stormwind, or the Zen’shiri Trading Post next to Grommash Hold in Orgrimmar, to check out the latest items they have available. Players can also visit the Trading Post in the capital cities of Dornogal (The Foregrounds) on the Isle of Dorn and in the renewed Silvermoon City, where representatives of both Tawney and Wilder and the Zen’shiri Trading Post are open for business.

Champions new to or returning to World of Warcraft and Midnight can tour the Trading Post to learn everything you need to know, from where to find it to how you can earn additional Trader's Tender through the Traveler's Log.
