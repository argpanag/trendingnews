Celebrity Traitors fans share warning about one star minutes into show

TheTools · 01.10.2026 19:49 · source · permalink

Brits are once again glued to their TV screens as the second series of the Celebrity Traitors has begun. With 21 celebrities fighting it out to win up to £100,000 for their chosen charities, the games have officially begun.

Amongst the new cast of detectives is Michael Sheen, Maya Jama, James Acaster, Sharon Rooney and Richard E Grant. However, only minutes into the show fans were already issuing a warning about one celebrity.

Starting off the series, all of the contestants arrived at an eerie Scottish church one by one. Eyeing each other up, one celebrity stood out amongst the rest.

Coronation Street star Julie Hesmondhalgh was seen to jump up and greet a variety of the celebrities warmly with a handshake. She also took the opportunity to speak to American actress Myha’la sharing that she loved her in Industry.

However, Traitors fans have been quick to warn that this kind of behaviour may be her undoing. One Reddit user wrote: "Julie shaking everyone's hand will get her killed/banished."

While another noted: "Julie’s kindness will be mistaken for treachery at the roundtable."

However, some fans noted that being a traitor could work in her favour. One fan wrote: "I need Julie to be a traitor just because she’s too nice for anyone to think she’s one."

Meanwhile, other fans admitted to falling in love with her wholesome attitude. One fan said: "Obsessed with Julie fangirling." As another person added: "Julie fangirling over Myha'la has me rolling."

This was followed by another viewer adding: "Lmao Hayley off Corrie just going around telling everyone how much she loves them."

Julie's positive attitude has already been noticed by the rest of the cast as they nominated her as the most trustworthy player without knowing what the consequences would be.

Thankfully for the actress she was awarded by being given the choice to give out two shields to protect against the first murder.

Choosing to protect herself from leaving the castle, she gave the other to comedian Joe Lycett as he was the only player to warn that voting Julie could end up with her being placed in a coffin.

Speaking ahead of the series, Julie shared that she is excited to take part in the mind games. She said: "I am a fair player but I'm incredibly competitive. I commit so strongly, I'm a maniac, so everyone's a bit worried about me in the Missions!

"I absolutely play to win. I don't mind losing. I'm not a sore loser but I give it my all. So much so I think people are genuinely quite frightened of me sometimes!"

Celebrity Traitors airs every Thursday and Friday at 8pm on BBC One and BBC iPlayer.
