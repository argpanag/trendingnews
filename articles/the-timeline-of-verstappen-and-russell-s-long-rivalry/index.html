The timeline of Verstappen and Russell’s long rivalry

TheTools · 24.11.2026 05:18 · source · permalink

F1.com charts how tensions first flared – and have since been reignited – between Max Verstappen and George Russell.

The Las Vegas Grand Prix proved to be an unforgettable, action-packed race – but there was also drama unfolding off the track, with an incident between Max Verstappen and George Russell in third practice leading to tensions once again flaring between the pair. F1.com charts the story of the rivalry so far, which was first ignited more than three years ago…

April 2023 – Tensions sparked by Sprint fight

Back in 2023, Verstappen was challenged by a fast-starting Russell for P3 as the Sprint at the Azerbaijan Grand Prix got underway. The pair went wheel-to-wheel through several corners and made contact along the way, before Verstappen later reclaimed third place.

The Dutch driver was seen surveying the damage to his car in parc ferme after the event – with a sizeable hole visible on the side of the Red Bull – and then shared a frosty exchange with Russell in front of the cameras.

Speaking to the media afterwards, Verstappen said that the situation was “not clarified” after their discussion, adding: “I just don’t understand why you need to take so much risk on Lap 1. Understeering into my sidepod, created a hole…

“We all have cold tyres, it’s all easy to lock up, but in his beautiful way of explaining, ‘Oh mate, locked up, look at the onboard’, it doesn’t make sense. It’s fine, we still got into P3, got some good points, but it is what it is.”

Russell said that he was taken aback when Verstappen confronted him post-Sprint, commenting: “I was quite surprised, I thought he was coming over to say ‘good battle’, to be honest.

“I’m here to fight, I’m here to win. I’m not just going to wave him by because he’s Max Verstappen in a Red Bull. [There’s] nothing really more to say. I thought the move was on and that’s about it, really.”

November 2024 – Verstappen hit with penalty after Russell incident

Over one year on, trouble brewed again at the penultimate round of the 2024 season in Qatar.

Verstappen and Russell were summoned to the stewards on Saturday night following an incident between them in Qualifying, with Verstappen being investigated for driving unnecessarily slowly on a cooldown lap which Russell felt he had been impeded by.

Following the stewards’ hearing, it was confirmed that Verstappen – who had originally claimed pole position ahead of Russell – had been handed a one-place grid penalty.

In a document released afterwards, the stewards noted that Russell had stated that "if a car was going slow in a high-speed corner, it should not be on the racing line", but they concluded that the case was "a complicated one" and did not warrant the usual three-place grid penalty as neither driver was on a push lap.

December 2024 – War of words escalates as Verstappen ‘loses respect’ for Russell

That grid drop did not deter Verstappen, who went on to win the race while Russell crossed the line in fourth. In the post-race press conference, the Red Bull driver admitted that he “couldn’t believe” that he was given the penalty.

He then shared his thoughts on the role that Russell had played in the process and stated that he had “lost all respect” for the Mercedes racer.

“I was quite surprised when sitting there in the stewards’ room [about] what was all going on,” said Verstappen.

“Honestly, [it was] very disappointing because I think we're all here, we respect each other a lot and of course, I've been in that meeting room many times in my life, in my career, with people that I’ve raced and I've never seen someone trying to screw someone over that hard. And that for me… I lost all respect.”

December 2024 – Russell fires back at Verstappen for ‘crossing a line’

A few days later, the paddock assembled in Abu Dhabi for the season finale – where Russell was quizzed for the first time on Verstappen’s remarks. The Briton spoke at length about the topic, firstly stating that what happened on the track was “not personal” to him.

“It’s part of racing, but the words he said [are] totally unnecessary,” said Russell. “He’s crossed a line and I’m not going to accept it, so somebody has to stand up to someone who thinks he’s above the law.”

On the incident, he added: “It wasn’t crazy dangerous, and was the penalty harsh? Maybe it was a little bit harsh, but these are the rules. We all have the rules and we all have to follow it.”

Russell went on to comment: “I’ve known Max for 12 years, I’ve respected him all of this time, but now I’ve lost respect for him because we’re all fighting on track and it’s never personal. Now he’s made it personal, and someone needs to stand up to a bully like this. So far, people let him get away with murder.”

The British driver also voiced his belief that Verstappen “lashes out with unnecessary anger and borderline violence” when things are not going his way, and stated that he wished the Dutchman would “be a better role model”.

June 2025 – Dramatic collision reignites tensions

A few months on, F1 went racing in Barcelona for Round 9 of the 2025 championship. When the race was restarted in the latter stages following a Safety Car phase, Verstappen found himself under pressure from Russell in a battle for fourth place.

There was contact between the pair and Verstappen was forced to go down the escape road, with Red Bull telling their driver to hand the position back. Another collision then occurred, leading Russell to comment over the radio: “He just crashed into me.”

Verstappen was subsequently handed a 10-second time penalty for the incident, dropping him down to a final result of P10 while Russell held onto P4.

Giving his perspective on the clash afterwards, Russell said: “I don’t really know why or what the thinking was behind it. In the end, I’m glad that I continued with little damage and ultimately it punished him a lot more than me.

“That’s how Max goes racing. As I said, he was in P4, I was in P5 – I ended up P4, he ended up P10, so from my side I’m glad that I managed to finish the race. Not really sure what he was thinking because he cost himself and his team a lot of points, so no conversation [is] really required.”

Verstappen kept his take on the collision brief, commenting: “I don’t need to say anything about it because it doesn’t matter anyway.”

June 2025 – Russell surprised by Verstappen’s later comments

One day on from the collision, Verstappen wrote in a social media post that the move he made on Russell “was not right and shouldn’t have happened”, adding that “our tyre choice at the end and some moves after the [Safety Car] restart fuelled my frustration”.

“I always give everything out there for the team and emotions can run high,” the World Champion continued. “You win some together, you lose some together.”

Ahead of the next race on the calendar in Canada, Russell argued that Verstappen was "just trying to get his elbows out and show who's boss" rather than committing a deliberate or premeditated act, before insisting that there were no issues between them.

“Obviously it would have been a different feeling had it taken me out of the race but ultimately I benefitted from it and he was penalised,” Russell explained.

“I was a bit surprised to see he had taken responsibility, so fine. But I haven't spoken to him about it. We actually bumped into each other at the airport the other day but I actually completely forgot we crashed into each other a few days prior. No issues.”

June 2025 – Wolff reacts as Verstappen to Mercedes speculation emerges

After a mixed start to the season for Red Bull, rumours began to emerge during the summer linking Verstappen with a move to Mercedes – given that the Silver Arrows had yet to confirm their 2026 line-up.

Russell admitted that it was “only normal that conversations with the likes of Verstappen are ongoing” but insisted that he continued to feel positive about his own future at the squad, telling Sky Sports F1: “From my side, if I'm performing as I'm doing, what have I got to be concerned about? There are two seats in every Formula 1 team.”

Reflecting on the speculation, Mercedes Team Principal Toto Wolff acknowledged that there were “some conversations you just simply need to have” whilst reiterating that his intention was to continue with Russell and Kimi Antonelli.

Wolff was also quizzed on whether Verstappen and Russell could ever race together, given the previous tensions between the two.

“I can imagine every line-up,” he responded. “I had [Nico] Rosberg and [Lewis] Hamilton fighting for a World Championship, so everything else afterwards is easy. There's pros and cons of having two drivers fighting each other hard. We've seen examples where that functioned and other examples where it didn't.”

July 2025 – Verstappen confirms future with Red Bull

Ahead of F1’s traditional summer break, Verstappen was asked whether his and Wolff's yachts might be located near to each other during the holidays – amid the continued speculation about Mercedes talks – but confirmed that he would be staying with Red Bull in 2026.

“I'm discussing with the team already the plans, the things that we want to change next year so that means I'm also staying with the team for next year,” he answered. “And if my boat is next to Toto's then the boat is next to Toto's.

"You can have a personal relationship with someone even if you don't have a working relationship with someone."

He also added to the media: “I think it's time to basically stop all the rumours, and for me it's always been quite clear that I was staying anyway."

Russell and Antonelli, meanwhile, were confirmed as Mercedes’ 2026 line-up a few months later in October – meaning that the potentially fascinating scenario of Russell and Verstappen becoming team mates would not be on the cards in the near future.

November 2026 – Russell criticises Verstappen over practice incident

Fast-forward to the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix, and Russell and Verstappen were summoned to the stewards once again following an incident between them in the third and final practice session on Friday.

Verstappen braked earlier than expected ahead of Russell and changed direction under braking before the pair made contact as they went wheel-to-wheel into Turn 14, with Verstappen subsequently receiving a warning for driving erratically.

Russell was critical of the Red Bull driver when speaking to Sky Italia after Qualifying – ahead of the pair lining up alongside each other on the front row for Saturday’s race, which each promoted one position following a penalty for the pace-setting Antonelli.

“I think the warning was fine,” Russell commented. “There's nothing in the rules to say that you cannot overtake someone who is starting their lap but when you do this, you are screwing this person over.

“He does this week in, week out, with many drivers. Everybody accepts it and I am not just going to accept somebody to screw me over like this and walk around like they are bigger and better than everyone else. Somebody has got to stand up to that guy."

November 2026 – Verstappen claims Russell is ‘so far below my level’

On the night of the race in Las Vegas, Verstappen and Russell spent much of a breathless Grand Prix exchanging places at the front of the pack, with Russell making a number of overtakes only for the Dutchman to retake P1 moments later.

Antonelli had also joined the battle by the latter stages, moving up into second ahead of Russell. The latter then tried to get back in front of his team mate but the move backfired, the Briton having locked up before hitting the barriers.

While he was able to continue, this dropped him down to P9 – and it was a sequence of events that did not go unnoticed by race winner Verstappen, who gave his reaction to what happened as well as addressing Russell’s comments from one day earlier during the post-race press conference.

“The thing is, he speaks a lot next to the track, and honestly for me it doesn’t really bother me because he’s so far below my level that it doesn’t really make any sense for me anyway to try and challenge that, so it doesn’t bother me,” said Verstappen.

“And today, karma is a b****. It was honestly fantastic when I looked in my mirror and I saw what was happening, so I was like, ‘Fantastic – my day is only getting better’. Of course when I then crossed the line in first, my day was complete.”

November 2026 – Russell defends his move

Russell later defended his attempted overtake in a social media post shared a few hours after the Grand Prix.

“I wanted that win today,” the Mercedes driver explained. “It was a bizarre race and I went for a high risk move and paid the price.

“Of course I’m disappointed with the outcome, however, I have no regrets. I fought and knew if I wanted the win, I needed to be in the top two positions into the final laps.”

With only a few days remaining until the action resumes again at the Qatar Grand Prix, the ongoing dispute is likely to be a hot topic during Thursday’s media day…
