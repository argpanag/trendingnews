India head coach Naushad Moosa confirmed that his players would shake the Pakistan players’ hands ahead of their SAFF Championship 2026 opener at the National Stadium in Dhaka on Thursday.

The clarifications come after players of both nations have failed to exchange pleasantries during cricket matches over the past year and a half, caused by heightened political tensions between the two countries following the Pahalgam terror attack in 2025, in which 26 people were killed, and the subsequent Operation Sindoor.

But Moosa and defender Nathan Rodrigues assured that the players would take part in the pre-game greetings, with the coach further adding that “If needed, we would love to go and hug them.”

The Blue Tigers, who are also the defending champions, are aiming for a record-extending 10th SAFF Championship title.

This will be the 30th meeting between India and Pakistan, with the former holding the advantage with 16 wins, while Pakistan has won four times. Nine games have ended in draws.
