Glenn Phillips’ 107 lifts New Zealand to an 87-run win over India in 4th T20

TheTools · 01.11.2026 09:06 · source · permalink

AUCKLAND, New Zealand (AP) — Glenn Phillips made 107 from 49 balls and Michael Bracewell a half-century from 19 deliveries as New Zealand turned the tables on India to win the fourth Twenty20 by 87 runs on Friday.
