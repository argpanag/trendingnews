Keisha Lance Bottoms’ priorities may be limited by lower budget reserves

TheTools · 06.11.2026 22:56 · source · permalink

Victories in the governor’s race and the General Assembly will give new life to longtime Democratic priorities come January.

But Gov.-elect Keisha Lance Bottoms and Democratic lawmakers will face a substantially different fiscal environment than the one enjoyed by their Republican predecessors, a new state report shows.

Georgia’s budget reserves shrank from about $16.5 billion two years ago to $8.7 billion as of the end of the 2026 fiscal year in June. That’s a decrease of nearly 50%.

The report shows lawmakers spent a whopping $5.9 billion of surplus funds over the last year alone on income tax rebates and other expenditures.
