“I Would Kiss Annie For The Rest Of My Life. Every Day. If You Need Me, I’m There. Sorry Josh.” —Dakota Johnson On Her Verity Co-Stars

TheTools · 21.09.2026 06:42 · source · permalink

By Nicole Stawiarski

Updated 12 hours ago, September 21, 2026

Dakota Johnson made her Anne Hathaway crush the centerpiece of a joint Verity interview, brushing off co-star Josh Hartnett in the process. The three star in the sexy thriller, which has them kissing their way through an intense, twisted plot.

When the interviewer called the shoot “a kissathon,” Hathaway agreed: “It’s a miracle nobody got sick.”

Hathaway then turned the praise on Hartnett: “You have every woman in the world lusting after you. I’m going to say nice things about you if you don’t quit it.”

Hartnett said the mood on set was lighter than the film suggests. “We had a fun working relationship the whole time on set,” he said. “For a film that is this intense and twisted and dark, it was just like, we just laughed. We had a really fun time. So as much as there was like the love and the tension and the lustiness in the movie, in the outside, we were just goofing around.”

Verity opens in theaters Oct. 2. Johnson hosts Saturday Night Live the following night, Oct. 3, with Turnstile as musical guest.
