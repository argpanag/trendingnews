Couple witnesses Donnie Wahlberg plane skid in Las Vegas - AOL

TheTools · 20.10.2026 04:08 · source · permalink

LAS VEGAS (KLAS) -- A couple visiting Las Vegas from Atlanta, Georgia witnessed Sunday's plane skid at Harry Reid International Airport. They later learned that the plane was carrying singer and actor Donnie Wahlberg. 

Donnie Wahlberg says he was on plane that skidded off runway, hit fence at Las Vegas airport

Jeff and Jacqui Jackson were touring a timeshare condominium around 10:50 a.m. on Sunday near Tropicana Avenue and Paradise Road when they heard what sounded like gunshots. 

"We heard the gunshot sounds, and I tried to hit the deck and start praying. I didn't know what was going on," Jacqui said. 

It was the couple's first visit to Las Vegas, and they were in town celebrating Jacqui's birthday. 

"They were still going pretty fast when they reached the end of the runway," her husband Jeff Jackson said of the plane. "When they made that turn, I was really surprised at how quickly the airplane was able to turn without turning up on its side."

The Jacksons, coincidentally enough, were in Las Vegas to see Wahlberg and the rest of the New Kids On The Block perform Saturday night at their residency at Park MGM. 

"The night before I had my sign up and [Wahlberg] pointed at me," Jacqui Jackson said. "I got the New Kids On The Block point for my birthday!"

The plane narrowly missed several gates and construction equipment.

Wahlberg later took to Instagram on Sunday to share a video explaining what happened and thanking the pilots for saving the lives of those on board the plane.

“We were taking off and didn’t get up in the air and ran out of runway,” Wahlberg said in the video. “I’m just going to tell you these pilots saved our lives. And God covered us.”

The Federal Aviation Administration told People.com that the plane had eight people on board “when the pilot aborted the takeoff and went off the runway.” There weren’t any injuries. 

8 News Now obtained audio from the Harry Reid International Airport traffic control tower when the incident occurred. 

"Aborting! Aborting!" the pilot of the Dassault Falcon 50 was heard saying. "We got the emergency vehicles on the way now," an air traffic controller said in response. 

The National Transportation Safety Board is investigating the crash.
