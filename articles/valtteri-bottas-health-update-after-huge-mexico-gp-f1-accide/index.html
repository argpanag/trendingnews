Valtteri Bottas health update after huge Mexico GP F1 accident

TheTools · 01.11.2026 22:07 · source · permalink

Valtteri Bottas suffered a high-speed suspension failure and huge impact in the Mexico City Grand Prix.

Valtteri Bottas has been released from the medical centre and is "okay" following a huge accident in the Mexico City Grand Prix. 

On his 43rd lap, as the Finn applied the brakes for Turn 1 at maximum speed, he suffered a front-left suspension failure, which meant he lost control of the Cadillac.

Fortunately, the car went into the TecPro barriers rear first and not broadside or head-first, which minimised some of the impact for the 10-time grand prix winner. 

Medical personnel and an ambulance were deployed, although Bottas was able to climb from the car himself before going off for a medical check. 

After this, the team provided a medical update on his condition. 

"Following a precautionary medical assessment, Valtteri Bottas has been released and is okay," read a team statement. 

"He has been excused from all post-race media duties, including the TV pen and written media pen."

Cadillac also pledged to "investigate the on-track incident fully" after Bottas's failure.
