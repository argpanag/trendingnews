    Williams/Williams v Chan/Joint

    For the first time in four years, Serena and Venus Williams are set to play doubles together at a Grand Slam.

    Not since the US Open in 2022 have the Americans competed together at a Slam, which was Serena's final tournament before she "evolved away" from tennis.

    After Serena returned to action in June, the pair played at last month's Cincinnati Open before being given a wildcard at Flushing Meadows.

    They will face Hao-ching Chan and Maya Joint in the first round.

  4. 'I am physically better than I thought'published at 21:57 BST
    21:57 BST

    Defending champion Carlos Alcaraz, speaking in the on-court interview: "I think today was a really high rhythm. The way he plays is unbelievable. I stayed there, I played aggressive and I played smart in the toughest moment, which I am really happy about. Could it be better? Yes, but overall I am really proud and happy with the performance today."

    How are you feeling on your comeback? "I am better than I thought, physically I thought I was going to be bad after the first match but I am recovering really well and I am doing great things off the court. I think my level is pretty good. Every time I step on the court I give it my all and I am just trying to be better and improve after every match. Hopefully the next round I will be just a bit better than today."

    What do you need to improve for the next round? "I think a couple of mistakes I did today I hopefully won't make in the next round. He will be playing at home which is a little bit of an advantage for him. He will be a little tired, so let's see.

    "Hopefully the next round I will be better than ever."
