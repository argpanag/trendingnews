SANFL U17 Futures give a glimpse on Grand Final day - Norwood Football Club

TheTools · 20.09.2026 05:53 · source · permalink

By ZAC MILBANK

SANFL’s AFL Draft class of 2027 will have an opportunity to shine in the U17 Futures match to be played at Adelaide Oval on Hostplus SANFL League Grand Final day.

Starting at 9am, the traditional clash between the Yellow and Blue team will be the curtain-raiser for the SANFL Reserves Grand Final starting at 11am.

Ones to watch include highly-rated Sturt pair Harvey Chapman and Taj Garrett while West Adelaide’s Laurence Andriani will also have his chance to display his supreme ball-winning ability after winning the AAMI Talent League MVP and McCallum Tomkins Medal.

Strongly-built Norwood midfielder Kai Ivins and Glenelg spearhead Josh Jarrad are others set to catch the eye, while Redleg Will Sheedy will line-up for the Blue Team before his father Jade coaches the Redlegs in the Hostplus SANFL League Grand Final at 2.30pm.

Other familiar surnames include West’s Percy Burgoyne, the son of Port and Hawthorn great Shaun Burgoyne, Levi Dudley – brother of Fremantle’s Isaiah – and Harry Schubert – brother of Hawthorn’s Aidan Schubert.

AAMI SA U16 MVP Logan Treverton headlines the bottom-agers provided with an opportunity, together with fellow U16 All-Australian Jacob Westley, and Glenelg pair Jack Gallagher – son of former Crow James – and Ryder Lindner.

Final line-ups for the Yellow and Blue teams are listed in the document below with the Blue team to be coached by South Adelaide’s Xavier Wagner while the Yellow side will be guided by Sturt’s Scott Prebble.
