Millie Bobby Brown Is Now Reportedly a Mom of Two

TheTools · 17.09.2026 18:24 · source · permalink

And baby makes four!

Congratulations seem to be in order for Millie Bobby Brown and her husband Jake Bongiovi, who have reportedly welcomed their second baby through adoption. People broke the news on Thursday with a report citing an “exclusive source.”

The new baby joins Brown and Bongiovi’s young daughter, whom the couple adopted last year, as well as the 10 dogs and many other animals that they keep on their farm in Georgia. The new baby’s sex and name have not been confirmed; Brown and Bongiovi have also kept many details about their first child private.

Speaking about how their dog Winnie behaves with their daughter, Brown, 22, previously told Vogue: “She does a really good job of being a big sister. She’s really patient.”

Brown has spoken about the joy of becoming a mother and her wish for a big family.

“It’s been a beautiful, amazing journey,” she told British Vogue last year. “She’s taught us so much already.” Brown added: “The smaller things in life are so much more precious. Our days are filled with lots of cuddles and laughter and love. It’s just endless joy.”

The actor also shared insight into how she and Bongiovi parent. “We are 50-50 on everything. That’s why I’m so grateful to have partnered with him in this life—he is just the most amazing dad.”
