Warrington residents can now recycle a new item in their blue bins

TheTools · 17.09.2026 07:22 · source · permalink

WARRINGTON residents can now put a new item in their blue bins.

This week, Warrington Borough Council has confirmed that toothpaste tubes can now be recycled.

“Instead of throwing them away, simply empty the tube and place it in your recycling. Together we could stop millions of toothpaste tubes going to waste each year,” a social media post by the council reads.

The following items can be put in your blue bin:

  • Aerosols (empty)
  • Cardboard
  • Envelopes (with windows) and junk mail
  • Glass bottles and jars
  • Plastic bottles
  • drinks bottles, household cleaner bottles, shampoo bottles etc
  • Plastic food trays/tubs/pots
  • eg. margarine tubs, yoghurt pots
  • Newspapers and magazines
  • Tetra pak drink cartons
  • Food and drink tins and cans
  • Kitchen foil, and clean foil trays
  • Yellow Pages and phone directories
  • Shredded paper (small amounts, please place in paper bags or small cardboard boxes to keep it contained)

"You need to rinse your containers to get rid of any leftover food or liquid, and put them straight in the blue bin," the council website reads.

"Do not put them in plastic bags.

"When putting cardboard packaging in the bin eg. pizza boxes please make sure they are clean and don't have any food in them."
