Two men arrested in connection with brawl that led to death of Siminic Rostas

TheTools · 09.09.2026 15:33 · source · permalink

Two men have been arrested in connection with the large fight that led to the death of Siminic Rostas in south Dublin last week.

Romanian-born Rostas, 24, died in hospital on September 1 after the incident at the South County Business Park in Leopardstown on the evening of August 29. Three men have already been charged in connection with the incident.

Gardai arrested two men yesterday in the capital, bringing the total number of arrests in connection to the assault to six. The pair, aged in their 20s and 40s, have since been released without charge.

A garda statement said: "Gardaí are continuing to investigate an incident of violent disorder that occurred in Leopardstown, Dublin 18, on Saturday 29th August 2026. The male (aged in his 20s) who was seriously injured during the course of this incident, was pronounced deceased on Tuesday 1st September 2026.

"Investigating Gardaí made two further arrests in connection with this investigation on Tuesday 8th of September 2026. Two males aged in their 20s and 40s were arrested in the Dublin area.

"Both have since been released without charge. Files will be prepared for the Office of the Director of Public Prosecutions. This brings to six (6) the number of individuals arrested to date.

"Investigations are ongoing."
