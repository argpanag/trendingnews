vivo gets starry-eyed with ENHYPEN trio for V80 campaign

TheTools · 19.11.2026 11:34 · source · permalink

share on

vivo has appointed three members of K-pop group ENHYPEN as brand ambassadors for its V80 smartphone across nine markets, as it looks to tap into youth culture and position portrait photography as a form of self-expression.

JUNGWON, JAKE and SUNGHOON have been named vivo Friends in Singapore, Myanmar, Cambodia, Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Taiwan, Thailand and Vietnam. The partnership will see the trio work with vivo on content exploring the intersection of fashion, personal style and portrait photography.

The collaboration is built around the V80's "Shine as we are" positioning, which celebrates individuality and the idea that everyone has their own moment to shine. Drawing inspiration from a star-filled galaxy, the smartphone's 'Starwish purple' colourway features sparkling details intended to reflect this theme.

As part of the campaign, the three ENHYPEN members photographed a series of portraits using the V80's ZEISS center-stage portrait capabilities, including its 100mm and 85mm focal lengths. The images aim to capture what vivo describes as authentic and radiant moments, with the trio's individual personalities and styles forming part of the campaign's creative direction.

The campaign also highlights the V80's AI creative camera capabilities, as vivo looks to appeal to younger consumers who increasingly use imaging to document their lives, communicate their identities and share experiences.

According to vivo, the partnership reflects its efforts to better understand the culture and lifestyles of younger consumers. The brand pointed to the members' distinctive personalities and styles, as well as their connections with young audiences globally, as aligning with the V Series' focus on portrait photography.

The V80 will be rolled out across Southeast Asia on a market-by-market basis.

MARKETING-INTERACTIVE has reached out for more. 

The appointment comes amid a wider push by brands to tap K-pop's regional fanbase through ambassador partnerships. In January, Skechers appointed SEVENTEEN members DK, Seungkwan and Dino as regional brand ambassadors across key Asia Pacific markets, including Singapore, Hong Kong, Macau, Malaysia, Thailand and Vietnam. The trio featured across the footwear brand's lifestyle and performance collections.

More recently, ACUVUE tapped rising K-pop group CORTIS as its regional ambassadors, bringing the partnership to life through a Singapore activation at Bras Basah MRT. The September campaign featured a customised reflective panel that allowed commuters to take photos alongside the group, drawing on fan behaviours such as creating social content and collecting photocards.
