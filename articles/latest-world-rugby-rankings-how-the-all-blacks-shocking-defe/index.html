Latest World Rugby rankings: How the All Blacks' shocking defeat to Wales has impacted the standings

TheTools · 14.11.2026 17:56 · source · permalink

Wales celebrate and All Blacks dejected after stunning Nations Championship clash.

The All Blacks missed the chance to usurp the Springboks in the World Rugby rankings after they suffered a shock 35-28 defeat to Wales at the Principality Stadium.

France had provided an excellent opportunity for Dave Rennie’s men to overtake their arch-rivals in the standings and, in fact, went into Saturday’s game ahead of the Boks.

Beating the Welsh looked relatively simple on paper, but the hosts produced one of their greatest performances to stunningly overcome the All Blacks for the first time in 73 years.

They did something the great Welsh teams failed to manage and, as a result, it stopped the visitors from moving to the summit of the world rankings.

It might have been a much-changed side but it was their experienced playmaking duo who particularly struggled; Richie Mo’unga and Beauden Barrett made a series of poor decisions.

One positive for All Blacks

The tourists did still manage to cross the whitewash four times via Cortez Ratima, Wallace Sititi, Fehi Fineanganofo and Peter Lakai, which earned two losing bonus-points and took them to the top of their Nations Championship pool, but Wales’ five scores undid them.

Tomos Williams, Dewi Lake, Archie Griffin, Eddie James and Louis Rees-Zammit all managed to cross the whitewash and earn them two ranking points which took them above Fiji in the standings.

As for the All Blacks, they went down from 92.65 to 90.65, which means they effectively remain in second, but it has closed the gap between the top-four.

It wasn’t long ago that South Africa comfortably sat atop the rankings, but the gap has been closed with just over two points now separating the Boks in first and France in third.

Les Bleus gained 1.56 points to go ahead of Ireland, who cannot return to that position in the Fiji encounter.
