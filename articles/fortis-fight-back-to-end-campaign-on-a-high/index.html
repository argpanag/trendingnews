Fortis fight back to end campaign on a high

TheTools · 23.10.2026 19:00 · source · permalink

Fortis FC came from behind to beat Tajikistan’s FC Regar-TadAZ 3-1 in their final Group C match of the AFC Challenge League 2026/27 on Friday, ending their campaign on a positive note.

Both sides finished their campaigns with three points each, bowing out of the competition. Fortis lost the previous two games, against Al-Qadsia (3-0) and FC Goa (2-1).

Regar-TadAZ took the lead in the 12th minute when Ndongo Foe’s clever flick sent Sherzod Makhamadiev through on the right. The forward cut inside Monir Alam before firing past Fortis goalkeeper Sujan Perera.

However, the match took a decisive turn five minutes later when Regar-TadAZ goalkeeper Severin Takam was sent off for tripping Noriki Akada outside the box after Martin Ako Assomo’s underhit back pass was intercepted by the Japanese forward.

Replacement goalkeeper Shakhobiddin Makhmudzoda immediately came under pressure, saving Pa Omar’s curling free-kick. Fortis made the most of their numerical advantage by pressing higher up the pitch but missed a golden opportunity to equalise in the 35th minute. Omar chested the ball down for Akada inside the box, but the forward blazed his effort over the bar.

The contest remained open after the break, with Regar-TadAZ opting against sitting deep to protect their lead. Fortis took advantage of the space and drew level in the 67th minute. Makhmudzoda dived to stop Shuki but diverted the ball into Omar’s path, allowing the latter to set up Rafayel Tudu at the back post. Tudu finished with a backheel to make it 1-1.

Fortis completed the turnaround just four minutes later. Ananta Tamang headed Mohammad Ibrahim’s cross into the path of Shian Kawasaki, who unleashed a powerful volley past Makhmudzoda to put the Bangladesh side ahead.

Mohammad Masud’s men sealed the victory in the 88th minute when Omar set up Shuki, who curled a side-footed finish into the net to complete a 3-1 win.
