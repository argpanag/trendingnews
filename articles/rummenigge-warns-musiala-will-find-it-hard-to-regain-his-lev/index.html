Rummenigge warns: Musiala will find it hard to regain his level

TheTools · 20.11.2026 22:40 · source · permalink

Jamal Musiala returns after two months out with a neurological disorder, and Bayern do not rule out cutting his salary.

By TuFutbolero Newsroom·November 20, 2026 at 05:40 PM

Jamal Musiala has returned to the pitch two months after being diagnosed with a neurological disorder that could affect his career. The attacking midfielder played the final 20 minutes of the 1-1 draw against Köln, and Karl-Heinz Rummenigge, a member of Bayern's Supervisory Board, spoke about his situation, as Mundo Deportivo reports.

Rummenigge believes a player cannot be judged on 20 minutes. He noted the health problem is public knowledge and that his contract involves very large sums, so the club has to consider how to handle it. Asked whether Musiala can return to his best level, he did not rule out that he might not, saying it will be difficult to match what he has shown at the Allianz Arena.

A contract under review

The former club chief also does not rule out a salary cut. He said the club would have to talk with the player's family, the player himself and his agency, because in that situation those figures would no longer be financially justifiable. He wants to see Musiala return and play well, but if that does not happen, the board and the supervisory board will have to make a decision.

His comments recall those Uli Hoeness made a few days ago, showing the issue has reached the top of the club.
