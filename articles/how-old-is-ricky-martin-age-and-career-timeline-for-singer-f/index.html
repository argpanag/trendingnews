The NFL's second trip to Brazil will prove notable for several reasons.

The matchup is enticing — the Ravens and Cowboys are two of the NFL's most prominent brands. There are questions about the viability of the field at the Maracana. And yet, the colosseum still holds special significance to masses of sports fans — both in Rio and beyond.

Then, there is the pomp and circumstance of the fixture. The NFL is rolling out the red carpet for Baltimore and Dallas' clash, giving it Thanksgiving-like treatment. The Shield recruited one of the Latin world's most successful musicians to perform in its halftime show. There's a good chance you have heard of him.

With that, here's what you need to know about the age and career timeline for Ricky Martin, one of two performers slated to light up the halftime show at the Maracana.

How old is Ricky Martin?

Ricky Martin was born on Dec. 24, 1971, making him 54 years old at the time of his performance at the Ravens vs. Cowboys halftime show.

Ricky Martin career timeline

Martin has enjoyed a splendid career as a musician, garnering billions of listeners and more than 70 million record sales. The two-time Grammy winner and five-time Latin Grammy winner enjoyed a humble start to his career, coming to prominence within his native San Juan by appearing in a handful of television commercials.

A natural showman, Martin joined boy band Menudo in 1984. He left the Grammy-nominated group when he was 17, embarking on an uber-successful solo career. Martin released his eponymous debut album in 1991. The project surged near the top of the Billboard Latin charts.

By 1995, Martin was an international superstar. His third album, "A Medio Vivir", was certified gold in the U.S. and four times platinum in Spain. He cemented his status with additional releases in the the late-1990s and early-2000s, dropping beloved tracks like "La Copa de la Vida", "La Bomba" and, most famously, "Livin' la Vida Loca."

Martin hasn't released a new album since 2015. He remains a formidable presences in the music industry, having performed at this year's Super Bowl alongside Bad Bunny.
