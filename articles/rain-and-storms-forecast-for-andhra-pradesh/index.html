The weather forecast for Andhra Pradesh indicates rainfall for the next three days. The change in the weather pattern over the Bay of Bengal will lead to light to moderate rain and thunderstorms in Coastal Andhra, Yanam, and Rayalaseema. Some areas may witness heavy downpours.

There is a cyclonic circulation over south-west Bay of Bengal and adjoining Tamil Nadu Coast up to a height of 5.8 km above the sea level. Under the influence of this system, a low-pressure area is likely to form in the same region within 24 hours. A trough associated with the cyclonic circulation extends towards the North Coastal Andhra at 3.1 km above sea level.

Another cyclonic circulation over Gulf of Thailand and adjoining parts of Thailand and Myanmar extends up to 4.5 km above sea level. It is likely to move into the northern Andaman Sea as a depression by the morning of 21st October.

As per the India Metrological Department, the conditions are favorable for the formation of a low-pressure area over south-eastern Bay of Bengal and adjoining east-central Bay of Bengal on 23rd October. It is likely to move west-north-west and intensify into a depression by 27th October over the central Bay of Bengal. There are also possibilities that it may intensify into a cyclonic storm and cross over North Coastal Andhra Pradesh and south Odisha by the end of the month.
