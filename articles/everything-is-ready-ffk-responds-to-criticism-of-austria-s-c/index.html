"Everything is ready", FFK responds to criticism of Austria's coach with a picture of the condition of the field at "Fadil Vokrri"

TheTools · 03.10.2026 16:34 · source · permalink

The Football Federation of Kosovo has indirectly reacted to the coach of the Austrian national team, Ralf Rangnick, with a post on social media. 

Rangnick criticized the condition of the field at the "Fadil Vokrri" stadium in tonight's press conference, ahead of the Kosovo - Austria match, which will be played tomorrow. 

The Austrian coach said that the condition of the field at the "Fadil Vokrri" stadium is meaningless. 

"In Vienna we played on a fresh, newly renovated pitch. In Pristina I saw a pitch that was not very well maintained. It's pointless. The weather is good and the pitch should have been level," he said today. 

In response, the Football Federation of Kosovo posted some photos of the stadium, the day before the match with Austria. 

"One day before the match! At the "Fadil Vokrri" stadium, everything is ready for the Kosovo - Austria duel. The Dardanians' house is expected to echo with the voices of the fans! Tomorrow, all together for Kosovo! FORCA DARDANË!", wrote the FFK.

The Dardanian national team coach, Franco Foda, had a direct reaction to the Austrian, saying that the field is the same for both sides. 

"The pitch is not in very good condition, because during the summer many teams have played here. We know that this condition of the pitch applies to both teams. I understand Rangnick's criticism," said Foda. 

The Kosovo – Austria match will be played tomorrow, starting at 18:00, at “Fadil Vokrri”. /GazetaExpress/
