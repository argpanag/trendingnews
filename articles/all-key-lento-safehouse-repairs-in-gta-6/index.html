All Key Lento safehouse repairs in GTA 6

TheTools · 19.11.2026 02:59 · source · permalink

Early in GTA 6 players will find themselves staying at one of Brian’s houses free of charge. The only condition is that you have to handle the repairs and upkeep yourself. If you want to upgrade your safehouse for all current and future occupants, here’s a list of everything you can repair and upgrade.

All safehouse repairs

Here’s a list of all the repairs and upgrades you can make to the Key Lento safehouse in GTA 6:

  • Fix the back deck
  • Drywall the bedroom
  • Paint the bedroom
  • Fix the roof
  • Repair the front steps

The five tasks can all be done quickly. You can find the back deck repairs near the workout equipment, the drywall sitting in the bedroom, and the paint will appear after you complete the drywall repairs. The roof is accessed via the ladder near the front door, and the wood to fix the front steps is near where you find Jason’s car. None of it will cost you any money, and you should have the house in shape in about 10 minutes of gameplay.

The reason these repairs matter is Lucia. If you want to get your relationship off on the right foot, make sure the home is properly taken care of before she arrives. Even if you don’t care, these repairs make the safehouse look much better, so why not?
