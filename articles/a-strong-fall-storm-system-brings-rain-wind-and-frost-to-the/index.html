A strong fall storm system brings rain, wind, and frost to the stateline

TheTools · 15.10.2026 17:16 · source · permalink
  • Periods of steady rain at times
  • Blustery Friday with a few showers, chilly temps
  • Frosty conditions expected Saturday morning

ROCKFORD, Ill. (Rockford News First) - Our next fall storm system approaches the area today, bringing widespread rain across the stateline. Light showers this morning, will eventually give way to steady, and at times heavy rain through the afternoon and evening. Severe weather is not expected, however a few rumbles of thunder will be possible. Rain totals through early Friday morning could range from .25″ to .75″ across northern Illinois, with locally higher amounts up to 1.00″.

What’s Coming Up...

As low pressure departs to our northeast, strong northwest winds will develop on the backside, giving us quite the blustery day on Friday. A few wrap around showers will be possible throughout the day, along with winds gusting upwards of 30 to 40 mph. Strong high pressure will move into the area Friday, in the wake of Thursday night’s cold front. This will usher in a cooler Canadian airmass across much of the Midwest with daytime highs in the lower 50s.

If you’re planning on heading out to any high school football games tomorrow night, make sure to bundle up. Wind chills will be in the 30s during the evening hours.

Looking Ahead...

As we close out the week, we’ll see some of the coldest air of the season dip into the region, with overnight lows near 32°. This keeps us on track to see our first frost/freeze of the season by Saturday morning. Temperatures will also be drastically cooler through the weekend, with highs in the 50s and 60s. Next week, temperatures look to rebound into the 70s by Tuesday, as winds take a turn to the southwest.
