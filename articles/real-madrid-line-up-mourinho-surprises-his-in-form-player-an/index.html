Mourinho hands Real Madrid star his first start

Jose Mourinho has named his Real Madrid side to face Sevilla on Sunday evening in the ninth round of the Spanish league season.

Brazilian forward Endrick gets his first start of the campaign, reward for that superb display against Roma in the Champions League.

Arda Guler also comes into the XI. The Turkey international began the Roma game on the bench.

Analysing the Merengue selection, Marca note that midfielder Fede Valverde will line up at full-back, a role he had not wanted to fill in previous seasons.
