Rob Lowe's niece Fiona tragically dies at just 13 years old as family mourns 'light of our lives' - AOL

TheTools · 30.09.2026 06:28 · source · permalink

Rob Lowe's niece has tragically passed away.

Rob's brother Chad Lowe's daughter, Fiona, who he shares with his wife, Kim Painter, died at the age of 13, leaving the family devastated over her passing. The Lowe family told TMZ in a statement, “We are deeply heartbroken by the loss of our beloved Fiona.” Fiona's parents, Chad and Kim, remembered her as a person who was smart, loving, and creative, with a passion for dance.

Also, she was adored by her sisters and parents, as the family also described Fiona as “the light of our lives" to the news outlet, sharing that her death devastated their entire family as they asked for prayers and privacy while they grieved. It comes after Rob flexed with his son in a gym selfie last year.

Chad, who is the youngest brother of actor Rob, 62, welcomed Fiona, his second child with Kim, on November 16, 2012.

The married couple didn't tie the knot until 2019, but they welcomed their first daughter Mabel in 2009 and welcomed their third child Nixie in 2016.
