  7. 'I cannot fault at all the attitude of the players'published at 22:59 GMT
    22:59 GMT

    FT: Fleetwood 1-1 Arsenal (5-3 pens)

    Arsenal

    Arsenal boss Mikel Arteta, speaking to Sky Sports: "First of all, congratulations to Fleetwood for going through in the next round, in the manner they've done it, the atmosphere they have created, and, yeah, a really sad day for us.

    "I cannot fault at all the attitude of the players. I haven't seen anyone not doing what is required at this level. But, obviously, we insisted so much, we have so much of the ball, so many chances, so many shots, and if you want the margins to be bigger than going to the penalties, obviously, you need to capitalize on that. And we didn't. And football is about that.

    "It is, and especially when they go ahead and, and they create that momentum. We gave them almost nothing in the whole game and we created so many chances, and didn't capitalise on that. And then the margins become so small, that's it. This football, that's the beauty as well of this competition, and, especially in this country, it's a very sad day."

    On Max Dowman's red card: "It's difficult to see on the cameras like that, so I prefer to watch it back properly and then I can assess the situation."

    On if Dowman will learn from this: "Yeah, we'll learn from everything."

    On if he saw enough: "Yeah, the attitude-wise, I have zero. Otherwise, I would be here absolutely fuming. I'm sad, I think we are all sad. We have a massive responsibility to carry this badge in the best possible way. It had nothing to do with effort or application or determination to win it, but we lack other things to, to create a much bigger margin than today, with what we produce, they really should have been shown in the scoreline."

  8. 'I don't think we should be scared of anyone, or any team now'published at 22:57 GMT
    22:57 GMT

    FT: Fleetwood 1-1 Arsenal (5-3 pens)

    Fleetwood defender Jake Batty, speaking to BBC Radio 5 Live: "The champions of England coming to our patch, we always knew it was going to be a tough game, we always knew we were going to have to grind out a result.

    "I think 1-1 was probably a fair result over 90 minutes, I think we did what we needed to do, I think everyone stuck to the game plan, but yeah, it gives us so much confidence, so much motivation, and it brings us lads closer together because we know we can trust each other arguably against the best players in the world at the moment."

    On if the occasion was extra motivation: "We have to replicate that every game. Our league form hasn't been as good as what we wanted it to be, and for some reason, cup competitions give everyone a bit more fire in the belly. We just came up with some magical moments, and this one, is one to tell the grandkids."

    On hanging on: "We knew they were going to come here and dominate the ball. We're not silly, we know what we are, we know what they are. Obviously, when they got the goal early in the second half, you're thinking 'oh god, here we go', but I think the resistance, the communication, and the culture of the lads.

    "Everyone is just doing a job for each other, fighting for their own place, which is what they need, and we need to go onto Saturday, and carry that momentum and confidence into the game to get three points. We knew we would give [Arsenal] a good game on our patch, and we knew the crowd was going to be there for us."

    On Arsenal: "I think we kept them quite in places that we needed to keep them quite and I just think, you can't stop that type of experience."
