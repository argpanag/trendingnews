    George Pickens (hamstring) is inactive for Week 9 against the Colts.

    Pickens played through the issue last week but will ultimately be held out in Week 9. Tight end Jake Ferguson is active after failing to practice until a limited showing Friday. Ryan Flournoy steps into a featured role for the Cowboys against the inside-funnel Colts defense. Fantasy managers can insert Flournoy into rosters as a viable FLEX considering he leads the team in slot rate through the first half of the season. Also inactive for the Cowboys are LB DeMarvion Overshown, WR KaVontae Turpin, CB Joey Porter Jr., LB Tyrus Wheat, OL Ajani Cornelius, and TE Luke Schoonmaker.
