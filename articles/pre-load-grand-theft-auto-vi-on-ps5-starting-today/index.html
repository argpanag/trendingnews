Pre-load Grand Theft Auto VI on PS5 starting today

TheTools · 10.11.2026 15:41 · source · permalink

Plus a new video showcasing unique PS5 features for GTAVI.

Grand Theft Auto VI is now the most pre-ordered game in the history of PlayStation Store. Given this unprecedented demand, we’ve joined with Rockstar Games to allow players to start pre-loading the game nine days early, so they’re ready to play on launch day. We’ve also adopted a rolling midnight launch by country, beginning at 12:00AM NZDT in New Zealand and running across the globe as respective countries hit midnight on November 19.

Pre-load begins today

Pre-loading is now available at PlayStation Store. Given the anticipation for Grand Theft Auto VI, we strongly advise that everyone who is hoping to play on launch day to pre-load the game as soon as possible. Players who purchased the code-in-box version can also activate their code as soon as they get it to begin pre-loading. If you still haven’t pre-ordered the game, now’s the time to do so to make sure you’re ready for November 19.

PS5 and PS5 Pro Features

Rockstar Games has developed Grand Theft Auto VI from the ground up to harness the power of the PlayStation 5 to create a stunning experience, with action-packed gameplay that brings the vast and rich world of Leonida to life. There’s plenty to look forward to when playing on PS5.

Grand Theft Auto VI for PS5 uses the DualSense wireless controller’s integrated speakers, haptics, and adaptive triggers that are fully used across the game  and are adjustable via accessibility settings. All in-game sounds take full advantage of Tempest 3D Audio for highly accurate audio positioning, while load times are improved with PS5’s ultra high-speed SSD, making Grand Theft Auto VI play best on PlayStation 5 consoles. Enhancements are also found on PlayStation 5 Pro, which offers breathtaking visuals, improved clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows.

Pre-order and pre-load GTAVI now at PlayStation Store.
