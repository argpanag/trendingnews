MBB Preview: Arkansas vs Gonzaga – Exhibition Parking Availability

TheTools · 22.10.2026 17:12 · source · permalink

Who: #12/13 Arkansas Razorbacks (0-0, 0-0 SEC) vs #15/12 Gonzaga Bulldogs (0-0, 0-0 PAC 12)
What: Game 1 of 2 home exhibitions for the Razorbacks.
When: Saturday – Oct. 24 – 3:00 pm (CT)
Where: Fayetteville, Ark. • Bud Walton Arena – Nolan Richardson Court (19,200)
How (to follow):
– TV/Stream: SEC Network+/Watch ESPN (Josh Haley and Joe Kleine)

FAYETTEVILLE – The #12/13 Arkansas men’s basketball program will host #15/12 Gonzaga for an exhibition game at Bud Walton Arena on Saturday (Oct. 24) at 3 pm. The game will be streamed on SEC Network+ and broadcast on the Learfield Razorback Sports Network.

Both Arkansas home exhibition games (Gonzaga Saturday and Memphis on Oct. 28) are not part of the 2026-27 season ticket package. Tickets can be purchased by clicking HERE. Also, parking will be free and on a first-come, first-served basis except for a few protected lots. While parking is free, lots will only be open for the basketball games two hours prior to tipoff. Click HERE for available parking lots for the exhibitions. As a reminder, doors to Bud Walton Arena open 90 minutes prior to tipoff.

• Arkansas is 76-11 all-time in exhibition games, including a 67-9 mark at home. The Razorbacks have won 40 straight home exhibition games.
• Playing one of the top teams in the country in an exhibition game is not new for Arkansas. In previous years, Arkansas hosted 2024-25 preseason #1 Kansas (and won 85-69) and hosted 2023-24 preseason #1 Purdue (and won 81-77 in OT). Prior to the 2022-23 campaign, Arkansas, preseason #10, played at #12 Texas in an exhibition to christen the Longhorns’ new Moody Center. Texas won 90-60.
