Is VALORANT Champions Shanghai The Most Stacked Ever? - Hotspawn

TheTools · 06.10.2026 16:42 · source · permalink

Zahk

Share:

Zahk

Share:

The VALORANT Champions Shanghai playoff field has a defending champion, three Masters winners, a trophy-starved perennial, a superteam and a rookie roster nobody saw coming. Five of the eight teams have lifted an international trophy since March 2025. Here is the case for the deepest Champions yet.

NRG beat Fnatic 3-2 to win Champions 2025 and arrived with four of the same five. Georgio “keiko” Sanassy is the only change, replacing s0m. They topped Group D by beating Karmine Corp and Santiago champions Nongshim without dropping a series.

Paper Rex (Toronto 2025), T1 (Bangkok 2025) and Nongshim RedForce (Santiago 2026) are all here as Masters winners. PRX and T1 each kept four of five from their winning lineups, with Adrian “invy” Reyes coming into the squad instead of Patrick “PatMen” Mendoza and Byeon “Munchkin” Sang-beom in for Ko “Sylvan” Young-sub. Nongshim’s Santiago-winning five is unchanged. 

G2 have made eight straight international playoffs and won multiple VCT Americas titles. They lost the Masters Bangkok final 3-2 to T1, with the last two maps going to overtime. VCT international trophy-less despite that record, they are still looking to fix it, and this might be their last chance to do so as a team.

Nikita “Derke” Sirmitev has two international VCT titles during his stint with FNATIC at LOCK//IN 2023 and Masters Tokyo. Timofey “Chronicle” Khromov has three, adding Masters Berlin 2021 during his time with Gambit, where he also came 2nd at Champions that year. Chronicle has pushed back on the superteam label, but the 2-0 win over LOUD suggests the Team Vitality roster is delivering. Dawid “PROFEK” Święć discussed the redemption arc before the event and has been incredibly consistent in Shanghai so far, as has the whole squad.

100 Thieves had not played an international VCT tournament since Masters Shanghai 2024. They won the Esports World Cup, then Stage 2 Americas over LOUD, and are the only team to reach the playoffs without dropping a map. Whether they can wrap up the year with a Champs Shanghai trophy remains to be seen.

LOUD rebuilt through the year and needed a Stage 2 final run to qualify. They are the youngest team in the field at their first international together with a roster of five Champions debutants. They swept 2024 champions EDG and then eliminated Global Esports. Roberto “erde” Lobos has led them while fragging his heart out. If they make a run, they could be the story of the year.

Few VALORANT Champions have opened with this many proven names in such form. Whether VALORANT Champions 2026 is the most stacked ever is debatable, but it’s definitely one of the most competitive brackets seen.
