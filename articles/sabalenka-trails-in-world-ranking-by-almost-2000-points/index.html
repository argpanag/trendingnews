Sabalenka trails in world ranking by almost 2000 points

TheTools · 27.10.2026 07:33 · source · permalink

The updated ranking of the world's best female tennis players has been released. Kazakhstan's Elena Rybakina has once again pulled away from Aryna Sabalenka by almost two thousand points.

The world's No. 1 player has 9501 points in the ranking, while the Belarusian has 7550.

Russian Mirra Andreeva has 6306 points, American Jessica Pegula has 6225, and Coco Gauff has 5973.

Ahead for the tennis players is the big WTA Finals tournament, featuring the eight best players. It is scheduled to take place in Riyadh from November 7 to 14.

Sabalenka, who has been in a slump in recent months, may also lose her ranking as world No. 2.

Aryna held the title of world No. 1 for 99 weeks: from September 19, 2024, to September 14, 2026. But the last tournaments she won were in March, victories at Indian Wells and Miami.
