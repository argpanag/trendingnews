  • Actress Tina Simmons, best known for her roles across the Star Wars and James Bond franchises, has died after being found in her flat on Sunday.
  • Former Bond girl Madeline Smith led emotional tributes to her close friend of nearly 60 years, expressing her heartbreak and devastation following the news.
  • Simmons was fondly remembered by Star Wars fans for playing a Rebel Technician in Return of the Jedi, and she appeared in multiple Bond blockbusters including Octopussy, A View to a Kill and No Time to Die.
  • Her extensive career in British television and film spanned several decades, featuring appearances in iconic shows such as Doctor Who, EastEnders, Coronation Street and Only Fools and Horses.
  • Alongside her screen roles, Simmons worked as a trusted body double and stand-in for Hollywood stars including Dame Helen Mirren and Meryl Streep, becoming a beloved figure among fans at conventions.
