Building on 30 years of play: First look at the LEGO® PlayStation™

TheTools · 03.09.2026 13:22 · source · permalink

Available from October 1, the LEGO set replica of the original PlayStation console is packed with iconic details.

Today at State of Play, we shared the first look at an all-new collaboration with the LEGO Group – the LEGO PlayStation – that celebrates the games, experiences, and the feelings of play we have shared as a community for over 30 years.

For the first time, players and LEGO fans will be able to create a LEGO replica of the original PlayStation console and PlayStation controller. Brick by brick, this 1,911-piece set represents a near 1:1 scale of the console, measuring 10.5 inches / 26 cm wide when fully assembled. 

To bring this set to life, we collaborated closely with LEGO designers to craft a building experience that is inspired by PlayStation’s origins. When building the set, players will notice hidden details from PlayStation’s history and references to two iconic games that launched on the original PlayStation console, Ape Escape and Gran Turismo. Each diorama features customizable elements – Gran Turismo includes a miniature track complete with scaled down cars, while the Ape Escape diorama has a nostalgic island scene with a swappable blue or red light on the monkey’s helmet. The interactive set has functional mechanisms for the console’s Power and Open buttons, a controller that can plug into the console, and the ability to remove both game dioramas so they can be displayed separately.

The LEGO PlayStation will be available directly from PlayStation on direct.playstation.com starting October 1, 2026, in the U.S., U.K., France, Germany, Austria, Belgium, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Italy, Spain, and Portugal. The set will also be available from October 1, 2026, for LEGO Insiders Early Access and for all via LEGO.com and LEGO Stores from October 4, 2026. The recommended retail price (RRP) is $179.99 USD | €159.99 EURO | £139.99 GBP | ¥27,980 JPY (including tax). 

Whether you have been with PlayStation since 1994 or are a newer fan, we hope you enjoy building a piece of PlayStation history with this upcoming LEGO set. Learn more about the LEGO PlayStation on LEGO’s official website: LEGO.com/PlayStation.
