Prince Harry ‘squeezing American accents’ out of Archie and Lilibet

TheTools · 24.11.2026 05:15 · source · permalink

Duke of Sussex reveals moving back to the UK a ‘big culture shift’ for family as he appears on This Morning

The Duke of Sussex has joked that he is slowly “squeezing” the American accents out of his children.

During a somewhat chaotic appearance on ITV1’s This Morning on Friday, Prince Harry downed a shot of whisky, operated the camera and berated Alison Hammond, the host, for failing to take his personal phone calls.

As he helped make a Christmas cake, he admitted he was “used to taking orders” and told Phil Vickery, the chef, that his kitchen needed a clean.

Discussing his family’s return to the UK from California, the Duke revealed that the confidence of both of his children, Prince Archie, seven, and Princess Lilibet, five, had soared since they started in their new British school in September.

He acknowledged that it had been “a big culture shift”, adding: “I think the kids in school uniform is a big change for them, but it has certainly helped. 

“The confidence in both of them has gone through the roof since being in a UK school, which is really, really nice.

“I’m squeezing the accents out of them, and their friends at school are doing the same. Lili does a very good impression of ‘Mummyyyyy’.”

The Duke said the children loved being back in Britain, although they had never really known the UK, and revealed they were both looking forward to seeing some snow.

Speaking about his own feelings on returning from America, he said it was “great being back on home soil” and that he was throwing himself back into the cold weather. “I have missed the winters,” he admitted.

He said the family had enjoyed their outing to Winter Wonderland a few days ago but acknowledged that, at the age of 42, the dodgems hurt his back and that it was exhausting running around after the children.

“It was much easier when it was just me and Meg,” he joked.

Although Archie was born in London, he travelled with his family to Canada when he was just a few months old before they moved to the US. Lilibet was born in California.

The Duke appeared on the daytime show to promote the 2027 Invictus Games, which takes place in Birmingham in July.

In the kitchen, he was roped into helping Vickery and hosts Hammond and Dermot O’Leary make a Christmas cake.

The Duke was tasked with reaming the oranges, but admitted he did not do much baking at home, choosing to leave it to the children.

Asked whether he would be involved in making Christmas dinner, he said: “My wife is very good in the kitchen and so is my mother-in-law, so I leave that to them.”

The Sussexes, who will not be invited to join the Royal family at Sandringham, are expected to spend the festive period with Meghan’s mother, Doria Ragland.

Asked about the traditional Christmas dinner at Sandringham, The Duke said it was a “turkey fest” with “every range of turkey you can imagine”, including roasted, pulled and smoked.

After they had each knocked back a shot of whisky, Hammond liberally scattered edible flowers all over the Christmas cake, and O’Leary asked: “Doesn’t Meghan eat flowers?”

“Edible flowers,” Harry agreed. He then asked about lighting the cake, only to be told that it was Christmas pudding, not a cake, that was traditionally set on fire.

The Duke had earlier admired the This Morning sofa before berating Hammond for failing to answer his calls after they had met in Birmingham for an Invictus event in July.

“I got your number from one of your team,” he said. “I Facetimed you when I got home with the kids and said, ‘this is the woman who does Bake Off’, they were so excited. No answer.

“Then I may have texted you when you were in hospital out of concern … and nothing.”

He laughed as an embarrassed Hammond apologised and said she never picked up calls from unknown numbers.

The Duke, who revealed he enjoyed a cold plunge, then took part in a quickfire quiz, in which he said he’d choose a full English breakfast over waffles, a pint in a pub over an iced Matcha in Malibu and a country walk over surfing.

Asked to choose between Glastonbury or Coachella, he said: “Both of them sound like a nightmare. I’ve been to Glastonbury once and it was a bit much for me.”

He chose James Bond over Mission Impossible and said he “can’t wait” to see Robert Pattinson as the new 007.

Asked whether he preferred EastEnders or Suits, the US legal drama that the Duchess of Sussex starred in, he said “Suits, obviously”, apologising to Ross Kemp, the actor who played Grant Mitchell in the soap.

Turning to the Invictus Games, the Duke said Birmingham was the right place to host the event, as it was home to the Queen Elizabeth Hospital, where many injured military veterans are treated.

He said hundreds of British soldiers had been treated there, and that returning to host the Games in the city “means a hell of a lot”, adding: “So yeah, it’s the right place for it.”

Prince Harry urged members of the public to sign up as volunteers, explaining: “It’s open invite to everybody.”

In an interview with The Telegraph earlier this week, The Duke said he was ready to serve in the Armed Forces again, warning that the threat from Russia meant “we should all be nervous”.
