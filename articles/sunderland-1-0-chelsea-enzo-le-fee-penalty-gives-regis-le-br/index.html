Sunderland 1-0 Chelsea: Enzo le Fee penalty gives Regis le Bris' side victory

Enzo le Fee has scored three penalties in the Premier League this season, more than any other player

Sunderland held on to their first Premier League win since August as Enzo le Fee's penalty helped the hosts sink Chelsea at the Stadium of Light.

Le Fee sent Chelsea goalkeeper Mike Penders the wrong way from the spot in the 36th minute.

But the awarding of Sunderland's penalty was controversial, with referee Jarred Gillett adjudging Levi Colwill to have pulled Brian Brobbey to the floor - despite both players grappling one another as Brobbey was driving towards goal.

Sunderland's win, just their second in the league this season but sixth in all competitions, will ease pressure on manager Regis le Bris - although his side remain in the Premier League's bottom three.

Chelsea, meanwhile, lost their second league match in three and stayed fifth, missing out on a chance to break into the top four.

Big boost for manager Le Bris

Sunderland had been booed off by their own supporters at full-time in their last fixture - a 2-0 loss to Dinamo Zagreb in the Europa League.

Last week, with pressure mounting, club sources told BBC Sport Frenchman Le Bris would be given more time to turn around his side's form - having led Sunderland to an impressive seventh-placed finish last season.

And the Black Cats produced a fine performance on Sunday, recording their first league win since beating Fulham 1-0 at home in the second game of the season.

That said, Le Bris will be concerned about two of his star players - Granit Xhaka and Brobbey - hobbling off with muscle injuries.

Brobbey put in a particularly impressive performance against Chelsea and almost scored early on when he headed Chemsdine Talbi's cross narrowly wide.

After Brobbey's substitution in the 77th minute Sunderland switched to a defensive-first approach but were rarely threatened by their opponents.

The win meant they climbed from 20th to 18th in the table, one point off safety.

Chelsea rue penalty calls going against them

Inconsistency seems to be the main issue with Chelsea this season.

The Blues were unable to build on last week's convincing 2-0 win over Manchester United, which came after a poor loss to Tottenham seven days before.

The visitors are still missing forward Joao Pedro through a thigh injury while Cole Palmer, who was not named in Thomas Tuchel's latest England squad, and Morgan Rogers did not have their best games.

With the scoreline goalless in the first period Rogers had a free header in the area which he nodded narrowly wide.

Palmer was also convinced his side should have had a penalty in the 25th minute when he was bought down by Luke O'Nien on the edge of the area.

The referee initially waved play on but the Video Assistant Referee reviewed the incident and judged there had been a foul by the Sunderland defender, but that it happened outside of the penalty area before Palmer fell into the box.

Chelsea, who had 10 shots but created few clear-cut chances, are two points off the top four following their defeat.
