Dell Technologies (NYSE: DELL) fell 10% Friday afternoon to $281.12 as the selloff in AI and chip stocks deepened, two trading sessions before the company reports earnings on Tuesday. No company news came with the drop. Dell is down 34% in November, on pace for its worst month since it returned to the stock market in 2018.

Other makers of AI servers fell with it. Hewlett Packard Enterprise (NYSE: HPE) was down 9% and Super Micro Computer (NASDAQ: SMCI) 7%. Nvidia (NASDAQ: NVDA), whose chips go into Dell's AI servers, was down 7%. Nvidia has closed lower six sessions in a row, and a lower close on Friday would be the seventh. Prices are from about 12:53 p.m. Eastern.

The drop reads as investors cutting their AI hardware holdings ahead of the report: no Dell news arrived on Friday, and its peers fell together. That reading has a limit. Dell's own numbers arrive Tuesday, and the stock has moved 13% on average the day after its last eight reports, more than Friday's drop.

Why Dell is falling

Friday's losses extend Thursday's selloff in AI and chip stocks, which spread to Asia overnight. Taiwan's stock market closed out its worst week since 1991, and AMD was already down 7% before the opening bell. By midday Advanced Micro Devices (NASDAQ: AMD) was down 11%, and the Invesco QQQ Trust (NASDAQ: QQQ), which tracks the Nasdaq 100, was down 4%.
