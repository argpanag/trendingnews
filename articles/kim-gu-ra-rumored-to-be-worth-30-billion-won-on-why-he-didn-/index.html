Broadcaster Kim Gu-ra cited the unstable income associated with being a celebrity as the reason he did not buy a home in Gangnam.

In a video released on the YouTube channel GreeGura on the 18th, Kim Gu-ra candidly explained why he had stayed out of Gangnam while talking with the other guests about property investment.

Kim Gu-ra said, “There were several reasons I didn’t go to Gangnam,” adding, “One of them was that I wondered whether I could really keep earning this much money.”

He explained, “That’s how it is for entertainers. It might not matter if someone was already wealthy to begin with, but I wondered whether I could keep earning money and afford this.”

His anxiety about not being able to count on future income, rather than his immediate financial means, affected his decision to buy a home. The point was that he had no choice but to be cautious, since even if he took out a loan to buy a home in Gangnam, he would have to shoulder the repayments over a long period.

Kim Gu-ra currently lives in Siksa-dong, Ilsan, Goyang, Gyeonggi Province. He bought an apartment in the area in 2011 and has also disclosed that he previously purchased an apartment through a presale in Cheongna, Incheon.

Last May, he was also asked by his son Gree on MBC’s “Where Is My Home?” why he had not bought a home in Gangnam. Kim Gu-ra said he had made a profit by investing in stocks instead of real estate and drew attention when he showed his son his Samsung Electronics stock account.

Recently, Gree estimated that his father Kim Gu-ra’s net worth was in the range of 10 billion to 30 billion won. Kim Gu-ra, however, said, “Even my son doesn’t know the exact size of my assets,” and declined to give a specific figure.

Meanwhile, Kim Gu-ra recently pushed back against rumors that his son Gree was getting married. On the 16th, during an appearance on KBS Cool FM’s “Paul Kim’s Gayo Plaza,” he responded to reports that Gree was preparing to marry his longtime girlfriend, saying, “That’s not the case,” and explaining, “He’s not ready yet. It would be like asking a seventh-grader to prepare for college entrance exams.”

Gree had previously mentioned plans to marry his girlfriend, who had waited for him during his military service, on E Channel’s “Seo In-young’s Trendy Kkondae.” Kim Gu-ra said, “I called my son and told him, ‘It’s fine to be seeing your girlfriend, but I don’t think it looks good to talk about this so much,’” adding that he had cautioned him against letting talk of marriage get ahead of itself.
