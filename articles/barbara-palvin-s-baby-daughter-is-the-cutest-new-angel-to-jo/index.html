BarbaraPalvin is balancing new motherhood with her baby daughter as she makes her comeback to the fashion world with Victoria’s Secret. The model recently appeared in a video shared by Victoria’s Secret, giving fans a glimpse of her life beyond the runway. The brand also had a sweet message for Palvin as she prepares for her next appearance in Los Angeles.

Barbara Palvin’s baby daughter becomes the newest and cutest Angel to join the Victoria’s Secret family

Victoria’s Secret shared a video of Barbara Palvin backstage, wearing an oversized white shirt, black tights, gray leg warmers, and black heeled boots as she pushed her daughter Sylvie’s stroller. A pink “Angel on Board” sign on the stroller added a playful touch. Palvin also says, “I’m back, bitches,” before shushing her baby.

The sign gives Sylvie an adorable honorary place in the Victoria’s Secret Angel family. The brand also celebrated Palvin’s return with the caption, “Motherhood looks good on you, @realbarbarapalvin. So does the runway—see you in LA.” The message welcomes the model back to the runway while acknowledging her new role as a mother.

The pink sign gives Sylvie an adorable honorary place in the Victoria’s Secret Angel family. Palvin and her husband, Dylan Sprouse, welcomed their daughter in September. She is also preparing to return to the Victoria’s Secret runway for the 2026 fashion show in Los Angeles, weeks after becoming a mother.
