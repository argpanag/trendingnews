Walter Gretzky Honoured By Upper Deck and the Hockey Hall of Fame

TheTools · 06.11.2026 21:41 · source · permalink

Walter Gretzky would’ve been proud and, no doubt, humbled by a special ceremony on Nov. 6 at the Ford Performance Centre in Toronto. Many members of his family, including his grandchildren, were on hand to see Walter honoured by the Hockey Hall of Fame with the newly renamed ‘Walter Gretzky Upper Deck Hockey Resource Centre.’

The tribute celebrates Walter’s profound influence on his son Wayne Gretzky’s career and his love for collecting keepsakes throughout Wayne’s life and career, as well as the lasting impact of the Gretzky family on the sport of hockey.

To celebrate the Gretzky legacy, Upper Deck and the Hockey Hall of Fame have curated a mini collection, which can be seen by families in a non-ticketed area in the foyer in front of the entrance to the Hockey Hall of Fame in Toronto.

The exhibit will feature a special 1-of-1 Upper Deck Authenticated piece donated to honour Walter, which has been signed and inscribed by Wayne. The display will also include artifacts from the Hall of Fame archive that tell Wayne’s career story. The display will run through Nov. 22, 2026, at the Hockey Hall of Fame in downtown Toronto.

Upper Deck proudly announced that they have expanded their longstanding exclusive agreement with Wayne, naming the global icon as an Upper Deck Legacy Partner.

Gretzky joins Michael Jordan in receiving this elite distinction from Upper Deck, which includes exclusive rights to produce premium and highly sought-after autographed collectibles, including trading cards and memorabilia, featuring his authenticated signature and likeness.

At the press conference announcing the new name for the Hockey Hall of Fame Resource Centre, Wayne talked glowingly about his dad and the impact his father had not only on him but also on the hockey community at large. “My dad and my family mean everything to me, so the opportunity to honor him and showcase his impact to fans and collectors around the world is one that is close to my heart,” said Wayne. “I’m honored to be named an Upper Deck Legacy Partner by a company that has become like a second family to me over the decades we’ve worked together, and I’m looking forward to expanding this relationship for years to come.”

Upper Deck is marking the Legacy Partner distinction by introducing exclusive new collectibles, the “Upper Deck Master Collection – Wayne Gretzky” and a new Upper Deck Authenticated portfolio that will celebrate the story of Wayne from the beginning of his career to the end, and all the accomplishments in between.

“For decades, we’ve worked to capture the moments that defined Wayne Gretzky’s unparalleled career. His impact on the game of hockey is impossible to measure by records alone, so we’ve worked to create collectibles that tell the story of the mark he left on hockey,” said Jason Masherah, President of Upper Deck. “We’re honored to welcome Wayne as a Legacy Partner and celebrate the impact his family had on his life and career, as well as the impact Gretzky continues to have on hockey at all levels.”

The Upper Deck Master Collection – Wayne Gretzky, which is returning for the first time in 25 years, will pay tribute to his historic career accomplishments. The collection is numbered to 250 and is available exclusively on UpperDeckStore.com. The checklist spans Gretzky’s World Junior Championship, his incomparable NHL accolades, his Hockey Hall of Fame induction and his number being retired league-wide.

The collection will also include rare Wayne Gretzky autographs, memorabilia, and inscription cards, as well as dual/triple/quad autograph and memorabilia cards and relic booklets featuring legends from other sports. Additionally, three special boxes have been signed and inscribed by Gretzky, commemorating standout moments in his illustrious career.

“Wayne Gretzky’s impact on hockey extends far beyond records and championships, and we are honoured to celebrate his enduring influence on our sport,” said Jamie Dinsmore, President and CEO of the Hockey Hall of Fame. “This partnership with Upper Deck has given us a unique opportunity to recognize not only Wayne’s remarkable legacy, but also the role Walter Gretzky played in helping shape it. We are proud that Walter’s name will continue to be honored through the Walter Gretzky Upper Deck Hockey Resource Centre for generations to come.”

To learn more about Upper Deck’s relationship with Wayne Gretzky and stay up to date on products, please visit www.UpperDeck.com.
