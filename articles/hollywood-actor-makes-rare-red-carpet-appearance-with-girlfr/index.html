Hollywood actor makes rare red carpet appearance with girlfriend at global premiere

TheTools · 10.11.2026 22:12 · source · permalink

A Hollywood actor made a rare red carpet appearance with his girlfriend at the global premiere of his new film.

Sam Claflin was supported by actress Dar Zuzovsky, who he met while filming limited series Vanished last year, at the global premiere of Ebenezer in London’s Leicester Square on Monday.

The 40-year-old actor, who grew up in Norwich, plays Fred, Ebenezer Scrooge’s optimistic nephew, in the Christmas film.

It was a rare red carpet appearance for the couple, who have been together for 10 months, according to comments made by Ms Zuzovsky in July.

Claflin wore a jacket with a polka dot pattern and a white shirt. He also wore an orchid buttonhole, black Tom Ford trousers and smart shoes.

Dar Zuzovsky (left) and Sam Claflin arriving for the global premiere of Ebenezer, at the Odeon Luxe Leicester Square in central London. (Image: Ian West / PA)

Ms Zuzovsky, 35, wore a plunging gown with a slit up the leg and strappy nude heels.

The Israeli actress spoke about their relationship to Ynetglobal, saying they were still getting to know each other.

Dar Zuzovsky said: "Listen, I'm in love and I'm very happy with where things stand, but it's still early.

"Sam and I have been together for 10 months, and we're still learning each other. Whatever is meant to happen will happen."

Sam Claflin arriving for the global premiere of Ebenezer, at the Odeon Luxe Leicester Square in central London. (Image: Ian West / PA)

She also discussed the possibility of living in London, where Claflin is based and said the pair first became friends after meeting during filming. 

They stayed in contact after production ended and met again in New York.

Claflin was born in Ipswich, but grew up in Norwich and he went to Costessey High School and City College Norwich.

He was also a member of the prestigious arts course at Norwich Theatre Royal and he played a number of roles, including Fagin in Dodger.

He has since gone on to star in worldwide blockbusters, including the Hunger Games film series as Finnick Odair and Me Before You as William Traynor.

He also joined the cast of hit BBC drama Peaky Blinders in series five as British fascist politician Oswald Mosley

For his performance in Daisy Jones & the Six, he was nominated for a best actor prize at the 2024 Golden Globes.
