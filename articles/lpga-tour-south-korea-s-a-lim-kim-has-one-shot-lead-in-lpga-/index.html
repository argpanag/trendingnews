LPGA Tour: South Korea's A-Lim Kim has one-shot lead in LPGA finale as Nelly Korda closes in

South Korea's A-Lim Kim holds a slender lead going into the final round of the CME Group Tour Championship, with world number one Nelly Korda among the chasers.

Kim, who held a three-shot overnight lead, carded a third round 69 which included six birdies and three bogeys, with two of those coming on the final three holes, to reach 18 under overall.

"I really focused on my game. Just aim and hit, that's all," said Kim.

"Today the wind direction [was a] little tricky, changed a lot, swirly in the air. That's golf."

In the chase for the $4m (£3.1m) top prize at the season-ending event, Japan's Yuri Yoshida and Jeeno Thitikul of Thailand are both on 17 under - one shot behind Kim.

Korda was six shots off the lead before round three, but produced a superb 65 to move to 16 under.

The American, who will qualify for the LPGA Hall of Fame if she wins on Sunday, fired nine birdies in a seven-under-par round which also included two bogeys.

England's Charley Hull is on 14 under following a 69 as she carded four birdies and a bogey on Saturday around the Tiburon Golf Club in Florida.
