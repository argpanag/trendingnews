5.1-magnitude quake hits Off Coast of Peru - GFZ

An earthquake with a magnitude of 5.1 jolted Off Coast of Peru at 1951 GMT on Monday, the GFZ German Research Centre for Geosciences said, Xinhua reports.

The epicenter, with a depth of 27.4 km, was initially determined to be at 15.07 degrees south latitude and 76.40 degrees west longitude.
