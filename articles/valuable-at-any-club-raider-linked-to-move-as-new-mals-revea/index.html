'Valuable at any club': Raider linked to move as new 'Mals' revealed

TheTools · 15.09.2026 13:00 · source · permalink

The Canberra Raiders' night of nights anointed the crowning of two new "Mals" as speculation swirled about the future of another key player.

Raiders hooker Tom Starling has been linked with Manly after the emergence of Owen Pattie over the final 10 games of the season.

Starling's good mate Hudson Young's future is also up in the air after the NRL kiboshed the PNG Chiefs' lowball offer for the Canberra star.

Simi Sasagi's 2026 NRL season was so dominant he was able to miss eight games through injury and still be crowned the Mal Meninga Medallist at AIS Arena on Tuesday night.

Similarly, Raiders centre Leianne Tufuga claimed the NRLW Mal Meninga Medal after forming a potent combination with winger Mackenzie Wiki.

The pair's gongs ended Joe Tapine and Simaima Taufa's reign of terror. For now.

Tapine and Taufa have dominated the award, winning it the previous three years.

Raiders captain Tapine went close to making it four in a row though, finishing just two votes behind Sasagi (26 votes).

Sasagi scored seven tries, set up another 11 and led the Raiders for average run metres (174.9m) and post-contact metres (57.6m).

Tufuga scored four tries, set up another six and led the Green Machine for post-contact metres (561m) and tackle busts (44).

Ethan Sanders and Ellie Brander were named rookies of the year, and Ata Mariota and Sheridan Gallagher took home the coaches' awards.

The Raiders moved to lock in Starling before the November 1 deadline, when he's free to talk to other clubs.

But it's been rumoured the Sea Eagles were interested in luring the 28-year-old away from the capital.

He's got one year to run on his current contract and given the Green Machine has released Jayden Brailey to join Wests Tigers it would be reluctant to let Starling go early as well.

That would leave them light on at dummy half, despite Pattie's emergence.

Manly is on the hunt for a hooker after Lachlan Croker's retirement last year and the club is yet to announce Brandon Wakeham's re-signing, despite rumours of a two-year extension in June.

Wakeham's Sea Eagles contract runs out this year.

Former Raiders captain Jarrod Croker wasn't surprised the Sea Eagles were interested in Starling.

"Well, they lost Lachlan Croker and Brandon Wakeham [has] done a good job, but they've been hanging out for a nine," Croker said on SEN League.

"I mean, Owen Pattie, [it's] gonna be hard to get that jersey back off him the way they're going.

"Good person to have around the club, on and off the field, really good fella, Tommy. And a good player.

"So is Owen Pattie. It doesn't surprise me that little bit of gossip.

"He'd be valuable at any club, Tommy. He's tough as nails."

The Raiders want to meet Young this week, having offered him the richest deal in the club's history - a $3.3 million, three-year extension to keep him in lime green until at least 2030.

They see him as a future captain, but he had his head turned by the Chiefs, who have the advantage of offering tax-free contracts.

But the NRL said they still have to pay market value and at least offer Young $1 million a season.

They originally offered him $900,000 a year, but were willing to go up to $950,000.
