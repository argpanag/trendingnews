Abbe Hae, 15 others spend night in lockup after giving statements at Kota Bharu police headquarters

TheTools · 18.10.2026 18:51 · source · permalink

Abbe Hae, whose real name is Mohd Azhan Mohd Adnan, is the founder of Pertubuhan Jejak Jalanan.

KOTA BHARU – Community activist Abbe Hae claims he and 15 other individuals were detained after appearing to give statements at the Kota Bharu District Police Headquarters (IPD) on Saturday, turning what he described as an effort to help the community into an overnight stay in a lockup.

Abbe Hae, whose real name is Mohd Azhan Mohd Adnan, is the founder of Pertubuhan Jejak Jalanan.

He claimed that he and the 15 others also underwent an identification parade and fingerprinting before being released on Sunday.

In a Facebook post on Sunday, Abbe Hae said they arrived at the Kota Bharu police headquarters at about 3pm on Saturday to give statements in connection with a police report which he claimed had been lodged by three individuals who employed foreign nationals.

“Yesterday afternoon, after celebrating the success of three of my foster children from Machang in obtaining their MyKid identity cards, the others and I rushed to the Kota Bharu police headquarters after being called to give statements. I arrived at 3pm.

“After my statement was taken, I was supposed to go to Jalan Pantai Cahaya Bulan (PCB) to help an elderly person who was living in a very small, roofed shelter without walls,” he said.

However, he claimed that he ended up spending the night in the lockup alongside the others who had also been called in to give statements.

Abbe Hae said those with him included Datuk Mizan from I Sejahtera Malaysia, Tengku Harith, who manages funeral arrangements in Pasir Puteh, and Ayahanda Paduka Abe Aziz, founder of the Manzilu Rahmah moral and drug rehabilitation centre.

He claimed the identification parade was conducted behind a glass partition, with the individuals lined up for identification.

Abbe Hae expressed disappointment over the experience but stressed that he did not blame the police officers involved.

Instead, he thanked the criminal investigation officers at the Kota Bharu district police headquarters for treating them well during their time there.

He also urged the public not to criticise the officers, saying they were merely carrying out the instructions given to them.
