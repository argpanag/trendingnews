A cool-down is finally arriving soon

TheTools · 19.09.2026 21:25 · source · permalink

QUINCY (WGEM) - After a pretty warm stretch during September, cooler temperatures are finally on the horizon. We will see a few stray showers and thunderstorms arrive tonight, with lows falling into the upper 60s in the northern half of the Tri-States and low 70s in the southern half. Then, Sunday will have a pretty wide range in temperatures across the Tri-State area.

Alon the northern tier of counties, such as Scotland, Clark (MO) Lee (IA) and Hancock, McDonough (IL), highs will only top out in the mid 70s. The further southeast you go, the warmer temperatures will get. Quincy and the rest of the central tier of counties will reach into the upper 70s and low 80s. The southwestern corner such as Shelby, Monroe, and Ralls counties (MO) will reach into the low-mid 80s while the southeast corner like Pike, Scott (IL) could see mid-upper 80s. A cold front will stall out pretty much through the middle of the Tri-States, which will cause this tight gradient in high temperatures.

We will also see some more scattered showers and thunderstorms to end off the weekend, staying pretty spotty in nature. Most of the Tri-States wont see much in terms of precipitation, just up to a quarter of an inch. But, some of us could get lucky with a stronger storms that dumps over half an inch of rainfall for some. These few stronger storms could cause some minor flooding, if enough rain continuously fall over the same areas. Some showers and storms will linger into the overnight as the cold front moves through the region.

This cold front will help cool us down tremendously, with Monday’s highs temperatures forecast to top out in the low-mid 60s. A few showers will linger into Monday as well, but nothing widespread. Highs in the 60s and lows in the 50s are in the forecast through Wednesday, then highs will sit around 70° through the rest of the work week. A few select areas along the northern tier could fall into the 40s Tuesday night, which will be the coolest night of the week.
