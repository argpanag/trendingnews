Red Bull recovery sends warning for 2027 | Grandprix.com

TheTools · 11.10.2026 15:32 · source · permalink

News October 11, 2026

Red Bull's transformation from early-season struggler to Singapore favorite has done more than rescue Max Verstappen's 2026 campaign. It has given the rest of Formula 1 a reason to look nervously toward 2027.

Verstappen arrived in Singapore after winning at Sepang, then took Sprint pole, won the Sprint and secured pole position for Sunday's Grand Prix. It was his 50th Formula 1 pole and another confirmation that the recovery is no longer tied to one type of circuit.

When you see where we stood at the start of the season, Verstappen said. This is very important for next year. You see that some teams are still hesitant about certain aspects of new updates, whereas everything is working well for us. The fact that everything turned out well is very positive.

The shift followed a substantial upgrade introduced in Baku. Oscar Piastri described it as clearly big, while Lewis Hamilton said Red Bull had done a fantastic job and put itself back at the front.

Team principal Laurent Mekies sees the range of circuits as the more significant evidence.

What gives us hope is that these are three quite different racetracks, yet we were competitive and capable of winning every time, he said.

McLaren team principal Andrea Stella said the unusually high rate of development in 2026 had allowed competitive order to move rapidly. In his assessment, Red Bull has recently executed that work better than anyone.

Red Bull probably have done the best job more recently in delivering the upgrades and obviously when Max has the material to perform, certainly he doesn't hesitate and delivers, Stella told reporters after qualifying.

McLaren is not conceding the development race. Stella said its Baku update was not expected to produce one spectacular leap, but established the basis for further parts arriving from Austin through the final races.

Hopefully once the full package is delivered, [we will be able] to compete for the victory, he said.

The turnaround is particularly sharp because Verstappen has admitted that the first part of the season was difficult enough to make him think seriously about retirement. Now he is also looking toward Red Bull's new wind tunnel.

The most important thing for next year is that we can use the new wind tunnel, he said.

Mercedes team principal Toto Wolff acknowledged the scale of the recovery.

You have to take your hat off for the way they come back again and again, Wolff said. He's clearly dominant at the moment. It seems to be the best package on the grid at the moment.

Red Bull still has to prove that the performance survives the winter and the next development cycle. For the moment, however, a season that began as damage limitation is ending as an early warning.
