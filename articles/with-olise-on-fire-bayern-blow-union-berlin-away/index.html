With Olise on fire, Bayern blow Union Berlin away!

Olise and Kane put on a show!

On Friday night, Bayern Munich treated themselves to a real festival away at Union Berlin (0-7) on Matchday 4 of the Bundesliga.

OneFootball Videos

The match got underway with a superb goal from Jamal Musiala (18th) before Harry Kane and Michael Olise stole the spotlight.

The England striker first bagged a record 100th Bundesliga goal (39th), then Michael Olise unleashed an absolutely sublime strike into Union’s top corner (43rd).

After the break, the duo kept shining, with the Frenchman setting up the Englishman (55th).

Introduced from the bench at half-time, Ismaël Saibari then got in on the act with a sublime goal of his own, skipping past three defenders before finishing brilliantly (70th).

Finally, Michael Olise put the seal on his masterpiece by completing his hat-trick with two more magnificent goals in the space of three minutes (73rd, 76th).

Thanks to this win, Bayern Munich temporarily move back to the top of the Bundesliga table while waiting for the results of Freiburg and Dortmund.

For their part, Union Berlin suffered their heaviest defeat since returning to the Bundesliga and remain stuck in 16th place with one point.
