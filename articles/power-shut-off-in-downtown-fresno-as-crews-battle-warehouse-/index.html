FRESNO, Calif. (FOX26) — A large warehouse fire broke out in downtown Fresno at H Street and Calaveras Street, prompting officials to cut power to the surrounding area.

Details about what sparked the fire were not immediately available.

Officials also had not released information on whether anyone was injured or the extent of the damage.

The fire spread from one building to another, and insulation and wind contributed to the fire’s movement according to a Fresno Fire Chief.

This is an active and ongoing investigation and we will continue to update the story as we learn more.
