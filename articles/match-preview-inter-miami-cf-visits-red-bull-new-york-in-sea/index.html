MATCH PREVIEW: Inter Miami CF Visits Red Bull New York in Search of Fourth Straight Win | Inter Miami CF

TheTools · 24.10.2026 23:03 · source · permalink

Inter Miami CF (15W-5L-10D, 55 points) carries on with MLS regular season action on the road, with the team heading to Harrison, New Jersey to take on Red Bull New York (10W-14L-6D, 36 points) this Saturday, Oct. 24. Kick off at Sports Illustrated Stadium is slated for 5:30 p.m. ET.

Where to Watch

Fans around the world can tune in to the action live on Apple TV.

Past Match

Inter Miami visits Red Bull New York in search of a fourth consecutive victory. Last time out, Inter Miami earned a 1-3 win on the road over Atlanta United FC. A brace from captain Leo Messi and a strike from Germán Berterame powered Inter Miami to victory.

Inter Miami CF This Regular Season

Overall, Inter Miami has recorded 15 wins, five losses, and 10 draws so far this MLS regular season for a total 55 points and sits second in the Eastern Conference standings. Notably, Inter Miami has been the top goal-scoring team in MLS this season with 73 total goals in 30 regular season games, nine more than the next highest-scoring side.

Messi, the reigning MLS Landon Donovan MVP Award and Golden Boot presented by Audi winner, leads both the team and the league in goals with 25. Additionally, our captain is tied for the lead for most assists this regular season with 15.

Previously Against Red Bull New York

Saturday's meeting presents the 14th matchup against Red Bull New York in Club history. The series is even with six wins per side, while another encounter resulted in a draw.

Inter Miami will aim to extend its unbeaten run against the New York side to five after recording three wins and a draw in the most recent matchups. In the first meeting between the sides this regular season back in April, with Inter Miami and RBNY playing out a 2-2 draw at Nu Stadium

Scouting Red Bull New York 

The New York club hosts Inter Miami after falling in its past two regular season matches. In all, Red Bull New York has recorded 10 wins, 14 losses, and six draws for a total 36 points and is ninth in the Eastern Conference table. 

Julian Hall has been the team's top contributor in attack so far this regular season, with the 18-year-old forward tallying 11 goals and five goals in 29 appearances.
