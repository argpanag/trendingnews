TheTools · 01.11.2026 21:07 · source · permalink

From another chance for Kimi Antonelli to wrap up the Drivers’ Championship to Lewis Hamilton looking to convert his first Ferrari pole position, here are a few things to keep an eye on as drivers face the test of racing at high altitude at the Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez.

1. Antonelli’s requirements for championship glory

A week ago we had to run through a set of permutations for Kimi Antonelli to win the Drivers’ Championship that highlighted how tough it would be for him to do it in Austin.

On that occasion, he needed a number of other results to go his way even if he won the race, and it very nearly came together, but both George Russell and Lewis Hamilton finished one place higher than Antonelli needed them to.
