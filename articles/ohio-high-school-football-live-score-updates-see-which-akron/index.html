Week 4 of the Ohio high school football regular season gets a 24-hour head start for several Akron-area teams.

Five games featuring Greater Akron teams will kick off at 7 p.m. Thursday, Sept. 10. Undefeated Wadsworth and Copley will both look to improve to 4-0.

Several big games also dot Friday's schedule, including Walsh Jesuit (3-0) hosting 2-1 Cleveland St. Ignatius, Nordonia (3-0) playing at Suburban League National Conference rival Highland (2-1), Norton (3-0) traveling to Streetsboro (2-1) for a Metro Athletic Conference showdown and Hudson (3-0) hosting North Royalton (3-0).
