Rybakina moves closer to No. 1 ranking with straight-sets win at US Open

TheTools · 04.09.2026 02:58 · source · permalink

Kazakhstan’s Elena Rybakina moved a step closer to becoming the world’s No. 1 player for the first time with a straight-sets victory over Jessica Bouzas Maneiro on Thursday night to reach the third round of the US Open.

Rybakina won 6-2, 6-4 in 1 hour, 8 minutes and is now three wins away from claiming the top spot. She needs to reach the semifinals in New York to guarantee herself the No. 1 ranking at the end of the tournament.
