Chiang Wan-an leads Puma Shen in tight Taipei mayoral race

TheTools · 28.11.2026 10:02 · source · permalink

AI-translated article with transliterated names. Read original Chinese article here.

Voting in Taiwan’s “nine-in-one” local elections closed at 4pm on Saturday (28 November), with vote counting getting under way across the island. The Taipei mayoral race is closely contested, with incumbent Chiang Wan-an of the Kuomintang, who is seeking re-election, holding an early lead over Democratic Progressive Party candidate Puma Shen.

According to TVBS News, Chiang Wan-an had secured 87,953 votes and Puma Shen 72,289 as of 4.30pm. However, SET News reported that Chiang had received 116,852 votes and Shen 101,568.

The gap between the two candidates continued to shift as polling stations released their counts.
