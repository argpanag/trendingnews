Liverpool's transfer plans should change after release clause revelation

TheTools · 21.11.2026 12:00 · source · permalink

James Brooke

Liverpool are expected to head back into the market to sign a new right winger next summer after failing to properly replace Mohamed Salah.

The Reds entertained offers for Cody Gakpo, almost selling the Dutchman to Manchester City, but pulled the plug once it became clear that neither Ismaila Sarr nor Yankuba Minteh were attainable.

Although Gakpo has started the season well, there is a feeling that Liverpool could look to sell him in 2027, before going in for a new wide man to rotate with Bradley Barcola, Victor Munoz and Rio Ngumoha.

Michael Olise would be a dream target, but after the revelation that Jamal Musiala has a release clause active in his contract next summer, Liverpool should switch strategy. Instead of adding a new winger, they should take the chance to snare Musiala and work out how to have him dovetail with Florian Wirtz.

Musiala’s release clause could be an opportunity

Liverpool have been linked with Musiala many times in the past, but with the German starring for Bayern Munich, a move never really felt possible. This time, things could be different.

The 23-year-old has been struggling with a neurological issue this season, and widely criticised comments from Bayern chairman Karl-Heinz Rummenigge this week have suggested that the German champions are looking for a way to cut Musiala’s salary after months of inaction.

“We would have to speak with his family, the player himself and his agency,” Rummenigge said. “Because, in that situation, those figures would simply no longer be financially justifiable, with all due respect.”

According to Sky Germany, Musiala has a release clause which becomes active next summer, and that coupled with Rummenigge’s comments and the stance of the Bayern board, should alert potential suitors across Europe.

Musiala has not had an easy couple of years, with an ankle fracture ruining much of last season for him, and health problems blighting him during the early part of this term. However, he made his comeback on Friday night, playing the final 20 minutes of Bayern’s 1-1 draw with Koln, and if he really is on the market, then Liverpool have to be involved.

Where could Musiala fit at Liverpool?

The obvious question to answer here is one of exactly how Liverpool could hope to fit Wirtz and Musiala in the same team at all, let alone alongside Alexander Isak, Bradley Barcola and their other midfield and forward talents.

While it is unclear what the answer could be immediately, sometimes players are that good that you have to pull the trigger first and ask questions later. Musiala would improve the Liverpool squad, no matter if he was in the XI every week, and when those kinds of talents are there to be bought, the smart thing to do is bring as many in as possible.

In the meantime, Liverpool should be keeping a close eye on how Jurgen Klopp chooses to fit Musiala and Wirtz into his Germany team. Wirtz has been the main man for Klopp so far, but if Musiala stays fit he will surely be in the next squad in March. The former Chelsea prospect is too good to be left out and Klopp will know it.

If the former Liverpool boss can get his two star attacking midfielders performing together in the same team, then the Reds should be taking note. It will be a different matter with the likes of Isak and Barcola to consider as well, but it should not be impossible.

There have been no details of the value of Musiala’s release clause yet published, but if it is somewhere in the £70-80m region, Liverpool should abandon their plans for a new right winger and put their efforts into bringing in a player who could be genuinely transformative.
