Catriona Gray heads to Victoria's Secret Fashion Show as Philippine special guest

TheTools · 18.10.2026 22:59 · source · permalink

MANILA, Philippines — The Victoria's Secret Fashion Show (VSFS) this year will be graced by one of the Philippines' most beloved beauty queens.

Miss Universe 2018 Catriona Gray confirmed on social media that she will attend the 2026 VSFS as a "special guest representing the Philippines."

The beauty queen-turned-host shared a social card by the local arm of Victoria's Secret announcing her participation at the New York event, which will stream live via YouTube on October 19, 7:30 a.m. (Philippine time).

Catriona further teased her involvement at this year's show by reviving her blonde Halloween costume from a couple of years ago, where she wore a pink lace bodysuit and large feather wings.

"Wings up, we're inbound," she wrote in the post's caption, naturally accompanied by the wings emoji.

A post shared by Catriona Gray (@catriona_gray)

Fellow beauty queens expressed their support for Catriona in the comments section including CJ Opiaza, Chelsea Manalo, Anita Rose Gomez, Krishnah Gravidez, Precious Lara Quigaman, Vanessa Pulgarin and good friend Nicole Cordoves.

The 2026 VSFS will take place in Los Angeles — in Sony Pictures Studios — for the first time in almost two decades.

The all-female line-up performers this year are Megan Thee Stallion, Megan Moroney, Tate McRae, and Katseye, the latter who will be without Manon Bannerman and Filipina leader Sophia Laforteza as both continue their temporary hiatus from the group.

Models Gigi Hadid, Adriana Lima, Candice Swanpoel, Ashley Graham, Imaan Hammam, Anok Yai, Devyn Garcia, Paloma Elsesser, Abby Champion, Alex Consani, Grace Elizabeth, Precious Lee, Liu Wen, Joan Smalls and Amelai Grey are confirmed to grace the show.
