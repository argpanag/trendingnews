FAQ: How to watch Wednesday’s Canadiens-Capitals game

TheTools · 14.10.2026 10:00 · source · permalink

Wednesday NHL games are exclusive to Amazon, and you can't get around that by switching to RDS.

On Wednesday, the Canadiens will play their first game of the season broadcast exclusively on Amazon Prime, against the Capitals in Washington at 7:30 p.m. And unlike the Amazon games on Mondays last season, Wednesday evening games this season (and for the foreseeable future) will be exclusive to Amazon in both languages, meaning you can’t get around it by switching to RDS.
