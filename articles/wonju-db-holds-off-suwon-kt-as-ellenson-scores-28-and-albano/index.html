Wonju DB holds off Suwon KT as Ellenson scores 28 and Albano adds 20

TheTools · 13.11.2026 12:11 · source · permalink

Wonju DB held off a late charge from Suwon KT and secured the win thanks to strong performances from Henry Ellenson and Eason Albano.

Wonju DB defeated Suwon KT 94-83 on the home court at the Wonju DB Promy Arena on Mar. 13 in the 2026-2027 KCC Professional Basketball regular season Round 2.

DB, escaping a losing streak, moved to 8-6 for the season. By contrast, KT, which had won three straight before dropping two in a row, failed to shift the momentum.

Back from injury, Ellenson posted a double-double with 28 points and 10 rebounds for DB. Albano also helped the victory with 20 points and nine assists.

For KT, Pariv Bass scored 22 points and grabbed eight rebounds, and Tray Porter fought with 14 points and 14 rebounds, but they could not prevent the loss.

DB got off to a strong start. In the first quarter, Lee Yu-jin hit a 3-pointer and scored on a fast break to seize the initiative. Albano completed a three-point play, and substitute Ellenson added points under the basket. DB closed the first quarter up 23-17.

DB kept control in the second quarter. Ellenson and Chinanu Onuaku Hammenz attacked KT's rim, and DB also scored from the perimeter. KT raised its shooting percentage but could not cut the deficit. At halftime, DB led 51-41.

KT's comeback began in the third quarter. Starting with Jeong Chang-young's 3-pointer, Porter and Bass followed with consecutive scores. Han Hee-won and Kang Sung-wook also contributed from outside to narrow the gap. DB held on with points from Ellenson and Albano and finished the third quarter up 69-66.

Early in the fourth quarter, KT flipped the game. Moon Jeong-hyun's 3-pointer tied it at 69-69, and Jeong Chang-young then stole the ball and scored on a fast break to put KT ahead 71-69.

DB quickly regained momentum. Jeong Hyo-geun scored five straight points to retake the lead, and Albano carried the scoring in the decisive moments.

After Jeong Hyo-geun's 3-pointer and Lee Yu-jin's free throws widened the margin, Albano's 3-pointer about a minute before the end pushed DB ahead 91-81.

KT continued to chase until the final whistle but could not overturn the result. DB protected the lead for the remaining time and finished the game 94-83.
