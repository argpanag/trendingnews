Costa Rica 2-0 Haiti: Mora and Soto save the 'ticos' from relegation

TheTools · 04.10.2026 21:00 · source · permalink

La Sele started with authority and quickly found the way to victory thanks to the goals of Carlos Mora and Andrey Soto to save himself from descent. The Ticos took advantage of their opportunities in the first half, while the Granaderos tried to react without being able to overcome the Costa Rican defense.

Concacaf Nations League · Matchday 4 - Grupo A, Ligue A · Ricardo Saprissa Aymá, San José
