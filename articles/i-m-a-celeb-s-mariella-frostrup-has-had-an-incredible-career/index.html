I'm A Celeb's Mariella Frostrup has had an incredible career - now she has a big point to prove in the jungle

TheTools · 17.11.2026 21:40 · source · permalink

The prestige broadcaster is this year's oldest campmate

Christmas is fast approaching and that can only mean one thing – we’re heading back to the I’m A Celebjungle.

We have a whole new group of celebrities to get acquainted with, as they compete in gruelling bushtucker trials and try to survive the perils of campsite life. Joining the likes of Wayne Rooney and Chloe Burrows in the star-studded cast is broadcaster Mariella Frostup.

She’ll be swapping her decades-long career as a high-flying to journalist for rice and beans around a campfire. Although, before heading into the jungle, she admitted that’s excited for a break from the news cycle. ‘In current times, it might be nice to be spared the headlines,’ she said.

Who is Mariella – and what is she best known for? Here’s what we know about the I’m A Celeb star.

Who is Mariella Frostup?

Mariella Frostup is a broadcaster, journalist and campaigner.

Starting out in music PR in the 80s and 90s, Mariella made her first TV debut in Channel 4’s Big World. Since then, she’s made a career hosting arts, culture, and current affairs programmes, including a 20-year stint hosting BBC Radio 4’s. Open Book.

As a journalist, she’s written for numerous major titles, and served as The Observer’s agony aunt in her ‘Dear Mariella’ column. She bagged the accolade of being voted the sexiest voice on television in 2005. These days, she’s also a regular panellist on ITV’s Loose Women.

Why is going on I’m A Celeb, you ask? ‘A good 50% of the reason that I am doing this is I am constantly telling other women we should grab our chances,’ she said before heading to the jungle.  ‘We shouldn’t allow ourselves to be written off at a certain age. So, I think this is me having a taste of my own medicine! I am putting myself through something I am frightened of.’

How old is Mariella Frostup and where is she from?

Born on 12 November 1962, Mariella Frostrup is 64 years old. She was born in Oslo, Norway, but moved to Ireland as a child, and lived in London from the age of 16.

Mariella Frostup’s menopause campaigning explained

Mariella has campaigned heavily to raise awareness of perimenopause and menopause. She co-chairs the group Menopause Mandate, which successfully pushed for menopause to be included as a topic in the NHS Over-40 Health Check and helped campaign for more affordable HRT.

She’s also written books on the subject, including 'Cracking the Menopause: While Keeping Yourself Together', and 'Menolicious: Eat Your Way to a Better Menopause'.

Who is Mariella Frostup’s husband?

Mariella is married to human rights lawyer and international justice campaigner, Jason McCue. The couple met on a charity trek in Nepal and tied the knot two years later. They live near Bruton in Somerset and have two children.

She was previously married to Richard Jobson, the lead singer of the punk rock group Skids.

Who are Mariella Frostup’s children?

Molly Mae, 22, and Dan, 21, are Mariella and her husband’s two children.

Mariella has previously shared her experience of having children, after struggling to conceive. ‘I was incredibly lucky, but it was my dream to have these two amazing characters come into my life,’ she said.

Does Mariella Frostup have Instagram?

She does indeed – you can follow what Mariella's up at @mariella_frostup.
