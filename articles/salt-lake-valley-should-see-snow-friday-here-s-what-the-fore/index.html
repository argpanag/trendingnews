Salt Lake Valley should see snow Friday. Here’s what the forecast says.

TheTools · 22.11.2026 02:59 · source · permalink

Salt Lake Valley residents are likely to see a fair amount of snow falling Friday, according to the National Weather Service.

A “minor” snowstorm should deposit 1 to 4 inches in the valley during the day Friday, meteorologist Julie Cunningham, with the National Weather Service, said Thursday afternoon.

After rain Thursday night, Cunningham said, a cold front would move through the valley, causing the rain to taper off and the snow to fall — likely after the Friday morning commute.

The Wasatch Mountains, and the Wasatch Back, are likely to see more snow Friday — as much as 8 inches in Park City, Cunningham said.

After Friday’s snowfall, Cunningham said, the weekend is expected to be drier. A brief high pressure system will move through northern Utah, meaning partly cloudy skies Saturday and Sunday. There is a slight possibility of more snow Sunday night, going into Monday.

The quick snowstorm that hit during Tuesday evening’s commute prompted the Garland Fire Department to post on Facebook a somewhat snarky reminder to drivers about being careful in hazardous weather conditions: “Snow is falling, and so are our expectations for Utah drivers. Prove us wrong. Please.”

With Thanksgiving travel coming next week, the American Red Cross’ Utah/Nevada offices sent out information Thursday about being prepared for emergencies on the road.

The Red Cross recommends keeping emergency supplies, with water, high-protein snacks, a first-aid kit, a flashlight, extra batteries, jumper cables, a cellphone charger, a map and an emergency contact card. Drivers should also check their tires, battery, fluid levels and windshield wipers before heading out, the Red Cross advised.
