The Phoenix Suns kick off Thursday’s double header with a matchup against the injury riddled Orlando Magic.

After losing their last three games in a row, the Phoenix Suns have fallen out of the playoff picture, sitting two games back from the Denver Nuggets for the last play-in spot in the West.

With the Orlando Magic on deck, the Suns will have a great opportunity to get back in the win column by taking advantage of a defense that struggles to contest shot attempts at multiple levels of the court.

Even though there are just two games tipping off on Thursday night, there are still a wide range of prop bets and markets to groom through which you can read a detailed breakdown of them below.

Best Betting Promos and Sportsbooks for NBA Prop Bets

Find the best promos and sportsbooks to use for today’s NBA prop bets.

Orlando Magic vs Phoenix Suns (9:00pm, EST)

Devin Booker Over 24.5 Points (-124) at FanDuel Sportsbook

Since the start of the regular season, Devin Booker has been able to generate high quality shot attempts at a consistent rate, averaging 32.0 Points per game while shooting 35.0% from three and 48.8% from the floor.

Even when surrounded by an underwhelming supporting cast, Booker has been able to keep the Suns afloat, leading an offense that ranks above league average in Offensive Efficiency.

Heading into Thursday night’s contest against the Orlando Magic, Booker will be able to continue to score at a high rate by attacking a defense that ranks near dead last in Adjusted Defensive Rating.

With the Magic struggling to shift their coverage across the court, the width of Booker’s lanes in the middle will widen, making it easier for the Suns guard to slash to the rim and generate a high quality shot attempt in the interior.

Wendell Carter Jr. Over 8.5 Rebounds (-118) at FanDuel Sportsbook

While the Magic have been dealing with injuries, Wendell Carter Jr. has been able to capitalize on the extra space in the interior, hauling in 8.9 Rebounds per game.

With Miles Bridges pulling Franz Wagner out to the perimeter, Carter will be able to anchor in the paint for the full duration of the contest, leading to an uptick in his total number of Rebound Opportunities.

Miami Heat vs Golden State Warriors (10:00pm, EST)

Giannis Antetokounmpo Over 41.5 Points+Rebounds (-112) at FanDuel Sportsbook

In his first year with Miami, Giannis Antetokounmpo has been able to play at a very high level, averaging 28.4 Points while hauling in 12.3 Rebounds per game.

With the offense running through the former two time MVP, the Heat have been efficient, climbing into the top half of the board in Net Rating and in Three Point Completion Percentage.

In a favorable matchup against the undersized Golden State Warriors, Antetokounmpo will be able to continue to stuff the stat sheet while blitzing through the middle of a defense that ranks in the bottom ten in Opposing Effective Field Goal Percentage.

For some more exposure, escalator bet Antetokounmpo's Points+Rebounds prop by placing smaller wagers on his alternate overs.

Golden State Warriors Team Total Over 121.5 (-110) at BetMGM Sportsbook

While the Heat have been competitive on offense, they have struggled to defend the rim on the other end of the court, limping into Thursday ranked 28th in Opposing True Shooting Percentage.

Miami’s lack of production on defense brings in a high amount of variance into their contests, making it difficult to pull away when matched up against a high quality offense.

Unfortunately for the Heat, their struggles on defense are poised to persist against the Warriors, battling it out against an offense that ranks fifth in Adjusted Offensive Rating.

Especially with Stephen Curry stretching out Miami’s coverage with his gravity around the three point line, giving his secondary scorers more room to work with in the middle which will improve the quality of their shot attempts near the rim.

Stephen Curry Over 3.5 Three Pointers Made (-154) at FanDuel Sportsbook

Speaking of Curry, the future hall of famer has been sensational from three, averaging 4.5 Threes per game while shooting 42.9% from deep.

With the Heat stretched out thin around the perimeter while Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo are on the floor together, Curry will be able to pick apart their gaps in coverage and generate a higher volume of shot attempts from three to help clear the over on his Three Pointers Made prop.
