India top Group B after 1-0 win over Maldives

TheTools · 11.11.2026 18:33 · source · permalink

India finished top of Group B in the SAFF Championship with a 1-0 victory over the Maldives at the National Stadium on Wednesday, completing their group-stage campaign with two wins from as many matches.

Having beaten Pakistan 3-2 in their opening fixture, India made seven changes to their starting XI but still had enough quality to dominate possession against a cautious Maldives side.

The Blue Tigers threatened early when Brison Fernandes played Suhail Ahmad Bhat through on goal in the fourth minute. However, the forward dragged his effort wide after cutting inside.

India continued to control proceedings but struggled to find a way through the Maldivian defence. The breakthrough finally arrived in the 39th minute when Abdul Rabeeh was brought down inside the box after weaving past a couple of defenders.

Suhail stepped up to convert the resulting penalty, sending the goalkeeper the wrong way to score his third goal of the tournament.

The Maldives offered some resistance, particularly from set-pieces, but India remained largely comfortable at the back. Chinglensana Singh dealt with a dangerous delivery in the 32nd minute, while goalkeeper Priyansh Dubey ensured India retained their advantage with a series of important interventions.

Despite enjoying spells of possession after the break, the Maldives threatened more frequently in the closing stages as India began to lose some of their defensive concentration.

Dubey was called into action in the 79th minute, tipping Ali Hamdhaan's free-kick over the bar, before making another good save to deny substitute Muzdhaan Hassan from the edge of the box five minutes later. Irfan then failed to capitalise on a promising opportunity, heading Khaiminthang Lhungdim's cross over the bar.

The Maldives continued to push for an equaliser, with Dubey producing his most crucial intervention in stoppage time when he bravely blocked Ibrahim Zaki's close-range effort.

India enjoyed 55 per cent possession and managed eight shots, with two on target, while the Maldives registered seven attempts, four of which were on target but failed to produce a goal.
