George Russell eyes Lewis Hamilton heroics as Brazil grid penalty confirmed

TheTools · 06.11.2026 09:40 · source · permalink

George Russell will have plenty of overtaking to do on Sunday in Interlagos.

George Russell will take a five-place grid penalty at this weekend's São Paulo Grand Prix after Mercedes opted to change the internal combustion engine on his car.

The change is a standard ICE replacement, drawn from the car's seasonal hardware pool and built to match its previously homologated specification. 

An ICE change of this kind involves replacing the V6 block along with its core mechanical components, including the cylinder heads, pistons, crankshaft and valvetrain, while other elements such as the turbocharger or motor generator units can be retained separately. 

Exceeding a driver's seasonal allocation of any power unit element automatically triggers a grid penalty under F1's cost-containment rules.

Russell addressed the penalty in a Mercedes Instagram story, pointing to the timing of the change.

"I had to take a grid penalty at some point from Singapore until the end of the season," said Russell. 

"We felt like this was a good place to take it. Lewis [Hamilton] had a big grid penalty here a number of years ago and did pretty well. Five places doesn't seem that bad, and we'll try and come back through."

Lewis Hamilton took a far heavier hit at Interlagos in 2021, dropping to tenth on the grid for Mercedes before fighting through the field to beat Max Verstappen on lap 59 and win the race.

Russell's penalty is considerably smaller, but the Brit is clearly hoping for a similar story at the same venue.
