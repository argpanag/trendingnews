Is Ciena Still Cheap on Cash Flow?

The Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) approach estimates what Ciena is worth based on the cash it can generate for shareholders. For the latest twelve months, Ciena produced free cash flow of about $857.3 million, and the 2 Stage Free Cash Flow to Equity model assumes that this cash flow is growing rather than shrinking over time. On that basis, the intrinsic value estimate comes out at about $657 per share.

This DCF value is around 41.7% above the current share price, which indicates that Ciena stock may screen as undervalued on this cash flow view. Recent commentary around ongoing supply constraints helps explain why the market may be reluctant to fully reflect the long term cash flow potential that the model is capturing.

Overall, the discounted cash flow work suggests Ciena appears undervalued relative to the cash the business is expected to generate.
