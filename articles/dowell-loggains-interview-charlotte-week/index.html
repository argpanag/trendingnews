DOWELL LOGGAINS INTERVIEW: CHARLOTTE WEEK

TheTools · 19.09.2026 22:45 · source · permalink

Appalachian State returns home to face Charlotte with a big road win in hand and a 3-0 start in the crosshairs.

Appalachian State head coach Dowell Loggains is an unapologetic fan of in-state matchups and shares his insights on this week's game against Charlotte.

The Niners are 0-2 but with weapons that demand attention, particularly on offense, as Loggains notes.  Defensively, CLT is struggling and while opportunities exist, Loggains will continue to emphasize Mountaineer consistency of preparation, both on and off the field.

Preseason night practices, especially during fall camp, begin to pay dividends this week with a 6:00 PM kickoff scheduled.
