Live: Virgin Atlantic A330 Returns To Origin 40 Minutes Into 8-Hour JFK Flight

TheTools · 18.09.2026 14:37 · source · permalink

* Last updated Friday, September 18, 2026, 11:22 AM EST by Josh Eyre *

Aircraft Entered A Short Holding Pattern After Departure

The playback from our flight tracker shows the aircraft entering a short holding pattern after departure before returning to land.

Virgin Atlantic Confirms Technical Issue

Friday, September 18, 2026, 11:10 AM EST

Responding to a request for comment by Simple Flying, a spokesperson for Virgin confirmed that "VS127 returned to Manchester following a minor technical issue, which has now been resolved." They added that "the safety and security of our customers and crew is always our top priority, and the aircraft will refuel before continuing its journey. We sincerely apologise for the delay and any inconvenience caused."

Safely On The Ground

Friday, September 18, 2026, 10:50 AM EST

Around 40 minutes after takeoff, the aircraft, which was operating flight VS127 and bears the registration G-VSXY and the name 'Beauty Queen,' is safely back on the ground in Manchester. It approached from the northeast following a TUI flight from Alicante and an easyJet flight from Lisbon, both of which had also circled near Ashton-in-Makerfield on the way. Simple Flying has contacted Virgin for comment.

A Virgin Atlantic Airbus A330-300 is squawking 7700 shortly after departing Manchester Airport (MAN) bound for New York JFK Airport (JFK). The aircraft appears to have entered a hold ahead of a return to Manchester. Many Airbus A330-300s are unable to dump fuel, so must fly in order to burn fuel and bring their weight down ahead of landing.
