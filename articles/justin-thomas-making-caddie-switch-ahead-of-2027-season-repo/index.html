Justin Thomas making caddie switch ahead of 2027 season: Report

TheTools · 24.11.2026 20:10 · source · permalink

After a winless season on Tour, Justin Thomas has reportedly parted ways with caddie Matt Minister ahead of his 2027 campaign.

It marks the second time in almost four years that Thomas has made a change on the bag, bringing in Minister ahead of the 2024 Masters. Their partnership yielded no championships though, as Thomas hoisted the RBC Heritage trophy in April 2025 with Joe Greiner filling in while Minister was sidelined with an injury.

According to a report from Golfweek, Thomas has tabbed Michael Cromie as Minister’s replacement. Cromie recently filled in for Ted Scott during Scottie Scheffler’s win at the 2025 BMW Championship.

Those expecting to find Cromie walking alongside JT at this week’s Skins Game will need to hit pause on that expectation though.

Thomas is having friend Brad Gehl carry this week, according to the report. It is unknown who Thomas will utilize at the Hero World Challenge the following week, as well as who will carry his clubs when he plays alongside his dad, Mike, at the PNC Championship.

The 16-time PGA Tour winner returned from offseason back surgery at the Arnold Palmer Invitational in March and went on to make 17 of 18 cuts in 2026.

A captain’s pick for the U.S. Presidents Cup team at Medinah in September, Thomas finished 43rd in the FedExCup standings after posting just two finishes inside the top 10.

Those two highlights came in big moments. In just his second start back, Thomas finished T-4 at the Players Championship and followed that up with a T-4 at the PGA Championship at Aronimink later in the spring. That result marked Thomas’ first finish inside the top 5 of a major since his triumph in Tulsa in 2022, his lone win with Jim “Bones” Mackay as his caddie.

Thomas won 14 of his Tour titles, including his first PGA Championship in 2017 and the FedExCup, with Jimmy Johnson. From Johnson, Thomas made his way through Mackay and Minister and now is set up with Cromie.

In addition to working with Scheffler, Cromie most recently carried Chris Kirk’s bag before the two split earlier in 2026.
