Nico O’Reilly is “100% available”, according to Enzo Maresca.

Jeremy Doku is closing in on a return from a calf injury, although the Manchester derby is expected to come too soon.

Ben White is “fine”, with Mikel Arteta reporting no fresh injury concerns following Arsenal’s midweek victory over Napoli.

Cristhian Mosquera is nearing a return. Arteta said: “Let’s see if he can make this game or the next one. He should be available around that time.”

Joe Rodon is expected to miss eight to ten weeks with the hamstring injury he sustained in Gameweek 2. He remains Leeds’ only absentee.

Mykhailo Mudryk is expected to miss six to eight weeks after sustaining an ankle injury in training.

Christian Nørgaard remains sidelined for Everton.

Luke Shaw will be assessed after the left-back was not risked during Thursday’s UEFA Champions League victory over Sabah.

Cody Gakpo remains a doubt. Andoni Iraola said: “He didn’t train yesterday. We will try with him today and see if he’s ready for tomorrow or if we need to wait for the next one.”
