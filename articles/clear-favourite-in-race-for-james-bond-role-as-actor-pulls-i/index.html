‘Clear favourite’ in race for James Bond role as actor ‘pulls into the lead'

TheTools · 28.10.2026 22:28 · source · permalink

THE race to become the next James Bond will soon be over – and one actor is thought to be a clear favourite for the role.

Final screen tests took place at Bray Studios in London over the weekend, with Jack Lowden, Callum Turner, Josh O’Connor, and a fourth unnamed actor, auditioning to be the seventh 007.

And it is Lowden who gets the nod from fans of the long-running franchise, with the Scottish actor given odds of 8/11 to win the coveted role.

Elsewhere, Turner is his closest competitor at 2/1, while the unfancied O’Connor gets less favourable odds of 8/1, with all other contenders priced at 16/1 or more.

Nicola McGeady of Ladbrokes said: “The Bond race looks to be reaching crunch time and punters are firmly backing Jack Lowden to get the call.

“He’s already odds-on and the latest speculation has only fuelled support for him, with Callum Turner looking like his biggest threat.”

Two British actors, both previously linked with the role, have been ruled out as the final candidate – widely thought to be Jack Barton. 

“We’ve heard, reliably so, that Harris Dickinson and Nicholas Hoult are not the 4th person. Testing is said to be occurring today and going into the weekend,” read an article shared by The Hollywood Reporter on Friday.

Australian star Jacob Elordi is also out the running, along with the much-fancied Idris Elba, Theo James and James Norton.

Instead, Amazon MGM Studios are said to be keen on the lesser known Barton, 31, whose past screen credits include the popular Netflix drama Heartstopper.

The coveted role has been up for grabs following the release of 007 blockbuster No Time To Die in 2021, Daniel Craig’s fifth and final outing as the Martini-swilling secret agent.

Other than a handful of other minor TV roles, little else is known about the young actor, but he reportedly impressed producers enough to earn critical screen tests alongside his three Bond rivals.

Despite Barton’s emergence, fans remain convinced they have already worked out who the final star in the James Bond race is – and Taylor Swift fans will be very familiar with him.

The latest Bond theory suggests that the singer’s ex-boyfriend Joe Alwyn, 35, who she dated for six years until 2023, is the secret actor.

Fans believe the link to the actor and the 007 role is Bond casting director Nina Gold, 59.

Taking to Reddit to discuss, a fan wrote: “Nina Gold was one of the casting directors for Thor, too, and cast Joe in his first movie role. She knows him as far back as drama school”

Agreeing this person penned: “Nina Gold is doing the casting and knows Joe and cast him in the brutalist and Hamnet so he’s known by the department and may be on the radar of the casting team”

A third added: “Yeah, and he is supposed to be filming in NYC for a Rom com. but was in London this week for a friend’s wedding and potentially this audition.”

A fourth agreed: “Never thought about him as a contender before, but it makes sense.”

Daniel Craig’s fifth and final outing as James Bond was five years ago, and since then a whole host of high-profile actors have been linked to the famous franchise.

But Amazon MGM Studios, who paid $20million for full creative control of the franchise, is said to be picking a more “alternative” actor, rather than a mainstream Hollywood star.
