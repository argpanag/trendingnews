Vivo launches X Fold 6, V80, S2 FE, OriginOS 7 and its mixed reality headset in India | Photo Credit: Special Arrangement

Vivo on Tuesday (October 6, 2026) launched Vivo V80, Vivo X Fold 6, Vivo S2 FE, OriginOS 7, and a preview of its mixed reality (MR) headset, the Vivo Vision Discovery Edition, in India.

The Chinese smartphone player will be competing with Samsung, Google and now Apple in the foldable space with its third generation folding phone.

Vivo V80 has a 6.59 inch 1.5K OLED display with a 144 Hz refresh rate and up to 5,000 nits peak brightness. It runs on a 7200 mAh battery, paired with 90W charger inside the box. It is IP68 and IP69 rated for dust and water resistance.

Vivo V80 features a 50 MP main, a 50 MP ZEISS telephoto camera with OIS and 3X optical zoom, and an 8 MP ultrawide lens. It has a 50 MP selfie camera as well.

Vivo V80 ships with Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 with 8 GB RAM and up to 512 GB storage. It is also first V-series smartphone to feature Vivo’s new operating skin, OriginOS 7 out of the box. It will get 6 years of security updates.

Vivo V80 comes in Sunrise Anthem, Horizon Blue, and Stellar Black, starting at ₹62,999 for the 8GB+128GB variant, ₹69,999 for the 8GB+256GB, and ₹79,999 for the 8GB+512GB variant. It will be available from October 13, across all partner retail stores, Vivo official website, Flipkart, and Amazon.

Vivo X Fold 6 has an 8.02 inch main display and a 6.51 inch over display, supporting 1–120 Hz adaptive refresh rates, and up to 5,000 nits peak brightness.

Vivo X Fold 6 comes with a 6900 mAh battery, supported by 80W FlashCharge and 40W Wireless FlashCharge. It is IPX8, IPX9, IP5X rated for water, dust, and freeze resistance.

Vivo X Fold 6 uses Dimensity 9500 Super Edition processor and comes in single format of 12 GB/512 GB. It also operates on OriginOS 7 out of the box.

Vivo X Fold 6 comes with a 200 MP ZEISS main camera, a 50 MP telephoto lens with 3x optical zoom and a 50 MP ultrawide lens. It gets dual 20 MP front camera on main and cover screen.

Available in Nebula Teal and Prism Black, vivo X Fold6 is priced at ₹2,19,999 for the 12GB + 512GB variant. It will also be available from October 13, across the Vivo India e-store, Flipkart, Amazon and all partner retail stores.

Vivo S2 FE runs on a 10,000 mAh battery with reverse charging feature. It has a 6.57 inch 1.5K AMOLED display with a 2,000 nits peak brightness and a 120 Hz refresh rate.

Vivo S2 FE ships with MediaTek Dimensity 7300e processor. It is IP68 and IP69 rated as well for dust and water resistance. It operates on OriginOS 6 based on Android 16 out of the box.

Vivo S2 FE sports a 50 MP rear and a 32 MP front camera.

Vivo S2 FE comes in Stellar Green, Zen White and Titan Black shades, starting at ₹38,999 (6GB/128GB) and ₹42,999 (8GB/128GB). It will go on sale starting October 20, across partner retail stores, Vivo official website, Flipkart and Amazon.

Vivo Vision Discovery Edition holds an optimised form factor with natural eye and hand-based controls. The headset weighs around 398 grams, offers four sizes of light seal and eight foam padding options for fit. The MR headset has 8K dual binocular micro-OLED displays and run on the Snapdragon XR2+ Gen 2 platform.
