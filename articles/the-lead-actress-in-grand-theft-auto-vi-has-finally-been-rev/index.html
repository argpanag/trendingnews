Manni L. Perez once said her "biggest fear" was playing a "damsel in distress." Her character in the year's most-anticipated game is anything but helpless.

There are a lot of things players can do in Grand Theft Auto VI that weren't possible in previous editions of the franchise. One of the most notable is that they can play as Lucia, the first major female protagonist in the history of the series. 

Iconic female main characters have been a staple of video games from the beginning (see: Ms. Pac-Man, Samus Aran, etc.), but the presence of a complex, fully fleshed-out woman at the core of GTA 6's story represents a significant change for a franchise that has been accused in the past of treating female side characters as equal parts eye candy and cannon fodder. 

In the lead-up to the game's release, most of its sprawling cast of voice actors had remained a mystery. That's still true of nearly all of its main characters. But the voice behind Lucia was seemingly confirmed on Tuesday when the Game Awards announced that Manni L. Perez had been nominated for Best Performance for her voice work in the game. GTA 6 received eight nominations in total, including Game of the Year.

The revelation came as little surprise to die-hard GTA fans, who had speculated for months that the little-known American actor was the voice behind one half of the game's core duo. GTA 6's story centers around Lucia and her boyfriend Jason, a pair of outlaws trying to make their mark in a cartoonishly criminal knockoff of Miami called Vice City. The actor who plays Jason is still unknown.

Who is Manni L. Perez?

The limited information we have about Perez's personal bio comes from her own recent social media statements and a few dated news stories. 

While deftly dodging a deluge of questions from GTA fans asking her to confirm her role in the game on Instagram Live last month, she described herself as having grown up in a "small, rural, rednecky hick town" roughly three hours from New York, where she now lives. "We grew up very lower middle class," she added. She said she has family in Harlem and is reportedly of Puerto Rican and Dominican descent. 

According to a 2018 interview with Paz Magazine, Perez studied at New York University's prestigious Tisch School of the Arts. She said she initially wanted to pursue a career as a professional belly dancer before being convinced to try acting by a college professor. Perez is also an avid weightlifter and has practiced a variety of martial arts, including Muay Thai and Brazilian jiu-jitsu.

"I have a very specific niche in the industry," she told the magazine. "A lot of the roles I get called in for require me to be fit, strong, and kick ass."

Perez has played a series of minor TV roles in recent years, including guest appearances on Jessica Jones, Chicago P.D. and Law & Order: Special Victims Unit.

On Instagram, she told her followers that her modest acting success so far hasn't yet fundamentally changed her life. 

"I live in a small studio apartment, which I love," she said. "I have my tiny, little itty-bitty kitchen, and it's just me and my dog. I do everything on my own, and I've done everything on my own for a very long time, and I love it, and I'm very grateful."

GTA 6 is her breakout role, but it isn't the first work she's done with the franchise. She was credited as the voice of Clubber #2 in Grand Theft Auto V and as an unnamed blackjack dealer in a 2019 update to Grand Theft Auto Online.

One comment Perez made to Paz Magazine eight years ago turned out to be remarkably prescient about the role that would turn into a major star in the video game world. 

"To be honest, my biggest fear is to play a woman that needs to be saved by a man," she said. "Like, I can't imagine playing the damsel in distress. … I'd prefer to play a character that, whatever she's going through, no matter how hard society wants to keep her down, she picks herself up, puts her big girl panties on (preferably cotton) and keeps it moving."

What do we know about the rest of the cast?

Very little. Fans have speculated that Lucia's partner, Jason Duval, is played by actor Dylan Rourke, who's appeared in Grey's Anatomy and Modern Family, but Rockstar has not confirmed that. More rumors abound about the other actors who voice the game's key characters, but so far only one other cast member has been officially announced. 

Celebrated comedic actor Stephen Root, who recently won an Emmy for his supporting role on Widow's Bay, has confirmed that he'll play a criminal named Brian Heder, who GTA 6's character guide describes as "a classic drug runner from the golden age of smuggling."
