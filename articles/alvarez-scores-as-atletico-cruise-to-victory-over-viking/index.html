Alvarez scores as Atletico cruise to victory over Viking

TheTools · 25.11.2026 22:18 · source · permalink
  • Report (active)
  • Scores
  • Tables
  • Line-ups

Alvarez has now scored twice in Europe this season

Goals from Alexander Sorloth and Julian Alvarez saw Atletico Madrid comfortably see off Norwegian side Viking FK.

Diego Simeone's side secured a timely second win in the Champions League this season to boost their hopes of reaching the knockout stages.

Atletico took the led in the 19th minute when Sorloth lashed in from a good pull back from Alejandro Grimaldo.

Their lead was doubled just five minutes later when Alvarez got his third of the season with a whipping free-kick.

Viking keeper Ľubomir Belko thought he had kept it out but the goal was given with it clear he pushed it back after it had crossed the line.

Atletico were comfortable throughout as they picked up their second win of the European campaign to move on to seven points and 18th in the table.

Viking, who finished with 10 men after defender Henrik Falchener was sent off in stoppage time, are 27th on four points.
