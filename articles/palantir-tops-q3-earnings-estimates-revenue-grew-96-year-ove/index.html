Palantir Technologies (PLTR) released its third quarter earnings results, beating both forecasts on the top and bottom lines. The stock is getting a lift in after-hours trading.

Market Domination Overtime's Jake Conley breaks down the takeaways from the earnings release, including its US commercial business growth.

Pateer third quarter earnings crossing the wire moments ago. Let me get you those numbers, diving in here. Q3 revenue coming in at 2.313 billion, growing by 96% year-on-year. That beat analyst estimates of $2.17 billion. Third quarter adjusted EPS coming in at 44 cents. Analysts have been looking for 41 cents. That's also a beat. Let's take a look at the revenue segments here. US commercial coming in at $984 million for the quarter versus estimates of 937 million. And US government revenue, Pateer reporting at $953 million, also surpassing analyst estimates. That marks the first quarter on record for Pateer that US commercial revenue has surpassed the US government business. It's another massive beat for Pateer on this quarter. Stock is moving up in after hours trading.
