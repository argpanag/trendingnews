Margarita Mix-Up Leaves Chili's Customers With Burns

TheTools · 01.11.2026 17:54 · source · permalink

A round of margaritas at a Chili's in North Carolina had an unintentional kick that left five people seeking medical attention. Health officials say three customers at the Forest City location suffered chemical burns in and around their mouths after sipping drinks made with a granular cleaning product that was mistaken for sugar, reports USA Today. Two employees who then tasted the drinks suffered similar symptoms, and all five went to urgent care centers.

Investigators say a power-washing contractor had left the degreaser in an unlabeled container that was later used at the margarita station. "It's almost like a little tray where they put water, lime juice," says Megan Rollins of the Foothills Health District, per the New York Times. "And then on the other side of the tray is a place to put salt or sugar." The health district briefly pulled the restaurant's food service permit, then reinstated it after a cleaning and inspection, per WRAL. No information was available on the severity of the injuries.
