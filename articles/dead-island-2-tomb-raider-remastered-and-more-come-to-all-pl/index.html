Dead Island 2, Tomb Raider Remastered and More Come to All PlayStation Plus Subscribers in November - CNET

TheTools · 28.10.2026 20:51 · source · permalink

Keep spooky season going a little longer with these PlayStation Plus games in November.

Senior writer

Zachary McAuliffe

Senior writer

Edited by Moe Long

October 28, 2026, 4:51 pm ET

Halloween traditionally marks the last day of spooky season, but PlayStation Plus subscribers can keep the ghoulish, monstrous vibes going right through November. On Nov. 3, all subscribers will be able to play Dead Island 2, Monster Train and a plethora of Tomb Raider games.

PlayStation Plus, which is Sony’s version of Xbox Game Pass, offers a large, constantly expanding library of games. Subscribers can choose from the Essential, Extra and Premium tiers, each with unique perks and benefits. Starting at $11 per month, the plans give subscribers access to monthly games and rewards, and each month, all subscribers can access a handful of games at no additional charge.

Here are the games all PlayStation Plus subscribers can play starting on Nov. 3.

More from CNET

Dead Island 2

The city of angels is more like the city of the dead in this first-person zombie survival game. You’ll fight the undead around LA, take on quests and meet quirky characters along the way. This sun-drenched, pulpy game is a bloody good time.

Monster Train 2

The monstrous deck-building roguelite is back with more clans, playstyles and monsters to fight with. You’ll be commanding clans of demons and former angels to oust powerful beings known as Titans who have seized heaven. To defeat them, you’ll have to journey through hell, heaven and beyond in order to bring them down before they destroy the world.

Tomb Raider 4, 5 and 6 Remastered

This collection of games includes remastered versions of Tomb Raider: The Last Revelation, Tomb Raider: Chronicles and Tomb Raider: The Angel of Darkness. You still play as the archeologist/adventurer Lara Croft as she uncovers mysteries and prophecies from the ancient world and today.
