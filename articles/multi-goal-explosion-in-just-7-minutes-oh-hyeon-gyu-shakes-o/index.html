'Multi-goal explosion in just 7 minutes' Oh Hyeon-gyu shakes off bench struggles with 'involvement in all 3 goals'... Besiktas defeats Hoffenheim 3-2, secures 2nd straight UEL win | STARNEWS

South Korea national team forward Oh Hyeon-gyu (25, Besiktas) led his team to victory in European competition, contributing to all three of his side's goals, including a brace.

On the 16th (Korean time), Oh Hyeon-gyu started and recorded two goals and one assist in an away match against Hoffenheim (Germany) at the SNP Arena in Germany for the second round of the league phase of the 2026-2027 UEFA Europa League (UEL). Besiktas (Turkey) won 3-2, extending their winning streak to two matches in the tournament.

The first attacking point came in the 15th minute of the second half. In a left corner kick situation, Oh Hyeon-gyu's right-footed shot connected with Amir Murillo at the front of the goal, and Murillo finished it off to score the opening goal. UEFA officially recorded this scene as an assist for Oh Hyeon-gyu.

Although Besiktas conceded an equalizer to Fisnik Asllani in the 23rd minute of the second half, the savior was Oh Hyeon-gyu. Oh Hyeon-gyu scored two goals within seven minutes to decide the match. In a counter-attack situation in the 27th minute of the second half, he received a penetrating pass from Junior Olaitan and shook the net with a right-footed shot.

In the 34th minute of the second half, he finished off a ground cross from Ilhan Palu coming in from the right with a sliding shot to widen the score gap. Oh Hyeon-gyu, who took off his shirt for a celebration immediately after his second goal, received a yellow card.

Besiktas conceded a chasing goal to Matsuda Goki in the 43rd minute of the second half but held onto their one-goal lead until the end without any further concessions.

Oh Hyeon-gyu, whose playing time had been reduced due to competition for a starting spot with this season's signing Dusan Vlahovic, created an opportunity for a turnaround with his performance on this day. Additionally, he increased his attacking points in official matches this season to three goals and three assists (one assist in the regular league, three goals and two assists in the UEL).
