10 key NFL players on new teams in 2026: Myles Garrett, A.J. Brown and more

TheTools · 10.09.2026 23:00 · source · permalink

Between trades and free agency, there were some major offseason moves that could shape the 2026 season.

From Myles Garrett and A.J. Brown to Mike Evans and Trey Hendrickson, here are some of the biggest NFL stars with new teams heading into the 2026 season.

A new NFL season is almost here, and it will feature some familiar faces in new places.

There was plenty of player movement across free agency and trades this offseason. Between veteran quarterbacks, star wide receivers and the reigning NFL Defensive Player of the Year, fans will need some time to get used to some players' new teams.

Before the 2026 season kicks off with the Seattle Seahawks hosting the New England Patriots in a Super Bowl LX rematch on NBC and Peacock, here's a refresher on some of the biggest acquisitions of the offseason.

Myles Garrett, EDGE, Los Angeles Rams

The Los Angeles Rams are all-in ... again. The team added five NFL Defensive Player of the Year awards to its defense this offseason: two from a new face and three from a returning franchise legend.

Los Angeles landed Myles Garrett in a blockbuster trade with the Cleveland Browns in July. Pro Bowl edge rusher Jared Verse, a 2027 first-round pick, a 2028 second-round pick and a 2029 third-round pick went to Cleveland, while the Rams got Garrett, who set the NFL single-season record with 23 sacks in 2025.

Joining Garrett on the Rams' defensive line is Aaron Donald, who came out of retirement to make a run at another Super Bowl at SoFi Stadium in February.

Somehow, that's not all for the Rams. The team also bolstered its secondary by acquiring star cornerback Trent McDuffie in a deal with the Kansas City Chiefs, who compiled four draft picks.

A.J. Brown, WR, New England Patriots

It took until June 1, but A.J. Brown finally became a New England Patriot.

The reigning AFC champions reunited the wideout with head coach Mike Vrabel, who coached Brown with the Tennessee Titans. The Philadelphia Eagles got a 2028 first-round pick and a 2027 fifth-round pick for Brown, who will now pair with quarterback Drake Maye.

The Patriots overhauled their wide receiver corps this offseason by bringing in Brown and free agent Romeo Doubs, while letting Stefon Diggs walk in free agency and trading Kayshon Boutte to the Houston Texans.

Jaylen Waddle, WR, Denver Broncos

Another AFC finalist traded a first-rounder for an upgrade in the passing game.

The Denver Broncos added a speedster at receiver in a deal with the Miami Dolphins, who acquired the No. 30 overall selection in the 2026 draft and additional picks. Miami selected Jaylen Waddle sixth overall in the 2021 draft.

Mike Evans, WR, San Francisco 49ers

Mike Evans moved from Tampa Bay to the Bay Area this offseason.

The veteran wide receiver joined the San Francisco 49ers in free agency after spending his first 12 NFL seasons with the Tampa Bay Buccaneers. His 11-season stretch of 1,000-yard seasons came to an end last year after he suffered a broken clavicle in October.

DJ Moore, WR, Buffalo Bills

Rounding out the run on receivers is Josh Allen's new No. 1 target.

The Buffalo Bills shipped a second-round pick to the Chicago Bears for DJ Moore and a fifth-rounder. Moore, who has not missed a regular season game since 2020, will look to add some stability to the Bills' receiving room.

Dexter Lawrence II, DT, Cincinnati Bengals

The Cincinnati Bengals had one of the NFL's worst defenses in 2025, and they paid a heavy price to improve it for 2026.

The team brought in three-time Pro Bowl defensive tackle Dexter Lawrence II from the New York Giants in exchange for the No. 10 overall pick in the 2026 draft. Lawrence is an immediate improvement for Cincinnati's interior defensive line, but it still left a void on the edge ...

Trey Hendrickson, EDGE, Baltimore Ravens

The Baltimore Ravens backed out of their failed Maxx Crosby trade with the Las Vegas Raiders and pivoted to a former division rival on the defensive line.

The team signed veteran edge rusher Trey Hendrickson to a lucrative, four-year deal just one day after the Crosby swap fell through. Hendrickson played just seven games in 2025 after undergoing season-ending core muscle surgery, but he was the NFL's sack leader in 2024 with 17.5.

Kyler Murray, QB, Minnesota Vikings

The Arizona Cardinals are paying a pretty penny for Kyler Murray to play ... in Minnesota.

The former No. 1 overall pick signed with the Vikings early in free agency and was named the starter over J.J. McCarthy before the preseason began. He has a $36.8 million salary for 2026, with the Vikings paying just $1.3 million and the Cardinals giving the rest.

Murray, who played just five games in 2025, will be throwing to wideouts Justin Jefferson and Jordan Addison as Minnesota looks to keep pace in a competitive NFC North.

Kirk Cousins, QB, Las Vegas Raiders

Kirk Cousins is in Sin City after two seasons in Atlanta.

The 38-year old was named the Raiders' starting quarterback, but the question is for how long. He is holding the position while No. 1 overall draft pick Fernando Mendoza sits in waiting.

Like the Vikings, the Raiders face an uphill battle in a crowded AFC West.

Tua Tagovailoa, QB, Atlanta Falcons

After six seasons with the Dolphins, Tua Tagovailoa joined fellow left-hander Michael Penix Jr. in the Falcons' QB room.

Similar to Murray and the Cardinals, the Dolphins are paying a fortune for Tagovailoa to be on another team. Miami has a historic and NFL-leading $182.7 million in dead cap space for 2026, with $55.4 million of it attached to Tagovailoa's departure.

Tagovailoa was named the Falcons' starting quarterback for their Week 1 game against the Pittsburgh Steelers, but it remains to be seen if he will keep the starting role once Penix is healthy.
