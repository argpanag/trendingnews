Fernandes puts Manchester United contract talks on 'hold'

Bruno Fernandes has revealed that he put contract talks with Manchester United on hold after being offered a new deal.

Speaking to Sky Sports ahead of the match against Liverpool, the United captain explained: “No, there’s no negotiations ongoing, but I’ve seen people saying that he’s asked for more money, he’s asked for more years. If I wanted more money, I wouldn’t be here. I had somewhere to go to get much more money than being at Manchester United. I could have been in a place that is much warmer than Manchester.

“Two summers ago, three summers ago, last summer, I always had the chance to leave and I always chose to be here. The thing is, I’ve always showed how much I care about the club and how much I want to win at this club. And I always said, in the moment the club thinks it’s time for me to move on, I will move on.

“I’m not going to be the player that I’ve seen many in this time at Manchester United. They’re going to stay here just by staying, just finish my contract and then we will see. In the moment the club says, ‘Bruno, it’s time to part ways’, I’m going to be the first one walking out the door.

“Yeah, the club offered me a new contract, so I think that, from their side, shows that they want me to stay. And as you said, there are no negotiations going on.

“We spoke about the contract at the end of last season, we came in the beginning of this season speaking about the contract, but about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to a side because I don’t think it’s the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations because the most important thing now is that the club gets back to win games.”

Fernandes’ current deal expires at the end of the season, but Manchester United have the option to extend it by another year.

How things stand in the Premier League

Aston Villa were held to a goalless draw at home by Sunderland. The point was enough to see the visitors move above Manchester United in the Premier League table.

And in Manchester, a second half-goal from Elliot Anderson saw Manchester City earn a 1-1 draw against Fulham.

On the south coast, Evanilson and Justin Kluivert both scored as Bournemouth eased past Nottingham Forest.

The Tottenham head coach Roberto De Zerbi compared Luca Williams-Barnett to Lamine Yamal following his heroics against Ipswich.

He said: “Luca is a special player. I’m not surprised because his talent is bigger than you think. He played very well. He’s lucky because he’s playing in a top club with top team-mates.

“I decided to play today with Luca from 10 days and in my plan, it was [James] Maddison and Luca very close to speak the same language, to play together and the experience of Maddison I thought could be important.

“The behaviour of Maddison could help him to play with freedom. And Thursday, I spoke with Luca and I asked him two things. Just two things. To play like he plays on the park with his friends, with the same freedom.

“And to play and to work hard without the ball, like [Ousmane] Dembele, like Raphinha, like [Lamine] Yamal, because football is going in a different way and the first defenders are the attackers.

“Even if they are Ballon d’Or winners, or any way the players with talent, they have to work on the pitch.”

In London, 18-year-old Luca Williams-Barnett scored and provided an assist for Tottenham in a 2-0 win against Ipswich Town.

Speaking after his first match as Crystal Palace manager, Thomas Frank said: “This was definitely clear this was the Premier League. The tempo, intensity, the mad moments and so on.

“It wasn’t the result we hoped for. I think what I’d praise the players for was the intensity, the willingness, the running power, the effort in every aspect. Satisfied we scored two good goals and created good chances.

“The first half we were a bit too open. The second half much more in control. We limited Coventry to basically their goal - which we should have avoided as well.

“I felt in the second half it was us closer to scoring the goal than Coventry.”

Coventry City played out a thrilling 3-2 win over Crystal Palace in Thomas Frank’s first game in charge of the Eagles.

After their 4-1 defeat to Leeds United, the Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso admitted his team are in a “fragile” moment.

He said: “We are in a fragile moment mentally. When we receive a punch we didn’t recover. The fans were disappointed because the expectations are high. I understand their disappointment. There were moments that we were not competing at the level required.”

Elsewhere, Leeds United stunned Chelsea to earn a 4-1 win at Stamford Bridge.

Arsenal suffered a blow during the match against Newcastle when goalkeeper David Raya was forced off due to injury.

Mikel Arteta said: “Obviously it’s not good David had to come off but at the moment I don’t know [the prognosis]. He will have an MRI tomorrow. We will have more information tomorrow. But we know how important David is to us.”
