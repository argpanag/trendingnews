Overwatch Announces Sombra’s New Support Rework At BlizzCon 2026

TheTools · 12.09.2026 18:07 · source · permalink

Overwatch is giving two of its heroes a major shake-up, with Blizzard officially announcing reworks for Sombra and Roadhog during BlizzCon 2026's opening ceremony. Both heroes are getting new roles and identities as part of the game's next big update, with the reworks arriving in Season 5 on October 6.

The biggest change is Sombra, who is leaving her DPS days behind to become a Support hero. Blizzard is transforming her from a damage-focused recon specialist into a Support technician. This gives her a completely different role within an Overwatch team.

Sombra's rework will see her abilities and gameplay adjusted around supporting her teammates rather than just hunting down enemy targets. It's a pretty significant departure for a hero who has spent years making everyone's life slightly more annoying from the other side of the map.

Sombra's last major rework was in October 2024, with the launch of Season 13, roughly a year after her previous overhaul in October 2023. So, she's now getting another major rework about two years after her last one, continuing a pretty unusually frequent cycle of redesigns.

Let's Not Forget Roadhog Too

Roadhog, meanwhile, is getting a rework focused on making him feel more like the Tank he's supposed to be. Blizzard says the changes are designed to finally make Roadhog feel like a Tank that supports his team.

That distinction is important for a hero who has historically been very good at surviving on his own. His rework aims to push him toward a more team-oriented Tank identity while retaining the personality that makes Roadhog, well, Roadhog.

During my interview with Aaron Keller last month, we talked about the possibility of reworks being announced at BlizzCon, so I'm happy to see that come to fruition - especially since these are two heroes I personally felt were overdue for a rework. I am so ready for Support Sombra.
