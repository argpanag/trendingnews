LIST: Pump price rollbacks set Tuesday, Sept. 29, 2026

TheTools · 28.09.2026 15:14 · source · permalink

Motorists will get a reprieve this week, as fuel retailers on Monday announced pump price rollbacks after three straight weeks of major hikes.

Below are the price adjustments announced by oil firms so far:

Petro Gazz

Gasoline: - P0.24

Diesel: - P7.57

Seaoil Philippines Corp.

  • Gasoline: - P0.24
  • Diesel: - P7.57
  • Kerosene: - P5.85

Petron Corp. / Shell Pilipinas Corp.

  • Gasoline: - P0.30
  • Diesel: - P7.60
  • Kerosene: - P5.90

Unioil Petroleum Philippines Inc.

  • Gasoline: - P0.30
  • Diesel: - P7.60

The adjustments will take effect at 6 a.m. on Tuesday, September 29, 2026.

Other firms have yet to make similar announcements for the week.

Oil industry sources earlier projected rollbacks this week, as oil prices declined on hopes of diplomacy over the Iran war during the United Nations General Assembly meeting, and easing supply concerns due to the recovery of shipments from Saudi Arabia.

Firms last week raised gasoline prices by P4.80 to P4.88 per liter, diesel by P8.80 to P8.82 per liter, and kerosene by P6.40 to P6.47 per liter, bringing year-to-date adjustments to a net increase of P69.90 per liter for gasoline, P73.68 for diesel, and P64.54 for kerosene.

President Ferdinand “Bongbong” Marcos Jr. last Friday ordered the suspension of excise tax collection on liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) and kerosene amid high crude oil prices in the international market and renewed tensions in the Middle East.—AOL/RF, GMA News
