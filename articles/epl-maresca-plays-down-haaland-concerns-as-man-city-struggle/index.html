EPL: Maresca plays down Haaland concerns as Man City struggle

TheTools · 27.11.2026 15:42 · source · permalink

Man City boss Enzo Maresca insists there is no problem with Erling Haaland despite a dip in form and a poor run of results ahead of Sunday's Arsenal clash.

Manchester City's Norwegian striker #09 Erling Haaland heads the ball during the English Premier League football match between Manchester City and Coventry City at the Etihad Stadium in Manchester, north west England, on September 5, 2026.

Manchester City manager Enzo Maresca has backed Erling Haaland amid the striker’s recent dip in form, insisting that there is no problem with the Norwegian despite the club’s poor run of results, The Independent reports on Friday. Haaland has scored just once in his last five matches, while City have managed only two victories in their last seven games across all competitions.

The striker appeared unhappy during a post-match interview after City’s 2-2 draw with Napoli in the Champions League on Tuesday, prompting questions about his mood and the team’s confidence. However, speaking to the press ahead of City’s next fixture, Maresca said Haaland’s frustration was a natural response to not scoring or winning.
