Report: Mayfield, Bucs strike 3-year, $165M extension | theScore

TheTools · 08.09.2026 21:42 · source · permalink

The Tampa Bay Buccaneers and quarterback Baker Mayfield agreed on a three-year extension Tuesday, sources told ESPN's Adam Schefter and NFL Network's Ian Rapoport.

The deal is worth about $55 million per season, according to NFL Network's Tom Pelissero.

The two sides discussed a new contract during the offseason, but talks broke off, putting the veteran passer on track to become a free agent in 2027.

Mayfield voiced his frustrations at training camp, saying he expected another long-term deal after the Bucs told him he was their franchise quarterback.

The 31-year-old has earned two Pro Bowl berths since joining Tampa Bay in 2023 while averaging 4,079 yards and 32 touchdowns with 12 interceptions per season.

The Bucs won consecutive NFC South titles in Mayfield's first two campaigns with the team but missed the playoffs entirely in 2025.
