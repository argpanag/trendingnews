Chelsea vs. AFC Bournemouth - Head-to-head record

Across 25 competitive meetings in all competitions, Chelsea hold the historical advantage with 15 victories, five draws and five defeats against AFC Bournemouth. At Stamford Bridge, the Blues have won eight of 13 encounters, with Bournemouth claiming three wins alongside two draws.

Recent Premier League history reflects an extended unbeaten run for the London club, with Chelsea avoiding defeat in their last nine top-flight meetings with the Cherries. Bournemouth have not beaten Chelsea since a 1-0 away triumph in December 2019.

However, the dynamic has tightened considerably over the past two seasons. Each of the last three league meetings between the clubs ended level, including consecutive 2-2 draws at Stamford Bridge and a scoreless stalemate at the Vitality Stadium.

Expect another closely contested London battle where small margins decide the outcome.

Chelsea - Searching for some consistency

Chelsea return from the international break seeking defensive composure under Xabi Alonso following an erratic opening month of domestic action. Below is a comprehensive breakdown of the hosts’ current momentum, squad availability and projected starting XI:

Recent form

Chelsea sit 10th in the Premier League with seven points from five matches (two wins, one draw and two defeats). While the Blues have scored 10 league goals, their backline has shipped 12—the joint-highest concession tally in the division—culminating in a 3-0 derby defeat at Brentford before the hiatus.

Stamford Bridge remains Alonso’s primary foundation. Chelsea are unbeaten in four competitive fixtures on home soil this season (three wins and one draw), scoring 14 goals. However, the hosts must tighten up to avoid becoming just the third team in Premier League history to concede multiple goals in each of their opening six matches.

Alonso confronts an extensive casualty list. Top scorer João Pedro (three goals, three assists) is sidelined with an injury, joining Cole Palmer, Reece James, Moisés Caicedo, Jamie Gittens and Emanuel Emegha in the treatment room. Veteran forward Danny Welbeck will spearhead the attack, supported by in-form midfielder Morgan Rogers, while Jordan Henderson partners Valentín Barco in the pivot.

AFC Bournemouth - Struggling for consistency

Bournemouth travel to West London looking to convert positive cup momentum into their elusive first league victory of the campaign. Here is an overview of the Cherries’ recent trajectory, injury report and tactical setup ahead of kickoff:

Recent form

Marco Rose’s men occupy 17th place with three points from five games (three draws and two losses). The Cherries have held leads in four of their five league fixtures, yet dropped points in each, most recently suffering a narrow 1-0 home defeat to Liverpool after Alexander Isak’s second-half strike.

Cup competitions have provided a sharp contrast. Bournemouth cruised past Real Sociedad 2-1 away in the UEFA Europa League group stage and thrashed Lincoln City 4-0 in the League Cup, proving their high-pressing structure can disrupt quality opposition.

Rose must navigate major absences across his squad. Influential midfielder Alex Scott faces an extended spell out with a muscle injury, while Justin Kluivert, Julián Araujo, Amine Adli and David Brooks remain unavailable. Former Chelsea goalkeeper Đorđe Petrović starts against his former employers, while Marcus Tavernier and Brazilian forward Evanilson carry the primary transition threat.
