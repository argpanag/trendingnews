Four mainboard IPOs — Orient Cables (India), German Green Steel and Power, SoftBank-backed AceVector and Runwal Enterprises — closed for subscription on Tuesday, with demand varying across the issues. Orient Cables attracted the highest investor interest, followed by German Green Steel and Power, while AceVector and Runwal Enterprises also received subscriptions exceeding their respective offer sizes.

Surat-based J B Ecotex Ltd has filed its Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP) with capital markets regulator Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) to raise funds through an initial public offering (IPO).

The IPO will comprise a fresh issue of equity shares worth up to ₹400 crore and an offer-for-sale (OFS) of up to 1,29,50,000 equity shares by the promoters.The company’s shares have a face value of ₹5 each.

The OFS will be undertaken by promoters Jitendrakumar Fulchand Arya, Ayodhyaprasad Jugalkishore Singhal, Rajesh Ramniwas Gupta, Vishal Kejriwal, Ramdas Laxminarayan Jindal, Himanshu Surendra Jariwala, Hemant Vimalkumar Dhandharia, Monika Rajesh Gupta and Sajjan Kumar Kejriwal.
