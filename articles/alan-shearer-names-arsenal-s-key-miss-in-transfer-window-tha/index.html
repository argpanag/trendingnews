Alan Shearer names Arsenal's key miss in transfer window that 'would have got them closer to winning Champions League'

TheTools · 07.09.2026 17:54 · source · permalink

Alan Shearer has labelled who he believes Arsenal's key miss was in the transfer window that "would have got them closer to winning the Champions League".

The Gunners lost in the final last season to PSG on penalties after drawing 1-1 in normal time, with Gabriel missing the deciding spot-kick.

Mikel Arteta's side brought in a couple of marquee names during the window, but missed out on a new striker.

Shearer, speaking to Betfair, suggested the signing of Julian Alvarez would have propelled their bid to win the Champions League this season.

He said: "I don't think Arsenal's lack of business in the transfer window to secure a left winger will affect them in the Premier League. They've recruited really well. They clearly wanted to bring one more, top class forward.

"I still think [Viktor] Gyokeres is a good player, but I don't think he's top class. Having said that, he did enough to help them win the league last season.

"But I think a signing like Julian Alvarez would have pushed them that bit further towards the Champions League as well."

Alvarez remained at Atletico Madrid during a window that saw him linked with moves both abroad and domestically after Barcelona were keen.

Shearer believes the Gunners did not need the Argentine for their league campaign, feeling they will still have enough to win it again.

He continued: "They would have won the league and had a great chance in the Champions League if they had signed Alvarez.

"But I'm not feeling too sorry for them with the squad they've got because they still recruited really, really well."

Despite missing out on a new striker, Arsenal still brought in Bruno Guimaraes from Newcastle United for £75million.

Arsenal came from behind to beat Chelsea 2-1

At the back, Ezri Konsa came in from Aston Villa for £55m, making his full debut yesterday in the Gunners' 2-1 win over Chelsea.

Illan Meslier was signed on a free transfer while Christos Tzolis joined from Club Brugge for £34m, impressing in Arsenal's Community Shield victory against Manchester City.

Arsenal have three wins from three so far in the league this season, keeping clean sheets against Coventry and Aston Villa in 3-0 and 1-0 wins respectively.

They had to come from behind against Chelsea yesterday, with Arteta describing the win over their London neighbours as "beautiful".

Arteta described his side's victory over Chelsea as 'beautiful'

The Spaniard said: "I think it was an exceptional performance. I enjoyed every minute of it, from the beginning to the end.

"The beginning was tough, but I enjoyed it because the team reacted in a spectacular way. The determination, the quality. It was beautiful. It was a fascinating game for the spectator.

"I think it's an indication for us of how capable we are to perform at an even higher level than last season. We still have a lot to grow and a lot to offer."

Next up for the Gunners is Napoli away from home on Wednesday, with the Italian outfit coming into the game on the back of a defeat after losing to Inter Milan 3-2.
