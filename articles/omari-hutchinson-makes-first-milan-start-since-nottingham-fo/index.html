Omari Hutchinson makes first Milan start since Nottingham Forest exit, it's the same old story

TheTools · 17.09.2026 18:00 · source · permalink

Omari Hutchinson was a surprise departure from Nottingham Forest this summer when he moved on loan to Milan.

Hutchinson was pretty integral under Vitor Pereira at the back end of last season, and it was anticipated that he would be able to kick on under Oliver Glasner.

However, the Austrian had other ideas, and wingers are not really prominent in his system, which meant Hutchinson was allowed to move to Milan on a temporary deal before the window closed.

What changes are you making to our predicted Forest XI to face Coventry? 👀

Last night, Hutchinson was handed his first start since arriving at the San Siro, as Milan took on Benfica in the Europa League, and it didn’t really go according to plan.

Omari Hutchinson criticised for Milan debut

Tuttosport gave Hutchinson a 4/10 for his performance against Benfica, as the visitors to the San Siro ran out 2-0 winners.

They wrote: “It was his first start of the season and Hutchinson lacked any sort of final product.”

This is a sadly familiar trait when it comes to Hutchinson, and playing for a club like Milan, he’s going to have to get up to speed pretty quickly.

Ruben Amorim certainly wasn’t impressed last night with Hutchinson’s performance, and he was hooked at half-time after a below-par first half.

It remains to be seen what role Hutchinson will play during Milan’s campaign domestically, and there has been a fee of £40 million agreed if he impresses.

Hutchinson was alright without being spectacular for Forest last season, and it does feel like a big few months for the winger now that he’s departed the City Ground on loan.
