Coco Gauff reveals why she hasn’t messaged Ben Shelton, Frances Tiafoe about US Open achievement

TheTools · 12.09.2026 04:29 · source · permalink

Ben Shelton and Frances Tiafoe are set to face off in a historic US Open semi-final. Coco Gauff was asked if she has reached out to them and the tennis star explained why she hasn't.

This has been a great US Open for American stars so far. Coco Gauff and Jessica Pegula have both made it to the women's singles semi-finals, as have Ben Shelton and Frances Tiafoe on the men's side.

Shelton and Tiafoe will battle each other for a spot in the final, and it's a clash that makes history. This is the first time two Black American men are battling in a Grand Slam semi-final in the Open era.

Naturally, there is a lot of excitement in the country about it. And Coco Gauff, as another Black tennis star, was asked about if she had reached out to them about this momentous occasion.

Credit: Al Bello/Julian Finney via Getty Images

Coco Gauff believes it's a 'bad luck omen' to message stars in the middle of the tournament

There is added significance to this piece of history, as it has been 58 years since the legendary Arthur Ashe won the US Open in 1968.

And when it was brought up to Gauff, she recognized that this is a huge moment, while also revealing her reason for not messaging either Shelton or Tiafoe about it.

"I don't really like to do it in the middle of the tournaments because I feel like it's a bad luck omen, so I usually don't like to text anyone while they're playing the tournament," Gauff said. I don't want to screw up momentum…

"With Arthur Ashe, I think it's so incredible. Especially on the men's side I never thought I'd have the chance to see an all black semifinal between the guys in my lifetime. Even growing up, just look at who was playing.

"I just never thought that that would be able to happen. To have also me in the semis, I think it's really cool. I have so much respect for both Ben and Frances. I'm just rooting for a good match. Hopefully whoever wins can bring it home."

Athletes have their own beliefs and superstitions, and it's hard to begrudge Gauff that. With her own semi-final against Elena Rybakina looming, there will be time enough for all celebrations when the job is finished.
