Russia Builds 33 Air Defense Positions Around Alabuga Drone Production Zone

TheTools · 21.10.2026 18:57 · source · permalink

A total of 33 air defense positions have been established around Russia’s Alabuga special economic zone, where Shahed/Geran-type drones are manufactured.

Satellite images showing the new positions were published by Radio Liberty.

Satellite imagery dated October 21 shows that, since spring 2026, concrete towers for air defense systems and new sites for surface-to-air missile systems have been built in the area.

Towers equipped with anti-aircraft machine guns and structures for stretching anti-drone nets over production buildings have also been installed.

Ukrainian drones last reached Alabuga in 2025.

In September, satellite imagery showed the emergence of new production workshops and warehouses within the Alabuga special economic zone.

The infrastructure is being expanded amid the recruitment of foreign workers to manufacture strike drones for Russia.

Russia has also increased production of the Geran-4 and Geran-5 jet-powered strike drones to 4,000 per month, twice the output of piston-powered Geran-2 drones.
