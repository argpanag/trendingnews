All PS5 users forced out of accounts as GTA 6 executes strictest measures yet

TheTools · 18.11.2026 21:15 · source · permalink

GTA 6 players NEED to download this PS5 firmware update.

Sam Cawley

GTA 6 has the same level of protection as some worldwide political leaders, with PlayStation practically being its head of security.

Both Rockstar Games and PlayStation have been banging on about how GTA VI plays best on the PS5, and both companies are taking every measure imaginable to keep the game on consoles.

We live in an age where hackers and modders can use AI to make unofficial PC ports, which would be catastrophic for GTA VI in more ways than one.

To combat this, PlayStation has released a new PS5 firmware update that’s essential for playing GTA VI at launch.

Advert

Anyone who doesn’t download this new firmware will lose access to PSN, which is needed to verify if your copy of the game is legitimate.

This is because hacked consoles cannot be used online, so it should in theory prevent any copies of the game from being ripped and leaked online.

Many believe it’s a futile effort though. Despite action from console manufacturers, hacking is becoming easier every day thanks to AI tools.

What will happen if GTA 6 gets leaked online

We’ve seen it happen with The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (2026). When GTA VI releases worldwide on November 19, there’s a good chance we’ll have an unofficial PC port later that week.

It’ll no doubt be motivated by there being no official way of playing the game on PC come launch day. The core playerbase for the Grand Theft Auto series exists on consoles, so like GTA V, Rockstar will likely wait a year or so to release a PC version itself.

Interestingly, it sounds like this might coincide with the launch of GTA VI: Online.

An unofficial PC port of the game would be a PR nightmare, but it could at least tackle one of the biggest complaints regarding the game’s launch: the framerate.

GTA VI is confirmed to target 30fps at all times, meaning everyone who bought a shiny, new TV and PS5 Pro in preparation for the big day may have wasted their money.

Even the mighty PS5 Pro won’t display the game at 60fps, though depending on your TV you might be able to simulate the feeling.

Should an unofficial PC port surface, there’s a good chance modders will be able to unlock the framerate like they’ve done with other games, for example Bloodborne.

Don’t get too excited though. To play it yourself you’ll have to pirate a copy of the game which we wouldn’t recommend, and there’ll probably be a suite of technical problems that come with the unlocked framerate. If Rockstar felt 30fps was enough, it was for a good reason.

GTA VI launches worldwide on November 19, but unlocks in New Zealand later today.
