A Pullback That Ignores Another Record Quarter

NVIDIA slipped 0.41% over the past week and 3.94% over the past month. It is up 16.2% year to date. Shares sit about 11% below the 52-week high of $243.37 and 32% above the low of $163.90.

The last report tells a stronger story. Fiscal Q2 2027 revenue reached $96.22B, up 105.8% and ahead of the $92.07B consensus. Non-GAAP EPS of $2.22 exceeded the $2.09 estimate. Q3 guidance calls for $108B in revenue.
