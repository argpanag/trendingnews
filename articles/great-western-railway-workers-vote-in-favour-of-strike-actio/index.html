Great Western Railway workers vote in favour of strike action

TheTools · 16.11.2026 09:11 · source · permalink

The union said GWR services would be impacted by a walkout

  • Published
    15 November 2026

Some Great Western Railway (GWR) workers have voted to strike in a row over pay and working arrangements.

Members of the Transport Salaried Staffs Association working in areas such as rostering, service delivery and performance strategy are involved in the dispute.

The union said separate votes in different areas of the business showed strong support for strikes and other industrial action but no walkouts had been scheduled yet.

A GWR spokesperson said: "We continue to work with colleagues and their trades union representatives to find a mutually beneficial solution to the dispute and avoid damaging strike action."

The union is calling for flexbile allowances which relate to shift work to come under basic pay, as a means of protecting salary and enhancing pension rights.

It said staff who voted to strike carried out critical work that kept the railways running and services would be impacted during any walkout.

General secretary Maryam Eslamdoust said strike action and action short of a strike was "now inevitable unless the company addresses these perfectly legitimate demands".

She said it is "simply not acceptable that they see their workloads increase without that reflecting both grading and pay".

The union said 85% of its members who work in rosters and resources and 71.5% of members working in train service delivery and performance strategy voted in favour of strike action.

GWR operates high-speed, commuter and regional services from London Paddington through the Thames Valley to the south-west of England, south Wales and Gloucestershire.
