Robert Pattinson has officially been cast as the next James Bond, but the announcement has disappointed some supporters of another contender for the role. Among those backing their preferred candidate are fans of Jack Lowden, who had been discussed as a potential successor to Daniel Craig.

After Pattinson's casting announcement, social media users have shared their disappointment, with some arguing that Lowden would have been a better fit for the role of the legendary spy.

@adinfinitum44 wrote, "Would've loved Jack Lowden as James Bond, but this ain't bad either. No wonder Slow Horses was renewed for 2 more seasons 1 hour ago." @A1Mish further added, "Don't mind Robert Pattinson as the next Bond but was really hoping we'd have our first Ginger Bond with Jack Lowden." X user @amorecna wrote, "Jack Lowden, you are the Bond in my heart. No one will ever replace you there."

Although Robert Pattinson has earned praise for his work in several recent movies, not every fan following the James Bond franchise is convinced he is the right fit for the role. Meanwhile, Lowden has already showcased his ability to play a spy through his role in Slow Horses, the British spy thriller series. Pattinson's casting has also reignited discussions about what fans wanted from the next 007.

Jack Lowden fans make their feelings clear after Robert Pattinson's James Bond casting

Jack Lowden gained wider recognition for his role as River Cartwright in Slow Horses, playing a young M15 agent whose career takes an unexpected turn after a training exercise goes wrong. His portrayal of the ambitious but often underestimated agent has helped establish him as a familiar face, prompting fans to select him to take over as James Bond.

For instance, @Seraphimmie wrote on X, "Rpatz as Bond…Jack Lowden, I'm so sorry!" In another tweet, they continued, "Like, Jack Lowden was perfect! I am happy that this seemingly makes it easier for him to do more Slow Horses (seeing as season 9 and 10 were just confirmed), but like? I would take Calum Turner over Pattinson. And I love him as an actor; he just does not feel like Bond at all."

The upcoming 007 movie will be directed by Denis Villeneuve and written by Steven Knight. An official release date has not been provided yet.
