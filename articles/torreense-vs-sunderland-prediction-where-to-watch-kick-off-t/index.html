Torreense vs Sunderland - Prediction, where to watch, kick-off time and line-ups 15-10-2026

TheTools · 15.10.2026 13:45 · source · permalink

Torreense and Sunderland meet in the second round of the 2026-27 UEFA Europa League league phase on Thursday, 15 October, with kick-off scheduled for 16:45 UK time. The match will be played at the Estádio Dr. Magalhães Pessoa in Leiria, almost 100km from Torreense’s home ground.

The fixture pits Portugal’s surprise domestic cup winners against a Premier League side, with both clubs looking to build on victories in their European openers. However, their respective domestic campaigns have been less encouraging, making a positive result in Europe all the more important.

Torreense vs. Sunderland - Head-to-head record

This Europa League fixture will be the first competitive meeting between Torreense and Sunderland. With a Portuguese second-division side taking on a Premier League club, the encounter offers a fresh challenge for both teams.

Sunderland have faced Portuguese opposition in European competition before, meeting Sporting in the 1973-74 UEFA Cup Winners’ Cup. The Black Cats won the home leg 2-1 but were eliminated after losing 2-0 away.

With no shared history to consider, current form and the tactical approaches of both managers are likely to prove decisive in this first encounter.

Torreense - Seeking consistency after European win

Surprise Portuguese cup winners Torreense kicked off their Europa League journey with an impressive away win. Below, we’ll take a closer look at their current form and tactical approach, as well as the expected Torreense vs. Sunderland line-ups.

Recent form

Torreense opened their European campaign with a 2-1 win over Lillestrom, even though they finished the match with 10 men. However, their domestic performances have been less consistent. They currently sit 15th in Portugal’s second division, with only one win in six league matches.

Thursday’s match will take place at the Estádio Dr. Magalhães Pessoa in Leiria because Torreense’s home ground does not meet UEFA requirements. This could limit their home advantage.

Midfielder Nuha Jatta is suspended following his dismissal in the opening European fixture. However, goalkeeper Adriel and defender Mohamed Ali Diadie remain available despite their domestic red cards against Tondela.

Sunderland - Seeking relief in Europe

Let’s examine Sunderland’s current situation. We will look at the team’s momentum, recent results, and standout players for their trip to Portugal.

Recent form

Sunderland began their Europa League campaign with a 1-0 home win over AZ Alkmaar. That victory has been one of the few bright spots in a challenging start to the season, with Régis Le Bris’s side recording only two wins in eight matches across all competitions.

A 5-3 defeat away to Manchester City was followed by a 2-0 home loss to Brighton, leaving Sunderland 17th in the Premier League table and just one point above the relegation zone.

The Black Cats are still searching for their first away win of the campaign and have failed to keep a clean sheet in any of their last eight matches on the road across all competitions, conceding 16 goals in that run.

Brian Brobbey, Dan Ballard and Habib Diarra are sidelined with hamstring injuries, while Romaine Mundle remains out with a long-term knee problem. Thomas Meunier is a major doubt after sustaining a thigh injury against Brighton, while Chemsdine Talbi is ineligible for the Europa League squad.
