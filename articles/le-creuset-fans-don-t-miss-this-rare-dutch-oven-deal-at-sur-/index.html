Le Creuset Fans, Don’t Miss This Rare Dutch Oven Deal at Sur La Table

If there's one cookware brand that knows how to make a kitchen look a little more beautiful, it's Le Creuset. The French cookware company has been crafting its iconic enameled cast iron pieces since 1925, and its colorful Dutch ovens have become something of a kitchen status symbol. Beloved by home cooks for their durability, cooking performance and timeless good looks, these are the kinds of pots that can stick around for generations. The only catch is they aren't exactly inexpensive.

That's why this latest Sur La Table find is worth a closer look. The gorgeous kitchen store is offering a pretty fantastic markdown on Le Creuset's Signature Deep Round Dutch Oven, giving shoppers a chance to bring home a coveted piece of cookware without paying full price. Even better, there are 12 gorgeous colorways to choose from, so finding one that suits your kitchen shouldn't be difficult.

The 6.5-quart Dutch oven is regularly priced at $459.95, but Sur La Table has marked it down to just $289.96. That's a savings of nearly $170. For a premium piece of cookware that can handle everything from weeknight dinners to holiday entertaining, that's a pretty appetizing deal.

And this isn't just a pretty pot to leave sitting on the stovetop. Its enameled cast-iron construction provides excellent heat distribution and retention, making it especially useful for recipes that benefit from slow, steady cooking. Think hearty beef stews, braised short ribs, homemade tomato sauce, soups and even freshly baked bread. The generous capacity also gives you plenty of room to prepare family-sized meals or make a big batch of something delicious for the freezer.

The deeper design is another nice feature. Its taller sides offer extra room for layered dishes, larger batches, and even deep-frying, while the snug-fitting lid helps circulate steam and retain moisture as food cooks. The light-colored interior also makes it easier to keep an eye on browning and cooking progress. It's compatible with all stovetops, including induction, and is oven- and broiler-safe up to 500 degrees.
