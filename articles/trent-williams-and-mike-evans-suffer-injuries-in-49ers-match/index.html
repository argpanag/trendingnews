Trent Williams and Mike Evans Suffer Injuries in 49ers Matchup

TheTools · 27.09.2026 22:27 · source · permalink

San Francisco 49ers offensive tackle Trent Williams was carted off with a shoulder stinger early in the third quarter of Sunday's 23-13 victory against the Arizona Cardinals at Levi's Stadium, following a similar exit by teammate Mike Evans in the first half.

The offensive line star was evaluated in the locker room and listed as questionable to return after suffering the injury during San Francisco's opening touchdown drive of the second half. His departure added to a mounting series of roster setbacks for the home squad.

Earlier in the contest, wide receiver Evans sustained a rib injury after absorbing a hit in the second quarter. Athletic trainers evaluated him on the field before carting him away, and team officials ruled him out for the remainder of the game after he struggled to catch his breath.

San Francisco entered the contest with substantial health challenges, including losing multiple defensive linemen over a two-week span while opting to keep defensive end Nick Bosa off injured reserve.

“Losing Bosa was rough yesterday. Losing six out of the ten D-linemen in the last two weeks, that made our team is a little unusual. Don’t think I’ve been a part of that. That has been different," said Kyle Shanahan, 49ers head coach.

Despite the injuries, the 49ers built a multi-possession lead over an Arizona squad also dealing with roster losses, including placing Will Johnson on injured reserve alongside Tip Reiman, Chase Bisontis, James Conner, and Kaleb Proctor.
