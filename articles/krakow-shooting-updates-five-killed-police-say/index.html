Krakow shooting updates: Five killed, police say

TheTools · 07.11.2026 16:15 · source · permalink

Summary

  • Polish police say five people have been killed, and about a dozen more are injured, after a shooting in Krakow - here's what we know so far

  • "Shots were fired in the Main Market Square," a statement says, adding: "We are awaiting official confirmation of the scale of the incident"

  • Images show emergency services at the scene

  • Police say the suspect - who was shot by officers - is being transported to hospital

  • In a social media post, Polish PM Donald Tusk calls the incident "tragic" and says he is being kept updated

  3. 'America stands with Poland at this dark hour' - US ambassadorpublished at 15:56 GMT
    15:56 GMT

    We can now bring you comments from Tom Rose, the US ambassador to Poland, who says there has been a "horrific mass shooting in Krakow".

    He describes it as a "savage and murderous attack that's shattered the peace of Poland".

    "What we know so far is that several innocent precious lives were viciously cut down on a Saturday afternoon in Kraków’s historic and iconic central square," he says in a post on X.

    "America stands with Poland at this dark hour as Poland stands with her beloved citizens of Krakow. May the blessed memory of those lost today assuage the grief of those who mourn."

  4. Polish President 'deeply concerned'published at 15:52 GMT
    15:52 GMTBreaking

    Polish President Karol Nawrocki says he is "deeply concerned by reports of the tragic events" in Krakow, and that his thoughts are with victims, those injured and their families.

    "I remain in contact with the relevant authorities and expect a full explanation of the circumstances surrounding these tragic events," Nawrocki says in a post on X.

    "The head of the National Security Bureau is on his way to Krakow. The safety of Poles is an absolute priority!" he adds.

  6. What we do and don't know about the shooting in Krakowpublished at 15:44 GMT
    15:44 GMT

    What we know:

    • At least five people have been killed and about a dozen injured in a shooting in Krakow's Main Market Square, local police say
    • The department says at around 15:00 local time (14:00 GMT) a man drove into the square and began shooting "at random" from his car
    • The perpetrator was shot by police and is being transported to hospital, the department adds
    • Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk is aware of the attack and is being kept informed by the country's interior ministry

    What we don't know:

    • Details of the number of victims and injuries are still being established, the official account for the city of Krakow says
    • The identity and condition of the suspect
    • A motive behind the attack
    • The conditions of those who are injured

    15:22 GMTBreaking

    Five people have been killed after a shooting in Krakow's Main Market Square and around a dozen people are injured, including police officers, Małopolska Police say.
