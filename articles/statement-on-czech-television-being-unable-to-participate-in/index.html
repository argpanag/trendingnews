Statement on Czech Television being unable to participate in Eurovision Song Contest 2027

TheTools · 06.10.2026 14:57 · source · permalink

Martin Green CBE, Director Eurovision Song Contest said:

"We regret that Česká televize will not be able to participate in the Eurovision Song Contest in 2027 because of the financial pressures they are facing. We completely understand that broadcasters are having to make difficult choices, and we recognize the severe challenges facing many of our Members. 

This decision reflects the broader financial pressure on public service media. Across our membership, broadcasters are working with constrained budgets and significant cuts, while costs rise and pressures mount. The Eurovision Song Contest is one part of that wider picture, and every broadcaster has to weigh its own priorities. 

The EBU continues to work closely with its Members to support public service media. 

We very much hope that Česká televize will return to the Contest when circumstances allow. They will always be welcome."
