Florida Mom Allegedly Admits to Having Sex With Adult Son “Only About 100 Times” | Oxygen

TheTools · 17.09.2026 19:30 · source · permalink

Christina Clemens is facing charges of incest after her adult son allegedly confessed to carrying out the acts for years.

A Florida mother and her son have allegedly admitted to having sex with each other for years.

Christina Clemens, 46, is now facing charges of incest and living in open adultry after investigators said she admitted to carrying out the acts inside her Lakeland home, according to jail records reviewed by Oxygen.

Christina’s biological son Shane Clemens, 23, was also facing a charge of incest, however, court records now list the charge as dropped.

Polk County Sheriff Grady Judd explained in a video briefing that the investigation began after Christina called authorities to report that her son “battered his mother in a domestic violence event.” 

“Shane, you ought not to ever lay a hand on your mama for a lot of reasons,” Judd said, “but you have one more reason why you shouldn’t.”

The sheriff’s office arrived and took Shane into custody, but he later gave investigators more details about what triggered the argument.

Shane told authorities, according to an affidavit obtained by Oxygen, that the fight started “because he was tired of having sexual intercourse with his mom.” 

Christina Clemens and Shane Clemens

Shane did not grow up with his mom, but rekindled the relationship once he turned 18. 

He told detectives that he asked Christina to have sex with him before he learned that she was his biological mother, however, the sheriff’s office noted in the affidavit that she was listed as his mother on his birth certificate.

Shane claimed the sexual acts began when he was 18 years old and included “oral sex, regular sex and anal sex,” and often utilized sex toys that were found next to her bed. Authorities said Shane told them that cameras in the living room and bedroom had also recorded the incidents, per the affidavit.

“So we asked mama, ‘have you been having sex with your son since he was 18,’” Judd explained. “She says, ‘only about 100 times’…that’s what she told us.”

Though Christina is married, her husband told investigators that he didn’t realize the pair were having sexual relations.

“He said, ‘No, I absolutely was not, but I think I am a little suspicious that they used to go into the master bedroom and lock the door,’” Judd added. “What else can you say? That’s nasty. That’s nasty and it’s illegal.” 

Shane told investigators that the pair also had sex when they were home alone or when everyone in the house was sleeping, according to the affidavit.

Christina remains in custody at the Polk County Jail, however, jail records show that Shane was released on Sept. 17.

Oxygen reached out to Christina’s attorney, but did not receive a reply. No attorney information was listed in court records for Shane.
