Rise of the AI shopping agent gets big banks hot under the collar

TheTools · 23.09.2026 06:00 · source · permalink

CBA sees AI as a tool to lift productivity but also a threat, as tools are increasingly deployed to take payments and offer financial services, bypassing banks.

Commonwealth Bank has long been one of the country’s most enthusiastic adopters of artificial intelligence.

Its chief executive, Matt Comyn, has repeatedly visited the United States since the launch of ChatGPT four years ago, gathering intelligence on AI’s growing capabilities. CBA even has a strategic investment in Claude’s developer, Anthropic.
