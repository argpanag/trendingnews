UECL: Onuachu to miss Trabzonspor’s clash against Hearts

TheTools · 22.10.2026 07:18 · source · permalink

Trabzonspor head coach, Thomas Reis has ruled out Paul Onuachu from the club's UEFA Europa Conference League clash against Scottish club Heart of Midlothian F.C.

The Turkish Super Lig club will welcome Hearts to the Papara Park for a matchday three encounter on Thursday night.

Onuachu sustained an injury in Trabzonspor's 4-2 victory over Finnish side KuPS last Thursday.

The lanky striker scored the Black Sea Storm's opening goal in the third minute before he was substituted seven minutes later.

Reis confirmed that the game against Hearts will come too early for Onuachu.

"Tomorrow will be a bit early for Mustafa, early for his return tomorrow. For Onuachu, we could say maybe the Rizespor match," Reis told a press conference.
