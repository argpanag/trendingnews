    The Washington Wizards will start Trae Young, Kyshawn George, AJ Dybantsa, Alex Sarr, and Deandre Ayton against Hawks on Friday.

    Anthony Davis is out with an ankle injury, so instead of starting Bilal Coulibaly who started in Dybantsa’s absence, the Wizards have elected to start Ayton at center. This is part of the reason they brought him in last summer, so if he’s available in a redraft league, he could be worth a stream.
