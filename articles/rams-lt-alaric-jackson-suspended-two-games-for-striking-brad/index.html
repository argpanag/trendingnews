Rams LT Alaric Jackson suspended two games for striking Bradley Chubb following Monday night's game

TheTools · 14.10.2026 01:04 · source · permalink

Los Angeles Rams offensive tackle Alaric Jackson has been suspended two games by the NFL for unsportsmanlike conduct. 

Jackson's suspension stems from striking Buffalo Bills defensive lineman Bradley Chubb during a postgame scrum following Los Angeles' 37-34 loss on Monday night. 

“After the game, as players were shaking hands and exchanging jerseys, Jackson struck Buffalo linebacker Bradley Chubb in the face after a brief confrontation, violating Rule 12, Section 3, Article 1, which applies to 'any act which is contrary to the generally understood principles of sportsmanship,'" the NFL explained in a statement.

The incident was captured by television cameras with on-field interviews still ongoing. Teammates quickly pulled apart Jackson and Chubb. 

Jackson plans to appeal the suspension, head coach Sean McVay said Tuesday. 

If the suspension is upheld, the Rams will start David Quessenberry  at left tackle in Jackson's absence. Barring the appeal being successful, Jackson is slated to miss the Rams' Weeks 6-7 games at home against the Arizona Cardinals and on the road against the Las Vegas Raiders, respectively.

Jackson, 28, is the Rams' starting left tackle, having started in each of Los Angeles' five games this season and 56 out of a total 62 played since joining the team as an undrafted free agent out of Iowa in 2021. 

This is Jackson's second suspension in his career and follows an unrelated June arrest that he could still potentially be disciplined for by the league. 

On June 9, Jackson was arrested on suspicion of of felony domestic violence. The case was assigned a prefiling diversion, which likely means he'll avoid criminal charges, but the NFL has yet to announce a decision on a possible penalty.

In 2024, Jackson was suspended two games for violation of the NFL's personal conduct policy. It stemmed from a woman suing him and alleging Jackson had recorded sexual acts with her on his phone without her consent.
