Cleveland Cavaliers vs. Philadelphia 76ers Live NBA Updates, Scores and Highlights

After picking up an open-night victory over the reigning-champion New York Knicks, the Philadelphia 76ers are putting their perfect record on the line against the Cleveland Cavaliers Thursday night.

This matchup will mark the regular-season home debuts for both LeBron James and Jaylen Brown. It is also Peyton Watson's first official game with the Cavs, as well as Cleveland's first overall tilt of the 2026-27 campaign.

Follow along below for updates, highlights, analysis and takeaways from this showdown between should-be Eastern Conference superpowers.

1 Big Question for Peyton Watson

How much time to the Cavs carve out on-ball for him?

Watson ran a lot of the Denver Nuggets' offseason for a beat last year while Nikola Jokić was sidelined. And the Cavs have a need for creation outside of James Harden and Donovan Mitchell.

Whether Watson's good enough to provide it is one thing. How the Cavs go about using him offensively when they also have Evan Mobley to account for is another.

The Sixers' Frontcourt Injuries Just Keep Coming

Dean Wade already missed the Sixers' season-opener with this same ankle injury. He is listed as day-to-day, so this doesn't profile as a long-term concern.

Immediately, though, we should expect to see more units with Jabari Walker—including lineups that feature him or LeBron James at the 5.

It's the Usual Starting 5 for Philly

This group was a plus-eight in 16 minutes against the New York Knicks on Tuesday night. Somewhat surprisingly, they cut their teeth on the defensive end, limiting the reigning champs to just 0.71 points per possession.

Shooting progression is the big thing to watch. Philly's Fantastic Five(?) hit just two of their 13 three-point attempts to open the season. Something tells me they'll want to convert more than 15.4 percent of their triples moving forward.

The Cavs Have Locked in Their Starters

This is the starting lineup everyone would have predicted. It also makes a ton of sense on paper.

Still, it'll be interesting to see how head coach Kenny Atkinson staggers these guys. Part of Peyton Watson's appeal is he might have more skill to explore on-ball. And we'll definitely be seeing one-big units peppered throughout the game.
