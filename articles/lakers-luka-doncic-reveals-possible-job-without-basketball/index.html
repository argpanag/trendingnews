Lakers’ Luka Doncic reveals possible job without basketball

TheTools · 07.09.2026 02:00 · source · permalink
  • NBA on ClutchPoints
  • Los Angeles Lakers

Luka Doncic was asked to share his thoughts on a life without basketball, and choose an alternate job few would have guessed.

The Los Angeles Lakers are preparing for another season of Luka Doncic at the helm of coach JJ Redick's offense, alongside fellow sharpshooter Austin Reaves, who has a special on-court relationship with the Lakers superstar.

Doncic's gregarious yet ultra-competitive personality has become a highlight of the Lakers' seasons, both on and off the court, lending itself well to game-day theatre at Los Angeles' Crypto.com Arena each season.

Doncic was asked a series of questions recently, placing a spotlight on his mindset ahead of the 2026-27 season, including the confidence he has in his deadly accurate outside shot. The Lakers forward also shared his thoughts on another sport that he enjoys playing, which he says would be his job if not for basketball: golf.

The topic was first broached when Doncic was asked what the one sport is that Reaves could beat him at. “Golf, for now,” Doncic replied. “If you weren't playing basketball, what job would you do?” he was later asked. “Golf,” Doncic replied, echoing his previous response.

Doncic's calm demeanor on the basketball court and laser accuracy from long distances will be on full display again this season, starting with the Lakers' regular season opener on Wednesday, October 21, against the Golden State Warriors at Crypto.com Arena. Doncic doesn't figure to leave his current job any time soon as he is currently on a three-year max salary deal that includes a player option in 2028.

Luka Doncic is once again entering the season with a slimmed-down look that caught the attention of NBA legend Dirk Nowitzki and could aid him in his quest for his first-ever NBA title.

Doncic has plenty of time to transition to a new job or sport, if he so chooses, but for now, he's content with being one of the basketball players on the planet heading into his ninth NBA season.
