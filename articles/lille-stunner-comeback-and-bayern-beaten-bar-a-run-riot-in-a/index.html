Lille stunner: comeback and Bayern beaten. Barça run riot in Azerbaijan, United held to 2-2 by Sporting

In the evening's other matches, Atletico Madrid and Liverpool made no mistake: the Colchoneros saw off Viking 2-0 in the first half, while the Reds hit three in Belgium with two goals from Isak and a strike from Wirtz

The surprise of Champions League Wednesday came from Lille, where the home side, coached by Davide Ancelotti, managed the feat of beating Bayern Munich. After Upamecano's initial headed goal from a corner for the Bavarians, the French side equalised in the second half through Ueda and then completed the comeback to win thanks to a truly incredible own goal by Pavlovic in the space of three minutes. In the 65th minute Ethan Mbappé broke away rapidly on the counter, leaving clear space behind him; one-on-one with Urbig, his shot struck the post, and then Pavlovic himself arrived and, entirely alone, hit the ball badly, sending it clumsily into his own net. For Lille it was their first victory in this Champions League and a chance to close back in on the play-off places. It was all plain sailing instead for Liverpool, who ran out 0-3 winners away to Club Brugge. Opening the scoring was a goal by Isak that stemmed from a mistake with his feet by former Inter player Sommer, who erred during build-up play. The Swedish striker doubled his tally late in the game, with Wirtz's goal sandwiched in between.

Barcelona hit four 

Barcelona's Champions League campaign resumed with a goal-fest. The Catalans confirmed their status as one of Europe's best attacks by scoring four against Sabah. For this trip to Azerbaijan, Flick chose to leave out several key players, and indeed the attack consisted of the untried front three of Adeyemi, Gabriel Jesus and Gordon. There was no trouble playing good attacking football or creating dangerous chances, however, as all three Blaugrana forwards found the net. Gordon scored in the first half and added a second early in the second half, Adeyemi made it three, and the fourth goal bore the signature of Gabriel Jesus. In the 63rd minute Sabah tried to get back into the game by scoring a consolation goal, but there was no time left, with Barcelona managing the closing stages well and taking home three points that moved them level on points with Betis and Como in the standings. The other 18:45 match saw Villarreal away at Slavia Prague: this fixture also produced an away win for a Spanish side, by a 0-1 scoreline. The star of the match was former Inter player Buchanan, who found the winning goal for Villarreal in the 62nd minute, but VAR also played an important role in the game by correcting two errors by Slovenian referee Rade Obrenović: late in the first half it overturned a red card shown to former Juventus player Renato Veiga, rightly prompting the referee to opt for a yellow, while in the second half a penalty for Slavia Prague that could have changed the course of the match was overturned.

UNITED STRUGGLE

Another uphill evening for Manchester United, who leave Lisbon with a point after a 2-2 draw against Sporting. Luis Suarez's brace was split by a brace from Bruno Fernandes, with a penalty scored by each side. The English side's second goal — which temporarily made it 1-2 — came courtesy of a heavy deflection after an unpredictable bounce caused by a divot right in the heart of Sporting's penalty area, allowing Bruno Fernandes to find his second goal of the night. The match remained fiery right until the end, with a goal ruled out for United for a foul on the opposing goalkeeper and a red card for Tielemans (for a second booking) almost on the final whistle. Atletico Madrid won with ease, dispatching Norwegian side Viking 2-0 with goals from a Norwegian in Sorloth and Julian Alvarez from a free kick. Another former Serie A player on the scoresheet was Muriqi, who decided the clash between Shakhtar Donetsk and Fenerbahce, played in London and ending 0-1.
