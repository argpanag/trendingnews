‘English with Dehati Madam’ filmed despite husband’s scolding, then Priyanka Chopra shared her video on Instagram, video crosses 30 million

TheTools · 07.10.2026 20:31 · source · permalink

    Yashoda Lodhi, known as ‘English with Dehati Madam’, has seen her online following grow after Priyanka Chopra shared her farm video on Instagram. The Uttar Pradesh-based content creator now has more than 583,000 Instagram followers and over 400,000 YouTube followers. Her work has also received attention from Mrunal Thakur, Abhay Deol and R Madhavan.
