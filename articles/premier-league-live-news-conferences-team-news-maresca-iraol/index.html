    "For Brian and Granit they want to play as soon as possible, so now it is a question of managing the situation. If you can't sustain it the rest for the season, then it doesn't make sense [to start them now]."

  11. 'I am thinking about starting Kulusevski soon'published at 15:38 GMT
    15:38 GMT

    Sunderland v Tottenham (Sun, 14:05 GMT)

    Tottenham Hotspur

    Roberto De Zerbi was asked about managing the minutes of various players, including Dominic Solanke and Destiny Udogie: "They both worked well this week. Solanke played very well in the last game.

    "With Udogie, I think we have to manage him better than Solanke because he needs to stay with the medical staff, focusing on him for another three or four weeks. If he goes over for the weeks, for him it is not a problem."

    On Dejan Kulusevski, he added: "I am thinking to start [him] - maybe not now but I think we are very close. He is working well.

    "I watched his games in the national team to understand his physical condition. He played the first game as a winger and the second as a number 10. In the second game especially he played very well but we have to understand the right moment to put him on the pitch. The medical and physical staff are working very well with him and we can't lose other time.

    "We can't take risks for him because he is a special player for us."

  17. Only way to enjoy the derby is to win - Iraolapublished at 15:27 GMT
    15:27 GMT

    Everton v Liverpool (Sun, 12:00 GMT)

    Aadam Patel
    Football Reporter

    Andoni Iraola will be delighted that his first Merseyside derby will come off the back of a pleasant week for Liverpool.

    His side have scored seven goals against Manchester United and Club Brugge without reply and will make the short trip over to the Hill Dickinson Stadium full of confidence.

    Liverpool have lost just two of their last 34 matches against Everton and won away against David Moyes' side last season after a 100th-minute winner from Virgil van Dijk.

    "I know I haven't lived this derby but it doesn't take a lot to know that this is a big game. I am happy to be a part of it and I want to enjoy it," said Iraola. "And to do that, there is only one way, which is to win."

    Iraola will again be without Cody Gakpo, who has a muscle injury but may return next week while "it's going to be difficult" for Ryan Gravenberch, who is still feeling pain in his foot, to feature. The Dutch pair haven't played for Liverpool since before the recent international break.

    Meanwhile, Victor Munoz will try to train on Saturday after he was forced off midweek in Belgium but there is good news for Liverpool in that it is not a major injury. Iraola described it as a "big knock" to his knee but said that there is no structural damage. Nevertheless. the Spaniard is still a doubt for Sunday.

  18. Gakpo out, doubts over Munoz & Gravenberchpublished at 15:26 GMT
    15:26 GMT

    Everton v Liverpool (Sun, 12:00 GMT)

    Liverpool

    A final word from Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola on team news.

    He offered an injury update on Victor Munoz, who limped off during their Champions League win against Brugge, and Cody Gakpo, who was injured on international duty:

    "With Victor, I think the good news is after assessing him, everything is fine but he has a big knock. He hasn't trained on Friday but wants to try on Saturday.

    "Gakpo, normally this injury is two or three weeks. He did it for Netherlands against Germany. I don't like to say timelines. It wasn't a big injury but there is something there and we have to give it time to heal."

    The Reds' head coach also said Ryan Gravenberch will likely not be fit to face Everton.

  19. Isak showing improvement - Iraolapublished at 15:23 GMT
    15:23 GMT

    Everton v Liverpool (Sun, 12:00 GMT)

    Liverpool

    Alexander Isak found the net twice in midweek during Liverpool's Champions League victory, just days after failing to find the net despite having chances against Manchester United.

    Discussing his striker, Andoni Iraola said: "He has definitely improved a lot on the ball and without. He is understanding the press better. The better he understands the press the less he will have to run also - this is the fights we have.

    "Also on the ball I think we are using him better in possession. He is linking up play very well. He is technically a very nice player so I think we have to use him not just in the finishing side but he can be very useful in our build up. If he helps us in the build up, sometimes those balls will end up with him. He is understanding everything and looking to improve more."

  20. Iraola eyes first Merseyside derbypublished at 15:20 GMT
    15:20 GMT

    Everton v Liverpool (Sun, 12:00 GMT)

    Liverpool

    Sunday will be Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola's first experience of a Merseyside derby.

    "I haven't lived this derby," said the Spaniard. "But it doesn't take a lot when you are here to decide it's a big game for everybody involved.

    "I am very happy to be part of it and I really hope I enjoy it. To enjoy it there is only one way, to win the game.

    "Derbies are always different. It doesn't matter where you are in the standings. Normally the type of game is different and you can feel it from the beginning.

    "We have to do our part, our duels, be at a good level. We have to know it's a derby and also be good on the ball, quality wise."

Only way to enjoy the derby is to win - Iraolapublished at 15:27 GMT
15:27 GMT

Everton v Liverpool (Sun, 12:00 GMT)

Aadam Patel
Football Reporter

Andoni Iraola will be delighted that his first Merseyside derby will come off the back of a pleasant week for Liverpool.

His side have scored seven goals against Manchester United and Club Brugge without reply and will make the short trip over to the Hill Dickinson Stadium full of confidence.

Liverpool have lost just two of their last 34 matches against Everton and won away against David Moyes' side last season after a 100th-minute winner from Virgil van Dijk.

"I know I haven't lived this derby but it doesn't take a lot to know that this is a big game. I am happy to be a part of it and I want to enjoy it," said Iraola. "And to do that, there is only one way, which is to win."

Iraola will again be without Cody Gakpo, who has a muscle injury but may return next week while "it's going to be difficult" for Ryan Gravenberch, who is still feeling pain in his foot, to feature. The Dutch pair haven't played for Liverpool since before the recent international break.

Meanwhile, Victor Munoz will try to train on Saturday after he was forced off midweek in Belgium but there is good news for Liverpool in that it is not a major injury. Iraola described it as a "big knock" to his knee but said that there is no structural damage. Nevertheless. the Spaniard is still a doubt for Sunday.

Gakpo out, doubts over Munoz & Gravenberchpublished at 15:26 GMT
15:26 GMT

Everton v Liverpool (Sun, 12:00 GMT)

Liverpool

A final word from Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola on team news.

He offered an injury update on Victor Munoz, who limped off during their Champions League win against Brugge, and Cody Gakpo, who was injured on international duty:

"With Victor, I think the good news is after assessing him, everything is fine but he has a big knock. He hasn't trained on Friday but wants to try on Saturday.

"Gakpo, normally this injury is two or three weeks. He did it for Netherlands against Germany. I don't like to say timelines. It wasn't a big injury but there is something there and we have to give it time to heal."

The Reds' head coach also said Ryan Gravenberch will likely not be fit to face Everton.

Isak showing improvement - Iraolapublished at 15:23 GMT
15:23 GMT

Everton v Liverpool (Sun, 12:00 GMT)

Liverpool

Alexander Isak found the net twice in midweek during Liverpool's Champions League victory, just days after failing to find the net despite having chances against Manchester United.

Discussing his striker, Andoni Iraola said: "He has definitely improved a lot on the ball and without. He is understanding the press better. The better he understands the press the less he will have to run also - this is the fights we have.

"Also on the ball I think we are using him better in possession. He is linking up play very well. He is technically a very nice player so I think we have to use him not just in the finishing side but he can be very useful in our build up. If he helps us in the build up, sometimes those balls will end up with him. He is understanding everything and looking to improve more."

Iraola eyes first Merseyside derbypublished at 15:20 GMT
15:20 GMT

Everton v Liverpool (Sun, 12:00 GMT)

Liverpool

Sunday will be Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola's first experience of a Merseyside derby.

"I haven't lived this derby," said the Spaniard. "But it doesn't take a lot when you are here to decide it's a big game for everybody involved.

"I am very happy to be part of it and I really hope I enjoy it. To enjoy it there is only one way, to win the game.

"Derbies are always different. It doesn't matter where you are in the standings. Normally the type of game is different and you can feel it from the beginning.

"We have to do our part, our duels, be at a good level. We have to know it's a derby and also be good on the ball, quality wise."
