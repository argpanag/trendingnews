CBS star explains why he quit Masters job with help of Justin Bieber - AOL

TheTools · 11.10.2026 15:31 · source · permalink

Ian Eagle admits an incident involving a poster of Justin Bieber led to him making a decision to step down from his Masters role.

The Masters is one of the most exclusive events in golf, as players fight for a chance to earn the prestigious Green Jacke. With exclusivity comes expense, as people that live near the course rent out their homes to those in need for the week, and it comes at a pretty penny.

Regardless of the price, staying in someone else's home can get interesting, and broadcaster Eagle recalled a moment that sent him over the edge and realizing that he should probably call broadcasting quits. It comes after ex-LIV Golf star considers quitting $3.25M tournament despite leading.

“Justin Bieber ended me,” said Eagle. “I did six years of The Masters for Amen Corner Live. You do not stay in a hotel, you stay in people's homes.

"They are not there, they rent them out. That would be really weird if you are just in their house with them. And the worst that I have ever experienced, and it was rough. But this is not the reason why I stopped doing it.

“My last year there, I was in a bedroom that shared another bedroom with a Jack and Jill bathroom. I had the room where there was a full-size poster of Justin Bieber on the ceiling right above the bed.”

Not only was one of the world's most famous pop stars taking a good look at him sleeping, but the allergies that came with working in Georgia in April also took a toll.

“So every night I was attempting to fall asleep, a great deal of phlegm and mucus building, staring at Justin Bieber,” said Eagle. “And not the more recent Justin Bieber, the full-on swooping hair version of Justin Bieber.

“And I think it was the Saturday night before the final round, I was staring at Justin Bieber with a great deal of mucus. And I had a conversation with myself, Jake. I said, ‘Do I need to be here? Is this something I need to do?’ And that was the end.”
