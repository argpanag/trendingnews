Your daily horoscope: November 5, 2026

TheTools · 05.11.2026 05:01 · source · permalink

Scorpio.iStockPhoto / Getty Images

IF TODAY IS YOUR BIRTHDAY

You need to keep your personal feelings out of situations of a purely practical nature over the next 12 months. Facts and figures that everyone agrees on will matter more than what you choose to believe is “right” or “wrong” on a personal level.

ARIES (March 21 - April 20):

You can be a bit aggressive at times Aries but the planets warn if you come on too strong today you could get the worst of it. If a rival confronts you it might be best for both of you if you just turn your back and walk away.

TAURUS (April 21 - May 21):

Although you may have a great deal of energy at your disposal you would be wise not to use all of it in one go. Come the weekend there will be more important matters demanding your attention, so keep something in reserve.

GEMINI (May 22 - June 21):

The only reason someone is trying to persuade you to be less ambitious is because they fear you will outshine them, and they are right. Opportunity is about to come knocking and you would be a fool to listen to those who urge caution.

CANCER (June 22 - July 23):

What would you begin if you knew it was destined to be a huge success? Well that is what you should be working on now and you must not let anything, including your own doubts, hold you back. Greatness beckons, so go for it!

LEO (July 24 - Aug. 23):

You may be optimistic about the changes now taking place but not everyone is convinced they will be for the best. Make it a priority today to convince friends and loved ones there is a wonderful new world out there waiting to be enjoyed.

VIRGO (Aug. 24 - Sept. 23):

You must be honest about your feelings over the next 24 hours, even if some of them are less than positive. The people who love you most will give you advice that cheers you up no end, so listen to what they have to say.

LIBRA (Sept. 24 - Oct. 23):

You need to be on your guard today, because the planets suggest that someone you think of as a friend may be trying to undermine your efforts behind the scenes. You don’t have to get super suspicious but you do have to keep your eyes and ears open.

SCORPIO (Oct. 24 - Nov. 22):

As one of the zodiac’s fixed signs you don’t change your opinions often, but when you do you tend to go from one extreme to the other. For that reason you need to think carefully today before trading in one set of views for another.

SAGITTARIUS (Nov. 23 - Dec. 21):

You may be of the opinion that a friend’s current woes are of their own making but don’t let that stop you from helping them. You know they would not hesitate to assist you if the roles were reversed, so put yourself out for them.

CAPRICORN (Dec. 22 - Jan. 20):

If someone oversteps the mark on the work front today your first reaction will be to come down on them hard. Before you do though you might like to give it a bit more thought and then take a less confrontational stance. It’s actually not that important.

AQUARIUS (Jan. 21 - Feb. 19):

True happiness does not come from having lots of money or being a big name in the world at large, but from knowing you are doing something useful. If you get the chance to help a fellow human being today don’t hesitate, just do it.

PISCES (Feb. 20 - Mar. 20):

If you can hold a clear vision in your head of what it is you are hoping to accomplish you may be surprised how easy it becomes to reach your goal. By the weekend at the latest you will see a massive improvement in your affairs.
