Ben Shelton’s Father vs. Novak Djokovic: What Really Happened? - AOL

TheTools · 12.09.2026 04:27 · source · permalink

Ben Shelton’s success at the 2023 US Open came with one of the more memorable controversies of his young career, which included Novak Djokovic and his father, Bryan Shelton.

The drama was about a celebration that had come to be linked with Ben. When Shelton won a game, he would make a “phone” gesture, putting his hand to his ear and then “hanging up”. The celebration was a part of his developing personality on the court.

Djokovic seemed to copy the move in his victory over Shelton in the 2023 US Open semifinals. It was soon a topic of conversation, as Bryan Shelton challenged what he saw as an intentional attempt to poke fun at his son.

Why Did Bryan Shelton Call Out Novak Djokovic?

Bryan Shelton was particularly vocal about Djokovic’s celebration after the match. In his statement, Ben’s father said that Djokovic was attempting to poke fun at his son, not just his own win.

The comments were an emotional addition to an already emotional night for Ben, who was playing his first Grand Slam semifinal. Djokovic went on to win the match in straight sets and then his 24th Grand Slam.

The debate was mostly over the significance of Djokovic’s gesture. For some, it was just Djokovic playing with Shelton’s signature celebration. But to Bryan, it was a sign of disrespect toward his son.

Ben Shelton’s Parents and the Djokovic Controversy

Bryan Shelton’s involvement garnered attention in part because of his unique role in Ben’s career. He is not just Ben’s father, but also his coach, having stepped down from his coaching role at college in 2023 to coach his son full-time.

That partnership has seen Bryan often play a pivotal role in the most significant moments of Ben’s career, such as his US Open run in 2023 and his dramatic win over Carlos Alcaraz in 2026.

Ben has previously commended his father’s honesty and stated that he respects him as a former player, coach and father. The Djokovic incident therefore remains an interesting chapter in the Shelton family story.
