SPRINTCAR DRIVERS PREPARE FOR BATTLE AS SUPER2 SHOWDOWN HEATS UP

bp Adelaide Grand Final

25 Nov 2026

A lucrative $140,000 prize pool awaits local and international drivers for the 2026 NAPA SprintCar Invitational as part of the NAPA Speedway in the City.

As part of the bp Adelaide Grand Final, James McFadden from Alice Springs and Garet Williamson from Missouri, USA were the first two drivers preparing for Thursday and Friday’s NAPA SprintCar Invitational.

Returning bigger than ever, the 342-metre temporary NAPA Speedway track will feature thrilling day and night racing across SprintCars, Formula 500, Modified Sedans and Junior Speedway categories.

Divided by a smaller points margin than the Supercars championship contenders, the stars of the future in the Super2 category are ready for a title battle for the ages.

Officially the closest ever title battle in Super2 history, Campbell Logan, South Australia’s Brad Vaughan, Lochie Dalton and Nash Morris will have the ambition of lifting the championship trophy come Sunday at the bp Adelaide Grand Final.
