In 1998, an investor writes a $100,000 cheque to a startup called Google, which was running out of a garage; founders Larry Page, Sergey Brin set it up as a business the very next day, now the company is worth more than $4 trillion

TheTools · 27.09.2026 07:53 · source · permalink

    Google's birthday: Google marks its 28th birthday on September 27, 2026, with a special Doodle bringing back its early logo. The occasion looks back at how a $100,000 cheque written to a garage startup in 1998 forced Larry Page and Sergey Brin to legally form Google Inc. overnight, a company now worth more than $4 trillion.
