Miles Kelly hits six triples, scores 24 points

TheTools · 08.10.2026 07:09 · source · permalink

    Miles Kelly hits six triples, scores 24 points

    Miles Kelly

    GSW Guard #26
    Player Stats

    Miles Kelly tallied 24 points (8-of-16 FGs, 2-of-4 FTs), seven rebounds, six assists, one block and six three-pointers in Wednesday’s exhibition against the Trail Blazers.

    Kelly, who spent some of his rookie campaign with the Mavericks on a two-way deal, is with the Warriors on an Exhibit 10 contract. His chances of making the team’s Opening Night roster are slim, but a night like Wednesday doesn’t hurt. He shot 6-of-11 from beyond the arc and led all scorers with 24 points while also providing solid value in rebounds and assists. Don’t be surprised if the Warriors look to keep Kelly with their G League affiliate this season.
    - Raphielle Johnson
