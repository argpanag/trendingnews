Biratnagar Kings Set 106-Run Target for Sudurpashchim Royals

TheTools · 31.10.2026 18:24 · source · permalink

Kathmandu. Biratnagar Kings has given a target of 106 runs to Sudurpashchim Royals.

The match played at TU Cricket Ground Kirtipur, affected by rain, was shortened to 5 overs. Where Biratnagar, who batted first after losing the toss, scored 105 runs for the loss of 2 wickets. For them, Shehan Jayasuriya played an aggressive innings of 39 runs in 11 balls and got out, while Lokesh Bam scored 25 runs in 10 balls. Bam hit 3 sixes and 1 four in 10 balls.

Avinash Bohora of Sudurpashchim took 2 wickets. Earlier, the match between Lumbini Lions and Kathmandu Gurkhas, which was supposed to be held on Saturday, could not be held due to rain. Both teams shared 1 point each.
