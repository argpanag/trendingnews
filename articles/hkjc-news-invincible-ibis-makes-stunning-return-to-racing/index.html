HKJC news: Invincible Ibis makes "stunning" return to racing

TheTools · 01.10.2026 08:39 · source · permalink

By RSN

Plenty of racing news has come out of Hong Kong after Sunday’s Sha Tin meeting.

On RSN, Paul Sebastiani relayed the news.

“Hong Kong Derby winner Invincible Ibis has made a stunning return to racing, claiming victory in the Group 3 Celebration Cup at Sha Tin overnight.”

“Ridden by Luke Ferraris for trainer Mark Newnham, Invincible Ibis held off a fast-finishing Galaxy Patch to win the 1400m feature by a neck.

“Invincible Ibis will now target the Sha Tin Trophy next month as he builds towards December's Hong Kong International races.

“And apprentice Nichola Yuen celebrated the first Hong Kong treble of her career.”
