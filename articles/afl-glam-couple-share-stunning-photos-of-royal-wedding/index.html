AFL glam couple share stunning photos of ‘royal wedding’

AFL glam couple Josh Daicos and model Annalise Dalins have shared photos of their “royal wedding” on social media.

Daicos, the 28-year-old Collingwood star, and Dalins (a 25-year-old model) tied the knot in a spectacular ceremony at wedding venue Redleaf in the picturesque Hunter Valley in New South Wales on November 21.

Five days later, on Daicos’s birthday, they shared photos of their special day, which influencer Tayla Broad (wife of freshly retired Richmond star Nathan Broad) was quick to describe as “the royal wedding”.

The beaming couple looked picture perfect as they showed off their wedding rings, Daicos in a stylish white jacket and black bow tie and Dalins (who has changed her name to Daicos) wearing a spectacular strapless bodice with white flowing train.

Channel 7 star Abbey Holmes said: “Breathtaking !! Congratulations love birds.”

Famous ‘WAG’ and high-profile influencer Bec Judd said: “Stunning you two!!!!!! ❤️❤️”

And Olympic swimmer Shayna Jack said: “Congratulations to you both 😍❤️ You look incredible.”

Even Matildas superstar Sam Kerr passed on her congratulations, while Daicos’s brother Nick said “the best”.

Ahead of the ceremony, Dalins posted an emotional video of the moment she asked Daicos’s father, the legendary Peter Daicos, to walk her down the aisle.

Peter has become a father figure for the model, who has been estranged from her own father since her parents separated.

Dalins said she was grateful for their special relationship and to have such a loving blended family.

“Dear PD … from day dot you accepted me into the family as much more than Josh’s girlfriend, so PD, will you give me the greatest honour of walking me down the aisle as I step into the next chapter of our lives together,’’ she said.

“But not just as Josh’s dad but as the man who has shaped my adulthood and my values.”

Peter was gifted a handkerchief for his happy tears as “father of the bride and groom”.

Dalins told News Corp during the Melbourne Cup Carnival earlier this month that they couldn’t wait to get married.

“We’re very excited for this next chapter,’’ she said.

“For such a long time it felt like this thing we were planning so far in the future. We’ve been together for such a big part of our lives, so I think more than anything we’re just so excited to be married and start that next chapter together.”

It comes after Daicos proposed to Dalins, who he met at a bar in 2021, while they were on holiday in Greece two years ago.

Fans have become obsessed with the couple due to their relationship blending AFL fame and Collingwood “royalty” with polished modelling, joint magazine covers and high fashion.
