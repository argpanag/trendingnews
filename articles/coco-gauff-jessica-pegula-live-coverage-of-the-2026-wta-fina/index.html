Coco Gauff – Jessica Pegula, LIVE coverage of the 2026 WTA Finals in Indian Wells, where to watch 

TheTools · 08.11.2026 16:56 · source · permalink

On November 8, a first-round match in the group stage will take place on Court “Stadium 2” at Indian Wells Tennis Garden (Indian Wells, USA), where the “FAGE WTA Finals Indian Wells-2026” tennis tournament gets underway today.

In “Group 1,” Coco Gauff will play Jessica Pegula. We can look forward to an intriguing “American derby.” Coco is ranked No. 4 in the world. Pegula is one place lower (5). Their head-to-head is tied (5-5).
They met twice on the tournament circuit this season, and Coco won both matches. She got the better of Pegula at Wimbledon (4:6, 6:3, 6:3) and at the Cincinnati WTA 1000 event (6:2, 6:4).

Coco has won one title this season, at the aforementioned Cincinnati tournament, and has also reached two finals. At the WTA 1000 event in Miami, she lost to Aryna Sabalenka (2:6, 6:4, 3:6). On the clay in Rome, she lost to Elina Svitolina (4:6, 7:67-3, 2:6).

Jess won titles in Dubai and Charleston. She beat Ukrainian players at both tournaments. On the hard courts in the UAE, she defeated Elina Svitolina (6:2, 6:4). On American clay, she beat Yuliia Starodubtseva (twice 6:2).
