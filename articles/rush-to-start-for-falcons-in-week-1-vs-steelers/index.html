    Rush to start for Falcons in Week 1 vs. Steelers

    Cooper Rush

    ATL Quarterback #13
    Player Stats

    Falcons QB Cooper Rush will start in Week 1 against the Steelers.

    With two days left to go until the Falcons kick off the 2026 season, head coach Kevin Stefanski already finds himself on his third-string quarterback. With Tua Tagovailoa (oblique) and Michael Penix Jr. (knee) both banged up, Rush will draw the start on the road against the Steelers. This was already shaping up to be a tough matchup for the Falcons’ offense, but with Rush under center, it’s fair to say everybody in that offense will receive a slight downgrade as far as fantasy production is concerned. Rush completed 65.4 percent of his passes while throwing for 303 yards, no touchdowns, and four interceptions last year with the Ravens.
    - Zachary Krueger
