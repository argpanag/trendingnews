Searches continue for boy missing from Suffolk playground

TheTools · 16.09.2026 05:49 · source · permalink

Searches are continuing for a missing three-year-old boy who was last seen at a playground in a Suffolk village.

Noah Woods was with a relative at the small park at Merriam Close in Brantham, but "became separated" from them as they left at about 15:00 BST on Tuesday, Suffolk Police said.

The toddler, who is partially deaf, autistic and non-verbal, was reported to have run down a nearby alleyway towards a newbuild housing estate.

More than 100 people joined searches for Noah, while emergency services carried out door-to-door inquiries into the evening.

Police described Noah as white, with curly ginger hair, and said he would not respond to his name being called.

He was wearing a white Bluey T-shirt, shorts and black Vans trainers with a white stripe.

In a statement released overnight, police said efforts to find him were still ongoing.

"We are grateful for the support from the public, however ask people not to undertake independent searches overnight. It's important that any public searches are coordinated with police."

A helicopter was seen flying over the scene, which is near the estuary of the River Stour and close to the Essex border.

Locals were asked to check their gardens, sheds and outbuildings for the youngster.

Passengers on trains between Manningtree and Ipswich were told their services must run at a reduced speed due to the searches.

Rescue teams have been carrying out door-to-door inquiries in the area.

Supt Pete Partridge said on Tuesday: "We have deployed specially trained officers to assist with our search of the area today, including helicopters and drones."

Posting on its Facebook page, Suffolk Police said: "It is overwhelming to read the support people are offering."

A spokesperson for HM Coastguard said: "We are assisting Suffolk Constabulary with an incident at Brantham by sending an HM Coastguard helicopter and Coastguard Rescue Teams from Felixstowe and Holbrook."
