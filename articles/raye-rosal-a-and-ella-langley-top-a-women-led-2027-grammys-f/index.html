Raye, Rosalía and Ella Langley Top a Women-Led 2027 Grammys Field

Langley is the first straight-up country artist to contend for the top album prize in eight years, and Raye’s 11 nods lead all nominees.

Rosalía, Olivia Rodrigo, Ella Langley, Phoebe Bridgers and Raye are among the top contenders for the 69th annual Grammy Awards, leading a nominee class that is dominated by young women.

Raye, a British singer-songwriter with a jazzy, R&B-throwback style, has the most nods this year, with 11. Rosalía, the Spanish singer who has filled arenas with boundlessly eclectic, multilingual music, has nine, the Recording Academy announced Monday.

Among the other top nominees, the chart-dominating new country star Ella Langley and her collaborator Miranda Lambert, along with Rodrigo and Bridgers, have seven nods apiece. Bruno Mars, a longtime Grammy darling, has six, as do the R&B singer-songwriters Leon Thomas and Olivia Dean, the latter another retro-flavored British singer who won best new artist at the most recent Grammy ceremony.

Taylor Swift, Ariana Grande and the anything-goes New York rock band Geese are also in the mix in top categories.

They will face off on Feb. 7 in Los Angeles, in a show to be broadcast live on ABC and Disney+, a new media partner for the Recording Academy after more than 50 years with CBS.
