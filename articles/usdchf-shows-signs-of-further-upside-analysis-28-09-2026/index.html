USDCHF Shows Signs of Further Upside – Analysis – 28-09-2026

TheTools · 28.09.2026 06:31 · source · permalink

 

 

The USDCHF pair rose during its latest intraday trading, with the main short-term bullish trend remaining dominant. The pair is moving along main and minor trend lines that support this bullish path, while also benefiting from trading above EMA50, which provides dynamic support and strengthens the chances of further gains in the near term. This comes as a bullish crossover begins to emerge in the relative strength indicators after the pair successfully eased its overbought conditions, giving it greater momentum to target new resistance levels in the coming period.

 

 

The NZDUSD price has been characterized by choppy trading in its latest intraday sessions, amid the dominance of the main short-term bearish trend, particularly with price movements along a trend line supporting this direction. This is further reinforced by continued negative pressure resulting from trading below EMA50, which is acting as dynamic resistance and limiting the chances of a full recovery in the near term. This also coincides with the emergence of a bearish crossover on the relative strength indicators after they entered excessively overbought territory compared with the pair’s price movement, increasing the intensity of the negative pressure surrounding the pair.
