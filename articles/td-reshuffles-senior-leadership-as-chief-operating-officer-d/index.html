TD reshuffles senior leadership as chief operating officer departs - CMT News

TheTools · 04.09.2026 20:31 · source · permalink

Fast, essential updates from across Canada’s mortgage market, housing sector and financial system.

Written by Steve Huebl• September 3, 2026• 5:20 PM• • Views: 1,134

TD Bank Group has announced a series of senior leadership appointments as it looks to accelerate its strategy, technology investments and adoption of artificial intelligence.

Effective Sept. 4, Vlad Shpilsky will become group head of Global Technology and Solutions, while continuing to lead the division. Renu Gupta has been appointed senior executive vice-president and chief strategy and commercial officer, bringing oversight of strategy, corporate development, digital assets, payments, partnerships and procurement under one role.

Paul Whitehead will become senior executive vice-president of Global Corporate Services, adding responsibility for global corporate affairs and enterprise AI to his existing mandate.

The bank also announced that Taylan Turan, group head and chief operating officer, will leave TD. CEO Raymond Chun credited Turan with helping advance the bank’s transformation agenda and strengthen its AI capabilities.
