New Images of Manchester City x C.P. Company 26-27 Fourth Kit Leaked Ahead of Debut

TheTools · 24.11.2026 17:15 · source · permalink
  • Manchester City
  • Kits
  • Puma
  • Premier League

Ahead of its official debut in tonight's UEFA Champions League clash against Napoli, new real-life images of the authentic Puma x C.P. Company Manchester City 26-27 fourth kit have been shared by BoriRafi.

The player-issue Ultraweave shirt features an olive green base with a faded, garment-dyed effect on the front, paired with black side panels, thin white accent lines, and a buttoned black collar. Vibrant orange is used for the Puma logos and Etihad Airways sponsor, while the left chest carries a circular black crest inspired by C.P. Company's signature lens. The photos also show the kit's Champions League sleeve badges and its solid olive back panel, which is printed with bright yellow Haaland 9 cup lettering and numbering.
