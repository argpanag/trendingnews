Bitcoin dips below $84,000 as oil jumps on Iranian tanker attacks

TheTools · 07.10.2026 14:25 · source · permalink

Bitcoin briefly broke under the level analysts flagged as the point where sellers take over. Oil and the dollar both climbed.

  • Bitcoin fell about 1.5 percent to just above $84,200 as escalating Iranian attacks on tankers pushed oil prices, Treasury yields and the dollar higher.
  • Dogecoin led losses among major cryptocurrencies with a 5 percent decline, while ether dropped 3.5 percent and XRP fell nearly 3 percent.
  • A sustained break below $83,000 could put Bitcoin on a quick path toward $80,000, according to FxPro, as traders await minutes from the Federal Reserve’s September meeting.
