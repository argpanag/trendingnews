Woman, 30s, dies after assault in Dublin on Monday

TheTools · 01.10.2026 15:12 · source · permalink

A woman in her 30s has died from injuries she sustained during a serious assault in north Dublin.

The incident took place near Main Street in Belmayne, Dublin 13 on Monday afternoon.

The woman was brought to Beaumont Hospital following the assault.

She died from her injuries on Wednesday gardaí said in an update.

A post-mortem examination has been carried out, the results of which will determine the course of the investigation.

A Family Liaison Officer continues to provide support to the family of the deceased.

A man in his 20s, who was arrested on Tuesday in connection with the assault, was released yesterday without charge, pending a file to the Director of Public Prosecutions.

Gardaí are appealing to anyone who may have witnessed this incident to contact them.

Anyone in the vicinity of Main Street, Belmayne between 3pm and 3.45pm on Monday, who may have video footage (including dash-cam) are asked to make this footage available to them.

Anyone with any information is asked to contact Coolock Garda Station on (01) 666-4200, the Garda Confidential Line on 1800 666 111, or any garda station.

Investigations are ongoing.
