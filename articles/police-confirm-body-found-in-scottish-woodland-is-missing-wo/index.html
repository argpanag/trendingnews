Police confirm body found in Scottish woodland is missing woman Kelsey Hunter

A body discovered in woodland in Dumfries and Galloway has been identified as that of Kelsey Hunter, a 20-year-old woman who went missing over the weekend, police have confirmed.

The discovery was made in a wooded area in the village of Springholm on Wednesday evening, after an intensive search had been under way in the village.

Ms Hunter had last been seen alive in Castle Douglas, a nearby town, at around 5pm on Saturday.

Police said her family are aware of the identification and continue to be supported by specialist officers.

A post-mortem examination has been carried out, the force also confirmed.

A 21-year-old man, Daniel McKnight, appeared in private at Dumfries Sheriff Court on Tuesday, charged with murder and attempting to defeat the ends of justice in connection with her death.

McKnight, who is understood to have been in a relationship with Ms Hunter, did not submit a plea.

He is due to appear again in court within eight days.

Police said extensive inquiries remain ongoing, with a number of personnel being deployed, including forensic specialists.

“Our thoughts remain with Kelsey’s family at this incredibly difficult time, and we are continuing to provide them with support,” Detective Chief Inspector Stephen McGrath, of Police Scotland’s Major Investigation Team, said.

“They have asked that their privacy is respected. Extensive work remains ongoing and a team of officers are continuing to carry out inquiries.

“I would like to thank everyone who has come forward with information which has helped our investigation.”

Officers will remain in the area while the investigation continues, with door-to-door and CCTV inquiries still being carried out.

Local Area Commander Emma Dodds said: “We understand how upsetting this has been for the local community, but we continue to have a significant amount of resources dedicated to this investigation.

“Anyone with any concerns, or information, can approach local officers or contact us on 101.”

Anyone with any information is asked to contact Police Scotland on 101, quoting incident number 2269 of 22 November.
