  7. 'We got the point, so very happy'published at 11:29 BST
    11:29 BST

    2UP Henseleit/Maguire v Lee/Duncan

    Team Europe's Leona Maguire and Esther Henseleit, speaking to Sky Sports...

    Henseleit: "I think we played really well at the beginning, made a lot of birdies. The back nine is tricky and playing a bit long with the wind and the rain.

    "The last six-seven holes were a grind. We made putts and made ups and downs. In the end, we got the point, so very happy."

    Maguire: "When the weather got bad and miserable - it was that extra little bit to grind it out. The crowds were amazing this morning. A few new chants and a few more songs that we haven't heard, so that was keeping us entertained."

  9. Europe level it up!published at 11:20 BST
    11:20 BST

    2UP Henseleit/Maguire v Lee/Duncan (F)

    After a nervy couple of holes, Leona Maguire and Esther Henseleit really gathered themselves nicely for that final hole to bring home a full point.

    They'd have felt those nerves too having been in charge all day only to let Andrea Lee and Lindy Duncan back into the contest late on.

    Coming down the final hole in foursomes is also a nervous time, but the European pair handled it brilliantly and after all that we're back to where we started the day with the Solheim Cup level at 2-2.

  11. 'If someone messes up, we don't stress'published at 11:13 BST
    11:13 BST

    Stark/Grant beat Zhang/Kupcho 3&2

    Team Europe's Swedish duo Maja Stark and Linn Grant, speaking to Sky Sports...

    Stark: "I think we're pretty decent players. That helps and that makes it a lot easier. It felt like we hit good shots from the start.

    "I think we're just so calm. If someone messes up, we don't stress out. It is easier to play in a pair. You never think that's a hard shot. You take so much pressure off of yourself. It is also pretty fun."

    Grant on holing out from the bunker on the 9th: "It was like a really good shot. Obviously, the three shots before that weren't very great."

  12. Final holepublished at 11:11 BST
    11:11 BST

    1UP Henseleit/Maguire v Lee/Duncan (17)

    It's a great matchplay hole the 18th this week with a creek snaking across the middle of the fairway which really makes club selection crucial.

    Both Lindy Duncan and Esther Henseleit take three woods to stay well back of the trouble - both go a little right off the tee but Europe have an advantage as the USA fell into the bunker.

    And remember they only need to halve this hole to win the match.

  13. Huge halfpublished at 11:05 BST
    11:05 BST

    1UP Henseleit/Maguire v Lee/Duncan (17)

    For as bad as their first two shots were, the recoveries were top draw. Leona Maguire played a blinder of a chip up over a big mound and onto a downslope but managing to stop it about five feet away.

    It's a nervy putt still for Esther Henseleit but she rolls it in beautifully to guarantee Europe at least a half in this match as they head to the 18th tee.

    Anna Nordqvist will want the full point though and get out of the opening session level.

  14. Europe notch first pointpublished at 10:59 BST
    10:59 BST

    Stark/Grant beat Zhang/Kupcho 3&2

    Rose Zhang puts a modicum of pressure on the Europeans by rolling in from around 15 feet.

    Two putts for the win for Europe. Maja Stark with a tentative stab. Yikes. That's not dead.

    Linn Grant now has a four-footer to win the match. Nanna Koerstz Madsen missed one from similar range a little earlier. But Grant makes no mistake. Blue point on the board.

  16. The end is nighpublished at 10:55 BST
    10:55 BST

    3UP Stark/Grant v Zhang/Kupcho (15)

    Linn Grant is in prime position for shot three. Just 69 yards to the flag. No need to take it on with the Americans in the water.

    She flicks a wedge on to the green and leaves Maya Stark a 20-footer down the hill.

    Jen Kupcho is heading to the drop zone, which is actually further away than Grant was. She has 80 yards left. And it has to go in close. It's decent but only a couple of feet inside the European ball.

    US have played four. Europe three. US need to win the hole to keep the match alive.

  17. Europe closing in on first pointpublished at 10:50 BST
    10:50 BST

    3UP Stark/Grant v Zhang/Kupcho (15)

    Back up the 16th and Rose Zhang is in the middle of the fairway. Will she take on the creek that runs in front of the green?

    She's got a three-wood in her hand and is deep in conversation with her caddie.

    The left hand comes off the club and she looks forlornly after her ball as it takes a hop short of the water and dives into it.

    If Maja Stark had any thoughts of also taking on the water, that Zhang shot made up her mind. It's a lay-up.

    This match is almost done in Europe's favour.

  21. Europe v USA head-to-headpublished at 10:44 BST
    10:44 BST

    USA power into a 2-0 lead, but Europe are ahead in the remaining two matches and if it ends up level in this first session it would be no surprise given how recent Solheim Cups have gone.

    Looking at the two sides coming in, USA held the advantage with a lower average world ranking and more major wins under their belts, but it's actually Europe that has more experience.

    Anna Nordqvist's side arrived with more Solheim Cup appearances and more points won than the Americans, even though they have twice as many rookies this week.

    Who do you see as the favourite? Thumbs up for Europe and down for USA.
