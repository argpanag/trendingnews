Killary Fjord 'one of world's best' travel experiences

TheTools · 23.09.2026 09:35 · source · permalink

Getty

Killary Fjord in Co Galway has been named as one of the world's best travel experiences, in the 2027 Lonely Planet listings.

It is among 50 destinations chosen for inclusion in the latest edition of the travel guide, billed as a collection of unforgettable locations.

Lonely Planet said the fjord is a strong contender for the most breath-taking place in the country, with "steep peaks that change dramatically with the seasons".

Tourism Ireland said the inclusion of Killary is a superb recognition of the fjord and the wider Conamara area.

The agency said the accolade is a reflection of the unique appeal the place has, as a special destination for oversees visitors, allowing them to take in "breath-taking scenery, rich history and to slow down and spend time in nature".

Other selections in the 2027 Best in Travel list include Quebec and Prince Edward Island in Canada, León, in Spain and the St Cuthbert's Way walk from the Scottish Borders to Northumberland in England.
