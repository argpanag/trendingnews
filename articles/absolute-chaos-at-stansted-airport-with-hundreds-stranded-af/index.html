Absolute CHAOS at Stansted Airport with hundreds stranded after 'power surge'

TheTools · 25.10.2026 20:35 · source · permalink

Passengers at Stansted Airport have been left stranded after reports of mass disruption caused by a power outage.

Holidaygoers have been pictured across social media queuing for security and baggage with huge crowds forming.

The airport has confirmed the issue was caused by a "power surge".

One traveller took to X calling it a "total systemic collapse" showing hundreds of people facing severe delays.
