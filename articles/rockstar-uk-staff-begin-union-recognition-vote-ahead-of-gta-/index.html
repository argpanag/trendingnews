Rockstar UK staff begin union recognition vote ahead of GTA VI launch | Межа

TheTools · 12.11.2026 11:02 · source · permalink

Photo source: The Register

The ballot will decide whether Rockstar formally recognizes the Rockstar Game Workers Union, while a separate dispute over alleged union busting approaches its final stages.

More than 1,600 Rockstar Games employees in the UK begin voting on union recognition on November 12. The ballot runs until November 26, and nearly all UK employees below director level are eligible to vote.

This is reported by The Register.

The vote will determine whether Rockstar recognizes the Rockstar Game Workers Union, part of the Independent Workers Union of Great Britain (IWGB). If successful, it would become the largest recognized union in the UK games industry.

The proposed recognition agreement follows four months of negotiations between management and employees. It would let workers negotiate over pay, overtime, pensions, bonuses, leave and other employment conditions, and secure consultation rights over HR policies and AI.

The ballot begins a week before Grand Theft Auto VI is due to launch on November 19, more than 13 years after its predecessor.

I am incredibly proud of the work we've put into making this game. I'm also proud of the union we've built here. For me, those two things are connected

- Nestor Uriate, an animation programmer and Rockstar Game Workers Union representative

The vote follows a year of conflict between Rockstar and the IWGB. The union accused the company of union busting after it fired 31 members in October 2025, saying they had been singled out for taking part in a private Discord group. Rockstar denied the allegations and said the workers were dismissed for gross misconduct, not their union activities.

The dispute reached an employment tribunal, where Rockstar unsuccessfully tried to have blacklisting allegations struck from the case. Closing submissions are expected on November 17 and 18.

Rockstar head of publishing Jennifer Kolbe recently told Bloomberg that management would respect the result of the recognition vote: “We go where the team goes. So if they want a union, they will have a union.”

The campaign has also received support from SAG-AFTRA and the Communications Workers of America (CWA), which represents workers at Blizzard. IWGB president Alex Marshall said the vote was an opportunity for Rockstar employees to take greater control over their working conditions.

What happens at Rockstar matters beyond the studio. Workers across the games industry are watching, and this ballot is an opportunity to show what can be achieved when people come together, organize and build the collective power to shape their own working lives.

- Alex Marshall, president of the IWGB
