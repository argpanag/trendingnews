Hans Isaac, 55, set to marry beauty queen Francisca Luhong, 31

TheTools · 16.11.2026 16:04 · source · permalink
    bookmark

Actor Datuk Hans Isaac has revealed that he will be tying the knot with former Miss Malaysia Francisca Luhong James in the near future.

Hans, 55, shared the happy news in an Instagram post on Monday (Nov 16), alongside a photo showing Francisca's finger adorned with an engagement ring.

“SHE SAID…….YES! Forever with my favourite person,” he wrote in the caption.

Hans, who is also the chairman of the National Film Development Corporation Malaysia (Finas), said his decision to marry Francisca was the best one he had ever made.

“To forever with you, my Kayan/Iban soon to be wife. Best decision I’ve made and she said yes.

"She’s stuck with me now,” he said.

The Kuching-born Francisca is a Malaysian model and beauty pageant titleholder who was crowned Miss Universe Malaysia 2020. 

Meanwhile, Hans also joked in his post that the announcement would finally put an end to the question he had been asked repeatedly about his marital status.

“No more ‘Hans, when are you getting married?’” he quipped.
