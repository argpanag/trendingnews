Lakers vs. Jazz Preview: Can L.A. grind out another win?

TheTools · 18.11.2026 08:21 · source · permalink
  • Los Angeles Lakers Discussion

The Lakers’ look to extend their winning streak to 10 on Friday against the Jazz.

The Lakers (13-2) are back at it on their home floor on Wednesday when they host the Utah Jazz (2-13). This is the second match between the teams with the Lakers currently leading the season series.

Start time and TV schedule

Who: Los Angeles Lakers vs. Utah Jazz

When: 7 p.m. PT, Nov. 18

Where: Crypto.com Arena

Watch: Spectrum Sportsnet

The Lakers’ winning streak remained intact in Tuesday’s gritty victory over the Charlotte Hornets. The reward? Another challenging game against the visiting Jazz no less than 24 hours later. The purple and gold will host a Jazz team that was on a 12-game losing streak prior to finally ending it against the Clippers on Tuesday.

When the Lakers last faced the Jazz, Luka Dončić dropped 52 points on them in a hard-fought game that went down to the wire. It was the same game when Los Angeles trailed by as many as 18 points because they couldn’t rebound and score the basketball to save their lives early in the contest. Luckily, Dončić and Austin Reaves caught fire at the right time to propel the team to victory.

While this formula was enough for the Lakers to win then, it’s not going to be the case on Wednesday. With Walker Kessler expected to return from a short absence, L.A. will need to impose their will inside the paint, especially given the lack of interior presence the Jazz are dealing with now due to injuries to Jaren Jackson Jr., Jaxson Hayes and Jusuf Nurkic.

It would be too much to demand Luka to score 50 points again — especially on the second night of a back-to-back — which is why it would be great if the supporting cast goes above and beyond again, just like how Quentin Grimes did in Tuesday’s victory.

Hopefully, this game sees a more balanced scoring effort from the Lakers. They’re going to have to push through fatigue since they played five extra minutes on Tuesday night, but this is one of those games where talent and hot shooting are very much needed. The Lakers’ defense will have to focus on Keyonte George, who has been the Jazz’s best player this season and is averaging 20.3 points, 4.5 rebounds and 6.6 assists per game. Jazz forward Brice Sensabaugh is also coming off a 40-point game performance, so look for him to stay hot.

If the Lakers have their legs and can manage to grit this one out as well, then they indeed have every right to be considered the hottest team in the NBA. It’s not going to be easy, but hey, the purple and gold have a 3-0 record in games with no rest thus far this season. Let’s see if that continues against the Jazz on Thursday.

Notes and Updates

  • Since both teams played on Wednesday, no injury report will be released until hours before tip-off.
