Strike says Hancock gas discovery may extend into its Perth Basin licences

[9:53 am] Strike Energy says Hancock Energy's Aulenti-1 gas discovery supports the potential of its nearby jointly held acreage in the northern Perth Basin, though the extent of the field remains uncertain.

  • Hancock's Aulenti-1 well intersected a significant conventional gas accumulation in the Kingia Sandstone, about 5km northeast of the West Erregulla gas field

  • Hancock interprets the field as extending into production licences L25 and L26, which Strike and Hancock own 50:50

  • Strike's Natta North prospect (2U: 30 PJ net) had already been mapped as a possible southern extension of the Aulenti structure

  • Strike sees potential to develop Natta North and Erregulla Deep (2C: 38 PJ net) through a tie-back to its proposed West Erregulla development (2P: 251 PJ net)

  • Further appraisal drilling is needed to confirm the field's extent and any resource across licence boundaries, subject to joint venture approval

  • Strike has begun planning the Kadathinni-Tathra 3D seismic survey across its 100%-owned EP503 and EP504 permits

Hormuz attacks escalate as Iran threatens to run US blockade with 14 tankers

[8:46 am] Attacks on shipping in and around the Strait of Hormuz intensified over the weekend as the US blockade squeezes Iran's oil revenue and efforts to end the war remain stalled.

  • Three tankers were struck leaving Hormuz on Friday and Saturday, according to maritime risk firm Marisks, which assessed Iranian involvement as likely

  • Bahrain accused Iran of striking a civilian industrial site on Saturday, while Iran's Revolutionary Guards said they hit US bases in Bahrain and two Patriot air defence radars

  • An Iranian lawmaker said 14 tankers are preparing to challenge the US blockade and threatened retaliation against regional oil facilities if they are attacked

  • About 43m barrels of Iranian oil are stranded inside the blockade zone, and US$3bn to US$6bn in monthly revenue could dry up by mid-December, according to Kpler

  • Shipping through Hormuz was about 65% below pre-war levels in the week to 18 November, according to UKMTO, with at least 22 attacks reported so far in November

  • Iran's foreign minister said talks on reopening the strait are pointless until Tehran's demands are met, including lifting oil sanctions and ending the US counter-blockade
