Irving posts 27/170/1 rushing line in Week 8 win

TheTools · 02.11.2026 00:38 · source · permalink

    Irving posts 27/170/1 rushing line in Week 8 win

    Bucky Irving

    TB Running Back #7
    Player Stats

    Bucky Irving rushed 27 times for 170 yards and a touchdown in the Bucs’ Week 8 win over the Falcons.

    The Bucs became the first team in over 50 years to not complete a pass for an entire game. In turn, Irving was able to rack up a career high in rush attempts. He then turned that into a career high in yards by averaging over six yards per carry. He could have posted even better numbers, but the Bucs put him on ice after his third-quarter touchdown. With the exception of one attempt from Irving, Gainwell saw 18 consecutive rush attempts to close the game. Gainwell wound up running for 140 yards on 25 attempts. To the Bucs’ credit, Gainwell was picking up chunk gain after chunk gain and Irving had already earned a massive workload. Irving failed to hit 100 yards on the ground in any of his first three games. He has since reached triple digits three times in his past four outings. Playing some of the best football of his career, Irving is pushing for an RB1 rank headed into Week 9 versus the Bears.
    - Kyle Dvorchak
