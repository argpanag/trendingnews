Reports this Tuesday suggest former Kerry GAA dual star Fionán Mackessy is absent from Henry Shefflin's Kilkenny hurling panel for 2027.

A dual star in his youth, Mackessy had been named on both the Higher Education Hurling and Football Teams of the Year in 2022, both in his capacity as an MTU Kerry player.

However, Mackessy turned heads in 2024, when he moved from his boyhood club St Brendan's to O'Loughlin Gaels, leading to his eventual transfer from the Kingdom to the Cats.

Kilkenny have seldom used "outside players" during their decorated history. Thus, Mackessy made history with his Leinster SHC medal in 2025, becoming the first player from outside Kilkenny to win a provincial medal with the county in 98 years.

Mackessy was an unused substitute for that year's Leinster final win over Galway, having played in three group games, and his Kilkenny career has largely continued in that vein, with the O'Loughlin Gaels star struggling to force his way into the starting lineup.

It now appears as though the 28-year-old will not be part of the setup in 2027, as county legend Henry Shefflin prepares for his first year in charge of the Kilkenny senior hurlers.

Fionán Mackessy missing from Kilkenny panel

Michael Verney of the Irish Independent reported on Tuesday afternoon that Fionán Mackessy will not be part of Henry Shefflin's Kilkenny panel for 2027, leaving his inter-county GAA future in doubt.

Mackessy is one of several intriguing names reportedly missing from the 2027 panel, including fellow O'Loughlin Gaels star Luke Hogan, as well as youngsters Pádraic Moylan, Jeff Neary, Rory Glynn, and Michael Brennan.

Their apparent departures follow the news in recent weeks that two of the greatest stalwarts of modern Kilkenny GAA have retired from inter-county hurling.

Eoin Murphy, a player many would put forward as the greatest goalkeeper of the modern age, announced his retirement early last month, after a season which saw him controversially float in and out of the starting XV.

That was followed last week by an even bigger blow, as veteran TJ Reid stepped away from the Kilkenny setup after 20 seasons in the black and amber.

Reid, the all-time leading hurling championship scorer, admitted late last week that a factor in his decision to retire was partly down to the length of time Henry Shefflin's Kilkenny rebuild is expected to take.

There are still no reports on Huw Lawlor's availability for 2027. Mackessy's clubmate missed the 2026 season for Kilkenny, and his club's SHC campaign.

Judging by the squad reforms rumoured on Tuesday evening, that rebuild is already beginning to take shape.
