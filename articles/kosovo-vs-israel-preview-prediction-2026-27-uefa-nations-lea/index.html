Key stats

  • Kosovo and Israel have met once in the Nations League, drawing 0-0 in Debrecen on Matchday 3 of this edition.
  • Coming into Matchday 5, Kosovo are level on five points with the Republic of Ireland in Group B3, three behind leaders Austria, while Israel are bottom with two points.
  • Kosovo opened their campaign with a 1-0 home win over Ireland but are winless in three Nations League matches since (D2 L1).
  • Kosovo have won ten of their 15 home Nations League matches (W10 D1 L4) and are unbeaten in their last five at home (W4 D1).
  • Israel are still looking for their first win of the 2026/27 Nations League (D2 L2), having scored two goals and conceded seven in their four matches.
  • Israel have not won any of their last four away Nations League matches (D2 L2) since a 2-1 win in Albania in June 2022.
  • Israel's 3-0 defeat to Ireland on Matchday 2 equalled the heaviest margin of defeat in their 24 Nations League matches.
  • Kosovo topped Group D3 unbeaten in the first Nations League edition in 2018/19 (W4 D2), and won both legs of their 2024/25 play-off against Iceland (2-1 and 3-1).
  • Kosovo's biggest Nations League win is 5-0 away to Malta in November 2018, and they have kept 12 clean sheets in 30 Nations League matches.
  • Israel topped Group B2 unbeaten in 2022/23 (W2 D2), finishing with eight points.
  • Vedat Muriqi has scored 14 goals in 23 Nations League appearances for Kosovo.
  • Eran Zahavi has scored six goals in eight Nations League appearances for Israel.

Kosovo vs Israel prediction

Kosovo will be banking on their notoriously strong home record to do them proud in this match, with three points needed to give themselves a chance of overhauling group leaders Austria. So far, Kosovo's only victory in this season's Nations League came in Pristina, a 1-0 triumph over Republic of Ireland. They also drew 1-1 at home to Austria, underlining how strong a side they are in front of their own fans.

Israel, meanwhile, are coming off the back of consecutive Nations League draws, one of them against Kosovo in Hungary. They've improved significantly at the back after shipping three goals in each of their opening two group fixtures, which should make for another tight encounter between these two nations.

However, Kosovo are on home turf and have to get three points. A win would keep them on Austria's tail and stop the Republic of Ireland from leapfrogging them into second place, so backing the hosts should be favoured.
