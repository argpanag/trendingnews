Sharon Stone's candid confession about dating

TheTools · 28.09.2026 00:28 · source · permalink

The 90s screen siren opens up about her dry spell

She’s one of Hollywood’s sexiest women, who is best known for playing sultry femme fatales and starred in some of the raunchiest scenes on the big screen. But in a surprising admission, Sharon Stone recently revealed that she has not slept with anyone ‘for quite a while’.

The Basic Instinct star spilt secrets about her love life in a new podcast, where she revealed she is currently single without a companion to share her bed or her life.

Sharon, 68, said, ‘I’ve been celibate for quite a while now because I’m at the point where I really only want to be with someone that I care for. And now that my kids are grown, I want someone that’s my companion, not just someone to have sex with. I’m kind of bored with that.’

The actress, who has been married twice, is mum to adopted sons Roan, 26, Laird, 21, and Quinn, 20. Her first husband was TV and film producer Michael Greenburg, whom she married in 1984 and divorced in 1990. She later wed journalist Phil Bronstein in 1998, but they split in 2004.

She has been publicly single since then, although she has been linked to several men, including Italian real estate investor and entrepreneur Angelo Boffa, Argentine model Martin Mica and actor David DeLuise. She also revealed that she once dated rapper Nelly.

‘I’m a really good girlfriend, but I haven’t been a girlfriend in a long time,’ she told The Big Bang Theory star Mayim Bialik on her Breakdown podcast. ‘And I’ve been a girlfriend to bad boyfriends. And I’ve been a girlfriend to good boyfriends. I’ve had good luck, and bad luck, and no luck at all. And for quite a long time, I’ve had no luck at all.’

And she admitted that, despite two marriages under her belt, she has yet to find her Mr Right. ‘I have had loves in my life, but I don’t think I’ve had the great love of my life, no.’

But she’s fine with it because psychics have predicted that she would find her soulmate in her late 60s. Sharon recalled that one psychic told her, ‘You’re not going to meet your person until you’re quite old, quite a bit older. And then they’re going to be like you. In a way, you’re going to identify with them. But it won’t be until you’re really quite a bit older, almost 70.’ Another psychic gave her the same prediction about meeting her partner as she approaches 70.

In recent years, Sharon declared she was ‘done’ with dating. ‘I’m done dating, I’ve had it with dating,’ she said in 2020. ‘I just find people to be insincere and not worth my time. I enjoy my alone time and time with my kids and my friends more. I don’t want any insincerity and bologna and game playing.’

The actress explained, ‘I have really good men friends, but I just feel when it comes to emotional maturity in relationships that men and women are seemingly in different spots. They aren’t at the same place right now.’

She revealed she was happy being single, with her three sons being the only men in her life. ‘This is a very good period of my life right now – my life is happy,’ she said. ‘I think somewhere in the back of your mind, you think maybe one day you won’t be a single parent. Then, eventually, you realise, I think it’s better. I’m no longer hoping for someone.’

Sharon, who spent years recovering from a brain aneurysm in 2001, had a very public custody battle over her eldest son, Roan, in 2008 with newspaper executive Phil Bronstein, following the breakdown of their relationship after a cancer scare. She decided to undergo a double mastectomy after tumours were found in her breasts, a decision he did not support. While her tumours ended up being benign and she did not have the operation, his callous response spelt the end of their marriage.

‘He thought I was ridiculous. He thought it was foolish. He thought I was making too many decisions myself,’ she said. ‘That was the end of the marriage. That was it. He was done with me then. It was over. It was just over in the room. You could just tell. It was over. It was just over.’

She continued, ‘I learned so much from having been so ill and regaining the gratitude for the simple things – for being able to see, for being able to talk, for being able to walk. I was ashamed that it took something so big. I was really ashamed of myself. I lost everything – my marriage, my career, my kids, everything.’

Having snogged some of Hollywood’s hottest stars such as Robert De Niro, Leonardo DiCaprio, Michael Douglas and Sylvester Stallone on screen, she shared that her Casino co-star Robert remains a favourite.

‘He’s the best kisser in the business. He kissed me right out of my shoes,’ she said. ‘He really just knocked me out, right? And Martin Scorsese cut, and he looks at us, and he goes, “I think we got it, but would you guys like another one?” We both looked at him and went, “Yeah, I think maybe we need one more”.’

One co-star she didn’t get on with was William Baldwin, who she worked with on the 1993 erotic thriller Sliver. He declared that he would ‘never, ever’ work with her again after they clashed over Sharon’s claim that the film’s late producer Robert Evans pressured her to sleep with him in an attempt to improve their on-screen chemistry. ‘If I could sleep with Billy, then we’d have chemistry on screen and save the movie,’ she said.

The film’s director, Phillip Noyce, explained, ‘The two were not at all happy opposite each other. There was such a great deal of animosity on set. Sharon’s open disdain for Billy resulted in him feeling extremely insecure.’
