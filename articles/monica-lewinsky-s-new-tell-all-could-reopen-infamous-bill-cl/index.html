Monica Lewinsky may have a tell-all book in the works.

According to Page Six Hollywood, the latest project from the 53-year-old has been making the rounds in publishing circles, with the book reportedly being shopped by WME. Per the outlet, a source close to Lewinsky confirmed that the tell-all is being pitched to publishers but declined to reveal any additional details.

As for what readers can expect, another insider told Page Six that the book could cover both Lewinsky's time in the White House — where she served as an intern under former President Bill Clinton — and their infamous affair, as well as her life in the years since, including her work as an anti-bullying advocate and podcaster.

Should the book make it to publication, it would mark the first autobiography from Lewinsky.

pandis media / UPI Photo Service/Newscom/The Mega Agency

She previously cooperated with a 1999 book by Princess Diana's biographer, Andrew Morton, called Monica's Story. Lewinsky has also been involved in other projects that depicted her affair with Clinton, 80, including serving as a producer on Ryan Murphy's 2021 TV series, Impeachment: American Crime Story, based on Jeffrey Toobin's book, A Vast Conspiracy: The Real Story of the Sex Scandal That Nearly Brought Down a President. She also penned a 2014 Vanity Fair essay about the aftermath of the scandal, titled "Shame and Survival."

Lewinsky entered the spotlight back in 1998, when she was 22, after it was revealed that she had an intimate relationship with Clinton during her time as an intern in the White House. The affair ultimately led to Clinton's impeachment, and in the years since, she's found herself the subject of public ridicule, being name-dropped in countless rap songs and serving as fodder for countless skits on Saturday Night Live.

Lisa OConnor/ AFF-USA.COM / MEGA

The intense public scrutiny prompted Lewinsky to become an anti-bullying advocate, launching a podcast on Wondery last year titled Reclaiming with Monica Lewinsky. The podcast, which draws on her own "journey in the public eye," per Apple Podcasts' logline for the show, has featured several big-name guests, including Lizzo, Sarah Paulson, Andrew Rannells, Kara Swisher, Miley Cyrus, Alan Cumming, Olivia Munn and more.

In addition to her advocacy, Lewinsky also has a production company, Alt Ending Productions, and has been a producer on various projects, including the Hulu limited series about Amanda Knox, The Twisted Tale of Amanda Knox, starring Grace Van Patten, which told the story of Knox's wrongful conviction in Italy and her life in the years after.
