Ben Shelton and Trinity Rodman Have Reportedly Split—Here’s Their Full Relationship Timeline

TheTools · 24.11.2026 18:12 · source · permalink

Nooooooo.

For the past two years, one of the sport’s brightest young stars, Ben Shelton, has been in a relationship with Washington Spirit soccer champ Trinity Rodman—and despite their busy schedules, they seemed to be head over heels in love with each other. That is, until November 24, when the Daily Mail broke the news that the power couple reportedly split.

Since you’re probs wondering how this couple came to be (and what led to their rumored breakup), I’ve done the work of rounding up all of their biggest relationship moments in a little timeline for your pleasure. Read it and weep!

March 2025

Ben hard launches their relationship on Instagram by sneaking a photo of Trinity kissing his cheek into a carousel post. Swipe to the end to see:

Soon after, Trinity clears up some of the discourse around her and Ben’s relationship—namely the story behind how they actually met—and makes a point to note that Frances Tiafoe, a friend of hers and Ben’s, did not introduce them to each other like he previously claimed.

“I can assure you Frances was not the reason we met or got together,” she said in an interview with ESPN. “I don’t know why he’s taking credit for that. Me and Ben can both cut that out. It was a solely me and Ben thing.”

For the record, their story went a little like this: “I was like, ‘Hey, when you gonna come visit me?’ and he was like, ‘I’ll come right now,’” Trinity explained in an Instagram video (below). “So then he flew out and the rest is history.”

By the end of the month, Ben appears on Trinity’s Instagram. In the photo, she leans back into Ben’s shoulder, while his face stays mysteriously hidden beneath a hat (despite her tagging him in the photo). Her caption? “Dibs 🤭.”

April 2025

Ben also chimes in on clearing up the story on how he and Trinity got together, telling the New York Post, “Tiafoe had nothing to do with it. He’s taking credit, [saying] ‘Oh, yeah, I put them together. I was like Danny Ainge.’ He’s a liar. He’s trying to make it about himself. It’s not about him, but that’s funny.”

May 2025

Ben gives Trinity prime placement on his feed with a super cute photo:

A few weeks later, Ben wishes Trinity a happy birthday on his Instagram page with a simple but sweet message that reads, “happy birthday beautiful girl, I love you.” Awwwww.

June 2025

Trinity later gives her followers an even better look into her and Ben’s relationship, sharing a compilation video of them sweetly holding hands during their adventures.

July 2025

Trinity is spotted in the stands with Ben’s family at Wimbledon.

And she later calls out Wimbledon commentators for mentioning her famous dad, Dennis Rodman, during Ben’s match:

So, so cute.

August 2025

Trinity roots for Ben on day one of the 2025 U.S. Open Tennis Championships, and literally pops out in the cutest ‘fit that consists of Ben’s face plastered all over her custom sweats.

October 2025

Trinity writes the sweetest tribute message for Ben’s 24th birthday:
“To the most beautiful soul I’ve ever met. Happy birthday Benny! You’ve changed me, loving me the way I never knew someone could. Even with your lifestyle you make me a priority every chance you get and I’ll always be grateful for every minute you love me. You inspire me everyday, and you always keep a smile on my face. Life with you is a dream. I love you NL❤️”

And Ben makes an appearance in the comments section, too, writing, “I love you ❤️.”

November 2025

Trinity’s soccer team, Washington Spirit, is beaten by Gotham FC in a match. Ben consoles her on the side of the soccer pitch like the great boyfriend that he is.

December 2025

Ben and Trinity’s relationship becomes wedding-guest official! The couple gets all gussied up for a friend’s wedding, and they look so good, it’s literally unfair.

January 2026

In a lil change of format, Trinity takes her followers along for her swanky date with Ben—and there’s a really intimate moment where Ben pops into frame to kiss her neck (squeal).

Trinity also sits down with Cosmo for an interview and reveals that Ben urged her to temporarily step away from soccer amid her injury.

“When I was going through the injury [this past season]. I was very scared to step away from my team because I was scared they might think, ‘Oh, Trinity just thinks she can do what she wants. She can leave and focus on herself. That's selfish.’ And Ben was like, ‘Your health is everything. You could get hurt and be done playing soccer, then what?’” she recalled. “And so he just reminded me that I need to prioritize myself before it's too late. And he said: ‘You are the driver. If you want to turn left, you're gonna turn left. Do you want to crash or do you want to keep going on the road?’”

She notes that birthdays and large celebrations can be difficult for her and Ben to do on the actual day, likely due to their sporty commitments. She added, “For us, it's so hard to celebrate literally anything. Even for our birthdays, we celebrated them, like the week before and the week after. We celebrate on the phone. Thankfully for preseason, with Spirit, I'll be going to Florida, and he'll be there.”

May 2026

Ben celebrates Trinity’s birthday with a carousel of images shared to his Instagram page, in true boyfriend style.

June 2026

Ben plays in—and wins—the ATP 250 tournament. While giving a speech after his impressive title win, Ben publicly thanks Trinity for coming out, and sweetly calls her his good luck charm: “I wanna thank my girlfriend for flying here from Brazil. I won a title at I think every tournament that she's been at this year. You’re definitely the lucky charm.”

That same day, Ben posts a photo roundup to his Instagram, which features Trinity front and center.

Trinity, meanwhile, gives a closer look at her adorable Ben 10 tank top and reminds her followers that she is Ben’s No. 1 fan.

September 2026

Wimbledon doesn’t go as planned for Ben (he was eliminated in the first round by Otto Virtanen in a shocking upset), but during the U.S. Open, he ends up besting Carlos Alcaraz in a five-set nail-biter.

And ofc, Trinity is there to show him all the love:

November 2026

The Daily Mail breaks the news that—after almost two years together—Ben and Trinity have reportedly called it quits, noting that “their conflicting work schedules and constant travel made it too challenging for the couple to continue maintaining their relationship.” Luckily, sources add that Ben and Trinity are “believed to remain close.”

Siiiiigh.
