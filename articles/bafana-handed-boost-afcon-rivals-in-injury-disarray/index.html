Bafana Handed Boost, AFCON Rivals In Injury Disarray

TheTools · 12.11.2026 14:02 · source · permalink

AFCON

Guinea Head Coach Paulo Duarte. Photo: Facebook: Fédération Guinéenne de Football-FGF

Guinea’s injury problems and disorganised preparations could benefit Bafana Bafana in their 2027 AFCON qualifying campaign.

South Africa have been handed a boost after Guinea suffered several setbacks ahead of their 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifying double-header against Eritrea.

The Syli National face Eritrea on Thursday before the return fixture, but head coach Paulo Duarte will be without several key players. Borussia Dortmund striker Serhou Guirassy and Celta Vigo midfielder Ilaix Moriba are among those unavailable.

Guinea Hit By Key Absences

Guinea have also seen further players withdraw from the squad, with replacements called up at short notice.

The absences have weakened a team that began their campaign with a defeat to South Africa before recovering with a narrow victory over Kenya, courtesy of Guirassy’s first-half goal.

With several attacking options unavailable, Swiss-based winger Aliou Baldé could be tasked with inspiring the attack. Valencia defender Mouctar Diakhaby’s return should provide greater defensive stability.

Duarte’s Preparation Problems Continue

Guinea’s difficulties have extended beyond player availability. Although their training camp began on Sunday, only eight call-ups had arrived by Tuesday amid reported airline-ticket problems.

Guinéefoot editor Saidou Diallo criticised the repeated organisational issues in an editorial titled “Syli loses its matches before it even plays them”.

The disruption comes as the Guinean Football Federation undergoes an administrative transition, leaving Duarte with limited time to prepare for the Eritrea double-header and providing Bafana Bafana with an early advantage.
