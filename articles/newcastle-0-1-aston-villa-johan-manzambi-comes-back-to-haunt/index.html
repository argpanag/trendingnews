Newcastle 0-1 Aston Villa: Johan Manzambi comes back to haunt Magpies in Premier League win

TheTools · 18.10.2026 00:49 · source · permalink

Report and free match highlights as Johan Manzambi's goal earns win for Aston Villa at Newcastle; midfielder had agreed deal to join Magpies in the summer before late swoop from Villa; Sven Botman escaped red card after a foul on Nicolas Jackson despite VAR review

Senior Football Journalist

Saturday 17 October 2026 20:56, UK

Johan Manzambi came back to haunt Newcastle as his first-half goal was enough for Aston Villa to win 1-0 at St James' Park on Saturday Night Football

The midfielder had agreed a deal to join the Magpies in the summer before a late bid saw him move to the Midlands instead, and he has impressed since making his debut.

He scored in the Champions League against Fernerbahce in midweek - on his 21st birthday - and continued his hot streak when he nodded home the only goal of the game in the 36th minute.

"I knew this team was very good. I played against them in the Europa League final last season. I knew it was a very good team with a big coach. I knew that it was perfect for me," Manzambi told Sky Sports after the game.

In conceding, Newcastle set a new club record of 16 successive games in all competitions in which they have failed to keep a clean sheet.

It came shortly after a controversial moment when Sven Botman should have been sent off. He was initially shown a yellow card for a reckless challenge on Nicolas Jackson, but VAR reviewed it for serious foul play. However, they decided not to upgrade the booking and Botman remained on the pitch.

Please use Chrome browser for a more accessible video player

"If VAR can't see that's a red, we're in trouble," Sky Sports' Jamie Redknapp said at half-time. "It is so dangerous. How he's not broken his leg there... I like to see aggression. I want to see 50/50 tackles, but you can't do that anymore."

After the game, Jackson told Sky Sports: "The referee said it was a yellow card but it was so high. Hopefully next time it is a red card. I nearly broke my leg, but it's fine. It's part of football."

However, Newcastle ultimately ended the first half with no shots on target. There were some better openings in the second half for the Magpies - including a wonderful save from Zion Suzuki to deny Botman - but that proved to be their only shot on target in a game where they lacked the final touch.

Newcastle were loudly booed at both half-time and the final whistle as the team's stuttering start under Matthias Jaissle continued.

Aston Villa were also guilty of not finishing their chances but looked far more like the team Unai Emery wants. The three points sends them level on eight points with Newcastle and just a place behind.

Jaissle: 'We were dominating, we just couldn't score'

Newcastle boss Matthias Jaissle speaking with Sky Sports: "It was a game where we were dominating. Overall I thought I was seeing a team giving a response.

"We had many situations where you saw that we were going to win that game and then we conceded a goal.

"We also need to be honest. We were shaky until half-time but then after that we showed a good response after half time.

"The second 45 minutes were again really aggressive. We won the important duels. Unfortunately we were just not smart enough in our decision making in the final third so that is something we need to improve.

"There were many occasions where we didn't find the right pass. Sometimes not the right timing. These things need to be combined pretty soon to be more efficient."

On Botman challenge: "It was really fast. The decision-making was clear from the referee. They even checked [VAR] the foul from Sven."

Mings: We're slowly grinding towards cohesion

Aston Villa captain Tyrone Mings to Sky Sports: "It was a perfect away Premier League performance. St James' Park is so difficult to come to, but you're see across the board that every game in the league at the moment is so difficult.

"It feels like a big win. I'm super proud of everything we've put into the season so far.

"It feels at times like we've had a lot of changes but the manager is keen to reiterate we have a 'no excuse'. We have enough players here who have experience playing at St James' Park and have experienced the league and we've added some fantastic players. Coming here is always difficult, but I'm super proud to keep a clean sheet.

"It feels like we're slowly grinding towards cohesion, but there's no other way than hard work.

"We've just had quite a long international break where players were away, but some were here and working hard but every day on the training pitch, in meetings, everybody is buying in to what the manager wants, how he wants to play.

"The league is tough, the standard is tough, we're playing in Europe is tough but that's the tough we want. We want to be up there, we want to play European football. This is the challenge we chose and we're super excited for the season."

Emery pleased as Aston Villa improvement continues

Aston Villa head coach Unai Emery to Sky Sports: "Hard, tough match, but the victory is fantastic. How we compete, how we play when we need it defensively, to feel in our structure, everybody, the commitment we needed.

"We were working on it in previous matches, in our analysis of how we needed to play, our game plan. Then we could have possibilities as well to dominate, sometimes with the ball possession.

"Try to impose with our positioning, try to use our skills, our game plan as well in transition sometimes, sometimes through keeping ball possession to stop them as well and to try to dominate.

"We did it in 90 minutes more or less, more in transition, but we defended well. We conceded not so many chances and we had a very good opportunity to score the second goal to finish the game.

"But how we are improving, how we are competing, how we are being aware, the things we need to work, I think is the most important how we did today."
