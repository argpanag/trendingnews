Jenna Dewan 'Likes' Video Providing Evidence Channing Tatum Isn’t Behind Alleged Burner Account

TheTools · 21.09.2026 19:29 · source · permalink

Over the weekend, a Channing Tatum theory overtook the Internet after some believed his burner account could have been unveiled.

If you did not see, an account was found to have been commenting on photos of Harry Styles and Zoe Kravitz, who is Channing‘s ex. Instagram users discovered that this particular account, dubbed “Nonya Bidness,” had only one follower: Channing. This led to the theory gaining a lot of steam.

Could Channing Tatum Be Behind Burner Account Leaving Comments on Harry Styles and Zöe Kravitz Posts?
by u/milflover4576 in Fauxmoi

Now, one pop culture vlogger, Stephanie Tleiji, from @stephwithdadeets, shared some evidence that it’s likely NOT Channing‘s burner Instagram account.

She explained that she believes the account was likely a Channing Tatum fan page at one point, as it had undergone three name changes over the years as confirmed on Instagram. That account might’ve decided to reboot, change their name, and remove followers. Of course, if this is truly what happened, then this anonymous person decided to keep Channing on as a follower. Channing does follow several fan pages dedicated to him.

In a video, she noted that resurfaced comments from the account in question claim to say they are a Gemini. Channing is not a Gemini.

And then, the account was apparently asked in a comment if they were actually Channing, and they replied, “I’m not him.”

Another compelling piece of evidence? Channing‘s ex wife, Jenna Dewan, actually “liked” Stephanie‘s post putting together all these theories to debunk the burner account rumors! A comment from the burner account did actually mention Jenna.

Channing has not made any sort of statement about this at this time.
