Tottenham player ratings vs Aston Villa with three 2/10s and lots of 3s in loss

TheTools · 19.09.2026 13:11 · source · permalink

Express Sport hands out player ratings after Tottenham's lunchtime clash with Aston Villa.

Roberto De Zerbi's Tottenham suffered their latest humiliation at home to Aston Villa, as the wait for a Premier League win extends beyond five matches. Having spent a fortune in the summer, the pressure continues to mount on Tottenham, who enter the international break in crisis.

It was a solid showing for Spurs up until the opening goal, with the home side creating plenty of chances. But, just like the four matches prior, Tottenham couldn't find a breakthrough, with Savio and Archie Gray efforts thwarted by the impressive Zion Suzuki. Having missed so many opportunities, Spurs always ran the risk of Villa taking the lead, and just before the break the visitors did just that. A horrendous defensive mishap saw the ball eventually land at the feet of Johan Manzambi, who was on hand to sweep home the opener.

De Zerbi shuffled the pack at half-time, and it looked to have worked a treat when Mohammed Kudus poked home to level things up. Although replays showed there was an offside in the build-up, meaning the wait for Spurs' first goal of the campaign went on. Instead, it was Villa who netted the second, as a simple route-one ball caused all manner of problems for the Tottenham defence, with Nicolas Jackson netting to double their advantage.

Emiliano Buendia put Villa even further ahead with a stunning effort from the edge of the box, with the Tottenham fans quick to boo their own players. Spurs did end their wait for a goal, with two late strikes proving nothing more than consolations in the end, with Tottenham once again left licking their wounds.

Here, Express Sport hand out player ratings...

Antonin Kinsky - 2

How often will he get away with these sorts of mistakes? Was culpable during the opening-day defeat to Brentford, and once again had holograms for wrists in the midday kick-off. Couldn't do anything for the Villa third mind.

Pedro Porro - N/A

Subbed off early due to an injury. Looked devastated.

Jan Paul van Hecke - 3

You'll see better defending on a Sunday League pitch tomorrow morning compared to the Dutchman's feeble efforts for the Villa second. Has looked good so far this season, and the late goal will do his confidence some good, but overall it was a poor showing.

Micky van de Ven - 3

Not much better. Far too standoffish and allowed Jackson to pick his spot with the shot. Otherwise fine, but his showing for the second goal was so bad it justifies the low rating.

Andrew Robertson - 2

Went walkabouts for the Villa goal and was sloppy in possession throughout the match. Was signed to bring some much-needed experience to the squad, but has arguably made things worse. Nice cross for the Van Hecke goal, but the damage was done.

Sandro Tonali - 3

Looks completely lost in the middle of the park and was once again given a schooling by his opposite numbers. Apart from a strike that whistled over the crossbar, the Italian showed absolutely nothing of note.

Mateus Fernandes - 3

Looking every bit like a player who has suffered back-to-back relegations. His decision-making is incredibly concerning for an £85million signing, who at one point earlier this summer was the club's record signing.

Rodrigo Bentancur - 4

Looked half a yard off it. Has been praised for his work rate this season, but didn't impact the game whatsoever.

Omar Marmoush - 2

Must've been playing with concrete in his boots. Every touch was heavy and his in-the-box movement left a lot to be desired. Hasn't started life at Spurs in the manner that he'd have wanted.

Dominic Solanke - 3

Noticed he was on the pitch for the first time when he was taken off. An utterly useless showing from a striker who looks out of his depth so far this season.

Savio - 3

If you asked ChatGPT to automate a textbook performance of his, it would be this. Always looks promising when on the ball, but his end product is borderline offensive at this point. Contrived to miss two golden chances in quick succession in the first half.

Substitutes:

Archie Gray - 5

Ought to have scored during the first half, but didn't particularly do much after that. To his credit, the young Englishman wasn't at the scene of the crime like some of his team-mates. But then again, the standards are on the floor.

Mohammed Kudus - 5

Was alive to the bouncing ball to score what he thought was an equaliser. Has to be starting if he's fit.

James Maddison - 4

Didn't impact the game when he came on and his effort to block Buendia's wonder goal was pathetic to say the least.

Conor Gallagher - 5

Ironic that his shot that led to the goal was actually going wide.

Lucas Bergvall - 5

Wasn't as bad as everyone else.
