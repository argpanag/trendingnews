Team Liquid Alienware Hosts Fan Pop Up at Best Buy 


To celebrate the 2026 season, Alienware is showing up for fans with Team Liquid to host a special free event in Brooklyn on Friday, November 13 – the day before Worlds finals. 
We’ve partnered with Best Buy to host the entire TLAW squad in their store directly across the street from Barclays Center, home of Saturday’s championship finale. Attendees can meet the LCS champions: Morgan, Josedeodo, Quid, Yeon, CoreJJ -- and special guests Pobelter, Broxah, Mayumi, with possible surprise appearances. 

  • When: Friday, November 13, 2 – 6pm ET
  • Where: Best Buy, 625 Atlantic Ave, Brooklyn NY 11217 (2nd floor, Atlantic Terminal Mall)
  • Cost: Free

This is more than a meet-and-greet. It’s a chance for fans to win prizes, get some TL swag, meet new and old friends and play some League against them -- or even TL creators. 

Event activities include: 

  • The Gear: See and play on the Alienware systems that will power Team Liquid Alienware’s practice and competition setups in 2027; attendees get exclusive hands-on playing time on the unreleased Alienware 25” 560Hz QD-OLED gaming monitor built for esports and competitive play. Coming early 2027, read more product info here.
  • The Challenge: Smite the Baron. Are you as skilled as Josedeodo? Smite just right on our Alienware 15 for a chance to win Alienware laptops and monitors, courtesy of Best Buy.
  • The Trivia: For those getting there early for a spot in the queue, join our hourly League trivia experience directly from your phone and test your knowledge about League and LoL Esports compared to everyone else in line. The Top 20 players across each Trivia wave will be eligible to win an exclusive Alienware Area-51 Lego Brick Kit.

The Exclusive Offer: Come by and ask for our exclusive, stackable 10% discount code for select Alienware products available on BestBuy.com.
