Arsenal prodigy Max Dowman has been spotted training in the brand-new Adidas F50 Messi 'Fútbol y Familia' signature boots, just days after being personally chosen by Lionel Messi for the latest Adidas 'Messi +10' campaign.

Max Dowman Trains in Adidas F50 'Fútbol y Familia' Messi Boots

Arsenal's training session ahead of their European fixtures revealed the 16-year-old attacking midfielder wearing the new Adidas F50 'Fútbol y Familia' Messi signature boots.

The boot sighting follows the official launch of Season 3 of Adidas' 'Messi +10' initiative. Under the campaign, the eight-time Ballon d'Or winner personally hand-selected ten rising stars from across global football to wear his signature F50 boots on the pitch alongside him.

Dowman is the youngest male prospect included in the exclusive group. The ten selected talents feature a mix of men's and women's future stars, including Can Uzun (Eintracht Frankfurt), Gilberto Mora (Tijuana), Kees Smit (AZ Alkmaar), Tomás Aranda (Boca Juniors), Zavier Gozo, Jesús Rodríguez, Chloe Sarwie, Giovanna Waksman, and Lotta Wrede.

Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta reacted positively when asked about Messi's public endorsement of the Hale End academy star.
