Get Ready to Enjoy the Premiere of the "Dragon Ball Super: Beerus" Anime even more! Here is a Roundup of All the Information Released So Far!

  • Dragon Ball Super: Beerus

The highly anticipated premiere of the "Dragon Ball Super: Beerus" anime in Japan is finally arriving this weekend!
The "Dragon Ball Official Site" has brought you the latest news on the series, but this time, we are bringing you a pre-broadcast special rounding up all the major topics revealed so far!
Let's perfectly review everything we know so you can enjoy the broadcast to the fullest!

The very first episode of "Dragon Ball Super: Beerus" airs on Sunday, October 11 at 11:15 PM!
Plus, the fastest streaming will kick off on "Prime Video" and "U-NEXT" at 12:15 AM, just 30 minutes after the broadcast ends!
Other video streaming services will sequentially begin unlimited viewing and rental streaming every Thursday at 12:15 AM. Whether you are experiencing the real-time excitement in front of your TV or rewatching your favorite scenes on demand, you can fully enjoy the show in your own style!

"Dragon Ball Super: Beerus" super launch visual

"Dragon Ball Super: Beerus" super surge visual

Here are two super visuals that symbolize the world of this series and the fierce battles to come!
In "super launch visual", which features a striking composition of Goku glaring up at God of Destruction Beerus, the giant Earth floating in the background immediately catches the eye.
Faced with Beerus reigning as a God of Destruction, how will Goku fight to protect the Earth? It is a dramatic piece that will surely make your heart pound.

On the other hand, the "super surge visual", which expresses the expanding scale of the world, hints at even more battles awaiting Goku!
If you look closely, you can see Beerus holding the Earth in his hand. You will find yourself captivated once again by the incredible level of detail in this artwork!

A total of three trailers have been released so far, each coloring the prologue of the story from a different angle.
The "SUPER Launch Trailer", first unveiled at the "Dragon Ball Genkidamatsuri" in January 2026, announced the arrival of the overwhelmingly majestic God of Destruction Beerus and the beginning of a story on an unprecedented scale.
Next, the "SUPER GEKITOU Trailer" released at the "Dragon Ball Games Battle Hour 2026" in April featured breathtaking action sequences and revealed the appearance of Frieza at the very end, instantly accelerating expectations for future story developments.
Finally, in the "Super Surge Trailer" unveiled in September 2026, just a month before the broadcast, fans were treated to a fast-paced video synchronized with the opening theme and ending theme, kicking off with a Kamehameha fired by Goku in his Super Saiyan God form!

Take this opportunity to fully enjoy the thrilling highlights scattered throughout each trailer once again!

Do not miss the music information that made its debut in the "Super Surge Trailer" introduced above!
The opening theme for this series, "ONOGAMI", is performed by MEGATERA・ZERO (pictured left).
Additionally, VK Blanka (pictured right) is in charge of the ending theme, "Kan Peki No Peki".
Both songs are packed with deep passion for "Dragon Ball Super: Beerus" and characters like Goku, making them an absolute must-listen, lyrics and all!

Both tracks are scheduled to hit various music streaming services on Monday, October 12! Keep the excitement of the anime alive and dive into the world of "Dragon Ball Super: Beerus" through its music!

A video message has arrived from Masako Nozawa, who plays the three roles of Son Goku, Son Gohan, and Son Goten in "Dragon Ball Super: Beerus"! It is currently available to watch in the "Weekly Dragon Ball News" episode released on Monday, October 5.
Masako Nozawa's powerful and heartwarming message begins around the 4:31 mark in the video linked in the related articles. Be sure to check it out before the premiere and raise your Ki to the max!



That wraps up our perfect review of all the information you need before the broadcast begins!
Once again, please look forward to the premiere of "Dragon Ball Super: Beerus", airing on Sunday, October 11 at 11:15 PM!
