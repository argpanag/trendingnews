  2. 'We have to take responsibility'published at 20:14 BST
    20:14 BST

    FT: Chelsea 0-2 Tottenham

    Chelsea

    Chelsea boss Xabi Alonso, speaking to BBC Match of the Day: "I think that there were many different moments in the game. First half I wanted more intensity. We wanted high pressing but we were not strong enough in duels.

    "We had some kind of chances in the first half and in the second half, we talked and we started a bit better. We had a good chance with Pedro [Neto]. We had spoken about how important the first goal would be and when they scored I felt 'ah, this is not what we wanted'.

    "Straightaway almost, came the red card. Disappointed with the result and we have to be honest with the performance, we could have played better."

    On the performance: "There were moments we planned to be more aggressive and with the ball we lacked a little bit of composure to give more passes. It was about it being a derby and going through difficult moments that you need to manage.

    "One key moment was the first goal."

    On Mike Penders' mistake: "This is football. It is about doing great things and dealing with difficult moments. We will support Mike. We come together from these moments."

    On missing Joao Pedro: "It would be so easy to talk about the players we're missing but with the squad that we have, we believe we can do better with the team that we have.

    "We have to be self-critical and take responsibility."

    On Cole Palmer red card: "I have to check. It was hard for everyone to deal with 1-0 when the game was so close. It affected a bit our emotional control."

  3. 'This is a benchmark'published at 20:09 BST
    20:09 BST

    FT: Chelsea 0-2 Tottenham

    Tottenham Hotspur

    Tottenham defender Andy Robertson, speaking to BBC Match of the Day: "Stamford Bridge is a difficult place to come and it always is for every team that comes here, and obviously it's a London derby and things like that so it adds to it but I just think the most pleasing thing was how we played, how we went about our business today.

    "Everyone was outstanding and then you get the rewards for that and the result you deserve because it's not always happened this season. We've probably played better than what our results suggested and we got the result Monday and we wanted to really kick on from that.

    "I think today was our best performance of the season."

    On what Tottenham were doing right today: "We believed in ourselves and I think that was key we cam here and believed we could get the result and I think we showed that from minute one.

    "We were comfortable on the ball, we played through their high press, we were aggressive when we didn't have the ball and believed we'd come here and get the result and that's what we've been working on and trying to get through the whole changing room.

    "It's just about the belief because we have the quality, I think nobody denies that when they look at the squad."

    Was belief more important or as important than any tactics today? "Of course, we've always been a good set up in terms of what we try and do and the different things we try and do in every single game. I think tactically, we've always been there but maybe the belief within it hasn't always been there.

    "Thankfully it all pieced together today."

    Are we seeing Roberto de Zerbi's Spurs now? "We've seen it today and I think that's all we can say just now because we hope that it is but it's up to us to go and do it now. I think today was exactly how the manager wants us to play in every single game.

    "That's the benchmark now - it's just one-off performance we've done against Chelsea, sent our fans home happy and don't kick on. This is a benchmark and we have to keep pushing."

  5. 'A big Tottenham statement'published at 20:05 BST
    20:05 BST

    FT: Chelsea 0-2 Tottenham

    Martin Keown
    Former England defender on Final Score

    Tottenham have made a big statement in a really convincing performance. Whether it was the win on Monday night that sparked something, or just what this fixture means to the players – it’s always been a bit feisty, shall we say? I think they stood up to it really well.

    I thought Maddison was magnificent in midfield. He's like the Artful Dodger, isn't he? That midfield is full of mischief, but he's brave against the ball. He sets the tone, and he got them all playing really well.

    I thought Tel was magnificent out on the left-hand side, Kudus on the right was also really effective, and everybody really managed to handle the ball really well on the night. Spurs had almost 70% possession, which is remarkable for a team which was just a few places from the bottom of the table.

    I think what’s changed for Spurs is the key personnel. De Zerbi said just a few weeks ago that this team lacks personality, lacks character. So, he put Maddison in, he took a gamble. Maybe he could have managed his minutes better because he's now injured. But he’ll be thinking, ‘Ok, that's paid off, it's got the momentum going’ - they've got a vital win. He has to take gambles and it's paid off – as long as Maddison is not out for too long, because he's really important to what they do.

  7. 'You have to run, to fight - and we did'published at 20:00 BST
    20:00 BST

    FT: Chelsea 0-2 Tottenham

    Tottenham Hotspur

    Tottenham manager Roberto de Zerbi, speaking to BBC Match of the Day: "I think we deserved to win. The players worked very well, prepared very well the game. This morning, yesterday, all days in the last week and the qualities of the players are very clear.

    "I'm happy for the spirit of the players. I think we have to change the last part of the history of our club. I would like to follow in this way."

    On fight spiriting: "Football is the quality of the play, the quality of the players, for sure, but it is a competition. It is a fight. You have to run, you have to fight, you have to arrive first to the second ball. You have to win the duels and we did."

    On tactics: "We knew Chelsea play very well, they have clear organisation, clear structure on the pitch. We prepared our players with two midfielders and James Maddison with freedom, where he smells the right position."

    On James Maddison injury: "He played very well while he was on the pitch then [Conor] Gallagher played with different characteristics. Different style but he played very well.

    "James is a different player. He feels football, he understands football. It is easier for me when you have a player of his talent.

    "I think it is not a big problem. We have to see."

    On Mathys Tel: "Mathys needs to play to understand football, become calmer but he is a big talent."

    On confidence after a big win: "I think it is important for the players to belief more in themselves because their qualities are very high. We are happy for the fans, first of all, because we know what it means to beat Chelsea and for the club as well.

    "In the last years they suffered, like the fans, they believe in this project, in this group and I'm happy for them."

    Best performance? "With Tottenham, of course."
