TheTools · 31.10.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

Renato Moicano, Tom Nolan clash in UFC Vegas 123 main event

MMA Fighting has UFC Vegas 123 results for the Moicano vs. Nolan fight card, a live blog of the main event, and more from Meta APEX in Las Vegas on Saturday night.

In the main event, Renato Moicano squares off against Tom Nolan in a lightweight contest. Moicano recently got back in the win column with a second-round face-crank submission win over Chris Duncan, while Nolan has rebounded from a loss in his UFC debut with five straight victories.

Randy Brown faces short-notice replacement Jacobe Smith in a welterweight contest in the co-main event.

Check out UFC Vegas 123 results below.
