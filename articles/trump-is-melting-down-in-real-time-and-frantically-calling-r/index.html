Trump Is Melting Down In Real Time And Frantically Calling Reporters

TheTools · 04.11.2026 00:52 · source · permalink

President Donald Trump spent early Tuesday night making desperate phone calls and blasting Fox News before midterm election results came in.

“I have the ultimate power — it’s called ‘veto,’” President Trump told CBS News in a phone interview before polls first began to close in some states. Already, Trump appeared to make excuses for a potentially massive Republican loss.

Trump also called the Financial Times, assuring reporter Edward Lucas that the U.S. has no immediate plans to strike Iran, the Middle East country Trump started a war with in February.

Trump had a particular grudge against Fox News Tuesday night, texting commentator Greg Gutfeld about his disdain for liberal Fox News commentator Jessica Tarlov.

Earlier in the evening, Trump blasted Tarlov and Fox News on his Truth Social page.

“Watching the Pre-Election Results on FoxNews, but I can’t stand Jessica Tarlov’s voice — DUMB AS A ROCK, but it’s the voice that gets me,” Trump posted on Truth Social. “Have to go somewhere else until that LOSER is off the air — I’ll be back later!”

Less than an hour later, Trump posted again:

“The FoxNews Poll, always the worst, is so insulting with the questions they ask,” Trump wrote. “Every question is a hit job, and every answer is ‘BULLSHIT!’ This has been going on for years.”

That same hour, a clearly frantic Trump blamed a potential Republican loss on everyone but himself.

“No wonder FoxNews Ratings are collapsing, and they’re going to go down a lot further,” Trump’s rambling post said in part. “They’re way down, not because of me, but because they are AGAINST me! This happened once before, and Fox almost collapsed. It’s happening again!”
