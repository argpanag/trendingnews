Follow Lecce-Juventus, a match in the eighth round of Serie A

Lecce-Juventus comes with a lengthy injury list and three points crucial to both teams. The penultimate fixture of Serie A 2026/2027's eighth round kicks off at 6pm at the Via del Mare in the Salento capital.

Eusebio Di Francesco's Lecce have lost three key starters in three days, Coulibaly, Ilici and Pierotti, but must maintain their good form. Victory would lift them above Luciano Spalletti's Juventus. Spalletti is on "a hot seat" (in the words of CEO Carnevali), while Juventus have gone two league matches without a win. They have also lost Nico Gonzalez and Celik to injury and arrive after a controversial Europa League draw against Rennes.
