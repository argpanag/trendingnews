How About Stat?: Pre-match facts for Ipswich

TheTools · 12.09.2026 13:25 · source · permalink

First-team

Crystal Palace host Ipswich Town on Saturday (15:00 BST) afternoon as we return to Premier League action against the Tractor Boys.

Here are some of the key pre-match stats, as the Eagles look to extend our unbeaten run to three games.

1995

Palace won both Premier League meetings with Ipswich Town 1-0 in 2024/25, last winning three league games in a row against them in February 1995.

22

Ipswich are without a clean sheet in their last 22 Premier League games, conceding 57 goals in that time (2.6 per game).

Five

Gary O’Neil is yet to beat the Eagles in his five previous Premier League encounters (D1 L4), only losing more often in the competition to Manchester United and West Ham United (both 5). His sides have conceded 2+ goals in all five games.

Sixth

Khalaili leads the Premier League for both tackles (17) and true tackles attempted (24), with his true tackle win rate – the proportion of time he touches the ball when tackling – is 70.8%, the sixth-highest in the league (min. 10 true tackles attempted).

103

When Tyrick Mitchell got his double in the win at Fulham, he had scored as many goals in one game as he did in his previous 103 appearances combined.

One in seven

The Eagles have lost just one of our last seven meetings in all competitions with Ipswich Town, a 3-0 defeat back in the 2013.

Four

Palace are yet to win four consecutive meetings (all competitions) with Ipswich, will that change on Saturday?
