Major outage closes Cork's 'Posh Dunnes'

TheTools · 05.10.2026 11:44 · source · permalink

Cork's biggest and (probably) best-loved Dunnes Stores is closed since this morning - thanks to a major IT outage that basically shut down its key systems.

CorkBeo understands the tills stopped working at the Dunnes Stores in Bishopstown Court at around 6am this morning, as staff were preparing to open for early-morning customers.

We were told that shoppers arriving to do some early morning shopping were told by staff that there hasd been a 'major IT outage' and apologised for the inconvenience. One shopper said it was 'weirdly quiet' and they were informed by a staff member that their broadband and connectivity had completely failed.

It's understood that the store - known to many Cork shoppers as 'Posh Dunnes' - will reopen in the afternoon, as IT experts are working quickly to restore the systems.

Dunnes Stores - as any proud Leesider will tell you - started in Cork with Ben Dunne Snr opening his first store on Patrick Street in 1944. The current Patrick Street store recently got planning permission for an outside terrace cafe that is expected to be in place by next Spring, as Patrick Street looks forward to a new lease of life with the regeneration of the landmark former Roches Stores/Debenhams building.

For the new terrace cafe on Patrick Street, Cork City council granted permission for the use of the existing terrace area as a Dunnes Stores café seating area, as well as the installation of a retractable canopy and side screen for extra weather protection. It will be available for use by customers of the café during normal opening hours until 5 pm.

The new terrace cafe will be on an existing outside terrace on the third floor at the rear of the Patrick's Street Dunnes building, facing Bowling Green Street and Drawbridge Street. Turning it into a covered outdoor space for enjoying a coffee or lunch is part of the retailer's big plans for its restaurant refurbishment. The Irish supermarket giant has transformed its third-floor restaurant - providing a major boost to its food offering, which includes the food hall (which was revamped in 2024) at the basement level and a popular ground-floor café.
