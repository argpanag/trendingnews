You can save 20% off detailed Christmas ornaments based on the original PlayStation and Xbox consoles, Link from Tears of the Kingdom, and many more. Let’s take a look.

PlayStation Hallmark Keepsake Christmas Ornament

PlayStation Console - Hallmark Keepsake Christmas Ornament

This ornament is a tiny version of Sony’s original PlayStation game console, which came out in the US in 1995. The whole thing is lovingly recreated in miniature, and it even plays the iconic startup sounds when you press the power button (you can see a video of this in the Amazon listing). For more on this important piece of hardware, check out our recent review of the LEGO PlayStation set and see how that buildable version compares to the real thing.

Xbox Hallmark Keepsake Christmas Ornament

Xbox Console - Hallmark Keepsake Christmas Ornament

Microsoft threw its hat in the game console ring with the Xbox console in 2001. This ornament is a tiny version of the hardware that also lights up and plays the startup sounds when you press its tiny power button. And if you’re interested in putting together a block-based build of the original Xbox, you can do that too, with the Mattel Brick Shop version.

Link Hallmark Keepsake Christmas Ornament

Link - The Legend of Zelda - Hallmark Keepsake Christmas Ornament

While the Switch 2 remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time is getting all the attention lately (see our review), this ornament celebrates the Tears of the Kingdom incarnation of the hero of Hyrule. It’s a finely detailed take on Link, and it lights up and plays Tears of the Kingdom music when you press a button.
