Dangerous Seduction in New Trailer for "The Scandal" - Kdrama Kisses

TheTools · 17.09.2026 07:00 · source · permalink

Another trailer is out for the new Korean drama The Scandal starring Ji Chang Wook (The Manipulated) and Son Ye Jin (Thirty-Nine). This one goes more in depth with the wager involved between Ji Chang Wook and Son Ye Jin as a dangerous game of seduction begins.

The Scandal is set during the Joseon Dynasty where a woman (Son Ye Jin) frustrated by the limitations society has placed on her orchestrates a high stakes game of seduction by recruiting a notorious charmer (Ji Chang Wook) to seduce a young widow (Nana) devoted to a life of chastity.

The Scandal is scheduled to premiere September 18 on Netflix.
