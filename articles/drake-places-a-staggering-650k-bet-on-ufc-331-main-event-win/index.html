Drake Places A Staggering $650K Bet On UFC 331 Main Event Winner - "I Don't Know Bro...But He Better Win"

TheTools · 19.09.2026 21:45 · source · permalink

Rapper Drake has placed a whopping $650k bet on the UFC 331 main event winner, even though he seemed unsure about his pick while also admitting that he doesn’t know who the fighter is.

The main event, which will take place at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, will see flyweight champion Joshua Van rematch Alexandre Pantoja.

With the fight only a few hours away, Drake shared his massive bet slip on his Instagram Stories, picking Van to emerge victorious. The Canadian rapper hilariously captioned the story:

I Don’t Know Bro…But He Better Win.

I Don’t Know Bro…But He Better Win.

Check out Drake’s Instagram story below:

Van is coming off a TKO win over Tatsuro Taira at UFC 328 in May 2026. Prior to that, he had beaten Pantoja at UFC 323, which also saw him win the flyweight championship.

For Pantoja, this will be the first fight since that loss to Van, where he also broke his arm.

Drake’s Curse Looms Large Over Joshua Van Ahead Of Alexandre Pantoja Rematch At UFC 331

It is a well-known fact that Drake’s picks, especially in MMA, mostly fail. The most notable one among them, also a very recent one, is his mind-blowing $1 million wager on Conor McGregor to beat Max Holloway in the rematch at UFC 329.

McGregor suffered an early TKO (injury) loss in the very first round. Similarly, at UFC 305, the 39-year-old placed a $450,000 bet on Israel Adesanya to beat Dricus Du Plessis, only for the New Zealander to lose via submission in the fourth round.

Charles Oliveira was another victim of Drake’s curse, as he lost via first-round KO to Ilia Topuria after the rapper had a $200,000 bet placed on the Brazilian. Over to Joshua Van now!
