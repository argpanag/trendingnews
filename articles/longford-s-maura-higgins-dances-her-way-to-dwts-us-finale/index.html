Longford's Maura Higgins dances her way to DWTS US finale

TheTools · 24.11.2026 06:00 · source · permalink

It is only seven years since Maura Higgins left her hairdressing job in Ballymahon, Co Longford, for Love Island.

And the rest, they say, is history.

Tonight, the 35-year-old Longford woman will compete in the Dancing with the Stars US finale alongside her dance partner Mark Ballas.

"People are very proud of Maura, and I think anyone who knows Maura knows she's put the hard graft in, she's a grafter," said Pat Byrne, who owns Skelly’s Bar in Ballymahon.

It was in Skelly’s Bar that Maura held her homecoming party after her Love Island success.

But even before that, Mr Byrne said she stood out.

"Maura always stood out, you could see Maura had something."

Like everyone else in Ballymahon, he has watched on with immense pride as Maura has built a career across reality TV, presenting, modeling, and brand partnerships.

Tonight, as she takes to the stage for the Dancing with the Stars US final, Mr Byrne said the country is behind her.

"Never mind the midlands, the whole of Ireland is rooting for Maura."

'She's the same person we grew up with'

Pat's son, Pedro Silva Byrne, said that despite her fame, Maura has never lost sight of her roots.

"She’s good craic and she wouldn't let the fame go to her head," he said.

"She's the same person we grew up with. Hopefully she can win, she’s done brilliant so far, it’s amazing for her," he added.

Ms Higgins grew up in the parish of Forgney, close to Ballymahon, and attended Ballymahon Vocational School.

One of her former teachers, Anne Tully, said everyone is so proud of her success.

"She is a testament to having your dream and working for it," said Ms Tully.

"Every competition she has passed through has been a lesson in resilience and perseverance.

"All of us here are so proud of her and wish her all the best in the final," she added.

The reality TV star and her dance partner earned a perfect 30 out of 30 for their samba in the semi-finals of the show.

However, Ms Higgins has been ranked an outside bet to go all the way on the show.

There are five dance couples in tonight's finale.

They will each perform three routines - a judges' choice dance, an instant dance challenge and a freestyle section.

Voting is unavailable in Ireland and is limited to the US and Canada.

Despite this, Ms Higgins says she still feels the support from home.

"Even though they can't vote for me, I can still feel the love and support even though we are here thousands of miles away," she told the programme.

"I can't let Ireland down, that's for sure."

Ms Higgins second cousin, Evie Higgins, said everyone is the family is so proud of her success.

"We’re just so proud of her," she said.

"She’s worked so hard to get where she is so we're delighted and we really hope she wins tonight."

It is understood that Ms Higgins' final performance will bring a distinctly Irish flavour to the ballroom.

Whatever happens tonight, she can be sure that people in her home county are bursting with pride.

"She really has put Ballymahon on the international stage," said local Fine Gael Councillor Paul Ross.

"She started in Love Island seven years ago, and now she's reached the very pinnacle of US television.

"She got to the final of Traitors (US) this year, and now she's in the final of Dancing with the Stars, they're two of the biggest shows in American TV. Everyone in Ballymahon is very, very proud of her.

"She's worked very, very hard, she's been extremely well managed, and everyone's rooting for her now tonight."
