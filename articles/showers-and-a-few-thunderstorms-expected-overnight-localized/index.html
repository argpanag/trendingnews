Showers and a few thunderstorms expected overnight | Localized flooding possible

TheTools · 12.09.2026 13:17 · source · permalink

Rain is slowly moving into western counties, and it will gradually move east overnight, ending in Lancaster and Lebanon Counties by 9 a.m. Sunday.

We are not outlooked for severe weather, but we are outlooked for a slight risk of heavy rainfall. If you're under a downpour, watch for ponding on the roads, and isolated flash flooding in low-lying areas with poor drainage. Overnight lows will fall to 70 degrees, and it will be muggy. Some patchy fog is possible.

The WGAL News 8 Storm Team has designated Saturday night as an Impact Night, meaning the weather could disrupt your normal schedule and routine.

Timing of the rain

  • Late Evening: Rain develops in northwestern counties
  • Overnight: Rain moves across the Susquehanna Valley from west to east
  • By 9 a.m. Sunday: Showers end in eastern counties
  • Sunday Afternoon: Partly sunny, warm and humid

Rainfall and impacts

  • Expected rainfall: 0.10 to 0.75 inches for most
  • Isolated higher amounts are possible
  • Watch for flash flooding in vulnerable spots

Sunday outlook

  • Gradual clearing
  • Warmer temperatures
  • Highs in the low-to-mid 80s
  • High humidity
