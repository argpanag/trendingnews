Athletics Ireland is deeply saddened to learn of the passing of Ciara Mageean.

Ciara was a much-loved and inspirational figure who touched the lives of so many. Her courage, warmth and spirit will leave a lasting legacy.

The thoughts and heartfelt condolences of everyone at Athletics Ireland are with Ciara’s family, friends and loved ones at this incredibly difficult time.

May she rest in peace.

Ciara Mageean 1992 - 2026
