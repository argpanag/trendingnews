Gameday: Texas A&M vs Kentucky

TheTools · 19.09.2026 20:16 · source · permalink

No. 9 Texas A&M opens SEC play hosting the Kentucky Wildcats in Week 3 at 2:30 p.m. on Saturday at Kyle Field.

The ninth ranked Texas A&M Aggies hosts Kentucky in its SEC opener Saturday at 2:30 p.m. on ESPN. 

Saturday's contest between Texas A&M and Kentucky will be just the fourth meeting all-time between the two programs, and the first meeting since 2018. Although the Aggies lead the series 2-1, the games have been close, with six points being the largest margin of victory for either team, and that was in an overtime win for Texas A&M in 2018. This will be the third time the two programs have played in College Station, with each team having won a game in Kyle Field.
