ECRL, Catalyst of Change For East Coast, Stations Poise to Be Economic Hubs- Pahang Sultan - MCI Group

TheTools · 24.11.2026 08:55 · source · permalink

KUANTAN, Nov 24 — The Sultan of Pahang, Al-Sultan Abdullah Ri’ayatuddin Al-Mustafa Billah Shah, has described the East Coast Rail Link (ECRL) service as a “game changer” that will propel development in the East Coast states.

Al-Sultan Abdullah noted that the rapid development previously seen on the West Coast was driven by the early establishment of road and rail networks; therefore, the construction of the ECRL, combined with the Central Spine Road (CSR) project is expected to accelerate development on the East Coast.

Regarding the seven ECRL stations in Pahang, His Royal Highness expressed hope that these locations would serve as hubs for economic development.

“It should not just be a matter of having a station, it must also drive local economic activity, particularly in the districts where these railway stations are located,” His Royal Highness said when officiating the opening of the KotaSAS ECRL Station here today.

Tengku Ampuan Pahang Tunku Azizah Aminah Maimunah Iskandariah and members of the royal family, including the Tengku Mahkota of Pahang, Tengku Hassanal Ibrahim Alam Shah, were also in attendance.

Also present at the event were Pahang Menteri Besar Datuk Seri Wan Rosdy Wan Ismail, members of the state government leadership, and the Chief Secretary to the Government who also serves as the chairman of Malaysia Rail Link Sdn Bhd (MRL), Tan Sri Dr. Shamsul Azri Abu Bakar.

Al-Sultan Abdullah also reflected on the journey of this national infrastructure project, which has spanned the administrations of five Prime Ministers and three Yang di-Pertuan Agongs, including His Royal Highness himself.

The Sultan mentioned that he had previously met with MRL chief executive officer Datuk Seri Darwis Abdul Razak to obtain a more detailed explanation of the project’s plans, in addition to playing a role in ensuring its continued implementation.

“…we extend our gratitude to all past leaders and every party involved who have successfully planned and completed this project,” His Royal Highness stated.

Furthermore, the Sultan of Pahang emphasised that maintenance and operational aspects must always prioritise safety to prevent any untoward incidents.

During the ceremony, His Royal Highness signed the inauguration plaque and viewed a video montage of ECRL KotaSAS station.

Al-Sultan Abdullah and his family also took the opportunity to experience the public transport service, traveling from the KotaSAS station to the Bentong ECRL station.
