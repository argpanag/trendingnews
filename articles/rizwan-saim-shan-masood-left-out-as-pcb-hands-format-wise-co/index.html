Rizwan, Saim, Shan Masood left out as PCB hands format-wise contracts to 38

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:03 · source · permalink

The Pakistan Cricket Board has announced a new central contracts structure for the senior men's team, switching to a format-based system for the cycle running until June 30, 2027. 38 players are covered under the new structure, with 19 falling under the Development category. The new system replaces last year's category-based list, which ran from Category B to D.

Test captain Babar Azam and former T20I skipper Salman Agha are the only players to be awarded a Test and ODI contract, while Mohammad Abbas, Azan Awais, Sajid Khan and Saud Shakeel have been handed a Test contract alone. No player has been offered a contract across all three formats.

A host of players, including recent Test debutants Razaullah, Tayyab Arif, Mohammad Taha, Ghazi Ghori and Saqib Khan, have received Development contracts. ODI skipper Shaheen Shah Afridi has been handed an ODI and T20I contract, while T20I captain Sahibzada Farhan has been awarded a T20I contract alone.

Wicketkeeper Mohammad Rizwan, who has found himself embroiled in off-field controversies and was sent home midway through the tour of England, is the most noteworthy omission from the list. Rizwan held a Category B contract last year.

Rizwan is one of 13 players from last year's list who do not feature this time. Saim Ayub and Hasan Ali, both in Category B last year, also miss out, as does Shan Masood. Abdullah Shafique, Mohammad Haris, Mohammad Nawaz, Noman Ali, Hussain Talat, Khurram Shahzad, Khushdil Shah, Mohammad Abbas Afridi and Mohammad Wasim Jnr are the others left out.

PCB Men’s Central Contracts (Until 30 June, 2027)

  • Test and ODI: Babar Azam, Salman Agha
  • Test: Azan Awais, Mohammad Abbas, Sajid Khan, Saud Shakeel
  • ODI and T20I: Abrar Ahmed, Arafat Minhas, Fakhar Zaman, Faheem Ashraf, Haris Rauf, Shaheen Shah Afridi
  • T20I: Hasan Nawaz, Maaz Sadaqat, Salman Mirza, Naseem Shah, Sahibzada Farhan, Shadab Khan, Usman Tariq
  • Development: Abdul Samad, Abdullah Fazal, Ahmed Daniyal, Ali Raza, Farhan Yousaf, Hunain Shah, Mohammad Taha, Tayyab Arif, Ghazi Ghori, Mohammad Imran Randhawa, Razaullah, Saad Baig, Saad Masood, Sameer Minhas, Saqib Khan, Shahzaib Khan, Shamyl Hussain, Sufyan Moqim, Ubaid Shah
