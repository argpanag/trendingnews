  • Barry-Murphy Reveals He Dropped Ireland Striker Over Failure To Meet Club 'Standards'

Jack Moylan has been winning a huge amount of plaudits for his performances at Cardiff City this season, but he is not the only Ireland international who has contributed to the club since their return to the Championship.

Callum Robinson has seen limited minutes for the club early on in the campaign, but he has still popped up with a couple of goals. The forward had scored in each of the club's last two fixtures prior to the game against Middlesbrough last weekend.

As a result, it was s surprise to see the 31-year old left out of the squad for the trip to the Riverside Stadium. Robinson was not even on the bench as his side earned a hard-fought point away from home after a 2-2 draw.

Barry-Murphy Reveals Why He Dropped Ireland Striker

There was no suggestion prior to the game against Middlesbrough that Callum Robinson was injured, leading to some confusion around his absence.

Speaking ahead of the meeting with Birmingham City this weekend, manager Brian Barry-Murphy revealed that the Ireland international had been excluded from the squad for over a failure to meet squad 'standards' in the buildup to the fixture.

We have certain standards that all the players have to adhere to before they're considered for selection.

Callum missed out on some of those before last Saturday, so he wasn't available, but he's back now...

The standards we expect in the dressing room are internal.

We're always judged in public, and that's fine, but the dressing room and how we work day by day are private for us...

Supporters want to see certain qualities in their team week to week. We have to make sure those qualities remain the case day to day...

Callum's a great guy.

He's been a great player for us and a great person, so there's no problem whatsoever. I'm very pleased to have him back in training this week.

Callum Robinson trained with the group this week and is set to be available for selection this weekend, with Brian Barry-Murphy insisting that there will be no issue between the pair moving forward.

Cardiff City aiming for further Championship improvement

Cardiff City have experienced a mixed start to the season after their return to the Championship, currently sitting in 19th place in the league after taking 11 points from their first 11 league fixtures.

Their performances have improved in recent weeks, with hopes high that they will be able to push their way up the table over the coming months.

Callum Robinson will play a big role in that, with this incident now put in the rearview mirror.
