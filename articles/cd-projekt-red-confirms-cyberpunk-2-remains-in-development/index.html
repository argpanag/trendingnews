CD Projekt Red Confirms Cyberpunk 2 Remains in Development

TheTools · 24.11.2026 04:58 · source · permalink

CD Projekt Red's Cyberpunk 2 sequel remains in development despite a joking cancellation remark from creative director Igor Sarzyński on November 24, 2026. The developer made the comment after experiencing Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6), praising Rockstar's achievement while discussing the creative direction of the upcoming open-world game.

The statement attracted considerable attention because Sarzyński holds a leadership position on Cyberpunk 2, making his reaction more significant than an ordinary player's review. However, his remarks did not represent an official announcement that CD Projekt Red had abandoned the project.

In a post published on Bluesky, Sarzyński expressed his admiration for GTA 6, highlighting its technical scale, artistic quality, environmental detail, and overall creative execution.

"GTAVI-induced-open-world-game-dev-depression is real. probably the single greatest artistic achievement in history, i mean it. scale, density, coherence, thoughtfulness of it all, just a technological and creative miracle. cyberpunk 2 is cancelled, imma go sweep autumn leaves in my backyard now. seriously though i think we're doing something of our own, with a different unique flavor. GTA is impressive in a way all great art is, it expands what is possible, a captivating vision flawlessly executed." Sarzyński wrote on Bluesky.

The cancellation reference was part of his humorous reaction to Rockstar's work rather than a formal development update. His comments also emphasized that CD Projekt Red intended to pursue a distinct creative identity instead of reproducing another studio's approach.

"different unique flavor" was how Sarzyński characterized the direction of CD Projekt Red's upcoming project in his follow-up clarification.

His explanation reinforced the distinction between appreciating GTA 6 and changing the studio's plans. Sarzyński described Rockstar's achievement as an example of how ambitious creative projects can expand the possibilities of the medium.

Cyberpunk 2 has officially entered pre-production

CD Projekt Red had already provided an official development update on May 28, 2025. The company announced that Cyberpunk 2, previously identified by its codename Project Orion, had completed its conceptual phase and moved into pre-production.

That announcement established the project's progression beyond its initial conceptual work. The studio's publicly stated development status therefore contradicts the suggestion that the sequel had been canceled.

Cyberpunk 2 remains the planned follow-up to the original Cyberpunk project, while Sarzyński's latest comments focus on maintaining a distinct creative approach alongside the industry's other major open-world productions.
