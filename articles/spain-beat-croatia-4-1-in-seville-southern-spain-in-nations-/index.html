Spain beat Croatia 4-1 in Seville, southern Spain, in Nations League

TheTools · 30.09.2026 07:00 · source · permalink

Madrid, Sept. 30 (SANA)  Spain maintained their perfect UEFA Nations League start with a 4-1 victory over Croatia on Tuesday, at the Ramón Sánchez-Pizjuán Stadium in Seville, southern Spain, taking their Group A3 tally to six points from two matches.

Lamine Yamal opened the scoring inside two minutes before Croatia drew level through Dion Beljo in the 28th minute.

Spain quickly regained the lead, with Marc Pubill heading home in the 31st minute for his first international goal.

Yamal scored his second of the match and Spain’s third in the 63rd minute, finishing from Pedri’s through ball. Substitute Nico Williams completed the scoring in the 89th minute.

The result gave Spain two wins from their opening two matches after they defeated England 3-2 at Wembley in their Group A3 opener on Sept. 26.

Spain lead the group with six points. England are second with three points after defeating Czechia 2-0, while Croatia also have three points and Czechia remain without a point.
