TheTools · 12.09.2026 19:02 · source · permalink

From Screen Rant on September 12, 2026 | OpenCritic

Despite the fact that it almost came out 10 years ago, there are still plenty of players who log in all the time to enjoy the wonders of Red Dead Redemption 2. As big as the single-player is, it would be no surprise if you've experienced everything it has to offer at this point.

That's where Red Dead Online comes into the equation, and Rockstar have been quite diligent about continuing to support the multiplayer modes in the absence of getting us any single-player DLC. Now, their latest event is live, and there are plenty of free items you can pick up for your cowboy or cowgirl.

Over on the official Rockstar website you can find the breakdown for the latest Red Dead Online event, with the focus this time around heavily on the moonshining industry.

The most time sensitive of those is the weekly Moonshiner weekly reward which you get for completing a Moonshiner Sale. Until September 14, you can pick up the “This Guy” emote, which will be replaced by the Black Woodland Gloves from the 15th.
