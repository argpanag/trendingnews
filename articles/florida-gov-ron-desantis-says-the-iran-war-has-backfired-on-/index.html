WASHINGTON (TNND) — Florida GOP Gov. Ron DeSantis said that the Iran war has backfired on the Republican Party, especially during election season.

DeSantis joined commentator Stephen A. Smith on SiriusXM Monday evening for an interview.

The Iran war was brought up and how it has impacted gas prices for everyday Americans.

“We just got to be honest,” DeSantis said. “Gas is higher. It hurts people, working-class people, and it’s higher because of this conflict. That’s just a fact.”

The average price of gas on Election Day is $4.11 per gallon, according to The Hill newspaper.

DeSantis said that Republicans can’t just brush off the struggles the war has brought upon Americans and can’t gaslight them.

“If you can’t admit that to voters, then you’re gaslighting them, and they’re not going to listen to you on other things,” he said.

DeSantis said that President Donald Trump’s 2024 campaign was about no new wars and bringing prices down for Americans.

“If it drags on for the rest of his presidency, it’s going to be devastating to his ability to get anything done, and it is going to be very, very difficult for Republicans in the next cycle,” he stated.

When asked what the GOP should change their stances on, he said the messaging on artificial intelligence, data centers and Big Tech needs to improve.
