Nifty 50 began today’s session with a gap-down at 23,222 versus yesterday’s close of 23,447. The index is now hovering around 23,260, down nearly 0.8 per cent.

The advance/decline ratio of Nifty 50 currently stands at 10/40, showing a considerable bearish bias. SBI Life (up 1.5 per cent) and Tech Mahindra (up 0.6 per cent) are the top gainers among Nifty 50 constituents in today’s early trade.

On the other hand, decline is led by HDFC Life (down 8 per cent) and Bajaj Finance (down 4 per cent). 

Among sectors, Nifty Midsmall Healthcare (up 0.4 per cent) is the top gainer whereas Nifty Financial Services ex-Bank (down 2.3 per cent) is the weakest.

The above factors and the chart indicates weak intraday outlook for Nifty 50 and Nifty futures today. Below is the analysis of futures. 

Nifty (Sep) futures began today’s session lower at 23,288 against Wednesday’s close of 23,450. The contract is now trading at 23,240, down 0.9 per cent.

The rally was rejected at the resistance level of 23,500. Today’s gap-down open and the subsequent fall so far shows that the bears are in control. Given the current downward momentum, we expect the September contract to witness further decline today.

While there is support ahead at 23,200, we anticipate a deeper decline to 23,000. Notable support below 23,000 is at 22,800.

That said, in case Nifty futures see a recovery from the current level of 23,240, it will face a barrier at 23,350. So long as this level holds true, the bias will be bearish. Resistance above 23,350 is at 23,500.

Overall, there is a bearish inclination keeping the probability for more fall high. Therefore, traders can consider fresh short positions. 

Short Nifty (Sep) futures now at 23,240. Target and stop-loss can be 23,000 and 23,375 respectively.

Supports: 23,200 and 23,000

Resistance: 23,350 and 23,500
