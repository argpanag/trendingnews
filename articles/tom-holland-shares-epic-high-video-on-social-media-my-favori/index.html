Hollywood actor Tom Holland gave a surprise shout-out to the group Epik High.

Recently, Tom Holland uploaded part of a past interview video he conducted with Epik High members Tablo, Mithra, and Tuck Cut on his personal account. Along with it, Tom Holland posted the phrase "my favourite interview ever."

The interview was conducted in July around the release of the film "Spider-Man: Brand New Day," during which Epik High met lead actors Tom Holland, Zendaya Coleman, and Jacob Batalon in London, UK.

After being uploaded to Epik High's official YouTube channel, the video garnered a massive response, recording over 3.61 million views. Furthermore, Tom Holland's surprise resurfacing of this interview via social media has drawn even more attention.

Meanwhile, Epik High will hold the "2027 Epik High Concert" for three days from January 22 to 24 next year at Seoul Olympic Park KSPO DOME.

The "2027 Epik High Concert" will take place at Seoul Olympic Park KSPO DOME on January 22 next year at 8 p.m., the 23rd at 6 p.m., and the 24th at 4 p.m., with tickets available for purchase through Melon Ticket.
