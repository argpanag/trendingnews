New Caney, Porter housing market trends for October

TheTools · 19.11.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

Two fewer homes sold in October 2026 than in October 2025 across the two ZIP codes combined that make up Community Impact's New Caney-Porter coverage area.

Data shows the median price of homes sold decreased in New Caney and increased in Porter.

On average, homes sold faster in Porter and stayed on the market the same amount of time in New Caney.

Nearly 69% of homes sold in October were in the $200,000-$399,999 price range.
