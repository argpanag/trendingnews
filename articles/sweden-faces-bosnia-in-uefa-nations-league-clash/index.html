Sweden Faces Bosnia in UEFA Nations League Clash

TheTools · 02.10.2026 18:30 · source · permalink

The Swedish national soccer team faces Bosnia and Herzegovina in a UEFA Nations League group match on Friday, October 2, 2026. Sweden enters the contest seeking to maintain its offensive momentum following a high-scoring opening stretch in the tournament.

Sweden has recorded five goals across its first two tournament fixtures, scoring twice against Romania before securing three goals against Poland. Team performance metrics show Sweden averaging 1.47 expected goals, five shots on target, and three major scoring chances per match.

Bosnia has demonstrated solid defensive figures in its initial two games, allowing an average of 0.84 expected goals against. While holding Poland scoreless in a clean sheet victory, Bosnia conceded two goals in its matchup against Romania.

Defensive statistics indicate Bosnia permits an average of four shots on target and 1.5 big chances per match to opponents. The upcoming fixture tests whether Sweden can sustain its shot conversion rate against Bosnia's defensive backline.
