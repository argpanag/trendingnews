Billionaire Lionel Messi buys 39-room ski hotel in Pyrenees

TheTools · 27.11.2026 22:23 · source · permalink

Lionel Messi's listed property company has bought a four-star ski hotel near Baqueira-Beret from Meliá, taking his hotel portfolio to eight properties.

Table of Contents

Lionel Messi has bought a four-star ski hotel in the Spanish Pyrenees from Meliá Hotels International, adding a third property in the Val d'Aran valley and taking the Argentine football star's hotel portfolio to eight.

Edificio Rostower Socimi SA, the listed real estate company that holds Messi's property investments in Spain, signed the purchase of the 39-room Hotel de Tredós on Nov. 24. The hotel sits in Naut Aran, in the province of Lleida, a few minutes from the Baqueira-Beret ski resort. The price was not disclosed. The deal also includes a rural plot next to the hotel.

Third hotel in the valley

Hotel de Tredós has a restaurant, an outdoor pool and services for skiers. Meliá, which owned the property until the sale, has run it since late 2022 under its Affiliated by Meliá brand.

The purchase deepens Messi's bet on winter tourism in the valley. He bought the former Hotel Himalaia at Baqueira in 2020 and reopened it as MiM Baqueira, a 141-room hotel by the Ruda slope. He also owns Hotel SNÖ Colomers, a 15-room boutique hotel in the village of Salardú.

His first hotel, MiM Sitges near Barcelona, opened in 2017 with 77 rooms. The MiM chain also has hotels in Sotogrande, Mallorca, Ibiza and Andorra. Meliá has managed the six MiM hotels under its premium label, The Meliá Collection, since Nov. 1, 2025, while Rostower keeps ownership. Half of Messi's eight hotels are in Catalonia.

Messi's property company

Edificio Rostower began trading on the Portfolio Stock Exchange, a European market for property companies, in January 2025 with a valuation of €223 million ($250 million). Besides the hotels, it owns a commercial building in Barcelona, luxury homes in the city's Sarrià-Sant Gervasi district, a home in Ibiza, a duplex in London, a house in Paris, a warehouse in Sitges leased to a supermarket chain and a restaurant in Castelldefels.

The company is still losing money as it expands. It posted a net loss of €7.73 million ($8.7 million) in 2025, compared with €7.48 million a year earlier, on revenue of about €7.4 million ($8.3 million). Depreciation of more than €8.6 million weighed on the result.

Rostower has also bought the former Galerías Wagner shopping arcade near Barcelona's Turó Park for about €11.5 million ($12.9 million), and plans to turn its roughly 3,000 square meters into offices for a bank's headquarters.

Messi, 39, left FC Barcelona in 2021 and now plays for Inter Miami in Major League Soccer. He has kept investing in Catalonia since his departure. In April, he bought all of Unió Esportiva Cornellà, a club that plays in Spain's fifth tier.

Forbes counts Messi among the world's billionaires, with a fortune built on decades of salaries, bonuses and endorsement deals.
