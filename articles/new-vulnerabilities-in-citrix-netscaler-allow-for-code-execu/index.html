New vulnerabilities in Citrix Netscaler allow for code execution

TheTools · 27.09.2026 21:57 · source · permalink

Security researchers warn: New vulnerabilities are being actively exploited. Updates are being rolled out and fix more than a half dozen vulnerabilites.

(Image: tete_escape/Shutterstock.com)

  • Dr. Christopher Kunz

Administrators of Citrix Netscaler instances should update their devices immediately. According to several sources, two new exploits (zero-days) are circulating, allowing code injection on vulnerable devices. The manufacturer has not commented, and there are no warnings from cybersecurity authorities. However, patches are now available and should immediately be deployed – Netscaler vulnerabilities are often exploited for cybercrime.

One of the warnings comes from the threat intelligence company watchTowr. It states: “Two vulnerabilities – both RCE. Unpatched, 0days. Exploited in the wild – discovered during forensics.” Citrix is supposedly working on an official statement and patches, which are expected by the beginning of the week.

Users on Reddit confirm this account. A supposed Citrix customer writes that the manufacturer confirmed the vulnerabilities upon inquiry and that developers are working around the clock to fix them. British security researcher Kevin Beaumont also considers the rumors credible: “Vuln is real though, I checked.”

Taking the devices offline immediately, as watchTowr founder Benjamin Harris advises affected parties on LinkedIn, is also recommended by government agencies, according to Beaumont. Citrix currently does not recommend shutting down, the Brit states. Instead, developers „

Official advisory by Citrix.

There is currently no official information available on the websites of CISA (Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency), the NCSC (National Cyber Security Centre) in the UK and the Netherlands, or the German BSI (Federal Office for Information Security).

Citrix has issued a security advisory in the early evening hours. It lists several CVEs with critical and high severities:

  • CVE-2026-88771 and CVE-2026-88772 allow for remote code execution and have a CVSS 4.0 score of 9.5/10 (critical). The former applies to all default configurations.
  • CVE-2026-88773 is also a critical vulnerability and applies to all Netscaler ADC or Gateway instances with HTTP enabled. It scores 9.3/10 in the CVSS 4.0 scale.
  • CVE-2026-88774, CVE-2026-88775, CVE-2026-88776, CVE-2026-88777 and CVE-2026-88778 have a high severity, having a score of 7.0/10 (the first vulnerability) and 8.8/10 (the other four vulnerabilites).

The vendor is also now distributing two new versions of NetScaler OS to its customers that resolve the vulnerabilities. As heise security learned, Citrix support representatives responded to customer inquiries as follows:

Thank you for reporting to us your concern involving your Citrix NetScaler instance. Our teams have been working around the clock to develop a fix to address the recently identified NetScaler vulnerabilities. We now have an early access build available for you to review.
This early access build of Citrix NetScaler includes updates designed to address the issues you've encountered. Please be advised that, as an early access build, this software has not yet completed the final stages of our rigorous testing process and therefore may contain bugs, errors, and other defects.

The build numbers are as follows:

  • NetScaler 13.1: 13.1-64.24
  • NetScaler 14.1: 14.1-37.73

The new versions are listed on the public NetScaler download page, though the downloads themselves are available only to customers. Administrators should install the new versions immediately to prevent exploitation. Zero-day exploits like the ones reported here are nothing new for Citrix; the security appliance suffered from critical security flaws as recently as August and July.

We have reached out to the BSI, watchTowr, Citrix, and Kevin Beaumont for comment and will update this report if we receive further information.
