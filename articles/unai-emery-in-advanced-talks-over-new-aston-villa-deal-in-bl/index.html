Unai Emery in advanced talks over new Aston Villa deal in blow to Man Utd

TheTools · 05.11.2026 05:54 · source · permalink

After ‘positive discussions’ with chairman, new long-term contract will end rumours of Spaniard replacing Michael Carrick if he is sacked

Unai Emery is on the verge of signing a new contract with Aston Villa in a move to kill off any interest in the head coach from Manchester United.

Emery has completely transformed Villa since his arrival four years ago and is close to making a firm sign of further commitment after advanced talks on Wednesday with Nassef Sawiris, the club’s chairman.

Since his appointment in October 2022, Emery has lifted the Europa League trophy and guided the club into the Champions League on two occasions.

The former Arsenal and Paris St-Germain manager has been linked with United in recent weeks amid the uncertainty over the future of Michael Carrick, but is now providing Villa with a huge boost by signing a new long-term contract beyond his current deal, which is due to run until 2029.

Emery’s name is understood to have been discussed by United’s football board as a potential replacement for Carrick should the former England midfielder be sacked. But the Spaniard would cost United close to £20m to release him from his Villa deal.

Former Newcastle United manager Eddie Howe, Italian Antonio Conte and Germany’s Julian Nagelsmann, who are all currently out of work, are also among those to have been considered, as has Brighton and Hove Albion’s Fabian Hürzeler.

Carrick’s fate remains unclear after United ended their nine-match winless run on Tuesday with a 3-2 comeback victory over Roma, with Emery’s Villa up next in the Premier League this Sunday in what could prove a pivotal fixture to determining his fate.

Emery met with Sawiris on Wednesday following the club’s Champions League match at Barcelona and an agreement is thought to be close.

In a statement the club said: “Aston Villa held an important working meeting today to plan the club’s future and lay the foundations for its long-term sporting and institutional project.

“The meeting brought together Nassef Sawiris, the club’s chairman and Unai Emery, alongside president of football operations Roberto Olabe and director of football operations Damián Vidagany.”

It is understood both Vidagany and Olabe are also in talks over contract extensions.

Olabe has played a major part in recruitment over the summer. Villa sold six of their starting XI from the Europa League final against Freiburg in May, but have reshaped their squad with Emery and Olabe at the forefront of the strategy by making 10 new signings including the record purchase of Nicolas Jackson as well as Zion Suzuki, Johan Manzambi and Ibrahim Mbaye.

Vidagany has also emerged as a key player in Villa’s progress and while close to Emery, is also in daily contact with Sawiris.

The statement added: “The discussions focused on jointly planning the club’s long-term strategic direction, with positive discussions establishing a clear commitment beyond 2029 for the joint development of the club under the sporting leadership of Unai Emery based on the shared ambitions and common goal to reach excellency and keep growing during the next years.

“The objective is clear: to build an ambitious, competitive Aston Villa capable of taking on the biggest challenges and achievements.”
