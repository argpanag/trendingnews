Slightly cooler weather in northeast New Mexico and dry around the state - AOL

TheTools · 13.11.2026 05:53 · source · permalink

NEW MEXICO (KRQE) - Northerly winds are moving through eastern New Mexico as the high pressure system to the west has nudged east. Clouds are also drifting east from around I-40 to the north, while some fog has once again developed, but for fewer areas in the Pecos River Valley to the Permian Basin. Temperatures are starting off chilly, either around average or at least slightly above average.

Gustier conditions are mixing the air around in the northeast quadrant of New Mexico, not allowing temperatures to drop as much. Consequently, many northern areas are starting off above the freezing mark, with the exception of far western, northern, and mountainous areas ranging from the high teens to the lower 30s. South of I-40, most communities are above freezing, ranging from the 30s to near 50°.

Fog will dissipate later this morning and the higher level clouds will straddle around I-40. Slightly cooler high temperatures will develop in northeastern areas, but elsewhere will be around or slightly milder than yesterday, still at least mostly above average. The vast majority of the region will reach the 60s. Far-southeastern areas will reach the low 70s, while communities to the north or in the high elevations will range from the 40s to the high 50s.

Dry conditions will remain today into tomorrow. However, as the next storm system approaches, spotty to isolated precipitation is more likely on Friday through this weekend. Afternoon temperatures will remain above average for most areas, but it will become slightly cooler with the precipitation for many.
