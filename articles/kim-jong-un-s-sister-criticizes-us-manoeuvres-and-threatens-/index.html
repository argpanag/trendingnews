Kim Jong Un's sister criticizes US manoeuvres and threatens "more offensive" response

TheTools · 18.09.2026 10:59 · source · permalink

North Korea may respond in a “more offensive” manner to frequent U.S. military exercises in the region, which worsen regional tensions, stated today Kim Yo Jong, the influential sister of North Korean leader Kim Jong Un.

“The current situation, in which new records are being set in terms of practical hostile actions against the DPRK (North Korea), underlines the need to set records in our responsive actions, of a proportional or more offensive nature,” declared Kim Yo Jong, cited by the news agency KCNA.

The younger sister of Kim Jong Un is officially director of the General Affairs Department of the Central Committee of the Workers’ Party of Korea, regularly speaking out to harshly criticize the country’s enemies.

The United States, South Korea, Japan, Australia, Canada, and New Zealand conducted, from August 29 to September 10, the annual joint naval exercise “Pacific Vanguard” near the American island of Guam, in the Pacific Ocean, aiming to strengthen the capacity to conduct joint naval operations in the region.

Classifying these exercises as a “U.S.-led war rehearsal,” Kim Yo Jong stated that they “constitute the main source of the constant worsening of the situation on the Korean Peninsula and in the region.”

“Our will to defend our sovereignty will manifest through clearer action, and there will never be any change in the path of intensifying nuclear deterrence,” warned the sister of the North Korean leader.

“I remind the enemy once again that, should it be considered that our nation’s supreme interests, as well as its military sovereignty and the regional security environment, have been gravely violated by their military manoeuvres, the armed forces of the DPRK will apply the relevant provisions, in compliance with their constitutional duties, and mobilize all available means to address the situation,” she added.
