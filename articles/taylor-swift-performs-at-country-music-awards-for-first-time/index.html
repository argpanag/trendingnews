Taylor Swift performs at Country Music Awards for first time in 13 years

TheTools · 19.11.2026 11:33 · source · permalink

Taylor Swift returned to the Country Music Awards (CMA) stage for the first time in 13 years – her lucky number.

The singer returned to her roots after being nominated for single of the year with her Toy Story 5 track I Knew It, I Knew You.

It marked her first CMA nomination in four years and the first in the category in 13 years.

It followed on from Swift becoming the youngest female artist inducted into the Songwriters Hall of Fame earlier this year.

Swift took the stage with a medley which featured the first song she ever performed at the CMAs in 2007, Our Song, along with the Toy Story track which she released earlier this summer.

The performance began with Swift walking backstage with cameo appearances from a number of country stars including Brad Paisley, Little Big Town, Sugarland, and Brooks & Dunn.

She later made it onto the stage picking up a blue guitar with bedazzled clouds on it, in a nod to the Toy Story franchise, before singing through her 2006 track with Keith Urban on banjo.

Despite her star-studded return to the CMA stage, Swift missed out in her category to Ella Langley’s breakout track Choosin Texas.

The 27-year-old dominated the night and took home six awards including entertainer of the year, song of the year, music video of the year and female vocalist of the year.

Langley also won album of the year with her second studio album, Dandelion, beating out Kacey Musgraves’ Middle Of Nowhere and Luke Combs’ The Way I Am.

The singer performed twice at Nashville’s Bridgestone arena on Wednesday night including an acoustic version of her hit Loving Life Again along with a Miranda Lambert duet singing Snakeskin Boots.

Following her win, Langley took to social media and said: “Hard to put this night into words and what it means.

“Thank you to my fans.

“Thank you for loving this record, thank you for coming to my shows, thank you for believing in my artistry.

“This year has been the most incredible journey, and I want to thank you for sharing in that with me. I love y’all.”

The evening also paid tribute to the late Dolly Parton with Lainey Wilson and Vince Gill singing a rendition of her song I Will Always Love You.

Other winners on the night include Chris Stapleton who won male vocalist of the year, The Red Clay Strays who won vocal group of the year, and Brooks & Dunn with vocal duo of the year.

Meanwhile Stephen Wilson Jr won the new artist of the year award while Charlie Worsham won musician of the year.
