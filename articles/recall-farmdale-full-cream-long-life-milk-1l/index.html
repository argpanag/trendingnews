Recall: Farmdale – Full Cream Long Life Milk 1L

TheTools · 26.10.2026 06:00 · source · permalink

Aldi has issued a recall for Farmdale – Full Cream Long Life Milk 1L due to microbial contamination (spoilage).

The products have been sold at Indian grocery stores, Aldi stores and online in Victoria and SA.

The affected batches are marked: Best Before 05Jun27.

Consuming spoiled food products may cause illness or injury.

Any consumers concerned about their health should seek medical advice. Consumers who have purchased the product are advised to return the product to the place of purchase for a full refund.
