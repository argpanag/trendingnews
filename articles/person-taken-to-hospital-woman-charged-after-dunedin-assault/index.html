Person taken to hospital, woman charged after Dunedin assault

TheTools · 16.11.2026 01:44 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

Police are investigating an alleged grievous assault in Dunedin after a person was taken to hospital with serious injuries.

A 49-year-old woman has been charged with wounding with intent to injure and is expected to appear in Dunedin District Court today.

Police said officers were called to a private address on Great King St, North Dunedin, about 6.10pm yesterday after reports of a person being injured.

The injured person was taken to hospital by emergency services.

“While we are in early stages of our investigation, we believe the incident involved people known to each other,” police said.

Hato Hone St John responded with one ambulance, one operations manager and one rapid response vehicle.

“One patient was assessed by the ambulance crew at the scene and transported to Dunedin Hospital in a serious condition,” a spokesman said.
