Andreeva Sets Up Fernandez Rubber Match in WTA Finals Chase - Sports Bytes Philippines

TheTools · 23.09.2026 19:44 · source · permalink

Posted By Staff Writer on September 24, 2026

Mirra Andreeva is one win away from a blockbuster decider that could shape her push for the WTA Finals. The top seed’s latest victory in Singapore has set up a rubber match against Leylah Fernandez, with the pair now tied 2-2 in their head-to-head series.

Andreeva reached the quarterfinals on Wednesday, September 23, after beating Aliaksandra Sasnovich 6-2, 6-2 at 2026 Singapore Tennis Open. It was their first meeting since 2024, and the result stretched Andreeva’s record against the Belarusian to a perfect 2-0.

Andreeva Grinds Past Sasnovich Test in Singapore

The scoreline suggested a straightforward afternoon, but the match carried more tension than the numbers let on. Sasnovich, fresh off a 7-5, 6-0 win over Daria Kasatkina in the previous round, brought her trademark flat, aggressive ball-striking into the contest.

Andreeva raced to a 4-1 double break in the first set before Sasnovich clawed one back, forcing the 19-year-old to close it out on her third set point. A similar pattern played out in the second set, where Andreeva lost an early break advantage before regrouping to finish the match on an ace.

Despite her age, Andreeva continues to show the composure of a seasoned veteran. The Roland-Garros champion, who also reached the US Open quarterfinals earlier this season, leaned on her defensive range and shot-making to keep Sasnovich off balance throughout.

Fernandez Showdown Adds Stakes to WTA Finals Race

With the win secured, attention now shifts to Andreeva’s quarterfinal clash with Fernandez, a matchup that carries extra weight given the tightly contested race for the season-ending WTA Finals. The pair’s even head-to-head record only raises the stakes further.

Speaking after her win over Sasnovich, Andreeva credited a calmer mindset for her improved performance. She said she has been working on her serve in practice and felt a shift in her approach during the match itself.

“Today, I was just feeling a little bit more calm,” Andreeva said, adding that she focused on internally encouraging herself rather than seeking outward validation. She also praised the Singapore crowd, saying she felt genuine support throughout the contest.

That balance of confidence and calm will be tested again when she faces Fernandez, with a place in the semifinals and valuable WTA Finals points on the line.
