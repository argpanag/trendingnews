Disney+ Finland updates terms: ads possible on all plans

TheTools · 12.09.2026 22:24 · source · permalink

Streaming service Disney+ in Finland is changing its subscription terms: ads may be shown to subscribers of all tariff plans regardless of the selected package. This was reported by YLE News.

Advertising in all packages

Disney's new terms of use state that all subscription packages may include advertising content, sponsored materials, and advertisements before and after content playback.

According to the terms, advertising materials may also appear on channels, during live broadcasts and special events, as well as in content from third-party services.

More current news is available on the UA.News Telegram channel Telegram.

Notification of price changes

Disney is also reducing the advance notice period for changes to subscription prices from 30 to 28 days. The company's notice of the new terms is dated September 9.

The updated rules are to take effect 30 days after this date, that is, on October 9.
