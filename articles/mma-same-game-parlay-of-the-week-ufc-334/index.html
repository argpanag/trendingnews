MMA Same Game Parlay of the Week: UFC 334

UFC 334 Middleweight Fight: Caio Borralho vs Yousri Belgaroui

Start Time: 5:00pm EST (Paramount+)

Third from the top on this card is a fight between middleweight contenders – both trying to stake their claim in a wide open division. Caio Borralho looks to defend his spot with a statement win as he tried to hold off the rising Yousri Belgaroui.

Borralho has always been a fighter who is a touch on the cautious side. His ascent up the rankings has undoubtedly been slower due to a number of decisions. Four of his last five fights have all seen the judges’ scorecards, which includes three straight. In every one of those decisions, Borralho managed to land at least 45 significant strikes. With that as a baseline, we have a starter for our parlay.

Belgaroui has been the opposite when it comes to finishes. He has finished all three of his opponents in the UFC and his last opponent on Dana White’s Contender Series as well. Despite ending all of those fights inside the distance, one of which was in the first round, he too managed to land at least 45 significant strikes in each.

If we assume this one will get a little deeper into the fight, we can cash a nice number on these strike totals.

UFC 334 SGP: Caio Borralho 45+ Significant Strikes and Yousri Belgaroui 45+ Significant Strikes = +115
