Ticketmaster Expands Middle East Sports Portfolio With Dubai Racing Club Deal

TheTools · 20.10.2026 20:40 · source · permalink

Home »

Dubai Racing Club has appointed Ticketmaster as its official ticketing partner for the 2026-27 season at Meydan Racecourse, including the Dubai World Cup and Dubai Racing Carnival.

Under the new agreement, Ticketmaster will operate Dubai Racing Club’s branded ticketing website, providing ticket purchasing and management services for events throughout the racing season.

The deal marks a change in ticketing providers for the organization, which previously worked with Vivaticket. The company was identified as the exclusive ticketing partner in Dubai Racing Club’s official hospitality materials for the 2026 Dubai World Cup.

“Every season, we look for new ways to make an evening at Meydan Racecourse even more enjoyable for our guests – whether they are here for the racing, the hospitality, the dining or simply the atmosphere,” said Ali Al Ali, board member and CEO of Dubai Racing Club. “We’re pleased to welcome Ticketmaster as our official ticketing partner, helping make it even easier for fans in the UAE and around the world to join us for another exciting season.”

The 2026-27 Dubai Racing Carnival begins November 6, with 16 race meetings scheduled through March 12. The season culminates with the Dubai World Cup on March 27, 2027, marking the 31st edition of one of international horse racing’s most prominent events.

The Dubai World Cup program will offer $30.5 million in total prize money across nine races, including $12 million for the headline race.

Andreas Müeller, managing director of Ticketmaster Middle East, said the company will provide ticketing technology while maintaining Dubai Racing Club’s branding throughout the purchasing process.

“Our technology will make buying and managing tickets simple, while keeping the experience unmistakably Dubai Racing Club from start to finish,” Müeller said.

Ticketmaster’s UAE website is already advertising tickets for the upcoming racing season, with general admission to Dubai Racing Carnival meetings starting at AED 10. Additional options include premium viewing areas, hospitality packages and season passes.

The partnership adds to Ticketmaster’s growing portfolio of sporting events in the Middle East. The company was previously named the official ticketing partner for the 2027 FIBA Basketball World Cup in Qatar and Khaleeji Arabian Dyar 27, also known as Gulf Cup 27, in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.

The Dubai agreement also follows a recent leadership change at Ticketmaster Sport, which appointed François Westcombe as managing director for Europe, the Middle East and Africa earlier this month. The regional division works with more than 100 clients across 15 sports.

Fans can view the upcoming racing schedule and purchase tickets through the Dubai Racing Club website.
