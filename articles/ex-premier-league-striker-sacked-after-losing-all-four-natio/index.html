Ex-Premier League striker sacked after losing all four Nations League games

TheTools · 09.10.2026 16:21 · source · permalink
  • Robert Calcutt, Sports Writer
  • Published: 17:21, 9 Oct 2026
  • Updated: 17:48, 9 Oct 2026

Vincenzo Montella has been sacked as Turkey national team manager following their dreadful start to the Nations League.

Turkey were beaten by France, Italy (twice) and Belgium during the recent international break, conceding 11 goals and scoring just two.

It means they can only finish as high as third in their Nations League group, which could see them compete in a relegation play-off.

Montella, who scored five goals in 14 matches across all competitions for Fulham on loan from Roma in the 2006/07 season, has now been dismissed from his role.

"It has been decided not to continue with our A National Team Head Coach Mr. Vincenzo Montella," the Turkish Football Federation said.

After managerial spells at Roma, Fiorentina and AC Milan, Montella was appointed by Turkey in September 2023.

He won all of his first three games, including a 3-2 victory away to Germany, and secured their qualification for Euro 2024.

Turkey were tipped for a strong tournament and finished second in Group A behind Portugal.

However, after beating Austria in the last 16, the Netherlands knocked them out at the last-eight stage.

Their 2024/25 Nations League campaign earned them promotion to the top tier of the tournament.

Turkey finished second behind Wales in Group B4 and then were 6-1 winners over two legs against Hungary in the play-offs.

Another play-off followed earlier this year as Montella looked to take the country to the 2026 World Cup.

They beat Romania and Kosovo to qualify as one of 48 competing nations at the finals.

However, they finished rock-bottom of Group D with just one win, a 3-2 triumph against co-hosts United States in the last match.

In fact, it's the only fixture that Turkey have not lost across their last seven in all competitions.

They won seven of their previous eight matches before then, and are now eyeing a successor to Montella.

Turkey meet Belgium and France for their reverse Nations League ties next month.

Victory against the former is a must, or they will be relegated from Nations League A to B.
