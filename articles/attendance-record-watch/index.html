Purdy, 49ers shock Rams in Melbourne

The star-studded Los Angeles Rams were stunned 27-7 by the division rival San Francisco 49ers in the NFL's first-ever game in Australia.

Brock Purdy threw for 205 yards, three touchdowns and an interception against the Rams’ new-look defense, including one to new arrival Mike Evans and another to the returning Deebo Samuel. Meanwhile, the 49ers’ defense limited Matthew Stafford to just 15-for-25 passing and an interception while reigning Defensive Player of the Year Myles Garrett recorded zero counting stats in his Rams debut.

Star 49ers linebacker Fred Warner marked his return from an ankle injury that cost him most of the 2025 season with two forced fumbles, the latter of which was recovered by teammate Keion White to effectively seal the game. However, San Francisco’s injury woes continued as wide receiver De'Zhaun Stribling and tight end Jake Tonges both had to be helped off the field.

Attendance record watch

Tonight’s game could be close to breaking some attendance records.

Melbourne Cricket Ground is the largest stadium in the Southern Hemisphere with a capacity just over 100,000, as you can see from Vic Tafur’s video above. NFL home stadium capacities range from about 60,000 to 82,500 at MetLife. The most-attended NFL regular season games are:

  • Sept. 20, 2009; Cowboys vs. Giants at Cowboys Stadium; attendance 105,121 (about 30,000 standing-room only)
  • Oct. 2, 2005; 49ers vs. Cardinals at Estadio Azteca in Mexico City; attendance 103, 467
  • Nov. 10, 1957; 49ers at Rams at L.A. Coliseum; attendance 102,368

Could tonight’s game break the six-figure mark? Of note: The highest-attended cricket match at Melbourne Cricket Ground was on Dec. 26, 2025 between Australia and England with 94,199 fans.

Goodell on NFL's next international step

Roger Goodell made an appearance on the Netflix broadcast and was asked about the possibility of starting an international NFL franchise. Last month, the commissioner said he had "no doubt" the NFL will eventually have teams outside of the United States, but the timeline doesn't seem imminent.

💬 “Our work here will be to get to 16 regular-season (international) games. When we get to that point we'll be looking for the next place to grow. We think we'll be there in a few years, maybe in four years, but the bottom line is this is going to be an incredible growth opportunity for us.”

International game schedule

Welcome to Melbourne, Australia, the first of a record nine international games for the NFL this season. Melbourne is one of three new destinations for the league this year, along with Rio de Janeiro and Paris.

Here’s a look at the full international lineup, which begins and ends with a 49ers matchup. Let’s hope their travel strategy works!

  • Melbourne, Australia; 49ers-Rams; Week 1
  • Rio de Janeiro, Brazil; Ravens-Cowboys; Week 3
  • London; Colts-Commanders; Week 4
  • London; Eagles-Jaguars; Week 5
  • London; Texans-Jaguars; Week 6
  • Paris; Steelers-Saints; Week 7
  • Madrid; Bengals-Falcons; Week 9
  • Munich, Germany; Patriots-Lions; Week 10
  • Mexico City; Vikings-49ers; Week 11

This spring, NFL team owners voted to have as many as 10 international games beginning in 2027. As recently as 2024, there were just five in a season.

Debut for new-look Rams defense

The Rams had the NFL’s best offense by several metrics in 2025, then went out and overhauled their defense with a stunningly bold offseason. They traded for All-Pro cornerback Trent McDuffie and signed his former Chiefs teammate Jaylen Watson to upgrade at cornerback, then swung a blockbuster deal to acquire reigning Defensive Player of the Year Myles Garrett from the Browns — luring fellow future Hall of Fame pass-rusher Aaron Donald out of retirement.

That new-look defense makes its debut against the 49ers tonight, with one exception — Donald, who did not travel with the team to Australia. The 35-year-old Donald, who last played an NFL game in January 2024, is still ramping up after officially unretiring on August 30. Still, Rams defensive coordinator Chris Shula’s new-look unit has sky-high potential even without Donald back in the fold just yet.

Two very different travel plans

For all the similarities the 49ers and Rams have shared since former colleagues Kyle Shanahan and Sean McVay took over the NFC West’s two California teams in 2017, tonight’s game (being played on Friday morning in Australia) is notable for a stark difference.

The 49ers have been on the continent for nearly a week, adjusting their body clocks and doing plenty of community engagement in a market the NFL desperately wants to gain more of a foothold in. The Rams took off from Los Angeles on Tuesday night local time and landed in Melbourne on Thursday morning in Australia, where they are scheduled to depart within hours of the end of the game.

Our Jonathan Jones, Nate Atkins and Matt Barrows have a phenomenal deep dive into how two extremely similar teams landed on opposite approaches to the unique challenges of Australia.
