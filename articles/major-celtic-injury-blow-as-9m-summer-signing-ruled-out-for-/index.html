Major Celtic injury blow as £9m summer signing ruled out for 'intensive treatment'

TheTools · 25.09.2026 13:24 · source · permalink

Celtic winger Haissem Hassan is injured and will not play in Egypt's forthcoming matches.

The 24-year-old was signed by Celtic for a fee of up to £9 million from Real Oviedo and has made nine appearances for the Scottish champions.

Hassan was set to feature in Egypt's squad for the two African Cup of Nations qualifiers against Angola and South Sudan, as well as a friendly against South Africa.

However, he has been left out due to an unspecified injury.

Egypt manager Hossam Hassan said: "Haissem Hassan is receiving intensive treatment just now.

"But I am not worried about his absence because we have a lot of excellent players to replace him.

"Haissem is a vital technical asset for our long-term vision, but his recent injury made it impossible for him to join us for this camp.

"We prioritise player health and full physical recovery above all else and we expect him back once he is 100 per cent fit.

"We cannot halt our progress or make excuses because of injuries.

"The secret to our success lies in navigating these difficulties, and I assure everyone that the team are fully ready."
