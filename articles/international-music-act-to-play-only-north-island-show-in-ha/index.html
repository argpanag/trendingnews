International music act to play only North Island show in Hamilton

TheTools · 19.10.2026 23:31 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

Chase and Status played at Rhythm & Vines in 2022.

British drum and bass duo Chase & Status will play one of two New Zealand shows in Hamilton next year.

Known for hits such as Baddadan, Backbone (a collaboration with Stormzy), Disconnect, Blind Faith and End Credits, Chase & Status sold out a 2025 United Kingdom arena tour.

Formed in London in 2003, Saul Milton (Chase) and Will Kennard (Status) are taking their seventh studio album Generic Everything on the road.

Presented by Handsome Tours & TEG Live, they will play at Dunedin’s Forsyth Barr Stadium on March 5 and Claudelands Oval in Hamilton on March 6, before shows in Sydney, Brisbane and Melbourne.

Hamilton Mayor Tim Macindoe said Hamilton is increasingly becoming a city where big things happen.

“Welcoming Chase & Status is another exciting and historic moment for our city.

“Having one of the biggest names in drum and bass choose Hamilton for an exclusive North Island show is a huge win for our city.

“It will bring thousands of fans to Hamilton and create a fantastic atmosphere, with the benefits flowing well beyond the gates at Claudelands.”

Hamilton and Waikato Tourism general manager Nicola Greenwell said it was great to have “such a big name” added to Hamilton’s events calendar.

“They add real energy and vibrancy and reinforce Hamilton’s reputation as a place to be, especially this coming summer.”

The New Zealand dates are supported by the Government’s one-off Events Attraction Package.

Chase & Status performed at Christchurch’s Electric Avenue Festival in 2025, a sold-out co-headlining show with Luude at Auckland’s The Trusts Arena in 2024, and Rhythm & Vines 2022.

Danielle Zollickhofer is the Waikato news director and a multimedia journalist at the Waikato Herald. She joined NZME in 2021 and is based in Hamilton.
