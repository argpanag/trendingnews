Athapaththu’s majestic spell guides Sri Lanka to mammoth win   | ICC

TheTools · 02.09.2026 18:59 · source · permalink

Defeating Indonesia, the defending champions made it two wins from two games at the Asia Cup 2026.

Sri Lanka captain Chamari Athapaththu led from the front as they breezed past Indonesia by 75 runs.

Defending a target of 125, Athapaththu collected seven wickets, which included a hat-trick, and gave away only five runs in 3.3 overs. 

The bowling figures are now the fifth-best bowling figures in Women’s T20Is. It's only the sixth instance of a bowler having picked up seven wickets or more in a Women's T20I.

Best bowling figures in Women's T20Is

 

Having been put in to bat first, Sri Lanka were put under pressure by the Indonesian bowlers.

The defending champions were reduced to 62/6. Nilakshika Silva would go on to revive the innings, scoring 50 runs off 35 deliveries. The innings included three fours and one maximum. Sri Lanka posted 124/7 in their quota of 20 overs.

In their chase of 125, Indonesia were unable to form a partnership as Sri Lanka were on the offensive from the get-go.

Athapaththu removed both openers, Maria Corazon and Rahmawati Pangestuti, as well as her Indonesian counterpart, Ni Putu Ayu Nanda Sakarini, in the powerplay.

She eventually wrapped up the game with a hat-trick to dismiss Indonesia for 49 in 18.3 overs.

Also among the wickets were Kawya Kavindi, Sugandika Kumari and Kavisha Dilhari as Sri Lanka registered a second successive win at this Asia Cup and are currently top of Group B.
