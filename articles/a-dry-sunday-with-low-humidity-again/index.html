A Dry Sunday with Low Humidity (Again)

TheTools · 27.09.2026 13:05 · source · permalink

SARASOTA, Fla. (WWSB) - The Suncoast has the best weather in the country today (in my humble opinion!) While a Nor’easter pounds the New England states, the remnants of Hurricane Polo bring flooding rains to the southwestern states, and Hurricane Nolo brings flooding rains to parts of Hawaii, our Suncoast weather is nearly perfect. Dew points hold in the low 60s again today. And no afternoon storms for Sunday and Monday.

By Wednesday, the moisture returns to more typical levels, and shower and storm activity increases. We’ll see showers likely and possibly a thunderstorm, particularly after 2 p.m. This pattern continues through the rest of the week with scattered to numerous showers and thunderstorms becoming more widespread, especially in the afternoons and evenings.

TROPICAL OUTLOOK - NEXT 7 DAYS

The remnants of Tropical Storm Fay continues to weaken in the Atlantic, far away from the Suncoast. This system is moving slowly toward the south at just 2 mph, with a turn to the southwest expected early next week. Maximum sustained winds are near 35 mph, and little change in strength is forecast over the next several days. This system poses no threat to our area.

Elsewhere, a surface trough of low pressure located several hundred miles southeast of Bermuda is producing disorganized showers and thunderstorms. Environmental conditions could become favorable for gradual development as this system moves northward this weekend and then turns eastward by early to mid-next week. However, the formation chance remains low at just 10 percent through 48 hours and 20 percent through 7 days. We’ll continue to monitor this system, but it’s not expected to impact the Suncoast.

🎾 Sunday Sports Forecast – Tennis, pickleball, and golf players—Sunday is your day! Mostly sunny skies with a high near 86 degrees will provide ideal conditions for outdoor sports. The light winds shifting from northeast to northwest in the afternoon will keep things comfortable. Low humidity and pleasant temperatures make this a perfect day to hit the courts or the links. Get out there and take advantage of this beautiful weather while it lasts!

🏖️ Sunday Beach Forecast – No Storms, Low Humidity

Rip Current Risk: Low Lightning Risk: Zero

The Suncoast beaches are calling your name this Sunday! Expect mostly sunny skies with a high in the mid-80s and light winds from the east around 5 to 10 mph, shifting to the northwest in the afternoon. The rip current risk is low, and surf heights will remain minimal at 1 foot or less. There’s no thunderstorm potential, so you can plan your beach day with confidence. Water conditions will be calm and pleasant—perfect for swimming, paddleboarding, or just relaxing by the water.

⛵ Suncoast Boating Forecast

Boating conditions remain favorable through the period with light winds and calm seas. Just keep an eye on afternoon thunderstorm development starting Tuesday.

Sunday: East winds 5 to 10 knots becoming north in the afternoon. Seas 1 foot or less with light chop in bay and inland waters.

Monday: East winds around 5 knots becoming southwest around 5 knots in the afternoon. Seas 1 foot or less with smooth conditions in bay and inland waters.

Tuesday: East winds 5 to 10 knots becoming south in the afternoon. Seas 1 foot or less with light chop in bay and inland waters. Scattered showers and thunderstorms possible in the afternoon.

Wednesday: Southeast winds 5 to 10 knots. Seas 1 foot or less with light chop in bay and inland waters. Isolated showers and thunderstorms in the morning, then scattered showers and thunderstorms in the afternoon.
