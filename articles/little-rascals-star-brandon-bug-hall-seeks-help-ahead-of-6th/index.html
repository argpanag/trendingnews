'Little Rascals' star Brandon 'Bug' Hall seeks help ahead of 6th baby

TheTools · 09.11.2026 15:47 · source · permalink

Former child actor Brandon "Bug" Hall is asking for financial support as he prepares to welcome his sixth child.

The "Little Rascals" star, 41, shared a GoFundMe fundraiser asking for assistance paying off various bills before his wife Jill is due to welcome baby No. 6 in less than a month. He explained that it's been six years since he "self-cancelled" and that he'd avoided seeking help in all that time.

"I always said this was my choice, and so I didn't want to offload the glorious burden of it unless there was no other choice. Well here we are," Hall wrote. "We've made it onto the new land in record time, but I'm a few materials short of having things working right, and I've fallen a little behind on the few bills we have (phone, propane for the camper, and an unexpected electric hookup service fee)."

He said that his family is all "overjoyed to be where we are, and we're even enjoying roughing it," adding that he really needs help "getting the last few things buttoned up beforehand."

"My hope is to finalize everything here over the next two weeks so I can take some work until the baby arrives. Times are hard for everyone, and I'm no more deserving than anyone else, but I ask in your charity that you help where you can, whether monetarily, by sharing this, or by prayer and sacrifice for the Hall-Barnetts of the Holy Cross," he concluded.

The fundraiser has raised over $15,000 as of Saturday, Nov. 7.

He and Jill, who have been married since 2017, share four daughters and one son, who are seen in a photograph included in the GoFundMe page.

Bug Hall-Barnett was arrested in 2020 for 'huffing'

Hall was arrested in June 2020 for "huffing," a form of substance abuse that involves inhaling fumes and vapors in order to experience a high. Officers responded to a call about a possible overdose/poisoning after eyewitnesses reported seeing someone huffing by a dumpster in Weatherford, Texas. 

Hall-Barnett was booked on possession for use to inhale, or ingest a volatile chemical. He was released June 21 after spending the night in jail.

Six years earlier, Hall had reunited with his co-stars to celebrate the film's 20th anniversary by re-creating iconic scenes from the movie.

The Fort Worth, Texas, native is best known for playing the cowlick-haired and dorky Alfalfa in 1994's "The Little Rascals." Before leaving his Hollywood career behind in 2020, he also appeared in movies like "Hercules," "The Big Green," "Honey" and "We Shrunk Ourselves, as well as several TV shows, including "Charmed" and "CSI: Miami."
