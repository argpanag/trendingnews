Sabah 1-2 Borussia Dortmund: Serhou Guirassy scores twice as visitors grind out win

TheTools · 20.10.2026 23:08 · source · permalink

  • Scores

  • Live Text

Dortmund came into the match on a three-game winless run in all competitions

Serhou Guirassy scored in each half as Borussia Dortmund ground out a 2-1 win against Sabah in Azerbaijan.

Dortmund were looking to bounce back after their 1-0 defeat at Bodo/Glimt a week ago, and went ahead in the 10th minute when Guirassy pounced on a rebound after Sabah goalkeeper Stas Pokatilov spilled a shot.

But Sabah, buoyed by beating Slavia Prague for their first-ever Champions League victory last week, improved and began to threaten through quick transitions and counter-attacks.

Their persistence was rewarded in the 39th minute when defender Aden McCarthy headed in from a free-kick on the right wing.

Half-time came at a bad time for the hosts, who had put Dortmund under pressure and had an effort from Joy-Lance Mickels ruled out for offside.

Dortmund regrouped after the break and looked more comfortable, and reclaimed the lead when Guirassy met Joey Veerman's excellent cross with a cushioned volley past Pokatilov to double his tally.

The game became stretched in the closing stages as both sides created chances - Guirassy and Fabio Silva spurned one-on-ones to extend Dortmund's lead, before Sabah substitute Orphe Mbina blazed wide and Veljko Simic mis-hit a decent opportunity.

Dortmund have six points and will host Real Betis next on Wednesday, 4 November (20:00 BST).

Sabah remain on three points and face a trip to Norway to play Viking at the same time.
