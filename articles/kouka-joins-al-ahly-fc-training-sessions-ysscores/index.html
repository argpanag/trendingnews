Kouka joins Al Ahly FC training sessions | YSscores

TheTools · 06.10.2026 17:24 · source · permalink

Al Ahly FC's Ahmed Kouka has recovered from his recent injury and has rejoined the team's training sessions after receiving the green light and full recovery as announced by the Red Devils.

Al Ahly FC trained today at the Tetch Stadium in preparation for their match against Petrol Asyut, scheduled to take place as part of the Cairo Capital Cup.

Al Ahly FC will face Petrol Asyut tomorrow, Wednesday, in the first round of the Cairo Capital Cup, with the match kicking off at 5 PM Cairo time.

Ahmed Kouka participated in the collective training of the red team today, Tuesday, after recovering from the injury he sustained.

Ahmed Kouka has been suffering from a second-degree strain in his hamstring, which has kept him from participating in training during the past period.
