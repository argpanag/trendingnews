GSIS launches eco-friendly mobility loan for Tacloban pensioners

TACLOBAN CITY – The Government Service Insurance System (GSIS) launched the Ginhawa Bike and E-Mobility Loan (GBEL) for Pensioners on Friday, a new financing program aimed at helping GSIS pensioners meet their daily transportation needs through eco-friendly options.


The launch is part of GSIS’ “Ginhawa for All” initiative under the Bagong Pilipinas program.


Present at the press launch were GSIS Senior Vice President for VisMin Group lawyer Isagani Del Rosario, GSIS Vice President for Visayas Office Maria Rosanna Sanchez, GSIS Board of Trustee Rolando Toledo, and GSIS Tacloban Branch Manager Rosenie Chavez. 


“The program was created to recognize that retirement does not end a person’s need to move around. Many pensioners still visit family, attend medical appointments, run errands and take part in community activities,” Toledo said.


Under GBEL, qualified regular, disability, and portability pensioners under 80 years old may borrow up to 100 percent of the purchase price of a brand-new bicycle or electric mobility device, with a maximum loan of PHP300,000. 


The loan carries a 5 percent annual interest rate, computed in advance, and is payable in 36 equal monthly installments over three years. For example, a PHP300,000 loan would have a monthly amortization of P9,583.33.


Covered units include pedal-assist bicycles, electric bicycles, electric cargo bikes, electric mini-bikes, and other comparable electric personal mobility devices intended for personal use.


To protect borrowers, GSIS requires pensioners to retain at least 25 percent of their basic monthly pension after deductions. Below that threshold, they may still obtain a loan with written consent, as long as total monthly deductions do not exceed their actual pension.


The PHP300,000 cap is not an automatic entitlement. The approved amount will depend on the purchase price and the applicant’s eligibility assessment.


Applicants must have received at least one monthly pension and have no outstanding amortizations under the Choice of Loan Amortization Schedule for Pensioners and/or the Program for Restructuring and Repayment of Debts at the time of application. Eligibility will be assessed on an individual basis.


The age limit of under 80 was set as part of GSIS’ Board-approved policy, in line with prudent lending and risk management guidelines, to protect the pension fund.


Applications can be submitted entirely online via the GSIS Touch mobile app, eliminating the need to visit a GSIS office. Pensioners must upload supporting documents, such as a quotation or official receipt. 


Once approved, loan proceeds will be credited to the borrower’s account within three business days.


GSIS said GBEL is voluntary and encourages pensioners to prioritize basic needs such as food and medicine. 


The agency also urged borrowers experiencing payment difficulties to contact GSIS promptly for proper assessment. (PNA)
