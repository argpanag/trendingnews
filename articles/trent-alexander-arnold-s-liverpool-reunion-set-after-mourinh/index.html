Trent Alexander-Arnold's Liverpool reunion set after Mourinho details Real plans

TheTools · 08.09.2026 07:30 · source · permalink

Trent Alexander-Arnold will be reunited with his former Liverpool teammate Curtis Jones this week amid his woes at Real Madrid under Jose Mourinho.

The right-back returns to European football following Real Madrid's tough 1-0 loss to Real Betis in La Liga. The Spanish giants will host Jones' Inter Milan on Tuesday in the first Champions League fixture of the season.

This reunion comes at a good time for Alexander-Arnold, who is struggling to make his mark at Real. So far, the defender has started just one game this campaign, as new manager Mourinho prefers to play Dumfries at full-back.

Meanwhile, Jones is enjoying life at Inter after leaving Anfield in a £30million summer transfer. He was brought on in the second half of Inter's 3-2 win over Napoli on the weekend, helping to turn the game on its head.

The midfielder hailed the Serie A side's fans following the win and exclaimed his love for his new club. Jones said: "I'm absolutely over the moon. I am so happy to be part of a team and a club like this. It was obviously a hard game, but the lads stuck to the game plan. The fans were great throughout the whole match, and that is why we won."

While both former Reds have started their seasons in very different ways, Alexander-Arnold will be looking forward to seeing a friendly face in the Champions League.

Mourinho has outlined his long-term plans for Alexander-Arnold, all but confirming he's not one of the first names on his teamsheet.

He told AS: "The team has always been good. I haven't seen the stats, but we created a lot. We missed three or four clear chances and their goalkeeper made three or four impressive saves.

"I didn't make changes because I wasn't happy; they were to take off the players who had been booked. It's better to play with Trent and Yan Diomande than with Denzel Dumfries."

With Diomande still a teenager, Alexander-Arnold will continue to see limited opportunities at Real. The right-back had been linked with a sensational homecoming to Anfield this summer as Andoni Iraola pursued defensive reinforcements.

As speculation continued to mount, the 27-year-old acknowledged that he will always consider Liverpool "home" in his heart. Alexander-Arnold told the Mirror in August: "I still watch every [Liverpool] game. I support them. I want to see them do well. I enjoy watching them, and there will always be a place in my heart for Liverpool, no matter what.

"It is home to me. It's the place that gave me the opportunities to go and do what I've done, and I will always love it. It's always a place that I'll call home."
