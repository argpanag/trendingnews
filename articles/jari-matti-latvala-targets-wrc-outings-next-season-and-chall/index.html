Jari-Matti Latvala targets WRC outings next season and challenges Sebastien Ogier - RallyJournal.com

TheTools · 12.10.2026 05:45 · source · permalink

Latvala remains Toyota’s WRC team principal in name, but deputy team principal Juha Kankkunen has stood in for him at most rounds. Latvala has competed in the European Historic Rally Championship over the past couple of seasons and has also been a familiar name on the Rally Finland entry list.

It is no secret that Latvala still has the desire to compete. The 41-year-old Finnish star continues to dream of WRC rallies too, albeit only one-off appearances. He still has the pace, as he demonstrated last summer when he was the fastest Rally2 driver at Rally Finland and finished an impressive eighth overall.

“Jyväskylä was a great experience and I really enjoyed driving there. I said at the time that it wouldn’t be my last rally,” Latvala told RallyJournal.com with a laugh at Rally Sardinia.

“I’ll probably drive a Rally2 car again because, as things stand, we already have so many Rally1 cars. There could be 5-6 drivers on the Rally1 side.”

Latvala also has another reason to contest more WRC rallies. Nine-time world champion Sebastien Ogier broke Latvala’s record for WRC starts in Sardinia. The French star now has 214 WRC starts to his name, one more than Latvala.

It is, of course, possible that Ogier will also compete next year or later in the future, but Latvala, a passionate rally man, issued a small challenge. Ogier has announced that he will not contest at least the opening round of the 2027 season in Monte Carlo, while the multiple champion has not shown much enthusiasm for the new-regulation top-class cars either.

“There really aren’t any cars available on the Rally1 side, but it would be good to do a couple of rallies in a Rally2 car. A certain former team-mate went and broke my record,” Latvala said.
