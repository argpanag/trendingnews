Quote Of The Day By Denzel Washington: 'I Never Shed A Tear For My Father. I Never Did All Through The Funeral. There Was...'

TheTools · 17.10.2026 19:09 · source · permalink

Denzel Washington is one of the greatest actors of his generation, earning 8 Oscar nominations over the course of his career, as well as winning the best actor in a supporting role Oscar in 1990 for playing a Civil War soldier in "Glory," before landing the coveted best actor in a leading role award for "Training Day" in 2002.

The star played Malcom X in Spike Lee's critically acclaimed film about the activist, as well as starring as heavyweight boxer Rubin Carter in "The Hurricane." But while most audiences know Washington for his shining Hollywood credits, it's important to know that a cornerstone of his early career was one of the best medical dramas of all time: "St Elsewhere." In it, he played Dr. Philip Chandler for 118 episodes before the character resigned from his job to move to Mississippi. Washington agreed to star in "St. Elsewhere" to provide a base for him where he could work in film at the same time.

Since then, he has become one of Hollywood's most bankable stars, and he has the ability to turn a simple post-apocalyptic adventure into a hit piece of pop culture. Yes, that means a prequel series to "The Book of Eli" starring John Boyega is in the works. But while we wait for that to materialize, Washington is today's quote of the day.

Quote of the Day by Denzel Washington

Static Media

"I never shed a tear for my father. That sounds like a book or a song. I never did all through the funeral and all that. There was no connection."

The quote comes from a 2012 profile of Denzel Washington in GQ where he recalled what his life was like growing up in a New York suburb with his parents. He explained that they divorced while he was 14, and although he spent time living with them both separately, he didn't have a connection with his father.

Washington explained that his father was a preacher and also worked other jobs, so they didn't have a huge opportunity to spend time together. He noted that when he wasn't at school in the summer, he wasn't interested in seeing his father, and their relationship started to "fade." He added: "Not to say that I didn't love him like a dad. But we didn't play ball, those types of things."

Deeper Meaning of Denzel Washington's quote — your feelings are valid, even if you don't feel anything

Fred Duval/Shutterstock

Denzel Washington's quote might seem more like an anecdote about his own life than advice, but there's still something we can learn from this perspective about family: Your feelings are completely valid, even if that means you aren't feeling anything at all. His reflection about his life growing up was that his parents were incredibly busy people working numerous jobs to provide for their family. Because of this, his distant relationship led to never shedding a tear when his father died in 1991.

That doesn't mean he wasn't affected by his father's death, but it seems like he was processing his grief in a different way. Working through something as complex as the death of a loved one isn't a straightforward path, and grief can manifest in different ways for different people. It's okay if your version of grief isn't the same as other people you know.

It's clear from the rest of the interview that Washington was grateful for having a father who was a good role model, as he also said: "Everyone I grew up with didn't have a father. I had a father. My father was a decent man. He was a very spiritual man and a gentleman." So, allow yourself to feel grief and loss in whatever way makes the most sense to you.
