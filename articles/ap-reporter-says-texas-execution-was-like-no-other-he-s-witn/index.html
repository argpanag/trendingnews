AP reporter says Texas execution was like no other he's witnessed before

TheTools · 20.11.2026 11:08 · source · permalink

Texas executed Reinaldo Dennes on Thursday in a procedure that lasted 63 minutes. Associated Press reporter Michael Graczyk, who has covered capital punishment in Texas since 1983 and witnessed nearly 500 executions, said Dennes' execution was unlike any he had seen before.
