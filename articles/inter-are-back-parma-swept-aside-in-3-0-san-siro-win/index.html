Inter are back: Parma swept aside in 3-0 San Siro win

TheTools · 10.10.2026 18:27 · source · permalink

First Team

First-half goals from Sucic, Calhanoglu and Thuram put the game against Parma to bed inside little more than half an hour, as Inter returned from the international break with a convincing victory

Perhaps it was only tonight that we truly realised just how much we had missed Inter. How much we had missed watching this team entertain, excite and make us feel good. Those familiar emotions returned in an instant on this October evening in Milano, with a cool breeze drifting through the city and the first signs of autumn beginning to show. Three goals, three points and a whirlwind opening half-hour were enough for Inter to take complete control of the game against Parma and all but settle the contest before the break.

It was an evening of surging runs into space, perfectly weighted through balls, sharp first-time combinations and a team determined to show its quality. Parma tried to play, to build from the back and impose themselves, but there was little they could do against this version of Inter – the one we have come to know so well in recent years. At this level, one mistake can open up an entire pitch. Petar Sucic made Parma pay in the 13th minute, pouncing on Keita after Corvi’s short goal kick before thumping the ball into the net. Two minutes later, Pio Esposito was brought down in the box by Diego Carlos. Hakan Calhanoglu, back from injury, stepped up and was as ice-cold as ever from the spot: by the 18th minute, it was already 2-0. Parma were rattled and struggled to recover. Just when they looked ready to push forward and gain territory, they left space behind and against an Inter midfield playing with this much rhythm and confidence, that was always going to be dangerous. The third arrived in the 31st minute. Mkhitaryan released Thuram into space and Marcus did the rest, racing clear before finishing across goal. It was his sixth goal in six appearances against Parma, and an almost perfect replica of the Scudetto-clinching strike he scored against the same opponents last May, at the very same end of San Siro.
