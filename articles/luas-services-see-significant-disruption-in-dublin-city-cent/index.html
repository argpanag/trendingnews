Luas services see significant disruption in Dublin city centre amid power fault

TheTools · 24.09.2026 09:13 · source · permalink

Luas Green Line services are not running through central Dublin on Thursday morning due to a power fault.

There is no service operating between St Stephen’s Green and Dominick, according to Luas.

The Green Line is operating between Brides Glen and St Stephen’s Green.

All Luas tickets are valid on Dublin Bus.
