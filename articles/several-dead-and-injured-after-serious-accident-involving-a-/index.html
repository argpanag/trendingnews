Several Dead and Injured After Serious Accident Involving a Tour Bus

TheTools · 10.09.2026 20:40 · source · permalink

Susch, GR

A Dutch tour bus was involved in an accident on Thursday evening while traveling from Susch toward Zernez, GR.

A tour bus was involved in an accident Thursday evening between Zernez and Susch in the canton of Graubünden. Several people were killed and others were injured.

Several people died and several others were injured Thursday evening in a serious traffic accident involving a tour bus between Zernez and Susch in the canton of Graubünden.

The Graubünden Cantonal Police reported this on the platform X. No information was initially provided regarding the exact number of fatalities and injuries.

A large number of emergency responders were on the scene, including several helicopters. The cantonal police did not specify whether only the tour bus was involved in the accident and said they would provide further information.

As a result of the accident, the Engadinerstrasse between Zernez and Susch was closed, a police spokesperson said. The Julier Pass–Ofen Pass–Münstertal route to South Tyrol was not affected.

A car carrying 48 people

Dutch media, citing the Dutch travel industry association ANVR, report that the tour bus was carrying 48 people. An ANVR spokeswoman told "Blick" : “We are very saddened by what has happened. It is a terrible accident.”

The tour bus was involved in an accident for reasons that are still unknown.

KEYSTONE/ENGADINER POST/JON DUSCHLETTA

The bus was operated by the well-known Dutch travel company Oad. Oad CEO Arjan Koster told the ANP news agency that he could not provide any further details about the accident and that helping the victims was currently the top priority. He added that he would first wait for official information from the Swiss authorities before making any further statements.

According to a report in the Dutch newspaper *Algemeen Dagblad*, the bus carrying the tour group had set out for Austria on Monday. On the day of the accident, a day trip to Switzerland was on the itinerary. The bus is reportedly owned by the tour bus company Hartemink, based in Eibergen.
