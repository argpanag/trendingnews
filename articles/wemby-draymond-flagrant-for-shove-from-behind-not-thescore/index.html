Wembanyama, who has history with Green following a heated NBA Cup matchup last season, offered a muted assessment when asked about the incident postgame.

"I mean, it's not surprising," he said, per Tom Orsborn of the San Antonio Express-News.

The shove is Green's first flagrant foul of the season. The 36-year-old committed three in 68 games last campaign.

Wembanyama finished the contest with 12 points, 12 rebounds, six assists, two blocks, and a steal in 21 minutes - nine fewer minutes than his season average entering Friday.

Green recorded four points, three rebounds, and three assists in 16 minutes. None of the Warriors' starters scored in double digits, and LJ Cryer led the team with 20 points.

Green and Wembanyama exchanged words and went face-to-face in another NBA Cup contest almost exactly one year ago Friday, after the Spurs star's alley-oop slam over the Warriors forward was called off.
