Nicole Scherzinger told 'you're warrior' as she shows Pussycat Dolls tour injury

TheTools · 08.10.2026 15:52 · source · permalink

Nicole Scherzinger suffered a nasty injury on tour with the Pussycat Dolls(Image: Debbie Hickey/Getty Images)

Nicole Scherzinger has been labelled a "warrior princess" after continuing to tour despite suffering a nasty injury while travelling with the Pussycat Dolls. The 48-year old and her fellow bandmates Ashley Roberts and Kimberley Wyatt are currently embarking on the UK leg of their PCD Forever Tour, with their next performance scheduled at Newcastle's Utilita Arena tomorrow (October 9).

Scherzinger's agonising mishap took place last week when she accidentally poured boiling water over her lower leg just before taking to the stage in Germany. Now - a week on - Nicole has shared an update on her bloodied and bruised limb.

In snaps posted to Instagram, Nicole highlighted the stark contrast between "what they see" and "what they don't see". Her opening shot featured her posing in full makeup and a sports bra, displaying her sculpted midriff.

Nicole then pulled a sultry expression for the lens, showcasing her rich brunette locks and glossy pink lips.

Subsequent images told a completely different tale, however, showing Nicole's lower leg bearing an horrific wound and multiple scars and grazed that had severely damaged her skin.

Responding with sympathy, one admirer wrote: "And you carried on.... if this was footballer they be on the bench for weeks." Another fan added: "So gorgeous and I wish you a speedy recovery."

A third proclaimed: "You're a warrior girl! Going on stage and performing like this with this injury. You have all my respect."

Meanwhile, a fourth Instagram follower reflected: "How you continued to put on a show even after that! Incredible."

And a fifth said: "Look after that babe, no sun for a bit (or at least a little bit at a time for the vitamin D). Warrior Princess is right."

The show must go on(Image: Debbie Hickey/Getty Images)

Addressing those wondering how Nicole had sustained such severe injuries, another fan was quick to explain: "For everyone asking what happened, she spilled boiling water on herself an hour before a show and those are the burns from it."

However, the fan admitted: "I'm sure it stung like a b**** during the show. Would have been nice if she said what happened in the caption though... it's definitely clickbait."

The girls recently took to the stage in Glasgow, with the tour set to continue at:

  • October 9 - Newcastle
  • October 10 - Manchester
  • October 13 - London
  • October 29 - Sao Paulo, Brazil
