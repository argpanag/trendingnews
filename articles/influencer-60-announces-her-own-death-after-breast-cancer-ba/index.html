Influencer, 60, announces her own death after breast cancer battle

TheTools · 24.11.2026 15:50 · source · permalink

Influencer Christie Miller has announced her own death at age 60 in a tragic pre-recorded video message, following a four-year battle with breast cancer.

Miller was diagnosed with the disease back in June 2022 and had amassed more than 50,000 followers on Instagram by documenting her health journey.

But the social media star revealed that she had passed away in an emotional video shared to Instagram on Monday.

In the clip, she donned a rainbow unicorn sweatshirt as she said: 'Hey, hey, hey, it’s Christie Miller and if you are seeing this video, it means that I am dead.'

Miller went on to explain that it also meant she was 'reunited' with her late husband, Mark, who died from pancreatic cancer in June 2025 as she shared a montage of photos of them together.

'That’s right. I have crossed the veil,' she continued. 'I am sitting with my sweetie and my dad and all my loved ones, feeling absolute bliss, with no regrets on how I played the game of life and how I won.'

Miller encouraged her friends, family members and followers to embrace their grief but to focus on the time they had together. 

'If you’re missing me, please be sad. It’s normal to be sad. But also be happy that we got to spend time together,' she urged.  

Influencer Christie Miller announced her own death in a pre-recorded video message that was shared to her Instagram account on Monday, a grab seen here. In it, the social media star, who has been battling stage four breast cancer, said she had 'crossed the veil'

'Whether it was in-person friends or a chance meeting, or we’ve known each other forever.'

She also reminded viewers that 'life is whatever we make it' as she reflected on how she focused on 'love, peace, gratitude and joy' amidst her cancer battle. 

'You can [feel] joy and still have grief. You can be at peace knowing you’re dying. It’s all good,' she added. 

'It’s about choosing what emotion we want to [focus on]. I have lived this beautiful life with no regrets and I am so incredibly [excited] to see Mark on the other side.'

She ended the video by sharing one last piece of 'advice.' 

'My last bit of advice for you is, go out there and live your life, feel the fear and do it anyway,' she said.

'Stop frickin' caring about what anybody thinks, they're too self-involved in their own life to worry about you. Go out there and find joy every single day.'

'Our sweet Christie graduated Earth school peacefully this morning, absolutely surrounded by love. Thank you all so much for the outpouring of love!' the caption of the post read.

Miller had amassed a following of more than 50,000 people by documenting her health journey on Instagram. She often posted videos like this one detailing her treatments, and her positive outlook despite living with cancer captured the hearts of tens of thousands of people across the web

Miller was diagnosed with stage 4 HER2-positive breast cancer in 2022, which means it had spread beyond the breast and nearby lymph nodes and that she had high levels of human epidermal growth factor receptor 2, a protein that helps cancer cells grow and divide.

Since then, her positive outlook despite living with cancer has captured the hearts of tens of thousands of people across the web.

'How can you be so content with a dead husband and stage 4 cancer that has recently progressed to my bones? It's something that I contemplate on a daily basis,' she shared in a video posted back in February.

'Can this really be real? The answer is yes. The reason why I'm so happy is, I have surrendered. But that doesn't mean putting your hands up in the air and giving up.

'What it is is releasing all resistance to what life throws your way. It means embracing the challenges, the lessons, the pitfalls with as much energy as you can.'
