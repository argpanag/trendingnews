Lakeshore GO trains halted after person struck in Toronto | insauga

TheTools · 25.09.2026 11:21 · source · permalink

By Karen Longwell

Published September 25, 2026 at 7:21 am

Last Updated September 25, 2026 at 8:03 am

Update: Before 8 a.m., GO Transit said trains were permitted to move through the area at reduced speeds. Delays, modifications and cancellations were expected as GO Transit works to restore full service.

Original story follows.

A person was struck by a train west of Exhibition GO early Friday morning.

The person’s condition is unknown. The area is now in the hands of emergency personnel, GO Transit said in a post.

“Trains cannot move through the area until a full investigation is completed,” GO Transit said.

As of 7 a.m., there was no service on the Lakeshore West line between Mimico GO and Union Station.

The Aldershot GO 4:52 a.m. and the Union Station 6 a.m. train were holding until further notice.

Trains will run between West Harbour GO and Mimico GO.

GO shuttle buses will operate between Port Credit GO and Kipling GO where passengers can connect to GO Train service to Union Station or the TTC.

Customers at Mimico GO, Long Branch GO and Exhibition GO are advised to use the TTC.

There will be continued delays, modifications and cancellations as we work to restore service, GO Transit said.
