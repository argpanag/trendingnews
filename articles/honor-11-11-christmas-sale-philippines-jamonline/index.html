HONOR 11.11 Christmas Sale Philippines | JamOnline

TheTools · 04.11.2026 15:38 · source · permalink

HONOR Philippines has officially kicked off its HONOR 11.11 Christmas Sale, bringing discounts and smartphone promos across Lazada, Shopee, and TikTok Shop.

Running from November 3 to 13, 2026, the holiday shopping event offers discounts of up to Php 30,000 alongside a headline smartphone deal priced at just Php 1,111.

Php 1,111 HONOR Play 10 Midnight Deal on Lazada

One of the best deals in the holiday promotion is an exclusive flash deal on the HONOR Play 10.

Lazada shoppers can purchase the smartphone for just Php 1,111 starting at 12:00 AM midnight on November 11, while supplies last.

Shoppers looking for premium folding hardware can also take advantage of up to 24 months of 0 percent interest installment plans on the flagship HONOR Magic V6 through participating bank credit cards.

Exclusive Shopee and TikTok Shop Promos

Over on Shopee, customers can participate in the Watch the CEO New Jowa and Win an HONOR 600S campaign for an opportunity to take home the device.

Shopee users can also look forward to the exclusive platform arrival and special introductory offers for the HONOR Play 20A.

Meanwhile, TikTok Shop provides early shopping access to the HONOR Pad X7b tablet with price reductions reaching up to Php 2,000.

Livestream shoppers on TikTok can also score bundled gift packages when ordering select devices like the HONOR 600S and HONOR Pad X9b Max.

Flexible Payment Terms and Ecosystem Hardware

To ease seasonal gift buying, HONOR pairs its device discounts with versatile financing options across popular payment channels.

Eligible orders qualify for up to 24 months of 0 percent interest financing via partner bank credit cards, or up to six months of 0 percent interest through SPayLater, LazPayLater, and TikTok PayLater services.

The holiday promotional catalog also spans HONOR broader lifestyle ecosystem, including markdowns on the HONOR Choice Earbuds Clip, HONOR Watch 6, and HONOR Choice Pencil.
