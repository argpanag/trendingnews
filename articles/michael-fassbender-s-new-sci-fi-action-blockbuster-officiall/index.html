Michael Fassbender's New Sci-Fi Action Blockbuster Officially Wins Over Critics on Rotten Tomatoes

TheTools · 10.09.2026 14:25 · source · permalink

Michael Fassbender is back on the big screen with a rip-roaring sci-fi blockbuster that critics have officially labeled one of the best action films of the year. The most expensive South Korean production of all time, Na Hong-jin (The Wailing) returns to the director's chair for an alien invasion thriller that not only boasts a star-studded cast, but more than enough action to fill out its 156-minute runtime. Anticipation has been building ever since the first trailer released, and now the final verdict has arrived from critics.

As of this writing, Hope has a Certified Fresh 80% score on Rotten Tomatoes with 95 critic reviews counted. Most reviews praise the sci-fi thriller for its non-stop action, which is hailed as the best of the year. MovieWeb's own review dubbed the film "an "insanely fun alien invasion epic" and a wild ride, "even when it’s testing your goodwill or letting you down." Despite much of the critic discourse surrounding the questionable digital effects, most believe that the positives vastly outweigh the negatives, making for a truly entertaining theatrical experience.

Hope first received mostly positive reviews following its premiere at Cannes earlier this May, but many reactions pointed out how the film, despite its visceral blockbuster action, used a heavy dose of poor-looking CGI. The epic, 160-minute version of the film that was screened has now been cut down slightly by Na Hong-jin. While it sounds like most of the effects remain the same, that has not stopped most critics from singing the action thriller's praises. In fact, Hope has been consistently described as a hilarious spectacle that features "well-staged action and nerve-jangling suspense."

The plot of Hope follows a group of police officers and hunters who live in a South Korean harbor town confronting an existential threat not of this world. As the trailer reveals, several grotesque extraterrestrials begin to destroy their home, leading to a guns-blazing confrontation. The film stars Hwang Jung-min, Zo In-sung, Jung Ho-yeon, Eum Moon-suk, Lee Sang-hee, and Im Hyun-sik. If you're wondering how Michael Fassbender fits into the equation, he is indeed voicing one of the aliens, alongside Alicia Vikander, Taylor Russell, and Cameron Britton.

Hope is now playing in theaters, and it is not shocking to see why early reactions hailed the film as "Transformers meets Avatar." From its blockbuster action and epic runtime, to the widely positive critic reviews, the makings of a modern sci-fi classic has arrived. Of course, it is too early to tell if Hope will have the kind of box office impact Neon hopes it will, but one thing has become very certain: this is an experience that should be seen on the biggest screen possible.
