  15. Match statspublished at 11:52 BST
    11:52 BST

    Sheff Utd v Wolves (12:00 BST)

    • Sheffield United have won three of their past four home league games versus Wolves (L1), most recently a 2-1 Premier League win in November 2023.
    • Wolves have won three of their past four league matches against Sheffield United (L1), keeping a clean sheet in all three wins in this period.
    • Sheffield United have both scored and conceded in 14 of their past 15 Championship games at Bramall Lane, the exception being their 0-0 draw with Birmingham in August.
    • Wolves’ three away games in the Championship this season have all seen at least four goals (7 for, 7 against). Indeed, they are just the third Championship side since 2004-05 to both score and concede 7+ goals in their first three away games of a season, along with Crewe in 2004-05 (7F 7A) and Blackburn in 2012-13 (9F 7A).
    • Sheffield United have faced the most shots of any side in the Championship this season (103), while they’ve recorded the fourth-highest non-penalty xGA (10.15).
