Title-holders Portugal maintained their 100% record in the Nations League with a 2-1 home win over Norway on Sunday to seal a place in ⁠the March quarter-finals, while Manchester City’s problems may have got worse after Erling Haaland limped off for the visitors.

Portugal hope their victory at the Estádio Do Dragão shifts the focus further away from Cristiano Ronaldo’s abrupt exit from the squad last week. Gonçalo Ramos netted 11 minutes from time to ensure they secured a top-two ⁠finish in Group A4 as ⁠they moved six points ​ahead of second-placed Denmark, who won 1-0 at Wales.

The home side in Porto were still under a cloud from Ronaldo’s dispute with coach Jorge Jesus, although they were triumphant without him in Denmark ⁠last Thursday after he walked out on the squad, and continued their winning ways on Sunday.

Norway took the lead in ​the 36th minute from centre-back Kristoffer Ajer, who hit a ‌dipping half-volley from the edge of ‌the box. But if that effort was sumptuous, João Cancelo’s thrashing effort to equalise two minutes later was even more awe-inspiring.

Ramos’s ‌winner came off his calf after João Félix had headed across goal for him to score.

Portugal’s win was soured by a red card for Francisco Conceição four minutes from time. He gave the referee a cynical thumbs up after receiving a yellow card and was immediately shown a second.

Afterwards, Jesus said Ronaldo can return to the squad if he wishes: “If he wants to, he’ll return to the national team … we can talk before the next call-up, but Cris is the one who’s going to decide.”

With their captain Virgil van Dijk celebrating a 100th cap, the Netherlands went ahead against Serbia when Teun Koopmeiners’s 31st-minute free-kick evaded all in the penalty area and ​crept into the corner. However, the visitors were level within four minutes as Aleksa Terzic crossed for teenager Mihajlo Cvetkovic to rise over Koopmeiners and head in his first international goal.
