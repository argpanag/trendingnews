Daniel Farke provides fitness updates on Stach, Okafor and Calvert-Lewin ahead of Chelsea - Leeds United

TheTools · 21.11.2026 17:14 · source · permalink

Leeds United return to Premier League action on Saturday following the international break, travelling to Stamford Bridge to face Chelsea.

Ahead of the clash, Daniel Farke spoke to the media and provided an update on the fitness of Anton Stach, Noah Okafor and Dominic Calvert-Lewin.

"Since today, I have a whole group back together. It's obviously always a bit complicated because the players who were still involved on Tuesday, they just had an individual rehab session today," he said.

"As it stands, I think everyone can join us tomorrow in team training. And as it stands, no injuries to report."

Providing further detail on Stach and Okafor, the boss added: "He [Stach] was a full week back in training, today, last week, still a bit like part integrated and a bit of individual rehab work.

"Similar to Noah [Okafor], so both of them had a full training week and it was definitely beneficial.

"Dominic [Calvert-Lewin], since he came back from England duties, is also back in training and no problems, so definitely good news."

Farke also commented on Joe Rodon after the central defender made his return from Welsh international action.

"I was a bit concerned after being out for 10 weeks that he started both games, but after he came through and returned without injury, having played more than three halves, I'm quite happy right now," said the boss,

"Joe is a key player for us and it's definitely good that he's back available. I think these minutes will also be beneficial."
