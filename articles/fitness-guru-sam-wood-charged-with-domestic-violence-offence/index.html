Fitness guru Sam Wood charged with domestic violence offences

TheTools · 04.10.2026 08:22 · source · permalink
  • Australia
  • Crime

Updated October 4, 2026 - 6.38pm. First published at 8.22am

Reality TV star and celebrity fitness guru Sam Wood has spent the weekend behind bars on remand after being charged with domestic violence offences.

The 46-year-old, who lives in Melbourne, was on holidays in Queensland when the alleged incident took place.

Reality TV star and celebrity fitness guru Sam Wood has spent the weekend behind bars on remand after being charged with domestic violence offences. Supplied

Police say they were called to Hastings Street on Noosa’s beachfront after reports of an incident about 9pm.

Wood, who starred with his future wife Snezana on The Bachelor in 2015, was arrested in the early hours of Sunday morning.

He was charged with assault occasioning bodily harm (domestic violence) and strangulation in a domestic setting.

The alleged victim cannot be identified.

The fitness guru sold his subscription business 28 by Sam Wood in 2022 but remains the face of it and had recently started up Ollo for Women earlier this year. Sam Wood

Wood was refused bail and is due to appear in Maroochydore Magistrates Court on Monday.

The TV star is associated with a number of fitness and health brands.

He launched an online fitness and weight loss business, 28 by Sam Wood, after his appearance on The Bachelor in 2015.

Wood sold that business in 2022 for a reported $71 million to myDNA, but remained the face of the brand.

He also became part of the Ollo telehealth business earlier this year.

The start-up focuses on health for women aged 40 and over, in a partnership with 28 by Sam Wood, and online nurse and practitioner network Medmate.

Wood has also been involved in its promotion and publicity.

Support is available from the National Sexual Assault, Domestic and Family Violence Counselling Service at 1800RESPECT (1800 737 732). In an emergency, dial Triple Zero (000).
