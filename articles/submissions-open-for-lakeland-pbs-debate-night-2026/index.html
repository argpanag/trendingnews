Submissions open for Lakeland PBS Debate Night 2026

TheTools · 10.09.2026 18:00 · source · permalink

BRAINERD — Submissions are now open for residents to submit their questions for the Lakesland PBS Debate Night 2026, an original broadcast series featuring candidates running for the Minnesota House of Representatives and Minnesota Senate.

Produced and broadcast live from Lakeland PBS’s Bemidji and Brainerd studios during the week of Oct. 5, Debate Night 2026 gives voters a direct line to the candidates seeking to represent their communities in St. Paul. While specific district schedules and times are currently being finalized, all debates will air live throughout the week.

To ensure the discussions reflect the issues that matter most across the region, Lakeland PBS is partnering with local media outlets, including the Bemidji Pioneer, Brainerd Dispatch, Park Rapids Enterprise and KAXE/KBXE Northern Community Radio to review submissions and shape a comprehensive, community-focused panel.

"Healthy democracy relies on informed, active citizens," Jeff Hanks, Lakeland PBS CEO, said in a news release. "By bringing viewer questions directly to local candidates, Debate Night 2026 ensures that the broadcast stays focused on the real concerns, values, and priorities of the people who call Northern and Central Minnesota home."

How to submit questions 

Lakeland PBS encourages a wide variety of perspectives and input from across the coverage area.

Questions must be nonpartisan and applicable to candidates from any political party. Submissions can be made in written form or as recorded video and audio clips. Anonymous submissions are also welcome.
