Over 40 Boats Join Search For Missing Westmeath Man On Midlands Lake

TheTools · 22.09.2026 18:41 · source · permalink
  • News Home
  • More from Midlands News

Tuesday, 22 September 2026 17:24

The search for a man in his 20s is set to continue into tomorrow.

Over 40 boats are currently out on a midlands lake searching for a missing Westmeath man. 

The search for a man in his 20s is entering its third evening after he was reported missing on Lough Ennell over the weekend.

A multi-agency operation got underway after his 17-foot fishing boat was found abandoned on Sunday morning.

Frankie Wright from Lilliput Adventure Centre, which is based on the lake, says they'll be continuing their search until around 7:30pm this evening, with the group expected to return in the morning.
