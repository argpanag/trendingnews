Oulai struggling, Vanoli turns to Brescianini: watch out for surprise inclusion Dodo in defence

Yesterday, ahead of the match against Inter at San Siro, Fiorentina released their squad list for the Serie A matchday 8 fixture. Apart from the known injury absentees, the Viola have a full squad available. Three Primavera players have also been included. Here is the full squad list:

GOALKEEPERS: Oliver Christensen, David De Gea, Luca Lezzerini DEFENDERS: Arnis Ademi, Dodo, Radu Dragusin, Alejandro Jiménez, João Mário, Marin Pongračić, Victor Valdepeñas, Viery MIDFIELDERS: Marco Brescianini, Nicolò Fagioli, Brando Mazzeo, Cher Ndour, Christ Oulai, Antonio Pirrò FORWARDS: Beto, Wilfried Gnonto, Franco Mastantuono, Alieu Njie, Mateo Pellegrino, Pedro Gonçalves

GOALKEEPERS: Oliver Christensen, David De Gea, Luca Lezzerini DEFENDERS: Arnis Ademi, Dodo, Radu Dragusin, Alejandro Jiménez, João Mário, Marin Pongračić, Victor Valdepeñas, Viery MIDFIELDERS: Marco Brescianini, Nicolò Fagioli, Brando Mazzeo, Cher Ndour, Christ Oulai, Antonio Pirrò FORWARDS: Beto, Wilfried Gnonto, Franco Mastantuono, Alieu Njie, Mateo Pellegrino, Pedro Gonçalves

Luca Ranieri and Arthur Atta remain notable absentees, having been struggling with fitness issues for over a week. The defender’s foot problem should not prevent him from being available as early as Thursday against Atalanta, while the Frenchman’s situation looks more complicated. The former Udinese player last played this season more than a month ago against Napoli, before an abdominal overuse issue sidelined him. He continues to train separately, and a return only after the November international break is looking increasingly likely. Oulai has also picked up a minor problem after taking a knock in yesterday’s final training session and is not fully fit for the match at San Siro. Vanoli will still play with a three-man midfield, bringing in Brescianini alongside Ndour and Fagioli. This is according to La Repubblica.

Vanoli comforts Dodo (photo OneFootball)

Dodo could be the surprise inclusion in the lineup, as he is ready to reclaim a starting spot against the very team that reportedly wanted him and pursued him this summer. His recent appearances against Genoa and Como may have changed the Brazilian’s standing in the coach’s plans. Dodo has impressed, putting himself in contention to start in today’s lunchtime match against Inter for the first time in over two months, since the opening-day fixture against Roma. The Brazilian full-back was tested extensively on both the right and left during the week. The feeling, however, is that if he starts, he will play on the right, with Jimenez ahead of Viery in the race to start on the left.
