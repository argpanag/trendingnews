ACC Football Week 7 Headlined by Conference Clashes

TheTools · 17.10.2026 12:47 · source · permalink

Football October 13, 2026

  • The ACC has 11 teams in ESPN’s FPI Top 50, representing nearly two-thirds of the league, while 13 of the conference’s 17 teams are .500 or better entering Week 7.
  • Five teams have one loss or fewer: Miami, Wake Forest, Pitt, SMU and Duke.
  • An FBS-high 15 of 17 ACC teams (88.2%) have already won a conference game.
  • The ACC also leads the FBS with 24 second-half lead changes and 13 fourth-quarter lead changes in conference games, while 12 league games have been decided by one possession.
  • The ACC has already played an FBS-high 17 non-conference games against Power Four opponents or Notre Dame, with 10 played away from ACC home stadiums.
  • ACC teams have earned six non-conference Power Four victories, second-most among the Power Four conferences.
  • All 17 ACC teams are scheduled to play at least 10 games against Power Four opponents or Notre Dame, the most of any conference.
    • Boston College, Georgia Tech and Louisville are three of only five FBS teams scheduled for 11 or more games versus Power Four teams and/or Notre Dame.
  • Boston College, Georgia Tech and Louisville are three of only five FBS teams scheduled for 11 or more games versus Power Four teams and/or Notre Dame.
  • Miami and Wake Forest are two of only three FBS teams with three Power Four road wins this season.
  • North Carolina and Wake Forest each own a top-25 road win, with the Tar Heels winning at then-No. 25 Pitt and the Demon Deacons at then-No. 16 Louisville.
  • ACC quarterbacks have produced an FBS-best 24 300-yard passing performances, five more than the next conference.
  • The ACC also owns seven 400-yard passing games — as many as every other conference combined — and is the only Power Four conference with a 400-yard passing performance this season.
  • The ACC has an FBS-best seven quarterbacks among the top 26 in passing yards per game, six among the top 25 in passing yards, six among the top 30 in passing efficiency and seven among the top 25 in total offense per game.
  • Miami QB Darian Mensah leads the FBS in completion percentage (84.2%), passing efficiency (212.2) and ESPN Total QBR (92.5).
  • Mensah has thrown 15 touchdown passes without an interception through five games and is the only quarterback among the nation’s top 25 in passing yards without an interception.
  • He is one of seven FBS quarterbacks this century with 15+ TD passes and zero interceptions through five games.
  • Mensah is the only Division I or NFL player in the last 30 seasons with at least 15 TD passes and an 84% completion percentage through five team games.
  • Duke’s Nate Sheppard leads the FBS in rushing yards per game (148.6), all-purpose yards per game (180.8) and scoring (13.2 ppg).
  • Sheppard and Louisville’s Isaac Brown own two of the top three single-game rushing performances in the FBS this season — Brown with 235 yards and Sheppard with 227.
  • Carlos Hernandez of Wake Forest leads the nation in receptions (49). He also ranks second in receptions per game (8.17), third in receiving yards (720) and fifth in receiving yards per game (120.0).
  • Miami wide receiver Malachi Toney ranks fourth nationally in receiving yards per game (120.8), seventh in receiving touchdowns (6), seventh in receptions per game (7.80) and eighth in receiving yards (604).
  • Syracuse’s Umari Hatcher also ranks among the nation’s top eight in both receiving yards per game (112.0) and receiving touchdowns (6).
  • California’s Kingston Lopa leads the nation with five interceptions and averages an FBS-best 1.00 interception per game.
  • Clemson’s Ashton Hampton leads the FBS in passes defended (10) and passes defended per game (2.00).
  • North Carolina’s Xavier Lewis ranks second nationally in tackles-for-loss per game (1.90) and fourth in sacks per game (1.30).
  • 10 different ACC teams have scored at least 56 points in a game this season.
    • That is already more than the seven ACC teams that reached 56 points during the entire 2025 season.
  • More than half of the ACC’s 17 programs have already produced a 56-point performance.
  • Five ACC teams rank among the FBS top 40 in scoring offense, all averaging 34.0 points per game or more on the season, led by Miami at a nation-best 49.9 points per game average.
  • That is already more than the seven ACC teams that reached 56 points during the entire 2025 season.
  • The ACC continues to expand Enhanced Replay Access in 2026, giving audiences greater access to real-time replay discussions during broadcasts.
  • The initiative was utilized for 22 games across Weeks 0-3, up from just five over the same span in 2025. Through Week 6, Enhanced Replay Access has been utilized in 43 games this season, with eight additional games scheduled for Week 7.
  • Enhanced Replay Access provides live audio and video among the referee, stadium replay official and ACC Collaborative Instant Replay Official in Charlotte, while four new cameras inside the Game Day Operations Center provide ESPN and ACCN audiences another look inside the conference's replay operations.
  • The ACC is scheduled to play a record 15 Friday games in 2026, including nine over the season’s first six weeks.
  • Friday night football continues throughout October and November, culminating with Florida at Florida State on Black Friday.
