Ryanair warns of delays to UK flights as heavy fog hits parts of the country

TheTools · 02.11.2026 07:38 · source · permalink

Ryanair has warned passengers that heavy fog could delay flights today.

The budget airline urged passengers to check its app for updates as flights to and from the UK could be disrupted.

A message posted on Ryanair’s website after 3am today said: "Due to heavy fog, some flights to and from the UK are delayed today (Sun 1 Nov).

"Affected passengers will be contacted directly through the app.

"Passengers travelling through the UK should monitor the Ryanair app for the latest flight information."

The warning came as the Met Office issued a yellow warning for dense fog across parts of the UK.

The warning covered Bristol, London, Birmingham, Nottingham and Hull, stretching from Norwich in the east to Brighton in the south and from east Wales to Somerset in the south-west.

The Met Office said visibility could fall below 100 metres, disrupting travel.

The warning was in place until 10am today (Sunday, November 1), but forecasters said fog patches could linger into the afternoon and warned that conditions could change.

Base - Ryanair's largest UK base is at Stansted Airport

The Met Office urged people to keep checking local forecasts, saying: "Be prepared for weather warnings to change: when a weather warning is issued, the Met Office recommends staying up to date with the weather forecast in your area."

Ryanair’s biggest UK base is Stansted Airport, where it operates 57 aircraft and serves more than 150 destinations across Europe, Asia and North Africa.

Ryanair has operated from Stansted since May 1991, when it moved its main London base from Luton.

Stansted is London’s third-busiest airport, serves more than 180 destinations and is home to several European low-cost carriers.

The fog was also expected to slow road journeys and delay bus, train and ferry services, with passengers advised to check transport providers before setting off.
