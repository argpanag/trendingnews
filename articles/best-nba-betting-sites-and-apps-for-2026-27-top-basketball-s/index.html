NBA Player Prop Rules to Know

Although not every accusation was proven, it did change how sports betting happens with NBA player props.

NBA Betting Markets Explained

The NBA offers plenty of different options for sports bettors, but you may not be familiar with all of them, so we have explained them for you here: 

Point Spread

A point spread bet is the most common wager for an NBA game. These lines are impacted by players, home court, travel, and rest for games. 

  • Example: Celtics -6.5 (-110) vs. Knicks +6.5 (-110): the Celtics must win by 7 or more.

Moneyline

Unlike a spread bet where a team has to win or lose by a certain amount of points, a moneyline bet is specifically on a team winning or losing the game. 

  • Example: Thunder -250 vs. Timberwolves +205: a $100 bet on the Timberwolves returns $305 total if they win.

Totals (Over/Under)

Totals are listed as either team totals or straight totals. Team totals are for the specific team in question; whatever the other team's score doesn’t matter. For straight totals, this combines both teams’ scores to be either an over or under a set amount. 

  • Example: Nuggets vs. Warriors Over 229.5 (-110): the teams must combine for 230 or more.

Player Props

Player props are common bets for the NBA game as you can focus on one player instead of a full team’s performance. The most common types of bets are on a player’s points, rebounds, or assists. There are also combinations of the three stats. For example, a player could have a line of 35.5 points, rebounds, and assists. They would win if they collected 30 points, five rebounds and one assist, or just 36 points and nothing else. 

  • Example: Star guard Over 27.5 points; center Over 11.5 rebounds; Over 3.5 made threes.

Quarter and Half Bets

Quarter and halftime bets are important because they avoid some of the garbage time play from teams. Quarter bets often can be good if you have one team whose starters play 10 minutes facing another team whose starters only play 8 minutes. The bets isolate sections of the game. 

  • Example: Lakers first-half -3 (-110): The Lakers must lead by 4 or more at halftime.

Same Game Parlays

With a same game parlay, you can wager on multiple things happening in the same game. Since it is often correlated, like a good defensive team winning and the total for the game going under, same game parlays are not paid out as high as non-same game parlays. 

  • Example: Heat moneyline + forward Over 22.5 points + Under 214.5: all three legs must win.

Live Betting

Live betting refers to any bet during the game. Lines can change frequently but are mostly offered during breaks in the game. 

  • Example: Down 12 at halftime, a team's second-half spread is +1.5 at -110.

Futures

Future bets are anything that is placed before the season starts or during the season on things like individual awards, division winners, team win totals, or the NBA Champion. 

  • Example: NBA champion, conference winners, MVP, NBA Cup winner, season win totals.

Common NBA Betting Mistakes

Betting on the NBA can be a lot of fun, but there are plenty of things to consider before placing the wager. Below are some of the things to examine before you lock in a wager: 

  • Betting props before the final injury report.
  • Ignoring rest days and back-to-backs.
  • Stacking long parlays and SGPs without understanding the lower payout.
  • Chasing losses on late West Coast games.
  • Using one sportsbook and never comparing prices.
  • Letting bonus bets expire before using them.
