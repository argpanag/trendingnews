Ipswich man scoops £2.8m lottery jackpot

TheTools · 21.11.2026 22:55 · source · permalink

A family man from Ipswich has scooped a life-changing lottery win amounting to more than £2 million.

The unnamed winner, from near Ipswich, landed the £2,832,772 National Lottery Lotto jackpot after matching all six main numbers in the draw on Wednesday, October 28.

He said: "We were really emotional when we found out we had won.

"It was the most wonderful feeling to know that we are able to help the family.

"Life has just got a whole lot easier, which is incredible."

A regular Lotto player and keen walker, he uses numbers with special meaning to him each week.

The winner plans to use the money to treat himself and his loved ones.

Kathy Garrett, winners’ advisor at Allwyn, operator of The National Lottery, said: "Congratulations to our lucky family man from near Ipswich.

"We hope he and his family enjoy this newfound fortune."

Every week, National Lottery players help generate around £33 million for good causes across the UK.

With over £53 billion raised for Good Causes since launch, this funds hundreds of projects in every postcode area across the UK.
