Rams vs. Raiders odds: Los Angeles at 61% to win as two skidding teams meet in Las Vegas

TheTools · 25.10.2026 19:15 · source · permalink

The Los Angeles Rams visit the Las Vegas Raiders with both teams trying to stop a skid. Kalshi traders have the Rams at 60% to win. Los Angeles is 2-4 after losing 18-16 to Arizona on a late field goal and 37-34 to Buffalo in overtime, and it is playing without receiver Davante Adams, who is on injured reserve with a ruptured hamstring.

Las Vegas opened 3-0 under first-year coach Klint Kubiak, then lost to Kansas City, New England and Buffalo, with Kirk Cousins still starting ahead of No. 1 overall pick Fernando Mendoza.

When and where do the Rams play the Raiders in Week 7 of the 2026 season?

The Rams and Raiders kick off Sunday, Oct. 25, at 4:25 p.m. ET at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas.

Who is favored to win the Rams vs. Raiders Week 7 game?

Los Angeles Rams: Los Angeles is 2-4 and has lost two straight at home, 37-34 in overtime to Buffalo and 18-16 to Arizona, a game in which Matthew Stafford drove the Rams 86 yards for a late touchdown before the Cardinals kicked a 43-yard field goal to win it. Stafford leads the league with 1,794 passing yards. As of this writing, Adams is expected to miss six to 10 weeks, defensive tackle Poona Ford is out for the season, defensive end Kobie Turner is sidelined for multiple weeks, cornerback Trent McDuffie (groin) and receiver Puka Nacua (calf) are questionable, and linebacker Nate Landman (concussion) is out. Kalshi traders have the Rams at 60%, a price that leans on Stafford's production and a Raiders defense that has struggled.

Las Vegas Raiders: Las Vegas is 3-3 after a 3-0 start and has lost three straight, 30-27 to Kansas City, 45-17 to New England and 33-30 to Buffalo. Cousins, who signed a one-year deal, has started all six games and went 23-of-41 for 313 yards, two touchdowns and an interception against Buffalo. The Raiders rank 28th in total defense and have allowed 30 or more points in three straight games. As of this writing, tight end Brock Bowers (hamstring and calf) and defensive tackle Tonka Hemingway (concussion) were full participants Friday, and running back Mike Washington (foot and back) is questionable after not practicing Thursday or Friday. Kalshi traders have Las Vegas at 40%.
