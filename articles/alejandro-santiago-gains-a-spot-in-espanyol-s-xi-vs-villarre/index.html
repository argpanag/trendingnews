Alejandro Santiago gains a spot in Espanyol's XI vs Villarreal

TheTools · 30.10.2026 16:30 · source · permalink

Espanyol can make important changes tonight against Villarreal, with Alejandro Santiago targeting a starting role. Sergio García, the interim coach, has called up several players from the B team ahead of Eder Sarabia's arrival.

Espanyol face tonight's Friday match against Villarreal in a period of transition on the bench and with possible changes in the starting lineup. Sergio García, who is serving as interim coach after the sacking of Manolo González, will lead the team before Eder Sarabia takes charge next Monday.

One of the key talking points from the build-up is the presence of B-team players. The coach has included Jan Peries, Timera, José Ángel and Alejandro Santiago in the squad, and the latter looks set to play a prominent role from the start.

According to information shared by Catedràtics, a contributor to Analítica Fantasy, Alejandro Santiago would be the one chosen to occupy the right flank against Villarreal. If confirmed, the academy player would get a major opportunity in a demanding match for the blanquiazul side.

The same report also puts Pol Lozano and Fortuño in contention to be part of the starting XI. In any case, it is a predicted lineup and not an official confirmation from the club.
