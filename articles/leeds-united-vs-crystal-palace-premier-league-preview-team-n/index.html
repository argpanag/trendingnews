Match Facts

  • Leeds have won four of their last six Premier League home games against Crystal Palace, losing the others in August 1997 (0-2) and April 2023 (1-5).

  • Crystal Palace have lost just one of their last five Premier League games against Leeds (W2 D2), though it was in this exact fixture last season (1-4).

  • Leeds have eight points from four Premier League games this season, their best start since 2001-02 under David O'Leary (also W2 D2). Should Leeds beat Crystal Palace, it would be their joint best start to a Premier League season after five games, having won 11 in that 2001-02 campaign.

  • Crystal Palace have conceded two or more goals in each of their last 10 Premier League matches - only Fulham have ever had a longer run in the competition's history, going 13 games in a row from January to April 2019.

  • Since December 2025, Leeds have won the fifth-most points in the Premier League (44), behind Arsenal (67), Man City (65), Man Utd (54) and Liverpool (45), losing just five of 29 matches (W10 D14).

  • Crystal Palace have faced the most xG (8.15), conceded the most goals (11), faced the joint most shots on target (26) and allowed their opponents the most touches in their own box (164) in the Premier League so far this season.

  • Since the start of February, Leeds winger Noah Okafor has been involved in eight goals in 11 Premier League appearances (7 goals, 1 assist) and has the best minutes per goal or assist ratio of any player to play 500+ minutes in that time (one every 79 minutes).

  • Dominic Calvert-Lewin has scored in each of his last five home Premier League appearances, only the second Leeds player to achieve that after Mark Viduka between March and August 2003. The last Englishman to score in six consecutive home top-flight appearances for Leeds was Arthur Hydes between October and December 1932.

  • Crystal Palace striker Jørgen Strand Larsen has scored in 16 Premier League matches, but he has ended on the losing side in eight of those (50%). He's one of three players to score in 15+ games and lose as many as half of those, after Danny Dichio (lost 9/17, 53%) and Heidar Helguson (lost 13/26, 50%).

  • Across the last two Premier League matchdays, Crystal Palace's Quinten Timber has the best duel success rate of any player to contest more than 20, winning 16 of his 22 in his two games for Palace so far (73%).
