ATP Basel Best Bets Including Wawrinka vs Lehecka

TheTools · 27.10.2026 04:51 · source · permalink

The ATP Basel tournament, also known as the Swiss Indoors, begins in Switzerland. This is a 500-point tournament which runs concurrently with the 500-point event in Vienna. It’s a chance for players in the ATP race to Turin to bank a significant amount of points and change the math in their favor before next week’s defining Paris Masters event, the 1,000-point bonanza which will finally and fully decide which eight players get to Italy for the year-end championship and which ones are either staying home or get to go to Italy as first alternate just in case a retirement or injury occurs. It’s crunch time for the ATP players on the margins of the race, those above and just below the cut line. A key point to remember is that with Jannik Sinner out, No. 9 in the race gets into the ATP Finals as the eighth and final player. Sinner is No. 2 in the race but won’t play in Turin due to his season-ending injury. The goal is to be first in this 2026 ATP Basel event, but ninth or better in the race in two weeks. The chase is on. LWOS has our best bets for three matches. Post your best bets and thoughts in the comments.

ATP Basel

Giovanni Mpetschi Perricard – Arthur Fils: 9:30 EST

H2H: 2-1

This ATP Basel match is an all-French battle. Giovanni Mpetschi Perricard leads the head to head, but none of the matches in this series have been played since 2022, so the head to head means essentially nothing. Arthur Fils is a top-10-ranked player, while Giovanni Mpetschi Perricard is ranked outside the top 140. Mpetschi Perricard is 2-3 in his last five matches. Fils is 3-2.

Best Bet to Make

Ben Shelton – Aleksandar Kovacevic: 12:00 EST

H2H: 3-2

This ATP Basel match contains a head-to-head series with five previous meetings, but none have been since 2022. Ben Shelton is ranked in the ATP top five, while Aleksandar Kovacevic is outside the top 80. It will be up to Kovacevic to try to make this match close.

Best Bet to Make

Parlay/hedge options: Kovacevic +3.5 games at 1.78 at @1xbet

This ATP Basel match points to a clean and decisive Shelton win. Ben is the far superior player here, and the indoor conditions suit his big and powerful game. Take Shelton in straights.

That being said, betting on Shelton -1.5 sets is the best bet you can make

Value bet/ the best odds: Shelton -1.5 sets at 1.70 at @interwetten

Stan Wawrinka – Jiri Lehecka: 14:00 EST

H2H: 1-1

This ATP Basel match could be the last match Stan Wawrinka plays. Wawrinka, the Swiss legend who will soon go into the International Tennis Hall of Fame, will end his career in Switzerland. Can he pull one big upset before he hangs up his racquet and shoes? Jiri Lehecka is a top-25 player, so this will not be easy for Stan The Man.

Best Bet to Make

Parlay/hedge options: Wawrinka +3.5 games at 1.98 at @unibet

Confidence level on a scale of 1-10: 6 – you could bet two thirds of a unit on this match.

This ATP Basel match should point to a decisive Lehecka win. Wawrinka is 41 years old. Lehecka is more than 15 years younger and is ranked in the top 25. The normal outcome in a situation such as this one is for the much younger and much higher-ranked player to win in straight sets.

That being said, betting on Lehecka -1.5 sets is the best bet you can make
