Why Kendrick Perkins Believes Lakers Will Have The Best offense In The NBA In 2026-27

TheTools · 16.10.2026 20:35 · source · permalink
  • NBA on ClutchPoints
  • Los Angeles Lakers

The Los Angeles Lakers might have the best offense behind Luka Doncic. That's according to Kendrick Perkins.

The Los Angeles Lakers are looking at a new era of basketball led by Luka Doncic, Austin Reaves, and Walker Kessler. With their Big Three and with so many new complementary pieces in the fold, it'll be interesting to see how this new comes together.

On a recent episode of ESPN's NBA Today, former player and current analyst Kendrick Perkins expressed his thoughts on this new-look Lakers team, and even ended with a hot take.

“Luka is so great when he has a dynamic roller that loves to finish at the basket,” Perkins said. “But also Luka is a different type of guy in the pick-and-roll. He's not just ahead of steam kind of guy and lobbing at the basket. He's a guy that likes to keep you on his hip, that likes to probe. So I want to see them develop that same synergy that he had with Daniel Gafford and Dereck Lively, right?

“Because they knew, when Luka was in the pick-and-roll, to always stay ready. The pass could come late, it could come early, and so just watching that because once they develop that — we know Luka is going to get buckets — but outside of that, it's going to open up so many wide open threes for Austin Reaves, for Quentin Grimes, and others that I believe the lakers are going to have the best offensive team in the league in my opinion when you think about what they're capable of doing and Luka just being an offensive threat himself.”

“The Lakers are gonna have the best offensive team in the league in my opinion.”

This will be the best-fitting roster that Luka Doncic has had since being traded to the Lakers at the February 2024 trade deadline. With a number of shooters, defenders, and athletic big men around him, this has a chance to be Doncic's best season in the NBA.

The Lakers will tip off their 2026-27 regular season campaign by hosting the Golden State Warriors on Wednesday, October 21st.
