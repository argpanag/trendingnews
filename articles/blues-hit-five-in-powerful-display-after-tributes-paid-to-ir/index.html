Blues hit five in powerful display after tributes paid to Irish League legend Roy Coyle

TheTools · 22.11.2026 11:01 · source · permalink

ON a day when Irish League legend Roy Coyle was honoured at all BOYLE Sports Premiership fixtures, Linfield – the club he led to 10 titles in 12 seasons – fired a warning about their present-day ambitions with a thumping 5-0 win over Limavady United at Windsor Park.

Coyle’s passing at the age of 80 was announced on Friday and the current Blues side produced a performance that would have impressed the man who managed the club for 15 trophy-laden years up until 1990 followed by stints at Derry City, Ards and Glentoran.

Goals from Kitt Nelson, former Antrim GAA star Matthew Fitzpatrick and Chris Shields powered Linfield into a 3-0 lead after just 20 minutes. Fitzpatrick doubled his tally after the break and Kobei Moore added a fifth, a result that means David Healy’s side maintain their one-point lead over Coleraine at the top.

Ruaidhrí Higgins’ Bannsiders battled to a 3-1 victory over Carrick Rangers at Taylor’s Avenue.

Will Patching put the visitors ahead from the penalty spot in the 31st minute with Danny Gibson leveliing for the hard-working home side three minutes before the break. But nine minutes into the second half Matthew Shevlin edged the Bannsiders ahead before completing a brace.

A Matty Lusty hat-trick inspired champions Larne to a 4-0 win over Ballymena United in the late-night game at Inver Park, the perfect tune-up for Tuesday’s tie-of-the-round Bet McLean Cup last-16 showdown at Glentoran.

Two first-half goals by Cahal McGinty put Dungannon Swifts in control against Crusaders at Stangmore Park, the home side eventually running out 3-1 winners. An Oli McCart own goal made it 3-0 before Kieran McKechnie grabbed a late consolation.

Late goals from Lewis Harrison and Jay Boyd helped Bangor to a crucial 2-0 success over basement side Portadown at Shamrock Park, with the second bottom Seasiders now six points ahead of their relegation rivals.

Bangor boss Lee Feeney felt his side were good value for the three points.

“We have worked tirelessly on the training ground and tried to educate each other and help each other and encourage each other to be the best version of ourselves,” he said.

“We came away from Larne disappointed we didn’t get something recently and I am disappointed we did not get anything against Glentoran last week.

“Our mentality has shifted. We are not accepting anything but three points. However, we are still down there.”

On Friday night, Cliftonville had a dramatic 3-2 success against Glentoran with visiting boss Duncan Ferguson sent from the touchline in the first half for overdoing the protests about what he felt had been a foul in the build-up to Cliftonville’s opening goal.
