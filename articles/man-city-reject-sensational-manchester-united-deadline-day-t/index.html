Man City reject sensational Manchester United deadline day transfer approach

TheTools · 01.09.2026 20:25 · source · permalink

Manchester United have enquired about a sensational deadline day deal for Manchester City full-back Rayan Ait-Nouri.

Michael Carrick's side made late contact with City over a possible move for the left back but the Algeria international will be staying at the Etihad.

United were offered the 25-year-old as an option at left-back by his agent a couple of days ago and made a call to the Etihad today to check on his situation.

While United were only willing to consider a straight for Ait-Nouri, City wanted a loan with an obligation to buy, which meant any shock deal between the cross-city rivals came to nothing.

Ait-Nouri joined City last summer in a £36million move from Wolves . He has made 31 appearances for City since arriving at the Etihad but injuries and the Africa Cup of Nations meant he didn't get a regular run in the side.

Nico O'Reilly established himself as City's first-choice left back and was outstanding, while Josko Gvardiol has started the opening two games of this season in that position with O'Reilly in midfield and Ait-Nouri on the bench.

United are seeking to strengthen at left back with Luke Shaw the only recognised senior option in that position. Carrick's side have been looking at short-term options at left-back since deciding they had no chance of signing top target Lewis Hall from Newcastle this summer.

They have ruled out a move for 19-year-old Jorge Salinas, despite being admirers of the Spanish youngster. He has a €16million release clause but there was no attempt to sign him on deadline day.

They have made enquiries over Barcelona left-back Alejandro Balde in recent days, but United were only interested in a loan deal and the Catalan giants were keen to sell the 22-year-old.
