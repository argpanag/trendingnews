Nepal win the toss and elect to bowl first against India A in their opening match of the ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026 in Doha. India A captain Tilak Varma says his side wants to bat first and post a competitive total, adding that the team is confident.

Nepal skipper Sandeep Lamichhane feels the pitch looks decent and backs his side to chase successfully. He also reveals that Nepal have only 12 players available due to the ongoing Nepal Premier League (NPL).

India A begin their ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026 campaign against Nepal in Doha, aiming to end a 13-year title drought after winning the inaugural edition in 2013. Led by Tilak Varma, the squad boasts exciting talents and established names such as Abhishek Sharma, Vaibhav Sooryavanshi and Rinku Singh, who serves as vice-captain.

Harshit Rana also returns from injury to strengthen the pace attack, while four spin options provide variety. Nepal, meanwhile, look to build on their promising T20 World Cup showing as they test their skills in the 50-over format.

India A: Abhishek Sharma, Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, Prabhsimran Singh [WK], Tilak Varma [C], Rinku Singh [VC], Kumar Kushagra [WK], Suryansh Shedge, Shahbaz Ahmed, Vipraj Nigam, Harsh Dubey, Tanush Kotian, Harshit Rana, Prince Yadav, Arshad Khan, Ayush Badoni.
