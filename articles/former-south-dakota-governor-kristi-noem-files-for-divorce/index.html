Former South Dakota Governor Kristi Noem files for divorce

TheTools · 29.09.2026 13:43 · source · permalink

CASTLEWOOD, S.D. (Dakota News Now) - Official documents have revealed that former South Dakota Governor and Secretary of Homeland Security Kristi Noem has filed for divorce from her husband Bryon Noem.

Court documents show that the divorce was filed on September 28, 2026, in Hamlin County, and that the divorce was filed on the grounds of irreconcilable differences.

Kristi and Bryon Noem have been married since 1992.

This is a developing story, and Dakota News Now will provide updates as more information becomes available.
