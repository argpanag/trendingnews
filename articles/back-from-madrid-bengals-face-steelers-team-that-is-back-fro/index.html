Back from Madrid, Bengals face Steelers team that is back from a bye

TheTools · 15.11.2026 23:49 · source · permalink

The Bengals host the Steelers on Sunday night in Week 10. Their Week 9 experiences couldn’t have been more different.

The Bengals played the Falcons in Madrid, Spain. The Steelers didn’t play anyone; they were on their bye.

The end result is that, for the sixth time ever, a team that played internationally will be facing a team that was on a bye. (One of those occasions involved the Bills playing in Toronto, so it’s not quite the same.)

The teams that were off the week before facing a team that played outside the U.S. have a record of 5-0 in those games.

There’s another bye-week fact that favors the Steelers. Pittsburgh has won nine games in a row following their bye week, since 2017. Via NBC Sports research, it’s the third-longest streak in NFL history, behind only the 1999-2011 Eagles (who won 13 in a row) and the 2015-present Bills (who have won 12 in a row).

Of course, those nine straight wins were accomplished by former coach Mike Tomlin. This is current coach Mike McCarthy’s first post-bye opportunity.

Having it come against a team that played across an ocean last Sunday can’t hurt.

It happens again next week; the Lions return from Germany to host a Bucs team that was off in Week 10.
