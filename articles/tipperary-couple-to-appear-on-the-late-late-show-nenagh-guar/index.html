Tipperary Couple to appear on the Late Late Show | Nenagh Guardian

TheTools · 23.10.2026 22:01 · source · permalink

RTÉ have revealed that Tipperary couple, Mikey Breen and Sharlene Mawdsley will be guests on the Late Late Show tomorrow evening.

The RTÉ Show takes place on RTÉ One and RTÉ Player at 9.35pm.

The pair will be chatting about competing at a national and international level. They will also be chatting about how they support each other as a couple and RTÉ has also hinted that they will be taking part in a fun sporting challenge as part of their appearance on the show.

The couple will be appearing alongside an line up of other guests such as Mayo actor, Phillipa Dunne, host of Ireland's fittest family, Laura Fox and their will be a musical performance by traditional Irish music group, Beoga.

A man known as The King of Chemo, Iain Ward, will be at the show to discuss a life changing diagnosis which led to him raise money for cancer research.

Investigatons Editor with The Sunday World, Nicola Tallant, will also be in studio to talk about the rise of crime in Ireland.

Niall Breslin will be performing a 'very different' performance to what people may have previously seen before.

Beoga will be performing music on the night.
