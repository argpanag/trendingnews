Confirmed line-ups: Spurs vs Crystal Palace

TheTools · 31.10.2026 18:27 · source · permalink
  • Spurs
  • Tottenham Hotspur Stadium

We can now confirm the starting line-up as we take on Crystal Palace at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium in the Premier League this evening (5.30pm).

Roberto De Zerbi has made one change to the side that started our win over Chelsea last Saturday, with James Maddison beginning this game amongst our substitutes with Dominic Solanke coming into the starting XI.

De Zerbi on when Tosin, Bergvall, Mudryk and Savio could return

Starting XI: Kinsky, Robertson, van Hecke, Tel, Gray, Tonali, M. Fernandes, Solanke, Kudus, Marmoush, van de Ven (c).

Substitutes: Dubravka, Senesi, Gallagher, Maddison, Udogie, Kulusevski, Pedro Porro, Davies, Williams-Barnett.

Here's how our visitors line up...

Starting XI: Henderson, Mitchell, Disasi, Canvot, Pino, McNeil, Mateta, Kamada, Wharton, Richards, Mingueza.

Substitutes: Benitez, Lerma, Nketiah, Hughes, Osorio, Khalaili, Doucoure, Guessand, Chilwell.
