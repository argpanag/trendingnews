Live Nation-commissioned research suggests live music perks boost loyalty program appeal

TheTools · 14.09.2026 23:48 · source · permalink

Live Nation has released research which it says indicates that 91 percent of local live music fans would consider joining loyalty programs for access to music-related perks.

The announcement:

Across Australia, you can tell when a big concert is coming. Friends compare setlist predictions. Merch lines wrap around venues. Restaurants and bars fill before the show. Then the lights go down, and everyone in the room shares an experience that can only happen live. Those experiences are taking on new value as more of life moves online.

Released today, Live Nation’s Global Loyalty Study 2026, The Return to Real report highlights a major opportunity for brands as Australians head into the busy summer festival and concert season. Drawing on surveys of tens of thousands of fans worldwide, the research reveals a growing desire for real experiences, with live music emerging as the world’s favourite form of entertainment.

While 65 percent of Australian fans are engaging more with loyalty programs than they did a year ago, 67 percent feel less loyal to any single brand, and 70 percent believe most programs feel the same. Retail currently leads loyalty program engagement in Australia, with 64% of Australians belonging to a retail loyalty program, followed by food delivery (53%) and travel (49%).

The study uncovers that live music provides a clear point of difference: more than nine in 10 Australian fans would sign up for or switch to a new brand’s program for live music perks, and 82 percent prefer exclusive experiences over discounts. Additionally, 66 percent would rather redeem loyalty rewards on live music than discounts on a brand’s own products and services, shifting loyalty programs from transactional to experiential.

Among the Australian population, live music ranks as the most appealing entertainment loyalty reward, with 81 percent interested in brands offering live music perks. This places live music ahead of cinema at 78 percent, theme parks at 64 percent, and comedy and sporting events at 63 percent. Two thirds of respondents said they would feel more positively about a brand offering live music rewards, compared with 62 percent for professional sports rewards.

“Australians are looking for more than points or discounts. They want access to experiences that genuinely mean something to them,” said Kristy Rosser, Senior Vice President, Media & Sponsorship Australia & New Zealand at Live Nation. “Live music gives brands a powerful opportunity to add real value by becoming part of moments customers care about and remember. When a loyalty program provides access to an artist or experience they love, it creates a meaningful reason to choose that brand over another.”

Brands across Australia are already finding ways to turn access to live music into meaningful customer experiences.

Live Nation recently announced a three-year strategic partnership with DoorDash across its portfolio of concerts, festivals and venues in Australia and New Zealand. Customers will have access to live music perks, while fans leaving shows will receive exclusive offers and post-event promotions. The partnership will also debut the DoorDash Food Village at major festivals.

This collaboration demonstrates how brands can leverage access to experiences customers genuinely value to strengthen their loyalty offering and create a meaningful point of difference.

Another strong example is Live Nation’s global partnership with Mastercard which demonstrates how exclusive access is becoming a new form of loyalty. “As people place greater value on real-world experiences, access has become one of the most powerful forms of loyalty,” says Monica Biagiotti, EVP, Global Consumer Marketing and Sponsorships. “Whether it’s travelling to experience a bucket-list concert, discovering a new destination through music, or sharing an unforgettable night with friends and family, these experiences create lasting memories. Through the Priceless Platform and our partnership with Live Nation, we’re making those moments more accessible for millions of cardholders around the world.”

Across industries, brands are finding new ways to make live experiences part of how they build customer relationships. The Return to Real explores what fans across Australia and around the world value about experiences they can see, feel and share for themselves, and what that shift means for brands.
