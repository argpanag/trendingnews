TL;DR

  • Apple's iPhone Duo opens for preorders on October 16 and ships October 23, starting at $1,999 for 256GB.
  • OnePlus 16 launches in China on October 12 with Qualcomm's fastest chip, a 185Hz screen, and a battery near 9,000mAh.
  • Oppo's Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max go global on October 21, with India following on October 28.
  • Vivo's X Fold 6 reaches global and Indian buyers in early October, but global models top out at 12GB of RAM.
  • Vivo's V80 lands in India on October 6 with a 7,200mAh battery and a 50MP 3x telephoto camera.

October is shaping up to be the busiest month for new phones this year, with Apple’s first foldable and a wave of Chinese flagships landing within the same three weeks. If you’re planning to buy a phone before the holidays, the timing matters: prices on current models usually drop when new ones arrive, and some of these launches won’t reach every country. Here are the five launches that matter most, ranked by how worthwhile they are to wait for, plus a few smaller phones worth knowing about.

October 2026 Phone Launch Calendar

Prices marked “expected” or “tipped” come from leaks, not the brands themselves.

One theme jumps out: every Android flagship on this list ships with a battery of 7,000mAh or more, and most have a 200MP main camera. Batteries are measured in mAh (milliamp-hours), and for reference, many current iPhones and Galaxy phones still sit around 4,000 to 5,000mAh. Chinese brands have moved to silicon-carbon cells, which store more energy in the same space, and that’s why the numbers keep climbing.

1. iPhone Duo: Worth Waiting For, If You Can Afford It

Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8

Available on Amazon

A next-gen foldable design with a spacious display, powerful performance, and dual 50MP cameras for work and entertainment.

Preorders: October 16 | Release: October 23 (US and 70+ countries), October 30 in 28 more regions

Apple’s first foldable is the biggest phone launch of the month, and maybe the year. The iPhone Duo preorder schedule confirms a 5.4-inch outer screen and a 7.6-inch inner screen, and Apple calls it the thinnest iPhone ever when unfolded.

If you’re already in Apple’s world and want a foldable, there’s no reason to buy anything else right now. Apple tends to support its phones with updates for many years, and the iPhone Duo will likely hold its resale value better than any Android foldable.

But $1,999 is a lot, and first-generation devices often have quirks that get fixed in year two. A closer look at the iPhone Duo’s price and specs explains why Apple’s late entry still reshapes the market.

2. OnePlus 16: Worth Waiting For, If You Can Get It

OnePlus 15

Available on Amazon

OnePlus 15 packs a 6.78-inch AMOLED display, Snapdragon chip, 50MP camera, and 7,000mAh battery.

Launch: October 12 (China)

The OnePlus 16 is the most aggressive spec sheet of the month. OnePlus has confirmed the October 12 launch and several key features:

  • Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6, Qualcomm’s fastest phone chip, built on a 2nm process
  • A 185Hz display with native 185fps (frames per second) gaming in select titles, plus 165Hz across the whole system
  • A 200MP main camera and a 50MP periscope telephoto (a zoom lens that uses a mirror to fit a longer lens sideways in the phone)

Leaks add a battery of about 9,000mAh, 100W wired and 90W wireless charging, and up to 16GB of RAM with 1TB of storage. Tipped China prices run from $745 (CNY 4,999) for an 8GB model to $1,118 (CNY 7,499) at the top.

On paper, it’s the best value flagship of October. The problem is availability. OnePlus has been pulling back from Western markets, and the OnePlus 16 leaks suggest US and European buyers may never get it officially. If you’re in China or India, it’s worth the wait. Elsewhere, you’ll likely be importing it, which means no local warranty and possible network band issues.

3. Oppo Find X10: Worth Waiting For Camera Fans

OPPO Find X9 Ultra 512GB

Available on Amazon

Find X9 Ultra 512GB sports a 6.82-inch AMOLED display, Snapdragon chip, 200MP camera, and 7,000mAh battery.

Launch: October 21 (global), October 28 (India)

Oppo launched the Find X10 series in China in September, and the global rollout covers the UK, Europe, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and India. Two models are coming:

In China, the Pro Max starts at $1,013 (CNY 6,799). Global pricing hasn’t been announced, but early UK preorder offers reportedly include £150 to £200 off plus free accessories.

The Pro Max is the camera phone to beat this fall. Three 200MP cameras sound like overkill, but having high resolution on the zoom lenses means sharper zoomed shots, which is where most phones fall apart. If you shoot a lot of photos and live in a supported market, it’s worth waiting three weeks. A head-to-head of the Find X10 Pro Max against the Galaxy S26 Ultra shows why the price gap isn’t as simple as it looks.

4. Vivo X Fold 6: Wait Only If You Want Android’s Best-Value Foldable

Launch: Early October (global), October 6 (India)

The Vivo X Fold 6 launched in China in June and is now heading abroad. It’s built to undercut the iPhone Duo and Samsung’s foldables on price while beating them on battery:

  • 8.02-inch inner screen and 6.51-inch cover screen
  • MediaTek Dimensity 9500 Super chip (3nm)
  • 200MP main camera, 50MP periscope telephoto, 50MP ultrawide
  • 7,000mAh battery with 80W wired and 40W wireless charging
  • IP58 and IP59 water and dust resistance (ratings that mean it can survive water exposure and high-pressure sprays)

In China, it starts at about $1,192 (CNY 7,999). Indian pricing is expected to be between $1,353 and $1,665.

It’s a strong foldable for about $600 to $800 less than the iPhone Duo. The catch: global models max out at 12GB of RAM, while Chinese buyers can get 16GB, as the X Fold 6 launch details show. That’s unlikely to matter for most people today, but it could make the phone feel slower sooner as apps get heavier. If you want a foldable and don’t need iOS, this is the one to wait for.

5. Vivo V80: Skip the Wait Unless Battery Is Your Top Priority

VIVO X300 Ultra 5G

Available on Amazon

Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 power, a 200MP ZEISS camera, 144Hz 2K AMOLED display, and 100W fast charging for flagship performance.

Launch: October 6 (India)

The Vivo V80 is the most “normal” phone on this list, and the one more people will actually buy. Confirmed and expected specs:

  • 6.59-inch 1.5K OLED screen, 144Hz, 5,000 nits peak brightness
  • 7,200mAh battery with 90W charging (Vivo says 50% in 30 minutes)
  • 50MP main camera with OIS (optical image stabilization, which reduces blur from shaky hands) and a 50MP 3x telephoto
  • 50MP selfie camera
  • OriginOS 7 based on Android 17
  • Expected Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chip

The expected price is around $624 (₹60,000), though Vivo hasn’t confirmed it.

A 3x telephoto and 7,200mAh battery are great at this price, but they aren’t unique anymore. If your current phone is struggling, you don’t need to hold out specifically for the V80; plenty of strong mid-rangers are already on sale. The V80’s bet on a 7,200mAh battery and 30-plus AI camera tricks is clear, but the final price will decide whether it beats current rivals.

Smaller October Launches Worth Knowing About

A few more phones are arriving this month, mostly in India and China:

  • Oppo F35 and F35 Pro (October 5, India): 8,000mAh batteries, 80W charging, a 50MP ultrawide selfie camera, and strong water-resistance ratings. Expected prices range from about $364 to $520 (₹35,000–₹50,000).
  • Vivo S2 FE (October 6, India): a 10,000mAh battery for about $416 (₹39,999).
  • RedMagic 12 Pro+ (October 15, China): a gaming phone with the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 and an 8,400mAh battery.
  • Pixel 11 Pro Fold: Google’s foldable reaches stores in October, which gives Android fans a third foldable choice this month.

If you want a foldable, October is the best month in years to shop: the iPhone Duo for Apple fans, the Vivo X Fold 6 for value, and the Pixel 11 Pro Fold for Google’s software. If you want a regular flagship, the Oppo Find X10 Pro Max is the camera pick, and the OnePlus 16 is the performance pick, as long as it’s sold where you live.

And if you don’t need anything this flashy, the smartest move might be to wait until these launches land. Older flagships like the Galaxy S26 Ultra and last year’s foldables tend to get their biggest discounts right when new models arrive.

FAQs

Is the Xiaomi 18 Pro launching globally in October? 

Xiaomi has confirmed a global version, but not an exact date. Chinese Xiaomi flagships usually reach other markets a few months after their China debut, often with slightly smaller batteries.

Are there cheaper alternatives to the iPhone Duo? 

Yes. Several Android foldables cost $500 to $1,000 less, including models from Vivo, Honor, and Samsung’s older Fold generations. 

Why do Chinese phones have bigger batteries than iPhones and Galaxy phones? 

Chinese brands adopted silicon-carbon battery cells early, which store more energy in the same space. Apple and Samsung have been more cautious, partly over long-term durability and shipping rules for large batteries. That gap is now as big as 4,000mAh between some flagships.

Should I buy a phone during October sales or wait for Black Friday? 

In India, October’s festive sales often beat Black Friday prices. In the US, Black Friday in late November usually brings bigger discounts on current flagships. 

Which Snapdragon chip is in October’s flagships? 

Most top Android phones this fall use Qualcomm’s new Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 or the faster Extreme version, both built on 2nm processes.
