Bella Hadid’s Curated Revolve Capsule Makes Her Supermodel Style Easy to Re-Create for Under $150 

TheTools · 18.10.2026 19:00 · source · permalink

The supermodel's Revolve edit spans hundreds of winter-ready layers, from suede and knitwear to matching sets and leather accesories. These are the under-$150 finds we'd wear ASAP through EOY. 

Bella Hadid has again teamed up with Revolve on a curated capsule that is perfect for the fall and winter seasons. 

The Victoria’s Secret model’s closet edit is a sprawling mix of brands and styles, with plenty of pieces at luxury prices. But you don’t have to splurge to find something worth adding to your wardrobe. We combed through Bella’s Closet Picks for stylish finds under $150 that feel polished, on-trend and easy to wear—and we want these outfits ASAP.

  • Bella's Blazer: All The Ways Trina Collarless Blazer Jacket
  • Ultimate Winter Sweater: SNDYS x Revolve Winnie Jumper
  • Fall's Handbag Trend: JW PEI Audrey Flap Lock Top Handle Bag

The 300-piece edit starts at $34 for the model's favorite stockings, Stretch Control Tights by Stems, and runs upward of $1,500 for a pair of genuine suede pants. Luckily, there are plenty of covetable pieces in between and near the lower end that are the exact fall and winter trends we're shopping for, obsessively. 

From cozy knitwear to sexy leather and suede styles, we found the top finds under $150, including knitwear, denim, handbags, and more, offering an accessible entry point to incorporate some supermodel-approved pieces into your rotation for less.

The juicy assortment includes some of the season's leading fashion trends. Matching suede and leather sets are the high-fashion trend that shoppers already love for the ease of a coordinated outfit. 

Shop office-friendly pieces that can refresh a whole work wardrobe, like strong tailored blazers, structured tops, and turtlenecks that pair effortlessly with work slacks and loafers. 

Hadid's edit thoughtfully covers outwear with a slew of trendy styles, like a faux-leather trench coat and faux-suede trucker jacket. As for accessories, winter's biggest bag trends—leather, studded hardware, top-handle silhouettes—are also up for grabs. 

Ready to refresh your wardrobe? Shop the Revolve picks above to find your favorite Bella-inspired layers, accessories and matching pieces that won’t break the $150 mark.
