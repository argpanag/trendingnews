Verstappen Valora la Calma bajo el Liderazgo de Mekies

TheTools · 08.10.2026 03:03 · source · permalink
  • F1
  • _Home

Max Verstappen recently shared that he does not miss the tension that was experienced with Christian Horner at the helm of the Red Bull team in Formula 1. In recent statements, the four-time champion emphasized how the current situation is perceived as less stressful with the arrival of Laurent Mekies as the new team director.

Internal conflicts within teams are common and often create an interesting narrative for fans. However, from the pilot’s point of view, this tension can complicate the dynamic of a professional environment solely dedicated to achieving victories.

“I prefer to see people getting along,” Verstappen commented, referring to the team’s current dynamic. “It’s not pleasant to live constantly with tensions between people.” This change of atmosphere seems to benefit teamwork, and the Dutch driver has expressed satisfaction with the atmosphere:

“It’s not as exciting, but I like how things are going currently, with everyone’s behavior.” With one victory already achieved this season, Verstappen enjoys his time in Milton Keynes.

Moreover, the current situation allows the team to approach its responsibilities with more calmness. “It’s less stressful. We all lead stressful lives and, if we can reduce a bit of stress, it’s a good thing,” added the Red Bull driver.

After a controversial period with Horner, who left the team last year, Mekies took over the roles of team director and CEO leveraging his previous experience with the sister team, Racing Bulls.

Despite the changes, Verstappen has kept the door open for a possible return of Horner to Formula 1, although it is not a topic that currently concerns him. He said that if Horner wishes to return, he will likely find an opportunity if it arises.

For Verstappen, the world of Formula 1 can maintain its drama; he prefers a calmer and more professional environment when competing.
