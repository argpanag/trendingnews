Ballon d’Or 2026: Kane, Yamal, Mbappe battle for football’s biggest individual award – Businessday NG

TheTools · 20.10.2026 14:53 · source · permalink

Harry Kane, Lamine Yamal and Kylian Mbappe are among the leading contenders for the 2026 Ballon d’Or, football’s biggest individual

Harry Kane, Lamine Yamal and Kylian Mbappe are among the leading contenders for the 2026 Ballon d’Or, football’s biggest individual award, to be held in London on Monday, October 26.

The ceremony is organised by France Football in partnership with UEFA and is scheduled to take place at the London Palladium, marking a departure from its traditional Paris setting.

Bayern Munich striker Kane is emerging as a strong contender following a prolific campaign, while Barcelona forward Yamal and Real Madrid star Mbappe are expected to feature prominently in the race to succeed reigning men’s winner Ousmane Dembele.

Kane leads the race

Kane has strengthened his claim through his goalscoring performances for Bayern Munich and England. His individual output and contributions to team success have placed the England captain among the leading candidates for his first Ballon d’Or.

The Bayern forward’s combination of goalscoring consistency, attacking influence and silverware could prove decisive when the international jury votes for the winner.

Kane’s challenge, however, comes against a field of elite players whose performances across the 2025/26 season have also strengthened their credentials.

Yamal makes strong case

Barcelona’s teenage forward Yamal remains a major contender after another impressive campaign for club and country.

His creativity, technical ability and influence in attacking positions have made him one of the most prominent young players in world football. His performances for Barcelona and Spain could strengthen his case for the prestigious award.

Yamal’s challenge will depend on how voters weigh his individual impact against Kane’s goalscoring numbers and the achievements of other leading candidates.

Mbappe remains in contention

Real Madrid forward Mbappe is another leading name in the race after maintaining his reputation as one of the world’s most prolific attackers.

The France international’s goalscoring record and attacking influence make him a serious contender, although competition from Kane, Yamal and other nominees could make the outcome difficult to predict.

Lionel Messi also remains a prominent name in discussions around the award following his performances for Inter Miami and Argentina. Manchester City forward Erling Haaland and Real Madrid midfielder Jude Bellingham are among the other high-profile players associated with the race.

London hosts landmark ceremony

The October 26 ceremony will mark the 70th anniversary of the Ballon d’Or, first awarded in 1956 to England forward Stanley Matthews.

The event will be co-hosted by French football legend Thierry Henry and British broadcaster Kate Scott.

Kane, Yamal and Mbappe headline the conversation ahead of the ceremony, but the final result will depend on the international jury’s assessment of individual performances, achievements and influence during the qualifying period.

The winner of the men’s award will succeed Paris Saint-Germain forward Dembélé, who claimed the 2025 Ballon d’Or. Barcelona midfielder Aitana Bonmatí won the women’s award last year.
