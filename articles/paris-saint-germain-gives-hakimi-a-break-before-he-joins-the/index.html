Paris Saint-Germain gives Hakimi a break before he joins the Atlas Lions

TheTools · 07.11.2026 20:47 · source · permalink

Paris Saint-Germain announced on Friday that Morocco international Achraf Hakimi will miss Saturday’s Ligue 1 match against Troyes, after the club decided to grant him a period of rest before he joins the Morocco national team camp, which is set to begin next Monday.

In a statement, the club said that “in agreement with the coach and the sporting management, Achraf Hakimi will be given a rest period until the start of the international break,” as part of efforts to prevent fatigue caused by a congested run of fixtures.

The decision comes after Hakimi’s standout performances in PSG’s recent matches, most notably his hat-trick against Le Havre last week.

Maghnes Akliouche will also miss the Troyes match due to a calf injury sustained during Tuesday’s 6-0 win over Villarreal in the Champions League.

Morocco is scheduled to play two matches against Niger on November 13 and 17, as part of the third and fourth rounds of qualifying for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations.
