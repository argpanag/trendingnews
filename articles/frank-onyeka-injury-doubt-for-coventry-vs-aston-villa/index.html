Frank Onyeka injury doubt for Coventry vs Aston Villa

TheTools · 15.09.2026 15:21 · source · permalink

Coventry City could be without Frank Onyeka when they face Aston Villa in the Carabao Cup on Wednesday night.

The midfielder is considered a doubt after suffering an injury during Coventry’s 5-0 Premier League defeat to Brighton on Sunday.

Daily Post reports that Onyeka will be assessed ahead of the third-round tie, leaving Frank Lampard facing another potential absence before Villa arrive at the Coventry Building Society Arena.

Onyeka started against Brighton but was replaced in the 73rd minute.

Frank Onyeka injury adds to Coventry problems

The Nigeria international had been one of Coventry’s more threatening performers before his withdrawal.

Sky Sports showed Onyeka forcing Bart Verbruggen into an excellent save with a powerful volley as Coventry enjoyed their strongest spell of the game.

His potential absence would create another selection headache for Lampard.

Taiwo Awoniyi is already suspended after being shown a straight red card against Brighton, while Coventry entered that fixture with several other injury concerns.

Onyeka missing out could also give Villa a better opportunity to control central areas.

João Gomes is available again after serving his domestic suspension, giving Unai Emery another option in midfield.

Villa still cannot afford to assume Wednesday will be straightforward.

Coventry created chances before Brighton took control, but losing Onyeka would remove another important presence from a team already dealing with suspension and injury problems.
