Traffic chaos as Luas ground to a halt for second time in a week

TheTools · 01.10.2026 06:32 · source · permalink

Commuters across Dublin have been hit with fresh travel chaos after a power failure brought the entire Luas Red Line to a standstill.

No trams are running on the busy east-west line between Tallaght and Saggart and the city centre, Heuston Station, Connolly and The Point.

The disruption began earlier this morning, when services were suspended between Blackhorse and Connolly-Point.

That has since been extended to the full length of the Red Line, leaving no trams operating. It is not yet known when services will be restored.

Luas confirmed the outage in a post on social media, telling passengers that their tickets are valid on Dublin Bus for the duration of the disruption and that Leap Cards can be used across all Transport for Ireland (TFI) services. The operator apologised for any inconvenience caused.

With no trams running, commuters and shoppers travelling through the affected area should allow extra time for their journeys and expect busier than usual buses as displaced Luas passengers switch over.

It is the second power-related failure to hit the Luas in little over a week. Last Thursday, a power fault forced the suspension of Green Line services through the city centre, between St Stephen’s Green and Dominick Street, during the morning rush hour, affecting thousands of commuters.

Passengers are being urged to check the TFI website and app for the latest before setting off, with the situation likely to change through the day.
