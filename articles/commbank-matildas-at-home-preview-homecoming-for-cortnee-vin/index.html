CommBank Matildas at Home Preview: Homecoming for Cortnee Vine as City take on Sydney

TheTools · 23.10.2026 14:41 · source · permalink

3 min read 23 October 2026

Cortnee Vine will face the club where she made her name, Sydney FC, in new colours this weekend as Melbourne City travel to take on one of their rivals.

Read how you can catch our CommBank Matildas across the A-League Women in action below.

Western Sydney Wanderers v Brisbane Roar

Date: Saturday, 24 October
Venue: CommBank Stadium
Kickoff: 2.15pm AEDT
Broadcast: 10Play and Paramount+
Buy tickets

The Western Sydney Wanderers host the Brisbane Roar with both sides looking for their first points of the season.

Bright shots abounded in round one for both sides. The Roar managed to score twice, with CommBank Young Matilda Daisy Brown and new signing Katrina Guillou impressing. Meanwhile, the Wanderers' youngsters - such as CommBank Young Matildas Talia Younis and Nikita Fazzari - made positive starts.

Chloe Lincoln is expected to line up in goals for the Roar and will hope to keep her first clean sheet of the campaign.

Central Coast Mariners v Canberra United

Date: Saturday, 24 October
Venue: Polytec Stadium
Kickoff: 3pm AEDT
Broadcast: 10Play and Paramount+
Buy tickets

The Central Coast Mariners will be high on confidence as they host a Canberra United side playing their first game of the season.

The Mariners are full of bright young talent, but perhaps will be concerned with the goals they conceded last week. If Morgan Aquino can begin to show the form that she did the last time she was in the A-League Women, that will go some way to helping them to the clean sheets they need.

Canberra will once again be spearheaded by stalwart Michelle Heyman, who continues to set goal-scoring records. It will be fascinating to see how they perform after having the bye in week one.

Adelaide United v Perth Glory

Date: Saturday, 24 October
Venue: ServiceFM Stadium
Kickoff: 5pm AEDT
Broadcast: 10Play and Paramount+
Buy tickets

Perth Glory are once again on the road in week two, this time travelling to Adelaide to take on the Reds on Saturday.

They got what they needed from round one, picking up an impressive three points against the Newcastle Jets. Head Coach Stephen Peters said that he has already seen growth within his group and they'll hope they can show all of that on Saturday.

Meanwhile, Adelaide were a mixed bag in their draw against Sydney FC, but will hope to tighten up and walk away with the points. Ilona Melegh will be in goals for the home side.

Wellington Phoenix v Melbourne Victory

Date: Sunday, 25 October
Venue: Hnry Stadium
Kickoff: 10.15am AEDT
Broadcast: 10Play and Paramount+
Buy tickets

After being unlucky to lose against defending Champions Melbourne City, the Phoenix will have a chance for revenge as the other team from Melbourne visits Wellington on Sunday.

Tameka Yallop will hope to spearhead the team's efforts to earn three points in front of their home fans. Courtney Newbon will be between the sticks at the other end with the intent to prevent that outcome.

Sydney FC v Melbourne City

Date: Sunday, 25 October
Venue: Allianz Stadium
Kickoff: 5.45pm AEDT
Broadcast: 10Play and Paramount+
Buy tickets

One of the great rivalries of the A-League Women reignites this weekend, with the added bonus of Cortnee Vine taking on her old club for the first time.

Sydney FC was the team in which Vine made her name, and her form for the Sky Blues was the reason she was selected in the FIFA Women's World Cup 2023 squad. Now, for City, she'll be hoping to help the side improve after last week's performance as they look to defend their Premiership and Championship double from last season. Jessika Nash will also be available for City.

With both sides missing players to injury and CommBank Junior Matildas duties, it will be a fascinating contest that will give insight into both sides this season.
