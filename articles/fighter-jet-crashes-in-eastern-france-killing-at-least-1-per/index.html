Fighter jet crashes in eastern France, killing at least 1 person

TheTools · 21.10.2026 09:21 · source · permalink

The French armed forces say that a fighter jet has crashed in eastern France, killing at least one person

PARIS -- A fighter jet crashed in eastern France, leaving at least one person dead, the French armed forces said on Wednesday.

The Mirage 2000D combat aircraft was returning from a mission when it crashed late Tuesday evening, according to the armed forces ministry.

The plane's crew consisted of a fighter pilot and a weapons systems officer from the French Air and Space Force. One body has been recovered and a search for the second crew member is ongoing.
