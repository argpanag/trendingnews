NC AG sues Dollar General, accuses store of overcharging customers

TheTools · 27.10.2026 16:48 · source · permalink
  • Related
  • Dollar General
  • money
  • Federal Trade Commission
  • lawsuit

North Carolina Attorney General Jeff Jackson has filed a lawsuit against Dollar General for illegally overcharging customers.

On Tuesday morning, Jackson said the company is raising prices for many of their products without updating the sticker price on shelves.

Other WRAL Top Stories

“We’re suing Dollar General because they’re ripping people off, and they know it," Jackson said. "They’ve failed state inspections and been told over and over to fix these mistakes. They haven’t, so we’ll see them in court."

North Carolina has over 1,000 Dollar General locations across the state, with at least one in every town. Jackson said inspectors found 400 pricing inaccuracies at locations       

"Over a million North Carolinians shop at Dollar General every week, and they are ripping people off," Jackson said.

This comes after the Federal Trade Commission and 19 others states filed the lawsuit against Dollar General for "long-running deceptive pricing practices." 

Jackson said the store is aware of the issue, and it's had multiple opportunities to fix it. He said they have failed thousands of state pricing inspections and have received nearly 30,000 complaints about overcharging nationwide.  Just last week, the North Carolina Department of Agriculture fined 10 Dollar General locations for overcharging customers beyond the listed price.  

What shoppers can do:

  • Check receipts before leaving the store
    • Ask store to correct the price if charged more
      • File complaint with N.C. Department of Agriculture Consumer Services' Standards Division by filling out the online complaint form or call 984-236-4751.

              The FTC and states allege these practices violate the FTC Act, the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act and state consumer protection laws.
