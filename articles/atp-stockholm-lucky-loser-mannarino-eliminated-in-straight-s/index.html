ATP Stockholm: Lucky loser Mannarino eliminated in straight sets by Shevchenko

TheTools · 10.11.2026 13:25 · source · permalink

Handed a last-minute lucky-loser spot after Luca Van Assche's withdrawal, Adrian Mannarino could not turn things around against Alexander Shevchenko. The Frenchman lost in straight sets, 6-1, 6-3, in the first round of the ATP 250 in Stockholm.

Beaten in the final round of qualifying by Toby Samuel, Adrian Mannarino was given a second chance at the ATP 250 in Stockholm.

A lucky loser following Luca Van Assche's withdrawal, the Frenchman faced Alexander Shevchenko on Tuesday in the first round.

A match in which Mannarino was never in it

Unfortunately for him, he was no match for the Kazakh. Broken immediately, Mannarino could not find a solution and lost the first set 6-1.

Quickly trailing 4-0 in the second set, he did manage to break his opponent twice. But it was not enough, and he fell 6-1, 6-3.

As for Shevchenko, he will face Casper Ruud in the next round.
