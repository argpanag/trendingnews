1 person pronounced dead following multi-vehicle crash in Vaughan

TheTools · 21.09.2026 02:01 · source · permalink

One person has died following a serious crash in Vaughan on Sunday night, paramedics say.

The crash happened near Jane Street and Brandon Gate Drive, south of Teston Road.

York Regional Paramedic Services said they were called to that area just before 7:45 p.m. for reports of a multi-vehicle collision.

They say five people were assessed on scene.

Two patients were transported to a local hospital, while two others were taken to a trauma centre.

A fifth person was pronounced dead on scene.
