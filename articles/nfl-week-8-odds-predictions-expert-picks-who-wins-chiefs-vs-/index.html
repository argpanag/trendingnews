NFL Week 8 odds, predictions, expert picks: Who wins Chiefs vs. Broncos showdown?

TheTools · 01.11.2026 21:02 · source · permalink

Here's what our experts and the SportsLine model are forecasting in Week 8

Sunday will deliver perhaps the best quarterback showdown of the 2026 NFL season thus far. Lamar Jackson and Josh Allen are scheduled to duel in a marquee matchup between the Baltimore Ravens and Buffalo Bills. It is a massive spot for a Ravens team that just suffered back-to-back losses in its quarterback's absence and one that could prove whether or not Jesse Minter's squad is a legitimate playoff contender.

Both teams won in primetime last week with their Sunday night and Thursday night triumphs, respectively. Only one of them can add another victory when the nation's eyes are trained directly on them on "Monday Night Football."
