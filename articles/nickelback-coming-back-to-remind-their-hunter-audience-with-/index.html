Nickelback coming back to remind their Hunter audience with rock hits

TheTools · 09.10.2026 13:01 · source · permalink

Your digital subscription includes access to content from all our websites in your region. Access unlimited content and the digital versions of our print editions - Today's Paper.

After more than a decade, Canadian rock band Nickelback are heading back to the Hunter.

The How You Remind Me and Burn It To The Ground hitmakers will perform at Pokolbin's Roche Estate on October 16 on the final date of their Everything Under The Sun Tour.

Nickelback have also announced shows at RAC Arena, Perth (October 2); AEC Arena, Adelaide (October 5); Rod Laver Arena, Melbourne (October 7); Mount Duneed Estate, Geelong (October 9); Brisbane Entertainment Centre (October 12) and Afterpay Arena, Sydney (October 14).

English four-piece Don Broco are supporting Nickelback throughout the tour.

The Grammy Award-winning band are due to release their 11th studio album, Everything Under The Sun, on October 30.

It will feature the band's latest singles Make Me Love You, Leave Me Behind, Rattle The Cage and Bones For The Crows.

Despite often being one of the most-maligned rock bands by music fans and critics, Nickelback boast a massive global audience.

The Chad Kroeger-fronted band have sold nearly 65 million albums worldwide and command a monthly Spotify audience of 29 million listeners.

Nickelback last played at the Newcastle Entertainment Centre in May 2015 on the No Fixed Address Tour and also visited the city in 2002 and 2004.

Presale tickets are available from 10am on Tuesday, before the general sale begins at 10am on Friday.
