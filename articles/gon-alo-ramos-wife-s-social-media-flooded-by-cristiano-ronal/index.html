Gonçalo Ramos’ wife’s social media flooded by Cristiano Ronaldo fans

A family post by Margarida Domingues, wife of Gonçalo Ramos, has been swamped by Cristiano Ronaldo supporters on Instagram.

According to Glorioso 1904, the upload drew more than 3,000 comments within two days.

OneFootball Videos

She shared several photos and a short video with Ramos and their son, Bernardo, who is just over one year old. Thousands used the comments to send messages of support to Portugal’s captain.

The surge followed Ronaldo leaving the national team camp in Denmark on 30 October after a disagreement with Jorge Jesus. The episode has fuelled fierce debate among Portuguese fans. Domingues’ recent posts had usually drawn fewer than 100 comments, so the scale surprised.

On the pitch, Ramos has responded with goals and strong displays for Portugal. The Milan striker scored twice and supplied one assist in the first three Nations League matches, establishing himself as a starter. That trend should continue in the next fixture, against Norway at Estádio do Dragão.
