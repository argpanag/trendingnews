Every Tease That’s Surfaced Ahead of Stranger Things Day

The build-up to today has been, shall we say, eventful. Here’s every tease, cryptic post and “clue” that’s done the rounds in the last few days and weeks:

  • Ross Duffer knows something you don’t. 
    The co-creator posted a meme to his Instagram Stories using stills from Nathan Fielder’s You Can See Everything, with the top panel reading “I know what’s coming on Stranger Things Day” and the bottom panel replying “No you don’t.” Cheeky.
  • Finn Wolfhard and Caleb McLaughlin, together again. 
    Earlier this week, the Stranger Things Updates account on X shared a 20-second clip of the pair walking side-by-side down a street before stopping to notice something ahead of them. Cue another round of “is this a secret episode?” chatter. Context on the clip is thin, so we’d treat it with caution.
  • “FLIP THE TAPE.”
     Last month, a verified Stranger Things brand account posted an Instagram Story of Eleven flipped upside down with “FLIP THE TAPE” emblazoned across it. Some read it as a Side B hint; others pointed out that cassettes simply have two sides or going back to the beginning… This has also been used by many socials since. 
  • Matthew Modine being Matthew Modine. 
    Dr. Brenner himself posted another cryptic message on Instagram on October 18th, because of course he did.
  • Fan-made “It begins again” teasers. 
    A number of fan accounts have spent months posting “November 6th, 2026” countdown teasers and posters. Several have since been tagged by X’s Community Notes as fan-made, not official Netflix material.

Where Does Stranger Things Go From Here?

Today arrives at an interesting moment for the franchise. The picture looks like this:

  • The Duffer Brothers have moved to Paramount. 
    Their exclusive film and TV deal kicked in back in April 2026. They remain executive producers on remaining Stranger Things projects, but their day-to-day involvement at Netflix is limited.
  • The live-action spin-off is a long way off. 
    The Duffers have described it as “1000% different” from the flagship series, with new characters and a new showrunner, and have said they won’t rush it. Sources previously indicated a prequel was an early priority, but there’s been no meaningful update since.
  • Stranger Things: Tokyo has gone quiet. 
    The reported anime spin-off about twin brothers in 1980s Tokyo hasn’t been heard from in years.
  • The First Shadow is closing, but the proshot isn’t going anywhere.
    The three-time Tony winner takes its final bows in December and January, with Netflix’s filmed version still awaiting a release date.

Our thinking then was that the franchise may be entering a quieter period. Today should tell us a lot about whether Netflix is going to let that happen.

What Netflix Has Revealed on Previous Stranger Things Days

Netflix has rarely let November 6th pass without at least one reveal. Here’s a look back:

  • 2018: Netflix officially declares November 6th “Stranger Things Day” to mark the 35th anniversary of Will’s disappearance, announcing it with a video on November 1st.
  • 2019: The Stranger Things writers’ room revealed the Season 4 premiere title, “The Hellfire Club,” and Netflix shared a series blooper reel.
  • 2021: One of the biggest Stranger Things Days to date. Netflix dropped a new Season 4 teaser, revealed the season’s episode titles, confirmed a summer 2022 release, unveiled the first official map of Hawkins by artist Kyle Lambert and opened pop-up stores in LA and New York.
  • 2022: We learned the title of the Season 5 premiere: “The Crawl.”
  • 2023: Folded into Netflix’s Geeked Week, the @strangerwriters account closed out the day by revealing the opening lines of Season 5, Chapter One, Scene One.
  • 2024: Netflix revealed the titles for all eight episodes of the final season and confirmed a 2025 release.
  • 2025: The final Stranger Things Day before the show ended brought our first look at Stranger Things: Tales from ’85, including its new voice cast and a behind-the-scenes featurette, plus a big TUDUM livestream.
