Novak Djokovic: Why Serb is Banned From Group Chat With Nadal and Federer

TheTools · 14.11.2026 18:26 · source · permalink

Tennis legend Rafa Nadal has now revealed the reason why Novak Djokovic is blocked from having a group chat also including Roger Federer.

The 22-time Grand Slam champion has maintained a close relationship with the latter, his longtime Swiss rival, which began before his retirement from tennis in 2024. Nadal, now 40, admitted he remains in close contact with Federer, 45, and revealed what the two sporting icons regularly discuss many things.

"We’re in touch almost every week," the Spaniard told AP. "We talk, and we think we were much better than the players today (laughs). In all seriousness, we usually talk about other things, like golf and other sports; we also share ideas, it’s a normal relationship."

But while Djokovic makes up the 'big three' in tennis history, his two greatest rivals don't currently have plans to get in touch with him. That's down to one big reason.

Nadal went on to reveal that a WhatsApp group chat between the sport's 'Big Three' has yet to be created because Djokovic, 39, is still competing. He admitted:

"We don’t have a chat - Federer, Djokovic, and I - because Novak is still competing."

Novak Djokovic’s 2026 season officially came to an end following a second-round defeat at the Rolex Paris Masters earlier this month - and, in truth, it's been a rather forgettable campaign for the Serb, who has failed to progress beyond the quarter-finals in any of the nine Masters 1000 events.

It’s the first time in 21 years that has happened, when Djokovic was playing in his first full season on the ATP Tour in 2005. The 39-year-old reached the final of the Australian Open and the semifinals of Wimbledon, but he was beaten in his opening round match of the Italian Open, the Cincinnati Open, and the Shanghai Masters.

The 'King of Clay' is over two years removed from calling time on his career but will pick up a racket in an exhibition match in Las Vegas next February ahead of Indian Wells.

Discussing his decision to return to the court, Nadal said, as per The Express: "At first, I wasn’t really looking forward to playing the exhibition in Las Vegas, but in a way, it forces me to start training again and get back into my routines.

"To be honest, I’m still hitting the ball quite well. But moving around the court, that’s a different story. I need to take it step by step; if I want to avoid injuries, it’s a process that requires time and patience."
