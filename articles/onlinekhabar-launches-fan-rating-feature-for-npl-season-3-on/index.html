OnlineKhabar launches fan rating feature for NPL Season 3 - OnlineKhabar English News

TheTools · 28.10.2026 09:10 · source · permalink

Kathmandu, October 28

OnlineKhabar has launched OK Sports Fan Rating for Nepal Premier League (NPL) Season 3, allowing cricket fans to rate teams and individual players based on their performances in each match.

The interactive feature is available on the NPL Season 3 Live Updates page on OnlineKhabar. Fans can rate teams and players on a scale of 1 to 10 and compare their assessments with those submitted by other users.

The feature aims to make OnlineKhabar’s digital coverage of the tournament more interactive by giving readers a platform to share their views on match performances.

To use OK Sports Fan Rating, users must log in to their OnlineKhabar accounts, visit the NPL Season 3 Live Updates page and open the relevant match page. They can then select the Fan Rating tab, rate both teams and individual players on a scale of 1 to 10, and submit their ratings. The submitted ratings remain available on the match page for the next 24 hours.

OK Sports Fan Rating is designed to assess performance in an individual match rather than a player’s overall career, long-term ability or cumulative performance throughout the tournament.

A player’s rating may therefore vary from one match to another, depending on their contribution in each game. Team ratings similarly reflect performance in the specific match being rated.

Through the feature, OnlineKhabar aims to give fans an opportunity to participate actively in cricket coverage beyond following live scores and match updates. Fans can log in to their OnlineKhabar accounts and access the Fan Rating tab on the NPL Season 3 Live Updates page to submit their ratings.
