Lee orders farmland probe near Seoul to curb speculation, denies seizures

TheTools · 26.10.2026 06:00 · source · permalink

President Lee Jae-myung on the 26th explained the reason for conducting a full survey of farmland, saying, "We said we should find out whether it's being used for something else without farming, and in this process, Agriculture Minister Song Mi-ryung proposed, 'We haven't done it for decades, so let's do a full survey,' and that's why it was done."

Lee said this while attending the "dialogue with farmers and fishers" held at the Blue House on the afternoon of the same day.

Lee said, "To implement policy on farmland, we need to know what the farmland situation is, and the law requires a survey," adding, "I heard it hasn't been done for 70 years, so we should at least find out. I said we should check the situation of farmland near the capital area that may have been purchased for speculative purposes."

He went on, "This is not about land inherited or farmed by ancestors for generations. We said we should identify cases where people reported 'I will farm,' then bought land on that basis and did not farm but used it for something else."

Lee said, "At that time, the Minister (Song) proposed to me, 'While we're at it, the law requires it, and there are surveys that haven't been done for decades, so let's do a full survey,' and that is how it came about."

He reiterated, "This is not about seizing farmland or taking adverse actions."
