Rajasthan LDC Result 2026 Released: The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) has announced the RSSB LDC 2026 result for the exam conducted on July 5, 2026. The result has been released online on the official website of RSSB. Candidates can get the direct link provided here and the steps to check the RSSB LDC result.

Key Points

  • The RSSB LDC 2026 result was announced on September 28, 2026.
  • The exam on July 5, 2026, aimed to fill 10,644 LDC/Clerk Grade-II vacancies.
  • Shortlisted candidates will proceed to the next round, a typing test.

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) has announced the RSSB LDC result on September 28, 2026. The RSSB conducted the offline exam on July 5, 2026, to fill a total of 10,644 vacancies of Lower Division Clerk (LDC)/Clerk Grade-II and Junior Assistant in various ministries and departments of the Rajasthan Government. The RSSB LDC 2026 result has been released online on the official website of RSSB- rssb.rajasthan.gov.in. Candidates can check and download their RSSB LDC results using their roll number. The shortlisted candidates will be eligible to move to the next round of the selection process, which is a typing test.

Candidates can view their individual scores and their qualifying status by logging in through the link provided on the official website of RSSB after entering their Registration Number and Date of Birth. 

RSSB Rajasthan LDC Result 2026

RSSB Chairman Alok Raj confirmed through an X post that the board have approved the RSSB LDC results. The list of shortlisted candidates for the typing test will be uploaded soon on the official website of RSSB.

How to Check LDC Result 2026?

The LDC result 2026 can be checked online at the official website. The candidates need to follow the below-mentioned steps to access and download the RSSB LDC 2026 exam result PDF.

Step 1: Visit the official website of the board, rssb.rajasthan.gov.in

Step 2: Click on the ‘RSSB LDC Result’ link.

Step 3: A new window will open; enter your roll number and click on the submit button.

Step 4: The result PDF will appear; check the results and download it.

Step 5: Take the printout for future reference.

Problems in downloading the Rajasthan RSSB LDC Result 2026

If any candidate faces any issues in downloading the Rajasthan RSSB LDC result 2026. He/She must do the following things before connecting with the exam conducting authority.
