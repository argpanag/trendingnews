Press Room | Comments after Juventus-Omonia - Juventus

TheTools · 26.11.2026 23:41 · source · permalink

26 November 2026

The Men’s First Team beat Omonia 4-1 on Matchday 5 of the UEFA Europa League to take another step towards the knockouts.

Luciano Spalletti, Francisco Conceicao and Jeremie Boga spoke to the press after the victory at the Allianz Stadium.

LUCIANO SPALLETTI

“We were a bit shaky at the start, and the opposition capitalised on that. [Andrea] Cambiaso’s brilliant goal put things back on track, and from then on the team gained confidence. It’s not easy to hit top form straight away, some players had been out for a while. Without consistency, it’s never straightforward, but we scored some fine goals and held our own well on the pitch. We’ve seen this level of commitment from the lads again this evening. [Jeff] Ekhator put in a good performance, even though he wasn’t fed the ball enough in the first half. Sometimes we hold onto the ball for too long and make life difficult for the striker, who then struggles to find space.”

FRANCISCO CONCEICAO

“We know that every match is tough in the Europa League, there are strong teams and everyone wants to play this sort of match. We must carry on like this, keep winning. I saw Ekhator’s run and played the ball to him, I’m happy for him, he deserves it. I’d like to thank the whole staff for their work in helping me recover. I dedicated the goal to my family in the stands, who know the difficult times you go through when you’re injured.”

JEREMIE BOGA

“We played well today. We had a few more difficulties in the first twenty minutes, but then we did the right things. Scoring goals is always a pleasure for us strikers. I’m always looking to score from that position, that’s what I need to do consistently to help the team more and more. In training, I always practise that sort of shot, as well as the build-up involving dribbling. I always give it a go, and when it works out, it’s great.”
