First Alert Weather Day: Flood Watch for parts of Tri-State, rainy morning commute

CINCINNATI, Ohio (WXIX) - A First Alert Weather Day is in effect Monday morning with a Flood Watch for parts of the Tri-State until noon.

Showers are crossing the area right now as the morning commute begins and will linger until mid-morning, according to the FOX19 NOW Weather Team.

The Flood Watch is mainly north of the Ohio River in the northern Cincinnati suburbs, including Butler, Clermont and Warren counties. Another quarter inch to half inch could fall this morning.

Flash flooding is a concern in these areas because the ground is already saturated from weekend rain.

Some areas in southern Warren County and northern Clermont County reported as much as 4-5 inches of rain Saturday and Sunday. West Chester residents in Butler County reported 2-3 inches of rain.

The Little Miami River near Washington Township in Warren County is expected to surpass the flood stage of 11 feet by about a half-inch Monday morning.

Much cooler air is moving in, finally bringing relief from the heat and humidity, according to the weather team.

Once the rain moves out, Monday will be cloudy with a high temperature in the low-to-mid-70s.

Monday night will remain mostly cloudy as lows dip into the upper 50s.

High temperatures will stay in the 70s the rest of the week.

Overnight lows will be in the 50s and dip into the upper 40s, according to the weather team.
