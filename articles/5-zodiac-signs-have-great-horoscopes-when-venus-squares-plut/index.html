5 Zodiac Signs Have Great Horoscopes When Venus Squares Pluto On September 17

TheTools · 16.09.2026 19:00 · source · permalink
  • Aria Gmitter

Written on Sep 16, 2026

Five zodiac signs have some really great horoscopes on September 17, 2026. Venus in Scorpio squares Pluto in Aquarius, which takes romance a step further. 

The planet of love in Scorpio makes us want to go a little deeper and get closer emotionally to the people we love. The square aspect is usually a sign of conflict, but on Thursday, these astrological signs see past surface issues and sense what's really going on. 

1. Scorpio

Design: YourTango

You were right all along, and you figure out exactly what it was that someone kept from you on Thursday. Your super-sleuth skills help you to deep-dive into the past of someone you worry about a lot. A conversation comes up that gives you a chance to casually get them to share more without them even really realizing that's what you're doing. 

The problems you experience aren't deal breakers or reasons to walk away. You spot emotional sensitivities without causing discomfort and, with Venus in your zodiac sign making you absolutely irresistible, say what needs to be said in a way that makes you both feel a lot better.

2. Taurus

Design: YourTango

There's someone you're not sure you can trust. Thursday is the day you figure it out by dropping them a little tidbit of information that only you know. That way, if they share it with anyone else, you'll know. 

Today's horoscope is a great one for you because you're about to discover how truly rock-solid your friendship is. I get the feeling it is, in fact, rock solid. This is someone you can let into your small circle of trust. It's a relief. Whew!

3. Aquarius

Design: YourTango

Aquarius, today is the day that great things happen in your career, especially if you've been doing some digging. With Venus squaring Pluto in your sign, you're about to figure out what you want to do next with your job and professional life as a whole. 

Some personal things no longer feel like they fit in with your interests. Yet, it's been really hard to know what to do next. On Thursday, the answer pops up from deep inside your heart. All the thinking you've done seems to have helped ideas rise to the surface. What you learn is great for your future, and you're ready to seize the opportunity.

4. Leo

Design: YourTango

Leo, you need room to think. Home may be where the heart is, but if there are too many things to distract you, it's hard to keep a single string of thought for long. That's something you haven't had a chance to truly enjoy in a long time: peace and quiet without any distractions. 

Venus in Scorpio has you acting like a recluse who is in hiding from the world. On September 17, you ask friends and family to grant you uninterrupted time, and they give it to you, no questions asked. Yay! You're so thankful for the opportunity, and the timing is perfect! 

5. Libra

Design: YourTango

With your ruling planet in Scorpio, you discover an opportunity to make easy money without having to work super hard. You feel empowered on Thursday. You have all the tools in place. You're not sure how you got so lucky, but you're not going to complain. Fate has given you a leg up, and you're going to take it. 

You take a chance on yourself and work hard to add value to your life in areas where you once thought doing so was a waste of time. You don't want to be the type of person who squanders an opportunity, and you don't let this one slide. You're going for a well-rounded, enriched life, Libra, and you're about to get it.
