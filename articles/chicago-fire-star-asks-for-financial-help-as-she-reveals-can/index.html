The Chicago Fire community is rallying behind one of their own.

On Friday, November 13, cast member Robyn Coffin revealed she was diagnosed with breast cancer, and was seeking financial help to cover her upcoming surgery and treatment.

The The Life and Death of an Unhappily Married Man actress joined Chicago Fire in 2012, and plays Cindy Hermann, the wife of David Eigenberg's character Captain Christopher Herrmann.

Cindy had a cancer plot line on Chicago Fire in 2023 (@ Getty Images)

"Hello Family and Friends of our Lovely Robyn Coffin. As many of you may know, Robyn has been diagnosed with breast cancer. Terrible news," the GoFundMe first stated.

It went on to share the "good news" that Robyn has "a crack team at the hospital organizing and planning her surgery and aftercare and recovery," and the "great news," that she has an "adoring" and supportive husband, Grant.

"Robyn has a really big village and she and Grant are calling on that village to help out. This is NOT an easy ask for Robyn or Grant, but they have been convinced this is the best way [to] ensure a stress-free journey from surgery to aftercare to recovery," it noted.

She is the on-screen wife of lead star David (@ Getty Images)

According to the fundraiser, Robyn will undergo a right partial mastectomy with magseed localization on December 3, followed by radiation. She has a pre-op visit two days before Thanksgiving, and should that visit confirm she needs a right partial mastectomy, she is expecting to be out of work for 3 weeks.

Moreover, the fundraiser added: "If a full mastectomy is required, Robyn's time off work will increase quite a bit, as the surgery is much more extensive."

It further stated: "When Robyn doesn't work, she doesn't get paid. So, this go fund me is not only to fill in the gaps that insurance will surely leave, but also to help sustain Robyn and Grant while Robyn is off work. Thank goodness they have insurance, but let's not get cocky – let's give them a nest egg because we know there will be surprises."

Some of her co-stars publicly supported her (@ Getty Images)

"We know that with insurance the surgery and hospital bill will amount to $40k. Because the surgery is hard upon, this amount is most urgent. The balance is for said surprises: a longer recovery period (see not working), medicine off the plan or not fully covered, or whatever is thrown at them," it ultimately emphasized.

With her co-stars in 2017 (@ Getty Images)

Among the Chicago Fire cast members to support the fundraiser — which has raised $15,846 out of its $75,000 goal, as of writing — was Miranda Rae Mayo, who shared it on her Instagram stories.

In season 11 of the procedural, Robyn's character, who has appeared in 64 episodes, also battled cancer after a lung cancer diagnosis.
