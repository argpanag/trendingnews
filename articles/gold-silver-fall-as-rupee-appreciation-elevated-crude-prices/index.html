Gold, silver fall as rupee appreciation, elevated crude prices weigh on bullion

TheTools · 21.10.2026 13:55 · source · permalink
  • Articles
  • 2 min read
  • PTI
  • Published On Oct 21, 2026 at 07:25 PM IST

New Delhi, Gold and silver prices declined in the national capital on Wednesday as appreciation in the rupee, elevated crude oil prices and weak global trends dampened the sentiment of investors.

The yellow metal of 99.9 per cent purity dropped Rs 1,300 to Rs 1,49,000 per 10 grams (inclusive of all taxes), according to the All India Sarafa Association.

Gold had closed at Rs 1,50,300 per 10 grams in the previous session.

Silver entered into a sliding path after remaining steady for two consecutive sessions, falling Rs 1,600 to Rs 2,13,400 per kilogram. The white metal had settled at Rs 2,15,000 per kg on Monday.
