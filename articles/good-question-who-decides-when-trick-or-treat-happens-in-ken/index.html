Good Question: Who decides when trick-or-treat happens in Kentucky?

TheTools · 22.10.2026 21:16 · source · permalink

LEXINGTON, Ky. — A viewer asked: “In Kentucky, who decides when trick-or-treat times happen? Is it always 6 to 8?”

There is no state law mandating when trick-or-treat has to happen in the commonwealth. That decision is up to local governments.

Who sets the hours

Trick-or-treat dates and hours are decided by city or county governments, mayors, or local police and fire departments. That’s why the date and hours can vary from one town or county to another.

Why 6 to 8 p.m. is common

In late October, sunset across most of the U.S. falls sometime after 6 p.m. Starting at 6 gives families time to get home from work and still lets younger kids begin while there’s some daylight left. By 8 p.m., it’s fully dark, and at that point cities don’t want kids wandering the streets, increasing the chances someone gets hurt.

The date and time are up to local leaders, but they tend to pick hours intended to keep kids safer.
