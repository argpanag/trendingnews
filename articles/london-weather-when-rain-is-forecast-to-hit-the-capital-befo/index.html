London weather: When rain is forecast to hit the capital before 27C temperature surge

TheTools · 09.09.2026 09:04 · source · permalink

Londoners are set for a soggy end to the working week as a band of heavy rain sweeps across southern England on Thursday (September 10), with Met Office maps showing the capital among scores of areas facing wet weather.

The rain is expected to develop over the Atlantic before moving steadily eastwards throughout the day. While forecasters are predicting temperatures could climb as high as 27C next week, Thursday is likely to bring a much gloomier picture for commuters across the capital.

After drenching western parts of the UK earlier in the day, the rain is forecast to reach London during the evening, bringing spells of persistent rainfall before conditions improve overnight, The Mirror writes.

Elsewhere, cities as far east as Newcastle and Hull will be hit by downpours, with heavy rain expected until the early hours of Friday.

"Cloud and rain will spread erratically eastwards throughout the day", The Met Office forecast predicts. "Rain turning heavier at times, particularly in the north, with the southeast likely remaining dry until after dark."

In total, the weather maps suggest 100 UK areas will see rain by Thursday afternoon.

BBC Weather forecast

The BBC Weather forecast for Thursday states: "Tomorrow, sunny spells in the east to start, but cloud and some light rain further west. Later on, a band of rain will feed in from the west, reaching the south-east by the end of the day. Breezy."

It adds for the rest of the week: "Friday looks to be a mainly dry and settled day, with sunny spells and areas of cloud. Saturday will see winds strengthen for all. Cloud and rain will spread across the north, while the south stays mainly dry with spells of sunshine. A generally dry day with variable cloud on Sunday, but further rain in the far north-west later in the day."
