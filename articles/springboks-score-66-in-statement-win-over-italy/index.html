Springboks score 66 in statement win over Italy

TheTools · 07.11.2026 16:16 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

The Springboks have beaten Italy 66-7 in Turin.

  • South Africa led 45-0 at halftime with six tries in the first half.
  • The Springboks outscored Italy nine tries to one, with hooker Malcolm Marx getting a double.
  • The Springboks went into the Northern Series of the Nations Championship at the top of the Southern Hemisphere table.
