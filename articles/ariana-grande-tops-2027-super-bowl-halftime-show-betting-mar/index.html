Ariana Grande Tops 2027 Super Bowl Halftime Show Betting Markets Ahead of Sabrina Carpenter, Taylor Swift

Ariana Grande sits atop betting markets for the 2027 Super Bowl halftime show at 3/1. Sabrina Carpenter follows at 4/1, and Miley Cyrus holds 5/1 odds.

BetOnline launched the market on Feb. 9, and several performers have seen their numbers shift since. Post Malone claims 6/1, while Taylor Swift lands at 8/1. The board features 33 acts, with Harry Styles at 9/1, plus Dua Lipa, Oasis, and Adele each sitting at 10/1.

Justin Bieber's odds jumped from 25/1 to 12/1 as money poured in. Dua Lipa rose from 16/1 to 10/1 during the same stretch.

Grande brings visibility through music and film. Her turn as Glinda in Wicked pushed her into new territory, while tracks like "Thank U, Next," "7 Rings," and "Positions" give crowds songs they know.

Carpenter opened for Swift during the Eras Tour, which means she's worked stadium crowds before. Her Short n' Sweet era and songs like "Espresso" and "Please Please Please" have lifted her status in recent months. Betting News wrote, "Her recent rise gives the NFL a younger option with substantial momentum." 

Cyrus carries a career spanning Disney pop, big hits, and rock. Post Malone has moved through pop, hip-hop, and country with his F-1 Trillion project. His known tracks include "Circles," "Sunflower," and "Congratulations."

Swift is married to the Kansas City Chiefs tight end, Travis Kelce. Other acts include Olivia Rodrigo at 16/1, Pink at 20/1, Billie Eilish at 20/1, and Ed Sheeran at 20/1. Lil Wayne, John Legend, SZA, and BlackPink each hold 25/1.

Super Bowl LXI will happen on Feb. 14, 2027, at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California. It's the first Super Bowl to fall on Valentine's Day.
