US to livestream execution of Texas gunman who killed 13

TheTools · 08.10.2026 22:27 · source · permalink

Getty

The US will livestream the army's upcoming firing squad execution of Nidal Hasan, who fatally shot 13 people at Fort Hood, Texas in 2009, according to a Pentagon official.

Hasan, a former Army psychiatrist, is set to become the first person to be executed by firing squad by the US military since 1945, shortly after the end of World War II.

The Pentagon official, who spoke on the condition of anonymity to Reuters, did not offer more information and said details would follow.

Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth earlier told the Human Events Daily podcast that he planned to make Hasan's execution on 3 December public, according to video of his comments posted on social media.

Mr Hegseth said deciding to execute Hasan by firing squad at Fort Hood on a Thursday at 1pm - the approximate time, day of the week and location of Hasan's shooting - was a "no brainer".

"We'll make sure that people are able to watch it, that it's public because people need to understand that ... there's serious consequences for these types of things," Mr Hegseth said.
