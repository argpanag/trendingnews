Match report: Czechia 0-2 England

Anybody can lose to Spain. But when England did so at Wembley on Saturday, it served to turn the temperature up on on this trip to Czechia, where England have form for finding it difficult. Remember the European championship qualifier here in 2019 when they lost 2-1?

It was not easy at first. Thomas Tuchel shuffled his pack, making the surprise decision to start Jude Bellingham among the substitutes. It was all a little angsty and Czechia, for whom this is the start of a new era under the Spaniard Santi Denia after their World Cup wash-out, dared to dream about another upset.

Then everything changed. It was a rash challenge in the 23rd minute by Pavel Sulc on Elliot Anderson, late and high, albeit nothing overtly nasty. The referee, Umut Meler, showed the Czechia midfielder a yellow card and on we went. Yet the video assistant referee was not happy and when Meler was called to the monitor, everybody knew what was coming next.

Anthony Gordon’s reaction

Good performance, quite dominant. Some things we need to work on – at times we were too passive when they a were a man down.

I said to Trent when I scored – I make that run time and time again but only certain players have the ability, technique and bravery to play that pass. That’s why he’s so good. I’ve been dying to play with him for that reason.

I miss playing with Hally [Lewis Hall]. He’s a top lad and it’s nice to share this stage with him.

I don’t know if I’ve ever scored a header! I work on it so much in training so I’m really pleased.

Full time: Croatia 0-2 England

A comfortable win for England in Prague. An early red card for Pavel Sulc made for a one-sided game, settled by second-half goals from the outstanding Anthony Gordon and the inevitable Harry Kane. Next up: Croatia away on Saturday.

90+2 min A low drive from Bellingham is pushed round the post by the diving Kovar. England could have scored three or four.

90 min It’s been a good night for England – a comfortable, drama-free victory that sets them up for the game in Croatia on Saturday. There will be two minutes of added time.

87 min It’s petering out, as you’d expect when it’s 2-0 and 11 v 10.

82 min: Good save by Kovar! Gibbs-White plays Gordon by beating his man and slipping a low cross into the area. It’s half cleared to Eze, whose shot from 15 yards is pawed over by Kovar. Excellent save, that.

81 min Substitutions plenty. Morgan Gibbs-White replaces Anthony Gordon, probably the player of the match, and Czechia bring on Daniel Vasulin and Roman Macek for Adam Hlozek and Lukas Cerv.

80 min Gordon’s clipped cross is headed just wide of the near post by Rogers.

79 min “Tom Hopkins appears to be forgetting a little thing we call ‘Wales at Euro 2016’,” begins Matt Dony. “(Yes, ‘threatening to win’ may be an exaggeration, but still, it was definitely delightful.)”

Not as delightful as your performance on the MBM of the semi-final, son. It makes me smile whenever I remember it. I wish I’d kept the unpublished emails.

77 min Czechia break promisingly through Hlozek, whose long-range shot is pushed away by the diving Trafford. A comfortable save. The ball is screwed back to Mac Millings’ hero Lingr, who can’t control under pressure.

77 min “Hi Bob,” writes your friend and mine Mac Millings. “Mostly just following in the hope that Czechia’s 45th-minute substitute gets sent off for something foolish, so that I can ask, ‘Did you have to, did you have to, did you have to, Ondrej Lingr?’”

76 min ITV’s Lee Dixon, an expert in full-backery, makes a point of praising Lewis Hall for his performance at left-back tonight. To this admittedly ignorant eye, he looks a superb player.

73 min “FYI,” says Paul Griffin, “the Czech for elephant in the room is slom v mistnosti.”

If I put that into Google Translate, will I get sacked?

71 min: Triple substitution for England Jude Bellingham, Rio Ngumoha and the debutant Alex Scott replaces Harry Kane, Elliot Anderson and Bukayo Saka.

GOAL! Czechia 0-2 England (Kane 69)

That’ll do. Eze plays a short pass into the area for Rogers, who is well challenged by Krejci. The ball runs loose and Kane sweeps a left-foot shot into the far corner. That – don’t adjust your browser – is his 87th goal for England.

67 min England are comfortable, and I don’t know what else to tell you. Czechia have been better on the break since half-time without really threatening to sully James Trafford’s gloves.

65 min “I’ll put my hand up and admit I was not sold on Anthony Gordon and I was wrong,” writes Tom Hopkins. “Tuchel made a number of other selection decisions which were baffling at the time and have not aged well, but that one’s on his side of the scales.

“On more nostalgic matters, as they often do when Czechia play, my thoughts turn to their 2004 vintage, Nedved and all. Are they the last (relatively) small nation to pop up with a team that threaten to win something in delightful fashion? Maybe unfairly, I feel like Croatia have disqualified themselves by means of consistent excellence.

Do Russia 2008 count? (Size of football nation ofc.) But yeah, that 2004 Czechia team, especially the attacking quintet, are the dictionary definition of joy.

61 min Eze releases Hall on the left. His superb low cross is poked onto the far post by Krejci, who was facing his own goal and tried to stab it behind for a corner. The ball rebounds kindly for Czechia and the danger is cleared.

Plenty to like about the combination of Gordon and Hall tonight.

60 min Kane wants a penalty match after a wrestling match with Krejci at a corner. Nothing doing, though Jake the Snake Roberts would have been proud of both men.

57 min: Czechia substitutions Lukas Ambros and Alex Kral replace Michal Sadilekj and Matej Radosta.

55 min Alexander-Arnold’s latest cross hits Saka on the back of the noggin and Czechia clear. This feels like a foregone conclusion, but, well.

52 min Gordon gallops into the area and pushes the ball back to Hall, who drags a left-foot shot wide of the far post. That was a pretty good chance.

48 min Nowt else to report. That was an excellent goal: a classic Alexander-Arnold assist and an accomplished header from Gordon.

GOAL! Czechia 0-1 England (Gordon 47)

England take the lead inside 70 seconds of the restrart. Alexander-Arnold retrieves a loose ball on the right and curls a fine cross around the Czechia defence. Gordon watches the bounce carefully and guides a header past across the keeper Kovar.

46 min England have brought on Eberechi Eze for Myles Lewis-Skelly. Different midfielders for different needs.

Half-time reading

Half time: Czechia 0-0 England

All square in Prague. England dominated but struggled to break down the home side after Pavel Sulc was sent off for a poor tackle on Elliot Anderson. They have 45 more minutes in which to do so.

45 min Lewis-Skelly plays a short pass into the Kane on the edge of the D. He turns his man smoothly and whacks a curler that is pawed round the post by the diving Kovar. That’s a pretty good save.

45 min: Czechia substitution The injured Adam Karabec is replaced by Ondrej Lingr.

42 min Alexander-Arnold’s cross is headed across goal and wide by Rogers. It’s been all England since the red card but they haven’t created much.

38 min The impressive Gordon beats Chaloupek and slides an excellent low cross that is cleared awkwardly in front of his own goal by Sadlek.

35 min Lewis-Skelly is booked for a late tackle on Cerv.

32 min Czechia continue to defend deep. England aren’t naturally suited to this type of game but you can feel them trying to move the ball more quickly.;

31 min A smart early ball from Anderson (I think) finds Gordon. whose shot from the angle is beaten away by Kovar. Decent effort but a comfortable save.

29 min A zig-zag move from England ends with Saka’s shot being deflected wide. Hall was offside in the build-up but it was nicely worked.

28 min Czechia were sitting deep before the red card so you can imagine the pitch map now. Two crosses from the left are cleared, first by Krejci and then by someone.
