Ulsan Munsu Football Stadium was full for the first time in three years and seven months.

The Korean national soccer team has been playing a friendly match against Venezuela since 8 p.m. on Oct. 2. Both teams have been in a 0-0 balance and have passed 30 minutes in the second half.

The heat of the stadium is hotter than ever. The number of spectators who visited Venezuela was 35,911.

It is the first time that Ulsan Munsu Football Stadium has recorded a full crowd against Venezuela since the warm-up match (2-2) against Colombia on March 24, 2023. The match against Colombia was the debut of Jurgen Klinsmann, who took the helm of the national team after Paulo Bento.

The match against Venezuela is the third match since Roberto Moreno took the helm of the Korean national team temporarily. Moreno won 3-0 against Ecuador on September 24, his debut match, and then lost 1-4 against Uruguay on the 28th.

After the match, South Korea will play Uzbekistan at 8 p.m. on October 6 at Yongin Mir Stadium. It is the last game of the four consecutive A matches between September and October.
