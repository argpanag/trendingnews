Cardi B Says Her Inner Circle's Resentment Is Making Her Evil & Dangerous

TheTools · 28.10.2026 16:00 · source · permalink

Cardi B describes growing distrust within her inner circle and explains why she wants distance from people as her career hits new heights.

Nolan Strong • Oct 28, 2026 @ 12:00 PM

Cardi B just revealed something most people never want to admit out loud: success has a price that money can’t fix.

During a recent live stream, the 34-year-old rapper got real about how reaching the top has isolated her from the people she thought she could trust, and the jealousy she’s experiencing from her inner circle is reshaping who she is as a person.

The Bronx native didn’t hold back when describing what it feels like to achieve at her level.

“It’s so crazy that at the age of 34, I’m still learning lessons. I’m still taking L’s and I’m still learning lessons,” she explained, adding that success has put her in a position where she’s constantly discovering new betrayals.

The hardest part isn’t the fame itself, it’s realizing that the people closest to her resent her wins instead of celebrating them.

“I’m experiencing jealousy and I’m experiencing people trying to humble me the most,” Cardi B said, making it clear that this isn’t coming from random haters online.

It’s coming from her circle. She described how impossible it’s become to share good news without triggering resentment from those she cares about.

“Being successful, it is so scary. You just don’t know who got that secret animosity for you,” she continued. “It’s like you cannot even express, hey, I got a new contract because deep down they’re upset that you getting it.”

The toll this has taken on her personality is real. Cardi B said she used to be nice and kind, but the constant attempts to tear her down have made her feel like she’s becoming someone colder and meaner.

She’s reached the highest point of her career and life, but that achievement has come with a loneliness that’s almost unbearable.

“It’s lonely at the top. I have never been as high as I am right now in my career, in my everything. And it just makes me want to be alone for real,” she said. “I want to change my number and just go away. Y’all turning me so evil and so dangerous.”

Cardi B’s comments about dealing with fake friends and secret resentment have resonated with people who understand that success can be isolating.

The rapper’s willingness to discuss these struggles shows a side of fame that rarely gets discussed in interviews.

Fresh off a $70 million tour and celebrating her achievements, Cardi B is grappling with the reality that reaching the top means losing the connections that once grounded her.
