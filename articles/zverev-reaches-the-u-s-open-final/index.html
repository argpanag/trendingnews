Zverev Reaches the U.S. Open Final

TheTools · 12.09.2026 04:27 · source · permalink

US Open

Alexander Zverev is in the final of a Grand Slam tournament for the third time in a row

Keystone

Alexander Zverev is the first player to reach the US Open final. The top-seeded German defeated Russia's Karen Khachanov 6-3, 7-6 (9-7), 7-6 (8-6) in the first semifinal.

Zverev has now won 52 singles matches this season—more than any other player on the tour. At the Grand Slam level, he has won 24 matches so far in 2026.

Six years ago, Zverev reached his first Grand Slam final at the U.S. Open, which he dramatically lost to Austrian Dominic Thiem in a fifth-set tiebreak after leading by two sets. Now he is in the final in New York for the second time; it is his third consecutive Grand Slam final.

At Flushing Meadows, only an American can stand in the way of Zverev winning his second major title following his triumph at the French Open in June. In the other semifinal, the two home-grown players, Ben Shelton (ATP No. 9) and Frances Tiafoe (ATP No. 12), will face off on Friday night.

For Khachanov, ranked No. 50 in the world, it would have been his first Grand Slam final. The 30-year-old Russian played well for long stretches, but couldn't rely on his forehand in the crucial moments. In the decisive tiebreak, he squandered two set points while leading 6–4.
