Ukraine war briefing: Zelenskyy claims US deliberately delaying interceptor missile deliveries to Kyiv

TheTools · 20.10.2026 00:44 · source · permalink

Ukrainian president tells US senators the delayed missiles have already been paid for by European countries, amid growing rift. What we know on day 1,700

  • Volodymyr Zelenskyy has said the US is deliberately delaying deliveries of interceptor missiles to Ukraine, deepening a public spat with Donald Trump. Speaking to US senators on Monday, the Ukrainian president claimed that “the Pentagon is delaying the delivery of missiles” already paid for by European countries under the prioritised Ukraine requirements list (Purl) program. According to Zelenskyy, the shipment is awaiting the signature of the US defence secretary, Pete Hegseth. A White House official, who spoke to the Washington Post on condition of anonymity, said the US “continues to play a constructive role in speaking to both sides in working towards bringing the war to an end”.

  • Zelenskyy also said several European countries were ready to transfer Patriot interceptor missiles from their own stockpiles but were still awaiting US approval. “All of this suggests that some political decision has been made to slow down the support for us,” he said in remarks released by the Ukrainian presidency.   • Trump has blamed Ukrainian strikes on Russian oil refineries for soaring fuel prices that are a major issue in coming US midterm elections. “To be honest, I think it’s time for somebody else to negotiate on behalf of Ukraine,” Trump said on Monday. Zelenskyy said US decisions taken ⁠on the war – including delays in arms shipments and restrictions on ⁠intelligence sharing – were “hard ⁠to explain”. Washington denies making any changes to intelligence-sharing with Kyiv.

  • Ukraine did not receive a warning from the US about an impending Russian missile attack on Sunday evening, Zelenskyy said, reviving concerns that Washington is withholding intelligence to bully Kyiv into capitulating to Moscow. A Ukrainian air force spokesperson said on Monday morning that Ukraine had not received a warning before a Russian missile barrage hit power plants in Kyiv about 11pm, Andrew Roth reports. Ukraine did receive a warning before a subsequent strike, the spokesperson added. Zelenskyy said on social media on Monday: “Once again, Russia launched a ballistic missile attack in multiple waves throughout the night, and once again, there was no warning from our partners.”

  • Russian strikes killed four people around Ukraine, authorities said on Tuesday, as Moscow continues targeting energy and transport infrastructure. Three people died in the Kyiv region, the State Emergency Service of Ukraine said on Telegram. One person was killed in strikes on Zaporizhzhia in the south-east, according to the regional military administration chief. At least 11 people were wounded in the attacks, they said.

  • Six people, including young children, were killed when a Russian drone struck a park in Ukraine’s north-eastern Kharkiv region on Monday, authorities said. Zelenskyy decried a “pure atrocity”, saying on Facebook the drone “struck people directly – in a park near an ordinary cafe”, and a one-year-old girl was among those killed. The strike in Nova Vodolaga had also killed the girl’s mother.
