Janakpur Bolts win toss, choose to bowl against Biratnagar Kings

TheTools · 03.11.2026 07:45 · source · permalink

Copyright © 2025 Nepal News. All Rights Reserved.

Nepal News

KATHMANDU: The Janakpur Bolts won the toss and elected to bowl first against Biratnagar Kings in the first match of the day in the Nepal Premier League (NPL) at the Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur on Tuesday.

Janakpur captain Harmit Singh said his side chose to bowl because he believed chasing would be easier.

Biratnagar captain Sandeep Lamichhane said his team would aim to post a competitive total and defend it.
