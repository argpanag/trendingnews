A man suspected of stealing a luxury Audemars Piguet watch from a store in Tokyo was arrested by police in Nagoya

TheTools · 11.11.2026 14:21 · source · permalink

Always see our news on Google

A man suspected of stealing a luxury Audemars Piguet watch from a store in the Nakano Broadway shopping complex in Tokyo was arrested by police in Nagoya, the capital of Aichi Prefecture. The theft allegedly took place on October 29, according to an investigative source cited by Kyodo News agency and Agerpres.

The man allegedly fled the store with the watch on his wrist. Three days later, the item was sent back by courier to the store in Aichi Prefecture, together with a letter of apology. Police subsequently managed to locate and detain him in the city of Nagoya.

The incident is the second reported theft of luxury watches in recent months at the shopping complex in Tokyo's Nakano district. In August, four watches were stolen from another store in Nakano Broadway. Their total value was estimated at 200 million yen, equivalent to more than €1.13 million.

In that case, the suspects were subsequently charged. Japanese authorities are continuing their investigation to establish all the circumstances surrounding the thefts and any possible links between the incidents.

Cu câteva săptămâni înainte, polițiștii germani au oprit doi români pe autostrada A3, după ce au găsit aproape 10.000 de euro în numerar și două ceasuri de lux. Verificările au arătat că unul dintre ceasuri fusese declarat furat în landul Hessa și era legat de un jaf, iar cei doi nu au putut prezenta documente privind proveniența banilor și a celui de-al doilea ceas; ancheta a vizat inclusiv suspiciuni de spălare de bani și bunuri provenite din furt.
