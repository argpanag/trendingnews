But the Cincinnati runner-up this year is ready to write a new chapter this time ​around, saying: "I'm just a different person in general. History awaits...


The winner of Frances Tiafoe vs Ben Shelton will be the first Black American man to reach a US Open Final since Arthur Ashe (1972) and the first Black American man to reach any major final since MaliVai Washington (1996 Wimbledon)

Zverev going about his business

While the men's draw has fallen to pieces in the absence of Jannik Sinner and with Novak Djokovic and Alcaraz falling by the wayside, Alexander Zverev has been quietly going about his business.

Luciano Darderi, the 21st seed, is the highest-ranked player Zverev has faced, while he eased past world No 70 Botic van de Zandschulp in straight sets.

The 29-year-old is a big favourite to beat unseeded Russian Karen Khachanov in the opening semi-final on Friday from 8pm on Sky Sports Tennis.

Zverev has beaten Shelton in all five of their career meetings and has lost just one of his 10 matches against Tiafoe.

Having won his first Grand Slam title at this year's French Open and then reached the Wimbledon final, the world No 2 should be feeling at home in the latter stages of major tournaments.

But he said: "I think the more often that you get to that stage, the more normal it becomes, which is something that you should never take for granted.

"I know it can change in a heartbeat in tennis. I think confidence is something that is incredibly important, but also can go away quite quickly.

"I found that out after Wimbledon where I didn't play well throughout the two tournaments on the hard courts, and then I found it again here.

"But yeah, of course I'm happy with how things are going, but looking forward, I still hope to play two good matches here."

Khachanov has been here before

Khachanov is seeking his first major final having reached the semi-finals of the US Open in 2022 and the Australian Open in 2023.

Khachanov was unseeded in a Grand Slam tournament for the first time since 2018 after a strange season.

He reached at least the third round of all four majors for the first time but is just 10-15 in other events, with losses in the opening rounds of all three hard-court events before coming to New York.

Zverev and Khachanov, both in the 1990s generation, have met nine times, with ​the German holding a 6-3 edge.

Their most notable encounter came in the 2021 Tokyo Olympics final, when ⁠Zverev won in straight sets to take gold, while their only previous Grand Slam meeting came at the French Open in 2018, when Zverev fought back from a set down to win in five.
