Flick's secret visit to Mourinho in Madrid

Hansi Flick has revisited the origins of his relationship with José Mourinho ahead of the Clásico, recalling an early visit to Madrid while serving as Joachim Löw’s assistant. Mundo Deportivo reports that the Barcelona coach praised Mourinho and noted German players’ admiration for him.

That first meeting came in 2011-2012, when Mourinho’s Real Madrid featured Mesut Özil and Sami Khedira. Löw made repeated trips to the capital to monitor his internationals and assess Europe’s elite.

OneFootball Videos

He attended the late-2011 Clásico at the Santiago Bernabéu, which ended 3-1 to Barcelona. Months later he returned with Flick to watch Lyon in the Champions League and meet Mourinho to discuss Özil and Khedira. Löw felt their Madrid moves were pivotal and highlighted Özil’s improved work without the ball.

Those journeys also served Germany’s Euro 2012 build-up after the 2010 World Cup semi-final loss to Spain. Their challenge ended in the Euro 2012 semi-final against Italy, Mario Balotelli scoring both, before they lifted the 2014 World Cup by beating Argentina 1-0.

Flick still speaks of Mourinho with respect despite their contrasting careers. They have faced each other only once as head coaches, in December 2019, when Bayern Munich beat Tottenham 3-1 in a Champions League group match.

Another meeting now looms in the Clásico, where both will test their ideas in a contest decided by fine margins.
