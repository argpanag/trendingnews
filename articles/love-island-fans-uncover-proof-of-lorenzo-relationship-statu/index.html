Love Island fans uncover 'proof' of Lorenzo relationship status amid split fears

TheTools · 09.09.2026 10:31 · source · permalink

Love Island fans reckon they've cracked the code on Lorenzo Alessi and Julia Majchrzak's romance after clocking a significant detail.

The 2026 champions initially crossed paths during Casa Amor in the most recent series and subsequently secured the ITV title. Their path to victory wasn't without its bumps, as Julia found it challenging to build solid connections within the villa.

She frequently became embroiled in disputes with the original islanders, while Lorenzo rarely came to her defence. Despite their rocky time on the Island, audiences warmed to the Polish stunner Julia and threw their support behind her when the final arrived.

Since lifting the trophy almost two months ago, speculation about their split has been rife. Followers were certain the pair had quietly parted ways, noting their lack of interaction on social platforms despite both being active on their own accounts.

Their recent appearance at the National Television Awards, though, appears to have changed public perception. The couple were snapped on the red carpet before Tuesday's (September 8) event got underway, reports OK!.

As footage of the reality stars has circulated online, observers have latched onto their intimate body language as evidence they are still together, and simply choosing to maintain privacy around their relationship.

Responding to a red carpet clip showing Lorenzo reaching for Julia's hand, one TikTok viewer enthused: "The way he reached for her hand."

Another viewer noticed the gesture: "The hand holding just shows and HIM initiating it makes it even more so," while a further fan posted on social media: "Muscle memory haha the way he grab her hand."

One person questioned: "Did he just grab her hand?" prompting a reply: "Yes, from the videos you can see that they are holding hands most of the time."

Others took to the internet to celebrate the pair's first public outing in recent weeks. "THEY LOOK SO HAPPY. I LOVE IT," remarked one viewer, while another commented: "Julia and Lorenzo walking in like they own the whole d**n room."

At the time of publication, fellow 2026 stars Aidan Murphy and Priya Jaswal have announced their separation. The pair confirmed the news via Instagram on Tuesday (September 8) afternoon, mere hours before the NTAs.

They posted matching statements confirming their decision to maintain a friendship going forward.
