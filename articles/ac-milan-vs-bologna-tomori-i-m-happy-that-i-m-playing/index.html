AC Milan vs Bologna – Tomori: “I’m happy that I’m playing”

TheTools · 28.10.2026 16:59 · source · permalink

Fikayo Tomori spoke to DAZN before Milan-Bologna, the midweek match valid for the ninth matchday of Serie A.

Tomori's words before Milan-Bologna

“Obviously, I’m happy that I’m playing. My job is to play and help the team. I have always had the mentality of being ready to help. In the last few matches, I have been ready, but I always want to be ready to help, and tonight is the same.”

Today, Tomori will also play as a right-back in place of Mario Gila, who is not at his best and will try to recover in time for the derby, as relayed via Milan Press.
