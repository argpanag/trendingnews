Why does Erling Haaland have name changed to ‘Braut’ on his Norway jersey?

TheTools · 24.09.2026 17:30 · source · permalink

Fans have noticed that the Norwegian striker has ‘Braut Haaland’ with the No. 9 on his shirt when representing his nation, and here’s why.

Erling Haaland was one of the biggest attractions at the 2026 World Cup and continues to be in the Premier League. Any development involving the Norwegian superstar quickly becomes news. And there is one detail surrounding the Manchester City forward, when representing his country that had largely gone unnoticed.

For some time, Haaland has been honoring his mother by adding her surname alongside his father’s on the back of his Norway jersey. It is a gesture he is expected to continue as the UEFA Nations League gets underway on Thursday against Denmark.

  • A Scandinavian showdown with history at stake

Haaland’s special tribute to his mother

Haaland’s mother is Gry Marita Braut, a former professional heptathlete. His father, Alf-Inge Haaland, was a well-known defender who played for Nottingham Forest, Leeds United and Manchester City.

It was during Alf-Inge Haaland’s time in England that Erling Haaland was born in 2000. While the forward is known worldwide simply as Haaland, his national-team jersey reflects both sides of his family name.

The decision allows the Norway international to publicly recognize his mother’s surname on soccer’s biggest stages.

Does Haaland have ‘Braut’ on Manchester City jersey?

The likes of the World Cup, Nations League and European Championships see Haaland wearing a jersey that highlights his mother’s family name alongside his father’s. However, that is not the case at club level.

For Manchester City, the Norwegian continues to wear the familiar, singular “Haaland” on the back of his shirt. The addition of “Braut” remains a distinctive feature of his appearances with the national team.
