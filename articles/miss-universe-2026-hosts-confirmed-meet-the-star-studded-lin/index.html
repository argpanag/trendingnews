Miss Universe 2026 hosts confirmed: Meet the star-studded lineup

TheTools · 18.11.2026 03:31 · source · permalink

The countdown to Miss Universe 2026 just got more exciting! The Miss Universe Organization has officially announced the hosts and presenters for its highly anticipated 75th anniversary celebration, and the lineup brings together familiar television personalities, former beauty queens, and an iconic pop star.

Television host Mario Lopez and Miss Universe 2022 R'Bonney Gabriel have been confirmed as the main hosts of the Miss Universe 2026 grand finale, scheduled for November 24 at the Coliseo de Puerto Rico José Miguel Agrelot in San Juan, Puerto Rico.

The announcement, made on November 17, also confirms the participation of former Miss Universe winners Dayanara Torres, Denise Quiñones and Zuleyka Rivera, alongside television personality Zuri Hall and NSYNC singer Lance Bass.

According to the Miss Universe Organization's announcement, Mario Lopez and R'Bonney Gabriel will lead the grand finale, while other personalities will take on commentary and hosting responsibilities throughout the celebration.

Telemundo Also Confirms Its Miss Universe 2026 Broadcast Hosts

Beyond the Miss Universe Organization's official presenting team, Telemundo has announced a separate lineup for its Spanish-language coverage.

More to explore

Mexican television personality Jacky Bracamontes and Puerto Rican presenter Carlos Adyan will lead the network's broadcast. They will be joined by Aleyda Ortiz and Clovis Nienow as co-hosts and commentators, while Jessica Carrillo will provide backstage coverage.

Jacky Bracamontes will co-host Miss Universe (@ Getty Images)

Telemundo's November 17 announcement also confirms additional pre-show programming, interviews and behind-the-scenes reporting from Puerto Rico.
