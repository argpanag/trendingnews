⚽️ Deportivo La Coruna vs Sevilla

🇪🇸 La Liga

⏰ Wednesday 16 September, 18:00

Entertainment could be in the offing at the Riazor on Wednesday night as newly-promoted Deportivo La Coruna entertain Sevilla in La Liga.

Antonio Hidalgo’s hosts have scored nine goals across their opening five matches back in the Spanish top flight, with seven (78%) of those coming across their last three matches.

Depor have found the back of the net in all five of their La Liga outings this term – scoring on multiple occasions in two of their past three – and will look to continue their goal-scoring record against a Sevilla backline that has conceded in all bar one of their opening five La Liga outings this term, with that clean sheet coming at home to rock-bottom Valencia.

Luis Garcia Plaza’s visitors have scored eight goals across their opening five La Liga clashes this term, with four (50%) of those coming across their opening two road trips.

Like their hosts, Los Nervionenses’ have made the net bulge in each of their opening five encounters this season – scoring two or more goals in two (40%) of them – and will also look to maintain their knack of getting on the scoresheet against a Depor defence that has yet to record a clean sheet since their long-awaited promotion back to the Spanish top flight.
