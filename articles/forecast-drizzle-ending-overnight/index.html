FORECAST: Drizzle ending overnight

TheTools · 10.11.2026 20:57 · source · permalink

WEATHER HEADLINES

  • NEXT 12: Falling into the 30s by Friday morning
  • SHORT TERM: Some may see their first flakes of the season Friday
  • LONG TERM: Drier with more sun, gradual warming

LATEST FORECAST

LOUISVILLE, Ky. (WDRB+WAVE) — Rain chances continue to decrease across the region. Temperatures tumble behind the front with lows in the 30s by early Friday morning.

Wind chill values a few degrees cooler than the actual air temperature. Periods of light rain and drizzle will be possible once again on Friday. The best time for this would be late morning into the early afternoon.

I can’t rule out a few snowflakes mixed in. Temperatures will be below average with highs in the 40s. Breezy!

Decreasing clouds Friday night will allow temperatures to drop near or below freezing in all areas. While that’s happening, some fog may develop by Saturday morning. This could be freezing fog, so watch for slick spots on elevated roads early Saturday!

We’re back in the 40s for highs on Saturday. However, it should be a bit better than Friday, with a little more sunshine and slightly warmer temperatures.

High pressure brings a stretch of dry, sunny weather next week. Chilly mornings gradually give way to warmer afternoons. Highs climb through the 50s, reaching the lower 60s by late week.
