Malaysia assured of final spot in women’s doubles at HK Open

TheTools · 20.11.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

Pearly Tan-M Thinaah and Low Zi Yu-Noraqilah Maisarah Ramdan will clash in one of the semi-finals of the World Tour Super 500 event.

World No 5 Pearly-Thinaah continued their relentless march, dismantling the American duo of Lauren Lam-Allison Quynh Lee with a 21-18, 21-11 win in 37 minutes at the Kai Tak Arena.

The win marks their sixth semi-final appearance on the World Tour this year — a season that has already seen them lift the Super 500 Indonesia Masters in January and the Super 750 Denmark Open last month.

Standing in their way are their junior compatriots, teenage sensations Zi Yu-Noraqilah who have been making waves with their fearless performances, edging past US pair Francesca Corbett-Jennie Gai in the quarter-final 21-19, 16-21, 21-16 to set up a mouth-watering clash with their senior compatriots.

This will be the first meeting between the two pairs.

The winners of this all-Malaysian affair will face either Japan’s Sumire Nakade-Miyu Takahashi, or Indonesia’s Amalia Cahaya Pratiwi-Siti Fadia Silva Ramadhanti in the final.
