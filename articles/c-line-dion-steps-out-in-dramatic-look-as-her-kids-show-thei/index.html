Céline Dion steps out in dramatic look as her kids show their support ahead of long-awaited comeback

Céline Dion is preparing for one of the biggest moments of her career with her family firmly by her side. As the superstar gets ready for her highly anticipated return to the stage in Paris, her children have been accompanying her in the French capital, showing their support during an exciting new chapter.

On September 9, the 58-year-old singer stepped out of the Royal Monceau hotel on her way to rehearsals for her upcoming concert. Céline appeared to be in great spirits, smiling, waving and taking a few moments to greet the fans waiting outside.

The 'My Heart Will Go On' singer even formed a heart with her hands for the crowd before getting into her car, adding to the excitement surrounding her return.

And naturally, Céline turned the outing into a major fashion moment. The Canadian superstar wore a charcoal gray midi dress that put a modern spin on classic tailoring. 

The fashion-forward design featured structured shoulders, oversized short sleeves and dramatic draping through the skirt, creating an architectural silhouette as she walked.

She added an unexpected touch of contrast with long brown leather gloves and coordinating knee-high leather boots. Oversized dark sunglasses and a sleek black clutch completed the sophisticated ensemble.

Céline kept her beauty look just as polished, wearing her hair swept back into a sleek style that highlighted her features and allowed the dramatic proportions of her outfit to take center stage.

Her kids are by her side for the big moment:

Céline isn't experiencing this exciting chapter alone. Her sons, René-Charles Angélil and twins Nelson and Eddy, whom she shares with her late husband René Angélil, have been spotted accompanying their mom during her time in Paris.

Their presence makes the countdown particularly meaningful. Céline has frequently spoken about the importance of her children, who have remained close to her as she navigated difficult years away from regular performing.

The singer revealed in December 2022 that she had been diagnosed with stiff-person syndrome, a rare neurological disorder that affected her ability to perform and ultimately led her to cancel the remaining dates of her Courage World Tour.

Despite those challenges, Céline made an unforgettable return to the spotlight at the 2024 Paris Olympics, performing Édith Piaf's "Hymne à l'amour" from the Eiffel Tower during the Opening Ceremony.

Now, Paris is once again serving as the backdrop for another important milestone in her journey. With rehearsals underway and her children nearby to support her, Céline's latest appearance captured a noticeably joyful moment in the countdown. 

Between the warm interaction with fans, her bold fashion choices, and the anticipation surrounding her return, the music icon looked ready to embrace what's next.
