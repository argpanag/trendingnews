TOP 5 RMELE: September 2026 Registered Master Electrician Licensure Exam Results

TheTools · 14.09.2026 12:11 · source · permalink

RMELE TOPNOTCHERS – The list of the top 10 passers in the September 2026 Registered Master Electrician Licensure Exam (RMELE) is available on this website as the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) Board of Electrical Engineering releases the full results five (5) working days after the exams.

The Registered Master Electrician Licensure Examination was conducted on September 7, 2026, at PRC testing centers in NCR, Baguio, Butuan, Cagayan de Oro, Calapan, Cebu, Davao, Iloilo, Legazpi, Lucena, Pagadian, Palawan, Pampanga, Rosales, Tacloban, Tuguegarao, and Zamboanga.

The Board of Electrical Engineering is headed by its Chairman, Engr. Adelino V. Garcia Jr., and its members, Engr. Jaime V. Mendoza and Engr. Alex C. Cabugao.

Release of Results

According to the Professional Regulation Commission’s official 2026 schedule of licensure examinations, the September 2026 Registered Master Electricians Licensure Examination (RMELE) results are targeted for release on September 10, 2026.

The RMELE was conducted on September 7, 2026. However, the actual release of results may occur earlier or later than the target date without prior notice.

In the previous RMELE held on April 23, 2026, PRC released the results on April 28, 2026. A total of 493 out of 882 examinees (55.90%) passed the April 2026 Registered Master Electricians Licensure Examination.

We advise examinees to bookmark this page and stay connected with us on our social media channels for the latest and most accurate result announcements.

Schedule of Registration

The schedule for online appointments to issue Professional Identification Cards (ID) and Certificates of Registration to new Registered Master Electricians was announced after the release of the September 2026 RMELE results.

Date of Registration: Starting October 12, 2026

PRC advises successful examinees to register through its official online services portal at https://online.prc.gov.ph/. Registrants are generally required to prepare the following:

  1. Notice of Admission (NOA) – for identification purposes only
  2. Completed Oath Form (Panunumpa ng Propesyonal)
  3. Two (2) passport-sized ID photos on a white background, each with a complete name tag
  4. Two (2) sets of documentary stamps
  5. One (1) short brown envelope

Successful examinees should also follow any additional registration instructions that PRC may issue together with the official September 2026 results.

Verification of Rating

Online verification of ratings and examination results for the September 2026 Registered Master Electricians Licensure Examination will be available after the results are officially released.

Examinees may check their ratings through the official PRC Verification of Rating service.

To verify their results, examinees generally need to provide information such as the examination name, examination date, application number, first name, last name, and date of birth.

Oath-Taking Ceremonies

The PRC will announce the schedule and instructions for the oath-taking of the new Registered Master Electricians after the release of the September 2026 RMELE results.

Successful examinees who wish to attend a face-to-face mass oath-taking should follow the registration instructions and deadline stated in the official PRC announcement.

Those who cannot attend the face-to-face mass oath-taking may participate in a virtual oath-taking or request a special oath-taking ceremony, subject to PRC’s schedules and guidelines.

For virtual oath-taking, passers may secure an online appointment through https://online.prc.gov.ph/ and select “e-OATH” as the transaction when a schedule becomes available.

After completing the oath-taking requirement, successful examinees may proceed with their Initial Registration according to PRC’s instructions.
