CHINA-HONG KONG-TUNG CHEE-HWA-PUBLIC MEMORIAL (CN)

TheTools · 20.09.2026 18:12 · source · permalink

(260920) -- HONG KONG, Sept. 20, 2026 (Xinhua) -- Shi Taifeng, a member of the Political Bureau of the Communist Party of China Central Committee and vice chairman of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference (CPPCC) National Committee, delivers a speech at a public memorial for Tung Chee-hwa, former vice chairman of the National Committee of the CPPCC and former chief executive of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (HKSAR), in Hong Kong, south China, Sept. 20, 2026. Tung died of illness at the age of 89 in Hong Kong on Sept. 8. He is regarded as an outstanding contributor to "one country, two systems," a renowned patriot, an outstanding social activist and a close friend of the Communist Party of China (CPC).

Tung served as vice chairman of the 10th, 11th, 12th and 13th National Committees of the CPPCC. He was the first- and second-term chief executive of the HKSAR. (Xinhua/Chen Duo)
