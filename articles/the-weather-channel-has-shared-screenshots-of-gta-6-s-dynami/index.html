The Weather Channel Has Shared Screenshots of GTA 6's Dynamic Weather

TheTools · 17.11.2026 11:48 · source · permalink

The new screenshots show heavy rain, storms, sea fog, and mist in Leonida.

Rockstar

GTA 6 will feature an immensely detailed, realistic world with over 170 animal species and NPCs with more than 600,000 animations, plus a lifelike weather system. Rockstar has teamed up with The Weather Channel and offered the weather provider an exclusive look at the game's dynamic weather.

The Weather Channel has shared several screenshots of GTA 6's Leonida, highlighting various atmospheric phenomena – heavy rain, storms, sea fog, and mist, with lighting and atmospheric effects that appear photorealistic.

"Nobody knows weather like we do. That's why [Rockstar Games] gave us an exclusive look at the forecast for Leonida and the dynamic weather in GTAVI," reads the post.

Owen Shepherd, VP of Art: Light and Rendering at Rockstar Games, explained that Florida, which served as the inspiration for GTA 6's Leonida, has one of the most unpredictable weather systems in the US:

"To match the fidelity of all aspects of what is the deepest, most immersive take on the Grand Theft Auto series yet, we had to completely reimagine our in-game weather systems to feel more localized, persistent, and above all dynamic. Weather can range with everything from clear blue skies to towering vertiginous cumulonimbus clouds, grounding players in a world through weather we hope Florida locals – and new visitors to Leonida – can appreciate."

Beyond the vibrant world and visuals not seen in previous GTA games, the new installment will have a slightly different tone and will encourage kinder, more empathetic player behavior.

Many people have prepared for the launch by taking time off, and some companies have even given their employees days off specifically to play GTA 6: Palworld developer Pocketpair is giving its employees five days off, and Logitech gave its employees a day off, calling it Logi Game Day. Xbox recently collaborated with DJ Khaled, who, as Dr. XBOX, gives players "medical advice" to take a day off and issues personalized prescriptions.
