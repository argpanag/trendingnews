Epic Games Store Officially Makes a Top Co-op Game Free for a Limited Time

TheTools · 26.11.2026 16:11 · source · permalink

100% off

Thanksgiving is today in the US, and what better way to spend time with your family and friends than by playing video games? For a limited time, the Epic Games Store has a great co-op PC game for free as well as free content for an idle RPG.

Bread and Fred

Bread and Fred is a challenging platformer that will put you and your gaming partner's communication to the test. Penguins Bread and Fred are attached to one another, and players will have to use their momentum to swing from platform to platform as they try to climb a mountain without falling. Unlike some co-op-centric games, Bread and Fred also includes a single-player mode. Instead of a fellow penguin, your character will be attached to a rock. Its name is Jeff.

Firestone — Idle Clicker Online RPG

Idle games are becoming more and more popular, and Firestone is an idle RPG where you assemble a group of fantasy heroes who never stop fighting on your behalf. It's free-to-play, and Epic is offering a $100 bundle of cosmetics and in-game currency for free. It includes the following:

  • Exclusive skin “The Gladiator” for hero Talia
  • Two avatars for your character profile
  • 10 Common chests, each containing at least 1 piece of Hero’s gear
  • Five Uncommon chests containing at least 1 Uncommon gear
  • 10 Wooden chests with at least 1 piece of Hero’s jewels
  • Five Iron chests with at least 1 Uncommon jewel
  • 360 Strange dust to train your Guardians or conduct Experiments
  • 50 scrolls of each type to enhance your stats in battles
  • 35 Game tokens to test your luck at the Tavern
  • Five Temple's tokens, to perform Epic empowerments
  • 200 gems

If Bread and Fred and free content for Firestone catch your eye, all you need to do is log in to the Epic Games Store and claim them. They'll be free until 11 a.m. EST on Thursday, Dec. 3.
