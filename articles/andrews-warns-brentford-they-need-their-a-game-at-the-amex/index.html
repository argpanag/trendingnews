Andrews warns Brentford they need their ‘A game’ at the Amex

TheTools · 08.11.2026 07:03 · source · permalink

Brentford boss Keith Andrews has warned his side will need to take their “A game” to Brighton on Sunday.

The Seagulls have emerged as this season’s surprise package and currently lie third in the Premier League.

Brentford are not far behind in sixth, and Andrews is expecting a tough test of his side’s European credentials.

“I think they’re playing really, really well,” he said at a press conference.

“The way they’re organised in every facet of the game is very impressive.

“They’ve clearly got really good spirit, they’ve recruited well, they’ve got an honesty around the group and they’re clearly playing with a lot of confidence.
