Pantaloni: "We're playing the best team in the world"

TheTools · 21.11.2026 17:16 · source · permalink

Nice – Paris

Ahead of the home match against PSG (Matchday 11, Saturday at 5.15pm), Olivier Pantaloni has "almost the whole" squad at his disposal, with the exception of Mohamed Abdelmonem and the long-term injured players, Moïse Bombito, Laurent Abergel and Antoine Mendy. Here are some highlights from the Aiglons’ manager’s press conference.

WHAT'S THE GAME PLAN FOR PSG?

“We’re on a good run. That said, they’re perhaps still the best team in the world. In any case, we’re going to give it our best shot. How? First and foremost, we’ll need to be very solid defensively. We’ve reviewed footage of our matches – against Strasbourg, Lille and Rennes – where we occasionally sat a little deeper, but remained very compact and disciplined, denying our opponents any opportunities.

Like most clubs in Europe, we’ll have to get it into our heads that this is the way to get a result against PSG.

Going forward, we’ll also need to cause them problems, and in that regard, we’ve been trying to put certain things in place, which I’ll keep to myself. I hope we’ll manage to find some solutions.”

“We’re keeping our feet on the ground”

Whilst his team has the second-best record in Ligue 1 since the September international break, behind the Parisian leaders, the manager emphasised that every performance depends on a serious and humble approach: “I hear a lot of talk about Europe. We’re not even a third of the way through the season! We had a very difficult start, but we’re on a good run now: let’s try to build on that, and then later, perhaps, we can talk about other things. For now, we’re keeping our feet on the ground. I’d like to remind everyone that we’ve just come through a difficult period. We had to get over it. We had to work hard to achieve the results we’re seeing today. We’re up against the best team in the world. We’ll try to put in a good performance. We want to be able to compete with this team.”

Emphasising the importance of the support of the red-and-black fans to keep picking up points at the Allianz Riviera: “(Staying unbeaten at home) That could be a target. It would be a massive achievement just to manage to stay unbeaten against PSG. It could be an interesting goal for us and an extra source of motivation, especially as the stadium will be full for this match. So perhaps that will encourage the fans to keep coming to support us right to the end.”
