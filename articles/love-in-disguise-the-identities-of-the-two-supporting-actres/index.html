Love in Disguise: The identities of the two supporting actresses paired with Seol In Ah, regardless of the male lead's jealousy, have been revealed.

TheTools · 10.11.2026 16:58 · source · permalink

HHTO - Seol In Ah exudes such "boyfriend charm" in "Love in Disguise" that she not only looks great alongside male lead Im Si Wan but also creates a fiery chemistry with the supporting actress behind the scenes.

Love in Disguise focuses on Kang Jae Hee (Seol In Ah), who disguises herself as a man to become Yoon Yi Jun's (Im Si Wan) secretary while investigating a major case. She gradually gains his trust, eliminates him from the list of prime suspects, and uncovers evidence leading to a larger secret about the Taekang Group. In the process of protecting Yi Jun, she unintentionally "steals" the CEO's heart, creating many humorous situations.

Seol In Ah's portrayal of a man doesn't feel artificial or forced. Like the other characters in the drama, viewers are instantly captivated by Jae Hee's strength, gallantry, and handsomeness. Seol In Ah shows off great chemistry with the effeminate Im Si Wan (Yi Jun), and displays explosive "boyfriend skills" with the supporting actress despite their limited scenes together.

Hye Jung (Yeonwoo) has a romantic storyline with Kim Si Hyun, but she's also being "shipped" with Kang Jae Hee. In the latest episode, Lee Si A, Yoon Yi Jun's fiancée, appears, but there are absolutely no romantic scenes with him. Instead, she becomes the object of his jealousy due to her intimacy and flirting with Jae Hee. Viewers jokingly say they feel "jealous on Yi Jun's behalf." A few minutes of screen time together, or even a few seconds of behind-the-scenes footage with the two supporting actresses, is enough material for fans to come up with "1001" romantic scenarios.

Besides being "sweet" with Seol In Ah, the two supporting actresses of Love in Disguise are also loved by viewers for their outstanding charisma on screen and in real life. The goddess Tae Kang - Lee Hye Jung is played by Yeonwoo, a former member of MOMOLAND. Her career remains uncertain. Yeonwoo transitioned into acting in 2018 and is currently working to improve her acting skills in supporting roles.

Choi Hee Jin, born in 1996, plays the role of Lee Si A. She went viral last year with a scene from "More Beautiful Than Heaven " thanks to her perfect portrayal of a cute cat transforming into a human. Hee Jin's haughty and coquettish demeanor even overshadowed the female lead in " The Impossible Heir ." Hee Jin is expected to be a new generation beauty on screen thanks to her bright appearance and solid acting. After nearly 10 years of acting, she is slowly making her mark on audiences through supporting roles, lacking only a truly impactful and memorable leading role.
