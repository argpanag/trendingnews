What time do the Packers play?

  • Green Bay Packers vs. Atlanta Falcons
  • When: Thursday, Sept. 24 at 7:15 p.m. CT
  • Where: Lambeau Field

How can I watch the Packers on TV?

The game will be broadcast across the country on Prime Video. If you live in the Green Bay market, it will also air on free, over-the-air television on NBC26 (WGBA), and if you live in the Milwaukee market, it will be shown on FOX6 (WITI). It will be available on mobile devices with NFL+ as well.

  • Play-by-play man Al Michaels joins analyst Kirk Herbstreit and sideline reporter Kaylee Hartung.

How can I listen to the Packers on the radio?

All Packers games can be heard on the Packers Radio Network, which is made up of 54 stations in Wisconsin, Michigan, Minnesota, Illinois, North Dakota and South Dakota. The flagship station of the Packers Radio Network is 95.7 BIG FM - WRIT. For a map and full listing of radio affiliates, go to packers.com/video/radio-network. Wayne Larrivee (play-by-play), two-time Packers Pro Bowler Larry McCarren (analyst), and three-time Packers Pro Bowler John Kuhn (sideline reporter) call the action. McCarren first joined the team's broadcasts in 1995 and enters his 32nd season calling Packers games. After originally being paired together in 1999, McCarren and Larrivee enter their 28th season of broadcasts together. They surpassed Jim Irwin and Max McGee for the most regular-season and postseason games broadcast for the Packers with 313 at the 2018 season opener against Chicago.

The radio broadcast can also be heard on Sirius XM NFL Radio on Channel 192 or in the app (Packers Radio Network feed).

Westwood One will be broadcasting on radio to a national audience with Kevin Kugler (play-by-play) and Derek Rackley (analyst) on the call and studio host Scott Graham.

How can I stream the Packers game?

  • Packers.com will stream the team's official radio broadcast worldwide to desktop users.
  • The Packers mobile app will also stream the radio broadcast within the Packers' home market, per NFL broadcast restrictions. The broadcast will start with the pre-game show at 5:05 p.m. CT.
