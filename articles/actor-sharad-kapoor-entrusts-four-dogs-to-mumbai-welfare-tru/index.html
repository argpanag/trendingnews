Actor Sharad Kapoor entrusts four dogs to Mumbai welfare trust

TheTools · 13.11.2026 14:12 · source · permalink

Actor Sharad Kapoor, known for his negative role in Shah Rukh Khan’s 2000 film Josh, became emotional after handing over his four dogs to an animal welfare centre in Mumbai. Kapoor entrusted the dogs to the Bezubaan Public Welfare Trust following a challenging period in his life.

In a video released by the non-governmental organisation, Kapoor is seen giving his dogs to the trust’s founder, Hema Choudhary. He said, “Hi, I’m going to give my dog, with my consent, to Hema, and when I change my house, I will get them, so they will come to me. So thank you so much.”

The NGO shared the clip on social media, stating: “Just now took Sharad Kapoor four beloved dogs to my shelter with his very much conceit he will get back soon and I’ll make sure he reunites with his kids soon he was a gem of a human he still is we should all come up together and help him to pass this difficult time he is my friend and I’ll stand by him forever.”

Sharad Kapoor began his Bollywood career with the 1996 film Dastak and subsequently appeared in notable movies such as Josh, Lakshya, and Jai Ho. His role in Josh earned him a Filmfare Award nomination for Best Actor in a Negative Role.
