A warm afternoon, pleasant weather continues

TheTools · 21.10.2026 10:58 · source · permalink

OTTUMWA, Iowa (KYOU) - Plan on another quiet, pleasant fall day.

We will see another sunny day with highs climbing into the mid to upper 70s and light winds out of the south. Lows tonight fall into the upper 40s and low 50s under mostly clear skies with light winds.

The rest of the week will continue to see pleasant weather with highs in the upper 70s to low 80s, lows in the low to mid 50s and plenty of sunshine with light winds out of the south.

The weekend brings more of the same, with highs in the upper 70s to low 80s and lows in the 50s. Skies are partly cloudy as southerly winds continue to keep temperatures mild.

Changes in the weather pattern arrive next week. Cooler air will work its way into the area. As it does, we will see shower chances return. The current forecast brings showers in Monday through Wednesday. Temperatures drop into the mid to upper 60s for highs with lows in the upper 40s to low 50s.
