Juan Mata announces retirement aged 38

TheTools · 18.11.2026 10:07 · source · permalink

Former Manchester United midfielder Juan Mata has announced his retirement from football. Pic: Martin Rickett/Press Association.

Former Manchester United midfielder Juan Mata has announced his retirement from football.

The 38-year-old was part of the Spain squad which won the World Cup in 2010 and European Championship in 2012, and played 285 matches in all competitions for United between 2014 and 2022.

He won the FA Cup with the Red Devils in 2016 and the Europa League the year after, having also won the FA Cup and Champions League with Chelsea in 2012.

Mata posted a video of him hanging up his boots on social media site X, and wrote: “Sometimes to love…is to let go. Thanks football.”

Mata co-founded the Common Goal initiative in 2017, which encourages those in the football industry to pledge one per cent of their earnings to community projects.

He broke through into senior football with Valencia before joining Chelsea in 2011. After leaving United he joined Turkish side Galatasaray for the 2022-23 season before spending a year in Japan with Vissel Kobe.

He spent the two most recent seasons in Australia with Western Sydney Wanderers and Melbourne Victory.
