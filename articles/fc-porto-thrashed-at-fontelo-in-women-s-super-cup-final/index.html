FC Porto thrashed at Fontelo in Women’s Super Cup final

Benfica won the Women’s Super Cup this Sunday for the fourth time, thrashing FC Porto 5-1 at the Estádio Municipal do Fontelo in Viseu, in Porto’s debut in the competition.

The team in red opened the scoring in the 13th minute through Norway’s Froia Dorsin. FC Porto equalized in the 33rd minute, with Fátima Pinto converting a penalty, but Benfica went back in front before half-time thanks to a goal from Brazil’s Nycole Raysla in the 42nd minute.

OneFootball Videos

In the second half, the Lisbon side sealed the win with three more goals: Marit Lund scored from the penalty spot in the 57th minute, Caroline Moller extended the lead in the 66th, and Anna Csiki completed the scoring in the 81st.

Benfica, six-time national champions and winners of the Portuguese Cup, succeed Torreense as holders of the Super Cup and now lead the competition’s honors list outright with four trophies, one more than Sporting.
