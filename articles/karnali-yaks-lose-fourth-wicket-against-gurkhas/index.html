Karnali Yaks Lose Fourth Wicket Against Gurkhas

TheTools · 02.11.2026 18:01 · source · permalink

Kathmandu. Karnali Yaks lost their fourth wicket for 80 runs in 9.3 overs against Gurkhas. Mark Watt was out for 17 runs off the ball of Dev Shah in the ongoing match at TU Cricket Ground.

Ariful Islam scored a maximum of 44 runs for Karnali. Zishan Alam was out for 7 and Deepak Dumre for 8. Pawan Sarraf is at the crease with 2 runs and Hussen Talat with 1 run.

Shahab Alam, Karan KC, Santosh Yadav and Dev Shah took one wicket each for Kathmandu, who chose to bowl first after winning the toss.
