'It was tough': Fox stays in contention in pinnacle European tournament

TheTools · 20.09.2026 18:11 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

Ryan Fox will go into the final round of the BMW PGA Championship well in contention, sitting three shots off the lead.

The Kiwi golfer sat in a tie for 11th after three rounds at 10-under-par after carding a two-under 70 in a tricky penultimate round at the Wentworth Club in Surrey, UK.

Playing alongside Northern Irishman Rory McIlroy for the third time in as many rounds of the DP World Tour’s pinnacle tournament, Fox made a clutch birdie on the par-five 18th to set himself up for a big finish to the tournament.

“It was tough. The wind was so gusty. [I] felt like I hit a lot of decent shots that didn’t end up where I thought they were going to end up,” he said of his round.

“I made a couple of nice par putts to keep it going on the front nine and then when you feel like you can score on [the] back nine, it just became hard to hit the ball close. I’m glad I snuck one in there on the last, even though I didn’t hit a very good second shot. I’m still in the hunt, at least.”

Italian Filippo Celli and Spaniard Manuel Elvira held the joint lead at 13-under going into the final round.

The tournament in Surrey has fond memories for Fox, who won the event in 2023. It was a win that went a long way in Fox securing a fulltime card on the PGA Tour for the following season as one of the top 10 players on the DP World Tour at the end of the campaign.

The 39-year-old will tee off alongside Englishman Matt Fitzpatrick in his final round of the tournament as he tries to chase down another tournament win.

Follow Kiwis Daniel Hillier and Kazuma Kobori were further back in the field, with Hillier sitting in a tie for 34th at six-under and Kobori one shot back in a tie for 43rd.
