Scottsdale Police arrest suspect in Valley-wide residential burglary investigation - Scottsdale Independent

TheTools · 08.10.2026 00:54 · source · permalink

Scottsdale Police detectives have arrested 41-year-old Tyler Johnston following an extensive investigation into a series of residential burglaries occurring across Scottsdale and other Valley communities over the past several months.

Detectives identified a pattern among numerous residential burglaries involving homes whose occupants were away at the time, according to a press release from the Scottsdale Police Department.

In several cases, the suspect disabled the power to the residence before forcing entry through a rear door or window, according to police.
