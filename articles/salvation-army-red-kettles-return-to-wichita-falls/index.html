The iconic red kettles have returned to Wichita Falls for the holidays as The Salvation Army gets underway with its national Red Kettle Campaign.

The long-time tradition is meant to represent a symbol of hope, generosity, and compassion for the community, officials said. The campaign helps raise funds for vital programs that serve thousands of individuals and families in need.

The Salvation Army's national campaign encourages community members to Give With Joy this holiday season and help uplift families and individuals in need.

The donations provide shelter, food, bill assistance and help with other hardships.

From November until Christmas Eve, red kettles will be stationed outside various stores and local businesses in Wichita Falls, Monday through Saturday.

Last year, the Salvation Army raised over $118,000 through the campaign and looks to raise over $125,000 this season for the community.

Donations can be made at the red kettle stations, by visiting The Salvation Army website at www.salvationarmywichitafalls.org or by phone at 940-322-9822.
