Susan Dell has been facing intense scrutiny on social media due to her facial appearance.

She joined her husband at Oval Office

Expert speaks out on Susan's look

After rumors emerged about Susan's appearance last year, we contacted Dr Ross Perry, Medical Director of Cosmedics skin clinics, to help shed light on the possible reasons behind her noticeably different look.

"Susan has sparked widespread speculation about whether she may have undergone cosmetic treatments," the medical director explained. "In comparison to earlier photographs, her face appears noticeably tighter and tauter, and her eye area looks more open than usual. Several aesthetic procedures, surgical or non-surgical, could create this type of result."

"There is a possibility she may have undergone a full facelift, as the skin around the jawline and mid-face appears lifted," he noted.

He continued, "Another possibility is upper blepharoplasty (eyelid surgery), which can make the eyes look more open, refreshed, and awake, an effect that looks likely in the recent images."

The expert further observed, "However, non-surgical treatments can also achieve significant changes. Botox can smooth forehead and frown lines, while dermal fillers in the cheeks or tear troughs can restore lost volume and create a more youthful contour.

"In some cases, overuse of injectables, such as excessive Botox around the eyes or too much filler in the mid-face, can even contribute to a slightly 'wide-eyed' or startled appearance."

Sharing how much procedures might cost, he said, "If procedures have been performed, costs can vary widely: a full facelift can reach up to $40,000, eyelid surgery around $8,000, Botox typically ranges from $300 to $800, and fillers from $600 to $1,500."

Cosmetic surgeon shares his thoughts

We also sought the expertise of Dr. Gerard Lambe, cosmetic surgeon and founder of the Reflect Clinic, who supported Dr. Ross's assessment.

"Based on recent public appearances and images, Susan Dell appears to have invested significantly in facial rejuvenation procedures," he said. "Without examining her personally, I can only offer professional observations about what treatments might contribute to such changes."

"Possible procedures observed include extensive facial fillers, particularly in the cheek and mid-face area, creating increased volume at $3,000-5,000 per session with likely multiple sessions, possible fat transfer to face for volume restoration at $8,000-12,000, likely upper and lower blepharoplasty at $8,000-12,000, potential facelift or mini-facelift procedure at $15,000-25,000, regular Botox treatments at $800-1,200 per session, and possible lip augmentation at $800-1,500," he noted.

"Total estimated investment could be $50,000-100,000 or more over time," the surgeon cautioned, adding, "The most noticeable aspect appears to be overfilling syndrome - when too much volume is added to the face, particularly in the mid-face area. This can create what we call 'pillow face' where the natural contours are lost."

He went on to say, "Additionally, there may be overcorrection of volume loss leading to an unnatural fullness, possible filler migration over time, loss of natural facial proportions, and the challenge of treating mature skin that may not respond as predictably."

Susan hasn't confirmed the speculation

Susan has not publicly addressed or responded to the speculation regarding her appearance.

There has been no confirmation or denial about the cosmetic procedure rumors.

Billionaire tech CEO's wife is a philanthropist

Susan is a prominent philanthropist, investor, and former fashion designer best known as the co-founder and board chair of the Michael & Susan Dell Foundation.

Alongside her husband, she has directed billions of dollars toward global initiatives in education, healthcare, and economic mobility.

The Michael & Susan Dell Foundation, established by the couple in 1999, manages hundreds of active projects annually across the United States, India, South Africa, and Israel.

To date, the Dells have committed approximately $10 billion to non-profits and social enterprise

Susan was inducted into Texas Women's Hall of Fame

In January 2019, Susan was inducted into the Texas Women's Hall of Fame as a member of the 2018 class of honorees.

Selected by the Office of the Texas Governor and the Governor's Commission for Women, she was recognized alongside notable figures like former first lady Laura Bush and Olympic gymnast Simone Biles.

Susan was honored for her philantrophic work and her long-standing dedication to improving education, childhood health, and economic stability for children living in urban poverty.
