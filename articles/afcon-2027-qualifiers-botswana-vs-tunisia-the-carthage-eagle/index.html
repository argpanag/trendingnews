AFCON 2027 Qualifiers/ Botswana vs Tunisia: The Carthage Eagles hold the edge

TheTools · 28.09.2026 11:41 · source · permalink

Botswana and Tunisia meet this Monday, September 28, 2026, in the second round of the 2027 AFCON qualifiers. The two nations have faced each other nine times, with Tunisia holding a narrow advantage in their overall head-to-head record. The Carthage Eagles have won four games, while Botswana have claimed three victories, with two draws.

Tunisia dominated the first two encounters, winning 4-1 in 2004 and 3-1 in Botswana in 2005. The Zebras then produced two memorable victories in 2010, winning 1-0 in Tunis before repeating the score at home several months later. Tunisia responded with a 2-1 win in 2014, followed by two goalless draws in November 2014 and June 2022.

The most recent official meeting ended in another convincing Tunisia victory. The Carthage Eagles won 3-0 in the 2023 AFCON qualifiers, with an own goal from Adem Velaphi and a late brace from Youssef Msakni.

Tunisia also won the teams’ December 2025 friendly 2-1. Across their nine meetings, Tunisia have scored 14 goals compared with Botswana’s seven, giving the North Africans the historical edge ahead of Monday’s clash.
