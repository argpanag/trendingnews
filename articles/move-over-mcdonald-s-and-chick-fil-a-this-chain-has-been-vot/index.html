  • Biscuitville, a chain with just 90 locations across three states, was voted to have the best fast-food breakfast in the business.

As the saying goes, breakfast is the most important meal of the day, and now more than ever, fast-food chains are all getting on the breakfast game. From McDonald's to Chick-fil-A to Taco Bell, the popular restaurants offer quick bites on the go. 

That being said, USA Today recently released its list of the 10 Best Fast Food Restaurants for Breakfast. While two of the three restaurants already mentioned made the cut, in what may come as a shocker for some, the chain voted to have the best breakfast has just 90 locations across three states. 

McDonald's and Chick-fil-A Miss the Top 3

Photo by Sundry Photography on Getty Images

Despite McDonald's and Chick-fil-A being two of the most lucrative fast-food chains in the country, they both missed out on rounding out the top 3 on USA Today's list, placing at four and five, respectively. Out of all the businesses in the top 10, McDonald's in particular seems like Old Faithful. Millions upon millions have at least tried their famous pancakes, hash browns, and sausage biscuits. Heck, the McMuffin is legendary. 

Then there's Chick-fil-A. The chain specializes in chicken sandwiches. Furthermore, from a customer service perspective, the employees have made it their business to provide fast service (even when the drive-thru lines look rather lengthy).  

Biscuitville Reigns Supreme

Biscuits (Photo Credit: Unsplash/Jodie Morgan )Photo Credit: Unsplash/Jodie Morgan

Unless you've been to North Carolina, South Carolina, or Virginia, you may have never even heard of Biscuitville. However, according to USA Today, it has the best fast-food breakfast in the business. Per the publication's article: 

"Biscuitville's fresh-baked biscuits can be enjoyed a number of ways, from simple bacon, egg, and cheese or fried chicken biscuits to more elaborate breakfast sandwiches like their Spicy Chicken & Honey Biscuit. With breakfast platters and sides of grits, hash browns, or country gravy, plus indulgent sweet treats like their blueberry muffin, it's no wonder folks return again and again."

Now, if I may add my two cents, having been raised predominantly in Virginia, I can attest to the fact that the biscuits at Biscuitville are damn good and breakfast is the restaurant's speciality.
