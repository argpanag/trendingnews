Hungarian authorities recover $19.5 million in cash during investigation into Orbán-era minister

TheTools · 16.11.2026 20:10 · source · permalink

Hungarian authorities said Friday that they'd recovered about $19.5 million in hidden cash, some of which they found buried underground, as part of an investigation into an ex-minister who served under former Prime Minister Viktor Orbán. Orbán has decried the investigations and detentions as political "tyranny" designed to sweep him off the political playing field.
