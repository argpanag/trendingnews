Lonzo Ball Retires From NBA At Age 28 | KFI AM 640

TheTools · 23.09.2026 16:25 · source · permalink

Photo: Tyler Clouse / Getty Images Sport / Getty Images

Lonzo Ball, a 28-year-old NBA player, has announced his retirement from professional basketball after nine seasons. Ball shared his decision on the Family Podcast, stating that he has given all he could to the game. Drafted second overall by the Los Angeles Lakers in 2017, Ball was named to the All-Rookie Team in his debut season.

Ball's career was significantly impacted by knee injuries, which led to him missing two full seasons with the Chicago Bulls. Despite these setbacks, he played for the Los Angeles Lakers, New Orleans Pelicans, Chicago Bulls, and Cleveland Cavaliers, averaging 10.6 points and nearly six assists per game throughout his career.

Ball's journey in the NBA began with high expectations. He was drafted by the Lakers and later traded to the Pelicans in a multi-team deal involving notable players like Anthony Davis. His time with the Bulls was marked by promising performances, including a career-high 42.3% shooting from beyond the arc in his first 35 games with the team. However, persistent knee issues led to multiple surgeries, including a cartilage transplant, which ultimately sidelined him indefinitely.

In July 2023, the NBA granted the Bulls a $10.2 million disabled player exception for Ball, acknowledging the severity of his injuries. Despite efforts to return, Ball's knee problems persisted, leading to his decision to retire.

Ball's retirement marks the end of a career filled with potential and challenges. His contributions to the teams he played for, particularly in defense and playmaking, will be remembered by fans and teammates alike.
