Home Alone in Concert to bring Christmas magic to Dublin's 3Arena

TheTools · 07.09.2026 12:41 · source · permalink

Everyone's favourite Christmas movie 'Home Alone' is being brought to the 3Arena this December.

Home Alone in Concert takes place on December 20 and brings the Christmas classic to life on the big screen, accompanied by the RTÉ Concert Orchestra and Dublin Gospel Choir, followed by an exclusive live conversation with Home Alone star Macaulay Culkin, joining live from Los Angeles.

The complete film will be shown on the big screen while the iconic, Oscar-nominated score by legendary composer John Williams is performed live to picture. When the end credits roll, the evening will continue with a very special opportunity for fans to hear directly from the star of the film.

Macaulay Culkin will join live from Los Angeles for an exclusive conversation, sharing behind-the-scenes stories and memories from the making of Home Alone and his experience of playing the unforgettable Kevin McCallister. The conversation will be moderated live on stage at the 3Arena by Ruth Scott.

Speaking about the 3Arena event Ruth Scott said: “Home Alone is such a huge part of Christmas for so many of us, so it’s going to be a real treat to experience the film with a live orchestra and choir at 3Arena.

"I’m especially looking forward to chatting with Macaulay and hearing some of his memories from making such an iconic Christmas movie. It’s going to be a very special night.”

Tickets from €69.90 (subject to fees) go on sale Thursday 10 th September at 10am at Ticketmaster.ie

A Three+ Presale will take place from Tuesday 8th September at 10am, giving Three customers early access to tickets.
