The initial public offering (IPO) of Kanohar Electricals continued to attract strong investor demand on the last day of bidding, with the issue subscribed 90.59 times as of 5:06 pm on September 10, according to NSE data.

The Rs 1,055.74-crore public issue received bids for 1,05,92,46,623 equity shares against 1,16,93,326 shares on offer, translating into an overall subscription of 90.58 times. Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) led the demand, with their portion subscribed 215.37 times. The Non-Institutional Investors (NIIs) quota was subscribed 87.74 times, while the Retail Individual Investors (RIIs) portion was subscribed 20.50 times.

In the grey market, Kanohar Electricals shares were commanding a premium of 35.13 percent on September 10, according to market-tracking platform InvestorGain.

At the upper end of the IPO price band of Rs 632, a 35.13 percent GMP indicates an implied grey-market price of around Rs 854 per share. However, grey market premiums are unofficial and can change rapidly depending on market sentiment. They should not be treated as an assured listing gain or a reflection of the company's fundamentals.

The price band for the IPO has been fixed at Rs 601 to Rs 632 per equity share, with a face value of Rs 2 per share.

The minimum lot size is 23 shares, meaning retail investors bidding at the upper price band will need to invest Rs 14,536 for one lot. Bids can be placed in multiples of 23 shares thereafter.

Under the IPO reservation structure, up to 50 percent of the net issue is reserved for qualified institutional buyers, while non-institutional investors have an allocation of at least 15 percent. Retail investors have been allocated at least 35 percent of the offer.

Ahead of the IPO, Kanohar Electricals raised Rs 316.72 crore from anchor investors. The company allotted 50,11,424 equity shares to anchor investors at the upper price band of Rs 632 per share.

The anchor book saw participation from 42 financial institutions from India and overseas. Investors included Ashoka Whiteoak ICAV – Ashoka Whiteoak Emerging Markets Equity Fund, Allianz Global Investors Fund – Allianz India Equity, HSBC Global Investment Funds – Asia Ex Japan Equity Smaller Companies, VQ FasterCap Fund and HDFC Life Insurance Company.

The Rs 1,055.74-crore IPO comprises a fresh issue of up to Rs 300 crore and an offer-for-sale (OFS) of up to 1.2 crore shares. At the upper end of the price band, the OFS component is valued at Rs 756 crore and is being offered by promoter K Sons Family Trust.

The company plans to use Rs 64.1 crore from the fresh issue towards capital expenditure. The planned expenditure includes the purchase of machinery and equipment for its Gangol manufacturing facility, expansion and automation of backward integration facilities, civil construction and interior development of an office building, and sustainability initiatives.

Another Rs 155 crore from the fresh issue has been earmarked for incremental working capital requirements. The remaining proceeds will be used for general corporate purposes.

Nuvama Wealth Management and IIFL Capital Services are the book-running lead managers to the issue, while MUFG Intime India is the registrar.

The shares are expected to be listed on both the BSE and NSE on September 16.
