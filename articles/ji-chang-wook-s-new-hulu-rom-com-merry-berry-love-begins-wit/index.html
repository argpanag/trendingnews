  • Popular streamers are embracing K-Dramas and C-Dramas, with Netflix and Disney+/Hulu+ offering a variety of hit series like Our Sticky Love and Perfect Crown.
  • Disney+/Hulu+ is set to release the new Korea-Japan rom-com Merry Berry Love starring Ji Chang-wook and Mio Imada, with the premiere of two episodes on Wed, Oct. 7 at 9 a.m. ET and a total of 10 episodes dropping weekly on Wednesdays.
  • The cast of Merry Berry Love includes Ji Chang-wook as Lee Yu-bin and Mio Imada as Karin Shirahama, with the series streaming on Hulu in the US and internationally on Disney+.

With the rising popularity of K-Dramas and C-Drama, many popular streamers are jumping on the K-Drama wagon, searching for the next big hit. Netflix has carved out a niche with their library of K-Dramas from Our Sticky Love to the upcoming Take Charge of My Heart, while Disney+/Hulu+ had a bona fide hit on their hands with Perfect Crown. Disney+/Hulu looks to have another hit on their hands not only with the upcoming The Remarried Empress in November, but also with Merry Berry Love, a cross-cultural rom-com starring popular Korean actor Ji Chang-wook and Japanese actress Mio Imada.

Let's take a look at Merry Berry Love, including more about the cast and when you can start streaming the new Korea-Japan rom-com.

What Is Merry Berry Love about?

When star Korean spatial designer Lee Yu-bin (Ji) loses everything, he heads off to a Japanese island where he meets Karin (Imada), a resilient strawberry farmer. Hailing from different worlds, their lives collide in a big misunderstanding.

When does Merry Berry Love premiere?

Merry Berry Love premieres with two episodes on Wed, Oct. 7 at 9 a.m. ET. Two episodes drop weekly on Wednesdays on Disney+/Hulu.

There are 10 episodes of Merry Berry Love.

Who is in the cast of Merry Berry Love?

Ji Chang-wookHulu+

Ji Chang-wook as Lee Yu-bin

Ji Chang-wook is a popular Korean actor best known for his roles in K-Drama series such as Healer, Suspicious Partner, and The K2. He recently starred in the Netflix original series, The Scandal, which is based off the 18th century French novel, Dangerous Liaisons. His other credits include Backstreet Rookie, Smile Again, and Welcome to Samdal-ri.

Mio ImadaHulu

Mio Imada as Karin Shirahama

Mio Imada is a popular Japanese model/actress who starred in the live-action version of My Happy Marriage based on a popular manga series of the same name. The film takes place in an alternative reality where spirits and magic are real, but in decline, Miyo (Imada) is born without supernatural talent and is forced into an existence of servitude by her abusive stepmother. When Miyo finally comes of marriageable age, her hopes of being whisked away to a better life crumble after she discovers her fiancé is Kiyoka Kudou (Ren Meguro), a commander apparently so cold and cruel that his previous would-be brides all fled within three days of their engagements. With no home to return to, Miyo resigns herself to her fate—and soon finds that her pale and handsome husband-to-be is anything but the monster she expected. As they slowly open their hearts to each other, both realize the other may be their chance at finding true love and happiness.

Imada's other credits include the Japanese series Boys Over Flowers, Trillion Game, and the upcoming film, The Swan and the Bat.

Is Merry Berry Love streaming on Disney+ and Hulu?

Merry Berry Love will stream on Hulu in the US and internationally on Disney+.
