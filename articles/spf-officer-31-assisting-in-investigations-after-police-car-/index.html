SPF officer, 31, assisting in investigations after police car crashes into road divider in Geylang Bahru

TheTools · 11.10.2026 08:35 · source · permalink

Investigations are ongoing.

October 11, 2026, 02:19 PM

Photos from SGRV Front Man/Facebook and @ketomprakkampung/TikTok.

A police vehicle was involved in an accident along Geylang Bahru Road towards Bendemeer Road on Oct. 10.

In response to Mothership's queries, the police confirmed that the incident took place at around 7:45am that Saturday.

No injuries were reported, and the 31-year-old male officer who was driving the vehicle is currently assisting with investigations.

In photos of the incident shared on the Facebook group SGRV Front Man, the police vehicle appeared to have struck a green divider and mounted a kerb.

Several police officers were shown standing nearby.

A video of the aftermath was also uploaded onto TikTok.
