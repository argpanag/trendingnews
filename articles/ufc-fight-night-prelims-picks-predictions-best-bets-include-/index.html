UFC Fight Night Prelim Predictions

UFC Fight Night Prelim Best Bet: Frye Jr vs Harris Prediction

  • UFC Fight Night Prelim Pick #1: RJ Harris to win by KO/TKO (-133 at BetMGM)

Harris enters this contest with a commanding physical presence. Frye Jr has shown durability issues when pressured early in fights.

Taking Harris on the straight moneyline at -357 eats way too much juice for my liking, effectively killing our return on investment.

The KO/TKO prop is the mathematically sound angle here. Harris will look to land heavy shots and close the show inside the scheduled three rounds. Betting him to find the knockout at -133 offers a solid plus-EV return.

Kareckaite vs Gatto Prediction: Melissa Gatto Moneyline

  • UFC Fight Night Prelim Pick #2: Melissa Gatto Moneyline (-115 at BetMGM)

This is a close fight on the number, so Gatto is preferable to a heavier favorite whose price leaves less room for error.

The key betting question is whether Kareckaite can consistently turn her offense into winning exchanges; if Gatto keeps the fight organized and avoids giving away long stretches of control, she has the more reliable route to a decision.

I do not need to predict a finish to back Gatto. At -115, the price is reasonable for a competitive matchup, and her composure should help her in the late rounds. The play is Gatto’s moneyline, with a decision win the most likely script.

Zheleznyakova vs Pereira Prediction: Alice Pereira Moneyline

  • UFC Fight Night Prelim Pick #3: Alice Pereira Moneyline (-133 at BetMGM)

Pereira is a modest favorite, which makes this a better spot than laying a premium number on the early slate. I expect her to focus on clean, repeatable work rather than chase a finish: win the striking exchanges, limit unnecessary scrambles and make Zheleznyakova work to create momentum.

The underdog’s best chance is to turn the fight into a messy, high-variance contest. Pereira becomes the stronger side if she can maintain distance, stay disciplined and bank rounds without overextending.

At -133, I’m backing Pereira to control enough of the action to earn the decision.

Price thrives in dirty brawls and brings a relentless pace that consistently wilts younger prospects.

Shahbazyan has struggled with late-fight cardio in his recent outings. Price will happily eat early damage to drag his opponent into deep waters. Ribeiro vs Franco Prediction: Felipe Franco by KO/TKO

  • UFC Fight Night Prelim Pick #5: Felipe Franco to win by KO/TKO (-105 at BetMGM)

Franco is a massive -385 favorite against Ribeiro in this all-Brazilian light heavyweight clash. The oddsmakers clearly expect Franco to dictate the pace and secure an early finish.

The Goes the Distance “No” prop is priced at a staggering -714, signaling massive finish equity.

Surviving all three rounds is beyond Ribeiro’s wildest dreams here. Instead of laying that absurd juice on the moneyline, I am targeting Franco to win by knockout.

Ribeiro leaves his chin exposed during boxing exchanges, and Franco packs enough power to turn those defensive lapses into a highlight-reel finish.

Bonfim vs Prado Prediction: Francisco Prado Moneyline

  • UFC Fight Night Prelim Pick #6: Francisco Prado Moneyline (-143 at BetMGM)

Prado and Bonfim are scheduled for a highly anticipated lightweight clash. Prado sits as a moderate favorite, and I am fully comfortable backing him at this current price tag.

He possesses a distinct technical striking advantage and excellent distance management.

Bonfim is certainly dangerous, but his output has cratered in the later rounds of his recent fights. Prado has the exact tactical toolkit required to exploit those fading cardio reserves. I expect him to bank the early rounds and cruise to a clear victory.
