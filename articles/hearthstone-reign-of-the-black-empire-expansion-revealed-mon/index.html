Hearthstone: Reign of the Black Empire expansion revealed, Monk coming in 2027

TheTools · 12.09.2026 19:09 · source · permalink

Following some exciting reveals for StarCraft and Diablo, the BlizzCon 2026 Opening Ceremony moved on to what's next for Hearthstone. Hearthstone is going into the distant past for its next expansion, as it explores the cataclysmic clash between the Old Gods and the Titans with the Reign of the Black Empire expansion. However, that's not all. The long-awaited Monk class is also making its way to the game and will be available in time for the next Standard year.

Hearthstone: Reign of the Black Empire will introduce a handful of new keywords, including ones that tap into the omnipotent powers of the Old Gods and the Titans. The Assemble keyword can fuse two monsters together to create something entirely devastating. It can be used across 250 different cards, offering hundreds of new possibilities.

However, that's not all that's coming to Hearthstone. Aberrations are coming to Hearthstone Battlegrounds, which are cards that tap into the power of the Old Gods. These cards will be playable on the BlizzCon showfloor.

The Hearthstone: Reign of the Black Empire expansion is set to release on October 20. The new Monk class will be available in March 2027.
