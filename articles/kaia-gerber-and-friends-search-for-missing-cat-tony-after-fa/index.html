Kaia Gerber's cat is missing.

Gerber, daughter of Cindy Crawford and businessman Rande Gerber, and her friends are searching for her gray cat, Tony, after a difficult week. The model and actress's brother, Presley Gerber, died from a suspected overdose.

On Sept. 23, Gerber's boyfriend, actor Lewis Pullman, along with director Carissa Gallo and influencer Jake Shane, were seen hanging missing-cat posters, according to TMZ-published photos.

The group is photographed hugging while putting up the flyers in Los Angeles. They included an image of Tony sitting and the location where he was last seen. They also reveal that he has a tracker on his neck and state that the cat is "shy."

Gerber's brother died earlier this week at a rehab facility on Sept. 20, according to Los Angeles County Medical Examiner records obtained by People. He was 27.

After an autopsy was conducted on Monday, the Santa Monica Police Department wrote in a statement, "the cause and manner of death have been deferred, which means additional testing and/or studies were requested in order to make a determination."

A month before her brother passed, Gerber spoke to Vogue for the Aug. 11 cover interview. She opened up about Presley's "decade-long struggle with addiction." She shared her parent's Crawford and Rande see him as an "absolute teacher" of honesty.

"My parents for most of our lives were very, very private and didn't share anything — and now we have this absolute teacher, my brother, who says, 'I'm gonna be very human for everyone,'" she said. "There's only so much you can hide from the world."

If you or someone you know is struggling with addiction, help is available 24 hours a day through the Substance Abuse and Mental Health Hotline at 1-800-662-4357.
