Updates: Goodson has career day vs. Colts

TheTools · 09.11.2026 05:28 · source · permalink

Dallas Cowboys Updates

Nov. 8

Tyler Goodson

#32 RB

  • Height: 5-9
  • Weight: 197 lbs
  • College: Iowa

6:30 p.m. — Cowboys RB Tyler Goodson had a career day on Sunday against the Colts, picking up a career-high 77 yards on 12 carries, good for 6.4 yards per carry.

Goodson accomplished the feat against Indianapolis, who he played for from 2023-25.

"It was very exciting," Goodson said. "I came back here in the preseason when I was in Atlanta, played well here. And I just want to do the same thing because I just want to show this team and everybody in the league that I can play football and play at the running back position."

P.J. Locke

#1 S

  • Height: 5-10
  • Weight: 205 lbs
  • College: Texas

6:28 p.m. — Following the Cowboys' 27-23 win over the Colts, Cowboys coach Brian Schottenheimer announced that S P.J. Locke suffered a concussion, his second in as many weeks.

"Got him covered in prayers." Schottenheimer said postgame.

LB Dee Winters was evaluated for a concussion, but he was fine. WR George Pickens' injury is more the hamstring now than it is the shoulder injury, and Schottenheimer thinks this coming week will be similar to last week in the sense that Pickens will need to go through the whole week and the team will see where things stand after Friday's practice. AS for CB Joey Porter, Schottenheimer said he's dealing with a foot and ankle and something that has got to do with his heel. TE Jake Ferguson suffered a stinger initially, and then a knee injury that Schottenheimer said would get more imaging on Monday.

Caleb Downs

#13 DB

  • Height: 6-0
  • Weight: 210 lbs
  • College: Ohio State

6:25 p.m. — Rookie Cowboys DB Caleb Downs had quite the day on Sunday against his younger brother, Colts WR Josh Downs, and Indianapolis.

Downs recorded his second sack of the season, and the first interception of his NFL career to ice the game. The last Cowboy with a sack and interception in the same game was Eric Kendricks against the Browns in 2024.

Additionally, Downs is the first rookie with a sack and interception in the same game since Tommy Haynes in 1987, which was a lockout year.

1:43 p.m. -- The Cowboys officially placed OLB Malachi Lawrence on injured reserve ahead of their Week 9 matchup against the Colts.

Jalen Thompson

#34 S

  • Height: 5-11
  • Weight: 190 lbs
  • College: Washington State

12:30 p.m. - Just as the Cowboys were getting whole on defense from injury, they're forced to place Caleb Downs and PJ Locke in the league's concussion protocol following the Week 8 matchup against the Cardinals.

That strikes another blow to the safeties room because though Downs has mostly played slot corner, he has also been deployed to aid the ailing safety depth chart — one that is still without Jalen Thompson.

Thompson suffered a hamstring early in the season and has been rehabilitating the injury since. He qualified to have his 21-day practice window activated this week but, on Wednesday, head coach Brian Schottenheimer stated Thompson's window has not yet been opened.

The Cowboys do have the remainder of the week to change their mind, but Thompson was also without pads during the open portion of practice, a sign he may still not be ready for Week 9.

Nov. 3

Ryan Flournoy

#19 WR

  • Height: 6-1
  • Weight: 205 lbs
  • College: Southeast Missouri State

2:43 p.m. - With 32 teams in the NFL, one might assume every team in the league has that true WR1 that is the go-to receiver. And while every team is built differently, some with more of a run-first mentality and others that incorporate the tight end more in the offense, there are some interesting stats for Ryan Flournoy this year.

After eight games, Flournoy ranks third on the team with 342 yards, which is no real surprise, considering he was always expected to be behind CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens.

However, Lamb ranks third in the NFL with 709 yards and Pickens is 14th with 533. But Flournoy is 31st among all wide receivers with 342 yards. Shockingly enough, he's got more yards than Minnesota's Justin Jefferson, who has just 335 yards in six games.

In fact, Jefferson and the Vikings are among the seven teams in the NFL whose No. 1 wide receiver has less yards than Flournoy, who also has two touchdown catches this year. Flournoy needs 133 yards to surpass last year's career-high total of 475 yards for a season.

  • Height: 6-3
  • Weight: 205 lbs

5 p.m. - The Cowboys came out of Philadelphia with both a win and mostly intact, a rarity for Dallas this season.

That said, there are a few who are "nursing bumps and bruises" as described by head coach Brian Schottenheimer, and that includes Caleb Downs (ankle) being limited on Wednesday.

George Pickens continues to work through a shoulder injury and, now, a hamstring issue as well that caused him to be limited in practice; and Quinnen Williams is also nursing a minor hamstring issue. Neither KaVontae Turpin (ribs) nor Dee Winters (calf) participated in practice.

Brian Schottenheimer

Head Coach

  • College: Florida
  • Hometown: Denver, CO

5:21 p.m. - Aside from all of the years he's been coaching in the NFL, Brian Schottenheimer learned about criticism at an early age, getting a front-row seat to the coaching life with his dad, Marty Schottenheimer serving as an NFL head coach for over 20 years.

So when the Cowboys struggled on offense and his play-calling duties were even questioned, Schotty responded with his best effort of the season on Monday. Was it because of the criticism? Schotty said on Monday in his press conference he does his best to focus on the task at hand for himself and the players.

"We're motivated as a football team, I'm motivated as a head coach to get our guys playing to the standard that I know they're capable of playing. Intense game against a real rival," Schotty said. "I'll be honest I really do a good job of blocking you guys out.

"Remember you're talking to a guy that grew up for 25 years in a household with a head coach in the NFL. So, no, it was more just my focus was getting the team to be focused and motivated. And I think you saw that (Monday) night, so I'm proud of our group."
