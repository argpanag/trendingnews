A crazy idea about pensioners and the cost of living

TheTools · 06.10.2026 14:58 · source · permalink

Sir, – In 2025, the minimum wage increased by 5.5 per cent to €13.50 per hour, whereas the State pension rose by 4.3 per cent, adding €12 per week.

This year, the minimum wage has increased by a further 4.8 to €14.15 per hour while the reported proposal for Budget 2027 is a 3.3 per cent increase in the State pension, just €10 extra per week.

I had the crazy idea that the cost of living rises the same for pensioners as for everyone else – am I missing something in my dotage? – Yours, etc,

RONAN QUINLAN,

Rooskey,

Co Roscommon.
