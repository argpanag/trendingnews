BBC Radio 1 DJ Charlie Hedges set to play at Sunderland Apres-Ski party

TheTools · 13.10.2026 19:44 · source · permalink

Charlie Hedges (Image: Matt Crossick/PA Media Assignments)

An Apres-Ski party is returning to Sunderland, with Radio 1 DJ Charlie Hedges providing the bangers. Silksworth Sports Complex and Ski Slope is hosting its second Après-Ski Party on Friday, October 23 following the success of last year’s inaugural event.

Running from 5pm until 10pm, the evening will feature skiing, snowboarding, tubing, food and drink stalls and other winter activities, while Charlie will be keeping energy high with her party set. Charlie is best known for hosting BBC Radio 1’s Dance Anthems and performed at Radio 1’s Big Weekend in Sunderland in May.

Tickets are priced at £10, with under-fives getting in free. Everyone Active’s contract manager, Ian Bradgate, said: “We’re delighted to welcome Charlie Hedges back to Sunderland and know she will play a huge part in making this an amazing event.

“Whether you’re coming for the snow-style sports, the party atmosphere or just great food and music, our Après-Ski Party is the place to be. We will have something for all ages, abilities and interests and we can’t wait to see people from across the local community for a memorable night on the slopes.”

For more information, phone 0191 528 5101 or speak to colleagues in the centre. Tickets can be bought here.
