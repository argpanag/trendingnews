Mourinho Makes Clear What Can Make Vinícius “Untouchable”

José Mourinho does not believe in untouchable players. Not even when it comes to Vinícius Júnior, one of the biggest names at Real Madrid.


“The badge is untouchable,” Mourinho said when asked about the Brazilian’s role. He then made his position even clearer: “What makes players untouchable is not their status, the captain’s armband or how much was paid for them. What makes them untouchable is what they do on the pitch.”

The comments come at a particularly delicate moment for Vinícius. His quality and importance to Madrid are unquestioned, but recent weeks have also brought several incidents that have once again put both his behavior and performances under scrutiny.

A Season Surrounded by Controversy

The Brazilian is coming off a four-match LaLiga suspension after being sent off for pulling an opponent’s hair, followed by his reaction to the referee’s decision.

That has not been the only source of tension. Vinícius has also openly shown his frustration when substituted, with his reactions reigniting the debate over how he handles moments when things do not go his way.

Mourinho has repeatedly defended his players, but this time his message drew a clear line: a big name alone does not guarantee special treatment.


Mourinho Wants Everything Decided on the Pitch

The comment carries even more weight because Mourinho did not question Vinícius’ talent or suggest he is no longer important to the team.

His point was different: at Real Madrid, performance has to come before hierarchy, transfer value or a player’s standing inside the dressing room.

That also puts the responsibility back on the Brazilian. If he wants to remain a central figure, Mourinho expects the answer to come through his football rather than the status he has built over the years.

Vinícius Is Back Under the Spotlight

The timing could hardly be more demanding. While serving his suspension in LaLiga, Vinícius still has European matches to rediscover his best form and move past the noise surrounding him.

Mourinho, for now, does not appear willing to hand him anything. Nor is he writing him off. His message is much simpler: if Vinícius wants to be untouchable, he has to prove it on the pitch.

Mourinho’s Message to Vinícius

  • Mourinho rejected the idea that any Real Madrid player should be considered “untouchable.”
  • He said status, the captain’s armband and transfer value do not guarantee anyone a place.
  • For Mourinho, performances on the pitch are what can truly make a player untouchable.
