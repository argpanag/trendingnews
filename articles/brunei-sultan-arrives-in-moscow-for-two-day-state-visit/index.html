Brunei Sultan arrives in Moscow for two-day state visit

TheTools · 25.11.2026 15:02 · source · permalink
    bookmark

MOSCOW: Sultan Hassanal Bolkiah arrived in Moscow, Russian Federation, on Tuesday (Nov 24) for a two-day State Visit.

The visit, from November 24 to 25, is at the invitation of Russian President Vladimir Putin.

Accompanying His Majesty is Prince ‘Abdul Mateen, Minister of Foreign Affairs.

Upon arrival at Moscow Vnukovo 2 Airport, His Majesty was welcomed by Russian Minister of Science and Higher Education Valery Falkov, Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs Andrey Rudenko and Russian Ambassador to Brunei Mikhail Vladimirovich Baranov.

His Majesty was accorded a static guard of honour, followed by the national anthems of the Russian Federation and Brunei. His Majesty was also introduced to senior Russian officials.

Also present to welcome His Majesty were Minister of Foreign Affairs II Datuk Seri Setia Erywan Mohd Yusof and Brunei’s Ambassador to the Russian Federation Muhammad Husaini Alauddin. - Borneo Bulletin/ANN
