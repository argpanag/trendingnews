Boy killed and man injured after train hits car in Mayo

TheTools · 04.09.2026 04:47 · source · permalink

Getty

A 12-year-old boy has died following a collision between a train and a car in Co Mayo last night.

The boy and a man in his late 40s were travelling in a car when the collision occurred at a user-operated level crossing at Kiltybo, near Ballyhaunis, in Co Mayo, shortly before 9pm.

The boy was pronounced dead at the scene.

The man was taken to hospital by ambulance for treatment. His injuries are described as not life-threatening.

The train was travelling from Heuston in Dublin to Westport.

The level crossing runs across a secondary road that intersects with the N60, which is situated a short distance away.

Following the crash, all passengers on the train safely disembarked and onward transport was arranged.

Iarnród Éireann said "our thoughts are with everyone affected".

Gardaí said a technical examination of the scene will be carried out by forensic investigators.

The coroner has been notified and the boy's body was removed to the mortuary at Mayo University Hospital where a post-mortem examination will be arranged.

Gardaí are appealing to road users and pedestrians with camera footage (including dash-cam) who were in the area of Kiltybo, Ballyhaunis, Co Mayo, between 8.15pm and 9.15pm last night, to make it available to them.

Anyone with information is asked to contact Ballina Garda Station on (096) 20560, the Garda Confidential Line on 1800 666 111, or any garda station.

Iarnród Éireann said the line between Westport and Athlone will remain closed until further notice.

It warned people to expect "significant disruption" on its services to and from Westport today.

"Customers who have already purchased tickets and no longer wish to travel can apply for a refund," it said.

Iarnród Éireann said that bus transfers will operate from Westport to Athlone, while Ballina to Manulla services will operate through Claremorris.

The collision was the second disruption on the Westport/Dublin line yesterday.

Passengers travelling in both directions were delayed when a train struck a tractor at a level crossing between Portarlington and Tullamore.

The train was stopped and bus transfers were arranged.

Passengers on the westbound service were transferred by bus to Athlone where they re-boarded a train to Westport, only for the replacement train to be involved in the second incident near Ballyhaunis last night.

Two incidents in one day 'frightening'

Emergency services attended the scene and one person was taken to hospital for treatment of minor injuries.

Minister of State for Road Transport Seán Canney extended his condolences to the family involved, describing the incident as "horrific".

He said the department's thoughts and prayers are also with the driver of the train and their family.

Mr Canney said safety reports are being carried out, adding that two incidents on the line in one evening was "frightening".

"Hopefully, we will see what has caused these accidents to see if there is anything we can do to prevent them into the future," he said.

He said rail is a very safe mode of transport and these incidents are "very rare" but "very tragic".
