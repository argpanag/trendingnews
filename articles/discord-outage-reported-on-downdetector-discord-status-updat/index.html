Discord outage reported on Downdetector. Discord status update - AOL

TheTools · 11.09.2026 05:49 · source · permalink

Discord users could find some trouble Wednedsay.

According to DownDetector, some are unable to get on the free voice, video and text service. The issues popped up at 12 p.m. EST and had about 500 reports as of 12:30 p.m.. Discord outage was trending on Google.

According to Downdetector, the biggest problem was with the app, where more than half of the issues were, but some also couldn't make voice calls either.

There is no timetable for when the issues will be resolved. It could be a quick fix, as these often are.
