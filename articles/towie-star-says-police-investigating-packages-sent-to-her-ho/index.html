TOWIE star says police investigating packages sent to her home

TheTools · 18.09.2026 05:43 · source · permalink

Gemma Collins has spoken about a deeply personal ordeal after receiving abusive letters and disturbing packages at her home.

The reality star revealed that police became involved after rat poison and dog excrement were allegedly sent to her address.

She discussed the experience in her new documentary, Gemma Collins: Everything I Am, which is available on Sky and NOW.

In footage from the programme posted on Instagram, Gemma is seen wearing a dressing gown and opening a letter in her kitchen.

The message contained a series of personal insults and threatening comments about her appearance and upcoming wedding.

The sender also appeared to suggest they were watching her, claiming they had driven behind her and referring to her new guard dog.

Gemma told the camera: “Being famous does leave you open to being a target.

People think your life is perfect, and they are going to watch this and realise it’s really not.”

She later revealed that the abuse had gone beyond letters, with rat poison and dog excrement allegedly delivered to her home.

The 45-year-old said she had kept the material and documented what had happened, adding that she believed the letters could have come from someone close to her.

She wrote on social media: “I’ve stayed quiet because I don’t believe in reacting out of anger, but there comes a point where boundaries have to be drawn.

Everything has been kept, documented and is being taken seriously.”

In the documentary, Gemma is seen wearing gloves while handling one of the letters.

Police reportedly took fingerprints from it as part of their investigation.

She said police, CID officers and forensic teams had become involved, and thanked them for treating the matter seriously.

Gemma’s fiancé, Rami Hawash, also expressed his anger after hearing the threats, saying he wanted to confront the person responsible.

The documentary, Gemma Collins: Everything I Am, will be available to watch on Sky One and NOW, with all eight episodes available to stream soon.
