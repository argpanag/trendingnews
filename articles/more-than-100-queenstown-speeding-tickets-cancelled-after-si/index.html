More than 100 Queenstown speeding tickets cancelled after sign error

TheTools · 17.11.2026 03:34 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Crime and Justice

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._v0.KrRTFtoPsaA8nSDloPZ4KejQJr59ANQpD9ULgGwzaM0/crime-and-justice/2638760%2Fmore-than-100-queenstown-speeding-tickets-cancelled-after-sign-error

Police are refunding 113 speeding tickets issued on a stretch of road in Queenstown where the speed limit signs have not been officially approved.

The fines were issued to people driving over the posted 60kmph speed limit on Gorge Rd over a two-and-a-half year period from May 2024 to November this year.

Police said the legal limit between between Industrial Place and the north end of Matakauri Park remained at 80kmph because NZTA was yet to officially approve the signs.

Acting director of road policing Peter McKennie said police were in the process of cancelling and refunding the fines and affected motorists would receive letters from this week.

Any infringements referred to the Ministry of Justice would also be cancelled, he said.

"While police were enforcing the posted speed limit in good faith - we apologise to all those affected, for any inconvenience this error has caused," McKennie said.

"Police continues to work with local council, Queenstown Lakes District, to ensure no other roads are affected."

Police asked drivers to contact the Police Infringement Bureau on 0800 105 777 if they had received a fine for breaching the 60kmph limit but had not received a cancellation letter by December 1.
