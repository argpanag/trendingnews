Six and out! World Cup hopeful granted two lives... before falling in Aussie stumble — LIVE

TheTools · 15.09.2026 09:55 · source · permalink

Australian ODI World Cup hopeful Cooper Connolly survived two near-dismissals following the drinks break before eventually being bowled just after raising his half-century, leaving the Aussies four wickets down.

It comes as Aussie gun Josh Inglis was dismissed earlier after a stellar catch from wicketkeeper Brendan Taylor.

Meanwhile, Travis Head’s return to the ODI side lasted just five balls after the opener chopped on for four.

It marks the fourth consecutive time the Aussie superstar has been dismissed this way in international cricket, continuing a worrying trend for the Australian opener.

Jason Gillespie said on Fox Cricket’s broadcast that “this is becoming quite a common dismissal for Travis Head”.

Australia have, remarkably, lost their last two international matches against Zimbabwe – a stunning upset at the T20 World Cup earlier this year, and a three-wicket loss in an ODI in September 2022.

It means the tourists have more to do than just tick a box in Harare ahead of three ODIs and three Tests against South Africa: they’ll be out for revenge as well.

“They’ve had the wood over us more recently at the T20 World Cup,” fast bowler Nathan Ellis said.

“So (we are) excited to not only try and get a little bit of redemption but also start building towards the one-day World Cup (co-hosted by Zimbabwe) in a year or so.

“There are no easy beats. I think people can have that perception at times, but it doesn’t feel that way in our change room.”

Marsh made four changes from the team that faced Bangladesh in Australia’s most recent ODI in June.

Marnus Labuschagne, Cameron Green, Ben Dwarhuis and Riley Meredith have been replaced by Head, Nathan Ellis, Spencer Johnson and Alex Carey.
