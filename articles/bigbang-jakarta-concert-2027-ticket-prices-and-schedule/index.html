BIGBANG Jakarta Concert 2027: Ticket Prices and Schedule

TheTools · 13.09.2026 05:05 · source · permalink

Reporter

Ecka Pramita

September 13, 2026 | 12:05 pm

TEMPO.CO, Jakarta - BIGBANG is back! Are you ready, V.I.Ps? The legendary K-pop group has returned to celebrate its 20th anniversary. Now performing as a trio, the group announced its comeback with a memorable showcase at last year's Coachella festival, thrilling music fans worldwide. Alongside dominating global charts with new releases, the group is embarking on a world tour titled "XX : COSMOS."

This musical extravaganza will span 19 cities worldwide, including Jakarta. Promoter PK Entertainment announced that the concert will take place at the Jakarta International Stadium (JIS) on January 16, 2027. Given the overwhelming fan enthusiasm, attendees are advised to prepare early for ticket sales.

Ticket Prices and Administrative Fees

The organizer announced 10 ticket tiers ranging from Rp1,550,000 for CAT 7 A & B to Rp6,500,000 for the Ultimate VIP category.

However, these baseline prices exclude the 10 percent government tax, a 6 percent platform fee, and processing charges. With these fees included, total prices range between Rp1,798,000 and Rp7,540,000.

Attendees should also plan their travel logistics to JIS in Tanjung Priok, North Jakarta.

Ready for the Ticket War

For the upcoming BIGBANG ticket war, sales are divided into four distinct time slots, each offering specific access privileges.

1. V.I.P Membership Presale

Thursday, September 17, 2026, from 10:00 to 11:59 Jakarta time (WIB).

This session is exclusive to verified membership holders. Access required registering on bigbang.bstage.in and completing the membership survey between September 7 and 11, 2026.

2. BCA Presale

Friday, September 18, 2026, from 10:00 to 11:59 WIB.

This sale phase is restricted to payments processed via the myBCA app.

3. Indosat Presale

Saturday, September 19, 2026, from 10:00 to 11:59 WIB.

Access requires an Indosat Presale Code, distributed starting September 14, 2026, via the myIM3 or bima+ applications.

4. General On Sale

Sunday, September 20, 2026, starting at 10:00 WIB.

Open to the general public, with tickets available exclusively through the official event site, bigbanginjakarta.com.

Setlist and Performance Highlights

The tour marks an emotional milestone as fans gather to hear the group perform live following a four-year hiatus. Across their two-decade career, BIGBANG's discography exceeds 300 tracks, including Japanese releases, solo work, and sub-unit projects.

Early "XX : COSMOS" shows in South Korea and the United States featured a 30-song setlist, ranging from their 2007 hit "Last Farewell" to "BiiiG," their newest single released on their debut anniversary, August 19.

Alongside group routines, each member is scheduled for solo segments. Daesung will perform his trot track "Look at Me, Gwisoon," Taeyang is set to sing his latest release "Live Fast Die Slow," and G-Dragon will deliver "Power." The COSMOS theme represents the enduring connection between the group and their fans from their debut into the future.

Good luck securing tickets.
