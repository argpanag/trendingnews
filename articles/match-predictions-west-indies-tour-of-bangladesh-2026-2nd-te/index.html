In the 1st Test Match, Bangladesh batted first and they posted a total of 272 before being bowled out. Shadman Islam played really well at the top while Tanzid Hasan and Mominul Haque failed. Najmul Hossain Shanto also got out without troubling the scorers much. Islam got good support in Towhid Hridoy. Hridoy made 42 while Islam went on to score 105 runs. The rest of the lower order failed to produce any impact and Bangladesh were eventually bowled out for 272.

West Indies in their first innings posted a total of 245. John Campbell and Tagenarine Chanderpaul played really well at the top. Campbell scored 54 while Tagenarine made 56 runs. Kavem Hodge and Amir Jangoo disappointed while Justin Greaves and Roston Chase contributed healthily. Greaves and Chase also threw it away after getting into their 40's and West Indies were eventually bowled out for 245.

Bangladesh in their second innings lost their way right from the outset. They were reduced to 35 for 5 and looked down and out. Nayeem Hasan fought hard and scored 52 runs but he kept losing partners in regular succession. Jomel Warrican and Roston Chase made full use of the spinning conditions. Warrican picked up 4 while Chase accounted for 5 scalps as Bangladesh were bowled out for an under par total of 142.

West Indies were quite dominant right from the start of the run chase. Brandon King and John Campbell scored runs in all directions of the ground. King made 81 runs while Campbell scored 49 to seal the deal in favor of West Indies. Kavem Hodge also played a nice cameo of 31 runs and West Indies chased the target down in the 36th over itself with 8 wickets in hand.
