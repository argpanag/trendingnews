SNP nightmare as nemesis Penny Mordaunt takes step towards Westminster return

TheTools · 04.10.2026 15:58 · source · permalink

SNP MPs will be on red alert after arch-nemesis Dame Penny Mordaunt said she was eyeing a return to Westminster. The former minister became a scourge of the Nats in her role as Leader of the House during the last Tory government.

Her weekly jousting with Deidre Brock at business questions saw her mock the separatist party over Operation Branchform and its numerous failings in Scotland. She has now revealed she has passed the candidate selection process despite speculation that the she had been blocked from standing by Kemi Badenoch.

Speaking to the BBC’s Sunday with Laura Kuenssberg, the former defence minister said: "I have passed my parliamentary assessment board. I’m waiting to see what seats I can go for, but I have a full pass from the candidates committee."She added: "I would love to be back on the squad. If I’m not selected, I’ll be in the stands cheering on the team and volunteering to chop the oranges at half-time."

The prospect of Dame Penny, who stole the show at the King’s Coronation with her sword-wielding role, back in government after the next election is the stuff of nightmares for Nat MPs. There are just eight SNP politicians at Westminster now, although they are expected to mount a comeback when the country goes to the polls in 2029.

Dame Penny insisted she now backs leaving the European Convention on Human Rights (ECHR) and watering down net zero targets. She said: "I’m very happy with the party position as it is on all those issues. And I think Kemi is right.

"I’ve always toed the party line. I haven’t always had my first choice as leader, but I have followed the party line. And I think Kemi is right to require her potential MPs to say they will deliver the manifesto they stand on."Dame Penny lost her Portsmouth North seat to Labour at the last General Election by just 800 votes. She was a victim of the Reform surge with the party finishing a distant third but its 8,501 votes damaging her chances of re-election. She will still need to be picked by a local Tory association as its candidate. In 2023, she made history when she became the first woman to carry the Sword of State at the Coronation of King Charles III.
