Ryan Fox makes a big gear decision ahead of India Championship

TheTools · 15.10.2026 19:03 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Sport

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._f0.YIZs2-J2FpwgcBcJJb29s1vXmFAWHLh_p1oUyv-l-RA/sport/1922948%2Fryan-fox-makes-a-big-gear-decision-ahead-of-india-championship

For the first time in his career, New Zealand golfing ace Ryan Fox will take to a big tournament without a key piece of equipment.

"I don't have a driver in the bag this week," Fox said before today's start in the DP World Tour India Championship at the Delhi Golf Club.

Fox has decided to leave his driver in his hotel room because the tight, short Delhi course calls for precision in shot placement.

Fox, the winner of this year's Open Championship, said other players didn't use a driver on the Delhi course last year and at practice this week he could understand why.

"On Monday there were one or two holes where I think maybe I can use one (a driver) down there, and you walk down the fairway and you think 'Nope, it's not worth the risk'," he said.

"I like being aggressive. I just have to temper that off the tee a little bit and just pick and choose the right iron shots to be aggressive with for a second shot.

"It's very different and it's a fun test in that regard."

DP World Tour leader Patrick Reed and grand slam winner Rory McIlroy are others who don't intend to use a driver this week.

Fox is sixth in the DP World Tour's rankings, with four tournaments remaining, including the Race to Dubai finale and a top five rankings finish would put the cap on a great year for him.

American Reed leads the rankings with 3423 points, from Spaniard Eugenio Chacarra (3257), McIlroy (3115), Casey Jarvis (2654), Aaron Rai (2408) and then Fox with almost 2267. Fellow New Zealander Daniel Hillier is 17th.

Fox is loving the vibe on his first trip to India and has found he has some fans, thanks mainly to his Open Championship victory, which he admits he is still getting used to.

"There's still a whole lot I haven't processed properly yet. The fact that I am a major champion, that still sounds and feels a little bit weird.

"The support here has been great this week. So many people have come up and said they watched it and were really excited for me and that's pretty cool.

Fox is in a first round grouping with Reed and Masters winner Aaron Rai on Thursday night.
