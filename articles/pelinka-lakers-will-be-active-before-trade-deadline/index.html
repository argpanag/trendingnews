Pelinka: Lakers Will Be Active Before Trade Deadline

TheTools · 21.11.2026 23:01 · source · permalink

The Lakers underwent a roster upheaval during the offseason and they’re not through making changes. President of basketball operations Rob Pelinka declared on Friday he will be “active” over the next few months to make more upgrades, he said on a Spectrum Sportsnet interview (Twitter video link; hat tip to RealGM).

“We’ll be active at the trade deadline to shore up things that we see,” Pelinka said. “We think this team has a real chance to make a run, so we want to give the group as much talent as we can. Don’t want to get too far over our skis with a hot start, because you don’t want to get big-headed, but clearly the way it’s gone so far, it’s been great, and we hope it continues.”

Carried by arguably the best backcourt in the league, the Lakers are off to a 14-3 start. Yet Pelinka’s remarks suggest he believes the current roster isn’t strong enough to compete with San Antonio and the other serious Western Conference contenders when the postseason comes around.

The Lakers were as active as any team during the offseason. They signed Quentin Grimes, Sandro Mamukelashvili, Collin Sexton, Kevon Looney, Matisse Thybulle and Ziaire Williams in free agency. They re-signed Austin Reaves to a massive new deal and traded away former starting center Deandre Ayton. Pelinka replaced Ayton in a sign-and-trade transaction with the Jazz, giving up multiple first-rounders, along with picks swaps, for Walker Kessler. Former first round pick Dalton Knecht was cut loose last month.

The Lakers currently rank third in the league in scoring (120 points per game) and first in forcing turnovers (17.9 per game). So where will Pelinka look to make upgrades? It stands to reason he’ll seek a more impactful forward for the lineup to complement Luka Doncic, Reaves and Kessler.

The Lakers are hard-capped at the first apron but have enough breathing room to make moves, though their remaining draft assets are limited to a first-round pick swap and three second-rounders. With Pelinka’s declaration, the Lakers will be one of the teams to watch leading up to the February 11 trade deadline.
