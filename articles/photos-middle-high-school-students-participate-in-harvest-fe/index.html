Photos | Middle, high school students participate in Harvest Festival at UC Santa Cruz Farm

TheTools · 31.10.2026 00:14 · source · permalink

Pajaro Valley High School students are all smiles after picking their flowers and pumpkins at the UC Santa Cruz Farm. The students were among more than 300 high schoolers and middle schoolers from throughout Santa Cruz County who were treated to a day of fun and exploration on Thursday at FoodWhat’s annual Harvest Festival. FoodWhat co-Executive Director of Programming Irene Juarez O’Connell said, “This is our 20th youth-led Harvest Festival. For the past nine weeks our five youth event planners coordinated across schools and community partners to put on a fun hands-on event for these high school and middle school youth.” The youth leaders who planned the event were Meganie Peguero, Marlene Echeverria, AJ Lemus, Carlos Dorado and Nahum Chavez. O’Connell said each year the event has three goals: to expose students to the things they do at FoodWhat, to provide students with an educational and hands-on experience while having fun, and to provide them a meaningful experience on a farm and college campus. Activities at the festival ranged from creating culinary treats to art projects to a tour of the farm. Since its founding in 2007, FoodWhat has provided youth an opportunity to “engage in relationships with land, food and each other in ways that are grounded in love and rooted in justice.”
