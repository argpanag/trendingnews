Al-Qadisiyah's victory takes them to 15 points, placing them second alongside leaders Al-Hilal, while Al-Ahli remains at 9 points, settling in eighth place in the standings.

Brendan Rodgers, the coach of Al-Qadisiyah, praised what his players delivered, confirming that the win was deserved and important against a big team, noting that his team continues to develop and grow.
