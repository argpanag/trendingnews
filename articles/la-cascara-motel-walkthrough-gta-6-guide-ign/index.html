La Cascara Motel Walkthrough - GTA 6 Guide - IGN

TheTools · 19.11.2026 07:10 · source · permalink

La Cascara Motel is an Illegal Operation led by the Kolt & Associates gang in Grand Theft Auto VI. It has three Key Valuables to find, including a standout vehicle and a unique weapon variant. This official IGN GTA 6 guide teaches you how to find every Key Valuable location and highlights additional, optional items you can grab while you’re there.

Kolt & Associates La Cascara Motel Location

The Kolt & Associates La Cascara Motel Illegal Opportunity is located on the west side of Port Gellhorn in Kelly County, just northeast of Wild Hog Point. It appears on your map after you find the safe code during the Brooks Boatworks Illegal Operation or after reading a notepad in the June Warehouse Illegal Operation.

There’s no specific entrance to the motel, so feel free to approach from any side you like. There is heavy gang activity outside the front, so if you’re coming from that side, make sure you have healing items and a good weapon on you. A shotgun or assault rifle works well here.

Key Valuable 1 Location

The first Key Valuable is the Vapid Firebug Dominator standout vehicle, which is located in front of the motel, parked on the east side.

The car is bright red and very hard to miss, so you shouldn’t have trouble finding it. Just be careful as you approach as if you haven’t taken out the thugs outside the motel yet, they’ll turn hostile and attack.

When you go to get in the car, you’ll notice it’s locked and you need to either break the window or use a Slim Jim to be able to hotwire and drive it.

Key Valuable 2 Location

The second Key Valuable is a safe, located in a room on the top floor of the motel.

There are multiple routes you can take up there, but I’ll detail mine below if you need some help finding it.

Editor Meg’s Route to the Top Floor

Note that I’ll be skipping optional pickups and explaining them later instead, but feel free to grab whatever you’d like as you pass it.

Go all the way to the right side of the motel and you’ll see an opening in the wooden paneling, to the left of an old, stained mattress. Go through and immediately go up the stairs to the right.

You’ll eventually reach a floor with green mesh coverings as walls. Go into the second door on the right.

Turn left and follow the path through the rooms until you see some graffiti that reads something like “SATIN RODIZ.” Go left here and through the door.

Continue on ahead, keeping tightly along the right wall to cross the gap, then go up the ramp ahead of you.

Go forward and then turn right into the second doorway.

Continue through to the back of the room, where you’ll reach a stone ramp you can climb up. Do so.

At the top, go out onto the balcony and turn left.

Follow the path all the way left and into a gated section of building.

Go through various rooms until you eventually reach a gated room; you can see a huge safe in there. That’s the Key Valuable.

You can either use Sticky Bombs to explode the safe door open or enter a safe code to get inside.

Safe Code Location

The code to the La Cascara Motel safe is not found inside the motel. Instead, it’s at the Brooks Boatworks to the northeast, which is another Illegal Operation led by the Kolt & Associates gang.

The safe code is located in the building to the right of the Brooks Boathouse main warehouse, on the east side of the compound.

Head inside and look on the wall above the weapon case and you’ll spot a map you can inspect. Do so with L2/LT and you’ll see three codes, including the one for the Brook Boathouse safe, too. The game will automatically add Tap to Reveal to your notes, which is the Las Cascara Motel safe code.

Open the safe however you see fit and you’ll find $1,558 in cash on the top shelf and $681 on the middle shelf, totaling $2,239.

Key Valuable 3 Location

The third and final Key Valuable is located on the top floor of the motel, just past the Key Valuable safe. Head right past the safe room and you’ll find the Scrapper VF5C SMG unique weapon variant in a weapon case in the back left.

You’ve found all three Key Valuables in the La Cascara Motel and can leave if you’d like, but there are some optional items to grab that can increase your payout.

Optional Items

Look on the wall to the left of the weapon case containing the Scrapper VF5C SMG to find some Body Armor you can wear for more protection. There’s also an explosives crate containing Sticky Bombs and Frag Grenades just to the right of it.

There’s a table directly opposite the weapon case with a money counting machine on it and some cash to grab: $338, to be exact.

Go back to where the safe is and then continue through the series of rooms. Eventually, you’ll reach a room with four bags of pills on the table.

Continue through for another table with four more bags of pills. You can sell all drugs you find here to a fence.

Go down to the floor below and you’ll find a room with five bags of weed on one of the tables.

Follow the orange and blue cables on the floor through to another room, where you’ll reach another table with three more bags of weed to sell.
