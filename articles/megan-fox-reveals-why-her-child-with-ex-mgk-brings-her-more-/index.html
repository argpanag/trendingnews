Everyone knows Megan Fox for her role in Transformers, and she also made headlines for her relationship with her ex-partner Machine Gun Kelly, also known as MGK. However, these days, the two share a co-parenting relationship as they raise their daughter. Amid this, the actress revealed why her child with ex MGK brings her "more peace."

Megan Fox explains how staying home with daughter Saga changed her postpartum experience

Recently, the 40-year-old spoke exclusively with People while discussing Ro's new erectile dysfunction medication campaign. During the conversation, the actress opened up about the campaign, her career, and her relationship. Amid all this, Megan Fox explained how staying home with her daughter, Saga, changed her postpartum experience.

Fox said, "This is the first time I did not have to go back to work right away, so my postpartum experience has been very different. I've been able to really be with her the way I wanted to be."

"I've been able to choose to be much more present, and I have a lot more peace in my connection with her because of that," Megan said in the same interview.

The actress has three sons, Noah Shannon, Bodhi Ransom, and Journey River, along with a daughter, Saga. However, her experience with her daughter has been different and special, as the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles star revealed.

Fox said, "She's also the first one I didn't have in a hospital. I had her at home, in a tub, in the backyard, outside by a fire pit."

For context, Megan Fox and Machine Gun Kelly welcomed their daughter, Saga Blade Fox-Baker, on March 27, 2025. The two are not currently in a romantic relationship and are instead focused on co-parenting their daughter.

The Jennifer's Body star has not shared any photos of her daughter directly on social media. She has only spoken about parenting in a lighthearted way on a few occasions. While the actress currently keeps Saga out of the spotlight, Machine Gun Kelly has shared rare glimpses of their daughter online.
