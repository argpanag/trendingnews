Kyle Kennedy, the granddaughter of Robert F. "Bobby" Kennedy and Ethel Kennedy, had died. She was 42.

She passed away in her home in Westwood, Massachusetts, on September 21, six years after a life-changing injury. In 2020, she suffered a spinal cord injury that resulted in long-term paralysis.

After her injury, she became an "advocate for people living with disabilities, including testifying at the Massachusetts state house in a hearing on discrimination in disabled health care," per her Burke Funeral Homes obituary

Her cause of death has not been disclosed.

Kyle was born on July 6, 1984, in Washington, D.C., to the late Michael Kennedy and Victoria Denise Gifford, whose father was Frank Gifford. Michael died when she was 13 years old after he was playing football while skiing and skied into a tree.

She received a history degree from Georgetown University and two master's degrees. She earned a Master of Science in Early Education from Pace University and a Master of Education in Education Policy and Management from Harvard University. She dedicated her career to work in early childhood education.

"Blessed with a formidable intellect, dry wit, and beauty, she will be remembered for her devotion to her children Conor, Josephine, and Declan, and her support for educational reform for disadvantaged children and for people living with disabilities," her obituary stated.

Kyle is survived by her two children, Conor, 12, and Declan, 10, as well as her former husband Liam, mother, brother Michael, sister Rory, and nieces and nephews McKie, Quinn, Jack, and Caleb.
