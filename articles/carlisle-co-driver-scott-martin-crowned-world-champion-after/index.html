Carlisle co-driver Scott Martin crowned world champion after dramatic WRC finish

TheTools · 20.10.2026 10:00 · source · permalink

A rally co-driver from Carlisle has reached the pinnacle of motorsport after being crowned World Rally Champion following one of the most dramatic finishes in the sport's history.

Scott Martin and his Welsh driver partner Elfyn Evans secured the 2026 FIA World Rally Championship title at Rally Italia Sardinia, ending years of near misses for the British duo.

Born in Carlisle, Martin navigated Evans to the championship with Toyota Gazoo Racing after a season-long battle that went down to the final stage of the final rally.

The pair had previously finished runners-up before finally getting their hands on the sport's biggest prize.

The title appeared to be slipping away during the closing stages in Sardinia. Evans suffered a puncture on Sunday and had to stop to change a tyre, leaving rival and Toyota team-mate Oliver Solberg with a chance of snatching the championship.

Everything came down to the decisive Wolf Power Stage, where the fastest of the two title contenders would emerge as champion. Solberg looked set to complete a remarkable comeback and was ahead on the timing splits with only a handful of corners remaining.

However, disaster struck for the Swede when he slid off the road just two corners from the finish line. The mistake handed the championship back to Evans and Martin, sparking emotional celebrations for the pair after years of coming agonisingly close to the title.

The triumph marks the first World Rally Championship title for Martin, whose rallying career began in the UK more than two decades ago.

He has competed at the highest level of the sport since the mid-2000s and has partnered several leading drivers before joining forces with Evans in 2019.

The championship success gives Carlisle a world champion in one of motorsport's most demanding disciplines, with Martin now able to add a WRC title to a career that has taken him from local rallies to the very top of the international stage.
