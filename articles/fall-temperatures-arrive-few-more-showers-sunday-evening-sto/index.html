Fall Temperatures Arrive, Few More Showers Sunday Evening, Storms Return By Late Week

TheTools · 20.09.2026 12:43 · source · permalink

TOPEKA, Kan. (WIBW) - Much-needed cooler air arrives Sunday with highs in the 60s and 70s, enjoy! Another round of showers and storms possible Sunday evening, being non-severe. Fall-like temps settle in next week before widespread storm chances return late week.

First Alerts:

  • Fall Temperatures Arrive, Few More Showers Sunday Evening: Fall-like temperatures finally settle into the region starting Sunday and continuing through next week, with highs ranging from the middle 60s to low 70s, a welcome and refreshing change after the recent heat. Another round of showers and storms is possible Sunday evening, though severe weather isn’t expected, keep an umbrella handy, but no major disruptions are anticipated.
  • Storms Return By Late Week: Widespread storm chances return by late week as another cold front approaches. Stay tuned for updates as timing and coverage become clearer.

First Alert Forecast:

Today turns much cooler compared to recent days, with ample clouds limiting highs to the upper 60s and low 70s. Isolated to scattered shower and storm chances continue through the day into this evening, mainly across far east-central Kansas near a lingering boundary, though severe weather isn’t expected.

A strong area of high pressure builds southward from the Plains into the Midwest through midweek, ushering in dry and cooler conditions. Extensive cloud cover and continued cold air behind the front keep highs well below average in the upper 60s to low 70s through Wednesday. The pattern shifts by Thursday as a disturbance moves through, bringing a chance for showers and storms Thursday into Friday, though timing remains uncertain.

Looking Ahead:

Today: Scattered Showers. Cloudy in the morning with scattered showers developing later in the day. High 75F. Winds NNE at 5 to 10 mph. Chance of rain 30%.

Tonight: Scattered Showers. Scattered showers during the evening, then cloudy skies overnight. Low 63F. Winds N at 5 to 10 mph. Chance of rain 30%.

Tomorrow: Cloudy. Overcast. A stray shower or thunderstorm is possible. High 73F. Winds N at 5 to 10 mph.
