DAYTON, Ohio (WDTN) – Thankfully, no one was injured after a reported structure fire on Saturday morning. 

According to the Montgomery County Regional Dispatch, the fire was called in at approximately 6:10 a.m. from the 1100 block of Chelsea Avenue.

The caller reported smoke in their home, which had woken them up. 

"The first-arriving unit (Engine 15) was on the scene in approximately six minutes and found smoke showing from a one and a half story residence. Crews quickly controlled the fire and completed searches of the structure. No injuries were reported," wrote the Dayton Fire Department.

"We just woke up to popping and smoke in our bedroom," said the caller.

Dispatch told them to leave the home.

Officials said no one was injured, and crews worked on the scene until approximately 7:40 a.m.

The cause of the fire is under active investigation by the Dayton FD.
