Soufiane Rahimi Becomes Al Ain’s All-Time Top Scorer

TheTools · 16.09.2026 12:21 · source · permalink

Rabat – Moroccan international Soufiane Rahimi has become Al Ain’s all-time leading scorer in the AFC Champions League Elite after scoring in the club’s emphatic 4-0 victory over Al Nassr on Tuesday.

Rahimi’s goal took his tally in the competition to 19, moving him clear of former Al Ain stars Omar Abdulrahman and Asamoah Gyan, who both finished with 18 goals for the Emirati club.

The Moroccan forward struck in the 72nd minute as Al Ain completed a commanding victory in their opening continental fixture.

A memorable night for the Rahimi Family

The victory was also a memorable occasion for Soufiane’s younger brother Houssine, who scored twice before Soufiane added Al Ain’s third. Georgios Giakoumakis completed the scoring late in the match.

Houssine, who was named Player of the Match, dedicated part of his post-match comments to their late father, Mohamed Rahimi, better known as “You3ari.”

Speaking to Emirati outlet Hadath Al Riyadi, the 24-year-old highlighted the role his parents played in the brothers’ careers.

“My parents are the secret behind the success of Soufiane and I. I pray for my father and tell him that he left behind men, and we will continue what you started in your life and move the Rahimi family forward,” he said.
Houssine also reflected on his time at Raja Casablanca, thanking the club for helping shape his career and supporting his family.

The night meanwhile marked another milestone for Soufiane’s Al Ain career, with the Moroccan striker moving to 19 goals in the AFC Champions League Elite and becoming the club’s outright record scorer in the competition.
