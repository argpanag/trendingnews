Inclement weather forces MLB to make scheduling change for Yankees-Orioles series

TheTools · 24.09.2026 21:02 · source · permalink

The New York Yankees are entering their final series of the regular season beginning Friday against a familiar divisional opponent in the Baltimore Orioles.

The Orioles will enter the series eliminated from postseason contention at 78-81. The Yankees, meanwhile, are locked into the fourth seed in the American League playoffs.

The Tampa Bay Rays have already clinched the AL East, meaning New York will have to play in the wild card round despite having the second best record in the AL.

It is almost certain the Yankees will host the Boston Red Sox, who are on the verge of clinching the fifth seed. This series between the Yankees and Orioles does not matter too much for either club.

How the Yankees-Orioles Series Schedule Will Look This Weekend

However, the games still have to be played, and MLB is about to announce a scheduling change. As the series currently stands the two sides are scheduled to play on Friday, then two more games over the weekend.

That will no longer be the case, per Randy Miller of NJ.com. Miller has reported that the Yankees will have no choice but to cancel Saturday’s matchup due to inclement weather, with rain expected.

It also may play a role for Game 162 on Sunday, with the storm expected to continue. MLB has not yet decided if that game will be played given it will have no impact on the postseason. For now, the clubs are expected to play a doubleheader on Friday, day and night.

The weather is not expected to continue into the wild card series that New York is set to host at Yankee Stadium next Tuesday, with the storm passing through before then. With the change, Yankees players could now get three full days off to prepare for October baseball.

For New York, it remains unclear whether superstar Aaron Judge will be available for the playoffs as the Yankees hope to capture their 28th World Series title in franchise history.
