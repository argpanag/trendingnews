Bridge expert deals out top tips

TheTools · 19.10.2026 09:02 · source · permalink

Bridge expert Andrew Robson joined Stamford Bridge Club members to share his knowledge and strategies for the popular card game.

Andrew, bridge columnist for The Times newspaper, presented two sessions to club members on Sunday, highlighting different aspects of bidding.

He illustrated this with opportunities to bid and play a series of hands. Members learned how to make the best of the most unpromising looking hands, how length often ‘trumps’ high card points, and were reminded that bridge is a competitive game in which it’s often best to keep opponents out of a contract.

Stamford Bridge Club is run almost wholly by volunteers and this event was a team effort headed by club secretary Elise Alliston with assistance from Bob Ambrose, Tim Burley, Karen Southwell, Maggie Knight, Mark Hodgson, Terry Knights, Matthew Ticehust, Gaynor Morris and the Mercury’s bridge columnist Trevor Thrower.
