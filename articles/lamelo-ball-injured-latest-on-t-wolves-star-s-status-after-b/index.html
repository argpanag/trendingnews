LaMelo Ball Injured, Latest on T-Wolves Star's Status After Being Helped Off Court

TheTools · 28.11.2026 01:17 · source · permalink

Minnesota Timberwolves guard LaMelo Ball will miss Friday's game against the Memphis Grizzlies after suffering an ankle injury late in Wednesday's win against the Oklahoma City Thunder.

Ball delivered a strong performance prior to the injury, recording 19 points on 7-of-18 shooting to go along with eight assists and seven rebounds.

Jon Krawczynski of The Athletic reported Wednesday night that Ball "was in very good spirits after the game, laughing and joking with teammates in the locker room" after the game.

Krawczynski added that Ball "seems to have avoided a major" injury.

Ball's injury is another blow to Minnesota. Superstar guard and face of the franchise Anthony Edwards has been out of the lineup and has been limited to only four games this season due to arthroscopic knee surgery.

With Edwards on the shelf, Ball has been the engine behind the Timberwolves' offensive attack, averaging 17.3 points per game to go along with 4.4 rebounds and a team-leading 5.7 assists and 1.6 steals.

During the offseason, Minnesota acquired Ball in a trade with the Charlotte Hornets, hoping he could be the ideal backcourt complement to Edwards.

It remains to be seen if that will be the case since Edwards has been out injured, but Ball has played a big role in keeping the T-Wolves afloat without Edwards, as they have still managed a 14-5 start, which is tied for third in the Western Conference.

With Ball out, much of the offensive burden would fall on the shoulders of Jaden McDaniels and Ayo Dosunmu, who are the Timberwolves' two leading scorers among players who have appeared in at least five games this season.

McDaniels was the star for Minnesota during Wednesday's win over OKC, leading all scorers with 32 points to go along with 10 rebounds and five assists.

Bones Hyland will likely see an uptick in playing time and importance in the event of Ball missing games as well.
