Bulgaria lacks technical capability to shoot down drones in the Black Sea

“Bulgaria currently lacks the technical military capabilities to shoot down drones”, Prime Minister Rumen Radev said at the ''Black Sea 2026: The Closer Middle East'' conference in Sofia organised by the Institute for Economics and International Relations and the Friedrich Ebert Foundation Bulgaria.

  • Defence Minister: It doesn't matter who the drones belong to if they pose threat
  • Opposition calls for Security Council meeting over ships sunk in Black Sea

He noted that detecting drone attacks is extremely difficult with the equipment currently available to Bulgaria along its coastline. Radev said that the country had received occasional assistance from allied patrol aircraft and added that efforts were underway with allies to ensure more regular aerial surveillance of potential threats in the Black Sea.

Speaking to reporters in Parliament, GERB leader Boyko Borissov described the government as “champions of making excuses” and asked: “Then why do we have a defence system if we cannot shoot down drones?”
