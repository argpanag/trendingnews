New Mexico’s Melissa Amaya fights for UFC contract

TheTools · 25.09.2026 23:39 · source · permalink

COMBAT SPORTS

Chaparral native Melissa Amaya faces Brazil’s Tina Black on Saturday in The Ultimate Fighter strawweight final.

New Mexico native Melissa Amaya weighed in at 116 pounds on Friday for her MMA fight on Saturday against Brazil’s Tina Black.

A UFC contract is at stake.

Amaya (8-0) advanced to Saturday’s The Ultimate Fighter final with victories over Ana Melisano (first-round submission) and Giovanna Canuto (second-round TKO, after nearly being submitted in round one).

Now, only Black (15-4, real name Valesca Machado) stands in the way.

“It’s insane,” Amaya said, during a UFC interview session, of the opportunity Saturday’s fight presents. “I don’t think I can put words to it.”

In a June 14 story, Amaya related to the Journal’s Geoff Grammer the journey that brought her to The Ultimate Fighter: a nursing degree from UNM, battles with severe depression, marriage and full-time work as a nurse in Spokane, her unexpected MMA career.

“This,” she said during the UFC interview, “is just the cherry on top for me.”

Of her opponent, Amaya said, “Tina Black has been training and fighting since before MMA was even a thought in my head … she’s dangerous anywhere.”

Win or lose on Saturday, contract or no contract, Amaya said she has no plans to leave nursing.

“I absolutely love nursing,” she said. “It’s my way of being of service in this world.”

The Amaya-Black fight likely will be the first fight on Saturday’s main card, scheduled to start at 6 p.m. The card will streamed live on Paramount+.
