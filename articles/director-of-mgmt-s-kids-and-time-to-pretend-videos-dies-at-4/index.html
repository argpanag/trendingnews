Director of MGMT’s “Kids” and “Time to Pretend” Videos Dies at 43

TheTools · 17.11.2026 18:28 · source · permalink

Ray Tintori, the filmmaker behind MGMT’s breakout Oracular Spectacular videos, has died, People and TMZ report. He was 43. No cause was provided, and his death is under investigation, according to both publications, citing the New York City Office of Chief Medical Examiner.

Born into a filmmaking family, Tintori started making shorts—Jettison Your Loved Ones and his award-winning thesis film Death to the Tinman—in the mid 2000s, before his break with the music videos for MGMT’s “Time to Pretend,” “Electric Feel,” and “Kids.” His fantastical sets and special effects dovetailed with a cozy, handmade feel that characterized his ongoing work. The “Kids” video starred Joanna Newsom as a mother oblivious to the writhing monsters that pursued her terrified toddler. (A behind-the-scenes short put to bed some lingering questions over whether the experience was a little harsh on the real-life toddler.) Tintori returned to work with MGMT on his final video, for the band’s Christine and the Queens collaboration “Dancing in Babylon.”

In the meantime, Tintori notched up a résumé that included the Killers’ “Spaceman,” Arcade Fire’s “Chemistry,” and Chairlift’s perpetually melting “Evident Utensil” visual. He contributed to the acclaimed magical realist drama Beasts of the Southern Wild, a product of the Court 13 collective he co-founded. Last year, Tintori co-directed a New York stage production of Ethan Coen’s Waiting and cameoed as a newsreel photographer in Marty Supreme.
