2.3 magnitude earthquake recorded in Lexington

TheTools · 18.11.2026 03:31 · source · permalink

A minor earthquake was recorded in Lexington early Nov. 16, according to the U.S. Geological Survey.

The roughly 2.3 magnitude earthquake hit Lexington just after 3 a.m., according to the report. Attached coordinates show the epicenter hit near Man o' War Boulevard, located about 90 minutes from Louisville.

Earthquakes with a magnitude of 2.5 or less are usually not felt, but can be recorded by seismograph, according to Michigan Tech's Earthquake Magnitude Scale. According to Michigan Tech, millions of similar earthquakes are recorded each year.
