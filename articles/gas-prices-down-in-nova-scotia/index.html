Gas prices down in Nova Scotia

TheTools · 06.11.2026 16:08 · source · permalink

By Natasha O'Neill

Posted Nov 6, 2026 12:08:10 PM.

Both gasoline and diesel have seen a decrease in the first week of November, after months of higher than usual costs.

The Nova Scotia Energy Board decreased the price of gas 9.9 cents to 171.7 per litre, a low not seen since August. Diesel, which was interrupted this week by the board, lowered again.

Prices now hover around 248.2 cents per litre, a 5.3 cent decrease.

It comes after Iran’s top diplomat said earlier this week that he hopes there is “rationality” on the U.S. side as diplomatic efforts seek to avert a return to all-out war.

In an interview shared on his Telegram channel, Abbas Araghchi said Iran hoped the U.S. would choose “diplomacy over other paths.” He said Iran was ready for both defense and diplomacy, depending on Washington’s “rationality.”

This also happens the same week, the U.S. Republican Party was dealt heavy losses in the midterms.

JD Vance publicly broke from President Donald Trump ‘s defense of the Iran war Wednesday after the midterm election results, attempting to distance himself from voters’ clear rebuke of the president as the vice president faces questions about his own political ambitions.

Vance, who has said he would decide after the election whether to run for president in 2028, issued a lengthy social media post blaming the unpopular conflict with Iran for the GOP’s drubbing. Vance said, “If you’d like to argue it was worth it, that losing the Senate for the next 6 years was worth it, then do so.”
