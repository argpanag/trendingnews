SunLive - Car crashes into water off Chapel Street - The Bay's News First

TheTools · 15.10.2026 20:55 · source · permalink

Cordons have again been lifted on Chapel St after a car crashed into Tauranga Harbour overnight.

One person was taken to hospital in a moderate condition after the crash just after midnight.

A second person was believed to have been in the vehicle.

Police have not confirmed whether a second person has been found.

A reporter at the scene said an ambulance arrived and left again about 20 minutes later.

She said the road reopened at 9.30am.

Tauranga City Council earlier said Chapel St was closed due to this morning’s “fatal accident” and expected to reopen shortly, but it would likely close again today to recover the vehicle.

“Drivers can take an alternative route through Brookfield.”

A police statement just after 9am said the road was likely to be cordoned and closed at “various times throughout the day” as police worked on recovering the vehicle and examining the scene.

When the cordon was in place, police were not allowing access to the Bay Central shopping centre, but were allowing cars to leave.

Stuff reported that a woman standing in a group of people near the water could be heard repeatedly wailing, “No, no, no”.

Chapel Cafe worker Steve Singh said he saw police speaking with people who appeared to be family members near the scene.

Singh said “they were looking upset” as they spoke with officers behind a building.

Singh said Chapel St had been blocked and the road was empty apart from police vehicles and officers investigating.

“There’s nobody coming in and going out.”

Singh said the blocked road was impacting business operations.

Police said the dive squad had been notified and was expected to arrive later today.

Emergency services were called about 12.40am after reports that a car had entered the water off Chapel St, near the shopping centre.

“One person in the vehicle is out and is in hospital, while police are continuing to work to determine whether a second person was in the car,” police said.

Chapel St was closed early this morning. It reopened during the morning rush, but was closed again before 9am.

Hato Hone St John was called at 12.36am. One person was taken to Tauranga Hospital in a moderate condition.

Fire and Emergency confirmed it was called to the incident, with one person already on land when firefighters arrived.
