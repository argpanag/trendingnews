West Ham vs Fulham Lineups: Confirmed XIs for Carabao Cup

TheTools · 15.09.2026 17:46 · source · permalink

West Ham have confirmed their starting XI against Fulham, with Finlay Herrick, Lewis Orford and Joshua Ajala handed opportunities.

Jarrod Bowen and Joël Piroe are among the substitutes for the Carabao Cup third-round tie at London Stadium.

Max Kilman captains the Hammers, who kick off at 7.45pm BST after Friday’s 6–0 Championship victory over Wrexham.

West Ham confirmed XI vs Fulham

West Ham: Herrick; Walker-Peters, Mavropanos, Kilman, Morato, Engels, Kanté, Orford, Mukasa, Pablo, Ajala.

Substitutes: Hermansen, Piroe, Solomon, Castellanos, Bowen, Magassa, Scarles, Golambeckis, Mayers.

Nuno Espírito Santo has combined opportunities for younger players with experience throughout the side.

Herrick starts in goal, with Kyle Walker-Peters, Konstantinos Mavropanos, Kilman and Morato also selected.

Lewis Orford and Mohamadou Kanté feature alongside Arne Engels, while Divine Mukasa, Pablo and Ajala complete the starting team.

The selection follows the balancing act explored in our assessment of Nuno’s rotation dilemma before Fulham.

Bowen and Piroe give Nuno options from the bench

Bowen’s place among the substitutes is one of the main decisions, with Kilman taking the captain’s armband.

Piroe, Manor Solomon and Taty Castellanos also offer attacking options if Nuno needs to change the game.

Airidas Golambeckis and Ezra Mayers are substitutes, rather than starters, while Mads Hermansen provides goalkeeping cover.

For Orford and Ajala, the tie offers an opportunity to make their case against Premier League opposition.
