Inter vs. Stuttgart: Last remaining tickets and information for fans

TheTools · 26.11.2026 04:48 · source · permalink

Tickets

The Nerazzurri will be in action at 21:00 CET on Wednesday 25 November on Matchday 5 of the Champions League league phase

Following their trip to Atalanta for Serie A, Inter return to San Siro for another European night. The Nerazzurri will face Stuttgart at 21:00 CET on Wednesday 25 November on Matchday 5 of the 2026/27 UEFA Champions League league phase.

Tickets for Inter vs. Stuttgart are still available and can be purchased at inter.it/tickets. On matchday, Wednesday 25 November, the ticket offices near the stadium will also be open from 18:00 CET.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION ON ACCESS TO SAN SIRO

The gates for Inter vs. Stuttgart will open at 19:00 CET. With a large crowd expected, all fans are advised to arrive at the stadium as close to the gate opening time as possible.

All fans are reminded that smoking is strictly prohibited inside the stadium. The following items are also prohibited inside the stadium: crutches, large umbrellas, lighters, laser pointers, smoke bombs, unauthorised flags or banners, helmets, pushchairs, wheeled suitcases, professional camera equipment, selfie sticks and laptops. In the event of rain, only compact umbrellas without pointed tips will be permitted. Failure to comply with the stadium regulations will result in the application of the Code of Conduct.

NEW GATE NUMBERING

The stadium’s gate numbering has changed, with gates 7 to 10 no longer in use. There are no longer any entry gates on the west side of the stadium perimeter, adjacent to the red sectors. These areas will be used exclusively as emergency exits and may be opened to facilitate the departure of fans after the match.

Fans with disabilities can access the stadium via PC4, located next to Gate 11.

RECOMMENDED AND MANDATORY ENTRY GATES

Each spectator’s ticket will indicate the recommended gate for entering the stadium. In most cases, this is a recommendation rather than a requirement: fans may use any other available entrance, opting for the one with the shortest waiting time. There are three exceptions, where fans must use the designated entrance:

  • Gate 2: mandatory for Second Green Tier ticket holders;

  • Gate 3: mandatory for Third Green Tier ticket holders;

  • Gate 15 bis: mandatory and reserved exclusively for Third Blue Tier ticket holders (away section).

HOW TO GET TO SAN SIRO The new stadium access arrangements, introduced as a result of preparations for the construction of the new stadium, will be in place from the opening matchday of the league season. As a result, the number of car parking spaces has already been reduced and will gradually decrease further throughout the season.

The recommended public transport options vary depending on your stadium sector:

  • Orange, Green and Red sectors: fans are advised to take the M1 metro (red line) and get off at Lotto.

  • Orange, Blue and Red sectors: fans can take the M5 metro (purple line) to its terminus at San Siro Stadio or tram 16 to the Stadio Meazza stop.
