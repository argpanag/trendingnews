Princess Beatrice's husband Edoardo reacts to royal baby update

TheTools · 26.10.2026 11:15 · source · permalink

Princess Beatrice's husband has an active social media presence and often celebrates royal news from other families around the world

Edoardo Mapelli Mozzi celebrated an adorable royal baby update over the weekend. 

Princess Beatrice's husband, 42, liked a recent post by Brunei's Prince Abdul Mateen on Instagram. 

Prince Mateen had shared a very sweet new photograph with his daughter, Baby Zahra, on his account @tmski, where he has over three million followers. 

Zahra, whom Mateen and his wife, Princess Anisha, welcomed back in February, had such long hair as she sat in her dad's lap. 

View post on Instagram
 

The son of Sultan Hassanal Bolkiah and his former wife, Puan Hajah Mariam binti Abdul Aziz, was seen in ultimate parent mode as he cradled his daughter, who was sitting in front of a fan and holding a pink star-shaped toy. 

Fellow girl dad Edo then showed his support by "liking" the post on his personal account. 

Edoardo's common ground with Prince Mateen

As fellow royal girl dads, Edoardo and Prince Mateen have much in common. It's not the first time they've shown support to one another on social media.

Edo previously commented a red heart emoji on a picture of Zahra in March, and sent his congratulations when she was born.

When the royals shared their first family photo as a three, Edo commented: "Congratulations Mateen and Anisha. Zahra ❤️."

Beatrice, Edo, and Prince Mateen attended the Jordanian royal wedding in 2023, so they may have crossed paths then.

Beatrice, who is the late Queen's granddaughter and the daughter of Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor and Sarah Ferguson, married property developer Edo in 2020 in Windsor. 

They share two daughters, Sienna, born in 2021, and Athena, born in 2025. 

Beatrice is also stepmother to Christopher Woolf, known as Wolfie, from Edo's previous relationship with Dara Huang.

Meanwhile, Beatrice's younger sister, Princess Eugenie, also welcomed a daughter earlier this year called Adelaide, her third child with husband Jack Brooksbank.

All about adorable baby Princess Zahra

Prince Mateen and Princess Anisha welcomed baby Zahra, their first child, on 8 February this year.

Zahra's birth announcement was shared by Bruneian news channel RTB News on Instagram. It revealed: "Following this release, 17 cannon shots were fired in the grounds of Istana Nurul Iman."

The couple married back in 2024 in an extravagant ten-day celebration at the Sultan's official residence, Istana Nurul Iman. 

They've shared several sweet photos from their family album with Zahra over the past year, including her mop of dark hair.

Commenting on the most recent photo, one person wrote: "Baby Zahra is growing up too fast. Definitely daddy’s little princess.❤️"

"Zahra is gorgeous! Beautiful family," said another.

In June, Zahra twinned perfectly with her mother for her "4 months old" photograph. Anisha wore the 'Ellie Nap' dress from Hill House featuring a pink floral print, while Zahra was dressed in the mini ruffled version from the same designer.
