Turkey vs France Lineups Confirmed: Kylian Mbappe Leads Zidane’s Frontline As Ousmane Dembele, Michael Olise Included For UEFA Nations League Opener | Check France Starting XI

TheTools · 25.09.2026 17:53 · source · permalink

Turkey vs France Lineups: Zinedine Zidane embarks on a fresh chapter as France head coach, taking charge of Les Bleus for the very first time when they travel to Kocaeli for their UEFA Nations League League A opener against Turkiye. Vincenzo Montella’s hosts are equally making history, launching their maiden League A campaign on home soil.

With Kylian Mbappe retaining the captaincy and a French attack brimming with elite talent, Zidane’s arrival lends the occasion an added layer of fascination and anticipation.

Turkiye Team Updates

Montella’s side will lean on a vociferous home crowd at Kocaeli Stadium as they step into unfamiliar League A territory for the first time. The hosts boast a sharp attacking edge, with Arda Guler tipped as a genuine threat capable of unpicking France’s defensive setup.

Turkiye arrive on the back of an encouraging platform, having beaten the United States at the World Cup alongside a mixed run of other results, giving Montella reason for cautious optimism as his side aims to make an immediate statement in their inaugural top-tier Nations League fixture against esteemed opposition.

France Team Updates

Zidane has blended proven quality with emerging talent in his first squad selection as France coach.

Mbappe has been passed fit following a minor fitness concern and is set to lead the line, joined by an attacking quartet of Ousmane Dembele, Michael Olise, Bradley Barcola and Desire Doue offering considerable variety and unpredictability going forward.

Jeremy Jacquet, Andy Diouf and Lucas Da Cunha all earn fresh call-ups, injecting new energy into the group, while Eduardo Camavinga makes his return to the fold.

However, Zidane will be without both Aurelien Tchouameni and Marcus Thuram, two notable absentees from his opening squad.

Substitutes: Butez, Restes, Diouf, Jacquet, Kalulu, Yoro, Akliouche, Camavinga, Da Cunha, Barcola, Doue, Lepaul
