‘GTA 6’ live updates: Record $3.8 billion launch, viral clips and the secrets players are finding in Leonida

TheTools · 21.11.2026 17:12 · source · permalink

The game sold a record 32.5 million copies before launch. Now players are digging up Easter eggs, celebrity cameos and hidden tributes.

Grand Theft Auto VI is here at last, and gamers around the world can finally play after it launched Thursday. So far, the game, which takes up a whopping 212GB on your PlayStation 5 or Xbox, is receiving rave reviews. GTA 6 is streaming all over Twitch, and the memes, clips and hot takes have been pouring in.

Rockstar Games said Friday that GTA 6 sales hit $3.79 billion on launch day, with a record 32.5 million copies sold through preorders alone. At its peak, nearly 8 million people were watching GTA 6 livestreams at the same time, Axios reported.

GTA 6 costs $79.99 for PS5 and Xbox, with an "ultimate" version priced at $99.99.

Players began pre-loading GTA 6 last week, even though they probably had to clear some space on their consoles. Xbox even offered a doctor's note from DJ Khaled (aka Dr. Xbox) for players taking time off to play. You'll probably want to clear it with your boss either way.

PlayStation's network suffered a brief outage Wednesday afternoon as the game became available and players made the required update to their consoles. But since Wednesday evening, it's been smooth sailing.

Here's what people are saying about GTA 6. Just be warned: there are spoilers ahead for those who haven't played all the way through yet.

    • Sat, Nov 21, 2026 04:55 PM UTC
      Jack Brewster

      The jet-skiing dog in ‘GTA 6’ appears to be based on a real Florida dog

      Swim too far out to sea in GTA 6 and a dog in goggles and a life jacket will come to the rescue on a jet ski. Players have nicknamed it "Cool Dog."

      Florida locals quickly recognized it. The dog appears to be a nod to Loosy, a golden retriever from Fort Lauderdale who went viral in May riding a jet ski down the New River while her owner, Eddie Inch, towed her along on a jet-powered hoverboard.

      "You can't go fast enough for her," Inch told NBC 6 South Florida. He said he started bringing Loosy along because he kept getting banned from places for asking people to watch her while he went hoverboarding, Axios reported.

      Rockstar hasn't said whether Loosy inspired the character.

    • Sat, Nov 21, 2026 04:35 PM UTC
      Jack Brewster

      A house Rockstar teased in 2021 hides an NPC who thinks he’s in a video game

      Five years before GTA 6 came out, Rockstar slipped a photo of a mystery house into the 2021 remaster of GTA: San Andreas. Fans guessed it was from GTA 6, and they were right.

      Players found the house in Belville, in Leonida's Leonard County, on launch day, IGN reported. Break into the garage and you'll find the hideout of a conspiracy theorist who has figured out he lives inside a video game.

      His notes say he can't buy a plane ticket to Los Santos or drive out of the state, so he's planning to escape by boat. He's also noticed people who look identical, neighbors who never go inside their homes, people walking through walls and a strange spike in violent crime. And he's concluded that two people, "J" and "L," aren't affected. Those are almost certainly Jason and Lucia, the game's two main characters.

    • Sat, Nov 21, 2026 04:32 PM UTC
      Jack Brewster

      About three-quarters of ‘GTA 6’ sales were on PlayStation, analysts estimate

      Around 75% of Grand Theft Auto VI sales have been on PlayStation 5, with the rest on Xbox, analytics firm Sensor Tower estimated, the BBC reported. The game isn't available on PC yet.

      The launch also pushed internet traffic to new highs. Cloudflare said PS5 download traffic in the U.K. peaked at 8.6 times normal levels around launch. Traffic to Rockstar's online services hit 9.3 times normal on the U.S. East Coast.

      In New Zealand, where the game came out first, traffic jumped nearly 15-fold four hours before launch. Cloudflare said that suggests some players switched their console region to start playing early.

    • Fri, Nov 20, 2026 09:36 PM UTC
      Kim Lyons

      Mark Zuckerberg may or may not be playing 'GTA 6'.

      Grand Theft Auto VI has drawn attention from a wide swath of celebrities, and there's already a bunch of AI-generated slop made to look like cut scenes from the game.

      It's a little weird then that Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg is kind of in both camps. He posted a faked screengrab that looks like one from the game, where he changed Lucia's name and picture to that of his wife, Priscilla.

      But it looks like Zuckerberg may have lifted the joke post from another social account that referred to GTA 6 as "the Sims for men."

      Some men online have expressed their displeasure with the amount of time Jason spends in the game doing thoughtful things for Lucia. Zuckerberg's thoughts on this are unclear — and it's also unclear if he actually picked up any groceries, and if so, whether his wife appreciated the gesture.

    • Fri, Nov 20, 2026 08:58 PM UTC
      Dan Thorp-Lancaster

      'GTA 6' player discovers nod to Tom Cruise's character in the 2004 film 'Collateral'.

      GTA 6 is dripping with pop culture references, including nods to films and television. One of the most interesting interactions so far was discovered by a player streaming the game, who was taken hostage while driving by a character that's eerily similar to Vincent, a contract killer played by Tom Cruise in the 2004 film Collateral.

      The character lets himself into the back of the player's car, then pulls a gun and tells him to drive. After forcing him to run a red light, the character tells him to drive to a specific spot. Once they arrive, the gun-wielding criminal tells the player that his car and face are now captured by every traffic camera between the red light they ran and their destination. The message? He better get out of there.

      The hitman then goes on to take out several other characters at their destination.

      It's a neat easter egg that mirrors the events of the film, where a taxi driver name Max, who is played by Jamie Foxx, is put in a similar situation by Cruise's Vincent. You can check out the scene below.

    • Fri, Nov 20, 2026 08:23 PM UTC
      Kim Lyons

      Rockstar Games says 'GTA 6' sales hit nearly $3.8 billion in its first day

      Rockstar Games said Friday that Grand Theft Auto VI sales hit $3.79 billion on its launch day, calling it the biggest entertainment launch in history.

      The company said it sold a record 32.5 million copies through preorders alone.

      For perspective: The highest-grossing movie of 2026 so far, Spider-Man: Brand New Day, brought in $2.5 billion worldwide over its entire run. GTA 5, which has sold roughly 235 million copies, has brought in more than $10 billion in global revenue since its release in 2013. GTA 5 made more than $800 million in the first 24 hours after its release.

      "We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn't be more excited to share this moment with you," Sam Houser, president and founder of Rockstar Games, said in a statement.

      Within 24 hours of its official launch, GTA 6 had a nearly perfect score on review site Metacritic.

    • Fri, Nov 20, 2026 08:13 PM UTC
      Kim Lyons

      'GTA 6' fit checks: Why Jason's and Lucia's wardrobe changes aren't just about looking good.

      In GTA 6, Jason and Lucia have many options for changing outfits that go beyond just looking fresh (although more than a few gamers have dropped a lot of in-game money on Lucia's outfits).

      Players can change what their character wears by using the safehouse's wardrobe, keeping spare outfits in a vehicle's trunk or using various changing rooms at gyms and clothing stores. Once you start free roaming in the game's first chapter and have control of Jason, you'll then be able to change what he's wearing. Later, you can switch outfits for Lucia as well.

      While it's always nice for them to look fancy, there are practical reasons for swapping Lucia and Jason's fits in GTA 6 as well. A new wardrdobe or look can make it easier to escape detection by law enforcement, espeically when you're in levels of the game where you're wanted by said law enforcement. And ideally you should change in-game outfits privately, not in a place where you might be spotted.

    • Fri, Nov 20, 2026 07:55 PM UTC
      Dan Thorp-Lancaster

      The Weeknd tracked down his $50 million waterfront mansion in 'GTA 6'.

      How realistic is GTA 6? Realistic enough for a famous singer to find his multimillion-dollar home in the game. While cruising around on a boat in GTA 6, The Weeknd found his waterfront mansion — which looks surprisingly close to the real thing. The artist snapped up the mansion, located in Miami's Coral Gables area, in 2025 for more than $50 million. It's located on a V-shaped peninsula and is a whopping 33,000 square feet with eight bedrooms and nine full bathrooms.

      In other The Weeknd x GTA news, the singer has a new song that was just found in the game. It's a collab with French electronic duo Justice, who composed the soundtrack for GTA 6. The song is part of a mission, and it's expected that it will feature on the game's soundtrack that is set for release on December 11.

    • Fri, Nov 20, 2026 07:20 PM UTC
      Kim Lyons

      What happens if Lucia gets away in the prologue of 'GTA 6'?

      Early on in Grand Theft Auto VI, Lucia is in Leonida Penitentiary and sits down with a counselor where she describes the robbery of a check-cashing store, which is what landed her in prison.

      Players can then take control of Lucia in the flashback, where cops spot her carrying a bag from the robbery and she attempts to escape. Most often, the sequence plays out with Lucia gets captured (which is necessary to get her to the present-day prison cut scene).

      But some players have discovered what happens if Lucia initially gets away from police during the robbery. She goes back to the scene to get something important she left behind.

      First, we flash forward to the prison where Lucia describes what happens next. We then go into the robbery flashback again where, as she's headed back to the coffee shop, a dye pack bursts in the bag of stuff she boosted in the robbery. Lucia can't see and the Vice City cops finally surround her and take her into custody. She begs them to let her go back to the coffee shop as they throw her in the back of a cruiser.

      No further spoilers here, but watch the video clip above at your own risk. It's a major plot point that explains a lot about Lucia's motivations.

    • Fri, Nov 20, 2026 06:51 PM UTC
      Dan Thorp-Lancaster

      'GTA 6' players have found tributes to NASCAR legend Kyle Busch and 'Fast & Furious' star Paul Walker.

      There are lots of nods to real-life pop culture throughout GTA 6, and players have stumbled upon some more solemn tributes to celebrities who have passed. One of the first to be discovered is an in-game version of actor Paul Walker's Mitsubishi Eclipse from the Fast & Furious movies. Walker died in 2013 following a fatal car crash at the age of 40.

      In another area of the game, racing fans noticed that a car parked outside a racetrack resembles the one driven by NASCAR legend Kyle Busch. It even features a prominent number 18, which is the number Busch drove for much of his career. Busch tragically died on May 21, 2026, after a battle with pneumonia turned fatal.

      Those are what fans have found so far, but we may see more as players explore the game's world further.
