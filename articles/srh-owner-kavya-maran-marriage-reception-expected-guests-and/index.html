Kavya Maran and Anirudh Ravichander's wedding plans come into focus

Sunrisers Hyderabad CEO Kavya Maran and music composer Anirudh Ravichander have married on November 11 in Greece. The news gained weight on Friday when Telangana Chief Minister A. Revanth Reddy said he had received an invitation to the couple's wedding reception. The reception will take place in Chennai on November 21.

The marriage talk began in June 2025 and stayed unconfirmed for more than a year. Neither Anirudh nor Kavya has issued a statement on the wedding. The first public date came this week, when a sitting chief minister said the Maran family had invited him in person.

The wedding ceremony itself was a destination event in Greece, held privately with close relatives and friends. The families did not release a guest list or the exact location, so they are still keeping most details under wraps.

The reception is the one event with a public date. Revanth Reddy wrote on X, "The wedding reception of Kavya Maran and Anirudh Ravichander is scheduled to be held in Chennai on November 21." He said Kalanithi Maran and Kavery Maran invited him at his Jubilee Hills residence.

Anirudh had rejected the talk when it first surfaced. In June 2025 he wrote on X, "Marriage, ah? Lol… Chill out, guys. Please stop spreading rumours."

A year later, his uncle, actor Y Gee Mahendran, said the marriage would happen.

Meanwhile, Tamil Nadu chief minister and former film star Vijay has also been invited to the wedding reception.

Revanth Reddy was also personally invited to the reception by Kavya's parents. Further, the reception guest list is still awaited.

The wedding ceremony in Greece is being described as a private function for close relatives and friends.

Who is Anirudh Ravichander?

Anirudh was born on October 16, 1990, in Chennai and turned 36 this week. He is the son of actor Ravi Raghavendra and classical dancer Lakshmi Ravichander. He has one elder sister. His aunt is Latha Rajinikanth, which makes Rajinikanth his uncle.

He graduated from Loyola College in 2011 and studied piano at Trinity College of Music in London. His first film as a composer was 3, directed by his cousin Aishwarya Rajinikanth, in 2012. The song 'Why This Kolaveri Di' from that film became a viral hit.

Since then, he has scored films for Vijay, Rajinikanth, and Shah Rukh Khan in blockbusters like Kaththi, Jailer, Leo, and Jawan. The Tamil Nadu government even gave him the Kalaimamani award in 2025. He launched his label, Albuquerque Records, in February 2026.
