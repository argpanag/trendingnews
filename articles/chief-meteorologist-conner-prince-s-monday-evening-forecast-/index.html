Chief Meteorologist Conner Prince’s Monday Evening Forecast: November 23, 2026

TheTools · 23.11.2026 22:34 · source · permalink

Lima, OH (WLIO) - The Thanksgiving Holiday Week has arrived! Many will be traveling over the hills and through the woods to get to grandma’s house for the big feast. If you are traveling, you will need to plan your trip around the weather as Thanksgiving Day will not be a pleasant travel day.

For tonight, though, it will be quiet and clear. However, those temperatures will be on the cold side, dropping solidly into the middle 20s.

Tuesday will be a good travel day with a mostly to partly sunny sky, thanks to high-pressure nearby. It will be chilly, but not unseasonably cold, with a high around 40. For Tuesday night, clouds will rapidly increase, signaling changes ahead, with temperatures falling into the middle to upper 20s.

Thankfully, Wednesday will be a good travel day in Ohio as we will sneak in one more dry day. Therefore, I highly suggest Wednesday to be the day of travel, from a weather perspective, with dry roads. Clouds will be thickening up throughout the day with highs in the middle 40s.

For Thanksgiving Day, a strong area of low-pressure will rapidly strengthen from the Carolinas and northward into the Appalachian Mountains. This system will have ample moisture and strong upper-level support to promote widespread soaking rainfall all day for Thanksgiving Thursday. While we need the rain, it is terrible timing with all the holiday plans. Rainfall totals will be substantial: around 1.5″ possible. Winds will be breezy as well.

For Thursday night, things get a bit interesting. While there will not be a significant source of cold air with this storm system, the low-pressure may attempt to produce its own cold air. If enough cold air is created within the system, there is the potential for the rain to mix with snow Thursday night and into Friday morning. This is far from certain, though, as a lot will have to come together just right for this to happen given a major lack of cold air from the polar regions. Therefore, confidence is low, but it is possible.

The consensus is for the precipitation to end by late Friday afternoon and Friday night. Seasonable cool weather will persist through the weekend and into next week. Another chance of rain may arrive by Sunday.

Stay tuned to the forecast as changes are quite possible!
