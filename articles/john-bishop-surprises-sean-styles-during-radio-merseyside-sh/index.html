John Bishop surprises Sean Styles during Radio Merseyside show

TheTools · 25.11.2026 12:19 · source · permalink

John Bishop phoned Sean Styles live on air to thank him for supporting him early in his comedy career

  • Published
    23 November 2026

A BBC radio presenter received a surprise farewell from comedian John Bishop during his final programme after more than 30 years on air.

BBC Radio Merseyside presenter, Sean Styles, signed off from his long-running Sunday show at the weekend.

During the programme, Bishop unexpectedly phoned in to thank the presenter for showing him kindness early in his comedy career.

The Liverpool comic recalled appearing on a television entertainment show alongside Styles many years ago, at a time when he was still finding his feet as a stand-up performer.

Bishop admitted he had "struggled" in front of the audience and feared his set had fallen flat.

"I appreciated that you didn't give it the big one or anything like that," he said. "You were just kind and supportive."

Bishop said people do not always realise how much encouragement can mean in the entertainment industry.

"Sometimes people don't recognise that," he said. "I just want to thank you for it when I've got a chance."

"I was in my dad's car and Radio Merseyside was on and I thought I've got to phone him and thank him," Bishop told listeners.

'Good luck'

The pair reminisced about the programme which featured a mix of traditional and alternative comedy acts and was recorded at the Batley Variety Club.

Styles recalled the challenge of performing in front of what he described as a club audience of around 800 people.

Bishop said he still remembered the occasion clearly and wanted to acknowledge the support he received.

He told Styles that he had become part of the soundtrack to many Merseyside lives over the years.

"It's been good having you in the background all the time," he told him.

"When I come home from the game, your voice is there."

He added: "Good luck with everything you're doing next."
