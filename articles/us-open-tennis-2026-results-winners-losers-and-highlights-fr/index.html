Match of the Day: Ben Shelton (8) vs. Denis Shapovalov

On Friday, Ben Shelton and Denis Shapovalov will face off in a highly anticipated match.

Shelton enters the third round after defeating the always dangerous Hubert Hurkacz, while Shapovalov cruised past Luca Van Assche.

Shelton has won all four of the previous encounters of the pairs, but these have all been close matches.

Friday's duel is expected to be no different.

Shelton enters as the clear favorite considering his current form and momentum.

If Shapovalov can find his aggressive tennis early, he has what it takes to pull off the upsets.

However, the match could hinge on his mental fortitude and any lapses in concentration.

Consider this: just last month Shapovalov had Spaniard sensation Rafael Jodar on the ropes at the Cincinnati Open, up 5-1 in the final set, before losing six straight games.

Shelton should be able to do just enough with the crowd behind him.

Prediction: No. 8 Ben Shelton def. Denis Shapovalov in 4 sets

Iga Swiatek was dominant in her straight-set win over Nadia Podoroska.

The six-time major champion won 77 percent of points on her serve while playing a stellar return game.

Then there was her signature consistency, as she committed just 14 unforced errors.

In other action, Naomi Osaka narrowly defeated Katerina Siniakova in three sets.

The former world No. 1 needed just over 30 minutes to take the first set, appearing poised for a routine win.

In the second set, Siniakova raised her level, matching Osaka's aggression to force a third set.

In the third set, Osaka used her heavy serve and baseline aggression to avoid what would have been a massive upset.

The best match of the day was at Stadium 17, where Madison Keys saved five match points to win a three-set thriller against Anna Bondar.

Trailing 5-2 in the third set, Keys won four straight games before relying on her aggression and experience in a 10-point tie-breaker to pull off the houdini act.

Coco Gauff defeated Paula Badosa in a two-set thriller to advance to the third round.

The two-time major champion played a stellar return game and used her aggression in the second set tie-breaker to persevere.

Match of the Day: Jessica Pegula (3) vs. Leylah Fernandez (31)

Jessica Pegula enters the third round of the US Open as one of the top contenders for the title.

The 32-year-old has breezed through her first two matches, dominating with her first serve and showcasing remarkable consistency.

However, the competition is about to intensify, with former US Open runner-up Leylah Fernandez up next.

Since reaching the 2021 US Open final, Fernandez has only made it to the Round of 16 at a major once, but she has been playing impressive tennis recently and could pose a serious challenge for Pegula.

Still, considering Pegula's outstanding form this summer, she appears well positioned to advance to the Round of 16.

Prediction: No. 3 Jessica Pegula def. No. 31 Leylah Fernandez in 2 sets
