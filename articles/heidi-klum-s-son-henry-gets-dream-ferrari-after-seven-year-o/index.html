HEIDI KLUM’S SON HENRY GETS DREAM FERRARI AFTER SEVEN-YEAR-OLD BET

TheTools · 13.09.2026 00:19 · source · permalink

Heidi Klum’s son Henry had a major surprise waiting for him when he turned 21 on Saturday: a red Ferrari F430.

Klum shared a video on Instagram that captured the moment Henry walked outside with her husband, Tom Kaulitz, and spotted the car.

“Oh my God! Jesus Christ. I don’t even know what to say,” Henry said.

Klum chimed in, “I can’t believe it is that time. You’ve been waiting for so many years.”

View this post on Instagram

Kaulitz then revealed that he purchased the Ferrari months earlier.

“I bought it last year. I had it in the garage the whole time,” he said. He added that he purchased it in October at the Barrett-Jackson auction.

As Henry continued taking in the car, Klum pointed out that Kaulitz had finally made good on a promise from years earlier.

“And he kept his promise,” she said.

“And I kept my promise,” Kaulitz replied.

Klum then reminded them how far back the agreement went.

“Literally, like, it’s what, seven years ago?” she asked.

That promise dated back to when Henry was still a teenager and Kaulitz was Klum’s boyfriend. The two had made a bet over a game of Mario Kart, with a Ferrari at stake if Henry won.

View this post on Instagram

In a throwback clip Klum shared earier, Henry explained the deal himself.

“It’s happening on Christmas, and if I lose Mario Kart, then I can’t play video games for three months, and I have to do a painting of me driving,” he said.

“But if I win, then Tom has to give me a Ferrari when I’m 21.”

Henry, who is also building a modeling career, made his runway debut in 2025 and later signed with Next Management. He has since followed his mother and older sister Leni into fashion while keeping much of his personal life relatively private.

Klum is mom to Leni, 22, Henry, 21, Johan, 19, and Lou, 16. She shares Henry, Johan and Lou with ex-husband Seal, who also adopted Leni during their marriage.
