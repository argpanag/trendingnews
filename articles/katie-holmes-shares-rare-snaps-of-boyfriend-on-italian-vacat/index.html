Katie Holmes shares rare snaps of boyfriend on Italian vacation

Katie Holmes, 47, has given fans a rare insight into her relationship with boyfriend Jason Bard Yarmosky, 39.

The loved-up pair spent the last few weeks of summer on a yacht in Lake Como, Italy, with the actress posting photos of the pair on a speedboat, swimming, eating at restaurants and sunbathing.

Katie Holmes, 47, has given fans a rare insight into her relationship with her boyfriend Jason Bard Yarmosky, 39.  Instagram/@Katieholmes

One photo showed the couple sitting side by side with the stunning lake in the background, while another showed Holmes laying in the sun in a white bikini.

In another shot, Holmes is doing a backbend on the rear of the boat.

“Life is a friend of art,” Yarmosky captioned the post.

The gallery of vacation photos comes just one week after Holmes hard-launched her relationship with Yarmosky on her Instagram with a sweet birthday post.

She shared a photo of the pair in an embrace, followed by a grainy picture of birthday candles.

“Happy Birthday to the best of the best❤️,” she wrote under the post.

The pair were first spotted together in July this year. They were photographed holding hands and walking into a date-night screening of The Invite.

Katie Holmes looked relaxed and gorgeous in the photos from their trip. Instagram/@Katieholmes

The pair spent the last weeks of summer boating around Italy.  Instagram/@Katieholmes

Yarmosky is a contemporary artist based in New York known for his paintings and drawings.

According to his website, his work “centres on themes of ageing, time, and memory,” which was largely inspired by his close relationship with his grandparents.

His artworks sell from anywhere between $US14,000 to $US21,000 ($19,000 to $29,000) on the primary gallery market.

Holmes has had many high-profile relationships, including a six-year relationship with Jamie Foxx between 2013 and 2019.

She also publicly dated her Dawson’s Creek co-star Joshua Jackson, whom she often calls her “first love.”

The pair hard launched just one week ago.  Instagram/@Katieholmes

Most notably, she was married to Tom Cruise from 2005 to 2012, with the pair sharing a daughter, Suri Noelle, 20, together.

Earlier this year, Suri dropped Cruise from her name, legally adopting her mothers middle name, Noelle, as her last name instead.

Suri hasn’t had much of a relationship with her Hollywood star father for most of her life. She first made headlines for dropping his surname at her high school graduation in June 2024.
