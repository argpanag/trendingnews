Fernandes leads Man United fury as commentators scream after Amad goal cancelled

TheTools · 25.11.2026 21:02 · source · permalink

Amad saw a goal ruled out(Image: X)

Ally McCoist and Owen Hargreaves were left baffled by Szymon Marciniak's decision to rule out Amad's equaliser for Manchester United against Sporting CP on Wednesday evening. The hosts had a chance to take the lead from the penalty spot half an hour into play, when Lisandro Martinez kicked Geny Catamo while challenging for the ball in his own area.

Luis Suarez stepped up to take the spot-kick, only to be denied by Senne Lammens. However, the Colombian showed resilience on his second attempt just moments later, when Maximiliano Araujo was clattered by the United keeper while looking to defend his box.

Amad looked to have levelled matters in the Champions League encounter in the build-up to the half-time interval, when Youri Tielemans threaded the ball through to the Ivorian winger deep behind the hosts' lines. While Kaique Pereira was equal to Amad's initial strike, it came off the crossbar to allow the United man to nod home from close range.

Celebrations were short-lived, however, with Marciniak quickly awarding a free-kick in Sporting's favour as a result of a foul in the build-up. Tielemans appeared to clash with Gonçalo Inácio as he played the ball through, with the official deeming the action enough to rule the goal out.

That decision ultimately left McCoist and Hargreaves, who were on commentary for TNT Sports for the match at the Estádio José Alvalade, baffled. McCoist said: "He’s given a free-kick. Wow," as Hargreaves replied: "No way."

McCoist then explained: "I think that’s a horrendous decision. I might be proved totally wrong here.

"I think he slides in, takes the ball, but does he catch the man? The referee, obviously, from his reaction, looks as though he might be in trouble."

Hargreaves then argued: "He plays the ball," with McCoist in agreement. The Scot said: "Of course he does," with his co-commentator adding: "I think he catches him on the follow-through."

McCoist then said: "That’s not a free-kick, surely, surely that’s not a free-kick," with Hargreaves noting: "He plays the ball. There’s a follow-through, but he’s got to go for the ball."

McCoist added: “Of course he has, that’s the natural momentum. He’s got to go for the ball; he wins the ball. I mean when things are going against you.”

Bruno Fernandes appealed the decision(Image: X)

Hargreaves then explained: "What’s he supposed to do? He can’t leave it," with McCoist venting: "Listen, Owen, you and I are going to destroy him if he does not make an attempt to win that ball.

“Not in a million years [is that a foul]. I’m baffled, I’m absolutely baffled at that decision.”

Pochettino was spotted shaking his head on the sidelines after the decision, while club captain Bruno Fernandes made his case to Marciniak throughout the entirety of the saga and almost got a booking for his protests.

The United skipper led by example immediately after the half-time break, converting a penalty of his own with 48 minutes played, before scoring from long-range with the ball bizarrely bouncing off a divot and bobbling past Pereira.

But Suarez equalised 10 minutes from time after a huge error from Senne Lammens. United thought they won it late on when the Sporting keeper punched a corner into his own net amid pressure from Martinez and Patrick Dorgu, but referee Marciniak controversially ruled out after a VAR review.
