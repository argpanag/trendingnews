    Some more stats from Como's 9-1 win at Lens if you'd indulge us.

    • Como have equalled the most goals scored by any club in their first four major European matches (19), matching Queens Park Rangers in 1976. They have surpassed the Champions League record by five goals, previously held by Juventus (14 in 1995-96).
    • Lens have suffered the joint-heaviest defeat by a French club in major European competition, alongside Strasbourg’s 10-2 loss at MTK Budapest in 1961. No French side had previously lost a major European home match by more than five goals.
    • Como are only the second club to score nine goals in a UEFA Champions League match, after Bayern München vs Dinamo Zagreb in September 2024 (9-2). They are the first Italian side to score nine in major European competition since Roma beat Altay SK 10-1 in 1962.
    • Como are the first side to have 11 different players score or assist in a UEFA Champions League match, overtaking the previous record of 10 set by Benfica in their 6-1 win against Maccabi Haifa in November 2022.

Liverpool suffer first Champions League loss to Fenerbahce

Liverpool's 100% record in the Champions League was ended by a remarkable comeback from Dirk Kuyt's Fenerbahce.

Alexander Isak came off the bench to restore Liverpool's lead midway through the second half after former Manchester United forward Mason Greenwood cancelled out Trey Nyoni's opener.

However, two goals in quick succession from Englishman Archie Brown and Vedat Muriqi gave Fenerbahce a stunning victory inside a raucous Sukru Saracoglu stadium.

Kuyt, who previously played for Liverpool and Fenerbahce, has been serving as interim head coach of the Turkish side - with former Newcastle player Emre Belozoglu to take over on a permanent basis on Thursday.

This turned into a special night for the Dutchman as Fenerbahce won for the first time in the Champions League this season.

Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola made five changes following the weekend defeat by Arsenal, with Bradley Barcola starting as the team's striker.

Fenerbahce's starting XI boasted over 1,000 Premier League appearances between them, containing the likes of Ederson, Ngolo Kante and Romelu Lukaku.

Liverpool started strongly and led when Nyoni's volley took a deflection off Kojo Peprah Oppong and went past Ederson.

It was the 19-year-old's first goal for the club and Liverpool's first goal against a Turkish side in Turkey since Kuyt scored against Trabzonspor in 2010.

The home side responded swiftly as Greenwood's free-kick deflected off Jeremy Jacquet and beat Alisson.

Both managers turned to the bench after the break, with Iraola throwing on Isak, Florian Wirtz and Cody Gakpo. Gakpo and Isak combined to restore Liverpool's advantage, with the latter heading in from close range.

But a spirited Fenerbahce side drew level just four minutes later when Gakpo's header from Kante's cross took the ball away from Alisson and Brown tapped in from close range.

Brown - born in Birmingham - then turned provider when Liverpool failed to deal with his cross and Muriqi headed into the right corner.

Liverpool have happy memories in Istanbul, winning the 2005 Champions League in the Turkish city, but have now lost six consecutive games against Turkish sides here.
