ROBESON COUNTY, N.C. (WPDE) — A Charlotte man was arrested Saturday after authorities said his vehicle struck a deputy and a patrol vehicle as officers tried to shut down an illegal drifting event at Thunder Valley Speedway.

Terell Harper, 21, was charged with misdemeanor assault on a government official and injury to personal property, according to the Robeson County Sheriff’s Office. He was released on a $2,000 unsecured bond.

Deputies responded multiple times to the speedway Saturday after reports that vehicles participating in the event had struck attendees, the sheriff’s office said.

Authorities decided to shut down the event because of safety concerns and the need to ensure emergency personnel could respond effectively.

As deputies worked to clear the speedway, attendees refused to leave, and several vehicles surrounded a deputy and his patrol vehicle, according to the sheriff’s office.

Harper’s vehicle struck the deputy and patrol vehicle while maneuvering around them, authorities said. Deputies arrested Harper and seized his vehicle.

The crowd also damaged a second patrol vehicle, according to the release.

The sheriff’s office said officers used PepperBall launchers to disperse the crowd after attendees threatened deputies’ safety and interfered with their duties.

The release did not specify whether the deputy was injured or provide details about injuries to attendees.

A similar event at the property was shut down in February after injuries and safety concerns, according to the sheriff’s office.

Investigators are reviewing officers’ body camera footage and social media videos. Additional arrests are anticipated, authorities said.

The sheriff’s office also plans to contact North Carolina Alcohol Law Enforcement for assistance initiating a nuisance abatement process involving the property.

Anyone with information is asked to call the Robeson County Sheriff’s Office at 910-671-3100.
