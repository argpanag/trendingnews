New Google Maps Feature on Android Auto: Gemini-Powered Destination Information

TheTools · 18.09.2026 03:36 · source · permalink

The most recent Android Auto build includes evidence that Gemini will power a new feature in Google Maps, and this time, users might actually love the idea. This is because Gemini will power a new destination card that will include additional information and even photos of the things you’ll discover once you get there.

The new destination card is built around a Gemini-powered AI summary similar to the one you see on your smartphone. Once you tap this section, Gemini offers more information about the destination based on reviews posted on Google. You can also see essential details, such as the address and the phone number, as well as “Good to know” info that includes parking and accessibility options.

The card will also include photos pulled from Google reviews that help you explore the region and get familiar with the destination before you begin driving. The idea is simple, as this new destination card allows you to obtain additional information from Android Auto without the need for picking up your phone.

Until then, Gemini is obviously becoming an essential part of the experience with Google Maps and Android Auto. However, while Google is bringing more advanced capabilities to Android Auto using its AI-powered assistant, it seems to be forgetting the things that users are mostly interested in after so many years with Google Assistant: stability and reliability.

Users have been complaining about all kinds of issues with Gemini in their cars, including the new assistant not even being able to make phone calls or send text messages. Others claimed that Gemini wasn’t by any means prepared for the driving experience because it was incredibly chatty, eventually getting them distracted in the middle of the drive. In some cases, Gemini even caused app crashes, with users being forced to pull over to interact with their apps manually.

Google isn’t giving up on Gemini, and its role will become more important in the long term. This makes sense, considering Google Assistant is going away this month, so expect more such features to appear in the coming updates.

Today’s changes were spotted in Android Auto 17.8, but they’re still hidden as the work continues behind closed doors.
