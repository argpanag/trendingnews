Top DPRK leader inspects nuclear material production sites

TheTools · 26.11.2026 14:50 · source · permalink

PYONGYANG – The top leader of the Democratic People's Republic of Korea (DPRK) inspected nuclear material production sites and a nuclear weapons research facility on Wednesday, state media said Thursday.

During the inspection, Kim Jong-un, general secretary of the Workers' Party of Korea and president of the State Affairs of the DPRK, visited the nuclear materials production base, the nuclear-powered equipment production base and the nuclear weapons institute as he reviewed the nuclear materials and nuclear weapons production in 2026, the official Korean Central News Agency (KCNA) said in a report.

Kim called for strengthening the country's defence capacity, stressing the necessity to address variable nuclear threats to the Korean Peninsula and safeguard the country's security, against the backdrop of "the serious geopolitical changes in the recent international nuclear security structure," said the report.

Lashing out at the recent moves of the "enemy states" to keep upgrading their nuclear capabilities, the DPRK leader noted that "the reckless nuclear arms build-up and expansion of the nuclear military alliance the enemy states are committing rapidly in the region of the Korean Peninsula are posing a real threat to the DPRK's security right."

"This is a current and long-term, prospective threat we should cope with in a thoroughgoing way," he said.

The DPRK will take "super-powerful and resolute" countermeasures in response to any move to abuse international law to infringe upon the country's sovereignty, Kim added.
