PV Sindhu (left) and Tanvi Sharma (right) will be in action on Thursday. | Photo Credit: (L-R) Reuters, Getty Images

PV Sindhu will face Japan’s Riko Gunji, while Tanvi Sharma takes on top seed Ratchanok Intanon in the Round of 16 at the Arctic Open in Vantaa, Finland, on Thursday, October 8.

The two are among five Indians in action on Thursday. Tharun Manepalli will face second seed Christo Popov of France, while Krishna Prasad Garaga and Pruthvi Krishnamurthy Roy will take on Oliver Butler and Samuel Jones, respectively.

Sindhu and Gunji have met once this year, with the Indian winning 21-9, 21-12 at the Singapore Open in May. Tanvi and Intanon have never faced each other.

Where can I watch PV Sindhu and Tanvi Sharma’s Arctic Open Round of 16 matches?

The Arctic Open, being held at the Vantaan Energia Arena, will be streamed live on the BWF TV YouTube channel.
