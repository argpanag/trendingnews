Confirmed Real Madrid lineup vs. Rayo Vallecano: Diomande makes first Bernabéu start

TheTools · 12.09.2026 18:52 · source · permalink

Summer signing Yan Diomande comes into the team, alongside Bernardo, Carreras and Rüdiger.

Real Madrid return to La Liga action on Saturday, looking to avoid falling further behind leaders Barcelona.

It’s a three-point gap but three more matches come up in nine days, starting against Rayo Vallecano. Following that, Tuesday’s trip to Elche and culminating with the derby against Atlético de Madrid (Sunday, September 20). Given that schedule, Mou is shaking things up with four changes from the midweek win over Inter Milan.

To face this new league stretch, Jose Mourinho is boosted by the return from injury of Aurelien Tchouameni and Thiago Pitarch. The trio of Arda Güler, Eduardo Camavinga and Bernardo Silva are also back in contention having been suspended for the Inter match.

Bernardo, a starter in the first two league matches, then came on from the bench against Málaga and Real Betis. He has 205 of 360 possible minutes (57%) and starts again against Rayo.

Diomande gets his chance

Güler does not get a spot in the starting XI, with Yan Diomande coming in to make his first start. He’ll join the likes of Mbappé, Vinícius and Bellingham in an ultra-attacking lineup.

Elsewhere, two more changes. In defense neither Cucurella nor Huijsen will start, with Carreras and Rüdiger coming in. The changes appear designed to keep the squad competitive and, above all, to give the most-used players some fresh legs for the grueling run of matches ahead.
