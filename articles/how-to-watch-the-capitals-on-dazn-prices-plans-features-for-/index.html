Will this be Alex Ovechkin's farewell season with the Washington Capitals? You never know with the ageless superstar, but the NHL's all-time goals leader remains a must-watch player entering his 22nd season in D.C. 

While the Great 8 remains the centerpiece for the Caps, fans will have a new way to watch the team this season, as Monumental Sports Network programming is now available through global sports giant DAZN. Just in time for the new NHL season, DAZN will offer regional subscribers direct access to Caps games and much more.

In addition to dozens of live games each year, DAZN subscribers can watch MNMT's on-demand highlights, original series and more in-depth programming around the Caps and other local teams. 

Here's everything fans need to know to check out the Capitals' new streaming home. 

What is DAZN?

DAZN (pronounced da-zone) is a sports streaming service that gives fans access to live and on-demand events without needing a traditional cable subscription. Available in more than 200 countries and territories, DAZN initially made its mark through its combat sports coverage, especially boxing, MMA and pay-per-view fight nights.

In 2026, DAZN secured regional streaming rights to several NBA teams' games, including the Wizards, Cavs, Knicks, Pacers, Timberwolves, Spurs, Nets and Pistons, along with the Capitals, Rangers, Islanders, Devils, Sabres and Kings in the NHL. 

One of DAZN’s biggest selling points is convenience. Instead of relying on a cable package or satellite provider, fans can stream DAZN through the app on smart TVs, phones, tablets, computers and connected devices. That makes it easier to watch live sports at home or on the go.

Sign Up

How do I sign up for DAZN?

  1. Go to the DAZN website or open the DAZN app.
    Visit DAZN on your browser or download the DAZN app on your preferred device, including a smart TV, phone, tablet or streaming device.
  2. Select your location, if prompted.
    DAZN’s content and prices can vary by country, so make sure you are viewing the correct version of the service for your region.
  3. Choose your preferred DAZN plan.
    Pick the subscription option that works best for you, Annual or Monthly. 
  4. Create your DAZN account.
    Enter the required information, including your name, email address and password. Make sure to use an email address you can access, since DAZN may send account details, receipts or event information there.
  5. Enter your payment information.
    DAZN accepts several payment methods, which may include credit card, debit card, PayPal or gift code, depending on your location and how you sign up.
  6. Review your order before confirming.
    Check the plan, price, billing terms and any pay-per-view purchases before completing sign-up. This is especially important if you are choosing an annual plan or buying a PPV event.
  7. Start streaming DAZN content.
    Once your account is active, you can log in and start watching live events, replays, original shows, highlights and on-demand programming available with your subscription.
