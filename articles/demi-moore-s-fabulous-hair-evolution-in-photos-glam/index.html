Demi Moore's Fabulous Hair Evolution, In Photos - Glam

TheTools · 02.10.2026 18:00 · source · permalink

When a celeb spends decades in the spotlight, we hope to see their career and their look take a ton of interesting turns. The stunning style transformation of Demi Moore has certainly been one to watch, and, so naturally, we've also had our eye on how the actor's hair has evolved through the seasons. Those signature brunette tresses have been nothing short of iconic year after year, red carpet after red carpet — and we've got the photos to prove it. Moore's hair was big and bouncy in the '80s, short and punky in the '90s, and, in the 2000s, she displayed the lengthy Cher-like hair that we can't help but imagine when we think of her. 

When Moore wasn't serving up stunning 'dos, she was stamping her name on projects that have basically become Americana. Hello, "Ghost"! She started acting back in the early '80s, and she hasn't let up since; "The Substance" earned her a best actress Oscar nomination in 2025. She's done all that while also raising three girls, and Demi Moore's daughters are absolutely gorgeous, by the way. Now, there's a reason for a dramatic hair flip. 

Late '70s: Demi Moore was California cute in sun-kissed highlights

Demi Moore may not have been famous yet, but she was dripping in it-girl energy when she was included in a photo story on the day in the life of a typical Los Angeles teenager in 1977. Moore was born in Roswell, New Mexico, moved around a bunch as kid, and eventually settled in LA. Demetria Gene Guynes, as she was known back then, posed in front of the Hollywood sign sporting a voluminous layered haircut, chunky blond highlights sparkling throughout. She rocked the same style as she browsed records for the shoot. 

Early '80s: Demi Moore was a total glamour girl in shaggy layers

From 1982 to 1984, Demi Moore was a regular on "General Hospital," and that soap opera glamour was on full display via her hairstyles. Moore's natural chestnut-brown hair had been darkened to a rich and sultry shade of espresso brown, and cut into shaggy layers. In 1982, Moore was photographed while at the "Summer Lovers" screening afterparty in June in Universal City, California, while she posed for a classic '80s beauty shot the same year. Swoon. Count these fluffy, bouncing 'dos among the '80s hairstyles that celebs are still rocking today. Talk about timeless.

Late '80s: Demi Moore's bouncy ringlet curls were so of-the-era

By the time the late '80s rolled around, Demi Moore's strands had gotten a bit lighter and a lot longer. She was rocking chocolate-brown hair, often styled in tight ringlet, doll-like curls. Photogs caught her at a "Saturday Night Live" afterparty in New York City in November 1988 with her hair loose and flowing. In March 1989, Moore swung by a party in West Hollywood after the Oscars. It was for the 61st annual ceremony, and, befitting the dressed-up affair, she wore her locks up in an elegant updo, wispy pieces left out here and there.

Early '90s: Demi Moore's fluffy short cut catapulted her into hair icon status

For her role in "Ghost," released in 1990, Demi Moore rocked that famous short and boyish bowl cut. In the years after the movie wrapped, her hair had grown out some but was still deliciously cute and snappy, and unlike the styles other Hollywood ladies were rocking at the time. She appeared on "The Oprah Winfrey Show" in September 1991, smiling brightly and sporting a rockabilly-reminiscent style with brushed sides. At the Oscars in March 1992, Moore's curls felt more elegant and effortless.

Late '90s: Demi Moore's snappy pixie cut was a '90s cool-girl go-to

In another iconic cinematic hair move, Demi Moore shaved her head midway through "G.I. Jane," revealing a buzz cut. The film was released in 1997 and, that year, Moore was out and about in Hollywood sporting a cheeky little pixie cut, seemingly grown out some from filming. Moore attended the 54th Golden Globes in Beverly Hills in January showcasing the style with piece-y front fringe. By October, she was rocking more of a natural, wet-look wave at the opening of the Christian Dior Paris showroom.

Early 2000s: Demi Moore debuted her signature long and sleek locks

By the time we hit the new millennium, Demi Moore's hair had grown out and she was sporting the super long, slick-straight locks that she's become associated with. The star's chocolatey strands flowed freely while onstage at the MTV Movie Awards in Los Angeles in May 2003. In June, she hit the red carpet at the Hollywood premiere of "Charlie's Angels: Full Throttle" — in which she starred — her luscious-looking hair tucked behind her ears and rocking a dress that Cher would totally wear. She must've been channeling the vibes of the other famous, long-haired diva.

Late 2000s: Demi Moore's soft layers were right on-trend

As we moved further into the 2000s, Demi Moore switched up her blunt hair by chopping it shorter and adding a bunch of layers: a go-to move for long hair lovers who want to add movement and dimension without losing too much length. When Moore attended a press event with her fellow "Mr. Brooks" castmates during ShoWest in Las Vegas, Nevada, in March 2007, she had a little more warmth in her hair. By September 2009, at the Toronto International Film Festival, her color was a rich, single tone but the layers were still on full display.

Early 2010s: Demi Moore's beachy waves felt effortless and ethereal

In the early 2010s, Demi Moore was regularly bouncing around Hollywood rocking hairdos that were beyond beach ready, apt for a SoCal sweetheart. The "Indecent Proposal" actor attended a Save The Children event in Beverly Hills in January 2011 showcasing a dark and dreamy sea of curls and waves. In October 2014, the spotlight was on Moore's silky-smooth water waves when she stopped for photos at the Hammer Museum's annual Gala in the Garden, meant to celebrate Los Angeles' artists and icons.

Late 2010s: Demi Moore was in her undone-hippie-hair era

Demi Moore's undone hair was incredibly cool in the late 2010s, delivering hippie girl meets rock 'n' roll glam with 'dos that looked air-dried and finger-fluffed. Moore stopped at former Harper's Bazaar editor Glenda Bailey's book launch party in Los Angeles in 2017, looking radiant with loose curls. Of course, Moore's own memoir, "Inside Out," came out in 2019. In it, she chronicled her tough upbringing, her career, and her high-profile marriages. That same year, her hair looked effortless yet chic again at an awards event for charity.

2020s: Demi Moore has traded in her long locks here and there

In the 2020s, Demi Moore has still been seen letting her trademark long hair down, flowing freely in trendy blowouts or flat-ironed bone straight. But she does switch it up here and there, too. She hit the annual Elle Women in Hollywood celebration in Los Angeles in October 2021 rocking a slicked-back ponytail with a center part and a bit of subtle teasing in the crown. A few years later, the actor sported a bouncy bob with rounded ends at the Thom Browne show during New York Fashion Week in September 2026.
