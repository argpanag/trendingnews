Sri Lanka, defending champion in the Women’s Asia Cup, will meet debutant Indonesia in a group stage fixture at the Dubai International Cricket Stadium on Wednesday.

Sri Lanka got its campaign started with a thumping nine-wicket win over host UAE, while Indonesia was handed a harsh welcome into the Asia Cup fold courtesy a tough 71-run loss at the hands of Bangladesh.

Here’s everything you need to know about their Women’s Asia Cup clash in this edition:

When is the Sri Lanka vs Indonesia Women’s Asia Cup 2026 match?

The Sri Lanka vs Indonesia Women’s Asia Cup 2026 match will take place at the Dubai International Stadium and start at 8 pm IST on Wednesday, Septembe 2.

Where to watch the Sri Lanka vs Indonesia Women’s Asia Cup 2026 match LIVE?

The Sri Lanka vs Indonesia Women’s Asia Cup 2026 match will be telecast on the Sony Sports Network and streamed live on the SonyLIV platform.
