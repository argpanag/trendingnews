Is Darnell Mooney a Fantasy Start or Sit if Malik Nabers is Out in Week 1?

TheTools · 13.09.2026 16:35 · source · permalink

Published: September 13, 2026

By: DK Network AI

Malik Nabers’ status for Sunday Night Football is very much up in the air, and if he cannot go, the entire target landscape in New York shifts in a hurry. Darnell Mooney becomes the de facto WR1 in that scenario, and a favorable Dallas matchup suddenly makes him a name worth taking seriously off the waiver wire or your bench. Find out whether you should start or sit Darnell Mooney if Malik Nabers is out in Week 1.

Darnell Mooney Start or Sit Advice for Week 1

The Giants host the Cowboys on Sunday Night Football, and Dallas presents a favorable matchup for receivers. Mooney is a capable route runner with the speed to attack soft coverage, and without Nabers on the field, Jaxson Dart loses his clear go-to option and needs someone to fill that void. In an empty target share, even a modest receiver becomes relevant, and Mooney is more than modest when the ball comes his way.

The honest concern is Dart himself. The Giants’ passing game is not projected to be a high-volume operation, and a young quarterback in primetime under pressure is not a guarantee to distribute efficiently to his next option down the depth chart. Mooney’s production ceiling is directly tied to how well Dart manages the game.

The Biggest Fantasy Risk for Darnell Mooney in Week 1

Even as the top option, Mooney is only as valuable as the quarterback getting him the ball. If Dart struggles, the target share boost means very little. There is also the possibility that New York leans heavily on the run game to take pressure off their young signal-caller, which would limit the passing volume across the board regardless of who is lining up at receiver.

Fantasy Outlook for Darnell Mooney in Week 1

If Nabers is ruled out, Mooney is a Start in deeper leagues. The favorable Dallas matchup combined with an elevated role makes him a legitimate WR3/FLEX play for Sunday night. Monitor the final injury report closely before kickoff and be ready to make the move the moment Nabers is ruled out. If Nabers plays, Mooney goes right back to the bench.
