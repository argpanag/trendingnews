UK weather: Fog and mist for next few days to create dangerous driving conditions

TheTools · 29.10.2026 09:37 · source · permalink
  • Published
    28 October 2026

Thick fog is forecast for parts of the UK for the rest of this week, creating dangerous conditions for motorists, particularly around the morning commute.

Fog formed through Wednesday night leading to difficult driving conditions on Thursday morning.

The most widespread fog is across central and eastern England, with a few patches elsewhere.

Whilst some rain is forecast for the end of this week across the north-west of Scotland, high pressure will build into November meaning more mist and fog could form next week.

Forecast for Thursday and Friday

After the recent stormy weather across the United Kingdom, things are calming down for the coming days.

Thursday's fog will take a good few hours to clear before periods of sunshine follow.

The day has started on a drier note generally but there are a few drizzly light showers across southern counties of England, in western Wales and north-west England.

Through the morning the showers will dry up with temperatures peaking between 13 and 16C mid afternoon, which is slightly above average.

Friday morning will see a chillier start with more extensive fog expected across east Wales, central and eastern England.

In places it will be dense with visibility below 100 metres.

Expect a cloudier, breezier day for Northern Ireland and the western half of Scotland, with brighter skies across eastern Scotland.

England and Wales will be fine with spells of sunshine once the fog lifts. Temperatures will be slightly lower but could reach 16C in some southern spots.

Fog will persist into the weekend

High pressure becomes established across the UK this weekend and into next week

During the night into Saturday, light winds will allow the mist and fog to reform and develop, particularly in east Wales, central and southern England.

This could be dense in places and lead to some hazardous driving conditions for Saturday morning travel. The fog may even linger in some parts until lunchtime.

But across Scotland, Northern Ireland more breeze and cloud is likely so the risk of fog here is lower - it is more likely to sit on the hills and coastal headlands.

By Sunday the weather front will have drifted south allowing a much chillier start across Scotland and Northern Ireland with more cloud as well as patchy fog for England and Wales.

Calmer weather likely to continue

High pressure will continue its residence over the UK into next week.

With light winds, more mist and fog is likely to form during the overnight periods and linger through the mornings.

In some places, the mist, fog and low cloud may not clear at all and make it feel quite chilly.

Much needed rain will also be in short supply.

Despite October giving the drought and severe drought areas of England and Wales around the average amount of October rainfall, some reservoirs are still exceptionally low.

This more settled weather pattern currently looks like it might continue for much of the first week of November. Beyond that, check the latest monthly outlook.
