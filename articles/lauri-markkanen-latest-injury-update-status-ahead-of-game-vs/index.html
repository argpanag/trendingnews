Utah Jazz star Lauri Markkanen avoided a serious injury and has been diagnosed with a sprained right wrist. Markkanen is expected to be listed as day-to-day moving forward.

Utah Jazz star Lauri Markkanen has avoided serious injury after leaving the game against the Memphis Grizzlies.

The team announced that Markkanen has been diagnosed with a sprained right wrist.

He is expected to be listed as day-to-day moving forward.

The injury occurred with 5:58 remaining in the first quarter after he got tangled up with Memphis rookie Cameron Boozer while Jaren Jackson Jr. was setting a screen.

He immediately grabbed his wrist and headed to the locker room, with Utah officially ruling him out shortly after.

The Finnish forward finished with just 2 points in six minutes before exiting.

Prior to Monday's contest, Markkanen had been averaging 20.7 points, 7.3 rebounds, and 2.3 assists through Utah's first three games.
