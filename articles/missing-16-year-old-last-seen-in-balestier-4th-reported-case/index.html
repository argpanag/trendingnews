Missing 16-year-old last seen in Balestier; 4th reported case in 3 days

TheTools · 03.10.2026 08:23 · source · permalink

The police are appealing for information on the whereabouts of a 16-year-old male, Sajid Rahman, who was last seen in Balestier.

Police said in a news release on Saturday (Oct 3) afternoon that Sajid was last seen at about 1.55pm on Friday near 16 Jalan Tenteram, wearing a dark blue T-shirt and dark-coloured pants.

The said address is a HDB precinct near Kallang River. 

Anyone with information about his whereabouts is urged to contact the police hotline at 1800-255-0000 or submit information online at www.police.gov.sg/i-witness.

All information will be kept strictly confidential.

4th case in 3 days

Sajid is the fourth young person to be reported missing in separate cases since Oct 1.

On Oct 1, the police sought information on the whereabouts of 12 year-old Arianna Qaireen, who was last seen in Hougang on Sept 29.

Then, on Oct 2, the police appealed for information on 15-year-old J'mies Oh Hui Min and Tianyi Chanel Maglio, 14.

They were last seen in Pasir Ris on Sept 24, and Bendemeer on Sept 25 respectively.
