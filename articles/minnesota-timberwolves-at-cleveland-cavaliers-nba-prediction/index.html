Minnesota Timberwolves at Cleveland Cavaliers NBA Prediction, Picks & Odds for Oct. 26, 2026 - AOL

The Cleveland Cavaliers will be playing at home against the Minnesota Timberwolves on Monday at 7 p.m. ET at Rocket Arena.

Coming off a road loss, Cleveland was happy to be home on Saturday, where its crowd gave the team all the motivation it needed, despite 23 turnovers. The Cavaliers managed a 125-121 win over the Brooklyn Nets.

Cleveland’s success was the result of a balanced attack that saw several players step up, but Evan Mobley led the charge by dropping a double-double with 20 points and 10 rebounds. Mobley has been seeing the basket well recently, as he’s shot 58.1 percent over his last 38 games vs. 51.6 percent in his previous 47 games. Another player making a difference was Jarrett Allen, who shot 87.5 percent (7-for-8) from the field en route to 21 points and eight rebounds.

Even though Cleveland won, the Cavaliers were careless with the ball and finished the game with 23 turnovers. They have not committed that many turnovers since Feb. 5 last year, when they racked up 24 against Detroit.

Meanwhile, Minnesota came tearing into Sunday’s game with four straight wins dating back to last season (a stretch where the Timberwolves outscored their opponents by an average of 14.3 points), and they left with even more momentum. They put the hurt on the Toronto Raptors with a sharp 117-94 victory. The oddsmakers were on the Timberwolves’ side, but they didn’t give the squad enough credit as the margin was unexpectedly wide.

Minnesota’s win was the result of several impressive offensive performances. One of the most notable came from LaMelo Ball, who went 5-for-8 on his way to 15 points and eight assists. Ball posted a three-point shooting percentage of 80 percent, which was his best since Feb. 27, 2023.

The Cavaliers’ win improved their record to 1-1. As for the Timberwolves, their victory brought them up to 3-0. Cleveland beat Minnesota 146-134 the last time they met, on Jan. 10 last season. Will the Cavaliers repeat their success, or do the Timberwolves have a better game plan this time around?
