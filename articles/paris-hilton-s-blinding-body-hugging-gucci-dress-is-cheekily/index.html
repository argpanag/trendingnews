Paris Hilton's Blinding Body-Hugging Gucci Dress Is Cheekily See-Through

TheTools · 11.09.2026 05:48 · source · permalink

Paris Hilton shimmered from head to toe while attending the fifth annual Caring For Women Dinner in New York City. She made a glamorous entrance in a dazzling custom Gucci creation. The media personality embraced full-on sparkle for the Kering Foundation’s annual charity gala. The star-studded event brought celebrities together to support programs dedicated to ending violence against women.

Paris Hilton’s body-hugging see-through dress featured a floor-length silhouette, which was completely covered in shimmering silver crystals that reflected light from every angle. Designed by Gucci, the custom gown hugged her figure with a sleek fit while featuring a sophisticated turtleneck neckline and long sleeves. Although the crystal embellishments offered coverage, the fitted design subtly revealed the silhouette underneath. This gave the gown a cheekily see-through illusion that elevated its glamorous appeal.

Hilton paired the sparkling dress with an equally eye-catching Gucci Dionysus crystal medium shoulder bag, valued at $7,900. Crafted in silver suede and adorned with all-over crystals, the luxury accessory featured the label’s signature tiger head closure and metallic hardware. The metallic detail perfectly complemented the brilliance of her gown. 

The entrepreneur kept the rest of her accessories refined yet luxurious. She wore long diamond drop earrings, a sparkling diamond ring, and a French manicure that balanced the heavily embellished ensemble without competing for attention.

Her beauty look echoed the elegance of the outfit. Hilton styled her platinum-blonde hair sleek, straight, and center-parted, keeping the shimmering high neckline as the focal point. Her glam featured softly sculpted cheeks with dusky blush, defined smoky eyes, fluttery lashes, and glossy pink lips with a subtle glitter finish that added another layer of sparkle.

The annual Kering Foundation Caring for Women Dinner took place at The Pool in Manhattan during New York Fashion Week. The high-profile gathering brought together stars in support of a meaningful cause. Hilton’s custom Gucci ensemble proved to be one of the evening’s standout fashion moments. She effortlessly commanded attention on the red carpet by combining timeless Hollywood glamour with modern crystal-studded sophistication.
