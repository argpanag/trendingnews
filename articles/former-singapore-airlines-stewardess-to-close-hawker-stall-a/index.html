Former Singapore Airlines stewardess to close hawker stall after two years of opening - VnExpress International

TheTools · 22.11.2026 10:58 · source · permalink

Tan, 31, who owns Kiang Kiang Taiwan Teppanyaki with her husband, announced in a TikTok video earlier this week that their outlet would close in January next year.

The business has struggled at the Woodlands location since February last year, generating just S$300 to S$400 (US$230 to US$310) in daily sales, Tan said as quoted by AsiaOne.

She revealed that she and her husband had not drawn salaries for three months.

Former SIA cabin crew Cherry Tan (L) speaks in a TikTok video about her hawker stall and herself at the stall. Photos courtesy of Cherry Tan

Previously, the couple had been able to cover losses at Woodlands with revenue from their Bedok outlet, where business had been relatively stable, Mothership reported.

Tan urged customers to visit the Woodlands stall and support the business before it closes. The outlet is expected to remain open until January.

She thanked customers for supporting the business over the past two years, saying it was a "miracle" that the stall had survived in such a remote location.

Cherry and her husband launched Kiang Kiang Taiwan Teppanyaki in 2024. The stall serves Taiwanese night market-style teppanyaki dishes.

Its menu includes chicken chop, Taiwanese fried chicken and pork collar chop. Each dish is served on a sizzling hotplate with sides such as eggs, beansprouts, rice, corn or noodles.

In October 2025, the couple opened the second outlet at a coffee shop in Bedok.
