Weather forecast today September 23: Central and Southern regions with heavy rain

TheTools · 22.09.2026 21:00 · source · permalink

Weather forecast for today, September 23, many areas have showers and thunderstorms, in which the Central and Southern regions have places with heavy to very heavy rain.

According to the National Center for Hydro-Meteorological Forecasting, the weather today September 23, the Northern region is cloudy, with showers and thunderstorms in some places at night, sunny during the day, light wind. Highest temperature 31-34 degrees C.

The area from Thanh Hoa to Hue is cloudy, with showers and thunderstorms in some places at night, sunny during the day. Hue City is cloudy, with heavy to very heavy rain and thunderstorms. The highest temperature in the North is 29-32 degrees C, and in the South is 27-30 degrees C.

The South Central Coast and Central Highlands are cloudy, with heavy to very heavy rain and thunderstorms. The highest temperature in the North is 26-29 degrees C, and in the South is 29-31 degrees C.

Southern region cloudy, with moderate rain, heavy rain and thunderstorms, locally very heavy rain. Highest temperature 29-32 degrees C.

People and tourists need to be wary of the possibility of tornadoes, lightning and strong gusts of wind during thunderstorms.
