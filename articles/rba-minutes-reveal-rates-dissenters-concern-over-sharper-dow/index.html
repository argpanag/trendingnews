RBA minutes reveal rates dissenters’ concern over ‘sharper downturn’

TheTools · 17.11.2026 02:18 · source · permalink

The three members of the Reserve Bank board who voted against raising interest rates on Melbourne Cup Day warned that the economy could be on the verge of a sharp downturn, raising concerns about the impact on unemployment and economic growth.

Minutes from the monetary policy board’s November meeting released on Tuesday revealed the six members who voted to raise rates to an 18-year high of 4.85 per cent argued inflation risks were still skewed to upside, such that a cash rate of 4.6 per cent “was not clearly sufficient to address these risks and return the economy to balance”.
