'One of the greatest shows ever made' compared to Marvel hit by fans lands on ITVX - AOL

TheTools · 14.09.2026 20:47 · source · permalink

Fans have compared comedy series Kevin Can F**K Himself to a hit Marvel series as it finally lands on ITVX. The series, which first hit screens in 2021, stars Emmy Award-winning actress Annie Murphy and attracted plenty of attention from US viewers when it was first released. Now, UK viewers are getting the chance to see what all the fuss is about.

US audiences have compared the show to superhero show WandaVision. Taking to X formerly known as Twitter, one fan summed up the comparison by writing: "This is Wandavision but with humans", while another posted: "This reminds me of @wandavision". Another viewer simply wrote: "Reminds me of WandaVision".

While another viewer who has watched the show said: "Kevin can F**k himself. It is one of the greatest shows ever made."

For those unfamiliar with WandaVision, the Marvel series starred Elizabeth Olsen and Paul Bettany as Wanda Maximoff and Vision and was released on Disney+ in 2021. The show began by deliberately recreating different eras of American sitcoms, with each episode adopting a different visual style and format.

However, as the story progressed, it became clear that the seemingly perfect sitcom world was hiding a much darker reality.

Kevin Can F**k Himself similarly plays with the idea of what happens when the audience looks beyond the traditional sitcom set-up.

Annie Murphy plays Allison McRoberts, a woman who initially appears to have all the ingredients of a classic sitcom wife. She is attractive, good-natured and seemingly willing to laugh along with her husband Kevin's jokes, despite often being the butt of them.

Kevin, played by Eric Petersen, is portrayed as an immature but supposedly lovable husband who appears to have landed on his feet. But Allison's life looks very different once the sitcom spotlight disappears.

The series switches between a traditional multi-camera comedy style and a darker, single-camera approach, allowing viewers to see the contrast between the version of Allison's life presented to the audience and the reality she is experiencing.

As Allison begins to recognise the unhappiness and unfairness surrounding her marriage, she decides to stop playing the role she has been given and take control of her own life.

The show also stars Mary Hollis Inboden as Allison's neighbour Patty O'Connor, Alex Bonifer as Kevin's best friend Neil, Brian Howe as Kevin's father Pete and Raymond Lee as Sam Park, who has a history with Allison.

Kevin Can F**k Himself is available to stream for free on ITVX from Sunday, September 13.
