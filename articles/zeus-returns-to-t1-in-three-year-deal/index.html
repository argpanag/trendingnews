Zeus Returns to T1 in Three-Year Deal

TheTools · 25.11.2026 10:50 · source · permalink

T1 has officially announced the return of Top-Laner Choi ‘Zeus’ Woo-je to the Korean organization in a three-year deal.

The League of Legends player returns to T1 after two years with Hanwha Life Esports, where Zeus won First Stand 2025 and MSI 2026.

Zeus Reunites with T1 Through 2029

T1 has announced that Zeus will return to T1 after two years of absence from the organization that saw Choi ‘Doran’ Hyeon-jun play in the Top Lane for T1.

The Top Laner will play for T1 through 2029 in the announced three-year deal, which will reunite four pieces of the legendary ZOFGK roster in what will now be ZOFPK.

In his first stint with T1, Zeus won LCK 2022 Spring, EWC 2024, and Worlds 2023 and 2024, earning the Finals MVP of Worlds 2023 in the process.

Shortly after HLE’s Swiss Stage exit at Worlds 2026, a report emerged from Korea which stated that Zeus had failed to reach an extension agreement with HLE.

The result was Zeus entering Free Agency upon the completion of Worlds 2026, with T1 swooping up the Top Laner in a reunion with three of his former T1 teammates.

Only Lee ‘Gumayusi’ Min-hyung is missing from that 2023 and 2024 Worlds-winning T1 roster, having moved to play with Zeus at HLE for 2026.

Besides Gumayusi’s absence and Kim ‘Peyz’ Su-hwan’s inclusion, Zeus will slot back into an extremely familiar group of players, playing topside again with Mun ‘Oner’ Hyeon-jun and Lee ‘Faker’ Sang-hyeok.

As for T1, they get arguably the best Top Laner in the world, and a player who has developed into a more versatile and imperious force since his departure.

The signing has massively raised expectations for T1 as they look to secure their first LCK title since that LCK Spring title in 2022.

The T1 roster for 2027 will be:

  • Choi ‘Zeus’ Woo-je
  • Mun ‘Oner’ Hyeon-jun
  • Lee ‘Faker’ Sang-hyeok
  • Kim ‘Peyz’ Su-hwan
  • Ryu ‘Keria’ Min-seok
