Dreamstate weekend plan at Queen Mary

TheTools · 18.11.2026 00:49 · source · permalink

Dreamstate Southern California is set for Friday and Saturday at the Queen Mary waterfront, and the weekend plan comes with a hard limit: there is no parking at the festival grounds.

Long Beach's city Facebook page posted the visitor graphics on November 17, pointing people toward Rainbow Lagoon and a walk over the Queensway Bridge. Organizer details are on Dreamstate's travel page, which repeats the no-parking rule and sends rideshares to a set stretch of East Shoreline Drive.

Leave The Car Somewhere Else

Both the city graphics and Dreamstate tell people to use a free or Premier shuttle from Rainbow Lagoon Park. The festival map marks a shuttle entrance and pickup, plus a separate Premier shuttle entrance. From the rideshare zone, visitors can board a shuttle or walk.

Dreamstate's travel page says shuttles run continuously to and from the event from 3 p.m. to 7 a.m. Anyone timing a last ride should check the operator's schedule.

Rideshare drop-offs are supposed to use designated areas along East Shoreline Drive between South Chestnut Street and Pine Avenue. No drop-offs or pickups are allowed immediately outside the Queen Mary. Walking is the other downtown option: the pedestrian path across the Queensway Bridge is reached from the aquarium side at Golden Shore and Aquarium Way.

Scooters get two instructions that point the same direction. The city graphic says they must be parked in designated areas, with roadways and walkways kept clear. Dreamstate asks riders to park on grass only and not leave scooters in an active roadway or walkway.

Afterparty Aboard The Ship

Dreamstate places the afterparty aboard the ship from midnight to 6 a.m. each festival night.

The festival has four stages: The Dream, The Sequence, The Void and The Vision.

Two Nights, Four Stages

Friday's posted bill puts Andrew Rayel presenting Extasia, ARTBAT and Dash Berlin on The Dream, with David Rust and Olly James, Hannah Laing and Marie Vaunt, MarLo, Matty Ralph, The Rocketman and Visiov. The Sequence lists Astrix, Burn in Noise, GMS, Infected Mushroom, Kaufmann, Maximizer, Neelix, Phaxe and Terra.

The Void on Friday includes A.N.I., BK, Enrico Sangiuliano, Lee Ann Roberts, Panteros666, Pegassi, Reinier Zonneveld live, Schrott Hagen and Shay De Castro. The Vision has BT with Matt Fax, Frankyfeffe, Karma, Marco V, Mauro Picotto, Mirage live, NRG2000, Steve Dekay and Warp Brothers.

Saturday on The Dream is billed with Aaron Hibell live, Above & Beyond playing a Janubeats classics set, Alpha 9 with Ilan Bluestone, Das Pharaoh, Ferry Corsten with Marsh, Joa, Nu Aspect and TH;EN. The Sequence lists Ace Ventura, Animato, Barakuda, Giorgia Angiuli, John 00 Fleming, Perfect Stranger, Rising Dust, Sighter and Vini Vici.

Saturday's Void card includes Aly & Fila with Billy Gillies, Discovery Project, Eddie Halliwell with Judge Jules, John Askew with Simon Patterson, Key 4050, Parallel Worlds, Paul van Dyk presenting The Essence of Acid, and Thick as Thieves. The Vision lists 4 Strings in a classics set, Allen Watts with Talla 2XLC, Andy Moor, Chris Metcalfe, Cold Blue, Darren Porter with Sean Tyas, Maria Healy, Sequence Six and Solarstone.

Midnight On Board

Dreamstate's afterparty page sets the shipboard party at midnight through 6 a.m. both nights.

Front Gate Tickets is the official ticket seller.
