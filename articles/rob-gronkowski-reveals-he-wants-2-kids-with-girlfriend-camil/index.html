Rob Gronkowski Reveals He Wants 2 Kids With Girlfriend Camille Kostek

TheTools · 27.10.2026 12:41 · source · permalink

Isabela Rangel

Rob “Gronk” Gronkowski says he wants to be remembered as a great football player, teammate and eventually, dad.

The four-time Super Bowl champion sat down with Craig Melvin for the latest episode of “Glass Half Full” and shared his thoughts on fatherhood, providing a rare glimpse at his future plans with longtime girlfriend Camille Kostek.

When asked whether or not he would want to have kids one day, he responded with no hesitation: “Of course.”

“I would like two, maximum three,” he revealed. Ideally, he said he would need at least one son to “carry on the Gronk football tradition” but said he wants girls as well.

Craig asked if Kostek has been consulted on these future family plans.

“Oh yeah, we’re all in,” the former New England Patriots tight end responded. “There’s no doubt about it.

“And my brothers have kids,” he added. “We love being around them, and they got to have some cousins to play with eventually.”

Gronkowski and Kostek have been together for more than 10 years. The couple began dating in secret back in 2013 while Kostek was still a cheerleader for the Patriots. The pair met at a charity event and went public with their relationship two years later in 2015.

Asked about marriage plans, Gronkowski said he and Kostek are “going with the flow.”

“I truly am basically married,” he said. “When it happens, it’s going to happen.”

In an interview with People, Kostek shared her own thoughts on having kids.

“When I was 21, I definitely thought I would have been married with kids by now and sometimes I go back and I judge myself — or even now,” she said. “I’ll be like, ‘Wow, I’m behind.’”

She said after a conversation with Gronkowski’s mother, though, she remembered “everyone has their own journey.”

“This is a reminder to self and a reminder to others that we are not meant to follow the same journey,” she said. “There is not a book of life. There is not a right way to live and a wrong way to live. Just letting go and giving yourself that grace.”

Gronkowski said he feels “settled” with Kostek. He said, based on his brothers’ experiences with fatherhood, “it looks easy now.”

“My brother when he had his first kid, I was like, ‘Oh, that’s a lot.’ Now ... my other brother who had his first kid now, like 10 years later? I’m like, that’s actually, it’d be super easy,” Gronkowski said.

“Spoken like a man that has no children of his own,” Craig joked.
