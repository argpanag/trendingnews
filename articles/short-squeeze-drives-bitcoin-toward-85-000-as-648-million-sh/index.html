Short squeeze drives bitcoin toward $85,000 as $648 million shorts liquidated

TheTools · 23.09.2026 00:08 · source · permalink

Open interest climbed 7.59% to $156 billion even as positions were closed out, a sign traders are chasing the move rather than stepping back.

  • Bitcoin traded at $84,984, up 4.7% since midnight UTC, extending its break above the $82,284 high of Sept. 4.
  • Shorts made up $647.9 million of $746.6 million in 24-hour liquidations, including $159.9 million in the past hour alone, on Coinglass data.
  • Open interest rose 7.59% to $156 billion despite the forced closures, suggesting traders are replacing positions rather than stepping back.
