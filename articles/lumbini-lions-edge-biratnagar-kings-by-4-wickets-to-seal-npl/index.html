Lumbini Lions edge Biratnagar Kings by 4 wickets to seal NPL playoff spot

TheTools · 14.11.2026 16:28 · source · permalink

Copyright © 2025 Nepal News. All Rights Reserved.

Nepal News

KATHMANDU: The Biratnagar Kings have been eliminated from the playoff race of the third season of the Nepal Premier League (NPL).

Following a 4-wicket defeat to the Lumbini Lions in a decisive match, Biratnagar’s tournament journey ended in the group stage.

With this result, the four teams advancing to the NPL playoffs have been finalized: the Lumbini Lions, Sudurpaschim Royals, Kathmandu Gorkhas and Chitwan Rhinos have kept their championship hopes alive.

Meanwhile, the Pokhara Avengers, Karnali Yaks, Janakpur Bolts and Biratnagar Kings have been knocked out from the group stage.

At the TU Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, Lumbini successfully chased down a target of 167 runs set by Biratnagar, finishing at 167/4 in 19.5 overs.

In Lumbini’s chase, openers D’Arcy Short and Asif Sheikh contributed 45 runs each. Ali Sheikh added 27 runs, while Sundeep Jora finished unbeaten on 25. Jora accelerated the innings by hitting 1 boundary and 3 sixes in just 11 balls, before Ruben Trumpelmann hit the winning six in the final moments to secure an exciting victory for Lumbini.

For Biratnagar, Aparajit Poudel took 2 wickets, while Charith Asalanka and Rupesh Singh claimed 1 wicket each.

Batting first after losing the toss, Biratnagar scored 166 runs for the loss of 4 wickets in their allotted 20 overs.

Charith Asalanka played a stellar half-century, scoring 64 runs off 43 balls with the help of 1 boundary and 6 sixes.

Shubham Ranjane remained unbeaten on 31 runs. Imran Sheikh scored 24 runs before retiring hurt, and Lokesh Bam chipped in with 15 runs.

For Lumbini, Kishor Mahato picked up 2 wickets, while Abhishek Gautam and Ruben Trumpelmann took 1 wicket each.

Defeated in a must-win encounter, Biratnagar’s playoff dreams are officially over, while Lumbini clinched a crucial win to secure their spot in the final four.
