GTA 6 missions: Full list and how many chapters to expect

TheTools · 21.11.2026 01:11 · source · permalink

Get ready for cultural event of the year.

  • Belle Law

After 13 years, GTA 6 is finally here. Luckily, the GTA 6 missions and open world are stuffed with plenty of content to last you for years to come.

From collectible cards to jet ski dogs, there's plenty to do in GTA 6 outside of the main story.

But whether you choose to meander through at your own pace or speedrun the story, you may be wondering how many missions there are in GTA 6, and how long it is.

So, to help you out, here's a breakdown of every GTA 6 chapter and mission, so you can get a rough estimate of where abouts you are in the story right now.

How many chapters does GTA 6 have?

GTA 6 has five chapters total, which might not seem like much. However, don't be fooled, as each chapter comes with several missions for you to sink plenty of hours into.

Overall, there are 93 main missions in GTA 6 for you to complete in the game. If you're ever unsure what counts as a main mission, have a look on the map and see which ones are NOT listed as 'optional' on the story tab.

On chapter length, Rockstar’s Scott Mitchell had this to say: "The game has 5 chapters in total, and they vary in length.

"Chapter 1 and 2 are slightly shorter if you want them to be, the longest chapters are 3 and 4, we tighten up again to close the game out in 5."

Clearly, a lot of thought has been put into the game's structure, and it's almost cinematic in its five acts, with more time required for Chapter 3 and 4 as the meat of the story.

Chapter 3 has the most missions at 26 missions total, followed closely by Chapter 4 with 25 missions to complete, so you're going to want to put some free time aside to tackle these!

Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

No, GTA 6 does not currently have a mission replay feature.

This marks a shift from GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2, which allowed you to replay missions and earn gold medals from them. These have also been removed for GTA 6.

We recommend having a few different save slots (one in each chapter, for example) if you want to revisit key moments again.

If such a feature is added later, we'll be sure to let you know!
