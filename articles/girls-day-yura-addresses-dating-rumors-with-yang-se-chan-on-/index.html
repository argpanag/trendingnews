Girls' Day Yura addresses dating rumors with Yang Se-chan on Running Man

TheTools · 16.10.2026 07:30 · source · permalink

Singer and actor Yura directly addressed the dating rumors with comedian Yang Se-chan. "we met, but it really didn't show," she added, devastating the room.

On Sunday the 18th at 6:10 p.m., SBS Running Man will feature Yura and Park Cho-rong, who will form an unexpected pink-tinged triangle with Yang Se-chan.

At a recent recording, special guests who would shake Yang Se-chan's heart, Yura and Park Cho-rong, appeared. First, Yura, who had repeatedly been named in the past as the subject of the scandal that "Yang Se-chan dated a beautiful female idol," directly addressed the dating rumor. With the members' attention focused on the two, Yura told Yang Se-chan, "we met, but it really didn't show," delivering a meaningful remark. The unexpected bombshell prompted explosive reactions from the members, and the scene immediately buzzed.

Then Park Cho-rong, whom Yang Se-chan named as his "ideal type" when he appeared on Running Man last January, appeared and fanned the pink-tinged atmosphere. When Park Cho-rong danced closer, Yang Se-chan couldn't hide his raised cheekbones. As members teased Yang Se-chan caught between "the rumored dating partner" Yura and his "ideal type" Park Cho-rong, the opening scene quickly turned pink as if on a dating show, the report said.

That day, the show was organized as a "transfer stick" race divided into Yura's team and Park Cho-rong's team, with repeated choices and transfers. Before forming teams in earnest, Park Cho-rong boldly flirted with Yang Se-chan, saying, "I came because of oppa, so you'll choose me, right?" Not to be outdone, Yura also sent a meaningful look to Yang Se-chan and countered, and receiving both of their affections, Yang Se-chan fell into a happy dilemma.

On the other hand, as Yura and Park Cho-rong kept wanting the same members, unexpected victims emerged: Ji Seok-jin, who was repeatedly not chosen by the two, eventually said, "You two, don't greet me from now on," showing lingering resentment and adding to the laughter, the report said.

Previously, rumors circulated that Yang Se-chan had dated a pretty girl group member, and suspicions were repeatedly raised that the person might be Girls' Day member Yura. Amid that, Yura herself went on Running Man to mention the once rampant rumors, and even said, "we met, but it really didn't show," raising anticipation for the broadcast.
