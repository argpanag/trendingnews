SINGAPORE – A 45-year-old man who had been reported missing on Oct 3 has been found, the police said in an update on Oct 4.

The police issued an appeal for information on Mohamed Faizal Mohamed Salim’s whereabouts earlier on Oct 4, and said he had last been seen in a light beige T-shirt and grey pants near Block 135 Simei Street 1on Oct 3 at around 11.30pm.
