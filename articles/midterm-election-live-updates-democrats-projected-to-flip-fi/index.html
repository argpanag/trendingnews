Updates

Democrat Phil Weiser will win Colorado governor race, CNN projects

Democrat Phil Weiser will win Colorado’s gubernatorial race, CNN’s Decision Desk projects, continuing his party’s hold on the top office in the blue state.

The state attorney general defeated conservative ministry leader and Marine veteran Viktor Marx, whose wild claims — including saying he was forced to kill a man at age 7 — have drawn widespread attention and skepticism.

Weiser will succeed term-limited Democratic Gov. Jared Polis. Colorado has not had a Republican governor since 2007.

Republican Rep. Harriet Hageman will win Wyoming's Senate race, CNN projects

Republican Rep. Harriet Hageman will win Wyoming’s open Senate seat, CNN’s Decision Desk projects, defeating Democrat James Byrd.

Hageman, who has represented Wyoming’s at-large congressional district since 2023, is endorsed by President Donald Trump. She came to Congress after defeating then-Rep. Liz Cheney in the 2022 Republican primary.

Hageman will succeed retiring Republican Sen. Cynthia Lummis, who in 2020 became the first woman elected to represent Wyoming in the Senate.

Democratic Sen. Ben Ray Luján will win reelection in New Mexico, CNN projects

Democratic Sen. Ben Ray Luján will win reelection in New Mexico, CNN’s Decision Desk projects, defeating Republican Larry Marker.

Luján, who in 2021 became the first Hispanic senator from New Mexico in more than 40 years, previously served six House terms and chaired the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee.

Democratic Gov. Kathy Hochul will win reelection in New York, CNN projects

New York Democratic Gov. Kathy Hochul will win a full second term leading the blue state, CNN’s Decision Desk projects, defeating Republican Bruce Blakeman.

Hochul was elevated to the top job in August 2021 following the resignation of former Gov. Andrew Cuomo, making history as the first female governor.

During her tenure, Hochul faced off with the Trump administration on issues like congestion pricing and immigration, while developing a strong working relationship with New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani.

Here are some of the key races: Republicans have carried Texas in each of the last 12 presidential elections — but this midterm cycle, the Senate race between Republican Ken Paxton and Democrat James Talarico has proven itself very competitive.

During the primary against incumbent Sen. John Cornyn, some Republicans had warned that Paxton would be the more vulnerable candidate in the general election. Now Democrats are eager to prove that they can win in the red state.

The Senate race in Michigan is another contest that has been extremely tight. Michigan is crucial to Democrats’ hopes of the net four-seat gain to win control of the Senate. Abdul El-Sayed clinched the Democratic nomination in a primary that was widely viewed as a test of the party’s ideological direction. Now, he’s trying to prove a more progressive candidate can win the general election against Republican Mike Rogers.

Money on both sides has been pouring into Kansas in the final weeks of the campaign after the Senate race there got uncomfortably close for Republicans. Republican Sen. Roger Marshall has made late moves to try and distance himself from President Donald Trump.

Iowa’s Senate seat is also back in play for Democrats, largely as a result of economic frustrations and high costs, especially for farmers.

Republican Sen. Shelley Moore Capito will win reelection in West Virginia, CNN projects

Republican Sen. Shelley Moore Capito will win a third term in West Virginia, CNN’s Decision Desk projects, defeating Democrat Rachel Fetty Anderson.

Capito, who has held the seat since 2015, chairs the Senate Environment and Public Works Committee.

She previously represented West Virginia’s 2nd Congressional District in the US House from 2001 to 2015.

Democrat Helena Foulkes will win Rhode Island’s governor race, CNN projects

Democrat Helena Foulkes, a former CVS executive, will win Rhode Island’s governor’s race, CNN projects, defeating Republican Aaron Guckian.

After losing the 2022 Democratic gubernatorial primary to Gov. Dan McKee, Foulkes this year defeated the incumbent by 25 points amid voter frustration over a key bridge closure in the smallest US state.

The upset marked the first time in eight years a sitting governor from either party lost their primary.

Democratic Sen. John Hickenlooper will win reelection in Colorado, CNN projects

Democratic Sen. John Hickenlooper will win a second term in Colorado, defeating Republican state Sen. Mark Baisley, CNN’s Decision Desk projects.

The former two-term governor and Denver mayor, a businessman turned politician, beat progressive primary challenger Julie Gonzales in June.

Hickenlooper has said he will not seek a third term if reelected.

North Carolinians "standing up for the people Washington has let down," Roy Cooper says

North Carolinians are “standing up for the people Washington has let down,” saying the people have made their “voices heard,” former Gov. Roy Cooper said in his victory speech for North Carolina’s Senate seat.

“We knew that the chaos and corruption in Washington were hurting our people. Today, you said this has to stop,” Cooper told the cheering crowd in Raleigh.

“Tonight the campaign ends, but tomorrow the work of governing begins,” he said.

GOP sources privately fear they've lost the House and that the Senate is looking shaky

Several top Republican sources fear that the early returns suggest a potential Democratic sweep of the House and Senate.

In the House, one big bellwether: Kentucky’s 6th Congressional District is trending toward the Democrat, Zach Dembo. GOP and Democratic sources believe Dembo is on track to win the district that voted for Trump by 15 points in 2024. Plus, poor showings by a number of GOP candidates and incumbents in Florida and North Carolina are sparking major GOP anxiety.

In the Senate, two senior GOP sources say they believe that Democratic candidates in the Ohio and Kansas Senate races are on track to win. Plus, with Republican Darline Graham struggling in South Carolina, Republicans now fear she could also lose in the deep-red state.

Ager will win North Carolina’s 11th District in first House seat flip for Dems, CNN projects

Democrat Jamie Ager will defeat Republican Jennifer Balkcom in North Carolina’s 11th District, CNN’s Decision Desk projects, marking the first House seat flip for her party tonight.

Democrats need a net gain of three seats to capture the House.

Ager will pick up the seat currently held by GOP Rep. Chuck Edwards. In August, he ended his reelection campaign after an ethics panel found “substantial evidence” that the congressman repeatedly made unwanted advances towards two female staffers.

A spokesperson for Edwards’ campaign maintains the outgoing congressman “did not violate federal sexual harassment law.”

Sununu concedes New Hampshire Senate race to Democrat Chris Pappas

Republican candidate John E. Sununu has conceded New Hampshire’s Senate race to Democratic Rep. Chris Pappas, video by WMUR shows.

“This has been an incredible campaign, but it is not our night,” Sununu said, thanking his campaign and adding he had called Pappas to congratulate him on his victory.

Pappas has made history as the first out gay man to be elected to the chamber. He focused his campaign on affordability and expanding healthcare access while frequently tying Sununu to the unpopular Trump administration.

Rogers campaign watching for drop-off between Michigan governor's race and Senate race

Right now, what the Mike Rogers campaign is closely watching when the votes start coming in is if there is a drop-off between the governor’s race and the Senate race. The early numbers they are seeing so far on this metric are positive for them, a senior adviser for the Michigan GOP says.

If there is a big drop-off between the two, that is a good sign for Rogers. Democratic gubernatorial nominee Jocelyn Benson led Republican nominee John James by double digits in two recent polls.

They are especially looking how wide that gap is in Detroit, where they are hoping to see Black voters who weren’t ready to vote for Republicans but would not cast a vote for Democrat Abdul El-Sayed either.

Trump is watching results come in from the East Room

President Donald Trump’s election night reception is underway in the East Room, a White House official says.

He’s hosting Cabinet officials and political advisers to watch results come in.

His mood, as evidenced by posts on Truth Social, has been sour. He has been complaining about Fox News coverage of the election.

As polls close and Republican optimism is wavering, White House officials insist that Trump will not interfere in the election by declaring martial law or encouraging officials not to certify election results.

A number of Trump supporters and election deniers had been publicly calling for Trump to use martial law during the midterms.

Some Trump allies who are watching from the East Room are wondering how painful tonight could be.

It’s not clear how long Trump plans to stay at the event.

Democrats “confident” but expecting a close Senate race in Iowa

In the moments before polls close, Iowa Democrats are feeling cautiously optimistic that the once-blue, now-reliably-red state could trend purple this year.

Democratic Senate candidate Josh Turek, who is running against GOP Rep. Ashley Hinson, voted in his hometown of Council Bluffs Tuesday morning before making his way east to Des Moines, where supporters are beginning to congregate at the State Historical Building at a party themed “Rotate the Crops.”

His team is enthused by high turnout reported across the Hawkeye State, but expects a razor-thin race.

“We’re confident. It’s gonna be close — we’ve always thought that,” a member of his team told CNN.

Democratic operatives in the state are closely watching how Rob Sand fares in his race for governor, suggesting that high margins for Sand, who has long been ahead in the polling, could help pull Turek over the finish line.

Turek has framed himself as a “prairie populist” willing to work across the aisle, and his final day campaigning underscored that bipartisanship: He told reporters after casting his ballot that he even voted for some Republican candidates, including races for county treasurer and county board of supervisors.

Abby Finkenauer, a former Iowa congresswoman who lost her seat to Hinson, said she’s feeling bullish about her party’s chances this time, particularly the state’s three competitive House seats.

“Iowa has broken my heart one too many times. It’s hard to think it’s really gonna happen. But I have let myself get very hopeful,” she said.

GOP's Mike Collins thanks supporters after failed campaign in Georgia for a Senate seat

Republican Mike Collins, whom CNN projected will lose the Georgia Senate contest to Democratic Sen. Jon Ossoff, thanked his supporters through his campaign.

“Y’all give me the fuel to keep going and seeing y’all in that fight is just the magic that’s allowed me to keep on trucking out there,” he told attendees at an election night event.

He also praised President Donald Trump, saying it was “an honor and privilege” to serve as a House representative for Georgia’s 10th Congressional District under the “best president in my lifetime.”

GOP Sen. Ashley Moody will win Florida Senate race, CNN projects

Florida voters will send Republican Sen. Ashley Moody back to Washington, CNN’s Decision Desk projects.

Moody defeated state Rep. Angie Nixon, a self-described democratic socialist whose campaign energized progressive and Black voters but proved too liberal in an increasingly conservative state.

Florida Gov. Ron DeSantis appointed Moody to the Senate last year to replace Marco Rubio, who resigned from the seat shortly after he was confirmed as President Donald Trump’s secretary of state.

Moody will serve the remaining two years of Rubio’s term and will have to run for reelection again in 2028. It’s the first time Florida has voted to send a woman to the Senate in four decades.

Moody previously served as Florida’s attorney general and in the Senate has positioned herself as a staunch Trump ally. She refused to debate Nixon, largely avoided the media and maintained a limited presence on the campaign trail. However, her cautious campaign was ultimately carried to victory in a state with 1.5 million more registered Republicans than Democrats.

Democrat Adam Hamawy will win New Jersey’s 12th District, CNN projects

Democrat Adam Hamawy will win New Jersey’s 12th District, CNN’s Decision Desk projects, succeeding Rep. Bonnie Watson Coleman in the solidly blue seat.

A retired US Army combat surgeon and sharp Israel critic, Hamawy is aligned with the rising progressive wing of the Democratic Party that saw strong momentum in this year’s primaries.

Hamawy will make history as the first Muslim member of Congress from New Jersey.

His win comes after critics resurfaced his past association with Omar Abdel-Rahman, a blind, Egyptian-born cleric who was convicted of seditious conspiracy in a case related to the 1993 World Trade Center bombing. Hamawy was called as a defense witness in the trial.

A campaign spokesperson told CNN over the summer that Hamawy condemns Abdel-Rahman, whom he saw speak in religious settings in his early 20s, and did not have contact with him following his arrest.

Democratic socialist Chris Rabb will win Pennsylvania’s 3rd District, CNN projects

Democratic socialist Chris Rabb will win the race for Pennsylvania’s 3rd District, CNN’s Decision Desk projects, succeeding incumbent Rep. Dwight Evans in the Philadelphia-based seat.

The five-term state representative was bolstered by progressive endorsements from New York Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, Minnesota Rep. Ilhan Omar and the Philadelphia Democratic Socialists of America.

Florida House races showcase Democratic momentum and offer signs of a big Latino swing

In Florida, Democrats are building huge momentum in the party’s bid for a House majority — and the results are also offering evidence of a potentially enormous swing among Latino voters in Democrats’ favor.

Democrats held onto three seats that Republicans had redrawn in an effort to guarantee victories this fall. CNN has projected that Reps. Kathy Castor and Jared Moskowitz will win in redrawn districts, as will Democrat Pia Dandiya in the 22nd District.

In another heavily Hispanic district in central Florida the GOP had redrawn, Democratic Rep. Darren Soto narrowly leads his Republican challenger in the 9th District.

But the biggest eye-opener might come in South Florida’s 27th District, which is based in the overwhelmingly Latino Miami-Dade County.

Republican Rep. Maria Elvira Salazar won by 20 percentage points two years ago. She might hold on this year — but she leads Democratic challenger Eliott Rodriguez by just a few hundred votes. But a swing of that size means the gains President Donald Trump made with Latino voters in 2024 have effectively evaporated, at least in Florida.
