Minor Injuries Reported in Three-Vehicle Crash Near Mendocino Blvd and 37th Ave

A three-vehicle crash near Mendocino Blvd and 37th Ave involving a parked pickup truck resulted in reported minor injuries in Sacramento on Wednesday afternoon, August 19, 2026, according to California Highway Patrol dispatch records.

Three Vehicles Involved in Sacramento Collision

The crash was reported shortly after 3:10 p.m.

Initial dispatch information identified the vehicles as a white pickup truck, a Volvo SUV, and a second white pickup truck that was parked at the time of the collision.

The available information does not establish the sequence of impacts or explain how the parked vehicle became involved.

Volvo Sustains Major Front-End Damage

According to CHP dispatch records, the Volvo SUV sustained major front-end damage in the collision. Towing assistance was requested to remove at least one of the damaged vehicles.

At approximately 3:26 p.m., CHP updated the incident classification to a minor-injury traffic collision.

Authorities have not released the number or identities of those injured or provided additional information regarding their conditions.

Investigation Ongoing

Investigators continue to review the circumstances surrounding the collision.

Additional information regarding what caused the crash may be released as the investigation progresses.

Multi-Vehicle Collisions Can Cause Unexpected Injuries

Crashes involving multiple vehicles can subject occupants to impacts from different directions. Even when a collision is initially classified as involving minor injuries, symptoms such as neck pain, back pain, headaches, or dizziness may develop or worsen after the accident.

Drivers should remain attentive to surrounding traffic and parked vehicles and maintain sufficient distance to safely respond to unexpected roadway conditions.
