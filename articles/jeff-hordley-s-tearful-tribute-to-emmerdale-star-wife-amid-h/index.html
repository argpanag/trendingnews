Okay, I watch the National Television Awards for potential scandals, slip-ups and gossip.

But now I have something in my eye. Thanks a lot for that, Jeff Hordley!

The talented actor was celebrated on the night as he won the coveted Best Serial Drama Performance prize for his portrayal of Cain Dingle during a long played out prostate cancer story.

He humbly defeated heavy competition from EastEnders icon Paul Bradley and his highly talented colleague Aaron Thiara and Coronation Street powerhouses Sue Devaney and Gareth Pierce.

Let’s face it – as Jeff indicated himself – any of them deserved the win.

The notoriously shy actor, who is very different to hardman Cain, I can assure you, took to the stage to accept the gong, and spent the majority of his time paying tribute to the issue of prostate cancer and the people he worked alongside who helped educate himself on it.

Not to mention his own father-in-law too.

Natalie J Robb, known as Cain’s passionate wife Moira (one of soap’s most chaotically enduring couples, utter faves to me), presented the prize to her colleague and ‘wing-man’.

But the most love Jeff threw into the crowd, alongside his own children Violet and Stan, was to wife Zoe Henry, his soulmate he met on the show itself.
