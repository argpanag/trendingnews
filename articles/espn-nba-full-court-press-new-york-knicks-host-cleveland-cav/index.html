ESPN NBA Full Court Press: New York Knicks host Cleveland Cavaliers in Eastern Conference Finals rematch Wednesday - ESPN Press Room

TheTools · 10.11.2026 15:19 · source · permalink

Summary

  • Defending NBA Champion Knicks and Finals MVP Jalen Brunson face Cavaliers and Donovan Mitchell
  • Rockets and former MVP Kevin Durant host Warriors and two-time MVP Stephen Curry
  • NBA Tip-Off and Inside the NBA feature Ernie Johnson, Charles Barkley, Kenny Smith and guest analyst Chris Webber

ESPN’s milestone 25th consecutive season of NBA game coverage continues on Wednesday, Nov. 11 with a doubleheader presented by State Farm on ESPN.

The action tips off at 7 p.m. ET as the Cleveland Cavaliers and Donovan Mitchell visit the defending NBA Champion New York Knicks and reigning Bill Russell Finals MVP Jalen Brunson in an Eastern Conference Finals rematch. Mike Breen will provide play-by-play alongside Richard Jefferson and Tim Legler, with Katie George reporting.

The nightcap features the Houston Rockets and former Kia NBA MVP Kevin Durant hosting the Golden State Warriors and two-time Kia NBA MVP Stephen Curry at 9:30 p.m. Dave Pasch calls the action with Doris Burke, while Jorge Sedano reports from courtside.

NBA Tip-Off presented by Eli Lilly precedes the doubleheader at 6 p.m., featuring Ernie Johnson, Charles Barkley, Kenny Smith and guest analyst Chris Webber. Inside the NBA presented by Eli Lilly airs immediately following the conclusion of the final game. Both shows are produced by TNT Sports.

All ESPN games are also available to stream via the ESPN App.

The 2027 NBA Western Conference Finals will air exclusively across ESPN and ABC, while ABC continues as the exclusive home of the NBA Finals.
