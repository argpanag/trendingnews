Millie Bobby Brown sizzles in glittering bikini bottoms as she jets off for holiday with husband Jake and tiny daughter

TheTools · 02.09.2026 15:35 · source · permalink

The Stranger Things star and her husband are making the most of their summer, having jetted off on a holiday with their baby daughter

It may seem as though summer is coming to an end, but Millie Bobby Brown has proved that she's still in holiday mode.

The British-born Stranger Things star, 22, was the ultimate bombshell on Wednesday, slipping into a pair of rhinestone-adorned bikini bottoms while abroad with her husband, Jake Bongiovi and their tiny daughter, whom they adopted in 2025.

"Fresh out of August," Millie penned alongside a series of snaps from their time away.

Millie paired the stand-out swimwear with an elegant sheer black beach cover-up, her look complete with a pair of oversized black sunglasses and a pair of hoop earrings.

The Netflix star wore her brunette tresses down with a natural wave, with her flawless face mostly obscured by her chic shades.

One of the first to weigh in on the snaps was her best friend and fellow Stranger Things star, Noah Schnapp, who simply left three love hearts in the comments section. 

While others echoed the Will Byers actor's sentiments, following suit with matching emojis, others were besotted by Millie's adorable photos featuring her baby girl.

The mother-of-one is dedicated to protecting her little girl's privacy and only shares photos of her facing away from the camera.

One new snap Millie shared saw the mother-daughter duo in matching red headwear, Millie in an elegant sun hat while her little one had a scarf over her to protect from the beating sun rays.

Other photos in the post included a loved-up snap of Millie and her aforementioned beau, Jake Bongiovi, the son of famous musician Jon Bongiovi, as well as a series of killer selfies.

Millie on motherhood

Millie announced that she and Jake had welcomed their daughter, whose name they have not publicly shared, via adoption in August 2025.

"This summer, we welcomed our sweet baby girl through adoption," Millie first announced in a post on Instagram on Thursday, August 21.

"We are beyond excited to embark on this beautiful next chapter of parenthood in both peace and privacy," she went on, confirming: "And then there were three." The comments section on the post was disabled.

Talking about her desire to become a mother ahead of welcoming her little girl, Millie told  Jason Bateman, Will Arnett and Sean Hayes on the Smartless podcast: "I'm still really young, but my mom actually had her first child at 21, and my dad was 19, and you know, it's been my thing since before I met Jake," she said.

I wanted to be a mom just like the way my mom was to me and ... my grandmother is - she was a huge part of my life," she added.

Speaking about adoption, she explained: "I don't see having your own child, you know, as really any different as in adopting.

"For me, like, my home is full of love for anyone and anything, and so that is what we stand by, Jake and I — our energy in the house is like, 'The door is always open.'"
