Rice rescues Arsenal as Como thrash Manchester United and Real Madrid hit four | MEO

TheTools · 21.10.2026 23:13 · source · permalink

Declan Rice rescued Arsenal with an 82nd-minute equaliser in a thrilling 2-2 draw against Bayern Munich on Wednesday, while Real Madrid defeated RB Leipzig 4-1 and Como stunned Manchester United by the same score in the Champions League.

Rice struck late after Harry Kane had put Bayern ahead from the penalty spot, ensuring Arsenal left Munich with a point despite losing Bukayo Saka to an early injury. In Italy, Nico Paz scored twice and supplied an assist as Como delivered one of the night’s biggest surprises.

Rice salvages point after Saka injury

Arsenal’s evening began badly when Saka limped off in the 10th minute after appearing to hurt his knee. Eberechi Eze put the visitors ahead in the 25th minute, racing on to Martin Odegaard’s pass and finding the net after a VAR review overturned an offside decision.

Bayern responded through Alphonso Davies, who finished calmly after Kane released him behind the defence in the 35th minute. Kane then converted a penalty in the 61st minute after Jurrien Timber handled the ball with a raised hand.

Rice had the final say, combining with Viktor Gyokeres inside the area before slotting past Manuel Neuer eight minutes from time. The draw preserved Arsenal’s unbeaten result on a difficult away trip, although Saka’s injury will concern the north London club.

Real Madrid hit four as Como humble United

Real Madrid maintained their perfect start to the league phase with a 4-1 win over Leipzig at the Santiago Bernabeu. Goalkeeper Thibaut Courtois made important saves before Bernardo Silva opened the scoring in the 43rd minute, capitalising on a spill by Maarten Vandevoordt.

Arda Guler extended the lead after the break, Kylian Mbappe curled in a superb third in the 83rd minute and substitute Yan Diomande completed the scoring with a volley. Tidiam Gomis briefly reduced the deficit for Leipzig between Madrid’s final two goals.

Como, meanwhile, overwhelmed Manchester United 4-1, with Argentine midfielder Paz at the heart of the performance. He opened the scoring inside two minutes, set up Anastasios Douvikas shortly after half-time and then scored again after dispossessing Carlos Baleba and driving towards goal.

Jacobo Ramon headed Como’s fourth in the 76th minute, while Joshua Zirkzee scored a late consolation for United. The result dealt a heavy blow to the English club, while Como’s attacking display established them as one of the evening’s standout stories.

Inter survive dismissal as Galatasaray win thriller

Inter Milan edged Shakhtar Donetsk 1-0 despite playing the closing stages with 10 men. Alessandro Bastoni was sent off after receiving a second yellow card, but Carlos Augusto scored the winner in the 77th minute, controlling Henrikh Mkhitaryan’s long pass before finishing.

Galatasaray came from behind to beat Lille 4-2 in France. Yunus Akgun gave the Turkish side an early lead, but Soriba Diaoune scored twice to put Lille in front. Alexey Batrakov equalised before half-time, and a Nabil Bentaleb own goal put Galatasaray ahead early in the second half. Victor Osimhen sealed the victory with a powerful strike.

Aston Villa beat Viking Stavanger 3-1, with Ross Barkley heading the opener before Emiliano Buendia converted a penalty and Alysson added a deflected effort. Real Betis defeated Feyenoord 1-0 through Isco’s second-half penalty, while Club Brugge beat Lens 3-0.

In Lisbon, LASK recovered from Luis Suarez’s early penalty to beat Sporting 3-2, with Christoph Lang, Sasa Kalajdzic and Krystof Danek scoring for the Austrian side. Suarez added a late goal, but Sporting could not find an equaliser.

The results continued an eventful third round of the Champions League league phase, with Real Madrid, Arsenal, Inter and several other leading clubs facing contrasting tests as the race for places in the knockout stages gathered pace.
