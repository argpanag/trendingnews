Weather expert's verdict on one final 'unusual, very warm' day

TheTools · 28.09.2026 06:58 · source · permalink

London is set to have an ‘unusually’ warm night tomorrow (Tuesday, September 29), according to weather experts at the Met Office. Temperatures could climb to a maximum of 27C, with thermostats hovering around 21C as we sleep.

It certainly stands out this week as it will be around 20C most other days, with downpours forecast all of Wednesday (September 30) morning and into the early afternoon. Tomorrow will be one last warm blast after a scorching summer which saw thermostats rocket to record-busting 38.1C in some cases, following numerous heatwaves.

After that it’s a return to the more reserved conditions we’ve seen in recent times as the colder months approach. In terms of avoiding the miserable weather of the darker months, we may also be in luck, according to the Met Office’s longrange forecast.

There will be a higher chance of wet and windy weather as temperatures are ‘likely to be above average’ at the time.

The unpredictable weather follows what can only be described as a London summer for the books. But, as we reach the final quarter of 2026, the heatwaves and long days have finally come to an end.

Leaves are falling, blue skies are disappearing and we're now enjoying much less daylight than in previous weeks. Sadly, the sunset took place after 7pm for the final time this year on September 21.

Summer was officially over on August 31, according to the Met Office, with autumn underway following the equinox on September 23. This is one of two points in the year when the Sun illuminates the northern and southern hemispheres equally.

Days will continue to get shorter by 2 minutes each day until the winter solstice, which occurs on December 21. This is the quickest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere.
