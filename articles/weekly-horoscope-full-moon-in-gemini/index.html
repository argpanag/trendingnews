Weekly Horoscope: Full Moon in Gemini

TheTools · 23.11.2026 09:20 · source · permalink

In a world where everybody seems to have an opinion on everything, where being the loudest voice in the room is often mistaken for being the wisest, where we’re so busy defending what we know, the most revolutionary thing you can say is: “I don’t know.”

We’ve got the ever-so-curious full moon in Gemini this week and it’s encouraging us to have the humility to question your own beliefs, to entertain perspectives that challenge everything we’ve been taught and to listen, not because we’re waiting for our turn to speak, but because we’re genuinely interested in learning what another other person has to say.

Overheard at the cosmic conference: the moment we admit we don’t know something, we create space for a whole new world of possibilities.

Sagittarius
This soulscope isn't a prediction of what's yet to come. Think of it instead as a mirror to your soul, one that brings your own beliefs and patterns into your awareness. So pay attention to where your self-limiting beliefs are keeping you paralyzed. Notice where you need to vocalize your needs, especially in relationships, as you banish the fear of being too much. On the creative front, you're stepping into a period of collaboration, attracting the kind of co-conspirators who will help you take your vision to the next level.

Capricorn
Your superpower right now? The ability to stay centered through the stormiest of storms. To look at the good, the bad, and the ugly through the lens of wisdom, and to honor every experience as part of the spiritual alchemy you're moving through. Creatively, you're letting go of rigid ideas and embracing the flow. You're showing up as a vessel for Divine Consciousness to express its magic through you. On the romantic front, the cards are indicating the fire of desire: a certain somebody who knows how to make you feel special and take the lead when needed.

Aquarius
What's being offered to you right now, whether it's a new opportunity, a raise, or a promotion, is the answer to a prayer you whispered a long time ago. But manifestation is a two-way process, so remember to play your part in the grand scheme of things. Overheard at the cosmic conference: it's time to walk boldly through that door, with the inner knowing that you are blessed and protected, Aquarius. As such, the new moon this week turns your gaze inward, inviting you to observe your patterns through the lens of wisdom and confront the demons that have been standing in the way of your growth.

Pisces
Pisces, you're reclaiming your magic this season! You're showing up with big manifestation energy, painting the canvas of your life in the brightest, boldest colors on your palette. What's more, some of you are experiencing a creative renaissance too. Let's just say you're more willing to color outside the lines as you break up with the idea of perfection for good. In love, your gift is loyalty and commitment, the ability to stand by each other through stormy weather and remind each other that the glass is always half full.

Aries
It's official, Aries: everything's coming up roses in your world. On the professional front, you're stepping into harvest season, and your hard work is finally paying off tenfold. Now is not the time for rash decisions around finances, though. Honor the invitation to build a nest egg and make the kind of investments your future self will thank you for. In relationships, your gift is reciprocity. This is what it feels like to be with someone who doesn't keep you guessing, someone who's genuinely invested in your well-being and wants to grow alongside you.

Taurus
Truth tea, Taurus: you're not looking for an ordinary romance. You're seeking commitment. Your intention in the coming year is to build something stable and meaningful with your person. So instead of leaving things to chance, have that conversation. Ask the difficult questions, despite the fear of being perceived as too much. And if you're the one keeping things ambiguous, take a moment to consider the other person's feelings. For some of you, the new moon could become a catalyst for a spiritual awakening, the kind that inspires you to study different philosophies and uncover the mysteries of the universe.

Gemini
Here's the thing, Gemini: healing is not linear. Some days you feel like you've left the past behind. Other days, the memories come back to haunt you. Clichéd as it may sound, make peace with the discomfort arising in the moment. The universe is offering you a chance to release outdated beliefs and redirect your energy toward what supports your growth. The good news? Because there's always good news. The new moon in your sign is bringing immense joy and sweetness into your personal life. It's a wonderful time to play host(ess) with the mostest and gather your coven for a wholesome home-cooked meal.

Cancer
You know what the ultimate love spell is? Believing in your own magic, remembering your divinity, and refusing to settle for anything less than you deserve. By saying no to what isn't a vibrational match, you create space for someone who can truly fill your cup, Cancer. Don't be afraid to take that next step. On the professional front, clarity is key. If you've got multiple tabs open, prioritize. Check in on whether the projects you're devoting your time and energy to are taking you closer to your desired destination. If not, course correct wherever necessary.

Leo
You're feeling defeated. It seems that despite your best efforts to move forward, life keeps holding you back. But here's what you've overlooked, Leo: the past doesn't have to repeat itself if you don't want it to. And that desired destination? It's much closer than you think. On the personal front, you're entering a season of drama. Let's just say the people close to you may be in the mood to have the last word. Your superpower: you know exactly when to be fierce and when to be soft.

Virgo
Here's the thing, Virgo: life is happening for you, not to you. Your lesson right now is to step into the role of the observer, to see what has served its purpose in the story of your life, to see which relationships you've outgrown, and to let go with grace. And if there's a conversation you need to have before you say goodbye, now is the time. The good news? Because there's always good news. You've got the Goddesses of Abundance in your corner, and they're bringing forth the sweet fruits of your labor.

Libra
Here's the thing, Libra: we can journal our intentions and whisper our wishes to Mama Moon, but we still need to play our part in the grand scheme of things. Something new and exciting is opening up on the career front, an opportunity that feels like an answered prayer. But it asks you to take a leap of faith, to believe in yourself and your big vision. It's time to show up as your own biggest cheerleader, beautiful. What's more, there are incoming blessings on the personal front, a chance to connect on a deeper level, not just with your partner but with your tribe. Besides, we all know how much you love playing host(ess)!

Scorpio
With the new moon in Gemini illuminating your financial landscape, you may find yourself confronting deeply ingrained fears around money and material security. Perhaps part of you believes you must constantly save for a rainy day, that financial stability is fleeting, or that no matter how much you accumulate, it will never quite be enough. But where do these beliefs come from? Are they rooted in your own experiences, or are they stories you've inherited from your family? Consider this soulscope an invitation to examine your relationship with abundance and to recognize where caution has quietly turned into fear. That said, there's a difference between surrender and recklessness. Financial wisdom is just as important as spiritual trust. Creating a budget, building your reserves, and making informed decisions are all ways of honoring the resources you've been entrusted with.
