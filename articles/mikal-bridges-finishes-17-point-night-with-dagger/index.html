Mikal Bridges finishes 17-point night with dagger

    Mikal Bridges finishes 17-point night with dagger

    Mikal Bridges

    NYK Guard-Forward #25
    Player Stats

    Mikal Bridges accounted for 17 points (7-of-11 FGs, 1-of-2 FTs), five rebounds, one assist and two three-pointers in Tuesday’s win over the Raptors.

    The much-maligned Bridges had one of his best offensive games of the season on Tuesday, shooting a high percentage from the field and reaching double figures for the fourth time. He appeared more confident against the Raptors, and Bridges’ three-pointer with 36.3 seconds left capped New York’s game-ending 9-0 run. Fantasy managers certainly hope that this performance serves as a catalyst for Bridges after a poor start to the season.
    - Raphielle Johnson
