Disability policy reforms have remained a key focus of governments across Australia since the establishment of the National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) in 2013. And rightly so. For too long, people with disability have been the passive recipients of policy intentions, rather than the leading trusted voices. 

Years of well-fought advocacy by Every Australian Counts led to a bipartisan agreement of “nothing about us without us” — a commitment to embedding lived experience into disability policy design and implementation.
