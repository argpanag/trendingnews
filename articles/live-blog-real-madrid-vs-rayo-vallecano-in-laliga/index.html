Real Madrid Looks for a Response After the Betis Setback

Real Madrid return to LaLiga looking to prove that the defeat against Betis was nothing more than a setback. The Spanish giants host Rayo Vallecano at the Santiago Bernabéu in a matchup that comes right after a European victory capable of lifting the mood in the dressing room, but that also raises expectations surrounding Mourinho’s side.

The 0-1 defeat against Betis left Madrid with little room for error in the league and turned this match into an important opportunity to respond in front of their home fans. The outlook is different in the Champions League, however, where Los Blancos opened their campaign with a 2-1 victory over Inter Milan.



Madrid Arrives Under Pressure, but With Reasons for Optimism

The Spanish giants are looking to respond after their setback in Seville, although the home-field advantage could work in their favor, as Real Madrid are unbeaten in their last two matches at the Santiago Bernabéu, a run they will look to extend as they try to regain ground in LaLiga.

In addition, the victory over Inter provided some positive signs. Mbappé stepped up on an important night, while Valverde added another attacking spark.



Rayo Vallecano Arrives on the Rise With Little to Lose

Rayo Vallecano head to the Bernabéu in a much more positive mood after securing their first win of the campaign. The team came from behind against Racing de Santander to earn a 3-2 victory, a result that could serve as a turning point after an inconsistent start.

Andrei Rațiu opened the scoring for Racing, but Rayo responded with a brace from Sergio Camello to turn the match around. The ending was especially intense after Fran Pérez and Jorge de Frutos were sent off, meaning neither player will be available against Real Madrid.

The team managed by Beñat San José have yet to pick up points away from home in this competition, adding even more pressure to a side that will have to withstand one of Spain’s most difficult venues.



History Favors Real Madrid, but Rayo Has a Chance

The recent head-to-head record also makes Real Madrid the favorite. In the last five meetings between the two sides, Los Blancos have won twice, while the other three matches ended in draws. The most recent meeting in LaLiga 2025-26 took place on February 1, when Madrid secured a 2-1 victory.

What Real Madrid are looking for is to win, convince and turn the momentum from the Champions League into a response in LaLiga. For Rayo, meanwhile, the opportunity is a major one. A positive result at the Bernabéu would not only give them points against one of the league’s leading contenders, but could also confirm that their first victory may mark a turning point in their season.
