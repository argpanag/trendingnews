One ticket in Canada wins jackpot in Saturday lottery draw | insauga

TheTools · 11.10.2026 10:09 · source · permalink

By insauga Staff

Published October 11, 2026 at 6:09 am

A Lotto 6/49 ticket matched all six Classic Draw numbers Saturday night, landing one of the biggest prizes of the evening.

The winning numbers for the Lotto 6/49 draw on Saturday, Oct. 10, 2026, were:

12, 18, 23, 27, 28 and 45

The Bonus number was 32.

One ticket matched all six numbers to claim the $5-million Classic Draw jackpot.

Another ticket matched five of the six numbers plus the Bonus number, resulting in a prize of $182,983.30.

Overall, there were 142,053 cash-winning tickets in the Classic Draw.

The Gold Ball Draw also produced a major prize.

The winning Gold Ball number was 53468307-02, which carried a $1-million prize.

The Encore number for the Oct. 10 draw was:

3708678

No ticket matched all seven Encore digits for the $1-million top prize, and no ticket matched the last six digits for the $100,000 prize.

Lotto 6/49 draws are held every Wednesday and Saturday.

The game features two draws with each $3 play: the Classic Draw and the Gold Ball Draw. The Classic Draw offers a fixed $5-million jackpot, while the Gold Ball jackpot can grow until the Gold Ball is drawn.

The next Lotto 6/49 draw is scheduled for Wednesday, Oct. 14.

The Gold Ball jackpot will be worth $22 million, or a $1-million prize if a white ball is drawn.

Lottery players should always check their tickets through an authorized lottery retailer or the official OLG website or app to confirm prizes.
