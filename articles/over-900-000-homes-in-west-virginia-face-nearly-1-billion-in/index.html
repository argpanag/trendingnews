Over 900,000 homes in West Virginia face nearly $1 billion in weather damage each year

Data analytics firm Cotality warns that West Virginia's roughly 900,000 homes face nearly $1 billion in weather-related damage each year, underscoring why homebuyers may need to look beyond a property's price to determine what they can afford.

Here's what to know

A Cotality ranking placed West Virginia fourth among states for average annual property losses across all forms of damaging weather, according to Scotsman Guide.

"West Virginia doesn't have headline-grabbing hurricanes or wildfires, but its topography leaves it highly exposed," Cotality Chief Scientist Howard Botts said in the report.
