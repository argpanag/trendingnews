Scan a code, get your child admitted: MCD rolls out drive to enrol 1 lakh students

Depth, context and analysis

New Delhi, Nov 18 (PTI) Parents looking to enrol their children in MCD schools will soon be able to do so by scanning a QR code outside a school and sharing basic details, as the Delhi civic body looks to bring one lakh more students into its classrooms, officials said on Wednesday.

With around 6.25 lakh students currently enrolled in its schools, the Municipal Corporation of Delhi (MCD) is targeting an increase of one lakh admissions and aims to take the total enrolment to 7.25 lakh, they said.

A senior civic official said the QR code-based registration system, expected to be put in place within a week, will allow parents to register their details and be contacted by the school nearest to their area to complete the admission process.

The initiative was discussed at a meeting of the MCD Education Committee, headed by its chairman, Yogesh Verma, where officials reviewed the enrolment drive and measures to improve facilities in corporation schools.

“Under the system, parents scanning the QR code installed outside an MCD school will only have to submit their name, address, phone number and the age of the child. The information will be used to connect them with the school catering to their locality, whose principal and teachers will follow up with them regarding admission,” Verma said.

He added that the civic body has sufficient space to accommodate additional students and that no child seeking admission would be turned away due to the enrolment drive under the National Education Policy.

“A similar facility will be made available on the MCD’s Edu Life Portal, where parents can register their details online. A nodal officer will be appointed at the Civic Centre to monitor applications and forward them to schools in the respective areas,” Verma further said.

The MCD also plans to publicise the initiative extensively through social media and other platforms to make parents aware of the admission facility.

Verma said he directed officials to expedite the enrolment campaign, noting that admissions had continued even during the Diwali holidays.

According to an official statement, the committee also directed officials to move forward, within a week, on the file related to the recruitment of around 3,000 sanitation workers and watchmen for corporation schools. The process of regularising long-serving watchmen was also discussed.

Additionally, 250 MCD schools will be made garbage-free in the initial phase, with arrangements for regular cleaning, waste disposal and monitoring.

The committee further directed the maintenance department to share work orders for repair and construction projects with the principals of the respective schools. Principals will also be required to issue no-objection certificates after completion of the work, while the concerned junior engineer will be kept informed. PTI MSJ KSI
