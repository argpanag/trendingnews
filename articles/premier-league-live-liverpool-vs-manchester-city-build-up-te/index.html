  3. Who is Koumas?published at 16:27 BST
    16:27 BST

    Liverpool v Man City (16:30 BST)

    Blocking out all the big questions around this match and beyond for a moment, what a day it is for Lewis Koumas.

    The 21-year-old Welshman is making his first Premier League start, almost 17 years after his father, Jason, made his final start in the competition for Wigan in December 2009.

    Koumas, who spent three years with Tranmere before joining the Reds as an under-11, has made three league appearances for Liverpool from the bench this season and started in the League Cup victory over Tottenham in September.

    He was handed his senior debut by Jurgen Klopp in 2024, and scored in the FA Cup against Southampton in February of that year.

    Speaking to BBC Sport Wales, former Liverpool Under-21 head coach Barry Lewtas described Koumas as "a coach's dream".

    16:14 BST

    Liverpool v Man City (16:30 BST)

    "They will go for it and so will we." That is what Andoni Iraola is predicting on the pitch today.

    While Manchester City have the off-field 'noise' around their situation to handle, Liverpool are attempting to go toe-to-toe with the league leaders missing several key players.

    Liverpool are the league-leaders in terms of high-intensity pressures to their opponent’s touches in the opposition’s half of the pitch (48.9%).

    So, who will do what they do best, better?

    16:09 BST

    Liverpool v Man City (16:30 BST)

    If you'll indulge me for a moment, here's what the situation is right here, right now, as far as the football is concerned.

    Manchester City have made a perfect start under Enzo Maresca, signing off for the international break with a fifth straight win as they beat Sunderland 5-3.
