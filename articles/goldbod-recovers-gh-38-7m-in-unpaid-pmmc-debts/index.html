GoldBod recovers GH¢38.7m in unpaid PMMC debts

TheTools · 13.10.2026 14:48 · source · permalink

By Ashiadey Dotse

The Ghana Gold Board (GoldBod) has recovered GH¢38.7 million out of GH¢55.9 million owed to its predecessor, the Precious Minerals Marketing Company (PMMC), by third parties.

The Chief Executive Officer of GoldBod, Sammy Gyamfi, disclosed this when he appeared before Parliament’s Public Accounts Committee on Tuesday, October 13, 2026.

He said the outstanding debts were uncovered during an audit of PMMC’s financial statements covering 2020 to 2024, after the company went five consecutive years without an audit.

According to Mr Gyamfi, the audit showed that third parties owed PMMC GH¢55.88 million as of December 31, 2023.

He said management had since taken steps to recover the money, including issuing demand notices to the debtors.

As of September 30, 2026, the Board had recovered GH¢38.71 million, representing about 69.2% of the total debt.

The remaining GH¢17.1 million is being pursued through legal action and other recovery measures.

Mr Gyamfi explained that about GH¢7.5 million of the outstanding amount is tied up in court cases, while the remaining GH¢9.6 million is being pursued through demand notices, negotiations and other civil recovery processes.

He expressed confidence that the Board would recover more of the outstanding funds before the end of the year.

“We hope that by the end of this year we will have even increased what we have recovered so far,” he said.

The recovery forms part of efforts by GoldBod to retrieve outstanding debts owed to PMMC before the establishment of the Board.
