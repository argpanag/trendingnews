Feyenoord welcome FC Porto to De Kuip on Matchday 5 of the UEFA Champions League in what promises to be a pivotal European clash.

Matchup

Feyenoord vs FC Porto

Competition

UEFA Champions League

Phase

League Phase (Round 5)

Date

Tuesday, November 24, 2026

Start Time (US)

3:00 PM Eastern / 12:00 PM Pacific

Location

Stadion Feijenoord, Rotterdam, Netherlands

With both sides sitting on just three points, the pressure is firmly on to secure a victory and keep knockout-stage ambitions alive.

The Dutch hosts have endured a testing European campaign under Giovanni van Bronckhorst. Feyenoord's only victory in the league phase came in a hard-fought 3-2 triumph over Como at De Kuip. Since then, the Rotterdam outfit has struggled to find consistency, suffering defeats against Inter Milan (3-0), Real Betis (1-0), and FC Barcelona (5-1). Currently sitting in 26th place with a goal difference of -7, Feyenoord will look to turn the tide before upcoming fixtures against Viking, Galatasaray, and RB Leipzig.

Porto, guided by Francesco Farioli, head to the Netherlands looking to build momentum. The Portuguese side revived their continental campaign with an emphatic 4-1 home victory against Napoli, rebounding from earlier setbacks against PSV (1-0), Real Betis (2-1), and Manchester City (2-0). While the Dragons sit 32nd due to earlier results, their overall form has surged, entering the fixture on the back of four wins and a draw across their last five matches.

Standings: Feyenoord occupy 26th position (3 points), while Porto sit in 32nd place (3 points).

Goal Difference: Feyenoord hold a -7 differential (4 scored, 11 conceded), compared to Porto's -1 differential (6 scored, 7 conceded).

Recent Form: Feyenoord are without a win in their last five fixtures (two draws, three defeats), whereas Porto have recorded four wins and one draw in their previous five outings.

Head-to-Head Record: In their previous European meetings, Feyenoord hold the edge with a 2-0 victory and a 0-0 draw.

Van Bronckhorst faces several selection headaches, with Thomas Beelen, Jordan Bos, Jakub Moder, Bart Nieuwkoop, Gijs Smal, and Jeremiah St. Juste all sidelined through injury.

Predicted Starting XI (4-3-3): Ernst; Read, Watanabe, Kraaijeveld, Mármol; Zechiël, Vanhoutte, Targhalline; Hadj Moussa, Ferri, Valente.
Manager: Giovanni van Bronckhorst

The visitors will be without experienced defender Chris Smalling, Vasco Sousa, and Gabriel Mec due to injury, while forward William Gomes remains a doubt ahead of kickoff.

Predicted Starting XI (4-3-3): D. Costa; A. Costa, Bednarek, Kiwior, Fernandes; Froholt, Rosario, Veiga; Pepe, Sainz, Silva
Manager: Francesco Farioli
