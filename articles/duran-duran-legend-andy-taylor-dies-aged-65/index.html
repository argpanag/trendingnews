Duran Duran legend Andy Taylor dies aged 65

TheTools · 25.11.2026 21:45 · source · permalink
  • Breaking
  • Entertainment
  • Celebrity
  • Music & concerts

Updated November 25, 2026 - 10.12pm. First published at 9.45pm

Legendary Duran Duran guitarist Andy Taylor has died aged 65, following an eight-year health battle.

He died of prostate cancer at his home on Monday, surrounded by his family.

Guitarist Andy Taylor of English new romantic group Duran Duran has died aged 65. Getty

“To the world, Andy was a brilliant guitarist, but to us, he was the most wonderful husband, a loving dad and the proudest grandfather,” his wife Tracey saidin a statement posted to the band’s official website.

“He was the rock of our family. For all the incredible experiences music brought him, family always came first.

“Nothing made him happier than having us all together, and no matter how far music took him, his heart was always at home.”

Taylor was diagnosed with prostate cancer in 2018. Getty

Andy was diagnosed with stage 4 metastatic prostate cancer in 2018 and was forced to step back from the stage as his health declined.

“Andy faced the last few years with so much courage and determination. He never gave up, fighting until the very end, never losing his love of life,” Tracey continued.

“The kindness and support shown to him by fans from all over the world throughout his illness brought him immense comfort and strength, and for that we will always be grateful.

“Andy knew just how deeply he was loved, and we take great comfort in that. We will miss him every single day.”

The family also asked that donations be made in his name to Prostate Cancer UK instead of sending flowers or other tributes.

The remaining members of Duran Duran celebrated Andy for being there at the very beginning of their story.

“From the moment he arrived in Birmingham from the North East with a guitar and an unshakeable belief in what we could become, he brought an indelible fire and swagger to our music,” they said.

“His playing drove the songs that took us around the world, and with a dry wit that was matched only by his wonderfully unsentimental, no-nonsense outlook on life he kept us grounded and gave the band its edge through the extraordinary years that followed.

“It was great to make music with him again when the original five reunited in the early 2000s, and that time together, both on stage and in the studio, meant a tremendous amount to all of us.”

Andy Taylor, Nick Rhodes (front), John Taylor (back), Roger Taylor, and Simon Le Bon of Duran Duran. Getty

The band also touched on their November 2022 induction into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame, which Andy was unable to attend due to his health battle.

“Andy faced his illness with remarkable courage, honesty and grace,” they wrote.

“Our thoughts are with his wife Tracey, his children Georgina, Isabella, Bethany and Andrew, and grandson Albie.

“They all loved him deeply and we ask that their privacy be respected at this very difficult time.”

The message was signed with love from Simon Le Bon, John Taylor, Nick Rhodes and Roger Taylor.

Each of them also penned touching individual tributes to their former bandmate.

Le Bon wrote of the “blond bombshell, firebrand teenager” he met in 1980 who could play a mean guitar riff and didn’t mince words.

The five original members of Duran Duran pose before their celebrity-filled gig at The Roxy Theater in 2003. Amanda Edwards/Getty Images

“Andy never took the easy musical route. His overdrive, overdrove us into being the only funk/rock/punk band in the New Romantic movement, with songs that still survive, decades later when his own life is cut so short,” he said.

“Me & him had a special bond right from the start. He’s the one who gave me my band name - ‘Charley’.  

“And after 2006, when he wasn’t touring with us, we spent happy days at the studio in his Ibiza villa, laying down tracks for our latest records.”

Le Bon recalled the wonderful, hilarious, tragic memories of their time together and said the “massive Andy Taylor shaped hole in my heart” will never close up.

John said Andy never really left the group, even when he departed the band in 1985 after Live Aid, and again in 2006 after reuniting in 2001.

“He was always there in the shadows, keeping an eye out, keeping it real. And he will continue to be there, we will keep him close,” he wrote.

“Our love goes out to Tracey and the kids, the awesome family that was Andy’s greatest achievement.” 

Duran Duran have remembered their former bandmate. Getty

Rhodes penned a sweet poem in Andy’s memory, calling him “ambitious, patient, tenacious” and the glue that held the group together.

Roger rounded out the tributes with a message to the “Geordie Genius” that was a part of their lives for so long.

“How fortunate we were to have found him,” he said.

“The incredible, talented musician and gifted songsmith arrived on a train from Cullercoats to knock us into shape and help us grow into the burgeoning version of Duran Duran that took on the world within a year or two of his arrival.

“The sadness cuts extremely deep, but admiration and respect run even deeper for the Taylor that wasn’t related but will forever be my brother. Rest in peace now, Andy.”
