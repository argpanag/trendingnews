Ilocos Norte officials welcome postponement of BSKE

BATAC CITY – Ilocos Norte Governor Cecilia Araneta-Marcos on Friday welcomed the postponement of the Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections (BSKE), following President Ferdinand Marcos Jr.'s signing of the law moving the polls from 2026 to 2028.

Palace Press Officer, Undersecretary Claire Castro, confirmed that President Marcos signed Senate Bill No. 2387, which amends Republic Act No. 12232, extends the term of barangay and SK officials from four to five years, and moves the next BSKE to November 2028, while conducting a press conference at the Mariano Marcos State University in Batac City.

For Governor Araneta-Marcos, the postponement provides barangay and SK officials additional time to continue implementing programs and projects for their communities. “Tuloy po ang trabaho natin sa ating mga ka-Barangay (Our work for our ka-barangay continues). Let’s focus on work instead of politics,” she said in an interview.

“The reasons for the postponement of the BSKE are very clear. Our Barangay and SK officials must work for the Barangay,” said Jojo Cumlat, an incumbent village councillor in Vintar town.

He said that barangay officials are at the frontline of government service, particularly during emergencies, disasters and community-level programs. With the additional time, local officials can continue focusing on programs that directly affect residents without the disruption of an immediate electoral transition.

For barangay leaders, the extension comes with the expectation that they will continue delivering services and responding to the needs of their communities, he added.

Meanwhile, Batac City Mayor Christian Mark Chua challenged village officials to prove their worth as leaders and deliver what is expected of them from the local and national governments.

“There's a positive and negative effect of the postponement, but I would like to see the positive side. There will be more work to be accomplished and for the barangay and province to be more productive,” he said in an interview.

The President encouraged barangay officials to make use of the postponement to improve public service, complete programs that have already been started, and focus more closely on the needs of every community, family and young person. (PNA)
