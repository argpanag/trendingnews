Match Preview

Ethiopia host Mozambique in a Group J Africa Cup of Nations Qualification fixture, with both sides looking to strengthen their positions in a tightly contested pool.

Ethiopia arrive into this match with momentum on their side. Yohannes Sahle's side claimed a 1-0 win over Mozambique in the reverse fixture earlier this month, a result that lifted them off the foot of the group standings.

That victory snapped a difficult run for Ethiopia, who had lost to Senegal and Sudan in their two previous qualification outings. The home side will be eager to back up that result on familiar ground.

Mozambique come into this contest sitting second in Group J, having built their campaign on a strong September. Chiquinho Conde's team beat Sudan 4-1 and drew 1-1 with Senegal, though they could not replicate that form when Ethiopia visited.

A second defeat to Ethiopia would put Mozambique's qualification ambitions under real pressure, making this a critical road trip for Conde's squad.
