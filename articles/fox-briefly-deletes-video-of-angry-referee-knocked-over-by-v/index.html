Fox briefly deletes video of angry referee knocked over by Vikings player

TheTools · 04.10.2026 21:55 · source · permalink

NFL referee Adrian Hill and his crew worked the Week 4 game between the Minnesota Vikings and Miami Dolphins. During the second quarter, Hill got knocked over. He appeared to be very upset about it, which made for an awkward situation since he appeared to be upset with the wrong person.

On a third-down play, two Vikings defenders jumped offside and the whistle was blown. As Hill ran in to make the call, some players had not completely stopped moving. That’s when linebacker Eric Wilson got pushed into Hill. The referee went down and Wilson tried to help him up, but Hill popped up and appeared to yell at him to get back to the huddle.
