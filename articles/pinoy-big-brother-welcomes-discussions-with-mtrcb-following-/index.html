'Pinoy Big Brother' welcomes discussions with MTRCB following complaint submissions

TheTools · 14.11.2026 10:03 · source · permalink

MANILA, Philippines — Reality series "Pinoy Big Brother" is opening its doors for a conversation with the Movie and Television Review and Classification Board (MTRCB) after the agency received over a hundred complaints from viewers.

Last November 12, Thursday, the MTRCB confirmed it would review the numerous complaints submitted to it and raise concerns with the production team.

While the agency did not specify the contents of the complaints, criticism has been growing from viewers over the actions of several "Collab 3.0" housemates.

Among the issues being discussed online is the so-called "dinosaur" challenge involving the male housemates, which drew criticism from viewers who felt that Gehlee Dangca was being treated like a trophy, with the male contestants seemingly competing for her attention.

Another incident that drew negative reactions involved housemates Anthony Constantino and Primo Alterado.

During a punishment imposed on the housemates for violations of the show's MFA rules, Anthony was seen calling Primo "dookie" and twisting a bottle inside his mouth while giving him a drink.

The scene also prompted discussions among viewers about the treatment of housemates during the program's disciplinary activities.

ABS-CBN released a statement to the media addressing the MTRCB complaints and though it has not yet received formal notice from the agency regarding them, the network is "ready to participate in discussions with the Board should we be called upon."

'"PBB' has always promoted positive values and respect for others. With Kuya's guidance, housemates reflect on their actions and learn from their experiences," said ABS-CBN. "Their welfare and well-being remain our top priority."

The statement ended with the network reiterating its commitment to responsible self-regulation. — with reports from Jan Milo Severo
