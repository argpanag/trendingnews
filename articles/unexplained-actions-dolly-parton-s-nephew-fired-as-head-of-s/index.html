'Unexplained actions': Dolly Parton's nephew fired as head of security for her estates, per reports

TheTools · 22.09.2026 12:40 · source · permalink

Nearly a month after her death, Dolly Parton's nephew, Bryan Seaver, has reportedly been fired from providing security to his aunt's properties.

Related video: Dollywood's plans to honor Dolly Parton

USA Today said a termination notice obtained by TMZ allegedly states that Parton's nephew and his security companies were "immediately dismissed."

The letter reportedly said Parton's properties and business matters are controlled by DP Dean Trust, and clarified that Seaver's personal interest in the trust remains the same.

According to TMZ, replacement security has already taken over both of Parton's Nashville homes, her museum, and her warehouse.

Seaver's team responded to TMZ, calling out Parton's longtime manager, Danny Nozell, and Pinnacle Bank.

"[We are] dismayed by the unexpected and, so far, unexplained actions," Seaver and his team said. "We remain unshakably resolved to fulfill her final directive to protect her family, and we refuse to be intimidated by those whose actions we believe are being taken in bad faith, and with the intent to profit from the life of someone who willingly gave so much."

They said they've been comforted by the worldwide outpouring of love for Parton— not only for her music, but for the life she led and the message of love she embodied.

The alleged termination comes weeks after Seaver— son of Parton's sister Cassie— announced his aunt's death on Aug. 25.

"This video announcement is something that she asked of me years ago, before I fully absorbed it as a future reality," Seaver said in the video posted on Dolly's Instagram account. "As her head of security for more than two decades - a role my father Larry held before me— I have imagined the heaviness of this moment but haven't truly felt it until now."

He continued, saying, "It is an honor— an honor that is mixed with absolute pride and great sadness. But sadness lies with us, not with Dolly. Dolly has lived in the light and is in the arms of Jesus, surely met by [late husband] Carl, her parents and countless others who have watched over her and longed for her to meet them in the heavens."

Parton died following a brief battle with cancer, her spokesperson confirmed.

In the wake of her death, Parton's life and legacy continue to be honored, with Tennessee lawmakers announcing plans to rename the Nashville International Airport after her.
