BAKERSFIELD, Calif. (KBAK/KBFX) — The Kern County District Attorney’s Office announced a criminal complaint has been filed against former YouTuber EDP445, or Bryant Turhanemmerson Moreland.

The DA’s office said the complaint was made following an extensive investigation into statements made by Moreland in “The Sick Mind of EDP445” documentary.

On November 2, 2026, criminal charges were filed against Moreland, including:

  • Attempt to Commit Lewd Acts Upon a Child (felony)
  • Meeting Minor for Lewd Purposes (felony)
  • Contact Minor with Intent to Commit Sexual Offense (felony)
  • Distributing or Showing Pornography to a Minor (felony)

The DA’s office said the charges stem from an alleged incident involving a child under 14 years old that happened on April 17, 2021.

In a release, it said, “When the Kern County District Attorney’s Office initially reviewed the incident in 2021 and 2022, there were significant challenges in pursuing prosecution, particularly due to the lack of cooperation from the reporting parties.”

The DA’s office said in light of recent statements made by Moreland in the documentary, the DA Office and the Kern County Sheriff’s Office reopened the investigation.

Following its review of the new evidence, the District Attorney’s Office determined that the evidentiary threshold for filing criminal charges had been met.

On November 4, 2026, with the help of the Las Vegas Police Department, Moreland was found in Las Vegas, arrested, and taken into custody.

An arraignment hearing will be scheduled following Moreland’s extradition to Kern County.
