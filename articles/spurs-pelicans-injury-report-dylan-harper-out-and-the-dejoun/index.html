Spurs-Pelicans injury report: Dylan Harper out and the Dejounte Murray mess

TheTools · 21.11.2026 19:33 · source · permalink

The Spurs and Pelicans are on completely different paths. San Antonio (13-3) has the best record in the West and the NBA's best net rating, while New Orleans (5-11) is 12th in the conference and spending its week figuring out what to do with a point guard in Dejounte Murray who wants out.

These teams will head into today's contest injuries on both sides, but the Pelicans' list is more extensive. Here's what you need to know.

Spurs injury report


Maliq Brown - Out (Two-Way)
Ja’Kobi Gillespie - Out (Two-Way)
Dylan Harper - Out (Left Hip External Rotator Strain)
Tobias Harris - Out (Left Calf Strain)
Jayden Quaintance - Out (Right Knee Surgery)

Pelicans injury report


Malik Dia - Out (Two-Way)
Bennedict Mathurin - Out (Left Plantar Fasciitis)
Bryce McGowens - Out (Left Elbow Infection)
Trey Murphy III - Out (Bilateral Patellar Tendinopathy)
Dejounte Murray - Out (Left Ankle Sprain)
Julian Reese - Out (Two-Way)
Trendon Watford - Out (Right Ankle Sprain)

Dylan Harper is the biggest name out on the Spurs' side. The team announced Thursday that he has a left hip external rotator strain and is likely out two to three weeks, depending on how his recovery goes.

He was hurt late in Tuesday's loss to Detroit, when he slipped on a wet spot with just over a minute left, so the Silver and Black are without their dynamic sophomore for a while.

Dejounte Murray's trade request has the Pelicans in scramble mode

On Nov. 13, ESPN's Shams Charania reported that Murray had requested a trade from the Pelicans, and the team later confirmed it. The team put out a statement saying they would evaluate trade opportunities that are in the best interest of the organization. Murray is owed about $32.8 million this season and holds a $30.8 million player option for next year.

The Suns are the franchise rumored to have the most interest, but Amick reports the two sides aren't anywhere close to a deal yet. Brian Windhorst characterized the former Spurs guard as a "distressed asset" in the eyes of teams around the league, so most won't be willing to give up anything of note for Murray's services.

It doesn't help that the Pelicans have won more games with him off the floor than on it. Every little negative note is a significant hit to his already suffering perception. New Orleans has gone 3-7 over its last 10 games, so they have enough to worry about without trying to find a trade partner for a player with a big salary and a lengthy injury history. But that's the way the cookie crumbled.

Spurs should handle the Pelicans even without Dylan Harper

The Silver and Black are in the opposite situation. They are 9-1 over their last 10, with the lone loss coming to Detroit on Tuesday. The Spurs' league-leading net rating comes off the strength of a top five rank in both offensive and defensive rating. Victor Wembanyama is leading the way with the highest scoring average of his career (28.6 ppg).

They also already know what life without Harper looks like. The Spurs won games without Dylan last season when he was sidelined for 10 games with a left calf strain. He sat out Thurday's game against the Clippers, and the Spurs won 124-112 with Julian Champagnie starting in his place. Harrison Barnes and Jordan McLaughlin also gave San Antonio's bench a much-needed lift.

When one piece is out, there is another capable guard or forward ready to step in. That's why I see this game as another easy Spurs dub. The Pelicans are on the struggle bus. Their record sucks, and they have a disgruntled player on their roster to deal with. San Antonio has none of that. They just need to lock in and treat the Pels like a threat so they never actually become one.
