PS Plus’s next Classics games include Ratchet & Clank and more | VGC

TheTools · 03.09.2026 13:45 · source · permalink

Sony revealed the next PS Plus Classic games

Sony has revealed upcoming games coming to PlayStation Plus Classics.

Later this month, Capcom’s PlayStation 2 action game, Mega Man X Command Mission, will join the service, Sony announced during a State of Play live stream on Thursday.

Another Capcom title, the PSOne survival horror sequel, Dino Crisis 2, will join PlayStation Plus Classics “later this year”, according to the platform holder.

Finally, Insomniac Games’ original Ratchet & Clank, first released for PlayStation 2 in 2002, will join Classics later this year, Sony said.

PlayStation recently added two retro games to the Classics Catalog; PS2 game Onimusha: Dawn of Dreams, and PS1 game Disney’s Atlantis: The Lost Empire.

While Game Catalog titles are available for PS Plus Extra and Premium subscribers, Classics games are only available on the Premium tier.

August’s PS Plus game catalog titles include Helldivers 2 and Kingdom Come: Deliverance 2 on PS5.

Joining them are Vampire Survivors (PS5, PS4), Hell is Us (PS5), Umamusume Pretty Derby: Party Dash (PS4), Two Point Museum (PS5), Metro Exodus (PS5, PS4) and Dying Light 2 (PS5, PS4).
