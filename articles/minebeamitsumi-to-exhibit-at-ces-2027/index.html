MinebeaMitsumi to Exhibit at "CES 2027"

TheTools · 09.11.2026 05:29 · source · permalink

Press release — 8 November 2026 07:21

MinebeaMitsumi Inc. (hereinafter "MinebeaMitsumi") will exhibit at "CES 2027," one of the world's largest technology trade shows, to be held in Las Vegas, Nevada, U.S., from Wednesday, January 6 to Saturday, January 9, 2027.

At CES 2027, MinebeaMitsumi will double its booth footprint from the previous year to 108 m², showcasing technologies under the booth theme "Precision Solutions Powering the Future of Intelligent Motion."

With the rapid evolution of AI (software), the social implementation of "Physical AI" (embodied AI) capable of performing advanced tasks in the physical world is accelerating. However, its realization requires advances in hardware that overcome real-world physical constraints.

At this event, MinebeaMitsumi will present its cutting-edge precision components and "INTEGRATION" technologies driven by our Electro Mechanics Solutions™ to the world, targeting "5 Focus Fields + 1"—the five high-growth markets identified as the five growth areas (humanoid robots, AI servers, commercial drones, autonomous driving (LiDAR), and new mobility) plus the aerospace field.

■The Five Growth Areas Plus One

・Humanoid Robots

Toward the practical implementation of humanoid robots, we will debut for the first time an evolved "multi-fingered robot hand" (including wrists) that achieves enhanced dexterity compared with the previous model.

・AI Servers

We will feature working demonstrations of comprehensive thermal management solutions, spanning both liquid and air cooling.

・Commercial Drones

Alongside mock-ups of a drone body and drone port, we will present solutions including our "low-cogging BLDC motor," which contributes to flight stability and low power consumption, as well as bearings with advanced environmental durability capable of withstanding agricultural chemicals.

・Autonomous Driving (LiDAR)/ New Mobility

In addition to a side-by-side display of “cartridge bearings” and three types of LiDAR actuators, we will showcase a product lineup designed to create more comfortable cabin interiors.

・Aerospace Field

We will exhibit ultra-precision bearings, pressure sensors, and other products engineered to withstand extreme environments.

MinebeaMitsumi aims to connect with global developers and partners by leveraging its strength as an "INTEGRATED* precision components manufacturer"—fusing its "Eight Spears" core product portfolio, with ultra-precision machining and mass-production technologies at its core, with its "10 Core Technologies". We will continue to support electrification, energy-saving, and high-functionality across a wide range of areas including industrial, automotive, and data infrastructure, contributing to the realization of a sustainable society.

* This term was adopted with the intended meaning "to be combined" rather than "to sum up." Create new value through synergy by combining all the resources of our group.

■ Main Products Exhibited (as of November 6)

  • Humanoid Robots

Multi-fingered robot hands, 3-axis sensors, frameless motors, slotless motors, thin-walled bearings, MEMS/strain-gauge 6-axis force sensors, joint modules

  • AI Servers

Small high-discharge pump motors for liquid cooling, ABLIC high-sensitivity leak sensors, high-efficiency EC fans, 45,000rpm ultra-high-speed power supply fans

  • Commercial Drones

BLDC motors for propellers, high corrosion resistance GP bearings, caseless fan motors, GNSS antennas, waterproof connectors (IP67)

  • Autonomous Driving (LiDAR)

Cartridge bearings, 3 types of LiDAR actuators (LATM / FDB motors / BLDC motors)

  • New Mobility

Winglet handles (strain gauge type), multi-modal feedback systems, V2X antennas, e-Mirrors, seat cooling fans, resonant devices

  • Aerospace

Hybrid ceramic bearings (CEROBEAR), ultra-thin-walled bearings, pressure sensors
