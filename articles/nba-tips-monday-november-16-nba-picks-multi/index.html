Best Bet

LA Lakers @ Phoenix Suns (8:10am)

No Austin Reaves for the Lakers which should result in some additional touches for Walker Kessler.

The big man has played one game without Reaves this season, and produced a 21-point performance against the Hawks.

In his favour is the matchup – the Suns are the third easiest team for big men to score against this season at 14% above average, so up against Ighodara we should see Kessler heavily involved on the offensive end in this.

Next Best

Value Bet

OKC Thunder @ Sacramento Kings (1:10pm)

With Chet and Jalen Williams out for the Thunder, Hartenstein has been monstrous in their absence.

In his last five games, iHart is averaging six assists per game, hitting 5+ assists in all five games while hitting 6+ ($3.32) in four of those games. Expect Hartenstein’s role in this OKC offensive to continue while Chet is sidelined.
