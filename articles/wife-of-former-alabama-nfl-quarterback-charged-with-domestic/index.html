Wife of former Alabama, NFL quarterback charged with domestic violence

TheTools · 11.11.2026 03:59 · source · permalink

LAUDERDALE COUNTY, Ala. (WAFF) - The wife of former Alabama Crimson Tide and NFL quarterback Richard Todd has been arrested and charged with domestic violence after allegedly hitting him with a pair of pliers at their Lauderdale County home.

According to court documents obtained by 48 News, Debra Todd allegedly hit Richard Todd with the pliers during an argument late Monday night. The documents say he had multiple cuts on his arms.

Richard Todd reportedly declined to go to the hospital.

Debra Todd is being held in the Lauderdale County Jail on a $30,000 bond.

Richard Todd played for Alabama from 1973 to 1975 and was named first-team All-SEC in 1975. The New York Jets drafted him in 1976, and he later played for the New Orleans Saints.
