Leaked video shows iPhone 18 Pro Max in Dark Cherry and 18 Pro in Blue

TheTools · 07.09.2026 11:27 · source · permalink

Login

Siddharth 07 September 2026

Apple iOS

We’re only a few days away from Apple’s upcoming iPhone launch event, where the company is expected to unveil its first foldable iPhone alongside the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max. Ahead of the event, the Pro models have now appeared in a hands-on video.

The leaked video allegedly shows the demo units of the iPhone 18 Pro Max in Dark Cherry colorway and the iPhone 18 Pro in a Blue shade.

According to a recent leak, the iPhone 18 Pro lineup will be available in Dark Cherry, Black, and Sky Blue color options.

The Dark Cherry, also referred to as Burgundy, is expected to be this year’s hero color.

As for the design, the upcoming iPhones are not expected to feature any major changes compared to the iPhone 17 Pro lineup, with the new color options being one of the most noticeable differences.

The iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max are rumored to feature a new primary rear camera with a variable aperture, along with the A20 Pro chipset, a new modem, and slightly larger batteries.
