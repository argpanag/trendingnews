Snake remains at large as it slithers out of sight

TheTools · 23.09.2026 20:20 · source · permalink

A snake remains on the loose in Plymouth after emergency services failed to retrieve it this evening.

Devon and Somerset Fire and Rescue Service were called by the police at around 5.10pm on Wednesday, September 23, after people spotted what's thought to be a carpet python coiled around a branch.

Firefighters were pictured near the toilets at West Hoe park on Hoe Road, rummaging through the bushes in an attempt to locate the snake.

In an update just before 9pm, the fire service confirmed that the snake is yet to be found.

They said a team would be sent out again tomorrow (Thursday, September 24).

A fire engine was blocking the road for a time while crews used an aerial ladder to reach the spot the snake was seen. The road is now clear.

Devon and Somerset Fire and Rescue Service said in an earlier update: “We got a call at 5.10pm, to the public toilets at West Hoe Park on Hoe Road.

“We have a fire engine and an aerial ladder platform at the scene.

“It's believed a carpet Python is coiled around a branch up high.

“A vet is unable to retrieve it and the police requested the help of the fire service with a ladder to get the Python down.

“Fire crews are working with the police to retrieve the snake from a tree approximately 3m high.

“The road was closed while crews dealt with the incident.

“The animal is still loose.”
