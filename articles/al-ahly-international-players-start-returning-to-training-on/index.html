Al Ahly International players start returning to training on Tuesday

TheTools · 07.10.2026 14:32 · source · permalink

Al Ahly football director Wael Gomaa has revealed when the club’s international players will return to first-team training as preparations continue for the resumption of official competition.

Gomaa said the players who joined the Egypt senior national team will return to training on 6 October, following the conclusion of their international duty.

Algerian midfielder Monsef Bekrar will join the squad’s training session on October 8 after completing his international commitments with Algeria.

Al Ahly resumed training this evening at the El Tetsh Stadium after the players were given a day off following their friendly against Misr El Maqassa.

The Red Giants are preparing for the return of competitive action, with a match against Petrojet scheduled for 7 October in the Egypt Capital Cup.
