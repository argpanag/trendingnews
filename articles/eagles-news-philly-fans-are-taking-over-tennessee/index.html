Philadelphia Eagles news and links for 9/20/26.

Welcome to The Linc, the daily Philadelphia Eagles links roundup at Bleeding Green Nation!

No surprise here since we all know Eagles fans travel very well in general. But it seems like there’s an especially large presence in Tennessee given the destination and the fact that the Eagles haven’t played there since the 2018 season. Add in the Titans drawing dead and the Eagles having optimism for their season … and it should certainly be a home game for the Birds on the road once again.

Let’s get to the Philadelphia Eagles news and links …

“We’re Gonna Own That Stadium” – Philadelphia Eagles Fan Are Taking Over Nashville Ahead Of Titans Game - Whiskey Riff

Just take it from Eagles super-fan Paul Edwards. He got stopped by the local news on his way from Philly to Nashville, and here’s what he had to say: “Let’s get this party started buddy. Here we go, here we go. Tennessee Titans? We’re gonna own that stadium. There’s no doubt about that. Get ready for a stompin’. Be ready for our offensive line to run you over. Go Birds!” Something tells me thousands upon thousands of “Pauls” will be at Nissan Stadium. Eagles fans appear to be getting to Nashville early to make the most of the weekend. There’s been plenty of videos of green-garbed Philadelphians walking around in Nashville and taking over bars on Broadway. They are even getting their “E-A-G-L-E-S EAGLES chants” going anywhere and everywhere they can.

NFL Week 2 betting guide: Top plays for Sunday afternoon’s games - ESPN

Tennessee Titans team total UNDER 15.5 (-105). The Titans’ offensive operation was the worst I saw in Week 1: play designs that were dead in the water, offensive linemen uncertain of their assignments, easy drops and bad routes from receivers and poor accuracy and timing from Cam Ward at quarterback. It’s tough to find anything to hang their hat on from a 23-10 loss to the Jets. It’s worth remarking that Brian Daboll’s offenses have been poor in Week 1 -- six, six and and zero points in the three seasons previous -- and then stronger in Week 2. But against this Philadelphia Eagles defense, it’s tough to have much faith. Philadelphia’s defensive front looked as deep and disruptive as ever against an average Washington Commanders front, and the Titans are comfortably one of the eight worst lines in football. Though defensive back Cooper DeJean was banged up in the Week 1 game and sat out practice Wednesday, the Eagles’ outside corner duo of Quinyon Mitchell and Riq Woolen still poses a huge problem for the Titans’ ability to create explosive plays through the air. The talent difference here feels simply insurmountable. The Titans will need defensive takeaways or big special teams outputs to get into scoring range. I don’t see them getting enough. I would play this up to -115.
