Join Us in Celebration of 25 Years of XBOX!

TheTools · 14.11.2026 09:57 · source · permalink

We are celebrating 25 years of unforgettable gaming experiences on XBOX! The Horizon Festival could not be left out of the party and decided to send all festival goers the 25th Anniversary Livery. The unique design of this array aims to remind players of XBOX's origins, evoking the textures and materials of the original XBOX console. To wear this celebratory livery, we chose Forza Horizon 6's cover car, the 2025 GR GT. Born out of an unending pursuit of excellence, there's no better car to match the spirit of tradition and innovation imprinted into this livery than the GR GT!

How to Get It

This livery will become available to all players across all platforms and will remain redeemable for some time, so there is no need to worry if you are planning to purchase Forza Horizon 6 for the holiday season; you'll still be able to download it.

Here's a quick guide on how to get the gift:

  • Pause the game

  • Go to the “My Horizon” tab

  • Select “Message Center”

  • Under the “Gifts” tab you will find the XBOX 25th Anniversary Livery

  • Press “A” to redeem your new design!
