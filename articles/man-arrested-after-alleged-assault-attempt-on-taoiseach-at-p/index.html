Man arrested after alleged assault attempt on Taoiseach at Ploughing Championships

TheTools · 16.09.2026 16:13 · source · permalink

Taoiseach Micheal Martin attends the National Ploughing Championships at Tullamore, Co Offaly. Picture: Clodagh Kilcoyne/PA Wire

A man aged in his 60s was arrested at the National Ploughing Championships after he allegedly attempted to assault Taoiseach Micheál Martin.

According to gardaí, an arrest was made shortly after 12pm on Tuesday, September 15.

No injuries were reported.

The man was arrested under the Public Order Act and was brought to a Garda station in Offaly. 

He has since been charged and released to appear before the District Court at a later date.

Gardaí said investigations remain ongoing.
