The New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) provides a daily pre-market update directly from the NYSE Trading Floor. Access today's NYSE Pre-market update for market insights before trading begins. 

Kristen Scholer delivers the pre-market update on October 12th

  • SSA Commissioner Frank Bisignano is among notable figures at NYSE for Columbus Day.
    • He will join NYSE Live to discuss his Italian American heritage.
    • Bisignano, who was confirmed by the U.S. Senate in May 2025, has focused on modernizing the SSA's technology and customer service.
  • Diraq has been selected by the U.S. Defense Research Projects Agency to advance to the final stage of its Quantum Benchmarking Initiative.
    • The company will deploy up to $300 million to support independent verification + validation of its quantum computing solution.
    • Diraq says it will deploy the world's first silicon spin quantum computer to operate in a shared commercial data center before the end of 2026.
  • Earnings season and inflation reports headline this week's market activity.
