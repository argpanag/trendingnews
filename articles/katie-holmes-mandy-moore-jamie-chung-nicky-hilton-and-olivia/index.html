Katie Holmes, Mandy Moore, Jamie Chung, Nicky Hilton and Olivia Palermo Attend Meiomi Club Noir

TheTools · 23.11.2026 00:08 · source · permalink

On Wednesday evening, Meiomi hosted a kickoff of its first Club Noir in New York for its three-day pop-up, open until Friday, at The Overlook at Bryant Park Winter Village.

Katie Holmes, Mandy Moore, Jamie Chung, Nicky Hilton, Olivia Palermo and Jason Bard Yarmosky, alongside a guest list of tastemakers and creators, attended the VIP event.

First debuting in February earlier this year in Vail, the Meiomi Club Noir launched as its take on après-ski. Guests sipped on the wine company's pinot noir and sauvignon blanc and light bites, while listening to tunes from DJ Mia Moretti overlooking the park and ice skating rink.

Olivia Palermo and Nicky Hilton

Holmes told WWD in an interview that, as "a fan of Meiomi's wine for years" and having worked with them since 2020, she was excited for the brand to bring their California wines to New York for the holiday season.

"I love being with my family during the holidays," said Holmes. "I love being at Bryant Park. I just passed Macy's store and it was all lit up, which was exciting. I also love going to Central Park."

When it comes to holiday dressing, a pop of color, snow boots and a good puffer are go-tos. Holmes' look at the event featured a Lafayette 148 coat, Banana Republic corduroy pants and The Row shoes paired with a Gucci bag.

As for her rules on gifting, she said, "Don't regift, do be generous and think beyond your little circle of people because there's lots of people who would appreciate things."

Katie Holmes and Jason Bard Yarmosky

While Holmes has many things on her own wishlist for this year, she's also simple in her requests — an egg cutter to make soft-boiled eggs and socks, which she said are always on her list.

A surefire way to get on Holmes' nice list is to have a bottle of Sancerre at the ready.

Upcoming project-wise, for Holmes is the film "Happy Hours," which premiered at Tribeca Film Festival earlier this June, starring opposite Joshua Jackson. She describes the film as a "New York love story," which is slated for release in February or March of 2027.

While she remained tight-lipped about what else is next for her on the horizon, her focus is on "writing, creativity and lots of different projects upcoming."
