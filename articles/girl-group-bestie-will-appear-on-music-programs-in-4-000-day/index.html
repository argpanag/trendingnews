Girl group BESTie will appear on music programs in 4,000 days as a whole group of four.

Bestie will air on MBC's "Show!" on the 31st! Through "Music Core", the entire group of four will appear on music broadcasts in 4,000 days and perform the "Love Options" stage.

Earlier on the 17th, Bestie's Hyeyeon, Yuji, Dahye, and Haeryeong announced their intention to reunite as a whole to mark the 13th anniversary of the release of "Dating Conditions." The four members, who have been active in their respective positions for a long time, are back together as besties, and the music broadcast stage has been completed, adding joy to their full-fledged activities.

"Dating Conditions" is a song that has been loved for its bouncy melody, addictive chorus, and bright and cheerful energy. Expectations are also high for Bestie, who has recently returned with a more mature look amid a back-to-back craze on various short platforms and music charts, to show a new appearance on the full stage.

"Dating Conditions" has been steadily popular, reaching 48th place on the Melon TOP100 chart and 58th place on the daily chart. The popular YouTube music song TOP100 also ranked 12th in Korea, 17th in Taiwan, and 44th in Vietnam, showing outstanding results not only at home but also abroad.

It also made meaningful records on major Japanese charts. "Dating Conditions" ranked third on the Oricon streaming chart, ranked ninth on the Oricon YouTube short chart and 31st on the TikTok music chart. Shazam, a global music search platform, has confirmed its popularity in several countries, topping the charts in Korea, Vietnam, and Thailand, respectively.

Meanwhile, after debuting with the single "DKDK" in July 2013, Bestie released "Hot Baby", "Dating Conditions", and "I Need You".

[Son Jin-ah, MK Sports reporter]
