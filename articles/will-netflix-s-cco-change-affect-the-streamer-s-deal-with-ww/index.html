Will Netflix's CCO change affect the streamer's deal with WWE?

As you might be aware, Netflix has laid off around 1,000 employees (or 6% of its workforce) in the streaming giant's largest staff reduction to date. While the cuts mostly affect product, technology, legal and HR teams, several notable management members have also been pink slipped, and that trend continued last night with a very surprising name.

Indeed, Bela Bajaria is out after a decade-long stint at the company, with former Fox and Disney exec Peter Rice set to succeed her as Chief Content Officer. It would be no exaggeration to say that Bajaria worked wonders in her now-previous role, overseeing film and TV hits such as Bridgerton, Squid Game, Adolescence, Beef, Stranger Things, Wednesday, KPop Demon Hunters and Frankenstein, in addition to the streamer's active expansion into live sports - that last bit being especially relevant to our little pop culture bubble due to Bajaria partially helping to mastermind Netflix's 10-year deal with WWE that, among other things, sees Raw premiere on the streamer each and every Monday night.

Amidst heavy criticism of Netflix "censoring" WWE, as well as the company's option to prematurely end their partnership with the sports entertainment giants at the halfway point, this bigwig shake-up could potentially offer a clue as to how both parties (Netflix and WWE, that is) could continue cooperating with one another... or it could just be a classic case of me overthinking this stuff.
