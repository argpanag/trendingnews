Artem Shmidt offers advice to American talents

“Whether you can break through at a young age depends very much on your nationality. If you’re a young Belgian and you can continue living at home, you can take that step more quickly. As a young American, it’s better to ride with the junior team first, because you’re suddenly far from home. You have to figure out your life first and not immediately face the stress of being a pro.”
