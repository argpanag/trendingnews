Holland America Line unveils ‘biggest trade incentive ever’ with Alaska prize

TheTools · 17.09.2026 13:20 · source · permalink

 

You are viewing 1 of your 2 free articles

Holland America Line is offering agents the chance to win a 10-day cruisetour to Alaska in 2027 to coincide with Clia Cruise Week.

The prize will be “a very unique itinerary” to give trade partners a taste of “the best of everything”, including a cruise, a visit to the newly refurbished Denali Lodge and a land tour.

Speaking on a Travel Weekly webcast with editor-in-chief Lucy Huxley, associate vice-president of sales and marketing for UK & Ireland Karen Farndell called it “the ultimate prize”, adding: “It’s Cruise Week and as the experts for Alaska, we wanted to do something big to celebrate for travel agent partners.

“This is the biggest trade incentive that we’ve ever launched and the team here is buzzing about this.”

Farndell said there are 10 places up for grabs and the trip will be hosted by herself and Huxley next July.

The prize includes return flights to Vancouver, accommodation in the city before a three-night sailing on Koningsdam to Juneau and Skagway, a seven-day land tour and a night on either side of the trip for travel.

To enter, agents need to make any Holland America Line booking by October 31 and go to the online Travel Weekly competitions page to enter the reference and their personal details.

Alaska bookings will earn double entries into the draw.

Winners will be announced in early November along with another phase of the competition related to an Online Travel Training course.

Holland America Line is marking 80 years of operations in the region next year and has won Travel Weekly’s Best for Alaska award.
