More rain, snow forecast as severe weather system moves north

TheTools · 27.09.2026 01:15 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Weather

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._g.pKhUMkRnoOZwX4iq2fhLAqba5Rz0jb3mINKRqASmxf0/weather/1606446%2Fmore-rain-snow-forecast-as-severe-weather-system-moves-north

Heavy snow is trapping cars and trucks in the South Island, with several major roads closed or restricted as strong winds and rain move over the country.

SH7 between the turnoff to Hanmer Springs and Springs Junction, including Lewis Pass, is now closed because of heavy snow.

NZTA said there is no detour and people should avoid the area or delay their journeys if possible.

Video player removed for printing

On SH73, the sections between Otira and Arthur's Pass, and Springfield and Castle Hill, are closed to towing vehicles, with snow chains required.

MetService warns the Tararua Range could receive another 40 to 60mm of rain on top of what has already fallen, while another 5 to 10cm of snow is forecast for parts of Canterbury.

Orange warnings remain in place for heavy rain in the Tararua Range, heavy snow in Canterbury and severe winds across parts of the lower North Island.

The Tararua Range is expected to receive peak rainfall rates of 15 to 20mm an hour from late this morning.

MetService said streams and rivers could rise rapidly, with surface flooding, slips and difficult driving conditions possible.

In the Canterbury High Country and plains near the foothills south of Arthur's Pass, another 5 to 10cm of snow is expected to settle above 400 metres, with lesser amounts down to about 200 metres.

A severe weather system moving north over the country is bringing further heavy rain, snow and severe gales across central New Zealand on 27 September 2026.

Screenshot / MetService

The snow is expected to ease from the south in the afternoon.

Severe west to northwest winds gusting up to 120km/h are forecast in exposed parts of Wairarapa, the Tararua District and Hawke's Bay south of Hastings.

A warning for Wellington is in place until noon, with northwest winds gusting up to 120km/h in exposed places before switching to strong or gale southerlies around midday.

The change could briefly bring southerly gusts of up to 90km/h.

NZ Transport Agency said roading crews were helping vehicles stuck on SH8 near Lake Pūkaki because of heavy snow.

Motorist Kyle Dinz said he was stuck near Lake Pukaki due to heavy snowfall while travelling on SH8 on Sunday 27 September 2026.

Supplied / Kyle Dinz

Kyle Dinz said he was caught in standstill traffic near Lake Pūkaki, behind several heavy trucks stuck on the road.

He eventually made it to Lake Tekapo, where he said the snow was even heavier.

Heavy snow blankets vehicles near Lake Tekapo on 27 September 2026.

Supplied / Kyle Dinz

NZTA also closed a section of road between Springfield and Castle Hill, including Porters Pass, to towing vehicles and advised drivers to use snow chains.

A driver told RNZ their car slid while crossing Porters Pass at about 9.30am because of the snow, forcing them to turn back.

Snow falls on Porters Pass on Sunday morning, 27 September 2026.

Supplied / Tracey Hunter

NZTA has issued snowfall warnings for SH8 between Omarama and Tarras, including Lindis Pass, SH85 between Omakau and Kyeburn, and the Crown Range Road.

MetService has also issued warnings for Porters Pass, which is expected to receive 10 to 15cm of snow above 600m between 10am and 4pm.

Arthur's Pass could get 2 to 4cm above 700m between 11am and 4pm.

A snowfall warning has also been issued for the Crown Range Road, where snow showers could affect the road around midday.

The Desert Road (SH1) is also under a snowfall warning from 7pm Sunday until 1am on Monday.

Several watches are in place across the country.

A Heavy Snow Watch remains in place for the Canterbury High Country and plains near the foothills about and north of Arthur's Pass, with rain expected to turn to snow above 200m and heavy snowfall possible above 400m.

Strong Wind Watches are in place for parts of Hawke's Bay, Horowhenua, the Kāpiti Coast, the Marlborough Sounds and the Chatham Islands.

The Heavy Snow Watch for Central Otago, the Queenstown Lakes District, inland Clutha and Southland north of Lumsden has been lifted after the threat of warning-level snowfall passed.

MetService said the front will continue moving north today, bringing heavy rain and west to northwest gales to parts of central New Zealand and the Chatham Islands.
