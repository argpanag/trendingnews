Messi vs. Evander: Inter Miami host FC Cincinnati in high-stakes clash | MLSSoccer.com

TheTools · 28.10.2026 23:01 · source · permalink

Lionel Messi and Inter Miami CF host Evander-led FC Cincinnati on Wednesday night for an Eastern Conference clash with major Audi 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs implications.

How to watch & stream

  • Apple TV

When

  • Wednesday, Oct. 28 | 7:30 pm ET/4:30 pm PT

Where

  • Nu Stadium | Miami, Florida

Standings

  • 3rd in Eastern Conference
  • 55 points (15W-6L-10D)

Last game

Inter Miami encountered a setback last weekend, falling 2-0 at Red Bull New York. The Herons previously had a three-game winning streak.

Players to watch

  • Lionel Messi: What more can you say about Messi? The legendary Argentine is well on his way to a third straight Landon Donovan MLS MVP award, leading MLS with 40 goal contributions (25g/15a).
  • Luis Suárez: A longtime strike partner of Messi, Suárez has reclaimed his spot as Inter Miami's starting No. 9 and tallied 15g/12a.
  • Germán Berterame: In his debut season with the Herons, the Mexican international has turned into a reliable forward with 9g/10a.
  • Rodrigo De Paul: The 2022 FIFA World Cup winner offers extra bite in midfield, while also bringing an attacking edge with 5g/14a.

Need to know

Despite all the star power up and down their roster, Miami are looking up at the New England Revolution – who leapfrogged them during Matchday 32 – in the Eastern Conference standings.

Although they hold a four-point lead over fourth-place Chicago Fire FC, the Herons will be eager to reclaim the No. 2 seed in the East and move closer to clinching a 2027 Concacaf Champions Cup spot.

With Messi, Miami are always a threat. And with the season winding down, expect the defending MLS Cup champions to carry a "must-win" attitude.

Standings

  • 8th in Eastern Conference
  • 42 points (11W-11L-9D)

Last game

FC Cincinnati enjoyed a six-point week, earning back-to-back wins over D.C. United.

In their latest matchup, a 2-0 Cincy victory, Evander recorded 1g/1a to become the second player in MLS history with at least 15 assists in three consecutive seasons. Tom Barlow added the insurance tally. 

Players to watch

  • Evander: A perennial MVP candidate, the Brazilian midfield maestro has a team-best 29 goal contributions (14g/15a).
  • Kévin Denkey: Cincinnati's leading goalscorer, the Togo international striker is up to 17g/5a during the 2026 campaign.
  • Tom Barlow: Although he doesn't get the shine of Denkey or Evander, the veteran forward is quietly enjoying a career year with 15 goal contributions (10g/5a).
  • Pavel Bucha: The versatile Czech international is one of Cincy's most consistent players and has registered 6g/10a this season.

Need to know

Unlike their South Florida counterparts, FC Cincinnati aren't slated to earn home-field advantage in the Audi 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs. Instead, they're simply looking to secure a postseason spot!

Thanks to two straight wins, the Orange & Blue now hold a three-point edge over Red Bull New York (ninth place) and a four-point advantage over rival Columbus Crew (10th place).

In fact, a win against Miami, combined with Columbus losing at FC Dallas, could secure a playoff berth for head coach Pat Noonan's side and take them out of the Wild Card places altogether.
