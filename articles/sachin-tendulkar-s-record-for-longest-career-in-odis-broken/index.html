Sachin Tendulkar's record for longest career in ODIs broken

TheTools · 18.09.2026 14:51 · source · permalink

Sachin Tendulkar's record for longest career in ODIs broken short by Anmol Sharma / 11:39 am on Tuesday, 15 September, 2026Zimbabwe's Brendan Taylor has broken Sachin Tendulkar's record for the longest career in men's ODIs after taking the field against Australia on Tuesday. Taylor, who made his ODI debut on April 20, 2004, has now had an ODI career spanning 22 years and 148 days. Tendulkar's ODI career had lasted 22 years and 91 days, from 1989 to 2012.read more at Hindustan Times
