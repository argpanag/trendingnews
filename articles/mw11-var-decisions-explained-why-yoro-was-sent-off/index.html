MW11 VAR decisions explained: Why Yoro was sent off

TheTools · 23.11.2026 00:13 · source · permalink

See the latest interventions by VAR as explained by the Premier League Match Centre X account

This article collates the X posts of the Premier League Match Centre which provides explanations for decisions made by the referees and Video Assistant Referees (VAR) in Premier League matches. 

Incident - 9min Manchester United defender Leny Yoro challenges Liverpool's Alexander Isak just outside the Man Utd penalty area. Referee Sam Barrott adjudges that Yoro fouled Isak, awarding a free-kick and showing Yoro a red card.

The Premier League Match Centre X post said: "The referee’s call of red card to Yoro for denial of an obvious goal-scoring opportunity (DOGSO) was checked and confirmed by VAR.

"REF: 'It's a red card for DOGSO. On-field decision is outside [the box], red card, DOGSO.'

"VAR: 'Defender bundles into him into the back, gets too tight, I think it's a foul.'"

Incident - 25min Manchester City striker Erling Haaland gets on the end of a cross, shooting towards goal. Fulham goalkeeper Bernd Leno stops the shot with this hands, but Haaland follows up to kick the ball from between Leno's hands into the goal. The referee Tom Bramall indicated that Haaland had fouled Leno. 

The Premier League Match Centre X post said: "The referee’s call of free-kick to Fulham and no goal to Manchester City was checked and confirmed by VAR – with the goalkeeper deemed to be in control of the ball.

"VAR: 'The ball is in control between both hands and then is played out of that possession by the attacker, and then into the back of the net.'"

IFAB's Law 12 states: "A goalkeeper is considered to be in control of the ball with their hand(s)/arm(s) when:
 • the ball is between their hands/arms or between their hand(s)/arm(s) and any surface (e.g. ground, own body)
 • holding the ball in their outstretched open hand(s)
 • bouncing it on the ground or throwing it in the air
"A goalkeeper cannot be challenged by an opponent when in control of the ball with their hand(s)/arm(s)."
