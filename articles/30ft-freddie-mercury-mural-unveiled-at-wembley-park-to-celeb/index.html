30ft Freddie Mercury mural unveiled at Wembley Park to celebrate music legend's 80th birthday

TheTools · 03.09.2026 20:46 · source · permalink

The spectacle will be free to view from now until December 2026

Wembley Park has revealed a brand-new artistic tribute to Freddie Mercury, marking what would have been the legendary musician's 80th birthday on September 5.   

The mural titled I’m Mr. Mercury, created by renowned British street artist Frank Styles, is located on Wembley Park’s Spanish Steps and comprises an iconic monochromatic image of Mercury against a vivid backdrop of colour and graphic elements.

Standing at 30ft tall, you can only view the whole installation from one place: a yellow circle marked on the ground opposite the piece, due to the artwork being a single anamorphic creation applied across all 58 steps. 

As well as the tribute, selected lyrics from Mercury’s song catalogue and quotations from the artist himself rise across the Spanish Steps towards Wembley Stadium, tracing a symbolic journey from the private man behind the performer to the global icon he became. 

 

Jim Beach, Queen's longtime manager and Mercury Phoenix Trust co-founder alongside Queen members Brian May and Roger Taylor, comments: “Freddie was known to millions around the world as the frontman of Queen but off stage, he was a quiet and humble man, happy at home surrounded by the things he loved most. ‘I’m Mr. Mercury’ perfectly captures the beautiful complexity of Freddie, with his captivating portrait drawing people into his world as he did so magically during his life.” 

Freddie and Queen have a longstanding relationship with Wembley itself, having been the stage for the iconic Live Aid performance in 1985, as well as multiple sold-out performances across the years. 

The installation is free to view 24 hours a day and runs from now until December 2026.
