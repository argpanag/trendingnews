Nis Offers Cholesterol Checks Without Referral Saturday

TheTools · 18.09.2026 11:59 · source · permalink

Nis Health Center is holding a walk-in cholesterol and triglyceride screening this Saturday, September 19, from 7 a.m. to 1:30 p.m., no referral or appointment needed.

Have you long needed a cholesterol check but never made it to the doctor for a referral? On Saturday, that will not be a problem. Nis Health Center is running a walk-in lipid panel screening, no referral and no appointment required.

When and where, exactly

The screening was announced today, September 18, and takes place the very next day, on Saturday, September 19, at Nis Health Center. It is organized by the facility’s Laboratory Diagnostics Service, running from 7 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. Showing up during that window is all it takes. No referral from your chosen doctor and no prior appointment are needed.

What exactly you can test

A full lipid panel covers four values:

  • total cholesterol, the overall amount of all forms of cholesterol in the blood
  • LDL cholesterol, the so called “bad” cholesterol, which carries fat to the cells
  • HDL cholesterol, the so called “good” cholesterol, which acts as a cleaner for blood vessels
  • triglycerides, a type of fat that stores unused energy from food

Nis Health Center says this test is not just a routine box to check off a list of exams. “Lipid status is a key laboratory blood test that directly reflects the health of your heart and blood vessels,” the facility said in its announcement.

Why this matters, even without symptoms

Doctors use this test to assess the risk of atherosclerosis, heart attack and stroke. Nis Health Center adds that a lipid panel measures the levels of different types of fat in the body and makes it possible to act on that risk in time, even when there are no symptoms at all. The advantage, in other words, is that the problem becomes visible before any symptom appears, at exactly the point when it is easiest to do something about it, whether through diet, exercise or treatment prescribed by a doctor.

This kind of preventive check is especially useful for people who rarely see a doctor, since elevated cholesterol is most often discovered for the first time at events like this one, with no warning sign beforehand.

What we know, and what we don’t

Before you head out, it helps to know what is certain and what the health center has not said.

What we know:

  • the date and location: Saturday, September 19, at Nis Health Center
  • the screening hours: 7 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.
  • that no referral or appointment is needed
  • which values are measured

What we don’t know, since the announcement does not say:

  • whether you need to arrive fasting
  • exactly which documents to bring
  • whether the test is free or paid

Our advice: before you go, call Nis Health Center and check these three details. Cholesterol testing usually requires the patient to be fasting, but until this specific screening confirms that officially, it is safer to ask than to assume.

Another screening this week, in another city

This is not the first such announcement this week. We also wrote about free checkups in Drmno, open without a health insurance card. The Nis screening goes in a different direction, focused solely on lipid status, but the principle is the same: the healthcare system is reaching out to people who keep putting off regular checkups because of long lines at their chosen doctor’s office or waits for a referral.

Is it worth going

If you live in Nis or nearby and have not checked your heart and blood vessels in a while, Saturday is a chance to do it without waiting in line at your chosen doctor’s office. A few minutes in the lab can reveal risk before any symptom shows up, and that alone is reason enough not to skip it, especially if your family already has a history of high cholesterol or heart problems.
