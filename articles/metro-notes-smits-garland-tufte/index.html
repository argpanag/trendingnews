Metro Notes: Smits, Garland, Tufte

TheTools · 10.11.2026 08:10 · source · permalink

The 2026 NHL Draft’s fifth overall pick has suffered his first injury in the NHL. According to Peter Baugh of The Athletic, New York Rangers defenseman Alberts Smits will miss the next few weeks with an upper-body injury.

New York held Smits out of yesterday’s matchup against the Seattle Kraken and recalled depth defender Marc Del Gaizo to fill his roster spot. The Rangers will presumably return Del Gaizo to AHL Hartford once Sean Durzi, who is on injured reserve with an undisclosed injury, is healthy enough to return.

Because of his recovery timeline, Smits will miss the rest of New York’s road trip through the Pacific Division and their next home game against the Montreal Canadiens on November 16th. However, after that, the Rangers have a six-day break. Before the injury, Smits struggled, going scoreless in 17 games with a -2 rating, averaging 14:29 of ice time per game.

Additional notes from the Metro Division:

  • Elsewhere in the Metro, the Columbus Blue Jackets are dealing with a new injury. Per Brian Hedger of the Columbus Dispatch, the Blue Jackets have ruled out Conor Garland tomorrow with an upper-body injury. Unfortunately, Garland may miss more than tomorrow’s game against the Florida Panthers, as head coach Rick Bowness couldn’t provide a return timeline. The nine-year veteran, who is in the first year of a six-year, $36MM extension, has registered one assist in 15 games for the Blue Jackets this season with a -5 rating.
  • The New Jersey Devils have recalled some forward depth ahead of tomorrow’s highly anticipated matchup against the Winnipeg Jets. According to a team announcement, the Devils have recalled Riley Tufte from the AHL’s Utica Comets. Tufte is in his first year with the Devils organization after signing a one-year, $850K contract last offseason. He spent the previous two years mostly with the AHL’s Providence Bruins, scoring 53 goals and 98 points in 122 games with a +42 rating.
