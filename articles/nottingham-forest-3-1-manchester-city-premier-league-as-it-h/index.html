Full-time: Nottingham Forest 3-1 Manchester City

Having won their first seven, City have now lost three in a row, but this was all about Forest. They had the right gameplan for City and executed it perfectly. Delap was excellent and Glasner’s words to fire up his side in the week clearly worked.

90+4 min Late yellow for Netz for bringing down Foden.

90+2 min Roars from Igor Jesus as he gets the better of Khusanov near the byline and wins a pressure-easing goal kick.

90+1 min We’re into four added minutes.

89 min Forest are still scrapping away, Gibbs-White gives away a free-kick deep in City’s half but that’ll help the second tick away. Very impressive performance from Forest.

86 min There are some City fans in the away end who have their replica shirts with ‘Cheetos’ as the main sponsor. That is great needle but does make it hard to have any sympathy. Off goes Delap to a standing ovation, no hat-trick for him. Igor Jesus is on to make his comeback from a calf injury.

84 min Big Vange is looking happy in the director’s box.

82 min Delap wins a free-kick high up the pitch as he outscraps Nunes. That’s great centre-forward play. Time is getting away from City now.

80 min Gibbs-White has been exactly what Forest need today. Nuggety and aggressive in the press, quality on the ball. His latest touch is a backheel nutmeg that befuddles Nunes.

78 min Fernández goes off to be replaced by Phil Foden, round of boos for Argentinian. Sometimes hard to believe that Fernández has been sold for fees of more than £200m. He was awful today.

76 min O’Reilly wins a corner, poor ball from Fernández and Sels claims.

75 min Glasner sprints off down the touchline to celebrate with his players. Without wanting to get all celebration police, that’s a bit much from a manager for a league game in November.

Goal! Nottingham Forest 3-1 Manchester City (Delap 73)

This is almost a carbon copy of his goal from the first half, Forest turnover, direct running at the City defence, this time from Gibbs-White, before Delap powers it into the top corner, with his left foot this time. Some finish.

69 min Murillo wins the ball off Fernández again and this time goes for goal from inside his own half. Woeful. That’s closer to the corner flag.

66 min Anderson has been excellent, Enzo Fernández less so. The Argentinian loses the ball to Murillo on the edge of his own box and the turnover allows Yates to get a shot off, it’s deflcted and clip the top of the bar. City eventually clear the corner and Ndiaye leads a quick counter that is halted by a the recently introduced Ndoye. Yellow card for what was almost a rugby tackle.

64 min Muñoz and McAtee are withdrawn, on come Ndoye and Netz for Forest.

63 min Forest fans and players are incensed as Delap is denied a free-kick when Khusanov bundles him over. VAR has a quick check on the follow-up challenge from McAtee as the Forest man piles into Anderson, yellow card.

59 min You have to say the changes have worked for City. O’Reilly shows nice feet in the box after linking up with Doku and it’s deflected away for a corner. Doku has been freed up and Nunes simply carries more threat down the right. A selection error from Maresca in hindsight.

57 min Chance for Haaland and that is an uncharacteristic miss. The City forward has a bit of space in the box to get a short off but his scoopy side-foot goes over.

55 min Gibbs-White is hobbling a little after a crunching tackle from Nunes. The Forest talisman was sold a little short by Nico Williams’ pass and the City man left a bit on him. Not nasty, just firm.

54 min Did that hit the post? Doku spins away from his man tries to sweep it across the Haaland, the ball takes a deflection and almost wrongfoots Sels. The Forest keeper does well to gather the resulting corner at the second attempt.

Goal! Nottingham Forest 2-1 Manchester City (Cherki 52)

And that is why he was kept on! Sweet, sweet hit first time on the half-volley from Cherki as the ball fell to him on the edge of the box. Game on.

49 min Jeers as Cherki misplaces a pass straight of play. I’d say he was lucky to survive the half-time cull.

48 min Khusanov has come into the centre of the City backline and has to stretch his legs up against Delap. The Uzbek is quick and wins a free-kick as Delap barges into the back of him.
