Schumacher: Wolff will eventually 'realise' he has to let Antonelli join Ferrari

TheTools · 02.11.2026 17:30 · source · permalink

David Comerford

Ralf Schumacher has expressed total confidence that Kimi Antonelli will ‘eventually’ race for Ferrari, even though he has just won the world championship with Mercedes.

Antonelli became Italy’s first Formula 1 world champion since Alberto Ascari in 1953 on Sunday when he sealed the title at the Mexico City Grand Prix. He has become one of the nation’s most popular athletes as a result.

There will inevitably be a clamour among Italian fans to see him race for their beloved Ferrari as soon as possible, but Toto Wolff will naturally be determined to lock him down long-term.

Ralf Schumacher convinced Kimi Antonelli is destined for Ferrari

Speaking on Sky Germany, via F1-Insider, Schumacher said even Wolff will eventually accept that Antonelli joining Ferrari is inevitable.

Wolff has effectively been the personal sponsor of Antonelli at Mercedes, handpicking him to succeed seven-time world champion Lewis Hamilton for the 2025 season and standing by him last year when many suggested the gamble had backfired.

How many championships do YOU think Kimi Antonelli will win in his career? Kimi Antonelli has already won one at 20 years old…

Antonelli is Mercedes’ fourth world champion after Hamilton, Nico Rosberg and Juan Manuel Fangio, but he is perhaps the first who is truly a child of the Silver Arrows. And yet, the allure of Ferrari is undeniable, as Wolff found with another superstar two years ago.

“It will happen eventually,” Schumacher said. “I believe it’s a must, [especially] if the Pope ever orders him to sit in a Ferrari!

“Toto Wolff’s emotions were incredible […] At some point, I think he will also realise that he has to let him go.”

It’s well-known that Ferrari turned down Antonelli as a child, though accounts of their reasoning differ. Some claim he was thought to be too small, others that the team weren’t prepared to take on an 11-year-old.

That allowed Mercedes to capitalise, but reports suggest Ferrari are intent on signing Antonelli and rectifying their past mistake.

Charles Leclerc signed a long-term contract extension earlier this year, while Hamilton will stay for at least one more season. Academy driver Oliver Bearman, currently racing for Haas, has been seen as Hamilton’s eventual heir, and he will be joined by another Maranello product next season in Rafael Camara.

However, if Ferrari have a genuine opportunity to sign an Italian world champion in Antonelli, they may alter those succession plans.

Antonelli has refused to rule out joining Ferrari one day and expresses his respect for the institution every time the subject is raised, while also reiterating his commitment and gratitude to Mercedes.
