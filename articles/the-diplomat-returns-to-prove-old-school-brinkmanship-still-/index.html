The Diplomat returns to prove old-school brinkmanship still sells

TheTools · 15.10.2026 07:00 · source · permalink

Keri Russell and Rufus Sewell deliver top-tier performances while navigating Windsor Castle security breaches and Russian nukes.

The Diplomat S4, US, drama ★★★★

  • Where to find it: Netflix
  • First aired: October 15
  • Who’s in it: Keri Russell, Rufus Sewell, Ato Essandoh, Ali Ahn, Nana Mensah, Rory Kinnear, Bradley Whitford, Allison Janney, Aidan Turner
  • Who made it: Created by Debora Cahn; directed by Alex Graves, Liza Johnson; executive producers Janice Williams, Keri Russell
  • Time commitment: 8 x 42-56 minute episodes
  • Synopsis: Allies become full-blown frenemies in this series return

What does it say about the times we live in when works of a political fabulist play straighter than real life? It must be challenging for writer-creators such as Debora Cahn (The West Wing), whose cracking drama, The Diplomat, is back for a fourth run.
