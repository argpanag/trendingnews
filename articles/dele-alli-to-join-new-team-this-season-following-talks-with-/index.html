Dele Alli to ‘join new team this season’ following talks with number of clubs

TheTools · 21.11.2026 16:22 · source · permalink
  • Kerrie Jacobs-Bryant, US Sports Journalist
  • Published: 16:22, 21 Nov 2026
  • Updated: 17:16, 21 Nov 2026

Dele Alli is expected to play in the 2026/27 football season.

The former Tottenham Hotspur star hasn't played professional football since March 2025 when he came off the bench for Como against AC Milan.

At the time it was his first senior appearance in almost 750 days, but his cameo was short-lived as he was sent off for a challenge on Ruben Loftus-Cheek just nine minutes later.

The 30-year-old is still without a club after the Italian outfit released him that following September, with his career trajectory after leaving north London having been called 'tragic' by talkSPORT's Ally McCoist.

But as he bides his time seeking a return to competitive action, Dele has been seen maintaining his fitness at several teams' training facilities lower down the football pyramid.

This includes League Two side Barnet, with manager Dean Brennan even extending an invite to the former Premier League star to join him for first-team training.

Dele was also pictured training with semi-professional side Windsor & Eton in October, before a shock revelation that then-manager Karl Robinson had offered Dele the chance to join Salford City, although such move never materalised.

However, there is growing belief that the two-time PFA Young Player of the Year will join a club this season, having also received interest from Major League Soccer, as well as clubs in Brazil and Japan.

Despite having turned to internet streaming in recent months, recently going viral for wearing - and subsequently burning - an Arsenal shirt after losing a bet to iShowSpeed, Dele is still very much committed to his training regime.

Inside Dele's rigorous training regime

In an interview with The Guardian, Connor Peters, Dele's elite performance coach, revealed that the MK Dons academy graduate trains with him between five and six days a week.

Four of those days are spent working out on the pitch, with the others taking place in the gym.

“We’ll do maybe two days on, one day off, and then three days on, one day off,” Peters, who has also worked with other players including Trevor Chalobah, said.

“We’ve been doing that for just over a year now. It’s full-on. It’s pretty much as if you were at a club.”

A typical day working with Peters includes an hour of activating and stretching at 9am before heading down to Windsor & Eton to get his on-pitch training in.

Dele then finishes off his day with a recovery session at home using his personal oxygen chamber and sauna, whilst in his free time, he likes to go for a swim, and has taken up padel.

Peters also shut down claims from some that Dele doesn't really care about football anymore due to the time he spends streaming online.

“When people see him online streaming or whatever he does in his private time, the initial thought is: ‘He’s done with football, then,’” Peters said.

“He’s been training for a year and a half by himself. If he was done with football he wouldn’t be doing that. His actions show that the hunger is still there.

"A lot of people would come out of the game and then stop and lose the hunger for it, but he’s still chipping away every day, trying to get back and get to somewhere that he’s happy with.”

Despite having received several offers from clubs all over the world, Dele has yet to put pen to paper on his future, with Peters explaining that his client wants to ensure he lands at the right place for him - somewhere where he is happy.

"Because he’s had such a long time out, he’s like: ‘What’s another few months if it means that I’m going to find something I’m actually happy with?’” Peters continued, further explaining that Dele's aim is to “carry on for as long as possible"

“He’s very much a person that’s happy with the little things,” Peters added of Dele. “He’s always the person that’s positive, the one that, if the group’s down or whatever, is going to pick everyone up.

"He’s happy with what he’s doing. His main happiness is playing football, and that’s what he wants to do.”
