Lee Soo-man's New Girl Group A2O LITE Draws Attention for Diverse Nationalities

TheTools · 12.10.2026 07:18 · source · permalink

The nationality breakdown of the members in Lee Soo-man (이수만)'s new girl group, A2O LITE, has recently drawn significant attention. The group consists of nine members hailing from various countries and regions.

Specifically, member RADA is from Thailand, while OA is of Japanese and American descent. The group also includes three members from China: XILI, AIJING, and MERCI.

Additionally, HIMEKI is from Japan. The three Korean members are YOUNG, VORA, and AYUNE. Overall, the nine-member girl group's nationality distribution comprises three Koreans, three Chinese, one Japanese, one Thai, and one Japanese-American.
