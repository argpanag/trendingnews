United Malacca 1Q net profit slips on weaker Malaysia operations, partially offset by Indonesia gains

TheTools · 24.09.2026 15:28 · source · permalink

KUALA LUMPUR (Sept 24): United Malacca Bhd (KL:UMCCA) posted a 15.8% decline in first-quarter net profit, with weaker contributions from its Malaysian operations partially offset by stronger performance in Indonesia.

Net profit for the first quarter ended July 31, 2026 (1QFY2027) was RM31.8 million compared to RM37.8 million a year earlier, while revenue dropped 6% to RM180.1 million from RM191.6 million, according to the group's Bursa Malaysia filing.

The group said its Malaysian operations’ earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation (Ebitda) saw a 39% drop to RM34.5 million from RM56.7 million in 1QFY2026, mainly due to lower output of fresh fruit bunches (FFB) and higher production costs, despite higher average crude palm oil (CPO) and palm kernel prices.

Average CPO prices for the group’s Malaysia operation rose to RM4,512 per tonne from RM4,001 a year earlier, with palm kernel prices also increasing to RM3,523 per tonne from RM3,151.

However, this contrasts with its Indonesian operation, which saw a surge in Ebitda to RM24.4 million from RM9.3 million a year earlier, supported by higher FFB production and lower unit costs, even as average CPO prices eased.

For Indonesia, average CPO prices fell to RM3,372 per tonne from RM3,470 a year earlier, while palm kernel prices marginally increased to RM3,089 per tonne from RM3,048.

Meanwhile, the group’s investment holding segment recorded a profit of RM926,000, down 19% from RM1.1 million in 1QFY2026, mainly due to foreign exchange losses arising from the weakening of the rupiah against the ringgit.

No dividend was declared for the quarter under review.

Looking ahead, United Malacca expects FFB production to remain stable in FY2027, supported by a more favourable plantation age profile and continued operational improvements.

“However, FFB production could be affected by the anticipated super El Niño in the second half of the financial year,” it flagged.

The group said it remains focused on improving labour productivity, mechanisation initiatives and cost efficiency as well as increasing oil yield.

Shares of United Malacca closed four sen or 0.67% lower at RM5.96 on Thursday, giving the group a market capitalisation of RM1.25 billion. The stock has gained 6.62% over the past 12 months.
