Electric scooter maker Ather Energy shares hit 10% upper circuit on October 26 after the company posted profit in September quarter as compared to loss in the year-ago period.

For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the company reported net profit of Rs 1.26 crore as compared to loss of Rs 154 crore in the year-ago period.

At 2:35 pm on October 26, Ather Energy shares were trading 10% higher at Rs 1,518 apiece.

The company said its board approved Rs 122-crore capex for Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra units.

The company's revenue rose 63% to Rs 1,464 crore as compared to Rs 899 crore a year ago while EBITDA was at Rs 11.3 crore in Q2FY27 as against Rs 133 crore in Q2FY26.

At the Hosur factory, the company has proposed capacity addition of 75,000 units per annum and is expected to be completed in December 2026. It requires investment of up to Rs 58 crore to "scale up long-term manufacturing output to meet the business demand".

At the Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, the company has proposed capacity addition of 1,50,000 units per annum and is expected to be completed in Q2FY28. It requires investment of up to Rs 64 crore.

Tarun Mehta, Co-founder & CEO, Ather Energy, said, “We delivered a strong quarter, with robust topline growth and a positive bottomline, despite surging commodity prices and supply chain disruptions. Underpinning this is a shift to electric two-wheelers that is now structural and playing out across India. At Ather, demand has more than doubled year-on-year this quarter, and to meet it, we are further augmenting our production capacity at Hosur and AURIC. With Konarc taking Ather to a wider set of customers, we see significant headroom for growth ahead.”
