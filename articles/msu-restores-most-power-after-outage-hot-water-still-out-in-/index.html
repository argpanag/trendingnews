MSU restores most power after outage, hot water still out in 80% of buildings

TheTools · 20.09.2026 22:59 · source · permalink

EAST LANSING, Mich. (News 10) - A power outage left staff and students on Michigan State University’s campus in the dark the evening of Saturday, Sept. 19, according to the university.

MSU said an issue with a central connection point at the T.B. Simon Power Plant cut off the power necessary to generate electricity and steam for campus. The outage halted the plant’s ability to provide steam and electricity to more than 100 campus buildings.

Power was restored to the plant and some areas of campus early Sunday, Sept. 20, but the outage left a lasting impact.

“I was walking back to my dorm and the lights just like flickered and they just went out,” said Sophia Wilson, a freshman at MSU.

Cell service, dorm access affected

The outage impacted cell service, deactivated the emergency green phones on campus, and made it difficult for students to enter dorm buildings.

“There was like, no light at all. Like, the hallways were like, empty, right? There’s no light, so it’s like a ghost town, right? People couldn’t charge their phones. Because I slept in most of it, my phone was the same, right? But I had no access to that,” said Sarosh Amjad, a freshman at MSU.

“The key cards that get us into our dorms, we couldn’t use those to get in and out of the dorms because, um, you need electricity and power for the IDs to work. I did try to call my parents and I know that like a few of those calls didn’t go through. The one to the RA didn’t go through,” Wilson said.

Most power restored, hot water still out

The university said as of Sunday, Sept. 20, most of the power to campus has been restored. However, the university said roughly 80% of campus buildings remain without hot water because of issues with the power plant’s steam distribution system.

“Yesterday I was gonna shower, but it was like freezing cold, so I’m like, I’m just gonna wait. And then this morning we still don’t have hot water, right? So I had to like, suck it through with the freezing water,” Amjad said.

University response

During the outage, the university deployed generators and emergency lights to support visibility and security. MSU Public Safety officers were also on patrol around campus.

To speed up the restoration of hot water, the university is encouraging students and staff to work remotely through Monday evening.

The MSU Veterinary Medical Center will also be closed Monday, Sept. 21, because of disruptions from the power outage.
