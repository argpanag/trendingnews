Saturday a blockbuster day of women's fourth-round U.S. Open matches

TheTools · 05.09.2026 13:37 · source · permalink

The U.S. Open is full of blockbuster third-round women's matches on Saturday. Every women's match on Saturday includes at least one seed, including some of the biggest names in tennis competing. Four Americans play on Saturday in the bottom half of the women's draw, seeking to join three compatriots from the top half in the fourth round.

Amanda Anisimova on Louis Armstrong and Madison Keys on Court 17 begin play for the American Women on Saturday at 11 ET. Coco Gauff plays the second-day match on Arthur Ashe Stadium on Saturday afternoon and Iva Jovic is the first prime-time match there.

The other four women's matches on Saturday are 13th seed Naomi Osaka against 23rd seed Elise Mertens, former US Open Champion Iga Swiatek versus 25th seed Marie Bouzkova, second seed Elena Rybakina playing Yulia Starodubtseva and 2026 French Open Champion Mirra Andreeva is matched against Nikola Bartunkova. 

Gauff continues her quest for a second US Open title and the No. 1 WTA ranking for the first time. Rybakina would have to lose before the semifinals and Gauff needs to win the title to earn the top WTA ranking. Gauff reached the third round with straight-set wins over Zeynep Sonmez and Paula Badosa.

Gauff plays Cristina Bucsa on Saturday. Both of Bucsa's first two 2026 US Open matches went three sets and she spent 5:08 on court. Gauff was on court for 3:20 in both of her first two matches in her straight-set wins to begin the US Open.

After dominating Ashlyn Krueger in the first round, Anisimova needed three sets to win in the second round. Anisimova will play Anastasia Potapova, who similarly won in straight sets in the first round and needed three sets to win her second-round match. 

Filipino star Alexandra Eala and Jovic could be the match to watch on Saturday. Ashe should be raucous, with the large Filipino fanbase backing Eala and plenty of Americans at Ashe to support Jovic. Add to the intrigue that Eala and Jovic are good friends. Jovic has defeated Eala both times they have played, including the 2026 French Open.

Keys has arguably the most difficult fourth-round match among the Americans playing on Saturday, against 2024 gold medalist Qinwen Zheng of China. After a dominant first-round win, Keys escaped in a three-set second-round win over Anna Bondar. Zheng had to go through qualifying, with her last three matches going three sets.
