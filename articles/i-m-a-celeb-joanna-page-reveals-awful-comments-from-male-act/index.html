I'm a Celeb: Joanna Page reveals 'awful' comments from male actor

TheTools · 21.11.2026 22:59 · source · permalink

Joanna Page has said she was subjected to "really inappropriate" and "awful" comments from a fellow actor while on set.

The Swansea-born actress, 48, told campmates on ITV's I'm a Celebrity… Get Me Out of Here! that the unnamed male actor had whispered "awful stuff" in her ear before a scene.

"He just spent the whole time just sort of making up this song and chanting about what he was going to do to me, and nobody said anything," she told fellow campmate Mariella Frostrup.

Page has previously described feeling forced to laugh off being groped while filming with a "handsy" TV presenter.

Page spoke of the inappropriate comments during Tuesday's episode of the reality show, as fellow celebrities, broadcaster Frostrup and model Leomie Anderson, discussed navigating male-dominated work environments in their careers.

Page said the actor had come up to her "before you were going to do a take or something".

"And it was just, like, really inappropriate, awful stuff."

Frostrup suggested that if she did speak up people would say she could not "take it", and would call it "banter".

Page then asked her campmates if they thought "we've changed".

"I think it has changed," Frostrup said, "because I look at my daughter now, and she just wouldn't put up with anything like that."

Page, who started out as an actress in 1999, previously revealed in an autobiography that she was warned about a TV presenter being "very handsy" and was groped but stayed silent because she did not want to "make a fuss".

She said she tried to keep it "light-hearted" and "would not have dreamed of making a complaint".
