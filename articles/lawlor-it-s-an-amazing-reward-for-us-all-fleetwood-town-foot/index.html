Lawlor: It's An amazing reward for us all - Fleetwood Town Football Club

TheTools · 16.09.2026 22:31 · source · permalink

Matt Lawlor described Fleetwood Town’s Carabao Cup fourth-round draw against Arsenal as an “amazing reward” for everyone connected with the club.

The Cod Army will welcome Arsenal to Highbury after making history with Wednesday night’s 1-0 victory over Sheffield United, securing their place in the fourth round for the first time.

Lawlor is looking forward to a special occasion for the players, staff and supporters, but insists Town’s immediate attention remains on Saturday’s league trip to Barnet.

“It’s the ultimate test,” Lawlor said. “It’ll be a major reward for the players and a really amazing reward for the club as well.

“The amount of work that’s been put in since the start of pre-season has been club-wide, let alone just the team.

“It’s something we’re all looking forward to. It’s full steam ahead in terms of Barnet, but this is an amazing reward for us all.”

The players shared the excitement of the draw with supporters in Jim’s Bar following the Sheffield United victory, while Lawlor was with the visiting staff when Town’s opponents were confirmed.

“I think all the boys were together over in Jim’s Bar,” the Head Coach explained. “The club came up with the slogan about being ‘More Fleetwood’, and they were in there afterwards with all the Fleetwood fans getting the Arsenal news.

“There was a lot of excitement, but there’s still a job to be done on Saturday.”

The tie also brings together two clubs connected by the Highbury name, something Lawlor revealed had made the draw particularly pleasing for the club’s ownership.

“I think the chairman was really, really happy,” he continued. “Jamie was saying it’s a tie he’s always wanted, Arsenal coming to our Highbury.

“It’s something that was really pleasing for the whole club, all the staff and players.”

Lawlor paid tribute to the atmosphere created by the Town supporters against Sheffield United and hopes to see that backing repeated when Arsenal visit.

The Head Coach felt the connection between the crowd and players helped drive the Cod Army towards their historic victory.

“I’d love them to be as amazing as they were tonight,” he said. “They drove us on to a great performance, and that’s what we’ll need at this place.

“I’ve always talked about the fans in terms of chicken and egg. Do we get them going or do they get us going? It was both tonight.

“There are going to be times when we need them more, and other times when we provide that spark. I think we’re always good under the lights at Highbury as well.

“The fans were amazing tonight and I can’t thank them enough. It’s a really good reward to get Arsenal here and be loud and proud for the town.”
