The reigning European champions look to put their difficult start behind them against a Slovan Bratislava side facing one of the toughest tests imaginable.

When and Where

Paris Saint-Germain begin the defense of their UEFA Champions League crown on Wednesday, September 9, when they host Slovan Bratislava on Matchday 1 of the 2026-27 campaign. The game will be played at the Parc des Princes in Paris, France, with kickoff scheduled for 3:00 p.m. ET and Ukrainian referee Mykola Balakin in charge.


The European opener comes at an ideal time for PSG. The reigning champions desperately need a convincing performance after an unexpectedly difficult start to the domestic season and will now have an opportunity to respond in front of their home supporters.

Slovan, meanwhile, travel to Paris fully aware of the enormous gap between the two squads. Staying in the game for as long as possible will be crucial if the Slovak champions are to turn what appears to be a one-sided matchup into an uncomfortable night for PSG.

How PSG Arrive

PSG’s start to the season has been far from what Luis Enrique would have expected. The French champions are still searching for their first Ligue 1 victory, having recorded two draws and two defeats in their opening four matches.

More concerning than the results themselves has been the manner in which they have unfolded. PSG have repeatedly found themselves trailing, managing to fight back and rescue a point on two occasions but failing to complete the comeback in the other two.

The Champions League, however, offers a completely different stage. PSG remain the defending European champions, and the difference in quality between the two squads is significant. Their home opener provides an ideal opportunity to rediscover their confidence, secure a first victory of the season and remind Europe why Luis Enrique’s team remain among the continent’s elite.


How Slovan Bratislava Arrive

Slovan Bratislava are also experiencing a difficult spell. The Slovak champions opened their domestic campaign with two victories but arrive at the Parc des Princes after suffering back-to-back defeats.

Yaya Touré’s side operate in a very different reality from their opponents. Slovan had to navigate the qualifying rounds to earn their place in the Champions League league phase, an achievement in itself for a club with significantly fewer resources than Europe’s biggest teams.

Now comes one of the toughest away trips possible. Defending with discipline, limiting space and preventing PSG from scoring early will be absolute priorities. Even if the result ultimately goes against them, Slovan will look to remain competitive and keep the margin as narrow as possible.


Key Players

Ousmane Dembélé | Paris Saint-Germain

Ousmane Dembélé remains one of Paris Saint-Germain’s main attacking stars. The Frenchman enters another Champions League campaign as the reigning Ballon d’Or winner and one of the most dangerous attacking players in world football.

Against a Slovan Bratislava side likely to defend deep around its own penalty area, his individual ability will be particularly important. Dembélé will be expected to find space and break down the Slovak side’s defensive block if PSG are to quickly impose their considerable superiority.

Nino Marcelli | Slovan Bratislava

Nino Marcelli represents one of Slovan Bratislava’s biggest attacking hopes during an extremely difficult trip to the Parc des Princes. The young Slovak forward stands out for his technical quality and ability to create danger with the ball at his feet.

Slovan are unlikely to get many opportunities, making every possession in PSG territory especially valuable. Marcelli will need to turn those limited moments on the ball into genuine scoring opportunities if the Slovak champions are to have any chance of stunning the reigning European champions.

Final Outlook

On paper, few Matchday 1 fixtures feature such a significant gap between the two teams. PSG are the reigning Champions League winners, have home-field advantage and possess a squad with considerably more individual quality than Slovan Bratislava.

Luis Enrique’s poor start in Ligue 1 introduces a small element of uncertainty, but even that has done little to change the overall expectations. PSG enter as overwhelming favorites, and a Slovan victory in Paris would rank among the biggest upsets of the opening round.

Everything points toward a game controlled by the hosts. Anything short of three points would be a major disappointment for PSG, while Slovan’s mission will be to compete, survive and prevent the difference in quality from turning into a lopsided scoreline.

Keys to the Match

  • The champions’ response: After four Ligue 1 matches without a win, PSG desperately need to regain confidence, and a commanding European victory could quickly change the mood.
  • Breaking down Slovan’s defensive block: The visitors are likely to defend with numbers around their own penalty area, making Dembélé’s creativity and one-on-one ability especially important.
  • Surviving the opening stages: The longer Slovan keep the game scoreless, the more pressure could build on a struggling PSG. An early goal for the hosts, however, could completely open up the match.
