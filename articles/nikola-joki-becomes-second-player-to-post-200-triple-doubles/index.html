Nikola Jokić becomes second player to post 200 triple-doubles | NBA.com

TheTools · 29.10.2026 16:53 · source · permalink

Top Stories

The Joker reached the milestone in his 12th NBA season.

Ben Lambert, for NBA.com

Nikola Jokić drops 31 points, 13 rebounds & 10 assists to reach historic milestone with 200th career regular-season triple-double.

Nikola Jokić became the second player in NBA history to reach 200 career triple-doubles Wednesday as the Nuggets defeated the Pelicans 104-91.

Jokić posted 21 points, 10 rebounds and 13 assists, leading the team with a +17 mark.

Russell Westbrook, the all-time leader in triple-doubles, amassed 209 in his 18 NBA seasons.

Jokić is now in his 12th campaign.
