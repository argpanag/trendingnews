From Stanley Matthews in 1956 to Ousmane Dembele in 2025, the Ballon d’Or winners’ roll spans generations of football’s biggest names. Johan Cruyff, Michel Platini and Marco van Basten each won it three times, before Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo took the award into a prolonged two-player rivalry.

Messi holds the men’s record with eight wins, ahead of Ronaldo’s five. Between them, they won every edition from 2008 to 2017, a run finally broken by Luka Modric in 2018.

The women’s Ballon d’Or began in 2018, with Ada Hegerberg its inaugural winner. Aitana Bonmati leads that honours list with three consecutive awards from 2023 to 2025, following Alexia Putellas’ back-to-back wins. Neither the men’s nor women’s award was handed out in 2020.

Which players have won the most Ballon d’Or awards?

Lionel Messi is the most successful player in the history of the men’s Ballon d’Or, having won the award eight times. Cristiano Ronaldo is second with five victories, while Michel Platini, Johan Cruyff and Marco van Basten have each won it three times.

Most men’s Ballon d’Or awards

  • Lionel Messi — 8: 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2015, 2019, 2021 and 2023.
  • Cristiano Ronaldo — 5: 2008, 2013, 2014, 2016 and 2017.
  • Michel Platini — 3: 1983, 1984 and 1985.
  • Johan Cruyff — 3: 1971, 1973 and 1974.
  • Marco van Basten — 3: 1988, 1989 and 1992.

In the women’s award, Aitana Bonmatí won three consecutive Ballon d’Or Féminin awards from 2023 to 2025, while Alexia Putellas won twice, in 2021 and 2022. The women’s award was introduced in 2018.

Which country has produced the most Ballon d’Or winners?

Argentina and France are tied for the most men’s Ballon d’Or victories, with eight each.

Argentina’s total comes entirely from Messi, while France’s eight awards have been shared by six players.
