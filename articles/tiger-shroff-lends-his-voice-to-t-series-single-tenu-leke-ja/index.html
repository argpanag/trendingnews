Tiger Shroff lends his voice to T-Series single Tenu Leke Jaana

MUMBAI: Tiger Shroff is adding another beat to his repertoire, and this time, he is not just dancing to it but singing along.

The actor has teamed up with T-Series for ‘Tenu Leke Jaana’, a new music single that brings together his vocals, signature dance moves and screen presence. Featuring Ketika Sharma alongside him, the track blends romance with a celebratory mood, giving Tiger another opportunity to bring his music and movement together outside the film screen.

Composed and written by The Rish (Rishabh Kant), the song features vocals by Tiger Shroff, Shreya Jain and RI Alia. Driven by upbeat arrangements and infectious beats, it leans on a catchy melody and an easy-going rhythm designed to find a place on party playlists.

For Tiger, the release brings two familiar parts of his performance style into one package. Known for his athletic dance movements and action-oriented screen presence, he also lends his voice to the track, adding a musical dimension to the performance.

The accompanying video, directed and shot by Manish Shunty, features Tiger and Ketika Sharma against vibrant settings and dynamic choreography. Their on-screen chemistry provides the romantic thread, while Tiger’s movement and athleticism supply much of the visual energy.

The video is built around the track’s upbeat character, with choreography and performance taking centre stage rather than an elaborate narrative. Ketika’s presence adds to the visual pairing, while Tiger moves between singing and dancing, bringing the song’s celebratory mood to life.

Speaking about the release, Tiger said music and dance had always been integral to his approach to performance, making the project a natural fit. He also spoke about enjoying the experience of recording the track and shooting the video with Ketika, while thanking T-Series and its chairman and managing director Bhushan Kumar for the opportunity.

The release adds to Tiger’s presence in the music space, where his ability to combine dance, action and performance gives him a distinct screen identity. The single also places him in a different setting from his usual film projects, allowing him to bring those skills together in a standalone music video.

‘Tenu Leke Jaana’ is now available on music streaming platforms, with its video streaming on the T-Series YouTube channel. Whether it becomes a fixture on party playlists will now depend on how quickly its beat catches on with listeners.
