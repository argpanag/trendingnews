Bitcoin extends losses as growth concerns increase amid stock market rout. What could change the trend?

TheTools · 20.11.2026 09:44 · source · permalink
    Tags
  • bitcoin
  • Bitcoin Technical Analysis

Summary

  • Bitcoin remains under pressure as worsening growth concerns and negative risk sentiment weigh on the crypto market. The combination of Fed tightening, US-Iran tensions, limited fiscal stimulus and AI-related risks creates a bad macro backdrop. 

Bitcoin extended the losses further yesterday following another rout in the stock market as growth concerns increased and the risk sentiment remained negative.

The market is seeing a slowdown in the economy in the next 6-12 months caused by Fed tightening, prolonged US-Iran stalemate, limited fiscal impulse due to the Democratic Congress and AI bubble burst.

This is a very negative macro backdrop that is likely to keep weighing on Bitcoin and the crypto market in general. Only a dovish Fed or a surprising breakthrough in the US-Iran situation are likely to reverse the current trend.

Next week, there’s only the US Flash PMIs as an important catalyst to watch. Weak data could exacerbate growth concerns and put further pressure on Bitcoin. Strong data, on the other hand, could trigger a short-term relief rally, although it wouldn’t change the bigger picture.

Of course, the focus will also be on US-Iran developments as an end to the war should lead to a positive repricing in growth expectations.

BITCOIN TECHNICAL ANALYSIS – DAILY TIMEFRAME

On the daily chart, we can see that Bitcoin broke below the support zone around the 62,400 level, opening the door for a move into the 58,000 support next. If the price gets there, we can expect the buyers to step in, with a defined risk below the support, to position for a rally into the major downward trendline around the 74,000 level. The sellers, on the other hand, will want to see the price breaking lower to increase the bearish bets into the 50,000 level next.

BITCOIN TECHNICAL ANALYSIS – 4 HOUR TIMEFRAME

On the 4 hour chart, we have a minor downward trendline defining the bearish momentum. If we get a pullback into the trendline, we can expect the sellers to lean on it, with a defined risk above it, to keep pushing into new lows. The buyers, on the other hand, will look for a break higher to increase the bullish bets into the major downward trendline next.

BITCOIN TECHNICAL ANALYSIS – 1 HOUR TIMEFRAME

On the 1 hour chart, we have another minor trendline that could act as resistance. The sellers will likely lean on the trendline, with a defined risk above it, to keep targeting the 58,000 support. The buyers, on the other hand, will look for a break higher to extend the pullback into the next trendline. The red lines define the average daily range for today.

UPCOMING CATALYSTS

Today we have Trump speaking at a press conference about his Asia trip, but he could certainly digress and talk about Iran. On Monday, we get the US Flash PMIs. On Thursday, we get the latest US Jobless Claims figures. The focus will remain on growth concerns, the Fed and US-Iran developments.
