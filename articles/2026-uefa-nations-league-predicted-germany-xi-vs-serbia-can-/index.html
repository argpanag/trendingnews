2026 UEFA Nations League | Predicted Germany XI vs Serbia- Can Germany get its first win under Klopp?  - Get German Football News

TheTools · 01.10.2026 06:50 · source · permalink

After a draw with the Netherlands and a disappointing home loss to Greece, Germany host Serbia at the Allianz Arena on Thursday. 

Nico Schlotterbeck, who sustained an ankle ligament injury during training on Wednesday, will be out of the game. Meanwhile, Bayern Munich keeper Jonas Urbig will make his senior national team debut. 

Klopp is once again expected to make changes to the starting line-up as he will try to make full use of his extended squad. 

Cologne’s Said El Mala and Juventus’s Nico Woltemade are expected to make the starting 11, having not featured in the previous two games. Florian Wirtz, who started on the bench against Greece, is also expected to play from the start against Serbia. 

When asked who will replace Joshua Kimmich as captain, Klopp said: “Whoever has the most international caps in the starting lineup will wear the armband tomorrow. Feel free to ask, but I won’t reveal the starting lineup now. You can look them up yourself. I can only say that Jonas Urbig won’t wear the armband in his first international match.” 

Germany Predicted XI vs Serbia 

Jonas Urbig – Ridle Baku, Waldemar Anton, Finn Jeltsch, David Raum –Alexander Pavlovic, Vitaly Janelt – Lennart Karl , Florian Wirtz, Said El Mala- Nico Woltemade
