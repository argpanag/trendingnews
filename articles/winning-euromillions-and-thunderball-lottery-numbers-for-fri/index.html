Winning EuroMillions and Thunderball lottery numbers for Friday's £31m jackpot

TheTools · 20.11.2026 20:35 · source · permalink

Need to know

Forms for the EuroMillions lottery in a display rack(Image: PA)

Everything you need to know about the Euromillions draw on Friday, November 20, including how much was up for grabs and the winning numbers.

  • The Euromillions jackpot was £31 million.
  • The Thunderball game has a top prize of £500,000.
  • The Thunderball draw took place at 8pm.
  • The winning Thunderball numbers are: 2, 9, 14, 29, 30, and the Thunderball is 11.
  • The Euromillions draw was announced at 8.33pm.
  • The winning Euromillions numbers are: 8, 21, 24, 36, 49, and the Lucky Stars are 1 and 8.
