Sauce Gardner Ruled out for Week 7 - Fantasy Football News

TheTools · 25.10.2026 17:29 · source · permalink

Gardner (calf) is inactive for Sunday's game against the Vikings.

The decision doesn't come as much of a surprise, given that Gardner is considered week-to-week due to a calf injury that he picked up during the Colts' Week 6 loss to the Titans. Cam Taylor-Britt will likely start opposite Charvarius Ward at outside cornerback in the absence of Gardner, whose next chance to play is Week 8 against the Jaguars on Nov. 1.
