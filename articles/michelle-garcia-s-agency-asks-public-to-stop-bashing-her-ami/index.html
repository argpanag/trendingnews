Michelle Garcia's agency asks public to stop bashing her amid Patrick Garcia issue

TheTools · 01.10.2026 16:15 · source · permalink

Amid the controversy surrounding Patrick Garcia, officials have stepped up to protect the actor's young daughter, Michelle Garcia.

On social media, talent agency Star Magic released an official statement regarding the hate comments directed at its talent.

READ: Patrick Garcia's daughter Michelle joins K-pop training

"Star Magic is deeply concerned about the hurtful comments directed at our artist Michelle Garcia, who is only 13 years old," they wrote.

"We ask the public to refrain from involving Michelle in matters concerning adults that happened before she was born."

The agency also said it is prepared to take legal action against bashers or individuals who harass Michelle.

"We continue to support Michelle and look after her well-being," they added.

A post shared by Star Magic (@starmagicphils)

Patrick recently opened up about his past relationship and heartbreak with Jennylyn Mercado and their son, Alex Jazz.

The actor said he was inspired to share his side of the story after his daughter, Michelle, encouraged him to speak. He also discussed how his family has faced criticism over the years because of what happened during his past relationship with Jennylyn.

"But until this day, the words of Jen is haunting me, my family, and my daughter. People are hating on us too much, and I think it's unfair. That's why I'm telling my story," he said.

"For me, hindi ako masamang tao. I'm not evil. Ayun yung nangyari sa situation namin. Ayun talaga ang nangyari."

In the interview, Patrick also said that he did not abandon Jennylyn or their son. He claimed that the actress eventually cut him off from their lives.

Meanwhile, Jennylyn's talent agency said that the actress refrained from commenting or granting interviews about the matter.

Amid the issue, Patrick's current wife and Michelle's mother, Nikka, showed her support for her husband with a sweet message.

"I won in this life. I fell in love with a good man and I'll never stop being grateful for that," she wrote.

"A self-made man. A hardworking man. A gentle man and safe man. A man who takes care of me in all the ways that matter."
