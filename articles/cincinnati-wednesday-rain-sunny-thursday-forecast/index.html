Cincinnati Wednesday Rain, Sunny Thursday Forecast

TheTools · 04.11.2026 13:24 · source · permalink

Showers are expected in Cincinnati Wednesday morning. The wet stretch will continue for much of the day before skies begin to clear and warmer-than-typical fall weather gives way to a cooler weekend.

Rain Lingers Through Wednesday

The National Weather Service expects rain before 7 a.m., followed by occasional showers and a slight chance of thunderstorms through the afternoon. Rainfall could total one-half to three-quarters of an inch, with stronger showers or storms possibly producing locally heavy rainfall.

South winds could gust to around 25 knots, adding a little extra drama to an otherwise thoroughly gray day. With low clouds and periods of heavy rain, drivers should allow extra time and use headlights during the morning commute.

Sunny Thursday, Cooler Weekend

Showers should taper from west to east late Wednesday afternoon into Wednesday evening, leaving a mostly cloudy night with a low near 51 degrees. Thursday looks like the weather reset button, with mostly sunny skies, a high near 66 and no rain expected.

Friday will be partly sunny with a slight chance of rain after 1 p.m. and a high near 60, followed by mostly sunny conditions Saturday and Sunday with highs in the mid-to-upper 50s. Overnight lows will dip into the mid-30s by Saturday and Sunday nights, while sunshine and milder temperatures return Monday and Tuesday.

No active watches or warnings are listed for the area, but the chance of locally heavy rain and gusty winds will make Wednesday the day to keep plans flexible. By Thursday, umbrellas can take the day off.
