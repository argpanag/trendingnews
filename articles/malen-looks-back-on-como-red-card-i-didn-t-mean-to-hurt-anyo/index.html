Malen looks back on Como red card: "I didn't mean to hurt anyone."

TheTools · 13.10.2026 12:29 · source · permalink

Donyell Malen’s performance against Como was marred by a red card that will force him to miss the next league match, but the Dutchman will be back in the Champions League.

The number 14 will lead the attack tomorrow, October 14th, at the Olimpico against Real Madrid, and will look to do what he does best: score.

The Dutchman spoke at a press conference alongside Gian Piero Gasperini on the eve of the European match.

The 1999-born player opened with this: “We have to focus on ourselves, not think about the gap. There’s frustration over the red card, but I think my intention was clear: not to hurt anyone. We’ll be competing against one of the biggest teams in Europe.”

For ‘Bobby’, playing at home is an extra edge: “It will be important to be able to count on the fans’ support. In difficult moments, the fans always inspire us.”

He then said about the evening: “It will be a unique experience to play in the Champions League, it was one of my objectives when I arrived. Real Madrid is a special team by definition.”

Malen focused on his adaptation to the capital: “Gasperini was crucial, but so were the staff, his teammates, and the support of the fans. They were all important factors.”

Then the number 14 spoke about Jude Bellingham, his former teammate at Borussia Dortmund: “I haven’t spoken to him; we haven’t played together in a while. Everyone knows his qualities; it will be an opportunity to reunite; we have a good relationship.”
