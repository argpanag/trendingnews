Sam Claflin put on a cosy display with his actress girlfriend Dar Zuzovsky at the global premiere of his new film Ebenezer in London's Leicester Square on Monday night. 

The pair were both dressed to the nines for the red carpet outing, with Sam, 40, looking dapper in a cream polka dot blazer, a white shirt and dark trousers, accessorising the look with an orchid buttonhole.

Sam Claflin was joined by his girlfriend Dar Zuzovsky at the Ebenezer premiere (@ Karwai Tang/WireImage)

Meanwhile, Dar looked stunning in a plunging, shimmering gown, featuring a daring slit. The 35-year-old actress wore her hair in an elegant updo, while styling the look with a pair of strappy nude heels. 

Sam plays Fred, the nephew of Ebenezer Scrooge, in the film, which also stars Johnny Depp, Rupert Grint, Andrea Riseborough, Daisy Ridley, Tramell Tillman and Ian McKellen.

Sam and Dar's low-key romance

Sam and Dar's glamorous date night marks a rare public outing for the low-key couple.

Sam met Israeli actress Dar on the set of the MGM+ mystery series, Vanished, in which they both starred.

In an interview with the Israeli media outlet Ynetglobal in July, Dar revealed that the pair had been dating for ten months. 

The couple were initially friends before their relationship turned romantic (@ Dave Benett/Getty Images for New)

Gushing over her blossoming romance, she told the publication: "Listen, I'm in love and I'm very happy with where things stand, but it's still early. Sam and I have been together for 10 months, and we're still learning each other. Whatever is meant to happen will happen."

When they met, Dar was fresh from a year-long relationship. "Sam and I immediately became good friends," she said, explaining that their friendship eventually evolved into something romantic. "We stayed in touch after filming ended, and when I was in New York, he told me he was coming there too to shoot a movie. We met up, spent time together, I introduced him to my friends, and at some point we both realised there was something much deeper than friendship," explained the actress. 

Sam met Dar on the set of Vanished, in which he starred opposite Kaley Cuoco (@ AGC Studios/Fragile Films/MGM+)

As for their future plans, Dar shared that the couple have spoken about starting a family. "I do think it will happen soon. Or rather, I would like it to happen. I would like to be a mother, God willing," she said. 

Sam was previously married to actress Laura Haddock. The pair, who wed in July 2013 and separated in August 2019, share a son and a daughter.

Sam was previously married to actress Laura Haddock (@ Getty)

Who is Sam Claflin's girlfriend Dar Zuzovsky?

Dar, who hails from Tel Aviv in Israel, has appeared in a number of Israeli productions, including the drama Hostage and the rom-com series, Beauty and the Baker.

Dar Zuzovsky is an Israeli actress (@ WireImage)

In 2024, she featured in the Martin Scorsese docudrama series Martin Scorsese Presents: The Saints, before starring alongside Sam and Kaley Cuoco in Vanished.
