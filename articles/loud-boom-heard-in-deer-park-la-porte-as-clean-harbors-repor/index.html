Loud boom heard in Deer Park, La Porte as Clean Harbors reports incident

TheTools · 18.11.2026 03:07 · source · permalink

Local News

KPRC 2 has received multiple calls from viewers reporting a loud boom or explosion-like sound in the Deer Park area

LA PORTE, Texas – Clean Harbors Deer Park has reported some sort of explosion incident has occurred at its facility.

A CAER message from the facility says it experienced an explosion and a fire/process upset at the facility located at 2027 Independence Parkway in La Porte. The message says the incident occurred at 8:48 p.m.

Recommended Videos

“At this time, Emergency Vehicles, Flames, Noise, Sirens, Smoke, Activity may be noticeable to the community. We are actively responding to the incident,” the message said.

The City of Deer Park Office of Emergency Management says it is currently monitoring reports of a potential tank fire at the facility.

Multiple viewers have called KPRC 2 to report hearing a loud boom in the area of Deer Park and La Porte.

KPRC 2 is working to learn more about the incident and will update this story with any new information we receive.
