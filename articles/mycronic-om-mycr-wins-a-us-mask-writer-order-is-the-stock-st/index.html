Mycronic (OM:MYCR) drew fresh attention after announcing a client order in the US for two SLX mask writers worth between US$14 million and US$16 million, with delivery planned for the first quarter of 2028.

Recent trading tells a mixed story. The share price is up 38.6% year to date and the 1 year total shareholder return is 51.07%, yet the stock has declined 13.49% over 90 days and 11.13% over the past week. This suggests that momentum for Mycronic may be cooling in the short term, even as longer term holders have seen strong compounding over three and five years.
