More rain expected Thursday - AOL

TheTools · 08.11.2026 23:32 · source · permalink

(WSPA) - Rain chances are back, with chances for showers and a few thunderstorms continuing into Thursday.

The highest coverage in the Upstate is expected Wednesday afternoon into Wednesday night. Locally heavy downpours are possible, mainly in the west, and an isolated but strong thunderstorm can't be ruled out.

A secondary area of low pressure could mean a few showers linger into Friday, especially over the eastern counties. Once that system passes, counties will see dry weather into early next week.

Temperatures will remain warmer than average through Friday, with gradual cooling over the weekend.
