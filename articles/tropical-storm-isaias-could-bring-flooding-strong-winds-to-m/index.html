Tropical Storm Isaias could bring flooding, strong winds to metro Atlanta this weekend

TheTools · 07.10.2026 14:31 · source · permalink

October 7, 2026 / 10:13 AM EDT / CBS Atlanta

The CBS News Atlanta NEXT Weather Team is closely tracking Isaias.

Isaias officially became a tropical storm as of 5 a.m. on Wednesday.

Isaias is likely to develop into a hurricane soon.

The official track from the Hurricane Center brings the storm into the northern Gulf as a category two hurricane by Friday morning with winds up to 110 mph.

It will likely make landfall as a category two hurricane somewhere between New Orleans and Panama City Beach, Florida.

The storm will weaken over land, but will still bring impacts to North Georgia and Metro Atlanta this weekend.

By late Friday and early Saturday, outer bands of Isaias will bring rain to North Georgia and Metro Atlanta. 

The main threats on the current track will be gusty winds, possibly up to hurricane strength, as well as flooding rain. Rain totals in much of Metro Atlanta could be between two and three inches by Sunday.

There is a Next Weather Alert for both Saturday and Sunday as we continue to track these changing weather conditions. It is still a bit early to pinpoint exactly what impacts can be expected.

Stay tuned to the CBS News Atlanta Next Weather team for the latest updates.
