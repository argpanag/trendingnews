Shots fired at Scottsdale short-term rental - Daily Independent

TheTools · 14.10.2026 01:06 · source · permalink

Scottsdale Police responded to multiple reports of shots fired at a house in the 7300 block of Papago Drive shortly after 3:30 a.m. on Sunday, Oct. 11.

No one was injured in the incident, according to a press release from the Scottsdale Police Department.

When officers arrived at the house, which is a short-term rental, they located several shell casings in the street, according to the release.
