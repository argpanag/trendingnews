Daniel Farke press conference: Chelsea vs Leeds in Carabao Cup

TheTools · 09.09.2026 13:40 · source · permalink
  • Published
    8 September 2026

Leeds United boss Daniel Farke has been speaking to the media before Wednesday's Carabao Cup third-round tie against Chelsea at Stamford Bridge (kick-off 20:00 BST).

Here are the key lines from his news conference:

  • Farke said there's "a little question mark" over Daniel James as he "reported an issue with his back after the warm-up at Brighton". He added: "The scan results were good. We have two more sessions [so] if he comes through those then he is hopefully available. Carefully optimistic."

  • Farke confirmed there will be some squad rotation against Chelsea to help "integrate new players". The German also said he wants Leeds "to be difficult to predict and tactical".

  • "It's possible" that goalkeeper Michael Zetterer will make his Whites debut in the cup, but they "won't make a decision before the training session this afternoon".

  • Farke added that James Trafford could also retain his place in goal as he continues to work towards "finding his rhythm" at the club.

  • On how Harry Wilson has performed in the opening three games: "We play with a different style of football to Fulham so it's normal that he would need time to adapt. I'm happy with Harry. He's a top professional."

  • On how Leeds are approaching the Carabao Cup: "We will try to win it. It's the quickest way to some glory."

  • On opponents Chelsea: "They are probably favourites, but we want to be competitive. We have to be ready for a very demanding game and be prepared for different scenarios. Be respectful, but have no fear."
