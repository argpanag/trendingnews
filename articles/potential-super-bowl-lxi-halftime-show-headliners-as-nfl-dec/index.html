Potential Super Bowl LXI halftime show headliners as NFL decision nears

TheTools · 11.09.2026 21:44 · source · permalink

The headline act for the Super Bowl LXI halftime show is expected to be announced some time this month.

While there is still an entire NFL season to be played before teams and players start worrying about the Super Bowl, fans, media, and the league itself are already honing their focus on one aspect of the game that will be watched by more than 100 million people worldwide.

The Super Bowl LXI halftime show, which will take place on Feb. 14, 2027 from SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles, California, has potential to be the most-watched live concert of all time.

Choosing the headline act is a monumental decision made by the NFL and several of its advisers, and it is typically announced to the public some time in September each year.

Here are 10 potential headline acts for the Super Bowl LXI halftime show, ranked from least to most likely based on current probabilities on prediction market platform Kalshi.

Metallica

Heavy metal band Metallica has long been linked with headlining the Super Bowl halftime show, but given the NFL’s recent theme of picking pop and hip-hop acts, it seems unlikely the LA-based rockers will be the ones gracing the stage at SoFi Stadium.

Harry Styles

Styles is one of the most popular pop acts in the world, and the former One Direction singer is currently in the middle of a 30-night residency at Madison Square Garden in New York City that runs through Oct. 31.

BTS

South Korean boy band BTS would certainly fit the NFL’s ambition for global reach if they are picked to headline the halftime show. The seven-man group recently performed their smash hit ‘Dynamite’ during the World Cup final halftime show in New Jersey.

Ella Langley

Langley is perhaps the newest phenom on this list, having risen to mainstream popularity only within the last year. The 27-year-old country music star’s hit song ‘Choosin’ Texas’ is currently on a 21-week run at the top of the Billboard Hot 100 charts.

Dua Lipa

Lipa’s decade-long career as a global pop superstar makes her a strong fit for the Super Bowl halftime show. Among her most famous songs are ‘Levitating,’ ‘Don’t Start Now,’ and ‘Dance the Night.’

Sabrina Carpenter

Another globally renowned pop artist, Carpenter made her debut in a Super Bowl commercial with Pringles in February 2026 and has a realistic chance of being the next name to headline the halftime show.

Billie Eilish

Eilish, mostly categorized as an alternative pop artist, is another name constantly linked with the Super Bowl halftime show. At the end of the 2024 Paris Olympics, Eilish performed at the official handover ceremony for the 2028 Summer Olympics in Los Angeles, further fueling fan speculation that she could perform at an LA-based Super Bowl.

Justin Bieber

Bieber has already performed at the 2026 World Cup final halftime show and headlined music festival Coachella this year, prompting fans to believe a Super Bowl appearance could be next. The 32-year-old Canadian undoubtedly has the global popularity and reach to be considered a strong fit.

Jay-Z

If Jay-Z ends up headlining the Super Bowl halftime show, it won’t be a decision solely made by the NFL. Since 2019, the Roc Nation co-founder has been one of the NFL’s chief advisers in selecting who headlines the Super Bowl halftime show, so him ‘picking himself’ would likely cause some controversy among fans.

Miley Cyrus

Cyrus is the consensus favorite to headline the Super Bowl LXI halftime show at the time of writing. The pop icon has stated before that she would be open to the task, and many fans believe it is only a matter of time until she takes on the job.
