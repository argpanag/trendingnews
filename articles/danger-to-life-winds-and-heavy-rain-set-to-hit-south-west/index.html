'Danger to life' winds and heavy rain set to hit South West

TheTools · 19.10.2026 10:48 · source · permalink

A period of 'damaging winds' is forecast to strike the South West over the coming days, with meteorologists warning of a potential "danger to life".

A yellow wind warning will remain in effect from 12am on Friday, October 23, until at least 12pm that day.

During the morning, wind gusts could reach up to 70mph in some areas, though the Met Office says there remains some uncertainty about where the strongest winds will occur.

The alert covers most of the region, including all of Devon, Cornwall, Somerset, and Dorset. Sections of Gloucestershire, Wiltshire and South Wales will also be impacted, including Bristol.

South Gloucestershire is primarily affected, with Thornbury and Falfield included, but not Dursley.

The Met Office warns that powerful winds may bring a slight risk of structural damage to buildings, personal injuries, and danger to life, alongside travel disruption affecting rail services, flights, and ferry operations. There's also a risk of electricity outages and interruptions to mobile phone coverage.

"There is a small chance that injuries and danger to life could occur from large waves and beach material being thrown onto sea fronts, coastal roads and properties," the forecaster says.

The Met Office continues: "Strong west to northwesterly winds may spread across southern parts of England and Wales on Friday.

"Gusts of 50-60 mph are possible and some coastal or exposed areas could see 70 mph or more, but at this time there is uncertainty in where the strongest winds will be.

"There will be some periods of heavy rain which will make travel more difficult. Whilst it will remain breezy the winds will probably ease late morning into the early afternoon."

The latest forecast suggests a changeable week ahead for the South West, with residents set to experience a mixture of downpours and sunny intervals.

Met Office South West Weekly forecast

Outlook for Wednesday to Friday: Turning wet and windy on Wednesday. Clearing before turning showery with sunny spells into Thursday with lighter winds.
