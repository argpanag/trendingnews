DJ Moore among Bills' inactives vs. Rams

TheTools · 13.10.2026 00:21 · source · permalink

Inactives Report

The Buffalo Bills are listing seven players as inactive for Monday's game against the Los Angeles Rams

INACTIVE

  • WR DJ Moore
  • WR Joshua Palmer
  • CB Dee Alford
  • G Ar'maj Reed-Adams
  • T/G Jude Bowry
  • DL Ed Oliver
  • DL Landon Jackson

Moore, Alford and Jackson were listed as questionable coming into the game, however they will officially not be available to play. The Bills ruled out Oliver and Palmer on Saturday.

Earlier today, Buffalo announced it signed CB Anthony Kendall from the practice squad and elevated DL Phidarian Mathis and WR Trent Sherfield Sr. from the practice squad. All three players are active.

Bills-Rams kicks off at 8:15 p.m. Here's how to watch.
