NFL Disciplines Deshaun Watson For 'Vulgar' Celebration In Week 4

TheTools · 10.10.2026 22:22 · source · permalink

Deshaun Watson learned this weekend that every celebration on the field is monitored by the NFL.

During last Thursday’s game between the Pittsburgh Steelers and Cleveland Browns, the camera crew for Prime Video caught Quinshon Judkins celebrating with Watson after a touchdown. Although it was a brief exchange, it looked like they were imitating a smoking gesture.

Well, the NFL has fined both Watson and Judkins for unsportsmanlike conduct (illegal celebrations and vulgar acts). Watson was fined $14,926, whereas Judkins received a $14,397 fine.

It’s unclear if Watson or Judkins will appeal the NFL’s decision.

Coming into the 2026 season, Watson was expected to be on a short leash. What fans didn’t expect was for him to regain his confidence in Todd Monken’s system.

In four games this season, Watson has completed 69.6% of his passes for 855 yards with six touchdowns and two interceptions. His resurgence is a big reason why the Browns are 3-1 heading into Week 5.

According to Mary Kay Cabot of Cleveland.com, the Browns could consider re-signing Watson if he continues to play well.

We’ll see if Watson continues his bounce-back season this Sunday against the New York Jets.
