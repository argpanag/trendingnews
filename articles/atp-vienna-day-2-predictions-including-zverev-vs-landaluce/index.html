ATP Vienna Day 2 Predictions Including Zverev vs Landaluce

TheTools · 27.10.2026 03:10 · source · permalink

All eyes are on the top players looking for momentum this week at the 2026 Erste Bank Open in Vienna, an ATP 500 event featuring six Top 10 players. The ATP Finals list is still taking shape, so valuable ranking points might be the difference between a season concluding in a few weeks or in Turin at the end of November. LWOT previews all the Tuesday first-round action, with this set of predictions featuring soon-to-be World No. 1 Alexander Zverev.

ATP Vienna Day 2 Predictions

Cameron Norrie vs Francisco Cerundolo

Head-to-head: Cerundolo 2-1

Norrie tends to thrive against power hitters, using his speed and counterpunching to his advantage on any surface. But Cerundolo dominated the Brit in their previous two meetings, the most recent being a 6-3 6-0 blowout. The pair haven’t played one another since 2023. Norrie’s form regressed in 2026, while Cerundolo should feel confident on these fast courts given his powerful forehand. Look for the Argentine to advance to the second round. 
Prediction: Cerundolo in 2

Flavio Cobolli vs Grigor Dimitrov

Head-to-head: First meeting

With a 9-15 Tour-level record this year, Dimitrov hasn’t been able to deploy his one-handed backhand and thundering serve against top players. However, he’s a former semifinalist at this tournament, while Cobolli’s topspin game thrives on slower courts. Dimitrov still has 43 Top 10 wins to his name, and while he probably won’t win this match, I wouldn’t be surprised if he nags a set with the Bulgarian playing free of pressure and expectations.  
Prediction: Cobolli in 3 

Alexander Zverev vs Martin Landaluce

Head-to-head: First meeting

Landaluce will look to return to his career-high ranking of No. 55 at the tail end of this season, but he’s on a disappointing losing streak, featuring a Lyon loss to the soon-to-be-retired Stan Wawrinka. His powerful forehand will make the highlight reel at the end of this match, but it won’t be enough to dismantle Zverev and his consistency. The German and No. 1 seed should win this opening round match in two.
Prediction: Zverev in 2
