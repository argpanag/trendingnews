Harvey Weinstein sentenced to 15 years after overturned #MeToo conviction

NEW YORK (TNND) — Harvey Weinstein was sentenced to 15 years in prison Wednesday for sexually assaulting a former TV production assistant, restoring a measure of accountability in the case that became a defining moment of the #MeToo movement after his original New York conviction was overturned.

Weinstein, 74, was convicted at a 2025 retrial of a first-degree criminal sex act for assaulting Miriam Haley at his Manhattan apartment in 2006. The Oscar-winning producer of films including “Pulp Fiction” and “Shakespeare in Love” has already spent more than six years in custody.

The New York Court of Appeals overturned Weinstein’s original 2020 conviction and 23-year sentence in 2024, finding he was prejudiced by testimony from women whose allegations were not charged in the case. That ruling required prosecutors to retry him.

At last year’s retrial, jurors acquitted Weinstein of a separate allegation involving Kaja Sokola and could not reach a verdict on a rape charge involving aspiring actor Jessica Mann. After a later jury again deadlocked on the rape count in May, prosecutors dropped it rather than pursue a fourth trial after Mann said she could not bear to testify again.

Haley, who testified at two trials, spoke before Wednesday’s sentencing about the lasting impact of the assault.

Having been sexually assaulted by Harvey Weinstein has had a devastating effect on my life and sense of security that may never go away,” Haley said. “Making the decision to speak up will have me looking over my shoulder for years to come. It’s a life sentence for me.

She said the effort to hold Weinstein accountable had further traumatized her and that she had considered ending her pursuit of justice many times.

According to The Associated Press, Weinstein apologized in court to people he may have hurt but again denied sexually assaulting anyone. “I do have remorse for Miriam Haley’s pain, but I have to reiterate my innocence,” he told the judge while seated in a wheelchair.

Manhattan prosecutors had sought a 20-year sentence, while Weinstein’s lawyers asked for three additional years beyond the time he has already served, citing his age and serious health problems. He can appeal the sentence.

Weinstein also awaits resentencing in a separate Los Angeles rape case. A California appeals court upheld his 2022 conviction but ordered a new sentencing hearing after finding the judge considered his since-overturned New York convictions as an aggravating factor.
