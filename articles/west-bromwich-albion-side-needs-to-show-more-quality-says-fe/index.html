West Bromwich Albion: Side needs to show more quality says Felix Horn Myhre

TheTools · 13.10.2026 21:09 · source · permalink
  • Published
    12 October 2026

West Bromwich Albion midfielder Felix Horn Myhre says he and his team-mates will have to up their game when they head for Wrexham on Tuesday on the back of two successive Championship derby defeats.

Losing at Wolves and then at home to Birmingham City either side of the extended international break has left James Morrison's men without a win in over a month.

Albion won 3-2 on their only previous league visit to Wrexham 14 months ago. But, after scoring 11 times in their first four league and cup games this term, the goals have since rather dried up for the Baggies.

They netted once in every one of their next five league games and then failed to score in their past two, which would both have been heavier defeats but for the fine form of goalkeeper Max O'Leary.

"Max made some really good saves again," Horn Myhre told BBC Radio WM. "But we shouldn't need to be reliant on our goalkeeper to keep us in it.

Aune Heggebo did hit the post against Blues and Horn Myhre went close with a header - but that was as close as Albion came.

"It's fine margins. If he scores with that one and I score with the header, then you never know what happens," Horn Myhre added.

"If you take your chances, eventually the momentum will change. But, after they scored it was an uphill battle and we didn't have enough quality.

"It was a bit flat. We never really got up to the pace we can and should - and we didn't create enough to win. We especially need to improve in the final third.

"The good thing is we don't have to dwell. We can get our heads straight and look for ways to beat Wrexham."

The main worry for the Baggies is that, following their 6-0 thumping at West Ham, Wrexham have since responded by picking up four points out of a possible six.

Felix Horn Myhre scored on his Albion debut as a second-half substitute in the opening-day EFL Cup win at Rotherham - but has not netted since
