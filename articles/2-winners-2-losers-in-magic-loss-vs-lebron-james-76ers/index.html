ORLANDO, Fla. — The Orlando Magic are seeing their win streak crumble after falling to the Philadelphia 76ers 121-116 inside the Kia Center.

The Magic led by as much as 17 points in the first quarter, but the Sixers kept chipping away throughout the night. In the fourth quarter, the 76ers outscored the Magic 27-16 to overtake them and come out on top.
