#13 Purdue Steals Set From #1 Nebraska

TheTools · 18.10.2026 05:32 · source · permalink

LINCOLN, Neb. - The No. 13 Purdue Boilermakers played its first four-setter of the season, forcing the No. 1 Nebraska Cornhuskers to additional sets, ultimately falling 13-25, 25-20, 19-25, 14-25).

Freshman Addy Tindall, coming off one of her best performances of Big Ten play at Iowa after going errorless on the attack with a Big Ten-high in kills, led all players with 16 kills.

Taylor Anderson dished out 36 assists in the match, adding three kills, five digs and a block.

Purdue controlled Set 2, going on to win it 25-20 to hand Nebraska its first set loss of the Big Ten season and first against a Big Ten opponent since November 14, 2025. In the set, Purdue out-hit Nebraska .237 to .118 and out-blocked the Huskers 4-1.

 

UP NEXT
The Boilermakers return home next week to Holloway Gymnasium, where the squad will take on its seventh opponent ranked or receiving votes in the AVCA poll in the last eight matches, hosting #23 Minnesota on Friday at 7 p.m. ET on B1G+. Then, Purdue will return Sunday at 4 p.m. ET to take on Northwestern on Big Ten Network.
