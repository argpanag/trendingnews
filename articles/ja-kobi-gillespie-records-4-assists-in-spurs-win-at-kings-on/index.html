Ja'Kobi Gillespie contributed 3 points, 4 assists, 2 rebounds, and 1 steal in 14 minutes of play during San Antonio Spurs' 123-107 victory against the Kings, shooting 33.3% from the field and from three-point range.

Insight

Gillespie recorded 4 assists in 14 minutes against the Kings, surpassing his season average of 1.3 APG. He added 3 points on 1-of-3 shooting, aligning with his 33.3% shooting from both the field and three-point range. Additionally, Gillespie contributed 2 rebounds and 1 steal in the victory.
