Rumer Willis is seeing a little bit of her famous father in her daughter.

The actress, 38, recently opened up about her 3-year-old daughter, Louetta, and the special ways she reminds her of her grandfather, Bruce Willis, as he continues to live with frontotemporal dementia.

In an interview with InStyle, Rumer said she has noticed Bruce's playful personality in her daughter, particularly in the way Louetta expresses herself.

"I see so much of him in her," Rumer said, pointing to the little girl's smirks as one of the things that reminds her of her grandfather. She also credited both of her parents, Bruce and Demi Moore, with encouraging imagination and curiosity when she was growing up.

"I have so much appreciation for being a parent, for the whimsy," Rumer said. "My parents did such an incredible job of cultivating our imagination and curiosity."

Jeffrey Mayer/JTMPhotos, Int'l. / MEGA

Louetta, whom Rumer welcomed in April 2023, has already formed a special relationship with Bruce. Rumer previously said she feels particularly grateful that her daughter has had the opportunity to know her grandfather, given his diagnosis.

Bruce was diagnosed with frontotemporal dementia in 2023, and Rumer has been candid about how the condition has changed her relationship with her father.

"There's a chapter that I've had for most of my life and now we're in this chapter," she told the outlet. "It'll never be the way it was, and there's waves of it, but as a family, we're doing the best to meet him where he is."

Rumer has also described a new tenderness in her father and said she remains grateful for the time she gets to spend with him.

For Rumer, watching Louetta develop little pieces of Bruce's personality is another reminder of the parts of him that continue through the next generation.

And while the circumstances surrounding Bruce's health have changed their family, Rumer's comments make clear that the love connecting the three generations hasn't gone anywhere.
