Nicola Coughlan to appear on this week's Late Late

Featured

News

Share with

Nicola Coughlan will be coming back to Ireland this week to chat to Patrick Kielty.

She will sit down for this week’s Late Late Show to chat about her upcoming projects.

She’ll discuss her new hard-hitting drama I Am Helen, what it was like hosting Saturday Night Live (SNL) UK, hanging out with her comedy heroes and heading back to the world of Bridgerton.

We can’t wait to hear from our favourite Galway Girl!

Comedian John Bishop will drop by to talk about his brand-new show Let’s Go Round Again, which will come to Ireland in May of next year.

He’ll also chat about heading to Patagonia with Jason Byrne, why raising pigs comes with its challenges, and why astronauts make better motorcyclists than comedians.

We’ll keep the comedy rolling as Neil Delamere will be in studio as he prepares to head out on the road with his latest stand-up tour, Neil’s on Wheels.

He’ll speak about the audience members he loves to chat to, the Irish urge to play fast and loose with the rules and making friends after 40.

Kilkenny GAA legend TJ Reid will chat to Patrick about his retirement from inter-county hurling, and celebrate his remarkable career as the all-time leading scorer of the championship.

Cork pop powerhouse Lyra will give a musical tribute to Shane MacGowan, as well as sharing some exciting personal news.

Oh and one child in Ireland will get some very exciting Late Late Toy Show news.

 The Late Late Show airs on Friday night at 9.35pm on RTÉ One and RTÉ Player.
