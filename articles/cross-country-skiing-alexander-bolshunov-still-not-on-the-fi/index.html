Cross-country skiing: Alexander Bolshunov still not on the FIS list of authorised neutral athletes | Nordic Mag | N°1 Biathlon | Nordic skiing

TheTools · 04.11.2026 05:59 · source · permalink

Cross-country skiing: permits granted on a case-by-case basis

Alexander Bolshunov’sname still does not appear on the list of authorised neutral athletes, which was updated yesterday, Tuesday 3 November, by the International Ski and Snowboard Federation (FIS). The triple Olympic champion from Beijing 2022 is therefore, at this stage, still unable to return to the World Cup. However, this does not mean that his application has been officially rejected.

Since 2 September, the International Federation has set out the conditions under which Russian and Belarusian athletes may compete in the 2026–2027 season. They may return to competition on an individual basis, without a flag, national anthem or kit representing their country.

Each candidate must sign a declaration of neutrality and comply with anti-doping requirements. The FIS also provides for ongoing monitoring of compliance with these commitments, with the possibility of sanctions.

Applications are assessed on a case-by-case basis, following verification by an independent body. Decisions are then taken by an FIS eligibility committee.

The list, which was updated yesterday, includes Russian and Belarusian athletes competing in cross-country skiing, ski jumping and the Nordic combined. Some have therefore been granted permission to take part in international competitions, whilst others are still awaiting a decision.
