An emotional Charles Barkley addressed Ernie Johnson's plans to retire after the 2026-27 NBA season.

Charles Barkley could only hold back tears as he addressed Ernie Johnson's plan to retire by the end of the 2026-27 NBA season.

Barkley offered an emotional and touching message to the main host of Inside the NBA during Wednesday's segment.

"That was tough. We knew the day was coming. It's still tough," Barkley admitted. "Been here for 26, 27 years, and it's been an honor and a privilege to work with you all this time, and Kenny [Smith] and Shaq [O'Neal], and the amazing people behind the scenes. Nothing like it, to be able to dribble a stupid basketball and get rich and famous. And then come here and get paid to watch the best players in the world, the best players in the world, you get paid to do it. We'd watch anyway. 

"But it's been an amazing journey. You've been our leader. Our unquestionable leader. The question I'm asked the most, I'm not gonna lie. Most of the time, I'm like, where's Shaq and where's Kenny? Like, I keep them in my damn back pocket. But the question I'm probably asked the most is, is Ernie Johnson as good a person as he seems? And I say, man, he's better than that. So I just want to say thank you. Thank you for being our leader for all these years."

Ernie Johnson announced that the 2026-27 season will be his last as host of Inside the NBA.

The legendary broadcaster declared that the decision was entirely his and that "it's just time" to step away after more than three decades.

"This will be my last season. Totally my decision. It's just time," Johnson said.

Johnson joined Turner Sports in 1989 and has hosted Inside the NBA since 1990, spending more than three decades at the center of the award-winning show.

Throughout that run, Johnson has shared the desk with Kenny Smith, Charles Barkley, and Shaquille O'Neal.

It remains to be seen what the future of the show will be with Johnson walking away by the end of the season.
