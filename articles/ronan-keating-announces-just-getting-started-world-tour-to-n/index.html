Ronan Keating announces Just Getting Started World Tour to New Zealand 2027

TheTools · 17.09.2026 18:14 · source · permalink

rova

The Breeze presents

An iconic voice returns to Aotearoa with three decades of hits. Here is everything you need to know about Ronan Keating's 2027 New Zealand tour dates and tickets.

Tickets

Pop royalty is heading back to our shores as The Breeze presents Ronan Keating on his massive Just Getting Started World Tour in March 2027. Celebrating more than 30 years of chart-topping solo hits and Boyzone classics, this landmark arena tour marks a fresh new chapter for one of music's most beloved showmen.

  • Artist: Ronan Keating

  • Dates & Venues: Tuesday 16 March 2027 - Wolfbrook Arena, Christchurch

    Thursday 18 March 2027 - Spark Arena, Auckland

  • Tickets: Pre-sale begins Tuesday 22 September. General on-sale starts Thursday 24 September. Get tickets here

Artist: Ronan Keating

Dates & Venues: Tuesday 16 March 2027 - Wolfbrook Arena, Christchurch

Thursday 18 March 2027 - Spark Arena, Auckland

Tickets: Pre-sale begins Tuesday 22 September. General on-sale starts Thursday 24 September. Get tickets here

Kiwi audiences have shared a long love affair with Ronan Keating, from his early days breaking hearts with Boyzone to his massive solo career. With over 45 million records sold worldwide and a Guinness World Record for 30 consecutive UK Top 10 singles, his catalogue is packed with songs that defined a generation.

This tour follows an extraordinary period of momentum for the Irish star, including a record-breaking documentary and a massive 2026 Boyzone reunion at London's Emirates Stadium in front of 88,000 fans. The New Zealand shows will bring fresh energy and brand-new arrangements to global anthems like 'When You Say Nothing At All' and 'Life Is A Rollercoaster'.

Expect an uplifting evening full of warmth, nostalgia, and powerful singalongs as Ronan takes the stage at Christchurch's Wolfbrook Arena and Auckland's Spark Arena. It promises to be a feel-good celebration of music that brings entire families together.

Pre-sale tickets open on Tuesday 22 September before general public sales open on Thursday 24 September. Treat yourself and your loved ones to an unforgettable night of music.
