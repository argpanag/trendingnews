Sam Gallagher's goal against Sheffield United was his second of the season

Sam Gallagher scored a spectacular, if controversial, winner for Stoke City as they battled back to beat Sheffield United in game dominated by stunning goals.

Former England international Kalvin Phillips had put the Blades ahead with clever free-kick and first league goal for more than five years early in the second half.

Not to be outdone, Jack McGlynn slammed an outrageous equaliser past Blades goalkeeper Michael Cooper and into the top corner from distance with 12 minutes remaining.

Gallagher then came up with the best of the day's finishes, thumping his dipping winner home on the half volley after the flag stayed down when he appeared to have taken Ethan Galbraith's searching pass when in an obvious offside position.

Match Facts

  • Stoke have lost each of their last five league meetings with Sheffield United since a 3-1 win in October 2022 under Alex Neil.

  • Sheffield United have won away to Stoke in both of the last two Championship seasons, and could win three successive away league games against the Potters for just the second time, after April 1960.

  • Stoke have won three of their last four Championship games (D1), as many as their previous 23 beforehand (W3 D6 L14).

  • Sheffield United are unbeaten in five away league games (W3 D2), the longest ongoing run of all current Championship clubs.

  • Only Rio Cardines (5) has more Championship goal involvements this season among defenders than Stoke’s Eric Bocat (4 – 2 goals, 2 assists).
