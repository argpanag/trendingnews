Malik Nabers injury: Giants star WR returns after shoulder scare

TheTools · 22.09.2026 01:03 · source · permalink
  • New York Giants injuries

Nabers’ injury adds to Giants’ misery with Jaxson Dart also hurt.

Malik Nabers left the New York Giants game against the Los Angeles Rams in obvious pain late in the first quarter after an incomplete pass from backup quarterback Jameis Winston. He returned later in the second quarter.

The Giants, of course, lost starting quarterback Jaxson Dart to a knee injury on their first possession of the game.

Nabers got mixed up with Los Angeles cornerback Trent McDuffie on the final play of the first quarter, but what happened was not immediately clear.

Nabers caught six passes for 69 yards in his Week 1 return from last season’s torn ACL.

The Giants’ offense, of course, is largely built around Dart, the second-year quarterback, and Nabers, the third-year wide receiver. They played less than half a game together in 2025.
