Lille 2-1 Bayern (Nov 25, 2026) Game Analysis - ESPN

TheTools · 26.11.2026 04:49 · source · permalink

Bayern Munich's hopes of securing a top-eight slot in the Champions League are hanging by a thread after they surrendered an early lead to lose 2-1 at Lille.

The French side hit back from conceding a 15th-minute opener to Dayot Upamecano with two goals in four second-half minutes to land their first win of this season's competition.

Injury-hit Bayern were without the likes of Harry Kane, Michael Olise and Manuel Neuer but still grabbed the lead when Upamecano rose to nod home a Tom Bischof corner.

Ayase Ueda missed a chance to level and Ethan Mbappé had a shot saved by Jonas Urbig as Vincent Kompany's men managed to hold on to their hard-earned halftime lead.

Lille pulled level just after the hour mark when Ueda made the most of a second chance after being played into the left side of the box after a run from Nathan Ngoy.

Three minutes later the hosts turned the came on its head when keeper Berke Özer sparked a counter-attack which ended in Mbappé striking a post and Aleksandar Pavlovic clumsily turning the ball into his own net.

Bayern had clawed their way into the top eight with a win over Atlético Madrid three weeks ago and despite their injury list they were favored to win at the Stade Pierre-Mauroy against opponents coming in off the back of a Ligue 1 defeat to Lyon.

The visitors looked well in control with Pavlovic bringing a full-length save out of home keeper Özer with less than three minutes on the clock before Upamecano was left unmarked to nod home the opener.

But hopes of an easy night for the visitors were hit by a stirring response from the hosts, with Ueda played through one-on-one with Urbig but failing to steer the ball past the keeper.

Özer saved Konrad Laimer's effort from a narrow angle and then denied Pavlovic from long range as Bayern looked to extend their lead before halftime.

But it was Lille who almost reached the break on level terms when Ngal'ayel Mukau moved to the edge of the Bayern box and found Mbappé, whose weak shot was gathered by a grateful Urbig.

Bayern received a warning two minutes in the second half when Ueda intercepted Upamecano's careless back-header and poked home only to be denied for a foul on Bayern defender Joshua Kimmich as he tried to clear.

Lille came again and got their reward as Ueda finished well on 62 minutes, then capitalized on Pavlovic's error to turn the game on its head and move to the brink of a stirring win.

Kompany threw on Luis Díaz and Jamal Musiala in a vain attempt to turn the tide, but it was Lille who came closest to the grabbing the fourth goal of the game as they held out for a famous win.
