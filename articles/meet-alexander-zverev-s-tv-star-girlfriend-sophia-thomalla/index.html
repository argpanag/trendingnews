Meet Alexander Zverev’s TV star girlfriend Sophia Thomalla

TheTools · 02.09.2026 22:00 · source · permalink

Despite domestic abuse allegations against the reigning French Open champion by exes Olya Sharypova and Brenda Patea, Thomalla has remained steadfastly supportive

“I’m just happy I won,” the French Open title holder said in a post-match interview with the ATP Tour. “Incredible battle. A lot of ups and downs. Nearly five hours for a first round. I think this is the longest first-round match I’ve ever played in my life.”

Zverev, who reached the Wimbledon final in July, came close to winning his first US Open men’s singles title in 2020, but ultimately lost to Dominic Thiem. “I still feel like I should have won in 2020, that doesn’t change,” he said at a press conference last week. “I feel like I’m one of the 128 players that are favourites in the draw,” he added.

Through his ups and downs, the 29-year-old star athlete has had one familiar face in his player’s box: his girlfriend of over five years, Sophia Thomalla.

Here’s everything you need to know about her.

What is Sophia Thomalla’s background?

Sophia Thomalla was born in East Berlin on October 6, 1989, to actor parents Simone Thomalla and André Vetters, who separated in 1995. She spent her early childhood in Berlin before relocating to Cologne and Kleinmachnow with her mother. Known for her striking body art, Thomalla began getting tattooed at a young age – with one statement piece featuring the name and birth date of actor Sven Martinek, who dated her mother in the late 1990s, per German tabloid Klatsch-Tratsch.
