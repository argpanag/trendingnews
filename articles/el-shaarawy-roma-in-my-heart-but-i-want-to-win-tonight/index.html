El Shaarawy: “Roma in my heart, but I want to win tonight.”

Former Roma player and current Genoa attacker Stephan El Shaarawy spoke to Sky Sport before the match against Roma.

“Roma gave me so much, so many emotions, now I’ll take a few minutes to look at the stadium but on the pitch we’ll have to fight and get the 3 points which are very important for us.”

“Against Fiorentina we got a crucial victory as winning helps you win. Today won’t be easy given the opponent, who despite the absences remains very strong. We’ll have to fight to get points.”
