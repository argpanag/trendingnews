When is the Carabao Cup fourth round draw?

TheTools · 16.09.2026 19:58 · source · permalink

After beating Ipswich Town 4-2 at Portman Road, we advanced to the fourth round of the Carabao Cup, but who could we face

Here are all the details about the next round draw, and who we could face!

WHEN IS THE CARABAO CUP FOURTH ROUND DRAW

The fourth round draw will take place on Wednesday, September 16, following the conclusion of the match between Manchester United and Brighton & Hove Albion at Old Trafford.

WHAT BALL NUMBER ARE WE?

The ball numbers for the Carabao Cup fourth round draw will be announced on the morning of Wednesday, September 16.

HOW TO WATCH THE CARABAO CUP DRAW

United Kingdom viewers can watch the draw on Sky Sports Main Event. Look out for news of our opponents on Arsenal.com and The Arsenal app (download on iOS/Android).

WHO CAN WE PLAY?

Ten of the 16 teams have progressed to the next round of the Carabao Cup, with four fixtures to come on Wednesday before Manchester City's match against Norwich City coming on Thursday night.

It's an open draw, meaning we can face all of the teams below, home or away. In 2025/26, we faced Brighton at this stage of the competition, winning 2-0 at Emirates Stadium.

Here are the remaining sides we could face:

  • Bournemouth
  • Bradford City
  • Chelsea
  • Coventry City or Aston Villa
  • Crystal Palace
  • Everton or Wolverhampton Wanderers
  • Fleetwood Town or Sheffield United
  • Liverpool
  • Manchester City or Norwich City
  • Manchester United or Brighton & Hove Albion
  • Newcastle United
  • Peterborough United
  • Brentford
  • Sunderland
  • Fulham

WHEN WILL WE PLAY IN THE FOURTH ROUND?

Our fourth round fixture will take place the week commencing October 26. All other ties will also take place that week.
