Rebook Free on JetBlue and Delta as NYC Storm Cancels Flights

TheTools · 13.11.2026 17:24 · source · permalink

JetBlue waives fees and fare differences for Nov. 12 and 13 flights at JFK, LGA, EWR, ISP and HPN; rebook by Nov. 18. Delta also lets travelers change free.

JetBlue is waiving change and cancellation fees and fare differences for flights on Nov. 12 and 13, 2026, at New York JFK, LaGuardia, Newark, Long Island MacArthur in Islip and Westchester County as a nor’easter disrupts New York air travel, with free rebooking through Nov. 18, according to JetBlue’s travel alerts. Customers whose flights are cancelled can ask for a refund instead.

As of 7:30 a.m. ET Friday, JFK had 23 outbound and 54 inbound cancellations, LaGuardia 18 outbound and 25 inbound, and Newark 17 outbound and 15 inbound, Travel Market Report reported, citing FlightAware. For more on airlines and how they compare, see our airlines hub.

  • Airline: JetBlue, with Delta also offering flexibility.
  • Travel dates: Nov. 12 and 13, 2026.
  • Airports: JFK, LGA, EWR, ISP and HPN.
  • Rebook by: Nov. 18, 2026, with no fare difference.
  • Booked by: Before Nov. 12, 2026.

The JetBlue Weather Waiver

The waiver covers JetBlue-operated flights on Thursday and Friday at the five New York area airports, JetBlue said. Travelers must have booked before Nov. 12 and can rebook online under Manage Trips before their original departure time.

New travel must take place by Wednesday, Nov. 18. JetBlue will waive change and cancellation fees and any difference in fare for rebooked trips.

If JetBlue cancels your flight, you can request a refund to your original form of payment instead of rebooking, the airline said.

Groups of 10 or more must call 1-800-JetBlue (538-2583) to change their reservations.

Travel Market Report said JetBlue’s waiver also covers regional points connected to the New York airports.

Other Airlines and Delays

Delta’s Northeast Severe Weather Flexibility advisory covers JFK, LaGuardia, Newark, Boston Logan and Westchester County and lets travelers rebook without change fees, according to Travel Market Report.

United and American have weather advisories for their Northeast hubs and encourage travelers to change itineraries online before heading to the airport. The report did not describe fee waivers for either airline.

Across the U.S., 242 cancellations and 656 delays involved U.S. flights as of early Friday, the report said. The FAA issued several ground stops at New York area airports on Thursday because of wind and rain, and similar stops could happen again on Friday.

Regional flights at LaGuardia faced holds, according to the report.

New York’s three major airports are among the most delay-prone in the country when wind shifts the runways in use, because their airspace overlaps and capacity drops quickly. Disruptions there often ripple into flights across the East Coast and beyond, even for travelers who are not flying to New York.

What to Do if You Fly Today

Check your flight status in the airline’s app before you leave for the airport. Many cancellations are announced hours ahead.

If you can move your trip, rebook now while seats are available. Waivers let you shift dates without paying more, but later flights fill quickly after a storm.

If your flight is cancelled and you choose not to travel, request a refund rather than accepting a credit, since U.S. rules entitle you to a refund when an airline cancels.

Allow extra time to reach the airport. Wind and rain can slow roads, and lines can grow as rebooked passengers arrive.

If you are connecting through New York on another airline, check that carrier’s own advisory, since waivers differ by airline and by airport.

Keep receipts for meals or hotels in case your airline offers reimbursement for delays it controls. Weather delays usually are not covered, but it is worth asking.
