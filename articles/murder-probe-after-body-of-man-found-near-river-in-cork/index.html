Murder probe after body of man found near river in Cork

TheTools · 16.10.2026 07:04 · source · permalink

Gardaí are treating as murder the death of a man whose body was found on a walkway near the River Blackwater, two kilometres from Mallow in Co Cork on Thursday.

It follows the completion of a post-mortem examination by the Assistant State Pathologist, Dr Margot Bolster.

Gardaí say they are not releasing the post-mortem examination results for operational reasons, but it is understood they indicate the man died violently.

He has been named locally as Paul O'Connor from Ballyphehane in Cork. He was 56 years old.

It is believed that Mr O'Connor travelled from Kent Train Station in Cork city to Mallow Train Station on Wednesday, arriving there after 7.20pm.

Gardaí say that at the time, he was wearing a dark green hooded jacket, khaki coloured combat trousers and black runners.

Throughout yesterday, detectives conducted house-to-house enquiries and gathered CCTV footage from Mallow and the surrounding area.

A divisional search team also conducted searches at Ballyellis, around the area where Mr O'Connor's body was found.

Gardaí are appealing for anyone with information to contact them at Mallow Garda Station.

They are looking to speak to anyone who was on the train that Mr O'Connor was travelling on on Wednesday evening.

They are also looking to speak to anyone who was in the vicinity of Mallow town between 7.20pm on Wednesday and 1.30pm on Thursday and may have seen Mr O'Connor.

Anyone with information is asked to contact Mallow Garda Station on 022-31450, the Garda Confidential Line on 1800 666 111 or any garda station.
