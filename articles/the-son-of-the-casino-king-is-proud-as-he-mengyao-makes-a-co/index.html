The son of the 'casino king' is proud as He Mengyao makes a comeback to Victoria's Secret.

TheTools · 21.10.2026 09:19 · source · permalink

He Mengyao spends time training to improve her physique, aiming to showcase the alluring charm of a mature woman on the Victoria's Secret runway.

He Mengyao strode confidently in the opening segment of the Victoria's Secret show in a sweet pink outfit - Photo: AFP

The Victoria's Secret 2026 lingerie fashion show officially took place on the morning of October 19th, Vietnam time, attracting the attention of fashion enthusiasts.

Notably, supermodel He Mengyao, wife of He Youjun – the son of Macau's "casino king" Stanley Ho and businesswoman Liang Anqi – returned to the runway after 8 years, surprising and exciting fans.

He Mengyao returns to Victoria's Secret after her downfall 9 years ago.

Ha Du Quan posted photos of his wife performing on Instagram, along with a sweet message: "You are the pride of the whole family! Our little angel has returned with her giant wings."

The post received many positive comments from netizens, with compliments such as "beautiful like a fairy descended from heaven," "Oh my god, so beautiful," and "Truly breathtaking."

He Mengyao "spreads her wings" on the Victoria's Secret runway - Photo: IGNV

Meng Dao expressed her happiness at returning to the lingerie runway after a long period focusing on her role as a mother. Before the show, she actively trained and lost weight. The beauty hopes her daughter will watch the video and say, "Mommy is so beautiful."

This Asian supermodel had appeared seven times at this prestigious lingerie show before taking an eight-year hiatus.

In her comeback, He Mengyao first appeared in a design inspired by flowers. In her second performance, she donned giant wings, showcasing a powerful and captivating demeanor that left the audience in awe.

Ha Du Quan posted a picture of his wife on his personal Instagram account - Photo: IGNV

He Mengyao transforms into a brown butterfly on the Victoria's Secret catwalk - Photo: AFP

In 2017, He Mengyao had an unfortunate incident where she tripped and fell on the Victoria's Secret runway. At the time, she wrote on Weibo: "I'm sorry for disappointing everyone, thank you for your concern. On my journey to becoming a model, I've fallen countless times."

But I understand that no matter how painful the fall is, I still have to get up and keep going. The road ahead is still very long, and I will continue to move forward."

Therefore, He Mengyao's comeback this time has received even more attention. Some netizens praised her as a prime example of the spirit of getting back up after a fall: "Where you fall, that's where you get up."

He Mengyao is one of China's successful models on the international market, having walked the runway for many luxury brands at fashion weeks. In 2019, she married businessman He Youjun and gradually reduced her activities in the fashion industry.

Ho Yu-Kwan is the son of Stanley Ho (1921-2020), known as the "king of casinos" in Macau, and his fourth wife, Angela Leong. According to Forbes in 2025, Angela Leong had a net worth of $2.6 billion.

The romance between He Youjun and supermodel Xi Mengyao has attracted much attention from the audience - Photo: Weibo

Last summer, He Mengyao and He Youjun held their wedding at an ancient castle in France, followed by a thank-you reception in Macau (China).

At a party at the end of September, the supermodel, born in 1989, was presented with 22 gold bracelets engraved with dragon and phoenix motifs, along with some jewelry belonging to her late father, by her mother-in-law.

Ms. Luong An Ky also publicly expressed her gratitude for her son finding a kind and intelligent life partner, and thanked her daughter-in-law for integrating into the family and giving birth to two healthy and adorable grandchildren.
