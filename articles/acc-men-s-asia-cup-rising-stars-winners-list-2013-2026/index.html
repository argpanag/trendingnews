ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars Winners List (2013–2026): Pakistan is the most successful team in the history of the Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars, formerly known as the ACC Emerging Teams Asia Cup. 

Pakistan has won three titles (2019, 2023 and 2025), followed by Sri Lanka with two titles. India won the inaugural edition in 2013, while Afghanistan claimed its maiden title in 2024.

The Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars is a cricket tournament organised under the Asian Cricket Council (ACC). It provides young and emerging cricketers from Asian countries with an opportunity to compete at the continental level.

The competition started in 2013 as the ACC Emerging Teams Asia Cup. It has featured teams such as India A, Pakistan A, Sri Lanka A, Bangladesh A and Afghanistan A.

Who Won the Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars 2025? 

Pakistan Shaheens won the Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars 2025 after defeating Bangladesh A in the final through a Super Over on November 23, 2025, at the West End Park International Cricket Stadium in Doha, Qatar.

Both teams scored 125 runs in their respective 20 overs. Pakistan then won the Super Over to secure its third title in the tournament.
