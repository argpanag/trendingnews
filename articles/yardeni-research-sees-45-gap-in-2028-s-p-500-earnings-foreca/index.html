Yardeni Research sees excessive optimism in S&P 500 earnings forecasts, with estimates yet to reflect the financial, political, and physical constraints it expects to slow the artificial intelligence (AI) investment boom.

In its Nov. 9 note, the firm highlighted a $45 difference in 2028 operating earnings per share (EPS) forecasts, with consensus estimates at $495 against its own projection of $450. For 2027, consensus stands at $426 versus the firm's $425 forecast.

The firm said analysts do not appear to anticipate a complete halt to AI infrastructure development, as consensus earnings forecasts continue rising."We suspect there is some irrational exuberance in earnings estimates, as they don't even reflect the speed bumps," the note said.

Yardeni Research noted that semiconductors account for approximately 20% of S&P 500 forward earnings and does not expect EPS to reach $500 until 2030.

Why AI Investment Could Slow

Yardeni Research identified three intensifying constraints on the AI investment boom: financial, political, and physical. The firm expects financing to become more expensive as investors demand clearer returns on hyperscalers' commitments.

Political opposition could require data center developers to fund grid upgrades, protect local resources and deliver greater community benefits. Power shortages, electricity-grid connection delays and equipment bottlenecks could also delay projects and extend the wait for positive free cash flow.

However, the firm expects the boom to continue as demand for computing power rises. It cited Anthropic's disclosure that customers generating at least $100,000 in annual revenue increased from more than 950 to over 11,900 within a year.

The strategists also expect increasingly autonomous AI agents to perform complex tasks and unleash another wave of computing demand while delivering productivity gains.

Meanwhile, Yardeni Research said stock investors appear to be discounting the constraints, with the S&P 500's forward price-to-earnings ratio falling to 17.6, below its Liberation Day level of 18.1.
