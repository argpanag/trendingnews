Brentford ‘Very Interested’ in Lazio Winger Gustav Isaksen

TheTools · 16.11.2026 09:33 · source · permalink

Brentford are reportedly tracking Lazio winger Gustav Isaksen, who has been one of Gennaro Gattuso’s best performers this season.

Gustav Isaksen Has Cemented Himself as a Lazio Pillar

The Danish international has endured several highs and lows since arriving in Rome in the summer of 2023, but he has seemingly taken his game up a notch this season. After making his return from a groin injury, he has been consistently delivering influential displays, producing four goals and an assist in Lazio’s last six games.

Brentford Following Isaksen

According to TuttoMercatoWeb, Isaksen’s exploits earned him attention from the Premier League, particularly from Brentford, who are ‘very interested’ in his services. The Bees replaced Bryan Mbeumo with Dango Outattara in the summer of 2025, but they might consider the Denmark international an upgrade on the former Bournemouth man, who has only scored once this season.

Interestingly, Brentford were heavily linked with Lazio’s other right winger, Matteo Cancellieri, last January, but the move didn’t materialise.

Isaksen Ready to Extend Lazio Contract

Amid growing interest in his services, Lazio are keen to lock down Isaksen with a long-term contract, as his current deal is set to expire in June 2028. The two parties have been negotiating a new and improved contract. Moreover, the 25-year-old has recently expressed his desire to extend his stay in Rome, ruling out an exit.
