Okon: It's A True Reflection Of South African Football

TheTools · 04.10.2026 08:58 · source · permalink

Bafana

Ime Okon has been a top performer for Bafana Bafana recently. Image: Gallo Images

Breaking into the top 50 on the FIFA rankings has given Bafana Bafana players motivation to keep moving up, and defender Ime Okon believes the national teams, as well as the juniors sides, are heading in the right direction.

Bafana Bafana are currently ranked 49th on the FIFA rankings and 10th in Africa, following their victories over Guinea and Eritrea. It's the first time the team is in the top 50 since October 2011.

Excited by the progress the national team has made, Okon is determined to see Bafana Bafana consistantly rising on the rankings.

"It's really motivating. You see the teams in front of you and believe that you can actually get more places (up on the rankings). It's motivating and we just look forward to getting better spots," the former SuperSport United defender said.

The last few weeks have been exciting for South African football, with the men's national teams getting positive results. Bafana Bafana started their 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers with victories over Guinea and Eritrea, while the national under-23 team won the Cosafa U/20 Youth Championship for a third consecutive year. The under-23s beat Algeria 3-1 in an international friendly on Thursday. There's good reason to believe that South African football will keep on getting better on the international front.

"Yes, I think we are very much headed in the right direction. The academies have been doing well over the past few years, bringing players into the set-up. It's a true reflection of South African football at the moment. We can only go up from here."

Bafana Bafana wrap up this international period with a friendly international against Egypt in Cairo this evening, in a game head coach Pitso Mosimane is expected to make wholesale changes to give players an opportunity to impress.

The team has been in camp for over two weeks, giving them an opportunity to get to know one another even more.

"We have adjusted well. We know each other quite well. It's been long, but it hasn't really been long for us. We get along well. We are winning, so it's a good feeling in the camp. We are just enjoying the time here," Okon said.

"As you know, Egypt is one of the biggest teams in Africa. As a player, you always want to play the best teams. You always want to challenge yourself. I think the team is excited to play against them and test ourselves."
