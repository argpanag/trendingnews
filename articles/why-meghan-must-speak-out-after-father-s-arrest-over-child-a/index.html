Camilla Tominey and Hannah Furness discuss the Sussexes’ first public appearance since the arrest of Meghan’s father, Thomas Markle Snr

On Sunday’s episode of The Royal T, Camilla Tominey and Hannah Furness discuss the ongoing investigation into the Duchess of Sussex’s estranged father, Thomas Markle Snr, after his arrest by the FBI on suspicion of possessing child sex abuse images.
