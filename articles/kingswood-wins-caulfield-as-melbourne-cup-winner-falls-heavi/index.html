Kingswood wins Caulfield as Melbourne Cup winner falls heavily

TheTools · 17.10.2026 06:47 · source · permalink
  • Breaking
  • Sport
  • Horse Racing
  • Horse racing

October 17, 2026 - 6.47am

A roughie has won the Caulfield Cup, with Kingswood leading home fellow grey Saint George in front of a packed crowd.

But the win has been overshadowed by a nasty fall at the top of the straight involving 2025 Caulfield and Melbourne Cup winner Half Yours. The other horse involved, Ohope Wins, was later euthanised.

John Allen on Kingswood has won the Caulfield Cup. Getty Images

Ridden by Mark Zahra, Half Yours was first to go down after contact with Kings Valley. Zahra himself also fell heavily onto the turf.

Ohope Wins was at the back of the field, and with little chance of avoiding a collision, clipped hooves, and fell heavily.

Half Yours was chased down by course clerk and then led back to his stall. He is reportedly uninjured.

In a statement, Racing Victoria vets said the Chris Waller-trained Ohope Wins was put down on the track.

Both Zahra and Ohope Wins jockey Jye McNeil have been taken to hospital for observation, but are believed to have escaped major injury.

Stewards will investigate the cause of the fall, which reportedly also involved the Rachael King-ridden Green Spaces.

The crash occurred in front of a packed crowd of 30,051 – the biggest Caulfield Cup crowd in a decade.

On Kingswood, jockey John Allen was on the outside and mounted his charge as the field hit the straight.

He was still two lengths back with 300m to go as the riders fell behind him, but took the lead 100m from home.

Kingswood paid $24.10 for the win and $8.30 for the place. Enfjaar finished third ahead of Cosmic Crusader.

Jye McNeil riding Ohope Wins during trackwork on Tuesday morning. Getty Images

Trainer Gavin Bedggood shed tears of joy, and paid tribute to his stepdaughter and partner, who are also part of his team.

“We haven’t had to do anything with him all week. He’s just been about managing him … thanks to the owners for backing me,” Bedggood said on Seven.
