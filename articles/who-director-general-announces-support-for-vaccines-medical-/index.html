WHO Director-General announces support for vaccines, medical equipment during official visit to Cuba

TheTools · 25.11.2026 22:15 · source · permalink

Today, World Health Organization (WHO) Director-General Dr Tedros Adhanom Ghebreyesus announced US$2 million to help strengthen Cuba’s national immunization activities and provided essential medical equipment to health facilities.

Dr Tedros arrived in Cuba on 22 November at the invitation of Cuba’s Minister of Public Health, Dr José Ángel Portal Miranda. During his visit, Dr Tedros toured health facilities and met with President Miguel Díaz-Canel Bermúdez. He was joined by senior officials from WHO and the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO).

WHO’s new funding will support Cuban health sector priorities, with a focus on the National Immunization Program, especially vaccination for children and adults. Cuba has maintained very high vaccination coverage, of above 98%, for many years. In 2025, coverage decreased to 95%. WHO’s contribution will support immunization for approximately 62 000 newborns, more than 780 000 girls and boys, and over 110 000 people in other risk groups, as well as support rabies prevention and control actions.

“Cuba’s hard-won public health achievements must be protected,” said Dr Tedros. “WHO/PAHO’s provision of essential vaccines is the latest demonstration of our commitment to support Cuba through significant health challenges and help maintain access to essential health services. Together with our partners, we will continue to stand with the Cuban people to protect health, save lives and sustain progress.”

During his visit, Dr Tedros reaffirmed WHO/PAHO’s continuing support for the country's health services, and ways to protect progress in public health. Strengthening health cooperation was a central topic of Dr Tedros’s visit to Cuba. Between 2025 and 2026, WHO and PAHO have mobilized more than US$14 million through 14 projects to support the quality and sustainability of health services and strengthen health workforce capacities.

During his mission, Dr Tedros visited the "William Soler" University Pediatric Hospital and the pediatric cardiocenter of the same name, meeting health workers and families of children receiving care there. The delegation delivered supplies and medical equipment mobilized with the support of the PAHO/WHO Emergency team, including basic medical care kits and equipment to strengthen the institution's response capacity: electronic monitors for blood pressure measurement; ophthalmoscopes/otoscopes, pulse oximeters, sphygmomanometers, stethoscopes, thermometers, among other resources.

Dr Tedros also held discussions with the United Nations Country Team and other cooperation partners in Cuba on sustaining financial and strategic support for the country’s public health sector. These meetings identified new opportunities to expand collaboration and coordination according to the country's main health needs.

The WHO Director-General was also updated by executives from Cuba’s main biotechnology companies on advances and challenges to the country’s pharmaceutical industry, which are developing new products in the areas of infectious disease prevention and control, Alzheimer's, cancer, and diabetes.

This was Dr Tedros’s second official visit to Cuba as WHO Director-General. His previous visit took place in 2018. On this trip, Dr Tedros was accompanied by Raúl Thomas, WHO’s Assistant Director-General of Business Operations, and Dr Ciro Ugarte, Director of the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO) Health Emergencies Department. Dr Tedros will visit other countries in the region this week.

 

About WHO 

Dedicated to the well-being of all people and guided by science, WHO leads and champions global efforts to give everyone, everywhere, an equal chance at a safe and healthy life. We are the United Nations’ agency for health that connects nations, partners and people in more than 150 locations – leading the world’s response to health emergencies, preventing disease, addressing the root causes of health issues and expanding access to medicines and health care. Our mission is to support all countries to promote, provide and protect health. “Together for health. Stand with science”, the theme of World Health Day 2026, marks a year-long campaign to highlight science as the foundation for protecting health and well-being worldwide.
