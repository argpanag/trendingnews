Bayern-Köln preview: Bayern lead the table with five injuries

TheTools · 19.11.2026 21:39 · source · permalink

Bayern host 1. FC Köln at the Allianz Arena on Friday 20 November in Bundesliga matchday 10, top with 21 points and five injury absences.

By TuFutbolero Newsroom·November 19, 2026 at 04:39 PM

Bayern München host 1. FC Köln at the Allianz Arena for Bundesliga matchday 10, on Friday 20 November. The referee will be Robin Braun.

The hosts are first with 21 points from nine matches and in their last five games they have three wins and two draws. Köln are 15th with 9 points from nine matches and in their last five they have one win, two draws and two defeats.

Both sides arrive with five injury absences. Bayern will not have A. Davies (hamstring), L. Díaz and S. Gnabry (muscle), M. Neuer (foot) and I. Saibari (ankle). Köln lose T. Dallinga and T. Hubers (knee), I. Rondic and R. van den Berg (muscle) and E. Skhiri (ankle).
