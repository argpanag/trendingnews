Alexa Minton’s Forecast | Fall feel finally arrives for the forecast

TheTools · 22.09.2026 10:16 · source · permalink

LEXINGTON, Ky. (WKYT) - Kentucky is trading our recent summerlike warmth for a noticeably cooler stretch of weather this week.

Split forecast for our Tuesday

The front that knocked down our temperatures yesterday has stalled over parts of the state, leading to lingering rain chances and a temperature gradient across our skies. A region of low pressure turning slowly over the southern Appalachians will keep the sky partly to mostly cloudy at times today, with a few showers possible -mainly across eastern Kentucky.

Northeast winds will add a little extra chill to the air. Afternoon temperatures will top out in the upper 60s to middle 70s for central Kentucky, while areas on the other side of the front will warm all the way into the upper 70s or low 80s.

More Scattered Storms Wednesday

Scattered showers and a few thunderstorms may spread into the state, following the track of the stalled front for our day tomorrow.

Any storm could produce a quick downpour. A widespread flooding concern is not expected, but isolated flash flooding cannot be ruled out in places that receive repeated rounds of rain. Showers may linger into early Thursday before activity fades.

Dry, Fall Weather Returns

Cooler and drier air will take hold Thursday and remain in place into early next week. Rainfall over the next seven days is expected to run below normal overall, and many communities will see several dry days after Thursday.

Temperatures will remain comfortable, with highs generally in the upper 60s to middle 70s Thursday and Friday. Weekend highs should stay mainly in the 70s.
