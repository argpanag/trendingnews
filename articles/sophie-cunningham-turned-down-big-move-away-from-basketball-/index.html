Sophie Cunningham Turned Down Big Move Away From Basketball - AOL

TheTools · 25.10.2026 23:09 · source · permalink

WNBA star Sophie Cunningham explained why she hasn’t been able to join “Dancing with the Stars” even though she wants to.

Cunningham spoke openly about the highly rated American reality dance competition during a recent episode of her “No Hesitation” podcast with trainer Drew Hanlen.

According to the 30-year-old guard, she believes she can thrive in the competition because of her passion for dancing.

“Dude, I think I’d kill it, I say that,” Cunningham started at the 1:01 mark. “But it’s really hard. I’m good with my TikTok dances, but this is like legit dancing. But I grew up dancing. I loved it.”

However, the second-round pick in the 2019 WNBA Draft revealed that she had turned down the opportunity to join DWTS due to scheduling conflicts with the WNBA season.

“They’ve been trying to get me on for the last couple of years,” Cunningham said. “But because I think they start filming in September, and it’s just not doable. So it kind of sucks.”

The start date for “Dancing with the Stars” overlaps with the latter part of the WNBA regular season and the start of the playoffs, making it hard for Cunningham to fit it into her schedule.

Cunningham was a crucial contributor off the bench for the Fever this past season. The former Missouri star averaged 7.9 points, 2.3 rebounds and 1.2 assists in 43 regular-season games, helping the Fever finish sixth in the league with a 28-16 record.

However, Indiana lost to the Las Vegas Aces in the first round of the playoffs in three games.

Cunningham is entering unrestricted free agency this offseason. It remains to be seen if she will continue to suit up for the Fever in 2027.
