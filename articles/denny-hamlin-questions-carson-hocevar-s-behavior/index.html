Carson Hocevar has carved out a distinct place for himself in NASCAR, both on and off the racetrack. He regularly streams, interacts with fans, and embraces a playful persona that keeps his audience engaged. Along with his performances on track, this approach has helped him build a following. However, not everyone in the garage shares the same view of his style.

Denny Hamlin recently weighed in on Hocevar’s approach to racing and his public image, comparing him to a social media influencer. He also questioned how seriously Hocevar approaches his racing career and suggested that this could affect his long-term prospects in the sport.

The season finale at Homestead-Miami Speedway has become a points calculation for Kyle Larson, Denny Hamlin, and Christopher Bell. Each driver has a different path to the championship, with Larson holding the advantage heading into the final race.

Larson’s path to the title:

The Hendrick Motorsports driver needs just nine points to clinch his third Cup Series championship. He can also secure the title by finishing 28th or better without scoring any stage points, or by earning a total of eight stage points.

Bell’s path to the title:

Bell needs a maximum-points day, along with Larson finishing 29th or worse without scoring any stage points.

Hamlin’s path to the title:

The veteran Joe Gibbs Racing driver also needs a maximum-points day. However, he needs Larson to finish 32nd or worse without earning any stage points.
