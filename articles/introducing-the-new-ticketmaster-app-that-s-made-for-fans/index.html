Introducing The New Ticketmaster App That’s Made for Fans

TheTools · 02.09.2026 16:19 · source · permalink

 

Nothing feels better than being there in person to see the artists, teams and performers you love. And now, we’ve made it even easier to discover, explore, and buy tickets to the concerts, shows, and games you don’t want to miss with the newly-refreshed Ticketmaster App. The new app has been updated specifically with fans in mind, and we’d love to know what you think! Drop us a line and let us know by tagging us on Instagram or X.

When you sign in to the Ticketmaster App, you’ll see an entirely new, more personalized experience. We’ve made it easier for you to find, follow, and act on the events that matter most to you. (Download the Ticketmaster App for iPhone or Android.)

Here’s what you’ll see with the new Ticketmaster App.

A Home Screen That Feels Like Home

Your new home screen will show you events you really want to see—it’s tailored to you and your interests. From the minute you sign in to the app, you’ll discover events relevant to you based on your interests and location.

Here’s what you’ll discover:

  • Trending events
  • Just-announced shows
  • Curated picks across music, sports, theater, comedy, arts, and family events
  • Last-minute tickets to events happening soon

And when you’re traveling, you can easily switch your location to explore events wherever you are.

Get Alerts For Your Favorites

You’ve been able to add to Favorites before, but now, when you add your top artists, teams and venues, your home screen will reflect those preferences. The more you engage with Favorites, the more you’ll get out of it—truly making the app your own.

To see all of your Favorites, go to the brand new Watchlist tab.

Keep Track of Events With Watchlist

With our brand new Watchlist feature, your favorite upcoming events are now saved in one place. Go to your favorite artist or team, then navigate to an individual event and hit the ribbon icon in the upper right corner to add it to your Watchlist:

In the Watchlist tab, you’ll see all of the events you’ve saved, along with your Favorites:

You can view events by date, and get timely updates on ticket sales, including presales. We’ll even send you reminders and other important info from the moment the event is announced all the way through the big day. You can also easily add individual events from the artists, venues and teams you’ve already favorited.

With the new Ticketmaster App, we’re helping fans like you discover more, get a more personalized experience that really feels like you, and make sure you never miss the events you love.

Download the Ticketmaster App for iPhone or Android.
