Week 6 Injury Report: Seahawks at Broncos

TheTools · 16.10.2026 01:10 · source · permalink

Details on the Seahawks’ injury situation heading into their Week 6 game at Denver.

Wednesday's Injury Report

The Seahawks left for Denver on Wednesday ahead of their Thursday night game against the Broncos, and held a light practice prior to heading to the airport.

Among those practicing fully on Wednesday was receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba, who was listed as limited the previous two days because of an ankle injury. Smith-Njigba, who leads the NFL in receiving yards and receiving touchdowns, does not have a game status on the final injury report, meaning he is good to go.

Also without a status, despite being listed as limited all week with an ankle injury, is defensive lineman Leonard Williams, who played last week through the injury he suffered in a Week 4 win over the Chargers.

The Seahawks will be without starting linebacker Drake Thomas, who injured his ankle in Sunday's loss to the 49ers. Tyrice Knight, who has started 14 games in the past three seasons, will take over for Thomas at the inside linebacker spot opposite Ernest Jones IV.

Also ruled out are linebacker Chazz Surratt, who will miss a third straight game with a calf injury, and running back Zach Charbonnet, who remains on the physically unable to perform list as he works his way back from the torn ACL he suffered in last season's Divisional Round win over the 49ers.

For the Broncos, All-Pro cornerback Patrick Surtain II has been ruled out with an ankle injury, as has starting safety Talanoa Hufanga (ankle). Starting cornerback Riley Moss, who missed last weekend's game with a rib injury, is questionable after being listed as limited in practice all week.

Tuesday's Injury Report

Tuesday's injury report, which like Monday's is an estimation of participation, was mostly unchanged from a day earlier. Guard Beau Stephens (shoulder) went from being listed as limited Monday to full participation Tuesday, while every other player's status stayed the same.

That means starting linebacker Drake Thomas was listed as a non-participant for the second day in a row due to an ankle injury, as was linebacker Chazz Surratt, who has missed two games with a calf injury.

Jaxon Smith-Njigba, the NFL's leading receiver, was listed as limited for the second straight day due to an ankle injury, as was defensive tackle Leonard Williams. Receiver Irv Charles, who was activated from injured reserve earlier in the day, was again listed as a full participant, another indicator that he is on track to make his Seahawks debut on Thursday.

For the Broncos, two starters in the secondary, cornerback Patrick Surtain II (ankle) and safety Talanoa Hufanga (ankle) were listed as non-participants for the second straight day. The only change for the Broncos from Monday to Tuesday was defensive end Sai'vion Jones improving from limited to full participation with a shoulder injury.

Monday's Injury Report

A Thursday night game in Denver means the Seahawks and Broncos were both required to put out their first injury report of the week on Monday even though neither team actually practiced.

The Seahawks' estimated practice report had two players listed as not practicing, linebacker Chazz Surratt, who has missed the past two games with a calf injury, and linebacker Drake Thomas, who injured his ankle in Sunday's loss to the 49ers, but was able to return to the game.

New to the injury report this week was receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba, who was listed as limited with an ankle injury, but was able to finish Sunday's game. Also new on the report is backup quarterback Drew Lock, who was listed as limited with a right forearm injury. Defensive tackle Leonard Williams, who injured his ankle in Seattle's Week 4 win over the Chargers, but was able to play on Sunday, was also listed as limited, as were guard Beau Stephens (shoulder) and running back Zach Charbonnet (knee).

Charbonnet, who is still on the physically unable to perform list, returned to practice ahead of Seattle's Week 4 game against the Chargers, but is unlikely to return to game action this week, Seahawks coach Mike Macdonald said.

"As of right now, the answer is no, subject to change," Macdonald said Monday. "Trying not to be boxed in there, some wiggle room."

Asked about Charbonnet's timing to return, Macdonald said, "We have to have consensus from all parties that he's ready to go, he's the most confident and ready to go, that he's had enough exposure to go play an NFL game. Those are the variables at play here. Can one area of that equation, can that adjust as we go? Yeah. But we're trying to do right by the process right now."

For the Broncos, two starters in their secondary were listed as non-participants, cornerback Patrick Surtain II, who missed last weekend's game with an ankle injury, and safety Talanoa Hufanga, who exited Sunday's win over the Chargers early with an ankle injury.
