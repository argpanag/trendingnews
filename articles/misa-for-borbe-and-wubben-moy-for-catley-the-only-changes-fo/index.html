Misa for Borbe and Wubben-Moy for Catley the only changes for Arsenal against Paris

TheTools · 30.09.2026 15:36 · source · permalink

Arsenal make two changes for tonight’s from Sunday’s 1-0 defeat at Chelsea for tonight’s UWCL game against Paris FC with Misa coming back in goal ahead of Anneke Borbe and Lotte Wubben-Moy coming in for the injured Steph Catley.

That means Misa will start ahead of a back four of Fox, Williamson, Wubben-Moy and Batlle. The midfield three will be Little, Stanway and Cooney-Cross. Baum and Mariona start either side of Alessia Russo upfront.

16.Misa; 2.Fox, 6.Williamson, 3.Wubben-Moy, 22.O.Batlle; 4.Stanway, 32.Cooney-Cross, 10.Little(c); 19.Baum, 8.Mariona; 23.Russo

Subs: 9.Foord, 11.Smith, 12.L.Maanum, 14.Reuteler, 24.Hinds, 25.Blackstenius, 28.Borbe, 29.Cerci, 31.Holmberg
