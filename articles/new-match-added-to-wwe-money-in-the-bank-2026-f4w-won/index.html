New match added to WWE Money in the Bank 2026 - F4W/WON

TheTools · 29.09.2026 01:08 · source · permalink
  • By Bryan Rose
  • Published: 28 Sep 2026, 18:08

A new match is set for Money in the Bank next month.

Bronson Reed will face Oba Femi in a battle of giants on October 10 in New Orleans, Louisiana. The match was made after Reed agreed to sign a contract Femi introduced during Raw on Monday.

Femi arrived to the ring and talked about how he’s been dealing with the Vision for months, but once Bronson Reed returned at Sunday Night’s Main Event, the temperature changed. Femi, however, declared that he was the captain now. He introduced the contract and asked for Reed to sign it. Reed came out and did just that before quickly making his exit.

The segment ended with Femi admitting Reed had “some balls,” for agreeing to the match, but his fate was sealed for Money in the Bank.

  • Women’s Money in the Bank ladder match: Sol Ruca vs. Lola Vice vs. Jacy Jayne vs. Roxanne Perez vs. Lash Legend vs. TBD
