Cramps Drummer Harry Drumdini Dies at 60 After Cancer Battle

TheTools · 25.11.2026 18:04 · source · permalink

The music world is mourning the loss of Harry Drumdini, the longtime drummer for American rock band The Cramps, who died at 60. The band announced his death on Facebook, prompting tributes from friends and fellow musicians who remembered his contributions to rock music.

The Cramps confirmed the news in a social media post dated November 22, 2026. The announcement expressed condolences to his loved ones and acknowledged the loss felt by his fans.

"R.I.P. Harry Drumdini (11/22/26). Condolences to his family, friends and fans.

Harry will be greatly missed!" The Cramps wrote in their Facebook announcement.

Drumdini, whose birth name was Harry Misenheimer, had previously disclosed a serious health condition. In an August 2024 update to his GoFundMe campaign, he revealed that he was battling terminal colon and prostate cancer that had spread to his lung.

His death prompted messages of remembrance from people who had known him personally and appreciated his musical career.

Josh Stamps shared his sadness over the loss, describing Drumdini as an inspiring person and a remarkable individual.

"This year has really taken the best away from us. Such a inspiration and such a awesome dude." Josh Stamps wrote on Facebook.

Another tribute came from Amazing Royal Crowns, who remembered Drumdini as both a talented musician and a close friend. The message highlighted their shared experiences, laughter and memories.

"RIP Harry Drumdini. Thank you for teaching us what cool meant, hanging with us, laughing, and making memories. You were the best drummer, and a good friend.

Please give our love to Lux if you see him. Sleep now Harry. You deserve it."

Amazing Royal Crowns wrote on Facebook.

Drumdini had two periods with The Cramps, first joining the band in 1994. He continued performing with the group through its final years, remaining associated with the band until 2019.

Beyond his work with The Cramps, he also performed with False Confession and Stalag 13. His extended association with The Cramps made him a familiar figure among the band's international fan community.

Giorgos Chloros also paid tribute, highlighting Drumdini's more than 13 years of involvement with The Cramps and the lasting influence of his musical contributions.

"Saddened to hear the news; The Cramps’ longtime drummer for 13+ years, Harry Drumdini passed away this morning. His legacy will live on forever, through his music and in the hearts of millions of Cramps fiends the world over." Giorgos Chloros wrote on Facebook.

Charlie Overbey remembered Drumdini for his friendship and the positive experiences they shared over the years.

"So sad to hear we lost Harry today. Such a good guy. Friend to all.

Thanks for the good times Harry. Harry Drumdini — See you on the other side Brother." Charlie Overbey wrote on Facebook.
