WWE ‘devastated’ as Australian star dies at 38

TheTools · 28.11.2026 00:42 · source · permalink
  • Breaking
  • Sport
  • Combat Sports
  • Wrestling

Updated November 28, 2026 - 1.00am. First published at 12.42am

WWE has announced that Australian wrestler Jermaine Haley, who competes under the name Bronson Reed, died on Saturday AEDT, age 38.

The company said Haley died due to medical complications. He is survived by his pregnant wife, Paige, and their daughter.

Jermaine Haley performing in WWE. @bronsonishere

A top performer on WWE’s Raw brand and a member of The Vision stable, Haley most recently competed on WWE’s most recent premium live event in Saudi Arabia “Crown Jewel”.

It’s believed that Haley suffered multiple broken ribs and a lacerated kidney following his performance in the ring at the Crown Jewel event.

Reed didn’t wrestle in the ring, but did brawl with Nigeria’s Oba Femi, leading to the Aussie getting tossed off the stage and through some tables by Femi, which is where he reportedly was injured.

The Australian wrestler had to stay behind in Saudi Arabia for additional medical treatment, but was reportedly back in the United States on November 18.

“WWE is devastated to announced that Jermaine Haley, also known as Bronson Reed, passed away earlier [Friday] at the age of 38 due to medical complications,” WWE said on X.

“Jermaine was a larger-than-life figure in WWE, but more importantly, he was a phenomenal person, a devoted family man, and a beloved colleague.

“Our thoughts and deepest condolences are with Jermaine’s entire family, including his pregnant wife, Paige, their daughter, as well as all his friends, co-workers and fans.”

Hailing from Black Forest, South Australia, Haley first signed with WWE in 2019 before departing in 2021.

He re-signed in December 2022 and went on to become one of the company’s biggest rising stars.

No other details surrounding Reed’s death have been announced at this time.
