Saint Louis University grad tops September 2026 social workers licensure exam

TheTools · 17.09.2026 18:06 · source · permalink

A graduate of Saint Louis University achieved the highest score in the licensure examination for social workers held earlier this month.

According to the The Professional Regulation Commission (PRC), 8,383 test takers out of 10,991 passed the exam, given by the Board for Social Workers in 18 testing centers across the Philippines.

Zirc Iram Dela Cruz Sanchez of Saint Louis University was the top scorer with 93.40%, while several schools achieved a 100% passing rate.

Schools with a 100% passing rate with 51 or more examinees:

  • Caraga State University-Butuan City (116 of 116 test takers)
  • Bicol University-Daraga (114 of 114 test takers)
  • Davao Del Norte State College (66 of 66 test takers)
  • Mindanao State University-Marawi City (61 of 61 test takers)
  • Bulacan State University-Malolos (57 of 57 test takers)
  • Assumption College Of Davao (56 of 56 test takers)
  • Ifugao State Universit-Lamut (56 of 56 test takers)
