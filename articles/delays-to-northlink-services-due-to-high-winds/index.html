Delays to NorthLink services due to high winds

TheTools · 04.10.2026 09:23 · source · permalink

NORTHLINK is warning of delays to its passenger and freight services over the next two days.

The freight vessel Helliar will leave Lerwick much earlier than scheduled today, sailing at 2pm.

Her arrival into Aberdeen tomorrow (Monday) may also be delayed by up to two hours.

Monday’s passenger sailing of the Hjaltland from Lerwick is also set to be affected.

She will sail from Lerwick at 5.30, but her arrival into Kirkwall on Monday night and Aberdeen on Tuesday morning may be delayed by up to one hour.
