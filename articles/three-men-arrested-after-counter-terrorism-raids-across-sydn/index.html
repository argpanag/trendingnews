Three men arrested after counter-terrorism raids across Sydney

Updated November 20, 2026 - 12.07am. First published November 19, 2026 - 11.57pm

Three men have been arrested following counter-terrorism raids across Sydney.

Heavily armed officers, including members of the NSW Police and the Australian Federal Police, swooped on several properties in Sydney’s south-west and north-west just after 5am on Friday.

Police were seen at a home in Campbelltown in Sydney’s south-west early on Friday. 9News

The raids were believed to have been sparked by a man allegedly possessing extremist material.

A 35-year-old man was arrested at a home in Ingleburn.

He has been charged with knowingly collecting/making a document connected with terrorism and using a carriage service-possess violent extremist material (VEM).

Police will allege the materials related to religiously motivated violent extremism, including ISIS.

He was refused bail and will appear at Campbelltown Local Court on Friday.

A second man, aged 34, was arrested at a Campbelltown home.

He is expected to be charged with hindering a police investigation.

A 33-year-old man was also arrested in Wooli Creek and taken to Kogarah Police Station, where he is expected to be charged with firearm offences.

A large police presence was seen at the home in Campbelltown in Sydney’s south-west.

Several armed officers conducted searches in a backyard at the home.

It appears forensic analysis is taking place at the scene.

Garbage, including an abandoned trolley and gardening tools, can be seen strewn across the unkempt backyard.

Another home on the same street is being searched by police.

Police have also been combing through cars sitting in the driveway at both properties.

Several items have been seized during the searches, which are still ongoing.

Police are expected to address the media shortly after 1.15pm.
