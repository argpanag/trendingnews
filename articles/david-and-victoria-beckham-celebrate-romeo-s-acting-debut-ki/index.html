David and Victoria Beckham celebrate Romeo’s acting debut; Kim Turnbull also joined the family

TheTools · 18.11.2026 18:22 · source · permalink

family outings

David and Victoria Beckham celebrate their son Romeo Beckham's first movie role at the Paris premiere of Forty Love, with girlfriend Kim Turnbull joining the family for his big night

David and Victoria Beckham had every reason to celebrate as their son, Romeo Beckham, stepped into the spotlight for a major career milestone.

The former footballer and fashion designer attended the world premiere of Romeo's debut film, Forty Love, at Le Grand Rex in Paris on Tuesday, November 17, 2026.

The 24-year-old model is officially making his acting debut, and his famous parents made sure they were there to support him. Romeo's girlfriend, Kim Turnbull, also joined the family for the special occasion, turning the premiere into a stylish family celebration.

David and Victoria Beckham Share Emotional Messages for Romeo

For David and Victoria Beckham, the evening was about more than a glamorous red carpet appearance. It was an opportunity to celebrate Romeo's decision to pursue a new career. David, 51, shared a photograph of himself and Victoria sitting inside the Paris cinema, with the movie screen visible behind them.

The former England football captain accompanied the picture with a touching message. "So proud of you mate, you see the challenge and you take it. We love you," he wrote on Instagram.

Victoria, 52, was equally enthusiastic about her son's achievement. The fashion designer and former Spice Girls singer posted a photograph of herself, David and Romeo together on the red carpet, writing, "We're so proud of you."

The family also shared affectionate moments in front of photographers. Victoria was photographed wrapping her arms around Romeo in a warm embrace, while David also hugged his son during the premiere.

Romeo Beckham Makes His Acting Debut in Forty Love

Romeo Beckham's first movie role is a significant departure from his previous work. In Forty Love, the 24-year-old stars opposite French actor Paul Kircher in a romantic drama set in the competitive world of professional tennis.

The film follows Sacha Gallo, a promising young tennis player portrayed by Kircher, whose ambitions are complicated by the arrival of a charismatic rival played by Romeo. What begins as an intense sporting rivalry gradually develops into a romantic relationship.

The story explores love, competition, personal identity and the pressures of pursuing success.

Directed by fashion photographer Pierre-Ange Carlotti in his feature filmmaking debut, the French-language movie features an established cast that includes Catherine Deneuve, Guillaume Canet and Benjamin Voisin.

The film is scheduled to arrive in French cinemas on November 25, 2026. International release dates have not yet been confirmed. 

From Football and Modeling to Movies: Romeo Beckham's Career Journey

Romeo Beckham has spent much of his life exploring different career paths. Like his father, David, he initially pursued football. He trained in Arsenal's youth system before continuing his football career with Brentford.

However, Romeo eventually moved away from professional football and focused more heavily on fashion. His modeling career brought opportunities to work with internationally recognized luxury brands, establishing him as a familiar face in the fashion industry.

Now, acting represents another major change. According to reports, he also worked with an acting coach to prepare for his first film appearance.

Romeo's movie premiere also took place against the backdrop of reported tensions within the Beckham family. His older brother, Brooklyn Beckham, 27, was not among the family members photographed celebrating the premiere.

Brooklyn and his wife, Nicola Peltz Beckham, have been at the center of a widely publicized family dispute.
