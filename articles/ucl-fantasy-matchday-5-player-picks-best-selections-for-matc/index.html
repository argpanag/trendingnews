UCL Fantasy Matchday 5 Player Picks: Best Selections for Matchday 5

TheTools · 23.11.2026 14:38 · source · permalink
  • Fantasy Player Picks

Barcelona, Real Madrid, Arsenal, Como and Paris Saint-Germain have all made impressive starts to the Champions League league phase, but which of their players offer the best value for Fantasy managers heading into Matchday 5?

Barcelona have scored 14 goals in their opening four matches, while Real Madrid have won all four of their games. Paris Saint-Germain have also been in good form in front of goal, scoring 13 times, which makes their meeting with Roma an interesting fixture for Fantasy managers.

Como are another team worth considering after their remarkable 9-1 victory over Lens, although they face AEK Athens this time around. Arsenal and Manchester City also have home fixtures against Borussia Dortmund and Napoli respectively, while Atletico Madrid welcome Viking.

Looking back at our Matchday 4 picks, Raphinha was the standout performer after scoring twice to collect 15 Fantasy points. Kylian Mbappe also found the net, while Harry Kane provided an assist to earn five points. However, some of our other selections failed to make a significant impact.

With another round of fixtures approaching, there are plenty of options to consider. Never Manage Alone now picks out the players who could deliver across all four positions in UCL Fantasy Matchday 5.

Goalkeeper

Alvaro Valles (€4.5m) vs Slovan Bratislava (A)

Real Betis have a good opportunity to keep a clean sheet in Matchday 5, with Slovan Bratislava finding goals hard to come by in the Champions League. They have scored just once in their opening four matches, which makes this a promising fixture for Valles.

The 29-year-old has started all four of Real Betis’ Champions League games and played every minute, making 10 saves and keeping one clean sheet, although he has conceded six goals so far.

He has also been reliable in LaLiga, starting all 13 of Real Betis’ matches and keeping six clean sheets, one of the best records in the competition. His recent performance against Sevilla was another positive, as he made five saves in the 1-1 draw.

At €4.5m, Valles offers an affordable route into the Real Betis defence, while his regular starting role and favourable fixture make him an option worth considering. With just 1% ownership, he could also prove to be a useful differential.

Defenders

Gabriel Magalhaes (€6.0m) vs Borussia Dortmund (H)

Gabriel remains an attractive option despite Arsenal facing a tough test against Borussia Dortmund. The Gunners have kept 10 clean sheets across all competitions this season, including two in the Champions League, while conceding just seven goals in their opening 11 Premier League matches.

The Brazilian also offers an attacking threat from set pieces, making him a valuable option at both ends of the pitch. His regular starting role adds to his appeal for Matchday 5.

Joao Cancelo (€5.0m) vs Sabah (A)

Barcelona have scored 14 goals in their opening four Champions League matches, making their trip to Sabah an appealing fixture. Cancelo has already contributed a goal and two assist in Europe, alongside 15 ball recoveries and 26 Fantasy points in 360 minutes played

At €5.0m, his attacking contributions and potential for a clean sheet make him an interesting option for Fantasy managers.

Alejandro Grimaldo (€5.5m) vs Viking (H)

Atletico Madrid welcome Viking after their opponents conceded 10 goals in their opening four Champions League matches. Grimaldo offers plenty of attacking potential, having registered five goals and two assists in LaLiga this season.

The Spaniard also takes set pieces, giving him additional opportunities to collect Fantasy points beyond clean sheets. His attacking numbers make him an appealing pick at €5.5m.

Jacobo Ramon (€4.1m) vs AEK Athens (H)

Como have made an impressive start to their Champions League campaign and will hope to continue that form against AEK Athens. Ramon has already recorded a goal, an assist and 34 ball recoveries so far this season.

At just €4.1m, the young defender offers an affordable route into the Como defence, while his recent contributions suggest he can deliver points beyond clean sheets.

Midfielders

Raphinha (€9.6m) vs Sabah (A)

Raphinha is an attractive option for Matchday 5 as Barcelona prepare to face Sabah. The Brazilian has scored four goals in three Champions League appearances this season, including a brace against Aston Villa.

He has also registered 15 goals and three assists in 12 LaLiga appearances. His attacking threat makes him worth considering, especially with Lamine Yamal ruled out of the trip to Baku due to discomfort in his right ankle. Barcelona have decided not to risk the teenager, which could give Raphinha a bigger role in attack.

Michael Olise (€9.0m) vs Lille (A)

Olise has been productive for Bayern Munich, registering six goals and three assists in the Bundesliga. He has also scored twice and provided an assist in his opening three Champions League appearances.

However, managers should monitor his fitness after he came off at half-time against FC Koln with a bruised forefoot. Checking Bayern Munich’s starting line-up before the deadline would be wise before making a decision on the Frenchman.

Nico Paz (€7.1m) vs AEK Athens (H)

Paz has been one of Como’s biggest attacking threats in Europe, recording three goals and five assists in their opening four Champions League matches. His eight goal contributions highlight the impact he has made so far.

He has also earned 43 Fantasy points, making him an appealing option at €7.1m. With Como hosting AEK Athens, the Argentine has a good chance to add to his tally.

Forwards

Kylian Mbappe (€11.1m) vs PSV Eindhoven (H)

Mbappe remains one of the safest premium picks for Matchday 5. The Real Madrid forward has scored in all four of his Champions League appearances this season, taking his tally to four goals and one assist.

He also faces PSV Eindhoven at the Santiago Bernabeu, giving him a good opportunity to continue his scoring run. His penalty-taking duties add to his appeal, making him a strong option for the forward line.

Julian Alvarez (€10.0m) vs Viking (H)

Alvarez is another forward worth considering, particularly with Atletico Madrid hosting Viking. The Argentine has registered one goal and one assist in four Champions League appearances, while his recent return to regular starts makes him an appealing choice.

He is also Atletico Madrid’s penalty taker, which gives him another route to Fantasy points. With Viking coming to Madrid, Alvarez could be a useful option for managers looking beyond the most popular forwards.

Tasos Douvikas (€6.6m) vs AEK Athens (H)

Douvikas has been one of the biggest surprises in this season’s Champions League, scoring six goals in four appearances. The Como striker took his tally to six with a hat-trick in the 9-1 victory over Lens on Matchday 4.

At €6.6m, he offers excellent value compared with more expensive forwards. Como also have a favourable home fixture against AEK Athens, giving the Greek striker another chance to add to his impressive goal tally.
