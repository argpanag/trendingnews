Paige DeSorbo and Hannah Berner criticized aspects of Dancing With the Stars after friend Ciara Miller's October 6 elimination, raising questions about how the competition communicates its voting methodology. On the Oct. 9 Giggly Squad podcast, DeSorbo alleged the voting was "rigged," while Berner questioned transparency around the Dancing With the Stars algorithm and bottom-three disclosures. Ciara Miller and partner Brandon Armstrong were eliminated on Mariah Carey Night after their jazz routine received 21 out of 30 points. Berner also suggested gender bias could influence results, and DeSorbo defended Miller while arguing producers often weigh audience appeal.
