Rain finally returns on Wednesday which ushers in much cooler air

TheTools · 03.11.2026 08:06 · source · permalink

CLEVELAND —

Wet, windy and warm Wednesday

The rest of Election Day will remain dry, mild and breezy. Lows will be in the upper 50s and low 60s overnight. It will be warm for one more day before the big chill.

Plan for a warm, windy and...wet Wednesday ahead. Wind gusts could top 30 mph, as temps make another run toward 70. But a cold front is going to bring back much needed rain and much cooler temperatures.

Showers will start in our West Region Wednesday morning. At first, showers will be very light and isolated, but as we near lunchtime, rain will become more widespread and impactful and will continue to spread east during the afternoon.

The late afternoon/early evening will be the best chance for everyone across NEO to see rain and also when the rain will increase in intensity. Moderate to heavy rain will be possible.

While widespread flooding is not expected, wet leaves can pile up over storm drains and slow down runoff during heavier downpours. That could add to the risk for street and poor-drainage flooding Wednesday, especially where drains are already covered.

The evening commute could certainly be a slow one, especially with the earlier sunset. It can be much harder to see any flooding at night.

November returns: Much cooler (although seasonable) temperatures

Wednesday’s system finally knocks down the warmth. Behind it, we’ll settle back into cooler, more seasonable November temps, plan for afternoons in the 40s/50s! Just a reminder that average afternoon or high temperatures for Cleveland in early November are in the mid 50s.

The widespread and heavy rainfall should clear NEO early Thursday, but a few lingering, lighter lake effect showers will be possible on Friday and Saturday.
