Enzo Maresca made it clear that Manchester City will need to achieve perfection to prevail in their Champions League debut


A demanding start in Europe


Manchester City is preparing to debut in the Champions League against Porto, and Enzo Maresca left no room for doubt, to win at the Estadio Do Dragao a "perfect" performance will be necessary. The Italian coach acknowledged the difficulty of the venue and the strength of the opponent, who arrives with five consecutive victories in the Primeira Liga and only one defeat in their last 14 European matches. "This is a difficult place to arrive at. They started the season very well. They are a very good team, with a clear style and dangerous on set pieces. To win here, you have to be perfect," he emphasized.



The present of City and the changes in the squad


The Citizens arrive with confidence after maintaining an ideal score in the Premier League, following a narrow 1-0 victory against Coventry City. With three consecutive victories at the start, they match a historical record that only Chelsea has surpassed more times. However, the focus is on Europe, where they seek revenge after last season's elimination against Real Madrid (5-1 on aggregate). The summer brought significant changes, with departures of key players like John Stones, Rodri, or Bernardo Silva, and high-profile arrivals of Enzo Fernández, Iliman Ndiaye, and other reinforcements. "In this club, the expectations are always high. I prefer to think day by day, the important thing is to improve match by match," explained Maresca, aware of the pressure that comes with managing the English champions.



Significant absences and the focus on the classic


The coach will be only the fourth to lead City in the Champions League, following in the footsteps of Mancini, Pellegrini, and Guardiola, none of whom lost in their debut. Regarding absences, Jeremy Doku and Nico O'Reilly did not travel to Portugal due to physical issues, although they could reappear in the Manchester derby. "The important thing is that they come back gradually. Jeremy is improving, we'll see if he makes it by the weekend," said Maresca. With a rival in great form and a squad in full transition, City knows that to start off on the right foot in the Champions League, they will have to come close to perfection.
