Cristiano Ronaldo could be entering his final months at Al Nassr. At least, that is the view of Saudi journalist and former club spokesperson Saud Al Sarami.

“Cristiano is no longer at his best at Al Nassr, and I think he will leave during the winter transfer window,” he said on an Al Arabiya Sports podcast.

The comments have caught many by surprise, although given his age, such a scenario would not be entirely unexpected. The 41 year old remains Al Nassr’s captain and biggest star. In fact, he continues to push himself in pursuit of one of the most remarkable milestones of his career: reaching 1,000 career goals.

For now, Al Sarami’s comments are merely his personal opinion. However, the fact that they come from someone with close ties to the club has set alarm bells ringing and sparked considerable speculation in Saudi Arabia and around the world.

It remains to be seen whether there is any truth behind his prediction.
