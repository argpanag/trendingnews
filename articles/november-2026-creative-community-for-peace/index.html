CCFP STATEMENT ON SHAWN MENDES’ COMMENTS ABOUT GAZA

Shawn Mendes says he has been educating himself about the Israeli-Palestinian conflict. Unfortunately, his recent statement suggests that his education has been deeply biased and one-sided.

By accusing Israel of genocide, Shawn is repeating a dangerous lie propagated by Hamas and rooted in Soviet-era propaganda. Recently, Creative Community for Peace released an open letter signed by more than 6,000 leaders and public figures from across entertainment, academia, government, religion, and public life, rejecting this accusation and warning of its role in fueling antisemitism worldwide.

Words matter. Especially when they come from an artist with nearly 70 million followers on social media.

Extremist accusations against Israel do not exist in a vacuum. Anti-Jewish hatred has surged around the world, fueled in part by these false and inflammatory charges. When influential artists amplify these accusations to millions of followers, they lend them credibility and help create an environment in which Jews are increasingly vilified, harassed, and targeted.

We do not question Shawn’s compassion for Palestinian civilians or his desire to alleviate their suffering. But compassion without context is not education. Amplifying a narrative without understanding its origins, including the role of Hamas and its disinformation campaign, is irresponsible.

If Shawn is genuinely committed to educating himself, we encourage him to engage with Israelis, Palestinians, Jewish voices, and credible sources across the spectrum, rather than relying solely on those who reinforce what he already believes or belong to the anti-Israel movement.

The entertainment community has an enormous capacity to promote understanding. It also has a responsibility to avoid amplifying misinformation and rhetoric that can fuel hatred. Having a public platform does not make someone an authority, and good intentions do not excuse misinformation.

Speaking up takes courage. Taking the time to understand what you’re speaking about takes responsibility.

We hope Shawn Mendes will do both. Creative Community for Peace would welcome the opportunity to engage with him, broaden his understanding of the conflict, and explore how he can use his platform to effect positive change.
