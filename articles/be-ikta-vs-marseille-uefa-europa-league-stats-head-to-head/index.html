Match Facts

  • The only previous games between Beşiktaş and Marseille came in the 2007-08 UEFA Champions League group stage, with both sides winning their home fixture (2-1 in Türkiye, 2-0 in France).

  • Marseille are winless on all five of their away trips to Turkish opposition in major European competition (D2 L3), most recently losing 4-2 to Galatasaray the 2021-22 UEFA Europa League group stage.

  • Beşiktaş have failed to progress from any of their last five group stage campaigns in major European competition, winning their opening game in just one of those (3-1 vs Sarpsborg 08 FF in 2018-19 UEFA Europa League).

  • Marseille have won just five of their 29 UEFA Europa League away games (D9 L15), including just one of their last 15 (D5 L9), 2-0 at AEK Athens in November 2023.

  • Václav Cerny has been involved in nine goals in 14 UEFA Europa League appearances (7 goals, 2 assists), including eight in his last campaign in the competition in 2024-25 for Rangers (6 goals, 2 assists), a total only three players could better.
