Kim Hellberg makes shock Will Lankshear decision for Middlesbrough's Cardiff game

TheTools · 17.10.2026 12:54 · source · permalink

WILL Lankshear is on the bench for Middlesbrough against Cardiff City.

Lankshear tops the Championship goal charts and scored against Stoke City and Wolves, but starts on the bench today, with Kyle Joseph coming in to lead the line.

That's one of three changes, with Riley McGree and Tommy Conway restored to the starting line-up. They replace Jeremy Sarmiento and Sebastian Berhalter. It looks like Adilson Malanda will start in midfield.

Callum Brittain, Luke Ayling, Ashley Phillips and Neto Borges keep their place in defence, with Aidan Morris in midfield, presumably alongside Malanda.

Amario Cozier-Duberry, McGree and Conway support Joseph.

It's the first time this season in the Championship that Lankshear has started on the bench.

MIDDLESBROUGH: Vitek, Brittain, Ayling, Phillips, Borges, Malanda, Morris, ACD, McGree, Conway, Joseph.

SUBS: Brynn, Byram, Berhalter, Lankshear, MPH, Sarmiento, Arfsten, Barron, Castledine

CARDIFF CITY: Trott; Kpakio, Bielik, Osho, Bagan; Robertson (C), J. Colwill, Asford; Tanner, Moylan, Willock.

SUBS: Tyrer, Fish, Turnbull, Scanlon, Tankiewicz, Davies, Moreno, Holt, Perrett.
