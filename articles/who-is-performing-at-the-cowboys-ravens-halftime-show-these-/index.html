Who is performing at the Cowboys - Ravens halftime show? These are the performers at the NFL game in Brazil

TheTools · 27.09.2026 19:54 · source · permalink

The Baltimore Ravens and Dallas Cowboys play today in Brazil at the Maracanã Stadium in Rio de Janeiro on Sunday.

The NFL 2026 season is underway, and this year the league has scheduled nine games outside the United States as part of the 2026 NFL International Games, with matchups taking place in the United Kingdom, Spain, Australia, Mexico, Germany, France and Brazil.

These are the NFL international games scheduled for 2026

To date, 62 NFL regular-season games have been played outside the United States, with games taking place in cities including London, Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt, Madrid, Dublin, São Paulo, Mexico City, and Toronto.

These are the international games confirmed for 2026:

  1. San Francisco 49ers vs. Los Angeles Rams | Melbourne, Australia, at the Melbourne Cricket Ground
  2. Baltimore Ravens vs. Dallas Cowboys | Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, at Maracanã Stadium
  3. Indianapolis Colts vs. Washington Commanders | London, England, at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium
  4. Philadelphia Eagles vs. Jacksonville Jaguars | London, England, at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium
  5. Houston Texans vs. Jacksonville Jaguars | London, England, at Wembley Stadium
  6. Pittsburgh Steelers vs. New Orleans Saints | Paris, France, at Stade de France
  7. Cincinnati Bengals vs. Atlanta Falcons | Madrid, Spain, at the Bernabéu Stadium
  8. New England Patriots vs. Detroit Lions | Munich, Germany, at FC Bayern Munich Arena
  9. Minnesota Vikings vs. San Francisco 49ers | Mexico City, Mexico, at Estadio Banorte, formerly known as Estadio Azteca

NFL confirms halftime show for 2026 Rio Game

The league announced that Brazilian DJ Pedro Sampaio and singer Ricky Martin will headline the halftime show at the NFL’s 2026 Rio Game between the Baltimore Ravens and Dallas Cowboys, which takes place today, Sunday, Sept. 27 at Maracanã Stadium.

This will be the second time the NFL has played a game in Brazil, having played their first last season in São Paulo. Ricky Martin’s halftime performance will also be the second of seven halftime shows scheduled for the 2026 NFL International Games, with the remaining performances set to be announced in the coming weeks.

DJ Pedro Sampaio is one of the most recognized artists in South America, with more than 14 billion audio and video streams and more than 10 million monthly listeners on Spotify. Sampaio was nominated for a Latin Grammy in 2024.

Meanwhile, Ricky Martin is one of the most successful Latin artists of all time, having sold more than 95 million albums worldwide. He also appeared as a special guest during the Apple Music Super Bowl LX halftime show.

“2026 has been a year full of wonderful opportunities, and this is without a doubt one of them. Being part of another historic NFL event alongside Pedro Sampaio is incredibly exciting, especially because it allows me to return to one of my favorite places in the world: Rio,” Ricky Martin said. “There is nothing like the energy of the Brazilian audience, and I can’t wait to reconnect with everyone and perform live again on such an iconic stage as Maracanã. Get ready to dance! See you in September!”
