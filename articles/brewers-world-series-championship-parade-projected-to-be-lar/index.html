Brewers World Series Championship Parade projected to be largest event in Wisconsin history

TheTools · 30.10.2026 21:58 · source · permalink

Authorities estimated more than 750,000 attendees Friday.

More than 750,000 people likely attended the Milwaukee Brewers World Series championship parade on Friday, according to an estimate from Milwaukee police.

"Preliminary research shows this may have been the largest crowd for a single-day event in state history," the department posted on social media. "Go Brewers! Better together!"

For comparison, the U.S. Census Bureau estimated Milwaukee's population in 2025 was 562,407.

The parade started at 10 a.m. and thousands of fans lined the streets to witness history. There were people on roofs, hanging out of windows and in the streets.

Many people watched the parade and then flocked to the rally at the Summerfest grounds.
