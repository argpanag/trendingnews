Stamford Bridge set for a clash of the giants

Stamford Bridge takes centre stage today, Saturday, as Chelsea host Manchester United in a mouth-watering ninth-round clash in the Premier League.

Enzo Maresca made some notable changes. Marc Guiu makes his first league start in a Chelsea shirt, while Romeo Lavia comes into midfield to cover for the absent Jordan Henderson.

Cole Palmer also returns to the XI after serving his suspension. Joao Pedro, though, misses out through injury.

United, meanwhile, hand Leny Yoro a start at right-back. Captain Bruno Fernandes returns to the starting line-up after settling for a place on the bench last time out.

Chelsea's line-up against Manchester United was as follows:

- Goalkeeper: Mike Penders.

- Defence: Josh Acheampong, Levi Colwill, Wesley Fofana.

- Midfield: Pedro Neto, Moises Caicedo, Romeo Lavia, Marc Guiu.

- Attack: Cole Palmer, Morgan Rogers, Estevao.

Meanwhile, Manchester United's line-up was as follows:

- Goalkeeper: Senne Lammens.

- Defence: Diogo Dalot, Harry Maguire, Lisandro Martinez, Leny Yoro.

- Midfield: Bruno Fernandes, Andre Santos, Carlos Baleba.

- Attack: Amad Diallo, Bryan Mbeumo, Matheus Cunha.

Chelsea go into the clash fifth in the table on 13 points. United, by contrast, are in far deeper trouble, having slipped to 16th on 7 points. There's plenty riding on this one for both sides.
