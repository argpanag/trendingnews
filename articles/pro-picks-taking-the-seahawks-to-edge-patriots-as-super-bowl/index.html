Pro Picks: Taking the Seahawks to edge Patriots, as Super Bowl rematch kicks off NFL season

TheTools · 08.09.2026 20:07 · source · permalink

Seattle Seahawks quarterback Sam Darnold (14) warms up before a preseason game against the Tennessee Titans last month in Nashville.John Amis/The Associated Press

Sam Darnold and the Seattle Seahawks will see their championship banner raised before hosting Drake Maye and the New England Patriots in a Super Bowl rematch to kick off the NFL season on Wednesday night.

Are you ready for some football?

The 2026 season begins in Seattle one day before the 49ers and Rams meet in Melbourne, Australia, in the first of nine international games.

Led by their “Dark Side” defence, the Seahawks dominated the Patriots 29-13 to win the franchise’s second Super Bowl in February.

They lost Super Bowl MVP Kenneth Walker, cornerback Riq Woolen, safety Coby Bryant and offensive coordinator Klint Kubiak. But they’re 3 1/2-point favourites against New England.

The NFL season starts on Wednesday. Here’s what to know

“It’s going to be electric,” Seahawks all-pro receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba said on AP’s Football Unfiltered podcast. “I’m excited. ... Definitely looking forward to being out there again on this new journey with our new team, with our fans, it’s going be awesome.”

The Patriots gave Maye a new No. 1 receiver, acquiring A.J. Brown from Philadelphia. They also signed veteran wideout Romeo Doubs and bolstered the secondary by adding all-pro safety Kevin Byard.

Pro Picks expects a close one.

Seahawks: 23-22

Los Angeles Rams quarterback Ty Simpson, left, scrambles during the first half of a preseason game against the Los Angeles Chargers last month in Inglewood, Calif.Katie Chin/The Associated Press

San Francisco vs. Los Angeles Rams, in Australia

Line: Rams minus 3 1/2

Aaron Donald is back to join Myles Garrett and Trent McDuffie on a star-studded defence in Los Angeles. With a dynamic offence led by reigning NFL MVP Matthew Stafford and all-pro receiver Puka Nacua, the Rams are Super Bowl favourites. But they’re in the toughest division in the NFL and the 49ers were one win away from the No. 1 seed last season. The teams split in 2025 and the Rams have won four of the last five. Los Angeles was 9-3 against the spread when favoured by at least 3 1/2 points last season.

Rams: 27-20

New York Jets at Tennessee

Line: Titans minus 1 1/2

Robert Saleh’s first game as coach of the Titans is against the team that fired him in 2024. He has Cam Ward. The Jets have Geno Smith. Saleh is in a better spot now.

Titans: 19-16

New Orleans at Detroit

Line: Lions minus 6 1/2

Jared Goff, Jahmyr Gibbs, Amon-Ra St. Brown and the Lions have too much offence for the Saints, who had a promising finish last season but lost a few key players in training camp.

Lions: 30-20

Cleveland at Jacksonville

Line: Jaguars minus 8 1/2

The Jaguars won 13 games last season and have most of their starters back. The Browns traded Garrett and are giving Deshaun Watson his first start since Oct. 20, 2024. This won’t be close.

Jaguars 27-12

Chicago at Carolina

Line: Bears minus 3

Both teams won their division last season though Carolina did it with a losing record. Expectations are high for Caleb Williams in his second season in Ben Johnson’s offence. Get ready for the first “Good, better, best” chant in Chicago’s locker room of the new season. Not predicting whether Johnson keeps his shirt on.

Bears: 24-18

Tampa Bay at Cincinnati

Line: Bengals minus 3 1/2

After starting 0-2 four times in Joe Burrow’s first five seasons, the Bengals opened 3-0 last year before things went downhill. They bolstered their defence to go with a prolific offence. Baker Mayfield and the Buccaneers will be a tough test for that new-look defence featuring Dexter Lawrence.

Bengals: 26-24

Atlanta at Pittsburgh

Line: Steelers minus 3 1/2

Tua Tagovailoa and Kevin Stefanski try to spoil Aaron Rodgers and Mike McCarthy’s reunion. Bijan Robinson gives the Falcons their best chance. Rodgers goes against a depleted pass rush so he should have time to throw to DK Metcalf and Michael Pittman.

Steelers: 24-17

Buffalo at Houston

Line: Bills minus 1 1/2

Joe Brady’s first game as Buffalo’s coach will be a major challenge against DeMeco Ryans and his stingy defence in Houston. C.J. Stroud plays his first game since his turnover debacle in the playoffs. Pro Picks has Josh Allen and the Bills beating the Texans in the AFC title game.

Texans: 19-17

Baltimore at Indianapolis

Line: Ravens minus 3 1/2

Lamar Jackson, Derrick Henry and the Ravens have high expectations in coach Jesse Minter’s first season. Daniel Jones is back for Indianapolis. So is Sauce Gardner. The Colts finished last season with seven straight losses after opening 8-2.

Ravens: 23-20

Green Bay at Minnesota

Line: Packers minus 1 1/2

Jordan Love and the Packers were 0-5 without Micah Parsons last season. He’s not back until October so they can’t afford to wait for a win. Kyler Murray makes his first start for the Vikings, aiming to become Kevin O’Connell’s next reclamation project.

Vikings: 23-20

Washington at Philadelphia

Line: Eagles minus 5 1/2

Jalen Hurts begins his fifth straight season with a new offensive coordinator as Sean Mannion replaced Kevin Patullo. Hurts could have a big year if Mannion unlocks Saquon Barkley and the run game. The Commanders had a terrible defence last season. They added Sonny Styles and some veterans but a banged-up offensive line will struggle protecting Jayden Daniels against Philadelphia’s fierce defence.

Best bet: Eagles: 31-16

Miami at Las Vegas

Line: Raiders minus 3 1/2

Two rookie coaches meet in a matchup of two teams that aren’t going to win many games. Kirk Cousins and the Raiders get the first one, though stopping Dolphins running back De’Von Achane will be a tough task for Las Vegas’ defence.

Raiders: 20-17

Arizona at Los Angeles Chargers

Line: Chargers minus 9 1/2

Jim Harbaugh’s Chargers have won 11 games in each of his first two seasons and brought in Mike McDaniel to run the offence led by Justin Herbert. Rookie coach Mike LaFleur is starting the season with veteran Jacoby Brissett. Los Angeles has dominated inferior teams under Harbaugh.

Chargers: 30-16

Dallas at New York Giants

Line: Cowboys minus 3

The Cowboys can score behind Dak Prescott, CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens. They hope new coordinator Christian Parker, rookie safety Caleb Downs and other additions improve the defence enough to make them a contender. John Harbaugh is the new man in charge in New York. Jaxson Dart, Cam Skattebo and the rest of the Giants will be better for it. Not enough to win this week.

Cowboys: 26-20

Denver at Kansas City

Line: Kansas City minus 2 1/2

Patrick Mahomes is back after tearing his ACL last Dec. 14 and K.C. is coming off its first losing season under Andy Reid. He faces one of the NFL’s best defences. Bo Nix returns for the Broncos after an ankle injury forced him to miss the AFC championship game. Denver has won three in a row in the series and four of five after losing 16 straight.

Upset special: Broncos 23-20
