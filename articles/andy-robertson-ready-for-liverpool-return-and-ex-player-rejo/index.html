Andy Robertson ready for Liverpool return and ex-player rejoins in new role

TheTools · 15.09.2026 05:00 · source · permalink

Liverpool will look to rediscover their winning momentum as they host Tottenham Hotspur in a Carabao Cup third-round clash. The cup tie arrives on the heels of a frustrating weekend for the Reds, who were held to a goalless stalemate by Alvaro Arbeloa’s embattled Fulham on Saturday.

Despite Andoni Iraola deploying a high-profile attacking trio of Alexander Isak, Bradley Barcola, and Florian Wirtz, Liverpool were remarkably subdued. The star-studded forward line offered little offensive bite, sleepwalking through a lifeless Premier League draw that asked few questions of the Cottagers.

This evening's encounter with Spurs presents Iraola with a valuable platform to assert his team's ambitions in this year's League Cup.

The fixture also promises to be a poignant occasion for the Anfield faithful, as it marks the highly anticipated return of revered club icon Andy Robertson following his departure in the summer. In broader club developments, a legendary figure has officially been welcomed back into the Liverpool fold.

Andy Robertson primed for Anfield return

Robertson will make his first appearance back at Liverpool since his poignant departure this summer as Tottenham visit Anfield tonight for the Carabao Cup fixture.

The Scotland international claimed eight major honours during a nine-year stint, including two Premier League crowns and the Champions League in 2019.

Robertson departed after Milos Kerkez displaced him from the Liverpool starting eleven. While Kerkez has found things difficult, and was omitted from the starting line-up against Fulham, the Scotland captain has started in three of Tottenham's four Premier League matches this campaign.

Spurs arrive at Anfield following a goalless stalemate against Everton on Saturday, a result that prolonged the side's winless streak in the Premier League.

Robertson, though, has been amongst the few positives for Roberto De Zerbi's team this season. Like Milner, fellow Scotsman Alan Hutton reckons Robertson will eventually return to the Reds in a coaching role.

"Could we see Andy Robertson return to Liverpool as manager someday or take the Scotland or Celtic jobs? I think he can go on and do that if he wants to," the former Spurs and Aston Villa right-back said.

James Milner makes Anfield return

Liverpool icon James Milner has returned to the club following his 2023 departure. The former Man City midfielder is pursuing his coaching career by assisting youngsters at the Academy in Kirkby.

Milner brought the curtain down on his remarkable 24-year playing career this summer after a stint with Brighton. The ECHO understands Liverpool's arrangement with the ex-Leeds United stalwart is informal in nature.

He is not an official permanent member of staff, but is accumulating experience towards securing additional badges. The 40-year-old was overseeing the under-11s at the weekend.

The three-time Premier League winner, who arrived at the club in 2015, is also set to collaborate with several other age groups in the coming months.

Milner has already secured his UEFA A Licence and is working towards completing his Pro Licence. He previously assisted with the Academy during his playing days at Liverpool.
