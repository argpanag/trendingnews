Carabao Cup Fourth Round: Predicted Lineups and Scores

TheTools · 28.10.2026 08:46 · source · permalink

The EFL Cup fourth round lands across Wednesday and Thursday with six all-Premier League ties.

Liverpool host Chelsea at Anfield as the clearest prestige collision. Manchester City open the night against Brighton, while Sunderland, Fulham, and Bournemouth face Brentford, Crystal Palace, and Aston Villa. Everton vs Newcastle United closes the round on Thursday.

Cup rotations, missing names, and five-game scoring trends decide who reaches the quarterfinals.

Liverpool vs Chelsea

Liverpool’s last five games show four wins (80%), 2.8 goals scored, and 1.79 expected goals. Chelsea sit at two wins (40%), 1.8 scored, and 0.95 xG. That volume gap matters in a one-off midweek, especially with both managers juggling absences: Dominik Szoboszlai, Hugo Ekitike, and Jeremie Frimpong are missing for the hosts, while Cole Palmer, Reece James, and João Pedro are out for the visitors.

Andoni Iraola’s projected 4-2-3-1 leans on Alexis Mac Allister and Trey Nyoni as the double pivot, with Cody Gakpo and Rio Ngumoha in the creative band behind Lewis Koumas. Xabi Alonso’s 4-3-3 answers through Roméo Lavia and Jordan Henderson, with Pedro Neto and Estêvão stretching the pitch for Danny Welbeck. Liverpool will try to pin Chelsea deep and attack cut-backs; Chelsea’s route is compact rest defense and early wide releases.

Anfield usually rewards the side that wins the first transitional duel. Liverpool’s recent scoring rate makes that the cleaner path.
