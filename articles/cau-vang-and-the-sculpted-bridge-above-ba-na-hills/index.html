Cau Vang and the sculpted bridge above Ba Na Hills

TheTools · 11.09.2026 05:52 · source · permalink

Cau Vang in Da Nang spans Ba Na Hills as a golden pedestrian bridge held by giant hands, with the mountain ridge setting its tone.

Cau Vang is a pedestrian bridge in Ba Na Hills, west of Da Nang, and its golden span is the sight that gives the place its name. Completed in 2018, it sits within Sun World Ba Na Hills in the Hòa Vang district and links the bridge to the resort landscape around it.

Cau Vang above the mountain ridge

The bridge runs for about 150 m (490 ft) in a gentle curve and carries a deck finished in a warm metallic colour. Its supports take the form of two giant hands, making the bridge feel as much like a sculptural object as a route across the hillside.completed in 2018

That combination of bridge and sculpture is the main reason Cau Vang stands out in the Ba Na Hills setting. The structure keeps a clear pedestrian function, yet its form is what stays in view from most angles.about 150 m

The bridge in Da Nang's hill country

Cau Vang sits high above the valley on a mountain ridge in central Vietnam. The bridge forms part of a larger resort landscape, so the setting is defined by planted areas, slopes and the continuous line of the walkway itself.

The link with Da Nang is direct, but the character of the place is alpine in scale rather than urban in feel. The bridge’s height and its mountain location make the route read as a promenade across the hills rather than a simple crossing.Sun World Ba Na Hills

From design object to landmark

The bridge is one of the clearest examples of how contemporary landscape architecture can work with a pedestrian route. Its gold-toned deck, curved plan and giant hand supports give the structure a recognisable profile against the hills.

Visiting Cau Vang: what travellers from Western Europe should know

  • The bridge stands in Sun World Ba Na Hills in Hòa Vang district, west of Da Nang. Reaching it involves the mountain resort setting rather than a street-level city walk.
  • The route is elevated and exposed to the mountain terrain. The bridge runs in a curve, with planted sections and sculptural supports alongside the pedestrian deck.
  • The official language is Vietnamese and the currency is the Vietnamese dong.
  • Entry requirements depend on your nationality; UK travellers can check the FCDO travel advice at gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice, Irish travellers the Department of Foreign Affairs at ireland.ie.

Frequently asked questions about Cau Vang

Where is Cau Vang?

Cau Vang is in Ba Na Hills, within Sun World Ba Na Hills in Hòa Vang district, west of Da Nang in central Vietnam. It is part of a mountain resort rather than an urban bridge.

What should travellers expect on a visit?

The bridge presents a long pedestrian deck in a curved line, with giant hand supports and planted edges. The setting is high above the valley and tied to the broader Ba Na Hills landscape.

What makes Cau Vang distinctive?

Its golden finish, 150 m length and hand-shaped supports give it a clear identity. The bridge reads as both infrastructure and sculpture in one mountain location.
