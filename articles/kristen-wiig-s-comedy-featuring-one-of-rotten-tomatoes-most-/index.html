Kristen Wiig's Bridesmaids arrives on Netflix US on November 1 again. However, its most talked-about disaster almost looked different. 

In 2019, Rotten Tomatoes included the bridal shop food-poisoning scene among its 21 most memorable movie moments. In addition, the site's film page gives the 2011 comedy an 89% critics' score from 294 reviews.

Bridesmaids nearly traded its bathroom chaos for Christian Bale

Initially, Wiig and writer Annie Mumolo imagined a fantasy sequence during the dress fitting. Instead of getting sick, Wiig's Annie would picture herself in a castle, surrounded by men who wanted her attention.

Then, the fantasy would send her into a forest to meet a Christian Bale chopping wood. 

According to Mumolo, the romance would continue on a bearskin rug while he combed Annie's hair. However, that description was a discarded script idea, not proof that Bale filmed a cameo. 

Ultimately, the filmmakers replaced the fantasy with the food-poisoning sequence, and the writers initially resisted that change.

Paul Feig needed eight or nine screenings to perfect it

Behind the scenes, director Paul Feig's team built a white shop to make the mess stand out. Meanwhile, the fake vomit mixed oatmeal with peas, carrots, and other vegetables.

Yet the biggest challenge involved timing rather than ingredients. 

Speaking to Rotten Tomatoes, Feig said, "But honestly it took about eight or nine test screenings to get the exact math right on that dress shop sequence, because there was some we let go a little too long and others we made too short."

Afterward, he explained the balancing act: "It was really having to discover how far we could push the audience without them turning on us." "We found we could actually push them pretty far."

Still, Feig wanted more than mayhem. Instead, Annie's refusal to admit a mistake gave the scene its engine and kept the chaos tied to character.
