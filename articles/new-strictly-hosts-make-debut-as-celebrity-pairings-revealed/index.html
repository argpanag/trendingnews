    Freya Scott-Turner
    Live reporter

    Strictly Come Dancing has returned to the small screen with plenty of glitz, a fresh cast of celebrities, five new professional dancers and three beaming hosts.

    Broadcaster Emma Willis, comedian Josh Widdicombe and former Strictly professional Johannes Radebe made their presenting debut - taking over from long-running hosting duo Tess Daly and Claudia Winkleman, who left the show last year.

    A new cohort of famous faces – including actress Melanie Walters and former England footballer Shaun Wright-Phillips – also stepped onto the ballroom floor for the first time.

    And this year, they're doing things a little differently, as the couples were revealed "through the medium of dance" in musical chairs-style routines.

    Among the newly-formed couples are actress Jaime Winston and last year's winning pro Carlos Gu, while Bethany Antonia is the third all-female pairing in the show's history, with pro Nancy Xu.

    One pair who won't need to get to know each other are Dani Dyer and Nikita Kuzmin - who are the first couple in Strictly history to be paired up twice.

    But some traditions have remained. Bidding us farewell as the show wrapped, we got to see our new hosts deliver the customary order to "staaart dancing".
