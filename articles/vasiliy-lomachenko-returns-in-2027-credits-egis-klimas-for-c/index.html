Vasiliy Lomachenko Returns In 2027, Credits Egis Klimas For Comeback

TheTools · 05.11.2026 15:22 · source · permalink

The manager says he never abandoned Lomachenko’s undisputed ambition, with Loma Power Promotions now joining Top Rank for the comeback.

Vasiliy Lomachenko says manager Egis Klimas kept working towards his return to the ring even after he considered his career finished. The Ukrainian has reversed his June 2025 retirement and will fight again in early 2027.

Klimas never accepted the final bell.

“I’m so happy Vasiliy has decided to return to the ring. He still has unfinished business and a dream that means so much to him: becoming the undisputed champion,” Klimas said.

Lomachenko credited his manager with doing the work needed to restart his career, despite his own conviction that he would not box again.

“He put in a tremendous amount of work and effort to get us back in the race,” Lomachenko said.

For Klimas, the comeback gives his fighter another opportunity to pursue a championship that escaped him at lightweight.

“I’ve never stopped believing in him, and I’m proud to stand beside him as he gives to that dream everything he has, and I strongly believe his comeback will bring tremendous joy to his team and, most of all, to his fans.”

Loma Power Promotions Joins Top Rank

Lomachenko returns with his own promotional company. Loma Power Promotions has signed a multi-year co-promotional agreement with Top Rank, which has promoted him since his professional debut in 2013.

The deal adds a new role for the former three-division champion as he prepares to campaign at super featherweight.

“Vasiliy is one of the most gifted fighters I’ve ever promoted, and I’m delighted he’s returning to the ring,” Top Rank chairman Bob Arum said.

Arum identified the depth at 130 pounds as a draw for the returning champion.

“There’s tremendous talent at super featherweight, and we’re looking forward to working with Loma Power Promotions to put on some terrific fights.”

Lomachenko brings an 18-3 record with 12 knockouts into the comeback. He won professional titles at featherweight, super featherweight and lightweight after collecting two Olympic gold medals.

His previous attempt to become undisputed ended in defeat to Devin Haney in May 2023. A year later, he stopped George Kambosos Jr. to capture the IBF lightweight belt before retiring as champion.

The next fight remains unnamed. Top Rank has set an early-2027 return at 130 pounds without announcing an opponent or broadcaster.
