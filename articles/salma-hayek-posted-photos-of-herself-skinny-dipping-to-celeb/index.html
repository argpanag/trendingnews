Salma Hayek Posted Photos of Herself Skinny Dipping to Celebrate Turning 60

TheTools · 09.09.2026 11:00 · source · permalink

By Nadia Santiago

Updated 2 hours ago, September 9, 2026

She captioned it “60 and grateful.” Salma Hayek marked the milestone by stripping down for a skinny dip, no filter and no fuss.

Hayek shared the naked shots on September 7, one showing her lying in shallow water biting a finger, another catching her from behind as she floats.

She’s spent years pushing back on the idea that aging shrinks your life. “I thought getting older meant I wasn’t going to work; I’m working,” she told Glamour in 2023. “I thought getting older maybe meant that you’re not in love anymore; I’m in love.”

She’s been married to French billionaire François-Henri Pinault since 2009, and their blended family includes her daughter Valentina and his three kids. In July, the couple became grandparents for the first time when Pinault’s son and his wife welcomed a baby boy.

Hayek posted an August video talking to the newborn in Spanish, saying she’d call him Panchito, “little Frank.” Her line under it: “I cannot love him more.”
