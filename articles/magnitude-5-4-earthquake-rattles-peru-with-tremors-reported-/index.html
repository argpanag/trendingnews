LIMA, Peru - A magnitude 5.4 earthquake struck off the coast of Peru on Monday, with tremors felt in the capital city of Lima, according to the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS).

Officials said the earthquake struck about 59 miles away from Changuillo, Peru, which is located to the south of Lima, at a depth of roughly 6.2 miles. It happened at 11:15 a.m. local time.

UNDERSTANDING THE PACIFIC RING OF FIRE: WHERE EARTHQUAKES AND VOLCANOES MEET

Peru is located along the Pacific Ring of Fire, which is known as one of the most active regions in the world. The USGS said this region is where the Pacific Plate meets many surrounding tectonic plates.
