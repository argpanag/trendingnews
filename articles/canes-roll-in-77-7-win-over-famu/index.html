Canes Roll in 77-7 Win over FAMU

After opening the season more than 2,500 miles from home, the Hurricanes returned to South Florida and notched a big win in their home opener against Florida A&M.

MIAMI GARDENS, Fla. – After opening the season more than 2,500 miles from home, the Hurricanes made it clear in the days leading up to their home opener: they couldn’t wait to get back to South Florida to play in front of their family, their friends, and their fans.

And once the ball was kicked off on Thursday night, the seventh-ranked Hurricanes wasted little time putting on a show for those who made their way to Hard Rock Stadium on a muggy South Florida evening.

Miami was efficient on offense, scoring on 11 of its first 12 possessions. The defense, meanwhile, put together another dominant showing and in the end, the Hurricanes notched a 77-7 win over Florida A&M.

The Hurricanes tied a school record with their 77 points, matching their efforts against Savannah State in both 2013 and 2018. They set a new school record with 856 total yards and set a new ACC record with 42 first downs.

And with the victory, the Hurricanes have now won 20 straight home openers and have won eight straight games against FCS opponents by at least 40 points. That’s the longest such active streak among FBS programs.

"I thought our guys came out, and we played efficiently and improved on some things as it relates to the physicality, execution, [kept the] ball secured until the end … better at the front seven, [and we] played a bunch of guys,” Miami head coach Mario Cristobal said. “When the twos and threes went in, we felt that they sustained a relatively decent level of execution and standard.

"We had an opportunity to play a lot of guys and see how they fit in and what their roles can be going forward. And nowadays in college football, that’s invaluable. You have to play guys, and you really can’t look at the clock or the scoreboard. You just have to continue developing your roster, which I think we attempted to do tonight.”

Quarterback Darian Mensah – who earned a trio of national honors after Miami’s 45-6 win at Stanford last week, including a Davey O’Brien Great 8 nod – picked up right where he left off against the Cardinal, completing 14 of 15 passes for 252 yards with three touchdowns.

Wide receiver Malachi Toney, named the AP National Player of the Week after his record-setting performance at Stanford, continued his hot start to the season with a team-high four catches for 96 yards with a touchdown, and running back Mark Fletcher Jr. had seven carries for 105 yards and two scores.

Miami held FAMU to 107 yards, including just 37 yards on the ground, continuing a trend of defensive dominance against the run.

The Hurricanes entered Thursday’s game having held opponents to an average of just 87.9 rushing yards per game at Hard Rock Stadium since the start of the 2023 season and after facing the Rattlers, that number will just continue to drop.

Abd while FAMU may have struggled to run the ball, the Hurricanes did not.

It was Fletcher who put Miami on the board first, scoring on a 46-yard run that capped a 3-play, 59-yard scoring drive on the Hurricanes’ first possession.

Fletcher would add another touchdown – a 1-yard run – later in the first quarter to push Miami’s lead to 21-0 after Jake Weinberg’s extra point.

Fellow running backs Girard Pringle Jr. and Javian Mallory also scored in the win, with Mallory – one of several freshmen who logged significant minutes Thursday – notching the first touchdown of his Miami career on 11-yard run in the second quarter. Mallory added another touchdown – on a 52-yard run – in the third.

He finished with a game-high 10 carries for another game-high 142 yards.

"It's just a blessing being able to get in the game while being in the best running back room in the country," Mallory said. "I feel like the older guys have all helped me from my first day here during the playoffs. They have all [taken] me under their wing, and they've guided me to get where I am today. I'm just very thankful for everybody.”

Mensah and fellow quarterbacks Luke Nickel and Dereon Coleman combined for 426 passing yards and six touchdowns and delivered the ball to 16 different pass catchers.

On the other side of the ball, JJ Dunnigan led the Hurricanes with a team-high five tackles, while Armondo Blount posted four stops. Both Marquise Lightfoot and Herbert Scroggins III collected sacks.
