Time to sign off – I’ll leave you with Sid Lowe’s report. Thanks for joining me; until next time, it’s adios.

Tomorrow night, the Ballon d’Or will be handed out in London. It’s long been framed as a race between Harry Kane and Lamine Yamal, but Kylian Mbappé’s odds have shortened today. He’s not going to win it this year, but if I could choose one player on earth to join my club, then on balance it would have to be Mbappé.

Here’s how the La Liga table stands – and it would be remiss of me not to mention Atlético, firmly in this title race after 10 games.

Full time: Barcelona 1-2 Real Madrid

Barcelona’s perfect start ran for nine games, but it’s been shattered by – yes – a José Mourinho masterclass. Kylian Mbappé struck on the break in both halves to earn a statement victory for Real Madrid, who are now just three points behind the league leaders.

95 mins: We play on, and Lamine Yamal almost picks out Gabriel Jesus with a cross … but Real Madrid break away again and Balde is booked for a reckless foul on Mbappe.

94 mins: Cucurella and Mbappe are booked in quick succession, presumably for dissent. Juan Martinez is dishing out a few yellow cards after a relatively quiet game.

93 mins: Two time-sapping substitutions from Mourinho, with Carlos Espi and Trent Alexander-Arnold replacing Diomande and Arda Guler.

92 mins: Lamine Yamal goes over under pressure from Cucurella on the edge of the area, but the referee waves him up. Adeyemi then powers in a cross which Lunin punches away from danger.

“I’m kicking myself for not putting ‘Performs knee slide’ on the Mou bingo card,” sighs Peter Oh.

90 mins: The stadium PA announces five extra minutes as a shell-shocked Barcelona search for a second equaliser.

It’s a second goal for Kylian Mbappé, created by a superb piece of skill from Bellingham to set Camavinga away. He gets beyond the weary Cubarsi and cuts back beyond Garcia, allowing Mbappé to roll the ball into an empty net. Mourinho knee-slides down the touchline in jubilation. He’s back!

GOAL! Barcelona 1-2 Real Madrid (Mbappé 87')

With time running out, Real Madrid unleash a lethal counter attack – and they may have just snatched all three points here!

85 mins: Karim Adeyemi and Gabriel Jesus are on, replacing Gordon and goalscorer Raphinha.

84 mins: Gordon tackles Guler to stop a potential Real Madrid break, and isn’t best pleased when he’s booked. A rare bad call from tonight’s referee.

83 mins: To return to my earlier, slightly tenuous boxing comparison, Real Madrid are on the ropes – but they could still find a knockout blow with Mbappe and Bellingham waiting to pounce on the break.

82 mins: Gordon plays a cute pass in to Lamine’s path, but his shot is blocked by Cucurella, who followed him for a good 20 yards there.

81 mins: Lamine Yamal, now suddenly everywhere, whips a shot across goal but it is blocked, inadvertently, by Dean Huijsen’s bonce.

80 mins: De Jong’s snap-shot is closed down, and Real Madrid deal with the subsequent corner. Bernardo Silva’s race is run; he’s replaced by Eduardo Camavinga.

78 mins: At the other end, Lamine Yamal goes close again, dancing beyond his marker and pounding an effort wide with the outside of his boot.

76 mins: Another Real break, although the first for a while, is led by Mbappe and ends with Gerard Martin expertly tackling Bellingham. He and Cubarsi have been left to fend for themselves in the heart of Barça’s defence.

An email from Colin Fordham, on this intriguing clash of styles. “After Mbappé’s rather lucky goal and the impressive Mourinho-inspired defensive effort of Real Madrid with everyone, Bellingham included, pressing Barca’s players to the nth degree, it was refreshing to see pure football. Pedri’s feint and pass for Rafinha, and the Brazilian’s finish, were sublime.”

75 mins: Dumfries looks for Mbappe over the top, but Garcia is off his line to deal with the through ball.

73 mins: Almost a moment of Lamine Yamal magic as he plays a cross-shot with the outside of his boot that just evades Raphinha at the far post. Barcelona make another change, with Frenkie De Jong replacing Fermin Lopez.

72 mins: Bernardo Silva is penalised for a foul in midfield. Barça looked the more leggy team for the first hour, but Real Madrid are beginning to tire.

71 mins: Arda Guler has another go from the edge of the area, a low fizzer that Garcia gets behind. I’m not sure if Guler is exciting, frustrating or a mixture of the two.

70 mins: Still, I reckon José might see it differently.

69 mins: Balde scrambles the corner away, but Mbappe appeals for handball. The ball definitely hit Balde’s arm, before passing under it, but it would be very harsh to give that with both players jostling each other.

68 mins: Dumfries steams down the right and his underhit cross towards Mbappe is cleared behind by Balde. Real Madrid get a corner …

66 mins: The xG for this game is currently at 0.96-0.86 in Barcelona’s favour. So, 1-1 seems a fair scoreline.

65 mins: Big save! Barça win another corner, met by Gerard Martin with a header six yards out – but Lunin claws it off the line to keep the scores level!

63 mins: A first change for the visitors, with Denzel Dumfries replacing Fede Valverde at right-back. Arda Guler takes aim from a distance, forcing Garcia into an awkward save-clearance combo.

GOAL! Barcelona 1-1 Real Madrid (Raphinha 61')

It’s played short, and Pedri plays a lovely reverse pass inside for Raphinha, who fires an effort through Bellingham’s legs. Lunin gets a hand to his effort, but it has too much power. After a torrid first hour, Barcelona are level!

60 mins: Gordon pings a low ball across the area, which Cucurella clears behind with undue haste. And from the corner …

59 mins: It looks like Diomande’s calf problem may have been caused by Joao Cancelo’s challenge, which is better news than a muscle injury.
