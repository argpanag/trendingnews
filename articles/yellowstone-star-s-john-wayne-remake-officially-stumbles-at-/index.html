Angel recently attempted a mainstream expansion with the action comedy Runner, starring Alan Ritchson and Owen Wilson. That film grossed around $15 million domestically against a reported budget of $45 million, and it seems like the new Angel release is going to fare even worse in its theatrical run.

The film stars No Country for Old Men icon Tommy Lee Jones, alongside Shazam! star Zachary Levi and Yellowstone alum Neal McDonough. The original's premise, about a rugged man being nursed by a god-fearing community, also influenced a 2003 television film starring Naomi Watts, and more famously, inspired the Harrison Ford-led mystery movie Witness. The new version was directed by Julio Quintana, and it continues Angel Studios' attempts to go mainstream by making genre movies with a religious undercurrent. We're talking about the aptly titled Western film Angel and the Badman. The movie received poor reviews upon release this week, and it is now sitting at a 23% score on the aggregator website Rotten Tomatoes. Like most movies aimed at religious audiences, however, it seems to have done well with its target demographic. Angel and the Badman received a B+ grade on CinemaScore, making it the top-rated movie of the week on the survey platform. This didn't, however, translate into box-office returns.
