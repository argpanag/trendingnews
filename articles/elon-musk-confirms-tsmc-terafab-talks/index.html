Elon Musk Confirms TSMC-Terafab Talks

TheTools · 03.10.2026 23:44 · source · permalink

[Exclusive] There’s more to be said on TSMC’s Texas exploration and Terafab goals

Good Evening from Taipei,

SpaceX and Terafab founder Elon Musk today confirmed my story on talks between TSMC and Terafab.

Late Friday I reported exclusively that TSMC was exploring ways to work with SpaceX’s Terafab project. “The most likely scenario is for TSMC to try to own and operate the new factory,” I wrote. “These plans are not set in stone, with other variations on the cooperation also being considered.”

Exclusive: TSMC Exploring Plans to Work With Elon Musk’s Terafab

“Just discussions, but something may come of it,” Musk wrote on X a few hours later.

Musk was guarded in his confirmation. He should be, and I can understand why.

He’s dealing with many competing issues when it comes to founding and scaling a hugely ambitious semiconductor project. Choosing a partner is very big decision, and he’d be wise to not rule anything out, or in, until a deal is signed.

Since publication I have had numerous further conversations with people familiar with the project.

The story of how a TSMC-Terafab collab came to be on the table is fascinating, and one which I plan to share with you soon.

To that end, make sure you’re subscribed to Culpium because I’ll be delivering the fuller rundown in coming days.
