🚨XIs confirmed: Depleted Bayern visit the Black Forest

FC Bayern Munich, top of the table with 14 points after thrashing RB Leipzig 6-1, visit third-placed SC Freiburg, who have 13 points, this Sunday on Matchday 7 of the Bundesliga.

The Bavarian side head into the match under Aaron Danks following Vincent Kompany's hospitalization, with key players including Musiala, Luis Díaz, Neuer, Minjae Kim and Kimmich unavailable. They face Julian Schuster's side, who have won all five of their home matches this season and are determined to bounce back from their recent defeats and claim top spot in German football.
