Release date announced for Hollywood blockbuster filmed in Suffolk

TheTools · 18.10.2026 22:53 · source · permalink

The name and release date of a Hollywood blockbuster filmed in Suffolk have been announced.

Brendan Fraser was spotted at MOD Woodbridge in September, filming scenes for the fourth film in The Mummy franchise.

Fraser is returning as Rick O'Connell, an American adventurer, treasure hunter and highly decorated First World War veteran.

Rachel Weisz is also returning to the series as Egyptologist Evelyn Carnahan.

It has now been revealed that The Mummy: The Book of the Dead will be released on October 15 next year.

During filming in Suffolk, Fraser was seen taking part in a record-speed trails sequence.

He wore a helmet and climbed behind the wheel of a silver-and-red rocket car.

The 57-year-old actor was wearing a black leather jacket and matching trousers.

It is not known whether any more scenes have been filmed in Suffolk.

They will be joined by John Hannah as Jonathan Carnahan, Evelyn's older brother.

John Hannah is also set to return (Image: PA)

The Scottish actor provided an update on the long-awaited fourth instalment during an appearance on Lorraine Kelly's ITV programme.

Speaking about returning to the franchise, Mr Hannah said: "Yeah, we've started now, we're a few weeks in.

"It's really lovely and it's extraordinary.

"The first one was, what, 28 years ago or something? But it's extraordinary how we've just kind of fallen back into it."

Hannah also joked about reuniting with co-star Weisz, who last appeared alongside him in The Mummy Returns.

According to IMDB, other original cast members are expected to include Oded Ferh as Ardeth Bay and Kevin J. O'Connor as Beni Gabor.

Arnold Vosloo is also rumoured to be returning as Imhotep.

Michael Johnston, who recently starred as Bear in Obsession, is rumoured to be joining the cast.
