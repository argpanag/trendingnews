☀️ David-Sorloth, Inter eye 2010 & Gonçalves on Amorim: AM news

Serie A returns today: Gasperini welcomes back four starters for the Parma clash, Lazio have Frattesi back and Fiorentina hope to have Njie available against Monza.

Meanwhile, the winter transfer window is approaching: Juventus and Atletico Madrid are working on a David-Sorloth swap, while Napoli are tracking Kovacic, also a target for Milan and Juventus. Inter are monitoring youngster Leon Da Silva, while Gonçalves reveals the background to his failed move to Milan.

OneFootball Videos

Let's take a look at the morning's biggest stories.

Juventus, David-Sorloth: a cash adjustment is needed

According to La Gazzetta dello Sport, Juventus and Atletico Madrid are continuing talks over a swap involving Jonathan David and Alexander Sorloth. Both strikers are reportedly open to the move, but the two clubs have yet to reach a financial agreement.

Juventus would like a straight swap for Sorloth or even a small cash payment in exchange for David, given the five-year age gap between the players and the Canadian's contract running until 2030. Atletico Madrid, however, want David plus cash to let the Norwegian go.

Milan, Gonçalves: "I waited for Milan because of Amorim"

Speaking to Record, Pedro Gonçalves revealed that he waited for Milan over the summer in the hope of reuniting with Ruben Amorim, his former coach at Sporting. The midfielder, who subsequently joined Fiorentina, praised the Portuguese coach, explaining that he had learned a lot from him tactically.

On Milan's interest, he said: "Did the chance to join Ruben Amorim make me wait a little longer before deciding my future? To be honest, yes. I waited a little longer because that possibility had been put to me. I waited as long as necessary, but in the end the opportunity didn't materialise. Anyway, I'm here at Fiorentina and, as I've already said, I'm really happy."

Inter interested in youngster Leon Da Silva

According to Gianlucadimarzio.com, Inter are reportedly interested in Leon Da Silva, Malmö's centre-back born in 2010, who is already close to making his first-team debut despite being just 16.

Milan and Torino are also reportedly tracking the player, who also holds Brazilian citizenship, with a view to bringing him into their youth academies. Da Silva currently plays for the Swedish club's Under-17s, having also featured for their Under-15 and Under-16 sides.

Napoli, Kovacic a summer target

According to The Sun, Napoli are reportedly interested in Mateo Kovacic, the Manchester City midfielder who is not expected to renew his contract, which expires in June 2027.

The Croatian is reportedly considering a return to Serie A, with Milan and Juventus also interested. The main stumbling block would be the high wages of the midfielder, born in 1994, who previously played in Italy for Inter between 2013 and 2015.

Roma, Gasperini has almost everyone back for Parma

As reported by La Gazzetta dello Sport, Gasperini has Svilar, Mancini, Koné and Dybala back for the away match against Parma. Gollini is also available again, with Rensch and Pellegrini the only players still unavailable.

Dybala is expected to return to the starting line-up behind Malen alongside Rodrigo Mora, while Soulé could start on the bench following international duty with Argentina.

Lazio, Doekhi and Diogo Leite favourites to start in defence

According to LaLazioSiamoNoi.it, Gattuso is expected to field Doekhi and Diogo Leite at centre-back, with Provstgaard starting on the bench. Sutalo, Marusic, Tavares and Zaccagni are unavailable, while Gudmundsson has recovered but is expected to start on the bench.

Frattesi has overcome the slight muscle fatigue he experienced on international duty and will start alongside Belahyane and Taylor. Isaksen, Pinamonti and Noslin retain their places in attack. There are some doubts over Cataldi, who is dealing with a muscle problem, while Rovella is expected to start on the bench.

Fiorentina, Njie closing in on return. Atta still a doubt

According to FirenzeViola.it, Alieu Njie has returned to partial training with the squad following a thigh problem. His condition is improving, and his inclusion in the squad for Monday's trip to Monza looks increasingly likely.

As reported by Corriere dello Sport, Arthur Atta is recovering, but his availability against Monza remains in doubt. After around two months out, Fiorentina want to manage the midfielder's return carefully to avoid any setbacks.
