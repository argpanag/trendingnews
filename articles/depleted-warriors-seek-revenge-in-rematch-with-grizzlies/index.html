Depleted Warriors seek revenge in rematch with Grizzlies

TheTools · 20.11.2026 02:08 · source · permalink

Golden State has dropped four straight since Stephen Curry tore an oblique, and Draymond Green will sit for rest as Memphis aims for another back-to-back sweep.

The Golden State Warriors hope to continue a run of successful revenge games when they get a road rematch with the Memphis Grizzlies on Thursday night.

Both will be playing the second night of a back-to-back, with the Warriors coming off a 116-94 road loss to the Dallas Mavericks and the Grizzlies following up a 124-121 road win over the Cleveland Cavaliers.

The clubs met in San Francisco the first week of the season, a game Golden State led 106-94 with 3:47 to play before Memphis scored 17 straight points for a 111-108 win. A four-point play by Jaylen Wells triggered the run, which included six points apiece from Cameron Boozer and Ty Jerome.

The game featured an early showdown between Boozer and fellow rookie standout Yaxel Lendeborg. Boozer won the scoring duel 25-18 in a matchup that was otherwise even, with both finishing with 13 rebounds and six assists.

Lendeborg had 13 points, five rebounds and four assists in Wednesday's loss at Dallas.

It didn't prevent a fourth straight loss on a trip that began with Stephen Curry suffering a right oblique tear in the first quarter at Houston last Wednesday.

Already down Jimmy Butler III, Kristaps Porzingis and Moses Moody, Warriors coach Steve Kerr elected to give Al Horford the night off in the back-to-back opener. He later said that while Horford is expected to return for the Memphis game, Draymond Green will get the load-management treatment.

"We recognize the circumstances right now; you can't fault the effort," Kerr said. "It's the NBA. It's an unforgiving league. Got another game (Thursday). Quick turnaround. Let's find a way to win."

The Warriors did exactly that the previous two times they faced an opponent that had beaten them in an earlier meeting. The Los Angeles Lakers and Phoenix Suns were the victims on those occasions.

While the Warriors have had success exacting revenge, the Grizzlies have excelled on the second night of back-to-backs. They have had two previous consecutive-day sequences this season, winning on the second night both times.

Like their current situation, the Grizzlies' most recent back-to-back featured a home game following a road outing. They captured both decisions last week when they won at New Orleans, then handled the Brooklyn Nets the next night at home.

Jerome, who had 28 points in the earlier win over the Warriors, missed his third straight game Wednesday in Cleveland with a sore hip. He and Isaiah Stewart (illness) will be game-time decisions against Golden State.

Taylor Hendricks, getting increased playing time off the bench with Stewart out of action, got a shout-out from Grizzlies coach Tuomas Iisalo after an 18-point effort against the Cavaliers.

"It's been great to see him blossom," Iisalo said. "He's been one of the most positive, bought-in guys ever since he came in from Utah. He wasn't playing early in the year. Now, because of injuries or foul trouble or guys needing a rest, he's getting his opportunity, and he's used it to the maximum."
