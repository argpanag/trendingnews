Ether Rises 1.22% in One Hour to $2,366 on Binance Spot Market | TokenPost

TheTools · 28.10.2026 18:40 · source · permalink

TokenPost’s hourly tally showed $7.27 million in short liquidations and $60,000 in long liquidations across three exchanges.

  • ETH Ethereum
  • USDT Tether

Ether (ETH) climbed 1.22% to $2,366 in the hour ending at 2:38 p.m. ET (18:38 UTC) Oct. 28, in TokenPost’s collection of Binance spot ETH/USDT prices.

At the start of the interval, ETH was $2,338, a $28 difference from the final quote. The reported 24-hour change was a gain of 0.75%. TokenPost collected public Binance spot prices and calculated the changes at the stated observation time.

A separate TokenPost aggregation of hourly liquidations across Binance, Bybit and OKX showed $7.27 million in short liquidations and $60,000 in long liquidations. The totals cover the same hour ending at 2:38 p.m. ET (18:38 UTC). Short liquidations exceeded long liquidations by $7.21 million.

The hourly price gain was larger than ETH’s 0.75% advance over 24 hours at the snapshot. The figures reflect one hour of Binance spot pricing and liquidation activity aggregated across the three exchanges.
