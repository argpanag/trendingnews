Shane Steichen defends decisions to kick off, kick field goal in overtime

TheTools · 21.09.2026 08:38 · source · permalink

Colts coach Shane Steichen made twice chose to kick in Sunday night’s overtime loss to the Chiefs. Questioned about those decisions after the game, Steichen defended his process even though his team lost the game.

Steichen’s two choices came when the Colts won the coin toss and chose to kick off to start overtime, and then when the Colts settled for a game-tying field goal rather than trying to score a game-winning touchdown at the end of their only overtime possession.

On the choice to kick off after winning the toss, Steichen said he didn’t want to score first because it would be too easy for Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes to move the ball down the field if the Chiefs knew they were going to go for it on every fourth down.

“If we go first with it, Mahomes gets four downs going the other way, so we wanted to play defense there and know we had four downs,” Steichen said. “It can go either way in those situations, but we decided to kick off there, and we had a chance at the end. Didn’t finish it.”

On the Chiefs’ opening possession, they kicked a field goal. That meant on the Colts’ possession, a touchdown would win the game, while a field goal would tie it and give the ball back to the Chiefs. The Colts marched down the field and faced a fourth-and-8 at the Chiefs’ 15-yard line, and Steichen initially kept his offense on the field, appearing ready to go for the win. But after seeing how the Chiefs’ defense lined up, Steichen told his offense to take a delay of game penalty and settled for a field goal.

“It was fourth-and-8, had a play called, had to love the look to run it there, and if not, we took the delay of game there and kicked the field goal there to tie it,” Steichen said.

That tied the game 30-30, and Mahomes proceeded to move the Chiefs into field goal range, where Harrison Butker’s kick gave Kansas City the 33-30 win. Steichen thinks he made the right choices, even if he doesn’t like the result.
