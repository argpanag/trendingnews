Gervonta Davis Targets George Kambosos Jr. For Chicago Return

TheTools · 14.11.2026 20:00 · source · permalink

Gervonta “Tank” Davis says he plans to return in Chicago in February or March against George Kambosos Jr., a matchup designed to ease him back into action after nearly two years away. On paper, it looks like a fight Davis should win without much trouble, though his extended layoff and unresolved legal issues make his actual form a serious question mark.

“Fuck it, they taking too long for the flyer..me and kam fighting in Chicago,” Davis wrote on X, adding “Like feb or march” in a follow-up post. Kambosos had already confirmed that both sides were negotiating, making him the clear target. Neither fighter has shared an exact date, venue, or confirmation that his active probation warrant in Maryland and criminal charges in Florida have been cleared for licensing.

Davis has not fought since his March 2025 majority draw with Lamont Roach Jr., where Roach forced him to work for openings and absorbed his clean shots without folding. Even at his peak, Davis could give away early rounds because his power was enough to erase an opponent’s early lead in a single exchange. Against Roach, that fight-ending punch never landed, and by next spring, Davis will have spent close to two years outside the ring.

Kambosos enters with plenty of wear of his own. The 33-year-old former unified lightweight champion has been sidelined since Richardson Hitchins stopped him in the eighth round in June 2025. He has gone 2-4 since upsetting Teofimo Lopez in November 2021, dropping two decisions to Devin Haney alongside stoppage losses to Vasiliy Lomachenko and Hitchins.

Kambosos looked limited long before Hitchins broke him down. While he dropped Lopez in the first round and survived a tenth-round knockdown to pull off his career-defining win, he has not beaten an elite fighter since. An early Davis knockout would give the Chicago crowd what it paid to see, but the real measure of how much Tank has left will come if Kambosos survives into the championship rounds and forces him to box for points.
