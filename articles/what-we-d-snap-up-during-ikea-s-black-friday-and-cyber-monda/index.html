What We'd Snap Up During IKEA's Black Friday and Cyber Monday Sale

TheTools · 27.11.2026 07:28 · source · permalink

We may earn revenue from the products available on this page and participate in affiliate programs.

If you’ve ever been to an IKEA, you’ve probably seen the advertisements for becoming an IKEA Family Member. You’ve also probably brushed it off and gone about your shopping. We’re here to tell you: get the perks. It’s free to join the retailer’s loyalty program and, while there are year-round rewards (a complimentary coffee every time you visit, 90-day price protection policy for returns), it also means you get to participate in Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales. Now through November 30, IKEA Family and Business Network members can save 10% for every $100 you spend on qualifying items (with a max savings of $100). Here’s what we’d buy.

MOSSJÖN Glass-Door Cabinet

IKEA

MOSSJÖN Glass-door cabinet

Meet the ultimate cabinet of curiosities. Whether used to store skincare in a bathroom or dishes in the kitchen, the fluted doors will disguise any sign of clutter and give your space a vintage-inspired feel at the same time.

STRANDMON Wing Chair with Ottoman

IKEA

STRANDMON Wing Chair

One thing all of our favorite Swedish home tours have in common? A super-comfy, patterned armchair. This gingham set is ready for a reading nook.

IKEA

RAMNEFJÄLL Upholstered Bed Frame

With its washable slipcover and delicate stripe pattern, this upholstered bed frame is giving us serious Nancy Meyers vibes.

IKEA

This low-pile, 5’x7’ rug comes in a range of solid hues, so you can work it into any existing palette. Pro tip for making this blush pink find look more expensive than it is: drench the walls around it in a matching paint color.
