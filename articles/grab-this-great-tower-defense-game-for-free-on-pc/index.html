Epic Games has just updated the “Free Games” section of the Epic Games Store with another title you can pick up completely for free.

For this week, the company is giving away the unique turn-based Tower Defense game, Knightica, released last year by the development team at Mad Mango Games.

This is a pure single-player title where players command an army and fight battles while building their own deck within a “Roguelike autobattle setting.”

You are tasked with building your army by recruiting various units and positioning them on the battlefield to maximize their abilities. Additionally, units can utilize synergies, be upgraded, and learn various spells. The core strategy lies in army preparation and finding the right combinations, as combat operates on an Auto Battler system rather than direct control of individual soldiers.
