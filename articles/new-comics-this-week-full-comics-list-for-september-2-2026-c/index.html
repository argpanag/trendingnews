New Comics This Week: Full Comics List For September 2, 2026 | Comic Book Club

TheTools · 31.08.2026 11:30 · source · permalink
  • News

New month, new comics, let’s goooo!! And this is a particularly great week with a slew of new books coming at you for September 2, 2026. So let’s get into some highlights for what’s coming to comic book stores, then the full list of single issues.

DC has a packed week led by three Next Level titles: Legion of Super-Heroes #1, Doom Patrol #1 and Teen Titans #1. That would be more than enough (they’re all be crackling good teams), but they’ve also got Superman: The Stranger #1, a Black Label book by Wes Craig going back to the Man of Steel’s origin. And also there’s Batman/Superman/Weird Al: World’s Weirdest, which sends Al to save Batman and Superman.

Marvel, meanwhile, celebrates a few anniversaries with the Spider-Woman 50th Anniversary Special, featuring a new story by Ann Nocenti and one by Dan Watters setting up the future. Marvel Mangaverse: Web of Blood #1 kicks off a month-long event celebrating the 25th anniversary of the Mangaverse. And Black Panther / Namor: Doomed #1 sends three kings to hell.

Speaking of which, BOOM! Studios opens the puzzle box with Hellraiser: Resurrections – New Wounds #1. Dark Horse Comics expands the origin of the title character in The Perfectly Monstrous Life of Adam Frankenstein #1 from Tate Brombal and Soo Lee. And Oni Press brings on a brand new Archie #1.

Looking for previous lists? Check out our New Comics This Week archive for back-lists of comics, so you can get caught up quickly.
