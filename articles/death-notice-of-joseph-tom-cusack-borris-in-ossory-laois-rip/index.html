The death has occurred of

Joseph (Tom) Cusack, Ballykevan, Knockaroo, Borris-in-Ossory, Co. Laois.

 

Joseph (Tom) passed away September 16th 2026, peacefully, at the Residence Nursing Home, Portlaoise, aged 103 years.

 

Predeceased by his wife Margaret (née Comerford).

 

Deeply regretted and will be sadly missed by his son Joseph (Joe), daughter Concepta, nieces, nephews, Joe's partner Mary, Concepta's partner Fergal, relatives, neighbours and friends.

 

“May Tom Rest in Peace”

 

Reposing at Guilfoyle's Funeral Home, Castletown, (Eircode R32TN84) this Friday evening (Sept 18th) from 5pm to 7pm concluding with prayers at 7pm.

 

Funeral Mass this Saturday morning (Sept 19th) at 11am in St. Kieran's Church, Knockaroo, (Eircode R32F886), followed by burial in the Church grounds.

 

Family Flowers only Please.

House Private Please.

 

The Cusack Family would like to extend their sincere thanks to the doctors, nurses and staff of St. Luke's Hospital, Kilkenny and the Midland's Regional Hospital, Portlaoise for the wonderful care and kindness shown to Joseph (Tom).
