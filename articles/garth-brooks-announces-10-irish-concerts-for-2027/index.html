Garth Brooks announces 10 Irish concerts for 2027

TheTools · 05.11.2026 08:08 · source · permalink

Getty

Country music superstar Garth Brooks has announced 10 concerts across five Irish cities for 2027.

The singer will perform two shows each in Belfast, Galway, Kilkenny, Limerick and Cork next May and June.

The tour will begin at Boucher Road Playing Fields in Belfast on Friday 21 and Saturday 22 May, before moving to Pearse Stadium in Galway on Saturday 29 and Sunday 30 May.

Brooks will then perform at UPMC Nowlan Park in Kilkenny on Friday 4 and Saturday 5 June, followed by TUS Gaelic Grounds in Limerick on Friday 11 and Saturday 12 June.

The final two concerts will take place at SuperValu Páirc Uí Chaoimh in Cork on Friday 18 and Saturday 19 June.

Speaking about his Irish tour Brooks said: "This has been a dream of mine since the first time we played Ireland. Once you get a taste of how the Irish love music, you can't play there enough!"

Tickets for all 10 concerts go on sale on Thursday 12 November at 8am.

Brooks last performed in Ireland in September 2022, when he played five sold-out concerts at Dublin's Croke Park, with more than 400,000 tickets sold.

The country music superstar took to the stage on 9, 10, 11, 16 and 17 September, marking his first Irish concerts in 25 years, having previously performed at Croke Park in 1997.

The hugely anticipated shows came eight years after his planned five-night run at the Dublin stadium was cancelled in 2014. Dublin City Council had granted licences for just three of the five concerts, prompting Brooks to cancel the entire run.

Brooks is on his Blame It All On My Roots arena tour, which began in the United States in August. The tour marks a return to the indoor arenas where he performed during the height of his success in the 1990s.

Earlier this year, the 64-year-old headlined a sold-out concert at London’s Hyde Park on 27 June.

The singer also completed a two-year residency at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace in Las Vegas in March 2025, having performed 72 shows at the venue.

Brooks is also preparing to release a vinyl edition of his best-selling live album Double Live on Friday, 6 November. Originally released in 1998, the album features recordings from his world tour between 1996 and 1998.
