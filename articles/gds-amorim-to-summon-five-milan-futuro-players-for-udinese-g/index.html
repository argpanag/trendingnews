GdS: Amorim to summon five Milan Futuro players for Udinese game amid injury crisis

TheTools · 25.10.2026 12:30 · source · permalink

The injury crisis that AC Milan are battling will see Ruben Amorim summon five players from Milan Futuro, a report claims.

Milan need to bounce back after the 3-0 defeat to Bournemouth in midweek, but their task has already been made harder. On the eve of the Udinese game, it was confirmed by Milan that the key players Mario Gila, Adrien Rabiot and Diego Moreira will all not make the trip to the Stadio Friuli.

With Matteo Gabbia, Alexis Saelemaekers, Filippo Terracciano and Samu Chukwueze all also absent, Amorim has drafted in some players from Milan Futuro to address the injury crisis ahead of the match against Udinese.

La Gazzetta dello Sport (seen below) write that the chosen ones are as follows: Luca Menon (right winger), Nirash Perera (left winger), Silvano Vos (ex-Ajax midfielder), Lorenzo Ossola (attacking midfielder) and El Hadji Malick Cissé (central defender).

Ossola and Vos are two players who saw a bit of first team action during preseason, while the others are players who have done enough to stand out with Sergio Navarro’s side in Serie D so far.

The issue with this is that the Futuro have a game against Club Milano today, so the head coach Navarro will be without some of his key men, such is the knock-on impact of the injury crisis above.
