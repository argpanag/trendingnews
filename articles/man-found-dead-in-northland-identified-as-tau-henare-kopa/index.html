Man found dead in Northland identified as Tau Henare Kopa

TheTools · 24.09.2026 14:47 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Regions

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._g.pKhUMkRnoOZwX4iq2fhLAqba5Rz0jb3mINKRqASmxf0/regions/1552943%2Fman-found-dead-in-northland-identified-as-tau-henare-kopa

The identity of a man found dead at a property in Waiomio earlier this month has been revealed.

Police on Thursday said he was Tau Henare Kopa, 35, of Kawakawa, Northland.

They were still working to establish what happened, Detective Inspector Michele Gillespie said.

His remains were found on the afternoon of 7 September.

"At this stage his death is still being treated as unexplained and our enquiries remain ongoing.

"Police extend our condolences to his family and loved ones at this incredibly difficult time."

A post-mortem had been carried out, but forensic testing was ongoing.
