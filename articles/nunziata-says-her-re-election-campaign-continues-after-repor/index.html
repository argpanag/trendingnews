Nunziata says her re-election campaign continues after report says she is dropping out | CBC News

TheTools · 09.10.2026 20:43 · source · permalink

Frances Nunziata has been a councillor for South York–Weston since 1997

Longtime city councillor Frances Nunziata says her "campaign remains unchanged" after a news report said she is dropping out of the municipal race.

"I do not comment on rumours. I continue to canvass. My campaign remains unchanged," she said in a statement issued by her campaign Friday.

CBC has reached out to Nunziata for comment.

Nunziata has been a mainstay in Toronto politics for decades, representing the South York-Weston area since the city's amalgamation at the end of the 1990s.

Before that, she was the mayor of York for three years, and before that a councillor in the municipality.

Nunziata has also served as the speaker of Toronto council since 2010, chairing debate among councillors for more than 15 years.

During the last municipal election, Nunziata came close to losing her seat.

Chiara Padovani — who is running again this year — was 94 votes short of unseating Nunziata.
