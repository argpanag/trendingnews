WATCH: Driver hits 100mph and goes wrong way around roundabout in police chase

TheTools · 25.11.2026 22:13 · source · permalink

A driver reached speeds of up to 100mph and drove the wrong way around a roundabout during a police chase.

Ellis Rabey-Prosser, 18, from Mildenhall, has been banned from driving after the pursuit.

He appeared at Norwich Magistrates’ Court on Monday and pleaded guilty to a string of offences including dangerous driving, failing to stop for police, failing to report a collision, fraudulently using a number plate, driving while disqualified, driving without insurance and failing to stop following a collision.

He crashed into a lamppost before fleeing the scene (Image: Norfolk Constabulary)

The offences relate to an incident on October 6, when police received a 999 call around 8pm reporting a blue Vauxhall Insignia being driven dangerously on the A47 near Hockering.

Officers located the vehicle and signalled for it to stop, but Rabey-Prosser failed to comply and a chase began.

The near 30-mile chase ended when the teen crashed into a lamppost at the Hardwick roundabout near King's Lynn.

He fled the scene on foot but was later found in a nearby supermarket and arrested nearly two hours after the chase began.

PC Richard Roe, the arresting officer, said: "The standard of driving shown by Rabey-Prosser was shocking.

"He ignored police, reached excessive speeds, drove the wrong way around a roundabout and ultimately crashed before trying to flee on foot.

"His reckless decisions endangered everyone using the road that evening.

"We would like to thank the member of the public whose report helped us quickly locate the vehicle and bring this incident to a safe conclusion."

During a search, he was found in possession of two black marker pens, which he had used to change the digit on his number plate.

He was sentenced to 12 months in prison, suspended for 24 months, and disqualified from driving for two years.

He was also ordered to pay £227 in fines and costs.
