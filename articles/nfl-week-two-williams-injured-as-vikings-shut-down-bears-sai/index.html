Phew what a funny old set of games. Quiet, quiet, quiet, LOUD. Those fourth quarters just burst into life. Except at Solidier Field when, just as Chicago looked like they would take a lead over Minnesota, Caleb Williams had to be carted off the field with a serious looking leg injury after falling without a defender touching him. Meanwhile the Vikings move to 2-0 with Carson Wentz at QB thanks to a massive day for their defense as they limited Chicago to just 163 passing yards and no touchdowns.
