Worlds Final Is First Without T1 Since 2021 After 3.6M Semi Peak

TheTools · 14.11.2026 18:27 · source · permalink

Gen.G and Bilibili Gaming meet in the League of Legends Worlds 2026 final at 2 p.m. ET Saturday at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, the first Worlds final without T1 since 2021.

(Photo by Bruce Bennett/Getty Images)

Gen.G and Bilibili Gaming meet in the League of Legends Worlds 2026 final at 2 p.m. ET Saturday at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, the first Worlds final without T1 since 2021.

The start time and venue were previously reported on the official LoL Esports schedule. Spilled.gg noted that this is the first final without T1 since 2021, and that every Worlds final from 2023 to 2025 featured T1 and peaked above 6 million viewers.

BLG-T1 Semifinal Set the Tournament Peak

Prensa Esports, citing Esports Charts, said the tournament peak so far is 3,605,295 concurrent viewers for Bilibili Gaming's 3-1 semifinal win over T1 on Nov. 8. The firm does not include certain Chinese platforms in that figure, the outlet said. The same source listed a 2,193,252 peak for Gen.G's semifinal sweep of Invictus Gaming.

Prensa Esports said those figures are estimates and can be revised.
