Mother of four of Musk’s children claims he ended relationship ‘with no warning’

TheTools · 03.10.2026 00:20 · source · permalink

I will miss him dearly, says Shivon Zilis after tech billionaire unfollowed her on X

The mother of four of Elon Musk’s children has claimed that their relationship ended “with no warning”.

Shivon Zilis, 40, said: “It’s hard to go from in love to let go in a week with no warning, but that’s just how it is sometimes.”

Mr Musk, 55, fathered four children with Ms Zilis over the past five years, all conceived via in-vitro fertilisation (IVF).

Ms Zilis, who is the director of operations and special projects at one of Musk’s companies Neuralink, posted on X on Friday: “I feel happy that I got to show Elon how it feels to be deeply understood, nurtured, and peacefully loved with stability and gentleness.

“I loved being that counterbalancing place of safety, sense of calm, and love – and delighted in bringing a smile back to his face when he was down. I will miss him dearly.”

Ms Zilis was responding to an X post, which showed Mr Musk had unfollowed her on the platform. 

She also posted a screenshot of a text exchange with a contact listed as “E”, which appears to be Mr Musk.

In the messages, Mr Musk said last week that he loved her and that he would have invited her to the state dinner between Donald Trump and Xi Jinping last week, where he was a guest.

“I didn’t realise that this Xi dinner included significant other [sic],” he wrote.

Details of the relationship between Mr Musk and Ms Zilis emerged during a high-profile lawsuit against his competitor OpenAI, where Ms Zilis previously served on the board.

Mr Musk was an early investor in OpenAI, and Ms Zilis often acted as a go-between after the relationship between the billionaire and the company soured.

She testified during the trial in May, revealing that her relationship with Mr Musk began as a “one-off” during a corporate event in 2015.

Mr Musk later offered to be a sperm donor when Ms Zilis decided to have children as a single mother. Their relationship later evolved into a romantic partnership.

Mr Musk reportedly has 14 children with four women. 

During his time campaigning for Mr Trump in 2024, some people close to him were apparently worried about his “fixation on having more children,” The New York Times reported last year.

In February 2025, as Mr Musk was overseeing the work of the Department of Government Efficiency (Doge), he appeared in the Oval Office with his son, X Æ A-Xii, on his shoulders.

Mr Musk had reportedly been battling with X Æ A-Xii’s mother, Grimes, who had apparently privately complained that they agreed to keep him out of the public eye.

Mr Musk has not commented on the end of his relationship with Ms Zilis.
