Nicola Coughlan, LYRA's Shane MacGowan tribute and more confirmed for <i>The Late Late Show</i> this week | Hotpress

TheTools · 12.11.2026 18:04 · source · permalink
  • Film And TV
  • 12 Nov 26

There will also be an announcement about this year's Late Late Toy Show.

The guest lineup for this week's The Late Late Show have been revealed. Patrick Kielty will be joined by Nicola Coughlan, John Bishop, Neil Delamere, TJ Reid.

Cork musician Lyra will perform a musical tribute to Shane MacGowan, ahead of the release of his tribute album 20th Century Paddy: The Songs of Shane MacGowan, which drops tomorrow, November 13. Lyra features on the record, with her version of 'Streets of Sorrow/ The Birmingham Six', from The Pogues 1988 album, If I Should Fall from Grace with God.

Kielty will chat with Nicola Coughlan about her new Channel 4 drama, I Am Helen, which explores abusive relationships. She will also talk about her recent SNL UK appearance, and returning for season 5 of Bridgerton.

Comedian John Bishop will join Kielty to discuss his new show Let's Go Round Again, which comes to Ireland in May 2027. He will also share a variety of humorous anecdotes about his time in Patagonia with Jason Byrne.

Neil Delamere will make an appearance on the show, as he chats to Kielty about his new stand-up tour, Neil's on Wheels. He will discuss his favourite audience members to riff with, as well as making friends after 40.

Kilkenny hurling legend TJ Reid will chat to Kielty about his recent retirement from inter-county hurling, and discuss his impressive career as the all-time leading scorer of the championship.

There will also be an announcement about the upcoming Late Late Toy Show, which airs on Friday, December 4, 2026.
