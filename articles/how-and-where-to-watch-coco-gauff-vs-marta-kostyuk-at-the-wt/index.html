How and Where To Watch Coco Gauff vs Marta Kostyuk at the WTA Finals? Start Time, Live Streaming, TV Channels, and More

TheTools · 12.11.2026 20:00 · source · permalink

Coco Gauff faces Marta Kostyuk in her final Group 1 round-robin match at the 2026 WTA Finals in Indian Wells, with the American still fighting to secure a place in the semifinals. Gauff heads into the encounter following a straight-set defeat to Linda Noskova, while Kostyuk has already been eliminated after losing her opening two matches.

The match is scheduled for Thursday, November 12, at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden, with fans across the world able to follow the action through various television broadcasters and streaming platforms.

Here’s everything you need to know about the Coco Gauff vs Marta Kostyuk match, including its start time, live-streaming options, TV channels and head-to-head record.

Gauff and Kostyuk will meet on Thursday, November 12, at 12:00 PM local time in Indian Wells. The match will open the singles session at the tournament.

For viewers in the US, the match begins at 12:00 PM PST and 3:00 PM EST. Fans in the UK can tune in at 8:00 PM GMT, while the start time in Central Europe is 9:00 PM CET. In Australia, the match gets underway at 7:00 AM AEDT on Friday, November 13.

Tennis fans in the United States can watch the match on Tennis Channel and Tennis Channel 2 (T2). Coverage is also available through Tennis Channel’s direct subscription and live TV streaming services such as YouTube TV and Fubo.

In the United Kingdom and Ireland, Sky Sports will broadcast the match, with streaming available through NOW.

Across continental Europe, broadcast coverage varies by country, with Sky Sport carrying the tournament in Germany, Austria and Italy, beIN Sports in France, and Canal+ in Poland.

Australian viewers can follow the action on beIN Sports, with streaming available through Stan Sport. In Canada, the match will be broadcast on TSN and streamed on TSN+.

Gauff holds a 4-2 advantage over Kostyuk in their career head-to-head record, including a 4-1 lead on outdoor hard courts.

Their most recent meeting came at the 2026 Cincinnati Open, where Gauff secured a dominant 6-2, 6-2 victory. The American also defeated Kostyuk at the 2024 Wuhan Open and Australian Open, while the Ukrainian claimed victories at the 2025 Qatar Open and 2024 Stuttgart Open.

Kostyuk, however, enters this encounter without a semifinal opportunity. She suffered straight-set defeats against Linda Noskova (6-2, 6-4) and Jessica Pegula (6-2, 7-5), leaving her with a 0-2 record and no sets won in Group 1.

Gauff’s situation is considerably more complicated. She defeated Pegula in her opening match but subsequently lost to Noskova, leaving her with a 1-1 record. With Noskova already through to the semifinals, Gauff needs to beat Kostyuk and hope Noskova defeats Pegula in the other group match.

If Gauff and Pegula both finish with 2-1 records, the second semifinal spot will be decided by the percentage of sets won. Therefore, a victory against Kostyuk alone may not be enough to guarantee Gauff’s progression.
