Inzaghi's decision ahead of Al-Hilal vs Al-Qadisiyah… | YSscores

TheTools · 23.10.2026 14:57 · source · permalink

Al-Hilal players held their final training session before departure, focusing on physical and technical aspects in preparation for the upcoming test against the league leaders.

The match is of great importance for Al-Hilal, as a victory would give them the chance to take the top spot from Al-Qadisiyah, who enter the game sitting at the summit with 25 points.

Al-Hilal will continue its stay in Dammam until Sunday before heading by bus to the Qatari capital Doha, in preparation for the match against Al-Shamal on Tuesday in the AFC Champions League Elite group stage.

Al-Hilal have a postponed match from the 9th round against Riyadh, scheduled to take place on December 19, providing the team with an additional opportunity to improve their position in the Saudi Pro League.

Inzaghi's team has delivered strong results this season, achieving 11 wins and just one loss in 12 matches across all competitions, ahead of the upcoming fixtures against Al-Qadisiyah and Al-Shamal.

Simone Inzaghi, the manager of Al-Hilal, has chosen to travel by road to Dammam on Thursday in preparation for the match against Al-Qadisiyah on Friday, as part of the eleventh round of the Saudi Pro League.
