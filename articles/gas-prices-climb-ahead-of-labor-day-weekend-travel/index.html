Gas prices climb ahead of Labor Day weekend travel

TheTools · 07.09.2026 14:51 · source · permalink

SAGINAW, Mich. (WNEM) - For millions on the road this Labor Day, the trip is coming with a higher price tag at the pump. Gas prices are up more than 29% according to AAA.

John Calce, founder and chairman of American First Refining, said prices have increased broadly.

“They’re up across the board and in some places substantially, even more noticeable for diesel prices, which are really up,” Calce said.

The increase can add up quickly, especially for families traveling farther from home.

“In terms of gasoline you’re paying, depending where you’re at, a dollar extra a gallon. So if the typical family drives a couple hundred miles and they get 20 miles a gallon, you’re getting an extra $10 for that weekend road trip,” Calce said.

Some relief could be ahead as the holiday travel stretch winds down, according to Calce.

“Typically, after the summer driving season gasoline prices will come off. That’s the typical pattern. The summer driving season is the most intense,” Calce said.
