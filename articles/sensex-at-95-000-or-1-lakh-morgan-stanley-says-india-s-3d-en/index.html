Sensex at 95,000 or 1 lakh? Morgan Stanley says India’s 3D engine is putting stocks at an inflexion point

TheTools · 17.11.2026 03:32 · source · permalink
  • BSE Share Price
  • Multi Commodity Exchange of India Share Price
  • Indian Energy Exchange Share Price

    Morgan Stanley sees the Sensex rising 16% to 95,000 by December 2027, with a bull-case target of 1,05,000. The brokerage cites India's durable, diversified and decoupled growth, an improving earnings cycle and strong domestic flows as key drivers, while flagging elevated oil prices and global slowdown risks.
