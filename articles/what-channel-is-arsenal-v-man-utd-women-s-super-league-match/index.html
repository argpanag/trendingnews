What channel is Arsenal v Man Utd Women's Super League match on? TV coverage, live stream and kick-off time

TheTools · 19.09.2026 17:07 · source · permalink

Check out how to watch Arsenal v Man Utd in the Women's Super League, including TV channel, radio coverage and kick-off time.

  • James Walters

Arsenal host Manchester United in the Women's Super League on Saturday evening with both sides having contrasting starts to their campaigns.

The Gunners beat Brighton 1-0 in their opener before playing out a 0-0 draw at home against Crystal Palace last weekend to leave them fifth in the table and just two points off league leaders Manchester City.

Arsenal, who finished four points behind title winners City last season, haven't won the WSL since 2019 and Renée Slegers is currently tasked with getting them over the line after finishing second or third in 11 of the last 12 campaigns.

  • Watch Arsenal v Man Utd on Sky Sports

United finished fourth last season and 15 points off top but Eva Olid's side have had a poor start this time out.

The Red Devils lost 2-1 in their opener against London City Lionesses before their 5-0 home defeat against Chelsea last Sunday to leave them bottom of the league.

Olid will be desperate for her side to bounce back on Saturday night, but it'll be a tough ask for United to win at the Emirates.
