Habs Daily: Demidov Speeding, Hutson Bowl, Larkin Rumours

TheTools · 15.10.2026 11:00 · source · permalink

Montreal Canadiens•NHL

Here are the Montreal Canadiens and NHL news items you may have missed on Wednesday.

Montreal Canadiens News

  • Ivan Demidov is in hot water with the province, as his license was set to be suspended until January due to a pair of tickets for speeding, but the decision has been appealed for the time being. He was clocked doing 145 km/h in a 100 km/h zone earlier in the year, and was recently caught driving 111km/h in a 70km/h zone. Young players driving much too fast is nothing new, but it’s definitely an issue the Canadiens will want to address. [Canadiens Prospect Report: Mittelstadt Immediately Impresses]
  • Even though Lane Hutson scored a highlight reel goal, he’ll be disappointed by the result of the first ever Hutson Bowl. His elder sibling Cole emerged with a 5-4 win, as the Canadiens were let down by poor goaltending versus the Washington Capitals. [Canadiens Highlights: Young Habs Face Capital Punishment]
  • The Dylan Larkin to Montreal rumour continues to be discussed, despite the fact that absolutely nothing has changed in the overall situation. Larkin and his family do not want to come to Montreal. [Evaluating The Canadiens’ Pursuit Of Dylan Larkin: Talent vs. Reality]
