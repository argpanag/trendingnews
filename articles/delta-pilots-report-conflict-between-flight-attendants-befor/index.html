(NewsNation) — A conflict between flight attendants broke out on a plane landing in Michigan, prompting the captain to call airline supervisors as the aircraft approached the airport runway, according to multiple reports.

The incident occurred on a Delta flight traveling from South Carolina to Detroit Metropolitan Wayne County Airport, according to Dublin Live.

It is unclear what caused the disagreement, but the captain alerted ground control to the situation and warned that crew members might need to be separated to prevent tensions from escalating further, according to the report.

"We are experiencing conflict issues among the flight attendants and will likely need to separate them to prevent further escalation or an incident," the captain told ground control, according to the report.

The plane landed safely, and no further incidents were reported.
