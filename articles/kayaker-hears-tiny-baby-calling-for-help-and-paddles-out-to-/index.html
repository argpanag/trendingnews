Kayaker Hears Tiny Baby Calling For Help And Paddles Out To Rescue Him

TheTools · 03.09.2026 20:05 · source · permalink

    He dropped everything to help.

    College student Carter Taylor was out kayaking and fishing when he heard someone talking about a cat stuck out in the middle of the water. Without hesitation, he left his gear on the bank and began paddling out to investigate.

    As Taylor got closer to a tree in the water, he began hearing cries for help. He soon spotted the source: a tiny kitten who had somehow found herself stranded.

    Taylor paddled as fast as he could to get to the kitten, as he was worried she might jump or fall into the water when she saw him coming. As soon as the kayak was within her reach, the kitten hopped aboard and immediately climbed onto her rescuer, crying her thanks.

    Once the kitten was safe, Taylor was incredibly relieved and began paddling them back to shore. She cuddled up to her rescuer immediately, claiming her as his person. Once they were out of the water, Taylor called his girlfriend to come and collect the kitten, whom they later named Lucky.

    The couple fed Lucky and cleaned her up as best they could. Their dog, Rigley, welcomed the kitten with lots of love, clearly pleased by the new addition.

    Lucky went to the vet the next day and received a thorough checkup and antibiotics. She was deemed overall healthy despite everything she’d been through. The couple felt like they were meant to find her, and it didn’t take them long to decide to keep her.

    “Looks like I have been chosen,” Taylor wrote on TikTok.

    Taylor hadn’t expected his day of kayaking and fishing to turn into a rescue mission, but everyone, including Lucky, is so glad it happened that way.
