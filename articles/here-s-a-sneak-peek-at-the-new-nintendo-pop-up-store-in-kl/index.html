Here’s A Sneak Peek At The New Nintendo Pop-Up Store In KL

TheTools · 14.09.2026 17:41 · source · permalink

Malaysia finally has its very own Nintendo pop-up store, and there's plenty for fans to check out.

By Tamara Jayne | 14 Sep 2026, 05:41 PM

Follow us on Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp for the latest stories and breaking news.

The Nintendo Pop-Up Store officially opened on 12 September at Ombak KLCC, right next to Malaysia's first-ever Pokémon Center pop-up store.

The limited-time store will be around until 31 December 2026, giving fans a few months to browse its selection of Nintendo goodies, pick up some games, and even get hands-on with the Nintendo Switch 2.

The pop-up carries official Nintendo merchandise from several popular game franchises, including The Legend of Zelda, Super Mario, Animal Crossing, Pikmin, Kirby, and Splatoon.

There's a mix of character goods and lifestyle items, including products sourced from Japan as well as newer releases.

Fans can also look out for exclusive Mario-themed merchandise created for the Malaysia pop-up.

Not sure whether the Switch 2 is worth the upgrade? You can actually try it out at the pop-up.

A Nintendo Switch 2 experience zone lets visitors play and test out the console, making the store more than just a shopping stop.

Just a heads-up: the opening weekend saw strong crowds around the Pokémon Center, so it's worth planning your visit accordingly.

Address
Level 3, Ombak KLCC, Kuala Lumpur

Date
12 September to 31 December 2026

Time
10am to 10pm daily
