Key updates to keep you informed:

15:36 CET - Spurs finally scored their first Premier League goals of the season... in a 3-2 loss at home to Aston Villa!

A dismal, dismal start to the campaign for Robert De Zerbi's men shows no sign of slowing down, and they are now winless in the league with just two points from their first five games, sitting in 18th. The pressure will be really ramping up on the Italian manager.

Late goals from Conor Gallagher and Jan Paul van Hecke were not enough, and it is a huge win for Villa, who were also winless before this game. They move out of the relegation zone into 15th, thanks to goals from Nicolas Jackson, Johan Manzambi and a wonderstrike from Emi Buendia.

The afternoon Premier League games begin in 25 minutes, with champions Arsenal visiting Brighton, while Newcastle and Everton are also in action.
