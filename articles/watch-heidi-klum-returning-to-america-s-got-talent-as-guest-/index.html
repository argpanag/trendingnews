Watch: Heidi Klum returning to 'America's Got Talent' as guest judge

TheTools · 05.09.2026 05:24 · source · permalink

Sept. 3 (UPI) -- Supermodel Heidi Klum is returning to America's Got Talent as a guest judge on Sept. 8.

"The chair is ready and waiting for you, Heidi," host Terry Crews said on Wednesday's edition of the competition show.

Klum, 53, served as a judge on the show from 2013 to 2018 and from 2020 to 2024.

She is one of the stars filling in for regular panelist Simon Cowell, who is recovering from back surgery.

Kenan Thompson was a guest judge on the NBC series this week.

The other regular panelists include Howie Mandel, Mel B and Sofia Vergara.

Season 21 wraps up Sept. 23.
