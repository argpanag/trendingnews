Luka Doncic just called out a difficult truth about Lakers' last three games

TheTools · 22.11.2026 02:30 · source · permalink

Listening to Luka Doncic speak after the Los Angeles Lakers' win over the Sacramento Kings on Friday night, you'd almost be convinced that the Lakers were in the middle of a losing season with a depressing record like the Kings' 1-14 mark.

That's far from the reality, of course. With the win on Friday, the Lake Show got to 14-3 on the young season, keeping them atop the Western Conference standings.

But Doncic, being the high-basketball-IQ player that he is, knows that there are still reasons for the Lakers to be concerned, particularly given their form over the last three games.

Lakers have regressed over their last few games

In truth, the Lakers could've easily lost all three of those games, and they are frankly lucky to have escaped with two wins out of three. Prior to the loss to the Utah Jazz on Wednesday, the Lakers needed a Quentin Grimes miracle game to come back against the Charlotte Hornets and win in overtime.

On Friday, the Lakers were outplayed by a feisty Kings team for much of the game and were lucky to end up with a victory.

What's going on with the Lakers this week? Can it be attributed to Austin Reaves' absence alone? That's definitely part of the problem, but it's also possible that the Lakers' focus has waned a bit since the start of the campaign. When asked about that focus, Doncic had a blunt response during Friday's postgame presser.

Luka Doncic believes the Lakers need to improve their focus

“Not very good. We have to work on that,” Doncic said. “We got to work on approaching the game a better way.”

Doncic himself has struggled over the past three contests, even though he's still putting up ridiculous scoring numbers that place him head and shoulders above the rest of the league.

A mediocre week is not the end of the world for the Lakers, who've still won 10 of 11 and are, as aforementioned, still at the top of the West for now. And with Reaves expected to return next week, things should be looking up.

If anything, Luka's somber demeanor following the win on Friday was a sign that the Lakers are holding themselves to a higher standard in 2026-27. Wins aren't something to over-celebrate if the team didn't play well.

In the bigger picture of the season, though, great teams find a way to win when nothing's clicking. That's an element of these Lakers that they probably aren't giving themselves enough credit for at the moment.
