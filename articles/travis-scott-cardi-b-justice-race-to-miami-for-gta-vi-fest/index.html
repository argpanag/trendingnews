Travis Scott, Cardi B, Justice Race to Miami for GTA VI Fest

Vice City is getting a real-world musical celebration.

Rockstar Games has announced Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival, a one-day event set for Nov. 21 at Miami’s Mana Wynwood, featuring performances by artists connected to the slavishly anticipated game and its official soundtrack.

The lineup includes Justice, which oversaw the GTA score, Travis Scott, Don Toliver, Cardi B, Metro Boomin, Kodak Black, SoFaygo, Ca7riel and Paco Amoroso, Nick Leon B2B DJ Python, Prospa B2B Scream, Rauw & Friends, Suero, Tainy and The Dare B2B Fcukers.

The event is billed as a celebration of the music of Vice City and the fictional state of Leonida, the game’s Florida-inspired setting. Tickets will be available through lottery via DICE starting Nov. 11.

Justice has already unveiled two tunes from the game: the Tame Impala-, SZA- and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard-featuring “Flower in a Hurricane” and the Gesaffelstein collab “Pusher.”

Both appear on Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, a 34-song soundtrack overseen by Atlantic’s Kevin Weaver and due Nov. 19 alongside the game. The release follows a series of standalone soundtrack singles, including tracks by Scott, Yung Lean, Morgan Wallen and Keith Richards.

The game’s in-world radio universe is similarly wide-ranging, with stations devoted to Latin, country, dance, Southern hip-hop and metal with the help of Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey, Robyn, Trick Daddy and Slayer.
