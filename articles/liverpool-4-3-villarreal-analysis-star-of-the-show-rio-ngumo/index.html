Liverpool 4-3 Villarreal analysis: 'Star of the show' Rio Ngumoha shines at Anfield

TheTools · 21.10.2026 04:02 · source · permalink
  • Published
    5 hours ago

Arguably the star of the show will be Rio Ngumoha.

We're starting to see signs that he is starting to show the potential we knew he had. Now we're starting to see a little bit more end product.

It was a big night for Liverpool and much-needed as well.

If you play against a better team and give them that start, you will get punished, and it will be a lot more difficult to come from behind.

However, you've got to praise the mentality, praise the changes of Andoni Iraola.

Something he did really well at Bournemouth was that he found solutions, whether that was with personnel who he didn't think would play in certain positions.

Tonight he put Ryan Gravenberch at right-back. I thought he was outstanding in the second half. Gakpo going more narrow, Wirtz coming on. I think it changed the game.
