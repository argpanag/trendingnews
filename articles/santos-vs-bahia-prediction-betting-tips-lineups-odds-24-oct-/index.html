Santos vs Bahia Preview

Santos meet Bahia, with kick-off set for 21:00 on Saturday 24 October.

Santos won last time out by a 2-1 scoreline. It was away from home against Fluminense. Cuca's team had 51% possession and five shots on target, while Gabriel Barbosa and Philippe Coutinho scored one goal each.

Bahia have suffered back-to-back Série A away losses. They were turned over by Flamengo in their previous league game, losing 3-0 at home. The Salvador club finished with just 37% possession and had three shots on goal.

Gabriel Barbosa leads the way with 8 goals, with Neymar and Christian Oliva getting 2 apiece. Benjamin Rollheiser has recorded the most assists with 2.

Bahia have 5 victories, 3 defeats and 2 draws, with an average of 1.5 goals from 4.4 shots on goal and 14.3 attempts. There’s been an average of 53.8% possession, 4.9 corners awarded and 6.4 corners against, while they’ve conceded 1.4 goals from 5.8 shots on goal and 15.8 attempts.

Luciano Juba, Erick Pulga and Alejo Veliz have top scored with 3, while Jean Lucas is next on 2. Rodrigo Nestor has managed to clock up 2 assists in the last 10 games.
