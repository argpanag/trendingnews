The 2027 ODI World Cup begins on October 2 and ends on November 21. The all-important India vs Pakistan clash is on October 10. With a year to go, the ICC confirmed that already over 100,000 applications for tickets have come. Such is the aura of the Mother of All Battles. Even now, it continues to be the main event in ICC tournaments, no matter where they are being held.

3 India vs Pakistan Games Possible

But there is some interesting news for fans. This edition of the World Cup could see as many as three face-offs between the rivals. Yes, it is true. Why so? Because the format of the tournament is designed in such a way, India and Pakistan could actually meet thrice. One clash is mind-blowing, two would be out of this world, and three would be the cherry on top. So, let’s see how it can happen.

October 10 Showdown

The first clash is the only confirmed one in the World Cup so far. October 10 will see India and Pakistan face off in their 9th World Cup game. As of now, India lead 8-0 which really puts the word rivalry to shame. From their 1st encounter in 1992 to last in 2023, it’s been India all the way. In the 14th edition, both sides are in Group A so will play with each other. But they can meet again.

Super 7 Face-Off

Should both India and Pakistan advance to the Super 7, they shall face off once more on November 7, based on the pre-seeding. This is because the Super 7 will witness all seven teams play each other to battle it out for the four semifinal spots.

India vs Pakistan ODI World Cup Final Possible

Now if India and Pakistan finish in the top four, they will both enter the semis. Now depending on their positions, they could have a semifinal showdown. Or even better, both teams could play different semis, win their respective matches and meet in the final, which has never happened before in the 50-over World Cup.

Historic 1st on the Cards

In the history of the ODI World Cup, India have never won more than once in a single edition. It did happen in the T20 World Cup in 2007 when India won the league match via bowl-out after a tie and beat Pakistan in a thriller in the final. In the 2017 Champions Trophy, India creamed Pakistan in the league stage and then got creamed by them in the final. Shall we see multiple India vs Pakistan encounters for the first time in an ODI World Cup next year? Let’s wait and find out!
