Weekend weather forecast for Congleton - 18 Sep to 20 Sep

Weather Weekend Forecast for Congleton

This weekend in Congleton is set to feature predominantly cloudy skies with intermittent chances of rain, especially on Friday and Saturday. Temperatures will stay relatively mild, with a slight drop as we progress into Sunday. The weather will present moderate wind speeds across all three days. Overall, it is advisable to be prepared for occasional showers but also enjoy breaks of sunshine by the end of the weekend.

Friday Friday will be characterised by cloudy skies throughout the day, coupled with a high likelihood of rain. Expect temperatures to peak at 16°C and dip to a low of 11°C. Humidity will be relatively high at 84%, with a substantial 87% chance of precipitation. Winds are expected to reach speeds of up to 30 mph, contributing to a brisk feel despite comparatively mild temperatures.

Saturday On Saturday, the weather mirrors Friday, with cloudy skies and continued chances of rain. However, temperatures are set to rise slightly, with a maximum of 18°C and a minimum of 14°C. Humidity will decrease marginally to 79%. The likelihood of precipitation remains at 87%. Winds will persist at 30 mph, maintaining a consistent breezy atmosphere throughout the day.

Sunday Sunday offers a change of pace with partly cloudy skies and a more settled feel. The temperature will range from a high of 15°C to a low of 10°C. Humidity will reduce to 75%, with a significantly lower chance of precipitation at 19%. Winds will ease to 24 mph, making it a more inviting day for outdoor activities.

Commuting Advice For those commuting on Friday and Saturday, it would be prudent to keep an umbrella or waterproof clothing handy due to the high probability of rain. Windy conditions may also affect travel, particularly if relying on forms of transport like cycling. Sunday’s reduced winds and precipitation probability present a smoother commute with less weather-related disruption.

Advice for Weekend Plans Adopt flexible plans for Friday and Saturday, as intermittent rain may disrupt outdoor activities. Consider indoor alternatives or check local live events with covered venues. Sunday’s outlook appears to be more favourable for outdoor excursions, so planning picnics or a walk in the park could be a delightful way to enjoy the day’s improved weather conditions.
