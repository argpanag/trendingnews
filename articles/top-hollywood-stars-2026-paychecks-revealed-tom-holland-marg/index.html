Top Hollywood Stars' 2026 Paychecks Revealed: Tom Holland, Margot Robbie, and More

TheTools · 22.10.2026 09:22 · source · permalink

It looks like 2026 will end up being a pretty lucrative year for some Hollywood stars.

Although certain A-listers have been pulling in big bucks for quite a while now, the paydays that were negotiated and agreed upon in 2026 are staggering.

For instance, Tom Holland has revealed that he's set to be paid $20 million for his latest spin as Spider-Man for Sony and Marvel. Of course, that's not to overlook the "back-end bonus potential," which could see him walking away with "at least $100 million … from Spider-Man Brand New Day," according to Page Six. That earning power is fueled, in part, by the fact that "Holland's fourth Spider-Man standalone is breaking records, … Globally it grossed $2.5 billion and in North America alone it's become the number one all time highest grossing film."

Fellow Marvel star Robert Downey Jr will also be padding his already full wallet this year thanks to his new role as Dr. Doom. Appearing in Avengers: Doomsday (hitting theaters on December 18, 2026) and Avengers: Secret Wars (scheduled to be released December 17, 2027), as well as one or more cameos, RDJ will also be walking away with $100 million, per Page Six.

If you need more proof that working for Marvel can make you rich (or even richer), Page Six notes that Chris Hemsworth, who, of course, plays Thor, and Chris Evans, who can be seen onscreen as Captain America, will each earn around $40 million for the new Avengers installments.

Last but not least, Margot Robbie's gig as the star and producer of Barbie (2023) led to a $50 million paycheck for the actress and businesswoman. While the film took in $1.5 billion in worldwide box office sales, The New York Times reported this past summer that Warner Bros. was "scrambling" to make a deal with the movie's stars, including Robbie and Ryan Gosling, as well as the writer and director Greta Gerwig, by December and before "[r]ights to 'Barbie' will revert to Mattel, the toy company behind the iconic doll."
