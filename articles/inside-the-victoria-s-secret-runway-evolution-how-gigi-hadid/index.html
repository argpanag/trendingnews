Inside the Victoria's Secret Runway Evolution: How Gigi Hadid, Gisele Bündchen and Heidi Klum Redefined the World's Most Polarising Fashion Spectacle | Collin Rugg News

TheTools · 19.10.2026 00:00 · source · permalink

When the curtain rises on the Victoria’s Secret runway tonight at Sony Pictures Studios in Los Angeles, the spectacle marks far more than another evening of high-wattage fashion: it represents the continuation of a high-stakes cultural rehabilitation that transformed a cancelled 2019 relic into a modern powerhouse. Leading the charge once again is 31-year-old supermodel Gigi Hadid, returning for her sixth appearance alongside an untouchable roster of runway legends who spent decades turning a commercial lingerie catalogue into global prime-time theatre.

The journey from hopeful hopefuls to certified runway icons has never been straightforward for those carrying the brand’s legendary wings. Hadid herself was rejected multiple times before securing her runway breakthrough, proving that even the most famous faces in British and international fashion faced intense hurdles to claim their spot.

“I tried out two or three times before I got it, so you can imagine what I felt when I got my first show,” Hadid revealed, recalling her 2015 debut at age 20. Countering critics who dismissed the event as outdated pageantry, she maintained that the production holds far deeper meaning for the women involved: “It’s not what people think. It’s not just about lingerie and looking hot.”

From Brazilian Megastars to Golden-Era Angels

Before the modern refresh, the runway belonged to an era defined by athletic glamour, multi-million-pound production budgets, and unabashed theatricality. The high-water mark of that period was forged by Brazilian icon Gisele Bündchen, who made her debut in 1999 at just 19 years of age.

While Bündchen captivated international audiences for years, her relationship with the high-exposure format carried private misgivings. In her 2018 memoir, Lessons: My Path to a Meaningful Life, she confessed to pleading with stylists: “Give me a tail, a cape, wings, please, anything to cover me up a little!”

Yet the sheer scale of the production remained unmatched. Reflecting on those peak runway years during a 2022 retrospective with British Vogue, Bündchen acknowledged the electrifying energy of the sets:

  • Live performance energy: “There was always someone singing, there was always something happening,” Bündchen noted, describing the runway as an immersive concert-theatre hybrid.
  • The pursuit of grandeur: For other veterans, the spectacle was pure joy. Heidi Klum, who spent 11 years as an Angel, confessed on Call Her Daddy in 2024: “I loved it always. I always wanted to wear the big wings.”
  • Casting pedigree: Decades of runway dominance relied on names like Adriana Lima and Alessandra Ambrosio, who became global household fixtures purely through the annual showcase.

The 2019 Cancellation and the Multi-Million-Pound Reinvention

The brand’s glittering façade fractured in 2019 when the annual broadcast was officially cancelled following years of declining viewership and fierce public backlash that the presentation felt disconnected from modern values. For half a decade, fashion insiders questioned whether the runway wings would ever reappear.

The turning point arrived with a revamped leadership strategy that brought the runway back in 2024. Rather than abandoning its historical cast, the show paired veteran Angels with a radically wider range of talent, blending heritage glamour with contemporary figures.

A Cross-Generational Fashion Alliance

The revival managed to pull together iconic figures from across distinct eras of British and global style. Runway luminaries including Irina Shayk and Candice Swanepoel shared the spotlight with industry trailblazer Ashley Graham, former French first lady and singer Carla Bruni, and British runway royalty Kate Moss appearing alongside her daughter, Lila Moss.

That balance of nostalgia and fresh energy has carried directly into tonight’s presentation, which brings back one of the brand’s most famous hallmarks: the coveted Fantasy Bra. Handcrafted this season in collaboration with luxury jeweller Jacob & Co., the identity of the model chosen to wear the multi-million-pound showpiece remains closely guarded under strict secrecy.

As the lights come up in Los Angeles, the runway once again tests whether a legacy brand can honour its high-octane past while maintaining relevance in front of a discerning worldwide audience.
