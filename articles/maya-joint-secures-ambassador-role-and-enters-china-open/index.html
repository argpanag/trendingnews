Maya Joint Secures Ambassador Role and Enters China Open

TheTools · 30.09.2026 05:09 · source · permalink

Australian tennis player Maya Joint signed as an ambassador for the Swisse Hot Shots Tennis program while preparing for her round of 128 match against Sinja Kraus at the WTA China Open on Wednesday, September 30, 2026. Statistical modeling from predictive analytics platforms gives Joint a 75 percent probability of defeating Kraus in their opening singles match. Head-to-head odds favor Joint to advance, alongside predictions favoring Kraus to secure the first set based on a 57 percent probability model.

The newly rebranded Swisse Hot Shots Tennis initiative aims to connect young Australian children with tennis, building confidence and physical activity habits. Joint joins fellow Australian professional Alex de Minaur as a representative of the grassroots development initiative.

Tennis Australia officials emphasized the role of foundational programs in youth development.

"Swisse Hot Shots Tennis is where so many kids take their first steps in our sport, and creating a positive first experience is incredibly important," said Tom Larner, Chief Tennis Officer at Tennis Australia. The partnership expands opportunities for young participants through exclusive events, competitions, and giveaways.

"We're thrilled to welcome Swisse as naming rights partner and Maya as an ambassador for the program," said Larner. The program aims to foster resilience, confidence, and foundational athletic skills across thousands of children annually.

"Swisse shares our belief in helping kids build confidence, resilience and important life skills, while Maya's story shows what's possible when young players develop a love for the game from an early age," said Larner. Brand leadership highlighted the social and emotional health benefits associated with youth sports participation. "We know the positive impact sport can have on children's physical, social and emotional wellbeing," said Stephanie Taylor, Marketing Director at Swisse Wellness.

The initiative seeks to offer a structured environment for kids to build health habits.

"Swisse Hot Shots Tennis provides a fun and welcoming environment where kids can build confidence, make friends and develop lifelong healthy habits," said Taylor. The partnership reinforces ongoing efforts by Tennis Australia to expand community engagement across the country. "We're proud to partner with Tennis Australia to help more kids discover the joy of tennis and the benefits of being active," said Taylor.

Joint's tournament appearance at the China Open in Beijing marks a key step in her international season alongside her national grassroots commitments.
