Captain Sandeep Lamichhane Blames Poor Fielding and Bowling for Nepal Premier League Loss

TheTools · 29.10.2026 16:45 · source · permalink

Kathmandu. After losing to Karnali Yaks by 6 wickets in the Nepal Premier League (NPL), Biratnagar Kings captain Sandeep Lamichhane said that the match was lost due to poor fielding and bowling.

Speaking at a post-match press conference on Thursday after the second consecutive defeat, Sandeep said that despite a good start and a strong total, they could not secure a win due to their own mistakes.

Our fielding was very poor today. Such performance is not acceptable for any team. We dropped four-five important catches. It feels like we dropped all the catches we were supposed to drop throughout the entire tournament in a single match,' he said.

According to Sandeep, Biratnagar was in a strong position until the first 8-9 overs of the match. However, the game went out of their control as they could not stop the opposing team's batsmen afterwards.

He said that the dew in the second innings made it difficult for the bowlers to hold the ball, and the pitch also became easier for batting. He stated that the team had to face defeat because they did not receive the necessary support from the fielders in such a situation.

Sandeep praised the performance of Karnali Yaks captain Sompal Kami. He said that Sompal, who came in to bat at number five, played an excellent innings under pressure and turned the tide of the match in his team's favor.

'Sompal dai deserves full credit for this. He performed well in both batting and bowling. He stayed at the crease until the end and, along with Pawan Saraf and Gulshan Jha, led the team to victory,' Sandeep said.

Sandeep also praised the performance of Biratnagar's batsman Lokesh Bam. He said that it was due to Lokesh's excellent batting that the team was able to score 187 runs.

'We had set a target of scoring 160 to 170 runs. But, due to Lokesh's excellent batting, we reached 187 runs,' he said.
Sandeep mentioned that Lokesh is continuously maturing as a player and said that he could become an important player for Nepali cricket. Sandeep stated that Lokesh has the confidence to face challenges on a big stage and can contribute significantly to the national team.

Sandeep, who is also a spinner, clarified his decision to bowl the final over himself. He said that although it was difficult to bowl spin in the second innings, he bowled with the belief that he could win the match for the team.

'It was very difficult to bowl spin in the second innings. If there were 12-13 runs left, we might have been able to drag the game. But, I was ready and confident to defend even eight runs,' he said.

Sandeep mentioned that small mistakes in cricket can have a big impact on the result and said that his team had to bear the consequences.

When asked about the claim by Bangladeshi player Jishan Alam, associated with Biratnagar Kings, that he could not contact the team, Sandeep gave a humorous reply.

'If I were the owner of the team, I might be able to answer this. How would I know about this!' he said. Despite losing two consecutive matches, Biratnagar faces the challenge of improving its weaknesses and making a comeback in the upcoming matches.
