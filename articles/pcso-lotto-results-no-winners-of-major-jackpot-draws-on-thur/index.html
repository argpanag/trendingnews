PCSO Lotto Results: No winners of major jackpot draws on Thursday, September 3, 2026

TheTools · 04.09.2026 17:37 · source · permalink

There were no winners of either of the major lotto draws on Thursday, September 3, 2026, the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO) announced.

No bettor picked the winning combination of 15-45-13-18-08-02 for the Superlotto 6/49 prize of ₱25,239,749.17.

There were also no bettors who chose the winning combination of 13-14-39-30-28-32 for the Lotto 6/42 prize of ₱25,013,742.59.
