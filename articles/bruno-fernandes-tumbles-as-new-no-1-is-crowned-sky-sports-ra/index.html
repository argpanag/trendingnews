Bruno Fernandes has been dethroned as the king of the Premier League in a major shake-up to the top 25 Premier League players.

We asked our reporters and journalists to rank a longlist of 40 of the best players in the competition right now and took an average of their answers to produce the Sky Sports top 25.

The champions Arsenal the best represented, accounting for nine of the top 25. But which player takes top spot? And how do the rankings below compare to our last vote at the start of the season?

The top 10

10. Martin Odegaard

Arsenal's conductor. The man who makes Mikel Arteta's attacking orchestra sing. When Odegaard finds his rhythm, so do the champions.

9. Joao Pedro

Chelsea aren't the same team when he's missing. Joao Pedro has the swagger, technical brilliance and imagination to unlock even the most stubborn defences. He leads the way for Chelsea when it comes to Premier League goals (20) and goal involvements (28) since the start of last season.

8. Elliot Anderson

Please use Chrome browser for a more accessible video player

Anderson has developed into one of the Premier League's most complete midfield operators, combining the bite of a ball-winner with the brain of a playmaker and his stock is on the rise courtesy of his performances for Man City and England.

Anderson tops the charts for successful passes and duels won of midfielders in the Premier League this season.

Since the last top 25 was published in March he has improved by 12 places.

7. Rayan Cherki

Football is supposed to be fun, remember. Cherki makes it. Man City's twinkle toes has turned his flamboyance into goal involvements this season, posting the second-best minutes per goal involvement (one every 81 mins) of players to have played a minimum of 500 minutes.

A gloriously unpredictable talent capable of turning a mundane passage of play into something spectacular.

6. William Saliba

The Rolls-Royce of Premier League centre-backs. Saliba makes defending look almost insultingly easy. A defender without an obvious weakness and it's been a surprise to see Arsenal cope so admirably without him this season.

5. Morgan Rogers

Rogers has blossomed into one of the Premier League's most devastating attacking forces. His ability to glide past opponents with that deceptively explosive running style is frightening. And his rise five places up this ranking since March showcases his standing within the game.

4. Gabriel Magalhaes

A defensive monster. Gabriel has become the beating heart of Arsenal's extraordinary defensive machine, combining old-school aggression and an unmatched aerial presence in both boxes. Gabriel has scored the most goals by a Premier League defender since 2020/21 (21).

Every title-winning side needs a warrior. Arsenal have theirs.

3. David Raya

The goalkeeper has helped redefine Arsenal's defensive output. Raya isn't merely a shot-stopper, he's an extra outfield player, a sweeper and a calming presence rolled into one who has kept more clean sheets since his debut (55) for Arsenal than any other goalkeeper in the Premier League.

The complete modern goalkeeper.

2. Declan Rice

Second again, ole, ole? Cheeky.

But Rice remains just one short of the top player award, just like he did in March's rankings.

Rice is the engine, heartbeat and insurance policy of Arsenal. His ability to dominate midfield battles, cover enormous distances and influence games in both boxes makes him almost irreplaceable.

1. Erling Haaland

A goalscoring phenomenon who now tops this chart, moving up three places from March. In that period he has scored 14 Premier League goals - almost double that of the next player on that list.

Haaland takes the crown from Bruno Fernandes.

Who are the big risers and fallers?

Just like Manchester United, Bruno Fernandes has tumbled. The United captain was sitting pretty at the summit of our Premier League top 25 rankings back in March. Eight months on? He's tumbled 10 places to 11th. Ouch.

It's a dramatic fall for a player who was previously regarded as the league's most influential performer. Fernandes remains a footballer of outrageous ability but United's struggles have made it increasingly difficult for their talisman to dominate matches.

And whose stolen his crown? A certain Norwegian goal machine.

Haaland has climbed three places from fourth to first. He's a cheat code. A footballing freak.

But the biggest shake-up comes further down the rankings.

A whopping 11 players have been given the boot out of the top 25. They are: Gianluigi Donnarumma, Jurrien Timber, Igor Thiago, Hugo Ekitike, Martin Zubimendi, Reece James, Bryan Mbeumo, Marc Guehi and Enzo Fernandez, with Bernardo Silva and Casemiro leaving the Premier League altogether.

Their replacements? Some serious star quality.

Cherki storms straight in at seventh, while Odegaard (10th), Isak (12th) and Palmer (13th) make eye-catching returns. Van Dijk (15th), Gross (17th), Konsa (19th), Doku (21st), Gibbs-White (22nd), Calafiori (23rd) and Alisson (25th) complete the new arrivals.
