Italian Grand Prix Lineup: Huge Penalties for Antonelli, Albon, Lawson Reshape the Field

TheTools · 05.09.2026 15:43 · source · permalink

Pierre Gasly is officially starting from pole position for the first time in his Formula 1 career, but the rest of the starting order for the 2026 Italian Grand Prix has been completely scrambled by the stewards. Following a dramatic qualifying session, a wave of strategic power unit changes and pending investigations has resulted in a heavily modified provisional starting grid.

Provisional Starting Grid for the 2026 Italian Grand Prix

The Engine Penalties

Several drivers will plummet down the order on Sunday after exceeding their seasonal allocation for power unit components. Because Monza’s high-speed layout offers excellent slipstreaming and overtaking opportunities, teams often use this circuit to strategically absorb these mandatory grid drops.

  • Andrea Kimi Antonelli: The current championship leader qualified seventh with a time of 1:22.093, but Mercedes chose to equip his car with a raft of new engine components. As a result, Antonelli will take a penalty that forces him to start from the back of the grid.
  • Alex Albon: The Williams driver was hit with a devastating 20-place grid penalty. The FIA confirmed that his FW48 was fitted with a fifth Internal Combustion Engine (ICE) and a fourth Control Electronics Unit (PU-CE), entirely exceeding the strict 2026 limits. He will join Antonelli at the back of the pack.
  • Liam Lawson: Red Bull also opted to take a heavy grid penalty for Lawson, dropping him down the starting order.

The Piastri Investigation

While McLaren’s Oscar Piastri put in a blistering lap of 1:21.966 to secure third place, his position on the second row is not yet locked in. Piastri was formally summoned by the FIA stewards following the session, meaning his final starting spot remains completely provisional pending the outcome of their investigation.
