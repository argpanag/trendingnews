Mauricio Pochettino: Man Utd strike agreement with Michael Carrick's potential successor

TheTools · 11.11.2026 18:34 · source · permalink

Manchester United powerbrokers set to make a final decision on Michael Carrick's future after Wednesday's Monaco summit; Mauricio Pochettino, who has a £7m release clause in the contract he signed with the USA after the World Cup, has been lined up as his replacement

Wednesday 11 November 2026 17:45, UK

Manchester United have reached a verbal agreement in principle with Mauricio Pochettino to take over as head coach.

Michael Carrick took training on Wednesday and officially remains United boss.

He is likely to be relieved of his duties before the end of the week, once Omar Berrada, the chief executive, and Jason Wilcox, the director of football, have returned from their meeting with co-owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe in Monaco on Wednesday.

Pochettino is then expected to be confirmed as Carrick's replacement sometime afterwards.

The former Chelsea and Tottenham manager is currently in charge of the USA national team and he could remain in charge for their games on Saturday and Wednesday - both against Haiti - unless an agreement with the US Soccer Federation can be reached to release him earlier.

Pochettino looks set to become United's eighth permanent boss since the retirement of Sir Alex Ferguson 13 years ago.

The £7m release clause in Pochettino's contract is not thought to be an issue for United.

Berrada and Wilcox met minority owner Ratcliffe at a pre-planned meeting in Monaco to review the club's start to the season.

Carrick was handed a stay of execution after United's 3-0 home defeat to Bournemouth in the Premier League last month, but momentum is now gathering behind a change in the dugout.

Why Poch can't turn down Man Utd job

United have held talks over appointing Pochettino on three separate occasions prior to this and came close to appointing him a few times, including in 2022 when they hired Erik ten Hag instead.

Ferguson has long admired Pochettino and often had long conversations with the former Spurs boss following his matches against United.

The pair were pictured at a Mayfair restaurant in May 2016 amid speculation about Pochettino, then Spurs head coach, taking over from Louis van Gaal at Old Trafford. United subsequently chose to appoint Jose Mourinho.

It is understood Pochettino loves his job with the USA but feels he cannot say no to United.

The 54-year-old is ready to return to the Premier League two years after he was sacked by Chelsea. Pochettino considers his time at Stamford Bridge as successful but ultimately feels it was not the right fit.

Man Utd's Poch pursuit comes full circle

Sky Sports' Laura Hunter:

Mauricio Pochettino and Manchester United have a well-established history. One where paths might have crossed long before now, numerous times, but somehow never managed to converge.

Could it be that we are finally to bear witness to the culmination of this elaborate 10-year dance of incompatible circumstances?

The Old Trafford hierarchy have always admired Pochettino from afar. He was on their radar while at Tottenham and again when it was clear his time at Paris Saint-Germain was coming to an end. The Argentine was eventually lured back to the Premier League but instead returned to London, signing with Chelsea.

All along this journey of complicated courtship, United were at play. There have been many sliding-door moments. At the time news broke of Chelsea's interest in Pochettino in 2023, United, coached by Erik ten Hag, had just been ripped to shreds by Sevilla.

The morning after that defeat all the back pages focussed on another humiliating capitulation in Europe. 'What the El was that?' one read. Another lay into the inadequacies of Harry Maguire's performance. No doubt both headlines have been recycled since. At United, the more things change, the more they stay the same.

Miraculously, it took another 18 months for the button to be pushed on Ten Hag. Four managers of varying permanency followed. Until another chance, a decade on from the first, has revealed itself to pursue the one that got away. Is this finally right place, right time for Pochettino?
