India and New Zealand shift to preparation for the 2027 World Cup on Tuesday for the 1st match of a 5-match ODI series at Eden Park in Auckland.

After India put away a five-match T20I series win by winning the first three contests, its tour in New Zealand continues on Tuesday with the first of five one-day international matches in preparation for next year’s World Cup at Eden Park in Auckland, New Zealand. The ODI series is part of a rare full international tour, with the sides to play two Test matches later this month. The host Blackcaps took a hit on Monday when Devon Conway sustained a concussion when he collided with Matt Henry during fielding drills and won’t play on Tuesday, at a minimum, with New Zealand already without Mark Chapman and Rachin Ravindra.

India enters the series without two big players in Jasprit Bumrah and Hardik Pandya. Shreyas Iyer figures to slot in at the No. 4 slot in the batting order in place of Ruturaj Gaikwad. The Men in Blue lost a three-match series to New Zealand at home back in January and enters this set with a 7-5 record in ODI play this year, most recently taking two of three from visiting West Indies from Sept. 27-Oct. 6. Captain Shubman Gill leads the world in runs in ODI play this year with 895 in just 11 innings.

New Zealand took three of five matches at West Indies in July and stands 6-5 in ODIs in 2026. That record includes a 2-1 loss at Bangladesh in April and a 2-1 series win in India back in January. The Blackcaps lost to the Men in Blue in the semifinals of the 2023 World Cup in India. The not-so-secret weapon for New Zealand is Daryl Mitchell, who scored four of his nine career ODI hundreds against India and stands as one of four players in the 2020s with more than 2,000 ODI runs with an average of 55 or better.

As for the venue, Eden Park still hosts international cricket because it predates the International Cricket Council’s standards for boundary length. While the ICC calls for no boundary of less than 65 yards from the center of the pitch, Eden Park has boundaries as short as 57 to 60 yards. The venue’s shape, being more rectangular than oval, presents challenges for setting fields.
