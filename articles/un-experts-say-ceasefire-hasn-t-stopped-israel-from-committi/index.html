UN Experts Say Ceasefire Hasn't Stopped Israel From Committing Genocide In Gaza

TheTools · 27.10.2026 07:39 · source · permalink

Like this article? Keep independent journalism alive. Support HuffPost.

The year-old ceasefire agreement brokered between Israel and Hamas and has not stopped the former from continuing its genocide against Palestinians in Gaza, according to a group of United Nations experts who also say Israel has committed war crimes and the crimes against humanity of extermination, persecution, torture, rape and other forms of ****** violence against Palestinians in the West Bank.

In a report released Monday, the UN Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory describe in graphic detail several cases of ****** assault by prison guards and soldiers on Palestinian detainees since October 2023. Israel has also waged "a systematic attack against civilians" in the West Bank through military-enabled settler violence, it continued.

The report added that Israel was "continuing to commit genocidal acts with the genocidal intent to destroy, in whole or in part, the Palestinians in Gaza," saying much of the harm is a direct result of Israel's expansion of territorial control during the ceasefire period.

"The intentional killing and injuring of Palestinian civilians and destruction of homes in the West Bank combined with Israel's persistent denial of responsibility form part of a broader strategy to exterminate and displace Palestinians and impose conditions of life that are unliveable," commission chair Srinivasan Muralidhar said in a statement.

Israel's mission in Geneva responded by calling for the commission's dissolution, saying on X that the report marked an example of the group's "misinformation campaign to demonize the state of Israel and portray its very existence as criminal."

The same commission concluded last year that Israel is committing war crimes, crimes against humanity and the crime of genocide against Palestinians in Gaza, calling on countries to stop sending weapons to Israel and ensure people within their jurisdiction aren't aiding or inciting it.
