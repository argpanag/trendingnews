First look at Lindsay Lohan filming new Netflix rom-com with The Gentlemen star

TheTools · 06.10.2026 07:00 · source · permalink

The star was spotted filming in Toronto.

Lindsay Lohan has been spotted for the first time on the set of her new Netflix movie Return to You. The actor looked sharp in a black dress and matching blazer as she was snapped filming scenes outside an office building in Toronto on 1 October.

Lohan was seen clutching a coffee cup as cameras rolled on the upcoming romantic comedy, which is set in 2008.

The former child star plays a powerful but lonely lawyer whose life takes an unexpected turn when she accidentally receives a deeply emotional DVD recorded by a dying woman for her husband.

Moved by what she sees, Lohan's character uses clues contained in the recording to set out on a mission to track down the man it was intended for – with the journey ultimately leading her towards a romance of her own.

It was announced earlier this week that The Parent Trap star – who also serves as a producer on the movie – will appear opposite Crazy Rich Asians and The Gentlemen actor Henry Golding, who plays her love interest Ben.

Saturday Night Live star Ego Nwodim, actor-comedian Randall Park, whose credits include The Residence and Always Be My Maybe, and Alias and Legally Blonde star Victor Garber round out the cast.

The project will also see Lohan reunite with director Mark Waters more than two decades after they worked together on 2003's Freaky Friday and 2004 teen comedy classic Mean Girls.

Lohan is no stranger to a Netflix rom-com, having enjoyed a string of releases on the streaming service since making her movie comeback.

She starred opposite Chord Overstreet in 2022 festive romance Falling for Christmas, followed by Irish Wish with Ed Speleers in 2024 and Kristin Chenoweth in Our Little Secret later that same year.

More recently, Lohan reprised her role as Anna Coleman opposite Jamie Lee Curtis in Disney sequel Freakier Friday, which was released in 2025.

Netflix has yet to announce an official release date for Return to You, although the movie is expected to arrive on the streamer sometime in 2027.
