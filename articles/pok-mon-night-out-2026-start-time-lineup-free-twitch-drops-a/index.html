Pokémon Night Out 2026: Start time, lineup, free Twitch drops, and how to watch for free

TheTools · 24.10.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

If you weren't able to snag tickets to see Pokémon's sold-out 30th-anniversary EDM spectacle live in LA, you don't have to miss out on the party.

Pokémon Night Out is available to watch for free online on Oct. 24. Headlined by Marshmello with support from Alison Wonderland, the event promises to unite "the eclectic world of Pokémon with high-energy electronic dance music for a truly unforgettable evening" at the Intuit Dome.

When is Pokémon Night Out?

The 2026 Pokémon Night Out starts at 7:30 p.m. PT / 10:30 p.m. ET on Oct. 24. This special event takes place at the Intuit Dome in Los Angeles.

How to watch Pokémon Night Out for free

You can tune into the official livestream across two major platforms for free:

  • Twitch: Stream live on the official Pokémon Twitch Channel

  • YouTube: Stream live on the official Pokémon YouTube Channel

Twitch: Stream live on the official Pokémon Twitch Channel

YouTube: Stream live on the official Pokémon YouTube Channel

Livestream viewers will be treated to a DJ set from electronic artist and multi-instrumentalist JVNA during the intermission. This set is exclusive to the Pokémon Night Out livestream.

We've linked the YouTube stream above, but you should know that if you watch the concert on Twitch for at least 30 minutes with your Pokémon Trainer Central account linked, you'll automatically earn an exclusive Pokémon Night Out Baseball Jersey code for your Pokémon GO avatar.

Watching Pokémon Night Out on YouTube or Twitch costs absolutely nothing. It's the perfect Saturday night activity for Pokémon fans all around the world.
