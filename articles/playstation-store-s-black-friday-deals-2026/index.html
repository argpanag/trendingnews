PlayStation Store’s Black Friday Deals 2026 

TheTools · 03.11.2026 11:00 · source · permalink

This year, the sale starts early with discounts on PlayStation Store beginning November 4. 

This year, PlayStation’s Black Friday Deals kick off early on PlayStation Store! Starting tomorrow, Wednesday November 4, you’ll find deep discounts up to 75% off*, across thousands of PS5, PS4 and PS VR2 games, expansions, and DLC. 

Below is a small preview of just some of the titles that will be discounted as part of the sale. Their discounts, and those of the full list of games in the promotion, will be revealed on the dedicated Black Friday promotion page on PlayStation Store when the Black Friday Deals begin. 

The sale runs through to Monday, November 30, new titles join November 16, and some items leave before the promotion’s end, so be sure to check the dedicated Black Friday promotion page for updates and discount pricing in your region*.
