GRAND ISLAND, Neb. — A Grand Island woman faces charges after police said she drove through a soccer field while children were playing.

Shoni Bussinger, 28, is charged in Hall County Court with multiple counts including child abuse, failure to stop following a crash and driving under the influence.

According to an arrest affidavit, around 7 p.m. Thursday, Grand Island Police responded to numerous reports of a vehicle driving recklessly at a high rate of speed near Grand Island Senior High School. The affidavit said the vehicle left the roadway, drove through a fence and entered a soccer field where numerous children were playing at the time.

The affidavit said Bussinger’s son was a passenger in the vehicle.

GIPD said the vehicle was found abandoned several blocks away due to sustaining disabling damage.

The affidavit said Bussinger was located walking in the area of 15th Street and Grand Island Avenue alongside her minor son. GIPD said Bussinger refused officers’ commands to stop and smelled of alcohol.

Bussinger was subsequently arrested and booked into the Hall County Jail. Her bond has been set at $150,000, 10% of which she would need to post to be released from jail. Her next court date is set for 2:30 p.m. Nov. 17.

Court records said Bussinger has two previous convictions of leaving the scene of a crash and one prior conviction of DUI.
