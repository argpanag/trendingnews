Home and Away teases "killer ending" in most emotional season finale ever

TheTools · 20.11.2026 15:35 · source · permalink

Plenty of drama in store!

Home and Away spoilers follow from Australian-pace episodes, which some UK viewers may prefer to avoid.

Home and Away is set for a dramatic season finale that fans do not want to miss. The Australian soap is set to air the final episode of the 2026 season Down Under in early December, though UK viewers will have to wait until 2027 to see the action unfold.

A new trailer has been released giving fans a teaser of what has been described as "the most emotional finale there’s ever been".

Related: Home and Away star Sarah Roberts reveals disappointment over Willow's exit

The advert ended by asking if fans are ready "Are you ready for a killer ending?" as it shows an underground party taking place.

One of the characters featured in the trailer is cardiothoracic nurse Jo Langham, who has turned to alcohol after feeling responsible for the death of a patient, who contracted sepsis following an operation.

Following an investigation, the medical negligence committee ruled that Jo only needs to undergo a refresher course.

Though she avoided a more severe punishment, she still feels guilty, and the trailer shows her father David comforting her while she is in tears.

In the teaser, fans also see Cash, who has plenty on his mind, going for a run as his partner Eden experiences health issues.

After undergoing a bone marrow transplant that saved her life, Eden suffers an infection. It remains to be seen if Eden will recover or if her health will deteriorate.

Related: Home and Away reveals UK finale drama in 14 spoiler pictures

Roo is shown in the trailer, back home after spending weeks hiding out in the bush and was feared to be dead. She was on the run from Phillip Osmond, who she saw kill his wife Sharna.

The trailer shows her pull back as someone reaches out to her, and it is her father Alf. Spoilers reveal Roo is due to testify in court, and Alf reassures her that the community will support her as she does so.

Levi is also seen shocking Mackenzie with a proposal, while David and Amelia's relationship may be in jeopardy. There's also a shot of Sonny, who may be leaving the soap next year, according to rumours.

Home and Away drops new episodes on weekdays at 6am on 5’s streaming service with a 6.30pm screening on 5STAR.

In Australia, the show airs Mondays to Thursdays at 7pm on Channel 7 and streams on 7Plus.
