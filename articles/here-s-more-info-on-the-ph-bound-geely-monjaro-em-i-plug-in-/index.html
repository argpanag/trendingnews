Here’s more info on the PH-bound Geely Monjaro EM-i plug-in hybrid

TheTools · 18.09.2026 05:45 · source · permalink

Geely Philippines has already confirmed the new Monjaro’s arrival by next month. Recently, the plug-in hybrid SUV has received homologation papers from the Land Transportation Office, giving us more information about this upcoming model. 

As a refresher, the specific variant coming to our shores is the Monjaro EM-i Luxury. It has a 1.5-liter four-cylinder gasoline engine and dual electric motors (comprised of a permanent-magnet synchronous motor and an induction motor) producing a combined output of 410hp and 723Nm of torque, delivering power to all four wheels.

The LTO papers confirm that the Monjaro can charge its battery from 20% to 100% in 4.2 hours using an AC charger, while DC charging can top up the battery from 30% to 80% in just 15 minutes. The documents also list a maximum speed of 190kph and an electric-only range of 116km.

This information matches the Monjaro’s specs abroad, which list the combined range at 1,095km. Furthermore, the e-AWD system packs some light trail capability with terrain and driving modes such as Off-Road, Sand, Snow, Power, Hybrid, and Pure—all of which can be selected depending on the scenario. 

Meanwhile, driver aids to be expected include adaptive cruise control, intelligent cruise control, lane-keep assist, lane-departure warning, lane-change assist, blind-spot detection, and a 540-degree panoramic view. Additional things to look forward to are ventilated and massage seats for the first row.
