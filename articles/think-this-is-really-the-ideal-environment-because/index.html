Think this is really the ideal environment because …

TheTools · 16.11.2026 01:39 · source · permalink

Victor Wembanyama praised the culture around the San Antonio Spurs after a win crushing 123-85 win over the Golden State Warriors on Friday. The center talked to media after the game answering a question in French about the culture in San Antonio. "What I like most is the respect they show us. Sometimes I fell a little uncomfortable being placed on such a pedestal," he said. "I think this is really the ideal environment because even in other cities, where we're not the home team, people aren't as respectful. People can be very persistent or intrusive sometimes, but never here."
