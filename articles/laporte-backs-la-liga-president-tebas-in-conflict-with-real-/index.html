Laporte Backs La Liga President Tebas in Conflict with Real Madrid

TheTools · 17.10.2026 19:12 · source · permalink

Barcelona president Joan Laporta has expressed support for La Liga president Javier Tebas in his ongoing conflict with Real Madrid, according to Mundo Deportivo. The Catalans are set to face the Madrid side on October 25.

Laporta stated that Barcelona is currently focused on their match against Real Betis, and there will be time to discuss the El Clásico later. He noted that the club is fortunate to have Tebas, who has the necessary experience for such matters.

Moreover, Tebas has a good sense for countering the attacks that Real Madrid is launching against La Liga. It seems they want to destroy everything: La Liga, the Technical Committee of Referees (CTA). They are also dissatisfied with the Royal Spanish Football Federation (RFEF); they are unhappy with everything.

On October 16, Real Madrid issued a statement demanding the immediate resignation of Tebas from his positions as president of La Liga and vice president of the RFEF.

In the same statement, the club called for a comprehensive reform of the refereeing system. AS reported that Real Madrid believes there has been a conspiracy against the club by football authorities since the start of the La Liga season. The situation escalated with a four-match suspension for forward Vinícius Júnior.

Tebas questioned the validity of the accusations against referees and urged Real Madrid to provide specific evidence.

After eight matchdays of the 2026/27 La Liga season, Barcelona sits at the top of the table with 24 points, while Real Madrid is in fourth place with 18 points. The El Clásico will take place on October 25 at 23:00 GMT+3 in Barcelona.
