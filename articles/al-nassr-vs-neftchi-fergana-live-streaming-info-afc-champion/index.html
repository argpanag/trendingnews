Cristiano Ronaldo’s Al Nassr will face Neftchi Fergana in its second AFC Champions League match in Riyadh on Monday. | Photo Credit: AFP

Saudi Arabian side Al Nassr will take on Uzbekistan’s Neftchi Fergana in its second league-stage match of the AFC Champions League Elite 2026-27 at the Al-Awwal Park in Riyadh on Monday.

Al Nassr began its campaign with a 0-4 defeat to UAE’s Al Ain and will look to secure its first points of the competition. Neftchi, meanwhile, opened with a 1-0 victory over Iraq’s Air Force SC.

Cristiano Ronaldo, who featured in Al Nassr’s recent 3-0 Saudi Pro League victory over Al Diriyah, is expected to be available for Monday’s match, although his place in the starting line-up is yet to be confirmed.

The AFC Champions League Elite 2026-27 match between Al Nassr and Neftchi Fergana will take place at the Al-Awwal Park in Riyadh on October 12.

The match is scheduled to kick off at 11:45 PM IST.

The Al Nassr vs Neftchi Fergana AFC Champions League Elite 2026-27 match will be streamed live on the FanCode app and website in India.
