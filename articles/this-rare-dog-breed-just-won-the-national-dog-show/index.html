This Rare Dog Breed Just Won the National Dog Show

TheTools · 26.11.2026 19:04 · source · permalink

He’s the best boy of all.

Thanksgiving is all about the attitude of gratitude. But it also wouldn’t be the same holiday we know and love without all the holiday fixings, from the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade on the TV to the mashed potatoes on the table.

One holiday classic we look forward to every year is the National Dog Show Presented by Purina, where dogs of all breeds compete to be Best in Show. With so many wagging tails and dogs of absolutely all shapes and sizes, it’s a Thanksgiving must-watch for animal lovers. That’s why over 20 million viewers tune in every year!

This year’s competition is extra special because it’s the show’s 25th anniversary—and a surprising dog breed won Best in Show.

This year’s top dog was Rocker, a Black and Tan Coonhound from Bedford, Iowa. The Black and Tan Coonhound is a scent hound that was bred to help hunt raccoons by chasing them up a tree. They are known for their long, velvety ears—and Rocker’s ears are part of what impressed the judges! “Those long ears actually catch the scent and bring it up to the dog,” explained Best in Show judge Lorraine Bisso, who praised Rocker’s appearance and athleticism.

This is not Rocker’s first win, either—it’s actually his 15th Best in Show. But because Rocker loves to compete, it’s still exciting every time. “He had a great time. He loves the crowd. He comes alive,” said Rocker’s handler, Mandy Clevenger, who has handled Rocker since he was six months old. “I think this is a great venue for him. He loves all the commotion, all the people, all day. He's had the best day.”

Rocker is expected to compete at the Westminster Dog Show in New York City early next year.

The following are the other 2026 National Dog Show Group Winners:

  • Non-Sporting – a Lhasa Apso named “Kissy” of Conroe, Texas (also the runner-up pup)
  • Working – a Newfoundland named “Biggie Smalls” of Boxford, Massachusetts
  • Terrier – a Norfolk Terrier named “Rambo” of Bowmansville, Pennsylvania
  • Toy – a Pomeranian named “Aslan” of Alpharetta, Georgia
  • Sporting – a Curly-Coated Retriever named “Raisin” of Tracys Landing, Maryland
  • Herding – a Pembroke Welsh Corgi named “Brandi” of Merced, California

If you missed watching the National Dog Show during turkey day, don’t fret. The show will be rebroadcast on NBC on Sunday, November 29, from 4-6 p.m. following the Purina Incredible Dog Challenge.
