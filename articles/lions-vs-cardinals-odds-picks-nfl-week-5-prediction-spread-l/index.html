Lions vs. Cardinals betting preview

These defenses need a lot of work done. The Lions are giving up the most yards and passing yards in the NFL this season and are 31st in scoring defense at 31.8. Only New Orleans is worse.

That's music to the ears of Jacoby Brissett and the Cardinals. Arizona has its faults, to be sure, but it has a lot of playmakers on offense. Tight end Trey McBride has 46 targets and has converted those looks to 33 catches, 242 yards and two touchdowns. Michael Wilson has 258 yards and a score this year, Marvin Harrison Jr. found the end zone last week and the team has an intriguing running back room with Jeremiyah Love and Tyler Allgeier. 

The Cardinals' defense is also suspect, and the Lions have one of the best offenses in the NFL this year. Arizona is 29th in scoring defense and the Lions are fifth in points per game at 29.8. Jared Goff has nine touchdowns to no interceptions, and Jahmyr Gibbs has seven total touchdowns on the year.
