Amanda Seyfried & Husband Thomas Sadoski Confirm Breakup, Say They've 'Been Separated for a While'

TheTools · 15.09.2026 22:20 · source · permalink

Amanda Seyfried and Thomas Sadoski are ending their marriage.

The longtime couple have confirmed their breakup in a new statement, saying that they’ve been separated for “a while.”

Amanda, 40, and Thomas, 50 met in 2015 while working together on the off-Broadway play The Way We Get By and they played love interests in the movie The Last Word later that year. They were confirmed to be a real-life couple in early 2016 and then got married in secret in early 2017.

Amanda Seyfried and Thomas Sadoski release joint statement on breakup

The couple confirmed their split in a joint statement provided to People.

“We have been separated for a while, and it’s proven to be the right move for us and our family unit. We’ve got each other forever—and it’s GOOD,” they said.

Amanda Seyfried and Thomas Sadoski share two kids together

The couple welcomed their first child into the world in 2017, just days after they got married. Their daughter Nina is currently 9.

Amanda and Thomas are also parents to son Thomas Jr., who turns 6 this month.

Amanda Seyfried put her Hollywood Hills home on the market last month

The breakup news comes just weeks after Architectural Digest reported that Amanda is selling her Hollywood Hills home for $3.2 million. She bought the house in 2011 for $1.85 million.

The outlet noted that Amanda is continuing to live on the east coast.

Over the summer, Amanda suffered a very personal loss.
