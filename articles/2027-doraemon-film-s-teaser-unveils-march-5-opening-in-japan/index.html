2027 Doraemon Film's Teaser Unveils March 5 Opening in Japan

TheTools · 16.09.2026 00:14 · source · permalink

Entertainment news website Cinema Today briefly posted on Monday (and reposted on Tuesday) that Eiga Doraemon: Nobita no Jōki Jikansha (Doraemon the Movie: Nobita's Steam Time Locomotive), the 2027 Doraemon film, will open in Japan on March 5, 2027. It also posted the film's teaser video and teaser visual.

The new film's story begins as Nobita and his friends time-travel to 19th-century Victorian London.

Rushio Moriyama, a storyboarder and episode director for a number of episodes of the Doraemon anime since 2020, is directing the film. Makoto Ueda (The Tatami Galaxy, Tatami Time Machine Blues, Penguin Highway) is penning the film's script.

The 2026 Doraemon film, Eiga Doraemon: Shin Nobita no Kaitei Kiganjō (Doraemon the Movie: New Nobita and the Castle of the Undersea Devil), opened in Japan on February 27.

Sources: Eiga Doraemon: Nobita no Jōki Jikansha anime film's website, Cinema Today
