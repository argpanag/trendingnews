Match Preview

BK Häcken FF host Manchester City Women at the Nordic Wellness Arena in Gothenburg in a Women's Champions League fixture with implications for both clubs' progression through the competition.

Elena Sadiku's Häcken side come into this match in reasonable domestic form, having beaten Piteå IF 2-0 in their most recent Damallsvenskan outing. Their European campaign has been less decisive, though, with draws against Paris FC and Chelsea FC Women sandwiching that league win.

Manchester City Women arrive in Sweden having lost their last match, a 1-0 WSL defeat to Chelsea at Stamford Bridge. That result will sting, but Andree Jeglertz's side showed their quality in Europe just days earlier, dismantling Inter 4-1 in the Women's Champions League.

City sit sixth in the Women's Champions League standings, with Häcken placed fifteenth. The gap in European standing makes this a significant test for the Swedish side at home.

Jeglertz will want a response after the Chelsea defeat, and a road win in Gothenburg would consolidate City's position in the top half of the table. Häcken, for their part, need points badly if they are to avoid an early exit from the competition.
