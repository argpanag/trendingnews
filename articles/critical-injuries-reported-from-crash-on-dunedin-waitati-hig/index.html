Critical injuries reported from crash on Dunedin-Waitati Highway

TheTools · 11.09.2026 18:22 · source · permalink
PHOTO

One person was taken to hospital with critical injuries after a two-vehicle crash on State Highway 1 north of Dunedin on Wednesday night.

The incident happened about 6.30pm and led to the closure SH1, police said in a statement.

Police advised motorists to avoid the area and to take alternative routes, if possible.

The road reopened, with a restricted speed limit, about 8pm.

The police serious crash unit had been informed. — Allied Media
