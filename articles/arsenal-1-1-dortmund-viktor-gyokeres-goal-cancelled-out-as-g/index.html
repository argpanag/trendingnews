Arsenal 1-1 Dortmund: Viktor Gyokeres goal cancelled out as Gunners frustrated in Champions League

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:08 · source · permalink

Match report as Arsenal are denied victory by Borussia Dortmund in a Champions League encounter at the Emirates Stadium; Viktor Gyokeres scored a spectacular opener but Maximilian Beier equalised as the Gunners wasted a string of chances

Football Features Writer @nicholaspwright

Tuesday 24 November 2026 21:57, UK

Arsenal were punished for failing to take their chances for the second game in a row as Maximilian Beier clinched a 1-1 draw for Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League.

Viktor Gyokeres gave Arsenal an early lead with a stunning strike from distance but the game followed a similar pattern to Saturday's 2-2 draw with Newcastle as they were unable to capitalise on a one-sided first half before conceding after the break.

Dortmund's leveller came when Beier slotted a clinical finish past Kepa Arrizabalaga, standing in for the injured David Raya, following a rampaging run from defender Joane Gadou.

Arsenal racked up 30 shots and had a string of chances to win it in the closing stages, hitting the woodwork through Jurrien Timber's deflected cut-back and again through Declan Rice's low shot.

Gyokeres also spurned opportunities, firing over from close range from Martin Odegaard's pass shortly before half-time, then heading awkwardly wide from a few yards out when it looked easier to score from another Odegaard ball.

Dortmund had dangerous moments too, including a Jobe Bellingham goal ruled out for offside and a penalty appeal for a handball by Gyokeres following a corner.

Arsenal remain unbeaten in the Champions League but the draw causes them to drop two places in the league phase table to fifth before they resume their Premier League campaign at home to Manchester City, live on Sky Sports on Sunday.

Arsenal fail to kill off another opponent

Arsenal's total of 30 shots was their highest in the Champions League either this season or last. But only one of them ended up in the net. Only four of them were even on target.

There were clear parallels with Saturday's game at St James' Park. Again, Arsenal were outstanding in the first half. Again, though, they reached the interval with only one goal to show for their efforts.

Dortmund, like Newcastle, came out with renewed belief, and a lot more intensity. Even before Beier's equaliser, the same player had missed a glorious chance when he failed to connect with a Joey Veerman cross. Arsenal were suddenly on the back foot.

They regained control after Dortmund's leveller, albeit while avoiding a scare when Jobe Bellingham had a goal ruled out for offside. But their attempts to break down the visitors were unsuccessful. Good positions were wasted. They should have been out of sight.

Arteta couldn't believe it when the otherwise impressive Gyokeres skewed his header wide from Odegaard's cross. The Arsenal boss was on his knees when Timber's cut-back ricocheted off the post.

It is positive that they are creating so many chances. Their performance featured another bright showing from Noni Madueke. But they will need to find some cutting edge against City.

Arteta: Unbelievable we didn't score more

Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta: "I think it was a brilliant performance.

"The way we played, bearing in mind the opposition that we played today, is something to be very, very pleased about.

"There is very little more that you can demand from the team.

"But obviously there is the feeling that we should reward ourselves much more when you put that kind of performance, with the amount of chances and situations.

"There is an element of luck and there is an element of us finishing the actions better. We need to insist, because I think what the team has done and shown today is the right direction.

"I love the way the team played. I think there's no question how superior, how dominant, how much of a threat we were, how connected everybody was to the game. It was great to watch.

"It was unbelievable that sometimes the ball hasn't ended up in the back of the net. That's my feeling."

Kovac: VAR should have checked Gyokeres handball

Borussia Dortmund boss Niko Kovac: "I thought VAR should have looked at the handball. It would have been worth a check.

"They are trying to intervene less, but it doesn't mean you should not intervene when you should do.

"In such a situation, you have to at least have someone in the VAR room saying, 'Let's just look at it.'

"I am not crying about it. It is what it is. We got a point. It is a big point away to Arsenal, a big one for all of us, our fans, our team, and the club."

Arsenal's shot record - Opta stats

  • Arsenal's 30 shots is their highest total on record in a Champions League game (since 2003/04).
  • It is also the Gunners' most in a game that they failed to win across all competitions since December 2023 against West Ham, when they also had 30.
  • Arsenal have failed to win a home game in the group stage of major European competition for the first time since November 2019 (2-1 defeat vs Eintracht Frankfurt), ending a run of 18 successive victories - the longest such winning run by any side in major European competition history
