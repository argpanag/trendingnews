Kerry has been chosen as one of seven locations nationally for new state-led early learning facilities.

Killarney has been selected, alongside venues in four other counties.

The proposed projects announced today largely involve the purchasing and fit-out of existing buildings, and the selection of not-for-profit providers to run the centres.

The goal is to provide early learning and childcare places in areas with very large undersupplies, such as Killarney.

The department says that detailed design works are underway and that the projects are expected to be operational next year (2027).

The announcement has been welcomed by Fianna Fáil TD Michael Cahill and Fine Gael Senator Mike Kennelly.
