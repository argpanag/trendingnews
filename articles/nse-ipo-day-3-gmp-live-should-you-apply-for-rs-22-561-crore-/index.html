NSE IPO Day 3 GMP Live: Should you apply for Rs 22,561-crore issue on last day? Check GMP, listing date, brokerage notes and more

TheTools · 21.09.2026 04:44 · source · permalink

Today, September 21, is the last day for investors to subscribe to the Rs 22,561.57-crore NSE IPO, giving those who have yet to apply their final chance to participate in the mega issue. The IPO was fully subscribed on the second day of bidding itself, with the issue receiving strong demand from investors across categories.

NSE IPO: Technology, cybersecurity risks flagged by Religare Broking

Religare Broking said technology infrastructure is critical for NSE, making system failures, cybersecurity issues and difficulties in adopting new technologies potential risks.

"Dependence on third parties and intermediaries also creates operational and fraud risks," the brokerage said

NSE IPO: Options dependence remains key risk, says Religare Broking

Religare Broking said NSE faces significant risks from any decline in the volume and value of transactions, which could reduce transaction-based income and adversely affect growth.

"NSE faces significant risks from any decline in the volume and value of transactions, which could reduce transaction-based income and adversely affect growth. Its high dependence on transaction charges, particularly from the options business, increases sensitivity to trading activity," the brokerage said.

NSE IPO: Religare Broking flags regulatory risks to trading volumes

Religare Broking said regulatory developments, including SEBI measures on options trading, remain key factors influencing trading volumes and transaction-based income.

"While the long-term growth opportunity in India's capital markets remains favourable, the sustainability of earnings growth will depend on trading activity, regulatory stability and continued market participation," the brokerage said.

"Given the balance between structural growth opportunities, regulatory uncertainties and valuation considerations, we assign a Neutral rating," Religare Broking said

NSE IPO: Religare Broking assigns Neutral rating, flags valuation and regulatory risks

Religare Broking, in its IPO note, said the NSE IPO presents a "balanced outlook", with financial performance in FY26 impacted by regulatory changes and moderation in trading activity, while operational metrics remained resilient, supported by increasing investor participation, fund mobilisation and market presence.

"At a P/E of 42.9x, the valuation reflects the company's established market position and future growth potential, while leaving limited room for earnings disappointments," Religare Broking said.

NSE IPO: Valuation offers favourable entry point despite regulatory headwinds, says Angel One

Angel One acknowledged near-term regulatory headwinds to derivatives volumes but said it believes the valuation offers a favourable entry point given NSE's strong competitive position and earnings potential.

"We recommend Subscribe for the IPO," the brokerage said.

NSE IPO: Strong market position, earnings potential support issue, says Angel One

Angel One said NSE's "dominant market position, significantly higher revenue and profitability, strong market share in equity derivatives, and long-term structural growth in Indian capital markets" provide further comfort to investors.

NSE IPO valuation attractive versus BSE, says Angel One

Angel One has highlighted NSE's valuation compared with its key listed peer, BSE, and said the exchange's strong market position and earnings potential provide further comfort to investors.

"At the upper price band of Rs 1,785, NSE is valued at a post-issue P/E of 35.4x, compared with BSE's P/E of 54.2x, making the issue attractive relative to its key listed peer," Angel One said in its IPO note.

NSE IPO: IPO proceeds will go to selling shareholders

Since the NSE IPO is an OFS, the money raised through the issue will go to the existing shareholders selling their shares. The proceeds will not be added to NSE's cash balance or used by the exchange for new projects, expansion or other business requirements.

-330

NSE IPO: Entire issue is an offer for sale

The Rs 22,561.57-crore NSE IPO is entirely an offer for sale (OFS), meaning NSE itself is not issuing fresh shares to raise funds. Existing shareholders are selling part of their holdings through the public issue.

NSE IPO: Allotment on September 22, listing on September 24

The NSE IPO has been priced in the range of Rs 1,700-1,785 per share. The allotment is expected to be finalised on September 22, while the shares are scheduled to list on the BSE on September 24.

NSE IPO: Issue subscribed in full on Day 2

The NSE IPO received bids for the entire number of shares on offer by the end of Day 2 of bidding, signalling strong demand for the issue. The public offer opened for subscription on September 17 and closes today, September 21.
