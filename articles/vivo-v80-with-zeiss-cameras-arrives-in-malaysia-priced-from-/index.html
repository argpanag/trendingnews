Vivo V80 with Zeiss cameras arrives in Malaysia, priced from RM2,399

TheTools · 19.11.2026 17:24 · source · permalink
    bookmark

Vivo unveiled its latest V80 smartphone today (Nov 19), featuring a 6.59in AMOLED display and a triple rear camera system co-engineered with Zeiss.

The camera setup comprises a 50-megapixel Zeiss Super Telephoto Camera with 3x optical zoom and up to 100x digital zoom, a 50-megapixel Zeiss main camera with optical image stabilisation and a Sony LYTIA 700V sensor recommended for low-light photography, and an 8-megapixel Zeiss ultrawide-angle camera.

For portraits, users can choose between two Zeiss modes. The 100mm Center-Stage Portrait uses Planar-style bokeh and a softer, low-saturation platinum tone for close-up shots, while the 85mm Center-Stage Portrait offers a wider composition with Sonnar-style bokeh and warm-cool cinematic tones. Vivo recommends the latter for three-quarter-length portraits.

On the front, the V80 features a 50-megapixel Zeiss Group Selfie Camera with a wide-angle lens and autofocus.

The front and rear cameras also support 4K 60FPS video recording.

The Vivo V80 introduces a Starwish Purple variant with a Laser Holographic Lithography finish etched onto the back cover to create a three-dimensional effect that changes with light and movement.

Alongside Starwish Purple, the model is also available in Wander Green and Vast Gray.

The device runs on a Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Processor with capabilities to support high-frame-rate gaming up to 144 FPS.

It is also equipped with a 7,200mAh BlueVolt battery and 90W FlashCharge, which the company claims can charge the device up to 50% in 30 minutes.

All models come with 8GB RAM and 16GB Extended RAM.

The Vivo V80 is priced at RM2,399 (before rebate: RM2,599) for the 128GB variant, RM2,699 (before rebate: RM2,899) for 256GB and RM3,299 (before rebate: RM3,499) for 512GB.
