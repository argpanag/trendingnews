Pete Hegseth’s impeachment: How each House member voted | CNN Politics

TheTools · 11.11.2026 13:04 · source · permalink

The House of Representatives voted on Tuesday evening to send an effort to impeach Pete Hegseth to committee, effectively killing the movement to oust the defense secretary before the end of the year.

The vote was 211-204. While Republicans will still be in charge of both chambers until the new Congress is sworn in January, Democrats in part gave House GOP leaders a window to sideline the impeachment effort due to absences. Eleven House Democrats did not vote on the measure, more than the seven-vote margin.

Some of the Democrats were unable to be there due to a traffic incident. Former House Speaker Nancy Pelosi, meanwhile, was in San Francisco for a Veterans Day event.

Seven House Republicans also did not vote on the measure Tuesday, which was led by GOP Rep. Thomas Massie of Kentucky – the only Republican member to vote against tabling the measure.
