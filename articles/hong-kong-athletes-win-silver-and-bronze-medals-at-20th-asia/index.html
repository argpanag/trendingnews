Hong Kong Athletes Win Silver and Bronze Medals at 20th Asian Games

SCST congratulates Cheung Ka-long, Ryan Choi, Leung Chin-yu, Harris Ho and Ng Ka-Ki on winning medals in Asian Games

The Secretary for Culture, Sports and Tourism, Miss Rosanna Law, today (September 25) extended her congratulations to fencing athletes Cheung Ka-long, Ryan Choi, Leung Chin-yu and Harris Ho on winning a silver medal for the Hong Kong, China Delegation in Men's Foil Team; and artistic gymnastics athlete Ng Ka-Ki on winning a bronze medal in Men's Vault at the 20th Asian Games.

The Secretary for Culture, Sports and Tourism, Miss Rosanna Law, Photo source: reference image

Miss Law said, "Cheung Ka-long, Ryan Choi, Leung Chin-yu and Harris Ho gave their all and fought to the very end, securing a podium finish for the Hong Kong, China Delegation in Men's Foil Team for the sixth consecutive times and winning the applause of the Hong Kong citizens. Artistic gymnastics athlete Ng Ka-ki, who participated in the Asian Games for the second time, has overcome a recurring injury to display peak form on the vault, delivering an outstanding performance and successfully winning the bronze medal! The Hong Kong, China Delegation will be achieving further success in the remaining events."

The Hong Kong, China Delegation has so far won twenty-five medals, namely four gold, eleven silver and ten bronze, at the Asian Games. The two medals won today are as follows:

SCST congratulates Daphne Chan, Valerie Cheng, Janelle Leung, Sophia Wu, Royce Chan, Hugo Ho, Aaron Ho, Leung Tin-ching, Chan Sin-yuk, Li Hin-hei, Wong Yuk-cheung and Yeh Man-ho on winning medals in Asian Games

The Secretary for Culture, Sports and Tourism, Miss Rosanna Law, today (September 26) extended her congratulations to fencing athletes Daphne Chan, Valerie Cheng, Janelle Leung and Sophia Wu on winning a bronze medal for the Hong Kong, China Delegation in Women's Foil Team; fencing athletes Royce Chan, Hugo Ho, Aaron Ho and Leung Tin-ching on winning a bronze medal in Men's Sabre Team; squash athlete Chan Sin-yuk on winning a bronze medal in Women's Singles; and esports athletes Li Hin-hei, Wong Yuk-cheung and Yeh Man-ho on winning a bronze medal in fighting games at the 20th Asian Games.

Miss Law said, "Daphne Chan, Valerie Cheng, Janelle Leung, Sophia Wu, Royce Chan, Hugo Ho, Aaron Ho and Leung Tin-ching worked together and fought until the end when facing strong opponents. They lived up to expectations and secured two more team medals for the Hong Kong, China Delegation in Women's Foil Team and Men's Sabre Team. Squash athlete Chan Sin-yuk delivered an outstanding performance and has reached the podium at the Asian Games for the second consecutive time. Wong Yuk-cheung, Yeh Man-ho and Li Hin-hei demonstrated brilliant skills and co-ordination in three fighting games, winning a medal for the Hong Kong, China esports team. Today, Hong Kong, China athletes also successfully advanced to the semi-finals in a number of events. I am confident that they will continue to achieve excellent results in the remaining competitions."

The Hong Kong, China Delegation has so far won twenty-nine medals, namely four gold, eleven silver and fourteen bronze, at the Asian Games. The newly added four medals are as follows:
