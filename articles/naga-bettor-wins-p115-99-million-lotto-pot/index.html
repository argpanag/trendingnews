Naga bettor wins P115.99 million lotto pot

TheTools · 18.10.2026 14:28 · source · permalink

MANILA, Philippines — A bettor in Naga City, Camarines Sur won the jackpot in the 6/45 Mega Lotto drawn on Friday night, according to the Philippine Charity Sweepstake Office (PCSO).

The player correctly guessed the six-digit winning combination 08-22-19-10-35-27, which carried a total jackpot prize of P115.99 million.

PCSO general manager Melquiades Robles said the winning ticket was bought from a lotto outlet in Barangay Triangulo.

Sixty-five other players won P18,461.53 each for correctly guessing five of the six-digit winning combination.

The 6/45 Mega Lotto is drawn every Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

Meanwhile, the jackpot in the 6/58 Ultra Lotto is expected to increase tonight after no one correctly guessed the six-digit winning combination 21-12-30-04-36-41.

As of Friday night, the jackpot prize in the 6/58 Ultra Lotto was P401.33 million.

The 6/58 Ultra Lotto is drawn every Tuesday, Friday and Sunday.
