Colorado sees an unusually late start to mountain snow this year

TheTools · 17.09.2026 03:28 · source · permalink

LEADVILLE, Colo. (KRDO) -- The first measurable amount of snow was spotted on Mount Massive, according to our Denver news partner, 9News.

Mount Massive is 14,427 feet high, making it the second-highest point in Colorado, behind Mt. Elbert. 9News reported that the snow was spotted Tuesday morning and appeared to fall as low as 13,500 feet in elevation.

This first snow on Colorado's 14ers marks an unseasonably late start to winter. An average winter for Colorado will have the first 14er snow around late August.
