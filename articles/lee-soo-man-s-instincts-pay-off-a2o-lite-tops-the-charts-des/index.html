Lee Soo-man’s musical gamble paid off. A2O LITE, a girl group unveiled after the non-compete period ended, made its mark by topping major music charts with its debut song “NEON.”

A2O LITE released its debut song “NEON” at 6 p.m. on the 13th. The dance track blends a heavy bass line, powerful beats and repetitive lyrics, and is marked by an addictive melody and dynamic performances. About 29 hours after its release, the song reached No. 1 on Melon Top 100 on the 14th and also topped Bugs’ real-time chart. It ranked No. 3 on Genie as well.

The achievement is also significant as it marks Lee Soo-man’s return to activities in Korea. After leaving SM Entertainment in 2023, Lee returned to the K-pop market through A2O LITE after completing a non-compete period covering domestic album production. Yoo Young-jin, who worked with him during his time at SM, also contributed lyrics, composition and arrangement to “NEON,” helping to refine the track.

The achievement is drawing attention in particular because it came despite the wage arrears controversy surrounding the mother of member Aiching. Separate from the controversy, the song’s own addictive quality and broad appeal appear to have captured listeners’ interest.

Of course, early chart performance alone is not enough to predict long-term commercial success.

Still, “NEON”’s performance is a starting point that shows Lee Soo-man’s musical choice has resonated in the market. As it demonstrates that music capable of capturing the public’s ear can deliver results, attention is also turning to A2O LITE’s future moves.

[Ji Seunghun, Star Today reporter]
