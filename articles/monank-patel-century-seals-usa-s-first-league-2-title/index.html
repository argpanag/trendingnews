Monank Patel century seals USA's first League 2 title

TheTools · 08.10.2026 02:08 · source · permalink

USA beat Namibia by four wickets in Dallas to secure an unassailable lead at the top of the standings.

October 8, 2026 · 7:53am NPT

USA clinched their first ICC Men's Cricket World Cup League 2 title with a four-wicket victory over Namibia on Wednesday, led by captain Monank Patel's century at Grand Prairie Stadium in Dallas.

The win made USA unreachable at the top of the eight-team competition, even with other sides still to complete their fixtures. They had already secured a place at the ICC Men's Cricket World Cup Qualifier.

Chasing 299, USA reached 299 for 6 in 48.5 overs with seven balls remaining. Monank struck 108 from 100 deliveries at a strike rate of 108, while Shehan Jayasuriya contributed 74 from 75 balls. Smit Patel added 50.

Jayasuriya also played a significant role with the ball, taking 3 for 30 from five overs. Ehsan Adil led the attack with 4 for 41 from eight overs, conceding 5.13 runs an over.

Namibia posted 298 for 9 from their 50 overs, built around Michael van Lingen's 124 from 121 balls and Gerhard Erasmus's 76 from 45. Erasmus scored at a strike rate of 168.89 to give the innings momentum.

Jan Nicol Loftie-Eaton returned 2 for 39 and JJ Smit took 2 for 46 in USA's chase, but their efforts were not enough to prevent the hosts from securing the title.

The League 2 crown marks a milestone for USA, but their World Cup qualification campaign continues. Their confirmed place is at the Qualifier, where they will compete for a berth at the 2027 tournament in South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia.
