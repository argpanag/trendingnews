South Africa defeats Italy 66-7 in the 2026 Nations Championship. See how the Springboks scored nine tries to remain undefeated alongside New Zealand.

South Africa delivered a commanding 66-7 victory over Italy in Round 4 of the 2026 Nations Championship on Nov. 7 at Allianz Stadium in Turin, Italy. The Springboks improved to 4-0, remaining undefeated alongside New Zealand as the tournament's only unbeaten teams.

The Springboks came out firing, overwhelming Italy's defense with six first-half tries and building a staggering 45-0 halftime lead. Malcolm Marx scored twice, while Sacha Feinberg-Mngomezulu orchestrated the attack with a try, conversions and a penalty goal. South Africa's relentless scoring pace had a 100-point performance looking like a real possibility.

However, the momentum shifted in the second half. The Springboks eased off their blistering pace, showing less urgency and making mistakes that allowed Italy to find some attacking rhythm. Federico Ruzza finally broke through for the Azzurri's lone try, with Tommaso Allan adding the conversion.

Despite the slowdown, South Africa maintained its attacking intensity in key moments. Tries from Wilco Louw, Paul de Villiers and Ethan Hooker helped the Springboks finish with nine tries and a dominant 59-point victory, preserving their perfect Nations Championship record.
