Team news: Dowman starts as two make debuts

TheTools · 27.10.2026 23:45 · source · permalink

Mikel Arteta has made nine changes for our trip to Fleetwood Town in the Carabao Cup fourth round, with Illan Meslier and Ceadach O'Neill making their debuts.

Meslier, who joined us in the summer, starts in goal at Highbury Stadium, with Northern Ireland international O'Neill, 18, making his first senior appearance.

Jurrien Timber starts at right-back, with teenager Marli Salmon, Cristhian Mosquera and Piero Hincapie completing the backline.

Martin Zubimendi, who made a substitute appearance against Everton, starts in midfield alongside Myles Lewis-Skelly.

Ceadach is on the opposite wing to fellow teenager Max Dowman, who was player of the round last month after a sparkling display against Ipswich Town.

Kai Havertz and Viktor Gyokeres complete the line-up as we bid to reach the last eight for the third year in a row.

LINE-UPS

Arsenal: Meslier, Timber, Salmon, Mosquera, Hincapie, Zubimendi, Lewis-Skelly, Dowman, O’Neill, Havertz, Gyokeres

Substitutes: Arrizabalaga, White, Odegaard, Eze, Konsa, Guimaraes, Harriman-Annous, Washington, Hashi

Fleetwood Town: Lynch; Rooney, Bennett, Potter; Haughey, McLean, Neal, Helm, Batty; Loupalo-Bi, Evans.

Substitutes: Pradic, Andrew, Cannon, Davies, Thomas, Murphy, Coughlan, Roberton, Cirino.
