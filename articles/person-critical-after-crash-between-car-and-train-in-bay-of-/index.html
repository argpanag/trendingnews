Person critical after crash between car and train in Bay of Plenty

TheTools · 24.10.2026 23:04 · source · permalink

A person has been critically injured after a crash involving a car and a train in the Bay of Plenty this morning.

Police said it was called to Te Puke Highway in Paengaroa at around 9am following reports of a collision between a car and a train.

Hato Hone St John said a helicoper, an ambulance, a rapid response unit and a manager attended the scene.

"Our crews assessed and treated one patient who was airlifted to Waikato hospital in a critical condition," it said.

Fire and Emergency NZ crews from Te Puke responded to a motor vehicle crash in Paengaroa shortly after 9am.

"Te Puke Highway will be closed briefly between Pah Rd and Showgrounds Rd, while a helicopter lands to assist the injured person," a police spokesperson said.

"The road is due re-open once the helicopter leaves the scene; however, motorists should expect delays,"

The Serious Crash Unit had been advised, police said.
