Jennifer Lawrence and Josh Hutcherson Reunite at the ‘Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping’ Premiere 14 Years After Their Franchise Debut

TheTools · 17.11.2026 12:56 · source · permalink

‘The Hunger Games’ prequel film premieres in theaters on Nov. 20

Dimitrios Kambouris/Getty; Barry King/FilmMagic

NEED TO KNOW

  • Jennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson, Woody Harrelson and Elizabeth Banks reunited on the red carpet for the premiere of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping
  • The four original Hunger Games stars posed together at the New York City premiere on Monday, Nov. 16
  • The original stars reprise their roles — Katniss Everdeen, Peeta Mellark, Haymitch and Effie Trinket — in the prequel film, which arrives in theaters on Friday, Nov. 20

Jennifer Lawrence and Josh Hutcherson reunited on the red carpet.

Fourteen years after the original Hunger Games movie premiered, the franchise leads were joined by former costars Woody Harrelson and Elizabeth Banks at the premiere of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping in New York City on Monday, Nov. 16.

The four starred together in the original 2012 film, The Hunger Games, and its sequels: The Hunger Games: Catching Fire (2013); The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 1 (2014); and The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 2 (2015). Lawrence, 36, and Hutcherson, 34, return as Katniss Everdeen and Peeta Mellark, respectively, in Sunrise on the Reaping. Meanwhile, Harrelson, 65, returns as the pair’s mentor Haymitch, and Banks, 52, reprises her role as Effie Trinket.

During the Oct. 28 episode of Call Her Daddy, Lawrence said rejoining the franchise was “unbelievable.”

“It was so emotional. Josh [Hutcherson] and I didn’t walk to the bathroom without our arms around each other. It was so beautiful,” she said.

“It was so crazy because it was the same crew,” the Oscar winner continued. “It was the same camera guys, the same AD who I’ve worked with a million times too because I love him, and Josh and Woody.”

Lawrence said the trio’s dynamic hadn’t changed in the 11 years since their last film together. “Woody obviously came through the window of my hotel room uninvited because it would not be the same if he listened to boundaries and used a door,” she said.

Sunrise on the Reaping is set 24 years before the first Hunger Games, as a young Haymitch faces the first Quarter Quell, which enlists double the number of tributes to compete in the games to the death.

Joseph Zada and Elle Fanning star as the younger Haymitch and Effie. Other characters returning include President Snow (Ralph Fiennes), Plutarch Heavensbee (Jesse Plemons) and Caesar Flickerman (Kieran Culkin). (Originally they were played, respectively, by Donald Sutherland, Philip Seymour Hoffman and Stanley Tucci.) Glenn Close, Maya Hawke, Mckenna Grace, Kelvin Harrison Jr. and Ben Wang also star in the new film.

Dave J Hogan/Dave J Hogan/Getty

Grace — who stars as twins Merrilee and Maysilee Donner, the latter of whom is picked as a District 12 tribute — told PEOPLE how Hutcherson offered encouragement while she filmed.

“Eventually, after I booked the film and I was shooting, I reached out to him, and we spoke, and I was like, ‘Were your games, did it feel as physically taxing and emotionally?’ ” Grace, 20, told PEOPLE, explaining that her body couldn’t tell the difference between the “trauma” it faced in the arena as her character is hunted to death for sport and real life.

“I was like, ‘This is crazy.’ And he was like, ‘Yeah. Yeah, no, they’re all like that. It’s definitely hard,’ ” Grace said her Five Nights at Freddy’s 2 costar replied. “He was like, ‘That stuff was intense and brutal, and you’d get up so early and be in this freezing water.’ ”

She then confessed that she was eager to hear stories about Hutcherson and Lawrence on the original franchise.

“That was more my focus as the resident kind of fangirl on this project was just trying to get stories versus advice, which was probably not the best course of action,” Grace said.
