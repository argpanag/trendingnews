Everyone suspected the congressman, then he took down the bomber himself - 3DVF

TheTools · 09.09.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

For six straight episodes, fans and critics building their own case files on Reacher season 4 kept circling back to the same name: John Sampson. The frontrunner for his party’s presidential nomination, a former Delta Force operator with a past in Indonesia, checked every box for the season’s hidden villain. Then “Vote for Sampson” aired on Prime Video this September 9, and the congressman spent his own fundraiser tackling a suicide bomber into a river instead of orchestrating one. The man everyone suspected turned out to be just really good at his old job.

A death sentence, interrupted

Reacher season 4 has spent 6 episodes teaching viewers to distrust Congressman John Sampson. The season’s penultimate chapter, “Vote for Sampson,” landed today on Prime Video, and it upends that assumption in spectacular fashion, while also resolving the cliffhanger that left the hero facing a firing squad in the woods.

The episode opens with Jack Reacher (Alan Ritchson) and Philadelphia Detective Tamara Green (Sydelle Noel) moments from execution, after mercenaries working for Leo Ahlquist drove them off-site in a transport truck. Springfield (Aaron Poole), who had trailed the van, arrives just in time to break up the execution and free them both.

Sampson answers the call himself

The centerpiece unfolds at a fundraiser hosted by Sampson, played by Marc Blucas and positioned all season as the leading contender for his party’s nomination. When a suicide bomber slips into the event, the congressman falls back on his Delta Force training: he spots the threat, tackles the man, and knocks him into a nearby river just as the bomb detonates.

Sampson knew nothing about the arms and chemical-weapons trafficking driving the season’s conspiracy. His former commanding officer turned chief of staff, Nolan Cahill, was the one deeply involved.

Why the Hoth sisters wanted him dead

The episode also explains why Sampson sat at the center of the revenge plot. During a Delta Force deployment in Indonesia in 1998, he trained General Putra, who later used those skills to commit atrocities. Sampson himself killed the general, making him the logical target of Amisha Hoth (Anggun) and Lila Hoth (Agnez Mo), the Indonesian assassin posing as a journalist.

Recaps published before this episode openly asked whether Sampson was the season’s real villain, pointing to his military past and political ambitions. Instead, the character everyone suspected turns out to be the man who saves a room full of donors with his bare hands.

One episode to go

With Cahill exposed, the Hoth sisters still active and Reacher back on the hunt alongside Green, the pieces are set for the finale, which streams on September 16, 2026.
