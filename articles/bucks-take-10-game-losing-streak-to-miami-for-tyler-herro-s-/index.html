Bucks take 10-game losing streak to Miami for Tyler Herro’s return | Межа

TheTools · 18.11.2026 18:19 · source · permalink

Photo source: Fox News

Milwaukee visits Miami amid a 10-game skid, as Tyler Herro faces his former team and Giannis Antetokounmpo meets the Bucks for the first time since leaving.

The Bucks are 1-13, with their only win coming against an injury-hit Indiana Pacers team. They have lost 10 straight since then, though their last three defeats were by 11, eight and seven points. Milwaukee has alternated home and road games since Nov. 8 and will play at home against the Portland Trail Blazers the day after its visit to Miami.

This is reported by Fox News.

Milwaukee is still trying to settle on a rotation. The team has struggled when opponents limit Herro’s scoring, while Ryan Rollins, who had a breakout season last year, has also struggled in his role.

Miami opened the season 0-4 but has gone 8-2 over its past 10 games, including five straight wins. The Heat have beaten three strong teams by double digits in their last three games. Antetokounmpo has averaged a double-double during that stretch, Bam Adebayo appears more comfortable, and moving Andrew Wiggins to the bench has strengthened the second unit. The team still has questions about who can make jump shots when needed.

The game is likely to mean more to Herro than to Antetokounmpo, the writer argues. Antetokounmpo gave Milwaukee a lot, the piece says, and the writer wonders how the Bucks’ fans and franchise will receive him when he returns in January. The writer does not expect Milwaukee to win either this game or the next night against Portland, but thinks the Bucks can keep this one close and picks Milwaukee +12.5.
