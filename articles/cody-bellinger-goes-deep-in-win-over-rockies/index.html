Cody Bellinger goes deep in win over Rockies

TheTools · 10.09.2026 21:56 · source · permalink

by Chris Schommer | Yankees Correspondent | Thu, Sep 10th 2026, 5:56pm EDT

Cody Bellinger went 1-for-4 in a 6-1 win over the Rockies with a solo home run.

Fantasy Impact:

Bellinger has caught fire again at the plate, collecting hits in 10 of his last 11 contests. The outfielder provides manager Aaron Boone with one of the more impactful bats in a streaky Yankees lineup, batting .264 with 13 home runs, 63 RBI, 65 runs scored, and 10 stolen bases through 118 games.
