Meliá Pattaya Hotel Brings Guests Closer to Thailand’s Fireworks Spectacle - Safari India

TheTools · 22.11.2026 10:51 · source · permalink

Meliá Pattaya Hotel is preparing a series of dining experiences for the Pattaya International Fireworks Festival on 27 and 28 November 2026. Under the theme A Season to Sparkle, Meliá Pattaya Hotel will offer Chinese cuisine, seafood buffets, free-flow drinks and beachfront dining, with views of the fireworks over Pattaya Bay.

Guests can choose from experiences at Yitong Chinese Restaurant, Yitong Sky Bar and LAY Beach Club, with different menus and seating options available across the venues.

Located on Meliá Pattaya Hotel’s 12th floor, Yitong Chinese Restaurant will serve a Cantonese and Sichuan sharing menu prepared by Chef Subin Chaimuangchuan. The menu will include Alaskan king crab and steamed black grouper.

The restaurant features floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking Pattaya Beach, alongside a ceiling installation inspired by calligraphy.

Dining packages start at THB 4,200 net per person with free-flow soft drinks, THB 6,000 with classic free-flow beverages and THB 7,000 with premium free-flow drinks. Bar seating with premium free-flow beverages is available at THB 2,800 net per person. A private dining room for 10 guests is priced at THB 70,000 net.

Yitong Sky Bar will host an open-air dining experience with views across Pattaya. The offering will include Chinese-inspired canapés, dim sum, Peking duck and free-flow drinks.

Packages are priced at THB 3,000 net per person with free-flow soft drinks, THB 4,800 with classic free-flow beverages and THB 5,800 with premium free-flow drinks.

LAY Beach Club will offer two dining options for the festival.

The ground-floor Beachfront Deck will serve a specially selected à la carte menu. Indoor seating is THB 3,000 net per person, while beach-view seating is THB 5,000 net per person. Both amounts are fully redeemable against food and beverages.

The Sky Deck will serve an international seafood buffet featuring grilled rock lobster and jumbo tiger prawns. Packages start at THB 4,200 net per person with free-flow soft drinks, THB 7,000 with classic free-flow beverages and THB 9,000 with premium free-flow drinks.

All three dining experiences will run from 7 pm to 10 pm on 27 and 28 November 2026. A 10% early-bird discount will be available for reservations, subject to limited availability. Prices include service charge and government tax.
