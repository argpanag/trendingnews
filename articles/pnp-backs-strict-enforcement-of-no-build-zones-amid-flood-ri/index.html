PNP backs strict enforcement of no-build zones amid flood risks

TheTools · 04.09.2026 06:18 · source · permalink

MANILA, Philippines — The Philippine National Police (PNP) on Thursday said it would assist local government units (LGUs) and concerned government agencies in enforcing no-build zones and other safety measures in flood-prone and hazardous areas.

This followed President Ferdinand Marcos Jr.’s call for stricter enforcement of “no-build zone” policies and other measures to reduce flooding and protect communities from the effects of climate change.

READ: Marcos pushes stricter enforcement of ‘no-build zones’ amid floods

According to PNP chief Gen. Jose Melencio Nartatez Jr., police units would provide assistance when needed to maintain peace and order and ensure public safety during enforcement operations in the “no-build zones.”

“The PNP will support local government units and concerned agencies in the lawful enforcement of no-build zones, particularly when police assistance is needed to maintain peace and order and ensure public safety,” Nartatez said in a statement.

He also said police assistance in these operations would remain focused on enforcing the law and protecting the public.

“Our police units will remain professional and impartial. We will perform our duties based on the law and will not allow political considerations to compromise public safety,” he said.

Nartatez also said police commanders had been directed to coordinate with local authorities, monitor areas identified as danger zones, and provide immediate assistance when necessary.

He urged residents and local officials to comply with existing laws and safety regulations, particularly in communities vulnerable to flooding and other hazards. — Rendell Ivan Manuel, INQUIRER.net trainee /das
