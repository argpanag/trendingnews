PAL to offer 60% discounts on business, premium seats

TheTools · 18.10.2026 18:01 · source · permalink

Flag carrier Philippine Airlines (PAL) on Friday announced discounts of up to 60% on business and premium seats on select domestic and international routes as part of its inaugural Premium Cabin Seat Sale.

In an advisory, PAL said the discounts will be available from October 19 to 25, 2026, with the sale fares and participating routes to be announced when the sale opens.

Domestic travelers will also be given the option to pay in installments through select credit card partners, with a minimum transaction of P40,000.

PAL reported a $25.1-million net loss in the first half of the year as fuel expenses rose by $219.5 million amid the Middle East conflict.

Shares of PAL Holdings Inc., the parent company of PAL, last traded at P3.72 each, down 1 centavo from Tuesday’s close. — RSJ, GMA News
