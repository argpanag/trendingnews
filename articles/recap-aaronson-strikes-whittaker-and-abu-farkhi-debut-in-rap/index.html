Recap | Aaronson strikes, Whittaker and Abu Farkhi debut in Rapids' 1-0 victory over CF Montréal | Colorado Rapids

TheTools · 13.09.2026 05:46 · source · permalink

The Big Picture

  • Midfielder Paxten Aaronson recorded his sixth goal of the season and the 11th of his MLS career.
  • The goal extends his club lead in goal contributions with 12 (6g, 6a). 
  • Forward Morgan Whittaker made his club debut in the match after being subbed on in the 54th minute. 
  • Forward Sayed Abu Farkhi made his club debut in the match after being subbed on in the 54th minute. 
  • With the shutout, the Rapids extend their home unbeaten streak to six, setting a new club record previously recorded in 2004.
  • In the club’s 14 matches at DICK’S Sporting Goods Park this season across all competitions, they have posted nine clean sheets.
