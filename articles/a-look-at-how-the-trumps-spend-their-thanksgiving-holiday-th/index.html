A look at how the Trumps spend their Thanksgiving holiday through the years

TheTools · 27.11.2026 07:27 · source · permalink

Trump's past holiday celebrations included Mar-a-Lago visits and calls to troops who can't spend Thanksgiving with family

Melania Trump thanks military families ahead of Thanksgiving

Melania Trump delivers a powerful message of gratitude to military families during a visit to Fort Rucker ahead of Thanksgiving. She honors the profound sacrifices made by military spouses and children while their loved ones are deployed across the globe. She expresses deep appreciation for their strength and resilience in keeping their homes full of love.

In keeping with tradition, President Donald Trump marked his sixth Thanksgiving in the executive office with the White House’s annual Turkey Pardon ceremony on Tuesday.

His plans for Thanksgiving Day this year remain unannounced — but previous holidays offer a preview of how the president likes to spend the day honoring those who can't be at home for the uniquely American holiday due to service to the country.

Last year, Trump spent Thanksgiving at Mar-a-Lago participating in his favorite pastime — golf. But he also used the time in what is known as the "Winter White House" to call Maj. Gen. Dave Gardner, commander of the 101st Airborne Division and Joint Task Force–Southern Border, by video conference and talk with troops deployed along the southern border.

First lady Melania Trump spent the days ahead of the holiday in 2025 visiting Camp Lejeune and Marine Corps Air Station New River with second lady Usha Vance on Nov. 19. They met with military families and made Thanksgiving crafts with children.

In 2020, during the final Thanksgiving of his first term, Trump remained at the White House and made calls to six deployed military units representing every branch of the armed forces, including troops in Kuwait and sailors aboard the USS Winston Churchill in the Red Sea.

Trump famously took a secret trip to Bagram Airfield in Afghanistan on Nov. 28, 2019, where he served turkey, posed for photos and thanked American troops for their sacrifices amid the ongoing war in the Middle East, which not even two years later, in August 2021, was brought to an end by then-President Joe Biden.

"There's nowhere I'd rather celebrate this Thanksgiving than right here with the toughest, strongest, best, and bravest warriors on the face of the Earth," Trump told the troops at the time.

"And you don't even know how much the people of our country love and respect you."

President Donald Trump and first lady Melania Trump greet military personnel during an unannounced visit to Al Asad Air Base, Iraq, Dec. 26, 2018. (Reuters/Jonathan Ernst)

The visit came as roughly 12,000 American troops remained in Afghanistan more than 18 years after the U.S. invasion.

During the visit, Trump met with Afghan President Ashraf Ghani to discuss efforts to end the war and told American troops that the Taliban wanted to reach a deal with the United States. He said his administration was reducing America's military presence in Afghanistan while pursuing a political solution to the more than 18-year conflict.

President Donald Trump visits with personnel at U.S. Coast Guard Station Lake Worth Inlet in Riviera Beach, Florida, on Thanksgiving Day, Nov. 22, 2018. (Mandel Ngan/AFP via Getty Images)

Trump spent Thanksgiving 2018 in Florida having a traditional dinner with his family, including Melania and his children and grandchildren.

He also held a video call with deployed troops before visiting Coast Guard members at Station Lake Worth Inlet with his wife, where the first couple thanked them for their service and hurricane rescues.

After that holiday in 2018, Melania and then-second lady Karen Pence assembled comfort kits for deployed troops at American Red Cross headquarters.
