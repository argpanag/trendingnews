Grace Lee Tiu is pregnant at 43: 'Our miracle baby'

TheTools · 25.10.2026 00:00 · source · permalink

A bundle of joy is on the way for Grace Lee Tiu and her husband!

On her Instagram account, the former TV host announced that she is getting ready for a new chapter in her life as she and her husband Alex Tiu are preparing to welcome a baby boy.

"Awaiting the arrival of our baby boy. Blessed to be pregnant at 43! Thank You, Lord, for our miracle baby," she wrote in the caption.

She accompanied her post with pictures of her baby shower, where the couple shared a tender moment against a dreamy pastel backdrop.

In the comments section, her fellow celebrities took the time to congratulate the couple on their new addition to their family. Among those who sent their well-wishes were Carla Abellana, Edu Manzano, Amy Perez, Mariel Padilla, and Gelli de Belen.

Grace tied the knot with her non-showbiz partner in a romantic ceremony in Seoul, South Korea, in October 2024.

She began her television career in 2007 as a segment host on Q11's The Sweet Life. She later moved to GMA Network, hosting various shows like Eateria, Diz Iz It!, and segments on Balitanghali and 24 Oras Weekend Edition.

She then transferred to TV5 in 2012 and co-hosted shows like Andar ng mga Balita, Good Morning Club, Wowowillie, and Aksyon sa Umaga. She eventually founded Glimmer Productions in 2019, focusing on distributing Korean films and producing original content.
