Vasiliy Lomachenko-Charly Suarez nearly set for Feb. 13

TheTools · 06.11.2026 10:35 · source · permalink

Three-division champion Vasiliy Lomachenko and recent junior lightweight title challenger Charly Suarez are expected to soon finalize agreement on a Feb. 13 bout, officials connected to talks told BoxingScene on Tuesday.

A formal announcement could arrive as early as this week, with the winner pegged for a title shot at new three-belt champion O’Shaquie Foster, Top Rank’s Bob Arum said.

The sides are waiting to firm up the venue, with each aiming to bring the fight to Las Vegas.

Arum said he wants to place the postponed 140lbs vacant IBF title fight between Mexico’s Lindolfo Delgado and Russia’s Arthur Biyarslanov on the card following Delgado’s decision not to fight on the conflict-endangered Saudi Arabia card this past weekend.

Lomachenko, 18-3 (12 KOs), hasn’t fought since winning the IBF lightweight belt versus George Kambosos Jnr in May 2024, while the Phillipines’ Suarez, 19-0 (11 KOs), posted a first-round knockout over Manuel Avila in July following his disputed no-contest with recent unified 130lbs champion Emanuel Navarrete in May 2025.

In that bout, the referee mistakenly ruled that an accidental headbutt cut Navarrete, leading the three-belt champion from Mexico to a narrow technical decision victory when Suarez should have gained the triumph because his punch opened the cut.

Foster defeated Navarrete by unanimous decision with two knockdowns Oct. 24 in his home state of Texas, adding the WBO and WBA belts to his existing WBC strap.

Top-ranked WBO fighter Suarez is owed an immediate title shot at Foster.

Both Suarez and Lomachenko are 38. Lomachenko moved toward a multifight agreement with Arum’s Top Rank after sitting next to the promoter at the Foster-Navarrete bout.

Suarez’s new promoter, Sampson Lewkowicz, said his fighter “has a shot” to topple two-time Olympic champion Lomachenko.

“Loma’s almost two years without a fight, he’s close to 40 and he’s been distracted by war in his country [Ukraine],” Lewkowicz said.

Additionally, Lewkowicz noted Suarez nearly defeated Navarrete after arriving in the U.S. on fight week, and will prepare for Lomachenko as he did ahead of his win over Avila in a July ProBox card – by training in the months before the fight in the U.S.

“He showed real power in that ProBox fight,” Lewkowicz said. “I see it as a very competitive fight.”
