Bengaluru Weather Today (September 20):  Bengaluru is expected to have moderate drizzle and cloudy weather on Sunday. The city will have a cool and damp Sunday. Here’s what you all need to know.

Humidity is expected to remain at an exceptionally high level at around 98% during the day.
