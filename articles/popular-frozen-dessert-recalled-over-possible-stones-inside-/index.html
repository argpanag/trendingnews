Popular frozen dessert recalled over possible stones inside. Here’s what to know

TheTools · 17.09.2026 10:12 · source · permalink

(Gray News) - Pints of So Delicious Dairy Free frozen dessert are being recalled because they may have small stones or other hard objects inside.

The recall includes pints of So Delicious Dairy Free Salted Caramel Cluster Non-Dairy Frozen Dessert that were sold in stores nationwide, including Walmart and Target. Only pints with UPC code 744473476138 and best-by dates on or before April 3, 2028, are affected.

After customer complaints, the Food and Drug Administration says it was determined these pints may have small stones or other hard objects inside the cashew clusters. So Delicious says it’s working to get the affected pints off store shelves.

Anyone who bought the pints shouldn’t eat them and should instead throw them away.

So Delicious is offering customers a replacement coupon or refund through its Consumer Care Line at 1-833-367-8975 or its website at https://www.sodeliciousdairyfree.com/contact-us/. Specialists are available weekdays from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. Eastern.
