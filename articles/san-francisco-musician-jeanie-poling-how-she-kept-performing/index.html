San Francisco Musician Jeanie Poling: How She Kept Performing Through Three-Year Battle With Two Rare Cancers

TheTools · 16.09.2026 21:55 · source · permalink

San Francisco’s vibrant bluegrass community is mourning the loss of one of its most cherished voices following the death of Jeanie Poling at the age of sixty-seven. The revered guitarist and vocalist passed away on August 26 from severe pneumonia, marking the end of a formidable three-and-a-half-year battle against two rare forms of cancer.

In a remarkable display of resilience, the Long Island transplant refused to let her diagnosis silence her music, continuing to draw crowds at farmers’ markets, street fairs, and festivals alongside her husband and musical partner, Chuck Poling, throughout her strenuous medical treatments, according to reports.

San Francisco Musician Jeanie Poling: How Did Jeanie Poling Balance Treatment And The Stage?

Despite facing the debilitating physical toll of fighting dual rare cancers, the dedicated musician maintained a rigorous performance schedule that defied medical expectations. Armed with her acoustic guitar and dressed in authentic country-western attire, she continued to take the stage with her husband, who accompanied her on the mandolin.

Music critics and local event organisers noted that her vocal performances never faltered, even as she underwent aggressive medical interventions behind the scenes. Her ability to seamlessly transition from hospital wards to the stage became a source of profound inspiration for her fellow musicians and dedicated local audiences.

“Jeanie was a remarkable singer, with a powerful voice,” Chuck Poling told The Chronicle, highlighting her commanding stage presence. “She could really get a room to shut up and listen.”

San Francisco Musician Jeanie Poling: What Was The Extent Of Her Underlying Health Battle?

The bluegrass star’s health struggles began more than three years ago when oncologists diagnosed her with two distinct and rare forms of cancer, initiating a complex and exhausting treatment regimen. While the specific nature of the cancers remained private, her family confirmed that the dual diagnosis required continuous and intensive medical care.

Her husband revealed that the extended fight gradually compromised her immune system, ultimately leaving her vulnerable to the severe bout of pneumonia that claimed her life late last month. “She passed away on August 26 from pneumonia after a three-and-a-half-year battle with cancer. She fought the good fight but knew when to throw in the towel,” Chuck Poling stated in an emotional social media tribute confirming her death.

San Francisco Musician Jeanie Poling: How Is The San Francisco Music Community Reacting?

The announcement of her passing has triggered an overwhelming outpouring of grief and tribute from across the Bay Area’s traditional music scene. Fans, fellow artists, and venue operators have flooded digital platforms to share memories of her commanding vocal talent and her unwavering commitment to preserving traditional country and bluegrass music.

Digital arts publications have highlighted her role in nurturing the local music ecosystem, where she was known as much for her welcoming demeanour as for her formidable musical prowess. Commentators on her husband’s public announcement praised the couple’s artistic partnership, characterising their dynamic as a rare and inspiring marriage that deeply enriched the cultural fabric of San Francisco.

San Francisco Musician Jeanie Poling: Who Did The Beloved Musician Leave Behind?

Beyond her lasting musical legacy, the late artist leaves behind a deeply devoted family who supported her throughout her prolonged illness. She is survived by her husband of forty-four years, Chuck, along with their two adult children, Isabel and Reuben, and her daughter-in-law, Kat.

In his public remarks, her husband expressed profound gratitude for the local community’s immediate mobilisation to support the grieving family during their mourning period. He credited his children for stepping up to sustain the household in the wake of the tragedy, describing his late wife as an amazing singer, an accomplished professional, and the cornerstone of their tight-knit family unit.
