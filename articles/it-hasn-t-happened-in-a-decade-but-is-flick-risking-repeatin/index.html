It hasn't happened in a decade, but is Flick risking repeating Enrique's 'nightmare'?

TheTools · 31.10.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

Barcelona fear they could slip up again when they host Deportivo Alaves in La Liga this Saturday night.

A defeat would return the team to a nightmare scenario, which Catalan fans would prefer to forget.

Hansi Flick's team aims to stabilize the situation in the match against Alaves, a match that takes on even more importance as it takes place at the "Camp Nou", as part of the 11th round of La Liga.

After starting the season in a fantastic way, with 11 wins in all competitions, Barcelona has now suffered two consecutive defeats.

Paris Saint-Germain inflicted their first defeat, defeating Barcelona 4-2 in the third round of the Champions League group stage.

Then, Real Madrid triumphed 2-1 in "El Clasico", within the 10th round of the Spanish championship.

A defeat to Alaves would mark the first time Barcelona have suffered three consecutive defeats in official matches in a season since the 2015/16 season, when the team was managed by Luis Enrique.

At that time, Barcelona went through a very difficult period between April 9 and 17, 2016. This happened ten and a half years ago.

The decline began with a 1-0 away loss to Real Sociedad, in the 32nd round of La Liga.

Then Atletico Madrid struck, winning 2-0 at home in the second leg of the Champions League quarter-final and eliminating Barcelona from the competition, having lost 2-1 in the first leg in Catalonia.

The complete collapse was sealed by Valencia, which defeated Barcelona 2-1 at the "Camp Nou", within the 33rd round of the Spanish championship. /GazetaExpress/
