Raiders vs. Jets odds: Opening lines for Week 8 matchup

TheTools · 26.10.2026 02:31 · source · permalink
  • Las Vegas Raiders Odds

The Las Vegas Raiders are underdogs once again for their Week 8 matchup against the New York Jets

The Las Vegas Raiders have lost four straight after a 3-0 start. This time, they fell at home to the LA Rams, 35-18. It’s been a struggle, and the coaching staff is looking for answers.

Next up for the Silver and Black is a matchup against the New York Jets. The Jets are 2-5 and lost to the Miami Dolphins, who hadn’t won a game heading into Week 7. It could be an opportunity for the Raiders to bounce back on the road.

Fanduel Sportsbook posted odds for all the games of the eighth week of the season. The Raiders are 1.5-point underdogs against the New York Jets, with an over/under (O/U) of 44.5.

With four teams on a bye in Week 7, we won’t see much NFL action. FanDuel lists the Cincinnati Bengals as the biggest favorites in the upcoming games, at -10.5 over the Tennessee Titans.

Raiders vs. Jets

Moneyline: Jets -118, Raiders +100

Opening point spread: 1.5

Opening point total: 44.5
