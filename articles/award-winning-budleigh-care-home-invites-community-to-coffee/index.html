Award-winning Budleigh care home invites community to coffee afternoon

TheTools · 31.10.2026 06:47 · source · permalink

A care home has invited the community to join its next monthly coffee afternoon.

Bay Court Residential and Nursing Home in Budleigh Salterton will host the event at 2.30pm on Monday, November 2, with staff promising homemade cake, conversation, and a warm welcome.

Dominique, Tracey, and Vicky, members of the Bay Court team, said that they would "absolutely love" to see everyone there.

In a message to the community, they shared recent achievements and expressed gratitude for ongoing support.

The home's registered manager, Tracey, has won Registered Manager of the Year, while Bay Court received a Highly Commended award for Care Team of the Year.

The team said: "We are very grateful for these awards and in particular grateful for the fantastic community around Bay Court.

"Your support, friendship, and enthusiasm for our events including the monthly coffee afternoons have helped make them what they are today.

"They are so much more than just a cup of coffee—they are about bringing people together, sharing stories, and creating lovely connections."

They also gave special thanks to Cathy, who has been working quietly behind the scenes, giving us so much support.

The care home team said: "To everyone who has supported us, attended our coffee afternoons, donated, helped organise events, or simply popped in for a chat—thank you.

"We really couldn't do it without you."
