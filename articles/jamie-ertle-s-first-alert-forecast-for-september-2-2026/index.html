Jamie Ertle’s First Alert Forecast for September 2, 2026

TheTools · 03.09.2026 00:43 · source · permalink

SAVANNAH, Ga. (WTOC) - Hot and humid conditions continue with some feels like temperatures Wednesday afternoon well into the 100s. High pressure remains in control. However, rain chances increase dramatically by Sunday as we track a cold front that’s near the Great Lakes.

Thursday morning could be foggy for some with temperatures near 71° inland and closer to 77° at the beaches. It’ll be sunnier in the afternoon than the morning with highs near 94° and a 20% chance of some afternoon showers.

Friday: 76/91 mostly sunny with a 30% chance of scattered afternoon storms following the sea breeze

Saturday: 75/95, may feel like 105°, mostly sunny, 20% chance of some afternoon showers

A cold front will approach the area from the north Saturday night and move south into northern Florida on Sunday. An area of low pressure could form along that front at the coast.

Sunday: 75/93 morning sunshine with rain chances increasing by the early afternoon with a 70% chance of scattered showers and storms.

Monday, Labor Day: 74/88 mix of sun and clouds, 50% chance of showers and storms

Tuesday: 73/88, 30% chance of afternoon rainstorms

Wednesday: 72/90, mostly sunny

RIP CURRENTS: There is a low risk of rip currents forecast for our South Carolina beaches, but a moderate risk of rip currents forecast for our Georgia beaches.

TRACKING THE TROPICS: Edouard is still a tropical depression and not moving that fast, dropping more than 20 inches of rain for portions of eastern Texas.

Stay Safe!

~JErtle
