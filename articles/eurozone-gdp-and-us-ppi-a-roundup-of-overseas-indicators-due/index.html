Eurozone GDP and US PPI: A Roundup of Overseas Indicators Due November 13 — BigGo Finance

TheTools · 12.11.2026 22:25 · source · permalink

In overseas markets on November 13, inflation and growth indicators for major economies will be released in succession, including Eurozone gross domestic product for July-September and the US producer price index for October.

First, at 19:00, Eurozone GDP (July-September) will be published. The market consensus is 1.3% quarter-on-quarter, with the previous reading also at 1.3%, pointing to a flat result. At 22:30, US PPI (October) is due, with the market pricing in 0.4%, a slowdown from the previous 1.3%. At 25:00, Russia GDP (July-September) is expected at 1.3%.

Elsewhere, Brazil retail sales (September) will be released at 21:00 at 0.4%, and India foreign exchange reserves (last week) at 20:30. China's preliminary current account (July-September) is expected at $193.7 billion, roughly in line with the previous reading. India's trade balance (October) is scheduled for release by the 16th, with the market forecast at -$33.688 billion versus the previous -$30.21 billion.

Note: Figures are market consensus and previous readings. China's preliminary current account is $193.7 billion; India's trade balance is -$33.688 billion (forecast) / -$30.21 billion (previous).

Remarks by officials will also draw attention. The president of the San Francisco Fed will take part in a discussion panel and Q&A session, and Fed Vice Chair Jefferson is scheduled to give a speech. With inflation data and comments from key officials falling on the same day, currency and bond market moves are likely to be closely watched.
