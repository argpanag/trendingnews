White House uses slur against journalist after Trump berates him in Oval Office

TheTools · 22.10.2026 16:13 · source · permalink

President Donald Trump directed his ire at yet another journalist this week, lashing out at The Independent’s Andrew Feinberg in the Oval Office and subsequently removing him from the White House press pool.

A verbal clash first broke out Tuesday at the Oval Office, when Feinberg asked Trump questions during a press gaggle.

“Wait, wait. Quiet,” Trump barked. “What’s wrong with you? Are you a sick person? I actually think there’s something wrong with you.”

At one point, Trump also warned Feinberg, “I don’t know who the hell you are, but you better be careful.”

The exchange was caught on video by other news outlets, and the White House later posted the footage on X, using a slur to refer to the reporter. Vice President JD Vance also shared the exchange on X and called Feinberg an “imbecile.”

The White House penalized Feinberg on Wednesday, barring him from the press pool scheduled to travel to Iowa for Trump’s Thursday campaign rally. White House Correspondents’ Association President Jacqui Heinrich called the removal of Feinberg from his pool duties “retaliation.”

“The American public’s right to an independent press depends on reporters being able to do their jobs without fear that challenging the president will cut off their access,” Heinrich, a Fox News correspondent, said in a statement.
