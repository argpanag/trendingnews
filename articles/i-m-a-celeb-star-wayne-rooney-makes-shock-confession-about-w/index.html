Wayne Rooney has made a surprising confession about his wedding day to wife, Coleen, revealing he nearly ended up being arrested just hours before they tied the knot.

The former England footballer shared the hilarious story while chatting to his I’m A Celebrity… Get Me Out Of Here! campmates, recalling a wild night out with his friends ahead of their lavish Italian wedding in 2008.

Wayne, 41, explained that Coleen had spent the previous day on a boat with her bridesmaids, while he was left celebrating with his pals.

Before heading off, however, she gave her future husband one important warning.

“Don’t get too p****d. You’re getting married in the morning,” she told him.

Wayne admitted that he promised to behave, but things quickly got out of hand once his friends joined him in his hotel room.

“Seven o’clock in the morning, had the police at the door,” he recalled.

“A few of the lads ended up in my room and we carried big, massive speakers up. And then a few bottles got thrown over the balcony and the police turned up.

“So, I nearly got arrested!”

Claire Sweeney also joined in, remarking: “That’s rock and roll.”

Wayne and Coleen married in the picturesque Italian town of Portofino in June 2008, with their four-day celebrations reportedly costing around £5million.

The lavish occasion included a luxury yacht trip, a masked ball and a ceremony at Villa Durazzo.

The couple also had an impressive line-up of musical entertainment, with Westlife performing during their first dance and Stereophonics frontman Kelly Jones entertaining guests the following day.

The wedding celebrations reportedly included five private jets transporting 64 guests from the UK, while Coleen wore a custom-made Marchesa gown.

Wayne’s latest revelation comes during his appearance on I’m A Celebrity, where he has already been open about his past struggles with alcohol and the role Coleen played in supporting him.

Earlier in the series, he admitted that he had turned to drinking during his early twenties as he struggled with the pressures of fame and his football career.

He also credited Coleen with helping him through his darkest moments, describing her as his “absolute rock.”
