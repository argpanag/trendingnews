Ancelotti Urges Ethan Mbappe to Improve Physical Strength

TheTools · 25.11.2026 20:57 · source · permalink

Lille OSC forward Ethan Mbappe is set to return to action against Bayern Munich after missing the club's previous three UEFA Champions League matches through suspension. Ahead of the fixture, assistant coach Davide Ancelotti identified physical development as a major priority for the 19-year-old Frenchman.

Mbappe has experienced recurring injury problems since joining Lille in 2024, following his departure from Paris Saint-Germain and the signing of his first professional contract. Several of those setbacks have involved muscular problems, while an ankle injury has already ruled him out of four matches this season.

Despite those interruptions, the younger brother of Kylian Mbappe has contributed two goals and two assists in 11 appearances across all competitions. He has established himself as an option on the right side of Lille's attack.

Ancelotti believes the young forward still needs considerable physical development to become more consistent and withstand the demands of professional football.

"He is a player who has played just one full 90 minutes in his career and that was this year against Stade Brestois. He has to progress physically. We benefitted from his absence to make him progress physically in training because he absolutely needs it in his progression, in order to be a player that is physically strong and resilient.

We are there to accompany him, to help him, because we know that he will be very important for the future of the club." Ancelotti said.

The coach highlighted Mbappe's limited experience of completing full matches as evidence of the physical improvements still required. Lille has used training sessions to work on his conditioning during his absence.

Mbappe's return against Bayern Munich provides Lille with another attacking option after his suspension kept him out of three consecutive Champions League fixtures. His development remains an important consideration for the club's long-term plans.
