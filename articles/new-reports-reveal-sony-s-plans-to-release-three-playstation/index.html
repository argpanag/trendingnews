New reports reveal Sony's plans to release three PlayStation 6 configurations.

TheTools · 16.10.2026 20:42 · source · permalink

According to recent reports from semiconductor industry insiders and analysts, Sony is planning a major expansion of its hardware ecosystem in the next generation and is preparing to release several types of devices under the PlayStation 6 brand. The platform's flagship product will be a classic, powerful desktop console, which will take the flagship position in the home gaming market. This system is being designed to deliver uncompromising graphics performance, support for next-level hardware ray tracing, and advanced scaling powered by machine learning algorithms. The flagship console will be a direct technological successor to the current platform and will retain the familiar format for interacting with high-budget, big-screen projects.

The second key element of the lineup is a fully-fledged portable gaming system capable of running games natively, without the need for a cloud connection. According to preliminary technical data, this portable device's performance in standalone mode will exceed the capabilities of the Xbox Series S console, and when docked in a proprietary dock, the chip's clock speed and graphics performance will significantly increase due to its increased thermal design and direct power supply. The project is being developed in close collaboration with AMD and focuses on a power-efficient architecture, allowing the Japanese platform holder to directly compete in the growing market for portable gaming PCs and next-generation portable consoles.

The third potential member of the lineup is an entry-level desktop version, codenamed PS6 Lite. According to sources, this model is intended to offer consumers the lowest possible entry point into the ecosystem by reusing the handheld console's silicon or the PSP3 architecture currently in development in the form of a compact home media box without its own screen or battery. This diversified approach will allow Sony to reach all audience segments: from enthusiasts demanding the maximum power of a desktop platform to mobile gamers and users seeking an affordable system for playing modern releases.
