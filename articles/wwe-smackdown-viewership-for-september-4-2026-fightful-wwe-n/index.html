WWE SmackDown viewership information for the September 4, 2026 episode.

Jed I Goodman reports that WWE SmackDown on September 4 drew 1.3 million viewers on USA Network. This number is up from the 1.12 million viewers the show drew on August 28.

Friday’s episode scored a 0.29 rating in the 18 to 49 demographic, down from the 0.23 rating last Friday.

The episode was taped.
