New and streaming TV: A ‘Ghosts’ Halloween special, a Matthew Perry docuseries, and more - The Boston Globe

TheTools · 26.10.2026 21:30 · source · permalink

Your TV GPS, a look at the week ahead in television, appears every Monday morning on BostonGlobe.com. Today’s column covers Oct. 26-Nov. 1.

The docuseries “The One about Matthew Perry,” out Tuesday on Netflix, is a three-episode look at the life of the troubled “Friends” star, with people who knew him well, including his sister Mia Perry Bowick, talking about his acting career and struggles with addiction.

Fans of the sweet CBS sitcom “Ghosts” can catch a one-hour Halloween special of the show at 9 p.m. Thursday on CBS, streaming on Paramount+. The series, about a woman whose near-death experience gives her the ability to see and speak to the ghosts living in her historic home, returns for a new season in a few months, but fans will also have a Christmas special to look forward to in December.

“Dexter: Resurrection” returns Friday on Paramount+ for a new season. The rebooted version of the show first came back in 2025, and season 2 (if we’re starting over again) sees Michael C. Hall’s serial killer with a cause facing a midlife crisis. Brian Cox, Dan Stevens, and Uma Thurman costar.

Liev Schreiber stars in “Nocturne,” out Friday on Apple TV. The series is based on the books “The Sandman” and “Lazarus” by the novelist Lars Kepler. The novels focused on Joona Linna, here renamed Jonah Lynn (Schreiber), a homicide detective in small town Pennsylvania who gets caught up in a case about a serial killer played by Stephen Graham of “Adolescence.” Lynn’s surrogate daughter, an FBI agent with the incredible name of Saga Bauer (played by Zazie Beetz of “Atlanta”), also gets involved in the case.

The beloved Canadian sketch comedy series “SCTV” helped launch the careers of an incredible array of comedians: John Candy, Martin Short, Andrea Martin, Catherine O’Hara, Eugene Levy, Dave Thomas, Joe Flaherty, Rick Moranis. In the documentary “SCTV: The Greatest Show You’ve Never Heard Of,” out Friday on Netflix, director Jason Hehir explores the show and its legacy, with appearances from cast members as well as performers who were influenced by them, including Conan O’Brien and Kathryn Hahn.
