'Pearl' Star Mia Goth Files For Divorce From Shia LaBeouf

Mia Goth has filed for divorce from Shia LaBeouf after their high-profile separation last year.

According to court docs obtained by TMZ, the actress filed Tuesday night, citing the boilerplate "irreconcilable differences" as the reason for the split. She lists their date of separation as July 28, 2025.

Mia is asking for primary legal and physical custody of their 4-year-old daughter, Isabel. She's also requesting to terminate the court's ability to award either her or Shia spousal support.

This divorce petition won't come as a shock to many people who follow the couple's relationship ... we reported way back in February that the two have been estranged for a long time.

Shia bought a $1 million New Orleans home after the split ... moving down to the Big Easy, where he's gotten into some trouble this year.

Remember, back in February, Shia was arrested after getting into a fight during Mardi Gras. He later pled guilty to misdemeanor battery charges.

We later learned cops were called to Shia and Mia's Los Angeles home multiple times before he moved to New Orleans ... and sources told us Mia wanted him to go to rehab.

Shia's clearly moved on from Mia ... regularly hanging with his new girlfriend, Nicole Mumphrey -- with whom he had a heated confrontation in Rome.
