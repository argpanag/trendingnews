The 'Wings' cast reunites for John Ritter Foundation event honoring the late actor

TheTools · 19.09.2026 17:04 · source · permalink

by Elizabeth Stanton

TOPICS:

The cast of “Wings” gathered for a special cause on Saturday night in Hollywood.

Tim Daly, Steven Weber, Tony Shaloub, and Amy Yasbeck, Ritter’s widow, came together for The John Ritter Foundation Evening From The Heart gala to support the foundations work raising awareness and funds for aortic dissection.

“I see one or two of them occasionally, very occasionally. We're all busy. We're all living in different places, but to have all of us together and Steve Levitan, who was of course a writer on WingsSo having the five of us together [is] really fun,” Tony Shaloub told PauseRewind’s Elizabeh Stanton.

Ritter appeared on several episodes of the sitcom in the 90s, playing a no good ex-boyfriend of his real-life wife, Amy Yasbeck.

“They all freaked out. It was so cute. And what they didn't know is John was excited to go work with all of them. He had been watching the show. He watched the show before he was on it,” Yasbeck said of the cast’s reaction to Ritter’s appearances on the show. “But it was just so cute because everybody wanted like a some kind of physical like bit to do with them, and they all got it. They all got a little piece of him.”

Shaloub said of Ritter, “This is a man who we've all admired, from of course from the success of ‘Three's Company’ and films, of course. But to have him with us on the set and see this brain at work and this kind of this comedic genius, physical comedy, it was just inspiring, and not to mention that even off camera, just bantering, schmoozing together, just so beyond funny, electrifying.”

Having the support of her “Wings” costars and others means the world to Yasbeck and Ritter’s family.

“It feels really good because sometimes, what happens when, and if anybody who's lost someone, you lose someone for the first weekend, you have all of the casseroles you'd ever want, then the flowers die, then the phone calls, and then people are awkward about [it] because oh you're single, you're a widowBut the fact that John even when he met people for five minutes or no or waving or they saw him on TV, they felt like they knew him, Yasbeck said.

Ritter died in 2003 from a misdiagnosed aortic dissection when he was just 54.

With the cast gathering for such a special cause, is there any room for a reboot?

Yasbeck noted that AI has the wrong information about how the who ended, confusing fan fiction for reality.

“I never say never. I mean, and I happen to be available,” Shaloub said with a smile.
