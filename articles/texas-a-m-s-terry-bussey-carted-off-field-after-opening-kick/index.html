Texas A&M’s Terry Bussey carted off field after opening kickoff

TheTools · 19.09.2026 19:58 · source · permalink

COLLEGE STATION, Texas (KBTX) - Texas A&M wide receiver Terry Bussey was carted off the field after the opening kickoff after suffering an apparent lower body injury.

After running a few yards with the ball, Bussey appeared to slip awkwardly on his right leg and eventually went down before Kentucky defenders made contact with him. The play was initially ruled a fumble, but Bussey was ruled down after review.

Bussey was able to stand up with help from trainers and limp onto the back of the cart.

The junior from Timpson High School has collected 92 receiving yards on seven catches and has rushed four times for 10 yards and a touchdown.

The Aggies were already without wide receiver Ashton Bethel-Roman, along with running back Jamarion Morrow, who were ruled out prior to the game.
