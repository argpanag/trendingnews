Mario Kart World update adds 8 courses and more

TheTools · 25.11.2026 12:20 · source · permalink

Today marks the release of the Ver. 1.9.0 update for the Mario Kart™ World game for the Nintendo Switch™ 2 system. Watch the video for a look at some of the new additions.

Eight additional courses

Eight courses have been added, each a remix of an existing course from the world of Mario Kart World.

The courses include Mario Bros. Circuit 2, DK Pass 2, Shy Guy Bazaar 2, and more.

You can enjoy races with a different flavor from the usual, like speeding through routes different from the ones you have driven before, or racing the usual course in the opposite direction.

Two new routes added to Knockout Tour

Knockout Tour is a mode where players race nonstop across the vast world from one end to the other. The race begins with 24 drivers, but checkpoints appear along the route. Drivers who fail to pass a checkpoint within the required ranking are eliminated. This update adds two new Knockout Tour routes: Feather Rally and Lucky Cat Rally.

  • Feather Rally: A route connecting Mario Bros. Circuit to Boo Cinema.

Feather Rally: A route connecting Mario Bros. Circuit to Boo Cinema.

  • Lucky Cat Rally: A route connecting Great? Block Ruins to Sky-High Sundae.

Lucky Cat Rally: A route connecting Great? Block Ruins to Sky-High Sundae.

The new routes become available after clearing at least one Knockout Tour rally. Additional Knockout Tour routes are planned to be added through future updates.

Enhanced Replay feature

A replay feature has been newly added to the menu screen. Replays of your most recent races and battles are saved, so you can watch again later.

*When the limit for saved replays is exceeded, the oldest ones are deleted first.

*When the limit for saved replays is exceeded, the oldest ones are deleted first.
