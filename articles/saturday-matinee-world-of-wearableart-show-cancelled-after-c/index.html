Saturday matinee World of WearableArt show cancelled after crew member's death

TheTools · 28.09.2026 15:13 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Business

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._g.pKhUMkRnoOZwX4iq2fhLAqba5Rz0jb3mINKRqASmxf0/business/1581493%2Fsaturday-matinee-world-of-wearableart-show-cancelled-after-crew-member-s-death

A second World of WearableArt performance has been cancelled following the death of a crew member.

Emergency services responded to reports of a medical event at the TSB Arena on Wellington's waterfront at 10.15am on Friday. Police said medical assistance was rendered but the person did not survive.

WOW said they were a beloved and valued member of its team and Friday night's performance was cancelled.

In an update just after midnight on Saturday, chief executive Meg Williams confirmed Saturday's matinee performance would also not go ahead.

"Following the tragic death of a beloved and specialised crew member, World of WearableArt (WOW) has made the difficult decision to cancel a second performance - the 2.30pm Saturday 26 September matinee," she said in a statement.

"While the result of a medical event - and the production has extensive contingency plans in place - we are dealing with an exceptional set of circumstances."

Williams said the crew member worked in a highly specialised area of production, with "a close-knit team who are deeply affected by this loss".

"Because of this, we have this [Friday] afternoon identified a need to make changes to the behind-the-scenes operation of the show to enable it to continue safely for the remainder of the season."

The team would be working on those adjustments during the day, she said, ahead of the scheduled performance that night.

Impacted ticket buyers had been contacted with information about arranging a transfer to another performance or a refund, Williams said.

"We understand there is deep disruption and disappointment to many WOW fans as a result of cancellation and are so very sorry. This has been an extremely difficult decision to again make," Williams said.

"Our thoughts and support continue to be with the crew member's family and the wider WOW whānau.

"We also extend our immense gratitude to the outpouring of aroha and support we are receiving. It means the absolute world to us at this extremely difficult time."

On Friday, a spokesperson for WorkSafe said it was carrying out initial inquiries.

"We understand the matter has been referred to the Coroner," they said.
