Why You Should Start Planning for Black Friday Today: The Ultimate Guide – Parents Africa

TheTools · 24.10.2026 00:25 · source · permalink

Every November, Black Friday comes around, but you forget to plan and end up frantically filling your cart at midnight, hitting sold-out checkout pages, and making impulse purchases you regret by December. Don’t forget ruined holiday budgets. It’s easy to think Black Friday is just a single day of rapid-fire clicking, but seasoned shoppers know that the best deals aren’t won on Friday morning but secured weeks in advance.

Starting your preparations today shifts you from a reactive impulse buyer to an intentional, strategic shopper. By spreading out your planning across November, you secure the high-ticket items on your wishlist, protect your finances, and bypass the annual shopping stress entirely.

Here is your step-by-step, week-by-week guide to mastering Black Friday sales long before that midnight.

Week 1 (Nov 1–7): Wishlist & budget audit

The first week of November is about setting boundaries and identifying what you actually need versus what algorithm-driven ads tell you to buy.

  • Walk through your routine. Are your core skincare serums running low? Is your winter wardrobe missing a quality wool coat? Are you planning to upgrade home tech or buy major Christmas gifts? Write down explicit categories.
  • Set a total spending budget for November sales and stick to it. Allocate exact spending buckets (e.g., 40% for high-ticket investments, 40% for holiday gift lists, 20% for personal beauty or apparel restocks).
  • Build a dedicated document or phone note listing exact product names, shade numbers, sizes, and model specifications. Specify the regular retail price so you can spot genuine discounts later.

Week 2 (Nov 8–14): Price tracking & account set-up

Retailers love raising prices slightly in October only to drop them back to “normal” under the guise of a 50% discount in November. Week two is all about price transparency.

  • Use price-tracking tools or save direct links to your items across major retailer sites. Note their baseline non-sale pricing today so you instantly know if a Black Friday “deal” is authentic.
  • Create accounts on key brand websites now. Pre-save your shipping address, sizing preferences, and payment details. When doorbuster deals go live, losing three minutes typing in a credit card number is often the difference between snagging an item and seeing “Out of Stock.”
  • Brands frequently launch 24-hour secret sales or offer exclusive subscriber discount codes up to a week before Black Friday. Create a dedicated shopping folder in your email inbox to track these incoming codes.

Week 3 (Nov 15–21): Early-bird & soft launch window

“Black Friday” is no longer a single 24-hour event; it is an entire week. By mid-November, major retailers begin soft-launching early access discounts.

  • Before making early purchases, read the fine print. Ensure early Black Friday items are eligible for standard returns or holiday exchange windows, especially for apparel or tech.
  • If a Holy Grail skincare set or tech gadget from your wishlist hits a 30% to 50% discount during early-access week, buy it immediately. High-demand items rarely get deeper discounts on the official day; they simply sell out.
  • Check credit card rewards, cashback portals, and store loyalty points. Combining a store sale with cash-back bonuses maximises your total savings.

Week 4 (Black Friday Week): Execution & calm reflection

Because you built a master list, audited prices, and pre-loaded your accounts, Black Friday week becomes an exercise in calm execution rather than frantic panic buying.

  • Open your pre-loaded carts early Friday morning, apply saved promo codes, and complete your purchases cleanly within minutes.
  • Ignore flash sales for random items that were never on your Week 1 wishlist. A cheap item you didn’t need is still money wasted, regardless of how big the discount tag looks.
  • Revisit your original budget cap from Week 1, tally your receipts, and celebrate executing a stress-free, highly curated holiday haul.

Black Friday doesn’t have to mean chaos, endless scrolling, or buyers’ remorse come December. By changing your mindset today and executing a intentional, week-by-week strategy, you take full control of your holiday spending. You’ll walk away with the exact items on your wishlist, protect your bank account, and enjoy the holiday season knowing you mastered the sales instead of letting them master you. So open up a blank note on your phone, start building that wishlist now, and let this November be your smartest, most seamless shopping season yet.
