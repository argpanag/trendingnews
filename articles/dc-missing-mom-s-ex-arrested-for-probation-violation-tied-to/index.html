DC missing mom's ex arrested for probation violation tied to 2021 assault on her

TheTools · 15.09.2026 18:52 · source · permalink

Antoinette Forbes was last seen Sept. 4. Russell Ashford, was arrested on a probation violation in Pennsylvania but not charged in her disappearance.

Antoinette Forbes, a D.C. mother, remains missing after more than a week. Her ex was arrested in Pennsylvania on a probation violation. He was on probation after pleading guilty to assaulting Forbes in Maryland in 2021. News4’s Aimee Cho reports.

As the desperate search for a missing D.C. mother continues, authorities say they arrested her ex in Pennsylvania after a probation violation. He was on probation after assaulting her in 2021, court documents say.

Antoinette Forbes, 53, was last seen Sept. 4 near her home in Northwest D.C. Loved ones describe her as a devoted mom who would never choose to leave her young son.

Russell Ashford – Forbes’ ex-partner and the father of her son – was arrested during a traffic stop in Pennsylvania on Saturday for violating the terms of his probation in Maryland, Pennsylvania state police said.

D.C.’s Metropolitan Police Department has not named Ashford a suspect in Forbes’ disappearance or charged him in connection to her disappearance.

Court records in Montgomery County say Ashford was on probation after he pleaded guilty to assaulting Forbes in Chevy Chase, Maryland, in 2021.

After that incident, a neighbor testified that he heard the couple arguing all night and became so concerned that he took the couple’s son into his home. The neighbor said he heard a thump, ran into the Forbes’ house and found Ashford “throwing closed fist punches at Forbes.”

Ashford was sentenced to probation before judgment in the case.

It wasn’t immediately clear which terms of his probation Ashford is accused of violating.

News4 tried to speak with an attorney for him, but the attorney listed in his assault case said they no longer represent him.

Pennsylvania police said Ashford was being held on an out-of-state warrant and is in custody. The Montgomery County State's Attorney's Office said Ashford likely will be extradited to Montgomery County and will appear in court regarding the probation violation.

No new information was immediately released on Forbes’ possible whereabouts. Her son is safe and being cared for by a relative, a source familiar with the matter told News4.

Forbes’ loved ones said they discovered she was missing after they went to check on her at home and found the front door unlocked, with her car keys, wallet and two phones inside.

'Something happened to her': Antoinette Forbes’ family pleads for help

Forbes’ family on Tuesday released a statement asking for help to find her.

“If you live or work near the 3200 block of Nebraska Avenue NW, please check your doorbell and security camera footage from Friday, September 4 through Sunday, September 6. If you drove through that neighborhood, walked the trails nearby, or were on the American University campus that weekend, please think back,” the statement said.

Anyone with information is asked to contact D.C. police.

“Antoinette is a sister, a friend, a colleague, and above all a devoted mom. She is careful and she is reliable. She would not leave her family by choice. Something happened to her,” the statement said.
