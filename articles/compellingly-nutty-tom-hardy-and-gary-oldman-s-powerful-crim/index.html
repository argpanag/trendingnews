"Compellingly Nutty": Tom Hardy and Gary Oldman's "Powerful" Crime Thriller is Streaming Now

TheTools · 13.10.2026 16:26 · source · permalink

This tense and twisty thriller is a wild ride with a stacked cast

Tom Hardy and Gary Oldman, together on the big screen? Two titans of British acting in a film that isn’t The Dark Knight Rises or Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy? Absolutely. And the result is just as thrilling as you’d hope.

Lawless is a wild ride: a gritty, violent Southern Gothic gangster film with plenty of flair. Fans of either actor, or of blood-soaked crime thrillers, should keep it on their radar if they haven’t seen it already. Here’s everything you need to know before diving into Lawless, including what it’s about, who’s in it and where you can stream it now.

What is Lawless about?

Lawless centres on the conflict between a corrupt lawman and three brothers running a moonshine-bootlegging business. After the eldest brother refuses to pay him off, the lawman sets out to take down the entire family operation.

Who is in the cast of Lawless?

The film has a star-studded ensemble cast, including Tom Hardy, Shia LaBeouf and Jason Clarke as the three brothers, alongside Guy Pearce as the corrupt lawman. Jessica Chastain, Gary Oldman and Mia Wasikowska also appear.

What have critics said about Lawless?

When Lawless opened at the Cannes Film Festival, it received largely positive reviews. It now has an approval rating of 67 per cent on review-aggregation website Rotten Tomatoes, where the critical consensus called it “beautifully filmed and powerfully acted”. Owen Gleiberman of Entertainment Weekly wrote that it “has the kick of a mule”, while Peter Travers of Rolling Stone called it “a solid outlaw adventure”. Chuck Bowen of Slant Magazine called it a “compellingly nutty” gangster film.

Where can I watch Lawless?

Lawless is available to rent on Apple TV+ and Sky Store.
