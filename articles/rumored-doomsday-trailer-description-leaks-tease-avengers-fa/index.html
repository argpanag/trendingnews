Rumored Doomsday trailer description leaks tease Avengers facing Doombots, Earth-10005 destroyed and more - AOL

TheTools · 23.11.2026 08:50 · source · permalink

Avengers: Doomsday is just a day away if Hayley Atwell is to be believed. However, that hasn't stopped fans from speculating about the third and final trailer for the MCU epic. Thus an X (formerly Twitter) fan account posted not one but two rumored descriptions of the trailer. It seems that we might be getting another Doom-heavy trailer.

We will also see the Avengers fight Doom, and even raid Doomstadt, the capital of Latveria, where they fight the Doombots, the robotic replicas of Victor von Doom. One description also teases the return of a character from an iconic Marvel franchise. However, it should be noted that these descriptions of the Avengers: Doomsday final trailer are just rumors, and should be taken as such.

What's in the description of the Avengers: Doomsday trailer?

As mentioned earlier, the X fan account posted two descriptions. One description suggests the appearance of Steve, Thor, Reed, Danny Ramirez, Beast, Sentry, and Nightcrawler. But the biggest highlight of this description is the rumored fight scene between Hugh Jackman's Wolverine and Tobey Maguire's Spider-Man.

If the first trailer description is to be believed, the Avengers Tower is destroyed in Doomsday. If Tobey Maguire is the biggest reveal in the first description, the second description promises us a first glimpse at Doomstadt. According to this description, Steve and Thor reunite for the first time since the Battle of Earth in Avengers: Endgame.

Then, the description also states that the trailer provides a glimpse of what an incursion looks like. Then, it also teases an intense battle between Reed Richards and Doom, and the Latverian ruler absolutely overpowering Cyclops. The description also teases a scene of Deadpool and Wolverine standing in a location with webs in the background (suggesting Spider-Man's presence).

The description then teases the destruction of the Baxter Building, Earth-10005, and the Avengers Tower. But the last scene is different according to this trailer description; it ends with countless Doombots flying towards the Avengers for a battle. Regardless of what happens in the trailer, one thing is clear.

Doom is the most dangerous foe that the Avengers have faced

The trailers confirm that the upcoming Avengers movie will faithfully adapt Victor von Doom on the big screen, something that the previous film franchises ignored. Doom is both a scientist and a sorcerer, who rules over Latveria. Avengers: Doomsday director Joe Russo described this version of Doom as "Gothic" in an Empire interview:

“Doom is a Gothic character. He says, ‘Hell answers to me,’ and he’s not kidding. He’s a very powerful character with some very dark magic, and he’s not above selling his soul to get what he wants. He’s worth being scared of.”

Joe Russo also revealed the tragedy that will push Doom towards his villainy:

“He’s a father who’s a victim of trauma. He has lost a child. When people see the film they say it’s a very scary movie. It’s scary because he’s so profoundly damaged.”

Several leaks have suggested that Doomsday will end with Victor remaking the multiverse according to his liking, and creating Battleworld, the reality created using parts of several Earths.

When is the movie releasing?

The movie is releasing in theaters across the US on December 18, 2026. Avengers: Doomsday and next year's Secret Wars will close the chapter on Marvel's Multiverse Saga. The movie will see the Avengers, Fantastic Four, and X-Men uniting to stop Victor von Doom and incursions. Thus, our heroes are surrounded by threats on two sides.
