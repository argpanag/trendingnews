‘Sorry, I’m still in love with Daniel Craig’: Ralph Fiennes, Brad Pitt and more react to Robert Pattinson as James Bond

TheTools · 17.11.2026 13:03 · source · permalink

Former 007 George Lazenby was supportive while The Lighthouse director Robert Eggers reveals what Pattinson was doing when the news broke

Film industry figures including Ralph Fiennes, Brad Pitt and former Bond actor George Lazenby have reacted to the news that Robert Pattinson has been cast as the new 007.

Pitt was asked about Pattinson while promoting his new film, The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, and responded: “I think it’s a great choice. I love what that guy’s been doing. He’s been making such interesting choices, and then for him to balance it out, it’s really cool.” Pitt then made a playful pitch for a villain role in Bond 26, winking at the camera and saying: “Get Denis Villeneuve on the flip phone. Let’s go.”

Fiennes, meanwhile, was being interviewed with his castmates from The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping (in which he plays President Snow) on Sirius XM, and initially appeared somewhat downbeat, saying: “I’m sorry. I’m in love with Daniel Craig still. It’s hard. I’ve got the wrench in my heart.” But Fiennes, who played Gareth Mallory (who became secret service chief M) opposite Craig in Skyfall, Spectre and No Time to Die, added: “[Pattinson] is a great actor. I’m sure he’ll be great.”

Lazenby, who played Bond in a single film, On Her Majesty’s Secret Service in 1969, posted a congratulatory message on social media, saying: “Robert, welcome to the most exclusive club in the world. But remember to enjoy yourself. Let’s see if you’ll have a ‘this never happened to the other fella’ moment.” Lazenby was making a callback to a fourth-wall-breaking joke in the opening scene of the film, in which his 007 appears to be referring to the role’s previous actor, Sean Connery, when Diana Rigg’s Tracy di Vicenzo drives away rather than succumbing to Bond’s charms.

According to the Hollywood Reporter, Pattinson only found out he had been given the role 24 hours before the public announcement, having been called by Villeneuve with the news. Amy Pascal, the film’s joint producer with David Heyman, is said to have called the four other contenders.

Villeneuve later said in a statement: “I want to express my deep gratitude to the remarkable actors who joined us on this journey. We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007. I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond. He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin.”

Robert Eggers, who directed Pattinson in the 2019 horror film The Lighthouse, said that, when the announcement was posted on social media, Pattinson was in a screening of Eggers’s new film Werwulf – adding that he “wasn’t surprised [the casting] panned out the way it did”.
