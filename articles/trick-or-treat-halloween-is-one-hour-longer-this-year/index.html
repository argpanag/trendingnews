(WJW) — It's almost time to "fall back!"

This year, daylight saving time will come to an end on Halloween night, giving trick-or-treaters and thrill seekers an extra hour of spooky season.

Daylight saving time officially ends Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, at 2 a.m. But, it's the night before that many turn their clocks back one hour.

The sun will set Halloween night at 6:22 p.m. in Cleveland. The very next day it will set an hour earlier at 5:21 p.m.

The days are getting shorter — very quickly!

According to Meteorologist Scott Sabol, Cleveland is currently losing about 2 minutes and 45 seconds of daylight each day.

The shortest day of the year will be Monday, Dec. 21 when Northeast Ohio gets just about 9 hours and 10 minutes of daylight.
