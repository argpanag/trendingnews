BlackRock stock: Firm bids for Singapore CPF investment mandate

TheTools · 27.11.2026 18:02 · source · permalink

BlackRock enters Singapore CPF bidding

  • BlackRock has submitted a bid to manage part of Singapore's Central Provident Fund investment option.
  • The selection process remains preliminary, with a decision expected in the first half of 2027.
  • The proposed CPF option would use age-based portfolios that gradually reduce equity exposure.

BlackRock has submitted a bid to manage part of Singapore's Central Provident Fund under a planned investment option, according to Briefs in a report published Friday, November 27, 2026. JPMorgan Asset Management and Endowus Singapore are also among the leading bidders.

Preliminary process remains open

The proposals cover part of Singapore's CPF, which holds S$701 billion, equivalent to 543 billion US dollars, according to the report. The process is still at a preliminary stage, and no final selection has been made.

People familiar with the process told Briefs that the successful managers are expected to be named in the first half of 2027. The planned CPF option is targeted for launch in 2028.

Age-based portfolios planned

The proposed product would offer portfolios that gradually reduce equity exposure as members age. Assets would move toward lower-risk holdings such as bonds and would be wound down by a specified target date.

JPMorgan is expected to work with DBS Group Holdings, while Endowus is discussing a partnership with Capital Group, the report said. The CPF Board said its review remained under way and declined to comment on specific applicants.

Stock trades above Xetra close

BlackRock stock traded at 1,063.25 euros on Lang & Schwarz at 16:01 CET on Friday, November 27, 1.96 percent above the Xetra close of Thursday, November 26.
