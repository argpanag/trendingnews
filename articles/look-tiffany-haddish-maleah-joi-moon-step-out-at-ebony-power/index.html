Nov. 10 (UPI) -- Girls Trip actress Tiffany Haddish and A Different World star Maleah Joi Moon stepped out Monday at the 2026 Ebony Power 100 gala.

Haddish, 46, and Moon, 24, attended the event at the Hollywood Palladium, along with Debbie Allen, Tia Mowry, Monica and other stars.

The Ebony Power 100 gala celebrates Black excellence across entertainment, sports, business, science and community, with the 2026 theme being "The Stories We Shape."

Honorees included Moon, Allen, Jay-Z, Megan Thee Stallion, T.I., Alicia Keys and Swizz Beatz, Common, Questlove, Keke Palmer, Gina Prince-Bythewood, Victor Wembanyama, Naomi Osaka, Jalen Brunson, and the New York Knicks starting five.

"The Power 100 has always documented the people moving our culture forward, and this year's class demonstrates the extraordinary breadth of that influence. 'The Stories We Shape' is about ensuring Black people remain at the center of telling our stories and defining our collective future," Ebony magazine CEO Eden Bridgeman previously said in a statement.

Tiffany Haddish attends the EBONY Power 100 Awards gala on Monday. Photo by Jim Ruymen/UPI

Moon, who plays the lead role of Deborah Wayne on Netflix's Different World reboot-sequel series, received the fan-voted Ebony Power 100 People's Choice Award, beating out RuPaul's Drag Race's Bob the Drag Queen, Summer House's Ciara Miller and Real Housewives of Atlanta's Phaedra Parks.

A Different World, also starring Alijah Kai, Cornell Young IV, Jordan Aaron Hall, Kennedi Reece and Chibuikem Uche, was renewed for Season 2 in October.
