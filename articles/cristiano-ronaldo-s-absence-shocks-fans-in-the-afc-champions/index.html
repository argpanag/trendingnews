Cristiano Ronaldo missed Al-Nassr's AFC Champions League clash against Pakhtakor. Find out why he was absent

Cristiano Ronaldo was the biggest absentee from Al-Nassr's squad for their clash against Uzbekistan's Pakhtakor on Matchday 4 of the AFC Champions League. The Portuguese forward did not travel with the team for Monday's match, November 2, 2026, following a decision made by head coach Ange Postecoglou.


WHY CRISTIANO RONALDO MISSED THE TRIP TO UZBEKISTAN

The 41-year-old forward's absence was not injury-related. Australian manager Ange Postecoglou decided to rest him due to Al-Nassr's packed schedule and the constant travel demands the Saudi club faces throughout the season.

During the pre-match press conference, Postecoglou explained that Ronaldo, Kingsley Coman and Mohamed Simakan were all rested because of the team's demanding fixture list and extensive travel commitments.

The decision aims to prevent unnecessary physical fatigue among three key Al-Nassr players as the club continues to compete for domestic honors and continental success.


HOW MANY GOALS HAS CRISTIANO RONALDO SCORED AND WHEN WILL HE RETURN?

Cristiano Ronaldo heads into this break following an outstanding performance in the Saudi Pro League. On Thursday, October 29, he scored a hat-trick in Al-Nassr's 5-3 victory over Al-Kholood, taking his career tally to 985 official goals, just 15 shy of the historic 1,000-goal milestone.

The Portuguese forward is expected to return to action on Friday, November 6, when Al-Nassr face Al-Hazem in the Saudi Pro League.

Therefore, his absence against Pakhtakor was the result of squad management and workload planning, not a physical issue. The goal is to ensure their captain is in better condition for the upcoming fixtures.


KEY TAKEAWAYS FROM CRISTIANO RONALDO'S ABSENCE

  • PLANNED REST: Ange Postecoglou decided to leave Cristiano Ronaldo out against Pakhtakor to prevent physical fatigue.
  • NO INJURY: The Portuguese star's absence was due to a packed schedule and extensive travel, not a physical issue.
  • EXPECTED RETURN: CR7 is set to return on November 6 against Al-Hazem in the Saudi Pro League.
