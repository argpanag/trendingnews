Samsung TV Plus Brings 2026 MAMA AWARDS to K-pop Fans Around the World - Samsung Global Newsroom

TheTools · 20.11.2026 11:08 · source · permalink

on November 19, 2026

Samsung Electronics is once again connecting K-pop fans worldwide by livestreaming 2026 MAMA AWARDS. Through a partnership with CJ ENM, a leading Korean entertainment company, Samsung TV Plus will broadcast the entire two-day event, with CHAPTER 1 taking place on November 20 and CHAPTER 2 on November 21.

2026 MAMA AWARDS will air live on the CJ ENM K-POP channel in 14 countries,1 featuring performances by some of K-pop’s biggest stars.

A Celebration of K-pop Across Generations

Held at Kyocera Dome Osaka in Japan, 2026 MAMA AWARDS kicks off on November 20 with the red carpet at 3:30 p.m. Korea Standard Time (KST), followed by the awards show from 6 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. KST.

Special collaborations will bring this year’s awards’ “A MUSIC INTERSTELLAR” theme to life. Under the slogan “All Generation,” this year’s MAMA AWARDS aim to connect K-pop’s past, present, and future by uniting artists, genres, artistic forms, and fans. Highlights include BIGBANG and Dooly the Little Dinosaur, ENHYPEN and “Dune: Part Three,” and Panic and younger K-pop artists’ special collaboration with WOODZ and members of CORTIS, ZEROBASEONE, ALLDAY PROJECT, and Hearts2Hearts.

The “Art Meets MAMA” project will further reflect the “All Generation” slogan by showcasing the work of acclaimed Korean contemporary artist Lee Bae onstage. Through themes of disappearance, creation, time, and cycles, the project will explore the enduring power of music.

“Samsung TV Plus is giving fans around the world a front-row seat to one of K-pop’s biggest celebrations,” said Hun Lee, Executive Vice President of Visual Display (VD) Business at Samsung Electronics. “Together with CJ ENM, we’re bringing fans two unforgettable days of live performances and special collaborations they can enjoy from the comfort of their homes.”

The MAMA AWARDS Experience at Home

To catch every moment, viewers can set reminders on their Samsung TVs to tune into the CJ ENM K-POP channel on November 20 at 3:30 p.m. KST and November 21 at 12:30 p.m. KST for CHAPTER 1 and CHAPTER 2, respectively. While watching, they can adjust their TVs’ sound and lighting settings to recreate the atmosphere of the awards show at home.

2026 MAMA AWARDS are the latest addition to Samsung TV Plus’s extensive lineup of Korean content. With more than 100 million monthly active users and availability in 30 countries, the free ad-supported streaming service continues to expand its premium entertainment offerings, making Korean entertainment accessible to audiences worldwide.
