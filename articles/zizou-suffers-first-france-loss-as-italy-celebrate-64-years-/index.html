Italy are celebrating a pivotal win as they look to rebuild their international standing, inflicting Zinedine Zidane’s first loss as France boss in a comeback 3-1 win at San Siro in the UEFA Nations League, a first home win for Italy in this fixture since 1962.

The hosts got off to a nightmare start when Italy keeper Gianluigi Donnarumma gave the ball away straight to Michael Olise, who had a bit of work to do still before punishing the Manchester City stopper.

But the hosts, now under the guidance of Euro 2020 winning boss Roberto Mancini showed character in launching a comeback, inspired by Pio Esposito’s equaliser off Nicolo Cambiaghi’s assist.

Esposito, who now has nine goals in 14 appearances, bagged the third via the penalty spot, after Davide Frattesi put the Azzurri ahead from Federico Dimarco’s ball.

Italy go top of Group A1, ahead of France on head-to-head.
