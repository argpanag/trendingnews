Pubs across the North East pay tribute to Kevin Keegan

TheTools · 19.09.2026 10:59 · source · permalink

Andy Morley of Creative Ginger has been asked to paint images of King Kev into the windows of boozers across the region

Pubs across the North East are continuing to pay tribute to Kevin Keegan following his death. Andy Morley of Creative Ginger has been asked to paint images of King Kev into the windows of boozers across the region.

The 58-year-old has been creating images of the former Newcastle United manager's famous "I would love it if we beat them! Love it!" rant from 1996. Keegan made the comments while fighting Manchester United for the Premier League title.

Mural artist Andy has also created a poignant image of Keegan standing alongside fellow former Toon manager Sir Bobby Robson, who passed away in 2009. They both sadly lost their lives to cancer.

Andy, from Denton Burn, Newcastle, has so far painted Keegan tributes at pubs in Newcastle, North Tyneside, Northumberland and County Durham. He said: "I think it just shows you how loved Newcastle United is by everybody across the North East and how loved Kevin Keegan was. The pubs want to promote the new season but they also want to have an image of someone everybody loved."

Keegan, who was a Newcastle and Liverpool legend, passed away on July 20 at the age of 75 following a battle with stage four cancer. Following his death, thousands of fans left tributes, including flowers, shirts, scarves and cards, outside of St James' Park.

During Newcastle's first game of the season against Liverpool in August, both teams paid tribute to Keegan. Wor Flags unveiled a huge display and players from both sides wore Keegan 7 on the back of their shirts during the warm up.
