    18:59 BST

    Man City v Norwich City (19:30 BST)

    There has been a lot of noise around Manchester City this week with Sunday's VAR error continuing to make headlines.

    Enzo Maresca was keen to move on from the subject in his press conference on Wednesday. But, he did still get in a little dig at a VAR mistake that went against them in the Manchester derby a couple of years ago.

    In 2023, Marcus Rashford was in a similar situation - in an offside position and interfering with play - when Bruno Fernandes scored. Maresca said his side have still received no apology for that error.

  2. Who is Floyd Samba?published at 18:56 BST
    18:56 BST

    Man City v Norwich City (19:30 BST)

    Shamoon Hafez
    Football reporter at Etihad Stadium

    Manchester City are giving a debut to academy product Floyd Samba tonight, the latest youth team star to graduate into the first-team.

    Samba joined the club's academy at Under-nine level and shone in his first full season in the U18s last season.

    He was one of the standout players, providing 34 goals or assists in 38 games, including a sensational 25-yard free-kick to help City win the FA Youth Cup final against rivals Manchester United.

    At 17 years old, Floyd is the younger of the two Samba brothers at City, with Tyrone being the other.

    Samba, though, will be most famously known for being the son of former Premier League defender Chris.
