FW Erik Joins Vissel Kobe on Permanent Transfer from FC Machida Zelvia | Official Website of J.LEAGUE

TheTools · 11.09.2026 10:54 · source · permalink

Wed, 9 Sep 2026, 17:40 (JST)

Vissel Kobe have announced the signing of forward Erik from FC Machida Zelvia on a permanent transfer. He will wear the No. 94 shirt.
 
Erik was on loan at Kobe from March to December 2025 and returns to the club approximately nine months after leaving.
 
Upon joining the club, Erik commented:
 
“Some stories never end. They are simply waiting for the right time for the next chapter to begin. The city of Kobe welcomed us warmly and gave us so much love. Kobe also became a special place for our family. Now we have returned to Kobe to write a new chapter. Our hearts are filled with gratitude, but our hunger for victory remains exactly the same as before.
 
“This time, I haven’t just come back. I’ve come back to stay. To all the fans and supporters, I want you to fight alongside us. Sing, fight and push the team forward from the first second to the last. This is a story that we will continue writing together. Let’s be united, strong and give everything we have. Let’s keep chasing victories and titles with hunger and determination. Let’s go together, Vissel Kobe!”
