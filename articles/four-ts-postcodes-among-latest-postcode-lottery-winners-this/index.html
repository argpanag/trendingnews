Four TS postcodes among latest Postcode Lottery winners this week

TheTools · 26.09.2026 14:07 · source · permalink

Generic UK notes and coins

Residents across several areas of the North East have scooped prizes with the Postcode Lottery this week. Stockton, Hartlepool and North Shields were amongst the winning postcodes, with players collecting £1,000 each.

The successful postcodes were drawn between Sunday, September 19 and Friday, September 25. Further winning addresses such as Trimdon Station were further afield, but still just representing the TS postcode.

The Postcode Lottery requires participants to pay £12.50 monthly, using their complete home postcode as their ticket. A minimum of 30% of each ticket price is donated directly to charities and local community initiatives.

Participants have collectively raised over £1.8 billion for thousands of worthy causes across Britain and globally, supporting charities such as Maggie's, Alzheimer's Society, Dogs Trust, Mind, NSPCC and numerous others, alongside thousands of smaller local community schemes.

With daily £1,000 draws, weekly Saturday jackpots and multi-million-pound shared prizes, North East residents have numerous opportunities to win each week, reports Chronicle Live.

All North East Postcode Lottery winners this week are listed below:

NE65 0GQ - Amble, Northumberland.

TS25 1NF - Hartlepool.

NE29 0LS - North Shields.

NE62 5NE - Guide Post / Choppington, Northumberland.

TS29 6LT - Trimdon Station, County Durham.

NE33 5PS - South Shields.

TS18 3PR - Stockton -on-Tees.

TS25 5BL - Hartlepool.

DH7 9BA - Waterhouses, County Durham.

SR3 2UE - Sunderland.
