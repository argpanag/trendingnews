Should Inter Miami’s second goal against Cruz Azul been disallowed?

TheTools · 17.09.2026 04:26 · source · permalink

Casemiro's goal remained at the center of controversy because two La Máquina players were left on the ground

Inter Miami put the Campeones Cup game against Cruz Azul beyond reach with a header from Casemiro, who once again found the net in a Herons jersey. However, the Brazilian’s goal raised questions over a possible foul in the buildup.

The Mexican side once again came up short against Lionel Messi and company, this time with the Campeones Cup title on the line.

Was there a foul on Inter Miami’s second goal against Cruz Azul?

In one of the most controversial moments of the afternoon, the Herons scored their second goal from a set piece. Casemiro beat the Cruz Azul defense to the cross, but questions over a possible foul arose after two La Máquina players challenging for the ball ended up on the ground.

The play was not reviewed by VAR. However, replays appear to show Micael blocking the path of the two Cruz Azul players attempting to mark Casemiro, although neither appealed for a foul.
