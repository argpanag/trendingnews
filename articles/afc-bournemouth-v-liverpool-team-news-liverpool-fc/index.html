AFC Bournemouth v Liverpool: Team news - Liverpool FC

TheTools · 20.09.2026 14:42 · source · permalink

Share

Liverpool’s injury situation is unchanged ahead of Sunday’s game at AFC Bournemouth.

The Reds visit Vitality Stadium in the Premier League and, before returning to his former club, head coach Andoni Iraola told reporters there are no fresh fitness concerns in his squad.

Conor Bradley, Federico Chiesa, Hugo Ekitike and Giovanni Leoni remain unavailable.

“No, everyone is ready to go,” Iraola replied, when asked if he has any new issues to consider.

“We are in the same place we were, with the three long-term injuries plus Federico – and the others, nothing [no concerns]. We still need to train tomorrow [Saturday] and travel but everyone is fine.”

Bournemouth are still awaiting a first top-flight victory of the season under Iraola’s successor, Marco Rose, but did win the first European fixture in their history by beating Real Sociedad 2-1 away in the Europa League on Thursday night.

That match took place some 48 hours after Liverpool had defeated Tottenham Hotspur 3-1 at Anfield in round three of the Carabao Cup.

Iraola therefore believes the Reds should hold the upper hand in terms of freshness, but is well aware of the challenge his side will face after journeying to the south coast.

The boss said: “I think we have it, I think we have an advantage. Most of the time it has been the opposite, we are the team that has played the last, but this time we played two days before they played, even if we also have played in midweek.

“So, I think there should be an advantage there but also I know where we are going and I know the stats they have there. Not only in their stadium, overall. I think if you see the games they have lost in a long while you realise how difficult it is to beat Bournemouth.

“But I suppose I have also the advantage that I know them very well and they also have the advantage that they know me very well. So, you never know who is with more of an advantage in that sense.”

Joe Gomez returned from an injury lay-off to start against Spurs in midweek and the Liverpool defender’s next appearance will be the 300th of his club career.

Rose’s pre-Liverpool press conference was held before Bournemouth played on Thursday and he said he will need to assess the fitness and freshness of his players following their trip to San Sebastian.

“Nothing new, everyone is in a good spot. Everyone is well recovered for the travel to Spain, then we are going to start to prepare for Liverpool,” he stated.

“Everyone will give their all on Thursday and then we start to rotate and select players.

“At the beginning, you can handle the load and everything around [playing on Thursday and then again on Sunday], but if you do it every week for several months then it gets really demanding.

“It’s up to me and the coaching staff to make good decisions in terms of who to play and how long to play them. In the end, we have to feel it and the lads have to be ready.”
