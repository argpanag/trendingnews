ATP Hangzhou Day 2 Predictions Including Marozsan vs Bolt

TheTools · 23.09.2026 16:11 · source · permalink

Day 2 of the AITO Hangzhou Open will be a busy one, with 10 first-round matches scheduled. This article features Fabian Marozsan, the highest seed in action, who will compete against Australia’s Alex Bolt. As always, we at LWOT will offer predictions for every match on the slate, but who will advance to the next round?

ATP Hangzhou Day 2 Predictions

Zhizhen Zhang vs Coleman Wong

Head-to-Head: first meeting

Coleman Wong has won many matches in the last two months, but he has come up short at the Tour level and will aim for a deep run this week. He has the upside with his big serve and forehand to open up matches. Wild card Zhizhen Zhang made the final here back in 2024 and generally plays well on his home soil. However, he is nowhere near the level he used to be at, and it will only take his best tennis to beat Wong here. I am going with the player from Hong Kong to advance.
Prediction: Wong in 3

Yunchaokete Bu vs Michael Zheng

Head-to-Head: Bu 0-1 Zheng

Michael Zheng had a successful North American hard court swing, reaching the third round of the US Open and the Cincinnati Masters. He has never played aTtour-level match in Asia before, and it’s going to be a tough one against Yunchaokete Bu, who is a local talent. This is also a rematch of the US Open second round, where Zheng defeated Bu in four sets, and I have a feeling that the Chinese will be seeking revenge on his home soil. The lack of experience for Zheng in Asia makes Bu the slight favourite.
Prediction: Bu in 3

Fabian Marozsan vs Alex Bolt

Head-to-Head: first meeting

Alex Bolt got a favorable qualifying draw, but he made things difficult for himself in the second round by dropping the second set to World No.320 Akira Santillan. Bolt did well to fight back in the third and will play his first tour-level match since April. However, he will be a heavy underdog as Fabian Marozsan is ranked much higher and has won more tour-level matches this season than Bolt has won in his career. If Marozsan plays well, he will win comfortably.
Prediction: Marozsan in 2
