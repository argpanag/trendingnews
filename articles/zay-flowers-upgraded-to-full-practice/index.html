Zay Flowers upgraded to full practice

TheTools · 10.09.2026 23:00 · source · permalink

Baltimore Ravens WR Zay Flowers (hamstring) fully participated in practice Thursday, Sept. 10. The Ravens only held a walkthrough Thursday, so this is an estimation.

SOURCE: BaltimoreRavens.com

Why It Matters: Flowers seems to be dealing with a minor injury and should be ready to play in the regular season opener. He should be among the team leaders in targets this week and will be a starting option in all fantasy leagues.
