Sanders votes in Burlington, calls midterms most consequential in modern history

TheTools · 03.11.2026 17:03 · source · permalink

BURLINGTON, Vt. (WCAX) - U.S. Sen. Bernie Sanders, of Vermont, cast his ballot in his hometown of Burlington on Tuesday, saying he believes this is the most consequential midterm election in modern history.

The senator voted at the Miller Center around 11 a.m.

Sanders has previously endorsed Democrat Amanda Janoo for Vermont governor.

He is supporting more than 70 candidates nationwide. Sanders has been traveling the country, stumping for candidates in the House and Senate, to try to flip it into Democratic control.

National polls favor Democrats to retake the House, but the Senate remains an open question.

Sanders says with the balance of power in Congress on the line, so too is oversight of the Trump administration.

“Whether or not we have a U.S. House and U.S. Senate which act as rubber stamps for Donald Trump to do anything he wants, to condone his behavior and terrible policies, that we have a House and a Senate that hold him accountable,” said Sanders, I-Vermont

If Democrats retake the House or Senate, Sanders says he is concerned the president will deny the results.

“I am convinced you have a president that is an authoritarian and doesn’t believe in the rule of law, still denies he lost the election in 2020 and has done everything he can to make people feel unconfident in the election process, so I am concerned,” Sanders said.
