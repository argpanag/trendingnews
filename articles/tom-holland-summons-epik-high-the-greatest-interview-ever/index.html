Tom Holland Summons Epik High… "The Greatest Interview Ever"

TheTools · 10.10.2026 22:28 · source · permalink

[Dispatch=Lee Myung-ju Reporter] Hollywood actor Tom Holland called out an interview with Epik High.

Tom Holland posted a clip of an interview conducted with Epik High before the release of the film 'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' on SNS on the 9th (Korea time).

Epik High visited London, England at the time. At a meeting with Tom Holland, Zendaya, and Jacob Batalon, they conducted a friendship test, which drew laughter.

Tom Holland praised the video, calling it "my favourite interview ever." He gave it a shout-out two months after its release.

In fact, this interview drew a enthusiastic response. After being uploaded to YouTube, it surpassed 3.61 million cumulative views and had a positive impact on the film's performance.

Meanwhile, Epik High will hold '2027 Epik High Concert' at Seoul Olympic Park K-Spo Dome on January 22-24 next year.
