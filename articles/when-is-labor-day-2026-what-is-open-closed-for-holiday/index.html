Labor Day weekend is here and many families will be making plans for family time during the three-day weekend. Others may be more interested in Labor Day sales.

The holiday honors those who fought for workers' rights in the late 19th-century labor movement. This year, Labor Day is Sept. 7 and it will celebrate more than 155 million people who make up the U.S. workforce.

Labor Day is celebrated on the first Monday of September, which means many government and administrative offices will be closed. Here is what to know about Labor Day openings and closings.

When is Labor Day?

Labor Day is Monday, Sept. 7.

Is US Post Office open on Labor Day?

No. All U.S. Post Offices will be closed Sept. 7 and there will be no regular service.

Is FedEx open on Labor Day?

No. FedEx will not deliver mail or packages on Labor Day, except for FedEx Custom Critical deliveries.

Is UPS open on Labor Day?

UPS will not deliver mail or packages on Labor Day.

Are banks open on Labor Day?

Banks are closed on Labor Day.

Is Walmart open on Labor Day?

Yes, Walmart will be open on Labor Day. Check hours at your location here.

Is Sam's open on Labor Day?

Sam's stores are open on Labor Day. The hours are 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. for Plus members, and 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. for Club members.
