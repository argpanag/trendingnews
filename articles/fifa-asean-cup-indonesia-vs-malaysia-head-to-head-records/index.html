FIFA ASEAN Cup: Indonesia vs Malaysia Head-to-Head Records

TheTools · 28.09.2026 09:20 · source · permalink

Reporter

Antara

September 28, 2026 | 04:20 pm

TEMPO.CO, Jakarta - The Indonesian national team has a record of winning over Malaysia in the last 10 meetings, ahead of the two teams' second match in Group A of the 2026 FIFA ASEAN Cup at the Gelora Bung Karno Main Stadium (SUGBK), Jakarta, on Monday, September 28, 2026, at 7:30 p.m. local time.

According to 11v11.com, Indonesia has won five of the last 10 meetings with Harimau Malaya, with the last victory coming in the 2020 AFF Cup match in Singapore. Indonesia thrashed Malaysia 4-1.

Meanwhile, the other five meetings ended in one draw and four losses. The draw occurred on June 6, 2008, in a friendly match that ended 1-1.

Of the four losses, one was suffered at home, specifically in September 2019 in a 2022 World Cup Qualifier at the GBK Stadium. 

At that time, Indonesia, having led 2-1 at halftime, lost after the visiting team turned the score around to 2-3.

In total, Indonesia and Malaysia have met 85 times since their first meeting on September 7, 1957, at the Merdeka Tournament. Indonesia finished with 33 wins, Malaysia 34, with the remaining matches ending in draws.

In the 2026 FIFA ASEAN Cup, both teams have three points. Malaysia topped Group A after a 3-0 victory over Bangladesh, while Indonesia finished second after defeating Singapore 2-0.
