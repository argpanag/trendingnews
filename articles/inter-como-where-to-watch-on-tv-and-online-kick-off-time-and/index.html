DAZN, Sky Sport and NOW will broadcast Inter v Como live. Here's how to watch on TV and via live stream.

At the Giuseppe Meazza, Inter and Como meet in a Lombardy derby between two in-form teams with very different ambitions. It's one of the most eagerly anticipated fixtures of this Serie A matchday.

Cristian Chivu's Inter arrive after a 3-0 Champions League win over Feyenoord in Rotterdam, with Djed Spence among their best performers. Their 3-1 league derby defeat to AC Milan still weighs on them, though, and they're looking for consistency.

Como, meanwhile, haven't lost in Serie A for weeks. Cesc Fabregas's side have just thrashed Lens 9-1 in the Champions League, a result that caused a stir across Europe. They've won their last five competitive matches.

Fabregas has built something rare in Italian football: a team that plays spectacular football and gets results. The 9-1 win over Lens was no one-off. Como had already beaten Leipzig and Manchester United emphatically, turning themselves from a surprise into an established force.

Chivu is still waiting for the English reinforcements who arrived in the summer, Stones, Jones and Spence, to find consistency. Spence showed positive signs against Feyenoord, but the other two remain sidelined.

Second-placed Inter face fourth-placed Como in a meeting that could shift the balance of the Serie A table.

For Fabregas, Trevoh Chalobah, Assane Diao, Jesus Rodriguez, Moise Kean and Jayden Addai are unavailable. Como can still field a competitive side: Butez in goal, Yan Couto, Ramon, Kempf and Kaiki in defence, Milla and Ricci in midfield, and Nico Paz, Baturina and Da Cunha behind Douvikas.

Inter have won four of their last five competitive matches and lost one, leaving them with a positive overall goal difference. They most recently beat Feyenoord 3-0 in the Champions League, while AC Milan inflicted their 3-1 Serie A derby defeat. Inter have looked solid in Europe and scored in each of those recent wins.

All five of Como's last competitive matches have ended in wins, with goals flowing. Douvikas scored a hat-trick in their latest result, the 9-1 Champions League victory over Lens. Before that, Como beat Manchester United 4-1 in Europe.

Previous meetings

The teams last met in April 2026, when Inter beat Como 3-2 in the Coppa Italia. Inter have won three of their five most recent meetings, with Como claiming one win and one draw. Their Serie A meeting in December 2025 ended in a 4-0 win for Inter in Milan.

League table

Inter are second in the Serie A table, while Como are fourth.
