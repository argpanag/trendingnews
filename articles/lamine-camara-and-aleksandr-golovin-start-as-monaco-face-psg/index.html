Lamine Camara and Aleksandr Golovin start as Monaco face PSG

Lamine Camara and Aleksandr Golovin start for AS Monaco at Paris Saint-Germain on Friday at 21h05 in Ligue 1. According to Nice-Matin, both are in the XI for the Parc des Princes meeting.

Camara features days after the club cancelled his late move to Chelsea on Tuesday night despite earlier approval, a U-turn linked to Folarin Balogun’s on-off switch to Everton at the same time.

OneFootball Videos

Golovin remains targeted in Russia, where the window closes on 10 September. Zenit Saint-Pétersbourg and Spartak Moscou still hope to bring him back eight years after his departure.

Filipe Luis names the same line-up that beat Marseille 2-0 last Sunday. Monaco XI: Hradecky - Vanderson, Dier, Sané, Nazinho - Camara, Zakaria (c) - Coulibaly, Golovin, Idumbo - Brunner.

Takumi Minamino returned to the squad at the weekend, nine months after a knee ligament rupture at Auxerre in the Coupe de France, but he is not on the teamsheet. The coach included him in the group on Thursday, and it is planned that he will rejoin full training next week according to the latest indications.

Ansu Fati, Folarin Balogun, Edan Diop, Mohammed Salisu and Pape Cabral are absent, with Cabral’s move to Al Ahli on standby since last night. For Paris, Maghnes Akliouche starts against his former club, which he left this summer.

Source: Nice-Matin
