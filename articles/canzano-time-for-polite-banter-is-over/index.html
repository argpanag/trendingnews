Canzano: Time for polite banter is over

TheTools · 12.09.2026 19:02 · source · permalink

When a football rivalry is about much more.

Fans of the University of Texas at San Antonio sometimes like to claim that Texas State is like their little brother.

The rival schools play each other in a college football game on Saturday with big political and regional ramifications. I bounced this whole ‘younger sibling’ thing off Kelly Damphousse, the president of Texas State, this week.

Damphousse told me: “We have to beat them.”

Texas State has been around a lot longer than UTSA. It has a former president, Lyndon B. Johnson, as a graduate. George Straight, too. But as you know, perception rules. Football is everything in Texas, and Saturday’s outcome will provide significant framework for this debate.
