Police incident closes SR-179 in Tooele County | Gephardt Daily

TheTools · 14.10.2026 23:19 · source · permalink

ERDA, Utah, Oct. 14, 2026 (Gephardt Daily) — State Route 179 in Tooele County is closed in both directions after an officer-involved incident and the subsequent investigation.

The incident is near milepost 15. Investigators have not yet shared details on what transpired.

The roadway is expected to be closed during the evening commute.

Gephardt Daily will have details as soon as they are available.
