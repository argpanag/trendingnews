Arteta on facing Giroud, Lille and his UCL message

TheTools · 12.10.2026 17:36 · source · permalink

Mikel Arteta could be coming up against a familiar face on Tuesday night when Lille rock up at Emirates Stadium.

Olivier Giroud, now 40, could be in the Lille line-up in N5, a player who featured alongside our boss between 2012 and 2016.

Giroud netted 105 times in 253 matches in north London and ahead of a potential reunion, Mikel has given an insight into playing with the French striker.

He said: "First of all, a great character and a great person to have around the dressing room. 

"I remember when he came in, he won the Ligue 1 with Montpellier. He came here and he really impacted the team in a very different way, a completely different profile than we were used to. What a career he had, and he definitely earned it as well.

"He was extremely intelligent first and he was really, really a handful. Every time you had him on the pitch, you had a massive threat inside the box. He was very smart. He was so good at connecting with other players as well and his work rate was phenomenal."

Lille head to north London in good form, picking up four wins from six games to sit second in Ligue 1 under new coach Davide Ancelotti, son of former Real Madrid, Milan and Everton boss, Carlo. 

Mikel knows the challenge the French outfit face and after guiding us to our second UCL final last season, the boss has called on our players to go one game at a time in the competition, starting with Lille on Tuesday.

He said: "[We're] going to have to earn the right to get in that position again. We know how tough it is and how many times we've done it in the history of our club. 

"Tomorrow we're going to have a really tough opponent in front of us. They are very, very well coached. What Davide [Ancelotti] has done is, I think, remarkable. You can just see the way that they are competing and playing and the problems that they have caused to big teams, and tomorrow is not going to be different until we are ready for it."
