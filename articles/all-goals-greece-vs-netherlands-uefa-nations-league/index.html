Greece hosts the Netherlands at Toumba Stadium on Matchday 3 of the UEFA Nations League, with the top spot in Group A2 at stake.

Greece Looks to Defend the Top Spot

UEFA Nations League action continues this Thursday, October 1, with one of the most intriguing matches of Matchday 3. Greece hosts the Netherlands at Toumba Stadium in a matchup between two national teams that remain unbeaten in this edition of the tournament.


The Greek side enters the match at the top of Group A2 after pulling off one of the biggest surprises of the previous matchday with a narrow victory over Germany. That result allowed Greece to remain unbeaten and enter this match with an opportunity to strengthen its position at the top of the group.

It will now face a Netherlands side that has also made a strong start to the competition. The Oranje has recorded one win and one draw in its first two matches and will look to collect three points on the road to improve its position in the group.
