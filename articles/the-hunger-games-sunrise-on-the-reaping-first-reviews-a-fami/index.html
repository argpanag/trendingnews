The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping First Reviews: A Familiar but Thrilling Return to Form

TheTools · 18.11.2026 12:52 · source · permalink

News

Critics say the latest chapter of Panem history follows a similar path as its predecessors, but fans will appreciate the briskly paced, action-packed journey.

By Christopher Campbell | Published November 17, 2026

TAGGED AS: Action, movies

Panem returns to the big screen this week with the release of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, and the first reviews of the prequel are now online. Based on the dystopian novel of the same name, this sixth movie in the franchise depicts the 50th Hunger Games and features a teenage Haymitch (played by a well-cast Joseph Zada) among the tributes. The ensemble also includes Mckenna Grace, Elle Fanning, Ralph Fiennes, Kieran Culkin, and Jesse Plemons, most as younger versions of characters we know and love. The result is said to be better than the last movie, but perhaps a bit too overstuffed with familiar roles and story beats.

Starring: Joseph Zada, Elle Fanning, Jesse Plemons, Mckenna Grace

Directed By: Francis Lawrence
