Netanyahu urges ‘strong, right-wing’ government as vote counting continues

TheTools · 28.10.2026 04:50 · source · permalink

Exit polls show no outright winner, but suggest Netanyahu’s coalition is tied with the main Jewish-led opposition alliance.

Israel’s Eisenkot says country voted for change

  • Israel’s Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu says right-wing parties have won a “decisive majority” among the Zionist public and calls for the formation of a “national right-wing government”.
  • His speech came as exit polls from Tuesday’s election showed no outright winner. They suggested Netanyahu’s coalition is slightly ahead of or tied with the main Jewish-led opposition alliance, though neither side can secure a majority.
