Hampton Roads is under flash flood warning

TheTools · 06.11.2026 18:22 · source · permalink

Chesapeake, Norfolk, Portsmouth and Virginia Beach are under a flash flood warning until 4 p.m. Friday, according to the National Weather Service at Wakefield.

A flood watch has also been issed for much of the region until 8 p.m. Friday.

A flash flood warning is an urgent alert that is issued by the NWS, meaning that a flash flood is imminent or already happening, compared to a flash flood watch, which means that conditions are favorable for a flash flood to occur.

The flash flood warning began at 12:56 p.m. and was issued due to heavy rain and thunderstorms. Between 1 and 3.5 inches of rain have fallen and up to 1.5 inches of rain may be possible.

Small creeks and streams, urban areas, highways, underpasses, and areas with poor drainage may be impacted.

Downtown Portsmouth, downtown Norfolk, Old Dominion University, Norfolk State University, Churchland, Ocean View, Batten University, Ghent, Cradock, Norview, Norfolk NAS, Western Branch, Norfolk International, Bowers Hill and South Norfolk may all experience flash flooding.
