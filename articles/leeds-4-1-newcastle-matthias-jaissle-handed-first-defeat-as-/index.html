Leeds 4-1 Newcastle: Matthias Jaissle handed first defeat as Magpies boss as Dominic Calvert-Lewin leads Elland Road rout

Report and free match highlights as Leeds defeat Newcastle 4-1 in the Premier League; Dominic Calvert-Lewin was the star of the show as Leeds move up to third in the table; defeat is the first for Matthias Jaissle as Newcastle boss

Leeds United maintained their unbeaten start to the Premier League campaign with a breathless 4-1 victory over Newcastle.

Both sides came into the game with five points on the board from the opening three games, but Matthias Jaissle's bubble was emphatically burst as he suffered a dramatic first defeat as Newcastle boss.

Without the electric Anthony Elanga, the visitors offered much less of a threat and could not handle the relentless press from the home side, led by the imperious Dominic Calvert-Lewin.

Farke hails 'outstanding' Calvert-Lewin

Leeds boss Daniel Farke speaking with Sky Sports:

“I wasn’t happy that we allowed them to score one goal. But, it doesn’t take anything away from the fact that my boys were brilliant and we fully deserved to win the game. There’s no such thing as a perfect performance though, and this is one of the rare points I have to criticise.

“We have energy, quality, good structure in and out of possession. We have a good fitness level, also. It’s not just the will, otherwise whoever had the strong will would always win the game. We have to have the right triggers and everyone needs to be spot on. It’s lots of work on the training ground to be that compact and intense.

On Dominic Calvert-Lewin:

“He’s in outstanding shape and he plays with lots of confidence. I expect him to lead the group and he’s taken the responsibility.

“His work rate is top. It’s not just scoring the goals. The way he plays for us is outstanding. He’s definitely one of the stand out strikers in the league.”

Jaissle: We didn't match Leeds physically

Newcastle boss Matthias Jaissle speaking with Sky Sports:

"That’s the Premier League. It was clear from the start and I have said many times we will have ups and downs and challenges along the way. Tonight, we faced that challenge. It’s really tough. Difficult to digest, but this is a chance to learn because there’s not long until the next match at home to show the right reaction.

"You need to bring the fundamentals on the pitch against a physical and strong team. Leeds are so intense, especially at home and we couldn’t match that physicality in the first half. That was a key element. No excuses, we have to learn from it.

"Injuries are also a challenge that we need to overcome and will. This is the message I have given to the players. There will be no issue with confidence."

Carragher: Newcastle got beaten up

Jamie Carragher on Monday Night Football:

"Leeds are fifth in the Premier League table since December. That should make Newcastle feel better as it has been a bad night for them when the world was watching. They have been beaten up by a really good Premier League team.

"It will be a tough season at times for Newcastle with the manager and young players finding their feet in the Premier League. They just got beaten up."

On Dominic Calvert-Lewin:

"Calvert-Lewin was unplayable. One of the greatest sights in football is seeing a centre-forward rise above everyone and attack the ball.

"Look how high his legs are. I don't think there is a better player in the air in the Premier League."

Big learning curve for Jaissle

Sky Sports News' Keith Downie on X:

"That has to be a huge learning curve for Newcastle tonight. Looking at the team sheet for that opening game against Liverpool, with a midfield two and Yoane Wissa in the No 10, it looked like they were going to get overpowered.

"They didn't. Perhaps it was the emotion of the Kevin Keegan tributes, maybe it was because it was the opening game, or perhaps it was the Alexander Isak motivation. There was a lot going on that day.

"But Leeds away on a Monday night, you need physicality & runners. Even a midfield three of Bruno Guimaraes-Sandro Tonali-Joelinton would find that a tough match tonight.

"Newcastle were bullied by Leeds' physicality and numerical advantage in the engine room.

"It's a long season, and it's been a very good start, but that's a real eye opener for the the new Newcastle manager."
