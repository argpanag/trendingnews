Nagaland State Lottery Sambad 12.10.2026 Today 1PM, 6PM & 8PM: Lottery Sambad Live; Full Winners List

TheTools · 12.10.2026 07:09 · source · permalink

The Nagaland state is declaring its Dear Monday weekly lottery on October 12, 2026. Every Monday, the state declares three lottery results, which is called Dear 1PM, Dear 6PM,, and Dear 8PM. They are part of the popular lottery Sambad and offer Rs 1 crore as the first prize, Rs 1,000 as the consolation prize and Rs 10,000 as the second prize. Also, these lotteries offer Rs 500 as the third prize, Rs 250 as fourth prize, and lastly, Rs 120 as the fifth prize. In total, there are six prizes offered by these lotteries, and they are legally run by the state government-backed lottery department.

Notably, the Dear 1PM lottery is popularly known as the Dear Rise 1PM Morning lottery, while the Dear 6PM lottery is also referred to as the Dear Day 6PM or Dear Legend Evening 6PM lottery. The last lottery, Dear 8PM, is popularly called as Dear Clover Night 8PM lottery. These lotteries are announced every Monday by the Nagaland state at 1PM, 6PM, and 8PM. However, the results PDF and winning numbers are out at 1:10 pm, 6:10 pm and 8:10 pm after the lucky draw.
