  3. Penalty 'absolutely unacceptable at this level' - Artetapublished at 22:40 BST 12 September
    22:40 BST 12 September

    FT: Sunderland 0-2 Arsenal

    Arsenal

    Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta, speaking to BBC Match of the Day: "I'm extremely pleased to win again. We had a really tough week, Chelsea, Napoli, winning there and now against a really tough opponent in a difficult place. We had really good periods but didn't score and after that they can suffocate you especially when they decided to be really physical and very direct and we had to overcome a situation that in my opinion was absolutely unacceptable at this level. I'm very worried and very sad because this can never be a penalty ever.

    "Are you surprised? Are you surprised? No. OK.

    "It's a judo roll from the opponent. Ezri [Konsa] does nothing. Just watch it. We have a lot of cameras and VAR we have to take responsibility to protect the game. Today the game was unprotected and it could have cost us the game and the momentum which is a really, really serious thing.

    "I loved the save from David [Raya] the way the team reacted, the way we go again and we go against everything, but this should never happen, it's not fair, and I am sad, very sad, in that sense.

    "I'm making it very clear publicly because it's for the game, it's not only for Arsenal, this cannot happen at this level.

    On David Raya's penalty save: "We should not talk about the save of the keeper, we should talk about what happened before that."

    On Bruno Guimaraes' performance: "Beautiful, he came on, the goal, the assist, remarkable. That why we talk about the finishers and making that impact and being able to support and help the team. He was outstanding."

    Sunderland

    Sunderland manager Regis Le Bris to BBC Match of the Day: "Frustrating result. Good game, especially in the second half. I think we went toe to toe with them and they are flying at the moment.     "We have to believe and then it's a question of fine margins. Those fine margins are so hard to improve because it is the last percent and you need to work hard to get this one. We are in a good place. It's important to digest quickly because we have an impressive week ahead."

Highlights: Forest beat Villa to give Glasner first win as head coach

Igor Jesus' late winner earned Nottingham Forest their first victory at Aston Villa in 32 years and left the hosts still searching for a Premier League win.

The substitute stabbed in with two minutes left as Oliver Glasner's side took a deserved win, the head coach's first with Forest.

Liam Delap had earlier scored his first goal since January to open his Forest account after his £50m summer move from Chelsea.

Alysson levelled for Villa with their first Premier League goal of the season, but the hosts were second best for much of the game.

They could have opened the scoring when Victor Lindelof's strike deflected off Murillo for Sels to turn it on to the post, with Murillo clearing straight to Lindelof – whose instinctive effort was blocked by the goalkeeper.

Yet it was a rare opening with Villa boss Unai Emery taking off £65m record signing Nicolas Jackson at half-time before Forest bossed the second half until Alysson's leveller.

Emery has called for patience after Villa's summer rebuild, but that will be tested after Jesus' late winner.

Aston Villa analysis: Work to do after Brugge false dawn

Unai Emery post-match interview - Aston Villa v Nottingham Forest

This was another poor performance, and as much as Emery will talk about the turbulent summer and changes Aston Villa have experienced, they were disjointed and unable to match Nottingham Forest.

Record signing Jackson was replaced at the break by Tammy Abraham – a player Emery was willing to let leave Villa this summer – in a sign this new-look side have not found their way yet.

It is not a surprise in a sense, because the manager is methodical and the players must take on an incredible amount of detail, but more is still expected of them.

A 3-2 Champions League win at Club Brugge perhaps raised expectations, but there is still a lot of work to do.

Nottingham Forest analysis: Glasner outsmarts Emery again

What does Glasner have over Emery?

After eight games since Glasner arrived at Crystal Palace in February 2024, Emery is still winless against the Austrian.

Glasner's Palace won 3-0 at Villa Park at the start of last season, and while the scoreline was closer this time, again the visitors deserved their victory.

After the draws against Liverpool and Tottenham, Forest's Glasner is getting to grips with his squad and a first win of the campaign will breathe further confidence into the squad.
