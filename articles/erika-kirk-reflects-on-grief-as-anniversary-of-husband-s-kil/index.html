Erika Kirk reflects on grief as anniversary of husband’s killing approaches

TheTools · 08.09.2026 13:53 · source · permalink

Erika Kirk is sharing her reflections on grief, faith and the words that have helped her navigate the past year as the anniversary of husband Charlie Kirk’s killing at Utah Valley State approaches. She said grief can be isolating and that she wanted to share her experience with others who have lost someone they love. (JULIA DEMAREE NIKHINSON/AP)
