Tangipahoa Parish Sheriff's Office arrests two suspects for church building theft

TheTools · 13.10.2026 21:26 · source · permalink

Two men have been arrested in connection with a large-scale theft at a church building in Hammond.

The Tangipahoa Parish Sheriff's Office has arrested Samuel Cutrer, 47, and Robert Magee, 45, in connection with the theft.

Magee is currently being held in the St. Helena Parish Jail as a fugitive from TPSO on one count of felony theft, and his transfer is pending unrelated charges out of St. Helena Parish.

On May 6, it was discovered that copper tubing as well as condensers and parts from multiple A/C units were missing from the property of the former Harvest Church building located at the 17000 block of north I-12 Service Road in Hammond.

The repairs for the damage and replacement of the parts exceeded $100,000.

Surveillance video of the suspects was released to the public on May 14th, and TPSO says tips helped detectives identify Cutrer, and a warrant was issued for his arrest.

On Sept. 24, Cutrer was located and arrested on the outstanding warrant connected to this case as well as almost a dozen other unrelated charges.

As the investigation continues, the second suspect was identified as Magee, and a warrant was issued for his arrest.

It was then learned that he was already in custody at the St. Helena Jail.

TPSO says this is Magee's fourth copper theft-related arrest in Tangipahoa Parish in the past six months.

This is an ongoing investigation.
