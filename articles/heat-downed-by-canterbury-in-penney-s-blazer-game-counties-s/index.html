Heat downed by Canterbury in Penney's blazer game - Counties Sports Hub

TheTools · 19.09.2026 21:30 · source · permalink

Sep 20, 2026 | Rugby

The Counties Energy Heat have crashed to a 46-0 defeat to Canterbury in their Farah Palmer Cup match at Navigation Homes Stadium on Saturday.

It was all one-way traffic in the contest, with the table topping Canterbury side in complete control.

Errors were an issue for the home side, and they just weren’t able to build any pressure.

It means the Heat fall to 0-4 and will face the Bay of Plenty Volcanix next weekend in Tauranga, needing to win the match to avoid relegation to the Championship.

Halfback Ffion Penney celebrated her blazer game for the Heat.
