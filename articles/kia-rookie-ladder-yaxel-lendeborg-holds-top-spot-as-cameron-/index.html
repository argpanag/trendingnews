Kia Rookie Ladder: Yaxel Lendeborg holds top spot as Cameron Boozer climbs to No. 2 | NBA.com

TheTools · 20.11.2026 02:06 · source · permalink

Kia Rookie Ladder

The forwards in Golden State and Memphis grab the top spots after the 1st month of the 2026-27 season.

Jeff Zillgitt

Yaxel Lendeborg threw down a Dunk Score of 103.4 against New Orleans.

As the 2026-27 NBA season unfolds in unique, wonderful and surprising ways, the rookie class has provided a handful of encouraging truths.

This was a highly anticipated class, no doubt, with talented players throughout the draft, from the lottery picks through the second round. Teams with at least one draft pick went into it thinking they were going to get a talented player.

And already, there have been remarkable and historic performances.

But another fact stands out: many of these rookies are NBA-ready in terms of IQ and understanding the game. That’s not to say they don’t make mistakes or are All-Stars in Year One, but they play with confidence and grasp the concepts of today’s game, from Darius Acuff’s patient offensive approach to Yaxel Lendeborg’s plug-and-play adaptability to Aday Mara’s sense of offensive motion.

It’s fun to watch them develop.

Here’s the latest Kia Rookie Ladder through the first four weeks of the season:

1. Yaxel Lendeborg, Golden State Warriors

Season stats: 20.8 PPG, 7.4 RPG, 3.4 APG (47.6 FG% / 39.2 3P% / 76.2 FT%)

Game of the week: 25 points, 10 rebounds and two steals in a loss to Houston.

With Stephen Curry sidelined due to an injury, Lendeborg is learning how much more difficult it is to produce offensively, but he still recorded at least 23 points in four of his past five games, including 28 points in a loss against New Orleans. It was his eighth 20-point game of the season as other rookies eye the top spot in a competitive class.

2. Cameron Boozer, Memphis Grizzlies

Season stats: 16.5 PPG, 9.5 RPG, 5.4 APG (39.3 FG% / 32.2 3P% / 87.5 FT%)

Game of the week: 29 points (career high), seven assists, seven rebounds and seven made 3s (career high) in a victory against Washington.

Boozer started hot, cooled off a bit and now is finding his offensive rhythm again, especially with his shooting. He’s 48.6% from the field (an impressive 59.5% inside the 3-point line) in his past five games. And his rebounding remains outstanding.

3. Darius Acuff Jr., Sacramento Kings

Season stats: 25.5 PPG, 7.1 APG, 3.5 RPG (41 FG% / 32.5 3P% / 84.5 FT%)

Game of the week: 26 points (10-for-25 shooting) and six assists in a loss to Oklahoma City.

Gaining confidence with his ability to run the offense, Acuff leads rookies in 20-point games with 10, including a career-high 38 earlier this season. He’s showing a command of the offense, too, with at least six assists in his past seven games.

4. Darryn Peterson, Utah Jazz

Season stats: 17.1 PPG, 4.3 RPG, 3.8 APG, 1.2 SPG (40.5 FG% / 35.6 3P% / 88.4 FT%)

Game of the week: 28 points, seven rebounds, two assists and two steals in a loss to Houston.

Peterson became the first teen in Jazz history and the second-youngest player in NBA history (behind Anthony Edwards) to score at least 20 points in five consecutive games. During that five-game run, he shot 47.3% from the field and 38.6% on 3-pointers.

5. AJ Dybantsa, Washington Wizards

Season stats: 15.6 PPG, 6.1 RPG, 2.3 APG, 1.3 SPG (40.4 FG% / 22.7 3P% / 70.6 FT%)

Game of the week: 21 points, five rebounds, three assists and three steals in a loss to Memphis.

Dybantsa is showing his commitment to both sides of the court with two games scoring at least 20 points, grabbing at least five rebounds and collecting at least three steals. He is the only rookie to have three steals in three separate games this season.

6. Caleb Wilson, Chicago Bulls

Season stats: 18.9 PPG, 6.2 RPG, 1.9 APG, 1.8 SPG (42 FG% / 32.4 3P% / 73.2 FT%)

Game of the week: 15 points (5-for-7 shooting) and five rebounds in a loss to San Antonio.

Wilson was off to a promising start, joining Allen Iverson as the only rookies in league history to record at least 150 points, 15 3-pointers and 10 steals in the first seven games of their career. Wilson is now sidelined through at least mid-December with a fractured right hand sustained against the Spurs.

Last week: No. 6 ↔️
Draft pick: No. 4

7. Aday Mara, Oklahoma City Thunder

Season stats: 9.1 PPG, 6.5 RPG, 1.4 APG, 1.7 BPG (63.3 FG% / 59.1% FT%)

Game of the week: 16 points (8-for-11 shooting), 13 rebounds (eight offensive) and three assists in a victory against Sacramento.

Mara has displayed impressive versatility for his size in Oklahoma City’s creative offense with two double-doubles and strong passing skills in his past four games.

Last week: No. 7 ↔️
Draft pick: No. 12

8. Keaton Wagler, LA Clippers

Season stats: 12.3 PPG, 3.4 RPG, 3.3 APG (35.4 FG% / 36.8 3P% / 86.4 FT%)

Game of the week: 14 points in 18 minutes in a victory against Oklahoma City.

Clippers coach Ty Lue said he would give Wagler minutes and space to grow, and that’s what is happening. Wagler can score, and per 36 minutes, he’s averaging 19.2 points, 5.3 rebounds, 5.2 assists and 8.6 free throw attempts – all good signs for Wagler and the Clippers.

Last week: Not ranked ⬆️
Draft pick: No. 5

9. Meleek Thomas, Cleveland Cavaliers

Season stats: 13.3 PPG, 2.9 RPG, 1.6 APG, 1.2 SPG (47 FG% / 42.3 3P% / 75 FT%)

Game of the week: 20 points (8-for-17 shooting), four rebounds and two assists in a victory against Dallas.

Teams love finding contributors in the second round, and Thomas has been that guy for the Cavaliers. He’s already started three games, logged at least 30 minutes in his past two games and has scored in double figures in his past five while registering efficient shooting on 3s.

Last week: No. 10 ⬆️
Draft pick: No. 34

10. Brayden Burries, Milwaukee Bucks

Season stats: 9.4 PPG, 5.4 RPG, 2.4 APG (41.1 FG% / 26.5 3P% / 73.8 FT%)

Game of the week: 13 points, eight rebounds, four assists and two steals in a loss to Atlanta.

Burries has shown off his explosive hops and rebounding skills as a 6-4 guard with big-time dunks and five games with at least six rebounds. Like Wagler, Burries produces solid per-36-minute averages: 15.5 points, 8.8 rebounds and 3.9 assists.

Last week: Not ranked ⬆️
Draft pick: No. 10

Other rookies to note:

Christian Anderson Jr., Charlotte Hornets: 16 points (5-for-10 from the field, 4-for-8 on 3-pointers) in 18 minutes in a loss to the LA Clippers.

Allen Graves, Toronto Raptors: 13 points (3-for-4 on 3s), five rebounds (four offensive) in 18 minutes in a loss to Boston.

Jaron Pierre Jr., New Orleans Pelicans: 13 points (5-for-11 shooting) in a loss to Denver.

Ryan Conwell, Miami Heat: 20 points (6-for-12 from the field, 4-for-9 on 3s) in a victory against Cleveland.

Koa Peat, Phoenix Suns: 10 points (5-for-7 shooting in the fourth quarter) in eight minutes in a loss to the Los Angeles Lakers.
