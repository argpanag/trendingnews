AT&T will take $1,200 off your preorder of the iPhone Duo - what to know - ZDNET

Apple's first foldable phone is available for preorder now.

Apple’s first foldable phone is finally available for preorder. The iPhone Duo, announced back in September, can be ordered now — though delivery times may be delayed.

If you want to scoop up savings on the new phone through a carrier, AT&T is offering $1,200 off the foldable when you preorder with them, with a trade-in of a device worth $230 or more and an eligible plan. This helps you nab the phone for about $22 per month.

Preorder the iPhone Duo for $800 (save up to $1,200)

That price is after up to $1,200 in credits over 36 months. Credits may vary by trade-in and wireless plan combination and could be less. Be sure to check out the full terms or visit your local AT&T store. AT&T also has several other offers you can consider, like saving up to $800 per line when you break your contract and switch to AT&T.

Carrier deals like this one are not no-strings-attached, but they often are the easiest way to obtain the latest and greatest models while still being able to finance the device. Here’s what you need to know about the trade-in offer.

If your device is valued at less than $230, it won’t be eligible for this promotion. If your trade-in device is worth $230 or more, you’ll be eligible for up to $1,200 off the new iPhone Duo, applied as 36 monthly bill credits. However, the final savings amount and trade-in value are determined by AT&T, and you can get a clear estimate online at AT&T. A down payment or activation fee may be required for some customers.

If you cancel your account before you receive all of the bill credits, you’ll be required to pay the remaining device balance. Bill credits end if you pay off your phone early. Tax on the pre-credit price is due when you buy the phone.

The phone is slated to officially hit shelves on Friday, Oct. 23 (though preorder delays have already been reported), and those interested in this deal from AT&T can read the full terms online.

The iPhone Duo is powered by the A20 Pro chip, making it 40% faster than the iPhone 17 Pro Max, according to Apple. Along with regular 5G cellular connectivity, the Duo supports satellite messaging and roadside assistance to help keep you in touch with friends and family in an emergency.

Along with an oversized 7.6-inch Super Retina internal display, the Duo also has a 5.4-inch outer screen for quickly checking notifications, streaming, and photography. The battery also got an upgrade, with up to 44 hours of use on a full charge.
