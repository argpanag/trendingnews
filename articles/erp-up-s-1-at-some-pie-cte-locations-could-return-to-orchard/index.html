ERP up S$1 at some PIE & CTE locations, could return to Orchard on Saturdays

TheTools · 16.11.2026 09:16 · source · permalink

Motorists are encouraged to plan their journeys and consider alternative routes.

November 16, 2026, 03:23 PM

Photo from Google Maps

The Land Transport Authority (LTA) will increase Electronic Road Pricing (ERP) rates at two expressway locations by S$1 from Jan. 4, 2027.

This is based on LTA’s monitoring of traffic conditions in October 2026, which showed traffic has built up along various stretches of expressways, it said in a press release on Nov. 16.

The increases will apply to:

Motorists are encouraged to plan their journeys and consider alternative routes, LTA said.

Reduced ERP rates in December

Meanwhile, ERP rates will be temporarily reduced at 12 locations during 31 specified time periods, during the school holiday period from Nov. 23 to Dec. 31, 2026.

These locations and time slots were identified in anticipation of lower traffic demand, LTA said.

The rates will revert to the prevailing ERP rates from Jan. 4, 2027.

The reductions will apply to:

Monitoring traffic

In addition, LTA shared that they are monitoring the traffic in Orchard and assessing whether to resume ERP operations for the Orchard Cordon on Saturdays in the next quarter.

LTA has also observed heavy traffic at CTE (SLE) near Braddell Road entry and KPE (MCE) near Airport Road entry, where speeds have persistently been lower than optimal.

Separately, LTA is monitoring congestion at two other locations: the Central Expressway (CTE) towards the Seletar Expressway (SLE) near the Braddell Road entry, and the Kallang-Paya Lebar Expressway (KPE) towards the Marina Coastal Expressway (MCE) near the Airport Road entry.

If road congestion persists, LTA will introduce ERP charging at these new locations, the authority said.
