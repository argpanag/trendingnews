Kim Kardashian's Boldest Red Carpet Gown Yet Will Make You Purr

TheTools · 24.10.2026 23:04 · source · permalink

Kim Kardashian showed off her curves in a leopard-print gown while stepping out at the All’s Fair season two premiere alongside her mom Kris Jenner in Rome Oct. 24.

Kim Kardashian is owning the spot-light.

Indeed, the Kardashians star turned the red carpet into a cat-walk by wearing a body-hugging leopard-print gown while attending the All’s Fair season two premiere at the Rome Film Festival on Oct. 24 in Rome, Italy.

And Kim—who stars as lawyer Allura Grant in the Hulu series—proved that she was feline fine with her red carpet look. After all, the 46-year-old showed off her curves in the gown, which featured a low-cut corset-style bodice with a lace-up tie and a mermaid silhouette.

To accessorize her purrfect ensemble, Kim—who was styled by Law Roach—rocked black pumps and a matching leopard print shawl, posing on the carpet with the scarf over her head, while also switching it up and putting the chic accessory around her shoulders.

Along with her All’s Fair costars, the SKIMS founder also had someone extra special keeping up with her on the carpet: her mom Kris Jenner.

And the 70-year-old paid homage to both her daughter’s role in the legal drama and Elizabeth Taylor with her look.

Indeed, in a scene from the upcoming season of All’s Fair, which was featured in the trailer, Kim donned what appeared to be a replica of the late icon’s hooded black velvet evening cape with scorpion embellishments that she notably wore at Princess Grace’s Scorpio Ball in 1969.

And while supporting Kim at the show’s season two premiere, Kris—whose birthday is Nov. 5, making her a Scorpio—also donned the elegant robe, which she paired with white gloves and matching shoes.  

The mother-daughter duo honoring Taylor surely won’t come as a surprise to fans as Kim has long expressed her admiration for the late Cleopatra actress.

Along with channeling Taylor on the red carpet, the mom of four also executive produced the 2025 docuseries Elizabeth Taylor: Rebel Superstar, praising Taylor as “unapologetically herself” and “a fighter.”

“She is proof that you can keep evolving and changing,” Kim shared in 2024, per The Hollywood Reporter, “have different chapters in your life—and she paved the way for all of us who came after her with that blueprint.”
