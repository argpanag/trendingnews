Julie Hesmondhalgh vows to be a 'traitor hunter extraordinaire' on latest series

TheTools · 02.10.2026 20:29 · source · permalink

Accrington actress Julie Hesmondhalgh has declared herself a "traitor hunter extraordinaire" ahead of entering the Celebrity Traitors castle.

The former Coronation Street star will join a glittering line-up including Michael Sheen, Maya Jama, Romesh Ranganathan and Bella Ramsey as the hit BBC series returns.

And while many viewers might expect her to thrive as one of the show's scheming Traitors, the 56-year-old says she has her sights firmly set on hunting them down.

Asked to describe her strategy in just three words, Julie replied: "Traitor hunter extraordinaire."

The actress, best known for playing Hayley Cropper in Coronation Street, admitted she is obsessed with the programme and has watched versions from around the world.

She said: "When somebody comes to you and asks you to be part of something like this, you don't hesitate.

"I'm very, very nervous about putting myself out there in the world, being myself rather than playing a role.

"I know that this is purely about the game, and I am a super fan and have been since the first episode. I've watched all the series from all over the world. I am absolutely obsessed."

Despite being tipped by fans to make a convincing Traitor, Julie insists her instincts lie with the Faithfuls.

She said: "I hate it when the Traitors win.

"People keep messaging me since it was announced and everyone's like, 'Oh, you'd be a great Traitor'.

"But I know myself, and I know that if you hate the Traitors winning then that says something about what is your natural state."

Her favourite former contestant was not a winner but comedian and actor Nick Mohammed.

Julie said: "I feel like Nick was very specifically himself.

"He made a mistake at the end, obviously, but I feel like he was kind, and he was himself. I think he played it beautifully, and as I would hope to play it as a Faithful."

Born in Accrington, Julie played Hayley Cropper from 1998 until 2014, with the character becoming one of the soap's most beloved figures.

She believes her public image could become both a blessing and a curse in the game.

Julie said: "People would assume I'm the same as Hayley from Coronation Street.

"Then again, people may think the opposite.

"There might be some suspicion on me to be a Traitor."

However, she believes being viewed with a little suspicion may actually help her survive.

She said: "You don't want to be the most faithful of Faithfuls.

"You need just the right amount of suspicion on you to be useful to the Traitors, so they keep you around as a distraction."

The actress admitted her biggest weakness could be being "too trusting", while her greatest strength is being a team player.

Celebrity Traitors returns to BBC One and BBC iPlayer tonight.
