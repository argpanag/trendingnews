The Albies: George and Amal Clooney, Emily Ratajkowski and more stars dazzle at 2026 awards ceremony

TheTools · 09.10.2026 19:15 · source · permalink

George and Amal Clooney brought Hollywood glamour to London for the 2026 Albies on Friday evening.

The A-list couple hosted the star-studded awards ceremony at the Natural History Museum, where they were joined by Emily Ratajkowski, Melinda Gates, Josh Hartnett, Felicity Jones and more famous faces.

The annual event, organized by the Clooney Foundation for Justice, honors individuals who have demonstrated extraordinary courage in their fight for justice and human rights around the world.
