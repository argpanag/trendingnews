Susan Dell's husband claps back at savage trolls calling her an 'alien'

TheTools · 13.10.2026 13:25 · source · permalink

Dell founder Michael Dell appears to have fired back at internet trolls who targeted his wife Susan's looks following a recent White House visit.

The 62-year-old's looks went viral on social media after she appeared alongside her billionaire husband — CEO and chairman of tech giant Dell Technologies — in the Oval Office on October 7.

In a widely circulated clip, President Donald Trump, 80, is seen addressing the room while Susan stands behind him wearing a red dress. The couple joined the president after contributing $6.25 billion to "help 25 million children start building a strong financial foundation." Susan's appearance sparked further online debate after throwback photos from before MAGA surfaced, revealing a strikingly different look.

Weighing in on the initiative, Susan stated, "Over the past month, as our contribution reached millions of children's accounts, Michael and I have heard from so many parents and kids and grandparents with these beautiful letters that have truly moved us.

"Children have told us that they want to become entrepreneurs... nurses, doctors, engineers. They want to build their own companies someday. We've heard from parents and grandparents who share what it means to them.

"To know that there is an investment in their child's future. These things are so wonderful and so important. It's not just the money that matters... Knowing someone believes in you and your future that matters just as much," she concluded.

However, their generous act of goodwill was overshadowed when vicious online trolls targeted Susan's appearance, labeling the clip "one of the weirdest things" and "not real."

A third user questioned, "What is wrong with Susan Dell's face/eyes?" while another echoed, "Yes, I can't get over it??? What happened? Don't want to be mean but??"

This prompted a fifth commenter to add, "On another note, can you tell me what is happening with Susan's face? It took me back a minute."

A sixth chimed in, "People are now saying Susan Dell is an alien for her incredible weird look and actions during an interview at the White House."

In response, her husband Michael has since shared numerous posts from individuals who called out the heartless remarks and urged people to back off from Susan.

One post reshared by Michael on his X account stated, "My grandson got 250 dollars in his Trump account from Susan Dell. Please leave that woman alone."

Michael also reposted another message written in response to a post condemning the attacks on Susan, which read, "It's truly insane how evil and full of themselves people are.

"I signed my kids up and wasn't expecting to get anything as we live in a wealthy county and city. Out of the blue $250 showed up from Dell for both of my boys. I will go out of my way to support them now, who else has done what they did? No one.."

Michael has since shared a heartwarming collection of letters, drawings, and cards sent by grateful children thanking him and his wife for their generous contribution.
