Forget Zelda: GTA VI Is Here to Shock the Gaming World!

TheTools · 16.11.2026 18:13 · source · permalink

Vice City, baby!

The Switch 2 remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time has been one of the biggest hits for gaming so far this year. With such a beloved video game franchise, there is always a certain amount of apprehension about remakes, especially when it is something that holds a certain level of nostalgia. However, with The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, the team at Nintendo clearly took care to reintroduce fans to a beloved world.

While there is currently an endless amount of joy being experienced by Zelda fans joining this world once again, some of that may be put on hold, or at the very least on the back burner, for the foreseeable future. On the horizon is an action-thriller event that is set to eclipse pretty much everything that has recently been released.

Originally launched in 1997, the Grand Theft Auto franchise has become one of the biggest in the world. In the fictional cities of San Andreas, Los Santos, Vice City, Liberty City, and more, the landscape of Grand Theft Auto has taken players through storylines and aimless chaos, defining a generation of gamers. Whether it was played for the plot or not, it never diminished the expansive fun players could have within these cities. Grand Theft Auto VI, which has been the priority focus of Rockstar Games' attention for over a decade, stands poised to make history.

The hype for Grand Theft Auto VI has been the biggest in gaming history

From advertisements to social media posts to brands getting in on the hype, the ramp-up to the release of Grand Theft Auto VI may seem excessive, but it's for a good reason. Grand Theft Auto V was released in 2013. Since then, it has been remastered for newer consoles, bringing in a new generation of players.

The long wait for Grand Theft Auto VI has also spawned a generation of internet memes, most notably the "we got ___ before GTA VI" meme. Fans have discussed at length what the return to Vice City will be like, as well as its impressive hyperrealistic graphics that Rockstar has long prided themselves on for this franchise. The world of Grand Theft Auto is also interconnected, with each new installment featuring Easter eggs for other games and characters.

With its release less than a week away now, the talk of Grand Theft Auto VI has successfully eclipsed major film launches, such as The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, which has its Thursday release the same day. In fact, little is heard of other major franchises, such as the final installment of Denis Villeneuve's Dune trilogy. Simply put, nothing else matters more right now than finally playing Grand Theft Auto VI.

The return to Vice City is more than just nostalgia

It's been confirmed that the map for Grand Theft Auto VI is the largest yet, with Vice City taking up less than a quarter of that area. The game also has a roughly 80-hour completion time, and both protagonists have numerous different paths that they can take, which will likely extend that time.

It could have been easy for Grand Theft Auto VI to lean on nostalgia, especially with Vice City being the main city in the game. In increasingly tumultuous and uncertain times, the franchise also serves as a form of escapism, especially for fans who have been replaying Grand Theft Auto V for over a decade. It transcends simple nostalgia, and Rockstar seems to recognize that.

There is a sort of warm, comforting feeling to stepping into these worlds, regardless of whether you're an avid player. Grand Theft Auto VI plans to give players an entirely new experience, which makes it less surprising that this game is all anybody can talk about.
