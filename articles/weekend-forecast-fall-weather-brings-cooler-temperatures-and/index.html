WEEKEND FORECAST: Fall weather brings cooler temperatures and sunshine to Oklahoma

TheTools · 02.10.2026 23:49 · source · permalink

Oklahoma residents can look forward to cooler temperatures this weekend, with highs near 70 degrees and lows in the 50s, along with a mix of clouds and sunshine.

Oklahoma residents can look forward to cooler temperatures this weekend, with highs near 70 degrees and lows in the 50s, along with a mix of clouds and sunshine.

The rain has cleared out of Oklahoma, making way for cooler temperatures and a pleasant fall weekend. Lows for Friday night are expected to dip into the 50s, with some areas around 60 degrees.

KOCO 5 Chief Meteorologist Damon Lane said if you enjoy fall weather, this weekend is shaping up to be a treat.

Saturday morning will start with cloudy skies and some drizzle, but the clouds are expected to break up, allowing temperatures to reach 70 degrees in the afternoon.

Northern Oklahoma may see a bit more sunshine.

Sunday will bring more comfortable conditions, with temperatures feeling wonderful as they move closer to the average high, a welcome change from the record highs experienced recently.

Looking ahead to next week, temperatures are expected to climb slightly, staying in the mid-80s, which is a bit above average.

As October continues, many in the region will begin to anticipate the season's first freeze.
