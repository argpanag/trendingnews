Nuggets conclude road trip against Pelicans | Denver Nuggets

TheTools · 15.11.2026 01:37 · source · permalink

The Denver Nuggets wrap up a three-game road trip in New Orleans.

INJURY REPORT

Denver:
DaRon Holmes II — OUT (G League Assignment)
Ryan Nembhard — OUT (G League Two-Way Assignment)
Julian Strawther — OUT (Left Knee Sprain)
Cam Whitmore — OUT (G League Two-Way Assignment)

The Nuggets play the Pelicans for the third time in 11 games.

They're 2-0 against New Orleans and have a 6-4 overall record to begin 2026-27.

Denver is coming off a strong win over the Memphis Grizzlies. Jamal Murray led the Nuggets in the scoring department with 30 points on 12-of-19 shooting from the field and 4-of-7 from behind the three-point arc. He scored 22 of Denver's final 38 points and has notched at least 30 points in half of his games.

Murray's also leading the NBA in three-point percentage at 51.3 percent for players who have taken at least 50 threes. The next closest on that list is Cleveland's Maleek Thomas, at 47.3 percent, so Jamal has a pretty sizable lead.

The Nuggets have won eight straight games against the Pelicans, their second-longest winning streak against an opponent. The only other team they've beaten more times consecutively is the Utah Jazz (10).

TUNE-IN: Saturday, November 14, 5:00 p.m. MT; Altitude TV; Altitude+; Altitude Sports Radio 950 AM
