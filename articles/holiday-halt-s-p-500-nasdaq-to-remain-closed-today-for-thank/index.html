Holiday Halt: S&P 500, Nasdaq to remain closed today for Thanksgiving Day

TheTools · 26.11.2026 14:50 · source · permalink

    US stock markets, including the NYSE and Nasdaq, remained closed on November 26 for Thanksgiving. Trading resumes Friday, November 27, with an abbreviated session ending at 1 p.m. ET. The holiday break comes amid heightened volatility, AI stock valuation concerns and investor uncertainty, with normal trading resuming Monday.

    The NYSE and Nasdaq will resume trading on Friday, November 27, but will operate for a shortened session.
