Google Outage Today: Is Google Down in US? Users Report Error 1155 'Can I Try Something Else for You', Missing History, Map Failures, Drive Problem & Loading Issue | Check Downdetector Status

TheTools · 08.10.2026 16:58 · source · permalink

Google Outage Today: Problems are being reported by Google and Gemini users on October 8, with the issues ranging from slowness to logins not working, to unavailable services. Gemini users especially have been reporting an issue where the AI is failing to generate anything and some users have lost their conversations.

As based on the most recent user report breakdown of Gemini, issues related to AI generation comprise 69% of the complaints, hence, being the largest category of reported issues. Website issues make up 13% of complaints, whereas application issues constitute 12%. There are user reports of service disruption for Google in a number of countries as well. User reports received on October 8th include slow speed and login issues experienced by US users, as well as unavailability of Google in Japan, Belgium and New Zealand.

These reports do not establish that Google Search or every Google service is experiencing a widespread outage however, they indicate that users are encountering problems across parts of Google’s broader ecosystem at the same time Gemini users are reporting issues with AI generation.

Google Outage Today: Users Report Slow Performance, Login and Access Problems

Recent Google user reports show several different types of problems.

Slow speed of Google was reported by a user from America at 8:52 PM on October 8, while a different American user experienced difficulty in logging into his account at 8:00 PM while other users from different countries also reported issues. Inaccessibility issue was reported by a user from Japan at 6:26 PM, while another one from Belgium reported this same issue at 5:11 PM and New Zealand at 4:05 PM.

Other recent reports include a Google error from a US user at 5:39 AM and another login complaint from the United States at 4:22 AM.

The reports are spread across multiple locations, suggesting that the problems are not limited to a single country.

Google Gemini Down Today: AI Generation Is the Biggest Problem

While the broader Google reports include login and accessibility complaints, Gemini’s largest reported problem is AI generation.

The latest Gemini outage breakdown shows:

  • AI generation: 69%
  • Website: 13%
  • Application: 12%

The distribution suggests that many affected users can reach Gemini but encounter problems when they attempt to submit prompts or receive responses.

That distinction is important where a user may be able to open Google’s website or the Gemini application normally but still receive an error when trying to generate an answer.

Several users say the problem occurs repeatedly rather than with one particular prompt.

Google Down Today: Users Report Repeated Error Messages

Affected Gemini users have described repeated failures when attempting to complete ordinary requests.

One user reported repeatedly receiving the message, “I have encountered an error.”

Another said: “ALL requests state: I encountered an error doing what you asked. Could you try again?”

A separate report described Gemini as returning an error instead of completing requests.

These reports suggest that at least some users are experiencing a service-side problem rather than an issue with a single prompt.

Google Gemini Not Working: Recent Conversations Reportedly Disappearing

Some users are also reporting problems with Gemini conversation history while one user said Gemini was removing a recent conversation automatically without providing a reason.

The issue could be particularly disruptive for users who depend on Gemini for ongoing writing, research and other projects because previous conversations can contain important context and information.

Gemini has experienced conversation history problems before. Google acknowledged an issue in February 2026 in which conversation history was not visible in Gemini’s web and mobile applications. Google said a background process had caused missing conversation metadata.

That earlier incident does not confirm that the current reports have the same technical cause, but it demonstrates that conversation-history failures have occurred previously.

Google Outage Today: Problems Reported Across Multiple Countries

The latest Google reports show complaints coming from several parts of the world.

Recent reports include:

  • United States: Slow, login and error problems
  • Japan: Google inaccessible
  • Belgium: Google inaccessible
  • New Zealand: Google inaccessible
  • Netherlands: Google slow
  • India: Google slow
  • Australia: Google inaccessible
  • France: Google inaccessible
  • United Kingdom: Google slow

A user in India also reported slow Google performance on October 7 at 8:15 PM.

The geographic spread shows that Google-related problems are being reported internationally, although the available user reports do not establish a single global outage affecting every Google service.

Google Workspace Outage Reports: India, UK and Czechia Among Top Locations

Separate Google Workspace outage monitoring also points to recent activity in several regions.

The top reported Google Workspace outage locations over the last 24 hours are listed as:

  1. India
  2. United Kingdom
  3. Czechia

This provides additional context for the broader Google service reports, but Workspace incidents should not automatically be treated as evidence that consumer services such as Google Search, Gmail or YouTube are also down.

Google Outage History: Previous Problems Were Reported in September

Recent outage records show that Google has experienced several periods of reported disruption. On September 7, 2026, multiple Google incidents were detected and later resolved while one reported outage lasted approximately 56 minutes, while another lasted about one hour.

A separate incident detected later that day was listed with a total downtime of approximately two hours.

Another Google problem began on September 7 and was resolved on September 8, with the recorded incident lasting approximately four hours.

These historical incidents show that Google has experienced intermittent service problems recently, although they do not establish a connection with the issues users are reporting on October 8.

Google Outage Today: Is Google Search Down?

The current reports do not establish that Google Search is experiencing a complete worldwide outage.

Some users are reporting that Google is slow or inaccessible, while others are reporting login or error problems while at the same time, Gemini users are reporting failures specifically when generating AI responses.

Because Google operates a large ecosystem that includes Search, Gmail, YouTube, Maps, Chrome, Android, Drive and other services, problems affecting one product do not necessarily mean the entire Google network is down.

Google Outage Today: Has Google Confirmed the Problem?

There is currently no official Google confirmation supplied for the specific Gemini and broader Google problems reported on October 8.

The available information consists primarily of user reports and outage-monitoring data.

Google has previously acknowledged Gemini and Workspace incidents, including problems involving elevated error rates and missing conversation history. However, those historical incidents should not be presented as confirmation of the cause of the latest reports.

Google Down Today: What Could Be Causing the Errors?

There is currently no confirmed technical explanation for the latest Gemini problems.

The fact that 69% of Gemini reports involve AI generation suggests that the main difficulty may be occurring when Gemini attempts to process or generate responses. However, there is not enough information to attribute the problem to a particular Gemini model, Google server, backend system or software update.

The broader Google reports also show different symptoms, including slow performance, login failures and inaccessible services.

Google is separately making changes to Gemini model access beginning October 9, including changes affecting free and AI Plus users. There is currently no evidence connecting those planned changes to the reported outage.

Google Outage Today: When Will Google and Gemini Be Back Up?

There is currently no confirmed restoration time for the reported Google or Gemini problems.

For Gemini users, the most prominent symptoms are repeated AI-generation errors and, in some cases, missing recent conversations.

For Google users more broadly, recent reports include slow performance, login failures, error messages and inaccessible services across several countries.

The available data therefore points to multiple user-reported problems affecting Google and Gemini, rather than providing confirmation of a single, company-wide Google outage.

Users experiencing problems can continue checking Google’s official service information and Gemini updates for confirmation, troubleshooting guidance and information about restoration.
