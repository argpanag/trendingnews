‘MobLand’ Season 3 Begins Filming With Tom Hardy, Pierce Brosnan and Helen Mirren | Popped

TheTools · 23.11.2026 12:28 · source · permalink

Paramount+ has confirmed that production is underway on the third season of its hit crime drama MobLand, with Tom Hardy, Pierce Brosnan and Helen Mirren all returning. Filming began during the week of November 16, just as the show’s second season reached its finale on Friday, November 20.

The latest update confirms that the Harrigan family saga will continue beyond its second season, although Paramount+ has not yet announced when Season 3 will premiere. The series is produced by Paramount Television Studios and 101 Studios, with Guy Ritchie among its executive producers.

Tom Hardy, Pierce Brosnan and Helen Mirren Return for ‘MobLand’ Season 3

Hardy is back as Harry da Souza, the fixer whose loyalty to the Harrigan crime family repeatedly puts him in dangerous situations. Brosnan returns as family patriarch Conrad Harrigan, while Mirren reprises her role as Maeve Harrigan, the formidable matriarch whose influence has been central to the drama.

The announcement follows the conclusion of Season 2, which debuted in September and finished its weekly release schedule on November 20. All previous episodes are now available to stream on Paramount+, meaning audiences can catch up with the story while work on the next instalment continues.

Season 2 also featured Paddy Considine, Joanne Froggatt, Lara Pulver, Jasmine Jobson, Johnny Flynn, Janet McTeer and Toby Jones. The new production announcement confirms the three leads but does not yet establish which other cast members will return for Season 3.

When Will ‘MobLand’ Season 3 Be Released on Paramount+?

Paramount+ has not confirmed a Season 3 release date. The broadcaster announced on November 20 that filming had begun earlier that week, but it did not provide a premiere window, episode count or new trailer. Any precise launch date would therefore be speculative at this stage.

The quick production turnaround will nevertheless be welcome news for viewers who have followed the Harrigans through the first two seasons. Paramount+ had already renewed the drama before Season 2 premiered, allowing the next chapter to move forward without a further renewal decision after the finale.

Who Is Behind ‘MobLand’ Season 3?

The third season is once again being produced in association with Paramount Television Studios and 101 Studios. The executive-producing team includes David C. Glasser, Guy Ritchie, Jez Butterworth, Tom Hardy, Ivan Atkinson, Dean Baker, Ron Burkle, David Hutkin, Bob Yari and Keith Cox. Paramount+ has not yet released further plot details.

The series launched in 2025, introducing Hardy’s fixer and the criminal world surrounding the Harrigan family. Popped previously covered the show’s original Paramount+ release announcement. The full production update is available through Paramount’s official press release.

MobLand Seasons 1 and 2 are streaming on Paramount+. Season 3 is now in production, with a release date yet to be announced.
