"A terrible start, but a very good response"

TheTools · 03.10.2026 23:43 · source · permalink

Reactions to the National Team

After the comeback against Slovenia, Gregor Kobel praises the Swiss team's response

Associated Press

Gregor Kobel calls it a nightmare start, but he and Remo Freuler praise the team's response: Reactions from the Swiss players on their 2-1 victory over Slovenia in Lucerne.

Gregor Kobel

“They tried a lot to play deep and caught us once or twice. In terms of energy, we could tell a little bit that this was our third game in a short period of time. Games like this are part of the deal, and I think we handled it very well in the end and came away with the win. The way we play, you have to put a lot of pressure on the ball; otherwise, the space behind it gets really big. You can’t have a much worse start to a game, but the Slovenians played well, too. It was a nightmare start for us, but we reacted very well by not letting it affect us—especially with so many young players who don’t have a lot of experience yet.”

Johan Manzambi

“Everything’s fine, including my knee. We knew they’d play hard, and we were prepared for that. I know my knee is fine, and I’m not worried about it anymore. Of course, I’m happy to be playing and hope I can continue to play well. We practiced playing against a deep block, and luckily we scored two goals.”

Manzambi: «Wir wussten, dass sie so spielen»

Remo Freuler

“The team’s response was very good; that shows their character. But we also have to be humble today—we didn’t play our best game or reach our full potential. It’s not easy to find solutions; above all, we didn’t do a good enough job of stopping the counterattacks. That’s definitely something we need to improve on Tuesday. It’s a long stretch where you don’t always play to your full potential, but you still have to win.”

Remo Freuler isn't sugarcoating the team's performance against Slovenia, but he isn't calling it bad either
