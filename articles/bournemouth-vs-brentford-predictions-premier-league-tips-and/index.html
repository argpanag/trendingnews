Expect early goals from high-pressing pair

Bournemouth have struggled to hold on to leads in this season’s Premier League. They have gone ahead in all three matches – against Manchester City, Everton and Newcastle – but have conceded late goals each time to lose 2-1 to City and draw the other two. 

They are not the worst results – and Marco Rose at least got a first competitive win under his belt with the 4-0 thrashing of Lincoln City in the EFL Cup on Tuesday – but the way his side have faded late in games is a cause for concern.

It could be mere coincidence but it may also be that the high-intensity style Bournemouth also adopted under Andoni Iraola is too difficult to maintain for 90 minutes.

Indeed, no team has dropped more points from winning positions since the start of last season than Bournemouth (29). But their tendency to excel early in games leads me to my first tip, which is over 1.5 first-half goals at 11/8.

All four of Bournemouth’s league goals in this season have come in the first half, in the 26th, 41st, ninth and 35th minutes. They start on the front foot and Brentford are also fast-starters.

The Bees have scored five goals in the league this term and the average time those have been scored is the 38th minute.

Goals are expected between these two high-pressing outfits with over 2.5 goals just 8/13 and both teams to score priced at 8/15.

But the smart way to get behind a high-scoring match is early goals and punters should expect plenty of drama before the break.

Kluivert can draw fouls from Bees’ midfield

The high-pressing approaches of these two sides mean they tend to throw themselves into challenges and look to regain possession quickly.

That, in turn, can translate to cynical challenges and Brentford have averaged more fouls per game (16.3) than any other side in the Premier League after the first three matches, with Bournemouth not far behind on 13.

Brentford’s midfield double-pivot is especially adept at breaking up play, with Vitaly Janelt averaging 3.7 tackles and 3.3 interceptions per game and new signing Mamadou Sangare averaging 2.3 tackles and two interceptions.

The midfield battle in this contest should therefore be fascinating with Tyler Adams and Alex Scott up against Janelt and Sangare for Bournemouth.

But one player who could be a problem for Brentford’s midfield pairing is Justin Kluivert.

Deployed in the No.10 role, Kluivert’s attacking output has been decent for a player who has yet to score or have an assist. He averages 2.3 shots and one key pass per game.

The Dutchman likes to get on the ball and is drawing an average of 1.7 fouls per game in the Premier League, so I am tipping him to win two or more fouls at 20/23 in this game against a Brentford team who get stuck into challenges through the middle.

Tavernier calls shots in 8.52/1 multiple

I have picked out a bet builder for this match that has combined odds of 8.52/1 with Betfred should all three selections prove successful.

The first is a simple one of over 2.5 goals. Even with Bournemouth changing from Iraola to Rose this summer, they play on the front foot, as evidenced by their 2-2 draw with Newcastle last time out.

Over 2.5 goals has been a winner in three of their four competitive games this season and their league fixtures averaged 2.95 goals per game last season, while for Brentford it was 2.82.

Next, I am selecting Bournemouth winger Marcus Tavernier to have two or more shots on target.

Tavernier has made a lively start to life under Rose, scoring twice in three games. Only Erling Haaland (eight) has managed more shots on target than his five across the opening three gameweeks.

Complete the bet builder with two or more fouls from Brentford’s midfield dynamo Vitaly Janelt.

I have already touched on the fact Brentford press high and make plenty of challenges and Janelt is key to that part of their game.

He has won seven tackles in three league games this season but he has also committed a league-high eight fouls and is the only player in the division to have been shown three yellow cards this season.
