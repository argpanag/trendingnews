Foord shifts to the point of attack

So it’s a reshuffle. Raso to the right wing, Fowler to the left wing, Foord to the middle of the attack.

Our first look at Sam Kerr on the bench, by the way, indicates there’s nothing wrong with her.

Meanwhile, Foord receives the first yellow card of the match for a lunging tackle on a Colombian opponent.

Australia 1-1 Colombia, 47 minutes

A half-time substitution: Sam Kerr is OFF

Yep, that’s right. Hayley Raso comes on to replace her. So who will be playing up top in the second half? Is Joe Montemurro just trying something out here, or is Kerr injured, perhaps? We’ll let you know if we find out for sure. It’s a bit of a surprise that she’s come off so early, although she was probably only going to get around an hour or so tonight.

Anyway. Let’s find out. The second half is under way.

Take a look at the half-time stats

HALF-TIME: Australia 1-1 Colombia

And there’s the whistle for half-time.

It’s been rocks and diamonds from the Matildas: a great goal from Alanna Kennedy, maybe the best of the Joe Montemurro era, which came in the 10th minute. Short passing brilliance, exactly what the coach is after.

But from that point onwards, Linda Caicedo took over, and she could have easily had a hat-trick from what we’ve seen so far. And that undersells the fact that she was pretty dangerous before Kennedy’s goal, too. Her pace and aggressiveness have just been too much for Australia’s defence, and they’ve not looked the same since her runs began to expose them.

Lots of work to do for the Matildas, but there have been a few very encouraging moments, too, which we shouldn’t forget about.

Carpenter shoots!

Better stuff from the Matildas, and a couple of repeat entries, building pressure on Colombia - this time, Ellie Carpenter flashes a ball into the box, and after a couple of passages to play it comes back to her, she cuts inside onto her left, shoots, and it goes out for a corner.

Catley’s delivery can’t find a head but Australia retains possession, recycles the ball and they’ll look to attack again, but Mary Fowler’s pass is cut off at the top of the box, and the visitors are out of jail. For now.

Australia 1-1 Colombia, 42 minutes

Caicedo hits the post. Again

The hectic pace of this match has, perhaps expectedly, dissipated for the moment. It would have been hard for both teams to keep that up for 90 minutes. I was getting tired just watchig it.

Even Linda Caicedo seems to have slowed down a little bit … for now. I’m probably tempting fate by typing that.

But it’s not only pace taht she has. Caicedo seems to have been given a free role for Colombia and is pulling the strings from midfield as well as driving through the heart of the Australian defence when she gets the chance.

And, like, it’s almost hilarious, but again, as I type that, Linda Caicedo hits the post from outside the box.

Somebody stop her!

Australia 1-1 Colombia, 35 minutes

Rough stuff from the Matildas since the opening goal

This performance is deteriorating from an Australian perspective. The Matildas started ever so well but the dominance of Linda Caicedo seems to have rocked their confidence and is affecting their retention of the ball and assuredness in their passing. This is a massive test for this team. Can they solve these problems, caused by a world-class player, on the run? Or do they need to wait for the half-time talk from Joe Montemurro? If they’re going to be any chance at going deep in the World Cup, they need to figure this out themselves and come up with a solution.

Australia 1-1 Colombia, 28 minutes

A golf celebration from Alanna Kennedy?

I’m guessing we’ll get the explanation for this after the game, but here’s one from the Joe Lolley files.

Caicedo is wreaking havoc

Linda Caicedo. That’s it, that’s the post.

She’s done it again. Another blistering run against two Australian defenders as Colombia counter-attacks after a corner by the Matildas. This time, she pulls the pass just a fraction too late and they have the ball in the back of the net but it won’t count due to offside.

But that’s four times now she’s exposed Australia at the back with her pace. As good as Australia’s goal was, Joe Montemurro must be concerned about what he’s seeing.

Australia 1-1 Colombia, 21 minutes

BOOM. Caicedo equalises for Colombia

Well, we didn’t have to wait long for another.

Linda Caicedo, my goodness. She went for another scintillating run through the middle, on the shoulder of Wini Heatley, who didn’t have the legs to keep up with her. Ellie Carpenter came to the rescue - she definitely has the legs - but it was too late. By that stage Caicedo was in the box, and from there it was a simple finish past Mackenzie Arnold.

As brilliant as that was by Caicedo, that also exposed some serious frailties in the Australian defence.

Australia 1-1 Colombia, 16 minutes
