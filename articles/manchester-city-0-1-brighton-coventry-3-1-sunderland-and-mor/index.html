Coventry climbed out of the relegation zone with a 3-1 win that leaves Sunderland rooted to the bottom of the Premier League.

Tatsuhiro Sakamoto’s first Premier League goal put Frank Lampard’s side ahead 17 minutes in, Taiwo Awoniyi’s first league goal for Coventry doubled the lead eight minutes before the break and Jack Rudoni sealed victory early in the second half.

Full report from the Sky Blues third win of the season:

Hull maintained their unbeaten home record but saw their winless run continue after Julio Enciso earned Ipswich a 2-2 draw at the MKM Stadium.

Full report from the MKM Stadium:

The Premier League action continues, Niall McVeigh is going minute-by-minute for Tottenham v Palace:

Accidents will happen. They also have consequences. Leeds were playing well, certainly better than Bournemouth for the first 30 minutes until James Trafford committed a grievous error. The goalkeeper made a horrible mess of a pass to an unwitting Ao Tanaka. In stole Justin Kluivert to set up Evanilson for a fifth goal in six matches. Where Leeds’ confidence sagged, Evanilson continued to find the best form of his time on the south coast.

The Brazilian was full of tricks, flicks and intelligent movement until he was subbed off. Thursday’s Prague assignment in the Europa League was being kept in mind. The continued rise of Bournemouth, celebrating a sixth successive win, is testament to changing times, a model club playing thoroughly modern football.

Read John Brewin’s report from the Vitality:

Nice moment for Lewis Dunk there on Sky Sports. The Brighton captain missed the penalty in midweek that saw City go through, now he gets a clean sheet to earn three points. He’s full of praise for bos Fabian Hurzeler, who Dunk says will go on to manage at the very top.

Poor Nottingham Forest. And, especially, poor Nikola Milenkovic. It would be unfair to say that Brentford fluked a win in the 100th Premier League game at the Getch Community Stadium but Forest had 15 shots to their six and have every right to wonder why luck has deserted them so absolutely this season. Only one of Brentford’s goals went down as an own-goal, but both went in after hefty deflections off Milenkovic. A sixth successive defeat for Forest in games played on Hallowe’en must have them wondering about curses and an inexplicable run of misfortune.

Jonathan Wilson was at the Gtech:

Manchester City have lost two consecutive league games but no alarm should be sounded. Enzo Maresca’s fast-passing unit that were defeated on Saturday because Yasin Ayari took one of Brighton’s rare chances, the 11th-minute winner deriving from what appeared a wrongly awarded free-kick for Elliot Anderson’s “foul” on the same player.

Fabian Hürzeler’s team can be proud of victory here – and of being third. They are worth the lofty position and who would bet against Champions League qualification for them?

Revenge for Brighton! Knocked out of the Carabao Cup at the Etihad in midweek, the Seagulls take all three points.

Full-time: Brentford 2-0 Nottingham Forest

Glasner under serious pressure after this one. Forest play City next.

League One table-toppers Salford have made it 2-1 against Exeter, Will Aimson with the late second for the home side. Remarkably, nine-man Rotherham have scored at Swindon but still trail 2-1.

Ooooo, it’s 2-2 at the Toughsheet Community Stadium where Sam Bell has levelled. Millwall are battering Derby, Jake Cooper has made it 4-0.

Will Lankshear has got his second of the afternoon for Boro, who now lead Charlton 3-0.

In League Two, Rotherham are down to nine and trail 1-0 at Swindon. Dominic Iorfa the second Miller to see red. Oldham are also down to nine men, they trail 1-0 at home against Crawley.

Leicester have been pegged back again by Stevenage, Tugra Turhan making it 2-2 at the King Power. At Stadium MK, Aaron Collins has four goals for the Dons who are 5-1 up against Doncaster.

Goal! Hull 2-2 Ipswich (Enciso 74)

That is classic Enciso, this boy loves a hit and this one from the edge of the box is arrowed into the corner and still rising as it hits the net. It was dogged work from the Ipswich man to keep it alive in the buildup.

Goal! Brentford 2-0 Nottingham Forest (Igor Thiago 76)

Brentford sucker punch Forest as Igor Thiago makes it 2-0 to end a swift counter. Stroke of fortune with the finish as it nicks off the defender to loop over Sels.

More goals in the Championship, West Brom now lead 3-1 at West Ham, Callum Styles has got his side’s third. Southampton lead QPR 3-1, the visitors pulling one back after Saints got their third.

Goal! Hull 2-1 Ipswich (McBurnie 68)

The Tigers trailed at the break but now lead, Ollie McBurnie firing in low a free-kick from the edge of the area.

Kamari Doyle has put Leicester back in front against Stevenage in League One and Salford have equalised at home against Exeter.

In the Championship, Swansea are now 4-0 up against Blackburn, Wolves are beating Cardiff 3-0 and Bolton have turned it around against Bristol City to lead 2-1.

Goal! Coventry 3-1 Sunderland (Le Fée 67, pen)

No mistake from Le Fée as he cuts the lead from the spot.

Penalty to Sunderland!

After a length VAR check Sunderland are awarded a penalty for handball, there was a moment of slapstick as the touchline screen froze while they were checking for offisde in the buildup.

Goal! Hull 1-1 Ipswich (Iroegbunam 58)

After missing a number of chances in the first half Ipswich have allowed Hull back into this one. it’s bizarre, Iroegbunam is allowed to keep going and going, unchallenged and eventually beats Walton with a low strike.

Brentford 1-0 Nottingham Forest

I mentioned Forest’s odd form pre-match. The Bees still lead this one, but neither side has had a shot on target.

Having had a second goal disallowed before half-time Southampton now have their 2-0 lead against QPR, Cameron Bragg has got it.

A fitba update from Simon McMahon (thanks, Simon): “Only two Scottish Premiership games today because of the League Cup semis this weekend. Kilmarnock currently lead 10-man St. Johnstone 2-1, while Motherwell v St Mirren is goalless. The first of those League Cup semi finals is a team time kick-off and features Rangers v crisis club one Aberdeen, whose fans have never really taken to manager Stephen Robinson. An Edinburgh derby tomorrow between Hearts and crisis club two Hibs will decide the other finalist.”

Goal! Coventry 3-0 Sunderland (Rudoni 52)

A huge slice of fortune in this but the Sky Blues are pulling away. It’s lovely buildup from Coventry to work it into the Sunderland half but Rudoni’s shot has not right to go in from there, it’s low and not particularly vicious until it takes a big deflection off of Omar Alderete to beat Roefs.

Great game at the London Stadium, it’s now 2-1 to West Brom, Jimmy-Jay Morgan has put the Baggies back in front.

Manchester City 0-1 Brighton

Big chance for Brighton for to go 2-0 up at the Etihad but De Cuyper has blazed over when found in acres of space in the City box. Will they pay for that?

Early goal in League One, Liam Kelly has put into his own net to give Luton a 2-0 lead at Peterborough.
