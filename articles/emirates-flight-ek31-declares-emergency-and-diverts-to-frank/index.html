Emirates flight EK31 declares emergency and diverts to Frankfurt | Cedar News

TheTools · 01.10.2026 14:09 · source · permalink

Emirates flight EK31, traveling from Dubai to London Heathrow, declared an emergency and diverted to Frankfurt, Germany.

The aircraft changed course while en route to London, with Frankfurt selected as the diversion airport.

The reason for the emergency was not immediately known, and no information was available about possible injuries or a technical problem.

Further details are expected from Emirates and aviation authorities.
