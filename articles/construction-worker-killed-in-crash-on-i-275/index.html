Construction worker killed in crash on I-275

TheTools · 18.09.2026 09:21 · source · permalink

A construction worker has died after being hit on Interstate 275 eastbound early Friday morning, according to Springdale police.

The worker, identified by Hamilton County deputies as 27-year-old Joshua Christopher Holland, was employed by a contractor and not the Ohio Department of Transportation.

The incident occurred in the dark and rain as Holland was stacking barrels in the construction zone.

“What happened, was it an auto accident?” a dispatcher asked during a 911 call.

“No – he was stacking barrels across there and he got hit,” the caller responded.

“Oh, a person was struck?” the dispatcher asked.

“Yeah,” the caller said.

“CPR in progress,” an officer said over the scanner as first responders worked to save Holland.

Holland was transported to Bethesda North Hospital, where he died from his injuries.

Investigators said a 2015 Ford Fusion approached the construction zone in the darkness, and Holland entered a single lane of open traffic when he was struck.

The crash led to the closure of I-275 eastbound, near the I-75 interchange, for several hours but reopened after sunrise.

Authorities said speed and impairment are not considered factors in the crash. The driver, who has not been named, is cooperating with investigators and has not been charged.

According to a social media post from Springdale police, the nighttime roadwork was being conducted to heat-straighten beams on a bridge in the area.
