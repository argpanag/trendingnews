KUALA LUMPUR (Nov 27): Here is a brief recap of some business news and corporate announcements that made the headlines on Friday:

AirAsia Group Bhd (KL:AAGB) has confirmed that it will temporarily delay salary payments for its Malaysian employees to the end of each month. It described the move as an "operational adjustment" to align internal payroll processes as it conserves cash. Under an email to Malaysia-based staff, salaries between November and March will be paid on the 30th of each month, or on the last day of February. Group chief executive officer Bo Lingam said wages will still be paid in full. The change comes as AirAsia Philippines cuts jobs. It also follows the Finance Ministry's statement that it would seek priority over other lenders if it decided to extend support to the airline. — AirAsia confirms delaying staff salary payments to end of month

Bintulu Port Holdings Bhd (KL:BIPORT) expects to incur a paper loss of RM147.91 million in the fourth quarter of 2026. The loss comes from its investments in two unit trusts that wrote off sukuk issued by Zetrix AI Bhd (KL:ZETRIX). The group had invested a combined RM403.59 million in the AmanahRaya Syariah Trust Fund and the AmanahRaya Syariah Income Fund, which are both managed by AmanahRaya Investment Management Sdn Bhd. The investments had a carrying value of RM354.87 million as of Thursday. Bintulu Port said it will pursue all available avenues to recover any losses, including taking action against the fund manager. — Bintulu Port flags RM147.9m paper loss from Zetrix AI-linked unit trusts

Separately, Bintulu Port's net profit for the third quarter ended Sept 30, 2026 (3QFY2026) fell 50.8% year-on-year (y-o-y) to RM15.87 million, as higher maintenance, fuel and manpower costs outweighed stronger cargo handling activities. Quarterly revenue rose 7.6% y-o-y to RM219.88 million. It declared a third interim dividend of four sen per share, payable on Dec 22. For the nine months ended Sept 30, 2026 (9MFY2026), net profit declined 20.2% to RM76.03 million, while revenue grew 8.8% to RM653.92 million. — Bintulu Port's 3Q net profit halves as rising costs outweigh stronger cargo revenue

RHB Bank Bhd's (KL:RHBBANK) net profit for 3QFY2026 fell 11.5% y-o-y to RM800.4 million, weighed down by higher credit charges and an unrealised loss on the revaluation of financial assets amid rising bond yields. Quarterly revenue slipped 2.1% to RM4.43 billion. It declared no dividend for the quarter. For 9MFY2026, net profit was flat at RM2.46 billion, while revenue fell 2.7% to RM13.05 billion. — RHB Bank logs lower 3Q profit as higher credit charges, rising bond yields weigh on income

Axiata Group Bhd (KL:AXIATA) returned to the black in 3QFY2026 with a net profit of RM35.72 million, against a net loss of RM27.35 million a year earlier. The turnaround came from higher revenue, improved associate earnings and lower financing costs, despite a larger RM366 million goodwill impairment on Link Net. Quarterly revenue rose 6.6% y-o-y to RM3.11 billion. No dividend was declared for the quarter. For 9MFY2026, net profit declined 12.7% to RM352.04 million, while revenue was flat at RM8.78 billion. — Axiata returns to black in 3Q as higher revenue more than offsets larger impairment on Link Net

PETRONAS Dagangan Bhd's (KL:PETDAG) net profit for 3QFY2026 fell 22.3% y-o-y to RM218.52 million, as higher fuel procurement costs during a scheduled maintenance shutdown at the Melaka refinery weighed on margins. Quarterly revenue surged 62.8% to RM15.52 billion on higher average selling prices. It declared an interim dividend of 25 sen per share, payable on Dec 24. For 9MFY2026, net profit rose 5.8% to RM888.73 million, while revenue jumped 54.5% to RM42.76 billion. — PETRONAS Dagangan 3Q net profit falls 22% on higher import costs, declares 25 sen dividend

IHH Healthcare Bhd's (KL:IHH) net profit for 3QFY2026 rose 10% y-o-y to RM679 million, underpinned by higher inpatient and daycase volumes and more complex cases. Quarterly revenue increased 8% to RM7.1 billion. No dividend was declared. For 9MFY2026, net profit increased 13% to RM1.8 billion, while revenue rose 8% to RM20.7 billion. — IHH Healthcare 3Q net profit rises 10% on higher patient volumes

MISC Bhd's (KL:MISC) net profit for 3QFY2026 jumped 77.5% y-o-y to RM961.8 million, driven mainly by higher freight rates and more earning days in its petroleum shipping business. Quarterly revenue climbed 40.2% to RM3.92 billion. It declared a third interim dividend of eight sen per share. For 9MFY2026, net profit rose 66.9% to RM2.86 billion, while revenue increased 39.3% to RM11.61 billion. — MISC 3Q profit jumps on freight rates, floating unit progress; declares eight sen dividend

TIME dotCom Bhd's (KL:TIMECOM) net profit for 3QFY2026 rose 8.3% y-o-y to RM114.96 million. The increase came from stronger sales, the absence of an impairment and a lower foreign exchange loss. Quarterly revenue rose 10% to RM499.28 million. No dividend was announced. For 9MFY2026, net profit rose almost 13.9% to RM368.8 million, while revenue rose almost 7.8% to RM1.43 billion. — TIME dotCom sees stronger 3Q on higher sales, absence of impairment and lower forex loss

Bank Islam Malaysia Bhd's (KL:BIMB) net profit for 3QFY2026 nearly halved y-o-y to RM67.35 million from RM129.23 million. Higher overheads and provisions, along with losses from investment and trading, drove the decline. Fund-based income rose 15%, while non-fund-based income fell 51%. It cut its interim dividend to 8.24 sen per share from 10 sen a year earlier, payable on Jan 8. For 9MFY2026, net profit declined almost 16% to RM321.5 million. — Bank Islam says 3Q earnings nearly halved as income drops, provision rises

OSK Holdings Bhd's (KL:OSK) net profit for 3QFY2026 rose less than 3% y-o-y to RM156.61 million, as strong operating profit barely offset higher impairment and tax expense. Quarterly revenue rose 37% to RM678.91 million. No dividend was declared. For 9MFY2026, net profit increased 4.7% to RM438.06 million, while revenue rose 19% to RM1.68 billion. — OSK Holdings 3Q earnings barely higher amid higher impairment, tax expense

Farm Fresh Bhd's (KL:FFB) net profit for the second quarter ended Sept 30, 2026 (2QFY2027) fell 21% y-o-y to RM28.92 million. Elevated fuel and packaging costs, together with higher operating expenses, weighed on earnings. Quarterly revenue rose 12% to RM330.59 million. No dividend was recommended. For the first half ended Sept 30, 2026 (1HFY2027), net profit fell nearly 20% to RM55.75 million, while revenue grew 14.7% to RM636.96 million. — Farm Fresh 2Q net profit declines as higher costs squeeze margins

Malakoff Corp Bhd's (KL:MALAKOF) net profit for 3QFY2026 fell 1.61% y-o-y to RM27.76 million. The decline was mainly due to lower contribution from Tanjung Bin Power Sdn Bhd following a steam turbine generator rotor failure. Quarterly revenue climbed 33.82% to RM2.26 billion. No dividend was declared. For 9MFY2026, net profit declined 36.35% to RM79.58 million, while revenue fell 3.35% to RM5.55 billion. The group expects earnings to improve in the fourth quarter after the affected unit returned to full operations. — Malakoff 3Q profit falls slightly, eyes recovery after coal-fired generation unit restored

Spritzer Bhd's (KL:SPRITZER) net profit for 3QFY2026 surged 50.2% y-o-y to a record RM35.46 million, as it sold more bottled water at higher prices. Quarterly revenue grew 18.5% to RM201.42 million. No dividend was announced. For 9MFY2026, net profit increased 41.1% to RM93.14 million, while revenue grew 16.5% to RM562.38 million. — Spritzer's quarterly profit hits new high, driven by stronger bottled water sales at higher prices
