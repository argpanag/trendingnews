Derby day: league or death

TheTools · 20.09.2026 14:37 · source · permalink

With Raphinha and Lamine Yamal firing on all cylinders, Barcelona currently look a cut above the rest in both style and substance. The blistering form of the Cules ensures this Sunday's derby carries decisive weight in the title race.

Despite being only matchday seven in the Primera Division, Atletico de Madrid and Real Madrid step out onto the Metropolitano turf trailing Hansi Flick's runaway leaders by eight and six points, respectively. A false move now would plunge their domestic aspirations into serious jeopardy far too early in the campaign.

The outlook is particularly precarious for the Rojiblancos, who could find themselves three games adrift of the summit (sitting seven or eight points back) should they fail to win. Yet Real Madrid cannot afford to blink either. Anything short of victory would consign them to an uncomfortable international break facing the grim reality of trailing Barca by five or six points—a concerning gulf bound to trigger searching questions over this Jose Mourinho 2.0 project.
