ALM MW3 Preview: Brisbane Roar vs Auckland FC

TheTools · 29.10.2026 02:49 · source · permalink

Home News

Brisbane Roar Media

29 October, 2026

Brisbane Roar return to Suncorp Stadium on Friday night to host the reigning A-League champions, Auckland FC, looking to make it two wins from two to start the 26/27 A-League Men season.

Michael Valkanis’ side opened their campaign with a 2-1 win over Perth Glory last Friday. Justin Vidic struck before half-time and Nam Tae-hee doubled the lead with a volley from outside the box. Nine players made their A-League Men debuts on the night, including 18-year-old goalkeeper George Plusnin.

Auckland arrive in Brisbane after a mixed start to their title defence. Under new head coach Pedro Moreira, they opened with a 3-1 home win over Melbourne City, with new signing Lachlan Bayliss scoring twice. Last week they were beaten 5-2 by Western Sydney Wanderers in Parramatta. Auckland will also head to Brisbane without striker Sam Cosgrove who has been suspended for Friday’s match.

Auckland are captained by Japanese international Hiroki Sakai, who will come up against Roar midfielder and South Korean international Nam Tae-hee.

Friday night is also Halloween Eve at Suncorp Stadium, and fans are encouraged to come in costume for their chance to win!

Head-to-Head

The Roar are still chasing their first win over Auckland FC. The sides have met four times since Auckland entered the competition in 2024, with Auckland winning three and one match drawn. The most recent meeting was a 2-0 win for Auckland at Suncorp Stadium in January.
