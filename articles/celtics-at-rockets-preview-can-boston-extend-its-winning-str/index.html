Celtics at Rockets preview: Can Boston extend its winning streak to six? - The Boston Globe

TheTools · 03.11.2026 00:55 · source · permalink

Can the Celtics keep their luck rolling?

The Celtics overcame a poor shooting performance from behind the arc to claim a 108-97 road win over the Magic Sunday. Boston missed 32 of 44 3-pointers, yet remained committed to a stingy defensive approach.

“Our defensive intensity didn’t wane, and our poise to continue to get great shots didn’t wane,” Celtics coach Joe Mazzulla said. “We have to be able to do that on both ends. We have to be able to trust the next possession and play through and continue to generate great shots, which we did. Our defense, even when we gave up some offensive rebounds, we stayed connected.

“That’s the mindset of the team.”

In the second night of a back-to-back on the road, the Celtics will need to rely on that same mindset to best the Rockets.

Paul George has been key to that defensive identity. He posted 17 points, six rebounds, eight assists, two steals, and two blocks against the Magic.

The 36-year-old George has long been renowned for his ability to contribute on both ends of the court, a skill set that made him an attractive target for the Celtics.

“We continue to get better,” said Jayson Tatum, who scored 32 points and added seven assists. “His awareness on the defensive end and his activity with his hands is next level. He just always has awareness for the ball, deflections, and he makes it easy for the rest of us, even this late in his career.”
