iPhone Duo vs iPhone 18 Pro: Here’s every key difference - 9to5Mac

TheTools · 16.10.2026 19:55 · source · permalink
  • iPhone
  • iPhone 18 Pro
  • iPhone 18 Pro Max
  • iPhone Duo

Apple has strong pitches for all of its flagship iPhones this fall, but which model is right for you? Here’s an overview of the key differences between iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 Pro.

Form factor and dimensions: Foldable vs traditional

It’s the most obvious difference, but should be stated regardless. With iPhone Duo you get a very different form factor: it’s a foldable device. With iPhone 18 Pro, you get a traditional iPhone experience.

If you’ve never used a foldable, it’s hard to explain the difference in how each model might feel in practice. But generally speaking, with iPhone Duo you get essentially an iPhone and iPad in one device. The displays are unique shapes and sizes compared to a traditional iPhone though, which may take some adjustment.

Here is how each model’s dimensions compare:

iPhone Duo might seem more sleek than iPhone 18 Pro Max, but it ultimately weighs slightly more.

Displays: Dual vs single in varying sizes

iPhone Duo has two displays: a 5.4-inch outer display and a 7.6-inch folding display.

With iPhone 18 Pro, you can choose from the standard 6.3-inch size or 6.9-inch Pro Max model.

So with the Duo, you can enjoy a “small iPhone” experience while also having the option of a larger inner screen than what iPhone 18 Pro Max offers.

iPhone Duo’s folding display also comes with a nano-texture finish, a first in an iPhone.

Cameras: Clear advantage for iPhone 18 Pro

Cameras are one of the most important iPhone features. And in this department, iPhone 18 Pro is the clear winner.

iPhone 18 Pro has the new 48MP Main camera with variable aperture. iPhone Duo lacks the variable aperture feature, so its Main camera lags behind the Pro line.

With iPhone 18 Pro, you also get the Telephoto camera that’s missing from iPhone Duo.

In terms of software features, each model has its own unique exclusives:

  • iPhone Duo: Smart Take, Kid Cue, Duo Preview
  • iPhone 18 Pro: Smart focus tracking, Pro controls, Apple Reference Image

iPhone 18 Pro also exclusively supports ProRes video recording up to 4K at 60 fps with external recording, a “studio-quality four-mic array,” Spatial video recording at 1080p at 30 fps, Spatial photos, ProRes RAW, Genlock, and more.

Battery: Excellent battery life on all models

Because iPhone Duo has two displays, its quoted battery life varies depending on which display you’re using. But overall, Apple is promising strong battery life on both the Duo as well as iPhone 18 Pro.

Here are Apple’s quoted numbers:

  • iPhone Duo outer display: Up to 44 hours
  • iPhone Duo folding display: Up to 31 hours
  • iPhone 18 Pro: Up to 36 hours
  • iPhone 18 Pro Max: Up to 45 hours

Everyone’s use patterns will differ, but if you split the difference between the two iPhone Duo battery estimates, you’re looking at around 38 hours in average video playback.

All the other big and small differences: Face ID, buttons, software, Apple Pencil, more

Authentication differs between iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 Pro, with the former using Touch ID and the latter Face ID.

iPhone Duo does not have an Action button, but does offer Camera Control.

iPhone Duo comes with several multitasking advantages in iOS 27, like the ability to use apps in Split View and have unique landscape views. It also has other fun software additions like a partially-folded StandBy mode.

Apple Pencil (USB-C model) will be supported on iPhone Duo later this year, another advantage over iPhone 18 Pro.

A new C2 modem is included in both iPhone Duo and iPhone 18 Pro, but iPhone 18 Pro Max uses a Qualcomm modem.

Colors for iPhone 18 Pro are Burgundy, Glacier, Black, and Silver. iPhone Duo only offers two colors: Star White and Night Sky.

Finally, design materials differ: iPhone 18 Pro has an aluminum unibody design, while iPhone Duo uses titanium.

Pricing and availability: Big gaps in both

Pricing is another major difference between Apple’s new models. Here are starting prices for 256GB models:

  • iPhone Duo: $1,999
  • iPhone 18 Pro: $1,199
  • iPhone 18 Pro Max: $1,299

iPhone 18 Pro has been available for nearly a month, while iPhone Duo pre-orders just went live today. However, if you haven’t ordered one yet, shipping estimates are already delayed into 2027. So if you need a new iPhone sooner, iPhone 18 Pro is the way to go.
