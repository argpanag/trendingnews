Man City 2-4- Paris Saint-Germain: What Enzo Maresca said

TheTools · 14.10.2026 23:50 · source · permalink
  • Published
    2 hours ago

Enzo Maresca spoke to TNT Sports after Manchester City's defeat against Paris Saint-Germain: "I think we start quite good until the first goal and then we concede the second one after one minute. That created some bad feelings. Second half, we were much better and probably deserved something more.

"It was a bit strange, to be honest. Until the first goal, we started quite good, we were in the game, then we conceded the first and then the second one. We didn't lose half-time, we adjusted something. We created enough to deserve something more.

"We adjusted a bit when we were pressing. I think we lost a bit of the balance.

"Even with 3-0 in the first half, with the amount of chances we created in the second half, we deserved more. Luis said at the end of the game that they were quite lucky today.

"You have to be clinical but not lose the balance.

On his message to the fans: "We are sorry for the first half for sure. Saturday is a Premier League game and we need to continue our run."

Did you know?

  • Manchester City conceded four or more goals in a home game in any European competition for the very first time.

  • City have lost four of their last seven UEFA Champions League home games – more than their previous 50 beforehand (three).
