Steph Curry, Brandin Podziemski not expected to play in Warriors vs. Suns game on Saturday

TheTools · 25.10.2026 07:30 · source · permalink

The Golden State Warriors have started their season 0-2, now heading to Phoenix with Steph Curry and Brandin Podziemski not expected to play.

The Warriors are 0-2 and are not expected to have both starting guards available when they play the second night of a back-to-back Saturday in Phoenix.

After Friday's 111-108 home-opener loss to the Memphis Grizzlies, Brandin Podziemski said he will not play, and Steph Curry said he probably will not.

Podziemski missed the season opener with a back injury and returned Friday to score 18 points. He said the issue was minor in his postgame press conference.

"Just tweaked it a little bit, nothing too serious," Podziemski said.

Asked how hard he pushed to play in the Warriors' season opener Wednesday after playing all 82 games last season, Podziemski explained the medical staff kept him from rushing back.

"Wasn't really on top of my mind. I think staff, medical, did a good job of just like not rushing me, saying whenever you're ready to play you can go out there and play, so I felt today was the day I was ready," Podziemski addded.

Asked if he could play Saturday, Podziemski did not leave much room.

Asked whether it is about his body or his energy level, Curry smiled.

"I think you know the answer to that," he conceded.

Curry did not say why, but the 38-year-old has played 21 minutes Wednesday, when he sat out the fourth quarter of a blowout loss to the Lakers, and 30 minutes Friday. He has scored 47 points across the two games: 23 against the Lakers and 24 against Memphis.

The Warriors blew a 12-point lead in the final 3:47 Friday and will play Saturday without Jimmy Butler (ACL rehab), Moses Moody (torn patellar tendon rehab) and Kristaps Porziņģis, who has been away from the team. They are now likely to be without their background in Curry and Podziemski as well.
