Brazil thrash Singapore as Vinicius Júnior steals the show

Brazil beat Singapore 7–0 on Tuesday (17) in their final match of the 2026 season. It was Brazil's biggest win since their 8–0 victory over China on September 10, 2012, at the Arruda stadium in Recife. The first-ever meeting between the two teams featured goals from Vini Jr. (3), Endrick, Estevão, Rayan and Allan. Brazil's first fixture of 2027 will come during the March FIFA international window, with preparations already focused on the 2028 Copa América and the start of qualifying for the 2030 World Cup.

TRIBUTE TO DINO SANI

OneFootball Videos

Before kick-off, a tribute was paid to former midfielder and 1958 World Cup winner Dino Sani, who passed away on Sunday (15) at the age of 94. Dino made 24 appearances for Brazil, recording 16 wins, four draws and four defeats. He scored four goals and also helped win the Oswaldo Cruz Cup (1958), the Bernardo O'Higgins Cup (1959) and the Roca Cup (1960). In addition to observing a minute's silence, the players wore black armbands.

Dino Sani was part of the first Brazil team to win the World Cup, in 1958Credit: Rafael RIbeiro/ CBF

THE MATCH

Brazil dominated the first half. Pressing hard and moving the ball forward quickly, they built a comfortable lead before the break. The opening goal came in the eighth minute. Vanderson found Bruno Guimarães inside the box, and he chested the ball down for Estevão to hit first time, but his effort struck the post. In the next move, Vini Jr. opened the scoring for Brazil, finishing off a lovely pass from Bruno Guimarães inside the box.

The second came from a quick move that began deep in Brazil's own half. Vini Jr. carried the ball forward at pace and played a through ball to Endrick, who burst into the penalty area and squared it back for the Real Madrid star to finish into an empty net.

Estevão and Endrick also scored in Brazil's 7–0 routCredit: Rafael Ribeiro/ CBF

Brazil kept up the pressure despite their lead. With intense pressing and firm control of the game, the third goal soon followed. In the 20th minute, Vinicius Jr. finished off a pass from Estevão inside the box. By the 30th minute, it had become a rout. Endrick went on a solo run and tried his luck from outside the box, finding the back of the net: Brazil 4–0.

Looking to build on the players' understanding, Brazil returned for the second half with the same team that had started the match. The tempo remained unchanged, too, and Estevão scored the fifth, capitalising on a mistake by the opposing defence.

With a commanding lead and victory all but secured, the coaching staff brought on Allan, Vitor Reis, Marlon Gomes and Gabriel Bontempo in the 60th minute, replacing Vini Jr., Gabriel Magalhães, Bruno Guimarães and João Gomes, respectively. Allan then helped create Brazil's sixth goal. The former Palmeiras player delivered a lovely diagonal pass to Rayan, who entered the box, rounded Singapore's goalkeeper and slotted into an empty net.

In less than ten minutes on the pitch, Allan had provided an assist and scored a goal. He netted Brazil's seventh of the match.
