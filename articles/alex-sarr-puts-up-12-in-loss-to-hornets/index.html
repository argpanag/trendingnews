Alex Sarr puts up 12 in loss to Hornets

TheTools · 27.10.2026 14:30 · source · permalink

by Garrett Ball | Wizards Correspondent | Tue, Oct 27th 2026, 10:30am EDT

Alex Sarr scored 12 points (4-6 FG, 0-2 3P, 4-6 FT) with six rebounds, an assist, and two blocks in the Wizards’ 110-103 loss to the Hornets.

Fantasy Impact:

It was Sarr’s best scoring night of the young season, and the second game in a row where he had multiple blocks. It’s been a slow start for the young center, who is having to find his way with Anthony Davis now in town. Hopefully we will see more from him as he figures things out.
