Fernandes flips over the wall and Zirkzee, right of centre and facing the touchline, backheels. The ball then ricochets off McCarthy, Dashdamirov and Pokatilov, before Martinez slams into the net from close range. the keeper and defenders won’t mind never, ever seeing that again.

58 min Mount swishes a cunning pass through the lines for Mbeumo, who finds Mazraoui and takes a man away with a run outside him. So the full-back cuts inside, tries a curler for the far top corner … and gets naewhere near it

Change for Sabah: Mbina for Nwachukwu.

I’m returning to the same issues: United haven’t yet worked out how to control a game while playing at pace, sustaining attacks and boxing opponents while being hard to get at – something their 98-99 and 06-08 iteration did brilliantly. But structurally, they’re improving, and once Baleba is fit, they should get better at recovering high and defending channels.

HALF-TIME: Manchester United 3-0 Sabah

It took United a while to get going, and Sabah have done a good job, but the gap in class is as chasmic as expected.

GOAL! Manchester United 3-0 Sabah (Sesko 45)

Mainoo into Bruno and over to Mbeumo, who plays into the space for Sesko, lanking between the centre-backs. He opts to go around the keeper – a finish we see too rarely these days and doesn’t go down when touched though, if he does, it’s a red card. But he stays on his feet, slides into the empty net, and this, as a contest, is did.

44 min United are starting to impose their class now, and excuse me while I interrupt myself.

GOAL! Manchester United 2-0 Sabah (Fernandes 43)

Tielemans slips out to Dorgu who goes wider to Cunha, who punches a diagonal ball into the box, where Tielemans collects, spins away from goal toward the touchline and lays back to Bruno, who passes into the net. That is very nice.

42 min …and again, the corner is to the back post and poorly executed, Cunha and Martinez jumping into each other’s way.

38 min United look pretty well-organised tonight – their shape is good and having Sesko makes that better – but their execution hasn’t been good enough.

36 min And here Sabah come again, Parris feinting to go outside Dorgu, deliberately leaving the ball behind, then going back to it and picking out Mickels at the back stick! this is a chance, but he can only bungle it wide – though, in fairness, there wasn’t much space between Lammens and the post.

33 min Tielemans drills a luscious ball out to Bruno, who kills it and goes backwards, United again prioritising keeping the ball. There are more goals in United, but they should be concerned at their inability to pen Sabah in.

GOAL! Manchester United 1-0 Sabah (Cunha 28)

Parris gives it away, passing infield to Mbeumo, and the ball moves to Sesko then Bruno, who feeds Dorgu. The low cross to the near post is good enough and Mbeumo, played onside by McCarthy, leaps to punch a close-range finish into the ground and in, off the keeper’s shoulder. That’s his first Champions League goal.

27 min “I just noticed that Sabah have a centre back called Aden (Aidan) McCarthym” writes John Brennan. “Taking over from Thomas Delaney as the most Irish sounding non-Irishman around.”

24 min Again, the corner comes to nothing. Without Casemiro, so far this season, they’re offering no threat form set-pieces.

23 min What United have lacked so far is players beating their men. When Tielemans did it earlier, it opened things up, but they need more of it from their attackers. Meantime, though, Mainoo sweeps a lovely lofted pass forward – the kind of thing he has to do more often – picking out Bruno, unmarked on the right of the box. He’s got Sesko in the middle, but his square-ball is careless and Sabah get it behind.
