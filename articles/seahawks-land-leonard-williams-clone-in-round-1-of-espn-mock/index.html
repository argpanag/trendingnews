Seahawks land Leonard Williams clone in Round 1 of ESPN mock draft

TheTools · 19.11.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

The Seattle Seahawks are going to need some defensive help sooner rather than later. The issue isn't talent, but the overall age of the group. This is why ESPN's having Seattle take Alabama's Devan Thompkins in the first round of the site's latest 2027 NFL mock draft makes so much sense.

Making 12s even happier, should that selection actually be the one the Seahawks do, is that Thompkins is a big defensive end and is compared to Leonard Williams. The latter player is arguably the best defensive lineman in the NFL and has been brilliant in his time in the Pacific Northwest.

Williams and Thompkins would play the same kind of spot in head coach Mike Macdonald's system. Both are traditional defensive linemen who play end quite a bit in a 3-4 base. Thompkins should be able to play defensive tackle too, just as Williams does, and his versatility would be something that general manager John Schneider loves.

Devan Thompkins could be just what the Seattle Seahawks need for many reasons

So far in 2026, the Alabama player has five sacks, his highest number at the college level, as well as 6.5 tackles for loss, the same number as in 2025. He is 6'5" and 300 pounds, while Williams is 6'5" and 310 pounds. 12s would be right to be confused about which player is which if they happen to line up next to each other in 2027.

Still, Seattle does need high-end players like Thompkins, not just because of his skill level. Since Macdonald came to the team after the 2023 season, the Seahawks have had an excellent defense, but many of the key players are aging.

Williams signed an extension before the season, so he will play for Seattle through 2029, but only if the defensive lineman is healthy enough to stay available for games. He has done so for much of his career, but this season he has missed a couple of games. Aging players don't tend to get healthier, and Williams is now 32 years old.

He's not the only player over 30 on Seattle's defense, though. Edge rusher DeMarcus Lawrence has already said he plays for this season to be his last, and he is 34. Fellow edge rushers Dante Fowler Jr. and Uchenna Nwosu are 32 and 30, respectively. Defensive line stalwart Jarran Reed is 34.

Seattle's defensive front is going to look a lot different by 2028, which means an influx of young talent -- the kind of talent that is comparable to what the Seahawks already have -- is a must. The team needs a good, young wide receiver to help Jaxon Smith-Njigba, but defense might be the priority in the 2027 draft.

Thankfully, the Seattle Seahawks are well-positioned in the 2027 draft to take presumably excellent defensive players through the first five rounds. Including projected comp picks (for free agents lost last offseason), Seattle has one pick in each of the first four rounds and three in the fifth round, the round that produced Richard Sherman, Kam Chancellor, and others.
