pixiv to Co-Exhibit with Kodansha's Women's Comics Editorial Department at AFA Singapore 2026, One of Southeast Asia's Largest Japanese Pop Culture Events!

TheTools · 27.11.2026 07:32 · source · permalink

Showcasing pixiv and pixiv Encyclopedia, plus a visitor survey with exclusive giveaways and bonuses

TOKYO, JP / ACCESS Newswire / November 25, 2026 / pixiv Inc. (hereinafter referred to as "pixiv"; Headquarters: Toshima-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director and CEO: Yasuhiro Niwa) will co-exhibit with the Women's Comics Editorial Department of Kodansha Ltd. at Anime Festival Asia Singapore 2026 (hereinafter referred to as "AFA Singapore 2026"). One of Southeast Asia's largest Japanese pop culture events, AFA Singapore 2026 will take place in Singapore from Friday, November 27th to Sunday, November 29th, 2026.

pixiv took part in the event in 2022 as a sponsor of its Creators Hub. Returning four years later, the company will set up a promotional area for pixiv and pixiv Encyclopedia inside the Kodansha Women's Comics Editorial Department booth to introduce both services to visitors.

The area will spotlight pixiv Encyclopedia's automatic translation feature, which launched in November 2026. To demonstrate the feature, pixiv has written explanatory articles on the six titles below under the supervision of Kodansha's Women's Comics Editorial Department. Automatically translated versions of these articles will be available on the English version of pixiv Encyclopedia for a limited time.

Titles
Koiseyo Mayakashi Tenshidomo (恋せよまやかし天使ども): https://dic.pixiv.net/en/a/Fall%20in%20Love%2C%20You%20False%20Angels
Koigakubo-kun ni Hajimete o Ubawaremashita (恋ヶ窪くんにはじめてを奪われました): https://dic.pixiv.net/en/a/Koigakubo-kun%20Stole%20My%20First%20Time
Hoshifuru Okoku no Nina (星降る王国のニナ): https://dic.pixiv.net/en/a/Nina%20the%20Starry%20Bride
Kono Koi, Chaban ni Tsuki!? (この恋、茶番につき！？): https://dic.pixiv.net/en/a/Matcha%20Made%20in%20Heaven
Horeta Hareta Panda (惚れたはれたパンダ): https://dic.pixiv.net/en/a/Oh%20My%20Panda%21
Tamaranai no wa Koi na no ka (たまらないのは恋なのか): https://dic.pixiv.net/en/a/A%20Love%20Too%20Captivating

All six titles are available to read on K MANGA, Kodansha's manga service for international readers.

Introducing pixiv Encyclopedia's automatic translation feature
In November 2026, pixiv Encyclopedia launched an automatic translation feature that makes articles written by Japanese fans about anime, manga, games, and more available in English. With translations that take series-specific terminology and the context of fan culture into account, pixiv Encyclopedia aims to help fans outside Japan enjoy Japanese content at a deeper level.

Giveaways for survey respondents
To help improve its services, pixiv will survey visitors about their manga preferences, their awareness of specific titles, and more. The company will also run a survey about pixiv Encyclopedia.
