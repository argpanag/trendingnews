Police hunt for escaped prisoner Cain Te-Kahurangi Hanara

TheTools · 21.10.2026 03:59 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Crime and Justice

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._f0.YIZs2-J2FpwgcBcJJb29s1vXmFAWHLh_p1oUyv-l-RA/crime-and-justice/2054590%2Fpolice-hunt-for-escaped-prisoner-cain-te-kahurangi-hanara

A man considered dangerous, is being sought by police after failing to return to prison after being granted compassionate bail.

Police are on the hunt for Cain Te-Kahurangi Hanara, 22, and a warrant has been issued for his arrest.

A police spokesperson said he was actively avoiding police.

Hanara was granted compassionate bail by the courts on October 15.

He had links to Tauranga and the Kāpiti Coast, but police believed he was in the wider Auckland area.

"Hanara is considered dangerous and should not be approached," police said.

Anyone who saw Hanara was urged to call 111 immediately. Information could also be provided to police via 105, quoting file number 261016/8427.

Alternatively, information could be provided anonymously to Crime Stoppers on 0800 555 111.
