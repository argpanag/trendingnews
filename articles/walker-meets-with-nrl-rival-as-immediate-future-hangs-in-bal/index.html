Walker meets with NRL rival as immediate future hangs in balance

TheTools · 14.11.2026 06:09 · source · permalink
  • Sport
  • NRL
  • NRL

November 14, 2026 - 6.09am

Roosters premiership winner Sam Walker has met with the Tricolours’ arch-rivals, the Rabbitohs, while on Kangaroos duties in Brisbane, as per The Sydney Morning Herald.

All eyes have been on Walker’s footsteps heading into the 2027 pre-season after it was revealed the Roosters were prepared to get him off their books immediately after the club’s contract extension talks with the star playmaker fell apart.

Roosters halfback Sam Walker. Getty Images

South Sydney quickly emerged as a possible landing site for the 24-year-old, with the two parties meeting while Walker was in Kangaroos camp to discuss that option.

While the Rabbitohs are open to taking on Walker as soon as next season, the club does not have the salary cap space to meet his $1 million-per-season contract hopes.

The Rabbitohs will be able to make some financial room in 2028 when a number of their stars come off contract, including Latrell Mitchell and Jack Wighton.

The pair have not been asked to make way immediately to open the door for Walker’s possible arrival.

For Walker to join the Rabbitohs immediately, the Roosters would likely need to pay part of his wage.

Still, it is unlikely the club would want to do that for the Rabbitohs, even if it did do so to get Brandon Smith off its books in 2025.

Other floated landing sites for Walker are the Bulldogs and the PNG Chiefs, with coach Willie Peters publicly declaring his interest in the premiership winner.

As for the Bulldogs, Walker’s arrival has been floated in the event Matt Burton seeks a release from the club to join Mal Meninga and the Perth Bears.
