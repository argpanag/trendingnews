Amazon introduces a completely redesigned Kindle family

TheTools · 01.10.2026 12:50 · source · permalink

Key takeaways

  • Kindle: The most pocket-sized Kindle ever, with a flush-front display, edge-to-edge color, and available with an aluminum back.
  • Kindle Paperwhite: Thinner and lighter with exceptional contrast and clarity for crisp, clear text.
  • Kindle Colorsoft: Paper-like color that’s easy on the eyes for hours of comfortable reading.
  • New accessories include a Bluetooth page-turn remote and magnetic case with integrated page-turn buttons.

New Kindle accessories in 2026

  • Kindle Click ($34.99): A compact Bluetooth remote for page turning—ideal for reading under the covers, on a treadmill, or on a tray table during a flight. Compatible with all new Kindle devices, as well as any Kindle model from 2024 or later.
  • Page-Turn Cover ($79.99): A magnetic cover with physical page-turn buttons that preserves the slim form factor, using pogo pin connectivity. Available for Paperwhite Signature Edition and Colorsoft Signature Edition.
  • Charging Dock ($59.99): A stand that keeps Paperwhite Signature Edition and Colorsoft Signature Edition upright and charged.
  • Kindle Covers (starting at $39.99): Available in knit fabric, plant-based leather, and pebbled premium leather covers that wrap fully around the device for a seamless finish, color-matched across the lineup. For devices with aluminum housing, slim magnetic-attach covers are also available.
