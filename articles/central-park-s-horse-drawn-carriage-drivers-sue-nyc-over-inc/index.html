Central Park’s horse-drawn carriage drivers sue NYC over incoming ban

TheTools · 20.11.2026 02:08 · source · permalink

NEW YORK (AP) — Hold your horses: Central Park’s horse-drawn carriage drivers sued New York City on Thursday to stop a new law that will ban the iconic tourist attraction, challenging Mayor Zohran Mamdani just minutes after he signed a bill to wind down the industry.
