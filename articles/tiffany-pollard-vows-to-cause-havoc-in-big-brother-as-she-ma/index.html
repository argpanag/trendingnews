Tiffany Pollard vows to cause havoc in Big Brother as she makes TV comeback

TheTools · 27.10.2026 19:30 · source · permalink

AFTER a week of chaos, Big Brother’s hierarchy is toppling – but reality TV queen Tiffany Pollard has vowed to give it an almighty shove as she heads in as a celebrity house guest.

And she’s promised to make far more of an impact than old nemesis Gemma Collins as she chats gameplan, big beef and more with The Sun hours before the stint.

Coming out fighting, Tiffany said: “It’s Halloween week and when I say I’m gonna put the w**** in horror and horrible.

“I’m gonna listen to people, I’m gonna have an authentic experience but I don’t want to be on my best behaviour.”

It’s exactly what fans are hoping.

Tiffany, who we will see arrive on Wednesday night’s episode, is aware of the house “hierarchy” – ruled by Rochelle Rackham and Philip Regan – who have been accused of bullying by fans and housemates including Samuel Keen and Twinks Burnett. 

And she’s ready to rule.

Tiffany told me: “It’s called the hierarchy? The king and the queen of the house? Oh, please. It’s time for me to knock down the bricks.

“I love a good backlash. I mean, I deserve a good backlash because I’m not a housemate – I’m in there to play it and have a good time.

“So I feel like I just want to make my presence felt and known and it doesn’t matter if I don’t make a friend although I would like to make a friend or two, if that happens. 

“But I just want them to remember me.

“I only go around this life one good time and I want to make sure I’m living it authentically and I don’t want to quiet a voice of something that should have been said.

“I have a lot of people that’s betting on me to be real and to just say what it is.”

Tiffany’s back a decade on from her career-defining stay in 2016 where fans will remember that “David’s Dead” misunderstanding. 

But some forget that away from the drama, she was fairly alone and isolated in the house, with viewers feeling that she herself was bullied by some. 

Complaints were made to Ofcom over soap star John Partridge’s comments about “eating chicken” – which some deemed racist. 

Speaking about those low moments, Tiffany remembers: “Oh, it’s not easy when you’re alone and you’re isolated, I don’t really wish that on anyone.

“It’s just a hard territory to be in, but you have to navigate through, but hey, when you do things sometimes, you could be on the outs and you have to learn how to survive in that element, because we paint ourselves how we paint ourselves.

“Nobody knew who I was when I walked in there.”

They swiftly got to know, though, and she’s also remember for her spats with our Towie treasure, Gemma Collins. 

The GC spent her own few nights on this series of Big Brother earlier this month – but, unsurprisingly, Tiff thinks she can do better. 

Tiffany said: “Well, no woman could ever pave the way for me. No, other entity could ever pave the way for me because I am in my own stead. 

“What she did was make room for the real reality TV icon and legend and I thank her for doing all the heavy lifting and uh, putting on the heavy because now I’m in here to be the heir and the breath of fresh air and the water of life that this house definitely needs.”

The two women might’ve shared a house – and a job – but their paths have not crossed since. 

She continued: “Hell no, I don’t have anything in common with that woman, even you just bringing her name up steers my blood, it steams it, I’m boiling.”

It’s the kind of fighting talk you’d expect from the Head B*tch In Charge, as she is known.

Aside from Rochelle and Philip, there’s another two housemates in her sights too – Omar El Mayer and his house ex Rhianna Collins. 

He has been under fire for his language towards Rhianna, with Ofcom and Women’s Aid both weighing in on what they have deemed controlling behaviour.

Big Brother has been working with the lad to understand his actions. 

Tiffany said: “I feel like I should sit down with them both, maybe one or one, and then maybe collectively. 

“But if he’s doing things in there that are not a good representation of himself, maybe he doesn’t even realise it.

“So maybe he needs a little pulling to the side so he can slow down and just rethink his actions.”

After finding fame on Flavor of Love, a US dating show, Tiffany has appeared on everything from Celebrity Big Brother and Celebrity Ex on the Beach to her own reality series I Love New York and House of Villains. 

It leaves her perfectly placed to comment on the difference between UK and US reality telly. 

She said: “I feel like sometimes when I’m in the States, it’s just like they don’t understand how to catch it, but there is nothing that goes on without the UK audience spotting it.

“I love that meticulous fine-tooth comb y’all be using. 

“I think with reality television, check your references numbers, they don’t lie. And check your TV Guide. It has been here from the beginning of the time.

“If you have an emotion and if you have a pulse and you have a camera in front of you, you are creating reality television.

“So it’s a genre that’s not gonna go away and it should be respected and it’s got to be in the Hall of Fame.”

Tiffany’s 2016 series – aired on Channel 5- is considered one of the very best. 

She said: “I think people really enjoy 2016 Big Brother because there were just people from all walks of life and I feel like you just couldn’t call it.

“Now just from being with people in the show, I know about The Only Way is Essex and Hollyoaks. 

“It just really brought you together in a way where you can appreciate other people’s artistry.”

Recently Tiffany has added a whole new aspect to her own artistry after becoming a recording artist, thanks to a cheeky sample on Zara Larsson’s hit track Hot & Sexy.

The pop princess featured Tiff on the empowering song with her “Beautiful, fly, hot and sexy” refrain.

Speaking for the first time about the collab, Tiffany told me: “Well it blows my mind – Zara is multi-talented – and for her to come for the HBIC to honour a track.

“I was in that studio spitting, it was like I was Beethoven because I wanted to get it that right and that iconic just for her.

“I was just dedicated to the piece and a lot of people dance to it, so we’re happy.”

The track from Zara’s multi-platinum album Midnight Sun was even played during last week’s Victoria’s Secret catwalk. 

But we’re not going to see Tiff on tour with Zara any time soon.

She added: “I don’t want to crush dreams or anything like that, but our girl is going to handle it. I’m not gonna be on tour – I got bad knees, honey.

“There’s no way I’d be on that catwalk, I love chitlins and ribs.”

She’ll squeeze in time while in the United Kingdom for her favourite British pastimes – watching Towie, Hollyoaks and tucking into our fish and chips. 

Just hours before heading on to Big Brother, she said: “Bring all that vinegar. People are so nice here.”

We’ll see if she feels the same following her stay in the spy house. 

Big Brother continues tonight at 9pm on ITV2 and ITVX.
