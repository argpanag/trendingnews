Mississippi woman arrested for threats against ICE agents

TheTools · 21.10.2026 20:58 · source · permalink

GULFPORT, Miss. (KNOE) - ICE Homeland Security Investigations (HSI) special agents arrested Lisa White, of Mississippi, after an investigation into threats made against ICE agents.

HSI Gulfport special agents took White into custody in Long Beach after reports she made explicit threats to “kill federal agents” while attempting to purchase a firearm and pepper spray.

According to reports, White asked about the capability of specific firearms to penetrate ballistic vests, stating she would target agents’ heads if she could not breach their armor.

HSI special agents responded to the scene, conducted interviews, and located and arrested White.
