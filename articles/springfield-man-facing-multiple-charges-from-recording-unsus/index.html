Springfield man facing multiple charges from recording unsuspecting people

TheTools · 11.09.2026 21:08 · source · permalink

BELCHERTOWN, Mass. (WGGB/WSHM) - The Northwestern District Attorney’s Office said that 22-year-old Jovante Gonzalez of Springfield will face 20 illegal wiretap and harassment charges following an investigation by the Amherst College Police Department.

Gonzalez is accused of installing a camera inside a restroom within the Amherst College Science Center. Officers of the Amherst College Police were made aware of the camera back in April, prompting an investigation which led to Gonzalez’s arrest on Thursday.

Gonzalez was released on personal recognizance, as long as he stays away off of Amherst College property and witnesses, and undergo a mental health evaluation.

Gonzalez is scheduled to re-appear at Eastern Hampshire District Court in November.
