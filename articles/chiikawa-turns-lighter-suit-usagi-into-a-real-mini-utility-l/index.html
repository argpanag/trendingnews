"Chiikawa" Turns Lighter-Suit Usagi Into a Real Mini Utility Lighter!

Illustrator Nagano’s Chiikawa quickly became a sensation, with its distinctively quirky anime adaptation winning even more fans. This year’s theatrical film pushed the franchise further into the mainstream, making it an even bigger talking point.

TOKAI Corporation has now unveiled the new Chiikawa Chakkaman mini, inspired by Usagi’s lighter-shaped costume from the series—and this version actually works. It will launch through the official Chiikawa Market online store on November 27, followed by a gradual rollout at major retailers across Japan beginning December 1!

Lighter-suit Usagi first appeared in Chiikawa’s 2024 online manga during the storyline celebrating Chiikawa and Hachiware’s birthdays. The look later became a plush toy that fans rushed to snap up—and it is easy to see why.

This release takes the compact version of TOKAI’s standard utility lighter and covers it in adorable Chiikawa artwork. Even the packaging features the original manga illustration of Usagi wearing the lighter costume.

It can light birthday candles, incense, and scented candles around the house, while also coming in handy for camping, barbecues, and other outdoor activities.
