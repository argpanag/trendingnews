Taylor Swift Doesn't Care About Her Haters In 'Cleveland!' And I'm Happy For Her

TheTools · 25.09.2026 06:43 · source · permalink

THE RUNDOWN

  • The second song on Taylor Swift‘s The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore is ‘Cleveland!’, and it’s an ode to Swift’s husband, Travis Kelce.
  • The song details the online hate and trolling Swift receives, concluding that Kelce loves her regardless so she is unbothered by the criticism.
  • The title nods to Travis Kelce’s hometown of Cleveland Heights in Ohio, with the exclamation point potentially referencing Swift’s description of Kelce as a “human exclamation point.”

Taylor Swift has no time for her critics — a point she makes abundantly clear in the second song on her just-dropped album The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore, “Cleveland!”. The song dives into the criticism Swift has long received in the spotlight, concluding that although she “can see what [her haters] mean” with their scathing remarks, she really doesn’t care because Travis Kelce — her husband, in case you missed it — loves her, and that’s all that matters.

The four-song album is a continuation of her record-breaking album The Life of a Showgirl, which Swift released close to a year ago in October 2025. Writing in her announcement post on Instagram, Swift said “About a year ago, you guys did a truly unfathomable thing. Something that completely blew my mind… You gave The Life of a Showgirl the biggest first week for an album in history.” She continued, saying that the forthcoming The Encore disc was “born out of pure gratitude for your exuberance for and celebration of the album we made.”

The vibe of The Encore is a shift from that of its predecessor — it’s far less theatrical than TLOAS and more in line with Swift’s signature earnestness. It’s riddled with Easter eggs (as per usual) but one song that requires very little digging for answers is ‘Cleveland!’, a song that is an unabashed, some may go as far to say, over-the-top, declaration of Swift’s love for her new husband. Below, allow us to unpack.

‘Cleveland!’ Meaning: Travis Kelce Is Taylor Swift’s Biggest Fan

Few artists have been forced to endure the criticism and scrutiny that Swift has faced over her 20-year career in the spotlight. Naturally, that kind of constant backlash would grate on a person. Throughout the song, she references commentary she’s received, ranging from “She’s not as hot as she was, but she was only a four” to “And with all of that money, why’s she dressed like trash?” — brutal. She concedes that “The taste of me is acquired,” but, in a fairly uncreative metaphor, says that it’s her husband, Travis Kelce’s, “favourite dish.” We’ll spare you the analysis of whether that’s an innuendo or not.

Moving on, the song’s title is a pretty clear reference to Kelce’s hometown of Cleveland Heights in Ohio, while the exclamation point might be a reference to Swift’s description of Kelce on an episode of New Heights, the podcast and YouTube show Kelce hosts with his brother, Jason Kelce. “He’s just a vibe booster in everyone’s life. He’s like a human exclamation point,” she said. Kelce agreed, saying he does use a lot of exclamation points when he texts, which we’re not sure is what Swift was saying, but anyway.

As cloyingly sweet as the song is, only someone without a heart would find it hard not to see the charm in ‘Cleveland!’. “You only need to find one person in this entire world / Who doesn’t find you completely insufferable / And I have an important announcement to make / I found mine.” Love is real! Leave it to Ms. Swift to impart some wisdom by way of a poppy toe-tapper.
