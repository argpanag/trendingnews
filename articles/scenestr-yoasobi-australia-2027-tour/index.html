scenestr - Yoasobi Australia 2027 Tour

TheTools · 12.11.2026 01:24 · source · permalink
  • Written by: Staff Writers
  • Published: 11 November 2026

Japanese duo Yoasobi will make their Australian live debut in 2027.

The J-Pop stars – composer Ayase and vocalist ikura - have taken the world by storm with their music adapted from newly published short stories and novels – often sourced from the Japanese writing platform Monogatary.com.

The 'Into The Night (Yoru ni Kakeru)' hitmakers will headline concerts in Melbourne and Sydney next March.

"Australia, get ready for liftoff! We're bringing SUPER PLANET your way for an out-of-this-world live experience. We can't wait to see you there 🌏💫!"

Built around the slogan 'novel into music', Yoasobi has built a distinctive musical identity by transforming stories into songs, bringing together vivid storytelling, expansive production and ikura's instantly recognisable vocals.

Their innovative approach has helped them become a defining force in modern J-pop, connecting with audiences far beyond Japan.

They've just completed their largest North American tour to date, drawing more than 70,000 fans across 6 shows.

The tour included a return to Lollapalooza for a second appearance on the festival's main stage as well as Yoasobi's debut at Osheaga Festival in Montreal, where they became the first J-pop act to perform.

The duo also made history as the first J-pop act to perform at and sell out New York's Barclays Center, before closing the tour with a performance at the iconic Hollywood Bowl, another first for a J-pop artist.

Tickets on sale from 12pm (local time) on 13 November.

Yoasobi 2027 Tour Dates

Wed 17 Mar - Rod Laver Arena (Melbourne)
Fri 19 Mar - Afterpay Arena (Sydney)
