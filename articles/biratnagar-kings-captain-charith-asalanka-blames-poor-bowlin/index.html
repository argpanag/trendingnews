Biratnagar Kings Captain Charith Asalanka Blames Poor Bowling in Final Overs for Loss

TheTools · 14.11.2026 16:29 · source · permalink

Kathmandu. Biratnagar Kings captain Charith Asalanka has said that the team had to face defeat after failing to bowl well in the final over against Lumbini Lions by 4 wickets in the Nepal Premier League (NPL).

Talking to reporters after the match, Asalanka said that his team made good runs but could not save them. He pointed out that bowling was difficult due to dew and fielding also needs improvement.

He said that although they made a good total by batting in the first innings, the bowling in the last 10 overs did not go as expected.

‘We had made a good score on such a pitch. Spinners were also getting turn on the pitch. But we couldn't do well in the last 10 overs. Lumbini's batsmen batted well,’ he said. He said that he had faith in his bowlers even when he had to defend 33 runs in the last two overs. However, he said that bowling was difficult in the second innings due to dew on the field.

‘It is very difficult to bowl in the second innings. Dew falls here quickly. The dew made it a bit easier for the batsmen. It was particularly difficult for the bowlers,’ he said.

Asalanka also admitted that there was some problem in the powerplay due to the absence of key player Angelo Mathews. He mentioned that Mathews had taken wickets by bowling well in the powerplay throughout the tournament and his absence was felt. However, he said that the available players should also be able to perform well.

He also cited fielding weaknesses as one of the reasons for the loss. He said that the opportunity to run out a batsman was missed during the match and the team's fielding has not been satisfactory throughout the tournament.

‘When you lose a game, you can say many things didn't happen. But overall, our fielding was not good. We need to improve on this in the coming days,’ he said.

Asalanka also commented on Biratnagar's inability to secure wins in the end despite playing some competitive matches this season. He said that in T20 cricket, it is important to know how to win a game under pressure.

‘There is a lot of pressure in T20 cricket. How to take the game to the end and win is important. We could have won the first two matches of the league as well. But we couldn't finish the game. We have to learn from this,’ he said.

Speaking about his experience playing in Nepal for the first time, Asalanka praised Nepal's beauty and its cricket. He mentioned that the NPL is competitive and expressed his belief that Nepali players have good potential and such tournaments will help in the development of Nepali cricket.
