Man wanted in 2019 Atlanta shooting that left 1 dead, 4 injured arrested in Alabama

TheTools · 21.09.2026 20:19 · source · permalink

ATLANTA, Ga. (Atlanta News First) — A man wanted for a 2019 Atlanta shooting that killed one man and injured four others was arrested in Alabama, police said.

Ashton Reed, 29, was arrested in Roanoke on Wednesday morning on multiple charges including murder and aggravated assault, according to the Roanoke Police Department.

Reed was one of the Atlanta Police Department’s 10 Most Wanted suspects.

On the night of March 31, 2019, Atlanta police officers responded to a report of a person shot at 3041 Landrum Drive SW, the address for the Woodlands at Cascade apartment complex.

Officers found two men with gunshot wounds. One of the victims, 27-year-old Tyriq Livatt, was pronounced dead at the scene. The other man was taken to the hospital in critical condition.

Responding officer learned three other people with non-life-threatening gunshot wounds had arrived at a RaceTrac gas station at 3503 Camp Creek Parkway by private vehicle. Investigators determined they were shot at the apartment complex.

Reed had shot the victims during an argument, according to Atlanta police.

Roanoke police said Reed was arrested at Roanoke Stockyard, a livestock auction house, where he was employed under an alias.

Reed was taken to the Randolph County, Alabama, jail to await extradition to Georgia. He faces charges of felony murder, aggravated battery, four counts of aggravated assault and 6 counts of possession of a firearm during the commission of a felony.
