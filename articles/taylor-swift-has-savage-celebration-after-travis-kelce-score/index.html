Taylor Swift has savage celebration after Travis Kelce scores first touchdown of 2026 season

TheTools · 21.09.2026 20:57 · source · permalink

The Kansas City Chiefs tight end found the end zone against the Indianapolis Colts on Sunday night at Arrowhead Stadium

Travis Kelce woke up Taylor Swift and the friends in her suite at Arrowhead Stadium on Sunday night in the first quarter of the Kansas City Chiefs’ matchup against the Indianapolis Colts.

Patrick Mahomes and the Chiefs capitalized on a turnover. Mahomes saw Kelce burst out to the right side. He caught the ball and ran into the end zone for a touchdown. It was a vintage play that so many NFL fans have seen for years.

The score caused a raucous celebration from Swift. The NBC broadcast caught Swift pointing to her wedding ring as Kelce celebrated in the end zone.

"That's my husband!" she shouted.

Swift has been a mainstay at Arrowhead Stadium since the two began dating a few years ago. She and Kelce hard launched their relationship nearly three years ago when she showed up to support the legendary tight end when the Chiefs took on the Chicago Bears.

Since then, it’s been a storybook romance for the two superstars. They got married at Madison Square Garden earlier this summer and began the next chapter of their lives together.

Kelce, at 36 years old, isn’t exactly the same tight end he was in 2023. But the 11-time Pro Bowler and three-time Super Bowl champion has shown he can still make massive plays on the field when he’s called upon. His mere presence on the field is enough to cause the defense to keep a special eye on him.

In the Chiefs’ 31-10 win over the Denver Broncos last week, he had three catches for 71 yards on five targets.
