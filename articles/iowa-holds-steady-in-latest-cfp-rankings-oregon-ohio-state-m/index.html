Iowa holds steady in latest CFP rankings; Oregon, Ohio State move in front of Hawkeyes

TheTools · 18.11.2026 02:52 · source · permalink

Iowa stayed put in the latest College Football Playoff rankings, which were released Tuesday night.

Video above: QB Hank Brown talks about why Iowa could make the playoffs

At the end of the season, the top 12 teams will get to compete in the College Football Playoffs, so the No. 15 Hawkeyes (8-2 overall, 5-2 Big Ten) will need to win their last two games of the year for a chance at the top 12. They play at Illinois (6-4, 4-3) on Saturday, Nov. 21, before finishing the regular season at home Friday, Nov. 27, against Nebraska (6-4, 3-4).

Two Big Ten teams — Oregon and Ohio State — leapfrogged Iowa in this week's rankings. Oregon moved up from No. 16 to No. 13 after beating Michigan, 31-7. And Ohio State went from No. 20 to No. 14 after beating Northwestern, 28-19.

Here are the dates for upcoming CFP Selection Shows.

  • 6:30-7 p.m. Tuesday, Nov. 24
  • 6-6:30 p.m. Tuesday, Dec. 1
  • 11 a.m.-2 p.m. Sunday, Dec. 6
