For someone as prolific as Guy Ritchie, you'd expect him to be one of the industry's most reliable box-office hitmakers. But the truth is that Ritchie hasn't had a box-office hit in five years. In this time, he has directed five movies, with two on the way. None of them have broken even commercially, although one was a direct-to-streaming release.

Ritchie has found greater success on streaming, having worked on a trio of popular shows — MobLand, The Gentlemen, and Young Sherlock. Several of his past theatrical underperformers have also gone on to find a large audience on streaming. Ritchie's most recent box-office hit is currently streaming on Tubi in the United States, but only for a little while longer.

The film was released in 2021 and marketed as a long-awaited reunion between the filmmaker and action star Jason Statham. The two came up together in the United Kingdom, in cult classics such as Lock, Stock and Two Smoking Barrels and Snatch. They also worked together on the ill-fated 2005 film Revolver. The 2021 movie was their first collaboration since Revolver; they have gone on to work together on Operation Fortune: Ruse de Guerre and have two movies lined up​​​​​​​.

The movie in question also featured Holt McCallany, Scott Eastwood, Laz Alonso, Josh Hartnett, and Eddie Marsan. It follows a London crime lord who goes on a vengeful rampage when his son is killed during a heist gone wrong.

Here's How Long You Have Left to Watch Guy Ritchie and Jason Statham's Hit for Free

We're talking, of course, about Wrath of Man — Ritchie's biblical crime thriller​​​​​​​ that grossed more than $100 million worldwide against a reported budget of $40 million. Statham repeated the feat with two subsequent thrillers directed by David Ayer — The Beekeeper and A Working Man. However, he has struggled this year with two underperformers, Shelter and Mutiny.

Wrath of Man received mixed-to-positive reviews and is now sitting at a 68% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes. The aggregator website's consensus reads, "Wrestling just enough stakes out of its thin plot, Wrath of Man sees Guy Ritchie and Jason Statham reunite for a fun, action-packed ride." The movie's "Verified Hot" 90% audience score shows how well it has been received since its theatrical release​​​​​​​. The movie will remain available on Tubi in the United States until December 1.
