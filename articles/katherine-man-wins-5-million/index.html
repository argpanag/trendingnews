Katherine man wins $5 million

TheTools · 21.10.2026 04:03 · source · permalink

A Katherine man is set for life after claiming a $5 million lottery win.

The man was scrolling on social media scroll when he spotted a post urging locals to check their Set for Life tickets, with officials searching for a mystery winner of draw 4092 on Monday, October 19.

The winning entry was purchased at Eastside Mini Mart on Grevillea Road.

As his winning ticket wasn't registered, lottery officials had no way of contacting the winner with the life-changing news and instead had to wait for him to come forward to claim $5 million - paid in monthly instalments of $20,000 for the next 20 years and $200K right away.

"Thank you! Oh, I'm overwhelmed!" he said when an official from the Lott confirmed his annuity prize.

"That's f****ing crazy. I'm freaking out a bit.

"I was at work when I saw all over Facebook that someone had won at the Mini Mart and I thought 'Hang on, that's where I bought my bloody ticket!'.

"When I checked it, I had an inkling, but I wanted it confirmed first.

"Never in my life have I won something like this. It's just life-changing."

When asked what might be first on his Set for Life wish list, the winner admitted he was still trying to process the news but knew his family would be front of mind.

"It'll be a huge help to my family. I'm lost for words right now," he said.

"I think I'm in a bit of shock. It's very exciting."

The Northern Territory resident held the only division one winning entry nationally.

His win continues the Territory's hot streak, marking the Top End's third major lottery prize this month, following a $1 million Weekday Windfall win scored by a Tanami man and a TattsLotto win of more than $900,000 by a Darwin man.

Eastside Mini Mart store owner Ethan Adidi said his outlet was celebrating selling a division one winning entry.

"It was a bit of a surprise for all of us to hear the news," he said.

"It's great to see a win land in town - it's a really positive for the Katherine community to know someone local has won.

"We haven't had a division one win in forever so it's just fantastic."

The winning numbers in Set for Life draw 4092 on Monday 19 October 2026 were 14, 42, 16, 19, 7, 30 and 15, while the supplementary numbers were 24 and 44.

Across Australia, there was one division one winning entry in Set for Life draw 4092 - the entry from the Northern Territory.

This marks the 180th Set for Life division one winner nationally since the game began and the 17th Set for Life division one winner so far this calendar year.
