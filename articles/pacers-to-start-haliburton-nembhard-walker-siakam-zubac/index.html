    The Indiana Pacers will start Tyrese Haliburton, Andrew Nembhard, Jarace Walker, Pascale Siakam, and Ivica Zubac against the Mavericks on Friday.

    Jarace Walker will take Kelly Oubre’s spot in the starting lineup after he started the first game. The Pacers also have Aaron Nesmith available to play tonight. It’s possible that Rick Carlisle is trying to match the size of the Mavericks by putting Walker in to start.
