Jess Wilson now Victoria's first popularly elected woman in top job

TheTools · 28.11.2026 08:44 · source · permalink

  • Topic:State and Territory Elections

Topic:State and Territory Elections

Jess Wilson will become the next premier of Victoria.  (AAP: Joel Carrett )

In short: 

Liberal leader Jess Wilson will become the next premier of Victoria after the Coalition defeated Labor in the state election.

The Coalition was last elected to government in Victoria in 2010, holding power for one single four-year term.

Ms Wilson has campaigned strongly on potholes, crime and the state's debt while also announcing cuts and privatisation plans just days ahead of election day.

Liberal leader Jess Wilson will become Victoria's first popularly elected female premier as the Coalition dislodges Labor from state government after 12 years.

Labor leader Ben Carroll called Ms Wilson to concede defeat before 8.30pm on election night.

It has been a swift rise for the 36-year-old who became the first woman to lead the Victorian Liberal Party after ousting Brad Battin in November 2025.

It was the party's fifth leadership change in seven years, with Ms Wilson's ascension marking the end of a tumultuous period for the Victorian Liberals.

In less than four years, Ms Wilson has gone from making her maiden speech in state parliament as the new member for Kew to premier of Victoria.

Ms Wilson's leadership has ended the Coalition's 12 years in opposition.  (AAP: Joel Carrett)

Ahead of the election, Ms Wilson ran a strong social media campaign on potholes, crime and the state's debt, with minimal slip-ups.

Just two days before polling day, while announcing the Coalition's costings, Ms Wilson announced plans to partly privatise Melbourne's sewerage network, cut and delay level crossing removals and walk back Labor plans to expand free kindergarten.

Those announcements drew sharp criticism from Labor, One Nation and the Greens, especially given 2.2 million Victorians had already cast their ballots in early voting by that time.

Despite this, Labor's popularity had already plummeted after more than a decade in government.

When he took power from Jacinta Allan in July, Labor leader Ben Carroll called a royal commission into allegations of crime and corruption on major infrastructure projects and distanced himself from Ms Allan and the premier before her, Daniel Andrews.

Ben Carroll replaced Jacinta Allan as Labor leader and premier in July. (AAP: James Ross)

But he inherited the baggage of a Labor government, which he had served as a senior minister, associated with questionable integrity, increased crime and soaring state debt.

Speaking to the ABC tonight, deputy Labor leader Gabrielle Williams said there was "deep community anger" over allegations of union corruption in the construction sector.

Ms Williams said the result was "simply devastating" for Labor and that she wanted to "stick around" and help the party rebuild.

Deputy Labor leader Gabrielle Williams. (ABC News: Alonso Gajardo)

Redbridge pollster and former Labor strategist Kos Samaras said Labor had lost seats in Victoria the party had held since the federation of Australia.

"This is just the most historic event the Labor Party has experienced nationally,"

Mr Samaras said it could take a decade or more for Labor to recover in Victoria and would require soul searching for "the full extent of next year".

"I think the next Labor premier is probably still in high school," he said.
