Fincantieri to build two smaller LNG-fuelled ships for Carnival Cruise Line

CruiseFincantieri has signed a contract with Carnival Corporation for the design and construction of two next-generation dual-fuel cruise ships for Carnival Cruise Line.
The ships will be built at Marghera, Italy, with deliveries in 2033 and 2035. Each will measure about 130,000 GT and have about 1,650 guest cabins. They will run mainly on LNG. Fincantieri says the design is a multi-brand platform that could later be used by other Carnival Corporation cruise lines.

Carnival Cruise Line said the new class is smaller by design, so the ships can call at more ports and offer more itinerary options. Christine Duffy, President of Carnival Cruise Line, said the new class gives the company “the best of both worlds”. Carnival has not yet named the class or the ships.

Fincantieri has delivered 76 ships to Carnival Corporation over 30 years, 15 of them for Carnival Cruise Line. Pierroberto Folgiero, CEO and Managing Director of Fincantieri, called the contract “a powerful endorsement of Fincantieri’s industrial leadership”.

Construction of CARNIVAL DESTINY, the first of three Ace Class ships for Carnival Cruise Line, began at Monfalcone in July 2026. The ship debuts in 2029. It will have eight pools, six of them infinity pools, with a combined water surface of more than 11,200 sq ft (about 1,040 m²). That is more outdoor pools than any other ship in the Carnival fleet. It will also have the line’s first main pool zone facing outwards to the sea, and an ocean-front lanai with pools, restaurants and bars.

CARNIVAL DESTINY will be the first Carnival ship with an adults-only zone. It will cover four decks and include the line’s first adults-only cabins, a larger Serenity retreat and a rooftop pool deck. Family space will be nearly twice that of Carnival’s newest ships. The ship will also have 25 new restaurant and bar concepts. Carnival has not yet announced its gross tonnage, passenger capacity or homeport.

Across the group, Carnival Corporation has 12 new ships on order for delivery through 2039 for Carnival Cruise Line, Princess and AIDA.

Carnival Cruise Line will also expand Celebration Key, its destination on Grand Bahama, in two phases, in 2028 and 2030. Each phase adds land-side capacity for 3,000 guests. The 2030 phase includes a water park with 18 waterslides.
