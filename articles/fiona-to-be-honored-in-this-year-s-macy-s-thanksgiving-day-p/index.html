Fiona to be honored in this year's Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade

TheTools · 21.11.2026 23:34 · source · permalink

Fiona is set to be honored at this year's Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade.

According to an Instagram story post put out by Macy's on Saturday, the handlers of this year's "Blue Hippo" balloon are all set to wear purple patches in honor of Fiona during the parade.

"Fiona brought joy to millions, including so many in the Macy’s community in Cincinnati," the company wrote on Instagram. "This year, as Macy’s welcomes our own beloved Blue Hippo back to the Parade, we wanted to mark Fiona’s memory. With the Cincinnati Zoo’s blessing, Macy’s has made a donation to the zoo in Fiona’s memory and Blue Hippo’s handlers will wear a purple Fiona patch during the 100th Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade."

The balloon, which first debuted at the parade in 1931, was announced in August by Macy's to be making its long-awaited return to the streets of New York City.

According to Macy's, the balloon has added significance this year because balloons were previously released into the sky at the end of the Thanksgiving Day Parade during the event's early years. Afterwards, people who found the balloons were given rewards for returning them to Macy's stores.

However, the Blue Hippo marked the lone balloon of the 1931 parade to never be found, after last being "seen soaring beyond the Empire State Building."

Now, in commemoration of the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, a host of iconic balloons are set to return to the parade's lineup, with the Blue Hippo balloon taking on the added purpose of honoring Fiona's life this week.

It is a sign that while the things we treasure can sometimes be lost far sooner than expected, those that make a lasting impact on us are never truly forgotten — no matter how much time has passed.
