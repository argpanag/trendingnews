Bill Gates says next 20 years to be the most tumultuous in human history as Microsoft announces buyouts to 60,000 employees

TheTools · 28.10.2026 11:15 · source · permalink

    Microsoft Layoffs: Bill Gates has warned that the next 20 years could be among the most tumultuous periods in human history as artificial intelligence and robots transform jobs and industries. The Microsoft co-founder believes AI could eventually create widespread abundance, but the transition may lead to job losses, economic uncertainty and a crisis of purpose for many workers. He has suggested shorter working hours, earlier retirement and selective AI adoption as possible ways to manage the disruption.
