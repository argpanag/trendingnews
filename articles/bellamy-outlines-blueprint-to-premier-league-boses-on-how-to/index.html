Bellamy outlines blueprint to Premier League boses on how to stop Haaland

TheTools · 14.11.2026 09:49 · source · permalink
  • Jack Cunningham, SEO and Live Blogs Writer
  • Published: 9:49, 14 Nov 2026
  • Updated: 9:49, 14 Nov 2026

Craig Bellamy believes he has concocted the ultimate recipe on how to stop Erling Haaland from scoring.

Wales beat Norway 2-1 in the Nations League last month in Cardiff, with the Manchester City marksman drawing a blank.

Haaland is Norway's record scorer with 65 goals from 59 caps, but Wales boss Bellamy found a way to stop him from getting on the score sheet.

And ahead of the reverse fixture in Oslo on Saturday, the ex-Liverpool forward was asked about how he sets up his side to stop the former Borussia Dortmund star.

To which he said: "You're talking about trying to deal with one of the best players in his position in the world, and we've been seeing that for several years now.

"He's different to what you would normally have to deal with. He has the profile of everything you want in a player, and that's quite rare.

"It's a collective responsibility, not just an individual one."

What has Bellamy said about how to stop Haaland?

While managers across the globe have tried and failed to stop Haaland, Bellamy insists teams cannot be afraid to leave space in behind the striker.

When asked to expand on the tactics behind it, the former Burnley assistant manager stated: "Drive up and don't let him dictate.

"It's easier said than done because then you could turn around and say to me, 'Why are you playing a high line against Haaland?'

"Trust me you don't want to, but you can't have him dictating the game.

"That's true of all players, really, but it's much harder when someone is getting in your face and stopping you from having time on the ball.

"A good player can look like a top player if you give him time. A top player can look world-class if you give him time.

"A world-class player might then have a chance of looking like an ordinary or good player, if you don't allow him time.

"Putting pressure on the ball is always important for us, and I thought we did that really well that night."

As a result of Bellamy's tactics, Wales limited Haaland to just two shots in the game.

Wales are currently safe from demotion to the 'B' League on goal difference with Norway currently occupying fourth spot.

And they are still in with a chance of reaching the quarter-finals providing they beat Norway and Portugal emerge victorious over Denmark.
