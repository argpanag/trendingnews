Actualizado el 15/09/2026 05:27 EDT

A few days ago, Kylian Mbappé made it clear in an interview with the legendary French magazine France Football that he believes he should win the upcoming Ballon d’Or. Not only ahead of Lamine Yamal, but also ahead of his France teammate Ousmane Dembélé.

And he did not stop there. Mbappé went as far as saying: “Dembélé? He was always seen as a talented player. I, on the other hand, was always seen as the chosen one.” Those comments have, unsurprisingly, deeply upset the Paris Saint Germain star.

That is according to former player Jérôme Rothen, who now works as a pundit for RMC Sport: “Dembélé took it very badly, that’s for sure. He is very angry with Kylian Mbappé. For him, it’s a form of betrayal. Those are strong words, but that’s exactly how he sees it...,” Rothen said.

He added: “And it’s not just him. It’s everyone around Ousmane, the people who are with him in the PSG locker room. His France teammates as well. I’m being told that there are many players on the French national team who don’t understand, once again, all of his comments, and that they are more on Dembélé’s side than Mbappé’s. And yet it’s the same team, but apparently there are now two camps.”

“The feeling we had at the World Cup is even more obvious today. Many players had problems with Kylian Mbappé’s behavior and his comments, that’s the reality. He has alienated a lot of people within the French national team. The World Cup played out the way it did, and it’s true that when you finish as the top scorer and behave impeccably, as Kylian did at the World Cup, that somewhat softens all the criticism within the group. But these kinds of comments are going to hurt him,” Rothen concluded.
