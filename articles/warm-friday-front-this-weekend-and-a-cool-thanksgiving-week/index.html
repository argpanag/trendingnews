Warm Friday, front this weekend, and a cool Thanksgiving week

TheTools · 20.11.2026 10:44 · source · permalink

Weather

Small rain chance with a front on Sunday

FORECAST HIGHLIGHTS

  • MOSTLY SUNNY & WARM FRIDAY: Temps to climb into the mid-70s today
  • FRONT SUNDAY: Small rain chance, cooler next week
  • THANKSGIVING WEEK: Generally cool, with a few, brief opportunities for showers

FORECAST

TODAY

  • MOSTLY SUNNY, HIGH: 74°

Some brief morning clouds and a patch of fog or two will give way to mostly sunny skies this afternoon. Temps will climb into the mid-70s.

WEEKEND FRONT

Saturday will start with another round of patchy fog, as humidity increases. Morning clouds will be slow to burn off, keeping temps in the 70s for highs. A stray shower can’t be ruled out during the afternoon, but odds are very low.

A front sweeps through on Sunday. The front may be enough to stir up a few showers (20%). Otherwise, it’ll be partly cloudy and mild. Temperatures may still make the 70s, before temperatures fall into the 40s by Monday morning.

THANKSGIVING WEEK

The main theme for the holiday week will be cool weather, with a few spotty chances for rainfall. Impacts to travel will be minimal. Highs will be in the upper-50s to low-60s most of the week. Tuesday brings a small chance for rain (20%), while another system is forecast to approach the area by Thanksgiving Day. Moisture will be limited, meaning rain chances, if any, will be low. Do expect chilly mornings, especially on Thanksgiving Day where temps may fall to near 40 in San Antonio.
