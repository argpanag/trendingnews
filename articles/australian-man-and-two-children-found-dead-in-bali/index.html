Australian man and two children found dead in Bali

  • Bali

Updated September 14, 2026 - 3.35am. First published at 12.17am

An Australian man and his two young children have been found dead inside a property in Bali in a suspected double murder suicide.

Police were called to a property in Kuta on the Indonesian island, where officers found the body of 56-year-old Fremantle man and his two children, aged six and four, on Sunday local time.

The 66-year-old man was found dead alongside two children in Kuta, Bali on Sunday. Today

It is understood the man’s wife had called for a welfare check before the bodies were found.

It is believed she was travelling to Jakarta when she raised the alarm.

Indonesian police forced their way into the home and made the grim discovery.

Local police are treating the investigation as a suspected murder suicide.

An autopsy is now under way.

A spokesperson for the Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade (DFAT) confirmed it is providing consular assistance to the family of three Australians who died in Bali.

“We send our deepest condolences to the family at this difficult time,” the spokesperson said.

“Owing to our privacy obligations we are unable to provide further comment.”

Western Australian Premier Roger Cook described the incident as “horrific” in a statement.

“A father and children have lost their lives,” Cook said.

“This is a very sad situation, and our hearts go out to everyone involved, and we hope that we can get to the bottom of what’s gone on here as soon as possible.”
