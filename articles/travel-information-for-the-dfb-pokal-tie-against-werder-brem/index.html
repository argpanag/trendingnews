Travel information for the DFB-Pokal tie against Werder Bremen

Train services

Getting there

  • The special TRI train (Borussia-Express) departs from Hamm Hbf. at 18:50 and stops at Signal Iduna Park station at 19:24.
  • The RE 57 from the Sauerland will make additional stops at Signal Iduna Park station at 18:13, 19:13 and 20:13, and at Dortmund Central Station seven minutes later in each case.
  • The RE 57 from Dortmund towards the Sauerland will run additional services departing from Dortmund Hbf. at 18:41, 19:41 and 20:41, and from Signal Iduna Park station five minutes later in each case.

Getting home

OneFootball Videos

  1. The special TRI train (Borussia-Express) departs from Signal Iduna Park station at 23:20 and terminates at Hamm Hbf. at 23:53.
  2. The special train to Lünen (RB 51) departs from Signal Iduna Park station at 23:15 and from Dortmund Hbf. at 23:20, stopping in Lünen at 23:43. From there, it continues to Coesfeld, arriving at 00:26.

Further information

  1. Due to engineering works, RB 52 services between Rummenohl and Hagen Hbf. are cancelled and will be replaced by buses.  
  2. Due to engineering works, RB 59 services between Unna and Soest are cancelled from 22:00, with replacement buses operating on this section. The two post-match trains departing from Dortmund Signal Iduna Park at 23:08 and 00:08 will therefore continue from Unna (at 23:33 and 00:33) to Hamm Hbf., stopping at Bönen (at 23:37 and 00:37) and arriving at 23:45 and 00:45. Replacement buses will also operate on the Hamm – Soest – Lippstadt – Paderborn route from 21:30.  

KombiTickets and passenger information

Match tickets include a public transport ticket for one outward and one return journey. The ticket is valid throughout North Rhine-Westphalia.

DSW21 is prepared for the possibility of extra time and a penalty shootout in the DFB-Pokal and reserves the right to adjust its post-match timetables accordingly.

DSW21's KombiTicket scheme has now been extended to matches involving all BVB teams. Whether you want to watch football (men, women or U23s), women's handball or table tennis in person, travel to and from the venue by public transport is included in the price with DSW21's KombiTicket.

All services can be found in the online journey planner (www.bus-und-bahn.de) and in the DSW21 and VRR apps. Further timetable details are also available at NWL-Info.de and zuginfo.nrw.

Travelling by car

For fans travelling by car, DOPARK sells parking tickets for home matches in advance. Tickets are available from the DOPARK ticket shop for DOPARK car parks and from the Westfalenhallen ticket shop for parking at the Westfalenhallen.

Ticket holders benefit from having their booked parking space reserved until kick-off, avoiding the sometimes lengthy search for an available space. Arrival is also easier, as there is no need to pay at the car park entrance.

Borussia Dortmund recommends car sharing to minimise the number of cars travelling to the match. The Bundesliga also offers its own car-sharing platform. The Bundesliga car-sharing platform connects fans who want to travel together to home and away matches, making the journey greener, cheaper and more sociable.
