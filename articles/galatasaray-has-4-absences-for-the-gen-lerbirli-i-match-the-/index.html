Galatasaray Has 4 Absences for the Gençlerbirliği Match! The training camp roster has been announced, with a note on Singo...

TheTools · 17.10.2026 12:47 · source · permalink

Galatasaray has completed its preparations for the match against Gençlerbirliği. The Yellow-and-Red team’s training camp roster has also been announced. Cimbom traveled to Ankara with four players missing. Here are the details...

Galatasaray will face Gençlerbirliği on the road tomorrow evening at 4:00 p.m. in Week 8 of the Trendyol Super League. Lucas Torreira and Ugochukwu, who are serving red-card suspensions, were not included in the squad. Additionally, Singo, who is still injured, will not be able to play in the match. Goalkeeper Günay Güvenç was also not included in the squad.

Preparations are complete

Galatasaray completed preparations for tomorrow’s away match against Gençlerbirliği in Week 8 of the Trendyol Super League with a training session. The training session, held at the Kemerburgaz Metin Oktay Facilities under Head Coach Okan Buruk, began with dynamic warm-ups and continued with 8-on-2 passing drills in two groups. The session concluded with tactical drills. Having completed their preparations, the Yellow-and-Reds will head to Ankara.

101st Meeting

Galatasaray and Gençlerbirliği have faced each other 100 times in the Super League to date. In those matches, the Yellow-Reds emerged victorious 57 times, while the Red-Blacks managed to defeat their opponent 22 times. In 21 matches, there was no winner. In the league, the Istanbul team scored 177 goals, while the Ankara team responded with 104 goals. Last season, Galatasaray won their league matches with scores of 3-2 and 2-1, while Gençlerbirliği defeated their rival 2-0 in the Ziraat Turkish Cup.
