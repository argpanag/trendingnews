With Grand Theft Auto VI just days away from its release, fans are getting their first look at the long-awaited video game's all-star soundtrack.

Photos and videos of the vinyl edition of Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album have surfaced on social media in recent days, revealing its full 34-song tracklist across two records. Sources close to the project have confirmed the tracklist to Billboard.

The collection features a genre-spanning lineup of artists, including Playboi Carti, Dua Lipa, Cardi B, Lana Del Rey, Gunna, Yung Lean, Future, Metro Boomin, Travis Scott, Fred again.., PinkPantheress, Morgan Wallen, Tame Impala, Fuerza Regida and more. Several of the songs are already available on streaming services.

Announced in September by Atlantic Records and Rockstar Games, Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album is set to arrive Nov. 19, the same day the game launches on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

The album's music is designed to capture the energy and atmosphere of Vice City and Leonida, the fictional locations featured in the game. It also continues the Grand Theft Auto franchise's long-standing tradition of introducing players to new music and emerging musical subcultures.

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album will be available through several retailers, including Blood Records, Best Buy, Walmart, Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Target and Urban Outfitters, as well as independent record stores. Each retailer will offer an exclusive vinyl pressing featuring its own unique design and poster.

In addition to the soundtrack, the game will feature several radio stations hosted and curated by artists including Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey, Morgan Wallen, Trina and Trick Daddy, Burna Boy and Robyn.

Beyond the album, Grand Theft Auto VI will be celebrated with a music festival at Miami's Mana Wynwood on Nov. 21. Dubbed Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival, the event will feature performances from Cardi B, Travis Scott, Rauw Alejandro and more, including a Cactus Jack set featuring Scott, Don Toliver, SoFaygo and a guest appearance by Kodak Black.
