Beignets Move Locations, Scat Cat's Club Closing for Multi-Month Refurbishment at Port Orleans - French Quarter - WDWNT

TheTools · 04.09.2026 12:20 · source · permalink

Scat Cat’s Club at Disney’s Port Orleans Resort – French Quarter will close for a multi-month refurbishment starting in October. During the closure, beignets will move to a new location.

Scat Cat’s Club Refurbishment

Both the café and lounge will be closed beginning on October 6. The café is expected to reopen in December, and the lounge is expected to reopen in January 2027.

The café previously went through refurbishment in 2024.

During the closure, a temporary bar will be available near the café from 5 p.m. to 11 p.m.

Don’t worry — you’ll still be able to get beignets. Beignets and coffee will be available at Sassagoula Floatworks & Food Factory during the refurbishment. The seasonal Ghoulish Delight beignets will also be there through November 1.
