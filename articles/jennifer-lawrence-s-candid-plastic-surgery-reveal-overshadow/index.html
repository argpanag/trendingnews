While some users celebrated Lawrence's candid approach to plastic surgery, others admitted the actress was "unrecognizable" in the photo spread.

In the profile published Tuesday, Lawrence confessed that part of the reason she wants to look her best has to do with her children.

"I want to be a sexy mom," she said. "At drop-off, I want to look like a woman that has her s--- together even when I’ve just woken up and don’t have my s--- together."

Lawrence and husband Cooke Maroney have two sons together: Cy, four, and a 19-month-old boy whose name has not yet been publicly disclosed.

She told The New Yorker in October 2025 that she was strongly considering getting a facelift, after previously admitting to dabbling in other cosmetic procedures in a Vogue interview.
