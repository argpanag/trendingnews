Nitto ATP Finals 2026 Andrea Bocelli Performance | Nitto ATP Finals | Tennis

TheTools · 22.11.2026 18:24 · source · permalink

Alexander Zverev and Carlos Alcaraz were not the only world-renowned stars to step onto centre court Sunday at the Nitto ATP Finals.

Prior to the championship-match clash between No. 1 player in the PIF ATP Rankings Zverev and No. 3 Alcaraz, celebrated Italian tenor Andrea Bocelli delivered a rousing musical prelude to the tennis at Turin’s Inalpi Arena.

Bocelli performed Con Te Partirò (Time To Say Goodbye) and Alla Gioia (Ode to Joy) as part of a spine-tingling pre-final show, setting the scene for the championship-match showdown. Bocelli has graced some of the globe’s most prestigious stages across generations and he has now added an appearance at the Nitto ATP Finals to his list.

With the Italian crowd suitably warmed up after witnessing a performance by a national treasure and global icon, top seed Zverev and second seed Alcaraz stepped into Inalpi Arena for the Nitto ATP Finals championship match.
