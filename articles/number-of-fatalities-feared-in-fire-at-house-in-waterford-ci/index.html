Number of fatalities feared in fire at house in Waterford City

TheTools · 13.10.2026 07:34 · source · permalink

Gardaí have said a number of people are feared dead following a fire at a domestic residence in Waterford City.

Gardaí and other emergency services are currently at the scene in the Birchwood estate.

A Garda spokesman said it was “an ongoing evolving incident” and no further details are available yet.
