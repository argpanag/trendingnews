Aaron Rodgers is going out with a bang.

Entering what he calls his final season in the NFL, the Pittsburgh Steelers QB took aim at former girlfriend, Danica Patrick, this week in a sit-down with teammate Cam Heyward.

"I dated Danica. She had just retired. She was depressed and not fun to be around. But I didn’t leave her because I was trying to be there and be a good partner. Then it was going a certain direction obviously. There was no intimacy; there was nothing," Rodgers said in the second part of the interview, which dropped Thursday morning.

"She tells a story that she was so blindsided and getting left by you, and that he leaves a trail of blood around him. And I’m like, what are you talking about? That’s not what happened."
