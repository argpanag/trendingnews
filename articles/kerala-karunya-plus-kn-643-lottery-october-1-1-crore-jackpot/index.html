Kerala Lottery Karunya Plus KN-643 draw will be held on Thursday, October 1, with the first prize of ₹1 crore up for grabs. The weekly lottery draw is scheduled for 3 p.m. at Gorky Bhavan near Bakery Junction in Thiruvananthapuram, according to the Kerala State Lotteries' official schedule.

The Karunya Plus lottery is conducted every Thursday by the Kerala State Lotteries Department. Each ticket costs ₹50, while the KN-643 draw offers a ₹30 lakh second prize and ₹5 lakh third prize. The official prize structure also includes several lower-tier awards and a ₹5,000 consolation prize.

Participants can check the winning numbers after the draw through the official Kerala lottery results system. The results are also published through the department's lottery information system, where previous draw results and upcoming lottery schedules are listed.

The KN-643 prize structure has nine main prize categories in addition to the consolation prize. The first three prizes are common to all series, while the lower prizes are decided based on the specified last four digits.

The official Kerala lottery information system confirms that KN-643 is the Karunya Plus draw scheduled for October 1 at 3 p.m. at Gorky Bhavan, near Bakery Junction, Thiruvananthapuram.

Once the draw is completed, ticket holders should verify their numbers against the official result published by the Kerala State Lotteries Department. The department's results portal provides downloadable result documents for completed draws.

Previous Karunya Plus draws show the weekly nature of the lottery. The preceding draw, KN-642, was held on September 24, while KN-643 is scheduled for October 1. The department has also listed KN-644 for October 8.

Winners should verify their ticket against the official result before proceeding with a claim. The original winning ticket and prescribed identification and claim documents are required, and prize claims must be made within the applicable deadline under Kerala's lottery rules.

For prizes above the applicable office-level limit, winners are required to submit the ticket and supporting documents through the designated lottery authorities or authorised banking channels. Winners should check the latest instructions issued by the Kerala State Lotteries Department before submitting a claim.

Ticket holders should rely on the official Kerala State Lotteries result document for final verification of KN-643 winning numbers. The draw schedule confirms that the Karunya Plus KN-643 draw is set for 3 p.m. on October 1 at Gorky Bhavan in Thiruvananthapuram.
