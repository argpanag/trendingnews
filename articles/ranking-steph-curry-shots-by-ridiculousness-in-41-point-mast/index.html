Ranking Steph Curry Shots by Ridiculousness in 41-Point Masterpiece vs. Magic

TheTools · 08.11.2026 05:25 · source · permalink

In this story:

Stephen Curry is known for being a tough shot-maker, but trust me when I say Saturday's 41-point game was outrageous even for him.

Curry went 13-of-19 from the field and 5-of-10 from three, and like any big game from any great player, some of the makes were conversions from great looks.

But a few were simply ridiculous.

Below, I've ranked five shots he made by how ridiculous they were. In other words, they are ranked by degree of difficulty.

I'm sure this looks difficult enough from the angle above. Jamal Cain plays great defense and yet somehow Curry finds an angle to bank it home.

But from other replays (not shown here), it looks even more impressive that he had the perfect amount of English while he was falling away from the basket.

After the game, Kerr called it an "impossible shot" and added, "I don't know if anybody else in the world could make that shot."

The shot clock was running down, and Curry had the 6'7" Cain draped all over him. His only choice was this rainbow deep two—did it go directly over the backboard?—that had no business going in.

Final Thoughts

Curry broke the record for the most 40-point games for a player over 35 years old. He had his since 16th such game Saturday, breaking the tie he had with LeBron James.

These scoring bonanzas are supposed to be incredibly rare for players in their late 30s, but Curry has two 40-plus-point games already this season.

The 38-year-old is showing no signs of slowing down, which is a testament to how hard he works to keep himself in peak physical shape.
