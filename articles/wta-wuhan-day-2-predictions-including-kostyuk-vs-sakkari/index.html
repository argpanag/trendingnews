WTA Wuhan Day 2 Predictions Including Kostyuk vs Sakkari

TheTools · 12.10.2026 18:04 · source · permalink

With places at the season-ending championships still on the line, there should be plenty of entertaning tennis on day two at WTA Wuhan and, as always, we here at LWOT will be offering our predictions for every match on the slate in China, including Sorana Cirstea vs Hanyu Guo and Yulia Putintseva vs Zeynep Sonmez. But who will reach the second round?

WTA Wuhan Day 2 Predictions

Liudmila Samsonova vs Katerina Siniakova

Head-to-head: Samsonova 2-2 Siniakova

After a relatively disappointing season by her standards, Liudmila Samsonova has four wins in her last six matches. She’s hit form at the right time. In contrast, Katerina Siniakova’s recent results don’t reflect how well she has played. She’s lost her last three matches against a current or former top 10 player but has generally acquitted herself well in defeat. The Czech is crafty enough to make this uncomfortable for Samsonova, but the Russian’s bigger serve and heavier groundstrokes should give her a clear edge at WTA Wuhan.

Prediction: Samsonova in 2

Katie Boulter vs Diana Shnaider

Head-to-head: Shnaider 2-0 Boulter

Katie Boulter has fared poorly since that semifinal run at Queen’s Club. She has won just three matches in the last four months. And this is a horrible matchup for the former-British #1. Boulter loves to set up the forehand and control matches, but Diana Shnaider is unlikely to give her room to dictate. The Russian lefty dominated during their match in Hong Kong. Expect more of the same in their second meeting on the surface with Shnaider very much the favourite to advance at WTA Wuhan.

Prediction: Shnaider in 2

Mayar Sherif vs Maja Chwalinska

Head-to-head: Sherif 1-1 Chwalinska

This is a complete change of scenery for Mayar Sherif. At this time last year, the Egyptian was trekking on the South American clay to compete in lower-level WTA events as she sought to rebuild her ranking and her confidence. It clearly worked, with the Egyptian previously having missed most of the Asian swing but having been more or less a fixture in 2026. Maja Chwalinska is also a clay-court expert, but the Pole has more variety in her game that makes her an all-surface threat. She should have the edge here at WTA Wuhan.

Prediction: Chwalinska in 2

Marta Kostyuk vs Maria Sakkari

Head-to-head: Kostyuk 2-2 Sakkari

Marta Kostyuk sits outside the Top 8 in the WTA Finals race. The Ukrainian has lost her bearings at just the wrong time of the season. She suffered a costly early defeat in Guadalajara and was forced to withdraw from Beijing because of a persistent wrist injury. At this point, the injury seems to limit Kostyuk’s ability to accelerate and generate pace off the forehand. I think Sakkari could take advantage of that uncertainty. The Greek was clinical in dismissing Donna Vekic in her opener, and if she sustains that intensity here, she can test Kostyuk physically.

Prediction: Sakkari in 3
