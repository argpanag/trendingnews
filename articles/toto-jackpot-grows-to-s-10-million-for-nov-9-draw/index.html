Toto jackpot grows to S$10 million for Nov. 9 draw

TheTools · 09.11.2026 15:44 · source · permalink

It's a big one.

November 08, 2026, 07:10 PM

Photos from Xin Hao/Google Maps and Singapore Pools website

A lucky winner could walk away with about S$10 million at the Nov. 9 Toto draw.

The jackpot prize snowballed after the last three draws produced no Group 1 winners.

The amount increased from almost S$1.2 million on Oct. 29, to S$2.9 million on Nov. 2, to almost S$5.6 on Nov. 5.

A cascade draw was then triggered for the next one at 9:30pm on Nov. 9.

It is the fifth time so far in 2026 that the jackpot has hit S$10 million, according to AsiaOne.

The previous year saw at least 12 cascade draws.

The prize will only snowball up to the fourth consecutive draw.

This means that if the Nov. 9 continues not to see a Group 1 winner, the final sum will be split equally among the winners of the next prize group.
