Terence Crawford makes fight plea to Floyd Mayweather after return decision

TheTools · 05.11.2026 17:22 · source · permalink
  • Ben Davies, Boxing and MMA Editor
  • Published: 17:22, 5 Nov 2026
  • Updated: 17:22, 5 Nov 2026

Terence Crawford has now suggested to Floyd Mayweather that the pair both come out of retirement and fight in 2027.

Crawford has been adamant that he wouldn't fight again after bowing out on top with a stunning undisputed title victory over Canelo Alvarez last September.

But 'Bud' has indicated he would now open the door to a potential return if the opportunity presented is correct.

'Bud' initially called out WBC welterweight champion Ryan Garcia for a clash, with the pair having gone back and forth on social media.

But now Crawford has suggested he'd be open to facing 50-0 boxing legend Mayweather, who is already in talks to return to the sport.

He wrote on social media: "There’s a reason they want me to come back.

"They had you all fooled thinking I couldn’t sell and wasn’t marketable.

"Now look I’m the ticket. F**k it Floyd, me and you would crash everything.

"GOAT vs GOAT - F**k it."

Any showdown between the pair would likely be the biggest commercial fight in boxing despite their age.

The prospect of the pair putting their undefeated records on the line in a legend vs legend match-up would likely sell, particularly on American shores.

Crawford is clearly gauging interest in a return, having also taken aim at Mayweather's former protege Gervonta Davis.

Could Mayweather vs Crawford actually happen?

Any showdown would likely require a record-breaking financial package to seal the pair's meeting in the ring.

Zuffa boxing chief Dana White and Turki Alalshikh have suggested they would be interested in bringing Crawford back.

However, Garcia may be a more realistic option with Mayweather yet to compete professionally since his win over Conor McGregor in 2017.

Mayweather has signed up to exhibition fights and announced his latest would take place in Thailand against Matt Floyd in December.

But his comeback plans are up in the air, having previously signed up to face Mike Tyson and risk his professional record against Manny Pacquiao.

He has also been in talks with Garcia himself, as the superstar pair have agreed to a bout in public.
