Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens from the Champions League, as Roma host Slovan Bratislava and Napoli try to get off the mark with Bodo/Glimt.

They both kick off at 20.00 UK time (21.00 CEST).

Italian football badly needs to turn the corner in the Champions League, as last week all four teams were defeated.

Roma fumbled a 3-1 lead to lose 4-3 to Real Madrid, and they seek their first win against Slovan Bratislava.

Napoli lost both games so far and are struggling in Serie A too as they welcome giant-killers Bodo/Glimt.
