  15. Why are people complaining about frame rate – and what is it?published at 08:25 GMT
    08:25 GMT

    Tom Gerken
    Technology reporter

    If you’re not particularly nerdy about gaming, you may have been left a bit confused by all the chatter about FPS - frames per second, also known as frame rate.

    As the name suggests, it’s the number of still frames a game displays per second. But really, it’s all about smoothness.

    Things look smoother at higher frame rates, so while a game running at 30 FPS looks great, one at 60 FPS is ultra-smooth. Some people with very powerful PCs even play games at 120 FPS.

    So, with higher frame rates now the standard, there’s been noticeable disappointment that GTA 6 is limited to just 30 FPS, which seems a bit archaic.

    In truth, most people won’t notice this. But it does mean the game isn’t as smooth as it could be, because it’s simply too demanding on modern hardware.

    And if you too are seething with jealousy that my colleague Joe gets to play the game, I’m with you - I’ll have to wait until this evening, like many of you reading this!

  17. 'I don't think anyone could have imagined the success of the series,' says GTA 3 artistpublished at 08:07 GMT
    08:07 GMT

    Freya Scott-Turner
    Live reporter

    "I love the new artwork, it's amazing," says Stephen Bliss. He's the artist behind GTA 3's iconic cover and worked on the later games up to GTA 5.

    "I don't think anyone could have imagined the success [of the GTA series]," he says, speaking to me from New York.

    His GTA 3 cover became something of a blueprint for the series, which has replicated the comic strip style in all subsequent games. But it wasn't plain sailing making it.

    "I'd finished painting [the original cover], it took a long time and then 9/11 happened," he explains. "That was terrifying."

    He remembers thinking: "There's no way there will ever be suggestions of violence [in video games] again."

    So he got to work designing new artwork, removing all the violent images, guns and explosions.

    And the result was the cover we know today.

  19. GTA owner's shares jump following game releasepublished at 07:36 GMT
    07:36 GMT

    Osmond Chia

    The GTA buzz has spilled on to Wall Street, lifting shares in parent company Take-Two Interactive as investors bet that strong reviews of the new game will boost earnings.
