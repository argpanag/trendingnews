Barefoot to boots: Kipgen’s long wait ends with debut goal for India vs Pakistan in SAFF Championship 2026

On the streets of Manipur, or sometimes at home, a toddler plays barefoot before school. The dream is simple: to play for India one day, in the senior team. When he finally did, Manglenthang Kipgen made it count, scoring on his senior India debut against arch-rival Pakistan in the 2026 SAFF Championship.

Receiving a pass from PN Noufal in the 32nd minute, he beat Yousuf Butt one-on-one to score India’s third goal in the tournament’s opening match.

Kipgen’s journey to glory, however, was never a bed of roses. Having spent over half a decade at Punjab FC’s academy, he saw players like Abhishek Singh Tekcham share the dressing room with him and move into the senior team well before him.

But he waited, and had his family as his biggest support.

“I started at a young age, around six. Before I went to school or while at home, I played football barefoot every time,” he had told Sportstar in an earlier interview.

“I’ve got a lot of support from my parents. They say they are proud of me, but I am the one who is proudest of them because they helped me a lot. When I don’t play well or have bad games, they encourage me a lot,” he added.

At the 2023 U-19 SAFF Championship, Kipgen had struggled to get into the main team. He got only eight minutes in the first two games, against Bangladesh and Bhutan, respectively.

“I was not getting a chance in the starting XI. But my brothers and parents encouraged me a lot not to give up,” Kipgen, who is the youngest among seven brothers and one sister, says.

File photo: Manglenthang Kipgen scored the winner as India began its SAFF Championship 2026 campaign with a 3-2 win against Pakistan. | Photo Credit: Punjab FC

In the final, he scored a brace in 21 minutes, helping India clinch the title with a 2-1 win. The opponent: Pakistan.

Kipgen noted that former star striker Sunil Chhetri’s longevity is a big motivation as he takes his first steps with the Indian team.

“I obviously look up to Chhetri bhai because he encouraged me a lot, and the picture of him playing at over 40 years of age keeps motivating me,” Kipgen said. Incidentally, Chhetri grabbed a hat-trick when the Blue Tigers faced Pakistan in the SAFF Championship three years ago.

Moreover, Kipgen’s goal on Thursday proved decisive as Pakistan’s late comeback attempt fell short in India’s 3-2 win.

“They (his family) used to say, ‘When your time comes, you have to give your best,’ and I did,” Kipgen said. And, against Pakistan, he did — in 2023 and repeated it in 2026.

India faces the Maldives six days later, and the young boy from Manipur, then playing barefoot, hopes he has his scoring boots with him. “I will try to give my best for my country,” he said.
