Cher's Miami Beach Mansion Profit: A Real Estate Success Story

TheTools · 24.10.2026 23:00 · source · permalink

    Cher's iconic Miami Beach mansion, originally acquired for $1.5 million, experienced remarkable appreciation, selling for $4.35 million just three years later. By 2020, it re-entered the market and fetched a staggering $17 million, following several attempts at higher listings. This transformation exemplifies the allure of celebrity homes, whose unique charm and history make them attractive investment assets.

    Cher, the singer later owned a waterfront mansion on Miami Beach’s La Gorce Island, which she bought in 1993 and sold three years later for nearly triple the purchase price (Image credit: Wikimedia Commons)
