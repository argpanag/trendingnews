Potential for flooding in south with yellow rain and wind warnings in effect

TheTools · 29.09.2026 20:36 · source · permalink

There are status yellow weather warnings for both wind and rain in place until 9pm for counties Cork, Kerry and Waterford, with potential for flooding, Met Éireann has said.

The wind warning is in effect for all of Munster as well as counties Galway, Mayo and Sligo in Connacht.

The forecaster said there will be “very strong and gusty southeasterly winds” in those regions.

Potential impacts include wave overtopping and flooding on coasts due to a combination of spring tides, surge and very strong onshore winds.

There could also be difficult travelling conditions, fallen trees and branches, as well as debris and loose objects.

The yellow rain warning for Cork, Kerry and Waterford means those counties will see “spells of heavy rain” with the potential for localised flooding, difficult travelling conditions, and poor visibility.

Later, a status yellow warning for rain will come into effect for counties Wexford and Wicklow from 9pm until 4am in the early hours of Wednesday.

Once again, spells of heavy rain are expected, which could lead to localised flooding, difficult travelling conditions, and poor visibility.

As for marine warnings, there is a status yellow gale warning in effect from Roches Point, Co Cork, to Slyne Head, Co Galway, and Fair Head, Co Antrim, until 6pm. Southeast winds will reach gale force 8 or strong gale force 9.

After 6pm, the gale warning is extended to all coasts of Ireland until midnight. Southeast winds will reach gale force 8 at times.

There is a small craft warning in place for all coasts of Ireland until midnight.

More generally, the weather on Tuesday night is to be wet for a time across eastern and northeastern parts, with an ongoing chance of localised flooding at first tonight.

Drier and clearer conditions to the south and west will gradually extend to all parts overnight, with scattered showers following from the Atlantic.

Strong and gusty southeasterly winds will ease light to moderate southerly overland as the rain clears, but winds will continue fresh to strong along coasts. Temperatures will range from 10 to 14 degrees.

Wednesday will be a bright, fresh day with a mix of sunny spells and scattered showers. Some of the showers will be heavy with a chance of isolated thunderstorms.

Temperatures will range from 15 to 18 degrees. There will be moderate southerly winds, which will be fresher for a time in the west and northwest.
