Bristol City predicted team for Watford as changes likely with 'tired legs'

TheTools · 18.09.2026 05:00 · source · permalink

For one last time before the international break, it is matchday. Bristol City kick off the footballing weekend with their first Friday night football of the season, as the Robins host Watford at Ashton Gate this evening.

Michael Skubala's side's meeting with the Hornets comes mere days after perhaps the most frustrating night of the 43-year-old's tenure so far. City suffered successive league defeats for the first time this term as they were beaten 1-0 by Lincoln City on Tuesday evening.

Unlike the Reds, Watford were able to enjoy a midweek without football. The Hornets have had a clear week in the run-up to tonight's contest, but it will likely have been a spell of disappointment after last weekend's defeat to Stoke City.

Alessio Dionisi's side suffered their third league loss of the campaign as they were beaten 2-1 by the Potters at Vicarage Road. Goals from Jack McGlynn and Eric Bocat either side of the interval ensured that Amin Nabizada's stoppage-time strike counted for very little.

Just two points and four places separate the Hornets and City after seven matches this term. On paper, it should be a good contest between two sides aiming to enter the extended international break on a positive note.

The Reds' chances of getting back to winning ways have been hit slightly by the news that Rob Dickie is set for a spell on the sidelines. Though it is not yet clear how long the Robins will be without the defender, he is certainly not expected to be involved this evening.

While the rest of Skubala's squad - outside of long-term absentee Luke McNally and new signing Brian De Keersmaecker - are said to be fit and healthy, the City boss did confirm there are a few "tired legs" at the High Performance Centre given the couple of quick turnarounds this week.

With that in mind, it feels like some changes are likely tonight. Here is how we think the Reds will line up against Watford...

Goalkeeper and defence

While he wasn't the Bristol City player to have the biggest hand in Lincoln City's winner on Tuesday evening, Sam Tickle did have a bit of a part to play in what Michael Skubala described as a "sloppy" goal and a "gift."

Did the Robins' number one need to try and play out from the back in that moment? Not really. While he could do little in the moments that followed Rob Dickie's backpass, the damage was already done. That being said, he is City's first-choice stopper, and we don't expect that to change tonight.

Tickle has, outside of one or two moments, started well in red and white. It would feel unnecessarily harsh to drop him and start Brad Collins this evening after what was, at worst, a slight error in judgement.

Ahead of the 24-year-old, the loss of Dickie to injury leaves a large hole to fill. In our mind, the best way in which Skubala can attempt to navigate life without his vice-captain is by handing Noah Eile his second start of the season, and his first in a central defensive role.

The Swede is the only right-footed option at the City boss' disposal and, particularly in possession, that different skillset will be valuable. However, he is still relatively new to the Championship and, as a result, our bet is that he will be partnered by the experienced Rob Atkinson, with Lisav Eissat dropping to the bench.

Such a pairing will give the Reds a left-footed defender, a right-footed defender, an aerially dominant centre-back and a quicker, more mobile centre-back. Eissat could start at left-back, of course, where he featured against Millwall, but we think Rio Cardines will get the nod there in order to afford Cam Pring a rest.

On the opposite side of the back four, that will leave space for George Tanner to return to the side. He impressed at Southampton last weekend and will be vital against a Watford team who are excellent in transition.

Midfield

It feels almost nailed on that Joe Williams will return to the Bristol City starting line-up tonight. The 29-year-old met the media yesterday and was rested against Lincoln City with the intention of being ready and able to feature from the off against the Hornets; that would be a strange approach to take if he was named amongst the substitutes.

Our bet is that the former Wigan Athletic man will partner Jason Knight in the middle of the park.

The Ireland international wasn't at his best in midweek but has been one of the Robins' most consistent performers so far this term. The only reason he won't slot in alongside Williams will be if he is to be deployed in a more advanced role, in attacking midfield.

If that is the case, we'd expect Adam Randell to feature ahead of Gibson Yah, purely on the basis that the Dutchman played a full game of Championship football for the first time just a matter of days ago. We think both players will take a place on the bench, however.

That leaves the question of who does start in attacking midfield? Well, our - perhaps slightly brave prediction - is Dom Ballard. Yes, if he is to feature from the off, the 21-year-old will be more like a second striker than a number 10, but putting him in this section just allows us to format the predicted team in a way that makes sense to everyone.

Ballard provided some real energy at the top end of the pitch in the second-half of Tuesday night's defeat. Considering the amount of running Scott Twine has got through in recent weeks, it feels sensible to try and freshen things up.

Attack and striker

As you'll have likely guessed by the section above, we don't have Scott Twine starting for Bristol City this evening. That isn't a reflection of the 27-year-old's performances, more so a prediction made on the basis that he is likely one of those with tired legs after the last week.

That leaves Jed Wallace, Tomi Horvat, Yu Hirakawa, Sam Bell and Sam Greenwood as the players capable of filling the two wide roles in the Robins' system. We wouldn't be shocked if Michael Skubala were to start Wallace and Hirakawa - the pairing that started against Lincoln - but with the intention of freshening things up slightly, we're going to bet that there will be one change made.

Our thinking is that Hirakawa will move over to the right flank, to operate in front of George Tanner, and Sam Greenwood will get the nod on the left. Having started the last three games on the bench, the summer signing should offer a bit more energy than his counterparts.

All that remains then is that single striker position. Given that we expect Dom Ballard to start just off the frontman, we're backing Lorent Tolaj to lead the line once more. Maybe the Swiss-born forward will get a rest after a busy week, but at this moment, we feel he's too important to leave out.

Hopefully, the Reds can get the job done nice and early and introduce Elijah Adebayo in his place at some point in the second half.

Bristol City predicted XI (4-2-3-1): Sam Tickle; George Tanner, Noah Eile, Rob Atkinson; Rio Cardines; Jason Knight, Joe Williams; Yu Hirakawa, Dom Ballard, Sam Greenwood; Lorent Tolaj
