'Nick of time': million dollar lottery win scored in Calgary

TheTools · 06.10.2026 14:39 · source · permalink

By News Staff

Posted Oct 6, 2026 8:39 am.

“The guy told me I won some money.”

The understatement comes from new million dollar lottery winner Julio Benitez.

The lucky High River resident says he recalled having a yet-to-be checked lottery ticket in his pocket “in the nick of time” while filling up gas, according to the Western Canada Lottery Corporation.

While inside the gas station, Benitez says the lottery machine went black “and I thought I broke it. I didn’t understand what was happening, but the guy told me I won some money.”

“I thought maybe $1,000, then $10,000… I couldn’t believe it!” Benitez tells the WCLC, adding “I left the store and jumped and screamed, ‘Thank you, God!'”

Benitez’s plans for the money may sound to modest to some, but he says it’ll make a difference to himself and those closest to him.

“I’m going to pay bills and help my family. My son has a baby on the way. This is great timing. It feels like a dream.”

Benitez won with a LOTTO 6/49 ticket purchased from the Flying J on 40 Street SE in Calgary for the Aug. 22 guaranteed prize draw.

His winning selection was: 30526413-01.
