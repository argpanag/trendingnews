Kentucky Football outlasts South Alabama after close first half

TheTools · 26.09.2026 20:12 · source · permalink

LEXINGTON, Ky. (WKYT) - Kentucky Football triumphed over South Alabama 45-21 after a close first half had the Wildcats on their toes.

Kenny Minchey struggled for most of the game but finished strong, going 19-26 with 177 yards and a fumble. Kentucky’s running game carried the team, with all touchdowns being rushing ones. Jason Patterson was the star of the game, rushing for 137 yards and 3 touchdowns. CJ Baxter rushed for 149 yards and 1 touchdown.

The game was off to a slow start with both teams going scoreless until late in the first quarter. With under one minute left in the quarter, CJ Baxter ran it in to put the first points on the board for Kentucky, 7-0.

The lead did not last long. To start the second quarter, South Alabama marched down the field. The drive ended in PJ Martin scoring a seven-yard rushing touchdown to tie the game, 7-7.

The second quarter continued being active with both teams trading touchdowns. South Alabama took the lead 21-14 with under two minutes to go in the half. Kentucky took the opportunity in the next drive and a 75-yard rushing touchdown from Jason Patterson tied it back up. A late interception let the Wildcats finish the half with a field goal, taking a 24-21 lead going into halftime.

Kentucky started the first half swinging, opening with a quick drive down field ending in Jason Patterson’s second touchdown of the day, bringing the score to 31-21.

Patterson scored his third touchdown of the day in the team’s first drive of the fourth quarter to extend Kentucky’s lead, 38-21.

South Alabama turned it over on downs, setting the Wildcats up for another scoring drive. Kenny Minchey connected with Shane Carr for his first passing touchdown of the game, 45-21.

After leading by only 3 heading into the second half, Kentucky held South Alabama scoreless, leading to the win.

Kentucky heads to Columbia next weekend to face South Carolina at 4:15 p.m. on the SEC Network.
