24-year-old Muay Thai fighter dies in Singapore after tournament

TheTools · 28.09.2026 17:25 · source · permalink

A 24-year-old Muay Thai fighter has died in Singapore after being taken unconscious to hospital following his participation in the AMC 13 Fight for Hope tournament at the National Stadium on September 12. Police confirmed the athlete's death on September 28, Channel NewsAsia reports.

Police investigation

Police received a report about the incident at around 8:00 a.m. on September 13. According to law enforcement, the man was hospitalised unconscious after taking part in the competition the previous day. Police did not identify the deceased or specify the date of his death.

Preliminary investigation findings found no signs of foul play. At the same time, the investigation into the circumstances of the incident is ongoing.

Medical assistance at the tournament

The Singapore Civil Defence Force said it received a call at about 7:05 p.m. on September 12 at 1 Stadium Drive. One person was taken to Tan Tock Seng Hospital.

More current news is available on the UA.News Telegram channel Telegram.

The Singapore Muaythai Association said on September 17 that an incident had occurred during the tournament. According to the association, medical assistance was provided by the medical team at the scene, after which the person was taken to hospital. The association has launched a formal review and is cooperating with the relevant parties to establish the circumstances.

The association later denied claims circulating online that the fighter had been knocked out during the bout. It said both athletes were present when the results were announced and left the ring on their own, while the medical emergency occurred later.

The Fight for Hope tournament was held over three weekends in August and September. It was organised by the Singapore Muaythai Association to raise funds and awareness in support of the Breast Cancer Foundation Singapore.
