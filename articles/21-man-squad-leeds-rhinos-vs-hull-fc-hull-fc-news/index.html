21 Man Squad: Leeds Rhinos vs Hull FC - Hull FC News

TheTools · 09.09.2026 11:00 · source · permalink

Hull FC’s final 21-man squad of the 2026 season is in

Interim Head Coach Andy Last has named his 21-man squad ahead of Friday’s Betfred Super League Round 27 fixture against Leeds Rhinos, in association with Steve Hodgins Plumbing, Heating & Electrical.

Last has made two changes to the squad for the final game of Hull’s 2026 campaign.

Ligi Sao is unavailable after failing a Head Injury Assessment in last week’s match against Toulouse Olympique, whilst Sam Lisone is unavailable due to personal reasons.

Zak Hardaker is set to return to action in what will be his final game in a Hull FC jersey, and James Bell is back available again, passing his return to play protocols following his failed HIA in Round 25.

Alongside Sao and Lisone, also absent for the Black & Whites this week are Will Pryce (ACL), Joe Batchelor (suspension), John Asiata (hamstring), Cade Cust (shoulder), Yusuf Aydin (knee), Brad Fash (knee), Callum Kemp (shoulder), Will Kirby (ACL), and Joe Ward (ACL).
