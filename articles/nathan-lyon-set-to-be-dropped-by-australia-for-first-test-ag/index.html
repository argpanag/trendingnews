Nathan Lyon set to be dropped by Australia for first Test against New Zealand

TheTools · 27.11.2026 07:28 · source · permalink

Australia will reportedly make the big call to drop frontline spinner Nathan Lyon for their opening Test against New Zealand on December 9; Lyon has been Australia's mainstay spin option for 13 years

Friday 27 November 2026 07:06, UK

Nathan Lyon is set to be dropped by Australia for their first Test against New Zealand in Perth, it has been reported.

Australia will name their squad on Saturday for the opening Test in Perth from December 9, but it seems the 39-year-old off-spinner will not be included.

A mainstay in the side, Lyon has been Australia's frontline spin option for over 13 years, taking 577 Test wickets from 145 matches.

Following his hamstring injury, which he suffered in the 2025 Ashes series, Lyon has begun to be more regularly omitted from the Test side.

Most recently, he missed Australia's second Test against South Africa when they opted to go for an all-pace bowling line-up.

In the rest of that tour, Lyon took six wickets at 43.67.

After Australia's loss to South Africa in Cape Town, in which South Africa spinner Simon Harmer took 6-16 in the second innings for victory, head coach Andrew McDonald was asked whether Lyon's position as Australia's continuous No 1 spinner would be assessed.

Lyon took four wickets in the game.

"I don't think it's something to review now," McDonald said. "We sit back and we'll go through Shield cricket. Nathan will be preparing for the first Test match in Perth."

Remarkably, until Australia's Test against West Indies in Jamaica in 2025, Lyon had not been left out of a squad since making his debut 15 years ago in 2011. He had also not missed a Test when available and fit since 2013.
