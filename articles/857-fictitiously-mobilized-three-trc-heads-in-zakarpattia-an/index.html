857 fictitiously mobilized: three TRC heads in Zakarpattia and Bukovina notified of suspicion

TheTools · 27.09.2026 01:16 · source · permalink

Under the procedural guidance of specialized prosecutor?s offices in the defence sector of the Western region, three heads of district TRC and SS — the current and former one in Zakarpattia and the former one in Bukovina — have been notified of suspicion; regarding two more former heads of RTRC in Chernivtsi region, the indictments have already been sent to court.

The Office of the Prosecutor General reports this.

On Friday, the agency informs in its Telegram channel that in December 2025 – February 2026, officials entered into the “Oberih” system data on at least 857 fictitiously mobilized persons, artificially inflating the indicators of fulfillment of mobilization plans.

“Among them were citizens who did not appear at the TRC and were not conscripted, active servicemen, as well as non-existent persons,” the prosecutor’s office notes.

Thus, according to the report, 54 such cases were identified in Zakarpattia. “One of the heads of the RTRC entered data on 8 actually non-mobilized citizens, and the former acting head of another TRC entered data on another 46,” the agency clarifies.

In Bukovina, according to the Office of the Prosecutor General, 803 such cases were identified.

“One of the colonels, according to the investigation, entered data on 631 non-existent citizens into ‘Oberih.’ Two more former heads of RTRC registered 99 and 73 persons as mobilized, respectively. Among them were also fictitious citizens and active servicemen,” the report says.

The officials’ actions were qualified under Articles 362 and 366 of the Criminal Code of Ukraine.

The court imposed preventive measures on three officials from Chernivtsi region.

Regarding the head of the RTRC from Zakarpattia, prosecutors filed a corresponding motion with the court.
