The Lakers assigned first-round rookie Cameron Carr to G League's Coachella Valley Lakers as he works back from an oblique injury and will miss his fourth consecutive NBA game vs. Portland on Tuesday.

The Los Angeles Lakers have assigned rookie guard Cameron Carr to the Coachella Valley Lakers of the G League, the team announced Monday.

Carr, the No. 24 overall pick in the 2026 NBA Draft, has yet to make his NBA debut after being sidelined by an oblique injury.

The injury also kept him out of the end of the preseason and the Lakers' early regular-season games.

Head coach JJ Redick recently described Carr as day-to-day, making the G League assignment a possible step toward getting the 21-year-old back into game shape.

Carr did appear in two preseason games before the injury, averaging 12.0 points, 1.5 rebounds, and 1.0 assist while shooting 47.1% from the field and 40% from deep.

Carr was originally selected by the New York Knicks with the 24th overall pick before his draft rights were traded to Los Angeles in exchange for Sergio de Larrea.

His athleticism and perimeter scoring were already on display during the Summer League, where he produced games of 18, 23, 14, and 17 points for Los Angeles.

Even once healthy, Carr will have to fight through significant perimeter depth to earn NBA minutes.

Austin Reaves is entrenched as one of the Lakers' starting guards alongside Luka Doncic, while Quentin Grimes, Matisse Thybulle, and Collin Sexton are already established in the backcourt rotation.

At small forward and on the wing, Carr is competing with Ziaire Williams, Jake LaRavia, and Adou Thiero, while players such as Jaden Hardy, Bronny James, and Chris Manon provide further guard depth.

The Lakers started the new season 3-0 and face Portland on Tuesday. Carr will miss this game as well.
