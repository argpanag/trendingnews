EFL club accused of ordering player back early risk FIFA sanction

TheTools · 06.10.2026 22:15 · source · permalink
  • Lee Davey
  • Published: 23:15, 6 Oct 2026
  • Updated: 23:15, 6 Oct 2026

QPR face a possible FIFA sanction after midfielder Glen Kamara returned to the club from international duty earlier than expected.

The Football Association of Finland has claimed that the Championship club withdrew Kamara before Tuesday's Nations League game against Belarus.

FIFA rules state a player released for international duty must be available to their nation for the entire international break, unless an agreement is in place for an early return.

In addition, any withdrawn player cannot play for their club five days after the end of the window, without the consent of the national team association.

Quoted on the Finnish FA’s website, head coach Jacob Friis said: "Glen was in a difficult situation as his club insisted he leave the camp and return to London.

"He had to make a decision that was obviously not what we wanted."

Marco Casagrande, general secretary of the Finnish FA said QPR had ordered Kamara to leave the camp prior to the clash with Belarus.

He added that the west London club had even organised a flight to return him to London with Friday’s crunch Championship clash at West Ham, live on talkSPORT 2, in mind.

However, Kamara is now at risk of suspension for the game at the London Stadium after the Finnish FA filed a complaint to FIFA.

Finland chief points finger at QPR

Casagrande said: “It’s really unfortunate that he’s being put in a situation where he’s practically forced to make a really difficult choice due to pressure from the club.

“The player is in a difficult position. We regret it and, of course, understand it.

“The regulations are very clear, and in our view, they were not fully followed in this case.

“FIFA will definitely be informed. The player has not been available for the entire international window, as the regulations say he should be.

“After that, it’s up to FIFA how they proceed with that matter.”

QPR boss Julien Stephan confirmed that the club are in dialogue with the Finnish FA over the situation.

“There is dialogue between the club and Finland federation which I am not involved in,” Stephan said this week.

“I will know in two or three days what the exact situation is with Glen for Friday’s game.”

Kamara has started three of QPR’s eight Championships games this season while coming off the bench on four occasions.

The 30-year-old, who has clocked up 72 caps for Finland, was an unused substitute in the first of his country’s Nations League fixtures – a 7-0 win against San Marino.

He played 45 minutes in each of the subsequent matches which saw Finland draw 0-0 with Belarus before edging past Albania 2-1.
