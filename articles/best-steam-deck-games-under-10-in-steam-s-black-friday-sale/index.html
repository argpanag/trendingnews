Best Steam Deck games under $10 in Steam's Black Friday Sale

TheTools · 26.11.2026 23:34 · source · permalink

Black Friday sales are now going live across different websites and retailers, and Steam has also kicked off its own special Black Friday Sale. The event features discounts on thousands of games across a wide range of genres, with some titles receiving discounts of up to 90%. This makes it a great opportunity to expand your Steam library without spending too much.

If you're a Steam Deck owner looking to try some new games on your handheld, there are plenty of solid compatible titles included in the sale. However, given the sheer number of games currently discounted, finding the ones worth spending your money on can take some time. 

To make things easier for you, we've compiled a list of some of the best Steam Deck-compatible games available for under $10 during Steam's Black Friday Sale. All of the games mentioned below also carry a Verified tag. 

  • GRIS - $2.99 (80% off)
  • Little Nightmares - $4.99 (75% off)
  • Enter the Gungeon - $2.99 (80% off)
  • Celeste - $4.99 (75% off)
  • It Takes Two - $7.99 (80% off)
  • Heavy Rain - $1.99 (90% off)
  • Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor - $6.49 (50% off)
  • Blasphemous 2 - $7.49 (75% off)
  • Detroit: Become Human - $3.99 (90% off)
  • Hades - $6.24 (75% off)

That said, the Black Friday Sale will remain live until November 30, so you have a few days to take advantage of the discounts. However, once the event ends, you won't have to wait too long for another opportunity to save money, as the Steam Winter Sale is scheduled to kick off just a few weeks later, on December 17.
