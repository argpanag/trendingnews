Manchester City x C.P. Company 26-27 Fourth Kit Revealed - Debut in Champions League

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:08 · source · permalink
  • Puma
  • Manchester City
  • Premier League
  • C.P. Company
  • Kits
  • Kit Debut and Usage: The Manchester City x C.P. Company 26-27 Fourth Kit made its debut in a Champions League match on November 24, 2026, and is intended for use in select Champions League fixtures.
  • Distinctive Design: The kit features a striking orange main color with black logos, drawing inspiration from C.P. Company's technical outerwear with subtle tonal shading, sharp white lines, and a collaborative crest.
  • Global Release Information: The kit will be available for global sale starting December 1, 2026, with an exclusive presale event scheduled for fans on November 30.

Update: Tuesday, November 24, 2026: Manchester City is right now wearing their Puma C.P. Company 4th kit against Napoli. The military green variant is not worn, but the standard keeper kit. Oddly, Manchester City wears the goalkeeper socks and not special socks.

The CP Company Manchester City kit will be worn for select fixtures in the Champions League, and forms part of a wider collection that will be on sale globally from Tuesday 1 December. There will also be an exclusive presale event for fans on Monday 30 November.

Manchester City 26-27 Fourth Kit

Check out the Puma x C.P. Company Manchester City FC 26-27 fourth shirt below.

The new Puma x C.P. Company Manchester City 2026-2027 fourth shirt features an orange main color, combined with black for logos. It is a striking look that has nothing to do with the traditional sky blue kits of Manchester City.

Drawing direct influence from C.P. Company's technical outerwear, the fabric boasts subtle, irregular tonal shading across the surface reminiscent of the Italian brand's signature garment-dyeing process. There are also dynamic, sharp white scratch-like lines slashing across the shoulders and sleeves, bringing an edgy, deconstructed look to the silhouette.

Puma combines this orange aesthetic with black and white. All logos are black.

One main piece of the kit is the collaborative Manchester City crest on the left chest. Styled to emulate C.P. Company’s iconic black rubberized lens and watch-viewer hardware, the badge features an orange-and-black club crest framed by "C.P. COMPANY" curved along the top and "PUMA" across the bottom.
