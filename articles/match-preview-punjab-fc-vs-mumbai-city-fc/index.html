Match Preview: Punjab FC vs Mumbai City FC

TheTools · 18.10.2026 14:24 · source · permalink

Mumbai City will take on Punjab FC in their second fixture of the MG Indian Super League 2026-27 season at the JLN Stadium in Delhi on 19 October, as the Islanders look to bounce back.

With the opening-day result behind them, Mumbai City face a tough test against a side that has started the season well. Head Coach Manolo Márquez spoke about the mindset in the camp and the work done since the last game.

“We lost the first game, and we are not happy, but this happens in football, and you need to continue. When you have some days to train, you train to improve for the next game, and this is what we did. It’s not how you start; it’s always how you finish the season,” he said.

Defender Carlos Delgado echoed the Head Coach’s message of unity while acknowledging the quality of the opposition.

“It has not been the best start for us, but in football, everything can happen. We need to be focused on the next game and go step by step because there are a lot of new players, and we need to be together, compact, and focused. Against Punjab, it will be difficult for sure because they have very good players and are a very good team. We need to be ready, and we need to fight with our tools,” he said.

With a blend of experience and young talent in the squad, Mumbai City will look to bring intensity and togetherness to the pitch as they aim for a positive result.

The match will kick off at 7:30 PM on Monday, 19 October 2026, and will be broadcast live on Sony Sports Network and streamed on Sony LIV. Free streaming will also be available on YouTube. Live updates will be available across the club’s official social media handles.

About Mumbai City FC

Mumbai City FC, formed in August 2014, is a club competing in the Indian Super League (ISL), the top tier of Indian football. The club also participates in the Super Cup (since 2017) and the Durand Cup (since 2022). Fondly known as The Islanders, Mumbai City FC won the ISL League and ISL Cup double in the 2020/21 season. The club has also lifted the ISL League Shield in 2022/23 and the ISL Cup in 2023/24, winning an enviable four trophies for the club.

In Asia, Mumbai City FC competed in the AFC Champions League in 2022 and in the 2023/24 seasons becoming the first Indian club to register wins in the continent’s premier club competition.

Mumbai City FC is owned by actor Ranbir Kapoor and businessman Bimal Parekh. The club plays its home matches at the Mumbai Football Arena in Andheri, Mumbai. The club’s crest houses key elements that represent the city of Mumbai and is a reflection of the city’s dynamism and relentless attitude while also reflecting the modern values that define the city and the club.
