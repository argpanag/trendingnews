Traffic gridlock at Malaysia-Singapore causeway after Woodlands blackout

TheTools · 04.11.2026 15:41 · source · permalink

Although all Malaysian customs counters were open, the jam lasted for many hours due to a build-up of vehicles from Singapore

Although all counters were operating at optimum level at the Bangunan Sultan Iskandar Customs, Immigration and Quarantine complex in the southern state of Johor, the jam lasted for many hours due to a build-up of vehicles from the Singapore side.

It is learnt that the issue started at about noon on the Singapore side. A Malaysian home ministry official said the situation was worsened by a truck heading to Singapore experiencing mechanical failure.
