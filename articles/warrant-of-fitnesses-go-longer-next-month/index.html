Warrant of fitnesses go longer next month

TheTools · 08.10.2026 21:06 · source · permalink

From next month a wide range of cars old and new will have time between getting a new Warrant of Fitness extended. 

For new cars their second WoF will not be required for four years, while vehicles aged between 4 and 14 years move from annual to two-yearly check, from November next year. 

For older cars from between the years 2000 and 2013 - they remain at annual checks, but for very old cars from before year 2000 these move from six-monthly checks to one year. 

There are also increases to the amount people are fined for out-of-date WoFs. 

There have been widespread industry concerns raised about the changes, with some expecting an increase in dangerous vehicles on the road. 

Jason Wilson, chief executive at Midas NZ, speaks to Kathryn.
