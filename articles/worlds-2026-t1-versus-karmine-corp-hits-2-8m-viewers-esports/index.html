Worlds 2026: T1 versus Karmine Corp hits 2.8M viewers | Esports Charts

TheTools · 07.11.2026 20:48 · source · permalink

Table of content:

    The semifinal line-up at the League of Legends World Championship 2026 has been confirmed. The last quarterfinal saw T1 face European favorite Karmine Corp in a match that reached 2.8 million Peak Viewers, according to Esports Charts.

    Battle between top regional sides becomes most popular Worlds 2026 match

      The second LoL Worlds 2026 Playoffs game peaked at 2,800,537 viewers   

    The series between T1 and Karmine Corp peaked at 2,800,537 viewers, becoming the most popular match at the 2026 LoL World Championship. No other game had surpassed 2M PV, but T1's presence and its battle with the popular French side Karmine Corp drove audience engagement to new heights.

    Explore our Tournament Dashboard for complete viewership stats, top matches, most-watched teams, and leading community casters.

    However, this peak audience was still lower compared to the last quarterfinal match-up at Worlds 2025. Also involving T1, this series against Anyone's Legend attracted almost 3.92 million peak concurrent viewers, with T1 edging an exciting encounter 3:2.

      The Lol Worlds 2026 is now the fifth most popular esports event this year   

    The first three quarterfinals were unable to break into the top five most popular matches list at the LoL World Championship 2026, even falling behind the most popular Swiss Stage games. However, T1's presence has helped lift audience interest, reiterating its status as the most-followed side in League of Legends esports.

    Final four once again boils down to LPL versus LCK

    T1's win has again proved that Chinese and Korean teams are far ahead of other regions at the World Championship for League of Legends. Two sides each from LCK and LPL have made it to the semifinals, meaning no team from outside these regions has reached a Worlds Grand Final since the 2019 edition.

    The first semifinal on November 7 will see Gen.G battle Invictus Gaming for a place in the finals. T1 will then take on Bilibili Gaming on November 8 in a repeat of the Worlds 2024 Grand Final. There is a chance we could see an all-LCK or all-LPL Grand Final, and fans can keep track of the results and viewership on the tournament page.
