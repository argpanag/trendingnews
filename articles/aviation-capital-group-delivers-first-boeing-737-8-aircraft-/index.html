Aviation Capital Group Delivers First Boeing 737-8 Aircraft in New Trinity Airways Livery

TheTools · 05.09.2026 16:50 · source · permalink

News provided by

Sep 04, 2026, 06:00 ET

NEWPORT BEACH, Calif., Sept. 4, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Aviation Capital Group LLC ("ACG"), a premier global full-service aircraft asset manager, is pleased to announce the delivery of a new Boeing 737-8 aircraft to Trinity Airways ("Trinity"), formerly T'way Air, a South Korean selective service carrier.

Delivered from the ACG orderbook, the aircraft is the first to enter into service featuring the airline's new brand identity and distinctive Trinity Gray and Rose Gold livery. The aircraft will enable Trinity's continued growth and sustainably connect South Korea to the wider Asia-Pacific region from its hubs at Incheon International Airport and Gimpo International Airport in Seoul.

"We are proud to deliver the first Boeing 737-8 in the new Trinity Airways' identity and to support the airline's continued international expansion as it enters this next stage of development," said Carter A. White, Executive Vice President and Chief Commercial Officer of ACG. "We congratulate the Trinity team as they celebrate this important milestone and we wish them every success as they begin this exciting new chapter in the company's history."

"This aircraft represents more than the addition of another aircraft to our fleet. It is the first aircraft to carry the new Trinity Airways identity into service, marking the first step in our journey with our new brand mission, 'Relaxed and Reliable.' We are excited to begin this next phase with the Boeing 737-8 and are pleased to take this important step together with ACG, our valued partner," said Chris Kong, Senior Vice President and Procurement Director of Trinity Airways.

This delivery represents the third aircraft delivered by ACG to Trinity under a seven aircraft Boeing 737-8 mandate, with the remaining aircraft scheduled for delivery from ACG's orderbook by the end of 2026.

About Aviation Capital Group

Aviation Capital Group is one of the world's premier full-service aircraft asset managers with approximately 500 owned, managed and committed aircraft as of June 30, 2026, leased to roughly 85 airlines in approximately 50 countries. It specializes in commercial aircraft leasing and provides certain aircraft asset management services and aircraft financing solutions for third parties. It was founded in 1989 and is a wholly owned subsidiary of Tokyo Century Corporation. Follow ACG on LinkedIn, and for more information, visit www.aviationcapitalgroup.com

About Trinity Airways

Trinity Airways Co., Ltd. is a South Korean Selective Service Carrier (SSC) formerly known as T'way Air and will begin operating under the Trinity Airways brand on September 10, 2026. Under its SSC model, the airline provides services tailored to each route and travel purpose, combining air travel with the hospitality expertise and network of Sono Trinity Group to create a seamless travel experience. Based in Seoul, Trinity Airways serves destinations across Korea, Japan, Southeast Asia, Europe and North America.
