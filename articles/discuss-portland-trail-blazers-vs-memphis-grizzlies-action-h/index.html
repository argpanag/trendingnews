Discuss Portland Trail Blazers vs. Memphis Grizzlies Action Here!

TheTools · 30.10.2026 01:00 · source · permalink
  • Portland Trail Blazers Discussion

Two undefeated teams meet. Which will emerge victorious?

UPDATE: Trail Blazers center Robert Williams III is OUT tonight due to left knee injury management. Blazers head coach Micah Nori said Williams is experiencing some soreness in his knee, and the team wanted to jump out ahead of it with the frequency of games picking up shortly.

The Portland Trail Blazers will attempt to keep their 2026-27 record unblemished as they face off against the similarly-unbeaten Memphis Grizzlies at the Moda Center tonight. It’s a return game for Jerami Grant, the first time Ja Morant will face his former team since the two stars were traded for each other this summer. It’s sure to be an exciting outing! You can talk about all the action with your friends and neighbors here, then tune in after the game for recaps, analysis, and a report from the arena!

Here’s the info you’ll need before the game:

Memphis Grizzlies (4-0) at Portland Trail Blazers (3-0)

Time: Thursday, October 29th - 7:00pm Pacific

How to watch via antenna or cable: See your options on the Rip City Television Network

How to watch via streaming: BlazerVision in Oregon and Washington; League Pass everywhere else

How to listen: Rip City Radio 620AM

Trail Blazers Injuries: Robert Williams III, Shaedon Sharpe, Branden Carlson, Chris Youngblood (Out).
