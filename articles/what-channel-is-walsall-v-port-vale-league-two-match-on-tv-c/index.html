What channel is Walsall v Port Vale League Two match on? TV coverage, live stream and kick-off time

TheTools · 19.09.2026 13:46 · source · permalink

Check out how to watch Walsall v Port Vale in League Two, including TV channel, radio coverage and kick-off time.

  • James Walters

Walsall host Port Vale in League Two on Saturday with the home side looking to extend their unbeaten league start to the season.

Lee Grant's side, who finished 13th last campaign, find themselves fifth in League Two and just two points off top after winning two and drawing four of their first six outings.

The Saddlers, who lost in the play-off final in 2025, are hoping to book their spot in League One for the first time since the 2018/19 season and securing a top-seven finish will be the aim this time out.

  • Watch Walsall v Port Vale on Sky Sports

Port Vale were relegated from League One last campaign and they've had a slow start to the season with Jon Brady's side winning just one of their six matches to leave them one point above the relegation zone.

Port Vale will be hoping to find form and string a few decent results together as Brady's men look to make a push for the play-off spots by the end of the campaign.
