3 arrested for planning to shoot up metro Atlanta school; guns and ammo seized, police say

TheTools · 27.10.2026 22:11 · source · permalink

CANTON, Ga. — Police in metro Atlanta say three people have been arrested for planning a shooting at Cherokee County high school.

Canton police say the FBI identified three people involved in an alleged conspiracy to carry out a shooting at Cherokee High School.

Investigators believe there is no ongoing threat to any Cherokee County school.

Last week, police identified and arrested a juvenile, whose identity is not being released.

Cherokee County School Superintendent Dr. Mary Elizabeth Davis confirmed that the juvenile is a former student at Cherokee High School.

Shane Keith Taylor, 17, was arrested in Metter, Georgia on Friday afternoon. He was brought to the Cherokee County Adult Detention Center.

Michael Brandon Terry, 21, was arrested in Chatsworth, California and is awaiting extradition to Cherokee County.

Both Taylor and Terry have been charged with conspiracy to commit murder.

Investigators collected guns, ammo and electronic devices as evidence.

“I share the same deep emotions that coincide with learning news of this nature. I, myself, recognize the seriousness of our responsibility every single day to ensure safe and secure schools for the students and staff who call one of our schools home,” Dr. Davis wrote.

Canton police are urging parents to use this as an opportunity to teach their children about the seriousness of making threats.

“The safety of our students and school staff is a top priority, and we take every threat seriously,” said Canton Police Chief Marty Ferrell. “I appreciate the quick action of our law enforcement partners and the dedication of our detectives in identifying and arresting the suspects.”
