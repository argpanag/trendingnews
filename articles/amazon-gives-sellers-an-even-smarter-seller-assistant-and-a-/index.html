Amazon gives sellers an even smarter Seller Assistant and a new plugin for Amazon Quick and Anthropic’s Claude

TheTools · 23.09.2026 13:00 · source · permalink

Key takeaways

  • New Seller Assistant workflows give sellers the power to continuously monitor and execute routine tasks like restocking and pricing adjustments with full audit trails and seller-defined guardrails.
  • A new plugin brings Seller Assistant’s intelligence into the AI tools sellers already use, launching first with Amazon Quick and in beta with Anthropic's Claude—with a free 12-month Quick Plus subscription to help them run their business on and off Amazon.
