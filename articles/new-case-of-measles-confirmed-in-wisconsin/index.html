New case of measles confirmed in Wisconsin

TheTools · 23.09.2026 00:12 · source · permalink

WISCONSIN — One person in Chippewa County has the measles, the Wisconsin Department of Health Services and the Chippewa County Department of Public Health confirmed Tuesday.

Health officials said this case was related to out-of-state travel and does not have a connection to the southwestern Wisconsin outbreak. 

This comes less than a week after two people in Vernon County were confirmed to have contracted the measles.

Wisconsin DHS said the state had seen 171 cases of measles so far this year. According to health officials, 170 of those cases were in unvaccinated individuals. There have been five hospitalizations. 

In Wisconsin’s largest outbreak — in Grant, Iowa, Vernon and Lafayette counties — there are currently 161 confirmed and six probable cases of measles.

Symptoms of measles appear 10 to 21 days after exposure and include:

  • Runny nose
  • High fever (may be greater than 104 F)
  • Tiredness
  • Cough
  • Red, watery eyes or conjunctivitis (pink eye)
  • A red rash with raised bumps that starts at the hairline and moves to the arms and legs three to five days after symptoms begin

DHS said if you notice any of these symptoms, stay home; call your doctor or clinic before heading in for an appointment.
