Morata: "Como were not the right situation for me. Fabregas is ready for a top club" | Goal.com

TheTools · 14.09.2026 04:36 · source · permalink

Translated by

The former Como striker explained the reason for his departure, using words of praise for the Spanish manager.

Alvaro Morata's spell in a Como shirt did not go as expected and, speaking to El Larguero on Cadena Ser, the Spaniard explained why he chose a move to Spain with Leganes in the summer, albeit on loan with an option to buy.

The former AC Milan and Juventus player repeated that everything had gone wrong and that, after speaking with Cesc Fabregas, they ultimately decided parting ways was the right move. He also had plenty of praise for the Spanish coach, who he believes is destined for a big club.

  • “It wasn’t the right situation for me to stay”

    "In the end, Fabrega and I spoke a lot. Well, I didn't think it was the right situation for me to stay. Last year I started well until I got injured. I had a very significant injury and I had never had an injury like this"

  • He will stay as long as he wants, but he is ready for a big club

    "He is a very good manager. I have no doubt that he will stay at Como for as long as he wants because I think he is already more than ready for any top-level team in world football"

  • “I thank him, he did so much for me”

    "I consider Fabregas a very intelligent person and he learned from Conte and Mourinho, as well as Guardiola. And I think that in the end he has a bit of all of them. I can only support him from afar and thank him, as always, for everything he has done for me."
