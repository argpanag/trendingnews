Hamilton Sees Ferrari Pace Despite Mexico GP Practice Setback

TheTools · 31.10.2026 10:06 · source · permalink

Lewis Hamilton sees strong potential in Ferrari’s pace at the Mexico City Grand Prix, but a suspected power issue cut short his second practice and left him without crucial long-run data. Ferrari is investigating the problem ahead of Saturday, while Charles Leclerc’s speed suggests the team is competitive—but the tight field leaves little room for error.

  • Hamilton was classified 11th in FP2.
  • Leclerc finished FP2 0.103 seconds ahead of his nearest challenger.
  • Last year, just 0.05 seconds separated second through sixth place in Mexico qualifying.

Hamilton and Ferrari believe the car has strong pace, even with the suspected power loss. But Hamilton missed much of FP1 and FP2, leaving him short on track time and without long-run data to judge Ferrari’s race prospects; rivals are also closing in.

  • Ferrari is investigating Hamilton’s car and plans to decide on corrective measures before Saturday’s running.
  • Hamilton says the setup was in a good place and expects few changes, but needs the car working reliably.
  • Leclerc led FP1, while the narrow FP2 margin underscored how close the competition is.

Ferrari must resolve the issue before final practice and qualifying. Hamilton will need to make up for lost preparation time as soon as competitive running resumes.

Ferrari’s pace looks promising, but Hamilton’s weekend depends on turning that potential into a reliable car—and quickly.
