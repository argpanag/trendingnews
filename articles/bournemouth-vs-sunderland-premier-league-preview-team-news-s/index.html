    Nottingham Forest v Arsenal (16:30 BST)

    There's no Morgan Gibbs-White because of injury for Nottingham Forest, who make a total of two changes.

    Nicolás Domínguez replaces the forward, while Jair Cunha also returns to the starting XI as he replaces Ola Aina.

    Nottingham Forest XI: Sels; Jair Cunha, Diomande, Murillo, Muñoz, Domínguez, Schlager, Williams, Ndoye, McAtee, Delap.

    Subs: John Victor; Sangaré, Hudson-Odoi, Wood, Kalimuendo, Yates, Netz, Milenković, Aina.

    Bruno Guimaraes and Eberechi Eze both start for Arsenal.

    The pair were on the bench for the Gunners' last Premier League outing against Leeds but come into the first XI, with Eze making his first Premier League start of the season.

    Martín Zubimendi is only fit enough for a place on the bench after suffering an ankle injury against Leeds.

    Arsenal XI: Raya, White, Gabriel, Konsa, Calafiori, Rice, Guimaraes, Odegaard, Eze, Saka, Havertz.

    Subs: Meslier, Timber, Lewis-Skelly, Mosquera, Zubimendi, Merino, Madueke, Dowman, Gyokeres.
