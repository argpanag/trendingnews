  2. 'I know the results will come'published at 19:24 BST
    19:24 BST

    FT: Nottingham Forest 0-1 Arsenal

    Nottingham Forest

    Nottingham Forest manager Oliver Glasner speaking to Sky Sports: "I think everybody feels that the result is unfair today but it is another defeat and a game where we can't score at the City Ground.

    "Defensively it was a top-level performance. A deflected shot and Arsenal take the win. But we had our moments in the game and it is important when you play the champions you take these moments. It is the same in every single game. Today the performance, the efforts, the spirit and the energy was on a really good level but the result is missing.

    "When you play Arsenal you can see how confident they are in possession and if you jump in the wrong moments they punish you. We did so well as a team not giving them spaces. If you keep the champions to one shot on target you have done very well. It feels like we are getting close but in the end we need to score.

    "I could see the effort was there, they created many dangerous situations against what is for me the best team in defence. We had enough moments but we need to do better. That is what we can blame ourselves for. But on the other side the performance was good and everyone feels we deserved more.

    "Now it is analysing, staying positive, sticking together and building on these performances because then I know the results will come.

    "Today it was a fantastic atmosphere, the fans appreciate the effort, but we all go home disappointed with the result while being proud of the performance."

  6. 'Results will come if the performances stay good'published at 19:16 BST
    19:16 BST

    FT: Nottingham 0-1 Arsenal

    Nottingham Forest

    Nottingham Forest boss Oliver Glasner speaking to BBC Match of the Day: "Very tough to take, bit of deja vu. Many things here we feel the result is unfair but it's what stands and it's zero points at home. Very good performance against the champions. We caused many problems, didn't give them a lot and at the end we have zero points it's tough today. It feels a bit unfair but on the other side it's our responsibility to not drop the head and build up on the performance and the many positive aspects. Feels a bit weird to have a third defeat in a row but I see progress in every game and today the effort was amazing - it was just the result we can't be pleased with.

    "Huge credit to the players, they stick to the plan and then we had our moments and if you want to win against the champions, you have to take these moments and this is what we have to work on - to take the moments. When I see the reaction at the end, three big opportunities in the end, many set pieces and things to improve. Eight corners but we didn't take one and this is what we have to learn and improve and we'll get the results we all think we deserve."

    Lack of confidence in front of goal? "I don't think so. When you see their goal it's deflected otherwise, it's straight to the gloves of Matz Sels. You have periods in football where you feel everything going against you and then there are periods when things are on your side. Now it is how we deal with the situation and react as a team and just how we can change this is by staying positive, sticking together and working hard. And having this coolness, not to worry too much because I can see good performances and not good results. The results will come if the performances stay good.

    "It's like the stock market - the past never tells you what happens in the future. Sometimes it takes a while - during my first year at Palace, we didn't start well but we had a turnaround. We are playing better right now but we also concede how competitive the Premier League is because every mistake you get punished. Today I was pleased with the team."

Madueke's strike enough as Arsenal pick up narrow win over Forest

Arsenal broke Nottingham Forest's resolve as a second-half strike by substitute Noni Madueke earned the defending champions a sixth Premier League win of the season.

The Gunners looked in danger of being held to their first draw of the campaign as they struggled to break down the home side's defence.

A dreadful first half did not feature any shots on target from either side, which is only the second time that has happened in the Premier League this season.

The first time was in a game that also involved Arsenal - at Aston Villa on 31 August, when the Gunners went on to win 1-0 thanks to Bukayo Saka's second-half strike.

Once again they ultimately found a way to secure all three points as Madueke - who had replaced Eberechi Eze at half-time - saw his shot deflect off Daniel Munoz and find the back of the net.

That was Arsenal's first shot on target and will have been a frustrating blow for the hosts, after they had looked like potentially upsetting the Gunners after making the better start to the second half.

But Forest paid the price for poor finishing as their search for a first home win of the season continues.

Arsenal, meanwhile, remain second in the Premier League, three points behind leaders Manchester City.

Another sub strikes as Arsenal find a way

Mikel Arteta post-match interview - Nottingham Forest v Arsenal

Arsenal have started their title defence - defeat at Brighton aside - in formidable fashion, with this now their sixth league win from seven games.

There was no scintillating display on this occasion, as they struggled to create chances, but this was a reminder of the strength in depth Arsenal have to help them overcome obstacles.

Eze had been handed his first Premier League start of the season, but after an ineffective first-half display he was replaced with Madueke, who delivered the one spark needed to secure all three points.

Mikel Arteta's options off the bench are proving important in the early stages of this campaign, with Arsenal having scored three goals via substitutes in the Premier League this season. Only Ipswich Town have scored more with four.

More frustration for Forest

Defeat 'feels unfair' on Forest - Glasner

Oliver Glasner delivered memories Crystal Palace fans perhaps never thought they would experience by winning the FA Cup and the Uefa Conference League with the Eagles.

He left and became head coach at Forest in the summer, but the City Ground's Glasner era is not quite going to plan.

Forest have managed just one win in the Premier League - 2-1 at Aston Villa - but their home form will be starting to become a serious concern.

Victory against the defending champions was always going to be a lot to ask, even with Arsenal not at their best, but Forest have still not yet scored a goal in four home league games this season.

Their struggle to make opportunities count in the final third is the main issue. They had 14 shots against Arsenal and only two went on target.
