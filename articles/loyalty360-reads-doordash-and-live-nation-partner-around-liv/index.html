DoorDash and Live Nation Turn Up the Volume with New Partnership 

DoorDash and Live Nation Australia and New Zealand (ANZ) have announced a three-year strategic partnership that will bring DoorDash closer to live music fans across concerts, festivals, and venues. As an official partner across Live Nation’s portfolio, DoorDash plans to offer customers exclusive music perks and promotions throughout the live event experience, while also launching the DoorDash Food Village at major festivals as an on-site hospitality activation. The partnership will extend beyond the show itself, with DoorDash offering fans exclusive deals and post-event promotions as they leave participating Live Nation venues, strengthening the delivery platform’s presence in entertainment and live music across Australia and New Zealand.

H&M, Chobani, UrbanStems and Teleties Rethink What Makes a Brand Partnership Work 

Brands are getting more strategic about collaborations, looking beyond immediate sales to consider audience fit, cultural relevance, and the doors a partnership can open over time, as Modern Retail reports. At eTail Boston, executives from H&M, Chobani, UrbanStems, and Teleties shared how those factors are shaping their approach. UrbanStems has found that a buzzy partner doesn’t necessarily translate into results when the two brands serve different demographics, while H&M uses relationships with designers and talent to stay connected to cultural moments and generate organic exposure. Chobani is tapping sports through its U.S. Soccer sponsorship to reach consumers around shared interests in nutrition and athletics, and Teleties has found that partnerships such as its relationship with the Orlando Magic can create valuable connections with other organizations even when the products themselves aren’t its biggest sellers. Together, their experiences suggest brands are increasingly judging collaborations not simply by the initial revenue they produce, but by how well partners align with their audiences and the longer-term opportunities they create, according to Modern Retail.

Salesforce and Siemens Expand AI Partnership to Connect the Front Office with Engineering 

Salesforce and Siemens are expanding their partnership to bring AI agents deeper into industrial sales, service, and customer operations. By connecting Salesforce’s Agentforce with Siemens’ Teamcenter Service Lifecycle Management, the companies aim to make engineering and product information directly accessible within customer-facing workflows, helping technicians identify compatible parts, sales teams validate potential upgrades, and customers get answers faster. Siemens is also using Agentforce to automate engagement and qualification for more than 2,500 previously unqualified inbound leads each month, supporting its sales organization across 132 countries. Looking ahead, the companies plan to extend AI automation into Siemens’ partner and supplier ecosystem, with agents assisting with everything from distributor recruitment to onboarding, certification and risk checks.
