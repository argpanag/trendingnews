FBI: Man accused of sending money to foreign terrorist groups

TheTools · 19.11.2026 23:11 · source · permalink

The FBI’s Atlanta office has announced the arrest of a Lavonia, Georgia, man who allegedly sent thousands of dollars to the terrorist organization ISIS.

Agents say Younes Moustafa Younes was charged with international money laundering and destruction of evidence.

According to investigators, Younes sent more than $26,000 to individuals and groups associated with ISIS.

During a search, agents say they found phones submerged in an unknown substance, and shredded documents were submerged in a solution in the bathtub.

Agents were not able to provide any additional details, but say more information will be released when available.
