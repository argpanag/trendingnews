Manchester City star striker Erling Haaland has opened up regarding his concerns over an unforgiving football schedule as the new season gets underway without his usual summer break. Despite making a sharp start on the pitch by scoring three goals in his opening three matches for the English champions the 26 year old Norwegian international admitted that skipping a proper preseason following World Cup duty has made it difficult to step away from the game leaving him uncertain about how his body and mind will adapt as the grueling campaign progresses.

Chopping wood as a traditional mental escape

To combat the intense mental fatigue that comes with elite competition Haaland has adopted a surprisingly rustic routine away from the training ground. Speaking in an interview published on his official YouTube channel the forward revealed that chopping wood and relaxing by the fire at home has become his favorite way to clear his head explaining that the solitude and connection with nature provide him with the genuine peace of mind needed to reset between high stakes matches.

Navigating physical demands and mental sharpness

The clinical goalscorer emphasized that while physical conditioning can be sustained through proper care staying mentally sharp presents a far greater challenge when there is no true break from the sport. Haaland noted that the constant transition from international tournaments back into club competition leaves little time to rebuild the hunger required for competition though he maintained a positive perspective regarding his privilege to play football at the highest level while navigating this unprecedented workload in his career.

Tactical squad rotation under Enzo Maresca

Recognizing the heavy physical toll placed on key players Manchester City manager Enzo Maresca confirmed that the coaching staff will actively manage player minutes to prevent fatigue and injuries throughout the long season. Speaking ahead of their Champions League opener against Porto the Italian tactician highlighted the importance of giving stars like Haaland scheduled rest periods ensuring the Norwegian forward remains in peak condition for the crucial stages of domestic and European competition.
