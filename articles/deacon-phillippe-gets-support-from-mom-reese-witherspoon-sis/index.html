Deacon Phillippe Gets Support from Mom Reese Witherspoon & Sister Ava Phillippe at 'Brother Save Us' Premiere in Nashville!

TheTools · 26.09.2026 20:21 · source · permalink

Deacon Phillippe is stepping out for the premiere of his new movie!

The 22-year-old singer, model, and actor had the support of his mom Reese Witherspoon and big sister Ava Phillippe at the premiere of Brother Save Us held during the 2026 Nashville Film Festival on Saturday (September 26) in Nashville, Tenn.

Also supporting Deacon at the event was his grandmother, Reese‘s mom, Betty.

What is Brother Save Us about?

“Cole works hard to fill Nat’s days with light and hope – to shield his brother from the harsh truths of their world. But after Pa buys an AI farm bot from the controversial tech company Agentico to ensure they make harvest, everything starts to change,” the synopsis reads. 

The movie is directed and written by Jesse Behrenwald. Thomas Murphy Molony, Chandler Riggs, Forrest Weber, Amy Sloan, Kroydell Galima and Victor Holstein also star. Deacon is also among the film’s producers.

While Deacon has appeared on a few TV shows, Brother Save Us marks his feature film acting debut.

As of right now, the movie doesn’t have a theatrical release date.
