There is a ‘Mbappé situation’ at Real Madrid

TheTools · 06.10.2026 09:49 · source · permalink

REAL MADRID

Discontent among some members of the locker room and within the club over the Frenchman’s attitude

  • Manel Bruña

    Journalist

Journalist

Actualizado el 06/10/2026 05:49 EDT

There is now undeniably a “Mbappé situation” at Real Madrid. Some members of the locker room and certain figures within the club are beginning to grow tired of the French star. Inside the locker room, frustration centers on his trips away while injured and, above all, the image he is projecting. This is not something that has emerged only recently. Similar concerns arose last season following another one of his getaways. Many feel that Mbappé does his own thing and does not put the club first.

Adding to the tension is the fact that many do not understand why Mbappé appears to be treated differently from the club’s other injured players. While Valverde and Konaté remained in Madrid, he left the city. Although he had the club’s permission, there are suspicions within the locker room over what some perceive as preferential treatment when it comes to managing his injuries.

Certain figures within the club are also struggling to understand Mbappé’s behavior, including images of him leaving a nightclub while injured and going out for a bike ride. Those within these circles increasingly view the Frenchman as something close to a lost cause, believing that he is focused primarily on himself rather than on the team and Real Madrid.

The club continues to stand by its biggest star, but it is also true that more and more voices believe that defending him is becoming increasingly difficult. Their concern is that Mbappé is focused solely on himself rather than on what it means to represent Real Madrid.
