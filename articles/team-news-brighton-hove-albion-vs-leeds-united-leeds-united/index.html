Team News: Brighton & Hove Albion vs Leeds United - Leeds United

TheTools · 05.09.2026 13:55 · source · permalink

Leeds United will be looking to continue their unbeaten start to the season this afternoon as they face Brighton & Hove Albion away from home.

Ahead of kick off at the Amex, Daniel Farke has made three changes to the team that drew with Brentford last time out, including a full Premier League debut for Nico Elvedi.

James Trafford remains in goal, behind a defence of Jayden Bogle, James Justin, Tarik Muharemović, Elvedi and the returning Gabriel Gudmundsson.

In the midfield, skipper Ethan Ampadu is joined by Anton Stach and Ao Tanaka, providing support for Dominic Calvert-Lewin and Noah Okafor in attack.

On the bench, deadline day signings Melvin Bard and Jean-Mattéo Bahoya are included, along with Michael Zetterer, Daniel James, Sean Longstaff, Harry Wilson, Brenden Aaronson, Lukas Nmecha and Jaka Bijol.

For supporters not in Brighton, you can follow the game with live commentary on LUTV.

Starting XI: Trafford (GK), Bogle, Gudmundsson, Ampadu (C), Muharemović, Calvert-Lewin, Stach, Okafor, Tanaka, Justin, Elvedi.

Subs: Zetterer (GK), James, Longstaff, Wilson, Aaronson, Nmecha, Bijol, Bahoya, Bard.
