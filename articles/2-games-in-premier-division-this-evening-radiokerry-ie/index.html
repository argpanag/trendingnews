There are two games in the SSE Airtricity Premier Division this evening.

St Pat's make the trip to the Ryan McBride Brandywell for a key game in the title race.

Stephen Kenny's side will be hoping to bounce back from their disappointing defeat at home to Shelbourne on Friday night.

The visitors come into this evening's encounter three points behind second place Bohemians, while they trail league leaders Shamrock Rovers by six points.

There's an 8pm start in Derry.

Waterford's battle against relegation continues when they play host to Galway United.

The FAI Cup finalists are second bottom and will move ahead of Drogheda United in eighth place if they pick up three points tonight.

Kick-off at the RSC is 7.45pm.
