Veterinarians Computer-Based Licensure Examination

TheTools · 11.11.2026 10:36 · source · permalink

MANILA, Philippines — The Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) announces that 1,083 out of 1,503 passed the Veterinarians Computer-Based Licensure Examination given by the Board of Veterinary Medicine in N. C. R., Baguio, Cagayan de Oro, Cebu, Davao, Iloilo, Koronadal, Legazpi, Lucena, Pampanga, Rosales, Tuguegarao and Puerto Princesa, Palawan this November 2026.

The members of the Board of Veterinary Medicine who gave the licensure  examination are Dr. Agnes Teodora A. Baylon, Chairperson; Dr. Claro N. Mingala and Dr. Tomas M. Acorda, Members.
