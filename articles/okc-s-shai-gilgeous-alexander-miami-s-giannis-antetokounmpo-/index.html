OKC’s Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Miami’s Giannis Antetokounmpo both out of Thunder-Heat matchup | NBA.com

TheTools · 23.11.2026 00:05 · source · permalink

Top Stories

Gilgeous-Alexander (neck, back) and Antetokounmpo (groin) will each miss their first game of the season.

Tim Reynolds | Associated Press

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander averaged 34 points per game against Miami last season.

MIAMI (AP) — Oklahoma City guard Shai Gilgeous-Alexander and Miami’s Giannis Antetokounmpo were both held out of the Thunder-Heat game on Sunday night, meaning the matchup was without a pair of Most Valuable Players and two of the league’s top four scorers so far this season.

Gilgeous-Alexander — the two-time reigning MVP — was out with a neck strain and a lower back contusion, the aftereffects of a hard fall he took Friday night in a game against New Orleans. Antetokounmpo — the MVP in 2018-19 and 2019-20 — was out with a right groin strain.

It was the first time either player, both of whom have also been an NBA Finals MVP in the past, has missed a game this season.

“It wouldn’t be wise for us to put him on the court tonight,” Thunder coach Mark Daigneault said of Gilgeous-Alexander.

Antetokounmpo went through a pregame warmup Sunday but was ruled out about an hour before game time. Miami doesn’t play again until an NBA Cup game on Wednesday at Philadelphia.

Oklahoma City has been absolutely rocked by injuries, with past All-Stars in Jalen Williams (ankle, possibly season ending) and Chet Holmgren (knee, out until likely January) missing from the lineup for extended periods. Also out: Alex Caruso, Cason Wallace, Jaylin Williams, Nikola Topic and Thomas Sorber.

Gilgeous-Alexander entered Sunday as the No. 2 scorer in the NBA at 31.7 points per game, behind only the Los Angeles Lakers’ Luka Doncic (37.4). He’s one of only two players — Isaiah Hartenstein is the other — to have started each of the first 16 Thunder games this season.

Antetokounmpo is fourth in scoring at 28.2 points per game.
