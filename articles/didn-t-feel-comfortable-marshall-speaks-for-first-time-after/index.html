‘Didn’t feel comfortable’: Marshall speaks for first time after Cats trade, missing Saints B&F

TheTools · 09.10.2026 01:51 · source · permalink

Rowan Marshall says he “didn’t feel comfortable” attending St Kilda’s best and fairest as the Geelong recruit prepares to begin a new chapter at his second club.

Marshall was granted his wish of a trade to the Cats a year after a deal couldn’t be reached between the two clubs.

The ruckman was traded from St Kilda along with a future third-round pick in exchange for Geelong’s Pick 14.

The saga began to turn ugly after Marshall decided against attending St Kilda’s Trevor Barker Medal on Monday night.

Marshall, who turns 31 next month, was surprised by the reaction but says he ultimately felt uneasy about the situation.

“I had no idea it would blow up like it did, to be honest,” Marshall said.

“Reality is I just didn’t feel comfortable at the time … a few different reasons but I didn’t feel comfortable having a lot of interactions with a lot of people.”

Despite that, and being disappointed last year’s trade fell through, Marshall believes he left St Kilda on good terms after 177 games for the club since 2017.

“I was pretty shattered at the time to miss out on getting to the Cats last year. But I moved on and I’m happy that it worked out a second time around,” he continued.

“I loved eventually getting back into St Kilda, I’ve got so many close mates down there and lifelong mates, that made it easier and I actually really enjoyed my year down there.

“I would’ve liked a little bit more success and you certainly get envious of the teams in finals this time of the year, but I loved my time at the Saints.

“I couldn’t be any more grateful to St Kilda and the opportunity they gave me 10 years ago. It’s going to be so hard moving on, it genuinely a great club.

“But I think it’s time for a fresh start, both for me and the club. I’m happy the trade got through and it’s all official now.”

Marshall found out the trade went through from the golf course on Thursday. It capped off another busy recruiting coup at the Cattery after the additions of Toby Greene and Jordan Ridley as Chris Scott’s side loads up for another flag tilt in 2027.

“Obviously you hear a lot externally about how good the program is and they’re such a successful club,” Marshall said.

“I’m looking forward to getting down there and working with Mitch Edwards, he’s such a promising, young ruck, really looking forward to teaming up with him.

“Hopefully be part of a successful team … they’ve got so many great players. I’m just really looking forward to getting in a new environment and to do it alongside Toby Greene and Jordan Ridley, it will be easier having a couple of other recruits. Just can’t wait.”
