Alberto Gilardino, the head coach of Parma, has confirmed the difficulty of the upcoming match against SS Lazio tomorrow, praising the positive state his opponent is in under Gennaro Gattuso. This was stated during the press conference preceding the match, which is part of Serie A.

Gianluca Giannini said: "We know the team we will face; they are going through a period of positive energy, and I congratulate them, especially Gattuso, he deserves it after a tough time. We are expecting a complicated match, but I have great confidence in my team, and discipline and focus from the start will be crucial factors in achieving a positive result."

Parma's coach revealed that Almqvist, Nicolussi, and Elvige will miss the match, emphasizing that competition for starting positions remains open, and that the team needs time to adapt to the tactical and technical changes he has implemented since taking charge. He also stressed the need to address the defensive errors that have led to conceding easy goals in recent matches.

Gianluca Giallardino has held frank discussions with his players about disappointing results, emphasizing that everyone is aware of the responsibility placed upon them, and that the team is required to show enthusiasm and commitment from the very first minutes.

He pointed out that improving performance requires placing players in positions that suit their abilities, while developing offensive pressure and defensive balance, emphasizing that changes need time to show their results on the pitch.
