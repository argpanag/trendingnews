  1. Consistency key for Sagepublished at 19:35 BST
    19:35 BST

    Crystal Palace v Middlesbrough (19:45 BST)

    The early rounds of the League Cup often present opportunities for managers to heavily rotate their squads, but Crystal Palace boss Pierre Sage says that is not something he believes in.

    “I'm not really a fan of the big rotations, because every time I did that [in the past] I'm unhappy at the end of the game – especially with the result but sometimes with the coordination of the players because they are in lack of coordination," he said.

    “But at the beginning of the season, we have to respect all the commitment of all players during the session. It's maybe the opportunity for them to show it, and we have to win this game because we are ambitious in this competition too.

    “It's not only about rotation. The first thing we have in mind is to win the game.”

  3. Will Hull keep strong start going?published at 19:30 BST
    19:30 BST

    Sunderland v Hull (19:45 BST)

    It is an all Premier League encounter at the Stadium of Light as Sunderland take on Hull.

    The Tigers have made a fine start to life back in the Premier League as they sit third in the table after three games, while Sunderland are 12th.

    Hull will certainly happy to emulate the season the Black Cats had last term on their Premier League return, as they surprised all by securing European football for this season.

    Hull assistant manager Dean Holden said: “It has been a fantastic start to the season there is no doubt about that.

    "When we looked at the start of the season, the games we had on the horizon before the international break, we knew there were some real tough games and still more to come.

    "The boys have stood up to the test so far.”

  4. Plenty of cup games coming up for Bournemouthpublished at 19:29 BST
    19:29 BST

    Bournemouth v Lincoln (19:45 BST)

    It is going to be a busy campaign for Bournemouth, who have European football to look forward to for the first time in the club's history.

    As well as a home game against AC Milan in October, the Cherries fans also have two trips to Spain (Real Sociedad and Celta Vigo), Czech Republic (Sparta Prague) and Norway (Lillestrom) to get excited about, with their Europa League campaign starting on 17 September.

    Bournemouth have gone out of the Carabao Cup in round two in both of the past two seasons, although by qualifying for Europe that has meant they don't enter until round three this year.

  5. Newcastle taking no chances for 'crazy game'published at 19:26 BST
    19:26 BST

    Millwall v Newcastle (20:00 GMT)

    Ciaran Kelly
    BBC Sport reporter

    Newcastle United head coach Matthias Jaissle did not know a great deal about Millwall when the Carabao Cup third-round draw was made.

    But tonight's team sheet shows his respect for the injury-hit Championship side and, indeed, the competition as a whole.

    Jaissle could have seen the game as an opportunity to make sweeping changes and give summer signings Sean Steur, Aladji Bamba, Bazoumana Toure and Matias Fernandez-Pardo a start.

    But it is rather telling that it is only the club's most experienced additions - Lukas Hornicek, Amar Dedic and Nico Gonzalez - who line up tonight.

    Jaissle heard it was going to be a "crazy game" at the Den and he is clearly not taking any chances after picking a side dominated by players who have previously played in these sorts of cup ties.
