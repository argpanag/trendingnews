Shock moment Corrie's Jacob Roberts hurls vile abuse at women in 2am rant at bar

TheTools · 21.11.2026 21:44 · source · permalink

CORONATION Street star Jacob Roberts is being investigated by ITV after he hurled abusive and misogynistic insults as he tried to force his way into a bar.

The actor, 36, who plays copper Kit Green in the soap, was filmed shouting at a group of men and women “you f*****g virgin c**t” — and then “slag, f**k off” at one woman trying to stop him.

Shocking footage shows him being restrained by another man while angrily jabbing his finger through a security gate that the staff had to pull across the bar’s doorway to keep him out.

A shocked woman is heard saying “Oi, Oi” to the actor as he continues to yell four-letter insults with a menacing snarl on his face.

Women’s group campaigners last night slammed Roberts for the chauvinistic barrage as his bosses confirmed they would speak to him as part of a formal probe.

The new footage of Roberts’ 15-minute meltdown on November 8 emerged after we reported last weekend that police were called following the 2.30am bust-up outside Kiely’s Irish Bar in Manchester.

Greater Manchester Police confirmed that officers attended after a report of a disturbance. Roberts was also caught on camera claiming to a policeman that the bouncer was “being a d**k”.

He is now likely to be in hot water with Corrie bosses, as ITV contracts stipulate talent should not bring the firm into disrepute.

Three members of staff at the bar have now spoken in detail about how the tirade unfolded.

One of them, the doorman who spoke to the police, said: “It is not acceptable. I think Coronation Street bosses should do something. I think he should be sacked. He is abusing his celebrity status.

“He was saying that he was a celebrity and ‘Do you know who I am?’. He is no better than anyone else. He has abused his position.

“It is up to the police if they take any further action. He was swearing but he didn’t hit me.”

He added that Roberts had turned up with some friends and had been told that the venue was closed — which led to him shaking the fence.

The doorman revealed: He said, ‘Do you know who I am?’ I said, ‘I don’t know who you are.’

“He just wanted to fight. I said, ‘You can’t come in mate.’

“He was saying, ‘You f*** this, you f*** that.’ I said, ‘I don’t care who you are.’ I told him to ‘f*** off’. Then he said, ‘You are being a d****d.’

“I told him we are closed and that everyone was leaving the venue. It was nearly 3am.

“The manager came out and called the police. As soon as the manager called the police, that is when he walked off.

“I told the police he was being aggressive and I just walked away. He started kicking off again. He said, ‘Do you want to have it?’.

“I don’t know what the police said. He was trying to make me angry. I was ready to go home.

“He was asking why we needed to call the police but the guy wanted to fight. He was shaking the fence to try to get at me. I said to him that he was not getting in but he was being aggressive.”

The doorman said ten years of experience in the industry has taught him to stay calm when confronted.

He added: “The more I was chill the more aggressive he got.”

The bar manager, who also asked not to be identified, said: “He was giving it, ‘Do you know who I am?’

“He called a woman a slag. He was trying to fight the doorman. I had to phone the police.

“I never call the police because we never have any trouble. He is a very calm doorman and never raises his voice.

“He [Roberts] was awful. We were trying to get people out and he was trying to get in.

“He just wouldn’t move. He should apologise to the doorman and to the woman he called a slag.”

A third bar worker said of Roberts: “He was really out of order.

“By the time the police came he was down the road. Everything was reported to the police. The doorman remained really calm; anyone else would have hit him.

“He was really pushing it. He wanted to have a fight.”

The bar worker alleged Roberts screamed at her, calling her a “slag”, and physically pushed a bar customer during the altercation.

She said he “certainly wouldn’t be welcome” back at the pub.

The woman added: “It would have been appreciated if he’d come in the next day and apologised and shown some kind of remorse but he didn’t. He clearly is not bothered, is he?”

Teresa Parker, a consultant on violence against women and girls, who has worked with Corrie on storylines in the past, said she was disappointed by the clip.

She said: “I know from working with Coronation Street on storylines that address abuse against women how seriously they take this as an issue.

“It is disappointing to see an actor from the show being verbally aggressive towards a woman and using misogynistic language.

“You can challenge what might be a frustrating situation without choosing to speak to women like this.

“Being in the public eye you need to consider you are likely to be seen as a role model to others, including younger people who may look up to you.”

Salford-born Roberts, who is dating Corrie’s publicist Caitlin Connolly, joined the soap two years ago after a six-year stint in Channel 4’s Hollyoaks.

He has been involved in some of the show’s major storylines, including being diagnosed with male breast cancer and fathering a child after an illicit affair with his girlfriend’s daughter.

Trailers for upcoming episodes aired on ITV yesterday showed Roberts will feature prominently in this week’s episodes.

An ITV spokeswoman said: “We are investigating this matter and will be speaking to Jacob Roberts.”
