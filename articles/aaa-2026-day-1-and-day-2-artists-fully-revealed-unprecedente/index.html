'AAA 2026' Day 1 and Day 2 Artists Fully Revealed.. 'Unprecedented Global Festival' [Official] | STARNEWS

The full artist lineup for the first and second days of the global No.1 singer and actor comprehensive awards ceremony, 'Asia Artist Awards (아시아 아티스트 어워즈)', has been unveiled.

On the 15th, the AAA Organizing Committee revealed the Day 1 and Day 2 artists for 'Asia Artist Awards (아시아 아티스트 어워즈), hereinafter referred to as 'AAA 2026',' which will be held over two days on December 5-6 at Kaohsiung National Stadium, heightening the excitement of the festival.

For the opening ceremony of the 'AAA 2026' awards on Day 1, global star Tzuyu (TZUYU) and Sung Han-bin, who is returning as an 'AAA' host for the first time in two years, will take the stage. Attention is also focused on the special synergy these two beloved by fans worldwide will create.

In the actor category, a diverse lineup of actors spanning generations and genres, including Ko Yoon-jung, Kim Sun-ho, Kim Soo-hyun, Dahyun (TWICE), Moon Sang-min, Park Ji-hwan, Park Ji-hoon, Bae Jong-ok, Ahn Hyo-seop, Yoon Kyung-ho, Lee Da-hee, Lee Hong-nae, Joo Young-woo, and Heo Nam-jun, has been announced, raising expectations.

In the singer category, Naniwa Danshi (나니와단시), NEXZ (넥스지), Dahyun (WJSN), RIIZE (라이즈), LNGSHOT (롱샷), RESCENE (리센느), MONSTA X (몬스타엑스), Super Junior-D&E, OURBIRTHDAY (아워벌스데이), IDID (아이딧), &TEAM (앤팀), Accusefive (어큐즈파이브, ), ATEEZ (에이티즈), Xdinary Heroes (엑스디너리 히어로즈), Federal Reserve (Tomorrow X Together), ALLDAY PROJECT (올데이 프로젝트), WOODZ (우즈), ONE PUL (DAY6), ITZY (있지), Jooheon (MONSTA X), YENA (최예나), KISS OF LIFE (키스오브라이프), KickFlip (킥플립), tripleS (트리플에스), and Hearts2Hearts (하츠투하츠) are set to perform, promising a rich stage that will further elevate the excitement of 'AAA 2026'.

For the Day 2 host of 'AAA 2026', 'AAA Queen' Jang Won-young, who has hosted 'AAA' for six consecutive years, and trusted actor Ji Chang-wook will take the stage to make the second day of the awards ceremony spectacular. Expectations are high for the chemistry these two, known for their stunning visuals and solid hosting skills, will display.

In the actor category, a diverse array of actors with unique charm, including Kim Yoo-jung through Kim Jae-won, Kim Hye-yoon, Moon Sang-min, Michieda Shunsuke (미치에다 슌스케), Byun Woo-seok, Yoon Kyung-ho, Eum Moon-seok, Lee Da-hee, Lee Sang-i, Lee Jong-won, Jang Da-a, Heo Sung-tae, and Hye-ri, have confirmed their attendance, captivating the attention of global fans.

In the singer category, NEXZ (넥스지), Dahyun (WJSN), LNGSHOT (롱샷), RESCENE (리센느), MEOVV (미야오), Park Ji-hoon, Sung Han-bin, ALPHA DRIVE ONE (알파드라이브원), ATEEZ's Min-gi, Seong-hwa, and Woo-young, NMIXX (엔믹스), Yuna (ITZY), ZEROBASEONE (제로베이스원), Tzuyu (쯔위), Chaeryeong (ITZY), CORTIS (코르티스), QWER (큐더블유이알), CLOSE YOUR EYES (클로즈 유어 아이즈), KiiiKiii (키키), TOMORROW X TOGETHER (Tomorrow X Together), TWS (투어스), TUIDE (튜이드), and tripleS (트리플에스) have confirmed their participation, promising to deliver unforgettable moments for the audience with a diverse lineup.

In the actor category, Moon Sang-min, Yoon Kyung-ho, and Lee Da-hee, and in the singer category, NEXZ (넥스지), Dahyun (WJSN), LNGSHOT (롱샷), RESCENE (리센느), and tripleS (트리플에스) will participate on both Day 1 and Day 2 of 'AAA 2026'. Day 1 hosts Tzuyu and Sung Han-bin will attend the singer category on Day 2. ATEEZ's Min-gi, Seong-hwa, and Woo-young will shine on stage with individual special solo stages on Day 2.

This 'AAA 2026' features K-pop representative groups, solo artists, and local popular artists all gathering in one place to prepare a high-quality performance that will captivate fans worldwide. Additionally, actors who have shown unique presence in their respective works have confirmed their attendance, previewing an unprecedented scale awards ceremony encompassing both music and acting.

Meanwhile, 'AAA 2026', hosted by StarNews and organized by the AAA Organizing Committee (Jo Jik-wi (Director) Park Jun-cheol), MOTIVE, and D-SOW (디쇼), will be held over two days on December 5-6 at Kaohsiung National Stadium.
