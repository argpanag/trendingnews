What Kim Hellberg told Southampton's Finn Azaz after 'disrespectful' Boro celebration

TheTools · 28.11.2026 09:57 · source · permalink

KIM HELLBERG says he told Finn Azaz that he "he had to be better" after the Southampton forward's celebration against Middlesbrough at St Mary's on Friday night.

Former Boro player Azaz sprinted the full length of the pitch to the away end and mimicked crying after his goal sealed Southampton's 2-0 win.

Hellberg was seen speaking to Azaz shortly after the incident and then again approached the 26-year-old at full-time.

"I told him I think you have to do better, you cannot do things like that," explained Hellberg in his post-match press conference.

"That was a key thing to tell him. I know it's a lot of emotions and I know emotions can get the better of you, but that's what I told him, you have to be better in that occasion."

He was also asked by Sky about the incident and his conversation with Azaz, and said: "I told him it was unclever and disrespectful. It's our responsibility to try and be better but it's also a boy with a lot of emotions and sometimes that can get the better of you."

Azaz was also interviewed on Sky after the game, and said: "Do you know what, they sang about my family home and away last season, so I was just waiting for my moment to get one back."

Asked whether he had any regrets about the celebration, he replied: "None whatsoever."

Southampton boss Tonda Eckert - who fumed at his own club staff for some of the antics surrounding the game - said he would speak to Azaz about the celebration.

Azaz's goal sealed the victory for Southampton after Ryan Manning had put the home side ahead just before the hour mark.

Boro had their chances, with Will Lankshear missing a string of opportunities in both halves.

Hellberg said: "We were good. We were, for me, for 60 minutes the better side and created a lot of good opportunities.

"We won the ball high, our pressing was very good, we were brave on the ball. For a long time they've tried to make this a final.

"I'm very proud of how we handled that and we were the better team for 60 minutes. We should have scored in those moments and should have found a pass in some moments where we could have hurt them a bit more, maybe shooting instead of looking for the extra pass.

"Then I'm disappointed with the goal and it comes more of a scrappy game after that. I'm impressed by our players and taking control of the game, I'm disappointed with the result."
