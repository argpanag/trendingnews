Ultraman Teo Actress Noa Nakada, Who Played Kanna Izumi, Dies at 23, Leaving Fans in Shock

A defining franchise in Japanese tokusatsu entertainment, Ultraman launched its 60th anniversary series, Ultraman Teo, this July. Eighteen episodes have aired so far.

Tragic news emerged today that actress Noa Nakada, who played Kanna Izumi in the series, has died at the age of 23. Her agency, Toho Entertainment, officially announced her passing on November 16, leaving many fans stunned.

The full statement from Toho Entertainment reads:

Notice Regarding Our Actress Noa Nakada

We would like to express our sincere gratitude for your continued support.

We regret to announce that Noa Nakada, an actress represented by our agency, recently passed away.

We share this news with profound sadness and extend our deepest gratitude for the kindness and support she received during her lifetime.

In accordance with her family’s wishes, the wake and funeral have already been held privately, attended only by close relatives.

We offer our heartfelt thanks to the fans who supported her throughout her career, as well as everyone who worked with her and extended their kindness and assistance.

November 16, 2026

Toho Entertainment Co., Ltd.

Born in Ishikawa Prefecture, Japan, Noa Nakada entered the entertainment industry after becoming a finalist in the eighth Toho Cinderella Audition in 2016. In the currently airing Ultraman Teo, she appeared alongside Amane Kamiya, who participated in the same audition that year.

Nakada also appeared in the upcoming film Kanojo ni Attara, Yoroshiku to, which is scheduled to open in Japan on December 4.

Following the announcement of her death, the film’s production team stated: “After consulting with her family and obtaining their understanding, we have decided to proceed with the release as scheduled.” The team also expressed its gratitude to Nakada for the dedication and passion she brought to the film.

No cause of death has been disclosed. The unexpected announcement has left many fans in shock, mourning a young actress whose career was still unfolding. Her passing at just 23, while Ultraman Teo remains on the air, makes the news especially heartbreaking for those who had been watching and supporting her.
