LIVE! UFC 333 Results: Volkanovski vs. Evloev

TheTools · 24.10.2026 04:00 · source · permalink
  • UFC

Alexander Volkanovski defends title against Movsar Evloev at UFC 333

MMA Fighting has UFC 333 results for the Volkanovski vs. Evloev fight card, live blogs of the entire main card, and more from Etihad Arena in Abu Dhabi on Saturday.

In the main event, two-time UFC featherweight champion Alexander Volkanovski defends his title against the undefeated Movsar Evloev. Volkanovski is coming off of consecutive championship fight victories over Diego Lopes, while Evloev is 10-0 since making his UFC debut in 2019.

The co-main event features a trilogy bout between bantamweight champion Petr Yan and Merab Dvalishvili. Yan defeated Dvalishvili this past December at UFC 323 to avenge a 2023 loss and claim the 135-pound belt a second time. He also prevented Dvalishvili from becoming the first fighter to successfully defend a UFC title four times in a calendar year and snapped Dvalishvili’s 14-fight win streak.
