IDBI Bank shares surge 13% today in a weak market - Q2 results expectations, share price trend

TheTools · 14.10.2026 11:44 · source · permalink

New Delhi, Oct. 14 -- IDBI Bank shares surged as much as 13% in morning trade on the BSE on Wednesday, 14 October, defying weak stock market sentiment. IDBI Bank share price opened at Rs.84.32 against its previous close of Rs.82.92 and jumped as much as 12.90% to an intraday high of Rs.93.62. On the other hand, equity benchamrk Sensex delcined by about 0.20% in morning trade.
