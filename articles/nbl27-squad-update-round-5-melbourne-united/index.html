NBL27 Squad Update: Round 5 | Melbourne United

TheTools · 18.10.2026 05:33 · source · permalink

17 Oct

1

min read

Melbourne United guard Chris Goulding will miss Sunday’s Grassroots Game against the Illawarra Hawks at John Cain Arena.

Goulding has been progressing well in his recovery from a low-grade calf injury, but a recent illness has delayed his return. He is expected to be back in the lineup for Round 7.

Nominated Replacement Player Austin Shelley will remain in the lineup, while Development Players Jai Fa’Ale and Jensen Bradtke will also suit up for the contest.

Tip-off is 5pm, with tickets still available via AXS. Can’t make it to JCA? Watch the game live on ESPN via Kayo or Disney+.
