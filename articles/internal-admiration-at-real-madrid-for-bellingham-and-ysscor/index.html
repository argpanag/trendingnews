Internal admiration at Real Madrid for Bellingham and… | YSscores

TheTools · 22.09.2026 20:03 · source · permalink

Real Madrid sources have expressed great admiration for duo Jude Bellingham and Ibrahima Konaté, given the positive impression the players have left within the team in recent times.

According to journalist Rodra, Bellingham and Konaté are receiving special praise within the halls of Real Madrid due to their great discipline, continuous hard work in training, and commitment to a healthy lifestyle off the pitch.

Their constant desire for development and improvement is highly appreciated by those around the team, as the duo is keen to work continuously to raise their level and deliver their best.

This praise reflects the image that Real Madrid is trying to establish within the dressing room, by relying on players who possess professionalism, discipline, and a continuous desire to improve.
