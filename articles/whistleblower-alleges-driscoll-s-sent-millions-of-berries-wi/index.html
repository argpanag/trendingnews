Whistleblower alleges Driscoll’s sent millions of berries with excess chemical levels to Canada

TheTools · 26.09.2026 19:40 · source · permalink

A whistleblower lawsuit in California alleges millions of trays of fruit containing non-compliant levels of pesticides and chemicals crossed the border between 2022 and 2024. The suit also accuses Driscoll's fruit company of trying to cover it up. A previous version of this video contained imprecise language that has been updated.
