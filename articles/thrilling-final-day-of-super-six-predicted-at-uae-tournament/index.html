Thrilling final day of Super Six predicted at UAE tournament | ICC Women's Emerging Nations Trophy, 2026 | ICC

TheTools · 22.11.2026 23:49 · source · permalink

Three teams are battling it out to join Sri Lanka in the final of the Women's Emerging Nations Trophy.

The last spot in the final of the ICC Emerging Nations Trophy event in the UAE will up for grabs on Monday when the Super Six phase of the tournament comes to an end. 

Sri Lanka have already qualified for the title decider courtesy of their unbeaten run through six fixtures at the tournament, while Pakistan, Ireland and the Netherlands are the teams still in with a chance of joining them in the final.

Pakistan kept their hopes alive of reaching the final with an impressive nine-wicket triumph over Scotland on Sunday, but face undefeated Sri Lanka on Monday in a match they must win to have any chance of reaching the decider.

"I am very relieved," stand-in Pakistan captain Muneeba Ali said after the win over Scotland.

"It was a matter of points and net run rate and luckily we managed to improve both things today."

Pakistan romped to a nine-wicket victory over Scotland in the ICC Women's Emerging Nations Trophy after an impressive partnership between Muneeba Ali and Gull Feroza

A victory over Sri Lanka will put Pakistan in the box seat to reach the final, but then they will have to sit and wait and watch the Netherlands take on Ireland and hope the victor of that clash doesn't improve their net run rate enough to surpass them on the standings.

It's currently Ireland with the superior net run rate courtesy of their narrow three-wicket triumph over Zimbabwe on Sunday, while the Netherlands will be rueing a missed opportunity from the same day after they were skittled for just 57 in a nine-wicket loss to Sri Lanka.

Ireland v Zimbabwe | Match Highlights | ICC Women's Emerging Nations Trophy 2026

Ireland clinched a thriller against Zimbabwe to keep their unbeaten streak going in the Super Six stage of the ICC Women's Emerging Nations Trophy 2026.

It was the Netherlands first loss of the Super Six phase thus far, leaving the European side requiring victory over Ireland on Monday at the very least if they are to qualify for the final.

"Tomorrow against Ireland, we know what we need to do," stand-in Netherlands captain Sterre Kalis said.

"It's like a semi-final for us I reckon."

Scotland and Zimbabwe are out of contention for Wednesday's final, though the winner likely to rise the Super Six standings.
