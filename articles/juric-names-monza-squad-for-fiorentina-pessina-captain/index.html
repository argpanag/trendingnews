Juric names Monza squad for Fiorentina, Pessina captain

TheTools · 22.11.2026 20:26 · source · permalink

Juric has named 25 players for Monday's clash against Fiorentina on 23 November, with Matteo Pessina wearing the captain's armband.

By TuFutbolero Newsroom·November 22, 2026 at 12:26 PM

Monza coach Ivan Juric has released the squad list for the Monza home game against Fiorentina on Monday 23 November. According to TuttoMercatoWeb, 25 players made the cut, and Matteo Pessina is the captain.

The squad by position:

  • Goalkeepers: Tornqvist, Thiam and Strajnar
  • Defenders: Goglichidze, Lucchesi, Ziolkowski, Antov, Mangas, Birindelli, Bakoune, Touré, Carboni and Kouadio
  • Midfielders: Foe Ondoa, Akinsanmiro, Colombo, Zeballos, Pessina and Mout
  • Forwards: Varela, Cutrone, Forson, Colpani, Robinson and Mota Carvalho
