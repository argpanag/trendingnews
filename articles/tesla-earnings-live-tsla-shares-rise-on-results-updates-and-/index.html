Tesla Earnings Live: TSLA Shares Rise on Results — Updates and Analysis

TheTools · 21.10.2026 09:01 · source · permalink

Oct. 21, 2026 at 6:55pm ET

Musk declines further comment on possible tie-up between Tesla, SpaceX

Tesla shares turned lower in after-hours trading after the company reported flat sales for the third quarter.

Speaking on Tesla's conference call, CEO Elon Musk said he didn't have anything to add from his comments yesterday on the merger talks between Tesla and SpaceX. He described his discussion of the matter as "tap dancing through a minefield."

The electric vehicle maker reported $28.05 billion in sales, compared with $28.1 billion a year ago. Analysts had forecasted $28.06 billion, according to FactSet. Net income was $787 million, down from $1.37 billion a year ago. Analysts were expecting $782 million.

Tesla generated free cash flow of $1.5 billion in the quarter, while analysts expected the company’s cash burn to expand to $4.3 billion. But executives flagged that limited battery availability is holding up production. Shares were down about 2% afterhours.

The results followed the artificial-intelligence trade getting a big boost, thanks to SpaceX. Shares in Elon Musk’s rocket company surged after it beat Wall Street’s expectations for revenue, including from its AI business. That helped lift the Nasdaq, which led indexes higher with a 1% gain. Chip stocks also gained, including Nvidia, advanced after bullish comments from CEO Jensen Huang during a keynote in Berlin.

—By Owen Tucker-Smith
