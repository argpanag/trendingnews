SRIT India IPO Day 3 Subscription Status 30 Sep 2026

TheTools · 30.09.2026 01:10 · source · permalink

SRIT India IPO closes today, 30 Sep 2026. Subscribed 4.16x through Day 2: Retail 6.05x, NII 5.25x, QIB 0.03x. Price band Rs 123-130.

Updated: 30 Sept 2026 • 1:10 am

Posted by:

Quick Answer

The SRIT India IPO is on its final day of bidding on 30 September 2026, with the subscription window closing today. The issue has surged from 1.00 times at the Day 1 close to 4.16 times by the close of Day 2, with retail at 6.05 times and NII at 5.25 times, while QIB, excluding anchors, remained thin at 0.03 times. With bidding open until the end of today's session, the final subscription figure depends on demand in the closing hours.

SRIT India Limited, a Bengaluru-based IT and IT-enabled services company, has reached the final day of its IPO subscription window today, 30 September 2026. The SRIT India IPO opened on 28 September 2026 and is a mainboard, book built issue worth about Rs 218.40 crore, entirely a fresh issue. The shares are proposed to list on both NSE and BSE.

Demand for the SRIT India IPO has built sharply through the window, driven by retail and non-institutional investors, while institutions have held back so far. Today is the last window for QIB bids.

How to Apply for the SRIT India IPO

  1. Open a Demat and trading account with a SEBI-registered broker such as Univest if you do not already have one.
  2. Select the issue on your broker app or net banking platform and enter your bid quantity and price within the Rs 123 to Rs 130 band, in multiples of 115 shares.
  3. Submit the application through the UPI-based ASBA method so the bid amount is blocked rather than debited immediately.

FAQs

What is the SRIT India IPO subscription status on Day 3?

Ans. On the closing day, 30 September 2026, the issue carries forward a Day 2 reading of around 4.16 times overall, with retail at 6.05 times, NII at 5.25 times and QIB at 0.03 times, and figures are updating through today's final session.

When does the SRIT India IPO close?

Ans. The issue closes for subscription today, 30 September 2026, having opened on 28 September 2026.

What is the SRIT India IPO price band and lot size?

Ans. The issue price band is Rs 123 to Rs 130 per share, with a lot size of 115 shares, taking the minimum retail investment to about Rs 14,950 at the upper price band.

Which category led the SRIT India IPO subscription?

Ans. Retail investors led the issue through Day 2 at 6.05 times, closely followed by NII at 5.25 times, while QIB, excluding anchors, stood at 0.03 times.

How do I apply for the SRIT India IPO before it closes?

When will the SRIT India IPO be allotted and listed?

Ans. The issue allotment is expected to be finalised on 1 October 2026, with listing on both NSE and BSE tentatively scheduled for 6 October 2026.

What does SRIT India Limited do?

Ans. SRIT India Limited is a Bengaluru-based IT and IT-enabled services company offering digital solutions, custom application development and system integration services across healthcare, e-governance, and telecom and broadband verticals.
