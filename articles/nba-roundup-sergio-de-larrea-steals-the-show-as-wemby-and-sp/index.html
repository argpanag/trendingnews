NBA Roundup: Sergio de Larrea steals the show as Wemby and Spurs beat Dallas

TheTools · 25.10.2026 09:07 · source · permalink

Aris Barkas

Make Your Preferred Basketball Source.

The Spaniard was one of the few bright spots for Dallas in their defeat by San Antonio

By Eurohoops team/ info@eurohoops.net

Rookie from Spain Sergio de Larrea led the Mavericks with 21 points off the bench, going 3-for-4 from the arc, but the Spurs got the 122-106 road win.

French superstar Victor Wembanyama had 23 points, seven rebounds, and four blocks; Dylan Harper scored 22 points.

San Antonio got its first win of the season, while Dallas played without their two marquee players, Cooper Flagg and Kyrie Irving.

Jokic one triple-double away from 200

Nikola Jokic scored eight of his 35 points in overtime and finished with 15 rebounds and 10 assists, and the short-handed Denver Nuggets rallied from down 23 to beat the New Orleans Pelicans 141-135 on Saturday night.

Jokic had his 199th career triple-double in the regular season, second to Russell Westbrook, who retired with 209.

Schroder and Sengun with the wins, Giannis in trouble

Brandon Miller scored 34 points, and the Charlotte Hornets beat the winless Miami Heat 115-110, with German Dennis Schroder adding 25 points and Giannis Antetokounmpo being limited to 14 points due to foul trouble.

The Cleveland Cavaliers won their home opener, 125-121 over the Brooklyn Nets, with Russian Egor Demin scoring 22 points for the losing side.

Turkish big man Alperen Sengun had a double-double with 17 points and 10 rebounds as the Houston Rockets beat the Atlanta Hawks 121-114.

Devin Booker scored 43 points, and the Phoenix Suns won their home opener for the fifth straight season by beating the short-handed Golden State Warriors 131-122.

Stephen Curry (right knee), Brandin Podziemski (low back), Draymond Green (low back), and Al Horford (low back) were all missing for the losing side.
