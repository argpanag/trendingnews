Alan Shearer and Jamie Carragher agree on Newcastle vs Arsenal score prediction

TheTools · 21.11.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

Alan Shearer and Jamie Carragher both believe Arsenal will come away from Newcastle with all three points.

The Gunners have won their last five games in the Premier League and, in fact, have dropped points in only one league game this season. In that period they have also kept an incredible seven clean sheets.

The Magpies, meanwhile, currently sit 12th and have struggled for consistency under Matthias Jaissle. They have won once in their last five games but the atmosphere at St James' Park is expected to be fiery, meaning previous form may not matter come 5:30pm on Saturday.

Shearer, the Premier League's all-time goalscorer with 260 goals, believes it will be a "tough game" for both teams. Speaking to Betfair, the former Newcastle striker said: "This is a tough one to predict. I'd love to see Newcastle win, but that would be my heart ruling my head. I think it's a tough game because of the way Arsenal are playing at this moment in time, and the way Newcastle are. I have to say an away win.

"The problem is Arsenal are so brilliant defensively. Over the last few years, Arsenal have known that coming to St James' Park because of the atmosphere is a tough game, and I'm expecting the same, but I just think that Arsenal might nick it by an odd goal."

Carragher agrees with Shearer and reckons Arsenal will keep their lead at the top of the table to at least five points. In his Sky Bet Super 6 prediction, the former Liverpool man backed the Gunners to win 3-1.

He said: "After Arsenal stretched their lead at the top to five points, I'm confident they'll leave Newcastle with a win!"

Chris Sutton, meanwhile, believes Arsenal will record their eighth clean sheet of the season against Newcastle. In his BBC column, where he predicted a 2-0 Arsenal win, Sutton said: "I am covering this game and I think it is a huge one for Newcastle boss Matthias Jaissle. he Magpies have picked up as many points - 12 - in their first 10 games under Jaissle as they did when Eddie Howe was in charge last season, but I still don't think their fans are overly convinced by him.

"Some of their unease is because Newcastle don't seem to have a defined style of play, and can be a bit kamikaze going forward. Because of that, I just think Arsenal will pick them off, and win comfortably.

"The Gunners have conceded only five league goals so far and have made an incredible start to their title defence with nine wins out of 10. The worry for their rivals is that it feels to me Mikel Arteta's side have not even got going yet, but I still don't see anyone being close to them."
