Man City fans stay away from 5-1 Champions League rout of AEK Athens

MANCHESTER, England (AP) — Manchester City played in front of thousands of empty seats as fans stayed away from the Champions League clash against AEK Athens on Tuesday.

A well-below capacity crowd of 40,042 attended City's 5-1 win, with swathes of empty rows visible inside the newly-expanded 61,000-seater Etihad Stadium.

The lowest attendance for a City home game this season is around 32,000 for the English League Cup match against second-division Norwich last month.

All other home matches this season have had attendances of 60,000-plus.

“I think the ones that were in the stadium, they were good,” City coach Enzo Maresca said. “In the last game against Ipswich they were top, and now we have next week two games at home against Brighton and I have no doubt that the environment will be top again.”

The low turnout was a surprise given City fans have been in defiant mood since the club was found guilty of widespread financial wrongdoing, which could see it face a range of punishments including a huge fine, points deduction or even expulsion from the Premier League.

More than 60,000 attended City's last Champions League game against Paris Saint-Germain, when the embattled club put on a pre-match fireworks show and a highlights reel of its greatest moments on the stadium’s big screens in what could be interpreted as a defiant response to the ruling, which it has appealed.

Fans have turned up with inflatable bananas, resurrecting a craze from the 1980s long before it became the dominant force in English soccer via the backing of its Emerati ownership. But there were few on show on this occasion.

Perhaps the low turnout reflected the fact it was City’s third home game in less than a week, following matches against Ipswich and PSG.

The traveling AEK fans chanted relentlessly despite its heavy loss.

“I don’t want to comment on the home team attendance. This is their thing,” AEK coach Marko Nikolić said. "I was focused on our fans and our team because I really enjoy being coach of this team.

"I hope you’re familiar with the ambience in our stadium. It’s sold out practically every game in the league and in Europe. These people follow us and I can remember now some away games during the last two years. It’s amazing and I am proud of how they follow the team. And you see after a big defeat also in the end of the game, how they escort the team from the pitch.

“It’s an obligation for us next time to be a bit more brave.”

The City fans who did stay away missed another slick performance from Maresca's team, which tops the Premier League and quickly bounced back from defeat to PSG with another win in the Champions League.

In the absence of the benched Erling Haaland, Phil Foden scored a hat trick, with Elliot Anderson and Rayan Cherki also on target.

Stavros Pilios replied for AEK.

PARIS (AP) — Paris Saint-Germain followed up its four goals against Manchester City with another four against Barcelona in the Champions League.

Two big tests passed and PSG’s bid for a third straight title is off to a rampant start with three wins in a row and 14 goals scored overall.

The 4-2 victory at home to five-time champion Barcelona on Tuesday followed the 4-2 win at City last week.

Home and away, PSG is showing its attacking might, the depth of its squad and the self-belief of its players.

“I have the best players in the world, at least for me they are the best," PSG coach Luis Enrique said. "That's what I always tell them.”

Despite losing Ousmane Dembélé and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia to injury, PSG found a way through as its relentless pressure led to two late goals against previously-unbeaten Barcelona in a blockbuster.

“We have the ability to overcome difficult situations,” Luis Enrique said. “It shows what type of team we are, with the capacity to adapt all the time. It’s easier for us to keep going because we can feel the energy of our fans."

A brilliant strike from Vitinha in the 81st minute and a close-range goal from Fabian Ruiz sealed victory for the two-time defending champion.

“We never feel like we're powerless, or that we're beaten in any way," said Désiré Doué, who scored PSG's opening goal. "We always know there’s an opportunity as long as the final whistle hasn’t gone.”

By the end, jubilant PSG fans taunted Barcelona’s players with chants of “Olé” every time they touched the ball.

Not even a latest mesmerizing solo goal from 19-year-old star Lamine Yamal was enough for Barca as the Catalan club's 11-game winning run came to an end at Parc des Princes.

Yamal equalized for free-scoring Barcelona for the second time, when his goal in the 79th minute made it 2-2.

The 19-year-old forward chested down a botched headed clearance from substitute Warren Zaire-Emery and ghosted past him down the right flank with ease before stabbing the ball inside the post. It was the Catalan club's 50th goal in 12 games this season, 10 of them for Spain sensation Yamal.

Two minutes later, PSG was back in front as inspirational midfielder Vitinha controlled the ball with his chest on the edge of the area and slammed a powerful shot past Joan García into the bottom corner.

Doué had scored with a fine effort late in the first half, while his corner was headed in by Lucas Hernandez in the 72nd.

Right back Havi Espart ran through unopposed and struck a low shot into the bottom corner to make it 1-1.

When Barcelona was on top, PSG's forwards defended from the front in a team which has gained in maturity and leadership.

“We have to highlight the work of the entire team,” Doué said. "The way our defenders worked, the midfielders, the attackers, it was incredible. We all did the job today.”

The only sour notes were the injuries, with captain Marquinhos also coming off in the second half.
