Commuter faints, falls on LRT tracks

TheTools · 27.11.2026 12:14 · source · permalink

The emergency brakes were activated on an approaching train, and no passengers were injured.

RapidKL said the commuter was immediately helped off the tracks by station officers, and was taken to the emergency room for treatment.

The platform intrusion emergency stop system was activated, and the emergency brakes on an approaching train were activated to allow safety measures to be taken, RapidKL said.

“No passengers were injured,” it said in a statement.
