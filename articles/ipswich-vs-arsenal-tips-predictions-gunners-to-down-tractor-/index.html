Ipswich vs Arsenal prediction

Newly-promoted Ipswich have had a solid start to the new season, picking up six points from a possible 12 in the Premier League.

The Tractor Boys strolled past crisis club Leicester City 3-1 in their second-round showdown, to set-up this heavyweight clash with Arsenal at Portman Road.

The Gunners have been in formidable form so far this season, with five wins in five matches in all competitions, beating Napoli and Sunderland away from home in the last week.

Mikel Arteta's men have only conceded one goal in this time, with a combination of Cristhian Mosquera, Ezri Konsa and Gabriel Magalhaes having been almost impenetrable in the centre of defence.

But the Gunners boss is likely to shuffle his pack with games coming thick and fast, which could spell an opportunity for Ipswich to catch Arsenal cold on the night.

Ipswich boss Gary O'Neil has said 'we'll give it a right go' and will field a strong side, looking to go as deep in the cup competitions as possible.

Zian Flemming, opening his Ipswich account by scoring a 90th minute winner against Crystal Palace last weekend, will boost their threat.

The Dutchman scored 11 goals in the Premier League for Burnley last season and could give the Arsenal defence a tough night.

Ipswich vs Arsenal tips

Watertight Arsenal

The Gunners have faced just 12 shots on target in five games, conceding just once in that time. New summer signing Ezri Konsa fitting into the back four like a glove.

Ipswich have scored 10 goals but haven't come up against a backline as strong as Arsenal's.

Arteta may make a raft of changes but it's hard to see Ipswich getting much joy in front of goal.

Arsenal to win to nil - 13/10 with Betfred

Big Vik on target

Viktor Gyorekes has started just once so far this season, and didn't have much joy in front of goal against Napoli.

But the Swedish striker, who netted 14 Premier League goals last season, should get his chance against Ipswich to get his campaign really up and running.

The 28-year-old scored five goals against newly-promoted clubs in the league last season. At his best, Gyorekes is a poacher in front of goal and we're backing him to test the keeper on a handful of occasions.

Viktor Gyorekes 2.5+ shots on target - 5/1 with Tote

Enciso to shoot on sight

Julio Enciso has been in impressive form this season. Having returned to the Premier League after a couple of years, the former Brighton man has been one of Ipswich's biggest protagonists.

The Paraguayan has already had 14 shots across the campaign, and while he hasn't notched in the league, he will likely want to give the Portman Road crowd something to cheer about.

Expect Enciso not to be scared to take shots on when he gets a sight of goal.
