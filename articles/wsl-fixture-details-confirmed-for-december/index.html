WSL fixture details confirmed for December

TheTools · 31.10.2026 18:22 · source · permalink

The fixture details for our two Barclays Women’s Super League matches in December have been confirmed following broadcast selections.

Our Round 12 clash sees us take on Everton at Goodison Park on Friday,December 11. The match will kick off at 7pm and will be broadcast live on Sky Sports.

Round 13 sees us head to south east London as we take on Charlton Athletic on Sunday, December 20 with a kick-off time of 12pm. The game will be broadcast live on Sky Sports.
