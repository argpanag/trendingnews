Five new TV shows and movies to stream this weekend - The Boston Globe

TheTools · 19.09.2026 04:28 · source · permalink

The season of sweaters, apple cider doughnuts, and pumpkin spice lattes is almost upon us, with the pending arrival of autumn also bringing a ton of new TV shows and movies to streaming. Here are five new titles to watch this weekend.

New movie to stream

‘Stop! That! Train!’

Director Adam Shankman’s campy and fierce disaster movie parody “Stop! That! Train!” streams on Netflix beginning on Sunday. Drag stars Ginger Minj and Lowell’s own Jujubee play attendants on a fancy and fast train barreling toward a dangerous storm, who need some help from President Gagwell (RuPaul Charles) to avert catastrophe. The comedy also features appearances by Sarah Michelle Gellar, Raven-Symoné, Jesse Tyler Ferguson, Charo, Joel McHale, and Rachel Bloom. Available to stream beginning Sunday on Netflix

New TV to stream

‘The Howard Stern Show’

It’s a new era for “The Howard Stern Show,” as the longtime shock jock brings his radio show to HBO Max. The first episode is now streaming, with new episodes set to drop weekly on Thursdays. Available on HBO Max

‘Stranger Things: Tales From ’85’

From a new season of “Slow Horses” to the debut of “Monster: The Lizzie Borden Story,” there’s a plethora of new TV to check out (see this week’s TV GPS for a full rundown). Among the long list of small-screen premieres is season 2 of Netflix’s “Stranger Things” animated spinoff, “Tales from ’85.” In the latest installment, the Hawkins gang must face another supernatural threat; this time, with Valentine’s Day on the horizon. Available on Netflix

Binge-worthy TV pick

‘MobLand’

Another show returning this week is the drama “MobLand,” which kicks off its second season Friday on Paramount+. Helen Mirren and Pierce Brosnan are back as the matriarch and patriarch of the Harrigan family, along with their fixer Harry (Tom Hardy), as they face new threats and navigate their world of crime. New episodes debut Fridays on Paramount+ through Nov. 20, with season 1 now available to binge-watch on the streamer. Available on Paramount+

Movie night pick

‘Supergirl’

DC’s “Supergirl” flick may have been box office kryptonite, but having watched the latest superhero adventure, I didn’t think it it was nearly as bad as some folks made it seem. Now streaming on HBO Max, the movie stars Milly Alcock as Superman’s cousin Kara Zor-El, who, along with her super-powered pup Krypto, decides to celebrate her birthday with a space-faring pub crawl. However, after an evil alien poisons her pet, Supergirl teams up with the young Ruthye (Eve Ridley), whose parents were killed by the same alien, for a mission of revenge and to save Kara’s dog. Available on HBO Max
