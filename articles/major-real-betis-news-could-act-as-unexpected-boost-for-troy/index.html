  • Major Real Betis Development Could Act As Unexpected Boost For Troy Parrott

While Troy Parrott has experienced an exciting start to life at Real Betis, it's fair to say many Ireland supporters have been slightly disappointed by the amount of playing time he has received at the club.

The striker has been heavily rotated by manager Manuel Pellegrini, rarely starting two games in a row. That has been the case since the return from the international break, with Parrott in the team for just one of their three games.

Pellegrini said last week that the Irishman would continue to be rotated for the foreseeable future. Real Betis are currently competing on three fronts and will face a very busy period in their schedule up until the Christmas break.

However, that situation may now have changed.

Troy Parrott receives Real Betis opportunity

Troy Parrott has shared the starting striker spot in the Real Betis team with Colombian forward Cucho this season, with both players performing well.

Cucho has been the more prolific in front of goal in recent times having scored three goals in his last six clubs appearances. However, he is now set for a spell on the sidelines.

Real Betis have confirmed that Cucho will be out of action for at least one month after suffering a muscular injury. The issue occurred during the loss to Barcelona over the weekend, with the player having come off just after the hour mark.

While this latest development will cause a headache for Manuel Pellegrini and his coaching staff, it does also present quite the opportunity for Troy Parrott.

Cucho's injury should result in far more playing time for the Ireland star, who is now the only fit established central striker in the squad. That should result in a major increase in playing time for the duration of the injury, starting with Wednesday's Champions League game against Feyenoord.

Troy Parrott set to feature in some big Real Betis fixtures

Troy Parrott is already a hugely popular figure among Real Betis supporters, with his performances on the pitch having won over quite a few of those who had questions about his arrival at the club.

He has scored a couple of hugely important goals this season, netting the winners against both Real Madrid and Lille. The latter of those goals secured the club's first Champions League victory in over two decades.

Parrott is set to start in the home game against Feyenoord tomorrow, with games against Celta Vigo, Malaga, and Borussia Dortmund to come over the next couple of weeks.

Manuel Pellegrini will still be careful with his playing time over that stretch, but it will be an opportunity to nail down his place in the first choice XI moving forward.
