(WLUK) -- A chilly start to the weekend over Northeast Wisconsin. A Freeze Warning is in effect until 9 a.m. this morning as low temperatures drop into the mid-20s to low 30s across Northeast Wisconsin. A Frost Advisory also remains in effect for Door County, where temperatures are expected to fall into the mid-30s.

After the cold start, a high-pressure system is expected to keep the weekend dry and sunny. Afternoon highs on Saturday should reach the mid-50s, then warm into the low to mid-60s by Sunday.

Winds are expected to pick back up Sunday, with southwest gusts of 25-30 mph. That could make for a breezy scene at Lambeau Field for the home Packers game against the Dallas Cowboys, with kickoff temperatures in the upper 50s and readings falling into the 40s by the end of the game.

Looking ahead, the extended forecast calls for mild, mostly dry weather during the upcoming work week, with highs reaching the 60s. There’s also a chance temperatures could reach the low 70s by the end of the week. A midweek system could bring a chance for precipitation, but chances look fairly low for Northeast Wisconsin.
