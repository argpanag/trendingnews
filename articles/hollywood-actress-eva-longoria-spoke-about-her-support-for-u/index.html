Hollywood actress Eva Longoria spoke about her support for Ukraine

TheTools · 06.10.2026 16:58 · source · permalink

At the start of the full-scale invasion, Hollywood actress Eva Longoria deliberately showed her support for Ukraine by choosing outfits from Ukrainian brands for public events and red carpet appearances.

In particular, the star walked the red carpet in Cannes wearing a dress by a Ukrainian designer to publicly express her solidarity. At the Le Défilé L’Oréal Paris show during Fashion Week, she appeared in a black corset and silk skirt by designer Alina Balykina. For a photo shoot on the cover of Madame Figaro Paris, Longoria also chose several looks from the Anna October brand, including a red backless dress, a white jacket-style dress, and a jumpsuit with cutouts.

The actress shared this in an interview with Ukrainian entrepreneur Tetyana Parfilyeva.

Mark Cherry, the creator, screenwriter, and producer of the popular series *Desperate Housewives*, has an idea to make a prequel set in 1966.

And Netflix has confirmed the release of the film “Peaky Blinders,” starring Oscar-winning actor Cillian Murphy as Thomas Shelby.
