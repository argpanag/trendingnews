How do you rate Gabriel Jesus’ performance in first Barcelona start against Getafe?

TheTools · 10.10.2026 23:00 · source · permalink
  • Cant del Blog: Barcelona Opinion

Time to talk the Brazilian

Gabriel Jesus made his first start for Barcelona in Saturday’s comfortable win against Getafe, and the Brazilian did a very good job as a replacement for the injured Raphinha.

Not only did Jesus find the net for the third time in the Blaugrana shirt with a very good solo goal, he had a complete display with his ability to move all over the final third to link up with his teammates and start the press high up the pitch.

It was a strong display from the new signing, who is certainly in contention to remain in the lineup for Tuesday’s Champions League trip to Galatasaray.

So today’s Barca Blaugranes Question of the Day is:

How do you rate Gabriel Jesus’ performance in first Barcelona start against Getafe?
