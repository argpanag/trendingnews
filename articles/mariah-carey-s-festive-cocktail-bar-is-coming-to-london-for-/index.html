Mariah Carey's festive cocktail bar is coming to London for first time

TheTools · 20.10.2026 14:50 · source · permalink

The Queen of Christmas is bringing her festive magic to London this November, with Mariah Carey's Holiday Bar opening in the capital for the first time.

Mariah Carey's Holiday Bar will open at the O2 on November 19, marking the first international stop for the immersive cocktail experience, which has already enjoyed two successful seasons in the United States.

The pop-up is a collaboration between Mariah Carey, event platform Bucket Listers, and her signature Black Irish cream liqueur.

Mariah Carey's Christmas-themed pop-up bar will open at The O2 (Image: Mariah Carey's Holiday Bar)

Ms Carey said: "The holidays are my favorite time of year, and I love finding new ways to share the magic with my fans. "This year, we're making my holiday bars even more fabulous and bringing the celebration to more cities than ever before! "Think festive cocktails, wall-to-wall sparkle, and plenty of surprises. "I can't wait for everyone to celebrate with us!"

Guests can expect a full Mariah-inspired winter wonderland, complete with festive décor, interactive photo opportunities, themed experiences, and a menu of holiday cocktails featuring Carey's Black Irish cream liqueur.

Andy Lederman, founder and CEO of Bucket Listers, said: "Mariah Carey's Holiday Bar has quickly become a beloved holiday tradition. "This year we're taking the experience to an entirely new level, going international and doubling our amount of cities, introducing unforgettable new moments, and bringing even more of Mariah's signature glamour and holiday magic to guests!"

The Queen of Christmas is bringing a festive cocktail experience to London (Image: Mariah Carey's Holiday Bar)

The holiday bar will also open in seven US cities this season: New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Atlanta, Boston, Houston, and San Diego.

London's edition promises drink menus and décor designed to immerse guests in Carey's signature holiday glamour.

Mariah-inspired cocktails will be crafted using Black Irish Original Irish Cream, Salted Caramel Irish Cream, and White Chocolate Irish Cream.

Featured drinks will include the Make My Wish Come True, MC Toasted Tini, and Imperfect Angel, all designed to add extra sparkle to the season.

Mona Scott-Young, chief marketing officer of Black Irish, said: "Mariah knows how to make the holidays feel extraordinary, and Black Irish brings that same spirit to the glass. "For our third year with Mariah Carey's Holiday Bar, we're serving up decadent cocktails, a little extra glamour and plenty of reasons to gather and celebrate. "It's the season to indulge, and we're making every toast a little more Mariah."

The bar is designed as a Mariah-themed winter dreamscape, featuring wall-to-wall decorations, interactive moments, and seasonal surprises that capture Carey's unique style.

The experience is recommended for both die-hard Carey fans and anyone looking for a festive night out.

This year's expansion reflects growing demand and the enduring appeal of Carey's connection to Christmas.

The singer will also perform three sold-out UK arena shows in Birmingham, London, and Manchester starting November 27, as part of her Christmastime tour.

Additional tour dates include stops in Brazil, Malta, and the United Arab Emirates.

To purchase tickets for the Holiday Bar, fans can join the waitlist on the Bucket Listers website for early notification of availability.

The immersive pop-up will open just days before Carey's London arena show and promises to deliver what organisers describe as the ultimate festive celebration of the woman who has "practically become synonymous with Christmas."

Fans are encouraged to follow @mariahsholidaybar on social media for the latest updates, special events, and promotions throughout the holiday season.
