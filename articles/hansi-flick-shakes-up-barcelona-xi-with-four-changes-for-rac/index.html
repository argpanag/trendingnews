Hansi Flick shakes up Barcelona XI with four changes for Racing clash

TheTools · 16.09.2026 18:49 · source · permalink

LALIGA

Flick continues to rotate his squad, making four changes against Racing from the lineup that started against Levante

  • Javier Gascón

    Journalist

Journalist

Actualizado el 16/09/2026 14:49 EDT

Barcelona has officially announced the starting lineup Hansi Flick will field against Racing at 3:30 p.m. ET at Spotify Camp Nou. The Catalan side is seeking its sixth consecutive victory to maintain a perfect start to the LaLiga season, with a possible 18 points from six matches guaranteeing first place.

Continuing his pattern of making around three rotations per game, the German coach has made changes from the team that started Sunday’s match at Levante. Andreas Christensen, João Cancelo, Dani Olmo and Karim Adeyemi come into the lineup, replacing Pau Cubarsí, Xavi Espart, Fermín López and Anthony Gordon. The biggest surprise is Cubarsí being rested, while the other changes are more in line with Flick’s usual rotation policy.

Barcelona will therefore start with Joan Garcia; Eric Garcia, Andreas Christensen, Gerard Martín, João Cancelo; Rodri Hernández, Pedri González; Lamine Yamal, Dani Olmo, Karim Adeyemi; and Raphinha Dias.

The substitutes are Wojciech Szczesny and Dominik Livakovic; defenders Jules Koundé, Pau Cubarsí, Xavi Espart and Alejandro Balde; midfielders Pablo Páez “Gavi,” Marc Bernal and Fermín López; and forwards Anthony Gordon, Gabriel Jesus and Hamza Abdelkarim.

Frenkie de Jong and Roony Bardghji miss out through injury, while academy player Brian Fariñas was also left out of the 23 man squad. With 24 players available, Fariñas was the player omitted for tactical reasons.
