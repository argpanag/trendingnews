The Golden State Warriors will be without Jimmy Butler, Kristaps Porzingis, Moses Moody, and Gui Santos as they prepare to face the Miami Heat at home on Thursday night.

The Golden State Warriors are set to host the Miami Heat tonight, but will be without four players due to injuries.

Warriors stars Jimmy Butler and Kristaps Porzingis highlight the injury report, as both have been sidelined since the start of the season.

Butler is still recovering from a right ACL injury he suffered last season. Meanwhile, Porzingis is not expected to return to the team at all, with the team reportedly withholding pay from the player, who has been away since the start of training camp.

Moses Moody also remains out for Golden State due to a left patellar tendon injury, while Gui Santos is ruled out for this game due to left knee tendinitis.

Everyone else, including Stephen Curry, Draymond Green, and Yaxel Lendeborg, is set to play tonight.

The Warriors have opened the season with 5 wins in their first 8 games.

Meanwhile, their opponents, the Miami Heat, have struggled to open the new season and currently hold a 3-5 record. They are coming off a last-second victory against the Los Angeles Clippers, in which former Warriors star Klay Thompson hit a game-winner.

The game at Chase Center tonight will begin at 10:00 PM ET.
