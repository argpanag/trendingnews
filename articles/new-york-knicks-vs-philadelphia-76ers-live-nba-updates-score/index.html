New York Knicks vs. Philadelphia 76ers Live NBA Updates, Scores and Highlights

Your long wait is over. The 2026 NBA Cup has officially arrived. We tip things off on Friday at Xfinity Mobile Arena in an opening-night rematch between the Philadelphia 76ers and New York Knicks.

Though Philly picked up the W last time around, New York has rattled off two consecutive come-from-behind wins amid Jalen Brunson rounding into form. The Sixers may also have to wage battle without Jaylen Brown, who was listed as "doubtful" on the latest injury report due to left patellar tendinopathy.

Jose Alvarado (ankle) remains out for the Knicks. Otherwise, they'll be at full strength as they look to reach .500 for the first time this season.

Nick Nurse Offers High Praise for Tyrese Maxey

On a team so laden with big names, most of whom prefer to operate on-ball, many wondered whether Tyrese Maxey might get lost in Philly's saturated hierarchy.

Like he has on numerous other versions of the Sixers, though, he continues to prove his stardom will scale to any situation. And if there was ever any doubt over who'd be this team's best player, he's put that to bed, too.

The Sixers' Starters Have Yet to Play Together

As expected, Dean Wade is sliding into Philly's starting five alongside Tyrese Maxey, LeBron James, VJ Edgecombe and Joel Embiid. While that's the right call relative to defensive volume the Sixers must paper over without Brown, this particular fivesome has yet to log a single minute together.

The biggest question: Does this group have enough shooting? Brown isn't the most deadeye sniper, but he's shooting well from deep to start the season. Generally speaking, he's also a more willing three-point taker than Wade.

Inserting Anfernee Simons instead was almost assuredly a non-starter. But with LeBron shooting 25 percent on looks away from the rim, the offensive output of this quintet is worth monitoring.

Karl-Anthony Towns is a GO for the Knicks

With the exception of Jose Alvarado (ankle), the Knicks will have their full cast of characters available to open the NBA Cup.

As far as Karl-Anthony Towns' wrist injury goes, Knicks fans can breathe a sigh of relief. Well that, or they should be terrified he's pushing the bill too hard in the good name of defending New York's NBA Cup title.
