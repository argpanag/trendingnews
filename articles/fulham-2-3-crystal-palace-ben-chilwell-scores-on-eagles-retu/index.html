    Hull v Aston Villa (17:30 BST)

    Chris Sutton
    BBC football expert

    Hull City will be on such a high after winning their first two games, and that makes this game even harder to predict.

    The Tigers are really well organised and are not bothered about how much possession they have, they are just bothered about being effective when they do get the ball.

    They defend the box brilliantly but there will still be a few weeks this season where teams run all over them because of the way they are set up.

    Aston Villa won't necessarily do that, but I still think they will come out on top despite their poor start to the new campaign.

    Ollie Watkins is a big loss but they have got Nicolas Jackson in to lead their line, and I think he's a good signing.

    It took Villa six games to register their first league win last season, but they won't have to wait as long this time.

    Sutton's prediction: 0-2

  4. 'I think we played well'published at 17:21 BST
    17:21 BST

    FT: Nottingham Forest 0-0 Tottenham

    Tottenham Hotspur

    Tottenham boss Roberto de Zerbi speaking to Sky Sports: "Disappointed for the result but for the performance very happy. Considering we started to work together last Thursday for the first time, for the first day. I think we can improve in the last decision, in the last pass. I remember the situation with [Omar] Marmoush when he shot and [Andy] Robertson was behind him overlapping. If we improve in the last third I think we can score many goals because we have the players with big qualities. Now is a tough moment to accept this result because I think we played well.

    "We have to work because I think we played very well with the ball, we found the solution. I think we can be much better in the final third."

    On the second half: "In the second half, I'm sorry because the medical team pushed to change Savio and Destiny Udogie after 60 minutes. [Mohammed] Kudus and [Andy] Robertson played very well but maybe two substitutions in that moment was not so good but now we cannot take risks again to injure Savio, he is too important to us and the same [with] Destiny. Maybe we lost some balance, some connection. We have to respect Nottingham Forest because they played always second balls and long balls."

    On injuries in the squad: "We have to improve in every part of football, organisation with and without the ball. Even today I thought we defended very well. We bought very, very good players but you can't just build the team with the names like in Football Manager."

    On Forest's disallowed goal: "I didn't see the replay, I can't answer your question."

As it stands...published at 17:03 BST
17:03 BST

Ben Chilwell has scored in back-to-back appearances for Crystal Palace, having scored in the final game of his first spell at the club against Wolves in May 2025

Ben Chilwell scored on his Crystal Palace return as the Eagles came from behind twice to earn a 3-2 win at Fulham.

Chilwell, 29, re-signed for Palace on Tuesday on a free transfer from Strasbourg, having spent the second half of the 2024-25 season there on loan from Chelsea.

Within three minutes of replacing fellow left wing-back Tyrick Mitchell off the bench, who scored Palace's first two goals, Chilwell latched on to a loose ball as Fulham failed to clear an attack from Pierre Sage's side and thumped the ball home.

The Whites had initially gone ahead early in the first half via a deflected effort from teenager Josh King, only to be cancelled out by Mitchell's close-range effort from a tight angle.

Spanish winger Cesar Palacios then opened his Fulham account following his summer move from Real Madrid to give Alvaro Arbeloa's side a lead at the break.

Mitchell equalised for a second time at the start of the second half, pouncing as the hosts made a mess of clearing his initial effort before his intervention trickled in to level the game.

Victory for Palace sees Sage claim his first win as head coach after he replaced Oliver Glasner over the summer.

Meanwhile, Fulham have lost three straight league games since the arrival of ex-Real boss Arbeloa.

Match Facts

  • Fulham have won just two of their last 13 Premier League matches against Crystal Palace (D6 L5), with both wins coming at Selhurst Park in 2022-23 and 2024-25.

  • Crystal Palace lost their first away Premier League game at Fulham in 2004-05 but are since unbeaten in seven at Craven Cottage (W4 D3).

  • Fulham are looking to avoid losing three successive Premier League London derbies for the first time since April 2023, after losing 3-0 to Arsenal in May and 3-2 at home to Chelsea on MD1 this season.

  • Crystal Palace have won six of their last 10 away London derby matches in the Premier League (D3 L1), as many as they had in their previous 44 (D15 L23).

  • Fulham have lost 3-2 to Chelsea and 1-0 to Sunderland this season. Only in 1951-52 and 2020-21 have they lost each of their first three games in a top-flight campaign, being relegated on both occasions.

  • After defeat to Manchester City last time out, Crystal Palace are looking to avoid losing their opening three Premier League matches of a campaign for the first time since 2017-18.

  • Crystal Palace have conceded at least two goals in each of their last eight Premier League matches – they’ve never done so in nine league matches in a row in their league history.

  • Alex Iwobi could make his 100th Premier League appearance for Fulham in this match.   • Crystal Palace boss Pierre Sage has lost both of his opening two Premier League games in charge of the club – never before in his managerial career has he lost three league games in a row.
