Brighton resident charged in fraud investigation

TheTools · 28.11.2026 09:58 · source · permalink

Ontario Provincial Police have laid charges against a Brighton resident following a fraud investigation.

On August 11, 2026, police received a complaint regarding fraudulent transactions involving an organization in Brighton.

After an investigation that spanned more than three months, officers took one person into custody on November 26, 2026.

Fifty-two-year-old Douglas Bennett of Brighton has been charged with:

  • Fraud over $5,000
  • Possession of proceeds of property obtained by crime over $5,000
  • Criminal breach of trust

OPP say no further information can be provided at this time.

The accused is due to appear before the Ontario Court of Justice in Cobourg in February 2027.
