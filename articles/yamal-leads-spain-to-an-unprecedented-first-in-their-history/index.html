🎥 Yamal leads Spain to an unprecedented first in their history

Lamine Yamal helped world champions Spain reach a milestone never before achieved in their 106-year history.

After scoring against England before the second minute had even elapsed at Wembley, he did it again this Tuesday (29).

OneFootball Videos

It took just one minute and ten seconds for the Barcelona forward to be deadly against Croatia at the Ramón Sánchez Pizjuán.

The match is underway for the second round of League A in the UEFA Nations League.

Croatia had no time to 'process' it

Croatia — beginning a new cycle under Slaven Bilić — did not even have the chance to try anything.

Spain’s move down the left came at high speed, with Fermín letting the ball run and Yamal firing home first-time, leaving Livaković with no chance. 

The goalkeeper has now become his Barcelona teammate.

Spain were completely dominating the clash in Andalusia.

Then Croatia equalized with their first chance.

A perfect cross from captain Perišić — Modrić started on the bench — and a goal from Beljo (28').
