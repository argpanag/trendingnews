The Serbia coach analyzed the defeat to Lithuania in the World Cup qualifiers but did not want to talk too much about the refereeing

By Stefan Acevski/ info@eurohoops.net

Lithuania achieved a triumph in the ninth round of Group J in the FIBA World Cup qualifiers, defeating Serbia 97-78 on their home court.

After the match, Serbia’s coach Dusan Alimpijevic commented on what was seen on the court in Klaipeda.

When it came to discussing the refereeing, Alimpijevic stopped himself.

“There’s not much to say. I’m proud of our guys and what they showed. They gave everything to stay in the game. What I would say further wouldn’t be good for us and our federation, so I’ll stop here.“

Once again, he emphasized that he does not want to talk about the referees and noted that Serbia now faces a return trip to Belgrade and preparation for the match against Italy, which is scheduled for Sunday (19:00 CEST).

“Unfortunately, it is not allowed to comment on this. Any comment would be unnecessary, as this is already the past. We are flying back to Serbia by charter tonight and starting to prepare for Italy. As I said, I have nothing to reproach my players for, I am proud of the way they handled this, and it was not easy.”
