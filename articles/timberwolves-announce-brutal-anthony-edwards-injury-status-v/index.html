Anthony Edwards (injury management) is questionable for Timberwolves vs. Warriors.

Anthony Edwards' availability for the Minnesota Timberwolves on Wednesday against the Golden State Warriors is yet to be seen.

The superstar guard has been listed as questionable to play with right knee injury management.

Anthony Edwards' knee condition will continue to be monitored. He was also initially marked as questionable on Monday against the Cleveland Cavaliers with the same injury label, but he went on to play and appear on the court.

He even exploded for 47 points in the 127-125 overtime loss in Cleveland.

Although head coach Chris Finch already clarified that Edwards' injury isn't a long-term concern, the T-Wolves can only be cautious in managing the health of the NBA star.

Edwards is averaging 33.3 points with 4.8 rebounds and 3.5 assists, leading Minnesota to a strong 3-1 start.
