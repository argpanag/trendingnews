Toby Greene Chooses Cats

TheTools · 21.09.2026 10:30 · source · permalink

Toby Greene has nominated Geelong Cats as his preferred home in 2027

Toby Greene has informed the Cats of his commitment to join the Club from next season.

As an unrestricted free agent Greene can join the Cats when the free agency period opens on Friday 2 October. 

Geelong Cats EGM Football Andrew Mackie said: 

"We are really excited Toby wants to join the Geelong Football Club. 

"We know this was a significant decision for Toby and his family, and we recognise what the Giants have meant to him and respect the contribution he has made to their Club. 

"We are looking forward to helping Toby write the next chapter of his career at the Cats."
