The story so far

Walkinshaw puts blowtorch on Triple Eight

Walkinshaw TWG Racing boss Ryan Walkinshaw turned up the heat on arch-rival squad Triple Eight, saying: "You have got the factory Ford team being beaten by two of their customers, so the pressure is on them."

Teams cautioned over illegal tactics

Triple Eight Team Manager Mark Dutton is hopeful teams won’t play “silly buggers” in Adelaide, after competitors were warned of the consequences for deliberate manipulation tactics.

Grand Finalists set

Matt Payne, Brodie Kostecki, Broc Feeney and Chaz Mostert will fight for the championship. Payne won the Sandown finale, and takes the top seed to Adelaide.
