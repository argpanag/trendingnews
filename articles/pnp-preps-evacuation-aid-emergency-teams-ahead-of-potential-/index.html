PNP preps evacuation aid, emergency teams ahead of potential super typhoon

TheTools · 19.10.2026 06:05 · source · permalink

NATIONAL

Oct 19, 2026

2:05 PM GMT+8

(Photo courtesy of PNP)

MANILA – The Philippine National Police (PNP) has alerted its field units and ordered the activation of disaster response measures in areas that may be affected by the approaching weather disturbance expected to enter the Philippine Area of Responsibility (PAR) on Tuesday.

 

PNP chief Gen. Jose Melencio Nartatez Jr. said police commanders have been directed to ensure the operational readiness of their personnel and resources before the storm's potential impact.

 

“Preparedness is our priority. Police units in vulnerable areas have been instructed to activate their disaster response and public safety contingencies, secure critical equipment, and ensure personnel are ready for immediate deployment,” Nartatez said in a news release Monday.

 

“Our units are closely coordinating with local authorities to provide timely assistance and maintain public safety before, during, and after the storm,” he added.

 

The PNP has started preparatory measures in vulnerable regions, particularly in Northern Luzon and the Bicol Region, where tropical cyclone wind signals may be raised as early as Monday evening or Tuesday morning.

 

Super Typhoon Champi will be locally named Rosal once it enters PAR. It may bring heavy rains over extreme Northern Luzon. (Christopher Lloyd T. Caliwan/PNA)
