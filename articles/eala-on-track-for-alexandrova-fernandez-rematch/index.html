Eala on track for Alexandrova, Fernandez rematch

TheTools · 11.10.2026 16:08 · source · permalink

Read this in The Manila Times digital edition.

FILIPINO sensation Alex Eala is on track for a rematch against Russian Ekaterina Alexandrova or a rubber match against Canadian Leylah Fernandez at the WTA 1000 Wuhan Open in China.

World No. 18 Eala needs to get past a qualifier in her opening match likely on Monday before facing either No. 28 Alexandrova or No. 26 Fernandez who is of Filipino descent.

Alexandrova and Fernandez were pitted against each other as the draw revealed on Saturday.

Tournament No. 13 seed Eala battled Alexandrova at the Abu Dhabi Open quarterfinals where she fell to the latter, 3-6, 3-6, last February.

Meanwhile, Eala first took on Fernandez at the Stuttgart Open round of 32, where she fell to the fellow lefty, 1-6, 4-6, last April.

Eala exacted revenge on Fernandez at the Mubadala DC Open, dethroning the Fil-Canadian in the round of 16, 6–2, 7–6(1), and eventually ruled the tournament for her first WTA title last August.

In the doubles of this Wuhan tournament, Eala will reunite with close pal Zeynep Sönmez.

The Filipino-Turkish tandem will face the Ukrainian duo of Anhelina Kalinina and Dayana Yastremska in the first round.

Eala and Sönmez last teamed up at the Madrid Open last May in Spain where they made a second round finish.

Meanwhile, Eala is set to slip a spot to No. 19 in the WTA rankings on Monday.

The live rankings on Sunday revealed Eala's slight descent from her career-high rank No. 18.

Elise Mertens leaping from No. 19 to No. 14 bumped Eala down the rankings.

Eala currently has 2,276 total ranking points.
