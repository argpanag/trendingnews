FURIA Tatu And Jojo Give Each Other Player Ratings - Duo Q&A - Hotspawn

FURIA will have a tough task ahead of them in the Worlds 2026 play-in stage. Having just come from the CBLOL finals last week, the team had very little time to prepare for the first phase of the tournament, where they will fight for one last spot in the Swiss stage alongside Karmine Corp, MVK Esports, and Cloud9.

Read what they had to say about coming into the tournament and what ratings they would give each other for their player card.

Davide: How was it after the CBLOL finals? Obviously, the loss wasn’t the greatest, but I heard it was a really nice event—the venue and the whole atmosphere.

Jojo: Losing isn’t nice. But the finals, the way we played, and the people there were really cool. We hadn’t played in front of that many people in Brazil before. I’ve been to a lot of finals, but this was my first time at a big venue on a stage like that. It was really awesome for me.

Tatu: For me too. It was insane. We’ve already played international tournaments, but this was our first time playing in Brazil. It was an insane experience.

Jojo: For sure. I think the Brazilian community is huge, so there would be people to watch.

Tatu: Yeah, I definitely think people would watch. People were saying that we aren’t the biggest team in terms of fans, so maybe the venue wouldn’t be full. But it was crowded, and every seat was full. People would enjoy watching events in Brazil.

Jojo: Rio again.

Tatu: I’d say Salvador. That’s my city, but I kind of want to make history in Rio too.

Davide: A question for Tatu. My colleague Dory asked you about Faker last time.

Tatu: In that scenario, winning Worlds for sure. But both would be insane.

Davide: I thought you would say beating T1 and Faker in a Worlds grand final.

Tatu: Oh, yeah. Sure.

Davide: Moving on to a more serious question. You’ve just come off the CBLOL finals, which were played on Patch 26.17, three patches ago.

Tatu: Maybe not at first. But later, if we make it to the Swiss Stage, I think it could start to become a problem.

JoJo: In the Play-In, people want to do things differently and try new stuff. But on the main stage, things will be different. Korean teams will try the new things they’ve been learning, and we’re pretty behind on that. But we can learn fast, so we’re okay with it.

Jojo: I can’t share.

Tatu: Since we got here, we haven’t scrimmed—no scrims, nothing. So I can’t remember anything. Maybe there was something, but we can’t tell people.

Davide: This question is more a philosophical one.

JoJo: Winning at life? I’m not sure. I’m trying to figure that out, so I don’t have an answer. No idea. I have no idea right now.

Tatu: I want to be good at what I’m doing right now, as a pro player. But later, I want to have a comfortable life and look back knowing that I did a good job. I’d like people to remember Tatu as a happy guy who enjoyed every moment.

After Tatu goes away, I’d like to start learning how to play drums or how to cook properly—stuff like that.Hobbies I can do in my life. I’ve been a pro player since I was 15, so there hasn’t been much time for that stuff.

Jojo
