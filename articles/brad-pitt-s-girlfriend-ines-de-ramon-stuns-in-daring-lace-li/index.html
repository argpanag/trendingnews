Brad Pitt's girlfriend Ines de Ramon stuns in daring lace lingerie sheer dress on solo night out

TheTools · 19.10.2026 23:43 · source · permalink

The jewelry executive brought romantic glamour to the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show in Los Angeles

Ines de Ramon, 33, embraced the lingerie-inspired dress code at the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show, arriving in a flowing gown with delicate lace and a dramatic thigh-high slit.

Brad Pitt's girlfriend stepped out without the actor for the star-studded event at Sony Pictures Studios in Culver City, California, on Sunday, October 18. 

Posing against the pink backdrop, Ines wore her dark hair swept into glossy waves over one shoulder, with rosy cheeks, defined lashes and a glossy pink lip completing her evening look.

Her pale gown combined a softly draped neckline with a lace panel and contrasting dark bow, creating the illusion of a bra peeking out beneath the fabric.

Ines de Ramon's lace-trimmed dress

The neckline was a clever detail built into the dress itself. Vogue España described the design as a trompe-l'oeil effect, with two layers combining lingerie-style lace and the draped fabric above it. 

The romantic details continued through the skirt, where diagonal lace inserts and cascading ruffles framed a high slit. A floral embellishment at the hip added a touch of color, while the sheer lower skirt flowed into a long train. Ines finished the outfit with nude peep-toe platform heels, keeping the focus on the intricate gown.

Ines de Ramon joins Victoria's Secret's star-studded evening

The evening brought together some of fashion's most recognizable names, with Gigi Hadid, Adriana Lima, Candice Swanepoel and Anok Yai among the runway models. 

Tate McRae, Megan Thee Stallion, Megan Moroney and Katseye were announced as musical performers, while Kylie Jenner made a surprise runway debut wearing the returning Fantasy Bra. 

Ines watched the spectacle as a guest, bringing her own interpretation of the evening's lingerie theme to the pink carpet.

Brad Pitt and Ines de Ramon's relationship timeline

Brad and Ines were first photographed together at a Bono concert in Los Angeles in November 2022, making their relationship public nearly four years ago.

They reached another milestone in September 2024, making their red carpet debut as a couple at the Venice Film Festival premiere of Wolfs. Brad's co-star George Clooney and his wife Amal also attended the premiere.

Ines, who works in the jewelry industry, has since accompanied Brad to several major events, including the New York premiere of F1 in June 2025. 

Their recent calendar has included both glamorous evenings and more relaxed dates. On October 9, they attended George and Amal's Albie Awards at London's Natural History Museum, before being photographed holding hands during a trip to see James Blake perform in Paris.

Brad Pitt's comments about life with Ines de Ramon

Although Brad rarely discusses their relationship in detail, he offered a glimpse into his private life during a May 2025 interview with GQ. "My life is fairly contained," he said, describing a sense of warmth and security within his close circle of friends and loved ones.

The actor was also asked whether appearing publicly with Ines at the 2024 British Grand Prix had been a calculated decision tied to his racing movie. "No, dude, it's not that calculated," he replied, laughing at the suggestion. He questioned how exhausting it would be to plan his personal life around those considerations. "No, life just evolves. Relationships evolve."
