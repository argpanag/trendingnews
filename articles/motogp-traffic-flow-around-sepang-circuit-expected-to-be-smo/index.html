MotoGP: Traffic Flow Around Sepang Circuit Expected To Be Smoother Compared To F1 Race - Police

TheTools · 30.10.2026 01:51 · source · permalink

SEPANG, Oct 28 (Bernama) -- Traffic flow around the Sepang International Circuit (SIC) is expected to be smoother during the 2026 Malaysian Motorcycle Grand Prix (MotoGP) from Oct 30 to Nov 1 compared to the Formula 1 (F1) race earlier this month.

Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) district police chief ACP M. Ravi said this is because there will be no road closures and vehicle inspection checkpoints along routes leading into the circuit.

"Traffic will be smoother this time because during the F1 race, there were checkpoints where auxiliary police were stationed at every junction to conduct inspections.

"This time, we are only deploying traffic police personnel to facilitate vehicle movement and prevent congestion," he told a press conference on traffic management and security for the event here today.

Ravi said a contra-flow lane will be activated from 6 am on Saturday and Sunday for police-escorted VVIP convoys and emergency vehicles, such as ambulances and fire engines, until the races conclude.

He said 649 police officers and personnel will be deployed across the three-day race to ensure visitor safety and smooth traffic flow.

He also advised visitors to plan their journeys early, obey instructions from traffic personnel, and park only in designated areas to avoid obstructing traffic.

Severe traffic congestion was previously reported around SIC and on exit routes leading towards Kuala Lumpur and KLIA following the conclusion of the 2026 F1 Gulf Air Bahrain Grand Prix (GP) in Malaysia.

The F1 event recorded the highest attendance in the circuit's history, drawing 261,000 spectators from Oct 2 to 4, including 103,000 on race day.

The figure surpassed SIC's previous record for an F1 race of 126,690 spectators set at the 2008 Malaysian GP.

Meanwhile, SIC chief executive officer Azhan Shafriman Hanif said attendance for the 2026 Malaysian MotoGP is projected at about 40,000 on Friday, before rising to between 60,000 and 80,000 each on Saturday and Sunday.

Youth and Sports Minister Dr Mohammed Taufiq Johari had on Oct 11 targeted an overall attendance of 200,000 spectators for the event.
