Jessica Alba and her eldest daughter Honor, 18, looked like twins in similar black minidresses on September 12.

The lookalike pair continued the party at a Raising Cane’s event after wearing coordinating dresses at the U.S. Open a few hours earlier.

Alba also shared a sweet moment with boyfriend Danny Ramirez in his-and-hers date night looks.

Jessica Alba and her eldest daughter Honor, 18, had fans doing a double take as they showed off not one, but two different coordinated style moments in one day. Just hours after twinning in preppy dresses at the U.S. Open on September 12, Alba and the teen showed off their versions of the slinky black minidress while attending an after-party in New York City.

The Honest Company founder traded out her tennis whites for a sultry LBD at a Raising Cane’s party for NYC’s Big Tennis Weekend. Alba looked radiant in an asymmetrical black silk slip dress, which featured a black lace trim on one side of her plunging neckline. Her LBD’s draped silky fabric gave way to a sheer black lace, which bared her legs and her left hip with a daring slanted design.

Alba added some height to her stature with a pair of towering black patent leather platform sandal heels, which were secured around her ankles with straps. The mom-of-three—who shares Honor as well as daughter Haven, 15, and son Hayes, 8, with ex-husband Cash Warren—added a pop of bold color to her look with a zebra-printed side bag, which featured a red animal print strap. Styling her blonde-highlighted brunette hair in middle-parted waves, Alba lit up her smoky eyeshadow and glowing complexion with a dainty diamond tennis necklace and silver hoop earrings.

Meanwhile, Honor was her mom’s mini-me in a black halterneck minidress with an empire-waist silhouette and a semi-sheer asymmetrical skirt. Styling her caramel-highlighted brunette locks in a sleek style, the teen added a pop of complementary color with bright red lipstick and a warm flush on her cheekbones. She finished off her LBD with a black shoulder bag and a pair of slouchy black leather flat boots.

Once inside the Bar CC venue, Alba added a sleek black blazer over her shoulders as she posed with boyfriend Danny Ramirez. The Captain America: Brave New World actor coordinated with his girlfriend in a black T-shirt and black sweatpants paired with an open black collared short-sleeved shirt. A green hat, white tennis shoes, and a gold pendant chain necklace broke up Ramirez’s otherwise monochromatic look.

Just hours earlier, Honor and Alba put their preppy mom-daughter style on display while attending the U.S. Open matches. The Fantastic Four actress looked summery in a plunging white bustier sundress, which she styled with a blue sweater tied around her waist. Donning a pair of sunglasses and white kitten heels slides, the 45-year-old was all smiles as watched the games with Ramirez and her eldest daughter at USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center.

Meanwhile, Honor switched up the same blue-and-white color combination, opting for a light blue gingham sundress and a white sweater styled over her shoulders. A pair of black sunglasses and espresso brown flats finished off her look.
