FPL gameweek 11 tips: Is it too late to sign Nicolas Jackson?

TheTools · 21.11.2026 09:23 · source · permalink
  • Published
    20 November 2026

The Premier League is back after the international break and BBC Sport's Fantasy Premier League expert Gianni Buttice gives his answers to some of the biggest dilemmas facing managers heading into gameweek 11.

Is it too late to buy Jackson?

With 33 points in the past three rounds, you might think you've missed your chance to own Aston Villa forward Nicholas Jackson (£7.2m). But he remains in superb form, with five excellent fixtures ahead, making him the round's most transferred player once again.

Are players from Liverpool being overlooked?

Liverpool have traditionally offered plenty of popular FPL options, but their players have attracted less backing this season. Only Arsenal, Brighton and Leeds have conceded fewer goals, making Jeremy Jacquet (£5.1m) an appealing differential with ownership below 5%.

In attack, Alexander Isak (£9.3m) is FPL's second among forwards for points scored. Florian Wirtz (£7.2m), meanwhile, is beginning to find a new level of consistency for Liverpool, with five attacking returns in his last six matches.

Why are defenders hauling so often this season?

Defensive contribution points have been a welcome addition to FPL and have contributed to the rise of playing a back four. Antonio Silva (£5.1m), Tarik Muharemovic (£5.1m) and Jan Paul van Hecke (£5.1m) are among the best at earning the extra two points, while also offering significant goal threat and playing for strong defensive sides. Bournemouth, Leeds and Tottenham rank among the top five for expected goals conceded, giving these mid-priced centre-backs an unusually wide range of routes to points.

Maxim de Cuyper (5.5m) is a unique case of an out-of-position defender playing in a front three and is therefore a must have.

Which players are close to missing a game through suspension?

Several popular players enter Gameweek 11 with three or four yellow cards. A fifth booking before their 19th league match will rule them out of their team's next league fixture.

Pascal Gross (£6.3m), Marcus Tavernier (£6.7m), Antonio Silva (£5.1m) are all on four yellows while De Cuyper (5.5m), Morgan Rogers (£8.2m) and Dominic Calvert-Lewin (£6.0m) are on three.

How best to prepare for the festive fixture congestion?

Many engaged FPL managers see this as the season's most important stretch: 10 gameweeks in 45 days, including several midweek rounds. Injuries, suspensions and rotation make a reliable first substitute essential. Where possible, choose players who regularly complete 90 minutes and rarely miss matches, and consider banking transfers so you can react when availability flags appear in your squad.
