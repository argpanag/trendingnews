'League of Legends: New Dawn' teaser promises new era for the game

TheTools · 17.11.2026 03:36 · source · permalink

“League of Legends: New Dawn” is here with a new teaser trailer, which showcases a brand new era.

League Studio Head Andrei "Meddler" Van Roon said at the start of the video that they want League to be the kind of game that is alive for many years.

"We want League to stand the test of time and be a game that's here for many years to come," he said. "And there are a lot of things that need to be true to make that happen."

The game is releasing a modernized client, which players use to select champions. More revamps include visually transforming Summoner’s Rift, evolving gameplay, and adding new ways to play and experience champions.

The trailer also presents an updated art style, including new character models for the champions and bosses including Baron Nashor and Elder Dragon.

“League of Legends: New Dawn” is said to be released in 2027.

“League of Legends” features two teams of five powerful champions who face off to destroy the other’s base.
