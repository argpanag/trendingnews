National Television Awards 2026 winners: Full list of winners - updated LIVE

TheTools · 08.09.2026 21:46 · source · permalink

The biggest night in TV is here once again.

  • James Hibbs

It's that time of year, with the National Television Awards once again being handed out at a starry ceremony in London.

Hosted once more by Joel Dommett, the ceremony is taking place at the O2, with winners chosen by public vote as usual.

Perhaps the biggest story from this year going into the ceremony was that the drama performance category features an all-female line-up for the first time, featuring Sheridan Smith for I Fought the Law, Vicky McClure for Trigger Point, Ella Bruccoleri for The Other Bennet Sister, Judy Parfitt for Call the Midwife and Ruth Jones for Run Away.

We will keep this page updated throughout the night as the winners come in.

Read on for the full list of all the nominees and winners from this year's NTAs.
