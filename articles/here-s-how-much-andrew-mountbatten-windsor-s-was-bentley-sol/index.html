  • A Bentley Flying Spur believed to have belonged to Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor has sold for £124,975 at a dealership in South Yorkshire.
  • The car sold for its asking price to the first person who enquired, just one day after going on sale at Portland Autos in Bawtry.
  • The dealership uncovered the vehicle's royal history through an unusual service history after acquiring it via a third party.
  • Mr Mountbatten-Windsor previously drove a Bentley Flying Spur with a personalised Duke of York number plate to several key royal events.
  • The vehicle sale comes after Mr Mountbatten-Windsor was compensated £302,000 for surrendering the lease on his former Royal Lodge home.
