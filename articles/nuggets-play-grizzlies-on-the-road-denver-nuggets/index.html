Denver and Memphis match up for the first time this season.

The Grizzlies have been one of the biggest early-season surprises.

With an average age of just 24.5 years, Memphis has the second-youngest roster in the league. They've raced off to a 7-3 record thanks in large part to Ty Jerome's All-Star-caliber start. The 29-year-old point guard is putting up a career-best 26.9 points per game on 50.0 percent shooting from the field and 37.9 percent shooting from three-point range.

Denver also boasts a point guard posting All-Star-worthy numbers, Jamal Murray. The 2025-26 All-NBA Teamer is currently averaging a career-high 25.8 points per game on 49.3 percent shooting from the field and a scintillating 50.0 percent from distance. He's also dishing out 5.7 assists and grabbing 5.1 rebounds per contest.

This should be a great battle between two of the best shooters at the lead guard position.
