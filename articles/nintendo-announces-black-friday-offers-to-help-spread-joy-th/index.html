Nintendo announces Black Friday offers to help spread joy this holiday season - News - Nintendo Official Site

TheTools · 14.11.2026 16:31 · source · permalink

Mark your calendar for holiday specials featuring the Nintendo Switch 2 system, select Nintendo Switch 2 and Nintendo Switch games, and more

The holidays are almost here, and Nintendo has Black Friday offers with a range of options for holiday shoppers. With holiday specials featuring the Nintendo Switch 2 system and games for both Nintendo Switch 2 and Nintendo Switch, there are more ways than ever to create lasting memories and connect with family and friends during this special time of year.

Starting Nov. 22 through Dec. 5, rev up for Black Friday with the Nintendo Switch 2 + Mario Kart World Bundle featuring a Nintendo Switch 2 system, a full download of the Mario Kart World game and a 3-Month (90-Day) Nintendo Switch Online Individual Membership for the suggested retail price of $499.99, a savings of $50¹.

Plus, from Nov. 22 through Nov. 29, select games for Nintendo Switch 2 like Donkey Kong Bananza and Nintendo Switch games such as Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2 will be $20 off the suggested retail price. All offers available while supplies last.

In addition to select retailers, these promotions will be available at the Nintendo NEW YORK and Nintendo SAN FRANCISCO stores. The Nintendo Switch 2 + Mario Kart World Bundle promotion will also be available on the Nintendo Store website.

The Nintendo NEW YORK and Nintendo SAN FRANCISCO stores will feature festive window displays and seasonal decor and are a one-stop destination for holiday shopping for Nintendo Switch 2 and Nintendo Switch, plus games, gifts, exclusive items and more.

Share the joy of Nintendo this holiday with exclusive products, collectibles and apparel inspired by your favorite characters and games including Mario, The Legend of Zelda, Animal Crossing, Pikmin and Splatoon. Holiday gift guides, festive shopping bags, and, for a limited time, a gift with purchase (requires minimum spend) will be available while supplies last.

Holiday shoppers looking to get an early jump on enjoying some games can also check out a sale on Nintendo eShop and the Nintendo Store website beginning Nov. 19 through Dec. 2.

With these offers and the latest games from the worlds of Nintendo, now is the best time to experience Nintendo Switch 2! Become the Hero of Time and save Hyrule in the recently released The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time game, go on an expedition with loyal and adorable plantlike Pikmin in today’s release of the Pikmin 4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy game, plus lots more to play.

For more festive fun, explore the latest Nintendo Holiday Gift Guide for ideas or let your kids create their own wish list!

Check out more information on Nintendo’s holiday offerings below:

Nintendo Switch 2 System Bundles:

  • Nintendo Switch 2 + Mario Kart World Bundle, featuring a Nintendo Switch 2 system, a full download of the Mario Kart World game and a 3-Month (90-Day) Nintendo Switch Online Individual Membership for $499.99 (savings of $50 off suggested retail price).

  • Nintendo Switch 2 + Nintendo Switch Sports Resort Bundle, which includes a Nintendo Switch 2 system, a full download of the Nintendo Switch Sports Resort game and a 3-Month (90-Day) Nintendo Switch Online Individual Membership for $529.99 (savings of $27.98 off suggested retail price).

Digital Deals and Black Friday Offers:

  • A sale on select digital games starts Nov. 19 through Dec. 2 at 11:59 p.m. PT. Visit the Nintendo Store website, Nintendo Store app and Nintendo eShop for offers on select games.

  • Black Friday begins Nov. 22, when select retailers including Amazon, Best Buy, GameStop, Target, Walmart, and both Nintendo US stores will have offers on physical Nintendo Switch 2 and Nintendo Switch games available while supplies last (savings of $20 off suggested retail price):

    • Super Mario Party Jamboree – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Jamboree TV

    • Metroid Prime 4: Beyond – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition, Donkey Kong Bananza and Kirby Air Riders

    • Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2

    • Nintendo Switch versions of Super Mario Party Jamboree and Metroid Prime 4: Beyond
