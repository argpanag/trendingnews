Two seriously hurt after crash, part of State Highway 1 west of Balclutha closed

TheTools · 15.10.2026 14:13 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Emergencies / Local

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._f0.YIZs2-J2FpwgcBcJJb29s1vXmFAWHLh_p1oUyv-l-RA/emergencies_local/1927967%2Ftwo-seriously-hurt-after-crash-part-of-state-highway-1-west-of-balclutha-closed

A section of State Highway 1 west of Balclutha has been closed after a crash on Thursday.

Two people were seriously injured and a third had moderate injuries after the crash at the intersection of Tutu Hill Road about 4.20pm this afternoon.

The Clinton Highway was closed at Waiwera South.

Traffic management was in place and motorists should expect delays.
