TAMPA, Fla. (KFOX 14, CBS 4) — The Milwaukee Brewers defeated the Tampa Bay Rays 4-1 in Game 5 to win the 2026 World Series, their first in franchise history.

Milwaukee wins the series 4-1. Brewers' infielder Joey Ortiz becomes the first ever former New Mexico State Aggie to win a World Series.

Ortiz played for NMSU from 2017-19.

Brewers manager Pat Murphy was the first ever El Paso Chihuahuas manager. He managed the team for 2014 and part of 2015.

Milwaukee finished the regular season with a 103-59, the best in Major League Baseball. The Brewers then went on a terrific run through the postseason, only losing two total games, and capped it off with their first title in their 57 year history.
