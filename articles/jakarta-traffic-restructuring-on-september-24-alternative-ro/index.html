Jakarta Traffic Restructuring on September 24: Alternative Routes from Sudirman-Thamrin to the House of Representatives

TheTools · 24.09.2026 01:52 · source · permalink

The Jakarta Metropolitan Police (Polda Metro Jaya) has prepared situational traffic management measures on Sudirman-Thamrin, Monas, and the House of Representatives (DPR). Road users are asked to use alternative routes.

24 Sep 2026 08:52 WIB · Metropolitan

KOMPAS

Share

Copy Link

By Rhama Purna Jati

JAKARTA, KOMPAS — The Metro Jaya Police are preparing situational traffic arrangements and engineering on Thursday (24/9/2026). This step is taken in line with several community activities and expressions of opinion in Jakarta. The arrangements are primarily prepared in the Sudirman-Thamrin area, Monas, and around the DPR/MPR RI building.

The Director of Traffic at the Metro Jaya Regional Police, Commissioner Firman Darmansyah, stated that the diversion of traffic will not be implemented immediately. Its application will be adjusted according to traffic conditions, the number of people, and the developments in the field.

"As many as 1,550 personnel are prepared to provide services and help smooth public mobility," said Firman, Thursday (24/9/2026).

He emphasized that traffic diversion will be implemented if necessary based on the conditions on the ground. Firman also urged the public who do not have interests around the activity location to consider alternative routes to ensure a comfortable and smooth journey.

For those traveling from West Jakarta towards the Monas area, the journey can be made via Tomang Raya Street-Suryopranoto Street-Harmoni-Monas. From South Jakarta, an alternative route can be taken through Tendean Street-HR Rasuna Said Street-HOS Cokroaminoto Street-Tugu Tani-Monas.

Meanwhile, from East Jakarta, the route can be taken via Jatinegara Street-Kramat Raya Street-Tugu Tani-Monas. For road users from Semanggi Intersection heading to Slipi or Tomang, an alternative route can be taken via Sudirman Street-Bendungan Hilir-Pejompongan-Letjen S Parman Street.

Meanwhile, from Jalan Gatot Subroto, the journey can be directed via Jalan Gerbang Pemuda-Patal Senayan-Jalan Tentara Pelajar-Jalan Letjen S Parman.

The Head of the Public Relations Division of the Metro Jaya Police, Commissioner Budi Hermanto, urged the public to plan their journeys in advance, adjust their departure times, and monitor traffic developments before traveling.

"Monitor the latest information through the social media of TMC Polda Metro Jaya, use alternative routes if necessary, and follow the instructions of the officers on the ground," said Budi.

He stated that traffic regulations will be adjusted according to the evolving situation so that community activities can continue to proceed safely and smoothly.

"We apologize to the entire community if their mobility is slightly disrupted. We implement this arrangement so that all community activities can continue to run smoothly and safely," said Budi.
