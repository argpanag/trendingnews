Greens deputy leader’s family trust hides Airbnb income

TheTools · 07.10.2026 18:00 · source · permalink

Rear Window

Luckily for Steph Hodgins-May, her party’s push to have Airbnb stays capped at 90 days a year in Victoria didn’t succeed.

The Greens have been railing against greedy Airbnb hosts locking long-term renters out of the market and fuelling the housing crisis for years. Awkward that the party’s newly minted deputy leader, Steph Hodgins-May, appears to be cashing in.

Hodgins-May’s register of interests discloses the federal senator co-owns a family farm with her sister, Carla Hodgins-May, is a beneficiary of the Rodney May Family Trust and receives “farm profits”.
