FORECAST: Warmer and dry this week

TheTools · 16.11.2026 23:01 · source · permalink

BOWLING GREEN, Ky. (WBKO) - Skies will be clear with lows down to around freezing on the overnight. Patchy fog could also develop on the late night. Freezing fog in some river valleys could lead to a few slick spots on roadways, but the overall risk is pretty low.

WARMING TREND: We will have a warming trend as we head through this week. Highs are near 60 degrees on Tuesday, but gradually make their way to 70 degrees by the end of the week. Low temperatures are also going up as well.

DRY WEATHER AHEAD: The forecast is mainly dry over the next 7 days. A spotty shower is possible on Sunday with a cold font, but the forecast is mainly dry for the next 7 days.

There are indications that we may see some rain closer to Thanksgiving so that is something we will be watching.
