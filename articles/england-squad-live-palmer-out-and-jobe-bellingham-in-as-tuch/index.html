England squad live: Palmer out and Jobe Bellingham in as Tuchel names 25 players

TheTools · 06.11.2026 10:29 · source · permalink

England face Croatia at Wembley before travelling to Madrid to take on group leaders Spain, as Thomas Tuchel’s side aim to book their place in the Nations League quarter-finals

Thomas Tuchel has left Cole Palmer out his England squad while Jobe Bellingham, the brother of Jude Bellingham, has received his first senior call-up.

Tuchel was critical of Palmer for withdrawing from September’s squad due to injury, with the England manager saying the Chelsea star missed “too many camps”. Palmer did not miss any games for Chelsea following the international break, but was sent off against Tottenham, and the 24-year-old has now been left out for the crunch Nations League games against Croatia and Spain as the Three Lions aim to book their place in the quarter-finals.

Elsewhere, Jobe Bellingham, 21, joins brother Jude in Tuchel’s squad after impressing for Borussia Dortmund, while there is also a first-time call-up for Fulham midfielder Josh King, 19. Trent Alexander-Arnold keeps his place despite his lack of minutes for Real Madrid while Arsenal right back Ben White also returns following injury, but there is no place for winger Noni Madueke despite the absence of Bukayo Saka due to injury.

England are third in Group A3 after four games, having won three and lost one, to Spain at Wembley. They host Croatia on Thursday before travelling to Madrid to take on Spain on Sunday, and a point from either of those two games would be enough to qualify for the knockout rounds in March.

Follow all the latest updates from England’s squad announcement and Tuchel’s Wembley press conference below.

Big questions for Tuchel

What are the big questions that will be asked of the England manager when he speaks to the media at 11am?

Why was Cole Palmer left out? Is perhaps the big one.

Who will play as the right-sided attacker? Is probably another.

There is also a matter of who will start at right-back and whether Tuchel will hand debuts to the likes of Josh King and Jobe Bellingham.

Their inclusions will be asked about as well of course.

No Saka or Madueke

Another surprising omission is that of Noni Madueke.

With Bukayo Saka injured there seemed to be a space available for a right winger and Madueke would have fit the bill.

As it stands the squad feels a little unbalanced in that area though Rio Ngumoha can play there.

Another option would be to start either Ebere Eze on the right or perhaps Josh King.

These are questions that Tuchel will no doubt be asked at his press conference shortly.

Tuchel's talking points

Hello from Wembley, where Thomas Tuchel has served up more talking points than we might have expected this morning.

“Great news for Josh King, who has made such a great start to the season at Fulham, and Jobe Bellingham enjoys a promotion from his role as U21 captain.

“But perhaps just as intriguing are the notable omissions. Noni Madueke is out despite being in some form – does that mean he’s carrying an injury?

“Cole Palmer has not been recalled after withdrawing from September’s squad. Is that down to more “load management”, or is Tuchel sending out a message about availability?

“We will get the chance to ask him in his press conference at 11am.”

Ben White returns

Thomas Tuchel hasn’t yet nailed down a right-back for his team and he has given himself another option in this camp.

That is in the form of Ben White as the Arsenal defender has been recalled.

Other players fighting for the spot in this camp are Trent Alexander-Arnold, Djed Spence and Jarrell Quansah.

Five No.10s selected

Thomas Tuchel has selected five players who play at No.10 for their clubs.

Those include Morgan Rogers and Jude Bellingham who were both expected to be included and have excelled on the international stage.

The others are Arsenal’s Eberechi Eze and Nottingham Forest’s Morgan Gibbs-White.

The fifth No.10 is Fulham’s Josh King who travelled with the squad before the World Cup and now receives a full call up.

Who has been excluded?

Some players who were included in the September squad have been omitted from this one.

Those players include Tino Livramento, Trevoh Chalobah, Nico O’Reilly, Cole Palmer, Alex Scott, Marcus Rashford and Bukayo Saka.

A few of those have injury niggles and are unfit but their exclusions mean there are places for others to step up.

Jobe Bellingham and Josh King called up

Away from Cole Palmer’s exclusion the big news is that Fulham midfielder Josh King has been called up to the squad as has Borussia Dortmund midfielder Jobe Bellingham.

Jobe is the younger brother to England star Jude Bellingham.
