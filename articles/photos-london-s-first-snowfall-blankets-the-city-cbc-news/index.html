PHOTOS: London's first snowfall blankets the city | CBC News

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:02 · source · permalink

Some parts of the city had more than 10 cm of snow, giving Londoners their first taste of winter

Winter has arrived in London.

The season's first snowfall has left a blanket of white across the city, transforming neighbourhoods, parks, and familiar landmarks.

Snowfall amounts varied across the city, with accumulations ranging from about 4 cm in the east to more than 10 cm in the west.

The fresh snowfall offered plenty of opportunities for winter scenes, and a reminder for drivers, pedestrians and anyone heading outdoors to take extra care.
