La Liga 2026-27 season table: Latest standings, scoring leaders

TheTools · 04.09.2026 19:01 · source · permalink

Hansi Flick’s Barcelona look to threepeat atop La Liga, and familiar faces will look to retake the Spanish top flight from the Blaugranas.

Diego Simeone continues to run rule over Atletico Madrid and seeks a first title since 2020-21, while Jose Mourinho’s returned to Real Madrid after a long absence and is aiming to restore Real to the top after two seasons away.

MORE — La Liga winners, all-time

No one else has won La Liga since 2004 when Valencia took its second in three seasons, and Deportivo de A Coruna’s 1999-2000 crown means just six sides have won the title in the last 40 years.

Kylian Mbappe, Jude Bellingham, and friends will hope to charge back into first, while Raphinha and Lamine Yamal want to defend the throne.

For the table and scoring leaders plus some notes on this new season, read on.

Real Mallorca, Girona, and Real Oviedo were relegated last season, making way for three new boys.

Back in the big time are aforementioned 1999-2000 champs Deportivo de A Coruna, where they are joined by Malaga and Racing Santander.

Barcelona have fortified their side with Ballon d’Or and World Cup winner Rodri as well as Newcastle’s Anthony Gordon and Dortmund’s Karim Adeyemi. Ferran Torres has left for PSG while Ansu Fati is off to Monaco.

Real Madrid signed Ivorian star Yan Diomande and Chelsea’s Marc Cucurella, also bringing in Denzel Dumfries and Bernardo Silva. David Alaba and Dani Carvajal did not stay with Real. They also bought Carlos Espi from Levante.

Atleti have impressed by adding Morten Hjulmand from Sporting Lisbon, Cristiano Romero of Spurs, Kang-in Lee from PSG, and Alex Grimaldo from Bayer Leverkusen, though Matteo Ruggeri, Thiago Almada, and Nahuel Molina all left and Julian Alvarez may still depart Madrid.

Villarreal added Canadian midfielder Nathan Saliba from Anderlecht, Real Betis signed Troy Parrott,

In terms of outgoings, Liverpool paid a hefty fee for Osasuna’s Victor Munoz, Pep Chavarria departed Rayo Vallecano for Chelsea, Juanlu Sanchez went from Sevilla to Bournemouth, and the Cherries also bought Alvaro Rodriguez from Elche.
