David Seymour launches campaign to buy land for new Epsom school

TheTools · 30.09.2026 00:13 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Politics / Election 2026

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._g.pKhUMkRnoOZwX4iq2fhLAqba5Rz0jb3mINKRqASmxf0/politics_election-2026/1657425%2Fdavid-seymour-launches-campaign-to-buy-land-for-new-epsom-school

ACT Party leader and Epsom MP David Seymour is campaigning on the government buying Auckland University's Epsom campus to create a new school.

The campus has been empty for years, and the university wants to sell it.

Seymour said the principals and boards of nearby Auckland Grammar School and Epsom Girls' Grammar school approached him in July, saying they wanted to co-sponsor a co-educational high school on the land.

"Since their approach, I have sought assurance from the Minister of Education the land will not be sold in the immediate future," he said.

"The cost of the land is substantial, I have also asked the minister to investigate other land holdings the Ministry of Education has that might be sold to cover the cost. For example, the ministry bought a substantial piece beside Alexandra Park in the 1990s that has not been used."

The new, unzoned school would take pressure off the existing schools' rolls without changing their zoning - so people currently in zone for the grammars would still be able to attend them.

Seymour said using the land for education would ensure the property remained open green space.

"Everyone accepts that central Auckland will intensify over time, but more terraced housing and apartments makes it even more important that green spaces and local facilities like schools are provided."

Seymour said he would tell the next government it is a critical project for his community.
