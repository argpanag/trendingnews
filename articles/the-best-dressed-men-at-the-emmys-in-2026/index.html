The Best Dressed Men at the Emmys in 2026

TheTools · 14.09.2026 23:28 · source · permalink

From Adam Brody to Noah Wyle, these were the standout menswear moments at the 2026 Emmys.

Television’s biggest night is finally here, and with it, the start of the 2026-2027 awards season. Hollywood’s leading men arrived to the red carpet at the Peacock Theater in Downtown Los Angeles in sharp tailoring, statement-making timepieces and accessories, and plenty of personal style, for looks that felt distinctly their own.
