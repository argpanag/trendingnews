Teachers at Forbes road tech school threaten to go on strike December 1st.

TheTools · 11.11.2026 02:48 · source · permalink

Teachers at the Forbes Road Career Technology Center are planning to go on strike in less than a month unless they reach a contract with school officials.

In a press release sent out by the Forbes Road Education Association, which represents the approximately 30 teachers in the union, teachers will begin their strike on Dec. 1 unless multiple demands are met.

"The fact is, our wages have not kept up with our counterparts," said Philip Greene, President of the union and a Multimedia Teacher at the school, in the statement released by the FREA. "Instead of falling further behind, we’ve approached negotiations as an opportunity to catch up our teachers and ensure our school and programs continue to be successful.”

According to the letter, negotiations for a new contract started in January, and their current contract expired at the end of June. Since the end of the previous school year, six teachers have left the school, according to the FREA.

The Monroeville school serves multiple districts, including Penn Hills, Allegheny Valley, East Allegheny, Woodland Hills, Franklin Regional, Highlands, Riverview, Plum Borough, and Gateway.

“A strike is the last option. We do not want our students to miss out on their critical training hours," Greene went on to say. "We would hope to instead see some reasonable proposals from the school, and settle this contract before Nov. 30 so there is no disruption to our students."

If the union does go on strike, students at the school would return to their respective home schools, according to our partners at the Trib.
