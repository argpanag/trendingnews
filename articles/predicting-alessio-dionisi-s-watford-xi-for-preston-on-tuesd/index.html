Predicting Alessio Dionisi's Watford XI for Preston on Tuesday

TheTools · 08.09.2026 18:33 · source · permalink

The XI for Watford's home clash with Preston is probably the toughest to call of the season so far, with so many players potentially absent.

A number of defenders are likely to be borderline when it comes to fitness, while Edoardo Bove is likely to miss out having sustained a knee injury that the club are hopeful is not as bad as it first seemed at The Hawthorns.

More: Pollock: 'It's refreshing to know we're not going to throw the towel in'

Here's how they could line up.

Goalkeeper: Federico Ravaglia

One figure of consistency so far this season has been Ravaglia, who has made a big impression in the early weeks of the season.

He is probably the most likely starter of anyone.

Right-back: Kevin Keben

Omar Traore missed out with a minor adductor issue on Saturday and it is not impossible he recovers for this one, but more likely is that Keben will have to switch to full-back.

He has actually done a decent job in this position over the last week and it may suit him to be moved from the more exposed central role.

Centre-back: Mattie Pollock

Pollock talked about fresh competition for his place ahead of this game but is still one of the first names on the team-sheet.

He says he feels a real hunger to win for the second time this season.

Centre-back: Soumaila Coulibaly

Soumaila Coulibaly on debut (Image: Alan Cozzi/Watford FC)

Coulibaly won't be at 100% yet but his performance at West Brom on Saturday showed he is ready.

Even if he can only go for 60 minutes, it could be worth getting him in. There is a decent chance Branimir Mlacic will be ready to replace him from the bench if needed.

Left-back: Jordan Zemura

There was hope on Saturday that Zemura had been substituted in time to keep him fit for this game.

It seems more likely than not that he will be patched up and made available - with Edo Kayembe, remarkably, next in line to start.

Centre midfield: Martin Payero

Payero has shown glimpses of ability but has still not grabbed a game by the scruff of the beck since signing this summer.

In a deeper role with Bove out, this could be his chance.

Centre midfield: Hector Kyprianou

Kyprianou has shown signs of fatigue in the last week but what choice does Dionisi have?

The midfielder is known for his fitness levels and has hopefully recovered in time to put in another shift.

Hector Kyprianou makes a challenge (Image: PA)

Right wing: Amin Nabizada

Othmane Maamma looks like he needs a rest and I expect him to sit out from the start of this one.

That provides Nabizada with another chance on his weaker right side.

Attacking midfield: Iker Bravo

Bravo is the star of the show in attack and should be restored to a central position here.

If Watford win, he will likely have played a big part in it.

Iker Bravo crosses (Image: Alan Cozzi/Watford FC)

Left wing: Stephy Mavididi

Mavididi showed enough on Saturday to earn a first start for the club.

He was inconsistent with his final product but his pace and directness could be key to getting on the front foot.

Striker: Luca Kjerrumgaard

Kjerrumgaard are Mamadou Doumbia are both struggling to have an impact, truthfully, but the former did show some signs of life at The Hawthorns, in the second half particularly.

Watford will want to dominate and get balls in the box here, which should suit him.

Predicted subs

Bachmann, Walker, Mlacic, Eames, Kayembe, Maamma, Vata, Doumbia, Boadu
