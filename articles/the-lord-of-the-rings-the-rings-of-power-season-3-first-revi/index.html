The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power Season 3 First Reviews: A More Focused, Cinematic, Action-Packed Return to Middle-earth

TheTools · 11.11.2026 18:30 · source · permalink

News

Critics say the epic fantasy series finally feels like it's finding its identity, even if its penchant for lore-heavy storytelling weighs it down a bit.

By Christopher Campbell | Published November 10, 2026

TAGGED AS: Action, movies

J. R. R. Tolkien’s iconic fantasy franchise returns this week with the Season 3 premiere of The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power, and the first reviews of the new episodes are now online. Amazon’s depiction of Middle-earth’s Second Age continues with a five-year time jump as Sauron goes to war with the Elves in his pursuit of all the titular rings. So far, there is some debate as to whether the third season is an improvement over the previous two or it’s still dragging, but most reviews continue to highlight the show’s visuals and performances.

Starring: Morfydd Clark, Robert Aramayo, Cynthia Addai-Robinson, Owain Arthur
