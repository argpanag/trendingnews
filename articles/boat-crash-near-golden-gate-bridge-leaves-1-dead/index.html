Boat crash near Golden Gate Bridge leaves 1 dead

TheTools · 17.09.2026 16:00 · source · permalink

A boater on a fishing vessel died after it ran aground near the north side of the Golden Gate Bridge on Thursday, while two other boaters were injured.

The boater who died was Daniel Zhengyu Qin, a 35, of San Jose, the Marin County coroner’s office said. The cause of death remains under investigation.

At about 6 a.m., Coast Guard personnel along with Marin County, Southern Marin and Tiburon firefighters responded to a call about the 22-foot vessel, which was stuck at Lime Point. The boat was disabled and taking in water, according to the Southern Marin Fire Protection District.

A Coast Guard crew rescued the boaters. One boater was in cardiac arrest, the fire district said. All of the occupants were treated and sent to a hospital.

Southern Marin fire Battalion Chief Jason Golden said the person who suffered cardiac arrest was revived on the way to the hospital, but he later died because of significant trauma.

The Coast Guard is investigating the crash.

The bridge itself sustained no damage, said Paolo Cosulich-Schwartz, communications director for the Golden Gate Bridge Highway and Transportation District.
