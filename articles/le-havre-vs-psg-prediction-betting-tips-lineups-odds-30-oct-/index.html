Le Havre vs PSG Preview

It's top against bottom as basement boys Le Havre take on Ligue 1 leaders PSG at Stade Océane, with kick-off set for 14:00 on Friday 30 October.

Le Havre sit bottom of Ligue 1 with just two points from eight matches, having suffered four consecutive defeats. Most recently, Didier Digard's side lost 4-3 away to Marseille at Stade Vélodrome, despite recording seven shots on target and enjoying 46% possession. Josh Maja scored twice, while Lamine N'Diaye added the third goal.

Paris Saint-Germain have collected 17 points from eight Ligue 1 matches and are on a five-game winning streak. Les Parisiens secured their latest victory with a 2-0 home win over Lyon at Parc des Princes, dominating possession with 68% of the ball and registering ten shots on target. Senny Mayulu and Mika Godts were the goalscorers.

H2H: PSG Are 5 From 6 on the Head-to-Head

PSG have four straight wins against Le Havre. In their last clash, PSG claimed a 1-0 away victory at Stade Océane. The previous 6 H2H meetings have featured five PSG wins and one draw.

Top goalscorer Josh Maja has found the net 3 times, with Rayan Fofana and Mbwana Samatta scoring 2 each. Kaito Mizuta, Hakim Dongopandji and Sota Nakamura have recorded the most assists with 1 apiece. Meanwhile, goalkeeper Gauthier Gallon has kept one clean sheet.

Ferran Torres has top scored with 4, followed by Marquinhos and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia on 3 and 2. Ousmane Dembele and Fabian Ruiz have had 3 assists in the previous 10 games, while Matvey Safonov has 3 clean sheets to his name.
