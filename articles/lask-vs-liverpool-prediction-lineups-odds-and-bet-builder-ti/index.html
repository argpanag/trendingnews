Liverpool will be looking to bounce back from their heavy and controversial defeat to Manchester City when they visit LASK in the Champions League. 

The loss at Anfield on Sunday ended Andoni Iraola's unbeaten start to life with the Reds and they now find themselves nine points off the Premier League summit. 

They did win their opener in Europe at home to Atletico Madrid and will be confident of making it two from two when they take on the Austrian Bundesliga outfit. 

LASK started the season on fire but have fallen off since losing their Champions League opener to AEK Athens and are now without a win in four. 

With the Reds still missing several players, could they pull off a shock?

LASK vs. Liverpool prediction: Who will win?

Iraola is still without Alexander Isak, Cody Gakpo and Dominik Szoboszlai but should have enough for a win here. 

It's time for the likes of Florian Wirtz and Bradley Barcola to step up and secure a second Champions League win of the season. 

It likely will be a tight and tense affair but we think they'll get it done in Austria. 

Score Prediction: LASK 1-2 Liverpool (8/1 with Bet365)

LASK vs. Liverpool best bets

We've picked three betting angles for LASK vs. Liverpool. Check them out below before placing your bets on the match.

Both teams to score (7/10 with Ladbrokes)

An obvious pick to start with given the prediction. 

LASK might not be in the best form right now but still have 19 goals from their eight European fixtures. 

As for Liverpool, even without several missing stars, they can still call on two £100m+ players among others.

Over 2.5 goals (2/5 with Bet365)

With a 2-1 score prediction, this is another straightforward selection. 

Liverpool leaked three at the weekend and have been hot and cold defensively at both ends under Iraola so far. 

We're backing them to win but for the hosts to make them work for it. 

Bradley Barcola to score anytime (8/5 with Coral)

Barcola has not yet got going for the Reds since his huge move at the end of the summer, failing to score or assist in his five games so far. 

However, he is a two-time Champions League winner and he should get plenty of chances against the Austrians. 

Is this the night he announces himself as a Liverpool player?
