Elfyn Evans faces unexpected dilemma after title triumph: "We still need to discuss it" - RallyJournal.com

TheTools · 19.10.2026 07:30 · source · permalink

The Welshman had previously finished runner-up in the championship no fewer than five times. Evans also led the standings for much of this season, but his team-mate Oliver Solberg ultimately emerged as a title rival at the final round.

The Toyota drivers are at very different stages of their careers. Evans will soon turn 38, while his young Swedish challenger is only 25.

Evans admits that two seasons in particular still rankle.

“There were two seasons when it really felt as though the title was within reach. One of them was last year. We lost everything at the season finale in Saudi Arabia, but until then we were very close to the title.”

“The other was the year when the snow caused us problems at Monza (2020). The switch to a Tarmac rally and the snow caught us completely by surprise. Those were probably the two seasons when I came closest to becoming world champion,” Evans recalled in an interview with Rallye-Magazin.

Now that Evans has finally won the title, he says retirement is not yet on the agenda.

“I had already decided a year ago that I would continue full-time for at least the next two seasons, 2026 and 2027. So winning the title did not change my plans in any way.”

The title has also left Evans with an intriguing choice. He has yet to decide whether to use the world champion’s number 1 or continue with his current number 33.

“At the moment, my own preference is to continue with number 33, but Scott (Martin, co-driver) is very keen to use number 1. We still need to discuss it.”

“I feel that number 33 has become part of my identity over the past few years. I don’t think winning the world title changes that.”
