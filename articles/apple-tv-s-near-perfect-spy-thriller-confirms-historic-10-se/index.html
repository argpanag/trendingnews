Apple TV's Near-Perfect Spy Thriller Confirms Historic 10-Season Run

TheTools · 16.11.2026 14:47 · source · permalink

Slow Horses has received some good news from Apple TV. The spy thriller recently completed its sixth season on Oct. 21, and now, the show will continue on for a few more years, making it the longest-running scripted series on Apple TV.

Apple TV announced an early renewal of Slow Horses for Seasons 9 and 10. Previously, Seasons 6 and 7 were announced at the same time. Then, in September 2026, Apple TV renewed the series for Season 8, ahead of the Season 6 premiere. Now, it looks like the show is getting at least four more seasons.

Slow Horses Is Making Apple TV History

Apple TV first launched its streaming service in November 2019 and has since become a hub for some of the most critically acclaimed and award-winning shows. Over the years, the streamer has produced hits like Severance, Ted Lasso, Silo, Pluribus, Widow’s Bay, and more.

Slow Horses premiered in April 2022, just about two and a half years into Apple TV’s run. The series is adapted from the Slough House book series by Mick Herron. It stars Gary Oldman as Jackson Lamb, alongside Jack Lowden, Kristin Scott Thomas, Jonathan Pryce, Hugo Weaving, Saskia Reeves, Rosalind Eleazar, Christopher Chung, Aimee-Ffion Edwards, Joanna Scanlan, Tom Brooke, Lucian Msamati, and Olivia Cooke.

Since its premiere, Slow Horses has been a major hit for Apple TV. Oldman has been nominated for the Golden Globes, Emmys, and BAFTA Awards for his performance as Jackson Lamb. The spy thriller has also won three Emmy Awards and five BAFTA Awards during its run. And finally, Slow Horses has been critically acclaimed, with a 97% critic score and 86% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes. Now, it’s making history as the longest-running scripted series on Apple TV.

What Can We Expect in Slow Horses Season 7?

With Slow Horses Season 6 having just ended, fans are already looking ahead to Season 7. For starters, Slow Horses Season 7 will be getting a major cast shakeup. Five new cast members are joining the series. New cast members include Varada Sethu (Andor), Ethan Moorhouse (Ludwig), Tuppence Middleton (Downton Abbey: A New Era), Merab Ninidze (Homeland), and Joshua McGuire (Anatomy of a Scandal). However, character descriptions have not been released.

Season 7 will be based on Bad Actors, the eighth novel in Herron’s book series. Jack Lamb and the Slow Horses will be on the hunt to find and neutralize the mole within the British Government.

Although a premiere date for Season 7 has not yet been announced, fans can expect to wait no longer than a year for the new season. At the Edinburgh TV Festival, Oldman claimed that one of the reasons for the show’s success is that fans never have to wait more than a year between seasons. He praised the team working hard to “keep the momentum of the show going.”

Oldman added, “You invest in these people, these fictional people, and then the show ends, and you’ve got to wait two and a half years.” He said that it’s a “struggle and a fight” to make sure the show returns every year and to “keep on top of the quality.”

Slow Horses is streaming on Apple TV.
