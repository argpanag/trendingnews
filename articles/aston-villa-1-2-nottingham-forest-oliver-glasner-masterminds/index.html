Aston Villa 1-2 Nottingham Forest: Oliver Glasner masterminds another win over Unai Emery

TheTools · 12.09.2026 16:53 · source · permalink

Report and free match highlights as Aston Villa remain without a league win this season; Oliver Glasner got his first victory as Nottingham Forest boss with Igor Jesus netting a late winner

Saturday 12 September 2026 17:39, UK

Please use Chrome browser for a more accessible video player

Nottingham Forest beat Aston Villa 2-1 at Villa Park to leave Unai Emery's side still searching for their first Premier League victory of the season.

Once again, Oliver Glasner got the better of Emery, who remains winless in eight matches against the Austrian.

Liam Delap went some way to paying back his £50m fee as he fired Forest in front after just 45 seconds of the second half.

Villa needed inspiration and Emery turned to his new signings Johan Manzambi and Ibrahim Mbaye. The changes injected much-needed energy into the hosts and they were rewarded when Alysson struck his first goal for the club to level.

At that point, momentum appeared to be swinging Villa's way.

However, with 10 minutes remaining, Ian Maatsen was forced off after hobbling badly. Villa had already used all their substitutes, leaving them with 10 men for the closing stages.

Forest sensed an opportunity and they took it.

With just two minutes remaining, Igor Jesus found the decisive finish to send the travelling Forest supporters wild.

Emery is having to rebuild following a turbulent summer, where a host of key players were sold, but progress is slow. The result leaves Villa with just one point from their first four games and in the bottom three.
