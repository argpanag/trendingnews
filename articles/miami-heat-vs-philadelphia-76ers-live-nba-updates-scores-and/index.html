Miami Heat vs. Philadelphia 76ers Live NBA Updates, Scores and Highlights

The East's top-seeded Philadelphia 76ers are looking to right the ship after seeing their five-game winning streak snapped by the Portland Trail Blazers on Monday, while the scorching Miami Heat ride their own seven-game surge into a Wednesday meeting that could decide the winner of Group B in the Emirates NBA Cup.

A victory for Giannis Antetokounmpo and the recently unstoppable Heat offense would clinch Group B and potentially eliminate the Sixers from Cup play (if the Cleveland Cavaliers also win).

It's hard to argue a game featuring LeBron James, Giannis, Jaylen Brown, Tyrese Maxey and two of the East's hottest teams needs any additional stakes, but the Cup implications mean this pre-Thanksgiving tilt has some extra juice.
