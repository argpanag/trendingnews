Bron's Triple-Double Snaps Heat's Winning Streak, Secures Cup Survival

LeBron James broke his own record for the oldest player to log a triple-double, notching 25 points, 12 assists and 10 rebounds for a shorthanded Sixers team in a 111-104 win over the Heat on Wednesday afternoon.

Tyrese Maxey chipped in 30 points; VJ Edgecombe and Dominick Barlow provided a mix of highlights and hustle; and the Sixers ended Miami's seven-game winning streak after Jaylen Brown went down with a hamstring pull in the second quarter. Neither he nor Joel Embiid (illness) suited up after halftime, leaving a heavy lift for the Sixers' remaining stars.

Miami scored 60 points in the paint and junked things up with a zone look, but the Sixers hit 14 threes and won the turnover battle to hand the Heat their first loss since Nov. 5.

Final Score: Sixers 111, Heat 104
