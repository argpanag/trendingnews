An Alligator Swallowed an XBOX Series X – You Can Win It

TheTools · 03.11.2026 17:00 · source · permalink

Introducing the XBOX Series X-BITE: a playable XBOX Series X swallowed whole by an alligator. There’s only one, and it can be yours.

This November, XBOX is heading to Florida – home to incredible wildlife, unforgettable places, and every now and then, stories you have to see to believe. That felt like the right inspiration for a custom XBOX Series X unlike anything we’ve made before: the XBOX Series X-BITE.

The idea is simple: an alligator swallowed an XBOX Series X whole. And yes, it still works.

Measuring more than five feet from snout to tail, the console sits inside a meticulously crafted alligator, visible through an illuminated XBOX Nexus in its body. A custom XBOX Wireless Controller with bite marks rests between its jaws, complete with XBOX-green irises and Nexus-inspired pupils, making the XBOX Series X-BITE ready to play some of the biggest games of the year. The result is part-XBOX Series X, part-creature fabrication.

Bringing XBOX Series X-BITE to Life

Turning the idea into something real required serious craft.

Creature animatronics specialists John Nolan Studio brought the XBOX Series X-BITE to life through 3D modeling, sculpting and fabrication. The studio has created creatures and animatronics for ‘The Witcher,’ ‘Jurassic World Dominion,’ and HBO’s upcoming ‘Harry Potter’ series.

Every detail was considered, from the texture and finish of the alligator to the custom XBOX Wireless Controller and illuminated console within. At the center of it all is a functioning XBOX Series X.

Only one exists. It could be yours.

Only one XBOX Series X-BITE exists. Starting today, one person will have the chance to own it.

Want a bite at the prize? The score is simple, there are 3 ways to enter: X, Instagram or TikTok.

  1. Follow @XBOX on your platform of choice.
  2. Repost the designated XBOX sweepstakes post.
  3. Manually add #XbiteSweepstakes to your repost.

Keep your account public so we can see your entry. That’s it. Choose your platform, make your move and you’re in for a chance to win.

Keep your notifications on and your eyes open after entering. If you’re the potential winner, you’ll have 48 hours to make your move and claim the prize. Miss the window, and this gator makes a clean getaway.

The XBOX Series X-BITE Sweepstakes runs from 9:00 a.m. PT on November 3, 2026, through 11:59 p.m. PT on November 10, 2026.

NO PURCHASE NECESSARY. Open only to legal residents of the 50 U.S. and D.C., 18+. Sweepstakes ends 11/10/26. For Official Rules, https://rules.XBOXpromotions.com/xbite.
