Bournemouth win again: Ten-man Cherries beat Celta Vigo 2-1 for 10th win in row

Rayan has scored three Europa League goals this season

Ten-man Bournemouth came from behind to beat Celta Vigo in the Europa League and extend their club-record winning run to 10 games.

The Cherries, in their debut European campaign, top the table with five wins from five.

Iago Aspas curled in a wonderful opener for Celta via the inside of the post moments after having a bicycle kick saved.

When Bournemouth's Bafode Diakite was sent off after video assistant referee intervention midway through the first half, for catching Couhaib Driouech high on the ankle, it looked as if the visitors were facing a first European defeat.

But Alvaro Rodriguez headed in Amine Adli's cross to level for Marco Rose's side soon after the break.

Rayan then swept home the winner at the end of a move that involved fellow substitutes Adrien Truffert and Marcus Tavernier.

Bournemouth are only the third side to win each of their first five European matches, after Nuremberg in 1961-62 and Ararat in 1972-73.

Celta, who have lost four of their first Europa League games this season, had goals disallowed for offside on three separate occasions.

Cherries show character to keep record run going

It feels a long time since head coach Rose's slow start at Bournemouth, after replacing Andoni Iraola, placed him under early pressure - externally at least.

They lost 5-1 to Chelsea on 10 October and sat one place outside the relegation zone with no wins from their opening six Premier League games.

Since then they have won every single fixture, 10 of them in all competitions, and have scored at least twice in each one.

That is 10 wins while juggling Europe and domestic football for the first time - with Rose making eight changes from their last league game here.

They are now fifth in the Premier League and top of this Europa League table.

And what character they had to show, after falling behind on the road and then playing three-quarters of the game with 10 men.

Funnily, they fared better with the one-man deficit. In the first half they managed three shots - and in the second half they had seven and scored with two of them.

Momentum turned when Rodriguez, whose five starts have all come in cup competitions, nodded in an inswinging cross delivered with pace from Adli.

And Rose's subs ultimately won them the game. Truffert won the ball on the left wing and found Tavernier, who squared it for Rayan to net the winner.

Bournemouth's German boss now has an increasingly familiar quick turnaround to contend with, preparing his players for a Sunday trip to Fulham as they bid to keep that winning run going.
