Emery on Manzambi's fitness, Madjo's Champions League miss and a 'time for hard work'

TheTools · 05.09.2026 16:47 · source · permalink

Aston Villa boss Unai Emery has been speaking to the media before Saturday's Premier League game against Hull City at MKM Stadium (17:30 BST).

Here are the key lines from his news conference:

  • Emery provided a fitness update on summer signing Johan Manzambi, who is is yet to make his Villa debut: "No, not yet. He's training with the group and getting better and feeling good but he will need some days more. I don't know if for the Champions League on Tuesday or for the next match against Nottingham Forest."

  • On the rest of his squad as they aim for their first points of the season against the Tigers who have won two from two: "No really bad news. I think the players they are ready and some players we have signed in the last week will get progressively better and better and understanding our way to play. Hopefully tomorrow we can do one step forward, playing better, competing better, and hopefully getting some points."

  • He also confirmed 17-year-old striker Brian Madjo is "still injured for the next two weeks minimum".

  • Speaking about leaving Madjo, Taylor Harwood-Bellis and Modou Keba Cisse out of Villa's Champions League squad, Emery said: "It's hard for the decision we took and for the players too. We decided trying to analyse everything deeply. Taylor signed here for a long time. He stays here for a long time and hopefully they will have again opportunity to play in Europe with us. Hopefully after January, maybe, or the next years. Madjo is 17 years old, still being so, so young, and of course he will have more opportunities to play with us this year or the next year in Europe."

  • Emery said Villa had a plan over recent years to be prepared for "changes" in the transfer market like they have just experienced. He said it will now be key for new faces to adapt and develop "as soon as possible".

  • On the incoming players, he said: "A lot of young players with huge potential. Some can show the potential quick and some take time. But, we are very motivated, very excited, how we can get and keep the same level we had in Premier League."

  • He was also asked about supporters who are concerned by the scale of the change: "We want to win and we want to build a team again as strong as possible and get the structure collectively and individually strong, to be a winner, to be very competitive."

  • On the fans: "The supporters must be passionate and supporting us in everything we are doing. The club is moving forward with the work as well as with Villa Park to try and get most supporters to be behind us and trying to get our level higher and higher. Now is not time to speak too much, now is the time for hard work and focusing on each match with the importance, especially tomorrow against Hull City."
