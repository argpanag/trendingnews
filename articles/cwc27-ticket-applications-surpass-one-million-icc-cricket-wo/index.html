CWC27 ticket applications surpass one million | ICC Cricket World Cup, 2027 | ICC

TheTools · 06.10.2026 11:39 · source · permalink

Global demand surges with more than one million ticket applications submitted in the ballot registrations for the ICC Men's Cricket World Cup 2027.

Register for the ICC Men's Cricket World Cup ticket ballot

Ticket demand for the ICC Men’s Cricket World Cup 2027 has surged past the one-million mark, with more than one million applications submitted in less than three days since the ticket ballot opened on 1 October 2026.

Over 320,000 fans have already registered for the ballot, submitting applications for matches across South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia as anticipation continues to build for the 14th edition of the ICC Men’s Cricket World Cup.

The India vs Pakistan fixture on 10 October is currently attracting the highest demand, with more than 100,000 ticket applications received. South Africa vs India follows with more than 86,000 applications, while India vs Australia has attracted more than 80,000.

The pre-seeded Super 7 fixture between India (A1) and Pakistan (A3) on 7 November has received almost 75,000 applications, making it the fourth most in-demand match. The 21 November Final has also proved popular, with almost 65,000 applications already submitted.

The tournament will feature 14 of the world’s best teams, with the first ball set to be bowled on 2 October and the Final scheduled for 21 November.

With excitement building across the three host nations, the ICC has opened the ticket ballot to give fans around the world the opportunity to register for a chance to attend the tournament.

Ticket prices range from USD $9.62 (R158) for entry-level tickets to USD $31.31 (R514) for the highest-tier matches. Special consideration has also been given to family bundles, student and pensioner pricing, early-bird local allocations, and community club and school group pricing.

ICC CEO, Sanjog Gupta said: “One million ticket applications in just over 48 hours of opening the ticket ballot is a demonstration of the attention, stature and affiliation for ICC Cricket World Cup 2027. ICC events are now spectacles which transcend cultural and geographic boundaries and serve as platforms to widen and deepen fandom for the sport.

“This is also Africa's moment to shine as an ICC marquee men's event is returning to the continent after two decades and brings with it the attention of 2.5 billion cricket fans across 120+ countries. We aim to ‘make the circle bigger’, for fans, for the athletes, for the game, and for communities who will gather to celebrate the competition and the culture. The ballot is open, and we can't wait to host the world so get in if you haven't already.”

Fans have until 23h59 (CAT) on 21 November to register for the ballot and select their desired matches at tickets.cricketworldcup.com.
