Below Deck Med’s Gael Cameron Breaks Silence After Ex Nathan Gallagher’s Domestic Violence Arrest

TheTools · 05.09.2026 15:52 · source · permalink

Below Deck Mediterranean star Gael Cameron is breaking her silence on ex-boyfriend and fellow Bravo star Nathan Gallagher’s 2025 domestic violence arrest.

“Hey guys, I just want to say thank you so much for the support you’ve shown for Kayden and I,” Cameron, 30, wrote via her Instagram Stories on Saturday, September 5, referring to the pair’s 14-month-old son. “It’s been a tough season, but I have been using the past few months   to quietly heal and rebuild for little man and myself.”

She continued, “I really appreciate the love you have sent our way, especially while everything that’s come to light, and I’m really working on finding myself again and moving forward.”

Us confirmed last month that Gallagher, 28, was arrested on domestic violence charges in Australia in December 2025 following an alleged incident with an unidentified 29-year-old woman. Gallagher was charged with “assault occasioning actual bodily harm, intentionally chok[ing] a person without consent and common assault,” according to a police memo.

The police report further stated that neither party sustained injuries and Gallagher was eventually granted conditional bail. It is not clear how he pleaded. (Gallagher was convicted of three domestic violence offenses earlier this month after failing to appear in court.)

Us also confirmed that Gallagher is the subject of an Apprehended Violence Application that was filed on behalf of Cameron in New South Wales. (An AVO is a legal order requested from a court to protect a person from someone who makes them fear violence, intimidation, harassment or stalking that is widely used in Australia, per NSW Government’s official website.)

Gallagher addressed the arrest and subsequent accusations in August.

“I’m not claiming I was perfect. I wasn’t. I made mistakes, and I will take responsibility for my own actions,” Gallagher told Rolling Stone in a statement. “I will not accept being portrayed solely through one person’s account of a relationship when there is another side and evidence relating to it. I have contemporaneous video footage from the night in question, as well as messages and other records, which I believe provide important context.”

In his statement, Gallagher confirmed his breakup from Cameron. (The two began dating in 2024 after meeting on a Below Deck Med season 9 charter.)
