    Player Stats

    Kenrich Williams finished Saturday’s loss to the Rockets with 11 points (3-of-7 FGs, 2-of-2 FTs), seven rebounds, two assists and three three-pointers.

    After totaling 10 points in his first two starts of the season, Williams scored 11 on Saturday. He was most valuable against the Rockets in three-pointers and rebounds, but this performance doesn’t make “Kenny Hustle” a must-add heading into Oklahoma City’s four-game Week 3. Also, with the Thunder playing two games against Cleveland to start the week, it’s fair to wonder whether head coach Mark Daigneault would consider a bigger lineup to match up with Evan Mobley and Jarrett Allen. If so, Williams or Jared McCain (10/4/2/1) could be headed to the bench.
    - Raphielle Johnson
