Record parcel peak predicted as busiest period looms

TheTools · 24.11.2026 17:13 · source · permalink

Parcel volumes could be 5% higher this festive season as UK shoppers splash out more on online and in-store goods for Christmas.

Figures from the Office for National Statistics (ONS) showed online spending was almost 10% higher (9.7%) than the same period in October 2025 and had increased by 1.6% in the three months to October 2026, compared to the previous three months.

Total spend, the sum of in-store and online sales, rose by 1.2% over the month.

ParcelHero said the figures were “very encouraging” and pointed to a record peak for parcels. It also said the ONS stats for non-store volume figures showed shoppers were buying more items online and not just paying more for them.

“All in all, we expect around 5% more parcels than last peak, looking also at the latest Black Friday figures, with the growth concentrated in the weeks around Black Friday and Cyber Monday,” said David Jinks, ParcelHero head of consumer research.

Jinks said Cyber Monday was likely to be the single busiest day, although the peak is now a five-week event rather than a weekend.

He also said Monday 7 December could be the busiest posting day for traditional mail.

“The big networks have invested heavily,” Jinks added.

“Royal Mail is recruiting around 22,000 seasonal staff, including 2,100 HGV and medium goods vehicle drivers, and Evri has increased its daily capacity by around half a million parcels.

“The pressure points will be local: driver availability, rural routes and small retailers with tight collection limits.

“So we’re advising retailers to confirm their collection allocations now and spread their carrier risk.”

As Black Friday looms, DPD is gearing up for a predicted record peak period by expanding its electric vehicle (EV) fleet and opening up seven new parcel depots.

The industry’s expectations for trading in October appear weak, with just 6% predicting growth according to official ONS figures.

More than 100 truck drivers were welcomed to a festive evening at Glews Services in Goole on 26 November 2025, where SNAP partnered with the site to provide free hot meals, Christmas stockings and a place to take a break during one of the most demanding periods of the year.

A trial to show how electric timber HGVs can operate in demanding forestry environments has won an environmental award at the Transport News Scottish Rewards.

Nominations are open for the 2027 Everywoman in Transport & Logistics Awards, as the programme celebrates its 20th anniversary.

Efforts this week to strengthen the UK-EU relationship, including an agreement to scrap unnecessary border checks on food, plants and animals, have been hailed as “very positive news” for hauliers.
