Shamrock Rovers part ways with women's first team head coach

TheTools · 29.10.2026 13:34 · source · permalink

SHAMROCK ROVERS HAVE confirmed that the first team Head Coach of their women’s’ team, James O’Callaghan, has departed the club.

In a statement released through their social media channels, Rovers said, ‘Following the conclusion of the 2026 season, we can confirm that James O’Callaghan will be departing his role as first team head coach.
