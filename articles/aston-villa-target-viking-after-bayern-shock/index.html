Aston Villa Target Viking After Bayern Shock

TheTools · 21.10.2026 15:07 · source · permalink

Aston Villa will look to maintain their impressive form when they host Viking FK in the UEFA Champions League today. Viking made headlines in their last outing after holding Bayern Munich to a thrilling 3-3 draw, having raced into a two-goal lead before snatching a late equaliser.

However, the Norwegian side face a tougher test at Villa Park against a Villa team unbeaten in their last five matches, with four wins and one draw.

Villa have also shown their attacking strength in Europe, scoring at least three goals in each of their first two leaguephase matches. They have found the net in 13 of their last 14 competitive home games.

Viking, though, should not be underestimated. They have scored in 18 consecutive matches across all competitions and have netted nine goals in four Champions League matches, including qualifying rounds.

Their ability to score against strong opposition could make today’s encounter an entertaining contest, particularly as both teams have shown defensive weaknesses.

Six of Villa’s last eight matches have produced goals for both sides, while Viking have conceded exactly three goals in each of their two league-phase games. Villa remain favourites to secure the points, but the visitors could trouble their defence.
