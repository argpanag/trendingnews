Sophie Cunningham Poses for Maxim in Photos, Talks 'Enforcer' Label and Viral Pointing Video

TheTools · 23.11.2026 21:06 · source · permalink

Indiana Fever guard Sophie Cunningham was featured on the cover of the Maxim November/December 2026 issue.

Cunningham explained why she doesn't embrace her reputation as Indiana's enforcer in an interview with Maxim's Jon Small.

"Nope. I don't really know what I'm enforcing," Cunningham told Small. "I get it, and maybe I feel that a little bit because of how I was raised; we were always taught to protect the ones you love. You go to war for the people to the left and right of you. So I do have a sense of protecting myself and my teammates. But to call me the enforcer, I know people love it, but I just don't view myself as that. It's become a fun storyline on the internet. All my family and friends, they get why people are saying that because they know how passionate and protective I am about my people. But they also know I'm the biggest goofball, just trying to laugh, smile, and truly enjoy the game of basketball. I'm unapologetically me. I really am a very chill, down-to-earth person. I love people. I want people to feel confident and feel successful, and whatever I can do to help them feel that way, I'm gonna do."

"Of course, when I'm on the court, I'm gonna do whatever it takes for my team to win," she added. "I'm the type of person who can flip the switch and also flip it off. I think a lot of people aren't used to people who can do that. A lot of people who don't know me think that I'm like this 24/7, but really, I'm just a very goofy, down-to-earth, go-with-the-flow, chill type of human."

During the 2025 WNBA season, Cunningham and former Connecticut Sun guard Jacy Sheldon were involved in a brief scuffle near the end of a game.

Cunningham also generated plenty of attention for pointing at forward DeWanna Bonner during a meeting between the Fever and Phoenix Mercury earlier this year.

"I'm just sassy sometimes, and that's totally okay," Cunningham explained to Small. "I know how to get in people's heads. Not saying that's a good thing or a bad thing, but sometimes it is to my benefit. Once I get in someone's head, my sassiness and my competitiveness come out. Sometimes it comes out in pointing. Sometimes it comes out in knocking someone's head off."

"[As for the pointing incident], I was just messing around," she added. "She said something, and I went to the ref, and her comment was, 'Don't point at me.' I was like, 'Oh, shouldn't have said that.' And that's when I just held it. She was just going ballistic, and I was like, 'Oh, this is fun.'"

Cunningham appeared in 43 games for Indiana last season, primarily operating in a bench role. She averaged 7.9 points, 2.3 rebounds and 1.2 assists per game while shooting 46.0 percent from the field and 41.9 percent from behind the arc.
