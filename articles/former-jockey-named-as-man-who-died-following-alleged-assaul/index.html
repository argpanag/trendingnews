Former jockey named as man who died following alleged assault in Co Down

TheTools · 15.09.2026 20:44 · source · permalink

A man who died on Sunday following an alleged assault in Newtownards a week previously, has been named as former jockey Patrick (Paddy) Donaghy.

39-year-old Donaghy was a flat jockey based in Britain for a decade between 2005 and 2014, partnering over 60 winners in a career that saw him ride frequently for north of England trainers Pat Haslam and Jedd O’Keeffe.

Donaghy died in hospital following an incident at a house in the Stratheden Heights area of the Co Down town on Monday, September 7.

His last ride was at Wolverhampton in October 2014 and the last of his 63 winners was on a horse named Hamble at Lingfield in June, 2014.

Donaghy also spent part of his career riding in Bahrain, where he rode several winners and Bahrain Racing posted and was a much-loved and respected member of the racing community.

His brother, Joseph Donaghy, 46, appeared at Newtownards Magistrates Court last week charged with causing grievous bodily harm in connection with the incident.

"It's very sad news," jockey Paul Mulrennan told the Racing Post. "I feel for his little boy Lennon, who's only five, and the whole family.

"Paddy was a great lad. Wherever he went, everyone liked him. I remember he was at Patrick Haslam's when he first came over. I was apprentice to Patrick, so we had a rapport and we got quite close.

"He was a good, solid rider. As with a lot of young lads, the scales were a struggle for him. Yet he was still great craic, very funny and very witty. Even though his career probably didn't pan out how he would have wanted, he was still happy-go-lucky. He had a good heart and was liked wherever he went."

The PSNI have not provided any further details regarding the nature of the incident or how the man ended up in hospital.

The investigation into the man's death, which took place six days after the incident at the property, was announced by the PSNI on Monday, September 14.

A statement from the Police Service of Northern Ireland (PSNI) said: “Detectives are investigating the circumstances surrounding the death of a man aged in his thirties.

“The man died in hospital on Sunday, following an incident at a house in Stratheden Heights, Newtownards, on September 7. There are no further details at this time.”

A death notice for Donaghy on Funeral Times said: “Peacefully in hospital. Late of Stratheden Heights, Newtownards. Dearly loved son of John and Sarah Donaghy. Much loved brother of Joseph, Brona and her husband Simon and Sarah. A loving and devoted father of Lennon, a dear uncle to his many nieces and nephews. RIP.”

“Service in St Patrick's Parish Church, North Street, Newtownards on Friday 18th September at 11:00am, followed by committal in Movilla Cemetery. House Strictly Private Family Only.

“Family flowers only please. Donations, if desired, to ICU Ulster Hospital may be made online at https://sclarkeandson.co.uk/donate. Or cheques payable to SEH & SCTrust. Lovingly remembered and sadly missed by the entire family circle.”
