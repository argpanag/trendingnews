University of Tsukuba DF Ajiki Set to Join FC Gifu in 2027 | Official Website of J.LEAGUE

TheTools · 11.10.2026 08:31 · source · permalink

Fri, 9 Oct 2026, 17:30 (JST)

FC Gifu have announced that defender Yuto Ajiki of the University of Tsukuba will join the club in 2027. The club also announced that Ajiki has been registered as a 2026/27 JFA/J.League Special Designated Player.
 
The Hokkaido native commented as follows:
 
“I’m extremely happy to be able to fulfil my childhood dream of becoming a professional footballer and to begin my career with a wonderful club like FC Gifu. I’m deeply grateful to my family for always supporting me, to Coach Koido and all the coaches who have guided me, and to everyone who has helped me along the way. I’ll carry that gratitude with me, stay humble and keep pushing myself to grow and reach even higher. I want to play with determination and passion, give everything I have for the team, and become a player that FC Gifu’s fans and supporters can truly get behind. Thank you for your support.”
