Two Italians killed in alleged Iranian drone attack on Kuwait; Tehran denies involvement | CNN

TheTools · 26.11.2026 14:14 · source · permalink

Two Italian nationals were killed on Thursday in an Iranian drone attack on the Gulf nation of Kuwait, Italy’s Foreign Minister said. Tehran denied any involvement in the incident.

Antonio Tajani said “several drones of Iranian origin targeted Kuwaiti military installations” including an “air defence detection station where two Italian technicians from (Rome-based defense company) Rheinmetall Italia were working.”

“A powerful explosion occurred, and, according to reports reaching us from Kuwait, both of our fellow citizens have unfortunately died,” Tajani said in a statement.

Kuwait’s defense ministry said its armed forces detected and engaged several hostile drones in Kuwaiti airspace. The ministry blamed what it called “Iranian aggression” for an attack on a “vital site,” saying falling shrapnel wounded two other people, a Kuwaiti national and a Pakistani national. Material damage also occurred as a result of the drone attack, it said.

The defense ministry confirmed the two deaths and extended its “deepest condolences to the families of the deceased.”

Iran denied carrying out the attack.

Brig. Gen. Abolfazl Shekarchi, a senior spokesperson for Iran’s armed forces, said: “No military or offensive operations whatsoever have been conducted by the Armed Forces of the Islamic Republic of Iran in the region.” He claimed it was a “false flag” operation aimed at escalating regional insecurity.

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi also denied any Iranian involvement during a phone call with his Italian counterpart, Antonio Tajani, according to Iran’s foreign ministry.

Araghch also “announced Tehran’s readiness to cooperate with the governments of Kuwait and Italy to clarify the origin of the incident and examine its various aspects.”

Speaking to reporters later Thursday, Tajani said the drone used in the attack was manufactured in Iran. Referring to Araghchi’s denial of Iranian responsibility, Tajani noted that “numerous pro-Iranian militias operate in the region.”

The Italian technicians were in Kuwait to maintain “detection antennas” for an air defense system used by the country’s armed forces, Italy’s foreign ministry said.

In response to the latest attack, Italy’s Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni expressed her “deepest sympathy” and offered her “heartfelt condolences” to the victims’ families, while Israeli Foreign Minister Gideon Sa’ar said the incident shows how Iran’s “unchecked aggression threatens stability and security in the Middle East and beyond.”

Iran has continues to target what it says are American targets in the region in retaliation for US attacks on Iranian sites. Earlier this month, Iran targeted a Kuwaiti air base that hosts US forces after the US military hit what it said was a missile launch site in Iran. Elsewhere, Bahrain on Sunday said an Iranian attack injured at least two people.

This story has been updated with additional details.

CNN’s Dana Karni contributed to this reporting.
