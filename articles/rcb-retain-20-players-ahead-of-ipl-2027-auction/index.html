RCB retain 20 players ahead of IPL 2027 Auction

TheTools · 21.11.2026 09:26 · source · permalink

Royal Challengers Bengaluru have kept the bulk of their squad intact ahead of IPL 2027, retaining 20 players and releasing six on the retention deadline day, November 17th.

The franchise will head into the IPL 2027 Auction in Goa on December 16th with ₹14.15 Cr in the purse and five slots to fill, including one overseas spot.

Mangesh Yadav (₹5.2 Cr), Jacob Bethell (₹2.6 Cr), Swapnil Singh (₹50L), Satvik Deswal (₹30L) and Vihaan Malhotra (₹30L) are the players released by the franchise along with Richard Gleeson (₹1.6 Cr), who joined as Nuwan Thushara’s replacement towards the end of last season.

The releases free up ₹9.15 Cr, and combined with the ₹50L left over from last year’s auction plus the additional ₹5 Cr added to the purse, RCB will have ₹14.15 Cr to work with in the IPL 2027 Auction.

One of the big releases was that of Jacob Bethell. Mo Bobat clarified on the release of the much-talked about English all-rounder, stating, "Unfortunately, he had a re-occurrence of a knee injury and at this stage, there is this considerable amount of uncertainty about his return from the injury in the stipulated timeline. Therefore, it puts him at risk about the availability ahead of the next IPL season. So given the information made available to us today, we have decided to release him but we do so with very heavy heart."

With seven overseas players already in the squad, RCB have room for one more overseas signing as they look to fine-tune their squad for the new season.
