Genmab’s Rina-S data beats Jefferies’ most optimistic scenario

TheTools · 26.10.2026 09:03 · source · permalink

The US investment bank says the phase 3 results in ovarian cancer change its view of the drug candidate and still point to upside for the shares.

US investment bank Jefferies says the phase 3 data for Genmab’s drug candidate Rina-S, which the Danish biotech company released late Friday, is exceptionally strong. According to the bank, the results from the Rainfol-02 trial exceed even its most optimistic scenario.

“The magnitude of benefit exceeds our most bullish scenario, while the implied absolute benefit likely exceeds what our previous expert considered practice-changing. Based on the latest phase 2 data [Rainfol-01], we believe the stock should still have upside of at least a high single-digit percentage,” Jefferies writes in a note on Friday’s results.

In Rainfol-02, Rina-S produced a statistically significant improvement in progression-free survival in patients with platinum-resistant ovarian cancer.

Ovarian cancer is platinum-resistant when it returns within six months of completing platinum-based chemotherapy, or when the patient does not respond to that treatment at all.

The trial met its primary endpoint, showing a statistically significant and clinically meaningful improvement over chemotherapy.

The study included two patient populations. In one, Rina-S cut the risk of disease progression or death by 71%. In the other, the reduction was 67%.

Genmab’s American depositary receipts (ADRs) rose 9.5% to USD 43.79 in US trading on Friday after the results. Ten ADRs represent one Danish Genmab share, so at current exchange rates the US closing price equals DKK 2,923.70 (USD 438.36) per share.

Genmab’s Danish shares closed Friday down 0.4% at DKK 2,644 (USD 396.42), leaving a 10.6% gap between the two closing prices.
