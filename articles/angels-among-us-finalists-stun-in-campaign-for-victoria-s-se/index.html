"Angels Among Us" Finalists Stun In Campaign For Victoria's Secret Bombshell Shine (Special Look)

TheTools · 06.10.2026 02:38 · source · permalink

The ten women look breathtaking in the new campaign.

Not all of the ten “Angels Among Us” finalists will win the competition associated with this year’s Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show, but all have already appeared in a stunning shoot for the iconic lingerie and beauty brand.

The women — Kassandra Marie Crespo, Riley Midgett, Kyani Mekōkualani Bateman, Andreína Martínez Founier, Solange Omon, Aria DuPont Andrew, Kailyn Shepherd, Tatyana Tinsley, Mar Supremacy, and Brielle Reimann — posed for the new Bombshell Shine collection campaign.

Shot by Carlijn Jacobs, the collection finds the women looking amazing as they showcase Victoria’s Secret lingerie and hype the new collection of beauty products like Diamond Glaze Body Luminizer, Ultra-Shimmer Fragrance Mist, Lip Drama Ultra-Shimmer Gloss, Shimmer Eau De Toilette, Glow Getter Shimmer Body Butter, Bombshell Bling Scented Shimmer Hairspray, and Diva Dust Face & Body Shimmer Powder.
