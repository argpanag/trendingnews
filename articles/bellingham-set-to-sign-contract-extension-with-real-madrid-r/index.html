Bellingham set to sign contract extension with Real Madrid -report

TheTools · 23.09.2026 15:04 · source · permalink
  • Real Madrid Transfer News & Rumors

Real Madrid and Jude Bellingham are exploring a contract extension which would keep the English midfielder in the club until 2031, according to a report published on MARCA. Bellingham would get a significant salary raise when this extension is agreed upon, per that same report.

The English star signed for Real Madrid in the summer of 2023 and his current deal expires in 2029. At 23 years old, Bellingham already is one of the world’s best players, although he also has room for improvement. That’s why Real Madrid want to avoid interest from other clubs and sign him to a longer contract.

Bellingham has been one of Real Madrid’s most valuable players this season after José Mourinho decided to deploy him as a pure number 10 behind the striker.
