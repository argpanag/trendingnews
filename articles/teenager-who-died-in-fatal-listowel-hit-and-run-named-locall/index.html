The teenager who died in the fatal hit and run in Listowel has been named locally.

17-year-old Rory O’Connor from Athea died in the early hours of Saturday morning when he was struck by a van which failed to remain at the scene.

A man has been arrested in connection with the incident which occurred on the N69 at Dromin Upper.
