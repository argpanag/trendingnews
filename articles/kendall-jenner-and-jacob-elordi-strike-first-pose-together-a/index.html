Kendall Jenner and Jacob Elordi strike first pose together as romance heats up

TheTools · 18.10.2026 21:10 · source · permalink

Kendall Jenner and Jacob Elordi have kept their reported romance largely out of the spotlight, but the pair appeared more comfortable showing off their connection at a major Hollywood event.

The Kardashians star, 30, and the Frankenstein actor, 29, attended the sixth annual Academy Museum Gala in Los Angeles on October 17. Although they walked the red carpet separately, Jenner and Elordi were later spotted posing together inside the Academy Museum of Motion Pictures, marking their first time taking photos together at an event.

One photo from the evening showed the pair sitting together for a meal at the star-studded gathering. Jenner leaned into Elordi as he wrapped an arm around her waist, while the supermodel rested a hand on him. The actor also gave the camera a smirk as they posed side by side. This comes after he protected her from paparazzi just days earlier.

Their appearance at the gala came just weeks after Elordi showed his support for Jenner during Milan Fashion Week. On September 26, the actor attended the Bottega Veneta show, where Jenner walked the runway.

Although Elordi was among the celebrities seated in the front row, footage shared on X showed him taking out his digital camera to photograph Jenner as she walked down the runway.

Other videos from the show captured Elordi cheering her on with a whistle from the crowd, giving fans another glimpse of the pair’s connection.

However, their growing visibility has not meant they are ready to share every detail of their relationship. Just days after the fashion show, the couple were spotted together in Paris, where Elordi appeared to ask photographers to give them some space as they tried to return to their hotel.

In a video posted to social media on September 28, the actor could be heard asking the photographers, “Wait, wait, wait, can we just walk home?”

He then repeated, “Stop, stop, stop.” With his arm around Jenner’s waist, Elordi continued, “Can we just walk home back to our hotel?” He added, “No flash, just let us walk,” before saying, “Just let us go.”

The exchange highlighted the challenges of maintaining a private relationship while being followed by photographers. Still, the pair have continued to be spotted together during outings in different cities.

Elordi has previously spoken about wanting a relationship that feels genuine, even when public attention makes that difficult. In a 2020 interview with Vanity Fair, the actor discussed the kind of romance he hoped to find, explaining that he wanted something that felt like the love stories he had read about.

“You want it to be genuine and real and have all the feelings of what you read in 1920s literature,” Elordi told the publication. “But when people are watching and talking about it, it makes it a little bit difficult.”

The actor has also described his ideal romantic evening, offering a glimpse into his more traditional approach to dating. Elordi said his perfect outing would be “a night in Paris with wine, and you’re dressed to the nines.”
