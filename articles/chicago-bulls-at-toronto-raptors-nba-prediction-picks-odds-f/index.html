Chicago Bulls at Toronto Raptors NBA Prediction, Picks & Odds for Oct. 21, 2026 - AOL

TheTools · 21.10.2026 23:06 · source · permalink

The Toronto Raptors will open their season against the Chicago Bulls on Wednesday. Tipoff is scheduled for 7:30 p.m. ET at Scotiabank Arena.

Toronto made a splash during the offseason by bringing Kawhi Leonard back to the franchise in a trade that sent Brandon Ingram and Gradey Dick to the Los Angeles Clippers. Darko Rajaković returns as head coach, with former Houston Rockets head coach Stephen Silas joining his staff.

Chicago added Nic Claxton and Norman Powell while making a change on the bench, replacing Billy Donovan with Tiago Splitter.

Looking back to last season, Toronto closed the year on the right side of .500 (46-36 in the regular season), and they’re hoping to kick off an even more successful campaign this year. On the other hand, Chicago will seek to improve after ending the regular season at 31-51.

Toronto had Chicago’s number last season, sweeping all three meetings. The Raptors won 123-107 on Feb. 5, 110-101 on Feb. 19, and 139-109 on March 18. The teams did not meet in the postseason, as Chicago missed the playoffs and Toronto lost to Cleveland in seven games in the first round.

Everything went Toronto’s way against Chicago the last time they met, on March 18 last season, as Toronto made off with a 139-109 victory. In that matchup, the Raptors amassed a halftime lead of 72-45, an impressive feat they’ll look to repeat on Wednesday.
