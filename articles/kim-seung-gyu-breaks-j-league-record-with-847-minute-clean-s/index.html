Kim Seung-gyu breaks J.League record with 847-minute clean sheet streak

TheTools · 07.11.2026 13:20 · source · permalink

"national team goalkeeper" Kim Seung-gyu (36, FC Tokyo) left a new mark in J.League history in Japan.

FC Tokyo displayed an impenetrable defense that went an astonishing 847 minutes without conceding, breaking the league's longest consecutive clean sheet record. At the center of that was veteran goalkeeper Kim Seung-gyu, who firmly protected the goal.

FC Tokyo defeated Kashiwa Antlers 2-0 in the 2026 season J1 League round 14 match held on the 7th at Ajinomoto Stadium in Tokyo, Japan. They earned a shutout victory against Kashiwa, last season's champion known for its powerful attack, bolstering their title race push.

With this victory FC Tokyo wrote a new chapter in J.League history. From conceding in the 53rd minute of the September 2 match against Shimizu S-Pulse through this match, they maintained 847 consecutive minutes without conceding in league play. This is a new record surpassing the previous mark of 823 minutes set by Nagoya Grampus in 2021.

At the center of the record was Kim Seung-gyu. He, who has appeared in every league match this season, defended the goal while repelling Kashiwa's fierce attacks. In particular, in the 19th minute of the first half he securely caught Naomi Ueda's one-touch shot, dampening the opponent's momentum.

FC Tokyo ceded initiative to the opponent throughout the first half and struggled against cross attacks, but, centered on the central defense led by Alexander Scholz and Inamura Hayato, they did not lose concentration. Kim Seung-gyu also calmly stopped the opponent's attacks together with the defensive line.

Kim Seung-gyu cited the team's overall confidence as the driving force behind the clean sheet run. He said, "We kept holding on, thinking that if we don't concede, an opportunity will definitely come to us."

He added, "Finishing matches with a clean sheet and creating the game's flow leads to confidence," and said, "Now, the moment we step onto the field, each player feels the atmosphere of 'we will not lose.'"

After patience came opportunity. FC Tokyo changed the game's flow by scoring the opener through a flank attack in the 15th minute of the second half. In the 31st minute of the second half, Kim Seung-gyu preserved the team's lead by saving Chavric's decisive shot.

About the situation at the time, Kim Seung-gyu said, "At first the ball was obscured as I overlapped with an opponent, but the moment I confirmed the ball I judged and reacted," and added, "If we had conceded in that situation it would have been a difficult match, so I'm glad I saved it."

Having beaten Kashiwa and secured three valuable points, FC Tokyo continued their challenge toward a first league title. Kim Seung-gyu said, "I expect this victory will give us strength and be a good starting point for the next match."

He did not attach great significance to personal records. He emphasized, "Rather than continuing the clean sheets themselves, I think fighting each match with our best leads to these good results."
