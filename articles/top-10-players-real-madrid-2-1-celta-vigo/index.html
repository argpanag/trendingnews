TOP 10 PLAYERS: Real Madrid 2-1 Celta Vigo

TheTools · 22.11.2026 02:39 · source · permalink
  • Real Madrid CF: Data Analysis

Los Blancos had to dig deep but finished the game with three points

Arda Güler was heavily involved in Real Madrid’s attacking play against Celta Vigo, leading the match with 20 progressive passes and 50 touches in the final third. The midfielder also created three shooting chances across his 94 minutes, sharing the highest total with teammate Vinícius Júnior and Celta’s Javi Galán.

Vinícius needed just 38 minutes to make his mark on the game. Alongside three key passes, he recorded seven progressive carries, the second-highest total, and generated 0.30 expected threat from open-play passes, ranking third. He was involved in the build-up of both of Real Madrid’s goals.

Jude Bellingham helped Madrid advance the ball throughout the game. He made eight progressive passes, joint second behind Güler, and completed two take-ons, also ranking second. His six progressive carries placed him joint third. Bellingham scored the crucial equaliser from the edge of the box to make it 1-1 on the night for Real Madrid.

Celta had five players among the top 10 for defensive actions, with Javi Rodríguez recording a match-high 17. Galán offered a creative contribution at the other end, matching Güler and Vinícius with three chances created.
