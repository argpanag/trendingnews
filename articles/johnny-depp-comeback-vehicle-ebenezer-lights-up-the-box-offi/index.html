Ebenezer topped the box office with $19 million domestically and $42 million globally its premiere weekend.

Ti West's gothic Christmas parable has courted controversy as the comeback of embattled Johnny Depp.

The Cat in the Hat, Ramayana, and Godzilla Minus Zero all tailed behind, while The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is set up for a big debut next weekend.

The Ghost of Christmas Past bodes a bright future for the holiday season ahead.

Ebenezer, a new comic-gothic riff on Charles Dickens' A Christmas Carol, enjoyed a merry first weekend at the box office. The unlikely duo of stalwart indie director Ti West and controversial star Johnny Depp snagged the No. 1 spot on both the domestic and global leaderboards with $19 million and and $42 million, respectively, per box office tracker Rentrak.

Critics may have all but eviscerated the film, but Ebenezer continues the trend of a booming box office reported throughout most of 2026. This weekend marked a 14.2 percent increase in total domestic grosses over the same time last year, when films like Now You See Me: Now You Don't, The Running Man, and Predator: Badlands duked it out for the top spot.

Stills from 'The Cat in the Hat,' 'Ramayana,' and 'Godzilla Minus Zero'
Credit: Warner Bros. Pictures;Sony

However, this weekend's total gross of $86.8 million marks a 28.5 percent decrease from last weekend. And while Paramount's Ebenezer cleared its reported $38 million budget, there's still a long way to go when factoring in marketing and promotional figures, which are routinely excluded from budgetary estimates.

With weekend 2 grosses frequently dropping 50 percent or more, and with Ebenezer's critical notices where they are, the film may struggle to achieve true profitability.

The No. 2 and 3 spots on the domestic leaderboard, both in their second weekends, paint a haunting portrait of Ebenezer's potential future. Warner Bros.' The Cat in the Hat took the silver medal with just $11.4 million, dropping 42 percent from its $20.5 million open.

Then there's Godzilla Minus Zero.

GKids' CGI spectacular had high ambitions after 2023's Godzilla Minus One over-performed at the box office. But the sequel dropped a staggering 73 percent in its second weekend in American theaters, earning just $8.3 million. Globally, its haul has reached $89.9 million — far more than necessary to clear its estimated budget of $15 million. But should Ebenezer follow the same path, the eventual result may not be so pretty.

Elsewhere, Sony's Ghost Soldier flopped with a $5.2 million domestic premiere atop its $18 million estimated budget. Amazon's star-studded How to Rob a Bank, featuring Nicolas Hoult, Zoë Kravitz, and Pete Davidson, earned $5.1 million in its own domestic premiere, an even more dismal open given its estimated budget range of $40-60 million.

Next week, a real tentpole is coming for the crown.

Though the festival favorite biopic, I Play Rocky, has real winning potential, the most obvious challenger to Ebenezer's hold on the top spot will be The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.

Budgeted anywhere between $170 million and $200 million, the dystopian actioner has its ambitions set high. But the sixth installment of the franchise must follow a precedent set by 2023's The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes, which earned $360 million atop a $100 million budget.
