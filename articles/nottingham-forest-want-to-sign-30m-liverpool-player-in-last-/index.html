Nottingham Forest Want to Sign £30m Liverpool Player in Last-Gasp Deal

TheTools · 30.08.2026 14:32 · source · permalink

Nottingham Forest are reportedly eyeing a move to sign Liverpool goalkeeper Giorgi Mamardashvili in the final days of the transfer window.

It's clear that Nottingham Forest are eyeing a goalkeeper before the end of the summer transfer window, after Fabrizio Romano reported that they submitted an offer to sign Crystal Palace's Dean Henderson.

Nottingham Forest's current number one option between the sticks is Matz Sels, while they also have John Victor as a backup.

Sels started in goal against Liverpool on Saturday lunchtime, but Oliver Glasner is clearly not happy with his goalkeeping options.

It was no surprise to see Forest make a move for one of Glasner's former players, Henderson, but it was always going to be difficult for them to convince the Eagles to part ways.

Now it appears that the Midlands club have moved on to other targets as they look to strengthen the goalkeeping department.

According to journalist Matteo Moreto, Nottingham Forest are interested in signing Liverpool's second-choice goalkeeper, Mamardashvili.

Mamardashvili made the move to Liverpool for a fee in the region of £30 million back in August 2024.

It remains to be seen whether Liverpool would be willing to sanction an exit for the goalkeeper, but it certainly wouldn't be a surprise if he were looking for first-team football.

A loan move could certainly make sense, with Mamardashvili significantly younger than Alisson Becker and capable of taking over from him in the future.

Liverpool would face difficult decision

It would certainly be a difficult decision for Liverpool in terms of allowing Mamardashvili to depart.

If Nottingham Forest presented a loan offer earlier in the transfer window, then there's every chance Liverpool would have accepted.

Now the Reds are given very little time to find a replacement, even if he is their only backup goalkeeper.

A permanent transfer would certainly seem unlikely, considering he's been brought to the club to eventually replace Alisson. Even a loan move could be a stretch at this stage of the transfer window.
