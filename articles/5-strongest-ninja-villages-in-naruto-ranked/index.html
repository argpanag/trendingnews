5 Strongest Ninja Villages in Naruto, Ranked

There are several ninja villages mentioned in Naruto, but the Five Great Shinobi Countries are the most focused on in the story. These include Konohagakure, Sunagakure, Kumogakure, Kirigakure, and Iwagakure. They are the strongest and largest villages, along with being the only ones led by a Kage, a title bestowed on the strongest ninja.

That being said, the five are not equal in power. There are various factors that contribute to why one ninja village is stronger than the other. Whether it's economic resources, geography, population, or leadership quality, some villages are bound to fall behind. Each of them has its own strengths and weaknesses showcased throughout Naruto, although only one is clearly the strongest.

Sunagakure Has Always Struggled to Flourish

The Hidden Sand Village, also known as Sunagakure, is located in the Land of Wind in Naruto. Although its harsh desert climate keeps invaders out, Sunagakure has a plethora of issues that make life worse for its citizens. With water shortages, sandstorms, and very little fertile land, the Hidden Sand Village struggles to support a large population.

However, geography isn't the only reason Sunagakure is the weakest ninja village. Under the domain of the Land of Wind Daimyo, he imposed significant budget cuts and outsourced Konohagakure shinobi, driving the village into poverty. The Fourth Kazekage, Rasa, was arguably the weakest Kage in Naruto.

Rasa allied with Orochimaru to attack Konohagakure, who then betrayed and assassinated the Kazekage. This forced a 15-year-old Gaara to become the new Kazekage. Overall, Sunagakure has been dealt a terrible hand, but under Gaara's leadership, proving himself to be a more powerful and wiser shinobi, it has grown stronger.

Kirigakure Suffered Through Internal Political Conflict

In the Land of Water, there lies Kirigakure, also known as the Hidden Mist Village. It is home to some of the strongest shinobi in Naruto and generations of the Seven Swordsmen of the Mist. Kirigakure also had two Tailed Beasts, giving the village the advantage over Sunagakure.

However, the Hidden Mist Village was torn apart on the inside through brutal and violent traditions, plunging into a dark era under the Fourth Mizukage, Yagura Karatachi, where it was called the Village of Bloody Mist. The internal civil war caused Kirigakure's population to grow more paranoid, even slaughtering Kekkei Genkai users in fear of their power.

Obito Uchiha was puppeteering Yagura the entire time, enforcing isolationism and tyranny across the Hidden Mist Village. Many citizens rebelled or fled the village. While the post-civil war unrest lingered, Mei Terumi, the Fifth Mizukage, rebuilt it into an era of peace and modernization, with her successor, Chojuro, continuing her vision. Despite their best efforts, Kirigakure is still one of the weakest villages.

Iwagakure is a Powerhouse that Remains Steady

The Hidden Stone Village, or Iwagakure, is located in the Land of Earth. Much like its element, the shinobi of Iwagakure are known for their stubborn will, unwavering obedience to their Tsuchikage's leadership, and immense discipline. The Tsuchikage, especially Mu and Onoki, are incredibly powerful, possessing Kekkei Tota, which combines Earth, Wind, and Fire Releases.

Iwagakure has also weaponized two Tailed Beasts in the past. The mountains surrounding Iwagakure provide natural defense. Despite the village's formidable nature, which once fought on equal grounds against Konohagakure, Iwagakure has some flaws that place it in the middle.

During the Third Shinobi World War, the Kannabi Bridge's destruction cut off its main supply lines. Since they were also dragged into other wars, Iwagakure lacked resources. In the final blow, the Hidden Stone Village lost both Tailed Beasts to the Akatsuki. Thus, while Iwagakure is powerful in its own right, the village lost the advantage and couldn't reach its peak.

Kumogakure Prospers Economically and In Strength

In the second-strongest position, Kumogakure, also called the Hidden Cloud Village, stands in the Land of Lightning. Unlike the previously mentioned villages, Kumogakure has a stable political environment, economy, and military. Thus, Kumogakure had a high budget to support its citizens. It is home to some of the strongest yet underrated Naruto characters, with a fast and aggressive combat style.

Kumogakure also possessed two Tailed Beasts, but they were far more cooperative with their Jinchuriki, Killer B and Yugito Nii. What keeps Kumogakure from being the strongest of the Five Great Shinobi Nations is that the shinobi are overly aggressive. Although the village has high defense from the surrounding mountain range, the shinobi rely on Lightning Release and lack combat versatility.

Moreover, despite their power, Kumogakure's past actions have earned them an untrustworthy reputation, leaving them more isolated from the others. The Hidden Cloud Village could have been at the top, if not for Konohagakure's overwhelming advantage.

Konohagakure Has Elite Clans And Powerful Leaders

Without a shadow of a doubt, Konohagakure is the strongest of all villages in Naruto. The Hidden Leaf Village is located in the Land of Fire and was founded by uniting the Senju and Uchiha clans, two of the most powerful military forces in the Warring States Period. Hashirama Senju and Madara Uchiha's partnership opened other prominent and powerful clans to join Konohagakure, such as the Sarutobi, Yamanaka, Hyuga, and Akimichi.

Konohagakure is the strongest by a great margin. Due to having such elite bloodlines, the Hidden Leaf Village has numerous specialized techniques and unique Kekkei Genkai. Moreover, it secured the most powerful Tailed Beast, Kurama. The Hokage have also been immensely strong and have led with respect rather than fear.

The most powerful characters in Naruto, namely the protagonist and Sasuke Uchiha, hail from Konohagakure. The Hidden Leaf Village will always remain at the top of the ladder.
