Live updates: Eagles visit steamy Nashville to face Titans in Week 2

TheTools · 20.09.2026 14:39 · source · permalink

Follow along for live updates as the Eagles visit the Titans for the Week 2 matchup.

What to Know

  • The Eagles are 6-0 in road openers under Nick Sirianni, but 0-3 all-time at Nissan Stadium.
  • The Eagles are sliding Cam Jurgens over to guard to replace Landon Dickerson, who went on IR after Week 1 with a bone bruise in his knee.
  • Drew Kendall will start at center.
  • Both teams will be up against some serious heat in Nashville, with temperatures reaching 97ºF.
  • The Titans are 0-1 on the young season and 9-36 in their last 45 games.
  • Jonathan Greenard has been ruled out with a pec injury.
