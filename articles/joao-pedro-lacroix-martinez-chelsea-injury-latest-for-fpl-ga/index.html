Joao Pedro, Lacroix, Martinez: Chelsea injury latest for FPL Gameweek 11

Speaking at Friday’s press conference, Xabi Alonso confirmed that Joao Pedro (quad) remains out, along with Jorrel Hato (back), Maxence Lacroix (hamstring), Reece James (knee) and Jamie Gittens (ankle).
