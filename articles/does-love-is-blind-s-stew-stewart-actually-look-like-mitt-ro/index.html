Does ‘Love Is Blind’s Stew Stewart Actually Look Like Mitt Romney? - AOL

TheTools · 17.10.2026 03:40 · source · permalink

This season of Love Is Blind, the most pressing question in the pods is not the typical, “Can you find love sight unseen?” Instead, everyone is asking a far more pressing question: “Does Stew look more like a Kennedy or Mitt Romney?”

Scott “Stew” Stewart stepped into the pods during Season 11, instantly telling viewers his personal philosophy when it comes to the dating experiment. He shared, “We all want to present an appearance, especially across the wall. We all want to give an indication of our status and who we are.” In the Men’s Quarters, Stew focuses on the finer things, and his overall old-money vibe quickly earned him a nickname from his fellow Pod Squad members: “Kennedy.”

Embracing the nickname, when he met his fiancée, Jacqueline “Jax” Guelli, face-to-face for the first time, he wasted no time telling her, “I may have undersold myself. Do you know the guys’ nickname for me on the other side? They all call me ‘Kennedy.'” Check out Stew and John F. Kennedy Jr. below to see if their assessment holds any water.

Netflix / Diane Freed / Getty Images

After Stew likened himself to a member of one of America’s most prominent political families, who are also known to have had quite a few lookers among the bunch, Jax told him, “Please. [You look] more like Mitt Romney.” Later, she elaborated, “They say, Kennedy. I think he’s more like a Mitt Romney.” Seconds later, she decided to leave the experiment without leaving Stew any contact info.

So, does Stew look more like a Kennedy or Mitt Romney? While we’re going to have to agree with the guys that Stew gives major Kennedy vibes, he definitely looks more like a Romney. See a photo of young Mitt Romney below to see what we mean.
