Russia Intensifies Offensive on Donbas Fortress Belt, Losing More Equipment

TheTools · 15.11.2026 14:46 · source · permalink

Russian forces have intensified mechanized assaults on the Dobropillia and Kostiantynivka axes, seeking to set the conditions for a further offensive against Ukraine’s defensive belt in the Donetsk region.

The Institute for the Study of War (ISW) reported this.

According to analysts, on November 13, Russian forces launched a mechanized assault toward the village of Hruzke, northeast of Dobropillia.

Russian forces committed three tanks, three infantry fighting vehicles, and more than 10 light vehicles to the assault.

Some armored vehicles managed to approach the line of contact, while infantry dismounted several kilometers from Ukrainian positions. Ukraine’s Defense Forces repelled the assault.

On November 14, operators of the 1st Separate Assault Regiment destroyed a Russian column using Darts drones. Dmytro Filatov, commander of the 1st Separate Assault Regiment, reported that seven vehicles, three quad bikes, and 30 Russian servicemen had been hit during efforts to disrupt preparations for an offensive on Dobropillia.

ISW suggests that this may have been the same mechanized assault toward Hruzke.

In addition, geolocated footage from November 11 indicates that Russian forces attempted a mechanized assault toward Druzhkivka. Ukrainian troops destroyed several Russian infantry fighting vehicles near Nova Poltavka, about 10 kilometers from the front line.

ISW analysts note that Russian command is seeking to take advantage of autumn weather conditions to advance armored vehicles. Fog, strong winds, and rain can reduce the effectiveness of Ukrainian drones, allowing Russian units to approach Ukrainian Defense Forces positions.

According to the institute, the buildup of armored vehicles and the high intensity of assault operations indicate that the Dobropillia and Kostiantynivka axes are priorities for Russian command.

Since early October, these two sectors have accounted for about 80% of Russia’s offensive actions against Ukraine’s fortress belt in the Donetsk region.

According to the Ukrainian General Staff’s operational update as of November 15, Russian forces launched 17 attacks on the Pokrovsk axis, which includes the Dobropillia sector, and another 10 on the Kostiantynivka axis over the previous day. A total of 171 combat engagements were recorded along the front.

Amid the intense fighting, Russian forces continue to suffer significant equipment losses. According to Ukraine’s Ministry of Defense, on November 14, the Defense Forces destroyed 34 armored combat vehicles. This was the highest daily figure since October 2025 and a record since the beginning of 2026.
