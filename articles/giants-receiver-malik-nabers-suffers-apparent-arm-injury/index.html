Giants receiver Malik Nabers suffers apparent arm injury

TheTools · 22.09.2026 01:00 · source · permalink

It’s been a bad night for the New York Giants.

After losing quarterback Jaxson Dart to a knee injury (he’s questionable to return), receiver Malik Nabers has been injured.

Nabers left the game after apparently injuring his upper right arm.

He tried to make a contested catch while being covered by Rams cornerback Trent McDuffie. Immediately after the play, Nabers put his left hand over the area between his bicep and shoulder.

Nabers was in considerable pain. His status will be updated, presumably sooner than later.

The Giants trail the Rams, 7-0.

Nabers returned to action in Week 1 after suffering a torn ACL in his knee last September. It was uncertain whether he’d play in the opener until pregame warmups. He caught six passes for 69 yards against the Cowboys. He has one catch for one yard against the Rams.

UPDATE 9:02 p.m. ET: It’s being described as a shoulder injury for Nabers.
