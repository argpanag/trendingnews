UFC Qatar: Kyoji Horiguchi Doesn't Care Who Wins Van-Kape, Will Be There

TheTools · 19.11.2026 23:09 · source · permalink

Despite coming off his first loss in years, Japanese flyweight Kyoji Horiguchi isn’t focused on what happened last time out.

During this week’s UFC Qatar media day, ahead of a fight with Asu Almabayev, he said simply that “I’m focused on the next fight.”

Adding that he feels “great right now,” Horiguchi (36-6, 1NC) stuck to the line that he’s just “happy to be here,” in a fight where he sees himself bringing “speed, power,” and “good experience.”

“My experience is a lot,” he put it later. “This is not the very first time for me, this fight.”

Despite his approach to the fight, the fact remains that Horiguchi, on his second stint with the UFC, was on the brink of a title shot prior to his loss to Manel Kape this past June. Instead, it’s Kape who will face champ Joshua Van at UFC 336 in Toronto this January.

Asked about who he sees as the winner in that fight, “I’m not sure. I don’t care which will win,” Horiguchi replied. “I will be there.”

The longtime Japanese star and former two-division RIZIN champ and Bellator title holder has the inside track on upcoming opponent Asu Almabayev, meanwhile. The pair have trained together in the past at American Top Team.

“It was a really good practice [with] each other,” Horiguchi admitted. Who got the better of that training? “Of course me!”
