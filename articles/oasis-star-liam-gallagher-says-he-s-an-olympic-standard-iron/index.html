Oasis star Liam Gallagher says he's an 'Olympic' standard ironer - AOL

TheTools · 11.10.2026 01:44 · source · permalink

Rock ‘n’ Roll star Liam Gallagher has gone from hell-raiser to home maker after telling fans that he’s an “Olympic” standard ironer.

The Oasis star took to social media explaining his routine, saying: “I’m gonna do some spiritual stuffs then a bit of ironing.” He added: “I don’t trust anyone ironing my clothes your just asking for theatre.”

Liam has previously told how he prefers washing the dishes by hand. He said: “I am a tidy man, I enjoy tidying, I don’t even do the dishwasher, I do all my gear in the sink like a proper old person.

Liam reunited with brother Noel last year for a 41-date world tour after a 15-year feud.

Leading up to the reunion tour, Liam quit cigarettes and alcohol - and using recreational drugs - stating a disciplined lifestyle was a necessary sacrifice and that the tour “saved my life”.

After a fan posted: “I’m sorry u have to live like a monk during the tour,” he replied: “Oasis saved my life it’s about time I sorted my nappa [head] out I know how much this means to people so stopping drinking and smoking and snorting.”

He did admit he missed having the odd Guinness, saying: “Yeah I miss talking absolute b*****ks when I'm bladdered.”

Liam built his 1990s Rock n Roll persona around heavy drinking and substance use - but has recently pivoted toward clean living, early mornings, and jogging to manage his health.

Liam’s brother Noel once described him as “the angriest man you’ll ever meet” after they fell out.

Noel too has embraced domestic duties. The legendary songwriter who once hoovered up cocaine is now hooked on vacuuming.

"I have a normal life...If my room needs hoovering, I’ll hoover it."

The brothers' breaking point came in Paris in 2009 with the band set to headline the Rock en Seine festival. A trivial argument reportedly escalated into a full-blown fight leading to Noel quitting the band.

But after the success of last year’s gigs they have another worldwide tour planned next year.
