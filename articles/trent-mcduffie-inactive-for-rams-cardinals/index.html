Trent McDuffie inactive for Rams-Cardinals

TheTools · 18.10.2026 18:53 · source · permalink

The Rams will not have their top cornerback for Sunday’s game against the Cardinals.

Trent McDuffie is inactive for the contest. He was listed as questionable after missing Friday’s practice with a groin issue.

McDuffie has registered seven passes defensed with an interception so far this season — his first with Los Angeles.

Rams safety Kam Curl (ankle) is active after he was also questionable.

For the Cardinals, safety Budda Baker is active after he was listed as questionable with a groin injury.

But running back Jeremiyah Love was previously ruled out with an ankle injury.

Arizona’s inactives are Love, quarterback Carson Beck, cornerback Starling Thomas, safety Andrew Wingard, safety Wydett Williams, safety Jerrick Reed, and defensive lineman Andrew Billings.

The inactives for L.A. are McDuffie, receiver C.J. Daniels, receiver Tutu Atwell, quarterback Ty Simpson, defensive end Larrell Murchison, and offensive lineman Bill Murray.

Beck and Simpson are the emergency third QBs for their respective teams.
