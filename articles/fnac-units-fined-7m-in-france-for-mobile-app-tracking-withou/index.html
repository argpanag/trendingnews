Fnac units fined €7m in France for mobile app tracking without consent | MLex | Specialist news and analysis on legal risk and regulation

TheTools · 31.10.2026 00:37 · source · permalink

( October 29, 2026, 11:02 GMT | Official Statement) -- MLex Summary: Retail group units Fnac Darty Participations et Services and Fnac Direct received fines totaling €7 million on Oct. 22 for tracking app users without consent, France’s privacy regulator CNIL confirmed on Thursday. The previously proposed penalty amount was €10 million. The CNIL ordered proof of compliant consent collection within one month of notification or daily penalties of €10,000.
