Bayern Munich handed Michael Olise injury boost

TheTools · 27.11.2026 18:04 · source · permalink

Update: FC Bayern coach Vincent Kompany has confirmed that Olise will miss Saturday's game against Hamburger SV.

Bayern Munich have received a welcome boost on the injury front, with Bild reporting that Michael Olise has resumed running training.

The Frenchman suffered a bruised foot in Friday's 1-1 draw with 1. FC Köln and subsequently missed Wednesday's 2-1 defeat to LOSC Lille in the Champions League.

With Bayern dealing with several injury problems in attack, the German champions will be hoping to welcome the influential winger back as soon as possible.

However, it's currently unclear if Olise will be fit in time for Saturday's Bundesliga clash with Hamburger SV.

In addition to Olise, Manuel Neuer, Alphonso Davies, Serge Gnabry and Ismael Saibari also missed Wednesday's game.

Neuer, Kane and Saibari are all set to remain sidelined long term.
