ASX closes lower on bank sell-off; Telstra rebounds

The Australian sharemarket closed lower on Wednesday, dragged down by a sell-off in the big four lenders.

The S&P/ASX 200 Index dropped 56 points, or 0.6 per cent, to 8727.9, with only five of the 11 sectors trading in the green, including energy companies that were buoyed by the higher oil price at $US106 a barrel.

The communications sector also crept higher as Telstra rebounded 1.3 per cent to $4.70 off a recent sell-off on SpaceX’s spectrum deal.

Local bank stocks fell amid a lukewarm reaction to the first round of Wall Street’s third-quarter earnings overnight, which included results from major lenders such as JPMorgan and Goldman Sachs. Investors also awaited the release of US inflation data.

Meanwhile, Asian stocks rebounded from a heavy chip stock sell-off during the previous sessions. Investor confidence was higher after New York Fed president John Williams signalled just one more rate rise this year, causing US Treasury yields to decline.
