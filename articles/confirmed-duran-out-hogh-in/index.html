CONFIRMED: DURAN OUT, HOGH IN

TheTools · 05.11.2026 18:52 · source · permalink

KASPER HOGH will lead Celtic against Besiktas in the Europa League at Parkhead this evening.

The club’s record purchase, who hasn’t scored in 14 successive games, gets the nod to start against the Turkish giants with Camilo Duran among the stand-by squad.

Martin O’Neill has also given starting places to Jordan Lotomba, James Forrest and Sebastian Tounekti with Colby Donovan. Haissem Hassan and Callum Osmand among the substitutes.

TEAM: Johnstone; Lotomba, Carter-Vickers, Scales, Tierney; Nygren, McGregor, Baur; Forrest, Hogh, Tounekti. Subs: Sinisalo, Doohan, Duran, McCowan, Sorensen, Osmand, Oxlade-Chamberlain, Hassan, McArdle, Murray, Donovan and Ralston.
