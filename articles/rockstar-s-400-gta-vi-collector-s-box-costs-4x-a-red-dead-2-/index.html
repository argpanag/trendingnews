Rockstar's $400 GTA VI Collector's Box Costs 4X a Red Dead 2 Collector's Edition, yet Still Ships without the Game

TheTools · 24.09.2026 17:00 · source · permalink

Today, Rockstar Games has unveiled Grand Theft Auto VI: The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection, a GTA VI Collector's Edition priced at $399.99 that doesn't even include the game. Here's the official description from the Rockstar Games Store:

An exclusive Collector’s Box containing everything you need to kick back and relax, inspired by Macca the Alligator—the hit TV series taking Leonida by storm. The collection includes:

  • 6” figure of Macca the Gator with a stash compartment 
  • Matte black GTAVI Oakley® Frogskins™ sunglasses with Prizm Sapphire lenses  
  • New Era® 9FORTY A-Frame snapback hat with Vice City Leonida embellishments  
  • Macca the Gator magnetic mirror 
  • Vice City swizzle spoon 
  • Vice City razor blade keychain 
  • Leonida Keys crossbody bag 
  • Chunkee the Manatee shot glass 
  • Leonida Keys enamel pin set in a custom metal tin 
  • Premium stickers in a stash bag  
  • Double-sided souvenir poster showcasing the full Leonida landscape 

Measurements / Dimensions: Approximately 48.26 cm x 34.29 cm x 31.75 cm.

Pre-orders are now available ahead of the November 19 launch date, though only for North America and Europe. More information on how to pre-order the collector's edition in Asia, Latin America, and Australia will be shared soon; Rockstar also teased the imminent arrival of more GTA VI-themed apparel and collectibles on the store.

Collector's boxes without the game aren't exactly new, and Rockstar pioneered the modern version. Red Dead Redemption 2's Collector's Box cost $99.99 and didn't include the game, with Rockstar explaining that it wasn't an edition of the game at all, but a separate companion package that players could pair with whichever version they wanted, physical or digital.

Blizzard later followed the same logic. Diablo IV's Limited Collector's Box cost $96.66 and included no copy of the game, not even in-game items, which were instead tied to pre-ordering the digital editions. Blizzard also pitched it as a way for players to mix and match the digital edition they wanted with the physical memorabilia, and the box didn't even ship until June 30, more than three weeks after the game launched.

EA's Mass Effect Legendary Cache, which dropped alongside the Legendary edition in May 2021, went a step further in the same direction: its centerpiece was a wearable 1:1 replica of Shepard's helmet, and it included a steel case for the game but no game to put in it, not even a download code.

Set against those precedents, the GTA VI box follows a familiar formula but at a very different scale: the Vice City Collection costs roughly four times as much as either the Red Dead Redemption 2 or Diablo IV box. It's also much pricier than the collector's editions Sony has released for its own recent blockbusters, which ran $230 to $250 and at least included a digital copy of the game. Go back one generation and the contrast is sharper still: Grand Theft Auto V's Collector's Edition launched at "just" $149.99 in 2013, and that one came with the game.

Then again, this is the most anticipated game in years, maybe in the whole history of gaming, and the collection's appeal lies in physical memorabilia rather than the game itself. With limited stock, Rockstar is presumably betting it will sell out anyway, and it's probably right.
