Mckenna Grace’s New Sci-Fi Movie On Track To Break 11-Year Box Office Record

TheTools · 18.11.2026 17:46 · source · permalink

A new day is dawning with The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.

The upcoming Sunrise on the Reaping, which is the sixth installment in the Hunger Games franchise, is based on the prequel novel of the same name by Suzanne Collins. It follows the 50th annual Hunger Games (24 years before Katniss stepped into the arena in the original 2012 Hunger Games movie), in which a young Haymitch Abernathy (Joseph Zada) fights for survival and lays the groundwork for a revolution. The movie's star-studded ensemble cast includes Mckenna Grace, Ralph Fiennes, Glenn Close, Jesse Plemons, Elle Fanning, Kieran Culkin, Ben Wang, Maya Hawke, Billy Porter, Edvin Ryding, Whitney Peak, and Iris Apatow.

Per TheWrap, early box office projections for The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, which debuts in theaters on November 20, see it expected to earn a 3-day total between $80 million and about $105 million at the domestic box office during its opening weekend. If it manages to hit the upper bound of that range, as exhibitors are expecting, it will exceed 2015's The Hunger Games: Mockingjay – Part 2's $102.7 million debut and become Lionsgate's first movie since that title to open with more than $100 million at the domestic box office.

Lionsgate has had major ups and downs over the past few years. While they have also distributed a number of critical or commercial disappointments, including Eli Roth's Borderlands and Francis Ford Coppola's Megalopolis, they also scored a major hit with the Sydney Sweeney thriller The Housemaid in 2025 and got close to surpassing $100 million with the debut weekend of the Michael Jackson biopic Michael, which slowly and steadily grew to become their highest-grossing movie ever, the highest-grossing biopic ever, and the fourth highest-grossing movie of the year so far with $1.029 billion worldwide.

Even at the lower bound of its projections, Sunrise on the Reaping will beat the previous installment in the franchise, which was the President Snow origin story The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes, by a wide margin. While the franchise has been experiencing diminishing returns since peaking with Catching Fire in 2013, Sunrise on the Reaping could reset it at its mid-2010s level. While it still remains to be seen if it will be able to break this record, there are some indicators that it could perform well.

This includes its Rotten Tomatoes score. While audience members have not been able to weigh in yet, critics' The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping reviews have earned it a solid score of 76%, which is significantly higher than Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes' 64% in addition to beating Mockingjay - Part 1 and Part 2, both of which earned scores of 70%.

Catching Fire is the best-reviewed Hunger Games movie with a Certified Fresh score of 90%.

If The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping follows the lead of Mockingjay - Part 2 and grosses more than $600 million, it will cement itself as a solid hit in a way that Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes fell short of doing. The movie reportedly cost roughly $200 million, which could place its estimated break-even point around $500 million, a total it seems set to pass and then some. However, Lionsgate is known for reducing costs with international pre-sales and other measures, so that benchmark is likely much lower.
