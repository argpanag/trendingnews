Jason Statham is gearing up for another explosive mission as Adam Clay in The Beekeeper 2. The first poster for the action sequel puts the returning star at the front and center. He's armed with heavy firepower as he prepares to take on a dangerous new threat.

Amazon MGM Studios has confirmed that the movie will arrive exclusively in theaters on January 15, 2027. Statham returns as the former assassin, but this time he fights the organization that trained him. Jeremy Irons, Emmy Raver-Lampman, and Bobby Naderi return, alongside newcomers Pom Klementieff, Yara Shahidi, and Adam Copeland. Jemma Redgrave also joins the cast as the President of the United States.

Timo Tjahjanto takes over directing duties from David Ayer, who helmed the 2024 original. Kurt Wimmer returns to write the story, with Zak Penn joining him on the screenplay. The sequel is rated R for strong bloody violence and language.

Photo Credit: Amazon MGM Studios

Jason Statham faces his most dangerous enemy yet in The Beekeeper 2

The new movie puts Adam Clay in a difficult position when the Beekeeper organization goes rogue and sets its sights on controlling the U.S. government. Pulled back into action, Clay must confront the very group that made him while facing a threat that reaches the country's highest office. Redgrave plays President Jessica Danforth, while Irons returns as Wallace Westwyld, who is also expected to have a significant role. The returning cast includes Raver-Lampman as Agent Verona Parker and Naderi as Agent Matt Wiley.

Klementieff, best known for playing Mantis in the Guardians of the Galaxy films, joins the new installment alongside Shahidi and former WWE star Copeland. The original The Beekeeper followed Clay, a retired operative living quietly as a beekeeper. After his landlady loses her savings to a phishing scam and dies by suicide, he sets out to punish those responsible. His search exposes his past as a member of a secret organization that targets corruption and restores order.

The sequel hits theaters on January 15, 2027.
