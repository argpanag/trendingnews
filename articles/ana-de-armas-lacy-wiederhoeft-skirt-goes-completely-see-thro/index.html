Ana de Armas’ Lacy Wiederhoeft Skirt Goes Completely See-Through at Gala

TheTools · 13.11.2026 10:28 · source · permalink

Home »

Ana de Armas made a striking appearance on the red carpet in effortless high fashion from Wiederhoeft. She attended “The Latin Recording Academy 2026 Person of the Year Gala” in Las Vegas, with boyfriend Beto Montenegro. The acclaimed actress turned heads in a dark, dramatic, and hard-to-miss sheer ensemble. It was also their first official red-carpet appearance as a couple.

Ana de Armas flashes her underwear in fully see-through Wiederhoeft lace skirt at gala

View this post on Instagram

For her appearance at the star-studded event, Ana de Armas selected a striking black gown with a lacy see-through skirt from Wiederhoeft‘s SS27 collection. Fashion tracking page Celeb Style Radar identified the piece and the runway image. The ensemble also featured a structured, off-the-shoulder corset top adorned with delicate lace trim along the neckline.

Her tightly fitted bodice highlighted her silhouette before seamlessly transitioning into a sheer, floor-length lace skirt. The transparent fabric fully revealed her matching black underwear beneath, adding a highly bold edge to the otherwise classic, gothic-inspired aesthetic. The gown also showcases the label’s trademark corsetry, softened by lace cap sleeves.

Styled by Samantha McMillen, the overall look was kept minimalist to let the intricate dress stand out. De Armas wore her dark hair straight and parted down the middle. This perfectly complemented her soft, glowing makeup. The intentional lack of heavy jewelry ensured that the craftsmanship of the sheer lace design was in focus.

Additionally, according to the brand’s official description, the dress is Look 21 of the collection. It features a cap-sleeved “Venus” corset crafted from structured black moiré, accented with delicate black Chantilly lace sleeves. Adding a distinct texture to the lacy see-through skirt worn by De Armas is an elaborate bead fringe, cut from black glass.

In addition to her fashion moment, the Hollywood star attended the Latin Grammy event in Las Vegas to support her boyfriend, Beto Montenegro. He complemented her sophisticated look by wearing an oversized black suit paired with a crisp white shirt and a dark tie.
