  18. Why has the casting process taken so long?published at 16:51 GMT
    16:51 GMT

    Steven McIntosh
    Entertainment reporter

    There are several reasons that it's taken five years since the release of Daniel Craig's final Bond film to find his replacement.

    Firstly, there have been a lot of changes behind the scenes - the previous Bond producers sold the franchise to Amazon MGM in early 2025, and it would've taken a while for a new creative team to settle in and decide on their strategy.

    The studio also prioritised appointing the director (Denis Villeneuve) and writer (Steven Knight) to set the tone of the next film, before turning to the question of which actor would be the best fit for their new era.

    Once those building blocks were in place, however, the casting process did not take long, and really ramped up in the last couple of months.

    Hollywood trade magazines have been packed with up-to-the-minute stories in recent weeks about when screen tests were taking place and which actors had dropped in and out of the race.

    The long gap since Craig announced in 2019 that he'd be leaving the role has meant that previous favourites such as Tom Hardy and Idris Elba aged out of the role. But once the search was under way, it didn't take long for producers to land on Pattinson.

Entertainment reporter

Daniel Craig announced in 2019 that No Time to Die - released in 2021 - would be his last

There are several reasons that it's taken five years since the release of Daniel Craig's final Bond film to find his replacement.

Firstly, there have been a lot of changes behind the scenes - the previous Bond producers sold the franchise to Amazon MGM in early 2025, and it would've taken a while for a new creative team to settle in and decide on their strategy.

The studio also prioritised appointing the director (Denis Villeneuve) and writer (Steven Knight) to set the tone of the next film, before turning to the question of which actor would be the best fit for their new era.

Once those building blocks were in place, however, the casting process did not take long, and really ramped up in the last couple of months.

Hollywood trade magazines have been packed with up-to-the-minute stories in recent weeks about when screen tests were taking place and which actors had dropped in and out of the race.

The long gap since Craig announced in 2019 that he'd be leaving the role has meant that previous favourites such as Tom Hardy and Idris Elba aged out of the role. But once the search was under way, it didn't take long for producers to land on Pattinson.
