Today presenter Andrea Crothers announces engagement to partner

TheTools · 14.09.2026 01:19 · source · permalink
  • Australia
  • Today Show
  • Aussie stars

September 14, 2026 - 1.19am

Today reporter Andrea Crothers has shared the wonderful news that she’s engaged to her partner, Amy Barton.

“YES! To my favourite person, in my favourite place 💍 🥂,” the Today star announced on Instagram.

Today reporter Andrea Crothers and partner Amy Barton are engaged! Instagram/@abcrothers

She also shared a snap of the newly engaged couple, while flashing her sparkling new “bling” to the camera.

Barton popped the question in Richmond, Tasmania, during an intimate picnic over the weekend.

Crothers opened up about the fairytale proposal during a live cross on Today on Monday morning.

“It went very well,” Crothers beamed.

“She surprised me when I was talking about the Richmond Bridge, which I said was a famous bridge but for no reason, it’s really boring.
