Notable injuries, news from Sunday's Week 2 games: Packers WR Jayden Reed (neck) carted off vs. Jets

TheTools · 21.09.2026 16:52 · source · permalink

Here are the injuries and news we're monitoring around the league on this Week 2 Sunday:

  • Arizona Cardinals cornerback Bo Melton (toe) was ruled out of Sunday's game against the Seahawks. 
  • Atlanta Falcons cornerback A.J. Terrell (groin) was ruled out of Sunday's loss to the Panthers. Defensive end Samson Ebukam (hamstring) also was ruled out.
  • Baltimore Ravens offensive tackle Ronnie Stanley (toe) did not return to Sunday's loss to the Saints.
  • Carolina Panthers safety Nick Scott (abdomen) was ruled out of Sunday's win over the Falcons.
  • Chicago Bears quarterback Caleb Williams was carted to the locker room after suffering a hamstring injury during the fourth quarter of Sunday's loss to Vikings.
  • Dallas Cowboys cornerback Cobie Durant (hamstring) and safety P.J. Locke (foot) did not to return to Sunday's game against the Commanders.
  • Green Bay Packers wide receiver Jayden Reed exited Sunday's win over the Jets on a stretcher after he was hit in the head area by a defender in the first quarter. He was quickly ruled out with a neck injury. Coach Matt LaFleur said after the game that Reed has movement in his extremities and will remain in the New Jersey area overnight. Offensive tackle Zach Bako-Bewele (knee) was carted off the field with an air cast placed on his injured leg. Bako-Bewele also was ruled out. Guard Aaron Banks (toe) was ruled out in the fourth quarter, and defensive tackle Anthony Campbell (ankle) did not return.
  • Indianapolis Colts wide receiver Alec Pierce was carted to the locker room during the first half versus the Chiefs and ruled out with a heel injury. X-rays were negative, but Pierce, who was in a boot and using crutches after the game, will undergo further testing on Monday, per ESPN. CB Charvarius Ward left Sunday night's loss with a groin injury. 
  • Los Angeles Chargers tight end David Njoku (knee) was ruled out of Sunday's loss to the Raiders.
  • Miami Dolphins running back Jaylen Wright (stinger) and defensive end Robert Beal Jr. (hamstring) did not return to Sunday's loss to San Francisco.
  • Minnesota Vikings defensive lineman Tyrion Ingram-Dawkins (hip) did not return to Sunday's win over the Bears.
  • New England Patriots offensive lineman Mike Onwenu (ankle) was ruled out in the first half of Sunday's win over the Steelers. ESPN.com reported after the game that initial X-rays showed Onwenu broke his right ankle. Tight end Eli Raridon (thigh) also was ruled out. Linebacker Dre'Mont Jones (shoulder) and safety Dell Pettus (ankle) did not return.
  • New York Jets defensive lineman David Onyemata (groin) was ruled out of Sunday's loss to the Packers. Linebackers Marcelino McCrary-Ball (ankle) and Kiko Mauigoa (quad) also did not return.
  • Philadelphia Eagles tight end Dallas Goedert (knee) did not return to Sunday's win over the Titans. Running back Saquon Barkley missed most of the first half after suffering a stinger. Barkley returned to the field after halftime. Barkley said after the game that he will undergo an MRI.
  • Pittsburgh Steelers running back Rico Dowdle (toe) missed part of the first half of Sunday's loss to the Patriots before returning after halftime.
  • San Francisco 49ers receiver Demarcus Robinson (ankle) was ruled out of Sunday's game against the Dolphins. Head coach Kyle Shanahan told reporters after the game Robinson and DT James Thompson Jr. have high ankle sprains. Wide receiver Mike Evans (hip) did not return to the game but the injury doesn't look serious, per Shanahan.
  • Seattle Seahawks running back Jadarian Price (chest) did not return to Sunday's game against the Cardinals. HC Mike Macdonald said after the game that X-rays came back negative on Price's injury.
  • Washington Commanders quarterback Jayden Daniels dislocated his left elbow in Sunday's loss to the Cowboys, head coach Dan Quinn said. Quinn told reporters that an X-ray on Daniels' elbow didn't show a fracture but the QB will undergo more testing. Daniels was ruled out after getting injured at the end of the first half versus Dallas. He was seen leaving the stadium with his arm in a sling, per local reporters.
  • Sunday's game between the Cleveland Browns and Tampa Bay Buccaneers was suspended in the fourth quarter due to lightning in the area. The delay lasted over two hours before play resumed at 6:10 p.m. ET.
