Um Sang-baek gives KT Wiz early lead with go-ahead RBI in Game 5

TheTools · 08.11.2026 06:01 · source · permalink

On the brink, first-place KT Wiz scored the first run in game 5, raising prospects of a trip to Suwon. The compensation player Um Sang-baek, who came from the Hanwha Eagles, recorded a go-ahead RBI for the second straight day.

Professional baseball club KT Wiz succeeded in scoring first in the top of the third inning in game 5 against the Samsung Lions in the 2026 Shinhan SOL KBO Korean Series (best-of-seven) held at Suwon KT Wiz Park on the 8th.

The lower part of the lineup made it happen. In the top of the third, leadoff hitters Han Seung-taek and Kwon Dong-jin faced Samsung starter Won Tae-in and hit consecutive singles to set up a no-out, runners on first and second situation. Choi Won-joon helped advance two runners with a sacrifice bunt, and Jang Jin-hyuk, who hit the opening solo homer in game 4, broke the 0-0 tie with a sacrifice fly to center field. However, the continued opportunity with two outs and a runner on second was erased when Kim Hyun-soo hit a ground ball to the pitcher.

KT is trailing in the series, one win and three losses. On the brink where one more loss would dash their championship hopes, Jang Jin-hyuk recorded the first RBI for the second straight day, raising prospects for a game 6 to take place.
