East Bengal will be set to represent India on the continental stage

A major continental tournament is ready to fly high after it has revamped its name from AFC Cup to AFC Champions League Two and become Asia’s second-tier football club competition, which carries a lot of legacy, and the competition is all set to begin its journey on 25th September 2026. With 32 clubs from across Asian nations competing in the second-tier tournament to reach the continental honour, this will be the 22nd edition of the competition.

As we all know, this is going to be the third season of ACL Two after Sharjah won the inaugural title in the 2024-25 season while Gamba Osaka clinched the title in the 2025-26 season, and now the stage is set for another champion who will witness the joy and be ready to write their name in the history books.

East Bengal FC will now return to action at the continental stage and are ready to represent India on the grandest stage, as they have already qualified for the AFC Champions League Two Group Stage. They have been drawn alongside Al Hussein SC, Al Shorta SC and Al Seeb Club in Group D.

Let’s take a wide look at the live streaming details of the AFC Champions League Two games in India as the Red & Gold Brigade are all set to battle their all out for the knockout qualification and set their eye on the title under the rightful guidance of Antonio Lopez Habas.

A look at the format of the AFC Champions League Two

With 32 teams are on the cards to feature in the group stage of the ACL Two 2026-27 season, the group fixtures are ready to start on 15th September 2026 and conclude on 9th December 2026, before the knockout rounds of the competition run from 9th February to 18th February 2027.

The quarter-finals and semi-finals are set for March 2-18 and April 6-14 respectively, with the final will be played on May 15, 2027.

Talking about the format shortly that contains two regional divisions at first, such as the East region and the West region and in both regions has split into four groups each, as it stands like East region has four groups with four teams in each group, and the West region also has the same manner in terms of division and separation.

All games will be played in a home and away round robin format, with each team playing against each other in the respective group twice, once at home and once away from home, as all the teams will fight for the top two finish in their respective groups to acquire the knockout berth and set an eye to avoid elimination.

When will the AFC Champions League Two 2026-27 season begin?

The AFC Champions League Two 2026-27 group stage will begin in September 2026, with the group-stage fixtures running until December 2026.

What is the format of the AFC Champions League Two?

The 32 teams are divided into eight groups of four, with four groups in each region. Every team plays six group-stage matches, facing each opponent once at home and once away.
