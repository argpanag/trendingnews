PNJ Play of the Month. Vote on the best plays from October

TheTools · 11.11.2026 18:34 · source · permalink

Vote for one of six plays to determine the first ever PNJ Play of the Month.

  • The Pensacola News Journal is launching a new "Play of the Month" series for local high school sports.
  • Fans in Escambia and Santa Rosa counties can vote for the best play from October.
  • Nominees include standout moments in football and volleyball from several area high schools.
  • Voting is open until noon CT on November 18.

It's time for something new, Escambia and Santa Rosa County sports fans.

The PNJ is taking part in a new Play of the Month series, which has debuted this year across the rest of USA TODAY Co's Florida Sports properties.

We were planning to run a September Play of the Month poll in October, but Hurricane Isaias got in the way. Now, Pensacola area high school sports fans can finally vote on which play they think was the best in the month of October.

Our reporters will continue to capture all the best moments they can from the games they cover, but we need your help too. Send the top plays for this month to sports reporter Justin Fitzgerald at JFitzgerald@gannett.com or upload clips using this link.

But now it's time for fans to decide the top Play of the Month from October. Voting is open unitl 12 p.m. CT on Nov. 18.

Pace football

The Patriots stopped Pine Forest on fourth-and-goal from the 1-yard line on Oct. 2

Javion Casanova, Pine Forest

Casanova made a sliding catch in the rain in overtime at Pace on Oct. 2.

Jaylin Brown, Pensacola Catholic

Brown eluded defenders, lost his balance and recovered while running for a touchdown against Freeport on Oct. 7.

Lexie Hardy, Tate

Hardy laid out for a pancake to keep a point alive against Pensacola Catholic on Oct. 6.

Zarion Harris, Pensacola

Harris returned the second half kickoff all the way to the West Florida 4-yard line at the Jaguars on Oct. 30.

Aaron Fieg, West Florida

Fieg snagged a deflected pass out of midair for an interception against Pensacola on Oct. 30.
