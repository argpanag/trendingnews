THE BEEKEEPER 2 Poster Released Ahead of Jason Statham Sequel in Theaters

TheTools · 18.11.2026 21:13 · source · permalink

Yara Shahidi and Jeremy Irons join the cast, with a screenplay by Kurt Wimmer and Zak Penn.

A new poster has been released for THE BEEKEEPER 2, the sequel starring Jason Statham as Adam Clay. The film opens only in theaters January 15, 2027.

Jason Statham is back as Adam Clay in The Beekeeper 2. Pulled back into the fight when the Beekeeper organization goes rogue and sets its sights on controlling the government, Clay must take on the very group that made him.

The film stars Jason Statham, Pom Klementieff, Jemma Redgrave, Emmy Raver-Lampman, Adam Copeland, Bobby Naderi, with Yara Shahidi, and Jeremy Irons. It is directed by Timo Tjahjanto, with a screenplay by Kurt Wimmer and Zak Penn. Story by Kurt Wimmer, based on characters created by Kurt Wimmer.

Amazon MGM Studios presents, a Miramax Presentation, a Punch Palace / Miramax / Longshot Production, a film by Timo Tjahjanto.
