Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie settle legal dispute over Château Miraval in the Var | Riviera Radio

TheTools · 26.11.2026 14:56 · source · permalink

Hollywood stars Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have reached an agreement ending their years-long legal battle over Château Miraval, the prestigious wine estate they acquired together in the Var.

Share

By Camille Bidard

The former couple announced the settlement on Wednesday, 25 November, bringing an end to one of the most contentious disputes following their separation in 2016.

Listen to this article

In a joint statement sent to American media outlets, the actors confirmed they had reached an amicable agreement in the interests of their family, without disclosing the terms of the settlement.

A dispute dating back several years

Pitt, 62, and Jolie, 51, entered into a partnership with a family of winemakers in 2011, developing Château Miraval into an internationally recognised rosé wine brand.

However, the estate became a source of conflict following their separation after 12 years together.

In February 2022, Pitt launched legal proceedings against Jolie, accusing her of violating his rights by selling her stake in the property to a subsidiary of the Stoli Group without his consent.

Jolie subsequently filed a countersuit, disputing his claims and accusing him of acting out of vengeance.

An end to years of legal proceedings

The dispute also brought private aspects of their relationship into the public eye.

Jolie alleged that negotiations over the sale of her shares had broken down after she refused to sign a confidentiality agreement concerning alleged abuse during their relationship.

The former couple, widely known as "Brangelina", began their relationship after meeting during the filming of Mr. & Mrs. Smith, released in 2005.

They married at Château Miraval in 2014 before separating two years later.

Neither actor has revealed what the settlement means for the future ownership of the wine estate.
