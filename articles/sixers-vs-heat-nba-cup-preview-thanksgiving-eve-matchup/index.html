Sixers vs. Heat NBA Cup Preview: Thanksgiving Eve Matchup

TheTools · 25.11.2026 22:14 · source · permalink

In a rare 5 p.m. EST start at Xfinity Mobile Arena on Wednesday, the Philadelphia 76ers (14-4) and Miami Heat (10-6) will tip off the NBA’s nationally televised NBA Cup tripleheader on Thanksgiving Eve. The Heat enter with an NBA-best seven-game winning streak.

Miami leads East Group B with a 2-0 Cup record, while Philadelphia is 1-1 after a win over New York and a loss to Cleveland. If the Sixers defeat the Heat and the Knicks beat the Cavaliers later Wednesday, Philadelphia will move into first place in the group heading into Friday’s group-stage finale in Indiana.

The Sixers had their five-game winning streak snapped Monday in a 128-111 loss to the Portland Trail Blazers while playing without Joel Embiid (illness). Portland tied a season high with 19 3-pointers, compared to nine by Philadelphia. LeBron James scored a season-high 23 points, shooting 8-for-13 from the field, 4-for-5 from 3-point range and 3-for-4 from the foul line.

The Heat acquired two-time NBA MVP Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bobby Portis from Milwaukee on July 6 in exchange for five draft picks and four players. Miami also signed five-time All-Star and four-time NBA champion Klay Thompson, Tim Hardaway Jr. and Nick Richards in free agency.

Miami’s seventh-consecutive victory came Sunday in a 123-113 win over the Oklahoma City Thunder behind a triple-double from Bam Adebayo while Antetokounmpo (right groin tendinitis) was sidelined. Adebayo finished with 24 points, 15 rebounds and 12 assists, tying LeBron James for the second-most triple-doubles in franchise history with nine.

By The Numbers

12: The Sixers have made an NBA-best 12 shots in the final second of a quarter this season, including LeBron James’ first-half buzzer-beater Monday against Portland.

20: James has scored at least 20 points in each of the past three games, averaging 22.0 points while shooting 58.1% from the field.

21: Sixers rookie Labaron Philon Jr. celebrated his 21st birthday Tuesday. Philon scored a season-high 10 points, shooting 4-for-5 from the field and 2-for-3 from 3-point range, in last Wednesday’s comeback victory at Orlando.
