MLS play-off berth awaits Mbokazi and Puso

TheTools · 14.10.2026 12:01 · source · permalink

Oct 14, 2026 - 12:01 pm By Lukhanyo Mtuta

Players Abroad

South African duo Mbekezeli Mbokazi and Puso Dithejane could secure their first-ever place in the Major League Soccer (MLS) Cup play-offs when their club, Chicago Fire, takes on Philadelphia Union on Thursday morning.

Chicago Fire are on a three-game winning streak since Mbokazi returned from an injury that kept him out for three months.

The three victories, which came against Charlotte FC, Vancouver Whitecaps and New York City FC, have seen Chicago Fire climb to third place in the MLS Eastern Conference.

This winning run means Chicago Fire are now mathematically just one victory away from securing their place in the MLS play-offs.

Chicago Fire will face Philadelphia Union in the early hours of Thursday morning, and a victory would take Mbokazi and Dithejane's side to 51 points, putting them out of reach of the chasing play-off hopefuls.

Notably, Chicago Fire face a daunting opponent, as Philadelphia Union are currently on a 13-game unbeaten run.

If Chicago Fire lose against the in-form Philadelphia Union, they will still have five matches remaining to secure their place in the MLS play-offs, which get underway in late November.
