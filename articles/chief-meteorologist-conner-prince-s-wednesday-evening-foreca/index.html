Chief Meteorologist Conner Prince’s Wednesday Evening Forecast: October 28, 2026

TheTools · 28.10.2026 21:38 · source · permalink

Lima, OH (WLIO) - West-central and northwestern Ohio has been very blessed with phenomenal weather for the second-half of October! Dry weather, sunshine, and warm temperatures continued for our Wednesday. That theme will persist as we finish the rest of the final work week of October.

For tonight, it will be a little milder than average with temperatures dropping around 50 degrees. Skies will be largely clear with a light south wind.

Thursday will feature near record-warmth high temperatures, along with lots of sunshine! Forecast high temperatures are 77 degrees; the record high is 79 degrees set back in 1946. One notable difference will be the breeze: winds will be out of the southwest at 10 to 20 mph, potentially gusting up to 25 mph.

For Thursday evening, a weakening cold front will move through the region. This front will be very weak with little upper-level dynamics and moisture to work with. Other than an increase in clouds, a stray to isolated shower, and a wind shift from the SW to the NW, there will be no impacts.

After the cold front passes Thursday evening, clearing skies will take place with seasonably cool air spilling in from the northwest. Low temperatures should drop into the middle 40s for Friday morning with a NW wind at about 5 mph.

The day Friday looks great with lots of sunshine, mild with highs in the middle 60s, and a northeast wind at about 5 mph.

Changes take place heading into the weekend. On Halloween, clouds rapidly increase and thicken. Despite the increased cloud cover, it is very unlikely any rain will occur Halloween Day and Evening, so I do not expect any issues for those who have festivities planned. It will be mild with highs in the middle 60s.

Starting November 1st and continuing into next week, a more active weather pattern will begin to take shape. This will cause rain chances to increase Sunday and through Wednesday next week with warm temperatures in the upper 60s to near 70. There remains quite a bit of uncertainty with the timing, location, and amount of any rain. Therefore, the forecast will need to be updated to take account for the latest trends.
