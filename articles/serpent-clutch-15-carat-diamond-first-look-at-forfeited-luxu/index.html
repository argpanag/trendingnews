Serpent clutch, 15-carat diamond: First look at forfeited luxury goods from S$3b money laundering case

TheTools · 14.09.2026 16:57 · source · permalink

Serpent clutch, 15-carat diamond: First look at forfeited luxury goods from S$3b money laundering case More than 600 forfeited luxury goods are going under the hammer, including fine jewellery, designer handbags and accessories linked to Singapore’s largest money laundering case. Among the eye-catching pieces is a pink enamel clutch shaped like a serpent’s head, part of the haul now open for online bidding. A 15.02-carat yellow diamond ring is estimated at S$200,000 to S$300,000. As of Thursday evening, its highest bid stood at S$190,000. A limited-edition Louis Vuitton Yayoi Kusama leather pumpkin bag has already drawn a S$39,000 bid – well above its S$12,000 to S$16,000 estimate. This rare Hermes Kelly bracelet, featuring 30 carats across 263 diamonds, is valued at S$150,000 to S$200,000. Its highest bid stood at S$80,000 on Thursday. Interest has been strong, with nearly all of the roughly 75 daily viewing slots from Monday to Saturday booked. The items are kept at Le Freeport, a high-security storage facility in Changi. The goods will be sold across 15 online auctions running until May 2027. The first two , featuring more than 600 items, have a combined pre-sale estimate of S$2.9 million to S$3.9 million.
