Whether you want jump scares, psychological thrills or something the whole family can handle, October's lineup has you covered.

Spooky season is finally here — but you can only rewatch Hocus Pocus, Scream and Practical Magic so many times. Luckily, the Halloween classics have some serious competition this year.

October is stacked with spooky new TV shows and movies, including fresh takes on old favorites Carrie and Friday the 13th, while Jessica Chastain's supernatural horror Other Mommy hits theaters Friday.

How scary you want to go is up to you. Some of these picks are firmly in horror territory, while others lean more into suspense or simply give you the creeps. There are also lighter options for families, so younger viewers can get in on the Halloween fun.

Here are 12 new movies and shows worth adding to your watchlist.
