How to watch Manchester City vs Norwich City in the Carabao Cup:

  1. Kick-off time and location: The Carabao Cup third round match between Manchester City and Norwich City takes place on Thursday, September 17, with kick-off scheduled for 7.30pm BST. The tie will be held at Man City’s home ground, the Etihad Stadium.
  2. UK TV channel and coverage details: Television viewers in the United Kingdom can watch the match broadcast live on Sky Sports+. The official channel coverage is scheduled to start at 7pm BST, giving supporters half an hour of build-up ahead of kick-off.
  3. Live stream information: Supporters wishing to watch the action via digital platforms can stream the game on the Sky Go app or NOW with an active subscription. You can access these streams on mobile devices, tablets, desktop web browsers, or smart TVs by signing into your account and selecting the Sky Sports+ stream. You can also use the Sky Sports app.
  4. How to watch in the US: American audiences can watch live coverage of the fixture online via Paramount+. Broadcast rights for the Carabao Cup in the United States belong to CBS Sports, with the stream starting live at 2.30pm ET.
  5. UK radio coverage: Live audio commentary of the cup clash will be broadcast across the UK on both BBC Radio 5 Live and talkSPORT. Football fans can listen in on national radio frequencies or stream the commentary digitally through the BBC Sounds app and the talkSPORT app.
