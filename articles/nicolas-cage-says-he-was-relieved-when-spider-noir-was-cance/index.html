Nicolas Cage says he was relieved when Spider-Noir was canceled | Межа

TheTools · 06.11.2026 22:29 · source · permalink

Photo source: IGN

Cage said he was happy with a single season and did not want to keep repeating the character.

In an early-November interview with IGN, Cage said he had no interest in continuing the story of Ben Reilly, the character he plays in Spider-Noir. The series, available on Prime Video in black and white and color, received strong reviews when it debuted earlier this year. The streamer canceled it in September after it fell out of its top 10 shows.

This is reported by IGN.

Cage had previously told Deadline that one season was enough for him.

For me, one season is perfect, having said what I wanted to say with this dangerous, innovative, pop-art Spider-Man. Onwards, upwards, inwards.

– Nicolas Cage

Asked by IGN’s Jeffrey Vega whether Ben Reilly’s story had more to tell, Cage replied:

No. I meant it when I said what I said. It’s a perfect season. You never wanna get stuck repeating a character and wear out your welcome. Ben Riley is so idiosyncratic. I was doing something that was pretty high-risk, tightrope stuff – if you do that for season 2, season 3, you could really wear out your welcome.

– Nicolas Cage

Cage also said he is unlikely to work with Amazon again because of the company’s position on AI. He said he refused to sign a waiver allowing AI to be used on Spider-Noir. In a statement, Cage said, “Amazon is a very AI-friendly company, to the tune of $50 billion invested in OpenAI. I’m not an AI-friendly actor,” and added, “I’m not part of their club. I didn’t sign the waiver to use AI on Spider-Noir. I’ll probably not work with that studio again, but that’s OK.”

At the 2025 Saturn Awards, Cage said he opposed AI being used to shape actors’ performances, arguing that it could replace artistic integrity, purity and truth with financial interests.

Cage plays John Madden in the sports comedy Madden, scheduled for release on November 18. His performance has already prompted viral debate among fans.
