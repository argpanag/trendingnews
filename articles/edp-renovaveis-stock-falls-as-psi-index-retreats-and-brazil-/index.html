EDP Renovaveis stock falls as PSI index retreats and Brazil deal reshapes portfolio

TheTools · 07.09.2026 15:29 · source · permalink

EDP Renovaveis stock slipped on the Lisbon exchange in early September 2026 as investors digested a weaker PSI session and the roughly EUR 700 million sale of its Brazilian renewables unit, while the wider utilities and energy sector remained in focus.

EDP Renovaveis stock (ISIN ES0144580Y14) closed at 13.34 euros on Euronext Lisbon on September 2, 2026, down 3.12% for the session and standing below recent levels around 13.82 euros seen at the start of the month according to market data compiled by Investing.com and local Portuguese media.

Price moves and PSI index context

According to a Portugal stock market overview from Investing.com, the PSI benchmark index declined 0.77% at the close of trading on September 2, 2026, with EDP Renovaveis among the weakest constituents as its share price fell 0.43 euros to 13.34 euros, a loss of 3.12% on the day.

A detailed recap by Portuguese outlet Jornal Economico likewise noted that EDP Renovaveis dropped 3.12% to 13.34 euros, while peers Galp Energia and EDP also retreated, dragging the PSI to 9,405.31 points.

Recent strength and sector attention

In the preceding session, EDP Renovaveis stock traded around 13.82 euros on Euronext Lisbon as of September 1, 2026, with the share price up about 1.17% on the day according to a Lisbon market recap by ad-hoc-news.de, underscoring the volatility as the stock swung from a modest gain to a clear decline within one trading day.

Additional market commentary from Aman Alliance reported that EDP Renovaveis shares rose 0.81% to 13.77 euros in an earlier session, while a separate note showed the stock up 1.46% to 13.86 euros alongside gains in parent company EDP and retailer Sonae, illustrating steady investor interest in Portugal’s energy names.

More on EDP Renovaveis stock and fundamentals

For investors who want a broader view of EDP Renovaveis stock, the ad-hoc-news.de topic page and the company’s investor relations site offer detailed information on recent financial results, strategy and guidance.

Brazil portfolio deal and strategic positioning

Beyond short term price moves, EDP Renovaveis remains in the spotlight because of changes to its portfolio structure in Brazil and continued attention from international investors to the renewables sector.

According to ECO News, Portugal recorded 327 mergers and acquisitions worth 5.4 billion euros in the first eight months of 2026, and among the largest deals was EDP Brasil’s purchase of 100% of EDP Renovaveis Brasil from EDP Renewables, valued at roughly 700 million euros.

This Brazilian transaction integrates power generation, trading and electricity supply operations in that market under EDP Brasil, while EDP Renovaveis as a group continues to focus on global wind and solar projects; for shareholders, the deal highlights ongoing portfolio optimization and capital allocation decisions in high growth regions.

Sector interest is also supported by inclusion of EDP Renovaveis S.A. among key energy names watched by institutional investors, with a recent global energy best ideas overview from a Canadian outlet listing EDP Renovaveis alongside Northland Power and other major energy companies as part of an extended stocks universe.

Representative asset base and projects

A representative example of EDP Renovaveis’ project portfolio is its onshore wind and solar assets across Europe, North America and Latin America, which together underpin the company’s power generation capacity and cash flow profile.

The group develops, owns and operates renewable energy projects that feed electricity into national grids under a mix of long term power purchase agreements and merchant exposure, positioning EDP Renovaveis to benefit from ongoing decarbonization policies and rising demand for clean energy.

Stock level and investor perspective

As of the close on September 2, 2026, EDP Renovaveis stock traded at 13.34 euros on Euronext Lisbon, compared with 13.82 euros a day earlier and intraday levels around 13.77 to 13.86 euros reported in recent market recaps, leaving the shares modestly below their latest short term peaks while remaining tied to sentiment on the PSI index and the broader European utilities sector.
