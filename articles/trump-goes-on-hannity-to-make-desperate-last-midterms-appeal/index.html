Heavy rain and severe flash flooding across Florida forced election officials to relocate multiple polling stations on Monday, NBC News reported.

'If we lose Texas, we lose America': Ken Paxton makes final pitch in tight Senate race

Trump says U.S. 'might go after Pickaxe Mountain' in Iran

Donald Trump told Fox's Sean Hannity that the U.S. may need to resume strikes on Iran to target Pickaxe Mountain, a fortified facility buried deep underground near one of Tehran's main nuclear sites.

He claimed the U.S. had already "bombed out all of their potential nuclear power" with Operation Midnight Hammer in June 2025.

"Now we might have to go after Pickaxe [Mountain], but that's not a big deal. We can do it in literally... one hour, soon as we want, at any time we want," he said.

He also played down the significance of the ongoing conflict, saying: "This isn't war... When was the last time we fired anything?"

Trump's remarks came shortly before US officials confirmed new defensive strikes targeting Iranian positions in the Strait of Hormuz.
