WEDNESDAY: We'll have mostly cloudy skies with afternoon highs in the mid to upper 80s and southwest wind at 5-10 mph. A few stray showers are possible, especially along and north of I-70.

WEDNESDAY NIGHT: There is a chance of showers, with low temperatures near 70. The best chance of rain is in areas west of HWY 63 and north of I-70.

EXTENDED: A cold front slowly moves through from Thursday into Friday and will cause rain chances to persist through early Friday and bring high temperatures down into the lower 70s.The weekend looks mostly dry and seasonal with afternoon temperatures reaching the low 70's and cool nights in the upper 40s and lower 50s.
