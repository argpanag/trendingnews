India’s Medals at Asian Games 2026 Medal Tally

India has made a strong start at the Asian Games 2026 in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan. As of 21 September 2026, India has won six medals – four silver and two bronze. Shooting has contributed five medals, while Suchika Tariyal has added a historic bronze in mixed martial arts.

  • Total medals: 6
  • Gold: 0
  • Silver: 4
  • Bronze: 2
  • Shooting: 5 medals
  • Mixed Martial Arts: 1 medal
  • India also has a medal assured in women’s cricket, with the final against Sri Lanka scheduled for 22 September.
  • The medal tally is ongoing and will change as more events are completed.

India’s Gold, Silver and Bronze Medal Winners at Asian Games 2026

India’s medals so far have come mainly from shooting, along with a historic bronze in MMA. The current medal winners are:

  • Elavenil Valarivan, Sonam Maskar and Vidarsa K Vinod won silver in the women’s 10m air rifle team event.
  • Elavenil Valarivan added another silver in the women’s 10m air rifle individual event.
  • Rudrankksh Patil, Himanshu Dhillon and Parth Rakesh Mane won silver in the men’s 10m air rifle team event.
  • Himanshu Dhillon won silver in the men’s 10m air rifle individual event with a score of 252.4.
  • Rudrankksh Patil secured bronze in the men’s 10m air rifle individual event with 230.9.
  • Suchika Tariyal won bronze in the women’s traditional 60kg MMA event. Her medal is particularly significant as it is India’s first Asian Games medal in MMA.
  • Gold medals: India has not won a gold medal so far as of 21 September.

India Medal Winners at Asian Games 2026 Sport-Wise

India’s current medal tally is concentrated in shooting and mixed martial arts, while athletes from several other sports are still competing.

  • Shooting – 5 medals: Indian shooters have won four silver medals and one bronze across the women’s and men’s 10m air rifle events. The men’s team of Dhillon, Patil and Mane also secured silver.
  • Mixed Martial Arts – 1 bronze: Suchika Tariyal secured bronze in the women’s traditional 60kg category, giving India its first MMA medal at the Asian Games.
  • Women’s cricket: India has already secured a medal by reaching the final against Sri Lanka. The colour of the medal will be decided in the final on 22 September 2026.
  • Hockey, kabaddi, badminton and table tennis: Indian teams have also recorded notable results on Day 2. The women’s hockey team defeated Indonesia 17-1, while the men’s kabaddi team defeated Japan 49-23. India’s women’s table tennis team also defeated Pakistan 3-0 to reach the quarterfinals.

India’s Performance and Medal Highlights at Asian Games 2026

India’s early performance at the Asian Games 2026 has been marked by strong results in shooting and a historic achievement in MMA.

  • Shooting has been the biggest contributor to India’s medal tally so far, accounting for five of the six medals.
  • The men’s 10m air rifle event produced three medals for India, with a team silver followed by individual silver and bronze medals for Himanshu Dhillon and Rudrankksh Patil.
  • Himanshu Dhillon made a notable Asian Games debut by winning individual silver in the men’s 10m air rifle. He finished behind China’s Sheng Lihao, who won gold with 254.2 points.
  • Suchika Tariyal’s bronze marked a new milestone for Indian combat sports because MMA is part of the Asian Games programme for the first time at Aichi-Nagoya 2026.
  • India has also produced strong performances outside the medal events, including victories in women’s hockey, men’s kabaddi, badminton and women’s table tennis.
  • The Indian women’s cricket team has already secured a medal and will compete for gold against Sri Lanka, adding another major medal opportunity to India’s campaign.
  • Since the Asian Games 2026 are still underway, India’s medal tally is provisional and will continue to change as more finals are completed.
