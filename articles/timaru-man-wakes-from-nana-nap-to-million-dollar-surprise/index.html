Timaru man wakes from ‘nana nap’ to million-dollar surprise

TheTools · 10.11.2026 22:17 · source · permalink
PHOTO

A quiet Sunday turned into an unforgettable day for a Timaru man who woke from a “nana nap” to find he’d won $1 million in Lotto.

The man said he was a regular player who uses his own numbers – a mix of birthdays and special occasions.

He always records the Lotto draw, and he watched it after his regular “nana nap” last Sunday, as he always does.

“I know my numbers off by heart, I’ve played the same ones for years.

“So once three numbers came out of the machine, I thought, ‘I’ve won a bonus ticket at least’. Then the fourth one came out and I thought, ‘what?!’ Then came the fifth number and I thought, ‘nah, I can’t watch this anymore.’ And when the sixth number came out, well, I’m surprised they couldn’t hear me all the way up in Auckland!” 

He said he celebrated with a beer, then shared the news with a family member.

“She thought I was having her on,” he said. “So I sent her a picture of the ticket.”

Still not convinced, she looked online, saw that someone in Timaru had won, and checked his numbers, to make sure.

“She was really chuffed for me when she realised I was the winner and it wasn’t a big joke.”

The man claimed his win in-store and said he finally felt a bit more relaxed after handing over his ticket to be checked.

“I was shaking for about 40 hours straight after finding out, and it’s only just stopped,” he said.

He said he was looking forward to the future, and planned to buy a campervan so he could travel the South Island.

“I’ve already been online looking for one. I can’t wait to hit the road and get away for the weekend whenever I feel like it. There’s a few special places I’d like to visit,” he said.

Apart from the campervan, the winner plans to take things slow and get some good advice on how best to handle his windfall.
