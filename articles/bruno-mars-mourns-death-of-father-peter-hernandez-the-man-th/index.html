Bruno Mars Mourns Death of Father Peter Hernandez: ‘The Man That Taught Me Everything I Know’

TheTools · 20.11.2026 01:18 · source · permalink

"In my eyes, he was the greatest dad in the world," the Grammy-winning artist wrote.

Trending on Billboard

Bruno Mars has announced that his father, Peter Hernandez, passed away. The Grammy-winning superstar broke the tragic news with a heartbreaking tribute post to Instagram on Thursday (Nov. 19), mourning the loss of his dad.

“Last week I got to hug my father one last time,” Mars began. “If you’ve ever seen me talk about him I’ve always said that he’s the man that taught me everything I know, especially when it comes to music. He raised my four beautiful sisters, my brother, and me.

The “I Just Might” singer continued: “In my eyes he was the greatest dad in the world, and I will miss him everyday until we meet again. I Love you Pops. Kiss mom up there for me.”

The 41-year-old included a hospital photo with his late father, while also posting several throwback pictures of his musically-inclined dad. Details of Hernandez’s death remain scarce. He was 74 years old.

Mars lost his mother, Bernadette San Pedro Bayot, back in 2013 to a brain aneurysm. Bayot and Hernandez got divorced when the singer was about 12 years old in the mid-to-late ’90s, which led to a split of the family’s The Love Notes band.

“My whole sense of rhythm is because my dad was teaching me bongos as a kid. He’s an old-school working musician, so that’s where the pinky rings come from, the patent-leather shoes, the suits, and the pompadour. It all stems from watching my father,” Mars said in a 2017 interview with Latina magazine, per Remezcla.

Amid the tragedy, Bruno Mars is slated to get back on the road and resume The Romantic Tour with five shows in Mexico City starting on Dec. 3 at Estadio GNP Seguros.

Mars released his The Romantic album in February, which topped the Billboard 200 with 186,000 total album-equivalent units.
