Grand Theft Auto VI Limited Edition DualSense Wireless

TheTools · 03.09.2026 16:15 · source · permalink

Alongside a bevy of video game reveals from PlayStation today, the State of Play also gave us a brief look at two new DualSense controller designs themed around the highly anticipated Grand Theft Auto VI. 

With the big game set to release on November 19, the new PlayStation designs should offer players a way to further celebrate their enthusiasm for Grand Theft Auto VI. The new controllers are understated but attractive – one in black and the other in white – each with an iridescent purple/blue backing in the middle that recalls the Grand Theft Auto VI logo. There’s also a prominent “VI” logo across the touchpad. 

No word yet on specific release date, but Sony usually aims to have these limited edition releases available by the time a game launches – if you can manage to snag one before they sell out.
