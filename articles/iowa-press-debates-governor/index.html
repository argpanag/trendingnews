Iowa Press Debates: Governor

TheTools · 11.09.2026 01:05 · source · permalink

Iowa PBS will host a debate for Iowa’s gubernatorial race, bringing together Zach Lahn (R-Belle Plaine) and Rob Sand (D-Des Moines) to answer questions from Iowa reporters and discuss their priorities and vision for the state during a 90-minute debate. Iowa Press Debates: Governor will air live Wednesday, October 21 at 7 p.m.
