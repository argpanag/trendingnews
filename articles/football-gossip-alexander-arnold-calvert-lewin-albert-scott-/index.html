Football gossip: Alexander-Arnold, Calvert-Lewin, Albert, Scott, Tresoldi, Batrakov

1 of 5

Trent Alexander-Arnold may become a target for Chelsea, Bournemouth's Alex Scott could also be heading to Stamford Bridge and Manchester United are looking at Leeds United striker Dominic Calvert-Lewin.

Chelsea have been linked with a move for England right-back Trent Alexander-Arnold if the 28-year-old Real Madrid defender becomes available. (Football Insider), external

Manchester United are considering a January move for Leeds United striker Dominic Calvert-Lewin, 29. (Athletic - subscription), external

Manchester City are favourites to sign 17-year-old United States winger Mathis Albert from Borussia Dortmund, with Chelsea also interested. (Teamtalk), external

Chelsea could have a deal in the region of £90m agreed with Bournemouth for England midfielder Alex Scott, 23, before January. (Mail - subscription required), external

Arsenal, Manchester United, Tottenham and Chelsea are keeping a close eye on German youth international Nicolo Tresoldi, but Club Brugge have no intention of selling their 22-year-old striker in January. (Football Insider), external

Chelsea are monitoring 21-year-old Como defender Jacobo Ramon, with Arsenal and Manchester United also being linked with the Spaniard. (Mail - subscription required), external

Paris St-Germain or Barcelona may edge out Liverpool and Arsenal in the pursuit of Russia's Galatasaray midfielder Aleksey Batrakov, 21, who is open to a move to one of Europe's elite clubs. (Teamtalk), external

Tottenham will not receive additional funds from Bayern Munich as Harry Kane's transfer to the Bundesliga giants in 2023 did not include a Ballon d'Or clause for the England forward, 33. (Goal), external

Barcelona president Joan Laporta is keen on bringing Spain and Tottenham full-back Pedro Porro, 27, to the club. (3Cat, in Catalan), external
