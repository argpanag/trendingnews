Queen Elizabeth died four years ago on Sept. 8, 2022

Long before Queen Elizabeth became Britain's longest-reigning monarch, she was a young princess whose future changed dramatically with one royal abdication.

Born in 1926, Elizabeth was not initially expected to inherit the throne. That changed in 1936, when her uncle, King Edward VIII, abdicated and her father became King George VI.

The years that followed saw Elizabeth serve during World War II, marry Prince Philip and welcome their first two children, King Charles and Princess Anne. She became Queen in 1952 following her father's early death at age 56.

During her 70-year reign, Elizabeth welcomed two more children, the former Prince Andrew and Prince Edward, and witnessed decades of change within the monarchy and around the world. She died four years ago on Sept. 8, 2022, at age 96, bringing Britain's longest royal reign to a close.

From official portraits to candid childhood moments, here's a look back at Queen Elizabeth's life before she wore the crown.

01 of 10

1926

Princess Elizabeth with her parents, the Duke and Duchess of York.
Credit: Central Press/Getty

Queen Elizabeth's parents, the Duke and Duchess of York, had her christened in the church in 1926. At the time, she was third in line to the throne behind her uncle, the future King Edward VIII, and her father. Edward abdicated after less than a year as king to marry American Wallis Simpson and later became the Duke of Windsor.

02 of 10

1927

A portrait of Queen Elizabeth II in 1927.
Credit: Picture Post/Hulton Archive/Getty

Her then-name — Princess Elizabeth Alexandra Mary — honored several women in her family. The late Queen shared Elizabeth with her mother, the Duchess of York, who later became the Queen Mother; Alexandra paid tribute to her great-grandmother Queen Alexandra; and Mary honored her paternal grandmother, Queen Mary.

03 of 10

1933

Princess Margaret and Princess Elizabeth in 1933.
Credit: AFP/Getty

In 1930, Queen Elizabeth was joined by her sister, Princess Margaret. Four years apart in age, Margaret, who died in 2002, was the Queen's only sibling.

04 of 10

1936

Princess Elizabeth in 1936.
Credit: Lisa Sheridan/Studio Lisa/Hulton Archive/Getty

At home at 10 years old, Queen Elizabeth poses with two of her beloved corgis. Decades later, the Queen's love of her pet corgis remained: she cared for more than 30 throughout her lifetime.

05 of 10

1944

Princess Elizabeth and Princess Margaret in 1944.
Credit: Lisa Sheridan/Studio Lisa/Getty

Wearing tiaras, Queen Elizabeth and Princess Margaret performed in a royal production of Old Mother Red Riding Boots at Windsor Castle during World War II.

06 of 10

1945

Princess Elizabeth in 1945.
Credit: Popperfoto/Getty

Being a princess — and future Queen — didn't stop the late monarch from rolling up her sleeves. After the royal family chose to remain in England during World War II — despite suggestions that she and Margaret be sent to Canada for their safety — Queen Elizabeth joined the Auxiliary Territorial Service. Here, she is pictured training as a mechanic in 1945.

07 of 10

1947

Princess Elizabeth in 1947.
Credit: Paul Popper/Popperfoto/Getty

In this photo, Queen Elizabeth is seen playing deck games with the crew of the HMS Vanguard while on a family trip to South Africa in 1947.

During the trip, the late Queen celebrated her 21st birthday and addressed the citizens of the Commonwealth in what is now remembered as one of her iconic speeches. In it, she spoke of her dedication to her role: "I declare before you all that my whole life whether it be long or short shall be devoted to your service and the service of our great imperial family to which we all belong."

08 of 10

1947

Princess Elizabeth with her then-fiancé Philip Mountbatten in 1947.
Credit: Topical Press Agency/Hulton Archive/Getty

Queen Elizabeth and Prince Philip — then Philip Mountbatten — announced their engagement in July 1947. The pair fell for each other early on — after a meeting when the late monarch was just 13 years old.

09 of 10

1947

Princess Elizabeth and Prince Philip on their wedding day in 1947.
Credit: Hulton Archive/Getty

Queen Elizabeth and Prince Philip wed at Westminster Abbey in London on Nov. 20, 1947. With postwar rationing still in effect, even the future Queen had to use clothing coupons to purchase the material for her satin wedding gown.

10 of 10

1951

Princess Elizabeth and Prince Philip with their children, Princess Anne and Prince Charles, in 1951.
Credit: Getty

In an intimate 1951 family shot, Queen Elizabeth holds her daughter, Princess Anne, as she stands next to her husband, who holds the future King Charles.

Less than a year later, her father would die, and she would accede to the throne.
