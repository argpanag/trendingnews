Netherlands coach Xavi criticised over call-up of Dortmund star Veerman

Xavi, now in charge of the Netherlands, has drawn a mix of criticism and praise for calling up Borussia Dortmund's Joey Veerman.

Germany's new head coach Jürgen Klopp also makes his first appearance on the touchline tonight. The sides meet in Amsterdam at 20:45 CET.

"Xavi, who was sitting in Feyenoord's stadium, probably realized that he made a big mistake by not nominating the Feyenoord captain. I consider him an excellent player. I think he has much more influence on a team than Joey Veerman." He was speaking to AS.

"Xavi loves football, and that's why Veerman is being called up again. I even think he could become a regular starter. He's a player who fits perfectly into the team, and that's exactly what the Dutch national team desperately needs."

The discussion reflects contrasting views on Xavi's early selections, alongside suggestions the Netherlands may need a broader rebuild.
