How Lionel Messi aims to ruin Mexican Independence Day

TheTools · 15.09.2026 16:32 · source · permalink

Inter Miami and Cruz Azul meet for the Campeones Cup, with Messi looking to add to his scoring record against Liga MX clubs.

Lionel Messi is looking to spoil Mexico’s Independence Day celebrations.

On Wednesday, September 16, Inter Miami and Cruz Azul face off with the Campeones Cup on the line. The Argentine superstar already has plenty of experience punishing Mexican opposition – including La Máquina.

Could Cruz Azul fall victim to Messi once again?

During his time at Barcelona, Messi came up against Liga MX clubs twice. Surprisingly, the first Mexican team he ever scored against was Atlante. The two teams met at the 2009 Club World Cup, with the Argentine coming off the bench and scoring in the 53rd minute of a 3-1 victory.

León became his second Mexican victim when it was invited to play Barcelona in the Joan Gamper Trophy. Barça ran riot that day, winning 6-0.

Since moving to Florida, the nature of the competitions Inter Miami plays in has given Messi far more opportunities to face Mexican clubs. In fact, his first opponent was Cruz Azul in the 2023 Leagues Cup.

The Herons won 2-1 thanks to a spectacular stoppage-time free kick from the former PSG star. Messi needed only 36 minutes on the field to decide the game.

His next encounter with Liga MX opposition came against Club América. Messi scored against Las Águilas in a friendly in January 2025, although América managed to earn a draw.

San Luis, Messi’s favorite victim

The most recent Mexican team to cross Messi’s path was Atlético de San Luis. A few weeks ago, Messi scored twice in the Leagues Cup league phase to end San Luis’ hopes of staying alive in the competition. Unfortunately for the Argentine, Inter Miami was also unable to keep its own run going in the following round.
