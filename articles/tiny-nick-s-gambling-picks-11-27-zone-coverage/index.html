Tiny Nick's Gambling Picks 11/27 - Zone Coverage

TheTools · 27.11.2026 15:46 · source · permalink

NFL (0.75 Unit) Jonah Coleman Over 99.5 Rushing+Receiving Yards (-110; Odds via Fanduel): 2:00 PM CT on Prime Video

I will decline to pick a side in this game, although the old saying of buy on bad news might apply to Pittsburgh here catching a sizable number at home. Or, they might be a dead team walking, in which case Jonah Coleman can cut right through them. This is a pretty rare situation, with Coleman being the unquestioned lead back in Denver, and even more so now that JK Dobbins was suspended. And he’s getting a ton of work, which is surprising on the whole since Sean Payton’s offenses are notorious for suddenly phasing players in or out. Pittsburgh hasn’t really faced a healthy, workhorse back on a functional offense in weeks, so I think Coleman is undervalued and will deliver if given a full workload.

Bonus Bet (0.75 Unit) Mason Rudolph Over 6.5 Rushing Yards (-110; Odds via Fanduel)

Containing quarterbacks on the ground has been a big problem for the Broncos this season. They’ve given up 201 total rushing yards to the position on 4.7 yards per carry, with 20.1 yards per game on average. Only two opposing QB’s have failed to clear this number, and both of them landed right on 6 yards, while Denver hasn’t faced a single team with what you’d consider a “running” quarterback. It’s a symptom of the Broncos pash rush being fierce, but not doing a good job of getting home, which opens running lanes for a QB to escape, and I think Rudolph does just that in his first start of the year.

 

NCAA Football (0.75 Unit) Daniel Bray Over 67.5 Rushing Yards (-110; Odds via Fanduel): 8:00 PM CT on FOX

It’s somehow still a well-kept secret that Daniel Bray is really good. In games where he’s gotten a sizable workload, either in mop-up duty or the past couple of weeks with Wayshawn Parker hurt, the sophomore back is averaging 8.2 yards per carry. It’s questionable as to whether Parker will be back for this game, although only getting 1 carry last week in his supposed return is a bad sign. Plus, Utah would be smart to hold him out because they don’t need him to run all over West Virginia tonight.

That can be Bray’s job, and it should be an easy job, as the Mountaineers are 124th in rushing yards allowed, 106th in yards per attempt, and are giving up 202.5 per game on the road this year. Utah might mess around in this game to pad Devon Dampier’s stats, but with a spread of 26 points and a total at 65.5, there will be plenty of Ute possessions and mop-up duty, so Bray has the opportunity to blow through this total.

 

NBA (0.5 Unit) Jaden McDaniels Over 29.5 Points+Rebounds+Assists (-110; Odds via Fanduel): 7:30 PM CT on DAZN-MIN

The Wolves backcourt can’t take any more damage, this is ridiculous. But with LaMelo now iffy for this game, I’m expecting Jaden to look to take over. He just seems like a different, more mature player who’s ready for this expanded role, and I think he can deliver again with Memphis in town, just as he has the past three games. He already has delivered against them, dropping 25/5/4 on the Grizzlies last week, taking advantage of Cam Boozer being out which will be the case again tonight. With this being another Cup game, starter minutes and usage will be way up so the expanded role will continue. And if he continues shooting 68% from the floor like he has the past 5 games, this combo prop becomes very reachable.

 

NCAA Basketball (0.5 Unit) Michigan/Kansas State Over 164.5 (-110; Odds via BetMGM): 4:30 PM CT on ESPNU

I said it the other day, I’ll say it again: general principle game here. These teams have been out in Vegas for Thanksgiving, lost both their games, and now play for the relative indignity of seventh place which nobody cares about. So defense will not be operating at its highest levels here, but I’m pretty sure pace will be.

Both Michigan and K-State love to run, as they’re both top-30 teams in adjusted tempo, and have been doing just that in this event. Apparently neither of them can stop anything either, as the Wolverines have given up 99 and 97 points while the Wildcats have surrendered 104 and 101, which is certainly one way to get ripped up in Vegas. If that level of defensive incompetence continues today, this total is going to look a little silly by the end of the game.

 

NCAA Basketball (0.75 Unit) Western Kentucky/Illinois Over 175.5 (-110; Odds via DraftKings): 5:00 PM CT on FS1

Speaking of making totals look silly, that’s what Illinois apparently does for a living now. The Illini just dropped 133 points in their last game and this is college, so that’s 3.3 points per minute, which would get you 159 in the NBA. It just can’t be overstated how insane this offense is right now, and books simply can’t make numbers high enough, especially in a situation like this.

I said before that 133-point outburst that my conspiracy theory is Brad Underwood scheduled a bunch of up-tempo teams in the non-conference to pad their offensive stats. Well, here’s another one with WKU visiting, as the Hilltoppers are 23rd in adjusted tempo. The Illini are now 5th in that stat and operating the most effective offense in the country, so maximized possessions can only lead to one outcome here. WKU has faced one other fellow up-tempo squad, getting into a 191-point affair with a Tennessee Tech squad that’s 91st offensively, so once again I see no upward limit for this total.

 

NCAA Basketball (0.5 Unit) Wichita State Team Total Over 76.5 (-110; Odds via Caesars): 8:00 PM CT on ESPN2

Kentucky has plenty of problems this season, and they all seem to be on the defensive side of the floor. The Wildcats simply can’t get ball pressure, and end up surrendering a lot of three-point production as they’re 255th in three-point percentage allowed. Teams with high three-point volume or that can shoot it well from deep have had no trouble scoring on UK, and I see that again here.

Kentucky has allowed at least 77 points in 4 of their 6 games, including to a couple of slow-paced teams outside the top-100 in adjusted offensive efficiency. Wichita State is willing to play up-tempo, which Kentucky will undoubtedly force on their home court, and the Shockers can shoot it as they’re 34th in three-point percentage. Extra possessions, bad perimeter defense, and their three-point acumen should get Wichita over this number and maybe even pull an upset.
