"I'm going to watch Arthur!!": Shelton's message after his Turin win

TheTools · 19.11.2026 15:42 · source · permalink

Ben Shelton made sure to give a nod to Arthur Fils after his ATP Finals victory. The American now awaits the result of the Frenchman's match against Alexander Zverev to find out whether his Turin run will continue.

"I'm going to watch Arthur!!" — that was the message Ben Shelton wrote after his match against Rafael Jódar at the Turin Masters.

Indeed, after his victory over the Spaniard, the American signed the camera with this message (see below), a reference to the showdown between his friend Arthur Fils and Alexander Zverev (tonight, starting at 8:30 PM French time).

Fils can no longer afford a slip-up

As a reminder, with Shelton's win, the Frenchman must now beat Zverev in straight sets to reach the ATP Finals semifinals.

Already guaranteed to finish first in the group, the German can approach this final round-robin match without any pressure on the standings.

Shelton, who has two wins, will therefore have to follow this last match closely to learn his fate. The suspense remains at its peak in Turin!
