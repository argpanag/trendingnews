Champions League 26-27 Winter Ball Makes On-Pitch Training Debut

TheTools · 20.10.2026 19:03 · source · permalink
  • Balls
  • Champions League
  • adidas
  • Arsenal

Adidas’ new UEFA Champions League Pro Winterball for the 2026-27 season, designed only for matches played in adverse winter conditions has officially made its appearance during Arsenal’s latest training session.

However, the ball was spotted alongside standard balls, suggesting it is unlikely to feature in upcoming official fixtures just yet.
