Done deal: Salah-Eddine set to join Panathinaikos

TheTools · 12.09.2026 13:33 · source · permalink

Tony D’Amico spent the summer looking for a place for Anass Salah-Eddine.

The Moroccan never figured into Gian Piero Gasperini’s plans, and several offers for the full-back arrived at Trigoria.

The 2002-born full-back, however, had rejected all possible destinations, at least until now.

As reported by Gianlucadimarzio.com, the Roman club has recently closed on the player’s transfer to Panathinaikos.

The clubs reached an agreement on a loan with an option to buy, and the player said “yes” to the Greek club’s offer.

Today, September 12, the number 34 will undergo a medical on behalf of the Greek club in Athens.
