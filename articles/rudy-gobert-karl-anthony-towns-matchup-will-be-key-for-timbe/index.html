Rudy Gobert-Karl-Anthony Towns matchup will be key for Timberwolves' test vs. Knicks

TheTools · 19.11.2026 17:00 · source · permalink

The Minnesota Timberwolves are now 8-3 without Anthony Edwards. At 11-4 overall, they are tied for the fifth-best record in the NBA, but just 1.5 games behind the top-seeded Los Angeles Lakers in the Western Conference.

Maybe it’s still too early to get into the standings, but it’s impressive that Minnesota has done this even though 73.3 percent of their outings have been without their superstar.

Nevertheless, Thursday night offers a litmus test for the Timberwolves. They welcome back Karl-Anthony Towns to the Target Center in a matchup with the defending champion New York Knicks. Not only will squaring off with the defending champs be a big test for the Wolves as a whole, but this Gobert-Towns matchup will be key.

Timberwolves can really get people talking about them with a win

Since Edwards has been sidelined, the Timberwolves have had some blowout wins (Los Angeles Clippers, Dallas Mavericks) and just pulled off a nail-biting win Tuesday against the Portland Trail Blazers. You can really only say they had one off night when they fell to the Chicago Bulls last Saturday.

Now, the reigning champs are in town. I recently gave the Timberwolves an A grade for their collective play without Ant-Man, but that might have to move to an A-plus if they can topple the Knicks.

New York isn’t off to the greatest start at 8-6, but they are 8-3 in their last 11 contests and just won two straight on the road against the Toronto Raptors. Some early issues, like Mikal Bridges and Josh Hart getting off to rough starts, are starting to smooth out. The shooting hasn't fully come together yet, but they still rank fourth in defensive rating.

The Timberwolves could be without Jonathan Kuminga for a second straight game. A bruised shoulder from Monday sidelined him on Tuesday, and Cody Williams started in his absence. This feels like a matchup where Kuminga would be quite useful, as maybe the best option to guard OG Anunoby.

As of this writing, the Knicks are 1.5-point favorites on DraftKings Sportsbook. Neither team has lost by more than 11 points thus far this season, so expectations point to a very competitive game.

Sentiment around KAT and Gobert is completely different

Ask any Knicks fan their thoughts about KAT’s play this season, and they will almost universally shout “PAY THAT MAN!” as he is eligible for an extension in talks that have been tabled.

Ask any Timberwolves fan what they think of Gobert’s play this season, and…well, they mostly won’t be positive.

Tuesday marked the fifth time already this season when Gobert didn’t make a field goal. Meanwhile, Towns is playing like someone who can put himself in the mix for at least the All-NBA Second Team.

The noise around Gobert’s play will only get louder if Towns thoroughly outplays him, especially since Towns spent his first nine years in Minnesota. Gobert is still a great rim deterrent, but guarding Towns takes more than paint protection.

This is not only a test for the Timberwolves as a whole, but also an important game for Gobert against one of the NBA's best big men.
