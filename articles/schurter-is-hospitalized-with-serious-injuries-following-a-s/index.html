Schurter is hospitalized with serious injuries following a scooter accident

TheTools · 08.09.2026 10:57 · source · permalink

Nino Schurter has rarely been injured during his career as a top athlete. Now, in his first season away from the racecourse, he ended up in the hospital: a dislocated shoulder and a broken arm after a fall off a scooter during a family outing.

It was supposed to be a casual family outing for Nino Schurter, including a ride on a scooter. The result: a dislocated shoulder and a broken arm, followed by transport by rescue helicopter. Schurter says he has never felt such pain. Nevertheless, his hospital stay was “only” an outpatient procedure; he was able to leave the clinic that same evening. However, he will now be sidelined for several weeks.

Schurter currently has to endure not only the pain of a broken bone, but also the mockery that the best mountain biker of all time—who has set new standards for riding technique on the racecourse—has now suffered a serious accident on a scooter of all things.
