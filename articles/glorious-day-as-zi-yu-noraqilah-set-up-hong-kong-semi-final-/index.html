Glorious day as Zi Yu-Noraqilah set up Hong Kong semi-final showdown with Pearly-Thinaah

TheTools · 20.11.2026 19:48 · source · permalink
    bookmark

PETALING JAYA: Young women’s doubles pair Low Zi Yu-Noraqilah Maisarah Ramdan pulled off an upset to reach the semi-finals of the Hong Kong Open after defeating world No. 25 Francesca Corbett-Jennie Gai of the United States on Friday (Nov 20). 

The world No. 41 pair were undaunted by their higher-ranked opponents as they battled for 58 minutes to secure a 21-19, 16-21, 21-16 victory. 

Zi Yu-Noraqilah will next face seniors Pearly Tan-M. Thinaah for a place in the final on Saturday (Nov 21).

Pearly-Thinaah defeated American pair Lauren Lam-Allison Lee 21-18, 21-11 in another quarter-final match.
