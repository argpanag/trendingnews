Alexander Zverev Shares Thoughts on Sinner's Recovery Ahead of Davis Cup

TheTools · 26.11.2026 12:27 · source · permalink

Alexander Zverev shares his thoughts on Jannik Sinner's recovery from knee surgery and prepares for Germany's Davis Cup quarterfinal against Great Britain.

Alexander Zverev is getting ready to play Germany against Great Britain in the Davis Cup quarterfinals. The match starts on Thursday. First, Cameron Norrie will play against Jan-Lennard Struff. After that, Zverev will play against Arthur Fery. Zverev knows that this will be a tough match, but he also believes that his team must be ready to give their best. During his press conference, Zverev also spoke about his fitness, his busy tennis schedule in 2026, Jannik Sinner's surgery and recovery, and the recent legal cases involving him.

Zverev Reflects on His Fitness and Sinner's Injury

Zverev said comparatively he is much healthier than last year because he had problems with his back and bones, as he felt tired after the US Open. But he was also happy that he played well and able to play in many matches throughout the year continuously.

Responding to comments about players using painkillers and injections, he said:

"I think it's an unfair comment from Godzi because he doesn't know how much painkillers we take. It's not like every time we take a painkiller, we let him know."

Zverev also said that he felt good for most of the year, as he wants to finish the season well. After that he plans to take a holiday and get ready for the 2027 season. He also spoke about Sinner's recovery, where he compared Sinner's injury with his own serious injury. He said that he had seven torn ligaments and broken bones. He hopes Sinner gets better soon and comes back fully fit. Zverev also said that he had sent Sinner a message after seeing him in Turin.

He said:

"I'm not a doctor, I'm not a medical expert. I don't know exactly what the recovery looks like for his type of injury, but I think he's mentally strong."

Zverev Expects Tough Davis Cup Quarterfinal

Germany will face a strong Great Britain team. The team includes Cameron Norrie, Arthur Fery in doubles players Patten and Skupski. Zverev praised Fery for his Wimbledon semi-finals. He also said that Norrie is a tough player who can be dangerous on the court.

"I expect a tough matchup. I think Arthur Fery had a great year. Cameron Norrie is a dangerous player," he said.

Zverev also said that the legal case against him would not affect his focus on tennis, as he repeated that he does not want to talk about the issue anymore because he has already said enough about it. Germany is now preparing for their next match. Zverev said he trusts his team and believes they are ready for the important quarterfinal match.
