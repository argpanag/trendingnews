Kate Middleton and Prince William Share a New Unseen Family Photo From Her Mountain Climbing Challenge

TheTools · 26.10.2026 13:58 · source · permalink

The Princess of Wales made the surprise endeavor earlier this year.

Kate Middleton and Prince William have shared another new look at the day Kate completed her 24-hour trek up the three highest peaks in Britain. The photo was posted to Instagram on Sunday to mark Mountain Rescue Awareness Day, which falls in the last week of October when the clocks go back in the UK and reminds people to stay safe in the mountains as darkness comes earlier.

The image, taken over the weekend of June 27–28 after Kate completed the challenge, shows the Prince and Princess of Wales at the base of Snowdon (also known as Yr Wyddfa in Welsh) with their children, Prince George, Princess Charlotte, and Prince Louis. They are surrounded by local mountain rescue team members and a red-coated golden retriever.

“Through our connection with this remarkable organisation, we have seen first-hand the dedication, professionalism and compassion that define their work,” the caption reads. “Meeting local mountain rescue teams during the Three Peaks Challenge this summer was an opportunity to say thank you in person for the vital role they play in keeping walkers and climbers safe on the mountains all year round.” The message is signed “W & C,” for William and Catherine. Prince William has been patron of Mountain Rescue England and Wales since 2007.

The National Three Peaks Challenge involves climbing the highest mountains in England, Scotland, and Wales—Scafell Pike, Ben Nevis, and Snowdon, respectively—within 24 hours. Kate did it to raise money for The Royal Marsden Cancer Charity, which supports the hospital where she was treated for cancer in 2024. Kate has since said she took on the challenge “not simply as a physical endeavour but as a chance to explore life beyond diagnosis and to give something back.”

In September, Kate also hosted a small tea party at Windsor Castle for fellow Three Peaks participants and their families. “Behind every summit was a personal story of resilience, determination and hope,” Kate said in a statement on social media at the time. “Listening to their experiences of the challenge and their reasons for taking it on was a powerful reminder that recovery is rarely a journey we make alone, and of the importance of the people who walk alongside us along the way.”
