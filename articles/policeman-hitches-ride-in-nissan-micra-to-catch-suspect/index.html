Policeman hitches ride in Nissan Micra to catch suspect

TheTools · 18.09.2026 18:14 · source · permalink

Footage shows moment woman helps officer to track down escaping criminal

A policeman got a lift in a Nissan Micra as he chased a fleeing criminal.

The officer flagged down a woman in a red car while chasing Jacob Roberts, 25, who was suspected of dangerous driving and possession of drugs.

When the policeman said he needed a lift, the woman replied: “Come on then.”

Body-worn camera footage showed the moment the officer hopped into the driver’s Nissan Micra and she asked him: “Where to?”

He pointed ahead and said he needed to catch up with Roberts, who was found sitting on a concrete block.

A court heard that police had noticed a smell of drugs coming from Roberts’s van in the village of Abercwmboi, South Wales.

When officers tried to pull him, he drove off, crashing into parked cars and a building in his attempt to escape. The footage showed his van being driven at double the 20mph speed limit through the terraced streets of the former mining village.

After ramming a police car, Roberts fled on foot. The officer chasing him then stopped the passing vehicle and asked for a lift.

Merthyr Tydfil Crown Court heard Roberts had cannabis on him when he was stopped.

He pleaded guilty to dangerous driving, driving whilst disqualified, driving without insurance, failure to stop after a road accident, criminal damage to property and possession of cannabis.

Jeremy Jenkins, the judge, said: “Only custodial sentences can be justified for this type of behaviour. No other sentence is appropriate or commensurate with this level of offending. You are a very, very serious accident waiting to happen.

“Hopefully in custody you will realise and come to your senses that you cannot go on behaving in this manner.”

Roberts, of Mountain Ash, was jailed for 14 months and banned from driving for three years and seven months.

Pc Peter Kerswell of South Wales Police said: “Roberts was already banned from driving when he committed these offences, he showed complete disregard for all other road users, including pedestrians. I hope it acts as a deterrent for others who think of driving dangerously.

“It’s lucky that no one was seriously injured during this pursuit.”
