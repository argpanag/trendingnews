Semeurs d’Espoir Monaco Launches Major Donation Drive for People in Need

TheTools · 19.09.2026 17:20 · source · permalink

Semeurs d’Espoir Monaco is holding a major collection drive at Carrefour Fontvieille on Friday, October 2, and Saturday, October 3, with the aim of assembling 1,800 care packages for people in need.

The goal is to assemble 1,800 care packages.

Volunteers from Semeurs d’Espoir Monaco will be at Carrefour Fontvieille on Friday, October 2, and Saturday, October 3, collecting donations from shoppers throughout the two-day drive.

Six Months of Local Street Outreach

Food and essential items will be collected in the store’s central aisle, with volunteers on hand to advise shoppers on what is most needed.

Related stories

Held twice a year, the collection drive provides the supplies for kits distributed during the association’s street outreach in Nice and Menton over the following six months.
