Kevin Vallejos Beats Aljamain Sterling By Decision At UFC Fight Night 294

TheTools · 21.11.2026 20:23 · source · permalink

Kevin Vallejos edged his way to a unanimous decision victory over Aljamain Sterling tonight at UFC Fight Night 294.

Round One

Front kick to the body from Sterling. He misses a head kick attempt. Vallejos misses with a big swing and Sterling was tempted to go for a takedown there, but didn’t commit to it.

Leg kick for Sterling. Vallejos with a few feints as he waits for his moment to strike. He misses a hook, but then nicely fends off a takedown counter from Sterling.

Inside leg kick for Vallejos. He lands that again. Jab for him. Low kick from Sterling after a light jab.

Nice calf kick for Vallejos. Jab for Sterling. He misses on a body kick attempt. Cautious fight so far as Vallejos stays mindful of Sterling’s takedown threat.

Nice sprawl from Vallejos as Sterling tried to sneak in a takedown behind a punch.

Well timed right hand lands for Vallejos late in the round.

Round Two

Vallejos comes close with an uppercut attempt as Sterling was going for a kick.

Kick to the body from Sterling. Body punch for Vallejos. Now an inside leg kick. Leg kick for Sterling now.

Right hand counter from Vallejos and then Sterling goes for a single-leg and is able to bring him down. Vallejos with very nice explosive movement though to get back up.

Sterling still clinched up now though against the cage. Sterling with a knee to the body and Vallejos responds with a punch. Another knee for Sterling. He tries for a foot sweep, but Vallejos stays upright.

Vallejos able to get free. Spinning back kick to the body from Sterling. Sterling fakes a takedown and throws winging punches instead that miss.

Punch for Vallejos and then gets a takedown of his own. He’s in Sterling’s full guard here, but not doing much with it for now. Vallejos opts to stand back up with 15 seconds to go.

Solid body shot for Vallejos. He lands a grazing right hook. Now a left hand.

Round Three

Right hand for Vallejos. Sterling with a side kick to the body. Good uppercut from Vallejos.

Jab for Vallejos as Sterling attempts a kick. Missed punch from Vallejos enables Sterling to land a takedown, but Vallejos nicely spins out to get back to his feet swiftly.

Jab for Vallejos. Thigh kick from Sterling. Now one from Vallejos. Vallejos lands an uppercut as Sterling goes in for a takedown, and then stuffs it.

Sterling into another takedown and Vallejos almost went for a knee, but adjusts and is again able to stuff the attempt and scramble free.

Sterling clinching up against the cage. Foot stomps from him. Final 90 seconds and still not much happening and now the ref steps in to seperate them.

Brief exchange at close quarters. Inside leg kick from Vallejos and then a great sprawl. Punches from Vallejos. Sterling with a takedown attempt off a kick, but has to settle for clinching up against the cage, with Vallejos facing the fence.

Vallejos turns into him and reverses the clinch position before trying for a last-gasp takedown that doesn’t work out.

Decision

A close and cautious fight here then, with Vallejos edging the striking action and showing good takedown defense, while Sterling repeatedly threatened with takedowns and had spells of control from the clinch too. In the end the judges favor Vallejos striking, leading him to a unanimous decision victory (30-27 x2, 29-28).
