Former Crusaders and Hurricanes back Daryl “Flaza” Lilley dies - Chris Lynch Media

TheTools · 10.11.2026 10:14 · source · permalink

Former Crusaders and Hurricanes back Daryl “Flaza” Lilley has died.

The Crusaders confirmed Lilley’s death in a tribute to the former player, identified by the club as Crusader number 36.

The club said its thoughts were with his loved ones.

New Plymouth Old Boys’ Rugby Club said Lilley died the previous night.

It remembered him as a fierce competitor on the field and “a genuinely good bugger off it”.

United Rugby & Sports Club described Lilley as a familiar face in the local rugby community and a “nemesis” when he played for New Plymouth Old Boys against United, while acknowledging his talent as a player.

Lilley represented Taranaki, Canterbury, the Crusaders and the Hurricanes.

He played 29 matches for the Crusaders from 1997 to 1999, then made 26 appearances for the Hurricanes from 2000 to 2002.

He later returned to Taranaki, where he played through 2003. 

One of his most memorable moments came in the 1998 Super 12 semifinal against the Coastal Sharks at Lancaster Park.

Lilley scored a crucial second half try as the Crusaders came back from a six point deficit to win 36 to 32 and reach the final. 

Lilley also coached in club rugby. Southern Rugby Club in Hawera said he was its premier assistant coach in 2010 and head coach in 2011 and 2012.

The club credited his work with helping lay the foundations for its 2013 success.
