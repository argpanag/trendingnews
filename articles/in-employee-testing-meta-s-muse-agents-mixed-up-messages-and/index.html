In employee testing, Meta’s Muse agents mixed up messages and pulled in unexpected chats from other apps.

TheTools · 18.11.2026 14:55 · source · permalink

Reuters reports employees found Meta’s AI accessing Signal and other apps they didn’t connect it to, and sending seemingly random off-topic chats, issues that Reuters says Meta’s Daniel Roberts described as “isolated incidents.”

Some of the incidents that raised the most concern involved cryptic out-of-context messages sent by the agent that appeared to contain other people’s information, in many cases phrased as replies to queries the users had never submitted.
