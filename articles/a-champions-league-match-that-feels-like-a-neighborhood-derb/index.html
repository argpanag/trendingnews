A Champions League match that feels like a neighborhood derby: These two teams from different countries are just 70 miles apart

TheTools · 21.10.2026 15:00 · source · permalink

For the visiting supporters, Wednesday’s Champions League trip is less an international journey and more a quick ride down the road.

We’ve seen a few UEFA Champions League games between teams from the same city over the years, including Real Madrid vs. Atlético Madrid, AC Milan against Inter Milan and Arsenal vs. Chelsea. But there haven’t been too many that have seen fans make a quick hop across a international border for a few hours before heading back home.

But that is exactly what will happen Wednesday when Lens, who play in France’s Ligue 1, visit Club Brugge, based in neighboring Belgium.

A short trip across the border

A quick look on Google Maps tells you that it is roughly a 68.5-mile drive from Lens’ Stade Bollaert-Delelis to Club Brugge’s Jan Breydel Stadium, a journey that takes around an hour and 20 minutes by car.

The straight-line distance between the two venues works out at just 55 miles.

Once they have arrived in Belgium less than 90 minutes after setting off, Lens fans will have to look out their Flemish phrasebooks, just to add to the quirkiness of the occasion. Flemish is a collective term commonly used for the varieties of Dutch spoken in the northern part of Belgium.

Other close encounters

The Club Brugge-Lens matchup beats the 2022 clash between Germany’s Bayern Munich and Austria’s RB Salzburg, which brought together teams from stadiums just over 80 miles apart.

In the first round of the 1979-80 European Cup, the predecessor to the Champions League, Dundalk of the Republic of Ireland took on Northern Ireland’s Linfield, whose grounds are roughly 45 miles from one another.

Although not in the Champions League proper, the 2025-26 third-round qualifying tie between Denmark’s FC Copenhagen and Malmö from Sweden saw the two teams travel just under 27 miles, across the Øresund Bridge, which connects the two countries, to get to the away leg.
