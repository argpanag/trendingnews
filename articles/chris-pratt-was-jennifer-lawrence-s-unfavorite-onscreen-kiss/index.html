Chris Pratt Was Jennifer Lawrence’s Unfavorite Onscreen Kiss

TheTools · 20.11.2026 22:00 · source · permalink

Jennifer Lawrence joined the “plead the Fifth” five-timers club last night on WWHL. Andy Cohen quizzed her on wigs, kisses, and Bradley Cooper collabs. She pleaded the Fifth on what her fave and least fave Cooper movies are, but told all on which onscreen kisses were best. “I mean, they were all so amazing,” she kidded. At the very bottom of the list was Chris Pratt (Passengers). Then came Timothée Chalamet (Don’t Look Up), Bradley Cooper (Silver Linings Playbook), and Liam Hemsworth (various Hunger Games). She ranked Josh Hutcherson as best, but that could have been because he was sitting right next to her. She also changed her love-triangle alliance from what she’d previously said on Call Her Daddy. On that podcast, she said she was Team Gale. But next to bread boy himself, she said it was Peenis all the way. J.Law was quite candid throughout the night. She shouted out her boob-job doc for giving her a new “full C” set after kids. She also stared on in abject horror as Cohen played Hutcherson’s episode of Cribs.

Lawrence said in the past that her love scenes with Pratt were “really weird,” as he was married to Anna Faris at the time. “It was going to be my first time even kissing a married man, and guilt is like the worst feeling in your stomach,” she told The Hollywood Reporter. “I knew it was my job, but I couldn’t tell my stomach.”
