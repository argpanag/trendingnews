Western Notes: Grizzlies, Jazz, Kyrie, Suns, Williams

TheTools · 20.11.2026 02:09 · source · permalink

Widely projected to be a lottery team this season after trading away star point guard Ja Morant for a modest return in June, the Grizzlies are off to an 11-4 start and improved to a remarkable 8-0 in road games with a thrilling 124-121 win in Cleveland on Wednesday.

“If you had told me before the season that we would be 11-4, 8-0 on the road, I would definitely be surprised by that,” starting center Zach Edey admitted after the game, per Joe Vardon of The Athletic. “But it’s the way we’ve been doing it, just taking it game by game, attacking the opponent that’s in front of us. You can’t win 11 games in one night … so we’ve been focusing on playing harder than our opponent, and it’s been great.”

Having a healthy Ty Jerome (25.4 points per game) has been a boon for the Grizzlies, and offseason acquisition Isaiah Stewart has been an important culture-setter. But Stewart (illness) has missed Memphis’ past two games and Jerome (right hip impingement) has been out for the past three — the team keeps winning, with reserves stepping up to play key roles.

On Wednesday, Cam Spencer scored 26 points on 9-of-11 shooting in Jerome’s place in the starting five, while forward Taylor Hendricks had 18 points and six rebounds in 21 minutes off the bench. As Vardon relays, head coach Tuomas Iisalo said after the game that Hendricks – a former ninth overall pick – is “only scratching the surface of what he can be.”

“We’re hanging our hats on playing hard,” said veteran forward Jerami Grant, who was acquired in the Morant trade. “We can’t get away with just being the more talented team, so it’s definitely different. Older teams, you can’t get away with more on skill and things like that.”

Let’s round up a few more items from across the Western Conference…

  • While there has been no indication yet that the Mavericks will be looking to trade Kyrie Irving prior to February’s deadline, Ian Begley of SNY.tv (Twitter video link) hears that the Jazz are among the teams who have had internal discussions about Irving and have done their homework on the star point guard. “Teams around the league are doing similar things,” Begley said, cautioning not to read too much into the due diligence Utah has performed. “But you’re not going to spend a lot of time on a player if you don’t have interest in him.”
  • The Suns’ starting five had an excellent night on Wednesday in a blowout win over Sacramento, but lineups that feature the quartet of Devin Booker, Jalen Green, Miles Bridges, and Dillon Brooks still have an overall net rating of -15.7 in 215 minutes so far this season. Phoenix’s starting lineup has been propped up this fall by one of the NBA’s best second units, according to Gerald Bourguet of Sports360AZ.com, who notes that Phoenix’s bench ranks among the league leaders in three-point shooting, steals, points off turnovers, and offensive rebounds. Bourguet argues that moving Collin Gillespie into the starting lineup in place of Green would provide the Suns with better lineup balance.
  • The Warriors providing a minor update on guard Brandon Williams on Wednesday, announcing (via Twitter) that he’ll be out again on Thursday vs. Memphis but has progressed to on-court work and will be reevaluated again on Saturday. Williams has missed the past three games due to a left hamstring strain.
