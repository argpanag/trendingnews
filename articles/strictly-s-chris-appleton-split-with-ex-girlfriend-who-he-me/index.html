Strictly's Chris Appleton split with ex-girlfriend who he met at Leicester salon

TheTools · 26.09.2026 16:00 · source · permalink

Celebrity hairdresser and author Chris Appleton has spoken candidly about his enduring relationship with Katie Katon, the mother of his two children.

The Strictly Come Dancing 2026 contestant, 43, was with Katie for eight years before publicly coming out as gay in 2009. The couple first met while working together at Georges Hairdressing in Leicester, where Chris launched his styling career.

They went on to have two children together - Billy Appleton, born in 2002 when Chris was just 19, followed by Kitty-blu Appleton in 2004 - before eventually parting ways.

Reflecting on their relationship during an appearance on Jamie Laing's Great Company podcast in May, he said: "Me and Kate got together when I was 19. When we broke up when I was 27, it was really dark. When I came out as gay, she saw me, when I couldn't see myself.", reports the Daily Star.

He added: "For a lot of years, I couldn't see myself, even with the shame of me being gay and it was so painful for her. She saw me and still does. We spent Christmas together with the kids this year, it was New Year's Eve, I put my arm round her and I said 'I did it, Kate'."

Chris went on to discuss their co- parenting arrangement, saying: "Although sometimes me and Kate get upset as our kids didn't have a conventional family but it's loyal and it's solid. So I think, look, we still argue like cat and dog but there was a pact we made when we met. She saved me.

"Having kids and this bond that never left, it never will. Even when she was in pain and shame, and hated me and probably didn't want to talk to me again, she didn't because she could see what I was going through. It takes a lot of compassion to do that."

Chris had previously disclosed that Katie was the first person he came out to, admitting he "didn't want to be gay". During his appearance on Jay Shetty's On Purpose podcast in August last year, he confessed: "Because I loved her, I really loved her, and I didn't want to be gay. I didn't want to be different."

Breaking the news to their children proved even harder, with Katie's mother ultimately stepping in to tell Billy and Kitty-blu. Chris recalled feeling devastated: "All of a sudden, I just felt like I'd just messed their life up, and I felt like I'd failed as a dad because my job was to protect them."

Despite everything, Chris and Katie have remained remarkably close over the years, even holidaying together. In 2025, the pair reunited to mark the Fourth of July celebrations with their children.

Reflecting on the occasion, Chris wrote: "So grateful for a weekend with my family, full of laughter, love, and those little moments that stay with you," adding, "Always everything I need."

Chris is said to be currently single following his split from The White Lotus actor Lukas Gage, whom he married in Las Vegas in April 2023, with close friend and client Kim Kardashian officiating the ceremony. The short-lived marriage endured for approximately six months before Chris filed for divorce in November 2023, citing irreconcilable differences.

The split was finalised in 2024, with Lukas describing the fleeting union as "a manic episode for six months."
