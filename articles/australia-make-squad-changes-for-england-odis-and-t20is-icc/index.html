Australia make squad changes for England ODIs and T20Is | ICC

TheTools · 15.11.2026 05:41 · source · permalink

Ben Dwarshius finds a spot in Australia's T20I side, while Riley Meredith joins the ODI side for the final two matches.

Australia have made changes to their squads ahead of the remainder of their white-ball series against England, with left-arm fast bowler Ben Dwarshuis added to the T20I squad.

Dwarshuis replaces Nathan Ellis, who continues his recovery from a side strain sustained during Australia's recent tour of South Africa.

Meanwhile, Riley Meredith has been added to the ODI squad.

Ellis suffered the injury during the ODI series against South Africa in September and has not yet recovered sufficiently to return to action.

Fast bowler Mahli Beardman is being managed for Sunday's second ODI against England in Adelaide as he continues to build capacity following recent injuries. He is expected to return for the third ODI in Hobart on Wednesday.

Beardman made a sensational start to his international career in the series opener in Perth, claiming 6/24 from 7.4 overs to record the best bowling figures by an Australia men's player on ODI debut. His performance also ranks fourth on the all-time list for best bowling figures on men's ODI debut.

The 21-year-old dismissed England captain Harry Brook and Joe Root before adding the wickets of Jordan Cox, Sam Curran, Liam Dawson and Jofra Archer as England were bowled out for 131. Australia went on to secure a seven-wicket victory to take a 1-0 lead in the series.

30-year-old quick Riley Meredith has joined up with the squad for the remainder of the ODI series, and is featuring in the ongoing ODI in Adelaide.

Australia v England Schedule:

Second ODI: November 15, Adelaide

Third ODI: November 18, Hobart

First T20I: November 21, Melbourne

Second T20I: November 24, Carrara

Third T20I: November 27, Brisbane

Fourth T20I: November 29, Sydney

Fifth T20I: December 2, Canberra
