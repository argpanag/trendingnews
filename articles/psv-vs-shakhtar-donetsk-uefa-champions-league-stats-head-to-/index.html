Match Facts

  • The only previous European meeting between PSV and Shakhtar Donetsk was in November 2024 in the UEFA Champions League group stages, with PSV winning 3-2.

  • In major European competition, Shakhtar Donetsk have won just one of their six away matches in the Netherlands (D1 L4), a 2-1 win over Feyenoord in the UEFA Champions League in October 2017.

  • PSV have lost their first UEFA Champions League match in each of their last five campaigns (2016-17, 2018-19, 2023-24, 2024-25, 2025-26) since beating Man Utd 2-1 in 2015-16.

  • 2026-27 will be PSV’s fourth UEFA Champions League appearance in a row, their joint second-longest run of consecutive appearances in the competition (including European Cup), after a run of 12 between 1997-98 and 2008-09.

  • Shakhtar Donetsk are making their 20th appearance in the UEFA Champions League since their debut season in 2000-01 , the second-most of any side outside of Europe’s top five leagues across that time after FC Porto (21).

  • PSV scored 16 goals in eight games in the UEFA Champions League in 2025-26 (2 goals per game), their best average in a single season in the competition since 1992-93.

  • Shakhtar Donetsk have won two of their last 15 away UEFA Champions League matches (D3 L10) and lost five of their previous six (D1) since a 3-2 win at Royal Antwerp in October 2023.

  • PSV lost four of their eight UEFA Champions League matches in 2025-26 (W2 D2); only in 1992-93 and 2008-09 (both L5) have they lost more games in a single season in the competition (including European Cup).

  • Ricardo Pepi has been involved in nine goals in the UEFA Champions League for PSV (6 goals, 3 assists) – only Luuk de Jong (14 - 11 goals, 3 assists) and Ruud van Nistelrooy (10 - 8 goals, 2 assists) have more. Pepi has the best minutes per goal or assist ratio of any player to feature in 250+ minutes in Champions League history (one every 49 mins).

  • Shakhtar Donetsk manager Arda Turan led them to the Conference League semi-finals last season in his first season in charge. Turan made 32 UEFA Champions League appearances for Galatasaray, Atletico Madrid and Barcelona, with his third and fourth games coming against PSV in 2006-07, both defeats for Galatasaray.
