PS5, PS4 Action Game Series Being Delisted, Last Chance to Buy - AOL

TheTools · 04.11.2026 02:03 · source · permalink

A PS5 and PS4 action game series is being delisted from the PS Store, giving players one last chance to buy it. GameMill Entertainment has announced that Street Outlaws: The List and Street Outlaws 2: Winner Takes All will be pulled from sale by the end of December, 2026.

PS5, PS4 action game Street Outlaws: The List is currently 60% off on PS Store

GameMill’s licensing agreement for Street Outlaws games has expired, and as a result, the games will be delisted from all storefronts.

For the uninitiated, the series is based on the reality television series of the same name.

The first game, Street Outlaws: The List, is currently on sale on the PS Store. Players can get the title for $15.99 (60% off) until November 5.

Street Outlaws 2 isn’t on sale, but considering the games won’t be delisted until end of December, the sequel might be discounted in the next PS Store promotion.
