One in custody after Wednesday attacks leave four injured in Topeka

TheTools · 28.10.2026 17:18 · source · permalink

TOPEKA, Kan. (WIBW) - One person was in custody after four people suffered injuries in a series of attacks early Wednesday afternoon at several locations in Topeka, officials said.

The first incidents were reported around 12:07 p.m. Wednesday at two different locations in the 1600 block of S.W. Fillmore Street in central Topeka.

Upon their arrival, police found two stabbing victims at locations on that block.

Both of the victims sustained life-threatening injuries, police said.

The injured individuals were transported to the hospital by American Medical Response ambulance.

Officers also were sent around 12:20 p.m. Wednesday to the 1800 block of S.W. Topeka Boulevard -- about five blocks south and east of the Fillmore address -- on a report of another possible stabbing. Information about that call wasn’t immediately available.

Meanwhile, around 12:59 p.m. Wednesday, another possible stabbing was reported in the 600 block of S.W. 3rd Street, just west of downtown Topeka.

Police responding to that location found an individual who had sustained injuries that weren’t believed to be life-threatening.

That individual was taken by ambulance to a local hospital for treatment.

Then, a short time later at around 1:08 p.m. Wednesday, officers were sent to Ward-Meade Park in the 100 block of N.W. Fillmore Street on a report of an aggravated battery. That scene was about five blocks northwest of the earlier incident on S.W. 3rd Street.

Police officers responding to Ward-Meade Park located an individual with injuries that weren’t considered to be life-threatening. The individual was taken by private vehicle to a local hospital for treatment.

Officials said the person injured at Ward-Meade Park wasn’t a member of the park staff or a volunteer.

Topeka police said a suspect had been detained in connection with all of the Wednesday incidents. At this time, there is no known threat to the public, police said.

The investigation remains active and ongoing, police said. Additional details will be provided as information becomes available.

The Topeka Police Department is asking for the public’s assistance with these ongoing investigations.

Anyone with surveillance camera footage from any of the locations or information related to these incidents may email telltpd@topeka.org or contact Topeka police detectives at 785-368-9400.

Anonymous tips can be made by calling Shawnee County Crime Stoppers at 785-234-0007.

Anonymous tips also can be made online at www.p3tips.com/128.

Check wibw.com for more information as it becomes available.

WIBW’s Shayndel Jones contributed to this report.
