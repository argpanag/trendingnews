Amandaland fans in shock as beloved BBC sitcom loses NTA for Best Comedy to 'wild' win

TheTools · 08.09.2026 22:45 · source · permalink

"Excuse me while I quietly seethe."

Fans of Amandaland were stunned on Tuesday evening when the much-loved sitcom lost out on the National Television Award for Best Comedy.

The BBC series, which stars Lucy Punch, Joanna Lumley, Samuel Anderson and Peter Serafinowicz, was up against Changing Ends, Last One Laughing, Not Going Out and Mrs Brown’s Boys, and it was the latter that ended up winning.

As the result was announced onstage at The O2 in London, viewers rushed to X - formerly known as Twitter - to share their thoughts.

"How Amandaland lost out to Mrs Brown’s Boys is wild," one person wrote, while another asked: "How did Amandaland, Changing Ends, LOLUK and Not Going Out lose out to Mrs Brown's Boys?"

A third person posted: "Mrs Brown’s Boys has just won Best Comedy at the NTAs, beating Amandaland, Changing Ends, Last One Laughing and Not Going Out. I genuinely did not see that one coming."

And someone else wrote: "Mrs Brown’s Boys beating Amandaland?!! Excuse me while I quietly seethe from the sofa."

Another X user added: "HOW did this beat Amandaland? The British public's lack of taste will never fail to astound me (sic)."

When the news was announced on the official NTAs Instagram page, the comments continued as one person simply wrote: "How?" while another said: "Really!"

Amandaland is a spin-off of the sitcom Motherland, and already has two series under its belt, with season two airing in May and June of this year.

The show follows recently divorced Amanda Hughes (Punch) as she navigates parenting teenagers in new surroundings after moving from Queen's Park and downsizing to a property in London's South Harlesden area.

Meanwhile, Mrs Brown's Boys has a total of five seasons and stars Irish actor and comedian Brendan O'Carroll as the titular character.

Of course, this isn't the first time O'Carroll's creation has won the NTA for Best Comedy, having taken the award home six times before. The show has also won a BAFTA TV Award as well as a TV Choice Award.

Elsewhere at the NTAs on 8 September, Clarkson's Farm won Best Docuseries, Gogglebox took home the gong for Factual Entertainment and Emmerdale reclaimed the top spot in Soapland as it won Best Serial Drama.
