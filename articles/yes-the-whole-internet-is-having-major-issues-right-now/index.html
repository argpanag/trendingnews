Yes, the whole Internet is having major issues right now

TheTools · 13.11.2026 05:53 · source · permalink

When you purchase through links in our articles, we may earn a small commission. This doesn't affect our editorial independence.

It's not just you. That service you use is down for everyone else, too.

Update 4:39 pm ET: An Amazon spokesperson reached out to Macworld to let us know that AWS is functioning normally and has been all day. Any disruptions today would have to be caused by something else.

Update 9:45 pm ET: Microsoft is “seeing positive results” with its efforts to correct the outage.

Whether you’re using a Mac, iPhone, or—shudder— a PC, dozens of popular internet services around the world are down right now, or at least acting slowly and erratically.

This sort of thing is often due to an outage with a large cloud service provider. A quick look at DownDetector.com and the ThousandEyes Outages sites show huge problems across a large host of services. It’s mainly affecting Microsoft 365 services, including Outlook, as well as Azure hosting, Cloudflare, gaming networks, Gmail…you name it, it’s got a problem. Even 911 emergency services are having issues, according to the site.

Some users are reporting issues with some Apple services like iMessage, but Apple’s Service Status page currently shows everything green across the board. But don’t be too surprised if you have problems with some Apple services despite this–if the error is because of problems on a Tier-1 network provider (a core internet backbone infrastructure issue) and not in Apple’s own systems, you could have trouble reaching Apple’s services even though they are functioning just fine.

This stuff usually tends to be rectified pretty quickly, but this one is lasting a while. In a post on X to its official 356 Status account at around 8pm ET, Microsoft wrote that it is “seeing positive results” with its work to “restart affected infrastructure and recover server availability in an isolated manner.” It’s unclear when the issue could be rectified, but a previous message said the company was “temporarily limiting which types of traffic can reach the affected infrastructure in some locations.”

So settle in, and here’s hoping it’s fixed bt the morning. We’ll keep this article updated as we learn more.
