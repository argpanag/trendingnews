RBC Capital reiterates Outperform rating on Micron Technology, $1500 price target

TheTools · 18.09.2026 21:35 · source · permalink

RBC Capital reiterated their Outperform rating on Micron Technology's stock with a price target of $1500.00.

The price target implies an upside of 53.5% from the Sep 17 close of $977.50.

Key takeaways from the analyst note

Strong HBM Roadmap and Pricing Tailwinds: Micron Technology's robust HBM4 roadmap positions it for significant market share gains, with expectations for HBM pricing to increase by 80-100% in 2027, contributing positively to blended pricing as DRAM prices are also anticipated to grow. This strategic advantage is likely to enhance revenue stability and profitability.

Consistent Revenue Outperformance: Micron has consistently outperformed revenue expectations in recent quarters, averaging a 10% beat over the last four quarters. This trend, coupled with forecasts for continued strong demand in the memory market driven by GenAI and supply constraints, reinforces investor confidence in the stock's performance.
