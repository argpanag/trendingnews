Staying Cool and Breezy Today

TheTools · 22.09.2026 11:05 · source · permalink
  • Showers come to an end this morning
  • High temperatures near 60 degrees this afternoon
  • Temperatures warm slightly for the end of the week and into the weekend

MADISON, Wis. (WMTV) - Happy Tuesday! We will see periodic chances for light showers through the early morning, especially for the southern and southwestern parts of the area. Rainfall amounts should generally be light, with an additional few hundredths to around a tenth of an inch possible. As the day goes on, the showers will gradually diminish as drier air moves in.

What’s Coming Up...

Clouds will remain stubborn for us today, though we may see some more breaks in the clouds into this evening. Temperatures will remain about ten degrees below average today, with highs near 60 degrees. The cooler air comes at the perfect time as the fall officially begins this evening at 7:05 p.m. with the fall equinox.

Overnight, lows will drop into the mid-40s to lower 50s, so it will be a chilly start to your Wednesday. Through the day Wednesday, dry conditions will remain as high pressure continues to linger over the Great Lakes region. Temperatures will also be a touch warmer in the mid-60s.

Looking Ahead...

As we head through the rest of the week, temperatures will gradually warm, getting a bit closer to late September averages (low 70s). With that said, we will still be a few degrees below average as highs are expected to be in the upper 60s late week and into the weekend. Mostly dry weather will continue during this timeframe with just a slight chance of showers returning over the weekend.
