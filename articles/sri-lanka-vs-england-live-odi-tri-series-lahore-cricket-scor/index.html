  1. Farewellpublished at 16:45 GMT
    16:45 GMT

    Time for us to say goodbye from Lahore, where England have completed a 55-run consolation win over Sri Lanka.

    Take a read of Ffion Wynne's report on the final group stage match of this tri-series.

    Incidentally 86% of you have said in our vote that you'd like to see more of these tri-series rather than sticking with bilateral fixtures.

    Pakistan will face Sri Lanka in the final at this ground on Saturday at 08:00 GMT - as we knew before the start of this fixture. You'll be able to listen to commentary from that match on this website, 5 Live Sports Extra and BBC Sounds.

    England are next in action against Australia - a three-match ODI series starts in Perth (03:30 GMT) on Friday, 13 November, with five T20s to follow.

  2. 'We had a clear plan'published at 16:45 GMT
    16:45 GMT

    England captain Harry Brook: "I think we've batted really well throughout the series, apart from that first game really. But the way we finished the game there, shows what we learned from when they chased 430 so that was really good to see.

    "I wasn't nervous, no. We had a clear plan, a lot of clarity with the bowlers. The lads have learned a hell of a lot from a couple of days ago, so it was an exceptional effort.

    "We haven't had the series that we'd have wanted, we obviously would like to be in the final on Sunday. But the lads have taken a lot of confidence from it, despite being on the wrong side. We're scoring runs and taking wickets, and we've got a lot of clarity in our roles despite the losses."

  6. 'Important to win tonight'published at 16:40 GMT
    16:40 GMT

    England batter Ben Duckett: "Obviously, that pitch was getting good again with the conditions.

    "We've come out on the wrong side. So, very important to win tonight. And, I think we played some really good cricket over the course of the few games and just fell on the wrong side of the result.

    "So, there is positives to take. It's just about getting over the line and winning games of cricket, certainly when we're in front."

    On Pavan Rathnayake: "He's an incredible player. He's had a couple of great knocks against us and obviously looking like he was going to get it done again. It was just really important for us to keep trying to take wickets today and we kind of saw at the end there with the tail that that it will crumble. So, credit to the bowlers for getting the job done and certainly Liam Dawson and his bowling is incredible."

    On his form in Pakistan: "Finish the summer fairly quiet, so I've toured Pakistan quite a few times and know the conditions well. I know how good the pitch can be to bat on. So really happy that I've put a couple of performances on the board.

    "We're going to completely different conditions where the lengths we bowl in and the areas we hit the ball are going to be extremely different going to Perth, especially.

    "I think there are so many different learnings. We scored 430 in one game, won 90% of the game against Pakistan the other day and obviously to get over the line here.

    "I think we've put some really good performances on. We just haven't quite got over the line. So, we've got guys in form, guys bowling well, so I guess for us that's what we got to take into Australia and we're just really happy we won today."

    Jonathan Agnew
    BBC Chief Cricket Commentator on Test Match Special

    On England's bowling attack...

    When you use seven bowlers again, I don't think Harry Brook is quite sure.

    He's a relatively inexperienced captain.

    I think he is still trying to work out what bowling formula sort of works.

    There have been some unexpected choices and bowling decisions throughout this tournament.

    Sam Curran has bowled a lot, certainly in the first couple of games. He hasn't really bowled very well.

    He has leant on him a lot. The Henry Crocombe thing today - I am not sure what that is all about.

    One minute he is bowling a vital over the other day, and then he just bowls three overs here.

    His first over was expensive, wasn't it? But to bowl seven bowlers and quite so many spinners when the ball is pretty damp as well.

    Like anything, Brook is learning as well and he is learning captaincy on the hoof.

    Responsibility is sitting on his shoulders easily as a batsman. We saw that responsibility, I think, in the summer when Joe Root came back, a bit of a change in tempo.

    He has had a terrific tour batting wise. That double 100, his disappointment at losing the game, but what a reminder of what an extraordinary talent he is.

    He is learning generally about bowling changes and the team that he wants around him.

    That will come with time, and he has got a year until the World Cup.
