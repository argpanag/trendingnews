JTBC has issued a sale notice and begun an earnest search for a new owner.

According to industry sources on the 9th, Ernst & Young Han Young (EY Han Young), the lead manager for JTBC’s sale, announced in a sale notice that “the sale will be conducted through open competitive bidding, with external capital raised through measures such as a paid-in capital increase via a third-party allotment and the issuance of corporate bonds.”

Letters of intent (LOIs) will be accepted for four weeks from the date of the notice, and the preferred bidder is scheduled to be selected in January 2027 after a preliminary due diligence review.

Earlier, on the 3rd, the Seoul Bankruptcy Court approved JTBC’s proposed sale notice. As a result, the M&A process ahead of approval of a rehabilitation plan has moved forward in earnest.

JTBC plans to focus this sale on attracting new capital rather than simply selling its existing shares. The aim is to secure investment funds and improve its financial structure through a paid-in capital increase and the issuance of corporate bonds, among other measures.

JTBC said, “We will conduct the sale transparently and fairly in accordance with relevant laws and the bankruptcy court’s procedures,” adding, “Through M&A, we will lay the foundation for sustainable growth and create an opportunity to strengthen our competitiveness in content and channels.”
