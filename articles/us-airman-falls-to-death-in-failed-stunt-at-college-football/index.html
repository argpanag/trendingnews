US airman falls to death in failed stunt at college football game

TheTools · 18.10.2026 22:53 · source · permalink

Serviceman plunges from stadium rafters during pre-match abseiling demonstration in Texas

An airman fell 150ft to his death during a pre-match stunt at a college American football game in Texas.

The serviceman was taking part in a abseiling demonstration when he struck the field at San Antonio’s Alamodome on Saturday in front of thousands of spectators.

Four airmen were scheduled to descend from the stadium roof on ropes about 15 minutes before kick-off of the University of Texas at San Antonio’s annual game against the US Naval Academy, which honours the armed forces.

But one airman, of the 6th Combat Training Squadron, appeared to lose control during his descent, sliding down the rope before crashing to the turf.

Footage filmed by a spectator shows the crowd gasping as he hits the ground. Shocked onlookers then rise to their feet as they realised the severity of the incident.

The man was rushed to hospital, but he later died from his injuries.

A spokesman for Nellis Air Force Base in Nevada told local media: “The incident is under investigation. More information will be released as it becomes available.”

Military officials have not released the serviceman’s identity.

“We are deeply saddened by the incident that occurred at the start of tonight’s game,” the university said in a statement.

“Our heartfelt thoughts and prayers are with the soldier who was affected, his family and his military colleagues.”

The university added it was offering psychological support to staff and students who might have been affected.

The Alamodome is a 64,000-seat indoor stadium in San Antonio, built in the early 1990s to host sporting events.

This incident follows a series of other mishaps during pre-match displays around the world in recent months.

Last December, a fireworks display at M&T Bank Stadium in Baltimore, before an NFL game between the Baltimore Ravens and the New England Patriots, went wrong when one firework shot directly down from the roof on to the playing field.

In January, during a pre-match show at the Armed Forces Bowl in Fort Worth, Texas, a parachutist became entangled in camera wires above the playing field and plunged towards the ground. He survived the incident.

Elsewhere, a flypast before an international rugby match between South Africa and New Zealand this summer prompted safety concerns after jets appeared to pass dangerously close to the top of the stadium.

Airlink, the South African airline that co-ordinated the flypast, insisted the manoeuvre had been carefully planned and that the aircraft had remained within permitted limits throughout.
