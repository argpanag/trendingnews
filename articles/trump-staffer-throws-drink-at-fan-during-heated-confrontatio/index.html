Former ESPN star and current U.S. State Department press adviser Britt McHenry reportedly tossed a drink at a heckling fan during a "heated confrontation" at Wednesday night's game between the Milwaukee Bucks and Washington Wizards.

Page Six first reported the news on Thursday, with TMZ doing the same a short time later.

A night out turns ugly

According to Page Six, McHenry was attending the game at Capital One Arena in D.C. with a male friend. The two were seated in front of a group of men who began taunting Bucks forward Kyle Kuzma, who is engaged to model and actress Winnie Harlow.

"They were saying he plays terribly because his girlfriend's ugly," a source told Page Six, adding that the "loud and obnoxious" men said other "terrible" things about Harlow.

At this point, McHenry reportedly confronted the heckler and asked him to "ease up," but instead he began harassing her instead.

"The guy then allegedly began hurling "misogynistic" comments toward the former Fox contributor and even "threw a drink on her," Page Six said.

Britt battles back

McHenry, 40, who also had been drinking, "responded by throwing a drink" at the man. TMZ apparently has some video footage of the situation.

TMZ also claims McHenry, who was booed as she left the building, "appeared unfazed by the hostile reception." She was not thrown out of the arena, the outlet says, but instead left on her own. Her male friend stayed behind and met up with her later.

McHenry, who worked at ESPN from 2014-18, was hired at her current State Department position back in September. Back in 2015, she was infamously suspended from the Worldwide Leader after getting caught berating a towing employee.

McHenry apologized for the incident at the time. In 2024, she claimed the viral video of the altercation had been "highly edited" and "highly doctored."
