The Dutch Caribbean island of Saba faces tropical storm Dolly

TheTools · 30.08.2026 13:35 · source · permalink

The Dutch Caribbean island of Saba has been preparing for the tropical storm Dolly, which was downgraded to an "open tropical wave.” On Sunday, the island is set to continue experiencing thunderstorms and heavy rainfall, which started on Saturday.

Officials on Saba asked its population of over 2000 people to use as little water as possible in the coming days. People are also strongly advised to unplug any electronic devices.

The U.S. National Hurricane Center had already warned on Friday that the remnants of the tropical storm were travelling across the Atlantic Ocean towards the Leeward Islands in the northeastern Caribbean Sea, which include Saba.

There is no freshwater source on Saba, ANP reports, which means that the island had to prepare drinking water supplies by desalinating saltwater.

Reporting by ANP and NL Times
