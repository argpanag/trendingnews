Hellebuyck makes trade request from Jets public  | NHL.com

TheTools · 03.09.2026 13:04 · πηγή · μόνιμος σύνδεσμος

Goalie says with training camp approaching, moving on from Winnipeg 'best path forward'

© Sam Hodde/Getty Images

Connor Hellebuyck has made public his desire to be traded by the Winnipeg Jets.

The three-time winner of the Vezina Trophy, which is awarded to the best goalie in the NHL, said in a statement on Thursday that he privately requested a trade “several months ago" but is now making it public with NHL training camps set to open on Sept. 16. 

"I have tried to handle the situation quietly and respectfully while giving the organization time to explore its options,” Hellebuyck said in the statement released by his agent, Ray Petkau. "With training camp approaching, I felt it was important to publicly confirm my request. My position has not changed. I believe moving on is the best path forward for me and my family."

The Jets will play their first preseason game on Sept. 19. Their first regular-season game is against the Boston Bruins at Canada Life Centre on Oct. 2.

Hellebuyck, who helped the United States win the gold medal at the 2026 Milano Cortina Olympics, was 23-23-11 with a 2.86 goals-against average and .895 save percentage in 57 NHL games last season. Winnipeg (35-35-12) finished eight points behind the Los Angeles Kings for the second wild card into the Stanley Cup Playoffs from the Western Conference.

After the season, Hellebuyck had this to say about the Jets and their ability to win the Stanley Cup: 

“It’s hard. It’s hard. Can you get the pieces that you need? Will the players come? These are always questions that you have in Winnipeg,” he said on April 18. “I’ve made it my home and I like it here, but the majority of the League doesn’t feel the same way.”

On June 26, Jets general manager Kevin Cheveldayoff said Winnipeg was listening to trade offers for Hellebuyck, but he did not provide a definitive answer as to whether his franchise goalie had requested a move.

“Certainly, as an organization we're going to listen,” Cheveldayoff said. “Everyone saw (Hellebuyck’s) press conference at the end of the year. He was passionate. Certainly, in our exit meetings we had some frank conversations as well, but again, what happens in those meetings certainly stays private.”

In response to his goaltender's public request on Thursday, Cheveldayoff said: "Connor has been open with us about his desire for a change. He has been an important part of the Winnipeg Jets organization for many years, and we have tremendous respect for what he has accomplished here. While we will continue to work through the situation in a thoughtful manner, there is no update at this time."

Hellebuyck has been one of the best goalies in the NHL since he entered the League during the 2015-16 season. 

In 2024-25, he had his best individual season, going 47-12-3 with a 2.00 GAA, .925 save percentage and eight shutouts in 63 games. He won the Hart Trophy as the NHL's most valuable player, the Vezina Trophy for the third time, and helped the Jets win the Presidents' Trophy as the team with the League's best record (56-22-4).

Hellebuyck, 33, is entering the third season of a seven-year, $59.5 million contract ($8.5 million average annual value) he signed on Oct. 9, 2023.

He has played his entire 11-season NHL career for Winnipeg, which selected him in the fifth round (No. 130) of the 2012 NHL Draft. Since entering the League, he leads goalies in games played (625) and starts (615), and is second in wins (345). Among goalies to play at least 300 games during that span, his .916 save percentage is third, and his 2.58 GAA is tied for seventh.

Hellebuyck is one of four goalies, along with Dominik Hasek, Martin Brodeur and Patrick Roy, to win the Vezina Trophy at least three times (2019-20, 2023-24, 2024-25) since NHL general managers began voting on the award in 1981.

The Jets signed goalie Stuart Skinner to a two-year, $7.5 million contract ($3.75 million AAV) on July 1. Their top goalie prospect, Domenic DiVincentiis, was 13-16-2 with a 3.03 GAA and .896 save percentage in 34 games with Manitoba of the American Hockey League last season. The 22-year-old has yet to play in the NHL. 

"I appreciate everything Winnipeg Jets fans have meant to me throughout my career and hope a resolution can be reached soon," Hellebuyck said.
