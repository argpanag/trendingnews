Hull’s Lucas Herrington: ‘Zlatan told me not to fear anyone in the Premier League’

Herrington made the headlines this summer with his impressive displays as an 18-year-old centre-back for Australia at the World Cup – before missing a decisive penalty in their shootout exit to Egypt in the last 32. Zlatan Ibrahimovic offered to have a chat with the Colorado Rapids defender after that penalty miss.

Herrington, who turns 19 today, has since sealed a move to the Premier League and sat down for an interview with Joey Lynch this week …

Zlatan said: ‘Really, don’t fear anyone; I’m here for a reason, so just go enjoy it.’ It’s a great league; he played in it. His message was just to go have fun, and don’t fear anything. Go, work hard, be yourself … because it’s a great experience to play in the Premier League.

It’s fair to say Aston Villa are have operated in a totally different transfer market to Hull this summer – they’ve spent over £250m and still made a healthy profit after the sales of Morgan Rogers and Ollie Watkins. Hull have shelled out over £150m on virtually an entire other team but not recouped nearly as much.

Preamble

Life is pretty good for Sergej Jakirovic right now – his unfancied Hull team have won their first two games back in the Premier League and yesterday he signed a new two-year deal as head coach. In the opposing dugout today is Unai Emery, already bordering on beleaguered, who is seeking to put a turbulent transfer window behind him and get Aston Villa off the mark for the season.

If it wasn’t for Tottenham and alphabetical order, Villa would have been bottom of the league after losing their opening two fixtures without scoring a goal. Emiliano Martínez, Morgan Rogers and Ollie Watkins are gone but there is hope in the arrivals of fresh faces like Zion Suzuki, Nicolas Jackson and the exciting teenager Ibrahim Mbaye – all three are involved today.

Villa’s first away trip of the season was a 4-0 drubbing at Brighton – how will they fare in their second? Kick-off is at 5.30pm (BST). If you want to get involved, feel free to send me an email.
