Having a whale of a time with Céline Dion | Brief letters

TheTools · 20.11.2026 16:42 · source · permalink

Rival interpretations | Orwell’s accent | Inappropriate questions | Bezos and Bond

The explosive surfacing of Iceland’s humpback whales at the opening bars of Céline Dion’s My Heart Will Go On (Country diary, 18 November) admits two rival interpretations. Either cetaceans possess an unsuspected weakness for late-1990s maritime melodrama, or those violent exhalations represented an urgent evolutionary plea to turn the speakers down before the key change.
David Woodard
Conductor, Tampere, Finland

Your review of Mona Chalabi’s book Ten Lives (11 November) refers to “her … criticism of George Orwell for conducting a narcissistic poverty safari with Down and Out in Paris and London” and says Chalabi describes him as “a rich boy who changed his clothing and, presumably, his accent, to pass as a ‘tramp’”. In The Road to Wigan Pier, Orwell makes clear that he made no attempt to disguise his accent during his time as a tramp.
Campbell McGregor
Glasgow

In your Barbara Kingsolver Q&A (14 November), she was asked, as interviewees often are: “How often do you have sex?” Isn’t it time to deem this question inappropriate?
Cynthia Thomas
Reading, Berkshire

Jeff Bezos says he was “heavily involved” in choosing the new James Bond (Report, 17 November). His more obvious role would have been picking the villain.
Derrick Cameron
Stoke-on-Trent
