Kick or Treat tournament draws teams from five states to Bowling Green

TheTools · 26.10.2026 04:46 · source · permalink

What This Story Is About

  • Sky Soccer Club held its annual Kick or Treat costume soccer tournament this weekend, drawing more than 100 youth teams from five states to Bowling Green.

Why It Matters

  • The tournament gives young players, including those as young as U8, early competitive exposure while driving business to local hotels and restaurants.

What Happens Next

  • The Tournament will return next year.

For Context

  • The Kick or Treat youth soccer tournament has been held in Bowling Green for over 30 years.

BOWLING GREEN, Ky. (WBKO) - Sky Soccer Club hosted its annual Kick or Treat tournament this weekend, drawing more than 100 youth soccer teams from five states to Bowling Green.

Players competed in costume during the event, the club’s largest youth soccer tournament of the year.

Tournament grows each year

“We’re consistently over 130 teams now. We had one record tournament a couple years ago. There was 165 teams. So we’re bursting at the seams. We’re using every field we can get a hold of in Bowling Green. This time we’re lucky enough to be able to host it on our brand new turf,” said Corey Coons, tournament director.

The tournament includes a U8 division for younger players. Sky Soccer Club fields two of its own teams in the boys division and two in the girls division.

“We actually have a U8 division where we have some of the youngest kids be able to start competing at tournaments. And we have two of our own teams in the boys division and two of our own teams in the girls division. They get that first exposure so that they can see whether they want to join us on the academy side in the future and this be a major part of their lives,” Coons said.

Local businesses see increased activity

Local vendors and businesses set up at the fields during the weekend as families traveled to Bowling Green for the tournament.

“It’s a huge impact to Bowling Green in terms of hotels, in terms of restaurant use, in terms of basically anything that these people want to do while they’re here that they don’t have in their hometown. They’re doing it while they’re here at our tournament,” Coons said.

The Kick or Treat youth soccer tournament has been held in Bowling Green for over 30 years.
