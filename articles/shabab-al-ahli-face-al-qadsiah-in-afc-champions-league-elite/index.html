Shabab Al Ahli Face Al Qadsiah in AFC Champions League Elite

TheTools · 24.11.2026 16:45 · source · permalink

Shabab Al Ahli will welcome Al Qadsiah to Al-Rashid Stadium in Dubai on November 24, 2026, for a 2026-27 AFC Champions League Elite league-stage encounter. Both clubs are targeting a deep run in the competition, with the hosts looking to build on their recent results and the Saudi side seeking to maintain its unbeaten record. According to The Stats Zone, Shabab Al Ahli have recorded three wins and one defeat in their opening four league-stage matches.

The Emirati club currently occupy fourth place in the UAE Pro League under head coach Andre Jardine. The hosts head into the continental fixture after beating Ajman 3-1 in their most recent outing. That result provides the Dubai-based side with positive momentum ahead of a challenging meeting with their Saudi opponents.

Al Qadsiah have made an equally strong start to their AFC Champions League Elite campaign. Managed by Brendan Rodgers, the Saudi club have collected two victories and two draws from four matches, remaining unbeaten in the competition.

Their most recent continental statement came earlier in November, when Al Qadsiah secured a commanding 5-0 victory over Al Gharafa. The result strengthened their position in the league-stage campaign. Al Qadsiah are also competing near the top of the Saudi Pro League title race, according to The Stats Zone. Their domestic form adds further significance to the encounter against Shabab Al Ahli.

The match is scheduled to begin at 4:00 p.m. on November 24, 2026, at Al-Rashid Stadium in Dubai. The fixture brings together two sides with strong early records in the 2026-27 continental competition.
