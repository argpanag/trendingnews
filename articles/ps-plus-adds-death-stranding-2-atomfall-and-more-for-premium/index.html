PS Plus Adds Death Stranding 2, Atomfall and more for premium tiers | TheSixthAxis

TheTools · 10.11.2026 20:18 · source · permalink

Sony has confirmed that Death Stranding 2 will be coming to PS Plus Premium, along with a handful of popular newer games from the past few years.

These aren’t to be confused with the complementary games every PlayStation Plus subscriber gains access to as part of the standard subscription. In case you missed it, November’s freebies include zombie-slaying sequel, Dead Island 2, as well as Tomb Raider IV- Remastered, and Monster Train 2.

PS Plus Extra Games for November 2026

Now, if you’re subscribed to the pricier Extra tier, you’ll unlock the following games this month:

  • Death Stranding 2 | PS5
  • Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii | PS5, PS4
  • Atomfall | PS5, PS4
  • Dragon Quest III HD-2D Remake | PS5
  • Trepang2 | PS5
  • MotoGP 26 | PS5
  • Hell Let Loose | PS5
  • Cricket 26 – The Official Game of the Ashes | PS5, PS4

Meanwhile, if you’re a PlayStation Plus Premium member, you’ll get all the above as well as a couple of bonus classics including:

  • Soul Reaver 2 | PS5, PS4
  • Freedom Wars Remastered | PS5, PS4

Within Sony’s latest blog update, it announced that the dedicated PlayStation Plus PC app will be go offline on December 10, 2026. Here’s the official statement:

“PlayStation believes in providing high-quality experiences to our players, and we regularly review offerings to ensure we are focusing on experiences our members value most. With a very small number of eligible PlayStation Plus Premium members (less than 0.4%) using the PlayStation Plus app on PC, making this change allows us to focus on enhancing high-quality experiences that more of our members want to use. As a result, the PlayStation Plus cloud streaming app on PC will no longer be available.”

Having booted up the app fairly recently, it’s no surprise to see that Sony is preparing to wind it down. Unlike the cloud streaming options available on PlayStation consoles (including the PlayStation Portal) the PC app offered a fairly limited library.
