Peyton Manning's Son Marshall Has CFB Offers From Top Programs Amid Freshman HSFB Season

TheTools · 09.10.2026 08:45 · source · permalink

Marshall Manning, the son of NFL legend Peyton Manning, is only a high-school freshman at Chattanooga's Baylor School. But he apparently is already receiving plenty of interest from a number of prominent college programs.

According to Brendan Sonnone of 247 Sports (h/t Anna Lazarus Caplan of People), Manning already has offers from Miami, Ohio State, Purdue and Missouri State.

The Manning football legacy—namely at quarterback—is well-documented.

Archie Manning is an Ole Miss legend and was a two-time Pro Bowler in the NFL. Peyton Manning is a Pro Football Hall of Famer, a five-time NFL MVP, two-time champion, seven-time first-team All-Pro and a 14-time Pro Bowler. Eli Manning will have an interesting argument for the Hall of Fame after winning two titles—he was the game's MVP both times—and being a four-time Pro Bowler during his career. And Peyton and Eli's nephew, Arch Manning, is currently the starting quarterback at Texas.

So he has plenty of excellent mentors when it comes to playing the quarterback position (and navigating the massive expectations that come with carrying the Manning last name).

For now, however, he's just a freshman in high school, albeit one who apparently already has the offers rolling in.
