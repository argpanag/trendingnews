Through Edificio Rostower Socimi, Lionel Messi completed the purchase of four star Hotel Tredós and adjacent rustic land in Naut Aran, Lleida.

Located in Spain Val d Aran ski region, the property offers thirty nine rooms and suites alongside operations tied to Meliá.

The deal builds on a real estate portfolio spanning Barcelona, the Balearic Islands, Paris, London, and Hincha restaurants.

Lionel Messi continues expanding his business footprint off the pitch with another major real estate move in Spain; through his investment vehicle Edificio Rostower Socimi, the Argentine star finalized the purchase on November 24 of Hotel Tredós, a four star property in Naut Aran in the Val d Aran region, along with adjoining rustic land. The deal deepens his commitment to the Catalan Pyrenees, an established mountain and ski destination where his MiM Hotels brand already holds a presence.

Meliá partnership and profile of the Val d Aran property

The acquisition aligns with a broader push to professionalize hotel management following Edificio Rostower agreement with Meliá Hotels International to operate six MiM Hotels properties under The Meliá Collection starting November 1, 2025. Marketed as Meliá de Tredós, the mountain hotel features thirty nine rooms and suites with valley views, a restaurant, an outdoor pool and ski oriented amenities. Meliá had managed the site since late two thousand twenty two and bought the property earlier in two thousand twenty six prior to the sale.

Barcelona office project, global homes and dining ventures

Edificio Rostower has kept up steady deal flow across Spanish real estate, including its recent acquisition of the former Via Wagner commercial arcades near Barcelona Turó Park for roughly eleven point five million euros. Measuring around three thousand square meters and unused for more than three decades after a two thousand one fire, that building is slated for full renovation into offices for a banking institution. The company portfolio also spans retail and office spaces, residences in Barcelona, Ibiza, London and Paris, and Hincha restaurants created with chef Nandu Jubany.

Financial backdrop and long term real estate strategy

Filings covering fiscal year two thousand twenty five showed Edificio Rostower generating more than seven point four million euros in revenue, up slightly from seven point one three six million euros a year earlier. While operating expenses topped four point six million euros and asset amortization exceeded eight point six million euros on the way to a seven point seventy three million euro net loss, high occupancy across commercial holdings supports its long term asset strategy.
