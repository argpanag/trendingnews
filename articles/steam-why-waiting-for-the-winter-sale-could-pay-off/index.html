Steam: Why waiting for the Winter Sale could pay off

TheTools · 26.11.2026 23:34 · source · permalink

Steam is once again skipping a dedicated Black Friday sale in 2026. Instead, its deals page will feature personalised deals and all discounted games with a suitably themed look.

On this page

  1. 1 Update, November 26, 2026, 7:05 p.m. CET

The year is AD 2026. The whole world is occupied by Black Friday deals. The whole world? No! One store populated by indomitable Valvians still holds out against Black Friday. And so, once again this year, Steam is going without a dedicated Black Friday sale page. There are still game deals and discounts, though. The next traditional Steam sale, however, does not start until 17 December 2026, when Valve once again invites players to its ‘Winter Sale’.

Steam’s developer site(Steamworks) states that deals around the big bargain Friday will be highlighted on the ‘Specials & Events’ page with a ‘Black Friday-style’ theme. For players, this means the deals page will show personalised recommendations and all discounted games rather than curated deals. These discounts come from developers and publishers and are not a Steam promotion.

Steam’s standard discount cooldown still needs to be taken into account: as a rule, there must be at least 30 days between two discount promotions. However, the four major seasonal sales are exempt from this rule. Games discounted for Black Friday can therefore also take part in the Steam Winter Sale. Whether publishers will offer bigger discounts on their games in December remains to be seen.

When do the Black Friday deals start on Steam?

Although Black Friday itself falls on Friday, 27 November, the special promotion starts on Thursday, 26 November 2026, at 19:00 German time and ends on Monday, 30 November (also at 19:00). What will be on offer is not yet known. It is worth checking the discounts page or your own wishlist in the Steam store between now and next Monday.

Update, November 26, 2026, 7:05 p.m. CET

Steam's Black Friday deals are now live. Despite the absence of a dedicated Black Friday Sale, numerous games are available at discounted prices, including some major releases from recent months. Star Wars: Galactic Racer is available at a 20 percent discount, Silent Hill f at 72 percent off, and Red Dead Redemption 2 at 75 percent off. Detroit: Become Human is even available at a 90 percent discount.

Since the discounts are set by individual publishers and developers, their duration may vary. Star Wars: Galactic Racer, for example, remains discounted until December 9, even though Steam's Black Friday promotion ends on November 30. Those hoping for even bigger discounts will have to wait until the Steam Winter Sale begins on December 17.
