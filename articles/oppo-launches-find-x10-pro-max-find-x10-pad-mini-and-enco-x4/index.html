Oppo launches Find X10 Pro Max, Find X10, Pad Mini and Enco X4 in India | Photo Credit: Special Arrangement

Oppo on Thursday (October 22, 2026) launched the Find X10 Pro Max and Find X10 smartphones in India. The Chinese smartphone maker also unveiled the Pad Mini and Enco X4 wireless earbuds.

With the Find X10 series, Oppo has officially rolled out its new operating skin, ColorOS 17, based on Android 17 in India. Gradually, it will arrive in other Oppo devices.

The Find X10 Pro Max has a 6.78 inch LTPO AMOLED, with up to 144 Hz refresh rate, Dolby Vision, HDR10+, and 3600 nits peak brightness.

Find X10 Pro Max ships with an 8,000 mAh battery, supported by an 80W wired charging, and 10W reverse wireless charging. It is IP68 and IP69 rated for water resistance.

Find X10 Pro Max runs on Mediatek Dimensity 9600 Pro chipset with up to 12 GB storage and 512 GB memory.

Find X10 Pro Max sports a 200 MP main camera, a 200 MP telephoto lens with 3x optical zoom, and a 200 MP ultrawide lens. It has a 50 MP front camera.

Find X10 has a 6.59 inch LTPO AMOLED, with up to 144 Hz refresh rate, Dolby Vision, HDR10+, and 3600 nits peak brightness.

Find X10 also ships with an 8,000 mAh battery, supported by an 80W wired charging and is IP68 and IP69 rated for water resistance.

Find X10 runs on Mediatek Dimensity 9600M chipset with up to 12 GB storage and 512 GB memory. It operates on ColorOS 17 based on Android 17.

Find X10 Pro Max sports a 200 MP main camera, a 200 MP telephoto, and a 50 MP ultrawide lens. It also has a 50 MP front camera.

Oppo Enco X4 comes with Dynaudio-tuned sound, adaptive ANC that adjusts active noise cancellation and transparency levels based on both ambient noise and user preferences.

It features dual-driver acoustic system of an 11mm TRF diaphragm woofer with a 6mm planar diaphragm tweeter. It offers five exclusive sound profiles. It support lossless audio, and is Hi-Res Audio wireless certified.

Enco X4 comes with a 3-microphone system and bone-conduction VPU for voice clarity. It supports Bluetooth 6.1 and above devices, dual connection.

Enco X4 supports live translation in real-time translation. It is IP55 rated as well for dust and water resistance.

Oppo Enco X4 claims up to 13 hours of music playback on a single charge, and up to 53 hours with the charging case.

Oppo Pad Mini has an 8.8 inch display with up to 144 Hz refresh rate, supported by stylus. It runs on Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 chipset and features a 13 MP rear camera and an 8 MP front camera.

It ships with an 8,000 mAh battery, supported by a 67W charger inside the box. The tablet operates on ColorOS 16 based on Android 16 out of the box.

Oppo Find X10 Pro Max sells at ₹1,99,999 for the 12 GB/512 GB model. The Find X10 starts at ₹1,39,999 for the 12 GB/256 GB variant. The 12 GB/512 GB costs ₹1,69,999. They go on sale from October 29.

Oppo Pad Mini comes at ₹94,999 for the 8 GB/256 GB model. It will sell Lavender, on Flipkart and the Oppo online store starting October 29.

Oppo Enco X4 has been priced at ₹19,999.
