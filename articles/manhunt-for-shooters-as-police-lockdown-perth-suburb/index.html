Manhunt for shooters as police lockdown Perth suburb

TheTools · 12.09.2026 23:07 · source · permalink

A man has been shot and areas surrounding the Perth suburb of Hamilton Hill are in lockdown as police look for the culprits.

The shooting happened at a house in Owen Plane, Hamilton Hill, sometime before 4.35am on Sunday.

A large contingent of police are at the house and searching the area for two men, said to have been dressed in all black.

The shooting victim was taken to hospital by ambulance and is said to have serious injuries.

Police have asked people to stay away from the search zone and anyone who lives within to lock their doors and windows and remain inside.

Anyone who sights the two men or sees any suspicious behaviour is urged to call triple zero immediately.
