CNA Explains: Could Singapore ever become a car-free city?

TheTools · 03.10.2026 06:15 · source · permalink

A stretch of Joo Chiat is going car-free for six hours from 3.30pm on Oct 3. It is part of a weekend festival of food, fitness and culture to mark World Car-Free Day. But can the idea of giving the streets back to the people work on a bigger scale across Singapore? Gladys Wee takes a closer look.
