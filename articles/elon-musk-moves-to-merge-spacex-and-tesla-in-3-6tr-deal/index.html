Elon Musk moves to merge SpaceX and Tesla in $3.6tr deal

TheTools · 24.10.2026 19:00 · source · permalink

NEW YORK/LONDON: Elon Musk is preparing for the “AI singularity” by attempting to fold SpaceX and Tesla into a single company spanning rockets, satellites, internet service, chatbots, social media, robots and cars, in what would be his most audacious deal yet.

The world’s richest man said that both companies had set up special committees of independent directors to study a tie-up, which he called “a good idea.”

If completed, the combined group would be worth about $3.6 trillion at current prices, placing it among the world’s five most valuable companies alongside Nvidia, Apple, Alphabet and Microsoft.

The merged entity would employ more than 155,000 people and generate a combined $153bn in revenue this year, according to analyst forecasts compiled by Visible Alpha.
