PAYNE COUNTY, Okla. — The Oklahoma Highway Patrol said an 81-year-old woman is dead after a crash in Payne County on Wednesday.

Patricia Zirk from Stillwater stopped at a stop sign on southbound Redlands Road, at the intersection of West 6th Avenue, around 10:32 a.m.

OHP said Zirk failed to yield before pulling onto West 6th Avenue, and was hit by another vehicle traveling westbound.

Zirk was pronounced dead by medical personnel. The driver of the other vehicle was injured during the crash.
