Several killed, wounded in after Dutch coach crashes in Switzerland

TheTools · 10.09.2026 18:48 · source · permalink

Several people were killed and others were wounded after a tour bus with Dutch license plates crashed in Susch, Graubünden, on Thursday in Susch, Graubünden, on Thursday evening. Dutch travel agent association ANVR confirmed that it was in contact with victims’ families, the organization told ANP.

The Dutch company in charge of the bus provides organized tours and holidays. "This is terrible,” the company said when contracted.

“We hope that there will be more clarity soon, but now it is primarily up to the authorities to investigate what happened,” the company said to Swiss media outlet 20 Minuten.

"Our thoughts are with all those affected by this accident."

"According to our information, some people are being cared for and treated on site, while others are in hospital,” the ANVR said. “Unfortunately, we cannot provide any further details at this time."
