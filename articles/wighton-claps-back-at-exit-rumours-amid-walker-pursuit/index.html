Wighton claps back at exit rumours amid Walker pursuit

TheTools · 15.11.2026 19:44 · source · permalink
  • Sport
  • NRL
  • NRL

November 15, 2026 - 7.44pm

Rabbitohs star Jack Wighton has responded to rumours of an early exit from the club, after he was unwittingly dragged into the Sam Walker saga.

Despite distancing themselves from the Roosters halfback initially, South Sydney have emerged as the front-runners to land Walker’s signature.

Roosters halfback Sam Walker. Getty Images

However, the issue for Souths may lie with getting Walker on their books and under the salary cap for 2027, should he leave Bondi before his deal ends.

It has prompted speculation about a number of current Bunnies players possibly being shopped to rival NRL clubs or the Super League.

Wighton’s name has been mentioned, largely due to his $900,000 salary at the Rabbitohs for next season, which is the fourth and final year of his contract.

Retired journalist Phil Rothfield revealed on The Bye Round podcast on Sunday that Wighton could be heading overseas to join Hull KR.

However, in a quick and cheeky social media reply, the 33-year-old seemed to indicate his future at Souths hadn’t been decided yet.

“How good’s life, trying to enjoy my Sunday and every man and their dog’s trying to sack me,” Wighton wrote on his Instagram story.

Campbell Graham and Latrell Mitchell are the two other names who have been touted as potential casualties of the Walker pursuit.

There is no guarantee Walker will even depart the Roosters early, with fresh reports surfacing on Sunday that coach Trent Robinson wants to keep the premiership-winning playmaker on deck for 2027.

Walker’s contract standoff has been the biggest story of the off-season, after he knocked back a multi-million dollar extension, angering chairman Nick Politis who pulled the offer and has refused to return to the negotiating table.

Souths officials met with Walker and his camp over the weekend in Brisbane, with the PNG Chiefs and Bulldogs also linked with the gun halfback.
