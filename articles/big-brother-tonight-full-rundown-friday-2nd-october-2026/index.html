BIG BROTHER TONIGHT: FULL RUNDOWN, FRIDAY 2nd OCTOBER 2026

TheTools · 02.10.2026 13:50 · source · permalink

Here's a full rundown of what to expect in tonight's episode of Big Brother, which continues on ITV2 and ITVX.

OMAR AND PAULINE TAKE ON ‘THE MILLION POUND SHOP’

Big Brother gathers the Housemates on the sofa and announces, “Two of you will now have the opportunity to win back the shopping list for the House. You’ll be able to order food for the week ahead. You must now decide amongst you which two Housemates will take on this task.”

After deliberating, the Housemates decide that Omar and Pauline will take on the challenge.

The pair head to the task room to play ‘The Million Pound Shop’.

Behind them are shelves packed with groups of food that Housemates would normally find on their shopping list, including meat, fish, eggs, biscuits, fizzy drinks, pasta and coconut water.

But there’s a catch…To win each food group, Omar and Pauline must correctly answer a series of questions. For each question, they must place the food groups against the answer they think is correct - A or B.

Not sure? They can split the items across both panels if they’re undecided. But once the correct answer is revealed, any items placed on the incorrect panel will drop - meaning those foods will be unavailable to the House.

And just when they think the challenge is over, there’s one final question…

Their answer will determine whether the Housemates receive an economy or luxury shopping budget for the week ahead.

Will Omar and Pauline rise to the challenge?
