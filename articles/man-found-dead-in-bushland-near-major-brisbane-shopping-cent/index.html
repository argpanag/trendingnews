Man found dead in bushland near major Brisbane shopping centre

An investigation has been launched into the sudden death of a man found dead in bushland in Brisbane’s north.

The 48-year-old man was found in Seventh Brigade Park in Chermside just before midday on Sunday, sparking a police investigation.

The 48-year-old man was found in Seventh Brigade Park in Chermside just before midday on Sunday, October 18, 2026, sparking a police investigation. 9News

It’s understood his wounds were extensive, covering various parts of his body.

Officers and detectives were seen heading in and out of the area, which borders one of Brisbane’s largest shopping centres, carrying evidence bags as they tried to piece together what happened.

It’s understood there were several people sleeping rough in a number of hammocks and tents nearby.
