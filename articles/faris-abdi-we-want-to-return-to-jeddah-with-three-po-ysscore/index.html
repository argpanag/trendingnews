Faris Abdi: We want to return to Jeddah with three po… | YSscores

TheTools · 13.09.2026 18:45 · source · permalink

Faris Abdi, Al-Ittihad player, has expressed his team's readiness for the upcoming match against Al-Shamal, confirming that the Tigers are aiming to return to Jeddah with a victory.

Abdi stated during the press conference before the match: "The game will not be easy, but we are ready to deliver the required level and achieve victory to collect the three points."

The Al-Ittihad player emphasized the importance of a strong start in the AFC Champions League, stating that the players' goal is to make the Al-Ittihad fans happy.

Abdi added that his teammates and the coaching staff helped him adapt quickly within the team, noting that everyone is working with a united spirit to achieve the best results.

Al-Ittihad begin their journey in the AFC Champions League against Al-Shamal on Monday evening at Al Bayt Stadium in Al Khor, in the first round of the group stage.

Ittihad are aiming for a positive start in the continental tournament, after Abdi emphasized the team's desire to return to Jeddah with three points, rather than just participating in the competition.
