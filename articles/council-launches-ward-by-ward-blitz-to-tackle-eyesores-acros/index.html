Council launches ward-by-ward blitz to tackle eyesores across St Helens

TheTools · 10.09.2026 13:29 · source · permalink

A NEW programme is calling on residents to help transform their neighbourhoods.

St Helens Borough Council has launched an "area blitz" scheme, inviting residents to report problem hotspots in their communities to help shape targeted clean-up operations.

The scheme brings together council teams, councillors and community groups to deliver intensive environmental improvements.

Councillor Michael Gibson, deputy leader of St Helens Borough Council and cabinet member for waste and environment, said: "Everyone wants to feel proud of where they live.

"That means when they look around their community they want to feel safe and see a clean, welcoming environment.

"This way of working, through community blitzes, is a way for us to empower you.

"We want you to work with us identify and to address the spots that need some care.

"It's a chance for everyone to come together to make a difference through a new way of community working."

Each blitz will target a different ward in the borough, with work including gully clearing, graffiti removal, hedge and grass trimming, litter picking, sign cleaning, and kerb and pothole repairs.

Council teams will review each area ahead of the blitz, and residents are encouraged to report locations in need of attention.

The first blitz will begin in West Park on Monday, September 21.

The council expects the programme to continue into spring 2027, covering each ward in turn.

Residents can find out when the blitz is coming to their area by following the council on social media or subscribing to the St Helens Borough Council Residents Newsletter.

You can report any issues in your area at www.sthelens.gov.uk/report-it.

The initiative is part of St Helens Borough Council’s ongoing commitment to environmental improvement and community engagement.

Residents are encouraged to take part and help shape the future of their neighbourhoods.
