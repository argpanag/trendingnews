Sir Tom Jones announces Wales gigs after cancelling shows

TheTools · 17.11.2026 12:53 · source · permalink

Sir Tom Jones cancelled or rescheduled four dates on the US-leg of his tour last month due to illness

  • Published
    16 November 2026

Sir Tom Jones has announced two headline shows in Wales just weeks after he was forced to cancel or reschedule a number of tour dates due to illness.

The 86-year-old Welsh music icon's homecoming, as part of his Come Gather Round world tour, will see him perform at Cardiff Castle on 30 May, followed by a fully-seated show at Llangollen Pavilion in Denbighshire on 28 June.

Heather Small will feature as a special guest at the Cardiff show, while soul band Stone Foundation will also join the bill at both concerts.

It is a return to home soil for the legendary singer from Pontypridd, Rhondda Cynon Taf, who last performed in Wales in summer 2025.

Last month, Sir Tom was forced to cancel or reschedule shows in the US-leg of the world tour, due to "a bad case of flu".

He said at the time that he was "truly sorry" for the disappointment caused to fans, later adding he feared his life was "over" after being admitted to hospital.

Tickets for his Welsh gigs go on sale on 20 November, and the sets will feature tracks from his 2021 album Surrounded By Time, alongside fan favourites including It's Not Unusual, What's New Pussycat?, Delilah and Green, Green Grass of Home.

Nick Saunders, founder of promoter Depot Live, said: "Tom Jones is a true Welsh icon, so it's a real pleasure to welcome him back to Cardiff Castle.

"He's one of Wales' greatest-ever musical exports, and there's always a real sense of pride in seeing him perform on home soil."

Dave Danford, artistic director of the Llangollen International Musical Eisteddfod, added: "His show here in 2024 was one of the most memorable evenings in the venue's history, and we've been trying to find a way to get him back here ever since."
