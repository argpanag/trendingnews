The latest EDGE review scores for issue #428 (November 2026 issue) have now been released. A number of Nintendo-related games are covered, including Onimusha: Way of the Sword.

Here’s the full roundup:

Below is excerpt from the Onimusha review:

It isn’t ruinous, but you can’t shake the sense that there’s a lethal 15-hour romp in Way of the Sword, cosplaying as a 25-hour game. Of course, for the more Issen-minded players, upgrades can be ignored and adventures can be tackled with an abandon that’d make Musashi proud. More regular samurai will want to engage with the stodgy safety net, driven by the desire to both see and survive the next best-in-class boss encounter. A shame to have to quibble about excess hours when few games do split seconds better than this.
