Fortnite Treasure Hunters Season: Hidden Mythics, Secret Loot, New Skins, and Upcoming Changes

TheTools · 16.11.2026 01:42 · source · permalink
  • Home
  • News
  • Fortnite Treasure Hunters Season: Hidden Mythics, Secret Loot, New Skins, and Upcoming Changes

Lina Johansson

summary

Fortnite's Treasure Hunters season introduces hidden Mythic weapons, the exclusive Sweet Dream Sprite, secret chests, map Easter eggs, and new celebrity collaborations. Players looking to enhance their experience can also buy FN Top Up to access additional in-game content and cosmetics. Upcoming golden rewards, Fortnite Crew changes, the rumored Radcow mode, and the December 5 live event add even more excitement as the season approaches its conclusion.

The Secret Sweet Dream Sprite Unlocks Three Mythic Weapons

One of the biggest hidden discoveries involves the new Sprite collection. After collecting all 22 regular Sprites, players can use a Lucky Locator to unlock the mysterious Sweet Dream Sprite. This special collectible is based on the original Dream Sprite design from Chapter 3, but it cannot be obtained through normal drops.

The Sweet Dream Sprite has another major advantage. Once upgraded to Level 5, it explodes and grants players one random Mythic weapon. There are three exclusive possibilities:

· Amplified Bundle Bus AR: A powerful assault rifle capable of dealing heavy damage to opponents.

· Head Hunter Gilded Dragoon Shotgun: Increases maximum headshot damage to 225.

· Throttle Tac SMG: Fires slightly slower than other SMGs but compensates with higher damage and improved accuracy.

This makes the Sweet Dream Sprite particularly valuable for players looking to secure powerful equipment without relying entirely on traditional loot sources.

New Collaborations Bring Tedto, IShowSpeed, and Lil Uzi to Fortnite

Fortnite has also expanded its collaboration lineup with several recognizable personalities. Tedto has finally arrived after being leaked for approximately a year. The collaboration includes a Perto Back Bling inspired by a popular fandom meme and a Baguette Bonker Pickaxe referencing Tedto's love of bread.

IShowSpeed has also returned with a second wave of cosmetics, this time featuring a Luffy-inspired outfit based on his favorite anime, One Piece. His pickaxe references Cristiano Ronaldo through the iconic number seven, while his signature backflip animation provides another recognizable detail for fans.

Meanwhile, Lil Uzi Vert's cosmetics feature several interesting design adjustments. His forehead tattoo, normally displaying the word "Faith," has been changed to "Save Fortnite." His second outfit recreates his appearance on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon, including the shoes, wings, and Sega jacket.

ASAP Rocky's helicopter outfit also contains a subtle modification. His usual chest tattoo has been replaced with an image of Peely, adding another Fortnite-themed detail to the collaboration.

Hidden Chests, Waterfall Secrets, and Map Easter Eggs

The latest update introduces another rare loot opportunity through Developer Chests. These extremely uncommon containers can drop multiple current Exotic weapons simultaneously, including the returning Super Flint-Knock.

Players can also discover several secret chest locations. Activating the lighthouse torch in the southern part of the island reveals two Rare Chests through sweeping beams of light. However, players must collect them quickly before they disappear. Another waterfall hidden in the desert provides a free Supply Drop every match.

Other returning and newly discovered Easter eggs include the Frog Family's garden near Lantern Landing, humorous advertisements scattered across the island, and the Buoy Boy fishing store at Busy Bay, where Big Dill and The Broad have started a new business.

Fortnite's Upcoming Golden Rewards and Crew Changes

Leakers have also revealed several upcoming Treasure Hunters rewards. Players can reportedly look forward to golden cosmetics arriving across multiple weeks:

· Week 3: Golden Meiku outfit and Chevrolet Corvette.

· Week 4: Golden Tedto outfit and Lamborghini Urus.

· Week 5: Golden Treasure Hunter Capex and another opportunity to obtain the Golden G-Wagon.

However, Fortnite Crew subscribers are facing significant changes. The OG Pass will no longer be included following the current season, while December 2026's Neco outfit is expected to be the final monthly Crew Pack. Starting in January, subscribers will no longer receive a new cosmetic set every month.

In exchange, Epic Games plans to offer increased V-Bucks rewards based on subscription duration, potentially reaching 1,600 per month, alongside a 20% XP boost.

New Game Mode Radcow and the December 5 Live Event

Another major development is a rumored game mode called Radcow. According to leaks, this could become a fully developed experience alongside Battle Royale, Reload, and OG.

The mode reportedly includes Squads and Zero Build options, Sprite extraction mechanics, augments, rebooting, and movement abilities from recent chapters. Its weapon selection may also feature equipment from previous seasons, including the Chains of Hades, Zeus' Thunderbolt, and Stormbeast's Claws. A dedicated map is another rumored feature.

Meanwhile, Fortnite's current storyline is approaching a major conclusion. The mysterious Seven member at Dusty has completed the rocket assembly, while the rifts in the sky are now active. The Chapter-ending live event is scheduled for December 5, 2026, coinciding with the conclusion of Fortnite OG.

Players can also earn additional free Festival cosmetics by completing weekly quests and participating in music activities.

More Secrets Make Treasure Hunters One of Fortnite's Busiest Seasons

Beyond its major updates, Treasure Hunters contains countless smaller discoveries. Charlotte's conversations reveal amusing details about her sword Daniel, while Chopper has become the first Sidekick to feature subtitles for its Japanese voice lines. Players can also unlock a special Chopper emoticon by spending enough time with him. For those looking to enjoy more Fortnite content and expand their cosmetic collections, cheap FN Top Up offers another way to enhance their in-game experience.

There are even unused Sprite variations, including a Curse version of Kelper that Epic reportedly withheld because combining its damage effect with Kelper's water healing ability created an unintended interaction.

With secret Mythics, hidden loot locations, celebrity collaborations, upcoming golden rewards, and a major live event approaching, Fortnite's Treasure Hunters season continues to reward players who explore beyond the usual objectives. Many of its best discoveries are still easy to miss, making every match an opportunity to uncover something new.
