Grand Slam of Darts results: Luke Humphries and Michael van Gerwen reach last 16

TheTools · 19.11.2026 20:13 · source · permalink

Luke Humphries is seeded second for the Grand Slam of Darts

  • Published
    17 November 2026

Luke Humphries and Michael van Gerwen booked their places in the last 16 of the Grand Slam of Darts with victories in their final group matches.

Humphries beat fellow Englishman Joe Cullen 5-2 despite averaging 98.67 to his opponent's 103.22.

The former world champion will now face compatriot Tom Sykes, the lowest-ranked player remaining in the tournament, after he defeated Krzysztof Ratajski 5-1 to top Group J.

Van Gerwen was well below his best as he scraped past Sweden's Jeffrey de Graaf 5-4.

The Dutchman missed several match darts at 4-3 up, allowing De Graaf to force a deciding leg.

However, Van Gerwen regrouped to seal victory and set up a last-16 meeting with James Wade.

"It wasn't a good game at all. We were both under pressure and that didn't do us any favours," Van Gerwen said.

"Sometimes you don't do what you want to do. That's frustrating because I know I am capable of a lot more."

Elsewhere, Wade, Gian van Veen and Nathan Aspinall all made light work of their opponents to secure their places in the knockout stage.
