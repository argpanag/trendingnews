Anak Krakatau Status Lowered to Alert, Residents Barred Within 2-Km Radius

TheTools · 22.09.2026 04:36 · source · permalink

Although the status of Mount Krakatau has been downgraded to Alert (Level II), the public is not permitted to enter a radius of two kilometers from the center of the volcanic activity.

22 Sep 2026 11:36 WIB · Lingkungan

Share

Copy Link

By Vina Oktavia

BANDAR LAMPUNG, KOMPAS - The status of the activity of Mount Anak Krakatau has been lowered from Alert (Level III) to Warnings (Level II) starting Monday (21/9/2026) at 18:30 WIB. This change in status was determined after the Geological Agency conducted an analysis and evaluation regarding the volcanic activity of the mountain.

Previously, the status of Mount Anak Krakatau was set to Alert on July 2, 2026, at 16:30 WIB due to increased volcanic seismic activity and deformation. Since then, Mount Anak Krakatau has recorded 224 occurrences up to September 3, 2026.

On September 4, 2026, from 11:07 PM WIB to September 6, 2026, at 12:04 AM WIB, a continuous eruption occurred for 25 hours, marking the peak of the volcanic eruption. Following this series of eruptions, the activity of Mount Anak Krakatau decreased again.

"Based on satellite imagery data monitoring, the continuous eruption has produced lava flows that move towards the northwest-west-southwest from the eruption center. Although it has experienced 7 strombolian eruptions after the cessation of the continuous eruption, the activity of Mount Anak Krakatau tends to decline until September 20, 2026, at 00:00 WIB," said the Head of the Geological Agency, Lana Saria, in a special report regarding the current activity of Mount Anak Krakatau, Monday (21/9/2026).

Lana added that the seismicity record of Mount Anak Krakatau on September 20, 2026, showed one low-frequency earthquake and continuous tremors with an average amplitude of 2 millimeters. "Based on the results of a comprehensive analysis and evaluation, the activity level of Mount Anak Krakatau has been lowered from Level III (Alert) to Level II (Warning) starting September 21, 2026, at 6:30 PM WIB," she said.

Previously reported, the continuous eruption of Mount Anak Krakatau that occurred from September 4 to 6 triggered the spread of volcanic ash to various provinces in Indonesia. In fact, the spread of volcanic ash paralyzed hundreds of flights at several airports in Sumatra and Java.

A report from the Center for Volcanology and Geological Hazard Mitigation (PVMBG) and VAAC Darwin, along with RGB image analysis from Himawari-9 on September 5, 2026, at 19:00 WIB, indicates that there are two clusters of volcanic ash distribution from Mount Anak Krakatau. The distribution from the surface up to an altitude of 20,000 feet was observed towards the southeast-south, covering parts of Banten and West Java, as well as the surrounding waters.

In addition, the distribution from the surface to an altitude of 50,000 feet was also observed in a southwest-northwest direction, covering parts of Banten, Lampung, Bengkulu, West Sumatra, the southern Sunda Strait, and the Indian Ocean west of Lampung and Banten. (Kompas.id, 6/9/2026).

Until now, the activity of Mount Anak Krakatau continues to be monitored through two volcanic observation posts in the regions of Lampung and Banten. In Banten, the observation post for GAK is located in Pasauran, Serang Regency, Banten. Meanwhile, in Lampung, the monitoring post is situated in Hargopancuran Village, Rajabasa District, South Lampung.

The Head of the Mount Anak Krakatau Monitoring Post in Hargopancuran Village, Suwarno, stated that although its status has decreased, the public is still prohibited from entering or conducting activities within a two-kilometer radius from the center of the volcanic activity. The community and fishermen are also urged to remain vigilant against the threats of incandescent rock eruptions and heavy ash rain.

On September 21, 2026, from 18:00 to 24:00 WIB, Mount Anak Krakatau experienced one hybrid earthquake or multiple phases with an amplitude of 46 millimeters and a duration of 8 seconds. In addition, continuous tremor earthquakes were also recorded with an amplitude of 1-5 millimeters.

Separately, the Head of the Regional Disaster Management Agency (BPBD) of South Lampung, Maturidi, stated that the community is still urged to remain vigilant even though the status of Mount Anak Krakatau has been downgraded. Currently, his agency continues to disseminate information regarding the downgrade of the volcano's status and the safety regulations that are still applicable to the public.

Previously, when the status was at Level III Alert, the public was prohibited from approaching within a three-kilometer radius of the crater's peak. Currently, the safe distance has been adjusted to two kilometers from the peak following the downgrade to Level II Waspada.
