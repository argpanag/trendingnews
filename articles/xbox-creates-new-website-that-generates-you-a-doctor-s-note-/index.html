Xbox Creates New Website That Generates You A Doctor's Note For GTA 6

TheTools · 16.11.2026 14:45 · source · permalink

And teams up with DJ Khaled in the process

Xbox is having a bit of fun with the GTA 6 launch this week, even if they're not allowed to officially market it in some situations (due to an apparent deal struck between Take-Two and PlayStation). For example, they've just created a doctor's note website for the game, but it's not spelt out that it's for GTA 6.

It obviously is for the new Grand Theft Auto though, and they've even teamed up with music star DJ Khaled to help promote this random new website. In a nutshell, if you head over to DrXbox.com, you can enter your name and get a generated doctor's note that says you're suffering from "Vitamin G deficiency".

Here's a closer look (via Tom Warren on social media):

It's obviously just a bit of fun that won't hold up in any self-respecting business, but you could at least give it a try if they have a sense of humour! Probably best to just book the day off though - it's the kind of game that deserves it.

Anyway, go and have a play with this website if you want to. We find it a little bit weird and random to have partnered with DJ Khaled for something like this, but it gave us a chuckle at least.
