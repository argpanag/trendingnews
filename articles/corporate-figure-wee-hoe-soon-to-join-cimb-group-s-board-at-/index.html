Corporate figure Wee Hoe Soon to join CIMB Group's board at start of 2027

TheTools · 20.10.2026 10:24 · source · permalink

KUALA LUMPUR (Oct 20): CIMB Group Holdings Bhd (KL:CIMB) has announced that Tan Sri Dr Wee Hoe Soon @ Gooi Hoe Soon, 66, will be joining its board as an independent and non-executive director on Jan 1, 2027. 

According to filings with Bursa Malaysia, CIMB noted that Wee will also become a member of the bank’s risk committee and audit committee.

Wee currently sits on several boards — both in the private sector and public sector — including as an independent director at the Securities Commission Malaysia (SC). He is also the chairman of insurance company AIA Bhd. 

At present, he also sits on the board of JaGaSolution Bhd (KL:JAGAAPP) as a non-independent director and Hydropipes Bhd (KL:HPI) as an independent director. 

He is also a member of the Employees Provident Fund's investment panel. 

Wee has close to four decades of experience in accounting and corporate finance, according to the SC’s website. 

In the filing, CIMB said Wee will be joining 10 others on the board that includes chairman Datuk Syed Zaid Syed Jaffar Albar and group chief executive officer Muhammad Novan Amirudin, among others. 

At market close on Tuesday, CIMB’s counter ended flat at RM7.15, valuing the bank at RM77.3 billion.
