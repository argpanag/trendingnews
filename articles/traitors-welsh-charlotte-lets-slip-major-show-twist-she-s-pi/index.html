Traitors' Welsh Charlotte lets slip major show twist she's pitched to bosses

TheTools · 30.09.2026 15:57 · source · permalink

A star of The Traitors has pitched a game-changing twist to BBC bosses.

Charlotte Berman appeared in series three of the civilian series alongside the likes of fan favourites Alexander Dragonetti, Francesca Rowan-Plowden and Freddie Fraser. Her season was ultimately won by Faithfuls Leanne Quigley and Jake Brown.

Throughout the series, Charlotte spoke with a Welsh accent and told her fellow players she was from Abergavenny. However, this was all a lie. While viewers were eventually let in on the secret, her co-stars only found out when they exited the show as she never revealed it to them during the main programme.

The Londoner has since explained the decision to appear Welsh was due to a belief it was a "trustworthy" accent, and her mum also hailed from the country, but it failed to save her from banishment. Deception is a common tactic past players have used on the BBC game show, with lies ranging from covering-up their real jobs to hiding relationships with fellow players.

Ahead of The Celebrity Traitors returning to TV screens for a second series tomorrow night (October 1), Charlotte, speaking exclusively on behalf of Buzz Bingo, revealed a major twist she wants to see unfold - and she has already pitched it to the powers that be.

"I have what I think is the master idea which I need to be implemented. I've mentioned this when I've seen and spoken to some of the executive producers," she started.

"It's to have identical twins. I think it would be so funny, so unnecessary, but put a pair of identical twins in there and have them play as the same person. I don't know if they would be allowed a debrief; I can't tell you what the construct would be. For the viewer, that would be so funny. If that happens, you heard it here first."

As for her Welsh accent, Charlotte insists she has "no regrets" about giving it a go. She added: "I feel very lucky to have been such a memorable part of my series. It was a lot of fun, and I think it was very much in the spirit of The Traitors and the kind of camp, pantomime style of this backstabbing parlour game that we're all playing."

Traitors fans don't have long to wait for their next fix of banishments and betrayal, as The Celebrity Traitors series two commences tomorrow night from 8pm. Fans can watch it on BBC One or stream it via BBC iPlayer.

Among the famous faces entering the castle are singer-songwriter James Blunt, Love Island host Maya Jama, actor Michael Sheen and a host of comedians including Miranda Hart and Rob Beckett.

The Celebrity Traitors returns tomorrow night at 8pm on BBC One and iPlayer.
