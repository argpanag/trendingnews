Sunderland vs Hull TV channel and how to watch Carabao Cup clash

TheTools · 08.09.2026 15:00 · source · permalink

Need to know

It's an all-Premier League affair at the Stadium of Light on Tuesday(Image: Zach Forster/Sports Press Photo/SPP/Shutterstock)

Is Sunderland's Carabao Cup clash against Hull City on TV:

  1. Kick-off time: The fixture takes place on Tuesday, September 8, at the Stadium of Light in Sunderland. The referee will blow the whistle for kick-off at 7.45pm BST.
  2. UK TV channel: In the UK, the match will be broadcast live exclusively on Sky Sports+, with fans able to watch by pressing the red button.
  3. Live stream options and app access: Subscribers can live stream the action through the Sky Go app or the Sky Sports app on iOS and Android devices, as well as on desktop browsers. Non-Sky subscribers can stream the match by purchasing a NOW Sports Day or Month Membership via the NOW app on smart TVs, streaming sticks, or mobile devices.
  4. UK radio commentary: Local live audio commentary is available in the North East via BBC Radio Newcastle, while Hull supporters can tune into BBC Radio Humberside.
  5. Team news: Sunderland will be without midfielder Habib Diarra, who is ruled out with a hamstring injury. Hull City will miss new signing Ilyas Ansah after Tigers boss Sergej Jakirovic revealed the striker is hampered by a spinal issue. Despite missing out on the goalless draw against Aston Villa, Tim Iroegbunam is expected to feature in the cup clash.
