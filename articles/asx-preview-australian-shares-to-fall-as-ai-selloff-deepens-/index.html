ASX Preview: Australian Shares to Fall as AI Selloff Deepens; ALS Fiscal H1 Underlying Earnings, Revenue Up

TheTools · 23.11.2026 21:00 · source · permalink
  • FRS.AX
  • DX-Y.NYB
  • ^IXIC

  • ^GSPC

Australian shares are poised to fall on Monday, tracking global market losses as a selloff in artificial intelligence (AI)-linked stocks, a stronger US dollar and expectations of further US interest rate hikes weighed on investor sentiment.

On Nov. 20, the S&P 500, the Nasdaq Composite, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 3.7%, 5.3%, and 1.9%, respectively.

In the macroeconomy, Australia's private sector activity contracted at its fastest pace in more than five years in November, as weakening demand, falling new orders and renewed job cuts weighed on business output and sentiment, according to an S&P Global survey released Monday.

In corporate news, ALS (ASX:ALQ) reported Monday fiscal first half underlying earnings of AU$0.42 per share on revenue of AU$1.72 billion, compared with underlying earnings of AU$0.346 on revenue of AU$1.66 billion a year earlier.

Forrestania Resources (ASX:FRS) started wet commissioning at its Edna May Gold Mine after delivering first ore into the primary crusher, advancing the project towards targeted first gold production in December.

Australia's benchmark index fell 2.6%, or 229.2 points, to close at 8,592.60 on Nov. 20.
