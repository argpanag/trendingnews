    Norman Powell has 33 points in win over Hawks Friday

    Norman Powell

    CHI Guard #24
    Player Stats

    Norman Powell had 33 points (12-of-19 FGs, 5-of-6 FTs), five rebounds, two assists, one steal and four three-pointers in the Bulls’ 125-117 victory over the Hawks on Friday.

    Powell led the Bulls with 33 points in the overtime victory over the Hawks Friday. The 33-year-old guard helped put the game away with an and-1 that put the Bulls up by five with less than two minutes left in overtime. It was Powell’s second game with at least 30 points this season. The Bulls will take on the Timberwolves on the second night of a back-to-back on Saturday.
    - Nick Shlain
