Palantir Stocks Rise With Ives's 2027 AI Endorsement

TheTools · 08.10.2026 15:27 · source · permalink

Palantir Technologies PLTR, the government-and-enterprise AI software company, rose approximately 0.3% to $192.68 around 11:24 a.m. ET on October 7. According to MarketWatch, Dan Ives included the company among his five favorite technology stocks heading into 2027. A bullish endorsement helps. Delivering the growth already baked into the price matters more.

The operating numbers pack a punch. Palantir's second-quarter release reported revenue of $1.94 billion, up 93%, while U.S. commercial revenue surged 149%. GAAP operating margin reached 47%, total contract value hit $3.37 billion, and cash plus short-term Treasury securities totaled $9.2 billion. That combination gives investors more than an AI growth story: it shows rapid expansion alongside substantial profitability.

The picture puts the shares 4.43% above the $184.51 GF Value estimate, suggesting a modest premium on that measure. But the broader sales multiple sets a much tougher hurdle. A roughly $458 billion market capitalization amounts to about 59 times second-quarter revenue multiplied by foura simple annualization, not a forecast. Palantir's business is racing ahead. At this valuation, investors need that pace to last, because slower commercial growth or thinner margins could hit the multiple hard.
