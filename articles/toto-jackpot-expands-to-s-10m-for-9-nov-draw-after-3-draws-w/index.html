TOTO jackpot expands to S$10M for 9 Nov draw after 3 draws without Group 1 winner

TheTools · 07.11.2026 17:49 · source · permalink

Latest News Singapore

This will be the fourth cascade draw of the year.

By Jeremy Lee - 8 Nov 2026, 1:49 am

TOTO draw on 9 Nov to be conducted at 9.30pm

Singapore will have its fourth TOTO cascade draw of 2026 after the jackpot for next Monday (9 Nov) expanded to an estimated S$10 million.

According to the Singapore Pools website, the eagerly anticipated draw will be conducted at 9.30pm that night.

Source: Google Maps

No Group 1 winner on 29 Oct, 2 Nov & 5 Nov

The TOTO jackpot snowballed after three consecutive draws — on 29 Oct, 2 Nov, and 5 Nov — failed to produce a Group 1 winner.

Additionally, the draw on 29 Oct did not even have a Group 2 winner.

Source: Singapore Pools

Source: Singapore Pools

There were five Group 2 winners on 5 Nov, but only two on 2 Nov.

This caused the Group 1 prize to increase from about S$1.2 million on 29 Oct to more than S$2.9 million on 2 Nov, and then more than S$5.5 million on 5 Nov.

9 Nov TOTO jackpot to be split if still no Group 1 winner

Accordingly, the draw on 9 Nov will be a cascade draw, which is typically called in such a situation where three consecutive draws have gone without a Group 1 Prize winner.

In this fourth draw, the jackpot will be split between the Group 2 winners if no Group 1 winner emerges.

If there are no Group 2 winners, the prize will be split among the Group 3 winners, and so on.

Source: Singapore Pools

Cascade draws are typically pushed back to 9.30pm from the usual draw timing of 6.30pm. Sales will also close later at 9pm.

Source: ong boon siong on XiaoHongShu

Previous cascade draw was on 13 July

The previous cascade draw — the third of this year — was on 13 July.

Its jackpot exceeded S$12.8 million, with two lucky Group 1 winners splitting the prize.

The other cascade draws this year were on 4 May, when one ticket won almost S$12.8 million, and 29 Jan.

Besides the three previous cascade draws this year, the TOTO Hong Bao Draw also had a jackpot of more than S$10 million. Its eventual jackpot, which swelled to S$12 million, was shared by three winning tickets.
