Christian Watson scores his fourth TD of the season, giving Green Bay a 7-0 lead

TheTools · 25.09.2026 00:50 · source · permalink

It is Week 3, and Christian Watson already has four touchdown receptions. His career-high for touchdowns is the seven he had as a rookie in 2022.

Watson’s latest touchdown came on a 4-yard throw by Jordan Love with 5:13 remaining in the first quarter on Thursday night.

The Packers drove 87 yards in nine plays on their second series to take a 7-0 lead.

Love was 5-of-6 for 76 yards and the touchdown. Matthew Golden had the big play, catching a 45-yard pass to the Atlanta 43 on third-and-11.

On their first possession, the Packers failed to take advantage of safety Xavier McKinney’s interception of Michael Penix. The Packers went three-and-out as Love was only 1-of-3 for 6 yards.
