FPL gameweek 12 tips: Is it time to sell Man City's Erling Haaland?

TheTools · 27.11.2026 17:56 · source · permalink

What to do with Erling Haaland?

  • Published
    27 November 2026, 06:58 GMT

The Premier League heads into another big weekend and BBC Sport's Fantasy Premier League expert Holly Shand gives her answers to some of the biggest dilemmas facing managers heading into gameweek 12.

Should you replace Erling Haaland?

Erling Haaland (£15.8m) has blanked in three consecutive games for Manchester City, who have taken just one point in this period. More than 100,000 managers have transferred Haaland out this week, despite him being the most owned player in the game. Form and fixtures collide, with league leaders Arsenal up next.

He has a good record against the Gunners, scoring in their last four Premier League meetings. Additionally, I believe Haaland will be the best captain pick in the game for his next three home games against Leeds United, Chelsea and Hull City so I recommend holding the highest-scoring forward in the game.

Who is the best captain for Gameweek 12?

With Haaland's appeal dwindling this week, there are other options to consider across our squads. Bruno Fernandes (£11.6m) leads the data informed approach, as Manchester United entertain Brentford for Mauricio Pochettino's first game at Old Trafford. Are you brave enough to back the new manager bounce?

Elsewhere, Dominic Calvert-Lewin (£6.0m) faces Coventry at home, scoring in three of his last five at Elland Road, while Nicolas Jackson (£7.5m) travels to Ipswich Town, with four goal involvements in four. Or could it be a week to back a defender with Brighton's Maxim De Cuyper (£5.5m)? He's the highest-scoring player in the game!

Should you play Free Hit in Gameweek 12?

If you own four of more players from Arsenal and Manchester City, then there's a strong case to play the Free Hit in Gameweek 12 to avoid that fixture. Back Leeds and Brighton defences, at home to Coventry and Newcastle respectively with James Trafford (£5.1m),Tarik Muharemovic (£5.1m), Luka Vuskovic (£5.3m) and De Cuyper.

Liverpool are among the form sides, with Florian Wirtz (£7.4m) and Alexander Isak (£9.1m) worth considering. Wirtz has hit a double digit return in his last two games. There's also places in the midfield for top performers Marcus Tavernier (£6.8m) and Pascal Gross (£6.3m).

Should you replace Cole Palmer?

Cole Palmer has just one goal in his last five games which has frustrated his owners, and he picked up a red card in that period too. Chelsea's attack has struggled to find the same intensity with Joao Pedro out injured, scoring just three goals in this time.

While Palmer's time in our squads is coming to a close, he is nailed on for 90 minutes in a congested period of games, with Chelsea not involved in Europe. He deserves the benefit of the doubt with Nottingham Forest and Crystal Palace up next. Liverpool in Gameweek 14 is the obvious exit point.

What should you do with David Raya?

The news coming out of Arsenal is still mixed on the fitness of the highest-owned goalkeeper in the game David Raya (£6.3m). We do know he is not believed to need surgery, which means he is not expected to be out long term. He's probably a hold.

If news arrives he will only miss Gameweek 12, you could Free Hit, or go without a goalkeeper if your substitute goalkeeper is non-playing. Spurs' Antonin Kinsky (£4.4m) leads the way for possible back-ups, with games against Sunderland and Fulham in the immediate schedule.

How best to navigate three gameweeks in 10 days?

It is worth noting there is just 48 hours between Gameweek 12 drawing to a close and the midweek Gameweek 13 deadline, coming on Tuesday, 1 December at 6:30pm GMT. Set those alarms so that you don't miss any action over the congestion in the next six weeks!

Be mindful that we might see increased rotation in the midweek rounds, particularly for wingers and full-backs. Those sides already involved in Europe are more likely to rotate, so having a strong bench in these instances could be key.

Also look out for players who are on four bookings, like Tavernier and Gross, as another yellow card could result in a one-match ban.
