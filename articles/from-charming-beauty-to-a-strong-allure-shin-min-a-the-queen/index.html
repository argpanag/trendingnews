Actress Shin Min-a is demonstrating the power of the Queen of K-Romance Novels.

In Disney+’s original series “The Remarried Empress,” which has reached its midpoint, Shin Min-a portrays the character “Nabie,” showcasing a three-dimensional “K-romance heroine.” Shin Min-a not only displays the dignity of a perfect empress but also delicately depicts the wounds hidden behind that facade.

Nabie is an elegant and perfect empress from a prestigious family, but she goes through a difficult time as Emperor Sobiesh (played by Joo Ji-hoon) becomes infatuated with Rasta (played by Lee Se-young), a concubine of runaway slave origin. Shin Min-a conveyed Nabie’s devastated state of mind, which requires enduring sorrow in silence, through restrained acting.

Nabie found relief through her interactions with an anonymous friend via the letter bird “Queen.” After learning that the owner of “Queen” was the foreign prince Heinrey (played by Lee Jong-suk), the distance between the two closed further, and the pink atmosphere that bloomed between them stirred viewers’ anticipation. During a hot air balloon date with Heinrey, Nabie’s clear smile, as she looked down on the vast Dongdae Empire, temporarily escaping her stifling reality, conveyed a sense of complete liberation and left a lingering impression. Shin Min-a naturally depicted the subtle emotional changes as Nabie gradually opened her heart in front of Heinrey. The relationship with Sobiesh headed further toward collapse. With news of Rasta’s pregnancy also revealed, the harsh reality tightened its grip on Nabie even more.

Shin Min-a is captivating not only Heinrey (played by Lee Jong-suk) in the drama but also viewers, showcasing both the empress’s beauty and Nabie’s charm through her acting.

From enduring pain within a crumbling relationship to gradually opening her heart to new love, Shin Min-a has ascended to the status of Queen of K-Romance Novels by meticulously building up Nabie’s emotional changes.

As “The Remarried Empress” reaches its midpoint, attention is focused on what kind of emotion will be conveyed through the narrative of Nabie, who will choose a new love and destiny.

“The Remarried Empress” can be watched exclusively on Disney+, with two episodes released every Wednesday, for a total of 10 episodes.
