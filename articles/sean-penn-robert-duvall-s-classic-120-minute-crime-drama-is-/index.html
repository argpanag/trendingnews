Sean Penn & Robert Duvall's Classic 120-Minute Crime Drama Is Leaving Prime Video Soon

TheTools · 30.09.2026 22:00 · source · permalink

Dennis Hopper is best known as one of the greatest actors in modern Hollywood, but he also dabbled in directing. One of his biggest movies, Colors, starring Sean Penn and Robert Duvall, is getting ready to leave Prime Video.

The 1988 crime movie won't be on Prime Video for much longer. Those looking to check it out should do so before the end of the month, as it leaves the streaming platform on Sept. 30.

Colors Explores the World of Gang Violence in Los Angeles

Hopper's Colors follows two LAPD officers: the experienced "Uncle" Bob Hodges and rookie officer Danny McGavin, who comes from the LAPD's C.R.A.S.H. unit, a division dedicated to gang intelligence. The two are paired up to patrol the streets of Northwest, East, and South Los Angeles.

Hodges and McGavin have different views of gang life and policing, with Hodges taking a more diplomatic approach during encounters. Throughout, the pair clash in their interactions with various members of gangs in the area, including Bloods, Crips, and Hispanic gangs, all while trying to navigate a way to broker peace between the groups.

Duvall and Penn lead Colors as Hodges and McGavin. The movie's ensemble cast also includes María Conchita Alonso, Randy Brooks, Don Cheadle, Glenn Plummer, Trinidad Silva, Grand L. Bush, and Damon Wayans. Hopper directed Colors from a screenplay written by Michael Schiffer.

Colors was a commercial success, grossing over $46 million against a $10 million production budget. It also received strong reviews and has a 76% rating on Rotten Tomatoes. The movie's exploration of gang life and different approaches to policing earned the most praise.

For Hopper specifically, the movie also helped establish him as a director 19 years after his directorial debut, the 1969 hit Easy Rider. He'd directed only sporadically since, helming The Last Movie in 1971 and Out of the Blue in 1980, but he went on to direct more consistently, making movies like Catchfire, The Hot Spot, and Chasers in the 1990s.

What Do We Know About Sean Penn’s Next Movie?

Colors was one of Penn's earliest roles in what would become an acclaimed career. Penn has won three Oscars, the first two for Best Actor in 2004's Mystic River and 2009's Milk. His latest win was a Best Supporting Actor Oscar for his role in Paul Thomas Anderson's One Battle After Another.

Up next for Penn is a role in Snake Island, a political thriller from director Doug Liman. Penn will star alongside Adrien Brody. Little is known about the movie's plot, but it will reportedly be about the sabotage of the Nord Stream natural gas pipeline in 2022.

Colors is available to stream on Prime Video until Sept. 30. It remains available to rent on platforms like Apple TV, Fandango, and Prime Video.
