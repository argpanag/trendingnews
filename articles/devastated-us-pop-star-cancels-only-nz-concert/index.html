‘Devastated’: US pop star cancels only NZ concert

TheTools · 18.09.2026 18:14 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

Charlie Puth has cancelled his Whatever's Clever! tour set for Auckland this November. Photo / Getty Images

American pop star Charlie Puth has cancelled his only New Zealand concert, with no reason given to his devastated fans.

The singer had been due to perform at Auckland’s Spark Arena on November 5 as part of his Whatever’s Clever! World Tour, before taking the show across the ditch to five Australian cities.

Ticketmaster notified ticketholders by email tonight that the event had been cancelled but didn’t provide any further explanation.

Its website also labelled the event “cancelled”.

The show was announced in April, with Puth saying at the time he had waited his “entire career” to stage this kind of live performance.

“We have worked so hard to bring you the sort of top-level musicianship and arrangements that you all deserve in a live show, and I am beyond excited to bring my music and this incredible band and show to some of the most iconic rooms in the world,” he told fans.

“It’s going to be fun!”

However, the Auckland concert and planned shows in Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, Adelaide and Perth have now been cancelled.

Puth had not publicly addressed the cancellations by tonight, but fans on social media said they were “devastated” by the news.

“My partner and I are so disappointed,” one commented.

“Just got the email half an hour ago, so upset,” another wrote.

Puth is best known for hits including Attention and We Don’t Talk Anymore, as well as Wiz Khalifa’s See You Again, on which he featured.

The song appeared in Furious 7 and served as a tribute to actor Paul Walker, who died before filming was completed.

Puth’s latest album, Whatever’s Clever!, was released in March.
