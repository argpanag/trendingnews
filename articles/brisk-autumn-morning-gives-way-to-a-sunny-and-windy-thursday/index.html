Brisk autumn morning gives way to a sunny and windy Thursday across New England - The Boston Globe

TheTools · 22.10.2026 09:22 · source · permalink

Other than a few predawn sprinkles Thursday, we’re going to see sunshine build throughout the day across New England. But you’ll still want to break out the winter gear for the morning commute because the wind is going to make temperatures of 40 to 45 degrees feel more like the mid- to upper 30s.

Eventually, we’ll climb to the upper 50s and low 60s across Greater Boston and areas west.

The pressure difference between our blocking high positioned to the northwest (around 1028 millibars) and the former nor’easter, now Subtropical Storm Josephine, over the Northern Atlantic (around 988 millibars) creates a very strong gradient, meaning persistent gusts of 30 to 40 mph, especially by the Cape. The strongest wind gust on Wednesday was 45 mph on Martha’s Vineyard, pretty gusty. There might be a rogue gust that tops this on Thursday.

Thursday night, the Patriots take on the Bears starting at 8:15 p.m. in Chicago, and the weather for the game is looking great — mostly clear, winds under 10 mph, and temperatures in the mid- to upper 50s.

Weekend sneak peek

I’ll keep it really simple with the weekend sneak peek since Dave has you covered with more details. It’s going to be beautiful out there with completely sunny skies and relaxing winds. Temperatures will be coolest on Saturday, reaching the mid- to upper 50s, while Sunday breaks back into the low to mid-60s.

Thursday breakdown

Greater Boston: Breezy with early-morning sprinkles, then dry from the commute through the rest of the day. Decreasing clouds. Highs in the upper 50s and low 60s.

Central/Western Mass.: Breezy with decreasing clouds, turning mostly sunny. Highs in the mid-50s in the Berkshires, low 60s east.

Southeastern Mass.: Breezy with decreasing clouds. A predawn sprinkle. Highs in the mid- to upper 50s.

Cape Cod and the Islands: A few early-morning sprinkles. Breezy to gusty. Decreasing clouds with highs in the mid-50s.

Rhode Island: Decreasing clouds with a breeze. Highs in the upper 50s and low 60s.

New Hampshire: A low chance for a morning coastal shower. Otherwise, breezy with partly to mostly sunny skies. Highs in the upper 50s and low 60s.

Vermont/Maine: More sun than clouds, highs in the mid- to upper 50s across both states.

Connecticut: Decreasing clouds with a breeze. Highs in the low 60s.
