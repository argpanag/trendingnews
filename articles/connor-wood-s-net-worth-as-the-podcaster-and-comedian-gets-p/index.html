Connor Wood’s net worth, as the podcaster and comedian gets paid thousands for DWTS

TheTools · 17.11.2026 23:00 · source · permalink

He could take home $300k from DWTS

BySydney Boyd

You might’ve seen him on Eighth Avenue drinking a smoothie and paid him no mind… Connor Wood is a podcaster and comedian who’s made it all the way to the semifinals of Dancing With the Stars. He’s been making content for years, and now he’s getting paid to perform on Dancing With the Stars, so what’s Connor Wood’s net worth?

He graduated from the University of Texas at Austin in 2018 with a bachelor’s degree in advertising. Connor then went on to work at Bumble after graduating, as a digital marketing intern and then field marketing coordinator, according to his LinkedIn.

After the pandemic, “I did not go back to work,” he told People. “That was so scary because the pandemic started wrapping up and everyone started going back to work. I had nowhere to go.”

He kept uploading videos to TikTok and his comedy career really took off. Connor then created the massively popular show Brooke and Connor Make a Podcast, that he hosts alongside his close friend Brooke Averick. There have been some insanely viral sound bits from this podcast too, like Connor’s iconic “I have purse” that never stops playing in my head.

Connor’s comedy career has also been wildly successful, as he headlined the sold-out Fibs and Friends comedy tour across the United States, the United Kingdom, and Ireland. He was named in Variety’s Comedy Icon Report, featured on The Hollywood Reporter’s Creator A-List, and named as a “comic to watch” at the New York Comedy Festival.

Now, he’s on Dancing With the Stars where he gets paid TONS. According to Variety, every single celebrity who goes on the gets an insane $125k just for being part of the show, with the pay covering the early rehearsals. Then for every week the celebrity manages to stay on the show they earn additional weekly bonuses that range from $10k to $50k per week. 

So, if Connor makes it to the final, he will potentially take home close to $300k.

With all this said, Connor’s net worth isn’t verified, but it’s estimated that it’s around $1 million from his comedy career, podcast, and now DWTS appearance.
