Carrie First Reviews: A Fresh, Modern Take with Powerful Performances and Thoughtful Themes

TheTools · 08.10.2026 00:49 · source · permalink

News

Critics say Summer H. Howell delivers a heartbreaking performance as the titular outcast, and Mike Flanagan's expansion of the source material offers welcome nuance, if not outright terror.

By Christopher Campbell | Published October 1, 2026

TAGGED AS: Horror, streaming, TV

Yet another adaptation of Stephen King’s Carrie is arriving this month, and the first reviews of the full series mostly say the idea was worth repeating. Mike Flanagan, who has made a few movies and series based on King’s work, returns to television with his latest, a reimagining of the 1974 horror novel of the same name. In this incarnation, Summer H. Howell plays the title character, a bullied high schooler who gets revenge on her classmates using telekinetic powers. Updates to the material are good and bad, but it’s still an apt, well-acted modern take.

Starring: Summer H. Howell, Samantha Sloyan, Siena Agudong, Alison Thornton
