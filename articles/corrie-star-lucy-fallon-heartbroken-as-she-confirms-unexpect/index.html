Corrie star Lucy Fallon heartbroken as she confirms unexpected death of dog

TheTools · 24.09.2026 07:55 · source · permalink

Coronation Street star Lucy Fallon has been left heartbroken as she shared with fans the news of her beloved dog's sudden death.

The Blackpool-born actress, who is best known for playing Bethany Platt in the ITV soap, which is filmed in Salford, shared the unexpected passing of her pup, Sushi, in posts shared to her Instagram Story.

The adorable Pomeranian often feature on Lucy's social media page, and has been seen by her side through many of her big milestones, including becoming a mum to her two children - Sonny and Nancy - with her footballer fiancé, Ryan Ledson.

Sharing a picture of Sushi to her Instagram Story, Lucy wrote on Wednesday (September 23): "Our lil sushi roll," before adding the dove, brokenheart and mending heart emojis.

She then followed up the post with another picture of her sweet pet, and confirmed: "Sushi passed away unexpectedly yesterday [heartbroken emoji]. A diva and an icon x."

Back in 2022, Sushi was seen helping Lucy reveal her pregnancy joy after she had earlier suffered a tragic miscarriage. The fur baby could be seen wearing a cute heart around her neck reading: "We’ve been keeping a secret!" As she shared a glimpse inside her pregnancy with Sonny, now three, Lucy and Ryan could be seen posing for the cameras as they revelled in their gorgeous news.

Back in November, Lucy was seen celebrating her 30th birthday before revealing that she and footballer Ryan were engaged after the midfielder for EFL League One club Huddersfield Town popped the question.

Confirming the news, Lucy took to Instagram with a string of photos showing off her sparkling engagement ring. In the first, she and Ryan, who share two children, posed for a selfie as she held her hand up to the camera, and it appeared the bride-to-be was emotional as her eyes filled with tears.

Another photo showed Lucy sitting in a restaurant, in which she could be seen holding up her glass with her gorgeous engagement ring dazzling on her wedding finger. Alongside the post, the soap star wrote: "Ending the birthday weekend with my best friend as a fiancé!!!! I can’t wait to marry you @ryanledson8."

While it's not clear if Ryan popped the question before or after her party, Lucy was clearly in the mood for celebrating as she was seen dancing the night away until 2.30am, and was seen letting her hair down to the Beyoncé classic, Crazy in Love.
