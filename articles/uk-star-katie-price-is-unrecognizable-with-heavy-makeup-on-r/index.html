UK Star Katie Price Is Unrecognizable with Heavy Makeup on Red Carpet After Having 'Correction Surgery'

TheTools · 08.09.2026 20:18 · source · permalink

UK tabloid star Katie Price is looking unrecognizable in her latest red carpet appearance.

The 48-year-old English media personality stepped out for the 2026 National Television Awards red carpet on Tuesday (September 8) in London, England.

Katie revealed on her Instagram Stories that morning that she just had “correction surgery” one day earlier on her breasts and face.

What kind of surgery did Katie Price have?

“What did Price do yesterday? So, I went to Brussels because one of my boobs was still slightly bigger than the other,” she said in the Instagram Stories video.

Katie continued, “They put a new implant in and while they were there, I said, ‘Oh, go on, just put a bit more in both of them.’ And at the same time, where my face was hollow here as you get older and lose weight, he put filler in for me, obviously while I was asleep… So I’ve woken up this morning, I didn’t have anything put here, nothing, but I think the swelling has dropped so I look like a hamster and it’s sore.”

Katie also made another video revealing how Supreme CBD oil is helping her recovery.
