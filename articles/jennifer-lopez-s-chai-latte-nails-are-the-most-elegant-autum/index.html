Jennifer Lopez’s ‘Chai Latte’ Nails Are The Most Elegant Autumn Nail Trend: 5 Ways To Wear Them

TheTools · 08.09.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

This creamy coffee-inspired hue is destined for manicure minimalists.

Some nail colours feel synonymous with a certain time of year; others are timeless enough to transcend the seasons. But with the minimalism movement at an all-time high – variations of sheer, natural finishes and intentionally understated manicures continue to dominate today’s trends – somehow, the newest hue on our radars seems to tick both boxes.

As seen on Jennifer Lopez during yesterday’s Labour Day celebrations, ‘chai latte’ nails are making a strong case for being the most universally flattering colour for autumn. Simultaneously embracing the cosy, coffee-inspired theme that defines the season and feeling as polished and pared-back as recent trends, the shade can be considered an elevated creamy nude beige.

Created by celebrity nail tech — and Lopez's long-time go-to — Tom Bachik, the singer's latest manicure revives a popular look from her past; at New York Fashion Week back in 2023, Bachik first debuted the chai latte hue on Lopez, dubbing it a ‘taupe-y lavender’ colour.

Her new iteration, however, ‘takes the milky nude everyone loves and warms it up with a cinnamon-spice undertone’, says Daisy Kalnina, founder and CEO of The GelBottle Inc. ‘It’s the same clean concept – a minimal base but with an autumnal depth and twist that transitions into the new season.’

Kalnina adds that chai latte reflects the current demand for nails which look expensive, considered and quietly luxurious. Better still, they’re modern and versatile enough to suit a range of nail shapes, finishes and designs, as well as any occasion – office, event or otherwise.

‘I love these when worn short and almond-shaped with a chic shine rather than an ultra-high gloss,’ she suggests. ‘They work beautifully on their own [take JLo for proof] or with one accent nail in a deeper cocoa for contrast.’

5 Chai Latte Nail Ideas For Autumn 2026

Short 'n' Sweet

Though simple, short, immaculately groomed nails rarely fail to flatter. When paired with a rich, creamy chai latte coat, the result is as classic as it is contemporary.

Milky Gloss

Truly leaning into the 'latte' element could mean opting for a milky, cashmere-tinted top coat or nail illuminator on top of your base coat. According to Hailey Bieber, 'milk nails' are making a comeback this season, too.

Cloudy French

Swap out traditional crisp white tips for a cloudy, near-translucent finish, and you'll find the chai latte colour is instantly made dreamier and more subtle.

Chrome Finish

Though if subtlety isn't on the cards, adding a glazed chrome finish is bound to attract compliments. This variation of chai latte adopts a distinct ivory undertone, rather than cinnamon.

Micro Polkas

Without straying into maximalism, minuscule reflective polka dots offer a modern edge to an otherwise plain chai latte manicure. Stick to short, rounded ovals or almonds to keep the overall look low-key.
