Nitto ATP Finals 2026 SF Qualification Scenarios 16 November | Nitto ATP Finals | Tennis

TheTools · 19.11.2026 20:13 · source · permalink

After four days of action at the Nitto ATP Finals in Turin, two singles players and two doubles teams have qualified for the semi-finals.

View below full Nitto ATP Finals semi-final qualification scenarios as of 18 November below.

Singles John McEnroe Group qualification scenarios after the completion of the 2nd round of the event's group stage. Alexander Zverev has qualified for the semi-finals and:

1) Regardless of score, if Alexander Zverev defeats Arthur Fils and Ben Shelton defeats Rafael Jodar, then Alexander Zverev wins the group and Ben Shelton qualifies 2nd.

2) Regardless of score, if Arthur Fils defeats Alexander Zverev and Rafael Jodar defeats Ben Shelton, then Arthur Fils wins the group and Alexander Zverev qualifies 2nd.

3) If Alexander Zverev defeats Arthur Fils in 2 sets and Rafael Jodar defeats Ben Shelton in 2 sets, then Alexander Zverev wins the group and Rafael Jodar qualifies 2nd.

4) If Alexander Zverev defeats Arthur Fils in 2 sets and Rafael Jodar defeats Ben Shelton in 3 sets, then Alexander Zverev wins the group and order of remaining players is determined by % of games won.

5) If Alexander Zverev defeats Arthur Fils in 3 sets and Rafael Jodar defeats Ben Shelton in 2 sets, then Alexander Zverev wins the group and Arthur Fils qualifies 2nd.

6) If Alexander Zverev defeats Arthur Fils in 3 sets and Rafael Jodar defeats Ben Shelton in 3 sets, then Alexander Zverev wins the group and Arthur Fils qualifies 2nd.

7) If Arthur Fils defeats Alexander Zverev in 2 sets and Ben Shelton defeats Rafael Jodar in 2 sets, then Alexander Zverev wins the group and Arthur Fils qualifies 2nd.

8) If Arthur Fils defeats Alexander Zverev in 2 sets and Ben Shelton defeats Rafael Jodar in 3 sets, then Alexander Zverev wins the group and Arthur Fils qualifies 2nd.

9) If Arthur Fils defeats Alexander Zverev in 3 sets and Ben Shelton defeats Rafael Jodar in 2 sets, then Alexander Zverev wins the group and Ben Shelton qualifies 2nd.

10) If Arthur Fils defeats Alexander Zverev in 3 sets and Ben Shelton defeats Rafael Jodar in 3 sets, then Alexander Zverev wins the group and Arthur Fils qualifies 2nd.

Singles Ivan Lendl Group qualification scenarios after the completion of the 2nd round of the event's group stage. Carlos Alcaraz has qualified for the semi-finals and:

1) Regardless of score, if Carlos Alcaraz defeats Flavio Cobolli and Daniil Medvedev defeats Frances Tiafoe, then Carlos Alcaraz wins the group and Daniil Medvedev qualifies 2nd.

2) Regardless of score, if Carlos Alcaraz defeats Flavio Cobolli and Frances Tiafoe defeats Daniil Medvedev, then Carlos Alcaraz wins the group and Frances Tiafoe qualifies 2nd.

3) Regardless of score, if Flavio Cobolli defeats Carlos Alcaraz and Daniil Medvedev defeats Frances Tiafoe, then Carlos Alcaraz wins the group and Daniil Medvedev qualifies 2nd.

4) Regardless of score, if Flavio Cobolli defeats Carlos Alcaraz and Frances Tiafoe defeats Daniil Medvedev, then Carlos Alcaraz wins the group and Frances Tiafoe qualifies 2nd.

Doubles Todd Woodbridge Group qualification scenarios after the completion of the 2nd round of the event's group stage. Marcelo Arevalo and Mate Pavic have qualified for the semi-finals and:

1) Regardless of score, if Harri Heliovaara / Henry Patten defeat Theo Arribage / Albaro Olivetti and Marcelo Arevalo / Mate Pavic defeat Orlando Luz / Rafael Matos, then Marcelo Arevalo / Mate Pavic win the group and Harri Heliovaara / Henry Patten qualify 2nd.

2) Regardless of score, if Harri Heliovaara / Henry Patten defeat Theo Arribage / Albaro Olivetti and Orlando Luz / Rafael Matos defeat Marcelo Arevalo / Mate Pavic, then Marcelo Arevalo / Mate Pavic win the group and Harri Heliovaara / Henry Patten qualify 2nd.

3) Regardless of score, if Theo Arribage / Albaro Olivetti defeat Harri Heliovaara / Henry Patten and Marcelo Arevalo / Mate Pavic defeat Orlando Luz / Rafael Matos, then Marcelo Arevalo / Mate Pavic win the group and Theo Arribage / Albaro Olivetti qualify 2nd.

4) Regardless of score, if Theo Arribage / Albaro Olivetti defeat Harri Heliovaara / Henry Patten and Orlando Luz / Rafael Matos defeat Marcelo Arevalo / Mate Pavic, then Marcelo Arevalo / Mate Pavic win the group and Theo Arribage / Albaro Olivetti qualify 2nd.

Doubles Mark Woodforde Group qualification scenarios after the completion of the 2nd round of the event's group stage. Kevin Krawietz and Tim Puetz have qualified for the semi-finals and:

1) Regardless of score, if Christian Harrison / Neal Skupski defeat Marcel Granollers / Horacio Zeballos and Simone Bolelli / Andrea Vavassori defeat Kevin Krawietz / Tim Puetz, then Kevin Krawietz / Tim Puetz win the group and Christian Harrison / Neal Skupski qualify 2nd.

2) Regardless of score, if Christian Harrison / Neal Skupski defeat Marcel Granollers / Horacio Zeballos and Kevin Krawietz / Tim Puetz defeat Simone Bolelli / Andrea Vavassori, then Kevin Krawietz / Tim Puetz win the group and Christian Harrison / Neal Skupski qualify 2nd.

3) Regardless of score, if Marcel Granollers / Horacio Zeballos defeat Christian Harrison / Neal Skupski and Simone Bolelli / Andrea Vavassori defeat Kevin Krawietz / Tim Puetz, then Kevin Krawietz / Tim Puetz win the group and Marcel Granollers / Horacio Zeballos qualify 2nd.

4) Regardless of score, if Marcel Granollers / Horacio Zeballos defeat Christian Harrison / Neal Skupski and Kevin Krawietz / Tim Puetz defeat Simone Bolelli / Andrea Vavassori, then Kevin Krawietz / Tim Puetz win the group and Marcel Granollers / Horacio Zeballos qualify 2nd.
