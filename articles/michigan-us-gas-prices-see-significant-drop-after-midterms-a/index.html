Michigan, US gas prices see significant drop after midterms, analysts say

TheTools · 09.11.2026 14:22 · source · permalink

Local News

Drivers are paying on average about 45 cents less for a gallon of regular fuel than they did last week nationwide

Gas prices across the nation have dropped significantly since last week, according to AAA, with drivers in Michigan paying an average of 38 cents less than they did the previous week.

Oil prices dropped below $100 dollars a barrel for the first time in weeks after last week’s election, and analysts say oil investors are hopeful tensions with Iran will ease after the political blue wave.

“Americans don’t have the patience to deal with high gas prices and now it looks like the needle may be moving,” said Patrick De Haan, head of petroleum analysis for GasBuddy. “The elections happened, Americans spoke, but it doesn’t necessarily immediately change the geopolitical tensions that have delivered the high prices.”

AAA says the national average for a gallon of regular unleaded gas is now $4.02 — about 34 cents cheaper than a month ago, but nowhere near where prices were before the Iran War began. The war between Russia and Ukraine is also affecting oil markets, according to analysts.

In Michigan, residents are paying an average of $4.14 for a gallon of regular unleaded gas, compared to about $4.52 last week. Metro Detroiters are paying an average of $4.10, compared to $4.55 the previous week.

The most expensive averages reported in the state were in Traverse City ($4.26), Benton Harbor ($4.25) and Ann Arbor ($4.24), while the least expensive averages were reported in Flint ($3.97), Lansing/East Lansing ($3.98) and Saginaw/Bay City/Midland ($4.01).

Some oil investors are hopeful that Democrats can ease tensions with Iran, once they take control of Congress in January. Before 2026, the national average had never gone above $4 per gallon during the month of November, according to AAA.
