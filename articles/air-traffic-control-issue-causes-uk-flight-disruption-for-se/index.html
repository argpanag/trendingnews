Air traffic control issue causes UK flight disruption for second time this month

TheTools · 21.09.2026 10:29 · source · permalink

Summary

  • Flights across the UK - particularly Scotland, Northern Ireland and the north of England - are being disrupted by a "technical issue" with air traffic control - here are the airports affected

  • National Air Traffic Services says the issue at the Prestwick centre in Ayrshire is "now fixed", and it's working to "safely lift air traffic regulations as quickly as we can"

  • Nats says the glitch was a "connectivity issue with part of our systems network", and was "nothing to do" with a software defect that led to the cancellation of 2,000 flights across the UK earlier this month

  • EasyJet - one of the airlines affected today - says this "once again calls into questions the resilience of Nats' systems"

  • "I'm cheesed off with air traffic control as it seems to be happening a lot," one delayed passenger at Birmingham airport tells us

Flights across the UK - particularly Scotland, Northern Ireland and the north of England - are being disrupted by a "technical issue" with air traffic control - here are the airports affected

National Air Traffic Services says the issue at the Prestwick centre in Ayrshire is "now fixed", and it's working to "safely lift air traffic regulations as quickly as we can"

Nats says the glitch was a "connectivity issue with part of our systems network", and was "nothing to do" with a software defect that led to the cancellation of 2,000 flights across the UK earlier this month

Live Reporting

Edited by Imogen James and Charlotte Hadfield

  1. Ryanair calls on Nats chief to step down after 'repeated system failures'published at 11:26 BST
    11:26 BST

    Ryanair has called on National Air Traffic Services (Nats) chief executive Martin Rolfe to resign after claiming over 140 of its flights have been delayed and more than 25,000 Ryanair passengers are expected to be disrupted following the outage.

    Ryanair's chief operations officer, Neal McMahon, says Nats has failed to deliver "either a reliable system or an effective back-up" in the wake of repeated technical issues in August 2023 and 8 September this year.

    He claims Rolfe has presided over "repeated system failures, repeated passenger disruption and repeated failures to deliver an effective back-up system", adding that "enough is enough".

    "How many times must UK passengers suffer disruption before Martin Rolfe is replaced by somebody competent?" he adds.

  2. Dozens of flights cancelled across UK as more disruption expected, says Ciriumpublished at 11:20 BST
    11:20 BST

    We've received an update from aviation analytics company Cirium, which says 35 flights departing from the UK have been cancelled or did not fly so far on Monday.

    It adds that of the 3,048 flights scheduled to arrive into the UK, 37 were cancelled or did not fly.

    "If this were the end of the day...this level of cancellations is close to normal (around 1%). However, as it's early in the day, we anticipate that operational performance will degrade quickly," a spokesperson says.

  3. 'I’m cheesed off with air traffic control as it seems to be happening a lot'published at 11:10 BST
    11:10 BST

    Alex Emery
    Your Voice

    Steve Davies, 74, from Evesham, is currently stuck at Birmingham airport where he is waiting to travel with easyJet to Edinburgh with his wife Sandie.

    He says they boarded the plane but were asked to disembark after 45 minutes.

    “We are feeling tired after an early start, and quite uncertain about what is happening," he says.

    “I’m cheesed off with air traffic control as it seems to be happening a lot. It’s not easyJet’s fault."

    Steve adds that "about 20 people left the flight and have gone home because they didn’t want to wait".

  4. Airports across UK report disruption as air traffic control fault fixedpublished at 10:59 BST
    10:59 BST

    While the air traffic control issue that caused disruption across parts of the UK has been fixed, delays are still expected while "things reset", the transport secretary has warned.

    Airports across the UK have reported varying degrees of disruption, they include:

    • Glasgow and Aberdeen - Arrivals and departures, primarily at Glasgow, "experiencing delays" with "further disruption expected", says operator of both airports AviAlliance
    • Manchester - Issue causing some delays, advises passengers to check with airlines before travel
    • Newcastle - Experiencing "some delays" and in contact with Nats
    • Liverpool - "Aware" of the issue and urges passengers to contact their airline
    • Bristol - Experiencing some delays and cancellations "similar to other UK airports"
    • London City - Experiencing "some disruption" and "working closely" with Nats to resolve the issue
    • Belfast City - Warns that there "will be disruption" to both arrivals and departures
    Share page

  5. Nats says 'connectivity issue' in network to blame for disruptionpublished at 10:51 BST
    10:51 BST

    In another statement, National Air Traffic Services says they "experienced a technical issue at our Prestwick centre in Scotland" early this morning.

    "To maintain safety, air traffic regulations were applied to manage traffic demand while our engineers investigated.

    "This was a connectivity issue with part of our systems network, specific to the operation in Scotland. It is nothing to do with the problem we had earlier this month."

    A short while ago, Nats confirmed the issue was fixed and said it was working with airports to lift air traffic restrictions as quickly as they can.

    Earlier this month, 2000 flights were cancelled after a software defect, affecting hundreds of thousands of passengers.

  6. Transport secretary says systems resuming but warns there 'may be some delays'published at 10:45 BST
    10:45 BST

    Transport Secretary Heidi Alexander says engineers have "fixed the problem and systems are resuming" after a technical issue with the UK's air traffic control.

    "I know this will be deeply frustrating for passengers after the previous issue.

    "There may be some delays as things reset. Please check with your airline for updates," she adds in a post on X.

    Nats has also confirmed the issue is resolved.

  7. Nats says technical issue now fixedpublished at 10:44 BST
    10:44 BSTBreaking

    National Air Traffic Services (Nats) says the issue in the Prestwick Centre is "now fixed".

    “We are working with airports and airlines to safely lift air traffic regulations as quickly as we can to minimise any further disruption," it says.

    "Passengers should still check the status of their flight with their airline. We apologise for the disruption.”

  8. Glasgow airport says flights are delayed with further disruption expectedpublished at 10:37 BST
    10:37 BST

    Glasgow airport says the technical issue affecting National Air Traffic Services's systems is causing delays, with "further disruption" expected.

    "A technical issue affecting NATS air traffic control services at the Prestwick Centre is causing disruption to some flight departures and arrivals," says the airport.

    "As a result, some services operating to and from our airports are experiencing delays," it says, adding that "further disruption is expected due to the knock-on impact across the network".

    "We apologise for any inconvenience and advise passengers to check with their airline for the latest flight information before travelling."

    Our reporter at the airport says the information board shows widespread delays to both domestic and international flights through the day.

  9. Growing list of cancellations at Glasgow airportpublished at 10:31 BST
    10:31 BST

    Paul Ward
    Reporting from Glasgow Airport

    When I arrived at Glasgow airport this morning there was a tannoy call announcing an easyJet flight to Paris was cancelled.

    That adds to a growing list. The departures board shows further cancellations on flights to Luton, Southampton, Belfast and Derry.

    There are also some cancellations on flights to Scottish islands and other flights are facing delays in taking off.

    Some passengers have been coming through on arrival flights, but again the board shows widespread delays to both domestic and international flights through the day.

  10. EasyJet: Disruption once again calls into question resilience of Nats’ systemspublished at 10:25 BST
    10:25 BST

    EasyJet says the flight disruption "calls into question the resilience of Nats' systems".

    The airline says in a statement the failure in the Prestwick Centre is causing delays, with some flights "unable to operate".

    “This, once again, calls into question the resilience of Nats’ systems and demonstrates the need for firm actions to prevent these repeated failures."

    It adds they will do "all we can" to minimise the impact on customers and advises passengers check their flight tracker for the latest information.

    It comes after an earlier disruption this month, caused by a "software defect" that left 2000 flights cancelled.

    10:09 BST

    Catherine Lyst
    BBC Scotland

    Lewis Fraser, from Inverness, is stuck on the runway at Glasgow airport.

    He had been hoping to make a connection flight to Chicago from Heathrow.

    "We boarded the flight as normal but were immediately told we were delayed," he says. "The latest update was around half an hour ago to say we might be waiting another hour and 45 minutes."

    He adds: "Some passengers have left the plane in the last few minutes. I’m on the 8am to Heathrow and due to fly to Chicago for work at 12:15 but that now looks unlikely... they’ve given us tea and snacks."
