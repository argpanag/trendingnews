What Makes The Traitors: New Blood Different from Celeb Traitors? How it Works

TheTools · 11.09.2026 15:39 · source · permalink

Need clarity about NBC’s The Traitors: New Blood versus The Traitors on Peacock? We explain.

Key Takeaways

  • The Traitors: New Blood is a civilian-focused spinoff of The Traitors, featuring 22 everyday Americans competing as Faithfuls or Traitors in missions and eliminations at Castle Ardross for a prize of up to $250,000.
  • Unlike the celebrity-driven Peacock seasons of The Traitors, New Blood airs on NBC and streams the next day on Peacock, while still following the franchise’s core gameplay format hosted by Alan Cumming.
  • The series includes a few celebrity appearances, including Harry Potter actors James and Oliver Phelps, but the competitors themselves are non-celebrities, marking a return to the format used in the original US season.

Fresh off his third straight Emmy win as Best Reality TV Host for the Peacock Original series The Traitors, Alan Cumming is back as Host of the franchise, but this time with a new twist. 

How to Watch

Watch the series premiere of The Traitors: New Blood Thursday, September 17 at 8 p.m. ET on NBC and next day on Peacock.

The Traitors: New Blood is NBC's all-new civilian version of the award-winning reality competition show which debuts Thursday, September 17 with a special two-hour extended premiere on NBC. It streams the next day on Peacock. 

If you’re a longtime fan of The Traitors franchise, then you're likely aware that the US version of The Traitors first debuted January 12, 2023 on Peacock, and has since dropped four award-winning seasons. But how does this new series, The Traitors: New Blood, fit into the universe?

What is The Traitors: New Blood?

This fresh iteration of The Traitors reality competition franchise has gone back to basics by casting average, everyday Americans to fill the 22 competitor spots for this inaugural season of The Traitors: New Blood. 

If you’re an OG fan, then you’ll remember that the first season of Peacock’s The Traitors featured an equal mix of celeb players and everyday people trying to Faithful or Traitor their way to winning up to $250,000. By The Traitors Season 2, the entire cast of players was comprised of reality celebs, entertainers, and even some royalty.

Is The Traitors: New Blood connected to The Traitors?

Yes, The Traitors: New Blood is both a throwback to Season 1 of The Traitors and a spinoff series that has its own twists. Both shows use the same basics: 22 contestants enter Castle Ardross in Scotland having to work together in a series of missions to build up a prize fund worth up to $250,000.

On day one, the players are secretly sorted by Cumming as either a Faithful or a Traitor. Then the latter will attempt to stay off the radar of the Faithful to avoid getting Banished by the voting players, and continue to undermine the Faithful's trust to claim the prize for themselves.

Where can I watch The Traitors: New Blood and The Traitors?

While New Blood first airs on NBC and streams the next day on Peacock, The Traitors US original celeb version is exclusive to Peacock. The streamer is the only place you can watch your favorite stars lie and deceive.

Does The Traitors: New Blood have any celebrities?

The new series certainly boasts the super-fabulous Alan Cumming as the Host and master of ceremonies for this cutthroat competition set amongst the Scottish Highlands. And then there’s Cumming’s princess of a pooch, Lala. 

Otherwise, there are no stars competing in this version. However, there are surprise celeb additions to New Blood with former Harry Potter film actors James Phelps (Fred Weasley) and his twin brother Oliver Phelps (George Weasley). Their introduction and broader role in this season is revealed in the first The Traitors: New Blood episode, "A New Dawn is Rising.” 

Which version of The Traitors should I watch first?

One unique aspect of The Traitors franchise is that you don’t have to watch any prior seasons of any version of the show to immediately understand and enjoy watching. However, if you’re a completist, we recommend starting with Season 1 of The Traitors on Peacock so you can see for yourself how the game has evolved and assess the vibe of how civilians play. 

Otherwise, you can dive into Seasons 2-4 (also streaming on Peacock) to get your fill of a whole array of celebrities battling for the bragging rights and the money.
