OPPO’s latest ColorOS 17 is scheduled to be unveiled in global markets, including India, on 13th October 2026, and ahead of this, the brand has announced Open Beta recruitment for several smartphones, including models from the Find X9 and Find X8 series, along with the Reno 15 Pro.
