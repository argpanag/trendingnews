Residents at Ohio senior facility targeted in $38K fraud scheme

TheTools · 12.11.2026 09:39 · source · permalink

Eleven people have been indicted on felony charges after a Cincinnati police investigation uncovered an alleged fraud scheme targeting senior citizens, including victims as old as 99, police said.

The investigation began after an elderly resident of The Kenwood by Senior Star in Madisonville reported that checks had been stolen from his apartment, according to Cincinnati police.

What initially appeared to be a single theft grew into an investigation involving stolen checks and credit card information belonging to elderly residents, police said.

A Cincinnati police detective spent months reviewing financial records, issuing subpoenas, interviewing witnesses and conducting surveillance. Police said the investigation ultimately identified six victims between the ages of 85 and 99 and documented more than $38,000 in financial losses.

Investigators identified multiple people they say were involved in negotiating stolen checks, depositing stolen funds and making unauthorized purchases.

Police did not immediately provide additional details about how the financial information was allegedly obtained or the relationship, if any, between the accused and the senior living community.

The case was presented to a grand jury, which returned indictments against 11 people on a total of 29 felony charges, police said.

Cincinnati police urged family members, caregivers and others to watch for signs of fraud or financial exploitation involving older adults and to report suspicious activity.
