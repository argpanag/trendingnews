See list of KFC stores closing after franchisee files for bankruptcy

TheTools · 19.11.2026 15:42 · source · permalink

NEW YORK -- Dozens of KFC restaurants have closed or will close soon, according to documents filed by Tasty Chick'n LLC. The company is seeking Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection.

The franchisee operated 75 KFC restaurants and another nine co-branded KFC/Taco Bell locations in 10 states, according to the filing.

Many of the restaurants appear to have closed already. More than a dozen restaurants are located in the Memphis area.

The KFC Corporation is a subsidiary of Yum! Brands, which franchises over 30,000 KFC restaurants in 150 countries and territories worldwide.
