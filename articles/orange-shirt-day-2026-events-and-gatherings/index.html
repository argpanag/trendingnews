Orange Shirt Day 2026: Events and Gatherings

TheTools · 29.09.2026 01:10 · source · permalink

Each year on Sept. 30th, known as Orange Shirt Day, Tribal Nations across North America pay tribute to the Native children who were forcibly removed from their families and placed in boarding and residential schools.

Here is Native News Online’s round-up of events happening tomorrow to commemorate Orange Shirt Day.
