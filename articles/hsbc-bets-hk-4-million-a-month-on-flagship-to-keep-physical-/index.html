HSBC bets HK$4 million a month on flagship to keep physical outlets relevant

TheTools · 08.11.2026 08:00 · source · permalink

The new flagship store combines banking with lifestyle and advisory services as the lender adapts its physical network to changing customer needs

HSBC is turning to coffee, macarons and essential-oil massages to draw customers into its branches, as traditional lenders rethink their physical networks amid growing use of digital banking.

Covering 32,000 square feet, it is “one of the biggest investments in recent years” for HSBC Hong Kong, the bank said.
