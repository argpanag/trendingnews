Updated Florida State Next Head Coach Odds: Schumann Leads, Fisher Passes Diaz

TheTools · 13.11.2026 00:15 · source · permalink

By Brady Trettenero in College Football

Published: November 12, 2026

  • Glenn Schumann leads the updated Florida State next head coach odds after Manny Diaz lost ground
  • Jimbo Fisher has moved ahead of Diaz, but his price is well below where he started the search
  • See the FSU coaching odds and how the leading candidates compare below

Georgia defensive coordinator Glenn Schumann has overtaken Duke coach Manny Diaz in the Florida State next head coach odds. The biggest move belongs to Diaz: he led our Friday board at 44¢, but buyers can now back him at 25¢. Jimbo Fisher has passed him without getting more expensive himself.

Florida State won 16-10 at Pitt on Friday under interim coach Rick Stockstill. That result goes in the standings, but it doesn’t settle a contract on who gets the permanent job. Here’s how the updated FSU coaching odds compare with Friday and our first look at the FSU coaching odds.

Why Glenn Schumann Leads the FSU Coaching Odds

Schumann leads the FSU coaching odds at 44¢, up three cents from Friday. The gap between him and Diaz went from three cents in the Duke coach’s favor to 19 cents in Schumann’s. Most of that swing came from traders lowering what they wanted for Diaz, rather than asking dramatically more for Schumann.

Georgia’s defensive coordinator brings a different résumé from either of the two head coaches behind him. He worked at Alabama before joining Kirby Smart at Georgia. Florida State athletic director Jeff Purinton also spent years at Alabama. That gives Purinton people he can call about Schumann’s work; it doesn’t tell us whether the two have discussed an offer.

At 44¢, Schumann is the most expensive candidate in the table, but he still costs less than half of the contract’s $1 payout. Florida State continues to list Stockstill as interim coach. The market is picking a favorite for an open job, not confirming who will get it.

Jimbo Fisher Passes Manny Diaz in Florida State’s Next Coach Odds

Fisher is second in Florida State’s next coach odds at 29¢. That’s six cents cheaper than Friday and less than half his 65¢ asking price in our first comparison. If you looked only at his place in the table, you might think a reunion had grown more likely over the weekend. The price moved the other way; Diaz simply fell further.

Fisher won the 2013 national championship at FSU. USA TODAY’s John Brice reported last week that Fisher had been calling potential staff members. That shows interest on Fisher’s side. It does not establish that Florida State has offered him the job.

Diaz has his own ties to Tallahassee. An FSU graduate, he was a graduate assistant on the 1999 national championship staff before eventually running programs at Miami and Duke. When the vacancy opened, he told reporters he was happy at Duke. The spread on his contract is a reason to be careful with any single number: buyers were bidding 17¢, while the lowest seller wanted 25¢. A last trade at 17¢ was not a price a new buyer could take.

Where Jake Dickert Fits in the Florida State Coaching Search

The clearest piece of news in the Florida State coaching search concerns a candidate well outside the leading three. Jake Dickert cost 49¢ in our first check. After Wake Forest announced a long-term extension last Thursday, his ask fell to 3¢ on Friday and now sits at 2¢. Unlike a price change alone, the extension gives readers a concrete reason his standing has changed.

For Fisher and Schumann, the more telling development would be confirmation from the school of an interview or an offer. Until then, the board answers a narrower question: what it costs today to back one candidate to become Florida State’s next permanent head coach. Stockstill leading the Seminoles against NC State on Saturday does not make him that hire.
