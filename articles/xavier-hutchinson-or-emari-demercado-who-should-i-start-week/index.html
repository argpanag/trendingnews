Xavier Hutchinson vs. IND — Trending Up With Marvin Harrison Jr. Out

Healthy for Thursday night against Indianapolis, Xavier Hutchinson has seen his roster rate jump to 64% after Marvin Harrison Jr. was ruled out with a hamstring injury. Listed behind Nico Collins on the outside alongside slot receiver Tank Dell, Hutchinson has capitalized on expanded opportunities over his last two games with 87 receiving yards and two touchdowns on 14 targets.

Our weekly projection calls for 10.2 PPR points on roughly three catches and 33 yards, keeping expectations measured despite his recent scoring surge. With Houston favored heavily at home, a run-leaning second half could cap passing volume, leaving Hutchinson as a touchdown-dependent flex or streaming option in deeper PPR formats.

Emari Demercado vs IND at home — expanded backup role as heavy favorites

Healthy entering tonight's home matchup against Indianapolis, Emari Demercado moves up to the primary backup role behind David Montgomery with Woody Marks ruled out due to a concussion. Claimed off waivers late last month, Demercado has handled 13 carries and two receptions over his last two games, though his efficiency has been modest at 2.5 yards per rush.

With Houston heavily favored at home, Demercado could see late clock-killing touches, though his low-volume share keeps his floor fragile.
