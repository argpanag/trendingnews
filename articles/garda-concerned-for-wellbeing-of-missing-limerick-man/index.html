Gardaí concerned for wellbeing of missing Limerick man

TheTools · 30.09.2026 13:39 · source · permalink
  • Limerick
  • News
  • Local News

Gardaí have appealed to the public for held in tracing the whereabouts of a 58-year-old man reported missing in County Limerick.

Gardaí say they are concerned for the wellbeing of 58-year-old Ian Webb, from Bruree, County Limerick, who was reported missing since Tuesday September 22.

They appealed for anyone with information on Ian’s whereabouts to contact Bruff Garda Station on 061 382 940, the Garda Confidential Line on 1800 666 111, or any Garda Station.

Gardaí described Mr Webb as being around 5’7’’, of stock build, with brown/grey hair and blue eyes.
