Pakistan guarantee boys’ doubles title as two home pairs reach ITF J-60 final

TheTools · 05.11.2026 20:25 · source · permalink

LAHORE - Pakistan’s emerging tennis stars guaranteed a home-country title in the boys’ doubles event of the ITF J-60 Bard Foundation Junior World Tennis Championships Islamabad-2026, with both local pairings storming into the final after impressive semifinal victories.
Ahtesham Humayun and Hamza Roman booked their place in the title clash by defeating China’s Aolun Ma and Haoxuan Xu 6-3, 6-4. In the other semifinal, M Haziq Asim and M Hassan Usmani outclassed Sri Lanka’s Aahil Kaleel and Pakistan’s Amir Mazari 6-1, 6-4 to complete an all-Pakistani final line-up.
However, Pakistan’s hopes in the boys’ singles ended at the quarterfinal stage, as Hamza Roman, Hassan Usmani and Ahtesham Humayun suffered defeats against international opponents. Aliaksandr Viarsotski of Belarus defeated Hamza Roman 6-3, 7-6(3), Thailand’s Phasin Sinchalong overcame Hassan Usmani 6-4, 6-4, while Sri Lanka’s Aahil Kaleel beat Ahtesham Humayun 6-1, 7-5.
The girls’ singles quarterfinals witnessed impressive performances, with Russia’s Varvara Rubtsova and Victoria Gruntova, China’s Meimei Hu and Hong Kong’s Chiu Kwan Nina Wang securing semifinal places. Rubtsova defeated Thailand’s Anyawee Paisarnpanuwong 7-5, 6-0, while compatriot Gruntova overcame Pimpisia Wongwanitkajon, also of Thailand, 6-4, 6-3. China’s Hu cruised past Thailand’s Ploypetch Daungkhiaw 6-1, 6-1, while Wang staged a comeback to beat China’s Yuqi Li 4-6, 6-0, 6-1. In the girls’ doubles semifinal, China’s Meimei Hu and Yijia Wang advanced to the final after defeating Thailand’s Ploypetch Daungkhiaw and Anyawee Paisarnpanuwong 6-3, 6-2.
