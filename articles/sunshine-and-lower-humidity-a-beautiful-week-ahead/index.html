Sunshine and lower humidity: A beautiful week ahead!

TheTools · 18.10.2026 22:59 · source · permalink

SARASOTA, Fla. (WWSB) - It’s been a beautiful sunny day today on the Suncoast, and it’ll continue to be comfortable throughout the night as the drier air has settled over our region.

Temperatures will be in the mid-to-lower 70s tonight with clear skies and a 0% chance of rain.

Gusty winds of up to 30 mph at times will continue into tomorrow, creating not the most enjoyable conditions to be on a boat. A Small Craft Advisory continues to be in effect until Tuesday at 3 am.

The start of the work week will begin windy on Monday, with gusts up to 30 mph before dwindling down to breezy conditions in the early morning hours of Tuesday.

Sunny skies are expected for the entire week, with afternoon highs in the lower 80s and overnight lows in the mid-60s. The most notable part of this week’s forecast will be the near 0% chance of rain, with dry conditions.

It will not feel like you are swimming in the air, nor will your glasses fog up when you step outdoors!

As for the tropics, the latest update by the National Hurricane Center has an area northeast of the Carolinas with a 30% chance of development within the next 7 days. Here on the Suncoast, we don’t expect any imminent threat if anything does organize.
