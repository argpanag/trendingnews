Half-hour call with 'idol' Yuvraj behind Sooryavanshi's record double

TheTools · 26.11.2026 04:47 · source · permalink

Vaibhav Sooryavanshi has credited a half-hour phone call with his "idol" Yuvraj Singh for the fastest double hundred in List A cricket. The 15-year-old hit 204 off 53 balls, featuring 28 sixes, against the UAE in the Rising Stars Asia Cup in Doha.

Sooryavanshi said he had gone to Yuvraj "very worried" about his form and came away with "a lot of technical things".

"Yesterday, I spoke to four people… only four people," Sooryavanshi revealed in an interview with bcci.tv. "One, of course, was my father. One was my childhood coach, Manish Ojha. And one was my friend who has been with me since U19 days, Pratham Jachak. The fourth was Yuvraj sir. I spoke to him for almost half an hour. He spoke to me a lot about batting.

"I was very worried, so I spoke to him openly. I told him, 'Sir, I am having these problems when I am going out to bat.' He explained everything to me very well for half an hour. He told me that if I play 20 balls, the score will be different. He also spoke a lot of technical things which helped me a lot today."

Sooryavanshi had made 26 against Nepal and 11 against Pakistan A in his first two matches in the ongoing tournament in Doha. Before that, he had made 18 for Bihar against Saurashtra in a Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy game.

"It's not easy. The two matches that I played here, and even before that the one-two matches in domestic cricket, I didn't make runs,” Sooryavanshi said. “So my mind was getting diverted a little, wondering what was going on. But I spoke to Yuvraj sir.

"He is my idol, of course, and if you talk to your idol, you listen to everything carefully. So I listened to everything carefully, and he told me a lot of things, point by point. I also watched videos of my batting later and I felt that I was making a mistake here. So I tried not to make that mistake today. I tried one-two things he told me and that helped me a lot."

The teenager said he had gone in looking to bat long, as he had in the previous two games, but changed his approach once he saw how the pitch was playing.

"When I was interviewed after the IPL finished in Ahmedabad, I had said that I can play in all three formats and that I can change my game according to the situation,” Sooryavanshi said. “I have that much control over my game. In the last two matches, I was trying to play in the same way. If it's a longer format, the game will run longer, so I was trying to play long. Even today, I had the same mindset, to go and see how the wicket is. So I did that, and I saw a couple of balls. I didn't feel the wicket was offering that much help. The ball was coming nicely onto my bat, so I played my game and backed my strength."
