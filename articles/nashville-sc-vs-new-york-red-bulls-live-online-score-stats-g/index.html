Nashville SC vs New York Red Bulls live online: score, stats, goals & updates | MLS 2026

TheTools · 01.11.2026 21:00 · source · permalink

Live updates as table toppers Nashville face New York Red Bulls at Geodis Park today, Sunday November 1, in Week 33 of the regular season.

After clinching the Supporters Shield last weekend, Nashville can afford to take their foot off the gas. But B. J. Callaghan’s boys will probably prefer to bring the regular season to a close in style with a victory in the last home game before the playoffs.

Only three MLS teams have managed to beat Nashville this year: Chicago Fire, Orlando and Toronto - all slim defeats by a single goal. The team’s 21 regular season victories include a 0-1 win against New York Red Bulls in New Jersey back in August.

New York need to win this game, and next weekend’s against Atlanta to have any chance of making it to the playoffs. The team is 10th in the Eastern Conference - two points off the play-in places and four behind Orlando in seventh, the last playoff berth.
