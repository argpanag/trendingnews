MCMC Records Statement Of Individual Over FB Content Allegedly Insulting Negeri Sembilan Royal Institution

TheTools · 22.10.2026 23:13 · source · permalink

CYBERJAYA, Oct 21 (Bernama) -- The Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission (MCMC) has recorded the statement of an individual to assist in an investigation into Facebook content suspected of containing elements insulting the Negeri Sembilan royal institution.

In a statement today, MCMC said the action was taken to complete an investigation under Section 233 of the Communications and Multimedia Act 1998 (Act 588).

“The individual believed to be operating the account appeared at the Cyberjaya Police Station today to assist in the investigation, and one mobile phone and one SIM card were seized,” MCMC said.

MCMC is investigating content uploaded through the Facebook account “Che GuBard”, which is suspected of containing elements insulting the Negeri Sembilan royal institution, as well as touching on the Yang Dipertuan Besar of Negeri Sembilan Tuanku Muhriz Tuanku Munawir and members of the Negeri Sembilan royal family.

MCMC takes seriously any use of online platforms that could incite hatred, insults or disrespect towards the royal institution.

“Freedom of expression must be exercised responsibly and in accordance with the laws of the country. Any further action will be taken based on the findings of the investigation and the applicable legal process,” MCMC said.
