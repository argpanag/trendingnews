Video: Crews find 'debris field' from air ambulance that disappeared off Boston coast

TheTools · 04.10.2026 19:59 · source · permalink

A search remains ongoing off the Massachusetts coast for an air ambulance carrying six people that disappeared during a flight from Bermuda to Boston. A “debris field” was found later Saturday off the coast of Nantucket, the Coast Guard said.
