Jaissle waiting to give green light to start Newcastle star exciting team-mates

TheTools · 12.09.2026 13:12 · source · permalink

Matias Fernandez-Pardo is getting closer to his first start for Newcastle(Image: 2026 Newcastle United)

Newcastle United head coach Matthias Jaissle has revealed he will make a decision on Sunday or Monday whether Matias Fernandez-Pardo is ready to make his first start for the club.

The Belgium international joined Newcastle in a £51million move from French side Lille on transfer deadline day and there is much excitement about what the 21-year-old forward will bring to Tyneside.

However, Jaissle has been keen to manage his load correctly given he last played a 90 minute match in May with Lille making him unavailable for selection while they sought to find a solution on his future amid Newcastle's interest.

He has made two appearances for the Magpies so far, both from the bench, showing some positive signs in the draw at home to Bournemouth and the Carabao Cup win at Millwall.

Yoane Wissa and Joe Willock have started up front together in both of those games and when asked if Fernandez-Pardo is closer to making his first start, Jaissle revealed when he will make a decision over his involvement ahead of Monday's trip to face Leeds United this weekend.

"Yeah, he's getting closer," Jaissle said during his pre-match press conference on Thursday. "I said that we need to be smart with his load management. Now he's getting more and more minutes.

"He has now almost a full training week to Leeds, that helps him a lot. I think it's the first time now since he arrived, and then we can make the decision on Sunday, maybe even on Monday."

Fernandez-Pardo's arrival has excited the dressing room, with captain Dan Burn among the Newcastle players to publicly reveal that his arrival has strengthened their faith in the club's brave new transfer direction.

"We welcomed Matias Fernandez-Pardo, a really sought-after player this window," Burn told Newcastle's official matchday programme.

"For us to get that over the line it shows the direction the club is going in. It takes lads time coming to England, learning the language, and the quicker they can get on board with that, the better.

"The club have taken a different approach from recent years and are building a younger, exciting squad. There is a lot of potential there, and lads who are big international players."

Jacob Ramsey has also been impressed with Fernandez-Pardo and he believes he is the embodiment of why Newcastle's decision to sign young, high potential players this summer will work.

"I think today he had a really good cameo," Ramsey told Chronicle Live after last weekend's 2-2 draw with Bournemouth. "He looks sharp, he looks quick. I think the recruitment this season has been really well. They want to sign young, hungry lads who have got big potential. He's one of them, 100%."
