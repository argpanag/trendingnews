First wave of GTA VI gamers report 'MAJOR' glitches making the game 'unplayable'

TheTools · 18.11.2026 22:48 · source · permalink

Lucky gamers in various time zones have managed to get their hands on GTA VI as the game celebrates its worldwide release date this week on November 19.

Despite years of waiting some gamers claim they have already encountered glitches while playing the highly anticipated game. Some overly dramatic players have even claimed these glitches could make the game "unplayable."

Posting a clip of a massive cargo ship clipping through a bridge one confused GTA player penned on social media: “Didn't even get to run the bike test, and this huge glitch is bugging the game.”

Responding to the clip another GTA fan said: “I love GTA as franchise, but jesus christ how did all of these outlets give this game a 10/10 with all of this ridiculous jank in it.”

Posting another supposed glitch on social media one player said: “Not even 15 minutes into the game and already found a ground breaking glitch.”

Social media users have pointed out previous games have been slated for having numerous glitches and problems after releasing.

Gamers have frequently pointed to Cyberpunk 2077 as an example of a game title which was flamed for its numerous day one glitches and issues

One X user stated: “Cyberpunk had bugs, but it got stoned and bombed; when Rockstar does it, it's somehow acceptable. The amount of dickriding these people do is crazy.”

Another commented: “Welcome back home, Cyberpunk 2077.”

A third stated: “Nah 15 minutes in and already breaking the game is crazy rockstar gonna patch this by tomorrow.”

Despite the criticism some online have defended the game and hit out against critics.

Some gamers have stumbled across numerous glitches while playing the game (Image: Getty Images)

One users on X – formerly known as Twitter – penned: “Why is Twitter being so weird about GTA 6 having bugs? I swear growing up bugs and glitches were laughed about but now when people see 1 issue they lose their s***.”

GTA VI’s release comes only hours after thousands of PlayStation Network users were unable to play their favourite games after a tech outage. PlayStation Network boasts 119 million monthly active users worldwide.

GTA VI releases worldwide this week on November 19 as analysts have predicted the game could make £3.3billion in sales this week alone.

The prediction by Wall Street’s Piper Sandler, citing a survey of 9,000 teenage gamers, suggests up to 45million copies of the PlayStation and Xbox title could fly off the shelves globally on release day.

The study by finance group Piper Sandler found that 70% of respondents with a PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series S/X plan to buy the game, including 85% on launch day across digital and physical formats.

As such, the firm said it expects GTA VI could sell more than 45 million units at launch, while industry expectations remain lower at 25 million units. With retail pricing ranging from £64.99 for the Standard Edition and £89.99 for the Ultimate Edition, it is suggested opening day global revenue could range from $2 billion to $4.5 billion.
