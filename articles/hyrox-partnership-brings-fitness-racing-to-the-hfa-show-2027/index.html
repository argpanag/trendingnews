HYROX Partnership Brings Fitness Racing to The HFA Show 2027 - Health &amp; Fitness Association

TheTools · 28.09.2026 17:20 · source · permalink

The HFA Show attendees will have an opportunity to experience a simulated HYROX race in the Centr booth while HFA members could fill one of 100 participant slots for HYROX Las Vegas or 100 spectator slots.

Health & Fitness Association (HFA) members will have an opportunity to sign up for participant and spectator slots on an HFA HYROX team for the HYROX Las Vegas event slated for March 12-14 at the Los Vegas Convention Center in Las Vegas. 

The opportunity is part of a partnership between HFA, HYROX, and Centr in conjunction with The HFA Show 2027 March 10-12 at Mandalay Bay in Las Vegas. 

With the two events overlapping, the partnership will connect the global fitness professionals gathering for The HFA Show with the athletes and fitness community participating in HYROX Las Vegas.

“The energy and active communities surrounding The HFA Show and HYROX complement each other incredibly well,” said Greta Wagner, HFA interim president and CEO. “The HFA Show brings together some of the best operators, suppliers and industry leaders from around the globe. Giving those leaders the opportunity to experience the excitement, community and results that come from fitness competition feels like a natural progression of what we are seeing our own club and studio members want.”

As part of the partnership, HFA will receive 100 participant registrations and 100 spectator tickets open only to HFA members. 

In addition, Centr and HYROX will create a simulated training environment within the Centr experience at The HFA Show. Attendees will be able to explore the HYROX Official Competition Equipment and training ecosystem behind HYROX and consider how fitness racing and hybrid training could translate to their own facilities and members.

“The Centr x HYROX experience offers HFA guests a firsthand look at how to bring Official Competition Equipment and performance-backed training solutions to their customers,” said Andrew Sugerman, CEO of Centr. “The experience will be a first of its kind at a commercial trade show and the start of a dynamic partnership between the three brands.”

The partnership will also create something new for exhibitors on the final day of The HFA Show. On Friday, March 12, the trade show floor will open to a select number of HYROX Las Vegas athletes and spectators with valid event passes. HYROX-affiliated gyms will also have access, bringing additional gym owners and coaches from across the HYROX network into the show. 

The move gives exhibitors an opportunity to put their products, equipment and innovations in front of a highly engaged fitness audience that typically would not attend an industry trade show. At the same time, HYROX athletes and spectators will get a look at the companies, products and ideas shaping the broader fitness industry. 

“HYROX is built around bringing people together through fitness, and this partnership creates a natural connection between the professionals shaping our industry and the athletes experiencing fitness racing firsthand,” said Eric O’Neil, head of affiliates at HYROX.

For HFA, the partnership reflects the continued evolution of The HFA Show as a place where the business and experience of fitness come together. By connecting industry leaders with athletes, coaches, and consumers, HFA is creating new opportunities for the global fitness community to experience the trends and innovations shaping the industry firsthand.

Additional details regarding the HFA HYROX team, VIP experience, member registration, and other event elements will be announced in the coming months.
