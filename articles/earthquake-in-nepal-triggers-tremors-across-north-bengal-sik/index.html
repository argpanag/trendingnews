The epicentre of the Nepal’s Koshi province which impacted north Bengal and Sikkim. People in North Bengal and Sikkim felt tremors triggering panic among residents. Photo: https://riseq.seismo.gov.in/riseq/earthquake

A 5.3-magnitude earthquake struck Nepal's Koshi province early on Tuesday (November 24, 2026), sending tremors across north Bengal and Sikkim, and triggering panic among residents.

Officials said there were no immediate reports of casualties or damage in either the north Bengal districts of Darjeeling, Jalpaiguri and Cooch Behar, where the tremors were felt, or in Sikkim.

The tremors were felt at around 7 a.m., prompting people in several areas to rush out of their homes.

According to the European Mediterranean Seismological Centre (EMSC), the earthquake occurred in Nepal's Koshi province at a depth of 10 km.

According to the National Centre for Seismology, the quake was located at 61 km north-northwest of Sikkim's Yuksom, at latitude 27.855 degrees north and longitude 87.909 degrees east.

Authorities were assessing the situation to determine whether the tremors had caused any damage in the affected areas.
