'Azur Promilia' Reaches No. 2 on App Store One Day Ahead of Launch

'Azur Promilia' has reached No. 2 on the free game charts of the Korean App Store.

Developed by Manjuu Games and published domestically by Nexon, 'Azur Promilia' climbed to second place on the Korean App Store's free popular game chart just one day ahead of its release.

Azur Promilia is a fantasy world RPG set on the fantasy continent of 'Promilia.' Players can bond with mysterious creatures known as 'Kibo' while enjoying content such as exploration, combat, and building. It is the latest title from Manjuu Games, the developer of Azur Lane, and is playable on both PC and mobile.

Officially launching on PC and mobile on Nov. 26, Azur Promilia will be available on Google Play, the App Store, and the Galaxy Store. According to an announcement on the official website, pre-downloads began at 4:00 PM on Nov. 24. During this period, users can only install the game and view the app's title screen, with actual gameplay becoming available after the official release.
