An Andor sequel? Not just yet, says Adria Arjona

Since Disney took the helm of the Star Wars franchise, few new entries have received the kind of universal acclaim that Andor has. While Andor Season 2 certainly felt like a finale to the story, it left the door open to a potential spin-off starring Bix Caleen. However, Adria Arjona insists that won’t happen unless series creator Tony Gilroy is on board.

During a recent interview, Adria Arjona was asked about the possibility of her returning to the Star Wars universe for another Andor story. While she seemed interested, there was one important stipulation that she had before she’d agree to an Andor spin-off. “Listen, I am the most loyal Gilroy there is,” she insisted. “If Tony Gilroy is in, I’m right there with him. I respect so much what he’s created, and I love him so dearly that I don’t want to put my fingerprints on it.”

So it is possible, but when could we get the Andor spin-off that was hinted at in the Season 2 finale? Not till a little more time has passed. “It might be too soon. I don’t want to say never, but I love the way he ended that show. I respect it, so I want to keep it like that for now.”

It has been more than a year since the Andor series finale in May 2025, but clearly fans are still eager to return to that time in the Star Wars timeline. However, we won’t be getting an Andor spin-off until a little more time has passed and Tony Gilroy comes on board.
