Bodø/Glimt’s unbeaten run at home in the UEFA Champions League (UCL) is set to be tested by a confident LASK side, who arrive at Aspmyra Stadion boosted by a five-match winning streak.

Match News and Current Form

Bodø/Glimt made it three wins in four (D1) with a 4-2 win away at Molde in the Eliteserien to put them in pole position to win their domestic league with just three games to go. It’s been a slightly different picture in the UCL league phase though, with their results to date (W1, D1, L2) leaving them outside the knockout spots ahead of this round. Playing at home could help turn things around, as Kjetil Knutsen’s men have won ten of their last 12 competitive matches at this venue (D1, L1) and remain unbeaten across their last seven European home games (D1), with their two home games this season responsible for their four points in this league phase.

LASK won 2-1 against Hartberg at the weekend for a seventh win in their last eight matches (L1), including five in a row. The visitors remain outside the top two in the Austrian Bundesliga, but with two consecutive UCL wins, they have moved into the top 16 ahead of this matchday. Dietmar Kühbauer’s men will feel comfortable on the road having won their last three competitive trips, but their away record in Europe remains a concern. The Black-Whites have won just one of their last 12 away games in continental action (D1, L10), and having conceded in each of those 12, their defensive struggles threaten to drag them down once again.

Head-to-Head History

This will be just the fifth match between Norwegian and Austrian clubs in the European Cup/UCL. Bodø/Glimt are facing an Austrian side for the third time (W1, L1), while LASK have won all four of their previous European games against Norwegian opponents.

Hot Stats and Streaks

Ten of the last 13 goals scored in Bodø/Glimt’s UCL matches have come in the second half.

Twelve of Bodø/Glimt’s last 19 UCL matches saw both teams score.

Seven of LASK’s last nine matches have produced over 3.5 goals.

None of LASK’s last 24 away UEFA matches finished goalless.

Key Players to Watch and Missing Players

Jens Petter Hauge has seven career UCL goals for Bodø/Glimt, six of which arrived in the second half, although the only exception came in this edition against Napoli. Christoph Lang’s last six club goals have come on the road, with four arriving on or before the 30th minute.

Bodø/Glimt have no new major injury concerns, while Andrés Andrade was injured in LASK’s last match.
