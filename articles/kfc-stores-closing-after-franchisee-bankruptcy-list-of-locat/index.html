KFC Stores Closing After Franchisee Bankruptcy: List of Locations Impacted - Newsweek

TheTools · 18.11.2026 21:15 · source · permalink

Reporter

A number of KFC locations are set to shutter or have already closed as one of its franchisees, Tasty Chick'n LLC, battles sliding sales that have now pushed it into bankruptcy.

Tasty Chick'n LLC owns 75 KFC locations and nine KFC/Taco Bell co-branded restaurants across 10 states, according to court records. On Monday, the company filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy to ensure KFC owner, Yum Brands Inc., does not end the pair’s franchise deal.

Newsweek has contacted Tasty Chick’n and its legal representatives via email for comment.

Why Is Tasty Chick'n Filing for Bankruptcy?

Tasty Chick’n was created in 2021 to “develop, acquire, own, and operate KFC and Taco Bell branded restaurants pursuant to franchise agreements with KFC and Taco Bell and an overarching relationship agreement with the Yum! Parties,” according to court filings.

In listing reasons for seeking bankruptcy, Tasty Chick'n cited sliding sales and said rising food, labor, operating, and rent costs had hurt profits. It also described “substantial remodel and upgrade obligations” resulting from its franchise agreement with KFC.

“These pressures have all led to more than $15.6 million in unpaid royalties and, on September 15, 2026, the Yum! Parties provided the Operating Debtors with a notice of intent to terminate the Franchise Agreements,” said the operator’s CEO and President Thomas C. Scott.

The Texas-based franchisee, along with three affiliates, has between $50 million and $100 million in assets and $100 million to $500 million in liabilities.

According to Bloomberg, an affiliate of Yum Brands has warned that sudden store closures could result in “significant harm to the KFC brand, causing KFC to ‘go dark’ in select markets and eroding brand presence and consumer confidence in those communities.”

Which KFCs Are Closing?

The company said it entered Chapter 11 to keep the restaurants open while it attempts to sell them. However, dozens of restaurants are shuttering or have already closed, according to court records, with some locations kept open for the court-supervised sale.

Court filings list nine locations where Tasty Chick’n will be exiting leases amid the bankruptcy proceedings.

  • 2017 North Hazel, Hope, AR

Virginia

  • 3401 Orange Avenue, Roanoke, VA

What's Next?

Tasty Chick'n's bankruptcy case will now move through federal court as the company attempts to sell its restaurant portfolio and avoid further disruptions to its KFC operations. The outcome may determine whether the franchisee can maintain its relationship with Yum Brands or whether more KFC locations ultimately go dark in affected markets.
