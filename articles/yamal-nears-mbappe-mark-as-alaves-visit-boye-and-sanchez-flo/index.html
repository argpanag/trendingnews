Yamal nears Mbappe mark as Alaves visit, Boye and Sanchez Flores battle Barcelona hoodoos

Spotify Camp Nou stages Barcelona’s meeting with Deportivo Alavés today, Saturday, in the 11th round of La Liga. Attention centres on Lamine Yamal and Raphinha’s prolific starts, while the visitors confront unwelcome streaks.

Goal.com notes that Yamal has scored 38 goals in 111 La Liga matches, with only Kylian Mbappé managing more before turning 20 in Europe’s big five leagues in the 21st century, 41 in 79.

OneFootball Videos

Raphinha leads this season’s scoring charts across Europe’s big five leagues with 13 goals in nine matches. He has already matched his La Liga total from last season, 13 in 22.

Alavés coach Quique Sánchez Flores has not won any of his seven previous league visits to Barcelona, drawing two and losing five. He has lost the last four, with his sides failing to score in four of those games.

Forward Lucas Boyé, Alavés’ top scorer in La Liga this season with five goals, is yet to net in eight appearances against Barcelona. His only longer droughts in the competition are against Celta Vigo and Sevilla, 10 matches each.
