Big Brother confirms that Tiffany Pollard has officially entered the house

TheTools · 27.10.2026 17:48 · source · permalink

Look who's back!

It's been rumoured for a while, but Tiffany Pollard is now officially back on Big Brother, with the legendary housemate now confirmed to have entered the iconic house as part of a major comeback.

In a post, which included a photo of the star, via the show's official social media channels, a caption read: "The HBIC is in town! Tiffany Pollard has entered the Big Brother House. Break a leg."

The comment section was full of excited fans of the ITV reality series, with one writing: "I think she will have a massive fight with Philip. I can’t wait for the chaos. House of horror indeed."

"THE HBIC IS IN THE HOUSE! One of the most iconic housemates of all time," added another. "Tiffany is gonna shake that house up & hopefully humble some people & put them in their place especially Rochelle & Philip & Rhianna & Omar the vile gossip group! I can't wait!"

"I can't wait to see this. Let's see how the HBIC deals with Rochelle and Rhianna lol," wrote another fan, while a fourth echoed prior sentiments as they wrote that a "queen and icon" had entered the house.

What to Read Next

One fan thought that Tiffany would bring some "madness" to proceedings, while another suggested that evicted housemate Johanne should also return so the pair could appear in the house at the same time.

Tiffany debuted in the franchise back in 2016 as she starred in the 17th season of Celebrity Big Brother, where she eventually finished in fourth place. She has returned for spin-offs Bit on the Side and Late & Live, but this latest comeback is what fans have wanted.

It was reported last month that she was set for a guest stint on the current season, with hosts AJ Odudu and Will Best effectively confirming the reports last week as Tiffany recorded a voice message ahead of her comeback.

Dubbing herself the 'Head Bitch in Charge' (HBIC), Tiffany pledged that housemates and viewers were not ready for the "queen of destruction", but what will she get up to upon her return to the series?

Big Brother airs on ITV2 and streams on ITVX.
