Winnipeg police officer charged with sexual assault, choking intimate partner | CBC News

TheTools · 18.09.2026 14:51 · source · permalink

Woman told officers 20-year police member broke into her home, threatened and assaulted her

A Winnipeg police officer is facing several charges, including sexual assault, after police say he broke into a woman's home and assaulted her on Wednesday.

West District patrol officers responding to a domestic-related 911 call just after 10 p.m. saw a man assaulting a woman inside a home when they arrived, Winnipeg police said in a Thursday news release.

The woman said the man — who is in his 40s and has been a member of the Winnipeg Police Service for 20 years — broke into her home, threatened to kill her and assaulted her, the release said.

Police said the officer's name will not be released to protect the woman's identity. He remains in custody.

He has been charged with sexual assault, as well as assault by choking, suffocating or strangling against an intimate partner, and with overcoming resistance by attempting to choke, suffocate or strangle another person.

He's also been charged with breaking and entering and committing assault at a dwelling house, and two counts of uttering threats to cause death or bodily harm.

The Independent Investigation Unit of Manitoba, which investigates serious incidents involving police in the province, has been notified since the matter has been deemed to fall within its mandate, the news release said.
