A pleasant week before widespread rain returns Friday

TheTools · 15.09.2026 17:24 · source · permalink

Pleasant weather will stick around for much of the week before our next system brings widespread rain and thunderstorms Thursday night through early Saturday. Locally heavy rain will be possible, especially Friday and Friday night, but there is still some uncertainty about where the heaviest amounts will fall. The rest of the weekend will trend drier, with comfortable, fall-like temperatures continuing into early next week.

The rest of this afternoon will be mostly sunny and pleasant, with highs near 70 degrees. Northwest wind will gradually decrease, setting us up for a clear, cool night with lows in the upper 40s.

Wednesday will bring a mix of sunshine and clouds, with highs in the low to mid 70s. A few light showers or sprinkles will be possible later in the day and Wednesday night, but most of the day will stay dry and any rainfall amounts will be light. Thursday will be a little warmer, with highs in the mid to upper 70s and increasing clouds.

Showers will begin moving in Thursday night, becoming more widespread Friday. Rain and scattered thunderstorms will continue Friday night before gradually ending from west to east Saturday morning, although a few showers could linger into the afternoon. Friday’s cloud cover and rain will hold highs near 70 degrees to the low 70s. A few stronger storms will also be possible near the Minnesota-Iowa border, depending on how far north a warm front moves and whether enough warming occurs after the morning rain.

Some areas could pick up one to three inches of rain, with locally higher amounts where thunderstorms repeatedly cross the same locations. Models still disagree on whether the heaviest band will set up farther north across Minnesota or closer to the Iowa border, so the amounts and placement will need some fine-tuning. The map shows the preliminary five-day rainfall forecast through 6 a.m. Sunday, and some of the higher amounts may be overdone. Locally heavy rain could lead to flooding concerns, especially where it falls over areas that have already received quite a bit recently.

Saturday afternoon will gradually dry out, with clouds lingering and highs in the low 70s. Saturday night will be mostly cloudy, with lows in the mid 50s. Sunday will bring a mix of clouds and sunshine, light wind and highs in the upper 60s to low 70s. A stray shower will be possible, but most of the day should stay dry.

That comfortable weather will continue into early next week. Monday will be partly cloudy and mostly dry, with highs near 70 degrees after morning lows around 50.

Keep your KEYC First Alert Weather app handy for updates, especially if you have plans Friday or early Saturday.
