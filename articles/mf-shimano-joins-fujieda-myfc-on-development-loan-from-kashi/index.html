MF Shimano Joins Fujieda MYFC on Development Loan from Kashiwa Reysol | Official Website of J.LEAGUE

TheTools · 11.09.2026 10:59 · source · permalink

Wed, 9 Sep 2026, 17:40 (JST)

Fujieda MYFC have announced the signing of midfielder Rei Shimano from Kashiwa Reysol on a development loan. The loan deal runs from 9 September 2026 until 30 June 2027. He will wear the No. 38 shirt.
 
Shimano joined Kashiwa in 2026 and did not make an appearance in the MEIJI YASUDA J1 LEAGUE this season.
 
Upon joining the club, Shimano commented:
 
“I’ll give everything I have to help the club win the J2 title and earn promotion to J1. Thank you for your support.”
