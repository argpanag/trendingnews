Truck hits overbridge on Auckland motorway, traffic banked up

TheTools · 06.10.2026 14:59 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

A truck collided with an overbridge on Auckland's Ellerslie Panmure Highway. Photo / NZTA

A truck has collided with an overbridge on an Auckland motorway, temporarily blocking several lanes.

NZ Transport Agency Waka Kotahi (NZTA) said a crash blocked lanes on the Ellerslie-Panmure Highway.

The scene was cleared about 1pm.

“Allow extra time for congestion to ease.”

Police told the Herald it received a report of a truck which hit an overbridge on State Highway 1, Penrose.

“No injuries and the scene has now cleared.”
