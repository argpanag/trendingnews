First Alert Weather: Turning drier and brighter tomorrow

TheTools · 13.09.2026 21:36 · source · permalink

Today’s *First Alert Weather Day* was focused on a round of rain, heavy at times with embedded storms, that impacted outdoor plans during the morning hours and led to numerous instances of flash flooding. Fortunately, we are now dry and will remain mostly dry overnight. We’ll start the workweek on a brighter note with decreasing humidity behind a cold front. Temperatures will run a bit below average over the coming days, but the cooler stretch won’t last long as highs head back to near 80 degrees by Wednesday. Our next chance for rain (other than an isolated shower overnight tonight) arrives late Thursday night into Friday morning.
