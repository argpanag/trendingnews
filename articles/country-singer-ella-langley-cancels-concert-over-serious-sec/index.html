Country singer Ella Langley cancels concert over 'serious security threat' to fans, crew

TheTools · 10.10.2026 00:36 · source · permalink

Langley said the decision followed guidance from local authorities, venue officials and her security team

Ella Langley on her sophomore album 'Dandelion'

Country music star Ella Langley abruptly canceled a scheduled concert after a "serious security threat" raised concerns about the safety of fans, venue staff and her team, the singer announced in an Instagram post.

"Due to a serious security threat involving the safety of my fans, the venue staff, and my team, the incredibly difficult decision was made to cancel tonight’s show. This decision was made under the advisement of local authorities, the venue, and my security team," the music star began.

She said the decision followed guidance from local authorities, venue officials and her security team, adding that she refused to put anyone in danger. The singer did not disclose the nature of the threat or provide additional details.

"I know how much time, travel, money, and planning goes into coming to a show, and I can’t tell you how sorry I am. This is not a decision that was made lightly. I was so looking forward to spending tonight with you, but your safety will always mean more to me than anything else. I refuse to put anyone in harm’s way," the country music star's statement concluded.

The BOK Center, where the concert was set to be held, also released a statement on Friday. "The safety and well-being of everyone involved is our absolute priority, and after careful consideration, we have determined that the show cannot proceed. We understand the disappointment this news brings and apologize to fans who planned to attend. The safety of fans is our foremost concern and will always come first," the statement read.

The official Instagram account for the BOK Center said that ticket refunds and additional information will be shared as soon as possible.

The Tulsa Police Department posted an update on Facebook Friday. 

"As with any large event, the Tulsa Police Department goes to great lengths to ensure that it is safe for eventgoers and those coming to perform," the department said, adding that before the event, its K-9 unit, Bomb Team, Patrol, and BOK security did a sweep for anything dangerous and "did not find anything."

The department continued, "A call was made threatening the lives of people attending the event. Out of an abundance of caution, we swept the building and surrounding area and again found no credible threat to the event's safety. We advised the performer's staff that we found no credible threat to the event, but they decided to cancel the performance anyway," the account said.

The Tulsa Police Department concluded with sharing that the "incident is under investigation." 

Fox News Digital has reached out to Langley's representative and the BOK Center for additional comment.

Langley is headlining The Dandelion Tour in support of her second studio album, "Dandelion." The arena tour began May 7 in Toledo, Ohio, and was extended with 21 additional dates.
