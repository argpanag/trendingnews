WALLOWA COUNTY, Ore. – A small city in Oregon was shaken by a magnitude 4.3 earthquake this morning. 

Joseph, Oregon, is the area where the earthquake occurred at 7:41 am UTC.

According to the USGS, the earthquake caused moderate shaking for residents in the area, with a chance of very light damage.

There is a 6% chance of an aftershock within the next week.
