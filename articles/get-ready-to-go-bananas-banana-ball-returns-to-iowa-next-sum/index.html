Get ready to go bananas: Banana Ball returns to Iowa next summer

TheTools · 16.10.2026 02:17 · source · permalink

Banana Ball is returning to Iowa in 2027, with games scheduled at Principal Park in Des Moines and the Field of Dreams site in Dyersville.

The Iowa Cubs announced that the Firefighters will play the Loco Beach Coconuts at Principal Park on June 25 and 26. The matchup was revealed during Banana Ball’s livestreamed Selection Show on Thursday.

In Dyersville, the Indianapolis Clowns will face the Firefighters at the This Is Iowa Ballpark at the Field of Dreams Movie Site on Sept. 10 and 11. Organizers have not yet announced game times or other event details.

Banana Ball is a fast-paced version of baseball built around fan interaction, trick plays and unusual rules. For example, a fan who catches a foul ball counts as an out.

Tickets are not on sale yet. Fans can enter the ticket lottery at BananaBall.com for a chance to buy tickets; entering does not guarantee an opportunity to purchase. The lottery closes Oct. 31, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. ET. Standard tickets start at $35, while meet-and-greet tickets start at $125.

Premium seating for the game at Principal Park will be sold through the Iowa Cubs.
