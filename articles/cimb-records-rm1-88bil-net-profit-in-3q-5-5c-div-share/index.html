CIMB records RM1.88bil net profit in 3Q, 5.5c div/share

TheTools · 25.11.2026 13:42 · source · permalink
    bookmark

KUALA LUMPUR: CIMB Group Holdings Bhd's net profit in the third quarter ended Sept 30, 2026, dipped to RM1.88bil from RM2.08bil in the year-ago quarter, underpinned by a lower topline of RM5.42bil against RM5.95bil previously.

Over the nine-month period, the bank turned in a net profit of RM5.73bil as compared to RM5.94bil in the same period in 2025, while revenue slipped to RM16.39bil from RM17.05bil in the previous comparative period.

The group said net interest income in 9M26 was RM11.14bil, supported by a 6.5% year-on-year (y-o-y) expansion in total assets and loans on a constant currency basis. Net interest margin, however, moderated 10bps y-o-to 2.03%. 

Non-interest income (NOII) was RM5.25bil, with growth driven by broader customer segment and product penetration, alongside healthy demand for foreign exchange and hedging solutions. Wealth NOII and Treasury client sales grew 10.3% and 5.6% YoY respectively.

Meanwhile, operating expenses declined 4.2% y-o-y despite one-off transformation costs in Thailand, resulting in an improved cost-to-income ratio of 46.3%.

The board declared a special dividend of 5.5 sen per share, with ex date on Dec 11, 2026, and payable on Dec 24, 2026.

Group CEO Novan Amirudin said the 9M26 performance reflects the value of the OneCIMB network and continued progress under Forward30, which delivered a resilient return on equity of 11%.

"We continue to invest and accelerate our four growth engines, which are profitable balance sheet growth, Wealth, Treasury client sales and fees, to sustain earnings momentum.”

He added the group will leverage its integrated Asean franchise to grow cross-border income beyond domestic markets. "Our differentiated digital ecosystem further strengthens our ability to deepen client relationships and expand our reach."
