Model weds AFL star in show-stopping $7000 gown

TheTools · 26.11.2026 21:21 · source · permalink
  • Entertainment
  • Celebrity
  • Aussie stars

Updated November 27, 2026 - 12.01am. First published November 26, 2026 - 9.21pm

AFL player Josh Daicos and model Annalise Dalins have finally shared the first photos from their lavish Hunter Valley wedding with the world.

The newlyweds shared a curated series of photos from their nuptials to Instagram on Thursday night with the caption: “Mr & Mrs Daicos 21.11.2026.”

Annalise Dalins and Josh Daicos looked utterly smitten on their wedding day in the Hunter Valley. TikTok/@annadaicos

Collingwood player Daicos, 27, looked smart in a cream tuxedo jacket, black trousers and matching bow tie, with a luxe watch peeking out from under his sleeve.

But it was Dalins, 25, who stole the show in a stunning strapless wedding dress from Kyha Bridal, which featured a corset bodice, drop waist and full tulle skirt.

The $6595 gown is described on the Kyha website as “a modern take on tradition”, with a long train and luxe lace lining hidden inside.

Dalins paired it with an understated veil and a simple gold and diamond earring stack, leaving the focus on her wedding band and huge diamond engagement ring.

Photos from their big day, snapped by photographer Jack Henry, show the lovebirds cosied up on the grounds of Redleaf, a luxury private villa and wedding venue nestled between wineries in the Hunter Valley.

One shot from their ceremony caught Daicos dipping his bride in a kiss as they were showered in rose petals.

In another they can be seen flashing their rings at the camera.

Model Dalins was clearly in her element for their posed portraits, but even she couldn’t keep the huge grin off her face in a few candid shots.

Dalins couldn’t keep the grin off her face as they posed for wedding portraits. TikTok/@annadaicos

Famous friends and family flooded the joint post with love, including Daicos’ brother Nick, who simply wrote: “The best”.

Fellow newlywed Rebecca Harding, who recently wed Andy Lee, said: “Gorgeous!!!! Congratulations guys.”

“Wooooow so gorgeous you two!!!! Biggest congratulations,” Jade Tunchy commented.

Bec Judd added, “Stunning you two!!!!!!”

Dalins also shared sweet behind the scenes footage from their big day on TikTok – where she has already changed her handle to her married name – with the song Heaven by Bryan Adams playing in the background.

The happy couple flash their new wedding rings. TikTok/@annadaicos

She and Daicos tied the knot on Saturday after a two-year engagement in what fans have dubbed Collingwood’s “royal wedding” of the year.

By the looks of the wedding photos, their big day turned out exactly as planned.

Dalins added, “We’ve just tried to keep the day super focused on what we love, our interests, and what reflects us.”

The dress reportedly cost $5900 and was complemented by Temelii jewellery and a short veil embroidered with two words: Mrs Daicos.

The model wore a bridal look for her hens night last month. Instagram/annalisedalins_
