Daniel Hall, Taxiride band member, dies unexpectedly aged 48

TheTools · 15.11.2026 02:39 · source · permalink
  • Entertainment
  • Celebrity
  • Showbiz

November 15, 2026 - 2.39am

Daniel Hall, the Aussie musician who co-founded Melbourne pop rock band Taxiride, has died aged 48.

Hall passed away suddenly on Friday, with his bandmates confirming his death in a statement posted on social media.

Tim Wild, Tim Watson, Daniel Hall, and Jason Singh were the founding members of Taxiride. Archive Photos

“It is with the heaviest of hearts we announce the passing of our youngest brother in arms, Daniel James Hall,” they began.

“We shared some amazing experiences and his departure doesn’t seem real. Rest In Peace Dan.”

Hall, Jason Singh, Tim Wild and Tim Watson were the founding members of the 90s Aussie rock band.

Hall’s girlfriend Jess shared her own statement on Facebook.

“As you may have already heard, Dan Hall passed away unexpectedly here at home in the early hours of Friday. I am completely devastated. Your prayers and kind messages are appreciated. Love, Jess,” she wrote. 

Hall was a vocalist, guitarist and pianist for Taxiride.

Their hit 1999 album Imaginate reached number one on Aussie charts and was certified double platinum.

Taxiride’s hit singles included Get Set, Everywhere You Go, Can You Feel and Nothing In This World, which all ranked highly in the Australian Top 50 singles charts. 

Hall’s talent was wide-ranging, he was a vocalist, guitarist and pianist.  Instagram/@taxiride_ft_ttsar

In 1999, they won Breakthrough Artist – Single for their hit song Get Set. In a historic tie, they shared the award with Alex Lloyd.

Get Set also grew popular in the US after it featured on the soundtrack to the Reese Witherspoon film Election.

Hall left Taxiride in 2000 but returned years later.

Though Hall left Taxiride in 2000, he returned to the band years later.  Taxiride

Hall was also a popular session musician and vocalist. His 2004 song Don’t Look Back became iconic on Aussie soap Neighbours. 

Fans were devastated by the news of Hall’s passing.

“Oh that’s so sad. He was an incredible musician and wrote some beautiful songs. Voodoo Sin and Stone in the Ocean are magical. Glad I got to see him perform over the years. Rest easy, Dan,” one wrote.

Another commented, “Oh god, this hurts. I’m so sorry for your loss.”
