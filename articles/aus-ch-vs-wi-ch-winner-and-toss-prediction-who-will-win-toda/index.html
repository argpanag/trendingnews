Sharjah Cricket Stadium Pitch Report

The surface at the Sharjah Cricket Stadium remains a traditional paradise for attacking batters, offering true bounce and minimal seam deviation under floodlights.

Sharjah’s compact boundary dimensions allow batters to clear the ropes with ease once they get their eye in during the powerplay overs.

Spinners must maintain strict line and length, as any flighted delivery outside off-stump risks being dispatched into the stands.

Heavy evening dew is expected during the second half of the match, making the ball slick to grip and heavily favouring the chasing side.
