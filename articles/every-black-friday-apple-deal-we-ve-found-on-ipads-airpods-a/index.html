Apple has had quite a year, releasing several reimagined devices and pushing into new product categories.

There’s the new MacBook Pro (the first touchscreen MacBook) and the exciting iPhone Duo (its first foldable iPhone). And the company launched its first-ever smart display, HomeView.

But none of Apple’s products were safe from the inevitable price hikes that have rocked the consumer technology industry this year. Due to the ongoing AI component shortage, the company’s products are now much more expensive.

So, finding a good deal is more important than ever — especially if you’re looking for a new iPhone, iPad, MacBook, or any other Apple gadget. To make the process easier and less daunting, we’ve gathered the best Black Friday discounts on our picks across Apple’s full lineup.
