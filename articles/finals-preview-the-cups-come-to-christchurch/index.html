FINALS PREVIEW - The Cups Come To Christchurch!

TheTools · 05.09.2026 05:30 · source · permalink

This time tomorrow, two things will be certain: there will be new holders of the Delivereasy Chatham Cup and Delivereasy Kate Sheppard Cup, and the first ever football match at Ōtautahi Christchurch’s amazing One New Zealand Stadium will have been played.

Over a decade in the making, the stadium now sits proudly in the Garden City’s CBD, and while there has been plenty of oval-ball activity, tomorrow’s cup finals will be the first time a football match has been played at arguably the best stadium in the country.

And there is good reason the cups have come to Christchurch for the first time since 2013. After brilliant seasons in both the men’s and women’s competitions, Cashmere Technical are in contention for both trophies, taking on 2019 winners Eastern Suburbs for the Delivereasy Kate Sheppard Cup, and first-time finalists Fencibles United for the Delivereasy Chatham Cup.

 



Delivereasy Kate Sheppard Cup – 1pm
Eastern Suburbs vs Cashmere Technical
Free entry
Watch live and free on FIFA+ via DAZN

Aucklanders Eastern Suburbs come into the final off the back of a brilliant Lotto NRFL season, where they pushed regional powerhouse Auckland United all the way, narrowly missing out on the title on goal difference.

In Maddi Ollington, they possess a real threat, with the attacker finishing top of the Lotto NRFL Golden Boot race with 17 goals.

 



Suburbs smashed Ellerslie 6-0 in the semi-final, including an Ollington hat-trick, and will be gunning to claim the cup they last won in 2019.

For Cash Tech, their semi-final was a much closer affair, coming from behind to win 2-1 with two late goals in the final minutes against last year’s finalists Wellington Phoenix Reserves.

But while it may be the first time they have qualified for the Kate Sheppard Cup final, they will come into the game with plenty of confidence, having comfortably won the Women’s South Island League, finishing 12 points clear of closest rivals Dunedin City Royals.

Tech also possess a real danger up top, with forward Margarida Dias netting 20 goals to finish comfortably top of the scoring charts. But it isn’t just offensively that the Christchurch side are strong, having conceded only four goals in an undefeated league season.


Delivereasy Chatham Cup – 4pm
Cashmere Technical vs Fencibles United
Free entry
Watch live and free on FIFA+ via DAZN

The second of the Cash Tech double-header finals sees first-time finalists Fencibles United travel to One New Zealand Stadium.

It has been a relatively rapid rise for the side from Pakuranga. Two years ago, they were promoted to the Northern League, and after a solid first season, finished fifth this year, only missing out on National League Championship qualification on goal difference.

 



Goals have come easily for the team at Northern League level, scoring 55, just three behind winners Birkenhead United, with Ronaldo Lopez Munoz netting nine. But the other end of the pitch has been more of a challenge, conceding 36 goals in 22 games.

Their route to the final saw them beat Nelson Suburbs 3-0 in the semi-final, so they will look to go back-to-back against South Island sides when they face Tech tomorrow.

For Cashmere Technical, their semi-final saw the Central League winners face the Southern League winners as they hosted Napier City Rovers at Garrick Park, with a 2-1 win securing their return to the final for the first time since winning in 2021.

Dominant in the league, winning the title by 11 points, there is no doubt about who their dangerman is, with Garban Coughlan scoring an outstanding 28 goals.
