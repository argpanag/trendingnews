Nintendo's Black Friday Deals Are Actually Pretty Good

TheTools · 12.11.2026 16:11 · source · permalink
  • Copied

Table of Contents

Black Friday is nearly upon us. From now through Cyber Monday, retailers and companies alike are offering deals and discounts on their wares. While I always keep an eye out for Nintendo deals offered by stores like Amazon and Best Buy, it seems the company is offering its own discounts this year—and, surprisingly, they're actually pretty solid.

Nintendo's Black Friday deals

On Thursday, Nintendo announced its Black Friday discounts. We've already seen Switch 2 sales ahead of the Black Friday season, but now, Nintendo has confirmed an official discount for the console. Starting Nov. 22 through Dec. 5, you can pick up Nintendo's Switch 2 Mario Kart bundle for $499.99, down $50 from $549.99. That includes the console, a download of Mario Kart World, and a 90-day Nintendo Switch Online Individual Membership.

If you already have a Switch 2 console and/or Mario Kart World, you're not left out of the Black Friday deals here. From Nov. 22 through Nov. 29, Nintendo is also running discounts on several high-profile Switch and Switch 2 games. During this promotion, each of the following is $39.99:

  • Donkey Kong Bananza

  • Kirby Air Riders

  • Metroid Prime 4: Beyond

  • Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2

  • Super Mario Party: Jamboree

Nintendo rarely offers official discounts on some of its biggest titles, especially in this current era. Games like Donkey Kong Bananza retail for upwards of $69.99, so you're saving as much as $30 on these games during this week-long promotion. In addition, Nintendo announced a sale on its digital store that runs from Nov. 19 through Dec. 2, though it didn't say which titles would be on sale. Just keep an eye on all these dates: The Switch 2 console promotion runs longer than the physical and digital games discounts, while the digital sale runs longer than the physical sale.

Nintendo says that these discounts will be available "at select retailers," as well as its brick-and-mortar locations in New York and San Francisco. You can also buy the Switch 2 bundle through Nintendo's website.

Back Friday 2026 FAQs

Are Black Friday deals worth it?

In short, yes, Black Friday still offers discounts that can be rare throughout the rest of the year. If there’s something you want to buy, or you’re shopping for gifts, it’s a good time to look for discounts on what you need, especially tech sales, home improvement supplies, and fitness tech. Of course, if you need to save money, the best way to save is not to buy anything. 

How do I make the most of Black Friday? 

Use price tracking tools to determine if the deals you're keeping tabs on are actually a deal. If you feel like that's too much work, we already do that for you, so follow our Black Friday coverage for the latest and best deals our team finds. Another good tip is to only buy what you need. It sounds simple, but making a list of the things you want before jumping into a sale with millions of products makes you focus and make smarter decisions.
