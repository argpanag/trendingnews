Jacksonville PorchFest returns to historic Springfield on Nov. 7

TheTools · 30.09.2026 18:26 · source · permalink

JACKSONVILLE, Fla. — Jacksonville PorchFest will return to the Historic Springfield district on Saturday, Nov. 7.

The free, public music festival, organized by Springfield Preservation and Revitalization (SPAR), will run from 12 p.m. to 8 p.m. and feature more than 20 musical acts performing on neighborhood porches.

The festival will also feature food trucks, craft beer and art vendors. Families and dogs are welcome.

Event information, VIP tickets, parking details and sponsorship opportunities are available on the festival’s website.

SPAR, a non-profit organization established in 1974, focuses on improving quality of life in the historic district. Proceeds from Porchfest benefit arts and culture programming in Jacksonville’s Urban Core.
