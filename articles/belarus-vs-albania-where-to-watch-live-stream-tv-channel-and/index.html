Match Preview

Belarus and Albania meet at Stadion Illovsky Rudolf in Budapest in a UEFA Nations League C fixture with real implications for the Group 1 standings.

Albania arrive as group leaders, sitting first in the table and carrying strong recent momentum into this away fixture. Rolando Maran's side have won their last two Nations League matches, including a 2-0 victory over Finland on November 12.

Belarus sit second in the group under Viktor Goncharenko, and they too arrive in form. The Belarusians have won four of their last five matches in the competition, with their only defeat coming against Albania earlier in the campaign.

That earlier meeting, played in September, ended 2-0 to Albania — a result that gives Maran's side a psychological edge heading into this rematch. Belarus will be eager to reverse that outcome on home soil.

Goncharenko's team has been consistent across this Nations League cycle, racking up wins over San Marino twice and Finland, while conceding just one goal across their last three matches. They will fancy their chances with the home crowd behind them.

For Albania, a positive result would strengthen their grip on top spot and keep promotion firmly in their own hands. Maran's squad has shown they can grind out results away from Tirana.
