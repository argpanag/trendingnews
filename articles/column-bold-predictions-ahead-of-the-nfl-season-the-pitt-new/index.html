Column | Bold predictions ahead of the NFL season - The Pitt News

TheTools · 09.09.2026 15:33 · source · permalink

The NFL season kicks off today. With new faces in new places and old ones returning, this NFL season is set to be one for the ages. Here are some of my bold NFL predictions for this season.

Aaron Donald wins DPOY and CPOY

In a move no one saw coming, legendary defensive tackle Aaron Donald is back in the NFL. After retiring in 2024 at only the age of 32, putting an early end to a Hall of Fame career, the former Pitt great comes back to a stacked Los Angeles Rams team. After acquiring Myles Garrett from Cleveland in the offseason, many see them as a Super Bowl favorite and boasting the best defense in the league. I think Donald will thrive in this situation. With him and Garrett in a four-man rush, I would have to imagine Garrett would get more attention, allowing Donald to take advantage of that and cause havoc in the trenches. I think this will result in Donald not only winning Comeback Player of the Year, but also Defensive Player of the Year. As a bonus, I think the Rams will win the Super Bowl. This will cement Donald as an all-time great and a first ballot Hall of Famer — that is, whenever he decides to fully retire. 

Steelers remain kings of the North

The Steelers might call me crazy, but I think change can be good. As much as Mike Tomlin won, it clearly wasn’t working as well as everyone wanted it to. I think with a new look offense and defense, the Steelers win the AFC North for the second year in a row. Head coach Mike McCarthy and defensive coordinator Patrick Graham are going to breathe new life into this old school football team. New additions to the team include wide-receiver Michael Pittman Jr., running back Rico Dowdle, cornerback Jamel Dean and safety Jaquan Brisker improve the team a significant amount from last year. Returning quarterback Aaron Rodgers won a Super Bowl with McCarthy, ironically against the Steelers. There is familiarity and comfortability between the two. They have experienced success together, and I believe that will continue in Pittsburgh. 

Despite his age, Rodgers can still sling it with the best of them, but he will need a good run game to support that. I think it will all happen as well as a possible playoff run which could lead to the stairway to seven in the Steel City. 

The City of Brotherly Love falls short of the postseason

The Philadelphia Eagles won the Super Bowl two years ago. Since then, their play has declined. Their stars are getting older in offensive lineman Lane Johnson, and who knows the wear and tear on running back Saquon Barkley? I also see a lot of tough games on their schedule, including the defending champion Seahawks, 49ers, Rams and Texans. The Dallas Cowboys serve as Philadelphia’s main competition in the division, and I do think the Cowboys have a bigger defensive upside with new additions like Quinnen Willimas from the Jets. I do think Dak Prescott and the boys will get it going on offense with the dynamic duo of CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens carrying the receiving game. The Eagles lost their number one receiver in A.J. Brown, which makes me question their offensive depth, and I am not as high on Jalen Hurts as others. I think Hurts is only as good as the weapons and coaching around him. The Eagles will come in second place in the NFC East, missing the playoffs for the first time since 2020.
