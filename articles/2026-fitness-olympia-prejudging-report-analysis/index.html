Highlights from the 2026 Fitness Olympia prejudging.

The 2026 Fitness Olympia is set to take the stage in Las Vegas with Michelle Fredua-Mensah looking to defend her title for the first time. After breaking through to capture her first Olympia championship in 2025, Fredua-Mensah returns at the top of a competitive 10-woman field.

Jaclyn Baker finished runner-up for the second consecutive year, while Taylor Learmont rounded out the top three. 

Baker is back in the lineup this year and should once again be one of the names to watch during prejudging. Anna Fomina and Amber Steffen also return after placing fifth and fourth, respectively, at last year’s Olympia.

The current field also includes Abby Bolton, Andrea Glass, Sophie Gregory, Stephanie Jones, Allison Kramer, and Samantha Radzai. 

Fitness is unique because the physique round is only part of the overall battle, with the routine round playing a major role in determining the eventual champion. Still, prejudging will give the first indication of which competitors have brought their best physiques to Las Vegas and who could be in position to challenge Fredua-Mensah.

Generation Iron will provide a complete breakdown of the 2026 Fitness Olympia prejudging below as the callouts unfold.
