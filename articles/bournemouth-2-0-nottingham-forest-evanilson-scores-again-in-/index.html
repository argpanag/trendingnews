    Newcastle have already lost a joint-high seven top-flight home games this calendar year and the omens suggest they may struggle against Arsenal. Only one of Evanilson's 22 Premier League goals for Bournemouth have come from outside the penalty area

Evanilson continued his hot streak in front of goal by scoring in a sixth successive Premier League match to help Bournemouth beat Nottingham Forest at Vitality Stadium.

It took the Brazilian just 19 minutes to pick up where he left off before the international break and put the Cherries on course for a ninth consecutive win in all competitions, while moving them up to fourth in the table.

The forward rifled the ball into the roof of the net from inside the area after a clever flick-on from defender James Hill, who continued his excellent form after receiving his first England call-up this month.

Bournemouth could have taken the lead sooner had it not been for an outstanding last-ditch tackle from Jair Cunha to thwart Evanilson just as his compatriot was about to unleash a shot inside the area.

Once the Cherries took the lead, Forest offered very little threat in response and failed to register a shot on target during an uninspiring first-half performance.

The afternoon went from bad to worse for the visitors in the third minute of stoppage time at the end of the first half, when Bournemouth launched a swift counter-attack that ended with Justin Kluivert doubling his side's advantage.

Unmarked at the back post, the Dutchman converted Marcus Tavernier's cross with ease at the end of a slick move involving Evanilson.

Forest manager Oliver Glasner summoned Xaver Schlager and Igor Jesus from the bench during the break in an attempt to turn the tide.

But with Forest having failed to earn a point in their previous 27 Premier League matches when trailing by two or more goals at half-time, it was always going to be a difficult task for them to fight back.

A sixth defeat of the season leaves Forest 17th in the table, and without a win at Bournemouth since the 2014-15 season.

Bournemouth analysis: Dream season continues

Is there a more in-form striker in the Premier League than Evanilson?

Manchester City's Erling Haaland and Liverpool's Alexander Isak might have scored more goals than the Brazilian this season, but neither are currently enjoying the rich vein of form Bournemouth's number nine finds himself in.

By scoring his sixth goal of the season against Forest, Evanilson has already matched his tally for the entirety of last season.

At this rate, it seems entirely possible he could finish this campaign with double, or perhaps even triple, the number of goals he managed last term.

He is not the only Bournemouth player arguably enjoying the best form of his career, but his turnaround underlines exactly the type of confidence flowing through Vitality Stadium at the moment.

Since beating AC Milan, Manchester United and Aston Villa in the space of six days last month, the Cherries have not looked back.

For all the fears that this could be a season of transition as they prepared for life without Andoni Iraola, Marco Rose has made the adjustment to life in the Premier League look seamless.

Next up for the Cherries is a trip to Spain to face La Liga side Celta Vigo in the Europa League.

It is turning into a dream season for Bournemouth, and their fans will be sure to savour every second.
