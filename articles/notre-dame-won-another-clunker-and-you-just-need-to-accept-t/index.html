Notre Dame won another clunker, and you just need to accept the clunking win

TheTools · 03.10.2026 21:00 · source · permalink
  • Notre Dame Football

Notre Dame fans should really put a game like this into perspective rather than go full panic mode.

Do you know the difference between a cupcake and a clunker? A cupcake is a terrible team that you lose to or play awful against, while a clunker is any game in which you should win easily — turns into a massive struggle for most or all of the game.

Maybe the differences between a clunker and a cupcake are too much to use as a comparison… but I don’t think so.

Notre Dame just had a clunker against North Carolina. It was “one of those games” the Irish and most top teams have at least once a year, and yet they won their clunker and did won it by double digits. An example of a recent one by Notre Dame was last year’s Boston College game.

But here’s the thing… it’s fine.

Instead of going upon 24-7, the Irish chose to go for it on 4th and goal from the two yard line. The decision to go for it wasn’t terrible. UNC had netted 19 yards to go along with two interceptions in their three previous drives before this one by the Irish. Obviously it all backfired as UNC drove the ball 97 yards against some of the worst defense the Irish have played this season. As dominant as the Irish had been for season up to know, and for most of the game up till that point — they simply played a bad series and UNC came away with a touchdown.

All of that was exacerbated by the Heels returning the opening kickoff of the second half for a touchdown. A kick from Dreschler that almost always had been unreturnable this season.

This is ALL very much a part of a clunker.

A clunker isn’t predictive or even means that there are some massive fixes that are needed. Almost always… a clunker is a clunker and you need to win your clunkers.

We could definitely sit here and start ranting and raving like lunatics about the negatives from this game, but again, I think that might be a giant waste of time. Clunkers happen, and winning a clunker is something good/elite teams have to do from time to time.

Perhaps take solace in (if you really need to) in the fact that Jonaz Walton really had himself a good day on the ground, and the offensive line as a whole looked much more physical and cohesive than in their previous games this year.

A receiver not named Jordan Faison or Micah Gilbert caught a touchdown pass… and it was Jaden Houdini Greathouse.

I understand that all of this sounds like putting lipstick on a pig (I’m really loving that phrase for this game) and maybe it is… but we’re talking about clunkers her and not chunkers. Winning is all that matters and all of the reasons we love this team and feel like this is a team that can win a national championship are still very true.
