Maura Higgins has admitted she is “struggling so much” as she shared a downcast update from rehearsals for Dancing With The Stars.

The Love Island star, 35, took to Instagram stories to give fans a glimpse behind the scenes of the demanding competition, posting a tired-looking selfie while taking a break from rehearsals.

Lying on a sofa and appearing to wipe her eye with her sleeve, Maura captioned the snap: “Struggling so much this week”, alongside a crying face emoji.

The candid update comes as the Longford native continues to put in long hours preparing for her weekly performances alongside professional dance partner Mark Ballas.

Maura has previously been open about the pressure of competing on the US version of the hit show, admitting that she has found the demanding rehearsals and constant feedback difficult at times.

Earlier in the competition, she revealed that she had broken down in tears during rehearsals after becoming frustrated with herself.

Speaking on her YouTube series Get Real With Me, she explained that she was finding it difficult to take on board the amount of corrections she was receiving.

She said: “I’m so hard on myself anyway, and then everything is wrong. I hear, ‘You’re doing really well, but fix your arm, fix your neck, fix hips and toes,’ like, how am I doing well? It’s a lot.”

Maura also admitted that she had cried in the bathroom after struggling to keep her emotions in check.

The reality star has nevertheless continued to impress viewers and judges with her performances alongside Mark.

Her recent Paso Doble, which she dedicated to Ireland, earned her a standing ovation and her highest score of the competition at the time.

The Longford beauty took to the dancefloor alongside her professional dance partner Mark Ballas for the show’s ‘Dedication Night’, paying tribute to her Irish roots with a high-energy paso doble.

The pair performed to Dropkick Murphys’ hit song ‘I’m Shipping Up to Boston’, delivering a passionate routine that earned them a standing ovation from the audience.

Maura and Mark were also joined by a very special guest performer, with Lucky Howard, star of Michael Flatley’s Lord of the Dance, showcasing his incredible Irish dancing skills during the performance.

The judges were clearly impressed by the routine, with Carrie Ann praising Maura’s energy and “attack” before awarding her an eight.

Derek Hough was equally enthusiastic, admitting he “loved every minute of the dance” and giving the pair a nine.

Bruno Tonioli and Johnson rounded off the scores with another two nines, praising the pair’s passionate choreography.

The impressive total brings Maura another step closer to the coveted Glitter Ball trophy as she continues to make her mark on the American competition.

Taking to Instagram ahead of the performance, Maura shared an emotional tribute to Ireland, explaining how much it meant to celebrate her home country on such a huge stage.

“Tonight, I’m dancing for home. I’ve been lucky enough to chase my dreams across the world, but no matter how far I go, I’ll never forget where I came from,” she wrote.

“I’m so proud to come from a little country with such a big heart. The kindness, the humour, the strength of our people and the way we always show up for one another is something I’ll never take for granted. To be able to celebrate my roots on a stage like Dancing With The Stars is something my younger self could only have dreamed of!!”

The former Love Island star also shared an inspiring message for young girls hoping to follow their dreams, encouraging them never to let their background hold them back.

“And to every little country girl out there with big dreams, never let where you come from make you feel like there’s a limit to where you can go. If anything, let it be the reason you dream even bigger. This one’s for every single person back home who has cheered me on, believed in me and supported me along the way!!”

She concluded: “I’ll always be your Irish girl, and I really hope I make you proud.”
