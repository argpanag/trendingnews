Springboks knocked off world number one spot after France defeat | rugby

TheTools · 14.11.2026 09:11 · source · permalink

By SABC Sport

14th November 2026

The Springboks' loss has allowed New Zealand to return to the summit for the second time this year, with the All Blacks benefiting from Rassie Erasmus' side slipping to their third defeat of 2026.

Dave Rennie's team initially moved to the top of the standings after beating South Africa in the opening match of Rugby's Greatest Rivalry series, only to surrender that position a week later following their defeat in Cape Town.

The latest rankings update sees New Zealand reclaim first place, with the Springboks dropping to second ahead of the remaining Nations Championship fixtures this weekend.

France, meanwhile, have gained 1.56 ranking points following their two-point victory over the Boks - their first win against South Africa in Paris since 2005. That result has lifted Fabien Galthie's side above Ireland into third place.

Andy Farrell's men cannot reclaim third spot with a victory over Fiji in Dublin this weekend, owing to the ranking-point difference between the two teams heading into the match.

South Africa could, however, return to the top of the rankings before the weekend is over, although that would require Wales to pull off a historic first victory over the All Blacks in the professional era.

Should Steve Tandy's side achieve that feat, New Zealand would drop back to second place. However, regardless of the margin of defeat, the All Blacks cannot fall any further down the rankings.
