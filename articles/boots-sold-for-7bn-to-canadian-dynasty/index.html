Boots sold for £7bn to Canadian dynasty

TheTools · 07.10.2026 14:26 · source · permalink

Boots has been sold to the Canadian Weston family for nearly £7bn.

Sycamore Partners, a private equity firm, and Stefano Pessina, an Italian businessman, sold the high street pharmacy chain to Wittington Investments, which will have day-to-day control of the 177-year-old company.

The deal is also backed by Canada’s Fairfax Financial.

Different members of the Weston clan own a controlling stake in Associated British Foods (ABF), which owns Primark, the budget clothing retailer.

Under the terms of the deal, Wittington will acquire Boots’ retail operations in the UK and Ireland, Boots Opticians, No7 Beauty Company and its Thailand and franchised businesses.

Sycamore and Mr Pessina and his family will retain ownership of the Boots Group’s other operations, Farmacias Benavides in Mexico and Alliance Healthcare Deutschland in Germany.

The deal is expected to be completed in the first quarter of 2027.

The Weston family is one of Canada’s wealthiest corporate dynasties. In the UK the Westons are best known as the controlling shareholders in ABF, the owners of Primark.

However, it is the Canadian branch of the family making the Boots acquisition. They previously owned Selfridges in the UK.

Galen Weston, the chairman of Wittington, who will become chairman of Boots, said: “Boots is one of Britain’s most enduring businesses, with a rich heritage, a trusted name and a vital role in everyday life across the UK and Ireland.

“We have great respect for Boots’ legacy and leading market position. We see a meaningful opportunity to make a great business even better through stable long-term ownership, further capital investment, and the renewed operating focus required to serve customers with excellence for generations to come.

“We are delighted to partner with Fairfax, which brings an extensive track record of long-term investing in leading retail brands, and shares our conviction in Boots’ future.”

Alex Baldock, the Boots chief executive, said: “Boots matters. Every day, our colleagues give millions of people longer, healthier and happier lives.

“Those customers and patients trust in Boots’ unmatched authority, capability and leadership across health, wellness and beauty. I’ve seen enough already of our colleagues’ passion and expertise to understand why they do.
