Patriots vs. Steelers live updates: Score, news, discussion from NFL Week 2

TheTools · 20.09.2026 16:30 · source · permalink
  • New England Patriots News

The Patriots will host the Steelers in their 2026 home opener at Gillette Stadium.

The New England Patriots began their 2026 regular season on the wrong foot. Dropping their opener against the world champion Seattle Seahawks, they have started their second campaign under head coach Mike Vrabel with an 0-1 record.

In order to improve to 1-1 and get their first victory of the season, the Patriots will have to defend their home turf against a Pittsburgh Steelers team that did win in Week 1. A win over Pittsburgh would not just mean a return to .500 on the year for New England, though, but also the first W in a home opener since the 2020 season.

Live score: Patriots 10 : 0 Steelers

Sunday, Sept. 20, 1 p.m. ET | Gillette Stadium (Foxborough, MA) | Broadcast information | Inactives | Game day roster | Weather forecast

First Quarter

The Patriots started the game on defense, and with a quick three-and-out. New England allowed just three combined yards on two Steelers carry, while Aaron Rodgers’ decision to target Christian Gonzalez on the game’s first third down led to an incompletion and subsequent punt.

New England’s first offensive series started well, with Drake Maye completing passes to DeMario Douglas and Hunter Henry to move the unit into Steelers territory. However, the drive came to a quick and disappointing end when Rhamondre Stevenson lost a fumble on his second touch of the day.

Before even starting their next series, a taunting penalty against safety Jalen Ramsey backed the Steelers up at their own 16-yard line. Things did not get better for Rodgers and company from that point on: a short run was followed by Gabe Jacas forcing a throwaway and another incompletion on third down.

Marcus Jones gained 19 yards on his first punt return of the day, allowing the Patriots to begin their second drive on the Pittsburgh 49-yard line. It could have been a quick one, with a 10-yard Jalen Ramsey sack on third down seemingly ending the series. However, a defensive offsides penalty negated the play and gave New England another chance. TreVeyon Henderson took advantage of it: not only did he convert the subsequent 3rd-and-1, he followed it up with a 39-yard touchdown run on the very next play. | Patriots 7 : 0 Steelers

After keeping the Steelers offense in check for the first two series, the unit did find some rhythm on its third. It did not matter: a well-time Robert Spillane blitz forced an incompletion on 3rd-and-6 in New England territory, while Pittsburgh kicker Chris Boswell sent the ensuing 57-yard field goal try wide to the left.

The Patriots offense continued to look good early into its next possession, quickly moving to the Pittsburgh side of the field again. Romeo Doubs caught his first pass as a Patriot, Rhamondre Stevenson converted a 3rd-and-1 after returning to the field following his fumble, and Drake Maye hit Eli Raridon for a 30-yard gain. However, once in the red zone, the unit ran into trouble: a Morgan Moses false start was followed by a 3-yard sack — a play that saw Mike Onwenu suffer an ankle injury. The right guard was carted off the field and quickly ruled out with an ankle injury.

Second quarter

Coming out of the quarter break, another sack led to the Patriots bringing out Andy Borregales. The second-year kickers split the uprights from 30 yards out to extend his team’s lead. | Patriots 10 : 0 Steelers

The Patriots had a chance to quickly end the Steelers’ first possession of the second quarter, but the offense was able to move the sticks on a 4th-and-1 to keep the series alive. The drive did take 6:50 off the clock and saw Pittsburgh march into potential scoring range, but it ended with a Christian Elliss/Robert Spillane doube-team sack of Aaron Rodgers: the veteran quarterback lost the ball, with neither team able to scoop it up before Steelers left tackle Troy Fautanu did so back at the Pittsburgh 42 — a net loss of 36 yards to set up a 4th-and-43.
