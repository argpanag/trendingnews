Firefighters find e-bikes 'intensified' flames in building blaze

TheTools · 18.09.2026 14:27 · source · permalink

By Natasha O'Neill

Posted Sep 18, 2026 10:27:47 AM.

Multiple 911 callers told firefighters they couldn’t leave their apartments as flames raged in a unit on the eighth floor.

It happened Friday around 2:33 a.m. The first call was from a monitoring agency for active alarms in a building in the 200 block of Donald Street, in the Overbrook community. Then, people started calling for help, saying they were trapped.

When crews arrived they saw heavy smoke and flames coming from an apartment on the eighth floor. Because so many people needed help leaving, firefighters called a second alarm to bring more support in case balcony rescues were needed.

One team quickly went up the building where they were met with heavy, thick smoke; they could not see anything. Two aerial ladders were near the rear of the building to douse flames but also in case crews needed to rescue people.

When officials arrived at the fire unit they brought a hose inside and did confirm no one was there. Several e-bikes were burning, which crews said “intensified the fire.”

By 3:22 a.m., the fire was contained and did not spread to other apartments. Widespread ventilation was needed to remove smoke from the fire floor to the 22 storey.

Multiple people are expected to be displaced as a result of the fire.

A fire investigator is remaining on scene to look into how it started.
