These are the top 10 trending dog and cat names in Canada for 2026

TheTools · 23.10.2026 19:32 · source · permalink

Pet-sitting website Rover has released its list of the top 10 trending dog and cat names in Canada for the year, and some of them may surprise you.

The list was based on the names of “millions” of pets recorded on the Rover website between August and October, according to the company.

In Canada, the top trending dog name for 2026 was “Ernie,” likely based on Toronto baseball player Ernie Clement, says Rover.

Koko took second place, largely inspired by Koko the Koala, a popular children’s character.

For cats, the top trending cat name this year was Minou. Rover says the name could have French Canadian roots, as Minou translates to “kitty” in French.

In second place was Ozzy, named after the late Ozzy Osbourne, who died in July 2025, followed by Sadie, likely tied to actress Sadie Sink.

Rover says sports, vintage pet names, and food have been big sources of inspiration for trending pet names in Canada.

Food in particular has struck a chord with Canadians, with the dog name Croissant increasing 374 per cent compared to last year, and Ramen for cats surging a whopping 624 per cent compared to 2025.

The overall most popular male dog name in Canada this year was Charlie, while the most popular female dog name was Luna.

For feline friends, Milo was the most popular cat name in Canada this year, while Luna was also the most popular female cat name.
