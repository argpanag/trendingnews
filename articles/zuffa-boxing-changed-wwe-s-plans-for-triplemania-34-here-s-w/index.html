Zuffa Boxing Changed WWE's Plans for TripleMania 34: Here's What Happened

TheTools · 12.09.2026 22:06 · source · permalink

TripleMania 34 made history this weekend as the first two-night edition of AAA’s signature event, but the unusual Friday-Sunday schedule wasn’t always the plan.

According to WrestleVotes Radio on Fightful Select, TripleMania 34 was originally scheduled as a one-night event on Saturday, Sept. 12. Those plans changed, however, with Zuffa Boxing playing a significant role in WWE and AAA’s decision to expand the event.

The report noted that WWE wanted to bring AAA to Las Vegas on Friday night in hopes of showcasing the promotion to network executives who would already be in town for Zuffa Boxing’s Ryan Garcia vs. Conor Benn fight Saturday.

That resulted in TripleMania being split across not only two nights, but two countries.

Night 1 took place Friday at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas, just one night before Garcia and Benn meet at T-Mobile Arena. TripleMania then takes Saturday off before Night 2 takes place Sunday at Arena CDMX in Mexico City.

WWE officially announced the two-night format in May, although the company did not publicly give Zuffa Boxing as the reason for the change.

The schedule allows WWE and AAA to capitalize on a major combat sports weekend in Las Vegas while also putting AAA in front of potential media partners. It comes as WWE continues to expand the Mexican promotion’s presence following its acquisition by WWE last year.

Zuffa Boxing is TKO’s professional boxing promotion, led by UFC President and CEO Dana White. The promotion launched in 2025 as TKO expanded beyond WWE and UFC and into boxing.

The name “Zuffa” will be familiar to longtime UFC fans. Zuffa was the company founded by Lorenzo and Frank Fertitta that purchased UFC in 2001, with White serving as president. TKO revived the name for its boxing venture.

Saturday now belongs to Zuffa Boxing, with Garcia defending his welterweight title against Benn in the main event. The card begins at 8 p.m. ET and streams on Paramount+ in the United States.

TripleMania 34 resumes Sunday at 9 p.m. ET from Mexico City, with Dominik Mysterio defending the AAA Mega Championship against El Grande Americano in the night’s main event.
