How to Get Emperor Modulator: Emperor Walkthrough - Arc Raiders Guide - IGN

TheTools · 15.10.2026 18:40 · source · permalink

The Emperor Modulator is a rare item that you’ll want to track down while playing ARC Raiders. It’s not a simple resource to get because of how many steps you have to follow to get it. There’s some setup that you need to complete, along with a good amount of luck, or some might call it patience, to get everything ready. Here’s what you need to know about how to get the Emperor Modulator while playing ARC Raiders.

How to Get an Emperor Modulator

The location where you get the Emperor Modulator is no mystery. It comes from the Fallen Emperor, which you can find on Pendola Pass. However, getting into the Fallen Emperor requires you to have an Emperor Gateway Conduit. It’s a special key that you can unlock through the Frozen Trail Pass, or be lucky enough to find the blueprint for one, allowing you to craft it.

For those who already have a key, you can skip down to the section about exploring the Fallen Emperor and completing that dungeon. For those who have to loot a blueprint and craft it, there are a few more steps for you to follow.

Getting Emperor Gateway Conduit Crafting Materials

There are three specific items that you need to loot to craft an Emperor Gateway Conduit.

  • ARC Conductive Coupler
  • ARC Insulated Coupler
  • ARC Plated Coupler

These items drop during the Redirection Activity, which is exclusively available on Pendola Pass. Here, you’ll find items that you can use to redirect cargo loads to your location.

When you arrive on a Pendola Pass map with the Redirection Activity activated, your goal is to look for the Damaged Emperor Relays. These are special containers scattered throughout the map. You’ll be able to see the exact location of these containers on your map. However, you cannot open them.

Instead, these containers open by themselves at random. You’ll know one is open if you see that it's shooting a red light up into the air. You’ll then have a short amount of time to loot these containers, likely competing against other players to try and get it first.

Here, you’ll loot an item called the Emperor Beacon. It’s a quick-use item that you can place down on the ground immediately. You don’t want to do it too fast, though.

I recommend putting it down at a spot close to where you can find cover, as you need to protect yourself. When you’ve found a suitable place where you can duck inside a building, or have some protection above your head, place the Emperor Beacon on the ground and rush into the air.

For a short time, debris will begin falling from the sky above you. The Emperor Beacon is sending a signal to Harvesters that it needs supplies, and it's sending those items to that location. When the debris is finished falling, head outside, and there should be a handful of lootable containers for you to go through. The main one you want to find will be where you placed the Emperor Beacon, and it should be sticking straight out of the ground.

Here, you’ll find a sizeable amount of ARC loot, and hopefully, you’ll find one of the three required ARC Couplers that you need to build your Emperor Gateway Conduit. There’s a good chance you might only find one of them, forcing you to go to another Damaged Emperor Relay and repeat the activity.

This may take you several Redirection matches to complete and get all three. Don’t be afraid of looting duplicates. It’s a good idea to have spares if you’re lucky enough to get the blueprint for the Gateway key, allowing you to open the Fallen Emperor multiple times in the future.

When you have all of the required items to craft an Emperor Gateway Conduit, or get it from the level 50 reward from the Frozen Trail Pass, it’s time to dive into the Fallen Emperor. It’s a miniature dungeon that you have to complete. I recommend bringing plenty of gear and weapons to protect yourself, and having a grappling hook on you for a certain section.

How to Unlock Emperor Gateway Conduit Blueprint

As with any blueprint in ARC Raiders, the Emperor Gateway Conduit is a random item that you potentially find inside the major container that drops from the Emperor Beacon. This means you need to be in the Redirection activity to get it. If you’re not, you won’t be able to find this blueprint.

Alongside the components you need for the blueprint, successfully getting it will take time. Once you do have it, or the level 50 reward as a one-time-use key, you’ll be able to enter the Fallen Emperor.

Completing the Emperor Dungeon

You’ll need to have the key in your inventory to use it and access the Fallen Emperor. The entrance is relatively straightforward, but your forward progress is blocked by a heavy turret known as a Hydra. This is a heavily armored turret blocking your pathway forward, and I don’t recommend trying to skirt it.

You’re better off taking your strongest weapons possible (the ones designed to destroy ARCs) and using the cover to carefully whittle it down. Explosives are also a good idea to make short work of it.

When the Hydra is defeated, you’ll have completed the first portion. From here, you’ll enter a dome-like room where you need to shoot the yellow lights in the middle area of the room. Destroy all of these lights, and you’ll activate the inner elevator. You want to wait for the elevator to provide you with enough room to jump on, and then ascend to the next floor.

The next series of rooms can be different for you based on what’s inside your Fallen Emperor. They are slightly similar, but not vary slightly.

Here is where it can get tricky. The next portion is a maze, and in this maze, you need to find those same yellow lights hidden somewhere in that dungeon. You’ll need to make your way across the many walls, searching for these lights. You can expect to find multiple different ARC units patrolling this area, and you will need to defeat them to safely make it to the next portion. After you find the yellow light, shoot it, and one of the large pillars will descend, allowing you to go up to another floor.

There’s another version of this maze where you merely have to destroy the ARCs in this area. When they’re all destroyed, a side panel opens up, allowing you to ascend, rather than having to look for an elevator.

In the next portion, you’ll enter a hallway-like room that’s full of gas. The gas doesn’t impact your character’s health, but it does impact their stamina. When this happens, the room will begin to close in around you. However, the way to avoid this is to find a spot on the bottom section where you can hide between the closing walls. Wait here until the walls open back up again (although the gas is still going) before going to the side with a dome door. You’ll take this path out, and enter a side maze section that should be straightforward, but involves a good deal of climbing.

Alternatively, the hallway full of gas could be a room full of deadly lasers. You'll need to use the available debris to avoid the lasers, or make some yourself using deployable barricades to advance.

When you reach the top, you’ll now be above the hallway that was attempting to crush you. The walls will part, in and out, as if the Fallen Emperor were still breathing. You want to reach a moving pendulum at the center of the room from where you’re standing, but expect to face off against two more Hyrda turrets. You need enough ammunition to take these ones out (and more explosives). When the Hydras are defeated, jump onto the moving part and climb your way up to the top section.

From here, it’s all about continuing your climb until you reach the final loot room. There should also be an exit nearby that takes you down the circular elevator that you used to reach the initial starting area after beating the first Hydra. There’s a good chance you can find an Emperor Modulator inside this area, and the best way to access it is with a snap hook or grapple hook.
