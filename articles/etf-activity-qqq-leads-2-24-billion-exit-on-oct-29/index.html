ETF activity on Thursday tilted toward redemptions, with 339 funds reporting a net estimated outflow of $1.90 billion as 17,500 positions decreased against 40,617 increases. QQQ led the retreat at an estimated -$2.24 billion, followed by SPY at -$1.97 billion and TECL at -$950.0 million. Inflows concentrated in financials and value, with XLF adding $885.1 million and AVLV $614.0 million, while XLV and XLE also gained. Among stocks, ExxonMobil drew $889.9 million in value change across 46 buying ETFs, even as NVIDIA and Apple were trimmed.
