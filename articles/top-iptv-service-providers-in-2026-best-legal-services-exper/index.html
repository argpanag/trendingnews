What Is IPTV?

IPTV stands for Internet Protocol Television. Instead of receiving television programming through traditional cable or satellite infrastructure, IPTV delivers television content through an internet connection.

This technology powers many mainstream streaming services.

A legal IPTV-style service can provide:

  • Live television channels
  • Local broadcast channels
  • Sports
  • News
  • Movies
  • Entertainment
  • Cloud DVR
  • On-demand programming
  • Multi-device streaming
  • Mobile viewing
  • Smart TV applications

  • Live television channels
  • Local broadcast channels
  • Sports
  • News
  • Movies
  • Entertainment
  • Cloud DVR
  • On-demand programming
  • Multi-device streaming
  • Mobile viewing
  • Smart TV applications

The important distinction is that IPTV technology itself is not illegal.

The legality depends on whether the provider has the appropriate rights to distribute the content it offers.

Therefore, consumers should not judge a service solely by the number of channels advertised. A smaller but transparent provider with official applications and legitimate programming rights can be a much safer choice than an unknown service promising thousands of channels for an unusually low price.
