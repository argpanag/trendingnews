Flick surprises Barcelona after Clasico defeat | Goal.com

TheTools · 26.10.2026 21:34 · source · permalink

What did he do?

Barcelona head coach Hansi Flick has decided against travelling to the Ballon d'Or ceremony after the El Clasico defeat to Real Madrid. The surprise call coincided with the absence of young defender Pau Cubarsi, who opted to stay home and rest after a punishing run of games with club and country.

According to the programme "Què T'hi Jugues" on Spain's "Cadena SER" radio, the Clasico loss threw Barcelona's plans for the ceremony into disarray. Flick settled on staying put, while Cubarsi chose physical recovery over the glamour of the event, despite ranking among the frontrunners for the Kopa Trophy for best young player.

Several Barcelona representatives are preparing to make the trip, led by Lamine Yamal, Rodri, Dani Olmo and Joan Garcia, alongside club officials and executives.

Yamal will travel separately aboard a private plane, joined by family and friends.

Cubarsi's absence comes with Yamal tipped to claim the Kopa Trophy for a second straight year. He has won it in each of the last two editions, putting a fresh landmark within reach at such a young age.
