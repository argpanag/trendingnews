Ric Flair focused on ‘healing and recovery’ after blood clot treatment at Tampa General Hospital - AOL

TheTools · 25.10.2026 17:38 · source · permalink

TAMPA, Fla. (WFLA) — Retired professional wrestler Ric Flair shared a health update on social media Saturday after reportedly receiving treatment for a serious blood clot.

Flair, whose real name is Richard Morgan Fliehr, said he received medical attention at Tampa General Hospital and is now focused on healing.

"Thank You All For The Messages And Prayers," Flair said. "I Will Get Back To You Each Individually As I Get Stronger. Thank You To All The Tampa General Hospital Staff And Doctors That Put Up With Me Until 4:30am. I Appreciate You All And Thank You For Everything You Did For Me! I Am Focused On My Healing And Recovery Right Now. I Will Be Okay!"

The post came one day after he announced he would undergo treatment for a blood clot in his leg, requiring him to cancel his scheduled appearance at Washington State Horror Con.

TMZ reported that Flair was hospitalized after suffering a fall outside a Tampa restaurant. Video obtained by TMZ appeared to show him being tended to by first responders.
