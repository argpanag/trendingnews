Further charges against two men accused of rape in Galway

TheTools · 23.09.2026 12:00 · source · permalink

Further charges have been brought against two men, who have already been accused of raping a woman in the car park of Ceannt Railway Station in Galway last month.

The men - aged 25 and 27 - were brought before Galway District Court this morning.

Proceedings were translated for them by an Arabic interpreter.

The accused men cannot be named, to protect the identity of the alleged victim.

The 25-year-old man faces an additional two counts of sexual assault, contrary to Section 2 of the Criminal Law (Rape)(Amendment) Act 1990, as amended by Section 37 of the Sex Offenders Act 2001.

The offences are alleged to have occurred at Ceannt Railway Station on 1 August.

The second man is also charged with sexual assaulting the woman, on the same date, at Victoria Place in Galway.

Both the accused were charged with the above offences this morning and made no reply when they were cautioned.

Each has already been charged with the rape of a female on 1 August, at Ceannt Railway Station car park.

They are also charged with falsely imprisoning the same alleged victim, on the same date, and at the same location.

There was no objection by the State to an application for legal aid today.

The men have already been denied bail by the High Court, after it considered an application, earlier this month, in relation to the rape and false imprisonment charges. Barrister Donal Cronin said his clients were reserving their position regarding bail applications in relation to the new charges.

Both have been remanded in custody, to appear via video-link at next Monday’s sitting of the District Court.
