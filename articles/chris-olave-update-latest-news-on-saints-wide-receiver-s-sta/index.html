Chris Olave update: Latest news on Saints wide receiver’s status after missing practice

TheTools · 14.10.2026 20:31 · source · permalink

The New Orleans Saints have not had a strong start to their season, currently owning a 1-4 record through the first five weeks. They are in last place in the NFC South and have lost three in a row.

However, some bright spots can still be found. Perhaps the biggest one has been the connection between quarterback Tyler Shough and wide receiver Chris Olave.

MORE: Commanders sign former Saints running back

Shough has targeted Olave 55 times already, and Olave has hauled in 39 receptions for 537 yards and one touchdown. He has picked up 29 first downs, which leads the entire NFL. He is also second in the league in receiving yards, trailing only Jaxson Smith-Njigba of the Seattle Seahawks.

Olave is one of the most important pieces of this Saints offense, so keeping him on the field is incredibly important. He has dealt with a couple of issues this year, including a tight hamstring back in September and knee and foot injuries popping up earlier this month. He has played through all of them.

Unexpected absence

On Wednesday, the veteran wasn’t seen by media during practice, which was surprising considering he didn’t have an injury designation in Week 5. But the reason was not anything physical.

Rather, Olave was held out due to an illness, according to Jeff Nowak of Audacy Sports. He was not the only player on the roster to sit out, as Nathan Shepherd also didn’t participate.

Since New Orleans doesn’t take on the New York Giants until Sunday, that should hopefully be enough time for him to get healthy and avoid any designation for the game.

Still, given what Olave has played through this season, it’s hard to imagine this being anything more than a minor setback.
