Portugal: 'Shame, it annoys me' - Denmark's Joachim Andersen reveals Ronaldo regret

TheTools · 11.11.2026 12:59 · source · permalink

Denmark defender Joachim Andersen has admitted it “annoys” him that he may not face Cristiano Ronaldo again.

According to Andersen, he would have loved one more meeting with the Portugal star and also swap a shirt with him.

The Fulham star stated this ahead of Denmark's Nations League clash with Portugal on Saturday.

Ronaldo had last week confirmed his retirement from Portugal national team.

Recommended

Speaking to Danish broadcaster TV2 on Tuesday, Andersen said of Ronaldo's retirement, "Actually, it annoys me a little.

"I think he deserved more, and I would have liked to play against him one more time and, maybe, even managed to get his shirt, which I still don’t have. It’s a shame."
