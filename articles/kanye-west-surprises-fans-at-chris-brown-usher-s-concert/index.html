Kanye West surprises fans at Chris Brown, Usher's concert

TheTools · 26.09.2026 06:09 · source · permalink

Published September 26, 2026

Kanye West, now known as Ye, sent fans into a frenzy by making a surprise appearance at Chris Brown and Usher’s concert.

On Friday, September 26, the unexpected appearance quickly became one of the night’s biggest moments.

Wearing a new mask reminiscent of his Yeezus era along with a sleek black leather outfit, the Bully maker took the stage at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles to perform several of his well-known tracks, including Runaway, All of the Lights, Flashing Lights, and Can’t Tell Me Nothing.

The packed stadium responded with loud cheers as videos of the performance began circulating online.

The electrifying rendition marked Ye’s first reported live appearance since September 4, when he performed the second night of his homecoming concert at Soldier Field in Chicago.

That Chicago show was presented as part of the global Ye Live Concert Tour, supporting his studio album Bully. The three-hour, 60-track performance drew a reported crowd of more than 70,000 fans.

One of the biggest moments of the Chicago concert came when Kim Kardashian's ex husband reunited with Kid Cudi onstage. The two artists embraced and performed Father Stretch My Hands Pt. 1, Ghost Town, and Cudi’s Pursuit of Happiness.

It was their first reported onstage reunion since 2019.

Now as he crashed the The R&B Tour, Ye, 49, once again became the focal point of a major stadium concert, giving fans a new performance and a new mask to talk about.
