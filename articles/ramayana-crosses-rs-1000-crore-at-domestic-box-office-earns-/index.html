Ramayana, featuring Ranbir Kapoor, Sai Pallavi and Yash, has earnerned over Rs 1000 crore at the domestic box office with its global collection standing at Rs 1651 crore.

According to the trade tracking website Sacnilk, the film has earned Rs 1,084 crore gross at domestic box office. The nett collection of the film is Rs 907.15 crore. The collection of the film overseas is Rs 567 crore.

The film is directed by Nitesh Tiwari and is produced by Namit Malhotra's Prime Focus Studios in collaboration with DNEG and Yash's Monster Mind Creations.

It follows Lord Ram's journey, his exile, the abduction of Sita by Ravana and the ensuing battle between the forces of good and evil.

The film's music has been composed by Oscar-winning composers Hans Zimmer and A R Rahman, while Terry Notary and Guy Norris are part of the action team.

It also features actors Sunny Deol, Anupam Kher, Kajal Aggarwal, Rakul Preet Singh, Vivek Oberoi, Adinath Kothare, Arun Govil, Kunal Kapoor, Indira Krishnan and Lara Dutta, among others.
