KFC stages fake funeral to give GTA 6 fans an excuse to chuck a sickie

TheTools · 16.11.2026 00:57 · source · permalink

Rest in 11 herbs and spices, Ann Alibi

KFC Australia will host a funeral for a fictional woman named “Ann Alibi” this Thursday, coinciding with the release of Grand Theft Auto 6 – and the restaurant promises to make Ann’s “final wish come true” by offering free delivery nationwide for the day.

The tongue-in-cheek stunt taps into “sickie” memes surrounding the highly anticipated launch of Rockstar’s GTA 6 — the first major release of one of the world’s most popular games since 2013.

KFC announced the “funeral” on Instagram.

“It is with heavy hearts, and a much lighter workload, that we announce Ann finally got her wings,” the post reads.

“By remarkable coincidence, her service will take place on the very same day something thirteen years in the making finally arrives.”

Ann was born on November 28, 1997 – the exact launch date of the original GTA, although the company has said that “any resemblance to a certain launch date is purely coincidental.”

Special, KFC’s creative agency, told Mumbrella it was not involved in the campaign. We Are Social, which handles KFC’s social work, is yet to verify it is behind the stunt.

The lengthy wait for GTA 6 has become something of an internet joke in its own right, spawning the viral “We got __ before GTA 6” meme, which pokes fun at the events and milestones society has reached before the game’s release.

Social media is awash with jokes about how gamers plan to clear their work schedules for Thursday’s launch. The phenomenon has even earned its own nickname, “GTA Flu”.

It appears that not everyone is actually joking.

According to data from Employment Hero, annual leave bookings for November 19 among more than 870,000 Australian workers have jumped 29% compared with the same day last year. Among men aged 18 to 44, requests have surged 84%.

The prospect has even caught the attention of the Fair Work Ombudsman, which took to Instagram to remind Australians that employers can request evidence of illness when employees take sick days, including on GTA 6 launch day.
