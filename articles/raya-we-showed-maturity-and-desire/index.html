Raya – ‘We showed maturity and desire’

TheTools · 18.10.2026 20:25 · source · permalink

David Raya was all smiles after we recorded a narrow victory away to Nottingham Forest on Sunday, with the keeper keeping his seventh clean sheet of the season.

The three-time Golden Glove winner recorded another shut out to lay the basis of our latest win, to keep up the pressure at the top of the table.

It was a day to be patient with few chances for either side, and David was delighted with the qualities the team showed, to eventually run out 1-0 winners: “(We showed) maturity and a lot of desire,” he said. “Keep doing the right things, and the ball will get into the net. We had experiences in the last two years where we drew 0-0 here, so I think we showed a lot of character to keep digging the result, and in the end with a few crosses and a few of Bukayo’s balls, the ball went in thanks to Noni.

“He’s a special player and it’s going to be a special goal for him to get the win at such a difficult ground and such a difficult team. So, I’m delighted for him.”

It was our tenth win out of 11 games in all competitions this season, and third in the space of a week after returning from the international break.

“Yeah, we came back very, very strong, especially after that tough defeat against Brighton and having 19 days since then to play a game. The way we responded, the way we played, the way we came back, is incredible, you know. So, delighted with the performances we have put in every single game, there are things to improve, of course, as always, but we just take the positives and we’re looking forward to Wednesday now.”
