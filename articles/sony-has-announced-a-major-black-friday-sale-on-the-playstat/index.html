Sony has announced a major Black Friday sale on the PlayStation Store.

TheTools · 15.11.2026 16:16 · source · permalink

Sony Interactive Entertainment has officially unveiled the detailed schedule for its massive annual Black Friday 2026 sale, which kicks off November 16 at midnight local time and runs through November 30 on the PlayStation Store, the official PlayStation Direct online store, and at authorized retail partners. This special promotion offers significant direct discounts on branded peripherals and virtual reality devices: the basic PlayStation VR2 headset will be $100 off, the branded 27-inch gaming monitor with a charging hook will be $50 off, the Pulse Elite wireless headset will be $40 off, the DualSense Edge and Access controllers, and the Pulse Explore headphones will each be $30 off, and the standard DualSense controller will be $20 off. Additionally, in ten European countries, including the UK, Germany, and France, existing PlayStation Plus subscribers will receive an extra 5% discount on accessories ordered directly from the manufacturer, while physical copies of Marvel's Wolverine, Saros, Ghost of Yōtei Complete Edition, and MARVEL Tōkon: Fighting Souls will also be available at reduced prices.

A separate section of holiday offers is dedicated to the development of the PlayStation Plus subscription ecosystem and the Sony Pictures Core multimedia service. New users will be able to sign up for annual plans with significant savings, while existing subscribers will be offered preferential terms for upgrading to the expanded Extra, Premium, or Deluxe levels, which unlock access to a catalog of hundreds of games, including The Last of Us Part II, Final Fantasy XVI, Helldivers 2, and Marvel's Spider-Man 2, as well as cloud streaming for PS5 and the PS Portal handheld. Additionally, from November 20th to 30th, all PlayStation 4 and PlayStation 5 owners will receive discounts of up to 40% on movie purchases in the Sony Pictures Core app, while subscription holders will be able to take advantage of even better deals on new releases.

The PlayStation Store's holiday promotion will feature hundreds of digital versions of games for both current console generations, with discounts of up to 75%. Major titles joining the sale starting November 16th include recent high-profile releases and multiplatform blockbusters, including Marvel's Wolverine. Resident Evil Requiem Deluxe Edition, Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced, 007 First Light, CONTROL Resonant, Crimson Desert Enhanced, Dune: Awakening, Forza Horizon 5 Standard Edition, Halo: Campaign Evolved, LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight, Baldur's Gate 3 Digital Deluxe Edition, and the latest sports simulators EA SPORTS FC 27, Madden NFL 27, and NBA 2K27. The exact price reduction for each item will vary by regional store, and some items may be removed from the promotional selection before the official end of the sale on November 30.
