HomePod Mini: Apple launches new £149 device to try and put Siri at the centre of your life

TheTools · 13.10.2026 15:42 · source · permalink

Apple has updated the HomePod Mini as it tries to put Siri at the centre of people's lives.

The HomePod Mini 2 brings support for Apple's overhauled and updated version of Siri to its smart speaker line-up for the first time. It also brings a new feature called "SoundGlow" – and an increased price.

The launch of the new HomePod Mini came alongside the announcement of the HomeView, Apple's first ever smart display, and an updated version of the Apple TV that is also better able to support Siri. All of them represent both a major push by Apple into the smart home, as well as a renewed focus on its virtual assistant.

Support for that new version of Siri is the central feature of the new HomePod Mini. It includes a new chip that allows it to support "Siri AI", which means that it will not only answer questions but can take actions on a users' behalf, by relying on the personal context gathered from their iPhone.

For many of those features, the HomePod Mini will have to be paired with an iPhone or iPad that runs Apple Intelligence. Once that is done, the HomePod can be used to send messages, for instance, or even to ask questions about those messages, as well as getting information about the world.

The HomePod Mini 2 is able to recognise up to six people from around the house from their voice, and the deliver personalised responses. That is similar to a new feature on the Apple HomeView, which uses its camera to recognise people and show personal information when they are nearby.

The new smart speaker also marks the first introduction of Apple's new "Sound Glow" feature. That uses the coloured lights on the top of the device – which had previously just shown a swirling design that matched the Siri branding – to match the album art of whatever song is playing.

The HomePod Mini 2 launches alongside an updated version of the Apple TV, which also features a new processor and the ability to use Siri AI. The Apple TV also now has a microphone built into the box, which lets people talk to Siri without having to find the remote.

The two new devices can also work together, so that four of the HomePod Minis can be attached together to offer surround sound. The new version of the speaker can also be attached to a Mac and used as its default speaker.

The launch of the new HomePod Mini marks the first proper update to the product since it was first launched, in 2020. Apple did not update the larger and more expensive HomePod, which was launched in 2018, discontinued in 2021, and then came back in 2023 and has not been updated since.
