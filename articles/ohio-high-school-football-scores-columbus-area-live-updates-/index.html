Ohio high school football scores, Columbus-area live updates from Week 4

TheTools · 11.09.2026 21:00 · source · permalink

It's Week 4 of the 2026 Ohio high school football season, and The Dispatch covered three games Sept. 11: Watterson defeated Cleveland Glenville 35-13 at Ohio Dominican, New Albany beat host Upper Arlington 28-21 and St. Charles routed visiting Ready 44-2.

Read our live updates from the games.

In central Ohio action Sept. 10, Beechcroft defeated Whetstone 52-0, Bexley beat Grandview Heights 14-3 and Columbus Academy defeated Granville 36-14.

OHSAA scores, updates from Week 4 of Ohio high school football season

Watterson extends winning streak

Watterson rallies from a 13-0 first-half deficit to beat Glenville 35-13. The Eagles improve to 4-0 and extend their winning streak to 34.

New Albany hangs on to beat Upper Arlington

UA got two 1-yard touchdown runs in the final minute from Drew Theado, but New Albany hung on to win 28-21. Both teams are 3-1.

Watterson adds late score

Caden Mangini’s 8-yard TD run increases the Eagles' lead over Glenville to 35-13 with 2:38 left. Nate Henderson’s interception set up the score.

Watterson pulling away from Glenville

Watterson's impressive second half continues as Ben Uhlenhake scores on a 28-yard run for a 28-13 lead with 6:42 remaining. Glenville had led 13-0.

Check out these early New Albany-Upper Arlington photos

St. Charles stays unbeaten

The Cardinals are now 4-0 after beating Ready 44-2. The Silver Knights drop to 2-2.

Jerwers run gives New Albany three-score lead

Jett Jerwers did what Jett Jerwers does, blasting down his sideline 43 yards for a touchdown that might have put this one away. New Albany now leads Upper Arlington 28-7 with 6:53 left. The drive covered 86 yards in seven plays in 3:14.

Randles caps another St. Charles scoring drive

Sebastian Randles broke a 17-yard run to the Ready 8, recovered a teammate’s fumble at the 1 and scored on the next play. St. Charles is up 44-2 lead with 1:29 remaining.

Ackermann's defensive play preserves lead

Not only did Mason Ackermann score New Albany's most recent touchdown, the linebacker just made a huge tackle of UA quarterback Charlie Hunt on fourth-and-3 to stop the Golden Bears from converting inside the New Albany 20. The Eagles have the ball outside their own 35, leading 21-7 with 9 minutes left.

Watterson extends lead against Glenville

Watterson opens a 21-13 lead late in the third quarter as Dash Hoy connects with Ben Lamielle for a 33-yard touchdown.

Ackermann TD extends New Albany lead

Mason Ackermann's first carry of the game went for a 2-yard touchdown on fourth-and-goal in the final minute of the third quarter, and New Albany has extended its lead on Upper Arlington to 21-7. That Eagles drive covered 77 yards in 14 plays and took 6:53

Watterson, Glenville trade turnovers

Watterson and Glenville trade interceptions to begin the second half. Watterson's Joe Hayes picked off a LaMarques Greenwood pass to end a Glenville scoring threat.

Ready turns scoring opportunity into its first points

Ready’s onside-kick recovery to open the second half led to its best scoring opportunity, but the drive ended with a fumble at the St. Charles 5. The field position still paid off. Markel Small blocked the Cardinals’ punt, and the ball rolled out of the end zone for a safety. St. Charles leads 37-2 with 4:34 left in the third quarter.

Halftime stats for New Albany-UA

The numbers are not gaudy by any stretch, but here are the top stats at half ...

Top New Albany stats: QB Drew Jackson 6-10, 74 yards, TD; Jett Jerwers 9 carries-40 yards; Chase Jackson 2 catches-47 yards, TD; Mason Ackermann 41-yard interception return for a TD

Top UA stats: Reign St. Clair 12-19 passing, 102 yards, INT; Charlie Hunt 2 carries-13 yards, TD and 3 catches-47 yards; Drew Theado 8 carries-40 yards and 3 catches-25 yards.

Watterson leads Glenville at halftime

After a slow start offensively, Watterson gets untracked with two late first-half touchdowns for a 14-13 lead at the intermission. The Eagles are putting a 33-game winning streak on the line against Glenville.

St. Charles-Ready first-half stat leaders

St. Charles quarterback Nolan Beyer completed 7 of 9 passes for 158 yards and three touchdowns. Liam Allwein had two catches for 75 yards and a touchdown, while Trey Davis caught three passes for 56 yards and two scores. Noah Gude and Brycen Brown added defensive touchdowns. 

Ready managed just 46 yards, with Markel Small and Luke Kamerer rushing for 18 yards apiece.

Watterson rallies to take lead

Watterson takes a 14-13 lead as Dash Hoy hits Austin Alles for a 28-yard touchdown with 46.2 seconds remaining in the first half. Alles' punt return into Glenville territory set up the score.

Halftime: New Albany 14, Upper Arlington 7

After a scoreless second quarter, New Albany takes a seven-point lead to the break. Neither team seriously threatened for points, but UA's Edward Oakley missed a 40-yard field goal wide left with 20 seconds left in the half. Some stats coming soon.

Beyer connects with Allwein for another long touchdown

After a false start pushed St. Charles back to its 37, Nolan Beyer completed a 10-yard pass to Liam Nordstrom and followed with a 53-yard touchdown to Liam Allwein. The Cardinals have a 37-0 lead with 54 seconds left in the first half. Beyer has three touchdown passes, while the Cardinals’ defense has scored twice and added a safety.

Watterson cuts into deficit

Watterson answers Glenville's second score with its most effective possession of the game. Dash Hoy connects with Carter Bellisari for a 30-yard touchdown, pulling the Eagles within 13-7 with 1:54 left in the first half.

Glenville takes two-score lead on Watterson

Glenville opens a 13-0 lead as LaMarques Greenwood connects with Dionte Shephard for a 45-yard touchdown off a slant pass with 4:35 left in the first half. Glenville missed the extra point.

Watterson can't convert deep in Glenville territory

Watterson picked up its first first down but loses the ball on downs at the Glenville 5-yard line with 8:00 left in the second quarter. The Eagles trail 7-0.

Beyer finds Davis again as St. Charles extends lead

St. Charles followed the safety with a touchdown drive despite two holding penalties. Nolan Beyer completed all three of his passes on the possession, including a 1-yard touchdown to Trey Davis. The Cardinals have a 30-0 lead on Ready with 8:45 left in the second quarter.

Glenville opens scoring in second quarter

Glenville takes a 7-0 lead against Watterson as Jahmal Goodson scores on a 1-yard run with 10:06 left in the second quarter. LaMarques Greenwood's 49-yard pass to Jaylen Edwards set up the score.

St. Charles defense adds a safety

A high pitch put Markel Small in trouble near the goal line, and Liam Allwein tackled him in the end zone for a safety. Just 8 seconds into the second quarter, St. Charles leads Ready 23-0.

Glenville, Watterson scoreless after one quarter

The visiting Tarblooders had the ball three times in Watterson territory in the first quarter but were unable to capitalize.

Long drive gets Upper Arlington on scoreboard

A 12-play, 70-yard march for UA – just the kind of drive it needed after a tough start – culminated in a 10-yard keeper for a touchdown by quarterback Charlie Hunt. Hunt, who alternates time with Reign St. Clair, had two catches on the drive for 28 yards. UA trails New Albany 14-7 after a quarter.

St. Charles defense scores again

Brycen Brown intercepted Ethan Mutnich at the Ready 34 and returned it for a touchdown, giving St. Charles a 21-0 lead with 1:21 left in the first quarter. It’s already the Cardinals’ second defensive touchdown. Noah Gude returned a fumble for the opening score, and St. Charles also has forced two punts without allowing Ready to cross midfield.

St. Charles defense sets up another score

The Cardinals’ defense forced its second consecutive punt and gave the offense the ball at midfield. St. Charles needed one play from there, as Nolan Beyer connected with Trey Davis for a 50-yard touchdown. The Cardinals lead 14-0 with 3:04 left in the first quarter.

Ackermann INT return increases New Albany lead

Eagles linebacker Mason Ackermann picked off Reign St. Clair in the center of the field, followed his blockers and returned the interception 41 yards for a touchdown to give New Albany a 14-0 lead at the 6:37 mark of the first quarter. Not a great start for Upper Arlington, which already had intentional grounding and false start penalties on its first possession before this turnover.

Watterson's Boyle gets another sack

Michael Boyle's second sack leads to a Glenville punt from the Watterson 29-yard line. The Eagles took over at their 20 following a touchback but were forced to punt for the second time.

New Albany strikes first at Upper Arlington

Both teams looked ragged in the first few minutes, with each punting on their first possession, but a big play opened the scoring when New Albany quarterback Drew Jackson found Chase Jackson streaking down the middle of the field for a 43-yard touchdown. The Eagles lead 7-0 with 8:03 left in the first quarter.

Watterson forces Glenville to punt

Glenville moves into Watterson territory but is forced to punt following a Michael Boyle sack on third down. Watterson will take over at its 6-yard line.

St. Charles scores first on fumble return

Ready moved from its 20-yard line to its 47 on the opening possession before St. Charles forced a turnover. Noah Gude scooped up the fumble at the Ready 30 and returned it for a touchdown. The Cardinals have a 7-0 lead with 9:27 left in the first quarter.

Glenville will receive opening kickoff

Watterson wins the coin toss and defers to the second half. Visiting Glenville will receive the opening kickoff.

Unbeaten St. Charles gets another test from Ready

St. Charles brings a 3-0 record into tonight’s game against Ready (2-1), which has scored 99 points through three weeks. The Cardinals escaped with a 27-26 win in this matchup last season. St. Charles won the toss and deferred, so Ready will get the first possession, and the Cardinals will receive the second-half kickoff.

Watterson will have new look tonight

It's a beautiful night at Ohio Dominican as Watterson (3-0) prepares to take on Cleveland Glenville (2-1). The host Eagles will be wearing black jerseys instead of their customary home cardinal red tops.

Ohio high school football schedule Week 4

Upper Arlington puts its 3-0 start on the line against a New Albany team that is 2-1 under first-year coach Mark Solis. UA is coming off a 43-22 win over two-time defending Division II state champion Avon and has outscored its opponents 107-29 behind junior quarterbacks Charlie Hunt and Reign St. Clair. New Albany rebounded from a 35-28 loss to Canal Winchester in Week 2 by routing Dublin Scioto 56-7 last week.

With seven seniors back on defense and eight on offense, St. Charles is 3-0 as it welcomes Ready (2-1). The Cardinals have a chance to start 4-0 for the third time in four years. Ready will have to stop the combination of sophomore quarterback Nolan Beyer and senior receiver Trey Davis.

Another 3-0 team, two-time reigning Division III state champion Watterson, has won 33 consecutive games entering its contest against 2025 Division IV state champion Cleveland Glenville (2-1) at Ohio Dominican. Glenville will have to find ways to limit a Watterson offense that features junior quarterback Dash Hoy, senior running back Caden Mangini and senior wide receiver Carter Bellisari.
