Two people found dead at Tauranga house

TheTools · 11.11.2026 10:34 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Crime and Justice

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._v0.KrRTFtoPsaA8nSDloPZ4KejQJr59ANQpD9ULgGwzaM0/crime-and-justice/2497461%2Ftwo-people-found-dead-at-tauranga-house

Two people have been found dead in a house in Kairua, Tauranga this morning.

Detective senior sergeant Natalie Flowerdew-Brown said police were called to the Mangatawa Lane address at about 8.30am after a report that somone had died.

Police officers found two bodies at the address.

Senior Sergeant Flowerdew-Brown said police were working to find out what has happened but don't believe there is a threat to the public.
