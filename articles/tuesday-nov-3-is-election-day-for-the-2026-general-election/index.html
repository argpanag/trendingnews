Tuesday, Nov. 3, is Election Day for the 2026 General Election

TheTools · 02.11.2026 00:00 · source · permalink

All 216 Wake County polling places open from 6:30 a.m. until 7:30 p.m.

Tuesday, Nov. 3, is Election Day, and Wake County voters have one final opportunity to cast a ballot in the 2026 General Election. Polling places across the county will be open from 6:30 a.m. until 7:30 p.m.

“It was great to see such strong turnout during the early voting period, and our focus now is ensuring all 216 polling places are fully prepared for Election Day,” said Wake County Board of Elections Director Olivia McCall. “We encourage voters to confirm their assigned polling place, review their sample ballot, bring an acceptable photo ID and arrive in time to be in line by 7:30 p.m.”

Voting on Election Day

Voters planning to cast their ballots in person should consider the following reminders:

  • Find your assigned polling place. Voters must vote at their assigned Election Day polling place. Use the N.C. State Board of Elections’ Voter Search tool to confirm your registration status, view your sample ballot and find your polling place.
  • Bring an acceptable photo ID. All voters will be asked to show an acceptable photo ID. Voters who cannot present an acceptable photo ID may still vote provisionally and complete a Photo ID Exception Form. A full list of acceptable IDs and information about exceptions is available on the N.C. State Board of Elections’ Voter ID page.
  • Be in line by 7:30 p.m. All eligible voters in line when polls close at 7:30 p.m. will be allowed to vote.
  • Same-day registration is not available on Election Day.

Returning an Absentee Ballot

Wake County voters who requested an absentee ballot must ensure their completed ballot is received by the Wake County Board of Elections by 7:30 p.m. on Tuesday, Nov. 3, to be counted. This deadline applies to ballots returned by mail or in person. A postmark on or before Election Day does not establish timely receipt of a ballot delivered after the deadline.

Absentee ballots may be returned in person to the Wake County Board of Elections Operations Center at 1200 N. New Hope Road, Raleigh, NC 27610. Absentee ballots cannot be returned at an Election Day polling place.

Election Night and Unofficial Results

Unofficial election results will be reported on the N.C. State Board of Elections’ Results Dashboard as they become available. Absentee and early voting results will be the first to appear after 7:30 p.m., followed by Election Day precinct results, which are expected to begin appearing around 10 p.m.

All eligible voters in line by 7:30 p.m. will be allowed to vote before officials begin closing procedures. Results from all 216 Election Day precincts, more than any other county in North Carolina, must be returned to the Wake County Board of Elections and processed before being uploaded to the statewide reporting system. Reporting times may vary depending on when voting concludes at each polling place and when results arrive for processing.

For more information about voting in Wake County, visit ReadyToVote.com.

About the Wake County Board of Elections

The Wake County Board of Elections is responsible for conducting all elections held in the county. The Board establishes election precincts and voting sites; appoints and trains precinct officials; prepares and distributes ballots and voting equipment; certifies ballots cast in elections and investigates any voting irregularities.

The Wake County Board of Elections also maintains voter registration and voting records for more than 860,000 voters. To learn more about the Wake County Board of Elections, follow us on Facebook, Instagram or X, or contact us at voter@wake.gov or 919-404-4040.
