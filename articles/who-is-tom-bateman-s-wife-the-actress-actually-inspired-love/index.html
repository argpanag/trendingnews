Who is Tom Bateman's Wife? The Actress Actually Inspired 'Love Hypothesis' Author's Fan Fiction!

TheTools · 23.09.2026 18:59 · source · permalink

Tom Bateman is the hunky male lead of The Love Hypothesis and fans are going to want to know more about him!

The 37-year-old actor had his big break when he starred in two seasons of the BBC series Da Vinci’s Demons, leading to his role in the 2017 movie Murder on the Orient Express.

While working on the Orient Express movie, Tom actually met and fell in love with his now-wife!

Tom is also known for starring in the Peacock series Based on a True Story, as well as the films Cold Pursuit, Death on the Nile, and Hedda.

So, who is Tom Bateman’s wife?

All about Tom Bateman’s wife Daisy Ridley

Tom Bateman is married to Daisy Ridley!

The couple started dating after meeting on the set of the 2017 movie Murder on the Orient Express. Rumors started swirling in 2020 that they got married, which they finally confirmed in early 2023.

Daisy got her big break in 2015 while playing Rey in the Star Wars franchise. She’s also known for her work in movies like Chaos Walking, The Marsh King’s Daughter, and Young Woman and the Sea. She’ll soon be seen in Ebenezer.

One of Daisy Ridley’s movie roles inspired the movie The Love Hypothesis

In an interview with Elle, Lili Reinhart and Tom addressed the rumors that the book for The Love Hypothesis was inspired by Daisy.

The book is said to by Reylo fan-fiction, referring to Daisy‘s Star Wars character Rey and the villain Kylo.

“People genuinely think that Tom and Daisy knew about this,” Lili told Elle. “If you knew both of them as I do—they did not know, I can tell you that with 100-percent certainty. They had other things going on in their lives. They didn’t know about the fanfiction.”

Tom added, “I said, ‘What do you mean?’ She goes, ‘Well, not really you and this book. Your wife and this book.’”

“I remember the moment the casting broke because I mentioned it to Daisy. She was away filming when I was offered the role. And I said to her, ‘Do you know that there’s this book…?’ and Daisy also goes, ‘Huh. I think someone told me there was a book written because this woman likes the characters. But no, I didn’t really [know].’ And because it’s set in a university campus and the [characters] are scientists, I was like, ‘There’s no [obvious connection].’ I can see now, looking back, where the inspiration comes from. But no, I didn’t know,” Tom said.

What is The Love Hypothesis about?

The Love Hypothesis follows Olive (Reinhart), a brilliant Ph.D. candidate focused on her future in academia. But when she realizes her best friend Ahn has fallen for her crush Jeremy, Olive impulsively kisses intimidating professor Adam Carlsen (Tom Bateman) to prove she’s moved on. What begins as a desperate act of friendship spirals into a fake relationship with rules, boundaries, and a mutually beneficial agreement. When their arrangement begins to blur the lines between performance and reality, Olive is forced to test her most daunting hypothesis yet: that love might actually be worth the risk.

Who else stars in the new movie?

Along with Lili Reinhart and Tom Bateman, the movie also stars Rachel Marsh as Ahn, Nicholas Duvernay as Jeremy, Jaboukie Young-White, and Arty Froushan.

When will it be released?

The Love Hypothesis is streaming now on Prime Video.

The new movie is based on the New York Times best-selling novel of the same name by Ali Hazelwood.
