Fulham 1-1 Man Utd: Matheus Cunha's deflected shot cancels out Lisandro Martinez horror own goal in draw at Craven Cottage

TheTools · 20.09.2026 18:11 · source · permalink

Report and free match highlights as Manchester United avoid defeat at Fulham thanks to Matheus Cunha's late deflected strike after a shocking own goal from Lisandro Martinez piled on the pressure; Fulham still searching for first Premier League win under Alvaro Arbeloa but move off bottom

Senior Football Journalist @psmithXI

Sunday 20 September 2026 19:09, UK

Matheus Cunha's deflected 89th-minute strike grabbed Manchester United a 1-1 draw at Fulham to take the edge off what appeared to be turning into a full-blown crisis for Michael Carrick's side going into the international break.

A horror-show own goal from Lisandro Martinez with 23 minutes to play, piled the pressure on United to snatch something from this match, following the midweek Carabao Cup collapse at home to Brighton and Manchester derby defeat last weekend.

A wicked flick off David Affengruber got them out of jail. But United are still in the bottom half of the Premier League after five games, 10 points adrift of table-topping neighbours City and looking miles off the level required to challenge the country's best sides this season. This is their joint-worst start to a Premier League campaign.

A joint-worst start to a Premier League season for Man Utd

Manchester United have won just five points from five league games this season – their joint-fewest at this stage of a Premier League campaign (also 5 in 2014-15).

"A real mess" was how Sky Sports' Gary Neville described the own goal. Cunha was levered off the ball by Fulham centre-back Calvin Bassey, whose stinging shot was parried by Senne Lammens. The 'keeper then saw Martinez stick out a boot and divert the ball back past him. Lammens' clumsy attempt to cut it out summed up United's chaotic defending.

It was their play on the ball before that which most worried Neville, though. He called their passive possession before the opener "the worst 15 minutes I have ever see Manchester United play".

The narrative could have been very different had Bruno Fernandes found the net rather than the post from Cunha's inviting cutback in the first half, or if the Brazilian's heavily deflected shot had gone in rather than clipped the woodwork. Bernd Leno also made a series of full-stretch saves.

Eventually, the Fulham 'keeper was beaten, with no chance of stopping the leveller. It was heartbreak for the hosts and their new boss Alvaro Arbeloa who is still searching for his first Premier League win. He will take encouragement from this performance after their draw at Anfield, though, as they move off bottom spot by goal difference.

As for United, the accumulation of poor performances already this season will have alarm bells ringing in Manchester. They got away with a point at Fulham but have to find a way to make a major gear change during the upcoming three-week break to get back on track and realise their ambitions of kicking on this season.

Cunha: They put in more energy than us - we cannot accept this

Man Utd's Matheus Cunha to Sky Sports:

"It is so frustrating for us. In the beginning, we put the plan in and created some chances. We didn't score and then they scored in one action. It is hard to explain.

"It is frustrating for us. After the goal, we played much better and had a lot of character. It needs to be from the start. It is only one point but we should never play for one point.

"It is hard. They put in more energy than us, we cannot accept this. We need to do our best. We need to improve in every single position. It is only the beginning. Last season was the same, with noise around, but we finished third."

Carrick: We created enough to win the game

Man Utd boss Michael Carrick to Sky Sports:

"I thought we should've won the game, we created enough to win the game. We had a lot of good play and the goal we did concede, it is one of those goals.

"I really liked the reaction and how we finished the game. It would be easy for it to go in another direction.

"We nearly won it at the end. We came to get the win; of course we did, but the way we finished the game is the positive we take from it.

"Their goalkeeper, the saves he has made, he was player of the match and we hit the post twice. A lot of it [our play] was good. There are things to take from it, for sure."

Arbeloa: Every decision was for them

Fulham boss Alvaro Arbeloa to Sky Sports:

"We were so close. So, so close. We were a little bit unlucky again. We knew it would be difficult against this big team.

"Also we knew what happened last week. We have seen what the referees have been doing in all the matches so it was a difficult game. And we were unlucky.

"All fouls for them. You can see before they scored, the foul against Maguire, it's a guy who is 100 kilos. Every decision was for them and I knew after the game against Man City that they [would not] let Manchester United lose here. We deserved a little bit more."
