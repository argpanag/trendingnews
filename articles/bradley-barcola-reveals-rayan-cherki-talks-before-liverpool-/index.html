Bradley Barcola reveals Rayan Cherki talks before Liverpool vs Man City, ' he really wants to win'

TheTools · 11.10.2026 08:00 · source · permalink

Liverpool fans will be desperate for Bradley Barcola to step up and deliver a £123m performance against Manchester City on Sunday.

The Frenchman has been a little underwhelming since making his big move from Paris Saint-Germain in August, but with the Reds missing both Alexander Isak and Cody Gakpo for the visit of City, the stage is set for Barcola to be the main man.

As for the City players, it will be fascinating to see how they respond to what will likely be an incredibly hostile Anfield crowd. All eyes will be on the likes of Erling Haaland to see how they react after their club was found guilty of breaching Premier League financial rules on an industrial scale. Barcola has spent the international break with one City player – Rayan Cherki – and has now revealed how his fellow Frenchman is feeling about facing Liverpool.

With Alexander Isak and Cody Gakpo missing Man City, who are you starting in their place? Would you make any other changes to the team too? 👀

Cherki really wants to beat Liverpool

Liverpool almost signed Cherki in the summer of 2025, with Arne Slot meeting the Frenchman in case the deal for Florian Wirtz fell through. As it happens, the former Lyon man has been a far better signing than Wirtz so far, and already has four goals and two assists to his name this season.

Nevertheless, Cherki remains a controversial figure, and his decision to swap shirts with Hugo Ekitike during Liverpool’s battering by City at the Etihad last April drew criticism from both sets of supporters.

Reds fans may be wary of something similar happening on Sunday after Barcola shared that he and his international teammate have been sharing a few jokes about the game while away with France.

“We talked and had a bit of a laugh about it, but we know that once we’re out on the pitch both of us will be really focused,” the Liverpool winger promised.

“He really wants to win this match. I really want to win it. It’s going to be a high-level match, we’ll see who wins.”

Barcola scored against City in his only previous appearance against them, for PSG, but when asked if that gave him extra confidence going into Sunday, the 24-year-old dismissed the suggestion.

“No because it was in a completely different context,” he said. “It was a Champions League match, plus I was in Paris. Here it’s different, it’s a new league now and I’m with a new team.

I know Manchester City but they have lots of new players, it’s a completely different match and I’m just as hungry to win this.”

“These are huge matches,” Barcola added. “They are very important for us and for the fans. Joining such a big club and playing against such great teams is the goal of every player here. We will do everything to win this match. We know it will be tough, but we will prepare well, so we are ready.”

Barcola reveals help from Ekitike and Jacquet

Barcola is clearly still finding his feet at Liverpool, and although Cherki is a wind-up merchant for opposition fans, having him close by in Manchester will be a help in that respect.

So too will be the presence of countrymen Jeremy Jacquet and Ekitike at Liverpool, and Barcola confirmed that both have been helpful while he gets his bearings in a new country.

“They recommended some good places for me to settle in,” he said. “They gave me a very friendly welcome here, so everything is going well. I’m happy.

“It’s going well, I was warmly welcomed by my teammates, by the staff, so it’s been good.”

Barcola also touched on the differences between Liverpool and Paris, claiming that it is quieter and not as busy on Merseyside, which he is in favour of, while also reconfirming his belief that he is a player perfectly suited to Andoni Iraola’s style of football.
