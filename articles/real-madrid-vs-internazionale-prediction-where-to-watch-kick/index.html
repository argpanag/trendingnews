Real Madrid - Looking to bounce back

Real Madrid enter their Champions League opener after a recent stumble in La Liga, which ended a long winning streak. In the following sections, we will take a closer look at their current form and tactical setup for this crucial first match of the group stage.

Recent form

Real Madrid have been in formidable shape, winning 13 of their last 21 matches. Their momentum was checked just before this European tie, however, with a narrow 1-0 defeat to Real Betis in La Liga. That result snapped an impressive winning run that included high-scoring victories against Malaga and Real Sociedad in their domestic campaign.

That recent league loss serves as a timely reminder that Los Blancos are not invincible, despite their obvious attacking firepower. Stepping into the UEFA Champions League for the first time this season, the Spanish giants will be determined to make a strong start. A home fixture gives them the perfect chance to bounce back immediately and set the tone.

A significant advantage for the hosts is that they have no reported injuries, allowing the manager to field his strongest possible lineup. With a full squad to choose from and a point to prove, expect Real Madrid to be on the front foot for the Real Madrid vs. Internazionale game.

Internazionale - On an impressive unbeaten run

This section will analyse Internazionale’s current form, recent match performances, and overall momentum. We will also take a look at their key players for the upcoming encounter.

Recent form

Internazionale arrive in Madrid with impressive momentum, having put together a strong run of results. The Italian side has been in formidable form, winning 13 of their last 21 matches across all competitions. They started their domestic Serie A campaign in perfect fashion, securing three consecutive victories.

Their recent league performances have been particularly noteworthy, showcasing both attacking prowess and defensive solidity. They kicked off the season with a convincing 4-1 win against Monza, followed by a hard-fought 1-0 victory over Cagliari. Most recently, they secured a thrilling 3-2 win against a strong Napoli side, demonstrating their ability to find the back of the net.

A major advantage for the visitors is their fully fit squad, with no players reported injured for this fixture between Real Madrid and Internazionale. This allows the manager to select his strongest eleven for this crucial opening match, with their current form suggesting they will be a tough proposition.
