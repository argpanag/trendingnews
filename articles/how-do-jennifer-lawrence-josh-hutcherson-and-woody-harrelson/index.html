How Do Jennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson and Woody Harrelson Appear in ‘Sunrise on the Reaping’?

TheTools · 20.11.2026 16:56 · source · permalink

Maddie Ellis

Trending Editor

Warning: Spoilers ahead for the ending to “Sunrise on the Reaping.”

“Sunrise on the Reaping” is set more than 20 years before the events of the first “Hunger Games” film, but it still features an appearance from Katniss Everdeen — twice.

Based on Suzanne Collins’ 2025 novel of the same name, “Sunrise on the Reaping,” which hit theaters Nov. 20, is the second “Hunger Games” prequel story.

Where “Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes” told the origin story of President Snow, “Sunrise on the Reaping” follows Haymitch Abernathy.

In 2012’s “The Hunger Games,” Haymitch, played by Woody Harrelson, is introduced as the oft-intoxicated, perpetually cynical District 12 Games victor serving as Katniss and Peeta’s mentor. He’s largely unlikable but honest, and steps up to support his tributes in the arena when he can.

By the end of the trilogy with “Mockingjay,” it’s revealed Haymitch has deep-seated alliances with other victors of the Games, and he becomes one of the reluctant leaders of the revolt against the Capitol.

Before “Sunrise on the Reaping,” fans only knew the basics of his story: He won the 50th Hunger Games, aka the Second Quarter, which had twice as many tributes.

The novel and film, starring Joseph Zada as 16-year-old Haymitch, go deep into how that went down, from his unconventional reaping, how he become a rebel, the roots of his alcoholism and finally, why he lives all alone in Victor’s Village.

But on the Oct. 28 episode of “Call Her Daddy,” Jennifer Lawrence confirmed she and Josh Hutcherson, who starred as Peeta, will appear in the new film. Here’s how.

Jennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson and Woody Harrelson Pop up At the Very End of the Film

“Sunrise on the Reaping” is much darker compared to previous franchise installments. Essentially every person Haymitch loves or grows to love throughout the film is dead by the end. And even the people who haven’t been killed by Snow and the Capitol, Haymitch’s fear of making them a target leads him to push them away.

This includes his friend from District 12 Burdock and his eventual wife, Asterid. When Haymitch returns after winning the Hunger Games, he’s iced out by most of the district due to the Capitol’s portrayal of Haymitch as ruthless, selfish and cowardly. But Burdock still tries to connect with his old friend, especially in the wake of the deaths of his family and girlfriend, Lenore Dove, in sudden tragedies.

Haymitch turns to alcohol and withdraws from society, living in Victor’s Village. As time passes, he’s forced year after year to mentor the next set of tributes in the Games as District 12’s only winner.

One of the final scenes sees Haymitch in the woods. He sees Burdock and Asterid out in nature with a newborn. He hides behind a tree as he watches the scene with the happy family. He locks eyes with the baby as she’s carried over her dad’s shoulder. He also overhears the parents coo their baby girl’s name: Katniss.

Yes — Haymitch’s old friends are Burdock and Asterid Everdeen, Katniss Everdeen’s parents.

That’s only the first time the character pops up.

“Sunrise on the Reaping” has an epilogue, set well after the events of “Mockingjay” and the end of the annual Hunger Games.

In the scene, Woody Harrelson reprises his role as Haymitch and stands in District 12 before a memorial dedicated to those killed by the Capitol. Lawrence’s Katniss and Hutcherson’s Peeta join him. They have a conversation reflecting on the end of the war and their newfound peace and wish Haymitch a happy birthday. It brings the film’s beginning, which reveals Haymitch’s birthday always fell on reaping day, full circle.

The somber moment also shows that Haymitch was eventually able to make Lenore Dove’s wish — “don’t let the sun rise on the reaping” — come true, as Haymitch was part of the forces that ended the Hunger Games for good.

Viewers later see Haymitch sitting in the field he used to meet Lenore Dove in. He’s surrounded by geese, an animal Lenore Dove used to raise.

What Have the ‘Hunger Games’ Star Said About Their Cameo?

After Lawrence shared that she appears in “Sunrise on the Reaping,” she called her experience returning to the franchise “unbelievable.”

“It was so crazy, because it was the same crew,” she said. “Josh and Woody — it was so emotional. Josh and I like, didn’t walk to the bathroom without our arms around each other.”

“And Woody obviously came through the window of my hotel room uninvited,” she added with a grin. “It wouldn’t be the same if he listened to boundaries and used a door.”

Lawrence, Hutcherson and Harrelson also attended the premiere of “Sunrise on the Reaping” in New York on Nov. 16 and appeared together in the cold open for “The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon” that same night.
