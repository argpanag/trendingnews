Carl Radke looking to 'manifest' Ciara Miller DWTS win after Summer House drama - AOL

TheTools · 14.09.2026 23:45 · source · permalink

Ciara Miller has one major fan in her corner as she prepares to hit the dance floor on Dancing With the Stars-Summer House castmate Carl Radke.

Miller is one of the celebrities participating in Season 35 of the dance competition show, which will debut on ABC on Tuesday, September 15, and Wednesday, September 16, with her participation on the show coming shortly after major drama on her Bravo show, with the reveal that her best friend, Amanda Batula, was dating her ex, West Wilson.

Now, as she prepares for her debut on the show and potentially prepares to take home a hefty paycheck with her appearance, she is getting major support from Radke, 41.

Speaking to People Magazine at the anniversary party for his Soft Bar, Radke shared his belief that Miller, 30, can take it all the way with her pro partner, Brandon Armstrong.

"I think she's going to win. I'm going to manifest it, put the energy out there," he said.

Sharing that he felt she would do well because she was a good dancer and he knew she would work hard, he also said that she was "just hitting her stride" right now.

"I think she's in the best place she has been in her career-just where she's at, what she's doing, the confidence level," he said. "And she's got a great dancing partner. So all the energy is going her direction."

He also confirmed that he and some of the other cast members from the show plan to travel to L.A. to cheer her on from the show's live audience and share their support. Among those potentially going are Lindsay Hubbard, Kyle Cooke, and Mia Calabrese.

He also jokes about getting "Burner phones" to help boost Miller's votes and keep her in the competition longer.

Miller was cast among a tough slate that features many competitive names, including Maura Higgins, Jackson Olson, Jenna Dewan, Julia Stiles, Harry Shum Jr, Tatyana Ali, Taylor Hanson, Guillermo Rodriguez, Ezra Frech and Amber Glenn.

Her appearance on the show comes after the Summer House cast was blown away by the reveal about Batula and Wilson earlier this year.

Miller dated Wilson from July to December 2023, while Batula announced her divorce from Cooke after four years of marriage in early 2026.

She and Wilson confirmed their relationship in March 2026, but faced drama over reports that he had been casually seeing Miller as well, while Cooke claimed that Wilson and Batula had begun hooking up six months before their divorce.

After backlash from their fellow cast members on the show's reunion, both Batula and Wilson announced they were departing the series.

Miller's fans can catch her on the two-night premiere of Dancing with the Stars on Tuesday, Sept. 15 and Wednesday, Sept. 16, on ABC and Disney+.
