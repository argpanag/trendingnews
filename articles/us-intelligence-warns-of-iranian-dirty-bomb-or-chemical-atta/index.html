US intelligence warns of Iranian dirty bomb or chemical attack plot targeting Trump officials: CNN

TheTools · 17.11.2026 03:40 · source · permalink

Reporting the Changing World

Anadolu Ajansı © 2026

US intelligence agencies have detected Iranian planning for potential attacks against senior Trump administration officials in Washington, DC, involving a radioactive dirty bomb or chemical weapons, CNN reported Monday, citing seven US officials familiar with the intelligence.

The reported plans have prompted heightened security concerns and contingency planning within the US government, although officials remain divided over whether Iran has the capability or intention to carry out an attack.

According to CNN, sources familiar with the intelligence characterized the Iranian activity as an effort to develop potential attack options rather than evidence of an imminent operation. However, two sources said the planning identified specific locations and potential targets in the US capital.

White House spokesperson Lauren Bis said the administration was monitoring potential threats but emphasized that there was no confirmed immediate danger.

The US is “monitoring any and all potential threats, but at this time, there are no credible threats to the homeland," Bis said, according to the report.

The intelligence has reportedly triggered several meetings among US officials over the past week to discuss possible responses and preventive measures.

One source familiar with the intelligence described the contingency planning as “prudent,” while another said officials were still debating whether Iran had personnel capable of executing such an operation.

The security concerns have also affected protection arrangements for senior administration officials. According to CNN, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent was moved into military housing last week following threats linked to Iran.

Iran's diplomatic mission to the UN in New York rejected allegations that Tehran was considering using chemical weapons.

In a statement, the Iranian mission said the country “considers the use of chemical weapons under any circumstances to be wholly illegitimate and categorically rejects and unequivocally refutes the false and fabricated allegations circulated in this regard.”

The Pentagon referred questions to the Office of the Director of National Intelligence, which declined to comment, CNN reported.

Two US officials told CNN that intelligence agencies are also monitoring indications that Iran could consider using chemical weapons against American military personnel stationed in the Middle East.

The concerns have prompted a separate contingency planning effort to ensure that US diplomatic and intelligence personnel in the region have appropriate protective equipment and training.

Several officials have reportedly raised concerns about whether American troops currently have sufficient equipment and preparation to respond to a chemical weapons attack.

The reported intelligence developments come as diplomatic efforts to end the US-Iran conflict remain stalled.

Washington and Tehran continue to face disagreements over the US blockade, the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz and the conditions needed to end hostilities.

A regional source told CNN that the coming weeks could prove critical for negotiations, warning that hardliners within Iran or other elements opposed to an agreement could attempt to undermine diplomatic efforts.
