Red Magic 12 Pro+ Details: Everything We Know So Far

TheTools · 12.10.2026 11:00 · source · permalink

Red Magic is finalizing preparations for its next major gaming powerhouse, the Red Magic 12 Pro+, slated for an official launch in China on October 15, 2026. Transitioning from a pure gaming brick to a more ergonomic daily driver, the upcoming flagship promises to merge extreme performance with a refined, pocket-friendly design.

Refined Design and Ultra-Thin Bezels

The Red Magic 12 Pro+ retains the brand’s signature transparent aesthetic but introduces crucial ergonomic updates. The device is notably thinner, lighter, and narrower than the previous 11 Pro+, featuring softened corners and a 2.5D micro-curved middle frame to reduce hand fatigue during marathon gaming sessions. The rear panel is now completely flat, allowing the dual-camera array to sit perfectly flush against the body, seamlessly integrating with the visible liquid-cooling channels and RGB-illuminated active fan.

On the front, Red Magic is pushing the limits of bezel reduction. The black border surrounding the flat display has been shaved down from 1.25mm to an incredibly thin 0.96mm. Combined with a next-generation under-display selfie camera, the device offers a completely uninterrupted edge-to-edge viewing experience free of notches or punch-holes. Buyers will have three striking color options: Deuterium Transparent Dark Night, Deuterium Transparent Ice Blue, and Deuterium Transparent Silver Wing.

Performance, Battery, and Software

Under the hood, the Red Magic 12 Pro+ is confirmed to run on Qualcomm’s latest flagship processor, kept in check by an aggressively upgraded RGB cooling system.

While official battery specs are still under wraps, credible leaks point to a massive 8,400mAh dual-cell battery—a staggering capacity for a device aiming to be thinner than its predecessor. This massive power plant is rumored to support 120W wired fast charging and 80W wireless charging.

Beyond raw gaming power, Red Magic is actively trying to make the 12 Pro+ a better everyday smartphone. The updated software introduces a smoother interface, faster system responsiveness, and a suite of new AI features designed to enhance daily usability outside of gaming apps.

Availability

The Red Magic 12 Pro+ debuts in China on October 15 at 2:30 PM local time. Eager buyers can already place a 100 yuan deposit via JD.com or Red Magic’s website to lock in up to 4,300 yuan in promotional bonuses, including a Red Magic Cooler 8 Pro. While international pricing and exact release dates remain unconfirmed, teasers on the company’s global social media channels strongly indicate a worldwide launch is imminent, likely under the standard “Red Magic 12 Pro” moniker.
