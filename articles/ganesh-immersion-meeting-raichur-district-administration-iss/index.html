Deputy Commissioner of Raichur, Poovitha S. chairing a review meeting of the Ganesha immersion procession in Raichur on Sunday. | Photo Credit: SPECIAL ARRANGEMENT

​Raichur Deputy Commissioner Poovitha S. urged all Gajanana Mandals to strictly adhere to government guidelines to ensure a peaceful and incident-free Ganesha immersion process.

​Chairing a review meeting at the District Administrative Building in Raichur on Sunday, the Deputy Commissioner stressed a peaceful Ganesh immersion.

“The people of the district and the Gajanana Mandals should join hands with the administration and the police by extending coordination to complete the peaceful and incident-free immersion of Ganesha idols,” she said.

Regarding safety, the Deputy Commissioner said that only experienced drivers must operate tractors or any vehicles carrying Ganesh idols, with no more than one idol per tractor. Youths are strictly barred from dangerously hovering around moving tractors or vehicles, she added.

“Entry into the Kasabhavi immersion site is strictly banned due to past slipping hazards; idols must be immersed using cranes or designated machinery provided by the corporation. A strict cutoff at 11 p.m. will be enforced at the barricades,” she said.

Organisers must strictly follow noise pollution laws, avoid excessive noise that affects elderly individuals and hospital patients, and prioritise light music or bhajan, the Deputy Commissioner specifically said.

​Raichur City Corporation Commissioner Jubin Mohapatra advised citizens against misbehaving with municipal staff and strictly prohibited intoxicated individuals from driving or entering immersion sites. He added that sound systems require valid permits under the Noise Pollution Rules.

​Superintendent of Police Arunangshu Giri emphasised road safety, urged mandatory helmet use and controlled driving speeds, and warned against the use of illegal DJ sound systems.

He disclosed that eight cases have already been registered against DJ sound system owners and operators for exceeding permissible decibel limits during recent processions.

​The meeting began with a one-minute silence to mourn the tragic deaths of two young boys who were run over by a tractor during a recent Ganesha procession in Zaheerabad, Raichur.

Assistant Commissioner Hampanna Sajjan, Additional Superintendent of Police Harish, and other district officials were present.
