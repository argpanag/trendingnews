What else are we looking forward to? I reckon Saints v Ravens could catch fire and also keen to see if the Jets can keep rolling with a big home win over the Packers. How the Patriots react after flopping in the season opener also intrigues.

Another potential cracker has another high-octane offense in the Cincinnati Bengals facing a mighty defense in the Houston Texans. Only will Cincy’s QB Joe Burrow be firing on all cylinders? He comes in not completely healthy with a back injury so expect Texans pass rusher Will Anderson Jr to be hungry to cause Burrow even more discomfort as Houston look to recover from a disappointing start last week.

Anderson has 93 quarterback pressures since the start of last season - second most in the league - with 40 happening in under 2.5sec. His explosive speed cause problems for a QB not exactly known for his mobility already.

There are some juicy matchups but by far the most enticing has to be Minnesota heading to Solider Field to take on Chicago for a little NFC North rivalry game. Both teams won impressively last week, the Bears maybe slightly more so as they shared the highest scoring shootout in week one history in their 59-37 win over the Panthers last week.

The linebacker was a nightmare last week recording one sack, 10 pressures, seven hits and forcing a fumble. The Vikings have won on their past six visits to Solider Field.
