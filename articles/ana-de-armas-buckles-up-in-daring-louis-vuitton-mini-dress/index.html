Ana de Armas buckles up in daring Louis Vuitton mini dress

TheTools · 23.10.2026 14:13 · source · permalink

The 38-year-old Bond Girl, who is rumoured to be dating Tom Cruise, wowed in this bold, high-fashion dress

Ana de Armas is known for pushing the boundaries when it comes to her high-end fashion choices, and the stunning star of Blonde did just that as she shared a series of life updates on her Instagram page. The carousel of images showed her attending various red carpet events, including one from earlier this month.

The Cuban-American star stepped out at Paris Fashion Week to watch Louis Vuitton's womenswear Spring/Summer 2027 offering. In keeping with her partnership with the famed fashion brand, Ana donned a blazer by the label, which she wore as a mini dress, over the top of a pair of shorts.

The leather jacket featured nude-toned harnesses at the sides, subtly exposing her torso and bust. With her waved raven tresses tumbling past her shoulders and sultry makeup highlighting her features, Ben Affleck's ex-girlfriend showcased her edgier side.

Elizabeth Hurley's safety pin dress

The side straps on Ana's dress reminded us of the iconic safety pin dress Elizabeth Hurley wore back in 1994.

Liz memorably debuted the style at the premiere of Four Weddings And A Funeral alongside her former boyfriend, Hugh Grant. The gown featured a mix of black silk and lycra and was held together with gold-toned, oversized safety pins. The safety pins drew attention to the cutout waist feature and were used to embellish the straps.

The glamorous gown oozed sultry chic through a plunging neckline and thigh-high slit. Thirty years later, Elizabeth admitted to People that she hadn't heard of Versace ahead of wearing the unforgettable dress. "It was just amazing to have something on that was so beautifully crafted," she told the publication in 2024, "and it looked incredibly daring, but it was actually completely safe. Like wearing the bathrobe, really."

Ana's latest plunging latest

We last saw Ana a week ago gracing the red carpet at the Academy Museum Gala, looking breathtaking in one of the most adventurous gowns we've ever seen the famously chic star step out in. 

Her sculpted black velvet dress by Louis Vuitton featured a plunging neckline that highlighted her décolletage. The star of Ballerina added black satin sandals and a myriad of dazzling jewels, including the French fashion brand's 'Sirius' necklace, which was crafted with tourmaline, aquamarine, tanzanite, sapphire, emerald and diamonds, with matching earrings from the Louis Vuitton Mythica collection.
