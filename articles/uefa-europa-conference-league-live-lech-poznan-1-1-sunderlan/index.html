  16. From Bury to AC Milan: Bournemouth's journey to 'dreamland'published at 19:54 BST
    19:54 BST

    Bournemouth v AC Milan (20:00 BST)

    "I'm pinching myself," says Bournemouth fan Kirk Tovey.

    "It's not long ago we were playing Oldham Athletic and Hartlepool United in League One. Now I'm about to watch us play AC Milan.

    "It's absolute madness."

    Fifteen years to the day after Bournemouth lost 2-1 at home to Bury in the third tier of English football, the Cherries host seven-time champions of Europe AC Milan in the Europa League on Thursday (20:00 BST).

    It is a game between the club that refused to die against the 11-time European Cup finalists on what promises to be a momentous night in the history of the 127-year-old Dorset club.

    "Over the last decade, my hometown club has achieved dreamland," Tony Pulis, who played for Bournemouth before managing them from 1992-94 and still lives in the area, tells BBC Sport.

    "It's an unbelievable story, when I think back to what the club was like when I was player, coach and then manager there in the 1980s and early 1990s, but one that hopefully just keeps going."

  19. Palace's defensive strugglespublished at 19:51 BST
    19:51 BST

    Besiktas v Crystal Palace (20:00 BST)

    Sami Mokbel
    BBC Sport Senior football correspondent

    Crystal Palace's recent DNA is rooted in defensive stability. Predecessors Oliver Glasner and Roy Hodgson produced well-organised teams with a solid base for its attacking players.

    Conceding close to 2.5 goals per game is so far removed from what the team's recent successes has been built upon that it has raised major concern from the club's powerbrokers.

    Palace are currently on course to concede 92 goals this season. No club has conceded more since Sheffield United shipped 104 goals to finish bottom in 2024.

    Personnel is contributing to those defensive deficiencies. Losing Guehi, Lacroix and Munoz in the space of six months represent significant losses.

    From the three centre-backs that started in Sunday's 5-0 loss to Brighton, Jaydee Canvot is 20 years old and Honest Ahanor only 18.

    Nevertheless, there is a clear sense from inside the club that Pierre Sage's system and approach has been a major contributing factor towards their troubles in defence.

    19:49 BST

    Besiktas v Crystal Palace (20:00 BST)
