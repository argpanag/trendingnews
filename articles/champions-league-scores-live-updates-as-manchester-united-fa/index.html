Champions League scores: Live updates as Manchester United face Como, Bayern Munich and Arsenal clash

TheTools · 21.10.2026 19:14 · source · permalink

Arsenal's trip to Bayern will be a rematch of a league phase match from last season, which the Gunners won 1-0 at Emirates Stadium

Matchday 3 in the UEFA Champions League comes to a close on Wednesday with a batch of intriguing fixtures, attention fixed firmly on games in Italy and Germany for two statement-making fixtures.

The day's action begins in the picturesque city of Como, where the hosts welcome Manchester United in a game that will test each side's mettle. Como are off to a strong start in their first-ever Champions League campaign, notably registering a lopsided win over RB Leipzig in their opener, and will like their chances against United. The Red Devils continue to underperform in Michael Carrick's first full season in charge and are only a week removed from Lisandro Martinez's criticism of the team's form, arguing the team needs to "do more" in order to turn things around this season.

Later, the focus shifts to Germany as Bayern Munich welcome Arsenal in a clash of title contenders eager to make their case. Bayern, though, will be understaffed for the meeting – Michael Olise is suspended and Jamal Musiala is injured, while head coach Vincent Kompany deals with a virus that could keep him away from the touchline. The hosts are also looking to bounce back after a surprise 3-3 draw at Viking on Matchday 2 that highlighted defensive vulnerabilities that the Gunners will be eager to exploit.

Real Madrid are also in action on Wednesday with a matchup against RB Leipzig, while Inter host Shakhtar Donetsk and Aston Villa welcome Viking in other matches of note.

Bukayo Saka subbed out

10' Arsenal start the game with a pretty notable setback -- Bukayo Saka has to come off for Eberechi Eze with an injury. He and Alphonso Davies both go for the same ball but they barely make contact and Saka appears to come out of it unscathed. He looks pretty awkward as he looks to make the pass and in no time at all, he's receiving medical attention and then comes off the pitch. Understandably, the details of the injury are unclear but his night's done.

Penalty for Sporting!

5' First goal of the late kickoffs goes Sporting's way, the Lisbon-based side winning a penalty in the early minutes of their game against LASK. Luis Suarez is cool, calm and collected and converts the spotkick with ease, putting his side on course for a third successive win in the Champions League.

Bayern Munich vs. Arsenal starting lineups

Harry Kane and Alphonso Davies headline Bayern Munich's understaffed attack, while Martin Odegaard and Kai Havertz do the same for Arsenal. Here's a look at both teams' lineups.

Manchester United's consolation goal

90'+2 Manchester United might get something out of the only dismal trip anyone's ever made to Como, substitute Joshua Zirkzee coming up with a simple finish to make the score 4-1. It's his first goal of the season but naturally, there is not much of a celebration and the home crowd barely even acknowledges the existence of Zirkzee's strike.

Como bag a fourth!

76' Make it four for Como! Kaiki Bruno connects with Jacobo Ramon, whose header from close range will serve as the first Champions League goal of his career. It was already an unforgettable night for Como but Ramon's goal is like the cherry on top, forcing serious questions of Manchester United in the process -- their winless run is certain to extend to seven games across all competitions, very clearly trending in the wrong direction once again.

Galatasaray add another!

61' Baris Alper Yilmaz connects with Victor Osimhen, who comes up with an impressive finish and finally has the goal many have been waiting for him to score all night. Lille's defense is struggling to keep up with Galatasaray's defense at this point, making things a bit too easy for the visitors so far in this second half. Galatasaray now have a 4-2 lead and maybe, just maybe, are on course for their first points of the Champions League campaign.

Another for Como!

57' It's going from bad to worse for Manchester United and it's happening so quickly! Nico Paz nicks the ball off of an opponent and takes advantage of the gift he was given, darting towards goal and completing the brace. He's really enjoying himself today -- he's up to three goal contributions in what is turning into a dominant showing for Como, while United are still without a shot on target.

Galatasaray back in front!

52' They won't stop scoring in Lille! This time it's Galatasaray, who are back in front as Gabriel Sara connects with Lucas Torreira, who scores with a header. Lille goalkeeper Berke Ozer did not cover himself in glory on that one, essentially turning the ball into his own net when he had it in his arms. The visitors now have a 3-2 lead but the way things have gone at Stade Pierre-Mauroy today, that lead may be a fragile one.

A second for Como!

49' Well, that didn't take very long for Como (again)! Nico Paz kicks things off with a smart pass and Anastasios Douvikas finishes things off with a powerful strike from close range for a 2-0 lead over Manchester United. The Red Devils did start the second half somewhat brightly but that feels like a long time ago now.

Two halftime changes for Manchester United

Down a goal after an unimpressive first half, Manchester United head coach Michael Carrick goes to the bench to change things up after the break. Carlos Baleba and Amad Diallo are on for Andrey Santos and Matheus Cunha, the newcomers hoping to add some energy to a team that registered just three shots and 0.1 expected goals in the first 45 minutes.

Galatasaray have their equalizer!

44' The action won't stop in Lille, where Galatasaray are now back on level terms! Roland Sallai whips in a brilliant cross so the ball finds itself just a few feet from the goal line, where Aleksey Batrakov gets a head on it and sends the ball into the back of the net. Batrakov was somehow completely unmarked on that play, making the finish an easy one.

Lille in front!

39' What a night it's turning out to be for Soriba Diaoune! Dilane Bakwa makes a run into the box and takes a crack at goal, Galatasaray goalkeeper Ugurcan Cakir coming up with the save. The ball heads straight back into play, though, and Hakon Haraldsson gets a light touch on the ball before Diaoune gets his feet on it, the 19-year-old Lille native coming up with another important finish to give his side a 2-1 lead.

VAR check in Lille

28' Victor Osimhen dashes towards goal and Lille goalkeeper Berke Ozer rushes out to stop him, Osimhen falling over in the process. The referee quickly points to the spot believing Ozer committed a foul but he then heads to the monitor and the check is a quick one -- Ozer got the ball, not the player, and so the penalty is canceled.

Manchester United taking a backseat

22' Manchester United have not exactly made a dent at Como yet, so far ceding the ball to the opposition and taking just one shot valued at 0.05 expected goals. It is not exactly for a lack of trying but they have struggled to make much of their forward runs, plays fizzling out pretty quickly once they get in and around the penalty area. You can add this slow start to the list of complaints about United struggling to live up to their potential -- until further notice, they seem stuck in the land of mediocrity.

Lille with an equalizer!

7' These teams are wasting no time getting down to business. It's Lille's turn to get on the scoresheet, finding an equalizer quickly after going down to Galatasaray just 75 seconds into the game. Center back Alexsandro makes a deep run and picks out an excellent pass to 19-year-old Soriba Diaoune, who then delivers a slick finish that puts his side level.

Como take the lead!

3' Como score the opener in their first attacking play of the game, courtesy of none other than Nico Paz. He receives the ball near the penalty area, works his way inward but takes an excellent hit from an angle and from distance to give his side the lead. That's one way to make a statement when Manchester United are in town!

Galatasaray are on the board!

2' The opening whistle only blew 75 seconds ago but Galatasaray already have a lead on the road at Lille! The visitors charge forward and though Lille goalkeeper Berke Ozer makes the initial stop, the ball goes straight back into play and Yunus Akgun comes up with a simple finish.

Manchester United vs. Como starting lineups

Bryan Mbeumo leads the line for Manchester United, who are looking for their first win in all competitions since Matchday 1 of the Champions League, while Nico Paz headlines Como's team. Here's a look at the starting linesup.

Como starting XI: Jean Butez, Yan Couto, Jacobo Ramon, Marc-Oliver Kempf, Alex Valle, Luis Milla, Maximo Perrone, Lucas Da Cunha, Nico Paz, Martin Baturina, Anastasios Douvikas

Manchester United starting XI: Senne Lammens, Diogo Dalot, Harry Maguire, Matthijs de Ligt, Noussair Mazraoui, Andrey Santos, Youri Tielemans, Matheus Cunha, Bruno Fernandes, Mason Mount, Bryan Mbeumo
