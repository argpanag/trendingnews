Jennifer Lawrence Joins Instagram in the Most Jennifer Lawrence Way

TheTools · 10.09.2026 00:25 · source · permalink

Jennifer Lawrence finally joined Instagram after spending years avoiding a verified account, though she said the username she wanted to use "was taken."

The odds weren't in Jennifer Lawrence's favor when she joined Instagram.

The Hunger Games alum finally launched a verified account on the social media platform, quipping in the description of her newly-minted @jlaw profile that "jenniferlawrence was taken."

And while she made her already almost one million followers wait for her first post, she soon delivered.

"So, I'm giving it a shot," she began her Seot. 10 video. "And if anyone says anything negative, I will quit. Immediately. No questions asked."

She further warned, "If anyone thinks something mean, I'll know. I'll know, and I'll quit."

But the 36-year-old wasn't done. As she added in a Story, "Please be mindful that any mean or aggressive comments can affect the baby. It's me. I'm the baby." 

After all, Lawrence spent over a decade without a public social media account. As she told BBC 1 Radio back in 2014, "I'm not very good on [the] phone or technology."

"I cannot really keep up with emails so the idea of Twitter is so unthinkable to me," she said at the time. "I don't really understand what it is. It's like this weird enigma that people talk about. It's fine. I respect that."

But that's not to say the Oscar winner is completely unplugged from the internet. 

"I’m on it. But I’m a voyeur: I watch, I don’t speak," she shared in a 2019 interview with InStyle, per Time. "There is always so much backlash. So many people are listening and paying attention, and they have so many opinions about absolutely everything."

"I really don’t want to welcome that unless it’s absolutely necessary," Lawrence continued. "I don’t want to put myself out there for no reason. Unless I’m promoting something or something really burns my onions, you won't hear from me."

Well, from an official account. After all, the Joy actress—who shares two young kids with husband Cooke Maroney—does have what she describes as "a secret life on TikTok."

"I get in fights on TikTok," she admitted in an interview with Fandango last year. "I get in fights in the comment section. One girl was like, 'How old are you? Get a life!'"

According to Lawrence, her incognito persona "started off" with online conversations about the Real Housewives franchise.

“And then it got really intense when I started commenting on the royal family," she added. "I guess I rage-bait on TikTok."
