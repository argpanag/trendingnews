Duchess Sophie battles the elements in flattering flares – with windswept bob

TheTools · 08.09.2026 18:44 · source · permalink

The Duchess of Edinburgh went full-on country chic during a visit to Whin's Farm in Cumbria

The Duchess of Edinburgh looked fabulous in a country chic ensemble during a visit to Whin's Farm near Kendal on Tuesday.

The 61-year-old royal braved the wind and the rain as she opened the first farm in the Addington Fund's New Entrant Scheme during her two-day visit to Cumbria.

Her outing served to highlight innovative pathways into farming for first-generation farmers, ensuring the sustainability of small-scale hill farms and the future of the rural economy.

Looking impeccable as ever, Duchess Sophie's fashion choices also took centre stage. Battling the elements as she chatted to members of the local community on the hillside, Sophie was dressed for the occasion in a smart tweed jacket and flattering chocolate flares.

She opted for a pair of sensible, weather-proof Timberland boots and layered up with an extra jacket for added warmth over her white shirt.

A gold-buckled belt cinched in the trousers at the waist, adding extra definition to the look. Sophie's new bob was the star of the show, looking tousled following a blustery morning and plenty of showers.

The Duchess of Edinburgh's two-day visit to Cumbria.

Prince Edward's wife is visiting Cumbria to lead a two-day royal tour focused on the rural economy, sustainable agriculture, and supporting young talent in farming.

As Patron of the Addington Fund, she officially opened Whins Farm near Kendal, marking the launch of the charity's New Entrant Scheme designed to give first-generation farmers a vital foot on the ladder and safeguard the future of small-scale hill farms.

Continuing her engagement with local food networks, the Duchess visited community initiative Home Grown Here near Penrith. The royal met growers, volunteers, and local organisers to learn about community-led approaches to sustainable agriculture and efforts aimed at widening regional access to fresh, locally grown produce.

The royal visit also involved meetings with representatives from the Royal Countryside Fund, the West Lakeland Farmer-Led Nature Recovery initiative, and the Penrith Young Farmers, who are celebrating their 90th anniversary.

Her Cumbrian tour concludes tomorrow with visits to traditional upland farming businesses in Borrowdale, near Keswick.

Duchess Sophie's new look – and fabulous hair transformation

Since entering her 60s, the Duchess of Edinburgh has been having more fun with her fashion – not to mention her hairstyles!

Debuting a new bob after decades of below-the-shoulder hair, Sophie stepped out at the Groundswell Regenerative Agriculture Festival at Lannock Farm over the summer, showcasing a fresh new blunt bob.

"Duchess Sophie's new bob is a beautiful evolution of the polished '90s bob," Elliot Bute, hairstylist at Hershesons Fitzrovia, told HELLO! at the time.

"It feels timeless rather than trend-led, with soft internal layers and a gentle side part that creates easy movement and a luxurious finish," the hair expert noted. "It's sophisticated without looking overly styled, which is what makes it feel so modern.

Sophie's new bob can be styled in many different ways, allowing the royal to be more playful with her looks for engagements.

 "The beauty of this cut is its versatility. It can be worn sleek for a polished occasion or left slightly undone for everyday ease, proving that truly elegant hair is about movement, condition and effortless confidence,”  Elliot explained.
