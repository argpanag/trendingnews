Partizan have officially parted ways with former NBA forward Jabari Parker after the two sides mutually agreed to terminate his contract.

Partizan Mozzart Bet Belgrade have officially parted ways with American forward Jabari Parker.

After his loan stint with Joventut Badalona came to an end last summer, Parker returned to Belgrade but was not part of the team's plans.

The 31-year-old forward was still under contract with Partizan until the summer of 2027, but both sides have now mutually agreed to terminate the deal.

According to earlier reports, Parker was set to earn $2.5 million this season.

After signing a two-year deal with the club, Parker made 15 EuroLeague appearances for Partizan last season, averaging 7.7 points and 2.5 rebounds in 19 minutes per game.

Parker eventually finished the season on loan with Joventut, while both Partizan head coach Joan Penarroya and president Ostoja Mijailovic later made it clear that he was not part of the club's plans for the 2026-27 campaign.

With Joventut, he averaged 16.4 points, 5.6 rebounds, and 1.4 assists in the FIBA Basketball Champions League, along with 12.6 points, 4.5 rebounds, and 1.5 assists in Spain's ACB League.
