75 of Alabama's Greatest Wins Under Nick Saban

TheTools · 31.10.2026 11:00 · source · permalink

In this story:

Former Alabama head coach Nick Saban turned 75-years-old on Saturday.

The College Football Hall of Famer won some incredible games during his 17-year tenure. The seven-time national champion (six at Alabama) logged a 206-29 record in Tuscaloosa from 2007-23.

In honor of his age, here's a look at Saban's 75 greatest regular-season and postseason wins with the Crimson Tide. Paired with each victory is at least one MVP selected by BamaCentral.

Saban's 75 Greatest Wins at Alabama

Note: This is in chronological order. It is not a ranked list.

Sept. 1, 2007: Alabama 52, Western Carolina 6 (first game/win as Alabama’s head coach)
MVP: Terry Grant – 18 carries, 134 yards, 3 touchdowns

MVP: Terry Grant – 24 carries, 173 yards, 2 touchdowns
