NextDC slashes earnings forecasts after Melbourne power delays

TheTools · 20.10.2026 22:33 · source · permalink

NextDC has flagged that it could sell data centre development sites after slashing its full-year earnings guidance by more than a third because of delays in connecting a Melbourne data centre to power networks.

Electricity distributor Jemena suspended power connections in late September after fierce community backlash against the installation of concrete power poles up to 22 metres tall, almost three times the height of traditional wooden poles.
