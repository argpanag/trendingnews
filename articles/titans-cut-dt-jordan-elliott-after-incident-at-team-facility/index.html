Titans Cut DT Jordan Elliott After Incident At Team Facility

TheTools · 20.10.2026 09:10 · source · permalink
  • NFL Transactions
  • Titans

The Tennessee Titans announced they have released DT Jordan Elliott following an incident at the team facility.

He had just signed a two-year deal worth up to $8.5 million this offseason, following HC Robert Saleh over from the 49ers.  

Elliott, 28, was a one-year starter at Missouri after transferring from Texas as a sophomore. The Browns selected him with the No. 88 pick in the third round of the 2020 NFL Draft. 

Elliott was scheduled to make $2.743 million when the Browns reduced his compensation to $1.7 million, with $1.5 million of that sum guaranteed. He finished out the final year of a four-year, $4,588,430 rookie contract that included an $897,041 signing bonus before joining the 49ers on a two-year deal worth $10 million back in 2024. 

The Titans signed Elliott to a two-year deal worth up to $8.5 million this offseason. 

In 2026, Elliott appeared in five games for the Titans and recorded 11 total tackles.
