The most hyped video game releases of the decade is, without a doubt, “Grand Theft Auto VI.” In fact, it’s believed the game is going to a complete pop culture takeover, as millions are expected to take the day off to play it on launch day — Thursday Nov. 19.

However, the best way to play “GTA 6” is with the Sony PlayStation 5, thanks to the gaming console’s advanced graphics, silky smooth motion, immersive audio quality and powerful performance. Although the PS5 Pro is sold out across the web, it’s still available to purchase for $899 at PlayStation Direct.

Sony PlayStation 5 Pro

Designed for better performance, the PS5 Pro, which comes with Sony’s PS DualSense Wireless Controller, has a higher refresh rate of up to 120Hz with 4K Ultra HD picture quality with enhanced AI-upscaling.

With the standard PS5, you had to pick one: Either high refresh rate or 4K. But, with the PS5 Pro, you can pick both out-of-the-box. This means games move more fluidly with zero lag and Ultra HD video quality. The gaming consoles has double the on-board storage capacity with 2TB instead of 1TB. It has faster Wi-Fi connectivity for speedy game downloads, stronger cloud gaming and quick data transfers.

The pro model features advance ray tracing for in-game shadows, reflections and illuminations. This adds to improved graphics and realism, while it adds to deeper immersion to feel more true to life.

Best of all? All PS5 games work on the PS5 Pro, including “GTA 6,” which starts at $79.99 for the standard edition, “007 First Light,” “Marvel’s Wolverine,” “Resident Evil Requiem,” “Castlevania: Belmont’s Curse,” “Saros,” “Nioh 3” and others.
