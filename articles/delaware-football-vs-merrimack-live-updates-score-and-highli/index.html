Delaware football vs. Merrimack live updates, score and highlights

TheTools · 03.09.2026 23:04 · source · permalink

Nick Minicucci was one of the most productive quarterbacks in the country last year.

That has earned the Delaware starter a heap of preseason recognition, including Conference USA preseason Offensive Player of the Year and watch list status for various awards.

While appreciating the notice and the notoriety, Minicucci’s sentiments are centered on his team, not himself.

As the Blue Hens kick off the 2026 season Sept. 3 against Merrimack at 7 p.m. at Delaware Stadium, Minicucci welcomes the opportunity to begin creating new successes while expanding his repertoire.

“I don't accept any of those because it's preseason,” Minicucci said of the early hype. “Why do I care about something I haven't earned yet? That stuff's all just out there. It's outside noise to me, and I don't really care about it. It feels good, but what is that going to do for me on Thursday?”

The fourth-year Blue Hen will be focused on operating coach Ryan Carty’s spread offense at peak efficiency while becoming more comfortable with his surrounding playmakers, which include several transfer arrivals.

“I really want to win a conference championship,” Minicucci added. “I don't really care about personal accolades. I've never really been that way. I'm not a selfish guy. I really care about just the team, and I want us to win and win together. A Conference USA championship here at Delaware would mean the world to me.”

Minicucci was one of just two quarterbacks last year nationally to have at least 3,500 passing yards and 10 rushing TDs, along with Vanderbilt’s Heisman runner-up Diego Pavia.

Carty has been pleased with Minicucci’s ongoing quest to be better, despite his 2025 accomplishments. Being a second-year starter allows him to have a deeper grasp of how to best move the football, and try new, different and potentially better things.

“Nick's done a really nice job,” Carty said on the eve of his fifth season as coach. “He's always asking for more. There's never a day that goes by that he's going to rest and say ‘I made it. I’m good.’ It's always something that's driving him.”
