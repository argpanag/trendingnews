Interview: Joseph Zada and Mckenna Grace on 'The Hunger Games: Sunrise On The Reaping'.

TheTools · 19.11.2026 11:33 · source · permalink

"It was amazing to see Haymitch and Maysilee bond and become almost like a brother and sister," shares Joseph Zada about his on-screen partnership with District 12, 'The Hunger Games: Sunrise On The Reaping' castmate, Mckenna Grace. Further explaining, Zada says: "It’s one of my favourite dynamics in the film because it has such a beautiful arc. They’re stuck together in this terrible situation - much like Peeta and Katniss were - as they go to war together."

Based on the novel by Suzanne Collins, Zada succeeds Woody Harrelson's portrayal of Haymitch Abernathy in this origin story, which also stars Maya Hawke, Jesse Plemons, Elle Fanning, Glenn Close, Ralph Fiennes, and Kieran Culkin.

Coup De Main spoke to Joseph Zada and Mckenna Grace about their new film, 'The Hunger Games: Sunrise On The Reaping', while the duo were in Sydney recently...

COUP DE MAIN: Jo, you've said that while you were filming 'East Of Eden' in my homeland, New Zealand, you dressed up in your character's costume and walked around casinos at midnight. Was there anything similar you did to get into the mind of Haymitch for this role?
JOSEPH ZADA: I bought a pair of mining boots, that I broke in. Did that for about two, three months. Actually, I probably wore them the entire time that I was filming. It felt good to have a bit more weight. I'm so used to wearing sneakers everywhere, so it was lovely to have that weight walking around and feeling the character that way. And I think I just went overboard on journaling a lot because I had the book, and the script, and the films - I have three journals for each of them and wrote down all sorts of notes, and then I had one book that connected them all that gave me page numbers and ways to come back.

CDM: Do you agree or disagree with Lenore Dove when she says that: "You can’t count on things happening tomorrow just because they happened in the past. It’s faulty logic."
JOSEPH: I completely agree.
MCKENNA GRACE: I agree totally; I agree with everything Lenore Dove says.
JOSEPH: I don't, but I agree with that 100%. I agree. I actually struggled a lot at the start to understand that, which was perfect for the character. I was like, 'What are you talking about? That doesn't make any sense.' Of course... and then by the end of the film, I was like, 'Okay, actually, I get it now.'

CDM: Mckenna, what was it like for you to contribute 'Half Alive' to the film's soundtrack?
MCKENNA: Oh my gosh, it was so exciting. It was beyond, for me. To be in 'The Hunger Games' in any capacity is a dream come true. I'm such a fan girl. So, also to have a piece of music that I've written about this character that I love so deeply, and get to be a part of this?! I wrote it, and I sent it over to Francis [Lawrence], and I was like, 'I wrote this by the way, I just wanted you to hear it', and then he responded two days later and he's like, 'Oh, I love this, I'm gonna send it to the composer, we'll put it on the album.' It was so scary. I just sent it to him, and then all of a sudden it was a thing. It was insane. Such an honour.
JOSEPH: It's so beautiful.
MCKENNA: Jo was the first person I played it for.
JOSEPH: I was crying.
MCKENNA: I was like, 'I totally wrote this about you.'
JOSEPH: I was crying so much.
