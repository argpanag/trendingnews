Second man charged over disappearance and death of Braydon Hepworth

TheTools · 22.10.2026 03:59 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Crime and Justice

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._f0.YIZs2-J2FpwgcBcJJb29s1vXmFAWHLh_p1oUyv-l-RA/crime-and-justice/2082117%2Fsecond-man-charged-over-disappearance-and-death-of-braydon-hepworth

A man has been charged in relation to the death and disappearance of Braydon Hepworth.

Hepworth's body was found buried in a shed in Linwood, Christchurch, on 8 October after he had gone missing early September.

Braydon Hepworth, whose body was found in Linwood, Christchurch, following his disappearance.

Supplied / Police

A 34-year-old Chistchurch man faces charges relating to the concealment of Hepworth's body.

He is due to appear in the Christchurch District Court on Friday.

A 60-year-old Christchurch man appeared in court on 10 October on charges of concealing Hepworth's body and making a false statement to police.

The investigation into Hepworth's death continues.
