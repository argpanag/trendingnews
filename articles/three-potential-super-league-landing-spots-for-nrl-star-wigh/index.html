Three potential Super League landing spots for NRL star Wighton amid interest

TheTools · 14.11.2026 16:04 · source · permalink

According to reports down under, South Sydney Rabbitohs star Jack Wighton is attracting Super League interest ahead of the 2027 campaign.

Souths are apparently open to the idea of moving Wighton on this off-season as they strive to create the space required on the salary cap to sign Sam Walker from Sydney Roosters. However, Super League has been mentioned as a potential destination for the 2020 Dally M medal winner who has won the World Cup and State of Origin over the course of his career.

Wighton turns 34 in February, but every Super League club would love to have someone of his standard within their ranks.

With that in mind, here's a look at three potential Super League destinations should he leave Souths in the coming months.

Hull FC

The Black and Whites are thought to be looking for another centre and Wighton would certainly fit the bill in that regard. The Australian provides another option in the halves, too, which is something the Black and Whites could potentially do with following Aidan Sezer's exit on Saturday morning.

Sezer's departure means Hull are currently compliant when it comes to Super League's overseas quota rules and it also frees up a chunk of salary cap space. However, with Sam Lisone facing an uncertain future at Hull as he awaits the conclusion to the internal investigation that saw the club suspend him back in September, and with FC open to moving on Cade Cust too, Hull might just be able to afford Wighton.

London Broncos

Jason Demetrious has already signed Wighton once, with the current Broncos boss being the man to take him to Souths in the first place, and he could move to repeat the feat once London's promotion has been confirmed.

The Broncos aren't short of outside back options and they might be more likely to play Wighton in the halves, but signing Wighton would certainly be a statement of intent as they step up to Super League and attempt to make a splash.

Leeds Rhinos

Jack Bird's future at Leeds is far from certain right now and if he were to leave the club, Wighton might just be the perfect replacement at Headingley. The Rhinos have already seen Harry Newman and Ryan Hall leave the club this autumn and with his former Canberra Raiders teammate Nick Cotric potentially taking up the wing spot, Wighton could slot in at centre.

Alternatively, he could take on the stand-off spot and ease the pressure on George Brown heading into the new season. Brad Arthur has put a lot of faith in Brown to be the man to replace Brodie Croft in the side and partner Jake Connor, but the opportunity to sign Wighton might just be too good an opportunity to miss for the Rhinos.
