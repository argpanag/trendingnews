Woman ‘appalled’ after stranger buried in her graveyard plot

TheTools · 13.11.2026 14:17 · source · permalink

Parish council apologises as pensioner wins three-year battle to have body exhumed from space beside her mother

A woman has spoken of her ordeal after winning a three-year battle to have a stranger’s body exhumed from a grave plot she had reserved.

Marie Lane, 69, paid in 2013 for the right to be buried in a space next to her late mother at Deeping St James Cemetery, Lincolnshire.

But she says when she visited the cemetery in 2023, she was “appalled” to find someone else had been buried in her reserved plot.

Now, following a lengthy appeals process, the Ministry of Justice has granted a licence to exhume the body.

Mrs Lane told the BBC: “I just stood there and didn’t know what to do, to be honest. A person I don’t know was buried in my grave.”

She said she had “sleepless nights” as a result of the mistake.

Deeping St James parish council, which manages the cemetery, said it acknowledged that a mistake was made, and apologised “for the distress this has caused both families”.

A letter from the Ministry of Justice to the parish council, as reported by the BBC, shows the department granted the licence despite “moral and religious” objections from the family of the person wrongly buried in the plot.

The letter added that the exhumation was granted because of a lack of alternative solutions to rectify the mistake.

Mrs Lane said she sympathised with the family of the person who was mistakenly buried in the plot, but that the parish council had rejected multiple requests she had made to meet them.

Her husband, Eric Lane, 77, said his wife no longer liked going to the cemetery.

“There’s so much nastiness, bitterness connected to here I don’t know if it will ever pass,” he told the BBC.
