NBA Basketball: Brooklyn Exercises Third-Year Options on Wolf and Sharaf

TheTools · 29.10.2026 17:48 · source · permalink

The Brooklyn Nets announced they have exercised the third-year options in the contracts of two Israelis, Ben Sharaf and Deni Wolf, ensuring their stay for next season as well. Alongside the two, the Nets also exercised options on Yegor Dyomin, Nolan Traore and Drake Powell. Sharaf is expected to earn $3.17 million next season, while Wolf will earn $3.08 million. These are guaranteed amounts, even in the event that either of them is cut from the roster.
