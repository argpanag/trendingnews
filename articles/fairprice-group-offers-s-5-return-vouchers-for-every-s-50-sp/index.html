FairPrice Group offers S$5 return vouchers for every S$50 spent, no minimum spend required

TheTools · 29.10.2026 06:19 · source · permalink

Deals Latest News Singapore

The Return Vouchers initiative will run from 29 Oct to 9 Nov, with a cap of four return vouchers per transaction.

By Rochelle Lee - 29 Oct 2026, 2:19 pm

Customers can receive return vouchers at all FairPrice outlets from 29 Oct to 9 Nov

In a press release on Friday (29 Oct), FairPrice Group (FPG) announced a revival of its popular FairPrice Return Vouchers initiative.

FairPrice shoppers can get a S$5 return voucher for every S$50 spent from 29 Oct to 9 Nov.

The vouchers are part of FPG’s ongoing efforts to help customers manage their daily expenditure on household needs.

Also, the scheme provides additional savings during the festive period, with year-end celebrations such as Deepavali and Christmas around the corner.

Vouchers capped at 4 per pax

Customers will be issued a S$5 FairPrice Return Voucher for every S$50 spent in a single transaction in stores.

Source: FairPrice Group

This initiative applies across all FairPrice, FairPrice Xtra, FairPrice Finest and Unity pharmacies.

Each customer is entitled to receive a maximum of four Return Vouchers per transaction.

Source: FairPrice Group

The Return Vouchers can be used at all FairPrice supermarkets and Unity stores. No minimum spend is required.

Shoppers should note that vouchers may only be redeemed a day after the date of issuance, until 30 Nov.

Source: FairPrice Group

More savings deals available throughout the year

In 2026, FPG has implemented and extended various savings initiatives, keeping budget accessibility for Singaporeans in mind.

The most recent initiative saw lowered prices on selected chicken and egg products across more than 100 FairPrice supermarkets.

Community Health Assist Scheme (CHAS) Blue and Orange cardholders get to enjoy doubled weekly discounts at FairPrice outlets, until 1 Jan 2027.

This year, over S$10 million in savings have been extended to CHAS Cardholders.

FPG locked prices on over 500 daily essentials from 1 June to 31 Aug. This included house-brand rice, cooking oil, fresh and frozen meat, and vegetables.

In honour of May Day, FPG offered a 50% discount on selected essential products for NTUC and Link members, providing them with over $5 million in savings.

From 19 March to 10 June, FPG launched the Best Sellers for Less campaign, offering customers up to 36% off house brand essentials.

This aimed to keep groceries affordable through its Own Brands business.

Source: FairPrice Group
