PS5 to Integrate Anime into Main Menu as Sony Tightens Ties with Crunchyroll

TheTools · 07.10.2026 21:45 · source · permalink

"Anime and gaming have overlapping communities"

Moments ago, PlayStation boss Hideaki Nishino took to the stage at the inaugural Crunchyroll Anime Future Forum to confirm it’ll be integrating anime into the PS5’s main menu with a “dedicated hub”, beginning Spring 2027.

He said the initiative will help “make it easier for the PlayStation community to discover and explore the anime content they enjoy”.

PS5 owners can already access Crunchyroll anime via the dedicated app available for the console, but this new initiative will place the streaming service front and centre.
