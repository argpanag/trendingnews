Save $300

WD Black SN850P 2TB

Along with a roomy 2TB storage capacity, you’ll get read and write speeds up to 7300 and 6600MB/s, respectively. It might not be the fastest gaming SSD on the market, but what it lacks in speed it more than makes up for in reliability. The integrated heatsink helps regulate waste heat, keeping your new drive running at optimal temperatures and preventing dangerous overheating that can cause data loss and serious damage.

While officially licensed for use in PlayStation 5 consoles, the M.2 form factor means that with a quick reformatting, you can easily drop the SN850P into a laptop or desktop for an affordable storage upgrade. Unfortunately for Mac users, it’s only compatible with the PlayStation and Windows operating systems.

The WD Black SN850P has an endurance tolerance of 1,200 TB, which means you can write and overwrite data for years without fear of drive failure.
