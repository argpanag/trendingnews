Kansas City weather: Hot, windy Monday ahead with highs near 90

TheTools · 13.09.2026 23:19 · source · permalink

Monday is going to feel like summer across the Kansas City.

Temperatures will start in the lower to mid-60s Monday morning before climbing, with mostly sunny skies.

Temperatures are expected to reach around 90 degrees, with the heat index potentially climbing into the 95-to-100-degree range.

It’s going to be windy.

Winds will run around 10 to 20 mph, with gusts up to 25 mph at times. A stray shower is possible early Monday morning, mainly before sunrise, but the afternoon should stay dry.

Chiefs fans heading to Arrowhead Stadium can expect hot and windy conditions for tailgating.

Temperatures will be around 90 degrees during the afternoon before falling to about 86 degrees around the 7:15 p.m. kickoff.

By the end of the game, temperatures should be near 81 degrees, with warm and humid conditions sticking around.
