JUST CAPITAL FOR CAMILO

TheTools · 24.10.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

CAMILO DURAN is looking to hoist his goal tally to double figures when Celtic take on Hibs at Easter Road in the midday kick-off tomorrow.

The Colombian striker fired in his ninth strike in the 7-1 demolition against Hearts at Parkhead last weekend and is eager to turn in another capital performance against the side that are currently propping up the Premiership, SEVENTEEN points adrift of the champions in pole position.

Shrugging off the disappointing Europa League loss to Celta Vigo on Thursday, Duran said: “We couldn’t get the three points we wanted, but now we have to focus on Sunday.

“We want to be champions, so we can’t afford to drop points. We’ll go out there looking for all three points.”

Discussing his role as attack-leader, the £6million summer recruit from Qarabag, speaking to CelticPlayer, admitted: “As everyone knows, that’s my position and I enjoy playing there.

“I know it’s a huge responsibility, but I’ll always try to give my best for this badge.”

Duran has played several games in the wide right position, but it now looks as though it is a straight fight between him and £11.5million Kasper Hogh for the main striking role.

The 24-year-old newly-capped international hitman revealed: “When he doesn’t play, he always encourages me. When I don’t play, I always encourage him.

“It’s healthy competition and whoever plays will always give their best.”

Preparing for his first game at Easter Road, Duran added: “I’m really looking forward to helping the team, getting the three points and scoring there, as well.”
