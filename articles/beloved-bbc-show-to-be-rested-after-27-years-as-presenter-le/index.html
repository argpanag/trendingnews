Beloved BBC show to be 'rested' after 27 years as presenter left 'devastated'

TheTools · 16.10.2026 21:43 · source · permalink

DIY SOS has been 'rested' by the BBC(Image: We Love TV)

DIY SOS has been shelved after 27 years on our screens. The BBC has confirmed that it is 'resting' the beloved renovation show hosted by Nick Knowles in order to cut costs.

The popular programme has been hosted by Nick since 1999. It sees the presenter and and his team joined by volunteers taking on various projects across the UK to help families in need. Sources claimed that Nick and the team are "devastated" by the decision.

The BBC has also confirmed there won't be a Big Build for Children In Need this year. A BBC spokesperson told Daily Star: "In light of savings challenges we have taken the decision to rest DIY SOS in 2027 and commission a new spin-off show instead from producers South Shore.

Nick and the team are said to be "devastated"(Image: Instagram)

"DIY SOS Rebuilt My Life (w/t) will revisit successes from the show’s past and expand the brand on the BBC in a more cost-effective way next year."

A source told The Sun: "Nick and the entire team are devastated. The decision, they were told, is down to cost-savings being needed. To them it’s a slap in the face because so many people give their time and materials for nothing. Tears have been shed by all. Like the viewers they see first hand how much DIY SOS has helped people over the years.

"For them, it feels like the BBC are turning their backs at a time when communities and charities need them more than ever."

There will also not be a Big Build for Children In Need this year(Image: BBC Studios/Paul White)

Last year, DIY SOS constructed a £1million children's cancer care unit in Northwich, Cheshire, in just 12 days. This week, in an episode from the 35th series, the team helped a family in Lancashire renovate their house to allow their son Dan, 17, come home from hospital after three years. He has been left with disabilities after a life-saving operation to remove a brain tumour.

His dad Nigel told the BBC of the help they received: "You can't help but be humbled about how many people have given up their time, companies giving materials to make this happen because without that it just wouldn't happen.

"I'm so grateful to everybody... I can't hammer home how many good people that are out there. It renews your faith in humankind."

Daily Star has contacted representatives of Nick Knowles and the BBC for comment.
