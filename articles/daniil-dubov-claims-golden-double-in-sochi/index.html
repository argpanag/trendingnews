Daniil Dubov Claims Golden Double in Sochi

TheTools · 18.10.2026 14:27 · source · permalink

Margarita Potapova became the Russian women's blitz champion.


Friday, October 16, the Zhemchuzhina Grand Hotel in Sochi hosted the Russian Blitz Championships among men and women. 

Daniil Dubov of Moscow followed up his rapid success by winning the Russian Blitz Championship. The grandmaster scored 11 points out of 13. GM Vladimir Zakhartsov (Moscow) finished second with 10 points. IM Aleksandr Usov of Moscow took third place with 9.5 points, edging out four other competitors on his superior tie-breakers.

WIM Margarita Potapova (Krasnodar Krai) became the Russian women's blitz champion, scoring 10.5 points out of 13. WGM Ekaterina Goltseva (Nizhny Novgorod Oblast) came in one point behind. WFM Maria Pershina (Saint Petersburg) claimed bronze, scoring 9 points and finishing ahead of four other competitors thanks to her superior tie-breakers. 

Congratulations to the winners and prize-takers!

The Russian Rapid and Blitz Chess Championships were organised by the Chess Federation of Russia with the support of the Ministry of Sports of the Russian Federation and the Russian Sports Foundation. 

General partner of the CFR is PhosAgro.

Media partner is the people’s social network Odnoklassniki.
