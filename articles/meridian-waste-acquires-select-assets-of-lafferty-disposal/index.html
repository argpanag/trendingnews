Meridian Waste acquires select assets of Lafferty Disposal

TheTools · 07.10.2026 14:31 · source · permalink

Strategic tuck-in deal will expand Meridian Waste’s collection services in south-central Virginia.

Meridian Waste has acquired select assets of Lafferty Disposal, a residential waste collection company based in Meherrin, Virginia. The deal closed Oct. 1.

According to Charlotte, North Carolina-based Meridian Waste, the deal will expand its residential collection presence in south-central Virginia and will operate as a tuck-in to Meridian Waste – Lunenburg Hauling, located in Lunenburg, Virginia. The hauling operation is co-located with Meridian Waste’s Lunenburg Landfill, which the company says creates an integrated collection and disposal platform to efficiently serve customers throughout the region.  

Lafferty Disposal provides residential waste collection services throughout Prince Edward and Nottoway counties in Virginia, including communities such as Farmville, Blackstone, Crewe, Burkeville, Green Bay, Rice, Prospect and Pamplin.

The deal represents Meridian Waste’s 40th acquisition since Warren Equity Partners took the Company private in 2018. The firm also acquired Sutton Disposal Service LLC of Hannibal, Missouri, in a deal that closed Aug. 1.

“This acquisition is an excellent strategic and geographic fit for Meridian Waste,” Meridian Waste CEO Walter “Wally” Hall says. “Lafferty Disposal adds residential route density in communities surrounding our existing Lunenburg operations while providing us the ability to internalize the collected municipal solid waste volumes into our own Lunenburg Landfill. It is exactly the type of tuck-in acquisition that allows us to leverage our existing people, equipment and infrastructure while providing customers with reliable collection and safe, environmentally responsible disposal.”

Meridian Waste says the ability to internalize the acquired municipal solid waste (MSW) volumes is an important component of its integrated operating model. Rather than relying solely on third-party disposal outlets, the firm can manage the waste stream from collection through final disposal, providing greater operational control, transportation efficiencies and long-term service reliability.

The Lunenburg Landfill offers permitted, professionally managed disposal capacity for the region’s municipal solid waste. Meridian Waste says it is committed to operating its disposal facilities safely and in accordance with applicable environmental regulations while protecting the land, air and water resources of the communities it serves.

“Solid waste management is an essential public service, and responsible disposal infrastructure is critical to keeping Virginia communities clean and healthy,” Hall says. “Our investment in Lunenburg allows us to provide Virginians with a locally based solution for both dependable waste collection and environmentally responsible disposal. We take seriously our responsibility to safely manage the waste entrusted to us today while protecting the environment for future generations.”

The acquisition also creates natural operating efficiencies by integrating the Lafferty Disposal routes into an established Meridian Waste hauling and disposal operation. Meridian Waste says the additional route density, combined with nearby company-owned disposal capacity, supports more efficient operations and strengthens Meridian Waste’s ability to provide consistent, long-term residential collection service throughout the market.

“Forty acquisitions is an important milestone, but our strategy remains focused on finding the right businesses in the right markets,” Hall says. “We look for opportunities that strengthen our existing operations, create meaningful efficiencies and enhance the essential environmental services we provide to our customers and communities. Lafferty Disposal checks each of those boxes.”
