Where to buy Al-Ahli vs. Al-Nassr tickets?

How much are Al-Ahli vs. Al-Nassr tickets?

Official general admission tickets released through primary club portals typically start around SAR 20 to SAR 50 for standard goal-end seating, going up to SAR 150 or more for central premium sections depending on stadium location.
