Cher Sets April 2027 Release for Second Memoir

TheTools · 09.10.2026 00:59 · source · permalink

Singer Cher will continue her life story with Cher: Part Two: The Memoir, scheduled for publication on April 20, 2027. The upcoming book will cover the next stage of her life following the events recounted in the first volume, which traced her childhood, rise to fame and marriage to Sonny Bono. According to a report published by Yahoo on October 8, 2026, the second installment will contain 480 pages and be available in hardcover, paperback, audiobook, ebook and large-print formats.

The book is already available for preorder, although its cover has not yet been revealed. The first volume, Cher: Part One: The Memoir, was released on November 19, 2024, and became a No. 1 New York Times bestseller and a global No. 1 bestseller. Its narrative ends around 1980, leaving the second book to continue the account from that point.

The debut volume explored Cher's early years and career, as well as her complicated relationship with Sonny Bono. Among its revelations were allegations of financial control and infidelity, alongside Cher's account that Bono had considered killing her.

The first memoir also recounted Cher's experience of being pursued by Warren Beatty when she was 15 and her efforts to help Tina Turner navigate an abusive marriage to Ike Turner. The audiobook edition is narrated by Cher herself. The forthcoming volume adds to the first book's 432 pages, extending the two-part account of the singer's life and career. Its release is scheduled for April 20, 2027, with multiple print and digital editions planned.
