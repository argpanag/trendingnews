Kaley Cuoco, 40, Jumps On The Cape Dress Trend With Her Sleekest Pregnancy Look To Date - Women

TheTools · 14.09.2026 22:28 · source · permalink

Kaley Cuoco stepped out looking radiant in a cape dress at the BAFTA TV Tea Party on September 13, 2026. "The Big Bang Theory" star wore a dark gray ELY ELY dress with delicate draping, beautifully showing off her baby bump, and styled the look with Aquazzura heels. Cuoco has undergone a stunning style transformation, and her pregnancy has only amplified her fashion trajectory. There's no denying that Cuoco's maternity style is slick, particularly evidenced by this flattering charcoal cape dress.

The actor announced that she was pregnant with her second child with fiancé Tom Pelphrey in June 2026, also sharing with fans that they were expecting a daughter. "[C]ompleting our little family, what a dream come true! This second time around journey has been a little windier in many ways, but wow we are so grateful for this moment!! Little sissy on the way!!!" Cuoco wrote in the caption on Instagram. Cuoco and Pelphrey welcomed their first daughter, Matilda Carmine Richie Pelphrey, on March 30, 2023, and the fact that Cuoco looked so stunning at the BAFTA TV Tea Party is a testament to her strong sense of fashion. Being both a toddler mom and a 40-year-old experiencing pregnancy again, Cuoco delivered a masterclass on how to rock maternity wear in style.
