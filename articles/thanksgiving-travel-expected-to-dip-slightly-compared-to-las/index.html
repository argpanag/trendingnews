Thanksgiving Travel Expected to Dip Slightly Compared to Last Year – AAA Newsroom

TheTools · 20.11.2026 19:52 · source · permalink

This year's forecast projects 81.5 million Americans will leave town for the holiday

WASHINGTON, DC (November 16, 2026) – AAA projects 81.5 million Americans will travel at least 50 miles from home for Thanksgiving between Tuesday, November 24 and Monday, November 30. This year’s domestic travel forecast is slightly lower than last year when 81.6 million people traveled for the holiday. That’s a 0.1% decline which is about 121, 000 fewer travelers. This year’s forecast ends a three-year streak of record Thanksgiving travel that started in 2023. The number of people taking flights and roads trips is a bit lower than last year, but travelers taking cruises, buses, and trains are on the rise. 

“Thanksgiving represents quality time with family and friends, and for many Americans that requires traveling to see loved ones,” said Stacey Barber, Vice President of AAA Travel. “After three years of record highs, the number of travelers is slightly lower partly because of higher travel costs this year. But travelers won’t notice much of a difference on the roads and at the airports, since projected volumes remain near record levels.” 

Thanksgiving Travelers by Mode of Transportation 

By Car: AAA projects 72.9 million people will travel by car for the Thanksgiving holiday, a slight drop from last year when 73.1 million travelers took road trips. This year’s projected decrease of 200,000 travelers is due in part to this season’s record high gas prices. The months of August, September, and October set new monthly records at the pump, and November could also do the same. The national average on Thanksgiving Day has never been above $4 per gallon. The highest Thanksgiving at the pump was in 2022 at $3.58. 

For most holiday travelers, driving remains the cheapest and easiest way to get around. Nearly 90% of Thanksgiving travelers are expected to hit the road, and AAA reminds drivers to check their vehicles’ tires, batteries, and fluids before leaving. Last Thanksgiving, AAA responded to more than half a million emergency roadside calls for flat tires, dead batteries, fuel delivery, lockout assistance, and towing services. 

For travelers renting cars, AAA car rental partner Hertz expects Tuesday and Wednesday before Thanksgiving to be the busiest pickup days. Hertz says Orlando, Phoenix, Las Vegas, Atlanta, and Denver are the cities with the highest demand based on advanced bookings. Those are popular holiday destinations where travelers frequently rent cars and drive to nearby attractions and national parks. 

By Air: AAA projects 5.96 million travelers will take domestic flights for Thanksgiving, a 0.2% decrease over last year. Air travelers make up more than 7% of the share of holiday travelers, like they did over Thanksgiving 2025. The main difference this year is how much more travelers are paying. According to AAA data, which is based on what travelers paid when they booked their Thanksgiving trips, roundtrip domestic flights are 17% more expensive, averaging $800 per ticket.  

By Other Modes: The category continuing to see growth includes travelers who are taking other modes of transportation. AAA projects 2.6 million Americans will take buses, trains, and cruises, a 4.1% increase from last year. Many travelers make a longer vacation out of the Thanksgiving break and go on cruises. AAA’s new 2027 Cruise Forecast highlights the record number of Americans cruising because of its value and variety.  

AAA’s Thanksgiving Weekend Destinations 

Florida tops the list of domestic destinations for AAA members who booked their trips through AAA Travel. Theme parks and cruise vacations are the main reasons why the Sunshine State is so popular this time of year. Detroit and Dallas are also in the Top 10 as the cities are hosting Thanksgiving Day football games. On the international side, river cruises and holiday markets are the main reasons why travelers are booking trips to Hungary, Switzerland, and the Netherlands. Thanksgiving travelers seeking warm weather and beach vibes are heading to Cancun and Puerto Vallarta. 

Booking on a Budget  

Travel doesn’t have to be expensive, but finding deals does require patience and flexibility. AAA travel advisors offer these tips for travelers booking on a budget:  

  • Book Backwards: When booking a trip, it’s normal to start with a specific destination in mind. But if the experience of travel is your priority and the location is flexible, AAA travel advisors recommend looking for the best airfare or promotional offers and building the trip around that value. 
  • Secondary Airports: Instead of departing from the airport closest to your house, consider other airports that may be a bit farther from home but could deliver meaningful savings. 
  • Shoulder Season: Traveling outside of peak season is an economical way to experience destinations with milder weather, fewer crowds, and lower prices.  
  • Avoid Weekends: This is where flexibility comes in! Look for flights that depart and return on Tuesdays and Wednesdays which tend to be cheaper than Friday-Sunday. 
  • Pack Lunch: Whether you’re flying or driving, bringing your own food will save you money. Just make sure you’re packing TSA-approved items!  
  • Save at the Pump: Sign up for gas station loyalty programs. AAA Members save per gallon when filling up at Shell and Love’s stations.  
  • Seek Savings: From discounted entertainment tickets to Dining Deals, AAA Members get move value when they take advantage of membership perks. 
  • Talk to a Human: In this age of AI, it’s nice to know you can speak directly with a AAA travel advisor who can guide you when deciding where to visit and how much to spend. From trip packages to travel insurance, travel advisors provide human connection and peace of mind. 

Best/Worst Times to Drive and Peak Congestion by Metro 

INRIX, a provider of transportation data and insights, expects that the busiest days on the road over the Thanksgiving holiday period will be Tuesday and Wednesday as most travelers head out, and Sunday and Monday when most people return home. Major metro areas like New York, Washington, DC, Los Angeles, and Boston could see more than double their normal traffic congestion. The worst times for traffic will be in the afternoon and early evening. Drivers should consider hitting the road in the morning to avoid the heaviest congestion.

Thanksgiving Holiday Travel Period  

AAA’s Thanksgiving holiday travel period spans seven days from Tuesday, November 24 to Monday, November 30, 2026. The Thanksgiving travel window was expanded in 2024 to include travelers departing on Tuesday and returning on Monday. Historical results were calculated dating back to 2019 using the same seven-day period.  

Holiday Travel Forecast Methodology  

In cooperation with AAA, S&P Global Market Intelligence (SPGMI), a world leader in critical information, analytics, and expertise, developed a unique methodology to forecast actual domestic travel volumes. The economic variables used to forecast travel for the current holiday are leveraged from SPGMI’s proprietary databases. These data include macroeconomic drivers such as employment, output, household net worth, asset prices including stock indices, interest rates, housing market indicators, and variables related to travel and tourism, including prices of gasoline, airline travel, and hotel stays. AAA and SPGMI have quantified holiday travel volumes going back to 2000. 

Historical travel volume estimates come from DK SHIFFLET’s TRAVEL PERFORMANCE/MonitorSM. The PERFORMANCE/MonitorSM is a comprehensive study measuring the travel behavior of U.S. residents. DK SHIFFLET contacts over 50,000 U.S. households each month to obtain detailed travel data, resulting in the unique ability to estimate visitor volume and spending, identify trends, forecast U.S. travel behavior, and position brands, all after the trips have been taken.   

The travel forecast is reported in person-trips. In particular, AAA and SPGMI forecast the total U.S. holiday travel volume and expected mode of transportation. The travel forecast presented in this report was prepared the week of October 12, 2026. 

Because AAA holiday forecasts focus on domestic leisure travel only, comparisons to TSA passenger screening numbers should not be made. TSA data includes all passengers traveling on both domestic and international routes, whether traveling for work or leisure. Additionally, TSA screens passengers each time they enter secured areas of the airport, therefore each one-way trip is counted as a passenger tally. AAA focuses on person-trips which include the full round-trip travel itinerary. As a result, AAA forecast volumes and daily TSA screenings represent different factors. 

INRIX Methodology 

INRIX blends statistically grounded models with real-world road behavior to forecast travel conditions on the most heavily traveled routes across the U.S. during peak holiday periods. INRIX’s holiday traffic forecast combines pre-selected high-impact travel routes, rich multi-source traffic data, and a statistically robust linear regression model to generate detailed, time-specific travel delay predictions. INRIX’s data sources include a wide array of vehicle-based and infrastructure-based inputs. These include connected vehicle GPS data, mobile apps and navigation services, roadside sensors and cameras, and commercial fleet telematics. With expansive coverage, high temporal resolution, and historical context, this forecasting process provides essential insights for travelers, media, and agencies alike.
