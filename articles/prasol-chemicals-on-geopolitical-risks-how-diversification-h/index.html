Prasol Chemicals On Geopolitical Risks: How Diversification Helps Manage Rising Costs And Volatility

Prasol Chemicals is navigating geopolitical disruptions and rising logistics and raw material costs through a diversified business model. Rahul of Prasol Chemicals explains how the company’s presence across products, customers and geographies helps reduce the impact of global volatility. Around 25–30% of Prasol’s sales come from exports spanning 69 countries. The company says it has remained resilient through disruptions including the Russia-Ukraine war, Strait of Hormuz concerns and COVID-era challenges. Prasol also works closely with customers to manage raw material price changes and gradually pass on cost increases. Transparency, planning and diversification remain central to its strategy.
