Denmark vs Bulgaria Davis Cup 2026 World Group 1: Holger Rune, Grigor Dimitrov In Action—Schedule, Time And Live Streaming

TheTools · 18.09.2026 05:44 · source · permalink

Denmark and Bulgaria meet in the Davis Cup World Group I on September 18 and 19, with plenty of attention on two of the biggest names in the tie, Holger Rune and Grigor Dimitrov.

Fans will finally get to see Rune returning to competitive tennis after an 11-month injury layoff. Dimitrov, meanwhile, is back in the Bulgarian Davis Cup team for the first time since 2015.

The tie will be played over two days, with the first nation to win three of the five matches securing the victory.

The winner will move into the Davis Cup Qualifiers 2027, putting them one step closer to the Davis Cup Finals.

The losing team will instead have to play a World Group I play-off in February 2027 to retain its place at that level.

Denmark vs Bulgaria Davis Cup 2026: When And Where?

The Davis Cup World Group I tie will be played at the Royal Stage in Hillerod, Denmark, on Friday, September 18, and Saturday, September 19.

Friday’s action begins at 4:00 PM local time, with the opening two singles matches. Saturday starts at 12:00 PM local time with the doubles rubber, followed by the two reverse singles.

For viewers in India, Friday’s action starts at 7:30 PM IST, while Saturday’s play begins at 3:30 PM IST.

Denmark vs Bulgaria Davis Cup 2026 World Group I: Full Match Schedule

Friday, September 18

  • Match 1: Elmer Moller vs Grigor Dimitrov
  • Match 2: Holger Rune vs Iliyan Radulov

Saturday, September 19

  • Match 3 — Doubles: August Holmgren/Carl Emil Overbeck vs Pyotr Nesterov/Alexander Donski
  • Match 4: Holger Rune vs Grigor Dimitrov
  • Match 5: Elmer Moller vs Iliyan Radulov, if required

The captains can still make changes to the nominated line-ups before Saturday’s matches.

Denmark vs Bulgaria: Davis Cup Squads

  • Denmark: Holger Rune, Elmer Moller, August Holmgren, Carl Emil Overbeck, Johannes Ingildsen
  • Bulgaria: Grigor Dimitrov, Alexander Donski, Pyotr Nesterov, Iliyan Radulov, Alexander Vasilev
  • Denmark captain: Frederik Lochte Nielsen
  • Bulgaria captain: Valentin Dimov
