How to buy Barcelona Champions League tickets

You can buy official Barcelona Champions League tickets directly through the FC Barcelona Ticketing Portal (site or Barça Tickets app).

Tickets go on sale first to club members (socios), followed by premium members (Culé Premium), and lastly to the general public online

The club's official seat-release program for season-ticket holders who cannot attend; these pop up on the official site usually 48 to 72 hours before kick-off.

Official tickets for Champions League fixtures typically go on sale 1-4 weeks before the match date, depending on the tier of the opponent and confirmation of the fixture schedule.

While the official club portal is the safest way for supporters to purchase Barcelona tickets, those looking to attend a Champions League match may wish to consider secondary resale sites such as StubHub and Ticombo, which could give them the best opportunity of obtaining tickets.

How much are Barcelona Champions League tickets?

Official Barcelona Champions League tickets direct from the club typically cost between €60-€250+. However, exact prices depend heavily on the opponent's profile and will unsurprisingly will increase the further the team progresses in the competition.

Ticket Prices by Competition Stage

  • League Phase / Group Stage: €60-€150 for standard matchups. Marquee matches (such as hosting Manchester City) see prices jump from €120-€250+.
  • Knockout Rounds: €100-€400 depending on the tier of the opposition.
  • Quarter-Finals & Beyond: €200-€800+ for premium sideline areas.

Keep tabs on the official Barcelona site for additional information and on secondary selling sites such as StubHub and Ticombo for current ticket availability.
