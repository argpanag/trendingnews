Kansas Democrats hope Adam Hamilton can harness Trump fatigue to oust Republican Sen. Roger Marshall

TheTools · 04.11.2026 02:01 · source · permalink

TOPEKA, Kan. (AP) — Democrats in Kansas went into Tuesday’s election hoping that dissatisfaction with President Donald Trump, combined with the appeal of megachurch pastor Adam Hamilton’s well-funded campaign to middle-of-the-road voters, would oust Republican Sen. Roger Marshall and end a 94-year streak of losing Senate races.
