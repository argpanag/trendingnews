Conroy calls for cultural change in ADF - ABC listen

TheTools · 15.10.2026 20:10 · source · permalink

There are widespread calls for cultural change within the Australian Defence Force, after a recording device was found in the women's quarters of a Navy vessel on Sunday.

 It was discovered on HMAS Leeuwin, docked at Cairns. An ADF member has been identified as a person of interest and Australia's Navy Chief - Vice Admiral Matt Buckley - has also ordered checks "across the Fleet".

 There'll also be new independent inquiry into Military Sexual Violence later this year.

Minister for Defence Industry,Pat Conroy.

Rachel Mealey: There are widespread calls for cultural change within the Australian Defence Force after a recording device was found in the women's quarters of a Navy vessel on Sunday. It was discovered on HMAS Leeuwin docked at Cairns. An ADF member has been identified as a person of interest and Australia's Navy Chief Vice Admiral Matt Buckley has also ordered checks across the fleet. There'll also be a new independent inquiry into military sexual violence later this year. Pat Conroy is the Minister for Defence Industry and says it reflects a cultural issue.


Pat Conroy: As we see more and more female Australians serving the ADF, I think it's one in five ADF personnel are women, they need to be treated with respect and dignity like all service personnel and the culture has to change to accommodate that. It was obviously a key finding into the Royal Commission into veteran suicide and Minister Keogh is launching a women's veteran strategy today that obviously deals with other issues associated with women's service. So this is a cultural challenge that the ADF leadership and the political leadership is up for and we'll continue to make sure that cultural change is occurring.
