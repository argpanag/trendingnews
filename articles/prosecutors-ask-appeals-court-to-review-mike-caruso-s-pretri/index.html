WEST PALM BEACH, Fla. (CBS12) — The Office of Statewide Prosecution is asking Florida’s 6th District Court of Appeal to review an Orange County judge’s decision denying pretrial detention for Palm Beach County clerk candidate Mike Caruso.

Court records show prosecutors filed a petition for writ of certiorari on Sept. 28, asking the appeals court to review the ruling that allowed Caruso’s release under bond conditions.

The petition follows a motion for rehearing filed by prosecutors after the judge found the State had not established a “substantial probability” that Caruso committed the qualifying dangerous crimes required for pretrial detention.

Prosecutors argued the judge applied too high an evidentiary standard, effectively requiring proof beyond a reasonable doubt rather than the lower standard used at a pretrial-detention hearing. The State asked the judge to reconsider and order Caruso held without bond on kidnapping and lewd or lascivious molestation counts.

Caruso’s attorneys opposed the request, arguing the judge properly weighed the evidence, credibility disputes and investigative concerns before finding the State had not met its burden. The defense also argued the court independently found that strict release conditions could reasonably protect the public and ensure Caruso appears in court.

According to the defense filing, those conditions include a substantial monetary bond, no direct or indirect contact with the alleged child victim or witnesses, surrender of Caruso’s passport, restrictions on returning to Orange County and a firearms prohibition.

The appeals court has not ruled on the merits of the State’s petition.

On Sept. 30, the appeals court ordered prosecutors to correct a confidentiality notice connected to their filing, saying it did not clearly identify the confidential information or specify whether the State was seeking to restrict access to the entire case file.

This court challenge comes after Gov. Ron DeSantis criticized the Palm Beach County Republican Party for endorsing Caruso in a voter guide.

CBS12 previously reported that the party posted a voter guide on social media Monday that included Caruso’s name, according to the Miami Herald. Caruso is suspended from his position as Palm Beach County clerk.

In August, Caruso, 67, was arrested on multiple felony charges involving allegations of sexual abuse of a child under 12. He has not been convicted.
