CM Punk Picks Sides in Tiffany Stratton-Charlotte Flair Beef? » TWNP-Wrestling News

TheTools · 11.11.2026 18:27 · source · permalink

It’s hard to be the father of the women’s locker room, but CM Punk bears that responsibility, claiming Cora Jade and Roxanne Perez as his wrestling daughters. As AJ Lee’s husband, many female Superstars see The Second City Saint as a safe space, such as Jade Cargill and Bayley. In Saudi Arabia, Tiffany Stratton defeated Crown Jewel Champion Chelsea Green to win the WWE Women’s Title. However, she did it via a Money in the Bank contract cash-in, which prompted Charlotte Flair to react with a viral meme. As long-term issues between Charlotte and Tiffy brew, CM Punk picks sides on social media.

CM Punk Picks Sides with Charlotte Flair After Tiffany Stratton Win

Many WWE women’s wrestlers have labeled CM Punk as “babygirl coded.” So, it isn’t beyond the 8-time WWE Champion to weigh in on women’s locker room drama.

Seemingly, CM Punk picks sides as he shares a pic of graffiti on a gym wall:

The now-expired Instagram story featured a wall inscribed with the name “Charlotte.” If anyone was wondering whether the message was for the 14-time Women’s Champion, CM Punk tagged @charlottewwe in the story. 

What made the post even more interesting was the song chosen to accompany Charlotte’s name. CM Punk played “Cashing In” by Minor Threat. It’s obvious that CM Punk was referencing the viral “via cash-in” meme that Charlotte Flair shared in response to Tiffany Stratton’s second Money in the Bank Title victory.

Is CM Punk a Hypocrite for Making Fun of Tiffany Stratton?

While CM Punk is an 8-time WWE World Champion, his first two WWE World Title victories followed subsequent Money in the Bank cash-ins. When Charlotte shared her “via cash-in” meme, Ric Flair’s daughter was fanning the flames of her rivalry with Tiffany Stratton. 

The last time Charlotte said “via cash-in” was to downplay Tiffy’s only World Title reign in 2025. In 2026, Tiffy is a 2-time Champion, both via cash-ins. So, when CM Punk picks sides with Charlotte Flair, some internet wrestling fans were quick to point out the hypocrisy.

Others say that wrestling fans are reading too much into things. CM Punk is good friends with Charlotte Flair. He’s even come out wearing her robes during House Show Main Events. In the past, Charlotte shared a pic of herself with Punker and Alexa Bliss, noting that CM Punk was the best,

Whether or not Charlotte Flair is instigating things with Tiffany Stratton, she’s enjoying the viralness of her meme. Charlotte has been active on social media, sharing fan-made “via cash-in” memes. So, perhaps CM Punk is just showing support to his friend and helping blur the lines for a future storyline between Tiffany Stratton and Charlotte Flair?
