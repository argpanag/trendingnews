  4. 9.00pm

    Van Gerwen survives Coates 5-4

    Michael van Gerwen takes the Group L points 5-4 after Henry Coates, 20 and without a Tour Card, took him all the way.

    Van Gerwen broke the opener with 68 (treble 20, 4, double 2) after they traded 180s, then held on double 18. Coates hit back with back-to-back 180s and a hold on double 10, then broke with 121 (treble 20, treble 11, double 14) to level at 2-2. He led 3-2, and then 3-4 after a 156 (treble 20, treble 20, double 18).

    Van Gerwen found tops to make it 4-4. In the last, Coates had the throw and six darts from 181, set 54, and missed two match darts; van Gerwen had left 56 with a 145 and took out tops.

    Jeffrey de Graaf still to play in Group L.

About this match

Michael van Gerwen plays Henry Coates in Group L of the Grand Slam of Darts 2026 on Saturday 14 November, in the evening session at 8.15pm (est.) UK time. It is the best of 9 legs. The match is at WV Active Aldersley, Wolverhampton, live on Sky Sports and NOW.

Only the session start is fixed. Players go on in order, so this match could begin earlier or later than the estimate.
