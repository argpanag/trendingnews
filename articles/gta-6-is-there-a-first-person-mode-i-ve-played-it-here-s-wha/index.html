GTA 6: Is there a first-person mode? I've played it - here's what you need to know

TheTools · 18.11.2026 13:46 · source · permalink

An official screenshot of Jason Duval in GTA 6

Does GTA 6 have a first-person mode? Well, the answer to that is - sort of. I've been hands-on with GTA 6 for 65 hours so far, reviewing it for The Independent.

Unlike GTA 5, GTA Online and Red Dead Redemption 2, there is not a dedicated first-person mode, where you can toggle between first and third-person perspectives at the touch of a button.

Instead, at launch, there is only a first-person perspective in certain situations.

If a certain weapon is used, or a scope for a weapon is bought, a first-person perspective can be toggled so you aim down the sights like you would do in a first-person shooter.

Also, usually when sat or stationary, there is the option to 'inspect', where you can see something from a first-person perspective. This can happen in missions when the character you're controlling is in a vehicle, or when you're sat on the sofa with your partner.

GTA 6 does have a first-person perspective for some weapons and scopes, turning it into a first-person shooter in some cases Rockstar Games

GTA 6 is Rockstar Games' latest entry into the world renowned Grand Theft Auto series. It's set in the fictional state of Leonida, a take on Florida, with Vice City (Miami) at its beating heart.

The game follows the story of Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos as they end up in the middle of a huge criminal conspiracy after what seems to be a simple score gets scuppered.

At times, they are forced to survive before they have any chance of thriving in this parody of The Sunshine State.
