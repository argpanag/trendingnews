BBC's near-perfect returning period drama unveils first look at Ruth Jones in 2026 Christmas special

TheTools · 29.10.2026 17:01 · source · permalink

The Other Bennet Sister will return later this year.

This Christmas will see the highly-anticipated return of one of the biggest TV hits of the year, with three brand-new episodes of The Other Bennet Sister set to arrive on the BBC.

Now we've got a further glimpse of what to expect, with newly-released pictures revealed – including the first reveal of Ruth Jones, who plays Mrs Bennet, in the returning three-part special.

Also returning will be Tanya Reynolds (Ted Lasso, Sex Education) as Caroline Bingley, Laurie Davidson (The Girlfriend) as Mr Ryder, Poppy Gilbert (My Oxford Year) as Lizzie Darcy, Grace Hogg-Robinson (Miss Scarlet) as Lydia Wickham, Molly Wright (The A Word, Our Girl) as Kitty Buncock, and Maddie Close as Jane Bingley.

The new photos of The Other Bennet Sister also reveal new cast member Nerys Amber Stocks (Out There) who joins for the festive special and will play the role of the Haywards' housekeeper Lucy.

Based on Janice Hadlow's acclaimed novel, The Other Bennet Sister's three-part Christmas series has been written by screenwriter Sarah Quintrell, who also penned nine out of the 10 episodes of the BBC series.

The Austen-inspired show centres on Mary Bennet, the often-overlooked middle sister from Pride and Prejudice, while she navigates all the pressures of society in Regency England.

What to Read Next

It became a breakout hit for the BBC when it premiered early this year, with the series becoming the biggest new drama across all platforms and streamers in the UK since March 2025, with 6.5 million watching in its first 28 days. According to the BBC, The Other Bennet Sister has continued to build in popularity, with episode one now up to 9.5 million viewers (7.3 million over 28 days).

Related: BBC's The Other Bennet Sister mercifully avoids a 'glow up'

The Other Bennet Sister was well-received by critics too, and currently sits with a near-perfect 98% score on Rotten Tomatoes.

Seattle Times branded the show "pure pleasure", whereas Mashable named it "a sweet spring TV treat".

"The Other Bennet Sister is so breezy and full of heart, most viewers will fall for it right away," read Slate's review, while Decider said: "Mary is a modern heroine who may not be as witty as her more famous sister, but her flaws and stumbles do make her a touch more relatable and her story just as enduring."

"With its refreshingly grounded tone, generous sense of empathy and, above all, absolutely marvelous lead, the series pulls off the neat trick of convincingly expanding on Jane Austen while standing proudly on its own two feet," said Hollywood Reporter.

"We always hoped audiences would fall in love with Mary Bennet's story, but the response to The Other Bennet Sister has exceeded even our most optimistic expectations," executive producer Jane Tranter previously said, when the festive return was first announced.

"These Christmas specials give us the chance to spend a little more time with characters audiences have taken to their hearts, and to explore what happens after the wedding bells fade and real life begins."

The Other Bennet Sister is available on BBC iPlayer now. The Christmas specials will follow later this year.
