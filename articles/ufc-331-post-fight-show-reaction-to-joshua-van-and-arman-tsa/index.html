UFC 331 post-fight show: Reaction to Joshua Van and Arman Tsarukyan’s big wins, Gable Steveson’s brutal knockout loss

TheTools · 20.09.2026 06:10 · source · permalink
  • UFC

UFC 331 had it all

UFC 331 delivered one of the craziest cards of the year, and in the main event, Joshua Van and Alexandre Pantoja put on a classic, but it was Van that emerged as the better man with an impressive fifth round to close the show and retain the flyweight title. Arman Tsarukyan also earned himself a shot at UFC gold with a brutal knockout win. Will Tsarukyan finally get his day in court?

MMA Fighting’s Mike Heck and Jed Meshew react to UFC 331, Van’s sensational performance, Tsarukyan’s cold-blooded knockout, and what’s next. Additionally, topics include Sean Sharaf knocking Gable Steveson unconscious to pull of the biggest upset in UFC history, Patricio Pitbull’s standout performance, Marlon Vera’s throwback outing in his stoppage of Charles Jourdain, and much more.
