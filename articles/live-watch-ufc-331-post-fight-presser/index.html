LIVE! Watch UFC 331 Post-Fight Presser

TheTools · 20.09.2026 03:15 · source · permalink
  • UFC 331

With the action officially in the books from UFC 331 last night (Sat., Sept. 19, 2026) LIVE on Paramount+ from inside Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, Calif., fight fans can check out the post-fight presser for all the best reaction and official bonus winners.

On a night that featured a long-awaited main event rematch between UFC flyweight champion Joshua Van and former titleholder Alexandre Pantoja, a co-headliner featuring a lightweight scrap between top contenders Arman Tsarukyan and Mauricio Ruffy, and the second Octagon appearance from blue chip heavyweight prospect Gable Steveson, there will be a lot to discuss when the fighters take to the stage.

The post-fight presser is scheduled to go LIVE in the above video player around 12:15 a.m. ET (Sunday). It will feature the biggest winners and losers from the card, as well as UFC CEO, Dana White, if we’re lucky.
