Decreasing clouds, with a beautiful and more comfortable end to the week

TheTools · 09.09.2026 22:35 · source · permalink
  • Partly to mostly cloudy tonight, a bit cooler
  • Thursday and Friday will be delightful
  • Showers and storms return this weekend

ROCKFORD, Ill. (Rockford News First) - Some of us saw decent rainfall amounts late last night through early this morning. Higher amounts were recorded west of Rockford and closer to the Mississippi River. Due to the on and off showers and cloud cover through the middle of the day, temperatures struggled to reach the 80° mark in many spots across the stateline.

The cold front responsible for the wet weather, will push through northern Illinois this evening, which will partially clear out our skies overnight. As winds shift to a northwesterly direction, expect humidity levels to drop off quickly tonight with temperatures settling into the upper 50s to low 60s by early Thursday morning.

What’s Coming Up...

Clouds will gradually decrease from northwest to southeast throughout the day tomorrow, as high pressure settles across the area. This will bring a string of beautiful days across the stateline with lots of sunshine and comfortable humidity levels. We’ll also have temperatures cooling off into the low 50s by Friday morning, with seasonable daytime highs in the upper 70s. This will set us up for a delightful end to the week.

A warm southerly wind will return late Friday and into Saturday, which will give our humidity values a boost through early Sunday, with the potential for a few morning showers and rumbles of thunder.

Looking Ahead...

Another cold front will approach the area early next week, with scattered showers and storms returning to the area by Monday. We’ll see a slight cool down in the wake of Monday’s cold front, along with more comfortable conditions with highs in the upper 70s and lows in the upper 50s.
