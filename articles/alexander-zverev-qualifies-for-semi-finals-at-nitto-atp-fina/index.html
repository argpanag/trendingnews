Alexander Zverev Qualifies For Semi-Finals At Nitto ATP Finals With Victory Over Shelton - UBITENNIS

TheTools · 17.11.2026 22:02 · source · permalink

Alexander Zverev is into the semi-finals at the Nitto ATP Finals.

Alexander Zverev has qualified for the semi-finals at the Nitto ATP Finals with a 6-2 7-6(4) win over Ben Shelton.

The German is into the last four in Turin after a professional straight sets win over Shelton as he extended his winning streak over the American to seven matches.

It was a decent second set from Shelton as he pushed Zverev all the way but he couldn’t break down the world number one.

Zverev will face Arthur Fils in his last round robin match while Shelton will face Rafael Jodar.

It was a quick start from Zverev who broke Shelton in the opening game and created several break points to gain a quick double break lead.

In what was an eight minute game, Shelton found some first serves to hold serve but continued to struggle once the rally got going.

This was evident as Zverev did eventually secure a double break lead before quickly wrapping up the opening set in comfortable circumstances.

The American needed to find a solution quick to Zverev’s crushing forehands as the German was bullying Shelton from the back of the court.

There was a much smoother second set from Shelton as he found his first serve and was proactive from inside the baseline but the American only won one point on Zverev’s serve before the tiebreak.

As the conclusion of the set loomed Zverev sensed an opportunity to wrap up this match quickly and had two break points in the ninth game.

However, a fired up Shelton saved the latest wave of attack and the second set eventually went to a tiebreak with a string of momentum changes happening in the tiebreak.

In the end an incredible Zverev forehand passing shot winner sealed the tiebreak 7-4 as the world number one moved into the semi-finals.

After the match Zverev wasn’t aware of the qualification scenario that he had to win in straight sets to qualify for the semi-finals, “I was not aware of the [qualification scenario],” Zverev explained to the ATP website.

“If I focus on myself and play good tennis, the result will take care of itself in a way… It’s about playing good tennis. I feel like I did that today. I did that against Rafa [Jodar] two days ago and that’s the outcome of it.”
