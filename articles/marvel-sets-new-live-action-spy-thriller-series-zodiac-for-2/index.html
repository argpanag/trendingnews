Marvel Sets New Live-Action Spy Thriller Series ‘Zodiac’ For 2028

TheTools · 08.10.2026 20:55 · source · permalink

Marvel Television announced a new live-action television series titled Zodiac.

The series, revealed at New York Comic-Con on Thursday, is a spy thriller and is set for release in 2028, per the studio.

Zodiac will join a waning live-action TV slate at Marvel, where many of the franchise’s most popular titles have either already ended or soon will. The WandaVision trilogy is coming to its conclusion later this month with the release of VisionQuest.

The Vision spinoff, headlined by Paul Bettany, is said to take place after the events in WandaVision where the character made a return after Thanos killed him in 2018’s Avengers: Infinity War. The new series is believed to follow Vision (Bettany) as he tries to regain his memory and humanity.

Daredevil: Born Again, which welcomed several beloved characters back into the MCU including Charlie Cox as Matt Murdock and Krysten Ritter as Jessica Jones, will end with Season 3 next year.
