Ilia Topuria shares video of Usman Nurmagomedov attacking Merab Dvalishvili during RAF interview

TheTools · 11.11.2026 17:39 · source · permalink
  • UFC

Watch footage of Merab Dvalishvili being attacked in Abu Dhabi

Ilia Topuria has released footage of Merab Dvalishvili being ambushed ahead of his upcoming RAF Abu Dhabi matchup.

On Wednesday, Dvalishvili posted a video to his social media in which he claimed he was the victim of a sneak attack by a group comprised of former PFL lightweight champion Usman Nurmagomedov, longtime rival Umar Nurmagomedov, and their associates. According to Dvalishvili, he and a friend were jumped by “20 guys” and the attack left them both visibly bloodied and bruised.

Now, Dvalishvili’s friend and fellow former UFC champion Ilia Topuria has shared video of the alleged encounter.

In the chaotic 37-second clip, Dvalishvili is shown rehearsing for an RAF promo when a group of people approaches him from behind. A taller man in a black shirt and hat, possibly Usman Nurmagomedov, gets Dvalishvili’s attention and then proceeds to strike him in the face.

There’s a quick cut and then Dvalishvili and his friend can be seen brawling with various members of the opposing group, with a figure that appears to be Dvalishvili even being lifted and thrown to the ground at one point. The parties eventually separate, though it’s unclear how long the altercation actually lasted.
