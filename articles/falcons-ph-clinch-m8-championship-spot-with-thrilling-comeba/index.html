Falcons PH Clinch M8 Championship Spot with Thrilling Comeback in MPL Playoffs

TheTools · 23.10.2026 21:38 · source · permalink
  • Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB)
  • Team Falcons PH
  • Mobile Legends MPL S18

MPL | Facebook

Team Falcons PH secured their ticket to the Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB) World Championship M8 after outlasting Aurora PH in the upper bracket finals of the MPL Philippines Season 18 playoffs at the Philippine Sports Arena on 23 October.

The Falcons pulled off a remarkable comeback in Game 4, overturning a 16-11 kill deficit and a nearly 9,000-gold disadvantage against the gritty Aurora squad to keep their championship hopes alive and force a deciding fifth game.

With momentum on their side, the Falcons proved why they finished as the regular season’s No. 2 seed, dominating the final game to send Aurora to the lower bracket finals and secure their place on the M-series stage.

Aurora, the defending M-series champion, must now win the lower bracket finals against top-seeded Team Liquid PH to keep its hopes of a back-to-back world title alive.

Falcons’ Flap, Kyle, Owgwen, Super Marco and newly added Hadji will represent the team on the biggest stage of the MLBB scene. All five have previously experienced the M-series, while Flap stands out as a two-time champion alongside former teammates KarlTzy, now with Team Liquid PH, Pheww, now a coach for ONIC, and Edward.
