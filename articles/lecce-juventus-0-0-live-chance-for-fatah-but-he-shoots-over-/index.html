Follow Lecce v Juventus, a match in the eighth round of Serie A

Lecce-Juventus already has a long injury list, with three points crucial to both teams. The penultimate match of the eighth round of the 2026/2027 Serie A season kicks off at 6pm at the Via del Mare in the Salento capital.

In three days, Eusebio Di Francesco's side have lost three first-choice players: Coulibaly, Ilici and Pierotti. They must maintain their good form. A win would take Lecce above Luciano Spalletti's Juventus, with the manager "in the hot seat", as managing director Carnevali put it. Juventus have gone two league matches without a win, have also lost Nico Gonzalez and Celik to injury, and arrive after a controversial Europa League draw against Rennes.

    25' - Lecce break well, moving the ball from right to left to Fatah at the corner of the penalty area. The Swede cuts inside onto his right foot, beats Kalulu and curls a shot, but sends it well over Vicario's goal.


    5' - Woltemade plays Kolo Muani through. He reaches the byline and crosses for the German centre-forward, who mishits the ball and cannot get a shot away.


    1' Chance - Vicario takes an enormous risk, waiting far too long to clear with his feet. When he finally does, N'Dri slides in to intercept, but the ball goes behind to the Juventus goalkeeper's relief.
