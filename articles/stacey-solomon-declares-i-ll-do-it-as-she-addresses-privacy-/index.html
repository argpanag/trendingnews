Stacey Solomon declares 'I'll do it' as she addresses privacy after moving home

TheTools · 03.11.2026 07:47 · source · permalink

Stacey Solomon has vowed that she'll protect her family when it comes to their privacy as she spoke about her concerns ahead of opening her home to the cameras once again.

The presenter and her husband Joe Swash first appeared in their own fly-on-the-wall reality series back in 2025, with a much-anticipated third series set to air on BBC One from next week. The show documents the couple and their family during day-to-day life at their home, Pickle Cottage, in Essex.

The famous couple moved into the Tudor-style Essex property in 2021, with Stacey documenting the renovations in her book, Tap To Tidy Pickle Cottage. She kept her millions of Instagram followers updated on any changes to her family's home along the way.

However, in early September, Stacey and Joe announced they had sold what they described as their "dream home" and confirmed soon after that they had already left Pickle Cottage, having bought a new home "further out" where they said they intended to grow their own vegetables, with Stacey's current vegetable patch on the land belonging to a neighbouring farmer, and have space to rescue more animals.

But as Stacey started to take fans on her family's new home journey, she admitted: "I’m not going to show the outside of the house this time around. Just for security and privacy reasons… we had some issues at Pickle Cottage in the end & it’s safer to just not show the building itself..."

Now, Stacey has opened up about her feelings towards the press attention into her and Joe's life together, having previously speaking on social media about being subject to a "pile-on". In an interview with the Press Association, the TV star said: “I think for a long time in celebrity culture, there was this expectation that you put yourself in the public eye so you deserve this treatment.

"There is an unwritten understanding. If me and Joe go out in the public and there happens to be somebody there and they get a picture of us, that’s fair game. That’s part and parcel of this industry. We do draw the line though."

But the Sort Your Life Out host, who is also a social media influencer, said the increased violations of her privacy has impacted what she posts online.

"Maybe it will make us think of things differently moving forward," she said. "Hopefully, I’ll be able to stick up for myself enough for them to stop being inappropriate. But if I need to do something to stop this happening, then I’ll do it. The safety of my family comes before absolutely everything."

The new series of Stacey And Joe will return to BBC One on November 10 at 8pm, and all episodes will be available on BBC iPlayer.
