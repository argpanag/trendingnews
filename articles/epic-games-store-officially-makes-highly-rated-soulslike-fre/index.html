Epic Games Store Officially Makes Highly Rated Soulslike Free for a Limited Time

TheTools · 19.11.2026 15:59 · source · permalink

100% off

Grand Theft Auto 6 is finally out today around the globe. Unfortunately, it's only available on console at the moment, meaning plenty of gamers will have to wait until it arrives on PC. If you only have a PC and you're looking for something new to play, head over to the Epic Games Store. For a limited time, you can grab a great action-adventure game at no charge.

Mark of the Deep

Mark of the Deep is an isometric pirate-themed action game that blends elements of Soulslikes and Metroidvanias. Expect plenty of challenge as you venture across a cursed island. Playing as a rookie pirate, you'll try to survive hostile environments while searching for the Angry Mermaids, the name of your missing crew. Like Kermit the Frog says — it's not easy bein' green!

Mark of the Deep came out last January and has a couple hundred Mostly Positive player reviews on Steam. "The game is wonderful, I really like the graphic of all scenarios, enemies, [and] cutscenes," one player said. Another compared Mark of the Deep to Death's Door and Tunic, but noted it didn't quite reach the same highs as those indie delights.

If Mark of the Deep catches your eye, all you need to do is log in to the Epic Games Store and claim it. The game will be free until 11 a.m. EST on Thursday, Nov. 26.
