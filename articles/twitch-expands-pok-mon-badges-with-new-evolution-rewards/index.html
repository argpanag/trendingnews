Twitch Expands Pokémon Badges With New Evolution Rewards

TheTools · 16.10.2026 23:46 · source · permalink

Twitch has introduced new Pokémon chat badge evolutions and a limited-time Gastly, Haunter, and Gengar collection, giving viewers additional rewards for watching eligible streams. Announced on October 16, 2026, the update coincides with Pokémon Night Out, a live music event scheduled for Los Angeles and London.

According to Twitch's official blog, viewers can unlock evolved badges by equipping each badge and watching qualifying content for 20 minutes per day over three days. Users must complete the viewing requirement again at each stage to progress through an evolution line.

The available evolution paths include Pichu to Pikachu, Bulbasaur to Ivysaur and Venusaur, Squirtle to Wartortle and Blastoise, and Charmander to Charmeleon and Charizard. Twitch said users do not need additional subscription purchases to unlock these evolutions, but the opportunity ends January 30, 2027.

The separate Gastly, Haunter, and Gengar collection runs from October 16 through October 24, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time. According to Twitch, viewers must purchase or gift a subscription to an eligible channel during this period to qualify for the initial reward.

Prime subscriptions and existing recurring subscriptions do not qualify for the promotion. The first eligible purchase grants a themed Poké Ball containing the Gastly badge reward, which viewers must unlock by watching qualifying content.

To earn Gastly, viewers must watch eligible content for 20 minutes per day over three days. The viewing days do not need to be consecutive, and users who have already made progress can continue completing the requirement until November 23, 2026.

After unlocking Gastly, viewers must equip the badge through the Drops Panel below chat and repeat the viewing requirement to earn Haunter. Equipping Haunter and completing another three days of qualifying viewing unlocks Gengar. The evolution opportunity for this collection also ends January 30, 2027.

Twitch's eligible content categories extend beyond Pokémon games to include Just Chatting, DJs, Art, Special Events, Sports, Music, Talk Shows & Podcasts, Animals, Aquariums, and Zoos, Co-working & Studying, and Makers & Crafting. The promotion applies to eligible live channels, and each user can receive a maximum of one Gastly badge reward through a qualifying purchase.

The new rewards build on Twitch's first Pokémon chat badge release in August 2026, which featured Pichu, Bulbasaur, Squirtle, and Charmander. The company said it plans to release additional Pokémon badges and evolution badges regularly, with many available for limited periods.

Pokémon Night Out, which is associated with the new Gengar badge series, is scheduled for October 24 at the Intuit Dome in Los Angeles and November 10 at The O2 in London. Twitch said the badges also provide creators streaming eligible content with another way to reward audience participation.
