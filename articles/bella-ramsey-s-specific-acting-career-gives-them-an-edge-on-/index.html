Bella Ramsey's specific acting career gives them 'an edge' on Celebrity Traitors (they hope)

TheTools · 02.10.2026 19:10 · source · permalink

Their past roles have prepped them for Missions

The actor Bella Ramsey is counting on their unique career to give them “a bit of an edge” on The Celebrity Traitors 2026.

Before the show was filmed, Bella said: “I’m quite sporty, and I’ve done a lot of stunt work on most jobs that I’ve done. I find that side of things really exciting. I love doing physical things and I love a challenge, especially a physical challenge, so the Missions are one of the things I’m most excited about.”

Bella Ramsey’s most famous roles include Ellie in The Last Of Us (which involves fighting zombies) and Lyanna Mormont in Game of Thrones (which involved fighting ice zombies). Bella Ramsey also had to do quite a few action scenes when they played the character Angelica in the His Dark Materials TV show. Perhaps some of those physical skills will come in handy in the Missions on The Celebrity Traitors season two?

A post shared by The Traitors UK (@thetraitorsukofficial)

They also reckon that “acting skills” can be an advantage on the show. Bella explained: “I do think they can give you a bit of an edge. For me, anyway, because again, if I was telling a lie, people would spot me instantly. Playing a character, it’s like that’s what I do!”

Bella continued: “No matter who I get selected to be, I’ll be playing the character I’m given essentially. I’m not a good liar, I’m a terrible liar, so I can’t do it that way. The best way for me would be treating this just like I’m playing a character, but myself within the character.”

This sounds like a very complex strategy, which probably means it’s a good one.
