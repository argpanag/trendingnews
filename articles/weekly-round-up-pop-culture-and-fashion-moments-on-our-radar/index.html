Weekly Round-Up: Pop Culture and Fashion Moments On Our Radar | Grazia India

TheTools · 11.09.2026 10:57 · source · permalink

Here’s a look back at this week’s notable fashion and pop culture moments.

Jennifer Lawrence Finally Joins Instagram, And @jlaw Is All Hers
Jennifer Lawrence has officially entered the Instagram chat. The actor launched her verified account this week under the handle @jlaw, because, naturally, @jenniferlawrence was already taken. After years of avoiding a public social media presence, Lawrence made her debut with a very on-brand video: funny, slightly suspicious and already setting boundaries. She jokingly warned followers that she would quit if anyone left a mean comment, before referring to herself as “the baby” in her Stories. Honestly, if Instagram has been waiting for a celebrity to make it interesting again, this might be it.

Dior And A24 Team Up, Because Fashion And Film Weren’t Close Enough Already
Dior and A24 have entered their collaboration era, and the pairing feels almost suspiciously perfect. The two-year partnership will centre around New York’s historic Cherry Lane Theatre, which A24 acquired in 2023, and will bring together film, theatre and fashion. The partnership launches with a campaign featuring Dior creative director Jonathan Anderson alongside Sabrina Carpenter, Josh O’Connor and Drew Starkey. Anderson has made it clear that this isn’t about putting a Dior bag in an A24 film. Instead, the idea is to create new ways for fashion and culture to meet. We’re listening.

Harry Styles Extends His Tour Into 2027, Because He’s Not Done Yet
Apparently, Harry Styles has decided that one global tour wasn’t enough. The singer has extended his Together, Together Tour into 2027, adding 25 stadium shows across North America and Europe. The new run kicks off in Phoenix on April 2 before travelling through cities including Los Angeles, Toronto, Chicago, Berlin, Paris, Madrid and Rome, ending with three nights at London’s Wembley Stadium. With Styles currently in the middle of a 30-night Madison Square Garden residency, his calendar is looking considerably fuller than ours. Tickets go on sale later this month, so prepare accordingly.

Maison Margiela Unveils A New Fashion Film Starring Rebecca Hall
Maison Margiela is leaning all the way into mystery with Anonymity, its latest fashion film starring Rebecca Hall. Directed by Thibaut Grevet, the Fall/Winter 2026 film follows an actress who encounters a masked double of herself, blurring the line between reality and performance. It’s part psychological thriller, part fashion fantasy and very Margiela. The film also revisits the house’s long-standing fascination with anonymity, identity and challenging the conventions of fashion. In other words, if you were hoping for a straightforward campaign, Margiela would like you to think again.

Duolingo Brings Fashion’s Hardest Names To New York Fashion Week
Duolingo is bringing its language lessons to New York Fashion Week, but this time, you can wear them. The language-learning app has teamed up with designer Megan O’Cain on The Phonetic Collection, a seven-piece capsule built around the fashion names we routinely butcher. The pieces spell out phonetic pronunciations of labels and designers, turning the likes of Hermès into something you can actually say with confidence. It’s part fashion joke, part public service announcement. Because if we’re going to talk about fashion, we should probably learn how to pronounce it first.

The Apple And Samsung War Continues, And This Time It’s About Foldables
Apple has officially joined the foldable phone game with the launch of the iPhone Duo, and Samsung was not about to let the moment slide. As Apple unveiled its first foldable, Samsung’s social media team was already firing back, posting a series of not-so-subtle jabs at the new phone and reminding everyone that it has been in the foldable game since 2019. From calling the Duo “reheated leftovers” to suggesting it was all a little too familiar, Samsung made its feelings very clear. Apple may have finally entered the foldable chat, but the Samsung rivalry clearly isn’t folding anytime soon.

Former Gucci Designer Sabato De Sarno Joins ICICLE As Creative Director
Sabato De Sarno is heading east. The former Gucci creative director has been appointed creative director of Chinese fashion house ICICLE, where he will oversee womenswear, menswear, accessories and eyewear. His first collection for the house will arrive for Autumn/Winter 2027-28. De Sarno’s appointment comes as ICICLE continues to build its presence between Shanghai and Paris, with the brand known for its minimalist approach and focus on craftsmanship and nature. After his high-profile Gucci stint, all eyes are now on what De Sarno does with a completely different fashion vocabulary.

Julie Kegels Wins The 2026 LVMH Prize And 400,000 Euro
Belgian designer Julie Kegels has taken home the 2026 LVMH Prize, winning the top honour from a pool of nine finalists. Known for her playful, deconstructed approach to femininity, Kegels launched her Antwerp-based label in 2024 and has quickly become one of the names to watch in emerging fashion. She received the award at Fondation Louis Vuitton in Paris, with Jennifer Lawrence presenting the prize. Kegels takes home a 400,000 Euro grant alongside a year of mentorship from LVMH experts. Not a bad way to make your fashion-world debut.

Adidas Originals And VegNonVeg Put An Indian Spin On The Samba
Adidas Originals and Indian sneaker retailer VegNonVeg have teamed up for their first collaboration, reimagining the iconic Samba as a mule inspired by the traditional Indian Jutti. Designed around the everyday ritual of slipping shoes on and off, the new silhouette brings together the familiar Samba T-toe with a pointed padded tongue, tonal plant and leaf embroidery and a red lining inspired by traditional Juttis. The shoe comes in Black and Silver, with the Silver colourway available exclusively at VegNonVeg in India for INR 10,999. The collaboration launches September 13, with adidas Originals India following on September 14.

Formula 1 And Pompeii Team Up For Their First Fashion Collaboration
Formula 1 and Spanish sportswear label Pompeii have teamed up for their first-ever capsule collection, bringing the paddock into your wardrobe. The nine-piece unisex collection includes pieces such as a Pit Crew Jacket and Mechanic Shirt, drawing inspiration from F1’s racing heritage and the 2026 Spanish Grand Prix. The collaboration was unveiled alongside activations in Madrid, including a takeover of Pompeii’s flagship store. For anyone who has ever watched a race and thought, “I would like the outfit, actually,” this one is for you.

Lea Salonga Becomes The First Filipina To Get A Hollywood Walk Of Fame Star
Lea Salonga has officially added another major milestone to an already impressive résumé. On September 4, the Filipina actor and singer became the first Filipina to receive a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame. She received the star in the Live Performance category, recognising a career that includes her Tony-winning role in Miss Saigon and the singing voices of Jasmine in Aladdin and Mulan in Mulan. For a performer whose voice has been part of so many childhoods, seeing her name on Hollywood Boulevard feels particularly fitting.

SZA And Vans Unveil Their First Collection, And Yes, There’s A Tabi
SZA and Vans are taking their collaboration beyond the campaign and into actual footwear. The collection is being previewed through a New York Fashion Week pop-up, and honestly, a SZA-designed Tabi Vans was probably not on anyone’s 2026 bingo card.

Alexandra Leclerc Gets Her Own Rhode Collection, And Of Course Leo Is In It

Gorillaz Add Guwahati To Their India Tour, Because Two Dates Weren’t Enough
India’s Gorillaz debut just got bigger. The virtual band has added Guwahati as the third and final stop on its first India tour, with a show at Sarusajai Sports Complex on January 31, 2027. The Mountain Tour will now travel from Bengaluru to Mumbai on January 23, then to Guwahati on January 27. The tour supports The Mountain, an album shaped in part by the band’s experiences and musical collaborations in India. Basically, if you thought two Indian dates were exciting, the Northeast said, one more.

Khushi Kapoor Teams Up With Cava For Soft Serve
Khushi Kapoor is getting into athleisure with Cava’s new Soft Serve collection, and this one goes beyond putting a celebrity name on a campaign. Kapoor was closely involved in the creative process, from shaping the mood and aesthetic to refining the silhouettes, colours, fits and details. Inspired by her own wardrobe, the collection is designed to move from workouts to everyday plans, with feminine shapes, playful details and performance-led functionality. At the heart of it all is the idea that “soft does not mean weak,” bringing together softness and strength in a way that feels very much like the actor herself.

Celine And Reebok Bring Back The 1982 Freestyle Lo, With A Luxury Twist
Celine and Reebok have given the 1982 Freestyle Lo, one of Reebok’s most recognisable early women’s athletic shoes, a major fashion makeover. Reworked under Celine creative director Michael Rider, the sneaker comes in eight colourways and swaps its original sporty feel for a more polished, fashion-forward finish in lambskin. First seen on Celine’s Spring 2027 runway, the collaboration officially launched this week. It’s another reminder that the fashion industry remains very committed to digging through the archives, and honestly, we’re not complaining.
