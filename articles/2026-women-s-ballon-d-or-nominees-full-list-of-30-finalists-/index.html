2026 Women’s Ballon d'Or nominees: Full list of 30 finalists - AOL

TheTools · 27.10.2026 01:21 · source · permalink

The day has arrived. Monday, Oct. 26 will see the 2026 Ballon d'Or presented at a ceremony in London.

Patri Guijarro, Khadija Shaw, Alexia Putellas and Temwa Chawinga are among this year’s nominees, but reigning winner Aitana Bonmatí won't be present.

Here is the full 30-player shortlist of women's nominees for the prize:

Who won the Women's Ballon d'Or last year?

Bonmatí earned the Ballon d'Or in 2025, winning the prize for the third straight year. But the Barcelona midfielder won't win a fourth in a row, having missed the shortlist entirely after a season marred by injury.

  • Time: 8 p.m. BST (3 p.m. ET)
