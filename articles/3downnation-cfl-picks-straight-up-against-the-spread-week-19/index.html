3DownNation CFL picks straight-up & against the spread: Week 19 - 3DownNation

TheTools · 09.10.2026 07:07 · source · permalink

Picks

Week 19 in the Canadian Football League is here, and every spot in the West Division playoff race remains up for grabs with all five teams in action.

We have ten contributors making picks straight-up and against the spread, and they’ll do so all season long. Enjoy.

Please note that contributors were asked to make their picks based on the consensus point spreads as of 10:00 a.m. ET on Thursday, October 8. Competing sportsbooks may have different spreads, and lines may change.

Today’s CFL news in your inbox every day at 3 p.m.

Eligible sports bettors in Ontario and Alberta can find relevant information for wagering on the games by clicking on their respective jurisdiction. Please bet responsibly.

Friday, October 9: Edmonton Elks at Hamilton Tiger-Cats (+6.5) at 7:00 p.m. EDT

The Edmonton Elks will visit the Hamilton Tiger-Cats at Hamilton Stadium on Friday night, where the Tabbies are touchdown underdogs at home. The Elks can clinch a home playoff game for the first time since 2015 with a win, while the Tabbies have already been eliminated from playoff contention. These two teams met two weeks ago, when Edmonton won in overtime thanks in large part to the play of kicker Vincent Blanchard, who booted a CFL-record NINE field goals in the 37-35 victory. The Elks will have David Beard and Joe Robustelli back in the lineup after a bye week, but will be without Kordell Jackson Jr. in the secondary. Meanwhile, the Ticats have had to rearrange their offensive line after the injury to Brayden Szeman.

Friday, October 9: Ottawa Redblacks at B.C. Lions (-13.5) at 10:00 p.m. EDT

The winless Ottawa Redblacks will visit the B.C. Lions in Vancouver on Friday night, where the Leos are two-touchdown home favourites. This is officially the widest point spread of the season thus far, the previous being 12.5 points when the Redblacks visited Montreal in Week 12. They did not cover that spread, losing by 30 points. Neither team has made significant changes this week, and Ottawa has elected to give McLeod Bethel-Thompson another start under centre, though rookie Bryson Barnes has been moved up the depth chart and could still make an appearance late.

Saturday, October 10: Calgary Stampeders at Winnipeg Blue Bombers (-1.5) at 7:00 p.m. EDT

The Calgary Stampeders will visit the Winnipeg Blue Bombers at Princess Auto Stadium on Saturday night, where the Blue Bombers are 1.5-point home favourites thanks to the return of quarterback Zach Collaros. These two teams have split the season series to date, with the Blue Bombers winning 30-28 in Calgary in Week 1, and the Stampeders winning 52-30 in Winnipeg in Week 8. A TON is riding on this game, as it will not only decide the season series but also possibly decide who makes the playoffs and who goes home. The Stampeders can officially clinch a playoff berth with a victory, while Winnipeg cannot clinch or be eliminated from contention based on the result of this game, but could fall four points behind in last place.

Monday, October 12: Saskatchewan Roughriders at Montreal Alouettes (-5.5) at 1:00 p.m. EDT

The Saskatchewan Roughriders will visit the Montreal Alouettes on Monday afternoon for the CFL’s annual Thanksgiving Day game, where the Alouettes are modest home favourites. The Alouettes officially have nothing but pride to play for, as they’ve clinched first place in the East Division, but this game means everything for Saskatchewan, as they’ve lost three straight games and have yet to clinch a playoff berth. KeeSean Johnson did not practice on Thursday and appears unlikely to play for the Riders, though Tevaughn Campbell was a full participant. This line could shift as we learn more about how Montreal intends to deploy their roster.

ABBOTT: I’m not convinced Montreal is ready to take their foot off the gas yet.

Straight-up: Montreal. Against the spread: Saskatchewan.

BALLANTINE: I like Montreal sending a message against the reigning and defending champions this week. 

Straight-up: Montreal. Against the spread: Montreal.

Straight-up: Montreal. Against the spread: Montreal.

Straight-up: Montreal. Against the spread: Montreal.

Straight-up: Montreal. Against the spread: Montreal.

HERRERA-VERGARA: The Alouettes can get some kind of revenge for last year’s Grey Cup.

Straight-up: Montreal. Against the spread: Montreal.

HODGE: The Alouettes are a cut above right now.

Straight-up: Montreal. Against the spread: Montreal.

HOSKINS: Sask can’t quite put it together like they did last year. The Montreal machine is hard to face on top of that.

Straight-up: Montreal. Against the spread: Montreal.

KLEIN: This game means nothing to Montreal and everything to Saskatchewan. But I don’t see the Als letting go of a chance to get some revenge for last November.

Straight-up: Montreal. Against the spread: Montreal.
