Calvin Harris Sydney 2027 tickets are on sale now: How to get them

TheTools · 24.09.2026 03:52 · source · permalink

Sydney, this is what you came for – superstar DJ Calvin Harris is heading our way this summer

Update Thursday, September 24: Due to overwhelming demand in yesterday’s pre-sale, Calvin Harris has added two new Aussie dates: one in Sydney and one in Melbourne. The general onsale is now on, so you can try your luck at getting any remaining tickets. Good luck!!

The last time I saw Calvin Harris perform was back in 2010 at Big Day Out (RIP). The Boiler Room was a sweaty, Cruiser-fuelled dance session as he electrified the crowd, going from hit to hit: ‘Ready for the Weekend’, ‘You Used to Hold Me’ and ‘Acceptable in the 80s’. It was one of the best DJ sets I’ve ever seen. I don’t remember much else from that day.

Now, the internationally renowned DJ is heading back to Australia for a national tour, and he’s coming to Sydney.

Next February, the Grammy Award winner and multi-platinum DJ and producer will light up stadiums across the country with his biggest global anthems, including ‘One Kiss’, ‘This Is What You Came For’, ‘Summer’, ‘Feel So Close’ and ‘Sweet Nothing’. Here in Sydney's, he's playing at The Domain.

Along with his record-breaking albums – such as 18 Months, Motion and Funk Wav Bounces Vol. 1 and Vol. 2 – The Calvin Harris - Australia Tour 2027 will celebrate his latest chart-topping collaborations and his recent compilation album 96 Months.

The global hitmaker last graced Aussie shores for Festival X back in 2022, making this his first dedicated headline tour Down Under. Here’s everything you need to know about getting tickets to Calvin Harris’ 2027 Australian tour. 

Millennials, this is our time.

When is Calvin Harris coming to Sydney?

Calvin Harris will bring his bangers to four Australian cities in summer 2027, including:

  • Perth: Langley Park – Thursday, February 18
  • Brisbane: RNA Showgrounds – Friday, February 19
  • Sydney: The Domain – Saturday, February 20 and Monday, February 22
  • Melbourne: Flemington Racecourse – Friday, February 26

Look, there’s not much wiggle room between the first three shows, but we’re crossing our fingers that he might add extra Sydney and Melbourne dates.

How to get tickets to Calvin Harris in Sydney?

Mark your calendars: general public tickets drop at 1pm local time on Thursday, September 24 via Live Nation.

When is the Calvin Harris pre-sale in Sydney?

Given this is Calvin Harris’ first-ever Australian headline tour, we have no doubt demand will be wild. Telstra Plus members can access an exclusive pre-sale running from Monday, September 21 at 11am local time to Wednesday, September 23 at 11am (though allocations will likely sell out much faster).

If you miss out, the official Artist pre-sale runs from Wednesday, September 23 at 12pm local time to Thursday, September 24 at 12pm local time. You can sign up for the Artist pre-sale here.

How much are Calvin Harris tickets in Sydney?

We’ll update this once pricing information becomes available.

Who is the support act for Calvin Harris in Sydney?

Calvin Harris hasn’t announced any special acts for his Aussie tour.
