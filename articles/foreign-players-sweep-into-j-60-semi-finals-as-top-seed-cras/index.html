Foreign players sweep into J-60 semi-finals as top seed crashes out

TheTools · 05.11.2026 19:00 · source · permalink

ISLAMABAD: Pakistan’s challenge in the boys’ singles competition suffered a major setback as all local seeded players, including top seed and much hyped Hamza Roman, crashed out of the J-60 Bard Foundation Junior World Tennis Championships at the PTF Complex courts on Thursday.

In a significant upset, Belarusian Aliaksandr Viarsotski brought Hamza’s campaign to an end, overcoming the Pakistani in straight sets, 6-4, 7-6 (3), to book his place in the semi-finals. The foreign players dominated the quarter-final action, leaving no Pakistani in contention for the singles title. Thailand’s Phasin Sinchalong displayed impressive form to defeat M. Hassan Usmani 6-4, 6-4, while Sri Lanka’s Aahil Kaleel outclassed Ahtesham Humayun 6-1, 7-5 to complete the home players’ singles exit. With the local contenders eliminated, all four semi-final places went to foreign players, underlining the tough international competition at the championship. However, Pakistan retained hopes of silverware in the doubles event, with local pairs advancing to the boys’/men’s doubles finals.

Results:

Boys singles quarterfinals:

Aliaksandr Viarsotski (BLR) bt Hamza Roman (PAK) 6-3, 7-6(3), Haoxuan Xu (CHN) bt Nikita Dogadin(RUS) 6-4, 7-6(2); Phasin Sinchalong (THA) bt M.Hassan Usmani (PAK) 6-4, 6-4; Aahil Kaleel (SRI) bt Ahtesham Humayun(PAK) 6-1, 7-5

Girls singles quarterfinal:

Varvara Rubtsova (RUS) bt Anyawee Paisarnpanuwong(THA) 7-5, 6-0; Victoria Gruntova(RUS) bt Pimpisia Wongwanitkajon (THA) 6-4, 6-3; Meimei Hu (CHN) bt Ploypetch Daungkhiaw(THA) 6-1, 6-1 ; Chiu Kwan Nina Wang (HKG) bt Yuqi Li (CHN) 4-6, 6-0, 6-.

Boys doubles semi-finals:

M.Haziq Asim / M.Hassan Usmani (PAK) bt Aahil Kaleel (SRI) / Amir Mazari (PAK) 6-1, 6-4; Ahtesham Humayun / Hamza Roman (PAK) bt Aolun Ma (CHN) / Haoxuan Xu (CHN) 6-3, 6-4.
