Liam Neeson Learns of Narnia Reboot

TheTools · 05.11.2026 05:54 · source · permalink

It might seem like everyone has heard about or seen the trailer for Greta Gerwig’s upcoming adaptation of The Magician’s Nephew, but apparently, there was at least one notable exception: Liam Neeson.

Neeson, who voiced Aslan in all three of Walden Media’s Narnia films between 2005 and 2010, was surprised to learn that a new adaptation was on the way during a recent interview with ScreenRant.

When the interviewer asked whether he had seen the trailer and how he felt about audiences returning to Narnia on the big screen, Neeson admitted, “This is news to me.”

SR: The new Narnia?

Neeson: Yeah.

SR: Well, I’m grateful to be the one to break it to you. Meryl Streep is taking on Aslan this time around.

Neeson: Meryl is playing Jesus Christ?

SR: She is.

Neeson: Well, if you’re going to make Christ a lady, nobody better than Meryl. Who’s directing it?

SR: Greta Gerwig.

Neeson: Okay. Wow.
