PPA TOUR ASIA REVEALS FIRST FOUR STOPS OF 2027 | PPA Tour Asia

TheTools · 25.10.2026 00:50 · source · permalink
  • News
  • PPA Asia 500

Author: PPA Tour Asia | October 25, 2026

PPA Tour Asia has revealed its first four stops of 2027. Starting at the end of January, the tour will run four PPA Asia 500 events across four destinations, including two brand new locations.

First Four Stops of 2027
Bangkok Open 2027 | Bangkok, Thailand | January 27–31
Manila Open 2027 | Manila, Philippines | February 24–28
Penang Open 2027 | Penang, Malaysia | March 16–20
Ho Chi Minh City Open 2027 | Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam | March 31–April 4

Thailand and the Philippines debut

2027 opens with two firsts for PPA Tour Asia.

The year kicks off in Thailand with the Bangkok Open 2027, January 27–31. New courts and clubs have been popping up across Bangkok, and now the city’s players and fans get to see Pro pickleball live for the very first time.

A month later, it’s Manila’s turn. The Manila Open 2027, February 24–28, marks the tour’s debut in the Philippines. Pickleball here has grown fast, and now local fans get a PPA Tour Asia event of their own, with a week of big matches and the some of the tour’s top talent up close on home soil.

Back to Malaysia and Vietnam

In March, the tour returns to Malaysia for the fifth time, this time in Penang, bringing pro pickleball to the country’s north.
Two weeks later, the tour heads back to one of pickleball’s biggest destinations also for the fifth time, with the Ho Chi Minh City Open 2027 in Vietnam, March 31–April 4.

Road to the PPA Finals

These four events complete the PPA Tour Asia schedule ahead of the PPA Finals at Life Time Rancho San Clemente in California, USA, from May 10–16. The top 8 men and women in the overall World Pickleball Rankings will qualify for the end of season event.
