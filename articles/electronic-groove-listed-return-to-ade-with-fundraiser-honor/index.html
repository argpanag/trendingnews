Electronic Groove & Listed return to ADE with fundraiser honoring Philipp Jung - Electronic Groove

TheTools · 11.09.2026 21:38 · source · permalink
  • NEWS
  • 2026-09-11
  • |
  • Aria Groove

On October 23rd at Duke of Tokyo.

Electronic Groove and Listed will return to Amsterdam Dance Event this October for the fourth edition of their ADE fundraiser series, taking place on Friday, October 23rd at Duke of Tokyo.

This year’s gathering carries a particularly personal meaning. The event will be dedicated to the memory of Philipp Jung, the late cofounder of Get Physical Music and a close friend to many of the artists, promoters, and people involved in the night.

The fundraiser continues a long relationship between Electronic Groove and Listed, who have spent recent ADE editions bringing artists and friends together around charitable causes. Previous gatherings have supported organizations including Save the Children Ukraine, Animal Rights NL, and UNICEF initiatives for children in Gaza.

For 2026, part of the proceeds from every ticket sold will be donated to VisionSpring, an organization Philipp had expressed an interest in supporting.

Founded in 2001, VisionSpring works to provide affordable eyeglasses and vision services to people vulnerable to poverty. The organization focuses on the connection between clear vision and access to work, education, safety, and greater independence. It reports that it has helped more than 16 million people gain clear vision through eyeglasses since its founding.

The organization estimates that more than one billion people worldwide live with blurry vision that could be corrected with glasses. Its programs include vision screenings, eyeglass distribution, support for students, workplace programs, driver safety initiatives, and partnerships with local organizations in underserved communities.

The musical program reflects the personal character of the night.

DJ T. will join the fundraiser, bringing with him a particularly close connection to Philipp and the history of Get Physical. As one of the founding figures behind the label, Thomas Koch played a central role in the community and culture that developed around Philipp over more than two decades.

He will be joined by Anja Schneider, M.O.N.R.O.E., Ivan Sandhas, Andrew Vogt, and Havi, with a Secret Guest also confirmed for the night.

The Secret Guest will be revealed two weeks before the event.

The fundraiser will once again take place at Duke of Tokyo, the intimate Amsterdam venue that has become the home of the Electronic Groove and Listed ADE gatherings. The event will run from 8 PM until 3 AM.

Rather than approaching the night as a traditional memorial, Electronic Groove and Listed want the gathering to reflect the way Philipp connected with people throughout his life, through music, friendship, generosity, and the community around him.

For both organizations, the ADE fundraiser has grown into something much more personal than an annual party.

Across four editions, the idea has remained simple: bring people together during one of electronic music’s busiest weeks and use that moment to support people and causes outside the club.

This year, that purpose feels especially close.
