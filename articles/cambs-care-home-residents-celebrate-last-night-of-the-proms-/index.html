Cambs care home residents celebrate Last Night of the Proms with flags and fizz

TheTools · 19.09.2026 20:19 · source · permalink

Residents at Oaklands care home in Bottisham enjoyed a lively evening of music and celebration during a Last Night of the Proms event.

The event, held on Saturday, September 12, brought together residents and staff to watch the iconic concert broadcast, complete with Union Jack flags, bunting, and plenty of patriotic spirit.

Cristina Monville, general manager at the home, said: "We’ve had a fantastic time celebrating the Last Night of the Proms, it is such a great event to watch and it is wonderful to see the crowds waving their flags and bobbing up and down.

Stanley and staff getting into the spirit of the occasion (Image: Oaklands care home)

"We all sang, some of us were even in tune."

The home was decked out in festive decorations, with residents donning Union Jack hats and raising a glass of fizz to mark the occasion.

One resident, Sarah, said: "I love the Proms and singing along to Jerusalem and Land of Hope & Glory, it is such good fun.

"I’m not much good at singing but it doesn’t matter so much when everyone joins in."
