Google announced that its Fitbit Air health tracker is set to be available in India from October 2, priced at ₹13,999.

The screenless device is Google’s smallest and lightest Fitbit tracker and is integrated with the Google Health Coach to guide users through their healthy lifestyle goals and activities.

Through integration with the Google Health App, users can monitor stats such as continuous heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, irregular heart rhythm (Afib) notifications, detailed sleep analysis, blood oxygen (SpO2), and automatic activity detection.

Coming to battery life, users can expect up to seven days, while fast charging provides up to a full day of battery in just five minutes.

The Google Fitbit Air comes with an interchangeable pebble design and three band styles, based on the user’s sport or lifestyle needs. Byers can choose from the Obsidian, Berry, Lavender and Fog colour variants, and avail a three-month-long trial of Google Health Premium.

The Fitbit Air will be available through the Google Store, Flipkart, Croma, Reliance Digital, Vijay Sales, and others across India.

“With Google Health Coach, Fitbit Air turns health and activity data into more personalized, actionable guidance. Users can automatically log workouts, follow guided sessions, or use their phone’s camera to capture gym equipment and workout routines for easier activity logging,” stated the company in a press release.
