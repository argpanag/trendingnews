Rolex Paris Masters 2026: tickets, transport and tips at the Plenitude Arena (La Défense Arena)

TheTools · 01.11.2026 08:37 · source · permalink

Going to the Rolex Paris Masters 2026? Dates, tickets from €15, how to get to the Plenitude Arena (ex-Paris La Défense Arena), best days to go and what to do on site.

The Rolex Paris Masters runs from Saturday 31 October to Sunday 8 November 2026 at the Plenitude Arena in Nanterre, the venue previously known as Paris La Défense Arena. Tickets start at €15. Here is everything you need to plan your visit to the last ATP Masters 1000 of the season, in its 40th year.

Rolex Paris Masters 2026: the key dates

  • Qualifying: Saturday 31 October and Sunday 1 November, from 11:00 (Centre Court from 12:00 on Saturday)
  • Main draw: Monday 2 November to Sunday 8 November
  • Day sessions start at 11:00 from Monday to Thursday; night sessions on Centre Court start not before 19:00 from Monday to Friday
  • Quarter-finals: Friday 6 November (from 14:00, then not before 19:00)
  • Singles semi-finals: Saturday 7 November (from 14:30, then not before 17:00)
  • Singles final: Sunday 8 November at 15:00, after the doubles final at 12:30
  • Wheelchair tennis: Friday 6 to Sunday 8 November on Courts 2 and 3

The full order of play is published each evening on the official tournament website and on the ATP Tour daily schedule.

Where is the Rolex Paris Masters played?

Since 2025, the tournament has been held at the arena in Paris La Défense, after 39 editions at Bercy (1986–2024). The venue was renamed Plenitude Arena in July 2026 (Visit Paris Region), so you will still see it called “Paris La Défense Arena” on many maps, apps and booking sites. It is the same building.

  • Address: Plenitude Arena, 99 Jardins de l’Arche, 92000 Nanterre
  • Capacity: around 17,500 seats on Centre Court for the tournament
  • Courts: five in total (four competition courts and one practice court)
  • Surface: resin on wood (GreenSet), indoor

The move has been a box-office success: attendance rose from 175,000 spectators in 2024 to a record 220,000 in 2025, according to the tournament’s 2026 press kit. “2026 will be the year that confirms the move’s success,” says tournament director Cédric Pioline.

How to get to the Plenitude Arena (La Défense Arena)

The arena sits at the heart of the La Défense business district, which is one of the best-connected spots in the Paris region. Organisers encourage all spectators to come by public transport.

From central Paris, Metro line 1 and RER A are the simplest options. The official tournament app (free on the App Store and Google Play) includes a door-to-seat navigation service, designed to be accessible to wheelchair users and visitors with visual impairments or reduced mobility.

Rolex Paris Masters 2026 tickets: prices and options

Tickets are sold on the official website, rolexparismasters.com. Prices start at €15.

  • Qualifying (31 October–1 November): €29, or €15 for under-25s, available only on the official website. Two qualifying matches are scheduled on Centre Court each day.
  • 40th-anniversary tickets at €19.86: a limited number of seats priced as a nod to 1986, the year the tournament was founded.
  • Special Court 1 tickets (Monday 2 to Friday 6 November): new for 2026, 500 numbered seats in the front rows of Court 1, where many top-10 players are scheduled. These tickets also give access to Courts 2 and 3.
  • Wheelchair tennis: no separate ticket is needed. Any Rolex Paris Masters ticket gives access to the wheelchair events on Courts 2 and 3.
  • Hospitality: VIP packages are offered by the arena (Plenitude Arena VIP offer).

Before you go, check the official access conditions for security rules and items allowed inside the venue.

Which day should you go?

  • On a budget: qualifying weekend. Tickets are cheap, crowds are lighter and you are close to the players.
  • To see the most players: Monday 2 to Wednesday 4 November, when all four courts are in use, with up to five matches per court.
  • For the legends: from Wednesday 4 to Sunday 8 November, former champions Guy Forget, Sébastien Grosjean, Jo-Wilfried Tsonga, Goran Ivanisevic, Boris Becker and Stefan Edberg will appear on Centre Court to mark the 40th anniversary.
  • For the big matches: Friday’s quarter-finals, Saturday’s semi-finals and Sunday’s final.
  • For wheelchair tennis: Friday 6 to Sunday 8 November. The doubles final is on Saturday (after 16:00) and the singles final on Sunday at 12:00.

What to do at the Rolex Paris Masters

On court

  • A revamped player walkout show, with new lighting, visual effects and video mapping on the court.
  • A vote to choose the music played during the players’ warm-ups, by scanning a QR code on the big screens.
  • The chance to hit a few balls on Centre Court with one of the day’s winners. To take part, catch one of the balls hit into the stands.
  • Quizzes, interactive lighting in the stands and selfie cams on the big screens.
  • A daily draw for spectators born in 1986, who can also collect a gift from the service area.

In the concourses

  • A 40th-anniversary photo booth, and photo sessions with players at selected times.
  • A Gaming Zone with table football, reflex games and more.
  • Partner stands, including a mini-tennis court at the evian stand and the Lexus stands.
  • Merchandise: a new 30 m² shop at Gate 114 with the 40th-anniversary collection, Lacoste shops at Gate 33 and in the concourse, a HEAD shop and Dunlop stands.
  • Food outlets where half of the options are vegetarian.
  • A DJ on the forecourt as spectators arrive for each session.

Accessibility at the Plenitude Arena

  • Inclusive navigation in the official app, guiding visitors from home to their seat, with an interactive map of the arena.
  • “Sound for All”: live audio commentary for blind and visually impaired fans via the app, from the semi-finals onwards (Saturday 7 November).
  • Quiet room: on the Level 1 concourse, area 103, for anyone who needs a break from the crowds.

Rolex Paris Masters 2026: FAQ

When is the Rolex Paris Masters 2026?

From Saturday 31 October to Sunday 8 November 2026. Qualifying is played on 31 October and 1 November; the main draw runs from 2 to 8 November.

Is the Paris La Défense Arena the same as the Plenitude Arena?

Yes. Paris La Défense Arena was renamed Plenitude Arena in July 2026. The address is unchanged: 99 Jardins de l’Arche, 92000 Nanterre.

Is the Paris Masters still played at Bercy?

No. The tournament was held at Bercy from 1986 to 2024 and moved to La Défense in 2025.

How much are tickets for the Paris Masters?

From €15. Qualifying tickets cost €29 (€15 for under-25s), and a limited number of 40th-anniversary tickets are priced at €19.86.

What is the nearest station to the Plenitude Arena?

La Défense (RER A, Metro line 1, Tram T2, Transilien L and U). Nanterre Préfecture (RER A) and Nanterre-La-Folie (RER E) are also close.

Do I need a separate ticket for wheelchair tennis?

No. A Rolex Paris Masters ticket gives access to the wheelchair events on Courts 2 and 3.
