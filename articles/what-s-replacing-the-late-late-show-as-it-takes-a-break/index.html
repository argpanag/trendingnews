What's replacing the Late Late Show as it takes a break

TheTools · 26.11.2026 16:16 · source · permalink

As Patrick Kielty gears up to host his fourth Late Late Toy Show, he's taking a wee break from our TV screens.

The Down man finished up on air on Friday, November 20, taking a week off hosting duties before the big day on Friday, December 4.

Today's top videos

Therefore, if you're curling up on the couch to watch the Late Late Show on Friday night, it won't be on. But fear not, it will instead be replaced by a hit movie.

The 2022 film See How They Run, starring Saoirse Ronan, Sam Rockwell, Harris Dickinson, and Adrien Brody will air in the show's usual 9.35pm slot on RTÉ One.

It's a comedy mystery set in 1953 London's West End, where plans for a film adaptation of Agatha Christie's stage play The Mousetrap halt after director Leo Köpernick is murdered backstage during its 100th performance.

World-weary Inspector Stoppard (Rockwell) and his eager rookie Constable Stalker (Ronan) investigate the cast of eccentric theatre suspects and real-life figures to catch the killer.

The two find themselves thrown into a puzzling whodunit within the 'glamorously sordid theatre underground, investigating the mysterious homicide at their own peril'.

See How They Run is a fun, switch-your-brain-off watch, and it's only 98 minutes long — which is a huge bonus!

The news of the Late Late Show's week-long break comes after a teaser trailer was released for the Toy Show.

The 2026 theme has been kept under lock and key, but the trailer teases 'extra magic' and 'extra OMG' Sign us up.
