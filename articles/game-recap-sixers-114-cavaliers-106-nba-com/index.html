Game Recap: Sixers 114, Cavaliers 106 | NBA.com

TheTools · 23.10.2026 04:01 · source · permalink

Oct 22, 2026

After trailing by 29 points, the Philadelphia 76ers stormed back to defeat the Cleveland Cavaliers 114-106, completing the largest comeback in franchise history since the NBA's play-by-play era began in 1997-98. This marks just the 10th time in the play-by-play era that an NBA team has overcome a deficit of 29 or more points to win.
