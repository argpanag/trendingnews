Brussels Airlines begins Starlink service on Copenhagen flight

TheTools · 09.11.2026 11:25 · source · permalink

Brussels Airlines has begun Starlink-powered high-speed Wi-Fi service, with its first equipped flight operating to Copenhagen on 9th November. The service is planned across the airline's fleet and will be free in every cabin for eligible passengers. The launch is part of Lufthansa Group's connectivity program.

Originally reported by economyclassandbeyond.boardingarea.com · Brussels Airlines takes flight with Starlink

Edited by Randy Petersen

Nov 9 · 12:25 PM · 1 min

Flight SN2257 to Copenhagen on 9th November was the first Brussels Airlines service with the system. The aircraft, registration OO-SBA, is an Airbus A320neo and the carrier's first Starlink-equipped plane. Brussels Airlines expects to have 15 aircraft fitted with high-speed internet by the first half of 2027, as installation expands beyond the initial aircraft. The initial deployment is part of a fleetwide plan.

Lufthansa Group is working with Starlink to equip approximately 850 aircraft across its airlines, making it Europe's largest airline group to adopt the service. Free access applies across booking classes to passengers with a Lufthansa Group Travel ID or Miles & More account, after they accept the terms. Users must wear headphones and may not make voice or video calls or livestream.

Free access requires a specified account and acceptance of the usage rules, so a passenger's booking class alone does not establish eligibility. Calls and livestreams are prohibited, constraining work and entertainment uses even when onboard Wi-Fi is available.
