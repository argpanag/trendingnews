Video: Ctrl+Alt+Del? Sepang chaos as cars stop before F1 can start | GRANDE PRÊMIO

TheTools · 04.10.2026 08:15 · source · permalink

Several cars encountered technical problems during formation running behind the Safety Car, forcing another interruption after torrential rain had already delayed the race.

News Summary

  • Hamilton stopped on the main straight, reporting no response from the accelerator.
  • Verstappen, Ocon and Bottas also experienced problems; Leclerc was told he had a software glitch.
  • Race Control suspended the starting procedure and ordered drivers back to the pits.

Ctrl+Alt+Del? Formula 1’s return to Sepang descended into further chaos when several cars experienced technical problems before the Bahrain Grand Prix could start. After torrential rain delayed proceedings, the attempt to begin formation running ended with cars stopping and Race Control sending the field back to the pits.

The video captures the disruption during the procedure, which began at 15:40 local time behind the Safety Car. Lewis Hamilton’s Ferrari stopped on the main straight, while other drivers also struggled to keep their cars running normally.

Hamilton presses the throttle — but nothing happens

Hamilton reported that his car was not responding to the accelerator, leaving him stranded as the rest of the field approached.

“I’m pressing the throttle, nothing is happening,” Hamilton said over the radio.

Yellow flags were displayed, creating uncertainty among following drivers about whether they could pass the stationary Ferrari during the formation procedure.

Pole sitter Max Verstappen had already reported an acceleration problem in his Red Bull.

“I can’t accelerate guys, it’s back,” Verstappen said.

Race engineer Gianpiero Lambiase informed him that the team was looking into the issue. Esteban Ocon also stopped, while Valtteri Bottas experienced technical difficulties.

Charles Leclerc was subsequently told that his Ferrari had suffered a software glitch. Hamilton, Ocon and Bottas all drive Ferrari-powered cars, but Verstappen’s separate complaint came in a Red Bull Ford. A common cause for the problems had not been confirmed.

Another interruption before lights out

Race Control suspended the starting procedure and instructed drivers to return to the pits, interrupting the planned sequence of running behind the Safety Car before a standing start.

The incident added technical disruption to the weather delay that had already prevented the Formula 1 race from beginning at its original 15:00 start time.

The keyboard-reset reference describes the extraordinary scenes; it does not establish that a computer restart, AI fault or shared software failure caused the problems. The confirmed sequence and radio messages are detailed in the report on Hamilton’s stoppage and Leclerc’s software glitch.

F1 today: FAQ

What time is F1 qualifying at Sepang?

Qualifying starts on Saturday, 3 October, at 16:00 Malaysia time. That is 09:00 BST in the UK, 04:00 EDT in New York and Toronto, 01:00 PDT in Los Angeles and Vancouver, 13:30 IST in India and 18:00 AEST in Sydney.

Did Lewis Hamilton receive a grid penalty for the Bortoleto incident?

No. Hamilton received a warning after impeding Gabriel Bortoleto during FP2 at Sepang. Ferrari was fined €10,000, but Hamilton will not lose grid positions over the incident.

Why were F1 lap times at Sepang over four seconds slower than expected?

Pirelli identified low grip, dust and an abrasive surface that increased tyre sliding and degradation. Friday’s benchmark was more than four seconds slower than its qualifying simulation reference, rather than four seconds slower than the 2017 race weekend.
