Odisha weather: Rain likely in these nine districts - OrissaPOST

TheTools · 21.11.2026 11:31 · source · permalink

Bhubaneswar: India Meteorological Department (IMD) Saturday forecast light to moderate rain or thundershowers in nine Odisha districts over the next 24 hours following the weakening of cyclone Akvan.

The low-pressure area formed after the cyclone weakened over north interior Odisha has shifted to north Chhattisgarh and adjoining areas, the regional centre of IMD said.

The system lay over north Chhattisgarh and neighbouring areas at 8.30 am Saturday. The associated cyclonic circulation extends up to 4.5 km above mean sea level, tilting southwestwards with height, the weather agency added.

The districts likely to receive rainfall are Baleshwar, Sundaragada, Jharsuguda, Baragada, Sambalpur, Debagada, Kendujhar and Mayurbhanj till 8.30 am of November 22.

Dry weather is likely to prevail in the remaining districts during the period, while shallow to moderate fog may occur at a few places in Jharsuguda, Khordha, Koraput and Sundaragada districts.

The IMD said dry conditions are likely to prevail across all districts of Odisha thereafter.
