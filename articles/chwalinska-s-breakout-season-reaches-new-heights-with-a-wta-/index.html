Chwalinska’s breakout season reaches new heights with a WTA 1000 title in Wuhan

TheTools · 18.10.2026 13:10 · source · permalink

It hadn't always been smooth sailing for Maja Chwalinska since her breakthrough at Roland Garros earlier this year, but the 25-year-old proved her run to the final in Paris was no fluke with another remarkable week in Wuhan, capturing her first career title. 

The No. 16 seed rallied from a set and a break down and faced a critical juncture at 4-all in the second set before forcing a decider. She then won the final four games -- saving three break points while serving for the match -- to defeat World No. 170 Sonay Kartal 2-6, 6-4, 6-2 in 2 hours, 43 minutes and capture the 2026 Dongfeng · Voyah Wuhan Open.

Secure your seats for the WTA Finals

After a season-long race, the world's top players converge in the California desert with the WTA's biggest title still to be won.

Chwalinska is the fourth player on the WTA Tour Driven by Mercedes-Benz since the format's introduction in 2009 to win her first title at the WTA 1000 level, joining Naomi Osaka (Indian Wells 2018), Bianca Andreescu (Indian Wells 2019) and Victoria Mboko (Montreal 2025).
