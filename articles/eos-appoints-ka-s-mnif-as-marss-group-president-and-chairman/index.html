EOS appoints Kaïs Mnif as MARSS Group President and Chairman - Unmanned airspace

TheTools · 19.11.2026 13:50 · source · permalink

Australian defence technology company Electro Optic Systems (EOS) has appointed Kaïs Mnif as President and Chairman of MARSS Group, the counter-drone specialist acquired by EOS in May 2026.

Mnif will be based at MARSS’s office in Nice, France, and report to EOS CEO and Managing Director Andreas Schwer. His appointment is intended to support MARSS’s continued growth and its integration into the EOS group.

Johannes Pinl will remain CEO of MARSS and continue to lead the company’s sales activities and expansion into new markets across Europe, the Middle East and Africa.

Mnif joins from Thales Group, where he spent 12 years in senior management positions, most recently as CEO of a subsidiary. He also held leadership roles at Motorola and Alstom, covering technology, defence, security, transport and air navigation.

EOS said Mnif’s experience in integrating acquisitions and growing international businesses would support MARSS’s next phase of development.
