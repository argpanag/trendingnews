Finn Harps end barren spell with impressive win at Wexford - Donegal Daily

TheTools · 11.09.2026 20:55 · source · permalink

Gavin McAteer. Photo: Joe Boland (North West Newspix)

After nine consecutive defeats and more than two months without a victory, Finn Harps produced a much-needed win away to Wexford at Ferrycarrig Park.

Wexford FC 1 Finn Harps 3

Gavin McAteer, Bernardo Monteiro and Oran Brogan bagged the goals to give Patrick McEleney his first win as Harps' interim head coach.

Harps could have been ahead before the break when Aaron McLaughlin somehow missed from close range after Monteiro’s clever work created the opening.

At the other end, Wexford came closest to breaking the deadlock when Kaylem Harnett’s curling effort crashed back off the crossbar.

Harps struck first four minutes into the second period. Darragh Coyle won possession before Monteiro released McLaughlin, whose lay-off gave Gavin McAteer the chance to take aim from the edge of the area. The 18-year-old made no mistake, drilling his finish into the bottom corner.

Wexford responded almost immediately. Gavin Hodgins found Jake Doyle, who turned sharply on the edge of the box before firing beyond Corey Sheridan to restore parity.

Harps, though, refused to let the game slip away.

Just before the hour, Tony McNamee’s long-range effort was brilliantly kept out by Alex Moody, but the rebound fell perfectly for Monteiro. The Portuguese midfielder cut inside from the left and squeezed his shot inside the near post to restore the visitors’ advantage.

Wexford pushed for another equaliser, but Sheridan produced several important saves to keep Harps ahead.

The points were finally secured late on when Brogan glanced Joel Bradley-Walsh’s free-kick into the net.

Wexford: Alex Moody; Ryan Butler (Max Murphy 66), Rhys Bartley, Zayd Abada; Kaylem Harnett, Ryan Ritchie, Jamie Wynne, Gavin Hodgins (Gideon Tetteh 66), Mikie Rowe; Liam Doyle (Zak O’Sullivan 76), Jake Doyle.

Finn Harps: Corey Sheridan; Josh Cullen, Joel Bradley-Walsh, Max Hutchison; Darragh Coyle, Ciaron Harkin, Tony McNamee (Adam McDaid 89), Finn McKenlay (Oran Brogan 76), Gavin McAteer, Bernardo Monteiro (Guilherme Rego Priosti 89); Aaron McLaughlin (Bridel Bosakani 66).

Referee: Mark Houlihan.
