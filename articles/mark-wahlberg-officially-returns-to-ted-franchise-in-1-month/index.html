Mark Wahlberg Officially Returns to Ted Franchise in 1 Month

Not many movies have managed to ruin a childhood wish as Mark Wahlberg’s Ted did. However, when the movie initially came out in 2012, it was an iconic comedy movie that forced the world to turn their heads to this Seth MacFarlane film.

Wahlberg received much love and fame for his role, a role that he reprised in 2015’s Ted 2. However, the actor went on a hiatus while Ted received a live-action TV show.

Well, according to Variety, Wahlberg is returning to the role of John Bennet in an upcoming animated series in less than one month. The series will pick up 12 years after the events of Ted 2, and apparently, Ted has his own family.

Ted Has His Own Family in Ted: The Animated Series

The animated show will release on December 17, 2026, and will see Mark Wahlberg returning to voice the role of John Bennet alongside Seth MacFarlane as Ted.

The premise of Ted: The Animated Series picks up twelve years after the conclusion of Ted 2. The series sees John (Wahlberg) and Ted (MacFarlane) as neighbors, both with families of their own and dead-end jobs. The two stoners enable each other’s bad ideas about marriage, parenting, and the challenges of adult life.

The series has received a TV-MA rating and is clearly intended for adult audiences and fans of The Family Guy (also Seth MacFarlane). However, the interesting thing to note is that this will be Wahlberg’s return to the Ted franchise after 11 years!

Mark Wahlberg Returns to Ted Franchise After 11 Years

Spending eleven years away doing other projects, Mark Wahlberg is heading back to the world of Ted. His final appearance was in the 2015 film Ted 2, which makes Ted: The Animated Series his reappearance after a decade.

Alongside Wahlberg, the cast also includes Amanda Seyfried and Jessica Barth, who will reprise their roles as Sam and Tami-Lynn, respectively. Two new characters are also introduced in the show, namely Apollo (voiced by Kyle Mooney) and Ruth (Liz Richman).

Although plot details have been kept vague, fans are indeed excited to see the return of an iconic franchise. Ted: The Animated Series will mark the second show of the franchise on Peacock, with the first being the live-action series Ted.

Ted: The Animated Series will premiere on December 17 on Peacock.
