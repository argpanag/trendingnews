Seamus Coleman has hinted that his playing days may have come to an end.

The Killybegs native, who left Everton after 17 years at the end of last season, says he has enjoyed spending time at home with friends and family and is unsure of whether or not he’ll be signing for a new club.

Coleman trained with the Killybegs GAA team in the summer and says he has tried to keep fit in case the right opportunity to keep playing arose.
