Westpac raises most home loan rates, but cuts a key one

TheTools · 18.09.2026 05:43 · source · permalink

Westpac has changed all its fixed home loan rates today, most about +20 bps high across the board.

But they have launched a spring special rate of 5.29% which is a -16 bps reduction. This level gives them a good rate advantage over all their main rivals.

At the same time they have raise most of their one year and longer term deposit rates by between +10 bps and +25 bps with the largest increases for the longest terms. However their two year TD rate is unchanged at 4.30%. All their TD rates for terms shorter than a year are also unchanged.

Globally, the benchmark interest rates have settled in at the new higher level, but the rising impulse has vanished in the past few days.

You can still get lower rates from the challenger banks, especially the Chinese ones. And banks like the SBS Bank and TSB are not so directly affected by swap rate shifts. Their cost of funds are more influenced by what they pay depositors. So for them, the reaction of their deposit base will play a key role in how they set home loan rates.
