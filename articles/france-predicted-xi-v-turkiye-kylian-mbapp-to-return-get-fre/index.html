France predicted XI v Turkiye: Kylian Mbappé to return - Get French Football News

TheTools · 15.11.2026 11:22 · source · permalink

Having missed the defeat to Italy on Thursday night, Kylian Mbappé will return for France against Turkiye in the UEFA Nations League on Sunday night.

Les Bleus’ captain missed the game with minor muscular discomfort. The Real Madrid forward also missed three of the four games in the last international break, the first of the Zinédine Zidane era.

Following the defeat to Italy, the first of his tenure, Zidane could make some changes. One of them is enforced, with Dayot Upamecano withdrawing from the squad for personal reasons. It is expected that Ibrahima Konaté will take his place. There could also be a start for Sael Kumbedi, who plays his football for Wolfsburg in the German second division, in the troublesome right-back position.

Mike Maignan; Andy Diouf, Ibrahima Konaté, Jérémy Jacquet, Sael Kumbedi; Warren Zaire-Emery, Eduardo Camavinga, Rayan Cherki; Bradley Barcola, Kylian Mbappé, Michael Olise. (L’Éq)
