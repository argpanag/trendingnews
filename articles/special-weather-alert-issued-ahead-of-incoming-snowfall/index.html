Across affected areas, total snowfall accumulations are expected to range from 5 to 15 cm

Calgary is expected to see several centimetres of snow beginning Tuesday afternoon, with snowfall continuing into Wednesday as a winter weather system moves across Alberta.

Environment and Climate Change Canada issued a special weather statement Monday afternoon, warning that snow could become heavy at times as the system moves north from southwestern Alberta.

The city’s forecast calls for about 2 cm of snow Tuesday around the evening commute, followed by another 5 cm overnight, with temperatures hovering around the freezing mark.

Across the affected areas, total snowfall accumulations are expected to range from 5 to 15 cm.

Environment Canada says the exact track and intensity of the system remain uncertain, and some locations could be upgraded to a snowfall warning.

The snowfall event is expected to end Wednesday night.

Residents are encouraged to monitor weather alerts and forecasts as conditions develop at weather.gc.ca.
