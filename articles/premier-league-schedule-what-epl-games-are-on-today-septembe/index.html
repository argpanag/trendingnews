Premier League schedule: What EPL games are on today, September 12?

TheTools · 12.09.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

Premier League fans in the United States have seven games to choose from Saturday, with five matches kicking off simultaneously

After three matchweeks, clubs are beginning to build form, address early problems and chase important points before the schedule becomes more demanding. Saturday offers a full day of EPL soccer, with several of the league’s biggest clubs playing at home or on the road.

Liverpool, Chelsea and Aston Villa are all set for morning home games. Tottenham will also play in front of their supporters before Arsenal close Saturday’s action with an evening trip to Sunderland.

The weekend continues on Sunday with Coventry City facing Brighton and the first Manchester derby of the season between Manchester United and Manchester City. Before then, seven Premier League soccer games will be played across Saturday.

Today’s biggest Premier League games

Tottenham host Everton in the first evening fixture, with kickoff set for 5:30 p.m. Spurs are still searching for their first Premier League win of the season and will aim to claim all three points in front of their home fans.

Sunderland then host Arsenal at the Stadium of Light in Saturday’s final EPL soccer game. Arsenal have won each of their first three league matches and will look to protect their 100% record this season against Sunderland.

Liverpool’s home meeting with Fulham and Chelsea’s matchup against Hull are also among the key games in the afternoon schedule.
