Davina McCall Reveals Memory Loss After Brain Tumor Surgery

TheTools · 13.10.2026 18:17 · source · permalink

Television presenter Davina McCall revealed that she experienced severe memory problems after surgery to remove a benign brain tumor in 2024, describing the period after the operation as a major challenge for her and her husband Michael Douglas.

Speaking on the Mad, Sad and Bad podcast with Paloma Faith, McCall, 58, said the memory issues followed removal of a colloid cyst, a rare non-cancerous brain tumor she discovered after a health scan.

McCall described 2025 as the "hardest year" for herself and Michael Douglas. The presenter explained that she struggled with daily memories after the operation.

"After I'd had my brain tumor taken out, I had advanced Alzheimer's, so I couldn't remember anything," said Davina McCall.

"When I came out of the operation, I didn't know I'd had an operation. The staff came in every day, and they'd go, 'What's my name?' and I'd go, 'I don't know'." said Davina McCall.

She said her short-term memory affected visits from her husband and made familiar activities difficult during recovery.

"Michael would come and visit for two hours, and he'd go to the loo and come back, and I'd go, 'Oh my God, you're here,' and I didn't know he'd been there. I couldn't watch a movie for six months because I couldn't remember what had happened in the movie 15 minutes before. A book I couldn't remember the page before." said Davina McCall.

McCall said music became part of her recovery process, which she referred to as her "mind physio" while rebuilding her memory.

"I did singing lessons. Music. That was my mind physio." said Davina McCall.

She also described a later improvement in her memory, saying she noticed a significant change after months of recovery.

"Yeah, music was what healed me. And last October was the first time I woke up, and I went, 'Oh my god, I remember what I did yesterday." said Davina McCall.

McCall previously shared that she felt affected by the tumor diagnosis and discussed her emotional response on her Begin Again podcast with Steven Bartlett.

"I felt like this thing had taken control of me and I was so angry about that," said Davina McCall.

"I couldn't let it go. I was like, 'How dare you control my daily life like this and make me feel every day like I'm in danger?'" said Davina McCall.

McCall said the tumor was discovered after a health scan offered following a menopause talk. She later underwent a craniotomy to remove the growth.

"A few months ago, I did a menopause talk for a company, and they offered me a health scan in return, which I thought I was going to ace," said Davina McCall.

She said the scan instead revealed the benign colloid cyst and explained its rarity.

"But it turned out I had a benign brain tumor called a colloid cyst, which is very rare - three in a million," said Davina McCall.

McCall was treated by neurosurgeon Kevin O'Neill after the diagnosis and later spoke about their ongoing friendship following the procedure.

"We're going to be friends for the rest of our lives," said Davina McCall.

"We are going to go out to dinner with our partners! We are going to do it." said Davina McCall.
