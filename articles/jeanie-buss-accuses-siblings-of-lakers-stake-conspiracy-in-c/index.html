Jeanie Buss Accuses Siblings of Lakers Stake Conspiracy in Court

TheTools · 31.10.2026 20:24 · source · permalink

Jeanie Buss is asking a Los Angeles judge to stop her siblings from selling the family’s remaining Lakers stake, arguing they violated a 2017 court order and conspired against her. The dispute could determine whether Buss keeps her role as the team’s governor as the family considers a sale.

  • The Buss family’s remaining stake is 17.8%—above the NBA’s 15% minimum for the family to retain control.
  • The Lakers are being valued at $12.5 billion in a proposed sale.
  • Each sibling would receive an additional $371 million if the family sells all its shares.

Buss says her siblings’ efforts undermine the trust’s terms and the 2017 court order; her siblings reportedly cast themselves as victims. The prospective buyer has said Buss would remain governor, but acknowledged that could change.

  • Buss wants the court to block the sale and remove two siblings as co-trustees, among other remedies.
  • Settlement talks have begun, and a hearing is scheduled for Dec. 8 in Los Angeles Superior Court.

The court hearing or a negotiated settlement could clarify whether the family can sell its stake without Buss’s consent—and whether she stays in charge of the Lakers.

This is more than a family fight over money: the outcome could reshape who controls the Lakers and whether Buss remains the team’s governor.
