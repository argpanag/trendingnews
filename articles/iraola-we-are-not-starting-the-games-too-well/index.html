Iraola: ‘We are not starting the games too well’

TheTools · 11.09.2026 17:17 · source · permalink

The Reds are expending extra energy by having to pull off multiple comebacks.

Andoni Iraola is back on the message of asking for a fast start from Liverpool.

The Reds earned successive victories for the first time under their head coach thanks to goals from Dominik Szoboszlai and Alexis Mac Allister against Atletico Madrid at Anfield. But the 2-1 win would see the Rojiblancos score first through Marcos Llorente as the hosts fell behind once more.

·‘We are probably playing better second halves than first halves’

Liverpool also needed to come back twice against Newcastle United and Nottingham Forest to earn 2-2 draws in their first pair of Premier League fixtures. The team’s match management needs work.

“Normally in all the games we've been dangerous, we've created chances, we've scored two goals. We could have scored more in some of those games. But now it's about being more efficient – not conceding two goals against Newcastle, not conceding the two goals against [Nottingham] Forest and especially also when the goals come because I think we are not starting the games too well.

“We had the positive present of Ipswich – the games look much more comfortable when you start like this. The other games, we've started losing and then you have to do a lot of effort to come back.

“I would like, obviously, to score the first goal [and] everything looks easier. But we need to have the right mentality. I think we didn't start badly the other day against Atletico. I think we had a chance in 15 seconds, something like this, Alexander Isak very clear chance.

“You score and everyone says, 'Incredible start.' You don't score and this changes. But I think the start of the games is probably one of the parts where we can be a little bit better. The second halves we've normally been better [in] but I want to try to impose ourselves from the beginning because everything becomes easier.

“We are probably playing better second halves than first halves. It means probably we are finishing the games quite strong but still you need to do a full 90 minutes, not just starts, not just the second half, we need to be complete all the time. But I see most of the players in a good physical condition.

“We have some situations – [for example] Bradley Barcola already hasn't had a proper pre-season [so] he's got cramps. The ones that have had some niggles, a little bit less preparation, they are still not probably at their best, but overall, I see the team in a good place.”
