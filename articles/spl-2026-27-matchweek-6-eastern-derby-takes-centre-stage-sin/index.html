SPL 2026/27 Matchweek 6: Eastern Derby takes centre stage – Singapore Premier League

TheTools · 30.10.2026 08:23 · source · permalink

A top-of-the-table clash between Eastern rivals Tampines Rovers and Geylang International headlines Matchweek 6 of the 2026/27 Singapore Premier League (SPL) season, with one final round of fixtures to come before the November international break. 

Meanwhile, Lion City Sailors will look to make it four consecutive SPL wins when they take on Balestier Khalsa, while Tanjong Pagar United and FC Jurong go head-to-head as both sides seek a return to winning ways. Elsewhere, Hougang United and Young Lions will be desperate to get their first win of the season.

Wounded Jaguars and White Swans face off

After three straight defeats in the SPL, FC Jurong will look to get back on track when they face fellow Jurong East side Tanjong Pagar United on Friday night.

Both sides head into the clash on the back of disappointing results, with Jurong narrowly losing 3-2 to Tampines Rovers, while Tanjong Pagar suffered a heavy 6-0 defeat to Lion City Sailors in their last outing.

The White Swans can take encouragement from their spirited second-half fightback against the Stags, having scored twice after trailing 3-0 at the break. However, they will need to tighten up defensively if they are to return to winning ways.

Tanjong Pagar, meanwhile, will be keen to put their heavy defeat behind them and deliver a stronger response. The Jaguars will need to be more organised at the back and make better use of their opportunities if they are to get their campaign back on track.

The White Swans have won their last four competitive meetings with the Jaguars, including all three league encounters last season, and will be keen to extend that run. However, with both sides looking to bounce back from disappointing results, Friday’s encounter offers either team a chance to regain momentum.

Tanjong Pagar vs FC Jurong | Friday, 30 October | 7:30pm | Jurong East Stadium | Watch on YouTube or meWATCH

Hougang and Young Lions eager to get off the mark

Hougang United and Young Lions will both be searching for their first win of the season when they meet on Saturday evening.

Hougang head into the tie after a 3-0 defeat to Geylang International, leaving them with four defeats and one draw from their opening five matches. 

The Cheetahs have scored just four goals so far, making them joint-lowest scorers in the league, while their 12 goals conceded highlight their defensive struggles. Akbar Nawas’ men will be eager to bounce back but need to be more clinical in front of goal and tighter at the back to improve their fortunes.

Hougang will also have to cope without defender Milos Drincic, who was sent off in their last outing.

Young Lions have faced similar challenges in attack, having also found the net just four times this season. Their defensive record has been a greater concern, however, with 21 goals conceded in their opening matches.

Firdaus Kassim’s charges will view this fixture as an opportunity to get their campaign going, but will need to show greater resilience at the back and greater cutting edge in attack against a Hougang side equally determined to claim all three points.

Hougang United vs Young Lions | Saturday, 31 October | 7:30pm | Bishan Stadium | Watch on YouTube or meWATCH

Sailors aim to maintain momentum against in-form Tigers

The Lion City Sailors will look to extend their winning run in the SPL to four games when they take on Balestier Khalsa on Sunday night. 

However, the defending champions face a Tigers side that went unbeaten throughout October and will be eager to carry that momentum into November.

The Sailors showcased their attacking threat with a 6-0 victory over Tanjong Pagar United in the SPL last Sunday before following it up with a 2-0 away win over Adelaide United in the AFC Champions League (ACL) Two in midweek. 

Mark Jackson’s charges will be eager to build on those two convincing performances and maintain their winning run in the league as they look to keep pace with the likes of Tampines Rovers and Geylang International.

Balestier, meanwhile, recorded two wins and a draw across their three matches in October. The Tigers began the month with a victory over Hougang United, then held Geylang International to a goalless draw before beating Young Lions in their most recent outing. 

They now face a stern test against the defending champions, but will take confidence from their recent run of form as they look to extend their unbeaten streak.

Balestier Khalsa vs Lion City Sailors | Sunday, 1 November | 7:30pm | Bishan Stadium | Watch on YouTube or meWATCH

Battle for supremacy in the Eastern Derby

Rounding off the matchweek is the first Eastern Derby of the season as Tampines Rovers and Geylang International lock horns in a highly anticipated top-of-the-table clash. 

With the Eagles sitting top of the table and the Stags boasting a game in hand, Sunday’s showdown at Our Tampines Hub could have an early bearing on the battle for top spot.

Tampines have made a perfect start to their league campaign, winning all four of their opening matches, despite facing difficulties on the continental stage with three defeats in three in the ACL Two. 

Their most recent outing saw the Stags suffer a heavy 5-1 loss to Cambodian outfit PKR Svay Rieng in midweek, following a hard-fought 3-2 victory over FC Jurong in the SPL last weekend. Nazri Nasir’s men will be eager to put their continental struggles behind them and maintain their winning run in the league as they look to keep pace with Eastern rivals Geylang at the top of the table.

Meanwhile, Geylang have emerged as serious contenders at the top of the table, having won four and drawn one of their opening five matches in what is their best start to the season since 2001. 

The Eagles ran out comfortable 3-0 victors over Hougang United last time out, with Yang Hyeonju scoring twice to take his tally to four goals for the season. Dejan Racic and Sahil Suhaimi have also contributed in front of goal, giving Noor Ali’s side plenty of attacking options heading into the derby.

Tampines Rovers are unbeaten in their last eight matches against Geylang International across all competitions, having last lost to the Eagles back in July 2023. 

Tampines Rovers vs Geylang International | Sunday, 1 November | 7:30pm | Our Tampines Hub | Watch on YouTube or meWATCH
