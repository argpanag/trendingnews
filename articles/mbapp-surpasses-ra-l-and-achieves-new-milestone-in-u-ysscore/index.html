Mbappé surpasses Raúl and achieves new milestone in U… | YSscores

TheTools · 14.10.2026 19:50 · source · permalink

Kylian Mbappé continues to make history at Real Madrid in the UEFA Champions League, having surpassed Spanish legend Raúl González in the all-time scoring list of the competition after scoring a goal during the match against Roma in the second round of the group stage.

The French forward has increased his tally to 72 goals in the UEFA Champions League, moving into fifth place on the all-time top scorers list, one goal ahead of Raúl, who scored 71 goals in the competition.

Kylian Mbappé is just steps away from advancing in the all-time scoring list, which is topped by Portuguese Cristiano Ronaldo with 140 goals, followed by Argentine Lionel Messi with 129 goals, then Polish Robert Lewandowski with 109 goals, while French Karim Benzema occupies fourth place with 90 goals.

The ranking of the top scorers in the UEFA Champions League throughout history is as follows:

Cristiano Ronaldo has scored 140 goals.

Lionel Messi has scored 129 goals.

Robert Lewandowski has scored 109 goals.

Karim Benzema has scored 90 goals.

Kylian Mbappé has scored 72 goals.

Raúl González has scored 71 goals.
