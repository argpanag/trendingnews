Daisy Edgar-Jones just wore a 149-year-old necklace with the most romantic history

TheTools · 16.09.2026 13:39 · source · permalink

The actress wore the piece to the Sense and Sensibility premiere

Daisy Edgar-Jones joined her fellow Sense and Sensibility castmates at the film’s London premiere on Tuesday night and, for the occasion, wore a suitably romantic Vivienne Westwood gown that looked as if it had tumbled straight out of an 18th-century oil painting.

Crafted from a beautiful, silk duchesse material in a champagne hue, Edgar-Jones’ corseted dress – which was custom-made by creative director Andreas Kronthaler – was adorned with bows and spilled out into a cascading train; the gown was practically screaming to be hiked up and dashed across a Devonshire moor in.

With loose waves woven into her delicate half-up, half-down hair and blushed cheeks being the centrepiece of her pared-back beauty look, Edgar-Jones was the picture of elegance, but it was her storied necklace that was the real show-stopper. Around the actress’ neck hung a diamond-adorned pendant worn on a velvet choker. Designed by Frédéric Boucheron and crafted from yellow gold, silver and agate, the piece dates back to 1877 and once served as a locket, complete with a hidden compartment to hold sentimental trinkets such as the lock of a loved one’s hair.

Edgar-Jones’ ring also came courtesy of Boucheron and originated in the same year. Set in yellow gold and decorated with Louis XV-shell motifs, a closer look at the magnificent piece of jewellery revealed two figures carved into stone. The luxury French house confirmed in an Instagram post that Edgar-Jones had selected the pieces herself from Boucheron’s private collection, writing: “On the occasion of [the] Sense and Sensibility premiere held on September 15th in London, Daisy Edgar-Jones selected creations from the Boucheron private collection.”

Although it’s thought Jane Austen’s Sense and Sensibility is set between 1792 and 1797, the book's first draft was penned in 1795, which likely explains why Edgar-Jones selected jewellery from the era. Her choice speaks to a wider industry trend in recent years, with many actors opting to stay in character, sartorially speaking, while promoting films. From Margot Robbie’s Wuthering Heights promo tour, which saw the actress take inspiration from Emily Brontë’s novel by leaning heavily into Georgian-inspired and gothic fashion on red carpets, to Ariana Grande mostly sticking to Glinda pink during Wicked’s promotional run, it seems method dressing is officially the new method acting.

Sense and Sensibility lands in UK cinemas on 25 September.
