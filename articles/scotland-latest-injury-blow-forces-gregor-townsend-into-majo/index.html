Scotland latest: Injury blow forces Gregor Townsend into major reshuffle as fresh captaincy call made

TheTools · 20.11.2026 10:40 · source · permalink

Rory Darge (right) has been ruled out of Scotland's meeting with Japan, forcing Gregor Townsend (left) into a reshuffle

Scotland have suffered a crucial blow ahead of their Nations Championship Test against Japan, with Rory Darge ruled out through injury.

The Glasgow Warriors man was set to start at openside flank for Saturday’s Test in Edinburgh, and lead the side out as captain too, but he picked up a shoulder issue in training and will now miss out.

Scotland forced into late reshuffle after injury blow

That blow has led to a reshuffle of Townsend’s starting side, with Matt Fagerson coming into the group at number eight and Ollie Leatherbarrow sliding over to the flanks. Elsewhere, Freddie Douglas comes onto the bench.

The tinkering of the back-row is not the only change coming from Darge’s departure, either, with Stafford McDowall taking over the captaincy duties as well. This isn’t the first time the Glasgow centre has led the side out as skipper, either, with him previously taking over the reins for Scotland’s meetings with Portugal and Fiji as well as acting as co-captain against Canada.

Elsewhere, Ollie Smith starts at full-back, with Darcy Graham and Kyle Rowe on the wings. Captain McDowall partners Rory Hutchinson in the midfield, with Tom Jordan and Jamie Dobie forming a new-look half-back combination. The former starts at fly-half with the latter at scrum-half.

Liam McConnell joins the aforementioned Fagerson and Leatherbarrow in the Scotland back-row, while Max Williamson and Jonny Gray start at lock. Zander Fagerson, Gregor Hiddleston and Nathan McBeth are named as the run-on front-row.
