  • Tonight’s EuroMillions draw has offered players the chance to win a jackpot of £111 million.
  • To win the prize, a player had to get five main numbers plus two lucky stars.
  • In the UK, every EuroMillions entry automatically includes a unique "UK Millionaire Maker" code, entering you into an additional draw with a guaranteed £1 million winner in every draw.
  • The winning Thunderball numbers were 12, 21, 26, 27 and 37. The Thunderball was 3.
  • The winning EuroMillions numbers were 11, 12, 15, 38, and 49. The Lucky Stars were 10 and 12.
