66' – GOOOAL! VAR confirms Yamali's goal, Barcelona scores for 5:0!

TheTools · 31.10.2026 21:35 · source · permalink

Barcelona 5:0 Alaves

Barcelona continues the spectacle at the "Camp Nou", as Lamine Yamal scores his second personal goal in this La Liga match!

In the 64th minute, the Spanish talent receives a beautiful pass inside the area, creates the necessary space and with a precise shot sends the ball into the lower right corner of the goal.

However, referee Adrián Cordero signals the review of the action through VAR, temporarily putting the Catalans' celebration on hold.

🚨 Lamine Yamal makes it five 🇪🇸 Barcelona 5-0 Alavés https://t.co/iQqAzKHuAK

In the 66th minute, after a VAR check, the referee confirms the goal!

Yamal bursts into celebration as Barcelona extends lead to 5-0 over Alavés.

With two goals from Raphinha, two from Lamine Yamali and one from Fermín López, Hansi Flick's team is having an extraordinary match./GazetaExpress/
