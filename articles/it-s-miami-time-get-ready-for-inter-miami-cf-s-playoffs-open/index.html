It’s Miami Time: Get Ready for Inter Miami CF’s Playoffs Opener This Saturday Presented by Nu | Inter Miami CF

TheTools · 21.11.2026 22:56 · source · permalink

It’s Miami Time presented by Royal Caribbean!

As Inter Miami CF begins its MLS Cup title defense in the Audi 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs, show up and support the champions as the team hosts Columbus Crew this Saturday, Nov. 21 for Game 1 of the MLS Cup Playoffs Round One Best-of-3 series between the sides, with Nu providing the entertainment for the evening!

Limited Tickets Remaining!

Don’t miss the first-ever Audi MLS Cup Playoffs match at Nu Stadium and get your tickets HERE.

2026 MLS Golden Boot presented by Audi Presentation

Make sure to be in your seat ahead of the match, as Inter Miami captain Leo Messi will be presented with the 2026 MLS Golden Boot presented by Audi ahead of kick off. Messi made history by leading the league with 26 goals in 31 appearances, becoming the first player to win consecutive MLS Golden Boot presented by Audi awards.  

Get Your Inter Miami CF x Nu Bandana at the Gates!

The first 10,000 fans who enter the gates of Nu Stadium will receive a co-branded Inter Miami CF x Nu bandana!

Sit to Win!

Head to your seats by 5:50 p.m. ET! You could win a squad-signed jersey or an Inter Miami CF ball. Select balls will also be signed!

2026 Inter Miami CF Team Photo Giveaway

After the match, 10,000 fans will receive a poster of our official 2026 Inter Miami First Team photo when exiting the gates at Nu Stadium.

Nu Cardholder Promotions

Nu cardholders will receive a 10% discount on all concessions at Nu Stadium, and 10% off on purchases at the official Inter Miami CF Team Store at Nu Stadium.

Link Your Mastercard to the Wallet in Inter Miami CF App for a Chance to Win a Signed Jersey

Add your Mastercard to the Official Inter Miami CF App for a chance to win a First Team-signed jersey! Every transaction made with your registered Mastercard earns you an entry into the raffle for this special prize. Don't have the app yet? Download the Inter Miami CF app and get in the conversation: iOS | Android

Enjoy Nu’s Activations at the Baptist Health Fan Zone

Make sure to stop by Nu’s activation at the Baptist Health Fan Zone, which opens at 3 p.m. ET:

  • Customize Your Inter Miami Jersey: Make your 2026 Club jersey your own! Stop by Nu’s activation to have Nu branding pressed onto your jersey.
  • Charm Bar: Get creative and design your own bracelet featuring Nu branding and a variety of fun charms to choose from.
  • Nu Popsicles: Beat the heat with a refreshing treat! Stop by Nu’s activation to grab one of 150 complimentary popsicles available to fans.
  • Polaroid Tunnel: Capture a special matchday memory! Take a Polaroid photo and add it to the Nu Tunnel, creating a lasting memory of your experience.
  • Goalie Game: Put your skills to the test! Step up, take your shot, and see if you can score against the goalie in this fun interactive challenge!

FIFA Women's Champions Cup 2027™ at the Baptist Health Fan Zone

Get your photo with the highly-regarded FIFA Women’s Champions Cup 2027™ trophy at the Baptist Health Fan Zone, and enjoy fun games and giveaways!

50/50 Raffle: Win an Inter Miami Jersey Signed by Our Full First Team Squad! 

Enter the Inter Miami CF Foundation’s 50/50 raffle during the match for your chance to win half of the funds raised and an Inter Miami Jersey signed by First Team players!
