Kallas: ‘The escalation in the Black Sea warrants particular attention’

“Russia is not winning on the battlefield in Ukraine. On the contrary, Putin is openly targeting civilians and stepping up hybrid attacks across the rest of Europe. The escalation of attacks in the Black Sea warrants specific attention,” said the EU’s High Representative, Kaja Kallas, at the European Parliament today.

“Six attacks in 10 days, believed to have been carried out by Russian drones, mark a dangerous and unacceptable escalation in the Black Sea. Rescue teams are still searching for the seven sailors missing following the drone attack on two oil tankers near the Romanian Black Sea coast,” noted the European diplomat. “Russia is targeting Ukraine’s agricultural infrastructure, Black Sea and Danube ports, border crossings, export facilities, ships and commercial shipping routes, whilst also destroying grain storage facilities,” Kallas specified, highlighting the food crisis that all this is causing. “As emphasised by Member States at the European Council last week, Russia is deliberately turning food into a weapon and undermining global food security, particularly in Africa, Asia and the Middle East,” Kallas stated.

Prosecutor: Russian drones over the Kyiv–Odesa motorway; at least three dead

Three people have died and four others have been injured in a Russian drone attack on the Kyiv–Odesa motorway in the Kyiv region, according to Timur Tkachenko, head of the Kyiv regional office of the Prosecutor General, as reported by the Ukrainian media. Zelensky: ‘I spoke to Pope Leo’ about the Russian escalation

“I spoke with Pope Leo XIV and wished His Holiness a speedy and full recovery following his operation. We are grateful that, even at such a difficult time for him personally, His Holiness is thinking of Ukraine and praying for our people.” So said Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky on his social media accounts.

“I briefed him in detail on the situation regarding Russia’s escalation and its ongoing attacks on Ukrainian towns and communities. We also discussed the humanitarian catastrophe in Oleshky, which is currently under Russian occupation. We are working to help our people in that area and are preparing measures to address the situation. His Holiness informed me that he had been in contact with the Russian side on this matter,” Zelensky wrote.

“Of course, we also discussed the current security challenges and threats, including the recurring failure of our partners to provide warnings about Russian ballistic missile attacks – an incident that has occurred three times recently. I would like to thank His Holiness for his support and for all his efforts aimed at achieving a dignified peace,” concluded the Ukrainian leader.

Ukraine: Witkoff is in Oslo today to meet Zelensky, then he will go to see Putin

US President Donald Trump’s special envoy, Steve Witkoff, is due to meet Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky and European representatives in Oslo today to agree on a comprehensive peace proposal to be put to Russia. Witkoff then plans to travel to Moscow to meet Russian President Vladimir Putin and present the proposals to him. This was reported by Axios journalist Barak Ravid, citing a US official.

“Following his visit to Oslo, Witkoff plans to travel to Russia, where he hopes to meet President Putin to present ideas developed in close consultation with Ukraine and European partners,” Ravid reported on the social media platform X, quoting the official.

According to the US source, Witkoff has already set off for the Norwegian capital, where he will also meet Prime Minister Jonas Gahr Store, as well as representatives of the E4 group, the European Union and NATO. The aim of the talks is to draw up a comprehensive proposal that can form the basis for the resumption of negotiations with Moscow.

Jared Kushner, Trump’s son-in-law and another key figure in the US mediation efforts, will also take part in the meeting via video conference, having cancelled his trip due to the flu to avoid infecting the other participants.

Four people have been killed in Russian attacks in various parts of the country

The Ukrainian authorities have reported that, following Russian attacks, four people have lost their lives in various parts of the country. The attacks came after Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy stated that Kyiv would resume strikes on Russian refineries in response to the ongoing bombardments it has been subjected to. Three people died in the Kyiv region, according to a report by Ukraine’s State Emergency Service on Telegram. One person was killed following attacks on Zaporizhzhia, in the south-east of the country, according to the head of the regional military administration, Ivan Fedorov. The attacks left at least 11 people injured.
