25-year-old man dies after being stabbed twice in torso in Brooklyn

TheTools · 05.09.2026 01:40 · source · permalink

EAST FLATBUSH, Brooklyn (WABC) -- Police are searching for two men wanted in a deadly stabbing in Brooklyn.

It happened Friday on Albany Avenue in East Flatbush around 5:30 p.m.

The 25-year-old was stabbed twice in the torso.

Police say the suspects took off in a black sedan.
