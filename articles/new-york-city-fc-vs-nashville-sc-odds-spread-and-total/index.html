New York City FC will attempt to break a six-game winless skid across competitions this Friday evening, when the Coyotes of Nashville FC appear at Yankee Stadium for a matchup beginning at 7:30 p.m. ET.

The series between NYCFC and Nashville SC is a tight one. Nashville has four wins to NYCFC's three past victories in the matchup, but both teams have bagged goals in each of the prior four meetings. New York's loan striker Luighi makes an interesting matchup against Sam Surridge, the Coyotes' key boot.

However, there's no comparison of Friday's opponents' form at this moment. Nashville is leading MLS with 52 points, and the Coyotes' 47 goals scored are second only to Inter Miami. Nashville's defense, allowing just 17 goals and also standing first in MLS in that category, could be even more impressive than Surridge's attack. The host Pigeons, meanwhile, are in their worst slump since last spring, and most certainly performing at their lowest overall level of quality since the aftermath of NYCFC's playoff quest.

New York City FC vs Nashville SC Predictions and Picks

Andrew Leigh of the New York Soccer Journal is trying to pin NYCFC's dreadful slump on the squad's lack of rest. “Six matches between August 6 and August 23, a period that saw the club not get more than three days' rest for the final five matches,” writes Leigh. However, there may be more to it than simple fatigue.

New York was defensively weak in its last MLS home game, a 3-2 victory for the Philadelphia Union on Aug. 16. Pigeons goalkeeper Matt Freese looked helpless enough against the Union to remind supporters of that awful night against Belgium in the 2026 World Cup, even if he didn't commit any howlers of such magnitude again. It's not a good vibe going into a bout against Surridge, who's got 13 goals this year.

Take advantage of the fact that as a road team, Nashville SC can be had at spreads of “Ev” against a slumping side this Friday. Given the low probability that manager Pascal Jansen's hosts can keep Surridge off the scoreboard at this time, the Nashville spread pick has a nice margin for error.
