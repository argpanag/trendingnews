Dodgers hire Guerrero from Marlins to be hitting coach

TheTools · 10.11.2026 22:12 · source · permalink

The Los Angeles Dodgers have added Pedro Guerrero to their staff as a Major League Hitting Coach.

Guerrero, 37, joins the Dodgers after spending the last two seasons with the Marlins in the same role. He served as an assistant hitting coach for the San Francisco Giants (2022-24) and Philadelphia Phillies (2018-21) and as the bench coach for the Rookie-Advanced Ogden (2016-17) prior to joining the Marlins in 2025.

During the 2026 season, the Marlins offense ranked in the Top 10 in the Majors in various categories, including on-base percentage (.320, 6th), batting average (.249, 7th), hits (1,347, 9th) and OPS (,727, 9th).

Guerrero spent his playing career in the Dodgers’ organization, playing 411 games from 2006-13. The infielder hit .236 with 23 homers and 163 RBI, reaching Triple-A Albuquerque in 2010. He was signed in 2006 out of San Pedro de Macoris, Dominican Republic.
