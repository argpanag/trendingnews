Mayo and Kerry confirm All-Ireland rematch in London

TheTools · 17.09.2026 20:19 · source · permalink

Kerry GAA and Mayo GAA have confirmed that they will face off in an exhibition game in London this coming November.

Dubbed the 'JRL Group Presents the Mayo Kerry Global Series', the news follows approval from the GAA Central Council for the All Ireland final repeat.

The clash will take place on Saturday 28 November at the StoneX Stadium in Barnet, home of Premiership rugby outfit Saracens.

The sides famously met in the 2026 All-Ireland final as Andy Moran's green and red ended 75 years of pain to finally lift Sam Maguire. The game will also see Mayo return to England for the second time this year, having defeated London in the early rounds of the Connacht Championship.
