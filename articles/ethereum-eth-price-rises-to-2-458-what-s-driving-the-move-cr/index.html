Ethereum (ETH) Price Rises to $2,458: What’s Driving the Move? – Crypto.com

TheTools · 04.09.2026 17:32 · source · permalink

Ethereum (ETH) price has rebounded to around US$2,458, putting the US$2,500 level back within reach after its recent pullback. 

Key Takeaways

  • ETH has recovered to around US$2,458 but remains lower over seven days.
  • Falling Treasury yields and a weaker US dollar have improved sentiment towards riskier assets.
  • The US$2,500 area and the next US jobs report could help determine ETH’s next move.

The Ethereum (ETH) price move comes amid a broader crypto recovery, although Bitcoin (BTC) is currently rising slightly faster.

Why is the Ethereum price rising?

ETH’s rebound has coincided with falling US Treasury yields and a weaker US dollar. The 10-year Treasury yield dropped to around 4.74% on September 3 as traders reduced their expectations for another Federal Reserve interest-rate increase.

Lower yields can make riskier assets more attractive relative to government bonds. In line with the broader crypto market, Ethereum (ETH) gained over 30% between 17 and 21 August. However, this does not prove that monetary-policy expectations caused ETH’s move. 

What else is moving ETH?

Exchange-traded fund demand has recently weakened. US spot Ether ETFs recorded US$48.2 million in net outflows on September 2, following a much smaller US$8.6 million inflow the previous day, Farside Investors data shows. ETF flows track money entering or leaving funds that hold ETH.

This suggests the recovery was driven more by wider market sentiment than fresh ETF demand. Continued outflows could limit the rebound, particularly if bond yields begin rising again.

What’s next for the ETH price?

The US$2,500 area is the nearest level to watch. 

In July, CME Group contributor Rich Excell identified it as a resistance area for Ether futures, while warning that momentum indicators were becoming stretched after the earlier recovery. His analysis also highlighted US$2,100 as an important longer-term breakout level.

A move above US$2,500, supported by stronger ETF inflows, could improve the short-term outlook. Conversely, renewed outflows or rising yields could put US$2,400 under pressure again.

Standard Chartered’s Geoffrey Kendrick has maintained a US$4,000 year-end target, arguing that Ethereum’s network activity remains stronger than its price suggests. However, that is an analyst forecast, not a guaranteed outcome.

FAQs about Ethereum (ETH) price

Why is Ethereum going up today?

Ethereum (ETH) is rising alongside the broader crypto market as US Treasury yields fall and expectations for another Federal Reserve interest-rate increase ease.

What is driving the Ethereum price higher?

Improved market sentiment is supporting ETH, while recent ETF demand and Ethereum network activity provide a wider backdrop. However, ETF flows have weakened in the past few trading sessions.

Could Ethereum rise above US$2,500?

ETH could move above US$2,500 if broader market sentiment improves and ETF inflows recover. Renewed outflows, higher bond yields or wider market weakness could put the price under pressure instead.
