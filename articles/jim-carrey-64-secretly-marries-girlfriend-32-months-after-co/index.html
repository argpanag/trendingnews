Jim Carrey, 64, secretly marries girlfriend, 32, months after couple debut

Jim Carrey has reportedly tied the knot with longtime partner Min Ah in a private Los Angeles ceremony.

According to multiple US outlets, the Bruce Almighty star, 64, quietly married the LA-based actress and artist, 32, in a small ceremony, just months after the pair went public with their relationship in February 2026.

Jim Carrey with Min Ah at the 51st Cesar Film Awards in February 2026. Getty

The reported newlyweds, however, have been seen together since 2022.

Carrey first spoke publicly of his relationship with Min Ah, known as Minzi, at the César Awards held in Paris on February 26. 

Upon accepting an Honorary César award, the Liar Liar star thanked Min Ah as his “sublime companion” during his speech.

He then added, “I love you, Min Ah”.

Nine.com.au have contacted Carrey’s team for comment.

It was during that same event, the actor was forced to respond to bizarre theories about his appearance, including that a makeup artist had impersonated him during the event with the help of a mask.

Carrey’s publicist, Marleah Leslie, issued a blunt statement at the time saying, “Jim Carrey attended the Cesar Awards, where he accepted his Honorary Cesar Award”.

The head of the awards had also refuted the wild claims that someone had appeared in Carrey’s place, even more bizarrely, that he had been cloned.

Jim Carrey's appearance during the 51st Cesar Film Awards caused fan concern. Getty

Carrey and Min Ah were joined by the star’s daughter Jane, 38, from his marriage to Melissa Womer, and his grandson Jackson, 15.

Carrey’s reported nuptials with Min Ah, would be the third time the actor has said ‘I do’.

The two-time Golden Globe winner married Womer, an aspiring actress and former waitress at The Comedy Store, in March 1987. The welcomed daughter Jane the same year.

Carrey pictured with first wife Melissa and daughter Jane in 1991. Sygma via Getty Images

Carrey married his Dumb and Dumber co-star Lauren Holly, pictured in 1994. Ron Galella Collection via Getty

Seven years later Carrey would score his first leading film role in the 1994 movie, Ace Ventura: Pet Detective.

The exes filed for divorce in 1993 before it was finalised two years later.

In September 1996, Carrey married his Dumb and Dumber co-star Lauren Holly. The marriage lasted less than a year before Holly filed for divorce.
