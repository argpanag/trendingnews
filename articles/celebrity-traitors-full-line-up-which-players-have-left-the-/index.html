Celebrity Traitors full line-up: Which players have left the game?

TheTools · 27.10.2026 19:56 · source · permalink

Our Ardross Castle expert shares his predictions for this year’s contestants as the game gets underway

At last, the TV mega-smash The Celebrity Traitors has returned, with plenty more shocks and twists in store for the starry contestants.

The inaugural VIP version of The Traitors was a runaway success last year, pulling in a record-breaking 15 million viewers, and this second run is already proving to be bigger and better, with two more contestants and one extra episode, making it a 10-part series.

Twenty-one players headed to the Scottish Highlands, and it’s not just the largest celebrity cast to date – but an impressively heavyweight one, from Bafta and Brit award-winners to social-media stars and a frankly worrying number of comedians. All battling to win up to £100,000 for their chosen charity. But in this ultimate game of backstabbing and betrayal, who is likely to scheme and strategise their way to victory? Here’s the full treacherous team sheet. The game is afoot and the floor is yours…

Like his film character Withnail, has Richard E Grant gone on holiday by mistake? The acclaimed actor and author is an impressively big name, and at 69, this year’s oldest male player. He recently played the lord of the manor in Saltburn, so will fit right into the castle surroundings, and with his wit, will charm cast mates and viewers alike. The danger is that Grant is a bit too good at playing the villain. Is he this year’s Celia Imrie? Presumably without the flatulence. He entered the game desperate to be made a Traitor and duly got his wish. However, the over-excited thesp blew his cover immediately. His days were numbered and Grant duly became the first ever Traitor to be banished at the earliest opportunity. Surely the worst cloak-clad conspirator in the show’s history.

As French and Saunders memorably said: “Do you know who really has got it all? Well, Jerry Hall. She’s got one leg in Texas, one leg in London and the whole world in between.” At 69, the American model and actress is this year’s oldest and most glamorous player, and her castmates are likely to be starstruck by her CV, her A-list name-dropping and her high-profile marriages to Mick Jagger and Rupert Murdoch. The highly quotable diva became a castle favourite and passed the midway mark. She was ultimately voted out for her pot-stirring and all-round chaos. 
 

The Welsh actor and presenter has inhabited a raft of real-life names on-screen, from Tony Blair and Brian Clough, to David Frost and a certain Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor. How will he fare playing himself? Sheen comes across well on chat shows and should be popular inside the castle; he’s also a Traitors superfan, so he’ll know the game’s twists and turns well. A “big dog” signing who could go a long way.

Bear with, bear with. Signing up the much-loved actor and author is something of a coup, as Hart has kept a low profile since she became chronically ill with Lyme disease and ME five years ago. Best known for her self-titled BBC sitcom and role in Call The Midwife, her self-effacing humour and infectious silliness made her a hit among her fellow players. She carved out a role as “Miranda Marple” but felt heat from Joanne McNally in the contest’s home stretch. She was murdered to incriminate the Irish comic and unsettle the Faithful, to whom she’d become a mother figure. Still, we’ll always have her Little Mix/Big Mix dance routine. Such fun.

The Celebrity Traitors will mark the singer-songwriter’s first proper appearance on a reality show, but it’s been brewing for a while, thanks to his hilarious social media presence. With his self-deprecating humour and witty replies to trolls, “Blunty” has transformed his image from naff balladeer to cult hero. As a former British Army captain, he will excel in the outward bounds-style missions. In the home stretch, however, it could become a case of Goodbye My Lover.

The EastEnders actor and TV presenter made a shock comeback to Albert Square recently; now he’s sprung another surprise by heading to the Highlands. Kemp is known for hard-man roles but is brighter and more emotionally intelligent than that might suggest. His hard-hitting documentaries have taken him into war zones and prisons, so he’s unlikely to be fazed by a load of luvvies in a castle. The risk is that his tough-guy image might play against him and arouse suspicion. Get outta my pub! And also my castle! Kemp proved paranoid early on and a man possessed on the missions, but was ultimately killed with a yawn. Murdered in plain sight as he’d feared all along was a cruel blow.

This year’s cast is over-stacked with comedians but Lycett is among the more versatile. He presents, he paints, he does tongue-in-cheek consumer journalism and he’s something of a wind-up merchant. Remember his viral appearance on Sunday with Laura Kuenssberg, when he voiced unwavering support for Liz Truss and claimed to be “incredibly Right-wing”? If the Brummie mischief-maker brings his canny brain and analytical skills to bear on the game, he could be a contender. Then again, he might tease his way into trouble. So it proved. He loudly and only semi-jokingly pleaded to be recruited by the Traitors. The Faithful became paranoid enough to suspect this might have transpired. A slip of the tongue at the Round Table sealed Lycett’s fate.

The Nottingham-born actor might only be 22, but is already a major screen star, with roles in blockbuster HBO dramas Game of Thrones and The Last of Us. Ramsey could become a similar castle contestant to fellow cult actor Ruth Codd, from the last series – an entertainingly outspoken figure who eventually makes themselves a target. So it proved. Ramsey was a dogged and influential sleuth, so the Traitors eliminated the threat.

The Crawley comedian has become rather ubiquitous, spread thinly across his Radio 2 shows, presenting gigs, panel games and travelogues. He’s known for his deadpan observational stand-up style, which might make him effective at Round Tables. As a former Maths teacher, he’s smart, logical and adept at handling raucous groups of people. The risk is that his inscrutable demeanour and close friendship with cast mate Rob Beckett could put him under suspicion.

Dubbed the “Mouth of the South”, south London stand-up comedian Rob Beckett is a familiar face on TV, from panel games to travelogues, and he also co-hosts hit podcast Parenting Hell. Two things could count against him: his unrepentantly gobby nature and his close friendship with Ranganathan. Tight alliances in the castle can be viewed with suspicion. Beckett was a loyal if ineffectual Faithful and was eventually murdered during the deadly game of midnight hide-and-seek.

The journalist and broadcaster is used to firing questions at undergraduates on University Challenge and politicians on the Today programme. He surely would have done the same to suspected Traitors, but this has already backfired. After going hard for Richard E Grant, the Traitors took him out at the first opportunity, slamming the coffin lid on Rajan’s castle stint after forcing him to plead for his life.

Could the nerdy Kettering-born comedian be this year’s Alan Carr? Acaster was among the first cast members whose name was leaked, sparking excitement among his cult fanbase. Acaster presents popular foodie podcast Off Menu with Ed Gamble, host of spin-off show Traitors Uncloaked, so he has an inside track on the game. His surreal humour and riffing rants make him something of a wildcard, but he’s the sort of dogged character who could become an effective “Traitor hunter”. That’s why the cloaked conspirators invited him to join them or die. Acaster duly survived at Amol Rajan’s expense and became a reluctant Traitor. But will he make an effective one?

The flame-haired mathematician has carved out a thriving broadcasting career as a science and technology expert. The Cambridge graduate’s academic credentials might damage her chances, since brainboxes can be perceived as a threat inside the castle. However, David Olusoga went all the way to the final last year, and Fry is less shy and wittier. It could just about carry her a long way, and when Richard E Grant left with his thespy tail between his legs, Fry and her spreadsheets were recruited to the turret. However, she never shook off suspicion, mainly due to the “big brain theory”, and bit the dust at the Round Table in episode five.

The popular Lancastrian actress is best known for portraying Hayley Cropper in Coronation Street, but she has built an impressive career since departing the cobbles, with roles in the likes of Mr Bates vs The Post Office, Broadchurch and Happy Valley. She’s also the narrator of The Weakest Link, so has a working relationship with the gameshow’s host, Romesh Ranganathan. Nobody will suspect the warm, down-to-earth Hesmondhalgh of being a Traitor, which initially worked in her favour. But being awarded a shield as the most trustworthy player aroused suspicions that it had actually anointed her as a Traitor. When she spearheaded the banishment of Myha’la, who was a Faithful, Julie came under further scrutiny and lost out to Professor Hannah Fry in a close vote.

Actress Myha’la Herrold, known mononymously as Myha’la, might be American, but she’s well known to Britons as the star of high-finance drama Industry, in which she plays ruthlessly ambitious banker Harper Stern. (She has also appeared in Black Mirror and horror film Bodies Bodies Bodies.) Something of an unknown quantity off-camera, Myha’la could find that her fellow players judge her by her best-known character and are naturally wary. It didn’t help when she defended Richard E Grant and gave him a wink at Round Table. Momentum gathered behind her banishment and she was voted out by a landslide.

The Irish comic signing up is hardly the biggest surprise. She accidentally hinted at it on My Therapist Ghosted Me, the podcast she co-hosts with pal Vogue Williams, letting slip: “I have a job coming up where I’m not allowed to have a phone on.” McNally is razor-sharp but a self-confessed motormouth, which could very well lead to her downfall.

Among the least-known players is this 24-year-old musician and actor from Oxford. Croft started his career in West End musicals before bagging the role of young Ned Stark in Game of Thrones, and since then he’s found cult fame as Ben Hope in Netflix romantic drama Heartstopper. His youth and lack of fame-factor could both be drawbacks and mean he’s beheaded early, a bit like Sean Bean.

The broadcaster and entrepreneur will bring the fashionista factor to the Scottish Highlands, so expect her outfits to garner copious column inches alongside Winkleman’s. However, Jama is also shrewd and determined, and should not be underestimated. As the presenter of Love Island, she’s familiar with the machinations of reality TV: she’ll stay cool at the Round Table and fight her corner. Swapping the villa for the Scottish castle is arguably her highest-profile gig yet. She was quietly thrilled to get the fateful shoulder-tap from Winkleman and is currently coolly bossing the Traitors’ turret while Grant panic-sweats.

The singer-songwriter is best known as a member of girl group Little Mix, who formed on The X Factor back in 2011. She’s smart and outspoken on social issues: such feistiness could make her a surprise package in the castle. Another young female pop star Cat Burns went all the way to the final last year. Could Pinnock follow in her Highland footsteps?

Flying the flag for Scotland is Glaswegian actress Sharon Rooney, who shot to fame with her award-winning breakout role in underrated teen drama My Mad Fat Diary. Rooney has since become a regular TV presence in the likes of Nightsleeper, Daddy Issues and Two Doors Down. She’s smart, and slyly funny, but describes herself as a “shy person” and a “home-bird”, so it will be intriguing to see how she takes to such a high-profile platform. At least she won’t have far to travel home.

It’s compulsory in reality TV nowadays to cast one “content creator” or “influencer” in a blatant bid to appeal to youth audiences. Here the job falls to YouTube prankster whose real name is Kenny Ojuederie. As Niko Omilana discovered last year, though, being a showbiz outsider doesn’t do you many favours inside the castle. Kenny looked like an early exit waiting to happen but survived to become a trusted, effective Faithful - until he was recruited to the dark side to replace Hannah Fry.
