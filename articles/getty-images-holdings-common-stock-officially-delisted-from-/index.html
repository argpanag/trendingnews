On October 2, 2026, the New York Stock Exchange filed a formal notification to delist Getty Images Holdings, Inc. common stock from its listing and registration under Section 12(b) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934.

Key Highlights

  • Security affected: Getty Images Holdings, Inc. Common Stock is the security class being removed from the exchange.
  • Exchange action: The New York Stock Exchange LLC confirmed compliance with its regulations to delist and withdraw registration of the securities.
  • Regulatory provision: The delisting was initiated under 17 CFR 240.12d2-2(b), the regulatory rule permitting exchange-initiated removal.
  • Commission File Number: The issuer’s file number is 001-41453.
  • Signing authority: The notification was signed by Anthony Sozzi, Analyst, Market Watch, representing the Exchange.

NYSE Officially Removes Getty Images Common Stock from Exchange Listing

The New York Stock Exchange LLC submitted a Form 25 notification on October 2, 2026, regarding Getty Images Holdings, Inc., a Seattle-based visual content and media company. The issuer is identified by Commission File Number 001-41453, with Common Stock as the class of securities being delisted.

The filing specifies that the NYSE invoked 17 CFR 240.12d2-2(b), which authorizes the exchange to initiate removal of a security class from listing and registration. The Exchange certified adherence to its internal rules for this delisting process. This notification and any accompanying notices fulfill compliance with 17 CFR 240.19d-1 as outlined in the filing instructions.

Anthony Sozzi, Analyst, Market Watch, signed the notification on behalf of the New York Stock Exchange LLC as the authorized representative. The filing does not indicate any voluntary withdrawal by Getty Images under 17 CFR 240.12d2-2(c.

Disclosure Summary

On October 2, 2026, the NYSE notified the SEC of its decision to remove Getty Images Holdings, Inc. common stock (NYSE:GETY) GETY (NYSE:GETY) from listing and registration, citing regulatory provision 17 CFR 240.12d2-2(b).
