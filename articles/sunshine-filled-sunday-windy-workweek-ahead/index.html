Sunshine-Filled Sunday, Windy Workweek Ahead

TheTools · 15.11.2026 12:40 · source · permalink

Weather

Sunday Evening Update:

We hope you enjoyed your weekend! If you have any other plans to finish out your Sunday, you will definitely want to grab your jacket. Temperatures will be rather seasonable, but still chilly.

A high pressure system is currently moving in, so we will be seeing some changes ahead, including clearer skies. By 2AM, hardly any clouds will be present, and we will not see many over the next couple days.

Winds will still remain on the gusty side for the remainder of the evening. Fortunately, that high pressure system will be moving in rather soon, which will result in us having all around calmer of conditions to start off the workweek.

Our nightly lows will be occurring just before sunrise, around 5AM. They are just a few degrees below what is normal for this time of year, so nothing too out of the ordinary.

Tomorrow morning will start off very similar to this morning. The main difference is there will be more sunshine to start, with winds calming down as we head towards the afternoon.

After tomorrow, we return into the 60s for those high temperatures!

Sunday Morning:

Good morning! It has been a very breezy weekend and we are still seeing the pattern continue this morning! Our temperatures are currently in the 20s, 30s and 40s as of 7:30 this morning.

When we factor in the high wind speeds, our “Feels Like” temperatures are once again tumbling. You will want to bundle up for any outdoor plans and as you are headed out the door today.

If you have been waiting for a warm up and calmer wind speeds, I have great news! Today and tomorrow will be quite breezy, but we get a brief break Tuesday before one lone windy day on Wednesday.

Along with the wind speeds settling down, our temperatures will rebound towards the middle and latter part of this upcoming work week.

Futurecast shows that along with the upcoming temperature pendulum swing, high pressure will take control of the area and keep us rain free for the entirety of the next week. It will be beautiful week to get outside, with abundant sunshine!

Our 7 Day looks stunning. Today we will see a bit of remaining cloud cover for the NRV, Roanoke and Highlands Zones this morning, but that cloud cover will burn off by lunchtime.

Lynchburg and Southside Zones you will stay cloud free and a bit breezy all day long today! That will be the case for all 5 of our zones for the remainder of the week! Have a great Sunday!
