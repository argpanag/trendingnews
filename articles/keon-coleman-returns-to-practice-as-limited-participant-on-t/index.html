Keon Coleman returns to practice as limited participant on Thursday

TheTools · 01.10.2026 20:51 · source · permalink

The Bills got one of their key offensive players back on the field on Thursday.

Receiver Keon Coleman was a limited participant in the day’s session after he was sidelined for Wednesday’s practice with an ankle injury.

Coleman, in his third season, has caught eight passes for 101 yards so far in 2026.

Linebacker Joe Andreessen (knee) and defensive lineman T.J. Sanders (illness) were also upgraded from DNP to limited.

Defensive lineman Deone Walker (quad) was upgraded from limited to full.

Cornerback Christian Benford (toe) missed a second consecutive practice.

Safety Damar Hamlin (ribs) and receiver DJ Moore (shoulder) remained limited.

Linebacker Terrel Bernard (knee), outside linebacker Bradley Chubb (thumb), guard Ar’maj Reed-Adams (elbow), and linebacker Dorian Williams (hip) remained full.

Running back Ray Davis (hamstring) was added to the report as a full participant. And defensive back Sam Franklin missed practice for personal reasons.

The Bills will host the Patriots on Sunday afternoon.
