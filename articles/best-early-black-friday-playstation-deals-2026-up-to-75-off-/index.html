Best early Black Friday PlayStation deals 2026: Up to 75% off PS5, PS4, and PS VR2 games

TheTools · 04.11.2026 11:56 · source · permalink

If you were planning to wait until the end of the month to pad your console library, Sony just pulled the trigger early. The PlayStation Store launched its official Black Friday 2026 sale on Nov. 4, bringing discounts of up to 75% across thousands of digital games, DLC packs, and expansions for PS5, PS4, and PS VR2.

The entire promotion runs through Nov. 30 at 11:59 p.m. local time, with Sony scheduling a mid-sale lineup refresh on Nov. 16. Just keep an eye on individual store listings before you buy: several early deals, including high-profile editions of Diablo IV: Lord of Hatred, NBA 2K27, and Silent Hill: Townfall, are scheduled to expire early on Nov. 20 before rotating out.

To save you from combing through thousands of digital store pages, here are our top picks worth grabbing before their limited-time launch prices rotate out.

Diablo IV: Lord of Hatred (Standard Edition)

Grabbing the Diablo IV: Lord of Hatred Standard Edition is easily one of the best values in the entire sale. The bundle packages both major storyline chapters — Vessel of Hatred and Lord of Hatred — into a single release, pitting you directly against Mephisto across the ancient Skovos region with two long-awaited playable classes: the holy Paladin and demon-binding Warlock.

Alongside substantial endgame reworks, transmutation crafting, and gear set bonuses, picking it up grants instant access to an extra stash tab, two bonus character slots for fresh seasonal builds, and three exclusive World of Warcraft decor items.

Astro Bot

Picking up Astro Bot during the sale is an absolute no-brainer for anyone who owns a PS5. When Astro’s mothership gets ambushed, and its crew gets scattered across the cosmos, you set off across more than 50 inventive planets to rescue stranded bots and patch the ship back together.

It serves as a love letter to console history — pairing you with iconic PlayStation legends — while also serving as the gold standard for DualSense immersion. Handcrafted power-ups like spring-loaded boxing gloves, booster rockets, and an oversized sponge turn every stage into a playground of creative platforming.

Silent Hill: Townfall

Grabbing Silent Hill: Townfall on sale gives psychological horror fans a fresh, claustrophobic spin on the franchise. Set against the rain-soaked backdrop of 1996 Scotland, it marks the series' first full-length entry played entirely in first person as you explore the fog-covered town of St. Amelia.

Survival leans on tense evasion, desperate melee scraps, and the CRTV — a pocket television that replaces the classic radio to decode eerie static signals and reveal hidden story clues. Backed by a reactive Pilotpriest soundtrack and branching endings, it delivers pure analog dread from start to finish.

NBA 2K27 (Deluxe Edition)

The NBA 2K27 Deluxe Edition solves the mode's biggest barrier to entry: the slow, brutal grind to make your custom baller competitive. Dropping 100,000 Virtual Currency (VC) into your balance lets you instantly soup up your MyPLAYER's key attributes, whether that's locking down perimeter defense or draining deep threes, without playing dozens of frustrating, underpowered games.

The included MyCAREER care package keeps that momentum rolling with 25 bundles across six skill boosts, 25 triple-pack Gatorade stamina boosts, a two-hour 2XP coin, and wearable Victor Wembanyama and Caitlin Clark jerseys. If you also like piecing together fantasy lineups in MyTEAM, the five Series 1 packs, Full '27 team selection, and Free Agent pack give you a competitive roster right out of the box.

Marvel’s Wolverine Digital Deluxe Edition

Grabbing Marvel’s Wolverine Digital Deluxe Edition on sale gives PS5 players the definitive way to experience Insomniac’s brutal, globe-trotting mutant epic. Logan’s journey pulls him back into Team X to stop Bolivar Trask’s mutant-hunting Reavers, slicing through incredible set pieces across Canada, Tokyo, and Madripoor alongside iconic rivals like Omega Red.

The combat lets you build up sheer Rage momentum to shred enemies with fluid adamantium claw strikes and swappable Techniques. Going with the Deluxe Edition loads you up right out of the gate: you get five exclusive suits (including Age of Apocalypse and Savage), five custom claw variants, four bonus Technique Points to kickstart your skill tree, and early unlocks for Logan’s classic brown suit and reflective claws.
