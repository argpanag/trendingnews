Man fatally shot on Green Line train near St. Paul-Minneapolis border

TheTools · 04.10.2026 14:59 · source · permalink

He was shot on a train traveling from the Stadium Village station in Minneapolis and the train stopped at Westgate station in St. Paul

A man was shot on a light-rail train in Minneapolis near the St. Paul border and died at the scene Saturday night, according to Minneapolis police.

Officers responded to reports of a shooting on a Green Line train traveling from the Stadium Village station in Minneapolis toward St. Paul. The train stopped at Westgate station near Emerald and Berry streets in St. Paul.

Metro Transit police officers found a man with at least one gunshot wound. EMS personnel and St. Paul firefighters attempted lifesaving measures at the scene, according to police.

The suspect reportedly exited the train shortly after the shooting, police said. An arrest had not been announced as of Sunday afternoon.

Minneapolis homicide investigators are working to determine what led to the shooting.

“Our hearts are with everyone affected by this senseless loss,” according to a Metro Transit statement. “Gun violence has no place on our transit system or in the communities we serve. Incidents like this are rare, but one is too many, and this fatality reinforces why our work to strengthen safety on transit is relentless and ongoing.”

Metro Transit increased its safety presence on Sunday, including additional officers at stations and on trains.

Minneapolis police asked anyone with information to contact them at policetips@minneapolismn.gov or 612-673-5845 to leave a voicemail. Anonymous tips can be submitted to CrimeStoppers at 800-222-TIPS (8477) or CrimeStoppersMN.org. Information leading to an arrest and conviction may be eligible for a financial reward.
