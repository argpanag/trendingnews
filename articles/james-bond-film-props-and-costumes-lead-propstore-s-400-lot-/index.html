James Bond film props and costumes lead Propstore's 400-lot auction | The James Bond Dossier

TheTools · 17.10.2026 10:10 · source · permalink

Home > News >

Paloma’s No Time to Die dress, a 14-volume set of Fleming first editions, and the first Dr No poster are among the auction highlights.

A suit worn by Daniel Craig in the opening chase of Skyfall is one of the headline lots in Propstore’s James Bond auction. The grey Tom Ford suit is estimated at £20,000 to £40,000 and comes with the shirt, tie and cufflinks Craig wore on screen. Bond wears it as he pursues Patrice through Istanbul, first by motorbike and then aboard a moving train.

The suit was previously sold at Christie’s in 2022, after being consigned directly by Eon Productions. Another leading film lot is Paloma’s navy silk dress and silver sandals from No Time to Die, also estimated at £20,000 to £40,000. Designed by Michael Lo Sordo, the outfit is accompanied by a certificate of authenticity from former Eon Archive Director Meg Simmonds. Ana de Armas wears it as Paloma joins Bond to infiltrate a SPECTRE party in Cuba.

The literary highlight is a complete set of 14 Jonathan Cape first-edition, first-impression Bond books by Ian Fleming, estimated at £20,000 to £40,000. The collection runs from Casino Royale to Octopussy and the Living Daylights, taking in all 12 novels and both short-story collections. The books have been rebound in full morocco leather, with gilt designs based on their original covers. The Live and Let Die copy is ex-library.

A British quad poster for Dr No, estimated at £8,000 to £16,000, returns to the first Bond film. Mitchell Hooks’ design features Sean Connery against a vivid yellow background. Propstore describes it as the first poster produced for the Bond series. Other lots include an Omega Seamaster from Tomorrow Never Dies, a Lada Niva from The World Is Not Enough and a toxin vial from Moonraker.

Propstore’s online auction includes 400 lots spanning every Bond film. Bidding closes on 27th October.
