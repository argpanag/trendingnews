PNG youth rescued in Kiribati after 41 days adrift at sea - ABC Pacific

TheTools · 27.10.2026 20:00 · source · permalink

A Papua New Guinean man has been rescued off Kiribati, 41 days after he set out on a fishing trip with a relative from their home in north Bougainville.

Luka Rahan was found yesterday afternoon by a group of Kiribati fishermen, his boat drifting between the islands of Tarawa and Maiana.

The 18-year-old was severely dehydrated and alone, his cousin was lost overboard earlier in the week.

Luka was taken to the capital, Tarawa, where he is recovering.

Jonathan Anthony Kunau, a Papua New Guinean engineer who works in Tarawa, rushed to the hospital when he heard about the rescue.

"He's from my country, he's my countryman," he said. "I need to be there for him, make him feel at home."
