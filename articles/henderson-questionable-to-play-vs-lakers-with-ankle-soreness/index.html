Henderson Questionable to Play vs Lakers With Ankle Soreness

TheTools · 27.10.2026 00:30 · source · permalink
  • Blazers Injury Report

The injury report for Tuesday night.

Portland Trail Blazers guard Scoot Henderson is questionable to play on the road Tuesday against the Los Angeles Lakers due to left ankle soreness, the organization announced Monday night.

Blazers reserve center Branden Carlson has also been upgraded to questionable to play as he remains in concussion protocol.

Elsewhere on the injury report, Portland’s three two-way contract players are out for Tuesday’s matchup due to their G League duties.
