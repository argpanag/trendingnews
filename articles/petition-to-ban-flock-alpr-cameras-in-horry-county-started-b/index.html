HORRY COUNTY, S.C. (WPDE) — As the debate over Flock and Automatic License Plate Recognition, or ALPR, cameras grows, there's now a petition to ban them in Horry County.

Conway resident Chuck Hooks started the petition on change.org.

The veteran tells ABC 15 he's pro-police and understands the technology can be useful in solving some criminal cases, but believes our personal right to privacy is more important than creating a constant surveillance state.

"It's that one officer who uses it to harass an ex or stalk her and takes away her privacy and her comfort of being able to live freely," Hooks said. "To me that doesn't override some of the good stuff it can do."
