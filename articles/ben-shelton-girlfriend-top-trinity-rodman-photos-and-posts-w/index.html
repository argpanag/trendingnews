Ben Shelton's girlfriend, Trinity Rodman, is not shy about expressing her love for the tennis pro.

She even wore an "I heart my boyfriend" shirt to the U.S. Open, where Shelton is competing in the September 13 final against German tennis pro Alexander Zverev. Rodman has not kept her feelings for Shelton private over the years, frequently gushing about him on social media.

Rodman is an Olympic soccer star and social media influencer who has 1 million followers on her Instagram page. She is the daughter of NBA legend Dennis Rodman, but she has accused him of being an absentee father and praised her mom instead. In contrast, her posts about Shelton are all positive. (For his part, Zverev is dating a German tennis star named Sophia Thomalla; he has denied abuse accusations from two former girlfriends in the past.)

Here are Trinity Rodman's top social media posts and photos about Ben Shelton.

Trinity Rodman's Post About 'Googly Eyes'

In July 2025, Rodman posted a photo showing her sitting next to Shelton. In it, they gaze into each other's eyes. "Watching you with googly eyes😍🤪❤️," she wrote in the caption.

Trinity Rodman's Post About 'Drive, Dedication and Commitment'

Rodman posted a photo showing her walking with Shelton on a tennis court. "Drive, dedication and commitment. Back to back Canada champ! Celebrate every win baby❤️I love you," she wrote in August.

Ben Shelton's Girlfriend Calls Herself His #1 Fan

Trinity posted another photo showing her with Shelton on the tennis court and wrote, "#1 fan I love you ❤️"

Trinity Rodman Posts Another Photo Showing Her Supporting Ben Shelton

"Just cuz 🤍 @benshelton," Rodman wrote with a photo showing them interacting near a tennis court.

"The cutest couple," a fan wrote in the comment thread, while another person commented that Shelton and Rodman are "adorable" together.

'To the Most Beautiful Soul': Trinity Rodman's Birthday Tribute to Ben Shelton

Rodman posted a video tribute to Shelton on his birthday. "To the most beautiful soul I've ever met. Happy birthday Benny! You've changed me, loving me the way I never knew someone could," she wrote.

"Even with your lifestyle you make me a priority every chance you get and I'll always be grateful for every minute you love me. You inspire me everyday, and you always keep a smile on my face. Life with you is a dream. I love you NL❤️"

Their Vacation Photos

Trinity posted a collage of vacation photos showing her kissing Shelton in a swimming suit. She wrote, "Not the summer that I had in mind but still loved every moment ❤️ can't wait to be back on the field."
