Cyberpunk 2077 Update 2.5 Adds Clampdown, a Night City Prison Quest | HappyGamer

TheTools · 04.11.2026 21:02 · source · permalink

Back in September 2022, CD Projekt Red named Cyberpunk 2077‘s Update 1.6 after the Netflix anime Edgerunners and added gear straight from the show. The official Cyberpunk 2077 account on X announced on November 4, 2026 that a new quest called Clampdown is now live in the Edgerunners 2 Update (2.5).

“Getting into Night City’s prison is easy. Getting out? That’s another story. The Clampdown quest is now available in Cyberpunk 2077 with the Edgerunners 2 Update (2.5). A CD PROJEKT RED – BRASIL and @Cicero_VP collaboration.” – @CyberpunkGame

“Getting into Night City’s prison is easy. Getting out? That’s another story. The Clampdown quest is now available in Cyberpunk 2077 with the Edgerunners 2 Update (2.5). A CD PROJEKT RED – BRASIL and @Cicero_VP collaboration.” – @CyberpunkGame

Clampdown Puts the Focus on Breaking Out of Night City’s Prison

The teaser sets up the whole premise in two short lines, and both of them lean hard on the idea of a breakout. Getting inside the prison is described as the easy part, while getting back out is framed as the real test of the quest.

The studio hasn’t shared rewards or a full plot summary yet, so the escape angle comes from that teaser line alone. Notably, the quest is listed as available now, which means current owners can head straight into it once the 2.5 update finishes installing.

CD PROJEKT RED BRASIL Shares the Credit With @Cicero_VP

The announcement credits Clampdown to a collaboration between CD PROJEKT RED BRASIL and the X account @Cicero_VP. Putting a regional team and an outside partner on a single quest gives this one a different feel from the studio’s usual in-house story drops.

Meanwhile, the Edgerunners 2 name ties the whole update back to the anime branding the studio first used in 2022. That earlier update leaned on the show to pull lapsed players back into Night City, and this one clearly carries the same banner forward.

Almost Six Years After the December 2020 Launch

Steam lists Cyberpunk 2077’s release date as December 9, 2020, which puts this update almost six years after the game first came out. That launch went badly enough that Sony pulled the game from the PlayStation Store barely a week later, and it didn’t return until June 2021.

The game’s track record since then has been a long climb back, with Update 2.0 and the Phantom Liberty expansion both landing in September 2023. The Steam store page currently shows a Metacritic score of 86 on PC, and the base game sits at $59.99 at the time of writing.

That history is what makes a quest like Clampdown land the way it does for longtime players. A game that once got pulled from a storefront within days of launch is now handing out prison break stories in its sixth year, credited to partners outside the main studio.
