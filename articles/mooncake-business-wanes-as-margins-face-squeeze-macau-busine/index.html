Mooncake business wanes as margins face squeeze | Macau Business

TheTools · 25.09.2026 06:37 · source · permalink

By Aries Un

It’s the time of the year moon cakes are back on the dining table, amid lackluster sales and compressed margins though.

Friday, or 25 September, is the Mid-Autumn Festival, a traditional harvest and family reunion celebration in the Chinese calendar.

According to a Friday report from Macao Daily News, a bakery owner flagged a year-on-year rise of nearly 20 per cent in raw materials.

The unnamed source cited also highlighted a more competitive landscape, with most corporate clients preferring a ‘greater value for money’ than ever.

Another service provider cited by the newspaper also echoed the less optimistic sentiment, saying that ‘corporations used to order thousands of mooncake vouchers as gifts, but large-bulk orders have drastically fallen in recent years,’ the media outlet reported.

A further profitability squeeze is also unavoidable as the connectivity between Macau and Hong Kong increases, the source added, underscoring a possible ‘price war’ among industry players.

In comparison with the picture a few years ago, there could be a fall of 20 to 30 per cent in turnover, the source also said.

In the gaming industry, a check by the MNA reveals that the moon cake boxes without festive add-ons being sold by the casino operators range between MOP398 (US$49.32) and MOP668.

Information supplied by a local Chinese restaurant told Macao Daily that it was relying on orders from corporations, with retail interest having appeared to be lukewarm.

However, a slide in orders has become evident over the years, the source said.
