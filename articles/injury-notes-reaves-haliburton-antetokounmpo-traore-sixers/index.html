Injury Notes: Reaves, Haliburton, Antetokounmpo, Traore, Sixers

TheTools · 24.11.2026 05:22 · source · permalink

Austin Reaves will miss a fifth straight game for the Lakers on Monday due to a left heel bruise, the team announced, per Jovan Buha (Twitter link). Still, head coach JJ Redick is seeing positive results from Reaves’ rehab work and said the guard was a full participant in practice on Sunday, notes Law Murray of The Athletic (Twitter video link).

“He’s made progress every day. They’re doing a great job of figuring out the most comfortable insert, the most comfortable shoe, the right combination,” Redick said. “…It’s really just a comfort and pain issue, and it’s getting better every day.”

Redick added that the team has struggled offensively over the last seven games, in large part due to Reaves’ absence, according to Benjamin Royer of The Orange County Register.

“Who would have thought that, you know, not having an elite offensive player, an All-Star level player would hurt your offense,” Redick deadpanned.

We have more injury notes from around the league:

  • The Pacers are still without star point guard Tyrese Haliburton, who is hoping to return from his left hamstring strain before the end of November, Tony East writes for Forbes. Indiana has dropped to 5-11 without Haliburton, heading into Sunday’s matchup with the Knicks. Also of note is that the extended absence will put the point guard at risk of missing the 65-game minimum required for award consideration and super-max eligibility. He will likely be at 14 games missed by Thanksgiving, which would only give him three games of wiggle room for the rest of the season.
  • Giannis Antetokounmpo missed the Heat’s win over the Thunder on Sunday due to right groin tendinitis, but head coach Erik Spoelstra is not overly concerned about the absence extending to Wednesday’s matchup with the Sixers, according to Eric Nehm of The Athletic (via Twitter). Spoelstra said that he’s optimistic that the star forward will play against Philadelphia, adding that Antetokounmpo “almost played” on Sunday.
  • Nolan Traore will miss the Nets’ matchup on Monday with the Timberwolves due to a right hamstring strain, C.J. Holmes reports for the New York Daily News (Twitter link). Traore played just under 18 minutes in Friday’s loss to the Magic after suffering the injury in the third quarter.
  • The Sixers have had players in and out of the lineup lately, but they will receive a series of reinforcements for Monday’s game against the Trail Blazers, as Jaylen Brown (hamstring), Dean Wade (hamstring), and Adem Bona (neck) are all probable to return, per Adam Aaronson of the Philly Voice. Brown dealt with injuries to both his head and hamstring in Monday’s game against the Nets and subsequently missed Philadelphia’s next two contests.
