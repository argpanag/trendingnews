Arteta on Fleetwood, Gyokeres and basics

TheTools · 27.10.2026 20:02 · source · permalink

Mikel Arteta said our basics and habits "must be through the roof" if we are to progress in the Carabao Cup as we head to Fleetwood Town.

After defeating Ipswich in round three, we face a trip to Highbury Stadium for our first-ever meeting with the League Two side.

It's our first visit to a fourth division side in the competition since losing to Bradford City in 2012, where Mikel was an unused substitute, and the boss knows everything is on the line as we head to Highbury.

He said: "Our reputation, our pride, everything is in play. So every time you play a football match, and especially against a team in a different division, respect the game, respect every opponent because they deserve to do that, and then try to win and try to play a bit better than them.

"We all have one [upset] at some stage. We know the risk, and in football, things can get very equal the moment you are able to match certain things, and that's why our basics and habits have to be through the roof to be able to win the game."

Viktor Gyokeres started our 2-0 win over Everton, his first Premier League start after starting in the third round against Ipswich.

While Viktor is yet to get off the mark this season, Mikel is seeing positive signs from the Swedish striker.

"He's done a lot of good things," Mikel said shortly after our win over the Toffees. "We need to keep insisting. I think he had a really good contribution in Munich as well when he came on, he helped us to change the game, and that's what we need from all of them."

Max Dowman was voted player of the round thanks to his two-goal contribution against the Tractor Boys at Portman Road, and will be looking to do the same again in round four.

Another player hoping to add to his appearance tally this campaign is Cristhian Mosquera. The defender came off with an injury during our win at Villa Park in August but has since recovered to feature in the last three matchday squads.

Speaking on his contribution, Mikel said: "When he has played, he's done really well. We have won some big games playing with him, and we need him. The same with Myles [Lewis-Skelly] in another way and looking at the way he's playing. 

"Or Max, today he came in [against Everton] and he seems to be very comfortable. In this kind of position. We're going to need everybody. I'm convinced of that."
