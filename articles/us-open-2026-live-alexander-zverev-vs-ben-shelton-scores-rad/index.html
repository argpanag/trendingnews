  10. Why the serve will be keypublished at 19:16 BST
    19:16 BST

    Zverev v Shelton

    Whoever brings their A game on serve today may well walk away as the champion.

    The two finalists lead the way in terms of aces served in the men's draw in New York - Alexander Zverev has hit 87 and Ben Shelton 71.

    Before the start of the US Open, only Taylor Fritz (687) had served more aces, external on the ATP Tour this year than Zverev (573), while the German was also second on the list for percentage of first serves in, external (71.45%).

    Left-hander Shelton was seventh (472 aces) and eighth (67.35% first serves in) respectively for those metrics on the tour, and is one of the biggest servers in the sport.

  18. Improving Zverev into US Open finalpublished at 18:56 BST
    18:56 BST

    Zverev v Shelton

    With Jannik Sinner out injured and Carlos Alcaraz making his return after four months out injured, Alexander Zverev entered the US Open as the number one seed and - for many - the player to beat.

    His first two matches went five sets, but he has not lost a set since.

    "After the second round, at about 3am I was sitting with my team in the locker room and we were laughing at how bad I was playing," Zverev said.

    "That sense of humour helped me. I obviously have improved a bit otherwise I would not be in the US Open final."
