LA Lakers vs Atlanta Hawks: Where to watch Monday's game and what time Los Angeles plays at State Farm Arena

TheTools · 09.11.2026 16:51 · source · permalink

Western Conference-leading Lakers visit a red-hot Hawks team at State Farm Arena

The Los Angeles Lakers, the NBA’s best team by record, land in Atlanta to face a Hawks squad arriving with the engine running hot. A clash of streaks in the heart of the Southeast.

There are games that matter more because of where each team stands than because of the schedule, and this Atlanta Hawks vs. Los Angeles Lakers falls squarely into that category. Two franchises going through a November on the rise, two offensive styles that promise a high scoreline, and a venue, State Farm Arena, that promises atmosphere.

The Lakers arrive as the team with the best record in the entire league; the home team, as the undisputed king of its division. The result could shake up both the top of the West and the hierarchy of the East.

When and where will you be able to watch the Lakers vs. Hawks game?

The matchup takes place this Monday, November 9, 2026, at 7:00 p.m. Eastern Time (6:00 p.m. Central Time and 4:00 p.m. Pacific Time) at State Farm Arena in Atlanta, a venue with a capacity of 18,118 spectators.

In the United States, the local broadcast of the game will be handled by Atlanta News First and Peachtree Sports Network in Atlanta, while Spectrum SportsNet will have it for Lakers fans. In addition, you can stream it on Fubo and with NBA League Pass.

How do Los Angeles and Atlanta come into today's game? Preview of the matchup

The numbers say a lot. The Lakers are in command of the Western Conference with a record of 9 wins and 2 losses (.818), the best mark in the entire NBA along with Philadelphia. Their offense is one of the most prolific in the league: 1,341 points scored in 11 games, with a +82 differential.

Atlanta, for their part, comfortably lead the Southeast Division with 7 wins and 4 losses (.636), holding a 2.5-game lead over Miami and Washington.

The Hawks are a scoring machine: their 1,305 points place them among the most offensive teams in the league, although they allow many as well, a sign that these matchups often turn into basket-scoring festivals.

Overall, Los Angeles ranks first among the 30 franchises, while Atlanta appears in 12th place.

The Lakers are coming in hot. They have won eight of their last nine games, with their only stumble coming against Portland along the way. During that stretch they beat Golden State, Toronto, Miami, Sacramento and, in their most recent game, won on the road against the Detroit Pistons 121-116. This is an offense that rarely scores fewer than 120 points.

Atlanta is also on the rise. After a rough patch in late October, the Hawks put together a solid response: they beat Cleveland, Indiana, Washington and, in their last outing, claimed a thrilling 124-121 home win over Brooklyn.

Four wins in their last five games put them back on the confidence track just before hosting the West leaders.

Names to watch in the Lakers and Hawks lineups

The inevitable spotlight will be on Luka Dončić, the Slovenian star of the Lakers and the engine of that offense that dominates the league. Alongside him, Austin Reaves provides scoring volume in the backcourt, while Collin Sexton and Quentin Grimes add depth on the perimeter.

In the paint, center Walker Kessler is the defensive and rebounding anchor the Angelenos need to slow the home team's transition pace.

In Atlanta's trenches, forward Jalen Johnson embodies the new generation of the Hawks, a 24-year-old player capable of doing it all on the court.

Veteran shooting guard CJ McCollum brings firepower and experience from the perimeter, Australian Dyson Daniels has established himself as one of the league's best on-ball defenders, and center Onyeka Okongwu anchors the interior game.

They are a young, athletic group that runs: exactly the kind of opponent that can make anyone uncomfortable at home.

What’s at stake in the NBA standings

For the Lakers, the objective is clear: to maintain the lead in the West, where Oklahoma City, San Antonio, and Minnesota are pressing within less than two games. Every road win, especially against an in-form opponent, is worth double in such a tightly packed conference.

Atlanta is fighting to consolidate its dominance in the Southeast and, above all, to climb in an Eastern Conference where the gap between the fourth place it holds and the middle of the standings is just a couple of games.

Taking down the league leader at home would be a statement of intent and a huge morale boost for a roster that has shown it knows how to win close games.

Lakers vs Hawks Prediction: Who will win tonight's game?

Everything points to a high-tempo game with a generous scoreline: both teams regularly surpass 120 points, and neither stands out for its defensive solidity.

The Lakers come in as favorites thanks to their better record, greater point differential, and an almost flawless streak, but home-court advantage and the momentum of four wins in five games make the Hawks a dangerous host.

At MARCA we are leaning toward a narrow Los Angeles win, although it wouldn't surprise me if Atlanta stole the game in the final minutes if they once again impose their transition pace as they did against Brooklyn. In any case, it will be a matchup to enjoy basket by basket.
