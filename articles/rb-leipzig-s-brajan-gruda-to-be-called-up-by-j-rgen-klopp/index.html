RB Leipzig’s Brajan Gruda to be called up by Jürgen Klopp

TheTools · 15.09.2026 14:24 · source · permalink

    The German speedster could get his first cap for country this window.

    Jürgen Klopp is making his first-ever squad announcement for Germany on Thursday and stories are starting to drop like wildfire. One of many questions on the minds of fans is what will Klopp’s attack look like and whether he will lean on the past wingers given the chance by Julian Nagelsmann. Well it appears as though there are at least a few changes to be expected in attack.

    According to Florian Plettenberg and captured by @iMiaSanMia, RB Leipzig winger Brajan Gruda will be called up by Jürgen Klopp for the September international window. While this is not the German’s first ever call-up, he has never been capped but that will likely change during this international break.

    Gruda has been in stellar form for RB Leipzig since joining and adds an element of all out pace that few players in the former German squads have brought to the table. If Klopp wants speed, he now has it and Gruda could even end up as a starter in this squad if he impressed over the next four matches.

    Is it time to give someone like Gruda a chance or continue to lean on Jamal Musiala and Florian Wirtz in this attack? Let us know in the comments!
