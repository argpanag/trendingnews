Meghan Markle's New Video About Her Business Journey Includes Adorable Lilibet Cameo - AOL

TheTools · 07.10.2026 00:33 · source · permalink

Meghan Markle is reflecting on just how far her As Ever business has come.

In a new video posted to Instagram on Tuesday, Markle shared behind-the-scenes clips of what goes into her lifestyle brand, showing how her jam is made in a factory, along with an adorable scene of her daughter, Princess Lilibet, cooking alongside her mom.

“What do you think, Lili?” Markle asked her daughter of their creation, to which she adorably responded, “I think it’s beautiful.”

The video also showed Prince Harry admiring one of the finished products. Markle’s caption shared her journey as a businesswoman, from making “handmade scrunchies I sewed on my grandmother’s Singer sewing machine and sold for $1 a piece” to selling Girl Scout Cookies and lemonade, and years later, launching As Ever.

“As I pinch myself looking at how this brand has grown, and as we introduce our holiday collection, I feel very proud, and grateful,” she wrote. “A huge thanks to my team at @aseverofficial. So proud of what we are creating together. Thanks for helping bring this vision to life.”Other clips in the video featured sweet shots of her mother, Doria Ragland, as well as snaps of Markle when she was younger. Along with jam, As Ever products include candles, wine, and honey. The product line’s holiday collection includes an Advent calendar, chocolate, and a curated gift basket.
