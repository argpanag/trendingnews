Tom Brady and Supermodel Candice Swanepoel Have Been Dating ‘For a While,’ Source Says

TheTools · 24.10.2026 22:57 · source · permalink

Tom Brady and supermodel Candice Swanepoel are dating, a source exclusively tells Us.

Brady, 49, and Swanepoel, 38, have been together “for a while,” per the insider.

Neither the retired NFL quarterback nor Swanepoel have publicly addressed their newfound romance. Us reached out for comment.

Brady was previously married to supermodel Gisele Bündchen from 2009 to 2022. The pair now coparent son Benjamin, 16, and daughter Vivian, 13. (Brady also shares eldest son Jack, 19, with ex Bridget Moynahan.)

Following their divorce, Brady has been romantically linked to the likes of Irina Shayk and Alix Earle. Despite a series of headline-making flings, Brady has remained relatively mum about his love life.

“I don’t have much time for a personal life or much time for myself, but I love working, and I love my kids,” Brady told People in January. “I like to be busy and try to really stay fulfilled in the things I’m doing, so I’m working on some really cool projects and I’m involved with some great teams of people that are doing some really positive things.”

He continued at the time, “My oldest [son] is 18. But seeing him thrive and grow, and there’s nothing greater for a parent when you’re seeing your kids do well. I’m so blessed in so many parts of my life.”

As for Swanepoel, she was most recently in a decade-long relationship with ex-fiancé Hermann Nicoli before their 2018 split. Swanepoel and Nicoli, 44, are parents of two sons: Anacã, 10, and Ariel, 8.

Swanepoel, who has kept her dating life private since the breakup, has been candid about balancing motherhood with her busy modeling career.

“Life is a constant search for balance, for me at least, and yes I do sometimes feel overwhelmed, but I’m very in tune with my children’s needs, then my own personal needs, which are also important,” she told V magazine in 2023. “Sometimes it’s easy to forget. I’ve realized to be a better mother, I know I need to also take care of myself, whether that be about my [physical] health or mental health or being able to excel in my career and business because it brings me joy and so my children have begun to understand that too.”

She added, “I think it’s very healthy and important for them to see everything I do. One of my most vivid memories as a child was seeing my father wake up at 4 a.m. to take care of our farm and his dedication and drive is something that is now part of me and something I hope they will learn from me.”
