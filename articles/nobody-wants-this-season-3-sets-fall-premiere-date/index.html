'Nobody Wants This' Season 3 Sets Fall Premiere Date

TheTools · 10.09.2026 16:00 · source · permalink

Breaking News

Nobody Wants This will return to Netflix for a third season next month.

The streamer officially revealed on Thursday that Season 3 is arriving on October 22. Along with the news, Netflix also dropped some first look images from the upcoming episodes, which are embedded below.

The photos include a sneak peek at the show’s creator Erin Foster making an appearance in the third season. She joined the cast this season alongside other recurring stars including Sarah Silverman and Andrew Rannells, who are also pictured below.

Silverman is Playing Rabbi Eden, the warm, quirky teacher of an Intro to Judaism class, and a supportive presence for Joanne (Kristen Bell) and Noah (Adam Brody). Girls star Rannells joins as Sebastien, Joanne’s nemesis in conversion class.

Watch on Deadline

Also featured in the photos released Thursday are all the show’s returning fan favorites, including Bell and Brody as Joanne and Noah, along with Timothy Simons as Sasha, Justine Lupe as Morgan, Jackie Tohn as Esther, and Tovah Feldshuh as Bina.

See the first-look photos below.

In Season 3 of Nobody Wants This, after recommitting to each other at the end of last season, Joanne and Noah are moving into a home — and a relationship — that finally feels solid, embracing every exciting “first” that comes with building their life together.

Sasha and Esther are thrust back into the dating world, whether they like it or not, stumbling through all the awkwardness (and unexpected thrill) of getting to know someone new, right as Morgan takes her career to the next level, on her way to becoming an (almost) media sensation. Meanwhile, Sasha and Morgan have been circling something neither of them has been able to admit to.

Additional guest stars who are not yet pictured but are expected in Season 3 include Avan Jogia (56 Days), who plays Travis, a confident and unbothered guy’s guy who enjoys getting under Joanne’s skin; Poorna Jagannathan (Deli Boys) plays Eleanor, Sebastien’s brassy and biting aunt; Sadie Sandler (Roomates) is Denise, a young psychic who gives a reading to Morgan; Stephanie Koenig (English Teacher) is Poppy, a sardonic dating app regular who tries her luck with Sasha, and Steven Weber (Wings) is Julian, a funny, grounded, cool and secure divorced dad.
