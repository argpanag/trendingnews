Vikings & Legends Global Announce U.S. Bank Stadium Enhancements for 2026

TheTools · 08.09.2026 21:48 · source · permalink

EAGAN, Minn. — The Minnesota Vikings and Legends Global are introducing a series of technology enhancements for the 2026 season at U.S. Bank Stadium. The changes, which include innovative entry procedures and an upgraded retail experience, are designed to make every visit, from arrival through departure, seamless for fans. Descriptions and links to video tutorials for each stadium enhancement are below.

Open the Gates: Faster, Frictionless Security Screening

U.S. Bank Stadium has enhanced the fan arrival experience with the installation of new OpenGate security screening technology at every entry point. The advanced contactless system allows guests to move through security screening without removing items from their pockets or having approved bags physically inspected, helping create a faster and more convenient entry process while enhancing stadium security. The clear bag policy that limits the size of bags allowed in the venue remains in effect.

Vikings Express Pass

After trialing the technology in limited fashion during the 2025 season, the Vikings are expanding Vikings Express Pass, a voluntary touchless entry program powered by biometric authentication technology. The optional program allows enrolled guests to enter through designated lanes and eliminates the need to scan individual tickets or present a mobile device at the gate. Fans simply walk through the entry lane while their full allotment of tickets are automatically validated, reducing wait times and creating a seamless and efficient arrival experience when paired with the stadium's touchless security screening.

Seven touchless entry lanes are available at five gate locations across the stadium, including the Legacy Gate, CFMOTO Gate, Ticketmaster Gate, Ecolab Gate and Upper 3M Gate. Participation is completely voluntary, and traditional ticket-scanning options remain available for all guests. Interested fans can sign up in advance here.

​

Faster Shopping Through RFID Self-Checkout

Fans shopping at the Vikings Locker Room Store on the main concourse will enjoy a faster and more convenient checkout experience with the introduction of RFID self-checkout technology.

The new system allows guests to simply place selected merchandise into a checkout station, where RFID technology instantly identifies all items at once and displays the purchase total for payment. The process eliminates the need to scan items individually, helping reduce lines and create a seamless shopping experience.

New Gate Signage

In 2026, the Minnesota Vikings welcomed CFMOTO and TCL as new partners and expanded the team's longstanding relationship with 3M. As part of these new and expanded partnerships, several key locations throughout U.S. Bank Stadium have been updated. Fans entering the stadium will now find the CFMOTO Gate, Lower 3M Gate and Upper 3M Gate, while premium seating sections V1-V5 are now part of the TCL Club.

Additional enhancements throughout the stadium include the introduction of the Miller Lite Bar on the main concourse and an expanded partnership with Factory Motor Parts, which now serves as the entitlement sponsor of the stadium parking garage. Guests are encouraged to review stadium maps, mobile tickets and Know Before You Go communications prior to arrival to ensure a smooth gameday experience.
