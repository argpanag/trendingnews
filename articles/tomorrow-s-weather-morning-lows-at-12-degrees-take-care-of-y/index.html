On Sunday, September 13, most regions across the country are expected to be cloudy.

Yonhap News Agency

According to the Korea Meteorological Administration, between late night and the morning hours, there may be spots of less than 0.1 mm of drizzle in parts of northern Seoul, Incheon, northern Gyeonggi Province, and central and northern Gangwon Province.

From the afternoon to night, rain is expected in some mountainous areas of Jeju Island, with forecasted precipitation between 5 and 10 mm.

The morning low temperatures are forecasted to range from 12 to 21 degrees Celsius, and daytime highs from 23 to 31 degrees Celsius. As the temperature difference between day and night will be significant, it is important to pay attention to your health.
