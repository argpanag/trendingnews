Antonio Cipriano Wants to Sing In ‘Off Campus.’ He Has an Idea to Make It Happen

TheTools · 20.10.2026 11:58 · source · permalink

Maddie Ellis

Trending Editor

Antonio Cipriano thought of one way to bring a classic romance trope — and one of his real-life talents — to the world of “Off Campus.”

Before playing college hockey player John Logan, Cipriano was best known for his work in music and on the stage. He was part of the original Broadway cast of “Jagged Little Pill” and had several cover songs go viral on social media.

Cipriano is now melting hearts as one of the leading men of the Prime Video hockey romance series (and the current leading man of Fireball’s latest campaign). But would he ever want to sing on the show? “Absolutely,” he tells TODAY.com.

“I think it would be really fun,” he adds. “Have you read the second book by any chance? ... You know about the list?”

That list is at the center of “The Mistake,” the book about John Logan and the second in Elle Kennedy’s “Off Campus” series on which the show is based. In it, Logan hooks up with a freshman named Grace Ivers, but tells her he was using her as a distraction. When he later realizes that he actually has feelings for her, she’s moved on. He refuses to give up, even when she makes a list of tasks for Logan to complete before she’ll give him a second chance.

Cipriano says that list offers a chance for his character to sing.

“Maybe, what could be really funny is if she tells him to write a love song and then he’s a horrible singer and he just bombs it,” Cipriano says.

“We don’t have any confirmation” of Logan’s story yet, he notes. While Logan is the star of the second book, Season 2 will leapfrog his story to spotlight Book 3’s romance between Dean DiLaurentis and Allie Hayes, after their secret hookup arrangement was a background plot in Season 1.

But the groundwork of Logan’s story is being set. The show cast India Fowler as Grace for Season 2 after her character was mentioned briefly by name only. Cipriano personally delivered the news of Fowler’s casting in a sweet video shared on social media.

Filming for Season 2 has already wrapped, and it appears the cast is closer than ever. When Cipriano joined singer Jamie Miller for a duet remix of his viral song “Babydoll,” Cipriano posted a video of him, Fowler and co-stars Ella Bright and Jalen Thomas Brooks jamming to the track while driving with a sunroof open.

Their remix came about after Miller posted a TikTok about trying to get “Babydoll” in “Off Campus,” Cipriano says. Cipriano commented, and the two singers were connected by a mutual friend.

“I went to L.A. — actually the day before I did the Fireball ad, I was in the studio recording ‘Babydoll,’” he says. “It was one of the most fun weekends I’ve had in a long time.”

Cipriano’s ads with Fireball also leans into his heartthrob status. In a clip that dropped Sept. 2, Cipriano plays a repairman who indulges in a shot of Fireball’s vanilla whiskey after fixing his “hot and bothered” client’s AC. Now, Cipriano stars in a series of images promoting Fireball’s Vanilla Smut Stash, a collectible that resembles a romantic fantasy book with a “secret compartment” for two airplane bottles of Fireball’s Torch’d Vanilla Flavor. The collectible will be for sale online and at in-person events at Brooklyn and Los Angeles’ Ripped Bodice bookstores.

“I’ve already been a Fireball fan for a long time, big Fireball advocate myself,” he says. “I’m a big vanilla guy as well.”

Speaking about his own reading habits, Cipriano says his favorite romance trope is friends-to-lovers but he’s more likely to read nonfiction, specifically philosophy and self-help.

“Right before I had landed the role in ‘Off Campus,’ a few months before that, like January of 2025 ... on my New Year’s resolution, I was like, I’m going to read five books this year. I’m going to become a reader again,” he says.

He’s currently reading Brené Brown’s “Daring Greatly,” given to him by “Off Campus” showrunner Louisa Levy, he says. As for his progress with the “Off Campus” book series, he’s read the first three.

“I haven’t gotten to ‘The Goal’ yet. I haven’t gotten to Tucker’s book,” he says. “But I definitely will before we get there in the show.”
