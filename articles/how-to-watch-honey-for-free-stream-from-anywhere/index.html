If you're wondering how it connects to Killing Eve, the show will focus on the character of Carolyn Martens (who was played by Fiona Shaw in the original series).

Naturally, this will show us a younger version of the character, who is undercover as an MI6 operative in Germany, going by the name of Martha Schmitt (and played by Ann Kelly).

There'll be six episodes to enjoy here as Martha/Carolyn finds her work compromised when she becomes embroiled in a love triangle. With characters crossing over from Killing Eve, and a similar narrative theme, this sounds perfect for those who have been craving something to follow-up the Jodie Comer series since it ended back in 2022.
