Pay DTH subscriber base falls to 48.56 million in Apr-Jun quarter, IPTV reaches 6.21 million

TheTools · 15.10.2026 19:00 · source · permalink

ADVERTISEMENT

India’s pay direct-to-home (DTH) television subscriber base declined to 48.56 million in the quarter ended June 30, 2026, from 49.09 million in the previous quarter, as IPTV services reported 6.21 million active subscribers, according to the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India’s (TRAI) Indian Telecom Services Performance Indicators report for April-June 2026.

The report shows that pay DTH operators lost around 5.3 lakh active subscribers over the quarter. The figures exclude subscribers of Prasar Bharati’s free-to-air DD Free Dish service.

The data also offers a snapshot of India’s television distribution landscape across DTH, cable, internet protocol television (IPTV) and headend-in-the-sky (HITS) services. While four pay DTH operators reported 48.56 million active subscribers, 127 multi-system operators (MSOs) reported 37.24 million active cable subscribers. The two HITS operators together reported 4.72 million active subscribers.

Pay DTH base declines by 5.3 lakh

The four licensed pay DTH operators reported a combined active subscriber base of 48.56 million as of June 30, down from 49.09 million at the end of March 2026.

Tata Play remained the largest pay DTH operator, with 14.84 million active subscribers, followed by Bharti Telemedia with 13.58 million. Sun Direct TV reported 10.93 million subscribers, while Dish TV India had 9.21 million.

The decline in the quarter comes amid competition across television distribution platforms, including cable, IPTV and free-to-air services. However, the figures alone do not establish how many subscribers shifted from DTH to other platforms.

IPTV reports 6.21 million active subscribers

IPTV operators reported a combined active subscriber base of 6.21 million as of June 30, 2026. The figure was reported by 12 operators to TRAI, even though 76 operators had submitted self-declarations to the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting (MIB) for providing IPTV services.

The ministry’s list includes operators that are yet to commission services, according to the report.

Bharti Airtel was the largest IPTV operator, with 4.09 million active subscribers, followed by Hathway Digital with 1.28 million. Together, the two accounted for nearly 5.38 million subscribers, or about 86.7% of the reported IPTV base.

Andhra Pradesh State Fibernet reported 346,160 subscribers, followed by Wired Entertainment with 297,533 and Fastway Transmission with 179,781. The remaining seven operators listed by TRAI each reported fewer than 4,000 active subscribers.

The figures indicate that reported IPTV subscriptions are concentrated among a small number of operators. The 6.21 million total represents subscribers reported by the 12 operators, not the entire universe of operators that have submitted self-declarations to the ministry.

Cable operators report 37.24 million subscribers

A total of 736 MSOs and two HITS operators were registered with the MIB as of May 31, 2026, according to the report.

Of these, 127 MSOs, covering a major portion of the subscriber base, reported 37.24 million active subscribers to TRAI as of June 30. The reported figure includes the subscriber base of their joint ventures.

The two HITS operators reported a combined 4.72 million active subscribers. GTPL Hathway accounted for 3.10 million, while Hinduja Global Solutions, which operates NXTDIGITAL, reported 1.61 million.

The cable and HITS figures are based on reporting by the operators covered in the report. The MSO subscriber count should therefore not be read as a complete count of all cable subscribers across India.

India has 342 pay TV channels

The MIB had permitted 915 private satellite television channels for uplinking, downlinking, or both, as of the reporting period. Of these, 906 were permitted for downlinking in India.

Broadcasters reported 342 pay TV channels as of June 30, 2026. This included 238 standard-definition (SD) channels and 104 high-definition (HD) channels. The remaining 566 permitted channels available for downlinking were classified as free-to-air (FTA) channels in the report.

TRAI’s data also listed 37 pay broadcasters. The number of permitted satellite channels is different from the number of channels declared as pay, as the overall permitted universe includes free-to-air services.

FM radio advertising revenue falls to Rs 376 crore

Private FM radio operators reported advertising revenue of Rs 376.16 crore for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, down from Rs 414.03 crore in the previous quarter. The sequential decline was about 9.1%.

The latest revenue figure was reported for 391 private FM radio channels, compared with 390 channels in the March quarter. The revenue reporting base differs from the number of operational channels at the end of the quarter.

As of June 30, India had 386 operational private FM radio channels across 121 cities, operated by 30 private FM radio operators. This was down from 390 operational channels across 120 cities as of March 31.

During the June quarter, permissions for five channels were surrendered with effect from June 14, 2026. HT Media also began operating a new channel in Moradabad from April 19, taking the number of cities with operational private FM channels to 121.

The report also recorded 578 operational community radio stations as of June 30, 2026.

Overall, the latest TRAI figures offer a snapshot of India’s television and radio distribution landscape, with pay DTH subscribers declining over the quarter, IPTV subscriptions concentrated among two major operators, and private FM radio advertising revenue lower than in the preceding quarter.
