Michael Carrick press conference recap ahead of Man United vs Tottenham

TheTools · 09.10.2026 13:04 · source · permalink

Michael Carrick spoke to the media today ahead of Manchester United’s clash with Tottenham.

Carrick enters the game under pressure after an underwhelming start to the Premier League season. The draw at Fulham three weeks ago left a lot to be desired, and the United head coach needs to start picking up consistent results quickly.

Spurs provide Carrick with a good chance to get back on track. They have yet to win in the league and sit bottom of the table. Nothing less than a win will be considered a good result against Roberto De Zerbi’s team.

United have a number of injury problems heading into Saturday’s game, and Carrick provided several injury updates.

Carrick was also asked about the 115 Manchester City charges verdict.

Recap what he said during the press conference here.

United issue injury update on seven players

Michael Carrick will be without Patrick Dorgu, Noussair Mazraoui and Benjamin Sesko for Manchester United's Premier League fixture with Tottenham this weekend.

There will also be late checks on Kobbie Mainoo and Marcus Rashford after they only recently returned to some training, while Amad also appears doubtful as he continues his recovery from injury.

Striker Sesko withdrew from international duty with a recurrence of his troublesome shin injury, while Mainoo and Rashford also pulled out of the England squad for their quartet of Nations League games.

Read the full story here.

United break silence on City 115 verdict

Michael Carrick said no medal would ever get the feeling back of the Premier League titles he missed out on to Manchester City, after revealing he had his own questions about the seismic verdict handed down to the Blues.

Carrick's press conference ahead of United's game with Tottenham is the first time anybody at United has spoken publicly since it emerged the independent commission found City guilty of almost every charge laid at them by the Premier League.

The panel's report pulled no punches when it came to the scale of City's cheating, using the word "sham" on eight occasions in a 40-page report which stated that £830million was invested into the club by the owners to secretly fund sponsorship deals.

That's a wrap

That's it from the first section of Carrick's press conference.

Carrick on Spurs pressure

"It's just another game."

Carrick on Spurs

"Points-wise, they have played a lot better than that at times. When you look at the games, they are a very good team with good players. They have assembled a squad this summer that is different to what they had. They haven't had the results they maybe deserve."

Should others get the titles?

"I think at the moment, it's not finalised. I think I'll hold that one until later on. It's the moment that you strive for. A medal here and there does not bring the feeling back for winning."

Carrick on 2011/2012 title

"I think it doesn't change how you feel in that moment. You can't take it back, so whichever way this goes, and I don't really want to speak about which way it could go, but whether we did end up getting... [a trophy], it doesn't change that moment, we can't get it back."

Carrick wants an outcome

"Speed is essential, as I just said. That's obvious for me to sit here and say that. There are so many angles, but speed is essential for everybody to be clear, to deal with it, and move on. It's unprecedented to be in this situation at this level. We all need to know what is going to happen."

On City again

"There's no getting that back [winning moments] in any direction. The feeling we all crave is coming out on top. That sums it up. Whatever is done in the past, is done in the past."

Carrick on City charges

"Difficult to say in many ways. It's thrown up a lot of questions. It's ongoing, and there's only so much I can say. I have my own questions about it because it has affected me personally. We'll have to see how it goes. The sooner it gets sorted for everyone, the better."

Carrick on team news

"It's been a long time since we sat here last. Patrick came off and looks like he'll be out for a number of weeks. Nouss came off and took a knock. He's not quite right, so we'll see how he is. Carlos is improving, has trained a lot, Amad, Kobbie and Marcus have done a little bit of training. Ben is further away with the injury he has, so he won't be ready."

'Carrick will be under huge pressure'

Alan Shearer believes this weekend's game against Tottenham is huge for Carrick.

He told Betfair: "You can imagine the noise at Old Trafford around Carrick if United lose on Saturday.He did a great job last year in terms of getting them into Champions League football, he did a great job in just calming the situation down. Then they stuck with him, didn't really back him as much as they could have and should have in the summer, and again, you just imagine the noise after three weeks, after the start to the season they've had, and if Spurs beat them, who haven't won a game all season.They’ve got Atletico Madrid on Tuesday evening as well. So, they've got two massive games and games that they need to get something out of and win. Whether they've got enough to do that, we'll have to wait and see but it is a huge game for both clubs. You can't underestimate it.I think it'll probably be a score draw. I think it could be 1-1 or 2-2, which I guess is a better result for the away team than it is for the home team."

Are City's trophies tainted?

Maresca denied that City's success is tainted during his press conference 30 minutes ago.

"Absolutely not," he said. "I know the effort from the players and the club so I know what they did for that target. Absolutely not [tainted]. For me and the players, we can control the pitch side, we can focus on that. We cannot control something different."

The eight days that will make or break Carrick's tenure

Carrick won't admit it, but the international break came at a good time after a string of disappointing results for Manchester United. The Manchester derby defeat was followed by a loss to Brighton in the Carabao Cup despite going two goals up, and a 1-1 draw against Fulham didn't offer much reassurance before the pause.

There was speculation about Carrick's future after Brighton, which saw the players disappear down the tunnel to a chorus of "what the f****** hell was that?", the closest the Stretford End will get to mutiny.
