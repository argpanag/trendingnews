Paramount+ Live-Streaming Combat Sports Doubleheader on Sept. 12

Erik Gruenwedel

September 4, 2026

Paramount+ returns to live-streaming combat sports with a UFC/Zuffa Boxing doubleheader on Sept. 12, in celebration of Mexican Independence Day weekend.

The fight card begins with “Noche UFC: Jean Silva vs. Jose Miguel Delgado” at 5 p.m. ET, followed by Zuffa Boxing’s first-ever super fight on Paramount+, “Ryan Garcia vs. Conor Benn,” starting at 8 p.m. ET.

Noche UFC streams live from Desert Diamond Arena in Glendale, Ariz. The featherweight contender Silva is set to meet Mexican-American star Delgado in the main event of the evening.

The card also features the return of Tommy McMillen, who recently broke the UFC record for most significant strikes landed in a three-round fight (252) in his last outing. In addition, former women’s flyweight champion Alexa Grasso takes on Manon Fiorot and former two-time flyweight champion Brandon Moreno fights Joseph Morales.

Zuffa Boxing’s first super fight on Paramount+ features Garcia, a Mexican-American professional boxer and the current welterweight world champion, against Benn, a British professional welterweight boxer.

While boxing is traditionally sold as a pay-per-view event, this fight will be available to Paramount+ subscribers as part of their existing subscription. The fight streams live from T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas.

In the co-main event, undefeated Australian star and reigning Zuffa Boxing cruiserweight world champion Jai Opetaia puts his title on the line against former champion Noel Mikaelian.
