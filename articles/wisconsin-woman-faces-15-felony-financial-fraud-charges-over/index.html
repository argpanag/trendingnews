Wisconsin woman faces 15 felony financial fraud charges over alleged loan scheme

TheTools · 11.11.2026 10:35 · source · permalink
  1. Financial fraud allegations involve elderly customers
  2. Kenosha court authorizes release without cash bail
  3. Identity theft and financial fraud remain central to the case

A 41-year-old Wisconsin woman faces 15 felony charges in connection with an alleged financial fraud scheme involving elderly customers, forged signatures, unauthorized loans and losses exceeding $18,000, according to Kenosha County Sheriff’s Office.

Candice L. DeFord of Kenosha was released without a cash bail requirement on November 9, 2026, following her arrest three days earlier.

Kenosha court authorizes release without cash bail
