    Julian Champagnie has nine points in win over Kings

    Julian Champagnie

    SAS Forward #30
    Player Stats

    Julian Champagnie had nine points (3-of-11 FGs, 0-of-1 FTs), four rebounds, two assists, one block and three three-pointers in Friday’s exhibition against the Kings.

    Champagnie came off the bench with Dylan Harper, Devin Vassell and De’Aaron Fox in the starting lineup, which is also the plan heading into Tuesday’s opener against the Thunder. If the Spurs stick with this lineup, it’ll be difficult for Champagnie to have much of a fantasy ceiling. He looks like a late-round dart throw in remaining fantasy drafts.
    - Nick Shlain
