Lee Sun-bin recalls 'Nonhyeon-dong bloodbath' punch score, charms NCT Dream

TheTools · 30.10.2026 11:30 · source · permalink

Actor Lee Sun-bin recounted a story tied to the old nickname "Nonhyeon-dong bloodbath," drawing laughter.

On the 30th, a video titled "I will act… I'm sorry I give up… | 빈집대성 ep.7 Lee Sun-bin" was released on the YouTube channel ZIP DAESUNG.

That day Lee Sun-bin met NCT DREAM's Jeno and Jaemin and displayed a delightful wit. Upon her arrival, Jeno and Jaemin showed their delight, saying "a famous person has come."

She then drew attention as she pondered how they should address her. Jeno and Jaemin debated between "Seonbin-ssam" and "auntie," and Lee Sun-bin drew a line, saying "I'm not an auntie," which provoked laughter. In the end, the two decided to call her "Seonbin-ssam."

Meanwhile, an unexpected story from Lee Sun-bin's past surfaced. It was the rumor known as "Nonhyeon-dong bloodbath." In response, Lee Sun-bin recalled, "It was roughly 8 to 9 years ago." She said she once scored 859 points on a punch game, boasting an extraordinary punching skill. When the anecdote about the high score was revealed, people on site even requested she demonstrate her skill. Lee Sun-bin also joked about the nickname "Nonhyeon-dong bloodbath," saying "it's a bloodbath, indeed," which brought laughter.

When Jaemin's past as a famous "kkabul-i" was mentioned, Lee Sun-bin also empathized, saying "me too." Discovering an unexpected commonality, the two formed a playful camaraderie that added to the humor.

Meanwhile, Lee Sun-bin is loved for her activities in various dramas and films.<
