On Thursday the Lee County Supervisor of Elections Office began mailing sample ballots for the Nov. 3 general election to approximately 385,000 voters.

The mailing, which will continue through Wednesday, Oct. 14, also includes early voting dates, times and locations, as well as important information about voting by mail and Election Day voting to help voters prepare for the election.

Voters who have already requested a vote-by-mail ballot for the general election will not be mailed a sample ballot.

Voters can access their voter-specific sample ballot at lee.vote, as well as the all-inclusive sample ballot, which includes every contest and ballot measure in the general election.

For more information about the 2026 general election, visit lee.vote or call (239) 533-8683.
