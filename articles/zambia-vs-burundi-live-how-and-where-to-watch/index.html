Zambia vs Burundi: How and Where to Watch the Match LIVE

Zambia will host Burundi on Friday, November 13, for Matchday 3 of the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations Qualifiers.

The encounter will take place at Levy Mwanawasa Stadium in Ndola, Zambia, with kickoff scheduled for 8:00 AM ET / 5:00 AM PT.

Fans can watch the match through beIN SPORTS CONNECT, with English-language streaming coverage beginning at 7:50 AM ET.

Zambia enters the fixture second in Group I with three points, while Burundi sits fourth with one point.



How Zambia Comes Into the Match

Zambia enters Friday's encounter looking to extend its recovery after a difficult start to the qualifying campaign.

The Chipolopolo began Group I with a 3-1 defeat against Algeria on September 25.

Fashion Sakala scored Zambia's only goal, but an own goal by Miguel Chaiwa and strikes from Ibrahim Maza and Anis Hadj Moussa secured victory for Algeria.

Four days later, Zambia responded with a 2-0 home victory over Togo.

Albert Kangwanda opened the scoring in the second minute before Eliya Mandanji sealed the result during stoppage time.

Interim head coach George Lwandamina initially named a 30-player provisional squad for November's double-header, but Zambia will have to deal with several important absences.

Patson Daka, Lameck Banda, and Gamphani Lungu have been ruled out through injury and replaced by Edward Chilufya, Derrick Bulaya, and Prince Mumba, respectively. Kingstone Mutandwa, Ackim Mumba, and Jackson Phiri are also unavailable because of injuries.

Meanwhile, Owen Tembo will miss the opening match due to an accumulation of yellow cards, while Klings Kangwa must complete his suspension before becoming eligible for the return fixture on November 17.

With several attacking and midfield options unavailable, captain Fashion Sakala will be one of the key figures for the Chipolopolo as they look to make home advantage count and secure three valuable points.



How Burundi Comes Into the Match

Burundi travels to Ndola searching for its first victory in Group I after collecting one point from its opening two qualifiers.

The Intamba mu Rugamba began their campaign with a 1-0 defeat against Togo on September 25.

Karim Dermane scored the decisive goal in the 17th minute, leaving Burundi empty-handed after its opening fixture.

However, the team produced an encouraging performance four days later, earning a 2-2 draw against Algeria.

Burundi established a two-goal advantage through an own goal by Farès Chaïbi and a strike from Jean Claude Girumugisha.

Algeria eventually recovered through goals from Mohamed Belloumi and Adil Boulbina.

Head coach Patrick Sangwa has named a 25-player squad for the November international window.

Among the selected players are Youssouf Ndayishimiye, Frédéric Nsabiyumva, Jean Claude Girumugisha, and returning midfielder Gaël Bigirimana.



Zambia vs Burundi: A Crucial Double-Header in Group I

The race for qualification remains extremely competitive after the first two rounds.

Algeria leads Group I with four points, followed by Zambia and Togo with three each. Burundi occupies fourth place with one point.

The top two teams will qualify for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations, hosted by Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda.

The teams will meet again on Tuesday, November 17, at Intwari Stadium in Bujumbura, Burundi.

With only three points separating the group leader and the fourth-placed team, these back-to-back encounters could significantly affect the qualification race.

For Zambia, the objective is to build on its home victory over Togo. Burundi, meanwhile, will seek to turn its promising performance against Algeria into a first qualifying win.
