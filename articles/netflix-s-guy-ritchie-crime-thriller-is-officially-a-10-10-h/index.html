Netflix’s Guy Ritchie Crime Thriller Is Officially a 10/10 Hit

TheTools · 04.09.2026 10:30 · source · permalink

Yesterday was a great day to be a Guy Ritchie fan. It wasn't long ago that the future of his hit crime thriller MobLand was in doubt after it was reported that star Tom Hardy had been fired from the show amid off-screen conflicts. Thankfully, it would soon be water under the bridge, as it was confirmed that Hardy would continue on the series, should a third season get the green light. That's exactly what happened, with Hardy's Harry Da Souza returning alongside Pierce Brosnan (Mamma Mia!) and Helen Mirren (Gosford Park)in the now-confirmed MobLand Season 3.

Yesterday is also wonderful for Ritchie fans as it marks the long-awaited return of his best small-screen project yet. Debuting in 2024 as a follow-up to the hugely popular 2019 movie of the same name, The Gentlemen introduced us to the brilliantly brutal and darkly comic world of Theo James' Eddie Horniman and his inheritance of his father's criminal empire. Alongside Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Men Tell No Tales alum Kaya Scodelario as Susie Glass, the pair quickly steered this stylish series to the top of the streaming charts.

Now, Eddie, Susie, Bobby Glass (Ray Winstone) and more are back for a second season promising to be bigger and better than before. In Season 2, Eddie's cannabis operation is moved beyond the British countryside and into international territories, as plans for an expansion see him and his colleagues run into the dangerous lives of Italian crime families. Ahead of the show's return, the embargo was lifted on reviews of The Gentlemen Season 2, hoping to improve on the "certified fresh" 75% score of the first installment. At the time of writing, Season 2 holds a perfect 100% score on review aggregator Rotten Tomatoes, albeit with more reviews still to be submitted.

What Have Critics Said About 'The Gentlemen' Season 2?

With a perfect 100% score on Rotten Tomatoes so far, it's no surprise that reviews for the second season of Ritchie's best series have been positively glowing. For Collider, Therese Lacson awarded the second season a 9/10 in her review, writing, "As The Gentlemen sheds some of its flash, it's replaced with a far more thoughtful and philosophical story, one that will carry it through to what is sure to be another outstanding season." For ScreenRant, El Kuiper offered plenty of praise, but ultimately viewed the second season as "overly ambitious." Other critics have so far called the second season "audacious" and "bombastic," as more reviews are submitted.

The Gentlemen Season 2 is streaming on Netflix. Stay tuned to Collider for more updates.
