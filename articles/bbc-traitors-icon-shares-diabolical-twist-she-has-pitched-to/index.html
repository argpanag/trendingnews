BBC Traitors icon shares diabolical twist she has pitched to producers

TheTools · 30.09.2026 16:30 · source · permalink

Charlotte adopted a fake Welsh accent throughout her time on the show(Image: BBC)

A contestant from The Traitors has proposed a revolutionary twist to BBC executives.

Charlotte Berman featured in series three of the civilian edition alongside beloved contestants such as Alexander Dragonetti, Francesca Rowan-Plowden and Freddie Fraser. Her series was eventually claimed by Faithfuls Leanne Quigley and Jake Brown.

During the programme, Charlotte adopted a Welsh accent and informed her fellow contestants she originated from Abergavenny. However, this was entirely fabricated.

While audiences were eventually made aware of the deception, her fellow players only discovered the truth upon leaving the castle as she never disclosed it to them throughout the main show.

She appeared alongside Alexander Dragonetti on series three(Image: BBC)

The London native has subsequently clarified that her decision to portray herself as Welsh stemmed from believing it was a "trustworthy" accent, plus her mother was originally from Wales, though it ultimately couldn't prevent her elimination.

Duplicity remains a frequent strategy employed by previous contestants on the BBC programme, with falsehoods spanning from concealing occupations to masking connections with other participants, reports Wales Online.

With The Celebrity Traitors set to return to television screens for a second series tomorrow evening (October 1), Charlotte, speaking exclusively on behalf of Buzz Bingo, disclosed a significant twist she hopes to witness - and she has already presented it to decision-makers.

"I have what I think is the master idea which I need to be implemented. I've mentioned this when I've seen and spoken to some of the executive producers," she began.

Charlotte revealed her 'master idea'(Image: BBC)

"It's to have identical twins. I think it would be so funny, so unnecessary, but put a pair of identical twins in there and have them play as the same person. I don't know if they would be allowed a debrief; I can't tell you what the construct would be. For the viewer, that would be so funny. If that happens, you heard it here first."

Regarding her Welsh accent, Charlotte maintains she has "no regrets" about attempting it. She continued: "I feel very lucky to have been such a memorable part of my series. It was a lot of fun, and I think it was very much in the spirit of The Traitors and the kind of camp, pantomime style of this backstabbing parlour game that we're all playing."

Traitors enthusiasts won't have to wait much longer for their next dose of banishments and betrayal, as The Celebrity Traitors series two kicks off tomorrow evening from 8pm. Viewers can catch it on BBC One or stream it through BBC iPlayer.

Amongst the well-known personalities entering the castle are singer-songwriter James Blunt, Love Island presenter Maya Jama, actor Michael Sheen and an array of comedians including Miranda Hart and Rob Beckett.

The Celebrity Traitors returns tomorrow night at 8pm on BBC One and iPlayer.
