Luton Town: Jack Wilshere on 'full-circle' return to Hatters

TheTools · 03.10.2026 12:20 · source · permalink

Jack Wilshere - The Football Interview

  • Published
    2 October 2026

In this week's edition of The Football Interview, Kelly Somers meets Luton Town manager Jack Wilshere.

They speak about the 34-year-old's years at Arsenal and his journey into coaching, which has taken him to Kenilworth Road and which he describes as a "full-circle moment".

They also discuss why he did not sign for Luton as a player under Nathan Jones, his relationship with Gunners boss Mikel Arteta and how Jack's own children have begun their own footballing journey.
