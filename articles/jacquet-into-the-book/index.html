Ipswich 0-2 Liverpool — reaction

Liverpool eased past Ipswich to begin the Premier League's third matchweek with their first win of the season.

Alexander Isak scored two early goals, rifling in at the near post then sliding home after a brilliant Cody Gakpo pass.

A drab second half saw £106million marquee summer signing Bradley Barcola make his club debut at Portman Road.

The result sees Liverpool temporarily climb to fifth in the table ahead of the weekend's fixtures, with Ipswich 13th.

Jacquet into the book

Ipswich 0-2 Liverpool (53)

Jacquet is shown a yellow card after catching Emersonn as the striker looked to break into the Liverpool half.

That was rather clumsy from the centre-back, and he tangles with Emersonn again soon after.

Ipswich want another card but a throw-in is the decision.

An auspicious sign for Liverpool

Here's a statistic to strike confidence into Liverpool hearts and pessimism into those of an Ipswich persuasion.

Since December 2016, Liverpool have gone two goals ahead away from home 22 times... and won 22 of those games.

Ipswich will hope to break that run today.

Will we see Barcola this half?

Liverpool fans will be waiting eagerly for signs of their big-money signing — if the score stays this way or even improves, then it could prove the perfect soft launch for his Premier League career.

Andoni Iraola's side have been able to flex their attacking muscles and Barcola will be raring to go.

Mac Allister has a free header

Ipswich 0-2 Liverpool (47)

A Liverpool corner right at the start of the second half — Mac Allister gets a free header from a Szoboszlai delivery but nods it into the ground and it bounces kindly in front of Scherpen.

No one was near the midfielder in the Ipswich box, he probably should have done better.

SECOND HALF: We're back underway

Ipswich 0-2 Liverpool (46)

No changes for either side at the break and we're off once more.

Can Ipswich find a way back into this or will Liverpool grow their lead?

Ipswich still have plenty to play for

Yes, they might be two down and feel the game is almost beyond them.

But Liverpool have been defensively vulnerable so far this season and have shown signs of that today as well.

If Ipswich get the next goal, which is eminently possible, they will get the home fans fired up and behind them and put familiar nerves in the visiting ranks.

Big team-talk required from manager Gary O'Neil, still.

Disappointing defending from Fatawu

Abdul Fatawu deservedly got plenty of praise after searing past Manchester United's Luke Shaw to set up Leif Davis' opening goal at Old Trafford.

He was bright and lively that day in attack.

Today, in defence? Not so much.

He was robbed by Milos Kerkez to give Cody Gakpo the ball for the first goal... and was the one napping and playing Alexander Isak onside for the second.

Can Liverpool keep a clean sheet?

Liverpool's leaky defence has been much talked about, but they are holding firm so far in this one.

Ronald Araujo has not looked hugely comfortable when under pressure at right-back but has settled into things.

Ipswich, meanwhile, just haven't had the answers to superior talent from Liverpool's attack.

But it's not all about Isak...

Transfer window speculation about a move to Manchester City behind him, Cody Gakpo is back and firing for Liverpool.

Two assists for Alexander Isak is a very nice return indeed.

Focused.

A happy haul for FPL players with Isak

Good news for those of you who splashed the Fantasy Premier League (FPL) cash on Alexander Isak (9.0m) — with his brace tonight that'll be 12 points already in the bag with the potential for more if he adds to his tally in the second half.

Similarly, nine points are heading to owners of Cody Gakpo after his two assists.

Less good news for the widely owned Ipswich defenders Issa Diop (owned by 15.6 per cent of players, the ninth-highest owned player in the game) and Leif Davis, who are both on zero after being deducted a point for conceding two goals tonight.

Photo of the half

Alexander Isak keeping his eyes on the ball here, literally.

Love this snap.

He had a chance of a hat-trick and did stick the chance away but was narrowly offside.

Keep it up and he could have his treble in the second half.

HALF-TIME: Ipswich 0-2 Liverpool

Much, much better from Liverpool so far.

Isak's two goals in the first 10 minutes see them lead at Portman Road.

Rare chances for Ipswich

Ipswich 0-2 Liverpool (45+1)

Ipswich have a chance to attack right at the end of this half. After a corner is cleared by Gakpo, Palacios' through ball is cut out by Mac Allister to halt a dangerous break.

That was a great opportunity gone begging. There are two minutes added on.

Scherpen kept busy, Enciso goes into the book

Ipswich 0-2 Liverpool (41)

Szoboszlai clearly wants a goal tonight — he sees another shot beaten away by Scherpen, but this time the goalkeeper only has to stand his ground.

Soon after, Enciso is shown a yellow card and Scherpen is called into action again to deny Wirtz.

Yellow card for Elphick

Ipswich 0-2 Liverpool (37)

Speaking of touchline antics, Max...

Liverpool's first-team coach Tommy Elphick was shown a yellow card not long after Liverpool's disallowed goal.

He seemed to see the funny side as he sloped back to his seat in the dugout.

Good control from Gary O'Neil

Fair play to the Ipswich manager, he got his leg up nice and high to bring down a high ball which was bouncing towards the managerial dugout.

Easy to mis-control and make yourself a meme.

Not this time though. O'Neil a former Premier League footballer, of course, with West Ham and Portsmouth, among others.

A disallowed goal but Liverpool are clicking

That's a pretty tight offside call just when Alexander Isak thought he had his hat-trick.

Something tells me he will have more chances to score a third here tonight and given the mood he appears to be in, he is likely to take them.

Liverpool are clicking nicely.

Real moment of quality from Wirtz

Florian Wirtz has had a quiet first half but sparked into action superbly there.

Really nice initial run, taper and then smart movement beyond his marker.

A lovely touch to control and allow the ball across his body and his left-footed centre was perfectly placed for Alexander Isak to stride onto and pass it in.

As it happens, Isak was narrowly offside.

Isak denied a hat-trick!

Ipswich 0-2 Liverpool (33)

For a split second, Isak thought he had a third!

It's so slick from Liverpool — Mac Allister finds Wirtz, who breaks the line and whips a lovely ball towards Isak.

The striker taps in but the flag goes up almost instantly for offside. Replays show it was a tight call — but the right call.
