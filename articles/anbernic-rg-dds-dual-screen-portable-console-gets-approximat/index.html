Anbernic RG DDS Dual-Screen Portable Console Gets Approximate Price - Rumor

TheTools · 10.10.2026 12:34 · source · permalink

Thanks to insider Wild Li, gamers were able to find out approximately how much the Anbernic RG DDS dual-screen portable console will cost, what its RAM situation is, and when to expect the device to be shipped. Naturally, all this information should be taken as a rumor for now.

The Anbernic RG DDS dual-screen portable console was announced only yesterday, and rumors about it have already started to appear online.

Thanks to the Retro Handhelds portal and Wild Li — a well-known insider in the English-speaking retro console community and a “reliable source close to Anbernic” — users learned that the aforementioned device will cost around $300 (25,547 rubles).

Therefore, it is possible to expect a price in the range of $250–350 (21,289–29,805 rubles). Also, according to him, the RG DDS will have 8 GB of RAM, and device shipments are expected in early December 2026.

This clamshell will feature a Snapdragon G2 Gen 2 chip, an active cooling system, a 6000 mAh battery, a built-in stylus, three color options, and two IPS displays: the first is a 4.7-inch display with a resolution of 1200 × 720 pixels, and the second is a 4-inch display with a resolution of 960 × 720 pixels.
