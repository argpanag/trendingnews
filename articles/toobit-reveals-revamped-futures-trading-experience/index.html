Toobit Reveals Revamped Futures Trading Experience

TheTools · 15.10.2026 14:04 · source · permalink

Home » Projects

The popular and award-winning international cryptocurrency exchange Toobit announced a series of upgrades to Toobit Futures.

The platform introduced a series of changes, including a redesigned trading interface, faster order execution, and enhanced position management and tracking tools.

What’s New?

The revamped Toobit Futures web interface comes with a much more intuitive trading panel, clearer order parameters, and customizable layouts and display settings.

In addition, users can also take advantage of keyboard shortcuts, one-click trading, faster trading pair search and switching, which further simplify order placement and navigation.

A new data timeline is also designed to display trading activity and position changes in a chronological way, making it a lot easier to review past transactions. An important improvement is separate isolated margin mode. It allows users to maintain many independent positions in the same direction on a single trading pair.

Unlike the previous approach of calculating positions collectively, now each position has its own profit and loss, margin allocation, and liquidation price calculations. This is intended to allow traders to manage different entry points, exit targets, and risk exposure separately. The feature is going to be first available in Demo Trading on the web and app version 2.3.8, with live support planned for a subsequent release.

What Does it All Mean?

The move is designed to make the overall futures trading experience a lot faster, more intuitive, and easier to manage. The interface brings key trading functions together in a way that is more accessible and clearer, while also featuring customizable display settings that allow users to tailor the platform to their specific preferences.

Moreover, the faster order execution, one-click trading, and keyboard shortcuts also help streamline the trading process, particularly for active traders who are looking for means to respond quickly to market movements.

The upgrade comes amid substantial activity in the field of cryptocurrency derivatives trading. According to CoinGlass’s 2026 Semi-Annual Cryptocurrency Derivatives Market Report, derivatives trading volume reached $35.08 trillion in the first half of 2026, averaging $193.8 billion daily. Average daily open interest stood at $112.7 billion, underscoring the importance of effective position monitoring and risk management.
