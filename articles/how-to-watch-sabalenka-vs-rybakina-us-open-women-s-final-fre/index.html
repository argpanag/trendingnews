What to expect for today’s match

Top-ranked Sabalenka is just a few sets away from etching her name into US Open history. With a win, the Belarusian powerhouse would become only the fifth women’s tennis player to win three straight at the American jewel of tennis.

Only four other women have achieved the feat: Serena Williams (2012-14), Chris Evert (1975-78), Helen Jacobs (1932-1935) and Molla Mallory (1915-18).

The 2026 campaign has been a fairly rocky road for Sabalenka, who finished second in the Australian Open, reached the quarterfinals at the French Open and was bounced in the fourth round at Wimbledon. Those statistics fail to live up to the reputation that she has built, thus making today’s opportunity a chance to wash away any doubt of her greatness.

She put on a near masterpiece in her one-hour, 20-minute dissection of No. 3 Jessica Pegula in the semifinals.

Rybakina will relish the role of underdog in the high-profile finale where she is a first-time finalist at Flushing Meadows. She has proven her might as a power tennis player, which makes her a fitting final hurdle for Sabalenka’s 3-peat.

Rybakina bested Sabalenka in the Australian Open final earlier this year. The Kazakhstani is gaining steam after second place finishes in Toronto and Indian Wells. She also captured her 13th career title at Stuttgart in April. She defeated Coco Gauff and Zheng Qinwen, both in three sets.

Sabalenka owns the all-time edge, 10-7. She has won the last two matches against Rybakina, which took place at Indian Wells and Miami.

Today’s match marks the first time since 2013 that the top two women’s seeds are facing each other in the final at the US Open.
