Patriots' Reggie Gilliam: Still working through knee issue

TheTools · 04.10.2026 16:40 · source · permalink

Gilliam (knee) was a limited participant in Wednesday's practice.

Gilliam was able to play in the Patriots' Week 3 loss to the Jaguars after working through a knee issue during practice prep. He played a total of 19 snaps (eight on offense, 11 on special teams) without showing up on the box score otherwise. Gilliam will have two more opportunities to log a full practice and avoid an injury designation for Sunday's AFC East tilt against the Bills.
