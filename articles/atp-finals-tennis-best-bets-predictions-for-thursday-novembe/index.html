Today’s ATP Finals picks, with matchup analysis and betting insights for Thursday, November 19.

Looking for the best bets for the ATP Finals on Thursday, November 19? We break down the day’s matches, analyzing form, playing styles, and the odds to find our favorite picks for the action in Turin, Italy. Here’s what we’re betting on — and why.

Rafael Jodar vs. Ben Shelton
Arthur Fils vs. Alexander Zverev

I haven’t had the best start to this tournament, but we’ll see if we can catch fire over the final couple of days — and hope our pre-tournament futures cash.

For Thursday, I’m keeping it simple and parlaying the two favorites.

Shelton needs to beat Jodar to reach the semifinals, and it would be borderline unacceptable if he can’t. Jodar was clearly dealing with something against Fils on Tuesday. He called two medical timeouts and won only four games after the first one. I expect Shelton to run through his service games, and I think he’ll have more success than normal in longer exchanges. The American leaves a lot to be desired as a baseliner, but he’s good enough to take advantage of an opponent who doesn’t have his legs.

With Zverev, the advantage on serve and return is too hard to ignore in these conditions. His serve has looked untouchable in Turin, and it will likely take Carlos Alcaraz to end his run. Zverev also enters having won two straight-set matches, while Fils has needed a decider in both of his. Nothing suggests Fils should emerge with the win.

PARLAY: Shelton ML & Zverev ML (+106)
