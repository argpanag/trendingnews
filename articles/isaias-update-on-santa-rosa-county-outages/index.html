ISAIAS: Update on Santa Rosa County outages

TheTools · 11.10.2026 16:04 · source · permalink

SANTA ROSA COUNTY, Fla. (WALA) - Escambia River Electric Cooperative, Inc. posted on its Facebook page and update on power outages in Santa Rosa County.

Here is the post:

EREC OUTAGE UPDATE – 10:05 A.M. | OCTOBER 11

We want to be as transparent as possible with our members about where things stand with power restoration and what is happening behind the scenes.

SANTA ROSA COUNTY OUTAGES

EREC has eight substations, six of which are located in Santa Rosa County. Currently, all six Santa Rosa County substations are without power due to damage to PowerSouth’s transmission lines. PowerSouth is our wholesale power provider, and until those transmission lines are repaired, we cannot restore power through those substations.

What does that mean for our Santa Rosa County members?

While we are waiting for PowerSouth to restore transmission service, our crews are NOT sitting idle! We have close to 450 lineworkers and staff working across both counties, repairing damaged poles, lines and other equipment throughout our system.

Our goal is to have as much of our distribution system repaired as possible so that when PowerSouth restores power to our substations, many members can have their electricity restored without additional delays.

ESCAMBIA COUNTY OUTAGES

EREC has two substations serving our Escambia County members. Restoration crews are working from the substations outward, repairing damaged equipment and power lines as they move farther into the system.

This means crews must first restore the main lines coming out of the substations before they can move on to smaller lines serving neighborhoods and individual homes.

If you haven’t seen crews in your immediate area yet, that doesn’t mean you’ve been forgotten. Restoration is a step-by-step process, and repairs made farther up the line are necessary to get power flowing to the areas beyond them.

DAMAGE ASSESSMENTS

By the end of today, we expect crews to have completed assessments of approximately 1,900 miles of power lines across our service territory. This will give us a much clearer picture of the extent of the damage, what repairs remain and what resources are needed.

OUTAGE MAP UPDATE

We know our members are frustrated that the outage map is not working, and we understand how important that information is. We have been in contact with AT&T regarding the communications issue affecting our outage map, and their crews are currently working to resolve it.

PLEASE BE PATIENT WITH THE PROCESS

As you can imagine, there are a tremendous number of moving parts involved in an operation of this magnitude. From transmission repairs and damage assessments to coordinating hundreds of lineworkers, equipment, materials and logistics, there is a lot happening simultaneously.

We know being without power is frustrating, especially when you cannot see the progress being made. But please know that work is happening throughout our system, even in areas where power cannot yet be restored.

We appreciate your patience, understanding and encouragement as our crews continue working toward getting everyone’s lights back on.

We are making progress, and we will continue keeping you informed. We are #coopstrong.
