Eck in Chicago for Patriots-Bears game

Alan Eck will be in Chicago on Thursday as the Patriots square off against the Bears in an interconference matchup. Bill Vinovich will preside over the first NFL game in France as the Steelers face the Saints in London. Clete Blakeman, Adrian Hill, and Shawn Hochuli are off this week.
