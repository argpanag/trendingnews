Warriors' De'Anthony Melton: Makes impact despite shooting woes

TheTools · 26.11.2026 04:46 · source · permalink

Melton totaled two points (0-7 FG, 0-5 3Pt, 2-2 FT), three rebounds, seven assists and two steals across 27 minutes during Sunday's 88-87 victory over Boston.

Melton continues seeing increased opportunities with Stephen Curry (oblique) sidelined. Although his scoring remains inconsistent, his playmaking and defensive contributions provide fantasy value. He's averaging 24.0 minutes and 2.3 steals over his last three appearances.
