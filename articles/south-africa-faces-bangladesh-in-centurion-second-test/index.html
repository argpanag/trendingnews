South Africa Faces Bangladesh in Centurion Second Test

TheTools · 23.11.2026 07:36 · source · permalink

South Africa will host Bangladesh in the second and final Test of their two-match series at SuperSport Park in Centurion, with Aiden Markram leading the home team in place of regular captain Temba Bavuma. The match begins Monday, November 23, as the Proteas seek to complete a series sweep after winning the opening Test.

South Africa enters the contest with a 1-0 advantage following a nine-wicket victory over Bangladesh at The Wanderers Stadium in Johannesburg. According to the match schedule, play is set to begin at 1:30 PM Indian Standard Time, with the toss scheduled for 1:00 PM.

Markram will captain a South African lineup featuring Marques Ackerman, Zubayr Hamza, Tony de Zorzi, Matthew Breetzke and Ryan Rickelton. The team also includes Wiaan Mulder, Marco Jansen, Simon Harmer, Kagiso Rabada and Anrich Nortje.

Bangladesh's playing XI had not been announced in the published match information ahead of the second Test.

Indian television viewers will not have a broadcaster for the match, meaning the contest will not be available through a domestic live television telecast, according to the published broadcast information.

However, Indian cricket fans can follow the match through online streaming. The FanCode app and website are listed as the streaming platforms for the second Test in India.
