Cavs at Hornets: How to watch, odds, and injury report

TheTools · 09.11.2026 15:30 · source · permalink
  • Cavs Injury Report

The Cavs will look to get right against the injury-riddled Hornets.

The Cleveland Cavaliers will look to reestablish some positive momentum as they take on an injured and struggling Charlotte Hornets team.

Things aren’t going well for the Hornets. They have been one of the worst teams in the league based on their point differential. It doesn’t help that they’ve been without one of their best players for most of the season, and their second-best player has struggled mightily.

Kon Knueppel will be missing this game with a hamstring injury. He has played just six minutes so far this season for Charlotte. The Hornets will also be without Brandon Miller (hip), who’s gotten off to a rough start to the season as he’s been unable to find his range from beyond the arc (26%).

This is a prime opportunity for the Cavs to pick up a win and start another busy week on the right foot.
