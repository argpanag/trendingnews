India is hosting the inaugural edition of the Commonwealth Kabaddi Championship. | Photo Credit: VIJAY SONEJI

The inaugural Commonwealth Kabaddi Championship will kick off at the Veer Savarkar Sports Complex in Ahmedabad on Friday.

The tournament will have 23 teams from 13 nations competing in 62 matches, including 39 men’s and 23 women’s fixtures.

The Indian men’s team is placed alongside Nigeria, Malaysia, Uganda, England, Tanzania and Sri Lanka in Group A, while the women’s side will share Group A with Singapore, Nigeria, Uganda and Malaysia.
