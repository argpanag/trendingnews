Here Are the Results from the 2026 Toronto Waterfront Marathon and Half Marathon

TheTools · 18.10.2026 13:25 · source · permalink

Catch up on the action from a beautiful morning in Canada.

On a brisk Sunday morning, the Toronto Waterfront Marathon and Half Marathon kicked off in Canada’s largest city. The mostly flat courses run through the city’s downtown and along the waterfront of Lake Ontario.

Canadians swept the half marathon: Dylan Alick won the men’s race in 1:03:49 and Teagan Robertson was first on the women’s side, running 1:13:58. The marathon finishers are still coming in.
