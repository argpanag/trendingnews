Mirassol Faces Internacional in Brasileirão Relegation Battle

TheTools · 16.10.2026 22:59 · source · permalink

Mirassol and Internacional opened Matchday 31 of the 2026 Brasileirão with both clubs fighting to avoid relegation, meeting at Maião on Friday, October 16, at 8 p.m. Brasília time. According to Yahoo Sports, Internacional entered the fixture in 17th place with 32 points, while Mirassol occupied 15th with 33 points after 30 league matches.

The standings placed Internacional inside the relegation zone, while Mirassol held a narrow one-point advantage over their visitors. The match offered both sides an opportunity to improve their position as the Brazilian Serie A season progressed, according to the two published previews.

Yahoo Sports reported that Internacional coach Eduardo Baptista made two changes to the lineup following a 1-1 draw against Grêmio in the Gre-Nal derby. Matheus Cunha replaced Anthoni, while Matheus Bahia took the place of Alan Patrick.

Internacional had collected seven wins, 11 draws and 12 losses across their first 30 league matches, according to Sportsgambler.com. The team had scored 33 goals and conceded 38, leaving them with a goal difference of minus five.

Mirassol entered the contest after a 1-0 defeat to Bahia, extending their winless run to two matches, according to Yahoo Sports. Sportsgambler.com reported that Mirassol had recorded eight wins, nine draws and 13 losses, scoring 34 goals and conceding 44 for a goal difference of minus 10.

The clubs' recent head-to-head record favored Mirassol. Sportsgambler.com reported that the team had won their previous two meetings with Internacional, including a 3-1 home victory and a subsequent 2-1 away win.

Mirassol's recent performance figures showed four draws, four losses and two wins across their last 10 matches, according to Sportsgambler.com. The team averaged 1.1 goals per match, 47.6% possession and 4.2 corners, while conceding an average of 1.7 goals.

Internacional recorded two wins, five draws and three losses over their last 10 matches, the same outlet reported. The side averaged 1.1 goals per game, 39.4% possession and 3.5 corners, while allowing 1.2 goals per match.

Among Mirassol's leading scorers, Carlos Eduardo had four goals, while Bruno Santos had two, according to Sportsgambler.com. Igor Marques led the team's assist statistics with four assists.

For Internacional, Vitinho led the listed scorers with four goals. Alan Patrick, Bruno Henrique and Johan Carbonero had two goals each, while Alan Patrick and Thiago Maia had recorded two assists apiece over their previous 10 matches, according to the same report.

Sportsgambler.com listed a projected 4-2-3-1 formation for Mirassol, featuring goalkeeper Walter, with Igor Formiga, João Victor, Willian Machado and Reinaldo in defense. Denilson Borges and Neto Moura were projected in midfield, behind Edson Carioca, Eduardo, Alesson and Bruno Santos.

Internacional's projected 3-4-2-1 formation included Matheus Cunha in goal, with Bruno Gomes, Gabriel Mercado and Juninho in defense. Braian Aguirre, Rodrigo Villagra, Thiago Maia and Alexandro Bernabei were listed in midfield, with Vitinho and Calebe supporting Alerrandro in attack.
