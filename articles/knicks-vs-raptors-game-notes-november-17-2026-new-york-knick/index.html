KNICKS EXTEND WINNING STREAK AGAINST RAPTORS TO 14:

Karl-Anthony Towns recorded 28 points, 17 rebounds, three assists and one steal against Toronto (Nov 15). This marks his 13th consecutive double-double to start the season, extending his own record for first in franchise history. Towns recorded his 85th 20-point double-double as a Knick, ranking ninth in franchise history. He also recorded his 343rd such game in his career, ranking fifth amongst active players.

Jalen Brunson recorded 28 points, nine assists, one rebound and one steal against Toronto (Nov 15). This marks his 131st game as a Knick scoring 20+ points with five or more assists, ranking second in franchise history (Walt Frazier- 144).

Landry Shamet recorded 23 points on 66.7-percent shooting, two rebounds, one steal and one block off the bench against Toronto (Nov. 15). This marks his sixth game as a Knick scoring 20+ points, all coming off the bench. Shamet recorded 20+ points on 60.0-percent shooting or better for the seventh time in his career, four of them coming with New York.

OG Anunoby recorded 21 points on 50.0-percent shooting, nine rebounds, two blocks, one assist and one steal against Toronto (Nov. 15). This marks his 10th game as a Knick scoring 20+ points on 50.0-percent shooting or better with two or more blocks, tying Marcus Camby for ninth in franchise history.

Andre Drummond (11,610) surpassed Patrick Ewing (11,607) for 25th on the NBA all-time rebounding list.

The Knicks had four or more players score 20+ points for the first time this season (Brunson, Towns, Anunoby, Shamet). The Knicks pulled down 50+ rebounds for the fifth time this season, tying with Minnesota for second in the NBA. The Knicks dished out 25+ assists for the 10th time this season, tying with six other teams for third in the NBA.
