Sri Lanka trumps Brook’s record double century to beat England by 2 wickets in cricket tri-series

TheTools · 24.10.2026 10:35 · source · permalink

RAWALPINDI, Pakistan (AP) — Sri Lanka trumped Harry Brook’s record double century Thursday with the second-highest successful run chase ever in one-day international cricket history to hand England a second successive defeat in the Pakistan tri-series.
