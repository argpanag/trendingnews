Sabrina Carpenter Teams Up With ‘La La Land’ Director for ‘Playful, Unexpected’ Whiskey Campaign

TheTools · 10.11.2026 18:17 · source · permalink

The singer's new spot takes inspiration from French New Wave cinema.

Sabrina Carpenter has a new favorite “Go Go Juice.” The pop singer stars in a new campaign by La La Land director Damien Chazelle for her ongoing partnership with Johnnie Walker, as revealed on Tuesday (Nov. 10).

Taking inspiration from French New Wave cinema, the visual shows Carpenter walking into a jazzy nightclub and grabbing a drink made with Johnnie Walker at the bar. The party kicks into gear only as the singer struts through the venue, with confetti flying through the air over her head as she finds a couple of friends and clinks glasses with them.

“Keep walking,” Carpenter says into the camera.

In a statement about the campaign, the Grammy winner said that she and the Oscar-winning director “wanted to create a true cinematic experience that was playful, unexpected and full of personality,” adding, “I’m a longtime fan of Damien’s work, so it was exciting to bring this visual world to life alongside him.”

In an interview with Elle alongside the Johnnie Walker collab, Carpenter highlighted why it was particularly special working with a director known for music-driven movies such as La La Land and Whiplash. “Acting and music have always gone hand in hand for me,” the musician — who was recently cast as Ginger Rogers in an upcoming Fred Astaire biopic — explained. “With music, I get to tell stories through my own perspective, and with acting I get to step into someone else’s world and bring their story to life, and that is always an exhilarating feeling for me.”

As for which movie musical she’d like to star in someday, Carpenter — who made her acting debut on Law & Order: Special Victims Unit in 2011 — told the publication, “I just rewatched 9 to 5, and you know I’m the biggest Dolly [Parton] fan. So that would be a fun one.”

The Girl Meets World alum last released music in 2025, scoring her second No. 1 album on the Billboard 200 with Man’s Best Friend. The album spawned Billboard Hot 100-topping single “Manchild.”
