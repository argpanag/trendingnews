Australia collapses to 60-7 in pursuit of 288 as South Africa closes in on victory in 3rd test

TheTools · 31.10.2026 13:28 · source · permalink

CAPE TOWN, South Africa (AP) — Kagiso Rabada and Simon Harmer shared six wickets as Australia collapsed to 60-7 at lunch in its second innings of the third test against South Africa at Newlands on Saturday.
