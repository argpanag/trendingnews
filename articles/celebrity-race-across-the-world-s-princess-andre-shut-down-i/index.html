Celebrity Race Across the World's Princess Andre 'shut down' in PTSD flashback

TheTools · 18.11.2026 11:39 · source · permalink

Princess Andre suffered a traumatic flashback during her time on Celebrity Race Across the World.

Last week on the BBC show, viewers watched as Princess and her brother Junior were trying to make their way to the next checkpoint. During their journey, the duo got a taxi at night, which suddenly pulled over at the side of the road.

Although their driver was stopping for petrol, this brought back memories for Princess, who quickly began to panic. It came after she was involved in a violent carjacking in 2018 alongside her mum Katie Price and Junior.

At the time, six men held the family at gunpoint, while the men demanded Katie hand over her children. In the episode, BBC viewers saw Princess become visibly distressed as her brother reassured her.

Speaking about the experience to Louise Minchin and Gethin Jones on Wednesday's Morning Live (November 17), the 19-year-old touched on what she was going through in that moment.

Gethin commented: "This is a very emotional part of the show; you're on a car journey, which brings back lots of memories for both of you. You're very open and honest about what happened, aren't you?"

Princess replied: "Yeah, it was a scary time, to be honest, and I didn't realise that it still affected me until that happened. Afterwards I felt silly though."

The ITV star shared that her memories were brought back during their time on the BBC show as they were travelling at night and stopped at the side of a road, a similar layout to what happened to her as a child.

She added: "I was like, 'Oh my god what is happening?' I kind of just shut down. But you [Junior] were there for me, as always."

Elsewhere during their interview, Princess revealed her brother was calling her weeks before they began their journey, making sure she was ready and prepared.

She told the Morning Live stars: "I was like, 'I'm fine!' but I got there, hadn't worn my shoes in, I forgot my watch, I forgot my fan, I forgot all these things. When I got there, I was like, 'This is a reality check; I'm not ready for this'."

Currently, the pair are first in the competition, having beaten the other celebrities to the checkpoint in the latest part of the leg. However, as another episode begins tonight (November 18) the positions could all change.

Junior and Princess told Louise and Gethin that they still remain in contact with the other stars on the show, and have a group chat to speak about their experience. Junior shared that he was having some of them over to his house late that day to watch the next episode.

Morning Live continues weekdays on BBC One from 9:30am. Celebrity Race Across the World airs Wednesday's on BBC One from 8pm.
