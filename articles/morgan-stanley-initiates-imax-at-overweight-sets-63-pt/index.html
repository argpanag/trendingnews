Morgan Stanley initiates IMAX at Overweight, sets $63 PT

TheTools · 28.09.2026 00:25 · source · permalink

Morgan Stanley initiated coverage of IMAX IMAX with an overweight rating and a $63 price target, implying about 20% upside from the stock's last close.

Analyst Sean Diffley expects global IMAX IMAX box office to reach $1.5 billion in 2026, $1.6 billion in 2027, and $1.7 billion in 2028, with screen growth and local-language content supporting the expansion despite a tough comparison following The Odyssey.

“More Screens + More Films => More Box Office $s => More Screens + More Films,” Diffley said, describing what it sees as a self-reinforcing growth cycle.

The brokerage forecasts roughly 5% annual IMAX IMAX screen growth through 2028, saying the company is “masterfully positioned” with consumers and filmmakers. It also expects local-language content to remain an increasingly important driver, noting its share of global IMAX IMAX box office rose from 16% in 2022 to 32% in 2025.

Analysts see EBITDA and EPS compound annual growth of about 16% and 21%, respectively, through 2028, while free-cash-flow conversion is expected to exceed 50%.

“2026 will be a record box office year for IMAX,” Diffley said, while acknowledging that The Odyssey creates a “tough comp” for 2027.

Key risks include film timing, installation constraints, limited China slate visibility, and elevated valuation. Morgan Stanley also cited potential strategic interest as “downside support.”

Shares were trading about 2% higher in the premarket trading on Thursday.
