Is Claude Down? Anthropic Confirms Fix for AI Chatbot Outage

TheTools · 29.09.2026 15:28 · source · permalink

Reports of 'elevated error rates' with Claude AI began around 10 a.m. ET on Tuesday.

Getting your Trinity Audio player ready...

UPDATE 11:28 a.m. ET: Anthropic confirms its AI tools are returning to normal after an hour-long outage affecting its chatbot, Code, Cowork, and more.

If you attempted to use Claude between 10 a.m. and 10:59 a.m. ET today, Anthropic warns your messages may not have been saved. Expect more updates from the AI brand throughout the rest of the day, as it says it will continue to monitor any continued errors in the coming hours.

Original story:
Struggling to access Claude AI? You're not the only one: another outage is preventing tens of thousands of users from using Anthropic's tools.

The outage began around 10 a.m. ET on Sept. 29, when Downdetector reports began to spike. Anthropic acknowledged the issues 20 minutes later, saying that almost all of its tools were experiencing issues, except Claude for Government.

Claude AI, Code, and Cowork are all experiencing partial outages, while Console and API tools are showing what Anthropic calls "Degraded Performance."

Anthropic is working on a fix and says it applied a "mitigation" at 10:36 a.m. ET, which saw error rates drop "substantially." However, the brand acknowledged that it wouldn't be enough to fully fix its services, meaning you may be locked out for longer.

In its most recent update, Claude's status website said, "Existing conversations are mostly working again." However, there's a second issue that prevents users from logging in via single sign-on or an Apple account. Anthropic recommends that you avoid logging out to maintain access.

It also says that starting new text or voice conversations, making purchases, uploading files, and Claude Code or Cowork sessions are still experiencing issues. Anthropic says it's working on a fix for these problems and will provide a further update by 11:30 a.m. ET.
