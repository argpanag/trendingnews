Ladislav Krejci returns from calf injury to captain Czechia against England

Ladislav Krejci marked his return from injury by captaining Czechia against England, starting Tuesday night’s UEFA Nations League tie.

According to ExpressAndStar.com, it was the Wolves defender’s first appearance since a calf problem suffered in training before the Black Country derby.

OneFootball Videos

The setback ruled him out of Wolves’ meeting with West Brom, after which he linked up with the national team for international duty.

He was left out of Saturday’s Nations League defeat by Croatia, then restored to face Thomas Tuchel’s side as captain.

Czechia meet Spain on Saturday before another game against England on Tuesday, which will conclude their international schedule.
