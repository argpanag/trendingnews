Black coach tentatively settles discrimination/retaliation suit vs. Covina Valley Unified

TheTools · 23.11.2026 19:24 · source · permalink

LOS ANGELES — A Black former Covina High School football coach and athletic director tentatively settled his discrimination and retaliation suit against the Covina-Valley Unified School District.

Plaintiff Kevin Glaspy’s Los Angeles Superior Court lawsuit also named as defendants Supt. Elizabeth Eminhizer, Assistant Supt. Michele Doll and Chief Business Officer Manuel Correa. On Wednesday, Glaspy’s lawyer filed court papers with Judge Thomas D. Long notifying him of a “conditional” accord in the case along with the anticipation a request for dismissal will be filed by March 18, 2027.

No terms were divulged. In their previous court papers, defense attorneys denied Glaspy’s allegations and alleged, among other things, that the plaintiff destroyed documents vital to the defense and that third parties shared in responsibility for any of his alleged damages.

Glaspy, now 53, was hired as a teacher in 1995. That same year, Glaspy was appointed Covina High’s varsity track coach and in 2002 he was named the school’s athletic director, positions he held when he was fired.

In 2022, Glaspy advocated for employee rights in a labor dispute, the suit stated. In 2024, he organized the SGV All-Star Bowl, which features top graduating high school senior football players from across the San Gabriel Valley. Before the game, he disclosed alleged fund distribution and safety needs and in retaliation for coming forward he was put on leave, according to the suit.

Unlike the other two coaches, who are not Black, Glaspy was fired in January 2024, the suit states. The district refused to clear Glaspy of any wrongdoing and instead issued a “factually inaccurate written reprimand” to justify the discipline imposed, the suit filed in February 2025 alleged.
