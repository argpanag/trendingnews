Larry Birkhead celebrates daughter Dannielynn's 20th birthday with rare photos

TheTools · 10.09.2026 08:52 · source · permalink

The proud father posted a photo collage set to Lenny Kravitz's 'Happy Birthday' as Anna Nicole Smith's daughter turned 20

Fox News Flash top entertainment headlines Aug. 31

Fox News Flash top entertainment and celebrity headlines are here.

Larry Birkhead is celebrating his daughter's 20th birthday with a rare collage of photos.

On Sept. 7, Birkhead uploaded a video collage with rare photos of Dannielynn to Instagram. "Happy 20th Birthday Dannielynn!❤️ Love, Dad ❤️," Larry captioned the post with "Happy Birthday" by Lenny Kravitz playing.

Included in the post were recent photos of Dannielynn and some from her childhood. The first picture showed her sitting on steps, dressed in a black outfit with black sunglasses.

Larry Birkhead celebrated his daughter's 20th birthday on Instagram. (Stephen J. Cohen/Getty Images)

The second photo showed Dannielynn smiling with short red hair. The video then flashed back to her childhood, with a young Dannielynn wearing a one-piece bathing suit and smiling.

Larry shares daughter Dannielynn with the late Anna Nicole Smith. The couple met at the Kentucky Derby in 2004 and welcomed their daughter in 2006. After Smith’s death in 2007, Birkhead became Dannielynn’s primary caregiver and has largely raised her away from the spotlight.

Anna Nicole Smith is Dannielynn's mother. (Jeff Snyder/FilmMagic)

Larry and Dannielynn have upheld their Kentucky Derby tradition and attend yearly. The father and daughter have attended the Kentucky Derby every year since Dannielynn was three-years-old.
