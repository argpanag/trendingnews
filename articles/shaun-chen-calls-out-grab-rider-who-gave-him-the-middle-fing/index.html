Shaun Chen calls out Grab rider who gave him the middle finger: 'Why so rude?'

TheTools · 12.11.2026 23:00 · source · permalink

Shaun Chen has taken to his Instagram to call out an aggressive Grab rider after the latter made a rude hand gesture at him.

The 48-year-old Mediacorp actor posted a dashcam reel and two photos of the incident as Instagram Stories on Thursday (Nov 12).

The reel showed Shaun's vehicle stationary on the road at an unknown location in Singapore. As the Grab rider rode past him, he gave the actor the middle finger.

Shaun tagged Grab Singapore's Instagram account and wrote in the caption of his post: "Why so rude?? Is it normal to point middle finger like that?"

The two other photos he posted were close-up images of the rider making the hand gesture and of his motorbike plate number.

In response to AsiaOne's queries, a Grab spokesperson said they are aware of the video circulating online and are currently looking into the matter.

"Road safety and mutual respect are important to us. We expect all our partners to uphold our code of conduct, and will take appropriate action where needed," they added.

This is not the first time Shaun was vocal about him and his family's experience on the road.

In 2025, after his father died in a hit-and-run accident in Malaysia, he posted an Instagram Reel containing a dashcam footage of the incident.

Shaun, who is Malaysian, also included a photo of the license plate number of the suspected vehicle that caused the accident. He clarified then that he is not seeking revenge but simply wants the person responsible to acknowledge the harm they had done.

The driver later turned themselves in to the Malaysian police.
