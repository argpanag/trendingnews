Officer injured during arrest involving dirt bike rider, police say

TheTools · 09.10.2026 01:24 · source · permalink

SPRINGFIELD, MA (WGGB/WSHM) – A Springfield police officer suffered minor injuries while trying to arrest a dirt bike rider Thursday afternoon.

Springfield Police spokesperson Ryan Walsh said an officer was working an extra detail on the 1300 block of Carew Street around 6 p.m. Thursday while a restaurant and radio station were holding an event. The officers reportedly saw scooters, motorcycles, and several dirt bikes committing various traffic violations in the area.

One of the dirt bike riders, identified by police as 28-year-old Chamil Guadarrama of Springfield, allegedly almost hit a pedestrian with the bike. The officer tried to place Guadarrama under arrest, but he refused to turn off or get off the dirt bike.

“Guadarrama is alleged to have driven the dirt bike at the officer, as the officer held onto the handles,” Walsh explained.

Police said Guadarrama fell off the bike and the officer sprayed OC spray. Two other suspects, who have not been identified, intervened. Police added one suspect took the dirt bike and fled, while the other pushed the officer and tried to choke him. Guadarrama then allegedly punched the officer.

The suspect who tried to choke the officer was tased, but continued to resist arrest as Guadarrama allegedly hit the officer with a motorcycle helmet. The unidentified suspect fled the scene in a vehicle, which was found abandoned on the 1200 block of St. James Avenue.

Assisting officers placed Guadarrama was placed under arrest by assisting officers on charges including reckless operation of a motor vehicle, unlicensed operation of a motor vehicle, unregistered motor vehicle, uninsured motor vehicle, operating an off-highway vehicle on a public way, unnecessary noise from an off-highway vehicle, resisting arrest, two counts of assault and battery with a dangerous weapon, and assault and battery on a police officer.

Walsh noted that the officer suffered minor injuries.
