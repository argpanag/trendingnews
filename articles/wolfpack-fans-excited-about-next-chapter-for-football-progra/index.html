Wolfpack fans excited about next chapter for football program

TheTools · 13.11.2026 04:44 · source · permalink

RALEIGH, N.C. (WTVD) -- Inside Sammy's Tap and Grill, energy is high with anticipation of the next chapter of NC State football and what that can mean for the program -- and for business.

"We have a lot of students that come here and eat, and we're just hoping it boosts business all the way around," said Patrick Humphrey, the manager of Sammy's, a fan favorite for fans. Right now on the football program has not been great as of late. It's going to bring excitement back."

NC State's Board of Trustees is expected to make Philip Rivers' contract final on Friday morning.

Humphrey said Sammy's is already a favorite for the Wolfpack, but a big name like Rivers could bring big business.

"I think they need something new and exciting. And I think Philip Rivers can 100% produce new, exciting excitement for NC State football," Humphrey said.

Rivers represents a storied past and a promising future for NC State football.

Rivers is a former Wolfpack quarterback legend who broke nearly every NC State and ACC record during his college career.

He led the Wolfpack to four straight bowl games and was part of NC State's only 10-plus-win season.

That's the kind of success fans want to see happen again.

"Philip Rivers, it's a full-circle moment. Really shows how big of a family Pack Nation is," said Dalton Gay, an NC State senior. "Like, you know, he played here, is a great quarterback. I've seen his name in the rafters since I was 13."

Gay is ready for a fresh start for the football program.

"It's my senior year, so my freshman year, we came in, we finished Top 25. And then last year I got to see us beat Georgia Tech and get to rush the field for the first time. That was awesome. And this year we've been struggling," said Gay. "I think it's a big boost to us, to the entire school, as well as the program."

The board of trustees meets Friday morning. Any official announcement about the Wolfpack's new football coach won't come until after that.

But Wolfpack fans can hardly wait.
