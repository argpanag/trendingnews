  • Talent agent Ari Emanuel recently parted ways with Meghan Markle, but her brand, WME, continues to represent her and Archewell.
  • Meghan Markle is reportedly considering a return to acting, with rumors linking her to a potential role in Netflix's The Gentlemen.
  • Despite previously stating she was "done" with acting, Meghan Markle has not ruled out the possibility of returning to the screen in the future.

Meghan Markle and talent agent Ari Emanuel parted ways a few months ago, although the extraordinaire's brand, WME, still looks after her and Archewell.

However, during a recent interview with the Sunday Times, Ari delivered a blunt answer when he was asked about the Duchess of Sussex's future career. "Here's the great thing: I don't have to think about that any more," he simply said.

Although neither Meghan nor Ari have ever commented on their professional relationship, things reportedly soured pretty quickly, with Variety reporting that Ari dropped off her account after just three months, although she remained signed to WME.

Meghan will be looking to restart her career after she returned to the UK with her husband, Prince Harry, and their two children, Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet, last month.

Ari didn't hold back when asked about Meghan's career (@ Getty Images for Academy Museum)

Just days after she stepped back into the country, it was reported that Meghan was being lined up to star in the next series of Netflix's The Gentlemen, which stars Theo James.

Deadline initially reported that Meghan has had preliminary conversations about a potential role in the show, which would mark her first significant acting role since her marriage to Prince Harry in 2018.

A source close to the family told HELLO! previously: "There have been inquiries in the entertainment space recently and things that she is considering but nothing is firm yet. There are only conversations."

After the show was officially confirmed to be returning for a third season, Manda Levin, Netflix's scripted chief in the UK, was asked about the possibility of the Duchess of Sussex taking on a role in the popular drama.

Meghan has been linked to a role in The Gentleman (@ Netflix)

"All bets are off," she cryptically responded, according to Deadline. Notably, she did not issue a denial, which means that the door could still be open for Meghan.

Meghan's acting career

Meghan's most famous role was paralegal Rachel Zane in Suits, but she also starred in Hallmark movies and films Horrible Bosses and Remember Me. She put her acting career on hold when she married Harry in 2018 in St George's Chapel, Windsor, telling the BBC at the time it marked the start of a "new chapter".

Meghan's breakout role was in Suits (@ Ian Watson/USA Network/NBCU Photo Bank/NBCUniversal via Getty Images)

However, she hasn't left her TV career in the past, and has appeared several times on-screen including with her 2022 Netflix docuseries, Harry & Meghan, and her cooking and lifestyle show, With Love, Meghan.

In 2022, Meghan shut down the possibility of a return to acting, telling Variety that she was "done". Although she did add: "I guess never say never, but my intention is to absolutely not."
