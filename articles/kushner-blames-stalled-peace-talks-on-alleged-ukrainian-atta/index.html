Kushner blames stalled peace talks on alleged Ukrainian attack on Putin’s residence — an attack both U.S. intelligence and Trump say did not happen — Meduza

TheTools · 26.11.2026 14:50 · source · permalink

U.S. presidential envoy Jared Kushner said peace negotiations between Ukraine and Russia stalled in early 2026 after an alleged Ukrainian attack on Vladimir Putin’s residence in Valdai. He made the comments in an interview with Axel Springer CEO Mathias Döpfner for Döpfner’s podcast MD Meets.

Kushner said he was asked to join the peace talks in November 2025, when the U.S. “started making some progress.”

“Then there was the attack the Ukrainians did on Putin’s house at the end of December which I think pushed [Putin] back,” Kushner said.

He added that the U.S. tried again starting in February to make progress in the talks, but “it seemed like there wasn’t really a way forward at that time,” because Ukraine and Russia wanted to see whether they could gain ground on the battlefield.

“Between February and September we were in constant communication but we didn’t see a lot of flexibility or opening from the sides where there would be overlap,” Kushner said.

On December 29, 2025, Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov said that Ukraine had attacked Vladimir Putin’s residence in Valdai, in the Novgorod region, with drones. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky called the statement “yet another lie” from Russia. Soon afterward, Donald Trump said that Putin had told him about the alleged attack in a phone call on December 29.

According to information from the CIA, Ukraine did not attempt to attack Putin’s residence. The CIA concluded that Ukrainian drones had tried to strike a military facility in the same region as Putin’s residence overnight on December 29, but that the facility is not located near it. On January 5, Trump said he did not believe reports of an attack on the Russian president’s residence: “I don’t believe that strike happened. […] There is something that happened fairly nearby, but it had nothing to do with this house.”
