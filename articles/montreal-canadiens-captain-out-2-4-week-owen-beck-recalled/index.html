Montreal Canadiens Captain Out 2-4 Week; Owen Beck Recalled

TheTools · 22.10.2026 17:21 · source · permalink

The Montreal Canadiens will face the Chicago Blackhawks tomorrow night at 8:00 PM EST. They got tough news today as their captain Nick Suzuki will be out two to four weeks with an upper-body injury. The injury will end Suzuki’s ironman streak which was at 546 consecutive games. The Canadiens recalled center Owen Beck from the Laval Rocket in the AHL. Beck was returned to Laval yesterday after appearing in two games for Montreal and he was a minus-two.

Suzuki in nine games this season coming off 12 points on five goals and seven assists. He’s a plus-six with a power-play goal and a 53.8% at the faceoff circle. He’s coming off a season in which he won his first Selke Trophy also finishing fifth in Hart Trophy voting. Suzuki in his 546 games has recorded 488 points on 172 goals and 316 assists. He’s a -14 with 54 power-play goals, eight shorthanded goals, 20 game-winning goals, and a 49.4% faceoff percentage.

Beck at Laval this season has a goal, and an assist; he’s a plus-two. In 127 AHL games he has 79 points on 29 goals and 50 assists. He’s a +18 with four power-play goals, four shorthanded goals, and six game-winning goals. In 18 Calder Cup Playoff games he has 11 points on five goals and six assists. He’s a plus-two with a power-play goal and a game-winning goal.
