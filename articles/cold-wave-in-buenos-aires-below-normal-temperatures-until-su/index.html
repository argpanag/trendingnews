Cold wave in Buenos Aires: below-normal temperatures until Sunday, gradual improvement from Monday in the AMBA - Noticias Ambientales

TheTools · 13.09.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

In recent days, the Buenos Aires Metropolitan Area (AMBA) has been under the effects of an intense cold wave, a phenomenon that will last until Sunday. The temperatures have dropped considerably, falling well below what is expected for the month of September. The weather conditions have been marked by persistent southeast winds and a slight sudestada, with values on some days not exceeding 12°C.

Saturday presented with a mostly cloudy sky, light drizzles, and temperatures between 7°C and 12°C, far from the usual highs of 20°C for this season.

Heat Forecast in the AMBA

The impact of the southeast wind, reaching gusts of up to 50 km/h, also caused moderate surges in the Río de la Plata, with tide peaks close to 2.30 meters in the region’s ports.

The National Meteorological Service (SMN) has forecasted that Sunday will be the last day of this cold period, predicting a low of 6°C and a high of 13°C, with partly cloudy skies and no expected rain.

Starting Monday, a change in the weather is anticipated. With the entry of northern winds, a gradual increase in temperatures is expected, reaching a high of 16°C, maintaining stable conditions.

For Tuesday, the trend of rising temperatures will continue, with values between 8°C and 19°C. However, there could be a temporary shift to the south in the wind on Wednesday, causing a thermal drop, with temperatures forecasted between 6°C and 16°C.

The most significant rebound in temperatures will arrive towards the end of the week. Thursday is expected to see highs reaching 23°C, and by Friday, they could reach 25°C, favoring sunnier and more stable days.

This change in the weather will allow more pleasant temperatures to replace the icy winter days, marking a transition to spring in Buenos Aires.
