Fire and water rescue teams from Alabama prepare to mobilize for the Gulf Coast

TheTools · 08.10.2026 02:27 · source · permalink

Vestavia Hills and several other local fire departments are preparing to assist with rescue operations on the Gulf Coast.

According to a news release from the City of Vestavia Hills, a five-engine team, a battalion chief and a rescue transport unit are preparing to mobilize to assist in storm emergency efforts.

The fire departments sending crews are from Chelsea, Auburn, Morris, Irondale, Pelham and Cahaba Valley.

Hoover, Trussville, Vestavia Hills, Mountain Brook, and Calera Fire Departments are preparing to deploy specialized water rescue teams to assist with potential flooding and swiftwater rescue operations, said the city's news release.

The deployment is being coordinated through the Alabama Association of Fire Chiefs Mutual Aid Consortium (AAFCMAC).

“This is exactly why the Mutual Aid Consortium was established. By coordinating resources before a storm makes landfall, we can position trained personnel and specialized equipment where they may be needed most. This deployment represents an important milestone in our ability to support communities across Alabama during major disasters,” said Fire Chief Marvin Green, Mutual Aid Coordinator for the Alabama Association of Fire Chiefs Mutual Aid Consortium.

The teams are expected to leave Thursday, October 8, and remain deployed through Sunday, October 11.
