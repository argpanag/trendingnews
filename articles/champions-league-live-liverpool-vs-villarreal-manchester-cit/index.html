  1. 'Another positive for Liverpool but there is plenty to work on'published at 22:05 BST
    22:05 BST

    Liverpool 4-3 Villarreal

    Alan Shearer
    Former England striker on Amazon Prime

    Liverpool's first 35 minutes were awful. They were so negative, second to everything, 2-0 down and then somehow they clawed their way back and for 10 minutes they were better.

    Iraola made a change at half-time, and put Gravenberch into the right-back position, and brought Wirtz on, and they were much better in every department.

    Closing down, passing, creating chances, bar the last few minutes where they made it difficult for themselves when Villareal came back into it, gave them a little scare but it's still a win, another positive for Liverpool but there is plenty to work on.
