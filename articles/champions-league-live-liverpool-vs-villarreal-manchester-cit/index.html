  8. Match of the daypublished at 19:54 BST
    19:54 BST

    Paris St-Germain v Barcelona (20:00 BST)

    Jonty Colman
    BBC Sport Journalist

    Paris St-Germain v Barcelona - a fixture that could prove to be the most exciting in this season's Champions League.

    The free-scoring teams start match week three as the league phase's top two, with PSG above Barcelona on goals scored and with no other sides having netted more in the competition so far.

    PSG were 2-1 winners when they met at this stage last season, with Luis Enrique's side going on to win a second successive Champions League title, having also beaten Barcelona over two legs in the quarter-finals three seasons ago.

    "It will be a fascinating game," European football expert Julien Laurens told BBC Sport's Euro Leagues podcast.

    Since the start of the 2025 Club World Cup, Barca and PSG rank second and third respectively for most goals scored per match by clubs in Europe's top five leagues.

    Barca have netted 192 times in 68 games at a rate of 2.82 per match, with PSG scoring 154 in 67 games at 2.3 per outing.

    Only Bayern Munich rank above both.

  11. 'I am grateful to be fit and healthy' - Guehi on busy schedulepublished at 19:47 BST
    19:47 BST

    Man City v AEK Athens (20:00 BST)

    It has been a busy week for Manchester City defender Marc Guehi.

    The 26-year-old has played every minute of every game for City since the international break - Liverpool, PSG and Ipswich.

    Tonight, though, he will get a bit of a rest as he is on the bench. But the England man doesn't mind the packed schedule.

    "It's been enjoyable, to be honest, not to train and to play games," Guehi said.

    "A lot of meetings and reviewing clips inside. When we can we go on the pitch to go through tactics before the game, but it is a lot of recovery and sleep to make sure the body is right for the next game."

    When asked if he is "learning on the job" because of the lack of training, he said: "The clips we go through [are] things we can do better. For example, in the last game [against Ipswich], I know I can do better. It's as much learning as you can do on the pitch or off the field with the clips we're watching. We try to do it in the small amount of time we have.

    "There is work we can do as a team because there are things that can happen before [goals], but when I look at those goals at the weekend, that is down to me. That's for me to look at and try to put right in the next game.

    "It's not a strain. I am grateful to be fit and healthy. It's not easy to be healthy at this level with all the games."

    Fenerbahce 2-2 Slavia Prague, Sabah 1-2 Borussia Dortmund

    In the early games, Borussia Dortmund gained their second win in three Champions League games with a 2-1 victory over Sabah in Azerbaijan.

  14. 'I hope we are at the level of the game'published at 19:39 BST
    19:39 BST

    Liverpool v Villarreal (20:00 BST)

    Liverpool

    Liverpool manager Andoni Irola, speaking before the game: “Obviously, I think we are going from two important wins for us, but we cannot stop, and it doesn’t stop. This is another big game, the Champions League with a very good team in front of us, and I hope we are at the level of the game.”

    On the six changes from the weekend: “Different circumstances. It’s their fourth game in 10 days, and always Florian [Wirtz] and Bradley [Barcola] have played almost every minute, so I think we have to be sensible, and also with Alex [Isak], who had a massive injury. He was feeling well, but to play again in less than three days. I think we have to be considerate and manage the minutes a little bit. But they are all ready to go in the second half.”

    On Lewis Koumas: “I think he is doing really well otherwise, we wouldn’t trust him to start this game, and I hope he continues doing what he’s doing, which is helping the team a lot. Being vertical, attacking the box, and finishing chances. I think he played well against Man City starting and I hope today is another one of those games.”

    On Rio Ngumoha: “I think of Rio very highly. He was very good in the other half against Brentford, and also in the Champions League last game, and I hope he can have a good performance. I think [Villarreal] full-backs are attacking so he’ll have to do the job both ways, but I think he’s in a good place.”
