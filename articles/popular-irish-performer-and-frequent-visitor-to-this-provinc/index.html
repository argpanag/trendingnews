Popular Irish Performer, and Frequent Visitor to this Province, Johnny McEvoy Dead at 81

TheTools · 10.11.2026 16:54 · source · permalink

Johnny McEvoy visiting the VOCM studios in 2018 with Irish Newfoundland Host Greg Smith (VOCM News)

An Irish performer who was a regular visitor to this province has passed away.

Johnny McEvoy was 81.

Irish broadcaster RTÉ announced the news today.

McEvoy has performed a long list of popular tunes – many of which were staples on the VOCM Irish Newfoundland Show – including Muirsheen Durkin, The Boston Burglar, and Long Before Your Time.
