Jennifer Burnham is mourning a devastating loss.

The lifestyle influencer shared that her and husband Bryce Burnham's 4-year-old son Baxter died on Oct. 5 as she penned an emotional message to their little boy.

"Baxter, from the moment you were born, you have always been our dream come true," she captioned the Oct. 12 Instagram video. "You have the most perfect angelic face and the sweetest personality. You are always kind to everyone and always love holding a car in each hand or a bag of them with you everywhere."

Jennifer, who also shared a montage of sweet moments with Baxter and his milestones over the years, reflected on what it was like being his mom.

"You love your family, cars, chocolate chip cookies, karate, and all of your friends," she continued. "You pick mommy flowers everyday on our walks."

The influencer—also mom to 2-month-old son Barron—concluded the post with a heartbreaking farewell to Baxter.

"We miss you too much but we know you are still with us everyday, watching over mommy, daddy and your baby brother Barron from heaven," she wrote. "We love you always and forever."

After welcoming Baxter in January 2022, Jennifer had shared just how much life had changed for her and Bryce.

"This year was our best one yet," she wrote that December. "Bax was born in January and we spent most of this year with quiet moments at home making memories as a family of 3! Looking forward to all that this next year brings. Happy New Year!"

Jennifer Burnham/Instagram

The following year brought even more joy as the couple watched Baxter reach new milestones. As they celebrated his first birthday, Jennifer couldn't help but beam with pride.

"You and your dad are my favorite guys in the whole world," she captioned her January 2023 Instagram post. "They say the days are long but the years are short and I can't believe how true that is."

"It feels like yesterday that you were born and now you're ONE," she added. "I can't wait to see what magic you create this next year my angel! I love you always and forever!"
