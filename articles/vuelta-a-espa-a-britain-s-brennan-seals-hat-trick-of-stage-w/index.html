Vuelta a España: Britain’s Brennan seals hat-trick of stage wins as Mas retains lead

TheTools · 02.09.2026 17:34 · source · permalink

  • Stage 11 won by 21-year-old as dazzling debut continues

  • Lewis Askey claims opening stage in Tour of Britain

Stage 11 won by 21-year-old as dazzling debut continues

Lewis Askey claims opening stage in Tour of Britain

Matthew Brennan triumphed again in the Vuelta a España on Wednesday, becoming the first cyclist to win three stages in his first Grand Tour since Tadej Pogacar seven years ago. The British rider secured his third victory on the 11th stage, a mostly flat route of 153.8km from Cartagena to Lorca.

“We took the initiative today again,” Brennan said. “The boys pulled as hard as they could even after a tough day in the legs yesterday. We still went full gas. Coming into the final, we really held our own place and knew that we were all strong enough to kind of get to where we want to be.”

The 21-year-old of team Visma-Lease a Bike also won the second and fifth stages to become the youngest cyclist with multiple stage wins in the Vuelta since Pogacar’s three victories in 2019.

“It’s massive [to have won three stages],” Brennan said. “I’m with a team full of massive champions. We’ve got guys who have podiumed in Grand Tours and won them as well, and guys who’ve won classics and huge races. And the fact they go for me is an amazing opportunity. I’m really thankful that they go full gas for me.”

A sprinter had not claimed at least three victories in his maiden Grand Tour since Fernando Gaviria in the Giro d’Italia in 2017. Pogacar also had already won three stages in this year’s Vuelta before a crash forced him to withdraw on Saturday while holding a big overall lead. He fractured his left collarbone and could not continue on his quest for a career sweep of the three Grand Tours.

Enric Mas, from Movistar Team, took the overall lead from Pogacar and has kept it since. He is 1min 18sec ahead of Primoz Roglic, from team Red Bull-Bora Hansgrohe. Roglic is chasing a record fifth Vuelta title.

On Thursday, riders will face a 12th stage featuring a tough mountain route in southern Spain. It includes a traditional double climb at Velefique and Calar Alto, marking an important day for the riders fighting for the general classification.

The three-week Vuelta finishes in Granada on 13 September.

Britain’s Lewis Askey, meanwhile, won the opening stage of the Tour of Britain, the NSN rider attacking on the cobbles of the Michaelgate climb in Lincoln and riding clear to victory. The Italian Filippo Ganna of Netcompany Ineos came home second, two seconds down, while the Belgian Tim Wellens of UAE Team Emirates-XRG was third.

“It feels amazing, especially to have my grandparents there at the finish line. It was super special,” said Askey. “I ride my bike to win races. It’s great when you come second, come third and do good performances, but nothing matches managing to put your hands up in the air.”
