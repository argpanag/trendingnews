‘DWTS’: Ezra Frech Addresses Hailey Bills Romance Rumors

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:02 · source · permalink

What To Know

  • Dancing With the Stars fans have been shipping Ezra Frech and pro Hailey Bills as a couple.
  • In a new interview, Frech reacted to the romance rumors for the first time.
  • He revealed his relationship status and shared what he’s going to be up to after the season ends.

Ezra Frech has his eye on the Dancing With the Stars Mirrorball trophy as one of the five contestants in the Season 35 Finals, but his journey on the show has also been consumed by buzz about his love life. For weeks now, fans have been shipping Frech with pro dancer Hailey Bills, and he finally addressed the rumors straight on.

“The internet is crazy,” Frech, who’s partnered with Daniella Karagach in the competition, told Cosmopolitan. “Right now, I’m definitely single, but not necessarily looking for a relationship. Because, again, once the dance season ends, I’ll be committing myself entirely to track again. I’m enjoying being single, and it’ll probably stay that way for a while.”

Fresh is a Paralympian who will be training for the 2028 Paralympics once Dancing With the Stars is over.

Speculation about a potential romantic relationship between Bills and Frech began when he included a picture of them together in a photo dump from the beginning of the season. The rumors intensified after Sharna Burgess and Emma Slater interviewed Bills on TikTok Live while backstage at a DWTS Live Show and brought up the gossip.

“Do people want to see Ezra Frech?” Burgess asked Bills, who sheepishly responded, “Probably.” Slater then got messy by adding, “How much do you want to see Ezra Frech?” and Bills nervously giggled and responded, “Me? Oh, I would love to see Ezra Frech.”

In that same live stream, though, Frech reiterated that he is “single” and insisted, “I’m locked in on dancing right now.”

All About 'DWTS' Pro Hailey Bills' Relationship Status

Regardless of whether a romance comes out of this for Frech, he’s developed lifelong friendships in his castmates. As he told Cosmo, “Amber [Glenn] is someone who is a real close part of my life and will be for a long time. I feel like her and I are on the same team together in this with Pasha [Pashkov] and Dani. Guillermo [Rodriguez] is someone I’m going to be close to forever. Like me, him, Connor [Wood], we’re in the group chat texting all the time. I love Tyler Cameron. Harry [Shum Jr.] is the sweetest and Jenna [Dewan]. It’s hard to pick and choose. I could see myself still talking to pretty much everyone on the cast.”

In the November 24 finale, Frech is going up against Glenn, Wood, Maura Higgins, and Tatyana Ali for the Mirrorball trophy. All five stars will dance three more times before a winner is named.
