‘DANCING WITH THE STARS’ SETS THE STAGE FOR A MAGICAL ‘DISNEY NIGHT’ ON ABC AND DISNEY+

TheTools · 03.11.2026 07:47 · source · permalink

Press release - Primetime – Unscripted

Cynthia Erivo Joins the Ballroom as Guest Judge on a Special Monday Night

Download Assets HERE

“Disney Night” – The ballroom transforms into Broadway as the remaining 8 couples bring the magic of Disney to life through showstopping performances inspired by beloved Disney stories and their iconic stage adaptations. From enchanting musical numbers to theatrical choreography, the stars take center stage for a night of Disney magic with a Broadway twist. “Disney Night” airs on a special Monday night with guest judge GRAMMY®, Emmy® and Tony® winner and three-time Academy Award® nominee Cynthia Erivo, MONDAY, NOV. 2 (8:00-10:00 p.m. EST/PST, 7:00-9:00 p.m. CST), simulcasting LIVE across both ABC and Disney+ in local time zones and next day on Hulu and Hulu for Disney+ subscribers. The show is produced in front of a live audience by BBC Studios.

The couples (along with their dance styles and songs) are the following:

  • Amber Glenn and partner Pasha Pashkov will perform a Quickstep to “A Star Is Born” from “Hercules”
  • Maura Higgins and partner Mark Ballas will perform a Jive to “You Can Fly! You Can Fly! You Can Fly!” from “Peter Pan”
  • Tatyana Ali and partner Jan Ravnik will perform a Contemporary to “Easy As Life” from “Aida,” with guest artist Heather Headley performing live
  • Harry Shum Jr. and partner Jenna Johnson will perform a Jazz to “Supercalifragilisticexpialidocious” from “Mary Poppins,” with guest artist Kyra Belle Johnson performing live
  • Ezra Frech and partner Daniella Karagach will perform a Samba to “Under the Sea” from “The Little Mermaid,” with guest artist Michael James Scott performing live
  • Julia Stiles and partner Ezra Sosa will perform a Paso Doble to “Satisfied” from “Hamilton”
  • Jenna Dewan and partner Val Chmerkovskiy will perform a Quickstep to “Be Our Guest” from “Beauty and the Beast,” with guest artist Rodney Ingram performing live
  • Connor Wood and partner Rylee Arnold will perform a Waltz to “I See the Light” from “Tangled,” with guest artist Heather Headley performing live

Pro and troupe routines will be performed to “Circle of Life/Nants Ingonyama” from “The Lion King,” “Belle” from “Beauty and the Beast,” “Seize the Day” from “Newsies,” “The Greatest Show” from “The Greatest Showman,” and “Friend Like Me” from “Aladdin.” Plus, the return of the team dances.

TEAM SLAYTOPIA: – “Zoo” from “Zootopia 2”
Host Alfonso Ribeiro with Tatyana (Team Captain) and Jan, Maura and Mark, Amber and Pasha, and Julia and Ezra

TEAM WILDCATS: – “We’re All In This Together” from “High School Musical”
Host Julianne Hough with Connor (Team Captain) and Rylee, Ezra and Dani, Jenna and Val, and Harry and Jenna

Fans will be able to vote during the live simulcast of the show in the ET/CT time zones. During this time, online voting will be available to fans in any time zone in the U.S., its territories and Canada via dwtsvote.abc.com, and fans on participating U.S. wireless carriers can also vote via SMS/text (fans in Canada cannot vote via SMS/text). Live viewer votes from the night will be combined with the judges’ scores to determine who may be in jeopardy of elimination.

After each episode, go behind the curtain with the “Dancing with the Stars Official Podcast,” with host Season 34 contestant Andy Richter sharing cast interviews, special guest appearances, behind-the-scenes insights and expert breakdowns of the night’s performances. An all-new episode airs Wednesday November 4 on Disney+ and Hulu, and wherever you get your podcasts.

“Dancing with the Stars” is the hit series co-hosted by Alfonso Ribeiro and Julianne Hough in which celebrities are paired with trained ballroom dancers to compete in themed choreographed dance routines that are judged by a panel of renowned ballroom experts, including Carrie Ann Inaba, Bruno Tonioli and Derek Hough.
