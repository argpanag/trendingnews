NASHVILLE, Tenn. (WZTV) — A man accused of posing as a San Francisco 49ers player to scam women out of about $1.3 million appeared in a video, apparently pretending to sign with the NFL team.

Authorities say 35-year-old Daejon Love used a fake NFL persona and romantic relationships to convince women to give him money for investments that did not exist.

In the video obtained by OutKick in 2024, Love appears to sign what he represented as an NFL contract.

When asked which team he was joining, Love responded, “Niners.”

However, representatives for the NFL and 49ers told investigators Love had never been employed by either organization, according to an FBI affidavit.

Investigators say Love used dating apps and social media to meet women and portrayed himself as either a 49ers player or wealthy real estate developer.

Authorities say 35-year-old Love and 18-year-old Taylor Jamie Chan, who allegedly posed as Love’s financial adviser, used fake investment accounts and promises of large returns to convince women to send money.

At least 26 women in California, Idaho, Oregon and Washington allegedly lost about $1.3 million.

Love and Chan were arrested Aug. 24 in Boise, Idaho. Both face charges of conspiracy to commit wire fraud and wire fraud.

The FBI believes there may be additional victims.

Love and Chan are presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.
