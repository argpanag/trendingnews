Seahawks at Broncos Week 6 Inactives

TheTools · 16.10.2026 01:08 · source · permalink

A look at the inactive lists for the Seahawks’ Week 6 game at Denver.

DENVER—With no Seahawks players listed as questionable for Thursday's game against the Broncos, there wasn't a lot of mystery coming into the game when it comes to the inactive list.

Linebackers Drake Thomas and Chazz Surratt are both inactive after being ruled out on Friday, while wide receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba is active, as expected, despite being on the injury report with an ankle injury.

With Thomas out, Tyrice Knight is expected to start at linebacker along with Ernest Jones IV.

Both players the Seahawks elevated off the practice squad, linebacker Jimmy Ciarlo and receiver Irv Charles, are active and will both be making their Seahawks debuts.

For the Broncos, two starters in the secondary, cornerback Patrick Surtain II and safety Talanoa Hufanga are both inactive after being ruled out on Friday. Starting cornerback Riley Moss, who was listed as questionable, is active.
