McDonald's brings back popular discontinued item after nearly four years

TheTools · 22.10.2026 23:04 · source · permalink

The fast food giant confirmed to Yahoo Lifestyle that a nationwide rollout has commenced.

McDonald's has confirmed that Diet Coke is finally returning to nationwide menus after a nearly four-year hiatus.

The fast food giant caused an uproar among customers when it permanently removed the soft drink from restaurants in January 2023.

But a spokesperson for the Golden Arches told Yahoo Lifestyle that the cries for change have been loud over the past few years.

McDonald's is bringing back Diet Coke

"We've heard the calls for Diet Coke to return, and we're excited to confirm it's coming back to Macca's, giving customers even more choice when it comes to their favourite drinks," they said.

"Diet Coke is now rolling out to restaurants across Australia and will continue to land on menus over the coming months."

Aussies raged in 2023 when the drink was removed, as they were left with Coke Zero as their main non-sugar substitute.

The fast food giant permanently removed the soft drink from restaurants in January 2023, causing an uproar online from customers who were displeased.

What's the difference between Diet Coke and Coke Zero?

Both Diet Coke and Coke Zero are sugar-free and calorie-free, according to the Coca-Cola website.

But the real difference comes down to the taste.

Coke Zero Sugar "looks and tastes more like Coca‑Cola original", while Diet Coke has a "different blend of flavours which gives it a lighter taste", the company states.

Diet Coke was also the brand's first sugar-free cola that launched in 1983, but Coca‑Cola Zero launched in 2006 and eventually replaced Coca‑Cola Zero Sugar in 2016.
