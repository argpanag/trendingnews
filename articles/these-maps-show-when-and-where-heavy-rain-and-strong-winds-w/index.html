These maps show when and where heavy rain and strong winds will reach Massachusetts - The Boston Globe

TheTools · 24.09.2026 09:08 · source · permalink

A possible rare September nor’easter packing heavy rain and strong gusty winds is bound for our region, expected to likely arrive in Southern New England late Friday afternoon as an area of low pressure intensifies over the Atlantic, just offshore of Virginia, throughout Thursday.

Waves of heavy rain to torrential downpours, gusty onshore winds, coastal flooding and beach erosion are likely with this coastal storm, stretching from southern Maine, through Boston, to the Cape, and along southern Rhode Island starting around 5 p.m., Friday, ramping up Saturday, and lingering into at least Sunday.

Expansive high pressure to the north and northeast will hold this storm pretty much in place over the weekend, with the center of circulation likely held just south of Long Island. Eventually, the storm will drift over Southeastern Massachusetts and Rhode Island.

Some of the latest forecast storm impacts we could see include:

• Coast flooding, beach erosion — Coastal flooding with beach erosion from Boston south, along the shoreline, Cape Cod, the islands, to Rhode Island. Several high tide cycles will increase flooding. South-facing beaches could see moderate beach erosion as this storm sends strong waves crashing into the shoreline.

• Gusty onshore winds — Winds of up to 50-55 mph with scattered power outages expected along Southeastern Massachusetts, including Boston to the Cape and South Coast, and Rhode Island.

• Heavy rainfall — Rounds of heavy rain to flooding downpours, with breaks in between, but the more torrential rains could overwhelm drainage systems at times. About 4-6 inches of rain expected for Boston, Providence, and the southeast; 2-4 inches along I-95 to I-495.
