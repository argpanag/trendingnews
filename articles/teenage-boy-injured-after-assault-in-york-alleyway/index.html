Teenage boy injured after assault in York alleyway

TheTools · 03.09.2026 14:47 · source · permalink

A teenage boy has been left injured after being assaulted by another teenager in a York alleyway, police said.

Officers are appealing for information about the incident in Tang Hall at about 8.55pm on Tuesday (September 1).

North Yorkshire Police said a teenage boy assaulted another teenage boy in the alleyway between Constantine Avenue and Hewley Avenue “causing injuries”.

The force urged anyone with information about the assault to email lily.ashworth1@northyorkshire.police.uk or phone 101.

Information can be passed anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or online via its website.

Quote reference 12260171589 when providing information to police or to Crimestoppers.
