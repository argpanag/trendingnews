Young Lions squad announced for November fixtures - Bristol City FC

TheTools · 06.11.2026 14:54 · source · permalink

England Under-21s have named their squad for the international double-header against Portugal and France at Ashton Gate on Friday, November 13th (7.30pm kick-off) and Monday, November 16th (7.30pm kick-off). 

Young Lions head coach Lee Carsley has selected a 24-man squad for the games, which will be the group’s first preparation matches ahead of next summer’s UEFA U21 EURO Finals.

Tickets for the game are just £15 for adults and £7.50 for children. 

Bristol City Season Ticket holders and Members can benefit from a discounted price of £10 for adults and £5 for 65-plus and Under-19s.

Buying both games? Use code 20DOUBLEHEADER21 for 20% off!

Want to bring your school, club, friends or family along to support the Young Lions? We can help with group bookings of any size for both matches at Ashton Gate.

For group bookings of eight or more, email groups@bristol-sport.co.uk or call 0117 963 0611 to secure your seats.

Among Carsley’s squad, there are first Under-21 call-ups for Arsenal forward Max Dowman and Norwich City’s Jovon Makama, while Liverpool’s Trey Nyoni, Everton’s Harrison Armstrong and Divine Mukasa of West Ham are also named in the squad after joining the group from the Under-20s during the last international window.
