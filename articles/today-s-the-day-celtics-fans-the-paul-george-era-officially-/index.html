Today’s the day, Celtics fans — the Paul George era officially begins. Here’s a preview. - The Boston Globe

TheTools · 20.10.2026 19:03 · source · permalink

Today’s the day, Celtics fans. The Paul George era officially begins Tuesday afternoon when the Celtics take on the Pistons in Detroit.

Let us give thanks and rejoice.

Despite trading Jaylen Brown to the 76ers, the team’s expectations remain high. Joining George as veteran newcomers are center Mitchell Robinson and guard Mike Conley.

They open against a Pistons squad that won 60 games last season and entered the playoffs as the No. 1 seed in the East before losing to the Cavaliers in seven games in the conference semifinals.

Start time is 3 p.m., with the matchup serving as the beginning of an opening day tripleheader on NBC.
