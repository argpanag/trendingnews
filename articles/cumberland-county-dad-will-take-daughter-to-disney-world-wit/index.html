Cumberland County dad will take daughter to Disney World with $200,000 win

TheTools · 20.11.2026 11:04 · source · permalink

Zachary Williford of Hope Mills just finished working a double shift when he stopped by the grocery store to grab cat litter, added a $5 scratch-off, and won a $200,000 top prize.

“It’s a lot of money,” he said. “I still can’t believe it.”

Williford plans to use his winnings to take his daughter on a magical trip.

“I’ve been saving to take her to Disney World,” he said. “She loves everything Disney.”

Williford bought his lucky Wheel of Fortune ticket from the Food Lion on Legion Road in Fayetteville.

“I was just like, ‘Oh my goodness,’” he said recalling his winning moment.

Williford arrived at lottery headquarters Monday to claim his prize and, after required state and federal tax withholdings, took home $144,021.

The Wheel of Fortune game debuted in July with five top prizes of $200,000. Three $200,000 prizes remain to be claimed.
