Tadej Pogačar is still coming to the road worlds - Canadian Cycling Magazine

TheTools · 11.09.2026 10:27 · source · permalink

The injured world champion won’t defend his rainbow jersey, but he’ll be in Montréal to cheer on Slovenia and fiancée Urška Žigart

Tadej Pogačar won’t be racing at the world championships in Montreal, but the world champion is still coming to Canada.

If you’ve just come back from a trip to Pluto, the Slovenian superstar was forced to end his season after crashing at the Vuelta a España. It was a heartbreaking and fluke crash on a relatively easy day. His collarbone injury might have healed in time—well, probably not—but the C5 fracture was the big problem.

The 2026 worlds

There simply wasn’t enough time for him to safely ride or race again. That not only took him out of the Spanish Grand Tour–which he led–but ruled him out of his bid for a third straight world title. But as we’ve seen so many times in cycling, some crashes have been much, much worse. But good news, according to Slovenian national team coach Uroš Murn, Pogačar plans to make the trip to Montreal anyway, as reported by Wielerflits.

He’ll arrive during the week and stay through the end of the championships, this time swapping his Colnago for a spot on the sidelines.

Pogi the superfan and Slovenian staffer

There are a couple of very good reasons for the trip. Pogačar will be there to support his Slovenian teammates, including Primož Roglič and Jan Tratnik, and to cheer on his fiancée, pro cyclist Urška Žigart, who will race the women’s events. You may very well see one of the greatest of all cyclists in the feed zone on bottle duty.

Pogačar has already shown he’s pretty good at the supporter thing. Earlier this summer, he was spotted cheering Žigart on at the Tour de France Femmes, including on Mont Ventoux. Maybe he has a career as a soigneur once..if..he ever retires from cycling? Although, he probably won’t be in need of cashflow when he’s all wrapped up with the sport. Still, everyone needs a hobby.

You know who else will be at the road worlds? I mean, apart from the best cyclists in the world? Canadian Cycling Magazine. The whole crew will be there reporting, interviewing, and taking lots of photos and videos. The races run Sept. 20-27.
