TL;DW: Season 1 2027 Dev Update

TheTools · 17.11.2026 03:36 · source · permalink

Hey everyone, Pabro and Meddler here with a quick TL;DW of today’s Dev Update:

  • We’ll be doing a full reveal of New Dawn in January, in place of our Season Start moment.
  • We’re going to pair the season start cinematic with New Dawn’s launch later next year.
  • We’ll start playtesting shortly after the reveal to get your feedback. Sign up to learn more on the New Dawn website.
  • The gameplay changes for January are focused on both short term health for League as well as changes that can be carried over into New Dawn.
  • We’re bringing back Kleptomancy as a Keystone Rune, which has a new unique loot reward that gives a small discount to items in the store.
  • We’re adding a new Keystone, Titanborn, that grants ramping armor and magic resist while in champion combat, and unleashes a shockwave when at max stacks.
  • We’re overhauling the on-hit item system to give on-hit users more options to create a full build.
  • Terminus will become more clearly about piercing through foes, rather than granting armor and magic resist.
  • Wit’s End is being updated into a go-to move speed item against magical opponents.
  • Blade of the Ruined King’s percent health damage is being bugged with its slow removed.
  • Fatetaker is a new on-hit item that grants attack speed, armor, and has a three-hit passive. It also inherits Blade of the Ruined King’s slow without a cooldown.
  • We’re reworking Riftmaker’s stats and uniques.
  • There’s a new AP item called Lifebloom Crown that grants onmivamp and allows overhealing.
  • Gravescourge is a new Lethality item that summons Mistwraiths to chase down nearby enemies.
  • Faelights are being removed.
  • We’re trying something new next season called “Ranked Rush.”
  • In January everybody’s visual rank will reset to Iron IV, but MMR will be the same to ensure match quality.
  • Everyone will climb from Iron IV together with massive LP increases.
  • You can earn Timeless Tokens to unlock Timeless Victorious skins—which are variants of previous Victorious skins.
  • You can unlock Timeless Victorious skins immediately, no need to wait until the end of the year.
  • In January we’ll have an update for ARAM: Mayhem that’s about breaking more of League’s rules than normal.
  • Augments can become overloaded and do things like increase inventory size.
  • We’re also doing community voting to bring back old Mayhem Augments, with the first coming shortly after the update.
