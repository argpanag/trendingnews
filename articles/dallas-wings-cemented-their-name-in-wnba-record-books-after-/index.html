Dallas Wings cemented their name in WNBA record books after beating Seattle Storm

TheTools · 24.09.2026 09:16 · source · permalink

The Dallas Wings made some WNBA history after they secured a win over the Seattle Storm in their last regular season game of 2026.

The Wings have secured seventh place in the league with a few teams still having one game left to play.

They beat the Storm 103-91 on Wednesday, and in turn, have now recorded the largest increase in wins from one season to the next in the history of the WNBA.

Dallas Wings won 17 more games in 2026 compared to 2025

As shared by Alexa Philippou on X/Twitter: “The Wings recorded the largest increase in wins from one season to the next in WNBA history (+17).

“The previous record was +16, done three times, most recently by the 2022-23 New York Liberty.”

The Wings finished the 2025 season in 13th place with a record of 10-34.

Meanwhile, after seeing off the Seattle Storm this week, their regular 2026 campaign ended with them boasting a much improved record of 27-17.

They are now due to compete in the WNBA playoffs for the first time since 2023.

The Wings lost in the second round to the Las Vegas Aces on that occasion, and could potentially face the same team in the first round of this year’s competition depending on how results go on Thursday night.

The Aces are third in the table right now, but they could overtake the Golden States Valkyries if they win their game and the Valkyries lose.
