Fans erupt over NRL club's $9m move to sign Nathan Cleary and Mary Fowler

TheTools · 08.11.2026 07:01 · source · permalink

The audacious play would have signed both via a package deal.

NRL fans have been left stunned after it was revealed the Wests Tigers made an audacious $9 million play to try and lure both Nathan Cleary and Mary Fowler. Cleary officially became a free agent for 2028 on November 1, but the latest reports indicate he's set to re-sign with the Panthers.

As for Fowler, she plays her club football for Manchester City and the golden couple of Australian sport maintain their relationship via long distance. Cleary has reportedly rejected over $8 million to join the PNG Chiefs, and another NRL club is said to have made a bombshell move to try and secure his services.

The Daily Telegraph is reporting that the Wests Tigers made a 'secret' bid to try and lure both Cleary and Fowler in a package deal worth a whopping $9 million. The deal would have seen Cleary join the Tigers and Fowler play for the Central Coast Mariners in the A-League Women.

It would have been worth a combined $3 million per season across three years, with Cleary receiving $1.7m per season and Fowler $1.3m. The A-League has a marquee player allowance that would have made the audacious move possible.

The idea was reportedly thought up by Tigers chief executive Shaun Mielekamp and signed off by Holman Barnes boss Daniel Paton. The Holman Barnes Group owns both the Wests Tigers in the NRL and Central Coast Mariners in the A-League Women.

Mary Fowler plays her club football for Manchester City. (Photo by Lewis Storey/Getty Images)

NRL world reacts to Cleary and Fowler bombshell

On social media, fans have been expressing sceptism that Fowler would have swapped Manchester City - a football powerhouse in Europe - for the Central Coast Mariners. One person wrote: "From Manchester City to Central Coast??? Seriously."

Another commented: "She's 23 and coming into her prime, why would she leave playing against the best players in the world?" However plenty of others gave the Tigers credit for trying. "That's actually smart to try that. She earns more over in England though," another person wrote.

It's being reported that the Chiefs were offering $8.4 million over three years when you take tax breaks and third-party deals into consideration. However the champion halfback has reportedly told his management that he won't be leaving Penrith.
