Google Birthday 2026: Why September 27 Is Celebrated as Google’s Birthday When the Tech Giant Was Founded on September 4, 1998?

TheTools · 27.09.2026 07:34 · source · permalink

Google Birthday 2026: For billions of people, September 27 is simply known as Google’s birthday. But the date hides an unusual piece of the company’s history. As Google marks its 28th birthday in 2026 with a nostalgic Doodle, the technology giant is celebrating on a date that does not match its official incorporation day.

Google Inc. was incorporated on September 4, 1998, while the company has used September 27 as its birthday for more than two decades. Google itself has acknowledged that the exact date of its founding is not straightforward, but September 27 became the established celebration date after several changes in the company’s early years.

The latest Doodle once again brings Google’s early history into focus, giving users a look back at the company’s beginnings and raising the familiar question: Why does Google celebrate its birthday on September 27?

Google Birthday 2026: Google Celebrates 28th Birthday With Nostalgic Doodle

Google marked its 28th birthday on Sunday, September 27, with a special homepage Doodle that looks back at its early identity.

The celebration comes nearly three decades after Larry Page and Sergey Brin began developing the search engine while they were Stanford University students. Their project eventually became Google, which grew from a research project into one of the world’s largest technology companies.

Google has regularly used its birthday Doodle to revisit its past. The company’s 2025 birthday Doodle, for example, featured its first-ever logo as part of a look back at its early days.

The 2026 celebration continues that nostalgic approach.

Google Birthday 2026: When Was Google Actually Founded?

The simplest answer depends on what is meant by “founded.”

Google Inc. was legally incorporated on September 4, 1998. Google itself confirmed the date in a 2018 retrospective, while also noting that the company had celebrated its birthday on September 27 for more than a decade.

The story began several years earlier at Stanford University, where Page and Brin worked on a search technology that ranked web pages using links between them.

Their original project was called BackRub. They later changed the name to Google, a play on “googol”, the mathematical term for the number 1 followed by 100 zeros.

Google’s incorporation followed an important early investment. Sun Microsystems co-founder Andy Bechtolsheim reportedly provided Page and Brin with a $100,000 check in August 1998, helping the project move toward becoming a formal company.

Google Birthday 2026: Why Does Google Celebrate Its Birthday on September 27?

Google did not always celebrate its birthday on September 27.

The company used several different dates during its early years. Google celebrated its birthday on September 7 in some years, September 8 in another and September 26 in 2005. The company eventually settled on September 27 from 2006 onward.

The shift became closely associated with Google’s search-index milestones.

In September 2005, Google announced a major expansion of its search index during a period when the company and Yahoo were competing over the size of their searchable databases. Contemporary reports show that Google used its seventh-birthday celebration to highlight the huge growth of its index.

Google said at the time that its new index had grown to around 1,000 times the size of its original index. The announcement came amid a public dispute over which search engine had indexed more web content.

That milestone helped establish September 27 as Google’s recurring birthday date.

Google Birthday 2026: Google’s Birthday Date Was Once Inconsistent

The September 27 celebration may seem like a long-standing tradition, but Google’s birthday history was anything but consistent in the beginning.

The company’s first birthday Doodle appeared in 2002, and Google used September 27 that year. But the company moved the celebration to different September dates in subsequent years.

Google celebrated on September 8 in 2003 and September 7 in 2004 before using September 26 in 2005. From 2006, September 27 became the regular date.

Google later described its founding date as something of an open question, while confirming that September 4 was its incorporation date.

Google Birthday 2026: What Happened Before Google Became Google?

Page and Brin began working on their search project at Stanford in the 1990s.

The early search engine initially operated under the name BackRub. The project focused on analysing links between websites to determine the importance of individual pages.

The name Google eventually replaced BackRub as the project developed.

The Google.com domain was registered on September 15, 1997, nearly a year before Google Inc. was incorporated. That creates yet another important date in Google’s early timeline.

So, depending on which milestone is considered, Google’s history includes the beginning of the Stanford research project, the registration of Google.com, the company’s incorporation and its later September 27 birthday tradition.

Google Birthday 2026: Google’s Early Logo Returns to the Spotlight

The birthday celebrations also offer a glimpse into how different Google looked in its early years.

Google has repeatedly used older versions of its logo in birthday Doodles to highlight the company’s transformation from a small search project into a global technology business.

The first Google Doodle itself dates back to 1998, when Page and Brin placed an “out of office” message on the Google homepage while they attended the Burning Man festival. Google says the first Doodle appeared before the company was officially incorporated.

The tradition later developed into one of Google’s most recognisable ways of marking important events, anniversaries and cultural occasions.

Google Birthday 2026: September 27 is Now a Symbolic Google Birthday

There is no real contradiction between September 4 and September 27 once the dates are viewed as different milestones.

September 4, 1998 marks Google’s legal incorporation. September 27 represents the birthday the company chose to celebrate publicly after several changes during its early years.

Google has now used September 27 consistently for its birthday celebrations since 2006.

The date has therefore become less about the company’s legal paperwork and more about celebrating the evolution of its search engine and the wider technology ecosystem that grew around it.

From a Stanford research project and the early BackRub days to Search, Gmail, Maps, Android, YouTube and its growing artificial-intelligence products, Google’s September birthday has become a yearly reminder of how dramatically the company has changed since 1998.
