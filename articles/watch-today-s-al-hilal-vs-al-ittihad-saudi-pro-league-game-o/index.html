Al Hilal host Al Ittihad at Prince Faisal bin Fahd Stadium in Riyadh in what is the standout fixture of Matchday 8 in the Saudi Pro League. Hilal lead by one point, but Nassr and Al-Qadisiyah are only two points further back, and Neom SC sit three adrift in fifth.

This is the first meeting between the two clubs since their 1-1 draw at this same venue in February 2026, and both managers will know the result could shape the early narrative of the title race.
