Our top Celtic vs Celta Vigo betting tips at a glance

Celtic vs Celta Vigo match preview

Celtic needed that. After a disappointing run of form both domestically and in Europe, the Hoops demolished Heart of Midlothian 7-1 on Sunday.

Celtic were 5-1 up at half-time at Parkhead after blitzing their opponents in the opening 45 minutes. It was by far their most impressive display of the season to date.

Celtic were actually outshot 20-18 by Hearts, but the xG told its own story: 4.16 to Celtic compared to 1.2 for Hearts. Martin O’Neill’s men were worthy winners.

One swallow does not make a summer, however, and while Celtic are four points clear of Rangers in the Scottish Premiership, they have struggled in the Europa League.

Defeats by Ferencvaros (1-3) and Benfica (1-4) have left Celtic under pressure even after just two rounds of games in the league phase.

In truth, Celtic have been poor in Europe for a while now. They scraped into the knockout phase last term, only to be dumped out in the play-offs.

O’Neill’s team were then knocked out of the Champions League qualifiers by LASK, losing 5-4 on aggregate despite winning the first leg 3-0.

So while Celtic were much improved against Hearts, that does not necessarily mean they will be able to produce a similar sort of showing on Thursday night.

Team form

Celtic recently lost three games in a row (in all competitions), but they have now won two of their last three. Celta Vigo have been inconsistent of late, with their last six results reading: draw, loss, win, loss, loss, win.

Ongoing injuries

Cameron Carter-Vickers is missing for Celtic, although he is closing in on a return to full fitness. Yang Hyun-jun and Alistair Johnston will play no part against Celta Vigo, who are unable to call upon the services of Pablo Duran, Borja Iglesias, Carl Starfelt, Miguel Roman and Jones El-Abdellaoui.

Head-to-head record

Celtic have only played Celta Vigo twice before. The two teams drew 2-2 on aggregate in the third round of the UEFA Cup (the forerunner to the Europa League) in 2002/03, with Celtic advancing on away goals.

Team news and predicted line-ups

After wins at the weekend, Celtic and Celta Vigo are unlikely to make too many changes to their line-ups for Thursday’s encounter.

  • Celtic predicted XI: Sinisalo; Donovan, Murray, Scales, Tierney; McGregor, Baur; Hassan, Nygren, Osmand; Duran.
  • Celta Vigo predicted XI: Radu; Rodriguez, Lago, Caceres; Carreira, Burcio, Moriba, Alvarez; Driouech, Jutgla, Swedberg.

Celtic vs Celta Vigo match prediction

Online betting apps rate Celta Vigo as the favourites. Celtic have had a difficult time in Europe of late, so we like the away team’s chances: Celtic 0-1 Celta Vigo.

Celtic vs Celta Vigo betting tips

Celtic will do battle with Celta Vigo on matchday three of the Europa League.

Celtic struggling in Europe

Celtic Park has lost its fear factor in Europe. Kairat, Braga, Roma and Stuttgart all avoided defeat at Parkhead last season, while Ferencvaros won comfortably in Glasgow on matchday one in 2026/27.

On a good night, the atmosphere at Celtic Park is still one of the best in Europe, but the team has not been able to match that of late.

The 7-1 destruction of Hearts will certainly have lifted the mood in the camp, but that feelgood factor will not last long if Celtic start badly on Thursday.

Celta Vigo are an inconsistent team but they are eighth in La Liga and have more quality in their ranks than Celtic.

High goal count unlikely

Celtic turned on the style against Hearts, but they struggled going forward in their previous few games.

The Hoops scored either zero or one goal in consecutive matches against St Johnstone, Rangers, Ferencvaros and Rangers again, as well as in the 4-1 loss to Benfica last Thursday.

Martin O’Neill’s team will not find the Celta Vigo backline as accommodating as that of Hearts. 

There have been under 2.5 goals in seven of Celta’s 11 matches in La Liga and the Europa League in 2026/27, including five out of five away from home.

Who will find net?

Celta Vigo have spread the goals around so far this season.

They have not exactly been free-scoring, finding the back of the net 10 times in nine La Liga encounters. It is noteworthy, though, that those 10 goals have been scored by eight different players.

Meanwhile, their one strike in the Europa League came courtesy of Hugo Alvarez, who has not got on the scoresheet in La Liga.

Ferran Jutgla is one of two players to have scored more than once for Celta. Their starting striker, he is our pick to make the net ripple against Celtic.
