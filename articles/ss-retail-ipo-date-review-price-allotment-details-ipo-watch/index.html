About SS Retail

SS Retail, founded in June 2016, is a retail chain for mobile phones, accessories, and other electronic items. The firm sells its products under the brands ‘SS Mobile’, ‘Mobile Exchange Wala ’, and ‘The Mobile Space’ through both company-owned and franchise-operated store formats. Its product portfolio includes mobile phones, pre-owned smartphones, accessories, televisions, laptops and tablets, along with ancillary services such as mobile protection plans, EMI facilities, anti-theft software and mobile recharge services. 

As of 2026, SS Retail has expanded to 536 stores across Maharashtra, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Goa and Gujarat, spanning approximately 2,60,597 sq. ft. Moreover, as of March 2026, SS Retail was the largest mobile phone retail chain in West India and Maharashtra and the third largest in India among its competitors.
