ESPN College GameDay 2026 Location, TV Schedule and Week 6 Predictions

TheTools · 10.10.2026 13:24 · source · permalink

Last week, ESPN's College GameDay took a break from the SEC to focus on a marquee Big Ten matchup between the Ohio State Buckeyes and the Iowa Hawkeyes. This week, though, it'll be back to SEC action for a matchup that should have significant College Football Playoff (CFP) implications.

Sixth-ranked Alabama is set to host second-ranked Georgia in one of the biggest matchups of the college football season to date. College GameDay will be in Tuscaloosa during the day to help set the stage.

Here's what you need to know for the GameDay broadcast and the week's headline matchup.

College GameDay Week 6

Date: Saturday, October 10

Venue: Denny Chimes on the University of Alabama campus in Tuscaloosa

Time: 9 a.m. - 12 p.m. ET

Crew: Rece Davis, Kirk Herbstreit, Desmond Howard, Pat McAfee, and Nick Saban

TV: ESPN, ESPNU

Live Stream: Disney+ and the ESPN app

No. 2 Georgia at No. 6 Alabama

Date: Saturday, October 10

Time: 7:30 p.m. ET

TV and Live Stream: ABC and ESPN app

Line: Alabama -1.5

Over/Under: 51.5 Points

*Odds from FanDuel Sportsbook

Game Preview and Prediction

Saturday's matchup should be one of the top college games of the season and is arguably the most anticipated since Lane Kiffin and LSU visited Mississippi in Week 3. The Bulldogs and the Crimson Tide each carry a 5-0 record into the game, along with the look of legitimate title contenders.

Georgia's offense ranks first in the nation in scoring, with an average of 51.4 points. Led by quarterback Gunner Stockton, running back Nate Frazier, and a bevy of difference-makers, the Bulldogs have regularly raced up and down the field in the early season.

Defensively, Georgia is no slouch either. The Bulldogs have surrendered an average of only 13.4 points this season.

While the Bulldogs might be the most complete team in college football right now, Alabama is a dangerous and familiar foe. The Crimson Tide rank second in scoring (49.6 points per game) and have surrendered just 20.8 points per game. Kalen DeBoer's squad will also have a massive home-field advantage.

Of course, it's unclear what sort of environment Georgia and Alabama will see on Saturday, if they face off as scheduled. Adverse weather conditions resulting from Hurricane Isaias could impact the game, though both sides are preparing as normal.

"It's uncertainty. [It's] beyond my control," Georgia coach Kirby Smart said, per Jordan D. Hill of 247Sports. "We as coaches game plan for multiple situations—weather conditions—when they're there. We had that same prep last week with the threat of those things. Doesn't change a lot about practice."

Georgia won convincingly when these two teams last faced, claiming a 28-7 victory in last year's SEC Championship Game. However, the Crimson Tide have won both regular-season meetings since DeBoer took over in 2024.

The prediction here is that weather conditions do take some of the proverbial air out of the offenses. In a bit of a defensive struggle between the SEC powerhouses, a raucous crowd and a few untimely mistakes by Stockton hand Alabama a close victory.

Prediction: Alabama 25, Georgia 23
