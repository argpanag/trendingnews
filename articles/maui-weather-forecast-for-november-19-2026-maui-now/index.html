One high pressure center northeast of the main Hawaiian Islands will weaken today. A stronger high will build north of the state by Friday, briefly increasing trade wind speeds through Saturday. Two passing upper level disturbances will keep trade wind weather in the forecast through the end of this week, with showers primarily favoring windward and mountain areas. The subtropical ridge will settle in over the island chain by early next week with an extended drying trend and lighter trade flow in the forecast.

A mid level shortwave trough is in place across the islands this morning. Numerous showers are ongoing across the islands, with some decrease in coverage and intensity from last evening noted across across Kauai and Oahu. These showers are is expected to continue into tonight, particularly in windward and mountain areas. Coverage will be greatest early this morning and again tonight.
Today's mid level trough will slide east but another is expected to arrive for Friday and Saturday. This feature will keep the wet trades going into Saturday night. Meanwhile, a 1035 mb surface high pressure to the north of the islands will slide east. The pressure gradient will tighten and result in increased trade winds to breezy and locally strong levels.
The trough will give way to subtropical ridging building in beginning Sunday and persisting through at least midweek. Strong subsidence from ridging aloft will produce stable conditions, lowering the temperature inversion. Drier low level air will also be in place with dew points in the lower 60s. Therefore, drier trades will prevail Monday through Wednesday with limited shower activity. In addition, trades will weaken and winds may become light and variable as a col develops in the pressure pattern between a surface high to the northeast and one the northwest, with their axes aimed toward Hawaii. Between the two highs will be a surface low well to the north. Global guidance suggests a front from that system may move into the region around Thanksgiving and increase shower chances again. A period of northwest winds may follow thereafter.

Moderate to breezy trade winds will continue into Friday and gradually strengthen. VFR conditions will prevail with occasional MVFR ceilings and visibilities across mainly windward and mountain areas with passing showers.
AIRMET Sierra is in effect for mountain obscurations on all islands above 2000 feet. The MVFR conditions should improve throughout the day.
AIRMET Tango may be needed Thursday into Friday as strengthening trades bring winds of 25 knots or greater, increasing the potential for low level moderate turbulence over and immediately downwind of island mountains.

Trade winds will hover in the fresh to locally strong range through this afternoon. Strong high pressure passing north of the islands tonight will lead to more widespread strong trades. As a result, the Small Craft Advisory (SCA) remains in effect for the typically windy waters around Big Island and Maui County through 6 pm, then expands to cover most coastal zones (with the exception of the windward waters of Kauai, Maui County, and Big Island) tonight and Friday. Trades then look to decrease through the weekend and could become light by early next week as the subtropical ridge sinks south over the islands. By the middle of next week, winds could ramp up quickly with a front arriving from the northwest.
