15 Years at Home | Numbers, Records, and Key Juventus Players

Fifteen years, hundreds of matches, goals, victories and star players. Since its opening in September 2011, the Stadium has become Juventus’ home and one of the iconic venues in the club’s recent history. These fifteen years can also be told through the numbers.

389 MATCHES, 282 WINS

The Men’s First Team have played 389 official matches at the Stadium, recording 282 wins, 76 draws and just 31 defeats. That’s a win rate of over 72 per cent.

In these matches, Juventus have scored 778 goals, conceded 265 and kept a clean sheet on 195 occasions: practically one clean sheet every two matches.

Serie A naturally accounts for the largest share, with 287 matches, followed by the UEFA Champions League with 59, the Italian Cup with 35 and the UEFA Europa League with 8.

FROM THE FIRST GOAL TO THE 500TH

The first official goal was scored by Stephan Lichtsteiner against Parma on 11 September 2011. A few weeks later, on 2 October against Milan, the first brace at the Stadium was scored by Claudio Marchisio.

The first hat-trick came on 27 November 2013, when Arturo Vidal scored three times against Copenhagen in the Champions League.

Juventus’s 500th goal at home, however, came on 7 August 2020: it was scored by Cristiano Ronaldo against Lyon.

100, 200… 282

Victories have also left their mark on the Stadium’s history. The 100th victory came on 20 August 2016 against Fiorentina, whilst the 200th victory dates back to 9 March 2021, in the European match against Porto.

Fifteen years after its opening, the total has risen to 282 victories.

Among the records, a run of 17 consecutive home victories stands out, achieved between 19 November 2016 and 23 April 2017. Even longer is the unbeaten run: 57 matches without defeat between 12 September 2015 and 27 September 2017.

SEVEN GOALS IN A SINGLE MATCH

Juventus have twice managed to win by scoring seven goals at home: 7–0 against Parma on 9 November 2014 and 7–0 against Sassuolo on 4 February 2018.

The match with the highest total number of goals, however, is much more recent: the 4–4 draw against Borussia Dortmund on 16 September 2025, with eight goals in 90 minutes.

Another statistic highlights the Bianconeri’s attacking consistency: between 5 May 2018 and 8 March 2020, Juventus scored in 34 consecutive home matches.

INDIVIDUAL RECORDS

Fifteen years at the Stadium also means there are many key players who have left their mark on Juventus’ home ground. Leonardo Bonucci is the player with the most appearances, 225, and also tops the list for individual wins with 172 victories. The top goalscorer is Paulo Dybala, with 68 goals, ahead of Cristiano Ronaldo (55), Gonzalo Higuain and Dusan Vlahovic (34) and Arturo Vidal (30), whilst Juan Cuadrado holds the record for assists, with 32.

As for the Women's First Team, Cristiana Girelli is both the player with the most appearances at the Allianz Stadium – 14 – and the top scorer, with 9 goals. Behind her in terms of appearances are Barbara Bonansea (13), Pauline Peyraud-Magnin and Cecilia Salvai (11), and Arianna Caruso and Lisa Boattin (10).

THE OPPONENTS

Milan are the team Juventus have faced most often at the Stadium, with 20 competitive matches.

However, it is Udinese who top two other rankings: against the Friuli-based side, Juventus have recorded 15 wins and scored 38 goals – more than against any other opponent at home.

THE HOME OF JUVENTUS WOMEN

Over time, the Stadium has also become the setting for some of the most important days and evenings in the history of Juventus Women.

It all began on 24 March 2019, with the Serie A match between Juventus and Fiorentina: the Bianconere’s first match played at the Allianz Stadium and a day destined to go down in the history of Italian women’s football and the Club.

Since then, Juventus have played 14 matches at the Allianz Stadium, winning six, drawing three and losing five. But above all, they have brought some of the greatest European clashes in their history to its pitch.

Lyon, Chelsea, Wolfsburg, Arsenal, Servette, Zurich, Benfica and Manchester United are just some of the teams they have faced in Turin in the UEFA Women’s Champions League.

Among the evenings to remember is certainly that of 23 March 2022, when Juventus beat Lyon in the first leg of the Champions League quarter-finals.

FIFTEEN YEARS, A HOME

Different numbers, records, matches and key players, united by the same venue. From Lichtsteiner’s first goal to the great European nights, from long winning streaks to the Women's First Team's matches.

It has been 15 years at the Stadium – the Allianz Stadium since 2017: fifteen years in which what was a new home in September 2011 has become, match after match, simply, home.
