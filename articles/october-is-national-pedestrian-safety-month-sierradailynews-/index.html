October is National Pedestrian Safety Month - SierraDailyNews.com

TheTools · 16.10.2026 14:58 · source · permalink

October is National Pedestrian Safety Month and the Susanville Police Department would like to remind drivers to take extra precautions to help protect people walking. On average, 19 pedestrians are killed in traffic crashes every day in the United States, according to the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration. In 2024, an estimated 7,080 people walking were struck and killed by vehicles. Of those
people, 1,090 were killed in California alone.
The Susanville Police Department offers these driving tips to keep everyone safe:
• Follow the speed limit, especially in areas with heavy foot traffic, around schools and in neighborhoods where children are present.
• Slow down at intersections. Be prepared to stop for people crossing or walking at marked and unmarked crosswalks.
• Use extra caution when driving at night or when driving conditions are less than optimal such as rain or fog.
• Avoid distractions such as using a mobile device, deep conversations with passengers or eating while driving. Keep your focus on the road.
• Drive sober.
Funding for this program is provided by a grant from the California Office of
Traffic Safety, through the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration.
