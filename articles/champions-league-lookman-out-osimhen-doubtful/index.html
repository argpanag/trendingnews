Champions League: Lookman out, Osimhen doubtful

TheTools · 24.11.2026 08:48 · source · permalink

Brief

On the eve of Matchday 5, the Atlético Madrid forward will miss the game against Viking FK. The Galatasaray striker is not certain to play against Aston Villa.

Ademola Lookman is reportedly ruled out of Atlético Madrid's Champions League match against Viking FK, scheduled for Wednesday 25 November 2026. The Nigerian forward would not be the only absentee: Marc Pubill and Romero are also unavailable through injury.

For this Matchday 5, played on 24 and 25 November 2026, Victor Osimhen remains doubtful for Galatasaray's home game against Aston Villa at Rams Park. The striker underwent knee surgery after being injured in Galatasaray's 2-0 win over Stuttgart. He returned to individual training on Thursday 19 November, but was not available on Saturday 21 November for his side's win over Samsunspor. His return to the squad against Aston Villa is therefore not guaranteed.

Among the other Nigerians, Zaidu Sanusi is expected to feature on Tuesday 24 November with FC Porto, who travel to Feyenoord. On Wednesday 25 November, Mubarak Suleiman and Emmanuel Ayaosi are expected in Slavia Prague's starting XI against Villarreal at the Fortuna Arena.

On Saturday 21 November, Moses Usor provided an assist in LASK's 2-1 win over Hartberg.
