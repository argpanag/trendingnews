Kaley Cuoco Flaunts Her Baby Bump at BAFTA TV Tea Party with Fiancé Tom Pelphrey

TheTools · 13.09.2026 23:57 · source · permalink

Kaley Cuoco and Tom Pelphrey are stepping out for a pre-2026 Emmy Awards event!

The 40-year-old pregnant actress showed off her growing baby bump as she hit the red carpet alongside her 44-year-old fiancé at the 2026 BAFTA TV Tea Party at The Maybourne Beverly Hills on Sunday (September 13) in Beverly Hills, Calif.

Earlier this summer, Kaley and Tom shared the exciting news that they were expecting their second child together.

The engaged couple is also parents to three-year-old daughter, Matilda, and they’re bringing another little girl into the world soon.

The BAFTA TV Tea Party is an invite-only social event hosted by BAFTA (British Academy of Film and Television Arts) and typically sees several of that year’s Emmy nominees in attendance for a gathering before the big show.

If you missed it, Tom is nominated at this year’s Emmys for Outstanding Supporting Actor in a Drama Series for his work in the HBO show, Task.

This marks Tom‘s sixth Emmy nomination. He was last nominated for a Primetime Emmy in 2022 for Outstanding Guest Actor in a Drama Series for his work on Ozark, and was previously nominated four years in a row, from 2005 to 2008, for Outstanding Younger Actor in a Drama Series for Guiding Light at the Daytime Emmys. He won the award in 2006 and 2008!
