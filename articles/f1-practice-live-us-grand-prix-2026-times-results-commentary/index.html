  1. Report: Leclerc fastest from Antonelli in US GP practicepublished at 23:15 BST 23 October
    23:15 BST 23 October

    Andrew Benson
    BBC F1 correspondent in Austin

    Ferrari's Charles Leclerc led Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli in Friday practice at the United States Grand Prix.

    Leclerc, who topped both sessions, was 0.042 seconds ahead of the Italian, who has a mathematical chance of tying up his first world title this weekend.

    Red Bull's Max Verstappen was third fastest, 0.189secs back, ahead of Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton, although the seven-time champion was 0.615secs off the pace.

    Mercedes driver George Russell was fifth ahead of Racing Bulls' Liam Lawson.

    McLaren's Lando Norris, whose team have introduced the first of three consecutive planned upgrades this weekend, was seventh fastest, although the world champion did not set a time on his qualifying simulation run on soft tyres after making a mistake at Turn 16.

    McLaren's Oscar Piastri was eighth quickest, ahead of Red Bull's Isack Hadjar and Haas driver Oliver Bearman.

    Antonelli will win the championship in Austin only if he wins the race and Russell finishes below eighth and Hamilton below fourth.

  11. 'Outstanding' Camara joins Haas for 2027published at 22:45 BST 23 October
    22:45 BST 23 October

    Esteban Ocon is leaving Haas at the end of the season, so who is replacing the Frenchman at the American-based team?

    Enter Brazilian Rafael Camara, who will be making his Formula 1 debut with Haas next year.

    The 21-year-old is leading the Formula 2 championship by seven points and beat the likes of reigning F2 champion Leonardo Fornaroli and Haas reserve drivers Jack Doohan and Ryo Hirakawa to the seat.

    Haas team principal Ayao Komatsu said Camara, who is a member of the Ferrari driver academy, had been the quickest driver at the tests in Portugal this summer.

    "In Portimao, he was just simply outstanding. We really wanted to go for someone who had that natural speed," Komatsu said.
