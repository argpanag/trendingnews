The death has occurred of

John Joe passed away peacefully, after a short illness bravely borne, surrounded by his loving family in the excellent and compassionate care of the wonderful staff at Midland Regional Hospital Tullamore.

 

John Joe is predeceased by his parents, William and Annie and his brothers, Billy (Mayo) and Dick (Geashill).

 

John Joe will be deeply regretted and sadly missed by his family, Enda, Eda, Shirley and Johnny, their mother, Trish Buggy, his son-in-law, Sean McRedmond, Johnny’s partner, Valerie, his adored grandchildren, Kaylee, Ella May, Ben, Sophie and Theo, his great granddaughter, Zara, sisters, Frances, Margaret and Ann, brothers-in-law, sisters-in-law, nieces, nephews, extended family, neighbours and friends.

 

Grant John Joe Eternal Rest and Peace

 

Funeral Arrangements:

 

Saturday, the 7th day of November – John Joe will repose at his residence, Curragh Hill, Geashill (R35 AD83) from 4 pm until 7 pm.

 

Monday, the 9th day of November – John Joe will make his final journey from his home to Raheen Graveyard (R35 K096), where he will be laid to rest at 11.30 am

 

House strictly private at all other times, please. The family kindly ask that their privacy be respected as they deal with this time of great sadness and loss. They wish to express their heartfelt gratitude to everyone for their kindness, understanding and support during this difficult time.

 

Family flowers only please, donations if desired, to Offaly Hospice.
