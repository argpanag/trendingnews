TheTools · 18.10.2026 05:37 · source · permalink

Storm Team4 has the weather forecast for D.C., Maryland and Northern Virginia.

4 things to know about the weather:

  1. Rain and clouds pushing SE through the morning
  2. Very windy & much cooler today
  3. Frosty Sunday morning
  4. Watching a potential Nor'easter that could impact us next week

Cooler, blustery conditions today after the rain. Showers could stick until 8 a.m. to noon, but temperatures Saturday will be in the mid-50s, marking about a 25° temperature drop.

After the rain and clouds move out, sunshine takes over for the weekend. Expect excellent visibility and very dry air.

Temperatures drop into the 30s Saturday and Sunday nights. Some of the colder suburbs could flirt with the mid-30s.

Temperatures recover slowly Monday and Tuesday with plenty of sunshine while dry weather continues.
