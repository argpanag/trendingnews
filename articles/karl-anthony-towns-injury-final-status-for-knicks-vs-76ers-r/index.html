Karl-Anthony Towns (Wrist) is available for the Knicks against 76ers.

Karl-Anthony Towns is in to help the New York Knicks' NBA Cup opener.

Ahead of their Friday showdown against the Philadelphia 76ers, Towns (wrist) has been made available to play by the defending champions.

Karl-Anthony Towns was initially listed as questionable to play for the New York Knicks against the Philadelphia 76ers due to a left wrist sprain.

The star big man sustained the injury during their last Wednesday win in Chicago against the Bulls, where Towns had to enter the locker room.

Despite the injury, Towns helped the Knicks' 133-127 comeback victory, dropping 19 points, 13 rebounds, and five assists.

In the end, despite his availability this Friday, Knicks coach Mike Brown might subject Towns to a minutes restriction.

Towns is averaging 21.8 points, 13.8 rebounds, 3.2 assists, and 0.6 blocks in 31.0 minutes per game this season so far with the defending champs.

He and the Knicks will face a shorthanded 76ers team that will be without Jaylen Brown.
