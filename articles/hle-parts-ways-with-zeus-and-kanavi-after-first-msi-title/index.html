HLE Parts Ways With Zeus and Kanavi After First MSI Title

TheTools · 19.11.2026 08:07 · source · permalink

Hanwha Life Esports announced Wednesday that its contracts with top laner Choi "Zeus" Woo-je and jungler Seo "Kanavi" Jin-hyeok have ended, after the two helped the club win its first Mid-Season Invitational and finish as LCK runner-up.

(Photo by Chung Sung-Jun/Getty Images)

Hanwha Life Esports announced Wednesday that its contracts with top laner Choi "Zeus" Woo-je and jungler Seo "Kanavi" Jin-hyeok have ended, after the two helped the club win its first Mid-Season Invitational and finish as LCK runner-up.

Inven Global reported that the club posted the Zeus announcement on its official social channels and, later the same day, a separate farewell to Kanavi, thanking him for captaining the team this season. The moves come during LCK free agency, after Gen.G parted ways with Kiin and Canyon and Nongshim extended Kingen, Calix and coach bonO.

Official Farewells on Wednesday

Zeus joined HLE ahead of the 2025 season after leaving T1. Inven reported earlier this month that he turned down a renewal offer before Worlds. Kanavi signed a one-year deal in November 2025 for the 2026 season after his LPL career, Etoday reported. Neither player has publicly said where he will play next, and T1 has not confirmed a move. Sheep Esports reported that Zeus agreed Wednesday to a three-year return to T1.
