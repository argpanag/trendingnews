Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpoge claims Claude helped prove the Riemann Hypothesis

TheTools · 20.11.2026 16:07 · source · permalink

Levent Alpoge credits Claude with key ideas in a Lean-only argument that he says also covers the generalized hypothesis.

By Ryan Merket · Published Nov 20th, 2026, 10:07am CT

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpoge says he and Claude have found a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, a claim that Yifan Zhang (@yifanzhang_) amplified on November 20th, 2026 with the words "JUST IN: Riemann Hypothesis has been solved." The linked four-page note, titled "Remark," makes a narrower statement than the post: "I believe I have, working with Claude, discovered a proof". No independent confirmation had been reported by the time same-day coverage appeared, and same-day reporting described the archives and final theorem as unchecked.…
