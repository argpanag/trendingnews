Passenger falls onto tracks at Kerinchi LRT station

TheTools · 27.11.2026 20:41 · source · permalink
    bookmark

PETALING JAYA: A passenger is believed to have fainted before falling onto the tracks at Kerinchi LRT Station, resulting in minor delays on the Kelana Jaya Line on Friday (Nov 27).

Rapid KL said the incident occurred at about 4.40pm, prompting the activation of the Platform Intrusion Emergency Stop (PIES) system and a temporary suspension of power supply to the tracks as a safety precaution.

The passenger was assisted by station personnel out of the track area and taken to the emergency room for initial treatment. No other passengers were reported to have been injured.

“Emergency brakes were activated as trains approached the station to allow safety measures to be carried out while the rescue operation was underway,” the statement said.

Normal operations resumed on the Kelana Jaya Line shortly after the rescue operation was completed.

Rapid KL advised passengers who feel unwell or require assistance at stations or on platforms to seek help from auxiliary police officers or station personnel.

Passengers are also reminded to remain behind the yellow line while waiting for trains as a safety precaution.
