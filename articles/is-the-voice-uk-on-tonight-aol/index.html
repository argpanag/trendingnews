Is The Voice UK on tonight? - AOL

TheTools · 03.10.2026 20:46 · source · permalink

The Voice UK was pulled from the schedule last weekend due to programming changes. However, enthusiasts of the ITV singing contest will be thrilled to learn that Sir Tom Jones, Kelly Rowland, will.i.am, Danny Jones and Tom Fletcher will return to screens tonight (Saturday, October 3).The programme typically broadcasts somewhat earlier in the evening, but audiences will need to wait until 8.50pm to view the latest instalment of blind auditions as the coaches continue selecting vocalists for their respective teams.

The synopsis for this weekend's episode states: "Emma Willis hosts the spinning-chair singing contest, with coaches Tom Jones, Will.i.am, Kelly Rowland, Tom Fletcher and Danny Jones assessing more acts. With only eight places available on each team, the mentors must choose carefully as they search for the UK's most exciting singing talent, with the Rewind button giving them the chance to bring back an artist they regret letting go".

The Voice UK made its comeback in August for series 14, with Destiny's Child singer Kelly Rowland replacing pop star LeAnn Rimes as a panel coach.

At the time, former X Factor judge Kelly said "can't wait to hear all the new talent and to go to battle for my team. Let's go!" and she's proved popular with viewers since her first appearance.

The current series introduces a significant innovation designed to assist coaches who perhaps react too slowly when pressing the button during blind auditions. The Rewind feature will allow each coach one opportunity to reconsider a performer who departed the stage without securing a chair turn.

The coach may subsequently activate the Rewind function and welcome the contestant back to their squad. Confirmation has emerged that host Emma Willis will not be returning for the fifteenth series next year, with Stacey Solomon and Alesha Dixon assuming presenting responsibilities.

Additionally, Sir Tom, Tom and Danny will not be returning as coaches, with rapper Aitch and Girls Aloud legend Cheryl joining the panel alongside will.i.am and Kelly.

The choice to exclude Sir Tom has sparked criticism from audiences, with numerous supporters demanding that ITV reinstates the Welsh vocalist.

A petition entitled Reinstate Sir Tom Jones on The Voice UK has garnered over 69,000 signatures from disappointed supporters campaigning for his comeback.

The Voice UK continues Saturday, October 3 on ITV1 and ITVX at 8.50pm
