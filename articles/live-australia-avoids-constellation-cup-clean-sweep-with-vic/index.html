Thanks for tuning in to ABC Sport's coverage of the Constellation Cup. Congratulations once again to the Silver Ferns on their 3-1 series win. We'll add some more detail to our match report above and also have some more analysis coming on the state of the Diamonds in due course.

Nice to see the Aussies get a victory on home soil in front of a sold-out John Cain Arena after such a tough series.

A lot of reflection still needs to be done on their selection and tactics as they build to the 2027 World Cup. But for now, let's relish the positive. A great team performance, a wonderful debut series by Jessie Grenvold and a full 60 minute performance from Georgie Horjus inside the circle.

Silver Ferns looked tired

B

By Brittany Carter

By Brittany Carter

Despite winning the overall series, the New Zealand head coach was a bit disappointed by her team's performance.

"We looked a bit weary, we looked a bit tired, I could feel it slipping away from us and we did try to hold on there," Noeline Taurua told Fox Netball.

"It's a bummer that we've lost this game, but the fire is still burning."

The team will now head home and play against the New Zealand men's team in a couple of weeks to blood some new players, which they didn't get a lot of opportunity to do in this series.

Grace Nweke is in tears after being named as the inaugural recipient of the Plummer-Aitken Medal. Happy tears we hope!

Marinkovich's success rate

B

By Brittany Carter

By Brittany Carter

After losing this Constellation Cup with one match still left to play, the Diamonds head coach now has a 50 per cent success rate in the series since she took over in 2020. Here's what her winning rate looks like overall. The heat increasing on her position is mostly recent results, seeing the Australian side miss the Commonwealth Games final for the first time in history and now losing these past three matches to the Ferns.

To be fair, Marinkovich is missing some key players in this Constellation Cup. Jo Weston opted out of the series, wanting to give her body a rest. Sunday Aryang is still in the rehab process trying to return to the court after rupturing her ACL in the final match of the last Con Cup this time last year. Jamie-Lee Price has been unavailable for most of this series with a calf injury. Kate Moloney has also had multiple finger injuries that have limited her time on court. Lastly, Matilda Garrett has been ruled out with concuission.

Gordon responds to boos

B

By Brittany Carter

By Brittany Carter

In the fourth quarter of Sunday's game, Silver Ferns midcourter Maddy Gordon was awarded possession in a contest and the 11,000 strong crowd in Sydney didn't like the call. A chorus of boos rang around the stadium and Gordon responded by mimicking a chattering mouth with her hand. A cheeky gesture that was caught on broadcast.

"I don't know what came over me," Gordon laughed.

"Oh my goodness, I honestly didn't think at the time anyone would see it, so this is quite hilarious.

"I was just a bit over the crowd booing blatant contact calls so that was just my reaction, a bit of an eye roll and a bit of a, you know.

"I back it. You know I'm all for loud and passionate crowds, love them, but just the booing yeah, got me. In that moment I just had enough … it was funny that the camera was right on me."

We'll keep an eye on Maddy and see if there is any more crowd interaction in game four. Read more on the story from New Zealand Radio Sport's Bridget Tunnicliffe in the article below.

By Brittany Carter

By Brittany Carter

Amy Parmenter also told the ABC that the Diamonds had bigger priorities this series than winning the trophy. The group believes they're in a good position after trialling different combinations and playing styles for next year's World Cup.

New Zealand could do something they haven’t done in 16 years.
