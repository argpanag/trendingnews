Celebrity Traitors fans can't believe Michael Sheen's A-list dating history - his new relationship is even more surprising

TheTools · 16.10.2026 20:46 · source · permalink

If you thought his career was star-studded, wait till you hear about his exes...

From Hollywood film sets to the Celebrity Traitors castle, Michael Sheen’s career has taken him to some unexpected places. Nothing, though, seems to be quite as surprising as his very star-studded dating history...

The Welsh actor is currently busy hunting down some Traitors in the Scottish Highlands, appearing in the second season of the hit BBC show Celebrity Traitors. But while his game-play is certainly garnering attention, so is another part of his life: his ex girlfriends.

In a TikTok featuring photographs of some of Michael's very famous ex girlfriends, viewers were quick to rush to the comments to share their thoughts.

'Michael Sheen has an insane dating history,' wrote one, adding, 'It's that Welsh charm, you know.' Another added, 'Michael Sheen isn't the kind of celeb that I've ever wondered about in regards to his love life, but that was very interesting.'

From Mean Girls to SNL comedians, here's a deep dive into Michael Sheen's love life...

Who has Michael Sheen dated?

1995-2003: Kate Beckinsale

In 1995, Michael struck up a relationship with London-born Kate Beckinsale after both starring in a stage production of The Seagull. In 1999, they welcomed their daughter, Lily Mo Sheen, who has followed in her parents’ footsteps and pursued her own acting career.  

In 2002, Kate and Michael once again worked together on Len Wiseman's film Underworld, which saw them star as a vampire and a werewolf. However, things took a turn in their relationship the following year. After relocating from London to Los Angeles Michael and Kate split in 2003. Kate later struck up a relationship with Underworld director Len.

After struggling to land jobs in America, Michael moved back to the UK. He previously told Annie Mac, ‘Going through that experience of a relationship breaking down and having a young child, and then because of my daughter and her mum living in another country, having to make a life in a new place whilst going through that whole experience which is difficult enough. It really made me question certain things about myself.’

However, the former couple now share a close bond. Speaking to E! Magazine back in 2016, Kate shared, ‘It’s so normal for us. We split up ages ago. We have not been together for far longer than we were ever together. But I really love him and like him, and we make each other roar with laughter.’

2004: Anastasia Griffith

In 2004, Michael struck up a relationship with Anastasia Griffith, who he met while filming the British TV series Dirty Filthy Love. Their fling was short lived, though, with the pair splitting after a few months. According to sources, Michael was focusing on co-parenting with Kate, and wasn't looking for any serious relationship.

2011-2013: Rachel McAdams

In 2011, Michael began dating Rachel McAdams, who is best known for playing Regina George in Mean Girls and Allie Hamilton in The Notebook. The pair met while filming Woody Allen's Midnight In Paris, although it was said romance didn't blossom until after filming had wrapped. Opening up that year about Rachel, the Welsh actor gushed, ‘She's talented, intelligent, beautiful. She’s warm and friendly. She’s very supportive, and very kind, very funny. She makes me laugh a lot.’ 

The pair went on to split in 2013, with sources saying the pair had 'different visions for the future.'

2013-2014: Carrie Keagan

Months after splitting from Rachel, Sheen was rumored to be dating Morning Buzz host Carrie Keagan, after the pair attended the Masters of Sex premiere together. The couple kept any potential romance under wraps - and it seems they had split by February 2014, when Michael debuted a new relationship...

2014-2017: Sarah Silverman

In 2014, Michael was introduced to comedian Sarah Silverman through friends. The pair began dating, and in an unusual twist, ended up both landing roles on the TV show Masters of Sex. Sarah later revealed how, although she was top of the producers' wish list to play the character of Helen, they almost didn't cast her when they realized that she was linked to the Welsh actor.

'We met at a completely different context, at a show I did,' Sarah shared, adding, 'They had always had me in mind for Helen, and then we started dating they went "Oh no!" So I guess they went to Michael and said, "We've always wanted to offer this [role] to Sarah Silverman, but now she's your girlfriend. Is that okay, comfortable for you?" Obviously he was like, "Yeah!"

The pair were together for three years before calling it quits in December 2017, with Sarah revealing distance was partly to blame. 'He moved back to the UK and his life is really there,' she explained, 'and my life is really not there. But I love him to pieces.'

Michael later touched on his breakup when sitting down to speak with the Daily Telegraph, revealing that political shifts in the world also led to their split. He shared, 'After the Brexit vote, and the election where Trump became president, we both felt in different ways we wanted to get more involved. That led to her doing her show I Love You, America, and it led to me wanting to address the issues that I thought led some people to vote the way they did about Brexit, in the area I come from and others like it.

'We both had very similar drives, and yet to act on those drives pulled us in different directions - because she is American and I’m Welsh. I felt a responsibility to do something, but it did mean coming back here [to the UK], which was difficult for us because we were very important to each other. But we both acknowledge that each of us had to do that we needed to do.’

2019-present: Anna Lundberg

Back in 2019, Michael confirmed his relationship with Anna Lundberg after attending the New York screening of Good Omens. The Swedish-born actress is best known for having appeared in stage productions such as Othello and Spring Awakening, as well as having starred on Celebrity Gogglebox with Michael.

At the time, the couple's 25-year age gap raised eyebrows (While Michael is 57, Anna is 32.) It was something Michael addressed while appearing on BBC's The Assembly, when he was asked, ‘How does it feel to be dating someone who is only five years older than your daughter [Lily Mo]?.

Opening up about his relationship with Anna, Michael replied, ‘Because of the age difference, I think both of us were quite surprised when we got together. I don’t think either of us were looking for that. It’s not like I’ve dated lots of people who are much younger than me, but you meet who you meet [...] We were both aware it would be difficult and challenging. Ultimately, we felt it was worth it because of how we felt about each other.’ 

In the years since, the couple - who are not married, as far as is publicly known - have welcomed two children together: Lyra, six, and Mabli, four. Michael previously shared, 'I’m aware that I’m a much older father. It does worry me, it makes me concerned. It makes me sad thinking about the time I won’t have with them. But ultimately, I think if you find someone who brings you happiness and you make them happy, you’ve got to go for that.'
