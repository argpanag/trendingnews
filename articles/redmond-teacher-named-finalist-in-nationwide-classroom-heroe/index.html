Redmond teacher named finalist in nationwide classroom heroes contest - Redmond Spokesman

TheTools · 07.10.2026 22:40 · source · permalink

Published 3:40 pm Wednesday, October 7, 2026

By Ian Haupt

A Ridgeview High School teacher was selected as one of 10 finalists for a national acknowledgement award.

English teacher Amanda “Mandy” Felton has the chance to win $10,000 for herself and $5,000 for Ridgeview. She could also claim a year’s supply of Kleenex.

Sponsored by Kleenex, the 2026 Heroes of the Classroom Contest recognizes K-12 teachers who have a lasting impact on their students, school and community.

“Mandy truly represents what it means to be an educator,” nominator Jill McNulty said in the submission. “She pushes students to think and grow for themselves, all while creating an environment that feels safe and comfortable.”

As one of Ridgeview’s senior class language arts teachers, Felton works with students during a pivotal moment in their lives, guiding first-generation college applicants and creating a classroom safe enough for students to share their stories and build confidence.

Felton runs the school’s Key Club branch, volunteers in the community, and opened her home to a student who needed it. She also fills her classroom walls with student photos and graduation announcements. Key Club is an international service organization.

The Heroes of the Classroom Contest features more than $50,000 in total prizes dedicated to supporting both winning teachers and their schools.

One grand prize winner, determined by the public vote, will receive a check for $10,000, while their school receives a $5,000 donation and a year’s supply of Kleenex Brand tissues.

Nine finalists will each receive two $1,000 Costco gift cards, one for themselves and another for their school. Additionally, the nominators of the grand and first prize winners will receive a $1,000 gift card to the big-box store.

Public voting opens Oct. 8 and closes Oct. 22.
