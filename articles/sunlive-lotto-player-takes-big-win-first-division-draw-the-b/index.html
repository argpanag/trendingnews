SunLive - Lotto player takes big win First Division draw - The Bay's News First

TheTools · 02.09.2026 23:50 · source · permalink

Lady luck continues to smile on Bay of Plenty, with more big Lotto wins for the region.

A Rotorua punter was one of three nationwide to win $333,333 each with Lotto First Division last night.

The winning ticket was sold via MyLotto.

The other tickets were sold in Wellington and Te Awamutu.

It comes after a winning Strike Four ticket worth $333,333 was sold at Tauranga City Lotto for last Saturday’s draw.

Powerball was not won last night and will be $28 million on Saturday, while Strike Four has jackpotted to $400,000.

Meanwhile, two Tauranga players have each won $14,093 with Lotto Second Division.

Twenty-three players won nationwide – including four who struck Powerball Second Division, taking their total winnings to $19,630.
