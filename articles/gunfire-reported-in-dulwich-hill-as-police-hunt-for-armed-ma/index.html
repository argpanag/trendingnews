Police have locked down part of Dulwich Hill in Sydney’s inner west after reports a man armed with a shotgun is in the area.

Heavily armed tactical police, along with a police helicopter and general duties officers, rushed to the area near Williams Parade and Constitution Road about 10.30am on Friday, after gunshots were reported to emergency services.

Nearby businesses and cafés have been put into lockdown, along with Dulwich Hill Public School, as officers hunt the man who is allegedly armed with a sawn-off shotgun.

Witnesses on the ground told Nine News there had been an altercation between the man and another group of people, which ended in a car being set alight by an apartment complex.

A large exclusion area has been established, as police search for the gunman. Locals have been urged to remain inside, while those travelling through Dulwich Hill have been redirected from the area.

No injuries have been reported, nor have any arrests been made. Light rail services between Dulwich Hill and Lilyfield have been stopped amid the ongoing operation.
