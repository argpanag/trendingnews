“The period drama of the year”: Oliver Twist is Sarah Lancashire’s “outstanding” series to watch this week

TheTools · 11.11.2026 12:20 · source · permalink

Sarah’s performance as the sharp-tongued Mrs. Corney is not to be missed.

There’s nothing quite like stumbling upon an outstanding drama that’s hiding in the archives and the latest to catch our attention is Oliver Twist. The period drama, which first aired on the BBC almost two decades ago and was described by fans as the period drama of the year, is a Charles Dickens adaptation with a difference. And with Sarah Lancashire playing the sharp-tongued Mrs. Corney, it really is up there with the best period dramas on TV.

The series is a modern retelling of the classic story about an orphan - Oliver Twist, if you haven't already guessed - navigating the criminal underworld of Victorian London. It’s a dark, gritty and perfectly-cast tale featuring standout performances from the likes of Sarah, Timothy Spall (he’s superb as Fagin), Sophie Okonedo, Tom Hardy and, of course, William Miller, who plays the titular young boy.

With the series now available to watch on the BBC, here’s everything you need to know about Oliver Twist before you do, including the full plot, cast and how to tune in.

What is Oliver Twist about?

The five-part adaptation of Charles Dickens' 1838 novel tells the story of the titular young orphan boy whose life starts inauspiciously in the fictional town of Mudfog. Oliver is the son of an unmarried mother, who dies shortly after his birth - and he's not exactly left in an enviable position.

Unfortunately for Oliver, his caretakers are the managers of the Parish, Mr. Bumble (Gregor Fisher) and Mrs. Corney (Sarah Lancashire). Populated with other orphans and unwanted children, the Parish’s residents are forced to work long hours and Oliver is given all but pitiful rations of gruel to survive on.

After a moment many will remember when Oliver dares to ask for more - "Please, sir, I want some more" is the famous line - he's cast out and runs away to London. There, he meets young pickpocket The Artful Dodger (Adam Arnold) and is subsequently drawn into the dark and murky underworld of Fagin (Timothy Spall) and his gang.

What to read next

And so, begins a series of thrilling adventures involving Nancy (Sophie Okonedo), her brutal lover Sikes (Tom Hardy), and the mysterious Mr Monks (Julian Rhind-Tutt). And while Oliver finds himself perilously close to death, there’s hope in the form of Mr Brownlow (Edward Fox) a generous benefactor with links to Oliver's past who takes him under his wing.

Sarah Phelps, who wrote the adaptation described it as “dark and edgy, dangerous and urgent, tragic and pretty frightening in places but there is comedy in it too.” She added in a BBC interview: “This is a fresh take on a much loved and familiar favourite but it remains true to the spirit of the book.”

Who’s in the cast of Oliver Twist?

The main cast of Oliver Twist includes:

  • William Miller (Above Suspicion) as Oliver Twist
  • Timothy Spall (Death Valley) as Fagin
  • Edward Fox (The Day of the Jackal) as Mr Brownlow
  • Adam Arnold (The Bill) as The Artful Dodger
  • Julian Rhind-Tutt (Green Wing) as Monks
  • Tom Hardy (MobLand) as Bill Sikes
  • Sophie Okonedo (Hotel Rwanda) as Nancy
  • Morven Christie (The Bay) as Rose Maylie
  • Sarah Lancashire (Last Tango in Halifax) as Mrs Corney
  • Nicola Walker (Annika) as Sally
  • Anna Massey (Frenzy) as Mrs Bedwin
  • Gregor Fisher (Only Child) as Mr Bumble
  • Rob Brydon (The Trip) as Mr Fang
  • John Sessions (Spitting Image) as Mr Sowerberry
  • Michelle Gomez (The Flight Attendant) as Mrs Sowerberry
  • Adam Gillen (Prisoners’ Wives) as Noah Claypole

Is Oliver Twist any good?

Yes – it’s excellent, in fact. For starters, the cast is exceptional, with Sarah Lancashire, Tom Hardy and William Miller among the standout performances. It’s a far darker version of events than many will have seen before - Tom Hardy is particularly fearsome! - but it makes for a genuinely compelling, brilliantly atmospheric watch.

How can I watch Oliver Twist?

You can watch Oliver Twist now on BBC iPlayer.

What other Sarah Lancashire dramas can I watch now?

If you’re a fan of detective dramas, we’d recommend Rose and Maloney. The series stars Sarah and Phil Davis as the titular Rose Linden and Marion Maloney, who work for the fictional Criminal Justice Review Agency, and Sarah’s performance as a hard-drinking, rule-breaking investigator is not to be missed. You can watch Rose and Maloney on Prime Video. For one of Sarah’s most adored dramas, we’d suggest Last Tango in Halifax. The series tells the story of two 70-somethings, Alan (Derek Jacobi) and Celia (Anne Reid), who reunite in later life and the repercussions for their respective families, including Alan’s daughter Gillian (Nicola Walker) and Celia’s daughter Caroline (Sarah). Last Tango in Halifax is available to watch on BBC iPlayer.
