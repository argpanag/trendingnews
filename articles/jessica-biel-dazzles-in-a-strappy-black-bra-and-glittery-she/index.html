Jessica Biel pulled a fashion magic trick on the red carpet on October 1. While promoting her new action film Matchbox: The Movie in Mexico City, the Candy star turned heads in a dramatic black-and-white ensemble that was anything but neutral.

Serving up a little bit of sparkle, Biel arrived in an enchanting, floor-grazing cape dripping in glitter. The sheer cape also featured an underlayer of see-through fabric in midnight blue. She layered the coverup over a strappy black bra and a high-waisted pencil skirt with a knee-high slit. Biel completed the outfit with black pointed-toe pumps with a satin finish.

Jessica Biel attends the "Matchbox: The Movie" premiere in Mexico City on October 1, 2026.
Credit: Getty Images

In photos posted to her Instagram account on October 3, the actress shared a close-up of her accessories, including a pair of diamond earrings and multiple pavé diamond rings. For her glam, Biel styled her golden-brown hair in tousled waves and opted for a bright pink lip and smoky eyeliner.

While promoting her new film on September 30, the Candy star also turned heads in a reverse black-and-white ensemble featuring a long-sleeved satin blouse in white and a black high-waisted skirt. In place of heels, she donned a pair of black knee-high boots with a crocodile print finish.

Jessica Biel attends a photo call for "Matchbox: The Movie" in Mexico City on September 30, 2026.
Credit: Getty Images

Shortly before the movie premiere, Biel also rocked an all-black ensemble consisting of a sleeveless, mock-neck halter top and a fitted leather skirt that draped all the way down to her calves. Keeping her accessories simple, she opted for the same diamond rings and black satin pumps she would pair with her cape a few hours later.

Jessica Biel shares photos from a trip to Mexico City to promote her film "Matchbox: The Movie" on Instagram on October 3, 2026.
Credit: Jessica Biel/Instagram

In her Instagram photo dump, Biel offered another glimpse at her wardrobe during her trip to Mexico City. In addition to her three red carpet-worthy looks, she also wore an all-denim ensemble while posing for photos in a parking garage alongside her co-stars. The full look included a baggy pair of jeans, a dark-wash denim jacket, and a light blue button-up shirt worn open below the bust to bare her midriff, breaking up the head-to-toe denim.
