More Singaporeans choose one-day funerals to spare loved ones the burden of lengthy wakes | The Independent Singapore

TheTools · 27.11.2026 19:30 · source · permalink

The image depicts a traditional funeral wake in Singapore. It is AI-generated for illustrative purposes and does not depict any actual individuals or funeral.

SINGAPORE: Grieving the loss of a loved one is undoubtedly one of the most heartbreaking experiences that a person has to go through in life. With this, more and more families nowadays are opting to have a one-day funeral to send off their deceased in a simple yet very solemn manner.

This has been on the list of choices because of a variety of reasons, including financial considerations and respect for the deceased’s wishes not to trouble their family members, given that long periods of keeping a coffin after death not only put pressure on the family’s mental and physical health but also require several days off work and are really a big toll on finances. 

With this one-day funeral, the entire process from collecting the body to cremation takes no more than 24 hours as they now follow simplified procedures. Due to the tight schedule, family members need to arrive at the crematorium during a break, and a farewell ceremony of no longer than 20 minutes will be held in a ceremony hall before the cremation of the body. 

A funeral parlour owner shared that they have handled more than 300 of these cases every year since 2023, Shin Min Daily News reported.

“We handled 399 cases in 2023, 419 cases in 2024, 385 cases last year, and 361 cases as of October this year,” the owner remarked. 

Another owner admitted that the deceased’s family say that each case has a different reason, and that the reasons are not always the same. 

Indeed, the preference for one-day funerals highlights how families are seeking a more practical and less troublesome way to say a solemn goodbye to their departed loved ones in this challenging economy. It is understood that these shortened arrangements may not suit every family, but it is important to remember that every family is different, and one’s choice should not be seen as better than the other.
