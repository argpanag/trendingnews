    22:43 GMT 31 October

    FT: Aston Villa 1-1 Fulham

    Aston Villa

    Aston Villa head coach Unai Emery speaking to BBC MOTD: "Disappointing result, but fair. The first half, we tried to dominate the match, to break their high build-up with the press. We did it more times than less. Sometimes, they could break our press and they created one or two chances but saved Zion Suzuki. We were creating chances, we were creating goals. We had a penalty shot. We scored a goal. In the first half, we more or less were [there]. Tough match, but we were more or less feeling comfortable. We wanted to carry on the second half in the same way, but we couldn't. I don't know why, but we couldn't. They were demanding it more, they were pushing more. They were creating chances more. We were defending. They scored the goal. They threw enough at it. It changed a bit. We started the game pushing, not being organised like we were but pushing. We created two or three chances to score the goal. I think the result is a fair result. Disappointing for us. Not enough, but we keep going forward."

    On Suzuki's mistake for the goal: "Yes, but it's part of football.

    "Keeping going forward. Keep improving. Today was about a very good test, but we need to continue working and improving things. Work to do. Try to adapt our players to get better, get confidence. Plus, we are improving our results. In our last five matches in the Premier League, three won, two draws. I think it is good, but our poor start is giving us a lack of points in the table. Today improving. A draw is a disappointing result, but a fair result but we must keep going. Next challenge is Tuesday against Barcelona. Next challenge is against Manchester United, so it's not time to stop. It's not time to be focussing on what was before. Our focus is the next match, next days. Each competition is giving us a lot of moments tot y to build the team. We are building and we are working to improve."

    On next two matches: "Yes, difficult. Every match."

    On going to Barcelona on his birthday: "We are looking forward to it."

  3. 'My players showed ambition and character'published at 22:40 GMT 31 October
    22:40 GMT 31 October

    FT: Aston Villa 1-1 Fulham

    Fulham

    Fulham manager Alvaro Arbeloa speaking to BBC Match of the Day: "Yes, of course [being pleased with his team's performance] especially in the second half. We were more aggressive, we controlled the tempo of the game, we had so many chances to score some goals, but in the end we couldn't take the chances.

    "I am happy because we knew before the match how difficult it would be to win here. Manchester City lost here one week ago and they are a Champions League team with a fantastic coach, so, for me I'm really proud of my players because they showed ambition and character, and we played our way, which is the most important thing."

    On Aston Villa goalkeeper Zion Suzuki: "He is a fantastic keeper, and I think he did really well tonight. He was unlucky in our goal but he saved two or three goals for Villa before."

    On being defensively solid: "I think we are deciding when to press better when we want to be aggressive, we are controlling the transition, and we are much better organised when we have the ball. It's important for us because we are a team that want to play in the opposite half with the ball creating chances, but you know in the Premier League how quick the other attacking players are and you need to control the transitions, second balls. I think we are growing, and improving as a team every week and this is the way."

    On Fulham's three game unbeaten run: "For me, what's important is how we played against a Champions League team in their stadium. My players are believing in the way that we play, and they are fighting, they are improving, they are working. I think we are starting a long journey I hope, I think we will improve, we can do better and for that we will work everyday."

    On saying thank you to supporters: "For me it's really important because we are here for them. This club belongs to them, they are so important, they are travelling so we can feel their support behind us. Today was a good night for everyone because we did well, and I hope they are proud of the team."

Player of the match

  • Manchester City (active)
  • Brighton & Hove Albion

After the opportunity to rate players has closed, the score displayed represents the average from all the submissions by BBC Sport users.

Brighton pick up first away league victory over Man City to go third

Brighton gained quick revenge after being knocked out of the Carabao Cup in midweek by stunning Manchester City and condemning Enzo Maresca's side to back-to-back Premier League defeats.

Fabian Hurzeler's men had twice taken the lead at Etihad Stadium on Wednesday but were edged out on penalties. On Saturday, they held on to a highly impressive victory to move just two points behind City in third.

Brighton scored the winning goal just 11 minutes in courtesy of a major helping hand from right-back Matheus Nunes, whose miscued clearance fell nicely for Yasin Ayari to convert from close range.

City should have levelled 10 minutes later when Iliman Ndiaye rose to meet Elliot Anderson's cross but planted his header the wrong side of the post, before the Senegal international stung Bart Verbruggen's palms at the near post.

Ndiaye was proving to be City's brightest spark as they looked for a way back into the game in the second half and the ex-Everton man ran clear and thumped a strike which was brilliantly tipped onto the crossbar by Verbruggen.

The Brighton goalkeeper was proving to be a difficult prospect to find a way past and he made another superb save to deny Nunes, while a weak header from Erling Haaland was more comfortable to deal with.

City's star striker Haaland was being starved of any real service and his side could well have fallen further behind at the other end – Maxim de Cuyper blazing over from 10 yards out and then forcing Donnarumma into a sharp save with a header.

But those missed opportunities from Brighton did not matter as they collected all three points to continue their exciting start to the season.

Testing times for Maresca

Enzo Maresca - post-match interview - Manchester City vs Brighton

Manager Maresca made a flawless start by winning his first seven league games in charge of City but has now hit his first sticky patch, seeing his side defeated at Aston Villa last Saturday and now beaten at home by Brighton.

Another individual error was their undoing, this time Nunes being the guilty party, failing to get enough purchase on De Cuyper's cross towards the far post and presenting Ayari with an opportunity that he could not miss.

The home side had opportunities in the final third too but lacked their usual clinical finishing in front of goal, failing to find the net for the first time in 25 Premier League matches despite having 21 shots at goal.

City needed penalties to edge past Brighton during the week but they extended their poor recent form against the Seagulls, now winless against the opposition in their past five league games.

Star man Haaland had little to feed off, while Maresca needs to address his formula in midfield – the three-man deployment of Elliot Anderson, Enzo Fernandez and Ayyoub Bouaddi were often disjointed and lacking in cohesion.

Well, it IS Halloween after all...

Brilliant Brighton march on

Fabian Hurzeler - post-match interview - Manchester City vs Brighton

Brighton had never won at City in 16 previous league visits but they ended that run through an early goal and stout defending.

Ayari made amends for his penalty miss in the shootout by converting at the same end for his third league goal of an eye-catching campaign.

Hurzeler's side have been the most entertaining Premier League side in terms of goals scored this season and their 24 strikes is unmatched by any other top-flight team.

They are a very well-drilled side, with relentless pressing and the reward for their supporters to make the second long trip up from the south coast in the space of three days was a thoroughly deserved three points.
