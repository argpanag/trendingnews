Salt Lake City Fire pays tribute to 27 year-old firefighter killed in BASE jumping incident - Daily Dispatch

TheTools · 15.10.2026 14:15 · source · permalink

The Salt Lake City Fire Department paid tribute to the 27-year-old who was killed in a Cache County BASE jumping incident in September.

Gabe Reilly, a Salt Lake City firefighter of four years, died while BASE jumping with a group of people near Leetham’s Hollow in Millville Canyon.

Investigators described Reilly as an experienced BASE jumper but said “something went wrong,” causing his wingsuit to not lift after jumping from a 700-foot cliff.

“He lived every single day to the fullest. He studied and trained hard with his crew, took friends on new adventures, and chased the mountains and open sky,” the fire department said in a post made to social media.

“He had no time for negativity, and you knew it the moment he walked into the station with his bright smile and contagious energy.”

KUTV CBS 2 Salt Lake City
