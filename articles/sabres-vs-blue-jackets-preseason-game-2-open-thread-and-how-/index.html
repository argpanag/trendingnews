Sabres vs Blue Jackets: Preseason Game 2 | Open Thread and How To Watch

TheTools · 22.09.2026 20:00 · source · permalink

The Buffalo Sabres will play the Columbus Blue Jackets tonight after a majority-Amerks side lost 4-1 last night to the Pittsburgh Penguins at the PPG Paints Arena. Expect to see a lineup that is more of what we will expect to start the season in October.

Buffalo will play four preseason games in six days, and with players only allowed to play in two games per the new collective bargaining agreement, there will be lots of rotation and not many opportunities for players to make an impression on fans and the coaching staff.

How to Watch

Puck Drop: 7:00 pm

Streaming: Sabres.com

Radio: WGR 550

Tonight’s roster

Forwards: Zach Benson, Sam Carrick, Josh Doan, Konsta Helenius, Peyton Krebs, Jiri Kulich, Beck Malenstyn, Ryan McLeod, Josh Norris, Noah Ostlund, Jack Quinn, Tage Thompson

Defensemen: Louis Crevier, Rasmus Dahlin, Owen Power, Mattias Samuelsson, Conor Timmins, Olen Zellweger

Goaltenders: Colten Ellis, Ukko-Pekka Luukkonen
