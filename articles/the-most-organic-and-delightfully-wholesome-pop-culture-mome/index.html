The most organic and delightfully wholesome pop culture moments of September belong to Céline Dion

TheTools · 25.09.2026 20:01 · source · permalink

Share this post

It’s not yet the end of the month, but the most organic and delightfully wholesome pop culture moments belong to one person: Céline Dion. Her hotel departures and returns have become twice daily events unto themselves, on top of the concerts she’s performing during this residency. The fans gather reliably on show days and she reliably greets them – with a great outfit and an even greater heart, taking the time to appreciate them, to acknowledge their patience, their devotion, and also entertaining them… while entertaining and stressing the f-ck out of her security.

By every measure her first week of concerts was a smash hit but the only measure is her own. She is having a great time, she is connecting to her music on another emotional level, she is letting the crowd see her anxiousness, her relief, her triumph, and she is proud of her own resilience. You can see it every time she hits a note or even when she doesn’t quite hold the note – because all that matters is that she’s at her best right now, in this time, in this body, with this voice.

I know this because she included it in the recap video that was posted yesterday, celebrating the first week. The part during “All By Myself” where she goes for the top, it’s imperfect before the big finish but we get to where we need to go. That’s what Céline is teaching herself every day.

And also, Rihanna makes an appearance. She’s been to two of Céline’s shows so far, receiving all the joy and gratitude that Céline was sending out.
