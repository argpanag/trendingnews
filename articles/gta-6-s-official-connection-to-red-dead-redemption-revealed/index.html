GTA 6’s Official Connection to Red Dead Redemption Revealed

TheTools · 20.11.2026 16:10 · source · permalink

Rockstar-verse confirmed

Grand Theft Auto 6 may have just come out, but players have already discovered many of Leonida's mostbizarre secrets, including the return of a certain legendary cryptid. One of these Easter eggs contains a big reveal: Red Dead Redemption and Grand Theft Auto are set in the same universe.

A book found in Brian’s Marina in Key Lento contains multiple references to Red Dead Redemption and its sequel. As GameRoll reported on X, the novel describes the demise of Flaco Hernandez, whom Arthur Morgan cut down in Red Dead Redemption 2’s “The Noblest of Men, and a Woman” mission. The story is told from the perspective of a new character, Rust McCreary, who comes across Hernandez’s corpse at Cairn Lake, where Arthur bested him in a duel. The book also references Red Dead Redemption 2 locations like New Austin, Armadillo, and Blackwater. The book isn’t the only reference to Rockstar’s western. You can spot license plates from New Austin, Red Dead’s fictionalized version of Texas. Jason can even rock a cowboy hat that looks exactly like Arthur's.

This isn’t the first time that Rockstar's franchises have crossed over. Franklin's house in Grand Theft Auto 5 contains a Red Dead book written by J. Marston. It’s hard to imagine John having time to document his exploits, but his son, Jack, may have provided an in-universe account of what went down in the first Red Dead game.

Are there other references to previous Rockstar games in GTA 6?

The following contains major story spoilers for GTA 6.

Beyond the ties to Red Dead, GTA 6 predictably includes many references to past Grand Theft Auto games. Jimmy De Santa, the son of GTA 5 protagonist Michael, makes a minor appearance. He's part of a mission where the player chooses whether to let him into a club. In Chapter 5, near the end of the game, Lucia and Jason begin working with GTA 4 protagonist Niko Bellic, who has returned to a life of crime.

This isn't the first time GTA games have crossed over. GTA 5 infamously included a scene where Trevor killed Johnny Klebitz, the protagonist of GTA 4: The Lost and the Damned. At least Niko seems to fare much better.
