Gabriel Martinelli's best Arsenal moments

TheTools · 03.09.2026 20:46 · source · permalink

Gabriel Martinelli has left Arsenal after seven sparkling years in north London.

From last-minute goals to stunning strikes in Spain, Gabi enjoyed big goals in big moments, leaving with plenty of memories and as a Premier League champion.

To celebrate his success, we've taken a look back at 11 key moments from our former number 11.

STARTING WITH A BANG

An 18-year-old Gabriel Martinelli made his first start against Nottingham Forest back in September 2019, and it was a night to savour for the Brazilian.

Donning the number 35, Gabi headed in his first goal for the club before doubling his personal tally late on in a 5-0 rout of the then-Championship side.

SOLO AT STAMFORD BRIDGE

Gabi had been making a splash in his first season in N5, netting in a 3-1 comeback at West Ham United as well as a double in the dramatic 5-5 draw against Liverpool at Anfield.

But one of his best goals came against Chelsea at Stamford Bridge in January 2020. Down to 10 men and trailing 1-0, Martinelli latched onto Shkodran Mustafi's headed clearance before running the length of the pitch to slot home the equaliser in front of the travelling supporters.

VOLLEY SINKS THE TOON

After picking up a knee injury in the summer of 2020, Gabi returned to full action during the 2021/22 campaign, where he made a big impact on Mikel Arteta's youthful side.

One of his best moments came with just his second touch against Newcastle United, volleying into the top corner to seal a 2-0 victory at a rocking Emirates Stadium.

LIFT OFF V LIVERPOOL

Gabi had a habit of netting against Liverpool, scoring against the Reds six times in our colours, and this was one of his best. 

Just 41 seconds into our home clash, Martinelli latched onto a fine through ball from Martin Odegaard to slam home and give us a 1-0 lead. We'd go on to win 3-2 in a thriller in N5. Gabi also netted at Anfield in the same season in a 2-2 draw.

SHINING IN SEVILLE

The Brazilian's first Champions League match ended in a roaring success as he found the back of the net in a 2-1 victory over Sevilla.

Gabi capped off a stunning counter-attack in Spain, using his pace to burst clear of the Sevilla backline and round the goalkeeper to make it 1-0 to the Gunners.

BANISHING THE CITY CURSE

We'd yet to beat Pep Guardiola's Manchester City heading into our clash with the defending champions in N5 in September 2023. 

With the game locked at 0-0 heading into the final 10 minutes, Martinelli's first-time finish deflected home to give us a huge three points against City on another memorable afternoon in north London.

BIG GAME PLAYER

Gabi had been developing a reputation for big goals in big moments when we entertained league leaders Liverpool in February 2024, needing a win to narrow the gap at the top.

Having opened the scoring, courtesy of Bukayo Saka, and being pegged back by an own goal, Martinelli put his mark on the game, pouncing on a mix-up between Alisson and Virgil van Dijk to slide home and restore our lead. We'd go on to win the match 3-1.

WRAPPING IT UP IN MADRID

Having won the first leg 3-0, our Champions League quarter-final second leg against Real Madrid required us to hold our nerve to secure safe passage into the semi-finals. With the game at 1-1, we were looking to finish with a flourish and maintain our perfect record at the Santiago Bernabeu.

Up stepped Martinelli, who picked up Mikel Merino's pass and burst towards the Madrid goal before slotting past Thibaut Courtois and clinching a famous win in the Spanish capital.

SUPER SUB

Martinelli put on a show against La Liga sides during his time in N5. With goals against Sevilla and Real Madrid already to his name, Gabi came off the bench to do some serious damage against Athletic Club in September 2025.

Within a minute of being introduced, Martinelli used his devastating pace to race clear and slot under Unai Simón to open the scoring for Bilbao. Gabi then weaved his way into the box to set up Leandro Trossard to wrap up a 2-0 victory at San Mames. 

The Brazilian would go on to become the first player in our history to net in five straight matches in the Champions League, including another famous goal in our 3-1 win over Bayern Munich

A PRICELESS POINT

An early pivotal clash in the Premier League title race came in September 2025 when Manchester City came to the capital. Erling Haaland's early strike put City ahead, and it remained that way as we ticked into stoppage time.

City looked to have claimed a huge victory, but we would deny them with an individual piece of Brazilian brilliance.

Eberechi Eze's inch-perfect ball over the top found Martinelli, who was able to control the ball and lob Gianluigi Donnarumma to give a 1-1 draw, which would go a long way to sealing our first Premier League title in 22 years.

FIRST CAREER HAT-TRICK

Gabi scored a host of goals in Arsenal red, but he had to wait until January 2026 for his first hat-trick for the Gunners in a 4-1 success at Portsmouth in the FA Cup third round. On a rain-soaked afternoon on the south coast, Gabi headed home twice and tapped in a third to take home the match ball. 

Gabi's 62nd and final goal came in a 4-0 win over Wigan in the FA Cup in February 2026. It means that Martinelli holds a remarkable record of never ending on the losing side whenever he scored for us.
