Decisive contributions: Salah shines in Trabzonspor's win over CSKA Sofia | Goal.com

TheTools · 26.11.2026 23:41 · source · permalink

Turkish side Trabzonspor came away with a valuable 2-1 win over their Bulgarian hosts CSKA Sofia on Thursday evening in the fourth round of the Conference League.

CSKA Sofia went ahead in the 33rd minute when Trabzonspor's Sidney Lopes Cabral turned the ball into his own net. The Turkish side trailed at half-time.

Trabzonspor refused to give up. In the 69th minute, their Egyptian star Mohamed Salah finished off a precise pass from Batista Mendy to draw them level.

They kept pushing and snatched a late winner through Noah Saviolo, who converted a cross from Mohamed Salah.

The win lifts Trabzonspor to nine points and eighth place. CSKA Sofia remain stuck on a single point.

Elsewhere in the competition, English side Brighton thrashed their Romanian visitors Universitatea Craiova 4-0.

Ibrahim Osman scored twice, with Maxim De Cuyper and Charalampos Kostoulas also on target.

Universitatea Craiova's Vladimir Screciu saw red in the 39th minute.

Brighton climb to 10 points and second place, two behind leaders Monaco, who have a perfect record.

Universitatea Craiova stay on seven points in 15th.

Italian side Atalanta, meanwhile, came from behind to beat their Bosnian hosts Borac Banja Luka 3-2.

The Italians trailed 2-0 in the first half but refused to give up.

It stayed that way until the 86th minute, when Nikola Krstović pulled one back.

Four minutes later, Sead Kolašinac equalised. Then, four minutes into stoppage time, Davide Zappacosta struck the winner for the Italian side.

That last-gasp win takes Atalanta to seven points. Borac Banja Luka stay on three.
