Armenian FM, UNHCR discuss long-term integration of Karabakh refugees

TheTools · 26.09.2026 19:39 · source · permalink

1 minute read

Armenia’s Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Trade Ararat Mirzoyan met with United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees Barham Salih in New York.

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Trade said they discussed cooperation on issues falling within the mandate of the High Commissioner for Refugees.

They also discussed “programs aimed at the long-term integration of refugees displaced from Karabakh,” the ministry said.

Views were exchanged on regional developments.
