Nintendo Direct September 2026 Live Report — Nintendo Set to Reveal Upcoming Games for Switch 2

TheTools · 09.09.2026 12:14 · source · permalink

Jumping for joy.

Hot on the heels of yesterday’s The Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary Direct, we have a brand new Nintendo Direct — and IGN will be reporting all the news live right here.

Nintendo Direct 9.9.2026 kicks off today, September 9, at 7am PT / 10am ET / 3pm UK time, and will last roughly 45 minutes. Nintendo has said it will focus on Switch 2 games launching this winter. After the Direct is complete, Nintendo Treehouse: Live | September 2026 will reveal gameplay of some games in the Direct itself in a livestream lasting roughly 110 minutes.

So, what can we expect to see at the Nintendo Direct? The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake got its time to shine in yesterday’s Zelda anniversary Direct, so I suspect Nintendo will focus on other games here. Nintendo Switch Sports Resort, due out October 22, seems like a safe bet, as does Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave, which hits Switch 2 next week. I also expect to see more of FromSoftware’s Switch 2 exclusive, The Duskbloods, perhaps with a release date.

The 100 Best Nintendo Games of All Time

Is it finally time for Nintendo to pull back the curtain on the next 3D Mario game? With Ocarina of Time remake the big Christmas 2026 focus and planet of other announced games coming down the line, perhaps we’ll have to wait until early next year for the reveal everyone’s waiting for.

Earlier this week, a notable leaker claimed Nintendo was developing an all-new Super Smash Bros., a new Nintendogs game, and Mario Kart DLC with Star Fox. Those games are unconfirmed for now, but worth keeping in mind as the Direct nears.

It’s worth remembering the games that showed up in September 2025’s Nintendo Direct: both Super Mario Galaxy games on Switch 2, a release date for The Super Mario Galaxy Movie, a release date for Metroid Prime 4: Beyond, the reveal of Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave, a physical remake of the Virtual Boy, the reveal of Pokémon Pokopia, new Donkey Kong Bananza DLC, and much more.

So, strap yourselves in, Nintendo fans — our fingers are crossed for this Direct. Be sure to check back closer to kick-off time for live updates, reaction, and of course everything announced.
