Chivu: Thuram's availability uncertain due to fatigue, Lautaro to play if he is not fit

TheTools · 25.11.2026 12:54 · source · permalink

autty

Published 2026-11-25 20:54:40

According to FCinter1908, Internazionale, coached by Cristian Chivu, will face Stuttgart. The Nerazzurri coach still has doubts about the starting lineup, including who will lead the attack.

Cristian Chivu said: "The mood ahead of the Stuttgart match is a bit concerning because of the injury news, and Thuram is not in top condition. Yesterday, after the warm-up, he left the main group before the rondo to work in the gym, and only returned to the pitch later for tactical work with the team."

"It was just a precaution. He is available to play," Chivu said simply, without fully dispelling the doubts. The pitch at Atalanta was slippery, and that match was very demanding for the French forward.

In any case, Thuram felt fatigued at the time. Barring any surprises, though, he will still partner Pio Esposito in attack. Otherwise, Inter would have to turn to Lautaro, whose physical condition is still not fully recovered and whose energy reserves are limited.
