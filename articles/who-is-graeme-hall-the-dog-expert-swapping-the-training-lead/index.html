Who is Graeme Hall?

Graeme Hall, known as "The Dogfather," is one of Britain's best-known canine experts and is the presenter of Channel 5's hit series "Dogs Behaving (Very) Badly."

Born in Selby, North Yorkshire, Hall took an unusual route to dog training, only getting his first dog around the age of 40.

After studying Spanish at the University of Hull, he spent 21 years at Weetabix, eventually becoming an operations manager.

Following redundancy in 2007, a chance encounter at a dog training class led him to switch careers, establishing his business, The Dogfather, in 2008.

His television career began with "Dogs Behaving Badly" in 2017, followed by "Dogs Behaving (Very) Badly" in 2019.

He has also presented "The Dog Hospital with Graeme Hall," made a cameo in "All Creatures Great and Small," and authored several bestselling books about dogs and dog training.

Hall, who has previously competed in amateur ballroom and Latin competitions, will now be bringing his skills to the Strictly dance floor, dusting off his dancing shoes and seeing if he can remember a few old moves.
