Yellow alert for heavy rain issued for parts of Karnatakashort by Saurav Joshi / 04:49 pm on Wednesday, 4 November, 2026The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has issued a yellow alert for heavy rainfall in Coastal and South Interior Karnataka.
