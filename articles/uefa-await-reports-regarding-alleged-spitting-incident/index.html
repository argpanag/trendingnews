UEFA await reports regarding alleged spitting incident

TheTools · 05.10.2026 13:26 · source · permalink

UEFA will assess both referee and delegate reports from the Nations League clash between the Republic of Ireland and Israel before deciding whether any disciplinary action is required following allegations of spitting.

Ireland's nominal 'home’ game was played behind closed doors in Serbia and ended in a draw after Troy Parrott's first-half penalty was cancelled out after the break.

With acrimony surrounding the build-up and both fixtures against Israel – the Boys in Green won the 'away’ fixture in Debrecen 3-0 – tensions bubbled away once again in Backa Topola.

There was a clash between players and staff of both teams as they entered the tunnel at half-time, with an allegation that an Israeli player spat at a member of the Republic of Ireland backroom team.

Ireland manager Heimir Hallgrimsson kept his counsel on the incident in his post-match media briefings, while his Israeli counterpart Ben Shimon said he was already in the dressing room and didn’t see the incident.

UEFA has said it awaits referee Jose Luis Munuera Montero’s report, as well as a UEFA delegate’s report.

Once they are assessed, the independent disciplinary body will decide on whether to proceed further with any possible sanctions.

Speaking after the 1-1 draw, Hallgrimsson said the incident may well be looked at more closely by the authorities.

"I was there. I'm not going to comment on it. It’s something that a disciplinary committee is probably going to look at," he said.

"There was an accusation of spitting on someone but I didn’t see it and I don’t want to talk about it, it will be reviewed like everything that has happened between the teams.

"I felt that the intention was to try and intimidate us into doing something stupid and I give the players credit for not responding to it".
