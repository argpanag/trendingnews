Civil servants in Johor to get special bonus, says MB

TheTools · 19.11.2026 12:24 · source · permalink
    bookmark

ISKANDAR PUTERI: Johor civil servants will each receive a special bonus amounting to two and a half months' salary this year, says Mentri Besar Datuk Onn Hafiz Ghazi.

He said the incentive would be given to state government employees as well as federal civil servants whose salaries are paid by the Johor government.

"I would also like to extend my appreciation to all Johor civil servants, who are the backbone of the state administration and the main driving force behind the state's development agenda," he said when tabling the Johor Budget 2027 at the state assembly here on Thursday (Nov 19).

"Their hard work and dedication deserve recognition.

"Therefore, I am pleased to announce (this) special incentive... which will be paid at the end of December."

Onn Hafiz also announced that 145,648 federal civil servants serving in Johor would receive a special incentive of RM500 each under the Bantuan Kasih Johor initiative.

"In line with the whole-of-nation approach adopted by Johor, the state government would also like to express its appreciation to the 145,648 federal civil servants serving here.

"We are pleased to announce a special incentive for each of them, with a total allocation of RM72.82mil," he said.

He also announced a special incentive of RM1,000 each for 200 media practitioners under the Bantuan Kasih Johor initiative.

He said the state government had allocated RM200,000 for the incentive, to be disbursed in phases in December and February.
