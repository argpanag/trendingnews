Pollies: Strategic voting and tributes to Adrian Rurawhe

TheTools · 29.09.2026 20:36 · source · permalink
  • On Air
  • Mike Hosking Breakfast

Today on Politics Wednesday, Mark Mitchell and Ginny Andersen joined Mike Hosking to dig into some of the biggest stories of the week thus far. 

They discussed the call from some parties for voters to engage in strategic voting and the disagreement between Chris Hipkins and Chlöe Swarbrick as to who would be Finance Minister if they were in government.  

And they paid tribute to former Speaker and Labour MP Adrian Rurawhe, who died at age 65.
