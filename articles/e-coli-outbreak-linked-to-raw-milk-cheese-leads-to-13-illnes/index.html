E. coli outbreak linked to raw milk cheese leads to 13 illnesses and eight hospitalizations across the US: FDA

A recall has been issued for raw milk cheese after more than a dozen people were sickened with E. coli across several states.

The recalled products include Sierra Nevada Graziers grass-fed raw milk cheeses: medium cheddar, sharp cheddar, jalapeño jack, and Monterey jack sold at retail in 8-oz or 16-oz packages.

A total of 13 people have been sickened so far, including eight hospitalizations. The last onset of symptoms was on Aug. 26.

"The FDA and CDC, in collaboration with state and local partners, are investigating a multistate outbreak of E. coli O26:H11 infections linked to Sierra Nevada Graziers grass-fed raw milk cheese," the U.S. Food and Drug Administration said in a release. "FDA’s investigation is ongoing."

When public health officials interviewed nine of those sickened, they all said they had eaten Sierra Nevada Graziers grass-fed raw milk cheese within the last week.
