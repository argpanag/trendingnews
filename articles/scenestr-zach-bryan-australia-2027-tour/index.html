scenestr - Zach Bryan Australia 2027 Tour

TheTools · 14.10.2026 20:32 · source · permalink
  • Written by: Staff Writers
  • Published: 15 October 2026

American country musician Zach Bryan will finally tour Australia, with the 'Something In The Orange' hitmaker to land down under in late 2027.

After performing an exclusive, one-off Melbourne concert at Flemington Racecourse in 2023 (which sold out in minutes), the singer-songwriter and guitarist (and US Navy veteran) will headline stadiums in Melbourne, Sydney and Brisbane (two concerts per city) December 2027.

The shows (that will also feature Vance Joy and Emma Ogier) are part of the Young Manhood & Other Donatable Organs tour that relates to the EP of the same name, which has dropped today and features collabs with Lana Del Rey and Turnpike Troubadours.

"Love you guys very much and hope you enjoy it," Zach posted to his Insta, which has been wiped clean (in anticipation of this new era?). "Thanks for a great year on the road."

Across six studio albums, his most recent being 2026's 'With Heaven on Top' (that charted #1 on the Australian Country Albums chart), Zach tells stories of raw emotion, self-doubt, addiction and heartbreak, capturing the complexities of love, loss and the human experience with a rare and unfiltered honesty.

A self-made artist who has built his following via non-traditional industry pathways, embracing the DIY ethos of social media, his raw storytelling has seen many compare Zach as a modern day Springsteen, who Bryan collaborated in 2024 on the song 'Sandpaper' that features on his album 'The Great American Bar Scene'.

Bryan has also collaborated with the likes of Noah Kahan ('Sarah's Place), Maggie Rogers ('Dawns'), Bon Iver ('Boys Of Faith'), Kings of Leon ('Bowery' and 'We're Onto Something'), John Mayer ('Better Days) and Kacey Musgraves ('I Remember Everything').

Zach made headlines earlier this month after wearing a 'Free Palestine' shirt at his concert at Gillette Stadium (playing to a crowd of close to 74,000 fans) in New England, which is owned by Robert Kraft who was active in leading to Macklemore's removal from the Ed Sheeran concert series last month.

Zach Bryan continues to cement his position as one of the most powerful live draws in contemporary music. Don't miss this opportunity to witness one of the leading voices in modern country and Americana.

Tickets on sale from 2pm (local time) on 23 October.

Zach Bryan 2026 Tour Dates

Sat 4 Dec - Marvel Stadium (Melbourne)
Fri 10 Dec - Accor Stadium (Sydney)
Sat 11 Dec - Accor Stadium (Sydney)
Fri 17 Dec - Suncorp Stadium (Brisbane)
Sat 18 Dec - Suncorp Stadium (Brisbane)
