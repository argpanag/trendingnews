Is Discord down? Discord down for many Tuesday.Dscord is down

TheTools · 15.09.2026 14:45 · source · permalink

Discord users could find some trouble Tuesday morning.

According to DownDetector, some are unable to get on the free voice, video and text service. The issues popped up at 10 a.m. EST and had about 26,000 reports as of 10:30 a.m.. Discord outage was trending on Google, as was "discord down."

According to Downdetector, the biggest problem was with the app. That's where about 70 percent of the issues are.

There is no timetable for when the issues will be resolved. It could be a quick fix, as these often are.
