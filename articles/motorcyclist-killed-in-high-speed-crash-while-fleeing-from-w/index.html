Motorcyclist killed in high-speed crash while fleeing from Wyandotte police

TheTools · 16.10.2026 10:04 · source · permalink

Local News

The driver fled from police at speeds above 100 mph in a chase spanning two Downriver communities

WYANDOTTE – Michigan State Police are investigating a high-speed police chase Thursday night that began in Wyandotte and ended with a deadly motorcycle crash in Riverview.

Wyandotte police say they attempted a traffic stop with the motorcyclist after receiving reports of a man on a motorcycle traveling over 100 mph on Biddle Avenue. When they located the man in the area of Biddle Avenue and Spruce Street, the driver failed to stop and fled from police at a speed above 100 mph, at one point entering into oncoming traffic, police said.

The high-speed pursuit continued into the city of Riverview, where the motorcyclist lost control on a dead-end road and crashed.

The driver was taken to a nearby hospital where he later died. Police say the motorcycle did not have a license plate and the identity of the driver has not been released.

An investigation into the crash is ongoing.
