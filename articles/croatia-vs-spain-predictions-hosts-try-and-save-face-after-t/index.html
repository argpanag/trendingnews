Neither side has a huge reason to open up

This is the fourth match in 11 days for many. Spain are already nine points clear at the top of the group, and there is nothing left for them to prove. They’ll almost certainly be top of the group whatever the scoreline on Tuesday night.

De la Fuente is also managing a stretched squad. Ferran Torres, Nico Williams and Álex Grimaldo all withdrew before Saturday, while Fabián Ruiz is away for the birth of his child.

La Roja keep allowing goals even though they are winning

Spain are not as watertight as the results suggest. They have conceded in all three of their Nations League games this season. England managed to put two past Unai Simón, while Croatia and Czechia both got one apiece.

Croatia still have quality in the middle of the park. Luka Modrić and Mateo Kovačić should have most of the ball at their feet. The veteran pair will be desperate to right the wrongs of their heaviest ever loss in Rijeka.

The problem is who will be on the other end of those passes to finish. Bilić has left out Beljo and Budimir of this squad. Igor Matanović is the likely replacement up front to start and probably score. Backing both sides to score at a 54% implied probability looks fair rather than generous.

Croatia will host Spain at Stadion Poljud in Split on Tuesday night. This is the fourth round of matches in League A, Group 3 of the 2026/27 Nations League. Thetwo sides met only a week ago in Seville, with Spain winning 4-1.

In their last match, Croatia were humiliated 7-0 by England at home in Rijeka. It was the Croatia national team’s worst defeat in their history. Slaven Bilic’s men were already four goals down by the half time interval, and the Three Lions continued attacking in the second.

Harry Kane and Bukayo Saka both scored twice in that rout, while Jude Bellingham made three assists aside scoring himself. Croatia have now conceded 12 goals in just three games of this campaign.

In response to this fixture, Slaven Bilić has left Martin Pongračić, Lovro Majer, Ante Budimir and Dion Beljo out of his squad. More so, there are five more players in the squad that are just one booking from a suspension.

During Croatia’s debacle against England, Spain beat Czechia 3-1 on the same night as Lamine Yamal, Rodri and Mikel Merino scored. Luis De la Fuente made nine changes for that game and they still won comfortably. Spain are unbeaten in 41 matches at the moment. They lead the group with nine points from a possible nine. Croatia sit third on three points, above Czechia who are the only team in the group to lose all three games.
