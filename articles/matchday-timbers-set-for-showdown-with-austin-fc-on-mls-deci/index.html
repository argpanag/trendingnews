Matchday | Timbers set for showdown with Austin FC on MLS Decision Day | PTFC

TheTools · 08.11.2026 00:24 · source · permalink

The Portland Timbers, winners of three in a row and unbeaten in four, head into MLS Decision Day Saturday with everything to play for in a match against Austin FC. Kickoff from Q2 Stadium is 4pm PT with a broadcast on Apple TV. 

A win will see Portland clinch a spot in the Audi 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs but learn all the scenarios that could see Portland through.

This evening's match is presented by Toyota.

How To Watch

The match airs live on Apple TV.

Fans can also listen to the game on the radio. This week, it is on 94.7 KNRK and in Spanish on La GranD (KGDD 93.5 FM, 1520 AM).
