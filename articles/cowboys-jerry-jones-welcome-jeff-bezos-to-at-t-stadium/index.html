Cowboys, Jerry Jones welcome Jeff Bezos to AT&T Stadium

TheTools · 21.09.2026 20:55 · source · permalink

Amazon founder Jeff Bezos “was at AT&T Stadium” on Sunday to watch the Commanders face the Cowboys, joining Cowboys owner Jerry Jones on the field ahead of kickoff. Bezos, speaking with KRLD-FM before the game, said there is “something special about this stadium... about this team, and of course, something special about Jerry.” Jones said that “he ‘pulled out all the stops’ to expand the energy by having Bezos as a visitor.” Bezos also weighed in on the Cowboys facing the Ravens in Brazil next Sunday, calling it a “great opportunity for the league to expose more people to American football.” Bezos: “That kind of exposure is a great deal” (DALLAS MORNING NEWS, 9/20).
