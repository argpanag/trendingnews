MMA Prop Pick of the Week: UFC 334

UFC 334: Gane vs Hokit

Start Time: 5:00 PM EDT 

I love me some knockout props for plus money, and I happily found a playable one for this weekend’s festivities. Middleweight Donte ‘Lockjaw’ Johnson is going to knock out Cesar Almeida on Saturday’s prelims.

Johnson used to fight at heavyweight (back when he was known as ‘2 Entrees’ – no joke), and his power from that weight class has come with him down to 185 pounds. Seven of Johnson’s nine pro wins have come via T/KO, including his last fight, and Almeida just got knocked out his last time in the cage. A perfect storm.

UFC 334 Prop Pick: Donte Johnson to win inside the T/KO (+165)
