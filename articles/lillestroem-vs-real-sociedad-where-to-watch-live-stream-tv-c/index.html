Lillestroem host Real Sociedad at Aaraasen Stadion in Skedsmo in the Europa League, with both sides locked in a battle to climb away from the lower reaches of the competition table.

Hans Erik Oedegaard's side come into this fixture with a degree of momentum after drawing 1-1 with Sparta Prague in their most recent Europa League outing. That point represented a solid return for a Norwegian side that has found European nights difficult, and Lillestroem will look to build on it in front of their own supporters.

Real Sociedad arrive under Pellegrino Matarazzo having drawn 1-1 with Union St. Gilloise last time out in the Europa League. The Spanish side have shown they can score goals — five in their last two LaLiga outings alone — but their continental form has been patchy, and they need a win to shift their position in the standings.

Both clubs currently sit 22nd in the Europa League table, which underlines just how much is riding on this fixture. A defeat for either side would leave them facing a very difficult path through the competition.

Lillestroem have the advantage of playing at home, and Aaraasen Stadion can be an uncomfortable venue for visiting sides. Real Sociedad will need to be sharp from the start if they are to take the points back to Spain.
