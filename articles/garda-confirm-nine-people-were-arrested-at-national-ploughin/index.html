Gardaí confirm nine people were arrested at National Ploughing Championships

TheTools · 22.09.2026 09:23 · source · permalink

Eight people were arrested under the Public Order Offences Act at the National Ploughing Championships, gardaí confirmed.

An Garda Síochána has confirmed there were eight arrests made for Public Order Offences at the National Ploughing Championships last week, while one person was arrested under the Non-fatal Offences Against the Person Act.

The figure comes as the Irish Farmers Journal reported that a Roscommon farmer in his 60s was arrested under the Public Order Act shortly after noon on Tuesday.

He was arrested following an incident involving An Taoiseach Micheál Martin.

Political staffers working alongside other senior politicians at the event said security had been heightened after the arrest.

The event took place from Tuesday to Thursday in Screggan, Co Offaly, and drew a crowd of 243,000 over the three days.

Next year's event will take place in Rathangan, Co Kildare, from 21 to 23 September.
