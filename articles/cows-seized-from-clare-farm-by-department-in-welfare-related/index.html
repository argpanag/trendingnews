Cows seized from Clare farm by Department in welfare-related operation

TheTools · 07.11.2026 09:50 · source · permalink

An Garda Síochána assisted the Department of Agriculture in the operation, which occurred on Wednesday.

The Department of Agriculture seized cows from a farm in Co Clare on Wednesday as part of an animal welfare-related enforcement operation.

An Garda Síochána assisted the Department of Agriculture in the operation.

The Department would not comment further on the matter, as it said it “does not comment on any individual cases”.

However, in general terms, a Department spokesperson told the Irish Farmers Journal that it has lead policy responsibility for animal welfare and takes the matter of animal welfare most seriously.

“Neglect of or causing unnecessary suffering to animals is not acceptable in our society."

Legal obligations

“The Department is committed to the policy that animal welfare standards are vigorously applied in a fair and even-handed manner.

“Every herd owner has legal obligations and responsibilities to ensure that the health and welfare of the animals they own or control is safeguarded.

“Under the Animal Health and Welfare Act 2013, authorised officers may enter and inspect, at all reasonable times, any land or premises where an animal may be or has been present,” the spokesperson added.

Where welfare issues arise, the Department spokesperson said its approach is to proactively engage with herd owners to: “Firstly, try and resolve non-compliance matters in a voluntary manner or, failing that, through the issuing of legal notices or formal direction.”

Last resort

The removal of animals from a farm is only undertaken as a last resort to protect animal health and welfare, they added.

“The Department is committed to the policy that animal welfare standards are vigorously applied in a fair and even-handed manner.

“Neglect or causing unnecessary suffering to animals is not acceptable.

“The Department operates a confidential animal welfare helpline through which members of the public can report incidents of animal cruelty or neglect for investigation,” the spokesperson added.

The dedicated email address and helpline in place for reporting suspected cases of animal welfare are animalwelfare@agriculture.gov.ie and 01-607 2379.
