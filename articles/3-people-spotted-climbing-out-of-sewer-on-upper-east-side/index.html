3 people spotted climbing out of sewer on Upper East Side

TheTools · 23.09.2026 12:51 · source · permalink

UPPER EAST SIDE, Manhattan (WABC) -- Three unidentified people were seen climbing out of a sewer on Manhattan's Upper East Side before fleeing in two vehicles early Wednesday morning.

Police said officers responded to a 911 call about a possible crime around 4:35 a.m. near 535 Park Ave. at East 61st Street. When officers arrived, they learned three individuals had exited a sewer and left the area, traveling northbound on Park Avenue.

The incident was captured on an NYPD surveillance camera, authorities said.

One vehicle was described as a silver sedan and the second was described as a four-door vehicle driven by a man wearing a black shirt and gray shorts.

In total, five men are believed to have entered the sewers through a subway access gate at East 63rd Street and Fifth Avenue. Based on the timing, they are believed to have been in the sewers for hours, searching for lost valuables and metal-like copper that can easily be resold.

No arrests have been made and the investigation remains ongoing.

The suspicious incident is believed linked to an open pattern of more than 20 cases of suspected Venezuelan nationals searching for lost valuables in the sewer, sources said.

The cars used as getaway vehicles have been linked, as many of the vehicles involved, to this ongoing pattern.

The unusual sighting comes as police maintain heightened security in areas surrounding the United Nations General Assembly, which has brought world leaders and international delegations to New York City this week. However, the suspicious sewer activity is not believed to be linked to Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's visit.

The incident is the latest in a series of reports involving people entering or emerging from sewer and manhole systems across the New York area in recent months. Similar incidents have been reported near Penn Station, in Queens, Brooklyn and on Long Island.

In some cases, those involved told police they were searching underground for valuables.

Law enforcement officials say the most recent incident seems to fit the same criteria, but out of an abundance of caution due to the General Assembly, the NYPD is conducting additional security sweeps with federal partners.

The city's Department of Environmental Protection has previously warned that entering the sewer system without authorization is illegal and dangerous. A spokesperson issued a new statement about the latest incident:

"DEP is aware of individuals illegally entering the sewer system at this location. DEP has inspected the sewer and our infrastructure is safe. If observed, residents should immediately call 911 and provide a description so law enforcement can quickly respond. DEP supports the NYPD in investigating these types of incidents to help ensure the safety and security of our critical infrastructure throughout New York City."

Investigators believe the three people on Wednesday morning got in the sewage system through the East 63rd and Fifth Avenue subway station before popping out on Park Avenue and jumping into the two getaway vehicles.
