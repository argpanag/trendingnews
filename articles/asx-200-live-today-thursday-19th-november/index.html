ASX 200 dragged lower by miners and jobs data fuels rate cut bets

Nufarm flags heavier second-half skew and elevated tax rate on results call

[1:26 pm] Nufarm management told analysts FY27 earnings will lean more heavily on the second half than usual, with most of the benefit from its latest cost-cutting program not arriving until FY28, as the shares trade down 5.8% after falling as much as 12% earlier.

  • On consensus, UBS’s analyst said the segment guidance gets “more or less” to consensus for FY27 EBITDA, which management didn’t dispute

  • On earnings phasing, management told Macquarie that FY27 will be more weighted to the second half than FY26, when 64% of EBITDA came in the first half, because of the timing of the Kwinana closure and a production catch-up after the Laverton and Wyke outages

  • On cost savings, management told UBS that only part of the new $50m program is included in FY27 guidance, with the majority of the benefit landing in FY28

  • On tax, management said the 40-45% underlying tax rate reflects non-cash deferred tax effects as omega-3 production shifts from North America to South America, and should normalise to about 30% over time

  • On North America, management told Citi the weakness reflects lower market volumes and the removal of low-margin products, not channel destocking

  • On the plant outages, management declined to tell RBC how much the Laverton and Wyke outages cost, saying the impact wasn’t material

Unemployment rate jumps to 4.9%, highest since 2021

[11:41 am] Australia’s unemployment rate rose to its highest level in five years in October as employment fell sharply, adding to signs of a weakening labour market.

  • The unemployment rate rose to 4.9% in October from 4.7% in September, the highest since October 2021

  • Employment fell by 44,000, while the number of unemployed people rose by 18,000

  • Youth unemployment rose to 11.3% from 11.1%, its highest since October 2021 and well above the post-Covid average of 9.2%

  • The participation rate fell 0.2 percentage points to 66.6%, and the employment-to-population ratio fell 0.3 points to 63.3%

  • Hours worked fell 0.2%, while the underemployment rate eased 0.1 points to 6.2%

  • Trend unemployment rose to 4.8%, with the number of unemployed people up 13.3% over the year in trend terms

Copper miners fall after copper price slumps

[10:44 am] The S&P/ASX Materials Index is down 2.5%, the largest drag on the ASX this morning. The index has lost 12.8% since last Thursday and is now falling for a sixth straight session.

The copper price fell 5.6% to US$5.74 a pound overnight. Copper has been one of the main commodity plays linked to AI, with investors betting that data centres and the power grid upgrades needed to run them would drive years of extra demand for the metal. That trade has come under significant strain lately, whether from the Democrats’ win in Congress or the fallout from Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s exit.
