Everything you need to know if you're going to Robbie Williams' NZ concert

TheTools · 23.11.2026 08:48 · source · permalink

Robbie Williams is bringing his BRITPOP World Tour back to New Zealand for two massive stadium shows this November.

It's been years since Kiwi fans last got the chance to see one of pop's greatest entertainers on our shores, and with hits like Angels, Rock DJ, Feel, Let Me Entertain You and Better Man expected to feature, both Auckland and Christchurch are shaping up to be unforgettable nights.

Here is everything you need to know:

Event Overview

Auckland Show

  • Name of Event: Robbie Williams BRITPOP World Tour
  • Date: Tuesday 24 November 2026
  • Location: Eden Park, Auckland
  • Gates Open: 5:00pm
  • Show Starts: 6:30pm
  • Age Restrictions: Licensed all-ages event. Under-18s must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian.
  • Support Acts: Drax Project, Lottery Winners
  • Tickets: Available through AXS

Christchurch Show

  • Name of Event: Robbie Williams BRITPOP World Tour
  • Date: Saturday 28 November 2026
  • Location: One New Zealand Stadium, Christchurch
  • Doors Open: 5:00pm
  • Show Starts: 6:30pm
  • Age Restrictions: Licensed all-ages event. Under-18s must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian.
  • Support Acts: Drax Project, Lottery Winners
  • Tickets: Final tickets available through AXS

Auckland: How To Get There

Venue Address

Eden Park
Reimers Avenue
Kingsland
Auckland

Public Transport Options

  • Train: Kingsland Station sits directly beside Eden Park and is the easiest way to arrive.
  • Bus: Special event buses and regular Auckland Transport services will run to the venue.
  • Free Travel: Concert tickets include free Auckland Transport bus and train travel from 3pm on event day.
  • Rideshare: Uber, taxis and Lime/Flamingo e-bikes and scooters are recommended.

Parking Information

  • No public parking is available at Eden Park.
  • Parking restrictions are heavily enforced around Kingsland.
  • Nearby free parking can sometimes be found in surrounding suburbs, but expect a 15-20 minute walk.

Christchurch: How To Get There

Venue Address

One New Zealand Stadium
218 Madras Street
Christchurch Central City
Christchurch

Public Transport Options

  • Bus: The Christchurch Bus Interchange is about 500m from the stadium.
  • Event Shuttles: Metro operates pre-booked event shuttles from multiple locations around Christchurch.
  • Rideshare: Taxi and rideshare drop-off zones will operate around the venue.
  • Walking/Cycling: The stadium is well-connected to central-city walking and cycling routes.

Parking Information

  • No on-site public parking.
  • Several commercial parking buildings operate near the stadium.
  • Expect heavy congestion after the concert and allow extra time for departure.

What You Can Bring

✔ Allowed Items

  • Mobile phones
  • Small bags
  • Digital tickets
  • Personal medication
  • Empty drink bottles (subject to venue policy)

✖ Prohibited Items

  • Professional cameras with detachable lenses
  • Drones
  • Selfie sticks
  • Weapons
  • Illegal substances
  • Outside alcohol
  • Large backpacks

Food & Drinks

  • Food and beverage outlets will be available throughout both venues.
  • Both venues operate largely cashless systems.
  • Bring an EFTPOS, debit or credit card.
  • Valid photo ID will be required for alcohol purchases.

Accessibility Information

Auckland

  • Accessible seating and viewing options are available.
  • Mobility parking allocations were administered through Eden Park's accessibility programme.
  • Contact Eden Park directly for accessibility assistance.

Christchurch

  • Accessible seating and services are available throughout One New Zealand Stadium.
  • Accessible parking information is available through the venue's accessibility team.
  • Companion seating options are available where required.

Safety & Security

  • Bag inspections and security screening should be expected.
  • Both venues operate a no pass-out/no re-entry policy.
  • First aid services will be available throughout the event.
  • Smoking and vaping are prohibited within venue seating and viewing areas.

Additional Tips & FAQs

  • Arrive early to avoid queues.
  • Download your ticket before arriving.
  • Make sure your phone is charged.
  • Bring ID if you're planning to purchase alcohol.
  • Merchandise stands are expected to be available inside both venues.
  • Stadium concerts can get cool once the sun goes down, so bring an extra layer.

Final Reminders

  • Auckland: Use public transport wherever possible. There is no public parking at Eden Park.
  • Christchurch: Plan your parking and transport before leaving home.
  • Check your ticket carefully for your allocated gate.
  • Download your mobile ticket before arriving at the venue.
  • Keep an eye on official Robbie Williams, Frontier Touring, AXS and venue channels for any last-minute updates.

For more information visit Robbie Williams' official website, Frontier Touring, Eden Park and One New Zealand Stadium Christchurch event pages.
