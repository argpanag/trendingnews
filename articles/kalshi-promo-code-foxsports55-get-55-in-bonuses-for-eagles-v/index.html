Players To Watch

QB Dak Prescott

Cowboys

This game puts Dallas’ passing attack front and center, and Prescott is the clearest reason why. Through six games, he leads the Cowboys with 1,639 passing yards and 10 touchdown passes, while Dallas ranks 6th in passing yards per game at 267.2 and 9th in scoring at 25.2 points per game.

What makes Prescott especially important in Philadelphia is the contrast between the Cowboys’ air game and the Eagles’ pressure numbers. Philadelphia has only 8 sacks through six games, tied for 29th in the league, so Prescott could have room to work if Dallas protects him. The Cowboys have also allowed just 7 sacks all season, which sharpens that matchup even more. If Prescott controls the pocket, Dallas can dictate the tempo under the lights at Lincoln Financial Field.

WR Ceedee Lamb

Cowboys

When Dallas needs a chain-moving target or a field-tilting play, Lamb is the kind of receiver who can swing this matchup. He has 48 catches for 577 yards and 4 touchdowns through six games, and the Cowboys are completing 66.3 percent of their passes as a team, signs of an offense that leans heavily on its pass catchers to create production.

His importance grows because Philadelphia has allowed 137 total points through six games and sits in the middle of the pack at 17th-fewest points allowed per game (22.8). That leaves room for a high-volume receiver to influence the game, especially in a rivalry where third-down conversions can decide long stretches of the night. Lamb is the player who can turn Dallas’ efficient passing game into explosive scoring chances.

QB Tanner McKee

Eagles

McKee is worth watching because he is now the Eagles’ starting quarterback with Jalen Hurts out for the rest of the season after being diagnosed with blood clots. He started in Week 6 with Hurts sidelined, throwing for 215 yards and a touchdown in a 25-14 loss to Carolina, and those are his totals on the year.

Dallas has 8 sacks and 1 interception on defense through six games and allows 26.5 points per game. McKee takes over an Eagles offense that has scored 106 points in six games, and a divisional game is the first chance to see what the offense looks like with him leading it from the start.
