GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto 6) - Official 'DualSense Controllers' Trailer | State of Play 2026 - IGN

TheTools · 03.09.2026 13:48 · source · permalink

Grand Theft Auto 6 is nearly here, and PlayStation 5 players will officially be able to play in style as the State of Play has revealed two Limited Edition DualSense controllers for PS5.

Featuring the ""style and luxury of Vice City,"" these controllers boast "a shimmering, iridescent finish, textured palm leaf details, and official Grand Theft Auto VI branding."

Both controllers will be available on November 19, the same day as Grand Theft Auto 6, and pre-orders will begin on September 10 at 10am local time.
