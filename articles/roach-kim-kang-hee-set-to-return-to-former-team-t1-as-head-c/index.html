'Roach' Kim Kang-hee Set to Return to Former Team T1 as Head Coach

T1 appears to be kicking off its preparations for the upcoming season in earnest by appointing Kim 'Roach' Kang-hee as its new head coach.

According to Inven's reporting on November 17, former coach Kim Kang-hee is expected to be appointed as the head coach of T1's primary roster for the upcoming 2027 season. Kim, who hinted at a return after completing his mandatory military service and signing with an agency, is set to continue his coaching career with his former team, where he contributed to back-to-back LoL World Championship (Worlds) victories.

Kim began his coaching career as a coach for T1's secondary roster in 2022 immediately after retiring as a player. Leading the secondary squad alone at the time while effectively serving as head coach, Kim got off to a smooth start by securing first place in the Summer regular season and winning the Best Coach award.

Recognized for his potential, he was promoted to the primary roster as a coach for the 2023 season and firmly established his credentials as a leader by helping the team lift the Summoner's Cup in both 2023 and 2024. After three consecutive years of success, Kim left T1 after the 2024 season to fulfill his military service.

Following a brief rest after completing his service last August, he is projected to take the helm of his former team as head coach rather than coach ahead of the 2027 season. His deep understanding of the organization's internal systems and existing roster, combined with his proven strategic capabilities on the international stage, are considered the decisive reasons for his selection as head coach.

Meanwhile, the contracts of T1's existing coaching staff all expired as of 9:00 AM on November 17 in accordance with Riot Games' official stove league regulations. An official announcement from the organization has not yet been released.
