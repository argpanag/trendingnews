Fans React to Gen's Controversial Flirting Amid Below Deck Med's Nathan-Joe Feud - AOL

TheTools · 15.09.2026 09:50 · source · permalink

As Below Deck Mediterranean tensions continue to mount between bosun Nathan Gallagher and lead deckhand Joe Bradley, fans seem to be focusing just as much — if not more — on the low-key drama brewing in the interior.

Genevieve “Gen” Lillie has been stirring controversy since she stepped aboard. From expressing frustration over not being hired as a second stew to her complicated romantic history with Luke Brumer and Chef Joy Lefaucheur, Gen has given viewers plenty to discuss. But Monday night’s episode took things to another level, and fans quickly made her increasingly messy behavior one of the biggest talking points of the episode.

Gen’s latest woe is her swollen wrist and the pain she says she’s experiencing while doing her job. But while she vents to Katrina “Kat” Johnston about her injury, she’s also openly flirting with Kat’s “secret” romantic partner, deckhand Cooper Dawson.

Gen’s behavior quickly crosses from flirtatious to uncomfortable for Kat, particularly after Gen asks Cooper for a hug and continues flirting with him at dinner. She even gives him the eyebrow-raising nickname “Massive C*ck Coop,” leaving Kat in tears.

Fans watching along in the episode’s Reddit discussion were decidedly unimpressed. One viewer said they were “embarrassed” for Gen and called her comments “disgusting,” while another bluntly wrote, “I hope after this season I never have to see Gen on my TV screen again.” Another fan argued that Cooper deserves some of the blame, writing that he is keeping his relationship with Kat secret to “keep his options open.”

The criticism didn’t stop there. One fan said Gen’s behavior was “SO OBVIOUS” in its immaturity, while another questioned why Kat was directing her anger at Gen rather than Cooper. And a separate commenter praised Chief Stew Aesha Scott’s earlier instincts, suggesting Gen may have looked qualified on paper, but Aesha was right not to put her in a position of greater authority.

But that’s not all.

Gen still seems to have feelings for Luke, who has left the boat but continues to text Joy. So when Joy receives a surprise flower delivery from Luke, it’s too much for Gen. She realizes the flowers aren’t for her and is visibly crushed, telling the cameras, “F—k this charter season. I want to go home.”

And fans are already anticipating the fallout. In the episode discussion, one viewer invoked “karma” while another called the preview of Gen deliberately washing Joy’s clothes with dirty cleaning rags “way beyond disrespectful.”

And this storyline appears only to be getting started.

Below Deck Mediterranean is on Mondays at 8 p.m. ET/PT on Bravo.
