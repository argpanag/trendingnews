Everything Kim Kardashian Has Teased About Whirlwind Romance With F1 Driver Lewis Hamilton

TheTools · 22.10.2026 19:12 · source · permalink

Kim Kardashian is keeping her romance with Lewis Hamilton close to the vest — but she’s dropped just enough hints to keep fans’ engines running.

The Skims mogul and the Formula 1 driver have been romantically linked since February after nearly a decade of friendship.

“They are really in love. Kim may want to have more kids with Lewis,” a source exclusively told Us in August. “They just get each other, they get each other’s quirks. She is really happy with him.

Kardashian, who shares kids North, Saint, Chicago and Psalm with ex-husband Kanye West, has subtly offered clues into her new romance via The Kardashians, social media and more. Scroll down to see what Kardashian has shared:

Kim Kardashian and Lewis Hamilton Are Instagram Official

Kardashian debuted her relationship via social media in June 2026, sharing an Instagram carousel captioned “Lately” that included pictures with her children, sisters and friends. Two of the slides also featured Hamilton, including a video he shot of the pair on a bike ride in which Kardashian appeared to veer into his lane.

Kim Kardashian’s Dating Mindset Before Dating Lewis Hamilton

In an October 2026 profile with The Times of London, Kardashian opened up about where she was emotionally before her romance with Hamilton began.

“I was really single for five years, and that felt great,” she said. “I could focus on my kids and projects that I really wanted to be involved with. I really felt no rush. Like, I really didn’t feel empty. I felt like I needed my time. I wanted to completely heal and just work on myself. I wasn’t ready to open up. I really wasn’t even thinking about it. The universe works in your favour when you just allow it to.”

When asked whether she wanted to discuss her romance with Hamilton, she replied: “In the most respectful way, I’d probably rather not.”

Kim Kardashian Teased Their Romance on ‘The Kardashians’

On the October 22, 2026, episode of The Kardashians, the reality TV star spoke with Nikki Glaser about a list of 50 attributes she had compiled for an ideal partner. She simply told Glaser that the person currently in her life checked every single box, prompting producers to press for more.

“Oh, you guys are really trying to get this out of me,” Kardashian replied in a confessional. “Let me just say this, I am just really, really happy. That’s all I’ll say for now.”

In a separate confessional, she elaborated: “Isn’t that crazy? Every single [box is checked]. 55 [of them]. To just look up one day and be like, ‘Wait, let me check the manifest list.’ And then be like, ‘Yes, yes, yes, yes, yes, yes, yes.’ It is a crazy feeling.”

Elsewhere during filming, Kardashian was shown taking a “private call” with Hamilton.

Kim Kardashian’s ‘Manifest List,’ Explained

Kardashian first described her wish list in a June 2023 episode of the Hulu series.

“[I need him to] protect me [and] fight for me. Good hygiene, and that is a given, so I should maybe take that off. [He should be] calm. No mom or dad issues,” the Skims cofounder stated at the time. “[I want someone who is] patient, supportive, genuinely happy for me [and] successful. [He should be] spontaneous, fun [and] my friends and family love him. [I want] someone that can be a role model for my kids—especially someone my boys can look up to.”

She added that her future partner should not have “heavy baggage,” saying, “I have enough. [I hope he is] taller than me and someone that loves to work out. [He should be a] motivated and independent person. Someone who is not clingy and someone with good taste.”
