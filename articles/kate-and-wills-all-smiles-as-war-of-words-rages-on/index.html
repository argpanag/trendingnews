Kate and Wills all smiles as war of words rages on

TheTools · 17.09.2026 14:47 · source · permalink

The Prince and Princess of Wales were all smiles when they arrived on Bute to celebrate the Scottish island’s community on Thursday.

The visit comes amid a war of words that broke out over a controversial upcoming memoir about Prince William's mother, Diana, Princess of Wales.

Kate and Wills travelled to the west of Scotland to highlight those bringing residents together, and their first stop was Bute Shinty Club to learn how the sporting centre has become a vital social hub.

William and Kate are known by their Scottish titles, Duke and Duchess of Rothesay, when visiting the country, and they arrived on Bute at their namesake town of Rothesay.

Kate looked chic for the engagement, wearing a casual outfit of brown skinny jeans, a green jumper, and a long brown coat.

She wore her hair down and styled into perfect waves, with Kate beaming for the cameras and chatting to several members of the public throughout the day.

The visit is the first time the couple has visited Bute, arriving by ferry from the mainland. They toured the islands of Iona and Mull in the Inner Hebrides last year.

The trip came as a public dispute blew up between the King, William's father, and Earl Spencer, William's uncle.

Earl Spencer claims in a new book about his sister, Diana, that the King said 'rest assured, we’ll forget her soon enough', days after her death.

A Buckingham Palace spokesperson said in response that 'fraternal grief can cloud reason.'

'While we do not comment on books as a matter of principle, His Majesty is mindful that the pain of fraternal grief can cloud reason, affect judgment and colour memory in ways others do not recognise, even many years after such a loss,' the statement read.
