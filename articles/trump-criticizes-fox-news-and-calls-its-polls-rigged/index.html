Trump criticizes Fox News and calls its polls rigged | Межа

TheTools · 04.11.2026 00:34 · source · permalink

Collage: Mezha. Photos: Daniel Torok / Білий дім (PD)

The US president said Fox News ratings had fallen by 27% and accused the network of conducting rigged polls.

On Wednesday, Donald Trump sharply criticized election polls and Fox News’ falling ratings, called the questions in the polls rigged, and accused the network’s management of bias.

This is reported by Інтерфакс-Україна.

“Fox News ratings are plummeting (by 27%!), because they spend way too much time covering the Democrats… Fox News polls are always the worst and so offensive because of the questions they ask. Every question is rigged, and every answer is complete nonsense! This has been going on for years”

– Donald Trump
