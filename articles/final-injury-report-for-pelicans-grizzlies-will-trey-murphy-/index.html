Final Injury Report for Pelicans-Grizzlies: Will Trey Murphy III, Ty Jerome Play?

TheTools · 02.11.2026 22:11 · source · permalink

The New Orleans Pelicans are on the road Monday night as they travel to take on the Memphis Grizzlies in a Western Conference matchup.

New Orleans enters Monday’s game with a 2-4 record after defeating the Atlanta Hawks at home Saturday night.

In Saturday’s victory, Saddiq Bey led the way for the Pelicans as he recorded 25 points and six rebounds off the bench while Jeremiah Fears added 20 points, five rebounds and seven assists.

The Pelicans will try to grab another victory Monday as they face off against the Grizzlies in a tough road matchup.

Memphis enters Monday’s contest with a 6-0 record after defeating the Sacramento Kings at home in dominant fashion Saturday night.

Cameron Boozer led the way for the Grizzlies in Saturday’s victory as he ended the game with 27 points, nine rebounds and three assists while Isaiah Stewart recorded 24 points and four rebounds off the bench.

While both teams are looking to pick up a victory in Monday night’s matchup, each may be without multiple key players due to injuries.

The Pelicans have three players listed on their injury report, including Trey Murphy III and Jordan Poole.

New Orleans has listed Murphy III as probable and he is expected to play despite dealing with bilateral patellar tendinopathy.

Trey Murphy III (knee) listed probable for Monday.

The Pelicans have ruled out Poole due to left ankle tendinopathy as he will miss his third consecutive contest.

With Poole still sidelined, Bennedict Mathurin and Herbert Jones could both continue to have increased roles.

Yves Missi has also been ruled out as he will miss his fifth straight game with a left knee contusion.

The Grizzlies have two players listed on their injury report, though Ty Jerome is not one of them.

Of course, Jerome missed Saturday’s victory due to back soreness but he is off the injury report and will return against the Pelicans.

Status Report for @memgrizz, 11/2/26 vs New Orleans:

QUESTIONABLE
Quinten Post — RT Ankle Sprain

DOUBTFUL
Scotty Pippen Jr. — Lower Back Soreness

Memphis has listed Scotty Pippen Jr. as doubtful due to lower-back soreness and he is expected to miss his second straight game.

The Grizzlies have listed Quinten Post as questionable due to a right ankle sprain and he is in danger of missing his second straight game as well.

Fans can catch Monday night’s matchup between the Pelicans and Grizzlies from FedExForum at 8 p.m. ET.
