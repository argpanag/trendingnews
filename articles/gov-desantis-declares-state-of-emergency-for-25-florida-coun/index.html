Gov. DeSantis declares state of emergency for 25 Florida counties due to Tropical Storm Isaias

TheTools · 07.10.2026 10:23 · source · permalink

Florida Gov. Ron DeSantis signed an executive order on Tuesday declaring a state of emergency in 25 counties as a precaution due to Tropical Storm Isaias in the Gulf.

A system in the Gulf was upgraded Wednesday morning to Tropical Storm Isaias, with current intensity models showing it could strengthen into the 2026 Atlantic hurricane season's first hurricane.

Forecasters at the National Hurricane Center have recommended that interests in the Yucatan Peninsula and the northern U.S. Gulf Coast closely monitor the progress of the system.

Computer models seem to agree on a path first east, then north toward an area within a range that stretches from Louisiana to the Florida Panhandle.

The executive order signed Tuesday by Gov. DeSantis — EO 26-202 — declares a state of emergency in the following counties: Baker, Bay, Calhoun, Columbia, Dixie, Escambia, Franklin, Gadsden, Gilchrist, Gulf, Hamilton, Holmes, Jackson, Jefferson, Lafayette, Leon, Liberty, Madison, Okaloosa, Santa Rosa, Suwannee, Taylor, Walton, Wakulla, and Washington.

“Today, I signed an Executive Order issuing a state of emergency ahead of potential impacts from Tropical Depression 9. This order will allow the Florida Division of Emergency Management to stage critical preparedness resources and ensure Florida is ready to respond,” DeSantis said in a statement. “Floridians should take this time to get disaster plans in place and ensure their hurricane supply kit is stocked.”
