Thai AirAsia X clarifies cause of delay to Narita–Don Mueang flight

TheTools · 22.11.2026 16:23 · source · permalink
    bookmark

BANGKOK: Several Facebook users said an airline had abruptly cancelled a flight from Narita to Don Mueang, prompting criticism online.

The flight was due to leave at 7.45pm on Friday (Nov 20). The users said the cancellation came at around 11.30pm, after passengers had waited for hours.

More than 300 passengers, including Thai youths, reportedly could not find accommodation so late at night, with the temperature outside at about 4 degrees.

Passengers reportedly had to sleep on the terminal floor overnight at Narita Airport in Japan, prompting widespread criticism on social media.

Thai AirAsia X later confirmed a delay to flight XJ607, which was due to leave Narita International Airport (NRT) in Japan for Don Mueang International Airport (DMK) in Bangkok, Thailand, on Nov 20.

The airline said a technical fault had been detected.

The flight had to be delayed so that repairs could be carried out and parts replaced in line with the highest safety standards.

The airline said it had provided food and drink for all passengers and would compensate them with the following amounts in credit:

- THB2,000 in credit for the delay.

- THB3,000 in credit for overnight accommodation. The airline said hotels were full over the long festival break and it had been unable to find alternative accommodation.

The airline said it would automatically add the full THB5,000 to passengers’ credit accounts.

It also gave affected passengers a choice of three options:

- Passengers can change their travel date free of charge and fly on the same route within 90 days of their original travel date, subject to seats being available on their chosen flight.

- Passengers can keep the credit in their Credit Account for future travel with AirAsia. They must make a new booking within 730 days of the date the credit is approved.

- Receive a full refund.

Passengers can request one of these options through the email or SMS message they received from the airline, through AskBo or by calling 02-078-1094.

Passengers without an AirAsia credit account can also request special compensation through the same channels.

The phone line is open from 9am to 6pm Monday to Friday and from 9am to 5pm on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays. - The Nation/ANN
