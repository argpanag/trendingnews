Formula 1 News: Hamilton slams F1 veterans who attack young drivers

TheTools · 16.11.2026 11:36 · source · permalink

(GMM) Lewis Hamilton (pictured) has criticized older and former Formula 1 drivers who attack younger competitors, insisting he never wants to become that kind of veteran.

The seven-time world champion’s comments, reported by Italy’s Autosprint, come amid renewed debate about driving standards following Fernando Alonso’s recent criticism of Alex Albon and other drivers further down the grid.

“I see a lot of former Formula 1 drivers, or older Formula 1 drivers, who do nothing but constantly badmouth young people,” Hamilton said.

“And even though they’ve been through similar experiences, they show no empathy. I’ve always wanted to make sure I don’t become that kind of driver.”

Alonso, 46, recently caused controversy after colliding with Albon in Austin, declaring that the “quality” of drivers in the bottom third of the field was “very low.”

Hamilton did not specifically name Alonso, his former nemesis at McLaren, but his comments present a contrasting attitude towards the younger generation.

“That’s why I always want to praise those who deserve it,” the 41-year-old Ferrari driver continued.

“It’s been fantastic to see how all these young drivers, including those who start further back on the grid and who race for other teams, give their best every week despite all the pressure they’re under.”
