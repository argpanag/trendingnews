Wayne Rooney follows in wife Coleen's footsteps as he lands in Australia for I'm A Celebrity

TheTools · 09.11.2026 19:29 · source · permalink

Wayne Rooney has officially touched down in Australia as he gets ready to hit the jungle for I'm A Celebrity... Get Me Out Of Here.

Wayne will lead this year's line-up alongside Love Island star Chloe Burrows. The fan-favourite reality show will return to our screens at the end of the month.

Gavin & Stacey's Joanna Page, comedian Leigh Francis, Victoria's Secret Angel Leomie Anderson, and Kaiser Chiefs frontman Ricky Wilson will also be heading to the jungle.

In a light grey tracksuit and sporting a baseball cap, Wayne tried to arrive without anyone noticing, but it wasn't long before the media were hot on his tail.

Arriving in Brisbane, reporters asked the star if he was excited about being in Oz; he told them, 'Yeah I've never been. I was meant to come once when going to Thailand.'

A big fan of karaoke, reporters asked him if he would be singing while in the jungle, to which he just laughed. Not one to hold back from picking up the mic, we have a feeling we will hear a tune or two from the footballer while among his campmates.

Presenter AJ Odudu, Paralympian Ade Adepitan, Loose Women's Mariella Frostrup, Corrie star Claire Sweeney, YouTuber Sharky and comedian Jack Skipper are pegged to make up the rest of the line-up.

Wayne Rooney is set to follow in his wife Coleen's footsteps, as it has been claimed that he will receive the show's biggest fee in its 24-year history. It has been reported that he was offered more than Coleen, who made a record-breaking £ 1.5 million (€1.8 million) for her jungle stint two years ago.

Talk about a payday! The announcement comes after Wayne revealed he wanted to make an appearance on the reality show, telling the MailOnline: '[I'm A Celebrity] is something I would never say no to, of course. I think I said on the aftershow when Coleen was in the jungle, that was the one we've always watched and the one we both said if there was anything we'd do it'd be that.'

'So, you never know in the future, but I've got other things I'm looking at at the minute,' Wayne teased at the time.
