Michelle Pfeiffer, 68, is a bare-faced Hollywood beauty as she shares personal update

TheTools · 11.09.2026 15:55 · source · permalink

Michelle Pfeiffer has showcased her impressive new talent as well has her famously flawless complexion!

Nobody does it quite like Michelle Pfeiffer, and the Hollywood actress proved she is still the reigning queen of beauty with a flawless selfie on Thursday.

Taking to her Instagram account, the Hairspray actress, 68, showed off her flawless, makeup-free face as she showcased her impressive bowl-making talents.

"I made a bowl," penned the Stardust starlet, holding up her creation proudly for the camera.

The Hollywood star was every inch the off-duty A-lister slipping into a jersey-style crew-neck vest.

"All the cool kids are making their way to this clay community I love so much. Welcome to the clay world!" one of Michelle's 3.8 million followers replied.  A second added: "You did a wonderful job." Meanwhile, a third replied: "Beautiful." 

Is Michelle Pfeiffer retiring from Hollywood?

Michelle's mindful moment came after she put speculation to rest that she was retiring from Hollywood. 

Last month, Michelle reportedly told guests at an industry event: "I never want to star in another movie, I swear to God."

She added: "I only want to do ensemble pieces. It's so much more fun, I love it so much."

However, during an appearance on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon on Thursday, the three-time Oscar nominee set the record straight about her future.

Clarifying that the comment was said in jest, Michelle said: "No, I mean, it's the most ridiculous thing. They're always saying that I'm retiring.

"Where does it say that I'm retiring? No, no. All I said was that I really love working with an ensemble cast."

The Scarface star admitted to Jimmy that she regretted the comment "the minute it came out of my mouth," because she realised it could be misinterpreted.

"I said something about 'I never want to star in another movie,' and they took that out of context. Of course I want to star in another movie, but I've done three ensemble pieces [recently]."

"I did Oh. What. Fun. and The Madison and then [Margo's Got Money Troubles]." She added: "It's wonderful to be able to live with a character for such an extended period of time."
