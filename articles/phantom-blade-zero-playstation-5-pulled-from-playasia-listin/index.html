Phantom Blade Zero PlayStation 5 Pulled From Playasia Listings, as Retailer Says No Physical Edition Is Coming

TheTools · 08.10.2026 10:33 · source · permalink

Phantom Blade Zero is among the most anticipated games launching in one of the most packed gaming Octobers of recent memory, and going by everything we have seen (and played, in my case and that of those who attended events like gamescom), the game developed by S-Game has the potential of being one of the Game of the Year candidates. Unfortunately, the enthusiasm of many may be dampened by the very real possibility that the game will not receive a physical release on PlayStation 5.

"Upon checking, we understand that you're asking about Phantom Blade Zero for PS5. Kindly note that the item has already been removed from our listing, as we have been informed that there will not be a physical edition available," reads a response received by Reddit user FMShiNoKama from customer service at known retailer Playasia.

As S-Game has yet to confirm if Phantom Blade Zero will be a digital-only release on both PlayStation 5 and PC, it may be too early for those wanting to get a physical edition to throw in the towel. However, as the game is also not being listed on other traditional retailers like Amazon, things are looking grim, bringing about the discless future PlayStation plans from 2028 onward closer than anyone would have liked.

While I would fully understand why anyone would be disappointed by Phantom Blade Zero not getting a physical release, it would be a shame for any fan of action games to skip the game solely because of this. Ever since playing the game's very early build at gamescom 2024, I have felt S-Game was building something special, and last year's longer hands-on opportunity and recent showcase only confirmed all the positive feelings I have about the game.

As such, it may be worth getting the game regardless of its availability at retail, if anything, to reward a developer that hasn't fallen into the Soulslike trend and is not afraid of trying to do something unique, like with its 66 Days Mode.
