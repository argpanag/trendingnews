'JAG' actor Larry Poindexter dead at 66

TheTools · 20.11.2026 02:15 · source · permalink

Poindexter was awaiting surgery for broken ribs after being struck by a car in LA's Studio City last week

Larry Poindexter as Quinton Bosh and Simon Baker as Patrick Jane appear in a scene from an episode of "The Mentalist." (Sonja Flemming/CBS via Getty Images)

Actor Larry Poindexter died Wednesday awaiting surgery days after being struck by a car in Los Angeles. He was 66.

Poindexter, a character actor known for roles on such television shows as "JAG" and "Days of Our Lives," died suddenly Wednesday morning in a hospital while awaiting surgery to repair broken ribs sustained in the crash, as Deadline first reported. He had been struck by a vehicle Friday while crossing a street in his Studio City neighborhood to pick up food. An official cause of death has not been released.

Poindexter is survived by his wife Carol Kritzer and their twin sons, Rees and Jake.

Paul Iacono, who played the title role of RJ Berger on the 2010-2011 MTV comedy series "The Hard Times of RJ Berger," posted a tribute to Poindexter, who played his father on the show.

"I’m heartbroken by the loss of Larry Poindexter, my dad on RJ Berger," Iacono wrote on Instagram on Thursday morning.

"He was opinionated, to say the least, and I loved him for it. When I was in my early twenties, Larry looked out for me in ways I didn’t fully appreciate at the time. He offered wisdom I wasn’t always ready to hear, and it took me a few years to realize just how right he was about so many things. He taught me that being a good person and collaborator mattered just as much as being a great actor.

"I’m forever honored to have played his son, and I’ll always cherish him as my second father. ❤️"

Born in Dallas in 1959 to Tony Award-winning lighting and set designer H.R. Poindexter and opera singer Sue Ann Erdman, Poindexter began performing on stage in college productions across Texas and Ohio before moving to Los Angeles. There, he worked as an actor and production coordinator with producer Franklin R. Levy’s Catalina Production Group, landing his first television credit on "A New Day in Eden" in 1982 and early guest spots on "Happy Days" and "CHiPs."

Over a career spanning more than 40 years and nearly 200 film and television credits, Poindexter became a familiar face across daytime soaps, primetime series and feature films.

He got his first major break in 1984 playing Ben Welch on "Days of Our Lives," returning to the series three decades later from 2014 to 2018 as Father Louis. He also logged a 23-episode run on "Santa Barbara" and a 12-episode arc on "General Hospital."

Movie audiences recognized him as by-the-book LAPD Capt. Thomas Fuller opposite Samuel L. Jackson and Colin Farrell in "S.W.A.T." (2003).

On television, he held a recurring role on CBS' "JAG" from 1997 to 1998, starred as Rick Berger on MTV’s "The Hard Times of RJ Berger" from 2010 to 2011, and appeared in dozens of hit series, including "Friends," "Quantum Leap," "NYPD Blue," "CSI," "NCIS," "Supernatural," "Weeds," "Scandal," "Curb Your Enthusiasm," "Station 19," "9-1-1," "The Rookie" and the fourth season of "The Lincoln Lawyer" earlier this year.

Poindexter won a Los Angeles Ovation Award for "Reefer Madness," appeared in theatrical productions such as "The Night of the Iguana" and "Tender Is the Night," and served as an executive producer on Broadway’s "The Cher Show," which won two Tony Awards in 2019.
