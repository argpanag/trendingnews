Williamson, Fears lead Pelicans past Kings for first win of season

TheTools · 30.10.2026 02:42 · source · permalink

NEW ORLEANS, La. (Acadiana News First) - Zion Williamson scored 22 points on 10-of-12 shooting, rookie Jeremiah Fears matched him with 22 points and 13 assists, and the New Orleans Pelicans defeated the winless Sacramento Kings 132-120 on Thursday night to secure their first victory of the season.

Trey Murphy III scored 19 points and Herb Jones added 16 for New Orleans (1-4), which took control by outscoring Sacramento 78-58 across the second and third quarters. The Pelicans dominated inside, generating an 80-62 advantage in points in the paint while converting 25 Sacramento turnovers into 40 points.

Darius Acuff Jr. paced Sacramento (0-5) with 34 points and 13 assists, while De’Andre Hunter and Precious Achiuwa scored 24 points apiece. Zach LaVine added 21 points for the Kings, who were undone by 25 giveaways despite shooting 52.3% from the floor.

After trailing by six after the opening period, New Orleans erupted for 39 points in both the second and third frames to build a lead as large as 19. Karlo Matković scored 12 points, and reserves Saddiq Bey and Bennedict Mathurin chipped in 10 each for the Pelicans, who shot 59.5% from the field.
