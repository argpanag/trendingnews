Multiple people injured in shooting near Legion Field following Magic City Classic

TheTools · 01.11.2026 01:22 · source · permalink

Multiple people have been injured in a shooting near Legion Field in Birmingham.

Shots were fired shortly before 8 p.m. on Graymont Avenue West, not long after the end of the Magic City Classic game. Tailgating festivities were still underway in the area.

Emergency crews are treating victims near Third Street West.

Information is limited at this time. Police say multiple people were wounded by gunfire, but that no conditions for any of the victims could be confirmed as of 8:45 p.m.

WVTM 13 has a crew en route to the scene.
