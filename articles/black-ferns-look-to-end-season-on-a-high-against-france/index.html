Black Ferns look to end season on a high against France

TheTools · 31.10.2026 06:52 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

Follow the action as the Black Ferns and France clash in Christchurch.

Match preview

It’s not all bad news on the injury front for the Black Ferns.

Ahead of their final test of the season against France in Christchurch on Saturday, coach Whitney Hansen has seen halfback Tara Turner (concussion), hooker Atlanta Lolohea (ankle), and now star loose forward Kaipo Olsen-Baker (ankle) ruled out of contention.

They will, however, welcome two players back from the injury ward, with midfielder Amy du Plessis and loose forward Mia Anderson returning to the starting line-up.

Du Plessis has been a star in the midfield for Hansen’s side this year in all areas of the game, but missed last weekend’s win in Whangārei due to concussion.

“She’s had a phenomenal year this year with us and I think her leadership and performance have been really standout for us,” Hansen told Newstalk ZB’s D’Arcy Waldegrave on Sunday after the win.

“I think we’ve got to make sure that we’ve got depth in that midfield because the reality is some weeks we won’t have her. We’ve tried a combination of that and we’ll keep thinking about different ways around being able to do that. The exciting thing is we’ve got a multitude of ways we can do that now.”

The side will also welcome loose forward Mia Anderson back after missing the past three tests – against England and two against France – due to an ankle injury and concussion.

Anderson slots straight into the starting side at blindside flanker, with Liana Mikaele-Tu’u moving to the back of the scrum in Olsen-Baker’s absence. They make up a refreshed loose trio alongside Elinor-Plum King at openside, who returns after not being selected last weekend.

The side remains largely unchanged otherwise; Maiakawanakaulani Roos returns to start at lock, switching places with Maama Mo’onia Vaipulu, while Maia Joseph starts at halfback, and Ariana Bayler – called into the squad as an injury replacement for Turner – provides cover from the bench.

Ayesha Leti-I’iga returns to the starting side on the left wing, seeing impressive rookie Shyrah Tuliau-Tua’a move back to the right and Mererangi Paul to the bench.

Black Ferns: 1. Chryss Viliko, 2. Georgia Ponsonby, 3. Veisinia Mahutariki-Fakalelu, 4. Maiakawanakaulani Roos, 5. Laura Bayfield, 6. Mia Anderson, 7. Elinor-Plum King, 8. Liana Mikaele-Tu’u, 9. Maia Joseph, 10. Ruahei Demant (c), 11. Ayesha Leti-I’iga, 12. Logo-I-Pulotu Lemapu-Atai’i Sylvia Brunt, 13. Amy du Plessis, 14. Shyrah Tuliau-Tua’a, 15. Braxton Sorensen-McGee.

Bench: 16. Vici-Rose Green, 17. Maddison Robinson, 18. Santo Taumata, 19. Maama Mo’onia Vaipulu, 20. Amarante Sititi, 21. Ariana Bayler, 22. Hannah King, 23. Mererangi Paul.

Unavailable: Atlanta Lolohea (ankle), Tara Turner (concussion), Kaipo Olsen-Baker (ankle).
