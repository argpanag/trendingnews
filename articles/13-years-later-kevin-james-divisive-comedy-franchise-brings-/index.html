13 Years Later, Kevin James' Divisive Comedy Franchise Brings Back OG Star

TheTools · 30.08.2026 19:05 · source · permalink

Kevin James' divisive comedy franchise is coming back.

James is a comedian and actor known for appearing in hit TV sitcoms and slapstick comedy films with notable titles such as The King of Queens, Hitch, Paul Blart: Mall Cop, and I Now Pronounce You Chuck & Larry. The actor began his career performing stand-up comedy in 1989 before landing recurring appearances on Everybody Loves Raymond, and rose to major fame when he starred in The King of Queens, leading to a successful career in comedy.

13 years after its sequel, Kevin James is returning to a divisive comedy franchise and is bringing a few familiar faces on board. Netflix announced that Grown Ups 3 is officially in production, with the original core cast returning, including Adam Sandler, James, Chris Rock, David Spade, Rob Schneider, Maya Rudolph, Maria Bello, and Salma Hayek Pinault. Now, an actor from the original franchise has announced his return, and that is Jake Goldberg, known for playing Greg Feder, the son of Lenny (played by Sandler).

Goldberg shared the news with a series of throwback images from Grown Ups and Grown Ups 2, with a recent photo holding a sign stating "We back for Grown Ups 3." The actor has appeared in other titles such as 30 Rock, Law & Order: Special Victims Unit, Bull, The Keys, and will be appearing in The Savant.

Other returning cast members include Steve Buscemi, Jackie Sandler, Shaquille O’Neal, Colin Quinn, Tim Meadows, Cheri Oteri, Ellen Cleghorne, Nick Swardson, Jake Goldberg, Alexys Nycole Sanchez, Sadie Sandler, Sunny Sandler, Ada-Nicole Sanger, Morgan Gingerich, Kaleo Elam, Nadji Jeter, and Kamil McFadden.

Netflix also announced new cast members such as Bailee Madison, Deon Cole, and Julie Bowen as filming begins in New Jersey. The official logline for Grown Ups 3 states, "Now that their kids are all grown up, the gang is finally free to go on the ultimate adventure." However, not much more has been revealed surrounding the long-awaited reunion between the beloved gang of actors.

At the time of writing, Grown Ups 3 has yet to reveal an official release date, but is expected to arrive on Netflix mid to late 2027 with production already underway for the comedy franchise's return.
