Montenegro and Albania sign cross-border radio frequency agreement

TheTools · 12.10.2026 07:12 · source · permalink

Montenegrin regulator Ekip and its Albanian counterpart AMA signed a technical agreement in Tirana to coordinate radio frequency usage for digital terrestrial television (DTT) in the 470–694 MHz band. The agreement sets out clear administrative and technical protocols to prevent signal interference in border regions, protecting DTT broadcasts and Montenegro's mobile services in the 694–790 MHz band.
