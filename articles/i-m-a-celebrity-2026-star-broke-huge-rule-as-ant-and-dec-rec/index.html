I'm A Celebrity 2026 star broke huge rule as Ant and Dec recall off-screen chat

TheTools · 20.11.2026 17:09 · source · permalink

Ricky hinted to Ant and Dec that he was doing I'm A Celeb seven months before being confirmed(Image: ITV/Shutterstock)

Ricky Wilson may have broken the I'm A Celebrity rules months before setting foot in the jungle. Hosts Ant and Dec had an interesting chat with the Kaiser Chiefs star prior to the show.

Ricky met the Geordie presenters on The One Show at the start of the year. It turns out he dropped a big hint about doing I'm A Celeb, way before the official line-up was announced.

As fans of the show know, campmates can't reveal they're going to be on the show before ITV share the line-up. Ant and Dec spoke about it on the latest episode of their podcast, Hanging Out With Ant & Dec.

Ant and Dec talked about it on their podcast(Image: Belta Box/Youtube)

Ant said: "We said, ‘Well, we’ll see you soon’ in a showbiz kind of encounter."

Dec said: "It was right at the end of the show, we’d done the show. We were back in the dressing room, saying goodbye, see you soon, take care, lovely to see you…"

Ant then revealed that Ricky told them: "And he went, ‘I might see you sooner than you think. I’ll see you in November'."

Ricky during the I'm A Celeb launch show(Image: ITV/Shutterstock)

But when the pair asked Ricky what he meant, the Kaiser Chiefs star walked off. Ant added: "He left it hanging for seven months."

Dec explained: "Apparently, he was one of the first ones to sign up and he signed up at the beginning of the year."

But Ricky did remember the interaction, and he brought it up when they reunited on the set of I'm A Celebrity.

Asked when they normally find out which celebrities are heading intot he jungle, Ant replied: "We normally find out pre going on our summer holidays. We’ll sit down with the producers of the show and they’ll give us a very early indication of who they want on."
