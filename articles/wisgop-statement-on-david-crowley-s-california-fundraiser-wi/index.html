WisGOP Statement on David Crowley’s California Fundraiser With Kamala Harris

TheTools · 30.09.2026 22:52 · source · permalink

 

MADISON, Wis. — Republican Party of Wisconsin Chairman Brian Schimming released the following statement ahead of David Crowley’s scheduled fundraiser in California with former Vice President Kamala Harris.

“While Wisconsin families are worried about the cost of living, David Crowley is flying to California to raise money with Kamala Harris and Hollywood donors. That tells voters exactly whose approval he’s chasing. Crowley can collect checks in California, but he still has to answer for his record in Milwaukee County. Wisconsinites already rejected Harris’s agenda in 2024. They don’t need Crowley bringing it back to Wisconsin under a new name.” — WisGOP Chairman Brian Schimming.
