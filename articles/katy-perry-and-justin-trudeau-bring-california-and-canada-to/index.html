Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau Bring California and Canada to Venice

TheTools · 24.11.2026 16:42 · source · permalink

Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau are continuing their Venetian holiday in style.

As the California Gurl and Mr. Canada traipsed through the Italian city today, Perry opted for a pop star’s take on prep. Bundled up in a Fair Isle sweater and black double-breasted coat, she added a pair of rectangular transition lenses, heeled penny loafers with white socks, and a cashmere argyle scarf from And Daughter, the latter tied under her chin à la Sophia Loren. (When in Italy, after all!)

Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau in Venice.

Trudeau, on the other hand, looked the part of an off-duty politician in a chore coat, fitted pants, and boots. But it was his baseball cap—emblazoned with a red maple leaf—that really brought his patriotism home.

At the opening of the Lucas Museum of Narrative Art.

Arriving in Venice.

The pair, who have been linked since the summer of 2025, have often embraced an opposites-attract approach in their couple style. While Perry tried her hand at first lady dressing at the World Economic Forum in Davos in January, she’s returned to her pop star roots. Just take the sculptural silver Christian Siriano dress she wore to the opening of the Lucas Museum of Narrative Art in September, or the camel cashmere set—plus Ralph Lauren hat, sunglasses, and over-ear headphones—for her arrival in Venice. Trudeau, to be fair, has met her halfway: the former PM went tieless at the Lucas Museum and arrived in Venice wearing a “will yap about books” T-shirt.

All the same, though they are clearly willing to change for each other, it looks like Katy Perry and Justin Trudeau are sticking to what they know during their Venice getaway.
