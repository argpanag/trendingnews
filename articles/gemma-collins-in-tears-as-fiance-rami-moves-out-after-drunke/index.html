Gemma Collins in tears as fiance Rami moves out after drunken row - AOL

TheTools · 18.09.2026 05:43 · source · permalink

Heartbroken Gemma Collins can't hold back the tears after discovering her fiance Rami Hawash has moved out after the couple's explosive drunken row and tells her: we're done.

In her new Sky documentary: Gemma Collins: Everything All At Once, fans see her break down as the first three new episodes drop today. The argument was captured on camera, and so was the TOWIE legend's reaction to the fall out after he told her he thinks they should go on a break.

Gemma and businessman Rami first met in 2011, and he proposed in 2013 with a diamond ring hidden inside a Christmas pudding. They split the following year, with Gemma, 45, going on to have a rocky on-off romance with her co-star James Argent. Rami and the GC rekindled their romance in 2020 and they got engaged again in 2024.

Earlier this year, the Gemmas poke about marrying Rami and starting a family after her tough year but the couple are in a tricky spot as she continues to come to terms with the death of her mother.

Fans saw it all spill out in the latest episode with tensions high over Rami’s drinking. Following the row, he decides to move out of her Essex home.

As she goes on the house hunt in London, she calls him while viewing the property in Hampstead. Gemma said: “That’s the thing, I want to do all of this with him, I do love him, I really love Rami.

But he's not having it and when he picks up he tells her: “No Gemma I’m sorry, you’re going to have to live there on your own. We’re over, we’re done.”

Gemma - who is a doting stepmum to Rami’s son Tristan - starts to get emotional. “I took all my stuff as that’s what you asked me, you’ve been so f***ing rude to me. I took my stuff today, I took everything.

“This is not healthy for me, this is not healthy for my son and it’s not healthy for nobody. It’s f***ing ridiculous what’s going on here.”

As Gemma wells up, he can be heard saying: “Let’s have a break me and you because it’s just going to get worse.”

She bites back: “But I haven’t done anything, it’s your drinking!” Rami then accuses her of "deflecting" and insists they need a break to see if they can come back together before he hangs up.

Earlier, fans watched Rami come home worse for wear as Gemma is cooking them a romantic meal.

She notices he’s behaving strangely and demands: “What are you calling me Gemma Collins for? Ram, you’re drunk. I can tell by the way you’re talking. Drink some water.

“You’ve had a drink haven’t you? Just be honest about it. Because you’re different.”

Looking annoyed by the accusation, Rami snaps back: “Don’t f***ing give me that alright. Sorry I’m swearing about it but this is who I am. I’ll drink some water. What is that going to help? What have I done wrong? I’m not f***ing drunk Gem. I’m telling you how it is.”

We're left guessing at what happens next with the couple having a big decision to make.

During breaks from her romance with Arg, Gemma had an ill-fated fling with TOWIE ’s Charlie King, who later came out as gay, and in 2015 she dated jailbird Stephen Mortimer, who had previously spent time in prison for GBH.

After appearing on Celebs Go Dating in 2018, she had a brief romance with car importer Laurence Hearn – before dumping him live on Loose Women. After splitting from Arg once and for all in 2020, Gemma and Rami secretly got back together, and in February 2024, he popped the question for a second time during a holiday in the Maldives.
