​​​​​DENVER (KDVR) — The Denver weather forecast remains beautiful through the weekend across all of Colorado. This quiet stretch looks to last statewide through at least Nov 15th.

Weather today: Sunny and mild

Warmer temperatures will rule the Front Range today as everyone climbs into the low to mid 60s under abundant sunshine paired up with light to calm winds.

Weather tonight: Clear and calm

Temperatures will again fall into the upper 30s, a slightly warmer start than we had on our Saturday morning. Skies will become completely clear as winds stay light.

Looking ahead: Quiet and mild

Temperatures will continue to say above average through next weekend with no signs of a pattern change to a cooler, stormier stretch until perhaps the very end of November.
