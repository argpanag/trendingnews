Novak Djokovic Faces Grim ATP Finals Verdict Before Paris Masters Campaign - AOL

TheTools · 03.11.2026 16:02 · source · permalink

Novak Djokovic faces a crucial test against Valentin Vacherot in the opening round of the Paris Masters, with his hopes of qualifying for the 2026 ATP Finals hanging in the balance.

The 39-year-old Serbian enters the final Masters 1000 tournament of the season outside the qualification spots and needs a strong run to keep his chances of reaching Turin alive. With a difficult draw ahead, there is growing uncertainty over whether Djokovic can secure a place at the year-end championships.

Former world No. 4 Greg Rusedski believes Djokovic still has a chance but considers the Serbian’s task extremely difficult.

Greg Rusedski Makes Honest Prediction on Novak Djokovic’s ATP Finals Chances

Speaking on Off Court with Greg Rusedski, the former British star explained why the Paris Masters could prove decisive for Djokovic. He also ruled out the possibility of the 24-time Grand Slam champion traveling to Turin as an alternate.

“He’s not going to go as an alternate [to the ATP Finals]. I’d be very surprised if Novak does that. But as I said in our previous podcast, it’s all going to come down to Paris.”

“The thing for Novak is Paris is usually historically a little bit quicker than most surfaces, and he’s going to try to up his game, but he’s in a brutal section.”

“I mean, the section Djokovic is in to try to make that… that’s what makes Paris so exciting because when the race is still on, and there are still places available, the last Masters series of the year usually decides who goes and who doesn’t.”

“And there are some packed sections in that draw. I can’t wait for it. I still think Novak has a shot, but you’re looking at it most likely going to come down to De Minaur and Jodar, who are going to be the ones to get the last slot in there.”

“But Djokovic, to me, has to win the title. But every time we say he can’t do something, he usually does. So don’t discount him.”

Rusedski’s comments underline the challenge awaiting Djokovic, whose opening match against Vacherot could prove crucial to his hopes of extending his season.

Where Novak Djokovic Stands in the ATP Finals Race

Djokovic currently sits 11th in the PIF ATP Live Race to Turin with 2,900 points, following his 2nd round defeat to Jan-Lennard Struff at the Swiss Indoors in Basel. With Jannik Sinner withdrawing from the ATP Finals due to injury, the qualification cutoff moves down to ninth place, currently occupied by Jodar with 3,194 points.

Frances Tiafoe sits eighth with 3,380 points, while Alex de Minaur is 10th with 3,160. Djokovic is therefore 294 points behind Jodar and 260 behind De Minaur, leaving him with considerable ground to make up in Paris.

The tournament offers the final opportunity to collect ranking points before the ATP Finals, meaning Djokovic needs a deep run to improve his position. Reaching the semifinals could keep his qualification hopes alive, although his chances will also depend on how his rivals perform.
