Pedestrian dies after crash on the M20 in Limerick

TheTools · 08.11.2026 22:26 · source · permalink

The road is currently closed with local diversions in place.

A pedestrian has died following a crash on the M20 Motorway in Limerick on Sunday evening.

At approximately 5.20pm, gardaí and emergency services were alerted to the collision involving a pedestrian and a car on the M20 northbound between Junction 3, Raheen, and Junction 2, Dooradoyle.

The pedestrian was pronounced deceased at the scene.

No other injuries were reported.

The local coroner has been notified, and an autopsy will be arranged.

The road remains closed on Sunday night as Garda Forensic Collision Investigators carry out a technical examination and local diversions are in place.

Gardaí are appealing for any witnesses to come forward.

Any road users who may have camera footage, including dash-cam, and were on the M20 between Junctions 3 and 2 from 5pm to 5.30pm today, Sunday 8th November 2026, are asked to make this footage available to gardaí.

Anyone with any information is asked to contact Henry Street Garda Station on 061 212400, the Garda Confidential Line on 1800 666 111, or any Garda station.

Earlier on Sunday, six teenage pedestrians were hospitalised after being struck by a car in Dublin. The driver, a woman in her 40s, was also taken to hospital. 

It is understood the car mounted the curb before colliding with the youngsters, with locals saying it was believed the driver may have suffered a medical episode prior to the crash.
