Ligue 1 Resumes With Monaco Leading the Sixth Round

TheTools · 09.10.2026 19:47 · source · permalink

France's Ligue 1 returns after the international break with AS Monaco defending first place, Olympique Lyonnais and Paris FC pursuing the leaders, and Paris Saint-Germain attempting to climb the standings. The sixth round begins Friday, October 9, 2026, with Lens hosting Lyon at 8:45 p.m., while the weekend schedule features nine league matches broadcast live on Ligue 1+, according to the league and French sports media.

Monaco enters the round unbeaten after five matches, with four wins and one draw, and leads the standings. The club hosts Toulouse on Saturday, October 10, at 8:45 p.m., aiming to maintain its advantage. Toulouse sits 13th and arrives after earning its first win of the season against Le Havre, a 3-2 result reported by 20 Minutes.

Lyon and Paris FC are also unbeaten and trail Monaco by two points, according to 20 Minutes. Lyon, which ranks second, travels to Lens, while Paris FC visits Lorient, currently 12th. Lens, coached by Yannick Cahuzac, is seeking to improve its position after losing to Monaco in the previous round.

Statistics compiled by Opta highlight Lyon's international squad composition. The club has fielded players representing 19 different nationalities in Ligue 1 this season, more than any other team across Europe's top five leagues, according to L'Équipe.

Lille, fourth with 10 points, will host bottom-placed Le Havre on Saturday at 5:15 p.m. The match comes after Lille's 2-1 defeat at Nice and ahead of its upcoming Champions League meeting with Arsenal. Opta data cited by L'Équipe shows Lille conceded 21 league goals in 2026, with only Arsenal recording a better defensive figure among teams in Europe's top five leagues over the same period, at 19.

Rennes, fifth with 10 points, welcomes Auxerre, which occupies 10th place. Other Saturday fixtures include Paris Saint-Germain against Le Mans at 8:45 p.m. and Brest against Angers. PSG sits sixth with eight points and is looking to continue its recovery in the standings. Ferran Torres will be unavailable after suffering an injury while on international duty, according to 20 Minutes.

Opta's figures also point to a recent decline in PSG's home results. The Paris club has lost three of its last seven Ligue 1 matches at the Parc des Princes, matching the number of home defeats it suffered across its previous 45 league games. PSG trails Monaco by five points, according to L'Équipe.

Marseille faces Troyes on Sunday, October 11, at 8:45 p.m. as it prepares for its upcoming meeting with Manchester City. According to Opta statistics reported by L'Équipe, Marseille has not led for a single minute in its away league matches this season. Troyes, meanwhile, has gone 19 consecutive home matches without a Ligue 1 victory, the longest such run in the competition's history.

Sunday's schedule also includes Nice against Strasbourg at 3 p.m. and Rennes against Auxerre at 5:15 p.m. Nice enters the round after defeating Lille before the international break, while Strasbourg travels to the south of France seeking points against a side ranked 14th, according to 20 Minutes.

Ligue 1+ will carry all nine matches and has planned additional coverage around several clubs. The programming includes interviews with coaches and players, behind-the-scenes footage from Paris Saint-Germain's training academy, and coverage of Troyes' preparations for Marseille. The broadcaster also announced a monthly awards feature recognizing outstanding goals, saves, and individual skills, with the first winners scheduled to be revealed during the weekend.
