LeBron James (back) ruled out Saturday

TheTools · 24.10.2026 17:04 · source · permalink

by Chris Schommer | 76ers Correspondent | Sat, Oct 24th 2026, 1:04pm EDT

LeBron James (back) has been ruled out for the Philadelphia 76ers Saturday according to Adam Aaronson. (Adam Aaronson on Twitter)

Fantasy Impact:

James will sit out the first leg of a back-to-back for head coach Nick Nurse, with Kentavious Caldwell-Pope in line to start against the Bucks Saturday night.

LeBron James has been downgraded to OUT for Sixers-Bucks tonight.
