Bosnia-Herzegovina vs Poland Preview

Bosnia-Herzegovina meet Poland at Bilino Polje Stadium in Zenica, with kick-off set for 11:45 on Monday 5 October.

Bosnia-Herzegovina started their Nations League campaign with a goalless draw away to tonight's opponent Poland before securing a 4-2 victory in Romania. In their last outing, Sergej Barbarez's side drew 1-1 at home to Sweden, having 53% possession and registering two shots on target, with Kerim Alajbegovic scoring a late equaliser.

After a 0-0 home draw against Bosnia-Herzegovina in their Nations League opener, Poland suffered a 3-1 defeat away to Sweden. The Eagles then responded with a dominant 6-0 home victory over Romania last time out, enjoying 68% possession and registering nine shots on target against a Romania side who had Radu Dragusin sent off in the 22nd minute.

Robert Lewandowski led the way with a hat-trick, while Piotr Zielinski and Jan Bednarek also found the net, with Poland also benefiting from a Virgil Ghita own goal.
