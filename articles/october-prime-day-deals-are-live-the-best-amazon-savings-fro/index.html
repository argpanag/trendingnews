✔ What to wait on this Prime Day (and how we pick the best deals)

Our writers and editors are deal-hunting experts who verify price drops, star ratings and more to help you save money and shop with confidence. Some deals are sneaky: starting prices can be inflated to make discounts look more impressive, and some items are cheaper elsewhere (or we expect a bigger price drop later this year for Black Friday). So we're here to do the hard work for you. Want to know what to skip this Prime Day? Here's what our experts recommend waiting to buy until later this year:

• High-end electronics: While we've found some strong tech deals, especially on Amazon devices, mid-range brands and smaller accessories like Apple AirTags, the biggest markdowns on higher-priced tech typically come during Cyber Monday, so it's probably worth waiting a little longer.
• Large home appliances: While there are some very worthwhile deals on vacuums and small kitchen appliances, we'll see the deepest discounts on bigger home appliances, like refrigerators and washing machines, come Black Friday.
• Toys: Aside from a few smart seasonal buys, like this sale on Lego advent calendars (grab one now, because they're unlikely to be discounted in November when everyone else is shopping for them), we'd skip other toys, as Black Friday is a better time to snag gifts.
