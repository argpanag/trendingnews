Aston Villa - Building on recent wins

Villa will be keen to turn their first league win into a more consistent run. We will now take a closer look at their recent form and Aston Villa vs. Brentford line-ups.

Recent form

Aston Villa sit 16th in the Premier League with four points from five matches. After opening the campaign with three defeats and a draw, Unai Emery’s side finally claimed their first league win by beating Tottenham 3-2 away from home before the international break.

Their home form remains a concern, with defeats against Arsenal and Nottingham Forest leaving Villa without a point at Villa Park this season. They have scored just one goal in those two fixtures, while conceding three.

Midfielder Johan Manzambi made an immediate impact on his first Premier League start for Villa, scoring and assisting against Tottenham.

However, Emery has several injury concerns for Aston Villa vs. Brentford, with Amadou Onana and Ian Maatsen ruled out, while Pau Torres, Matty Cash, Alysson and Marco Bizot are doubtful.

Brentford - Unbeaten and full of confidence

Brentford have made a strong start under Keith Andrews and will be confident of extending their run against Villa. Their recent results suggest they have the attacking quality to trouble the hosts again.

Recent form

Brentford are fourth in the Premier League with nine points from five matches, recording two wins and three draws. They opened the campaign with a 3-0 victory over Tottenham and followed it up with another convincing 3-0 win against Chelsea before the international break.

The Bees have also drawn with Leeds, Sunderland and Bournemouth, showing they can remain competitive in different types of matches. However, their away form remains a concern, as they have yet to win on the road in the league this season.

Forward Kevin Schade has been a key attacking threat, scoring three league goals so far.

Brentford will be without Mikkel Damsgaard, who sustained an ankle injury on international duty, while Dango Ouattara is doubtful. Mathias Jensen, Joshua Dasilva, Antoni Milambo, Nathan Collins and Sepp van den Berg will also be assessed before kick-off.

Aston Villa’s first league win should provide a confidence boost, but their home form remains a concern. Brentford have made a more convincing start, which suggests a decisive result one way or the other is a strong possibility.

Brentford have scored in every league game this season, while Villa’s attack has recently come alive. With both sides confident going forward, there is a good chance of seeing at least one goal before the break.

Considering both teams’ momentum, we expect a winner and over 2.5 goals, anticipating an entertaining and open contest at Villa Park.
