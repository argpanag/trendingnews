I'm modding GTA 5 every day in a quest to either transform it into a PC version of GTA 6 or break it entirely. Come back tomorrow to find out what new horrible thing I've done to Rockstar's baby, or check out what I got up to earlier.

Right, I've had it. I have been rolled out like bread dough by errant minivans one too many times, and I've decided to do something about it. I am abolishing cars (within a very specific radius, sometimes).

Cycletopia – Replace cars with bicycles is a Grand Theft Auto 5 mod that does the thing it says in its title, which I wrote just there. All cars within a certain radius (I never figured out what radius, exactly, but there was definitely one involved) will, at the touch of a button, transmogrify into bicycles.

They will also, quite often, take flight, owing to some confusion regarding the laws of physics, leading to a hundred different iterations of that one bit from ET.

They are also all ridden by Franklin, since the button to turn on Bike World is the same button that turns the world's pedestrians into carbon copies of your player character. You'll tap the button and, nary moments later, hear "Aw shit!" as your doppelganger gaily endos down the freeway.

It is a nicer, quieter city. Gone are the vrrms of engines and the kablooeys of explosions that now make up the aural world of my modded Los Santos. In their place is the whirr of pedals and the occasional bell, with some allowances having to be made for Franklin's commentary, which is frequent and ribald. I hope this is what GTA 6 is like. Probably it will be.
