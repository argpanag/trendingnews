Stuttgart Coach Calls Bilal El Khannouss’ Goal ‘World-Class’

TheTools · 05.09.2026 18:03 · source · permalink

Rabat – Stuttgart coach Sebastian Hoeneß has praised Moroccan international Bilal El Khannouss for his impressive performance in the club’s 4-1 victory over Cologne in the second round of the Bundesliga.

El Khannouss played a key role in Stuttgart’s comeback victory, scoring a stunning goal and contributing to another as the German side secured its first win of the new Bundesliga season.

Speaking to Sky Sport, Hoeness highlighted the Moroccan midfielder’s influence on the game and his role in turning the match in Stuttgart’s favor.

“He played a great game, there is no discussion about that. He played a decisive role in turning the game in our favor,” the coach said.

Hoeness also singled out El Khannouss’ goal for particular praise, describing the effort as an exceptional piece of finishing.

“The goal was world-class. Not many players score goals like that,” he added.

El Khannouss put Stuttgart ahead with an impressive individual effort before playing a part in the team’s second goal. His contribution helped Stuttgart respond strongly after suffering a heavy defeat to Bayern Munich in the opening round.

The performance also provided a strong response from the Moroccan midfielder after a summer dominated by transfer speculation surrounding his future.

Several clubs had reportedly shown interest in El Khannouss during the transfer window, but the 21-year-old ultimately remained at Stuttgart.

His latest display could further reinforce his importance to the German club as he continues to establish himself as one of Morocco’s most promising midfielders in European football.
