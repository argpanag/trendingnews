ATP Hangzhou Quarterfinal Predictions Including Daniil Medvedev vs Coleman Wong - Last Word On Tennis

TheTools · 26.09.2026 18:25 · source · permalink

It is quarterfinal day at ATP Hangzhou, as eight players look to get their 2026 Asian hard-court swing off to the perfect start at the ATP Hangzhou Open. We have a former World No. 1 in action, along with a local player and several other well-known names. As always, we here at LWOT will be predicting every match for you, but who do you think will make it to the semifinals?

ATP Hangzhou Quarterfinal Predictions

Fabian Marozsan vs Kyrian Jacquet

Head-to-head: First meeting

This matchup sees a complete contrast in styles, as the all-court variety and craft of Fabian Marozsan take on the counterpunching skills of Kyrian Jacquet. Although Jacquet has very limited experience on the ATP Tour, his consistent baseline game should be able to trouble Marozsan, a player who relies more on singular moments of magic than being consistent from the back of the court. I expect a competitive match here, but ultimately, Marozsan’s experience at this level, along with his higher ceiling, makes me give him the edge in this match.

Prediction: Marozsan in 3

Roman Safiullin vs Yunchaokete Bu

Head-to-head: First meeting

Despite being ranked outside the top 100, Yunchaokete Bu showed at the US Open that when he is dialled in, he can outhit some of the best players in the world. Now he takes on Roman Safiullin, a player who is also outside the top 100 currently but has the firepower to always be a tough out on hard courts and faster surfaces. Both players’ serves will be key here, as they will rely on winning large quantities of their points on serve without much trouble. While Safiullin has the greater redlining ability of the two, I trust Bu’s return game and nerve management more than the Russian, which in a match that should be close could prove to be the difference.

Prediction: Bu in 3

Hugo Gaston vs Andrey Rublev

Head-to-head: Gaston 0-1 Rublev

After having several physical issues to start the year, Hugo Gaston has done well to make the quarterfinals here, using his baseline game to great effect. Now he takes on Andrey Rublev, a player who is not quite what he was a couple of years ago but still has the firepower off his forehand side to trouble many players. This match will be played from the back of the court, as Gaston will look to target Rublev’s backhand and second serve, while the Russian will want to take the initiative with his heavy forehand. Rublev played a solid match to start his tournament here, and if he brings the same intensity, it should be too much for Gaston.

Prediction: Rublev in 2

Daniil Medvedev vs Coleman Wong

Head-to-head: First meeting

It has been yet another strange season for Daniil Medvedev, with a few highs and very low lows throughout the year, as he looks to end his year on a high after his year at the Majors ended disappointingly. In this ATP Hangzhou match against Coleman Wong, all will be determined by how well the Russian can return Wong’s serve, as from the baseline, Medvedev should still be too much for his opponent. While I do expect Wong to serve well here for stretches, Medvedev’s baseline game and own serving prowess should just be too much of a challenge for Wong to overcome to win this match.

Prediction: Medvedev in 2
