What to watch this weekend in international sports | CBC Sports

TheTools · 19.09.2026 09:17 · source · permalink

Canada goes for Olympic volleyball spot, wheelchair hoops world title and more

A berth in the 2028 Olympic men's volleyball tournament is up for grabs in Moncton, where Canada is among four teams vying to represent the region encompassing North and Central America and the Caribbean at the Los Angeles Games.

The single-elimination bracket gets underway with Canada facing Mexico today at 3:30 p.m. ET. Cuba plays the Dominican Republic at 6 p.m. ET, and the winners square off for the Olympic spot on Saturday at 6 p.m. ET. Watch all of the matches live on CBC Gem and the CBC Sports website.

Canada, at No. 13 in the world, is the highest-ranked of the four teams. Cuba is next at No. 16, followed by No. 23 Mexico and the 45th-ranked Dominican. Last weekend, the Canadians beat Mexico in straight sets in the group stage of their regional championship tournament on the same court in Moncton, then did the same to Cuba in the semifinals before losing the final to the United States.

The U.S. did not continue on to the Olympic qualifier because it receives an automatic berth in the L.A. Games as the host country. The Americans, Canada and Cuba also qualified for next year's men's Volleyball World Cup.

Wheelchair basketball: Canada goes for world title

After dropping 49 points on Japan in the quarterfinals, Kady Dandeneau had 35 points, 11 rebounds and six assists last night to lead the Canadian women's wheelchair basketball team to a 85-73 upset of the reigning world and Paralympic champion Netherlands in the semifinals of the world championship in Ottawa.

Arinn Young added 34 points, 12 rebounds and 13 assists for the Canadians, who will face the United States in the gold-medal game on Saturday at 2:30 p.m. ET on CBC Gem and the CBC Sports website. The Americans lost the 2024 Paralympic final to the Dutch.

Last night's win guaranteed Canada its first medal at the worlds since the women won gold in 2014 in Toronto. Canada finished fourth in both the men's and women's tournaments at the 2024 Paralympic Games and has not won a Paralympic medal since the men struck gold in 2012.

The Canadian men's team was eliminated in the round of 16 earlier this week by Poland. It's Australia vs. Italy in the men's gold-medal game on Saturday at 4:45 p.m. ET.

Tennis: Canada counting on Felix in Davis Cup qualifier

With a spot in the Davis Cup Finals on the line, Canada will lean heavily on its top player as it takes on France in a second-round qualifier in Quebec City.

The best-of-five contest begins this evening with a pair of singles matches, including Canada's fifth-ranked Felix Auger-Aliassime vs. No. 49 Quentin Halys. Auger-Aliassime will also team with Duncan Chan for the doubles match on Saturday afternoon before facing 32nd-ranked Arthur Rinderknech in another singles matchup immediately after that. Canada's other singles player is 205th-ranked Liam Draxl, who would face Halys in the fifth and deciding match if necessary. You can watch on TSN.

Canada is without two of its best players as 50th-ranked Denis Shapovalov opted out for undisclosed reasons and No. 125 Gabriel Diallo is sidelined with a leg injury. France is missing No. 11 Arthur Fils.

Auger-Aliassime led Canada to its only Davis Cup title in 2022 and owns a lifetime record of 13-4, including 10-2 in singles. He did not play last year as Canada fell to Hungary in the first round of qualifiers. The 26-year-old withdrew before his opening match at last month’s National Bank Open in his hometown of Montreal due to a back injury, then lost in the third round in Cincinnati before exiting in the second round of the U.S. Open.

The eight-team Davis Cup Finals take place in late November in Italy.

Other stuff to watch:

* The Canadian women's rugby team visits World Cup champion England on Saturday at 10 a.m. ET in Exeter. It's their first meeting since the Red Roses defeated Canada 33-13 in last year's World Cup final in front of a record crowd of 82,000 in London. You can watch the match on TSN.

* The road cycling world championships begin Sunday in Montreal with the women's individual time trial at 9 a.m. ET and the men's time trial at 12:45 p.m. ET on CBC Gem and the CBC Sports website. Canada's Derek Gee-West, who finished fifth overall in the prestigious Giro d'Italia this year, is in the men's event. Magdeleine Vallières Mill will defend her world title in the women's road race next week.

* Sixteen Canadians are set to compete in the World Athletics Road Running Championships in Copenhagen, including sisters Gabriela DeBues-Stafford and Lucia Stafford in the women's mile on Saturday. National marathon record holders Natasha Wodak and Cam Levins and Boston Marathon standout Rory Linkletter will race the half on Sunday. Norwegian track star Jakob Ingebrigtsen, the reigning Olympic 5,000m champion, will face France's Jimmy Gressier, the 10,000m world champ, in the men's 5K on Saturday.
