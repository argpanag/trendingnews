Downdetector recorded more than 17,000 reports for Facebook and more than 5,000 for Instagram around 9:21 p.m. New York time. Reports fell substantially about two hours later. Meta had not publicly commented on the incident in the source material.

The market response instead points to growing attention around Muse, Meta's personal AI agent. The tool can perform tasks such as opening apps, completing forms and sending emails. The source said Muse recently reached No. 1 on Apple's U.S. App Store, putting its early user adoption in focus.

Analysts cited by TipRanks see a potential opportunity in the emerging consumer AI agent market, while also pointing to improving confidence in Meta's returns on AI investment.

Wall Street's consensus remains Strong Buy, based on 38 Buy ratings and six Holds over the past three months. The average price target of $759.43 implies about 14% upside from the cited share price.

The next test will be whether Muse can sustain user engagement and translate adoption into measurable revenue or returns as Meta continues investing in AI.
