Josh Allen had a classic Josh Allen reaction at podium post-Chargers win

TheTools · 28.09.2026 21:41 · source · permalink

Buffalo Bills quarterback Josh Allen had reason to both smile and not smile after Week 3.

On Sunday, the Bills (3-0) beat the Los Angeles Chargers (0-3) in a 24-16 game which saw the offense stuck in the mud. Allen is still likely the NFL MVP favorite in 2026, but he did have poor moments against the Bolts.

Allen had his first two interceptions of the season and added a fumble. Instead, this one saw the Bills' QB lifted up by 154 rushing yards from James Cook and some big plays on defense from the likes of Greg Rousseau (2 sacks) and safety CJ Gardner-Johnson (interception).

When it was all said and done, Allen took the podium for his weekly post-game press conference. With a sense of relief off his shoulders after the win, Allen put that exact feeling on display in front of the cameras.

As the quarterback walked up to the podium, he looked at the stat sheet there waiting for him. Presumably looking at his own numbers, Allen made a funny cringe face which cause a chuckle.
