TV Host and Actress Hasna Cabral Passes Away - When In Manila

TheTools · 25.11.2026 10:37 · source · permalink

TV actress and host Hasna Cabral has passed away.

Photo: Hasna Cabral/Instagram

On November 25, the family officially confirmed the news of her untimely death. She died due to a “stroke attack that affected brain activity.”

According to the family, she was immediately buried according to Islamic rites.

“We understand the eagerness to see her last moments. And we truly appreciate that. However, we ask for your understanding that she was laid to rest according to our faith,” it said.

“Please keep her memory alive by keeping yourselves in always perfect condition. Rest, in behalf of the rest she deserved, but couldn’t afford herself. Enjoy your life as she would. And express your love to your loved ones in every chance possible,” it added.

May she rest in peace.
