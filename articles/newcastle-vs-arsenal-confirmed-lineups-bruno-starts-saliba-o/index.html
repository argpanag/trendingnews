Newcastle vs Arsenal Confirmed Lineups: Bruno Starts, Saliba on the Bench

TheTools · 21.11.2026 16:44 · source · permalink

Bruno Guimarães starts against his old club and William Saliba is on the Arsenal bench, while Newcastle bring in Lewis Miley and Joe Willock for the 5.30pm kick-off at St James' Park.

Bruno Guimarães starts against his old club at St James’ Park, and William Saliba, who hasn’t played this season, is on the Arsenal bench. Those are the headlines from the Newcastle vs Arsenal confirmed lineups for tonight’s 5.30pm kick-off, and the selection that should decide the game is in Newcastle’s midfield: Matthias Jaissle has handed the job of stopping Declan Rice, Martin Ødegaard and Bruno to an 18-year-old and a 20-year-old.

Earlier coverage: the Newcastle vs Arsenal predicted lineups and team news (21 November), the Tzolis FPL leak story (20 November), Zubimendi’s hamstring injury (20 November), Saliba’s fitness update (20 November) and Joelinton’s injury update (19 November).

Newcastle vs Arsenal confirmed lineups

Newcastle (4-2-3-1): Horníček; Dedić, Thiaw, Burn (c), Hall; Miley, Steur; Murphy, Willock, Barnes; Osula.
Subs: Pope, Livramento, Botman, Schär, Nico González, Wissa, Elanga, Fernandez-Pardo, Ramsey.

Arsenal (4-3-3): Raya; Timber, Konsa, Gabriel, Calafiori; Ødegaard, Guimarães, Rice; Madueke, Havertz, Eze.
Subs: Arrizabalaga, Saliba, Mosquera, White, Hincapié, Gyökeres, Tzolis, Merino, Lewis-Skelly.

Kick-off is 5.30pm UK time, live on Sky Sports Main Event and Sky Sports Premier League. Chris Kavanagh is the referee.

Miley and Willock in for Newcastle

Jaissle has made two changes from the side that started the 1-1 draw at Fulham before the international break. Lewis Miley and Joe Willock come in, Nico González and Yoane Wissa drop to the bench. Wissa’s demotion leaves William Osula as the lone striker, with Willock in the number 10 role behind him.

Willock starts against the club that raised him. He came through the Hale End academy and played 78 times for them, scoring 11, before joining Newcastle, initially on loan, in February 2021. Tonight is his 175th Newcastle appearance and his 100th Premier League start for the club. Harvey Barnes needs one more goal for 100 in his senior career.

The bigger call is Miley next to Sean Steur. Steur is 18, Miley is 20, and against them sit Rice, Bruno and Ødegaard. Leaving Nico González on the bench looks like Jaissle picking legs to press over a holding player who can keep the ball, and it’s a gamble. If Arsenal get Ødegaard on the half-turn between the lines, Newcastle’s back four will be defending a lot of the game in their own box. Newcastle’s best hope is Willock pressing Rice and not letting him turn.

Joelinton misses out with the injury that has kept him out since the 1-0 home defeat by Everton at the start of the month. “We will get feedback from the specialists and we will find out how long Joe will be out,” Jaissle said on Thursday. Jacob Ramsey is back from suspension and on the bench, and so is Tino Livramento.

Bruno and Eze start, Saliba on the bench

Arteta has made one change from the 1-0 win over Hull a fortnight ago, with Eberechi Eze coming in for Christos Tzolis in the front three. Bruno starts despite a short turnaround after playing for Brazil in the international break. He scored 31 goals and made 31 assists in 195 appearances for Newcastle before his £75m move in August, and tonight he’s back at St James’ Park in Arsenal colours.

Saliba’s return to the squad is a big lift. He hasn’t played this season because of a back injury, so Ezri Konsa partners Gabriel, with Ben White also among the substitutes. Bukayo Saka (thigh) and Martín Zubimendi (hamstring) are injured and Max Dowman is suspended.

Arsenal have won nine of their 10 league games and conceded five goals. A win tonight would make them only the fifth side in Premier League history to win 10 of their first 11 matches and would put them eight points clear of Brighton, who play Hull tomorrow. Newcastle have taken five points from five home games under Jaissle, and on these teams Arsenal should be too strong for them.
