WEATHER: Cape Town faces rain and strong winds on Tuesday as Gauteng heats up

TheTools · 15.09.2026 04:08 · source · permalink

Kabous Le Roux

Cape Town faces rain and strong winds on Tuesday, while Johannesburg and Pretoria can expect warm conditions, showers and a cooler night.

  • Cape Town weather
  • Weather warning

Johannesburg, Pretoria, and Cape Town weather forecast for Tuesday, 15 September: The Mother City faces rain and strong winds, while Gauteng heats up with showers possible across the province. (Picture credit: paintingcat/123rf)

Cape Town is in for a cloudy, increasingly windy Tuesday, with rain expected during the day before conditions turn even wetter later in the evening, according to AccuWeather.

The Mother City will reach a high of 17°C. Rain is forecast during the day, with a 61% chance of precipitation and winds from the north-northwest reaching 37 km/h, with gusts of up to 65 km/h.

Cloud cover is expected to reach 94%, while the forecast puts rainfall at 4.2mm during the day.

Cape Town could see heavier rain overnight

Conditions will remain unsettled into Tuesday night, with temperatures dropping to a low of 12°C.

A steady breeze is expected during the evening, followed by occasional rain and a thunderstorm late. The chance of rain rises to 80%, with 14.6mm of rain forecast and wind from the west at 24 km/h.

Gusts could reach 54 km/h, while cloud cover is expected to reach 99%.

Johannesburg: Warm with morning showers

Johannesburg will be considerably warmer, reaching a high of 23°C on Tuesday.

The forecast calls for a couple of morning showers, followed by some clouds and then sunshine. There is a 65% chance of precipitation, with 1.7mm of rain forecast.

Winds from the northwest will reach 17 km/h, with gusts of up to 46 km/h.

The UV Index is expected to reach 9, classified as very high.

By night, Johannesburg will be clear and much cooler, with a low of 10°C. The chance of rain drops to just 2%, with no thunderstorms expected.

Pretoria: Hotter, with an afternoon shower possible

Pretoria will see the highest daytime temperature of the three cities, reaching 27°C.

The forecast calls for hazy sunshine with a passing shower in the afternoon. There is a 58% chance of precipitation, although only 0.9mm of rain is forecast.

Winds will come from the north-northwest at 15 km/h, with gusts reaching 39 km/h. The UV Index is again expected to be 9, or very high.

Tuesday night will be mostly cloudy in the evening with showers, then mainly clear late. The temperature will fall to 12°C, with a 70% chance of precipitation and 1.2mm of rain forecast.
