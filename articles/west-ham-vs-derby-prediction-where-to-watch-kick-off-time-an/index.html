West Ham vs Derby - Prediction, where to watch, kick-off time and line-ups 05-09-2026

West Ham and Derby meet in round 5 of the Championship. The match takes place Saturday 05 September 2026 at 14:00 (UK time), at the London Stadium in London.

Both sides have had a shaky start to the season and are in desperate need of a win to climb the table. The hosts are looking to bounce back from recent disappointments in front of their fans, while Derby are still searching for their first victory in the Championship. Our West Ham vs. Derby prediction anticipates a tense match.

Discover what our experts are predicting for today"s encounter.

West Ham vs. Derby - Head-to-head record

These two teams have not crossed paths very often in recent years, making each encounter a noteworthy event. Their meetings have been scattered across different competitions, including the Championship and the FA Cup, which adds a layer of unpredictability whenever they face off.

Looking at their last three matches, West Ham holds a slight advantage. The Hammers have secured two victories compared to Derby’s one, with no draws between them in this recent period. The most recent clash in the West Ham vs. Derby fixture saw the London side win 2-0 in an FA Cup tie back in early 2023.

This pattern suggests that the games between these sides are often decisive, with a clear winner emerging. While the overall head-to-head history is limited, the most recent results give West Ham a psychological edge coming into this match, as they came out on top in the last encounter.

West Ham - Struggling for home form

West Ham have had a challenging return to the Championship and are still searching for their first league victory. Here, we delve into the Hammers’ current situation, examining their recent performances and what to expect from them in this home fixture. We will also look at the probable West Ham vs. Derby line-ups.

Recent form

West Ham United sit 19th in the Championship with just two points from three games, recording two draws and one loss. In contrast, they have shown their attacking strength in the League Cup with solid wins against Southampton and Portsmouth.

Their home form in the league is a concern, as their only match at the London Stadium resulted in a 1-0 defeat to Charlton. The Hammers are under pressure to secure a positive result and climb the table.

West Ham United has a fully fit squad for this encounter. Captain Jarrod Bowen is expected to provide the creative spark needed to break down defences. This blend of inconsistent league results and cup success makes them an unpredictable side.

Derby - Still searching for a win

Let’s take a look at Derby’s situation. We will analyse the team’s current form, recent results, and key players for their trip to London.

Recent form

Derby County currently sits 21st in the Championship table, having secured just one point from their opening three matches. They remain winless, holding a record of one draw and two losses. A significant concern is their defence, which has conceded seven goals in the early season, indicating a vulnerability opponents have exploited.

Their away form has also been shaky, marked by a 2-1 Championship defeat to Charlton in their only away fixture. An early League Cup exit against Lincoln further highlights their struggle to build positive momentum. This match at the London Stadium will truly test their resilience.

Positively, Derby County arrives in London with a full, injury-free squad for this fixture. The availability of all players is a boost, especially with midfielder Bobby Clark in good form. Clark has been a standout performer, contributing a goal and an assist, and will be vital to their attacking play.

Predictions for West Ham vs. Derby - A Hammers Victory

West Ham are desperate for a win at the London Stadium, especially against a Derby side that has conceded seven goals in three games. With both teams looking to make a statement early on, there is a good chance of at least one goal in the first half.

The Hammers are expected to control the game and put Derby"s shaky defence under constant pressure. This attacking intent should lead to more set-piece opportunities, making West Ham likely to win the corner count during the match.
