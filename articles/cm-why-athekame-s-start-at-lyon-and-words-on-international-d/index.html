CM: Why Athekame's start at Lyon and words on international duty are pleasing for Milan

TheTools · 25.09.2026 17:30 · source · permalink

Zachary Athekame is shining for Lyon in Ligue 1, under the orders of former AC Milan coach Paulo Fonseca.

Athekame has also earned a call-up to the Switzerland senior national team, where he will be available during this break for the various UEFA Nations League matches that Murat Yakin’s team will face.

The 21-year-old was loaned by Milan to Lyon during the last summer transfer window (without any option, obligations or other possible buy-out options). His growth so far early this season is something that the management are delighted to see, as Calciomercato.com write.

Zach on the attack

Athekame has been one of the most important weapons at Fonseca’s disposal, with two goals and two assists in the first six games of the season across Ligue 1 and the qualifiers for the UEFA Champions League.

He has nailed down a starting spot already, which is music to Milan’s ears. Speaking of the Rossoneri, they will welcome the defender back next July 1st. Their idea was for him to spend one year in a top-level, before returning to Milanello and entering the rotations.

The fact it was a dry loan was a precise choice that certifies the trust of the entire Milan environment in him. Athekame made no secret of his desire to return to the Rossoneri in an interview with RSI on international duty.

“I rate my first season with Milan positively. I enjoyed it; it was a new, more tactical league. Then there was the change on the bench. I spoke with the new manager and decided to change shirts,” he said.

“I enjoyed my time in Milan, I’m a Milan player. Now I’m in Lyon, but I’d like to return.” It is a clear signal of the mutual intentions between the parties.
