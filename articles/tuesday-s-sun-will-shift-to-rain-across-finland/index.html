Tuesday's sun will shift to rain across Finland

TheTools · 15.09.2026 05:05 · source · permalink

Tuesday will still be dry and sunny in many areas, but a rain system is due to move across the country on Wednesday.

  • Yle News

Finland is due for rain on Wednesday, according to Yle meteorologist Matti Huutonen.

He said Tuesday will be relatively dry and sunny in many parts of the country, but clouds will spread in the west over the course of the day, and by evening western areas could start seeing showers.

The precipitation system is expected to become more widespread in northern and western areas overnight.

Tuesday's daytime high temperatures will range between 10 degrees Celsius in the north and 17 degrees in the south.

However, things are set to be slightly cooler on Wednesday — with temperatures below 10 degrees in the north and around 15 degrees in the south.

Southerly winds will strengthen to moderate when the rain falls, while brisk winds will prevail in coastal areas.

The weather will stay unsettled through the end of the week. Scattered rain will move across Finland from southwest to northeast, according to the Yle forecast.
