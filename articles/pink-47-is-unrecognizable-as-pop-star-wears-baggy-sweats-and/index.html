Recent controversy with Ed Sheeran and Macklemore

Pink faced public backlash in September after she re-shared a post related to Macklemore’s removal from Ed Sheeran’s Loop Tour.

The singer reposted a meme that stood against Macklemore, who was kicked off Sheeran’s tour after he voiced his support for Palestine. The “Thrift Shop” rapper said, “Free Palestine” at a show in New Jersey and delivered a political speech. He also performed his song “HIND’S HALL.”

She later wrote a follow-up message of her own.

A 1,300-word Instagram post

Pink scribed a 1,300-word caption for an Instagram post in which she sought to explain herself and clarify her feelings about the Sheeran-Macklemore conflict.

“A repost isn’t a statement, and a meme isn’t a spine,” she wrote, adding that she would “rather be disagreed with for what I actually said than hated for what anybody else wrote, so let me say it.”

She penned a lengthy message, including saying that she’s “been portrayed as the villain before. I can take it. I just won’t take it quietly, and I’m not going to teach my kids to.”

She added, “I have never asked for another artist to be silenced and I’m not asking now. I don’t need anyone fired, and I don’t need a refund.”

Petition calling for her removal as a UNICEF ambassador

In her lengthy Instagram post, Pink referenced her role as an ambassador for UNICEF.

“I’ve spent a decade as a UNICEF ambassador for kids who didn’t choose the country or the war they were born into, and I have grieved every one of them, on both sides of that fence. I didn’t do it from behind a screen; I went to those children and held them,” she wrote.

A Change.org petition circulated calling for her to be removed as an ambassador because her comments were “not just unfortunate but also incompatible with the role of a UNICEF Ambassador.

"UNICEF's mission is to uphold the rights and dignity of all children, regardless of political affiliations or geographic boundaries. We rely on ambassadors to be torchbearers of these principles, fostering inclusive dialogue and meaningful support."

The petition continues, urging UNICEF to “remove Pink as an ambassador.” It had brought in over 162,000 signatures by September 22.

September NYC sighting

Pink was seen out and about in New York City on Tuesday, September 15. She wore a black tracksuit and carried a takeout bag from the smoothie and açaí bowl shop Playa Bowls. She also had a white purse slung over her shoulder. She wore gold earrings and had a pair of glasses perched upon her head.

Her late September sighting marked one of the first times Pink was spotted following her controversial response to Macklemore’s removal from the Loop Tour.

Pink’s dog Habañero Mountain Guy Kadashi Hart

Pink has two dogs: Habañero Mountain Guy Kadashi Hart and Graham Cracker.

She adopted Habañero Mountain Guy Kadashi Hart in March 2021 from The Labelle Foundation.

"Thank you to the @labellefdn for helping us rescue our newest family member, Habañero Mountain Guy Kadashi Hart. We love the work that you do #AdoptDontShop," she wrote on Twitter at the time, adding a tag for the animal rescue organization The Labelle Foundation.

Pink's latest adoption

Pink rescued Graham Cracker in October 2024 after fostering him.

Pink began her time with Graham Cracker by fostering him while she was on tour. She then admitted to being a “foster fail” as she ended up adopting him.

“It’s official … I am a foster fail! We are keeping Graham Cracker! (Never been happier to fail),” she wrote on Instagram.

“Thank you to @thebond_between for not only bringing him into our lives, but for all the tireless hard work they do to help rehome all their animals,” she added before encouraging her followers to donate to the organization.

Mexico concert cancellations

Pink was set to play two concerts at Mexico City’s GNP Seguros on April 26 and 27 but recently canceled the performances.

Ocesa, the promoter for the show, explained on social media that the two concerns were canceled because of “complications arising from an unforeseen circumstance that affects the logistics of the event.”

They added that the cancellations were “beyond the control of the artist, the promoter, and the venue.”

”We understand how much fans were looking forward to these concerts, and we deeply appreciate their understanding,” continues the statement.
