Street Fighter First Reviews: Colorful, Weird, Noisy Nonsense Affectionately Made for Fans

TheTools · 18.10.2026 00:45 · source · permalink

News

Critics say it boasts plenty of Easter eggs, breathtaking animated sequences, and lively action, but it's tonally messy, a little juvenile, and faithful to a fault.

By Christopher Campbell | Published October 15, 2026

TAGGED AS: movies, Video Games

The latest film adaptation of Street Fighter arrives in theaters this week, and the first reviews are now online. Based on the martial arts video game franchise of the same name, the movie is a mix of faithful fan service and non-serious nostalgia bait. While some gamers will appreciate the visuals and fight sequences, critics say the script fails in its attempts at humor and for its lack of interest in crafting a compelling narrative.

Starring: Noah Centineo, Andrew Koji, Jason Momoa, 50 Cent

Directed By: Kitao Sakurai
