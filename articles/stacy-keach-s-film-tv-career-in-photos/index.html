Stacy Keach’s Film & TV Career In Photos

TheTools · 11.11.2026 01:14 · source · permalink

Breaking News

Stacy Keach, who died Monday at the age of 82, leaves behind a long resume of film, television and stage work.

Early highlights include John Huston’s Fat City opposite Jeff Bridges. Keach showed his comedic abilities with appearances in Cheech & Chong’s Up in Smoke and Nice Dreams.

And then there was Mike Hammer, a character that Keach played for decades across multiple shows.

Scroll through the gallery below to see photos from his long and varied career.
