GTA 6 Home improvements explained

TheTools · 19.11.2026 00:01 · source · permalink

Why you should complete the home improvements explained.

Home improvements in GTA 6 let you improve Jason's house, with the added bonus of boosting your relationship with Lucia once she's out of prison. If you're planning on having a more romantic relationship with her rather than simply being partners in crime, then taking the time to do some DIY is definitely worth the effort! With that in mind, let me tell you everything you need to know about home improvements in GTA 6 and, warning, small spoilers lie ahead.

Home improvements in GTA 6 explained

To complete a home improvement in GTA 6, you need to find spots around Jason's house which are marked with a paintbrush icon. If you're having trouble finding them, then search his house for anything that looks like a half finished DIY project. Panels leaning up against a wall? That's a home improvement! Spot some planks of wood? Looks like you've found another home improvement!

Once you've found a home improvement, you'll need to complete a short button command - be it for painting a wall or drilling in a screw - and watch a little cutscene of Jason finishing the work. (Which is a lot easier than doing DIY in real life to say the least.) In my experience, you can complete a home improvement at any time of day so, if you fancy fixing those steps at night, go for it!

There's five improvements you can complete in total and doing them will help boost your relationship with Lucia when she's released from prison during 'Coming Home'. Let's take a look at the home improvements you can complete:

  • Fix wall
    • Interact with the wall panels near Jason's bed.
  • Paint wall
    • You'll unlock this home improvement after you've fixed the wall in Jason's bedroom. There's three paint colours you can choose from: Tropical Green, Stormy Blue and Pastel Peach. The only difference between the three appears to be a cosmetic change.
  • Fix steps
    • This refers to the main outdoor steps leading up to Jason's house. To complete this, you need to interact with the planks of wood at the bottom of the stairs leading up to Jason's house.
  • Fix leaky roof
    • Climb up the ladder by the front door to reach the roof for this home improvement.
  • Fix railing
    • This is located on the balcony where Jason's home gym sits.

After you've completed them all, you'll get a notification that the house repairs are complete!

If you do the home improvements, then you'll get a different version of the cutscene where Lucia returns to Jason's house with him during 'Coming Home'. This will include some dialogue between the pair about the improvements to the house. The cutscene will also be more romantic in nature since, as mentioned, completing the home improvements helps you have a more romantic relationship with Lucia as it allows Jason to showcase how it's committed to having a life with her.
