Ethiopia vs Mozambique: How and Where to Watch LIVE

Matchday 4 of the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations Qualifiers brings an important Group J encounter to Bahir Dar International Stadium, where Ethiopia hosts Mozambique on Tuesday, November 17.

The Mambas enter the match in second place with four points from three games, while Ethiopia follows with three points after its recent victory in Mozambique.

Kickoff is scheduled for 8:00 AM ET / 5:00 AM PT, with English-language coverage beginning at 7:50 AM ET / 4:50 AM PT on beIN SPORTS CONNECT in the United States.



Ethiopia Looks to Build on Dramatic Victory in Maputo

Ethiopia, managed by Yohannes Sahilu, secured its first points of the qualifying campaign with a surprising 1-0 away victory over Mozambique on November 13.

The Walia Ibex had started the competition with consecutive 1-0 defeats against Sudan and Senegal, leaving them without a point after two matches.

However, Ethiopia responded in Maputo with a disciplined defensive performance before Kenean Markneh scored the decisive goal in the 87th minute.

The forward reacted to a rebound after Mozambique goalkeeper Ernan Siluane saved an effort from Carlos Damtew, securing an important victory for the visitors.

Goalkeeper Abubeker Nura also played an important role in preserving the clean sheet.

Sahilu's squad features Gatoch Panom, Biniyam Fikru and Abel Habtamu, with Ethiopia hoping to capitalize on home advantage and move into the qualification places.



Mozambique Seeks Redemption After First Qualifying Defeat

Mozambique, coached by Chiquinho Conde, suffered its first defeat of the campaign after previously collecting four points from two matches.

The Mambas opened with a 1-1 draw against Senegal before recording an impressive 4-1 home victory over Sudan.

However, their momentum was interrupted by Ethiopia, despite Mozambique controlling possession and creating several scoring opportunities.

Diogo Calila and Edmilson Dove both struck the woodwork, while Geny Catamo also threatened from a free kick.

Conde's 26-man squad includes Catamo, Bruno Langa, Edmilson Dove, Manuel Kambala and Stanley Ratifo.

The coach also welcomed back Diogo Calila and Keyns Abdala, while defender Cremildo Mussanganhe received his first international call-up.



A Direct Battle for Second Place in Group J

Senegal leads Group J with ten points after defeating Sudan 4-2 on November 16.

Mozambique follows with four points, while Ethiopia and Sudan have three each, although Sudan has already played four matches.

The top two teams will qualify for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations, hosted by Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda.

A victory would allow Ethiopia to move ahead of Mozambique in the standings, while the visitors could strengthen their hold on second place with a positive result.

With the final qualifying rounds scheduled for March 2027, the encounter could play a decisive role in determining which team joins Senegal among the qualification contenders.
