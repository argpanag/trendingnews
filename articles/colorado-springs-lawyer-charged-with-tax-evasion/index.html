Colorado Springs lawyer charged with tax evasion

TheTools · 20.11.2026 18:45 · source · permalink

A Colorado Springs lawyer is potentially facing over a decade of federal prison time for failing to file tax returns for his law firm and himself since 2020, according to an indictment unsealed Friday.

Terry Fjeld of law firm Terry Fjeld PC was indicted Nov. 4 on 12 counts related to the willful failure to file a tax return for his business and himself. Each count represents a maximum penalty of one year in prison, the U.S. Department of Justice for the District of Colorado said in a news release Friday.

Authorities said Fjeld’s lack of filing dates back to 2010, but the charges apply to 2020-25. The IRS issued more than 20 separate notices informing Fjeld of his need to file individual tax returns, according to the release. Multiple tax preparers also informed Fjeld he had a legal obligation to file his taxes.

It was not explained why he was not charged for alleged behavior prior to 2020.

Fjeld established his law firm in 1995. It primarily litigates Social Security Administration benefits and disability claims for clients.

The charges are part of the Trump administration’s “laser-focused” initiative to combat fraud “against the American people,” the news release states. Prosecutors from the DOJ’s Fraud Division, created by the administration on April 7, will be working the case.
