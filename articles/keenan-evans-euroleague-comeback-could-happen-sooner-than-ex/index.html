Zalgiris Kaunas are close to recalling Keenan Evans from the London Lions, per BasketNews sources. The American guard, who signed a two-year deal with Zalgiris this summer, has been rebuilding his rhythm in the EuroCup after a series of serious injuries.

Zalgiris Kaunas are looking to address their slow start to the EuroLeague season and are closing in on a roster move.

According to BasketNews sources, the Lithuanian champions are close to bringing Keenan Evans back to Kaunas earlier than initially planned.

Evans signed a two-year deal with Zalgiris this summer but was immediately loaned to the London Lions to regain his rhythm and confidence after suffering multiple serious injuries in recent years.

However, injuries within the Zalgiris roster and the team's struggles on the court have prompted the club to consider bringing Evans back sooner than expected.

Zalgiris retained the option to recall Evans from his loan spell, potentially allowing the American guard to rejoin the team during the season.

According to BasketNews sources, Zalgiris are close to recalling Keenan Evans from the London Lions to bolster their EuroLeague roster in the coming days.

Evans has appeared in three EuroCup games for London this season, averaging 11.7 points and 2.7 assists in 25 minutes per game. He has shot 53% from the two-point range, 36% from beyond the arc, and 92% from the free-throw line.

His best performance came against La Laguna Tenerife, when he scored 21 points and dished out two assists in 25 minutes.

In an October 12 exhibition game against the Portland Trail Blazers, Evans scored 7 points, grabbed 3 rebounds, and dished 1 assist in 20 minutes, but the London Lions suffered a heavy 119-66 defeat.

In the British Basketball League, Evans averaged 9.8 points, 2 rebounds, and 3.5 assists in 21 minutes per game.

Evans previously spent two seasons with Zalgiris, establishing himself as one of the EuroLeague's standout guards before moving to Olympiacos Piraeus.

However, his career has been repeatedly interrupted by serious injuries.

He suffered an Achilles injury toward the end of the 2022-23 season, then sustained a patellar ligament tear late in the 2023-24 campaign.

The latter setback forced him to miss the entire 2024-25 season.

Evans eventually made his EuroLeague debut for Olympiacos in the 2025-26 season, but suffered another Achilles injury in his first appearance.

That ultimately brought his time with the Greek powerhouse to an end after just one official EuroLeague game.
