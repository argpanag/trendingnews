PCG conducts K9 security checks

TheTools · 31.10.2026 18:37 · source · permalink
  • PCG K9 security inspections Batangas
  • Batangas City Grand Terminal Undas 2026
  • Coast Guard holiday travel security

Ahead of the 2025 midterm polls, K9 units are already stationed at train stations across Metro Manila to ensure the safety of the commuters and to check on suspicious activities that may be related to the elections.

PHOTOGRAPH BY JOEY SANCHEZ MENDOZA FOR THE DAILY TRIBUNE

The Philippine Coast Guard (PCG) conducted random K9 security inspections at the Batangas City Grand Terminal as part of heightened security measures for Undas 2026.

Coast Guard Station Batangas carried out the inspections with the Coast Guard K9 Field Operating Unit and the Southern Tagalog Explosive Ordnance Disposal team.

The operation focused on passenger buses and other public utility vehicles to help detect and prevent potential security threats amid the expected increase in holiday travelers.

The PCG urged the public to remain vigilant, observe security procedures and cooperate with authorities to ensure safe and orderly travel during Undas.
