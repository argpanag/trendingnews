Emmerdale baby Tate's kidnapper 'rumbled' and it's 'not Thea' - AOL

TheTools · 22.10.2026 09:21 · source · permalink

Baby Tate's kidnapper on Emmerdale may have been exposed.

Fans think they know who is to blame, after spoilers confirmed Joe Tate's baby son would be taken. Baby Tate is currently being looked after by Graham Foster in a cottage, and only Kim Tate knows this.

Joe had wanted his young son adopted after the death of his partner Dawn Taylor. But next week the baby goes missing, and Graham and Kim fear the worst.

Detective Thea Ramsden, Kim's secret daughter, is accused, leading to a violent showdown. But fans think the kidnapper, whose identity is revealed onscreen next week, might be someone else.

Some fans wondered if it could be a returning character, maybe a Tate relative wanting to target Kim and the family. Another theory is that it is an unexpected existing villager.

Taking to social media, one fan theorised: "If it's not Joe, maybe it is the return of Jamie Tate." Another fan said: "It could also pave the way for the return of a character or the introduction of a new Tate or Sugden."

A third fan posted: "I’m actually starting to think could it have been Sarah. Thea is faking concern and using it as an opportunity to get close to Kim when she’s at her weakest and she’s told Sarah to take the baby given she told her that she owed her for getting Charity out of the nick."

A viewer then speculated: "Could be Serena steals the child so Thea can't be implicated although we know she did it. It would be such a shock if Thea and Serena were more than good friends."

Another fan wondered if it actually is Thea after a promo clip teased sinister scenes. The fan said: "One word of the kidnapper - THEA!" while another asked: "But I'm wondering if, IF she and (girlfriend) Serena are in it together."

Spoilers for next week reveal that Thea ends up getting stabbed as her and Kim have a showdown amid the kidnapping. Kim keeps the news about the baby from Joe, who remains unaware that Kim and Graham are caring for the little one.

Kim becomes convinced that Thea is to blame. As she accuses her, events take a turn and Thea accuses Kim of trying to sell the child, and a dramatic showdown leads to Kim stabbing her secret daughter.

As Thea passes out, Kim rushes her to the hospital but will Thea survive, and will Kim face trouble? Meanwhile, Graham rushes to Home Farm and is relieved to find Baby Tate, but there's a discovery.
