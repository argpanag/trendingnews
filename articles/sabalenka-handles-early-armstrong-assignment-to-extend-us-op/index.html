Sabalenka handles early Armstrong assignment to extend US Open win streak to 16

TheTools · 04.09.2026 16:57 · source · permalink

NEW YORK -- In recent years, New York sports teams have given their fans a run of championship celebrations.

The Liberty won the first WNBA title in franchise history. Gotham FC captured a second NWSL championship. The Knicks ended a 53-year wait for an NBA crown.

But amid all of that recent success, perhaps no athlete has been more at home under the New York spotlight than Aryna Sabalenka.

The two-time defending champion extended her US Open winning streak to 16 matches on Friday, defeating Kamilla Rakhimova 6-3, 6-4 in 1 hour and 29 minutes to reach the fourth round and continue her pursuit of a third consecutive title.

“Honestly, it was the first time I played the 11 a.m. session on Louis Armstrong, and it was a full stadium,” Sabalenka said on court. “I’m so grateful. It’s a dream, and thank you guys for supporting me.”

Sabalenka will next face the winner of the match between Taylor Townsend and No. 15 seed Diana Shnaider.

The match

Rakhimova came into the match knowing the size of the task. Sabalenka had won all three of their previous meetings, including a 6-2, 6-2 victory at Roland Garros in 2023. Their only hard-court meeting, in Washington in 2024, went three sets.

Sabalenka broke her opponent in the second game and took the opening set 6-3. But Rakhimova, making her fourth third-round appearance at a Grand Slam, gave the World No. 1 a more sustained test in the second.

At 4-4, Rakhimova held game point to move within a game of forcing Sabalenka to serve to stay in the set. Instead, three consecutive unforced errors handed Sabalenka the break. Six points later, the match was over.

“She played incredible tennis,” Sabalenka said. “She pushed me so hard in the second set. She made me work hard and try my best. I’m super happy to close this match in straight sets.”

The victory improved Sabalenka to 4-0 against Rakhimova and sent her into the US Open fourth round for a sixth consecutive year.

Sixteen straight in Queens

Sabalenka’s last US Open defeat came in the 2023 final, when Coco Gauff beat her in three sets. Since then, the World No. 1 has won back-to-back titles in Queens, defeating Jessica Pegula in the 2024 final and Amanda Anisimova last year.

Friday’s win made Sabalenka the first player to win 16 consecutive US Open matches since Serena Williams won 26 in a row from 2012 through 2015.

The victory also added a few more lines to Sabalenka’s growing New York and Grand Slam résumé:

  • 16:  Sabalenka has now reached the fourth round at 16 consecutive Grand Slam events she has played, the first player to do so since Lindsay Davenport made the round of 16 at 20 straight majors from Wimbledon in 2000 through the 2006 US Open.
  • 8:  Since the start of 2025, Sabalenka is the only player to reach the fourth round at all eight Grand Slam events. Her only other season reaching the second week at all four majors was 2023.
  • 2:  Sabalenka is the second player since 2000 to reach the fourth round at every Grand Slam in consecutive calendar years while ranked World No. 1, after Serena Williams in 2015 and 2016.
  • 29:  Sabalenka has now won 29 US Open matches in straight sets, tying the Australian Open for her highest total at a single WTA-level event.

Bigger stakes

Since making her main-draw debut at the US Open in 2018, Sabalenka has collected more singles main-draw victories at the tournament than any other player. Her total now stands at 37.

She also leads the tour with 16 Grand Slam match-wins this season.

“I completely shut out the noise from outside and focus on myself,” Sabalenka said. “If I push myself every second, I know I’ll have my chances.”

Sabalenka arrived in New York with another major objective in view: She must win the title to retain a chance of staying atop the PIF WTA Rankings after the tournament.

For now, she has earned another week in New York.
