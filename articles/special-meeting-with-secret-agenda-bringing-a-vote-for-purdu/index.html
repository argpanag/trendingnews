Special meeting with secret agenda bringing a vote for Purdue University president?

TheTools · 05.11.2026 21:24 · source · permalink
  • Purdue University's trustees have scheduled an unusual special meeting for Thursday afternoon.
  • The meeting's timing and lack of a public agenda have led to speculation about naming a new university president.
  • A university spokesman confirmed the meeting will be "newsworthy" and followed by a media availability.

WEST LAFAYETTE — Purdue's trustees have announced a special meeting for Thursday afternoon that is unusual in its timing and lack of an agenda, prompting speculation that they might be poised to name a permanent successor to former President Mung Chiang.

The board of trustees typically meets on Friday mornings, after a Thursday executive session. But the board's emailed announcement Tuesday for this week's "special meeting" at 3:30 p.m. Thursday in Room 326 of Stewart Center says only that "an agenda will be provided at the meeting."

Purdue spokesman Trevor Peters did not confirm or deny the topic will be a vote and announcement about Chiang's replacement.

"It will be newsworthy and a media availability will follow," he said.

The first home regular-season basketball game of the season is scheduled for Friday night, and it is homecoming for Purdue Football on Saturday in Ross Ade — possibly good timing to introduce a new leader to Purdue faithful.

Chiang, Purdue's 13th president, became the president of Northwestern University as of July 1, when former President Mitch Daniels took the reins temporarily.

At the time, Daniels said one of his top priorities was to vacate the interim position "as fast as I can."

According to university records, Chiang's tenure as Purdue's president was the shortest in Purdue history second only to Abram Shortridge, who left after 18 months in 1874.
