Babysitting turns into gogo’s nightmare!

TheTools · 21.10.2026 15:03 · source · permalink

Gogo left in shock after finding mum dead in rented room

A GOGO who agreed to babysit her makhi’s toddler was left traumatised.

This after discovering the toddler’s mum dead in her rented room.

Gogo Victoria Mhlongo (66) from Waterloo in Durban, KZN, said the nightmare unfolded on Monday, 19 October, when the woman’s husband allegedly asked her to look after their 15-month-old baby while he went to buy cigarettes.

She agreed, never imagining that the short babysitting favour would end in such a shocking discovery.

Gogo Victoria told Daily Sun that the baby became restless and started crying a few minutes after the man left.

“I didn’t have a problem with that, and I willingly took the baby. But a few minutes after he left, the baby started being restless and crying. I waited for him to return because he said he was going to a local spaza shop,” Victoria said.

When the man failed to return, the gogo decided to check on the baby’s mum.

“I went to the house and knocked. Nobody answered until I pushed the door open. Shockingly, I found the mum lying on the floor. When I checked her, she was already dead,” she said.

Gogo Victoria said she rushed outside to alert other neighbours, who were also shocked by the discovery.

She said the man had still not returned to collect his baby or come back to the house.

“We don’t want to speculate. We will hear from him when he’s found.”

A resident claimed that the man had contacted him and said his wife had slipped, fallen and lost consciousness.

The resident said the man’s cellphone was later switched off.

The resident also alleged that neighbours had previously witnessed the man assaulting his wife while he was drunk. These allegations haven’t been independently verified.

The resident said the couple had been renting a room in Waterloo and were not originally from the area. Neighbours were trying to contact the woman’s family.

KZN police spokesman Lieutenant-Colonel Paul Magwaza confirmed that Verulam cops have opened an inquest docket after the body of a woman (36) was found on Monday, 19 October.

He said no visible injuries were found on the body.

“The circumstances surrounding the incident are under investigation,” he added.
