'I was talking about me and my own situation' - McClean clarifies mental health comments

TheTools · 23.10.2026 09:42 · source · permalink

JAMES MCCLEAN HAS clarified his remarks in relation to mental health in an interview last weekend, explaining that his comments referred to his “own situation” and that he commends men who seek therapy.

McClean was heavily criticised following an interview with Paul Kimmage of the Sunday Independent which he was doing to promote his recently released autobiography, ‘The Walls’.

When asked about his thoughts on considering therapy, McClean said, “I was back at my [parents’] house a week ago, and I heard a voice upstairs, ‘Who the f**k is that?’ And it was my brother on a call to the club psychologist, having a lovely chat.

“And a lot of the players speak to him and for me that’s just weak. As a man I see that as weakness. I don’t need to open up. I see it as a weakness that I’d have to speak to someone about my feelings; my emotions…”

But McClean has since explained his comments in an interview with UTV Live News, saying that he applauds men who seek therapy.

“Any man that goes to therapy, any man that seeks help, I couldn’t commend them any bigger for doing that. But I was talking about me and my own situation. And probably using the word weakness or having that view that it’s a weakness for me. And maybe that’s my weakness.”

In the wake of his interview with the Sunday Independent, Derry City released a statement distancing the club from remarks made by their estranged player. Meanwhile, McClean’s wife, Erin, published a separate statement on social media, outlining that the former Ireland international “is struggling at the moment, and has been for some time.”
