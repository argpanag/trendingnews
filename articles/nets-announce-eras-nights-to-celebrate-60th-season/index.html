Nets announce “Eras Nights” to celebrate 60th season

TheTools · 04.09.2026 13:23 · source · permalink

Top Stories

The Brooklyn Nets will celebrate their 60th season with six 'Eras Nights' games, honoring different decades of Nets basketball.

From NBA.com News Services

‘Hello Brooklyn Night,’ will celebrate the Nets’ arrival in Brooklyn.

The Brooklyn Nets have announced they will host six games under the theme “Eras Nights” in commemoration of the team’s rich 60-year history.

From Julius Erving to Jason Kidd, Joe Johnson and more, the Nets have had a handful of stars grace the franchise throughout six decades. With this celebration, the organization will not only honor those eras, but also the different styles and uniforms that defined them.

All season long, we’re bringing six decades of Nets history to life with special era-inspired experiences, exclusive giveaways, and more.
