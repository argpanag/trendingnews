Western Sydney Wanderers v Adelaide United Prediction: 3.50 hosts to shut out Reds - bet365 News UK

It has been a miserable start to the season for Adelaide United and things won't get much easier for the Reds on Friday when they head to CommBank Stadium to take on the Western Sydney Wanderers.

Adelaide have a strong recent record in this fixture but, given their current form, the Wanderers will fancy their chances of getting revenge in Parramatta before the weekend.

Best Bet: Western Sydney Wanderers to win to nil @ 3.50

Yet to win in the A-League this season, it's been a tough start for Adelaide ahead of their trip to New South Wales this week.

While the Reds have won their last three league games against the Wanderers, there’s value to be had siding with a home win to nil on Friday.

Adelaide have failed to score in their last two away games and Wanderers to win to nil is well priced at 3.50.

Alternative Bet: Nicolas Milanovic anytime goalscorer @ 2.62

Enjoying a positive start to the season, Nicolas Milanovic is worth considering in the anytime goalscorer market for this one.

The 25-year-old found the back of the net last time out against Melbourne City, meaning he’s now scored in two of his last three games for Western Sydney.

In this sort of form, former Aberdeen forward Milanovic offers good value at 2.62 to find the back of the net against the Reds.
