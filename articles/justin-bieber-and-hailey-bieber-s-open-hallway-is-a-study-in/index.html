Justin Bieber and Hailey Bieber's Open Hallway is a Study in Using Art to Elevate a Minimalist Space

TheTools · 11.10.2026 15:31 · source · permalink

Credit: Jon Kopaloff via Getty Images

The chicest homes use hallways not as a transition point, but as a site of design all their own. They are home to art, color, pattern, and warmth.

This balance can be more difficult to strike in open-plan homes, but Justin and Hailey Bieber nailed the technique in their former Beverly Hills home.

Justin captured the hallway between their kitchen and living room on his Instagram, highlighting the equilibrium between natural wood and bright, pop art sculptures. A shelved sideboard divides the two rooms and is home to numerous KAWS figurines (like these from 1stDibs).

Whether your style is contemporary like Justin and Hailey's or more traditional, keeping everything neutral except the art is an excellent way to make your hallway pop. No matter if you have an open or closed layout, it works.

The first piece to invest in if you want to recreate this method of decorating with art, is a set of horizontal shelves, or a sideboard.

You could select one the same color as your floors like Justin and Hailey, or opt for a darker wood to create a touch of textural contrast. Design expert Shelley Cochrane explains: 'First impressions count, and as hallways are often the first room we see as we enter our homes, it's important that the hallway makes an impact.'

She continues: 'If space allows, a sideboard with beautifully styled accessories or planters will help to seamlessly tie in this room with the rest of the home, whilst providing helpful storage to conceal everyday items.'

As the cobalt blue and bright yellow artworks in Hailey and Justin's hall demonstrate, a hallway is the perfect place to experiment with these bold statement shades.

'An unexpected combination of primary hues – think sunshine yellow combined with sky blue and striking red – offers a mood-enhancing effect that works well in every room, from the kitchen to the living room, but perhaps especially in the hallway,' advises Shelley.

For a more traditional take on the look, she recommends: 'To style this space, combine a mid-century-inspired wooden sideboard with a modern color palette of lively reds. Display an eclectic selection of vases or ceramics while a canvas or tapestry adds a final flourish of artistry. Bonus points if it's your own work!'

Finally, part of what works so well in Justin and Hailey's California property is the use of wood-drenching across the floors and furniture. You can easily recreate this look with accessories.

Georgia Metcalfe, Design Expert & Creative Director at French Bedroom, states: 'Incorporating natural materials such as wooden lamps into your lighting design not only enhances the aesthetic but also introduces a sense of warmth and natural beauty to any room. The organic textures and tones of wood create a cozy and inviting atmosphere, making rooms feel more comfortable and lived-in.'

The hallway is an unexpected place to embrace your style, and you will always be grateful for building this into your design.
