All-Ireland champions Galway take seven LGFA All-Stars, Síofra O'Shea named best footballer

TheTools · 15.11.2026 09:42 · source · permalink

Kerry footballer Síofra O’Shea is interviewed by Máire Ní Bhraonáin of TG4. Pic: Paul Phelan/Sportsfile

All-Ireland senior champions Galway lead the way with seven representatives on the 2026 TG4 Ladies Football All-Star team.

Kerry captain Síofra O’Shea was named as the Senior Players’ Player of the Year at the annual banquet at The Bonnington Dublin Hotel on Saturday night.

The individual accolade capped a memorable night for the Southern Gaels player, who also collected a second successive All-Star award.

Galway’s seven winners are defenders Brónagh Quinn, Kate Geraghty, Hannah Noone and Nicola Ward, midfielder Niamh Divilly and attackers Olivia Divilly and Leanne Coen.

Runners-up Kerry have been rewarded with five awards, namely goalkeeper Mary Ellen Bolger, defenders Róisín Rahilly and Emma Costello, midfielder Mary O’Connell, and team captain O’Shea, the leading scorer in this year’s championship.

There is one award each for All-Ireland semi-finalists Armagh and Dublin, with Aimee Mackin and Orlagh Nolan honoured in attack.

Waterford’s Kellyann Hogan is also included in the half-forward line, which means the Déise have a TG4 All-Star for the second year in succession, following Emma Murray’s award in 2025.

2026 TG4 All-Star team: 1. Mary Ellen Bolger (Kerry) 2. Róisín Rahilly (Kerry) 3. Brónagh Quinn (Galway) 4. Kate Geraghty (Galway) 5. Hannah Noone (Galway) 6. Nicola Ward (Galway) 7. Emma Costello (Kerry) 8. Niamh Divilly (Galway) 9. Mary O’Connell (Kerry) 10. Olivia Divilly (Galway) 11. Orlagh Nolan (Dublin) 12. Kellyann Hogan (Waterford) 13. Aimee Mackin (Armagh) 14. Leanne Coen (Galway) 15. Síofra O’Shea (Kerry).
