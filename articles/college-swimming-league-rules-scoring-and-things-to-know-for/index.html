College Swimming League: Rules, Scoring, And Things To Know For Season 1

TheTools · 20.09.2026 18:40 · source · permalink

The College Swimming League (CSL) kicks off later this week with matches 1 and 2 in Westmont, Illinois. 12 of the top NCAA programs will compete in the league’s first season. Here are some of the highlights to the rules and scoring.

The season consists of six regular-season matches and two post-season matches. Teams ranked 1-3 will automatically advance to the final while teams ranked 4-7 will meet in the wild card match, with only one advancing to the final.

Each of the regular season matches includes four teams. The top team at each match will score four points, 2nd place three points, 3rd place two points, and 4th place one point. Match scoring is combined for each school, points earned in men’s and women’s events accumulate to a single team score for each institution.

Each school is allowed to have two entries in every event. Each athlete has an entry limit of three individual events and two relay events. Super Skins count as a relay event.

Individual Event Scoring

  • 1st: 9 points
  • 2nd: 7 points
  • 3rd: 6 points
  • 4th: 5 points
  • 5th: 4 points
  • 6th: 3 points
  • 7th: 2 points
  • 8th: 1 point

Relay Event Scoring

  • 1st: 18 points
  • 2nd: 14 points
  • 3rd: 12 points
  • 4th: 10 points
  • 5th: 8 points
  • 6th: 6 points
  • 7th: 4 points
  • 8th: 2 points
