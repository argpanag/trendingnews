Daniil Medvedev ended his season after losing in the semifinals of the ATP Finals 2026 against Alexander Zverev in a match defined by small details. The Russian surprised everyone in the press conference by referring to his rival's game and the possible improvements that have led him this year to become world number 1.

They have known each other since their teenage years, faced each other 23 times, and their contrasting personalities both on and off the court have led to some friction between them. Perhaps all this explains the curious words of Daniil Medvedev in the press conference following his loss to Alexander Zverev in the semifinals of the ATP Finals 2026.

Far from being in awe of Sascha's game, the Russian player downplayed the possible improvements of the current world number 1 and reduced the difference between them to serving performance. "I didn't have a single break point, so obviously, it's not easy to return his serves right now—I deserved to lose, I should have done better in the tiebreak of the first set and then too," commented the man who finishes as the world's number 5.

Medvedev attributes Zverev's improvement to serving and greater consistency

"There have been few differences, everything was decided in a few points. He was good from the baseline in those, but the important thing is that he served better," he noted before being asked if he had noticed any obvious improvement in Zverev's game.

Indeed, many of the detractors of the German believe he has reached world number 1 more due to the injuries of Sinner and Alcaraz than any significant change in his tennis that has allowed him to make a leap in quality. Medvedev seems to align with that line of thought, judging by his response.

"All tennis players nowadays are aggressive, there are no defensive players. I haven't noticed that Sascha is more aggressive than before, nor do I think he has improved his forehand. I thought it was good before, although he made more errors than usual, and now he continues to make them. What he is doing better is serving and he has also gained consistency. He has stopped losing matches he shouldn't lose," he asserted emphatically.
