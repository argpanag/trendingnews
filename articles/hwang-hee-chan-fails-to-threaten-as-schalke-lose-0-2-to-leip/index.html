Hwang Hee‑chan fails to threaten as Schalke lose 0-2 to Leipzig

TheTools · 31.10.2026 17:00 · source · permalink

Hwang Hee-chan (30, Schalke 04) played 83 minutes and created three scoring chances but could not prevent his team's defeat. He recorded an 89% pass completion rate, contributing to attacking build-up, but did not create any direct scoring opportunities.

Schalke 04 lost 0-2 to RB Leipzig in the eighth round of the 2026-2027 German Bundesliga held in Gelsenkirchen, Germany, on the 31st of last month (Korea time).

The loss left Schalke in 16th place in the league (1 win, 3 draws, 4 losses, 6 points).

Hwang Hee-chan started as the central forward. He led the attack alongside Kenan Karaman, Chukwubuike Adamu and others but had difficulty breaking through Leipzig's defense.

Leipzig attacked aggressively from the early stages. In the 15th minute of the first half, Antonio Nusa's shot was saved by goalkeeper Loris Karius, and shortly after Homulu's header went wide of the goal.

Hwang Hee-chan set up an attacking chance in the 25th minute of the first half with a pass to Adamu. Adamu attempted a left-footed shot from the left side of the penalty area but it was blocked by the defense.

The opening goal came in the 37th minute of the first half. Leipzig's Homulu drew a foul from Tanaka Satoshi inside the penalty area and won a penalty kick. Nusa, who took the kick, struck the net with a right-footed shot.

Schalke tried to counterattack in the second half. In the 17th minute of the second half Hwang Hee-chan played a pass to Dejan Ljubičić but the shot went over the goal. A minute later he delivered a pass that led to a long-range shot by Adil Aouchiche, but the opposing defense blocked it.

Leipzig scored a second goal in the 33rd minute of the second half. Homulu, who received a pass from Nusa, finished with a right-footed shot from the center of the penalty area to make it 2-0.

Hwang Hee-chan was substituted off for Bryan Lasme in the 38th minute of the second half. Schalke ultimately failed to score and fell 0-2.

According to football statistics outlet FootMob, Hwang Hee-chan touched the ball 38 times in 83 minutes. He attempted 28 passes and completed 25. His success rate was 89%.

He was credited with three chances created. He also completed five passes into attacking areas and accurately connected one long pass. He attempted one dribble and succeeded, and won three of five ground duels.

However, as the central forward he left some disappointing numbers. Hwang Hee-chan did not touch the ball even once inside the opponent's penalty area that day. He had no shots and recorded neither a goal nor an assist.

While he showed positive signs in pass accuracy and chance creation, he failed to produce direct threats in front of goal. Despite Hwang Hee-chan's attacking build-up, Schalke could not break down Leipzig's defense and accepted a goalless defeat.
