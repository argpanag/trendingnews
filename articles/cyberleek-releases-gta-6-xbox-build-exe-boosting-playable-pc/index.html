Cyberleek releases GTA 6 Xbox build .exe, boosting playable PC port chances

TheTools · 23.11.2026 21:04 · source · permalink

The notorious Cyberleek circulated a 209 GB GTA 6 Xbox build on November 19th, but it was missing an essential file. They have now released the executable ahead of schedule, along with an update on a secret project. The development is welcome news to groups working to adapt the build to a playable PC port.

To unlock an earlier release, the individual had asked supporters to reach a $15 million market cap for his meme coin. The cryptocurrency goal was later lowered to $6 million. Regardless, once the November 23rd deadline passed, they promised to distribute the .exe. Just before the countdown clock expired, the it began appearing on piracy sites.

The race for a Windows port

Cyberleek predicted that the GTA 6 Xbox build would lead to a bootable PC version within a week. Possibilities included an emulator, although no app currently simulates the console. GitHub projects like Leonida have also endeavored to modify the files to run natively on Windows. The Series X uses the familiar x86-64 CPU architecture, but many roadblocks remain.

Unfortunately, for Steam users who also support piracy, the executable doesn’t make the game playable right away. Hackers still have to tackle issues like translating shaders and memory management. Regardless, developers can study the file, in hopes of creating Windows compatibility layers.

Will there be more leaks?

With the .exe for the Xbox build, Cyberleek attached a text file, reiterating their intentions. They explain that past gameplay leaks included no story spoilers to avoid hurting players. Instead, the target is Take-Two Interactive and Rockstar, who delayed a release date for a GTA 6 PC port.

The cryptocurrency funds are supposedly raising money for a project that is “for the gamers”. While details are scarce, it’s teased as more significant than any Grand Theft Auto leak.

Despite achieving a record-tying average Metacritic score of 99, the Rockstar project has suffered review bombing. The user rating stands at around 5.7/10 after over 100,000 evaluations. While most of the 0 scores may be unwarranted, performance limits are a common criticism. Cyberleek identified the 30 fps cap as a potential major advantage to a GTA 6 PC option.
