In June 2026, Cuoco announced they were expecting a second daughter.

Kaley Cuoco is a mom of two!

The Big Bang Theory alum and her fiancé, Tom Pelphrey, first became parents in March 2023, welcoming daughter Matilda Carmine Richie Pelphrey. Her arrival came a little under a year after the couple confirmed their relationship in May 2022."We have the same manager [Andrea Pett-Joseph], and we were set up by [her], which is so Hollywood," Cuoco recalled to USA Today.

She added that her first meeting with Pelphrey was nothing short of magical. "I heard his voice, and I turned around, and it was like my life was over, or just starting. It hit me. It was love at first sight…We're ready to build a life together."

After several years of publicly dating, the pair confirmed their engagement in an August 2024 Instagram Stories post. A month later, People asked her how wedding planning was going. Cuoco replied that they "haven't started planning anything yet" and joked that "maybe another kid will have to come first." In the same conversation, she recalled the sea of questions she got after Matilda's arrival."The minute I had my baby, they're like, 'When's your second one?' I'm like, 'Oh my God, is anyone gonna just let me just enjoy this?'"

In June 2026, the actress took to Instagram to share that she and Pelphrey were indeed expecting their second child.

Ahead, learn what Kaley Cuoco has shared about her two daughters and her approach to parenting with Pelphrey.

Matilda Pelphrey

Kaley Cuoco, Tom Pelphrey, and daughter Matilda at Disneyland in January 2025.
Credit: @kaleycuoco

Cuoco and Pelphrey welcomed Matilda Carmine Richie Pelphrey on March 30, 2023, announcing her arrival and name on Instagram soon after the birth."We are overjoyed and grateful for this little miracle 💓," the new mom captioned her post, adding that Matilda was "the new light of our lives!"

Cuoco later told Today that she and Pelphrey picked the name Matilda when they'd first started dating. After hearing someone say the name, the actress recalled, "We looked at each other and it was like, 'Would it be crazy to say if we had a baby we'd name her Matilda?'"

"You know when you first start dating and you're so obsessed, and you're planning your future children. It was that sort of thing," she explained. Just as Eva Mendes has said that she never wanted to have kids before meeting now-partner Ryan Gosling, Cuoco told Emmy Magazine motherhood was "not a goal of mine" until she connected with Pelphrey. Then, "It was instantaneous, 'Oh my God, I want to have a kid with you.'"

Tom Pelphrey and Kaley Cuoco at the Golden Globe Awards on January 10, 2023.
Credit: Kevork Djansezian/NBC via Getty Images

Her pregnancy was written into the comedy-thriller series Based on a True Story. Per Emmy, the show wrapped filming three weeks before Cuoco's due date—and Matilda was already making her presence known on set by kicking during scenes. "The child already thinks she's the star of everything," the new mom told the outlet soon after her daughter's arrival.

Matilda is certainly the star of her parents' Instagram accounts, whether they're sharing snaps from trips to Disneyland or her Little Mermaid–themed third birthday celebrations. In the latter Instagram post, Cuoco described her toddler as "the most special babe on the planet!"

Baby No. 2

In June 2026, Cuoco announced that Matilda would soon be a big sister, sharing sweet family snapshots of Matilda enjoying a gender reveal cake to Instagram. In the post's caption, she wrote, "This second time around journey has been a little windier in many ways , but wow we are so grateful for this moment!! Little sissy on the way!!! [Pelphrey is a] girl dad for life." The post also included snapshots from different moments in Cuoco's pregnancy.

On August 16, the Flight Attendant star had a fiesta-themed baby shower with various celebrity friends in attendance, including Lacey Chabert, Kimberly J. Brown, Ashley Jones, and Amy Davidson. Per People, her Instagram Stories about the event showed older daughter Matilda eating a burrito in front of a Taco Bell-themed cake.

On September 14, 2026, Pelphrey took home the Emmy for best supporting actor in a drama series. In his acceptance speech, he thanked Cuoco and their girls. "And to my favorite actor in this room, my love, my best friend, my partner, the very pregnant Kaley—I love you more than anything, and our family is the crown jewel of my life," he shared, bringing Cuoco to tears. "Matilda, daddy loves you. Baby girl, daddy loves you. Thank you. God bless."
