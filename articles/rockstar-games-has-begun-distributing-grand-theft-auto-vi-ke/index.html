Rockstar Games has begun distributing Grand Theft Auto VI keys to gaming journalists.

TheTools · 27.10.2026 07:30 · source · permalink

Rockstar Games has officially begun the extensive distribution of digital keys for press copies of the open-world action game Grand Theft Auto VI to industry media and leading reviewers. Early copies are being distributed more than three weeks before the scheduled worldwide release, giving journalists ample time to thoroughly explore the sprawling State of Leonidas, complete story missions as protagonists Jason and Lucia, and thoroughly evaluate the technical aspects of the RAGE game engine on the current-generation PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S consoles.

Along with providing access to pre-release builds, the company has established strict regulations for the distribution of materials, setting the end date of the review embargo for November 18, 2026. Therefore, the first ratings, detailed text reviews, and the consolidated average score on the Metacritic aggregator will become available to the general public approximately 24 hours before the official launch of the project, scheduled for November 19. This timeline completely mirrors Take-Two Interactive's traditional publishing strategy, used for the release of the company's previous flagship blockbusters, including Grand Theft Auto V and Red Dead Redemption 2.

The non-disclosure agreement (NDA) imposes strict technical restrictions on revealing plot twists, key mechanics, and gameplay footage before the official release of digital versions in stores. Publication of written articles is expected to begin before the release of extensive video reviews to prevent the spread of plot spoilers on social media before the release date. Meanwhile, pre-loading of encrypted game files for digital and retail pre-orders will begin on November 12th, ensuring systems are ready to launch immediately on release day.
