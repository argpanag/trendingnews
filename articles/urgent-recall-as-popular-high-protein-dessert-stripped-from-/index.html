Urgent recall as popular high protein dessert stripped from shelves

TheTools · 25.10.2026 23:36 · source · permalink

A recall has been issued after glass fragments were found in pudding cups sold at supermarkets in Queensland, NSW, and the ACT.

The high-protein puddings by Arla were sold at Coles, Costco, IGA, Harris Farm and Supabarn, the recall said.

The affected 200g pots of the salted caramel flavoured puddings, and chocolate flavoured puddings, will display best before dates of April 28 and March 9 respectively.

The salted caramel protein puddings were only sold at Costco in NSW.

The chocolate protein puddings were sold at Coles in NSW and QLD, at independent food retailers including IGA and Harris Farm in NSW, and at IGA and Supabarn in the ACT.

“Food products containing glass fragments may cause illness/injury if consumed,” the Food Authority recall said.

“Consumers should not eat this product.

“Any consumers concerned about their health should seek medical advice.

“Consumers should return the products to the place of purchase for a full refund.”
