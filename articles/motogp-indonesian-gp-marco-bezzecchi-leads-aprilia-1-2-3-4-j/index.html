MotoGP Indonesian GP: Marco Bezzecchi leads Aprilia 1-2-3-4; Jorge Martin reclaims points lead

TheTools · 11.10.2026 08:36 · source · permalink

Aprilia bounce back from a nightmare sprint race to lock out the top four positions in the MotoGP Indonesian GP

Marco Bezzecchi, Aprilia Racing

Photo by: Gold and Goose Photography / Getty Images

Marco Bezzecchi outduelled Raul Fernandez to claim his first MotoGP win in over four months at the Indonesian Grand Prix, as his team-mate Jorge Martin retook the championship lead with third.

Just a day after they made contact in an opening lap melee and crashed out of the sprint - an incident that left Fernandez with a broken finger and triggered a major outburst - Bezzecchi and Fernandez engaged in a thrilling race-long battle for top honours in Mandalika.

After biding his time in second, factory Aprilia rider Bezzecchi passed the Spaniard on lap 19 of 27 and then pulled away at the front, taking the chequered flag with a winning margin of 4.2s.

Martin had no answer to his Aprilia compatriots but a third-place result put him back at the top of the standings, with reigning champion Marc Marquez only managing sixth on the factory Ducati.

At the start of the race, both Marquez and Bezzecchi challenged polesitter Fernandez, with Bezzecchi the first to reach the braking zone for Turn 1. But the Italian rider ran too deep into the corner and dropped back to second place, allowing Fernandez to reclaim the lead.

Meanwhile, erstwhile championship leader Martin made a poor launch from fifth on the grid and dropped to sixth, behind the Ducatis of Fermin Aldeguer and Fabio di Giannantonio. But the Spaniard quickly began working his way towards the front, dispatching di Giannantonio before moving to fourth when Aldeguer crashed at the end of lap 2.

Jorge Martin, Aprilia Racing Team

Photo by: Gold and Goose Photography / Getty Images

This put him directly behind his title rival Marquez, who appeared to struggle in the early laps after taking a shock win in Saturday’s sprint. Sensing an opportunity, Martin wasted little time behind the Ducati rider, passing him with ease on lap 3 to move into podium positions.

But by this stage, Fernandez and Bezzecchi had already checked out at the front, with the pair emerging as the only contenders for victory.

The Trackhouse rider initially built a lead of nearly a second, but Bezzecchi came back at him on lap 9, closing the gap down to half a second.

The pair then began to trade fastest laps, with Bezzecchi responding to Fernandez’s pace whenever the latter tried to pull away at the front.

But by the middle phase of the race, Bezzecchi was clearly the quicker of the two, and on lap 19, he made the decisive move into Turn 1 to hit the front. 

Once ahead, the Italian immediately pulled out a lead of over a second, helped by Fernandez running wide at Turn 1.

With Fernandez unable to fightback, Bezzecchi cruised to the finish line, securing his first MotoGP win since May’s Italian Grand Prix and the first for Aprilia since the British Grand Prix at the beginning of August.

Marco Bezzecchi, Aprilia Racing

Photo by: Gold and Goose Photography / Getty Images

Fernandez, who was not even sure about contesting the race after his injury, secured a solid second place, with Martin claiming the final spot on the podium.

Meanwhile, Ai Ogura, who admitted to being the slowest Aprilia rider on Saturday, rose from seventh on the grid to pass KTM’s Pedro Acosta on the penultimate lap to complete a 1-2-3-4 for Aprilia.

Acosta still finished in front of Marquez after the pair traded positions several times through the middle of the race as they tried to keep their tyre pressure above the legal levels.

With Marquez only managing sixth, Martin leads the championship again - by two points - heading into the Australian Grand Prix in two weeks’ time.

Meanwhile, Alex Marquez crossed the finish line in seventh place in the sole-surviving Gresini, with di Giannantonio managing only eighth after starting on the front row.

Fabio Quartararo (Yamaha) and Enea Bastianini (Tech3) completed the top 10.

Brad Binder was next up in 11th on the factory KTM, while Honda delivered an improved showing over the sprint as both Luca Marini and Johann Zarco finished inside the points in 12th and 13th respectively.

Franco Morbidelli (VR46) and Alex Rins (Yamaha) were the last of the points scorers in 14th and 15th respectively.

Fermin Aldeguer (Gresini), Jack Miller (Pramac) and Francesco Bagnaia (Ducati) all crashed out in the early stages of the race, with Toprak Razgatlioglu (Pramac) later joining them in the list of retirees.
