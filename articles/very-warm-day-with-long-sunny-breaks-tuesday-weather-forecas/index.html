Very warm day with long sunny breaks – Tuesday weather forecast

TheTools · 27.10.2026 03:30 · source · permalink

featured

Good morning, Joburg Etc family. Here is your weather forecast for Tuesday, 27 October 2026.

Today’s weather in Johannesburg will feature very warm conditions with long sunny breaks and decreasing cloud cover, with a high of 32°C and a low of 13°C. Expect a hot and bright day with plenty of sunshine to kick off the week.

The UV index will reach an extreme level of 12. The AccuLumen Brightness Index™ is rated at 8, meaning it will be a bright day; sunscreen is essential if you’re spending time outdoors.

Expect around 34% cloud cover during the day, decreasing to about 1% overnight as conditions clear.

A westerly wind will move through Johannesburg at around 17kph, with gusts reaching up to 44kph.

This evening, winds will shift south-southeasterly at around 11kph, and gusts reaching 33kph.

There is a 1% chance of precipitation during the day and overnight, with no chance of thunderstorms.

You can catch the sunrise at 05:23 and the sunset at 18:21, giving Johannesburg around 12 hours and 58 minutes of daylight.

Wishing you a peaceful and pleasant Tuesday!
