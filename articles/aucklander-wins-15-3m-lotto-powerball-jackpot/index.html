Aucklander wins $15.3m Lotto Powerball jackpot

TheTools · 04.10.2026 05:04 · source · permalink

An Auckland Lotto player won $15.3 million overnight, taking home both the Powerball and a Lotto First Division prize.

The winning numbers are 03 04 05 17 18 36 Bonus 7, and Powerball is 1.

The Powerball prize was worth $15 million, while the First Division was $333,333

The winner joined two other MyLotto customers in collecting $333,333 from the First Division draw, one from Christchurch and another also from Auckland.

A Strike One player gained $200,000, with the winning ticket being sold at Woolworths Rangiora East in Rangiora.

From today, all triple dip tickets sold until 7.30pm on October 17 will go into a draw to win one of 100 $20,000 extra prizes.
