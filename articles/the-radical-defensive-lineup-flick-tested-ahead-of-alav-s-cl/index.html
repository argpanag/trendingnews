The radical defensive lineup Flick tested ahead of Alavés clash

The German coach tested Fariñas at right back, Cubarsí and De Jong at center back, and Cancelo at left back during this morning’s training session

Hansi Flick will be without four defenders for Barcelona’s upcoming match against Alavés. Andreas Christensen was already sidelined, and the German coach has since lost Jules Koundé and Eric Garcia, who both suffered injuries against Real Betis, as well as Xavi Espart, who was forced off just 30 minutes into last Sunday’s Clásico. Flick had hoped to have Koundé back for Saturday’s game, but the Frenchman has yet to fully recover from discomfort in his left ankle.

With so many defensive absences, Flick will have to put together a makeshift back line for Saturday’s clash against the Basque side at Spotify Camp Nou, with kickoff scheduled for 9 p.m. local time.

The Barcelona coach has limited options but is considering some significant changes, including a few surprises. Based on what he tested during Friday’s training session, after members of the media had left the Tito Vilanova training field, Barcelona could line up with Joan Garcia in goal and a back four consisting of Fariñas, Cubarsí, Frenkie de Jong, and Cancelo.

That would mean a rest for Gerard Martín, while Balde would once again start on the bench. The left back featured against PSG and Real Madrid, but Flick has acknowledged that he is still lacking confidence.

The rest of the lineup Flick tested featured Rodri and Pedri as the two holding midfielders, Lamine Yamal and Adeyemion the wings, Fermín in the attacking midfield role, and Raphinha leading the attack as a center forward.

It remains to be seen whether Flick was satisfied with what he saw in training and will stick with that lineup on Saturday, or whether he will ultimately opt for a more conventional defense and potentially rest additional regular starters.
