Game Of Thrones’ Daenerys Targaryen Officially Returns December 2026 In The Old Raven

TheTools · 19.11.2026 12:30 · source · permalink

Daenerys Stormborn of the House Targaryen, the First of Her Name, the Unburnt, Queen of the Andals and the Rhoynar and the First Men, Queen of Meereen, Khaleesi of the Great Grass Sea, Protector of the Realm, Lady Regnant of the Seven Kingdoms, Breaker of Chains, and Mother of Dragons has cemented herself as one of the most iconic fictional characters of all time, and now, she is set to return next month in an exciting new event.

First introduced in 1996's Game of Thrones by George R.R. Martin, it was thanks to Emilia Clarke's portrayal of Daenerys Targaryen in the HBO television show of the same name, that the Mother of Dragons is as recognizable as she is today. Known for bringing dragons back to Westeros for the first time in centuries, Daenerys' legacy continues despite the show coming to an end back in 2019.

Now, whilst we wait to see how her story continues in The Winds of Winter (if it should ever be published), the Mother of Dragons is returning in a brand-new event which is set to take place this December in Game of Thrones: Legends.

First launched back in 2024, Game of Thrones: Legends is an iOS and Android puzzle role-playing mobile game developed and published by Zynga, and despite being over two years old, it still welcomes monthly updates which introduce new, yet familiar, characters, events, and limited-time content.

Featuring characters from not just Game of Thrones, but also House of the Dragon and A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms, Game of Thrones: Legends is home to the likes of Jon Snow, Rhaenyra Targaryen, and Ser Duncan "Dunk" the Tall. Now, it seems as though Daenerys Targaryen is about to be in the spotlight once more during The Old Raven update.

Running from November 4, at 4 a.m. ET – December 2, at 2 a.m. ET, The Old Raven event will introduce Legendary Champions Maester Aemon, Benjen Stark, Samwell Tarley, Daenerys Targaryen (Mother Of Dragons), Rhaenyra Targaryen (The Black Queen), and Grey Worm. During this time, you will be able to use exalted tokens to summon these characters to your team, and there is no doubt that, alongside her dragons, Daenerys will make a formidable addition.

The Old Raven will not be the last major update to take place during December in Game of Thrones: Legends, as, from November 18, at 4 a.m. ET – December 16, at 2 a.m. ET, players can also dive into Crow of the Free Folk. In comparison, this one will feature Legendary Characters such as Jon Snow (Crow Of The Free Folk), Benjen Stark (First Ranger), Yoren (Recruiter of the Night's Watch), Ygritte (Peerless Archer Of The Free Folk), Mance Rayder (King Beyond The Wall) and Tormund Giantsbane (Champion Of The Free Folk).

It has been another jam-packed year for Game of Thrones: Legends players, and it seems as though the iOS and Android mobile game is here to stay. With monthly updates and even synchronization with the shows when they are airing, it may just be the ultimate Game of Thrones experience.
