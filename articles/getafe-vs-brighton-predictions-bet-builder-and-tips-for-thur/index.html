Getafe vs Brighton predictions: Bet builder and tips for Thursday’s Conference League tie

TheTools · 05.11.2026 21:29 · source · permalink

Liam Flin’s tips for Thursday’s Conference League tie between Getafe and Brighton at the Estadio Coliseum include a 7/1 bet builder

Following their hard-fought win over Manchester City on Saturday, Brighton are in Conference League action again tonight, taking on Getafe at the Estadio Coliseum (kick-off, 8pm GMT).

Unsurprisingly, Brighton are the favourites to win at 8/15 after a superb start to the season, while the hosts are 9/2 to record an eye-catching victory and 11/4 to hold the Seagulls to a draw.

I cannot see past another positive result for the Seagulls and both my tips favour them at odds of 7/5 and 5/4. I have also put together a bet builder with total odds of more than 7/1.

Brighton can grind out another win

Brighton have rightfully earned a reputation as one of the most entertaining teams in the Premier League under Fabian Hurzeler but it was their defensive heroics that got them over the line in the 1-0 win at Manchester City on Saturday.

Despite City recording 63 touches in the opposition box and having 21 shots in the game, Brighton limited clear-cut chances for the Citizens to see out a memorable win.

To those who have followed Brighton closely this season, that is not really a surprise.

Hurzeler’s men have recorded the joint-highest number of clean sheets in the Premier League this term, shutting out their opponents in six of their nine games, helping them to achieve the best goal difference (+17) in the division.

They have kept the door shut in both of their Conference League matches this season, smashing Kauno Zalgiris 4-0 on matchday one before a 0-0 draw with Jablonec in their first away game.

Brighton were not particularly troubled in that match and racked up 26 shots, so they were a touch unfortunate not to get more from the game.

I considered a bet on them to just keep a clean sheet against Getafe at around evens, but it makes sense to go the step further and back them at 7/5 to win to nil given the attacking depth they have at their disposal.

The Getafe squad is more stretched and that has started to show recently with a 5-2 defeat at home to Lugano in this competition and a 4-0 league defeat at Athletic Bilbao last month.

Those were uncharacteristic of the side under Jose Bordalas, who tries to make his teams tough to break down, and their league games have still averaged just 2.18 goals per game.

They have drawn a blank in five of their 11 league fixtures this season and could be shut out by in-form Brighton tonight.

Selection: Brighton to win to nil – 7/5

 

Hinshelwood could make the difference

There are no guarantees when it comes to team selections for this game with both Getafe and Brighton set to make changes.

But there are suggestions Jack Hinshelwood could be set for a run-out in midfield, perhaps playing in a more advanced role, and I like him at 5/4 to either score or assist.

Hinshelwood’s game time has been limited this season but he has started both of Brighton’s games in this competition, contributing one goal and managing six shots, including four on target.

In just 146 minutes of action in the Premier League this term, he has scored three goals and last season he contributed four goals and three assists in the top flight.

He could be one of Brighton’s most dangerous players in this one, so the odds-against price about him scoring or assisting  is worth taking.

Selection: Jack Hinshelwood to score or assist – 5/4

 

Tricky Terrats a foul magnet for Getafe

I have picked out four legs for this match bet builder, which has odds totalling nearly 8/1.

To start I have kept things simple with under 3.5 goals, given Getafe’s games are traditionally low-scoring and Brighton are tight at the back. This has clicked in five of Brighton’s last eight games.

Next, while I think Brighton should edge the game, Getafe could look for opportunities from set pieces and I am adding in the hosts to have four or more corners.

Getafe have racked up 19 corners in just two Conference League matches this term and average 4.83 per game at home in La Liga.

I also think Getafe could catch the attention of referee Filip Glova. They have been pretty disciplined in the Conference League, but against stronger opponents they can lose their cool, as demonstrated by their division-high 35 yellow cards and five reds in La Liga this season.

Against a team who move the ball as well as Brighton, they could be forced into some cynical challenges, so back them to receive three or more cards.

Finish the bet builder with Getafe’s midfielder Ramon Terrats to win three or more fouls. Terrats has been winning fouls consistently across all competitions this season.

He is among the most fouled players in La Liga, winning an average of 2.7 per game, while no player has been fouled more times than Terrats (11) across the first two Conference League matchdays.

Selection: Bet builder - under 3.5 goals; Getafe to take 4+ corners; Getafe to receive 3+ cards; Ramon Terrats to win 3+ fouls – 7.91/1
