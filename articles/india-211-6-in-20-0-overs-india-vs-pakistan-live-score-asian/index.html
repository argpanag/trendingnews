India 211/6 in 20.0 Overs | India vs Pakistan Live Score, Asian Games Cricket Final: Tilak Varma, Shivam Dube's late hitting powers India to 211/6 vs Pakistan

TheTools · 03.10.2026 06:15 · source · permalink

India finish in style - A final on the Asian stage against arch-rivals Pakistan, a fantastic half-century from Tilak Varma - the script sounds familiar, doesn't it? History has repeated itself, albeit with a twist. In the 2025 Asia Cup, Tilak's match-defining contribution came while chasing; here, he has finished with a flourish to, dare we say, bat Pakistan out of the final. It was complete demolition in the last four overs, with the Men in Blue smashing 69 runs, and on a surface like this, they will feel they have their fingers firmly on the gold medal.

Ahmed Daniyal to Tilak Varma, DROPPED! Seemed to be going over, but Sufyan Moqim did reach it, and he'll be disappointed to have put it down. Tilak Varma gets a single, and India finish with 211/6 on the board. Ahmed Daniyal hits a hard length and bowls it slightly wide of the off stump. Varma makes room to go over the off side, but slices it off the outside edge over short third, where Moqim puts in a late leap over his right shoulder and spills it, allowing the batters to cross.

Ahmed Daniyal to Tilak Varma, Excellent running, and the throw isn't the best either. Tilak Varma gets to his FIFTY off just 21 balls. Slower and shorter, wide outside off. Varma leans back and across to the off side, angling the pull through square leg for two runs. This has been a momentum-shifting knock from Tilak.

Ahmed Daniyal to Tilak Varma, SIX! That's a shot and a half from Varma. Good length on middle, Varma collapses the back knee a bit and swings it handsomely behind square leg for another biggie. That was more of a wallop from Varma, and a classy one as well.

Ahmed Daniyal to Tilak Varma, SIX! Ah, a bit unlucky for the bowler. A sharp bumper, right around the front shoulder. Tilak Varma is rushed as he tries to pull and gets a top edge that flies down to fine leg, pretty fine as well and goes over the fence. 200 up for India.

Ahmed Daniyal to Axar Patel, Back of a length, angled into the batter. Axar Patel stays back and tucks it down through mid-wicket for a single.

Ahmed Daniyal to Shivam Dube, OUT! CAUGHT! A bit of respite for Pakistan, but again, the damage has already been done. A well-directed bumper from Ahmed Daniyal, dug in right around the batter's face, and a bit slower too. Shivam Dube takes it on and swivels around to pull it down to deep mid-wicket. Maaz Sadaqat is just inside the ropes and judges the flight of the ball well, taking it to his left whilst staying away from the ropes. India are 195/6 with 5 balls remaining in the innings.

Ahmed Daniyal will bowl the final over.

Saim Ayub to Tilak Varma, A bit of cat and mouse between the batter and the bowler. Varma moves around in the crease and Ayub floats it well down leg. Varma ends up shovelling it wide of Sufyan Moqim at short fine leg for a couple of runs.

Saim Ayub to Tilak Varma, FOUR! That's gone through as well. Full and attacking the stumps, Varma goes for the reverse and hits it hard, along the turf and to the left of the diving fielder at backward point for a boundary. 21 off the over with a ball remaining.

Saim Ayub to Shivam Dube, Dragged down outside off, turning away from the left-hander. Dube reaches across and heaves it flat and down to long on for a single.

Saim Ayub to Shivam Dube, FOUR! Through the off side now, and things are going from bad to worse for Pakistan. Fired in, full and outside off. Dube shows quick hands as he jams his bat down and smacks it between cover and point for a boundary.

Saim Ayub to Shivam Dube, SIX! Wow, that's been clobbered by Dube. Ayub overcorrects and bowls it shorter, angling it into the batter. Dube lays into it and whacks it over deep mid-wicket for a biggie. The 50-run stand is up between these two as well.

Saim Ayub to Shivam Dube, SIX! BANG! Tossed up outside off, Shivam Dube skips down the pitch and flat-bats it over long off to make it three sixes in a row for India. This is great acceleration at the death.

Saim Ayub will bowl the penultimate over.

Sufyan Moqim to Tilak Varma, SIX! In the slot and out of here. Floated up on middle, Tilak Varma takes the front leg out again and hits through the line of the ball, swinging it back over the bowler's head for another biggie. 18 off the over, and 190 is well in sight for India right now.

Sufyan Moqim to Tilak Varma, SIX! Smoked! A very full ball, drifted into the pads. Tilak Varma clears the front leg and thwacks it away in front of mid-wicket, sending the ball sailing over the ropes.

Sufyan Moqim to Shivam Dube, Lobbed up on off, enticing the batter to go after it. Dube extends the long levers but fails to get under the ball, hitting it down to long on for just a single.

Sufyan Moqim to Tilak Varma, A bit fuller and around off, punched away through the cover region for a single.

Sufyan Moqim to Tilak Varma, On a length around off, turning away from the left-hander. Varma tries to reverse sweep it over short third but misses.

Sufyan Moqim to Tilak Varma, FOUR! Straight back past the bowler! Tossed up, very full and outside off. Tilak Varma drills it down the ground and Sufyan Moqim fails to react in time, unable to get down to his left and the ball races away to the fence.

Abrar Ahmed to Shivam Dube, Flatter one, sliding it quickly across the batter. Dube tries to play a late cut but the ball keeps low and beats the bat.

Abrar Ahmed to Shivam Dube, FOUR! A bit more agricultural from Dube. Short and over leg, Dube uses his muscles to swing it away down to the square leg fence for another boundary.

Abrar Ahmed to Shivam Dube, WIDE! Oh, that's barely carried through. Slower and into the pitch, way outside off. Shivam Dube goes chasing after it but misses. 150 up for India now.

Abrar Ahmed to Tilak Varma, Good length, angled in from outside off. Varma plays a short-arm jab off the back foot and down to deep mid-wicket. The batters look for two but will have to settle with the single.

Abrar Ahmed to Shivam Dube, Dube thinks about stepping out, but Abrar bowls it quick and flat. Dube slams the brakes and punches it down to long on for a run.

Abrar Ahmed to Tilak Varma, Nagging length, drifted well into the batter. Varma backs away and hits it through wide mid on for a single.

Abrar Ahmed to Tilak Varma, FOUR! Varma bisects the fielders in the deep to perfection. Floated up wide outside off, Varma lunges across and drags the sweep aerially, hitting it between deep backward square leg and deep mid-wicket for a boundary.

Saim Ayub to Shivam Dube, The streak of singles is broken with a dot ball, but the boundaries have completely dried up at the moment. Flatter delivery, fired into the pads, and Tilak Varma nurdles it down to short fine leg.

Saim Ayub to Tilak Varma, Make that nine singles on the trot. Floated up on leg stump, Varma works it away in front of square leg and picks up a run.

Saim Ayub to Shivam Dube, Pushed into the legs again, gripping and turning yet again. Dube fends it away onto the on side and rotates strike. A string of eight singles in a row now for India.

Saim Ayub to Tilak Varma, Drifted into the pads, grips a bit and turns back towards the left-hander Varma dabs it around the corner on the leg side for a single.

Saim Ayub to Shivam Dube, Slower one, on a dragged length, turning from leg to middle. Dube plays it late, with gentle hands and in front of square leg for one more.

Saim Ayub to Tilak Varma, Ayub returns and starts with a gentle full toss on middle. Varma clips it to mid-wicket and picks up a single.

Abrar Ahmed to Tilak Varma, Looped up, full and on off, Tilak Varma turns it to the leg side and sets off for the run. Abrar Ahmed quickly moves to his left to get to the ball, picks it up, and scores a direct hit on the turn at the batter's end. However, the replay confirms that Dube has dragged his bat inside.

Abrar Ahmed to Shivam Dube, Flatter, shortish and on off, turns in sharply. Shivam Dube gets stuck in his crease and just hangs his bat out. It takes the inside edge and rolls towards square leg for one.

Abrar Ahmed to Tilak Varma, Drops it short, on middle, Tilak Varma punches it down to long on for another run.

Abrar Ahmed to Shivam Dube, Flighted, full and on leg, Shivam Dube clips it towards deep mid-wicket or a single.
