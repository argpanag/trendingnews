Joel Dommett turns harrowing lowest moment into new comedy drama

TheTools · 17.09.2026 11:20 · source · permalink

Joel Dommett is revisiting his catfish sex scandal - for a TV show based on his own life.

The comedian and TV presenter, who hosts ITV's The Masked Singer, is writing a comedy drama based on his experience of being blackmailed in 2016, several years after being lured into making an online video in which he performed a solo sex act.

Joel, 41, explained: “They say you should write about what you know so basically I am focusing on my own story at various points in my life and going into detail about the catfish scenario, the attempt to extort money from me and what happened there.

“I think it’s a really interesting story to tell and I’m actually finding it a cathartic, therapeutic experience to write about a decade on from when it happened even though, at the time, it could have finished my career.”

A decade ago the star, host of last week’s National Television Awards, was tempted to engage in online sexual activity by someone who pretended to be a woman called Staci Taylor. Several years later, just as Joel was about to receive his major TV break in the 2016 series of I’m A Celebrity… Get Me Out Of Here!, the creators of ‘Staci Taylor’ attempted to blackmail him and posted naked pictures online of the future star performing Skype sex.

In a further twist to the story, Joel then met with the woman whose face had been used by the extortioners to blackmail him, called Natalia, and they dated for six months.

The funnyman, who is now married to model Hannah Dommett-Cooper and father to two sons, added: ‘I’m part way through writing the scripts for the show and won’t be rushing to submit them to a TV company - I would prefer to have a really good product before I give it to people. But I love the idea of doing a comedy drama about my own life with myself in it.”

Joel says he hasn’t settled on a title for the series, joking: "Who knows what it will be called? I Got Caught With My D**k Out, probably that!”

Speaking about the experience on Davina McCall’s Begin Again podcast earlier this year, Joel said he was glad he hadn’t paid off the blackmailers just as he was starting on the I'm a Celebrity, Get Me Out of Here spin-off show.

“I could have been sad and frustrated and thought ‘my career is over because however big I become, this thing is always going to be in the background’. This was always going to be there as this skeleton in a closet if I don’t sort it now.

“I also knew - I’m the victim here! It’s not like I cheated on my wife or did anything bad. It was a very new crime. I went to the police, they didn’t know what to do with it.”

In the end he dealt with the situation by basing his Edinburgh stand-up show on it that year. “I thought, you know what, it is horrible what’s happened but also, it’s pretty funny.” Speaking about Natalia, who was half Russian, he laughed that he’d ended up having Skype sex with her for real after she went to live abroad.
