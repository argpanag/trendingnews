Barry Manilow's return to Las Vegas lasted exactly one full show.

The 83-year-old has canceled his Friday and Saturday concerts at the Westgate Las Vegas after coming down with bronchitis, a rep confirmed. "After a triumphant welcome-back concert on Thursday, Barry got hit with bronchitis and had to cancel his Friday and Saturday shows," the rep said.

That word carries extra weight for him. Bronchitis is what he'd just gotten over late last year when his doctor ordered the MRI that found a cancerous spot on his lung.

The rep's statement calls Friday canceled, but a Las Vegas entertainment blog reported that Manilow did take the stage that night. He left after two songs, telling the crowd, "Talk amongst yourselves," the blog said.

The band kept playing for a bit, according to the blog, before an announcement came over the speakers: "Due to circumstances beyond our control, that will be the conclusion of tonight's show."

It's the second time since August that the "Copacabana" singer has left that stage early. In August, he cut a show short and canceled two more.

Thursday Was Supposed to Be the Comeback

Thursday was his first show in six weeks.

"This is the first time back in a long time. I have some medical problems, but I'm back!" he told the crowd, according to a report from that night.

A couple of fans tossed underwear onstage. He held up a pink lacy thong and joked, "I'll keep this pair," then added, "God, I feel like I'm a teenage idol again."

He'd stepped away in August after a summer run of "29 shows in 68 days," he told fans at the time. "But it seems that we were pushing a little too hard," he said.

Manilow shared his diagnosis on Dec. 22, 2025. "As many of you know I recently went through six weeks of bronchitis followed by a relapse of another five weeks," he wrote.

He was over it and back onstage at the Westgate when his doctor ordered an MRI "just to make sure that everything was OK." It found "a cancerous spot on my left lung that needs to be removed."

He had surgery and made his first public appearance afterward in April. He was back performing by June.

As of Saturday afternoon, the Westgate's show page still lists his next dates as Oct. 15 to 17. The rep's statement didn't say whether those shows are affected.

His Westgate calendar runs through Dec. 19.
