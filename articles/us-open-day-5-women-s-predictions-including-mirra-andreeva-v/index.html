US Open Day 5 Women's Predictions Including Mirra Andreeva vs Eva Lys - Last Word On Tennis

TheTools · 02.09.2026 22:27 · source · permalink

Second round action at the 2026 US Open continues on Thursday with Mirra Andreeva and Elena Rybakina in action. Our panelists offer their predictions and previews for every matchup from the BJK national tennis center.

Elena Rybakina vs Jessica Bouzas Maneiro

Tope:
Bouzas Maneiro rode a break-point clinic at the US Open past Vidmanova to reach round two, but round three brings a far sterner test in an in-form Rybakina, who barely broke a sweat against Frodin and can climb to world #1 with a deep run. The Spaniard has never beaten the Kazakh, and history says this one won’t be close.

Prediction: Rybakina in 2

Ateet:
Elena Rybakina played a rare match in which her opponent served bigger than her. 17-year-old Thea Frodin hit 11 aces in the match against Rybakina and showed promise for the future, but the Kazakh was better in every other category. Jessica Bouzas Maneiro is consistent but doesn’t have outright weapons to trouble the second seed.

Prediction: Rybakina in 2

Steen: Rybakina chases a world #1 ranking depending on the outcome of the US Open. 43-13 on the year, she doesn’t lose often. Bouzas Maneiro is below .500 on the season and still outside the top 100, a significant fall from last year. It would be a shock to see an upset here.

Prediction: Rybakina in 2

Mirra Andreeva vs Eva Lys

Tope:
Mirra Andreeva cruised past Janice Tjen 6-2 6-2 in just 68 minutes, dropping no break points en route, while Eva Lys battled back from a rough patch to see off qualifier Gabriela Knutson 6-3 6-3, saving 10 of 12 break chances. Andreeva now looks to carry that form into a stiff step-up test against Lys and enters as the clear favourite to advance.

Prediction: Lys in 2

Ateet:
Mirra Andreeva played a solid opening match against Janice Tjen, who had no answers for the Russian’s game. Andreeva didn’t face a break point all match and looks promising to make her best run here at the US Open. Eva Lys also made a strong start to her campaign, but, like last year, she will meet a very difficult opponent in the second round. Lys will put up a great fight, but Andreeva is the stronger player and should win.

Prediction: Andreeva in 2

Steen: Andreeva has climbed to #5 in the world with a 41-13 record on the season, Lys is just inside the top 100 and only has six tour level wins this year, including a win over a qualifier in the first round. This feels like a significant mismatch and Lys may not be able to sustain resistance for long.

Prediction: Andreeva in 2

Maria Sakkari vs Yuliia Starodubtseva

Tope:
Sakkari clawed back from a set and a break down and 14 break points faced to outlast Robin Montgomery, while Starodubtseva cruised past Seidel dropping just five games. Expect a battle, and the Ukrainian to stun her more accomplished opponent.

Prediction: Starodubtseva in 3

Ateet:
Maria Sakkari made a huge comeback from a set and double break down to win against Robin Montgomery. The Greek has been in good form lately, and losing in the first round here would have dented her confidence. Now that she has escaped, she could make a good run. Yuliia Starodubtseva got her first US Open main-draw win by easily defeating Ella Seidel in the first round. I expect Sakkari to raise her level in this one and get the win.

Prediction: Sakkari in 3

Steen: Sakkari has been a consistent player on tour these days but she’s off that elite level of the top players. Starodubtseva is having a career year this year, and has impressed, moving up to 19-18 at all levels this season. Starodubtseva is peaking at the right time.

Prediction: Starodubtseva in 3

Cristina Bucsa vs Himeno Sakatsume

Tope:
Cristina Bucsa enters as the higher-ranked favorite, but Himeno Sakatsume arrives red-hot off a statement upset over Anhelina Kalinina and a deep run through qualifying. Expect the Spaniard’s experience to edge out the Japanese underdog’s surging momentum in what should be a tighter battle than the rankings suggest.

Prediction: Bucsa in 2

Ateet:
Cristina Bucsa got a great win over the in-form Sara Bejlek in the first round. Bejlek loves pace on the ball, but Bucsa gave her none, and that turned out to be the difference. Bucsa has had a mediocre last couple of months, but now she has a good shot at making at least the third round. I will give her the edge against qualifier Himeno Sakatsume.

Prediction: Bucsa in 3

Steen: Bucsa has struggled this year but had an impressive first round win, Sakatsume rides the momentum from qualifying into the second round, despite having limited WTA experience at age 25. Sakatsume’s momentum matters here given Bucsa hasn’t been consistent in months.

Prediction: Sakatsume in 2
