NFL Week 5 bold predictions: Jahmyr Gibbs breaks Barry Sanders' record; 'Hawks punish Brock Purdy

TheTools · 11.10.2026 20:03 · source · permalink

NFL.com analysts provide bold predictions -- spanning everything from team-based takes to fantasy forecasts -- for Week 5 of the 2026 season.

NOTE: All statistics come courtesy of Next Gen Stats, unless otherwise noted.

Grant Gordon

Digital Content Editor

Jahmyr Gibbs bounces back in style, breaking Barry Sanders' franchise record

Held to 46 ground yards by the Panthers in last Sunday night's loss, Gibbs rebounds in a major -- and historic -- way against the Cardinals in Week 5. The Lions running back sprints past Sanders’ single-game franchise rushing record (237 yards) and also tallies four scrimmage touchdowns, guiding Detroit back into the win column.

Gennaro Filice

Editor at Large

It's a Seattle sack party at Brock Purdy's expense!

Purdy's off to an MVP-caliber start, having thrown an NFL-high 11 touchdown passes against just one interception (a pick that came on his third drive of the season). The quarterback is confidently dealing like a man without a care in the world -- which makes sense, considering the impenetrable fortress surrounding him in 2026. Four games in, the 49ers have yet to give up a sack. It's remarkable protection from the Trent Williams-led line, as no other team has allowed fewer than five sacks. But the dam breaks in San Francisco's first true road game of the season. Boasting the league's fourth-highest pressure rate at 43.0%, Seattle has a posse of pass rushers to rattle Purdy's cage. With the 12s cranking up the volume for this rivalry bout at Lumen Field, Purdy takes four sacks from four different Seahawks. And the Niners suffer their first loss.

Bobby Kownack

Digital Content Producer

Jalen Hurts’ only TD pass in London game goes to Travis Hunter

An Eagles squad in crisis will find no respite against the Jaguars’ second-ranked defense in points allowed. Saquon Barkley and DeVonta Smith are sidelined by hamstring injuries, and the team’s anchor at right tackle, Lane Johnson, retired on Tuesday. It’s not a good equation with Philly heading off to Europe to face a stingy, opportunistic defense that’s already tallied eight takeaways. Travis Hunter accounted for one of those with his first career interception in Week 3, and he brought down another pick in Week 4 that was wiped out by a roughing the passer penalty. His next INT comes Sunday -- this one stands, and he takes it to the house. Better yet, he’s the only player on either team to score a touchdown off a Jalen Hurts pass.

Matt Okada

NFL & Fantasy Content

Jameis Winston finds leading pass catchers for BIG day against division foe

Malik Nabers and Isaiah Likely are getting closer and closer to the unique page Winston works on. In Week 4, the pass-catching duo combined for 13 catches (on 19 targets) for 178 yards and a score. Going against a Commanders defense that has allowed 970 passing yards and 11 touchdown passes through the first month, Nabers and Likely take it to another level on Sunday, combining for 300 yards and three scores in yet another Giants win.

Raza Umerani

Seasonal Fantasy Football Researcher

The Carnell Tate breakout continues

Last week, the fourth overall pick set career highs with 28 routes, 12 targets, nine receptions and 145 yards against the Ravens. This week, against a Texans team allowing the second-most yards to wide receivers, Tate will one-up himself and go for over 10 catches and 150 yards while reaching the end zone for the first time in his young career. But it won't be enough to get Tennessee its first win of the season.

Brooke Cersosimo

Senior Staff Editor

Jets rookie doubles sack total against Browns

David Bailey leads all rookies with two sacks and 17 quarterback pressures entering Week 5. The second overall pick takes advantage of a banged-up Browns offensive line and doubles his sack total, taking down Deshaun Watson twice on Sunday at Metlife Stadium.
