Rain worsens situation in UP, 12 killed: Police announce, ‘Do not step out’; storm and rain continue across state for 45 hours - Uttar Pradesh News

Heavy rain has worsened the situation across Uttar Pradesh, with downpours continuing for the past 45 hours. Winds are blowing at 40–50 kmph, turning roads into waterlogged stretches and flooding residential colonies. Fallen trees and electricity poles have blocked several roads. At least 12 people have died, and eight have been injured in rain-related incidents.

The risk of flooding has increased in 32 districts. Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath has urged people not to step out unnecessarily and to take care of their families, assuring them that the government is standing with them.

In Lucknow, water has entered homes, including kitchens and bedrooms. People were seen riding bikes and bicycles through waist-deep water in the streets, while many remained stranded at crossings after failing to find cabs.

In Bareilly and Fatehpur, 2–3 feet of water has accumulated in streets and neighbourhoods. Hundreds of bighas of paddy crops have been flattened in Deoria and Barabanki, with fields inundated. In Chitrakoot, the Mandakini River is flowing above the danger mark. The DM and SP are making announcements asking people to close their shops and return home if the water level rises further.

Three people each died after walls collapsed in Lakhimpur and Barabanki, while an 8-year-old boy was killed in Fatehpur. In Agra, a speeding car skidded and overturned amid the rain, killing three people. In Lakhimpur, an Ertiga collided with a trolley and fell into a ditch, killing two people on the spot.

Varanasi BHU meteorologist Prof. Manoj Srivastava said the weather has deteriorated under the impact of Cyclone ‘Arnab’, which formed over the Bay of Bengal. He said similar weather conditions are likely to continue until tomorrow.
