"It's up to us to prove it" — Rose urges Cherries to maintain momentum

TheTools · 18.10.2026 14:26 · source · permalink

MARCO Rose said his Cherries side must prove that they can keep the momentum going after their 3-0 win over Sturm Graz in the Europa League as they host Sunderland in the Premier League on Sunday.

The boss got the reaction he wanted from his team after their heavy defeat to Chelsea last weekend by beating their Austrian visitors, but he will want his side to carry that into the Sunderland game.
