Mostly sunny and dry for Sunday; Marine hazards continue

WEST PALM BEACH, Fla. — Our early taste of fall-like weather continues through Sunday.

Drier air across the area will keep rain chances almost non-existent on Sunday.

Highs will still reach the mid and upper 80s Sunday, but the bigger change will come overnight. Wake-up temps Sunday morning will range from the low and mid-60s near Lake Okeechobee, with upper 60s and lower 70s elsewhere. West Palm Beach and Boca Raton are forecast to fall to around 70 degrees. Temps will stay in the low and mid 70s at the immediate coastline

MORE WEATHER: Radar | Alerts | 7-Day Forecast | Hourly Forecast

Hazardous conditions will remain along the Atlantic coast.

A northerly to northeasterly swell generated by a strong system over the northeastern United States will keep seas around 6 to 8 feet through Sunday morning. A High Rip Current Risk remains in effect along Atlantic beaches through Sunday evening.

The king-tide cycle is also increasing the coastal flooding threat.

A Coastal Flood Advisory is in effect for coastal Palm Beach County through Sunday afternoon. The National Weather Service says observed water levels have recently been running higher than forecast guidance, increasing the possibility of minor to moderate coastal flooding around high tide.

Residents and visitors near vulnerable coastal areas should be prepared for water covering some low-lying locations during peak high-tide cycles.
