Quick Answer

The EAAA India Alternatives IPO opens for subscription today, 26 October 2026, and will remain open until 28 October 2026. EAAA India Alternatives Limited is the Edelweiss group's alternative asset manager, and the issue of about Rs 1,200 crore is entirely an offer for sale of 72,07,207 shares, so the company receives no proceeds. The price band is set at Rs 1,585 to Rs 1,665 per share, with a lot size of 9 shares, and the shares are proposed to list on BSE and NSE. Since bidding has only just opened, subscription figures will start reflecting real demand as the days progress, so investors should track the live numbers through the session before deciding.

EAAA India Alternatives Limited, the alternative investment management platform of the Edelweiss group, has opened its IPO for subscription today, 26 October 2026. The EAAA India Alternatives IPO is a mainboard, book built issue worth about Rs 1,200 crore, made up entirely of an offer for sale of 72,07,207 equity shares by Edelweiss Securities and Investments, and it will remain open until 28 October 2026. Since the issue is a pure offer for sale, the company will not receive any of the proceeds.

EAAA works across real assets and private credit, with assets under management of about Rs 95,345 crore as of 30 June 2026. Ahead of the opening, the anchor book on 23 October raised Rs 360 crore, with mutual funds taking about Rs 216.51 crore, foreign portfolio investors about Rs 77.99 crore and alternative investment funds about Rs 65.50 crore. The shares are proposed to be listed on BSE and NSE.

EAAA India Alternatives IPO Share Reservation

The issue offers not more than 50 percent of the net offer to qualified institutional buyers, not less than 15 percent to non-institutional investors and not less than 35 percent to retail investors. The anchor book of Rs 360 crore was completed on 23 October ahead of public bidding.

About EAAA India Alternatives Limited

EAAA India Alternatives Limited, incorporated in May 2008, is an alternative investment management platform focused on India's real assets and private credit markets, with a wholly owned subsidiary, Sekura India Management Limited, and 277 employees as of 30 June 2026. Total income was Rs 584.25 crore in FY24, Rs 786.54 crore in FY25 and Rs 963.76 crore in FY26, while profit after tax rose from Rs 175.16 crore to Rs 229.78 crore and Rs 265.20 crore. For the quarter ended June 2026, total income was Rs 336.13 crore and profit after tax Rs 80.71 crore. Promoters hold about 95.03 percent before the issue and about 83.88 percent after it, and the upper band implies a market capitalisation of about Rs 10,765 crore.

Conclusion

The issue has opened for bidding today, 26 October 2026, offering exposure to an alternative asset manager with rising income and profit through a pure offer for sale. Investors considering the issue should weigh the valuation of about 33 to 41 times earnings, the dependence on fund performance and fundraising, and the fact that the company receives no proceeds, and read the RHP on bseindia.com before applying after independent due diligence.
