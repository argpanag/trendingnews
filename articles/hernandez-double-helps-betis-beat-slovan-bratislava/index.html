Hernandez double helps Betis beat Slovan Bratislava

Cucho Hernandez is Real Betis' top scorer in the Champions League this season with three goals

Cucho Hernandez scored twice as Real Betis bounced back to winning ways in the Champions League with a 2-0 victory over Slovan Bratislava.

The Spanish side had won their three opening matches in this season's competition before suffering a first defeat by Borussia Dortmund earlier this month.

Hernandez opened the scoring just before half-time when Angel Ortiz floated a cross in at the back post and he leapt highest to head past Dominik Takac.

And the Colombia international added a second in the 57th minute, glancing a header in at the near post from Isco's corner.

The win boosts Betis' hopes of a top-eight finish to qualify automatically for the knockout stage, with Manuel Pellegrini's side sitting fourth in the table on 12 points from a possible 15.

Their next Champions League match is at home to an in-form Como on Wednesday, 9 December. Slovan Bratislava, who have lost all five games in the competition with an aggregate goal difference of -18, face Shakhtar Donetsk on the same day as they continue their search for a first point in Europe this season.
