Bournemouth news: Celta Vigo up next as Marco Rose's side target top-eight finish in Europa League

TheTools · 24.11.2026 17:16 · source · permalink
  • Published
    8 hours ago

It is time for Bournemouth's travelling fans to get their passports out again this week as Marco Rose's side, having extended their winning run to nine successive games by disposing of Nottingham Forest on Saturday, are at Celta Vigo on Thursday.

It is the third of the Cherries' four away fixtures in the league phase of the Europa League, and their last such trip of 2026.

Those nine wins are a new club record, beating eight-game sequences in 1998-99 and 2012-13, which were both achieved when the Dorset club were in English football's third tier.

And there will be a further opportunity to break new ground at the Estadio Municipal de Balaidos on Thursday.

In conquering Real Sociedad, Sturm Graz, AC Milan and Sparta Prague, Bournemouth became only the second English team to win their first four European matches, matching Liverpool's feat in the 1964-65 European Cup.

That Liverpool side, under Bill Shankly, won both legs of their opening games against Reykjavik-based KR and Anderlecht - but could not make it five in five as they were held to a goalless draw in Cologne in the first leg of their quarter-final.

Whether or not the Cherries can record a fifth European win (and a 10th in all competitions), their target must be a top-eight Europa League finish, which would allow them to bypass the knockout play-off round and earn a free pass to the last 16.

In the two seasons since the 36-team league phase was introduced, 15 points was sufficient to guarantee a top-eight place in 2024-25, while 17 was the figure needed last season.

And with 12 points from 12 so far, that should be an eminently realistic target for Bournemouth, given that their last four games are all against teams in the bottom 10 places in the table at the halfway mark.
