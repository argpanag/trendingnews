Haaland: Fighting spirit against Napoli was important

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:04 · source · permalink

Rasmus Højlund opened the scoring for the Italian side before Ayyoub Bouaddi rattled in his first City goal to level.

Noa Lang volleyed the visitors back ahead before Enzo Maresca’s side fought back a second time to level through a splendid Rayan Cherki effort.

Despite not picking up all three points at the Etihad Stadium, Haaland revealed in his post-match interview with City Studios that the spirit shown was important.

“I think it’s important to do that,” he said. “But we have to find out things. 

“We have to perform better and we have to perform better as a group because in the end we want to win games. 

“Both home and away, but especially at home all you want to do is play good football and perform in front of the fans.

“It’s important at all times to stick together.”
