Three passengers hospitalized after cabin fumes cause emergency landing of Boston-bound Delta flight, airline says - The Boston Globe

TheTools · 29.09.2026 08:05 · source · permalink

Three passengers aboard a Delta Air Lines flight from Barcelona to Boston were hospitalized in Porto, Portugal, where the plane made an emergency landing on Sunday after fumes were detected in the cabin, the airline said.

Delta officials said Monday that the three passengers had been discharged from the hospital. Two flight crew members also sought medical attention when the plane landed, officials said.

Officials said the airline is working to help the 283 passengers on board the flight reach their final destination.

“Delta flight 251 from Barcelona to Boston diverted to Porto, Portugal following a reported mechanical issue,” Delta spokesperson Grant Myatt said in a statement. “The flight landed safely, and we apologize to our customers for the delay in their travels as we work to get them to Boston as safely and quickly as possible.”

According to FlightAware tracking data, the flight took off from Barcelona-El Prat Airport around 3:15 p.m. and landed at Porto Airport three hours and 44 minutes later.

Local emergency personnel met the aircraft upon arrival, airline officials said.

Portuguese newspaper Jornal de Notícias reported that as many as 14 passengers received medical attention on the tarmac. Local medical officials could not immediately be reached for confirmation.

The emergency landing took place just days after a Delta flight departing Logan International Airport for London had to turn around on Thursday night following reports of an odor on board. No further details were available on Monday, but officials confirmed the incident occurred on a different type of aircraft.

Delta officials added that smoke, fume, and odor events are rare but something the airline takes seriously. These odors can originate from many different sources, they said.
