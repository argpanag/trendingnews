Jason Statham & Scott Eastwood's (Very) Intense 119-Minute Action Thriller Is a Global Streaming Hit

TheTools · 20.11.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

One of Jason Statham's best action movies from the last ten years has become one of the biggest streaming hits in the world. Co-starring modern action star and the son of a Hollywood icon, Scott Eastwood, as well as a whole roster of familiar faces, the 119-minute film offered a different take on the classic loner Statham is known for playing. Only this time, there’s a very good reason why he’s so quiet and emotionless.

Jason Statham vs. Scott Eastwood – What More Can You Ask For?

The film is Wrath of Man, Guy Ritchie's brilliant remake of the 2004 French thriller Le Convoyeur. Using a Tarantino-esque narrative, Wrath of Man tells the story of H, a new guard at Fortico, an armored-truck company. The mysteriously quiet new guard barely passes his tests, and though his colleagues make fun of his poor skills, H shows who he really is during an attack. The story then jumps back to reveal H’s real identity. As it turns out, there is a reason he wanted to become a Fortico guard. He is trying to uncover the identity of the thief whose crew infiltrated the company and who was responsible for a horrific event in H's past.

Wrath of Man marks Statham and Ritchie’s fourth project together, after Lock, Stock and Two Smoking Barrels, Snatch, and Revolver. Joining the action duo is Scott Eastwood, who has already established his action credentials in movies such as The Fate of the Furious and The Outpost, among others. Joining Eastwood and Statham are Holt McCallany, Josh Hartnett, Jeffrey Donovan, Andy Garcia, Laz Alonso, and Niamh Algar, among others.

In Wrath of Man, Eastwood plays Jan, a villain who goes head-to-head with Statham. He embodies a character driven by malice, making him a fitting counterpart to Statham's mysterious character. Sure, Jan isn't the only villain, but he is the most prominent and the one H is most determined to confront. Amid all the action sequences – some of the best in the Ritchie-Statham catalog – the characters have plenty of emotional depth, and the stakes end up feeling personal.

The intense action thriller arrived in theaters in May 2021 after delays due to the COVID-19 pandemic. Despite a rocky release schedule, it grossed over $104 million against a $40 million production budget. Not only that, the film earned a positive reception from critics, and it currently has a 68% critics' score on Rotten Tomatoes. The audience score is quite high, at 90%, to no one's surprise.

Jason Statham Remains on the Iron Throne of Streaming

Statham has already released two action movies this year, and though they haven't been box office hits, they have still found an audience on streaming. Shelter has been a fixture on the charts since its release in January 2026. Mutiny, on the other hand, got off to a rocky start, especially after an online leak spoiled the movie for fans eagerly waiting for its theatrical release. It also had a poor performance at the box office, but after its digital debut, the film has attracted renewed attention.

Once again, Statham is the unbeatable king of streaming. Whenever he releases a new action movie, it instantly climbs the charts, and his older hits regularly show up in the rankings of the most popular movies. Five years after proving that the Ritchie-Statham formula still works, Wrath of Man has become a global sensation and his latest hit.

According to the latest rankings update, Wrath of Man sits at No. 4 on Starz's global chart, with Statham's very own The Beekeeper holding the No. 1 slot on the same platform. Never doubt who belongs on the iron throne of streaming: the one and only action legend known as The Stath.
