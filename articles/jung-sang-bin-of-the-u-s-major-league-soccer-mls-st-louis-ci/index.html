Jung Sang-bin of the U.S. Major League Soccer (MLS) St. Louis City SC scored against his former team.

Jung Sang-bin started the away game against Minnesota United FC at Allianz Field on the 18th (Korea Standard Time) and played for 69 minutes, scoring one goal with an expected score (xG) of 0.21.

He scored his team's second goal in the 10th minute of the first half. Rafael Navarro broke through the opponent's defense on the left side, and Jung Sang-bin calmly kicked the pass he gave away with his right foot.

It was a 2-0 lead following Navarro's goal in the fourth minute of the first half. Third point of the season.

Even after scoring a goal, Jung Sang-bin refrained from celebrating and showed respect for his former team, which he had been involved with for three years from 2023.

St. Louis led the game with two goals early in the game, but it was a difficult game when it lost the atmosphere of the game to home team Minnesota.

Ajiel Jackson scored a comeback goal in the fourth minute of the second half. Kelvin Yebo-ah's cross from the right was connected with a right-footed shot to break the net.

Two minutes later, Jung Sang-bin, who participated in the defense, made a dangerous foul against his old teammate Will Trap and received a warning card.

After that, Minnesota's unilateral offensive continued, eventually allowing Yeboa to score an equalizer in the 13th minute of the second half. Mauricio Gonzalez's cross from the left side was caught by Yebo-ah once and connected with a right-footed shot.

Shortly after the loss, St. Louis replaced Jung Sang-bin and Simon Betcher and put in Damien Downs and Lucas McNaughton to reorganize the line, but failed to avoid an upset goal. In the 41st minute of the second half, Marcus Caldeira scored a come-from-behind goal in a counterattack.

In the end, the game ended in a 2-3 come-from-behind defeat. St. Louis ended its unbeaten streak of 21 games, with 14 wins, nine draws and seven losses and 51 points. It is still second in the Western Conference.

Minnesota rose to 10th place in the Western Conference with 8 wins, 10 draws and 12 losses and 34 points.

[San Francisco (USA) = KIM JAE HO, MK Sports Correspondent]
