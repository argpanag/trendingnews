6 Winners and 5 Losers from Italy

TheTools · 08.09.2026 10:58 · source · permalink

Kimi Antonelli delivered a drive for the ages as he powered through the field from 19th to take a sensational victory on home soil at Monza. But as the Italian sampled champagne and ate pasta from his trophy amid elation at Mercedes, it was a more sombre affair elsewhere in the paddock. Lawrence Barretto selects his winners and losers from an extraordinary Italian Grand Prix...

Winner: Kimi Antonelli

Kimi Antonelli delivered one of the most impressive drives Formula 1 has seen in decades as he recovered from 19th on the grid (following a suite of grid penalties for engine component changes) to assume the lead of the race in just 18 laps.
