Cortez Ratima selection sparks debate ahead of England rematch

TheTools · 28.11.2026 10:01 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Sport

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._v4.laWzzQVil69j0DGqQk4Ywh3JMAvc5jXoA1Fn8ldP8DY/sport/2824937%2Fcortez-ratima-selection-sparks-debate-ahead-of-england-rematch

Seldom in All Blacks history is there unanimous fan consensus following a team selection.

However, Friday morning's announcement of the side to take on England in the Twickenham sequel has prompted plenty of discussion over one position: halfback.

Dave Rennie made the surprise call to start Cortez Ratima in the No.9 jersey in Sunday morning's rematch against England.

His elevation pushes Cam Roigard, who has been the first choice for the past two years, to the bench while Kyle Preston remains on the outer.

The All Blacks social media channels posted the line-up across their pages, with the halfback position prompted plenty of debate among fans.

"Cortez Ratima instead of Kyle Preston????"

"Big hiding coming that half back pairing."

"Ratima, really?? Too slow getting to the ball."

"We all know he plays in slow motion."

However, Rennie has defended his decision, citing a combination of form and fatigue.

"Cam's had a great year, and he's started just about every test, but we're really happy with how Cortez has gone."

Ratima has played in seven tests this year, six off the bench.

"We thought he had a strong game against Wales and off the bench last week, and so we are keen for him to have a crack."

Despite his move to the bench, Rennie said Roigard can still have a significant impact on the game.

"Bringing Cam's experience on late in the game, we think it will work for us."

While the All Blacks were off the pace in almost every aspect of their record 37-12 loss to England last week, Rennie pointed to their kicking game as one area that needs improvement.

"We kicked poorly. Cam's been excellent, kicked really well in the Australian series. But I think one of them he was under a little bit of pressure and it was too deep. So, if we're going to kick, you either want to find grass or you kick somewhere where we can apply a bit of pressure. It's got to be better."

If the the All Blacks can't be better and fall to England again, it will mark the equal worst season performance since 1949, when the New Zealand side lost six matches.

"Last weekend hurt. We own that performance. We've been really honest with ourselves about where we fell short and now we have an awesome opportunity to go again against a quality England side," Rennie said.
