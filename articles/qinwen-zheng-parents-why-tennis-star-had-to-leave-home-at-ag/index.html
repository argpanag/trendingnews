Qinwen Zheng Parents- Why Tennis Star Had to Leave Home at Age 8

TheTools · 09.09.2026 16:19 · source · permalink

Qinwen Zheng’s journey to the pinnacle of women’s tennis is not just a testament to her natural talent but also to her relentless dedication and perseverance. The Chinese star’s strong performance is underpinned by a family saga of tough choices, financial sacrifices and years apart.

Zheng was born in Shiyan, Hubei, and started playing tennis at about seven years old. Her father, Zheng Jianping, was aware of her potential from an early age and was deeply involved in her development. The family made the difficult decision to send their young daughter away from home within months to receive better training.

Zheng left her parents behind and moved to Wuhan to train at the age of 8. The young player has said she cried and clung to her mother when she was separated from her.

Her mother, Deng Fang, would come to see her on weekends, but still work. Zheng has stated that the arrangement lasted for several years, and that her childhood was very different from that of most children her age.

The family eventually made an even bigger commitment to Zheng’s career. Her parents reportedly sold their family home to support her tennis development, showing the extent to which they were willing to invest in their daughter’s future.

Zheng’s mother also sacrificed a lot. Deng had been traveling to see her daughter for years, and after a few years, she quit her job and started to live with Zheng when the tennis star was about 13.

That decision provided Zheng with more stable support at a crucial time in her development. At the age of 17, another big move was made, Zheng and her mother moved to Europe. Zheng eventually moved to Barcelona and developed there.

Those sacrifices have become a part of Zheng’s story. Her parents’ early focus on tennis helped set her on the path to a career.
