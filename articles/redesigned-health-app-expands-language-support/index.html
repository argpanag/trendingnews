Redesigned Health app expands language support

TheTools · 03.11.2026 21:46 · source · permalink

Media

  • Text of this article

    November 3, 2026

    UPDATE

    Redesigned Health app expands language support

    Today, Apple’s redesigned Health app will expand language support to global English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), French, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, Turkish, and Vietnamese, bringing powerful, personalized health insights to millions of people worldwide.1 Built with Apple Intelligence at its core, the Health app dynamically adapts to an individual’s health metrics across iPhone and iPad, offering insight into how to optimize their day and a comprehensive view of long-term wellbeing.2, 3 The Health app has been built to securely and privately protect a user’s data at every step.

    The updated experience includes a new Insights tab that surfaces timely, holistic summaries spanning heart, sleep, readiness, fitness, vitals, and cycle tracking data throughout the day. A dedicated For You section delivers intelligent recommendations tailored to a user’s daily habits — such as practical guidance to adjust a wind-down routine following consecutive nights of interrupted sleep. The app pairs data with dynamic health content, offering educational videos and articles featuring Health app clinical experts.

    To help users better understand their health trajectory, a new Longevity tab analyzes longitudinal metrics across heart health, sleep, mental wellbeing, movement, metabolic health, hearing, and nutrition. Central to this tab is Health Age, a feature that translates data from Apple Watch — including VO2 max, resting heart rate, sleep, and heart rate variability — into a metric that reflects how a user’s body is tracking relative to their chronological age. Users can also incorporate clinical laboratory data, such as A1c and LDL cholesterol, to enrich their long-term health picture.

    Additionally, the update introduces at-home movement evaluations, which make physical assessments accessible without requiring specialized equipment. Using an iPhone camera and Apple Watch, users can measure their flexibility, strength, balance, movement mechanics, and VO2 max with real-time audio and visual feedback. Powered by on-device vision-based AI models and developed with clinical experts, the experience runs entirely on device; no video is ever recorded, stored, or shared.

    The redesigned Health app is available in the iOS 27.3 developer beta today and will roll out to users as a free software update later this year. Apple will continue to expand support for the redesigned Health app in new languages.

    1. The redesigned Health app will be available on Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone and iPad models with the latest software, with select features available only on iPhone. Some features may not be available in all regions or languages. Health Age requires Apple Watch. The test to estimate VO2 max requires Apple Watch, AirPods Pro 3, or a third-party heart rate sensing device. Some features require users to be 18 or older. For information on Apple Intelligence availability, visit support.apple.com/en-us/121115.
    2. Apple Intelligence is available with Siri and device language set to Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), Danish, Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, Swedish, Turkish, or Vietnamese. Some features may not be available in all regions or languages. Some devices may not be available in all regions. Certain Apple Intelligence features that rely on server-side models are subject to daily usage limits, including but not limited to Siri AI, intelligent photo editing tools, Image Playground, and AFM 3 Cloud models in Shortcuts. Daily limits may vary by feature, request complexity, system demand, system policies, and other factors. Expanded access to such features will be available for a fee in the future. Use of these features is subject to Apple Intelligence terms and conditions. Learn more at apple.com/apple-intelligence. Siri AI is not available for users under 13.
    3. Features are subject to change. Some features may not be available in all languages or regions, and availability may vary due to local laws and regulations. For more information about availability, visit apple.com.

UPDATE

Redesigned Health app expands language support

Today, Apple’s redesigned Health app will expand language support to global English, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), French, Japanese, Korean, Norwegian, Portuguese, Spanish, Turkish, and Vietnamese, bringing powerful, personalized health insights to millions of people worldwide.1 Built with Apple Intelligence at its core, the Health app dynamically adapts to an individual’s health metrics across iPhone and iPad, offering insight into how to optimize their day and a comprehensive view of long-term wellbeing.2, 3 The Health app has been built to securely and privately protect a user’s data at every step.

The updated experience includes a new Insights tab that surfaces timely, holistic summaries spanning heart, sleep, readiness, fitness, vitals, and cycle tracking data throughout the day. A dedicated For You section delivers intelligent recommendations tailored to a user’s daily habits — such as practical guidance to adjust a wind-down routine following consecutive nights of interrupted sleep. The app pairs data with dynamic health content, offering educational videos and articles featuring Health app clinical experts.

To help users better understand their health trajectory, a new Longevity tab analyzes longitudinal metrics across heart health, sleep, mental wellbeing, movement, metabolic health, hearing, and nutrition. Central to this tab is Health Age, a feature that translates data from Apple Watch — including VO2 max, resting heart rate, sleep, and heart rate variability — into a metric that reflects how a user’s body is tracking relative to their chronological age. Users can also incorporate clinical laboratory data, such as A1c and LDL cholesterol, to enrich their long-term health picture.

Additionally, the update introduces at-home movement evaluations, which make physical assessments accessible without requiring specialized equipment. Using an iPhone camera and Apple Watch, users can measure their flexibility, strength, balance, movement mechanics, and VO2 max with real-time audio and visual feedback. Powered by on-device vision-based AI models and developed with clinical experts, the experience runs entirely on device; no video is ever recorded, stored, or shared.

The redesigned Health app is available in the iOS 27.3 developer beta today and will roll out to users as a free software update later this year. Apple will continue to expand support for the redesigned Health app in new languages.
