‘It wasn’t spicy enough’: Jamie Oliver recalls Robert De Niro sending back his pasta

TheTools · 22.11.2026 02:58 · source · permalink

KUALA LUMPUR, Nov 21 — Jamie Oliver joined Robert De Niro on The Graham Norton Show last night with a story about cooking for the actor 30 years ago, when a plate of pasta came back to the kitchen at London's River Cafe.

London-based news site Bang Showbiz, said Oliver recalled being thrilled when De Niro ordered arrabbiata, a dish the chef felt he had mastered.

"He sent it back! It wasn't spicy enough apparently. I still love him!" Oliver reportedly told Norton.

De Niro appeared on the BBC One programme with Ben Stiller to discuss their film Focker-in-Law, while Oliver was there to talk about his cookbook 20-Minute Meals.

Oliver said some of his happiest memories were of sharing a meal and that cooking remained a way for people to spend time together as more of daily life moved online.

The chef also spoke about earning his pilot's licence, a pursuit that began when his wife Jools bought him a flight as a gift.

Oliver said he put off taking the flight several times, but once he tried it, he enjoyed the challenge of having to focus entirely on flying.
