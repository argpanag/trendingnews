The 2026 Formula 1 World Championship stages its high-altitude showdown tonight, Sunday, 1 November 2026, at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez for the 71-lap Mexico City Grand Prix (Mexican Grand Prix). Lights out is scheduled for 8:00 PM GMT in the UK (3:00 PM EST / 2:00 PM CST local) with live British television coverage on Sky Sports F1 and Sky Sports Main Event.

Seven-time world champion Lewis Hamilton leads an all-British front-row lockout after snatching his 105th career F1 pole—and his first in Scuderia Ferrari colours—just 0.009 seconds ahead of Mercedes' George Russell, with Charles Leclerc (P3), championship leader Andrea Kimi Antonelli (P4), Max Verstappen (P5) and McLaren's Lando Norris (P6) right behind.
