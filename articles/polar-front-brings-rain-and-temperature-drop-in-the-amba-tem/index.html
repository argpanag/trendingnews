Polar front brings rain and temperature drop in the AMBA: temperatures will fall to 6°C this weekend - Noticias Ambientales

TheTools · 10.09.2026 15:15 · source · permalink

The Metropolitan Area of Buenos Aires (AMBA) is preparing for a drastic change in the weather, with the arrival of a polar front from the south. After pleasant days with temperatures close to 20 degrees, the weekend is expected to bring a marked temperature drop and intermittent rain.

Temperature Drop and Rain in the AMBA

This weather change is driven by the entry of a cold front, which will alter the weather over the weekend. Experts from the National Meteorological Service (SMN) warn about the magnitude of the event, highlighting the strong impact on the central region of the country.

A pattern of gradual instability will be established, accompanied by the settling of cold air. Moderate to strong southeast winds are anticipated, which will intensify the feeling of cold in Buenos Aires and its surroundings.

According to meteorologist Mauricio Saldivar, “the rotation of the wind towards the southeast will not only bring humidity and moderate rain but also a marked drop in temperatures, which could reach 6°C on Sunday.”

The wind will play a fundamental role in this climate change. From Friday, gusts are expected to reach up to 50 km/h, extending throughout the weekend. The persistence of the southeast wind could cause a sudestada, with the risk of flooding in coastal areas of the AMBA.

Cindy Fernández from the SMN explained that the entry of the cold front from Patagonia will collide with a mass of humid air in the center of the country, causing instability and isolated showers.

Saturday will be characterized by a sharp drop in temperature due to the cold air. The afternoon could present cloudy skies and a chance of rain in the city and surroundings.

Given these weather conditions, the population is advised to take necessary precautions to mitigate the effects of the cold and precipitation.
