BYD’s 3 million USD supercar Yangwang U9 Xtreme crashed in China

TheTools · 02.11.2026 06:18 · source · permalink

The BYD’s 3 million USD supercar Yangwang U9 Xtreme with a combined peak power of 2,220 kW (2,977 hp) crashed in China, possibly during a test attempt to reach the top speed of 500 km/h. Although the car’s rear axle was completely detached, the driver avoided injuries.

The Yangwang U9 Xtreme is a quad-motor sports car that stands on the 1,200 V silicon carbide platform. Its combined peak power output reaches 2,220 kW, providing a power-to-weight ratio of 895 kW (1,200 hp) per ton. The U9 Xtreme adopts an 80 kWh battery with a 30C discharge technology. This model previously became the world’s fastest production car, reaching the top speed of 496.22 km/h at Germany’s Automotive Testing Papenburg.

Recently, it was confirmed that the Yangwang U9 Xtreme will make an attempt to break the 500 km/h speed threshold in the UAE in November. This information was first disclosed by BYD vice president Stella Li. Later, Yangwang sales division general manager Hu Xiaoqing confirmed this intention once again.

On November 2, video and photos were released in China showing an accident with the Yangwang U9 Xtreme during a run on a closed track. The vehicle deviated from its line at high speed and struck a barrier, resulting in the complete detachment of its rear axle. It seems that this occurred during a test attempt to reach the top speed of around 500 km/h. Despite the car’s body taking severe damage, the driver remained unharmed. According to reports, he managed to get out of the car on his own.

This incident illustrates the full complexity of the record that BYD is aiming for. The U9 Xtreme runs on 20-inch dual five-spoke wheels paired with GitiSport e·Gtr2 Pro semi-slick tires. It also adopts a special aerodynamic kit with a large rear wing. In any case, we will see the U9 Xtreme attempt to reach a speed of 500 km/h in the UAE.
