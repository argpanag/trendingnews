Cooper Flagg scores 17 points in loss to Celtics

TheTools · 16.11.2026 01:45 · source · permalink

    Cooper Flagg scores 17 points in loss to Celtics

    Cooper Flagg

    DAL Forward #32
    Player Stats

    Cooper Flagg tallied 17 points (5-of-15 FGs, 5-of-5 FTs), three rebounds, four assists, one steal and one block over 27 minutes in Sunday’s loss to the Celtics.

    The Maine native did some real damage in the fourth quarter once the Celtics had already put the game away. It may have been garbage time, but it counts all the same for fantasy purposes. Simply put, Dallas looked like a completely different offense without primary initiator Kyrie Irving at point guard as he sat out the second night of a back-to-back in this one. The 19-year-old phenom has cooled off a bit over the last week, but he’s still averaging 23.5 points, 6.7 rebounds, 6.2 assists, 1.9 steals and 0.8 blocks through 13 games as one of the most impactful all-around contributors in fantasy. The Mavericks have a three-game week on tap that features showdowns against the Warriors, Clippers and Jazz.
    - George Bissell
