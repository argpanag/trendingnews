Bulgaria to host Iceland in Nations League

Bulgaria will host Iceland in Plovdiv on Friday, 13 November, in their fifth UEFA Nations League Group C4 match. Kick-off is at 9.45 p.m. local time. 

The match will begin with a minute’s silence for those killed in the Trakia motorway crashes, Bulgarian National Television reported. Bulgaria’s players will also wear black armbands in solidarity with the victims’ families.

Iceland lead the group with eight points, while Bulgaria are third with four.
