Introducing the OM SYSTEM PEN Interchangeable Lens Camera.

PR Newswire

BETHLEHEM, Pa., Sept. 9, 2026

Featuring an Electronic Viewfinder and the Series' First Dust & Splash Proof Design, made to take anywhere for travel and everyday photography.

BETHLEHEM, Pa., Sept. 9, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- OM Digital Solutions Corporation proudly announces the launch of the OM SYSTEM PEN, the first PEN series camera to bear the OM SYSTEM brand, scheduled to deliver in late October, 2026.

Since the release of the first-generation PEN film camera in 1959, the PEN series has been beloved by many people as a compact, lightweight camera that allows the user to enjoy a wide range of shooting styles, from serious photography to casually taking shots of everyday life. Building on this philosophy, the PEN is an interchangeable lens camera that conforms to the Micro Four Thirds System standard. It adds an electronic viewfinder to a conventional rangefinder-style design that is the first in the series to offer dust & splash proof capabilities1, and incorporates OM SYSTEM's unique creative expression functions.

The PEN unlocks a wide range of expressive capabilities unique to interchangeable lens cameras, empowering everyone from seasoned photography enthusiasts to those just starting out, to capture and enjoy images with creativity and confidence. 

Key Features of the OM SYSTEM PEN:

  • A design that inspires you to take it everywhere and keep shooting—dust & splashproof and equipped with an electronic viewfinder.
  • Color & Monochrome Profile Control that provides film-like image tone and expression.
  • Computational photography features, a new photographic expression driven by digital technology.

At OM SYSTEM, we believe creators deserve tools that support their vision without adding bulk or complication. The OM SYSTEM PEN is built to be your everyday camera — refined for those who explore more, carry less, and enjoy the art of photography.

OM SYSTEM PEN – Main Feature Details

A design that inspires you to take it everywhere and keep shooting—dust & splash proof and equipped with an electronic viewfinder. 

The PEN is the first camera to be introduced under the PEN name by OM SYSTEM. With a rangefinder-style design that has been loved for generations for its everyday usability, the PEN features a design that is both timeless and modern. Although the overall aesthetic focuses on straight lines, this camera also incorporates firm, curved surfaces that fit comfortably in the hand, balancing a quality feel with a secure grip. v

It is also compact and supremely portable, making it easy to carry in everyday situations or on a journey, and is equipped with an electronic viewfinder for better visibility and usability when shooting. Users can enjoy the unique process of shooting with a camera, looking through the viewfinder while adjusting the aperture value and shutter speed to achieve the image they want.

This model is also the first camera in the PEN series to feature IPX1-rated1 splash proof performance and a dustproof design. Sealing materials are incorporated at key points throughout the camera body, allowing photographers to shoot with confidence even when faced with sudden changes in the weather.

In addition to design aesthetics and portability, the PEN offers excellent shooting performance. The TruePic IX image processing engine, 20.37 million effective pixel Live MOS sensor, and high-resolution M.ZUIKO DIGITAL lens deliver excellent image quality. The PEN also features 5-axis image stabilization compatible with all mountable Micro Four Thirds System standard lenses, greatly enhancing hand-held shooting with up to 5.5 stops2 of stabilization in the center and up to 4.5 stops2 at the edges.

Color & Monochrome Profile Control that provides film-like image tone and expression.
Presets can be selected such as choosing a color or black-and-white film setting, making it easy to explore different photographic styles.  Users can enjoy photographic expression with colors and textures that reflect their own preferences, whether rendering city streets with a nostalgic air, creating a soft, delicate atmosphere, or capturing striking monochrome images. Users can also check and adjust the effects of presets when shooting through the viewfinder, making it easier to create original photographic styles. The Profile Control Switch on the front of the camera offers single-press access to this function while holding the camera.

Profile control can also be applied when shooting video (with the exception of S&Q).

Color Profile Control
During color photography, the saturation of 12 separate colors can be adjusted in 11 levels to achieve the ideal tones. This can also be combined with Highlight & Shadow Control or shading effects for an expanded range of expression. We offer four preset types: "Film Rich Color," for rich, deep tones, "Film Vivid," for vivid saturation and rich colors, "Film Soft Tone," which delivers light, soft tones, and Standard (Natural).

Monochrome Profile Control
Users can combine five effects that are essential for black and white photography, including color filter effect, Highlight & Shadow Control, shading effects, film grain effect, and tone effect for truly unique black and white images.

Four presets are available: Film B&W, which makes use of a film grain effect, Film IR, which emphasizes a red color filter effect that looks like infrared film, Film Low Contrast, which reduces the contrast for a soft impression, and Standard (Monotone).

Computational photography features, a new photographic expression driven by digital technology
Compositing techniques that generally require a PC during post-processing are now available in-camera thanks to computational photography. Simply capturing images with the PEN allows users to create new photographic expressions with the latest digital technology, from more dramatic landscapes and cityscapes to depictions of flowing water as smooth and delicate as silk.

The CP button is included on the camera to activate computational photography for easier access to some of these functions. Press and hold the CP button while turning the Control Dial to switch to the following shooting functions: Tripod High Res Shot Mode, Handheld High Res Shot, Live ND (ND2-16), Focus Stacking, HDR (HDR1 and 2), and Multiple Exposure.

Other Features

  • 121-point all cross-type On-chip Phase Detection AF
  • The PEN series' first AI Subject Detection AF for people and animals (cats and dogs)
  • Starry Sky AF
  • A dust reduction system that removes dust from the image sensor, allowing lens changes without the need to worry about dust appearing in photographs
  • AUTO mode, which automatically detects the scene being shot and captures images using the optimal settings
  • 24 SCN modes with 6 themes which supports shooting more complex scenarios, such as starscape photography
  • 16 types of Art Filters for expanded creative expression
  • Pro Capture shooting (records up to 12 previous frames at a maximum of 30 fps beginning with the moment when the shutter button is fully pressed, RAW sequential shooting is compatible)
  • Set custom mode name for easy recall (up to 12 alphanumeric characters)
  • Easily record cinematic video in OM-Cinema1 or OM-Cinema2 picture modes specifically designed for videos
  • Supports vertical video and S&Q (slow/quick motion) video shooting
  • Equipped with a video recording light (tally light)

Supporting Software and Apps

OM Image Share App (OI.Share v1.5):
This smartphone application enables users to connect to a camera for image transfer and remote shooting.

The latest update adds support for the new "Profile Library" feature. "Profile Library" reads color, tone, and other image settings from photos captured using the "Color/Monochrome Profile Control" and stored on their smartphone, and registers their preferred profiles to the camera's preset slots.3 By recalling the registered profiles from the camera's "Color/Monochrome Profile Control", users can easily reproduce their preferred photographic expressions. OI.Share can also be used to update the firmware on the camera body and backup/restore camera settings. With this latest release up to 10 OM SYSTEM cameras can be registered.
