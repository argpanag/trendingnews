NORCET 11 Prelims Result 2026 OUT Today, Result Date, Cut Off Marks & Steps

TheTools · 18.09.2026 10:50 · source · permalink

AIIMS will release the NORCET 11 Stage 1 Result 2026 today, September 18, 2026. Check the result date, Stage 1 and Stage 2 exam schedule, cut off marks, qualifying status, and steps to download the result.

Share

 

AIIMS NORCET 11 Prelims Result Date 2026: The All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi is scheduled to announce the NORCET 11 Stage 1 Result 2026 today, September 18, 2026 (Friday). The NORCET 11 Stage 1 examination was conducted on September 12, 2026, while the Stage 2 examination is scheduled for September 30, 2026. Candidates can check their results through the official AIIMS examination portal.

The NORCET 11 Pre Result Date is especially important for candidates waiting to know whether they have qualified for the next stage. The result will determine the candidates eligible to appear for the NORCET 11 Stage 2 examination.

The important details regarding the NORCET 11 Result Date 2026 are given below:

As per the schedule provided, the AIIMS NORCET 11 Stage 1 Result 2026 is scheduled for September 18, 2026. The Stage 1 exam was held on September 12, following the release of the admit card on September 9.

The NORCET 11 Result Date 2026 for the preliminary or Stage 1 examination is September 18, 2026. Candidates who appeared for the Stage 1 CBT can check their qualifying status once AIIMS activates the result link.

The Stage 1 examination is the first stage of the NORCET 11 selection process. Candidates who qualify according to the prescribed criteria will be eligible to participate in Stage 2.

The NORCET 11 prelims result date 2026 is therefore an important milestone for candidates preparing for the next phase of the recruitment process.

Candidates searching for how to check NORCET 11 Stage 1 result date can follow these steps once the result link is activated:

Visit the official AIIMS examination website, aiimsexams.ac.in.

Go to the Recruitments section.

Find the NORCET 11 recruitment link.

Look for the NORCET 11 Stage 1 Result 2026 notification or result link.

Click on the result link.

Enter the required login credentials, if applicable.

Download or view the result.

Check your qualifying status and keep a copy of the result for future reference.

The official AIIMS examination website is the safest source for checking the result and recruitment updates.

After the declaration of the Stage 1 result, qualified candidates will need to prepare for the NORCET 11 Stage 2 examination scheduled for September 30, 2026.

The complete schedule indicates that the Stage 2 result is scheduled for October 10, 2026.

Candidates should regularly check the official AIIMS examination portal for result notices, qualification lists and further instructions. AIIMS uses its examination portal for recruitment-related updates and results.

The NORCET 11 prelims cut off marks 2026 are expected to be available along with or around the declaration of the Stage 1 result. Candidates should note that the final cut-off can vary depending on factors such as candidate performance, category, number of candidates and the selection requirements.

The NORCET 11 cut off 2026 should not be confused with the qualifying marks. Candidates need to satisfy the prescribed qualifying criteria and the applicable selection cut-off to progress to the next stage.

Until AIIMS officially publishes the category-wise cut-off, candidates should avoid relying on unofficial cut-off figures circulating online.

Candidates can check the following details when the cut-off is released:

Category-wise cut-off marks

Qualifying status

Roll numbers/qualified candidates

Stage 2 eligibility

Result or merit-related information, wherever provided

Candidates who qualify in Stage 1 should immediately focus on Stage 2 preparation instead of waiting for further updates. Keep the result/qualification details safely and regularly monitor the official AIIMS website for the Stage 2 admit card and examination instructions.

Candidates who do not qualify should also check the result carefully and review their performance to identify areas requiring improvement for future nursing recruitment examinations.
