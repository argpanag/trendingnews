GOG Gives Away Dead Age 2 Free Until November 30 | Wisevoter

TheTools · 27.11.2026 20:39 · source · permalink

The free promotion lets PC users with a GOG account permanently add the 2021 role-playing game to their libraries.

Updated on Nov. 27, 2026 in Gaming

GOG has made Dead Age 2 free for all PC users with an account, a title that normally sells for $17.99. The offer runs until November 30, and claimed copies are permanently added to GOG libraries.

Why it matters

The giveaway offers players a free route to a full role-playing game that would otherwise cost $17.99. It is also the first free game GOG has offered in November.

The promotion sets the price of Dead Age 2 at $0 from its usual $17.99 until November 30, with claimed copies kept permanently. Steam reviewers rate the game 75% positive across 1,164 reviews, while GOG reviewers give it 3.2 out of 5 stars across 27 reviews.

The players

GOG

GOG is a digital storefront for PC games known for selling DRM-free titles and running periodic free giveaways for account holders.

Silent Dreams

Silent Dreams is the independent studio that developed the Dead Age series, a role-playing franchise built around survival and party-based storytelling.

Headup

Headup is a publisher that has released a range of independent and mid-sized games and published Dead Age 2.

The details

Users with a GOG account can claim Dead Age 2 during the promotion window, and the game is then permanently added to their library. Developed by Silent Dreams and published by Headup, the game is set 10 years after the original Dead Age and takes about 50 to 60 hours to finish the main story.

Timeline

Platform Strategy

The Dead Age 2 giveaway follows GOG's established habit of rotating free titles to account holders, adding a November entry to that calendar. Platforms such as GOG use these promotions to draw users back to their storefronts while offering older or lesser-known games a new audience.

Any user with a GOG account can claim Dead Age 2 at no cost until November 30, and the copy stays in their library permanently after claiming. The offer applies only to PC users, so players on other platforms cannot take part.

The takeaway

Account holders who want Dead Age 2 should claim it before the November 30 deadline, since the offer is the only way to get the game at no cost. Checking free-game promotions on storefronts they already use can add titles to a library without spending money.

What happens next

The free claim window for Dead Age 2 on GOG closes November 30, 2026.
