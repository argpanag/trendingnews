Legend of French football Olivier Giroud has spoken honestly about his time in Major League Soccer after a short spell playing for Los Angeles FC.

Giroud, 40, is one of the finest strikers of his generation, having played for clubs like Montpellier, Arsenal, Chelsea and AC Milan. He was also in the France squad that won the 2018 World Cup in Russia.

Back in 2024, following his time playing for Milan, Giroud opted to ditch European football for the MLS, signing for LAFC, linking up with fellow Frenchman Hugo Lloris.

During his time in the United States, Giroud scored just five goals in 38 matches across all competitions for LAFC. In the summer of 2025, he left the club and made a return to European football, joining French side Lille. Since then, Giroud has scored 12 goals in 50 appearances across all competitions for the Ligue 1 outfit.

Olivier Giroud's Honest Assessment of Football in the United States

Speaking recently to Ligue1+, Giroud revealed why he left the US, stating that he missed the passion within European football. He said:

“On the football front, it wasn't really for me. I missed this passion, this fervour, these boiling stadiums of Europe and the competition.

“For me, the United States is not a football country.”

At the age of 40, Giroud was recently speaking to French news outlet L’Equipe and stated that this season could be his last.

He said: “We can have interviews with L’Équipe next year, with pleasure, but, as a player, I think that this is most probably my last season. That is why I am making the most of every moment.”

So far in his career, Giroud has scored a total of 303 goals in 806 matches. On the international stage, he has been capped 137 times by France, scoring 57 goals, and was only recently overtaken by Kylian Mbappe in the all-time scoring list for Les Bleus.

Speaking back in 2022, Liverpool captain Virgil van Dijk named Giroud as his toughest opponent.

The Dutchman explained of the former Arsenal and Chelsea star: “Funnily enough, [Olivier] Giroud. I always felt like I got him, but somehow, he always managed to score. Whether it was for Arsenal, Chelsea or France, he always scored.

“I said to him when we won the league, we won 5-2 we were 3-0 up or something and he scored a scrappy goal, I think it was against the crossbar and fell down, scrappy goal, I said to him: ‘You scored again?!’

“So I would say he is the guy that scores against me, and just that annoying person.

“There’s so many fantastic strikers I’ve played against, Messi, [Sergio] Aguero, [Erling] Haaland is a difficult one because he has everything, but I’m going for Giroud as he’s my bogeyman – annoying!”
