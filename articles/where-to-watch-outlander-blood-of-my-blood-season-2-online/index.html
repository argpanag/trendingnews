Season two premieres on Sept. 18 at 8 p.m. PT/ET on Starz, which, thanks to a limited-time promo, is free for three months with DirecTV’s Entertainment, Choice or Ultimate Signature packages. Where to Stream Outlander: Blood of My Blood Without Cable for Free

This gives subscribers live access to all Starz channels, plus access to watch via Starz.com through their provider’s credentials (meaning fans can watch new episodes as soon as they drop on the app at 12 a.m. ET).
