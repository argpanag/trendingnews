Young jockey in critical condition after horror race fall

TheTools · 08.11.2026 20:57 · source · permalink
  • Sport
  • Horse Racing
  • Horse racing

Updated November 8, 2026 - 9.20pm. First published at 8.57pm

Apprentice jockey Izzy Neale remains in a critical condition after being airlifted to hospital on Sunday following a fall.

The 22-year-old and fellow rider Diego Montes de Oca were both dislodged from their mounts Maroo and Shadow during a race at Warren.

Apprentice jockey Izzy Neale. Instagram/@izzy.neale21

Marnoo was later euthanised, having suffered a near foreleg injury.

Racing NSW confirmed that Neale had “lost consciousness” after the incident and was flown to Dubbo Hospital. Montes de Oca suffered a broken foot.

In a statement on Monday morning, the governing body confirmed the scheduled race meeting at Scone had been called off.

“The decision follows the serious race fall involving apprentice jockey Izzy Neale at Warren yesterday. Izzy remains in Dubbo Hospital in a critical condition, with her family by her side,” the statement said.

“The thoughts and heartfelt support of Racing NSW and the entire community are with Izzy, her family, friends and colleagues during this extremely difficult time.”

Neale has won 88 races from 699 career starts in her young career.
