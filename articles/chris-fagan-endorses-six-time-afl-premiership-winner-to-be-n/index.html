Chris Fagan endorses six-time AFL premiership winner to be next Brisbane coach

TheTools · 14.10.2026 11:43 · source · permalink

Fagan has given a huge tick of approval to his potential successor at the Lions.

Brisbane's triple premiership coach Chris Fagan has given a massive tick of approval to one of the top candidates to replace him as Lions coach next season. Fagan stepped down days after guiding Brisbane to an epic three-peat with victory over Fremantle in the AFL grand final.

It leaves the Lions with a massive hole to fill but the legendary mentor says they don't need to look far to find his coaching successor. Speaking on SEN radio on Wednesday, Fagan gave senior assistant Cameron Bruce a glowing endorsement for the top job, and insists the 47-year-old is ready to step into the top job.

Fagan believes the six-time premiership winner would already be in a head coaching role at another AFL club, had he not chosen to stay in Brisbane. Bruce served under Fagan as Lions assistant and midfield coach in four grand finals and three premiership victories. And he was also senior assistant under Alastair Clarkson for Hawthorn's own three-peat between 2013 - 2015.

"He's been involved in a large number of premierships. Six, I think it is, three here (at Brisbane) and three at Hawthorn, so eight grand finals," Fagan said. "He's been around a long time...

"The only reason he hasn't put his hand up for jobs in more recent years is that he loves it up here. He's got a young family that love it up here as well.

Chris Fagan (L) has given Lions assistant Cameron Bruce (R) a glowing endorsement to succeed him as Brisbane coach. Pic: Getty

"So he hasn't been inclined to move anywhere else, whereas, here's an opportunity where he doesn't have to go anywhere. I honestly think he would have an AFL coaching job right now if he had have decided to go for one of those jobs."

As far as endorsements go, they don't come much bigger. And Brisbane bosses will surely take their legendary former coach's opinion into consideration when making a call on his successor.

Murray Davis in frame for vacant Lions coaching job

Cameron is not the only name in the running, with the Lions reportedly approaching Adelaide coaching director Murray Davis for the vacant head coaching job. Davis spent 13 years at Brisbane in various coaching roles - including their 2024 premiership season - before being poached by Adelaide to work alongside Matthew Nicks.

Davis was among four candidates shortlisted for the Essendon head coaching job, before Mark McVeigh was appointed earlier this year. He has a reputation for fostering relationships with players, coaches and footy department staff and reportedly has strong support from within the Brisbane club.

Lions footy boss Danny Daly was also an early frontrunner to succeed Fagan but withdrew from the race last week after earning a promotion at the club. Daly has been given "elevated" responsibilities in his new role and is part of the five-person selection panel tasked with choosing Fagan's successor.
