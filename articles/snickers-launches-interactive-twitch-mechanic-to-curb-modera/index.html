Snickers Launches Interactive Twitch Mechanic to Curb Moderation Trolls

TheTools · 01.10.2026 00:50 · source · permalink

Snickers expanded its Feed The Trolls marketing campaign onto Twitch during Australia's AFL and NRL Grand Final week, introducing a moderation mechanic designed to intercept heated chat messages from hungry fans before publication.

The custom system analyzes potentially aggressive comments in live stream chats, prompting users to reconsider their tone or grab a candy bar before posting. The media push features creators Nalopia and Midbeast, alongside paid campaigns executed by Publicis Groupe OneMars across gaming, sports, and Amazon networks.

The digital expansion follows an August launch that featured television and online video placements across Kayo and the Melbourne Cricket Ground. Rather than displaying standard banner advertisements, the current setup directly modifies the chat interface by utilizing built-in moderation technology.

Surveys conducted by Lonergan Research reveal that 87 percent of Australians acknowledge hunger alters their behavior. Additionally, 58 percent of sports fans report increased emotional responses while watching games on an empty stomach, while Mi3 data shows 39 percent of hungry viewers overreact during tight matches compared to 26 percent of fed fans.
