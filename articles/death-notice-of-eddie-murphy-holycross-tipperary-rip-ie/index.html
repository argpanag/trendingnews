The death has occurred of

The death has occurred on the 14th September of Eddie Murphy Galbertstown, Holycross and formerly Roskeen, Drombane following a road traffic accident. Predeceased by his father Dan and infant daughter Frances Marie. Deeply regretted by his family Niamh, Daniel, Edward and their mother Martina (Ceaser), son Calum and his mother Sonia, his mother Teresa in her 101st year, grandchildren Sean Oisin Paidi and Nainsí, brothers Jimmy, Martin, Donal and twin brother Oliver, sisters Mary (Berkery) and Noreen, brother-in-law John Berkery, sister-in-law Marie Ivers, Jimmy’s partner Grainne, Oliver’s partner Chrissy, Aunt Sarah Quinn, nephews, nieces, cousins, work colleagues at Coolmore stud, good neighbours and many great friends especially Johnny Bourke, Helena, Fiona, Thomas Spillane and Thomas Flannagan.

May he rest in Peace

The Murphy family and Eddie’s family would like to thank the Gardaí and all the emergency services who attended the scene and all who helped all of whom have been so kind especially Garda liaison officer Lucy Davern of Cahir Garda station.
