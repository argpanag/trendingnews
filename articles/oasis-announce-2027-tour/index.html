Oasis Announce 2027 Tour

TheTools · 14.09.2026 15:49 · source · permalink

Didn’t snag tickets to the coveted Oasis comeback tour last summer? Don’t look back in anger just yet. The Gallagher brothers are getting back on the road next year—today, they announce a series of 2027 shows in across the U.S. and Europe. The run includes 11 performances in their hometown of Manchester and a four-night return to Knebworth Park, where Oasis played a pair of legendary concerts all the way back in 1996. Check out a full itinerary below.

“Start the celebrations!!!!”, Oasis said in a press staement. “After a period of reflection ‘The Most Damaging Pop Cultural Force In Recent British History’ has declared itself fit for purpose and will once again return to lift the spirits of the people. Come witness the fitness. It shall be a sight to behold.”

The announcement arrives on the heels of the premiere of Oasis: Don’t Look Back In Anger, a documentary chronicling the band’s summer 2025 return to the stage after a 16 year hiatus (and, in true Gallagher fashion, some brotherly ardor.) The film screened as part of the Venice International Film Festival on September 5.
