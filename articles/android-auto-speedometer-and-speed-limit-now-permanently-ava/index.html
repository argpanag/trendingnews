Android Auto: Speedometer and speed limit now permanently available

TheTools · 20.09.2026 14:43 · source · permalink

Google continues to work on improving navigation with Android Auto. The speedometer and speed limit are now always displayed.

(Image: heise medien)

  • Dirk Knop

Google has recently been working on further improvements to navigation with Android Auto. The latest optimizations appear to be a server-side implementation: after Android Auto could display the current speed limit as well as the instantaneous speed according to GPS on external displays in cars when controlled with Android smartphones, the developers are following up. Even without active route planning, the car displays show the speed and the current limit.

The first improvements started rolling out around mid-July. In response to a query from heise online, Google confirmed in mid-August the gradual rollout and staggered distribution of this long-awaited feature update:
“Google is just starting to roll this feature out more broadly and will release further updates as they become available.” First users are already seeing the feature in their vehicles.

Google apparently confirms the new updates

On social networks like Reddit, users are reporting problems with the display – it later turned out that no route planning was active there. However, an alleged Google employee responded with the information that the Android Auto development team is looking into such issues. According to this, the speed display without active destination guidance is a function that is also being rolled out systematically and gradually and is not available to all users at once.

In response to our inquiry regarding the newer function update and any further plans, Google did not respond immediately.

There also seem to be exceptions when it comes to external Android Auto displays from car manufacturers. For example, we were unable to achieve a display of the instantaneous speed on a built-in display in a Volvo. Manufacturers may be holding back the distribution of new features or controlling the updates accordingly.
