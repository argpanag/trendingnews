Trinity Rodman's Net Worth: Salary, Highest-Paid Women's Soccer Player Contract, Endorsements & More Details

TheTools · 13.09.2026 18:41 · source · permalink

Trinity Rodman has become one of the biggest names in women’s soccer, both on and off the field. The Washington Spirit star has built a successful career with the U.S. women’s national team and is now the highest-paid player in NWSL history.

The 24-year-old has also been enjoying an impressive form during the 2026 NWSL season, scoring 10 goals and providing six assists in 23 appearances. Most recently, Rodman provided an assist in Washington’s 2-1 win over the Portland Thorns.

Rodman has also been making the headlines because of her relationship with American tennis star Ben Shelton, who is playing Alexander Zverev in the 2026 US Open men’s singles final today.

Trinity Rodman’s estimated net worth in 2026 is around $2 million to $2.6 million, according to published estimates. However, Rodman has not publicly released a full breakdown of her finances, so her exact net worth cannot be confirmed.

Rodman’s income comes from several sources, including her Washington Spirit salary, earnings from the U.S. women’s national team, bonuses and endorsement deals.

Rodman signed a new three-year contract with the Washington Spirit in January 2026 in a deal worth more than $2 million per year, including bonuses, and runs through the 2028 season.

The contract made the Washington Spirit star the highest-paid player in NWSL history and, at the time, the highest-paid female soccer player in the world.

The deal is reportedly worth at least $6 million over three seasons based on the reported $2 million annual figure, although the exact value can be higher depending on bonuses.

Rodman’s new contract was an important moment for women’s soccer because it showed the growing value of the sport’s top players. The deal made her the highest-paid player in NWSL history and came after the league introduced its High Impact Player rule, which allows teams to spend additional money to retain top players.

Before signing the new deal, the 24-year-old had already established herself as one of the league’s biggest stars and won NWSL Rookie of the Year and the NWSL Championship in her first season in 2021. 

In another big moment for her career, she also helped the United States win Olympic gold at the 2024 Paris Games.

Adidas is one of her biggest commercial partners, while her reported partnerships have also included Red Bull and Oakley. She has also worked with brands including Verizon, Bose and Rexona.

Rodman has worked with adidas on soccer products and campaigns, including the F50 Sparkfusion cleat, which was designed specifically for female players.
