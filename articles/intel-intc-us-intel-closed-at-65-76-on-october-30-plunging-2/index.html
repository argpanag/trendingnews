$Intel(INTC.US)

Intel closed at $65.76 on October 30, plunging 28.6% after its Q3 report. Revenue rose 21% to $16.51 billion and adjusted EPS beat estimates at $0.52. However, Q4 revenue guidance of $15–16 billion and EPS guidance of $0.25 disappointed. Strong AI demand contrasts with weak PC outlook; volatility remains high.

Verdict: Cautious. Watch Q4 execution before investing.
