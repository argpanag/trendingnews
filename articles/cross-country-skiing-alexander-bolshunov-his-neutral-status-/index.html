Cross-country skiing: an official decision is still pending

Alexander Bolshunov is not expected to be granted neutral athlete status. This is according to a report published on Wednesday byNRK, following a meeting of the FIS Council on the interpretation of the eligibility rules for Russian and Belarusian athletes.

According to several internal sources quoted by Norwegian television, the discussions have confirmed a negative stance towards the three-time Olympic champion. However, no formal individual decision has yet been taken.

“The Council did not examine any specific cases. It agreed on certain clarifications that will serve as the basis for applications for neutral status,” the FIS told our colleagues. It is now up to the International Federation’s administration to rule on the Russian’s case.

But another uncertainty remains. Several NRK sources fear that a potential appeal to the CAS could allow Alexander Bolshunov to return to the world circuit. Recent decisions by the sports tribunal are fuelling these concerns about the robustness of the current criteria.

For his part, Dmitry Svishchev, president of the Russian federation, insists he has not been officially informed. “I read about this in the Norwegian press, but I would like to receive a response from the FIS itself. I will be contacting them, ” he told theTASS news agency.

With three weeks to go until the World Cup gets underway in Ruka (Finland),Alexander Bolshunov’s international future therefore remains in the balance, pending a decision that could subsequently be challenged.
