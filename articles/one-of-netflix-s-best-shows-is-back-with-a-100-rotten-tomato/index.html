One Of Netflix’s Best Shows Is Back With A 100% Rotten Tomatoes Score

TheTools · 20.10.2026 04:05 · source · permalink

A Netflix hit has returned, its best new show to hit the service in a while, and it has a perfect 100% Rotten Tomatoes critic score.

Netflix always likes to see its shows return and not lose any steam, and that's certainly the case this week, with the reappearance of one of its most critically beloved series snagging a 100% Rotten Tomatoes score for its new season.

It's The Diplomat, the Keri Russell-led political thriller that has done a good job of getting its seasons out relatively quickly, with season 4 releasing just a year after season 3, the same gap between seasons 2 and 3. That's a true rarity in the streaming game, especially for a relatively high-profile series like The Diplomat. Season 3 was nominated for seven Emmys, including Best Drama and Best Lead Actress for Russell. Allison Janney won Best Supporting Actress.

While still collecting reviews, season 4 of The Diplomat has a perfect 100% Rotten Tomatoes review score from critics. Sure, that could drop, but if the history of the show is any indication, the score probably won't drop far. With dozens of reviews in, season 3 of The Diplomat has a 97%, season 2 has a 95% and season 1 is the lowest with an 85%. Overall, the show is at a 94%, albeit with a decently lower 74% audience score, which is something of a mystery to me.

Here's the synopsis of how season 4 of The Diplomat begins:

"On an unplanned stopover at Windsor Castle, Kate grows wary of Hal and Grace's dynamic as she tries to contain the fallout from their covert actions."

The Diplomat. Keri Russell as Kate Wyler in episode 405 of The Diplomat.

Looking over at IMDB, the finale of season 4, Warships, has a 9.6/10, the highest of any episode of the series. Though it's worth keeping in mind that only super-bingers have been able to finish the entire thing at this point, so we'll see how those numbers shake out later on.

The Diplomat is one piece of a wild Hollywood story: Russell and Matthew Rhys starred together as a married couple of covert Russian agents in the all-time great spy show, The Americans. Then, they got married in real life. That series ended, Russell went on to the hugely popular The Diplomat, and Rhys ended up on Widow's Bay. His Emmy win this year for that show secured him a record for winning the best actor award for Drama, Comedy and a miniseries.

Netflix now relies on The Diplomat as a rare, yearly, quality staple, and it stands to reason it's going to air as long as it can, unlike other Netflix shows which seem permanently in limbo. The Diplomat isn't renewed for season 5 yet, but if viewership is close to as good as these critic reviews, that shouldn't be a problem.
