    Fulham v Man Utd (16:30 BST)

    Simon Stone
    BBC Sport Chief Football News Reporter at Craven Cottage

    Fulham are unchanged after claiming first point of the season at Liverpool last weekend.

    Ryan Sessegnon comes onto the bench for Kenny Tete as the only change to Alvaro Arbeloa’s matchday squad.

    Fulham are looking for their first home win over today’s opponents since 2009.

    Fulham XI: Leno, Castagne, Affengruber, Bassey, Robinson, Berge, Charles, Bobb, King, Iwobi, Garcia. Substitutes: Lecomte, Andersen, Palacios, Muniz, Kevin, Larsson, Manuel Angel, Sessegnon. Smith Rowe,

    Luke Shaw returns for Patrick Dorgu as the only change to the United team that started the home defeat by 10-man Manchester City seven days ago.

    Striker Benjamin Sesko is not involved and neither is Matthijs de Ligt, even though the Netherlands defender travelled with the squad to London on Saturday.

    Manchester United XI: Lammens, Dalot, Maguire, Martinez, Shaw, Tielemans, Mainoo, Mbuemo, Fernandes, Rashford, Cunha. Substitutes: Darlow, Mazraoui, Mount, Zirkzee, Dorgu, Yoro, Andrey Santos, Heaven, Baleba, Lacey.

Match Facts

  • Manchester City are unbeaten in all 14 of their Premier League home games against Sunderland (W12 D2). Their last home league defeat against the Black Cats was in the second tier in January 1998 (0-1).

  • Sunderland are winless in nine Premier League games against Manchester City (D2 L7) since a 1-0 home win in November 2013 under Gus Poyet.

  • Manchester City have won all four Premier League games in 2026-27, only starting a top-flight season with five wins on the bounce in 1912-13 (5), 2015-16 (5), 2016-17 (6) and 2023-24 (6).

  • Sunderland have scored in each of their last eight away Premier League matches, their best run of scoring away from home in the top-flight since a run of eight between November and February in the 2015-16 season. They last had a longer run between April and October 1956 (10).

  • Man City have lost just two of their last 30 Premier League matches (W20 D8), with their 68 points the joint most of any side since the start of that run on 29 November last year, level with Arsenal.
