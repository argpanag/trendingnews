44 Aces in One Match: Shelton Delivers a Monster Serving Performance in Basel

TheTools · 28.10.2026 09:46 · source · permalink

Ben Shelton unleashed his serve against Aleksandar Kovacevic at the ATP 500 in Basel. With 44 aces in three sets, the American joined several of the game's biggest servers in the history books.

Returning Ben Shelton's serve is no easy task.

His latest match in Basel against Kovacevic proved it once again, as the American shined on serve in a three-set thriller featuring three tie-breaks: 6-7 (4), 7-6 (3), 7-6 (4).

44 aces for Shelton

The 24-year-old fired a staggering 44 aces over the course of the match — a monstrous number.

To put the performance into perspective, Jeu, Set et Maths revealed the ranking for the most aces hit in a best-of-three-set match.

Here is the full list:

- 47 aces: Raonic (Queen's 2024)
- 45 aces: Karlovic (Halle 2015)
- 44 aces: Philippoussis (Kuala Lumpur 1995)
- 44 aces: Karlovic (Zagreb 2014)
- 44 aces: Kyrgios (Brisbane 2019)
- 44 aces: Isner (Dallas 2023)
- 44 aces: Opelka (Brisbane 2026)
- 44 aces: Shelton (Basel 2026)

Note that these statistics have been tracked since 1991, the year the ATP began archiving match stats.
