Transport Minister in London for talks on infrastructure and investment

TheTools · 17.11.2026 11:43 · source · permalink

Transport, Communications and Works Minister Evi Tsolaki is in London for a series of meetings and engagements focusing on infrastructure, transport and attracting investment to Cyprus, according to an announcement issued by the Ministry on Tuesday.

On Wednesday, the Minister will take part in a discussion on the prospects for studying and developing a railway connection between Cyprus’ major cities, as part of efforts to explore sustainable transport solutions.

On Thursday, she will be the keynote speaker at the GBCY Business Orientation Cyprus 2026 business forum, organised by the Great Britain–Cyprus Business Association.

According to the Ministry, Tsolaki’s visit will conclude on November 20, with her participation in a discussion organised by the Hellenic Observatory at the London School of Economics (LSE), entitled “From Recovery to Transformation: Investing in Infrastructure in Greece and Cyprus”.

The discussion will also feature Greece’s Minister of Infrastructure and Transport, Christos Dimas and Dimitris Sourvanos, representing the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD).

The announcement added that the Minister’s visit to London is part of efforts to strengthen international cooperation and attract investment to Cyprus.
