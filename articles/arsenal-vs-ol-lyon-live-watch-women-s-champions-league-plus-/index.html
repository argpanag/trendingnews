    Arsenal will have to keep a close eye on Marie-Antoinette Katoto this evening.

    The French striker is the top scorer in the Women's Champions League this season with seven goals to her name - two more than anyone else.

    And the goals don't stop there for Lyon.

    Melchie Dumornay is also prolific and is the top goalscorer in the French top flight this term with 11 goals from nine games, with Ada Hegerberg following closely behind on 10.

  20. 'Our ambition is to spend more time on the ball'published at 19:48 GMT
    19:48 GMT

    Arsenal v Lyon (20:00 GMT)

    Arsenal

    In her pre-match news conference Arsenal boss Renee Slegers was asked how she wants her team to defend against a Lyon side that have scored 20 goals in four games:

    "We played against Barcelona, and I think at that point they had scored 19 and conceded two. So it’s a very similar scenario in that sense, but two different types of teams.

    "What we'll have to do is we want to play our game. The past two games we spent less time on the ball. Our ambition is to spend more time on the ball tomorrow, and that helps us defend as well. If you're on the ball, you're automatically not defending. So that's important for us tomorrow."
