Jesus Sanchez DFAed

TheTools · 11.09.2026 18:21 · source · permalink
  • Toronto Blue Jays News

So this surprised me. The Jays DFAed Jesús Sánchez as the counter move to bringing up Rudy Martin.

Sánchez just didn’t work out. He was supposed to be the power bat we needed and then he hit 8 home runs in 99 games for us. And there was the defense, or lack there of. 3 official errors (leading the league) and a .975 fielding average (why does baseball insist on three significant digits?) in the outfield. League average is .987. All three of his errors were in left field, in right he hadn’t made an error, and we were told that he was more used to right field which had be wondering why not play him in right?

But the errors were just part of the story. Cause baseball has this silly idea that if a player makes too big of a mistake, we can’t call that an error. Ball goes off the glove, error. Player doesn’t get within 30 feet of a catchable ball, not an error.

Anyway, the last couple of weeks of the season will be Sánchezless. I don’t suppose he’ll be greatly missed.
