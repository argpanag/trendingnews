Grey’s Anatomy’s New Spinoff Officially Casts New Meredith Grey Replacement

TheTools · 11.11.2026 20:55 · source · permalink

The world of Grey's Anatomy has a new lead.

Despite its reputation as one of the most-watched shows on television and a continued streaming juggernaut, the Grey's Anatomy franchise has had only two spinoffs since launching in March 2005. First, there was Private Practice. It centered on Dr. Addison Montgomery (Kate Walsh), taking the Grey's original character to a new location with a new cast. There was also Station 19, a firefighter-focused drama, which ended in May 2024 after seven seasons.

Grey's Anatomy will be getting a third spinoff, with franchise creator Shonda Rhimes returning to the medical drama as a writer for the first time since season 11. That spinoff, slated to premiere on ABC in the latter half of 2027, is getting its first casting details.

The Hollywood Reporter reveals that How to Get Away with Murder star Karla Souza has been cast in the upcoming Grey's Anatomy spinoff. Souza will play Carolina 'Lina' Hall, described as a “haunted and cynical doctor” who's in her 40s. She runs a medical center in a small West Texas town.

Souza is a veteran of Shonda Rhimes' series and ABC more broadly. Her breakout role was as Laurel Castillo in How to Get Away with Murder, which Rhimes executive produced, and appeared opposite Viola Davis throughout the show's run. She had a lead role opposite Topher Grace in the ABC sitcom Home Economics. More recently, Souza was a series regular on the Prime Video crime thriller 56 Days. On the movie side, she starred alongside Jamie Foxx in Netflix's vampire comedy Day Shift.

Souza, a two-time International Emmy Awards nominee for Prime Video's Dive, is the first cast member announced for the Grey's spinoff. The show is described as an edgy drama about a team at a rural West Texas medical center that serves as the last chance for care for miles.

Specific details about the plot are under wraps, but early reports suggest the intention is still to focus on a group of doctors and include links to the main series. It's co-created and co-written by Rhimes and current Grey's showrunner Meg Marinis. The project hails from 20th Television, and the co-creators executive produce alongside Betsy Beers and franchise veteran Ellen Pompeo.

The spinoff is notable for a few reasons. It is the first time that Rhimes has co-written an episode for the medical drama since the season 11 episode “How to Save a Life”, which centered on Derek Shepherd's death. Rhimes is currently based at Netflix, where she has helped develop Bridgerton into an enduring, sprawling success, but the Texas drama is not covered by her existing agreement with the streaming service because it belongs to the Grey's universe rather than being a completely new show.

The upcoming Grey's Anatomy offshoot is the first time the franchise has filmed outside of Los Angeles. It will be based in Albuquerque, New Mexico, as reports indicated the production couldn't secure a California tax credit. More casting announcements are expected in the coming weeks and months.
