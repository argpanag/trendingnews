Steelers Cornerback Ahead of Curve in Injury Return

TheTools · 08.10.2026 20:37 · source · permalink

Steelers Injury Updates•Steelers News

PITTSBURGH — After Pittsburgh Steelers cornerback Jamel Dean suffered an aggravation of his ankle injury against the Cleveland Browns on Thursday, NFL Network reported that the veteran cornerback was expected to miss a couple of weeks, but Dean might be ahead of the curve already.

Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy did not rule Dean out for even this week when he spoke on Monday, and he said before practice on Thursday that Dean has improved over the course of this week.

“He was feeling better Monday than we expected,” McCarthy said. “But we’ll see where he is.”

Dean confirmed that he is aiming for a return in Week 6, against his former team, the Tampa Bay Buccaneers.

That would be a huge boost to the Steelers secondary that lost Dean and any hope of a return from Joey Porter Jr. within 24 hours last week. The Steelers signed veterans Sean Murphy-Bunting and Terrell Smith from other teams’ practice squads this week, but McCarthy did not seem keen about throwing them right into the mix on Sunday against the Indianapolis Colts.

“I mean, the new guys really just having one day … the biggest thing is having a plan for those guys to make sure they’re getting the work, so we can evaluate them and have a full week this week to see how it plays out for the Colts game,” McCarthy said. “But I think, like all of our guys with the injuries, they’re working through it and we’ll see how that goes.”

Asante Samuel Jr. and rookie Daylen Everette are both expected to start for the Steelers against the Colts, with Jalen Ramsey continuing to work at safety and slot cornerback.
