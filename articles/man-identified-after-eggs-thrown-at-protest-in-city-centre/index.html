Man identified after eggs thrown at protest in city centre

TheTools · 13.10.2026 15:42 · source · permalink

Police have identified a man in connection with an incident in which eggs were thrown at a group of people attending a protest.

It follows a report of a disturbance at the Clock Tower in Brighton at about 1.50 pm on September 19.

Eggs were thrown towards a small group of people.

A suspect is believed to have been outside the Waterstones shop and then ran away from the area via West Street.

Officers have completed CCTV enquiries from the area and have now identified a man in connection with the incident.
