Klook Brings AI-Assisted Booking to Meta’s Muse in Japan | Branding in Asia

TheTools · 25.10.2026 16:18 · source · permalink

Klook has partnered with Meta to integrate its bookable travel experiences with Muse, Meta’s personal AI agent, as the service makes its debut in Japan.

The integration allows travelers to search, compare, book and pay for Klook experiences through a conversation with Muse. Klook’s inventory includes 426,000 bookable offerings across more than 4,600 destinations.

Muse considers travelers’ destinations, interests, availability and preferences when recommending experiences. Users can ask follow-up questions, compare options and check booking conditions, including cancellation and rescheduling policies.

The integration is also intended to help travelers discover experiences beyond usual attractions and give smaller, independent merchants greater visibility, according to the release.

“For over a decade, Klook has worked with local operators to bring a world of diverse and authentic experiences online, connecting them with travelers around the world,” says Jakii Chu, Senior Vice President, Global Marketing at Klook.

“Now, we’re taking travel discovery to the next level with Meta’s Muse, innovating together as travelers increasingly turn to AI for inspiration and trip planning. With a personal travel agent at their fingertips, travelers can discover Klook’s breadth of experiences faster and with ease, finding options that match their interests and travel plans.”
