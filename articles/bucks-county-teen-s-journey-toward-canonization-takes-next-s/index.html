Bucks County teen's journey toward canonization takes next step

TheTools · 20.11.2026 02:11 · source · permalink

PHILADELPHIA (WPVI) -- The U.S. Conference of Catholic Bishops has voted to advance the cause for beatification and canonization of a Bucks County teenager remembered for her faith and optimism in the face of terminal illness.

Maria Middleton died in 2020 from brain cancer at the age of 18.

A member of the North American Irish dance community, Maria became known to family, friends and fellow dancers for her positive outlook and deep Catholic faith, particularly after her diagnosis.

The bishops' vote allows the process for possible sainthood to move forward within the Catholic Church.

The Archdiocese of Philadelphia is now preparing to open a formal inquiry into Maria's life, virtues and reputation for holiness.
