Paris Hilton is standing her ground after facing backlash over her family's Halloween costumes.

On Monday, October 26, the Simple Life alum, 45, shared photos of her family dressed as Beauty and the Beast characters.

Hilton looked stunning as Belle in a yellow ballgown, while her husband, Carter Reum, dressed as the Beast. The couple's children, son Phoenix, 3, and daughter London, 2, looked equally adorable as Lumière and Chip, respectively.

However, as In Touch reported on Tuesday, October 27, many reacted to the costumes negatively, with some accusing Hilton of stealing the spotlight from her kids.

Paris Hilton/Instagram

Hilton addressed the criticism in a comment on an Instagram post from a user, explaining why she chose supporting characters for her children's costumes.

"Beauty and the Beast is one of their favorite movies," Hilton wrote. "And London said she wanted to dress up as Chip. She said it was her favorite character in the movie."

Fans chimed in on Hilton's comment, with one saying, "If her husband is the Beast, it makes sense for her to be Belle…"

"I didn't even know it was Paris Hilton at first, but the kids being side characters make sense, they're the kids!" wrote another.

A third noted, "I feel like no matter WHAT Paris does, someone will always find a problem with it."

Paris Hilton/Instagram

Last year, Hilton and her family dressed as characters from Toy Story, per People.

At the time, Hilton wore a Little Bo Peep costume, while Reum was dressed as Woody. Phoenix and London, on the other hand, dressed up as Buzz and Jessie, respectively.
