Get the Drop on New Rewards During BlizzCon 2026

TheTools · 12.09.2026 16:58 · source · permalink

BlizzCon 2026 will be streaming select programming* live for everyone and we’ve got a bucket full of rewards just waiting for you to claim.

Watch any eligible World of Warcraft stream on Twitch.tv from September 12 at 9:30 am PDT to September 27 at 10:00 am PDT or on the official World of Warcraft YouTube channel from September 12 at 9:30 am PDT until September 13 at 7:00 pm PDT to claim a bucket full of rewards including: 200 Trader’s Tender, Cuddly Blue Grrgle Housing decor item, Fluffy Comfy Flying Quilt Mount, and the Venomous champion’s Illustrious Banner.

Attending BlizzCon in person? You can still receive these rewards by linking your BlizzCon Pass to your Battle.net Account no later than September 18, 2026.***

Rewards and eligibility vary by game, stream, and platform.

Eligibility

To participate, you must link your Twitch account by visiting https://account.battle.net/connections or on YouTube by visiting the YouTube Connected Apps page. If you have changed your password or made any changes to your Twitch or Battle.net account, you will have to relink your accounts. When you relink, there will be a 7-day cooldown before you can relink a new Twitch account. After you’ve claimed your rewards on Twitch, please make sure you’re logging into the Battle.net region you’d like to receive the drop(s) on, as the first region you log into will be where your items will be delivered. It can take up to 24 hours to receive your reward(s) in-game after claiming. Read our Frequently Asked Questions section for more information.

Earn Viewership Rewards On YouTube

Start Time: September 12, 9:30 am PDT
End Time: September 13, 7:00 pm PDT
Where to Watch:  World of Warcraft Official YouTube channel

Earn Viewership Rewards On Twitch

Start Time: September 12, 9:30 am PDT
End Time: September 27, 10:00 am PDT
Where to Watch:  Eligible World of Warcraft streams.

Watch two, four, eight, and twelve hours of  World of Warcraft content while Drops are active on any eligible Twitch channel or on the official World of Warcraft YouTube channel to earn the following items**:

  • 2 Hours: 200 Trader’s Tender
  • 4 Hours: Cuddly Blue Grrgle Decor item
  • 8 Hours: Fluffy Comfy Flying Quilt Mount
  • 12 Hours: Venomous Champion’s Illustrious Banner Toy

Players who have claimed the new Housing decor item from the Twitch Drop will also be able to visit Dennia Silvertongue in Silvermoon City to purchase more copies of this adorable item. Additional copies can also be purchased from Tuuran in Stormwind and Gabbi in Orgrimmar (near the Trading Post).

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. How do I link my battle.net and Twitch accounts?
A. Sign into your Battle.net account at https://account.battle.net/connections and click the Connect link for Twitch, then follow the instructions to sign in and link your Twitch account to Battle.net.

Q. How do I link my Battle.net account to my YouTube account?
A. You can link your Battle.net account to your YouTube account through the YouTube Connected Apps page to earn game rewards and drops.

Q. What devices can I use to earn and claim drops?
A. You can earn and claim drops using your PC/Mac web browser or on the Twitch or YouTube app for Android or iOS. Twitch apps or YouTube apps on game consoles, smart TVs, and other TV apps do not support drops.

Q. Do I need to watch the same channel the entire time to earn my drops?
A. You can earn progress towards a drop on any eligible channel in the World of Warcraft category on Twitch. You can, for example, watch half an hour on one channel and then switch to another channel without losing your progress. You cannot earn progress faster by watching more than one channel at a time. If watching on YouTube, you’ll need to watch the World of Warcraft official channel for the entirety.

Q. Will I receive my drop rewards in-game automatically?
A. To receive your rewards, you must claim the drop on the channel you’re watching or in the Drops Inventory menu on Twitch. YouTube rewards will be delivered automatically in-game if the Battle.net and YouTube accounts are connected while viewing.

Q. Do Twitch Drop rewards expire?
A. Earned Twitch Drop rewards will expire seven (7) days after they’ve been claimed if a Battle.net account has not been linked.

Q. As a streamer, how do I get Twitch Drops on my channel?
A. All you need to do is ensure your Twitch and Battle.net accounts are linked and that you’ve opted into Drops (see Drops Streamer Home).

Q. What regions are eligible for these Twitch Drops?
A. Participating regions include North America, Latin America, Europe, The Middle East, Africa, Japan, Taiwan, Southeast Asia, Australia, and New Zealand.

Q. What regions are eligible for these YouTube Rewards?
A. Participating regions include North America, Latin America, Europe, The Middle East, Africa, Japan, Taiwan, Southeast Asia, Korea, Australia, and New Zealand.

Blizzard has the right to change, pause, or cancel these drops or rewards and their terms if it cannot run as planned for any reason, including but not limited to technical issues or unexpected factors beyond Blizzard’s control.

*Select BlizzCon programming will be livestreamed over official channels, including Opening Ceremony, major panels, and esports competitions. Musical performances and some other elements will not be livestreamed and are exclusive to in-person attendees at the Anaheim Convention Center only.
**Not available in World of Warcraft Classic® games. Requires World of Warcraft® Subscription or Game Time. Use and placement of decor items is limited to the Housing feature, which requires access to World of Warcraft®: Midnight.
*** Rewards will be delivered during the week of September 21 to attendees who have properly linked their BlizzCon Pass to their Battle.net Account.
