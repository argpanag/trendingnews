    A major firefighting operation was launched after a fire broke out on Friday, October 23, 2026, in the historic building high above Berchtesgaden, BGLand24 reported. According to the South Upper Bavaria Police Headquarters, the first responders to arrive found a major fire at 9:52 p.m., Bayerischer Rundfunk reported.

    According to district fire chief Sebastian Walch, firefighters were alerted at around 9:16 p.m., and units from Berchtesgaden and other nearby municipalities were gradually dispatched, BGLand24 wrote. The building’s difficult-to-access location hampered the arrival of fire engines, Traunsteiner Tagblatt reported.

    Firefighting continued past midnight

    Intensive firefighting continued past midnight, with approximately 150 emergency responders taking part in the operation, police spokesperson Lisa Maier told BGLand24. In a statement published at 12:31 a.m., she said the building was completely engulfed in flames and responders wearing protective gear were checking whether anyone might be inside, the local news portal wrote.

    Based on the information available at the time, police did not believe anyone had been inside the building when the fire broke out, and no injuries had been reported, Bayerischer Rundfunk reported. The cause of the fire was still unknown in this overnight report, BR24 said.

    Police investigating whether detained person is connected to fire

    The criminal police in Traunstein took over the investigation, and one suspected person was detained, according to a police spokesperson, BGLand24 reported. “Whether this person is connected to the fire is now the subject of an investigation by the criminal police,” Maier said, according to the local news portal.

    The newspaper Bild had earlier reported, citing a police spokesperson, that arson was suspected and that a motorcyclist encountered by police on their way to the scene had been detained. However, this report also did not confirm that the detainee had started the fire, and said it was unclear whether they were connected to it, according to the Bild article.

    Historic building now serves as a mountain restaurant

    Kehlsteinhaus stands at an elevation of 1,834 meters and was completed in 1938 as a ceremonial building for the Nazi leadership, Bayerischer Rundfunk notes. It has served as a mountain restaurant since 1952, and a local exhibition recounts its history, BR24 reports.
