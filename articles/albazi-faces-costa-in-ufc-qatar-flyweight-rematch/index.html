Albazi Faces Costa in UFC Qatar Flyweight Rematch

TheTools · 21.11.2026 07:02 · source · permalink

A high-stakes flyweight rematch between Amir Albazi and Alessandro Costa will headline the preliminary action at UFC Fight Night: Prochazka vs Stirling on November 21, 2026, in Al Rayyan, Qatar. The contest at Ali Bin Hamad al-Attiyah Arena gives both fighters an opportunity to strengthen their positions in the 125-pound division, with Albazi seeking to end a losing streak and Costa pursuing another significant victory.

According to The Stats Zone, the fighters are expected to walk out at approximately 4:50 p.m. Albazi enters with a 17-3 professional record, while Costa holds a 17-5 record. Their first encounter took place at UFC Vegas 66 in 2022, when Albazi stopped Costa with a third-round uppercut knockout.

Their circumstances have changed considerably since that meeting. Costa was making his UFC debut on short notice when they first fought, whereas his recent performances have established him as a more experienced and dangerous contender.

Albazi has struggled to regain his previous form after consecutive unanimous decision defeats against former champion Brandon Moreno and Kyoji Horiguchi. His February loss to Horiguchi followed an extended absence caused by injuries, according to MMA Sucka and Yahoo Sports.

The Iraqi fighter relies primarily on wrestling and Brazilian jiu-jitsu. His approach involves closing the distance, securing takedowns and pursuing back control before attempting rear-naked chokes. However, The Stats Zone notes that his takedown execution and striking output have been inconsistent.

Against Horiguchi, Albazi struggled to establish his offense, recording 16 significant strikes across three rounds and failing to complete any of seven takedown attempts, according to Yahoo Sports. His recent performances have raised questions about his ability to maintain competitiveness against increasingly challenging opponents.

Costa arrives with considerably stronger recent momentum. According to Yahoo Sports, the Brazilian recorded three consecutive finishes in 2026 against Stewart Nicoll, Matt Schnell and Cody Durden.

His July victory over Durden came through a rear-naked choke after Costa defended takedown attempts. He had previously stopped Schnell with first-round punches in June. These performances demonstrate his ability to finish opponents through both striking and submission.

Costa has accumulated seven knockout victories and seven submission wins during his professional career. His striking arsenal includes calf kicks, powerful body shots and combinations delivered at close range. The Stats Zone also highlights his ability to counter opponents and reverse unfavorable grappling positions.

However, Costa has vulnerabilities that Albazi could exploit. His aggressive exchanges and limited head movement can leave openings for counterpunches and takedowns. His UFC defeats against Steve Erceg and Alden Coria also illustrate difficulties against higher-level opposition.

For Albazi, controlling the grappling exchanges could provide the clearest route to victory. His previous knockout against Costa demonstrates that he has already found a successful approach, although the Brazilian's improved defensive wrestling and recent performances present a different challenge.

The rematch therefore carries significant implications for both fighters. Albazi is pursuing his first victory since June 2023, while Costa has an opportunity to avenge his 2022 defeat and strengthen his position among the UFC flyweight division's emerging contenders.
