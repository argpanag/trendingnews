Referee named for Champions League match between Real Madrid and Inter Milan

  • Real Madrid CF: Champions League

UEFA have appointed English referee Michael Oliver as the man in charge of Tuesday’s match between Real Madrid and Inter Milan in what will be the Champions League debut for both sides.

Per Realmadrid.com
Michael Oliver will referee the match between Real Madrid and Inter Milan, corresponding to the first matchday of the Champions League, which will be held at the Bernabéu (Tuesday, 9:00 pm CEST; Orange TV and Movistar Plus+). The Englishman will officiate a match involving our team in European competition for the sixth time.

Matches refereed by Michael Oliver for Real Madrid in European competition
Real Madrid-Bayern Munich (1-2). Champions League 2025/26. Quarter-finals (first leg).
Real Madrid-Juventus (1-3). Champions League 2017/18. Quarter-finals (second leg).
Real Madrid-Eintracht (2-0). UEFA Super Cup 2022.
Braga-Real Madrid (1-2). Champions League 2023/24. Group stage.
Olympiacos-Real Madrid (3-4). Champions League 2025/26. First phase.

Oliver has the experience to control what should be an intense game between these two teams, with Real Madrid needing to bounce back from their disappointing loss to Betis last Friday.
