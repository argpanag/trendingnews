Juventus fans protest outside stadium ahead of Napoli clash on November 1 - VIDEO

TheTools · 01.11.2026 18:30 · source · permalink

ByLorenzo Bettoni

Juventus ultras are protesting outside the Allianz Stadium before a home Serie A match against Napoli: ‘Respect our shirt.’

Juventus sit ninth after nine rounds and have lost two of their last three league games.

Tonight, they host Napoli at the Allianz Stadium in Turin, but the atmosphere among the home supporters is far from welcoming.

A video posted by Ilbianconero on X shows hundreds of ultras outside the Curva Sud shouting: ‘Respect our shirt.’

Die-hard Juventus supporters also held a banner reading: “129 years of glory, our shirt is history, give us back our Juventus.”
