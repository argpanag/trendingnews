Team news: Havertz and Calafiori return against Sunderland

TheTools · 12.09.2026 22:06 · source · permalink

Mikel Arteta has made four changes to the side that beat Napoli in midweek as we take on Sunderland at the Stadium of Light.

After our European exploits on Wednesday night, the manager has reverted to the team that started our last league match, the win over Chelsea last weekend.

That means Riccardo Calafiori comes back into the defence, while Myles Lewis-Skelly is restored in midfield. Christos Tzolis – fresh from his match-winning assist in the Champions League – starts on the left, while Kai Havertz leads the line up front.

The four to drop to the bench are Piero Hincapie, Mikel Merino, Eberechi Eze and Viktor Gyokeres.

There is also a place among the subs for Jurrien Timber, back from injury to be included in the squad for the first time this season.

Sunderland beat Hull City 1-0 in midweek in the Carabao Cup, but their line up today is the same that started their last Premier League match, a 1-1 draw at Brentford last weekend.

So former Gunners Granit Xhaka and Dan Ballard – who scored in this fixture last season – both start.

LINE-UPS

Arsenal: Raya, White, Gabriel, Konsa, Calafiori, Lewis-Skelly, Rice, Odegaard, Saka, Tzolis, Havertz.

Subs: Arrizabalaga, Hincapie, Timber, Zubimendi, Merino, Guimaraes, Eze, Madueke, Gyokeres.

Sunderland: Roefs, Reinildo, Danso, Mukiele, Ballard, Meunier, Le Fee, Sadiki, Angulo, Xhaka, Brobbey.

Subs: Ellborg, Hume, O’Nien, Alderete, Methalie, Talbi, Rigg, Fofana, Isidor.
