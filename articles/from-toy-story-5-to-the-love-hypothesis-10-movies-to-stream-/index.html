From 'Toy Story 5' to 'The Love Hypothesis,' 10 movies to stream now

TheTools · 26.09.2026 07:16 · source · permalink

Need a new movie to watch? Stream these 10 at home for free with Netflix, Hulu, Prime Video, HBO Max, Disney+ and Paramount+.

  • One of these movies is what you should watch tonight.
  • Check out theatrical releases you can watch at home like "Toy Story 5," "Backrooms" and "Jackass: Best and Last."
  • Be sure to check out original movies on streaming, too, like Netflix's "Unabomber" and Prime Video's "The Love Hypothesis."

Did you miss "Backrooms," the box-office horror hit that makes a scary movie out of couches, tables and other furniture? HBO Max has your hookup – no layaway plan needed.

Kane Parsons' mind-bending chiller is one of several movies hitting your favorite streaming services. Woody, Buzz and the gang return for another animated adventure in "Toy Story 5," now playing on Disney+. And Lili Reinhart and Tom Bateman headline the rom-com "The Love Hypothesis" on Amazon's Prime Video, while Johnny Knoxville closes his "Jackass" film run with a "Best and Last" effort on Paramount+.

Here are 10 new and notable movies you can stream right now:

'Backrooms'

Kane Parsons tapped into a generation of young horror fans with this unnerving chiller. Chiwetel Ejiofor stars as the owner of a furniture store who discovers an otherworldly area on the other side of the walls. When he goes missing, his therapist (Renate Reinsve) searches for him and also finds a plethora of creepy claustrophobia and overwhelming anxiety.

Where to watch: HBO Max

'Best of the Best'

In what's pretty much "Pitch Perfect" with South Asian dancing, Maya (Maitreyi Ramakrishnan) and Anjali (Priyanka Kedia) are besties and UCLA freshman who make the Bollywood-fusion dance team. They navigate personality issues within the group and go for a national championship in a derivative comedy with fun dance sequences.

Where to watch: Netflix

'Jackass: Best and Last'

What are we going to do without a movie series that features human marionettes getting tased and dudes getting creatively smacked in the groin? Johnny Knoxville finishes off his "Jackass" film run with a bunch of new bits showing people doing stupid things to each other, plus greatest hits (sometimes literally) from the previous efforts.

Where to watch:Paramount+

'The Love Hypothesis'

Based on Ali Hazelwood's best-selling novel, the rom-com centers on a brilliant doctoral candidate (Lili Reinhart) and a dreaded biology professor (Tom Bateman) – nicknamed "Dr. Evil" by students – who fake a relationship to help each other out. Naturally, they catch feelings for each other but not without a good helping of emotional drama.

Where to watch: Prime Video

'Rich Flu'

In this "eat the rich" social satire, Mary Elizabeth Winstead stars as a corporate executive who lands a fortune. It couldn't happen at a worst time, though, as a mysterious virus begins to kill the wealthiest people in the world, the sickness causes everybody else to turn on them, and the fat cats try to get rid of their money in order to save themselves.

Where to watch: Hulu

'Stop! That! Train!'

Like "Airplane!" with drag queens, the disaster-movie spoof stars Ginger Minj and Jujubee, who hop from being low-level Stank Rail train stewardesses to the luxury line Glamazonian just in time to deal with a "Stormaganza" and a haunted railway. Everything about this comedy is campy and fabulous, from fun cameos to RuPaul as the president.

Where to watch:Netflix

'Supergirl'

Milly Alcock is a joy to watch as Kara, a jaded, self-destructive protagonist living in a gnarly cosmic landscape in this scrappy DC superhero adventure. When her beloved dog Krypto is poisoned, Kara teams with a vengeful teen (Eve Ridley) and a rambunctious bounty hunter (Jason Momoa) to hunt down the vicious outlaw responsible.

Where to watch: HBO Max

'Toy Story 5'

Ragdoll cowgirl Jessie (voiced by Joan Cusack) takes the reins in the newest installment, which smartly brings the franchise into the Internet era. Bonnie gets a tablet computer named Lilypad (Greta Lee), who helps connect the girl with friends but is a smarmy existential threat to Woody (Tom Hanks), Buzz (Tim Allen) and the gang.

Where to watch: Disney+

'Tuner'

Leo Woodall stars in the heist thriller as Niki, a piano tuner with sensitive hearing, perfect pitch and an allergy to noise. When his partner Harry (Dustin Hoffman) is hospitalized, Niki uses his ears to make some extra cash as an ace safecracker, though his criminal side gig threatens a new romance with a gifted composer (Havana Rose Liu).

Where to watch: Paramount+

'Unabomber'

The unconventional biopic depicts two time periods in the life of domestic terrorist Ted Kaczynski. Jacob Tremblay plays a teenage Ted in a story line set in the 1950s, as the young Harvard student is manipulated by his professor (Russell Crowe), while in the mid-1990s, Shailene Woodley is the FBI agent leading the hunt for Kaczynski's capture.

Where to watch:Netflix
