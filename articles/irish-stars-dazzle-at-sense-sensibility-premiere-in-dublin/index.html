Irish stars dazzle at Sense & Sensibility premiere in Dublin

TheTools · 18.09.2026 10:31 · source · permalink

Getty

It was a starry night in Dublin last night, as the stars of the new adaptation of Sense & Sensibility took to the red carpet for the film's Irish premiere.

The event took place at Dublin's Stella Cinema in Rathmines and saw a host of Irish celebrities attend.

The film, which has been adapted for the big screen by Georgia Oakley, stars Daisy Edgar-Jones and Caitríona Balfe and is a reimagining of the classic novel of the same name by Jane Austen.

Edgar-Jones, who plays the lead role of Elinor Dashwood, looked ethereal on the night in a white silk bias-cut slip dress by Stella McCartney, featuring ruffled detailing on the straps that extended down the back to create the illusion of angel wings.

She paired this with classic pearl-drop earrings and wore her hair in a tousled updo.

Balfe, meanwhile, continued her homage to Irish designers, which saw her wear looks by Standing Ground and Dior by Jonathan Anderson while promoting two other projects at the Toronto International Film Festival.

For last night's event, she once again represented Ireland, wearing McQueen by Dubliner Seán McGirr, styled by Nicky Yates. She chose a black co-ord, pairing a short black jacket with a matching mini skirt, hemmed with ornate white floral appliqué detailing.

She paired this with an oversized white bow worn at the neck and trailing behind her, as well as classic black stiletto heels.

Doireann Garrihy stepped in as host on the night, wearing a dusky pink mini dress with lace inserts and full sleeves. She paired this with gold strappy sandals.
