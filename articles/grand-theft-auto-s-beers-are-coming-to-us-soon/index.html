GRAND THEFT AUTO's Beers Are Coming to Us Soon

TheTools · 26.10.2026 21:32 · source · permalink

Grand Theft Auto VI beer is coming to you in real life! The US Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau have approved labels for three special edition GTA beer products. Two of the labels are featured in GTA titles, Patriot Beer and PiBwasser (Pisswasser). And also on the way is a new label called No Mames Vice City.

Nerdist’s Take: This Seems Like Fun for Those Who Drink, But a Non-Alcoholic Option Would Be Great, Too

The new label will feature a small dog wearing sunglasses and a vice city chain. Not only is No Mames Vice City new, it is the only label that will contain 6% of alcohol. In contrast, the two labels featured in the game will contain 4.2%, similar to the percentage in a Bud Light. I’m sure kids are disappointed knowing this treat isn’t for them. It would be great to see them announce a non-alcoholic version so all players can enjoy!

Tripping Animals, a Florida brewery, is creating the new product. There isn’t any information on when the beer will release, even though Grand Theft Auto VI is releasing on November 19 for Xbox Series X|S and PS5.

One might assume the product will be exclusive to Miami bars as Rockstar Games has filled the city with GTA 6 advertisements. The company has taken over Florida altogether with a giant Vice City blimp flying over the entire state. Also, the Miami Heat basketball arena will undergo transformation in honor of a Vice City themed NBA game in November. 

Hopefully the drinks are online for those who wish to indulge, but it may be limited to in-person activations. We certainly hope not considering that there are fans all over the USA who would love to get a GTA beer. I wouldn’t be surprised if they announce a major food collaboration next.
