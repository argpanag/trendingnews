ROB RUN Servers Back Online After Nearly 4-Hour Maintenance: Wipe Status and Patch Recap

TheTools · 19.11.2026 11:34 · source · permalink
  • Home
  • News
  • ROB RUN Servers Back Online After Nearly 4-Hour Maintenance: Wipe Status and Patch Recap

Maya Soler

ROB RUN servers are back online after November 18 maintenance stretched from an estimated 30 minutes to nearly four hours. Kefir's servers went offline at 21:00 UTC on November 18, and the developer confirmed their return shortly before 00:50 UTC on November 19. Several servers failed to restart, forcing the team to involve its data center staff.

Kefir has not announced an extra wipe tied to this outage. A maintenance restart does not itself trigger a Federal Raid, ROB RUN's scheduled seven-day State wipe. Players whose bases or stored loot are missing should check their State's age and raid schedule. No separate gameplay patch notes accompanied the maintenance announcement; the latest detailed patch remains the November 17 update.

November 19 Server Status and Maintenance Timeline

The shutdown was scheduled for 22:00 CET (21:00 UTC) on November 18, with an estimated duration of 30 minutes or longer. Kefir extended the window twice as the restart ran into problems. Some servers remained offline after the maintenance work, and the game could not reopen until the affected machines were restored.

The team eventually brought in the data center staff. Its restoration notice arrived around 00:50 UTC on November 19, ending an outage of approximately three hours and 45 minutes. Players can log back in following that recovery notice.

The distinction between a restart and a Federal Raid matters. Every State reaches its wipe seven days after opening, so ordinary raids can fall close to a maintenance window. A server returning online does not mean that all States have started a new seven-day cycle. Check the specific State you were playing before treating missing property as a rollback or an additional wipe.

November 13 Anti-Cheat Update: Over 20,000 Accounts Banned

On November 13, Kefir reported banning more than 20,000 accounts for cheating. The studio said it was improving cheat detection to identify offenders faster and working on further ways to limit their impact on matches. That figure was announced before this week's maintenance; it is not a ban count from the November 18 shutdown.

Kefir followed up with an updated ROB RUN Ban Policy on November 17. The rules cover unauthorized cheats and automation, exploit abuse, real-money item trading, ban evasion and stream sniping used to gain an unfair advantage. Penalties depend on the violation and can range from temporary account restrictions to bans lasting several years. The more visible death-screen Report button gives players a faster route to flag suspicious encounters.

Future Q&A Roadmap: Weapon Repairs and Easier Server Switching

Kefir's November 13 developer Q&A confirmed that weapon repair is planned for a future update. At present, gear loses durability when its owner dies, and players cannot repair weapons. The system is intended to remove equipment from circulation and maintain demand for replacement gear, keeping ROB RUN Cash relevant to the game's equipment economy. The developer has not announced how repairs will work, what they will cost or when they will arrive.

The team also wants to make server switching simpler. Currently, players have to surrender and return to the Prison to move to another server. Kefir acknowledged the inconvenience but has not announced a replacement interface or launch date.

Police duty is under review too. Officer slots remain limited to preserve the cops-versus-criminals balance, and the team plans to improve the experience of waiting for an available slot. Meanwhile, drop-off point camping remains permitted, and solo criminals retain their starting Notoriety level 4 and bonuses for collecting loot and earning ROB RUN Cash through sales. These points describe the current rules, not changes scheduled for the next patch.

Future Seasons: Nine Features Without Release Dates

ROB RUN's in-game In Future Seasons preview shows Weapon Table, Investments, Cash-in-Transit Guards, Plane, Civilian Jobs, Journalist, Base Defense, Grenade Launcher and Laboratory.

These nine cards are future-season previews. Kefir has not published release dates or detailed mechanics for them, and none was identified as a feature added during the November 18 maintenance.
