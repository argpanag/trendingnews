Haze: Johor, Melaka, Negri Sembilan and Putrajaya schools to close on Oct 13

TheTools · 12.10.2026 16:13 · source · permalink

PETALING JAYA: All schools in Johor, Melaka, Negri Sembilan and Putrajaya will be closed on Tuesday (Oct 13) due to haze.

Education Minister Datuk Seri Fadhlina Sidek said the closure also involves schools under the Hulu Langat, Petaling Perdana, Kuala Langat and Sepang District Education Offices (PPDs) in Selangor, as well as those under the Kuantan and Rompin PPDs in Pahang.

“Schools in Kuala Lumpur, Labuan, Kedah, Kelantan, Perlis, Penang, Perak, Terengganu, Sabah and Sarawak will operate as usual.

“Schools in other districts in Selangor and Pahang that are not affected by the closure will also remain open,” she said in a Facebook post on Monday.

Schools that remain open are advised to ensure that outdoor activities are monitored in accordance with current air quality conditions.

She urged parents and school communities to refer to official announcements by their respective schools, state education departments and their respective district education offices for the latest updates.
