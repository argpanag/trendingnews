Former number one overall draft pick Australian Ben Simmons has agreed to a one-year, $US3.5 million ($A4.9 million) NBA deal with ‌the Sacramento Kings, his agents confirmed to ESPN.

The three-time All-Star point guard returns after sitting ‌out the 2025-26 campaign while recuperating from chronic back issues.

Simmons, 30, last played with the Los Angeles Clippers for 18 games in March and April in 2025.

Reports last ‌month indicated ‌that ⁠Simmons was healthy, practising without restrictions and ​eyeing an NBA comeback. 

He also is looking to compete with the Australian Boomers at the 2028 Summer Olympics in Los Angeles.

Drafted No. 1 by Philadelphia in 2016 ⁠out of Louisiana State University, Simmons ‌made ​his injury-delayed NBA debut a year later and ​won 2017-18 Rookie ‌of the Year honours. 

He was a two-time All-Defensive ​pick before more injuries derailed his career. He has not played more than 58 games ​in ​a season since 2018-19.

Simmons ​has averaged 13.1 points, ‌7.4 rebounds, 7.2 assists and 1.5 steals in 383 career games (344 starts) with the 76ers (2017-21), Brooklyn Nets (2022-25) and the Clippers.

The Kings open the NBA preseason on October ​5 against the visiting Los Angeles Lakers.

Sacramento visits ​the Los ⁠Angeles Clippers for the regular-season opener on ​October 21.
