Lam Research Stocks Fall 3.2% as Evercore Cuts Target Before Earnings

TheTools · 22.10.2026 03:55 · source · permalink

Chipmaking-equipment supplier Lam Research LRCX traded at $263.48, down approximately 3.20%, on October 20, as investors weighed valuation ahead of its next earnings report. The earlier quote was recorded at 11.34 a.m. ET; the updated price has no specified intraday timestamp. The Fly reported that Evercore ISI lowered its price target to $315 from $355 while keeping an Outperform rating. That leaves a bullish recommendation with a smaller cushion.

The cash numbers deserve attention. Lam's June-quarter release showed operating cash flow of $1.457 billion against net income of $2.277 billionroughly 64% conversion by calculation. Receivables climbed 29.2% sequentially to $5.340 billion, outpacing revenue growth of 15.1%. More sales were sitting in unpaid customer balances at quarter-end. That warrants scrutiny, but does not establish collection trouble or fading demand. Jul 29, 2026

The valuation graphic adds pressure: shares stand 64.59% above the $160.08 GF Value estimate, suggesting substantial expectations are already priced in. That estimate is a benchmark, not a guaranteed destination. My next checkpoint is simple: can cash collections catch up with sales? Stronger operating cash flow and slower receivables growth would help support the premium. Another quarter of earnings racing ahead of cash would leave investors with more questions.
