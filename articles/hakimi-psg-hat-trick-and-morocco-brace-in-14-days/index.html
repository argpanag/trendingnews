Achraf Hakimi is enjoying an extraordinary run of form. Just two weeks after scoring the first hat-trick of his career for Paris Saint-Germain, the Morocco captain recorded his first brace for the senior national team, once again taking center stage in a 5-1 victory.

Two Fridays, Two Big Wins, and Five Goals From Hakimi

Friday, October 30, 2026, became a memorable date in Achraf Hakimi's career. The Moroccan full-back was the star of PSG's 5-1 victory over Le Havre in Ligue 1, scoring the first hat-trick of his professional career.

That night, Hakimi found the net in the 28th, 55th, and 69th minutes, while also contributing an assist as the Parisian side came from behind to secure the victory.

Exactly 14 days later, on Friday, November 13, he delivered another unforgettable performance, this time representing his country.

In Morocco's 5-1 victory over Niger in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations Qualifiers, the PSG star scored his first brace for the senior national team.

Two matches, five goals, two victories by identical scorelines, and a remarkable streak that highlights the Moroccan captain's outstanding form.


Two Stunning Goals for an Unforgettable Night

What made his performance against Niger so impressive was not simply the brace, but the spectacular manner in which he scored both goals.

In the 27th minute, Hakimi unleashed a sensational strike from outside the penalty area, sending the ball into the top corner and leaving the opposing goalkeeper with no chance.

But he still had another moment of brilliance in store.

Deep into first-half stoppage time, after Niger had pulled a goal back and threatened to equalize, the captain produced an extraordinary direct free kick to restore Morocco's comfortable advantage.

The full-back also provided an assist for Youssef En-Nesyri, who scored the fourth goal of the match in the 77th minute.

Hakimi finished the night with two goals and an assist, directly contributing to three of the Atlas Lions' five goals.

Hakimi Is Enjoying One of the Best Periods of His Career

At 28 years old, Achraf Hakimi continues to demonstrate that his influence extends far beyond his defensive responsibilities.

His pace, ability to join the attack, and exceptional striking technique have made him one of the most important attacking weapons for both Paris Saint-Germain and Morocco.

With his brace against Niger, the captain inspired the Atlas Lions to their third consecutive victory in the AFCON 2027 Qualifiers, maintaining their perfect record with nine points from a possible nine.

But the statistic that best captures his remarkable form is even more impressive: in just 14 days, Hakimi went from celebrating his first professional hat-trick to scoring his first international brace.

And he achieved both milestones in matches that ended with exactly the same scoreline: 5-1.


Key Takeaways From Hakimi's Incredible Run

  • October 30: Hakimi scored his first professional hat-trick for PSG against Le Havre.
  • November 13: He recorded his first brace for Morocco against Niger, scoring two spectacular goals and providing an assist.
  • 14 days and five goals: The Moroccan captain inspired two 5-1 victories for his club and national team.
