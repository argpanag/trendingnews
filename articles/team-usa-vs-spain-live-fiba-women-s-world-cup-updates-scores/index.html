Halftime: USA 39, Spain 36

It looked like Team USA was starting to pull away, but Spain fought right back to make it a one-possession game at halftime.

The United States' offensive success has almost entirely come from drawing fouls. They shot just 36 percent from the field, with 19 of their 39 points coming at the free-throw line.

On the other side, Spain has attempted just four free throws. Their success has come from quick pace and ball movement, assisting on 10 of their 15 field-goals made.

Breanna Stewart leads all scorers with nine points on 2-of-3 shooting from the field and 4-of-7 from the free-throw line. Spain's 20-year-old duo of Iyana Martín and Awa Fam has combined for 15 points on 6-of-8 shooting.

Iyana Martín Magic

As the shot clock winds down, 20-year-old guard Iyana Martín gets Angel Reese in the air with a pump fake to create space for this ridiculous off-balanced 3-pointer to give Spain their first points of the second quarter.

Oh yeah, then two minutes later, she went to work in isolation against Breanna Stewart, and hit this shot at the rim.

End of the 1Q: USA 21, Spain 18

After a slow start, Team USA fought back and ended the quarter on a 9-2 run to retake the lead, sparked by their defense.

Team USA started to apply pressure defensively, forcing seven turnovers over the final six minutes of the quarter. This created plenty of opportunities for them in transition.

Nine of Team USA's 23 points have come at the free-throw line. Spain scored 18 points on 44 percent from the field, with Awa Fam and María Conde combining for 10 of those points.

Awa Fam Comes Out Firing

Awa Fam is only 20 years old, but she isn't afraid of the moment. She got the action started with a contested catch-and-shoot 3-pointer, then found Raquel Carrera with a beautiful pass on a cut a minute later.

Spain has started 6-of-8 from the field to take a 13-6 lead over Team USA through the first five minutes. Fam leads all scorers with five points early.

Starting Lineups

No surprise here. Team USA is sticking with their all-veteran starting lineup for a fifth straight game:

Chelsea Gray, Jackie Young, Kahleah Copper, Napheesa Collier and Breanna Stewart.

On the other side, Spain is starting Iyana Martín, Maite Cazorla, María Conde, Raquel Carrera and Awa Fam. All of which have WNBA experience.
