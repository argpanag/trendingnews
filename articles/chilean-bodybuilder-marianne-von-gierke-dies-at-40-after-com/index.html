Chilean Bodybuilder Marianne von Gierke Dies at 40 After Competing at Mr. Olympia

TheTools · 16.10.2026 19:29 · source · permalink

NPC Worldwide Chile announced von Gierke’s death in an Oct. 15 Instagram post, calling her a woman of exceptional strength who brought deep passion to bodybuilding. The organization did not disclose where or when she died or provide a cause of death.

Von Gierke recently realized her goal of reaching the Olympia stage and placed 13th in the Women’s Physique field. The organization said bodybuilding had lost an accomplished athlete whose smile, enthusiasm, and influence would endure.

The organization said her work and competitive accomplishments would remain part of the sporting community’s memory. It offered condolences to von Gierke’s relatives, friends and others grieving her death.

Von Gierke’s death follows several deaths involving prominent bodybuilders and fitness figures this year.

Santos earned professional status with the International Federation of Bodybuilding and Fitness in April 2023 after capturing the overall amateur Men’s Physique championship at the Arnold Classic South America in São Paulo. He later competed professionally in Brazil while building an Instagram audience of about 32,000 followers.

Earlier this year, Brazilian fitness influencer Gabriel “Bbzinho” Ganley, 22, died May 23 from sudden cardiac death caused by hypertrophic cardiomyopathy. Ganley had amassed more than 2.4 million Instagram followers.
