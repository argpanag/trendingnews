Vivo V80 Lite 5G Launch Soon: Vivo V80 Lite Expected to Debut in India - Check Expected Date & Time, Price, Specs, Camera, Battery, Features & What to Expect

TheTools · 08.10.2026 08:57 · source · permalink

Vivo V80 Lite 5G Launch Soon: The Vivo V80 Lite 5G has generated immense buzz following its recent international rollouts, which showcase massive battery tech upgrades. While the standard Vivo V80 officially debuted in India on 6 October 2026, its “Lite” sibling is expected to join the Indian market shortly. Interestingly, Vivo just released the closely identical Vivo S2 FE in India. Since the global V80 Lite shares nearly identical DNA with the S2 FE, the final Indian variant of the V80 Lite 5G is highly anticipated to follow this blueprint.

Vivo V80 Lite 5G Launch Soon: Expected Date

Vivo has not locked down a specific date for India yet, but industry insiders expect the device to debut by the end of October 2026 or early November 2026 at 12:00 PM IST.

Vivo V80 Lite 5G Launch Soon: Expected Price

Based on regional conversions from its international launches, the Vivo V80 Lite 5G is anticipated to start at around ₹28,999 to ₹31,999 for the base variant.

Vivo V80 Lite 5G Launch Soon: Key Specifications & Features

  • Display: 6.83-inch AMOLED curved-edge panel, 1.5K FHD+ resolution, 120Hz refresh rate, 3000 nits peak brightness.

  • Processor: MediaTek Dimensity 7360-Turbo (or Dimensity 7300e) 4nm-class octa-core processor.

  • Memory & Storage: 8GB RAM paired with 128GB or 256GB of internal storage.

  • Rear Camera: Dual setup 50MP primary sensor (Sony IMX852) with OIS + 2MP depth/bokeh sensor, accompanied by a ring LED flash.

  • Front Camera: 32MP or 50MP selfie shooter nestled in a punch-hole cutout.

  • Battery & Charging: Massive 7,050mAh or 10,000mAh battery with 44W or 100W fast charging support (depending on the exact global variant brought to India).

  • OS & UI: Funtouch OS 17 (or OriginOS) based on Android 16/17 out of the box.

  • Durability & Build: IP68/IP69 dual water/dust resistance rating and MIL-STD-810H military-grade drop durability.

Vivo V80 Lite 5G Launch Soon: Regional Pricing & Launch Availability Context

While India eagerly awaits the official V80 Lite 5G release, its international pricing gives a clear indicator of where it will sit:

  • Middle East Pricing: The phone launched at 1,499 AED (approx. ₹34,000) for the 128GB version and 1,699 AED (approx. ₹38,900) for the 256GB version.

  • The India Angle: Vivo recently launched the Vivo S2 FE in India for ₹38,999, which features the 10,000mAh battery configuration. Vivo V80 Lite 5G Launch Soon: What to Expect

The standout narrative for the Vivo V80 Lite 5G is its battery endurance.
