Furyk falls for hurling as Ryder Cup countdown continues

TheTools · 06.10.2026 16:24 · source · permalink

The task facing USA Ryder Cup captain Jim Furyk isn't an easy one.

The 2003 US Open champion is hoping to become the first US captain to win on European soil in over 30 years. His team will also head to Ireland on the back of consecutive Ryder Cup losses in 2023 and 2025.

Speaking at a media briefing at Adare Manor, where the 2027 edition is to be held, Furyk was asked about his crowd expectations given some of the unruly scenes witnessed at Bethpage, New York, last year.

Former USA captain and player Tom Watson said he felt "ashamed" over the behaviour, but Furyk doesn’t expect to see similar scenes in Limerick next year.

"The players (European and American) see each other all the time and are friendly, but for the Ryder Cup, they put that aside for the week," he told RTÉ Sport.

"When done properly, you can be the fiercest competitors, and also be a gentleman and embrace the spirit of the Ryder Cup.

"I expect the European crowd to be loud and boisterous, but I do believe it will be done in the proper fashion.

"Things crossed the line at Bethpage, no doubt about it. The PGA of America, Ryder Cup USA have taken steps forward for that not to happen.

"We want the games to be played in the proper spirit, both teams want to win in the proper way."

It marks Furyk’s third trip to Ireland since he was named as team captain - he was the losing Ryder Cup captain at Paris in 2018 – and was an interested spectator in May at TUS Gaelic Grounds as Limerick cruised past Tipperary in the Munster Hurling Championship.

The 56-year-old has built up a relationship with Adare Manor owner and Limerick GAA benefactor JP McManus and says he has fallen in love with the sport.

"I’ve taken a real liking to hurling," he said. "I know it is JP McManus’ first love. He and his wife Noreen pretty much call the Limerick team their children.

"I got to learn the sport a little bit, go to my first game in May against Tipperary and kind of fell in love with it.

"My son was a college lacrosse player. The sports have some similarities. In the United States they likes to say that lacrosse is the fastest sport on the field. It has nothing compared to hurling."
