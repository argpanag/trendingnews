Nguyen Quynh Anh stripped of Miss Universe Vietnam title

TheTools · 19.11.2026 06:33 · source · permalink

Thursday, November 19, 2026, 13:33 GMT+7

The Miss Universe Vietnam organizers have stripped Nguyen Quynh Anh of her Miss Universe Vietnam 2026 crown, citing her continued participation in performances and events without prior approval from her management company, which they said had affected the pageant’s image.

Quynh Anh, Miss Universe Vietnam 2026, has been stripped of her beauty queen title. Photo: Kieng Can Studio

The Miss Universe Vietnam organizers on Wednesday evening announced that Nguyen Quynh Anh had been stripped of her title, effective the same day.

According to the announcement, following her first violation, the organizers and relevant parties reviewed the case and decided on disciplinary measures, including suspending Quynh Anh’s artistic performance activities for six months.

However, she subsequently attended a community event without notifying or obtaining prior approval from Five6 Entertainment JSC, her management company.

Certain incidents at the event led to misunderstandings and affected the image of Miss Universe Vietnam and its organizers, the announcement said.

“A few days ago, Nguyen Quynh Anh informed the organizers and Five6 of her mental health condition, saying she needed time to rest and receive treatment and was not yet able to sign the management contract as initially agreed.

“However, she continued accepting performance engagements and attending events without reaching an agreement with Five6.

“In addition, the organizers noted that Nguyen Quynh Anh had appeared at an event of another management company, where an exclusive contract was announced, without first consulting the Miss Universe Vietnam organizers and Five6,” the statement said.

Earlier, on the evening of October 13, Quynh Anh apologized after screenshots of messages from a private group chat involving contestants of Miss Universe Vietnam 2026 circulated online.

The messages contained disparaging comments and discussions about several people in the pageant.

In her apology, she wrote: “Quynh Anh apologizes to the women concerned and everyone affected by this incident, especially the audience.

“I understand that holding a beauty queen title does not make me incapable of making mistakes. However, the title requires me to be more mindful and careful about my words, conduct and responsibilities, even in seemingly minor matters.

“I sincerely acknowledge anything I have said or done that was inappropriate, and I will work to correct and improve myself.”

She added that personal growth was not about always pleasing everyone or presenting only a positive image, but about learning to behave more thoughtfully and strive to become a better person, even when confronted with her own shortcomings.

She said the criticism had taught her an important lesson about moderation, respect and responsibility.
