Toukie Smith—Model, Actress and Robert De Niro's Ex—Dead at 74

TheTools · 09.11.2026 20:50 · source · permalink

Toukie Smith, a model and 227 star who also shared two kids with ex Robert De Niro, died Nov. 4, her and De Niro’s children confirmed in a statement. She was 74. 

A model has left the world after making a tremendous impact. 

Toukie Smith, a model who was also known for her role as Eva Rawley in 227, died Nov. 4 after a “long, courageous battle” with multiple sclerosis, her and ex Robert De Niro’s children Airyn and Julian confirmed to The Hollywood Reporter. She was 74. 

Smith, born Doris A. Smith, was born in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania in 1952 and made her debut in the modeling industry in the 1970s, inspired by her brother Willi Smith, a renowned fashion designer and often used her as his muse. 

In 1989, she scored the role as Eva on the final seasons of the NBC sitcom that also starred Regina King, Marla Gibbs and Helen Martin. She went on to appear in Joe’s Apartment, Miami Vice and Goosed but later admitted it was hard for her to break through in Hollywood. 

"Everybody has to get it right," she told the New York Times in 1992. "I am an actress first. I'm not a sometime model.” 

Nevertheless, she joked, “I thought modeling was rough with rejection. Huh! Welcome to the real world of acting!”

But Smith’s life couldn’t simply be narrowed down into one career. In addition to acting as well as appearing in modeling campaigns for Chanel, Versace and Yves Saint Laurent, she also opened a restaurant, Toukie’s, in 1994. The establishment sat on West Houston in Manhattan and donated some of its profits to help those fighting AIDs in honor of her brother, who died from illness related to the disease in 1987. 

“I opened this place because big parties are one thing,” she told the New York Times after the restaurant opened, “but now it's time for us to come together, to connect.”

Smith and De Niro—who began dating in 1988—welcomed Airyn and Julian in 1995 through a surrogate. The former couple split about a year later, but while they often stepped out together, they rarely shared more insight into their romance. As she told the Los Angeles Times when asked about the romance in 1990, “What’s great about a private life is it’s private.”

What Smith was most known for, however, is synonymous with her nickname, which she said her grandmother gave her from a children’s song about a determined fire engine.

As she told the Los Angeles Times of the moniker, “I’ve always had lots of energy.”

For a deeper look at De Niro’s family tree, keep reading… 

First Wife Diahnne Abbott

Robert De Niro met his first wife, Diahnne Abbott, while making Martin Scorsese's Taxi Driver, in which she had a bit part as the ticket taker at a porn theater Travis Bickle patronizes in the gritty 1976 classic.

They tied the knot April 28, 1976.

The couple also appeared together in Scorsese's 1977 tortured-romance drama New York, New York and his 1982 satire The King of Comedy.

Daughter Drena De Niro

The actor adopted Diahnne's daughter Drena, born Sept. 3, 1967, when they got married.

Drena's first Instagram post is a black-and-white snap of her as a child out to dinner with her parents and then-couple Jack Nicholson and Anjelica Huston, captioned "At this point all I wonder is WTF was I doing there ???"

She caught the creative bug early, though, and remained in show business as an actor and producer.

Growing up, "it definitely wasn't the typical family lifestyle," she told Everything Zoomer, "but there was a lot of warmth, fun, craziness." She described her father as "a non-judgmental, open-minded person, always accepting of what people feel their paths are."

Son Raphael De Niro

De Niro and Abbott's son Raphael De Niro was born Nov. 9, 1976.

Raphael later recalled the family moving from Los Angeles (where his father was born) to New York (the city De Niro is most associated with) when he was about 10, telling The Real Deal it was a major "culture shock."

De Niro and Abbott divorced in 1988.

Raphael went into real estate, which also runs in the family. Per his website, he leads The De Niro Team at Douglas Elliman in Manhattan, and he's a partner in TriBeCa's Greenwich Hotel. 

He shares three kids with ex-wife Claudine de Matos and has been married to stylist Hannah Carnes since March 2020.

Acknowledging his last name alone turned him into fodder for tabloid coverage, Raphael told The Real Deal, "I just try to keep my head down and keep my private life private."

Grandson Leandro De Niro Rodriguez

Leandro De Niro Rodriguez, Drena's son with artist ex Carlos Rodriguez, died of a suspected overdose on July 2, 2023. He was 19.

On Aug. 11, 2023, which would have been her son's 20th birthday, Drena wrote on Instagram, "Thank you for the happiest and most profound 2 decades of my life, little angel. I hope you are at peace and your spirit is filled with joy today and always."

De Niro called his grandson's death a shock, telling People, "And I just then started thinking about all the things I could have, should have done maybe with him. I don't know if that would've made a difference. And so that's always playing through my mind. It shouldn't have happened."

Ex-Girlfriend Toukie Smith

De Niro and model-actress Toukie Smith started dating in 1988, but ended their romantic relationship several years before their twins Julian and Airyn were born via surrogate on Oct. 20, 1995.

The exes would be leading "separate personal and professional lives," De Niro's rep said in a statement at the time, but "look forward to sharing the parenting of the children."

A rep for Smith said, per the New York Times, that the pair "obviously still have a very strong bond."

Daughter Airyn De Niro

Airyn came out as a transgender woman in April 2025. She told Them Magazine that her journey also brought her closer to her racial identity.

"I think a big part of [my transition] is the influence Black women have had on me," she said. "I think stepping into this new identity, while also being more proud of my Blackness, makes me feel closer to them in some way." 

As for who the actress aspires to be like? Her mother, of course.

"Whenever I'm at a family gathering on my dad's side, people would always talk about how much they love my mom and how memorable she was and how happy my dad was when he was with her," she shared. "I want to be like that… I want to be remembered the way that people remember her."

De Niro signaled that his feelings for his child hadn't changed in any way, saying in a statement, "I loved and supported Aaron as my son and now I love and support Airyn as my daughter. I don't know what the big deal is. I love all my children."

Son Julian De Niro

Julian is a stage and screen actor who appeared on Broadway in the Tony-nominated The Sign in Sidney Brustein's Window. In 2022 he played a young Barack Obama in the Showtime limited series The First Lady and appeared on the Netflix series Zero Day, starring his dad as a former POTUS investigating a deadly cyberattack.

"For my kids, I tell them, 'If you want to be an actor or you want to do this or that, that's fine as long as you're happy. Just don't sell yourself short,'" De Niro told People in January 2020. "That's the most I would say—push yourself a little more and reach for what you really think it is you want to do. Don't be afraid."

Second Wife Grace Hightower

De Niro married Grace Hightower on June 17, 1997.

The welcomed son Elliot De Niro on March 18, 1998. De Niro filed for divorce from the Precious actress in August 1999, but they reconciled and renewed their vows in 2004.

“They can make sure this one sticks," the actor joked of the two judges who officiated, per People.

In 2009, De Niro, then 66, told the Los Angeles Times, "My whole thing now is just to have the strength to keep my kids in line and get them to the point where they’re independent and self-reliant. I want to make sure my kids know I’m there, and you’ve got to have the stamina to do that.”

Daughter Helen Grace De Niro was born via surrogate on Dec. 23, 2011.

De Niro and Hightower split up for good in 2018, the Oscar winner saying in a statement, "Grace and I have two beautiful children together. We are entering a period of transition in our relationship which is a difficult but constructive process. I honor Grace as a wonderful mother and ask for privacy and respect from all as we proceed to develop our roles as partners in parenting."

Elliot De Niro

When promoting Silver Linings Playbook, in which he played a father whose grown son (played by Bradley Cooper) has bipolar disorder, De Niro opened up about having a child with special needs.

"I don't like to get emotional," he told Katie Couric in 2013, "but I know exactly what [my character] goes through."

He shared in 2016 that Elliot has autism.

Helen De Niro

"She's so adorable," De Niro said of his then 1-year-old daughter in 2013. He told CBS News, "You just look at her, and it's all there...Nothing else matters."

Helen made a rare red carpet appearance with her dad and his girlfriend Tiffany Chen at the 2025 Cannes Film Festival.

Girlfriend Tiffany Chen

De Niro was first photographed with Chen in August 2021, but his relationship with the tai chi expert wasn't the subject of much notice until after their daughter Gia Virginia Chen-De Niro was born on April 6, 2023.

"This baby was planned," Gayle King relayed in announcing Gia's arrival a month later on CBS Mornings, and "brought here by love."

 

Daughter Gia Chen-De Niro

While De Niro admittedly doesn't do the "heavy lifting," he waxed rhapsodic about having a baby in the house. 

"Everything that I'm consumed with or worried about just goes away when I look at her," he told AARP in January 2024. "It's wondrous."

And at 2 years old, Gia continued to be "pure joy," De Niro told Entertainment Tonight in June 2025. "It's always wonderful."
