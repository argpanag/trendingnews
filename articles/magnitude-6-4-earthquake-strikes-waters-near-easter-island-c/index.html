Magnitude 6.4 Earthquake Strikes Waters Near Easter Island, Chile - The Asia Business Daily

TheTools · 29.10.2026 09:12 · source · permalink

by Park SeungUk

Published 29 Oct.2026 18:12(KST)

A magnitude 6.4 earthquake struck near Easter Island, Chile.

Earthquake information, including instrumental intensity, is displayed on a monitor at the Korea Meteorological Administration in Dongjak-gu, Seoul. Yonhap News Agency

The Korea Meteorological Administration, citing foreign observatories and other sources, reported that an earthquake of this magnitude struck waters southeast of Easter Island, Chile, at 4:49 p.m. on October 29 (Korea time).
