Jay Shah, the ICC chair, is in attendance at the Asian Games men's cricket final and he, along with Hiroki Kondo (Japan), Mayor of Nisshin City, and Nilo Jayatolake (Sri Lanka), Olympic Council of Asia Executive Board Member and Vice-President, National Olympic Committee of Sri Lanka, will be at the medal ceremony. Shah is expected to present the medals, it is learnt.

This website already reported that Mohsin Naqvi, the Asian Cricket Council (ACC) president and the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) chair, wouldn't turn up for the Asian Games final. The ACC confirmed that via a post on its X (formerly Twitter) handle that said: “ACC President, Mr. Mohsin Naqvi, will miss Finals Day at #AsianGames2026 due to prior commitments, but sends his very best wishes to all four teams ahead of the Gold and Bronze Medal Matches.”

Naqvi’s absence means that there will be no drama at the presentation ceremony, like the Women's Asia Cup final last month when Harmanpreet Kaur and her troops refused to take the trophy from him. A year ago, the Indian men's team under then T20I captain Suryakumar Yadav did the same. Naqvi is also Pakistan's Interior Minister and made a series of provocative statements targeting India in the wake of the Pahalgam terror attack last year that killed 26 civilians.

Meanwhile, batting first in the final, India appear to be running away with the game. At the time of going to the print, they were 128/4 after 13.1 overs. Abhishek Sharma weighed heavy on the Pakistan bowlers, scoring 61 off 28 balls, hitting seven sixes.
