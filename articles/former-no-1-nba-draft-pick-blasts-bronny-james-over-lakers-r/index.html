Former No. 1 NBA Draft pick Kwame Brown criticized Bronny James' place on the Lakers roster, arguing that other deserving young players have missed out on NBA opportunities because of LeBron James' influence.

Former No. 1 overall NBA Draft pick Kwame Brown has called out Los Angeles Lakers guard Bronny James, questioning whether he deserves a roster spot in the NBA.

Brown believes it's a shame that Bronny has secured a place in the league ahead of other young players who have worked hard to earn an opportunity.

"It's mind-blowing how somebody lost a job to that," Brown stated on his YouTube channel.

"Some kid that worked their butt off that really feel like they could have made the NBA, they lost their job to that."

"And the only reason why everybody's okay with it is because of his father," he added.

"That's a shame what we actually are seeing. Call it hate, call it whatever you want. To me, it's a shame."

Bronny James is in his third season in the NBA after being selected by the Lakers in the second round of the 2024 NBA Draft.

This season, he has appeared in four games, averaging less than 3 minutes per game.

Last season, Bronny averaged 2.9 points, 0.6 rebounds, and 1.2 assists in 42 appearances for the Lakers.

James signed a four-year, $7.9 million rookie contract in 2024. He is scheduled to earn around $2.3 million in 2026-27, while the Lakers hold a team option worth roughly $2.5 million for the following season.
