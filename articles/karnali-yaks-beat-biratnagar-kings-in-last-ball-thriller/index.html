Karnali Yaks beat Biratnagar Kings in last-ball thriller

TheTools · 29.10.2026 16:45 · source · permalink

Copyright © 2025 Nepal News. All Rights Reserved.

Nepal News

KIRTIPUR: Karnali Yaks defeated Biratnagar Kings by four wickets in the Nepal Premier League (NPL) on Thursday, chasing a target of 187 on the final ball of the match to secure their second consecutive win.

Chasing 187, Karnali scored 187-6 in 19.5 overs. Captain Sompal Kami led the chase with an unbeaten 62 off 32 balls, including three sixes and three fours. Pawan Sarraf scored 36 off 28 balls, Ariful Islam added 32 off 12 balls, and Gulshan Kumar Jha remained unbeaten on 28 off 21 balls.

Earlier, Biratnagar posted 186-7 in 20 overs after Lokesh Bam scored 87 off 52 balls, including nine sixes and three fours. Angelo Mathews contributed 31 off 23 balls, while Andre Russell scored 27 off 15 balls.

Kami took four wickets for 33 runs for Karnali, while Mathews claimed three wickets for Biratnagar. Kami was named Player of the Match.

The win gave Karnali two victories in two matches, while Biratnagar suffered its second consecutive defeat.
