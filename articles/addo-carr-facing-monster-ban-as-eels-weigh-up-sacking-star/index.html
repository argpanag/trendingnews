Addo-Carr facing monster ban as Eels weigh up sacking star

TheTools · 17.10.2026 20:39 · source · permalink
  • Sport
  • NRL
  • NRL

October 17, 2026 - 8.39pm

Eels superstar Josh Addo-Carr is facing a monster suspension that could rub him out for half of next season, while the club weighs up whether to sack him.

The Sydney Morning Herald reports the winger is out of a rehabilitation clinic and is now awaiting the B sample after allegedly testing positive for cocaine.

Josh Addo-Carr allegedly returned a positive roadside test. Getty Images

Addo-Carr was stopped for a random roadside drug test last month in Sydney and will face police charges, pending the result of the secondary sample.

It’s a potential career-altering moment for Addo-Carr, who this time last year was playing for the Kangaroos in the Ashes series against England.

Now, while some of those same Australian teammates are vying for Rugby League World Cup glory, the 31-year-old is fighting for his future.

Addo-Carr was sacked by the Bulldogs at the end of 2024 after testing positive for cocaine, as well as misleading club officials during their investigation.

Parramatta threw the speedster a lifeline and extended his contract until the end of next season, after Addo-Carr became an integral part of the side’s backline and a popular figure among teammates, staff and fans.

Addo-Carr’s management have reportedly “pleaded” with the Eels not to tear up the former State of Origin star’s deal, worth an estimated $400,000.

Even if the Eels keep him, the NRL are expected to slap Addo-Carr with a 12-game suspension, which would account for half of the regular season in 2027.

Addo-Carr is one of the greatest wingers of the modern era, having won two premierships with the Storm and scoring 167 tries in 224 NRL matches.
