Argentina vs Burkina Faso: Preview

Argentina's legend Messi announced his international retirement following the World Cup, although he remains part of Scaloni's squad for the latest international break and is expected to make his final appearance for Argentina against Benin on October 6.

The legendary Barcelona and PSG forward is unlikely to feature against Burkina Faso, meaning this match provides another opportunity for Argentina's next generation to take centre stage.

The spotlight will naturally fall on the likes of Julián Álvarez, Lautaro Martínez, Alexis Mac Allister and Enzo Fernández, with Argentina possessing plenty of quality even without their iconic captain.

Burkina Faso arrive in Buenos Aires having secured a 1-0 victory over the Central African Republic in their latest 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier. The Stallions therefore have some momentum of their own, having only lost one of their last six games in all competitions.

Wrexham defender Issa Kabore featured for the whole game against the Central African Republic recently and will relish the chance to play against some of the games biggest stars in this one.
