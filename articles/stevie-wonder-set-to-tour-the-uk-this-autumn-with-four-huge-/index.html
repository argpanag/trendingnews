Stevie Wonder set to tour the UK this autumn with four huge live shows

The American singer-songwriter will visit four UK cities later this year

Stevie Wonder is set to bring his legendary catalogue of hits to UK stages for his Songs In The Key of Life Performances, 50th Anniversary Tour. The era-defining artist will visit Utilita Arena Birmingham on 13 October and The O2 in London on 3 November, before shows at Manchester’s Co-Op Live on 8 November and Glasgow’s OVO Hydro on 11 November.

Beginning his career at just eleven years old and breaking records since twelve, Stevie Wonder remains one of the most influential singer-songwriters, musicians and activists of the last century. ‘Superstition’, ‘Sir Duke’, ‘Isn’t She Lovely’, “Signed, Sealed, Delivered I’m Yours” and ‘I Just Called to Say I Love You’ are just a few of his most enduring hits – across his career, he’s had 32 No.1s in the US and 65 million streams.

Since the release of his debut album The Jazz Soul of Little Stevie, he’s sold over 100 million albums worldwide, won 25 Grammy Awards including a Lifetime Achievement Award, and seen his album Songs In The Key of Life archived in the National Recording Registry of the Library of Congress. This new tour celebrates the 50th anniversary of that historic album.

This announcement follows the success of 2025’s Love, Light & Song tour, which saw him play dates across the UK, including a headline slot at BST in Hyde Park.
