4 Young People Reported Missing in Singapore Over 3 Days; Latest Is 16-Year-Old Boy

TheTools · 03.10.2026 08:40 · source · permalink
  • News

Four young people have been reported missing in separate cases over three days in Singapore, with the latest police appeal involving 16-year-old Sajid Rahman.

According to The Straits Times, police said Sajid was last seen near 16 Jalan Tenteram at about 1.55pm on Friday, 2 October 2026.

He was wearing a dark blue T-shirt and dark-coloured pants when last seen.

Police issued the appeal for information on his whereabouts on Saturday, 3 October.

4 Missing-Person Appeals In 3 Days

Sajid is the fourth young person to be the subject of a police missing-person appeal over three days.

On Friday, 2 October, police appealed for information on 14-year-old Tianyi Chanel Maglio.

She was last seen near 1 St Wilfred Road in Boon Keng at about 9.30am on 25 September.

The Straits Times reported that the address is that of Bendemeer Secondary School. She was last seen wearing a white polo T-shirt with a dark collar and a greyish-blue skirt.

A day earlier, on Thursday, 1 October, police issued two other appeals involving girls aged 12 and 15.

The first involved 12-year-old Arianna Qaireen, who was last seen near Block 997C Buangkok Crescent at about 5.30pm on 29 September.

According to the police appeal, she was wearing a black shirt and black long pants when last seen.

Another 15-Year-Old Girl Last Seen In Pasir Ris

Police also appealed on 1 October for information on 15-year-old J’mies Oh Hui Min.

She was last seen near 3 Pasir Ris Rise at about 6.30am on 24 September.

Police said she was wearing a light blue polo T-shirt with dark short sleeves and a dark blue skirt when last seen.

Based on the public information available as of Saturday afternoon, 3 October, the appeals concern separate missing-person cases. Police have not stated in the appeals that the four cases are connected.

Anyone With Information Can Contact Police

Members of the public with information on any of the missing youths can call the Police Hotline at 1800-255-0000.

Police said all information provided will be kept strictly confidential.
