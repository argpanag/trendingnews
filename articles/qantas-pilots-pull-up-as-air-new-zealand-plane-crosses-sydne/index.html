Qantas pilots pull up as Air New Zealand plane crosses Sydney runway

TheTools · 26.11.2026 23:38 · source · permalink

Australian authorities are investigating after a Qantas flight climbed away from the runway at Sydney Airport when its pilots - who had not been yet cleared to land - spotted an Air New Zealand aircraft crossing the runway during their approach.

Flight QF426 performed a go-around shortly before 10.15am on Wednesday, before landing safely.

"Our pilots were following Air Traffic Control instructions and elected to conduct a go-around as the other aircraft was crossing the runway,” a Qantas spokesperson told 1News.

“We'll support the ATSB as it conducts the investigation."

The Australian Transport Safety Bureau (ATSB) has launched an investigation into the "aircraft separation issue".

It said the Qantas flight had been advised by air traffic controllers to expect a late landing clearance due to aircraft crossing the runway. The pilots had not been cleared to land.

"As the 737 continued the approach, and prior to receiving a clearance to land, the crew reported seeing an Air New Zealand A321 crossing the runway at taxiway Golf. The crew of the 737 elected to conduct a go-around."

Investigators will interview flight crew and air traffic controllers, review recorded air traffic control audio and CCTV footage, and examine relevant procedures.
