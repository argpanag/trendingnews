Steven Pressley plays down Dundee plaudits as he insists they can get much better

TheTools · 21.11.2026 17:12 · source · permalink

Steven Pressley is adamant his much-vaunted Dundee side still have so much to improve upon as they bid to justify the early-season hype.

The Dark Blues were widely praised after winning four of their opening eight William Hill Premiership games before a slight dip in form leading into the international break.

Pressley said ahead of Saturday’s visit from his former club Hearts: “The break has given us time to reflect on the opening 12 games of the season and have a more in-depth look at our performances.

“This has been a healthy period because we’ve obviously had some positive results, some real positive feelings around the club.

“But when I look further into things, there’s a lot of improvement that we need to make to our performances. We’ve won five, lost five and drawn two. And in the five games we’ve lost, every game has been by one goal.

“That just shows you how fine the margins are at present and I think that’s why the league’s very competitive. But it’s why I’ve not been getting overly excited by the plaudits the team has been receiving because I think the margins are fine.

“I think there’s a lot of improvements to come from us. But again, context. I remind ourselves of just how many players we lost in the summer. We’re still in our infancy in terms of the growth of this group of players.”
