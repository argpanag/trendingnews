Jackson County Judge Jeff Phillips judge announces retirement

TheTools · 10.11.2026 03:06 · source · permalink

JACKSON COUNTY, Ark. (KAIT) - Jackson County Judge Jeff Phillips is retiring at the end of his current term. Phillips has served in county government for 20 years and has served Jackson County residents for more than three decades, he said in a news release announcing his retirement.

Phillips said he was first elected to public office in 2007 as a justice of the peace for District 3. In 2010, he was elected Jackson County judge and championed economic development, infrastructure improvements, and fiscal responsibility.

“Serving the people of Jackson County not only as County Judge, but also 21 years in the Fire/EMS profession has been the greatest honor of my professional life,” Phillips said. “For most of my adult life, the residents of this county have placed their trust in me, and I have worked every single day to earn it. Together, we have overcome challenges of historic measures, and built a stronger, more resilient community. While I am retiring from office, my love for this county and its people will never fade.”

Colleagues and community leaders praised Phillips and the legacy he is leaving for residents.

“I am grateful to have worked with Judge Jeff Phillips for so many years. His faithful leadership to Jackson County will be felt for years to come. I wish him the best in his well-deserved retirement,” Ronald Caldwell, an Arkansas senator, said.

“I’ve had the pleasure of working with Judge Phillips since I first came into office in 2011. He’s been an outstanding leader for Jackson County and a friend. I’m grateful for his service to the people of Arkansas, his leadership during the natural disasters that Jackson County has faced, his steadfast partnership in supporting our farmers and agricultural economy, and the many ways he has helped those he serves. As he begins this next part of his life, I’m praying for him and his family to have both success and happiness,” U.S. Rep. Rick Crawford said.
