Taylor Swift Chose a Timeless Little Black Dress for a Dinner Date With Travis Kelce

TheTools · 20.09.2026 17:25 · source · permalink

THE RUNDOWN

  • Taylor Swift chose a structured LBD for her latest date night with Travis Kelce.
  • Swift and Kelce celebrated Patrick Mahomes’s 31st birthday at one of their go-to restaurants.
  • This specific strapless silhouette is one Swift gravitates toward.

Taylor Swift is making a case for the little black dress—again. On September 19, the singer stepped out for a date night with Travis Kelce in Kansas City, wearing a sleek strapless black midi dress.

Swift and Kelce celebrated Patrick Mahomes’s 31st birthday at the teammates’ Kansas City steakhouse.

The dress featured a fitted silhouette that skimmed Swift’s figure before falling into a midi-length skirt, with the curved neckline serving as its defining detail. She kept the rest of the look relatively pared back, carrying a small black handbag.

It’s hardly the first time Swift has turned to a black dress for a night out. Just days earlier, she wore a sleeveless black Rails midi dress with a dramatic V-neckline for another dinner at the steakhouse.

Her June appearance at the Songwriters Hall of Fame induction ceremony featured another strapless look, too. For that occasion, she wore a custom black Givenchy gown featuring a boned corset, a draped waist, a thigh-high slit, and a demi-train.

The two dresses are worlds apart in formality, but the similarities are hard to miss. Both rely on a structured, strapless bodice to create a defined silhouette, suggesting that Swift’s latest date-night look isn’t so much a departure from her recent style as a more pared-back take on a shape she clearly enjoys.
