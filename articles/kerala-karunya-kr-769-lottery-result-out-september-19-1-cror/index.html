The Kerala State Lotteries Department has declared the Karunya KR-769 lottery result for Saturday, September 19, 2026, with ticket KP 524700 from Payyanur winning the first prize of ₹1 crore. The second prize of ₹25 lakh went to KS 127632 from Mananthavady, while KS 536896 from Pattambi won the third prize of ₹10 lakh.

The draw was conducted at Gorky Bhavan near Bakery Junction in Thiruvananthapuram under official supervision. The results include several prize categories, giving winners across Kerala an opportunity to claim amounts ranging from ₹1 crore to ₹100, depending on the matching ticket number.

Karunya is one of the weekly lotteries conducted by the Kerala State Lotteries Department. The ticket is priced at ₹50, while the KR-769 draw carries a first prize of ₹1 crore. The second and third prizes are ₹25 lakh and ₹10 lakh, respectively. A consolation prize of ₹5,000 is also available for specified tickets carrying the same number as the first-prize ticket.

The fourth prize is ₹5,000, with winning numbers including 0682, 1026, 2521, 3113, 3524, 3696, 4142, 4144, 4921, 6194, 7026, 7223, 7422, 7806, 8205, 8243, 8319, 9169 and 9462. The fifth prize of ₹2,000 includes numbers 1038, 1274, 2086, 2407, 3219 and 9608.

The sixth prize of ₹1,000 includes 0582, 0657, 0796, 1535, 2396, 3348, 3580, 3810, 3863, 4345, 4804, 5562, 5676, 5784, 6116, 6617, 6711, 6898, 7089, 8038, 8232, 8974, 9073, 9974 and 9980. Winners should check the complete official result list for all applicable prize categories.

Winners should verify their ticket numbers against the official Kerala State Lotteries result before beginning the claim process. Prize-winning tickets must be claimed within 30 days from the date of the draw. The ticket holder should sign the reverse of the ticket and keep copies of both sides for their records.

For prizes of up to ₹1 lakh, winners can approach the concerned District Lottery Office. For prizes exceeding ₹1 lakh, the required documents have to be submitted to the Director of State Lotteries. Winners may be required to provide identity proof, photographs, PAN details and a completed prize receipt. Additional documentation applies in cases involving minors or joint winners.

The Karunya lottery is associated with the Kerala government's broader welfare efforts. Its proceeds contribute to the Karunya Benevolent Fund, which supports medical assistance for economically disadvantaged patients with serious illnesses. The programme has been associated with assistance for conditions including cancer, kidney failure, cardiac diseases and neurological disorders.

The Kerala State Lotteries Department operates the state's lottery system under the Lotteries (Regulation) Act, 1998. Draws are conducted under government supervision, and results are published through official channels. The lottery network also supports the livelihoods of licensed agents and vendors across Kerala.

For the KR-769 draw, ticket holders should rely on the official result and gazette before making any claim. Matching a number on an unofficial list does not by itself establish eligibility for payment. Winners are advised to preserve the original ticket and complete the claim process within the stipulated 30-day period.
