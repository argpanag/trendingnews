Snoopy Is Coming To Starbucks Malaysia With The Cutest Merch

TheTools · 09.09.2026 11:31 · source · permalink

Snoopy, Woodstock, and the rest of the Peanuts gang are popping up on Starbucks cups, mugs, bags, and even a plushie this September.

By Tamara Jayne | 09 Sep 2026, 11:31 AM

Follow us on Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp for the latest stories and breaking news.

Starbucks is teaming up with Peanuts for a limited-edition collection inspired by the classic Halloween special It's the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown.

The collection is launching in Malaysia on 15 September, with 10 collectable pieces featuring Snoopy, Woodstock, Charlie Brown, Linus, and Lucy.

Assuming you can resist keeping them for yourself. 😛

The standout is probably the Snoopy Glass Cold Cup, which comes with a Woodstock straw topper, a pumpkin-coloured hat, and a seasonal green scarf.

There's also a Stainless Steel Cold Cup featuring Snoopy and Lucy having a little coffee moment, plus a Great Pumpkin Tumbler decorated with Snoopy and Woodstock artwork inspired by the Halloween special.

For something a little more subtle, the 14oz Ceramic Mug features the Peanuts gang, with a surprise Snoopy detail that appears as you drink from it.

Other drinkware includes a 24oz Cold Cup with Snoopy and the Great Pumpkin, and a 16oz Stainless Steel Tumbler decorated with a Woodstock charm and dancing Snoopy.

And because apparently we can never have too many tiny Snoopy things, the collection also includes a Snoopy Plush, Bag Charm, Mini Tote, and Enamel Pin Set.

The collection will be available at participating Starbucks outlets in Malaysia from 15 September, while stocks last.

It was part of the XPLOSIVE campaign, which ran across 28 developments nationwide.
