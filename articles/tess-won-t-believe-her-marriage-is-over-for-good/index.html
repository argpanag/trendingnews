Tess won't believe her marriage is over for good

TheTools · 04.09.2026 08:38 · source · permalink

She’s still holding out hope of a reconciliation

Following their shock marriage split earlier this year, Vernon Kay is finally set to leave the family home he shares with Tess Daly. But while his move appears to mark the end of a chapter for the couple, sources tell heat Tess is hoping they could find their way back to each other.

‘Tess still isn’t ready to believe it’s over for good,’ says an insider. ‘They were married for 22 years, so she can’t give up on Vernon just yet. She knows he feels this is something he needs to do, so she’s letting him do it. She’s not going to beg him to stay or make things difficult. But there is still a part of her that thinks that once he’s out there in his own place and living the single life, he might realise he misses what he had at home.’

The source continues, ‘She’s not sitting there expecting him to walk back through the door. She knows he might not. But they’ve had more than 20 years together, and you can’t just turn all of that off. She still loves him. However much she tries to be realistic about it, she hasn’t completely given up on him.’

Keen to stay close to his daughters, Phoebe, 21, and Amber, 17, Vernon, 52, has reportedly purchased a £3 million home, which is currently under renovation, around 15 minutes away from the family home in Buckinghamshire. He and Tess, 56, had remained living together despite going their separate ways, and have even continued socialising, having been spotted at events and interacting on social media in recent months.

And while it’ll be a major adjustment not having Vernon at home, insiders say Tess knows he needs to do his own thing. We’re told, ‘Tess feels Vernon needs to go off, have this time on his own and let off some steam. Maybe he needs to experience what life without her is like. At the moment, having his own place and doing his own thing probably feels exciting and new, but she wonders how he’ll feel a year down the line.’

While they were considered to be one of showbiz’s strongest couples, TV presenter Tess and radio DJ Vernon – who wed in 2003 – faced some rocky times over the years. In 2010, it was revealed that Vernon had been sexting Page 3 model Rhian Sugden, as well as other women, but he and Tess managed to weather the storm and come out the other side. But fast-forward to May 2026, and the couple announced the end of their relationship, although they insisted no other parties were involved in their decision to split.

Tess has been navigating a change in her career, too. After quitting Strictly Come Dancing last year after more than two decades as host, she’s been focusing on her swimwear brand NAIA Beach, and recently teased an upcoming project with her eldest daughter, Phoebe.

But while she’s trying to look to the future, we’re told it’s been a tough time. Our insider explains, ‘Tess loved the idea of her and Vernon becoming a TV double act. She saw them doing something together and becoming a modern-day Richard and Judy, but he just couldn’t see himself as one half of that kind of TV couple. The project with Phoebe is exciting for Tess because she loves the idea of working with her daughter, but it’s very early days, and nothing is set in stone. She went from having Strictly as this huge constant in her life to suddenly not knowing exactly what her next big job is going to be.’

However, the source adds that Tess is determined to keep moving forward, whatever that looks like. ‘Tess knows she has to carry on with her own life,’ the insider says. ‘But she’s still got that hope in the back of her mind that this might not be the end for them after all.’
