Over 130 Major Easter Eggs, Comics References, And MCU Ties In VisionQuest's Finale

TheTools · 11.11.2026 21:15 · source · permalink

Warning! This post contains SPOILERS for VisionQuest episode 8

Marvel's VisionQuest has finally brought the MCU's WandaVision trilogy to a close while kicking off a brand-new chapter for the titular synthezoid and his family as well. Additionally, the new finale is absolutely packed with some incredibly fun and exciting Easter eggs and references to the greater MCU and beyond.

Across the board, VisionQuest episode 8 excels at connecting the new series to the legacy of all the relevant Marvel movies and shows that have come before it, particularly Avengers: Age of Ultron, Avengers: Infinity War, WandaVision, and Agatha All Along. However, the finale also has some great nods to the original comics, showrunner Terry Matalas' past non-MCU shows, and key reveals teasing the MCU's future (but also some of the best computer/television puns you'll ever see).

Keeping that in mind, here are over 130 different Easter eggs, references, cameos, and major MCU ties we found in VisionQuest's incredible finale.

Yogi Bear or Scooby-Doo?

Excited about the meal he's forcing Vision and Jocasta to serve him, Virgil Horton references classic cartoon characters Yogi Bear and Scooby-Doo and the classic trope where cartoons smell food and float in the air.

GoBots

Criticizing Ultron's name, Virgil suggests Tony Stark and Bruce Banner played with too many GoBots, once a rival toy franchise to the much more successful Transformers.

Ultron (HAL 9000)

Trapped within a device not unlike a hard drive with a large central red eye, Ultron very much resembles the murderous HAL 9000 from 2001: A Space Odyssey.

Tamagotchi

Virgil jokes that Ultron is too much fun like a toy pet, comparing him to the old egg-shaped digital pets, a reference to the classic Tamagotchi toys.

"I Think My Brother Saved Me"

After watching the SWORD "re-runs" of WadaVision, Thomas theorizes that Billy Maximoff used his magic to save him, which is indeed what happened as seen in 2024's Agatha All Along.

“Still The Cool Twin"

Finally meeting his brother via a telepathic link spell known as "dreambinding", Joe Locke makes an appearance as William Kaplan/Billy Maximoff, who confirms that he's been searching for Tommy ever since Agatha All Along's finale. He also refers to Tommy as "still the cool twin", calling back to their time and banter as kids in WandaVision.

Astral Projection

Billy reveals that dreambinding has been part of his training along with astral projection and portals, both classic magical powers we've seen before in the MCU from the likes of both Doctor Strange and Wanda Maximoff, Billy's mother.

New Jersey

Revealing that he's in New Jersey, it seems as though Billy is still based out of Eastview (Westview's neighboring town), an idea that's further supported when Eastview gets name-dropped as a place where the Maximoff Twins and Vision plan to meet and have dinner. That said, New Jersey is notably where Kamal Khan's Ms. Marvel is based as well, who was last seen recruiting young heroes for a new team...

Agatha’s Ghost

Billy is also shown arguing to a presence that's invisible to Thomas, who is undoubtedly the ghost of Agatha Harkness, whom Billy joined forces with to find Tommy as seen at the ending of Agatha All Along.

“In The Beginning…"

Full of ego with his own major god complex (not unlike Ultron), Virgil quotes the first verse of Genesis while discussing his grandfather's work as the godfather of AI: "In the beginning, my grandfather said 'let there be life', but instead he got light, a broken robot with a penchant for catching on fire."
