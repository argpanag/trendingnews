Sienna Miller's daughter Marlowe dresses just like her mum in rare red carpet appearance

TheTools · 17.09.2026 10:51 · source · permalink

Miller was accompanied by her daughter at the premiere of her new TV show, War

Sienna Miller was joined by her daughter Marlowe, 14, at the premiere of her new TV series, War, in London on Wednesday night. Miller stars opposite Dominic West in the hotly anticipated legal drama, which follows a famous actress and her tech billionaire ex as they navigate an explosive divorce.

Miller, 44, arrived on the red carpet in a beautiful, feather-adorned black dress by Dilara Findikoglu. Sculpted to perfection and encompassing Turkish designer Findikoglu’s gothic aesthetic, the gown marked a departure from the lighter, bohemian-inspired style Miller is known for. The actress’ exact dress was from Findikoglu’s autumn/winter 2023 collection and the full runway look included a pair of long latex gloves, which Miller waived. Ensuring nothing distracted from her intricately designed gown, Miller opted for a pair of pared-back, open-toe heels in midnight black and pinned back her hair, allowing a handful of tousled strands to frame her face.

And although Miller chose to switch up her style game, it was those hard-earned boho-chic credentials that seemingly inspired her daughter’s look. For the event, Marlowe, who Miller shares with ex Tom Sturridge, wore a vintage yellow T-shirt and lace-trimmed skirt, and the latter wasn’t dissimilar to the lace-adorned maxi skirt Miller wore to Chloé’s autumn/winter 2025 show (albeit in black, not white) – it seems Miller has schooled her daughter well in the art of chic, understated style.

Lace-trimmed pieces have dominated the summer trend cycle, with everyone from Alexa Chung to Hailey Bieber wearing lace-lashed shorts, skirts and dresses on repeat. Perfect for adding a romantic, vintage feel to any outfit, subtle trimmings of lace add a feminine edge and have the power to make an outfit look that much more considered. Plus, contrary to popular belief, lace can be cold-weather appropriate too; just remember to toughen up delicate pieces with leather or chunky knitwear.

As for Miller’s new eight-part TV show, you’ll have to wait until October 2 to watch it on Sky or NOW TV, but with Miller at the helm, it’s safe to assume it’ll be worth the wait.
