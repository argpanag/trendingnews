Arthur Rinderknech vs Miomir Kecmanovic prediction

Arthur Rinderknech arrives in Stockholm after an encouraging showing at the Paris Masters, where he defeated Francisco Cerundolo and Mattia Bellucci to advance to the round-of-16. Rinderknech was a break up in the deciding set when Cerundolo decided to retire, while the Frenchman comfortably beat Bellucci 6-4 6-1. Rinderknech took world number four Ben Shelton to a deciding set tiebreak, narrowly losing 3-6 6-4 7-6 (5). He has reached quarter-finals in Dubai, Geneva and Gstaad in 2026. 

The 31-year-old takes on Miomir Kecmanovic in the opening round. The Serb has endured a difficult few months, suffering early defeats in Almaty, Basel and Paris. His standout performance this season came when he won the Valencia Challenger in May, while he also reached the semi-finals of the Mexican Open.

Rinderknech leads the head-to-head 3-1 and won their most recent meeting in Montreal earlier this year. He enters with greater confidence following his performances in Paris and he should benefit from these conditions.
