Grand Theft Auto VI Comes to XBOX One and XBOX Series X|S Through XBOX Cloud Gaming on November 19

TheTools · 10.11.2026 15:45 · source · permalink

Grand Theft Auto VI, the most pre-ordered title of all time on the Microsoft Store, launches November 19. Considering the unprecedented demand, we encourage everyone to begin pre-loading as soon as possible to ensure you’re prepared for launch day.

Grand Theft Auto VI will be playable on day one through XBOX Cloud Gaming on XBOX One and XBOX Series X|S, starting at 1am ET. That means more players can experience one of the most anticipated games of the year on the consoles they already own. The game uses XBOX Series S graphics settings when cloud-streamed with XBOX Cloud Gaming.

Here’s how you can play:

  • XBOX Series X: Play natively with the best XBOX console experience, available now to pre-install.
  • XBOX Series S: The most affordable way to play GTA VI natively on XBOX, available now to pre-install.
  • XBOX One: Stream Grand Theft Auto VI through XBOX Cloud Gaming on the XBOX One.

We’re excited to bring Grand Theft Auto VI to more XBOX players at launch, and we will also be adding more titles from Rockstar Games to XBOX Cloud Gaming including Grand Theft Auto V, Red Dead Redemption 2, and more in the coming months.

Starting November 11, players in supported markets can buy cloud playtime to stream supported games without a Game Pass subscription. Streaming for Grand Theft Auto VI begins at 1am ET on November 19.

Pre-order Grand Theft Auto VI today on the Microsoft Store.

Cloud gaming availability, purchasing options, supported devices, account eligibility, and features may vary by market and device. Grand Theft Auto VI must be purchased separately.
