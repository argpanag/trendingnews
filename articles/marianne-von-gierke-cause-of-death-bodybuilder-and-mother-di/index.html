Marianne von Gierke cause of death: Bodybuilder and mother dies at 40 just weeks after Mr. Olympia and hours after posting on social media; here's what we know

TheTools · 16.10.2026 06:53 · source · permalink

    Marianne von Gierke passed away at the age of 40 just weeks after competing in Mr. Olympia. The announcement of her death was made by NPC Worldwide Chile and Felipe Isaac Fierro. She leaves behind an 11-year-old son and has been honored by the bodybuilding community. Von Gierke made significant achievements during her career, including winning the Rising Phoenix World Championship title.
