The Allahabad High Court on Friday (November 27, 2026) cancelled the Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission’s (UPPSC) preliminary examination results of the Provincial Civil Services (PCS) 2025, holding that the commission had wrongly excluded meritorious reserved-category candidates from the unreserved category while preparing the category-wise merit lists.

A Bench of Justice Manish Kumar Nigam directed the commission to redraw the preliminary merit list and conduct the mains examination afresh on the basis of the revised results.

The ruling came on a petition filed by Sanjay Kumar and three other candidates belonging to the Other Backward Classes (OBC) and Economically Weaker Sections (EWS). The petitioners contended that the UPPSC had failed to consider reserved-category candidates who scored more than the last selected unreserved-category candidate for the open-category list.

In a supplementary affidavit before the court, the commission disclosed that 2,993 OBC, 464 Scheduled Caste (SC), 155 Scheduled Tribe (ST), and 921 EWS candidates had scored more than the last selected unreserved-category candidate in the preliminary examination. The combined figure stood at 4,533.

The commission also disclosed that, after the mains examination, 579 OBC, 158 SC, five ST, and 168 EWS candidates had scored above the last selected unreserved-category candidate.

The petitioners argued that candidates securing marks above the unreserved-category cut-off should be considered against open-category vacancies, irrespective of their social category. They contended that restricting such candidates to their respective reserved categories violated the constitutional guarantees of equality and equal opportunity in public employment under Articles 14 and 16. They also relied on Section 3(6) of the Uttar Pradesh Public Services (Reservation for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and Other Backward Classes) Act, 1994.

Rejecting the commission’s approach, the court observed that the unreserved category was open to all candidates and could not be treated as a category reserved for those who had not claimed reservation.

“The respondents have wrongly applied the law... in preparing the list category-wise, both at the stage of Preliminary Examination as well as mains examination, treating unreserved category to be a category reserved for candidates, who have not claimed any reservation,” the court said.

The court also quashed Condition 19 of the PCS-2025 advertisement, which provided for the adjustment of reserved-category candidates against unreserved vacancies only at the final selection stage, subject to their not having availed any relaxation in the qualifying standard. It held that the condition had no legal basis after the commission’s office memorandum dated January 9, 2020, on the issue had already been quashed in a connected case.

Consequently, the court set aside the preliminary examination result declared on December 1, 2025, and directed the UPPSC to redraw the merit list for the advertised vacancies and hold the mains examination afresh on the basis of the revised result. The commission will thereafter proceed with the remaining stages of the selection process.
