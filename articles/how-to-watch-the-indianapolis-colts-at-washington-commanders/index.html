How to watch the Indianapolis Colts at Washington Commanders on October 4, 2026

How To Watch

TV broadcast, radio and streaming information for Indianapolis Colts at Washington Commanders on Sunday, October 4 at 9:30 a.m. ET (Week 4).

Colts fans can stream LIVE local and primetime regular and postseason games with NFL+, plans starting at $6.99/month. LEARN MORE

MATCHUP

The Indianapolis Colts will travel to London to take on the Washington Commanders in Week 4.

The contest will mark the 33rd all-time regular season matchup between the teams, with the Colts leading the series 21-11. In their last matchup in Week 8 of 2022, Indianapolis hosted Washington and lost, 16-17.

In Week 3, the Colts hosted the Texans at Lucas Oil Stadium and won, 19-17. It marked Indianapolis' first win despite being minus three in the turnover battle since 1999.

Can't Stream The Radio Broadcast?

If you are within the 100 mile radius of Indianapolis and can't stream the radio broadcast it could be your Wi-Fi network or cellular provider. Check your IP address at whatismyip.com. If that shows you as outside of the radius you won't be able to stream the radio broadcast on mobile.
