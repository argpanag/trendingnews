Atlantic Notes: Brown, Celtics, Tatum, George, Graves

TheTools · 13.10.2026 00:26 · source · permalink

Nets lottery pick Mikel Brown Jr. is expected to make his preseason debut on Wednesday in Miami, per Brian Lewis of The New York Post (Twitter link).

Brown, who was selected sixth overall in June’s draft, has been sidelined since late September after spraining his left ankle. However, the 20-year-old guard was able to go through full practices over the weekend and participated in another 5-on-5 session on Monday, according to Lewis.

The former Louisville star will be out for Monday’s contest vs. Washington but is expected to be ready for Wednesday’s game against the Heat, Lewis adds.

Here’s more from the Atlantic:

  • The Celtics rested most of their rotation players in Thursday’s preseason opener in Cleveland, but that changed in Saturday’s lopsided win over Philadelphia, writes Jay King of The Athletic. Head coach Joe Mazzulla started Derrick White, Jordan Walsh, Paul George, Jayson Tatum and Mitchell Robinson, with Payton Pritchard, Neemias Queta, Sam Hauser, Hugo Gonzalez and Baylor Scheierman used as the main reserves. Tatum and George said it was the first time they’d been on the same team together, as they’ve practicing against each other throughout the offseason and training camp. “It was great because we’ve been teammates for a few months now, but it was like my first time sharing the floor with him and being on the same team,” Tatum said. “Somebody that I’ve obviously admired for a very, very long time, and when I was in high school and college and competing against him and now to be on the same team and to see some of the special things that he brings to the team.”
  • George recorded 13 points, five rebounds, five steals and four assists in 25 minutes in his Celtics debut, and his ability to force turnovers brings a new element to the team’s defense, King notes. Mazzulla says George’s defensive activity is “contagious” for his teammates. “He’s got great instincts,” Mazzulla said of George. “Great instincts, great technique, great positioning. It’s something he takes pride in, but I do think it’s very contagious.”
  • What type of role will first-rounder Allen Graves play as a rookie for the Raptors? Michael Grange of Sportsnet.ca explores that question, writing that the versatile forward has been turning heads in training camp with an improved outside shot, even though he’s been limited by a groin injury. “He brings a lot of things with him: the intangibles, how hard he plays. He’s got great hands. Creates a lot of steals. He’s very disciplined when it comes to rebounding the ball, and he’s a player who is pretty good catch-and-shoot three-point shooter,” said head coach Darko Rajakovic. “As a coach, you’re always looking for players where there are things that they catch on to and can build on, and he’s that type of player. So I believe that he’s going to be able to help us throughout the whole season, and I can see him being on the floor very soon.”
