Pack Away the T-Shirts and Bring Out the Jackets; The Winter Is Finally Here 

TheTools · 23.11.2026 23:55 · source · permalink

The Greater Toronto Area (GTA) has enjoyed a stretch of above-seasonal temperatures throughout much of the fall, with daytime highs regularly reaching double digits. For many Torontonians, it has felt more like an extension of summer than the final weeks of November.

However, that will change as early as tonight.

According to The Weather Network, southern Ontario can expect impactful lake-effect snow and chilly temperatures tonight and into Tuesday.

As Monday draws to a close, temperatures are expected to drop to single digits as the cold front moves through the region. Northwesterly winds will bring lake-effect snow off Georgian Bay and Lake Huron from Monday afternoon into Tuesday.

Travel may be challenging, as wind gusts of up to 60 km/h could reduce visibility for commuters.

By Tuesday morning, areas such as Collingwood, Stayner, and Wasaga Beach (all southeast of Georgian Bay) may receive anywhere from 10 to 20 centimeters of snow.

Barrie, Hanover, Stratford, and surrounding areas can expect three to five centimeters of snow. A few centimeters are also forecast near Lake Huron and for places such as Goderich, Owen Sound, London, Grand Bend, and Orangeville.

The Greater Toronto Area could see flurries on Monday night, although not enough to accumulate.

Key Points to Note:

  1.   Daytime highs will hover between 4°C and 6°C for much of the week, with Monday expected to reach around 9°C.
  2.   Overnight temperatures could drop to around -4°C as the city returns to colder conditions.
  3.   Rain is expected on Thursday and Friday, while the final weekend of November should bring drier but still chilly weather.

Warming Centers Open for GTA Residents:

As temperatures drop below zero tonight, the City of Toronto has activated five warming centers for residents seeking shelter.

Schedule: Centers will open on Monday, November 23, at 5 p.m.

Locations:

– 136 Spadina Rd.

– 81 Elizabeth St.

– 12 Holmes Ave.

– 855 Scarborough Golf Club Rd.

– 349 George St.
