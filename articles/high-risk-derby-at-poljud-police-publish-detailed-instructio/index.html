High-risk derby at Poljud: Police publish detailed instructions for fans

TheTools · 09.10.2026 13:44 · source · permalink

On Saturday, October 10, 2026, starting at 15 p.m., at the Poljud city stadium in SplitThe football match of the 9th round of the SuperSport HNL between HNK Hajduk and GNK Dinamo will be played.

The public gathering has been declared a high-risk match and, with the coordinating role of the High-Risk Match Security Headquarters of the Police Directorate, measures and actions will be taken in accordance with the Protocol on Security at High-Risk Matches, the Act on Police Affairs and Powers, the Act on the Prevention of Disorder at Sports Competitions and the Act on Public Gatherings.

Given the great interest in this public gathering and the expected arrival of a large number of fans, the police urge all visitors to familiarize themselves in detail with the instructions and recommendations listed below.

Notice for visiting fans: Reception of fans at the Kozjak rest area

Police officers will receive organized and unorganized groups of visiting fans at the Kozjak South rest area on the A1 motorway, after which, after the checks have been completed, they will be escorted directly to the Poljud stadium.

All visiting fans are recommended to arrive at the Kozjak rest area no later than 12 noon, which is also the latest arrival time at which visitors can be guaranteed timely entry to the stadium.

Notice for home fans: Use the Prgomet, Vučevica and Bisko exits, and avoid the D1 and the Dugopolje toll station

Considering that in the period from 11 am to 15 pm in the area of ​​the Dugopolje toll station, as well as on the D1 state road and the expressway in the direction of Klis - Solin, expect increased crowds and traffic jams, and that a special traffic regulation will be established on the D1 road with the possibility of its complete closure, the police urge local fans coming to the match from other cities not to use the exit from the A1 highway at the Dugopolje toll station, but to use the Prgomet, Vučevica and Bisko toll stations.

Note: Avoid driving on the D1 road on the Klis section - Solin and use of the Dugopolje toll station. We expect increased crowds and delays a few hours before the start of the public gathering, as well as during the departure of visitors after the end of the match. We advise all visitors to arrive at the stadium on time and, if possible, to use public transport.

Entering the stadium

The police would like to remind all visitors once again that they must have a personalized ticket and a public ID with a photo when entering the stadium.

Given the increased interest in this public gathering, visitors are asked to arrive at the stadium on time on the day of the match. The stadium entrances will be open from 12:30 p.m., so police are urging visitors to arrive early so that, after security checks, identity and ticket checks, they can enter the stadium on time and take their seats.

Special traffic regulations in the city area Splita

The police informs the citizens that on the day of the match, the organizer and the police will restrict the movement of vehicles in such a way that:
 
- from 10 am until the end of security, traffic blockades will be established on Ulica VIII. mediteranskih igrace at the entrance to the Poljud swimming pool parking lot, at the "Mandrach" catering facility, and at the intersection of Ulica VIII. mediteranskih igrace and Ulica Sedam Kaštela (except for police vehicles and vehicles with a pass);
- from 11 am, traffic blockades will be established in Zrinsko-frankopanska Street, from the intersection with Hrvatske mornarice Street to the entrance to Lora, and in Put Supavla Street, from the intersection with Put Brodarice Street (except for Croatian Army vehicles, tenants, public transport vehicles and vehicles with a pass).

Visitors will not be able to park their vehicles in the immediate vicinity of the stadium or in the wider area around the stadium. Therefore, the police advise that they park their vehicles in parking lots in other parts of the city and reach the stadium on foot or by public transport.

Traffic wardens, together with police officers, will sanction drivers of illegally parked vehicles on the day of the public gathering, especially on Zrinsko-frankopanska Street, Hrvatske mornarice Street, and Sedam Street. Kaštela and Put Supavla Street. Vehicles that meet the legal requirements for relocation will be relocated. The above also applies to illegally parked vehicles left on or next to the roadway of the aforementioned roads.

The police are asking all road users to be extra patient and cautious due to the expected increased traffic loads and possible temporary closure of certain roads in the city area. Splita.

Law on the Prevention of Riots at Sports Competitions

Citizens are hereby informed that, in accordance with the Law on the Prevention of Riots at Sports Competitions, it is prohibited to:

- bringing or attempting to bring in alcoholic beverages and drugs and entering and staying in the premises of a sports facility under the influence of alcohol,
- bringing in pyrotechnics, weapons and other objects suitable for causing injuries or inciting disorder and violence, as well as lighting and throwing pyrotechnics and other fan paraphernalia,
- masking the face with a hat, scarf or other means to conceal identity,
- inserting and displaying banners, flags or other objects with text, pictures, signs or other features that express or incite hatred or violence based on racial, national, regional or religious affiliation,
- singing songs and sending messages whose content expresses or incites hatred or violence based on racial, national, regional or religious affiliation,
- attempted unauthorized entry or unauthorized entry into the competition area, the area intended for judges or other officials, and into the spectator area for which the person does not have the appropriate ticket,
throwing objects into the competition area,
- staying or remaining in a place in the audience area for which the person does not possess an appropriate ticket or other document issued by the organizer.

All of the above illegal behaviors will be sanctioned in accordance with the Act on the Prevention of Disorder at Sports Competitions.

The police will, in accordance with Article 79 of the Law on Police Affairs and Powers, film the stadium and access routes to the city before, during and after the match. Splitu.

Also, in accordance with Articles 51 and 52 of the Law on Police Affairs and Powers, the police will temporarily restrict movement and presence in the streets of Put Supavla, VIII. Mediterranean Games and Put Lore four hours before the start of the match and two hours after the end of the match, as necessary.

- We invite all citizens and visitors to respect the instructions, warnings and orders of police officers so that the public gathering can take place safely, without incidents and with as few difficulties as possible in the flow of traffic - the police say.
