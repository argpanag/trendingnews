Fired NFL coach takes break from selling Cameos to speak at Donald Trump rally - AOL

TheTools · 04.10.2026 20:01 · source · permalink

Super Bowl-winning coach Jon Gruden spoke at Donald Trump's GOP rally in Ohio on Saturday, showing his support for the President of the United States as he took the podium.

Trump spoke of becoming friends with Gruden after playing golf with the long-time NFL coach, who has become a beloved figure as a sports analyst.

Trump named Gruden his favorite coach in the world after his speech, as the former Tampa Bay Buccaneers and Oakland Raiders leader took a break from his day job with Dave Portnoy's Barstool Sports. In addition to being with Barstool, Gruden is prominent on Cameo, charging $340 for a short video.

Trump said: "We even have one of my favorite coaches in the whole world here, Jon Gruden. He's a great coach, and he's a very good golfer too. We had a great time. He's a great winner. He's a winner."

Gruden spoke before Trump took the podium, explaining his decision to attend the rally.

While being cheered on, Gruden said: "I came back (to Ohio) for one reason. I'm from Ohio. My mom and dad are from Cleveland.

"I was born in Sandusky and I went to the University of Dayton and I'm damn proud of it, man!"

Gruden was also showing his support for Senator Jon Husted, who had been on the same Dayton Flyers football team as him in the 1980's.

He said: "Jon Husted is a lot more than my friend; he was a great player. He intercepted 17 passes on the gridiron. He was a great player.

"And I know why he was a great player. It was because of his work ethic and his commitment and his day-to-day mental toughness. He will never quit on you or anybody."

Gruden was not known to be too political, but his beliefs reportedly came to light in 2021 when he was the Raiders' head coach, after his emails leaked, some of which landed him in hot water.

He reportedly had strong words for Barack Obama and Joe Biden, as well as apparent racist and homophobic comments. He soon stepped down from his position with the Raiders.

Gruden has not coached in the NFL since, having joined Barstool Sports in recent years as an analyst.

He remains in a legal battle with the NFL, claiming that the league leaked his emails while suing commissioner Roger Goodell for $150 million. The league denies the claims with a jury trial scheduled for May 2027.
