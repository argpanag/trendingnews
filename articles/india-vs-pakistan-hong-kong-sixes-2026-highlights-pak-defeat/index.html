A few months before he made his international debut, India’s most successful white-ball captain MS Dhoni played in the 2004 edition of the tournament.

Dhoni was part of a squad that comprised Iqbal Siddiqui, Subroto Banerjee, Nikhil Chopra, Pravin Amre, and Debasis Mohanty. Dhoni, just 23-years-old at the time, scored 20 off 10 balls against South Africa.

Its fast-paced, full of action, rains sixes and fours but its starkly different to your average T20 game. The Hong Kong Sixes features a six-over-per-side match, hence the name. Each team comprises seven players, with every player except a designated wicket-keeper required to bowl.
