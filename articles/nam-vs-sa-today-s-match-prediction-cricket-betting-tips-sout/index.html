Can Namibia pull the trigger on South Africa, this time in ODIs?

Namibia are set to script another historic chapter as they host South Africa in their first‑ever ODI series, comprising three matches. The hosts have already stunned the Proteas twice in T20Is, first in October 2025, when they secured a famous win in Windhoek, and then again last month, reinforcing their reputation as giant‑killers. The question now is whether Namibia can replicate that success in the longer white‑ball format.

Led by the inspirational Gerhard Erasmus, Namibia boast a well‑balanced unit. Jan Frylinck’s all‑round brilliance, Ruben Trumpelmann’s pace threat, and Michael van Lingen’s top‑order resilience provide the backbone of their challenge. Bernard Scholtz’s spin, JJ Smit’s power hitting, and Louren Steenkamp’s middle‑order stability further strengthen their arsenal.

For Namibia, this ODI series is not just about competing, it’s about proving they can consistently challenge established cricketing nations. South Africa will be wary of a side that has already dented their pride twice. If Erasmus and his men can rise to the occasion, Namibia could well pull the trigger once more and etch their name into ODI history.

South Africa won’t take Namibia for granted and will look to start well

South Africa enter their first‑ever ODI series against Namibia with caution, knowing full well what their hosts are capable of. Namibia stunned the Proteas twice in T20Is over the past year, and although this format offers more overs to recover, South Africa’s inexperienced lineup must be wary of another upset.

Skipper Bjorn Fortuin will be tasked with guiding a youthful side, while Tony de Zorzi’s top‑order stability and Dewald Brevis’s explosive stroke play remain crucial to setting the tone. Connor Esterhuizen’s wicketkeeping duties add reliability, and the pace duo of Kwena Maphaka and Duan Jansen will be expected to provide breakthroughs with the new ball.

For South Africa, the key lies in starting strong and not allowing Namibia’s spirited unit to seize momentum early. The Proteas will look to impose themselves with disciplined batting and sharp bowling, ensuring they don’t repeat past mistakes. With pride and reputation at stake, this young South African side will aim to begin the ODI series on the front foot and remind Namibia that, despite their recent heroics, the Proteas remain a formidable force in international cricket. Let’s move ahead and check the cricket tips.
