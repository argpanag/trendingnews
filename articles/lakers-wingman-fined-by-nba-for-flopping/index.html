The NBA fined Ziaire Williams $2,000 for flopping during the Los Angeles Lakers’ game against the Utah Jazz on Sunday.

Ziaire Williams has been fined $2,000 by the NBA after a flopping act during the Los Angeles Lakers' Sunday game against the Utah Jazz, the league announced Monday.

While guarding Jazz star Keyonte George, Williams appeared to exaggerate his fall and reaction after being bumped by George with just over four minutes left in regulation.

George was called for an offensive foul.

Williams recorded 11 points, two rebounds, and an assist while playing 30 minutes for the Lakers' 132-123 win this past weekend in Utah. He shot 3-of-7 from beyond the three-point arc.

Ziaire Williams has been one of the massive early revelations for the Los Angeles Lakers' strong start to the 2026-27 season.

Imposing his length and versatility to anchor the Lakers' wing presence, Williams has been huge for the Lakers so far, helping the team to go 3-0 while averaging 11.3 points, 3.3 rebounds, and 1.7 assists in 28.3 minutes.

He is converting 52.2% of his field goal attempts and 41.7% of his shots from three-point range.

Williams and the Lakers will look to stay perfect in the league standings as they face the Portland Trail Blazers on Tuesday.
