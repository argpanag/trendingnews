Pacers must overcome lengthy injury report in second leg of back-to-back at Bucks: Preview, Starters, How to Watch

TheTools · 26.10.2026 20:49 · source · permalink

In this story:

Game date, time and location: Monday, Oct. 26, 8:10  p.m. ET, Fiserv Forum, Milwaukee, Wisconsin

TV: WVTV-DT2 - MyMilwaukee24 (Milwaukee), DAZN (Indiana)

Radio: WTMJ (Milwaukee), 1070 “The Fan” (Indiana)

VITALS: The Pacers look to post victories on both legs of their first back-to-back on the 2026-27 season after overcoming an early injury to starting center Ivica Zubac in Brooklyn on Sunday night. Indiana rolled 136-109 and got into Wisconsin post-game after thriving behind Tyrese Haliburton's 24 points and 10 assists.

It's great news that Haliburton isn't on the injury report, but nine teammates are listed and two-way players could be in the mix to help make up for potential absences. The Pacers survived losing Zubac. and is looking to move to 3-1 as Rick Carlisle is forced to use a different starting lineup. Haliburton's backcourt mate, Andrew Nembhard, is on the questionable side, while starting wing Aaron Nesmith is expected to play.

The Bucks look to avoid an 0-4 start and has squandered second-half leads against the Wizards, Bulls and 76ers in their losses. They've surrendered 129 points per game and will be playing their second home game and their first set to tip off in the evening after moving Friday’s opener vs. Chicago to the afternoon to accommodate the Brewers and Game 1 of the World Series.

Tyler Herro (23.7 ppg) has led his new team in scoring in two of the first three, while Jaime Jaquez Jr. (20.7 ppg, 8.7 rpg) has filled the stat sheet and led the way in Philly with 21 points.

The Bucks lead the Pacers in the regular season series 122-97 and swept all four meetings in ‘25-’26. Milwaukee won both home dates in action played earlier this calendar year, prevailing 105-99 on Feb. 6 and 134-123 on March 15. Giannis Antetokounmpo finished with 31 points and 14 rebounds in the win in his last contest with Milwaukee.
