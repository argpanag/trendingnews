Isabella Jones posted photos of herself with Presley Gerber and wrote that she would miss him for the rest of her life. He died on September 20 at the age of 27.

Presley Gerber’s former girlfriend, Isabella Jones, published an emotional message nearly three weeks after the death of the son of supermodel Cindy Crawford at the age of 27. She shared photos of them together and confessed that she would miss him for the rest of her life. E! News reports, according to UNN.

Details

On October 9, Isabella posted a series of photos and videos on Instagram capturing happy moments from their relationship. In the images, the former couple embraces, spends time at home, and gazes tenderly at each other.

My dear Presley. I will miss you for the rest of my life

— Jones wrote, dedicating touching words to her former boyfriend.

What is known about Presley Gerber’s death

Twenty-seven-year-old Presley Gerber, the son of Cindy Crawford and businessman Rande Gerber, died on September 20 at a rehabilitation center in Los Angeles. According to preliminary information from law enforcement, the cause may have been an overdose. An investigation into the circumstances of his death is ongoing.
