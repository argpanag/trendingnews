Community resources and healing support offered by state agencies following Bend tragedy

Following the Silver Rail Elementary School shooting, the Oregon Department of Emergency Management (OEM) offers resources to support the community.

On Thursday, Oct. 15, former Silver Rail student Griffin J. John II shot at a passing vehicle and forced his way into a classroom. He shot roughly 15 rounds, killing three people and seriously injuring two more.

READ MORE | Bend community mourning after teen shooter kills 4 at elementary school, shot by witness

“Oregon stands with the Bend community during this incredibly difficult time,” said OEM in a written statement. “As families process the tragedy, officials encourage people to seek support, spend time with loved ones and take advantage of community resources designed to provide connection, comfort and space to grieve.”

OEM encourages community members to follow the Bend Police Department on social media or look at its press release page for official information regarding the investigation. OEM asks individuals to avoid spreading unverified information and to verify facts before accidentally sharing misinformation.

The National Mass Violence Center designed a Bend-specific resource guide with information to help individuals and families following the tragedy.

A verified GoFundMe hub was launched, with several fundraisers being linked. GoFundMe said it is closely monitoring all fundraisers related to the event and will pause funds until the verification process is complete. The hub includes a link for Rhoam Summer’s family, the five-year-old killed at the scene.

The Oregon Parks and Recreation Department has waived parking fees to give community members time to connect with family and friends. Parking fees are waived through the weekend.

OEM shared many resources from the National Child Traumatic Stress Network, as it recognized the situation greatly impacts youth. The resources include:

  • Parent Guidelines for Helping Youth After the Recent Shooting
  • Talking to Children about the Shooting
  • Helping Youth After Community Trauma: Tips for Educators
  • Talking to Children: When Scary Things Happen
  • Creating Supportive Environments: When Scary Things Happen
  • For Teens: Coping After Mass Violence
  • Helping School-Age Children with Traumatic Grief: Tips for Caregivers
  • Helping Young Children with Traumatic Grief: Tips for Caregivers
  • Helping Teens with Traumatic Grief: Tips for Caregivers
  • Guiding Adults in Talking to Children about Death and Attending Services
  • After a Crisis: Helping Young Children Heal
  • Age-Related Reactions to a Traumatic Event
  • Once I Was Very Very Scared
  • I Don’t Know How to Address Worries About My Child’s Safety at School

For immediate crisis and family support, OEM shared:

  • Deschutes County Stabilization Center: Open 24/7 at 63311 NE Jamison Street in Bend.
  • Deschutes County Crisis Line: Call 541-322-7500, ext. 9 for immediate support.
  • Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administrations’ Disaster Distress Helpline: Call or text: 1-800-985-5990.
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988

OEM said that these resources are valuable for community members, students, educators, first responders and families. It urges Bend residents to check in with love ones, have age-appropriate conversations with children, respect affected families’ privacy and take the time to grieve.
