Two men found dead in same NSW house two weeks apart | Nine.com.au

TheTools · 28.09.2026 13:28 · source · permalink
  • Australia
  • NSW
  • Crime

September 28, 2026 - 1.28pm

Police are investigating the suspicious deaths of two men found dead in the same northern NSW house two weeks apart.

Officers were first called to the home in Gurranang, about 25 kilometres north of Grafton, on September 13.

Police were first called to the home in Gurranang, about 25 kilometres north of Grafton, on September 13. FILE Sam Mooy/The Sydney Morning Herald

A body had been found inside and local officers established a crime scene and started investigating the circumstances around the 79-year-old’s death.

They returned to the Summerland Way home on Monday afternoon to make more inquiries about the first death, only to find another body.

They set up another crime scene and began trying to find out what happened to the man, believed to be in his 50s.

Strike Force Faneel was spun up to investigate as police declared both deaths suspicious.

Anyone with information was urged to contact Crime Stoppers
