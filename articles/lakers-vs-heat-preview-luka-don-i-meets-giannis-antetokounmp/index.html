Lakers vs. Heat Preview: Luka Dončić meets Giannis Antetokounmpo

TheTools · 02.11.2026 07:01 · source · permalink
  • Los Angeles Lakers Discussion

The Lakers will face the new-look Heat squad Monday.

The Lakers (5-2) will welcome the Miami Heat (2-4) to town on Monday for their first meeting of the season. Both teams enter this matchup coming off wins.

Start time and TV schedule

Who: Los Angeles Lakers vs. Miami Heat

When: 7 p.m. PT, Nov. 2

Where: Crypto.com Arena

Watch: Spectrum Sportsnet

In case you didn’t know, the Lakers have three sets of back-to-back games this month, starting with Sunday and Monday. The purple and gold will face a Miami team that has won two straight games after opening the season with four consecutive losses. Miami also has the rest advantage in this one after having had two consecutive nights off.

That factor alone makes this a challenging game for Los Angeles, yet it’s one that they can conquer. The spotlight will obviously be on Luka Dončić and Giannis Antetokounmpo, but the key to victory will be which team can do a better job of limiting the other’s superstar.

The Greek Freak is averaging 28.2 points, 12.3 rebounds and 5.5 assists to begin the year. The Lakers’ frontcourt will also have to look out for Bam Adebayo, who is coming off his best performance yet with 27 points in the win against the Indiana Pacers. This frontcourt duo is what makes the Heat a tricky matchup.

That’s why it’ll be interesting to see how head coach JJ Redick game plans against Antetokounmpo and Adebayo. Ideally, the Lakers also expose the Heat’s lack of shooting, defensive inconsistencies and the shortage of production from their role players. It’ll be nice to see Dončić — who just had his best all-around game of the season in Sunday’s victory — remain hot and torch the Heat defense.

Speaking of things that would be nice to see, the Lakers’ minutes without their Slovenian superstar on the floor will be crucial. Against the Raptors, it didn’t matter so much because Dončić and co. were able to open up a huge early lead.

However, this Heat team is different in a sense that they have more firepower than the Raptors. Contributions from across the board will be necessary and that’s where the Lakers’ bench and shooters need to step up.

Facing Miami is an early test to see how the Lakers handle tough matchups like this, especially with no rest. Let’s see if the purple and gold can successfully take care of business against the Heat on Monday.

Notes and Updates

  • Since the Lakers played on Sunday, there isn’t an injury report yet for this one.
  • As for Ziaire Williams (left foot injury management), there was an update prior to Sunday’s game regarding his recovery but his status still remains uncertain.
  • The Heat list Tim Hardaway Jr., (personal reasons) as questionable.
