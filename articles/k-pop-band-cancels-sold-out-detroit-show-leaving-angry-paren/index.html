DETROIT (FOX 2) - A K-pop band cancels their sold-out Detroit show 30 minutes before taking the stage. Little girls and their moms traveled hundreds of miles, spending thousands to see the girl group ‘Katseye' at Little Caesars Arena. 

Big picture view:

On Tuesday, LCA was packed with people already in their seats when, just 30 minutes before the show, they canceled because one of the band members was sick.

Lots of angry moms took to social media, super upset over Katseye's last-minute cancelation. FOX 2 talked to one mom named Jessica Lenhardy from Snokie, Illinois, which is a five and a half hour drive from Detroit.
