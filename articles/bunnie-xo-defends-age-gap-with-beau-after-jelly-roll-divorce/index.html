Bunnie Xo defends age gap with beau after Jelly Roll divorce

TheTools · 23.09.2026 23:28 · source · permalink

Bunnie Xo is "living her best life" as she moves on from her divorce from Jelly Roll earlier this year.

The "Dumb Blonde" podcast host, 46, went social media-official with her beau, "Calabasas Confidential" cast member Dylan Wolf, on Sept. 20. Unlike past posts, in which Bunnie alluded to Wolf, the two were both on camera, with the reality TV star teasingly feeding her a bite off his plate during his first experience at Waffle House.

After several commenters criticized their two-decade age difference, Bunnie defended the romance in a comment shared on both her Instagram and TikTok posts.

"Hey guys, I’m having fun & he gave me my smile back 💞," she wrote. "Ya girl is living her best life."

When "Calabasas Confidential" premiered in May on Netflix, Wolf was described as being 24 years old.

The pair was publicly linked together after TMZ shared a video of them kissing at a Nashville bar owned by Jelly Roll over the Fourth of July weekend. Bunnie condemned the person who recorded the clip in a July 9 episode of her podcast and clarified that her ex-husband, with whom she is on good terms, "gets it."

"I don’t want to date anyone," Bunnie added. "I am here for fun, baby. I am having fun."

Bunnie has repeatedly clarified that she and Jelly Roll are amicable, revealing on a June 18 episode of "Dumb Blonde" that even as they settle their divorce, they are "still having a baby together" as planned through in vitro fertilization.

Bunnie and Jelly Roll separated on May 9, according to a May 18 divorce complaint filed by the Grammy-winning singer. Their divorce after nearly a decade, which cited "irreconcilable differences," was finalized by a judge on July 17.

According to a marital dissolution agreement filed in Tennessee on July 17, Jelly Roll will pay his ex-wife a lump sum of an undisclosed amount. Per the court filing, Bunnie and Jelly Roll have "agreed to a certain confidentiality and non-disparagement agreements pursuant to the terms of the Settlement Agreement."
