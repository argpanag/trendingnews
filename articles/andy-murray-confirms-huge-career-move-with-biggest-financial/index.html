Andy Murray confirms huge career move with 'biggest financial gamble' statement

TheTools · 27.10.2026 06:47 · source · permalink

It's a bold move(Image: Julian Finney, Getty Images for Laver Cup)

Andy Murray and his wife, Kim, are set to appear in a fresh Channel 4 documentary series, detailing the revamp of Cromlix, their upmarket Scottish hotel. The three-time Major winner purchased the property, located close to his native Dunblane, in 2013.

Following his retirement from professional tennis in 2024, Murray and Kim have devoted themselves to developing the hotel, with the new three-episode series offering a year-long glimpse into Cromlix's most recent makeover, featuring an additional wing housing two eateries.

The announcement of the documentary follows recognition for one of their establishments, Cradle at Cromlix, in the Michelin Guide. Our Scottish Hotel, arriving on Channel 4 in December, will offer audiences exclusive behind-the-scenes insight into the pair and their staff as they execute the refurbishments, described as "the biggest financial gamble" Murray and Kim have undertaken since acquiring the hotel over ten years ago.

Andy and Kim Murray will feature in the new Channel 4 documentary series, Our Scottish Hotel(Image: Channel 4)

"The last couple of years have been really full of change at Cromlix with a huge building project and a totally new direction for fine dining and wellness, and I was really keen to document that as there were so many personal stories and interesting discoveries along the way," Kim remarked about the upcoming series.

"Andy and I haven't worked on anything together before, so that was a fascinating process - and a learning curve! I've loved shaping this building and estate into what's now become a really thriving and much-loved Scottish business. I hope people enjoy watching the show and meeting the team behind the hotel.", reports the Express.

Our Scottish Hotel charts the journey of the Cromlix team as they strive to maintain their high standards of guest service while major construction work takes place across the grounds. The programme also captures Murray and Kim's return after a five-month shutdown, discovering whether their gamble has been worthwhile.

Our Scottish Hotel follows Kim, Andy Murray and their team as they face a new challenge at Cromlix(Image: Channel 4)

Channel 4's Commissioning Editor Emily Shields commented: "Cromlix is a fascinating world to step inside, and this series offers a rare glimpse behind the scenes of a business where no detail is overlooked.

"Kim and Andy are embarking on an ambitious transformation, but at its heart this is a story about people: the team whose hard work brings Cromlix to life, and the shared desire to create something truly special. We're delighted to be bringing viewers this warm, intimate and revealing look at life behind the doors of one of Scotland's most distinctive hotels."

The programme consists of three hour-long instalments, created by Rogan Scotland for Channel 4. James Rogan, co-CEO of Rogan Scotland, commented: "It's been such a privilege for Rogan Scotland to be invited into Cromlix at this pivotal time. What fascinated us was not simply the scale of the transformation, but how personal every decision became.

"From the food on the plate and the David Shrigley works on the walls to the search for water beneath the estate, the series captures Kim and Andy's contrasting instincts, ambitions and sense of humour. Cromlix is also the story of an extraordinary team, responding to each new challenge with ingenuity, pride and a determination to make every guest feel that nothing unusual is happening at all."
