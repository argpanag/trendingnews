Georgia vs Hungary - Prediction, where to watch, kick-off time and line-ups 14-11-2026

TheTools · 14.11.2026 14:01 · source · permalink

Georgia and Hungary meet in the fifth round of the 2026-27 UEFA Nations League on Saturday, 14 November, at 17:00 (UK time). The match takes place at the Boris Paichadze Dinamo Arena in Tbilisi, with both teams still fighting for promotion from League B.

Hungary sit second in Group B2 with seven points, just one behind leaders Northern Ireland, while Georgia are bottom with two points. The visitors won the reverse fixture 1-0 in October, but the hosts must secure a victory to keep their promotion playoff hopes alive.

Georgia vs. Hungary - Head-to-head record

Georgia and Hungary have met just once, with their first encounter taking place during the current Nations League campaign.

Hungary secured a narrow 1-0 home victory on 2 October, taking all three points in a closely contested match.

Georgia will now have the opportunity to respond in Tbilisi, although their struggles in front of goal could make it difficult to reverse that result. Hungary, meanwhile, will be looking to complete a double over their opponents and strengthen their promotion chances.

Georgia – Crusaders Searching for Their First Goal

Georgia have endured a difficult Nations League campaign, collecting just two points from their opening four matches without scoring a goal. With their promotion playoff hopes hanging by a thread, let’s examine their recent performances and expected line-up.

Recent form

Georgia began their Group B2 campaign with a 1-0 home defeat to Northern Ireland before recording a goalless draw against Ukraine. A further 1-0 loss away to Hungary followed, leaving Ramaz Svanadze’s side struggling at the bottom of the standings.

Their most recent fixture ended in another goalless draw against Northern Ireland, extending their scoring drought to four consecutive Nations League matches. Despite conceding just twice in the competition, Georgia have been unable to turn their defensive solidity into victories.

With six points still available, the Crusaders can mathematically finish second and qualify for the promotion playoffs. However, they must win against Hungary to keep that possibility alive, while their current position also leaves them facing the prospect of a relegation playoff.

Georgia will be without Giorgi Kochorashvili through suspension, while star winger Khvicha Kvaratskhelia is sidelined by injury. Georges Mikautadze, who has scored 23 goals in 47 international appearances, will therefore carry much of the attacking responsibility, with Giorgi Chakvetadze also expected to start.

Hungary – Visitors Closing In on Group Leaders

Hungary have recovered from a slow start to their Nations League campaign, winning their last two matches to move within one point of Group B2 leaders Northern Ireland. Let’s take a closer look at their recent form and expected line-up.

Recent form

Hungary opened their campaign with a disappointing 1-0 home defeat to Ukraine before drawing 0-0 away to Northern Ireland. However, Marco Rossi’s side have since turned their fortunes around with consecutive victories.

A narrow 1-0 home win over Georgia was followed by an impressive 2-1 away success against Ukraine, lifting Hungary to second place with seven points. They have now lost just once in their last eight matches across all competitions, recording five victories during that period.

With Northern Ireland only one point ahead, Hungary remain firmly in contention for automatic promotion to League A. Their final group fixture against the leaders on 17 November could prove decisive, although securing a positive result in Tbilisi is an important first step.

Milos Kerkez returns after serving a suspension, providing a boost to the visitors’ defence. Alex Toth and Milan Toth are both absent from the squad, while Roland Sallai and Barnabas Varga are expected to start. Dominik Szoboszlai should also retain his place in midfield.
