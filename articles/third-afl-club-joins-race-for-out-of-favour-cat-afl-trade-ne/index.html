Third AFL club joins race for out-of-favour Cat: AFL trade news

TheTools · 28.09.2026 21:53 · source · permalink

Adelaide has joined the race for Geelong's Jack Bowes alongside North Melbourne and Essendon.

The Crows are on the hunt for versatility through the midfield and across halfback, which Bowes, throughout his 152 games with the Cats and Gold Coast, has perfected.

According to the Herald Sun, the West Lakes club is keen to lure Bowes to his third state across his AFL journey.

It's understood Bowes will meet with the Bombers and their list manager Matt Rosa as the Tullamarine club looks to add some much-needed experience.

Essendon is also into former Melbourne captain Jack Viney after he was told to explore his options, despite having a contract until the end of 2028.

The Kangaroos have backed up their interest from 12 months ago.

Geelong, North Melbourne and Essendon have all tabled offers, but given he fell out of favour under Chris Scott, playing only nine games this year, more AFL opportunity at a rival could be enough to lure him out of Kardinia Park.

Bowes was famously part of the pick-selling trade by Gold Coast at the end of 2022 in a bid to relieve their salary cap stress.

The Suns parted ways with Bowes and Pick 7, which Geelong used on Jhye Clark, in a move that landed them a third-round selection.

The Cats picked up Bowes' contract, which has finally come to a conclusion at the end of 2026.

He was Pick 10 in the 2016 AFL Draft.
