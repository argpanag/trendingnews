Status yellow rain warning issued for five counties overnight | BreakingNews

TheTools · 15.10.2026 03:56 · source · permalink

From The Irish Times

A status yellow weather warning is in place for five counties overnight and into Thursday morning, with the possibility of localised flooding Met Éireann has said.

Heavy spells of rain are expected in Donegal, Galway, Leitrim, Mayo, Sligo with poor visibility and impacts on travelling conditions.
