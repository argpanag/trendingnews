FC Tokyo vs Urawa Red Diamonds Preview

FC Tokyo square off against Urawa Red Diamonds at Ajinomoto Stadium, with kick-off set for 01:00 on Saturday 10 October.

FC Tokyo secured their third consecutive league win in their latest J1 League game, defeating Nagoya Grampus 1-0 at home at Japan National Stadium. The Chōfu team enjoyed just 38% possession and had two shots on target, with Kota Tawaratsumida netting in the 56th minute.

Urawa Red Diamonds have won their previous two J1 League matches away from home. Their most recent league game was at home at Saitama Stadium where they recorded a 3-1 win over Tokyo Verdy. Gwi-Jea Jo's men enjoyed 47% possession and had four shots on goal, while Matheus Savio was on the scoresheet twice and Harumi Minamino also found the net.

H2H: Tokyo Claimed Penalties Win in Last Head-to-Head

Tokyo have avoided defeat against Urawa Reds in the past three meetings, with a win on penalties (0-0 AET) at Saitama Stadium in the last encounter. The previous 10 H2H meetings have ended in four Tokyo victories, four draws and two Urawa Reds wins.

Tokyo have 5 wins, 2 losses and 3 draws, averaging 1.7 goals from 4.6 shots on goal and 15.5 attempts. The Gas Men have averaged 50.9% possession and 5.3 corners per match, conceding 1.3 goals and 4.3 corners from 3.3 shots on goal and 11.0 attempts.

Motoki Nagakura leads the way with 5 goals, while Marcelo Ryan and Soma Anzai have 4 and 3 respectively. Kashifu Bangunagande, Marcelo Ryan and Motoki Nagakura are the leading assists makers with 2.

Urawa Reds have won 4, lost 5 and drawn 1, with an average of 1.7 goals from 14.1 attempts and 4.0 shots on goal. There’s been an average of 53.1% possession, 4.1 corners awarded and 5.4 corners against them, while they’ve conceded 2.1 goals from 14.3 attempts and 6.0 shots on goal.

Matheus Savio and Harumi Minamino have top scored with 4 goals, while Kotaro Hayashi is next on 1. Look no further than Takuro Kaneko for the top assist maker (5) in the last 10 games.
