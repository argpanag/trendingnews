After UFC 330 in Philadelphia, the UFC heads to the “City of Angels” for UFC 331. Two top fighters face off in the main event, as Burmese-American Joshua Van (17-2-0) defends his flyweight title against Brazilian fighter Alexandre Pantoja (30-6-0) on Saturday (Sept. 19).

UFC 331 takes place at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, California, with a start time of 5 p.m. ET/2 p.m. PT. The main card begins at 9 p.m. ET/6 p.m. PT, with the main event starting around 11:15 p.m. ET/8:15 p.m. PT.

What Is Van’s and Pantoja’s Walkout Music for UFC 331?

Although the UFC has yet to announce each fighter’s walkout songs for UFC 331, both usually go out to the octagon to the same songs during their matches. Van has walked out to “I Don’t Know Dem” by Chief Keef, while Alexandre Pantoja prefers to walk out to “Many Men (Wish Death)” by 50 Cent.
