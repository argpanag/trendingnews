MATCH PREVIEW: Inter Miami CF Visits Atlanta United in Pursuit of Third Consecutive Win | Inter Miami CF

TheTools · 18.10.2026 00:48 · source · permalink

Inter Miami CF (14W-5L-10D, 52 points) hits the road for its upcoming MLS regular season matchup, with the team heading to Atlanta, Georgia to take on Atlanta United (8W-15L-6D, 30 points) this Saturday, Oct. 17. Kick off at Mercedes-Benz Stadium is set for 7:30 p.m. ET.

Where to Watch

Fans around the world can tune in to the action live on Apple TV.

Past Match - Playoffs Clinched

Inter Miami heads to Atlanta in search of a third straight win after defeating NYCFC 2-0 at home on Wednesday to clinch a spot in the Audi 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs with six regular season games in hand. With its qualification, the Club is set to feature in the postseason for a third consecutive season and fifth time overall.

Inter Miami CF This Regular Season

Overall, Inter Miami has recorded 14 wins, five losses, and 10 draws so far this MLS regular season for a total 52 points and sits second in the Eastern Conference standings. Notably, Inter Miami has been the top goal-scoring team in MLS this season with 70 total goals in 29 regular-season games, seven more than the next highest-scoring side.

Messi, the reigning MLS Landon Donovan MVP Award and Golden Boot presented by Audi winner, leads both the team and the league in goals with 23. Additionally, Messi and Rodrigo De Paul lead the team in assists this regular season with 14 each so far.

Previously Against Atlanta United

Saturday's matchup will be the 21st between the sides in Club history. Inter Miami leads the series with eight wins to seven for Atlanta, while five encounters resulted in draws. 

Inter Miami aims to extend its unbeaten run after recording two wins and a draw in the most recent regular season meetings against Atlanta. Last time out, Inter Miami and Atlanta played out a 2-2 draw at Nu Stadium in September.

Scouting Atlanta United

Atlanta United hosts Inter Miami after a 0-3 midweek win on the road against CF Montréal. In all, the Georgia-based side has put together a record of eight wins, 15 losses and six draws for a total 30 points, and occupies the 14th spot in the Eastern Conference table.

Midfielder Miguel Almirón and Alexey Miranchuk have been Atlanta United's top contributors in attack this regular season, with each tallying six goals and seven assists so far.
