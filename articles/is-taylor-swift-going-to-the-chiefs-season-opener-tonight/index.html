Is Taylor Swift Going to the Chiefs Season Opener Tonight?

TheTools · 14.09.2026 21:28 · source · permalink

Ariana Brockington

Trending News Reporter

After a summer filled with love and celebration, Travis Kelce is switching his focus to the football field as his Kansas City Chiefs are set to take on the Denver Broncos in their opener to the 2026 NFL season.

The star tight end and Taylor Swift tied the knot in July at Madison Square Garden, surrounded by their family and A-list friends. Since then, Travis Kelce has been sharing a few details about the nuptials, in between discussing his hopes for the Chiefs’ season with brother Jason Kelce on their “New Heights” podcast. Now that football is officially back, the newlywed, who is entering his 14th season, is looking toward his team’s championship aspirations.

Throughout the season, he’ll likely have his bride cheering him on from the stands, where she has been frequently spotted at both home and away games since the two started dating in 2023.

So will the pop icon make an appearance at the season opener, marking her first return to Arrowhead Stadium since saying “I do”?

So far, Swift has not publicly indicated if she will attend. Here’s what we know so far.

When Do the Chiefs and Broncos Play?

The Kansas City Chiefs will kick off their 2026 NFL season against the Denver Broncos at Arrowhead Stadium on Monday, Sept. 14. It will be the last game of Week 1.

What Time Do the Chiefs and Broncos Play?

The game, which will see the teams go head to head for the first time since the Broncos defeated the Chiefs on Christmas Day last year, will begin at 5:15 p.m. PT/8:15 p.m. ET.

How to Watch and Stream the Chiefs-Broncos Game?

The game will air live simultaneously on ABC and ESPN. Fans who want to stream the game can watch via Disney+.
