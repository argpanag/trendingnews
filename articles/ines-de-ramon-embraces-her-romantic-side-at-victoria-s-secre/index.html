Ines de Ramon attended the 2026 Victoria's Secret Fashion Show, held at Sony Pictures Studios in California on Sunday.

Maya Dehlin/Getty Images

We are used to seeing the jewellery executive alongside Brad Pitt, but she has increasingly been making red carpet appearances in her own right, including a recent front-row outing at Chloé during Paris Fashion Week.

Ines has developed a fondness for lace gowns, but this Elie Saab Spring 2027 chiffon design feels like an elevation of her signature style.

What I love most is how naturally it fits the occasion. The delicate lace inserts, softly draped neckline and barely-there chiffon capture the femininity and sensuality associated with Victoria's Secret, without looking like she has taken the lingerie theme too literally.

The floral appliqués and cascading ruffles add a romantic touch, while the thigh-high slit introduces a subtle hint of sensuality. I also prefer how the gown looks on Ines, as her dark hair and olive skin tone complement the delicate ivory chiffon beautifully.

This is still very much the ultra-feminine, delicate aesthetic she gravitates towards, but there is a greater sense of occasion here. And with each appearance, I am becoming more interested in seeing how her personal style develops.

Makeup Artist: Cedric Jolivet. Hair Stylist: Lauren Leuning.
