First Teaser of Diablo V - Diablo Won and Players Are Being Prepared for a "New Era"

TheTools · 12.09.2026 18:30 · source · permalink

For the currently active Diablo IV, gifts are being given away as part of a Twitch promotion.

At the September BlizzCon 2026 presentation, the developers unveiled a teaser for Diablo V. The debut short trailer showed the logo and revealed the synopsis. According to the plot, Sanctuary has fallen, and Diablo has won.

Diablo V does not yet have a specific release date, but Blizzard Entertainment has outlined a release window — the ARPG is expected to launch in spring 2029.

Currently, the developers urge to follow the news: a promo page for the game has been launched on the company's website.

Today at 11:45 PM Moscow time, the developers plan to hold an event called “Diablo V: On the Path to a New Era.” The project creators will share their plans “for this fallen world and your role in it.” The broadcast will be held on the franchise's official Twitch channel, as well as on YouTube.

For the current Diablo IV, a Twitch Drops promotion is currently underway: you can receive several in-game gifts for watching streams.

As part of BlizzCon 2026, the Blizzard Entertainment team also unveiled a new installment of the StarCraft franchise. However, details of the project have not yet been revealed, and the release will have to wait several years.
