Macau joins Hengqin in building earth data hub | Macau Business

TheTools · 10.09.2026 11:14 · source · permalink

By Aries Un

Hengqin is soon to house a centre dedicated to managing earth-system data for international exchanges, with construction work on this initiative kicking off on 10 September on this island.

According to a Wednesday announcement from the Macao Meteorological and Geophysical Bureau, the construction project is expected to take three years to reach fruition, with another three years earmarked for operation.

This new initiative, a collaboration between the Macau government and the China Meteorological Administration, aims to ‘further build and nurture an ecosystem for the development and utilisation of data elements’.

An earth system refers to an interconnected set of natural components making up the planet.

The press release from the government agency described earth system data as ‘new oil’ in today’s digital era, with its strengths being pronounced across multiple fronts, such as full coverage, deep penetration, and constant availability.

Chen Zhenlin, the director of the China Meteorological Administration, said in the Thursday release that the facility is the first data exchange hub constructed under an overall plan by the agency.

‘Its joint construction by three parties not only serves principal national strategies and supports Macau’s industrial diversification, but also represents concrete action to advance high‑quality meteorological development in China and to promote the global application of China’s intelligent early warning solution,’ Chen stated.

He also said that the latest initiative was built on existing foundations in meteorological science and technology between the two sides.
