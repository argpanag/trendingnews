Aidan Hutchinson injury: What we know after Detroit Lions star hurt knee celebrating

Detroit Lions’ Aidan Hutchinson hurts knee celebrating sack

MUNICH, Germany – Aidan Hutchinson hurt his knee Sunday while celebrating a sack during the Detroit Lions’ game against the Patriots.

How did Aidan Hutchinson get hurt?

Recommended Videos

The New England Patriots reached the red zone quickly on the opening drive of the game, but a delay of game penalty set up third-and-goal from the 13-yard line.

Hutchinson beat the left tackle and tackled Patriots quarterback Drake Maye from behind at the 15-yard line, forcing a field goal attempt.

What was the celebration?

After the play, Hutchinson leapt into the air in celebration, pumped his right arm into the air, and landed with his feet spread apart.

His left foot hit first, but when his right foot landed, his leg was at an odd angle, and Hutchinson immediately fell back onto his hands and back, holding his right knee.

Did Hutchinson walk off the field?

After he spent a couple of minutes on the field with athletic trainers, Hutchinson did manage to walk slowly off the field under his own power.

But during the Lions’ ensuing offensive drive, the broadcast showed Hutchinson being escorted back into the locker room.

What is the official injury update?

According to the Lions, who provided an update before the end of the first quarter, Hutchinson is questionable to return to Sunday’s game due to a knee injury.

Aidan Hutchinson’s injury history

Hutchinson’s outstanding career has twice been interrupted by major leg injuries.

First, during his junior year at the University of Michigan, Hutchinson broke his right ankle, causing him to miss the final three games of the COVID-shortened 2020 season.

In October 2024, when the Lions were playing the Dallas Cowboys, Hutchinson sacked quarterback Dak Prescott and fractured his tibia and fibula. That caused him to miss the rest of that season.

Drake Maye also injured

Maye also went to the locker room after the Hutchinson sack, and the Patriots originally listed him as questionable to return with an ankle injury. He has since been ruled out for the rest of the game.

He was replaced by Tommy DeVito.

Detroit Lions this season

The Lions entered Sunday morning with a 6-2 record, largely thanks to a defense that has allowed just 41 points across three games since the bye.

Detroit is led by its explosive offense, but before the bye, this didn’t necessarily feel like a Super Bowl contending team, despite a 3-2 record.

The improvement by the defense over the past three weeks has changed the feel of this season, and losing Hutchinson for any extended period of time would be a devastating blow.
