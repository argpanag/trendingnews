Rivalry Renewed: Sporting KC Welcomes Road-Cold Real Salt Lake

TheTools · 17.10.2026 22:16 · source · permalink

Sporting KC

Officially eliminated from postseason contention, Sporting Kansas City returns home looking to build on their three-match home scoring spree against a Real Salt Lake squad winless in 12 straight on the road.

Sporting Kansas City vs. Real Salt Lake

Date: Saturday, October 17, 2026

Time: 7:30 PM CT

Venue: Sporting Park, Kansas City, Kansas

How to Watch: Apple TV

Bouncing Back on Short Rest

After enduring a humbling midweek trip to Music City where Sporting Kansas City missed the mark in a 4-0 defeat to Nashville SC, Raphael Wicky’s squad returns to the friendly confines of Sporting Park. Following Wednesday night’s results around the league, Sporting KC has been officially eliminated from postseason contention, confirming they will miss the MLS Cup Playoffs for a third consecutive year.

Yet, there is still plenty on the line in Kansas City. SKC remains locked in an agonizing battle to steer clear of the Wooden Spoon, while visiting Real Salt Lake arrives desperate to keep their faint postseason hopes alive, sitting five points below the Western Conference playoff line with five matches to play.

Can Sporting KC rediscover the home scoring form that saw them bag three goals in three straight games at Sporting Park, or will RSL snap their brutal road woes?

Matchup Trends & Storylines

  • RSL’s Road Misery: The Utah side carries the worst away record in Major League Soccer into Saturday night at 1W–10L–3D. RSL has suffered five straight road losses and is currently riding a 12-match road winless skid (0W–9L–3D), representing the longest active away drought in the league.

  • Home Goals Flowing at Sporting Park: Despite SKC’s defensive issues, their home attack has been buzzing. Sporting KC has scored three goals in three consecutive regular-season matches at Sporting Park for only the second time in club history.

  • Roster Reshuffle in Salt Lake: The RSL squad visiting Kansas City looks vastly different from the team that beat SKC 3-1 in Sandy back in April. RSL brought in eight players during the summer transfer window while transferring standout Homegrown forward Zavier Gozo to Crystal Palace.

Tactical Matchup Keys

  • RSL Danger Men: Rookie forward Sergi Solans leads RSL with 14 goal contributions (11 goals and three assists), while Designated Player Morgan Guilavogui remains their main creative playmaker with two goals and eight assists.
  • Re-establishing the Transition Threat: Sporting KC struggled to build any forward momentum in Nashville, registering just one shot on target. Re-engaging Andre Luiz on the right flank against RSL’s makeshift backline will be essential to dictating tempo early.
  • Defending the Box: Wicky lamented his squad’s passive defending on crosses Wednesday night, calling the goals conceded immature. With Solans lurking as an aerial poacher, Jansen Miller and the central defense must compete for first balls. SKC are just six goals allowed away from tying the all-time worst mark set by the San Jose Earthquakes in 2024.
  • Exploiting Away Fragility: RSL has conceded late goals repeatedly away from home this season. If Sporting KC can get on the board in the opening 30 minutes, RSL’s confidence on the road could unravel quickly.

Prediction: Sporting Kansas City 2-1 Win

Real Salt Lake enters with more to play for on paper, but their league-worst road record and depleted roster make Sporting Park a daunting stop. Sporting KC should benefit from returning regular starters to the lineup after Wednesday’s rotation, and their home attacking output gives them the edge to take all three points.
