'Let’s Pretend GTA 6 Is Coming Out Tomorrow' — The Australian Government Helps Workers Know Their Rights for Taking Time Off for GTA 6

TheTools · 08.10.2026 11:20 · source · permalink

You haven't been talking to the cops, have ya mate?

Australia's workplace regulator, The Fair Work Ombudsman, has put out a helpful graphic for people who might want to take time off when GTA 6 comes out this November. “Let’s pretend GTA 6 is coming out tomorrow, but you don’t have any paid leave available to play on launch day” the graphic says. “Can you take unpaid leave?”

According to the Ombudsman, the National Employment Standards give Australians the right to unpaid leave “under specific circumstances.” So what are those circumstances? Being a carer is one, but that obviously doesn’t apply to playing an open world video game. Helping out with an emergency is another you can scratch off.

It’s all very daft and tongue-in-cheek, but it speaks to the massive impact GTA 6 is going to have across the world. When Game Informer covered GTA 6, it crashed the publication's app because demand was too high. The game is expected to break records, with GTA 6 predicted to make $5.2 billion in launch week alone.

Marketing is going all-in. The lights on the top of Miami’s Kaseya Center have lit up to celebrate GTA 6. If that wasn’t enough, NBA team the Miami Heat will rock Vice City-themed jerseys as part of the same GTA 6 marketing deal.
