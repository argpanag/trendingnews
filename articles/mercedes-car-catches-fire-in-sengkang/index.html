Mercedes car catches fire in Sengkang

TheTools · 03.10.2026 10:48 · source · permalink

An internal combustion engine (ICE) car caught fire along Sengkang East Road at about 7.55pm on Friday (Oct 2) evening.

Videos of the incident posted on social media show the car stopped at the bus bay outside The Vales executive condominium.

Flames are seen coming out of the bonnet area of the vehicle, while several passers-by use fire extinguishers to help keep the fire under control.

A Go-Ahead Singapore bus captain also stops briefly to hand over a fire extinguisher to those helping, before continuing with his journey. 

Meanwhile, a member of the public is seen helping to divert traffic away from the area.

In response to AsiaOne's queries, the Singapore Civil Defence Force stated that the fire involved the engine compartment of said car.

Firefighters used a water jet to extinguish the fire.

There were no reported injuries in the incident and the cause of the fire is under investigations.
