1 dead, 4 injured in Goliath ride incident at Six Flags Over Georgia

TheTools · 13.10.2026 00:55 · source · permalink

COBB COUNTY, Ga. — One of Six Flags Over Georgia’s biggest rides was evacuated after a tragedy at the park.

NewsChopper2 flew over the theme park, where people on the Goliath ride were seen being removed from the ride.

[DOWNLOAD: Free WSB-TV News app for alerts as news breaks]

Officials confirmed to Channel 2 Cobb County Bureau Chief Michele Newell that one park visitor died. Four others were injured.

Goliath riders were being evacuated four at a time.

The ride was stopped after the incident, which police said happened just before 4 p.m., with a set of its cars on the top of the ride.

In a statement shared with Channel 2 Action News, Six Flags Over Georgia said:

We are deeply saddened by the guest incident at our park today. Our thoughts and heartfelt condolences are with the guest’s family, friends, and loved ones. We are also providing support to the guests and team members affected.

The park team and local authorities responded immediately, and we are cooperating fully with local authorities as they complete their investigation. Out of respect for the family’s privacy, we will not be sharing additional details at this time.

—  Six Flags Entertainment Corporation spokesperson

After the death and Goliath evacuation, the City of Mableton, where SFOG is located, released a statement.

We are deeply saddened by the tragic incident at Six Flags Over Georgia today. Our thoughts and heartfelt condolences are with the individual’s family, friends, and loved ones during this difficult time.

Mableton Public Safety Director Dr. Kerry Stallings is coordinating with Cobb County Police and park security as the situation is addressed. We are grateful to the personnel responding to this incident and will continue to support coordination with the appropriate authorities.

Out of respect for the family and the ongoing investigation, we will refrain from commenting further at this time.

—  Mableton Mayor Michael Owens
