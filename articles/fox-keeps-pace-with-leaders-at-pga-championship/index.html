Fox keeps pace with leaders at PGA Championship

TheTools · 19.09.2026 21:31 · source · permalink

New Zealand golfer Ryan Fox is three shots off the lead at the BMW PGA Championship in England after three rounds..

The Open Championship winner is well placed at 10-under par, just three shots off the lead shared by Spaniard Manuel Elvira and Italian Filippo Celli.
