India begin Asian Games gold-medal defence against Japan in women’s cricket

TheTools · 18.09.2026 05:44 · source · permalink

    India faces a busy day at the Asian Games 2026 with multiple sporting events. The Indian women's cricket team begins its gold medal defense against hosts Japan. Soft tennis and teqball athletes will also compete in various categories throughout the day. India enters the cricket tournament as a top contender after winning the Asia Cup. This match marks the first encounter between the Indian and Japanese women's cricket teams.
