Sri Lanka A Bats First Against Hong Kong China in Doha

TheTools · 21.11.2026 07:50 · source · permalink

Sri Lanka A chose to bat first against Hong Kong China in the fifth match of the ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026 at the University of Doha for Science and Technology in Doha, Qatar, on Saturday, November 21. The Group A contest gives Sri Lanka an opportunity to extend its winning start, while Hong Kong seeks its first victory.

The match is scheduled to begin at 11:30 AM Indian Standard Time, corresponding to 9:00 AM local time in Doha. According to the tournament broadcast information published by Lokmat Times, viewers in India can follow the television coverage on Sony Sports Network, with online streaming available through the SonyLIV app and website.

Sri Lanka A entered the fixture after defeating Afghanistan A by six wickets in their opening match. The team successfully chased a target of 140, reaching 144-4 in 21.4 overs at the West End Park International Cricket Stadium in Doha.

Afghanistan A had managed 139 in 35.3 overs after being sent in to bat. Darwish Rasooli top-scored with 80 from 89 deliveries, including eight fours and two sixes, but received limited support from his teammates.

Chamika Heenatigala led Sri Lanka's bowling performance with three wickets for 11 runs. Janith Liyanage and Wanuja Sahan contributed two wickets apiece.

Lasith Croospulle anchored the successful chase with 40 from 48 balls, while Nishan Madushka scored 30 from 24 deliveries. Sineth Jayawardena added 28 off 15 balls before captain Pavan Rathnayake, unbeaten on 29, and Liyanage, unbeaten on 12, completed the victory.

Hong Kong China, meanwhile, entered the match following an eight-wicket defeat against Bangladesh A in their opening Group A fixture. Batting first, Hong Kong posted 219-8 in 50 overs at the University of Doha for Science and Technology.

Anshy Rath scored 77, while Aizaz Khan contributed 67 to give Hong Kong a competitive total. Bangladesh A bowler Iqbal Hossain Emon claimed three wickets for 33 runs.

Bangladesh A completed the chase in 41 overs, reaching 220-2. Captain Saif Hassan remained unbeaten on 86 from 116 deliveries, while Yasir Ali scored an unbeaten 71 off 51 balls. Shahadat Hossain also contributed 43 runs.

Saif Hassan additionally claimed a wicket and received the Player of the Match award for his contribution to Bangladesh's eight-wicket victory.

The ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026 runs from November 19 to 29, bringing together eight emerging Asian teams. The 11-day competition marks the inaugural 50-over edition of the Asia Cup Rising Stars tournament.

For the Sri Lanka A and Hong Kong China fixture, the playing conditions and opening toss decision place Sri Lanka in the batting position, with Hong Kong preparing to begin its bowling innings at the Doha venue.
