Ireland pitted against Finland in Euro U21 play-offs

TheTools · 09.10.2026 09:08 · source · permalink

The Republic of Ireland U21s will play Finland for a place in at the place 2027 European Championships in Albania and Serbia.

Jim Crawford's side were paired with the Finns for the play-off round at the draw in Nyom, Switzerland this morning and the Boys in Green will be relatively pleased with their opponents.

Ireland avoided the Netherlands, Denmark and Czechia all of who are ranked higher than them in the UEFA Under-21 coefficient. Crawford's team are ranked 18th with the Finns in 20th, pointing to what looks like being an evenly match tie.

The play-offs will be played over two legs between 13 and 17 November, and Ireland will be away for the first leg before playing the second leg at home.

Ireland will be full of confidence heading into the play-off given how they secured their place in the draw with a stunning 3-1 victory over England at Turner's Cross to finish runners-up in Group D and edge out Slovakia, who finished third and missed out.

Trailing to an eighth-minute goal by Tyler Dibling, Ireland roared back with two goals from Tommy Lonergan and another from Conor McManus to book their place in the eight-country draw.

Albania (co-hosts), Belgium, Croatia, England (holders), France, Germany, Italy, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Serbia (co-hosts) and Spain have already all qualified for the tournament.

2027 European Championship U21 play-off draw
Denmark v Netherlands
Iceland v Greece
Finland v Republic of Ireland
Turkey v Czechia
