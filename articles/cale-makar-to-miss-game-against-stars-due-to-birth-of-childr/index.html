Cale Makar to miss game against Stars due to birth of children

TheTools · 14.10.2026 23:46 · source · permalink

DALLAS — When the Colorado Avalanche hit the ice for morning skate on Tuesday ahead of their big matchup with the Dallas Stars, they were missing superstar defenseman Cale Makar.

He has a good excuse, though.

The Avalanche will play without Makar on Tuesday as the defenseman stayed back home to be with his wife, Tracy, who is set to give birth to twins at any moment. Head coach Jared Bednar confirmed Makar will not play in Dallas, but there’s a possibility he plays on Wednesday when the rivals meet again in Denver.

In Makar’s absence, Devon Toews will play on the top power play unit, while Sam Malinski will run the second unit. Those two will make up the top defensive pair for the Avalanche. Malinski was a healthy scratch two games ago in Calgary and will get an opportunity to step up in Makar’s absence.

“A guy in our three-hole, he’s got to make a contribution to help us win. He can’t be quiet. He’s moving in the right direction. He’s aware of what he needs to do,” Bednar said of Malinski the other day.

Veteran Noah Juulsen will re-enter the lineup, giving the Avalanche the traditional 12 forwards, six defenseman setup.

With no Makar, expect to see different combinations on the defense on Tuesday.

“They’re going to be bouncing around quite a bit,” Bednar said. “We need other guys to sort of step in in different areas of the game. Hopefully everything goes good for him and Tracy and the family.”

Nathan MacKinnon, who took a maintenance day on Monday, was on the ice for morning skate and will play. Mackenzie Blackwood will start in net for the Avalanche against Jake Oettinger.
