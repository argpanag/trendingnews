Kennedy Center board to propose new options for honoring Trump as center confronts solvency worries

TheTools · 14.09.2026 16:52 · source · permalink

WASHINGTON (AP) — The Kennedy Center Board of Trustees is planning to propose adding President Donald Trump’s name to the facility’s exterior in recognition of his saving the building from its “precarious fiscal position,” according to a new court filing in a suit aimed at blocking the addition of his name to the building.
