Rajat Patidar blasts unbeaten 135 off 48 balls as MP pile on 250 against Assam

Rajat Patidar has produced one of the most extraordinary innings of the tournament, smashing an unbeaten 135 off just 48 deliveries to propel Madhya Pradesh to 250/6 against Assam.
