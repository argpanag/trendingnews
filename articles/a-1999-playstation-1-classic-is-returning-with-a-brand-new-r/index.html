A 1999 PlayStation 1 Classic Is Returning With A Brand-New Release

TheTools · 03.09.2026 16:15 · source · permalink

From Screen Rant on September 3, 2026 | OpenCritic

In this era of remakes, ports, and remasters, it is no surprise that the vast majority of the PlayStation 1's catalog is seeing a revival of sorts. It helps, too, that the PS1 aesthetic has risen in popularity rather significantly within the indie scene, empowered by a deep sense of nostalgia and longing for the vibrant and far simpler era of gaming. Fortunately, this means that many of the genuinely excellent PS1 games that still hold up today are being wrenched from the woodwork and given a new lease on life.

One of the greatest PS1 games is among these titles being revived, as after nearly three decades, it is finally getting a brand-new release. Fans will almost certainly rejoice, but even those who've never heard of the game should check out the project it is a part of, as it looks set to be one of the best products to come from PlayStation in quite a while. At the very least, it should prove an entertaining way to re-experience the glory days of the console and send a little appreciation towards this aforementioned PS1 masterpiece.
