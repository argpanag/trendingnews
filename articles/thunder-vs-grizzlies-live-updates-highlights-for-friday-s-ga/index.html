Thunder vs. Grizzlies: Live updates, highlights for Friday's game

TheTools · 07.11.2026 02:42 · source · permalink

The Oklahoma City Thunder travel on the road to face the Memphis Grizzlies on Friday. This will be the first matchup between the two squads in the 2026-27 regular season. This is also part of the 2026 NBA Cup West Group B stage.

The Thunder (6-2) are finding their footing in their new reality. They had a 124-116 win over the Cleveland Cavaliers on Wednesday. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander led the way with 42 points and eight assists. Ajay Mitchell helped with 23 points and seven rebounds.

Meanwhile, the Grizzlies (6-2) have cooled off from their hot start. They fell in a 107-103 loss to the Indiana Pacers on Wednesday. Ty Jerome and Cedric Coward each scored 23 points apiece.

Half: Thunder 71, Grizzlies 49

A complete second-quarter takeover by the Thunder. Gilgeous-Alexander has 17 points, but Mitchell leads the way with 21 points. They're shooting 64% from the field and 9-of-17 from 3. Meanwhile, the Grizzlies looked completely overwhelmed by OKC's Red Bull-esque energy. Jerome has looked pedestrian. Same with Boozer. Let's see how the second half looks, but the Thunder are on a roll with a lead as large as 25 points.

2Q, 7:26 left: Thunder 50, Grizzlies 36

The Thunder second unit is playing well. Mitchell blasts by Jerome for the wide-open transition layup. Grizzlies call a timeout.

End 1Q: Thunder 35, Grizzlies 28

The Thunder have come out guns-a-blazing. They're 5-of-10 from 3. McCain and Mitchell have combined for 21 points. Gilgeous-Alexander has nine. Offense is running well. Defense could improve, though. Jerome has dissected OKC within the perimeter. He has eight points.

1Q, 6:44 left: Grizzlies 14, Thunder 10

Thunder are going with a much more team-wide approach on offense. Mitchell has six points already. Gilgeous-Alexander hasn't even taken a shot. Meanwhile, Jerome is cooking with six points.

Starting lineups

The Thunder will start Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Jared McCain, Ajay Mitchell, Kenrich Williams and Isaiah Hartenstein. The Grizzlies will start Ty Jerome, Jaylen Wells, Cedric Coward, Cameron Boozer and Zach Edey.
