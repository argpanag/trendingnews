Reigning Most Improved Player Flynn Cameron returns from an ankle injury to give Adelaide a boost.

The Adelaide 36ers have received a massive boost just hours before Thursday’s blockbuster against Melbourne United.

Flynn Cameron, who injured his ankle in Round 1 against United, has passed a game-day fitness test and will suit up for the Sixers in their Round 9 clash at the Adelaide Entertainment Centre.

The Tall Blacks international is coming off a career-best Hungry Jack’s NBL26 campaign, averaging 11.3 points, 4.3 rebounds and 3.3 assists on his way to winning the League’s Most Improved Player award.

The fourth-year guard/forward will be a welcome addition for Trevor Gleeson’s side, who have been desperate for extra creativity alongside six-time MVP Bryce Cotton.

Thursday’s clash tips off at 7.30pm AEDT, live on ESPN.
