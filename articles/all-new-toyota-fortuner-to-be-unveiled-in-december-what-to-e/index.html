All New Toyota Fortuner To Be Unveiled In December: What To Expect

TheTools · 11.11.2026 18:30 · source · permalink

The Japanese automotive giant Toyota is finally getting ready to unveil the next-generation Fortuner. According to recent reports, the Japanese manufacturer will officially unveil the all-new Fortuner on December 1, 2026, in South Africa. This will be the first major generational update for the SUV in nearly a decade. The upcoming Fortuner is expected to get a completely redesigned exterior, a more premium interior, additional features and updated powertrain options. So, here is everything we know about the next-generation Toyota Fortuner so far.

All-New Toyota Fortuner - Expected Exterior Design

First off, let's talk about the design of the upcoming Toyota Fortuner. The next-generation SUV has already been spotted testing multiple times in Thailand. Now, even though the test vehicles were heavily camouflaged, they have revealed a number of important details about the new model.

The biggest change is expected to be at the front, where the Fortuner will get a completely redesigned fascia inspired by the ninth-generation Toyota Hilux. The upcoming Fortuner is expected to feature a much larger front grille, slimmer LED headlamps, a redesigned bumper and more prominent air intakes.

Toyota could also introduce a version of the new Hilux's Cybersumo-inspired front design, although this has not been confirmed. Coming to the side profile, the SUV is likely to retain its familiar tall stance and muscular proportions. However, it could get newly designed alloy wheels, more prominent wheel arches and revised body cladding.

The overall dimensions could also increase slightly, but Toyota has not revealed the exact measurements. At the rear, the new Fortuner is expected to get a redesigned tailgate, updated bumper and a connected LED light bar. Apart from this, the SUV could continue with a prominent roof-mounted spoiler and an upright rear design.

New Interior And Premium Features

Moving inside, the next-generation Toyota Fortuner is expected to get one of its biggest upgrades. The current model always gets criticised for its outdated dashboard and fewer features compared to other SUVs in its price segment.

However, Toyota is now expected to address this with a completely redesigned cabin inspired by the latest Hilux. The upcoming SUV could get a larger 12.3-inch touchscreen infotainment system, which will replace the existing 8-inch unit. Apart from this, a fully digital 12.3-inch instrument cluster is also expected.

The dashboard could feature more premium materials, soft-touch surfaces, updated switchgear and a redesigned centre console. Toyota is also likely to improve the overall fit and finish of the cabin.

In terms of features, the new Fortuner could offer wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay, ventilated and powered front seats, wireless smartphone charging, multi-colour ambient lighting and a 360-degree camera. Toyota could also offer a panoramic sunroof, which has been missing from the current model.

However, this feature has not been officially confirmed. The SUV is expected to retain its seven-seat layout, with three rows of seating. Also, improvements to the second-row comfort and additional convenience features for rear passengers are expected.

Updated Platform And Engine Options

Coming to the mechanical details, the next-generation Fortuner is expected to use a ladder-frame chassis. However, Toyota will most likely introduce an updated version of its IMV platform, which also underpins the latest Hilux pickup truck.

This means that the SUV will continue to offer a rugged body-on-frame construction. This has been one of the biggest reasons behind its popularity among off-road enthusiasts. Mechanically, the new Fortuner is expected to retain the existing 2.8-litre, four-cylinder turbocharged diesel engine.

In its current configuration, this engine produces approximately 201 bhp and up to 500 Nm of torque. The diesel engine is also expected to continue with Toyota's 48V mild-hybrid technology, which is already available on the Fortuner Neo Drive in India. This system uses a small battery and a belt-driven motor-generator to assist the engine during acceleration and recover energy during deceleration.

As a result, it helps in improving fuel efficiency and refinement, mainly during city driving. However, unlike a strong hybrid, this system cannot drive the SUV using electricity alone. Apart from this, Toyota is expected to retain the existing 2.7-litre naturally aspirated petrol engine, which produces approximately 164 bhp and 245 Nm of torque.

Transmission options could include a 6-speed manual and a 6-speed torque converter automatic. Both 4x2 and 4x4 drivetrain configurations are also expected to continue.

Toyota Safety Sense And ADAS

Another major upgrade expected on the next-generation Fortuner is the introduction of Toyota Safety Sense. The SUV could get a Level 2 ADAS package with features such as adaptive cruise control, autonomous emergency braking, lane departure warning, lane keeping assist and automatic high beam assistance.

Apart from this, Toyota could also offer blind spot monitoring, rear cross-traffic alert, a 360-degree camera, electronic stability control, traction control and multiple airbags. However, the company has not yet revealed the complete safety equipment list or confirmed which features will be available on the Indian version.

India Launch And Expected Pricing

Finally, coming to the launch timeline, the next-generation Toyota Fortuner is expected to make its global debut on December 1, 2026, in South Africa. Following its international unveiling, the SUV is expected to enter the Indian market sometime in 2027. However, Toyota Kirloskar Motor has not announced an official launch date.

As for pricing, the all-new Fortuner is expected to command a premium over the outgoing model. Recent estimates suggest that it could be priced between Rs 38 lakh and Rs 52 lakh (ex-showroom), depending on the variant. Once launched, the new Fortuner will continue to compete against premium SUVs such as the MG Majestor, Skoda Kodiaq, Jeep Meridian and others.
