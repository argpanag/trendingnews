Michelle Keegan has been pulled back into one of husband Mark Wright's more revealing radio moments after footage of an Olly Murs prank resurfaced almost exactly a year later.

The incident happened on September 20, 2025, while Wright and Murs were presenting their Saturday show on Heart. Murs ran around the studio desk and pulled down Wright's shorts in the middle of a live promotional link, leaving his longtime friend standing in black briefs as studio cameras recorded the moment.

Wright continued speaking, but the reaction changed once the footage reached social media. Several viewers focused on his appearance and brought Keegan into the comments with suggestive remarks about her husband.

A new Mirror report has revived the clip while Keegan is promoting her ITV drama The Blame. She was not present during the original prank and has not publicly said that the comments embarrassed her.

Olly Murs Pulled Wright's Shorts Down While He Was Live on Air

Murs made his move while Wright was standing behind the Heart studio desk explaining how listeners could win tickets to see Lewis Capaldi.

Wright initially carried on with the link despite being left in his underwear, while Murs laughed behind him. Once he was off air, Wright pulled his shorts back up and confronted his co-host.

"What are you doing? I'm live on the radio, you dope," Wright told him as Murs apologized while continuing to laugh, according to coverage published when the clip first circulated.

Murs uploaded the footage to Instagram with the caption, "Pulled Wrighty's pants down live on air. Sorry mate... couldn't resist." Wright later shared the clip himself.

The two had begun presenting Saturday Breakfast with Mark Wright and Olly Murs in March 2025 after years of friendship, with Heart promoting the show around their off-air banter and competitive dynamic.

Viewers Brought Michelle Keegan Into the Comments

The black briefs became the main topic for some viewers once the clip moved from radio to social media.

"What a lucky girl Michelle is!" one person wrote, according to Entertainment Daily. Other commenters made similarly suggestive jokes about Wright's appearance.

Keegan's name entered the conversation solely because she is Wright's wife. She did not appear in the video or participate in the exchange between the two presenters.

The available reporting from the time contains no response from Keegan to those comments.

Wright Got His Revenge on Murs a Few Weeks Later

Wright retaliated several weeks later after convincing Murs to recreate one of their previous attempts at couples yoga for another social media video.

Murs allowed Wright to lift him over his shoulders, apparently expecting them to repeat a pose they had previously attempted together. Wright instead pulled down his own trousers and farted toward Murs, sending the singer scrambling away.

Wright shared the revenge prank on Instagram, where Murs conceded in the comments that they were now even.

Jessica Wright also reacted to her brother's stunt and joked that their family had been dealing with that side of him for years.

Keegan Caught Wright Playing Golf During Another Heart Appearance

Keegan found herself in another live Heart exchange with her husband on September 16 while promoting The Blame on Heart Breakfast.

Wright joined the interview by FaceTime, and Keegan immediately noticed that he was on a golf course rather than looking after their daughter Palma as she had expected.

"Shock. He's at golf," Keegan said before asking where the baby was. Wright explained that Palma was with his mother, Carol.

Keegan then reminded him that he had told her he would not be playing that day. "You're in trouble," she said, joking that she had never met anyone so addicted to golf.

Hosts Amanda Holden and Jamie Theakston later tested Wright on his knowledge of Keegan's career, with one of his golf clubs at risk for an incorrect answer. After he got a question wrong, wrestler Grado snapped his pitching wedge on camera.

Keegan was appearing on the show ahead of The Blame, the six-part ITV crime thriller in which she stars as DI Emma Crane opposite Douglas Booth's DI Tom Radley.

The ITV series follows Crane and Radley as the murder of teenage figure skater Sophie Madsen leads them into a wider investigation involving lies, institutional cover-ups and corruption inside the fictional Wakestead police force.

The Blame premiered September 20 on ITV1 and is available on ITVX.
