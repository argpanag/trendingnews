Devin Booker and the Phoenix Suns have a SCORE TO SETTLE with the Portland Trail Blazers and everyone doubting them! After a frustrating loss to Portland in the NBA Play-In game, can Booker and the Suns get revenge, make a statement and prove their critics wrong?

The Phoenix Suns are looking to show that this team is the same yet different under head coach Jordan Ott. But can Devin Booker lead Phoenix to a bounce-back performance against the Blazers and throughout this season, or will the same problems that have frustrated Suns fans show up again?

On today’s Southwest Bias, Espo breaks down what’s at stake for Devin Booker, the Suns’ opportunity for payback against Portland, and whether this team can silence the doubters. What needs to change? Who needs to step up? And is this a chance for the Suns to show they’re better than people think?
