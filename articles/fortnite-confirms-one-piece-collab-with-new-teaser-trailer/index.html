Fortnite Confirms One Piece Collab With New Teaser Trailer

TheTools · 24.10.2026 19:35 · source · permalink

Fortnite has confirmed its One Piece collaboration with a new teaser trailer. Back in September, it was teased that the mini-season closing out Chapter 7 would center on One Piece. The official Fortnite Twitter account even played into some of these rumors.

Now, they have officially confirmed the mini-season that will close out Chapter 7 of Fortnite, including a One Piece crossover that has fans excited.

Fortnite Teases Its Next Adventure for November 1

Before confirming the One Piece collab, Fortnite revealed Treasure Hunters in a short teaser featuring a treasure map and a November 1 date. The short trailer also teased the return of Hatsune Miku as a Fortnite Sprite, along with a tease for a Kasane Teto Fortnite Sprite. The trailer also teased another One Piece collaboration.

One Piece is not only a very popular anime with nearly 1,200 episodes to date, but also a long-running manga that has been running for nearly 30 years. VIP has even crossed over into live action with a Netflix-exclusive series. It’s not yet clear which variant the Fortnite crossover will draw inspiration from. Insider Gaming will continue to monitor the story and report any updates as more information becomes available.

One Piece has had its fair share of video games over the last quarter century. Most recently, One Piece: Grand Gourmet brought a light-hearted cooking and restaurant-management sim to Nintendo Switch, PC, and mobile. One Piece characters also appeared in the 2019 Jump Force video game.
