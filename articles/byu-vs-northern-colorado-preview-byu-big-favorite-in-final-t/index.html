BYU vs Northern Colorado Preview: BYU Big Favorite in Final Tune-up Before Maui

TheTools · 18.11.2026 03:34 · source · permalink
  • BYU Basketball

No. 21 BYU wraps up its four-game homestand Wednesday night when Northern Colorado comes to the Marriott Center. This is BYU’s last tune-up before the Maui Invitational starts Monday, when the Cougs open versus Washington. The Cougs are 4-0 and have won every game by double figures. They haven’t covered in their last two games as big favorites, though, so this is a chance to clean some things up before the competition gets a lot better in Maui.

The game tips off at 7pm MT on ESPN+.

BYU and Northern Colorado by the Numbers

BYU KenPom: 24

AP Rank: 21

Northern Colorado KenPom: 248

KenPom Prediction: BYU 94, Northern Colorado 68 — BYU 99% win probability

Point Spread: BYU -30.5

Point Total: 162.5

Northern Colorado Overview

After beating the Big Sky favorite Montana State last week, BYU hosts the team picked to finish #2 in the Big Sky Wednesday night. The Bears went 20-12 last season and return Preseason Big Sky Player of the Year Langston Reynolds after he spent last season at Minnesota; he was at N Colorado for 3 seasons prior.

N Colorado is 2-2 and both wins came against non-Division I teams: 117-64 over Colorado College and 102-52 over Northern New Mexico. Against Division I teams the Bears lost 90-63 at Arizona State and 71-46 to South Dakota State on Saturday.

On paper the Bears move the ball well. They’re averaging 18.3 assists per game and shooting 49.4% from the field and 38.1% from three, but the two blowouts pad those numbers. They’ve also been careless with the ball, with 59 turnovers through four games compared to 51 for their opponents.

Players to Watch

Langston Reynolds, Guard — The 6-foot-4 fifth-year senior runs the offense for the Bears. After 3 seasons at Northern Colorado (he averaged 16 points his third season), Reynolds transferred to Minnesota last season. Reynolds had a solid season for the Gophers, averaging 11.6 points and 4.5 assists. Reynolds is back and has struggled against both D1 teams they’ve played. In four games, he averages 8.5 points and 4.5 assists. Reynolds is not a three-point threat. He is 0-2 season and has averaged less than 1 attempt per game in his career. He is a great passer and can get to the rim with his big frame.

Andrew Crawford, Forward — The 6-foot-8 forward leads Northern Colorado in scoring at 12.3 points per game on 62.5% shooting from the field, and he averages about 7 rebounds. He had 16 points on 6-10 shooting against South Dakota State and a double-double against Northern New Mexico. Crawford does his damage around the basket, so he’ll be the main test for Jaxon Kohler and Khadim Mboup on the glass.

Hunter Caldwell, Center — The 6-foot-10 center averages 10.8 points per game and shoots 45% from three. Caldwell will test BYU’s pick and pop coverage and force Kohler to be away from the rim more.

Prediction

The Bears have been a disappointment to start the season, getting boat raced against both D1 opponents. Reynolds is a good point guard with starting experience in the Big 10, but he’s been underwhelming so far. If he plays up to his potential, he will provide a good test for BYU’s backcourt.

BYU has won comfortably both of their games last week, but have didn’t blowout either team. This is a chance for BYU to gain confidence and tighten up defensive rotations before 3 games in Maui next week. I’ll take BYU to win big, but not quite cover the massive spread versus a N Colorado team that has a good mid major point guard in Langston Reynolds.

Prediction: BYU 93, N Colorado 68
