Man accused of sexually assaulting 14-year-old runaway gets 3 to 6 years

TheTools · 19.11.2026 10:54 · source · permalink

A Marcus Hook man accused of sexually assaulting a 14-year old runaway he met on TikTok was sentenced to 3 to 6 years in a state prison after pleading guilty to statutory sexual assault of a minor, a first-degree felony.

Jihad Matues-Floyd, 24, of Fronefield Avenue, was also ordered to register for life as a sexual offender under Megan’s Law as part of the plea worked out by Assistant District Attorney Bryan Barth and defense counsel Andres Jalon.

Another 23 counts, including indecent assault of a person less than 16, involuntary deviate sexual intercourse and photographing or filming sex acts by a minor were dismissed under the plea accepted Tuesday by Common Pleas Court Judge Stephanie Klein.

Matues-Floyd was charged last year after Chester Police Capt. El’Lan Morgan interviewed the 14-year-old on Dec. 2, 2025.

According to the criminal complaint filed by Delaware County Detective James Simpkins:

Morgan learned the girl made contact with Matues-Floyd on TikTok. She initially thought he was 21 years old.

The two agreed to meet at the Chester transportation center Nov. 17, 2025, and she left her placement residence in Philadelphia at 2:23 p.m. that day.

The victim told authorities that Matues-Floyd arrived in Chester with a friend who was driving a gray or blue sedan.

She told the defendant and his friend that she wore an ankle monitor at that meeting. Mateus-Floyd then searched his phone and found she was 14 years old and had been reported missing.

They went to Matues-Floyd’s house in Marcus Hook where they cut off the monitor and threw it into a neighbor’s yard. He told her at that time that he was actually 24 years old.

Matues-Floyd had the girl crawl through the basement window of his home and then had sex with her on multiple occasions over the next four days. He recorded at least one of these interactions on his phone, but the girl deleted the images.

Matues-Floyd and the same friend with the sedan took the victim to her mother’s house after the four days and warned her, “Don’t tell anyone. I know where you live.”

Police took the victim to Marcus Hook, where she was able to identify the house where she had been staying with the defendant.

The victim gave a second interview to detectives Dec. 4 in which she again detailed her sexual interactions with Matues-Floyd. He turned himself in to police in Lower Chichester that same day and gave a statement admitting to contacting the girl over TikTok, but said she told him she was 21.

He also admitted to picking up the girl in the week before Thanksgiving with a friend in Chester and returning her to his house where they used bolt cutters to remove her ankle monitor.

Matues-Floyd told police he had sex with the victim over the four days they stayed there and filmed her. He said he gave a friend his cellphone because he knew police could check his Internet history.

Matues-Floyd was given credit for time served going back to Nov. 17, 2025, but Klein noted the prison would determine his actual credit time.

Matues-Floyd did not speak at the hearing but Jalon noted he has no prior offenses and had cooperated with police.

“This is a young man who did everything that he was supposed to do after he committed a crime, which is generally an identity crime regarding age more than anything else, and I appreciate the commonwealth’s offer and I think that kind of bears witness as to how he responded and acted in this way,” Jalon said.

In addition to prison, Matues-Floyd was ordered to serve one year of probation and provide a DNA sample to state police. He is not eligible for early release.

Daily Times reporter Pete Bannan contributed to this report.
