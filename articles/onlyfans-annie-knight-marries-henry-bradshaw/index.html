OnlyFans’ Annie Knight Marries Henry Bradshaw

TheTools · 02.10.2026 21:05 · source · permalink

OnlyFans star Annie Knight tied the knot with Henry Bradshaw in an Australian wedding Oct. 1. And once the couple returns from their honeymoon, the model noted she’ll “still be on the platforms.”

Annie Knight has reached a new chapter in her personal life.

The OnlyFans model known for her viral group sex stunts tied the knot with Henry Bradshaw in Australia Oct. 1, nearly two years after they got engaged.

In pictures shared to her Instagram, Knight donned a lavish wedding gown with a flowing train and veil as Bradshaw, in a classic black tux, dipped her back and kissed her.

And now the newlyweds are off on their honeymoon.

“We thought it would be weird to get married... and then just go back to normal life!” Knight told Daily Mail in an interview published Oct. 1. “So, we are going away for a week.”

As for how the adult star’s new title will affect her career? She emphasized that she’ll “still be on the platforms.”

That said, she is making a few changes to her content. “But, yeah, I am winding down the big stunts,” she added. “I will be too busy being a wife to do that!” 

Before getting married, Knight, 29, often participated in viral stunts on the adult-centered content platform, including flying to Fiji in November 2024 with fellow OnlyFans star Bonnie Blue to have sex with high school graduates. Months later, a challenge during which she had sex with 583 men within six hours landed her in the hospital.

For the latter, her then-fiancé—who had proposed two months prior—was supporting her throughout the journey. 

“He was actually at work the day of the event, but he called me in the morning, wished me luck, said, ‘Make us proud. Have fun. Be safe,’” Knight told Us Weekly in May 2025. “And then called me the night of and was like, ‘How was it?’ And I told him all about it.”

She added, “He was proud of me for completing it, because I think he was a bit worried that I wouldn’t be able to and that I’d be upset that I couldn’t do it. He was just really happy for me.”

Plus, Knight emphasized that the experience brought her and Bradshaw even closer.

“It has really made Henry and I just have amazing communication,” Knight told Us Weekly in a separate interview in August 2025. “We have the best communication of anyone ever in the history of the world. In order for it to work the way that it does, that’s all we need is communication.”
