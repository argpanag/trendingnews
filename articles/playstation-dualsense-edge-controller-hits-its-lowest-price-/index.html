PlayStation DualSense Edge Controller Hits Its Lowest Price in Months, and There's No Telling When Sony Will Do It Again - Kotaku

We are now just days away from the biggest game release of this decade. Grand Theft Auto 6 drops on Thursday, November 19. This clocks in two console generation and over 13 years after the last entry in the GTA series. Do you have everything you need to make the most of the game when it finishes installing on your PlayStation 5? What controller are you using? Because now might be the time to make that upgrade to the PlayStation DualSense Edge. It’s even part of one of Amazon’s Early Black Friday Deals.

The PlayStaion DualSense Edge is Sony’s premium wireless controller that adds on features not present on the standard controller which ships with the PS5. It normally sells for $199, but ahead of Black Friday, Amazon now has the controller for just $169. This 15% discount will lend up saving you $40 for a limited time. You can take that some of that money you saved and upgrade to the Ultimate Edition for GTA6.

Customized Gaming Experience

The PlayStaion DualSense Edge comes with far more than the standard controller. With it, you get three sets of swappable analog sticks. The first of these are just the normal default sticks, similar to those of the regular DualSense. The next pair are made with a more rounded surface which are more closely aligned with the sticks of the PlayStation 3’s DualShock controller for folks who miss the old ones. The last set are like the second set except feature a much longer stick. This means you can have more precise control for the actions as it covers a longer radius of movement. You can also replace the entire stick modules down the line if they ever give out to wear and tear through extended use.

The triggers of the PlayStaion DualSense Edge also get some extra attention. Here you can adjust the travel distance to your liking. for fast-paced FPS games, you might want to enable the short hair trigger to fire off quick, repeated shots.

The largest improvement the PlayStaion DualSense Edge has over the standard DualSense is the presence of mappable back buttons. On the reverse side of the controller where your middle or ring fingers lay, you have a pair of buttons which you can assign to any input. This is particularly great for any game that relies heavily on using the right thumbstick as it allows you to keep your thumb controlling the camera while other fingers can now be used to activate an action typically assigned to the face. You can jump or swap your weapon in an FPS or spring in a third-person action game without having to lose control of what you see.

It also comes with a carrying case. We love a good carrying case.
