Kawhi Leonard (knee) is out for the Raptors vs. Timberwolves.

Kawhi Leonard will miss his first game of the 2026-27 NBA season for the Toronto Raptors.

Facing the Minnesota Timberwolves on Sunday, Leonard has been ruled out with left knee swelling.

Many anticipate the Toronto Raptors' face-off against the Minnesota Timberwolves this weekend. However, Kawhi Leonard's unfortunate inactivity lessens the matchup's star power.

The NBA superstar has helped the Raptors off to a solid 2-0 start to their season. However, while averaging 19.5 points in a strictly limited 26.0 minutes, he is shooting just 32.4% from the field (28.6% from threes).

Since they reacquired Leonard this offseason, the Raptors have taken a staunch stand to manage the star forward's load and playing time in hopes of preserving him throughout the course of the year.

Ja'Kobe Walter is expected to start for the Raptors to fill for Leonard.
