KBS comedian's military discipline scandal surfaces again... "If you make a mistake, you get dragged to the boiler room" [Ah Hyung] | STARNEWS

Comedian Ko Myeong-hwan mentioned the military discipline within KBS's comedy division.

On the the 12th broadcast of JTBC's "Knowing Bros," when Ko Myeong-hwan referred to his nickname as "Mung-mung-i," Lee Su-geun revealed, "There is another nickname. KBS juniors called him a traitor. He said he disliked garbage-like broadcasting stations and even became the head of the comedy division at MBC."

Ko Myeong-hwan, a 11th cohort public recruitment comedian for KBS, recalled, "I won third place in the University Comedy Festival. However, I left after just one year. Director Shim Hyung-rae produced 'My Love Jine.' All rookies were cast as Jine's feet. But I had a role as a beetle and appeared on screen. That made me feel burdened. So I quit, loaded bullets, and went to MBC."

Notably, he drew attention by stating regarding the military discipline in comedy, "KBS was stricter. There was such a thing as military discipline. If you made a mistake, you were dragged to the boiler room."

Meanwhile, on this day, Ko Myeong-hwan boasted that he is a restaurant owner with annual sales of 1 billion won, saying, "It now exceeds 1 billion won. After operating franchises, I started a meal kit business."
