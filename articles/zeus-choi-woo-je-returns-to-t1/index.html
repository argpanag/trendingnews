'Zeus' Choi Woo-je Returns to T1

'Zeus' Choi Woo-je is returning to T1.

On the 25th, T1 officially announced the signing of 'Zeus' Choi Woo-je at the '2027 T1 Announcement' held at the T1 Basecamp in Gangnam, Seoul. Zeus will wear the T1 jersey once again after two years. The contract period is reportedly three years.

Having come up through the T1 Academy and been called up to the main roster for the 2021 season, Zeus served as the team's starting top laner through 2024, reaching the League of Legends World Championship (Worlds) finals for three consecutive years and winning back-to-back titles. Heading into the 2025 season, he transferred to Hanwha Life Esports, where over two years he experienced victories in the LCK Cup, First Stand, and the Mid-Season Invitational (MSI).

Following the expiration of his contract with Hanwha Life Esports, Zeus chose his former team, T1, as his next destination. Rejoining his former teammates 'Oner' Mun Hyeon-jun, 'Faker' Lee Sang-hyeok, and 'Keria' Ryu Min-seok—with whom he shared moments of glory—he now embarks on a journey to reclaim the top spot next season.

Zeus stated, "The two years away from T1 felt short yet long to me. It feels like an immature kid has returned as a bit of an adult. I'm excited to see what performance I can deliver, and I hope the fans look forward to it as well." He added, "Naturally, I came back aiming for top results. I want to show consistently strong performances."
