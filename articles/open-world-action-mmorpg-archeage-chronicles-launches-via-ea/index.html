Open-world action MMORPG ArcheAge Chronicles launches via Early Access on November 3 | RPG Site

TheTools · 04.11.2026 02:05 · source · permalink

XLGAMES has announced that open-world action MMORPG ArcheAge Chronicles will launch on November 3 via Steam Early Access. The Early Access package is priced at $29.99.

Originally announced as ArcheAge II in 2022, the MMORPG reappeared with its current title in a 2025 State of Play presentation. The game ranked 5th in Steam's list for Most Played Demos during the most recent Steam Next Fest.

Pre-orders for the MMORPG will reward players with a Dawn Pioneer Set costume for their chosen class: Warrior, Berserker, Ranger, or Mage.

A release date trailer, description, and screenshot set can be found below, via XLGAMES. The official website is here.

XLGAMES today announced that ArcheAge Chronicles, its open-world action MMORPG developed and published by the studio, will launch in Steam Early Access on November 3 at 3:00 PM PST. Pre-orders are open on Steam now!

A Strong Showing at Steam Next Fest

ArcheAge Chronicles ran a playable demo during Steam Next Fest: October 2026 Edition, October 19 to 26. When the festival closed, the game ranked 5th on Steam’s official Most Played Demos list.

Players had plenty to say. They praised the Unreal Engine 5 visuals and a combat system built on the interplay of offense and defense, with parries and cancels at its core. The PvP drew positive reactions too. Many players saw real potential in the game and said they are looking forward to further improvements once Early Access begins.

Pre-Order Bonus: The Dawn Pioneer Set

Pre-order ArcheAge Chronicles and get the Dawn Pioneer Set, an exclusive costume set available only as a pre-order bonus. Each pre-order unlocks one set for a single class of the player’s choice. Claim it in-game once Early Access launches on November 3.

Early Access Plans and Pricing

On October 20, XLGAMES released a developer video laying out what is planned for Early Access: the scope of content, how player feedback will shape future updates, and the game’s pricing. The Early Access package is $29.99 USD in North America. Regional pricing varies in other markets.

“We’d like to thank everyone who played the demo during Steam Next Fest and took the time to share their feedback,” said Yongjin Ham, Executive Producer at XLGAMES. “We plan to continue incorporating your valuable feedback into regular updates throughout Early Access. Even beyond Early Access, we’re committed to staying connected with our players and continuing to improve the game together as we work toward its full potential,” he added.

Key Dates

  • Pre-orders open: October 27 at 8:00 AM PDT, on Steam
  • Early Access launch: November 3 at 3:00 PM PST, on Steam
  • Price: $29.99 USD in North America, regional pricing varies
  • Pre-order bonus: Dawn Pioneer Set, one set for one class of the player’s choice
