Saanich Fair returns this long weekend, more than 150 years since inception

TheTools · 04.09.2026 13:45 · source · permalink

The long running Saanich Fair is returning to Central Saanich this weekend, nearly 160 years after its initial inception.

The Saanich Fair runs from Sept. 5 to Sept. 7 at the Saanich Fairgrounds at 1528 Stellys Cross Rd.

The gates are open from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. on Saturday and Sunday, and from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. on Monday. The event includes midway rides, livestock exhibitions, produce competitions, live music, local food vendors and more.

Organizers say there will be 21 bands and entertainers across two stages during the weekend, and exhibits will range from produce to flowers to baking to crafts and more.

“Our Small but Mighty mini horses are ready for their big weekend, along with horse shows, animal displays and all kinds of agricultural activities throughout the Fairgrounds,” said the Saanich Fair in a social media post this week.

Parking for the event is free for vehicles, and a free bike valet service will be set up at the fairgrounds.

Festival goers will have to purchase admission tickets, as well as additional tickets for midway rides. A full schedule can be found on the Saanich Fair website, and tickets can be purchased at the same link.

The first Saanich Fair kicked off in 1868, and organizers describe it as “one of the oldest continuously running agricultural fairs in British Columbia.”
