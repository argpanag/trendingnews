NCM: Thursday’s weather to be partly cloudy with a chance of rain

TheTools · 18.11.2026 21:17 · source · permalink
  • FAJAR
  • Duhur
  • Asr
  • Maghrib
  • Isha

The National Centre of Meteorology (NCM) forecasts that Thursday’s weather will be partly cloudy to occasionally cloudy, with some convective clouds and a possibility of rain in certain northern, eastern, and coastal regions. Winds will be light to moderate, occasionally picking up in speed.

Winds will blow from the north-west to south-east at speeds of 10 to 25 km/h, reaching up to 40 km/h. In the Arabian Gulf, sea conditions will be light to moderate. The first high tide will occur at 09:11 and the second at 19:15, while the first low tide will be at 13:59 and the second at 02:33.

In the Sea of Oman, conditions will be light. The first high tide will occur at 00:00 and the second at 05:24, while the first low tide will be at 12:08 and the second at 00:00.
