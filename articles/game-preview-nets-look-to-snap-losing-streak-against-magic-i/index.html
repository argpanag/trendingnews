Game Preview: Nets look to snap losing streak against Magic in NBA Cup

TheTools · 20.11.2026 18:17 · source · permalink
  • Nets Game Previews

Brooklyn looks to halt its three-game skid as Orlando visits Barclays Center.

New lows were hit on Wednesday.

The team hosted Julius Erving and some of the ABA championship teams. On live television, Dr. J innocently said he “fully anticipated the Nets to be so fired up having us here” as the team lost by 30. Veteran leader Julius Randle said it was one of the worst games he’s been part of in his 13-year career. Head coach Jordi Fernandez continues to say effort is an issue.

When you lose as much as the Brooklyn Nets have, it all feels like one giant losing streak. To be precise, the Nets have lost three straight games by double figures, allowing at least 120 points in each.

The Orlando Magic also enter Friday looking to bounce back. They surrendered a 17-point lead in a 121-116 loss to the Philadelphia 76ers on Wednesday. Orlando will take a win any way it can get one, especially with Paolo Banchero sidelined by an ankle injury.

Game Info & How To Watch

Who: Brooklyn Nets (3-11) vs. Orlando Magic (6-8)
When: Friday, November 20, 7:30 p.m. ET
Where: Barclays Center, Brooklyn, NY
TV/Streaming: YES Network, DAZN

Nets vs. Magic Injury Report

Brooklyn Nets

  • OUT: Grant Nelson (right ankle sprain)

Orlando Magic

  • OUT: Paolo Banchero (ankle)
  • OUT: Jonathan Isaac (knee)
  • OUT: Izaiyah Nelson (ankle)
  • OUT: Noah Penda (hamstring)

The Game: Hello Brooklyn?

Hard to expect much from the home team. Whether you like Julius Randle or agree with his approach is entirely subjective, but he was right to rip into the team. Someone ought to. He isn’t innocent in all this, certainly not while finishing an entire game with zero rebounds.

Either way, maybe this will light a fire under their you-know-whats.

At 3-11, Brooklyn has reached the point where something needs to change. Win a couple games and you’re back in the play-in picture. Maybe some of the noise calms down, but right now it’s deafening.

As far as Friday is concerned, the Magic have been better than the Nets, but they are not unbeatable — especially without Paolo Banchero. They’re looking for wins any way they can get in a buzzsaw of an Eastern Conference.

They enter this game as the eight seed with a 6-8 record. Franz Wagner had 27 points, eight rebounds, and 11 assists, while Jalen Suggs added 18 points and eight rebounds.

“(We’re) headed back out on the road,” said Magic guard Jalen Suggs. “This (loss) didn’t kill no momentum, but it was a tough one to drop.”

It’s a get-back game for both. Let’s see who can actually execute.

Player to Watch: Franz Wagner

Franz Wagner is coming off one of his best all-around performances of the season, and the Nets will have their hands full trying to slow him down.

It continues his onslaught of late. He’s now coming off three consecutive games with at least 27 points, and his ability to create for himself and others makes him the engine of the Magic offense.

Friday also brings a personal storyline. Wagner will face his older brother, Moritz, for the first time as opponents after the latter spent the first five seasons of Franz’s NBA career as his teammate in Orlando before signing with Brooklyn this summer.

“It’s definitely going to be a little weird,” Franz said. “We’ve obviously played in practice against each other. But I look at it as a huge blessing to be able to share the court together at this level. I’m really happy for him in the situation that he’s in. I think me, Moe and the rest of the family will really enjoy the game as much as anything.”

Oh yeah and it’s an NBA Cup Game. We’ll see if that means anything.
