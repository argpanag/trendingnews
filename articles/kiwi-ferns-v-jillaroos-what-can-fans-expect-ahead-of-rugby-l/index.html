Kiwi Ferns v Jillaroos: What can fans expect ahead of Rugby League World Cup final?

TheTools · 15.11.2026 09:01 · source · permalink
  • On Air
  • Weekend Sport with Jason Pine

The Rugby League World Cup finals are set to get underway this evening, and the Kiwi Ferns are facing off against the Australian Jillaroos.

This will mark the fourth straight time the teams clash in a  Rugby League World Cup final, and the Kiwi side will be hoping to score their first win against the Aussies since 2023.

Ahead of the action getting underway at Brisbane's Suncorp Stadium, Kiwi Ferns assistant coach Ron Griffiths joined Piney to discuss.
