Police: 10 arrested, 1 wanted after more than $38K stolen from Cincinnati seniors

TheTools · 09.11.2026 21:38 · source · permalink

Ten people were arrested and one person is wanted after more than $38,000 was stolen from seniors in Cincinnati in a complex scheme.

The Cincinnati Police Department said it began when an elderly resident of The Kenwood by Senior Star reported stolen checks from his apartment.

That report led to police finding six victims, ranging in age from 85 to 99 years old, who had nearly $40,000 stolen from them combined.

Police said the investigation found that the suspects allegedly used stolen checks, deposited stolen funds and made unauthorized purchases.

The following people have been arrested and indicted: Robyn Brown, Monae Mosley, Sean Caldwell, Taliah Telich, Jaylon Malone, Arnando Miller, Sabryna Overton, Melyana Green, Jordon Malone and Morrigan Crutcher.

Another suspect, Daryl Bullocks, is wanted by police.

WLWT is working to learn what charges the suspects are facing.

“This case is a reminder that financial exploitation of older adults is a serious crime and the Cincinnati Police Department remains committed to protecting our seniors and holding those who target vulnerable members of our community accountable,” the Cincinnati Police Department said in a statement.
