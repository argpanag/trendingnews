The best soccer clubs in Europe compete in the UEFA Champions League from September 2026 to June 2027. Here's how to tune in with the right streaming service, free trial, or VPN.

Getting your Trinity Audio player ready...

The 2026 World Cup brought national teams together to compete on the biggest stage in the world, and now it's time to focus on the individual pro teams. The Union of European Football Associations holds an annual tournament that brings together the best domestic clubs from across Europe to compete in the UEFA Champions League.

The 2026-27 season kicked off in September. The league phase will run through January 2027 before the four-month knockout phase starts in February. The Final will take place on June 5, 2027. In total, this year's UEFA Champions League will feature 81 teams from 53 UEFA member countries.

There's a lot at stake here. Other than bragging rights, the winner will automatically qualify for the 2027–28 UEFA Champions League league phase, the 2027 FIFA Intercontinental Cup final, the 2029 FIFA Club World Cup group stage, and the 2027 UEFA Super Cup. If you're coming off the recently concluded Leagues Cup, or are following along with England's Premier League, here's what you need to know if you're looking to keep up with this season's UEFA Champions League.

Where Can You Stream the UEFA Champions League in the US?

Looking to tune in this season? CBS holds broadcasting rights in the United States through the 2029-30 season. That means most matches will be on Paramount+, but some will also be broadcast on CBS and CBS Sports Network. To tune in to these matches, you'll need a cable subscription or a live TV streaming service. Separately, the Spanish-language broadcast rights are held by TelevisaUnivision and will be on TUDN. Here's how to watch:

  • DAZN: You can watch UEFA Champions League matches by subscribing to DAZN Standard for $25 per month or Ultimate for $50 per month.

  • DirecTV: DirecTV's $90-per-month Entertainment package includes CBS, but you'll need the $125 Ultimate tier to access CBS Sports Network. TUDN can be purchased as an add-on or is included with Ultimate.

  • Fubo: Fuco's $65-per-month Sports plan includes access to CBS and CBS Sports Networks.

  • Hulu + Live TV: For $90 per month, you get CBS, CBS Sports Networks, and TUDN.

  • Paramount+: The primary platform for watching the UEFA Champions League matches is Paramount+, which can be had for as low as $8.99 per month.

  • YouTube TV: Though the standard plan for YouTube TV costs $83 per month, you can pay for the cheaper $65 Sports package to get CBS, CBS Sports Network, and TUDN.

Which Teams Will Compete in the 2026–27 UEFA Champions League?

The league phase of the UEFA Champions League includes 36 teams. Following the qualifying and play-off rounds, seven clubs survived to join the 29 squads that qualified in advance. Among them are five teams from England and Spain; four teams from Germany and Italy; three teams from France; and two teams from the Netherlands, Norway, Portugal, and Turkiye.
