Paige Bests Nikki Bella; Nikki To Face Chelsea Green For The Title In Two Weeks | Diva Dirt

TheTools · 17.10.2026 16:52 · source · permalink

Paige takes round two against Nikki Bella on this week’s SmackDown. And boy did they make it personal.

Throughout the match, Paige mocked Nikki with John Cena moves while Nikki attempted to get a “New Day Rocks” chant started. The final moments of the match had Nikki stack up Paige, but Paige reversed it and used leverage with her feet on the ropes to get the win.

Despite the loss, Nikki is still getting a chance at the WWE Women’s Champion Chelsea Green in two weeks. Green will be facing Liv Morgan at Crown Jewel in a Champion vs Champion match. Will she still be champion for that match to occur, or will Nikki become champion before that?
