Meghan Markle's father charged with possessing child pornography

TheTools · 06.11.2026 16:29 · source · permalink

Thomas Markle photographed in California in 2018, the year his daughter married Prince Harry. (Supplied: MEGA/GC Images)

US authorities have charged Thomas Markle, the father of Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, with possessing child pornography, alleging the 82-year-old had pictures of himself with prepubescent girls.

FBI agents swooped on the Florida aged-care home where Mr Markle lived on Friday, local time. He is expected to appear in court in Tampa later today.

The US Justice Department alleges Mr Markle kept child sexual abuse material in two storage units in the Los Angeles area for nearly two decades.

"These allegations are extremely serious, and we will prosecute Mr. Markle to the fullest extent of federal law," said First Assistant United States Attorney Bill Essayli in a statement.

FBI Director Kash Patel said: "The FBI and our partners have arrested Thomas Markel Sr. after an FBI investigation revealed he was in possession of Child Sexual Abuse Material."

"Mr Markle allegedly targeted innocent children and sought to steal their innocence through his criminal actions. 

British media ran images of FBI agents arresting Mr Markle on Friday morning.

His daughter, the Duchess of Sussex, is an actor and entrepreneur and among the world's most identifiable people.

She married King Charles II's second son, Prince Harry, in 2018. She has been estranged from her father from around that time.

Last month, the Duchess criticised her father when it was revealed police were investigating him.

"I am deeply distressed at the news that has come to light in the last 24 hours, and my thoughts are with any children who may have been harmed," the Duchess said in the statement, issued on October 23, adding: "I hope it can now be understood that, despite certain media publications' distortion over the years, any distance from my father and my choice to not allow him to have contact with our children, was for one reason: to protect them."

The ABC understands she will not comment on his arrest.
