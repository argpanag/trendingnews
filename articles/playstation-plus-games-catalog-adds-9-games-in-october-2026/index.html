PlayStation Plus Games Catalog Adds 9 Games In October 2026

TheTools · 14.10.2026 15:58 · source · permalink

Sony has announced the latest additions to the PlayStation Plus Games Catalog for October 2026. This month, players will get access to as many as nine games, depending on their plan, along with new game trials, DLC packs, and more.

This month, there are seven games joining the catalog for both PlayStation Plus Extra and Premium members. Premium members, however, get two extra games and a number of other perks that you can check out below. There’s even a day-one release on October 20.

Check out the full list of games from Sony below, and be sure to let us know which games you’re interested in checking out down in the comments, or join the discussion in the official Insider Gaming Discord.

PlayStation Plus Game Catalog Games – October 2026

As mentioned, nine games are coming to the catalog depending on your membership. Most of the games will be available starting October 20, unless otherwise noted.

Horizon Forbidden West is a re-release to the service, while Mycopunk is a day-one launch.

PlayStation Plus Extra and Premium

  • Mafia: The Old Country | PS5
  • Horizon Forbidden West | PS5, PS4
  • Mycopunk | PS5
  • Poppy Playtime: Chapter 2 | PS5, PS4 – Available October 27
  • The Midnight Walk | PS5 – Available October 15
  • Tales of Arise – Beyond the Dawn Edition | PS5, PS4 – Available October 27
  • Monster Energy Supercross 25 – The Official Video Game | PS5 – Available October 27

PlayStation Plus Premium

  • Dino Crisis 2 | PS5, PS4
  • Roller Coaster Tycoon 3 Complete Edition | PS5, PS4
  • Game Trials for Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy and Mortal Shell II on October 20

Other PlayStation Plus Perks for October 2026

In addition to the games and game trials, PlayStation Plus members will also get access to the World War Z – Vibrant Halloween Skin Pack as well as the Back 4 Blood – Tunnels of Terror Pack. The World War Z pack is available starting October 20, while the Back 4 Blood pack will be available from October 21 until November 4.
