London City Lionesses opened the Women's Super League season with victory over Manchester United as Alexia Putellas made her debut.

Eder Maestre's side were in control in a more one-sided affair than many expected, as United began their new era under manager Eva Olid in disappointment.

The hosts, watched in the stands by US owner Michele Kang, took an early lead through Julia Zigiotti's own goal when she deflected in Alanna Kennedy's header.

It was game over when Danielle van de Donk's superb flick made it 2-0 to London City with less than 10 minutes remaining and Simi Awujo's stoppage-time strike was little but a consolation goal for United.

They came close on other occasions through Delphine Cascarino and United defender Jayde Riviere also had to clear the ball off the line in the first half.

Two-time Ballon d'Or winner Putellas, who was named captain this week, had a quiet debut but showed glimpses of her well-known quality.

Spain team-mate Mapi Leon had an impressive debut, while Kennedy also stood out in London City's new-look midfield.

United, who had debutants of their own in Andrea Medina and Monica Jusu Bah, did improve in the second half and looked more threatening but it was not enough.

It will raise further questions about United's capability to challenge for a Champions League qualification spot this season.

There will be a much more positive response to London City's showing, though they were dealt a blow with a serious-looking injury to Lucia Corrales in the second half.

London City analysis: Living up to the hype

Nobody really knew what to expect from Maestre's side in the opening game having signed so many players in the summer transfer window.

One of them - Putellas - was met by excited cheers when she ran out before kick-off and London City's billionaire owner Kang greeted fans wearing a match shirt with her name on the back.

London City's two-time Ballon d'Or winner Putellas was aware of the hype her debut would cause and earlier on Friday she launched a new YouTube channel to show the WSL in Spain this season.

The celebrities were in Bromley but could they deliver when it mattered?

It did not take long for London City to prove doubters wrong as Kennedy's deflected header gave them a deserved lead and they quickly grew in confidence, with Van de Donk's flick the icing on the cake late on.

If this performance was anything to go by - albeit against a disappointing United side - they showed they are capable of competing higher up the table this season.

Man Utd analysis: Tough start to Olid era

It was not a strong start to Olid's new tenure as Manchester United manager and some of the same errors from last season were on show.

She promised attacking football and, while patience is obviously needed, there was little to suggest United will be delivering it any time soon.

They had no shots in the first half and were outplayed by an energetic London City side, who took advantage of United's ongoing weakness at defending set-pieces, scoring from two.

The visitors struggled to break through the hosts' press and there was no connection between United's midfielders when they did get possession.

After an underwhelming transfer window, this performance did little to ease concerns by United supporters that they will drop off from their rivals this season.
