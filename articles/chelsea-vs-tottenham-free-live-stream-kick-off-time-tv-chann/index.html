Chelsea vs Tottenham: Preview

Chelsea and Tottenham Hotspur renew their London rivalry on Saturday evening, with both sides facing a significant test as they look to strengthen their position in the Premier League. Stamford Bridge provides the setting for one of the weekend’s most intriguing fixtures.

The Blues will be eager to make home advantage count against their neighbours and were good value for their victory on Merseyside last weekend, with Neto grabbing a brace.

Emiliano Martinez has undergone surgery on a fractured jaw suffered in the win at Everton, and he has now joined Reece James (knee), Jamie Gittens (ankle), Pep Chavarria and Emmanuel Emegha (both unspecified) in the treatment room. Malo Gusto (illness) is also a doubt.

In the absence of Martinez, 21-year-old Mike Penders is set to become Chelsea’s youngest-ever goalkeeper to start a Premier League match, with the 6ft 7in stop-stopper likely to have a three-man defence in front of him in Josh Acheampong, Maxence Lacroix and Levi Colwill.

Tottenham had a lovely moment to begin their 3-1 win over newly-promoted Coventry City last week, as James Maddison, starting his first game in over 500 days, netted a sublime opener to really kickstart their season.

That then followed goals from Omar Marmoush and Ben Davies, as the Lilywhites grabbed a first win of the season to ease some early pressure on gaffer Roberto De Zerbi.
