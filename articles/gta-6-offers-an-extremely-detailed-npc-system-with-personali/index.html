GTA 6 offers an extremely detailed NPC system with personality traits and 600,000 animations: Know more about the features that once delayed it

TheTools · 29.10.2026 21:41 · source · permalink

    Grand Theft Auto 6 is set to introduce a sophisticated NPC system, enhancing player engagement significantly. Each NPC will have individualized personalities and responses based on player interactions, adding depth to gameplay. The game boasts over 600,000 unique animations for its characters, allowing for realistic and diverse behaviors. Rockstar Games established a new studio specifically to create and animate these NPCs, after they caused development delays.
