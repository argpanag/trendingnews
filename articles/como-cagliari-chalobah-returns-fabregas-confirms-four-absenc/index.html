Como-Cagliari: Chalobah returns, Fabregas confirms four absences

TheTools · 20.11.2026 19:11 · source · permalink

Fabregas names 24 players for Saturday 21 November at 15:00 at the Sinigaglia, with Addai and Diao ruled out.

By TuFutbolero Newsroom·November 20, 2026 at 12:11 PM

Como host Cagliari on Saturday 21 November at 15:00 at the Sinigaglia, in the twelfth round of Serie A after the international break. Cesc Fabregas has released a squad of 24 players and the big news is the return of Chalobah, back after a month out with injury, according to TuttoMercatoWeb.

Four players are confirmed absent:

  • Addai
  • Diao
  • Jesús Rodríguez, still sidelined long term
  • Kean, also sidelined long term

The squad also includes the youth players Papaccioli and Fustini, both from the Primavera side.
