Thailand to impose up to 30% duty on online imports to protect local firms

TheTools · 11.11.2026 10:06 · source · permalink
    bookmark

BANGKOK: Thailand's cabinet has approved import duties of up to 30 per cent on goods bought from foreign e-commerce platforms and delivered by express courier or international post, in a move the government says will give local small businesses a fairer chance to compete.

Deputy Prime Minister and Finance Minister Ekniti Nitithanprapas said fast-growing e-commerce had given Thai consumers convenient access to cheap foreign goods, while domestic businesses bear the costs of taxes, wages and compliance with laws and product standards, putting them at a disadvantage.

The government said it was concerned that some cheap imports sold online may be substandard or unsafe and that the rules aim to discourage imports for which no business based in Thailand is accountable for meeting product standards.

This measure is not aimed at blocking online trade or limiting consumer choice, Ekniti said, adding that the tax system and trade rules would be updated to reflect a changing economy. - Xinhua
