CEDAR RAPIDS, Iowa (KCRG) - Clouds will linger across southern Iowa through the overnight hours.

Lows will fall to the upper 40s and low 50s with light northeast winds.

Showers and storms will return during the morning on Monday.

These will linger into the afternoon before some clearing arrives in the evening. Highs on Monday will be in the low to mid 70s.

Winds will pick up in the southeast at 15-25 mph, gusting higher at times.

The active pattern returns Monday night into Tuesday. Widespread showers and storms will move in with the cold front, bringing heavy rain to the region. Tuesday will remain unsettled with showers and storms likely throughout the day.

Highs will reach the low 70s with northwest winds of 5-10 mph.

Heavy rainfall is the main concern this week. With multiple rounds of storms expected through Wednesday, rainfall totals of 2 inches or more are likely across much of eastern Iowa, with localized areas potentially receiving 4+ inches.

Saturated ground conditions will increase the risk of flooding on area creeks and streams. Portions of eastern Iowa are under a Flood Watch through Tuesday morning, where pockets of heavier rainfall may lead to flooding.

The unsettled pattern continues through the week. Wednesday brings additional showers and storms with highs in the upper 60s and low 70s. Thursday shows a slight chance of showers early, with highs in the mid 70s. Friday and Saturday remain stormy, with highs in the mid to upper 70s.
