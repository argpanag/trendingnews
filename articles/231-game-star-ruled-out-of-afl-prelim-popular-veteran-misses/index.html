231-game star ruled out of AFL prelim, popular veteran misses grand final

TheTools · 18.09.2026 10:53 · source · permalink

The Sydney Swans have confirmed a big in, but a teammate is in serious doubt.

The Sydney Swans have confirmed that Justin McInerney will make his long-awaited in the AFL preliminary final against Fremantle, however Joel Amartey will remain on the sidelines and Harry Cunningham has also been ruled out. Meanwhile, Jake Kolodjashnij has reportedly been ruled out of playing in the VFL final for Geelong.

Both McInerney (hamstring) and Amartey (Achilles) haven't played at AFL or VFL in three months. Swans coach Dean Cox revealed on Thursday that McInerney will play against the Dockers, but the form of key forward Hayden McLean has kept Amartey out.

Harry Cunningham (L) is out for Sydney, while Jake Kolodjashnij (R) won't play in the VFL grand final. Image: Getty

The Swans are without captain Callum Mills after he tore his ACL, while Isaac Heeney, Chad Warner, Nick Blakey, James Jordon and Riley Bice are all banned. In a further blow, the Swans have lost Cunningham after he suffered a calf injury at training on Wednesday.

The veteran defender has played 231 career games and his experience will be sorely missed for an already-depleted Swans team. Lewis Melican has come in to replace Cunningham.

"Things fell pretty quickly last night (with Cunningham), and it's just an opportunity (for Melican)" Cox said on Thursday. "Lewis has had some form, played some footy."

Harry Cunningham is out of the Swans' AFL preliminary final. (Photo by Cameron Spencer/Getty Images)

Cox admitted McLean's three-goal performance in the qualifying final upset over Brisbane kept him in the team over Amartey. "Justin will play and Joel won't. That's just the decision we had to make," Cox said on Thursday.

"We need to acknowledge that the players that played in that forward line group, whether they be tall or small, played their role as well as we needed them to. The decision was, 'do we have to turn that around and change it' or 'do we just go in with what worked?', and that's what we're going to do."

Jake Kolodjashnij won't play in VFL grand final

Meanwhile, The Herald Sun is reporting that Kolodjashnij won't play in the VFL grand final between Geelong and Box Hill. Cats coach Chris Scott said last week before the AFL semis that Kolodjashni was unavailable for selection at any level.

Jake Kolodjashnij (centre) after a game for Geelong in Round 6. (Photo by Michael Willson/AFL Photos via Getty Images)

The 31-year-old hasn't played at AFL or VFL level since the revelation he signed a 'waiver' with the Cats to mitigate the club's exposure to future concussion risks. Last month Geelong were fined $140,000 by the AFL for failing to officially notify the league about the waiver, although it was found they didn't intend to conceal its existence or deceive the league.

The waiver was put in place after an ambiguous finding from the league's concussion panel as to whether Kolodjashnij would need to retire. There was some hope that he'd be able to play in the VFL grand final this weekend, but it looks like that won't happen.
