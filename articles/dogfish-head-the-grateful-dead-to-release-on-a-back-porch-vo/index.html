Dogfish Head & the Grateful Dead to Release “On A Back Porch, Vol. 4” for Record Store Day’s RSD Black Friday

TheTools · 05.11.2026 14:23 · source · permalink

Limited-edition, collaboratively curated vinyl offers six live Grateful Dead recordings

MILTON, Del., Nov. 05, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Continuing their partnership of more than a decade, Dogfish Head and the Grateful Dead will release a new collaborative vinyl album, "On A Back Porch, Vol. 4," on Record Store Day's RSD Black Friday, Friday, Nov. 27. Curated by Grateful Dead Archivist & Legacy Manager, David Lemieux, and Dogfish Head Brewer & Founder, Sam Calagione, it features six live recordings from the Grateful Dead archive and will be sold in limited quantities at participating independently owned record stores while supplies last.

"ON A BACK PORCH, VOL. 4" AT A GLANCE:

  • Release Title: "On A Back Porch, Vol. 4"

  • On-Sale Date: Friday, November 27

  • Availability: Participating independently owned record stores nationwide; Limited quantities, while supplies last

  • Curators: David Lemieux, Grateful Dead Archivist & Legacy Manager; Sam Calagione, Dogfish Head Brewer & Founder

BEER & MUSIC PAIRINGS:

Like its predecessors, "On A Back Porch, Vol. 4" brings together songs selected to enjoy alongside a beer on the back porch. Lemieux and Calagione recommend these pairings:

  • Sam Calagione recommends Grateful Dead Juicy Pale Ale with "Blow Away." Dogfish Head's Grateful Dead Juicy Pale Ale (5.3% AV) is a refreshing pale ale brewed with Kernza grains, granola and heaps of good karma. Calagione says, "it's a song all about life's peaks and valleys, and learning to go with the flow, which is nicely complemented by the easy-drinking nature of Juicy Pale Ale."

  • David Lemieux recommends Grateful Dead Citrus Daydream Lager with "Doin' That Rag." Dogfish Head's Grateful Dead Citrus Daydream Lager (5.3% ABV) is a crisp and clean lager brewed with rice, Fonio grain, lemongrass, lime and lemon peels, and a mix of Lemondrop and Contessa hops. Lemieux says, "like Citrus Daydream's interesting mix of ingredients, the song's lively, playful and sometimes, wandering lyrics may seem a bit nonsensical when examined in isolation, but when they come together, it's a rather magical experience."
