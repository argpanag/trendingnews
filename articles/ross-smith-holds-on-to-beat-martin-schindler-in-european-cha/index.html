Ross Smith holds on to beat Martin Schindler in European Championship final

TheTools · 27.10.2026 07:34 · source · permalink

Smith previously won the European Championship in 2022

  • Published
    25 October 2026

Ross Smith held off home favourite Martin Schindler to win the European Championship title for a second time, with world champion Luke Littler having been knocked out in the quarter-finals.

England's Smith reeled off six legs in a row as he took an 8-2 lead in the best-of-21 final against Germany's Schindler in Dortmund.

Schindler got it back to 9-7 before Smith, who won the tournament in 2022, managed to cross the line for an 11-8 victory.

"It's absolutely amazing," Smith, who beat Luke Woodhouse 10-6 and Gerwyn Price 11-7 to get to the final, told ITV.

"I'm not going to lie, I was a bit sweaty there. He wouldn't let up would he? Full credit to him."

Smith won the World Series of Darts Finals in September and added: "To win another major, not just this year, but just to win another major, I'm absolutely over the moon and drained. I'm buzzing."

Schindler thrashed Jonny Clayton 10-1 before beating Littler's conqueror Krzysztof Ratajski 11-6 to reach the final.

Littler's shock exit came in the final match of the quarter-finals in the afternoon session, with Ratajski beating him 10-6.

"Played well in the lead up to today, but just didn't click for me earlier," wrote Littler, who has yet to win the European Championship, in a social media post.

"Thank you for all the support this weekend. The wait for this one goes on."

Sunday's results

Quarter-finals

Ross Smith 10-6 Luke Woodhouse

Gerwyn Price 10-3 Stephen Bunting

Martin Schindler 10-1 Jonny Clayton

Krzysztof Ratajski 10-6 Luke Littler

Semi-finals

Ross Smith 11-7 Gerwyn Price

Martin Schindler 11-6 Krzysztof Ratajski

Final

Ross Smith 11-8 Martin Schindler
