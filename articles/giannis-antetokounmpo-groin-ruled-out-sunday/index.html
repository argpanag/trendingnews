Giannis Antetokounmpo (groin) ruled out Sunday

TheTools · 22.11.2026 22:13 · source · permalink

by Chris Schommer | Heat Correspondent | Sun, Nov 22nd 2026, 5:13pm EST

Giannis Antetokounmpo (groin) has been ruled out for Monday night’s matchup between the Miami Heat and Oklahoma City Thunder according to Marc Stein. (Marc Stein on Twitter)

Fantasy Impact:

Antetokounmpo will receive the night off due to right groin soreness, with Bobby Portis Jr. likely sliding into the starting five while Bam Adebayo, Andrew Wiggins, and Davion Mitchell see increased usage against the Thunder Sunday night.

And now Giannis Antetokounmpo has been ruled out of tonight’s game by the host Heat.
