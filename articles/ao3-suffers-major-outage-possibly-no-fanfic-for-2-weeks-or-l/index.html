AO3 Suffers Major Outage: Possibly No Fanfic for 2 Weeks or Longer - CNET

TheTools · 29.10.2026 21:26 · source · permalink

Archive of Our Own, one of the world's biggest fanfiction sites, went down hard.

Senior Editor / Computers and Gaming Hardware

Lori Grunin

Senior Editor / Computers and Gaming Hardware

October 29, 2026, 5:26 pm ET

October has been a rocky month for readers of the fan-run fiction site Archive of Our Own, popularly known as AO3. Now, a routine disruption has escalated into a crisis. What began as an estimated 12-hour unplanned outage has ballooned into a multi-week shutdown, leaving millions of users locked out of the site following a series of intermittent disruptions throughout the month.

The platform is operated by the Organization for Transformative Works.

An update posted Thursday to the site’s status page and shared via Bluesky and Tumblr said that service restoration will take at least two weeks, possibly four. It went on to say: “We are hopeful that we will be able to bring the site back up in a degraded state sooner. If that does happen, there may be times when the site has to be brought down temporarily until this is fully resolved.”

More from CNET

That update followed an initial notification, 12 hours earlier, with the estimate “AO3 maintenance is in progress. We expect it to take at least 12 hours, but it may take longer.”

An Archive of Our Own representative didn’t immediately respond to a request for comment.

While it’s possible the outage is related to the web-wide PHP patch emergency from earlier this month, the Organization for Transformative Works patched its sister site, Fanlore, on Oct. 21.

So it seems like patching the humongous AO3 repository would come with ample warning, especially from a volunteer team with a reputation for solid communication. Instead, an outage of this magnitude hit readers out of nowhere, landing with all the subtlety of a Looney Tunes anvil.

If you need a stopgap for your fanfic needs, you can try Wattpad or FanFiction.
