Bernardo Silva opens up on Cristiano Ronaldo’s Portugal exit and his Real Madrid struggles: “A lot of sadness”

TheTools · 14.11.2026 09:50 · source · permalink

The new Portugal captain reflects on the end of an era while addressing his changing role at club and international level.

Bernardo Silva has taken center stage for Portugal. The Real Madrid midfielder is assuming a leadership role following Cristiano Ronaldo’s international retirement, taking over as national team captain at the age of 32.

Ahead of the game against Denmark in Braga (today, 8:45 p.m. ET), Bernardo joined head coach Jorge Jesus at the pregame press conference. He reflected on Ronaldo’s departure and discussed his own situation at Real Madrid, where he has yet to establish himself as a regular starter. But he isn’t giving up. He wants to get back to making a difference.

“Cristiano had a long career. I had the pleasure of playing alongside him for 12 years. Of course, there’s a lot of sadness. This day was always going to come sooner or later. But we move forward, obviously trying to honor Cristiano and thank him for everything he did for the national team. The new generation moves forward, as it does in every aspect of life,” he said of his former teammate.

Bernardo is embracing his new responsibilities as captain.

“It’s a tremendous honor. The captaincy isn’t just about one player. It never has been, and it never will be. It’s about a group of experienced players. We’ll do everything we can to welcome the younger players, make sure the locker room is in a good place and maintain a very high level of professionalism and commitment to the national team.”

“Everyone knows the responsibility that comes with Cristiano. Every time he steps onto the field, most of the people in the stadium are there to watch him. That’s why our national team players are used to media attention, pressure and responsibility at their clubs. They’re used to coming under scrutiny when things aren’t going well. It’s not a major problem,” he admitted.

Bernardo Silva opens up about his situation at Real Madrid

Asked about his situation since joining Real Madrid, Bernardo was candid.

“It’s always difficult. We all want to play, we all want to contribute. Throughout my career, I’ve always been used to playing a part and being involved. When I’m not playing, obviously it’s tough. The coach has 27 players, which is a lot, and only 11 can play at a time. It’s a pretty difficult job choosing from a squad with so much talent.

“What I can guarantee is that I’ll always give my best and be willing to keep improving and help the team in whatever way is needed, whether that’s for five, 10, 30 or 90 minutes. Of course, I want to play and feel useful.”

“These days, I don’t know exactly why, but I’m more conservative, more thoughtful, very different from the player I was 10 years ago. That’s led me to play in different positions, a little deeper in midfield.

“But I’m always available to help the national team and my club in whatever way the coach thinks is necessary. The coach will have to answer that question. I think he sees me as an 8 or a 10, a link between defense and attack, rather than as a winger.

“He’s looking for someone who can provide balance between defense and attack, help with transitions, press and contribute defensively. He expects me to be a player who can do a little bit of everything,” he concluded.
