Why VfB Stuttgart will beat Inter Milan in the UEFA Champions League

TheTools · 25.11.2026 22:17 · source · permalink

VfB Stuttgart will continue their UEFA Champions League campaign with a mouth-watering trip to the San Siro, where they will take on reigning Serie A champions Inter Milan. While the Nerazzurri will provide a tough test, Stuttgart have plenty of reasons to believe they can prevail on Wednesday night...

Demirović on fire

Ermedin Demirović has been a regular goalscorer for Stuttgart since his arrival from Augsburg two years ago, but he has clearly taken his game to another level this term. The Bosnia & Herzegovina international is not only his side’s leading Bundesliga goalscorer with five strikes, but he has also strutted his stuff on the continental stage.

Manchester City’s Erling Haaland and Club Brugge striker Nicolò Tresoldi are the only two players to net more than Demirović’s four goals in this year’s league phase, which include a hat-trick on Matchday 1 against Viking – making him the first Stuttgart player to do so in the competition. Despite the summer arrival of Dženan Pejčinović, Demirović has asserted his position as the team’s star striker, and he has what it takes to cause Inter all kinds of problems.

Undav with a point to prove

For one of the first times during his stint at the MHPArena, Deniz Undav has not been firing on all cylinders. Ten games into the Bundesliga season, the Germany international has no goals, and Jürgen Klopp has left him out of his squads because of his lack of form.

Undav, though, has built his career on proving people wrong, and he will be eager to do it again. Despite his lack of goals in the Bundesliga, he still has five assists in all competitions, as well as a strike apiece in the DFB Cup and the Champions League, illustrating that he can still make a telling contribution even when not at his best.

In response to Klopp’s snub, Undav said, “It's tough proving myself to every coach, but I've done it often enough, so I'll do it again”. What better way to show the Germany head coach what he is missing than a match-winning performance on the biggest stage?

Strong Champions League start

Stuttgart have shown they belong in the Champions League on their return to Europe's top table. They kicked things off with back-to-back wins, scoring three goals apiece against both Slovan Bratislava and Viking. That got their feet under the table before a far bigger challenge as they hosted Atlético Madrid.

The game may well have ended in a 1-1 draw, but Stuttgart will have felt desperately unlucky not to win the game, taking the lead through Jamie Leweling and creating a whole host of chances. Ultimately, Atlético had Jan Oblak to thank for coming away with a share of the spoils, and the encounter was further proof that Stuttgart are a match for any opponent. They subsequently lost to Galatasaray 2-0 in their next fixture, although on another day could've scored several goals of their own, and will therefore be eager to bounce back immediately.

Return to form

Stuttgart’s form has been mixed this campaign. While they have shone in the Champions League, they have struggled in the league, losing five of their first seven games. However, they have slowly started to return to their electric past in the Bundesliga, too, with a series of good results.

A trip to Bayer Leverkusen could have overawed them given their problems, yet they picked up an impressive 4-2 win. Their next opponents, Werder Bremen, have been in good form themselves in 2026/27, yet Sebastian Hoeneß’s men churned out a narrow 1-0 victory - only their second Bundesliga clean sheet of the campaign. A third immediately followed in their game before the Inter clash, signalling a recent defensive improvement as they were held to a 0-0 draw by Schalke, to go three league games unbeaten for the first time this season.
