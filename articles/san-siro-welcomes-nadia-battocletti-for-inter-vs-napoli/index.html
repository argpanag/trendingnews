San Siro welcomes Nadia Battocletti for Inter vs Napoli

TheTools · 05.09.2026 16:46 · source · permalink

Community

The Italian middle-distance star takes centre stage ahead of the big match at the Meazza

One of the biggest names in Italian athletics and a passionate Inter fan, Nadia Battocletti was a special guest at San Siro for Inter vs Napoli, enjoying a memorable pitchside experience ahead of kick-off. It was a return to the Inter family for the Italian middle-distance runner, who visited the team at the Inter Training Center in Appiano Gentile last April and spoke about her special bond with the Nerazzurri colours.

Before the match, President and CEO Giuseppe Marotta presented Battocletti with a personalised Inter shirt to celebrate her recent outstanding achievements. At the latest European Athletics Championships in Birmingham, the Italian star claimed a historic double with gold in both the 5000m and 10,000m, becoming the first athlete in history to successfully defend both titles and taking her career tally to four European gold medals. A historic achievement that further cemented Battocletti's status as one of the leading names in international middle-distance running and one of the most prominent figures in Italian sport.

A special evening for a champion with a deep-rooted connection to the Nerazzurri colours. Her love for Inter began at home and grew stronger over the years, culminating in the unforgettable experience of taking centre stage at San Siro.
