Ubisoft's Extreme Sports Playground 'Riders Republic' Launches On Switch 2 This December

TheTools · 21.10.2026 01:55 · source · permalink

Jump into a massive multiplayer sandbox

Ubisoft has shown strong support for the Switch 2 since its launch last year, bringing franchises such as Assassin's Creed to the system. Now, in a special 5th anniversary livestream this week, the Riders Republic team has announced a Switch 2 version of the game is on the way.

This massive multiplayer outdoor playground, focused on all sorts of extreme sports, will be launching for Nintendo's new hybrid system on 9th December 2026. This will also line up with the release of "Offline Play" on the same date.

Ubisoft: "Jump into a massive extreme sports multiplayer open-world across America’s National Parks. From bike races to snow parks and more, your journey starts now! Immerse yourself in iconic American national parks including Bryce Canyon, Yosemite Valley, Mammoth Mountain. Play solo or squad up with your friends and compete in a wide range of events, or give it your best shot in epic mass PvP races with 50+ other players."

Riders Republic was originally released on other platforms in 2021, and includes all sorts of extreme sports including mountain biking, BMX, skateboarding, snowboarding, scooter, wingsuit racing, and much more.

When this game arrived on the Xbox, our sibling site Pure Xbox called it a fantastic open-world sports game with plenty of content to dig into. Since then, it's also received multiple game updates expanding the gameplay.
