Trump wants to bring Nurmagomedov onto the White House press secretary’s team. | Межа

TheTools · 07.11.2026 19:15 · source · permalink

Donald Trump said he offered UFC chief Dana White the White House press secretary job and proposed bringing several fighters into his team. No formal appointment announcement had followed.

U.S. President Donald Trump said he had offered UFC chief Dana White the position of White House press secretary. He also wanted to bring well-known fighters onto his team, including Khabib Nurmagomedov and Islam Makhachev.

Trump wrote this on November 7 on Truth Social.

Photo: Getty Images.

In his post, the president called White a “fighter and warrior” and said he had asked him to bring on Islam Makhachev, Khabib Nurmagomedov, Justin Gaethje, Daniel Cormier, Amanda Nunes, and Kayla Harrison as aides. Trump said they were needed to “protect us from the crazy left.”

Trump urged White to accept the offer. The White House had made no separate staffing announcement by the time this article was prepared.

White heads the UFC and is a longtime friend and public supporter of Trump. He has spoken at Republican Party conventions, attended the president’s second inauguration, and helped organize a UFC event near the White House in June 2026.

Khabib Nurmagomedov is a former UFC lightweight champion from Dagestan who represented Russia during his career. He has had public ties to the Russian authorities: in October 2018 Vladimir Putin met with the fighter and his father after his victory over Conor McGregor.

In September 2019 Putin met with Nurmagomedov again in Makhachkala after his victory over Dustin Poirier. These meetings demonstrated public support for the athlete from the Russian authorities, although Nurmagomedov was not formally part of the government.
