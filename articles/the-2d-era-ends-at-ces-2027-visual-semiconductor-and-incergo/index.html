The 2D Era Ends at CES 2027 - Visual Semiconductor and INCERGO Unveil GF3D™ Immersive Glasses-Free 3D Across Six Product Categories

TheTools · 29.10.2026 09:43 · source · permalink

No glasses. No goggles. No eye tracking.

FREMONT, Calif., October 27, 2026--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Visual Semiconductor, Inc. (VSI) and INCERGO will present GF3D™ Immersive Glasses-Free 3D at CES 2027. VSI regards GF3D as the most significant advance in the viewing experience since television's move from black-and-white to color.

From January 6–9 in Las Vegas, visitors to LVCC Central Hall, Booth 24539 will discover entertainment reborn across six product categories:

- Television
- FreeRange Rolling Display
- Monitor
- Laptop
- Tablet
- Smartphone

A dragon breaks through the plane of the screen. A spacecraft rockets straight toward the viewer. The chase pulls everyone on the couch forward, and movie night becomes a living immersion the whole room feels.

Series ignite with spectacular worlds, astonishing creatures, and unforgettable scenes that draw viewers deeper with every episode—each one another reason to gather and watch.

Gamers feel the thunder of the starting grid, the crackling tension before a showdown, and the breathtaking scale of virtual worlds. GF3D delivers that visual electricity to the player and to the friends right beside them.

There is nothing to put on and nothing to pass around, so the experience is never rationed to whoever has the glasses. Everyone in the room sees it together, and the shared screen becomes a shared moment again.

GF3D is built on VSI's proprietary plenoptic display technology, which delivers multi-viewer immersion across a wide viewing zone with no glasses, goggles, or eye-tracking cameras. AI processing converts supported 2D content to 3D in real time and enhances native 3D, so favorite movies, series, and games become thrilling new experiences.

Every advance in television since color—higher resolution, richer contrast, bigger screens—has sharpened the same flat picture. GF3D leaves the flat picture behind. It brings immersive 3D to the image, and once audiences have seen it, flat 2D no longer sets the standard. It belongs to history.

GF3D is the new color. Flat 2D is the old black-and-white.

Six product categories. One new standard for the screen itself: immersive 3D without glasses, from the living room to the pocket, from the moment audiences press play.

CES 2027. LVCC Central Hall, Booth 24539. Experience GF3D—then decide whether flat is enough.

Manufacturers, technology partners, and media are invited to book demonstrations.

**About Visual Semiconductor**

Visual Semiconductor, Inc. (VSI), based in Fremont, California, develops the proprietary plenoptic display technology and AI-based content conversion behind GF3D™ Immersive Glasses-Free 3D.

**About INCERGO**

Incergo S.A. (Vienna MTF: ICG) partners with VSI on the commercialization of GF3D.
