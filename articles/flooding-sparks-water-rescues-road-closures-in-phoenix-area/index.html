Flooding sparks water rescues, road closures in Phoenix area

TheTools · 16.09.2026 19:50 · source · permalink

SCOTTSDALE, AZ (AZFamily) — Storms packing heavy rain flooded roads in parts of Maricopa County on Wednesday, sparking water rescues and road closures.

Scottsdale Fire Capt. Dave Folio said the monsoon storms dropped up to nearly 3 inches of rain in the northern parts of the city.

The roads currently closed in Scottsdale are:

  • Scottsdale Road between Grayhawk Drive and Deer Valley Road
  • 83rd Street at Pinnacle Peak Road
  • Hayden Road at Medlock Drive

Other road closures in north Phoenix include Cloud Road at 19th and 24th streets.

Just after 2 p.m., three drivers were stranded in about 2 feet of standing water on the Loop 101 on-ramp at Princess Drive in Scottsdale. Arizona Department of Public Safety (DPS) troopers used four-wheel-drive vehicles to push those vehicles out of the water by 2:40 p.m. No one was hurt.

Around 12:30 p.m., first responders were called to a water rescue along State Route 238, about 20 miles west of the city of Maricopa. The Maricopa County Sheriff’s Office (MCSO) reported that a car with two people in their 80s inside was stuck in a wash. An MCSO rescue helicopter was called to the scene.

Video from the Goodyear Fire Department showed rescue teams in the helicopter saving the two people in the car.

State Route 238 is closed between 75th Avenue and near 99th Avenue.

Back in Scottsdale, Folio said the Indian Bench Wash has started to fill up with floodwater, which resulted in closed crossings at:

  • Roosevelt Street
  • Osborn Road
  • Murray Lane
  • Oak Street at 77th Street
  • Medlock Drive
  • Starlight Way
  • Jackrabbit Road
  • Pecos Lane
  • Safeway Plaza entrance north of Chaparral Road along Hayden Road

Mesa Fire and Medical Department crews also responded to calls about vehicles stuck in standing water. Everyone has made it out safely, officials say.

MCSO reports that Bush Highway is closed in both directions at State Route 87 and before Granite Reef Road. The sheriff’s office says Usery Pass is also closed and that residents should avoid the lower Salt River area.

The Flood Control District has issued area lake alerts due to the weather. That includes Bartlett and Horseshoe lakes, which expired at 3 p.m., and Lake Pleasant, which expired at 5 p.m.

Heavy rain also led to partial ground stops at Phoenix Sky Harbor International Airport, impacting departures to the Valley. The first was lifted shortly after 1 p.m., but another ground stop affecting flights to Phoenix was reissued around 2:30 p.m. and expired at 3:45 p.m.
