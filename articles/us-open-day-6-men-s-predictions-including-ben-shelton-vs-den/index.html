US Open Day 6 Men’s Predictions Including Ben Shelton vs Denis Shapovalov - Last Word On Tennis

TheTools · 03.09.2026 18:30 · source · permalink

A place in the round of 16 and the second week at the US Open is on offer on day six. There should be plenty of entertaining tennis and, as always, we here at LWOT will be offering our predictions for every match on the slate, including Carlos Alcaraz vs Wu Yibing, but who will advance?

US Open Day 6 Predictions

Ben Shelton vs Denis Shapovalov

Nurein: Ben Shelton is taking one of the toughest paths to reach the second week, having to face two big hitters in back-to-back matches. But the good thing is that he’s beaten Denis Shapovalov in Dallas this year, and that matchup showed how much Shelton’s serve, forehand and slice can hurt the Canadian.

Prediction: Shelton in 4

Zain: Denis Shapovalov has done a good job so far in this tournament of managing his error count and using his aggressive style of play to overwhelm his opponents. Unfortunately for him, he now takes on Ben Shelton in a battle of lefties, a player against whom he has never won in four meetings. This match should still not be a guarantee for the American, but behind his big serve and more margin baseline game, Shelton should still be able to win.

Prediction: Shelton in 4

Harsh: Shelton starts as the favourite. Shapovalov has been hot and cold this year while Shelton loves playing here. Shelton should get a good test but he should get the win eventually.

Prediction: Shelton in 4

Mariano Navone vs Tomas Martin Etcheverry

Nurein: Mariano Navone backed up his massive win over Novak Djokovic by taking out former semifinalist Matteo Berrettini. He’s looked extremely comfortable grinding through these conditions despite spending over eight hours. Tomas Martin Etcheverry has reached the third round much more efficiently and has had success in this matchup.

Prediction: Etcheverry in 4

Zain: Both of these players are much more suited to the slower clay-court conditions but now find each other just one match away from a second-week run at the US Open. Mariano Navone has shown so far this week that his baseline game can physically suffocate the very best players in tennis, but Tomas Martin Etcheverry is still the more comfortable player on this surface. In an all-Argentine battle, I think Etcheverry comes through in four tightly contested sets.

Prediction: Etcheverry in 4

Harsh: Navone has taken out two big players and he should be feeling pretty good. Etcheverry does not play his best on the hard courts but he has played decently this week. Navone should start as the favourite based on recent form but Etcheverry has more experience at this level and that should help him just edge this one.

Prediction: Etcheverry in 5

Tommy Paul vs Alexander Bublik

Nurein: Tommy Paul leads the head-to-head 5-2, but Alexander Bublik surprisingly knocked him out in last year’s US Open. That said, Paul is still the more dependable athlete and should make Bublik work during rallies. That could take away the Kazakh’s biggest weapons, and when he’s on defense mode, that’s when the errors should creep into his game.

Prediction: Paul in 4

Zain: Arguably the match of the third round sees Alexander Bublik take on Tommy Paul in a rematch of their five-set classic at last year’s US Open. Surprisingly, that was Bublik’s only ATP Tour-level win against the American, as Paul has typically been able to frustrate him into making more unforced errors with his counterpunching skills. This year, Bublik is not quite playing at the level he was in 2025, and that difference should be enough for Paul to exact revenge for last year.

Prediction: Paul in 5

Harsh: Tough match to call. Paul should just get the win in the bag based on home support at the US Open and more consistent tennis.

Prediction: Paul in 4

Daniil Medvedev vs Arthur Rinderknech

Nurein: Daniil Medvedev was once a benchmark in terms of consistency on hard courts, but the Russian has suffered some shocking defeats on this surface. That should give Arthur Rinderknech some belief that he might cause an upset here. The Frenchman has a massive serve to earn cheap points and put pressure on Medvedev’s own serve. This could go the distance.

Prediction: Medvedev in 5

Zain: Daniil Medvedev has flown under the radar to start the tournament, as he is yet to drop a set or even see his serve get broken so far this tournament. Up next is Arthur Rinderknech, a player who has had success against the Russian before. But Rinderknech has been quite inconsistent this season, and if Medvedev keeps serving the way he has so far this tournament, it is very hard not to envision the 2021 champion in New York reaching the second week at the US Open.

Prediction: Medvedev in 4

Harsh: Medvedev is looking good and he may very well have an outside chance to reach the semis or even the final at the US Open. Rinderknech won’t enjoy facing Medvedev due to his counterpunching abilities and superior defense.

Prediction: Medvedev in 3
