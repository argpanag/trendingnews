Lausanne Signs Philipp Kurashev

TheTools · 18.10.2026 05:35 · source · permalink

Transfercoup

Philipp Kurashev at the Olympic Games in Milan.

Keystone

Lausanne has signed Philipp Kurashev, one of the most sought-after Swiss players at the moment. The 27-year-old forward has signed a contract with the Vaud-based team that runs through the end of the season.

Over the past six years, Kurashev has played in 360 NHL games for the Chicago Blackhawks and the San Jose Sharks, recording 150 points. After failing to secure a new contract in North America, he attracted interest from several top clubs in the National League. He has now decided to make the move to Lausanne.

According to the 2024 and 2025 playoff finalists, the contract includes an opt-out clause for the NHL as well as an option to extend it beyond the current season. This keeps open the possibility for Kurashev to return to North America once again.

Kurashev came up through the SC Bern system, where he played alongside Nico Hischier, among others, in the youth ranks. At age 17, he moved to North America. Chicago selected him in the fourth round of the 2018 NHL Draft with the 120th overall pick. He had his best NHL season to date in 2023–24, recording 54 points—including 18 goals—in 75 games.

Kurashev already has experience in the National League. During the 2020–21 season, which was affected by the pandemic, he played 13 games for Lugano and recorded nine assists.
