Which politicians will fill the chamber of the 15th Saeima?

TheTools · 05.10.2026 08:47 · source · permalink

This is only a provisional composition, which may still change slightly when the official results are confirmed. It is also worth bearing in mind that elected candidates sometimes step aside in order to let the next person on the party list pick up the mandate.

It looks like being a mainly male chamber with 38 women included among 100 Saeima deputies. Latvia First (9 out of 17 deputies), the Progressives (6 out of 10) and New Unity (4 out of 7) are the only parties recording a majority of female deputies, more in line with the split between the sexes in the general population.

But as things stand at the moment, the following candidates can expect to occupy a Saeima seat for the next four years. You can find full details here.

Andris Kulbergs, Jānis Jenzis, Līga Meņģelsone, Karīna Miķelsone, Guntars Vītols, Liene Apine, Mārtiņš Brencis, Edgars Štelmahers, Jānis Urtāns, Viesturs Vitovskis, Māris Ozoliņš, Agnese Kupača, Lauris Gruntmanis, Raimonds Bergmanis, Edvards Smiltēns, Aiva Vīksna, Česlavs Batņa, Dagnis Straubergs, Pēteris Dimants, Renārs Putniņš, Dāvis Melnalksnis, Andrejs Svilāns, Anete Biķe, Andis Caunītis, Valdis Kārkliņš, Mārtiņš Prīsis, Edgars Tavars, Andris Skride, Inga Priede, Ilmārs Luksts, Juris Lazdiņš, Baiba Atmane, Māris Kučinskis, Linda Matisone, Edgars Putra, Raivis Kalējs, Jānis Džeriņš, Nellija Kleinberga, Juris Viļums, Valdis Kotāns, Raimonds Vējonis.
