Breezy And Cooler Weekend - WAKA 8

TheTools · 16.10.2026 23:24 · source · permalink

by Shane Butler

A return to fall is underway and we’re expecting some of the coolest air so far this season. Gusty northerly winds will be ushering in the cooler air throughout the weekend.  You can expect lots of sunshine but temps will struggle to reach the 70 degree mark both days.

Saturday and Sunday will have the look and feel of fall.  We’re likely to see a blue sky and feel a cool northerly wind flow across the area.  Winds will be from the north at 10-15 mph with gust 25 to 30 mph.  Daytime temps will reach the upper 60s to lower 70s while overnight temps drop into the upper 40s to lower 50s Saturday and mid to upper 40s Sunday.

Monday will start out colder with temps dropping into the lower to mid 40s.  Abundant sunshine will help warm us back into the lower 70s Monday afternoon.  The rest of the work week is looking sunny and dry.  Mornings will start out chilly in the upper 40s to lower 50s while afternoon highs will manage the upper 70s to lower 80s.
