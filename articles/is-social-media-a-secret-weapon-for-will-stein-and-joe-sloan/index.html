Is social media a secret weapon for Will Stein and Joe Sloan?

TheTools · 25.09.2026 21:58 · source · permalink
  • Kentucky Wildcats Football

“We see something cool, something that fits us, we’ll test it out in practice and see how it looks.”

Following Kentucky’s breakthrough win over Texas AM, head coach Will Stein took time to jump on an interview with Liam Blutman of Barstool Sports on an edition of “Blut Talks Ball.” In that interview, Stein was asked a ton of interesting questions by Blutman, the self-proclaimed ‘No. 1 ball watcher in the world.‘

Stein was asked the classic question: Is Kentucky a basketball school?

“I do think it’s a football school. I think it’s a basketball school. I think it’s a women’s volleyball school. I think it’s a baseball school. I think it’s an elite athletic department. I really do.”

Overall, that was the best way you can possibly answer it, so we thankfully won’t have anything like the Calipari vs. Stoops spat coming out of this.

Stein was later asked, “What’s that one SEC venue you can’t wait to coach in?”

His response: “Every venue you go to is something you dream of.” And then Stein talked about how he and his dad used to collect model stadiums as a kid of college football stadiums, saying, “We had like every SEC stadium.” Stein added that Neyland Stadium is a stadium he has always wanted to go to but never has, and mentioned South Carolina, as it’s on deck for Kentucky next week.

Maybe the most interesting question from Blutman was, “Are you and Coach Sloan just seeing plays on Twitter and being like, hey, we’ll write that one down, maybe we’ll get to that in a few weeks?”

Stein’s response may shock you, saying, “We definitely do that; there’s no doubt about it. We see something cool, something that fits us, we’ll test it out in practice and see how it looks. And if it doesn’t fit for that week, it might fit for the following.”

Twitter can be full of nonsense, but there are truly some incredible football minds on the app. From people in the media to actual coaches, there are amazing plays that you can find any given day that you could try out in real life.

To traditionalists, it’s probably ridiculous, or they would say that the game is lost, but it’s truly a resource for coaches to get their minds spinning a little bit, and it’s working to this point. In this world of sports, it’s adapt or die, and Stein and Sloan are leading the way when it comes to adapting and using any advantage you can.
