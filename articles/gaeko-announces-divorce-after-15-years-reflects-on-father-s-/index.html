Gaeko announces divorce after 15 years, reflects on father’s death through music

TheTools · 16.10.2026 22:20 · source · permalink

Group Dynamic Duo's Gaeko announced his divorce after 15 years of marriage and, revealing that he also suffered the loss of his father this year, drew sympathy.

On the 16th, Gaeko appeared as a guest on KBS 2TV's The Seasons-Sung Si-kyung's Ear Candy and frankly shared stories ranging from his music career to his longing for his late father.

That day Gaeko opened the stage by singing Dynamic Duo's signature song "Jukil nom" alone. He explained, "'Jukil nom' was a song released before I entered the military," adding, "I released it because I couldn't promote music while in the military, and it became the most beloved song among those I released afterward."

Sung Si Kyung especially praised Gaeko's vocal skills. He said, "He is a truly attractive vocalist. A precious vocal," and lauded Gaeko, who has a distinctive charm not only as a rapper but also as a vocalist. Gaeko, feeling bashful, replied, "It feels like being in front of a professor." He then unexpectedly recalled, "I was once out-sung by Park Hyo-shin."

Gaeko humorously described his experience, saying, "In the military I had no choice but to sing a duet and was severely assaulted by singing," meaning he was overwhelmed by Park Hyo-shin's overwhelming vocal power. Hearing this, Sung Si Kyung empathized and laughed, saying, "Hyo-shin shows no mercy. He just does his thing."

But that day Gaeko disclosed an unexpected family story, bringing a solemn mood. He explained why he chose Kim Gunmo's "Areumdaun Ibyeol" as the duet song to sing with Sung Si Kyung. When asked why he picked the song, Gaeko confessed, "My father passed away this year, and I will sing thinking of him." He also said the leather jacket he wore that day had a special story. He explained, "It is a piece of clothing my father passed down to me long ago," saying it was so precious to him that he wore it on stage that day.

Wearing the clothes left by his late father and thinking of him, he decided to sing "Areumdaun Ibyeol." Gaeko's calm confession left a deep impression. In particular, "Areumdaun Ibyeol" contains the meaning of keeping a loved one in one's heart while enduring the pain of parting. Gaeko poured his longing for his father into this song and completed his own special performance.

Recently Gaeko has undergone major personal changes. He announced his divorce from his wife Kim Sumi after 15 years of marriage. Kim Sumi is also continuing a new path, such as returning to modeling after the divorce, and has even announced plans to appear on terrestrial variety shows. After drawing a period on his marriage and then losing his father this year, Gaeko conveyed his feelings through music. Having experienced the two major farewells of divorce and the loss of his father, the sight of him taking the stage wearing the leather jacket his father left him felt even more poignant.

Meanwhile, Gaeko said he plans to hold a concert in December and promised active music activities.
