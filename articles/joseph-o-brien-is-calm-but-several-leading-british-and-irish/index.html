Joseph O'Brien is calm but several leading British and Irish Melbourne Cup contenders at risk of being scratched

TheTools · 29.10.2026 16:50 · source · permalink

Click here to add us to your Google preferred sources or find out more here

One of Joseph O'Brien’s leading Melbourne Cup contenders is at risk of being scratched from the race and will require a further veterinary examination, although the trainer is confident his big hope will be cleared to race.

Defiantly was among five horses to be referred for re-examination ahead of Tuesday’s A$10 million (£5.28m/€6.31m) Lexus Melbourne Cup at Flemington after showing changes in gait during Thursday's mandatory inspections.

The four-year-old, who is ballot-exempt from the Melbourne Cup and currently guaranteed a run after winning the Prix Kergorlay this summer, also presented without a right hind shoe. He will be re-examined on Saturday morning after being re-shod.

Speaking to the Racing Post at Keeneland, O'Brien said: "The vets obviously have their last few checks to get through and they're coming to see him again which is fine. I'm very happy with how they have all prepared and we're looking forward to seeing the draw. 

"It's the same as here [in the US]. There's a whole bunch of horses here to get through vet's checks over the next few days. That's what you have to go through with international racing nowadays, which is right. Hopefully we get a smooth few days. It should be fine."

British contenders Enfjaar, who finished third in the Caulfield Cup on his Melbourne Cup prep run, and Wine Dark Sea have also been flagged for re-examination on Saturday.

Former Willie Mullins' runner Vauban will also be re-inspected after showing a change in gait compared to previous examinations. The 2022 Triumph Hurdle winner is a general 66-1 chance for the Melbourne Cup, in which he finished sixth last year.

A total of 36 horses underwent inspections by Racing Victoria vets on Thursday, with 29 passed as suitable to accept. 

The inspections form part of the rigorous veterinary protocols preceding the final acceptance deadline on Saturday. Any horse not cleared will be unable to take their place in the Melbourne Cup field.

Among those missing out is the Ciaron Maher-trained Saint George, who had been second-favourite behind Point Of Law. The 10-1 shot was ruled out of the race on veterinary advice when deemed to be at heightened risk of injury following a review of CT and PET scans by an independent panel of diagnostic imaging specialists. The Euphrates was also withdrawn.

Last year's winning rider Jamie Melham still faces a nervous wait to discover whether she will get another crack at the race, with her intended mount Future History needing a withdrawal to make the cut for the 24-runner field.

The latest reshuffle leaves Future History 25th on the order of entry, with So You Are moving into the final qualifying position after receiving a 1kg penalty for his victory in the Bendigo Cup on Wednesday.

Melham made history last year when partnering Half Yours to victory in the Melbourne Cup, completing the Caulfield Cup-Melbourne Cup double. However, the Tony and Calvin McEvoy-trained gelding will not defend his title as he continues his recovery from a fall in this month's Caulfield Cup.

The final field will be confirmed on Saturday, with the barrier draw to follow at Flemington after the last race on Derby day.
