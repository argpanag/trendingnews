Forever chemicals found in most waterways, drinking water

TheTools · 16.10.2026 19:07 · source · permalink

WASHINGTON (Gray DC) - A new report from Environment America found that 83% of water samples collected across the United States tested positive for PFAS, also known as forever chemicals.

A U.S. Geological Survey study found nearly half of tap water in the country contains forever chemicals. Bottled water also contains the chemicals.

Trey Sherard, Anacostia Riverkeeper, said, “Their solution? Bottled water may be just a different toxin.”

Where the chemicals come from

PFAS are used in many everyday products. The chemicals are what make non-stick cookware non-stick. Products branded as waterproof or stain-resistant are also likely to contain PFAS.

The chemicals are known carcinogens, specifically linked to ovarian and testicular cancer. They can also cause infertility, and in children, developmental delays.

The biggest sources of PFAS entering water supplies are industrial runoff and firefighting foam.

Calls for regulation

Environment America is calling attention to the issue.

Lisa Frank, who heads Environment America said, “When we allow the use of toxic chemicals and we allow those chemicals to get into our environment without considering the downstream consequences, the problem can get out of hand very quickly,” adding “Removing all those parts from our environment is going to be a very tall order. But stopping new people’s contamination can start right now.”

The organization is calling on lawmakers to take action. U.S. Rep. Paul Tonko, D-New York, is calling for an overhaul of the Safe Drinking Water Act to include forever chemicals.

Tonko said, “Americans deserve to have confidence and peace of mind that when they turn on the tap, they’re being delivered safe, safe drinking water.”

Some of the worst contamination can be found in the Northeast and along the northern Mississippi River.
