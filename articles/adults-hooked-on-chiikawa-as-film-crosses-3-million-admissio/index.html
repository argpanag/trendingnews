Adults hooked on 'Chiikawa' as film crosses 3 million admissions, tops anime chart - The Herald Business

TheTools · 21.11.2026 02:02 · source · permalink

View Korean Original

Young adults connect with the grind of 'Chiikawa' characters

Cumulative admissions surpass blockbuster 'Toy Story 5'

"Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island," a Japanese animated film in which small, adorable creatures act out the weary realities of adult life, has surpassed 3 million admissions in South Korea. The film has claimed the top spot among animated releases this year, drawing not only children but also viewers in their 20s and 30s.

Daewon Media, the film's local distributor, said Saturday morning that cumulative admissions had exceeded 3 million. The milestone came 53 days after the film's Sept. 30 opening, making it the eighth release this year to reach that threshold.

The film has overtaken "Toy Story 5," which drew about 2.95 million admissions, to lead the domestic anime box office this year. It has held the No. 1 spot on the weekend box office for six consecutive weeks starting from its second week of release.

Chiikawa is a character created by Japanese artist Nagano, known in South Korea by the nickname "Meonjak-gwi" — a Korean shorthand for "something small and cute." The characters are small and lovable, but the world they inhabit is far from carefree.

They work to earn money, face setbacks when they fail exams and encounter unexpected dangers — everyday anxieties woven throughout the story. Despite their cute appearance, the characters' struggles with insecurity, competition and financial hardship are widely cited as the reason the film resonates so strongly with adult fans.

A CGV audience analysis released in October showed that viewers in their 20s accounted for 45 percent of admissions and those in their 30s for 20 percent, meaning the two age groups together made up 65 percent of the total. Contrary to the assumption that character-driven animation is for children, young adults have been driving the film's success.

The momentum shows no sign of slowing. As of 10:30 a.m. Saturday, the film held a reservation rate of 8.1 percent — about 23,000 tickets — ranking third overall, behind "Blizzard" (20.5 percent), starring Kim Yun-seok and Gu Gyo-hwan, and Lim Young-woong's concert film "Lim Young-woong: I'm Hero the Stadium 2" (8.5 percent).
