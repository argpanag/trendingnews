Enzo Maresca Backs Manchester City

Manchester City manager Enzo Maresca broke his silence on the situation facing the English club following the Premier League's verdict in the financial irregularities case.


In an interview with the club's official media on Thursday, the Italian coach expressed his calmness regarding the situation and reiterated his complete confidence in the club's leadership.

"Personally, I am very calm. The club has been very clear, and I completely trust it," Maresca said, acknowledging the considerable attention surrounding the team.

However, the manager made it clear that his priority is not administrative matters but maintaining his players' performances on the field.

The Italian insisted that the coaching staff and players must focus exclusively on the aspects they can control during matches.

Manchester City Players Remain Focused

Another topic addressed during the interview was the mood inside the locker room following the ruling related to financial irregularities.



Maresca assured that his players were in excellent spirits and had a positive attitude heading into their upcoming fixtures.

According to the manager, Manchester City's position at the top of the standings provides significant motivation for the squad to remain focused.

He also emphasized that the players fully understand their responsibilities and that outside headlines have not affected the team's preparations.

For the Italian coach, the best way to respond to the situation is to continue working hard and pursue positive results in the competitions the club is involved in.

Liverpool and PSG: Manchester City's Next Challenges

Maresca's comments come during an important week in Manchester City's schedule, with two demanding matches ahead.

First, the Citizens will visit Liverpool at Anfield in a Premier League clash that will mark their return to action following the international break.

The English side will then face Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League, another important fixture for the club's sporting ambitions.

Before those matches, Maresca will address the media on Friday in his first press conference since the verdict was announced.
