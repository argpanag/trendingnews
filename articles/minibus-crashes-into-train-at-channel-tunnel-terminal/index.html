Minibus crashes into train at Channel Tunnel terminal

TheTools · 15.10.2026 22:12 · source · permalink

Two people airlifted to hospital after vehicle carrying HGV drivers collides with Le Shuttle freight service near Calais

A minibus and a freight train have collided at an entrance to the Channel tunnel in France, injuring 19 people.

French authorities said a minibus transporting the drivers of heavy goods vehicles had crashed into a Le Shuttle train just before 7.30pm on Thursday evening.

The crash took place at a freight terminal in Coquelles as the minibus was transporting the drivers to board the shuttle.

The driver and a passenger were seriously injured and airlifted to hospital in Lille, Agathe Cury, the under prefect of  Pas-de-Calais, told a press conference.

 A further 17 people were taken to other hospitals nearby but none of them were said to be in a critical condition, she added.

Ms Cury said the cause of the crash was unclear but an inquiry had been launched.

“For reasons currently unknown,” the minibus struck the shuttle, she said.

 “This is the first time an incident of this type has occurred.”

Police, an ambulance and a helicopter attended the scene. A photograph, published by La Voix du Nord, a news outlet, showed staff in high-visibility jackets surrounding a badly damaged minibus on the platform.

Injured people, who were blurred by the outlet, appeared to be receiving treatment.

‘Head and leg injuries’

Initial reports in the French media suggested that several nationalities were among the injured, and that some of the passengers had suffered head and leg injuries.

Evan Davis, the BBC presenter, witnessed the scene from another shuttle service passing through.

He told the broadcaster: “What happened is we understand this service for lorry drivers – the lorries are parked on these big, open vehicles and the drivers are taken by minibus to a closed carriage, a safe closed carriage for the journey – and it seems to be that a minibus collided with a freight vehicle on the platform.”

The Channel tunnel has three services between Folkestone in Kent and Coquelles in Pas-de-Calais on the European mainland.

Eurostar and Le Shuttle said their services were running normally. Le Shuttle said in a statement that its services were running normally, but its live board showed several departures were yet to be confirmed. 

Eurostar said trains were running normally. Two trains from London St Pancras were cancelled, but the operator said that was because of engineering work. 

A Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office spokesman said: “We are following reports of a collision on the French side of the Eurotunnel. Our thoughts are with all those impacted. We stand ready to provide consular assistance to any British nationals involved”.
