Gossip: Barcelona reignite interest in Arsenal’s Piero Hincapie ahead of January transfer window

TheTools · 03.11.2026 14:53 · source · permalink
  • Barcelona: Transfer Rumors & News

The defender ticks a lot of boxes.

Barcelona have reportedly added Arsenal defender Piero Hincapie to their list of defensive targets as Deco and Co. consider bolstering their backline in the January transfer window.

A new report from Diario Sport claims that Hincapie is a perfect fit for what the club are looking for ahead of the winter transfer window.

Hincapie is a left-footer, with bags of experience and can play at center-back or left-back. He’s also not first choice at Arsenal, meaning there’s a possibility he could be tempted away from the club.

However, convincing Mikel Arteta to part ways with a player who only signed permanently in the summer, after a decent loan spell, may be a different matter entirely.

Barcelona were linked with Hincapie earlier this season and it appears he remains a target for the Catalan giants.
