New Jersey faces another wave of cold temperatures Monday night into Tuesday morning, but a nice warmup will follow on Wednesday and Thursday.

The National Weather Service says clear skies and calm winds will allow temperatures to fall into the upper 20s across much of the state overnight Monday, with lows in the upper 30s along the Jersey Shore due to the moderating influence of warmer ocean waters.

High pressure building into the region will keep skies clear and conditions dry through the week, with northwest winds of 10 to 15 mph on Monday diminishing to less than 10 mph by Monday evening, the weather service said.

New Jersey residents can expect a long stretch of sunny and dry weather this week, with high temperatures climbing into the 60s by Wednesday.National Weather Service

New Jersey will see slightly above-normal high temperatures Tuesday under continued clear skies, with light west-northwest winds under 10 mph.

Wednesday through Friday, temperatures will continue to moderate as high pressure settles over the Mid-Atlantic, with highs reaching into the 60s by Friday.

The dry, fair weather pattern is expected to persist through at least Saturday, with above-normal temperatures of 60 to 65 degrees continuing through the weekend.

A cold front may bring a chance of a few showers Sunday night into Monday, though no significant impacts are expected.
