Relive all the action as England lose to Pakistan in ODI tri-series

  • Scorecard

Summary

  • Pakistan shock England to win by one wicket and secure place in tri-series final

  • Hosts were chasing 242 but slipped to 154-9

  • Shadab hits brilliant 113 not out and shares unbroken 89 for last wicket with Haris Rauf

  • England bowled out for 241 in 46.4 overs after being put into bat

  • Duckett hits defiant 105 off 111 balls - his fifth ODI century

  6. 'Couldn't ask for any more'published at 16:33 GMT
    16:33 GMT

    England captain Harry Brook, speaking at the presentation: "I don't think it went wrong with the ball to be honest. I thought we did an exceptional job to restrict them to us nearly winning the game. We probably didn't quite get enough runs on the board, but the effort the boys put with the ball and in the field was phenomenal and I couldn't ask for any more.

    "Ben Duckett played an exceptional innings - his fifth ODI hundred which came at a good time. He played really well and that partnership that he and Sam Curran had together put us in a decent position to kick on. Unfortunately we lost a few wickets in quick succession but the way that Gus Atkinson finished the innings off was awesome.

    On his side being mathematically still in with a hope of reaching the tri-series final: "Yes. We have to hope Pakistan beat Sri Lanka but we have got to just focus on what we can do against Sri Lanka really and try to beat them by a big margin."
