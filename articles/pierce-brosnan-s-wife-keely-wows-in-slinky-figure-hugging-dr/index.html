Pierce Brosnan's wife Keely wows in slinky, figure-hugging dress in spotlight-stealing photo

TheTools · 18.10.2026 11:32 · source · permalink

The celebrity couple stepped out to attend the Sixth Annual Academy Museum Gala in LA on Saturday October 17

Pierce Brosnan's wife Keely Shaye Smith stole the show on Saturday night as she stepped out on the red carpet alongside her husband to attend the star-studded Annual Academy Museum Gala in LA. 

Keely looked stylish in a slinky, black, ankle length dress, which she teamed with a figure-hugging black jacket and open-toe heels, accessorizing her look with a matching black clutch and a statement silver necklace. 

Her long brunette hair was worn straight in a side parting. For makeup, she opted for a smoky eye, defined brows and a bold red lip. Pierce, meanwhile, looked smart in a tuxedo and held his wife's hand while posing for pictures. 

Other stars in attendance including Goldie Hawn and Kurt Russell, Charlize Theron, Kate Hudson, Pamela Anderson and Demi Moore. 

Pierce Brosnan and Keely Shaye Smith's love story 

The happy couple have been married for 25 years, marking their wedding anniversary in August. Pierce and Keely tied the knot at Ballintubber Abbey in Ireland in 2001, seven years after they first met at a party in Mexico in 1994. 

In honor of their 25th anniversary, Keely shared a heartfelt tribute on social media, alongside a series of throwback photos from over the years. 

She wrote: "What an adventure life is with you. Looking forward to the next 25 years beside you @piercebrosnanofficial." 

Meanwhile, Pierce opened up about Keely during a recent interview with Fox News Digital. "Keely and I love each other and are blessed to have each other as companions in this life," he said. "And we've created a good life for ourselves of hard work and perseverance of life."

He added: "You know, it's just solving one problem after the [other], whether it's the washing machine or how far the finances are going to go or what your dreams are. But, ultimately, we just enjoy each other's company enormously." 

"She's given me wings to fly and lets me, you know, gallivant around the world here making movies," he gushed. "But we're here, and Hawaii is her home and our home." 

Pierce and Keely's family life 

The couple are doting parents to two sons: Dylan, now 29, and Paris, 25. They split their time between their tropical retreat in Hawaii and their luxurious $100 million beach house in Malibu. The couple purchased the Malibu land back in 2000 and built an almost 13,000-square-foot compound on the property. 

Pierce and Keely spent 10 years renovating it into their dream haven that they have dubbed 'Orchid House'. The five-bedroom, 14-bathroom home boasts 117 feet of oceanfront and features a sprawling open deck. 

Pierce is also dad to three children from his first marriage to the late Cassandra Harris - Charlotte - who sadly passed away from ovarian cancer aged 41 in 2013, Christopher Brosnan, born in 1972, and Sean Brosnan, born in 1983.
