VAR denies Forest in unusual circumstances at the City Ground

TheTools · 05.09.2026 16:46 · source · permalink

Nottingham Forest were denied an opening goal in controversial circumstances in their Premier League match with Tottenham.

After absorbing some pressure from Spurs in the second half at the City Ground, Forest thought they had forced an opener in the 66th minute when Neco Williams bundled home after Sandro Tonali had failed to clear Morgan Gibbs-White’s goalbound shot.

Williams, who was sliding forwards, was on the line and protecting his face as Tonali had a second attempt at clearing the ball, but the Wales international got there first.
