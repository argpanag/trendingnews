The FTSE 100 Index Opens 0.03% Higher

TheTools · 04.09.2026 08:45 · source · permalink

The FTSE 100 Index is gaining 3 points or 0.03 percent on Friday.

Leading the gains are Barratt Redrow (1.85%), Vodafone (1.78%) and Halma (1.73%).

Top losers are Experian (-3.60%), Coca-Cola (-1.44%) and British American Tobacco (-1.40%).
