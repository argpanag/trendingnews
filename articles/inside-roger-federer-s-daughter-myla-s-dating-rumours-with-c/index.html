Roger Federer dominated the headlines this past week at the US Open, first because of the USTA's tribute celebrating his legacy and then because of his induction into the International Tennis Hall of Fame in Newport. After an emotional ceremony, Federer and his family returned to New York, where the Swiss legend received his Hall of Fame ring at Arthur Ashe Stadium.

While Federer was enjoying the attention at Flushing Meadows, some of the spotlight shifted toward his daughter, Myla Federer, who was spotted alongside tennis player Lleyton Hewitt's son, Cruz.

The two were seen sitting next to each other while watching the US Open a few nights ago. Cameras captured them sharing smiles and laughs as they looked at something on their phones.

Myla's twin sister, Charlene, and her younger brother were also spotted at Arthur Ashe Stadium, although they were seated separately. Cruz, meanwhile, is one of the leading contenders for the US Open junior boys' title this year.

Are Myla Federer and Cruz Hewitt Dating?

Myla and Cruz have been seen together on several occasions, although there has been no confirmation that the two are dating.

The Federer and Hewitt families have crossed paths regularly throughout the year, with Cruz also spending time with Myla and Charlene when the twins were at Wimbledon earlier this year.

According to an insider, the closeness between the two families could make it natural for Myla and Cruz to develop a friendship.

The source told the publication that it would "make sense" for the two to "at least be mates", given how closely the families have been connected and Cruz's relationship with both Federer twins.

The insider also suggested that if the two were to become a couple, their families would be comfortable with the relationship because they have grown up around similar values.

"If they are really an item, then for both sets of parents, it would be a relief of sorts because they each know that they have grown up together with very similar values," the insider said.

For now, however, there is no confirmation from either family that Myla and Cruz are romantically involved.

Why Could Cruz Hewitt Move to Switzerland?

Cruz has reportedly shown interest in moving to Switzerland, where the Federer twins live, as he looks to further develop his tennis career.

Having the Federer family nearby could provide Cruz with an opportunity to remain close to a family he has known for years while also being around one of the greatest players in tennis history.

However, the move would also represent a major change for the young Australian, and his parents are reportedly conscious of the potential distractions that could come with it.

According to the insider, Lleyton understands why his son would be attracted to the idea but wants Cruz to remain focused on his tennis before making such a major decision.

"Lleyton understands why Cruz is excited about it, but he's also been around tennis long enough to know how quickly distractions can creep in," the insider said.

"He's not shutting the door on Switzerland, but he wants Cruz to earn the right to make those big decisions by putting the work in first."

Cruz is one of three children in the Hewitt family, alongside his sisters Mia, 20, and Ava, 15.

With both the Federer and Hewitt families deeply connected to tennis, Myla and Cruz have grown up around the sport from an early age. Their recent appearance together at the US Open has naturally attracted attention, but for now, the relationship between the two remains a friendship according to the available reporting, rather than a confirmed romance.
