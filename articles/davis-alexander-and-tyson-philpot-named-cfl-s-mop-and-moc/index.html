Davis Alexander and Tyson Philpot Named CFL’s MOP and MOC

TheTools · 07.11.2026 16:12 · source · permalink

Montreal, November 5, 2026 – The Canadian Football League (CFL) announced Thursday the winners of its Most Outstanding Player and Most Outstanding Canadian Awards for the 2026 season.

Quarterback Davis Alexander was named the CFL’s Most Outstanding Player, while Tyson Philpot took home the Most Outstanding Canadian Award.

The voting was conducted by 36 local members of the Football Reporters of Canada (FRC), who had previously selected their respective teams’ individual award winners, along with 20 national FRC members, for a total of 56 voters.

Most Outstanding Player: Davis Alexander

In 16 starts, Alexander led his team to a 13-3 record. The 28-year-old quarterback threw for 5,257 yards, surpassing the 5,000-yard passing mark for the first time in his career. He completed 380 of his 527 pass attempts, including 35 touchdown passes.

The Portland State alum surpassed the 400-passing yardmark five times and threw for more than 300 yards on five other occasions during the season. Alexander ranked second in the CFL in passing yards, touchdown passes and completions despite sitting out the final two games of the regular season.

Most Outstanding Canadian: Tyson Philpot

Philpot enjoyed an outstanding season. He led the CFL with 1,809 receiving yards, nearly 400 more than the next closest player, who was teammate Tyler Snead. He also finished the season with 124 receptions and 13 receiving touchdowns.

He broke the CFL record for most receiving yards in a single season by a Canadian player, previously held by Terry Evanshen, who recorded 1,662 yards in 1967. He also set a new league record for most receptions in a single season by a Canadian player, surpassing the mark held by Brad Sinopoli since 2018.

Philpot also shattered the Alouettes' single-season receptions record of 112, set by Ben Cahoon in 2003 and later matched by Jamel Richardson in 2011.
