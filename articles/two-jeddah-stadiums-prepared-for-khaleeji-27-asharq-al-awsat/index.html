Two Jeddah stadiums prepared for Khaleeji 27 — Asharq Al-Awsat

TheTools · 23.09.2026 08:02 · source · permalink

In Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, preparations have been completed at the King Abdullah Sports City Stadium and the Prince Abdullah Al-Faisal Sports City Stadium for matches of the 27th Arabian Gulf Cup, Khaleeji 27. This was reported by Asharq Al-Awsat, citing the Saudi Press Agency.

Preparing the venues

The local organizing committee prepared the stadium pitches, infrastructure facilities and support services. Work was also completed on spectator entry and exit areas, while separate facilities were prepared for national teams, delegations and media representatives.

The work was carried out in accordance with the tournament's approved operational plans. Arabian Gulf Cup matches will be held at the two venues in Jeddah.

More current news is available on the UA.News Telegram channel Telegram.

Security and fan movement

The organizers also reviewed operational plans, crowd-management measures, security, protection and medical services. Fan movement inside the stadiums and in surrounding areas was organized separately to facilitate entry and exit on match days.

The services required by visitors are expected to be available at the venues during match days.
