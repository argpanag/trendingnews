Nov 9 Toto jackpot snowballs to $10m for fifth time in 2026

TheTools · 07.11.2026 00:00 · source · permalink

An estimated $10 million Toto prize money is up for grabs next Monday (Nov 9) after a cascade draw was triggered.

With no Group 1 winners in the past three draws, the jackpot amount snowballed from nearly $1.2 million on Oct 29 to $2.9 million on Nov 2, and over $5.5 million on Nov 5.

It is the fifth time this year that the top prize pool has reached $10 million.

The previous instances were on Jan 29, May 4 and July 13, as well as the Toto Hongbao draw on Feb 27.

At the July cascade draw, the grand prize of $12.8 million was split between two punters.

The upcoming draw will be held at 9.30pm.

If there is no Group 1 winner at the end of the fourth draw on Monday, the final jackpot amount will be shared by the next prize group's winners.
