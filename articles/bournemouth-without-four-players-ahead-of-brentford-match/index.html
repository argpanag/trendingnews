Bournemouth without four players ahead of Brentford match

TheTools · 12.09.2026 15:39 · source · permalink

Bournemouth will play Brentford in a Premier League match at Vitality Stadium on September 12. Kick-off is scheduled for 15:00. Ahead of the game, Marco Rose's team will be unable to count on four players.

Bournemouth absences

Eli Junior Kroupi continues to recover from a foot injury. Amine Adli, Veljko Milosavljevic and Julian Araujo also remain unavailable, Sky Sports News reports.

Ryan Christie could start after scoring a hat-trick in the League Cup match against Lincoln in midweek. His inclusion in the starting line-up would potentially mean Tyler Adams remains on the bench, with Christie playing in midfield alongside Alex Scott.

More current news is available on the UA.News Telegram channel Telegram.

Start of the season

Bournemouth have yet to record a Premier League victory this season. The team took the lead in each of their first three matches but lost to Manchester City and drew with Everton and Newcastle.

Brentford have not suffered defeat in their first three league matches. At the same time, the team surrendered a lead in their two most recent fixtures.
