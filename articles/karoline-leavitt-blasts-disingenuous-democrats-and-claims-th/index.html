Karoline Leavitt blasts 'disingenuous' Democrats and claims they 'fool' voters

TheTools · 03.11.2026 14:40 · source · permalink

Karoline Leavitt has admitted the one thing Democrats are "good at" while dubbing James Talarico as "disingenuous."

On November 3, Election Day, the 29-year-old former White House press secretary appeared on Fox News' Fox & Friends to urge Texas voters to keep the Lone Star State red, even after Talarico, who is battling Ken Paxton for a Senate seat, made some moderate remarks. During an appearance on the YouTube show The Diary of a CEO on Monday, the Democrat claimed he's willing to work with "builder" President Donald Trump.

Fox News co-host Brian Kilmeade, 62, asked Leavitt if she bought Talarico's remarks. The mother of two said she didn't buy it "for a second" because Democrats always change their tune heading into an election just to win.

On The Diary of a CEO, Talarico said, "I would love, as the next U.S. Senator from Texas, to work with Donald Trump to actually fulfill some of the promise of his political project... If he wants to make sure that every single kid in this country gets high-quality accessible childcare, I'm there.

"If he wants to tackle the housing problem - Donald Trump is a builder. If he wants us to slash the red tape in this country so we could build millions of new homes and ensure everyone in this country has a place to call home? I'm there. I'll work with him to do it. I would love to do that."

Kilmeade said, "Well, the problem is most Democrats have extreme environmental restrictions, which always bring red tape... Karoline, are you surprised to hear him talk so moderate for the last few months, and now he says he wants to work with the builder Donald Trump. Do you buy it?"

Leavitt replied, "I don't buy it for one second, Brian. This is exactly what the Democrats do in every election cycle. They are incredibly disingenuous.

"In fact, it's the one thing they are good at. They talk like the radical leftists that they are until they win their Democrat nomination, and then they try to fool voters by appearing moderate so that they can win their votes.

"But when they're elected, god forbid if Talarico is elected and we pray that he isn't after polls close tonight, he will govern as a far-left extreme liberal that he truly is. Just listen to his own words, some of the things he's said in the past.

"He said he's a Christian who hates Christianity. He said god is non-binary; he said he believes there are six genders; he said that the United States southern border in the great state of Texas could be like a front porch where everybody is welcomed.

"Well, James Talarico, we had that for four years under the Biden administration, and it resulted in the worst border crisis in American history. He wants to abolish ICE; he wants to open our prisons. This is not somebody who is going to work with President Trump to secure our border and continue deporting illegal aliens.

Leavitt concluded, "This is somebody who would join the ranks of the extremely liberal Democrat Party to engage in lawfare and obstructionism of all of the common sense policies that the Trump admin is continuing to pursue, and that's exactly why the great people of Texas need to get out and vote."

She later claimed Trump is "willing to work with anyone," and that she's seen that first hand. Meanwhile, Talarico said in Houston, "This election is about whether you want a senator who puts money in your pocket or who puts money in his own."
