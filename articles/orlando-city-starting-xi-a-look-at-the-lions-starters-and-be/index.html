Orlando City Starting XI: A look at the Lions' starters and bench as they host D.C. United | Orlando City

TheTools · 18.10.2026 00:46 · source · permalink

Digital Content Manager

ORLANDO, Fla. — On the heels of a midweek draw on the road in Toronto, Canada, Orlando City SC plays host to D.C. United tonight at Inter.co Stadium.

Just two changes have been made to the starting group from the Lions' match at Toronto. Vinícius Noguiera wo was ruled out prior to the match, is replaced by Zakaria Taifi, while Daryl Dike starts ahead of Harvey Sarajian, who is on the bench.

Both Justin Ellis and Marco Pašalić are on the bench after missing the last match and will be available if needed tonight. 

Check out the rest of Orlando's lineup and bench for when the team takes the pitch at 7:30 p.m. ET on Apple TV.

Orlando City lineup: Maxime Crépeau, Zakaria Taifi, David Brekalo, Iago, Iván Angulo, Wilder Cartagena, Eduard Atuesta, Luís Otavio, Martín Ojeda, Antoine Griezmann, Daryl Dike

Substitutions: Javier Otero, Justin Ellis, Joran Gerbet, Adrián Marín, Nolan Miller, Braian Ojeda, Marco Pašalić, Harvey Sarajian, Tiago
