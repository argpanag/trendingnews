Orlando City Starting XI: A look at the Lions' starters and bench as they face Atlanta United in a midweek match on the road | Orlando City

ATLANTA, GA.— The first of two matches this week for Orlando City is set to get underway tonight as the Lions take on Atlanta United at Mercedes-Benz Stadium.

One change for the Lions as Martín Ojeda returns to start in place of Daryl Dike, who is on the bench.

Check out the rest of Orlando's lineup and bench for when the team takes the pitch at 7:30 p.m. ET on Apple TV.

Orlando City lineup: Maxime Crépeau, Griffin Dorsey, David Brekalo, Robin Jansson (c), Bernardo Rhein, Iván Angulo, Joran Gerbet, Eduard Atuesta, Justin Ellis, Antoine Griezmann, Martín Ojeda

Substitutions: Javier Otero, Wilder Cartagena, Daryl Dike, Luís Otávio, Iago, Marco Pašalić, Tyrese Spicer, Tiago, Zakaria Taifi
