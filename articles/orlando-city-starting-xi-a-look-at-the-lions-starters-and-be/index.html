Orlando City Starting XI: A look at the Lions' starters and bench as they host Toronto FC | Orlando City

TheTools · 13.09.2026 00:42 · source · permalink

Digital Content Manager

ORLANDO, Fla.— Orlando City return home to host Toronto FC, before heading on the road for back-to-back matches.

Some rotation for the starters as Zakaria Taifi, Iago, Marco Pašalić, Luís Otávio, and Tyrese Spicer all come in to start in plac of Eduard Atuesta, Justin Ellis, Robin Jansson, Bernardo Rhein and Griffin Dorsey. 

Harvey Sarajian joins the bench in place of Dorsey, who misses out due to injury.

Check out the rest of Orlando's lineup and bench for when the team takes the pitch at 7:30 p.m. ET on Apple TV.

Orlando City lineup: Maxime Crépeau, Zakaria Taifi, David Brekalo, Iago, Iván Angulo, Marco Pašalić, Joran Gerbet, Luís Otávio, Tyrese Spicer, Antoine Griezmann, Martín Ojeda (c)

Substitutions: Javier Otero, Eduard Atuesta, Wilder Cartagena, Daryl Dike, Justin Ellis, Robin Jansson, Bernardo Rhein, Harvey Sarajian, Tiago
