Egypt vs Malawi Lineups: Salah Leads Maher's First Match in Charge (13 November)

TheTools · 13.11.2026 18:59 · source · permalink

Egypt open the Ali Maher era against Malawi in a 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifier in Cairo, with Salah, Marmoush and Trezeguet in the front three and Group B tight at the top.

Egypt against Malawi in Cairo is the first match of the Ali Maher era, and it should be a win. Group B of 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying has Egypt top on goal difference only, so a slip at home to a Malawi side that lost 3-1 to Angola would be a bad way to start with a new coach. Kick-off at Cairo International Stadium is 19:00 UK time on Friday 13 November, and Mohamed Salah captains a front three as strong as Maher could have picked.

The XI Maher chose

The BBC lists Egypt in a 4-3-3: Mostafa Shobeir in goal, a back four of Mohamed Hany, Ramy Rabia, Hossam Abdelmaguid and Ahmed Fatouh, and Mohanad Lasheen, Zizo and Nasser Maher in midfield. Salah leads the line with Omar Marmoush and Trezeguet either side of him. Mohamed El Shenawy, Marwan Attia and Hamza Abdelkarim are among the substitutes.

One detail stands out. Egypt Independent’s expected lineup had Marwan Attia in midfield, and he starts on the bench instead, with Zizo coming in. That says Maher wants runners who join the front three, not a holding midfielder, and Malawi will need to be ready for it. Malawi, managed by Callisto Pasuwa, also play 4-3-3, with Adebare Babatunde, Mbulu and Mpinganjira up front. Abdelaziz Bouh is the referee.

A coach in the job for six days

The Egyptian FA ended Hossam Hassan’s tenure on Saturday 7 November, saying the board acted over “the fallout from those events and the damage done to the positive image and stability of the national team”. The statement did not say which events it meant. Hassan had taken Egypt to the round of 16 at this year’s World Cup. Maher, 52, arrived from Ceramica Cleopatra, according to Sport News Africa, and the two Malawi games are his first matches in charge.

Six days is no time to change a system, so expect the shape to look familiar and the differences to be in who runs where. Maher has said himself that time is short and that he wants a strong team playing attractive football.

Group B so far

Egypt drew 0-0 with Angola in Cairo in September despite 15 shots, then beat South Sudan 5-0 in Juba, with Marmoush scoring twice. That leaves them level on four points with Angola.

Standings before the matchday, from Wikipedia’s qualification page and Sport News Africa.

Malawi beat South Sudan 2-1 and lost 3-1 at Angola, so they come to Cairo with three points and four goals conceded. Sport News Africa notes that Egypt beat them 2-0 and then 4-0 away in March 2023.

Why the Lilongwe game counts for more

Egypt play Malawi twice within four days, and the return is in Lilongwe on Tuesday 17 November, at the Bingu National Stadium (13:00 GMT, according to Sport News Africa). Winning tonight gives Egypt seven points and makes the trip a chance to open a gap, not a scramble. A draw in Cairo and the group stays tight until Angola host Egypt on 24 March 2027.

The qualifying format is 12 groups of four with home-and-away games over six matchdays. Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania host the finals in summer 2027 and qualify automatically. Angola play South Sudan on 14 November, so the table will move again by the weekend whatever happens in Cairo.
