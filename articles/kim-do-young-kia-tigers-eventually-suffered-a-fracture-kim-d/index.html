Kim Do-young (KIA Tigers) eventually suffered a fracture.

Kim Do-young was listed in the KIA starting lineup as the third batter and third baseman in a home game against the NC Dinos in the 2026 professional baseball KBO League regular season at Gwangju-KIA Champions Field on the 9th.

Kim Do-young, who turned to a ground ball to shortstop in the bottom of the first inning, faced bad luck in the bottom of the fourth inning. With no outs and no outs, he tried to bunt on the first 148km fastball of right-hander Song Myung-ki, but was hit in the face by a foul ball.

Kim Do-young, who was complaining of severe pain, even showed bleeding and was eventually replaced by pinch hitter Park Min. When he returned to the dugout, he got angry with himself with kicking and shouting.

After that, Kim Do-young headed to the hospital, and eventually faced a fracture.

An official from KIA said, "Kim Do-young conducted X-ray and CT tests at Central Hospital, the team's designated hospital," and added, "The test found a bone fracture."

Kim Do-young, who was called by KIA for the first nomination in 2022, is a superstar who represents not only KIA but also Korean baseball. Prior to the day, he had a batting average of 0.310 (516 hits in 1,664 at-bats) with 95 homers and 301 RBIs in 479 games (on-base plus slugging percentage) of 0.952. In the 2024 season, he played 141 games with a batting average of 0.347 (189 hits in 544 at-bats), 38 homers, 40 steals, 109 RBIs, 143 runs, and OPS of 1.067, leading KIA's V12.

He also played well this year. Before the NC match, he played in 121 games and posted a batting average of 0.307 (137 hits in 446 at-bats) with 40 home runs, 99 RBIs, and 10 steals. However, he was away for the time being due to the injury.

An official from KIA said, "The treatment of the injured area will be decided after checking the doctor's opinion and condition," adding, "It does not appear to be in a very severe condition." We have to watch the situation first," he explained.

Meanwhile, Kim Do-young's injury is more painful for the Korean national baseball team, which will participate in the 2026 Aichi and Nagoya Asian Games. The national team led by head coach Ryu Ji-hyun was scheduled to convene on the 14th. However, the bad news has made Kim Do-young's participation in the tournament unclear.

[Lee Hanjoo, MK Sports Reporter]
