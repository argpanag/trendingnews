Pooja Bumper First Prize of Rs 12 Crore Goes to Palakkad for Second Consecutive Year

TheTools · 28.11.2026 09:59 · source · permalink

English

Kerala

Published on Nov 28, 2026, 03:18 PM | 1 min read

Thiruvananthapuram: The first prize of Rs 12 crore in the Kerala State Lottery's Pooja Bumper draw has gone to Palakkad for the second consecutive year. Ticket number JC234427 has won the top prize.

The winning ticket was sold by King Star Agencies in Palakkad. E S Suresh is the agent.

The second prize of Rs 50 lakh each will be awarded to 10 winners. The winning ticket numbers are JA 400634, JB 346893, JC 682550, JD 304468, JE 632364, JA 969347, JB 866718, JC 648565, JD 766007 and JE 324064.

The third prize of Rs 5 lakh each will go to 10 winners, while five winners will receive the fourth prize of Rs 3 lakh each. The fifth prize of Rs 2 lakh each will be awarded to 10 winners. Four consolation prizes of Rs 1 lakh each will also be given.

In addition, 8,100 winners will receive Rs 5,000 each, 64,800 will get Rs 1,000 each, 1,29,600 will receive Rs 500 each and 1,40,400 will win Rs 300 each.

The ticket price was Rs 300. A total of 45 lakh tickets were printed across five series — JA, JB, JC, JD and JE. All tickets were sold out.
