    Newcastle v Hull (15:00 BST)

    Fourth-placed Hull City are looking to become the first promoted Premier League side to avoid defeat in their opening five games since Portsmouth in 2003-04.

    They appear to be thriving from continuity, with the core of the team which earned promotion are still entrusted with implementing the approach which brought success last term despite 18 summer signings.

    14:48 BST

    Newcastle v Hull (15:00 BST)

    Newcastle are seeking a first home league win of the season against Hull City, the only team to play at St James' Park more than once in the Premier League and never lose (W2, D1).

    This season, the Magpies have faced the most shots in the division, while only three teams should have conceded more goals based on the quality of chances against them.

    In attack, Newcastle have had the joint-second fewest shots but scored seven times from an expected goals tally of 3.9 - suggesting an over-performance in front of goal that will be hard to sustain.

  9. 'Villa fans deserve everything'published at 14:45 BST

    FT: Tottenham 2-3 Aston Villa

    Aston Villa's Emiliano Buendia speaking to TNT Sports, first on his goal: "It was my first thought when I saw everyone running into the box, I just tried to get to the edge of the box free and not think about it."

  11. Rock-solid Gunnerspublished at 14:43 BST
    14:43 BST

    Brighton v Arsenal (15:00 BST)

    Arsenal are seeking a 10th consecutive league victory, a feat they have not achieved since 2002. Should they manage that, it would leave them four short of the club record set in 1998.

    Arsenal have conceded just twice in their previous nine league games, despite the absence of key centre-back William Saliba.

    They have conceded only once in the league in 2026-27, while their totals for shots faced, shots on target faced and expected goals against are all the lowest in the division.

  12. Top-scoring Seagullspublished at 14:42 BST
    14:42 BST

    Brighton v Arsenal (15:00 BST)

    Brighton's 5-0 victory at Coventry City equalled their biggest Premier League away win and took their tally to 13 goals from their opening four matches, a club record at this stage of a top-flight season.

    The result also made them the league's top scorers after four matchrounds. That is no fluke as the Seagulls are the only team to have accumulated an expected goals total of 10 or more.

    But they have also made the most of their chances, scoring almost three goals more than Opta's xG model would predict.

  13. 'Arsenal could expose Brighton'published at 14:41 BST
    14:41 BST

    Brighton v Arsenal (15:00 BST)

    Chris Sutton
    BBC Sport columnist

    There might be a bit of an edge to this one after Brighton boss Fabian Hurzeler had a pop at Arsenal over their time-wasting when they beat the Seagulls near the end of last season.

    I love the way Brighton play. It doesn't seem to matter who they are missing from their team, they are just bold as brass with the way they press and get men forward.

    Arsenal are the real deal and I don't see any weaknesses in their team, as their impressive win at Sunderland underlined.

    They are brave going forward, but they are also streetwise defensively and can see out a game when they have to, time-wasting or not.

    They have that steel about them but I also think they could expose Brighton if Hurzeler's side go toe-to-toe with them, which is what I am expecting.

    Sutton's prediction: 1-2
