SpaceX Starship orbits Earth for first time | NHK WORLD-JAPAN News

TheTools · 06.10.2026 00:41 · source · permalink

SpaceX has successfully launched a large spacecraft into orbit. The company, led by Elon Musk, is trying to develop a lunar lander that will carry astronauts to the moon under the US space agency NASA's international lunar exploration program.

The Artemis program aims to send astronauts to the moon's surface in 2028, and commercial enterprises are developing lunar landers. SpaceX has been developing its Starship launch system for this purpose.

This time unmanned, the Starship vessel lifted off from a launch pad in the US state of Texas on Monday in its first attempt at an orbital flight.

The spacecraft is about 120 meters tall, including a large booster rocket. During ascent, one of the vessel's engines shut down. The flight, which was originally scheduled to last 10 hours, was reduced to three hours due to the engine problem. This remains a challenge to be solved for the company's future launches.

SpaceX and Blue Origin, led by Amazon founder Jeff Bezos, are trying to develop lunar landers for the Artemis program. But experts point out delays in their development schedules and technical challenges.
