Montreal weather: The sun does not get to take Labour Day off

TheTools · 07.09.2026 11:39 · source · permalink

Workers of the world unite, perhaps outdoors, as it’s going to be a lovely Labour Day in Montreal.

Environment Canada is forecasting a sunny Monday with a high of 26 C. Factoring in the humidex, it will feel more like 28.

Overnight, as the holiday comes to an end, the skies will become cloudier, the low will drop to 18 C. and there will be a 40 per cent chance of showers.
