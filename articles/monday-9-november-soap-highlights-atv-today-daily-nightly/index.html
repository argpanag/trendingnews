Monday 9 November: Soap Highlights - ATV Today | Daily Nightly

TheTools · 09.11.2026 00:04 · source · permalink

Pick of the Plots

Coronation Street’s Debbie’s Angels face an evening of revelations

Debbie brings a group of familiar faces together for a special birthday surprise in Coronation Street, but old tensions threaten to derail the evening.

Meanwhile, Linda seeks answers in EastEnders, Caleb’s health takes a worrying turn in Hollyoaks and Cain’s actions have devastating consequences in Emmerdale.

ITV1 / STV, 8.30pm stream on ITVX/STV Player or YouTube

In a special episode focusing on Debbie’s Angels, Debbie invites Abi, Bernie, Christina, Sally and Glenda to join her for an evening with a difference. She has a surprise in mind for Carl’s 40th birthday and needs their help to put it together.

Emptying a plastic crate onto the carpet, Debbie reveals hundreds of photographs collected over the years. Her plan is to turn them into a special photo book for Carl, but what begins as a fun opportunity for the women to reminisce and bond soon descends into chaos.

As they sift through the memories, old tensions and unresolved issues begin to surface. Heated confrontations bring family history and past traumas back into the open, threatening to overshadow the birthday preparations.

Yet, amid the arguments, there are also moments of genuine connection. Some of the women find comfort in confiding in one another about the personal burdens they have been carrying, while others are forced to confront difficult decisions about what comes next.

By the end of the evening, the air has been cleared and the group manages to come together, determined to move forward. However, although the women leave on better terms, several are still facing significant, life-changing choices of their own.

5 Star at 6.30pm, stream on 5

Remi opens up to Tane about how difficult he is finding it to be excluded from Eden’s inner circle before heading home. Sonny tries to lift his spirits, but only manages to push him further away. Remi reminds him that Eden is close to being on her deathbed, while Sonny seems more concerned with having fun.

At the hospital, Eden and Cash spend time reminiscing about their life together. Grateful for what they have shared, they dream of simply having another ordinary day together. But their quiet moment takes a sad turn when Cash reveals that Melanie has died. They share their grief, mourning Melanie herself as well as the loss of the hope they had been holding on to.

Elsewhere, Tane confides in Mackenzie about how much he is struggling with being separated from his son and missing out on precious time with him. Mackenzie suggests that, if he wants greater flexibility, she could hire a manager for the gym, allowing Tane to make much longer visits – even staying for weeks at a time. Tane likes the idea, although it also sparks a separate thought of his own.

E4 at 7pm, stream on Channel 4

Frankie finds an excuse to spend time with her ex, Caleb. With unresolved feelings still lingering between them and Caleb remaining optimistic about his recovery, could there be a chance of a reconciliation?

Meanwhile, Beth is devastated when Maddox admits that he is planning to travel to Switzerland to meet his biological family. He reluctantly reveals that Caleb is going with him, prompting Beth to confront Caleb over encouraging her son to go.

As tensions within the family escalate, Caleb suddenly suffers a seizure.

At the hospital, Frankie, who is still on work experience, is horrified to see Caleb being rushed in. Jonah puts on a show of concern but tries to keep Frankie away from his son. Determined to find out what is wrong, Frankie secretly breaks the rules and looks at Caleb’s test results, where she discovers a suspicious drug in his system.

Elsewhere, Vicky is due to be discharged from hospital. Back at home, Prince declares his love for her and reminds her why getting better is so important.

Misbah continues to threaten Cindy into keeping quiet, while also asking Donny to start protecting Imran.

BBC One 7.30pm stream on iPlayer

Linda is stunned to discover that Lee spent the night on Sean’s floor after drinking too much. Before she has a chance to give him a telling-off, she answers a call on his phone and is left shocked by what she hears.

Seeking support, Linda turns to Elaine and Sharon before later confiding in Max. However, the couple’s conversation is interrupted when Lee causes a drunken scene in The Vic, forcing Linda to step in.

Realising that she needs answers, Linda sets off to uncover the truth and heads straight to confront Janine.

Elsewhere, Sharon and Eve arrange a play date with Ria and Albie in The Vic. Lauren and Mark continue to struggle with their guilt following Ryder’s attack, while Ryder grows increasingly close to Ian. The pair later head to the Arches to spend some time alone, but their privacy is short-lived.

ITV1 / STV, 8pm, stream on ITVX/STV Player or YouTube

Joe continues to use Caleb’s affair against him. Caleb insists that Ruby would never believe Joe if he revealed the truth, but Joe points out that she would believe Sadie. Knowing that Sadie still has feelings for Caleb, Joe is confident he can use her to his advantage.

Meanwhile, Sam, Belle and Kammy are busy preparing for Samson’s return when Charles arrives with alarming news: Tom has been beaten up and is in a critical condition. Belle is furious when Cain admits that he organised the attack.

The situation takes another turn when Sam receives a call informing him that Samson has assaulted someone and will not be coming home after all. The penny drops for everyone as they realise Cain arranged for Samson to attack Tom.

Sam is appalled, accusing Cain of not caring about his son and suggesting that he and Moira have never forgiven Samson for what he did to Matty. Their argument quickly spirals out of control, and Sam launches himself at Cain. Cain storms off, leaving Sam in tears.

Elsewhere, Nicola is taken aback when Laurel suggests that Kev might have feelings for her. Laurel remains suspicious, but Nicola dismisses the idea.

Kev, meanwhile, gives Lewis his own account of events, and it becomes clear that he also believes there could be something between him and Nicola. Lewis is left concerned by the possibility.

At the Smoke House, Robert reluctantly agrees to give Kev a chance to cook some steaks. Impressed with the results, Robert offers him an opportunity to prove himself in the kitchen, despite Aaron’s objections. Lewis is proud of Kev, although Kev is saddened at the prospect of having to break the news to Nicola.

Later, Nicola welcomes a rather nervous-looking Kev, and the pair share a joke about their previous evening together. Their mood changes, however, when Kev explains that he is leaving the café. Nicola suggests they have one last cup of tea, but Kev turns her down, leaving both of them feeling a little heartbroken.
