    For now, Liverpool will have to fend without Isak, Gakpo and Szoboszlai as they prepare to take on LASK in Austria in the Champions League on Wednesday night.

    You will be able to follow live text updates and radio commentary of that match on the BBC Sport website.

  3. Barcola needs more time - Kerkerzpublished at 16:52 BST 13 October
    16:52 BST 13 October

    LASK v Liverpool (Wed, 20:00 BST)

    Liverpool

    That's it from Andoni Iraola, who is now off to hold an open training session with his Liverpool team in Austria before Wednesday's game in Linz against LASK.

    Defender Milos Kerkez has also been speaking about Bradley Barcola, here is what he had to say in full:

    "He arrived, played a few games, needs a bit more time, so does everyone who arrives at a club like this.

    "Yeah he's a real talent, he has great skills, he can help us a lot scoring goals going forward, you know.

    "It's just the thing is, we should talk more and build that connection more that's going to help us get better combinations down the line, I think we are on a good way and I hope it will get better.

    "You have to help new players off the pitch and just in general get to know him better and talk outside of the pitch, obviously on the pitch we have to communicate a lot."

  4. 'More Barcola plays the better he will become'published at 16:50 BST 13 October
    16:50 BST 13 October

    LASK v Liverpool (Wed, 20:00 BST)

    Liverpool

    Andoni Iraola on freshness and not being able to fit in too many training sessions: "We don't have a lot of time to train, we have to prepare with some videos, tactics and the ball outside a little, but we have to prioritise the freshness of the players.

    "And especially playing four games in 10 days, we want to keep the legs in the game, but I think in cases like Bradley [Barcola] especially, the last one to arrive, the more he plays, the more connections with those around him and the better he'll become, yes."

  5. 'Hard to have same starting XI'published at 16:48 BST 13 October
    16:48 BST 13 October

    LASK v Liverpool (Wed, 17:45 BST)

    Liverpool

    Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola on whether other players will step up in the absence of Alexander Isak, Cody Gakpo and Dominik Szoboszlai, following Sunday's 3-0 defeat at home to Manchester City:

    "We will see. It's a matter of us because we lost at the end, we are now trying to find why. But I think I liked the team a lot the other day. Not when we were with 10. You should play much better at 10, but before that I liked the spirit, work-rate, attitude. I liked the offensive players, yes."

    On changing the team for the Champions League: "It is a game that we want to win and we want to start this competition, we have the first win and we want the second one.

    "It is always difficult to win in this competition because there are some niggles, so it will be hard to have the same starting 11 as last time."

    16:40 BST 13 October

    LASK v Liverpool (Wed, 17:45 BST)

    Liverpool

    Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola on missing Alexander Isak, Cody Gakpo and Dominik Szoboszlai and hoping players like Florian Wirtz and Bradley Barcola can step up: "I think that is true, Cody, Alex and Dom are top scorers this season because they play more this season and now we don't have them, so we need the other players to give us those numbers.

    "The injuries are because of the national teams and some were injuries from the last game.
