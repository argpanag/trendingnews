Best NBA Bets Tonight for Friday, October 23

CJ McCollum Over 16.5 Points (-110)

FTN projects McCollum for 20.88 points, 4.38 above his 16.5 line. The model gives the over a 16.29% edge at -110, the largest edge on this three-play card.

McCollum scored 19 points and made four 3-pointers in Atlanta’s 132-125 opening win at Orlando. The Hawks had four players score at least 19, and McCollum still cleared this number in a balanced offense.

Charlotte held Brooklyn to 103 points in its opener. This bet is driven by the 4.38-point gap between FTN’s projection and the posted line, not an assumption that Atlanta repeats its 132-point opener.

Anthony Davis Under 22.5 Points (-118)

FTN projects Davis for 17.68 points, 4.82 below his 22.5 line. The under shows a 16.26% edge at -118.

Davis scored 34 points against Milwaukee on opening night. He took 20 shots and attempted 14 free throws, so another heavy scoring workload is the main risk to the under.

Toronto opened the season by holding Chicago to 92 points and forcing 27 turnovers. Washington will also be without AJ Dybantsa That absence can protect Davis’ usage, but FTN still leaves a 4.82-point cushion at 22.5. A move to 21.5 would materially weaken the bet.

Naz Reid Over 7.5 Rebounds (+108)

FTN projects Reid for 9.30 rebounds, 1.80 above his 7.5 line. The over carries a 15.98% edge and is the only plus-money bet on the card at +108.

Reid grabbed 11 rebounds in Charlotte’s 110-103 opening win at Brooklyn. He added 20 points and seven assists in his regular-season debut with the Hornets.

Grant Williams remains out for Charlotte, while Reid has no injury designation. Reid’s 11-rebound opener supports FTN’s 9.30 projection.
