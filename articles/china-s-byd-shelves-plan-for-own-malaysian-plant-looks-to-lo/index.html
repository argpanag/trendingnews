China's BYD shelves plan for own Malaysian plant, looks to local partner

TheTools · 12.09.2026 04:29 · source · permalink

EV maker says talks with domestic player over assembly are at an 'advanced stage'

BYD did not disclose the exact reason for abandoning plans to build its own assembly plant in Malaysia.  © Reuters

KUALA LUMPUR -- Chinese auto giant BYD has shelved plans to set up its own assembly plant in Malaysia and is instead in "advanced talks" with a local partner to meet its production aims in the country.
