Everything you need to know: Pitt vs UCF gameday

TheTools · 12.09.2026 14:29 · source · permalink

PITTSBURGH — This article originally appeared on PittsburghSportsNOW

After making easy work of Miami (Ohio) in its season opener, Pitt will welcome in a Power Four opponent, UCF, on Saturday afternoon. Here’s everything you need to know about the matchup.

PITT PANTHERS vs UCF KNIGHTS GAMEDAY INFO

Pitt Panthers (1-0) vs. UCF Knights (1-0)

Saturday, Sept. 12, 2026, 3:30 p.m. at Acrisure Stadium

BETTING LINES

Latest CFB lines: Pitt -7.5, over/under 56.5, UCF +215

THE LAST TIME

The Knights walked into Acrisure Stadium, they were ranked No. 15 in the country, and Kenny Pickett and the Panthers raced out to a 21-0 lead. Bridging the second and third quarters, UCF scored 31 straight points.

Pickett’s 3-yard touchdown reception from receiver Aaron Matthews with 59 seconds left gave Pitt the final say, however, in a 35-34 triumph that snapped the Knights’ 27-game non-bowl winning streak.
