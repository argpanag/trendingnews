Zii Jia set for decade-old rematch with Merkle

TheTools · 27.11.2026 00:00 · source · permalink
    bookmark

PETALING JAYA: A decade after Lee Zii Jia beat Arnaud Merkle in the Dutch Junior final, the two will meet again with a place in the Syed Modi International semi-finals at stake.

The Malaysians and Frenchman will go head-to-head at the Babu Banarasi Das Uttar Pradesh Badminton Academy in Lucknow today, with Zii Jia hoping to repeat his victory over Merkle in Haarlem.

Zii Jia booked his place in the quarter-finals with a 21-11, 21-18 win over India’s Pranay Shettigar yesterday, while Merkle had to work harder to overcome another home player, Ginpaul Sonna.

The Frenchman eventually prevailed 21-5, 9-21, 21-5 after a second game stumble. Zii Jia was pleased to reach the quarter-finals but admitted there was still room for improvement.

“Into the quarters here in Lucknow after a win against Pranay, a first meet between us, happy but always room to improve,” he said in a social media post after the match.

Zii Jia will be hoping to make the most of his quarter-final opportunity as he looks to regain his confidence and go as far as possible in the tournament after an injury-hit 2025 season.

The Malaysian last won a title at the Australian Open in 2024, and will be eager to get back to winning ways.

Merkle, meanwhile, will be looking to put his disappointment from four years ago behind him after missing out on the Syed Modi International title in 2022.

The Frenchman was due to face compatriot Lucas Claerbout in the final but was forced to withdraw after contracting Covid-19. However, fellow Malaysian Eogene Ewe suffered a disappointing exit after losing 5-21, 14-21 to South Korea’s Jeon Hyeok-jin.
