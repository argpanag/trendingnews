TheTools · 28.11.2026 04:36 · source · permalink

HONOLULU (HawaiiNewsNow) - Happy Aloha Friday - Black Friday! Winds will veer towards standard ENE trade wind direction by tomorrow.

Winds have begun to veer today,and are expected to reach a typical ENE trade wind direction with moderate speeds by tomorrow. As the winds veer, the cold air advection will decrease, causing humidity and temperatures to slightly increase by Sunday onward.

An upper-level trough and associated surface trough are expected to develop just west of the state late Sunday into Tuesday, bringing a higher likelihood of showers.
