Hugh Jackman's New "Ridley Scott Meets Logan" R-Rated Action Thriller Is Already the #1 Movie on Streaming

TheTools · 18.10.2026 21:01 · source · permalink

Despite bombing at the box office, Hugh Jackman's new R-rated action thriller is officially the number one movie on streaming after a single day. After years of playing Wolverine in the X-Men franchise, Hugh Jackman capped off that run (before Deadpool & Wolverine undermined it all) with James Mangold's Logan. The film was unlike any other superhero movie before it, placing the hero in a gritty, neo-western road trip movie to save the new generation of mutants. It was dark, R-rated, and intensely violent, and easily better than anything the MCU has pumped out. Earlier this year, Hugh Jackman gave another beloved character the Logan treatment, and it just landed on streaming.

After a disastrous box office run, Hugh Jackman's The Death of Robin Hood is now officially on streaming. Written and directed by Michael Sarnoski, the film takes the iconic English hero (whether he was real or fictitious, we'll leave up to the internet historians in the comments) and places him in an incredibly gritty and violent world as he fights for redemption and salvation. Hugh Jackman stars as Robin Hood, who feels like a spiritual successor to the version found in Ridley Scott's 2010 version with Russell Crowe, with a supporting cast that features Jodie Comer, Bill Skarsgard, Noah Jupe, and Murray Bartlett.

'The Death of Robin Hood' Is Now a Streaming Sensation

To call The Death of Robin Hood a flop would be an understatement. It grossed just $9.9 million worldwide against a small $20 million budget. However, it has swiftly found redemption after being added to streaming. The Death of Robin Hood is now streaming on HBO Max. After a single day on the platform, it is already the number one movie in the US. The Housemaid has been pushed down to second, with Backrooms in third and Coraline in fourth.

'The Death of Robin Hood' Divided Critics & Viewers

It might be the number one movie on HBO Max, but that's not to say that The Death of Robin Hood was up everyone's street. Despite its prestigious appearance, it hardly earned prestige reviews, as it holds a 69% Rotten Tomatoes score from 189 reviews. Things are looking even worse on the Popcornmeter. Only 500 verified user reviews have been submitted to Rotten Tomatoes so far, but from those The Death of Robin Hood only has a 56% user score.

The critics' reviews are somewhat confusing. The marketing made it obvious that this is a hyper-violent and relentlessly dark and grim story. Yet, the Critics' Consensus on RT has noted that several critics complained about the lack of levity in the story compared to past adaptations. "Swapping out derring-do for ultraviolence, this grim take on the merry legend of Robin Hood could use more levity in its quiver, but its aim of exploring the complicated humanity behind a folk hero nails the target," the full Critics' Consensus reads.
