The 78th Emmy Awards will be hosted by Mariska Hargitay live from Los Angeles on NBC and Peacock on Sept. 14.

The 2026 Emmy Awards air Monday night on NBC, with the stars of TV's most acclaimed shows competing for the industry's top honors.

The 78th Emmy Awards will be broadcast live on NBC and stream live on Peacock from the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles on Monday night, starting at 8 p.m. ET.

The Pitt led the way with 25 nominations, followed by Hacks with 24 nods — a record for a comedy series — and newcomers Widow's Bay (19), Pluribus (18), Beef (16) and DTF St. Louis (13).

Jason Bateman, star of DTF St. Louis, received two nominations for the HBO dark comedy (one as a performer, one as a producer) and two for the Netflix drama Black Rabbit, in which he starred and directed.

Other stars with multiple nominations included Matthew Rhys (two for Widow's Bay as a performer and producer, one for The Beast in Me as a performer), Quinta Brunson (three for performing, writing and producing on Abbott Elementary) and Paul W. Downs (performing, writing and producing on Hacks).

Rhys won an Emmy in 2018 for his acting work in The Americans. His wife and Americans costar Keri Russell is still looking for her first — and she's up again for The Diplomat. Carey Mulligan, who has been nominated for three Academy Awards, earned her first-ever Emmy nods — two for acting in and producing Beef.

Sisters Elle and Dakota Fanning were each nominated for two Emmy Awards. Elle was recognized as both a performer and producer for Margo's Got Money Troubles, while Dakota earned an acting nomination for All Her Fault and shared a producer nomination for Margo's Got Money Troubles.

HBO Max led all networks with 122 nominations, followed by Netflix (111) and Apple TV (87). ABC topped the broadcast networks with 40.

Winners so far

More than 100 Emmy Awards have already been handed out. The Television Academy presented the 2026 Creative Arts Emmys during a two-night gala in Los Angeles on Sept. 5 and Sept. 6.

Widow's Bay grabbed eight, followed by Bad Bunny's Apple Music Super Bowl LX Halftime Show with seven and DTF St. Louis and The Traitors with six apiece.

The Late Show With Stephen Colbert received five in its final season and is up for another for Outstanding Variety Series.

The late actor and director Rob Reiner received a posthumous Emmy for his guest-starring role on The Bear.

The 78th Emmy Awards will be broadcast live on NBC and stream live on Peacock from the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles on Monday night, starting at 8 p.m. ET.

Mariska Hargitay, the Emmy-winning star of Law & Order: Special Victims Unit, will host the show — the first woman to do so in 15 years. My Mom Jayne, Hargitay's HBO documentary about her mother, Jayne Mansfield, received three nominations and won two.
