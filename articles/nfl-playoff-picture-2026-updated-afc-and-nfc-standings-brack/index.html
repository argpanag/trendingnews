NFL Playoff Picture 2026: Updated AFC and NFC standings, bracket, tiebreakers for Week 12

TheTools · 25.11.2026 16:01 · source · permalink

The five-game football feast surrounding Thanksgiving has major playoff implications for all 10 teams playing around the holiday. We look at the playoff race for Week 12 below:

DIVISION LEADERS

1. Bills (9-1): Will have the inside track for the No. 1 seed if they win on Thanksgiving night against the Chiefs.

2. Broncos (8-2): Currently ahead of the Chiefs in the AFC West based on the better record in common games

3. Jaguars (7-3): Loss to the Giants was a big blow to their hopes of earning the No. 1 seed in the AFC.

4. Bengals (7-3): In control of the AFC North, but Monday night’s loss in Washington hurts.

WILD CARDS

5. Chiefs (8-2): Huge game coming against the Bills on Thanksgiving night.

6. Browns (6-4): Cleveland’s path to the playoffs is looking surprisingly easy.

7. Steelers (6-4): Major questions about the direction of this team after benching Aaron Rodgers.

OUTSIDE LOOKING IN

8. Patriots (5-5): Hard to see them competing without Drake Maye.

9. Texans (4-6): Running out of time to go on a run.

10 Raiders (4-7): The rest of the season is about developing Fernando Mendoza.

11. Ravens (4-7): Lamar Jackson’s injury just about killed their playoff hopes.

12. Jets (3-8): Aaron Glenn is on the hot seat.

13. Colts (3-8): No longer competing.

14. Chargers (1-9): The NFL’s most disappointing team.

15. Dolphins (1-9): The playoffs this year were never a realistic possibility.

16. Titans (1-9): Need to figure out if Cam Ward is their quarterback.

DIVISION LEADERS

1. 49ers (9-1): Inside track for the No. 1 seed after a big win Sunday night against the Vikings.

2. Lions (8-2): First place in the NFC North is on the line in the Thanksgiving showdown with the Bears.

3. Giants (6-3-1): Clear path to the playoffs in John Harbaugh’s first season.

4. Panthers (5-5): Best record in the NFL’s worst division.

WILD CARDS

5. Bears (8-2): They’ll lead the division if they beat the Lions on Thanksgiving, but if they lose they’ll be 0-3 in the NFC North.

6. Vikings (7-3): Have lost three of their last four after a hot start.

7. Packers (6-4): Big game on Thanksgiving Eve against the Rams.

OUTSIDE LOOKING IN

8. Seahawks (6-4): Currently have the tiebreaker edge over the Rams, but those two teams still play each other twice.

9. Rams (6-4): Big game on Thanksgiving Eve against the Packers.

10. Cowboys (5-6): It won’t be easy to catch the Giants in the NFC East.

11. Commanders (4-5-1): May need to run the table to win the division.

12. Buccaneers (3-7): Still in the race in the terrible NFC South.

13. Falcons (3-7): Still in the race in the terrible NFC South.

14. Saints (3-7): Still in the race in the terrible NFC South.

15. Eagles (3-7): Showed some fight in upsetting the Steelers.

16. Cardinals (3-8): Worst team in the NFC.
