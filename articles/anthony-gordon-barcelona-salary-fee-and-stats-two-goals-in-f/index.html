Anthony Gordon Barcelona Salary, Fee and Stats: Two Goals in Four Days

TheTools · 13.10.2026 21:04 · source · permalink

Anthony Gordon scored again for Barcelona at Galatasaray, his second goal in four days. Here's what Barcelona paid Newcastle, what he reportedly earns and his stats so far.

Anthony Gordon has scored twice for Barcelona in four days. His goal in Istanbul on Tuesday night, a finish from Lamine Yamal’s pass on 53 minutes, put Flick’s side 3-1 up at Galatasaray after they had gone a goal down, and Barcelona went on to win. On Saturday he scored two minutes into the 3-0 win over Getafe. If you’re searching for Anthony Gordon’s Barcelona salary, transfer fee and stats, here’s what he cost, what he’s reportedly earning and what he’s done since leaving Newcastle.

Anthony Gordon’s transfer fee

Barcelona completed the deal on 29 May, and Sky Sports News reported the fee as £69.3m (€80m) including add-ons. He signed a five-year contract. Newcastle sold their top scorer: Gordon got 17 goals in all competitions in 2025/26, 10 of them in the Champions League, though his Premier League return was much lower. He was an unused substitute in Newcastle’s last four games before the move.

“As soon as I knew Barca was a serious option, there was never any question,” Gordon said when the deal went through.

Anthony Gordon’s salary at Barcelona

Barcelona haven’t published his wages. The Guardian reported he would earn around £300,000 a week, which Sports Illustrated put at roughly double his Newcastle pay. Salary trackers have him lower than that, so treat every figure as an estimate. On the reported numbers, he’d sit level with Raphinha and behind only Yamal in the squad.

Anthony Gordon’s Barcelona stats so far

He set up two goals on his debut, the 5-0 win at Elche on 23 August, and then waited until October for his first goal. Since then it has come quickly. The opener against Getafe arrived inside two minutes, and Tuesday’s finish in Istanbul came three minutes after Fermín López had put Barcelona in front. He’d already wasted a good chance after a move involving Fermín and Yamal, and he was taken off on 58 minutes, with Gabriel Jesus and Karim Adeyemi coming on.

Hansi Flick wasn’t worried during the dry spell. “He is doing great. He was also doing well in the previous matches when he did not score,” the Barcelona coach said after the Getafe game, as quoted by Sky Sports. “He gives us a lot. He has speed, moves well with the ball, all the dribbling he has.”

He arrived off a big summer, too. Gordon scored England’s opener in the World Cup semi-final defeat by Argentina and has 28 caps and seven goals.

Should he keep his place when Raphinha is fit?

Raphinha missed the trip to Turkey, and that’s the selection call coming for Flick. We’d keep Gordon on the left. His pace in behind stretches teams on the opposite side to Yamal, and two goals in two games is a hard run to drop. Raphinha is the bigger name and the better finisher, but he can play on either flank or behind the striker, and Flick has the depth to rotate. The first big test of that call is the Clásico later this month.
