Last season’s victory over La Liga outfit Rayo Vallecano was one of the greatest nights in Crystal Palace’s history, but manager Oliver Glasner has now gone and the Eagles have struggled in the first weeks of the new campaign under Pierre Sage.

That victory in Leipzig enabled them to qualify for this season’s Europa League, but they face a tough early examination against Lech Poznan, who won the Polish Ekstraklasa by four points last season and suffered their first defeat in 10 matches at the weekend.

Team news

Palace striker Eddie Nketiah faces a fitness test but the Eagles are set to be without Ismaila Sarr, Dean Henderson, Jean-Philippe Mateta and Chadi Raid.

Centre back Alex Douglas, defensive midfielder Antoni Kozubal and winger Ali Gholizadeh are all set to miss out for the visitors.
