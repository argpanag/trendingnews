Kelsey Grammer's Beast will return in Avengers: Doomsday, and fans now get a closer look at the character's new outfit. Hot Toys has unveiled a new 1/6th scale Beast Collectible Figure, based on the X-Men character's appearance in the upcoming film. Grammer will reprise the role in the upcoming crossover project, following his appearances in X-Men: The Last Stand and The Marvels.

Photo Credit – Hot Toys

Hot Toys' Beast figure brings Kelsey Grammer's X-Men character to life in detail

The new Beast collectible figure features a refined head sculpt with rolling eyeballs. The figure depicts Beast shirtless, wearing a yellow belt featuring the X-Men logo. The getup is different from Grammer's last appearance, when his character was in a lab coat in The Marvels.

The figure stands roughly 32.5 cm tall and wears dark blue pants with yellow accents. It features fiber-material hair across its torso and limbs to recreate Beast's appearance. The figure also comes with interchangeable hands, including pairs of fists, claw gestures, and grabbing gestures. In total, the figure includes 30 articulation points and comes with a specially designed stand with the Avengers: Doomsday logo and character nameplate.

Photo Credit – Hot Toys

Fans will have to wait a while to get their hands on it. Hot Toys has announced that the Beast Collectible Figure will be released sometime between the last two quarters of 2027, placing its launch near the release of Avengers: Secret Wars.

Beast has yet to be shown in the marketing for Avengers: Doomsday. Although Kelsey Grammer recently revealed that he doesn't know whether the character will return in Avengers: Secret Wars, the actor has praised Marvel Studios, saying he was delighted to be invited back to play the role.

Avengers: Doomsday releases on December 18, 2026. As of now, Avengers: Secret Wars is scheduled for release on December 17, 2027.
