EPD ask for help finding missing teens

TheTools · 25.09.2026 22:23 · source · permalink

EVANSVILLE, Ind. (WFIE) - The Evansville Police Department are asking for the public’s help in finding two missing teenagers.

Officials say 16-year-old Katelyn Smith and 15-year-old Paige Belcher were last seen leaving Hillcrest Youth Home Tuesday, September 22.

Katelyn is around 5′1, 100lbs with blonde hair and blue eyes, last seen wearing a black long sleeve shirt and black leggings.

Paige is around 5′3, 100ls with brown hair and green eyes, last seen wearing a purple jacket and black leggings.

If you have information on either teen or their locations, you are asked to call the Juvenile Investigation Unit at (812) 436-7905.
