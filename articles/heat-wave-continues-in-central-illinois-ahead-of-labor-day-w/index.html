Heat wave continues in Central Illinois ahead of Labor Day weekend - IPM Newsroom

TheTools · 03.09.2026 16:48 · source · permalink
  • By IPM News
  • September 3, 2026
  • Photo: IPM News

 

Extreme heat in Central and Southern Illinois will continue until Friday evening. The National Weather Service said conditions are dangerously hot, and that it could feel like 105 degrees. This means heat related illnesses are more likely. NWS recommends residents to:
-Drink plenty of fluids, stay in an air-conditioned room

-Stay out of the sun

-Check up on relatives and neighbors

– Wear lightweight and loose-fitting clothing.

– Take action when you see symptoms of heat exhaustion and heat stroke

– Never leave pets or children unattended in parked vehicles

 

Labor Day weekend will cool down a little bit with a slight dip into the 80’s. Showers are a possibility this afternoon, evening and Friday night into Saturday.
