LdC: Dembélé’s perfect cross for Ferran Torres

TheTools · 09.09.2026 20:01 · source · permalink

PSG walks on its lawn against Slovan Bratislava of Yaya Touré. Already 3-0 after a good half hour of play. After a double from Dembélé, it was Ferran Torres who worsened the score, on a service from Dembélé.

The two men kept switching, and it was from the right that Dembélé delivered a perfect cross for Ferran, who touched the leather with the tip of his foot to deceive the opposing goalkeeper.
