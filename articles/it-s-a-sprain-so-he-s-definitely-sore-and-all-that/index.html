The Cavs seem to be breathing a sigh of relief when it comes to Evan Mobley’s injury. Ruled out for Friday night’s matchup with the Heat because of a left great toe sprain suffered in the final minutes of Wednesday’s 115-107 win over New York, Mobley underwent an MRI that came back “clean.” “It’s a sprain, so he’s definitely sore and all that,” Cavs coach Kenny Atkinson said Friday night. “But we don’t expect it to be a long-term thing at all.”
