Predicted line-ups for Tottenham Hotspur v Coventry City

TheTools · 19.10.2026 19:13 · source · permalink

See which players we think will start for their clubs on Monday

Football writer Ben Bloom has predicted how Tottenham Hotspur and Coventry City will line up in the final game of Matchweek 7.

See: Injury updates for every club and player
See: Who's suspended or close to a ban?

 

Spurs: Roberto De Zerbi's pre-match press conference was on Sunday morning, following Spurs’ training camp in Spain. The Spurs boss said that Savio (muscle) and Lucas Bergvall (knee) will miss the match with injuries, the former set to be out for "some weeks". Rodrigo Bentancur is also out through suspension. James Maddison could make his first Premier League start since April 2025, while Mateus Fernandes is another option. Pedro Porro is not yet fit to return after a thigh injury, but could return next week. De Zerbi did reveal that Dejan Kulusevski will make the matchday squad for the first time in over 500 days to go on the bench. Omar Marmoush and Conor Gallagher offer alternative attacking options.

Coventry: Frank Lampard deployed a back four against Newcastle and was treated to a glorious 4-1 triumph. He may well stick with the same shape here, or else revert to the three-man defence that has been evident in most away games. A change would likely see Stephen Mfuni come in, with one of the front four making way. Ephron Mason-Clark could be the man to miss out, while Lampard must decide whether to keep faith in young striker Sidiki Cherif or recall Taiwo Awoniyi who is available after suspension. Frank Onyeka and Caleb Yirenkyi continue to fight for one spot.
