Canadiens aim to rediscover win column on first Molson FANatic Saturday of the season

MONTREAL – The Canadiens welcome the Detroit Red Wings to the Bell Centre on Saturday.

Here’s what you need to know heading into the game:

GAME FACTS

WHAT’S HAPPENING AT THE BELL CENTRE

Pregame party 

Game night gets underway early in Canadiens Plaza outside the arena on Saturday. 

From 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m., fans can enjoy: 

  • Live music courtesy of Monster Energy guest DJ Nick Robidoux
  • Convenient shopping at a Tricolore Sports pop-up team store

Molson FANatic Saturdays 

Saturday games at the Bell Centre feature a rotating selection of programming thanks to Molson FANatic Saturdays. Here's what else is in store tonight: 

  • Former Canadiens players Mathieu Dandenault and David Desharnais will meet fans in Section 117 from 5:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m.
  • The Monster Energy DJ Series returns with Fafa Khan providing pregame and intermission tunes in bowl 
  • Local artist Mathieu Dionne will create a painting live in M2 Marché Montréal Loto-Québec as part of the Artist Series presented by Loto-Québec. To enter for a chance to win the unique artwork, click here  
  • One lucky fan will get to shoot three pucks for a chance to win three incredible prizes during second intermission

Fans aged 18+ can also take part in this month’s exclusive Molson postgame party featuring surprise guest appearances, giveaways, and more! To unlock the secret party location, follow @MolsonQuebec on Instagram.
