EastEnders confirms death of popular character 4 years after disappearance

TheTools · 10.11.2026 00:05 · source · permalink

EastEnders has confirmed the death of long-missing character Mick Carter.

The former pub landlord, played by Danny Dyer, was last seen in a dramatic Christmas Day episode in 2022.

More than three million viewers watched as Mick disappeared while trying to save his ex-wife Linda after her car plunged into the English Channel.

It was later revealed that she had escaped, but Mick never returned, leaving fans to speculate about his fate.

EastEnders confirms death of iconic character after 4 years

EastEnders has now confirmed that Mick drowned, and his body has been found.

Linda, played by Kellie Bright, received the news in Monday night’s episode (November 9) and told her mother, Elaine Peacock, and Sharon Watts: "They’ve found Mick’s body.

"They wouldn’t give me any details but the death certificate’s been issued.

"I always knew.

"They wouldn’t even tell me where they’d found him, just that the next of kin is dealing with things."

Elaine responded: "It’s been so long, there can’t be much of him left."

The news sets up a tense confrontation with Janine, Mick’s second wife and next-of-kin, who has recently been released from prison after serving time for her involvement in the crash.

Linda tracks her down at a hotel in Mayfair and demands: "I’m not leaving until you tell me where my husband is."

EastEnders star to leave after 14 years

Ms Bright, who first joined the soap in 2013 alongside Danny Dyer, is set to leave the show next year.

The soap star said: "I have absolutely loved being part of the show but I have decided that it is now the right time for me to say goodbye.

"I made the tough decision over a year ago, but agreed to extend my stay because of an exciting storyline.

"I will miss my friends and colleagues hugely but I am looking forward to my next chapter and spending more time with my wonderful boys."

Ben Wadey, executive producer, praised the actress and her contribution to the show.

He said: "Kellie’s characterisation of the fiercely lovable, protective and brilliant Linda Carter has made her a favourite among viewers.

"Everyone at EastEnders will miss Kellie and Linda dearly.

"We’ve still got a way to go before we say goodbye but I can promise that Kellie’s exit storyline will be full of unmissable drama."

Linda Carter’s arrival in Albert Square in 2013 marked the beginning of a new era for the Queen Vic pub, which she ran alongside Mick for several years.

Her departure will close a major chapter in the soap’s long-running history.
