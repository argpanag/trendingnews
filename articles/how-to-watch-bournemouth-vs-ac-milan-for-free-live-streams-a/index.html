Bournemouth vs AC Milan: Preview

Rose couldn't have asked for better preparation for a milestone match at the Vitality Stadium, where Marcus Tavernier's early goal and a red card for Sunderland's Kevin Danso gave them a serene start and a 2-0 win.

It was Bournemouth's first league win of the season and followed successive defeats to former manager Andoni Iraola's Liverpool and Chelsea, who exposed every weakness in a 5-1 win at Stamford Bridge.

Milan might not be the imperious presence they were but they are in fine fettle at the start of the new season. Ruben Amorim's Rossoneri are unbeaten in Serie A and won on Sunday despite Atalanta's second-half rally at San Siro.
