Saturday Night Live Season 52 Reveals Its First Round of Hosts

TheTools · 09.09.2026 17:00 · source · permalink

Live from New York, it’s Saturday Night Live season 52. While the cast is still not set in stone (there must be some new members, right?) ahead of the premiere on September 26, the hosts and musical guests are ready to go. The show confirmed what the first four episodes of the season will look like, and it’s a good mix of old pros and fresh faces.

The season will kick off with the MVP of the 2026 NBA Finals, Knicks player Jalen Brunson, who will be joined by Katseye. Then comes Dakota Johnson, returning for her third round as host to promote Verity and joined by rock band Turnstile. Tires creator (and former almost cast member) Shane Gillis will then also host for the third time, alongside Spanish pop sensation Rosalía, ahead of Grammy nominations. Then, after a two-week break, Obsession star Inde Navarrette will host make her hosting debut with Gracie Abrams as her musical guest.

Below, find the full list of hosts and musical guests for season 52 as they’re announced.

﻿Sept. 26: Jalen Brunson and Katseye

Jalen Brunson will make himself an even more iconic New Yorker by making his SNL hosting debut. Earlier this year, he led the New York Knicks to their first NBA finals championship in 53 years and was named MVP. Katseye is making their musical guest debut, though how many members will be included in that is currently TBD.

Oct. 3: Dakota Johnson and Turnstile

Oct. 10: Shane Gillis and Rosalía

Oct. 31: Inde Navarrette and Gracie Abrams
