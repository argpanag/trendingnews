PlayStation Plus Monthly Games for November 2026 Announced

TheTools · 10.11.2026 19:33 · source · permalink

Death Stranding 2 and Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii are on offer.

November’s lineup of games on PlayStation Plus has been revealed by Sony.

Ahead of the holiday season, PlayStation Plus members are getting a wide variety of free games that they can try out on the service, including two big titles from Kojima Productions and Ryu Ga Gotoku.

The first game on this list is Death Stranding 2: On the Beach. The sequel to the first Death Stranding game, this follow-up takes eleven months after the events of its predecessor, featuring Sam Porter Bridges and Fragile as they set out across Australia to connect surviving colonies with the Chiral Network. It’s well worth a play, especially with this deal on PS Plus; in our review, IGN’s Simon Cardy scored the game a 9 out of 10, calling it a “triumphant sequel that emphatically delivers on the promise of its original.”

Like a Dragon: Pirate Yakuza in Hawaii is the second game on this month’s list. Join an amnesiac Goro Majima after he wakes up stranded on an island, gather a crew of pirates, and uncover a hidden treasure in this spinoff of the Yakuza series. It’s a delightfully fun romp, and another title that’s well worth taking advantage of if you’re a PS Plus subscriber; IGN’s Tristan Ogilvie scored the game an 8 out of 10 in our review, calling it “a consistently captivating voyage that kept my timbers shivering whether on land or at sea.”

Other games included in this month’s PS Plus lineup are Dragon Quest III HD-2D Remake, a reimagining of the beloved classic that earned a 9 out of 10 from our reviewer, as well as 2025 survival-action game Atomfall, first-person shooter Trepang2, and racing game MotoGP 26.
