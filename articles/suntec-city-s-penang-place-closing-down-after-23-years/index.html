Suntec City’s Penang Place closing down after 23 years

TheTools · 21.11.2026 01:16 · source · permalink

The founders remain open to opportunities that allow the Penang Place legacy to continue.

November 20, 2026, 06:25 PM

Photo from Penang Place/Facebook.

Penang Place at Suntec City will close down on Mar. 31 2027, after 23 years, as announced on their Facebook post.

The family owned restaurant opened in August 2003.

Founders Paul and Hannah Ooi wrote: “We are deeply grateful for your love and support throughout the years. It has been our joy and privilege to serve you and your families.”

Founded after a retrenchment during SARS

After 21 years at a Fortune 500 global technology firm, Paul Ooi was retrenched during the SARS outbreak. He then went on to found Penang Place with his wife, Hannah Ooi, as noted on Penang Place’s website.

As the sole breadwinner with six young children to support, Ooi took a leap of faith to pursue his childhood dream of opening a Penang restaurant.

The restaurant started at International Business Park in Jurong East before moving to Fusionopolis, and later, Suntec City.

Over the past two decades, the eatery became well-known for its affordable, all-you-can-eat Penang buffets, featuring staples like Assam Laksa, Char Kway Teow, Kueh Pie Tee and Chendol.

The eatery also provides catering and wedding services.

Continuing the legacy

While the Suntec City outlet is winding down, the Ooi family hinted that this might not be the definitive end for the brand.

They stated in the post that they remain “open to opportunities that will allow the Penang Place legacy to continue,” inviting interested parties who share their vision to reach out directly via email or WhatsApp.

They also encouraged regulars to drop by for their favourite Penang dishes before the restaurant closes down.
