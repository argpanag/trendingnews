Paramount reportedly eyeing Nashville for possible move

NASHVILLE, Tenn. (WZTV) — Paramount Skydance is reportedly taking a closer look at Nashville as the entertainment giant considers moving at least some of its operations out of Los Angeles, but Nashville Mayor Freddie O’Connell says Metro has not had direct contact with Paramount officials.

Politico reported Wednesday that Paramount is actively searching for office space in Nashville, citing two people familiar with the search who were granted anonymity to discuss it.

The report comes as Paramount remains involved in a legal fight surrounding its effort to acquire Warner Bros. Discovery.

According to Politico, Paramount representatives have reached out to at least two real estate developers about potentially constructing new Nashville offices that the company could occupy within three years.

One source also told Politico that Paramount representatives were recently shown a vacant, multi-acre property outside downtown Nashville where the company could potentially build a new headquarters.

Despite those reported steps, there has been no announcement from Paramount that it plans to relocate its headquarters or other operations to Nashville.

Nashville mayor: Metro is 'tracking' the situation

FOX 17 News asked Nashville Mayor Freddie O’Connell directly whether Metro has been involved in discussions surrounding a potential Paramount move.

O’Connell said Metro's economic development office has communicated with the state about Paramount, but his administration has not had direct contact with company officials.

"Our ECD office has talked directly to the state about that," O’Connell said. "I'd say we're in a tracking state right now. We know obviously the state is very interested in that. We have not had direct contact with Paramount officials."

O’Connell also pointed to Nashville's newly established Office of Entertainment Industry Development and its director, Sarah Childress.

The mayor said the city is listening to people on both the creative and business sides of Nashville's entertainment industry as it works to remain attractive to companies considering relocation or expansion.

"Nashville continues to be a very attractive place for corporations looking to relocate," O’Connell said.

Councilmember says Nashville could be a logical fit

Metro Councilmember Jacob Kupin also told FOX 17 News he has not had any official conversations about Paramount moving to Nashville.

Kupin said a major economic development project would typically begin at the state and mayoral levels before Metro Council becomes involved in considering issues such as potential incentives.

Still, Kupin believes Nashville's existing entertainment industry could make the city attractive to Paramount.

"We've got world-class composers and recording studios," Kupin said. "So it's a logical place to me for a TV, film and entertainment company like Paramount to come to."

Kupin pointed to television and film productions already taking place in Middle Tennessee, as well as the area's production workers, camera crews, actors and other entertainment professionals.

Both Kupin and O'Connell also pointed to Metro's new entertainment office as Nashville looks to grow beyond its well-established music industry and attract additional film and television business.

Kupin said creating the office was intended, in part, to give Nashville people on the ground who can build industry relationships and advocate for entertainment investment.

What is the state saying?

Politico reported that Tennessee has been actively courting Paramount.

FOX 17 News reached out to the Tennessee Department of Economic and Community Development to find out whether state officials have been in direct discussions with Paramount and what role the state may be playing.

TNECD would not comment on the specifics of any potential discussions.

"Paramount is a globally recognized company and well-respected brand," TNECD said in a statement to FOX 17 News. "While we cannot comment on specifics, TNECD remains committed to working with companies across a wide range of industries that are exploring opportunities to invest and grow in Tennessee."

The response neither confirms nor denies whether state officials are currently negotiating with Paramount.

O'Connell's comments, however, confirm Metro officials have discussed the situation with the state and that the mayor believes Tennessee is interested in the company.

Nashville among possible destinations

Nashville is not the only location connected to speculation about Paramount's future.

Other states, including Georgia, have also been floated as potential destinations should Paramount shift operations away from California.

Nashville has landed several major corporate investments in recent years, including Oracle. The technology company announced in 2024 that it planned to establish its world headquarters in Nashville.

For now, there is an important distinction between Paramount scouting Nashville and Paramount deciding to move here.

Politico's reporting indicates the company has taken concrete steps to examine its options in Nashville, including looking for existing office space, communicating with developers and reportedly viewing land that could accommodate a future headquarters.

But no relocation has been announced.

FOX 17 News is continuing to seek additional information from Paramount, as well as state and local officials about the company's reported interest in Nashville.
