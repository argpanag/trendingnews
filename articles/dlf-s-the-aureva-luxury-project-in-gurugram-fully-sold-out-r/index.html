DLF’s The Aureva luxury project in Gurugram fully sold out, records ₹1,985 crore in sales - CNBC TV18

On Thursday (October 1), shares of DLF Ltd ended at ₹662.60, down by ₹9.40, or 662.60%, on the BSE.

Realty major DLF Ltd on Sunday (October 4) announced the complete sell-out of The Aureva, its luxury retirement residential community in Sector 63, Gurugram, with the project achieving sales of around ₹1,985 crore.

The development spans approximately 1.6870 hectares (4.17 acres) and comprises 172 luxury residences, with a total carpet area of over 37,540 square metres (over 4 lakh square feet) and a total saleable area of over 7.5 lakh square feet.

DLF said The Aureva has been designed as an exclusive enclave for homeowners seeking a lifestyle supported by amenities, wellness offerings, hospitality-inspired services, integrated healthcare access and opportunities for social connection.

The development has been envisioned around the principles of wellbeing, independence, and community. Residents will benefit from a holistic amenity ecosystem featuring a luxury clubhouse, fitness and wellness facilities, an indoor swimming pool, spa, yoga pavilion, meditation centre, library, banquet space, landscaped garden, outdoor gathering areas, and curated social infrastructure

The Aureva: 172 luxury residences

The project is a standalone tower rising to G+45 floors and offers spacious 4 BHK residences with dedicated staff dormitories, expansive private decks, premium finishes and universally accessible design elements aimed at comfort, safety and ease of living.

Each residence comes with three dedicated parking spaces and access to wellness, recreational and lifestyle amenities. The development combines open spaces, curated landscaping, integrated healthcare infrastructure and future-ready residences.

Global consultants involved

DLF said The Aureva was designed in collaboration with globally renowned consultants, including HB Design as master planner, SHMA as landscape architect, BO Steiber Lighting Design for lighting, Hewshott for acoustics and ESD Global for sustainability and green building advisory.

Gurugram location and connectivity

The Aureva is located in Sector 63, Gurugram, abutting The Arbour. The development has connectivity to Golf Course Extension Road, Southern Peripheral Road and NH-48, as well as the city's commercial, educational and lifestyle destinations.

DLF said the project is positioned in the premium residential corridor of Gurugram and combines luxury living with wellbeing, convenience, healthcare access and community engagement.

Aakash Ohri, Managing Director and Chief Business Officer, DLF Home Developers Ltd., said, "The Aureva represents DLF's vision of redefining luxury retirement housing in India. As expectations evolve beyond traditional markers of luxury, residents increasingly seek homes that enhance everyday life through thoughtful design, holistic wellbeing, seamless convenience, and a strong sense of community.

Conceived around this understanding, The Aureva brings together wellness, hospitality, healthcare, and luxury living within a carefully curated residential ecosystem. Every element of the development has been designed to create an elegant, future-ready environment that empowers residents to embrace their next chapter with confidence, dignity, and purpose."
