Shares of Tesla, Inc. (TSLA) slipped nearly 2% overnight heading into Thursday as money managers warned of political hurdles and risks to any takeover premium in a potential SpaceX merger, while Cathie Wood's ARK Investment Management bought $47 million of SpaceX stock.

TSLA shares fell 3% to $385.19 on Wednesday, bringing the week's decline to nearly 4%.

Politics Cloud Tesla-SpaceX Merger Hopes

Mark Tepper, CEO of Strategic Wealth Partners, cast doubt on a potential Tesla–SpaceX merger, warning that rising political pressure on Elon Musk could bring tougher regulation for both companies. "I don't see the SpaceX merger happening. Elon Musk is about to face a lot more political pressure, and that means more regulation," he said on X.

His warning followed Democrats' takeover of both chambers of Congress and victory in the Texas governor's race. Texas hosts major Tesla and SpaceX operations, along with their upcoming Terafab chipmaking project.

XTB research director Kathleen Brooks also warned that increased congressional scrutiny could delay a transaction. "While Congress cannot stop a merger, they could slow it down via extra scrutiny," she said. Tesla and SpaceX have formed special committees to study a possible merger.

Black Warns Merger Premium Could Vanish

Gary Black, managing partner of The Future Fund, warned that an all-stock SpaceX-Tesla deal might deliver less value than bulls expect, pointing to Marvell Technology's (MRVL) acquisition of Lumentum Holdings (LITE).

"As we've learned (again) from $MRVL - $LITE deal, a +24% premium can quickly go negative as the acquirer's stock is sold down," he said on X. Black cited declines of 27% in Lumentum and 31% in Marvell from their pre-announcement levels. Earlier this week, he highlighted Lumentum's 15% slide to $750 as Marvell fell 26% following deal rumors.

Marvell's initial offer promised 4.5 shares for each Lumentum share, worth about $1,132, a 24% premium to Lumentum's Oct. 28 closing price. But the fixed exchange ratio means the offer's dollar value falls alongside Marvell's stock.
