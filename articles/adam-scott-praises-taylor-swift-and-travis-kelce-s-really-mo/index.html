Adam Scott Praises Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce’s ‘Really Moving’ Wedding

Adam Scott is praising Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce's wedding.

During his guest appearance on the Wednesday, Sept. 2, episode of Esquire's What I've Learned podcast, the Severance star, 53, reflected on his experience at Swift and Kelce's nuptials in New York City. He attended the black-tie event two months ago on Friday, July 3.

Scott said it was an honor for him and his wife, Naomi, to even be invited, recalling them feeling "so flattered and delighted" to have made the star-studded guest list.

"It was really something," Scott told Esquire's editor-in-chief, Michael Sebastian. "It was really an incredible thing to obviously be invited to, and to go witness."

From left: Paul Rudd, Julie Yaeger, Naomi Scott and Adam Scott
Credit: Adam Scott/Instagram

The actor found it "striking and really lovely" how the celebration was much like any other couple's wedding rather than a spectacle, despite the bride and groom's stardom. Fellow wedding guest Graham Norton previously shared a similar sentiment.

"At the end of the day, it really just felt like a regular wedding," Scott said. "At its core, I mean, it was, you know, a lot of people and a big deal and stuff, but at its core, it was just a really sweet wedding with people who really care about each other and these two families coming together."

He continued, "And that just happened in front of us, and it was really, really moving."

He added that Swift, 36, is "such a nice, generous person."

The Parks and Recreation alum first spoke out about the wedding during an appearance on the Today show on Monday, Aug. 24. He again emphasized how "real" the wedding was.

"You know what, I think the thing we walked away with, the impression we had more than anything, was that it was a real wedding. You had the sense that these are just people who love each other," Scott said. "It was kind of on a grand scale — there were more people than were at my wedding — but at its core, it just felt like a regular wedding with two people coming together."

Travis Kelce and Taylor Swift in March 2026

He continued, "Everyone teared up. It was a really lovely, lovely event."

The Big Little Lies star said at the time that he scored a few dad points with his teenage daughter, Frankie — who is a big Swiftie — by attending the wedding and filling her in on "all the details" once he returned home.
