Magnitude 5.6 earthquake hits offshore Davao Occidental

TheTools · 08.10.2026 21:15 · source · permalink

A magnitude 5.6 earthquake jolted parts of Mindanao on Thursday afternoon, the Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology (PHIVOLCS) said.

The earthquake struck at 4:07 p.m., with the epicenter located 28 kilometers northwest of Balut Island in Sarangani, Davao Occidental. The tectonic quake, initially measured at magnitude 6.4, occurred at a depth of 26 kilometers.

The agency said the earthquake was an aftershock of the magnitude 7.8 offshore Sarangani and magnitude 6.5 offshore Davao Occidental earthquakes in June 2026.

The following intensities were reported on Thursday:

  • Intensity V: Kiamba, Sarangani, and General Santos City.
  • Intensity IV: Alabel, Glan, Maasim, Malapatan, and Malungon, Sarangani; Lake Sebu, Polomolok, T’Boli, and Tupi, South Cotabato.
  • Intensity III: Antipas, Kidapawan City, and Matalam, Cotabato; Maitum, Sarangani; Banga, Koronadal City, and Tampakan, South Cotabato; and Kalamansig and Lebak, Sultan Kudarat.
  • Intensity II: Zamboanga City; Davao City; M’lang, Cotabato; Norala, Santo Niño, Surallah, and Tantangan, South Cotabato; and Bagumbayan, Columbio, Esperanza, Lambayong, President Quirino, Senator Ninoy Aquino, and Tacurong City, Sultan Kudarat.

Instrumental intensities:

  • Intensity IV: Alabel and Malapatan, Sarangani; Lake Sebu and T’Boli, South Cotabato; General Santos City; and Palimbang, Sultan Kudarat.
  • Intensity III: Digos City and Matanao, Davao del Sur; Jose Abad Santos, Santa Maria, and Sarangani, Davao Occidental; Kidapawan City, Cotabato; Maitum, Sarangani; Banga, South Cotabato; and Lebak, Sultan Kudarat.
  • Intensity II: Magsaysay, Davao del Sur; Davao City; M’lang and President Roxas, Cotabato; Maasim, Sarangani; Santo Niño and Surallah, South Cotabato; Esperanza, Isulan, Kalamansig, and President Quirino, Sultan Kudarat; and Carmen, Surigao del Sur.
  • Intensity I: Zamboanga City; Kalilangan, Bukidnon; Nabunturan, Davao de Oro; Bansalan, Davao del Sur; Magpet and Pikit, Cotabato; and Tantangan, South Cotabato.

PHIVOLCS said no damage is expected, although aftershocks may still occur.

In a Facebook post, the General Santos City government announced the suspension of classes at all levels in both public and private schools “until further notice.”

Work in all government offices in the city was also suspended to allow authorities to conduct structural inspections and safety assessments following the earthquake, the local government unit said. — Jean Mangaluz/VBL, GMA News
