Call for regional accountability on transboundary haze 

TheTools · 04.09.2026 00:00 · source · permalink

AS a Sabah rights activist dedicated to public welfare and environmental health, I find it imperative to directly address the recent public remarks made by Indonesia’s Minister of Forestry, Raja Juli Antoni.

Dismissing the reality of transboundary haze by asserting that “smoke has no ID card (asap gak ada KTP-nya)” downplays a severe environmental, health and economic crisis that periodically afflicts millions of people across South-East Asia, including Sabah and the wider Malaysia.
