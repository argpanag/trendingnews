After missing the Spanish Super Cup, the Ukrainian will also miss the EuroLeague opener

By Javier Molero/ jmolero@eurohoops.net   

Baskonia head coach Paolo Galbiati spoke to journalists ahead of the team’s first EuroLeague game, which Baskonia will play against Olympiacos in Vitoria on Thursday at 20:30 CEST.

The main topic was Alex Len, who joined the team from Real Madrid this summer and is still waiting to make his Baskonia debut. He also missed the Spanish Super Cup game, in which Baskonia lost to Joventut 96–84.

When asked whether Len would be available against Olympiacos, Galbiati gave a clear answer.

“The same 12 players.”

Journalists asked him about Len and he said:

“The same 12 players means the same 12 players for next game.”

They also asked him about an explication on the Alex Len situation:

“You need to talk with the club.”
