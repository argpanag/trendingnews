SPRING 2027 READY-TO-WEAR

By Victoria Beckham

In a season when so many fashion brands are suddenly vying to show women how wearable they are, Victoria Beckham is already on a winning streak with that. “Everything should be effortless and comfortable. Elegance, rooted in reality,” as she put it at a preview.

The magnetism of her brand was crisply readable on her runway, from the red lipstick (denoting her wildly successful makeup line) through a series of dresses to the simplified excellence of the tailoring she has distilled over many seasons.

“Everything’s obviously very considered, even if there are complicated constructions,” she said, proffering the example of the lightness of dresses she said had been influenced by looking at the 1920s, adding, “It’s not a fitted silhouette this season.” That idea started with semi-sheer printed shifts, weighted at the edges with concealed beads, and culminated with pleated asymmetric dresses suspended from jewelry chains “dancing around the body,” as the designer described it.

In between, there was a parade of the typologies of clothing that are proving an immediate hit in her newly opened New York store. “It’s been a phenomenal success, even though it’s only been open three or four weeks,” said Beckham. Much of the response has been to her light outerwear—hence the series of couture-volumed but useful bombers here—and her clean-lined jackets and perfectly slouchy trousers.

It’s a one-stop wardrobe and beauty shop she’s so cleverly facilitating. Getting yourself together isn’t easy, no matter how many clothes there are out there. No wonder so many women are buying into the VB world right now.
