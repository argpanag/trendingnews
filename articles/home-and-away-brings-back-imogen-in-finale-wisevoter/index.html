Georgia Blizzard returns as Imogen in the Australian season finale, with Levi and Tane's storylines set to shift.

Updated on Nov. 25, 2026 in Celebrities

Imogen, played by Georgia Blizzard, returns to Home and Away in the last episode of the Australian season, which airs on Tuesday, 8 December 2026. Tane is also returning to Summer Bay after previously leaving to be closer to his son Archie.

Why it matters

Imogen is Levi's ex-wife, and the two split two years before her return, which sets up a fresh complication for Levi, who is engaged to Mackenzie. The episode also brings Tane back to Summer Bay, where his past shared kiss with Mackenzie could affect their friendship.

The return is tied to the show's Australian season finale airing on 8 December 2026, with the broadcast scheduled 13 days after the date of this story. Imogen's two-year-old daughter is part of the storyline, though the extraction does not give further viewership figures.
