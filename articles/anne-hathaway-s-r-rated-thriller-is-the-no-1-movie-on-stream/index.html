Anne Hathaway's R-Rated Thriller is the No. 1 Movie on Streaming

Anne Hathaway has had a great 2026. From Mother Mary to The Devil Wears Prada 2, The Odyssey, and more, the popular and talented actress has kept a busy schedule. And now, she's dominating the PVOD charts with her latest R-rated thriller based on a book.

Per FlixPatrol, Verity is currently the No. 1 most-watched movie on the Apple TV Store in the United States, and also on Prime Video in the United States and Canada. The psychological thriller starring Anne Hathaway, Dakota Johnson, and Josh Hartnett premiered on October 2, 2026, and was a box-office hit, grossing $163.5 million against a $40 million budget.

Verity Is Based on a Colleen Hoover Novel

Colleen Hoover has taken over the world with some of her most popular books, from It Ends with Us to Regretting You and Reminders of Him, all of which have received movie adaptations in recent years. Verity is the latest book-to-screen adaptation of a Colleen Hoover bestseller, and unlike the previous novels, this one is an R-rated thriller.

Verity follows Lowen Ashleigh, a writer who, after a strange encounter, gets a job finishing a book for a famous bestselling author, Verity Crawford. Verity was in the middle of writing a book in her world-famous series but fell into a catatonic state after a tragic accident. Verity's husband, Jeremy Crawford, hires Lowen to finish the book.

However, what starts as a unique opportunity for Lowen soon turns into a scary and dangerous job. She suspects that Verity might be faking her catatonic state, and even wonders whether the accident happened exactly as she was told. Especially after finding Verity's memoirs, where she discovers some scary and disturbing secrets about the author.

Verity stars Anne Hathaway as Verity Crawford, Dakota Johnson as Lowen Ashleigh, and Josh Hartnett as Jeremy Crawford. The thriller also stars Ismael Cruz Córdova, Brady Wagner, Irina Dvorovenko, K. K. Moggie, and Michael Abbott Jr. Verity is now the second-highest-grossing Colleen Hoover movie adaptation after It Ends with Us, but received polarizing reviews.

Verity Was Not a Hit with Critics

Despite being a box-office hit, Verity received mostly negative reviews from critics. On Rotten Tomatoes, the R-rated thriller holds a "rotten" 31% Tomatometer score based on critics' reviews, but a far more positive 72% Popcornmeter score based on audience reviews. On IMDb, the film has a 6.0 rating with over 26,000 reviews.

Many critics praised Anne Hathaway's performance even if the thriller had its flaws. Kristen Lopez from The Film Maven said, "Verity is certainly the best of the Hoover adaptations we've gotten over the last two years, mostly thanks to Anne Hathaway." Jinal Bhatt from Variety India wrote, "The film is carried by its setting, atmosphere, the many twists and Anne Hathaway’s campy portrayal of the complicated titular character."

Verity might not be a hit with critics, but its box-office success has now translated to streaming, making the R-rated thriller an undeniable hit with viewers. Many badly rated movies have become cult classics, and Verity could earn the title as well. And after it goes from PVOD to a wide streaming release, Verity might find new life as a streaming hit.
