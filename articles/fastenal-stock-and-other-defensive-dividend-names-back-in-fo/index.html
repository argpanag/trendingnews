Fastenal (FAST)

Overview: Fastenal is a US based wholesale distributor of fasteners and industrial and construction supplies that keeps manufacturers and maintenance heavy customers stocked for day to day operations.

Market Cap: US$57.8b

Fastenal fits this defensive dividend screen because it is a large, lower volatility US industrial that leans on recurring maintenance driven demand and a long running payout rather than aggressive leverage or speculative projects.

"The company is expanding its Fastenal Managed Inventory technology beyond the prior 43% of revenue, with recent data pointing to FMI at 44.6% of sales and signings of 109 weighted devices per day. This supports a view that higher density of devices at customer sites can keep lifting revenue and earnings over time."

MSC Industrial Direct (MSM)

Overview: MSC Industrial Direct distributes metalworking and maintenance, repair and operations products and services to manufacturers and institutions, giving this screener a more resilient industrial dividend profile.

Operations: MSC Industrial Direct generates about US$3.9b in revenue mainly from metalworking and MRO distribution, with most sales coming from US customers.

Market Cap: US$7.3b

MSC Industrial Direct matters here because its maintenance heavy product mix can shorten economic swings for investors who want steadier dividends from an industrial stock.

"The expansion of MSC Industrial Direct's In-Plant programs and vending machine installations remains a live driver, with vending machines at about 30,800 units and in-plant programs at 426 and both channels reporting mid teens average daily sales growth, which continues to support future revenue and earnings potential as these solutions scale from a larger base."

What happens to MSC Industrial Direct's appeal for dividend seekers will hinge on how one unresolved pressure shapes the payoff from these growth channels.

American States Water (AWR)

Overview: American States Water provides regulated water and electric utility services to residential, commercial, industrial, and military customers across California communities and US military bases.

Operations: American States Water generates about US$486.6 million from water, US$61.6 million from electric utilities, and US$149.3 million from contracted services, all in the United States.

Market Cap: US$3.28b

American States Water offers exactly the kind of regulated, bills-keep-coming utility exposure many income investors look for when rates stay higher for longer, and when cash flow reliability matters more than rapid expansion stories.

"The shift to new CPUC mechanisms such as MRAM and the incremental supply balancing account from 2025 introduces more direct exposure to customer usage and supply mix, which can translate higher consumption and more efficient sourcing into stronger revenue and net margins in favorable periods."
