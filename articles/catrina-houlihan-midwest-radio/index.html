14 Sliabh Cairn Kiltimagh, Co. Mayo, surrounded by the love of her heartbroken family.

Predeceased by her beloved grandparents James P. and Bridie Durkin.

Catrina will be forever loved and deeply missed by her adored daughter Samantha, her loving partner Darren, her devoted mother Christina, her five sisters and four brothers, her cherished nieces and nephews, the entire Houlihan and Clarke families, the extended Murphy and Muldowney families, and her many relatives, neighbours and friends.

Catrina will also be fondly remembered by the close-knit community of Kiltimagh, her wonderful neighbours in Sliabh Cairn, her loyal customers and her many friends within the Race to Glory and wider running community, where she was so well known and loved.

Her warmth, kindness and wonderful spirit will be deeply missed by all who had the pleasure of knowing her.

May her beautiful soul rest in eternal peace.

Catrina will repose in O Hora’s Funeral Home Main St Kiltimagh F12Y1F7 on Friday evening from 5 o’clock until 7 o’clock.

Funeral Mass on Saturday at 12 noon in the Church of the Holy Family, Kiltimagh followed by burial in  St. Joseph’s Cemetery,  Midfield, Swinford

Family flowers only please donations if desired to Cancer Care West.

Catrina’s Funeral Mass can be viewed on www.kiltimaghparish.org

House private please.
