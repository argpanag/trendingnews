North Korean leader Kim Jong-un pays unexpected visit to Chinese border city of Dandong | South China Morning Post

TheTools · 25.10.2026 07:30 · source · permalink

One-day visit came on the eve of the 76th anniversary of Chinese troops’ entry into the Korean war.

Read more: https://sc.mp/03be2c

North Korean leader Kim Jong-un visited the Chinese border city of Dandong on Saturday, with both sides underlining their traditional friendship and calling to boost cooperation. The trip was Kim’s first visit to China in more than a year and had not been announced in advance.
