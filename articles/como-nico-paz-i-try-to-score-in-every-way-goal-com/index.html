Como, Nico Paz: “I try to score in every way” | Goal.com

TheTools · 28.10.2026 04:53 · source · permalink

The number 10 registers an assist and a goal against Torino

Nico Paz, Como's number 10, discussed the 3-1 win away to Torino after scoring a goal and providing an assist.

"I knew my moment could come. Today it was about getting into the box and waiting for the cross: fortunately, I managed to score," reports TMW.

  • "I try to score in every way. Today I did it with a header and I want to keep it up," the Argentinian adds.
