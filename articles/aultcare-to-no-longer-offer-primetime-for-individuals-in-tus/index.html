AultCare to no longer offer PrimeTime for individuals in Tuscarawas

TheTools · 10.11.2026 22:14 · source · permalink

AultCare will not provide Tuscarawas County residents with individual Medicare Advantage plans next year.

The Canton-based insurance provider has mailed notices to the affected PrimeTime Health Plan enrollees. PrimeTime employer group plans in Tuscarawas County will be continued.

"At AultCare, we’re committed to providing the best possible customer service to our clients and community," according to a statement provided by the health system.

"AultCare’s PrimeTime Health Plan has been reevaluating its Medicare Advantage offerings because of changes in the market, changes in CMS reimbursement and rising medical expenses, much like many other insurance plans across the nation."

AultCare, which is part of the larger Aultman Health System, did not share the number of people affected. The nonprofit insurance provider offers PrimeTime Health Plan as its Medicare Advantage option, and PrimeTime Choices as a Medicare supplement or Medigap plan.

"This allows us to focus where we can provide the strongest value, highest quality and most sustainable Medicare Advantage offerings for our members," the health system statement continued. "We have sent letters to impacted enrollees to help them evaluate their options and make coverage decisions for 2027 during the Annual Enrollment Period, which is going on now."

Chelsea Youngman is an independent insurance agent who focuses on health care and Medicare in the region. She has a number of Tuscarawas County clients on PrimeTime and said she evaluates each person's situation and options with them.

"Obviously, they're kind of frustrated and upset," she said.

Youngman said she's not aware of any other insurance providers dropping clients in Tuscarawas County this year but said it's not "uncommon" for Medicare Advantage insurance options to change, particularly in rural areas. She encouraged anyone affected to reach out to PrimeTime plan representatives or a local insurance agent.

"We don't want them to panic," Youngman said. "We're here to help them through the situation."

Patricia Doby, who has worked as an agent for 35 years at the independent Nelson Insurance | SeibertKeck Agency in New Philadelphia, currently writes for 13 carriers with Medicare Advantage plans. She said sub-par reimbursement in Tuscarawas County is a factor in the service area reduction.

Doby has 160 or more clients on AultCare's PrimeTime. She said in an email that many of them feel "blindsided" by the decision.

"In helping clients get past the hurt and frustration that this PrimeTime decision has caused, my goal is to provide my clients with honest answers & have open conversation," Doby said. "To calm their nerves and help find them a smooth transition to another plan."
