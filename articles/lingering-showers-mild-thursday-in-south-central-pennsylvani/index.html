Lingering showers & mild Thursday in south central Pennsylvania

After more than two straight weeks of dry weather, much-needed rainfall returns to the Susquehanna Valley on Thursday.

After more than two straight weeks of dry weather, much-needed rainfall returns to the Susquehanna Valley on Thursday.

Good afternoon,

A few showers may linger this afternoon as a cold front passes over the region. Otherwise, look for mostly cloudy skies and highs in the upper 60s to lower 70s. Rain totals will amount to a few tenths of an inch to 0.50" in most areas.

The cold front will move east Friday morning, bringing partly to mostly cloudy skies. Temperatures will remain mild, near 70 degrees. A few showers will return after dark Friday, as the upper-level system moves toward Pennsylvania.

Aside from a few showers Saturday, the big change will be temperatures falling back to the upper 50s to lower 60s under mostly cloudy skies. It will also be breezy.

Sunday will feature partly sunny skies and highs in the upper 50s.

We're in for breezy, seasonable weather next week. High temperatures will stay in the 50s to low to mid 60s, with low temperatures in the 30s and low 40s. Most of next week looks to remain dry.

Have a great day!
