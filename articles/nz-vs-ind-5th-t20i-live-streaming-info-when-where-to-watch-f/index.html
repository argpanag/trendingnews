India heads into the fifth T20I with a 3-1 lead in the series. | Photo Credit: AP

India will hope to end the T20I series on a high when it takes on New Zealand in the fifth match at the Seddon Park in Hamilton on Sunday.

The Shreyas Iyer-led side sealed the series in the third game itself but suffered an 87-run defeat after conceding 259 in the 4th T20I.

The India vs New Zealand fifth T20I is scheduled to begin at 12:30 PM IST.

The toss for the India vs New Zealand fifth T20I is scheduled to take place at 12:00 PM IST.

The India vs New Zealand fifth T20I will be played at Seddon Park in Hamilton.

The India vs New Zealand fifth fourth T20I will be streamed live on the SonyLIV app and website and televised live on the Sony Sports Network.
