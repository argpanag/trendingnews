CONFIRMED lineups: Betis vs Real Madrid, 2026

TheTools · 04.09.2026 17:41 · source · permalink
  • Real Madrid CF: La Liga

Real Madrid have published their starting lineup for the upcoming match against Betis in La Liga.

STARTING LINEUPS

Real Madrid starting XI: Courtois, Dumfries, Konaté, Huijsen, Cucurella, Camavinga, Valverde, Güler, Bellingham, Vinicius; Mbappé.

Betis starting XI (TBC): Valles, Bellerín, Diego Llorente, Natan, Fran García, Facundo, Fornals, Antony, Isco, Riquelme, Cucho.

Camavinga and Valverde start in Real Madrid’s double pivot, with Arda Güler on the right flank and Bellingham in his usual spot behind Mbappé. Dumfries returns to the starting XI.
