Rex Ryan is arrested after allegedly running over feet of pedestrians with his truck

TheTools · 11.11.2026 17:30 · source · permalink

No, it’s not The Onion. No, it’s not April Fool’s Day.

Former Jets and Bills coach and current (for now) ESPN analyst Rex Ryan has been arrested in Nashville after allegedly running over the feet of two pedestrians with his truck.

Via Edward Lewis of the New York Post, the Metropolitan Nashville Police Department said that Ryan turned himself in “on six outstanding misdemeanor warrants” for “striking two pedestrians with his pickup truck” just on November 5.

“The pedestrians were crossing Broadway from 4th Avenue with a walk sign when the pickup truck turned right from 4th Avenue running over both victims’ feet and making contact with one of the individual’s legs,” the Metropolitan Nashville Police Department said.

“The pedestrians said the vehicle kept going, but stopped for the red light at Broadway and Representative John Lewis Way where they waved down the driver. They said he stepped out of the truck to argue with them, got back in, and drove away. They took pictures of the truck’s license plate and the driver.”

Ryan faces two counts of leaving the scene of an accident, two counts of failure to give information/render aid, and two counts of failure to obey a traffic control signal.

Ryan became notorious in 2010 for a foot-fetish video that appeared online. He made light of the situation last month on ESPN, saying “I’m an expert in a few things. One of them is feet.”
