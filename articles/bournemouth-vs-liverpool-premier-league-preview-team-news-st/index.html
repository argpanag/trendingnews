    Manchester United

    Manchester United manager Michael Carrick speaking to BBC Sport: "I think we did enough to win the game. I think we could win it.   9. 'It hurts - but wins will come'published at 18:54 BST 20 September
    18:54 BST 20 September

    FT: Fulham 1-1 Man Utd

    Fulham

    Fulham goalkeeper Bernd Leno speaking to Sky Sports: "It hurts... but there are still a lot of positive things to take from the game.

    "I know Manchester United have a lot of individual quality, I have played against them for many years and know them really well. I know to be alert in case they shoot from distance. When you make some saves in the game you feel very focused. Unfortunately I couldn't keep out the goal but that is football."

    On their development under Alvaro Arbeloa: "I think you could see the more we play, the more control we have. In the first couple of games we were too emotional. You can't change things in one or two months, it takes time. We are much more balanced now.

    "A lot of positive things to take, and still a lot of improvement. The team is alive, it is fighting, it is running. You can see how we play and it is very positive. With our new manager and philosophy I think we will win games. Then the confidence will come."

  10. 'I didn't think we were passive'published at 18:52 BST 20 September
    18:52 BST 20 September

    FT: Fulham 1-1 Man Utd

    Manchester United

    Manchester United manager Michael Carrick speaking to Sky Sports: "I thought we should have won the game, I thought we created enough to win the game. The goal we did concede is one of them goals. The boys could have thought it is one of those days but I really like the reaction. We nearly won it in the end. It's the Premier League and you will have spells where you don't have it all your own way. To expect total dominance for the whole game, I don't think anyone gets that in this league."

    On if he was happy with the team's energy: "Yes."

    "I think we can take a lot from today. I think we can learn a lot as well. We can be better at certain things, that is pretty clear, but there were some god signs today."

Player of the match

  • Bournemouth (active)
  • Liverpool

After the opportunity to rate players has closed, the score displayed represents the average from all the submissions by BBC Sport users.

Liverpool boss Iraola makes a winning return to old club Bournemouth

Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola made a winning return to Bournemouth as his side signed off for the international break with a narrow victory at Vitality Stadium.

Alexander Isak's fourth Premier League goal of the season settled a poor game - and left the Cherries seeking a first top-flight win of the campaign going into October.

A determined run by Cody Gakpo ended with a ball into the box that Bournemouth failed to deal with and Sweden forward Isak tucked home from close range.

Isak was mobbed by Liverpool's travelling fans after running over to celebrate with them and was booked by referee Michael Oliver.

Iraola, who secured European qualification for Bournemouth before leaving to take charge of Liverpool in the summer, received a warm welcome from the Cherries fans on his return.

But he was on course to suffer a frustrating afternoon in Dorset until Isak pounced in the 57th minute.

Before then Bournemouth had gone closest to scoring through Evanilson's 18th-minute flick which Alisson did well to put behind for a corner.

At the other end, Florian Wirtz skied an attempt over the bar from close range that was flagged offside when replays showed he was, in fact, onside.

Meanwhile, expensive summer signing Bradley Barcola forced Djordje Petrovic to save with his legs, while Dominik Szoboszlai's 25-yard dipping free-kick dropped just wide.

Liverpool climb to sixth with nine points from five games, while Bournemouth have three points and drop to 17th.

Liverpool analysis: Reds remain a work in progress

Andoni Iraola post-match interview - Bournemouth vs Liverpool

Liverpool go into the three week break having won back-to-back Premier League away games, while they have now kept three top-flight clean sheets in a row in a single season for the first time since the start of 2024-25 - when they went on to win the title in Arne Slot's first season.

In addition, they have won four of their last five games in all competitions.

Sounds good yet Liverpool remain a work in progress.

This was far from a fluent performance by the Reds, whose next game is at home to leaders Manchester City on 11 October, despite extending their unbeaten start under Iraola.

They struggled to impose themselves against opponents who travelled to northern Spain to play Real Sociedad in the Europa League on Thursday.

It took 33 minutes before Liverpool registered a first attempt on target while it was another 11 minutes before they won a first corner.

At least Isak is starting to show some form.

The £125m signing has already scored more top-flight goals than last season, while the defensive partnership between Virgil van Dijk and 21-year-old summer signing Jeremy Jacquet continues to flourish.

Bournemouth analysis: Timely international break

Marco Rose post-match interview: Bournemouth 0-1 Liverpool

This is the first time Bournemouth have had to balance playing in Europe on a Thursday before a Premier League game on a Sunday.

They secured a memorable victory in the Basque Country over Real Sociedad in midweek but are still without a top-flight win under Marco Rose, who took charge after Iraola left for Anfield.

The international break has come at a good time for the Cherries.

It was revealed before kick-off that Dutch winger Justin Kluivert would be out for several weeks with a damaged hamstring he suffered against Real Sociedad.

Against Liverpool, they missed his attacking flare and managed just two attempts on target.

These are exciting times for Bournemouth, who host seven-time champions of Europe AC Milan in the Europa League on 22 October.

But they will need to get to grips with juggling the demands of playing in both the Premier League and Europe quickly if they are to stay away from the lower reaches of the table.
