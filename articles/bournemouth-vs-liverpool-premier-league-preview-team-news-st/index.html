Match Facts

  • Bournemouth have lost 14 of their 18 Premier League games against Liverpool (W3 D1), though all three victories have come at home – 4-3 in December 2016, 1-0 in March 2023, and 3-2 in January 2026.

  • Liverpool have only failed to score in one of their 18 Premier League games against Bournemouth. They average three goals per game against the Cherries (54 goals in 18 games), their highest average against any side they’ve faced 10+ times in the competition.

  • Bournemouth are winless in their first four Premier League matches this season (D3 L1) – only in 2023-24 have they had a longer winless start to a Premier League season, when they failed to win their first nine under current Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola.

  • Liverpool have drawn three of their first four games (W1) in a Premier League season for the first time since 2005-06. They drew their fifth game that season and that remains the only time the Reds have drawn four of their five league games in a season.
