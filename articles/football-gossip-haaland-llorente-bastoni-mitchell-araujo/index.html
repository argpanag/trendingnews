Football gossip: Haaland, Llorente, Bastoni, Mitchell, Araujo

TheTools · 26.09.2026 12:53 · source · permalink

1 of 4

  • Published
    25 September 2026

Erling Haaland is without a relegation clause, Premier League clubs are eyeing Marcos Llorente, Juventus are considering Crystal Palace's Tyrick Mitchell.

Manchester City's Norwegian striker Erling Haaland, 26, does not have a clause in his contract with the club in the event of relegation or any other circumstances. (Sport - in Spanish), external

Manchester City, Liverpool and Chelsea are interested in Spain midfielder Marcos Llorente, 31, who is yet to decide his future with Atletico Madrid. (TeamTalk), external

The same three Premier League clubs are keeping an eye on Inter Milan and Italy defender Alessandro Bastoni, 27, who is also attracting interest from Barcelona. (TeamTalk), external

Brentford are in discussions with their winger Kevin Schade about a new long-term contract amid increasing interest in the Germany international from clubs including Arsenal and Liverpool. (Sky Sports), external

Brighton are planning to trigger an extension in the contract of 22-year-old Sweden midfielder Yasin Ayari in the coming weeks. (Football Insider), external

Liverpool are giving serious consideration to making 27-year-old Uruguay defender Ronald Araujo's loan switch from Barcelona a permanent one. (Mundo Deportivo - in Spanish), external

Juventus have been offered the chance to sign Crystal Palace defender Tyrick Mitchell, 27, amid other interest from Liverpool and Manchester United in the Englishman. (Tuttosport - in Italian), external

Real Madrid are interested in Stuttgart's 20-year-old Germany U21s centre-back Finn Jeltsch. (AS - in Spanish), external

Nottingham Forest and Aston Villa both made enquiries over the availability of France youth international Valentin Atangana. The 21-year-old is currently with Saudi club Al-Ahli. (L'Equipe - in French - subscription required), external

Newcastle sporting director Ross Wilson is overseeing a new recruitment approach which will see the club prioritising young talent and a new player trading strategy. (The I - subscription required), external
