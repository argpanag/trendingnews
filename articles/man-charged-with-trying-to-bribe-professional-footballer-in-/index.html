Man charged with trying to bribe professional footballer in Donegal - Donegal Daily

TheTools · 16.11.2026 16:54 · source · permalink

A man has appeared in court charged in connection with an investigation into the attempted bribery of a professional League of Ireland football player in Donegal.

Ronan Madden, with an address at Kylegan, Kylebrack, Loughrea in Co Galway, is accused of trying to bribe the player to intentionally receive a red card during a match.

The incident allegedly took place at Inishowen Gateway Hotel in Buncrana, Co Donegal, on 17 February 2024.

The 36-year-old appeared before Blanchardstown District Court in Dublin.

Mr Madden is charged with corruptly offering the footballer €40,000 to "do an act in relation to his employment as a professional football player" which was to intentionally receive a red card during an unspecified football match.

He is charged under section 5 of the Criminal Justice (Corruption Offences) Act 2018.

Mr Madden was remanded on bail to appear before Blanchardstown District Court on 2 December.
