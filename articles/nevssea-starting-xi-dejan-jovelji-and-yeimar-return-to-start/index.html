NEvsSEA Starting XI: Dejan Joveljić and Yeimar return to starting lineup, Sebastian Gomez gets the nod | Seattle Sounders

TheTools · 11.10.2026 01:45 · source · permalink

Head Coach Brian Schmetzer reveals his lineup for tonight's home match against New England Revolution (4:30 p.m. PT; Watch on Apple TV, 950 KJR AM, El Rey 1360AM) on Saturday evening at Gillette Stadium. Here is the Delta Starting XI:

LINEUP NOTES:

  • The Rave Green are coming off a 2-1 win over Sporting Kansas City on October 1. The result extended Seattle's unbeaten streak to eight matches (3-0-5).
  • Striker Dejan Joveljić returns to the starting lineup after missing the previous three matches while away with Serbia on international duty. 
  • Center back Yeimar makes his first start since March 7 after dealing with injuries. 
  • Homegrown Player Sebastian Gomez makes his fifth MLS start of the campaign. The 20-year-old winger has tallied two goals and four assists from 783 minutes of action in his debut season with the First Team.
