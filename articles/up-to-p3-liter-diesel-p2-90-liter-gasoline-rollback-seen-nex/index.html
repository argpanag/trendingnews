Up to P3/liter diesel, P2.90/liter gasoline rollback seen next week

TheTools · 22.11.2026 10:59 · source · permalink

Motorists should expect a sigh of relief next week as another round of pump price rollback might be implemented.

Citing Mean of Platts Singapore trading and foreign exchange averages during the November 16 to 20 week, an oil industry source said fuel price reductions could be as follows:

  • Diesel - P2.70 to P3.00 per liter
  • Gasoline - P2.60 to P2.90 per liter

The source noted that ocean freight and insurance costs are not included in the estimated price adjustments for the coming week.

“Gasoline eased as increasing prompt supply coupled with the end of the peak demand moved the Asian gasoline market towards more balanced supply-demand fundamentals,” the source said.

“Diesel weakened amid stronger supply outlook, although physical supply risks remain,” the source added.

Oil companies announce official price adjustments every Monday, to be implemented on the following day.

This week, gasoline prices went down by P1.50 per liter while diesel and kerosene were slashed by P3.30 per liter and P5.80 per liter, respectively. —VAL, GMA News
