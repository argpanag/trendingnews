Bucks finally get a Tyler Herro scoring explosion, but it's still not enough

TheTools · 21.11.2026 22:30 · source · permalink

Taking on the biggest role of his NBA career, Tyler Herro was expected to reach new heights with the Milwaukee Bucks. For most of the season, that had not been the case, with his numbers worse than a few of his Miami seasons. Just when things were getting bleak, Herro delivered not just his best game in a Bucks jersey, but the best in his entire career. Hopefully it ignites something.

Herro lights it up against 76ers

Prior to Saturday's game, Herro had scored more than 30 points once this season, and it was almost a month ago. After a mediocre stretch, he didn't score 30 or 40; Herro went for 50.

The two guard had it going from the opening tip, finishing with 15 points in the first quarter to give the Bucks an early lead. Herro carried that momentum through the next two periods before really turning it on in the fourth. With the Bucks trailing, Herro once again tallied 15 points in the period, shooting an efficient 4-of-7 while knocking in all six of his free throw tries.

With the Milwaukee Bucks trailing by three as just a few seconds remained on the clock, Herro caught the ball and fired up a triple. In the biggest moment of his early Bucks tenure, he buried it. Overtime... almost. The ball hadn't entirely left Herro's fingertips yet, and the shot was waived off. Bucks lose. The ending was tough, especially with a terrible missed call, but Herro was certainly a bright spot.

Bucks need Herro to turn a corner

This is the Tyler Herro the Milwaukee Bucks need if they want to string together any sort of success. No, they shouldn't expect him to score 50 every night; it's more about the style of play. Herro played with incredible confidence and didn't force the shots. When he didn't have a great look, he wasn't shy about passing, finishing with seven assists in this tough loss.

It was also refreshing to see Herro knock down some shots in the clutch. Earlier this season, he had a stretch of poor performances late in games, and it was plaguing the Milwaukee Bucks. He didn't seal the deal, but still, he looked far better than a few weeks ago. The Bucks have been searching for their closer, and Herro remains their best bet.

Despite the loss, Herro's career game offers hope that he has finally found a groove. It took a while, but perhaps this was what he needed to flip that switch. The Milwaukee Bucks can only hope, at least.
