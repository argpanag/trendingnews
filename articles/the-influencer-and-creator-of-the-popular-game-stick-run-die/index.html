German influencer Manuel Otto, who created Stick Run at the age of 14, died at 30 after an incident during a stream.

Manuel Otto — a German influencer who created a popular game when he was only 14 years old — died after an incident during a livestream, UNN reports, citing TMZ.

A representative of the Stick Run game, which Manuel created, issued a statement. It mentions a video recording and says that Manuel "appears to have died by suicide."

The project’s representatives asked that the privacy of everyone involved be respected during this difficult time and also urged internet users not to spread unverified rumors.
