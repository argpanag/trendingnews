OpenAI Says Access To New Model's Advanced Cybersecurity Capabilities Will Be Restricted

TheTools · 03.09.2026 17:47 · source · permalink

Like this article? Keep independent journalism alive. Support HuffPost.

OpenAI on Tuesday said its newest model, Astra, is so advanced it "can find previously unknown security flaws and develop ways to exploit them across many well-protected systems without a person guiding each step," prompting the company to implement stronger safeguards for it in the wake of the Hugging Face incident.

"We plan to make Astra available soon, but access to its most advanced cybersecurity capabilities will be more limited," the company said. "Advanced cybersecurity work will initially be available to a group of testers."

This comes over a month after the AI giant revealed that two of its AI models hacked another AI company, Hugging Face.

"While Astra was not involved in the Hugging Face incident, we have incorporated our learnings from that incident into our safety approach," it said. "We have since implemented even stronger safeguards for Astra, including training the model to more reliably refuse harmful cyber requests and respect safety restrictions, additional protections against misuse, and monitoring that can stop potentially unauthorized activity."
