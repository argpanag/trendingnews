Princess Beatrice and Princess Eugenie are reportedly "reevaluating" their relationship with their mother, Sarah Ferguson. However, the sisters—whose father is the former Prince Andrew—aren't shutting her out completely.

Royal commentator Phil Dampier claims that Beatrice and Eugenie recently visited the one-time Duchess of York. "Friends tell me they have both been to see her at her new house in the past couple of weeks," he told Hello!

Princess Beatrice, Sarah Ferguson, and Princess Eugenie in 2023.
Credit: Getty Images

Dampier clarified: "I don't think they want to be seen with her in public at the moment, and she probably realizes it's not a good idea either."

This hasn't stopped Beatrice and Eugenie from "trying to be supportive" from "behind the scenes," as the three of them are still "talking."

Sarah Ferguson and Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor in 2019.
Credit: Getty Images

Last fall, Ferguson and her ex-husband, Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor, were officially forced to surrender their titles in light of their connections to sex offender Jeffrey Epstein. More recently, they were evicted from their longtime home at Royal Lodge, where they both lived for decades, even after divorcing.

Beatrice and Eugenie are "very embarrassed" as a result of the fallout, Dampier added. "They're obviously sort of reevaluating their own lives and their own contacts and the things they get involved with because they don't want to be tainted by scandal," he said.

Princess Eugenie and Princess Beatrice in 2022.
Credit: Getty

Despite remaining in contact with their mother, Beatrice and Eugenie's relationship with her won't ever be "as close as it used to be."

"I think the days of them being the tripod and inseparable have been fragmented," Dampier explained. "They have grown up and gone their own ways a bit, and they've got their separate lives and separate families."

Eugenie recently welcomed her third child with husband Jack Brooksbank, while Beatrice and her husband Edoardo Mapelli Mozzi share two daughters.
