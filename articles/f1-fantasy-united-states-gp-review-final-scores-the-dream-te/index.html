F1 Fantasy United States GP Review: Final Scores, The Dream Team, and Market Shifts

TheTools · 28.10.2026 05:07 · source · permalink

The F1 Fantasy United States GP 2026 points wrap-up saw Kimi Antonelli convert pole into another victory, but Charles Leclerc stole the fantasy spotlight with P2, fastest lap, and Driver of the Day. Ferrari delivered a huge haul, with Lewis Hamilton also finishing P4, while DNFs for Oliver Bearman, Franco Colapinto, and Valtteri Bottas wrecked plenty of lineups. Positions-gained and overtaking bonuses also played a major role, with Gabriel Bortoleto and Lance Stroll delivering surprising returns despite finishing outside the points.

Austin brought another round of price changes, with Nico Hulkenberg, Racing Bulls, and Haas among the biggest gainers. Leclerc, Antonelli, and Ferrari also continued their steady climbs, while Franco Colapinto, Gabriel Bortoleto, and Williams were among those losing the most value. Looking ahead to Mexico, Leclerc, Ferrari, and Racing Bulls appear well-positioned for further increases, with extremely favourable price thresholds. With the average constructor price continuing to climb and only a handful of races remaining, finding affordable drivers to accommodate premium constructors remains an important part of fantasy team building.
