Jessica Pegula vs Leylah Fernandez live streaming, TV channel, where & how to watch: US Open 2026

TheTools · 04.09.2026 17:37 · source · permalink

US Open

Pegula leads the head-to-head against Leylah Fernandez by 3-1

As the top-ranked American and a crowd favourite, Jessica Pegula shoulders massive expectations. Yet the American has looked untroubled in her opening two rounds, dropping only nine games so far.

The third seed has improved her serving and possesses one of the best return games currently on the WTA tour.

On the other end of the net will be Leylah Fernandez, who, like Pegula, has enjoyed a comfortable ride so far in New York.

With two former US Open finalists facing each other, this could prove to be a spicy contest. The American holds the advantage in the head-to-head, including a win in their most recent meeting at Miami this year.

When and where will Jessica Pegula vs Leylah Fernandez third-round match take place at US Open 2026?

World No. 3 Pegula takes on Leylah Fernandez in the third round of the 2026 US Open. The match is scheduled to take place on September 4 at the Arthur Ashe Stadium, with a tentative start time of 11:30 am local time (9 pm IST).

Where to watch live telecast and live streaming of Jessica Pegula vs Leylah Fernandez third-round match in India?

Fans in India can watch the Pegula vs Fernandez contest on Star Sports, which holds the rights to this year’s US Open. JioHotstar will stream the contest for Indian viewers.
