2026/27 Singapore Premier League Matchweek 3: Roundup – Singapore Premier League

TheTools · 16.10.2026 12:15 · source · permalink

SINGAPORE, 14 OCTOBER 2026 – Geylang International moved to the top of the Singapore Premier League (SPL) table with a win over FC Jurong, while Lion City Sailors secured their first league victory of the season in Head Coach Mark Jackson’s first SPL match in charge. Tampines Rovers also recorded a convincing win over Tanjong Pagar United, and two early goals helped Balestier Khalsa edge nine-man Hougang United 2-1. We recap all the action here…

Geylang International overcome Jurong to go top

Geylang 3-1 FC Jurong | Our Tampines Hub | 10 October
(Hassan Sunny 8’ (O.G.), Sahil Suhaimi 68’, Dejan Racic 78’| Akram Azman 4’ )

A second-half double from Sahil Suhaimi and Dejan Racic gave Geylang International a 3-1 win over FC Jurong at Our Tampines Hub, lifting the Eagles to the top of the Singapore Premier League table.

Akram Azman gave Jurong the lead in the fourth minute, converting Christopher van Huizen’s corner for his first goal for the club. Geylang levelled within five minutes from a corner of their own. Yang Hyeonju’s delivery was met by Racic, whose header was kept out by Hassan Sunny. However, the ball struck the post before rebounding off the goalkeeper and crossing the line.  Both sides created chances before the break, but neither could find a second goal. 

The hosts took control after the restart. Nazrul Nazari found Kaisei Ogawa, whose cross was converted by Sahil to make it 2-1 in the 68th minute. Racic sealed the win ten minutes later, dribbling past the Jurong defence in the box before finishing calmly.

Facts

  • Akram Azman scored his first goal for FC Jurong.
  • Geylang have won all three of their matches this season and lead the SPL on nine points, three clear of Jurong and Tampines.
  • Dejan Racic has now scored 26 SPL goals in 31 appearances.
  • Sahil Suhaimi has scored in successive league matches, ending a goal drought that stretched back to May 2025 in Matchweek 2.
  • Jurong’s winning run in the SPL ended at eight games, their first defeat since losing to Balestier in April earlier this year.

Sailors cruise past Young Lions in Jackson’s first league match

Sailors 6-1 Young Lions | Jalan Besar Stadium | 11 October
(Bart Ramselaar 14’, Hugo Gomes 37’, Bailey Wright 51’, Ilhan Fandi 69’, Matthias Phaeton 74’, 83’ | Toi Kagami 10’)

Lion City Sailors recovered from an early setback to beat the Young Lions 6-1 at Jalan Besar Stadium, securing their first league win of the 2026/27 season in Head Coach Mark Jackson’s first SPL match in charge.

Toi Kagami gave the Young Lions a shock lead with a long-range effort over Ivan Sušak in the tenth minute, but the Sailors equalised four minutes later. Bart Ramselaar, captaining the Sailors for the first time, drilled an effort into the top corner from the edge of the box. Hugo Gomes then headed in Giovanni Haag’s corner for his first goal for the club in the 37th minute, and Bailey Wright scored from the same source six minutes after half-time, his first goal since returning from injury.

Substitute Ilhan Fandi, playing against his brother Iryan, extended the lead to 4-1 after Matthias Phaëton squared Ramselaar’s flicked pass into his path. Moments later, the Guadeloupe international returned the favour, converting Ilhan’s header across goal with a finish off the underside of the crossbar. Phaëton scored again in the 83rd minute following good work down the left by Nur Adam Abdullah, and was denied a hat-trick in added time by Iryan Fandi’s goal-line clearance.

Facts:

  • Mark Jackson made eight changes from the side that lost in the ASEAN Club Championship in midweek, while Bart Ramselaar captained the Sailors for the first time.
  • Matthias Phaëton scored twice and provided two assists. 
  • Hugo Gomes opened his Sailors account, while Ilhan Fandi scored his first SPL goal since October 2022.
  • Toi Kagami’s goal was the Young Lions’ second of the season, following Hisatoshi Nishido’s strike in Matchweek 2.
  • The Young Lions have conceded 11 goals in their last two matches.
  • Nur Muhammad Asis made his first Sailors appearance since returning from loan spells in Portugal. Meanwhile,  17-year-old Kenneth Ng came on in goal for the injured Izwan Mahbud.

Stags strike four to stay in the hunt

Tanjong Pagar 0-4 Tampines | Jurong East Stadium | 11 October
(Hide Higashikawa 63’, Seiga Sumi 82’, Yuma Kimura 86’, Faris Ramli 90’+4)

Second-half goals from Hide Higashikawa, Yuma Kimura, Seiga Sumi and Faris Ramli sealed a 4-0 win for Tampines Rovers at Jurong East Stadium, despite the hosts creating the better chances before the break.

Boris Kopitovic, facing his former club, had two chances to break the deadlock. He fired wide from close range in the 32nd minute after good work from Riku Fukashiro, and seven minutes later saw a low shot from Taufik Suparno’s pass saved by Syazwan Buhari. Koya Kazama’s free kick then struck the crossbar for Tampines in the 42nd minute.

The Stags struck first in the 63rd minute when Higashikawa headed in Shah Shahiran’s cross at the far post. Substitute Kimura converted another Shah delivery four minutes from time, and Sumi struck into the roof of the net in the 82nd minute after being found by Irfan Najeeb. Substitute Faris Ramli completed the scoring in the fourth minute of added time.

Facts:

  • Tampines have won all seven of their most recent league meetings with Tanjong Pagar, scoring 22 goals and conceding one.
  • Shah Shahiran provided two assists, for Hide Higashikawa’s opener and Yuma Kimura’s goal.
  • Yuma Kimura and Faris Ramli both scored off the bench.
  • Tampines made six changes from the side that drew 2-2 away against Kuching City in the ASEAN Club Championship on 7 October.
  • Tampines have won both of their opening two matches and sit second on six points, level with Jurong and three behind Geylang, with a game in hand.
  • Tanjong Pagar have three points from three matches and sit sixth.

Balestier hold off nine-man Hougang for first win of season 

Hougang 1-2 Balestier | Bishan Stadium | 12 October
(Lucas Soares 30’ | Marin Kuzminski 8’, Jakov Katusa 11’)

Balestier Khalsa secured their first win of the 2026/27 SPL season with a 2-1 victory over Hougang United at Bishan Stadium, scoring twice in the opening 11 minutes before holding on against the nine-man hosts. 

The Tigers, who were without two suspended players, struck twice in the opening 11 minutes. Marin Kuzminski opened the scoring in the eighth minute after a run in behind, and Jakov Katusa doubled the lead three minutes later with a calm finish, set up by Kuzminski. Hougang, who made four changes after their 3-0 defeat to FC Jurong, pulled one back in the 30th minute when Lucas Soares met Aymen Kassimi’s delivery with a free header into the back of the net.

Hougang were reduced to ten men in the 42nd minute when Huzaifah Aziz was sent off for a foul.  Khairin Nadim then received a second yellow card in the 84th minute, reducing the Cheetahs to nine men, but they pushed for an equaliser. Arya Igami fired over as Hougang searched for an equaliser, but Balestier held firm to secure all three points. 

Facts:

  • Syafi Hilman played his first full SPL match for Balestier, while Woo Chun Wei started against his former club Hougang, as Head Coach Marko Kraljevic turned to younger players in the absence of two suspended players.
  • Balestier’s first win of the season followed defeats to Tampines and Tanjong Pagar in their opening two matches.
  • Marin Kuzminski scored once and assisted the other goal.
  • Hougang remained winless this season, with one point from three matches.
  • It was the second consecutive match at Bishan Stadium in which a team finished with nine men, after Balestier’s own dismissals against Tanjong Pagar in Matchweek 2.
