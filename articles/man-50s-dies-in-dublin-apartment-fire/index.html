Man, 50s, dies in Dublin apartment fire

TheTools · 04.10.2026 04:20 · source · permalink

A man in his 50s has died following a fire at an apartment complex in Dublin in the early hours of this morning.

Dublin Fire Brigade said it had received reports of a fire at an apartment in Mount Merrion at 1.23 am.

Emergency services attended the scene and the fire was brought under control at 1.46am.

The man was pronounced dead at the scene. His body has been removed to St Colmcille's Hospital Loughlinstown, where a post-mortem examination will take place.

No other injuries were reported.
