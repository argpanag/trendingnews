Bills QB Josh Allen’s wife is an award-winning actress and singer: Everything you need to know about Hailee Steinfield

TheTools · 17.09.2026 14:13 · source · permalink

Josh Allen is winning on and off the field. The Bills’ QB is got married back in 2025 and is trying to lead his team to the Super Bowl.

Buffalo Bills quarterback Josh Allen is growing up fast. The QB, now 30, is now a father and is married to singer and actress Hailee Steinfeld.

All about Hailee Steinfeld

Steinfeld got her big acting break at a young age when she played in the 2011 Coen brothers film “True Grit” at just 14 years old, a role that got her an Oscar nomination for Best Supporting Actress and earned her a Critics Choice Award for Best Young Performer.

View this post on Instagram

She later starred in the “Pitch Perfect” film series, which showed the world how talented she is as a singer as well. She performed an original song called “Flashlight”, which got her a deal with Republic Records. She released a single called “Love Myself” followed by her debut EP “Haiz” in 2015. She later released a series of successful singles and in 2020 released her second EP, “Half Written Story”.

View this post on Instagram

Her acting career has been even more successful than her musical one. In addition to her roles in “True Grit” and “Pitch Perfect”, Steinfeld has starred in (among others) “Ender’s Game” (2013), “Begin Again” (2013), “The Edge of Seventeen” (2016), and “Bumblebee” (2018), and was the voice of Gwen Stacy in “Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse”. In 2020, she became a part of the Marvel Cinematic Universe as the new Hawkeye. She’s signed a multiproject deal, ensuring that she will return to play Hawkeye in all future movies and shows.

View this post on Instagram

In May 2025, Steinfeld revealed that her Netflix series “Arcane: League of Legends” will release a second and final season in November. Steinfeld stars as Vi in the animated series inspired by Riot Games’ League of Legends online game.

Allen and Steinfeld’s relationship

The “it” couple got married on May 31, 2025.

The rumors of Allen and Steinfeld’s relationship began back in May 2023, when they were spotted together in New York and again later in Los Cabos, Mexico. Steinfeld was also spotted out shopping for Buffalo Bills’ gear in New York with Allen’s mother.

In July 2024, Allen finally made the relationship public and Instagram official when he posted a photo of them together in Paris, France.

View this post on Instagram

Steinfeld actually posted photos from that same trip back in April 2024, but the pictures were only of her, though it now seems quite likely Allen was the one who took the photos. Last month, Steinfeld debuted her newsletter, Beau Society. In her fifth issue, she actually spoke about the Paris trip with Allen. “What makes Paris Paris is its specificity,” she wrote. “I think about my most recent trip often. I was in the City of Love with my love (highly recommend).”

“Every morning we went on a hunt for the perfect chocolate croissant,” she added. “We went to random places and ordered a chocolate croissant and we’d rate them 1-10.”

Although Steinfeld attended almost all of the Bills games last season, she has tried her best to stay away from the cameras and keep a low profile. Now that the relationship is more public than before, however, that might become more difficult.

Even after Allen posted the photos, he still remained a bit private about the relationship. In 2024 in an interview with “Up & Adams” reporter Kay Adams, she referred to him as “Mr. Hard Launch”, to which he just said, “we love love”. Allen’s teammate, tight end Dalton Kincaid, was a little more direct, saying, “I think everybody’s kind of known about it. Hailee’s awesome.”
