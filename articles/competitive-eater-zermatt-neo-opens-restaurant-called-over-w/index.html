Competitive Eater Zermatt Neo Opens Restaurant Called Over With $469 Giant Burger

TheTools · 27.10.2026 14:15 · source · permalink

Singapore competitive eater and content creator Zermatt Neo is taking his appetite from the screen to the kitchen with his first restaurant, Over, opening on Wednesday (Oct 28) at Tras Street.

Known for his food challenges involving everything from massive bowls of mala noodles to dozens of burgers, Neo opened the restaurant in partnership with Singapore lifestyle and entertainment group The Wolfpack Investment Holdings.

The restaurant will serve local and western comfort food in three portion sizes: Lesser, for individual diners; Standard, for sharing; and Challenge, featuring the largest, Zermatt Neo-sized servings.

His favourite dishes are…

Menu items include Belly Good Time, a pork belly dish with shiso leaves and crispy chilli crunch, and Curry Me Over, a crispy chicken cutlet served with hom mali rice, Hainanese curry, nanban sauce and egg mayonnaise.

Neo tells 8days.sg that the standard portions are meant for two to four people. For example, his personal favourite, Somen Like You, comes in a lesser portion for $28 and a larger standard portion for $54. The dish features somen noodles with crispy chicken cutlet and nanban sauce.

For those dining solo, there’s also Wing Me Over, priced at $10 for six pieces. The standard portion comes with 18 pieces for $28.

“My parents are vegetarians”

There are vegetarian options too, something Neo wanted to include for his parents. “My parents are vegetarians, and I need to have something for them,” he says.

Neo recommends the Rolling Over Wakame ($10 for lesser; $18 for standard), crispy spring rolls served with wakame salad. Another option is Over BBB ($18 for lesser; $34 for standard), which he describes as a combination of achar (pickled vegetables) and bibimbap.

The drinks will only come in oversized servings, with no regular-sized options available.

Eat like Zermatt

Over will also offer an Ultimate Food Challenge featuring an 8kg platter that must be finished within one hour. Diners can attempt the challenge alone or in teams of up to four. Those who succeed will earn a place on the restaurant’s Hall of Fame, receive $1,000 in cash and get a refund for the challenge-sized dish.

Challenge-sized dishes include a mountain of fried chicken wings in house sauce called Wing Me Over ($239), a huge slab of pork belly named A Belly Good Time ($269) and the Over Jett burger ($469). The ginormous burger stars a 1.4kg bun stuffed with 3kg of beef ribs, fries, onion rings and — hilariously — seven smaller burgers. Each smaller burger contains beef cheek, fried chicken, onion rings, egg mayo and Jett’s signature sauce.

The challenge portions require seven days’ advance booking and a 50 percent deposit.

Beyond its regular menu, Over will introduce rotating collaborations with Singapore chefs and food businesses.

The first is with popular local barbecue brand Jett Barbecue, featuring the Over Jett burger ($28 for lesser; $54 for standard), stacked with smoked beef short rib, crispy onion rings, fried eggs and mayonnaise. The burger will be available in all three portion sizes from Oct 28 to Nov 30.

Neo is no stranger to Jett Barbecue’s delicious grub. In July, he took on an 8kg barbecue sandwich featuring beef ribs, beef cheeks, coleslaw and onion rings at Grilled Fest. Though the challenge was designed for a team of two, he finished the sandwich solo in 45 minutes.

“This might be my best-tasting official food challenge,” he said in a video of the feat shared on his socials.

Prefers eating with friends, not solo

"People always ask how I eat that much. Honestly, it's way more fun with friends," Neo said in a statement. "Over is the table I’ve always wanted: big plates, loud conversations, and a friendly fight over the last wing or spring roll."

Over opens on Oct 28 at 54 Tras St, S078993. Open daily 11.30am–12am. More info via Instagram.
