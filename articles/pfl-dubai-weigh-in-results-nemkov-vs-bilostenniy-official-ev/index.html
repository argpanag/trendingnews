PFL Dubai weigh-in results: Nemkov vs. Bilostenniy official, everyone on point

TheTools · 15.11.2026 16:15 · source · permalink

Official weigh-ins for PFL Dubai are in the books, and everyone made weight in the Middle East.

PFL heavyweight champion Vadim Nemkov (20-2) weighed in at 244.6 pounds for his first title defense, almost 20 pounds lighter than his opponent Sergey Bilostenniy (15-4), who weighed in at 262. Undefeated knockout artist Patrick Habirora was a tad under the welterweight mark at 169.8 pounds, with Egypt's Omar El Dafrawy weighing in at 170.8.

The weigh-ins took place at the PFL host hotel in Dubai. The nearby Coca-Cola Arena hosts Saturday's event (ESPN+).

The full PFL Dubai weigh-in results included:

MAIN CARD (ESPN+, 1 p.m. ET):

  • Vadim Nemkov (244.6) vs. Sergey Bilostenniy (262)
  • Patrick Habirora (169.8) vs. Omar El Dafrawy (170.8)
  • Oleg Popov (251.2) vs. Pouya Rahmani (245.1)
  • Joji Goto (135.4) vs. Sergio Pettis (135)
  • Lewis McGrillen (135.6) vs. Nawras Abzakh (135.8)

PRELIMINARY CARD (ESPN app, 9 a.m. ET):

  • Shem Rock (156) vs. Connor Hughes (155.6)
  • Adam Borics (145.8) vs. Khasan Magomedsharipov (146)
  • David Martinez (170.4) vs. Saygid Izagakhmaev (170)
  • Ernesto Rodriguez (170.2) vs. Rhys McKee (170.8)
  • Alexei Pergande (154.8) vs. Akhmed Magomedov (155.8)
  • Denis Goltsov (248.8) vs. Maxwell Djantou Nana (265)
  • Khabib Nabiev (205.4) vs. Styve Ngono (205.1)
  • Aleksandra Savicheva (115.2) vs. Jacinta Austin (116)
