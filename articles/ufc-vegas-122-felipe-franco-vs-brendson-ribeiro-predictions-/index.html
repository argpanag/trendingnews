UFC Vegas 122: Felipe Franco vs. Brendson Ribeiro Predictions & Odds

TheTools · 10.10.2026 22:20 · source · permalink

An exciting collision of light heavyweight contenders will be part of the UFC Vegas 122 card on Saturday, as Felipe Franco takes on Brendson Ribeiro inside the Meta APEX in Las Vegas.

Franco is fresh off a victory, which makes him the betting favorite heading into the bout with -375 UFC odds, while Ribeiro, who’s on a four-fight losing streak, will be the +300 betting underdog.

UFC Vegas 122: Felipe Franco vs. Brendson Ribeiro Fight Odds

  • Felipe Franco: -375
  • Brendson Ribeiro: +300

UFC Vegas 122: Felipe Franco vs. Brendson Ribeiro: Tale of the Tape

UFC Vegas 122: Felipe Franco vs. Brendson Ribeiro: Prediction

I predict a TKO/KO victory for Franco over Ribeiro because of the one-punch knockout that the former possesses. Franco is also a very explosive striker who uses his wide stance as leverage to throw a solid punch.

With Ribeiro being the longer fighter, expect Franco to apply pressure and push him against the cage to negate that edge. This strategy will also help Franco shut down Ribeiro’s kicking game because he won’t have enough power to throw.

Finally, Ribeiro’s durability and chin have been compromised against power punchers like Franco, which is why I expect him to swing early to try and finish the fight. But Franco should be economical and smart with his shot selection because he doesn’t want to tire himself out.

Felipe Franco’s Recent Fight Results

Franco was a top Brazilian prospect when the UFC signed him earlier this year. He quickly competed in two bouts inside the octagon, losing by decision to Mario Pinto and defeating Levi Rodrigues Jr. by second-round stoppage.

Brendson Ribeiro’s Recent Fight Results

Ribeiro was the former Shooto Brazil champion before he joined Dana White’s Contender Series in 2023 and earned a UFC contract. But he has only recorded one win in his last five outings, with a victory over Diyar Nurgozhay and defeats to Azamat Murzakanov, Oumar Sy, Abdul-Rakhman Yahkyaev, and Magomed Tuchalov.
