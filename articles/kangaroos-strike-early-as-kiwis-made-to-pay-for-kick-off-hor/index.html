Kiwis off to horror start

The Kiwis have botched the opening minutes of the World Cup decider – and it all started with the kick-off.

Jamayne Isaako booted the kick-off over the dead-ball line on the full, the Kangaroos marched up the other end, and Liam Martin crashed over for the first try.

The gun back-rower stormed onto a Harry Grant ball and bashed through three defenders to slam the ball down, scoring less than 90 seconds into the match.

The Kangaroos lead 6-0 after a successful Cameron Munster kick.

Walker’s secret meeting while in Kangaroos camp

Roosters premiership winner Sam Walker has met with the Tricolours’ arch-rivals, the Rabbitohs, while on Kangaroos duties in Brisbane, as per The Sydney Morning Herald.

All eyes have been on Walker’s footsteps heading into the 2027 pre-season after it was revealed the Roosters were prepared to get him off their books immediately after the club’s contract extension talks with the star playmaker fell apart.

South Sydney quickly emerged as a possible landing site for the 24-year-old, with the two parties meeting while Walker was in Kangaroos camp to discuss that option.

South Sydney officials have met with Roosters and Australia half Sam Walker regarding his future in the NRL. Getty Images

While the Rabbitohs are open to taking on Walker as soon as next season, the club does not have the salary cap space to meet his $1 million-per-season contract hopes.

The Rabbitohs will be able to make some financial room in 2028 when a number of their stars come off contract, including Latrell Mitchell and Jack Wighton.

The pair have not been asked to make way immediately to open the door for Walker’s possible arrival.

Where’s Latrell?

Kangaroos super sub Latrell Mitchell has been ruled out of the Rugby League World Cup final due to illness.

The South Sydney superstar has been replaced on Australia’s six-man bench for Sunday night’s final against New Zealand at Suncorp Stadium by Roosters champion James Tedesco.

Mitchell did not take part in the team’s captain’s run at Suncorp Stadium on Saturday morning.

Unstoppable superstar leads Jillaroos to clinical World Cup triumph

Superstar outside back Jaime Chapman bagged a hat-trick in a devastating attacking display to lead the Jillaroos to a 34-10 victory over the Kiwi Ferns in the Rugby League World Cup final on Sunday.

There was little New Zealand could do to stop Chapman as she ran amok on the left wing at Suncorp Stadium in an Australian win that sealed an undefeated tournament run.

The triumph marks the Jillaroos’ fourth consecutive World Cup title – a dominant run that kicked off in 2013.
