National Lottery results with Thunderball on Wednesday, September 30

TheTools · 30.09.2026 19:46 · source · permalink

A tempting jackpot of £2m is up for grabs for Lotto ticket-holders in tonight's National Lottery draw.

The draws changed earlier this year, with the National Lottery explaining: "New Lotto is here and it's twice as exciting. Each Wednesday and Saturday Lotto draw will feature two rounds.

"That means two chances of winning a prize with every line of numbers you play, for the same price. And best of all, more than double the number of millionaires are expected to be made."

Matching all six numbers in either round will result in a jackpot win as before - but the jackpot will be shared across both rounds. That potentially means although there is more chance of winning, there is also more chance of having to share.

The National Lottery says the change will "make more winners and millionaires than before".

On Saturday (September 26), someone won the £5.8m jackpot and three new millionaires were made.

We bring you the crucial winning Lotto numbers below as well as the results for the Thunderball.

Today's winning Lotto numbers:

The winning Lotto numbers are:

Round 1: 18, 26, 48, 49, 51, 53 Bonus ball: 33

Round 2: 25, 52, 53, 54, 55, 57 Bonus ball: 26

Tonight's winning Thunderball numbers revealed

The winning Thunderball numbers are: 19, 24, 29, 33, 36 and the Thunderball: 4

You can buy a ticket throughout the week, but on draw days - Saturday and Wednesday - until 7.30pm.

And if you want more games to play, there is also EuroMillions every Tuesday and Friday and the Set for Life game on Mondays and Thursdays.

While the Thunderball draw takes place every Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday, with a £500,000 top prize.

Playing the lottery also raises millions of pounds for good causes.

Good luck!
