Who Is Victor Munoz? Liverpool's £34.5m Winger From Barca and Real Madrid

TheTools · 17.10.2026 16:14 · source · permalink

Victor Munoz, who started at Brentford on Saturday, joined Liverpool from Osasuna for a EUR40m (£34.5m) release clause in June after coming through at Barcelona and Real Madrid. Here is his age, position, fee, contract and last season's stats.

Victor Munoz is the Liverpool winger who started at Brentford on Saturday 17 October, four months after joining from Osasuna on a €40m (£34.5m) release clause. He came through the academies at both Barcelona and Real Madrid, which makes his route to Anfield an unusual one.

How much did Liverpool pay for Victor Munoz?

Liverpool triggered a €40m release clause, which Sky Sports put at £34.5m, to be paid in two instalments. He signed a six-year contract when the move was confirmed in June 2026.

Real Madrid also come into it. When he left the Bernabeu for Osasuna in 2025, Madrid kept 50% of his rights for any future sale, according to TransferFeed. Neither club has confirmed how Liverpool’s fee was split.

Age and background

Munoz was born on 13 July 2003, in Barcelona according to Osasuna. That makes him 23, even though some June reports had him at 22.

He was a youth player at Barcelona and then at Real Madrid. Carlo Ancelotti gave him his LaLiga debut for Madrid in May 2025, sending him on for Vinicius Junior against Barcelona, his old club, according to Sky Sports. He joined Osasuna that summer and spent one season there before Liverpool came in.

What position does Victor Munoz play?

He’s a pacey winger who plays mainly on the left, and Sky say he can also play on the right or through the middle. That flexibility suits a Liverpool front line that has Isak, Wirtz and Barcola competing for the same spaces.

Victor Munoz stats at Osasuna

Last season he played 34 league games for Osasuna and scored 6 goals with 2 assists. That’s a goal every five or six games from a wide player in a side not built to dominate possession. It’s a decent return, not a startling one, and it’s all we’d lean on until he has a proper run in a red shirt.

His Liverpool start so far

The line-up at Brentford, confirmed on liverpoolfc.com, had Munoz in the starting XI alongside Wirtz, Isak and Barcola, with Gakpo on the bench. We haven’t checked his Liverpool goals and assists, so we’re not quoting any here.

Our view: he should keep the place. A £34.5m signing needs minutes to settle, and Gakpo has had years of them. We’d rather see what Munoz does with a run of games, but plenty will disagree.
