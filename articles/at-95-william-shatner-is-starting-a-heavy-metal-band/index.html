At 95, William Shatner is starting a heavy metal band

TheTools · 18.09.2026 23:58 · source · permalink

William Shatner is ready to rage.

Shatner, 95, will debut his new heavy metal band, The *uckers, at Chicago's Riot Fest on Sunday, Sept. 20. The band takes the Rebel Stage at Douglass Park between 5:25-6 p.m. local time.

"I decided you're such big fans of mine, that I'm going to debut my heavy metal record at Riot Fest and I hope you're there and I hope I'm there," Shatner said with a smile in a video promoting his performance, shared by Riot Fest.

Shatner, best known for his role as Captain Kirk in the "Star Trek" franchise, isn't new to the music industry. Over the years, Shatner has released spoken-word, rock and blues albums, beginning with his debut album, "The Transformed Man" in 1968. He released his most recent spoken-word album, "Love & Other Mysteries," in collaboration with country music star Brad Paisley, in August.

In 2023, Shatner was diagnosed with Stage 4 malignant melanoma. The cancer spread to his lungs and brain, but after two years of immunotherapy and surgery, he told USA TODAY he is cancer free.

Kicking off on Friday, Sept. 18, Riot Fest features headliners including Twenty One Pilots, Iggy Pop, Pixies, Tool, Pierce the Veil and Elvis Costello. Tickets are available on the Riot Fest website, starting at $130 for one-day passes and $360 for three-day passes.

Who makes up The *uckers?

Joining Shatner is guitarist Marcus Nand, guitarist and bassist Phil Soussan (Ozzy Osbourne, Last in Line, Vince Neil Band, Big Noize), guitarist Britt Lightning (Vixen) and drummer Fred Aching.

What is The *uckers upcoming album? When does it release?

The *uckers debut album is "What the F Is Heavy Metal." The album did not have a release date, as of Sept. 14.

Why did William Shatner go metal?

In an interview with former Vice News correspondent Michael Moynihan, Shatner said it was his label, Cleopatra Records, that brought up the idea of a heavy metal album.

"I said, 'I don't know what to do.' How do you do heavy metal? How do you do rage against the dying of the light? How do you do that to music?" Shatner said during "The Moynihan Report" interview. "I thought, 'Heavy metal has got to be about ... passionate emotions. The music is a distillation of emotion."

During an interview with CNN anchor Elex Michaelson in July, Shatner also said he would be playing half-an-hour at the music festival "in the desert" in spring 2027, which Michaelson confirmed to be Coachella. As of Sept. 14, Coachella had yet to release its lineup for its festivals in April 2027.
