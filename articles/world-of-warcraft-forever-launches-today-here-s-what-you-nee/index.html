‘World of Warcraft: Forever’ launches today. Here’s what you need to know. - The Boston Globe

TheTools · 04.11.2026 23:28 · source · permalink

Gamers, it’s time to return to Azeroth with the highly anticipated release of “World of Warcraft: Forever.” As newcomers and longtime fans of the franchise prepare to play, here’s what you need to know about the game.

What is ‘World of Warcraft: Forever’?

Like Blizzard Entertainment’s long-running mainline game and its “Classic” spinoff, “Forever” is a massively multiplayer online role-playing game (MMORPG) set in the fictional world of Azeroth. Playing as elves, orcs, dwarves, and more, users create their own unique high fantasy character, often teaming up with other players online before embarking on various quests and battles against enemy factions.

What’s new about the game?

According to a press release, “Forever” serves as “a new beginning” for the franchise, reimagining Azeroth with updated graphics and “revamped gameplay, all rooted in authenticity to the original WoW experience.” Users will have the option to choose between classic and enhanced settings, with the latter providing options like quest guidance and a single-bag view for item inventory. Players must choose between various rulesets in “Forever,” as well, instead of selecting a specific realm, with offerings ranging from normal and roleplay to player vs. player options (a “hardcore” mode is in the works for after the game’s launch).

Giving the original game a bit of a makeover, “Forever” will have new areas to roam and explore as players level up their characters, promising over 1,000 new quests and nine new dungeons. “Forever” also introduces Skyborne elves, a new race that players can select for their characters and can join either of the two available factions: Alliance or Horde. In addition to the new elves, users can try out new race-and-class combos ranging from orc mages and troll warlocks to gnome priests and undead paladins.

When does ‘Forever’ release?

For fans in Boston and on the East Coast, “Forever” will go live at 6 p.m. on Wednesday, Nov. 4.

How can I play it?

In order to play “Forever,” users must first create an account on Battle.net and have an active “WoW” subscription or purchase game time. Players must also download Battle.net to their computers and sign in before installing “WoW.” Once installed, players can select “Forever” once the game is live. Available for both Widows and Mac users, players can enjoy “Forever” with the traditional keyboard and mouse set-up, or via a gamepad.
