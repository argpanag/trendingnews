Planned Power Outage in Thayer Monday - Ozark Radio News

TheTools · 11.10.2026 23:47 · source · permalink
  • Local News

Residents in Thayer will experience a planned power outage Monday as city electric crews complete upgrade work. According to a public notice, power will be shut off beginning at 8 a.m. and will remain out throughout the day for customers in the first block of Front Street, Market, Second, Chestnut, and the alley behind the Post Office.

City officials say the outage is necessary to complete system improvements and apologized for the inconvenience, thanking residents for their patience and understanding during the upgrade work.
