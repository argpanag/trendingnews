Champions League - Game Week 5
26 Nov 2026 - 02:00
Parc des Princes
PSG host Roma at Paris's Parc des Princes in a Champions League clash between the table leaders and a side chasing valuable points.

The reigning European champions have won 12 straight matches in all competitions. Most recently, Luis Enrique's side came from behind at Nice to win 3-2, with Ferran Torres scoring five minutes from time.

That win on the French Riviera came at a heavy cost. Desire Doue tore the anterior cruciate ligament in his right knee and will undergo surgery by the end of the month. Khvicha Kvaratskhelia has also been sidelined with the same injury since the October match against Barcelona.

Gian Piero Gasperini's Roma are flying in the league. Donyell Malen scored four times in a 5-1 win over Parma that moved them to within two points of leaders Lazio.

Europe has been less straightforward. A 3-2 defeat to Manchester United in their last Champions League outing has left Roma looking to improve their position in the table when they visit Paris.

PSG scored six against Villarreal in their previous European home game, so Gasperini faces a tough task. He must contain an attack that still has plenty of quality despite its absentees.
