Major Barcelona deal: Raphinha to stay in Catalonia until 2030 — TodayPress.tv

TheTools · 06.10.2026 15:01 · source · permalink

Real, Reliable, Objective

Barcelona winger Raphinha has signed a new contract with the club.

TodayPress TV reports that renowned football journalist Fabrizio Romano announced this on the social media platform X.

According to the source, the new contract will run until 2030. An official announcement of the agreement is expected to be made later this week.

The 30-year-old Brazilian has represented the Catalan club since 2022. During his time at Barcelona, he has won the Spanish championship three times, as well as the Copa del Rey and the Spanish Super Cup three times.

The experienced winger previously played for English club Leeds United, French side Rennes, and Portuguese clubs Sporting CP and Vitória Guimarães.
