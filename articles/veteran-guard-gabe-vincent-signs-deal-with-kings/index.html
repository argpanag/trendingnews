Veteran guard Gabe Vincent signs deal with Kings

TheTools · 26.11.2026 04:51 · source · permalink

Guard Gabe Vincent signed a deal with the Sacramento Kings to fill their 15th roster spot, it was announced on Monday.

Terms were not disclosed.

The Kings have been in the market for backcourt depth after veteran Ben Simmons tore his Achilles in the preseason, leaving them without requisite help behind lottery pick Darius Acuff Jr., who is averaging an NBA-high 36.9 minutes per game.

Vincent, 30, played a combined 53 games for the Los Angeles Lakers and Atlanta Hawks last season, averaging 4.4 points and making 34.3% of his 3-point attempts in 16.6 minutes per game.

After two extended losing streaks in the opening month, the Kings sit at the bottom of the Western Conference standings with a 2-14 record, though they did upset the Boston Celtics 97-96 on Saturday to become the final NBA team to get a home win. In it, Acuff scored 23 points in 40 minutes. The Kings outscored the Celtics by 16 points when he was on the floor and were outscored by 15 in the eight minutes he was off it.

Vincent has been signed, in part, to help in those non-Acuff minutes and provide some needed perimeter spacing. The Kings rank in the bottom 10 in the league in 3-point attempts and percentage. Vincent has made 459 career 3s.
