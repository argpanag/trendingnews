GTA VI Festival 2026 Lineup: Travis Scott, Cardi B, Justice and More

Grand Theft Auto VI is getting its own real-world music festival, with Travis Scott, Cardi B, Metro Boomin and Justice among the biggest artists confirmed.

Rockstar Games and PlayStation are presenting Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival on Saturday 21 November 2026 at Mana Wynwood in Miami.

The one-day event celebrates the release of GTA VI, bringing together artists associated with the game’s music and official soundtrack.

The lineup was announced on 9 November, giving fans a completely new way to experience the musical world of Vice City. Rockstar Games

Who is playing GTA VI: The Festival?

The confirmed lineup includes a Cactus Jack presentation featuring Travis Scott, Don Toliver, Kodak Black and SoFaygo.

Cardi B, CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, Justice performing a DJ set, Metro Boomin, Tainy and Rauw & Friends are also appearing.

Several special back-to-back performances have been announced.

Nick León will play B2B with DJ Python, while Prospa joins Skream for another collaborative set.

The Dare and Fcukers will also perform together, with Suero completing the announced programme.

Travis Scott and Cactus Jack lead the lineup

Travis Scott provides one of the biggest attractions.

The American rapper has become internationally recognised for his atmospheric production, high-energy performances and collaborations with numerous major artists.

Rather than appearing alone, Scott is part of a Cactus Jack presentation alongside Don Toliver, Kodak Black and SoFaygo.

That makes the booking closer to a collective performance than a conventional solo festival set.

Justice, Metro Boomin and the sound of GTA VI

Electronic duo Justice will deliver a DJ set.

Their appearance is particularly interesting because the French group are also connected with GTA VI’s original music.

Metro Boomin brings a different side of contemporary music production, having worked extensively with some of hip-hop’s biggest performers.

Tainy and Rauw & Friends provide a major Latin music presence, while the local electronic bookings connect the festival with Miami’s club scene.

Why Miami?

GTA VI’s fictional Vice City draws heavily from Miami, making the real city a natural location for the launch celebration.

The event is being produced by the creators of III Points, another major Miami music festival.

Performances will take place across two stages, combining hip-hop, Latin music and electronic sounds. Rockstar Games

When is the GTA VI Festival?

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival takes place on Saturday 21 November 2026 at Mana Wynwood in Miami, Florida.

Entry is restricted to attendees aged 17 and over. The original ticket lottery has closed, although an official waitlist is available.

This is a particularly unusual festival because it connects a major video game release with a live music event.

For fans of GTA, Travis Scott, Cardi B and electronic music, it offers one of the most distinctive live events remaining in 2026.

Official details: Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival
