North Carolina midterm election guide: Find out when polls open, where to vote, what's on the ballot

TheTools · 02.11.2026 18:30 · source · permalink

RALEIGH, N.C. (WTVD) -- Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3. Polls across North Carolina will be open from 6:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. Voters who are in line by 7:30 p.m. will still be allowed to cast a ballot.

Voters must vote at their assigned polling place on Election Day.

Use the state's voter search tool to find your polling location and review your voter information before heading out.

You can also find your party affiliation, absentee ballot information and more.

North Carolina voters will be asked to show a photo ID when checking in to vote. Learn more about acceptable identification on the North Carolina voter ID page.

Absentee ballots must be received by county boards of elections by 7:30 p.m. on Election Day. Military and overseas voters may have different deadlines.

Voters who qualify for assistance may receive help at their polling place under North Carolina law. Curbside voting is also available for eligible voters who cannot enter the polling place because of age or a physical disability.
