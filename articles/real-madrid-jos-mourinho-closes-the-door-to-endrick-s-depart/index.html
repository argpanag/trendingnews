Real Madrid: José Mourinho closes the door to Endrick's departure

TheTools · 13.10.2026 19:03 · source · permalink

With a very small appearance (4 minutes) since this summer, Endrick is experiencing a difficult start to the season with Real Madrid. He was certainly injured but the young Brazilian is especially far in the hierarchy behind Mbappé and Carlos Espi. Several clubs have already expressed interest in recruiting him this summer. AC Milan would even be the favorite to welcome him in January.

We still need to convince Real Madrid to repeat an operation similar to that of winter 2026, where the striker was loaned to OL during the second part of the season. “Forget it! There’s no chance.
