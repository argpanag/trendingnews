Liverpool's dream January window with £58m hijack and Chelsea raided for defender - AOL

TheTools · 15.11.2026 09:01 · source · permalink

Liverpool are within touching distance of the Premier League's summit after their opening 10 fixtures, but some Reds supporters will want moves to be made in January if they harbour ambitions of competing for the title. Andoni Iraola's tenure at Anfield has started well enough. His squad have suffered defeat in just three of their 16 fixtures and currently sit fourth in the Premier League with 18 points. They trail Manchester City in third by three points, sit four behind Brighton in second, but remain nine adrift of table-toppers Arsenal.

Several positions are in need of reinforcement at Liverpool, with Milos Kerkez failing to persuade at left-back and Jeremie Frimpong potentially considering an exit at right-back.

A more defensively-orientated midfielder is also required, regardless of Trey Nyoni's emergence.

With that in mind, here's a look what an ideal January transfer window might look like for Liverpool and two targets they would be wise to pursue...

£58m move hijacked

Nyoni has impressed when given the chance this season, but he remains a teenager and cannot yet be depended upon to guarantee trophies.

As such, the Reds will need a more disruptive, defensively-minded midfielder capable of functioning in Iraola's pivot.

Currently, Alexis Mac Allister, Dominik Szoboszlai and Ryan Gravenberch are rotated amongst themselves.

Yet when Mac Allister and Szoboszlai are paired together, the midfield is deficient in ball recovery and middle-third tackling during open encounters.

Gravenberch, while an exceptional talent, has not yet fully mastered the discipline of aggressive ball-winning, either.

That's precisely where Lamine Camara excels. The Monaco midfielder ranks amongst Europe's elite for ball recoveries, interceptions and middle-third tackling.

Chelsea were poised to sign Camara in the summer for £47million before Monaco abandoned the deal, and his performances have only improved since.

Reports indicate around £58m would be sufficient to complete his signing, and the Reds could attempt to raise the funds and scupper the move should Chelsea decide to return to the table.

Chelsea raided for defender

Speaking of Chelsea, they may hold the answer to Liverpool's defensive vulnerabilities. As matters stand, the left-back role remains a worry.

Liverpool would benefit from a versatile, energetic full-back capable of operating in both positions and available on a risk-free loan arrangement. Malo Gusto could be that individual.

The Frenchman's involvement has plummeted under Xabi Alonso and he has featured for less than 500 minutes so far this campaign.

It has been suggested that Gusto is looking for a departure and is priced at £67m.

He represents an ideal match for Iraola's philosophy. His recovery pace is outstanding, he excels at inverting and operates as an attack-minded defender, but that price tag would constitute quite the punt.

Liverpool have previously invested heavily in that position, and should Gusto fail to deliver, that could prove problematic.

However, if the Reds could persuade Chelsea to release him on loan with an option to buy, as they sought to arrange on deadline day, it would represent a favourable outcome for both clubs.
