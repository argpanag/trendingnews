NBC reporter, pilot among the victims identified in deadly Los Angeles helicopter crash

TheTools · 18.09.2026 14:51 · source · permalink

NTSB and FAA investigators are heading to the Los Angeles crash site to probe what caused the NewsChopper4 to lose altitude

3 dead after helicopter crash in Los Angeles

WARNING: Graphic footage—Investigators are working on the site of a helicopter crash in the Chatsworth neighborhood of Los Angeles, Cali.

Two victims of a deadly Tuesday night helicopter crash in Los Angeles have been identified as journalist Eliana Moreno and pilot George Marciniw, NBC4 Los Angeles confirmed.

Moreno and Marciniw were employees of Angel City Air, a helicopter charter company that operated NBC Los Angeles' NewsChopper4, NBC4 reported.

The pair were covering a deadly bus crash in downtown Los Angeles when the helicopter, which NBC4 confirmed belonged to the network, lost altitude and crashed, killing a third person on the ground who has yet to be identified.

Moreno had been a stalwart of both NBC4 and Telemundo 52's aerial coverage for years, going by the moniker "Eli in the Heli."

She had worked closely with Marciniw since 2023, with the pair sharing social media photos of them working and dining together through the years.

Marciniw was, according to NBC4, a Southern California native who graduated from Burbank High School in 1974.

Eliana Moreno and George Marciniw pose together in front of NewsChopper4. (Facebook/George Marciniw)

"We are deeply saddened to share that a helicopter providing aerial news coverage for NBC4 and Telemundo 52 was involved in a fatal accident in Los Angeles on Tuesday evening. We extend our heartfelt condolences to the families, friends and colleagues of those who lost their lives," NBC4 and Telemundo 52 said in a statement.

The chopper crashed between two buildings in Los Angeles' Chatsworth neighborhood, producing a large fireball and injuring a fourth person.

At least three people were killed when a helicopter crashed not far from a deadly bus crash in Los Angeles. (KABC)

The injured person was transported to a local hospital in unknown condition, Los Angeles Fire Department Capt. Branden Silverman told reporters.

"Our hearts are with the victims and families of tonight’s devastating Metro bus and helicopter crashes in Chatsworth, and with the entire NBC4 family," California Gov. Gavin Newsom wrote in a statement on X.

"Thank you to the first responders working tirelessly tonight," he added.

The National Transportation Safety Bureau (NTSB) and the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) said they will be sending investigators to the crash site on Wednesday to probe the crash's cause.
