Royal Caribbean cruise passenger who went overboard rescued after she spent 20 hours at sea, Greek coast guard says

TheTools · 18.10.2026 05:33 · source · permalink

Updated on: October 14, 2026 / 9:51 AM EDT / CBS/AFP

A woman who allegedly jumped off a cruise ship into the Ionian Sea was rescued over the weekend after 20 hours in the water, the Greek coast guard said.

The incident occurred early on Friday when the Royal Caribbean Cruises ship Odyssey of the Seas was sailing southwest of the island of Zakynthos, the coast guard said.

The 44-year-old Salvadorian woman subsequently told her rescuers she had jumped because "she wanted to disembark," a coast guard spokesperson told AFP.

"She was 20 hours at sea," the officer said.

After an alert went out early on Friday, two coast guard boats and a navy helicopter were dispatched to the area and other nearby ships were rerouted.

The Odyssey of the Seas also joined the search, the officer said.

A passing freighter eventually located the woman, fully conscious, in a life ring early on Saturday and she was picked up by a Greek coast guard patrol boat.

A video released by the coast guard shows a crew throwing a rope to a woman floating inside an orange life ring in the water in the dark.

She was taken to the port of Katakolo and then to hospital in the nearby city of Pyrgos for health tests that include a psychiatric evaluation ordered by a local prosecutor, the officer said.

According to Royal Caribbean, Odyssey of the Seas is 1,138 feet long and can accommodate 4,198 guests. The ship offers seasonal cruises to the Greek Isles.

Last year, a man jumped off a Royal Caribbean cruise ship near the Port of San Juan — allegedly to avoid paying off more than $16,000 in gambling debt to the cruise line. Jey Gonzalez-Diaz jumped into the water as the ship disembarked, during U.S. Customs and Border Protection inspection, according to a criminal complaint. He was then brought to shore by someone passing by on a jet ski, and later charged with attempting to avoid monetary reporting requirements when traveling into the U.S.
