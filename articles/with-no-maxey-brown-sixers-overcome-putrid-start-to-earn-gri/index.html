With no Maxey, Brown, Sixers overcome putrid start to earn gritty win

TheTools · 19.11.2026 02:36 · source · permalink
  • Sixers Scores

Tyrese Maxey was a late scratch, Jaylen Brown was out and the Sixers were down 17, but they found a way to move to 13-3 with a win in Orlando.

The Sixers overcame their leading scorer being scratched late and a 17-point deficit to defeat the Orlando Magic 121-116 Wednesday night.

Joel Embiid led the Sixers with 26 points along with six rebounds and seven assists, shooting 7-of-18 from the floor. LeBron James went for 22 points, three rebounds and six assists on 8-of-18 shooting.

VJ Edgecombe excelled with a bigger workload, finishing with a season-high 24 points, going 8-of-15 from the floor, along with eight rebounds and six assists. Franz Wagner led all scorers with 27.

Tyrese Maxey was ruled out right before gametime with soreness in his right foot. Jaylen Brown and Dean Wade were both out for the Sixers with hamstring strains, along with Adem Bona and Dominick Barlow. Paolo Banchero, Jonathan Isaac and Noah Penda were out for Orlando.

Here are a few takeaways from the win.

First Quarter

  • Orlando’s first six shots of the game were from behind the arc, and they made three of them before making their first three two-pointers of the night. LeBron opened the game by burying a three and set up Embiid for an easy layup. Edgecombe was able to get to the line trying to create a fast break, but threw a bad pass that turned into a turnover a few moments later.
  • Not only did the Sixers go nearly five minutes without a field goal after that Embiid layup, but the big fella came up limping a bit after getting fouled by Wendell Carter Jr.. He also bumped into Goga Bitadze on the play, but didn’t leave the game.
  • The Sixers were also not giving Orlando any type of resistance. The Magic made 11 of their first 14 field goals of the game. They haven’t looked this bad in healthier lineups, but the Magic seemed pretty happy to pick on combo of Anfernee Simons and Labaron Philon Jr. on the perimeter.
  • On the other end, Philon did make his first three shots of the night and ran down a pass that nearly sailed out of bounds to save a fast break. They didn’t come up with any answers for a Magic team that shot 69% from the field in the first quarter, getting out to a 13-point lead over Philly.

Second Quarter

  • Tacko Fall did check into the game late into the first for a near six-minute shift. It feels like the Sixers almost have to sit in a zone when he’s out there. So far, he’s presented a challenging and different enough look to alter shots near him, he also caught a lob from LeBron. His mobility really doesn’t help the team’s defensive rebounding, but being able to swat a shot was the most resistance the defense had to that point.
  • Nick Nurse continued to try out his two-way guys, subbing in Caleb Love early in the second. His first shot of the night was a three that bounced off the rim and backboard before falling in. It took a bit to fall, but it helped the Sixers climb back as LeBron had begun to carry the offense forward.
  • The Magic had successfully squashed the momentum of a 9-0 Sixers run, then Desmond Bane decided to grab Embiid’s crotch for some reason as he fought through Embiid’s screen. Despite a review, the play was still deemed a common foul.
  • There was another scary moment where LeBron appeared to slip on a wet spot on the floor and immediately came up favoring his groin area. The Sixers immediately called timeout, but James remained in the game.
  • After that timeout Embiid started to shake off his slow start to the night. He worked a two-man game to get Simons a three before going to work himself in the post. He cleaned up his own miss and drew a foul in the process, then filled the paint to give Edgecombe an easy assist. He picked up two blocks on the other end, one of which had to be overturned by a challenge. They capitalized on the stop as Edgecombe drilled another pull-up at the buzzer to pull the Sixers within five at the break.

Third Quarter

  • Thanks to LeBron turning the ball over and getting his shot blocked, the Magic easily pushed their lead back up to double digits. The Sixers called a quick timeout, mostly to give Edgecombe a breather after getting hit in the eye. The stoppage worked to their favor as they picked up a couple buckets and forced a turnover coming out of the timeout.
  • Wagner was able to exploit some weak points, especially as they continued to sit in a zone, but every Sixer on the floor had dramatically turned around their defensive effort. They’ve looked pretty good at scrambling and rotating over to help, which is impressive given how many new parts there are.
  • After going scoreless in Brooklyn, Kentavious Caldwell-Pope had a much easier time finding the basket, making three of his first four shots on the night. Embiid knocked down a fadeaway jumper and drew a foul on a drive to help the Sixers keep slowly crawling back.
  • All quarter the Sixers were able to get within a basket, but weren’t able to put together a stop and a score that would give themselves the lead. They only had given up seven offensive rebounds, but that turned into 18 second-chance points for the Magic. The Sixers got three chances of their own to close the quarter, but Embiid got swung a grenade he couldn’t get off in time, leaving the Sixers still down five after the third.

Fourth Quarter

  • Justin Edwards also got his first real minutes of the year. He made a nice hustle play or two in the first half, but has just been in a terrible shooting slump going back to the preseason. He had a really bad airball as the Sixers started another quarter slow offensively. Edgecombe drilled a couple threes to keep the offense afloat, and thanks to more solid Tacko Fall minutes, the Sixers didn’t lose any ground.
  • Even with his four turnovers, Edgecombe scaled up very well. The shot he likes to go to is that midrange pull-up off a pick-and-roll and he was in a groove from there tonight. After making another, Embiid got to the line to tie the game for the first time since the beginning of the game. Using an Embiid screen, Edgecombe got downhill to get Jabari Walker wide open for three. Walker drilled it to give the Sixers their first lead since those opening minutes, and force a Magic timeout with five minutes to go.
  • That timeout allowed LeBron to check back into the game, and he powered his way for a layup to keep the Sixers up by two shortly after. Embiid followed that up by swatting away Anthony Black’s heave to beat the buzzer. Black fouled Embiid as he came down with the rebound, putting him at the line and the Sixers in the bonus.
  • After Embiid drilled both free throws, Wagner got to the basket then found Jalen Suggs on the wing to put the Magic back in front with two minutes to go. Edgecombe responded with a grenade to put the Sixers back in front, then tapped a long rebound off of Bane to come up with a big stop on the other end.
  • LeBron drilled a pull-up to extend that lead, but ended up fouling Suggs on the other end. Suggs split the pair though, and James found Simons in the corner for a three that sealed the game. Simons finished with 18 points in his spot-start, drilling the dagger in front of his friends and family.
