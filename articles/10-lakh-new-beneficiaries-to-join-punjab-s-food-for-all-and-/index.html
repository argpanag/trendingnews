10 Lakh New Beneficiaries to Join Punjab’s Food For All and Meri Rasoi Schemes

TheTools · 17.11.2026 12:02 · source · permalink

Chief Minister Bhagwant Singh Mann affirms transparent ration card distribution for economically weaker households

Chandigarh, Nov 17: Punjab Chief Minister Bhagwant Singh Mann has expanded the ambit of the state’s flagship Food For All and Meri Rasoi schemes to cover another 10 lakh beneficiaries, reinforcing welfare support for low-income families ahead of the upcoming Assembly polls.

At present, nearly 1.48 crore individuals in the state avail themselves of free wheat and additional ration items through the two programmes. Introduced earlier this year to complement wheat allocations under the National Food Security Act (NFSA), the Meri Rasoi initiative continues to anchor the administration’s food security efforts as the beneficiary base widens further.

Mann maintained that his government has eliminated the earlier culture of issuing ration cards in exchange for money or political favours, ensuring benefits reach deserving families directly. “Earlier, these ration cards were issued only by accepting cash (bribery) or on ‘farmaish’ (recommendation) of political bosses. We are issuing the cards only to those who are the most needy and at the bottom of the economic ladder,” he asserted.
