Democratic US Senate nominee Abdul El-Sayed makes 3 stops in UP Thursday

TheTools · 15.10.2026 16:33 · source · permalink

UPPER MICHIGAN. (WLUC) - The Democratic nominee for the 2026 U.S. Senate seat in Michigan is in the U.P. as part of his statewide bus tour.

Abdul El-Sayed has three U.P. stops on Thursday: Escanaba, Iron Mountain and Marquette.

El-Sayed is facing former Congressman Mike Rogers in the race to succeed retiring Senator Gary Peters. El-Sayed will speak alongside Congresswoman Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez at each location on issues facing Michiganders today.
