Fans of championship leader Kimi Antonelli could not believe their eyes: the 19-year-old Italian was too aggressive approaching Turn 1 of the Baku City Circuit, crashed into the wall, and that was that.

By

25.09.2026 - 17:20

This article is an automatically generated English version. The Original Article was published in German.

At first, there was just the radio message from Mercedes rookie Kimi Antonelli: “Ah, blimey, I’ve hit the wall.”

Advertisement

Advertisement

Then the TV cameras caught sight of the damaged Mercedes. The front-left tyre had come off the rim; the track rod had clearly taken a knock too, as the wheel was standing at an angle.

Championship leader Antonelli was instructed by his race engineer, Pete Bonington, to pull the car over to the side; the Bolognese driver nevertheless tried to limp the Mercedes back to the pits (something the stewards really don’t like to see), but then gave up – out of the race in the first qualifying segment, oh dear!

The replay showed that in Turn 1, Kimi was too aggressive; he took the left-hander a touch too tight and promptly clapped the inside left-hand side against the concrete – the impact was quite severe.

Advertisement

Advertisement

Kimi on the radio: “I’m so sorry, lads.” That’ll probably mean 16th on the grid for the Italian, depending on any penalty grid positions his rivals might incur.

After returning to the paddock at the Baku City Circuit, the eight-time Grand Prix winner was really fed up: “The track was building up more and more grip. As I turned in, I immediately realised that the front axle was gripping much more than on the previous lap, and before I knew it I’d hit the wall. A very unnecessary mistake on my part.”

“To be honest, I’m very annoyed with myself. I hope I can make up as many places as possible in the race.”

Make up places? Let’s not forget: at his home race in Monza, Antonelli drove to victory – starting from 19th on the grid.

Kimi again: “I’m going into the Grand Prix knowing that we have a fast car – just look at what George Russell has shown with it.”
