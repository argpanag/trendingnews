Ivory Coast captain Franck Kessie has described Ghana as a formidable opponent ahead of the highly anticipated clash between the two West African giants in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifiers.

The Elephants are set to host the Black Stars on Thursday, September 24, in their opening Group C fixture as both sides begin their respective journeys toward qualification for the continental showpiece.

With the two nations boasting a rich footballing history and passionate supporters, Kessie expects a fiercely contested encounter when the sides meet at the Peace Stadium in Bouake.

Speaking ahead of the crucial fixture, the experienced midfielder acknowledged Ghana’s pedigree in African football and praised the quality within the Black Stars squad.

“When you play Ghana, it’s a derby. Ghana are also a great African nation with very good players. We’re working hard and following the coach’s instructions to be ready from the first whistle tomorrow,” Kessie told the press.

The Ivory Coast captain’s comments underline the level of respect within the Elephants camp for their opponents. Rather than taking Ghana lightly, the home side has been focused on preparations as they seek to make a winning start to their qualifying campaign.

Kessie will also be expected to play a key leadership role on the pitch, particularly in a fixture where composure, experience and tactical discipline could prove important.

Ghana, meanwhile, have been in camp since Monday as the Black Stars intensify preparations for the encounter. The team started its build-up in Accra before making the trip to Côte d’Ivoire, with the technical team working to ensure the players are ready for what promises to be a demanding opening fixture.

The Group C encounter is scheduled for a 7:00 p.m. kick-off at the Peace Stadium in Bouake.

With three points at stake, both Ivory Coast and Ghana will be eager to begin their AFCON 2027 qualifying campaigns on a positive note. The Elephants will have home advantage behind them, while the Black Stars will be hoping to produce a disciplined performance and secure a valuable result away from home.

All eyes will therefore be on Bouake as two of Africa’s respected footballing nations renew their rivalry in a match expected to provide an early test of their ambitions for the 2027 AFCON.
