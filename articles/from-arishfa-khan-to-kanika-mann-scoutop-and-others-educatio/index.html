From Arishfa Khan to Kanika Mann, ScoutOP and others: Educational qualifications of Bigg Boss 20 contestants

TheTools · 16.11.2026 09:13 · source · permalink

Bigg Boss has been winning hearts ever since its beginning, entertaining audiences with a mix of drama, friendships, arguments and more. The 20th season of the reality show is no different, with contestants from different backgrounds bringing their own personalities and experiences into the house. While viewers are enjoying their journeys on the show, let’s take a look at the educational qualifications of some popular Bigg Boss 20 contestants.

Kanika Mann, who became popular for playing Guddan in Guddan Tumse Na Ho Payega, holds a Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) degree from Panjab University, Chandigarh. Before becoming a familiar face on television, Kanika also worked in modelling and appeared in Punjabi music videos. She later participated in Khatron Ke Khiladi 12.

Yung DSA, whose real name is Harsh Vijay Machare, is a rapper from Pune who has gained recognition in India’s independent hip-hop scene. He reportedly completed his schooling in Pune before focusing on music. He started writing and performing rap at a young age and gradually built his identity as a rapper. His song Yeda Yung, released through Divine’s Gully Gang Records, brought him wider recognition.

Uditi Singh, who has been making headlines for her journey in Bigg Boss 20, reportedly holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA) degree in Global Business. According to reports, she pursued the course at Coventry University, London Campus. Before entering the entertainment industry, Uditi also completed her schooling and college education. However, details about her academic journey have not been independently confirmed.

Arishfa Khan started working as a child artist and gradually established herself in the television and digital entertainment industries. Talking about her education, her mother Arshi Naz recently revealed that Arishfa completed her regular schooling up to Class 10 and later completed Class 12 through open schooling.

Amrapali Dubey is a popular name in Bhojpuri cinema and has also worked in Hindi television shows. Before making a name for herself in regional films, she completed her schooling and graduated from Bhavan’s College in Mumbai.

Unlike many contestants who entered the entertainment industry at a young age, Gullu also has a professional academic qualification. He holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Hotel Management from the Institute of Hotel Management, Catering Technology and Applied Nutrition (IHMCT), Pune. He later explored content creation and reality television.

ScoutOP, whose real name is Tanmay Singh, is one of India’s popular gaming personalities and esports players. He became widely recognised through PUBG Mobile and later established himself in the BGMI gaming community. According to reports, Scout completed his basic schooling but did not pursue higher education as he chose to focus on gaming.

Love Gill, whose real name is Lovepreet Kaur Gill, is a Punjabi actress and model who has worked in music videos, television and films. She completed her graduation from Guru Nanak Dev University in Amritsar. She initially gained attention through modelling assignments and her appearance in Karan Aujla’s music video Facts.
