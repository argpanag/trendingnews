Theodore “Ted” G. Kondos Obituary

Theodore “Ted” G. Kondos, age 92, of Elizabeth, passed away Friday, September 18, 2026 at Mountain View Senior Living in Greensburg. An Army veteran, he was a retired millwright expediter at U.S. Steel Clairton Works and a former member of St. Anthony Greek Orthodox Church in Clairton. He enjoyed collecting stamps. Born August 20, 1934 in McKeesport, he was a son of the late Constantine and Fotoula Kondos and husband of the late Katherine “Karen” (Cavanaugh) Kondos. He is survived by his stepson, Richard Miller of Monongahela; and nephews, Dean and James Kondos. No visitation or services are planned. Memorial contributions may be made to the Salvation Army, 821 Walnut Street, McKeesport, PA 15132 or the National Down Syndrome Society, 1155 15th Street, Suite 540, Washington, D.C. 20005. Arrangements by the Paul E. Bekavac Funeral Home, Elizabeth.
