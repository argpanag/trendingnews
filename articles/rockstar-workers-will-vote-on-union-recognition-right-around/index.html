Rockstar workers will vote on union recognition right around GTA VI’s launch.

TheTools · 27.10.2026 18:02 · source · permalink

GTA VI: all the news on Rockstar’s next entry in the Grand Theft Auto series

See all Stories

The union vote will take place from November 12th through November 26th, according to an emailed press release from the Independent Workers’ Union of Great Britain (IWGB). GTA VI is scheduled to release on November 19th.

If the staffers vote in favor, “the Rockstar Game Workers Union will become the biggest recognised union in the UK games sector,” the IWGB says.
