Flight of the Conchords Announce New Zealand Tour for 2026 | Ticketmaster NZ Blog

TheTools · 13.09.2026 20:00 · source · permalink

Comedy

Vincent Aballe / September 14, 2026

Guide

New Zealand’s favourite comedy duo, Flight of the Conchords, are coming home for their first national tour in 14 years.

After an eight-year pause from performing live, Flight of the Conchords surprised Kiwi fans in May with five intimate warm-up shows at Wellington’s Meow Nui, ahead of two sold-out performances at Los Angeles’ Greek Theatre as part of Netflix Is A Joke Fest. Now, Bret McKenzie and Jemaine Clement are set to return to Aotearoa this November for their first New Zealand tour since 2012.

The legendary duo will bring their blend of music, comedy and deadpan Kiwi humour to Michael Fowler Centre in Wellington on Tuesday 10 November and Saturday 14 November, Wolfbrook Arena in Christchurch on Thursday 12 November, and The Civic in Auckland on Wednesday 18 November and Thursday 19 November.

Flight of the Conchords New Zealand Tour 2026 Dates

  • November 10, 2026, – Michael Fowler Centre, Wellington
  • November 12, 2026, Wolfbrook Arena, Christchurch
  • November 14, 2026, Michael Fowler Centre, Wellington
  • November 18, 2026, The Civic, Auckland
  • November 19, 2026, The Civic, Auckland

Remember: there are a lot of fans

Tickets are sold as long as seats are available, and when an act is this popular, sometimes they go very quickly.

Be sure to follow the above tips to ensure you have the best chance of snapping up some tickets.
