QUICK READ: Magnus Ditlev sets a 7:19:17 Ironman world best

TheTools · 07.11.2026 23:50 · source · permalink

Magnus Ditlev won IRONMAN Florida on 7 November 2026 in 7:19:17, the fastest race-legal full-distance triathlon on record and a world best (world record).

  • The Dane swam 47:52, rode 3:51:16 and ran 2:33:31 at IRONMAN Florida in Panama City Beach, winning by more than 14 minutes.
  • He beat Sam Laidlow’s 7:21:04 at Challenge Roth and Kristian Blummenfelt’s 7:21:12 at IRONMAN Cozumel, the previous fastest times.
  • His 3:51:16 bike split is also the fastest at the full distance, beating Cameron Wurf’s 3:53:32 from IRONMAN Texas 2025. He reached T2 more than 11 minutes ahead of Sam Long, Rudy von Berg and Ben Kanute.
  • Von Berg was second in 7:33:25 and Matt Hanson third in 7:35:48 after a 2:28:07 marathon.
  • Alanis Siffert won the women’s race in 8:07:37, taking 14:51 off Skye Moench’s course record and earning her first Kona place. Holly Lawrence was second in 8:23:56 and Laura Jansen third in 8:32:21.

What it means for triathletes

Triathlon has no official world records, because courses and conditions differ too much to compare, so “world best” is the correct term for what most would call a world record. Florida’s bike course is flat, which helps.

The bike is interesting because since March 2026, IRONMAN professionals must stay 20 metres apart on the bike instead of 12, which cuts any drafting benefit, and Ditlev spent most of the ride alone at the front anyway.

Quick answers

What is the fastest Ironman time ever?
Magnus Ditlev’s 7:19:17 at IRONMAN Florida on 7 November 2026 is the fastest full-distance time on record. Sam Laidlow’s 7:21:04 at Challenge Roth was the previous best.

What were Magnus Ditlev's splits at Ironman Florida?
He swam 47:52, rode 3:51:16 and ran 2:33:31. The bike split is also the fastest at the full distance.
