Helen Skelton says hospice 'means a lot' to her family as she competes for charity

TheTools · 07.09.2026 11:27 · source · permalink

CUMBRIAN TV presenter Helen Skelton said Eden Valley Hospice 'means a lot' to her family while appearing on ITV's Celebrity Lingo.

She appeared as a contestant on episode nine of season two of the ITV game show, hosted by Adil Ray.

Helen competed alongside Rav Wilding, from Morning Live, against Judy Murray and Mark Pougatch.

The pairs played a game to win a cash prize for their chosen charities.

Helen Skelton said: "I'm playing for Eden Valley Hospice who provide end of life care and respite care for everyone from zero to 100.

"And yeah, it just means a lot to my family. They're wonderful people who run the place."

Helen and Rav did not reach the final, where Judy and Mark were champions.

Eden Valley Hospice thanked Helen for supporting it and said hearing the hospice mentioned on national TV was a surprise.

Writing on social media, they said: "Huge thank you to Helen Skelton for supporting Eden Valley Hospice on Celebrity Lingo!

"Hearing our hospice mentioned on national TV was a lovely surprise, and we're incredibly grateful to Helen for her support and for helping shine a spotlight on the care our teams provide every day. 

"Helen's kind words about the care we provide means so much as we prepare to mark 35 years, we couldn't have reached this milestone without the generosity, kindness, and support of our community.

"It certainly made our day!"

Based on Durdar Road, Carlisle, Eden Valley Hospice is a charity that provides specialist care and support for adults with life-limiting conditions across north Cumbria and south-west Scotland, as well as emotional and bereavement support for their families and loved ones.
