"Pokemon Day 2027 Collection" Revealed for January! - PokeBeach

TheTools · 12.11.2026 16:49 · source · permalink

Like last year, a new "Pokemon Day 2027 Collection" will release on January 29th for $14.99!

It will come with a holo Pikachu from Ascended Heroes with a special "Pokemon Day 2027" stamp, three booster packs, a metallic coin, and a code card. The Pikachu should be printed on cosmos holofoil.

Pokemon Day is celebrated on February 27th every year, marking the original release of Pokemon Red & Green in 1996.
