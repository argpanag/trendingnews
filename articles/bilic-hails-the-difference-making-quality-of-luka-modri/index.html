Bilic hails the difference-making quality of Luka Modrić

TheTools · 03.10.2026 06:26 · source · permalink

An iconic midfielder is still making a mark for Croatia, with Slaven Bilic hailing Luka Modrić as a unique, difference-making captain.

Slaven Bilic sung the praises of veteran Luka Modrić.

The midfielder was breaking into the first team for Croatia during the first stint of Bilic in the dugout between 2006 and 2012, so the head coach has witnessed firsthand the growth of a remarkable player.

Since that spell, Modrić has moved from Tottenham to Real Madrid, become an icon for Los Blancos in the act of winning five Champions League titles, been the key player in his country’s ‘Golden Generation’, and defied time with his impact at AC Milan in his 40s.

His completeness and longevity have left an indelible mark, making him one of the greatest midfielders of the 21st Century.

‘He has become a global superstar’

Fittingly, Modrić was the man whose goal marked the start of Bilic’s second stint in the dugout as his nation earned a 2-1 win over Czechia last week. He was greeted with applause by fans in Seville during the 4-1 defeat to Spain, and an impact on the game against England would not be a surprise.

"He simply keeps going because he is unique and because he means so much to the team. He is more than a player, more than a captain. He is an icon. His importance can be seen everywhere, at the table, at dinner, in meetings. But above all, he is here because he still gives so much on the pitch and makes a difference.

"He makes a difference in Serie A. AC Milan don't look the same when he is on the pitch and when he isn't. He also makes a difference for the national team. He did it the other day against Czechia.

"He is definitely recognised because he won the Ballon d’Or. Luka Modrić has been a global story for a long time. Maybe before 2018, he didn't receive the recognition we would have wanted, and that he might have received if he came from a bigger footballing nation. Since 2018, that World Cup, and with Real Madrid and the Champions League added to that, he has become a global superstar.

One of the most complete midfielders

"It is very difficult to compare different eras and say who is the best, but I would say he is one of the most complete midfielders of the modern era. I've seen him take a corner that leads to a goal, and then from that same corner, where he is practically the furthest player from his defence because he took it, he gets back and stops a counterattack in his own box.

“There are players who take corners better than him. There are players who make better tackles in their own box. But I don't know a player who has done both, and a third and fourth thing, at that level for so long.

Managing Modrić’s workload

"Of course, as with many other players, we have to be mindful of his workload, which is why we take special care of him. He is simply unique. The old saying is that age is just a number, but with him, it really seems that way and he proves it."
