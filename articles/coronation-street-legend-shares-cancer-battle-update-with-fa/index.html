  • Coronation Street legend Beverley Callard has shared an update with fans after undergoing stomach biopsies.
  • The actor was advised to have further scans on her stomach following a previous breast cancer diagnosis.
  • Tests have now confirmed that Callard does not have cancer in her stomach.
  • She revealed in an Instagram video that she had burst into tears on set prior to the biopsies because she felt so unwell.
  • She added that she feared she was ‘losing the battle.’
