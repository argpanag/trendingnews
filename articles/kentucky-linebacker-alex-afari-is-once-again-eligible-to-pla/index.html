Kentucky linebacker Alex Afari is once again eligible to play for the wildcats

TheTools · 16.10.2026 21:35 · source · permalink

LEXINGTON, Ky. (WKYT) - Kentucky linebacker Alex Afari is once again eligible to play for the wildcats.

Afari’s attorney Abe Mashni confirmed on social media that Afari is cleared to play this week against Oklahoma.

Afari was eligible for the first four games of the season before missing the previous two after the NCAA won its appeal. But after another court appearance this week, it looks like Afari is now eligible.

The court battle is far from over but for now, the Cats have Afari available to play.
