Milan, Hutchinson and Jashari start against Olympiacos: Cisse and Musah set for the bench

Amorim could leave the Rossoneri's top scorer in the Europa League out of the starting eleven, relying instead on the Englishman alongside Moreira and Pulisic to play behind Gonçalo Ramos. A choice also made with the league in mind: on Monday there is the away match against Cagliari

Cisse and Musah on the bench? Yes, likely. The line-up Ruben Amorim tested before the away trip to Athens against Olympiacos does not include Alphadjo Cisse, Milan's top scorer in the Europa League, nor Yunus Musah, a strategic player for the Portuguese coach. Milan should play with Moreira, Hutchinson and Pulisic behind Gonçalo Ramos. In the middle of the pitch, Jashari and Rabiot. It makes sense.

WHY CISSE IS OUT

Amorim is without Saelemaekers, Chukwueze, Loftus-Cheek, Modric, Pavlovic, Camarda and Bartesaghi. Many, too many absentees. Benching Cisse would mean saving him for the second half, when he could change the match. And after all, at the weekend against Frosinone he did very well in the closing stages. Meanwhile, in defence, Gila, De Winter, Diawara and Estupinan are expected to start.

WHO PLAYS IN CAGLIARI

The Musah matter is different. Yunus can be useful both from the start – the atmosphere will be tough, and a midfielder bringing his energy would help – and in the second half, because he can play in three or four roles. In the middle there will be Jashari, who has just scored against Frosinone, and of course Rabiot. Rather, it will be interesting to see how Amorim manages Monday's line-up in Cagliari. He is likely to ask for an extra effort from Pulisic, Ramos, Rabiot and all his leaders. The best eleven will play, also considering that the following match will be on Sunday in Parma. Six days to recover, finally.
