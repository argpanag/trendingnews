Lotto results: No winner of ₱285.83M jackpot on Sept. 11

TheTools · 12.09.2026 05:54 · source · permalink

MANILA, Philippines—No ticket won the ₱285.83 million Ultra Lotto 6/58 jackpot or the ₱51.73 million Mega Lotto 6/45 jackpot in the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office draws Friday, Sept. 11.

According to the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office, the winning combination for Ultra Lotto 6/58 was 09-03-22-02-26-34. No ticket matched all six numbers for the ₱285.83 million jackpot.

The Mega Lotto 6/45 winning combination was 12-18-14-34-24-45. No ticket holder won the ₱51.73 million jackpot.
