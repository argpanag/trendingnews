Iraola insists ‘Koumas has the trust’ of Liverpool’s coaching staff

TheTools · 09.10.2026 19:47 · source · permalink

Liverpool may lean on academy graduate Lewis Koumas up front against Manchester City, with Iraola confirming Alexander Isak and Cody Gakpo are injured.

Andoni Iraola offered updates about the state of the Liverpool attack.

The Reds host Premier League leading Manchester City on Sunday afternoon, and many people are hoping for fireworks that are reminiscent of previous table topping encounters between the two teams.

‘Alexander Isak and Cody Gakpo will not play’

Unfortunately for those of a red persuasion, the top two forwards, who have contributed five goals and three assists across five league fixtures, will be out of action after injuries on international duty.

That means Iraola must adapt, and he could call on the services of an academy graduate upfront.

"Alexander Isak and Cody Gakpo will not play the game. They are not ready.

"Unfortunately for us, both got injuries during the international break. As a club manager, you are always waiting until the games end, that the doctor doesn't call you and then gives you bad news.

"But in this case, we've been unlucky with Alex and Cody – both of them are injured and they are not going to be involved on Sunday.

"Cody has an ankle injury. He has had some before in his career and it's a matter of him dealing with the pain because when you twist your ankle... it was a really bad tackle, I think.

"He is doing things individually but [he is] not ready for Sunday. And Alex has a thigh injury. It's a small injury but it's a muscle injury. The same [as Gakpo], whenever he feels ready. They both don't look like long term – we will be analysing day to day. But they are not going to arrive on Sunday."

Ekitike out, Chiesa closing in

“Even before, we don't have Hugo Ekitike who is another very good striker but I am looking forward to have them available.

"We maybe recover Federico Chiesa but he has not played minutes, he's recovering from an injury but he's been doing a good job in the international break and training with the small group we had.

Koumas trusted at number nine

"For number nine, we probably only have Lewis Koumas right now but we have some other options. We have wingers who have played up front in other teams and I think we will put out an 11 with a level enough to compete.

"Koumas has our trust. I don't think it's a big surprise, I always liked the player and he has already shown in important minutes that he has ready to go and we have also other players who can go in those positions."
