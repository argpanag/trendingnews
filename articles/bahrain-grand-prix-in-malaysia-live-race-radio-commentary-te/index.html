  1. Report: Verstappen wins Bahrain GP in Malaysia after long delayspublished at 12:35 BST
    12:35 BST

    Andrew Benson
    BBC F1 correspondent

    Max Verstappen took his and Red Bull's first win of the year with a dominant drive in a dramatic Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia that started amid chaotic and unprecedented scenes.

    Verstappen led home the Mercedes of Kimi Antonelli, who took a giant leap towards his first world title when team-mate George Russell retired from third place with a power unit failure with five laps to go.

    Antonelli now leads Russell by 84 points with either six or seven races remaining.

    Lewis Hamilton drove an extraordinary race after dropping towards the back with an incorrect tyre choice for a wet start to fight back to pass Red Bull's Isack Hadjar for third place with three laps to go.

    All that came after a delay of an hour and a half caused initially by heavy rain and then by a series of bizarre technical problems that left several cars crawling as they completed formation laps on the wet track behind the safety car.

  4. 'We don't have the performance that we want' - Leclercpublished at 12:28 BST
    12:28 BST

    Ferrari's Charles Leclerc, who finished just outside the podium in fourth, speaking to Sky Sports: "Honestly, in the first half of the race, everything that could have gone wrong, went wrong. On the second part, everything that could have gone right, went our way. So at the end of the day, I prefer it that way to the other way around. That means we are P4 today, I honestly think that with a normal race, we would have finished P3, P4. The two guys at the front were just too quick for us.

    "I think Lewis had good pace, for me I just struggled a lot with the car, which we will look into. I had a good feeling on Friday, a good feeling until Q2 and then in Q3, I started to lose out and today in the race, it was tricky.

    On whether the car can perform at Singapore: "I hope so. At the end of the day, we went through the track characteristics in the last two, three races and clearly the tendency is that we are losing performance compared to Red Bull and Mercedes, so we have got to bring upgrades, eventually. We don't quite have the performance that we want."

  6. 'You've almost got to laugh' - Russellpublished at 12:21 BST
    12:21 BST

    Mercedes' George Russell, speaking to Sky Sports, after his DNF at Sepang: "You've almost got to laugh. I'm beyond disappointment. It's just so many times this season it's happened. It's a real shame. Of course, but just keep on fighting. Just keep on trying to go podium to podium.

    "Solid race. Not amazing race but solid race. Not the first time so I'm probably used to these feelings and these questions, let's say."

    On whether the hope of winning the championship is gone: "I don't need to say I think it's gone but, of course, it's exceptionally unlikely and that's just the reality. I knew if I wanted to come back, I needed luck on my side. Unfortunately, I had luck not on my side. So, you know, a three point loss today being behind Kimi turned into an 18 point loss. Of course, if it was on the other side it would have been an 18 point gain. So it's a real shame, but that's just how it is."

  7. 'One of my worst experiences' - Wolffpublished at 12:16 BST
    12:16 BST

    Mercedes boss Toto Wolff speaking to Sky Sports about the issue with George Russell's car: "It was a PU failure, wasn't caused by the safety car. We haven't assessed it yet.

    "I am so massively annoyed and not in a happy place. First of all, we didn't have the speed we were hoping to have before the weekend, so there is a lot of homework to be done in order to recover. Which I am sure we will.

    "And then the race literally went against us. We had such an advantage, one VSC, two safety cars, you couldn't be more unlucky. To make things worse, PU failure with a few laps to go until the end with George. Which is terrible for his championship and not good at all for our constructor points. As much as I love to be here, when I look at the pure races, one of my worst experiences to be honest.

    "[Kimi Antonelli] scored a few more points than Lewis behind him. I am happy because he did the job. But it is just this overarching topic of the DNFs that could cost us dearly, also for the remaining seven races. Our championship has quickly turned around against you if you have a DNF and that is why that can't continue."
