Ticketmaster keeps its spot as the top NFL ticket reseller

TheTools · 12.11.2026 09:34 · source · permalink

NFL owners approved Ticketmaster continuing as the league's top ticket reselling partner as part of a new deal, although an open distribution strategy will give out other ticket platforms a chance to sell on a lower tier.

Ticketmaster will be considered the NFL's platinum-level secondary ticketing partner and run the league’s ticket exchange, while other NFL-authorized secondary marketplaces will have full access to all 32 teams’ season-ticket holder resale inventory, although on a limited basis.

Financial terms of the ticketing reselling arrangement were not disclosed. Privately held SeatGeek is reportedly joining the new model at a gold tier.

"The inventory will get broadcast out across the entire network, making it easier for season ticket holders to get wider distribution and a bigger reach," highlighted NFL SVP/Club Business Development Bobby Gallo to Sports Business Journal. "This adds greater liquidity for their season tickets, which we’re really excited about. And it’s also better for the fans because you’re gaining access to more of this inventory across more sites," he added.

Together, the NFL and Ticketmaster plan to advance technology that makes finding and buying tickets a more seamless part of being an NFL fan.

Ticketmaster is owned by Live Nation Entertainment LYV.
