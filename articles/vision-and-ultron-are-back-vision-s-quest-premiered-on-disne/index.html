Vision and Ultron are back - "Vision's Quest" premiered on Disney+

TheTools · 14.10.2026 09:16 · source · permalink

The first two episodes are already available for viewing.

The first two episodes of "Vision's Quest", concluding the Marvel trilogy that also includes "WandaVision" and "Agatha All Along", have been released on Disney+. The series holds an 82% positive rating on Rotten Tomatoes based on 45 reviews, and a score of 62 out of 100 on Metacritic based on 16 reviews.

The plot follows a rebooted Vision as he hides from those who tried to turn him into a weapon, attempting to find a new purpose for existence. He is helped in understanding himself by the personalities of artificial intelligences embedded in his software code: Jarvis, F.R.I.D.A.Y., E.D.I.T.H., and Ultron.

Meanwhile, a mysterious young man appears in the real world, claiming to be Vision's son. Simultaneously, the mercenary Paladin hunts the hero, and the robot Jocasta intends to kill him and destroy the planet.

Paul Bettany returned to the role of Vision, and James Spader once again played Ultron. Ruairi O'Connor played Vision's presumed son, Todd Stashwick played Paladin, and T'Nia Miller played Jocasta. James D'Arcy and Faran Tahir also starred in the series.

"Vision's Quest" has a total of eight episodes — the final episode will be released on November 11, 2026.
