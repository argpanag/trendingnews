Gas tops $2.15 per litre on Avalon Peninsula for first time since 2022 | CBC News

TheTools · 14.09.2026 10:25 · source · permalink

The price of gas increases 8.2 cents per litre across N.L. following Saturday adjustment

The price of gas rose by 8.2 cents per litre across Newfoundland and Labrador on Saturday, marking the first time the price of a litre of gas has crossed $2.15 per litre on the Avalon Peninsula in over four years.

The Public Utilities Board made the change on Saturday, the latest of daily adjustments from Tuesday to Saturday as a result of fluctuating oil prices and conflict in the Middle East.

The adjustment puts the price of a litre of unleaded fuel at $2.155 per litre on the Avalon Peninsula, marking the first time the price has passed $2.15 per litre since July 1, 2022, according to pricing data made available by the PUB.

Gas prices range between $2.16 and $2.31 across the rest of Newfoundland, and between $1.89 and $2.26 per litre across Labrador.

All other fuels also saw increases in price on Saturday.

The price of diesel increased by 11.1 cents per litre across Newfoundland and Labrador.

The price of furnace oil increased by nearly 9.6 cents per litre across Newfoundland, while stove oil customers in Labrador will pay almost 9.7 cents more per litre after Saturday's increase.

The next pricing adjustment from the PUB is scheduled for Tuesday.
