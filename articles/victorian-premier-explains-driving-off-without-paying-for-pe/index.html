Victorian premier explains driving off without paying for petrol

TheTools · 20.10.2026 22:09 · source · permalink

Victorian Premier Ben Carroll has admitted to accidentally driving off without paying after filling up his personal car with petrol.

In a statement on Wednesday, Carroll said he paid his bill after police contacted the Department of Parliamentary Services, which then relayed the message to the premier.
