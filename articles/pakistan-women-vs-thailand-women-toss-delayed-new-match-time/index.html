Pakistan Women vs Thailand Women Toss Delayed: New Match Time, Reduced Overs For PAK-W vs THA-W Asian Games 2026 Cricket Quarter-Final Match

TheTools · 17.09.2026 06:17 · source · permalink

Pakistan Women vs Thailand Women has been delayed in the Asian Games 2026 Cricket Quarter-Final at Korogi Sports Park in Nisshin, Japan, after the wet outfield prevented the match from starting as scheduled.

The weather has already affected the day’s cricket at the venue, with the earlier quarter-final between Bangladesh Women and China Women abandoned without a ball being bowled. With Pakistan and Thailand now waiting to get their knockout contest underway, here is the latest update on the revised match schedule, overs and weather conditions.

When Will Pakistan Women vs Thailand Women Start Today?

The toss is now scheduled for 3:10 PM (11:40 AM IST) local time at Korogi Sports Park.

The match will get underway after the toss and ground assessment, provided the playing conditions are suitable.

How Many Overs Will Pakistan Women vs Thailand Women Be Played For?

The quarter-final has been reduced to 11 overs per side because of the delay.

Pakistan Women and Thailand Women will therefore have 11 overs each in the shortened contest, subject to any further interruption.

 Nisshin Weather Forecast Today

The latest weather update for Nisshin shows no rain, partly sunny conditions, but showers remain a possibility. The current forecast puts the chance of precipitation at around 43%, meaning it would be inaccurate to describe the weather as completely dry.

More importantly for Pakistan and Thailand, the forecast becomes less favourable during the next few hours, with the rain probability rising into the 60% range around 4-5 PM.

The hourly rain probabilities are:

  • 3 PM: 43%
  • 4 PM: 68%

There is no official announcement of an abandonment at this stage. The latest update is that the match has been reduced to 11 overs per side, with the toss scheduled for 3:10 PM local time.

The ground condition and weather around the toss will determine whether the shortened quarter-final can proceed as planned.
