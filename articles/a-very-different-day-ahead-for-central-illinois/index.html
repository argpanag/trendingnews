A very different day ahead for Central Illinois

TheTools · 16.10.2026 09:48 · source · permalink

DECATUR, Ill. (WAND) – Chilly and Windy Friday

Expect cloudy skies to dominate most of Friday with northwest winds gusting between 30 and 40 mph. These winds will keep temperatures from warming much at all, holding steady in the 50s through the afternoon. That’s roughly 20 degrees cooler than yesterday’s high.

Spotty showers are possible this evening, but any rainfall should remain light. If you’re heading out to Friday night football games, bundle up — temperatures will drop quickly once the sun sets.

Frost Possible Overnight

Winds will gradually die down overnight as cloud cover clears out. Temperatures will plunge into the mid to upper 30s, creating conditions favorable for frost. A frost advisory has been issued for areas west of a line from Bloomington to Decatur to Shelbyville. If you have sensitive plants or fall crops, take precautions.

Beautiful Weekend, Then Warming Begins

Sunshine takes over for the weekend. Saturday will feature sunny skies with cool highs in the upper 50s. Overnight lows Saturday night will again dip into the mid-30s, and another frost advisory is likely for areas east of I-55 for early Sunday morning.

By Sunday afternoon, a warming trend begins. Temperatures will climb into the mid to upper 60s, with gradual warming continuing into next week. Highs in the 70s are expected to return early next week, and no rain is anticipated at this time.
