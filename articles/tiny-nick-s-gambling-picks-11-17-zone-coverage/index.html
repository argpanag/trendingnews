Tiny Nick's Gambling Picks 11/17 - Zone Coverage

TheTools · 17.11.2026 14:16 · source · permalink

NBA (0.75 Unit) Portland Trail Blazers/Minnesota Timberwolves Under 227.5 (-110; Odds via Caesars): 7:00 PM CT on DAZN-MIN

If books are going to hang the exact same total (and spread by the way) for this rematch tonight, then what choice do I have but to run it back with the under? It was a strange game, I’ll be the first to admit that, as neither team shot it well and key players had very unusual performances. So there’s room for more points to be found than what was put up last night, but going under the total by 23 points is a significant miss by the books and I’m just surprised to see no adjustments being made.

The same principles that led me to the under yesterday are leading me there today regardless of the 204-point outcome though. These are two defensive-oriented teams, with Minnesota proving why they have the best defensive efficiency mark in the league, and last night was another in a string of games for both teams that have failed to reach this total. While I’d expect players like LaMelo and Ayo to have better scoring nights, there should also be a drop-off by Terrance Shannon to be a counterbalance, so I’m sticking to my original handicap of liking the under at this number.

 

NBA (1 Unit) Luka Doncic Over 4.5 Made Three’s (-130; Odds via Fanduel): 10:00 PM CT on NBC

There are rare instances where I’ll swallow a significant amount of juice for a bet, and this is one of them. I’m not sure what took them so long, but books are finally attempting to juice Luka’s three-point prop into a range where they’ll scare off bettors, but it’s not working on me.

Not in this matchup anyway, as a new awful three-point defense has entered the chat. Charlotte is now the NBA’s worst three-point percentage defense, while allowing the 5th-most makes per game, and they haven’t faced anything like Luka yet. With the Lakers finally back home, that gives a slight boost to Doncic’s three-point percentage, but the main thing is his consistency.

His attempts are very steady and his makes are even steadier, draining either 5 or 6 from deep in 8 of 12 games this season. He’s only failed to reach this total twice on the year, and against this forgiving defense I think he can potentially have a field day in a nationally televised game.

Bonus Bet (0.25 Unit): Luka Doncic Alternate Over 5.5 Made Three’s (+160; Odds via Fanduel)

I’ll put a sprinkle on one rung up the ladder here, as it’s a juicy enough return compared to the heavy price on the standard prop. There’s a very real angle here that Luka will want to show out on national TV and could go absolutely wild, so keep climbing the ladder if you’re feeling sporty. But I’m glad to take this kind of return on something he’s done in half of his games this season.

 

NCAA Basketball (0.5 Unit) Old Dominion/Virginia Over 146.5 (-110; Odds via DraftKings): 8:00 PM CT on ACC Network

This could be a let-down spot for Virginia after a big comeback win at Maryland over the weekend, but that would probably benefit Old Dominion if anything. Virginia is playing excellent basketball right now, especially on the offensive end of the floor as the 9th-best team by adjusted efficiency. Add in their up-tempo style that feels very unusual for the Virginia we knew for so long, and that’s how they’ve gotten to a 5-0 record to the over.

Even if they do experience a let-down here, that gives an ODU team that can really shoot it from deep an opportunity to hang around and push the Cavaliers higher. I don’t think UVA’s offense is going to struggle to score against a Monarchs team that hasn’t seen anything like this level of offensive talent, and I also don’t see ODU dragging this game down to their preferred tempo. They’ve only faced one relatively up-tempo team and that game got to 154 points, so a very fast-paced and skilled UVA squad should get what they want to help push this over the number.

 

NCAA Basketball (0.5 Unit) UNLV Team Total Under 68.5 (-110; Odds via Caesars): 8:30 PM CT on ESPN

This Vegas event might be essentially a home game for UNLV, but I don’t think that can help them enough in this matchup. The Rebels are up against a truly elite Kansas defense, as the Jayhawks are second nationally in adjusted defensive efficiency. And KU really shuts down what UNLV wants to do, which is get inside, score via the two, and get to the free throw line.

The Jayhawks are 6th in two-point percentage defense, they foul at the 11th-lowest rate in the country, and give up the 11th-lowest percentage of points via the charity stripe. KU just took on Kentucky and held a high-octane offense with much more varied scoring ability to 69 points, so I think they can limit the Rebels to a little less.

 

No degenerates today.
