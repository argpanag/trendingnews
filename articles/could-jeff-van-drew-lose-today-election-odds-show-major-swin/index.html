Could Jeff Van Drew lose today? Election odds show major swing

TheTools · 04.11.2026 02:00 · source · permalink

Could Jeff Van Drew lose his New Jersey congressional seat today?

Van Drew remains favored to defeat Democrat Zack Mullock in NJ-2, but live Election Day odds show the Republican-held South Jersey seat could be considerably more competitive than broader forecasts predict.

The latest public NJ-2 poll listed by The New York Times polling tracker, conducted by Impact Research Oct. 7-10, showed Van Drew leading Mullock by 10 points, 50% to 40%. But Election Day prediction markets have shown a much tighter race.

By early Tuesday afternoon, Van Drew was favored by roughly 55% to 45% on Kalshi and 56% to 44% on Polymarket, putting Mullock within striking distance.

That raises the bigger Election Day question: Could Mullock pull off an upset and flip Van Drew's Republican-held NJ-2 seat blue?

Broader election forecasts still favor Van Drew. Decision Desk HQ's freshest Election Day update gives Van Drew a 68% chance of winning, while PollsMax puts Van Drew at about 82% and PoliAgg at 88%, though PoliAgg flags high uncertainty in the race.

Here's what the latest Van Drew-Mullock odds, forecasts and available NJ-2 polling show about who is winning, who could win and whether Democrats could flip NJ-2 today.

Could Jeff Van Drew lose NJ-2 today?

Yes. Jeff Van Drew losing NJ-2 is a real possibility. The Republican incumbent remains favored, but Zack Mullock is within striking distance in live Election Day prediction markets.

A Mullock victory would flip the Republican-held South Jersey seat to Democrats.

Who is winning Jeff Van Drew vs. Zack Mullock right now?

No Election Day votes have been reported yet, so neither candidate is actually leading in counted votes.

Late Tuesday morning, however, live prediction markets narrowly favored Van Drew over Mullock, though just hours earlier the odds were split 50-50.

What are Jeff Van Drew's odds of winning today?

Election Day forecasts favor Jeff Van Drew over Zack Mullock, but they disagree sharply on how safe the Republican incumbent is.

  • Decision Desk HQ: Van Drew 68%, Mullock 32%, according to its 11:45 a.m. Election Day update.
  • PollsMax: Van Drew 82.1%, Mullock 17.9%, with NJ-2 rated Likely Republican and a projected 6.1-point Republican margin.
  • PoliAgg: Van Drew 88%, Mullock 12%, but with a high 89/100 uncertainty score because of limited recent polling.

Live NJ-2 prediction markets show an even closer race:

  • Kalshi: Van Drew 55%, Mullock 45%
  • Polymarket: Van Drew 56%, Mullock 44%

Prediction-market odds reflect trading activity and can change throughout Election Day.

What are the latest Jeff Van Drew-Zack Mullock polls?

The latest public NJ-2 polls show Jeff Van Drew leading Zack Mullock, although both polls were conducted before Election Day.

  • Impact Research, Oct. 7-10: Van Drew 50%, Mullock 40%, Van Drew +10. The New York Times polling tracker identifies the survey as having a Democratic sponsor.
  • Public Policy Polling, May 21-22: Van Drew 46%, Democrat 36%, Van Drew +10.

The October poll is the more relevant Election Day snapshot because the Public Policy Polling survey was conducted months earlier.

The key tension? The latest October poll had Van Drew ahead by 10 points, while Tuesday's live prediction markets show a much closer NJ-2 race.

Who is favored to win NJ-2 today?

Jeff Van Drew remains favored to win another term in Congress.

The latest October public poll had Van Drew ahead by 10 points, and broader Election Day forecasts still favor the Republican incumbent. PollsMax rates NJ-2 Likely Republican and projects roughly a 53%-47% Van Drew victory, while PoliAgg also rates the race Likely Republican but flags high uncertainty.

Live prediction markets updated in real-time, however, show a considerably closer contest.

Could Zack Mullock flip NJ-2 blue?

Yes. A Zack Mullock victory would flip NJ-2 from Republican to Democratic control. Jeff Van Drew is the Republican incumbent, while Mullock is the Democratic nominee challenging him in Tuesday's election.

That possibility has become more interesting on Election Day as live prediction markets show a significantly closer contest than the broader forecasts.

Why does NJ-2 matter in the 2026 midterm elections?

The Van Drew-Mullock race is part of the broader fight over control of Congress in the 2026 midterm elections.

Democrats are trying to flip Republican-held House seats as they seek control of the chamber, and NJ-2 was added to the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee's target list in October. Van Drew won another term by a wide margin in 2024, making today's tightening particularly notable, according to 270toWin.

What are New Jersey voters deciding in the 2026 elections?

New Jersey voters are deciding races for U.S. Senate and all 12 U.S. House seats in the 2026 midterm elections. The NJ-2 contest between Jeff Van Drew and Zack Mullock is one of the congressional races on the ballot.

What time do polls close in NJ today?

New Jersey polls close at 8 p.m. ET today, Tuesday, Nov. 3.

How can I track NJ election live results?

New Jersey election results for the U.S. Senate and all 12 U.S. House districts, including the Jeff Van Drew-Zack Mullock NJ-2 race, will begin updating after polls close at 8 p.m. ET.

For live Election Day results, race calls and updates from Monmouth and Ocean counties and across New Jersey, follow the Asbury Park Press Election Day 2026 live coverage.
