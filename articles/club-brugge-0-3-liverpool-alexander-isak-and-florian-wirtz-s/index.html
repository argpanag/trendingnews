Clinical Liverpool beat Club Brugge to return to winning ways

Alexander Isak scored twice to take his tally for the season to 10 as Liverpool won at Club Brugge in the Champions League.

Isak opened the scoring early in the first half after Yann Sommer had passed straight to Bradley Barcola, who found Dominik Szoboszlai inside the box and the midfielder teed up the striker to slot in.

Liverpool should have been two up midway through the second half but Sommer produced a great save to deny Szoboszlai at point-blank range.

Alisson was rarely tested although made a good save down at his near post from Felix Lemarechal's low drive.

The hosts did start to put more pressure on the Liverpool defence as the second half wore on, but Florian Wirtz ensured there would be no late drama as he continued his good form this season with a finish at the near post.

Isak then wrapped up the scoring late on with a confident chipped finish after running through one-on-one.

The one negative on the night for Liverpool was Victor Munoz suffering an injury in the first half after clattering into a defender at the end of a good run into the box. He had to be taken off and was replaced by Rio Ngumoha.

The win was Liverpool's fourth in five games in the league phase of the Champions League and moves them up to fifth in the standings.
