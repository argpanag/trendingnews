Sylvester Stallone, who stars as Dwight Manfredi, has been married to former model Jennifer Flavin since 1997

Tulsa King may be full of complicated family dynamics, tangled romances and shifting loyalties, but the cast's real-life relationships tell a different story.

The Paramount+ series follows Dwight Manfredi (Sylvester Stallone), a New York mob boss who, after serving a 25-year prison sentence, is exiled to Tulsa, Okla., where he builds a new criminal empire. Heading into season 4, which premiered Oct. 16, Dwight continues to navigate the dangers of organized crime while juggling his complicated relationship with his daughter and his romance with Margaret (Dana Delany).

Off screen, Stallone has built a family of his own. The Rambo actor has been married to former model Jennifer Flavin since 1997, and the couple share three daughters, including Scarlett, who stars alongside her father in Tulsa King. After recurring as Spencer since season 1, Scarlet was promoted to a series regular for season 4.

Here's everything to know about the real-life loves of the Tulsa King cast.

01 of 07

Sylvester Stallone and Jennifer Flavin

Sylvester Stallone and Jennifer Flavin Stallone.
Credit: Dimitrios Kambouris/Getty

Stallone — who leads the series as the eponymous king of Tulsa — has been married to Flavin since May 17, 1997, when they wed in a small London ceremony.

Over the years, the couple have welcomed three daughters: Sophia in 1996, Sistine in 1998 and Scarlet, who is part of the Tulsa King cast, in 2002. Stallone previously welcomed sons Sage and Seargeoh in his previous marriage to Czack; in 2012, Sage died of heart disease at 36 years old.

In August 2022, Flavin filed for divorce from Stallone. The following month, however, she called off the divorce.

Speaking to The Sunday Times in October 2025, Stallone opened up about reconciling with his wife.

"There was a reawakening of what was more valuable than anything, which is my love for my family," he said. "It takes precedence over my work, and that was a hard lesson to learn."

In honor of their 29th anniversary in May 2026, Stallone wrote on Instagram that Flavin "[gives his] life meaning."

02 of 07

Dana Delany

Dana Delany.
Credit: Kristina Bumphrey/Variety via Getty

Delany may portray Stallone's on-screen love interest, Margaret, but in real life, she's an independent woman.

The actress has never been married and doesn't have kids, which she credits with keeping her young. In an interview with the Daily Mail in October 2008, she said that her parents' divorce discouraged her from getting married.

"My mother went through a horrible divorce from my father that was all about money," she said. "I saw that marriage did not make people happy, that being true to yourself makes you happy."

In September 2016, she reiterated that sentiment in an interview with AARP. "[Getting married] was never something I really seriously considered," she said. "I've had a very full life, and I've dated very interesting people."

03 of 07

Martin Starr and Alex Gehring

Martin Starr and Alex Gehring.
Credit: Jesse Grant/Getty

Martin Starr, who plays Bodhi, is married to musician Alex Gehring.

The couple tied the knot in May 2024 in Austin, Texas, surrounded by friends and family. Starr's former Freaks and Geeks costar, Seth Rogen, also played an important role in the ceremony, acting as ring bearer, per Rolling Stone.

A few months after their wedding, Starr shared an Instagram post for his new bride.

"Happy Birthday to my favorite human! I celebrate you every single day and today is no different!" he wrote. "Thank you for blessing me with the incredible warmth of your love! You are a dream come true! Perfection! My angel!"

04 of 07

Garrett Hedlund

Garrett Hedlund.
Credit: Matt Winkelmeyer/VF24/WireImage

Garret Hedlund, who plays Mitch, is currently single.

The actor was previously in a relationship with Emma Roberts, and the pair welcomed a baby boy, Rhodes Roberts, in December 2020. The couple split two years later but remain dedicated to co-parenting their son.

"Oh man, I am wrapped around [Rhodes'] finger like you won't believe it," he shared during a September 2025 Today appearance. "It's the journey, adventure, risk, reward, all day long."

05 of 07

Neal McDonough and Ruvé McDonough

Ruvé McDonough and Neal McDonough on Dec. 10, 2024, in Los Angeles
Credit: Tommaso Boddi/Getty

Neal McDonough may play a tough guy on Tulsa King, but in real life he's a softie, particularly with his wife and kids.

The Band of Brothers actor has been married to his wife, Ruvé McDonough, since December 2003.

The couple also share five children together. They welcomed their older son, Morgan, in 2005, followed by daughter Catherine in 2007. They went on to welcome two more daughters, London and Clover, in 2010 and 2011, respectively, before completing their family with their youngest child, son James, in 2014.

06 of 07

Gretchen Mol and Tod Williams

Gretchen Mol and Tod Williams attend the American Academy in Rome 2022 New York Gala.
Credit: Sean Zanni/Patrick McMullan via Getty

Gretchen Mol, a newcomer to Tulsa King season 4 as politician Amanda Clark, has been married to filmmaker Tod "Kip" Williams since 2004, per her Instagram.

Mol and Williams share two children, son Ptolemy John and daughter Winter Morgan.

07 of 07

Tatiana Zappardino and Alexander Waugh

Tatiana Zappardino and Alexander Waugh.
Credit: Tatiana Zappardino/Instagram

Tatiana Zappardino wed Alexander Waugh, a U.S. Army Green Beret, in December 2024.

The actress shared the news of her wedding in January 2025 with a sweet Instagram post.

"And that's how you end 2024 with a bang! 🤟🏼," she wrote. "I love you Alex...My husband 🥹❤️💍 #hitched."

The couple are parents to two girls. They welcomed their older daughter, Emilia, in June 2023, and their younger daughter, Maisie, in March 2025. A few days after Maisie's birth, Zappardino shared the happy news on Instagram.

"Cat's out of the bag! Maisie Louise has arrived, and so I feel comfortable telling the world we have welcomed a second baby girl to the family," she wrote alongside a carousel of photos. "My husband and daughters are the most important part of my life, and I couldn't be more honored to serve them."
