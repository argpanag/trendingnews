FIVE November 1 NRL contract targets: New Zealand Warriors

TheTools · 26.10.2026 22:31 · source · permalink

The New Zealand Warriors continue to string together impressive seasons, but success in September continues to sit beyond arm's reach of Andrew Webster's side after their 2026 campaign.

Webster, who has now been extended through to beyond the end of the decade in a monster show of faith by a club that has often battled inconsistency, has won just one game in six finals matches in his time at the Warriors, which has become a concern.

2026 was supposed to be the year where their finals record changed too; after a top-two finish, a home qualifying final, a home semi-final, and with two straight losses, they found themselves watching crunch time from the sideline.

There is no doubt the Warriors squad has an awesome amount of talent, but the bottom line is success is measured in September, not August.

What the Warriors do have is stability moving forward both on and off the field, and while that could work in one of two ways, there are tasks the club needs to accomplish come November 1.

Here is the current state of play for the Warriors as November 1 approaches.

The Warriors already have 20 players locked in for 2028, which is stability they can build from.

But they will be looking for upgrades. The recent re-signing of Wayde Egan is a boost and means they won't attack that area of the free agency market heavily.

The likely retirement of Kurt Capewell and the breakout of Leka Halasima in the centres means a second-rower is likely on the radar, while the centres are also short.

The middle third potentially could add another enforcer, while Taine Tuaupiki has been linked to the Super League and may not be the fullback to deliver a premiership to Auckland.

Here are five players the Warriors should target after November 1.

Option 1: Billy Smith (Sydney Roosters)

Smith has been outstanding for the Roosters when he is able to stay on the field, and while injuries are his biggest concern, if he can string full seasons together, he could become one of the best outside backs in the competition.

His versatility and ability to play in the centres or on the wing will likely intrigue clubs, and the Warriors could be one.

The Auckland-based outfit is on the lookout for a centre, and Smith fills that void perfectly.

He has all the tools to contribute effectively to the Warriors, and he also defends well.
