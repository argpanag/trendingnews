Jihyo, Contract with JYP Ends…"Launched Independent Label EMIT"

TheTools · 21.10.2026 09:23 · source · permalink

[Dispatch=Reporter Lee Ajin] Jihyo of TWICE is leaving JYP Entertainment. She is establishing an independent label and embarking on a new challenge.

On the 21st, Jihyo posted a handwritten letter on her SNS. She stated, "I have come to conclude my journey with JYP Entertainment, which I have been with for 21 years since childhood."

She also announced the establishment of independent label 'EMITHAUS'. EMITHAUS is a name derived from 'EMIT', an English word meaning 'to emit' or 'to give off'.

Jihyo explained, "Independence is less a conclusion about the time that has passed and more a choice about how to live going forward," and added, "I want to freely unfold the potential within me that I have yet to discover."

EMITHAUS will expand its business scope starting with Jihyo's solo activities. It plans to discover and recruit various artists in the future. It will expand its scope of activities beyond music to overall content.

TWICE activities will continue. Jihyo stated, "TWICE is an indispensable precious part of my life," and emphasized, "I want to continue standing on the same stage with the members and spending time creating together for a long time to come."

Finally, she said, "I am grateful to the fans who have believed in and supported me over the past 11 years," and pledged, "I will continue to grow as an artist worthy of the love you have shown me through choices and efforts that are true to myself."
