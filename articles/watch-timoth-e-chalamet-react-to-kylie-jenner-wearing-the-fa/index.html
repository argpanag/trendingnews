Um, surprise! Kylie Jenner showed up at the 2026 Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show, and she wasn’t just in the audience supporting her model friends. Not only did Kylie walk the runway, she wore the 2026 Fantasy Bra—which made its debut for the first time in eight years.

And whomst was there to support her? Both Kris Jenner AND Timothée Chalamet, who was seen cheering in the audience.

The 2026 Victoria’s Secret Fantasy Bra was created with JACOB & CO. jewelers and features an “angel-cut stone.”

“I wanted to create the most expensive Fantasy Bra ever made, but value alone was never enough,” jeweler Jacob Arabo told Victoria’s Secret. “For me, it had to be about the diamonds, the light, and the movement. We brought together exceptional diamonds in different shapes and sizes, including our proprietary Angel-Cut, and designed the piece so that it catches light with every step. The craft is extraordinary, and what matters most is the emotion it creates when you see it come to life on the runway. I wanna make this [the] most expensive bra ever made.”

Meanwhile, Adam Selman added, “When we decided to bring back the Fantasy Bra this year, I couldn’t think of a more perfect person to design it than Jacob. I have been a huge fan of his work for a very long time, and he has played a big role in shaping pop culture. I love his artistry, his craft, and his fearlessness. Jacob understands that a Fantasy Bra has to be spectacular, but what made him the right partner is that he designs for the person wearing it. That’s what Victoria’s Secret is all about.”
