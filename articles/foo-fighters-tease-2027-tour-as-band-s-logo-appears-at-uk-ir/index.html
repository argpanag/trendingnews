Foo Fighters tease 2027 tour as band's logo appears at UK & Ireland locations

TheTools · 16.11.2026 09:11 · source · permalink

It appears they're playing four shows next year

Foo Fighters are seemingly poised to announced 2027 UK & Ireland tour dates next week.

On Thursday (12th November), Wembley Stadium shared a photo of Foo Fighters’ iconic ‘FF’ logo outside the world-famous venue along with the caption ‘16.11.2026’ – strongly suggesting there will be an announcement on Monday.

Foo Fighters’ promoters SJM Concerts also posted two images on Instagram, one of Wembley and the other of the Foo Fighters’ logo projected onto Darlington Clock Tower in County Durham, and wrote: ‘16.11.26 9am 👀🎸’

The exact location of the potential Darlington gig is unclear, however fans have speculated whether it could be at the 25,500-capacity rugby stadium Darlington Arena.

Scottish promoters PCL Presents also shared in image of the FF logo outside Edinburgh’s Scottish Gas Murrayfield Stadium and other locations in the Scottish capital, with one person quipping: ‘Franz Ferdinand! Excellent!’

At the same time as the UK teases, the band’s Irish promoters MCD Productions posted a video of a drone display of the Foo Fighters logo above Dublin’s Croke Park.

Foo Fighters played two standalone concerts at Liverpool’s Anfield Stadium back in June, after playing two ultra-intimate concerts at London O2 Shepherd’s Bush Empire and Manchester O2 Ritz in February.

Dave Grohl and co. were strongly rumoured to be headlining Download 2027, however last night it was announced that blink-182, Bring Me The Horizon and Evanescence will top the bill at the Donington rock extravaganza.
