One Piece Creator Oda Officially Confirms The Battle of Gods for The First Time

Elbaf is heading toward one of the biggest battles in One Piece history. With Imu entering the battlefield in a new, terrifying form and Luffy showing a frightening new side of his Devil Fruit, Eiichiro Oda is bringing two of the story’s most important god-like figures closer to a clash.

Interestingly, their connection goes back to the Void Century, making this much bigger than an ordinary fight. Following Chapter 1198, all eyes are on Chapter 1199, scheduled for November 22, 2026, in the US. The upcoming chapter has several exciting developments in store.

The biggest attraction of Chapter 1199 is undoubtedly the upcoming clash between Luffy and Imu. However, this isn’t just another fight between two powerful characters. Their conflict is connected to Elbaf’s ancient lore, the Void Century, Joy Boy’s legacy, and the World Government’s efforts to hide the truth about the past.

Imu arrived in Elbaf in Chapter 1179 and quickly turned the island into a battlefield. The mysterious ruler has already shown terrifying powers, including the ability to control others through Domi Reversi. Even Loki, who possesses the legendary Ryu Ryu no Mi, Model: Nidhogg, struggled against Imu.

One Piece Chapter 1198 made things even more intense. After overpowering Loki, Imu sensed something connected to Nika as Luffy disappeared from the battlefield. Meanwhile, the Straw Hat captain appeared in Western Village with a completely different appearance.

His monkey-like body, dark appearance, strange behaviour, and uncontrollable laughter caught everyone’s attention. He even destroyed the Rain God Zaza MMA, showing just how dangerous this new form can be.

This transformation also raises a serious problem for Luffy. His Hito Hito no Mi, Model: Nika, represents freedom, laughter, and liberation. However, the latest events suggest that the Devil Fruit’s awakened Zoan instincts are taking control of his body.

Meanwhile, the contrast between Sun God Nika and “Devil God” Imu makes this fight even more exciting. While Nika stands for freedom, Imu rules through fear and control. Their connection to Elbaf’s ancient lore and murals, as well as the ancient war involving Imu and Joy Boy, gives this confrontation an importance that goes far beyond Elbaf.

And as things stand right now, the next chapter is set to bring back that ancient confrontation to the present timeline with the long-awaited conflict between the two Gods.

Luffy and Imu aren’t the only reasons to look forward to One Piece Chapter 1199. Oda has also been building several interesting storylines involving Elbaf’s past, ancient technology, and Nefertari Lily.

One of the biggest mysteries revolves around the Owl’s Library, where Biblo has been sharing information about the Void Century. A hidden chest containing a mysterious tool and a message connected to Lily has also caught Robin and Chopper’s attention.

Lily was one of the original twenty rulers who helped establish the World Government but refused to become a Celestial Dragon. Her connection to the Void Century makes this discovery extremely important.

The mysterious tool is another major talking point. Imu specifically demanded Elbaf’s ancient technology while confronting Loki, suggesting that the island possesses something the World Government desperately wants. The connection between Lily’s belongings and Imu’s demands makes this mystery even more interesting.

Meanwhile, Zoro and Sanji are also unlocking new abilities during their clash with the Holy Knights. And with Lily’s message, ancient technology, the Void Century, Luffy’s new transformation, Imu’s terrifying new form, and several major story developments coming together, Elbaf is entering its most important stage yet.
