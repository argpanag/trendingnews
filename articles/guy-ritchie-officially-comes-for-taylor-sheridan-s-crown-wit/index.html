Guy Ritchie Officially Comes for Taylor Sheridan’s Crown With 4 Series in Production

In his time at Collider, he has also helped drive diversity by writing stories about the multiple Indian film industries, with a goal of introducing audiences to a whole new world of cinema. 

Guy Ritchie has cracked the code. Despite not having enjoyed a box-office hit in five years, he has successfully cemented himself as one of the busiest filmmakers in the business. Not only has he released five movies in the last half a decade — he has three more lined up — but he has also been producing hit streaming shows for different platforms. It's reached the point where he's right up there among the best in the business, all of whom are just as prolific.

Ritchie's movies, including the less successful ones, seem to find an audience at home. Their theatrical runs essentially serve as marketing for their eventual home-video releases. For instance, no film directed by Ritchie since 2021's Wrath of Man has broken even at the box office. These films include​​​​​​​ the caper movie Operation Fortune: Ruse de Guerre, the World War II action movie The Ministry of Ungentlemanly Warfare, the war drama The Covenant, and most recently, the action thriller In the Grey, which grossed less than $30 million worldwide against a reported budget of $70 million.

Ritchie also directed the big-budget Apple TV movie Fountain of Youth, which received poor reviews and didn't exactly turn into the franchise-starter it was supposed to be. Next up, the filmmaker has the crime thriller Wife & Dog lined up for release in early 2027, followed by Viva La Madness in early 2028. He is also working on a Netflix original movie starring Jason Statham.

Guy Ritchie Is Expanding His Empire

As if this weren't enough, Ritchie has four streaming shows in different stages of development. He's working on the third season of the Paramount+ hit MobLand and the third season of his Netflix hit The Gentlemen. The second season of the Prime Video series Young Sherlock has wrapped and is ready for release. Ritchie is also putting together a new crime drama set in contemporary London, titled Capital.

Taylor Sheridan is perhaps the only other television creator with a comparable output​​​​​​​, whose work typically dominates the Paramount+ streaming charts at any given time of the year. Beginning with Yellowstone, Sheridan has worked on a series of blockbuster shows for the streamer, with more in the pipeline. Ritchie outdid him​​​​​​​ with the premiere of MobLand, which broke records Sheridan had set for Paramount+ last year​​​​​​​. It'll be interesting to see if Ritchie is able to take Sheridan's place as Sheridan transitions from Paramount to Universal in the coming years. Stay tuned to Collider for updates.
