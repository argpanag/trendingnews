Strategies Using NBA DraftKings Betting Splits Data

One of the most touted features on the VSiN.com website, and a feature that we believe to be a fantastic resource for bettors, is the betting splits pages. These are the pages that we have built utilizing the data that DraftKings so graciously provides to us, detailing the breakdowns of the money & ticket splits for point spreads, money lines, and totals. 

In an article published on the VSiN.com website on opening day, Steve Makinen outlined 14 different systematic strategies for successfully using the DK Betting Splits Data that were developed in the last three seasons. Here are the systems and qualifying plays for today’s games as of 11:45 a.m. ET. These can and will change, so continue to track and qualify the systems up until tip-off for best usage.

Looking back at the DK betting splits data for the full 2025-26 NBA regular season, there were 1322 games played for the sample. To summarize the overall findings, the side categories are almost identical to the 2024-25 season results, meaning, we should be continuing to see great results for all or most of the systems we’ve continued to track. The total numbers were much worse, indicating the public’s successes in the 2024-25 season may have been a fluke occurrence. In all, four of the six categories showed losses worse than -7% ROI. These were the results:

– Majority handle on point spreads: 653-652 ATS (50%), -64.2 units – ROI: -4.9%
– Majority number of bets on point spreads: 625-652 ATS (48.9%), -92.2 units – ROI: -7.2%
– Majority handle on money lines: 847-458 (64.8%). -131.65 units – ROI: -10.1%
– Majority number of bets on money lines: 894-424 (67.8%). -104.3 units – ROI: -7.9%
– Majority handle on totals: 650-643 (50.3%). -57.3 units – ROI: -4.4%
– Majority number of bets on totals: 636-667 (48.8%), -97.7 units – ROI: -7.5%

DK NBA Betting Splits system #1: When 63% or more of the handle was on the side of a home team playing as a four-point favorite or less (or as an underdog) on ATS wager, this “super” majority group has gone 235-205 ATS (53.4%) over the last four seasons, good for +9.5 units of profit and a ROI of 2.2%. This is fairly a profitable system that the public seems to get right consistently.
System Matches (PLAY ALL): NEW YORK (-3.5 vs DET), LA LAKERS (-2.5 vs POR)

DK NBA Betting Splits system #2: When 59% or more of the handle has been on the road side of an ATS wager, this “super” majority group has gone 976-890 ATS (52.3%) over the last four seasons. This has produced -3.0 units and a ROI of -0.2%. When a good portion of the overall money is on a road team in the NBA, it wins more often than not.
System Match (PLAY): SACRAMENTO (+18.5 at SAS)

DK NBA Betting Splits system #4: In another somewhat rare situation, but when there was a majority handle backing the team with fewer wins on the season in the NBA over the last 3+ seasons, that group produced a 565-511 ATS record (52.5%), good for +2.9 units of profit and a ROI of 0.2%.
System Matches (PLAY ALL): NEW YORK (-3.5 vs DET), SACRAMENTO (+18.5 at SAS)

DK NBA Betting Splits system #7: The number 63% or higher has been a key super-majority number in non-divisional conference games over the last 3+ seasons, with handle groups of that size boasting a 676-597 ATS record, good for 53.1%. This is about a 3% improvement on the overall majority figures and has netted +19.3 units of profit and an ROI of +1.5%.
System Matches (PLAY ALL): NEW YORK (-3.5 vs DET), SACRAMENTO (+18.5 at SAS), LA LAKERS (-2.5 vs POR)

DK NBA Betting Splits system #9: There has been a massive disparity of success levels for majority groups on handle when they back the home or road team on money line wagers. On home teams, these majorities have gone 1738-779 over the last three seasons but for -315.86 units and an ROI of -12.5%. On road teams, these majorities have gone 1,329-878 but for just -54.25 units and an ROI of -2.5%. This 10%+ disparity warrants fading ML majorities on home teams consistently.
System Matches (FADE ALL): NEW YORK ML (-155 vs DET), LA LAKERS ML (-135 vs POR)

DK NBA Betting Splits system #11: One of the best, simplest, and most frequent angles I have been able to uncover is when a majority of handle bettors were backing the Under in an NBA game. When any majority number has favored the Under option over the last four seasons, the results have been strong, with Under the total on these games going 554-461, good for 54.6%, +46.9 units, and an ROI of +4.6% since January 2023.
System Match (PLAY UNDER): BKN-BOS (o/u at 223.5)

DK NBA Betting Splits system #12: The average NBA game total since January of 2023 has been a bit over 228. In NBA games with “low” totals, or those less than 220, majority handle bettors have gone an impressive 487-422 (53.6%), while majority number of bets groups are a bit less at 480-438 (52.3%).
System Match (PLAY): OVER – DET-NYK (o/u at 218.5)
