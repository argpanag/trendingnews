    Zack Gelof homers and triples in win over Mariners

    Zack Gelof

    ATH 3rd Baseman #20
    Player Stats

    Zack Gelof homered and tripled to help the Athletics to a 7-6 win over the Mariners on Friday.

    Gelof has homered in three-straight games, and he hit the fourth and final homer of the third inning off Logan Gilbert to give the A’s a 5-2 lead. His homer was his 17th, and the triple was the second of the 2026 campaign. Gelof has looked the part as of late, and reminded why he was one of the top hitting prospects in baseball not all that long ago.
    - Christopher Crawford
