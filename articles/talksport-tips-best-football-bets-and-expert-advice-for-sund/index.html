Bet of the day

Rangers vs Celtic - Celtic to win @ 29/20

Celtic have been dominant on the domestic stage this season, winning all seven matches, and they can strike a blow against bitter rivals Rangers to reach the Scottish Cup semi-finals.

Hoops boss Martin O'Neill clearly had one eye on this cup clash when naming his side for Wednesday's 1-0 win at St. Johnstone, with Camilo Duran, Benjamin Nygren and Kieran Tierney all named on the bench.

It was a different story for Rangers, who named a strong XI for their 1-0 home win over St. Mirren on the same night and the Gers' inability to rest key players could work against them.

Celtic are unbeaten in their last three meetings against Rangers, drawing one and winning two (one on penalties), and the likely recall of several key players should give the Hoops the edge.

Bet builder

Sheffield United vs Wolves - Over 2.5 goals, Sydie Peck to be booked & Fernando Lopez 1+ shots on target @ 29/4

Sheffield United welcome Wolves to Bramall Lane and it looks difficult to pick a winner between two evenly matched teams. But there is still value to be had in the form of a bet builder for this Championship clash.

Begin with over 2.5 goals, which has landed in the Blades' last five matches and in six of Wolves' seven fixtures this season.

The second leg is for United midfielder Sydie Peck to be booked. The 21-year-old was cautioned in the draw against Cardiff last month and he has committed two fouls in each of his last two appearances.

Peck's luck in avoiding a second yellow could be about to run out against a Wolves side who boast plenty of attacking quality and may see their fair share of possession.

Round off this bet builder with Wolves forward Fernando Lopez to have at least one shot on target, something he has achieved in four of his six starts.

Acca

Brighton to beat Coventry, Barcelona to beat Levante & Draw in Celta Vigo vs Malaga @ 6/1

Coventry have no goals and no points from their first three Premier League matches and more disappointment could be in the offing when they host Brighton.

The Seagulls have suffered just one defeat in all competitions this season - a 4-3 loss at Chelsea - while they romped to a 4-0 win over Aston Villa on the opening weekend.

Fabian Hurzeler's side are playing free-flowing, attacking football and they can add to the early woes for the Sky Blues.
