Gleeson calls time on Waterford career at the age of 31

TheTools · 18.09.2026 07:22 · source · permalink

Waterford's Austin Gleeson has announced his retirement from inter-county hurling, bringing an end to a Déise senior career that began in 2014.

Gleeson, now 31, was part of the Waterford side that contested the 2017 and 2020 All-Ireland finals, this after he was named both Hurler of the Year and Young Hurler of the Year in 2016. A year previous, the Mount Sion clubman collected senior silverware after his county got the better of Cork in the Division 1 league final.

There was also All-Ireland success, with a minor medal won in 2013 and an Under-21 victory three years later.

After opting out of the Waterford set-up in 2024, Gleeson returned and was part of the squad during Peter Queally's two years at the helm, though he did not see any championship action, with injuries curtailing the forward's involvement.

Gleeson's decision, perhaps, is not surprising, given the signals he was getting earlier this year.

Speaking on Off The Ball, he said: "I'm done now. I knew it at the start of 2026. There was one training session I remember in particular where it didn't go the best. It was 23 December; we trained down in Passage.

"I remember we were running. My head was two or three steps ahead of my body. I remember thinking, that’s the sign there."

Gleeson also admitted to not managing properly the levels expected of a modern day inter-county player.

"I probably didn't look after my body in the early and mid 20s and paid the price for it," he added.

"It's probably well-documented that I didn’t look after myself in the off-seasons as well as I should have.

"I’m fairly content I’m stepping away now but I do wish that I looked after myself a bit more seven or eight years ago."
