Avril Lavigne has welcomed her first child, a baby girl. The Canadian singer shared the news on Instagram on November 13 with a series of photos celebrating the new arrival. Lavigne has kept the identity of the father private.

In the first photo, Lavigne and another person could be seen holding the baby's small hand. The second slide showed Lavigne holding the child, who wore a leopard print onesie and matching headband. A third photo showed the family's matching black and white checked Vans lined up on a skateboard.

"Our Mini Fucking Princess has arrived 🎀👑🍼🩷," she wrote alongside the photos.

Avril Lavigne visits the SiriusXM Studios on May 29, 2025, in New York City.

The 42-year-old singer was previously married to Deryck Whibley, the Sum 41 frontman, from 2006 until 2010. She later married Nickelback frontman Chad Kroeger from 2013 until 2015.

Appearing on the "Call Her Daddy" podcast on May 15, 2024, the singer reflected on her romantic history. "I never really did the dating thing," she said. "I was always, like, in a pretty major relationship."

She added that she was "fucking awesome" in a relationship. "I would date me. I cook. I'm a really big cook, really big into, like, the house and decorating, and I have a lot of fun with that," she said.

Lavigne hasn't spoken much about parenthood in the past, but way back in 2013 after marrying Kroeger, the subject came up. She explained to Fame Magazine that she didn't feel ready at the time. "You know what, I want to be ready," she said of welcoming a baby. "Focusing on my music, just releasing a new record this is my priority and my baby right now."
