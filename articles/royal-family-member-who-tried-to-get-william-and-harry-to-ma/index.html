Royal family member 'who tried to get William and Harry to make up' named - AOL

TheTools · 30.10.2026 08:18 · source · permalink

The surprising member of the Royal family who has reportedly tried to broker a reconciliation of Princes William and Harry has been revealed. It's thought the brothers have not spoken for years, with the duo said to have last met up in 2024.

The key change in their dynamic is that Harry has now moved across the Atlantic to return to the UK with wife Meghan and their children, Archie and Lilibet.

And efforts have been made to get Harry and William to reconnect, it was reported on Thursday, October 29. The Royal who has tried to get the brothers to make up has been named in Woman and Home magazine. Royal editor Emily Andrews wrote "Harry has always been close to Zara and Mike Tindall".

She added: "Mike repeatedly tried to broker reconciliation between the brothers by inviting both of them to dinner or drinks - or just to knock heads over a beer."

The efforts "came to naught", it was reported. The comments come after Harry said he wants to "work out" a "sibling disagreement" when asked about his relationship with older brother William in a recent interview.

However, he added "some people don't want it to work out". A spokesperson for Harry later clarified that his comment about "some people" not wanting to work things out was not intended as a swipe at William or any other member of the Royal Family.

The path to reconciling has appeared to become clearer this week as Harry and Meghan have reportedly decided to step back from public appearances temporarily to avoid sharing the limelight with the King and Prince of Wales.

The Duke and Duchess of Sussex faced some criticism when a recent trip to Nottingham fell on the same day as Charles' ceremonial opening of the Welsh Parliament. The diary clash was caused by the Sussex office and Buckingham Palace not sharing enough information of their respective schedules accidentally.

But the Sussexes' step back from the spotlight means King Charles' Caribbean tour, and William and Catherine's trip to India next month, will be the sole focus of the Royal universe.
