Up-and-coming actress Noa Nakada dies at 23 - TokyoReporter

TheTools · 16.11.2026 04:39 · source · permalink

TOKYO (TR) – Up-and-coming Japanese actress Noa Nakada has died at the age of 23, her talent agency, Toho Entertainment, announced on Monday.

“We would like to express our deepest gratitude to the fans who supported her and to all the people who took care of her,” Toho Entertainment said in a statement.

The agency did not disclose the cause of death or the exact date of her passing, stating only that she died “recently.” A private funeral was held by the actress’s family members.

Nakada, a native of Ishikawa Prefecture, rose to prominence after becoming a finalist in the 8th Toho Cinderella Audition in 2016. At the time of her death, she was appearing as Kanna Izumi in the ongoing TV Tokyo series “Ultraman Teo.”

Her final social media post, dated November 6, showed no signs of distress. In the post, she thanked fans for watching the 16th episode of the show and provided details about the upcoming 17th episode.
