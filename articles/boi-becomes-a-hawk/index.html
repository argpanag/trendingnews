Boi becomes a Hawk

TheTools · 20.11.2026 11:05 · source · permalink

Keenan Boi is officially a Hawk!

Hawthorn has officially welcomed Next Generation Academy product Keenan Boi to the club after being selected with Pick 59 in the 2026 Telstra AFL Draft. 

A powerful and skilful forward-midfielder standing at 179cm, the Warragul junior claimed Gippsland Power’s best and fairest award in their Talent League premiership campaign while averaging 15 disposals and four tackles per game, in addition to booting 23 majors from his 17 matches.

Boi also impressed in Victoria Country’s win over the Allies to open the National Championships, picking up 16 disposals, laying five tackles and kicking two goals.
