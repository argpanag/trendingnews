Bank of Baroda Manager Held in Rs 9 Lakh Bribery Case

TheTools · 20.11.2026 03:16 · source · permalink

Bank of Baroda Manager | The Central Bureau of Investigation has arrested a Bank of Baroda manager and two direct selling agents in Dehradun in a Rs 9 lakh bribery case, the agency said on Thursday.

The manager heads the bank’s Indira Nagar branch. A court has sent all three to judicial custody.

The CBI registered the case on November 18 against the manager and three private persons who work as direct selling agents. The complaint alleged a demand for 5 per cent of a Rs 1.8 crore loan, which comes to Rs 9 lakh. The money was allegedly meant to facilitate the sanction and disbursement of the loan.

The CBI said the complainant had applied for the Rs 1.8 crore loan at the Indira Nagar branch. The accused had earlier taken Rs 2.5 lakh in cash, the agency alleged. They then pressed for the remaining Rs 6.5 lakh.

As instructed, the complainant handed over two undated cheques, the CBI said. They were for Rs 3.8 lakh and Rs 2.2 lakh, a total of Rs 6 lakh. The complainant also said Rs 50,000 would be paid in cash.

The CBI laid a trap on November 18 and caught one agent red-handed accepting the Rs 50,000. The branch manager and another agent were arrested afterwards. The agency also searched the homes of the three arrested accused. It seized incriminating documents and cash, but the release does not give the amount.

The three were produced before the competent court in Dehradun on Thursday and sent to judicial custody. The plea for police custody remand will be heard on November 23.

The CBI said further investigation is in progress. These are allegations at this stage, and no court has found anyone guilty.
