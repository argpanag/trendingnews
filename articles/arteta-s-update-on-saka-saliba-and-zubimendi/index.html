Mikel Arteta has issued an update on the fitness of our first-team squad after the international break, as we prepare to travel north to face Newcastle United on Saturday afternoon (5.30pm UK). 

Our only injury concern from the November internationals saw Martin Zubimendi forced off for Spain in their 3-2 defeat to England, with what the boss revealed was a hamstring injury. 

"He's out," Mikel said on Martin, "unfortunately he has a slight problem in his hamstring. 

"He wasn't too concerned to be fair after the game, but after the MRI scans, it showed that there is an injury there and he's going to be out for a few weeks."

As well as missing Martin , we'll still be without Bukayo Saka for a few more weeks as the forward recovers from a muscle injury. 

The boss reported that Bukayo's recovery is going well, but that he still has steps to take before he's ready for a return to action. 

"We had another test, he's evolving really well, but it looks like it's going to be an eight-week injury more or less. I think he's at four [weeks] at the moment, so it's still a while for him to be back with the team."

While we'll be without Bukayo and Martin for a few weeks, we'll be able to welcome back a long-term absentee in William Saliba for our trip to Tyneside. 

Wilo was on the bench for our 2-1 win over Slavia Prague in the Champions League and started for our under-21s in their 5-2 win over Aston Villa before the break, and now the Frenchman is ready for first-team action. 

"He's ready, he's in good condition. We want to ramp up his minutes. He's played two games, one [behind] closed doors, and the other one more official. He did very well [in both games] and he's in good condition." 

With no other specific injury concerns to note, Mikel was asked about the heavy travel load some of our players have experienced during the international window. 

Brazilian pair Gabriel and Bruno Guimaraes flew back from Singapore where they'd been with their national team, but Mikel is confident they'll be ready to go at St. James' Park. 

"Both Gabi and [Bruno] came back, the same as the other internationals apart from Zubi, as I mentioned, so they are in good condition. 

"We have another training session this afternoon, we have to make sure we nail the preparation because we know the game to expect tomorrow, how difficult it is to play against Newcastle. 

"[They have] a new manager, new ideas, obviously a new philosophy, and Bruno knows them really well, so we're ready for that."
