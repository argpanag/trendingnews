The best early October Prime Day Apple deals

Apple AirPods Pro 3

AirPods Pro 3 are comfortable to wear and have great noise-canceling and transparency modes, call quality and battery life. The buds also have advanced on-ear controls, including swiping on each ear stem to adjust volume. They come with five ear-tip sizes, which is more than most brands, so you get the most comfortable fit for your ears. These AirPods were at a lower price during Prime Day in June, so it is worth keeping an eye on them to see if they drop even lower over the next few weeks.

Apple AirPods Max

Apple’s over ear headphones have excellent sound quality, noise cancelling, transparency and call quality. Currently, only the starlight color option is on sale, but that may change as Amazon Prime Day gets closer.

Apple Pencil Pro

The Apple Pencil Pro acts more like a real pencil than other models, making it a better fit for artists and serious note takers. That’s because it has extra features like double-tap and squeeze functions, pressure sensitivity and more. It’s compatible with the iPad Air, iPad Pro and latest iPad Mini.

Apple Magic Keyboard Folio

Compatible with a 10th-generation iPad, this keyboard turns your tablet into a laptop-style workstation. It has a comfortable scissor-mechanism keyboard, a click-anywhere trackpad, and a 14-key function row. The two-piece magnetic design offers front and back protection, a detachable keyboard, and an adjustable stand for flexible viewing angles.

Best early October Prime Day Beats deals

Beats Studio Pro

Beats are also on sale, and these over-ear headphones pair easily with Apple devices and have super long battery life (up to 40 hours). They connect wirelessly over Bluetooth or over a wired connection using the included cables, which increases sound quality in certain apps. Plus they have noise canceling and transparency modes.

Beats Powerbeats Pro 2

The large ear hook shape keeps these earbuds locked in place. The buds themselves have active noise cancelling and transparency modes, which can help cut down background noise at the gym or at home.

Beats Solo 4 Headphones

These on-ear headphones last up to 50 hours on a single charge, far more than AirPods. Plus, 10 minutes of charging time gets you up to five hours of listening time. Like the Beats Studio Pro, they connect wirelessly but can also be plugged in with the included USB-C cable for more detailed audio quality in supported apps. They don’t have automated noise-cancelling or transparency modes, though.

Beats USB-C to USB-C Woven Cable 2-Pack

This pack includes two 5-foot cables with a tangle-free, reinforced braided design that resists fraying. They support 60-watt fast charging, syncing, audio, CarPlay, and USB 2.0 data transfer. Compatible with USB-C Apple and Android devices, they’re built to withstand daily wear.
