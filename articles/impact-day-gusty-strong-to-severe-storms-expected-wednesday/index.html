Impact Day: Gusty strong to severe storms expected Wednesday

TheTools · 13.10.2026 21:33 · source · permalink

It’s a warm one out there today!

LIVE RADAR // LATEST WEATHER ALERTS // TRAFFIC MAP

High temperatures will climb into the upper 80s this afternoon with plenty of sunshine, giving us a very summer-like feel for this time of year. If you’re a fan of the warmth, enjoy it while it lasts because changes are coming quickly over the next couple of days.

>>Go to the WLWT weather page | Subscribe to WLWT's YouTube channel

We have two cold fronts on the way, and each one will bring a chance of rain and thunderstorms before much cooler air settles in by Friday.

The first front arrives Wednesday. Scattered rain is possible between about 3 and 5 p.m., with a better chance for rain and storms from 6 to 10 p.m. These storms will be quick-moving, but a few could produce brief downpours and gusty winds.

The second front moves through late Thursday, with rain and storm chances mainly between 3 p.m. and midnight. This front will be the one that really brings in the cooler air.

Temperatures will start Friday in the low 70s around midnight, but they will tumble through the day. By Friday afternoon, we’ll already be falling through the 50s, so it’s going to feel like a completely different season compared to today.

Plan on a cool fall weekend, too. Saturday’s high will only be in the mid-50s, so you’ll definitely want the jackets ready!
