Tip of the Day: More European woe for Celtic

TheTools · 22.10.2026 08:16 · source · permalink

Celtic have struggled in Europe this season and our Tip of the Day predicts more misery for Martin O'Neill's men

Last Update:

The Europa League and the Conference League take centre stage tonight and we have identified two teams we fancy in Thursday’s Tip of the Day.

First up, we have Celta Vigo. The Liga side visit a Celtic outfit that enjoyed a resounding win over Hearts at the weekend to again demonstrate their strength domestically but they are struggling to make the step up to continental competition.

Martin O’Neill’s men exited the Champions League in the qualifiers, squandering a 3-0 first-leg advantage to LASK Linz, losing the second leg 5-1 in Austria.

They haven’t found the Europa League any easier, a 3-1 defeat at home to Ferencvaros followed by a 4-1 pummelling at Benfica.

Tonight, they host a Celta Vigo side who are also pointless in Europe’s second tier competition but sit eighth in the Spanish top flight and are unbeaten in six domestic games.

Our second tip is for AZ Alkmaar to win at Ararat Armenia.

AZ were unfortunate to lose 1-0 at Sunderland in their Europa League opener but bounced back with a 2-1 victory over Hapoel Beer Sheva.

They are going well in the Eredivisie, sitting second, and ought to be too good for a team that has yet to win in the competition, suffering convincing defeats to Sparta Prague and Jagiellonia.
