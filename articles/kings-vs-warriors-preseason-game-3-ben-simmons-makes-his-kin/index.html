Kings vs. Warriors Preseason Game 3: Ben Simmons Makes His Kings Debut - The Kings Herald

TheTools · 10.10.2026 23:08 · source · permalink

Independent Sacramento Kings Coverage. By Fans, For Fans. | Support Us On Patreon

Sep 28, 2026; Sacramento, CA, USA; Sacramento Kings guard Ben Simmons (25) during media day at Golden 1 Center. Mandatory Credit: Sergio Estrada-Imagn Images

After two lackluster preseason games against the Lakers, the Kings will have another opportunity to show us what they’re made of tonight against a different but familiar opponent: the Warriors.

Who: Sacramento Kings @ Golden State Warriors
Where: Chase Center, San Francisco, CA
When: 5:30 PM PT / 8:30 PM ET
Watch: League Pass, possibly other methods

Despite reports from NBA insider Marc Spears that Ben Simmons would be making his Kings debut against the Lakers on Thursday, that plan was apparently scrapped, and he didn’t play. Well, according to KCRA news reporter Sean Cunningham, tonight is the night. Ben Simmons is active and playing. Probably.

How many other Kings decide to show up remains to be seen.

Prediction: Kings 118, Warriors 109.
