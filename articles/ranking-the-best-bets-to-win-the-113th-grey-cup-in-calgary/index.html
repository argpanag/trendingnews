Ranking the best bets to win the 113th Grey Cup in Calgary

TheTools · 27.10.2026 14:41 · source · permalink

The 2026 CFL regular season delivered a few surprises.

The Calgary Stampeders scored a league-high 632 points and still missed the playoffs with a 9-9 record.

The Edmonton Elks entered the season with the longest odds to win the Grey Cup at FanDuel, then went on to finish with the best record in the West Division at 13-5.

The Ottawa Redblacks will be remembered as the CFL’s easiest team to fade, although fortunately they managed to avoid the first 0-18 season in CFL history.

Meanwhile, the Montreal Alouettes dominated again with a league-best 14-4 record, while the BC Lions, Toronto Argonauts and defending Grey Cup champion Saskatchewan Roughriders all made the playoffs.

Then there’s the Winnipeg Blue Bombers, which clinched the final playoff spot via the crossover with a 34-14 win over the Lions in Week 21.

The Bombers will visit Toronto in the CFL East Division Semi-Final on Saturday afternoon.

The Riders visit BC in the CFL West Division Semi-Final.

Which teams are the best bets to win the 113th Grey Cup in Calgary on November 15th?

Let’s rank the remaining contenders counting down from six through one in this Morning Coffee column for Tuesday October 27th, 2026.

Ranking The Best Bets To Win The 113th Grey Cup In Calgary

6. Saskatchewan Roughriders +1300

Riders Nation won’t like this, but no team faces a tougher road to the 113th Grey Cup in Calgary than Saskatchewan.

The Riders need to win games in BC and Edmonton just to make it back to the Grey Cup, where the odds are Montreal will be waiting for a potential rematch.

First up, Trevor Harris and company head west for a showdown with Nathan Rourke and the Lions.

Saskatchewan has the veteran QB and the experience, but they’re +235 to upset BC on Saturday – the longest odds to advance of the four teams playing this weekend.

Repeating as Grey Cup champions is a difficult task.

The Riders potential path to the 113th Grey Cup is as tough as it gets in the CFL this season.

5. Winnipeg Blue Bombers +1400

Brady Oliveira led the CFL with 1493 rushing yards.

The Winnipeg defence held opponents to a league-low 272.1 passing yards per game this season.

Zach Collaros is 7-2 all-time in the Grey Cup Playoffs, but he might not be available for Saturday’s game in Toronto.

The Bombers allowed a league-high 42 sacks, so even if Collaros is available, he could be in for a long day.

Winnipeg has the longest odds to win the Grey Cup of any remaining team at +1400 at FanDuel but is also considered far more likely to advance this weekend at +130 to upset the Argonauts.

An upset win in Toronto would leave the Bombers one win away from the 113th Grey Cup in Calgary.

4. Toronto Argonauts +550

The Argonauts were fortunate to play in an East Division that features the Redblacks and Hamilton Tiger-Cats, but they also put up some big numbers when quarterback Chad Kelly was healthy this season.

Toronto led the CFL with 342.5 passing yards per game.

The question is whether the Argonauts offensive line can hold up in the playoffs and allow Kelly the time he needs to connect with his four 1,000-yard receivers.

Toronto is a 3.5-point favourite versus Winnipeg.

However, with the rested Alouettes waiting for the winner of Saturday’s game in the CFL Eastern Final, I wouldn’t be willing to put my money on the Argos or the Bombers to reach the 113th Grey Cup in Calgary.

3. BC Lions +370

The Lions are one of the most talented teams in the CFL, but they’ll need to prove they can rise to the occasion against the tested Riders in the Western Semi-Final.

Nathan Rourke is one of the league’s best QBs, but he’ll need to outduel an established veteran who just led the CFL in passing yards and threw 32 touchdown passes in Trevor Harris.

If BC survives the defending Grey Cup champions, they’ll head to Edmonton to face the underdog Elks in much colder temperatures at Commonwealth Stadium.

Can the Lions win three straight against the Riders, Elks, and the Alouettes to secure the Grey Cup?

Rourke and company will need to play their best football, and get a little luck, for anybody who bets BC at +370 to win the Grey Cup at FanDuel to net a winner.

2. Edmonton Elks +240

The Elks were +1900 to win the Grey Cup at FanDuel entering Week 1 – the longest odds of any CFL team.

Fast-forward 22 weeks later, and the Elks have a first-round playoff bye and home-field advantage for this year’s CFL Western Final after going 13-5.

Cody Fajardo and the offence can still score in bunches, while the Edmonton defence held its opponents to a league-low 437 points.

The Elks went 8-1 at Commonwealth Stadium.

They need one more win at home against either the Lions or the Riders to punch their ticket to the biggest game of the year.

With the 113th Grey Cup in their own backyard in Calgary, Edmonton has the potential to cap off a remarkable year by becoming one of the biggest surprise champions in a long time in the CFL.

1. Montreal Alouettes +150

The Alouettes have gone nearly wire-to-wire as the obvious Grey Cup favourite at FanDuel.

After falling just short against the Riders last year with star quarterback Davis Alexander hurt, Montreal put together one of the most impressive seasons in recent memory by going 14-4 straight up and against the spread.

The Alouettes finished the regular season on a high note with five straight ATS covers and left little doubt they deserve their title as the Grey Cup favourite.

With Alexander performing at an elite level and a defence that helped Montreal finish with the CFL’s best point differential, it’s obvious this team has everything it needs to take care of unfinished business.

It helps that the Alouettes went 9-0 at home and will need one more win in Montreal over either Toronto or Winnipeg to book their trip to Calgary.

The Alouettes will be the favourite in the Eastern Final and as long they advance without any serious injuries, they will be a favourite again in the 113th Grey Cup.

It’s last call for anybody who wants to bet Montreal at +150 to win it all.
