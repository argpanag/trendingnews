The end of the saga: Jabari Parker and Partizan terminated the contract

Jabari Parker and Partizan terminated the contract by mutual agreement, which ended the saga that marked his stay in the black and white jersey

Basketball club Partizan announced on Friday evening that there was a mutual termination of the contract with the American Jabari Parker.

Parker arrived in Belgrade the previous summer as the most expensive signing in the club's history. Second spade NBA in the 2014 draft, he had problems with discipline, and Željko Obradović and Đoan Peñaroja removed him from the team.

Džabari Parker is no longer a basketball player Partizana Mozzart Bet! After his engagement in Huventudu ended, Parker returned to Belgrade, but was not part of the black and white team.

Today, the player and the club found a solution and to their mutual satisfaction reached an agreement on the end of cooperation.
