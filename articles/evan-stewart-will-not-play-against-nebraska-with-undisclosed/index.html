Evan Stewart will not play against Nebraska with undisclosed injury

EUGENE, Ore. — Oregon's beleaguered offense will be without its top receiver when it takes on Nebraska in a pivotal Big Ten game on Saturday night. Evan Stewart has been ruled out, the school announced via the final availability report of the week.

Stewart has caught a team-high 27 passes for 352 yards and four scores. His 44-yard touchdown against UCLA brought the Ducks within two points of tying the game. The specifics of his injury are currently unknown.

Stewart isn't the only major part of Oregon's offense unavailable against the Huskers. Starting quarterback Dante Moore is set to miss his second straight game with a concussion, and wide receiver Jeremiah McClellan, who'd been the team's top receiver over the first two games, will miss his fourth consecutive game. 

Starting right guard Dave Iuli will be a game time decision the report also stated. Iuli has started the previous four games for the Ducks. He had been listed as probably on Friday's availability report.

Game-time decision: OL Dave Iuli
OUT: QB Dante Moore, DB Aaron Flowers, WR Evan Stewart, WR Jeremiah McClellan, RB Simeon Price, LB Brayden Platt and OL Trent Ferguson

Nebraska also reported on its injuries offering the following designations:
OUT: DB Jamir Conn, DL Riley Van Poppel, WR Larry Miles, DB Kahmir Pritchett, LB Jase Reynolds, LB Jacob Bower and DL Gabe Moore
