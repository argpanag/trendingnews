Man United player ratings vs Villa with one 2/10 but Dorgu good in crazy draw

TheTools · 08.11.2026 18:36 · source · permalink

Manchester United produced another incredible comeback at Old Trafford as two stoppage-time goals saw them rescue a 4-4 draw against Aston Villa.

United found themselves 2-0 down within four minutes at Old Trafford. Alysson forced a shot through Senne Lammens after 60 seconds before Lammens spilt a shot from the same player at the feet of Nicolas Jackson who converted

Once again they found a response when faced with a dire predicament. Bruno Fernandes scored a penalty before Youri Tielemans drew United level just before half-time.

Villa restored their lead right at the start of the second half, John McGinn scoring from the spot after a poor tackle by Diogo Dalot, before a defensive disasterclass allowed Leon Goretzka to get a fourth.

Joshua Zirkzee scored late on before Patrick Dorgu headed home from a corner to rescue a point in the 96th minute.

This is how the United players rated at Old Trafford:

Senne Lammens: At fault for both of Villa's goals in the first four minutes and then slow off his line later in the half to close down Alysson. A few questions being asked for the first time in his United career and was better in their box than his own. 3

Diogo Dalot: Should have done more to stop Alysson getting his shot away in the first minute. Played one awful pass into trouble and then conceded a penalty with a lazy tackle. 2

Leny Yoro: Passing a bit sloppy at times and Jackson gave him a few problems. Header against his own bar for Villa's fourth was a bizarre decision, although might have needed a shout from Lammens. 4

Harry Maguire: Recovered well from the early defensive chaos and was strong and decisive in the first half, keeping United on the front foot before being forced off. 6

Noussair Mazraoui: Good assist for Tielemans' equaliser but shaky at the back at times and his use of the ball still isn't good enough on a consistent basis. 5

Kobbie Mainoo: Strong in the tackle and won plenty of duels again. Makes such a difference to United's ability to play on the front foot. 6

Your Tielemans: Taunted by the away end so would have loved scoring in front of the Stretford End and it should have counted for more. Looked sharper and better on the ball than early in the season. 7

Amad: Worked hard off the ball and was a threat down the right at times, but missed a really big chance in the first half. Will get his rewards if he keeps it up like this. 6

Bruno Fernandes: Better than he has been and brought the stutter run back for the penalty, but frustration was getting the better of him after that dismal start to the second half. Kept going, however. 6

Matheus Cunha: Tested Suzuki with one shot and did well to win the penalty. Stuck to the left rather than getting a freer role but had his moments. 6

Bryan Mbeumo: Struggled to get involved in the game and can have matches like that as a striker. Doesn't have the physical presence to assert himself on defences. 5

Substitutes

Ayden Heaven: Booked 11 minutes after coming on for a poor challenge on Alysson. 4

Patrick Dorgu: Back in action and made a huge contribution to the comeback with an assist and a goal. 7

Mason Mount: Came on for Tielemans, who probably didn't deserve to go off. Hit the bar late on then wasted a chance. 6

Shea Lacey: Some neat footwork and tried to make something happen. 6

Joshua Zirkzee: Scored late on and caused problems. 7

Subs not used: Darlow, De Ligt, J. Fletcher, Santos
