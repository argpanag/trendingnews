Road closures and diversions announced for Pattaya’s 2026 fireworks

TheTools · 27.11.2026 05:28 · source · permalink

News

The Pattaya International Fireworks Festival 2026 opened on Friday (November 27, 2026) and continues on Saturday (November 28).

The festival takes place along the full length of Pattaya Beach in Chon Buri province.

Thansettakij has compiled the following travel information for visitors planning their journeys to the festival.

Three sections of road will close to traffic from 3pm to 1am the following morning.

Parking will be prohibited on both sides of these sections from 4am to midnight.

Drivers seeking to bypass the city can use the following routes.

Metal barriers will separate the leftmost lane of Pattaya Beach Road for use as a service lane between the Dusit Curve and the entrance to Walking Street.

Officers will oversee traffic along the entire route and open and close crossing points for members of the public and tourists heading to the beach.

Access to side streets numbered 1 to 13/4 will be closed off on both Pattaya Beach Road and Pattaya Second Road.

Officers will be stationed at each side street.

People staying or working in the area must show officers proof, such as written confirmation of an accommodation booking or a staff ID card.

Phettrakul Road

Traffic will run from the Hollywood junction to the Phettrakul junction.

Side streets 2 and 3 off Pattaya Second Road

Drivers must head towards Phettrakul Road, then turn left towards North Pattaya Road. A separate entry in the plan gives the same one-way direction between Pattaya Second Road and Phettrakul Road without identifying the street.

Pattaya Second Road

Vehicles may travel only towards the Dolphin Roundabout.

Central Pattaya Road

Between 10pm and 2am the following morning, traffic will run one way towards Sukhumvit Road. The same entry lists a parking ban from side street 1 to side street 13/4 without specifying separate hours for the restriction.

Free shuttles will carry passengers to and from the festival between 4pm and 11.30pm.

Thansettakij refers to two service points and lists the stops on each route in the following order.

Parking charges depend on the venue.

The programme begins at 5pm.

Highlights include spectacular fireworks displays from five countries (Japan, Malaysia, Italy, Finland and the Philippines), as well as free concerts by well-known acts PALMY, ROOFTOP, YourMOOD and Slapkiss.
