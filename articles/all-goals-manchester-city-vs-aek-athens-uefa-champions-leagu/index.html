Manchester City returned to winning ways in European competition after suffering a home defeat to two-time defending champions PSG, bringing joy back to their fans at the Etihad Stadium.

With Phil Foden scoring a hat-trick, Manchester City thrashed AEK Athens at the Etihad Stadium to get their UEFA Champions League campaign back on track


Manchester City secured a commanding 5-1 victory over AEK Athens on Matchday 3 of the 2026-27 UEFA Champions League league phase. 


Phil Foden was the star of the show, scoring a hat-trick at the Etihad Stadium as the English side showcased its attacking firepower and claimed three valuable points in the European competition.


FODEN SHINES WITH A HAT-TRICK

The scoring opened in the 12th minute when Elliot Anderson capitalized on a rebound after Enzo Fernández's free kick struck the crossbar.

Foden doubled City's lead in the 34th minute, finishing from close range after a well-worked combination with Rico Lewis.

AEK Athens responded in the 52nd minute through Stavros Pilios, who finished a counterattack with a low shot. 

However, Rayan Cherki restored City's two-goal cushion in the 57th minute with a brilliant individual effort, cutting inside the penalty area before firing the ball into the net.


MANCHESTER CITY SEAL THE ROUT

Foden scored his second goal of the night in the 65th minute, curling a precise finish from inside the box after another assist from Rico Lewis.

Finally, in the 76th minute, he completed his hat-trick by converting from close range after receiving a pass from Ryan McAidoo.

With a 5-1 victory, Manchester City bounced back from their defeat to two-time defending champions PSG, returning to winning ways to reach six points and climb to sixth place in the standings.


MANCHESTER CITY ROUT AEK ATHENS IN THE CHAMPIONS LEAGUE

  • PHIL FODEN’S HAT-TRICK: The English star scored three goals to lead Manchester City to victory.
  • ETIHAD STADIUM ROUT: City defeated AEK Athens 5-1 in the Champions League.
  • ATTACKING FIREPOWER: Elliot Anderson and Rayan Cherki also scored to seal the emphatic win for the Citizens.
