Liverpool picked up their first win of the Premier League season thanks to an Alexander Isak double against Ipswich at Portman Road on Friday night.

The Reds had underwhelmed under new boss Andoni Iraola, drawing their opener away to Newcastle before fighting back again to salvage a draw at home to Nottingham Forest.

However, two goals in a matter of minutes from lucrative striker Isak proved enough for Liverpool to win 2-0 on the night, with Cody Gakpo adding two fine assists to his resume this season.

Gakpo, who was heavily linked with an Anfield exit during the transfer window, has been the chief creator for Isak, having also set up the Swede for his goal against Forest.

Isak has come under a lot of pressure after failing to impress in his injury-laden debut season at Anfield, but fans will be enouraged by the fact he already has three goals this campaign.

Florian Wirtz stat concern for Liverpool fans

However, fellow marquee signing Florian Wirtz, who was signed in the same window as Isak for an astonishing fee, continued to frustrate despite the win.

Former manager Arne Slot was unable to get a tune out of the Bundesliga winner, something which played a part in his sacking at the end of last season.

It is now Iraola's task to get the German back to the levels he showed with Bayer Leverkusen in their league winning campaign under Xabi Alonso.

However, renowned Premier League statisicians WhoScored have shared an ominous statistic regarding Wirtz after Liverpool's first three games of the season.

Wirtz, who has been wasteful with the ball, has already lost possession a remarkable 53 times, averaging almost 18 infractions per game.

The 23-year-old was brought in to be Liverpool's primary creator in midfield, however it was the aforementioned Gakpo who supplied Isak so effectively for his goals.

Slot did trial Wirtz on the left-wing at times last season but those opportunities will likely be few and far between for Wirtz given the arrival of superstar winger Bradley Barcola.
