Spot gold declined 1.63 percent hovering just above 4,215 per ounce in the early session on September 28, while US gold futures traded with a 1.75 percent decline to trade at $4,245.40 per ounce (02:02 GMT).

MCX gold futures for the October 5 delivery declined 1.83 percent to Rs 1,48,115 per 10 grams, and silver futures for the December contract fell 2.6 percent to Rs 2,28,603 per kilogram from its previous close.

The domestic spot gold closed the Friday (Sept. 25) session at Rs 1,51,377 per 10 grams, while silver held its price at Rs 2,31,793 per kilogram.

Inflation pressures increased slightly as Brent moved above $106, with the Dollar Index rising to 101.15 this morning. Treasury yields remained elevated, and markets now price in a 68 percent chance of an October Fed rate hike.
