Social media reacts to excruciatingly awkward moment between Devils Jack Hughes and ESPN analyst

TheTools · 03.10.2026 12:19 · source · permalink

The ESPN analyst's quip about the Devils star's injury history during the Flyers game fell completely flat

A Caller From Somewhere In Time | Sports Talk with Cal McCallister Ep. 3

Cal and Jody hear from a caller wanting to talk about the Chicago Bears. That’s as normal as sports radio gets, until it starts to seem like this listener could be phoning in from across time and space.

The NHL season is just a few days old, and it's safe to say that we have a frontrunner for "Awkward Moment of the Year" already.

This one comes courtesy of New Jersey Devils star Jack Hughes and ESPN analyst AJ Mleczko.

The Devils hosted the Philadelphia Flyers for their season opener at the Prudential Center. After the first period, the Devils were down 1-0, and Hughes was called over to do an interview with Mleczko, who was stationed between the benches.

New Jersey Devils star Jack Hughes had an awkward moment with ESPN's AJ Mleczko. (Photo by Rich Graessle/NHLI via Getty Images)

Hughes offered the usual interview platitudes and seemed to be pleased with how his team was playing.

But then Mleczko decided to make a joke about the Team USA golden goal scorer's injury history, including an infamous moment in which he sustained a cut during a team dinner.

WYNDHAM CLARK TROLLS CANADIAN OPEN CROWD WITH TEAM USA JACK HUGHES JERSEY, GETS SHOWERED WITH BOOS

How did it go? Well, about as well as a dog fart at a wake.

Cue the "Curb Your Enthusiasm" music.

It's a rough one, and you've got a clear line of demarcation, with some going after Mleczko for making the joke.

Others said criticism of her was misogynistic or saw it as an opportunity to go after Hughes for laughing at a joke the president made after the gold medal game.

Personally, I think it was a perfect storm of awkwardness. Just an accidental mixing of social bleach and ammonia.

I think Mleczko is a really solid broadcaster, and everyone has moments where they say something they wish they could grab out of the air and pretend it never happened.

ESPN analyst and former Olympian AJ Mleczko had a joke fall flat on Thursday, and to make matters worse, she made it to the wrong player. (Photo by Bill Wippert/NHLI via Getty Images)

Any analyst who tried to make that joke would've ended up with egg on their face too.

Buuuuuuut...

Hughes is not known to be one of the more gregarious NHLers. If you make a joke like that, someone like the Flyers' Trevor Zegras or Florida's Brad Marchand would field it and probably volley one back in the other direction.

I think Mleczko's biggest mistake was taking the wrong approach with the wrong player.
