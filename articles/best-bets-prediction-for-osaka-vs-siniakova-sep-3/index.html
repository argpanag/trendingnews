The US Open Women Singles round of 64 continues Thursday with a fascinating clash between Naomi Osaka and Katerina Siniakova.

Osaka enters the court as the clear betting favorite, bringing her massive baseline game and world No. 13 ranking (2,846 points) into the matchup. Siniakova steps up as the underdog, currently sitting at No. 42 with 1,234 points

Osaka vs Siniakova goes at 11:30am ET from Arthur Ashe Stadium at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in Flushing Meadows, New York.

American viewers will be able to catch the action on ESPN, while viewers in Canada can tune in on TSN.

Check below for my best bet and predictions.

Osaka vs Siniakova Best Bets and Predictions

Osaka and Siniakova have crossed paths three times on the professional tour, with Osaka holding a 2-1 edge. Their most recent meeting took place in the 2025 Wimbledon round of 64, where Osaka dictated the terms of engagement from the first serve and won in straight sets.

The full series provides useful context without overstating its predictive value. Osaka has won both of the hard-court or grass-court meetings, including the 2018 Doha match in straight sets, while Siniakova’s lone victory came on clay at Roland Garros. That surface split reinforces the case for Osaka on US Open hard courts, particularly alongside her current hard-court Elo advantage and superior serving efficiency.

  • Pick: Osaka -4.5 Games (-113 at Polymarket)

My primary betting thesis centers on Osaka’s advantage in both current strength and hard-court performance. The Elo gap is large enough to support a meaningful favorite, and Osaka’s 77.9% service-games-won rate gives her a strong platform to protect leads once she gets ahead. Her 1.321 dominance ratio and 1.956 match-efficiency rating also show a player producing substantially better overall results than Siniakova’s 1.029 and 1.312 marks.

The matchup profile is favorable as well. Osaka averages more than three times as many aces per game and wins 68.8% of tiebreaks, compared with Siniakova’s 50.0%. Siniakova can make an impact on return, but Osaka’s superior serve-point numbers—72.3% won behind the first serve and 50.5% behind the second—should limit the underdog’s opportunities. With Polymarket offering -113 odds (53 cents), laying the 4.5 games is my most actionable recommendation.

  • Pick: Under 21.5 Total Games (-117 at Polymarket)

My second pick targets the Under 21.5 total games. The case for a short match begins with Siniakova’s weaker serving profile: she wins only 69.5% of service games and 45.4% of second-serve points in the 52-week comparison. Osaka’s aggressive returning and superior baseline power can turn those vulnerable second-serve points into early breaks, while Osaka’s own service numbers reduce the chance of a prolonged exchange of breaks.

Their three previous meetings also show that this matchup can produce a decisive result. Osaka won 6-3, 6-2 at Wimbledon in 2025 and 6-4, 6-0 in Doha in 2018, while Siniakova won 6-4, 6-2 on clay at the 2019 French Open. The hard-court meetings therefore favor Osaka, and the most recent encounter produced just 17 total games. I expect Osaka to secure breaks early and keep the final game count comfortably under the 21.5 threshold.
