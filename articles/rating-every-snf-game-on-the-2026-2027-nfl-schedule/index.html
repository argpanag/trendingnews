Rating every SNF game on the 2026-2027 NFL schedule

TheTools · 07.09.2026 17:40 · source · permalink

The 2026 NFL season is loaded with intriguing primetime matchups, and NBC’s Sunday Night Football schedule has plenty of games that should have fans counting down the days. From Super Bowl rematches to divisional rivalries and a historic Mexico City matchup, there are some excellent games on the slate.

Here is how DraftKings rates every game on the 2026-27 SNF schedule, including the NFL Kickoff Game and Thanksgiving night matchup. The official schedule confirms the slate through Week 17, with Week 18 still subject to being determined later in the season.

The highest-rated games

There are several games that immediately stand out.

The Chiefs at Bills Thanksgiving night matchup gets the highest rating at 9.8/10. Patrick Mahomes and Josh Allen have developed into one of the NFL’s premier quarterback rivalries, making another meeting between Kansas City and Buffalo a must-watch.

The NFL Kickoff Game between the Patriots and Seahawks earns a 9.6/10, largely because it is a rematch of Super Bowl LX. That’s an incredible way to begin the season.

The Cowboys at Packers receive a 9.0/10, while Rams at Broncos get an 8.8/10. The Chiefs at Seahawks also earn an 8.8, adding another Super Bowl rematch to the schedule.

Some sneaky-good matchups

Not every game needs to feature two Super Bowl contenders to be worth watching. The Buccaneers at Bears matchup gets a 7.7, while Vikings at 49ers in Mexico City earns a 7.9. Those could end up being much better games than they look on paper.

The Eagles at 49ers finale is another one to circle. At 8.5, it could have major playoff implications and potentially become one of the most important games of the entire regular season.

Overall, this is a strong SNF lineup. There are a few weaker matchups on paper, but the schedule is loaded with games that could become significantly more important once the 2026 season gets underway.
