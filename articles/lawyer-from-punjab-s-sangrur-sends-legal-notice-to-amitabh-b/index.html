Lawyer from Punjab’s Sangrur sends legal notice to Amitabh Bachchan over purported KBC video - The Statesman

TheTools · 22.11.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

Advocate Indrajeet Arora has alleged that the question shown in the video portrays Punjab in a negative light and has demanded its immediate removal from national media.

Neeraj Bali | Chandigarh | November 22, 2026 2:30 pm

Image: IANS

A lawyer from Punjab’s Sangrur has sent a legal notice to Bollywood actor Amitabh Bachchan and Sony TV over a video circulating on social media in which the actor is purportedly seen asking a question about bomb attacks on police stations in Punjab during the popular quiz show Kaun Banega Crorepati (KBC).

Advocate Indrajeet Arora has alleged that the question shown in the video portrays Punjab in a negative light and has demanded its immediate removal from national media. He has also sought an unconditional apology from Bachchan and the television channel if the video is found to be from an actual episode of the programme.

The authenticity of the video has not been independently established, and there are claims that it may have been created or manipulated using artificial intelligence (AI).

Arora said the matter came to his notice three to four days ago when one of his clients approached him with screenshots and a video purportedly showing Bachchan asking a contestant which state had recently witnessed criminals carrying out bomb attacks on police stations without fear.

“Three to four days ago, one of my clients approached me and showed me a screenshot and a video from KBC, the quiz show that airs on Sony TV, where general knowledge questions are asked to contestants and they win prize money as they give correct answers,” Arora said.

He said the question shown in the video had nothing to do with general knowledge and appeared to target the reputation of a particular state.

“I did not feel that this was a general knowledge question; this is a direct attempt to defame a state,” he said.

According to the purported clip, Bachchan introduces a question worth Rs 12.5 lakh and asks which Indian state has recently witnessed criminals carrying out bomb attacks on police stations without fear.

The four options shown are Uttar Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, West Bengal and Punjab. The contestant is then seen using the 50:50 lifeline, following which Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal are eliminated. Bachchan is purportedly heard identifying Punjab as the correct answer.

Arora said he had issued the notice to both Bachchan and Sony TV, seeking the removal of the video and clarification about whether the footage was part of an actual broadcast.

“For this reason, I have sent a legal notice to Sony TV and Amitabh Bachchan, demanding that it be immediately removed from national media, and if this video was made from their show, they should issue an unconditional apology,” he said.

The lawyer said he did not want Punjab or any other state to be portrayed in a manner that could damage its reputation.

He also expressed concern over the circulation of such content at a time when political activity was increasing ahead of elections in Punjab.

Asked about claims that the video could have been generated using AI, Arora said its authenticity would become clear after receiving a response from the actor and the television channel.

He said that if the footage turned out to be fabricated, legal action could be considered against those responsible for creating or circulating it.

The video, reportedly lasting about two minutes, has been shared on social media, where questions have also been raised about whether Bachchan actually asked such a question during the programme.

There has been no independent confirmation that the question was aired on KBC. The authenticity of the circulating clip remains unverified.

No public statement from Amitabh Bachchan or Sony TV addressing the legal notice could be independently verified at the time of filing this report.
