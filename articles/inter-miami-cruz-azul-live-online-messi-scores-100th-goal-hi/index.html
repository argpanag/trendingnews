One more title for Messi?

To no ones surprise, Lionel Messi is the winningest soccer player in the history of the game. Depending on how you classify official title with super cups and regional competitions, Messi has won 45-48 titles in his career. He won 35 with Barcelona including 10 La Ligas and 4 Champions Leagues, six with Argentina, 3 with PSG. He is on the verge of winning a trophy he has never won before, which is hard to come by when you have won as much as he has.

Cruz Azul's trophy room 

Cruz Azul is one of the winningest, most historic clubs in all of Mexico. From Ciudad de Mexico, and the famous Estadio Azteca, they have won Liga MX 10 times, the Copa MX four times and the Super Copa once. Internationally they are one of the most successful clubs CONCACAF, having won the Champions Cup seven times. They have also won the Leagues Cup once, and tonight they have a chance to put a Campeones Cup in their trophy cabinet after finishing runners up in 2021. 

Inter Miami's trophy room 

The South Florida side is one of the newest teams in the MLS, and for a young club their trophy room is quite impressive. Not surprisingly, all of those trophies were won once Lionel Messi arrived in Miami and took the league by storm. Their first title was the League’s Cup in 2023. Then they won the Supporters Shield in 2024, and even though that isn’t quite considered a massive trophy, it’s still hardware. After winning the MLS Cup last year they are looking took add to the trophy case today with a Campeones Cup.

History of the competition 

The Campeones Cup has been around since 2018, and this is the eighth edition of the yearly match. We saw the 2020 match get canceled because of COVID, but since the Liga MX and MLS Champs have met in this match around this time each summer. Tigres UANL beat Toronto FC in the inaugural Campeones Cup match back in 2018, and then the MLS took control for a few years. Atlanta United, Colombus Crew and NYCFC won in over the next three years, before Liga MX took the throne back and have held it for the last three years. Tigres UANL, America and Toluca all won the Cup over the last three years, and now Cruz Azul has a chance to keep the Cup in Mexico. 

Last year's cup

Last year the LA Galaxy and Toluca met in the Campeones Cup from Dignity Sports Park in Carson, California. The Liga MX side had to come back on two different occasions. Diego Fagundez scored the opener for the Galaxy, but Nicolas Castro equalized in the early minutes of the second half. Garbiel Pec put LA up in the 84th, but Franco Romero equalized four minutes later, and then Federico Periera scored the winner in the third minute of stoppage time. 

Cup setup 

The format of this cup is pretty simple. You take the winner of last year's MLS Cup and the winner of the aggregate in the apertura and the clausura and set them up in an all or nothing one match final. The host of the match is always the MLS side. 

Cruz Azul's recent form

Cruz Azul have found their form again after losing three straight in mid to late August. Wins over Necaxa, Santos Laguna and Club America have them sitting fourth in the Liga MX table. Eight matches into the apertura they are just four points behind Toluca in first place after the shootout win over Club America. Cruz Azul went up 3-0 in the first half, allowed a goal in the second as Club America tried to claw back in the match, but Gabriel Fernandez buried the match with the fourth goal in the 72nd minute. 

Inter Miami's recent form

The MLS champions have been in dreadful form over the last month and some change. They have only won one of their last nine matches dating back to the Leagues Cup matches back in early August. The one win came in a 7-1 smashing of CF Montreal at the end of August, but since then they have drawn three straight against Atlanta, Chicago and Nashville. Despite the dip in form, they are still sitting second in the MLS Eastern Conference standings, but with 45 points they trail first placed Nashville by nine points. 

Cruz Azul's path to Campeones Cup

Cruz Azul won the Clausura after finishing third in the league table. They finished three points behind Pumas UANL and Guadalajara to earn the three seed in the playoffs. They beat Atlas in the quarterfinals before taking on Guadalajara in the semis. They won that two legged tie 4-3 to set up a final with Pumas. After a 0-0 first leg, Cruz Azul won the Clausura with a 2-1 win away from home. They were named champions of the Liga MX after the aggregate from the apertura and clausura saw them finish one point above apertura winners Toluca. 

Inter Miami's path to Campeones Cup

While they had stars like Lio Messi, and Luis Suarez and Jordi Alba Inter weren’t the top team in the Eastern Conference last season. They finished third in the East, and had to get through a best of three match up in the first round of the playoffs. They took down Nashville winning the rubber match in the third game, and then moved on the Conference semifinals. Inter Miami thrashed FC Cincinnati 5-0 to get to the Eastern Conference finals against New York City FC. Another 5 goal performance moved Inter Miami to the MLS Cup final where they met the Vancouver Whitecaps. They would beat the Canadian side 3-1 to claim their first MLS Cup.
