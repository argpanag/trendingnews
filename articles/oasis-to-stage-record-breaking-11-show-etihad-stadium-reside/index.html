Oasis to stage record-breaking 11-show Etihad Stadium residency

TheTools · 15.09.2026 22:06 · source · permalink

With more than half a million tickets available across the run, the residency will be the biggest summer concert series in the history of the Etihad Stadium and one of the largest ever staged in the UK. 

For lifelong Manchester City fans Noel and Liam Gallagher, it also represents a return to Manchester City’s stadium for the first time in more than 20 years, reconnecting two of the Club’s most famous supporters with the team they have followed since childhood. 

The shows take place on: 

Friday 4 June 2027 

Sunday 6 June 2027 

Tuesday 8 June 2027 

Friday 11 June 2027 

Saturday 12 June 2027 

Tuesday 15 June 2027 

Friday 18 June 2027 

Saturday 19 June 2027 

Tuesday 22 June 2027 

Friday 25 June 2027 

Saturday 26 June 2027

For generations of Manchester City fans, the connection between Oasis and the Club has been a unique part of our shared story. 

From following City home and away as children to becoming one of the world’s most celebrated bands, Noel and Liam Gallagher have proudly worn their love for Manchester City throughout their careers.

Their famous Maine Road performances in 1996 remain among the most iconic moments in the histories of both the band and the Club, bringing together football and music in a way that only Manchester could. 

Next summer’s residency will mark the third time Oasis have performed at the home of Manchester City, further strengthening a connection that has endured for more than three decades. 

Demand for Oasis Live ’27 is expected to be extremely high, and so a registration process has been put in place to reduce queuing times and organised ticket touting in order to give genuine fans the best possible chance to buy tickets.

Registration is open now at www.oasisinet.com.  

Manchester City will only be responsible for the sale of hospitality packages at the Etihad Stadium. Further details will be announced in due course. 

We look forward to welcoming Oasis fans from Manchester, across the UK and around the world to the Etihad Stadium next summer for what promises to be a memorable month of live music.
