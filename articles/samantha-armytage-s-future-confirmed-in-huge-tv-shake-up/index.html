Samantha Armytage’s future confirmed in huge TV shake-up

TheTools · 17.09.2026 07:00 · source · permalink
  • Entertainment
  • Television

September 17, 2026 - 7.00am

Nine has announced its biggest line-up of TV shows yet, and with it, some fresh new faces for the coming year.

Married At First Sight welcomes a new relationship expert, The Block takes on the most ambitious shake-up in the show’s history, while Golden Bachelor Australia promises double the trouble for 2027.

Samantha Armytage will return in 2027. Two Golden Bachelors? That’s double the heartbreak and double the romance.  Nine

In addition to exciting new programming in 2027, there’s still so much incredible entertainment to come later this year, as announced during Nine’s annual Upfronts event.

Expect the biggest ever year of sport, plus an unmatched slate of news, entertainment and current affairs coverage from the 9Network, the only Australian-owned, fully integrated media network.

Biggest shake-ups set for 2027

The Block will be back but not as you know it.  Nine

The Block

In 2027, Australia’s biggest renovation show will take on the most ambitious project in the show’s history.

Season 23 will see five new teams tackle epic heritage restorations after two years of new builds. The 2027 Block location has also been confirmed: Jacksons Hill in Sunbury, Victoria.

The historic site boasts some of the state’s oldest and most magnificent heritage architecture, with buildings constructed 170 years ago.

Returning to co-host, Scott Cam and Shelley Craft will challenge next year’s newest Blockheads to preserve and revitalise these landmark buildings while transforming them into high-quality family homes.

The Block: The House Next Door

Scotty and Shelley have a special Block spin-off plan.  Nine

That’s right, 2027 can expect a brand-new Block spin-off series.

Premiering in 2027 on Stan, The Block: The House Next Door will give Blockheads plenty to talk about when fan-favourite all-stars tackle a sixth renovation over the fence.

The Golden Bachelor

After capturing the heart of the nation, The Golden Bachelor Australia returns to the 9Network bigger, bolder and with the double the romance.

In a major new twist, Season 2 will feature two very different Golden Bachelors, each bringing their own story, personality and approach to love as they find their second chance at forever.

That’s right, not one but two Golden Bachelors.

Will we find two happily ever afters next year? Nine

And this time, the power of choice is in the women’s hands. Our remarkable group of vibrant Australian women will have the opportunity to follow their hearts, choose their own path and decide which Golden Bachelor could be the one for them.

Beloved host Samantha Armytage returns in a season that brings twice the heartbreak, unexpected twists and plenty of unforgettable golden moments.

Married At First Sight

Australia’s No.1 reality TV show and most talked-about social experiment will make its highly anticipated return for Season 14.

MAFS experts Alessandra Rampolla John Aiken will return alongside the newest addition to the expert team, highly respected relationship expert Marryam Chehelnabi.

John Aiken and Alessandra Rampolla will be joined by new expert Marryam Chehelnabi on Married At First Sight in 2027.  Nine

The 2027 season promises sharp twists, explosive dynamics, fiery Dinner Parties, with romance at the very centre of every story.

Stan Original series MAFS: After The Dinner Party, streaming exclusively on Stan, with hosts Jules Lund, Brittany Hockley and Laura Byrne, will also make a hot return.

Travel Guides

Expect some new (and familiar faces) coming to Travel Guides 2027.  Nine

Hot on the heels of winning its fifth consecutive TV Week Logie, Travel Guides is back in 2027 with a new and returning cast announced.

All of our original Guides will return: the Fren Family, Kevin and Janetta, Matt and Brett, Kev, Dorian and Teng, and Karly and Bri.

And after their debut last year, Callum and Mitch from Love Island, and married country musicians Brooke and Adam, are back for more fun in the sun.

Plus, there will be three new cast groups joining the action: married couple and fan favourites from The Block, Tom and Sarah-Jane; Olympic swimming royalty and sisters Cate and Bronte Campbell; and best mates Fletch and Lachie, hot off travelling the world and creating their own way of accessible travel under the handle Two Mates 1 Chair.

The Campbell sisters will be going from the pool to the plane!  Nine

This year our Guides will hit ten more bucket-list destinations: from the Pyramids of Giza, to the shores of Santorini, and wonderfully weird tropical North Queensland.

Today

Today enters a new era in 2027, confirming hosts Sylvia Jeffreys and Tom Steinfort to helm Nine’s breakfast show.

Introducing your 2027 Today hosts.  Nine

Offering a fresh and light-hearted take on news, entertainment, lifestyle and celebrity, Today Extra returns each weekday from 9.00am with David Campbell and new host Belinda Russell.

What’s new in 2027?

The biggest night in music is coming to Nine.  Francis Specker/CBS via Getty Images)

The 2027 GRAMMY Awards®

Your all-access pass to the biggest night in music.

Broadcasting live from Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, the 9Network will give you front-row access to unforgettable live performances and history-making awards from the global music industry’s most prestigious night.

Australian viewers can catch every moment live on Channel 9 and 9Now.

Police

A premium documentary series taking viewers beyond the flashing lights and inside the real world of the Western Australia Police Force.

With remarkable access across the frontline and specialist policing, cameras ride alongside officers as ordinary shifts become extraordinary.

WA’s police like you’ve never seen them before.  Nine

Sweat It Out!

This hot new program charts the wild, untold rise and fall of the iconic Australian music scene affectionately dubbed Oz Rock.

From AC/DC to Zoot, the story comes alive through rare archival footage and first-hand accounts from the survivors who built a globally legendary sound out of beer-soaked pub floors and endless stretches of highway.

Returning in 2027

Rodger Corser is back for The Floor.  Nine

The Floor

Get ready for round 3! The Floor is back in 2027, with another 81 contestants ready to put their knowledge, nerve and strategy to the ultimate test.

Host Rodger Corser returns as a new class of Australia’s sharpest minds fight for the chance to take home the ultimate prize of $200,000.

Expect some familiar faces to step onThe Floor in 2027, including comedian Peter Helliar, The Golden Bachelor‘s Kim Morgan-Short, Olympic champion Kerri Pottharst, and Love Island’s Jess Losurdo.

With brand-new contestants, fresh categories and unexpected twists, Season 3 takes the competition to another level.

Tipping Point

Fresh off its victory as the 2026 Logie Award winner for Most Popular Entertainment Program, afternoon powerhouse Tipping Point Australia returns for another thrilling season.

Host Todd Woodbridge will also be back next year.  Nine

Beloved host Todd Woodbridge will return to host the hit series crowed as Australia’s No. 1 afternoon game show.

FLEX

Fresh off its groundbreaking debut, Australia’s premier micro-reality series returns for Season 2 to dissect the price of perfection.

Returning favourites Marcus, Henry, and Sarah are joined by a fresh batch of ultra-toned strivers navigating love, clout, and career meltdowns in Sydney’s most ruthless postcode.

Bigger drama. Sweatier secrets. Zero filters. Because in this playground, if you aren’t flexing, you’re fading.

Fans can catch daily micro-episodes on socials and full episodes weekly on 9Now.

The 2027 Australian Open

Maria Sharapova and Serena Williams pictured during day 13 of the 2015 Australian Open. Getty

The 2027 Australian Open will headline a smashing summer of tennis on Nine to kickstart the year of exclusive coverage of all four Grand Slams.

Beginning in January, the United Cup and Brisbane and Adelaide Internationals will provide the perfect entrée into Nine’s wall-to-wall, 15 day coverage of the 2027 Australian Open.

Expect a stellar commentary lineup featuring Todd Woodbridge, Jim Courier, Jelena Dokic, Alicia Molik, John McEnroe, Dylan Alcott, John Millman, Casey Dellacqua and Sam Smith.

Los Angeles 2028 Olympic Games

Nine will be the only destination for the Los Angeles 2028 Olympic Games.

Our Aussie heroes plot their path to glory in LA for what promises to be an unmissable, global sporting festival.

What history-making moment will we witness in 2028?  Getty

Chef’s Ahoy

Peter Helliar leads an exciting new cooking competition.  Nine

A vibrant and fast-paced new cooking competition will be sailing onto our screens.

Hosted by Australian comedian Peter Helliar, this light-hearted and colourful series drops two top chefs per episode into a high-seas culinary battleground.

Part high-stakes battle, part travel adventure, and all foodie delight, each episode puts destination-inspired cuisine front and centre.

Australia’s culinary favourites will go head-to-head in epic high-seas showdowns: Miguel Maestre vs. Dan Hong, Minoli De Silva vs. Courtney Roulston, Vincent Yeow Lim vs. Alice Zaslavsky, and Mark LaBrooy vs. Khanh Ong.
