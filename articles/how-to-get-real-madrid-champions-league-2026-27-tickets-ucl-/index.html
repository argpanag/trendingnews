How to buy Real Madrid Champions League tickets

Official Real Madrid Champions League tickets can be purchased online through the club's sales channels, Real Madrid Tickets and Entradas. Tickets do not go on sale months in advance, instead the club first releases them roughly 10-12 days before the specific match using a strict priority window waterfall system:

1. Phase 1: Socios (Club Members) - They receive the first opportunity to buy, usually around a fortnight before kick-off.

2. Phase 2: Madridista Premium Members - Cardholders get the next exclusive window, usually 7-8 days before kick-off.

3. Phase 3: General Public - Any remaining tickets are released to the general public approximately 4-6 days before the game.

For high-demand Champions League fixtures, matches frequently sell out during the member phases, leaving little to no inventory for the general public sale.

While the official club portal is the safest way for supporters to purchase Real Madrid tickets, those looking to attend a Champions League match may wish to consider secondary resale sites such as StubHub, which could give them the best opportunity of obtaining tickets.

How much are Real Madrid Champions League tickets?

Official Real Madrid Champions League general admission tickets typically start from €100 for standard League Phase matches, scaling up significantly to €300-€400+ for prime standard seats depending on the location and stage of the competition. Official VIP packages are priced starting from €375.

Real Madrid classifies its home fixtures into distinct categories that dictate base pricing:

  • Category A: Reserved for high-stakes fixtures like El Clásico or Champions League knockout rounds. Standard face-value seats for these matches often increase by 20% to 50% or more compared to earlier group/league phases.
  • Category B: Standard European League Phase games (such as matches against teams like RB Leipzig or PSV Eindhoven) and higher-tier domestic games.
  • Member Discounts: Official club members (Socios) and Premium Madridistas receive a 20% to 30% discount off the standard public face-value prices when purchasing directly via the Official Real Madrid Tickets Portal.

Keep tabs on the official Real Madrid site for additional information and on secondary selling sites such as StubHub for current ticket availability.
