India will aim to book its place in the SAFF Championship 2026 semifinal when it takes on Maldives in a Group B fixture in Dhaka on Wednesday.

Naushad Moosa’s side began the campaign with a 3-2 win over Pakistan last week, but his squad has been hit by a few withdrawls since then.

India, which is fielding a second-string team, has seen Farukh Choudhary, Noufal PN and Hmingthanmawia Ralte been released from the squad to return to their club, Mumbai City FC.

Bijoy Varghese, Muhammad Suhail and Liston Colaco have been asked to link up to the senior squad under Khalid Jamil for the two friendlies against New Zealand starting November 12.

Maldives, ranked 173rd in the world, lost its opening fixture to Pakistan 3-0 on Sunday.

When will the India vs Maldives SAFF Championship 2026 match kick off?

The India vs Maldives SAFF Championship 2026 match will kick off at 3:00 p.m. IST on Wednesday, November 11.

Where will the India vs Maldives SAFF Championship 2026 match be played?

The India vs Maldives SAFF Championship 2026 match will be played at the National Stadium in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Where to watch the India vs Maldives SAFF Championship 2026 match?

The India vs Maldives SAFF Championship 2026 match will not be telecast anywhere in India. The match will be livestreamed on the Veto app and website.
