All the latest team news and insights ahead of round four of Australia's premier first-class competition

Victoria v Western Australia, MCG, November 11-14 (10:30am AEDT)

Click here for the Match Centre

Victoria squad: Will Sutherland (c), Austin Anlezark, Liam Blackford, Peter Handscomb, Sam Harper, Marcus Harris, Harry Hoekstra, Campbell Kellaway, Blake MacDonald, David Moody, Todd Murphy, Mitch Perry

 

Ins: Blake MacDonald, Harry Hoekstra, David Moody

 

Outs: Oliver Peake (Australia duty), Matt Hennig (managed), Fergus O'Neill (neck injury)

Victoria squad: Will Sutherland (c), Austin Anlezark, Liam Blackford, Peter Handscomb, Sam Harper, Marcus Harris, Harry Hoekstra, Campbell Kellaway, Blake MacDonald, David Moody, Todd Murphy, Mitch Perry

 

Ins: Blake MacDonald, Harry Hoekstra, David Moody

 

Outs: Oliver Peake (Australia duty), Matt Hennig (managed), Fergus O'Neill (neck injury)

A high-profile return to the MCG could be just the tonic the Vics need to kick-start their faltering season that has left them rooted to the foot of the Sheffield Shield ladder with zero wins and just 4.98 points to their name. They were perhaps fortunate to escape the Gabba with a draw after a rain-ruined final day too. Four batters passed fifty in the first innings at the Gabba, but a second-innings collapse was worrying.

And this week they will be without young star Ollie Peake who has joined Australia's ODI squad for their series against England that starts in Perth this Friday, while Fergus O'Neill, one of those batters who passed fifty at the Gabba, has been sidelined with a neck injury. Rookie quick Matt Hennig has been rested after a fine debut with four first-innings wickets in Brisbane, with young gun Harry Hoekstra and David Moody returning to the fold. 

WA squad: Aaron Hardie (c), Ashton Agar, Cameron Bancroft, Hilton Cartwright, Brody Couch, Joel Curtis, Kieran Elliott, Albert Esterhuysen, Jayden Goodwin, Liam Haskett, Corey Rocchiccioli, Corey Wasley, Sam Whiteman

 

In: Aaron Hardie

 

Out: Jhye Richardson

WA squad: Aaron Hardie (c), Ashton Agar, Cameron Bancroft, Hilton Cartwright, Brody Couch, Joel Curtis, Kieran Elliott, Albert Esterhuysen, Jayden Goodwin, Liam Haskett, Corey Rocchiccioli, Corey Wasley, Sam Whiteman

 

In: Aaron Hardie

 

Out: Jhye Richardson

WA are coming off a big win against defending champions South Australia at home so will arrive in Melbourne full of confidence. That win included a century for Cameron Bancroft that was celebrated with incredible gusto ... perhaps coincidentally, it came while Australia's Test team was in action in Cape Town. He's been backed by WA coach Beau Casson to "let his bat do the talking" as the events of 2018 return to the headlines and given the focus on the Aussie red-ball batting line-up, another century here could see him in the mix for a return to that Test team. 

WA welcome back captain Aaron Hardie for the trip to Melbourne. Hardie injured his shoulder in the round two draw with NSW and was subbed out. While that's a boost to an already strong batting line-up, fast bowler Jhye Richardson has been managed out and will miss the match against the Vics at a venue where he made his Test return last summer after a four-year absence. Richardson's absence opens the door for fellow quicks Brody Couch and Albert Esterhuysen to return to the side, having retained their places in the 13-man squad, while Ashton Agar is set to continue his spin partnership with Corey Rocchiccioli. 

WA's Test players are not available for this round of Shield cricket but Cameron Green, Josh Inglis and Cooper Connolly – who all played in Cape Town – will be available to play the next match.

NSW v South Australia, Cricket Central, November 11-14 (10:30am AEDT)

NSW squad: Nic Maddinson (c), Sean Abbott, Oliver Davies, Ben Dwarshuis, Liam Hatcher, Ryan Hicks, Sam Konstas, Nathan Lyon, Kurtis Patterson, Will Salzmann, Jake Scott, Lachlan Shaw 

 

In: Nathan Lyon

 

Out: Bryce Jackson

NSW squad: Nic Maddinson (c), Sean Abbott, Oliver Davies, Ben Dwarshuis, Liam Hatcher, Ryan Hicks, Sam Konstas, Nathan Lyon, Kurtis Patterson, Will Salzmann, Jake Scott, Lachlan Shaw 

 

In: Nathan Lyon

 

Out: Bryce Jackson

The Brad Haddin era at NSW is still looking for its first win, and it will not get any easier when they host back-to-back Shield winners South Australia this week. NSW's batters will be keen to rebound from a disappointing outing in Tasmania last round, but Sam Konstas, Ollie Davies and Kurtis Patterson have all put centuries on the board this summer. Perhaps more pressing for Haddin and his coaching staff is that the bowling unit is yet to take 20 wickets in a Shield match this summer.

To that end, the addition of Nathan Lyon will surely be a benefit. The 38-year-old Lyon is expecting the birth of another child so it remains unclear if he will play a full part in the fixture. But given Lyon was out-bowled by South Africa off-spinner Simon Harmer on the recent Test tour he will no doubt be keen to return to his best ahead of the summer Test series against New Zealand.

Seamer Bryce Jackson drops out of the squad, while allrounder Jack Edwards - who was managed out last round – is now in Perth with the Australia ODI squad for their series against England, while Josh Philippe continues to be left on the outer, omitted for a second straight round. 

SA squad: Wes Agar, Brendan Doggett, Matthew Gilkes, Mackenzie Harvey, Jake Lehmann, Ben Manenti, Nathan McAndrew, Nathan McSweeney, Lloyd Pope, Jason Sangha, Liam Scott, Jake Weatherald

 

Ins: Jordan Buckingham, Henry Hunt, Noah McFadyen, Henry Thornton

 

Outs: Brendan Doggett (managed), Mackenzie Harvey, Nathan McAndrew (injured), Liam Scott (Australia duty)

SA squad: Wes Agar, Brendan Doggett, Matthew Gilkes, Mackenzie Harvey, Jake Lehmann, Ben Manenti, Nathan McAndrew, Nathan McSweeney, Lloyd Pope, Jason Sangha, Liam Scott, Jake Weatherald

 

Ins: Jordan Buckingham, Henry Hunt, Noah McFadyen, Henry Thornton

 

Outs: Brendan Doggett (managed), Mackenzie Harvey, Nathan McAndrew (injured), Liam Scott (Australia duty)

The back-to-back champs are coming off back-to-back losses having gone down to WA (by 105 runs) and Queensland (by six wickets) in their past two matches. They will no doubt be keen to arrest that slide and revert to a performance more like their round one run fest against Victoria where they put up 508 in an innings win. Liam Scott misses after his call-up to Australia's ODI squad, and South Australia will wait until the next match to welcome back Alex Carey, who is currently in Perth with the ODI squad.

Fast bowlers Jordan Buckingham, Henry Thornton and Noah McFadyen all return, with experienced quicks Brendan Doggett and Nathan McAndrew both unavailable. SA's leading run-scorer from last season Henry Hunt returns from concussion to bolster the top order with Mackenzie Harvey to make way.

Queensland v Tasmania, Gabba, November 11-14 (10am local)

Queensland squad: Marnus Labuschagne (c), Hugo Burdon, Jack Clayton, Cameron Gannon, Lachlan Hearne, Hayden Kerr, Angus Lovell, Michael Neser, Jimmy Peirson, Tom Straker, Mitchell Swepson, Hugh Weibgen, Tom Whitney

 

Ins: Marnus Labuschagne, Michael Neser, Tom Whitney

 

Outs: Jem Ryan (side strain), Tom Campbell (omitted)

Queensland squad: Marnus Labuschagne (c), Hugo Burdon, Jack Clayton, Cameron Gannon, Lachlan Hearne, Hayden Kerr, Angus Lovell, Michael Neser, Jimmy Peirson, Tom Straker, Mitchell Swepson, Hugh Weibgen, Tom Whitney

 

Ins: Marnus Labuschagne, Michael Neser, Tom Whitney

 

Outs: Jem Ryan (side strain), Tom Campbell (omitted)

Marnus Labuschagne is back to captain the Queensland Bulls after being axed from Australia's Test squad, and he will be joined by Michael Neser, who was an unused squad member on the South Africa trip. The Bulls are playing a second straight match at the Gabba and will be hoping for clear weather after rain ruined their chances of securing victory against Victoria in the previous round. 

Last round's first-innings centurion Jack Clayton will be looking to back that up after his ton against the Vics pushed him into the conversation for a Test match berth, while Labuschagne has the opportunity to reset his Test ambitions with a spell in the Shield. The 32-year-old who still boasts a career Test average of more than 42 is a huge boost to the young Bulls brigade, and he will be coming in hot with a point to prove after stating his desire to win his Test spot back, even after selectors put a pen through his name for the New Zealand series.

Neser's return is timely for Queensland who will be without promising young quick Jem Ryan after he suffered a side strain in the last match. That will put Ryan out of action for the Bulls until after the Big Bash break (the 22-year-old does not have a Big Bash contract). 

Tasmania squad: Jordan Silk (c), Marcus Bean, Gabe Bell, Jackson Bird, Nikhil Chaudhary, Jake Doran, Caleb Jewell, Matt Kuhnemann, Ben McDermott, Riley Meredith, Tim Ward, Beau Webster, Teague Wyllie

 

Ins: Beau Webster, Matt Kuhnemann, Ben McDermott, Nikhil Chaudhary, Marcus Bean

 

Outs: Mitch Owen (shin soreness), Brad Hope, Nivethan Radhakrishnan, Raf MacMillan (all omitted)

Tasmania squad: Jordan Silk (c), Marcus Bean, Gabe Bell, Jackson Bird, Nikhil Chaudhary, Jake Doran, Caleb Jewell, Matt Kuhnemann, Ben McDermott, Riley Meredith, Tim Ward, Beau Webster, Teague Wyllie

 

Ins: Beau Webster, Matt Kuhnemann, Ben McDermott, Nikhil Chaudhary, Marcus Bean

 

Outs: Mitch Owen (shin soreness), Brad Hope, Nivethan Radhakrishnan, Raf MacMillan (all omitted)

Test squad members Beau Webster and Matt Kuhnemann lead a raft of inclusions for Tasmania for this clash in Brisbane. Webster was dropped for the third Test in South Africa and will be attempting to win his spot back in the squad for the New Zealand Test series that is due to be announced after this round of matches. Kuhnemann was unused in South Africa and will have his eye on the Border-Gavaskar Trophy tour of India that begins in January. 

Ben McDermott has recovered from the back spasms that kept him out of the last round match to resume his spot in the middle order while Nikhil Chaudhary has overcome knee issues to take his spot in the side, while Marcus Bean has been added as a fast-bowling option. Mitch Owen's shin soreness eased the selection squeeze as he misses the trip north, while Brad Hope - who hit 99 last round - is among the omissions along with spinners Nivethan Radhakrishnan and Raf MacMillan.

The ageless Jackson Bird showed ex-state NSW what they were missing with a masterful display last round that saw him collect seven wickets, and you wouldn't put it past the 39-year-old to repeat the dose on his old stomping ground at Cricket Central this week. Teague Wyllie continues to find his feet in the Tasmania middle order.
