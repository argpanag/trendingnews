The latest ANZ Business Outlook survey finds firms 'relatively optimistic' in October, although future cost pressures are still top of mind

TheTools · 29.10.2026 21:44 · source · permalink

From interest.co.nz

Businesses are feeling “relatively optimistic” according to the ANZ Business Outlook (ANZBO) survey for October, despite the vast majority of them remaining concerned about future cost pressures. Every month, ANZ surveys 1,500 New Zealand businesses to analyse where the economy is heading over the next year. Business confidence edged up just 1 point to +53 in October, despite businesses being “relatively optimistic” last month, according to ANZ chief economist Sharon Zollner.
