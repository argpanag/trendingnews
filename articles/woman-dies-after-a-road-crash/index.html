Woman dies after A-road crash

TheTools · 14.10.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

A woman has died at the scene of a crash near Totnes.

Emergency services were called just before 8pm on Tuesday, October 13, following reports of a three-vehicle crash on the A381 in Halwell.

The crash involved a black Perodua Myvi compact hatchback, a blue Skoda Kamiq and a White Hyundai 130.

A 33-year-old local woman, the driver of the Perodua, died at the scene. The driver of the Skoda is in a critical condition in hospital, police confirmed. Their families have been informed.

Officers from the Roads Policing Team closed the road for several hours while investigations were carried out at the scene, and have thanked the public for their patience during that time.

The driver of the White Hyundai i30, a local man in his 40s, was arrested for failing to provide a preliminary drugs test and is currently being treated in hospital for minor injuries.

Devon and Cornwall Police are now calling for witnesses or anyone with relevant information and dashcam footage which may assist their investigation to come forward.

They ask those who can help to contact them via their website or by calling 101 quoting reference number 50260275198.
