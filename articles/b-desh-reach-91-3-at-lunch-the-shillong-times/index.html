1ST TEST | Day 1

Johannesburg, Nov 15: Kagiso Rabada took three quick wickets, but Bangladesh fought back to reach 91-3 (26 overs) at lunch on day one of the first test against South Africa on Sunday.
Tanzid Hasan scored 51 not out off 82 balls, while Towhid Hridoy made 23 not out off 43 balls, as the duo added 69 for the fourth wicket to help the touring side recover after a poor start.
Rabada picked 3-16 in a fiery six-over spell that left Bangladesh struggling at 22-3 in 8.5 overs after Bangladesh had won the toss and opted to bat.
Debutant Mahfijul Islam was caught first ball, edging a wide delivery to third slip as Marco Jansen made no mistake.Rabada made it two wickets in eight balls as Mominul was caught behind for a seven-ball duck.
Skipper Najmul Shanto scored 12 runs but was undone by a sharp inswinger from Rabada, leaving the top-order in tatters.The early dismissals brought Hasan and Hridoy together, and they batted out the remainder of the session, first reaching drinks at 37-3.The second hour of play belonged to Bangladesh as the duo added 50 off 74 balls. (AP)
