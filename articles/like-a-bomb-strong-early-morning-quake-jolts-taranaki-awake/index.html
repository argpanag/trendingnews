‘Like a bomb’: Strong early-morning quake jolts Taranaki awake

TheTools · 02.10.2026 15:37 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

A magnitude 5.3 earthquake has been felt by thousands of people across the North Island after it rattled Taranaki early this morning.

The quake struck 25km east of New Plymouth at a depth of 11km at 2.57am, GeoNet said.

More than 7300 people reported feeling the quake. GeoNet classified the shaking as strong.

“Remember, drop, cover and hold is the right action to take in an earthquake and when in bed, cover your head,” the earthquake-monitoring service wrote.

People on Facebook described being jolted awake by loud rumbling and sharp shaking, with some reporting items had fallen or broken.

One person said it was the “worst l ever felt in my 78 years”, while another wrote: “Might have been the longest and strongest I’ve ever experienced.”

“You could hear the massive rumble before the shaking. Was scary,” one person wrote.

“Was like a bomb the floor in my shed jumped upwards then the shaking started... I thought the mountain woke up,” another wrote.

Others reported household items falling or breaking.

One described the shaking as “violent” and said it had “smashed stuff”, while another reported: “things crashed and broke at our place”.

The Fire and Emergency New Zealand central communications shift manager confirmed to the Herald at 3.25am that no calls relating to the earthquake had been received.
