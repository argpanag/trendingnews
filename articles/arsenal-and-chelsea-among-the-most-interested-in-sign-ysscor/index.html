Arsenal and Chelsea among the most interested in sign… | YSscores

TheTools · 27.09.2026 15:06 · source · permalink

Arsenal and Chelsea have entered the race to sign young Spanish defender Jacobo Ramón, currently of Como, as interest in the player and his future grows in the coming period, according to AS.

Jacobo Ramón is being closely monitored by two English clubs, who are watching his progress with Como closely, as the young defender continues to build his experience in Serie A. There are expectations that his future will see strong movements during the upcoming summer transfer window.

Despite English interest, Jacobo Ramón is not currently rushing to decide his future, as his main goal is to continue developing and gaining as much experience as possible with Como during the current season.

This season is seen as a pivotal stage in Jacobo Ramón's career, as his performances and participation with Como will be key factors in determining his next step, whether to continue his experience in Italy or move to one of the big clubs that are monitoring his situation.
