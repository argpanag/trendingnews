How to Redeem Grand Blue Codes

Ruling the Grand Blue is not an easy task and requires a lot of effort and materials. So, here is how you can redeem the codes for Grand Blue to get stronger much quicker in the game:

  • Launch Grand Blue on Roblox.
  • Click on the Settings option in the top-left part of the screen.
  • Select the Codes option from the top bar.
  • Type a working code in the ‘Enter code…’ box.
  • Click on Redeem to claim the rewards.
