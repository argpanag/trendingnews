Mateo Silvetti Named to MLS’ 2026 22 Under 22 List | Inter Miami CF

TheTools · 21.10.2026 19:17 · source · permalink

Inter Miami CF attacker Mateo Silvetti has been named to Major League Soccer’s (MLS) 2026 22 Under 22 list, a ranking of the top 22 players in MLS under the age of 22 – as voted by on by an expert panel consisting of MLS coaches, general managers, sporting directors, MLS on Apple TV talent, and select media. The versatile Argentine winger ranks 14th in this year’s class. 

This year Silvietti featured in 17 matches across all competitions, 14 of them this 2026 MLS regular season, before being ruled out with a season-ending injury at the start of September. He contributed three goals and four assists in regular season action along the way. 

In 2025, upon joining the Club in August, Silvetti went on to play a crucial role as Inter Miami clinched the title in the 2025 Audi MLS Cup presented by Audi. He was in scintillating form throughout the Audi 2025 MLS Cup Playoffs, tallying two goals and three assists in six appearances, including valuable goals in the Eastern Conference Semifinals against FC Cincinnati and the Eastern Conference Final against New York CIty FC. 

Silvetti, 20, joined Inter Miami last year from Newell’s Old Boys, where he graduated through the youth ranks and had spent the entirety of his professional career thus far after making his debut in 2024. The Rosario, Argentina native racked up a total 37 appearances across all competitions for Newell’s, contributing six goals and two assists.

On the international stage, Silvetti has represented Argentina’s U-20 national team, making nine appearances and registering four goals and an assist since his debut in 2024.
