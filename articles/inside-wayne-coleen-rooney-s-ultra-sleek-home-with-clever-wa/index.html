Inside Wayne & Coleen Rooney’s ultra sleek home - with clever wardrobe hack and lots of entertaining space

TheTools · 17.11.2026 03:34 · source · permalink

Wayne Rooney is set to spend the next few weeks sleeping and living in the Australian jungle as he takes part in I'm a Celebrity...Get Me Out of Here! And while he'll be living off rice and beans, sleeping in a hammock and showering under a creek, what is his actual house like?

Well, in the last year we've managed to see inside a lot of Wayne and wife Coleen Rooney's home thanks to their Disney+ series The Real Rooneys. During the first episode of the docu-series Coleen invites the cameras into their home, and as a drone shot goes over their 50 acre property Coleen can be heard saying in voiceover says: "This house is everything that I dreamed of as a little girl. When you're a child and you draw a house everything's symmetrical. It's a massive house but there's a lot of rooms within the house so I do lose my kids."

The couple's house is a £20million property in Cheshire and has six bedrooms, a swimming pool, wine cellar, trophy room and of course a football pitch.

Disney+

The home has an ultra sleek modern look to it, with large windows letting in lots of light and traditional ornate panelling throughout giving the house more character. Panelling is an easy and affordable way to give a room more texture and interest without having to spend a lot of money.

Lighting is a key part of the Rooneys' home, with each room having statement lampshades and chandeliers. One of the couple's sitting room features four glass chandeliers and using a statement light fixture such as a chandelier is a great way to bring the eye up and make a focal point in the room.

The house tour during The Real Rooneys also showcases Coleen's love of all things neutrals. Creating a refined appearance, the majority of the soft furnishings appear to be in shades of white, taupe and beige and she uses different textured cushions and ornaments to break up the colour palette. It gives a calming effect although we would not be able to trust clumsy selves around that much white!

Their home also appears to be the ultimate place to host a party or lots of friends and family over. There's the large white kitchen, complete with silver accessories that leads into an open plan dining space and a living room too. This appears to be separate from another more formal seating area in a different part of the house.

Coleen also has the dressing room of dreams, telling the cameras during The Real Rooneys, she really wanted a big room while they were designing the house. The end result is a large dressing room with glass cabinets for her handbags and drawers that hold her sunglasses.

While you may not have space a for a designer bag section in your home, one element can you incorporate into a wardrobe is Coleen's clever hack of doubling up the hanging space in one singular wardrobe, with one lower rail for trousers and skirts and a higher rail for tops, meaning you get more in your wardrobe.

Given what a lovely home he has usually, we wonder just how well with sleeping in camp for three weeks!
