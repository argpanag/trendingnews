Michael Douglas Confirms Kathleen Turner Romance After 40 Years of Denial

TheTools · 03.10.2026 00:20 · source · permalink

Michael Douglas just made a bold confession about his relationship with former co-star Kathleen Turner after 40 years.

In the ’80s, Douglas and Turner starred together in three films: Romancing the Stone (1984), its sequel The Jewel of the Nile (1985), and the unrelated black comedy, The War of the Roses (1989). (Danny DeVito also starred in all three movies.) Additionally, the pair appeared on Seasons 2 and 3 of The Kominsky Method in 2021.
