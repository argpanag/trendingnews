YouTooz to open pre-orders for plush versions of Valorant agents Cypher and Omen on September 18

TheTools · 15.09.2026 10:51 · source · permalink

Thanks to YouTooz, fans of the Valorant shooter will be able to get not only plush agents Cypher and Omen, but also tactical bear and rabbit figures that can be placed on a monitor.

On September 18, 2026, at 10:00 PM Moscow time, YouTooz is set to open pre-orders for new collectible items dedicated to the Valorant shooter. Plush versions of Cypher and Omen will be available to buyers, as well as a set with tactical bear and rabbit figures designed for monitors.

Each of these items will cost $29.99 (2520 rubles). The 23-centimeter Cypher looks forward with bright blue eyes from under a dark mask on his face.

He wears a gray hat with a brim, a short antenna, brown trim, and bands around the crown. The high collar of a pale cloak covers the lower part of his face, and a long hem flares out below the waist. The sleeves and chest are adorned with brown and black elements. He also wears gray trousers and blue gloves.

The 23-centimeter plush version of Omen is notable for three bright blue dots on his dark face. His head is covered by a purple hood with a long train descending down his back. A blue triangle on his chest matches the light shining through his bandaged arms, and he is dressed in purple pants, black gloves, and matching shoes.

It is worth noting that these toys are tightly stuffed with 100% polypropylene cotton and made of soft "minky" material. The tactical bear and rabbit figures, which will watch players from atop the monitor, are 6 cm tall.

The bear wears stylish black glasses over its pale, smiling muzzle, and its brown body is clad in a black bulletproof vest. The rabbit sitting next to it, with a large red bow, sports a white mohawk on its gray head, sharp front teeth protruding from its mouth, and long ears hanging down on either side.

The plush toys will be shipped in February 2027, and the figures between March 5 and April 5, 2027.
