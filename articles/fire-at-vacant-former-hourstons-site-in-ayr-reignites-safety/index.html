Fire at vacant former Hourstons site in Ayr reignites safety concerns

TheTools · 10.10.2026 00:35 · source · permalink

A FIRE has reignited concerns about site safety behind the former Hourstons department store in Ayr.

The blaze broke out on the upper floor of the vacant building just after 6pm on Thursday, October 8.

Emergency services raced to the scene and were seen putting out the flames, with nearby roads closed off.

The Scottish Fire and Rescue Service confirmed four appliances and a high reach vehicle were sent to the scene.

The site is currently marked for development by South Ayrshire Council following a failed bid to create a Wellness Village due to funding.

Ayr MSP Siobhian Brown has taken to social media to raise concerns about security at the prominent town centre site.

She said: "There will be a lot of anger regarding this. Many have been saying for months this would happen.

"When there were security issues a few months back and the broken windows were repaired by South Ayrshire Council - we were given assurance that the building would be secured."

The adjacent Arran Mall has now been completely cleared, ready for redevelopment, with the Hourstons building still standing.

South Ayrshire Council has confirmed that a security company will review the site, and that the building is set to be demolished once a tender has been issued.

A spokesperson said: " Following the fire, a security company is set to review the site. They will make recommendations on how security can be improved.

"As part of the redevelopment of the site, a tender for the demolition of the former Hourstons building will be issued shortly.

"Building standards will be carrying out an assessment of the building on October 9."

A Scottish Fire and Rescue Service spokesperson said: “We were alerted at 6.10pm on Thursday, October 8, to reports of a fire affecting a building on High Street in Ayr.

“Operations control mobilised four fire appliances and a high reach appliance to the area, and the fire was extinguished.

“There were no reported casualties, and crews left the scene at 8.39pm after ensuring the area was safe.”
