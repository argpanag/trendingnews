Crystal Palace vs Ipswich: Team News and Predicted Lineups

TheTools · 11.09.2026 19:30 · source · permalink

Last Word on Football brings you the latest team news and predicted lineups ahead of the Crystal Palace vs Ipswich Premier League clash at Selhurst Park.

The sides have identical records after three league games, with both securing one win, while they also share the joint-worst defence in the league after conceding eight goals apiece.

The Eagles have enjoyed recent success over the Tractor Boys and have won the last three meetings between the sides, including doing the double over their opponents in the 2024/25 campaign.

Crystal Palace vs Ipswich Predicted Lineups and Team News

Date: 12th September 2026
Venue: Selhurst Park
Kick-off: 15:00 GMT
Last meeting: Crystal Palace 1-0 Ipswich (8th March 2025, Premier League)
Referee: Chris Kavanagh

Crystal Palace Team News 

Palace manager Pierre Sage has outlined that he doesn’t want to make too many changes to his starting eleven as he looks to build on Saturday’s win over Fulham. The Eagles will definitely be without Ismaila Sarr and Jean-Philippe Mateta, with both struggling with injury, but otherwise Palace will most likely line up similarly to their victory over Fulham.

“Sarr is not with the team at the moment, I thought he could train with us on Monday, but that was not the case, we expect him to be back next Monday, and we want him on the pitch,” stated Sage in his pre-match presser.

Midfielder Cheick Doucoure hasn’t featured for Palace since January 2025 but could play his first minutes for 20 months on the weekend.

“Today went well, but tomorrow will be better, we will see tomorrow because of the week, but all will be good for him,” outlined Sage.

Norwegian striker Jorgen Strand Larsen scored two and set up another in Palace’s comfortable 3-0 win over Middlesbrough in the Carabao Cup on Tuesday night and could be preferred over Eddie Nketiah as the focal point of Palace’s attack.

The coach faces a dilemma in defence after impressive performances from recruits Takehiro Tomiyasu and Honest Ahanor against Middlesbrough, and it will be interesting to see whether he opts for them over the established Chris Richards and Jaydee Canvot.

Ipswich Team News 

Ipswich manager Gary O’Neil outlined his excitement about the challenge ahead and expects a tricky game at Selhurst Park.

“It’s an exciting game for us, we have to respect Crystal Palace and the quality they have, but we will go their full of optimism and intensity,” stated O’Neil in his pre-match presser.

The Tractor Boys have experienced a successful week in terms of absentees, with both Jaden Philogene and Azor Matusiwa returning to training after spells on the sideline.

“We have everyone available apart from Jack Taylor, Azor (Matusiwa) and Jaden (Philogene) are back fully training, to go to Crystal Palace with basically a fully fit squad is a testament to the work that’s been done by the players,” outlined O’Neil.

Ipswich’s new signing Zian Flemming started on the bench against Liverpool, but O’Neil could be tempted to start the striker over Emersonn, who scored on the opening day against Sunderland.

Julio Enciso has impressed for the Tractor Boys and is expected to start alongside Daizen Maeda and the dangerous Abdul Fatawu.

The Last Word: Match Prediction

Palace and Ipswich share the misfortune of having the worst defensive record after three games so goals should be guaranteed at Selhurst Park on Saturday.

Palace were effective in beating Fulham last week and showed their ability to keep a clean sheet against Middlesbrough in midweek, while Ipswich are coming off the back of defeats against Manchester United and Liverpool.

Palace’s attacking threat should be enough to see off the Tractor Boys, and an in-form Jorgen Strand Larsen could make the difference for the South London side.

Prediction: Crystal Palace 3-1 Ipswich.
