Injury Notes: Ball, Lillard, M. Wagner, KAT, Kyrie, More

TheTools · 28.11.2026 01:17 · source · permalink

The Timberwolves can clinch a spot in the NBA Cup’s knockout round with a win at home over Memphis on Friday, but they’ll be missing both members of their usual starting backcourt in that matchup. The team announced today (via Twitter) that LaMelo Ball will sit out against the Grizzlies as a result of the left ankle sprain he suffered on Wednesday, joining star guard Anthony Edwards on Minnesota’s inactive list.

Ball was originally listed as questionable for Friday’s game, which is a signal that the ankle injury likely won’t cost him much – if any – additional time beyond today. His next chance to play will be on Sunday in Sacramento.

Minnesota won’t be the only Northwest team vying for an NBA Cup quarterfinal spot without both of its starting guards available. Star point guard Damian Lillard will be out on Friday due to right calf tightness as the Trail Blazers try to secure their place in the knockout round with a victory over Golden State. Ja Morant (hamstring strain) also remains sidelined, though it sounds like he’s getting close to returning.

Here are more injury updates from across the NBA:

  • After playing in each of the Nets’ first 17 games of the regular season, big man Moritz Wagner will be out on Friday due to left knee injury management, writes Brian Lewis of The New York Post. Wagner exited Wednesday’s game in the first quarter after experiencing soreness in that knee. “I was about to sub him in, and I saw him limping, and I was like, ‘I got to save him from himself,'” head coach Jordi Fernandez said. “He was there to do whatever it took for his team and his teammates, and at the end I decided to not bring him back in.”
  • Two star players are late additions to their teams’ respective injury reports for Friday’s games, with the Knicks now calling Karl-Anthony Towns questionable to play vs. Miami due to a right abdominal contusion (Twitter link via James L. Edwards III of The Athletic), while the Mavericks say Kyrie Irving is questionable to suit up in Phoenix because of left knee injury management (Twitter link via Marc Stein).
  • For the second time this week, the Pacers assigned injured guards Tyrese Haliburton (hamstring strain) and Johnny Furphy (ACL strain) to the G League to practice with the Noblesville Boom, according to Tony East of Circle City Spin (Twitter links), who says the duo has since been recalled to the NBA. Haliburton’s return should happen soon, whereas Furphy is likely still a ways off from making his season debut.
  • After recovering from an avulsion fracture in his right foot, Raptors two-way player Nate Bittle is poised to make his season debut in the G League this weekend. Blake Murphy of Sportsnet.ca has the details on the rookie big man, who was a Summer League standout.
