UFC 331 Live Results, Scorecards, Highlights And Reactions

TheTools · 20.09.2026 05:56 · source · permalink

LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA - SEPTEMBER 18: (L-R) Opponents Joshua Van of Myanmar and Alexandre Pantoja of Brazil face off during the UFC 331 ceremonial weigh-in at Crypto.com Arena on September 18, 2026 in Los Angeles, California. (Photo by Chris Unger/Zuffa LLC)

Joshua Van eliminated all doubt on Saturday in the UFC 331 main event in Los Angeles. Van won a hard-fought, but clear unanimous decision over Alexandre Pantoja. Van's takedown defense, speed and striking proved to be too much for the rugged Brazilian all-time great.

Van won 49-46 on two card and 50-45 on the third. The last card was a little too wind for my tastes, but the right man got the decision.

Van ended the fight in style, not resting on his laurels despite being up on all three cards.

Arman Tsarukyan def. Mauricio Ruffy via First-Round KO

LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA - SEPTEMBER 19: Arman Tsarukyan of Georgia knocks out Mauricio Ruffy of Brazil in a lightweight fight during the UFC 331 event at Crypto.com Arena on September 19, 2026 in Los Angeles, California. (Photo by Chris Unger/Zuffa LLC)

Tsarukyan just proved anyone who doubted him wrong. Tsarukyan locked up his No. 1 contender status by destroying Ruffy with two nasty elbows.

Patricio Pitbull def. DooHo Choi via First-Round TKO

LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA - SEPTEMBER 19: Patricio Pitbull of Brazil punches Dooho Choi of South Korea in a featherweight fight during the UFC 331 event at Crypto.com Arena on September 19, 2026 in Los Angeles, California. (Photo by Chris Unger/Zuffa LLC)

Pitbull has strung together back-to-back wins in the UFC and this one was his best. Pitbull smashed UFC Hall-of-Famer DooHo Choi with a crushing right hand and ground and pound. Here is the finish.

Sean Sharaf def. Gable Steveson via First-Round KO

LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA - SEPTEMBER 19: Sean Sharaf knocks out Gable Steveson in a heavyweight fight during the UFC 331 event at Crypto.com Arena on September 19, 2026 in Los Angeles, California. (Photo by Cooper Neill/Zuffa LLC)

That was one of the most shocking upset wins you'll ever see. Highly touted heavyweight prospect, Gable Steveson just got eradicated by Sean Sharaf. The latter came into the fight as a major underdog, but he flipped the MMA world on its ear with this punch.

In a classless move, Steveson left the Octagon without staying for the announcement of the official decision. He walked out under his own power with a smirk on his face. That's not cool, professional or respectful. Take your L like a man.

Alonzo Menifield def. Iwo Baraniewski via Split Decision (29-28x2, 28-29)

LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA - SEPTEMBER 19: A detail view of the injured thumb of Alonzo Menifield after his light heavyweight fight against Iwo Baraniewski during the UFC 331 event at Crypto.com Arena on September 19, 2026 in Los Angeles, California. (Photo by Chris Unger/Zuffa LLC)

The split decisions keep coming. Menifield earned the victory with a hard-fought and gutsy performance. Menifield's thumb was mangled in the first round, but he pushed through to outlast the previously undefeated prospect.

Marlon Vera def. Charles Jourdain via Third-Round TKO

LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA - SEPTEMBER 19: Marlon Vera of Ecuador punches Charles Jourdain of Canada in a bantamweight fight during the UFC 331 event at Crypto.com Arena on September 19, 2026 in Los Angeles, California. (Photo by Chris Unger/Zuffa LLC)

Vera delivered a big-time performance with his back against the wall. He snapped a four-fight losing streak with a surgical destruction of Jourdain that culminated with this left hook that had the latter waving off the rest of the contest as he went to the canvas.

Vera is back on track. We'll see who he gets next at bantamweight.

Robelis Despaigne def. Tai Tuivasa vai Split Decision (29-28x2, 28-29)

LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA - SEPTEMBER 19: (L-R) Robelis Despaigne of Cuba punches Tai Tuivasa of Australia in a heavyweight fight during the UFC 331 event at Crypto.com Arena on September 19, 2026 in Los Angeles, California. (Photo by Chris Unger/Zuffa LLC)

This fight was all action, but it was a messy brawl that did not show what one would call top-notch heavyweight MMA. In the end, Despaigne outlasted Tuivasa with better striking and some uncharacteristic, but timely takedowns.

Tuivasa has now lost an unbelievable eight fights in row.

Michael Aswell Jr. def. Joo Sang Yoo via Split Decision (29-28x2, 28-29)

LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA - SEPTEMBER 19: JooSang Yoo (L) of South Korea is punched by Michael Aswell Jr. of the United States in their featherweight bout during UFC 331: Van v Pantoja 2 at Crypto.com Arena on September 19, 2026 in Los Angeles, California. (Photo by Luke Hales/Getty Images)

Michael Aswell Jr's pressure and approach led to a close but just unanimous-decision victory over Joo Sang Yoo. It was a strong performance that keeps the 25-year-old in the mix to remain a fighter battling toward a ranking.

Ryan Gandra def. Ozzy Diaz via First-Round TKO

LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA - SEPTEMBER 19: (R-L) Ryan Gandra of Brazil punches Ozzy Diaz in a middleweight fight during the UFC 331 event at Crypto.com Arena on September 19, 2026 in Los Angeles, California. (Photo by Chris Unger/Zuffa LLC)

Ryan Gandra continues to dominate in the UFC. He just secured his latest first-round finish as he smashed Ozzy Diaz in the first round.

He earned a contract on Dana White Contender Series in August 2025 with a first-round KO and has followed that up with three more. Gandra called out Israel Adesanya during his post-fight interview. That might be a bit premature, but he is a dangerous up-and-comer.

Edmen Shabahyzan def. Bruno Ferreira via Unanimous Decision (30-27, 29-28x2)

LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA - SEPTEMBER 19: (R-L) Edmen Shahbazyan punches Brunno Ferreira of Brazil in a middleweight fight during the UFC 331 event at Crypto.com Arena on September 19, 2026 in Los Angeles, California. (Photo by Chris Unger/Zuffa LLC)

The keys to this one were leg kicks and length. Edmen Shahbazyan attacked Bruno Ferreira's legs early and often and he used his length to pick the Brazilian apart en route to an easy unanimous-decision win.

Casey O'Neill Just Won With One of the Best Submissions Ever

LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA - SEPTEMBER 19: (L-R) Casey O'Neill of Scotland secures and arm bar submission against Eduarda Moura of Brazil in a flyweight fight during the UFC 331 event at Crypto.com Arena on September 19, 2026 in Los Angeles, California. (Photo by Chris Unger/Zuffa LLC)

Casey O'Neill looks like a real contender in the UFC women's flyweight division. she battled through a precarious early situation against Eduarda Moura to secure thsi fantastic transition into an armbar submission.

Joanderson Brito Makes Giga Chikadze Pay For Major Error

LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA - SEPTEMBER 19: Joanderson Brito of Brazil punches Giga Chikadze of Georgia in a featherweight fight during the UFC 331 event at Crypto.com Arena on September 19, 2026 in Los Angeles, California. (Photo by Chris Unger/Zuffa LLC)

Giga Chikadze was doing well early in the fight. His kickboxing skills seemed to be giving Joanderson Brito some issues. Unfortunately for Chikadze, he slipped on a head kick attempt and went to the canvas. Brito took advantage and battered Chikadze with hammer fists to force the stoppage.

Chikadze has lost four in a row and he could be in danger of losing his roster spot.
