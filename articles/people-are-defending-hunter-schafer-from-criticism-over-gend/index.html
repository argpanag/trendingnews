You know Hunter Schafer. She shot to fame as Jules on Euphoria and has since starred in movies such as Kinds of Kindness, Cuckoo, and The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.

Taylor Hill / Getty Images

She's also quite the fashion icon, too. I mean, who could forget this look?!

Doug Peters - PA Images / Getty Images

Though Hunter is proudly trans, she has talked in the past about not wanting her gender identity to overshadow her work as an actor. Speaking with GQ in 2024, Hunter said that she had turned down multiple trans roles and had tried not to say the word "trans" in interviews, to avoid being known primarily as a "Transsexual Actress," rather than just an actress.

Julian Hamilton / Getty Images

Related: "Woah, Collin Gosselin's New Look Is Sparking A Ton Of Reactions"

"It took a while to learn that and it also took a while to learn that I don't want to be [reduced to] that, and I find it ultimately demeaning to me and what I want to do. Especially after high school, I was sick of talking about it. I worked so hard to get to where I am, past these really hard points in my transition, and now I just want to be a girl and finally move on," she said, adding that "it's a privilege" to be in the position to shift focus beyond her gender identity.

Chad Salvador / Getty Images

"It's been very intentional. I've gotten offered tons of trans roles, and I just don't want to do it. I don't want to talk about it," she went on. "I know for a fact that I'm one of the most famous trans people in media right now, and I do feel a sense of responsibility, and maybe a little bit of guilt, for not being more of a spokesperson. But ultimately, I really do believe that not making it the centerpiece to what I'm doing will allow me to get further. And I think getting further and doing awesome shit, in the interest of 'the movement,' will be way more helpful than talking about it all the time."

Dia Dipasupil / Getty Images

Well, fast-forward more than two years, and Hunter has opened up about a gender-affirming procedure she privately underwent last year.

Monica Schipper / Getty Images

In a new interview with Elle magazine, the 27-year-old revealed that she had bottom surgery in December 2025. This refers to the gender-affirming operation that alters a person's genitals to align with their gender identity.

Valentina S. Frugiuele / Getty Images

Related: "23 Celebs Who Fumbled Their Careers In The Dumbest Ways Imaginable"

Every trans person is different, but Hunter explained to Elle that she decided to undergo the major medical procedure after "hitting a wall again and again where this theoretical queer utopia didn't match up with the world she encountered every day."

Michael Buckner / Getty Images

"For a long time I've embraced the gender-expansive part of myself, and I really wanted to try to make that previous body work, because I thought there was something really cool and badass about it," Hunter explained, before giving some insight into the intense recovery period after the operation. "I think my limit was 150 steps per day, which is nothing."

Pascal Le Segretain / Getty Images

Hunter said that the ability to evolve is the "magical thing about queerness," and that, "on an ideological level, it's about possibility and expansion and growth, and being on that journey and finding yourself." While she's had an expansive view of her gender identity for many years, she said she's landed somewhere "flatter" now. "I'm a woman. I'm a girl," she said.

Neilson Barnard / Getty Images

As we previously acknowledged, every trans person's experience is different, and Hunter chose to medically transition for the reasons outlined above. Despite this, some have judged Hunter's decision, prompting Kim Petras to speak out in her defense.

Gilbert Flores / Getty Images

Related: "17 Mind-Blowing Secrets About Old Hollywood's Biggest Stars"

The German pop singer, who became famous at 16 as one of the world's youngest people to undergo gender-affirmation surgery, took to X on Oct. 19 to call out the online criticism in response to Hunter's Elle interview.

Chad Salvador / Getty Images

"Reading some really weird ass shit on here about beautiful Hunter Schaffer talking about getting vaginoplasty," Kim wrote. "And just wanna say it's so brave of her to speak on this and it's exposing so many of the gross ways in which trans women get fetishized ."

Emma McIntyre/Oscars / Getty Images

She continued: "The idea of someone aligning their body with how they feel on the inside and people saying YOU ARE WORTH LESS TO US NOW is what I wrote a song called brutalist about … 🩷."
