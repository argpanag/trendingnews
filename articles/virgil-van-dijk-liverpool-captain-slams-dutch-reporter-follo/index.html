Virgil van Dijk: Liverpool captain slams Dutch reporter following claims he forced Ronald Koeman to change World Cup tactics

TheTools · 24.09.2026 22:07 · source · permalink

Virgil van Dijk claimed media in the Netherlands had said that he had 'ruined Dutch football' after allegedly telling Ronald Koeman to change formation during the World Cup; Koeman switched to a back five against Morocco, but would be eliminated on penalties

Football Journalist

Thursday 24 September 2026 08:39, UK

Virgil van Dijk hit back at suggestions that he forced former Netherlands boss Ronaldo Koeman into changing his tactics during the World Cup.

The Liverpool defender captained his country during the tournament, where they were knocked out by Morocco on penalties in the round of 32.

The 35-year-old has retained his captaincy under new boss Xavi, and was involved in a frosty confrontation with a journalist during a spiky press conference ahead of Netherlands' Nations League clash with Germany on Thursday.

"One of your colleagues also said, of course, that it was my idea to switch to playing with a back five. That is utter nonsense," Van Dijk told one reporter.

"The fact is we simply failed at the World Cup. Those are the hard facts. We didn't play a good World Cup. It's not about me. You perform together. You're a team. Your claim that I destroyed Dutch football after the World Cup - that sort of thing is completely baseless".

Koeman utilised a back four throughout the group stages of the World Cup, but switched formation for the game against Morocco which the Netherlands led thanks to Cody Gakpo.

However, a stoppage-time equaliser from Issa Diop sent the game to extra-time and eventually penalties which saw Koeman's side crash out.

"The plan is devised by the staff. Nonsense is being spread so that I look even worse. That happens far too often," Van Dijk said.

"I was told that it was my fault we played with five, that Ronald Koeman and I have destroyed Dutch football.

"The plan worked for 89 minutes, even though it was a bad match. We almost broke through. The idea that I destroyed Dutch football makes absolutely no sense. I didn't decide that."

"I'm super open," Van Dijk continued when asked why he was giving short answers during the press conference.

"Sometimes things need to be said, right? Otherwise you're just listening to all the whining. With most of the media, I have zero issues. Never have. When nonsense gets put out into the world, how else am I supposed to deal with that?"

With Xavi now at the helm, Van Dijk says he is motivated to continue with the Netherlands as they begin their Nations League campaign before next year's Euro 2028 qualifiers.

"I needed a holiday with my family and then an important meeting with the national coach. After 10 seconds it was already clear that I would continue," he added.

"What did he say? That I am Virgil van Dijk. It is about trust, appreciation, how important you are for the team. Physically there is - and was - no doubt but you also want to feel appreciation and pleasure."
