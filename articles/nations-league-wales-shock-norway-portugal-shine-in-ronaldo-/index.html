Nations League: Wales shock Norway, Portugal shine in Ronaldo absence

TheTools · 01.10.2026 21:17 · source · permalink

HOUSE OF THE DRAGON: Wales' Neco Williams celebrates scoring a corker of a free-kick in Cardiff

Israel and Kosovo played out a dull scoreless draw in Debrecen in the night's other Group B3 contest. The result means Kosovo go second in the group ahead of Ireland on goal difference with Israel still rooted to the bottom on one point.

Elsewhere, in Group A2, Netherlands secured a point following a late Tijjani Reijnders strike after the Dutch found themselves two down in the first half. First half goals from Fotis Ioannidis and Giorgos Masouras had Xavi's men stunned. 

Ioannidis took his chance well, put through on goal in the 23rd minute and calmly slotting past Bart Verbruggen with his favoured right. It was Ioannidis who set up the second too but only after a woefully misplaced Dutch pass that set off the goalscorer to set up Masouras.

Virgil van Dijk nodded the Netherlands back into the game before the hour mark before Reijnders smashed home from close after a Greek block on an attempted bicycle kick, sealed a point.

In injury news, Arsenal winger Christos Tzolis also went off with a suspected hamstring injury.

Jurgen Klopp got his first win as German manager against Serbia. Goals from Tom Bischof and Florian Wirtzin either half was enough to secure all three points for the former Liverpool manager's charges.

In what was definitely the shock of the night, Craig Bellamy's Wales toppled Norway 2-1 and had to come from behind to do it after an early Oscar Bobb goal gave the Norwegians the perfect start in Cardiff. Goals from Daniel James and Neco Williams either side of half time flipped the game on its head. 

James' found himself in the right place at the right time slotting home from a squared ball in the area. A Torbjorn Heggem sending off a minute after the restart didn't help the Norwegian effort and from the resulting free Neco Williams curled one in for the winner.

From the biggest shock to the biggest thrill. Portugal put four past Denmark in a six-goal thriller in Copenhagen. Mere hours after the official departure of Cristiano Ronaldo from the Portuguese camp, Jorge Jesus' side proved why they don't need the former Real Madrid and Manchester United legend in their ranks.

The visitors scored twice in the first half through Joao Cancelo and Goncalo Ramos, but on each occasion the Danes found an equaliser as Mikkel Damsgaard and Rasmus ​Hojlund netted for the hosts.

But ​Portugal would not be ⁠denied and Vitinha grabbed a 67th-minute goal and Joao Felix a late fourth to give Jorge Jesus’ side a six-point lead in the table after what has ​been a difficult few days.

Finally, Malta and Gibraltar shared the points at the National Stadium in Ta'Qali, the game finishing 1-1.
