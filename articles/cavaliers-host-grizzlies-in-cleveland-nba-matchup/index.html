Cavaliers Host Grizzlies in Cleveland NBA Matchup

TheTools · 19.11.2026 02:57 · source · permalink

The Cleveland Cavaliers will welcome the Memphis Grizzlies to Rocket Arena in Cleveland, Ohio, on Wednesday, November 18, with both teams entering the NBA matchup carrying different early-season records. Cleveland stands at 8-7, while Memphis has compiled a 10-4 record ahead of the 7:00 p.m. ET tipoff.

The game will be broadcast live on NBA League Pass. Cleveland enters the contest looking to use its physical approach and frontcourt size against a Memphis side that has won its previous three games.

According to the Yahoo Sports preview, Memphis has benefited from the NBA's easiest strength of schedule so far this season. Its recent winning run includes victories against the Washington Wizards, Brooklyn Nets and New Orleans Pelicans.

The preview also highlighted Memphis' recent performances as evidence that its early success should be considered in the context of the opposition it has faced. The upcoming matchup against Cleveland represents a different physical challenge.

Ty Jerome's absence is another significant factor for Memphis. The Grizzlies have been operating without the guard, while Cam Spencer has taken on a supporting role in his absence.

Advanced performance metrics, including Estimated Plus-Minus (EPM), have indicated that Jerome has made a substantial contribution this season. The Yahoo Sports analysis compared his statistical impact with that of Nikola Jokic, although it did not treat the comparison as a direct measure of his value to the team.

The Memphis preview described the team as a group of early-season "League Pass" favorites, reflecting its entertaining performances during the opening stretch of the campaign. However, the absence of Jerome remains an important consideration as the Grizzlies prepare to face stronger opposition.

Cleveland, meanwhile, enters the matchup after handling the Chicago Bulls. Although the Bulls and a fully healthy Memphis team are not necessarily at the same competitive level, the Cavaliers' recent performance demonstrated their ability to control games through physical play.

The Yahoo Sports analysis characterized Cleveland's approach as "big boy basketball", emphasizing the team's size, strength and physical presence. Those characteristics could create difficulties for Memphis, particularly in the frontcourt.

Cleveland's size also gives the team an opportunity to disrupt Memphis' offensive rhythm and make it harder for the visitors to establish comfortable half-court possessions.

The matchup therefore brings together Memphis' strong opening record and Cleveland's physical style of play. The Cavaliers will host the Grizzlies at Rocket Arena, with tipoff scheduled for 7:00 p.m. ET on Wednesday, November 18.
