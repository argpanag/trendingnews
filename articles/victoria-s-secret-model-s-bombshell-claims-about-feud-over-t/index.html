Victoria’s Secret model’s bombshell claims about feud over Tom Brady

Joan Smalls has confirmed her public falling out with fellow Victoria’s Secret model Candice Swanepoel was caused by NFL star Tom Brady, whom they both dated.

In her first-ever podcast appearance, Smalls, 38, told The Cutting Room Floor host Recho Omondi that Brady was indeed “the catalyst” for her years-long friendship with the South African model falling apart.

Joan Smalls has confirmed her friendship with Candice Swanepoel ended over Tom Brady. Stacy Revere/Dimitrios Kambouris/Getty/AP Photo/Andy Wong

Smalls and Brady, 49, dated on-and-off for about eight months between 2023 and 2024, then Swanepoel, 38, was romantically linked to the footballer in June 2026.

“If I have a friendship for over 15 years where I’m vulnerable with you, we have a lot of intimate conversations,” Smalls began.

“You’ve seen the relationships, situationships, people I’ve dated, and I’ve confided in you ... and we never had a crossover.

Smalls said she was shocked when her former friend confirmed she was dating Smalls’ ex-flame. YouTube/The Cutting Room Floor

“Then all of a sudden, I stop seeing this person, and three months later, you text me and say, ‘Oh, we have to speak about something’, which is usually not a conversation we normally have,” Smalls continued.

“My instinct spoke to me, and I was like, ‘I know what this is about’. So I called her and I asked her, ‘Is this about him?’ And she said yes.”

Smalls felt betrayed that Swanepoel would date a man she had already been with.

She had shared intimate details about their relationship with Swanepoel, and claimed the other model even helped her craft texts to Brady when they dated.

Smalls said they had a conversation about Brady moving on with Swanepoel, which left her perplexed.

“She came across as justifying her actions, that what I had wasn’t valid, or it was casual, and she started diminishing my experience to make herself feel better about the situation,” she claimed.

“Which is what kind of infuriated me, as my friend, to diminish me or devalue my friendship so much that you didn’t have consideration towards my feeling.

“It was just this attitude of entitlement and lack of care towards someone who’s your friend, and something that I would never do to you.”

Smalls also claimed she caught Swanepoel has “done this before” and said she caught the other model in multiple lies about the situation.

Smalls walked the runway for the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show 2026. Dimitrios Kambouris/Getty

Swanepoel has reportedly been dating Brady since the start of the year. Getty

In the end, she decided to cut off their friendship and removed all photos with Swanepoel from her Instagram in April.

Smalls also unfollowed the other model on social media, which sparked rumours about a falling out between them.

When fans asked if they were still friends, Smalls wrote on Instagram that she is “not friends with backstabbers”.

Elsewhere in the podcast, she accused Swanepoel of manipulation but said she mourned their friendship, which she had hoped would last a lifetime.

“I hope she gets what she wants, and that the juice is worth the squeeze,” she said.

Prior to his relationship with Swanepoel, Brady was married to yet another Victoria’s Secret model.

He and Gisele Bündchen started dating in December 2006 and were married in February 2009.

Brady and Gisele Bundchen finalised their divorce in 2022, ending their 13-year marriage. Charles Sykes/Invision/AP

They welcomed two children together – Benjamin, 16, and Vivian, 13 – before divorcing in 2022, after 13 years of marriage.

Brady also has an older child, 19-year-old John ‘Jack’ Moynahan, with his actress ex Bridget Moynahan.

They dated from 2004 until 2006 but split soon after she fell pregnant with his child. Brady and Bündchen got together soon after.
