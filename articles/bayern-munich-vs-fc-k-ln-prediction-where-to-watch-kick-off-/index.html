Bayern Munich vs. FC Köln - Head-to-head record

Bayern Munich have dominated recent meetings with FC Köln, winning eight of their last ten encounters across all competitions. The Bavarians have consistently enjoyed the upper hand, making this a particularly challenging fixture for the visitors.

FC Köln’s only victory in those ten matches came in a friendly in 2021, while their last competitive win against Bayern dates back to February 2011. Their most recent positive result in the Bundesliga was a 1-1 draw at the Allianz Arena in January 2023.

Bayern have won the last five meetings between the sides, including a commanding 5-1 home victory in May 2026. Their attacking quality has regularly proved decisive in this fixture, and they will be confident of extending that winning run on Friday.

Bayern Munich – Looking to Continue Their Dominance

Bayern Munich have made an unbeaten start to their Bundesliga campaign and will be looking to strengthen their position at the top of the table. However, Vincent Kompany’s side has shown some defensive vulnerabilities in recent weeks.

Recent form

Bayern Munich have collected 21 points from their opening nine Bundesliga fixtures, recording six victories and three draws. They currently sit level with Borussia Dortmund at the top of the standings and could establish a three-point advantage before their title rivals play on Saturday.

The Bavarians entered the international break with a 2-1 away victory over Mainz 05, although their finishing was less convincing than the result suggests. Bayern registered 26 shots but converted only two of their four big chances. Nevertheless, that result marked their fourth victory in five matches across all competitions.

Their home record remains impressive, with five victories and two draws from seven matches at the Allianz Arena this season. Bayern have averaged five goals per game in their four home Bundesliga fixtures, scoring 20 and conceding just four. However, they have kept only one clean sheet in their last eight matches across all competitions.

Kompany is expected to return to the dugout after missing five matches following his collapse in October. However, Bayern remain without several important players, including Manuel Neuer, Alphonso Davies, Luis Díaz and Serge Gnabry. Jonas Urbig should start in goal, while Harry Kane is expected to lead the attack.

FC Köln - Struggling on the road

FC Köln have endured a difficult start to the Bundesliga season and will be hoping to produce an improved performance against the league leaders. Their away record and recent attacking struggles make this a particularly demanding fixture.

Recent form

FC Köln currently sit 15th in the Bundesliga with nine points from nine matches. René Wagner’s side have won just one of their last eight league fixtures, recording four draws and three defeats during that sequence. Their latest outing ended in a 2-1 home defeat to Bayer Leverkusen before the international break.

The visitors have struggled for consistency in attack, scoring just twice in their last four Bundesliga matches and failing to find the net twice. Their recent results include goalless draws against Schalke 04 and a narrow defeat to Eintracht Frankfurt.

FC Köln’s away form is another concern, with their only away win in five fixtures across all competitions coming against Würzburger Kickers in the DFB-Pokal in August. Since then, they have suffered three defeats and recorded one draw on their travels.

Wagner also faces defensive selection problems, with Timo Hübers and Rav van den Berg unavailable through injury. Joel Schmied, Jahmai Simpson-Pusey and Tom Krauß are expected to form the back three, while Marius Bülter and Said El Mala should lead the attack.
