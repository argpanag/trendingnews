Tom Cruise Recalls ‘Lovely’ Princess Diana’s Set Visit With Prince William — Report - AOL

TheTools · 03.10.2026 23:40 · source · permalink

Tom Cruise recently recalled his experience of meeting Prince William as a kid and his late mother, Princess Diana. The Hollywood megastar made a guest appearance on the popular talk show, “The Jennifer Hudson Show.” While on the show, the actor revealed that he met the late Princess, whom he called “lovely,” and the Prince of Wales on the set of his movie, “Mission: Impossible.”

Tom Cruise recalls Princess Diana and Prince William’s visit on ‘Mission: Impossible’ set

Tom Cruise recently talked about Princess Diana and Prince William’s visit to the set of his film, “Mission: Impossible,” while on “The Jennifer Hudson Show.” During the interview, the show’s host, Jennifer Hudson, branded the Hollywood star as a “big deal” and then went on to mention how the Prince of Wales and his wife, Kate Middleton, attended the London premiere of the actor’s new movie, “Digger.”

While talking about Prince William and Kate Middleton’s appearance at the “Digger” London premiere, Tom Cruise said, “It was beautiful for them to be there and they’re such an elegant charismatic couple you know as I said you know William William chose well.” Furthermore, the actor also claimed that the Prince of Wales and the Princess of Wales are “very down to earth.”

In the meantime, the show’s host, Jennifer Hudson, also asked Tom Cruise about whether Princess Diana and Prince William visited the set of one of his films in the past. In response to that question, the Hollywood actor disclosed that they indeed visited the set of the first “Mission: Impossible” movie. After that, the actor recalled, “Man, was she lovely,” while referring to the late Princess.

Furthermore, Tom Cruise talked about how Prince William was when he came to the set of “Mission: Impossible” with his mother. According to the actor, the Prince of Wales was also “lovely” and “very kind to everyone and just a kid.” The Hollywood star further called Prince William “mannerly and fun.” “Same person, same guy,” the actor also claimed.
