When is "Hexed" Coming To Disney+? - What's On Disney Plus

TheTools · 20.11.2026 12:23 · source · permalink

This Thanksgiving, Walt Disney Animation Studios is releasing a brand new original film called “Hexed”, which is an all-new magical coming-of-age saga about an impulsive and unconventional teenage girl named Billie (voiced by Hailee Steinfeld) who discovers secret magical abilities which, once unleashed, hurtle her out of suburbia and into a spectacle-filled journey through a vast witch realm called Hexe. Forced to team up with her cautious mother, Alice (voiced by Rashida Jones), they uncover family mysteries that could change the world of witches forever.

Walton Goggins voices Beef Roger Crummchuk, a reclusive, magical three-eyed cat who forges an uneasy alliance with Billie and Alice, while two-time Academy Award winner Jodie Foster voices Queen Celeste, a sinister figure who will stop at nothing to maintain her power in perpetuity over Hexe.

The voice cast also includes Henry Winkler, Dan Stevens, Tracey Ullman and Stephen Fry. Directed by Fawn Veerasunthorn and Jason Hand; co-directed by Josie Trinidad; produced by Roy Conli and Yvett Merino; and written by Walt Disney Animation Studios Chief Creative Officer Jared Bush and Dana Ledoux Miller.

When Is “Hexed” Released?

“Hexed” is being released exclusively in cinemas on November 25, 2026.

When Will “Hexed” Be On Disney+?

Currently, no official Disney+ release date has yet been announced, but based on previous movie releases from Walt Disney Animation Studios, we should expect to see “Hexed” arrive on Disney+ usually around three months or so from the theatrical release, following a digital release just 60 days later. So I’d expect to see “Hexed” arrive on Disney+ at the end of February 2027, or potentially a little later in early March 2027.

What Time Will “Hexed” Be Released On Disney+

Disney+ updates daily at the following times:

  • Pacific Time: 00:00 AM / Midnight
  • Eastern Time: 03:00 AM
  • UK: 08:00 AM
  • Sydney, Australia – 19:00 PM

Sometimes films can arrive a few minutes later as the servers update. The Disney+ front page usually updates about an hour later.
