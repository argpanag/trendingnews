US retailer Costco eyes Christchurch location

TheTools · 14.10.2026 01:02 · source · permalink

US retail giant Costco appears to be eyeing a potential large-scale development in Christchurch, with the council asked to provide feedback on a fast-track application.

The development would reportedly be on land between Memorial Ave and Avonhead Rd, near the airport.

Waimairi Ward councillor Sam MacDonald told 1News he couldn't say much, but said the council had been asked to give feedback on a Costco fast-track application.

The application has not yet been published on the Government’s fast-track application website.
