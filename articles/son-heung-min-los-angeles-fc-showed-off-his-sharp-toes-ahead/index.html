Son Heung-min (Los Angeles FC) showed off his sharp toes ahead of the A-match period.

Son Heung-min scored the first goal in the 34th minute of the first half in the 26th round of the 2026 Major League Soccer against San Jose Earthquakes at Levi's Stadium in Santa Clara, California on the 20th (Korea time).

Son Heung-min, who started as a front-line striker in a 4-3-3 formation, led the team's attack along with Dnie Buanga and Armindo Jeep.

Son Heung-min penetrated the path of Los Angeles FC in a somewhat frustrating flow due to San Jose's five-back. Son Heung-min, who received Marco Delgado's pass in the 34th minute of the first half, continued to break through at the half-line. After lightly beating the opponent, he hit the net with a left-footed mid-range shot near the left side of the penalty arc.

Son Heung-min, who scored his sixth goal in the league, scored a goal for the first time in three games since the match against Real Salt Lake on the 6th.

However, the bad news immediately broke out for LA FC. In the 36th minute of the first half, two minutes after Son Heung-min's first goal, center defender Aaron Long received a red card for blocking the opponent's attack.

LA FC faced the second half amid numerical inferiority.

[Kim Young Hoon, MK Sports Reporter]
