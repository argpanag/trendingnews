Kings vs. Grizzlies Preview: Can Sabonis-Acuff win against the undefeated Grizzlies? - The Kings Herald

Feb 4, 2026; Sacramento, California, USA; Sacramento Kings forward/center Domantas Sabonis (11) rebounds tagainst Memphis Grizzlies guard Cedric Coward (23) during the third quarter at Golden 1 Center. Mandatory Credit: Ed Szczepanski-Imagn Images

The Sacramento Kings are back in action Sunday at Golden 1 Center against the Memphis Grizzlies. Sacramento has a chance to build on the progress made in the last game against the Portland Trail Blazers, in which the offense clicked better and overall the team produced a much better performance than in the season opener.

Who: Sacramento Kings (0-2) vs. Memphis Grizzlies (2-0).
Where: Golden 1 Center, Sacramento, CA
When: 6 PM PST / 9 PM EST
Watch: NBCSCA / League Pass

The Kings ran the offense through Domantas Sabonis against the Blazers and everything functioned a lot more smoothly. He finished with a triple double as a result. The defense, of course, remained a huge problem, and I don’t expect that to change today because it never does in Sacramento. In terms of the rotation, let’s see what happens with De’Andre Hunter and Precious Achiuwa, Malik Monk as the backup point guard and if Emanuel Sharp gets significantly more minutes than Nique Clifford.

The Grizzlies and rookie Cameron Boozer come in undefeated. Memphis is averaging 117 points per game and only allowing 105 points per game. The Kings are averaging 108 points per game and allowing 124 points per game. For the Kings to have any chance, today and really any game this season, Sabonis is going to have to have another monster game.

And everyone will once again be on Darius Acuff Jr. usage and field goal percentage watch. The rookie is averaging 22.5 points, 3.5 assists and 2.5 rebounds per game. He is shooting 30% from the field and 11% from three.

Another tough matchup for the Kings. They will have the home crowd behind them though.

Prediction: Grizzlies, 123, Kings, 110.

I really like what Memphis has done with their team. They’ve bought into the small market model of accumulating picks by doing a proper tear down like OKC and the Spurs. They are going to be relevant far faster than the Kings will ever be.

To recap, once they realized they hit their ceiling they sold high on Bane for 4 unconditional first round picks and a pick swap. Then they sold high on JJJ for 3 firsts and a nice prospect in Taylor Hendricks. Next they absorbed Big Stew from the cap strapped Pistons for 3 second picks. They then capped it off by moving a damn near untradeable contract in Ja for two move movable deals in Grant and Murray.

They’ve got a ton of young wings (Wells, GG, Lopez), swooped in on Ty Jerome, have a very 2 way guard in Cedric Coward, a rim protecting big in Edey, then landed Boozer at #3.

They have all that youth, no bad contracts, and a ton of future picks.
