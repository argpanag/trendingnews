Big Brother delivered a shock eviction last night, with 62-year-old Pam, the mother of former housemate Kieran Davidson, becoming the second housemate to leave the house.

The surprise announcement came during a game of pass the parcel, with Ryan delivering the news to stunned housemates as the departure of the season’s oldest contestant shifts the house’s age dynamic considerably.

Supplied:

There ain’t no party like a surprise party, and nobody does surprises quite like Big Brother!  

After the housemates woke up to find a big red button installed in the house, Big Brother delighted them with all sorts of happy surprises. From ice cream to a very special video call from home, and even an early birthday party for Pheobe, everyone loved the button.  

But all good things must come to an end, and unfortunately Big Brother had one surprise in store that would send shockwaves through the house. With a whopping seven housemates nominated this week, Big Brother decided to surprise the house with an early eviction for the housemate with the least number of votes to save.  

Unfortunately, it was Pam’s time to go!   

Speaking to the housemates, Pam said:

“It’s been a great privilege to be in here… Good luck everyone. Make sure that you win it!”  

“It’s been a great privilege to be in here… Good luck everyone. Make sure that you win it!”  

Returning to the real world, Pam reflected on her shock eviction saying:

“It was a melting pot of emotions. So sad to say goodbye to my friends, yet euphoric stepping through the doors as I knew I would now be seeing my family.”  

“It was a melting pot of emotions. So sad to say goodbye to my friends, yet euphoric stepping through the doors as I knew I would now be seeing my family.”  

Pam’s time in the house has been an absolute whirlwind. As the oldest housemate this season, the 62-year-old teacher had very little patience for shenanigans, attempting to play mediator for some of the clashes in the house.  

After Harmon mocked her age in his message from the grave, Pam proudly spoke about representing older women, proving that they can do anything they put their minds to and hoping to inspire others around the country.  

While Pam’s time in the house has come to an end, there are still six housemates at risk for eviction on Sunday night. Don’t forget to vote to save your fave! Who knows what other surprises Big Brother has in store for our housemates.
