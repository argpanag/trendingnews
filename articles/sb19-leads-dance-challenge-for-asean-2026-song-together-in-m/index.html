SB19 leads dance challenge for ASEAN 2026 song 'Together in Motion'

TheTools · 14.10.2026 06:07 · source · permalink

Attention, A'TIN! We now have a dance challenge for "Together in Motion," the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) 2026 official song performed by SB19.

The Department of Tourism launched the challenge on Saturday with a video of the P-pop Kings dancing to the chorus.

The Philippines is serving as the chair of ASEAN for 2026.

SB19 is a tourism ambassador for the Philippines. The group recently made history as the first Filipino act to perform at Lollapalooza, one of the world's biggest music festivals.

Earlier, Senator Bam Aquino filed a bill seeking support for Filipino creatives who are paving the way for global recognition. SB19 and their company 1Z Entertainment helped craft the bill.

Composed of Pablo, Josh, Stell, Ken, and Justin, SB19 is known for their songs "MAPA," "GENTO," and "Dungka!"
