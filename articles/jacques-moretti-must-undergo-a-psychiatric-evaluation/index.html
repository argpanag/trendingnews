Jacques Moretti must undergo a psychiatric evaluation

TheTools · 03.10.2026 17:08 · source · permalink

Domestic Violence

The owner of Bar Le Constellation must undergo a psychiatric evaluation.

KEYSTONE

Following allegations of domestic violence against his wife, Jessica, Jacques Moretti must undergo a psychiatric evaluation. The evaluation is also intended to assess the risk of him reoffending.

Here's what it's all about

  • According to a media report, Jacques Moretti, the owner of the ill-fated bar “Le Constellation,” must undergo a psychiatric evaluation.
  • Moretti has been in pretrial detention for about seven weeks on suspicion of domestic violence against his wife.
  • The expert opinion is intended to assess Moretti's criminal responsibility and the risk of reoffending.

Jacques Moretti, the owner of the ill-fated bar Le Constellation, must undergo a psychiatric evaluation. This reports RTS, citing the Valais Public Prosecutor’s Office, which ordered the investigation. The office is investigating Moretti on suspicion of domestic violence against his wife, Jessica.

Accordingly, the psychiatric evaluation is aimed in particular at determining Jacques Moretti’s criminal responsibility at the time of the events. In addition, the court must assess the risk of reoffending.

The 50-year-old has been in pretrial detention since his arrest in late August, and the psychiatric evaluation is likely to prolong his detention.

"Significant" head injuries

Jacques Moretti was arrested on the night of August 11–12 at the couple’s home in Lens, Valais, as reported by blue News reported. Moretti is alleged to have assaulted his wife, Jessica, who subsequently had to be treated at a hospital. According to a media report, she is said to have suffered “significant” head injuries.

The investigation into the alleged domestic violence is being conducted by the Valais Public Prosecutor's Office through a separate division and independently of the investigation into the fire at the bar "Le Constellation." The presumption of innocence applies.

The couple who own the restaurant are among the main suspects following the inferno in Crans-Montana. The Valais Public Prosecutor’s Office has charged the two with, among other things, negligent homicide, negligent arson, and negligent infliction of serious bodily injury. In the tragedy on January 1, 41 people lost their lives, and 115 others were injured, some seriously.
