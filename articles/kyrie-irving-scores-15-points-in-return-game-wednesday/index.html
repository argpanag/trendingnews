Kyrie Irving scores 15 points in return game Wednesday

TheTools · 22.10.2026 15:33 · source · permalink

by Leo Sells | Mavericks Correspondent | Thu, Oct 22nd 2026, 11:33am EDT

Kyrie Irving scored 15 points (6-9 FG, 1-4 3PT, 2-2 FT) and grabbed four rebounds in Wednesday's 128-110 loss to the Houston Rockets.

Fantasy Impact:

Irving took the court again on Wednesday after missing a long period of time while recovering from a major knee injury. He looked pretty good in his first game back, scoring 15 points throughout his 24 minutes of action. He will likely be subject to a lot of load management this season, but outside of that, fantasy managers have every reason to expect him to be productive when he's on the court and healthy.
