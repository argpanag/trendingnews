Model Presley Gerber, son of supermodel Cindy Crawford and American businessman Rande Gerber, has died at 27.

According to People and celebrity gossip blog TMZ, the Los Angeles County Medical Examiner confirmed that Presley – older brother of model and actor Kaia Gerber – died on Sunday (US Pacific Daylight Time) while at a rehabilitation facility.

TMZ reports that an autopsy is yet to be completed, and that a representative of Rande Gerber confirmed Presley had died.

“The family is asking for privacy during this very difficult and painful time,” the representative told TMZ and People.

In 2019, Presley was arrested and charged with drink-driving, according to the Hollywood Reporter.

He was sentenced to three years of probation and ordered to complete a drink-driving program in addition to two days of community service.

Presley began modelling as a teenager alongside his sister Kaia, and was signed by the agency IMG Models at 15.
