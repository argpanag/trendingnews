MacKinnon, Makar sustain lower-body injuries in Avalanche loss | NHL.com

TheTools · 15.11.2026 01:36 · source · permalink

Forward leaves following reverse hit on Zub of Senators; defenseman exits four days after leaving practice early

© Jeanine Leech/Icon Sportswire via Getty Images

Nathan MacKinnon and Cale Makar each sustained a lower-body injury in the second period of a 4-3 overtime loss for the Colorado Avalanche against the Ottawa Senators at Canadian Tire Centre in Ottawa on Thursday.

MacKinnon's last shift came with 8:15 remaining in the second. He grabbed his right side after attempting a reverse hit on Senators defenseman Artem Zub and stayed on the bench for a few minutes before heading down the tunnel.

Makar took a shift at 10:44 that lasted only four seconds before heading to the locker room. He briefly returned for a 23-second shift that ended with 5:20 remaining, but stayed on the bench for the rest of the period.

Makar's injury comes four days after he left practice on Sunday for precautionary reasons.

The Avalanche do not practice Friday, but recalled forward Gavin Brindley from Colorado of the American Hockey League ahead of their game at the Montreal Canadiens on Saturday (7 p.m. ET; TVAS, SNE, ALT2, KTVD).

MacKinnon leads the Avalanche and is fifth in the NHL with 30 points (10 goals, 20 assists) in 20 games. Makar is third on Colorado and third among NHL defensemen with 23 points (five goals, 18 assists) in 19 games.

“Obviously, they’re two of the best players in the world, so you like to have them, no doubt,” Avalanche captain Gabriel Landeskog said. “But I don't think it's any different, right? Like you just play with whoever you got, whoever's ready to go, whoever's healthy and up. They play. So, hope those guys will be just fine.”

The Avalanche (11-6-3) are third in the Central Division.

“I liked our effort all the way around (against Ottawa). It's a good hockey team we played, and you're running without a couple of horses, you know, for half the game,” Colorado coach Jared Bednar said. “So, pretty gutsy effort from our group. I'll take it. I mean, we weren't perfect, we made some mistakes. It cost us, but all in all, pretty happy with the game and what we did. It's a big point.”
