Actress faces online backlash after mentioning close friend Karina, fans say 'stop using her for attention'

Updated Nov 11, 2026, 10:24 a.m. KST Published Nov 11, 2026, 5:45 a.m. KST

Actress Han Su-ah is suffering from malicious comments after mentioning her close friendship with aespa member Karina on YouTube. Some users left critical comments on her Instagram, claiming that Han Su-ah is trying to gain recognition by leveraging her relationship with Karina.

On Wednesday, a series of comments appeared on Han's Instagram, including "Why do you keep mentioning Karina's name to promote yourself?" and "Stop using Karina's name."

There were also malicious comments written in English and Chinese. One user left a comment in English to the effect of "Do not use Karina to gain fame." Beyond demands that she stop mentioning Karina, there were also comments interfering with the two's relationship, such as "Just cut off your friendship with Karina."

Previously, Han named Karina as her closest friend on the YouTube variety show 'There Is a Letter Between Us 2,' which was released the previous day. In a letter to Karina, she recalled the day when tears she had been holding back burst out as soon as Karina opened the front door of her house, expressing her gratitude by saying, "Jimin, you have saved me so much without even knowing it."

The two first met at the home of ITZY member Ryu Jin. It is said that near the end of their meeting, Karina was the one who asked Han Su-ah for her contact information, and they became so close over a period of just over a year that they met almost every day. It also became a natural part of their daily routine for Karina to stop by Han Su-ah's house after finishing her schedule to have dinner together.

Han revealed that she had avoided mentioning Karina publicly until now, worried about putting the aespa member in an uncomfortable position. She said she also sought Karina's understanding beforehand regarding this appearance.

According to Han, Karina readily accepted this and even personally selected photos to be used on the broadcast. It is reported that she sent photos from days when they went hiking together in pajamas or visited a zoo, adding explanations of the memories and circumstances at the time for each photo.
