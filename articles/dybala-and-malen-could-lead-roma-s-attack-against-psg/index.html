Gian Piero Gasperini faces two selection decisions ahead of the trip to face the European champions, with Paulo Dybala in contention for a starting spot.

Dybala in Line to Start in Paris

Paulo Dybala could return to Roma's starting lineup against Paris Saint-Germain this Wednesday at the Parc des Princes on Matchday 5 of the Champions League.


The Argentine has the edge over Rodrigo Mora in the race to join Donyell Malen and Matías Soulé in the Italian side's attack.

Dybala's inclusion would give Gian Piero Gasperini a creative player capable of generating chances between the lines and exploiting gaps in PSG's defense.

UEFA Champions League

Malen and Soulé Pose a Major Threat

Donyell Malen enters the match after scoring four goals in Roma's 5-1 victory over Parma. The Dutch forward is enjoying an outstanding run of form and will be the focal point of Roma's attack.

Alongside him, Matías Soulé will have the freedom to operate behind the forwards and link midfield with attack.

Javier Pastore, who played for both Roma and PSG, praised the three attackers in an interview with La Gazzetta dello Sport, highlighting Malen's exceptional goalscoring form and Dybala's ability to pose a threat from different areas of the field.


Molina or Lulli on the Right

Injuries to Lorenzo Pellegrini and Devyne Rensch limit Gasperini's options for the trip to Paris.

Argentine international Nahuel Molina has the edge over Emanuele Lulli at right wing-back, while Wesley is expected to start on the left. Bryan Cristante and Manu Koné would complete the midfield.

At the back, Gianluca Mancini, Evan N'Dicka and Mario Hermoso are expected to form a three-man defense in front of goalkeeper Mile Svilar.

Roma's Predicted Lineup Against PSG

According to the predicted lineups published by GOAL, Gian Piero Gasperini could field the following starting XI:

Roma (3-4-1-2): Mile Svilar; Gianluca Mancini, Evan N'Dicka, Mario Hermoso; Nahuel Molina, Bryan Cristante, Manu Koné, Wesley; Matías Soulé; Donyell Malen and Paulo Dybala.
