Bacuna seals Curaçao’s third straight win | Concacaf

TheTools · 02.10.2026 03:40 · source · permalink

Curaçao continued its strong run alongside wins for Haiti and Costa Rica in Group A.

MIAMI – League A closed Group Stage Thursday action of the 2026/2027 Concacaf Nations League with big results from Curaçao, Haiti and

Curaçao 1, Trinidad & Tobago 0

Curaçao keeps on their tally in Group A with a 1-nil win against Trinidad & Tobago at Stadion Ergilio Hato in Willemstad, Curaçao.

After a goalless half, The Blue Wave found its breakthrough in the 58’ thanks to a ready Juninho Bacuna, who capitalized an assist in the box to send a right foot volley to the back of the net to secure Curaçao’s third straight win in Group A.

Dominican Republic 1, Haiti 4

Haiti secured a 4-1 away victory against hosts Dominican Republic at Estadio Olímpico Félix Sánchez in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic.

The Grenadiers kicked off their goal count of the night in the first minutes of the match with Louicius Deedson sending the ball to the back of the net in the 9’.

Wilson Isidor was ready in the box to take advantage of a loose ball after a free kick in the 51’, scoring Haiti’s second goal of the night with a header. Dorny Romero pulled one back for the Dominican Republic in the 55’ with a left foot shot in the area.

Isidor sealed a brace, and Haiti’s third goal in the match, in the 67’. The last goal of the night came from a header by Yassin Fortuné in the 77’.

Nicaragua 0, Costa Rica 3

Costa Rica closed League A action with a 3-nil win against Nicaragua at Estadio Nacional in Managua, Nicaragua.

Los Ticos managed to break the Nicaraguan deadlock in the second half, with Orlando Sinclair netting the first goal for his team in the 57’.

Carlos Mora doubled Costa Rica’s lead in the 60’, ready in the area to finish a great collective play with a right foot shot in the box.

With a right foot long shot from the edge of the box in the 90+7 Kenyel Michel scored the third and final goal for Costa Rica, securing their first win in the 2026/20267 CNL campaign.
