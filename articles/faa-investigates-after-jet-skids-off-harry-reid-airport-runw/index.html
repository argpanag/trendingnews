FAA investigates after jet skids off Harry Reid Airport runway as officials review records

LAS VEGAS (KSNV) — A private jet carrying actor Donnie Wahlberg narrowly avoided a far more serious crash Sunday morning at Harry Reid International Airport when the plane aborted takeoff, ran off the end of the runway, and struck a perimeter fence.

Wahlberg, the brother of actor Mark Wahlberg, was one of eight people on board the Dassault Falcon 50. After the close call, Wahlberg had an emotional message: “Just finished praying and I looked out the window and thought we are not going up in the air today and all is well okay don't worry.”

The jet remained on the other side of the airport’s perimeter fence, surrounded by yellow tape. The fence had already been repaired.

The Federal Aviation Administration is investigating the incident. In a statement, the FAA did not say why the flight crew aborted takeoff, saying only that the cause is under investigation.

Pilot and aviation expert Reed Yadon said the aircraft likely was traveling at high speed when the crew rejected the takeoff.

“It should have been doing close to 150 miles an hour at the time of the incident,” Yadon said. “That's pretty fast, and to get that aircraft stopped in that distance, that's pretty good.”

Yadon praised the pilots for aborting the takeoff without injuries and credited airport personnel for their response.

“In the scheme of things, this was about as - it was textbook. Everybody did what they should,” Yadon said.

Yadon said investigators will examine multiple possibilities, including mechanical issues and human factors.

“Was the cause within the aircraft, or was it a human failure?” Yadon said. “They'll look at both, go over training records, go over all the maintenance records on the aircraft.”

He said investigators will also have data to review, including recordings and video.

“And they have a cockpit voice recorder in the aircraft, so they have a lot of data,” Yadon said. “They'll have security cameras from Harry Reid.”

A visualization from FlightFollower.net showed the timeline leading up to the mishap, starting at 10:50 a.m. Sunday. Audio from air traffic control captured the Falcon 50 being cleared for takeoff and then the moment the tower realized the aircraft was not airborne.

“7 sierra golf... You okay?” a controller asked. The response came back: “Aborting aborting.”

After the jet went off the end of the runway, the tower told the aircraft emergency vehicles were on the way and asked whether there were injuries.

The incident also disrupted airport operations. A passenger jet that had been cleared to land was waved off, and a ground stop was put in place. During a ground stop, departing flights are not boarded or must return to the gate, while incoming flights may be held at their origin, diverted to other airports, or required to circle if they are already close.
