Victor Wembanyama (back) is in for the Spurs' NBA Cup game against the Blazers, but De'Aaron Fox (shin) is unavailable to play.

The San Antonio Spurs will see the return of Victor Wembanyama in the lineup, although De'Aaron Fox will remain sidelined.

Going up against the Portland Trail Blazers for a Wednesday NBA Cup showdown, the Spurs have officially announced their final injury report regarding their two franchise centerpieces, as the French superstar is officially back in action to lead San Antonio.

Victor Wembanyama will rejoin the San Antonio Spurs' road clash against the Portland Trail Blazers after missing his first game of the 2026-27 NBA season in the team's Monday win in Sacramento against the Kings.

Wembanyama was initially listed as questionable and a game-time decision in his status to play due to lower back tightness, but he has been cleared to go for the major NBA Cup clash with the Blazers.

Through 17 games played so far in the regular season, Wembanyama is averaging a dominant 29.9 points, 12.3 rebounds, 3.9 blocks, and 3.2 assists while shooting over 54% from the field. 

Unlike Victor Wembanyama, De'Aaron Fox has been ruled out by the San Antonio Spurs against the Portland Trail Blazers due to a right shin contusion.

The star point guard will now miss his second consecutive game for San Antonio. He also did not suit up for the Spurs' previous victory over the Sacramento Kings.

Fox is averaging 12.4 points, 3.9 rebounds, and 5.8 assists in 16 games for the Spurs so far this season. 

His next chance to play for San Antonio might come on Friday's NBA Cup face-off against the Los Angeles Lakers.
