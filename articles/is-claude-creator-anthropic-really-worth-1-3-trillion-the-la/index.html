Is Claude creator Anthropic really worth $1.3 trillion? - The Latest

TheTools · 20.11.2026 18:53 · source · permalink

The AI firm and Claude chatbot maker Anthropic has made a historic stock market debut, with a peak valuation of $1.3tn on its first day of trading. It marks a remarkable ascent for a start-up launched in just 2021 by a group of ex-OpenAI staffers. Anthropic’s CEO, Dario Amodei, said the record-breaking IPO had ‘strengthened’ the company’s resolve to ‘do the right thing’. Despite the excitement, the Guardian’s head of business John Collingridge tells Lucy Hough that there are still challenges ahead for the global AI industry
