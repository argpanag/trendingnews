Why There's No Boston Blue Tonight (November 20) & When New Season 2 Episodes Release

TheTools · 20.11.2026 22:00 · source · permalink

Boston Blue won't be airing tonight, November 20, 2026, in its usual scheduled time slot, so when do the new season 2 episodes release? Blue Bloods ran for far longer than anyone would have guessed considering the delays, the 2023 SAG-AFTRA strike, and pay cuts, but it went on to complete its story in season 14.

Now the show is back in the form of a spinoff, Boston Blue, which aired its first season on CBS in October 2025. The series still stars Donnie Wahlberg as DS Danny Reagan, but he's since moved from New York City, where Blue Bloods takes place, to Boston, Massachusetts.

After Danny's son Sean (Mika Amonsen), who worked for the BPD, is injured in a suspected murder attempt, Danny moves departments in order to look after him. He's partnered with Lena Silver (Sonequa Martin-Green), a member of BPD royalty and a talented detective who is looking to rise fast.

Season 1 was a solid hit, earning an 82% on Rotten Tomatoes and solid viewership numbers. The change of scenery has done a lot for the story, and Wahlberg is just as charismatic in his hometown of Boston as he is in New York City. The series adds just enough shake-ups to the familiar plot to keep things fresh,

In December 2025, Boston Blue was renewed for a second season, which premiered on October 9, 2026, in the same Friday-night time slot that used to be reserved for Blue Bloods, CBS' 10:00 PM ET/PT slot. So far, the show has run weekly, but tonight, November 20, there is no new episode of Boston Blue, and it will be a while before we get episode 7.

Boston Blue Season 2 Is Going On A Mini-Break

Boston Blue season 2 is going on a mini mid-season hiatus before episode 7 airs. This isn't the first time the series has taken a break like this. Back in 2025, Boston Blue also took some time between episode 6, "Code of Ethics", which aired on November 21, 2025, and episode 7, "Baggage Claim", which aired on December 5, 2025.

It's unclear exactly why the series is taking such a long break before episode 7, titled "Crisis". The week of Thanksgiving is usually a no-go for new episodes, which explains why there's no new episode on November 27, but it's less clear why there's no episode in the week before and after.

Tonight, a rerun of Sheriff County season 2, episode 1, will take the 10 PM slot. On November 27, a rerun of Boston Blue season 1, episode 12, "St. Patrick's Day" will take the 10 PM slot. It's not clear what will be airing in Boston Blue's usual timeslot on December 4, 2026.

When Boston Blue Season 2, Episode 7 Will Release (& What To Expect)

Boston Blue season 2, episode 7, "Crisis" will air at its usual 10 PM timeslot on December 11, 2026. The episode promises to be an action-packed return of the series. Maria Baez's (Marisa Ramirez) daughter Elena is kidnapped the morning of Sarah Silver's (Maggie Lawson) wedding.

The Reagan and Silva families go on the hunt for the lost girl, which will bring them into contact with Elena's biological father, Arturo Muñoz, whose cartel was responsible for Danny's wife's death in Blue Bloods. In Blue Bloods, Arturo was played by Juan Francisco, but in Boston Blue, he's been recast by Amaury Nolasco.
