The weather on Friday, October 9, is expected to be fair to partly cloudy, with some clouds appearing eastward by afternoon, according to a forecast by the National Centre of Meteorology (NCM).

Conditions are likely to get humid by night and Saturday morning over some coastal and internal areas with a chance of fog or mist formation.

Light to moderate southeasterly becoming northwesterly winds are set to blow, with a speed of 10-20 km/hr, reaching 30 km/hr.
