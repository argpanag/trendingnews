Dame Valerie Adams pays tribute to the late Phil Gifford

TheTools · 30.09.2026 22:56 · source · permalink
  • On Air
  • Mike Hosking Breakfast

One of our greatest athletes has shared her memories of renowned broadcaster Phil Gifford. 

The man who first rose to prominence as Loosehead Len in the 1970s has died in Auckland at the age of 79.  

Gifford's decades long career spans working at multiple radio stations, including hosting on Newstalk ZB – most recently on the Afternoons show alongside Simon Barnett. 

One of his friends, Dame Valerie Adams told Mike Hosking she felt immediately at ease with Gifford before they started writing her book. 

She recalls being greeted by Polynesian artefacts and an incredible warmth within his home.
