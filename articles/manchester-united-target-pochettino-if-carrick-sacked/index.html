Manchester United target Pochettino if Carrick sacked

TheTools · 11.11.2026 08:31 · source · permalink

Getty

Manchester United are preparing to turn to Mauricio Pochettino if Michael Carrick is sacked.

It has been reported that plans are afoot to recruit the Argentinian should Carrick, as expected, be dismissed by United with the club in the relegation zone on the back of their worst start to a season since 1986.

Club executives have been doing their due diligence regarding Pochettino, who is understood to be interested in the Old Trafford post.

The former Tottenham, Paris St Germain and Chelsea boss signed a contract extension to remain as United States head cdoach through to the 2030 World Cup just three months ago, and US Soccer would be due compensation should he agree to move to United.

Chief executive Omar Berrada and director of football Jason Wilcox are set to hold talks with United co-owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe in Monaco on Wednesday.

Carrick was photographed arriving at United's Carrington training ground on Wednesday morning.
