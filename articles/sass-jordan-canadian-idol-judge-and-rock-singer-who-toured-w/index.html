Sass Jordan, 'Canadian Idol' judge and rock singer who toured with Aerosmith, Van Halen, has died

TheTools · 05.10.2026 12:19 · source · permalink

Sass Jordan, Canadian Idol judge and rock singer who had toured alongside Aerosmith and other artists like Van Halen at the start of her career, has died.

Video above: Remembering those we lost in 2026

The singer's family said on Oct. 2 that she was surrounded by loved ones when she "peacefully crossed over to the other side."

"She remains in our hearts, an inimitable force of nature," her family said in a post on Instagram. "An irreplaceable being of light and joy!"

Jordan was born in England and moved to Montreal at three years old. There, she began playing guitar with her friends in Westmount Park as a young teen, her publicist Eric Alper shared after her death. By the time she was 16, Jordan was fronting bands in downtown Montreal clubs.

In a statement to People Magazine, Alper described Jordan as a "woman whose generosity and fire touched everyone who shared a stage, a studio or a kitchen table with her."

People Magazine said two years after she began her recording career as co-lead vocalist on “Come On (Baby Tonight)” from Bündock’s album Mauve, Jordan released her solo debut, Tell Somebody.

The title track rose to number 11 in Canada and she won the Juno Award for Most Promising Female Vocalist the following year. She was later nominated for Best Female Vocalist in 1990, 1993 and 1995.

Jordan then went on to tour alongside Aerosmith, The Rolling Stones, AC/DC, and Van Halen and joined "Canadian Idol" as a judge in 2003.

She was a judge on the show for all six seasons.

According to the Fort Frances Times, Jordan canceled her performances three months before her death, due to what her team described as a "serious medical condition."
