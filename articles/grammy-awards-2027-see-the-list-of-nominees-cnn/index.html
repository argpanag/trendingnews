Grammy Awards 2027: See the list of nominees | CNN

TheTools · 16.11.2026 12:41 · source · permalink

Nominations for the 69th Grammy Awards were unveiled on Monday, with a new generation of female musicians leading the honorees, but big-name icons like Paul McCartney, Madonna, The Rolling Stones and the late Dolly Parton also received ample recognition.

The British singer-songwriter Raye became this year’s most nominated performer, scoring 11 nods, while Spanish artist Rosalía and country star Ella Langley weren’t far behind, with nine and seven nominations, respectively.

Miranda Lambert, Olivia Rodrigo and Phoebe Bridgers all received seven nominations apiece, while Bruno Mars, Leon Thomas and Olivia Dean (last year’s best new artist) received six nods.

Also making history? Beyoncé, who scored an astonishing 100th Grammy nomination. Already the most-nominated artist in Grammys history, Queen B’s latest nod was for the song “Morning Dew (Donk)” in the Best R&B Solo Performance category.

This year’s ceremony will be broadcast live on ABC and streamed simultaneously on Hulu and Disney+ from Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles on February 7.
