Nicole Kidman Says She’s Planning to See Adéla Live in Nashville & The ‘Nicole Kidman’ Singer Is Freaking Out

TheTools · 27.08.2026 00:42 · source · permalink

"She's coming to Nashville and I'm going to go see her," the Oscar-winning actress says of the pop singer's current Red Bottoms Tour.

Trending on Billboard

Nicole Kidman may have needed a second to place the name, but once a fan mentioned Adéla‘s upcoming song titled after her, the Oscar-winning actress was all in.

At the London premiere of Practical Magic 2 on Wednesday (Aug. 26), Kidman was asked by a fan whether she knew Adéla, the Slovak singer/songwriter whose rapidly rising pop career is about to get an especially Kidman-esque addition.

“Do you know who Adéla is?” the fan asked in a video.

“No!” Kidman initially replied.

After the fan explained that Adéla is a singer with a song called “Nicole Kidman,” however, Kidman’s memory quickly kicked in.

“Oh, yes! She is! I do know!” Kidman exclaimed. “I think she’s Slovakian, right?”

When the fan confirmed that the singer was “amazing,” Kidman doubled down on her fandom and revealed she may soon be seeing Adéla herself in concert.

“I absolutely know who she is and she’s coming to Nashville and I’m going to go see her,” Kidman said.

Kidman also seems to be putting her newfound fandom into action. On Thursday (Aug. 27), the actress launched her official TikTok account with a nearly minute-long montage documenting her day at the Practical Magic 2 London premiere, featuring Adéla’s “Ain’t in LA.” The video follows Kidman getting ready for the event, greeting fans on the carpet, posing for photos and standing alongside her castmates. Coincidence or not, the TikTok was posted just a day after she was asked about the singer, adding yet another unexpected crossover between the pair and their budding connection.

The timing couldn’t be better for the ascending, pink-haired pop star. Adéla is set to release her debut album, PRIMA, on Sept. 4, and the newly revealed 11-song track list includes “Nicole Kidman” as its second track. The project will also feature her breakout single “Ain’t in LA,” which became her first entry on the Billboard Hot 100, debuting at No. 85.

Adéla is currently bringing the PRIMA era to fans on her Red Bottoms Tour, which stops off in Nashville on Sept. 21 at The Basement East.

According to Adéla Instagram fan site @adeladiary, the pop singer reacted to Kidman’s kind words later Wednesday, commenting: “WOW OH MY GOD NICOLE WOW OH MY GOD HOLY SH– WOW.”

As for whether the actress has heard the song that bears her name? That’s a question the fan didn’t get to ask. But with PRIMA on the horizon, maybe Kidman will soon be able to report back.
