Watch: Blackpink's Lisa, Jisoo tease 'SaWaDiKa,' 'Click' music videos - UPI.com

TheTools · 04.09.2026 03:48 · source · permalink

Sept. 2 (UPI) -- Blackpink members Lisa and Jisoo are both teasing new solo music.

The K-pop stars released previews of their upcoming music videos Wednesday, with Lisa teasing her song "SaWaDiKa" and Jisoo giving a glimpse of her "Click" video.

In the "SaWaDiKa" teaser, Lisa is seen wearing a red and black bodysuit as she walks through a hallway lit with a wall of TV screens.

Lisa will release "SaWaDiKa" and the song's full music video Thursday. The track is the lead single to debut from her forthcoming solo EP, Press Play, set for release Oct. 23.

In the "Click" preview, Jisoo is seen surveying a post-apocalyptic landscape and visiting an abandoned amusement park where she finds a teddy bear.

Jisoo will release "Click," its music video and her single album of the same name on Friday.

Blackpink also consists of Jennie and Rosé. Jennie released the solo EP Fallen Angel and a music video for her song of the same name last week.
