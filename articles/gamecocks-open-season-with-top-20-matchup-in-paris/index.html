Gamecocks Open Season with Top-20 Matchup in Paris

TheTools · 01.11.2026 11:02 · source · permalink

  • Live Stats
  • Game Notes

COLUMBIA, SC – No. 1/1 South Carolina officially opens its season with a top-20 matchup at Oui-Play in Paris for the second time in four seasons. The Gamecocks take on No. 20/17 Maryland on Mon., Nov. 2, at noon ET, at Adidas Arena. The game is the first half of a doubleheader that will air on ESPN.

South Carolina Gamecocks (0-0)
Head Coach: Dawn Staley
Top Returning Scorer: Joyce Edwards (19.2)
Top Returning Rebounder: Joyce Edwards (6.8)

Maryland Terrapins (0-0)
Head Coach: Brenda Frese
Top Returning Scorer: Oluchi Okananwa (17.8)
Top Returning Rebounder: Oluchi Okananwa (5.4)

The Maryland Series
The Gamecocks are 7-2 all-time against Maryland after winning the last five meetings, including a 7-1 mark in the Dawn Staley era with all eight games coming with both teams ranked in the top 20.

The most recent meeting saw then-No. 2 South Carolina defeat then-No. 18 Maryland in the 2025 NCAA Sweet 16 in Birmingham, Ala., on March 28. Then-junior Chloe Kitts posted a 15-point, 11-rebound double-double, including 10 and 6, respectively, in the first half to keep the Gamecocks close at the break before a 72.7 percent shooting third quarter gave them the edge they needed to secure the victory.

 

Ranked Readings
Against ranked teams, South Carolina is 147-75 (.662) in the Dawn Staley era (beginning 2008-09), and even that mark is skewed by the early seasons of her tenure. Since the start of the 2014-15 campaign, the Gamecocks are 132-36 (.786) against ranked teams, including a 62-9 (.873) mark since the start of the 2021-22 season. The Gamecocks are 130-25 (.839) under Staley when ranked higher/better than their opponent.

 

Back in (Garnet and) Black
South Carolina’s 15-player roster includes eight returning letterwinners all of whom have at least one SEC championship and appearance in the NCAA national championship game. The six letterwinners returning from the 2025-26 squad accounted for 52.2 percent of that team’s scoring and 51.0 percent of its rebounding.

All-America forward Joyce Edward headlines that group as its top returning scorer after pouring in a program-record 768 total points and ranking among the nation’s most efficient scorers with a .571 field goal percentage. All-SEC guard Tessa Johnson also hit high efficiency marks with her 44.8 percent 3-point shooting ranking fourth in the nation. Sophomore Agot Makeer thrived in the 2026 NCAA Tournament, ranking second on the team with 14.0 points per game on 55.0 percent shooting (46.2 from 3-point range) at the event.

Re-joining that core are fifth-year forwards Chloe Kitts and Ashlyn Watkins, who both missed the 2025-26 season due to injury. Both have drawn All-SEC honors in their careers behind strong paint presence and versatility. Kitts was an honorable mention All-American and MVP of the SEC Tournament and NCAA Regional in 2025, finishing her junior season with 10.2 points and a team-high 7.7 rebounds per game. Watkins’ last full season was 2023-24 when her 9.2 points and 7.4 rebounds per game off the bench made her an All-SEC selection. She played in 14 games in 2024-25 before suffering a torn ACL at Mississippi State on Jan. 5.

The remaining three returns are all looking for more experience and to make an impact. Junior Adhel Tac is looking to shake off injuries that have consistently interrupted her on-court contributions. Sophomores Ayla McDowell and Alicia Tournebize are poised to elevate their contributions this season as well.

 

New to the Nest
South Carolina welcomes seven newcomers for the 2026-27 season, including five freshmen who made up the No. 2 signing class in then nation.

Junior guard Jordan Lee leads the transfer duo as the No. 6 impact transfer in the nation, according to ESPN. Last season at Texas, she started 38 games with career-high marks of 13.2 points per game on 42.2 percent shooting to go with her defensive bona fides.

Fifth-year guard Brooke Daniels joined the Gamecocks in early September, bringing Big Ten All-Defensive Team honors to the backcourt.

A pair of top-10 signees highlight Staley’s elite Class of 2026 with No. 3 Oliviyah Edwards and No. 6 Jerzy Robinson, who was a Naismith Player of the Year finalist. No. 19 Kaeli Wynn and No. 30 Kelsi Andrews further bolstered the class, despite injuries curtailing their senior seasons and higher class rankings. French freshman Justine Loubens finalized the incoming class after spending a season averaging 6.5 points on 47.2 percent shooting in France’s first division for women’s basketball.

Learn more about each of the newcomers on and off the court in the New to the Nest series, which includes a short video on each first-year Gamecock at: https://scgamecocks.info/NewNest.
