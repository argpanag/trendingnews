Lukashenko appealed to the United States: I am a "Trumpist", but return the debt: EADaily

TheTools · 12.09.2026 09:14 · source · permalink

Belarus is open to contacts with the United States. Alexander Lukashenko said this today, September 11, answering journalists' questions.

"We are open. They have removed sanctions from a number of our enterprises, and they are ready to continue to remove them. There are a lot of questions. Embassies — let's discuss how we will resume. Nuclear materials and stuff — we have a whole program there — please. If you want potash fertilizers from us, if you want to buy them, you're welcome."

According to Lukashenko, he is a "Trumpist" and supports Donald Trump.

"I see his mistakes. I am talking about this directly to his best friend, who should come to us," Lukashenko said.

The President of Belarus also mentioned the World Gas Council, confirming his readiness to work in this format as well.

"Okay, let's get together. Just don't play along there — in the form of scenery. This is no good for me. I tell them directly that I am not ready to meet with Donald Trump for this… To meet with him (this is good, it would be very useful for Belarus), you need to understand: we met, but what next? Did you say hello and leave?" — Lukashenka asked a question.

As for the possibility of a "big deal" between Belarus and the United States, which was mentioned earlier, the Belarusian leader confirmed that this is still relevant today. According to him, "we are ready for both a small and a big deal."

Speaking about the readiness to conduct a dialogue with the United States on various topics, the Belarusian leader mentioned the potash sector. According to him, "now we are shipping potassium — we are selling it to the United States of America."

"Do you want potash fertilizers from us (if you want to buy) — Please. We can't do it right away. Here we have freed up. We have contracted them for this year, we will consider them next year. Now it is possible to load the vessel (we will definitely finish loading tomorrow) — take it away… But return the favor. Our $ 43-44 million in Citibank was stuck — the company that cooperated with us paid the money. Rich America is stuck. Please return this money to us," the President of Belarus said.

Recall that with the coming to power of Trump, relations between Minsk and Washington have become less complicated. The United States lifted some sanctions from Belarus and expressed its readiness to conduct a dialogue with Lukashenko.
