Shaun Chen calls out Grab driver who pointed middle finger at him

TheTools · 16.11.2026 09:16 · source · permalink

"Why so rude?" he wrote.

November 13, 2026, 04:00 PM

Photo from shaunchenhongyu/Instagram

A Grab delivery driver gave Shaun Chen the middle finger.

The 48-year-old actor then took to Instagram on Nov. 12 to call him out, uploading a dashcam video and two images of the incident.

In the video, Chen's car can be seen at the side of the road, in what appears to be an HDB housing estate.

The Grab driver then drove past him on a motorcycle and gave him the middle finger.

However, it is not known what happened after.

He wrote in the caption, "Why so rude?? [Is it] normal to point middle finger like that?" and tagged the official Grab Instagram account.

The other two images he posted show the licence plate of the motorcycle, and a clear shot of the middle finger gesture.

In response to Mothership's queries, a Grab spokesperson said they are aware of the video circulating online and are currently looking into the matter.

"Road safety and mutual respect are important to us. We expect all our partners to uphold our code of conduct, and will take appropriate action where needed."
