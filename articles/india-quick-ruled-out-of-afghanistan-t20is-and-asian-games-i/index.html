India quick ruled out of Afghanistan T20Is and Asian Games | ICC

TheTools · 10.09.2026 08:08 · source · permalink

An injury has ruled Harshit Rana out of India’s upcoming assignments, with Yash Thakur added to the squad.

India fast bowler Harshit Rana has been ruled out of the upcoming T20I series against Afghanistan and the Asian Games 2026.

Rana, who was progressing well in his recovery from a hamstring injury picked up during the tour of England, sustained a strain while bowling during a match simulation on September 7, the BCCI confirmed in a statement.

View this post on Instagram

Yash Thakur has been named as his replacement in both squads. The 27-year-old recently made his T20I debut during India’s tour of Zimbabwe in July, taking four wickets in two matches.

Rana was initially named subject to fitness clearance when India announced their squads earlier this month. Jasprit Bumrah, Washington Sundar and Nitish Kumar Reddy were also named subject to fitness at the time.

India’s updated squad for Afghanistan T20Is: Shreyas Iyer (c), Abhishek Sharma, Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, Sanju Samson, Ishan Kishan, Tilak Varma, Shivam Dube, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Axar Patel, Washington Sundar, Varun Chakaravarthy, Ravi Bishnoi, Jasprit Bumrah, Arshdeep Singh, Yash Thakur

India squad for the 2026 Asian Games: Shreyas Iyer (c), Abhishek Sharma, Sanju Samson, Ishan Kishan, Shivam Dube, Tilak Varma, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Axar Patel, Washington Sundar, Varun Chakaravarthy, Ravi Bishnoi, Jasprit Bumrah, Arshdeep Singh, Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, Yash Thakur
