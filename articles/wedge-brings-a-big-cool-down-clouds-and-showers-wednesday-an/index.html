Wedge brings a big cool down, clouds and showers Wednesday and Thursday

COLUMBIA, S.C. — Fall officially begins at 8:05PM tonight, and a major change in our weather is already knocking on the door.

Scattered showers and thunderstorms will continue through this evening as our final warm and humid day comes to an end.

The real change arrives overnight as a front pushes south through the Midlands and high pressure builds across the Northeast.

That combination will establish a wedge, also known as cold air damming, across the Carolinas beginning Wednesday.

A wedge happens when cool, dense air gets trapped against the eastern side of the Appalachian Mountains, and once it settles in, it can be stubborn to leave.

For us, that means we'll trade the recent heat and humidity for much cooler temperatures, plenty of clouds, breezy northeast winds and occasional light rain.

Wednesday will likely be locked in with clouds for much of the day, with pesky light showers or drizzle possible from time to time.

Temperatures will struggle to warm, ranging from the lower 70s across the northern Midlands to around 80 degrees farther south, depending on exactly how far the wedge pushes.

That is a drop of 10 to 15 degrees or more compared to Tuesday for areas firmly inside the cooler air.

Northeast winds will also pick up Wednesday at 10 to 15 mph, with occasional gusts of 20 to 25 mph.

The wedge becomes even more established Wednesday night into Thursday, when periods of light rain become more likely across the Midlands.

Thursday looks like the coolest and dreariest day of the stretch, with widespread clouds, occasional rain and highs only reaching the upper 60s to lower 70s for many communities.

Breezy conditions continue Thursday with northeast to north winds occasionally gusting between 20 and 30 mph.

We'll finally begin breaking free from the wedge later Thursday into Friday as an upper-level trough helps push the cooler air out and drier northwest flow takes over.

Sunshine returns Friday with highs rebounding into the upper 70s, followed by a beautiful fall weekend with highs around 80 degrees.

The cooler nights may be even more noticeable, with lows falling into the 50s Friday night and Saturday night.

After months of summer heat, this will be our first real stretch of fall-like weather, arriving right on cue for the beginning of astronomical fall.
