Kerala Lottery Prize Claim Process

Winners of the Kerala Lottery Bhagyathara BT 71 must follow the prescribed prize-claim procedure.

According to the information provided for the draw, winners of prizes up to Rs. 5,000 can claim the amount through authorised lottery shops in Kerala.

For prizes above Rs. 5,000, winners must surrender the winning ticket at the designated bank or government lottery office, along with valid identification and other required documents.

Winners should keep the original ticket safely and ensure that it is not damaged before completing the claim process.
