The arrival of the handheld PSP 3 could cancel the release of the stationary PlayStation 6.

TheTools · 15.09.2026 04:03 · source · permalink

Shawn Layden, former chairman of Sony Interactive Entertainment Worldwide Studios and former head of SIE America, commented on rumors of the development of the new PSP 3 portable platform alongside the PlayStation 6 desktop system in an interview on the Kiwi Talkz channel. According to the top executive, the simultaneous promotion of two fundamentally different hardware lines creates insurmountable difficulties for the company. If plans to release a portable device are realistic, it would make sense for the manufacturer to completely abandon the traditional PlayStation 6 desktop system and consolidate all gaming capabilities around a single compact solution, as supporting two systems simultaneously inevitably leads to a blurring of focus and internal competition.

As an example, the industry veteran cited the parallel life cycles of the PlayStation Portable and PlayStation 3. Despite strong global sales of the first portable, the home PlayStation 3 experienced a rocky launch and stagnation, as the corporation's resources were spread thin. Layden emphasized that this constraint affects not only development studios but also advertising budgets, which are forced to reallocate resources from one division to the detriment of another. To smoothly support the desktop PlayStation 6 and the portable PSP 3 simultaneously, the company would effectively have to establish two completely independent entities with their own management.

The expert cited the saturation of the global desktop platform market, where the maximum audience has already been reached, as an additional factor. Sony's interest in returning to the PSP 3 format is driven by the commercial performance of its competitors' hybrid devices, but attempting to gamble on both ends of the spectrum poses a direct threat to both businesses. Layden is confident that, given the current economic climate, the platform holder will be unable to manage the marketing and technical support for two separate devices, so choosing a single portable format will bring the company greater practical benefits.
