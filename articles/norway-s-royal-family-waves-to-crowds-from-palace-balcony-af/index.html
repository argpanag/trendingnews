    Joe Coughlan
    Live reporter

    King Harald V of Norway has been laid to rest following a funeral procession that drew tens of thousands of people to the streets of Oslo to pay their respects.

    The late monarch, who died last month aged 89, spent 35 years on the throne and was remembered during a public service in the capital's cathedral.

    Family and special guests then gathered for a private ceremony in the Palace Chapel at Akershus Fortress for the king's burial - the end of which was marked by flags being raised from half-mast and a royal salute.

    Figures in attendance included the Prince of Wales on behalf of King Charles III, as well as the Princess Royal, King Abdullah II of Jordan and Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelensky.

    The procession ended with the new King Haakon VIII waving from the Royal Palace balcony, marking the beginning of a new reign for the country.

  2. Who is Haakon VIII, Norway's new king?published at 17:14 BST 9 September
    17:14 BST 9 September

    Haakon VIII was born in Oslo in July 1973, the the only son of the late King Harald and his wife Queen Sonja - and younger brother to Princess Märtha Louise. He is also a great-great-great-grandson of Britain's Queen Victoria.

    By Haakon's own admission, he struggled at times to accept his royal status in his early years, writing in his 2023 autobiography: "Yes, I was a royal, but I did not want to talk about it... ​At school it made me anxious."

    He became crown prince in 1991 at the tender age of 17, following the death of his grandfather King Olav V. After graduating from the Norwegian Naval Academy in 1995, he enrolled at the University of Berkeley to study political science.

    Shortly after his return to Norway in 1999, he met his now-wife Mette-Marit - a single mother to a four-year-old child whose father had served time for a conviction of cocaine possession.

    Their relationship was met with a fierce public debate from its early days but the pair ultimately married in 2001 and had two children, Crown Princess Ingrid-Alexandra and Prince Sverre Magnus.

    Haakon has become the king at a turbulent time for Norway's royal family. Only this year, Mette-Marit, now Queen of Norway, apologised for her friendship with late sex offender Jeffrey Epstein, and her son has been convicted of rape in a high-profile trial. Marius Borg Høiby is appealing against the conviction.

    The new queen, who was diagnosed with pulmonary fibrosis in 2018, is also recovering from a lung transplant.

    The royal family has just come out onto the Royal Palace balcony to greet the tens of thousands of people who have gathered outside.
