November kicks off with chilly temperatures, morning rain and snow showers

Morning shower chances continue to welcome in November. Light rain and snow showers are expected across the region, however, the snow is expected to stay in the Sierra and Tahoe Basin.

Reno has about a 20% chance to see some light rain showers during the morning hours, mainly before 8 AM. Clouds will decrease throughout the day, leaving room for a little bit of sunshine before the evening hours. South Lake Tahoe can expect light snow showers in the morning, with less than a half inch of snow accumulation.

Temperatures will stay below-average for the day. Around 8 AM, expect temps in the upper 30s, warming into the upper 40s by noon. Reno's forecast high Sunday is 54 degrees. Temperatures in areas out to the east of Reno will see similar temperatures in the low to mid 50s. Temperatures around the Tahoe Basin and in the Sierra are expected in the mid to upper 40s. South Lake Tahoe's forecast high is 45 degrees.
