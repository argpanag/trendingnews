TheTools · 13.10.2026 22:08 · source · permalink

Harry Kane, Bayern Munich's forward, expressed his disappointment after his team's 3-3 draw against Viking in the second round of the group stage of the UEFA Champions League, in the match held at the Lise Arena.

Harry Kane said after the match: "The first half was weak from our side. We started the second half well and fought to regain control."

The English striker spoke about the red card received by Michael Olise: "The red card changed everything for us. It will be very frustrating and he will have to take responsibility for what happened. It was a mistake, and mistakes happen, but he has to learn from it. It's unfortunate, especially since we have important matches coming up."

Harry Kane concluded his statements by asserting that Bayern Munich deserved to win considering their level in the second half, saying: "We should have won this match based on the performance we delivered in the second half, but when you make mistakes like that, you get punished."
