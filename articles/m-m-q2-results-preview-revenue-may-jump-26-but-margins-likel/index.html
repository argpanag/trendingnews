M&M Q2 Results Preview: Revenue may jump 26%, but margins likely to take a hit

TheTools · 21.10.2026 03:56 · source · permalink

M&M Q2FY27 Results Preview: Mahindra & Mahindra (M&M) is expected to report mixed results for the second quarter of the financial year 2026-27 (Q2FY27), according to Zee Business estimates. Revenue is expected to rise, but margins may come under pressure.

The company's financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, will be considered by its board next month. In a regulatory filing, the automaker said that "the meeting of the Board of Directors of the Company will be held on Thursday, 5th November 2026 to consider and approve the Unaudited Standalone and Consolidated Financial Results of the Company for the Second Quarter and Half Year ending 30th September 2026."

M&M's standalone revenue is estimated to rise 26 per cent year-on-year (YoY) to Rs 42,115 crore, compared with Rs 33,421 crore in the same quarter last year.

EBITDA is expected to increase 2.9 per cent to Rs 5,000 crore from Rs 4,861 crore a year ago. However, the EBITDA margin is estimated to fall to 11.9 per cent from 14.5 per cent.

Profit after tax (PAT) is estimated to rise 2.3 per cent to Rs 4,625 crore, compared with Rs 4,521 crore in the year-ago quarter. These estimates exclude profit or loss from investments related to subsidiaries, analysts said.

The company's total volumes are estimated to rise 10.6 per cent to around 3.86 lakh units. Tractor volumes may decline due to a high base in the year-ago quarter.

Realisations are expected to increase 13.7 per cent to around Rs 10.86 lakh. However, higher steel and rubber costs may put pressure on tractor margins, as per Zee Business estimates.

Auto margins may also decline to around 8 per cent from 9.2 per cent. Investors will watch the demand outlook for new products and the company's upcoming launches, it added.

M&M reported total vehicle sales of 1,14,874 units in September 2026, including exports. This was a 15 per cent increase year-on-year. Domestic SUV sales stood at 64,092 units, up 14 per cent. Domestic commercial vehicle sales rose 14 per cent to 30,420 units. The company also said it had crossed one lakh cumulative billings of its Electric Origin SUVs since launch.

In the first quarter of FY27, M&M reported consolidated revenue of Rs 58,188 crore, up 28 per cent year-on-year. Consolidated PAT increased 34 per cent to Rs 5,455 crore from Rs 4,083 crore.

The auto business reported consolidated revenue of Rs 34,387 crore, up 32 per cent. Its consolidated PAT rose 21 per cent to Rs 2,129 crore.

The farm business reported consolidated revenue of Rs 12,501 crore, up 15 per cent, while consolidated PAT increased 15 per cent to Rs 1,520 crore.

The company's services businesses also reported growth. Consolidated services revenue rose 31 per cent to Rs 12,899 crore, while consolidated services PAT increased 80 per cent to Rs 1,805 crore, representing M&M's share of PAT.

M&M shares closed at Rs 2,907 on the NSE on October 19, 2026. The stock has gained 1.95 per cent over the past five trading days but has fallen 4.85 per cent over the past month.

The stock has declined 9.77 per cent over the past six months and 22.71 per cent since the beginning of the year. It has also fallen 19.23 per cent over the past year.

M&M shares have traded between a 52-week high of Rs 3,839.90 and a 52-week low of Rs 2,744.30.
