Alaska U.S. Senate Election 2026 Live Results: Mary Peltola, Dan Sullivan and More

TheTools · 04.11.2026 17:07 · source · permalink

Alaska’s “top four” primary process means there are multiple top candidates, including GOP Sen. Dan Sullivan, who is running for a third term. Former Democratic Rep. Mary Peltola, who lost re-election in 2024 by 3 points, is his Democratic opponent. Two other Republicans are running, one is Dan J. Sullivan who has the same name as the current senator and who’s denied accusation of being a Democratic plant, and the other is Gerald Keikes.
