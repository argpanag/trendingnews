3 military officers injured in unidentified explosion during operation at DMZ: JCS | Yonhap News Agency

TheTools · 21.09.2026 04:51 · source · permalink

SEOUL, Sept. 21 (Yonhap) -- Three military officers were injured Monday in an unidentified explosion during a search operation at the Demilitarized Zone (DMZ) separating the two Koreas, the Joint Chiefs of Staff (JCS) said Monday.

The explosion occurred south of the Military Demarcation Line (MDL) in the western section of the DMZ earlier in the day, according to the JCS.

The military did not specify, but the accident is suspected to be a land mine explosion.

Two of the three injured officers were transported to a hospital via an emergency helicopter, the JCS said.
