Golden Joystick Awards 2026: All the Winners From Today’s Show

TheTools · 11.11.2026 18:18 · source · permalink

The first major award show of the year is an early indicator for the GOTY race, but GTA 6's imminent release casts a big shadow

This year's gaming awards season kicks off in earnest today with the 44th annual Golden Joystick Awards. It's an especially interesting event given just how up in the air 2026's Game of the Year race has been. Unlike 2025, which had a clear frontrunner in Clair Obscur: Expedition 33, this year's slate of games has been full of quality titles, but nothing has singularly captured the public's interest in a way that assures it will take home awards. The Golden Joysticks could be an early indicator of what games will rise to awards recognition, especially at Geoff Keighley’s The Game Awards in December.

There are two elephants in the room, however, with this year's Golden Joystick Awards. The first is Grand Theft Auto 6. Rockstar Games' much-anticipated blockbuster won't be released until Nov. 19, meaning it isn't in competition at this particular awards show. GTA 6 will undoubtedly upend the awards landscape, so later shows could look completely different than what we see today.

There is also the Big Walk issue. Developer House House revealed this week that it used AI, specifically large language models (LLMs), in the final phase of development. While the studio says that under 0.1% of the game's code had anything to do with generative AI tools, this will likely be a hardline for many that puts the critical darling out of contention. As The Golden Joystick Awards closed voting on Nov. 5, voters weren't aware of this controversy, but Big Walk winning awards may put a bad taste in some people's mouths that may be reflected in fewer accolades later this year.
