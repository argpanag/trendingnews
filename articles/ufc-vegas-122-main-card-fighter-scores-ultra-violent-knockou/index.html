UFC Vegas 122 Main Card Fighter Scores Ultra-Violent "Knockout of the Year" Contender

TheTools · 11.10.2026 01:18 · source · permalink

A longtime UFC veteran produced arguably the most violent highlight of his career during the main card for UFC Vegas 122.

Taking place at the Meta APEX in Las Vegas, NV following a lengthy road trip for the UFC, UFC Vegas 122 featured 11 fights leading up to a huge middleweight main event between Brendan Allen and Christian Leroy Duncan.

All but one of the card’s seven prelim fights ended inside the distance, and that trend of violence continued with the start of the UFC Vegas 122 main card.

Lackluster UFC Vegas 122 Card Produces Knockout-Heavy Night

After Ernesta Kareckaite and Melissa Gatto fought to a draw in the night’s opening bout, fans were treated to one submission and four knockouts during the remainder of the preliminary card.

Things moved to the featherweight division next, where longtime UFC veteran Andre Fili entered the cage looking to snap a two-fight skid when he took on Kai Kamaka III.

Andre Fili Sleeps Kai Kamaka III for Brutal "Knockout of the Year" Contender

A member of the UFC roster since 2013, Fili had alternated wins and losses through eight fights dating back to 2022 before being knocked out by Vinicius Oliveira in a “Fight of the Night” winning scrap in June.

That result came after Fili also dropped a split decision to Jose Delgado in March, and despite
that two-fight skid “Touchy” was lined as a small favorite heading into UFC Vegas 122.

The highlight-reel finish was arguably the most violent win of Fili’s career, and it also marked his first victory by knockout since he stopped Lucas Almeida in the opening round in late 2023.

Kamaka rejoined the UFC earlier this year and defeated Dakota Hope via split decision, but “Da Fighting Hawaiian” now finds himself on a two-fight skid after he was also knocked out by Luke Riley at UFC 329.
