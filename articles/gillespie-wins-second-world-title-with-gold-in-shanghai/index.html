Gillespie wins second world title with gold in Shanghai

TheTools · 18.10.2026 08:40 · source · permalink

Getty

Ireland's Lara Gillespie is world champion for the second time after taking gold in a dominant performance in the points race at the UCI World Track Championships in China.

The Wicklow woman, who won gold in the elimination race in Chile last year, entered the closing stages of the event in a three-way battle for top spot with Denmark's Amalie Dideriksen and Belgium's Shari Bossuyt.

Sunday's contest consisted of a 100 laps, with a sprint every 10 laps. Gillespie showed her credentials early by winning the second sprint, and then taking another victory on the fifth sprint.

Gillespie claimed strong second place finishes in the seventh and ninth sprints, the latter edging her ahead of her closest competitor Dideriksen.

The 10th and final sprints saw double points awarded and the Dane looked to have the momentum early. However, Gillespie blew past her on the final bend to take another sprint second place.

Gillespie claimed the title on 42 points, five clear of Dideriksen, with Bossuyt taking bronze.

The new world champion said afterwards that the points race victory was all the more satisfying after her disappointment at finishing second in Wednesday's scratch race.

"It's all sinking in now," Gillespie told Cycling Ireland. "I'm just so happy I could pull this off.

"I came really close the other today and I think that really just pushed me on there today because I was really annoyed with that second place.

"Overall a really good week and I really just can't thank Cycling Ireland and Sport Ireland enough for all the support over the last few years and all the people back home who are getting those key sessions in with me, helping me get fast for the track, so I really just want to say thank you to everyone."
