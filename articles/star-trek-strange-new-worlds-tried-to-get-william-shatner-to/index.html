‘Star Trek: Strange New Worlds’ Tried To Get William Shatner To Return As Kirk For TOS Episode Sequel [EXCLUSIVE]

TheTools · 27.09.2026 13:41 · source · permalink

| September 23, 2026 | By: Anthony Pascale 78 comments so far

William Shatner’s last official onscreen appearance in his iconic role of James T. Kirk was back in 1994 in the feature film Star Trek: Generations. But for the past three decades, there has always been talk about some kind of return, often from the man himself. There have been a few tries to get Shatner back as Kirk officially, and TrekMovie has the inside scoop on how Star Trek: Strange New Worlds tried to make it happen directly from executive producer Henry Alonso Myers.

Tried for Shatner every season

Early last year, William Shatner hinted at a return to Star Trek without specifically mentioning if it was for a film or movie, but said he was “impressed” with a writer who had reached out and said he was “awaiting a pitch.” Henry Alonso Myers confirmed to TrekMovie that pitch was coming from the Strange New Worlds team:

“Yeah, we were trying to [get Shatner]. We were genuinely having a long discussion with him about it…”

“Yeah, we were trying to [get Shatner]. We were genuinely having a long discussion with him about it…”

In fact, the executive producer revealed getting Shatner onto Strange New Worlds has been a long-term goal for him and his co-showrunner Akiva Goldsman:

“I have tried to hold up with Akiva — it’s been his great gift to try to get Shatner on the show, and we have tried every season. We came very close that season. We had a wonderful discussion with him. We were trying to make something that was something he could be a part of, that’s something we could actually show on screen. That something could show some aspect what it was like in the past, and what does it look like in the future. We had a really interesting plan for it.”

William Shatner as Kirk in Star Trek: Generations

Follow-up to a classic TOS episode… with a TAS twist

The executive producer revealed what they were hoping to do with Shatner on Strange New Worlds, and it was another big swing:

“What we were trying to do was an animated episode with him. We wanted to do like a very serious one. We had a whole plan to do it around him and to redo ‘City on the Edge of Forever’ and kind of do a retelling — not a retelling, like a part two kind of thing.”

“What we were trying to do was an animated episode with him. We wanted to do like a very serious one. We had a whole plan to do it around him and to redo ‘City on the Edge of Forever’ and kind of do a retelling — not a retelling, like a part two kind of thing.”

“The City on the Edge of Forever” is often cited as one of the best (if not the best) episode of The Original Series, or even the franchise. The Hugo award-winning episode written by sci-fi legend Harlan Ellison (and famously re-written by Gene Roddenberry and other Star Trek writers, much to Ellison’s chagrin) featured Kirk and Spock traveling back to Great Depression-era USA. Kirk fell in love with Edith Keeler (Joan Collins) but had to heartbreakingly allow her to die in order to restore the proper timeline.

Joan Collins as Edith Keeler and William Shatner as Kirk in TOS’ “The City on the Edge of Forever”

Akiva Goldsman has often cited this classic episode to contrast how Strange New Worlds is different from the original Star Trek, saying that on SNW, Kirk wouldn’t just move on and forget a week after such an event. It appears that Goldsman wanted to take this idea to the next step and follow up with Shatner’s Kirk after the events of that classic episode, albeit using TAS-style animation, which would allow Shatner to play his younger self.

Tomorrow, Star Trek: Strange New Worlds wraps up its fourth season with the time travel episode “Tomorrow’s Enterprise,” which will feature Paul Wesley as Captain James T. Kirk. TrekMovie first reported there was a different season 4 finale planned to be directed by Goldsman before they pivoted to “Tomorrow’s Enterprise,” but Henry Also Meyers confirmed the pitch to Shatner was not the original idea for the that, saying “those are two separate things.” He previously confirmed that while they had tried to do a crossover episode with the BBC’s Doctor Who, that also wasn’t the original season 4 finale.

William Shatner as Kirk in TAS’ “Yesteryear”

Why didn’t it work out?

The last Trek TV show that we know tried to get Shatner was Star Trek: Enterprise over two decades ago. There were serious talks, but in the end, UPN decided they didn’t want to spend what Shatner wanted. However, it might not have been money this time. Henry Alonso Myers talked about how things just didn’t work out for Shatner on SNW:

“We wanted to try to get him involved. And we had a long discussion with him about it. And sometimes these things work out, sometimes they don’t. We wanted to find something that would have pulled him in and made him interested. Sometimes it’s hard to find something on the same page as the people you want to work with.”

“We wanted to try to get him involved. And we had a long discussion with him about it. And sometimes these things work out, sometimes they don’t. We wanted to find something that would have pulled him in and made him interested. Sometimes it’s hard to find something on the same page as the people you want to work with.”

We haven’t heard yet from Shatner on this one, but back in early 2025 he did lay out what it would take for him to return to Trek:

“I have been asked to come back as Kirk so many times. If Kirk is going to come back, it has to mean something. It has to be the show.”

“I have been asked to come back as Kirk so many times. If Kirk is going to come back, it has to mean something. It has to be the show.”

William Shatner at STLV 2026 (Photo: TrekMovie/Jon Spencer)

There was also an attempt to bring Shatner back for the 2009 Star Trek movie and TrekMovie first revealed the scene that was written for him. However, director J.J. Abrams decided not to reach out to Shatner as he felt the legendary actor would see the role as just a cameo. There was also talk that Shatner could show up in the Star Trek movie that was more recently developed by Quentin Tarantino, but that project was shelved. It may just be that Shatner’s iconic performances as Kirk in three seasons of Star Trek and seven feature films (along with some video games) will have to suffice.
