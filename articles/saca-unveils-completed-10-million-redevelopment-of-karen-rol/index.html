SACA unveils completed $10 million redevelopment of Karen Rolton Oval | South Australian Cricket Association

TheTools · 16.11.2026 09:13 · source · permalink

The South Australian Cricket Association (SACA) today unveiled the completed $10 million redevelopment of Karen Rolton Oval ahead of a blockbuster Adelaide Strikers clash against the Sydney Sixers.

Jointly funded by the South Australian Government and SACA, the transformation further cements Karen Rolton Oval's position as a world-class sporting and community precinct, delivering significant upgrades to lighting, spectator amenities and accessibility.

The completed redevelopment includes the installation of new 1500-lux lighting, meeting the latest Australian standards for domestic cricket and enhancing the venue's capability to host elite matches under lights. The venue's former 750-lux lighting system has also been relocated to Oval 2, creating an additional fully lit venue to support cricket and broader community sport.

New accessible spectator terracing has been established along the eastern embankment, transforming a previously steep and inaccessible area into a welcoming space for match-day patrons and community members.

Complemented by landscaping that aligns with the established aesthetic of Park 25, the redevelopment improves both accessibility and the overall functionality of the precinct.

As a designated Tier 2 venue, Karen Rolton Oval is equipped to host major events, including international tournaments when Adelaide Oval is unavailable.

The upgraded facilities further strengthen South Australia's sporting infrastructure and reinforce the venue's reputation as one of the state's premier cricket destinations.

The redevelopment was officially unveiled today by Minister for Sport, Recreation and Racing Rhiannon Pearce, SACA Chairman and President Will Rayner and Adelaide Strikers and Australian fast bowler Darcie Brown.

Minister for Sport, Recreation and Racing Rhiannon Pearce said the investment would strengthen one of South Australia's premier cricket venues while supporting the continued growth of women's and girls' cricket.

"Karen Rolton Oval is such a great venue, centrally located in the western Parklands, and these works make it even more accessible for fans.

"The new terracing and lighting create a better experience for spectators and have come just in time for what will be a great summer of women's cricket here in Adelaide.

"With women's cricket continuing to grow at an extraordinary pace, it's fundamental we have the appropriate facilities to cater for this increase in participation.

"Karen Rolton Oval is synonymous with women's and girls' cricket in South Australia, and the State Government is pleased to partner with SACA to deliver this project."

SACA Chairman and President Will Rayner said the redevelopment reflected a shared commitment to providing world-class facilities and growing the game at every level.

"This investment in Karen Rolton Oval is a major step forward for women's cricket in South Australia, not only for the professional game, but for community and junior cricket.

"The upgraded lights will enhance night matches and lift the experience for players, fans and broadcasters alike, while ensuring this venue serves the next generation of cricketers.

"It reflects a strong shared commitment with the State Government to providing world-class facilities and growing the game at every level."
