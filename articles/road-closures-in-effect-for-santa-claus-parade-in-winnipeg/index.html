Road closures in effect for Santa Claus Parade in Winnipeg

TheTools · 14.11.2026 17:28 · source · permalink

By News Staff

Posted November 14, 2026 12:28 pm.

Several streets in Winnipeg will see traffic closures for the Santa Claus Parade on Saturday.

The parade is set to begin at 5 p.m. and will start westbound on Portage Avenue at Main Street, travel down Portage Avenue to Memorial Boulevard, then take Memorial Boulevard to Broadway where it ends.

Full road closures will be in effect from 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. on the following routes:

  • Memorial Boulevard in both directions between Broadway and Portage Avenue
  • Portage Avenue in both directions between Main Street and St. Mary Avenue
  • Southbound Main Street from William Avenue to York Avenue
  • Edmonton Street (northbound one-way) from Graham Avenue to Portage Avenue
  • Vaughan Street between Graham Avenue and Portage Avenue
  • Portage Avenue East from Main Street to Westbrook Street

Drivers and parade-goers may also experience other traffic impacts including local and parkade-access only for all other north-south streets from Main Street to Memorial Boulevard between Ellice Avenue and Broadway. Vehicles will not be able to cross Portage Avenue on any of these streets.

Drivers also won’t be able to cross Memorial Boulevard on eastbound York Street or westbound St. Mary Avenue but can use the road on either side of Memorial.

Broadway and northbound Main Street will stay open throughout the day but are expected to be busy. Authorities warn that many turn and travel restrictions will be in place on Ellice Avenue, York Avenue, Broadway, and Portage Avenue east of Main Street.

Transit will be rerouted during the closures. Details and updates can be found on @TransitAlerts on X.

Drivers are advised to allow additional travel time, use alternate routes to get to their destinations while the closures are in effect.

They can find real-time traffic updates on the Waze app or see the lane closures on the city’s interactive map.
