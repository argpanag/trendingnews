Broadcast Sport Awards 2026: All the winners revealed

TheTools · 07.11.2026 02:47 · source · permalink

The great and good of sports broadcasting came together at the Broadcast Sport Awards 2026 last night. Here is the full list of winners.

The Broadcast Sport Awards 2026 winners were revealed last night (5 November), at a celebratory gala Awards dinner at London Hilton on Park Lane, hosted by comedian Tom Allen.

The Awards welcomed a long list of sports stars and presenters including Wayne Rooney, Alex Brooker, Alex Scott, Gabby Logan, Michael McIntyre, Dame Sarah Storey, Will Buxton, Dermon O’Leary, Hazel Irvine, Sally Nugent, Sam Matterface, Iwan Thomas, Alex Aljoe, Orla Chennaoui and Karel Prince.

The night was a huge celebration of sports production, sports broadcast and sports content - many thanks to the Awards sponsors, Appear, Gravity Media, NEP, Net Insight, Premier League Studios, Reuters Imagen, and Sunset+Vine.
