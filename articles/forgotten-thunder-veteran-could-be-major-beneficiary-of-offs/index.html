Forgotten Thunder veteran could be major beneficiary of offseason shakeups

TheTools · 02.10.2026 11:00 · source · permalink

Cason Wallace and Ajay Mitchell are generally the players recognized as the major beneficiaries of the shakeups executed by the OKC Thunder this offseason. Of course, lost in this hype is the fact that Kenrich Williams could also end up gaining from the club's summer moves.

Yes, both Wallace and Mitchell are seamless, one-for-one positional replacements for now-departed guys like Lu Dort, Isaiah Joe, and Aaron Wiggins.

The belief is that Caso will succeed Dort as the starting two guard in Oklahoma City's rotation, while Ajay should now see his on-court production skyrocket with the opportunities created by the dismissal of the latter two talents.

However, Kenny Hustle is someone who could also very well be leaned on more often as the Thunder attempt to make up for these player absences.

Not only will he now be looked to as a seasoned veteran in OKC without Dort in tow, as he's one of the longest-tenured players on the roster who has gone through the highest of highs and lowest of lows with the club, but his career 36.8 percent long-range shooting clip is something that could absolutely fill in the perimeter scoring void created with the loss of Wiggins and I-Joe.

As we have heard time and time again from headman Mark Daigneault throughout training camp so far, this coaching staff absolutely adores Kenny Hustle.

With the offseason departures, coupled with the uncertainty of the team's young newcomers, the Thunder could very realistically look to Williams on a more consistent basis in 2026-27.

Kenrich Williams excited about particular Thunder rookie

Though it's impossible to tell what the future holds for incoming rookie players before they so much as log a single minute of NBA action, Kenrich Williams seems to foresee a bright career trajectory for one of Oklahoma City's high-profile youngsters.

During a practice-day media session on Thursday, Williams was seen gushing over lottery pick Aday Mara, shooting his shot on a prediction for his future by saying he sees All-Defensive potential and that, in his eyes, "Sky's the limit" when it comes to his upside.

The forward's remarks are a breath of fresh air when it comes to assessments of the Michigan product, as his play in NBA Summer League has led to serious criticisms and worrisome predictions about his upcoming rookie campaign.

Fortunately, it seems his surge in play with Team Spain in this summer's FIBA World Cup Qualifiers has carried over into Thunder training camp.

Clearly, he's done enough to warrant serious praise from a fellow frontcourt running mate in Williams.
