Portugal redesign No. 7 jersey to honor Cristiano Ronaldo's legacy

TheTools · 14.11.2026 22:49 · source · permalink

Reporting the Changing World

Anadolu Ajansı © 2026

The Portuguese Football Federation (FPF) announced Saturday that it has redesigned the national team's iconic No. 7 jersey to honor Cristiano Ronaldo following his retirement from international football.

The new design, featuring a horizontal stroke through the number 7, will make its debut in Portugal's UEFA Nations League match against Denmark on Saturday in Braga, the team's first game since Ronaldo's retirement.

"From today onwards, we will have a different number 7 on the jersey, but with the same essence and the same pride, reserving the previous one for the immortalization of a story that will never be forgotten," the FPF said in a statement.

The federation said the change would preserve the original design associated with Ronaldo's international career while allowing future players to continue wearing the number.

FIFA and UEFA squad-numbering regulations prevent national teams from permanently retiring shirt numbers, prompting Portugal to redesign the No. 7 instead.

Club Brugge winger Carlos Forbs is expected to become the first player to wear the redesigned No. 7 jersey against Denmark.

Ronaldo, 41, announced his international retirement on Nov. 5 after a 23-year career, finishing as the most-capped player and leading goalscorer in men's international football with 234 appearances and 146 goals.

The five-time Ballon d'Or winner helped Portugal claim their first major international trophy at Euro 2016 and two UEFA Nations League titles in 2019 and 2025.
