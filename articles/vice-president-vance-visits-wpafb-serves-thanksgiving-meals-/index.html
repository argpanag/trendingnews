Vice President Vance visits WPAFB, serves Thanksgiving meals to service members

TheTools · 25.11.2026 23:45 · source · permalink

WRIGHT-PATTERSON AIR FORCE BASE — Vice President JD Vance spent the afternoon at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, serving Thanksgiving meals to service members.

Vance was joined by Second Lady Usha Vance and their four children.

The vice president and his family joined the service members for lunch as well on Wednesday.

He told reporters that he wanted to come back to his home state to give thanks to the people who serve our country.

“I just wanted to come, and if I’m being totally honest with you, I think it’s good for the kids to learn a little bit about service and a little about the people who serve our country,” Vance said. “And so it’s always a joy for us as parents to be able to bring them here and make them serve some food and actually sit down and talk with the people that keep us free and keep us safe.”

As shown on News Center 7 at 6:00, the vice president made Thanksgiving lunch plates for the first 50 service members in line, making sure to ask almost every person where they were from.

“And I just love that incredible geographic diversity that is very much part of our nation’s armed forces,” he said. “So these are great people.”

While he was there, News Center 7’s Trinity Corbett asked the vice president when families would start to see prices come down. CBS’ Consumer Price Index says that just in the last month, prices were up .5 percent.

“I think American families have every right to be impatient. They want to see costs come down. They want to see better jobs for the American people,” Vance said. “And we just have to work as hard as we can every single day to make life more affordable.”

Despite the amount of work this current administration has to do to turn the economy around, Vice President Vance said they have done some of the work already.

“There’s a lot of investment coming into our country; there’s a lot of new factories opening up. So there’s a lot that we’re proud of, but there’s a lot of work left to do,” he said.

The vice president also spoke on Sherrod Brown being the newest Senator-elect for Ohio. He said, despite their many political disagreements, if Brown succeeds, then the state of Ohio succeeds.
