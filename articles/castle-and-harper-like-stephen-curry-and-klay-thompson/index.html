Castle and Harper like Stephen Curry and Klay Thompson?

TheTools · 09.09.2026 00:02 · source · permalink

After returning to the NBA Finals, the Spurs are looking to the future with Castle and Harper. Barkley even compares them to Stephen Curry and Klay Thompson

The Spurs are coming off their first NBA Finals appearance since 2014 and are looking to the future with plenty of confidence, thanks in part to Stephon Castle and Dylan Harper.

During The Bill Simmons Podcast, Charles Barkley praised the young duo, going as far as comparing them to one of the best backcourts in NBA history: Stephen Curry and Klay Thompson of the Golden State Warriors.

Those two guards are guys I would not want to face over the next 10 years if I were an NBA guard. They’re going to be the new Steph and Klay

Charles Barkley

An especially ambitious comparison, considering that Curry and Thompson won four NBA championships together and reached the Finals five times. The numbers are still a long way from what the Splash Brothers accomplished during their prime, but according to Barkley, Castle and Harper have everything it takes to become one of the NBA’s strongest guard duos over the next decade.
