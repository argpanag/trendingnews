Thunder vs. Kings: Live updates, highlights for Sunday's game

TheTools · 16.11.2026 01:45 · source · permalink

The Oklahoma City Thunder travel on the road to face the Sacramento Kings on Sunday. This will be the first matchup between the two squads of the 2026-27 regular season.

The Thunder (8-5) are amid an ultra-rare three-game losing streak — their first since April 2024. They suffered a heartbreaker in a 113-112 loss to the LA Clippers on Friday. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander had an efficient 40 points, but failed to hit the game-winner twice.

Meanwhile, the Kings (1-11) have lost five in a row. They're easily the worst team in the league. They had a 120-107 loss to the Portland Trail Blazers on Friday. De'Andre Hunter had 24 points and five rebounds. Darius Acuff Jr. dropped an inefficient 17 points.
