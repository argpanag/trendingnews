Real Madrid Suffers First Loss Under José Mourinho

TheTools · 04.09.2026 22:56 · source · permalink

Spain

In Seville, José Mourinho watched as Troy Parrott—whom he had once helped make his professional debut with Tottenham—scored a late goal against his team

Keystone

Real Madrid suffers its first loss since coach José Mourinho's return.

In the fourth round of the Spanish league, the Madrid team lost the clash between the two Champions League contenders 0-1 at Betis Sevilla.

The only goal was scored by Irishman Troy Parrott, who found the back of the net for his new club for the first time since his transfer from Alkmaar. In the 81st minute, the forward scored off a corner kick.

Real Madrid, which had started the season with three wins, failed to tie the game in the closing minutes, in part because Kylian Mbappé missed a penalty kick.
