Suki Waterhouse has seemingly reflected on her past relationship with Bradley Cooper while discussing her dating history in a recent interview. Although she did not mention the actor by name, her remark about a two-year relationship ending in a "disaster" has drawn attention to their romance, which lasted from 2013 to 2015.

The singer also reflected on how her experiences with public relationships have shaped her outlook on love and privacy.

Suki Waterhouse's two-year "disaster" remark may hint at Bradley Cooper

Speaking to The Sunday Times, Waterhouse admitted she is surprised by how happy she is with her current partner, Robert Pattinson. She shared that she had never dated anyone for more than two years before him.

"If it was two years, it would have been a disaster at the end," she remarked. Waterhouse did not identify the relationship she was referring to. However, the timeline has prompted speculation that she was discussing her romance with Cooper. The former couple also attracted attention because of their 17-year age difference. Waterhouse was 21 and Cooper was 38 when they began dating.

Reflecting on her younger years, Waterhouse admitted, "When you're younger you go into everything so blindly and you don't think of consequences, including maybe of relationships." She also recalled feeling isolated after a painful breakup and questioning her "stifling" modeling career. "I started feeling like, 'I have to find a way out of here or I'm going to drown,'" she said. Moreover, she added that she channeled feeling "quite depressed" into songwriting.

Waterhouse previously discussed the impact of her highly publicized breakup in a 2024 British Vogue interview. "When something very public happens to you and the story behind it is dark and difficult, and you're actually not doing well, and you can't explain yourself to the world, that's very isolating," she said.

She has been with Pattinson since 2018, and the couple shares a two-year-old daughter whose name remains private. Calling their relationship "beautiful," Waterhouse also revealed that their wedding plans are on hold for this year. Dismissing speculation that they have secretly married, she said they would prefer a big celebration. "If we were going to go for it, we'd have a massive party, blow it out," she said.

Meanwhile, Cooper has been dating Gigi Hadid since 2023. He also shares a nine-year-old daughter, Lea De Seine, with his former partner Irina Shayk.
