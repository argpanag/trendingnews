Los Angeles FC of the U.S. Major League Soccer (MLS) swallowed its anger in the away game.

LAFC lost 1-3 away to Sporting Kansas City at Sporting Park on the 13th (Korea Standard Time). The defeat pushed them to fifth place in the Western Conference with 11 wins, 7 draws and 8 losses and 40 points.

If LAFC stays in its current position, it will play the first round of the MLS Cup playoffs on the road.

Son Heung-min, who started as a forward-thrower, played full-time in the 90th minute and recorded an expected score (xG) of 0.22. There was one shot on target, but he couldn't open the goal.

He attempted a left-footed shot from the left side in the 14th minute of the first half, but his shot was blocked. In the meantime, the team was dragged 0-2 by Andre Ruiz in the 18th minute and Taylor Calheira in the 31st minute.

Son Heung-min, who withdrew without any income in the first half, contributed to his team's comeback goal in the second minute of the second half. As the defense was focused on him from the center, he opened the pass to Jacob Schaeffelberg on the left. Schaeffelberg connected on a ground ball cross after breaking through the side, and Denny Buanga completed it with a score.

LAFC, however, has not continued its mood since then. Son Heung-min also failed to make a meaningful scene. Rather, the gap widened in the 26th minute of the second half by allowing Jacob Bartlett to score his third goal.

[San Francisco (USA) = KIM JAE HO, MK Sports Correspondent]
