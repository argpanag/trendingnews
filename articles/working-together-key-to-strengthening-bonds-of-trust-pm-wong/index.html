Working together key to strengthening bonds of trust: PM Wong

TheTools · 12.09.2026 16:56 · source · permalink

Prime Minister Lawrence Wong says the work to keep Singapore cohesive, strong and united is becoming even more important, as societies around the world are becoming more fragmented, divided and polarised. While Singapore generally has a harmonious and stable society, he says it is not immune to these challenges.
