Flooding delays Thai Asian Games teams at airport by 8 hours

TheTools · 27.09.2026 03:19 · source · permalink
  • News

BANGKOK — 27 September 2026, Flooding in Thailand disrupted the travel plans of members of the Thai national team heading to the 2026 Asian Games in Nagoya, Japan, with athletes from wrestling, golf and rugby stranded at the airport for several hours after baggage and sports equipment could not be loaded onto their flight.

The athletes and team officials were scheduled to travel on Thai Airways flight TG644, which was due to depart at 00:05 on 27 September 2026.

However, the flight was delayed after baggage-handling staff were unable to report for work due to the flooding. As a result, the athletes’ luggage and training equipment could not be loaded onto the aircraft.

Had the flight departed as scheduled, the teams would have had to travel without their baggage and sporting equipment.

Thai national rugby coach Navy Capt. Thanyawit Kruasint said the disruption affected both the men’s and women’s rugby teams, as well as athletes from other sports scheduled to travel on the same flight.

The airline initially informed passengers that the flight would be delayed indefinitely.

At 06:40 on 27 September, the athletes and other passengers were still waiting at the airport gate, more than six hours after the scheduled departure time.

Thanyawit said he understood the situation and hoped the flooding would ease quickly so baggage-handling staff could return to work.
