First man charged with Kpod trafficking gets nearly 4 years' jail

TheTools · 23.11.2026 04:36 · source · permalink

The first individual to be charged with trafficking e-vaporiser pods containing etomidate under the Misuse of Drugs Act was sentenced to nearly four years in jail on Monday (Nov 23).

Derek Khor Boon Chun, 41, was sentenced to three years, 10 months and two weeks' jail, after he pleaded guilty to five charges, including for consuming, possessing and trafficking Kpods.

He was also given two strokes of the cane and disqualified from driving for 20 years. 

This is not Khor's first brush with the law, having been previously convicted for meth consumption in 2016.

It is also his third conviction for driving while under disqualification.

The case 

Khor was arrested on Sept 6, after police responded to a call for assistance at Block 631, Ang Mo Kio Avenue 4. 

He was found in possession of an e-vaporiser and pod. Checks on his phone revealed his alleged involvement in vape trafficking activities.

A subsequent search of the vehicle by Health Sciences Authority (HSA) officers uncovered 43 vape pods containing etomidate and eight vape devices.

On Sept 8, Khor was charged by HSA. He was escorted back to the crime scene in Ang Mo Kio on Sept 12, and handed four additional charges on Sept 15.

Etomidate and its analogues were temporarily listed as Class C controlled drugs under the Misuse of Drugs Act from Sept 1, 2025 to April 30 this year.

Responding to a parliamentary question from MP for Sembawang GRC Vikram Nair on Sept 26, 2025, then-Senior Minister of State for Home Affairs Assoc Prof Faishal Ibrahim described the situation involving vapes laced with etomidate as "urgent".

Under the Tobacco and Vaporisers Control Act which came into effect on May 1 this year, etomidate is listed as a "specified psychoactive substance". 

Those found guilty of consuming, possessing or purchasing products containing such substance could be fined up to $20,000, jailed for up to 10 years, or both.

Suppliers will face up to 10 years' imprisonment and up to five strokes of the cane, while importers will face up to 20 years' jail and up to 15 strokes of the cane.
