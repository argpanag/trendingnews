CBOE Volatility Index Falls to One-Week Low of 16.97

Forecast Trend Report by Period

The Cboe Volatility Index, or VIX, a gauge of volatility in U.S. equities, fell to its lowest level in about a week.

Walter Bloomberg said on Nov. 4 that the VIX dropped 3.87 points from the previous session to 16.97.

That marked its lowest level in about a week.

The VIX measures expected market volatility over the next 30 days based on options prices tied to the S&P 500.
