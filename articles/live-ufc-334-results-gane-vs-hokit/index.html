LIVE! UFC 334 Results: Gane vs. Hokit

TheTools · 14.11.2026 05:00 · source · permalink
  • UFC

Ciryl Gane, Josh Hokit clash for UFC title in UFC 334 main event

MMA Fighting has UFC 334 results for the Gane vs. Hokit fight card, live blogs of the entire card, and more from Madison Square Garden in New York City on Saturday night.

In the main event, Ciryl Gane tries to defend his UFC heavyweight title and hand undefeated challenger Josh Hokit his first career loss. Gane has won three of his last four fights with one no-contest.

UFC bantamweight champion Kayla Harrison welcomes back former UFC two-division champion Amanda Nunes back to the Octagon in the co-main event. Nunes defeated Irene Aldana via unanimous decision in her last UFC appearance at UFC 289 on June 10, 2023.

Check out UFC 334 results below.
