Gjorgiev: Throughout the entire European Championship we had problems with reception, the Portuguese discovered that weak point - Free Press

TheTools · 16.09.2026 07:40 · source · permalink

The best Macedonian men's volleyball team did not achieve its goal, failed to secure a place in the round of 16 after losing to Portugal in the fourth match of the group stage of the European Championship in Sofia.

We had a bad start to the match and in the first set the opponent's seven blocks, as well as the problems we had in our court play, were crucial.

In the second half, we consolidated our play, we were more specific both at the net and in defense, but we were unable to "break" the stubborn play of the Portuguese. Technical errors were our main problem, 26 in total.

"Portugal played smarter than us. They didn't make that many mistakes. Throughout the tournament we had problems with reception, and they discovered that weak point," said Macedonian national team player Gjorgji Gjorgiev after the match with the Portuguese, reports cev.eu
