Rob Lowe Recalls Oscars Backlash Following 2022 Will Smith Slap

TheTools · 25.09.2026 19:20 · source · permalink

Actor Rob Lowe revealed he briefly felt relief following the 2022 Academy Awards, hoping Will Smith slapping Chris Rock would eclipse his own widely criticized opening performance from the 1989 ceremony.

Speaking on SiriusXM's Andy Cohen Live, Lowe revisited his infamous musical duet with Snow White. The performance of "Proud Mary" remains one of the most mocked moments in Oscars broadcast history.

"The worst opening ever," said Lowe. "When Will Smith slapped Chris Rock, I was the only person happy. Because I was like, 'Oh, good.

So now that's the worst moment in Oscar history.' But it turns out not really. Turns out I still am dragged, dragged every year."

The actor noted that critics continue to reference his 1989 appearance during modern award seasons whenever broadcasts face public backlash.

"This year even, I guess some people didn't love some of the Emmys and they were like, 'At least it wasn't Rob,'" told Lowe. "It's like, guys, there should be a statute of limitations."

Despite the enduring criticism, Lowe recalled receiving unexpected praise immediately following the 1989 performance from a Hollywood legend.

"Lucy came up to me afterwards," noted Lowe, "and said, 'Young man, I didn't know you were such a wonderful singer.'"

He also shared that Creedence Clearwater Revival songwriter John Fogerty held no grudge over the rendition of his track.

"John Fogerty was watching it at home," said Lowe. "And he liked it. He forgave me."

The 2022 incident occurred when Smith walked onto the Oscars stage and slapped host Rock following an unscripted joke regarding Jada Pinkett Smith's shaved head. Pinkett Smith has publicly detailed her ongoing experience with alopecia.

Other performers involved in the 1989 ceremony have previously expressed mixed feelings about the infamous broadcast. Actress Ricki Lake, who performed in a separate segment of the show, described the spectacle to Entertainment Weekly in 2018.

"The Rob Lowe thing with Snow White was so painful," said Lake, "like a car accident that you can't look away from."

Actor Keith Coogan, who also participated in the gala, noted that Lowe's segment drew most of the media attention away from other young actors.

"Thankfully people don't necessarily remember our nine minutes," said Coogan.

Lowe currently stars in the film feature The Musical, a project in which he does not perform any musical numbers.
