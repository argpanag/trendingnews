Channing Tatum responded to claims that he is behind a burner account on social media that has been hating on his ex-fiancée, Zoë Kravitz, and her new fiancé, Harry Styles.

After internet rumors circulated that he was the owner of an account called @bidness_nonya, Tatum posted to his Instagram Story: "Yo i truly can't believe yall think this is what I'm doing with my free time. Fuckin hilarious. Also why would I follow a burner account if I didn't want people to connect me to it? Wtf come on. But if I really need to say it, I only have one IG account @channingtatum."

Tatum and Kravitz began dating in 2021, and got engaged in 2023. The couple broke up in October 2024. Kravitz began dating Styles at some point after that and confirmed their relationship in August 2025. They got engaged in April.

The account in question, which seems to have been deleted in the past day, regularly commented on posts and rumors involving Kravitz and Styles, including a recent PDA post on Deuxmoi featuring the couple. Fans speculated that it was actually Tatum running the account, in part because he followed it. The speculation got so intense that Tatum himself had to clear the air.

Styles launched his Together, Together residency tour in May in support of his fourth studio album, Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally. The run kicked off with a stretch of stadiums in limited cities and recently made it to New York, where Styles is performing 30 nights at Madison Square Garden. He will extend the fun to six stadiums for his biggest North American shows yet, before heading to Europe for performances in Berlin, Paris, Madrid, Rome, Dublin, and London, where he closes out the tour with three shows at Wembley Stadium.

Kravitz's next film, How to Rob a Bank, will be released on Nov. 13. Tatum, meanwhile, stars in awards contender Josephine, which is in theaters on Nov. 20.
