Fairgoers turn out for The Big E despite nor’easter

TheTools · 27.09.2026 10:30 · source · permalink

WEST SPRINGFIELD, Mass. (WGGB/WSHM) - The nor’easter hanging over the skies of New England was more than enough to cancel a wide range of events across western Massachusetts. But it certainly wasn’t enough to stop hundreds of fairgoers from enjoying the sights, sounds, and smells at The Big E.

We were on the fairgrounds at the Eastern States Exposition on Saturday evening to see how people were holding up as the rain poured down and the wind blew. Fairgoers Angelina Bradfield and Christopher Rosero drove all the way from Long Island in New York as the weather was only a minor problem for both of them, “we had to make it happen regardless,” Angelina Bradfield said, “It’s a once a year type of thing. We just duck undercover when it rains and then we go right back out and enjoy the festivities.”

Other fairgoers told Western Mass News the same, come rain or shine, the big e holds a special place in their hearts that stormy weather wasn’t enough to stop them to see musicians like Deadmau5 perform at The Big E Arena, pick up some fried Oreos, or see what each state has to offer along the Avenue of States.
