(FOX40.COM) — The Sacramento County Coroner's Office is asking for the community's help in identifying a man who was found dead in the American River on Sunday.

The Sacramento Metropolitan Fire District responded to reports of a man in the river around 1:07 p.m. near Crondall and Estates drives in Arden-Arcade. Metro Fire said the man was recovered and appeared to be underwater "for a while" before being pronounced dead.

Officials with Sacramento County said the man has no signs of trauma and was wearing clothing consistent with active gear.

Responding crews did not find any identifying documents and fingerprint testing found no results.

(IMAGE: Sacramento County Coroner's Office) // The sketch is a combination of a human-generated sketch and real post-mortem photos of the decedent, collected by AI to create the most accurate version of the decedent.

According to officials, the man appears to be between 20 and 40 years old and is either Hispanic, Asian or possibly of mixed race.

He is around 5 feet 7 inches tall and weighs 175 pounds, with brown eyes and black, straight and long hair.

There are no identifying marks, scars or tattoos, but he did have a black mustache with a beard and goatee of a similar color, officials said.

Anyone with information on the identity of this person is asked to contact the Sacramento County Coroner's Office at 916-874-9320 or via email at DeputyCoroner@saccounty.gov.

The cause and manner of death are pending toxicology and other ancillary test results and rangers from the Sacramento County Parks Department are investigating.

Meanwhile, Metro Fire officials reminded the community to wear a life jacket around water and call 911 if someone is in danger.
