The U.S. Air Force is being asked to study whether America should restart production of the C-17 Globemaster III, more than a decade after Boeing shut down the production line. Around 220 C-17s remain in U.S. service, with the fleet potentially flying into the 2070s. But aging aircraft and disappearing suppliers are making parts increasingly difficult to source. Congress wants answers on the cost, tooling, workforce and timeline required to restart production, while the Air Force also considers extending the existing fleet or developing a clean-sheet replacement. The decision could shape America's strategic airlift capability for decades.
