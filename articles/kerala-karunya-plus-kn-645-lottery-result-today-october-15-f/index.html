The Kerala State Lotteries Department is scheduled to conduct the Karunya Plus KN-645 lottery draw on Thursday, October 15, 2026, with participants competing for a first prize of ₹1 crore. The draw is due to begin at 3 p.m., while the results are expected to be published by around 5 p.m. Ticket holders should check the official results once they are announced to verify whether their numbers have won a prize.

Karunya Plus is one of Kerala's weekly lotteries, conducted every Thursday. Each ticket costs ₹50 and offers a chance to win prizes across several categories, from the top award to smaller cash prizes. The second prize listed for the KN-645 draw is ₹30 lakh, while the third prize is ₹5 lakh. A consolation prize of ₹5,000 is also available for eligible tickets from other series that match the first-prize number.

The Karunya Plus lottery features multiple prize categories. The published prize structure for the KN-645 draw includes the following amounts and winning-number criteria.

The prize structure lists the first prize at ₹1 crore, the second at ₹30 lakh and the third at ₹5 lakh. Ticket holders should compare their complete ticket details with the official winning list rather than relying only on the final digits.

Once the results are released, ticket holders can check the winning numbers through the official Kerala State Lotteries Department channels and the relevant government gazette. The results should be verified against the published list before a prize claim is made.

The draw is scheduled for 3 p.m., and the results are expected around 5 p.m. The timing may vary, so participants should check official updates for confirmation. The winning numbers should not be treated as final until they have been checked against the department's published results.

Winners must follow the department's prescribed procedure to collect their prize money. According to the claim guidance provided in the source material, claims must be submitted within 30 days of the result announcement. Winners should confirm the applicable deadline and submission requirements with the Kerala State Lotteries Department before filing a claim.

For prizes of up to ₹1 lakh, winners can approach the relevant District Lottery Office. For prizes exceeding ₹1 lakh, the ticket should be submitted to the Director of State Lotteries, following the department's instructions. The winner should sign the reverse of the original ticket and write their name and address on it.

The documents listed for prize claims include a self-attested photocopy of both sides of the winning ticket, a copy of the winner's PAN card, valid government-issued identification and passport-sized photographs attested by a Gazetted Officer or notary. Claimants may also need the prescribed application and receipt, including a ₹1 revenue stamp. Additional requirements may apply to minors and joint claims.

The Kerala State Lotteries Department operates the state's lottery programme, which includes weekly, seasonal and bumper draws. Participants should use authorised ticket sellers and rely on official notices to verify winning numbers, prize amounts and claim procedures.
