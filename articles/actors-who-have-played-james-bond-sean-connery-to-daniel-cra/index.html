Actors Who Have Played James Bond: Sean Connery To Daniel Craig

TheTools · 28.10.2026 18:30 · source · permalink

Fictional British Secret Service agent James Bond was created in 1953 by writer Ian Fleming, who wrote 12 novels and two short-story collections about the character.

Other authors have written Bond novels since Fleming’s death in 1964, and 25 films have been made in the franchise, featuring six different actors portraying the stealthy sleuth, who is also known as Agent 007.

Sean Connery was the first actor to portray James Bond in the film Dr. No (1962), and since then, five more actors have taken on the mantle. Amazon MGM Studios will be making more films, and they have narrowed down the casting to screen tests, planning to soon announce the seventh actor who will appear as 007.

Scroll below to see the six actors who have portrayed James Bond and their corresponding film titles.

  • Sean Connery

    Sean Connery was the first actor to portray Agent 007 in Dr. No (1962), From Russia with Love (1963), Goldfinger (1964), Thunderball (1965), You Only Live Twice (1967), Diamonds Are Forever (1971) and Never Say Never Again (1983).

  • George Lazenby

    George Lazenby portrayed the agent in On Her Majesty’s Secret Service (1969).

  • Roger Moore

    Actor Roger Moore set a record for seven movies as Agent 007. They were Live and Let Die (1973), The Man with the Golden Gun (1974), The Spy Who Loved Me (1977), Moonraker (1979), For Your Eyes Only (1981), Octopussy (1983) and A View to a Kill (1985).

    Moore also portrayed detective Sherlock Holmes in the TV movie Sherlock Holmes in New York (1976).

  • Timothy Dalton

    Timothy Dalton portrayed Bond in two films: The Living Daylights (1987) and Licence to Kill (1989).

  • Pierce Brosnan

    Pierce Brosnan played the assassin in GoldenEye (1995) and in Tina Turner’s GoldenEye song music video, Tomorrow Never Dies (1997), The World Is Not Enough (1999) and Die Another Day (2002).

    He also portrayed Thomas Crown in the 1999 remake of The Thomas Crown Affair (1999).

  • Daniel Craig

    Daniel Craig donned the suit from 2006 to 2021 in Casino Royale (2006), Quantum of Solace (2008), Skyfall (2012), Spectre (2015) and in No Time To Die (2021) as his final outing in the undercover role.

    Craig will soon be seen as Uncle Andrew in Greta Gerwig’s Narnia: The Magician’s Nephew coming to theaters in February.

  • Josh O’Connor, Callum Turner and Jack Lowden

    Josh O’Connor, Callum Turner and Jack Lowden are in the running to portray Bond in the next film, which will be made by Amazon MGM Studios.
