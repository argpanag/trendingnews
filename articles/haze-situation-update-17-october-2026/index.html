Haze Situation Update (17 October 2026)

17 Oct 2026

Air Quality Forecast

PSI Forecast: 150 - 220 (Unhealthy to Very Unhealthy)

Health Advisory

Healthy persons :PSI Forecast-Unhealthy to Very Unhealthy. To plan for 18 Oct, avoid prolonged or strenuous outdoor physical exertion. For immediate outdoor activities, use 1h PM2.5. If Elevated PM2.5, reduce strenuous outdoor activity. If High PM2.5, avoid strenuous outdoor activity. 
Elderly, pregnant women & children :PSI Forecast-Unhealthy to Very Unhealthy. To plan for 18 Oct, minimise outdoor activity. For immediate outdoor activities, use 1h PM2.5. If Elevated PM2.5, avoid strenuous outdoor activity for the next hour. If High PM2.5, avoid all outdoor activity for next hour, may wear a mask to reduce exposure.
Persons with chronic lung/heart disease :PSI Forecast-Unhealthy to Very Unhealthy. To plan for 18 Oct, avoid outdoor activity. For immediate outdoor activities, use 1h PM2.5. If Elevated PM2.5, avoid strenuous outdoor activity for the next hour. If High PM2.5, avoid all outdoor activity for next hour, may wear a mask to reduce exposure.

Refer to the 1-hr PM2.5 for immediate activities, and the 24-hr PSI forecast to plan ahead.
