Walmart's $35 Thanksgiving feast for 10. How to save $10 more

TheTools · 15.11.2026 21:32 · source · permalink

Walmart's annual holiday meal basket is already a bargain at $35 to feed ten people. But shoppers can get an even better deal.

New customers of Walmart's pickup and delivery services can get $10 off their first three orders of $35 or more, the company told USA TODAY. Make the holiday meal basket one of your first three pickup or delivery orders, and the discount brings the cost of the feast for 10 down to $25.

Just enter the code "TRIPLE10" at checkout on each of your first three orders to get the $10 off. The TRIPLE10 code is good through the holiday season, Walmart said.

Customers who have placed one or two previous pickup or delivery orders – and are registered Walmart customers – can also get $10 off their scheduled pickup or delivery order of $35 or more (use the code DOUBLE10). That code can be used up to two times, if the customer is eligible at the time of each order.

What is in the Walmart holiday meal basket for Thanksgiving, Christmas?

The Walmart holiday meal deal comes with the ingredients needed to make a full dinner for Thanksgiving or Christmas. (Availability may vary by store; through Dec. 24 or while supplies last.)

Here's a look at the ingredients and what can be made:

  • Pumpkin pie with whipped topping.
  • Butterball turkey for 97 cents per pound.
  • Stove Top stuffing.
  • Cranberry sauce.
  • Mashed potatoes and gravy.
  • Green bean casserole made with Campbell's Cream of Mushroom Soup.
  • Dinner rolls.
  • Sweet potato casserole with Jet Puffed mini marshmallows.
  • Corn casserole made with "JIFFY" corn muffin mix.

Your order must include at least one of the following: Butterball Turkey, Campbell's Mushroom Soup, and/or Stove Top Stuffing mix.

How to get the Walmart Thanksgiving meal deal

You can order the holiday meal basket through Dec. 24 in Walmart stores or online at Walmart.com/HolidaySavingsBasket.

Walmart said it is also offering free express delivery on the meal basket through Nov. 30 (use promo code THANKFUL at checkout for free express delivery).

Back on May 28, Walmart said it was expanding its 30-minute-or-less delivery service to 33 cities from the original seven. You can find recipes on Walmart's website at Walmart.com/holidayfood.

How to get an additional $10 off on Walmart's holiday meal basket

If you haven't used Walmart's pickup and delivery services before, you can get $10 off your first three orders of $35 or more. Use the code TRIPLE10 at checkout on each of your first three orders to get the $10 off.

When ordering the holiday meal basket, that brings the feast for 10 down to $25. Depending on your total order, you may need to add an item to hit the $35 mark. The TRIPLE10 code is good through the holiday season, Walmart said.

Previous pickup and delivery customers can use the code DOUBLE10 to get $10 off their order of $35 or more.
