Leitrim's Donal Casey has been eliminated from RTÉ’s The Traitors Ireland.

Donal was forced into a three-way eliminator at the round table along with fellow contestants Carla and Kayleigh after all receiving the same amount votes.

Both Donal and Carla then received an equal amount of votes.

After another eliminator, the Leitrim Gaels clubman was voted out after receiving the most votes.

Before he was banished from the game, these were his parting words.

The Traitors sees a group of contestants entering Slane Castle to compete for a prize of up to €50,000.

Hosted by Siobhán McSweeney, contestants are divided into Faithfuls and Traitors, with the players required to form alliances, read their fellow contestants and ultimately work out who they can trust.
