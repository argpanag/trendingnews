What Is the Binance Red Packet Code?

If this is your first time using the reward feature, here's what you need to know.

The feature takes inspiration from the traditional Chinese hóngbāo, a custom involving the gifting of money during festivals and celebrations. The exchange has adapted this idea into a digital reward system through Binance Pay, allowing users to distribute cryptocurrency through redemption codes.
