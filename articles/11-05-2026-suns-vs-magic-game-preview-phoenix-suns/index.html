PHOENIX - The Suns (1-5) fell to the Trail Blazers, 132-119, at home on Nov. 4. Phoenix won the first quarter 34-29 thanks to 12 points from Devin Booker, who went 4-of-6 from the field. Portland responded with a 43-point second quarter, taking a 72-59 lead into the break. The Trail Blazers had three players score in double figures in the first half. Booker totaled 19 points and Dillon Brooks put up 11 points before the break for Phoenix. Portland took its largest lead of the game in the third quarter, a 22-point advantage, and outscored the Suns by six points in the period. Booker scored all of his game-high 33 points in the first three quarters. The Trail Blazers, who shot 51.2 FG%, had a better field goal percentage, three-point percentage, more rebounds and assists than the Suns. Despite the loss, the Suns forced 18 Portland turnovers that turned into 21 points for Phoenix. Jalen Green (22) and Miles Bridges (18) joined Booker and Bridges in double figures for the Suns.
