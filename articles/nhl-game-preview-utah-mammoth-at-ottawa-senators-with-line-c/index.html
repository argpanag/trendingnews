NHL Game Preview: Utah Mammoth at Ottawa Senators with Line Combinations 11/07/2026

Utah Mammoth News & Notes

Utah’s last outing ended in a 5-4 overtime loss in Montreal on Thursday, a game in which Logan Cooley had a hand in all four goals with three goals and an assist. Cooley has 21 points in 17 games, with 11 goals and 10 assists, and 15 points over his last 10 contests. Defenseman Nate Schmidt did not return to the game after sustaining an upper-body injury, the team announced.

In Tucson, the Mammoth’s AHL affiliate carries points in nine straight games and sits first in the Pacific Division at 7-2-2-0. Goaltender Michael Hrabal, a 2023 second-round pick out of UMass, is 4-1-1 with a 1.85 goals-against average, a .926 save percentage and one shutout. First-round forwards Tij Iginla and Caleb Desnoyers each have five points in 11 games, while Yegor Borikov leads the group with six points.

Ottawa Senators News & Notes

Ridly Greig is the newest name on an already crowded Ottawa injury list. The forward was listed as day-to-day with a lower-body injury earlier this week and was called doubtful for Saturday’s home game against the Utah Mammoth. The Senators return to Canadian Tire Centre short-handed up front against a visitor riding Logan Cooley, who scored a career-high four goals in Utah’s win over Vegas and now owns five career hat tricks.

Further down the organizational ladder, forward Cal Uens is working his way through the system’s lowest rung. The Napanee, Ont., product was invited to Ottawa’s training camp in September, joined the organization on Sept. 17 and was assigned to AHL Belleville a week later. He is now skating with the ECHL’s Allen Americans. Belleville, meanwhile, announced a new team captain this week as the farm club settles its leadership group.
