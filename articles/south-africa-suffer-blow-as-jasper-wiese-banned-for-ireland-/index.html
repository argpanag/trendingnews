South Africa suffer blow as Jasper Wiese banned for Ireland clash

TheTools · 18.11.2026 15:20 · source · permalink

NO BACKWARD STEP: Jasper Wiese and Andrew Porter get up close and personal at the Stade de France. Pic: Ramsey Cardy/Sportsfile

South Africa have suffered another blow after bulldozing number eight Jasper Wiese was banned for the remainder of the Nations Championship because of a dangerous clear-out against France.

Wiese received a 20-minute red card for making direct contact with the head of France centre Kalvin Gourgues and a foul play review committee has issued a four-match suspension, which rules him out of Saturday’s clash with Ireland and finals weekend on November 27-29.

The ban will be reduced to three games if he takes World Rugby’s coaching intervention programme.
