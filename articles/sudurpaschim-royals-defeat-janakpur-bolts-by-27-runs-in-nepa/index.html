Sudurpaschim Royals Defeat Janakpur Bolts by 27 Runs in Nepal Premier League

TheTools · 10.11.2026 16:59 · source · permalink

Kathmandu. Sudurpaschim Royals have defeated Janakpur Bolts by 27 runs in the third edition of the Nepal Premier League (NPL) cricket. Tuesday Tuesday Chasing the target of 121 runs set by Sudurpaschim, Janakpur was all out for 93 runs off the fourth ball of the last over at the TU International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur.

Among Janakpur, Jimmy Nisham scored 20 runs off 25 balls with 1 six and 1 four, Tritaraj Das scored 21 runs off 29 balls with 1 six, and Aditya Mahata scored 10 runs off 11 balls with 1 six, while other batsmen could not score in double digits.

Among Sudurpaschim, Scott Kuggeleijn took 3 wickets for 13 runs in 4 overs. Similarly, Hemant Dhami, Tharindu Ratnayake, Captain Dipendra Singh Airee, Anil Kharel, and Ishan Pandey took 1 wicket each. Earlier, batting after winning the toss at the TU Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, Sudur lost 9 wickets in the stipulated 20 overs and scored 120 runs.

Among Sudur, Brandon McMullen scored 33 runs off 23 balls with 2 sixes and 2 fours, and Tharindu Ratnayake scored 27 runs off 28 balls with 2 sixes and 1 four. Among Janakpur, Captain Harmeet Singh took 4 wickets for 22 runs in 4 overs. Similarly, Harry Tector, Rahul Yadav, Aditya Mahata, and Abhishek Tiwari took 1 wicket each.

With this victory, Sudurpaschim climbed to second place with 8 points from 6 matches, while the defeated Janakpur remained in fifth place with 4 points from the same number of matches.
