Ethereum (ETHUSD) Attacks a Stubborn Resistance – Analysis – 04-09-2026

TheTools · 04.09.2026 17:32 · source · permalink

 

 

Ethereum (ETHUSD) price is holding onto strong gains during its latest intraday trading, attacking the pivotal and stubborn resistance level of $2,500. The price benefits from the dynamic support provided by trading above EMA50, which has restored the dominance and stability of the main bullish trend in the short term. However, we are also noticing the beginning of a bearish crossover in the relative strength indicators after they reached extremely overbought areas, which may temporarily hinder the price’s rise.

 

 

Brent crude oil fluctuated during its latest intraday trading, attempting to gain bullish momentum that might help it to recover and rise again, amid the dominance of the main bullish trend on the short-term basis, with the continuation of the positive pressure that comes from its trading above EMA50, which represents a dynamic support base that reinforces the chances of a near term recovery, on the other hand, we notice the emergence of negative signals from the relative strength indicators, which obstructs this recovery until this moment.

The (USDCHF) price rose during its latest intraday trading, following the pair’s rebound from EMA50’s support, providing positive momentum that helped the price halt the losses witnessed during yesterday’s trading and supported its latest gains, accompanied by the emergence of positive signals from the relative strength indicators after they reached extremely oversold areas. This may enhance the chances of extending those gains in the near term, amid the dominance of a short-term bullish corrective trend.

The (NZDUSD) price continued to rise during its latest intraday trading. Nevertheless, the pair remains under bearish pressure resulting from trading below EMA50, which represents dynamic resistance that limits the chances of a sustainable recovery in the near term. This comes as a short-term bearish corrective wave continues to dominate so far, while the relative strength indicators have entered extremely overbought areas, suggesting the beginning of a negative divergence that could intensify the bearish pressures surrounding the pair.
