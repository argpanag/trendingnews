Doomsday is coming. Marvel Studios’ Avengers: Doomsday reunites MCU veterans Robert Downey Jr., Chris Evans, and Chris Hemsworth for the first time since 2019's Avengers: Endgame, alongside a Multiverse of heroes. The upcoming Avengers film rolls out the green carpet for Doctor Doom, inducting the masked mastermind to the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

From who’s in the movie to what comes next, read on to learn all about the imminent Doom headed to a movie theater near you on December 18.

Avengers: Doomsday follows beloved heroes from three distinct universes as they’re set on a deadly collision course and face an existential threat unlike anything they’ve ever encountered.

Marvel Studios’ Avengers: Doomsday assembles a star-studded cast of heroes that spans three different universes. The film’s ensemble features characters from throughout the Multiverse Saga and beyond, with actors from the 2000s X-Men films also reprising their roles. Their foe? The notorious Doctor Doom, played by returning Marvel veteran Robert Downey Jr. 

The film’s cast was unveiled during a massive livestream in March 2025, revealing one actor’s chair at a time over the course of almost six hours.

Victor Von Doom is the ruler of Latveria, a master of both sorcery and technology. Introduced in the pages of FANTASTIC FOUR (1961) #5 by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby, Doctor Doom first appeared in the comics as a rival to the brilliant Reed Richards and would go on to battle heroes across the Marvel Universe, from Spider-Man to the Avengers.

The fearsome Doctor Doom now makes his Marvel Cinematic Universe debut in Avengers: Doomsday, with original Iron Man actor Robert Downey Jr. returning to play the masked villain. He announced his casting as Doom at San Diego Comic-Con 2024 with a simple mission statement: “New mask, same task.” 

After a series of character-focused teaser trailers, Marvel Studios revealed the first official trailer for Avengers: Doomsday in July 2026. Narrated by Robert Downey Jr.’s Doctor Doom, the trailer set the tone for the danger awaiting the Avengers, Fantastic Four, and the X-Men as their worlds collide.

The Countdown to Avengers: Doomsday Collection on Disney+ provides fans with a curated list of films and TV series to dive into the Multiverse and meet the characters of Avengers: Doomsday. The Countdown to Avengers: Doomsday Podcast, hosted by Frank Alvarez and Ms. Marvel star Iman Vellani, also invites fans to watch along as the duo revisits some of the most iconic films across Marvel’s cinematic history.

Read more: Countdown to ‘Avengers: Doomsday’ with Marvel Movies and Shows on Disney+

Following their saga-concluding work on Avengers: Infinity War and Avengers: Endgame, brothers Joe and Anthony Russo return to direct the next installment in the Avengers franchise, Avengers: Doomsday. They reunite with writer Stephen McFeely, with whom they previously worked on Captain America: The Winter Soldier and Captain America: Civil War. 

“Audiences are coming to Avengers: Doomsday with a lot of emotional connection to prior experiences within the MCU,” Anthony Russo told Marvel.com in September. “And we have those same emotional connections. So, we want to honor that and figure out how we are using that value and that emotional investment to go somewhere new.”

Infinity Vision is a certification for Premium Large Format (PLF) theaters, guaranteeing moviegoers an exceptional film presentation with big screens, bright images, and outstanding sound. Marvel Studios’ Avengers: Endgame Encore and Avengers: Doomsday are the first films to play in Infinity Vision, with more upcoming releases to follow.

Get ready for Avengers: Doomsday with a popcorn bucket worthy of Doctor Doom, available soon at select theaters while supplies last.

Marvel Studios’ Avengers: Doomsday will be followed by Avengers: Secret Wars, releasing in theaters December 17, 2027. Inspired by the Marvel Comics series of the same name, Secret Wars will also be helmed by directors Joe and Anthony Russo.

Avengers: Doomsday arrives in theaters everywhere on December 18. Experience the film in Infinity Vision and get your tickets today!
