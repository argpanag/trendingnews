North Platte bowler wins Nebraska Youth Bowling Partnership

by: The North Platte Bulletin - Oct. 9, 2026

On Sunday, Oct. 4, the Nebraska Youth Bowling Partnership (NEYBP) held a Dual pattern partnership tournament in Council Bluffs, Iowa at Bowlero Lanes.

Student bowlers from all over Nebraska, Iowa and surrounding states attend the year-round tournaments that NEYBP holds at different locations to compete and earn scholarships for their future education.

Lorena Williams of North Platte competed Sunday and won the championship in the 16 and under girls’ division.

In five qualifying games, one lane had a short sports pattern, and the other lane had a long sports pattern. These conditions make it so the bowlers must adapt to the changes in lanes by moving feet, target or changing bowling balls.

After five games, Lorena was seeded second and qualified for the stepladder semifinals. She won the first game to reach the finals for first place. She earmed the title with a score of 180, which also earned her a scholarship.

Lorena has bowled in the partnership for three years and won other scholarships for third and fourth place, respectively.

“This tournament was a big one to win,” she said after claiming the title. “I think this is one of the hardest tour stops that the youth bowling partnership does.”

Lorena is the daughter of Kailin and Patricia Williams and a sophomore at North Platte high school.
