Match information for the Meiji Yasuda J1 League Matchday 12th Sec. against Kyoto on October 24th (Saturday)

TheTools · 25.10.2026 07:27 · source · permalink

NEWS

2026.10.23［INFORMATION］

[Match Date and Time] Saturday, October 24th, MEIJI YASUDA J1 LEAGUE 12th Sec., 16:03 Kick-off
[Opponent club] Kyoto Sanga FC
[Location] Panasonic Stadium Suita
[TV Broadcast] DAZN
[Advance admission gates open] 13:00-13:20
[General opening time] 13:30
*Entry will be via gates 1, 2, 3, and 4.

*This information is current as of October 23rd (Friday). Changes will be updated as needed.

*Although the stadium has a roof, rain may blow into the front rows depending on the wind direction. Please bring a poncho or similar item in case of rain. Umbrellas are not permitted inside the stadium.
*Cans and bottles are not permitted inside the stadium. Please be sure to transfer them to paper cups at the entrance.

■ Ticket counter on the day
・Some tickets will be sold on the day of the event.
*A handling fee of 400 yen per ticket will be charged for ticket purchases made at the ticket counter on the day of the event.

■To our visiting team supporters
• Visitor seats are located on the Upper levels(5th floor).
・If you wish to wear visiting team support goods, please purchase tickets for Visitor Reserved Seats, Category 4 Mixed Fan Seating, or Category 4 FV Mix. Areas other than Visitor Reserved Seats, Category Category 4 Mixed Fan Seating, and Category 4 FV Mix are considered home areas, and therefore, wearing or using support goods team uniforms or merchandise is prohibited. Furthermore, even if you are not wearing visiting team uniforms or merchandise, you are not permitted to support the visiting team. Thank you for your understanding.
・Customers holding tickets for visitor seats or wearing merchandise of the visiting team will not be allowed to enter the Gamba Supporters Seat area. Please be aware that we will be checking tickets and merchandise at various locations throughout the concourse when you enter each area.
A preliminary explanation regarding the display of banners will be given between 12:30 and 12:45. If you wish to participate, please come to the visitor entrance gate at the designated time.
*Please be sure to check this beforehand.
https://www.gamba-osaka.net/c/english/stadium/rule_visiter.html

■ Prohibited Actions
- Items displayed that are not approved by the club (home club only)
- Placing banners, flags, gate flags, large flags, etc. in areas not permitted by the club.
- Use of confetti and paper streamers
- Hitting or kicking stadium equipment (including hitting or kicking chairs)
*Any other actions that the club deems to be disruptive to the match operation.

■ GAMBA OSAKA Spectator Rules and Permitted Locations for Displaying Posters
・For details, please check the Spectator Rules below.
https://www.gamba-osaka.net/c/english/stadium/rule.html

■ Rules for using support banners and flags
- Only official flags (flags sold and distributed by GAMBA OSAKA as merchandise) and items that have been pre-approved by the club and approved for display will be permitted.
*Visiting teams do not need to apply for banners.

■ Information Desk
- Outside the stadium: Next to the FC booth inside Gamba Well-being Park (from 12:00)
- Inside the venue: Concourse near Gate 1(from opening time)

■Fan Club Booth
- Outside the stadium fan club booth (12:00 until kickoff)
After kickoff, please inquire at the "General Information Desk" inside the stadium.

■Merchandise Sales
GAMBA OSAKA Official Shop Blu SPAZIO is,
[From outside the venue: 11:00 to 12:20]
[From inside the venue: 13:00 to 16:00]
[From the end of the match until 30 minutes later]
- The merchandise shops inside the stadium will be open from opening time until approximately 30 minutes after the end of the match.
The large, specially set up merchandise shop outside the stadium will be open from 11:00 AM until kickoff.

■Gourmet
○Inside the venue
The venue will feature 30 Oi G stalls on the Lower levels(including drink booths) and 5 stalls on the Upper levels.
For detailed store locations, please see the Stadium Gourmet page.
https://www.gamba-osaka.net/english/stadium/gourmet/
*Please note that cans and bottles are not permitted. Please transfer any contents to paper cups before entering.

○Outside (12:00~)
Outside the stadium, in "Aokuro Yokocho" (on the back stand side), there will be 5 official stores and 14 other stores!
Additionally, nine shops will be set up at Gamba Well-being Park (on the main stand side)!
Please enjoy food and drinks outside the venue as well!

■ Kids Room
- Located in one place on the main side (from opening time until 30 minutes after the end of the match)

■Smoking area
The smoking area (for both conventional cigarettes and heated tobacco products) will only be available until the last 10 minutes of the session.
*Please note that the designated smoking areas for cigarettes are as follows.

■Temporary exit/re-entry
- You may leave the stadium until 10 minutes into the second half, and re-enter until 20 minutes into the second half.

■ Exit Instructions
We will be implementing staggered exits through four gates. Please cooperate with our measures to reduce crowding.

■For customers arriving by car
・If you are coming by car, you will need to purchase a parking ticket in advance.
You cannot enter the "South Parking Lot" without purchasing a parking ticket.
*Please note that entering and exiting the parking lot may take longer than usual.

■To customers arriving by motorcycle
• Parking is available in the "South Parking Lot".
*Reservations are not required.
Please pay the fee (210 yen) directly at the South Parking Lot.
• The entrance and exit to the South Parking Lot are the same as for cars, as described below.

■To customers who purchased parking tickets for the South Parking Lot
On match days, the entrance to the South Parking Lot will be different from usual; it will be the entrance on the side of Gate 1. Please note that the usual South Parking Lot entrance/exit will be for exit only and you will not be able to enter.
*For customers who have purchased a parking ticket, please park in the South Parking Lot after 10:00 AM.
*Please note that entry and exit to the parking lot may take longer than usual. We ask that customers arriving by car be aware of this in advance. In addition, please refrain from illegal parking near the venue, unauthorized parking at surrounding facilities, and disruptive parking by drop-off/pick-up vehicles on surrounding roads, as this causes considerable inconvenience to local residents. On match days, we conduct parking prevention activities under the guidance of the local police.
https://www.expo70-park.jp/guide/parking/

■To customers arriving by bicycle
Customers arriving by bicycle on the day of the match must
(1) Bicycle parking area north of OFA Banpaku Football Center
(2) Please park at either the former clubhouse site behind Gate 3 or Gate 4.
*Please refrain from illegally parking bicycles near the stadium or parking them without permission at surrounding facilities, as this causes significant inconvenience to local residents.
*Two-wheeled vehicles (including mopeds) cannot be parked in the bicycle parking area.
*Customers arriving by motorcycle should park in the South Parking Lot.

■ Osaka Monorail
• Heavy congestion is expected after the match ends.
We may implement restrictions on entry to the ticket gates. Your cooperation is greatly appreciated.

■Temporary bus information
We will be operating a temporary bus service (for a fee).
*Please note that the number of extra buses is limited.
Please be aware that we may not be able to accommodate your request to board the train.

Kintetsu Bus
[Before the match begins]
・From JR Ibaraki Station to Expo'70 Memorial Stadium(approximately 15 minutes on foot)
[Operating Hours] Approximately 4 hours before kickoff
[Fare] Adults 250 yen, Children 130 yen
[Platform] West Exit Platform 4

[After the match ends]
・From Expo'70 Memorial Stadium to JR Ibaraki Station
[Fare] Adults 250 yen, Children 130 yen

■Regarding your visit
Please review the contents of the press releases regarding "Requests concerning stadium access" and "Requests concerning safety and manners in the surrounding area during matches," and we appreciate your understanding and cooperation.
