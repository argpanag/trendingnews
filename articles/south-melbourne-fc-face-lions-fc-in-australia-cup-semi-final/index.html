South Melbourne FC face Lions FC in Australia Cup semi-final showdown

TheTools · 15.09.2026 01:35 · source · permalink

·

South Melbourne FC will face Lions FC at Lakeside Stadium tonight, with a place in the 2026 Australia Cup final against Melbourne Victory at stake.

The historic Hellenic heritage club reached its third Australia Cup semi-final after comeback victories over Adelaide United and Preston Lions, as well as a dramatic penalty shootout win against Fremantle City.

South Melbourne enter the contest following an inconsistent end to their NPL Victoria campaign, but will be looking to make another national statement after winning the 2025 Australian Championship.

Lions FC arrive on a 12-match winning streak, having eliminated A-League clubs Perth Glory and Melbourne City before defeating North Sunshine Eagles 4–0. The Queensland side has already collected four trophies in 2026 and kept clean sheets in five of its past six matches.

The clash will be the first competitive meeting between the two clubs. South Melbourne’s Arran Cocks and Lions FC veteran Mitchell Hore are among the key attacking players to watch.

A large crowd is expected, with supporters strongly encouraged to purchase tickets before arriving.

  • Date: Tuesday, 15 September 2026
  • Venue: Lakeside Stadium
  • Kick-off: 7.30 pm
  • Gates open: 6.30 pm
  • Tickets: Gate sales will be available at the Futsal Entrance.
