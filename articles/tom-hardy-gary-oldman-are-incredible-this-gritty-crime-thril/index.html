What is Lawless about?

Set in Virginia in 1931, Lawless follows the notorious Bondurant brothers, Forrest (Tom), Howard (Jason Clarke) and Jack (Shia LaBeouf), who run a successful bootlegging operation during Prohibition.

With alcohol banned across America, the brothers have built a thriving business producing and selling moonshine, earning themselves a reputation as men who aren't afraid to bend the rules.

But their relatively comfortable arrangement comes crashing down when corrupt lawman Charlie Rakes (Guy Pearce) arrives in town and demands a cut of their profits.

When Forrest refuses to play along, the brothers find themselves caught in an increasingly violent battle for control.

Meanwhile, the youngest brother, Jack, has ambitions of expanding the family business, bringing him into contact with dangerous Chicago gangster Floyd Banner, played by Gary.

And as rivalries intensify, the brothers soon discover that protecting their empire could cost them everything.

Think Peaky Blinders meets a classic American gangster film, complete with family loyalty, double-crossing and plenty of bloody confrontations.

Who stars in Lawless?

It's not just Tom and Gary who make this film worth revisiting.

While, the Taboo star plays the formidable Forrest Bondurant, the middle brother whose intimidating reputation makes him a force to be reckoned with.

Slow Horses actor Gary, meanwhile, takes on the role of Floyd Banner, a powerful Chicago gangster whose dealings with the brothers add another layer of danger to their already complicated lives.

It also stars....

  • Shia LaBeouf stars as ambitious younger brother Jack
  • Jason Clarke plays the eldest sibling, Howard
  • Jessica Chastain appears as Maggie Beauford
  • Mia Wasikowska plays Bertha Minnix, Jack's love interest
  • Guy Pearce as Charlie Rakes, the deeply unpleasant lawman

Basically, it's a cast that would be impressive in any decade.
