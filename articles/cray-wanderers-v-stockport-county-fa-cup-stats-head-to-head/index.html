  6. 'Means a massive amount to us' - Cray captain Raymondpublished at 14:11 GMT
    14:11 GMT

    Cray Wanderers v Stockport (14:15 GMT)

    Ben Ashton
    BBC Sport England

    If at first you do not succeed, try and try again. And again. Until, at the 78th attempt, you finally make it.

    Formed in 1860, Cray Wanderers are one of the oldest football clubs in the world - and the oldest club in London still in existence.

    And yet the Wands had never reached the FA Cup first round in their 166-year history.

    Until now.

    "It means a massive amount to us all," club captain Frankie Raymond told BBC London.

    "It's a club steeped in history and to be the first set of players and staff to take the club to the first round is a massive achievement for everyone."

  8. Can the oldest club in London spring a surprise?published at 14:10 GMT
    14:10 GMT

    Cray Wanderers v Stockport (14:15 GMT)

    Stockport County are aiming for a seventh consecutive season in the FA Cup second round and they will have every belief they can get there.

    Currently second in the League Two table, they face a team that are 112 league places below them.

    Cray Wanderers are 18th in the Isthmian League Premier Division, the seventh tier of the English football pyramid, but underestimating a minnow would be at the Hatters peril.

    The Wands have put themselves through seven gruelling qualifying rounds, including three replays, to get to their first ever FA Cup first round match. Back down now? Don't bet on it.
