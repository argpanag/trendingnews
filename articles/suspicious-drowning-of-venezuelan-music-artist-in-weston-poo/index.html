‘Suspicious' drowning of Venezuelan music artist in Weston pool under investigation

TheTools · 18.10.2026 10:37 · source · permalink

Broward Sheriff's Office officials said they received a report of a possible drowning at a home near the 2100 block of Quail Roost Drive around 9 a.m.

Officials are investigating possible drowning of a Venezuelan music artist, who was found dead in his Weston swimming pool. NBC6’s Valerie Ryan reports

Detectives are investigating the "suspicious" death of a man whose body was found in a pool at a Weston home on Friday morning, officials said.

Broward Sheriff's Office officials said they received a report of a possible drowning at a home near the 2100 block of Quail Roost Drive around 9 a.m.

Watch NBC6 free wherever you are

The man was found submerged in the pool and pronounced dead at the scene.

Authorities have not released the victim's identity, but family members identified him to NBC6 as Gustavo Ormo, a Venezuelan music artist. Public records show Ormo is listed as the owner of the home where deputies responded.

BSO's Homicide and Crime Scene Units will investigate the incident that they're calling "suspicious."

Video from Chopper 6 showed what appeared to be rope leading into the pool. NBC6 blurred images of the victim's body. Authorities have not confirmed any details about the circumstances surrounding Ormo's death or whether the rope is connected to the investigation.

Neighbors said they were shocked by the discovery in the typically quiet community.

"It's sad what happened, and it wasn't something I expected to happen in Weston," resident Bruno Gedikinan said.

Another neighbor, Mariano Cascata, called the incident unusual for the area.

"It's weird to happen. It's not normal, of course," Cascata said.

Investigators have not said what led them to classify the death as suspicious, and no arrests have been announced.

The investigation remains active.

No other details were immediately known.
