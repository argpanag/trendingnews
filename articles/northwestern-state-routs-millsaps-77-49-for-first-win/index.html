Northwestern State routs Millsaps 77-49 for first win

TheTools · 11.11.2026 10:33 · source · permalink

Isaiah Vick tallied a double-double with 12 points and 11 rebounds, helping the Demons secure their first victory of the season before heading to Little Rock on Saturday.

NATCHITOCHES, La. (AP) Kai Simpson had 17 points in Northwestern State’s 77-49 victory over Division III member Millsaps on Tuesday.

Simpson also had five rebounds for the Demons (1-3). Isaiah Vick scored 12 points while shooting 4 of 6 from the field and 4 for 6 from the line, and added 11 rebounds. Kordrick Turner had 11 points and shot 4 of 6 from the field and 3 of 4 from the free-throw line.

Davis Joyner led the Majors in scoring, finishing with 11 points. Millsaps also got eight points from Caleb Hunt, and Devin Petersen had seven points.

Northwestern State visits Little Rock in its next matchup on Saturday.
