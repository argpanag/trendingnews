Jayden Reed posts to social media after leaving Packers game on cart

TheTools · 21.09.2026 16:52 · source · permalink

Green Bay Packers wide receiver Jayden Reed left his team's game against the New York Jets on Sept. 20 immobilized on a cart after taking a hit on the second play from scrimmage, suffering a neck injury.

Reed was expected to stay overnight in a New York hospital for testing and observation, but he posted on social media after the Packers' overtime win.

"Doing much better, appreciate all the prayers and support," Reed wrote on X.

Reed and right tackle Zach Bako-Bewele were lost on consecutive plays in a costly start to what became a mostly frustrating day.

Reed was tackled by Demario Davis and ex-Packers defensive end Kingsley Enagbare after a 4-yard screen play. He was given lengthy attention on the turf at MetLife Stadium before departing on the cart.

Packers coach Matt LaFleur confirmed after the game that Reed has movement in all his extremities.

Reed missed 10 games in 2025 after suffering a broken collarbone in Week 2 against Washington. He also had surgery to repair a Jones fracture in his foot.

Before this season, he signed a three-year. $50 million contract extension.
