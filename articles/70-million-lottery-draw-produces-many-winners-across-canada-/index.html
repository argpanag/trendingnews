$70 million lottery draw produces many winners across Canada | insauga

TheTools · 03.10.2026 11:29 · source · permalink

By insauga Staff

Published October 3, 2026 at 7:29 am

The Lotto Max jackpot reached a massive $70 million for the Friday, Oct. 2 draw, with millions more up for grabs through Maxmillions and Max Plus prizes.

The winning Lotto Max numbers for the Oct. 2 draw were: 02, 04, 20, 23, 25, 41 and 47 – The Bonus number was 26.

There were 203,919 cash-winning tickets across the main Lotto Max draw, according to the draw results.

The top prize of $70 million was not claimed, as no ticket matched all seven winning numbers.

However, three tickets came extremely close, matching six of the seven numbers plus the Bonus number. Each of those tickets is worth $92,391.90.

There was also plenty of money handed out through the Maxmillions draws.

Three Maxmillions combinations produced $1-million prizes, for a total of $3 million.

The $1-million Maxmillions winning combinations were:
01, 02, 13, 28, 37, 38, 48
02, 06, 09, 18, 35, 46, 50
03, 23, 29, 34, 39, 44, 45

Another $1.4 million was won through Max Plus.

The ENCORE number for the Oct. 2 draw was: 3097442

No ticket matched all seven ENCORE digits for the $1-million top prize. However, two tickets matched the last six digits, 097442, to win $100,000 each.

Lotto Max is one of Canada’s largest national lottery games. Players select seven numbers from 1 to 50, with draws held twice a week on Tuesdays and Fridays.

When the Lotto Max jackpot reaches $50 million, additional Maxmillions prizes are offered. Each Maxmillions prize is worth $1 million and is drawn separately from the main jackpot.

Players should always check their tickets through an authorized lottery retailer or official provincial lottery channels to confirm any prize.

With the $70-million jackpot still in play following Friday’s draw, Lotto Max players will have another shot at one of the game’s biggest prizes in the next draw.
