Steffi Graf sends fans wild with ultra rare appearance in family photo: 'Surprise'

TheTools · 08.10.2026 11:34 · source · permalink

Steffi has been married to fellow tennis pro Andre Agassi since 2001 and they share two children

After years in the limelight as professional tennis stars, Steffi Graf and Andre Agassi now live a low-key life in Las Vegas. 

While Andre keeps his fans updated on social media, his wife rarely makes an appearance. So, when she cropped up on Andre's Instagram recently, fans couldn't believe their eyes. 

Andre shared a photo dump of memories and eagle-eyed followers quickly honed in on one particular image featuring Steffi.

In the shot, Steffi, Andre and their daughter, Jaz, were chatting to former French tennis professional Yannick Noah, who appeared to be making the family laugh. 

Steffi looked casual in a white t-shirt, and baggy jeans, with her hands on her hips. Jaz — who bears a strikingly resemblance to her mom — donned a similar outfit and hairstyle. 

They looked deep in conversation with Yannick in the candid moment captured on camera. 

Despite Steffi's face only being visible side-on, fans went wild for the rare appearance. 

They commented: "Surprise by Steffi" and added her name and on-fire emojis. Others called the dump "the best," and adored seeing the family.

The secret to their 25 year marriage

In October 2026, the couple will celebrate a quarter of a century of marriage. Andre recently opened up to HELLO! and chatted about what makes them tick. 

Andre — who was leading a six-man line up as captain for Team World at the Lavers Cup — said they have mutual respect and understanding for one another.

"She likes seeing me happy, just like I like seeing her happy," the 56-year-old told us when asked how Steffi feels about his return to the high-stakes environment. "She knows this is a week in Laver Cup that I value, that I bond with, that I connect to. It's a lot of work, but she's never been scared of me in work [mode]."

Their 'shockingly balanced' children

The couple share two grown children and kept them as grounded as possible despite their worldwide fame. 

Their son Jaden is a talented baseball pitcher, and Jaz has carved out success as a dancer and equestrian. Despite having two sporting icons as parents, Andre insists their children have stayed remarkably clear-headed about fame. 

"They got the best seat in the house to realise what life is and what life isn't," Andre reflected. "They see us, they learn from what we do, not from what we say. And then they see how other people sometimes treat us. So it's very easy for them to understand ideas of grandeur versus the reality of life." 

"They're shockingly balanced," 

"I attribute that to a tool of being a more accomplished, recognisable parent. But again, it's only a tool if you live a certain way. We live pretty grounded. They see how you're treated, and it makes a lot of sense to them on what they choose for themselves."
