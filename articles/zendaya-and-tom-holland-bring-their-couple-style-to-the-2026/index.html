Zendaya and Tom Holland Bring Their Couple Style to the 2026 Emmys

TheTools · 15.09.2026 01:08 · source · permalink

Zendaya and Tom Holland have offered up no shortage of couple style moments this summer. Now, they’re capping off their fashion tour at the 2026 Emmys.

The newlyweds kept plenty busy this year, promoting both of their shared projects—Spider-Man: Brand New Day and The Odyssey—back-to-back. After a short break from their globe-spanning press tours (during which they reportedly celebrated their wedding at a palatial estate in England), Zendaya and Holland are back on the carpet.

Zendaya in Prada.

Tonight, Zendaya was nominated for best actress in a drama series for the role of Rue Bennett in the final season of Euphoria. At the time of her first win for the role, in 2020, she became the youngest-ever winner in her category, at 24. With her second Emmy, in 2022, she broke another record as the youngest-ever repeat winner for best actress in a drama series.

For her third nomination for Euphoria—plus her presenting duties—Zendaya and stylist Law Roach turned to Prada for a custom dress: a nude tulle gown adorned with beads and crystals. They maximized the drama with the silhouette, opting for a plunging V-neck and an operatic train.

While Holland has gotten on board with his wife’s famous method dressing in the past—especially during the Spider-Man tour—tonight he dressed the part of a supportive spouse in a dapper Dior suit.
