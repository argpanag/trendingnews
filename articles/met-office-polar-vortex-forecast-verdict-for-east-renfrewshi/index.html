Met Office polar vortex forecast verdict for East Renfrewshire as temperatures drop

TheTools · 14.10.2026 23:52 · source · permalink

East Renfrewshire's weather will turn cooler and drier by Saturday, while the Met Office rejects claims of imminent polar-vortex snow.

Today's forecast to be cloudy, with a 60% chance of rain from 3pm.

Gusts could reach a high of 23mph at 1pm.

Temperatures are likely to range between 14°C and 16°C throughout the day.

Heavier rain is expected on Thursday, with temperatures dropping to an average of 11°C.

It will be less windy and the rain will be lighter on Friday, with highs of 11°C.

Saturday is expected to be sunnier and drier as well as less windy, with a maximum temperature of 10°C.

However, a Met Office article published on October, 5 said the current forecast does not support claims that the UK is about to experience widespread snow linked to changes in the polar vortex.

It said El Niño can raise the likelihood of wetter, stormier and generally milder weather across the UK in late autumn and early winter.

Later in winter, after El Niño peaks, the stratospheric polar vortex may become weaker. This could increase the chance of Sudden Stratospheric Warming events, and therefore the potential for colder weather.

The Met Office said these are changes in probability, not guarantees. It also said possible polar-vortex changes concern the end of winter, not a prediction of snow in particular UK locations now.
