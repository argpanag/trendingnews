Thunder's Jalen Williams: Set to play Wednesday

TheTools · 08.10.2026 00:55 · source · permalink

Williams appears primed for exhibition action after sitting out Oklahoma City's preseason opener. He was limited to 33 regular-season appearances while dealing primarily with wrist and hamstring issues in 2025-26, but Williams is healthy for the Thunder this fall.
