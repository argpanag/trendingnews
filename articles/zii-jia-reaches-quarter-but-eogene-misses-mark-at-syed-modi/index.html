Zii Jia reaches quarter but Eogene misses mark at Syed Modi

TheTools · 26.11.2026 17:25 · source · permalink
    bookmark

PETALING JAYA: Lee Zii Jia stayed on course for the Syed Modi International title after easing into the quarter-finals with a convincing win over India's Pranay Shettigar on Thursday (Nov 26).

The second seed lived up to expectations at the Babu Banarasi Das Uttar Pradesh Badminton Academy in Lucknow, defeating the home player 21-11, 21-18 in 36 minutes.

However, fellow Malaysian Eogene Ewe suffered a disappointing exit after losing 5-21, 14-21 to South Korea's Jeon Hyeok-jin.

Zii Jia will face sixth seed Arnaud Merkle of France on Friday (Nov 27) for a place in the semi-finals.
