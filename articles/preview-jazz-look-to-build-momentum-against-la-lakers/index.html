Preview: Jazz look to build momentum against LA Lakers

TheTools · 18.11.2026 13:57 · source · permalink
  • Utah Jazz Game Coverage

Fresh off snapping a 12-game losing streak, the Jazz look to build momentum against the NBA-best Lakers.﻿

Last night, the Jazz finally snapped a 12-game losing streak in Los Angeles against the Clippers. Now, Utah will take on the LA Lakers on the second night of a back-to-back. The Lakers currently hold the best record in the NBA at 13-2. Flip that record around, and you have 2-13, where the Jazz currently reside!

The Jazz ended their skid with a 132-107 victory, a decisive win fueled by a massive fourth quarter in which they outscored the Clippers 44-21. Brice Sensabaugh accounted for 25 of those 44 points.

The Lakers also played yesterday, beating the Charlotte Hornets for their ninth straight victory, making them the hottest team in the association. Walker Kessler and Austin Reaves were both out, yet the Lakers still managed to pull out an overtime win.

Luka Doncic, who dropped 52 points on the Jazz in their last meeting, finished with 36 points on 34 shots. Not exactly the most ethical game.

You wanna talk ethical? We already mentioned Sensabaugh, but let’s talk about Quentin Grimes, who erupted for 47 points on just 24 shots, knocking down 10 of his 14 attempts from beyond the arc.

The statuses of Kessler and Reaves remain uncertain. This is purely my own speculation, but I think Kessler is more likely to play, while Reaves feels closer to a 50/50.

The Jazz could definitely benefit from facing a shorthanded Lakers team. To be fair, Utah is dealing with plenty of injuries of its own. Guys like Mo Bamba, Josh Green and Josh Okogie have emerged as legitimate depth pieces throughout the injuries.

The Jazz have a massive opportunity to build some real momentum after a disastrous start to the season.

Their last matchup against the Lakers came in Salt Lake City, and Utah probably should’ve walked away with a win. The Jazz dominated the first quarter and kept things close all the way into the final minutes. Ultimately, the Lakers pulled away behind some ridiculous shot-making from Doncic and Reaves.

That being said, Lauri Markkanen and Jaren Jackson Jr. played in that game, while Darryn Peterson wasn’t playing at the level he is now. The previous matchup probably isn’t the best game to go off of, but Utah’s recent performances suggest the Jazz should be capable of keeping things competitive.

While the Jazz receive very few nationally televised games, they’ll have plenty of attention on them tonight as they take on the league’s best team, looking to string together consecutive wins for the first time this season.

Tipoff is scheduled for 8 p.m. MT tonight in La La Land.
