Columbus farm showcases Brewers’ spirit with giant logo

TheTools · 27.10.2026 04:04 · source · permalink

MADISON, Wis. (WMTV) - A Wisconsin family farm is showcasing their Brewers’ spirit in a big way.

The Damm family painted a giant Brewers logo next to their field, showcasing their team spirit.

A little extra Brewers pride, right in Columbus.

The Brewers took a 3-0 lead with a 10-1 win over the Tampa Bay Rays in Game 3 on Monday night.

Game 4 will take place at 7 p.m. Tuesday at Tropicana Field.
