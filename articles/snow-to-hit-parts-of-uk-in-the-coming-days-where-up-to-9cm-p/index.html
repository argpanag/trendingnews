Snow to hit parts of UK in the coming days - where up to 9cm per hour could fall

TheTools · 16.11.2026 09:20 · source · permalink

Snow has been forecast across parts of the UK early next week, with some areas set for falls of up to 9cm per hour.

The Met Office has forecast "wintry showers", along with hill snow, and strong coastal winds between Monday (November 16) and Wednesday (November 18).

The forecast reads: "Sunny for most on Monday, but wintry showers over northeast Scotland and rain for the southwest later.

"From Tuesday, rain, hill snow in the north, showers, and strong coastal winds."

Where exactly will it snow in the UK?

Snow is expected to fall in the Scottish Highlands as early as Sunday (November 14), according to WX Charts weather maps.

By late Monday, snow could move into South Wales around Merthyr Tydfil before spreading north overnight toward Newtown and Welshpool.

Early Tuesday (November 17) could see further snowfall in parts of Yorkshire, Cumbria, and southern Scotland, reaching as far as Glasgow and Stirling.

The most widespread snowfall across the UK is expected early on Tuesday (November 17). (Image: WX Charts)

WX charts shows snow lingering around small areas of Scotland until the following Wednesday (November 25).

Will there be any more snow in November?

This is likely to be the only snow the UK sees for the rest of November, according to the Met Office.

The Met Office’s long-range forecast (for November 19 to 28) suggests a mix of rain and showers, particularly in the south and west, with drier, colder spells in between.

The forecast reads: "A changeable pattern is likely to continue, with areas of rain or showers affecting parts of the UK, particularly across the south and west.

"These will be interspersed with drier, colder interludes as shorter-lived ridges of high pressure develop.

"Weather patterns may become slower evolving during the latter stages of November, leading to some uncertainty over how much progress Atlantic frontal systems will make into the UK.

"Nonetheless, further rain or showers are likely at times, with the potential for some periods of strong winds.

"Incursions of milder air are likely towards the south, although it could remain on the cold side overall further north, where there is potential for overnight frosts and occasional wintry precipitation, particularly over higher ground."

Cold health alerts in place across the UK from Sunday

Cold weather health alerts have also been issued for large parts of the UK.

The warnings are expected to affect the North West, West Midlands, and Yorkshire and the Humber from 9pm on Sunday (November 15) until 9am on Thursday (November 19).

The UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA) said the alerts mean the weather is likely to have minor impacts on health and social care services, including an increased use of healthcare services and a greater risk to life to vulnerable people.

Paul Coleman, consultant in public health at the UKHSA, said: "This is the latest Cold-Health Alert of the season, and we can expect more as we move further into winter.

"Cold weather can have a serious impact on the health of some people, including those aged 65 and over and those with pre-existing health conditions, so it is vital to check in on friends, family and neighbours who are most vulnerable."

Mr Coleman said people should try to keep the rooms they use most heated and dress warmly during colder spells.
