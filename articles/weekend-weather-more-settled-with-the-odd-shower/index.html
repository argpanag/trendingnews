Weekend weather: more settled with the odd shower

TheTools · 02.10.2026 08:13 · source · permalink

High pressure to the south will dominate, with largely dry conditions and lighter winds compared to recent days.

Friday

Friday morning will start off with some showers in the south and east, with further spells of rain moving into the western half of the country during the morning. These will move eastwards during the afternoon.

There may be some heavy bursts with hail and isolated thunderstorms, along with fresh southerly winds. However, sunshine and easing winds will follow from the west during the afternoon and into the evening. Temperatures will see highs of 13°C to 19°C, coolest in the north.

On Friday night, any lingering rain will clear for a mainly dry night, with light breezes and lowest temperatures of 7°C to 11°C.

Saturday

According to Met Éireann, Saturday will be mostly dry with sunny spells, the best of these over Leinster and Munster. Highest temperatures will range from 14°C to 18°C, in light southwesterly breezes.

Overnight, it will remain mostly dry with some light rain or drizzle possible near northwestern coasts.

It will be cloudy in the northwest and some patchy mist and fog may form on southern costs. Temperatures will range from 7°C to 12°C in light to moderate south to southwest winds.

Sunday

“Sunday looks like a cloudier day with outbreaks of light rain and drizzle in the west and north that will linger through much of the day,” Met Éireann has said.

Some sunny spells are forecast, with the best of these set for the southeast. Highest temperatures will be 15°C to 19°C, with mostly moderate southwesterly winds.

Next week

Monday is set to be cloudy with light rain and drizzle over the northern half of the country. Drier and brighter weather will persist further south. Temperatures of 16°C to 20°C are expected, in light to moderate southwesterly winds.
