VidiFab Streaming Video Downloader V2.5.0 Adds Crunchyroll and Pluto TV Support, Offering 30% Off for New Users

TheTools · 15.10.2026 03:51 · source · permalink

CALFORNIA CITY, CA, October 14, 2026 /24-7PressRelease/ -- VidiFab is a developer of software utilities for downloading online streaming videos. The brand focuses on helping users bypass platform and device compatibility limits for offline viewing. In line with this mission, VidiFab has officially released version 2.5.0 of its flagship product, VidiFab Streaming Video Downloader. Developed directly from user feedback requesting broader platform access, this update adds download support for Crunchyroll and Pluto TV, alongside performance optimizations and bug fixes.

To celebrate this monumental upgrade, VidiFab is launching a limited-time global promotion, offering a 30% discount for new users, while promising existing users a completely free upgrade to version 2.5.0.

VidiFab Streaming Video Downloader is an all-in-one desktop software that saves videos from major streaming platforms, including Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Max, Hulu, Apple TV, and now Crunchyroll and Pluto TV, into universal MP4 or MKV formats at up to 1080p. The software features high-speed batch downloading for full seasons, ad-stripping capabilities, and flexible multi-language audio and subtitle retention options.

"Our development roadmap is entirely driven by our user community," said the Product Manager at VidiFab. "This version 2.5.0 release responds directly to user requests by integrating Crunchyroll and Pluto TV to expand the software's functional range. Our engineering team remains focused on implementing regular performance enhancements and resolving bugs based on technical user feedback to improve overall application stability."

Key Highlights of VidiFab Streaming Video Downloader V2.5.0

• Support for Crunchyroll & Pluto TV: Users can now download anime series, television shows, and movies from Crunchyroll and Pluto TV for permanent offline playback on any compatible media player.

• Performance Optimization & Bug Fixes: Technical improvements increase application stability, enhance background processing speeds, and resolve known runtime glitches.

• Automated Advertisement Removal: The software automatically removes embedded ads during the downloading process, ensuring uninterrupted offline viewing for free-tier streaming services.

• Subtitle and Audio Track Selection: Downloads retain multi-language audio tracks and custom subtitle files, outputting them as embedded media layers or independent external SRT documents.
