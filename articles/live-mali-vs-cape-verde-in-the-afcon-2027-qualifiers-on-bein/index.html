Mali Wants to Begin Its Campaign by Taking Advantage of Home Field

The race to reach the AFCON 2027 begins with an important test for two national teams that have legitimate ambitions of challenging for the top spots in Group K.

Mali hosts Cape Verde this Friday, September 25, at the Stade du 26 Mars in Bamako, with kickoff scheduled for 3:00 PM ET / 12:00 PM PT. You can watch the match LIVE on beIN SPORTS.

For the Eagles, playing at home represents an important opportunity to begin the campaign with three points before having to hit the road.

The team coached by Anthony da Silva faces two matches during this opening international window that could begin to shape its qualification campaign. After facing Cape Verde, Mali will travel to take on Liberia on September 29.

There will not be much room for a slow start.

Cape Verde Arrives After Making World Cup History

On the other side is a national team that can no longer simply be considered a surprise.

Cape Verde experienced the greatest moment in its soccer history in 2026 when it competed in the FIFA World Cup for the first time.

The Blue Sharks secured their historic qualification after finishing ahead of Cameroon in the African qualifiers, becoming the second-smallest country by population ever to reach a World Cup, behind only Iceland.

And their story did not end simply by reaching the tournament.

Cape Verde managed to advance from the group stage in its World Cup debut. A 0-0 draw against Saudi Arabia in Houston secured its place in the Round of 32 without losing any of its first three matches in the competition.

Its run eventually came to an end against Lionel Messi’s Argentina, but the World Cup provided something even more important: the belief that this generation can compete on stages that once seemed out of reach.

Now, another cycle begins.

Following the departure of Bubista, Humberto Bettencourt took charge of the national team and will face his first major test on the road to the next Africa Cup of Nations against Mali.

The Blue Sharks also arrive with confidence after defeating Serbia 3-0 in their most recent international friendly.

Group K Begins With Very Little Margin for Error

Mali and Cape Verde share Group K with Rwanda and Liberia.

Unlike groups that include one of the three AFCON 2027 host nations, the formula here is straightforward: the top two teams will qualify for the tournament. The qualifying campaign will consist of six matchdays, with home-and-away fixtures.

That makes this opening matchup more than just an introduction.

After visiting Bamako, Cape Verde will return home to face Rwanda on September 29, while Mali will travel to take on Liberia on the same day.

The points earned during this opening window could prove extremely valuable when the qualification places begin to be decided in March.


The AFCON 2027 Awaits in East Africa

The ultimate goal is to become one of the 24 national teams that will compete at the AFCON 2027.

The tournament will be jointly hosted by Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda from June 19 through July 17, 2027, marking the first edition to be hosted by three countries.

For Mali, the journey begins by trying to make home-field advantage count.

For Cape Verde, it begins with an opportunity to prove that what happened at the World Cup was not an isolated chapter.
