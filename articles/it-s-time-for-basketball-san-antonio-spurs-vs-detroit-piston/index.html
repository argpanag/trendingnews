It’s time for Basketball: San Antonio Spurs vs Detroit Pistons

TheTools · 18.11.2026 00:30 · source · permalink
  • San Antonio Spurs Discussion

Spurs are home as they try to defeat Cade Cunningham and the Pistons for their 13th consecutive victory

Welcome to the Game Thread. Veterans of the Game Thread know how we do things around here, but for all you newbies we have a few rules. Our community guidelines apply and basically say be cool, no personal attacks, don’t troll and don’t swear too much.

The Spurs just took a short trip to Utah, where the 1-13 Jazz made the them work a little harder than expected as they extended their current win streak to an even dozen. The Spurs have beaten a lot of bad teams and teams with key players out of the lineup throughout that span of games, but tonight’s game is against a contender that’s close to full strength—the Pistons are without Jalen Duren, whose size could cause some issues for the Spurs’ frontcourt. Sharpshooter Duncan Robinson is also out tonight, and they’ll miss his shooting. Cade Cunningham is the Pistons’ superstar, and he’s just five days removed from his career best 52 points showing in a 144-140 double overtime thriller win over the Wizards. Castle will probably draw the defensive assignment on Cade, and he will have to be careful to stay out of foul trouble. With Victor Wembanyama creating a zone of exclusion1 in the paint, the Silver and Black have a good shot of limiting the damage from Detroit’s superstar.

De’Aaron Fox was out for the Jazz game, but he’s not listed on today’s injury report, so he should be back in the starting lineup. Fox’s steadiness has been a big factor in the Spurs’ win streak, and his absence in Utah could have been part of the reason that the Jazz were able to keep the game close until a late fourth quarter surge from the Silver and Black.

If the Spurs can extend the streak to 13 with a win tonight, they have a good shot at making it last even longer, since the next three games are against the Pelicans, Clippers, and Kings, which are all near the bottom of the Western Conference standings. In the way too early to matter playoff positioning race, the Spurs can keep pac with the Lakers for first place tonight with a win, or even take sole possession of first with a Lakers loss to the Hornets. There are a ton of different viewing options tonight, so if you don’t like the food segment that Sean and Jacob do at the start of the third quarter, you might want to watch on Peacock. I used to hate that part of the show, but it’s kind of growing on me as the win streak keeps getting longer. Let’s Go SPURS, and Stephon must dominate Cade.
