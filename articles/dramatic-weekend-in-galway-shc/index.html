Dramatic weekend in Galway SHC

TheTools · 20.09.2026 17:56 · source · permalink

Loughrea hurler Paul Hoban. Photo by Ray McManus/Sportsfile

Galway Senior Hurling Championship Preliminary Quarter-Finals

Gort 0-22 St Thomas' 2-15

Loughrea 2-21 Castlegar 2-17

Turloughmore 0-22 Liam Mellows 2-9

Craughwell 1-23 Ardrahan 0-25

Holders Loughrea eventually overcame Castlegar to book a place in the last eight of the Galway SHC.

Substitute Jamie Ryan starred in the closing stages for Loughrea, who defeated a Ger Farragher inspired Castlegar outfit.

Former Galway midfielder Farragher struck 1-7 for Castlegar, but Loughrea stayed cool.

Ryan landed five points, while Tiernan Killeen and Martin McManus supplied crucial goals for the winners.

Gort edged out local rivals St Thomas in a keenly contested match at Kenny Park on Saturday.

Thomas' settled impressively with a Conor Cooney goal from a free edging them six points clear.

Gort, though, responded with Aidan Helebert providing important scores.

Greg Lally and Nathan Gill were influential figures as a gritty Gort team prevailed despite a second Thomas' goal for Eanna Burke.

Turloughmore also booked a place in the quarter-final, defeating Liam Mellows.

Adrian Morrissey was in good scoring form for the city club, but Turloughmore emerged convincing winners.

Dara Zimmer's last gasp goal, at the end of extra-time ensured Craughwell beat Ardrahan.

Trailing by two, Craughwell required a goal, and Zimmer provided the flash of inspiration, netting in the closing seconds.

Galway SHC Quarter-Finals draw

St Mary's, Athenry v Loughrea

Sarsfields v Craughwell

Clarinbridge v Gort

Tommy Larkins v Turloughmore
