Vikings at Saints Inactives for Week 5 of 2026

TheTools · 11.10.2026 20:01 · source · permalink

NEW ORLEANS — The Minnesota Vikings announced the following players will be inactive for their Week 5 game of 2026 at the New Orleans Saints.

Receiver Jordan Addison

Safety Jakobe Thomas

Receiver Odell Beckham Jr.

Quarterback Max Brosmer (3rd QB)

Cornerback Chuck Demmings

Tackle Christian Darrisaw

Defensive lineman Elijah Williams

View photos of the team arriving at Caesars Superdome for the Week 5 game vs. the Saints.

Beckham was elevated from the practice squad on Saturday because Addison and Justin Jefferson were questionable heading into the game. Jefferson rehabbed an ankle injury and is good to go in his "homecoming" game.

Demmings is recovering from a hamstring injury, and Darrisaw is in concussion protocol.

Thomas and Williams are inactive for the fifth consecutive week.

Brosmer will dress but is only eligible to play if Kyler Murray and Carson Wentz suffer injuries.

View photos of the Vikings traveling to New Orleans, Louisiana for their Week 5 game against the Saints.

Saints Inactives

New Orleans announced the following players are inactive:

Quarterback Zach Wilson (3rd QB)

Edge Anfernee Jennings

Safety Mike Reid

Linebacker Kaden Elliss

Guard Jeremiah Wright

Defensive lineman Vernon Broughton

Defensive lineman Nathan Shepherd

Wilson will dress but is only eligible to play if Tyler Shough and Spencer Rattler suffer injuries.
