Transition boost: new platform funds emerging-market clean energy upgrades

TheTools · 07.09.2026 04:00 · source · permalink

World may need up to US$20 trillion over five to eight years to build or upgrade renewable energy facilities, HSBC executive says

The Green Accelerator Programme, rolled out on Monday with backing from HSBC, the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank, the Silk Road Fund and others, will channel private capital into feasibility studies and due diligence for emerging-market companies and governments adopting green technology.

“Geopolitical disruptions such as those in the Strait of Hormuz have accelerated demand for resilient, alternative energy sources,” said Julian Wentzel, chief sustainability officer at HSBC, in an interview with the South China Morning Post.

This included global corporate transition spending, currently estimated at US$2.3 trillion per year and expected to increase to US$3.6 trillion per year by 2030.

It is probably the most significant and fastest scale of deployment of capital I have seen in my over 30 years in banking
