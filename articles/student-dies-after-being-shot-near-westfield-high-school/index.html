Student dies after being shot near Westfield High School

TheTools · 22.09.2026 20:17 · source · permalink

Local News

Search continues for suspect

HARRIS COUNTY, Texas – Authorities confirm a 15-year-old student has died after being shot near Westfield High School Tuesday.

The Harris County Pct. 4 Constable’s Office says the shooting happened off Ella Boulevard, near the high school.

A suspect reportedly fled the scene on foot and an active search continues.

Authorities say the call dropped at 2:47 p.m. School had just gotten out at 2:30 p.m.

Gonzalez says the shooting happened on a sidewalk near the Spring ISD administration building and the high school. There was some type of encounter between the 15-year-old student and at least one other male when the student was shot.

Gonzalez says they do not believe there is an active threat to the community, although there is still an active search. Law enforcement are using drones to assist them.

Spring ISD says all after-school activities, meetings, and athletic events have been canceled.
