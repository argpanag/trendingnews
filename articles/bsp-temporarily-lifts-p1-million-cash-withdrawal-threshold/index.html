BSP temporarily lifts P1 million cash withdrawal threshold

TheTools · 16.11.2026 01:45 · source · permalink

MANILA, Philippines — The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) has temporarily lifted the P1-million threshold that triggers additional scrutiny of large cash withdrawals, to facilitate legitimate transactions and meet higher cash demand during the holiday season.

In a memorandum, the BSP’s policy-making Monetary Board suspended the enhanced due diligence (EDD) threshold for large cash transactions until Jan. 31, 2027.

EDD refers to additional checks made by banks and other financial institutions to verify the legitimacy of transactions and assess potential money laundering risks.

The suspension took effect on Nov. 13, following the Monetary Board’s approval on Nov. 12.

The BSP said the move was intended “to facilitate legitimate transactions and support the public’s cash requirements during this period,” citing an anticipated increase in economic activity and cash transactions during the holidays.

The central bank stressed that the suspension does not exempt financial institutions from complying with existing anti-money laundering, counterterrorism and proliferation financing regulations.

Banks and other BSP-supervised financial institutions must continue applying customer due diligence and enhanced verification measures when transactions involve unusual activity, significant changes in customer profiles or other suspicious indicators.

They must also maintain effective transaction monitoring systems and file suspicious transaction reports whenever required under existing regulations.

The BSP directed financial institutions to “exercise appropriate vigilance while ensuring that legitimate transactions are not unnecessarily impeded.”

The temporary relief comes months after the BSP doubled the EDD threshold to P1 million from P500,000 under Circular 1230.

The adjustment followed consultations with banks and industry groups, which found that many legitimate business transactions exceeded the original threshold, including payroll payments, loan releases and project-related disbursements.

The previous P500,000 threshold was introduced under Circular 1218 in September 2025 to strengthen safeguards against money laundering and other illicit financial activities involving cash.

The BSP had earlier clarified that the threshold was not an absolute withdrawal limit, as customers could still make larger cash withdrawals after satisfying the necessary verification requirements.

Financial institutions may also impose stricter internal limits based on their risk assessments and customers’ financial profiles.
