Finance minister remains in hospital, condition stable

TheTools · 04.11.2026 02:08 · source · permalink

"The minister remains under medical supervision for now. His condition is stable," his adviser told ELTA on Tuesday.

She said Valys felt unwell at work on Monday morning and consulted his family doctor, who referred him to a cardiologist. However, his condition later worsened, prompting him to go to the emergency department at Santaros Clinics.

"After assessing his condition, doctors decided to admit the minister to the Centre of Cardiology and Angiology," the adviser said.

According to Butkevičienė, the minister’s duties have been delegated to deputy ministers for the time being.

ELTA notes that on Monday evening it was reported that Valys had been kept at Santaros Clinics for observation on doctors’ advice, with a decision on further treatment expected on Tuesday morning.
