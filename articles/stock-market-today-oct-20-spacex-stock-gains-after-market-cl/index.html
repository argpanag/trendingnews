Stock Market Today, Oct. 20: SpaceX Stock Gains After Market Close on Q3 Earnings Report

TheTools · 20.10.2026 23:05 · source · permalink

Space Exploration Technologies(NASDAQ:SPCX), better known as SpaceX, a reusable rocket launch and satellite broadband services provider, closed at $147.79, down 0.11%, on Oct. 20. However, shares rebounded to $155.40, up 5.15%, in early after-hours trading as investors weighed an after-close Q3 report.

Trading volume reached 89.4 million shares, coming in nearly 5.5% below its three-month average of 94.5 million shares. SpaceX IPO'd in 2026 and has fallen 8% since going public.

How the markets moved today

S&P 500(SNPINDEX:^GSPC) closed at 7,713, up 0.28%, while the Nasdaq Composite(NASDAQINDEX:^IXIC) finished at 26,425, down 0.02%. Among aerospace and defense space rivals, Rocket Lab(NASDAQ:RKLB) closed at $50.90, down 0.90%, and AST SpaceMobile(NASDAQ:ASTS) closed at $41.13, down 1.77%, as satellite broadband and launch names tracked company-specific earnings and spectrum headlines.

What this means for investors

On Tuesday, Oct. 20, investors were focused on SpaceX's after-close Q3 report, with options trading earlier in the day implying an 8% swing with attention centered on $170 calls and $140 puts. The company announced earnings after the closing bell, delivering 161% year-over-year revenue growth to $14.6 billion in the third quarter.

SpaceX also notched its first revenue-generating Starship mission in Q3. Last week, it executed a successful classified national-security launch for the U.S. Space Force, reinforcing the company's launch cadence and government demand. This supports the valuation case for its launch business.

In its Super Intelligence division, where SpaceX seeks to capitalize on the artificial intelligence boom, the company produced sales of $7.6 billion, a substantial increase from the prior year's $813 million.

Moreover, the AI segment produced a profit in Q3 with operating income of $431 million. Not only is this an enormous improvement from 2025's loss from operations of $1.7 billion, it's also a dramatic turnaround from Q2's loss of $1.3 billion. The outstanding result demonstrates SpaceX's ability to grow AI operations profitably, which is critical since the company believes this area possesses its biggest market potential, calling it a $27 trillion opportunity.
