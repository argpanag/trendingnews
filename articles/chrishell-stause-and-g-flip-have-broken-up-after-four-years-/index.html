Former “Selling Sunset” star Chrishell Stause and musician G Flip are no longer together.

The daytime soap star–turned–celebrity real estate agent announced her breakup with the Australian musician in a tearful video shared Monday on Instagram.

“I have a pretty humbling update to share with you guys,” Stause says in the clip. “G and I are no longer together. We have agreed to treat this public separation with love and respect for each other. That’s not hard for me because I am in love with this person.”

She added that she didn’t “have all the information or the reasons” for the split but was sure she would learn “the lessons [she was] meant to” with a little bit of time. The breakup was reportedly initiated by G Flip.
