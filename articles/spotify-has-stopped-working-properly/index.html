Spotify has gone down in a major outage.

Users reported problems listening to music, instead seeing an error messaging indicating that “something went wrong”.

But the problem appears to be limited to only some users, and some parts of the app. Some found that playing music seemed to work, for instance, but searching for other songs or artists showed nothing or an error message.

Others found that the errors only appeared to occur on the mobile version of the app, while it loaded as normal on the desktop.

The problems began just before 3pm UK time, or 10am in New York, according to tracking website Down Detector.

”We’re aware of some issues right now and are checking them out!” the company said on its official page on X, formerly Twitter. Unlike many other large tech platforms, the company does not operate an official status page on its own website.
