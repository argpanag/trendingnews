JioStar secures broadcast rights for Abu Dhabi T10

TheTools · 26.10.2026 09:56 · source · permalink

 The Abu Dhabi T10 has announced the appointment of JioStar as its official broadcast partner.

As per the partnership, all 32 matches of the 2026 Abu Dhabi T10 will broadcast live across Star Sports’ extensive linear television network and streamed on JioHotstar.

Beyond live coverage, JioStar will also support the tournament with a range of enhanced fan engagement initiatives including exclusive content, behind-the-scenes access, and innovative digital features.

The tournament will take place from 7 November to 20 November at Abu Dhabi’s Zayed Cricket Stadium and will feature 32 matches across 13  playing days, including six triple-header days and seven double-header days.

Matt Boucher, CEO, Abu Dhabi T10; and CEO, ADCSH, said, “This partnership represents a special moment in the continued evolution of the Abu Dhabi T10. India has always been our biggest and most passionate market and collaborating with JioStar enables us to connect with an even broader audience through platforms renowned for delivering world-class cricket coverage. As we continue building the tournament’s next growth chapter under Abu Dhabi’s stewardship, expanding our broadcast footprint is central to our long-term strategy. We are committed to establishing the Abu Dhabi T10 as a leading sporting and entertainment product, and this partnership gives us an exceptional platform to inspire new fans while strengthening our connection with existing audiences across India and the subcontinent.”

Prashant Khanna, head – sports: rights and innovation, JioStar, said, “T10 has found its audience in India for its mile-a-minute format, and we are excited to bring that same energy to the Abu Dhabi T10. In a market that lives and breathes cricket like no other, JioStar’s unparalleled reach can take the event far and wide while pulling in a whole new set of fans along the way.”

“The partnership with JioStar is expected to significantly increase audience engagement in India while reinforcing the Abu Dhabi T10's position as a leading global cricket property. By combining elite international talent with world-class broadcast production, multilingual coverage and enhanced broadcast storytelling, the tournament is poised to reach its largest audience yet while further cementing Abu Dhabi's reputation as the global home of cricket's fastest format,” added Boucher.
