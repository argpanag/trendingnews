Red-hot Crew face playoff test from Inter Miami, Lionel Messi

The Columbus Crew are the hottest team entering the first round of the MLS playoffs and their momentum will be put to the test in a visit to Inter Miami on Saturday in the opener of a best-of-three series.

The Crew won eight of their final nine matches to climb from last place in the 15-team Eastern Conference on Sept. 1 to the No. 7 playoff seed in two-plus months. Their high level of play comes after a significant roster shuffle during the turnaround.

Inter Miami struggled down the stretch with a 1-1-2 mark to finish second in the East. The defending MLS Cup champions can be brilliant offensively but prone to defensive lapses, such as in a 3-3 tie against Charlotte FC on the last day of the season after blowing a three-goal lead.

Inter Miami appear vulnerable, except they do have No. 10.

The incomparable Lionel Messi won the MLS Golden Boot for the second straight season with 26 goals and finished tied for second in assists with 17.

“They’re a team that likes to have the ball. They like to break you down,” Crew goalkeeper Patrick Schulte said. “They combine very well — one-twos, give-and-gos. They try to get behind you and exploit the open space.

“They also have No. 10, so that definitely helps. Messi is like a professor. If you slip up one time, it could be costly.”

The Crew like their chances after defeating Miami 2-1 at home on Sept. 27 in a fiery match that included a masterpiece Messi free-kick goal.

“Everybody wants to beat us, regardless,” Miami forward Yannick Bright said. “So it’s always a motivation but we know that teams are going to come out even stronger.

“The team feels good and we are ready. We have to play every game the way we know how.”

While the Crew were left for dead two months ago, coach Laurent Courtois made it clear this week that they are not satisfied just making the playoffs.

“Job’s not done,” Courtois said. “No, it’s incredible what they accomplished, but everyone’s looking at me like, ‘What’s next?’ They want to move forward. They’re competitors and they want more.”

The second game of the series is set for the day after Thanksgiving at Columbus. The deciding match, if necessary, is Dec. 2 in Miami.
