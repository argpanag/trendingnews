Drone strike targets gas field, disrupting electricity supplies in northern Iraq

TheTools · 09.11.2026 00:44 · source · permalink

Reporting the Changing World

Anadolu Ajansı © 2026

A drone strike targeted the Khor Mor gas field in Iraq’s Kurdistan region late Sunday, cutting gas supplies to power plants and sharply reducing electricity output, regional authorities said.

The regional electricity ministry said about 2,800 megawatts of generating capacity had been knocked offline following the disruption.

Initial reports indicated extensive damage and a halt in production at the field, located in Chamchamal district of Sulaymaniyah province.

Iraqi Prime Minister Ali Falih al-Zaidi ordered a joint investigation with authorities in the Kurdish region, the country’s Security Media Cell said.

It said no casualties had been reported, citing preliminary information.
