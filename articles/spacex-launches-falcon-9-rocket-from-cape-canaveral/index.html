SpaceX launches Falcon 9 rocket from Cape Canaveral

TheTools · 13.09.2026 18:47 · source · permalink

SpaceX’s Falcon 9 targeted a launch of the SES O3b mPOWER mission to medium Earth orbit on Sunday.

The rocket lifted off at 2:49 p.m. from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station.

The launch window was 87 minutes. If needed, a backup opportunity was also available Monday, Sept. 14 at the same time.

This was the 29th flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission.

Following stage separation, the first stage successfully landed on the A Shortfall of Gravitas droneship, which was stationed in the Atlantic Ocean.
