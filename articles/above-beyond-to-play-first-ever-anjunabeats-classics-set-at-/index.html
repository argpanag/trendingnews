Above & Beyond to Play First-Ever 'Anjunabeats Classics' Set at Dreamstate Festival

TheTools · 19.11.2026 23:37 · source · permalink

More than 25 years after founding Anjunabeats, the storied trio is dedicating an entire festival performance to the records that helped turn their independent label into a defining force in trance music.

Above & Beyond are opening the vault on more than two decades of dance music history.

The iconic trance trio will perform their first-ever ‘Anjunabeats Classics’ set Saturday, November 21 at Dreamstate SoCal, dedicating a full performance to the catalog that helped establish their influential independent label.

Jono Grant, Paavo Siljamäki and Tony McGuinness founded Anjunabeats in 2000. Over the following 25 years, the imprint became a major force in trance and progressive electronic music, introducing a generation of fans and music producers to a sound that helped shape the contours of those genres. Its catalog has since expanded across thousands of releases, while Above & Beyond have continued to develop their own sound through studio albums and global tours.

The Dreamstate appearance marks the first time the trio have assembled a full set around the label’s musical history, according to a press release. The set will revisit tracks from Anjunabeats’ earlier years, offering longtime followers an opportunity to hear formative releases in a festival setting and introducing newer fans to the music behind the label’s rise.

Dreamstate, the largest dedicated trance festival in the nation, provides a fitting backdrop for the retrospective. Above & Beyond are joined on this year’s lineup by Infected Mushroom, Dash Berlin, Solarstone and MarLo, among other must-see acts.

The first-ever ‘Anjunabeats Classics’ set takes place November 21 at Dreamstate SoCal 2026. Tickets to the festival are available here.
