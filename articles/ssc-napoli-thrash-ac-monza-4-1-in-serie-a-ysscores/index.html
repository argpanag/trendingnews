SSC Napoli thrash AC Monza 4-1 in Serie A | YSscores

TheTools · 28.10.2026 21:44 · source · permalink

SSC Napoli secured a big win against AC Monza, winning 4-1 in their Serie A match. The team delivered a strong attacking performance and successfully clinched the encounter in their favour.

Rasmus Højlund opened the scoring for SSC Napoli in the 10th minute, before Gustavo Faria equalised for AC Monza in the 43rd minute, leaving the first half tied at one goal apiece.

With the start of the second half, SSC Napoli regained their lead through Matteo Politano in the 49th minute, before Rafa Marín added the third goal in the 62nd minute, allowing the home side to maintain their dominance throughout the match.

Kevin De Bruyne capped off Napoli's goal fest with a fourth goal in the 68th minute, confirming his team's dominance and sealing a 4-1 victory. This emphatic win boosts Napoli's ambitions to compete for the top spots in the Serie A table.

SSC Napoli has increased its tally to 14 points in sixth place after playing 9 matches, achieving 4 wins, 2 draws and 3 losses.

In contrast, AC Monza's tally remains at 7 points in 16th place, having suffered their sixth defeat of the season, alongside two wins and one draw from 9 matches.
