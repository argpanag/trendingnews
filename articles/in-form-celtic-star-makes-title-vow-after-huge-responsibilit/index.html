In-form Celtic star makes title vow after ‘huge responsibility’ admission

TheTools · 25.10.2026 13:03 · source · permalink

Camilo Duran says Celtic "can't afford" to drop points against Hibernian.

The Colombian striker is preparing for his first visit to Easter Road since joining Celtic and has made it clear that a win is the only target for Martin O'Neill's side.

Celtic return to league action following their 2-1 Europa League defeat to Celta Vigo on Thursday night.

Duran said the focus has quickly shifted towards Hibs. He told CelticPlayer: "We couldn’t get the three points we wanted, but now we have to focus on Sunday.

"We want to be champions, so we can’t afford to drop points. We’ll go out there looking for all three points."
