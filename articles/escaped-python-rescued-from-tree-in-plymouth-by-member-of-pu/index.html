Escaped python rescued from tree in Plymouth by member of public

TheTools · 24.09.2026 22:07 · source · permalink

The snake was retrieved from a tree

  • Published
    8 hours ago

An escaped python has been plucked "successfully" from a tree by a member of the public, say firefighters.

The snake, thought to be a carpet python which is non-venomous, was retrieved at about 10:00 BST from a tree at Plymouth Hoe near the seafront, according to Devon and Somerset Fire and Rescue Service, which was alerted on Wednesday.

"The person had used a ladder to climb a tree the snake was located in and took it down. The snake has been sent to the local vets," a spokesperson for the fire service said.

Crews have been stood down.

Firefighters took part in the rescue operation on Wednesday
