SPCX, ANT, SKHY Trade Below IPO Prices as Coinbase CEO Says Listings Were ‘Not So Great’ for Average Investors, Calls for Private-Market Access

TheTools · 25.11.2026 22:14 · source · permalink

Space Exploration Technologies Corp. SPCX, Anthropic PBC ANT and SK Hynix Inc. ADR SKHY are trading below their U.S. offering prices, as Coinbase Global Inc. COIN CEO Brian Armstrong said the 2026 IPOs were “not so great” for average investors and urged access to private markets.

Armstrong Calls For Private-Market Access

In a Wednesday post on X, Armstrong described the three offerings as the largest IPOs in history and said they benefited the companies and investors who owned shares before the listings.

He called the outcome “not so great for average investors who can only buy at IPO prices.”

“It’s time to update accredited investor laws,” Armstrong wrote, calling for average investors to access private markets if they “pass a financial literacy test, instead of using a financial means test.”

His remarks focused on opportunities available before companies go public, rather than attributing the subsequent stock declines to restrictions on private investing.

Three Offerings Trade Below Issue Prices

SpaceX priced its June IPO at $135 per share, according to its offering announcement. Shares closed Tuesday at $125.81, a 6.81% discount to the issue price.

Anthropic priced its November offering at $83 per share, according to the company. Shares ended Tuesday at $81.91, or 1.31% below the offer.

SK hynix’s July U.S. offering priced American depositary shares at $149 each, its SEC prospectus showed. The securities closed Tuesday at $111.98, 24.85% below the offering price.

SK hynix was already listed in South Korea, meaning the U.S. ADS transaction was not its first stock-market listing.

Offering Prices Versus Retail Entry Prices

These comparisons represent hypothetical returns for investors who obtained shares at the official offering price and held through Tuesday, excluding transaction costs.

They do not establish how many retail investors received an IPO allocation or bought after public trading began.

Benzinga reported SpaceX opened at $150, while SK hynix’s U.S. depositary shares opened at $170. Anthropic began public trading at $111.50, according to its first-day market data.

Investors buying at those opening prices and retaining their shares faced larger unrealized losses than investors allotted shares at the offering prices.

How Have Newly Listed SPCX, SKHY and ANT Performed?

Shares of SPCX have fallen by 16.13% since its listing in June this year. It was down 14.70% over the last month and closed 5.65% lower at $125.81 on Tuesday.

Similarly, SKHY dropped 34.13% since its listing in July. It was down 18.27% over the last month and closed 9.90% lower at $111.98 per share on Tuesday.

Additionally, ANT was down 26.54% since its listing last week, and it closed 6.54% lower at $81.91 on Tuesday.
