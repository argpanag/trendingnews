Two Toyotas, three titles to settle: When and where to watch the WEC finale at Monza | GRANDE PRÊMIO

TheTools · 08.11.2026 03:00 · source · permalink

The 6 Hours of Monza starts at 11:00 local time on Sunday, 8 November. FIAWEC+ carries the full race live, with HBO Max in the United States, TNT Sports in the United Kingdom and Stan Sport in Australia among the local options.

News Summary

  • Sunday’s race starts at 10:00 GMT in the UK, 05:00 ET in the US and Canada, and 15:30 IST in India.
  • Australian viewers can watch the start at 21:00 AEDT in Sydney and Melbourne. The race begins at 23:00 NZDT in New Zealand.
  • Toyota’s two crews are separated by 17 points in the Hypercar drivers’ standings, and both LMGT3 titles are still up for grabs.

The 6 Hours of Monza closes the 2026 FIA World Endurance Championship on Sunday, 8 November, with the race starting at 11:00 CET. Viewers in North America will need an early start, while fans in eastern Australia can catch the opening stint on Sunday evening. In New Zealand, the race starts at 23:00 and runs into Monday morning.

FIAWEC+ is the championship’s official streaming service and carries the full six hours live. Its event pass also gives viewers access to Saturday’s qualifying and Hyperpole, alongside onboard cameras and replays.

What time does the 6 Hours of Monza start?

The table shows the race start. Local broadcasts may begin earlier. In the UK, for example, the TV listing puts coverage at 09:30 GMT, half an hour before the start.

The race is scheduled to finish at about 17:00 CET, or 16:00 GMT in the UK and 11:00 EST in eastern North America. That puts the finish at approximately 03:00 AEDT on Monday in Sydney and 05:00 NZDT in Auckland.

Where to watch the 6 Hours of Monza live

US viewers looking for the TNT Sports feed should go to HBO Max. The US rights deal puts all eight WEC races on the streaming service, with full-race coverage on truTV reserved for Le Mans, São Paulo and Austin. In the UK, TNT Sports streaming moved to HBO Max in March.

When is Monza qualifying and Hyperpole?

Qualifying takes place on Saturday, 7 November, with LMGT3 running first. Each class has its own qualifying session and its own Hyperpole shootout for the top 10.

Which WEC titles will be decided at Monza?

Toyota clinched the Hypercar manufacturers’ title with a one-two in Barcelona. At Monza, the contest is between its two crews. The #8 Toyota of Sébastien Buemi, Brendon Hartley and Ryo Hirakawa leads with 114 points, while the #7 crew of Mike Conway, Kamui Kobayashi and Nyck de Vries has 97. Kobayashi has said the team will let the two cars race without team orders. It will also be Conway’s final professional race.

In LMGT3, both the drivers’ and teams’ titles remain open. The #33 TF Sport Corvette leads the teams’ standings on 92 points, ahead of the #92 Manthey Porsche on 74, the #34 Corvette on 70 and the #69 Team WRT BMW on 66. The Porsche’s weekend got off to a difficult start when a drivetrain problem restricted it to a late run in Friday’s opening practice.
