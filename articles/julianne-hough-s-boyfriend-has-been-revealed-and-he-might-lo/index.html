Julianne Hough’s Boyfriend Has Been Revealed and He Might Look Very Familiar

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:02 · source · permalink

Julianne Hough is officially dating! At least according to E! News, which—on the eve of the Dancing with the Stars finale—went ahead and revealed that the host is in a relationship with Peloton instructor Adrian Williams.

The pair have already soft launched on Instagram Stories—and by that I mean Adrian posted a photo of Julianne holding his dog, which she went ahead and shared to her own account. Oh, and Adrian was at Disney night!

Meanwhile, back in September Julianne told E!, "I might not be single," before cryptically adding "There's a lot to discuss off-camera."

The professional dancer also told the outlet that when it comes to dating post-divorce from Brooks Laich, "It's all energy-based. I don't really have a type. It's more connection, and that's it. I've been very intentional about what I'm putting out and what I'm excited to attract and hopefully be attractive to, but I will say that I kind of just threw that out the window. It brought me back to the joyful, playful side, versus being so serious. Sometimes, when you're intentional, you can get a little bit tunnel vision, and so, I kind of was like, 'You know what? I'm just gonna be playful and fun.'"

So, will Adrian show up in the Dancing With the Stars audience to support Julianne tonight? Tune in to find out at 8 p.m. ET, and reminder that the show is airing its finale live on both the East Coast AND the West Coast, for the first time literally ever!
