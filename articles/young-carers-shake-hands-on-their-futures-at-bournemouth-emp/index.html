Young carers shake hands on their futures at Bournemouth employability summit

TheTools · 15.11.2026 21:31 · source · permalink

A handshake challenge set the tone for a day of ambition and opportunity.

More than 100 young carers from over 10 Dorset schools and around 30 employers attended an Employability Summit held at Bournemouth University on Wednesday, November 4.

The event, hosted by the charity MYTIME Young Carers in partnership with Bournemouth University, Dorset Chamber, and Dorset Careers Hub, aimed to break down barriers to work experience and future careers.

Krista Cartlidge, CEO of MYTIME Young Carers, said: "This was one of the best days in MYTIME's history.

"Young carers are some of Dorset's most overlooked talent, but too often they don't get the chance to show what they can do.

"Watching them walk up to employer after employer and introduce themselves with such confidence showed exactly what's possible when that door is opened."

The summit included a careers fair where young carers were encouraged to greet employers with a handshake, make eye contact, and introduce themselves.

Exhibitors included the British Army, Dorset County Hospital NHS Foundation Trust, AFC Bournemouth Community Sports Trust, and Marsham Court Hotel.

Employers also took part in a session on creating carer-friendly workplaces.

Each organisation was asked to offer opportunities for young carers, with MYTIME matching young people to placements in the weeks ahead.
