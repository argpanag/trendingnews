Gazzetta: Amorim fires up his team against Frosinone and wants goals from Ramos

TheTools · 22.11.2026 07:52 · source · permalink

The objective is to prevent the break from becoming difficult to digest. As happened after the September-October break, when Milan drew away at Sassuolo. This time, the opponent, with all due respect to the Neroverdi, will be even tougher, and Amorim knows it. For this reason, he has demanded maximum attention from his men and has fired up Gonçalo Ramos, who has scored twice so far in the first eleven league matchdays.

SOS striker...

The first problem to solve for the Portuguese player is that of shooting: in four matches, he did not take a single shot (against Juventus, Lecce, Bologna and Inter), while in two (against Lazio and Genoa), he did not hit the target, as pointed out via Gazzetta dello Sport in this morning's print edition.

Far too little for a striker who cost 70 million plus bonuses and commissions. The blame, according to the former United coach, however, does not lie with Gonçalo. “Having another striker to lend a hand to Ramos and Camarda would indeed be an additional option, but our problem is that we do not reach the final 30 metres, that we are among the worst teams in Serie A in terms of shots and chances created. As for Gonçalo, we do not put him in a position to score. Cisse gets into the penalty area with his forward runs, while Ramos needs build-up play. In Genoa, we scored the two goals thanks to him. And we must give him a hand with more passing and passing triangles from the attacking third onwards. My objective is to win matches, helping the players give their best. We bought Ramos to score goals, but for now he is helping me more than I am helping him.” Honest words in defence of the striker he wanted.

Adjustments...

Compared with the victory in Genoa two Sundays ago, Amorim is inclined not to field four attacking players from the very first minute, namely Moreira, Cisse, Pulisic and Ramos. In yesterday's training exercises, Cisse was left out of the starting lineup, where Musah and Modric were in midfield, with Rabiot pushed forward behind Gonçalo. Pavlovic has been called up and would like to be on the pitch from the first minute, but he is not at his best.

"The choice of formation depends on the opponent and on the team's current situation. I do not think that scoring or winning more is linked to fielding more attacking players. Frosinone are enjoyable to watch and make speed their main weapon. We are the second team in Serie A in terms of number of sprints, and we have the weapons to counter both their speed and their one-on-one play on the wings. We will need to be careful because they bring more players than we do into the final third of the pitch and are better than us when they win the ball back. My assessment of our league campaign so far? I am not happy with fourth place or with all the performances we have produced. We must play better and do more in Europe, but we have grown as a team and in our attitude. We must continue like this."

The article ends with a transfer-market dream ahead of Christmas: "Signings in January are made not by looking at the league standings, but by following long-term planning. The Christmas dream I have is to win the next seven matches and get our injured players back. That is what I would like."
