MATCH RECAP, pres. by Provident Bank: Red Bull New York Fall to Revolution | New York Red Bulls

TheTools · 29.10.2026 02:23 · source · permalink

Director of Communications

Red Bull New York battled back from a two-goal defecit, but couldn't hold on, eventually falling 3-2 to the New England Revolution.

Cade Cowell and Julian Hall scored for Red Bull.

Adri Mehmeti assisted on both goals and became the youngest player in RBNY history to have two single match assists at 17 years and 205 days old.

He broke Julian Hall's record, who did so at 18 years and 18 days old on April 11, 2026, against Inter Miami CF.

Ronald Donkor and Dylan Nealis recieved yellow cards in the match and will both be suspended for RBNY's next match.

New York will make their second stop on a two-match road trip, as they travel to GEODIS Park to face Nashville SC on Sunday, November 1. 

Kickoff is set for 4:30 p.m. ET with coverage on Apple TV in English and Spanish, and Red Bulls Radio in English via the Red Bull New York app and RedBullNewYork.com.
