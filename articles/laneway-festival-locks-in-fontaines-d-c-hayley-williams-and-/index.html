Laneway Festival locks in Fontaines D.C., Hayley Williams and more - triple j

TheTools · 29.09.2026 22:10 · source · permalink

It's time – you've been waiting all year and it's finally here. Your Laneway Festival 2027 line-up has landed and it's an absolute pearler.

Returning for its 22nd year, the annual late summer festival has finely tuned and perfectly plucked a selection of acts from big hitters to your-new-favourite-band.

Post-punk Dubliners Fontaines D.C. are set to return with a shift to the main stage, while The Hayley Williams Show is bringing the Paramore vocalist's solo project to Australia for the first time.

Slayyyter simply cannot keep away from us, coming back for a second time in six months for her Laneway Festival debut. The pop heads are going to be eating well in 2027, as ADÉLA, Kim Petras, MUNA and Rachel Chinouriri bulk out the line-up.

For those who love to get their sweat on, the electronic artist offerings at Laneway Festival 2027 are the cream of the crop. Expect absolute heaters from the likes of Ninajirachi, HorsegiirL, Rommulas, PC Music pioneer Danny L Harle, Daine, Jane Remover and Tiffany Day pumping through the speakers.

That's not all! Across Laneway Festival you can also catch red-hot sets from artists like boisterous rapper Rico Nasty, shoegaze icons DIIV, pop auters Automatic, New Zealand hooky duo Balu Brigada, and a little-known name: Violet Grohl.

Still hungry? How about you try and squeeze in some performances from local faves Ecca Vandal, Public Figures, Swapmeet and Trials? Or perhaps a suite of Irish breakouts like Bleech 9:3, Florence Road and Gurriers?

And if that's not enough, 1TBSP and his live-coding genius mate DJ_Dave will be sound-tracking the day between sets.

Jeez, we're truly spoilt for choice with Laneway Festival in 2027.

For our friends in Adelaide and Perth, you're not forgotten! Although Laneway can't make it to you this time around, a run of sideshows from Fontaines D.C., Jane Remover and Tiffany Day are coming for you. 

We'll bring you more details about those gigs when we can.

We know the group chat is absolutely fanging to get out of the chat and into the pit. Tickets for Laneway Festival are on sale at staggered times on Thursday 8 October, with a presale happening the day beforehand. Check out the Laneway Festival website for more information.

Get right around the full line-up, dates and details below, and we'll catch you on the barrier.

Laneway Festival 2027

  • Friday 12 February - Centennial Park, Gadigal Bidjigal Land, Sydney NSW
  • Saturday 13 February - Brisbane Showgrounds, Turrbal Targun Land, Brisbane QLD
  • Friday 19 February - Flemington Park, Wurundkeri Biik Land, Melbourne VIC
  • Sunday 21 February - Western Springs, Tāmaki Makaurau, Auckland NZ
