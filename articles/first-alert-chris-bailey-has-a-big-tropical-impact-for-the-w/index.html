FIRST ALERT | Chris Bailey has a big tropical impact for the weekend

TheTools · 08.10.2026 18:26 · source · permalink

LEXINGTON, Ky. (WKYT) - The focus of the forecast continues to be on a Gulf tropical system that will impact Kentucky this weekend. What’s left of Isaias will bring heavy rain, a few strong storms and gusty winds to the Commonwealth.

Here’s a breakdown of what it means for us this weekend:

  • Spiraling bands of showers and some storms arrive from south to north Saturday afternoon and evening.
  • This action increases during the evening and overnight as the threat for a few storms increases.
  • Winds will increase as well with gusts Saturday evening and night topping 30-40mph at times.
  • The remnant low enters southwestern Kentucky late Saturday night then rolls east across the state Sunday.
  • More in the way of spiraling bands of showers and storms spread east along and ahead of this low.
  • We will need to watch for a few strong or locally severe storms to fire up during this time.
  • Areas of 1″-3″ rains may show up this weekend, with the opportunity for a little more in a few spots.
  • Local flash flooding issues can’t be ruled out.
  • This system moves way Sunday night and early Monday, taking any lingering showers with it.
