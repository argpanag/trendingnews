Airbus Completes Commissioning Flights of A320 Flying Radar Lab With Eurofighter Nose

TheTools · 08.11.2026 08:21 · source · permalink

Airbus has completed commissioning flights of its A320 ATRA flying laboratory, which will test next-generation radar systems for Eurofighter fighters.

The company announced this.

The aircraft now has a pointed radome, similar to the one used on fighter jets. The Eurofighter Common Radar System Mk1 (ECRS Mk1) is mounted beneath it.

The tests are being conducted ahead of the new radar system’s planned installation in 2027 on Eurofighter Typhoon fighters intended for Spain and Germany.

“The upcoming Eurofighters under Germany’s Quadriga and Tranche 5 programs, as well as those under Spain’s Halcón program, will be equipped with a new radar called the Eurofighter Common Radar System Mk1,” said Erik Lorch, head of the E-Scan Mk1 program.

The new Mk1 radar provides a wide 180-degree field of view and high-frequency agility, allowing it to detect and track large numbers of targets much faster. Its capabilities also include ultra-high-resolution synthetic aperture radar (SAR) imaging of ground and surface targets, as well as specialized air-to-ground modes for monitoring moving objects.

To accommodate the Eurofighter nose section, engineers reinforced the A320’s fuselage and laid hundreds of meters of data cables. They also installed a liquid-and-air cooling system to regulate the radar’s temperature. Several workstations were set up in the cabin, allowing specialists to access the necessary information in real time.
