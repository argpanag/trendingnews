Al Ahly face Smouha as Saudi League provides Thursday football schedule

TheTools · 03.09.2026 09:36 · source · permalink

A number of matches are scheduled across competitions on Thursday, 3 September 2026, with Al Ahly’s meeting with Smouha the main fixture in the Egyptian League, while three Saudi League games are also due to take place.

Al Ahly will play Smouha in the third round of the Egyptian League. The match is scheduled to begin at 20:00 and will be broadcast on On Sport.

The day’s fixtures include four matches in total, with the Saudi League programme beginning before the Egyptian game and continuing into the evening. The following schedule lists the kick-off times and broadcast channels for each match.

Egyptian League

Al Ahly v Smouha Kick-off: 20:00 Broadcast: On Sport

Al Ahly’s game against Smouha is the only fixture listed in the Egyptian League schedule for Thursday. It forms part of the competition’s third round and is set to take place at 20:00.

Saudi League

Al Khaleej v Neom Kick-off: 19:30 Broadcast: Thmanyah 3

The Saudi League schedule begins with Al Khaleej against Neom at 19:30. The match will be shown on Thmanyah 3.

Al Fayha v Al Kholood Kick-off: 19:05 Broadcast: On Sport

Al Fayha will meet Al Kholood in a match listed for 19:05. Coverage will be available on On Sport.

Al-Diriyah v Al Qadsiah Kick-off: 21:00 Broadcast: Thmanyah 1

The latest match in the Saudi League programme is Al-Diriyah’s fixture against Al Qadsiah, which is scheduled for 21:00 and will be broadcast on Thmanyah 1.

The listed matches offer a full evening of football on Thursday, with fixtures from the Egyptian League and Saudi League spread across the schedule. Fans can follow Al Ahly’s meeting with Smouha at 20:00, alongside the three Saudi League games featuring Al Khaleej, Neom, Al Fayha, Al Kholood, Al-Diriyah and Al Qadsiah.
