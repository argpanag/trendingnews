GAA club send best wishes to former star who captains Ireland in Nations League

TheTools · 24.09.2026 14:57 · source · permalink

Dara O'Shea will captain Ireland for the first time tonight as they face Kosovo in Pristina in the Nations League.

It's been quite the sporting journey for a ultra-talented all-rounder, who shone on the GAA fields of Dublin before focusing on his soccer career.

Ahead of arguably the proudest moment of his life in leading out his country, O'Shea has been sent a supportive message from his former GAA club.

The St Jude's club stated: "Congratulations and the best of luck to our former player Dara O’Shea who will captain the Irish soccer team in tonight’s game v Kosovo for the first time!"

The Templeogue club shared a picture of O'Shea as a young teenager, picking up a trophy for the club.

As well as starring for his club, O'Shea famously appeared in a Cumann na mBunscol final for St. Pius X school in Croke Park.

The current Ipswich Town defender attracted the attention of St Kevin's Boys as a child, the famous Dublin schoolboy club who count the likes of Liam Brady, Jeff Hendrick, and Robbie Brady amongst their past players.

St Kevin's PRO Neil Fox told Balls.ie that O'Shea was a fairly quiet character, doing his talking on the pitch.

"He had a bit of everything. He could play football, was good in the air. Even for a kid he had a bit of height as well," said Fox.

"He’s a good all-rounder and I think with his Gaelic background as well that would have helped him as well. He trained well, played well, and a great lad all-round."
