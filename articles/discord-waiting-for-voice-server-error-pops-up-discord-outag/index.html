Discord waiting for voice server error pops up. Discord outage impacts calls

TheTools · 11.09.2026 05:49 · source · permalink

Discord users could find some trouble Friday morning.

According to DownDetector, some are unable to get on the free voice, video and text service. The issues popped up at 12 a.m. EST and had about 1,400 reports as of 12:30 a.m.. Discord outage was trending on Google, as was "discord waiting for voice server."

According to Downdetector, the biggest problem was with making voice calls. That's where more than 90 percent of the issues are.

There is no timetable for when the issues will be resolved. It could be a quick fix, as these often are.
