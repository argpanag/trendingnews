Texans' Henry To'oTo'o: Back in action in Week 6

TheTools · 18.10.2026 14:21 · source · permalink

To'oTo'o (shoulder) is active for Sunday's game against the Jaguars in London.

The 25-year-old linebacker had been listed as questionable heading into Sunday's contest, but after being activated from injured reserve Saturday, the writing was on the wall for him to suit up in London. To'oTo'o will be suiting up for the first time since the Texans' Week 1 loss to the Bills, when he exited in the fourth quarter after injuring his shoulder.
