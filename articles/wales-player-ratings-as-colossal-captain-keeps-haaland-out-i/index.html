Wales player ratings as colossal captain keeps Haaland out in Norway win

TheTools · 01.10.2026 21:10 · source · permalink

Wales secured their first win of 2026 by claiming a superb 2-1 victory over Norway in the UEFA Nations League.

Daniel James popped up to cancel out Oscar Bobb's early opener, before Torbjorn Heggem's red card tipped the contest in favour of the hosts, who took the lead through Neco Williams from the resulting freekick.

It was a terrific night for Wales. Here's how the players rated.

Danny Ward 7

Wasn't perhaps as busy as many might have been expecting, but did really well when called upon. A solid outing.

Sorba Thomas 9

His side's most creative outlet by far. Put cross after cross into the box, many of which should really have been converted. Did get an assist when Daniel James scored just before half-time. A pain in Norway's side all night.

Neco Williams 8

Superb freekick to win it for his side. Made more tackles than any other player and was a key component in his team's efforts going forward.

Ben Cabango 9

Solid all night. Competed with a gigantic Norway forward line brilliantly. No player won more aerial duels on the night.

Ben Davies 9 - STAR MAN

As above in many ways. Kept Norway quiet all evening, including Erling Haaland, who simply couldn't quite get the better of him. A colossal performance.

Jay DaSilva 7

Solid presence out wide and made a couple of good runs. Did his job defensively and had a role going forward too. Accomplished.

Daniel James 8

Pace caused problems for Norway all evening. Decision-making wasn't always the best, but he was still a constant threat. Took his goal well and won the red card after the break. Subbed.

Jordan James 8

Calm and collected in the middle of the park. Kept things ticking over really well, and was often at the heart of his team's most promising moves. Subbed.

Ethan Ampadu 7

Looked a little shaky at first, and was a little untidy in possession. However, he grew into the game really well and led by example in the middle as Norway pushed for an equaliser.

Liam Cullen 7

Pressed really well out of possession to unsettle the Norwegian backline. Denied a goal in the first half, but still a good showing. Subbed.

Lewis Koumas 8

Just a nuisance for Norway all night. Performance deserved a bit more, but played his part in the opening goal. Subbed.
