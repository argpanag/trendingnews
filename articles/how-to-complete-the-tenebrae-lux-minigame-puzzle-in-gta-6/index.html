How to Complete the Tenebrae Lux Minigame Puzzle in GTA 6

TheTools · 18.11.2026 15:29 · source · permalink

Tenebrae Lux is the first minigame you will play in GTA 6. Rockstar’s action title isn’t all about shooting and killing, and this little teaser requires you to complete a very basic puzzle to progress through the GTA 6 campaign. It’s not difficult, but here’s a quick guide to completing the Tenebrae Lux puzzle.

Once you reach a certain point in GTA 6, your character will reel off a bunch of somewhat morbid (but hilarious) dialogue as your character “wakes up” and finds their legs. From there, you’ll need to head to the hut on the left-hand side in the distance, and this is where you’ll find the first GTA 6 minigame, which looks somewhat like a chessboard, but with bigger squares.

How to Complete the Tenebrae Lux Minigame

Completing the first minigame in Tenebrae Lux is relatively straightforward and requires you to destroy all the objects on the board.

You’ve likely found this guide because you’re destroying some objects, but not all of them.

Here’s a quick guide with the right steps to destroy all the objects on the board:

  1. Make sure it’s a new game with all six objects on the board—consisting of four towers and two flat pieces.
  2. Move the bottom tower in the left corner upwards; this will destroy two towers and one flat piece.
  3. Now position the middle tower downwards to destroy the remaining flat piece.
  4. Finally, move the top-right tower down to destroy all the towers on the board.
  5. This should take three moves in total, and once done, you’ll be able to progress through the GTA 6 campaign again!

If your board doesn’t look like the one above, you can also “complete” it by trying several times. After you fail the minigame puzzle several times, the next cutscene will automatically begin.

Just like that, you can get back to completing every chapter and story mission, as well as progressing through GTA 6’s MANY challenges.
