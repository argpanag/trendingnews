Skubal joining Mets for 9 years, $450M (source)

TheTools · 23.11.2026 00:10 · source · permalink

The Mets have landed this offseason's big prize.

Two-time Cy Young Award-winning left-hander Tarik Skubal and the Mets have agreed to a nine-year contract worth $450 million, a source told MLB.com's Mark Feinsand on Sunday afternoon.

The club has not confirmed the deal, which includes a full no-trade clause, no opt-out clauses and comes with a $45 million signing bonus, per Feinsand, and will set a new record for overall value of a pitching contract (topping Yoshinobu Yamamoto's $325M pact with the Dodgers) and average annual value of a deal for a pitcher ($43.33M for Justin Verlander, Max Scherzer).

  • Largest free-agent contracts in MLB history

Skubal, who turned 30 on Friday, reached free agency on the heels of an extraordinary four-year run from 2023-26, during which he recorded a 2.47 ERA, a 169 ERA+, a 2.42 FIP and a 6.94 K/BB ratio. The left-hander became the 12th pitcher to win the Cy Young Award in consecutive seasons when he earned American League honors in 2024 and ’25.

Skubal’s 2026 season hit a snag when he needed to undergo surgery to remove loose bodies from his left elbow in May. However, he was able to return more quickly than expected, opting for a less invasive NanoScope procedure that reduced his recovery time. He was back starting for the Tigers on June 13, roughly six weeks after his surgery.

With Detroit on the fringes of playoff contention and Skubal set to hit the open market at the end of the year, the club made the decision to trade its ace prior to the Trade Deadline. The Tigers shipped Skubal to the Dodgers for three prospects on Aug. 2.

Skubal ended up with a 2.36 ERA and a 6.32 K/BB over his final 16 starts -- including 10 for Los Angeles -- erasing any lingering concerns about his elbow before he entered free agency.

The Seattle University product proved to be a steal by the Tigers as a ninth-round Draft pick in 2018. He may have gone earlier, but he missed all of 2017 following Tommy John surgery and pitched to middling results in ’18.

After being drafted, Skubal quickly established himself as a prospect to watch in the Minors, though it took some time for him to find his ace form once he joined the Tigers in 2020.

Skubal showed signs of improvement in 2022, but his season came to an end prematurely when he underwent flexor tendon surgery in August. He didn’t return from the procedure until the following July, but he was dominant after coming off the IL, laying the groundwork for what was to come in 2024.

Skubal went on to win the AL pitching Triple Crown with 18 wins, a 2.39 ERA and 228 strikeouts in 2024 and was a unanimous choice as the league’s Cy Young winner. He led the AL in ERA again in 2025 (2.21) while striking out 241 batters over 195 1/3 innings to lock down another Cy Young.
