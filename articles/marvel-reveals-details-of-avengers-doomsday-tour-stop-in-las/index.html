Marvel reveals details of ‘Avengers: Doomsday’ tour stop in Las Vegas

TheTools · 22.11.2026 17:52 · source · permalink

Earlier this month, Las Vegas was listed by Marvel Studios as the first stop out of nine cities on the “Doomsday” Global Tour.

Well, that was underwhelming.

Marvel Studios made international headlines earlier this month by announcing the “Doomsday” Global Tour via social media, with Las Vegas listed as the first stop.

A countdown clock was placed over an image of Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom in “Avengers: Doomsday” as the names of nine cities flashed onscreen: Las Vegas, São Paulo, Gold Coast (Australia), Rome, London, Seoul, Shanghai, Toronto and Los Angeles.

Ahead of the release of “Avengers: Endgame” in 2019, Marvel deployed cast members to fan events in Mumbai, London, Seoul and Shanghai in the weeks leading up to the movie’s world premiere in Los Angeles.

That tour included red carpets, photo ops and interactions with cast members, leading to complete fan meltdowns.

There was no telling what was in store for Las Vegas. Literally, because zero information was available.

Late Saturday night, Marvel Studios took to its social media channels and posted a photo of three of the movie’s stars in front of the Strip skyline: “Off we go. Chris Evans, Anthony Mackie, and Simu Liu kick off the Avengers: Doomsday Global Tour at @F1Las Vegas Grand Prix 2026.”

The post also included photos of an “Avengers: Doomsday”-branded race car and air taxi, as well as a photo of a “Doomsday” spot shown on Sphere.

So, yeah. Three actors watched a Formula 1 race during the most celebrity-studded weekend of the year.

“Avengers: Doomsday” will open in theaters Dec. 18.
