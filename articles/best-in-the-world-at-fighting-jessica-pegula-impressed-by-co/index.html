“Best In The World At Fighting”: Jessica Pegula Impressed By Coco Gauff’s Fighting Spirit At WTA Finals

TheTools · 09.11.2026 12:18 · source · permalink

Jessica Pegula praised Coco Gauff’s fighting spirit after their three-set battle at the 2026 WTA Finals, calling her the best in the world at fighting for every ball.

Coco Gauff made a winning return to tennis at the 2026 WTA Finals, defeating fellow American Jessica Pegula 6-4, 6-7, 6-2 in a close match lasting two hours and 39 minutes. The world No. 4 had not played an official match for about a month after leaving the tour following the Beijing tournament because of general fatigue. Despite losing the second-set tie-break after a tough battle, Gauff stayed calm and took control of the deciding set. Pegula praised her opponent’s fighting spirit, while Gauff said staying focused helped her win.

Pegula Praises Gauff’s Fighting Spirit

Pegula said both players produced a high level of tennis, with their different styles making the match exciting. She tried to attack and move forward, while Gauff used her speed and strong defence to chase down balls and fight for every point.

“It was a really high-level match. It showed me trying to play aggressively and come forward, while she showed what she does best: playing great defence, making smart choices and competing really well,” Pegula said.

Pegula also admitted that the effort needed to win the second set affected her afterwards. “What she forced me to do to win the second set was the key to her dominating me easily afterward. I had to expend a huge amount of energy, and she knocked me out of my rhythm,” she said.

She also praised Gauff’s determination, saying she was “the best in the world at fighting for every ball, without thinking about the score.”

Gauff explained that staying mentally strong was important. “It was essential to stay focused. I didn’t let myself get carried away by either euphoria or discouragement. That’s where I made the difference to win a match of such quality. Mentally, I was able to make the difference.”

Gauff Looks Ahead to Her Next Match

Pegula also enjoyed the atmosphere, praising the packed stadium and support from both American crowds. She said the fans made the match more enjoyable and helped both players keep fighting.

She also noted that every game and set matters in the WTA Finals format, giving players another reason to keep competing until the end. Gauff will next face Linda Noskova, while Pegula meets Marta Kostyuk. Pegula acknowledged that Kostyuk had beaten her earlier this season but said she would try to turn that record around. She also wondered whether Kostyuk had been surprised by her place in the draw, but said she was ready for the challenge.
