THE TAKEAWAY

  • Tropical Depression Edouard is lashing Texas and Louisiana after making landfall Tuesday afternoon.
  • Edouard has unleashed heavy rain and flash flooding along the Gulf Coast, as well as in parts of southeast Texas and southwestern Louisiana.
  • Edouard strengthened from a Potential Tropical Cyclone to a full-blown tropical storm within the span of nine hours on Monday.

Tropical Depression Edouard is unleashing heavy rain and flash flooding in Texas and Louisiana, and will continue to do so through Wednesday, after making landfall Tuesday afternoon.

The tropical depression made landfall near Johnson Bayou, Louisiana, roughly 15 miles south-southeast of Port Arthur, Texas, at 3:20 p.m. EDT, packing maximum sustained winds of 60 mph and a minimum central pressure of 996 mb, according to the National Hurricane Center (NHC).

Edouard reached land while maintaining tropical storm status before weakening to a tropical depression Tuesday night.

According to atmospheric data collected by Air Force Hurricane Hunter aircraft, Edouard strengthened as it approached the Gulf coast, with maximum winds increasing in the hours before landfall.
