Taiwan issues sea warning for Typhoon Champi - Focus Taiwan

TheTools · 22.10.2026 01:00 · source · permalink

Taipei, Oct. 22 (CNA) The Central Weather Administration (CWA) issued a sea warning for Typhoon Champi late Wednesday, urging vessels operating in the Bashi Channel as well as waters east and north of Taiwan to remain on high alert.

Based on the typhoon's current projected path, the CWA said it could issue a land warning around Thursday noon.

As of 8 a.m. Thursday, Champi was located 560 kilometers east-southeast of Eluanbi, Taiwan's southernmost point, moving northwest at 13-18 kilometers per hour, CWA data showed.

The typhoon had a radius of 300 kilometers, with maximum sustained winds of 184 kph and gusts of up to 227 kph.

Long waves are likely Thursday along Keelung, the northern and eastern coasts, including Lanyu and Green Island, the Hengchun Peninsula and Matsu, the CWA said.

(By Y.F. Low)
