Liverpool transfer news: Bajetic rejoins Celta Vigo on permanent deal

TheTools · 03.09.2026 20:43 · source · permalink
  • Published
    31 August 2026

Liverpool midfielder Stefan Bajcetic has returned to Celta Vigo on a permanent deal, five-and-a-half years after leaving the La Liga side to join the Reds' academy

Bajcetic made his senior Liverpool debut under Jurgen Klopp as a 17-year-old in August 2022 and made 19 appearances across the 2022-23 season.

He scored his only goal in a 3-1 win at Aston Villa on Boxing Day 2022.

However, injuries limited the now 21-year-old's opportunities and he only made three more appearances for the club - spending time on loan at Red Bull Salzburg and Las Palmas during the 2024-25 campaign.
