Paris St-Germain 1-2 Monaco: PSG's poor start continues

TheTools · 04.09.2026 22:57 · source · permalink
  • Report (active)
  • Scores
  • Tables
  • Line-ups

  • Live Text

Monaco last won the Ligue 1 title in 2017

Champions Paris St-Germain are still searching for their first Ligue 1 win of the season after surrendering the lead to suffer a late defeat by Monaco.

The Champions League holders have now failed to win any of their three domestic fixtures after goals from Flavio Nazinho and Stanis Idumbo in the second half secured a rare win for the visitors at Parc des Princes.

PSG boss Luis Enrique, who has agreed a contract extension, watched his side take the lead after 31 minutes through a close-range header from captain Marquinhos, but they were unable to extend their advantage before the break despite Monaco failing to register a shot on target.

Monaco restored parity 13 minutes into the second half when Nazinho connected with Jordan Teze's cross at the back post.

Fourteen minutes later, the visitors moved ahead when Idumbo rifled the ball past Matvey Safonov at his near post.

It was the first time PSG had lost a game when leading at half-time since March 2021, when they were beaten by Nantes.

The result means Monaco move top of the table after maintaining their 100% start, while PSG fall to 12th.
