Get codes for exclusive Timed Research during the Pokémon Night Out Twitch broadcast! — Pokémon GO

TheTools · 03.10.2026 00:19 · source · permalink

Timed Research Codes via the Pokémon Twitch Live Stream

To celebrate the exciting Pokémon Night Out event taking place at Intuit Dome in Los Angeles, USA, on October 24, 2026, we’re excited to share that Trainers globally will have the opportunity to join in on the fun and receive codes for exclusive Timed Research by watching 30 minutes of the Pokémon Twitch livestream!

Completing this Timed Research will award Trainers with an exclusive Pokémon Night Out baseball jersey for their avatar.

How to Obtain and Redeem a Code

  1. Follow the official Pokémon Twitch channel.
    Make sure you’re following the official Pokémon Twitch channel so you’ll get a notification when the broadcast begins.
    Follow the link below to our channel. https://www.twitch.tv/pokemon
  1. Connect your Pokémon Trainer Central account to Twitch.
    Follow steps 1–3 on this FAQ page to link your Twitch account
  1. Watch the livestream.
    Be sure to catch the Pokémon Night Out livestream on October 24, 2026, for 30 minutes. (Please note that minimizing the stream will pause the Twitch Drop progress timer on Twitch.)
    The Pokémon GO Twitch livestream dates are as follows:
  • Saturday, October 24, 2026, starting at 7:30 p.m. PDT.
  1. Claim your Twitch Drop.
    Once you’ve watched a stream for 30 minutes, a pop-up in Twitch chat will prompt you to claim your Twitch Drop. You may also claim your drop by visiting the Twitch Drops Inventory Page here.
  1. Find your code.
    After you select Claim, go to rewards.pokemon.com and select View Inventory.
    Find the drop and select Show Code.*

*Trainers can only claim the Timed Research code once.

  1. Redeem your code.
    Copy the revealed code to your clipboard. Open the Offer Redemption page here to redeem your code.

Having trouble? Please refer to this article to learn more about redeeming codes.

Please be aware of your surroundings and follow guidelines from local health authorities when playing Pokémon GO. Upcoming events are subject to change. Be sure to follow us on social media, opt in to receiving push notifications, and subscribe to our emails to stay updated.

—The Pokémon GO team
