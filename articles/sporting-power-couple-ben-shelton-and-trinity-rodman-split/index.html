Sporting power couple Ben Shelton and Trinity Rodman SPLIT

TheTools · 24.11.2026 15:22 · source · permalink

Tennis star Ben Shelton and soccer sensation Trinity Rodman have split after almost two years together, the Daily Mail can exclusively reveal. 

The American sporting couple began dating around March 2025, frequently supporting one another at their various events, but have recently decided to call time on their relationship.

Their conflicting work schedules and constant travel made it too challenging for the couple to continue maintaining their relationship, the Daily Mail understands. However, the pair are believed to remain close, despite their split. 

The couple were frequently forced to maintain their romance over long distance with Shelton constantly traveling around the globe while playing on the ATP Tour. 

Despite multiple sacrifices and last-minute trips to attend each other's matches, it appears they could no longer make it work. 

The Daily Mail has reached out to both Shelton and Rodman's representatives for comment.

Shelton, 24, suffered defeat in the semi-final of the ATP Finals on Saturday, losing 3-6, 6-3, 7-6 (7-1) to Carlos Alcaraz in Turin, Italy. The same evening, Rodman, 24, and the Washington Spirit lost the NWSL final to the KC Current. 

The following evening, the American tennis star was spotted at Madison Square Garden for the New York Knicks' game against the Indiana Pacers without Rodman, instead sitting on Celebrity Row alongside a friend and actress Inde Navarette.

The couple first went public with their relationship in March 2025 following speculation from fans. 

Mystery surrounded the early stages of their relationship, as they initially dropped a playful hint as part of a TikTok trend last March then 'hard launched' their romance soon afterwards. 

Rodman was a near-permanent fixture in Shelton's box throughout the US Open in September. The USWNT star and the Spirit had played an NWSL game in Washington on the day of the final but Rodman left the capital almost immediately after the final whistle to make the mad dash to New York for Shelton's final. 

The forward was captured crying inside Arthur Ashe Stadium in the wake of her boyfriend's loss to Alexander Zverev. 

Rodman, the daughter of NBA legend Dennis Rodman, had cheered on the World No 4 at multiple other events, making her Wimbledon debut in May 2025. 

Meanwhile, the tennis superstar was every bit the supportive boyfriend when he consoled Rodman after her team lost the NWSL championship game last November.

The World No 4 attended the Knicks game on Sunday, one day after losing the ATP semifinal

The Washington Spirit were stunned by underdogs Gotham FC in California, as footage from the game showed Shelton consoling an emotional Rodman as she burst into tears on the sidelines.

Shelton opened up on the challenges of their relationship in September, admitting that it was tough for the couple to continuously make time for one another. 

'It is really difficult,' the Grand Slam finalist told US Weekly. 'I think what a lot of people don't see is when we actually do have time or a couple of days off, we're so exhausted that it's tough to do anything. It is tough to make time.'

He added: 'Sometimes I leave these tournaments and I'm a bit of a zombie. I'm not the most fun person to be around. But we make a lot of sacrifices for our sport. I'd say both of us are chasing things for our sport. We're not where we want to be yet, we're not in our primes. So this is kind of crunch time.

'This is going to be the hardest, most grueling time in our relationship, from my perspective. None of it is easy. But being able to have someone to lean on who's such an elite athlete and doing things at a really high level as well, it helps in so many ways.'
