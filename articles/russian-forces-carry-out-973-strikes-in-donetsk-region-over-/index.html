Russian forces carry out 973 strikes in Donetsk region over past 24 hours

TheTools · 10.09.2026 08:51 · source · permalink

The regional police reported this on Facebook, according to Ukrinform.

“On September 8, police recorded 973 Russian attacks along the front line and in residential areas of the region. A total of 105 civilian structures were damaged, including 103 residential buildings. Three settlements came under fire,” the statement said.

In particular, the Russians struck Sloviansk twice with “Smerch” multiple-launch rocket systems, damaging four private homes.

The enemy dropped three KAB-250 bombs on Bilenke, damaging 96 private homes.

In Petrivka Druha, three private homes and two outbuildings were damaged after being struck by a “Molniya-2” UAV.

In addition, at approximately 3:35 a.m. today, the Russians attacked Yasnohirka with a drone, damaging an outbuilding.

As previously reported, on September 7, Russian invaders killed two residents of the Donetsk region and wounded 10 others.
