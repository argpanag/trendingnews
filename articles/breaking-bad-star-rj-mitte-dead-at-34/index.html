UPDATE

1:42pm PT -- RJ's reps tell TMZ ... "It is with profound sadness we share the tragic passing of beloved actor, philanthropist, and passionate disability rights advocate RJ Mitte."

RJ Mitte -- who played Walter White Jr. on "Breaking Bad" -- is dead ... TMZ has learned.
