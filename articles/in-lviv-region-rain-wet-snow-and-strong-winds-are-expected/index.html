This is reported by the Lviv Regional Center for Hydrometeorology of Ukraine.

In particular, the amount of precipitation may range from 15 to 49 mm. During the night and morning hours in the southern part of the region, heavy rain and wet snow are forecasted, with precipitation amounts from 30 to 49 mm.

Additionally, meteorologists warn of gusts of western wind at speeds of 17–22 m/s, which may lead to complications on the roads, damage to trees, and local disruptions in the operation of municipal infrastructure.

Rescuers urge residents and guests of the region to be especially careful while outdoors, to consider weather conditions when planning trips, and to avoid traveling during bad weather if possible.

In case of emergencies, it is necessary to call the Rescue Service at 101.
