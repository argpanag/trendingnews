Gen.G Leads Kalshi Worlds 2026 Odds After LCK Sweep

TheTools · 24.10.2026 13:24 · source · permalink

Gen.G was the favorite to win the 2026 League of Legends World Championship on prediction market Kalshi on Saturday, after all four LCK teams won and all four LPL teams lost in the opening round of the Swiss Stage.

(Riot Games)

Gen.G was the favorite to win the 2026 League of Legends World Championship on prediction market Kalshi on Saturday, after all four LCK teams won and all four LPL teams lost in the opening round of the Swiss Stage.

As of 9 a.m. ET on Saturday, Oct. 24, Kalshi's Worlds winner market priced Gen.G at 35%, ahead of T1 at 20%, Hanwha Life Esports at 18%, Bilibili Gaming at 11% and Dplus KIA at 10%. The market showed more than $1.2 million in trading volume. On Polymarket, Gen.G was priced at about 33% at the same time.

LCK Perfect, LPL Winless in Round 1

The Swiss Stage opened Friday, Oct. 23, with eight best-of-one matches. Gen.G beat Invictus Gaming, Hanwha Life Esports beat Top Esports, Dplus KIA beat G2 Esports and defending champion T1 beat Brazil's LØS, giving the LCK a 4-0 start, Esports.net reported.

All four LPL teams lost. Movistar KOI beat Bilibili Gaming and Karmine Corp beat Anyone's Legend, while CTBC Flying Oyster edged Team Liquid Alienware and LYON beat Team Secret Whales.

Gen.G-HLE and T1-DK Headline Round 2

Round 2 on Saturday is the final best-of-one round. The 1-0 matchups are Dplus KIA vs. T1, Hanwha Life Esports vs. Gen.G, CTBC Flying Oyster vs. Movistar KOI and LYON vs. Karmine Corp, guaranteeing at least two LCK teams a 2-0 record.

The 0-1 matchups are G2 Esports vs. LØS, Invictus Gaming vs. Team Secret Whales, Top Esports vs. Anyone's Legend and Team Liquid Alienware vs. Bilibili Gaming.

From Round 3, matches move to best-of-three with Fearless Draft. Teams that reach three wins advance to the Knockout Stage, and teams with three losses are eliminated.

The Swiss and Knockout stages are being played at the Credit Union of Texas Event Center in Allen, Texas. The final is set for Nov. 14 at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, New York. More coverage is available in RG.org's esports news section.

Fans in states with legal sports betting can compare sports betting apps or check options in New York and New Jersey. Sports betting is not legal in Texas.
