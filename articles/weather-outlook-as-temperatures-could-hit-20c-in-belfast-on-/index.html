Weather outlook as temperatures could hit 20C in Belfast on Monday

TheTools · 13.09.2026 18:31 · source · permalink

Summer may be behind us, but the weather forecast suggests the sun may try to cling on for just a bit longer. According to the Met Office, we are in for some changeable weather for the week beginning September 14, with some thundery showers rolling on Tuesday.

The forecaster has said that temperatures could reach up to 20C in Belfast on Monday, with "sunny intervals" on the cards to break up the rain.

The Met Office said: "Warm and humid in the south with cloudy skies, although some brighter spells are possible. A dry start in the north, before heavy rain moves into Northern Ireland and Scotland.

This is what the Met Office has forecast for Northern Ireland:

Monday:

Mainly dry and cloudy in the morning. Persistent rain, heavy at times, will extend east across all parts in the afternoon. Maximum temperature 18C.

Outlook for Tuesday to Thursday:

Showers, some heavy and thundery Tuesday. These clear in the east for a time Wednesday. A band of heavy rain pushes southeast Wednesday night, turning showery Thursday.
