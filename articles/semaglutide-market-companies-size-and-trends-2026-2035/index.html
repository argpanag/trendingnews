Semaglutide Market Companies, Size and Trends 2026-2035

TheTools · 17.09.2026 22:01 · source · permalink

The semaglutide market reached USD 30.53 billion in 2025. This report deeply analyzes the use of semaglutide to manage diabetes, weight, and cardiovascular risk. This report has been curated by Deepa Pandey, Senior Research Analyst, focusing on dynamics in pharmaceutical production, product formulation, distribution through a variety of pathways, and regulatory updates in the global obesity management and cardiovascular health industries. Her analysis facilitates pharmaceutical producers, distributors, medical professionals, and stakeholders in performing competitive analysis and making well-informed decisions.

Semaglutide Market Growth, Demand and Production Forecast

The semaglutide market is forecasted to expand from USD 34.03 billion in 2026 to USD 90.27 billion by 2035, growing at a CAGR of 11.45% from 2026 to 2035. Regulatory approval of innovative medications for metabolic conditions, effective formulations, and a rapidly improving distribution ecosystem are benefiting the market. Deepa Pandey, our Senior Research Analyst, has over 5 years of experience in the healthcare and medicine industry. According to her, the widening range of applications and advancements in the means of administration are boosting the semaglutide industry. The rising prevalence of diabetes, obesity, and cardiovascular conditions due to modern lifestyle and diet issues, and the swift development of healthcare infrastructure in middle-income countries are propelling the market growth.

  • By end user, the hospitals segment held the largest revenue share of 24.85% in 2025.

  • The fragmentation in the market is increasing as new players are launching competitive products, such as Tirzepatide developed by Eli Lilly, which acts via GLP-1 as well as GIP approaches.
  • Companies such as Pfizer and Eli Lilly have developed oral GLP-1 medications to rival semaglutide, which is administered via injection.
  • Consumer preferences seem to be shifting towards combination formulations which provide a good balance between affordability, efficacy, tolerability, and accessibility.

Semaglutide Market Size and Forecast

The market is anticipated to grow to USD 90.3 billion in 2035, nearly tripling in size from 2025 to 2035. The most prominent factors driving this growth are its increasing use to control weight and the development and approval of oral medications, which are increasingly preferred by patients. Diabetes still holds the largest share in terms of the use of semaglutide to manage medical conditions.

Expert Analyst View - Market Size

The rise in the prevalence of diabetes is a significant driver of market growth; however, the increasing presence of semaglutide in the management of blood sugar, obesity, cardiovascular health, and various other metabolism-related conditions is a bigger factor. The approval of novel medications for the use of semaglutide for such conditions helps pharmaceutical firms target a much wider consumer base than traditional diabetes patients.

Segmentation Analysis

Which Semaglutide Product Type Holds the Largest Market Share?

Injectable Semaglutide segment held the highest market share of 76.85% in 2025 and is expected to expand at a CAGR of 10.2% during the forecast period. It benefits from a well-established prescription environment because of its reputation for efficacy and reliability in managing type 2 diabetes using GLP-1 therapy. The oral semaglutide segment is expected to grow at the fastest CAGR of 15.35% from 2026 to 2035. This growth is driven by the entry of various competitive oral medications in the market, such as Wegovy. It eliminates various obstacles related to needle-based administration and caters to the preferences of a wider base of patients.

Real-World Semaglutide Development

Wegovy was launched in January 2026 and has secured more than 2 million prescriptions by April 2026. It reached around 1.3 million prescriptions in the first quarter of 2026 itself. It provides a 25 mg dose of semaglutide and is usually recommended by healthcare professionals to be taken once daily. These figures prove that there is a strong preference among patients to take the oral route of administration.

Expert Analyst View - Product Type

The trend is gradually shifting from injectable semaglutide to oral semaglutide, owing to high patient convenience and medication adherence. The oral segment is estimated to be the major disruptive segment, accounting for a CAGR of 15.35% during the forecast period, which is slightly above the injectable segment.

Which Was the Most Widespread Indication in the Semaglutide Market in 2025?

Type 2 diabetes was the largest indication in 2025, as it accounted for a 53.75% share and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 9.3% from 2026 to 2035. Its share is expected to decrease to 44.85% by 2035, as the obesity and weight management segment is expanding at the fastest CAGR of 13.65% during the forecast period, which is expected to hold the largest share of 49.10% by 2035.

Cardiovascular Expansion

The approval of Wegovy by the FDA to decrease the threat of fatal cardiovascular emergencies in adults with heart disease and obesity drives the growth of this segment. This medication expands the use of semaglutide beyond just obesity management to maintain cardiovascular health long-term in vulnerable patients. This enables pharmaceutical companies to market such medications for the management of chronic heart conditions, rather than as a short-term solution to contain obesity.

Global Obesity Burden Supporting Semaglutide Demand

According to WHO, over 1 billion people suffered from obesity in 2022, out of which 890 million are adults. The proportion of obese adults has almost doubled from 1990 to 2022, and quadrupled in the case of adolescents. The number of obese children under 5 in 2024 reached 35 million.

Expert Analyst View - Indication

Growing prevalence of obesity drives the demand for dedicated obesity treatment, but on-the-ground demand for semaglutide will depend on diagnostics, cost-effectiveness, the insurance ecosystem, prescription methodologies, and chronic treatment consistency.

Which Dosage Form Was Most Adopted in 2025?

The pre-filled injection pens segment was the dominant segment with a 63.45% share in 2025 and is expected to expand at a CAGR of 10.35% during the forecast period. Its share is expected to decrease to 57.25%, as the tablets segment is expected to grow at the fastest CAGR, leaping from a 22.35% share in 2025 to a 30.95% share in 2035. Pre-filled injection pens have the most widespread usage due to the presence of a well-established portfolio, some of which include Ozempic and Wegovy. Tablets are being increasingly used as a means of oral administration for obesity, along with conventional diabetes management.

Product Portfolio Intelligence

Ozempic and Rybelsus are used for type 2 diabetes, with the latter taken orally. In the U.S., these 2 medications have been approved to control blood sugar levels in patients suffering from type 2 diabetes and for cardiovascular risk management in vulnerable individuals with the same disease. Wegovy is available as a pill as well as an injection, wherein the latter is used for obesity management and to reduce cardiovascular risk, and the pill offers an oral route to take semaglutide to manage weight.

Novo Nordisk Semaglutide Product Sales

Novo Nordisk remains the central commercial company in semaglutide. Its 2025 annual report provides unusually strong product-level evidence.

In 2025, Novo Nordisk witnessed Ozempic sales of DKK 127.09 billion, Wegovy sales of DKK 79.11 billion, and Rybelsus sales of $22.09 billion. The combined portfolio demonstrates the commercial scale of semaglutide across injectable diabetes, injectable obesity, and oral diabetes applications.

Important Commercial Insight

Novo Nordisk reported total sales of DKK 82.347 billion for its obesity care segment, growing 26% in reported currency and 31% at constant exchange rates. Global branded GLP-1 obesity volume increased 104%, while Novo Nordisk reported a 59.6% branded volume share.

Expert Analyst View - Dosage Form

Tablets are expected to gain substantial share from pre-filled injection pens. The commercial launch of oral Wegovy is proof that the transition is already underway.

Which Distribution Channel Segment was Dominant in 2025?

The retail pharmacies segment dominated the market with a 42.6% share in 2025, and is expected to expand at a CAGR of 10.7% from 2026 to 2035. This segment benefits from a mature ecosystem and compatibility with a wide range of patients. The online pharmacies segment is expected to gain the highest amount of market share, growing from 15.25% to 21.4%, as it is expected to grow at the highest CAGR of 15.25% during the forecast period.

Digital Channel Intelligence

This rise can be attributed to the rapid uptake of telehealth, digital prescriptions, and home delivery. Novo Nordisk also acknowledges this trend, as it has provided a self-pay feature in the oral Wegovy unveiled this year to drive prescription uptake. This segment influences patient acquisition as well as enhanced accessibility to treatments.

Expert Analyst View - Distribution Channel

The demand for online pharmacies is increasing as patients and healthcare professionals can directly purchase semaglutide medications from manufacturers, reducing exorbitant costs of medicines. Retail pharmacies and online pharmacies are widely preferred distribution channels.

Which Route of Administration Segment Dominated the Market?

The subcutaneous segment held the largest market share of 77.05% in 2025, as the injectable route helps deliver the drug directly into the bloodstream. This results in a faster onset of action and fewer side effects. However, in the coming years, the oral segment is expected to expand rapidly with a CAGR of 15.4%, owing to high convenience for oral tablets and the need for self-administration.

Expert Analyst View - Route

The oral route is widely preferred as it addresses one of the biggest behavioral barriers associated with injectable therapies. The widespread availability of oral semaglutide for diabetes and obesity leads to expanded treatment adoption. Specific dosing requirements and adherence considerations are essential for tablet administration.

Which Patient Groups Accounted for the Most Commercial Activity in 2025?

The adults segment led the market with an 89.65% share in 2025 and is expected to grow at a significant CAGR of 11.1% from 2026 to 2035. The elderly patients segment is expected to grow at the fastest CAGR of 14.05% during the forecast period, because of the growing population of this age group and a rising prevalence of cardiometabolic conditions among them.

Diabetes Population Supporting Demand

The most recent IDF Diabetes Atlas reports that an estimated 589 million people in the age range of 20 to 79 suffered from diabetes in 2024, which translates to approximately 11.1% of the net global adult population. Diabetes is a major driver of semaglutide treatment adoption among adults in various regions such as South-East Asia, the Western Pacific, and the Middle East & North Africa.

Expert Analyst View - Patient Group

The adults segment will remain dominant over the studied period, but the geriatric population demonstrates faster growth, owing to the increasing prevalence of diabetes, obesity, and cardiovascular diseases.

Which End Users Led the Market in 2025?

The hospitals segment accounted for the major revenue share as it held a 24.85% share in 2025 and is expected to expand at a CAGR of 10.3% during the forecast period. Its share is expected to decrease in the upcoming years, as fragmentation in the market increases and patients are seeking treatments from various other types of medical institutions. Diabetes and obesity centers are expected to increase their market share at the highest CAGR of 13.05% from 2026 to 2035.

Expert Analyst View- End Users

Specialty diabetes and obesity centers and home care are being increasingly preferred by patients to facilitate chronic outpatient treatment and self-administration of medications. This trend is expected to decrease patient reliance on hospital pharmacies and boost retail, specialized, and digital pharmacy segments.

Which Regions Accounted for the Most Commercial Activity in the Market?

North America dominated the market with a 57.35% share in 2025 and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 10.75% from 2026 to 2035. The availability of advanced healthcare infrastructure and the growing demand for obesity care drive market growth in North America. Asia Pacific is expected to grow at the fastest CAGR of 14.05% during the forecast period, taking its global share from 13.2% in 2025 to 16.65% in 2035.

Novo Nordisk Regional Obesity-Care Growth

North American Obesity Demand

In the U.S., around 40.3% of adults qualified to be in the category of being obese in the period from August 2021 to August 2023, and 9.7% fell in the range of severe obesity. Latest data show that the rate of obesity peaks in the 40-50 age group at 46.4%, with the 20-39 and 60+ age groups at 35.5% and 38.9%, respectively.

Expert Analyst View- North America

The most standout insight is that each and every state in the country had an obesity rate over 25% in 2024. High rates of obesity drive the demand for semaglutide treatment and make it a lucrative region for pharmaceutical companies to generate significant revenue.

Regulatory Expansion and Indication Development

Ozempic has a mature ecosystem in diabetes treatment, and Rybelsus is a well-established oral treatment. Wegovy has a sizeable portfolio of approved treatments which includes:

  • Wegovy Injection: Obesity management indication
  • Wegovy Cardiovascular Risk Management: Boosts its presence in the cardiometabolic segment.
  • Wegovy Pill (approved in 2025): The first oral GLP-1 therapy for obesity control in the U.S.
  • Wegovy HD 7.2 mg (approved in 2026): Injectable treatment; provides a higher dose of semaglutide.

According to our Senior Researcher Aditi Shivarkar, future potential for pharmaceutical companies is related to lifecycle optimization of semaglutide, which includes doses, means of administration, and treatment demographics. The oral route and higher doses of injectable treatments can expand the patient base while boosting the lifecycle of the foundational molecule.

Expert Analyst View- Regulatory Environment

There was a significant expansion in the portfolio of various types of treatments in 2026. Novo Nordisk reported the approval of Wegovy HD 7.2 mg by the FDA and its launch in the U.S., and the STEPUP trial showed that it enabled a mean weight loss of 20.7%.

Manufacturing and Supply Intelligence

The manufacture of semaglutide injections was not able to keep up with the demand from 2022 to 2025, as reported by the FDA. The FDA had relaxed certain rules mentioned in the 503A regulation, which states the rules that compounding pharmacies have to follow to produce semaglutide for individuals, to alleviate the shortage issue. When the issue was resolved to a significant degree by 2025, the normal rules were reinforced if a compounding pharmacy wanted to manufacture FDA-approved semaglutide formulations for individuals. The FDA had also relaxed certain rules for registered outsourcing facilities that manufacture semaglutide, and this relaxation also ended in 2025.

Expert Analyst View - Manufacturing

This shows that manufacturing capacity is an important factor for companies to gain an edge against their rivals. Pharmaceutical firms that aspire to hold a leading status will have to optimize API manufacturing, peptide production, formulation, sterile filling, injector capacity, capable infrastructure, and a robust global supply chain.

Major Competitive Companies

Novo Nordisk has an excellent lineup that consists of Ozempic, Wegovy, and Rybelsus and has an edge from starting early and a mature ecosystem across various stages of the supply chain. Currently, it has the strongest semaglutide ecosystem and massive sales across the globe. One of our senior researchers, Aman Singh, suggests that the fragmentation in the market is increasing as rivals are competing through the development of combination formulations, oral therapies, and advanced metabolic treatments. Eli Lilly and Pfizer are prominent players in the oral medications segment, and Roche has developed advanced obesity therapies. Boehringer Ingelheim/Zealand Pharma and Amgen focus on the obesity and metabolic segments, with the latter emphasizing the development of long-lasting therapies.

Some of the medical parameters that have a significant effect on patient preference include weight loss efficiency, blood sugar control, heart health management, administration frequency, and treatment persistence. Competitive Landscape

Since Novo Nordisk has a wide portfolio of Ozempic, Wegovy, and Rybelsus for the treatment of diabetes and obesity, it has a significant first-mover and installed-base advantage in the market. However, the competitive environment is becoming more difficult. Tirzapetide creates pressure through dual GIP/GLP-1 activity, while oral competitors could challenge semaglutide's historical injectable advantage. Companies focus on developing novel metabolic therapies with better efficacy, convenience, tolerability, price, and success compared to market products. This increases competition among manufacturers related to the entire treatment ecosystem rather than molecule-level differentiation alone.

Semaglutide is primarily procured based on clinical efficacy, cardiovascular outcomes, glycemic control, safety/tolerability, and drug availability. Patients' procurement criteria also depend on dosing convenience, patient adherence, and manufacturing reliability. Patients, prescribers, payers, pharmacies, and manufacturers are influenced by the commercial pathway. Cost-effectiveness and production capability are important factors for widespread accessibility.
