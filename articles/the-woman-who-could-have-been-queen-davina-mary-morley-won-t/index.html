The woman who could have been queen: Davina Mary Morley won the heart of a young Prince Charles – so why did their romance end?

TheTools · 11.09.2026 11:06 · source · permalink

Davina Mary Morley, the enchanting Sheffield scion who was considered a potential future queen, died at the age of 75 on 2 September

Davina Mary Morley, whose romance with a young King Charles was so profound that she was considered a future queen, has died at the age of 75. Five decades ago, when the media obsession with finding the then-Prince of Wales a bride was at a fever pitch, she was dubbed the most beautiful of ‘Charlie’s Angels’, as the press referred to the future king’s girlfriends. The glittering list also included Lady Jane Wellesley, Georgiana Russell, Lady Sarah Spencer (now Lady Sarah McCorquodale), Anna Wallace and Sabrina Guinness.

Born Davina Mary Sheffield on 1 March 1951, she was a scion of the Sheffield baronets. Her father, Captain George Berkeley Sheffield, was a member of the Grenadier Guards and her mother was the Hon Agnes Wilson McGowan, daughter of lauded industrialist 1st Baron McGowan. Tragedy struck the family in 1976, when Agnes was murdered during a robbery at her home.

Prince Charles and Davina on a drive through Smith's Lawn in the Aston Martin Db5 Volante that Charles was gifted by Queen Elizabeth II for his 21st birthday

There was little surprise when Prince Charles started dating Davina. The press wrote avidly of her beauty and eligibility – she was a modelesque 5’10” blonde former debutante who loved horse riding and rubbed shoulders in royally approved social circles (her cousin, Samantha Sheffield, is better known nowadays as Samantha Cameron, wife of former Prime Minister David Cameron). She was also a passionate philanthropist, spending time volunteering in Saigon.

It didn’t hurt that historians discovered an impressive royal connection: Davina’s ancestors in the Sheffield line had previously owned Buckingham Palace back when it was Buckingham House, hundreds of years before it fell into the hands of Queen Charlotte.

Her romance with the heir to the throne began in the summer of 1975, when they crossed paths at a dinner party hosted by another of ‘Charlie’s Angels’, Lady Jane Wellesley. Soon, Davina was invited to spend the weekend at Balmoral. Speaking to the Daily Mail at the time, a friend of Davina’s described the pair as ‘wonderful together’, and, according to sources, Charles’ beloved grandmother, the Queen Mother, approved of the match.

An accomplished horse rider, Davina spent time at the Cotswolds Hunt Cross Country team events

Davina in 1979, following her split from the future king

But it was not always smooth sailing. Indeed, when the pair first met, Davina was dating the Old Harrovian powerboat racer, James Beard. According to royal historian Penny Junor, she refused Prince Charles’ dinner invitations, but the heir was ‘so persistent that she eventually succumbed and the boyfriend soon fell by the wayside.’ Indeed, one biographer wrote that the Prince ‘could not disguise his yearning, even in public’ and noted that she was the only girlfriend with whom Prince Charles was happy to be photographed in public.

But Davina’s previous love match would prove to be a thorn in the side of this royal romance. Tradition at the time decreed that any woman who married into the royal family must be a virgin. Through Beard, the press learnt that Davina did not meet this archaic stipulation, and, to the devastation of both parties, the romance was called off.

The pair were forced to separate when a previous boyfriend revealed to the press that he had had a ‘full sexual relationship’ with Davina, meaning she was not a virgin and thereforre could not marry into the royal family

Davina went on to marry Jake Morley, the brother of the Duchess of Beaufort

The similarities between the star-crossed lovers throughout the rest of their lives are uncanny. Davina married the same year that Charles tied the knot with Princess Diana. Her husband, Jonathan Derek ‘Jake’ Morley, came from a military family, the son of Brigadier-General Michael Frederick Morley and Primrose Elizabeth Gordon. Jake’s sister, Miranda, became the Duchess of Beaufort following her wedding to the 11th Duke of Beaufort; he died in 2022 at the age of 80.

Davina Mary Morley is remembered by her three sons. The first, Thomas, was born in 1983. Poignantly, she named her two following sons William and Harry, both of whom were born after Prince William and Prince Harry, the sons of her one-time boyfriend.
