Expertise.com Best Truck Accident Lawyers in Phoenix 2026 recognition - Phillips Law Group

Phillips Law Group - Injury Lawyers in Phoenix, Arizona

Expertise.com lists the firm among 18 Top Picks after reviewing 1,239 Phoenix-area providers.

PHOENIX, AZ, UNITED STATES, September 4, 2026 /EINPresswire.com/ -- Phillips Law Group has been recognized among Expertise.com’s Best Truck Accident Lawyers serving Phoenix for 2026. The current Expertise.com list identifies the firm as one of 18 Top Picks selected from 1,239 reviewed providers and 750 curated candidates in the Phoenix truck-accident category.

According to Expertise.com, its researchers score providers on more than 25 variables across five categories: availability, qualifications, reputation, experience, and professionalism. The company describes a process that begins with identifying providers in a local market and includes reviewing public information, assessing service capabilities, and mystery-shopping calls. Selection is distinct from a provider’s option to advertise after it has passed the review and selection process.

On the 2026 Phoenix list, Expertise.com identifies Phillips Law Group’s Phoenix office at 3101 N. Central Avenue, Suite 1500, and notes that the firm is available 24 hours a day. The directory’s provider profile discusses the firm’s work evaluating the parties that may share responsibility after a commercial-truck collision, including drivers, motor carriers, maintenance providers, and cargo-loading companies.

Commercial truck accident claims can involve evidence and business relationships that are not present in an ordinary passenger-vehicle collision. Depending on the facts, a case may require review of driver logs, electronic vehicle data, inspection and maintenance records, dispatch communications, cargo documents, and company or contractor records. The facts of each matter determine what investigation may be appropriate and which parties may be responsible.

The recognition adds a truck-accident category distinction to Phillips Law Group’s 2026 recognition by Expertise.com among Phoenix car accident lawyer selections. Each listing reflects its own category-specific provider pool and review process.

Phillips Law Group is an Arizona-based personal injury firm representing individuals and families in matters involving motor vehicle collisions, commercial trucking crashes, wrongful death, premises liability, product liability, and other serious injuries. The firm states that it has served clients for more than 30 years and recovered more than $2 billion for injured clients. Past results do not guarantee a similar outcome.
