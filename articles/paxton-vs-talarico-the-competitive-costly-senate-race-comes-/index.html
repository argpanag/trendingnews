AUSTIN, Texas — The Texas Senate race is shaping up to be one of the most competitive in the nation, with Republican Attorney General Ken Paxton challenging Democrat James Talarico for the seat currently held by Sen. John Cornyn.

The race is driving record-breaking turnout and record-breaking spending.

ALSO| More than 7.7 million Texans voted early in 2026 election, surpassing 2018 record

7.7 million Texans voted early this election, shattering a record for midterm early voting turnout first set in 2018. A report from AdImpact found the Senate race in Texas is the most expensive non-presidential race in U.S. history, with $573.2 million in ad spending.

On Election Day, the race remains competitive, although recent polls show Talarico with a slight lead in the race.

A report from Texas Southern University’s Barbara Jordan Public Policy Research and Survey Center last week showed Talarico with a 2% lead over Paxton among surveyed voters in the final days of early voting.

The Talarico campaign is set to host a watch party in Austin on Tuesday night, while the Paxton campaign will be in Dallas.
