BVB eye club record – a special match for Ryerson

A year ago, the Black & Yellows also made an outstanding start to the Bundesliga season, taking 13 points from their first five matches. A 2-2 draw in the top-of-the-table clash with Leipzig followed. Now Borussia have the chance to do (even) better and equal the club record with an away win at 1. FC Union Berlin: they have taken 16 points from their first six games on three occasions, most recently in 2017/18 – never have they had more at such an early stage.

To do that, however, scoring the opening goal is absolutely essential! Why? This fixture has always been won by the team that went 1-0 up – whether in the league (14 meetings, ten BVB wins) or the DFB-Pokal (two meetings, two BVB wins).

OneFootball Videos

Another remarkable fact: there has never been a draw! None of the 14 Bundesliga matches between the two teams has ended level. No other fixture in Bundesliga history has been played so often without the points ever being shared.

For Köpenick's Iron Ones, the Black & Yellows from Germany's former steel heartland have become a real bogey team. Union Berlin have lost more Bundesliga games against Borussia Dortmund than against any other side (ten, including six of the last seven meetings). BVB have won all three of the most recent encounters by an aggregate score of 12-0.

Former Union player Julian Ryerson has played his part in that record. The Norwegian has scored two of his seven Bundesliga goals for BVB against his former club. Should he fail to find the net on Saturday, hopes will rest on Serhou Guirassy. The striker has scored eight goals in eight games against Union! (br)
