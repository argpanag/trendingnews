How Ammos turned a long shot into a Liberatore coup - Surf Coast Times

TheTools · 22.10.2026 02:54 · source · permalink

Former Western Bulldogs premiership player Tom Liberatore has signed with Geelong Amateur for the 2027 BFNL season. Photo: Wikimedia Commons.

Geelong Amateur set out to find a guest speaker for its grand final luncheon and ended up securing one of the biggest signings in Bellarine Football Netball League history.

The club announced on social media on Thursday that former Western Bulldogs premiership player Tom Liberatore had signed for the 2027 season.

Ammos president Simon Farrell said Liberatore enjoyed his time with the playing group at the September function so much that the club began pursuing him as a player.

“The ball started rolling when we had [Liberatore] as the guest speaker at our grand final luncheon,” he said.

“He loved it, they had a great day. He was with the boys, he had a ball.

“It all sort of just grew from there … it just got legs.”

Liberatore retired following the 2026 AFL season, having played 268 games and won an AFL premiership and club best and fairest medals.

His close friendship with Ammos co-coach Sam Lloyd, his former Bulldogs teammate, helped turn the ambitious pitch into reality.

“He’s got a few other mates at the club, so we threw it out there,” Farrell said.

“You always throw it out there thinking it’s not going to happen, but then all of a sudden he said, ‘Yeah, I’d be happy with that. I’d enjoy that.’”

The blockbuster signing comes alongside the addition of former Melbourne midfielder-forward Jack Henderson and the appointment of former Werribee VFL defender Nick Coughlan as co-coach alongside Lloyd.

Farrell said the club was thrilled to secure Liberatore’s services but acknowledged the challenge of turning a strong recruiting period into on-field success.

The Ammos finished second on the 2026 BFNL ladder but failed to progress beyond the preliminary final, losing to eventual premiers Queenscliff.

The club last won a BFNL senior premiership in 2016, the same year Liberatore broke his premiership drought with the Western Bulldogs.

“That’s our main aim every year [winning a premiership], but as you well know, it’s not that easy,” Farrell said.

“Every club’s trying to get that ultimate, and if Libba can add another 10 per cent, amazing.

“The club’s pumped, we’re so rapt and over the moon.”
