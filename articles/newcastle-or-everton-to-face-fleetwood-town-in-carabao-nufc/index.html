Latest. Newcastle or Everton to face Fleetwood Town in Carabao Cup quarter-finals

Newcastle United will face an away tie against Fleetwood Town in this season's Carabao Cup quarter-finals should they defeat Everton in Thursday night's fourth round encounter.

The Magpies aim to reach the last-eight stage of the competition for a fifth consecutive campaign when facing David Moyes' side at the Hill Dickinson Stadium (kick-off 7:45pm GMT).

Their reward, should they prevail against the Toffees, will be an away meeting with Fleetwood after the League Two side overcame Arsenal via penalties following last night's 1-1 draw at Highbury Stadium.

The quarter-final matches will take place on the week commencing Monday, 14th December.
