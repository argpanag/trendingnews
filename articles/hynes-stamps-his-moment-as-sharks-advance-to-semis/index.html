Sharks advance to semis

That’s all she wrote!

A late tackle from Trindall ends any chance of a late Cowboys comeback, forcing an error from Luki and sending the Sharks fans into cheers.

Cronulla hold on to win 26-16 and will now face the loser of tomorrow’s clash between the Panthers and Roosters in the semi finals.

Nicho Hynes celebrates a try. Getty Images

‘Never say never’ as Purdue pounces

Purdue bursts past some defenders after a much smarter pass from Drinkwater and scores to keep the Cowboys alive in this sudden-death clash.

“Never say never, don’t say it’s not possible,” Phil Gould said on Nine.

Sharks lead by 10 with seven minutes to go.

Hynes catches defence napping

Hynes darts over for the Sharks and this one might be all over!

The much-maligned halfback shapes to kick across field to Mulitalo, as he has done a few times tonight for mixed results.

But instead, Hynes cleverly catches the Cowboys defenders napping as they rush out to pressure him, stepping past Taumalolo and scoring under the posts.

Cronulla lead 26-12 with just 13 minutes to go and it’s going to take something special for the Cowboys to get back into this one.

Trindall, Kennedy combine in style

This is becoming a bit of a ding-dong battle the past 10 minutes.

Cronulla hit back to extend their lead, as Trindall drifts across field and finds his fullback Kennedy with a nice pass and he dives over to score.

“Cowboys are appealing for an obstruction ... it was just a defensive decision, this will be all good,” Andrew Johns said on Nine.

That marks 31 try assists for Trindall this season – the most in the NRL – as he continues to defy his clear discomfort and lead the Sharks towards a win.

Cowboys hit back after Dearden pearler

Just when the momentum seemed to be with the Sharks, the visitors have surged back into the contest after a try for Laybutt.

Dearden throws a bullet 20-metre cut-out pass to his Cowboys winger and they trail by only four points now heading into the final quarter.

The crowd at Allianz Stadium has been announced as 17,429.

Katoa races away with intercept

Katoa extends the lead for the Sharks, latching onto a poor pass from Drinkwater and racing away to score a long-range intercept try.

It continues a night to forget for Drinkwater – the fullback has made four errors including the wayward pass to Katoa.

It’s not looking good for North Queensland with 25 minutes to go.

Scott Drinkwater’s intercept pass to Sione Katoa. Nine

Gus stunned by high tackle penalty

Mulitalo shoots out of the line and puts a shot on Burns.

A penalty is blown immediately, before Mahoney comes in to incite a melee.

The two teams are separated rather swiftly without much issue, but Phil Gould is more concerned about why a penalty was even given to the Cowboys.

“Where’s the contact with the head? It’s a brilliant tackle,” Gould said on Nine.

“What’s the penalty there? As soon as the crowd makes a noise, they want to pull up the game and penalise it. Leave me out, fair dinkum.”

Ronaldo Mulitalo’s tackle on Braidon Burns. Nine

Trindall’s brave moment for naught

Cronulla get an early attacking opportunity in the second half, but Clifford comes up with an intercept from a Jones offload.

Clifford then puts up an attacking bomb, but the Sharks let it bounce and Katoa is tackled in-goal for a dropout.

“That’s someone’s job to catch it – there’s half a dozen players just standing there looking at it, someone had to take it,” Andrew Johns said on Nine.

Trindall almost comes up with an incredible play, going short from the dropout for himself and diving on the ball, but bobbling it in the process.

Given that’s how he injured his shoulder, that’s a brave choice from Trindall.

“That’s gotta hurt,” Phil Gould said.

Braydon Trindall dives on a loose ball. Nine

‘Game of inches’ as Sharks gun denied

Cronulla are in again – or are they?

Hynes places a pinpoint cross-field chip kick for Mulitalo, who soars in the air and gets the offload away to Iro for a try.

“He’s placed that in the perfect spot,” Brenton Speed said on Nine.

However, the try is overturned after replays showed Iro was offside.

“It’s a game of inches,” Andrew Johns said.

Sharks players celebrate what they thought was a try. Nine

Trindall in trouble, Katoa strikes

Hess comes off for a HIA for the visitors after a heavy blow.

Trindall, who has been favouring his shoulder all night, reels out of a tackle and clutches at it again and there are genuine fears for the Sharks star.

“His arm has gone limp, he won’t be able to go on ... there’s the game plan, get at Trindall and play off that,” Andrew Johns said on Nine.

“He’s as tough as they come.”

Braydon Trindall clutches at his injured shoulder. Nine

Despite the injury concern for their star playmaker, Cronulla get another chance in good-ball territory after another error from Drinkwater.

The hosts make no mistake as Hynes, Kennedy and Katoa combine, with the latter diving over in the corner after passing his HIA.

It marks his 100th try for the Sharks in what could be his final game for the club before heading to North Queensland next season.
