Captain Cool to Mentor: The beginning of a new innings for MS Dhoni

TheTools · 17.11.2026 12:30 · source · permalink

News

17h ago

After 19 seasons, five titles and countless unforgettable moments, the upcoming edition of the TATA IPL will see MS Dhoni in a new avatar, not as a player, but as team mentor of the Chennai Super Kings (CSK), bringing the curtains down on a glorious playing career.

Every time he walked onto the field, the stands roared. The anticipation soared. The excitement went through the roof, and Thala made sure he didn't disappoint. He brought an aura that became synonymous with the league. Over his IPL career, Dhoni broke records and set benchmarks.

Across his 19-season IPL journey, including 17 seasons associated with CSK and two with Rising Pune Supergiant, he scored 5,439 runs from 278 matches at an average of 38.30 and a strike rate of 137.45, hitting 24 fifties. For CSK alone, he scored 4,865 runs, finishing as the highest run-getter for the franchise.

It wasn't just about how many he scored; it was about when he scored them as well. Striding out in precarious and tricky situations, he absorbed pressure, took a calculative approach and orchestrated famous finishes.

He also finishes with the most dismissals by a wicketkeeper in the league, with 154 catches and 47 stumpings, taking his total tally to 201.

Dhoni, the captain and leader, brought an altogether different dimension to the league and redefined leadership. He was smart, razor-sharp and consistent. He won five titles (2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, 2023) as captain, joint-most with Rohit Sharma, registered a record 136 wins, making him the only captain with more than 100 wins in the league, and led CSK to a record 10 finals. Under his leadership, CSK became the first team to defend the IPL title, lifting the trophy in 2010 and 2011.

Dhoni's legacy, however, extended well beyond numbers. That calm demeanour, calculative mind, street-smart decisions, lightning-fast stumpings, thunderous helicopter shots, out-of-the-box thinking, masterful finishes and cheeky 'definitely nots' brought joy to millions across the world.

Thala's legacy will forever remain etched in IPL history. We may not witness him walk out for those iconic tosses or innings, but those deafening cheers will definitely be heard every time he steps out in his new role as team mentor.
