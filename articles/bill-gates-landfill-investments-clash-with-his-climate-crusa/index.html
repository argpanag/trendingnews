Bill Gates' Landfill Investments Clash With His Climate Crusade

TheTools · 10.11.2026 19:26 · source · permalink

Bill Gates has spent years urging businesses to get serious about climate change, but a new ProPublica investigation suggests one of his biggest investments hasn't kept pace with his rhetoric. Reporter Alex Cuadros finds that Gates, through his investment office, has quietly built a $29 billion stake in Republic Services, the country's second-largest landfill operator—a holding ProPublica says now represents more than one-fifth of his net worth. Landfills are a major US source of methane, and EPA inspectors have repeatedly found Republic sites leaking the potent gas far above federal limits, including one Houston landfill singled out as a chronic violator.

Despite marketing itself as a climate-conscious waste company and signing on to an emissions-cutting initiative, Republic has resisted tougher targets that might require more than $1 billion in new spending, according to the story. It has also, through industry groups, pushed back on stricter federal rules, advanced methane monitoring, and policies to divert food and yard waste away from landfills—measures scientists say are key to curbing emissions. "It seems very likely that Bill Gates has the power to influence Republic's behavior to make it more climate-friendly, even if that costs the company profits," Ann Lipton, a legal scholar at the University of Colorado, Boulder, tells the outlet.
