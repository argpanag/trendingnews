Croatia lose to Spain in Split

TheTools · 06.10.2026 20:34 · source · permalink
  • by croatiaweek
  • October 6, 2026
  • in Sport

Slaven Bilić

Croatia has lost to Spain 2–1 at a packed Poljud Stadium in Split on Tuesday night.

Ivan Perišić put Croatia ahead in the first half before Mikel Merino equalised and then scored the winner after the break as Spain dominated possession and territory.

The result means Spain remain unbeaten in the group and extend their unbeaten streak to 42 games.

Under pressure following the record 7–0 defeat by England at Rujevica, Slaven Bilić’s side began with considerably greater energy, aggression and intensity. The approach paid off in the 17th minute.

Croatia won possession in midfield and Mateo Kovačić carried the ball towards the penalty area before finding Igor Matanović on the right. His effort from the edge of the box was saved by Simon, but the rebound fell to Perišić, who made no mistake with his head from close range. Watch the goal here.

The goal was Perišić’s 40th for Croatia.

Croatia then came close to doubling their lead six minutes later. Spain goalkeeper Simon was caught out while playing out from the back under pressure, and after Aymeric Laporte lost possession, Nikola Vlašić found Matanović inside the box.

The Croatia forward had a clear chance from just a few metres out, but Simon recovered to make an important save.

Spain gradually took control of the game, enjoying the majority of possession and creating openings, particularly down the right through Lamine Yamal.

However, Croatia defended their lead and went into half-time 1–0 ahead.

Spain began the second half as they had finished the first, keeping Croatia pinned back and repeatedly attacking around the Croatian penalty area.

The pressure eventually told in the 63rd minute. Josko Gvardiol’s clearance fell to Dani Olmo, who worked his way past Jakić before finding Merino. The Arsenal midfielder took aim from around 10 metres and beat Dominik Livaković to make it 1–1. Watch the goal here.

The equaliser reflected Spain’s dominance after the interval. Bilić made changes, with Martin Baturina and Petar Sučić introduced. Croatia’s cause was not helped with ten minutes remaining when Perišić was shown red after earning a second yellow card for a foul on Cucurella.

Spain continued to press for a winner and it could come in the last minute of regulation time. It would be Merino again who would find the back of the net after a pass from Zubimendi to give Spain a 2-1 win. Watch the goal here.

Croatia now sits third in the group behind Spain and England, and three points ahead of Czechia who remain bottom after losing to England tonight.

Croatia starting XI

Livaković – Stanišić, Šutalo, Vušković, Gvardiol – Kovačić, Jakić, Modrić – Vlašić, Matanović, Perišić

Spain starting XI

Simón – Pubill, Cubarsí, Laporte, Cucurella – Rodri, Pedri – Yamal, Olmo, Baena – Oyarzabal
