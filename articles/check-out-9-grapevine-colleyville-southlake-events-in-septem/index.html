Check out 9 Grapevine, Colleyville, Southlake events in September, October

TheTools · 18.09.2026 05:00 · source · permalink

Country and western music, Thomas the Tank Engine and pumpkin patches make up some of the events in Grapevine, Colleyville and Southlake that will help welcome in the autumn season.

Boot Scootin' Saturday Night

Attend a night celebrating country and western music, complete with live music, a mechanical bull and corn hole. The first hour will include classes on two-stepping and food and drinks will be sold throughout the event.

  • Sept. 26, 6-10 p.m.

  • Free

  • Heroes Park, 104 Terra Cotta Way, Colleyville

Sept. 26, 6-10 p.m.

Free

Heroes Park, 104 Terra Cotta Way, Colleyville

Southlake Hispanic Heritage Celebration

Celebrate the second annual Southlake Hispanic Heritage Festival as it returns with live music and performances, food trucks, lawn games and more. The event will also feature kids zones for the whole family to have fun.

  • Sept. 26, 3-10 p.m.

  • Free

  • Southlake Town Square, 285 Grand Ave., Southlake

Sept. 26, 3-10 p.m.

Free

Southlake Town Square, 285 Grand Ave., Southlake

Sundae Funday

Churn your own ice cream in this family friendly experience that allows you to make a favorite frozen treat the old fashioned way. The event will also feature lawn games to play while enjoying your homemade delight.

  • Sept. 27, 2-4 p.m.

  • $10

  • Settlement to City Museums, 206 W. Hudgins St., Grapevine

Sept. 27, 2-4 p.m.

$10

Settlement to City Museums, 206 W. Hudgins St., Grapevine

Trick or Treat Trail

Hike your way into Halloween as the city of Southlake transforms White-tailed Deer Trail into a Halloween wonderland fit for all ages. This 0.25-mile nature hike event will include stories, games and costumes.

  • Oct. 3, 5-6:30 p.m. and 7-8:30 p.m.

  • $35

  • Bob Jones Nature Center, 355 E. Bob Jones Road, Southlake

Oct. 3, 5-6:30 p.m. and 7-8:30 p.m.

$35

Bob Jones Nature Center, 355 E. Bob Jones Road, Southlake

Fire Department Open House

Join the Colleyville Fire Department for a tour of its facilities in this family-friendly event. Learn about the fire department, participate in activities and enjoy food and beverages at this fun and educational event.

  • Oct. 4, 10 a.m.-1 p.m.

  • Free

  • Central Fire Station, 5209 Colleyville Blvd., Colleyville

Oct. 4, 10 a.m.-1 p.m.

Free

Central Fire Station, 5209 Colleyville Blvd., Colleyville

Celebra Grapevine
The event honoring Hispanic Heritage will include live dance performances, music, food and a fashion show.

  • Oct. 4, 2-8 p.m.

  • $35

  • 1209 S. Main St., Grapevine

Oct. 4, 2-8 p.m.

$35

1209 S. Main St., Grapevine

Nash Farm Barn Dance

Enjoy music, dancing and a fried chicken dinner at Nash Farm’s family-friendly barn dance. The event also features a pie auction to raise money for the farm's educational programs and encourages attendees to bake pies for the auction.

  • Oct. 9, 6:30-9 p.m.

  • $25 for advance tickets and $35 for walk-up tickets

  • 626 Ball St., Grapevine

Oct. 9, 6:30-9 p.m.

$25 for advance tickets and $35 for walk-up tickets

626 Ball St., Grapevine

Day out with Thomas: The Let’s Rock, Let’s Roll Tour

Attend this family-centered event to meet and take a ride with Thomas the Tank Engine. The event also includes live entertainment, photos with Sir Topham Hatt and musical fun. There are several ride times each day and it is recommended to get tickets early.

  • Oct. 9-11 and 16-18, times vary

  • $29.85 or $25.85 for groups of 20+

  • Grapevine Vintage Railroad, 705 S. Main St., Grapevine

Oct. 9-11 and 16-18, times vary

$29.85 or $25.85 for groups of 20+

Grapevine Vintage Railroad, 705 S. Main St., Grapevine

PumpkinFest

Embrace autumn with an assortment of seasonal activities and photo opportunities while walking the Meadowmere Park pumpkin path. Fridays and weekends will also feature local artisan vendors. Pumpkins and snacks will be available for purchase.

  • Oct. 9-18, 10 a.m.-9 p.m.

  • $20 per car, $40 per bus

  • 3000 Meadowmere Lane, Grapevine

Oct. 9-18, 10 a.m.-9 p.m.

$20 per car, $40 per bus

3000 Meadowmere Lane, Grapevine

Adult Crafternoon: Fall Wreaths

Relax and unwind with an afternoon of crafting paper wreaths in seasonal colors hosted by the Southlake Public Library. Take your wreath home and display it when you’re done. Registration requires a library card and opens on Sept. 29.

  • Oct. 15, noon-2 p.m.

  • Free

  • 307 Parkwood Drive, Southlake

Oct. 15, noon-2 p.m.

Free

307 Parkwood Drive, Southlake
