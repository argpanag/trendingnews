Christian Watson injured on Thursday Night Football

TheTools · 30.10.2026 01:19 · source · permalink

by Garrett Ball | Packers Correspondent | Thu, Oct 29th 2026, 9:19pm EDT

Christian Watson was injured after making a long reception in the Packers’ game against the Panthers on Thursday night. He went into the blue medical tent and subsequently left to the locker room. (Vince Lovergine on X)

Fantasy Impact:

As for right now, what injury Watson suffered and whether he will return is up in the air. He was on his way to a huge night, catching six passes for 100 yards before going down.
