Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie settle legal battle over French winery

TheTools · 28.11.2026 01:16 · source · permalink
  • Culture

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have reached a settlement in their four-year legal battle over Château Miraval, the French winery where they married in 2014.

Representatives for the former couple confirmed the news Wednesday in a joint statement to ABC News.

"We have settled out of court in the best interests of our family and will not be commenting further," the statement read. "Any comments in media stories attributed to 'sources' are in fact not coming from either of us, or anyone close to us."

The former couple purchased the estate in 2008. Their legal battle over the winery began in February 2022 when Pitt sued Jolie, alleging she had sold her stake in the business to a third party without his consent.

In his lawsuit, Pitt claimed he and Jolie had agreed not to sell their respective interests in the winery without the other's approval.

Jolie's lawyers disputed that claim in an October 2022 cross-complaint obtained by ABC News, alleging that Pitt had rejected such an agreement in writing just days before they purchased the estate.

According to the filing, Jolie began negotiating with Pitt in June 2020 to sell him her stake in the winery.

Jolie's lawyers alleged that the deal fell through after Pitt demanded she sign a nondisclosure agreement that would have prevented her from speaking outside of court about his alleged abuse of her and their children. According to the filing, Pitt walked away from the deal after Jolie refused to sign the agreement.

In the same cross-complaint, Jolie alleged that Pitt physically and verbally abused her and some of their children aboard a private plane in September 2016.

Pitt's attorney Anne Kiley pushed back against the allegations at the time in a statement to "Good Morning America."

"Brad has owned everything he’s responsible for from day one -- unlike the other side -- but he's not going to own anything he didn't do," Kiley said. "He has been on the receiving end of every type of personal attack and misrepresentation. Thankfully, the various public authorities the other side has tried to use against him over the past six years have made their own independent decisions. Brad will continue to respond in court as he has consistently done."

Jolie filed for divorce from Pitt on Sept. 19, 2016, five days after the alleged incident, citing irreconcilable differences.

In April 2019, a judge declared Jolie and Pitt legally single as their divorce proceedings continued.

The former couple reached a divorce settlement in December 2024, eight years after Jolie filed for divorce. Their legal dispute over Château Miraval remained unresolved at the time.

Jolie and Pitt, who met on the set of the 2005 film "Mr. & Mrs. Smith," share six children including Maddox, Zahara, Pax, Shiloh and twins Vivienne and Knox.
