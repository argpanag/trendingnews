Davies brothers finally set to unite as Blues' One-Day Cup squad revealed | Cricket NSW

TheTools · 16.11.2026 01:41 · source · permalink

The Davies brothers could play their first NSW Blues match together this week, both called up for Monday's One-Day Cup contest against South Australia at Cricket Central.

Joel Davies, who has recovered from a hamstring injury he sustained during September's ODI tour of South Africa, has been named in a 12-player Blues squad alongside sibling Oliver. 

Despite each representing New South Wales on multiple occasions across formats, the Davies brothers have never been picked in the same starting XI. 

Nic Maddinson also returns to the NSW white-ball side having missed the last couple of One-Day Cup matches while in South Africa with the Test squad. 

Jack Edwards is unavailable due to his involvement in Australia's ongoing ODI series against England, while Will Salzmann is nursing a hamstring strain he suffered during the recent Sheffield Shield match against South Australia. 

Kurtis Patterson is being managed for this week's one-day match, while Jack Nisbet drops out of the squad. 

Elsewhere, Sean Abbott is two scalps away from becoming the fourth Blues player to take 100 One-Day Cup wickets, joining Stuart MacGill, Stuart Clark and Doug Bollinger.

The NSW Blues’ One-Day Cup match against South Australia commences at Cricket Central on Monday at 10am AEDT. Australian fans can watch the action on cricket.com.au and Kayo Sports.  

NSW Blues' One-Day Cup squad vs South Australia, November 16 at Cricket Central 

Lachlan Shaw (c), Sean Abbott, Joel Davies, Oliver Davies, Ben Dwarshuis, Bryce Jackson, Riley Kingsell, Sam Konstas, Nic Maddinson, Josh Philippe, Tanveer Sangha, Jake Scott
