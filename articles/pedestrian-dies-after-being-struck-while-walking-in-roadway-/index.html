ORANGEBURG COUNTY, S.C. (WCIV) — A pedestrian walking on US 301 in Orangeburg County died after they were struck by a vehicle early Saturday, according to the South Carolina Highway Patrol.

SCHP said that a SUV was travelling south on US 301 around 3:15 a.m. Saturday morning when it struck a pedestrian near Cross Creek Drive. The pedestrian was walking in the roadway and succumbed to their injuries on scene, according to SCHP.
