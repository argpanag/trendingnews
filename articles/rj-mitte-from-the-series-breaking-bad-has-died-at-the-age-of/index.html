RJ Mitte from the series "Breaking Bad" has died at the age of 34

TheTools · 13.10.2026 00:23 · source · permalink

Death

RJ Mitte played the son of chemistry teacher Walter White—portrayed by Bryan Cranston—in the TV series “Breaking Bad.” (File photo)

Keystone

Actor RJ Mitte, who played the son of chemistry teacher Walter White in the television series “Breaking Bad,” has died at the age of 34. His spokesperson did not disclose the cause of death.

"It is with deep sadness that we announce the tragic death of RJ Mitte," his spokesperson, Sean Reilly, was quoted as saying in a statement on Tuesday.

In "Breaking Bad," a series considered one of the greatest in television history, RJ Mitte played Flynn, a teenager with cerebral palsy.

RJ Mitte himself lived with cerebral palsy, a condition that results in a permanent movement disorder due to early brain damage—which occurs before birth or during childhood. “Inspired by his own experiences with cerebral palsy, RJ Mitte brought authenticity to his role and created a platform dedicated to raising awareness about disability and inclusion,” the press release stated.

Commitment to Combating Stigmatization

The actor used his career in Hollywood to advocate for people with disabilities. He served as an ambassador for the organizations “United Cerebral Palsy” and “Shriners Children’s” and ran his own nonprofit foundation, the “Mitte Foundation.” This foundation supports various philanthropic initiatives and is committed to combating prejudice against people with disabilities.

In addition to his role in “Breaking Bad,” RJ Mitte also appeared in the series “The Guardians of Justice” and “Switched,” as well as in the films “The Recall” and “The Oak Room.” He was on the verge of the release of a new film, “Talking to Strangers,” which he had been promoting just a few days ago.
