Flooding possible as thunderstorms, rain move through region

TheTools · 10.09.2026 20:44 · source · permalink

Multiple rounds of heavy rainfall and storms could lead to localized flooding and damaging winds in the Louisville area Sept. 10, according to the latest forecast by the National Weather Service in Louisville.

The rounds of heavy showers and thunderstorms are expected as a slow moving cold front and upper disturbance converge in the region, officials said in a Sept. 10 briefing. Repeated rounds of strong storms could result in localized damaging winds and a localized flooding threat, according to the briefing.

Some areas could see new rainfall amounts up to 3 inches, though most will see less than an inch.
