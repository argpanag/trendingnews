Over $446K raised for Michigan grocer harassed by streamers

TheTools · 31.10.2026 06:55 · source · permalink

by CHARLOTTE HAZARD | The National News Desk

TOPICS:

WASHINGTON (TNND) — Over $400,000 was raised for a Red Baron employee through a GoFundMe after he was filmed by streamers for a prank video.

A Red Baron employee from Michigan went viral after being filmed at work while he was stocking a grocery store freezer.

Some streamers approached him and began to film him and they pretended to steal money from him and film his reaction. The man, known as Walt Z by the internet, snapped at the influencers.

“It’s not a joke to me,” he said in the video. “I don’t have time for this crap in my life. My life is hard enough as it is.”

He said that he has children at home and he was supposed to be home with them and he has to be up at 5 a.m. to stock more stores.

The video went viral and a GoFundMe was started to support the man who was being harassed.

Walt Z has been “blown away” by the outpouring of support he has received after the video went viral, according to The Detroit News.

His wife had said they are declining media interviews at the moment.
