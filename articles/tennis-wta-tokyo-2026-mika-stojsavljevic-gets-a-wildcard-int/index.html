Mika Stojsavljevic [W] v Alexandra Eala [8] | H2H & Match Fundamentals

Mika Stojsavljevic is granted a wildcard into Tokyo for a second time, as she bids for a first WTA main draw wi, when she faces Philippines rising star Alexandra Eala.

 

Tale of the Tape – Key Stats (2026)

Not surprisingly the more experienced Eala leads most of the stats, with Stojsavljevic just edging her out on overall service points won.

 

 

Playing Styles & Prediction

The rangy British teen already has a potent first serve, which should get her a few free points and against the more compact Eala. What she might lack in height and power, Eala makes up for in precision and variety on her serve.

Because the Brit has a better overall service points won stat, it will be up to Eala to dig into those service games, and her reputation as a solid returner will be key, as she will look to grind out the points, making Stojsavljevic work for every point.

At this early stage of her career, the Brit has a lot to improve in terms of her footwork around the court, so expect her to try and end points early. It’s a great chance to measure herself against one of the tour’s rising stars this year, and all counts as good experience at this stage.

Prediction: Eala in straight sets.
