Fire at Novoshakhtinsk oil refinery is visible even from space, Ukraine's General Staff says

TheTools · 24.11.2026 05:16 · source · permalink

Ukrainian forces struck the Novoshakhtinsk oil refinery in Russia's Rostov Oblast overnight on 22-23 November.

Source: General Staff of the Armed Forces of Ukraine

Quote: "Explosions rocked the refinery, and the resulting fire is visible even in satellite imagery.

A bitumen unit, a crude oil distillation tower, a reboiler furnace and a tank farm were hit."

Details: The Ukrainian military also reported hitting a Pole-21 electronic warfare system and a Pantsir anti-aircraft missile and gun system.

For reference: The Novoshakhtinsk refinery can process up to 7.5 million tonnes of crude oil a year. It produces diesel, marine fuel and fuel oil. The plant is just a few dozen kilometres from the Ukrainian border, giving its fuel the shortest supply route to Russian forces on the southern front.
