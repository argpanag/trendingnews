Multiple European flights forced to divert due to bomb threats

TheTools · 05.11.2026 10:35 · source · permalink
  • World
  • UK & Europe
  • Aviation

Updated November 5, 2026 - 12.13pm. First published at 10.35am

Bomb threats have forced emergency landings to a string of flights across Europe for the second day in a row.

More than a dozen flights to at least half a dozen cities were subject to the threats on Thursday morning (evening AEDT) and many had to divert to other airports.

An Aer Lingus Airbus A320 aircraft is seen at Evreux military air base after a flight from Dublin to Rome was diverted to France. AP Photo/Thomas Padilla

The travel disturbances come amid a series of disruptions to everyday life across Europe, including a bizarre string of cyberattacks targeting supermarket refrigerators.

Bulgaria’s Ministry of Transport and Communications said Ryanair flights from Madrid, Copenhagen and London to Sofia were subject to bomb threats.

Two of them continued on as normal while one was diverted to nearby Plovdiv.

No issues were identified after landing and both airports had returned to normal, the ministry said. 

Czech police said searches of four planes that landed in Prague after being targeted by bomb threats were clear.

Aer Lingus said flight EI402 from Dublin to Rome diverted to Normandy “as a security precaution”.

Three Ryanair flights bound for Sofia, Bulgaria, had to make emergency landings AAP

“The flight landed safely and Aer Lingus will work to reaccommodate customers to their final destination as soon as possible,” the company said.

LOT Polish Airlines said flight LO235 from Warsaw to Brussels landed safely at its destination after a “potential security threat”. 

An onboard security check failed to find any suspicious items before authorities in Brussels carried out further searches. 

“This inspection also confirmed that no hazardous or suspicious items were found,” the airline said.

A Finnair flight from Helsinki to Rome landed safely at Rome’s Leonardo da Vinci airport escorted by Italian air force fighter jets, according to an airport official with knowledge of the situation.

Nothing out of the ordinary was found on the plane after it landed, Finnish broadcaster Yle reported, citing the airline.

The flight landed safely at the base in Evreux in Normandy. Aer Lingus

A flight to Warsaw landed in Vienna following a letter to Polish authorities containing an “abstract threat”, said Stefan Loidl, a police spokesperson in Lower Austria province. 

He said Vienna Airport was also threatened in relation to four other flights, which he declined to identify.

An Aer Lingus flight from Ireland to Italy was diverted to a military air base in western France because of a bomb threat and landed safely, the French prime minister’s office said.

Flight tracking sites showed that the Aer Lingus Dublin-Rome flight was approaching Paris when it turned sharply off its trajectory and made several loops before heading toward Evreux.

An Aer Lingus Airbus A320 aircraft is seen at Evreux military air base after a flight from Dublin to Rome was diverted. AP Photo/Thomas Padilla

Romania's intelligence agency said a Wizz Air flight from Milan to Bucharest received a bomb threat, but landed safely at its destination.

An Aegean Airlines flight from Thessaloniki, and a Ryanair flight from Porto landed safely at their destination at Milan's Malpensa, an airport source said on Thursday.

Several flights across Europe received hoax bomb threats earlier this week that prompted searches and diversions.

The flights included LOT, Scandinavian Airlines, Wizz Air and Ryanair.

Such threats aren’t a new phenomenon but come at a time of increased geopolitical tensions and signs of “disruption”.

In addition to the supermarket attacks, there have been drone incursions and mysterious triggers to alert apps in and around Russia and Ukraine as authorities have warned that hybrid threats abound.

No one has claimed responsibility for the bomb threats.
