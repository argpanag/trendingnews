Hailey Bieber opens up about intense online hate amid reported Selena Gomez ‘feud’

Model and Justin Bieber’s wife Hailey Bieber has spoken about the harassment she faced following her marriage to the singer. She revealed that the experience took a severe toll on her mental health.

Jelena were fan favourites

Justin was previously in an on-and-off relationship with Selena Gomez for nearly a decade before the two parted ways. Months after his split from Selena, Justin married Hailey in 2018. Since then, the internet has frequently pitted Hailey and Selena against each other, with fans often comparing the two.

Although the two women attempted to put the speculation to rest and even posed together at the Academy Museum Gala in 2023, the online discussions have continued over the years. Speaking at an event in New York on Wednesday, Hailey recalled the disturbing treatment she received from strangers and social media users. “The way people were making me feel... I had people telling me to kill myself in the comments every single day. I was going through a time where people were yelling at me in the street,” she shared.

Hailey vs  Selena

She further recalled how people would publicly pit her against Selena. “People were putting tip jars up in Starbucks of me versus this person,” she rued referring to Gomez. Opening up about how deeply the constant hostility affected her, Hailey admitted, “I had never experienced anything like that before. So the way that people were making me feel was that maybe it would be better if I wasn’t here anymore. Because that’s how intense I felt other people’s feelings were towards me at the time.”
