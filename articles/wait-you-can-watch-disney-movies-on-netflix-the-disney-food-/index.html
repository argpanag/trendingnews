Wait! You Can Watch Disney Movies On… Netflix? | the disney food blog

TheTools · 04.10.2026 05:03 · source · permalink

By Sydney Leever

Between Disney+, Hulu, HBO, Apple TV, and Netflix, movie and TV show lovers have SO many options to choose from for entertainment. But in the age of movies and shows being exclusive to one streaming service, it can be hard to afford everything at once.

©Netflix

If you’re a Netflix subscriber craving some Disney shows, then today is your lucky day. Disney Entertainment has licensed Disney and Hulu shows to stream on Netflix!

Disney Entertainment and Netflix have entered a major new content licensing agreement that will bring a mix of library titles and current-season TV shows to Netflix for varying periods of time, according to Variety.

©Netflix

Seasons one and two of the Disney+ original series Percy Jackson and the Olympians will be available on Netflix globally beginning October 4th as part of a three-month promotional window in advance of its season three premiere on Disney+ on November 20th.

©Disney

Additionally, all five of 20th Century Studios’ Ice Age films will be available on Netflix worldwide beginning October 4th for three months ahead of the franchise’s sixth installment, Ice Age: Boiling Point. The six-quel will release in theaters on February 5th, 2027.

©Disney

Seasons one through four of police procedural Will Trent will be on Netflix globally on November 3rd, ahead of the season five premiere on ABC in early 2027.

©Disney

The Tim Allen and Kat Dennings sitcom Shifting Gears will come to Netflix beginning December 1st in the U.S. and January internationally, ahead of the season three premiere on ABC in early 2027.

Matt, the widowed owner of a classic car restoration shop, is confronted with his most unexpected and challenging project yet when his estranged daughter, Riley, and her adolescent children come crashing back into his life. ©Disney

You can also watch some iconic Walt Disney Animation and Pixar movies on Netflix. In early 2027, Soul, Elio, Raya and the Last Dragon, and more children’s movies will stream on Netflix globally, with title availability and launch dates varying by region.

Soul ©Pixar | Disney
