‘Fire Country’ Fans, You’ll Be Absolutely Devastated by This Season 5 Episode News

TheTools · 21.11.2026 01:12 · source · permalink

fi

Fire Country fans, it's going to be a while before we return to Edgewater.

Season 5 has been a roller coaster for viewers, with new characters joining Station 42 and potential romances heating up. This is all as Bode Leone (Max Thieriot) creates a volunteer fire department that seems to have had more disasters than triumphs as of late.

While viewers were hoping to see more from our favorite firefighters, a new episode of Fire Country isn't airing tonight—here's why.

Sergei Bachlakov - CBS

When will Fire Country return with new episodes?

The next Fire Country season 5 episode will air on Friday, December 11, 2026, at 9 p.m. ET on CBS.

Why isn't Fire Country on tonight?

The change is due to multiple scheduling conflicts.

On November 20, the University of Kentucky men's college basketball team, the Wildcats, will play its rival team, the Indiana University Hoosiers. The game is scheduled for 8 p.m. ET and will air on CBS during the time slots for both Fire Country and its spinoff, Sheriff Country.

In the weeks following the big game, Thanksgiving and other sports events will affect the schedule.

What will the next Fire Country episode be about?

According to an official promo, the next episode, titled "Fight the Future," will follow Station 42 as it races to save a man who got buried in a frozen industrial accident. Elsewhere, Sharon Leone (Diane Farr)'s life is at risk when she's caught in a dangerous situation that could expose a secret she's been holding. Additionally, Morena Baccarin crosses over from Sheriff Country to guest star as Sheriff Mickey Fox.
