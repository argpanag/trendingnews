Two storms to track

TheTools · 22.11.2026 10:53 · source · permalink

Sunday will be mainly dry for the Philadelphia Marathon, but rain will arrive in the afternoon.

Runners for the Philadelphia Marathon may cross the finish line without getting rained on, but that won’t be true for the whole day Sunday. NBC10 First Alert Meteorologist Justin Godynick has the full forecast. 

Overnight, skies become mostly cloudy, with mild conditions. Lows around 50 in the City.

For the marathon on Sunday, conditions are mainly dry, with only a slight risk of a passing shower. More rain arrives during the afternoon though. Highs in the lower 60's, with the heaviest rainfall during the evening hours.
