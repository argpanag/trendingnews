Nations Championship finals confirmed as England face immediate All Blacks rematch

TheTools · 22.11.2026 02:58 · source · permalink

England face an immediate rematch against New Zealand at Allianz Stadium on Saturday in the battle to decide third place in the inaugural Nations Championship.

Steve Borthwick’s side demolished the All Blacks 37-12 and their reward is the chance to claim an unprecedented hat-trick of victories against their rivals as one of six matches at Twickenham next weekend.

Both teams finished second in their hemisphere pools with Six Nations champions France and World Cup holders South Africa topping the tables to contest the overall final on Sunday week in a repeat of their classic two rounds earlier.

Ireland will battle it out with Australia for fifth place on Friday night, while Scotland take on Argentina and Wales meet Japan.

Friday, November 27 –

1640 Italy v Fiji

2010 Ireland v Australia

Saturday, November 28 –

1310 Wales v Japan

1640 England v New Zealand

Sunday, November 29 –

1310 Scotland v Argentina

1640 France v South Africa
