Strictly’s Johannes says doing the same would become "monotonous" as he responds to 'shakeup' talk

TheTools · 15.09.2026 07:00 · source · permalink

"Strictly is never the same..."

Thanks to a trio of new presenters, who are replacing previous show staples Tess Daley and Claudia Winkleman, a number of brand-new professional dancers, and a series of other switch-ups, there's been a lot of chatter about how Strictly Come Dancing is going to be fresh and new for 2026.

But new hosts Emma Willis, Johannes Radebe and Josh Widdicombe don't necessarily feel that way.

"Strictly is never the same," Johannes told Digital Spy and other media recently. "I feel like every year we have twists and turns, and surprises, and I think this year more than ever - you know, it does become monotonous when we just do the same thing…"

Emma added: "The heart of Strictly will always be the same, but you can't be on air for over two decades and never change anything…"

Johannes also pointed to the "Instant Dance" twist being introduced last year, confirming it will be back this year due to its popularity.

"It's not even changing it, is it," Emma mused. "You don't want to change it, but little tweaks and turns every now and then, good!"

Johannes then teased that "there’ll definitely be more dancing in this launch show than there ever was", with Emma and Josh even getting involved in the action too.

"I'm looking forward to that," Johannes said. It sounds like Strictly's new presenters will be part of "opening the whole show" with everyone set to take part, and Emma clarified that she'd be a "tiny" part of it.

As for experiencing the rest of the series in the presenting spot, Josh explained that he is "really looking forward to Halloween week".

"We really want to dress up. We want to be part of it," he added, before later revealing that he was also looking forward to the bit where the voting opens, "because that's something you don't get to do on the Christmas one, and that always looks like the most fun!"

The comedian named "one of my favourite bits" as being the part where they "cut to Craig looking annoyed".

Emma added: "I'm also really looking forward to Blackpool –", before Josh jumped in: "Yeah! That's a better answer."

Emma said she hasn't been in Blackpool Tower since she was a kid. "I really want to go back," she explained.

This is your chance, Emma!

Strictly Come Dancing returns on BBC One and BBC iPlayer on Saturday 19 September.
