Second, stronger quake jolts NZ just hours after first

TheTools · 26.10.2026 10:00 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

A second magnitude 4-plus earthquake has jolted parts of New Zealand today.

The latest, a magnitude 4.5 quake, struck near Blenheim at 7.24pm.

GeoNet said it hit 20km southwest of the town at a depth of 31km. It described the shaking as moderate.

The quake was primarily felt across the upper South Island, although some people in the lower North Island also reported feeling it.

More than 1900 people submitted felt reports to GeoNet. Most said the shaking was weak or light, while 118 reported moderate shaking, 10 strong shaking, one severe shaking and one extreme shaking.

“Good wee shake and rattle here in Picton,” one person commented on Facebook.

“Quite a wobble,” another added.

It came just hours after a magnitude 4.0 earthquake struck near Whanganui.

That quake hit at 3.33pm, about 30km south of Whanganui, at a depth of 12km, according to GeoNet.

The shaking was classed as light, although some residents reported stronger shaking.

Locals raced to social media to describe the quake, with several comparing the rumbling to a passing heavy truck.

One person said they heard the quake approaching before feeling a sudden jolt.

“Heard it coming, thought it was the wind then bang!” they wrote on Facebook.

Another described hearing “a bang, then a rumble then the shake”, while a resident in Aramoho reported a “big jolt”.

GeoNet reminded people to drop, cover and hold during an earthquake.
