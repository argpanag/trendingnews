Giants vs. Commanders: How to Watch, Listen & Live Stream Week 10

TheTools · 12.11.2026 23:34 · source · permalink

The New York Giants will host the Washington Commanders on Thursday Night Football in Week 10. Kickoff is scheduled for Thursday, November 12th at 8:15 p.m. ET on Prime.

In regular season play, the teams have met 187 times. The Giants lead the all-time series, 107-74-6. In their most recent regular season meeting, the Giants and Commanders tied, 27-27, at Northwest Stadium in Week 5 of the 2026 season. The Giants last defeated the Commanders, 31-19, in Week 11 of the 2023 season.

The Giants are looking for their first win at home against the Commanders since Week 7 of the 2023 season.
