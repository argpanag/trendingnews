Barcelona Face Athletic Club in Liga F Top Four Clash

TheTools · 22.11.2026 17:45 · source · permalink

Barcelona Femení prepared to defend its Liga F leadership against fourth-place Athletic Club at the Estadi Johan Cruyff on Sunday, November 22, 2026. The 7 p.m. meeting represented a significant challenge for Pere Romeu's side, which aimed to strengthen its position before the international break.

According to the match preview published by Yahoo Sports, Barcelona had collected 28 points from 11 league matches, recording nine victories, one draw and one defeat. Athletic occupied fourth place with 19 points, making the encounter an important test between two teams competing near the top of the standings.

Athletic arrived in Barcelona following an impressive 4-1 victory over Real Sociedad. The Basque side had also secured a 1-1 draw against Real Madrid, demonstrating its ability to compete against leading Liga F opponents, according to Mundo Deportivo.

Barcelona, meanwhile, had dropped points in a league draw against Deportivo de A Coruña. The team had also continued competing in the UEFA Women's Champions League, where it secured a comfortable victory, according to comments from Athletic manager Victor Martín reported by Mundo Deportivo.

Martín acknowledged the difficulty of facing the defending league leaders while emphasizing that his players were prepared for the challenge.

"They are a tremendous team. We cannot base our assessment solely on results. This week they competed in the Champions League and won comfortably.

I did not like them as much as in previous matches. They had come from two draws, and there is too much focus on results. If we judge performances by a draw or a victory...

We know they are the best team in the world and have the best coach in the world. It will be difficult, but we are ready," said Martín, Athletic Club's manager.

The Athletic coach also cautioned against allowing recent victories to create unrealistic expectations. He emphasized the importance of maintaining a balanced assessment of the team's performances throughout the season.

"We must not get carried away at all. We have had matches with chances that we failed to convert. We need to maintain a balanced assessment, especially from my position," Martín said.

Preparing for Barcelona's attacking quality will require discipline throughout the match, according to the Athletic manager. He highlighted the individual ability of Barcelona's players and the importance of maintaining concentration under pressure.

"We have to do many things well, hope they are not at their best, and avoid being carried away by emotions. We must remain focused. They have players who have won Ballon d'Or awards and can make the difference.

We need an extremely high level of concentration. It is a very interesting challenge to expose ourselves to that level of competition," Martín said.

Barcelona also faced selection difficulties ahead of the fixture. Romeu could not call on Kika Nazareth, Vicky López, Laia Aleixandri and Kerolin Nicoli, who remained unavailable. Laia Cabetas was also absent from the squad compared with the previous league selection, according to Yahoo Sports.

Despite those absences, Barcelona intended to use its home advantage to maintain its position at the summit. The match also marked the team's final club competition commitment before the international break.

The fixture was scheduled for 7 p.m. at the Estadi Johan Cruyff as part of the 12th round of Liga F. Viewers in Spain could follow the match through TV3, Teledeporte, DAZN, RTVE Play and 3Cat.
