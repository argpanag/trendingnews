  4. 'This is the foundation'published at 22:44 GMT 27 November
    22:44 GMT 27 November

    FT: Nottm Forest 3-2 Chelsea

    Nottingham Forest

    Nottingham Forest manager Oliver Glasner, speaking to Sky Sports: "A very good game tonight and of course when you can win against Chelsea here and have back-to-back wins at home here at the City Ground against [Manchester] City and Chelsea you must be doing many things really well and I think the players did very well today.

    "A great start, a fantastic goal from Igor Jesus, came out great from half-time and scoring the second goal. I think from then we need to improve our game management because we gave them two goals after losing the ball very easily and here we need to do better.

    "I think the third goal was an amazing pass from Schlager to Morgan Gibbs-White to finish amazing[ly] so, overall a very good performance a great win."

    On gaining the trust from players now in his project: "We had the buy-in [from the players] from day one to be honest. When you're talking about this you need the requirements because if we had played [the match] we also would have done the same but I'm definitely not able to do it!

    "It's not just to want it, it's also to be able to and you get this resilience and physicality in the training sessions. This is hard work from them we're talking about every single day. If the players wouldn't have bought in, we couldn't have played how we are out-running Chelsea by six kilometers and this is what we've massively improved and this is the foundation.

    "If we want to press like we did today, especially in the first half, we need the highest intensity otherwise they are too good to find the spare player.

    "If the press is not high enough or if you're a little bit late or too slow, then the quality is too good and they find this free player.

    "But we need to get more benefit from these kind of pressures in winning the ball. We're then a little bit in a rush or sloppy with the passes and then we don't get the clear finish. We should have had two or three more clear chances but in the end 3-2 is a good result."

    On building on these results: "It's every single game, every single result is a part of this development on this journey. We learn a lot from the defeats as well.

    "We were struggling at the beginning to score the goals, especially here at the City Ground. We could see the progress but we couldn't get the results in many other games and now it's of course to keep working. Just 12 games played with 12 points so it's still not satisfying for us.

    "Many games to go and for us it's to stay humble and keep working."

  5. 'We are heading into the right direction'published at 22:41 GMT 27 November
    22:41 GMT 27 November

    FT: Nottm Forest 3-2 Chelsea

    Nottingham Forest

    Nottingham Forest manager Oliver Glasner, speaking to BBC Match of the Day: "A great win, fantastic atmosphere at the City Ground and we could have made it a bit easier for us but we also have to consider and see the quality Chelsea has individually and of course in the box converting.

    "But big win and I think we scored very nice goals. At the end there I think it was a very exciting Friday evening."

    On forcing errors out of Chelsea: "Of course, but that was just one side of the medal. We're doing everything to win the ball and for the next action. I think here we still have a lot of potential, especially in the first half. We won so many balls in a lot of good positions and then the first one or two passes we were not accurate enough.

    "The effort was top and the tactical side the plahyers did really well and this is how we could win against City at home and make it very difficult for Chelsea."

    On Igor Thiago's goal: "If he scores like that, he can hit it with his knee, we don't care. But I think it was a great attack before and we were well-organised.

    "We know the reason for starting Igor Jesus was because he's so smart in the box with his movements and he has a feeling like a striker and he showed it in this situation."

    On recent performances: "We're talking about it [now] but it's really from day number one. It's getting stability and consistency into the club and into the team. The players are working very hard and we can see a great performances.

    "We are still a little bit having too many ups and downs but my feeling is we are heading into the right direction.

    "The players are believing in what we are doing but we're still talking about game management. We were 3-1 up and we're giving the ball away too easily or score 2-0 and lose the ball away easily - letting Chelsea back into the game.

    "So there is still a lot to improve but every win, every good performance helps us in our development."

Premier League highlights: Nottingham Forest 3-2 Chelsea

Igor Jesus' stunning volley and two goals from Morgan Gibbs-White helped Nottingham Forest beat struggling Chelsea, who lost their third Premier League game in a row.

Brazil striker Jesus connected with a right-wing cross from Daniel Munoz and the strike soared past a diving Emiliano Martinez into the top corner to give the hosts a fifth-minute lead.

Forest doubled their advantage six minutes into the second half when Gibbs-White converted a penalty after ex-Chelsea striker Liam Delap, who constantly caused problems for his former club, was bundled over by Wesley Fofana.

The French defender had a troubled night where he was repeatedly booed by Chelsea fans following an unauthorised interview with French YouTuber Zach Nani.

His side failed to register a shot on target in the first half, but pulled a goal back in the 54th minute.

Pedro Neto ran at the home defence and his shot bounced to substitute Emmanuel Emegha, who netted for the second successive match.

Forest, though, restored their two-goal advantage in the 72nd minute as Xaver Schlager played a perfectly weighted pass for Gibbs-White to provide a lofted finish past Martinez.

Emegha set up a tense finale with his second of the night in the 89th minute, heading home a Neto cross, but Forest held on for a first win over Chelsea at the City Ground since 1997.

The victory took Oliver Glasner's side up from 17th to 14th place, while Chelsea remain eighth.

Chelsea analysis: More defensive problems for Alonso

Xabi Alonso admits difficult moment as Chelsea lose third successive league game

After failing to qualify for European football, Xabi Alonso was appointed Chelsea manager in May with the hope of reviving their fortunes, but his side have now only won one game in six in all competitions as their season threatens to unravel.

After losing 4-1 at home to Leeds last Saturday, the Spaniard responded by dropping centre-half Levi Colwill, with Moises Caicedo moving from his usual midfield role into the defence.

But Forest, who had only scored nine times in their first 11 league matches, needed just five minutes to open the scoring, although no blame could be attributed to Caicedo as Jesus struck in superb style from 15 yards out, albeit off his shin.

Delap moved from Chelsea to Forest in the summer in a deal that could be worth £50m, after netting just one Premier League goal for the Blues.

But he was a constant threat, with Fofana struggling to cope with the forward's power. After conceding the penalty which led to Forest's second goal, Fofana was replaced shortly afterwards by Colwill.

Chelsea could have done with Delap still in their side as they offered little in the opening half, with forward Danny Welbeck only touching the ball seven times before being replaced by Emegha at the break.

Dutchman Emegha claimed his first goal in the loss to Leeds, but finished well twice off the bench as his side lost for the sixth time in the league under Alonso.

The Blues boss, though, would have been furious by Forest's decisive third goal amid more slack defending, with Schlager allowed to play a fine ball in behind and Gibbs-White getting ahead of the visiting backline to score his second.

That is now seven goals conceded in two matches and 23 in their opening 12 Premier League games - the joint-most since the 2015-16 campaign at the same stage - with only bottom club Crystal Palace conceding more.

Forest analysis: Glasner rewarded for attacking team selection

Oliver Glasner praises Igor Jesus as Nottingham Forest beat Chelsea

While Chelsea are on a downward turn, Forest - under Glasner - are moving in the right direction.

They now have won three times in the league, but two of those have come in back-to-back home matches - the first time since April 2025 - after they produced arguably their best performance of the season in beating Manchester City 3-1 on 7 November.

Forest could not build on that success as they lost 2-0 at Bournemouth, with Glasner expecting a response against Chelsea.

He would have been delighted to get that on a cold Friday night in the rain at the City Ground.

Delap gave an indication of what was to come as he shot on the turn inside the opening 90 seconds as Chelsea struggled with Forest's intensity and pressing and Glasner was rewarded for playing with two strikers.

Jesus, on his first league start since August, scored a fine goal soon after and the hosts were good value for their two-goal lead after Gibbs-White's penalty.

Glasner, who won the Conference League with Crystal Palace last season before becoming Forest manager in the summer, had received public backing from owner Evangelos Marinakis earlier this week following a disappointing start to the campaign.

His side responded by scoring a quarter of their league goals so far this season in one match to delight the City Ground faithful.

There is still a lot of work to do, but Glasner and Marinakis will hope this will be the start of a push to pull them well away from the relegation zone.
