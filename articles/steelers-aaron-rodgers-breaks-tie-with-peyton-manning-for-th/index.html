Steelers' Aaron Rodgers breaks tie with Peyton Manning for third all time in career TD passes

TheTools · 12.10.2026 00:25 · source · permalink

Aaron Rodgers has passed up Peyton Manning.

Rodgers threw his 580th career touchdown pass on Sunday in the Pittsburgh Steelers’ 34-22 win over the Indianapolis Colts. The 80-yard score to running back Jaylen Warren in the first quarter broke a tie with Manning for third all time (including postseason).

Rodgers, a future Hall of Famer, and Manning, a 2021 Hall of Fame inductee, had been tied at 579 TD tosses after the Steelers signal-caller’s three-TD night in a Week 4 loss to the Cleveland Browns. Now, he’s officially surpassed "The Sheriff" on the career list.

The Steelers QB followed up that score with two TD tosses to Pat Freiermuth in the second and fourth quarters, his 581st and 582nd TD passes.

Rodgers has had at least three TD passes in three straight games for the first time since Weeks 11 to 14 of his 2021 MVP season with the Packers, per NFL Research

Sunday's win over the Colts was also Rodgers' 90th career game with at least three touchdown passes, joining Tom Brady (104), Drew Brees (97) and Manning (93) as the only players in NFL history with at least 90 such games.

Rodgers now trails only Tom Brady (737) and Drew Brees (608) in career touchdowns passes, postseason included

With the 42-year-old having previously stated he’ll be retiring at season’s end, he’ll more than likely finish his career as third in NFL history.

It’s but the latest accomplishment in an astounding career for Rodgers.

Atop his résumé, Rodgers is a four-time AP NFL Most Valuable Player, each of his awards claimed during his 18 seasons with the Green Bay Packers. During his time in Green Bay, Rodgers also won a Super Bowl, was voted to 10 Pro Bowls and earned four All-Pro first-team honors.

Rodgers is also fifth all time in completions, fifth in passing yards and, after Sunday, all by himself in third place for TD passes.
