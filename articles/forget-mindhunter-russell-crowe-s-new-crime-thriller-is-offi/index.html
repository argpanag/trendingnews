Russell Crowe Is Coming Off His Best-Reviewed Movie in Nearly 3 Decades

His latest film is Unabomber, a psychological thriller starring Jacob Tremblay as the infamous Ted Kaczynski and Russell Crowe as the Harvard professor who studied him as part of an academic exercise. The film unfolds in dual timelines; the first follows Kaczynski in college and the second during his years-long manhunt. According to FlixPatrol, Unabomber emerged as an instant hit on Netflix, replacing Best of the Best at the top of the streamer's viewership charts. However, the poor reception definitely dents some of the goodwill that Crowe had earned only last week, with the release of his best-reviewed movie in nearly three decades: The Weight.
