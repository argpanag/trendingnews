How to watch and follow the England Men v Pakistan and Sri Lanka

TheTools · 20.10.2026 14:49 · source · permalink

England Men take on Pakistan and Sri Lanka in an ODI tri-series, with live coverage on Sky Sports and BBC Sport

The buildup to the 2027 ICC Cricket World Cup is well underway, with England Men in Pakistan for an ODI tri-series with the hosts and Sri Lanka.

Brendon McCullum's side play four round-robin matches, followed by a potential final on 31 October. 

Here's how you can follow all of the action from Pakistan.

Scorecards

You can follow all the action on the ECB website and through the England Cricket app. Scorecards will be updated live throughout each ODI, with additional insights available through in-app match centres.

To download the England Cricket app use the links below.

Download on iOS
Download on Android

You can watch every ball of England Men's ODI tri-series against Pakistan and Sri Lanka on Sky Sports Cricket.

Subscribe to Sky through your television provider, or sign up for a NOW TV Sports pass.

BBC Test Match Special will be bringing you ball-by-ball commentary of every England Men's match in the ODI tri-series.

You can listen to live coverage of the England Men v Pakistan series on BBC Sounds, BBC Radio 5 Sports Extra and the BBC Sport website and app.

20 October - England Men v Pakistan, Rawalpindi (9.00am BST)
22 October - England Men v Sri Lanka, Rawalpindi (9.00am BST)
25 October - England Men v Pakistan, Lahore (8.00am GMT)
29 October - England Men v Sri Lanka, Lahore (8.00am GMT)
31 October - ODI Tri-Series Final, Lahore (8.00am GMT)
