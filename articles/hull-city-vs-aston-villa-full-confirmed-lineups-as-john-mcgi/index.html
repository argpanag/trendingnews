Hull City vs Aston Villa: Full confirmed lineups as John McGinn starts in Premier League 2026-27 match

TheTools · 05.09.2026 16:50 · source · permalink

Premier League

Villains are still looking for their first win of the season.

Hull City and Aston Villa are scheduled to meet in Matchday 3 of the Premier League 2026-27 season. The two teams enter the game in quite different situations. While the hosts have had a solid start to the season, Aston Villa are still hunting for their first points following two defeats.

Looking at recent history, these two teams have met eight times in the not-too-distant past. Aston Villa have won three of these games, whereas Hull City has only won once. The last four matches ended in draws.

Hull City have had an excellent start to their Premier League season, exceeding expectations. They are third in the table, with an impeccable record of two victories in two matches. This amazing record is based on a rock-solid defence that has yet to yield a single league goal.

Their return to the top division includes a remarkable 2-0 home win over Manchester United. This was followed by a fiercely contested 1-0 away victory over Coventry. These results have created enormous momentum and a positive attitude within the club.

Aston Villa have had a rough start to their Premier League season, presently lying in 19th position with zero points. They have lost their first two matches, failing to score and surrendering five goals. This is a major departure from their successful finish to last season, when they collected the UEFA Europa League trophy.
