Juwai Teer results for 2024 are arranged here as a month-by-month chart of the Jowai draws: Juwai Teer, Juwai Morning Teer and Juwai Night Teer. Reading down a month's table gives each day's first and second round numbers in order, which makes the Juwai teer chart the quickest way to find an old result, confirm a number someone has quoted, or look at the houses and endings that appeared over several weeks.

Juwai Teer Result Chart 2024: the first round (FR) and second round (SR) results of Juwai Teer, Juwai Morning Teer and Juwai Night Teer from September 2024 to December 2024, one table per month.

Use Select Year to open the chart of another year, Select Teer Type to show one game only and Select Month to jump to that month's table.

Scheduled times: Juwai Teer FR at 2:00 PM and SR at 2:40 PM; Juwai Morning Teer FR at 10:30 AM and SR at 11:30 AM; Juwai Night Teer FR at 9:30 PM and SR at 10:30 PM.

A round shown as XX has not been declared yet, and OFF means no result was published for that round.
