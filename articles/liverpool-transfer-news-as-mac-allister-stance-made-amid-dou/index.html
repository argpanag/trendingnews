Liverpool transfer news as Mac Allister stance made amid double midfield blow

TheTools · 24.11.2026 05:00 · source · permalink

Alexis Mac Allister has a dwindling contract at Liverpool(Image: (Photo by Steffan Bowen/Visionhaus/Getty Images))

Liverpool has made a good start to the season under Andoni Iraola, with hopes of a positive finish in the Premier League for his first campaign in charge being boosted by the recent thrashing of rivals Manchester United.

Attention quickly has to turn to the next set of challenges, with the task of balancing the league alongside the Champions League and the EFL Cup in the coming weeks.

By the time the Reds are out of the festive fixture congestion, it will be January, where some focus will be placed on the transfer window with some options out there to help bolster the squad.

So, with plans being put in place for the winter market, here's a look at the latest Liverpool transfer news and rumors ahead of another busy day of action.

Alexis Mac Allister (Liverpool)

Alexis Mac Allister has a dwindling contract at Liverpool(Image: (Photo by Gaspafotos/MB Media/Getty Images))

Alexis Mac Allister has made no secret of the fact that he wants to sign a new contract with Liverpool, after publicly revealing on a few occasions that he is unhappy with the club's original stance surrounding a deal.

There is the chance of a U-turn, however, with recent reports claiming that talks have been opened up about a possible stay beyond the final 18 months he has left. With that in mind, Football Insider claims that Mac Allister wants to wrap up talks "as soon as possible" to confirm his future with the club.

There is some hope that it can be completed, though it remains to be seen whether Liverpool will progress discussions beyond exploratory talks.

Liverpool.com says: Alexis Mac Allister has been in inspired form for Liverpool this season, so it would be no surprise to see him handed a brand new contract.

There is little rush from Liverpool's side, regardless of the midfielder wanting to get a deal done quickly, so the Reds don't need to sign a deal that isn't viewed as ideal on their terms.

Lamine Camara (AS Monaco) and Alex Scott (Bournemouth)

Lamine Camara is among the players linked with a move to Liverpool(Image: Getty Images)

Chelsea have not ruled out the possibility of signing both Lamine Camara and Alex Scott in the January transfer window, according to TeamTalk, amid the pair being linked with a switch to Liverpool.

Camara, 22, came close to joining the Blues in the summer, but a move fell through on transfer deadline day. That has since opened up the possibility of the Reds swooping in, but that will depend on whether a move is made in January, especially if their rivals make an offer.

As for Scott, 23, he has also been rumored with a move to Anfield, having played under Andoni Iraola in the past. With his contract nearing the final 18 months, there is a chance of a deal this January, but it won't come cheap for whichever team does make an offer.

Liverpool.com says: Liverpool seems to be keeping an eye on the midfield ranks for the future, with Camara and Scott both appearing prominently when discussing potential transfer options.

While Chelsea's interest in both initially meant that a move for one may free up the other, the fact that the Blues could land both could make for an awkward situation.

That may prompt Liverpool to move in January, instead of the summer, to sign one or both of them, depending on the funds available.
