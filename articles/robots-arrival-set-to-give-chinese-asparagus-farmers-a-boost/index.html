Robots’ arrival set to give Chinese asparagus farmers a boost

TheTools · 08.11.2026 03:00 · source · permalink

Intelligent robots that can harvest, transport and sort the lucrative vegetable made their debut in Lianshui county last month

In the flat, typhoon-prone fields of Lianshui county in eastern China’s Jiangsu province, a once-obscure vegetable has become a local economic engine.

Greenhouses for growing asparagus now cover more than 1,333 hectares (3,294 acres), produce 50,000 tonnes (55,116 short tons) a year and generate over 1 billion yuan (US$150 million) in value, according to local government data.

Lianshui accounts for about 30 per cent of China’s greenhouse-grown asparagus output, and much of the high-value white asparagus it produces ends up on plates in Japan, South Korea and Europe – markets where local production is expensive and shrinking.
