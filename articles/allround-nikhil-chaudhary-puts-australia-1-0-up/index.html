Allround Nikhil Chaudhary puts Australia 1-0 up

TheTools · 24.11.2026 05:16 · source · permalink

Nikhil Chaudhary starred with an all-round show as Australia edged out England by three wickets in a thrilling last-over finish at the MCG to go 1-0 up in the five-match T20I series. Chaudhary picked 2-21 with the ball before making his T20I career-best 66 off 38 balls to set up a successful penultimate-ball finish.

After Australian captain Mitchell Marsh won the toss and elected to bowl, England suffered an immediate setback when opener Phil Salt was dismissed for a duck off the very first ball of the match. Aneurin Donald fell for 10 at the start of the third over, leaving England reeling at 13 for 2.

Captain Harry Brook then launched a fierce counterattack, capitalizing on two early dropped chances to smash 65 off just 28 balls, including four boundaries and half a dozen sixes. That included a hat-trick of maximums off Riley Meredith in the fourth over. Brook reached his half-century in 20 deliveries and added 97 runs for the third wicket with Tom Banton, propelling England to 74 for 2 inside the six-over powerplay and past 100 in the eighth over.

Australia regained momentum through Chaudhary, who dismissed Brook - caught by Sam Harper at 110 for 3. Ben Dwarshuis removed Banton for 39 off 22 balls in the next over, and Chaudhary then trapped Sam Curran to finish with impressive figures of 2 for 21. Despite Australia dropping six catches in the field, disciplined middle-over bowling restricted England's scoring rate. Jordan Cox (27 off 20) and Liam Dawson (17 off 14) stitched together a crucial 30-run seventh-wicket stand. Dawson was run out off the final ball as England finished with 187 for 9.

Australia got off to a sedate start in the chase, with Liam Dawson squeezing out two tight overs and an early wicket of Jake Fraser-McGurk in the opening over. Sam Harper injected early momentum with a brisk 32 off 15 balls, striking four fours and two sixes during a 50-run partnership with Marsh. After Harper fell to Henry Crocombe at the end of the Powerplay, at 53/2, Chaudhary anchored the chase alongside Marsh.

The Australian captain chipped in a vital 37 off 32 balls before falling to Sam Curran in the 11th over. Chaudhary, meanwhile, continued his all-round heroics, bringing up a 32-ball fifty and keeping Australia in contention alongside Tim David.

A late collapse of 4 for 25 then threatened to derail the hosts: Adil Rashid removed David, Will Jacks claimed two wickets in the 17th over to dismiss Lachlan Shaw and Joel Davies, and Jamie Overton had Chaudhary caught by Donald for 66 off 38 balls at the end of the 18th over, leaving Australia at 168 for 7.

Needing 11 runs off the final over bowled by Curran, Jack Edwards sealed the victory in dramatic fashion - hitting the first ball over fine leg for six, running two braces, and steering a full toss through point for four on the penultimate ball.

Brief scores: England 187/9 in 20 overs (Harry Brook 65, Tom Banton 39, Jordan Cox 27; Nikhil Chaudhary 2-21, Jack Edwards 2-27) lost to Australia 191/7 in 19.5 overs (Nikhil Chaudhary 66, Mitchell Marsh 37, Sam Harper 32, Jack Edwards 25*; Will Jacks 2-29) by 3 wickets
