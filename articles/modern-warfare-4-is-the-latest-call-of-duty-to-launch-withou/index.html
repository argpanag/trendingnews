Modern Warfare 4 Is the Latest Call of Duty to Launch without a PS5 Platinum Trophy

TheTools · 13.10.2026 17:30 · source · permalink

Back to HQ

Call of Duty is set to continue a trend that's been frustrating fans for a good few years; the PS5 version of the next instalment will arrive without a platinum Trophy.

That's because Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 will be part of the Call of Duty HQ launcher when it arrives later this month. Because the game is not technically a standalone app, it's counted as DLC, so won't have a full Trophy list.

The information comes via PlayStation Game Size, which has also confirmed the download size for Modern Warfare 4's campaign, multiplayer, and more.

Subscribe to Push Square on YouTube172k

Basically, it seems you'll be able to earn a handful of trinkets by playing the campaign, but if you were hoping to score a platinum, you're out of luck.

That's not to say the game will never have one, though. A few past Call of Duty games have retroactively been granted one when they're removed from CoD HQ and become separate downloads.

In future, Modern Warfare 4 might gain a plat, but initially you'll have to go without.
