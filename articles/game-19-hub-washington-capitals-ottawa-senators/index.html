Sens lines

Sam Ersson is in net, with noted Sens fan – Jackson Parsons – backing up for his first taste of NHL action. Ridly Greig returns as the team's third-line centre, meaning Dylan Cozens is back on Stützle's wing.

Caps lines

Logan Thompson is in net for the Caps, who are just missing blueliners Rasmus Sandin and Vincent Desharnais to injury.
