‘That’s my husband!’ Taylor Swift takes on full WAG status during SNF

TheTools · 21.09.2026 12:48 · source · permalink

KANSAS CITY, Mo. (Gray News) – Kansas City Chiefs tight end Travis Kelce found the end zone for the first time this season Sunday night, but it was Taylor Swift who stole the show from the luxury suite.

Cameras caught the 36-year-old superstar flashing her left ring finger and seemingly mouthing, “That’s my husband!” while celebrating Kelce’s touchdown.

Standing alongside Kelce’s father, Ed Kelce, Swift was seen laughing, cheering and showing off her ring to fellow suite guests in a vintage Chiefs tank and denim shorts.

The moment instantly set social media ablaze, with fans crowning her arrival into full NFL WAG status as Kelce racked up nine catches for 101 yards and a touchdown.

The viral celebration capped off a wild Sunday night showdown at GEHA Field at Arrowhead Stadium.

Swift married the tight end at Madison Square Garden on July 3.

After trading field goals in overtime, Harrison Butker drilled a 40-yard game-winner as time expired to keep Kansas City unbeaten at 2-0.

The Chiefs beat the Indianapolis Colts 33-30.

“I mean, I think guys were resilient, and we just strained to finish,” Chiefs quarterback Patrick Mahomes said. “It wasn’t always pretty. There were times we didn’t execute at a high level. But guys continued to fight and continued to push and we found a way at the end.”
