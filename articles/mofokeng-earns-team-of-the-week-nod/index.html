Mofokeng Earns Team of the Week Nod

Union SG Star Relebohile Mofokeng. Photo: Facebook/Royale Union Saint-Gilloise

Relebohile Mofokeng's goal for Union Saint-Gilloise in their 5-0 win over Lommel earned him a spot in Voetbalkrant's Team of the Week.

Goals have come thick and fast for the South African international following his move to the Belgian side from Orlando Pirates ahead of the 2026/27 season.

Mofokeng Earns Weekly Nod

Belgian publication Voetbalkrant revealed their team of the week after matchday 7, with Mofokeng one of three Union stars to make the side.

Earning a place on the right side of the attack, Mofokeng was praised for his attacking performances, including a well-taken goal.

"Relebohile Mofokeng was given a lot of freedom in his role and made eager use of it. He combined smoothly with Zorgane and Guilherme, popped up in the spaces Lommel left open, and capped off his performance with a goal," the publication said.

Life in Belgium

Mofokeng started life perfectly in Belgium by coming off the bench to score on debut against KVC Westerlo.

He made his first start against Sint-Truiden as a centre-forward, scoring in a 3-0 win. Benched a week later, he returned to the starting lineup against Zommel and scored his team's second goal.
