Singlife Account Is Closing by 16 Dec 2027: Bonus Returns End 16 Dec 2026

TheTools · 18.11.2026 00:00 · source · permalink

Singlife is discontinuing the Singlife Account, its app-based insurance savings plan. New applications have stopped, the 1% Booster Bonus and 0.5% Top-up Bonus end on 16 December 2026, and every remaining account will close by 16 December 2027. Here are the closure dates, what happens to your money and insurance cover, and what to do now.

> Quick view: Singlife is closing the Singlife Account. New applications have stopped, the 1% Booster Bonus and 0.5% Top-up Bonus end on 16 December 2026, and every remaining account closes by 16 December 2027. Make sure a valid bank account is linked in the app, because that is where your money goes when your account closes.

Singlife told customers this week that it will discontinue the Singlife Account, the app-based insurance savings plan that many people used as a spare savings pot. Its FAQ says the decision follows a review of the product's performance and competitiveness in the market over the years. The Straits Times reported the move on 18 November 2026.

Key dates

Singlife says it will tell each customer their own closure date.

What you earn until then

After the bonuses end, the account pays only its base return: 1.5% p.a. on the first S$10,000 and 1.0% p.a. on amounts above S$10,000, up to S$100,000. There is no return above S$100,000. Singlife declares these rates periodically and they are not guaranteed, so they can change before your account closes.

What happens to your money and cover

  • Money: whatever is left, including returns earned, is credited to your linked bank account when the account closes. Top-ups and withdrawals still work until then, subject to the usual limits.
  • No linked bank account: the account still closes on schedule, but Singlife cannot return the funds automatically and will contact you using your registered details.
  • No opting out: you cannot keep the account open past its closure date.
  • No direct transfer: you cannot move the money straight into another Singlife policy or a GROW account. Withdraw first.
  • Insurance cover: the death and terminal illness cover continues while the policy is in force and your daily account value is at least S$100. It ends when the account closes, though you can still claim for an insured event that happened while the policy was in force.
  • Other policies: your other Singlife policies are not affected.

What this means for you

  • Check your linked bank account today. In the app, make sure a valid account is linked and the details are current. This is the one step that avoids a delayed refund.
  • Switch any PayNow UEN payments. If you pay merchants through the Singlife Account PayNow UEN, move to another payment method before 15 December 2026.
  • Compare the base rate with alternatives. Once the bonuses stop on 16 December 2026, compare 1.5% on your first S$10,000 with bank savings accounts and fixed deposit promotions. You can withdraw any time, so there is no need to wait for the closure date.
  • Small balances close first. If your account has sat under S$100 for months, expect it to close from 16 December 2026.

Read the official details on Singlife's Singlife Account closure FAQ and the Singlife Account page.

The fine print

  • Base returns are declared periodically and are not guaranteed. Any revision takes immediate effect.
  • Closure dates follow Singlife's FAQ (accurate as at November 2026). Your own date will come in Singlife's closure notice, so contact Singlife Customer Service using the details in that notice if anything is unclear.
  • This article explains Singlife's announcement and is not financial advice. Please confirm the details with Singlife before you move money.
