Why Brandon Miller for NBA First Basket

Add in that Atlanta embarrassed Charlotte 128-81 in the first meeting and is laying 5.5 points tonight, and I expect the Hornets to try to get their best player going early instead of falling behind again.

Now the first basket data, which is where the value comes from.
