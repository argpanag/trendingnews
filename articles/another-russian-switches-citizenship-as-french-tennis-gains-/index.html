Another Russian switches citizenship as French tennis gains a top 100 player

TheTools · 17.09.2026 19:32 · source · permalink

Daria Kasatkina, Anastasia Potapova are just a few of the big Russian names who have decided to change their nationality in the past year from Russian and now France has a new Russian player to add to their ranks after the confirmation that Anna Blinkova will ditch her previous nationality.

According to RMC Sport who confirmed the news, the Moscow-born star who is 28 has changed her nationality after representing Russia since the start of her career.

But unlike perhaps others who have used it for other gain or perhaps don't have huge links to the countries they defect to, Blinkova does in this case with the now ex-Russian living in France for a number of years.

Blinkova has been based in Cannes for around 10 years and speaks French so in reality calls France her home anyway as opposed to the country of her birth. She trained there while learning French and has now decided to follow in the footsteps of Varvara Gracheva who became a French citizen in 2023.

Currently ranked World No.94, Blinkova is competing in the WTA 250 in Sao Paolo this week and will face Vendula Valdmannova. She becomes the second WTA player in the top 100 behind Diane Parry. Albeit Gracheva who also went down a similar route has previously struggled when it has come to getting into the Billie Jean King Cup team for instance.

But it seems that in the case of Blinkova, she is doing it more not only due to the current red tape for Russians in parts of the world but given too that she technically doesn't live in the country most of the time anyway.
