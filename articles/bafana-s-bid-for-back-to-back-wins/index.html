Bafana's bid for back-to-back wins

TheTools · 30.09.2026 19:04 · source · permalink

JOHANNESBURG - Bafana Bafana are targeting a second straight win in their AFCON qualifying campaign.

Pitso Mosimane’s charges are in the Egyptian capital Cairo, where they face Eritrea in their Group D clash, after opening with victory over Guinea.
