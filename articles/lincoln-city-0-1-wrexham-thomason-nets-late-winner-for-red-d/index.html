Lincoln City 0-1 Wrexham: Thomason nets late winner for Red Dragons

Wrexham have won each of their previous seven meetings with Lincoln City

George Thomason struck a late winner to earn Wrexham a crucial victory over Lincoln City, whose losing streak was extended to four matches.

Callum Doyle and Ollie Rathbone both spurned inviting chances for Phil Parkinson's side as Adam Reach, Ben House and Rob Street were among those to have efforts for the Imps.

But as the match appeared destined to end level, Thomason won possession from Daniel Oyegoke before coolly slotting beyond George Wickens for his second goal in three matches.

Wrexham - who have claimed seven of the previous nine points on offer - stay 14th but are just six points adrift of the Championship play-off spots, having kept a first away shutout in five games.

Defeat means Lincoln - who sit 20th, five points above the relegation zone - have lost four successive league games for the first time since between December 2023 and January 2024.

Analysis: Imps' defensive frailties show as Wrexham's fight evident

Lincoln looked on course for a first clean sheet in nine matches until Wrexham capitalised on some sloppy play to nick a late winner.

The Imps went into the match having conceded more goals at home (16) than any other side in the second tier, and their defensive concerns were once again exposed.

Having started the campaign so positively, Lincoln have now lost seven of their past nine league outings as the rigours of the Championship have become increasingly difficult to deal with.

Wrexham, meanwhile, were once again without the likes of last season's top scorer Josh Windass, Nathan Broadhead, Callum O'Hare and Sam Smith due to injury.

The lack of forward options at Parkinson's disposal has been a concern over recent weeks and months, and it was certainly evident for long periods on this occasion.

But their battling spirit was fully evident as they fought to the bitter end, with Thomason providing a rare moment that combined awareness, endeavour and quality as Wrexham ensured they would return to north Wales with a hard-fought victory.
