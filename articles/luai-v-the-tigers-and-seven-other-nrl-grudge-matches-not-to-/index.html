Wests Tigers fans have been handed an early Easter gift – a match-up against former marquee signing and Parramatta recruit Jarome Luai.

Luai’s departure with the Tigers became one of the biggest stories of the NRL season, after the joint venture’s form began plummeting from the moment the playmaker revealed he had become the PNG Chiefs’ inaugural signing.

Things hit rock bottom between the player and club when Luai headed to Port Moresby without telling the Tigers, then chose a World Cup promotion in Samoa over attending the end-of-season awards night.

The Tigers and Eels will clash on Easter Monday in round four at CommBank Stadium. The same game could feature Josh Addo-Carr playing against the Eels, should his management team strike a deal with the club where it all started for the ‘Foxx’.

Another superstar player eager to go up against his old club will be Payne Haas, whose South Sydney team will meet the Broncos in round ten on a Thursday night at Suncorp Stadium.

Haas’ shock signing with the Bunnies at the start of the year was one of the biggest stories of the year, and the front-rower started training with his new teammates earlier this month.

Ben Te’o, the Souths premiership winner who rejoined the club this week as part of Wayne Bennett’s coaching team, will also look forward to the Broncos clash given he quit the club after three games this year after a reported falling-out with Michael Maguire.

The Rabbitohs and Roosters will lock horns again in round two on a Friday night at Souths’ spiritual home ground, Allianz Stadium. Tension between the game’s oldest rivals has been tested again in the past week after Souths emerged as one of the key contenders to sign unwanted Roosters half Sam Walker for 2028 and beyond.

Penrith will kickstart their campaign at their new $309 million home, Helloworld Stadium, on a Friday night in round four against the Dolphins.

The Panthers were forced to call Parramatta home for two seasons while their home turf was redeveloped, and despite fans showing little enthusiasm to make the trek down the M4 Motorway to support Nathan Cleary, Isaah Yeo and their team, Penrith still reached the preliminary finals twice.

As reported by this masthead, St George Illawarra will take a home game to Auckland’s Eden Park in round 15, three days before Origin II is played at the same venue. Four days later, the Warriors will host the Perth Bears – completing a run of three games in eight days in New Zealand, a tempting prospect for league lovers planning a trip across the ditch.

The NRL grand final rematch between Newcastle and the Roosters will take place on Saturday in round ten at McDonald Jones Stadium.

Former Canterbury stars Toby Sexton and Josh Curran – and possibly Matt Burton – will run out for Perth against the Bulldogs in round eight on the Monday after Anzac Day.

The NRL’s full 2027 schedule will be released on Friday.
