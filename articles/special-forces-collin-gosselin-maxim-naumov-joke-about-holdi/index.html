Special Forces’ Collin Gosselin, Maxim Naumov Joke About Holding Hands Over the Stall While Pooping

TheTools · 09.10.2026 01:15 · source · permalink

Collin Gosselin and Maxim Naumov took the bathroom conversations on Special Forces up a notch by discussing plans to poop together — while holding hands.

During the Thursday, October 8, episode of the Fox series, Collin, 22, told Maxim, 25, he hasn’t “s*** since we got here,” to which the Olympian replied, “I am going to have to s*** too, you know. I feel it coming.”

Collin suggested they “hold hands” while doing it together.

“You want to hold hands over the stall?” he quipped before Maxim agreed. Later in the episode, the duo were seen walking over to the stall to jokingly try out the system.

Matt Barnes, meanwhile, asked Hannah Stocking about her bowel movements as well.

“How are the Hannah bananas? Are they ripe? Are they ready for eating?” he asked Hannah, 34, who described her movement in the bathroom as “hard on the outside but soft on the inside.” Oliver Hudson then jumped in to joke that his was “the opposite.”

The season 5 cast of Special Forces has often discussed their issues with the concept of having to go to the bathroom outside. Earlier this month, LeSean McCoy got into a conversation with his fellow recruits about his issues.

“I’m not s***ting outside,” he told the group. “I won’t s*** for three days or eight days.”

Despite hoping to push through, LeSean’s visit to the restroom, which was out in the open, caused him to officially withdraw, saying, “I am not ready for all this thing. I am not taking no s*** here.”

LeSean, 38, then called the Directing Staff over to deliver the news.

“Staff, your bed is getting mosquitoes all night, but it’s cool. This stuff is great, but I am not s***ting outside,” he noted. “I am cool. I am not doing nothing else.”

LeSean later spoke to Us about having to deal with the elements.

“We’re out here with little stuff like this. But in real life, I saw a big mosquito, just as big as this, and they were doing a lot of biting. When I woke up the next morning, I had so many bites on my back,” he exclusively told Us about the challenges. “On my first day there, we got into the jungle and the bathroom was outside. Now that was hard. Like, whoa, we gonna use the bathroom outside? So in my head, I said, ‘I gotta hold this thing in for as long as I’m here.’ That’s what I’m thinking.”

He continued: “When I looked into the bathroom, it really was just like a hole, some wood, a circle and a bucket. That’s it. And there was a big snake in there. I’m like, ‘Oh, hell no.’ So I said, ‘I’m not using the bathroom.’ They got snakes. I saw a king cobra.”
