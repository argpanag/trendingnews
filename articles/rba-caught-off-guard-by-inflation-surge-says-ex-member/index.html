RBA caught off guard by inflation surge, says ex-member

TheTools · 07.10.2026 07:55 · source · permalink

Former Reserve Bank of Australia board member Ian Harper acknowledged the RBA was caught off-guard by the resurgence in inflation last year because it assumed the Australian economy could sustain more demand without generating price pressures.

Harper also said the central bank could raise interest rates again before the end of the year and was starting to give more weight to short-term inflation expectations in its cash rate decisions; a shift from its traditional approach.
