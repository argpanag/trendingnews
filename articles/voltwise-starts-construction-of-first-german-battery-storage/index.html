Voltwise starts construction of first German battery storage system

TheTools · 24.11.2026 17:15 · source · permalink

Voltwise is building its first large scale battery storage system in Germany in Leopoldshöhe, and hosted a groundbreaking ceremony on 19 November 2026. The system, with an output of 56 MW and a storage capacity of 125 MWh, is scheduled to start operation in 2H27.

The new battery storage system provides the flexibility needed to integrate the growing share of renewable energy efficiently into the power system, contributing to a resilient, secure, and affordable energy supply. It uses the SmartStack battery storage system from Fluence, global market leader delivering intelligent energy storage systems, services, and asset optimisation software.

The storage system will be connected to the grid via the Bechterdissen substation of Bielefelder Netz at the 110-kV level, which covers an area of around 4000 m2. The plans also include a fire safety concept with a fire-water tank and a 4 m noise barrier to limit noise emissions. Further project partners include Vattenfall and Santander CIB, which acted as arranger, original lender, and original hedge counterparty.

“Leopoldshöhe marks a decisive step in building our business in Germany. Starting construction of our first large scale battery storage system shows that we are turning our plans into concrete projects. We want to build on this and develop and deliver further storage sites in Germany,” said Thorsten Klöpper, Managing Director Germany at Voltwise.

Alongside Mayor Prof. Dr-Ing. Martin Hoffmann, the groundbreaking in Leopoldshöhe was attended by the landowners, representatives of the project partners, and further guests from politics and the region.

Hoffmann added: “The energy transition is not decided at the federal level alone, but also through concrete action at the local level. The new battery energy storage facility in Leopoldshöhe represents an investment in infrastructure that will make our electricity system more flexible and future-proof. I am delighted that our municipality has been chosen as the location for such an investment, allowing us to contribute to the energy supply of tomorrow.”

“Germany is one of the most important energy storage markets in Europe. With the Leopoldshöhe project, we are making a concrete contribution to reliability in Germany and laying a foundation on which we intend to build our presence here,” commented John D. Jones, CEO of Voltwise.
