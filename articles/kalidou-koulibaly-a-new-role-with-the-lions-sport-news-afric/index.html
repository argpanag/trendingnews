Kalidou Koulibaly, a new role with the Lions? - Sport News Africa

TheTools · 23.09.2026 10:31 · source · permalink

A true cornerstone of Senegal's defense, Kalidou Koulibaly has been in freefall these past few months. Far from his true level, the Lions' captain could lose both his captaincy and his starting spot in the center of defense.

One injury for Kalidou Koulibaly and everything is turned upside down. Last April, the 35-year-old player suffered a muscle contusion in his thigh with a subcutaneous hematoma during training with Al Hilal. Since this physical setback, he has struggled to rediscover his true level. The proof lies in his 2026 World Cup, which was far below his standards. Patrick Vieira could shake things up.

Kalidou Koulibaly reported to be on the bench during the September international break

A new challenge for Senegal with a new head coach. The Lions will play two 2027 AFCON qualifying matches against Mozambique (September 25) and Ethiopia (September 29). Kalidou Koulibaly, included in the squad by Vieira, could watch both matches from the bench.

According to information from Match360, the former Chelsea centre-back is not expected to start in either of the upcoming matches. The player with 105 caps, 2 goals and 6 assists is said to have been called up by Patrick Vieira for a specific role: to mentor the younger players and bring his top-level experience to the squad.

Koulibaly could lose his captain's armband. The same source reveals that the head coach is considering naming Pape Gueye or Ismaïla Sarr as the new captain of the Lions.

A mediocre start to the season with Al Hilal

Kalidou Koulibaly's poor form is persisting. Even this season, the 35-year-old has still not returned to his true level. Although he retains his status as a starter at club level, his performances leave much to be desired.

Kalidou Koulibaly still has the confidence of his club manager Simone Inzaghi. But on the pitch, the 35-year-old defender rarely meets expectations. This injury has hit him hard. 1.7 tackles, 0.7 interceptions, 4.9 clearances, 62% of duels won — that is the record of the Senegal captain after 7 matches in the Saudi Pro League.
