In its daily weather bulletin, the NCM said winds would be light to moderate, becoming fresh to strong at times with clouds, and causing blowing dust. They will be south-easterly to north-easterly, with speeds ranging from 15 to 25 km/h, reaching 50 km/h at times.

Waves in the Arabian Gulf will be slight. The first high tide will occur at 16:25 and the second at 08:14, while the first low tide will occur at 09:40 and the second at 00:16.

In the Sea of Oman, waves will also be slight. The first high tide will occur at 13:17 and the second at 03:16, while the first low tide will occur at 08:19 and the second at 20:19.
