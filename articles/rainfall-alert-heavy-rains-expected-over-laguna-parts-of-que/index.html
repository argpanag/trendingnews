RAINFALL ALERT: Heavy rains expected over Laguna, parts of Quezon, Nueva Ecija

TheTools · 07.09.2026 11:49 · source · permalink

Heavy rains are expected over Laguna and parts of Quezon and Nueva Ecija on Monday morning due to the effects of Southwest Monsoon or Habagat, state weather bureau PAGASA said.

In its 8 a.m. advisory, PAGASA said light to moderate with occasional heavy rains are expected over the following areas:


• Laguna
• Quezon (General Nakar, Infanta, Tiaong, Dolores, Candelaria, Sariaya, Tayabas, Lucban, Pagbilao, Atimonan, Mauban, Sampaloc, Real, Lucena, Agdangan, Alabat, Buenavista, Calauag, Catanauan, General Luna, Guinayangan, Gumaca, Lopez, Macalelon, Mulanay, Padre Burgos, Perez, Pitogo, Plaridel, Quezon, San Andres, San Francisco, San Narciso, Tagkawayan, Unisan, San Antonio)
• Nueva Ecija (General Tinio, Gabaldon, Laur, Bongabon, Pantabangan, Carranglan, San Jose, Rizal, General Mamerto Natividad, Palayan)

Meanwhile, light to moderate with occasional heavy rains are affecting the following and nearby areas:


• Bataan
• Zambales
• Tarlac (Capas, Bamban, San Jose, Mayantoc)
• Pampanga (Porac, Floridablanca, Masantol, Sasmuan, Macabebe, Lubao)
• Bulacan (Bulakan, Malolos, Paombong, Hagonoy)
• Batangas (Calatagan, Lian, Nasugbu)
• Cavite (Maragondon, Ternate, Magallanes)
• Metro Manila (Parañaque, Pasay, Taguig)
• Rizal (Taytay, Angono, Binangonan)

PAGASA advised the public as well as concerned disaster risk reduction and management offices to monitor the weather condition.

The next rainfall warning is set to be issued at 11:00 a.m. —Joviland Rita/KG, GMA News
