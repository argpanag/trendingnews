Stuttgart vs Gladbach line-ups confirmed for Bundesliga matchday eight opener

Stuttgart and Borussia Mönchengladbach have confirmed their starting XIs for the opening fixture of Bundesliga matchday eight at MHP Arena.

According to BuLi News, Sebastian Hoeneß has rotated again after a hard-fought midweek draw with Atletico Madrid, while Alexander Blessin is without Kevin Diks for personal reasons, adding to a lengthy absentee list.

OneFootball Videos

Gladbach are seeking to avoid setting a new record for the worst Bundesliga start, having already equalled it last week. Stuttgart were beaten late at HSV.

Stuttgart XI: Seimen, Assignon, Jeltsch, Hendriks, Mittelstädt, Karazor, Stiller, Sauer, Pejcinovic, Führich, Demirović ©.

Gladbach XI: Nicolas, Hashioka, Sander ©, Chiarodia, Scally, Uno, Neuhaus, Ullrich, Bolin, Mohya, Machino.
