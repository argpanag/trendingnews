David Reinbacher recalled from Laval Rocket | Montréal Canadiens

TheTools · 14.10.2026 23:47 · source · permalink

The defenseman has registered two assists with Laval this season

WASHINGTON – The Canadiens announced on Wednesday that defenseman David Reinbacher has been recalled from the Laval Rocket in the American Hockey League. 

Reinbacher, 21, has tallied two points (2A) in four games with Laval this season. He’s also posted a +18 differential and totaled 56 penalty minutes with the Canadiens’ minor-league affiliate in 57 games in 2025-26.  

A fifth overall pick in the 2023 NHL Draft, Reinbacher collected an assist in two games with Montreal last season.
