Grandmother of newborn found dead near Moss Landing appears in court

TheTools · 07.10.2026 22:58 · source · permalink

The mother of a 14-year-old girl whose newborn was found dead near Moss Landing in August made her first court appearance Wednesday.

It was a short appearance, and her arraignment was continued until Thursday because a Mixtec interpreter was unavailable.

Ofelia Garcia Ortega, 36, was arrested Tuesday and faces charges related to her alleged conduct involving her daughter, Monterey County prosecutors said.

The infant was found dead along the side of a roadway on Aug. 1. The baby’s mother, identified as Jane Doe, was 14 at the time and is a victim of sexual assault, the DA’s Office said.

Investigators determined the infant’s father was Jane Doe’s biological father, Sergio Galvez Perez, who has been charged with sexual assault offenses related to his abuse of her.

Galvez Perez and Jane Doe were found in the Mexican state of Chihuahua this past weekend. He was arrested there on drug charges and has been charged in Monterey County with sexual assault charges, according to prosecutors. Jane Doe is in the care of Mexican child protective services.

The investigation into the infant’s death also examined Garcia Ortega’s conduct involving her daughter. Investigators allege Garcia Ortega put Jane Doe to work in agricultural fields at age 13 using falsified documents, allowed her to drive without a license and kept her out of school for many months.

Garcia Ortega faces two felony counts of displaying or possessing a forged identification card, along with misdemeanor counts of permitting a child to suffer or be endangered and contributing to the delinquency of a minor.

This is the second time Garcia Ortega has been arrested; back in August, she was booked for child endangerment. It is unclear whether she is still facing that charge from August.

A bail enhancement has been granted, and she is being held at $150,000, per the sheriff's office.

In August, the Sheriff’s Office said it had obtained an arrest warrant for Jane Doe in connection with the infant’s death. The case remains under investigation, and no charges have been filed against her to date.

Galvez Perez has been charged in Monterey County Superior Court with three felony counts:

  • Lewd act upon a child: The complaint alleges that, between Nov. 1, 2025, and April 21, 2026, Galvez Perez committed the crime against a female child under the age of 14. The charge includes an enhancement alleging aggravated circumstances and stating that the “defendant personally inflicted great bodily injury in the commission” of the alleged crime.
  • Incest: The complaint alleges that, between Nov. 1, 2025, and April 21, 2026, Galvez Perez committed incest with the same female victim.
  • Child abuse: The complaint alleges that, between Nov. 1, 2025, and Aug. 5, 2026, Galvez Perez willfully and unlawfully injured, caused and permitted Jane Doe, a child in his care and custody, to suffer unjustifiable physical pain and mental suffering. It also alleges that he placed her in a situation in which her person and health were endangered.
