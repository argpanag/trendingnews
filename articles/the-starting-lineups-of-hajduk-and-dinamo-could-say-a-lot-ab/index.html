The starting lineups of Hajduk and Dinamo could say a lot about how the match will look.

TheTools · 10.10.2026 08:05 · source · permalink

There is no better way to mark the end of the international break than the eternal derby, Hajduk will face Dinamo at Poljud and we will once again watch the biggest match in Croatian football. Both teams are entering this match with gloves on and somewhat wounded.

Hajduk is having a great season so far, they are at the top of the league, and after 16 years they will also be playing in Europe. But they went into the break with a painful defeat to Rijeka, that is never a good feeling. On the other hand, Dinamo has had obvious problems since the beginning of September, even the results are not as bad as their game seems. It is a special situation, both teams will probably enter the match a little more cautiously.

Hajduk's lineup is quite easy to predict, Pajaziti's injury has not been repaired, so del Moral will certainly be in midfield, Fayad and Dalisson should be in front of him. There are two questions left, who will start in the defense, which is in hot-cold mode this season, they know how to play great games, but they also know how to cause chaos, as was the case in Rijeka. However, Garcia's announcements are such that the impression is that Raci and Marešić will start. The second question is on the right wing, but only because Bamba had a minor injury during national team activities, so if he is not ready for 90 minutes, it is possible that Šutalo, a Dinamo player until this season, will start in that position.

On the other hand, according to some media reports, a former Hajduk player could also start. It's Kovačević's gamble, Almena is undoubtedly a quality player, but mentally a bit moody, the question is how he will react to the Split crowd, who will not spare him, it is not difficult to find the best Spanish swear words on the internet these days. It is a seesaw, will this create additional motivation for the Catalan or will he burn out mentally.

The biggest plus for Dinamo's coach is the definitive return of Belje to the squad, the tall striker is the alpha and omega of Zagreb's offensive operations, without him they often acted like headless flies. It is questionable how much good the dependence on one player does in the long run, but that's how it is now, the main threat to Hajduk comes from the blue nine.

Dinamo's biggest minus is not playing Mišić, the engine and brain of the midfield cannot play due to a yellow card, Kravcov will take his place. They don't even lose much in organization there, he's not the Russian Mišić, but he's not bad as a playmaker either. However, he can be a bit forgetful in zone defense, although Dinamo's captain has not been at the level he was two or three years ago lately, he is definitely better than Kravcov in that. And when you know that one of Hajduk's striking pins is Dalisson, who is looking for space behind the midfield, it can be assumed that this position will be one of the keys to the derby.

Another potential problem for Dinamo is the questionable physical condition of Stojković, their most dangerous player from the second plan. And the most unpredictable, he is one of those who has pulled Dinamo out even when the team has played poorly in general. After the injury in Vukovar last weekend, information says that he is not ready for 90 minutes. But it makes a big difference whether he can play 30 or 60 minutes.

The starting lineups could give an indication of what the game will be like. More precisely, who will play on the flanks for Dinamo. If Galesic and Goda end up there, it will be a sign that Kovačević's plan is to close the approaches to his goal, and then mainly through Dominguez's exits and long balls, directly look for Belja. Valinčić and Perez-Vinlof in those places would be an announcement that the Blues will try to squeeze the Whites. However, Kovačević has so far shown himself to be mostly a ziher, this line-up would force defensively questionable defenders to fight with Hajduk's wings, first of all, defensively limited Valinčić would have to go to Šotiček, who even in the break, playing for the young national team, showed that his ranges are huge. And although Hajduk before Rijeka used to settle matches in the early stages of duels, it can be assumed that now they will still go conservatively and more calmly. Šego will probably be tasked with closing Dominguez, the only question now is whether he will really press him, or go for Kotarski, keeping the Spaniard in his shadow. That could open up a lot of space for Kotarski's long passes, Hajduk would simply be defending a larger area of ​​the pitch, and Dinamo's goalkeeper is great at those deep passes. So if I had to guess, I would say it will be more of a direct pressure on the defender, in order to shorten the space being defended.

Anyway, today at 15pm, Poljud, Hajduk – Dinamo. The HNL is finally back after a three-week break. Those who won't be able to go to the Poljud game can watch it on Hajduk Digital TV and Max Sport.
