Blue Jays non-tender fan favourite Schneider

TheTools · 20.11.2026 22:46 · source · permalink

It appears the Davis Schneider era is over in Toronto.

The Blue Jays announced Friday evening that the 27-year-old outfielder has been non-tendered, while all remaining unsigned players on Toronto’s 40-player roster have been offered contracts for 2027.

Schneider was a fan favourite during his time with the Blue Jays, appearing in a total of 321 big-league games in parts of four seasons.

He had an incredible start to his career, homering in his MLB debut at Fenway Park in August of 2023 and going deep six times in his first 13 games.

He also played a role in the Jays’ run to the World Series in 2025, turning in a .797 OPS with 11 home runs and 31 RBI in 82 games as a versatile part-time bat during the regular season. He also homered leading off Game 5 of the Fall Classic against the Los Angeles Dodgers.

However, Schneider was unable to produce at the same level in 2026, slashing .145/.310/.315 with five homers and 16 RBI. He spent a significant portion of the season at Triple-A Buffalo where he hit .220.

All in all, Schneider is a career .206 hitter at the MLB level with 37 home runs and 113 RBI in 825 at-bats.

Jays deal Lee to Rockies

Earlier on Friday, the Jays announced they have traded right-handed reliever Chase Lee to the Colorado Rockies for cash considerations.

Lee was designated for assignment by the Jays on Thursday.

He appeared in eight games for the Jays at the MLB level this past season, pitching to a 6.52 earned-run average with five strikeouts in 9.2 innings pitched. He was much better at Triple-A Buffalo, turning in a 2.01 ERA with 47 punchouts in 53.2 innings.
