Arizona Diamondbacks at Colorado Rockies odds, picks and predictions

TheTools · 23.09.2026 00:08 · source · permalink

The Arizona Diamondbacks (82-74) and Colorado Rockies (57-99) meet Tuesday to begin a 3-game NL West series. First pitch from Coors Field in Denver, Colorado, is set for 8:40 p.m. ET. Let's analyze MLB odds around the Diamondbacks vs. Rockies odds and make our expert MLB picks and predictions for the best bets.

Season series: Diamondbacks lead 6-4

The Diamondbacks beat the New York Yankees 8-4 Sunday, closing as a -126 favorite with the Over (8) hitting. Arizona won 2 of 3 games in that series, but is just 3-5 over its last 8 contests. On the outside looking in, the Diamondbacks are 4 games behind the Philadelphia Phillies for the final NL Wild Card spot with 6 games remaining.

The Rockies are just 2-12 over their last 14 games and are coming off a 2-1 loss at home against the Seattle Mariners, closing as a +135 underdog as the Under (11) hit with ease.
