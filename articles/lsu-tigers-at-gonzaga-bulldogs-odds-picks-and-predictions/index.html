LSU Tigers at Gonzaga Bulldogs odds, picks and predictions

TheTools · 14.11.2026 21:11 · source · permalink

The LSU Tigers (3-0) and the No. 12 Gonzaga Bulldogs (2-0) meet Saturday at Spokane Numerica Veterans Arena in Spokane, Washington. Tip-off is set for 3:30 p.m. ET (USA Network). Let's analyze BetMGM Sportsbook's NCAA basketball odds around the LSU vs. Gonzaga odds and make our expert college basketball picks and predictions for the best bets.

LSU kept its perfect start rolling, improving to 3-0 with a convincing 90-50 victory over the Louisiana Tech Bulldogs Monday. The Tigers easily covered as 29.5-point home favorites, while the Under (148.5) cashed. C Michael Ruzic led the way with 18 points off the bench, shooting an efficient 7-for-9 from the field. Fellow reserve G Abdi Bashir Jr. added 16 points, including 4 3-pointers, as LSU cruised to another comfortable win without much drama.

Gonzaga cruised to a 109-68 victory over the New Mexico State Aggies Nov. 7, improving to 2-0 on the season. Just like LSU's last outing, Gonzaga covered as 29.5-point home favorites -- the Over (151.5) cashed, too. G's Davis Fogle and Isiah Harwell led the way with 22 points apiece, while F Braden Huff added 20. Gonzaga shot an impressive 61% from the floor.

This will be the first meeting between LSU and Gonzaga.

Against the spread

BET LSU +5.5 (-110).

LSU has been getting it done defensively through its first 3 games, allowing fewer than 0.71 points per possession in each matchup. The Tigers have also been a solid bet away from home, covering 8 of their last 10 road games and 7 of their last 8 following a win by more than 20 points.

Gonzaga is still working some new faces into its rotation, which could lead to a few early growing pains. LSU's defense and experience should make this a competitive game from start to finish.
