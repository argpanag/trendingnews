Denver Broncos vs. Seattle Seahawks: How to watch, listen and live stream the Broncos' Week 6 game

TheTools · 16.10.2026 01:05 · source · permalink

The Broncos will host the Seattle Seahawks at Empower Field at Mile High on Thursday, Oct. 15 at 6:15 p.m. MT.

MATCHUP

The Broncos (3-2) will look for their second home prime-time win of the season when they host the Seattle Seahawks (3-2) for "Thursday Night Football" in Week 6. Denver currently boasts a 2-0 record at Empower Field at Mile High this season, and the team will aim to remain unbeaten at home as they take on the reigning Super Bowl champions.

The Broncos will also look to secure their second consecutive victory after a comeback win over the Los Angeles Chargers in Week 5. Wide receiver Courtland Sutton and running back J.K. Dobbins, who both found the end zone for the first time this season last week, could aim to make an impact again on Thursday.

After recording a season-high three takeaways in Week 5, the Broncos' defense will look for another strong performance against Seahawks quarterback Sam Darnold and reigning NFL Offensive Player of the Year Jaxon Smith-Njigba, who currently leads the league in receiving yards.
