Joanne McNally admits huge blunder with Joe Lycett on The Traitors

TheTools · 27.10.2026 16:38 · source · permalink

Joanne McNally has admitted that her blunders on The Celebrity Traitors didn’t stop after the show finished up.

The Dublin comedian is facing the chopping block alongside Sharon Rooney, as both she and Sharon are separated by just one vote from being banished. Traitor Kenny, who has the dagger that gives him two votes, is now torn between banishing Joanne or Sharon, and viewers of the show won’t find out what happened until this Thursday’s episode.

Today's top videos

While Joanne’s ‘strategies’ haven’t been exactly foolproof, including her saying that she’d ‘eat her bonnet’ if Richard E Grant was a traitor (he was), and targeting Leigh-Anne Pinnock and Sebastian ‘Bash’ Croft in roundtables (despite them having no heat on them), she’s gained a cult following for her apparent lack of awareness in the game.

Taking to her podcast with Vogue Williams, My Therapist Ghosted Me, Joanne explained that her logic behind being the lone voice in voting for obvious faithfuls was that since the obvious traitors were going home, she might as well throw a lone dissenting vote into the mix.

‘I stand by my strategy,’ Joanne said. ‘If everyone’s voting for the same person, what is the point of joining that pile on and voting for that person? I don’t see the point.’

Despite her wildcard voting strategy, Joanne has also joined the masses in banishing people before, namely Joe Lycett, who was banished — although she forgot that she had actually voted for him, only realising that she had done so after the episode where he was banished aired.

‘So you know that I’m not watching [the show as it airs], because I don’t do that,’ Joanne told Vogue. ‘I messaged Joe when I knew that his episode had gone out, that he’d gone out.

‘I said something along the lines of, “Delighted I didn’t vote for you, because I don’t like pile ons.” And he wrote back, “Joanne, you did.”‘

As well as having to eat her hat at Richard E Grant being outed as a traitor, and having to apologise for joining in the pile-on on Joe, Joanne also joked that she had to apologise to Little Mixers after trying to banish Leigh-Anne Pinnock.

In an apparent olive branch to the Little Mixers, Joanne shared a video of her dancing to the song Sweet Melody by the iconic girl group, writing in the caption: ‘When you keep falsely accusing Leigh-Anne from Little Mix and throwing her under the bus on The Traitors, so now you’re desperate to appease her army of fans.’

People loved Joanne’s moves, and joked that she would’ve gotten through on The X Factor alongside Leigh-Anne, Jade, Perrie and Jesy, with one commenting: ‘If this was 2011 you’d have got through to judges houses.’

‘I love whatever’s wrong with you honestly,’ another joked, while another commented: ‘POV: I’m showing my mam a dance me and my cousins made up to convince her to let us have a sleepover.’
