Lincoln City 1-1 Southampton: Mikkel Ladefoged rescues point for Imps against Saints

TheTools · 05.09.2026 13:51 · source · permalink

Mikkel Ladefoged scored his first goal for Lincoln in his second appearance for the club

Lincoln City's record signing Mikkel Ladefoged came off the bench to rescue a point against Southampton with his first goal for the Imps.

The Danish striker met a Reeco Hackett cross at the back post to deny Southampton their first win away from home this season.

Brazilian defender Welington had put Saints ahead in stunning fashion in the first half, the defender's thumping shot from 30 yards flying past keeper George Wickens as he dived at full stretch.

Leo Scienza was denied a second for Saints after the break, as the winger bent a shot off the far post before the ball bounced across the face of goal and off the opposite upright.

While Lincoln squandered a number of chances to pull a goal back, substitute Ladefoged eventually came up with the finishing quality they have desperately lacked in the early weeks of the season.

  • In 2026, no side has kept more clean sheets in England’s top four tiers than Lincoln City (13), who have kept a shutout in their last two Championship games.

  • No player has scored more Championship goals this season than Southampton’s Cyle Larin (4), who has also scored seven goals in his last seven league appearances for Saints.
