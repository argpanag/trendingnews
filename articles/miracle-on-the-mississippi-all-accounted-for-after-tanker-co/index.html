Miracle on the Mississippi: All accounted for after tanker collides with cruise ship

TheTools · 13.11.2026 19:38 · source · permalink

Federal, state and local agencies responded to a boat collision on the Mississippi River in New Orleans, Louisiana, on Friday, Nov. 13.

A tanker struck the American Cruise Lines’ American Heritage, prompting a massive search and rescue response.

Latest updates:

  • All passengers aboard the American Heritage have been accounted for, officials said.
  • LCMC Health is treating 32 injured passengers
  • The Atlantic Asphalt tanker struck the docked cruise ship on the Mississippi River in New Orleans at about 12:30 p.m. The cause has not been confirmed.
  • Hotel rooms have been secured for any passengers impacted by the cruise line collision

In what is being called "A miracle on the Mississippi," all passengers aboard the American Heritage cruise ship struck in New Orleans have been accounted for.

VIDEO: New Orleans mayor says all accounted for after collision

LCMC Health is treating 32 passengers who were injured during the collision.

Here’s what we know as emergency responders work through the night to determine how to remove the boat:

What happened:

The Atlantic Asphalt tanker struck the American Cruise Lines vessel at about 12:30 p.m. near 1350 Port of New Orleans Place, according to Port NOLA. The cruise ship had docked approximately two hours earlier and was beginning to disembark passengers.

VIDEO: Watch the moment a tanker struck a docked cruise ship in New Orleans

About 129 passengers, along with crew members, were aboard the American Heritage at the time of the collision.

The crash happened near the Crescent City Connection on the New Orleans side of the river.

Port NOLA Harbor Police, the New Orleans Fire Department, the New Orleans Police Department, the U.S. Coast Guard and emergency medical personnel responded to the scene to conduct rescue and response operations.

VIDEO: Watch as people are rescued from the frigid Mississippi River

Passengers were brought inside the Port of New Orleans Administration Building, located at 1350 Port of New Orleans Place, for triage and assistance, according to Port officials.

"Our immediate priority is the safety of all passengers, crew members and first responders. This remains an active and evolving incident. The number of injuries and the cause of the allision have not yet been confirmed. Additional updates will be provided as information is confirmed," the Port issued a news release.

The crash happened near the Crescent City Connection on the New Orleans side.

Injuries and passenger assistance:

LCMC Health said it is treating 32 injured passengers. Passengers were brought to the Port of New Orleans Administration Building for triage and assistance, according to port officials.

Lt. Gov. Billy Nungesser said he has secured 85 hotel rooms for passengers affected by the collision.

VIDEO: Live look outside University Medical Center amid mass casualty response to cruise ship collision:

LCMC Health issued the following statement regarding the treatment of patients involved in the cruise line collision:

"University Medical Center New Orleans and other LCMC Health hospitals have received an estimated two dozen patients following the reported maritime incident. Our medical teams are currently evaluating patients and providing appropriate care while coordinating with emergency responders. As this remains an active situation, we will provide additional information as appropriate."

The American Red Cross issued a statement regarding the cruise line collision:

“Our hearts go out to everyone affected by the collision between a tanker and a cruise ship on the Mississippi River near the Port of New Orleans. We are thinking of the passengers, crew members and families who are living through this frightening and uncertain time.

American Red Cross Louisiana Region disaster volunteers are on the ground supporting cruise guests and staff affected by this incident. Working alongside first responders and community partners, we are providing water, snacks, blankets and emotional support. As the situation unfolds, we will continue working closely with our emergency service partners.

If you can’t reach a loved one who may have been affected, please know that help is available. The American Red Cross may be able to help reunite families in certain circumstances. Please call 1-800-RED-CROSS (1-800-733-2767) for assistance.”

American Cruise Lines issues statement:

"American Cruise Lines is grateful to the U.S. Coast Guard, our crew, and the local first responders on the ground in New Orleans for their tremendous rescue efforts. We are getting everyone who was aboard the ship settled into hotels and making sure they have everything they need. We are also in close contact with the U.S. Coast Guard, New Orleans Police, and other agencies, and will continue to coordinate with them."

River traffic and ferry service:

The Regional Transit Authority reopens ferry service after river traffic was suspended due to a tanker crashing into a cruise ship on the Mississippi River.

President Trump briefed on collision:

President Donald Trump was briefed on the New Orleans boat crash, per a senior White House official.

"Thank you to the United States Coast Guard for doing such an incredible job of rescue and other work, having to do with the crash of the two ships in Louisiana. Likewise, all of Law Enforcement and First Responders were fantastic! Incredibly, nobody was killed by this major collision. 32 people are in the hospital. Thank you to Governor Jeff Landry for your outstanding leadership during this very agonizing period of time."

Federal, state, local officials respond:

Mayor Helena Moreno issued the following statement regarding the response:

"Emergency crews are responding to a collision involving a cargo ship and a riverboat cruise ship near the Port of New Orleans. Multiple individuals have been rescued, and search-and-rescue operations remain ongoing. Public safety agencies are coordinating response efforts, with the immediate priority being the safety and rescue of those involved. This remains an active and developing incident."

Louisiana Gov. Jeff Landry flew to New Orleans to help with the emergency response to the cruise line collision.

His office issued the following statement regarding the incident:

"We are aware of the incident on the Mississippi and we are in close contact with local officials and law enforcement. I am headed to New Orleans now."

Congressman Steve Scalise spoke with WDSU live on television regarding federal resources made available to the United States Coast Guard during the search and rescue operation in the river.

Secretary Markwayne Mullin issued the following statement regarding resources for the search and rescue response in New Orleans:

"The United States Coast Guard is supporting the state of Louisiana and our local partners in responding to a vessel collision near mile marker 96 on the Mississippi River. We are throwing every asset we have to help rescue missing passengers. The Department of Homeland Security is coordinating closely with the state of Louisiana. Our prayers are with all those impacted by this terrible tragedy."

New Orleans and Company:

New Orleans & Company continues to closely monitor developments following this afternoon’s collision involving a cargo vessel and the American Heritage river cruise ship on the Mississippi River.

We are profoundly grateful for the news confirmed by Mayor Helena Moreno during a late-afternoon press conference with Governor Jeff Landry and other officials that all passengers have been accounted for and no lives were lost. Thirty-two individuals were transported to local hospitals with injuries reported as non-critical. Our thoughts are with those injured, their families, and everyone affected by this frightening incident.

We extend our deepest appreciation to the brave men and women of the Port of New Orleans Harbor Police Department, U.S. Coast Guard, New Orleans Police Department, New Orleans Fire Department, Louisiana State Police, Port of New Orleans, and all first responders whose swift, courageous, and coordinated actions helped save lives. We are also grateful for the leadership of Governor Landry, Mayor Moreno, and our local, state, and federal partners throughout this emergency response. What has been described as a “Miracle on the Mississippi” is a testament to the extraordinary dedication of those who answered the call.

We remain in close communication with American Cruise Lines, the operator of the American Heritage and a valued longtime partner of New Orleans & Company. Our team has worked to secure hotel accommodations for affected passengers, and we stand ready to provide additional assistance as needed.

While a portion of the Mississippi River remains closed by the Coast Guard, we are not aware of any other disruptions to New Orleans tourism operations resulting from this incident.

Separately, we are also closely monitoring the nationwide ground stop issued by the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) and coordinating with Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport regarding potential impacts on air travel. Passengers are encouraged to check directly with their airlines for the latest flight information.

New Orleans is a community that takes pride in welcoming and caring for visitors from around the world. Today, we are especially grateful for the people whose professionalism, compassion, and courage helped ensure that our visitors and neighbors are safe.
