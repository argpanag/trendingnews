Vigil season 3 cast: Meet the new and returning characters of gripping BBC drama

Need something to make you feel a bit better about the fact that summer is basically over? Well, my friend, this is it: Vigil will return to screens for a third season very soon. Yay!

The gripping crime series — which follows military investigator DCI Amy Silva as she investigates mysterious deaths and high-level military conspiracies — returns to BBC for season three this weekend. When, I hear you ask? Episode one airs on BBC One at 9pm on Sunday 30 August. The six-part series will then roll out across three weeks, with two episodes a week (on Sundays and Mondays). You'll also be able to stream the series on BBC iPlayer.

As for what the third instalment of Vigil is about? According to a BBC synopsis, season three will see DCI Silva (along with DI Kirsten Longacre) head to a remote Arctic research station, where a member of a covert British special forces mission has been murdered.

The synopsis goes on: "Amy and Kirsten need to catch the killer and defuse a potential international confrontation, as different nations compete for control of the Arctic and its resources in the changing polar climate, with both their careers and relationship on the line."

So, who can viewers expect to see in this Arctic edition of Vigil? Here's all the information on the full cast and character list for Vigil season three...

Vigil season three returning cast and characters

Suranne Jones as DCI Amy Silva

Who is Amy? Amy has been the lead character of Vigil since season one. In season three, she has to work a new case that's unfolding in the Arctic. Per the BBC, Amy is "is feisty, she's a maverick, she's straight talking, she has a really good heart and a strong belief for the need for the world to be good."

What else has Suranne Jones been in? Doctor Foster, Hostage, Gentleman Jack, Frauds, Save Me, Unforgiven.

Rose Leslie as DI Kirsten Longacre

Who is Kirsten? Kirsten is Amy's partner – in both work and life. She joined the show in season two, and recently gave birth to their child. In season three, she travels to Svalbard in the Arctic to crack their latest case. According to the BBC, Kirsten is a "strong-willed, tenacious police officer who is loyal but also very passionate about her job."

What else has Rose Leslie been in? Game of Thrones, The Time Traveller's Wife, Death on the Nile, Miss Austen, The Hack.

Gary Lewis as DSU Colin Robertson

Who is Colin? DSU Colin Robertson is one of the few returning characters in Vigil, working alongside Amy and Kirsten.

What else has Gary Lewis been in? Billy Elliot, Outlander, My Son.

Dominic Mafham as Sir Ian Downing

Who is Ian? Sir Ian Downing – who appeared in Vigil season two – is a high-ranking MI5 official.

What else has Dominic Mafham been in? Grantchester, Coronation Street, The Marlow Murder Club.

Vigil season three new cast and characters

Jeppe Beck Laursen as Henrik Becken

Who is Henrik? "Henrik Becken is the Director of Svalbard and Chief of Police, so he gets very involved in the investigation," the BBC explains. "He's working closely with Kirsten and Amy, as well as Fyodor, the Russian Colonel."

What else has Jeppe Beck Laursen been in? The Last King, Cold Feet.

Tornike Gogrichiani as Fyodor

Who is Fyodor? "Fyodor is a Russian colonel who arrives in Svalbard to investigate the murder of the Russian citizen and British soldier who was found dead in a Russian-owned building," the BBC notes. "He works closely Kirsten and Amy on the investigation."

What else has Tornike Gogrichiani been in? Extraction 2, Drops of God.

Naomi Yang as Shen Jia

Who is Shen? Shen Jia is a microbiologist conducting research on Svalbard. "She has very strong values and won't compromise those even when things get tough," the actor who plays her told the BBC. "She's someone with a lot of integrity."

What else has Naomi Yang been in? Brassic, Peaky Blinders, Grace, Under Salt Marsh.

Steven Miller as Mark Cunningham

Who is Mark? Mark Cunningham is a Special Boat Service operator.

What else has Steven Miller been in? Father Brown, Casualty, The Walk-In, Dept. Q, Shetland.

Benjamin Wainwright as Oli Pickford

Who is Oli? Like Mark, Oli Pickford is a Special Boat Service operator.

What else has Benjamin Wainwright been in? The Split, Gangs of London, Halo.

Danusia Samal as Jasmine Kelly

Who is Jasmine? Jasmine Kelly works at MI5 with Sir Ian Downing.

What else has Danusia Samal been in? The Great, Gangs of London, Red Eye.

Shereen Cutkelvin plays Holly Mitchell

Who is Holly? Holly is a DS working with Amy and the team at their Scottish base.

What else has Shereen Cutkelvin been in? Saturday Mash-Up, Summerwater.
