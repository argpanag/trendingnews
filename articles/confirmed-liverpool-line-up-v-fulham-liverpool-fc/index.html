Confirmed Liverpool line-up v Fulham - Liverpool FC

TheTools · 12.09.2026 13:26 · source · permalink

Share

Bradley Barcola starts as Liverpool make three changes for this afternoon’s visit of Fulham.

The France forward is fit to begin the Premier League contest at Anfield after cramp forced him from the field in the second half of Wednesday’s win over Atletico Madrid.

Elsewhere, Ryan Gravenberch, Kostas Tsimikas and Victor Munoz come into the line-up in place of Milos Kerkez, Alexis Mac Allister and Rio Ngumoha.

Cody Gakpo returns to the matchday squad after a fitness issue and is named on the bench.

Liverpool: Alisson, Van Dijk, Jacquet, Wirtz, Szoboszlai, Isak, Tsimikas, Munoz, Barcola, Araujo, Gravenberch.

Subs: Mamardashvili, Endo, Kerkez, Mac Allister, Gakpo, Frimpong, Nyoni, Koumas, Ngumoha.
