NFL Tips - Cardinals @ Chargers - Week 1 2026

TheTools · 12.09.2026 03:19 · source · permalink

Expectations are high in Los Angeles this year with the Chargers the biggest favourite of Week 1 when they host the Cardinals.

Best Bet

Week 1 underdogs of 6.5 points or more are 32-13-1 against the number since 2013, better than 71%, and Arizona are getting nine and a half.

They also fit the rebound spot. Teams coming off a decline of five wins or more are 26-13 against the line in the opening week, and the Cardinals dropped from a competitive season to 3-14 in 2025.

Los Angeles are the better team. Jim Harbaugh is 6-0 straight up and against the number in Week 1 as an NFL head coach, and the Chargers went 4-1 both ways in their last five at home with a defence conceding 20 points a game.

But they are breaking in two new co-ordinators, a rookie second-rounder starts at centre after Tyler Biadasz tore an ACL, and Rashawn Slater plays his first football since January 2025. Arizona still have Trey McBride, Marvin Harrison Jr and Michael Wilson, so the floor for the offence is higher than the market suggests.n

Expect Los Angeles to win and Arizona to at least stay competitive.
