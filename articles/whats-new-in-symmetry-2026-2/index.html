Join this webinar for an explanation of the Symmetry™ 2026.2 release updates, focusing on Tela™ for Symmetry software and new thermodynamic improvements, with tips and tricks for sizing and rating relief valves. You’ll also learn how to model a multi-point ground flare system.

Multi-point ground flares are designed to handle large volumes of hydrocarbon gases in industrial processes. These systems utilize multiple flames arranged in a staged array, typically enclosed by a wind fence to shield the flames from plant personnel and nearby communities. They are usually used in densely populated areas, and where plants experience high variability in waste gas volumes that conventional flares cannot manage effectively.
