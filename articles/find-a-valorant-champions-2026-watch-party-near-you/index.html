Find a VALORANT Champions 2026 Watch Party Near You

TheTools · 18.10.2026 05:34 · source · permalink
  • Oct 12, 2026

VALORANT Champions 2026 is well underway. We’ve had back-and-forth Bo3’s, 13-1 beatdowns, and everything in-between. With the knockout bracket well underway, we’re narrowing in on that final matchup that will crown the 2026 champion. 

If you’ve been watching VALORANT Champions 2026 but want something bigger for this weekend’s finals, this list is for you. 

It’s a combination of watch parties regional Riot teams are putting on and ones sourced directly from the community. This is certainly not an exhaustive list so if you’re not seeing a watch party near you, it’s still worth asking around to local venues. 

Also, if this sounds like fun but there’s no watch party near you this year, consider hosting one in the future. If you do, we’ll put it on the 2027 list to help you get the word out. 

Here’s our list for VALORANT Champions Finals Watch Parties for 2026:

  • Melbourne, Australia - Fortress Melbourne
  • Perth, Australia - The Royale Theatre at Planet Royale

  • Singapore, Singapore - Select Haidilao Branches
  • Seoul, South Korea - Sangam SOOP Colosseum

  • Tainan, Taiwan - Sports Nation
  • Taipei, Taiwan - Taipei Music Center
  • Bangkok, Thailand - Dragon Town
  • Bangkok, Thailand - Select Haidilao Branches
  • Hanoi, Vietnam - Select Haidilao Branches
  • Hanoi, Vietnam - Tay Ho Sport Center
  • Ho Chi Minh, Vietnam - Select Haidilao Branches

EMEA
