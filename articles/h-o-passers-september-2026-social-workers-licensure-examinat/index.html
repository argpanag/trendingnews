H-O Passers: September 2026 Social Workers Licensure Examination Results

TheTools · 16.09.2026 16:33 · source · permalink

MANILA, Philippines – The September 2026 Social Workers Licensure Examination (SWLE) results, including the complete list of passers, are released today, September 16, 2026, three (3) working days after the exam.

The Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) administered the Social Worker board exam on September 9, 10, and 11, 2026 in the cities of Manila, Baguio, Butuan, Cagayan de Oro, Calapan, Cebu, Davao, Iloilo, Koronadal, Legazpi, Lucena, Pagadian, Palawan, Pampanga, Rosales, Tacloban, Tuguegarao, and Zamboanga.

Release of Results

According to PRC Resolution No. 2113 s. 2025, the September 2026 Social Workers Licensure Examination (SWLE) results will be released on Wednesday, September 16, 2026, within three (3) working days after the tests.

However, results might be released ahead of the target or on later date, without prior notice.

Registration and Oathtaking

Successful examinees must complete their initial registration online for the issuance of their Professional Identification Card (ID) and Certificate of Registration. Please visit https://online.prc.gov.ph/ and follow the registration instructions.

Date of Registration: To be announced (TBA)

Those who will register are required to bring the following:

  • screenshot of system-generated Registration Form
  • valid government-issued ID

All successful examinees are required to register in person and sign the Roster of Registered Professionals.

The schedule of the oathtaking ceremony will be announced at a later date.

PRC Offices Directory

If you have any concerns or need assistance from the Professional Regulation Commission, please refer to the contact details of the PRC Central or Regional Offices available on the agency’s official website.

You may also reach out the Licensure Exam Division through the following email contacts: licensure.division@prc.gov.ph or licensure.office@prc.gov.ph.

LERIS Account Assistance

For assistance with your LERIS account, including resetting password and technical issue, you may email technicalassistance@prc.gov.ph with a screenshot and a brief description of the problem.
