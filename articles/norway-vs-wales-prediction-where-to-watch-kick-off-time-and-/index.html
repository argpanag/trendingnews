Norway vs Wales - Prediction, where to watch, kick-off time and line-ups 14-11-2026

TheTools · 14.11.2026 14:07 · source · permalink

Norway and Wales meet in the fifth round of the 2026-27 UEFA Nations League on Saturday, 14 November, at 17:00 (UK time). The match takes place at Ullevaal Stadion in Oslo, with both teams still fighting for a place in the quarter-finals.

The two sides are level on three points after four matches, with Norway sitting bottom of Group A4 and Wales just above them. The visitors won the reverse fixture 2-1 in October, but the hosts will be determined to respond in front of their own supporters.

Norway vs. Wales - Head-to-head record

Norway and Wales have met 12 times, with the overall record perfectly balanced. Both nations have recorded four victories, while the other four encounters ended in draws.

However, Wales have enjoyed the better of their recent meetings, winning each of the last three fixtures between the two sides.

Their latest encounter came in October, when the Dragons secured a 2-1 victory in Cardiff. Norway will be hoping to reverse that result in Oslo, although their recent Nations League performances suggest they face a difficult challenge.

Norway – Haaland and Company Looking to End Losing Run

Norway have endured a difficult Nations League campaign, collecting just three points from their opening four matches. With qualification still mathematically possible, let’s examine their recent performances and expected lineup for Saturday’s encounter.

Recent form

Norway began their Group A4 campaign with an entertaining 3-2 home victory over Denmark, but their form has deteriorated since that opening success.

Stale Solbakken’s side suffered a 2-1 home defeat to Portugal before losing by the same scoreline away to Wales. Their most recent fixture brought another 2-1 defeat against Portugal, leaving them bottom of the group.

Despite those results, Norway remain only three points behind second-placed Denmark. Victories over Wales and Denmark in their remaining fixtures could therefore give them a chance of reaching the quarter-finals. However, they will need to improve defensively, having conceded two goals in each of their four group matches.

Erling Haaland is expected to lead the attack and could make his 60th international appearance. The striker has scored an impressive 65 goals for Norway and remains their biggest attacking threat. Martin Odegaard should retain his place in midfield.

Wales – Dragons Hoping to Keep Qualification Hopes Alive

Wales have also struggled for consistency in Group A4, losing three of their first four matches. However, their recent victory over Norway offers encouragement ahead of the trip to Oslo. Let’s take a closer look at their form and expected line-up.

Recent form

Wales began their Nations League campaign with consecutive defeats, losing 1-0 away to Portugal and 2-0 to Denmark. Craig Bellamy’s side responded with a 2-1 home victory over Norway in October, but they were unable to build on that result, suffering a 1-0 defeat to Denmark in their following fixture.

The Dragons therefore sit third in Group A4 with three points, level with Norway and three behind Denmark. Their chances of qualification remain alive, although losing both matches against the Danes means they would be disadvantaged by a head-to-head tiebreaker.

Wales have also struggled away from home in the competition, losing both of their previous road fixtures without scoring.

Bellamy will be without Benjamin Cabango and Sorba Thomas, who are both suspended. Six other players, including Ethan Ampadu, are also one booking away from missing the final group match. Harry Wilson returns after missing the previous international period through injury and is expected to start.

Predictions for Norway vs Wales – Hosts Favoured to Bounce Back

Wales have struggled to create chances away from home, failing to score in their two previous road fixtures. Norway will be eager to take control early, making Norway to win the first half another market worth considering.

Despite losing their last three matches, Norway possess considerable attacking quality, particularly with Haaland leading the line. Wales have also struggled for consistency, and their poor away record makes the hosts strong favourites. Our main prediction is Norway to win.
