    Luka Dončić drops 43 points, nine assists vs. PHX

    Luka Dončić

    LAL Forward-Guard #77
    Player Stats

    Luka Dončić finished Sunday’s win over the Suns with 43 points (13-of-28 FGs, 12-of-14 FTs), three rebounds, nine assists and five three-pointers.

    With no Austin Reaves (heel), Dončić was even more assertive on offense at the start of Sunday’s game. The Lakers star scored 20 points in the first quarter, shooting 7-of-8 from the foul line. Dončić did a lot of his damage against the Suns’ starters, scoring 32 points in the first and third quarters. He finished one assist shy of a double-double, continuing to play like a bona fide MVP candidate. The Lakers play three games next week, starting with the Hornets on Tuesday.
    - Raphielle Johnson
