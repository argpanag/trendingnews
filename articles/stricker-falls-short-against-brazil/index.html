Stricker Falls Short Against Brazil

TheTools · 18.09.2026 21:33 · source · permalink

Davis Cup

He got off to a good start but ultimately lost to Fonseca, a top-30 player: Davis Cup player Dominic Stricker

Keystone

As expected, Switzerland trails Brazil 0-1 in the Davis Cup. Dominic Stricker narrowly misses pulling off an upset against João Fonseca

Dominic Stricker, ranked No. 262 in the world, forced top-30 player João Fonseca into a third set, but as the match wore on, he was unable to win the crucial points. In the end, the Brazilian won the match 4-6, 6-3, 6-2 in just under two hours, giving his team a 1-0 lead.

Both players got off to an extremely nervous and error-prone start in the very lively Farmasi Hall in Rio. Stricker, however, was able to immediately make up for an early break deficit and then broke serve himself at 5-4 to seal the match. The 20-year-old rising star Fonseca, the winner of the most recent Swiss Indoors in Basel, could not hide his lack of match practice after having to sit out the U.S. Open due to an abdominal muscle injury. He also appeared to be quite nervous in what was his very first Davis Cup home match.

But then Fonseca steadily improved. In the second set, a single break was enough to put him up 5–3; in the third, after taking a 3–2 lead, he added another break to make it 5–2. For a long time, Stricker justified the confidence placed in him by Captain Severin Lüthi, who had chosen him over the higher-ranked Rémy Bertola and Jérôme Kym. In the end, however, the left-handed player from Bern didn’t serve well enough on the very slow indoor court and eventually lost his momentum.

In the second singles match, Stan Wawrinka (ATP No. 134) will face Gustavo Heide, who is 17 years younger and ranked 11 spots higher. A doubles match will follow on Saturday, and if necessary, two more singles matches. The winner will rejoin the elite next year and will have the chance to compete for a spot in the Davis Cup Finals.
