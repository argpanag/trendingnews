A lottery ticket worth $100,000 was sold at a store in Allentown.

A Powerball ticket that matched four of the five white balls drawn Wednesday was sold at the Sat Mini Mart at 1323 N. 19th St., the Pennsylvania Lottery said in a news release. It would have been worth $50,000 without the $1 Power Play option, the release states. The multiplier was two.

The store that sold the ticket will get a $500 bonus for selling it.
