Abdul Rahman Baba Ruled Out of Ghana’s Squad With Injury - Africa Top Sports - #1 African Sports News

TheTools · 22.09.2026 18:57 · source · permalink

Ghana’s Black Stars have suffered another injury setback ahead of their upcoming 2027 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) qualifiers, with experienced defender Abdul Rahman Baba ruled out of the squad.

Baba reported to the Black Stars camp for medical assessment after arriving for international duty. However, following further checks, the left-back was confirmed to be unfit to feature during the upcoming international window.

The development is another blow to head coach Carlos Queiroz, who will now have to make adjustments to his defensive options ahead of Ghana’s upcoming fixtures.

Baba’s experience and versatility have made him an important option for the national team, particularly at left-back, but his injury means he will not be available for the forthcoming AFCON qualifying matches.

The Black Stars have already dealt with several injury-related changes to the squad, making player availability a growing concern as Ghana prepares for the qualifiers.

Ghana will now await further medical updates and any decision from the technical team regarding a possible replacement for Baba.
