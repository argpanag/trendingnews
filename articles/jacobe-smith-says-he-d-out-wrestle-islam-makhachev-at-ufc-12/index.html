Jacobe Smith has gone from an unbooked fighter to a UFC co-main event in just over a month, stepping in on short notice twice. The unbeaten welterweight prospect faces veteran Randy Brown at UFC Vegas 123, convinced his wrestling and recent finishing run can carry him toward the division’s top 15.

  • Smith stopped Bruce Whitehead in 92 seconds after accepting that fight on nine days’ notice.
  • He is competing for the second time in three weeks, taking the Brown bout on 12 days’ notice.
  • Smith has won his last three UFC fights by finish: a submission of Nico Price and first-round TKOs of Josiah Harrell and Whitehead.

A win over Brown could put Smith in position to face a top-15 opponent and accelerate his rise in a crowded welterweight division. First, he must make good on his bold claims against a more experienced opponent.
