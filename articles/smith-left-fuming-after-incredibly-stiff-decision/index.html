CA’s captaincy stance receives support

Moises Henriques has weighed in after Cricket Australia’s decision to persist with Pat Cummins as captain, but conceded the three players who wore the brunt of the 2018 ball-tampering scandal would have loved the same treatment.

The governing body put out a statement over the weekend and confirmed that Cummins would remain at the helm for the Test team, despite calls for the fast-bowler to stand down amid the second coming of Sandpapergate.

Cummins has publicly been at odds with Usman Khawaja, David Warner and Cam Bancroft over the events of the 2018 South Africa tour, while former skipper Steve Smith hasn’t publicly addressed the messy situation.

“I’m just glad Cricket Australia aren’t making the wrong decision twice,” former Test all-rounder Henriques said on the Fanatics podcast.

“I’m sure Steve, Davey and Bangers would have loved to have been backed like the players are being backed now.

“I’m not saying anything should happen differently now, but maybe a bit of a concession from Cricket Australia to say ‘we got it wrong in 2018 and we’re not gonna get it wrong this time’ ... it’s good, they’re learning from their mistakes.

“I just want to acknowledge, it would have been nice to see in 2018 as well.”

Henriques believes the Cummins-Smith combination will be beneficial for the Aussie team heading into a massive stretch of Test cricket.
