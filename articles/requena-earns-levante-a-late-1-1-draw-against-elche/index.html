Requena earns Levante a late 1-1 draw against Elche

TheTools · 25.11.2026 22:14 · source · permalink

Levante drew 1-1 with Elche in round 13 of LaLiga in Valencia. Roy Revivo scored in the 61st minute, but Dani Requena levelled in the 88th. If you follow young players in Spain, note that Matia Baržić Guiterrez came on late for Revivo and made his second appearance in the top flight.

Levante rescue a point against Elche late on as Croatian international makes his second LaLiga appearance

Levante and Elche shared the points in their 13th-round Spanish league encounter, drawing 1-1 in Valencia. The visitors came close to securing a victory after Roy Revivo put his team ahead in the second half, but Dani Requena scored an equaliser late in the match. Elche therefore failed to preserve their advantage until the final whistle, while the home side avoided defeat thanks to their persistence in the closing minutes. The match had additional significance for Croatian football fans because young Croatian international Matia Baržić Guiterrez was once again given an opportunity to play. It was his second appearance in Spain's top division.

Levante took the initiative but could not find a way through

According to official LaLiga data, the match was played at the Ciutat de València stadium and attended by 18,260 spectators. Levante sought to impose their rhythm and take control of possession from the outset, while Elche initially had to devote more attention to defensive duties. The home side tried to break through their opponents' defence with combinations down the wings, crosses into the penalty area and passes behind the last defensive line. Thiago Fernández had the first notable opportunity, but his shot from inside the penalty area went wide. Levante continued to apply pressure, but their initial territorial superiority did not translate into a goal.

Injuries disrupted the home team's plans

Home coach Luis Castro had to react during the first half because of an injury problem within his team. Following a challenge, Toljan was unable to continue, and Nacho Pérez replaced him in the 22nd minute. Despite that change, Levante continued looking for attacking solutions. Roger Brugué delivered a cross towards Thiago Fernández, who attempted a header, but his effort went over the crossbar. In one of the following attacks, Brugué and Iván Romero combined inside the penalty area, but that move also failed to change the score. The home side maintained the initiative until the end of the first half, but Elche managed to preserve the initial 0-0 scoreline.
The match was played on Monday, November 23, 2026, and LaLiga's official match chronology shows that Levante had several attempts from different positions in the first half. As early as the third minute, Roger Brugué's shot was blocked by the visiting defence, and Thiago Fernández soon tried his luck following a pass from Dani Requena. Requena then attempted a shot from outside the penalty area, while Oriol Rey had another effort that failed to find the net. Shortly before half-time, Iván Romero shot following a headed assist from Roger Brugué, but the ball went just wide of the post. This sequence of opportunities confirmed that Levante were able to work their way into promising attacking positions, but lacked precision at crucial moments.

Elche found room to take the lead after half-time

The second half brought a shift in the balance of play. Elche introduced Thomas Lemar at half-time in place of Abiel Osorio, and after returning to the pitch, the visitors began to play a greater role in possession. Levante simultaneously lost another player to injury: Manu Sánchez injured his knee and made way for Marc Santos in the 56th minute. According to Levante's report, the home side had to readjust their formation on the pitch following that stoppage and substitution. The visitors gradually began to find more space, and their growing pressure eventually resulted in a goal.
Roy Revivo found the net following a pass from Germán Valera to put Elche ahead. LaLiga records the goal in its official match report as having been scored in the 62nd minute, and that move changed the course of the encounter. Revivo finished the attack with a right-footed shot from inside the penalty area, beating goalkeeper Mathew Ryan. Elche had now reached a position they were determined to protect, while Levante had to take greater attacking risks. After conceding, the home side continued searching for a response, but could no longer rely solely on their previous control of the game.

Matia Baržić Guiterrez given an opportunity in the closing stages

Croatian footballer Matia Baržić Guiterrez came on in the 81st minute, replacing goalscorer Revivo. His introduction was recorded in LaLiga's official match chronology, while tportal reports that his previous appearance in the competition came in the match against Espanyol, which Elche won. This time, the Croatian international entered the game while his team still held the lead, but Levante were increasing their pressure and searching for an equaliser. His introduction was part of a double substitution, as Tete Morente also replaced Lucas Cepeda in the same minute.
Elche had opportunities to extend their lead even after going ahead, but failed to take advantage of them. Before leaving the pitch, Roy Revivo tried to threaten the opposition goal again, but his shot following an assist from Fer Niño missed the target. Levante, meanwhile, continued applying pressure down the wings and looking for space to shoot. In the 77th and 78th minutes, the home coach introduced further attacking options, bringing on Petar Ratkov and Karl Etta Eyong in place of Iván Romero and Oriol Rey. These changes pushed the game further towards the visitors' penalty area, forcing Elche to spend an increasing amount of time defending.

Requena equalised, while Ryan stopped the final attempt

The home side's pressure produced a result in the 88th minute. Dani Requena received the ball in the centre of the penalty area and scored with his right foot to make it 1-1. Points shared after an open and eventful finish
