Light to moderate rainfall moves in tonight: more rain chances by the end of the week | 10/26 PM

TheTools · 26.10.2026 22:45 · source · permalink

LAWTON, Okla. (KSWO) -Rain chances will increase tonight as a disturbance moves in from the west. Just expect light to moderate rainfall out of this system, and I can’t rule out a few rumbles of thunder. Lows tonight will be in the upper 50s to low 60s. We dry out late tomorrow morning and afternoon with highs in the upper 70s to near 80 with partly cloudy skies. We remain dry on Wednesday before our next storm system moves in late Thursday and into your Friday. It will have help from the remnants of Tropical Storm Vance in terms of the moisture content. We could have some some decent rainfall associated with this system as well. As of now, we look to dry out by your Halloween afternoon and evening with mostly cloudy skies. We are watching for another potentially potent storm system early next week as we start November.

Meteorologist Michael Amankwaa
