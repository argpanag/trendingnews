Pongbot Aura: the ultimate training gift for tennis, pickleball and padel players - Phandroid

TheTools · 18.11.2026 22:03 · source · permalink

The problem with racket sports is that sometimes it can be hard to find someone to play or practice with. As working adults, schedules can clash; there are family obligations, and courts can fill up and get booked out. The Pongbot Aura is a training robot built to solve that problem. With Black Friday pricing now live, it makes a strong gift for tennis, pickleball and padel players.

The Aura is a ball-launching machine powered by AI. However, unlike more typical ball launchers, it does more than fire balls at one fixed speed. Pongbot developed it using “hundreds of thousands” of hours of actual match data. It can also build practice sessions around what a player wants to work on. Because one machine handles three sports, a single gift covers a lot more ground than you’d expect.

Why the Pongbot Aura Is an Easy Gift to Say Yes To

Most ball machines are bulky and usually need a car to move them around. The Aura weighs about 7 kilograms, or roughly 15 pounds. Pongbot says that makes it significantly lighter than most traditional machines. It can be carried by hand or over the shoulder. The ball basket folds flat when you’re not using it. Pongbot’s product page lists room for up to 120 tennis balls or 100 pickleballs.

There is also a reason the gift should not feel outdated next year. Pongbot says the Aura gets over-the-air software updates. This means users can look forward to new features every now and then without having to buy new hardware. The company says those updates will eventually cover other ball sports such as softball and baseball.

Who on Your Gift List Will Get the Most Out of It

So, who is this gift for? It could be for yourself. Or maybe it could be for a friend or family member who loves ball games and simply wants to get better. Tennis, pickleball, and padel fans who want to lift their level can run focused drills on their own schedule. This is perfect if you’re looking to get in some practice, but no one is free.

If you have kids, the Aura could be a great gift in helping them develop their skills. Kids tend to improve with repetition, and the Aura makes at-home practice more productive than hitting against a wall.

Pongbot also points out that the machine helps players who can’t afford a private coach. Yes, admittedly it has a higher upfront cost, but honestly, with multiple sessions whenever you want, it kind of pays for itself in the end. It also helps those who live far from a top training facility.

If you’re a competitive player, this could help take your game to the next level. You can train with your coach on training days, but you can also fit in the Aura between lessons to keep practicing what your coach taught you. Think of it like extra credit.

What the Pongbot Aura Can Do Once It Hits the Court

The Aura’s AI assistant works using plain, natural language. A player can describe a goal, and the system builds a training plan around it. Players can also upload match footage. From there, the AI uses the footage to study things like ball patterns, placement, and rhythm of the match. After that, it recreates them as drills you can practice along to. For instance, if you had a particularly bad game from last weekend’s match, you can practice that exact situation.

For anyone who prefers to pick their own routine, the machine ships with more than 10,000 built-in drills. These drills can be tailored in the Pongbot app, including ball speed, spin, depth, and feed timing.

Another useful feature is voice commands. You can actually speak/command the Aura through a pair of headphones or an Apple Watch. This lets players make adjustments without having to walk across the court to the machine.

Pongbot also sells a variant called the Aura S, which adds a dual-camera module called Spotter. Spotter is what supports Pongbot’s AI Coach. Pongbot says the coach gets to know how you play and changes the practice as you improve. Spotter gives it something to work from by tracking your shots and how you move around the court.

After a session, you get training feedback based on what it saw. Spotter also brings a feature called Recovery Trigger, which paces the feeding around how quickly you get back into position after each shot. That should come fairly close to mimicking an actual rally.

Pongbot Aura Black Friday Pricing and Availability

Pongbot’s Black Friday sale runs from October 20 through December 6, 2026. Details on every offer are on Pongbot’s Black Friday page.

The standard Aura costs $599.99 and includes activation for one sport of your choice: tennis, pickleball, or padel. The Aura 3-in-1 costs $649.99 and activates all three. The Aura S costs $799.99 and includes one chosen sport plus the Spotter module. The Aura S 3-in-1 costs $849.99 and includes all three sports and Spotter.

Tennis-only players who want to go further can look at the Pace S Pro at $1,219.99. It adds AI coaching and unlimited custom drills. It comes with 564 preset drills and 8 hours of play, plus the ability to test against any level. The AO Pace S Pro costs $1,599.99 and is aimed at pro-level performance. It has 564 preset drills, or you can design your own.
