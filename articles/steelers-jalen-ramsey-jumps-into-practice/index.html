Steelers' Jalen Ramsey: Jumps into practice

TheTools · 11.09.2026 00:58 · source · permalink

Ramsey (knee) was a full participant in Wednesday's practice, Teresa Varley of the Steelers' official site reports.

Ramsey was activated off the active/PUP list in late August prior to cutdown day. The starting slot cornerback for the Steelers missed most of training camp with a knee injury but has regained his health in time for the regular season.
