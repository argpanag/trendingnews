World Gym Plans 30 Thailand Clubs as Global Fitness Giants Target Market

TheTools · 16.10.2026 15:45 · source · permalink

Fitness•Industry News

The Taiwan-based gym operator has big plans for Thailand, citing the country’s relatively low gym penetration, rising consumer spending power and growing interest in fitness

World Gym is making a major push into Thailand, with plans to open 30 clubs across the country over the next five years as international gym operators increasingly target the Southeast Asian market for expansion.

The Taiwan-based fitness giant has signed a lease for its second Bangkok location at S-Oasis, a commercial and office development owned by Thai real estate company Singha Estate.

Spanning more than 32,000 square feet, the new club is scheduled to open in the first quarter of 2027 and will target office workers, corporate tenants and residents in the surrounding area.

The announcement comes as World Gym prepares to open its first Thailand location at Imperial World Samrong in the Bangkok metropolitan area. That club signed up more than 1,000 presale members in just two weeks after launching presales in late September, according to the company.

“We have ambitious plans for Thailand and see the market as our beachhead for growth across Southeast Asia,” World Gym Corporation president John Caraccio said. “We are working with major real estate groups, including Singha Estate, to accelerate our expansion and establish our clubs in prime commercial and mixed-use developments.”

World Gym is also bringing Hyrox to Thailand, building on its existing partnership with the fitness racing brand in Taiwan, where it is Hyrox’s largest gym partner in the Asia-Pacific region.

The company plans to extend the partnership to Thailand later this year, entering a market where Hyrox Bangkok attracted more than 17,000 athletes in March.

Global Fitness Brands Bet on Thailand

World Gym’s expansion comes as Thailand emerges as a major growth target for international fitness brands, with several operators announcing ambitious development plans in recent months.

Earlier this month, Middle Eastern gym giant GymNation announced plans to open more than 20 locations across Thailand over the next three to five years, beginning with a 40,000-square-foot flagship in Bangkok in early 2027.

Xponential Fitness-owned Club Pilates is also expanding in Thailand. The boutique fitness brand signed a master franchise agreement in 2025 to develop 20 studios across Bangkok and Chonburi Province, opening its first location in the Thai capital earlier this year.

For World Gym, the push into Thailand represents an opportunity to replicate its success in Taiwan, where it operates 142 locations and ranks as the country’s largest fitness chain.

In 2024, Taiwan-based World Gym Corporation acquired World Gym International, taking ownership of the global brand and its operating rights. The company now oversees an international franchise network spanning 298 locations across 10 countries.

World Gym has identified Thailand’s relatively low gym penetration, rising consumer spending power and growing interest in fitness as key factors behind its expansion strategy, with the country serving as a potential springboard for further growth across Southeast Asia.
