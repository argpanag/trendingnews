Papa Salt appoints first Global Marketing Director - The Shout

TheTools · 05.11.2026 22:30 · source · permalink

Papa Salt Coastal Gin has appointed its first Global Marketing Director, with Richard Pilbeam joining the brand as growth in Australia and overseas accelerates.

Since its launch in 2023 when the initial product run sold out in less than 48 hours and first-year sales quadrupled the original forecast, Papa Salt has become one of Australia’s top 10 super-premium gins. Last year, the Byron Bay-distilled gin sold more than 46,000 bottles – equating to approximately one bottle sold every 11 minutes.

Building on this momentum and looking to further international expansion, Pilbeam has been appointed to lead brand strategy and marketing worldwide, bringing experience across established global drinks brands and emerging businesses. As Director of Marketing, Pilbeam’s six-year tenure with Monster Energy overlapped a period in which its Great Britain business grew by approximately 52 per cent. Most recently, he built and led Goodrays’ marketing department, with the business recording revenue growth of 218 per cent in 2025.

In the newly created role at Papa Salt, Pilbeam will be responsible for building brand awareness, driving consumer engagement and supporting growth across Australia, the UK and new markets.

“I’m stoked to be joining Papa Salt. It’s a brand that’s doing things a bit differently and not following the category conventions. I think spirits is one of the toughest categories to stand out in right now, and that’s exactly what makes this so exciting. The growth so far has been incredible, but the real excitement is in what this brand can become globally,” Pilbeam stated.

Charlie Maas, Co-founder and CEO of Papa Salt, added: “We’ve built strong momentum in Australia since launching in 2023, and creating our first global marketing role is an important investment in the next stage of the business. Our ambition is to become Australia’s number one super-premium gin while continuing to grow internationally. Richard brings the combination of big-brand experience and challenger instinct to help us achieve that.”
