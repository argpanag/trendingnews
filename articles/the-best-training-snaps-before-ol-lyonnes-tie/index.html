We face eight-time European champions OL Lyonnes in the UEFA Women's Champions League on Thursday night, and you can find out what we've been up to in training before the tie with our latest training gallery. 

Our Gunners battled to a 1-1 draw with Barcelona in Spain last week, ensuring we enter the final two rounds of the league phase in the top four, where we'll need to finish in order to progress to the quarter-finals without the need for a play-off. 

OL Lyonnes have been a familiar opponent in the past two seasons, with Thursday's game set to be our eighth meeting in the last four seasons.
