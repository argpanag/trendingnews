Prince Albert II and Princess Charlene Attend Red Cross Meeting in Monaco

TheTools · 17.09.2026 13:08 · source · permalink

Monaco is hosting representatives from several of Europe’s smaller National Red Cross Societies from September 15 to 18, with discussions focused on sharing experience and strengthening humanitarian cooperation. Prince Albert II and Princess Charlene attended alongside Camille Gottlieb.

Organized by the Croix-Rouge Monégasque (CRM), the meeting is taking place in Monaco from September 15 to 18.

This year, it brings together the National Societies of Andorra, Cyprus, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, and Malta, along with representatives from the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies and the International Committee of the Red Cross.

View this post on Instagram

The gathering provides an opportunity for Europe’s smaller National Societies to share experiences and strengthen cooperation.

It also highlights Prince Albert II’s commitment to international humanitarian action and cooperation within the International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement.

Prince Albert II and Princess Charlene, respectively president and vice-president of the Croix-Rouge monégasque, attended alongside Camille Gottlieb, who heads the organization’s Youth section.
