Dplus KIA Youth Sweeps Both First and Second-Half LCK Academy League Titles

Dplus KIA Youth (DK) achieved back-to-back championships in the 2026 LCK Academy Series (LCK AS) Academy League, winning both the first and second-half seasons. In the second-half finals, they defeated T1 Esports Academy (T1A) after a hard-fought five-game series.

The Korea e-Sports Association (KeSPA) announced on the 17th the conclusion of the second-half Academy League. Dplus KIA Youth received the championship trophy along with 2.5 million won in prize money. Runner-up T1 Esports Academy was awarded 1.5 million won, while third-place Hanjin BRION Academy (BRO) received 1 million won.

In the full league standings prior to the finals, T1 Esports Academy finished in first place and Dplus KIA Youth in second. T1 Esports Academy posted a 26-1 record during the full league and reached the finals undefeated in the playoffs. Dplus KIA Youth lost to T1 Esports Academy in the upper bracket of Round 3 before defeating Hanjin BRION Academy in the lower bracket finals to earn a rematch.

T1 Esports Academy took the opening game of the finals behind a strong performance from 'Weather' Lee Tae-yang. Dplus KIA Youth answered back in game 2 through solid teamwork led by 'Nevid' Park Min-woo, and took the lead in game 3 by securing key objectives. Although T1 Esports Academy leveled the series in game 4 by substituting their mid laner, Dplus KIA Youth sealed the title in game 5 by playing around 'Xdp' Lee Ju-chan's Aphelios.

"I am thrilled that we were able to win the second-half championship following our victory in the first half," said 'Risto' Kang Woo-seok of Dplus KIA Youth. "After losing to T1 Esports Academy in the Round 3 upper bracket, we worked hard to address our weaknesses in macro execution and draft strategy. Properly executing what we prepared for the finals on stage led to this great result."

In the final standings, behind the top three teams, Hanwha Life Esports finished in 4th place, DN Supers Academy in 5th, and KT Rolster Academy in 6th. By dominating both the first and second-half leagues, Dplus KIA Youth established themselves as the undisputed overall champion of 2026.

As the official third-tier league under the LCK, LCK AS features academy rosters from LCK organizations alongside top teams from the School League. In this second-half season, Eunpyeong Meditech High School Gaming also competed after earning a spot through the School League Preseason. The 2026 LCK AS was hosted by Riot Games, co-organized by Riot Games and KeSPA, and sponsored by Woori Bank.
