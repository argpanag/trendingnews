Scott Disick has a new appreciation for a longtime family member.

On the Thursday, Oct. 29, episode of The Kardashians, Disick reflected on the time he'd recently spent with Caitlyn Jenner at Kendall Jenner's new home. The trio's hangout was featured on the Oct. 22 episode of the reality show, as Caitlyn, 76, and Scott, 43, helped Kendall get her house ready for her horses. (They had a bit of an awkward interaction when Caitlyn made a NSFW comment.)

"I loved it there that day," Disick told Kendall, 30, of her new house. "And I felt like your dad, too, was in such a good mood. I've never gotten along that good with her in, like, ever."

"You guys chopped it up," Kendall said of their dynamic.

Caitlyn Jenner, Scott Disick and Kendall Jenner on 'The Kardashians' season 8
Credit: Hulu

"I don't know, she was just fun to chill with," Disick said of Caitlyn. "She was funny."

Caitlyn's appearance on the Oct. 22 episode was a rare one, and marked only her second time on the Hulu reality series. She was more of a mainstay on Keeping Up with the Kardashians, which concluded in 2021 after 20 seasons.

She first appeared on Hulu's The Kardashians on season 7, when she surprised daughters Kendall and Kylie and former stepchildren Kim, Kourtney, Khloé and Rob, by arriving for the family farewell dinner at ex-wife Kris Jenner's Hidden Hills home.

"It's only right for Caitlyn to be here tonight too because she actually was part of all those amazing memories," Kris said in a confessional of her last-minute decision to invite her ex, to whom she was married from 1991 to 2014. "It wasn't just my house — it was our house."

In a confessional, Kendall expressed her surprise at Caitlyn's arrival. "I definitely wasn't expecting my dad to be here, just knowing the relationship that my mom has with my dad — not that it's bad, it's just distant," she said.
