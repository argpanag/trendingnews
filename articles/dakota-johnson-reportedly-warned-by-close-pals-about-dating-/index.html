Dakota Johnson Reportedly Warned By Close Pals About Dating ‘Childish Bad Boy’ MGK As Romance Rumors Swirl

TheTools · 11.10.2026 01:46 · source · permalink

Dakota Johnson is reportedly receiving warnings from people close to her about her budding romance with Machine Gun Kelly, as concerns grow over whether the musician is the right match for the actress.

The pair have sparked romance rumors in recent months, but an insider claims the actress's friends are questioning whether the rapper can offer the stability she wants in a relationship.

The alleged concerns come as Dakota Johnson and MGK continue spending time together following their connection at Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce's wedding in July.

Dakota Johnson's Friends Are Reportedly Concerned About MGK

ZUMAPRESS.com / MEGA

According to an insider, Johnson has long been attracted to musicians who are confident, creative, and comfortable standing out from the crowd.

MGK reportedly possesses those qualities, but some of her friends are concerned that his rebellious public image may not translate into the kind of lasting partnership she wants.

"Dakota definitely has a type, and there's something about musicians that she finds incredibly compelling. She likes creative people who are confident in their own skin and don't feel they have to conform, and Colson has that in abundance," a source told OK! Magazine.

The insider added that MGK's confidence and larger-than-life personality appeal to Johnson, but those closest to her are reportedly taking a more cautious view of the relationship.

"They see someone who has spent years embracing this wild, provocative rock-star persona, and they're questioning whether the characteristics that make him exciting at the beginning of a romance are necessarily the qualities Dakota will want from a partner several years down the line," the source explained.

"They are essentially telling her he is a childish bad boy," the insider added, saying friends want Johnson to consider whether MGK is someone she could realistically build a future with rather than simply enjoy dating.

Insiders Question Whether MGK Wants A Conventional Family Life

ZUMAPRESS.com / MEGA

The source claimed Johnson is increasingly focused on motherhood and finding a partner with whom she can establish a stable home.

Her friends reportedly believe those ambitions make it important for her to understand whether MGK wants the same things before the relationship becomes more serious.

"Dakota is at a point where she's openly talking about wanting children. She's not approaching relationships as though she has unlimited time to discover whether somebody shares those ambitions," the insider said.

Johnson has also spoken openly about wanting to become a mother. In a recent interview with Vogue Magazine, she said, "I really do. I didn't expect to be this age, and at this place in my life, but for whatever reason, that's the plan of the universe. So I would love that."

The source suggested that MGK's established lifestyle could make settling into a more conventional family arrangement difficult.

"[MGK] has already lived a complicated romantic life. He's a father, he's had major relationships, and he's built a career around being fiercely individualistic and free-spirited. Music remains an enormous part of his world, and he appears comfortable living life on his own terms," the insider explained.

"People around Dakota aren't convinced that suddenly becoming somebody's conventional husband and settling into a quieter existence is necessarily what he's searching for," the source continued.

There Is Chemistry Between MGK And Dakota Johnson, But There Are Questions About Commitment

ZUMAPRESS.com / MEGA

Despite their reported concerns, the insider emphasized that Johnson's friends are not necessarily suggesting MGK has bad intentions or that the attraction between them is insincere.

Instead, they reportedly want her to look beyond the excitement of a new romance and consider whether their long-term expectations align.

"That's why there have been warnings not to get swept away by the chemistry. Nobody around Dakota is suggesting Colson has bad intentions or that what is happening between them isn't genuine. They clearly enjoy one another's company, and there's a strong attraction," the source said.

The insider noted that the people closest to Johnson want her to consider what both partners ultimately expect from the relationship.

"Her friends simply don't want excitement to obscure some fairly fundamental questions about what each of them wants," the source explained.

The source also revealed that Johnson has gained a clearer understanding of herself following previous relationships, particularly her long romance with Martin.

"She doesn't want to spend years trying to turn the wrong relationship into the right one," the insider stated.

The Actress And The Rapper's Different Personalities Could Get More Noticeable Down The Lane

ZUMAPRESS.com / MEGA

The insider described Johnson and MGK as "also very different characters," suggesting their contrasting personalities may be exciting at first but could create challenges if they begin discussing marriage, children, and where to settle down.

"Dakota can be private, understated and quite grounded in her everyday life. Colson is naturally much more flamboyant. He likes making an entrance; he's eccentric, and there's still a rebellious streak to him," the source said.

The insider continued, "Right now, those differences probably make things more exciting because neither is dating a version of themselves. But friends wonder what happens once the novelty wears off."

Dakota Johnson Is Not Looking For Something Serious Immediately

ZUMAPRESS.com / MEGA

According to OK! Magazine, the source stressed that Johnson is not demanding an immediate commitment or planning a wedding.

Rather, her friends are said to be thinking about whether her and MGK's different lifestyles could become a problem over time.

"Nobody is suggesting Dakota is already planning a wedding or demanding some enormous commitment from him. This is still relatively new, and they're enjoying discovering what they have together," the insider said.

However, the source warned that attraction alone may not be enough to sustain a serious relationship, adding that Johnson ultimately needs to decide whether the man behind MGK's rock-star image wants the same future she does.
