Ignoring Mbappé: Raphinha says this duo are the driving force behind Real Madrid

TheTools · 25.10.2026 13:21 · source · permalink

Explosive statements from the Brazilian star

Brazil's Raphinha turned heads before the Clasico clash against Real Madrid. The Barcelona star named the duo he sees as the driving force of the Royal side, and France's Kylian Mbappe didn't make the cut, despite his brilliance with Los Merengues.

The website "Tribuna" published Raphinha's remarks ahead of the meeting between the two sides. He spoke about the biggest threats to Barcelona, warning that the absence of Vinicius Junior won't make life any easier for the Catalan side in a match of this magnitude.

Raphinha said: "I can't say his absence will be a good thing for us, because in the end Real Madrid have many players capable of deciding matches."

He added: "It's true that Vini won't be there, but there are other players who can fill his position and make the difference in a match of this magnitude."

Vinicius will miss the Clasico, yet Raphinha insists Real Madrid's squad is packed with players able to cover for him. That makes the Royal side a tough proposition for Barcelona.

Valverde earns special praise from Raphinha

His comments weren't limited to Vinicius. Raphinha singled out Uruguay's Fede Valverde for clear praise, highlighting his tactical flexibility and his ability to shine in more than one position.

Raphinha said: "Fede can push forward from midfield, and he also gives the team many options when he plays at full-back."

He continued: "In the end, he's an amazing player, he produces great performances in midfield, and he also shines when he plays out wide."

Real Madrid's driving force

Why Valverde and Vinicius? For Raphinha, the pair represent the team's driving force thanks to the effort and work they put in, regardless of their positions on the pitch.

The Barcelona star said: "Fede and Vini are Real Madrid's driving force. They are the type of players who put in a lot of effort and work for the benefit of their team, regardless of the position they play in."

These words land before a huge match for both sides. Barcelona and Real Madrid go to war again, and the Clasico brings its own calculations, from individual readiness to the tactical details that could decide the clash.
