ASML celebrates start of campus construction that will open up an extra 20,000 jobs

TheTools · 08.09.2026 12:30 · source · permalink

ASML is celebrating the start of the construction of a new campus that will help with the significant expansion of the chip machine manufacturing company near Eindhoven. The new location will ensure that the high-tech company’s most important activities will stay in the region. The expansion is meant to accommodate an extra 20,000 jobs, which almost doubles the number of employees at the company in the Netherlands.

Prime Minister Rob Jetten, his predecessor Dick Schoof, and Eindhoven Mayor Jeroen Dijsselbloem are present at the ceremony. The company’s CEO Christophe Fouquet thanked the local and national governments for the “close cooperation” that made the construction possible.

The government launched an initiative to ensure the region remained an attractive location for the chip industry before construction began on Brainport Industries Campus (BIC) North. The reports stating that ASML could expand outside the Netherlands if the business climate didn’t improve resulted in the caretaker government at the time starting “project Beethoven” to facilitate further growth. As the world’s only supplier of machines needed to make the most advanced chips, ASML fulfills an important role that the Dutch government wishes to keep.

One example of this is the tens of millions of euros that went to training courses and investments in accessibility in the region. ASML also needed to solve two important bottlenecks before it could start construction of the campus: the granting of a nitrogen permit and finding a connection to the overloaded power grid.

ASML has since stated that the expansion of the electricity supply has been arranged for the campus because TenneT is going to extend a high-voltage station by Best.

In addition, the Noord-Brabant company was granted a nitrogen permit, which is rare in the province. The expansion could only go ahead after authorities established that it served a major public interest and that measures would be taken to compensate for the impact on nature.

The construction of BIC North will happen in phases. The target is for the location to offer at least 3,000 jobs by the end of the first phase in 2029. The expansion is also meant to help ASML work more efficiently and make it possible to produce parts of its chip machines at a larger scale.

Chip machine companies are planning significant expansions due to the popularity of artificial intelligence, which will require more chip machines. For ASML, one of their biggest concerns is whether they can meet this increase in demand.
