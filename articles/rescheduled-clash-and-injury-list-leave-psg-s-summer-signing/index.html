Rescheduled clash and injury list leave PSG’s summer signings centre stage against Lyon

Paris Saint-Germain will be without Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, whose cruciate ligament rupture probably rules him out for the season, and Ousmane Dembélé, sidelined by a thigh injury for around a month. The club will have to rely on their summer signings against Lyon in Ligue 1.

It could be their first real domestic test. Le Progres reports that the fixture was pushed back to Monday at 20:30 at the request of the police prefecture.

The capital club host an OL side who were still top before Lille’s 1-1 draw with Monaco on Sunday.

The situation hands extra responsibility to Désiré Doué and the new arrivals, starting with this meeting.

That assignment begins on Monday night, then continues over the coming weeks.
