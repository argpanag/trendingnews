The largest buy was Apple Inc, up 933,204 shares for $315.7 million, or 8.15% of assets. NVIDIA Corp followed with 1,540,938 shares added for $311.4 million at an 8.03% weight, then Microsoft Corp at 474,810 shares for $240.8 million (6.21%). None of the top buys was a new position, and the fund sold out of nothing.

How does QQQ invest?

INVESCO QQQ Trust (QQQ) is an equity fund holding 101 stocks, with 46.5% of assets in its 10 largest holdings. Technology accounts for 56.8% of the stocks that could be classified, Consumer Cyclical 11.4% and Communication Services 11.2%, and the portfolio is 100.0% large-cap. It charges an expense ratio of 0.18%, and its top holdings are Apple Inc at 8.15%, NVIDIA Corp at 8.03%, Microsoft Corp at 6.21%, Amazon.com Inc at 4.37% and Micron Technology Inc at 4.29%. The fund has a GF Score of 88, a P/E of 28.72, a P/B of 6.99, a dividend yield of 0.44% and a year-to-date return of 11.93%.
