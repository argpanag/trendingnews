Paris Hilton Sent Love on Presley Gerber's Final Instagram Post Hours Before Death Was Publicly Revealed

TheTools · 21.09.2026 03:24 · source · permalink

It looks like Paris Hilton knew about Presley Gerber‘s death before the rest of the world.

Paris sent love to her late friend Presley in the comments section of his final Instagram post just hours before his death was publicly revealed. He passed away on September 20 at the age of 27.

Presley‘s final post on Instagram was on August 5 and featured him modeling clothing from Vuori Clothing, a brand his family has been partnered with for the past year.

Presley Gerber’s final Instagram post

Presley‘s final Instagram post went live on August 5 and he’s been inactive on his main feed since then.

Paris Hilton left a comment on the post on Sunday, September 20 and it’s now getting attention because of the timing of his death.

Paris Hilton’s comment on Presley Gerber’s post

Paris commented on the post, “Love you bro.” She added the heart-face emoji and the heart hands emoji.

One fan seemingly had the accurate view of what happened, commenting, “he probably died hours ago and she already knew about it. We the public find out last. Many hours later.”

Paris and Presley were longtime friends.

UPDATE: Paris has now released a lengthy statement about the death of Presley.

Presley Gerber’s final red carpet appearance was at Paris Hilton’s event

Presley Gerber‘s final appearance on a red carpet was on January 20, 2026 for the premiere of Paris Hilton’s Infinite Icon: A Visual Memoir.

The documentary is now streaming online.

Kaia Gerber recently spoke out with rare comments on Presley‘s “decade long struggle with drug addiction.”
