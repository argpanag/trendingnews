After three weeks of tennis, the U.S. Open comes to an end today with the men's finals between Ben Shelton and Alexander Zverev. The crowd inside Arthur Ashe Stadium will undeniably be rooting for Shelton as he looks to become the first American man to win a Grand Slam title since 2003. "My ideal crowd is…Go to the U.S. Open every year," Shelton told T&C last year. "Watch any American playing, and that's kind of my ideal crowd."

Ahead of the finals, Shelton said in a press conference, "This whole tournament now at this point has been unchartered territory. I'm in positions I've never been in before. I've beaten guys that I have never beaten before. I'm just excited to put it all out on the line."

The last day of the tournament is typically the starriest, and today was no different. In attendance for the match at Arthur Ashe Stadium included three of T&C's September cover stars—Karl-Anthony Towns, Martha Stewart, and Chase Sui Wonders—alongside Lesley Manville, Lin-Manuel Miranda, and more A-Listers.
