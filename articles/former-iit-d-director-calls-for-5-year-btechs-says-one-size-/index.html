Former IIT-D director calls for 5-year BTechs, says one-size-fits-all hurts students

TheTools · 23.09.2026 07:10 · source · permalink

V Ramgopal Rao has urged IITs to consider five-year BTech programmes amid the IIT Bombay controversy around professor Suryanarayana Doola. He said a flexible structure could better support students in highly heterogeneous classrooms without lowering standards.
