Man dead after hit-and-run crash near Harry and Woodlawn, search continues for 2 who fled scene

TheTools · 22.11.2026 05:11 · source · permalink

WICHITA, Kan. (KWCH) - Update: The Wichita Police Department confirmed that 21-year-old Tuan Phan of Wichita died from his injuries in a three-vehicle crash reported Saturday night in the 1600 block of South Woodlawn, near Harry and Woodlawn.

The search continues for two others who fled from the scene.

The preliminary investigation into the crash, reported a little before 10 p.m. Saturday, indicated that a Chevy Silverado was traveling north on Woodlawn when it crossed the center line into southbound traffic and collided with the front of a white Toyota Corolla. Police said the Silverado then struck a white Chevy Impala that was also southbound.

The truck’s driver and a passenger exited the truck and fled the scene, heading west, police said. Police didn’t provide an approximate age for either suspect, but said they are both males.

Police said that Phan was driving the Toyota Corolla. A passenger in that vehicle, a 19-year-old woman, was injured and transported to a local hospital.

The Impala’s driver remained at the scene, as did a second passenger in the Silverado.

Police said alcohol may have been a factor in the crash, and investigators are working to locate the pickup’s driver and one passenger who fled the scene.

Anyone with information should call WPD Accident Follow-up detectives at 316-350-3689 or submit an anonymous tip to Crime Stoppers at 316-267-2111.

One person has died after a crash in southeast Wichita.

Sedgwick County dispatch confirms that the crash took place on Saturday night at 9:49 p.m. at the intersection of Harry Street and Woodlawn Boulevard.

Another person was injured in the crash, although not critically.
