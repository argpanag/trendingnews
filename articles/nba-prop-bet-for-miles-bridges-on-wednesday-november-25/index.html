NBA Prop Bet for Wednesday, November 25

  • Miles Bridges Over 8.5 Rebounds + Assists (-119) 

Phoenix walks into Toyota Center on a four-game winning streak with its NBA Cup life on the line. Houston was knocked out Tuesday. It's a rematch of a November 7 game the Suns stole 102-99, a night Houston missed 58 shots and Miles Bridges cleaned up the mess with 10 rebounds and seven assists.

The books have Houston by 9.5, but the way this game gets played sets up Bridges just like it did three weeks ago.

It starts with how Phoenix defends now. On Nov. 5, Jordan Ott moved Oso Ighodaro into the starting lineup in place of rookie Khaman Maluach, and the Suns started switching more, which pulls their centers away from the rim.

Ott admitted early in the season that defensive rebounding caught him off guard: "I did think we would be bigger this year." So Phoenix rebounds as a group, and the wings have to finish possessions.

Houston makes that job harder. The Rockets rebound 34% of their own misses, the best rate in the league over their last eight games.

When a shot goes up, Ighodaro and Maluach are busy putting bodies on Alperen Sengun and Steven Adams, and it shows in their numbers: Ighodaro grabs just 47% of his rebound chances, Maluach 42%.

The long ones fall to the wings. Bridges is averaging 11.3 rebound chances a game since the lineup change, converting 66% of them, and only about four in 10 of his boards come against a contest.

The offense flipped on that date too. Devin Booker became the hub: his touches went from 51 a game to 61, his drives from 13 to 19 and his potential assists from 7.3 to 11.2 — and Bridges became the release valve. He's creating 5.6 potential assists a game on 44 touches, and Houston gives up 5.3 assists a game to power forwards, the fourth-most in the league.

Adams rotates on time, but Sengun and Kevin Durant both showed up late on drives in Houston's last two Court Lab games. When Booker draws that first help, the ball swings to Bridges with the defense already scrambling.

Since the lineup change, he's cleared 8.5 in seven of 10, averaging 11.1, including four of his last five (10, 11, 13, 16).

The risk is minutes. All three misses came in games decided by double digits. With the Cup on the line and the Suns playing their best ball of the season, I don't see Ott pulling his starters while this is competitive. At his usual 28 or 29 minutes, I project 10.3 rebounds and assists.

Pick: Miles Bridges Over 8.5 Rebounds + Assists (-119)
