'Wasteful' Gunners, Man City suffer European setbacks

TheTools · 25.11.2026 00:27 · source · permalink

Arsenal have dropped points from a winning position for the second time in as many games following a 1-1 Champions League draw with Borussia Dortmund at the Emirates Stadium.

Mikel Arteta's men led at the interval on Tuesday night (Wednesday AEDT) after Viktor Gyokeres scored his first goal on home soil in 206 days with a wonderful 25-yard opener inside 10 minutes.

But just as they did in the Premier League at Newcastle on the weekend, the Gunners were unable to see out the victory, with Maximilian Beier netting for the visitors after halftime to leave Arsenal fifth in the standings on 11 points from five games.

"It was a brilliant performance ... bearing in mind the opposition, is something I can be very, very pleased about," Arteta said.

"It was great to watch, but it was unbelievable that sometimes the ball didn't end up in the back of the net."

At Etihad Stadium, Rayan Cherki scored a fine late equaliser as Manchester City snatched a 2-2 draw against Napoli.

Noa Lang put the Italian side ahead for a second time with a thumping volley after 76 minutes.

Former Manchester United striker Rasmus Hojlund opened the scoring for Napoli, before Ayyoub Bouaddi levelled.

Lang's effort put the visitors in sight of a first victory in England, but Cherki's solo effort four minutes from time salvaged a point for City.

"Football is easy and difficult – when you win it's easy and you know what to do. When you don't you start questioning everything," said City striker Erling Haaland.

"We have to regroup, stay positive and bring energy on the pitch and try to do our best."

Enzo Maresca's side have now won just one of their last seven matches in all competitions and are on 10 points, along with Dortmund in sixth and seventh.

In the Spanish capital, Kylian Mbappe bagged a brace as Real Madrid came from a goal down to hammer PSV Eindhoven 5-1 and make it five wins out of five in the competition.

Jose Mourinho's side trailed at the break as Ivan Perisic netted from the spot.

Federico Valverde levelled matters shortly after the resumption and Jude Bellingham came off the bench to put Real ahead before Mbappe scored twice, with Vinicius Junior completing the rout.

The comprehensive win moves Real up to the top of the 36-team standings, although reigning European champions PSG could leapfrog them when they welcome Roma to the Parc des Princes.

Elsewhere, RB Leipzig won 3-1 over Lens in Germany and Real Betis triumphed 2-0 at Slovan Bratislava.

There were 1-0 home wins for Bodo/Glimt, Galatasaray and Como, while FC Porto won by the same scoreline away to Feyenoord.
