Yann Sommer says Tresoldi can become a top European striker

Yann Sommer has backed Club Brugge team-mate Nicolo Tresoldi, saying: "Tresoldi can, without any doubt, become a top European striker." He told Gazzetta dello Sport ahead of Wednesday's Champions League trip to Inter Milan.

Sommer, 37, spent years in Inter's goal before moving to Bruges. Tresoldi, 22, captains Germany's under-21s and has 16 goals in 27 games.

He would not be drawn on Tresoldi's senior international choice, saying: "Don't ask me which national team he'll choose; that's up to him." Germany head coach Jürgen Klopp and Italy's Roberto Mancini are keen to win him over, and Tresoldi will not decide until next summer after the European youth championship with the DFB.

Sommer described the right-footed striker as "modest and professional" and added: "He has the qualities to play for a big club."

Tresoldi joined Bruges from second-division Hannover 96 in 2025 for €7.5 million and helped win the Jupiler Pro League in his first season. He has 6 goals in 10 competitive games this term.

He also found the net in the 3-2 opening Champions League defeat against Europa League winners Aston Villa, then converted a penalty to make it 3-0 in a 4-1 win at RAAL La Louviere at the weekend.

Speculation over his future persists, with Borussia Dortmund and Atletico Madrid linked. Tresoldi is under contract in Bruges until 2029.
