‘He Gets Spurs Culture,’ ‘Protect This Man’ — NBA Fans React to Victor Wembanyama’s Latest Admission About San Antonio Fans - AOL

TheTools · 16.11.2026 01:39 · source · permalink

The San Antonio Spurs are off to a great start in the 2026-27 season. After Friday’s 123-85 win against the Golden State Warriors, the Spurs are currently having a record of 11-2.

But it was not just the scoreboard making headlines. What Victor Wembanyama said after the game has drawn widespread attention online, and it has little to do with basketball tactics.

Victor Wembanyama Shared Positive Words Regarding San Antonio

After Friday’s game, the 7’4″ center explained in French that the respect people show players in San Antonio is something he particularly enjoys.

While he sometimes feels uncomfortable being placed on such a pedestal, he appreciates that fans understand the importance of giving players their personal space. The Frenchman even suggested that it is the kind of place where he could imagine spending many years.

“What I like most is the respect they show us. Sometimes I feel a little uncomfortable being placed on such a pedestal. I think this is really the ideal environment because even in other cities, where we’re not the home team, people aren’t as respectful.”

“People can be very persistent or intrusive sometimes, but never here. I’d say it’s the ideal environment to spend 15 or 20 years,” Wembanyama said during the postgame press conference (Translated from French).

It did not take long for the interview to circulate widely online. Clips and translations circulated quickly, and the reaction from NBA fans soon followed.

The first wave of reactions centered on how naturally Wembanyama has settled into his surroundings. Some fans felt his comments showed that he has become attuned to San Antonio’s way of doing things and completely understands what the franchise and its community are about.

“He gets Spurs culture. Best in the @NBA,” one fan said, whereas another one wrote, “The rest of the Western Conference just fell to their knees reading that quote.”

Wembanyama joined the San Antonio Spurs after being selected as the No. 1 overall pick in the 2023 NBA Draft on June 22, 2023.

He is now in his fourth NBA season with the franchise, and his latest comments only strengthened the belief that his future is in Texas. One fan even predicted that he will stay with the Spurs for life.

“Easily a Spurs lifer, wishing him a long career,” the fan said, whereas another one commented, “Protect this man. Also give him $1Bn when he asks.”

One user also highlighted how impressive the Spurs have been despite being a small-market franchise.

“Spurs are Lucky….a small market (in comparison with Los Angeles or New York) became one of the best franchises in NBA history …. Popovich was the mentor of several coaches ( Kerr, Mike Brown, Ime Udoka,…..),” the user wrote.

The sentiment is hard to ignore. In an era when player movement and trade rumors dominate headlines, a generational talent speaking so warmly about his team’s city is rare.
