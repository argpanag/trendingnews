Forget Predator, a New 96-Minute Military Action Thriller Substitute Officially Hits Streaming

TheTools · 10.11.2026 11:26 · source · permalink

After decades of waiting, the Predator franchise has finally found its footing once again thanks to filmmaker Dan Trachtenberg and his recent additions Prey and Predator: Badlands. While we await the next installment (likely a Prey follow-up that brings action movie icon Arnold Schwarzenegger back into the fray), the perfect new military action thriller substitute has officially landed on streaming and unleashes new monstrous terror on an unprepared band of soldiers.

Much like Schwarzenegger and his band of merry men who "ain't got time to bleed," a new elite group of soldiers ventures into the depths of the jungle on a secret mission. What they don't expect is to come face-to-face with a being they cannot understand...and will struggle to kill.

An elite military unit ventures into a Colombian jungle on a mission to eliminate an enemy target. What begins as a tactical operation quickly descends into a frenzied nightmare when a primeval force awakens in the darkness. Five hundred years earlier, a Spanish conquistador expedition was consumed by the same relentless curse. Separated by centuries but connected by the same hostile territory, both groups fight to survive the jungle’s curse.

Directed by Jaime Osorio Marquez (The Sacrifice, The Squad) and co-directed by first-time feature film director Federico Durán, and José Luis Rugeles (Rebellion), The Awakening stars Claudio Cataño (Pacific), Alejandro Buitrago (Narcos), Héctor Sánchez (Mi Bestia), Andrea Olaya (Thousand Fangs), Quique Sanmartín (Pepe Caceres), Jaisson Jeack (Thousand Fangs), Carlos Mariño (The Initiated), Andres Londono (Narcos: Mexico), Salvador Bridges (Adiós al amigo), and Ricardo Leguizamo (Vampire Academy). The Awakening comes courtesy of Well Go USA, with the studio and distributor releasing a new trailer that reveals the explosive terror at hand. Unlike Arnie and his pals, it doesn't look like getting to the chopper will be an option this time.

Adding a historical spin absent in Predator but since introduced more overtly in the recent Predator: Killer of Killers, The Awakening's mysterious beast has been fought before 500 years ago, with modern weaponry seeming to be just as ineffective as that wielded by Spanish conquistadors.

Smashing Predator into the claustrophobic horror classic The Descent to create an action war thriller draped in fantasy and horror that boasts the gripping action sequences of the former and the oppressive atmosphere of the latter. The Awakening was nominated for “Best Feature Film” at the 2026 Guadalajara International Film Festival and is due to hit Digital streaming on Nov. 10, before landing on Blu-ray and DVD exclusively through Amazon on Dec. 15, 2026.
