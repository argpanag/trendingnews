With less than a month on the clock, time runs out as Doomsday approaches. Marvel Studios’ Avengers: Doomsday introduces a villain like no other in the fearsome Doctor Doom, played by returning Marvel icon Robert Downey Jr. The upcoming film assembles the Avengers, X-Men, and Fantastic Four on-screen for the first time in Marvel’s cinematic history. As worlds collide, heroes from three different Earths must band together or risk facing extinction. Watch the final trailer for Avengers: Doomsday and get tickets to see the film, in theaters everywhere on December 18.

When 2019’s Avengers: Endgame faded to black, Steve Rogers (Chris Evans) and Peggy Carter (Hayley Atwell) danced off into their happily ever after – or so they thought. While the former Captain America may have been ready to leave his Super Hero days behind, an existential threat puts him on a collision course with allies old and new in Avengers: Doomsday.

Joe and Anthony Russo return to direct Avengers: Doomsday alongside Evans’ Steve Rogers. The directing duo previously shepherded Steve’s story from Captain America: Winter Soldier through Avengers: Endgame. With the release of Avengers Endgame: Encore, the directors reframed Steve’s storybook ending as the beginning of a new chapter. “We sort of played one beat past where Steve Rogers’ story ended with Peggy Carter,” Anthony Russo explained. “And in that moment, that's where Doomsday is born.”
