Super-sized rats set to invade our homes with warnings Christmas could be ruined

TheTools · 31.10.2026 07:00 · source · permalink

Rodents could be attracted by discarded pumpkins, experts say(Image: Getty Images)

Rats will invade our homes to feast upon discarded Halloween pumpkins, pest control experts have warned. They fear rodents may be attracted if we leave the carved sweet winter squashes at doorways after our October 31 celebrations - particularly as the cold snap begins.

And one expert has advised us to dispose of the round orange vegetables as soon as possible. Kevin Lawrenson, chief executive officer of the National Pest Technicians Association said the hot summer has led to a high number of rodents looking for a place to go for the colder months.

He said: "Halloween is such a great time for families to take their young kid out trick or treating and decorate the house, carve pumpkins, leave them out on display.

Boffins say we could see an invasion of rats(Image: Getty Images/iStockphoto)

"When people carve pumpkins and leave them outside the house for periods on end, they literally are an attraction to rodents, such as rats. You wouldn't go and leave your rubbish outside the front of your house to attract rats to it, would you?"

Mr Lawrenson recalled rats seen in Birmingham last year, including some which were described as "cat-sized", after tonnes of rubbish piled up in the streets amid a strike by bin workers.

He said food and waste left outside homes contributed to the rodent issue in the city. Mr Lawrenson said: "Please don't do the same with pumpkins because the last thing you want on Halloween, or in the days afterwards, is to have rats running around your house.

Households are being urged to properly chuck their pumpkins away(Image: Getty Images)

"(Pumpkins) are quite sweet, strong-smelling items and they will rot, and they will serve to attract any rodent that's foraging for food." He said as it becomes colder, the rodents will look further afield to find warm places to live.

Mr Lawrenson advised anyone with a rodent problem to contact pest control services, adding: "What you don't want to do, I guess in reality, is something that you've done at this time of year leads into ruining both Halloween and also Christmas."
