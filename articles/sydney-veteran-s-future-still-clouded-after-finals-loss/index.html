Sydney veteran's future still clouded after finals loss

TheTools · 18.09.2026 17:33 · source · permalink

Sydney stalwart Dane Rampe may have played his last game in Swans' colours, with coach Dean Cox remaining non-committal about the veteran's AFL future.

Standing in as captain for an injured Callum Mills, Rampe led an understrength Swans outfit in the 12.11 (83) to 10.11 (71) preliminary final loss to Fremantle.

Sydney had slammed five-straight goals across the first half en route to building a game-high 28-point lead, but succumbed to fatigue in the final quarter.

A former co-captain, the 36-year-old Rampe was re-drafted as a rookie by the Swans with fellow defender Jake Lloyd at the end of last year.

While Lloyd has signed on for a 15th season, neither Rampe nor the Swans have made a call on whether he will continue next year.

But the door is ajar, with defender Mills facing the possibility of missing the entire 2027 season with an anterior cruciate ligament tear.

"I haven't spoken to Dane about the possibilities next year," Cox said.

"What he's earned, he's the right to get through this year as much as he possibly can. 

"Like every player, we'll have discussions in the coming weeks about what the future looks like."

Asked if he would like to see Rampe play on, Cox said: I haven't really thought about it. 

"I think he's earned the right to say if he wants to play on or if he doesn't, and then we'll just have those conversations from there."

In his 290th AFL game, Rampe finished with 17 disposals and five intercept possessions as he helped keep Fremantle's star-studded attack at bay early with Lloyd and Tom McCartin.

Despite 31 forward entries to Sydney's 27 at halftime, Dockers cult hero Patrick Voss didn't register a single disposal until the end of the second quarter.

Fellow forward stars Josh Treacy and Jye Amiss had a combined seven touches.

It was the second time Rampe had led the Swans through finals after Mills was ruled out of the 2024 decider with a hamstring injury.

Drafted at age 22 in 2013, Rampe has played in four grand-final defeats (2014, 2016, 2022 and 2024).

"I can't speak highly enough of what Dane has done for our football club ever since he's been here," Cox said.

"Just unfortunate we couldn't get it done for him."
