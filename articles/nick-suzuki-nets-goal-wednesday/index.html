Nick Suzuki nets goal Wednesday

TheTools · 16.10.2026 08:59 · source · permalink

by Nicholas Rodriguez | Canadiens Correspondent | Fri, Oct 16th 2026, 4:59am EDT

Nick Suzuki scored a goal on three shots in Wednesday's 5-4 loss to the Washington Capitals.

Fantasy Impact:

Suzuki scored a power-play goal at 18:21 of the third period, but it ultimately wasn't enough for the Montreal Canadiens to tie the game and force overtime. He has recorded at least one point in five of six games in October, totaling four goals and five assists with a plus-three rating during that span.
