CMA Awards 2026: Full winners list

TheTools · 20.11.2026 02:09 · source · permalink
  • Culture

Ella Langley was the big winner at the 2026 CMA Awards, taking home entertainer of the year, the night's top honor.

The country star, who led this year's nominations with nine nods, also won female vocalist of the year, album of the year, single of the year, song of the year and music video of the year for her hit "Choosin' Texas."

Other winners included Stephen Wilson Jr., who was named new artist of the year, and Brooks & Dunn, who won vocal duo of the year and received the Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award, the highest award in country music.

The 60th annual Country Music Association Awards were held Wednesday, Nov. 18, at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, Tennessee, with country star Lainey Wilson returning as host.

The star-studded ceremony featured performances from artists including Langley, Miranda Lambert, Kacey Musgraves, Taylor Swift, Luke Combs and Wilson.

The 2026 CMA Awards aired live Wednesday at 8 p.m. ET on ABC and Disney+, with the show available to stream the following day on Hulu.
