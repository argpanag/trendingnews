Two Grand Theft Auto 6 DualSense Controllers Were Just Been Revealed

TheTools · 03.09.2026 13:47 · source · permalink

SO COOL!

PlayStation has revealed two Limited Edition Grand Theft Auto 6 DualSense controllers.

One is black with that iconic fluorescent Vice City purple colouring on the bottom and the other is white with that same fluorescent purple bottom faceplate. Both controllers have the game’s logo on the touchpad, purple coloured buttons as well as little palm trees on the controller handles and that iconic OG Grand Theft Auto logo on the back too.

GRAND THEFT AUTO 6 DUALSENSE CONTROLLER PRE-ORDERS

Both controllers will release on November 19th with pre-orders starting at 10:00am AEST on September 10th.
