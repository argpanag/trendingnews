UFC Vegas 122: Allen Frye Jr. vs. RJ Harris Predictions & Odds

TheTools · 10.10.2026 22:19 · source · permalink

Young heavyweight contenders Allen Frye Jr. and RJ Harris are scheduled for an exciting showdown at UFC Vegas 122 on Saturday inside the Meta APEX in Las Vegas.

Because of his perfect pro MMA record, Harris heads into the match as the betting favorite with -350 UFC odds, while Frye Jr., who has a 100% finish rate, will be the betting underdog with +275 odds.

UFC Vegas 112: Allen Frye Jr. vs. RJ Harris: Prediction

A TKO/KO win for Harris is my prediction over Frye Jr., as he’s the better overall striker. Both fighters have incredible knockout power, but Harris sets up his power shots with jabs and combinations, while Frye Jr. needs to take opponents down first to finish them with ground-and-pound.

Harris is more technical and patient, while Frye Jr. forces action by pushing opponents toward the cage and into a clinch battle. With Harris’ solid wrestling credentials, I don’t think Frye Jr. can take him down easily or beat him in the clinch.

Additionally, Harris is quicker with his punches and beats opponents with precision and elite timing. Harris will land shots on Frye Jr. several times, and they’ll add up as the match progresses until he secures the finish.

Allen Frye Jr.’s Recent Fight Results

Frye Jr. was the former Ultimate Battle Grounds champion before the UFC signed him last year. He fought Guilherme Pat in his octagon debut, but suffered a decision defeat at UFC Vegas 112 in December.

RJ Harris’ Recent Fight Results

Harris is an undefeated heavyweight contender who was signed by the UFC earlier this year. He made a successful promotional debut in July, stopping Alvin Hines at UFC OKC and announcing his arrival among the division’s contenders.
