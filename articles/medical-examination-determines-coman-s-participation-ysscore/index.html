Medical examination determines Coman's participation… | YSscores

TheTools · 11.09.2026 14:42 · source · permalink

Al Nassr are awaiting the fitness status of Frenchman Kingsley Coman, who is set to undergo a medical examination today, Friday, ahead of the match against Al Khaleej.

The examination comes before Al Nassr's final training session, as the coaching and medical staff await the results to determine the possibility of French winger Kingsley Coman being included in the plans for the upcoming match.

Al Nassr prepare to face Al Khaleej on Saturday evening in the seventh round of the Saudi Pro League, in a match where the team aims to continue their positive results.

Kingsley Coman missed the last match against Abha, which Al Nassr won 2-1, allowing the team to continue their pursuit of the top positions.

The player's return to Al Nassr's squad depends on the results of the medical examination, as well as an assessment of his physical condition during the last training session and his ability to participate.

Al Nassr's coaching staff are hopeful of regaining Kingsley Coman's services for the match against Al Khaleej, especially given the importance of the upcoming encounter and the team's desire to strengthen their position in the Saudi Pro League table.
