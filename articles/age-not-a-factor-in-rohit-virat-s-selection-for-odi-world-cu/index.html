Age not a factor in Rohit-Virat's selection for ODI World Cup squad: BCCI president

TheTools · 21.10.2026 07:14 · source · permalink

BCCI president Mithun Manhas has said Virat Kohli and Rohit Sharma's selection for the 2027 ODI World Cup will depend on fitness and performance, not age. Manhas also highlighted the importance of senior players in supporting Shubman Gill's leadership as India prepare for the tournament.
