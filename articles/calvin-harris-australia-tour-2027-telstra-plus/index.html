Get exclusive pre-sale access to Calvin Harris

 

Over a two-decade career, Calvin Harris has redefined the landscape of dance and pop music with an astonishing career tally that includes over 35 billion streams, multiple GRAMMY Awards, two Brit Awards - including British Producer of the Year and British Single of the Year - an Ivor Novello Award, and Billboard Music Award wins.

Marking a monumental milestone for Australian fans, this tour will be Calvin Harris's first full dedicated headline run in Australia.

Having headlined the world’s largest festival stages including Coachella, Creamfields, and Glastonbury, alongside maintaining legendary residency runs in Las Vegas and Ibiza, Harris’s return to Australian shores promises to deliver an unparalleled live experience.

As one of the biggest summer touring announcements of the decade, the Calvin Harris – Australia Tour 2027 is primed to be the ultimate party event of 2027, bringing world-class electronic music production to thousands of fans nationwide.
