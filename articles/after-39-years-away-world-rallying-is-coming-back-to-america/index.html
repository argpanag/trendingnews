After 39 Years Away, World Rallying Is Coming Back to America

TheTools · 02.11.2026 03:58 · source · permalink

The World Rally Championship returns to the US in August 2027 for the first time since 1988. Inside the new Rally US, its route and the business case.

Key Takeaways:

  • The World Rally Championship returns to the US from 26 to 29 August 2027, its first American round since the 1988 Olympus Rally.
  • Rally US will be based in Knoxville, start in Nashville and run across more than 15 counties in Tennessee and Kentucky.
  • A 15-round 2027 calendar, new commercial backing and Ford’s planned 2028 works return create fresh openings for brands and host cities.

The last time the World Rally Championship held a round in the United States, it was 1988, at the Olympus Rally in Washington state, which counted towards the championship from 1986 to 1988. Now, after almost four decades away, the series is coming back. Rally US will run from 26 to 29 August 2027, based in Knoxville, Tennessee, and on 28 October organisers revealed a route that stretches across more than 15 counties in Tennessee and Kentucky.

It’s a sporting story, but it’s also a business one. The FIA has called the United States one of the series’ most important growth opportunities, and host cities are planning a festival around the event. Here’s what’s planned, why it matters and what it could mean for brands, host cities and the sport itself.

Why the World Rally Championship Is Returning to America

The FIA confirmed the US round on 8 October as part of an expanded 2027 calendar. According to Reuters’ report, the event will use stages trialled at a candidate rally held in June 2026. FIA president Mohammed Ben Sulayem described the US as “one of the most important growth opportunities” for the championship.

There is a recent precedent in motorsport. Formula 1 now holds three US Grands Prix a year, in Miami, Austin and Las Vegas, a growth story we covered in our look at Formula 1’s record revenue.

A rally car throws up dust on a gravel stage, the surface Rally US will use across Tennessee and Kentucky. Photo by David Barros on Unsplash

Inside Rally US: Knoxville, Nashville and the Appalachians

The route was revealed in late October. Stages will run from the Appalachian Mountains to the Cumberland Plateau, mixing fast gravel roads, technical forest sections and dramatic changes in elevation. Here’s how the event is shaping up:

  • Headquarters: Knoxville, with a WRC Arena at World’s Fair Park and the Knoxville Convention Center housing team bases and a fan festival.
  • Ceremonial start: Lower Broadway in Nashville.
  • Footprint: More than 15 counties across East Tennessee and Eastern Kentucky.
  • Fan experience: Live music, driver appearances and community celebrations, with priority tickets, hospitality packages and volunteer roles on offer.

“Tennessee is in the hospitality and entertainment business, and nobody does it better.” Matt Crews, President and CEO, Rally US

Organisers are presenting Rally US as a destination event, pairing a ceremonial start in Nashville with mountain stages and a fan festival in Knoxville, as Crews’ comment suggests. Priority tickets, premium hospitality and volunteer sign-ups are handled through the official Rally US website.

A Bigger, More Global World Rally Championship Calendar

The US return is part of a wider shake-up. The 2027 calendar grows to 15 rounds, up from 13 in 2026, making it the largest since 2008. Scotland joins for the first WRC visit to the UK since 2019, with an Aberdeen-based event agreed under a three-year deal, according to Autosport, and Italy’s round moves from Sardinia to Rome. Portugal drops out, at least for now.

Behind the calendar sits new commercial muscle. In July the FIA announced a long-term commercial rights agreement with Cosmobilis and Park Square Capital, and WRC Promoter chief executive Éric Boullier has said the aim is “a stronger, more accessible and truly global championship.” Ford, meanwhile, plans to return as a full works team in 2028.

Other details of the calendar show how the championship is reshaping itself. Kenya’s Safari Rally keeps its March slot after a deal was reached, with government funding being replaced by private-sector financing, according to Autosport. Italy’s round moves from October to July as it switches from Sardinian gravel to asphalt around Rome. And Portugal, absent in 2027, could return in 2028. WRC Promoter’s chief operating officer, Julien Vial, joined Boullier and Ben Sulayem in welcoming the expanded schedule. Elsewhere, the 2027 World Rally Championship keeps established fixtures such as Rally Sweden in February, Rally Japan in June, Rally Estonia and Rally Finland in late July, and includes a late-season South American double of Rally del Paraguay in October and Rally Chile Biobío in November before the Saudi Arabian finale.

A Short History of the World Rally Championship in the US

America’s previous spell on the calendar was brief. The Olympus Rally, held in Washington state, counted towards the world championship from 1986 to 1988, an era of turbocharged Group B machines giving way to the more production-based Group A cars.

Getting back has taken time. A candidate event held in June 2026 tested the stages before the FIA confirmed the round in October, and organisers have planned a fan festival at Knoxville’s WRC Arena around the competition.

What spectators can expect

Unlike a circuit race, a rally unfolds over several days on closed roads. Cars start one at a time and race against the clock, so fans tend to choose a vantage point on a stage, watch every crew pass, then move on to another location or head back to the service park. Rally US plans to pair that with a city-centre fan zone in Knoxville and a showpiece start in Nashville, giving casual visitors an easy way in.

The Business Case for Tennessee and Kentucky

Why would two states want a rally? Host regions typically hope for visitors, hotel stays and restaurant bookings, and rally stages run through many communities rather than a single stadium. We found no published economic impact estimate for Rally US, so it’s too early to put a number on it. What is clear from the organisers’ plans is that Knoxville and Nashville are presenting it as an entertainment event as much as a race, from the WRC Arena fan festival to the ceremonial start on Lower Broadway.

Opportunities for brands

  • Car makers get a world championship stage in the US to show performance and durability.
  • Outdoor, energy drink and lifestyle brands can explore sponsorship and activation around a four-day event, much as brands have in F1, where sponsorship passed $3bn.
  • Hospitality and travel firms can build packages around the event; organisers are already selling premium hospitality.
  • Media platforms gain a new live-sport property in the US market.

Can the World Rally Championship Win Over America?

There are real challenges. Rallying is harder to watch in person than circuit racing: fans pick a spot in the woods and see each car for a few seconds. Television and streaming coverage will be crucial, and so will storytelling around the drivers and teams that Americans can get behind. One US round a year is a start, not a strategy, and the series will need to build year-round interest in between.

Still, the ingredients are promising. A destination city with a music-and-hospitality identity, a stunning natural setting, fresh commercial backing and a returning American manufacturer. If Rally US delivers in August 2027, the World Rally Championship will have re-established itself in a market it has been absent from for almost 40 years, with an opening for brands that get involved early.
