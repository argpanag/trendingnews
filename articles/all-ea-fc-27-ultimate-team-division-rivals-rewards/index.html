All EA FC 27 Ultimate Team Division Rivals Rewards

TheTools · 17.09.2026 15:23 · source · permalink

Rewards are the name of the game in EA FC 27 Ultimate Team, as every step towards bettering your team needs to be taken. Thankfully, there are tons of game modes to sink your teeth into, and one of the most popular is Division Rivals.

This game mode pits you against other players online as you try to string wins together to climb the ranks and earn a higher tier of rewards. Whether you earn 15 or 30 points a week determines your reward tier, and more often than not, it means more packs and coins to add to your Club.

It can be quite the grind, but it serves as a core pillar of the experience. With that in mind, here is everything on offer for Division Rivals in EA FC 27 Ultimate Team, including both tiers for each rank.

How To Earn Points In Division Rivals

You’ll earn points in Division Rivals by playing matches in the game mode, with a win earning you three points, and a draw offering one.

There are two reward tiers for each rank in Division Rivals, and you’ll need to earn them every week if you want to keep claiming the various packs and Coins on offer.

The standard tier requires 15 points a week, whilst the premium tier needs double that at 30. Of course, you can keep playing Rivals after you’ve earned the necessary points, as you’ll likely want to grind away and climb the ranks so you can earn better rewards in the weeks to come.

All EA FC 27 Ultimate Team Division Rivals Rewards

There are 11 ranks to climb through in Division Rivals for EA FC 27 Ultimate Team, with the Elite Division offering the best rewards.

Of course, as you climb through the ranks, it’ll become more and more difficult to reach the next Division. With longer win streaks needed and fewer checkpoints to save your progress, it’ll take some decent skill to earn the best rewards on offer.
