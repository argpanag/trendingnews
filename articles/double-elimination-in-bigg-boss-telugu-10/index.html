Double Elimination in Bigg Boss Telugu 10

TheTools · 14.11.2026 03:12 · source · permalink

Bigg Boss Telugu is gearing up for a double elimination during its tenth week. Rumors are doing the rounds that the show's makers are planning a double eviction. If you recall, the makers skipped elimination last week due to the Diwali festival.

Anchor Varshini, Mukesh, Thirgun, Naresh, Ram Prasad, Neethu, and Swetha, among others, have been nominated for this week's elimination. Naresh and Rama Krishna are likely to get eliminated, as they are in the bottom positions with fewer votes.

Bigg Boss Telugu is hosted by Nagarjuna and airs on Star Maa.

The show's grand finale is likely to be held in the third week of December.
