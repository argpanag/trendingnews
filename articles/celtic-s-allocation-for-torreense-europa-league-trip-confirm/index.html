Celtic's allocation for Torreense Europa League trip confirmed

TheTools · 18.11.2026 14:27 · source · permalink

Martin Crawford

Celtic have been handed an allocation of 3,600 tickets for their trip to face Torreense.

The Portuguese second-tier side are one of the Hoops’ eight League Phase opponents in the competition, and the match is set to take place on November 26th.

Because Torreense’s regular stadium is too small for UEFA requirements, the game is being played at the Estadio Municipal de Leiria. The ground sits roughly 47 to 50 miles north of their native Torres Vedras.

The club have confirmed that tickets are priced at £26 for blocks 1A-1e and at £17.50 for block 4A, plus a £1.60 booking fee. It will be the Hoops’ second trip to Portugal after their defeat to Benfica in Lisbon back in October.

What are the alternative ideas?

Martin O’Neill’s men are off the mark in the League Phase after their 3-2 win over Besiktas earlier this month. With three points, they are still seven places away from the qualification spots.

O’Neill said after said win: “I think this will give us a big boost of confidence. We’ve got a tough game ahead on Sunday.

“Then there’s the international break. But tonight, to win the European game tonight is great. The sides that have beaten us, I think Ferencvaros have got 12 points on the board. Benfica the same. The games are tough.

“Tonight was really tough. But we came through that test, which is a big, big boost for us I just thought it was a terrific win. In every aspect, I thought we played some really lovely stuff as well, too, and they are a very fine side.”

He continued on the team’s performance: “The character shone through in the side. No question about that. Big, big time. Some of these lads who have been at the football club for some time, who have been big winners at the football club, I thought they came to the fore.

“Also, some of the lads who joined us had big performances. Listen, it’s hard to single people out. Tierney was fantastic for us. Back four did really well. Colby coming on to a game as well. He’s got a massive heart.

“And wee Mika Baur was magnificent for us in the middle of the pitch. But you have to find the net. And we did that. Callum comes on, first touch, puts the ball in the net. That’s really difficult. He definitely can score a goal; he plays a little pass as well, too, for one of the other goals. And Duran, who’s been fabulous for us the whole season regardless of where he plays.”
