UFC 331 tonight: key fights and estimated fighter purses

TheTools · 19.09.2026 22:46 · source · permalink

Photo: MMA Fighting

The main event of the evening will be a rematch between UFC flyweight champion Joshua Van and former titleholder Alexander Pantoja.

The first bout took place in December 2025. The fight ended after just 26 seconds following an elbow injury to Pantogea. Van subsequently won the championship belt and then successfully defended his title against Tatsuro Taira. Now the American fighter will face Pantogea again, putting his championship belt on the line.

Arman Tsarukyan will feature in the co-main event. This will be his first fight of 2026. The Russian-Armenian fighter will face Brazilian Mauricio Ruffi. The bout will be contested in the lightweight division and is scheduled for five rounds. The winner could gain a strong case for their next title fight.

Tsarukyan believes the fight won’t go the full distance. He is confident he will defeat the Brazilian before the final bell.

How much will the fighters earn?

The fighters’ purses are of particular interest. The UFC does not disclose exact figures in advance, so the published figures are merely estimates.

According to Championat, Tsarukyan could earn around $450,000–$600,000 for the fight against Ruffi, including base pay. Arman himself has previously stated that this bout will bring him the largest pay-out of his career.

Ruffi’s earnings are estimated at around $200,000. For the Brazilian, this could be his biggest pay-out in the UFC. That said, he has already received around $200,000 in bonuses alone for previous fights.

There are other estimates as well. For example, prior to the event, some sources predicted that Tsarukyan would receive between $300,000 and $500,000, whilst Ruffi would receive between $100,000 and $250,000. This demonstrates once again that the final figures will only be known after the fight and the publication of reliable financial data.

It should be noted that UFC 331 will take place on the evening of 19 September local time. In the CIS countries, the main bouts will begin in the early hours of 20 September.
