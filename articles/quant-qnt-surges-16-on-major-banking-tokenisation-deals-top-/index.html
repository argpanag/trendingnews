Quant (QNT) Surges 16% on Major Banking Tokenisation Deals | Top Stories | CoinMarketCap

TheTools · 27.09.2026 21:54 · source · permalink

Quant (QNT) has seen a significant ~16 percentage point increase in the last day, driven by major banking tokenisation deals and a high-profile technical breakout in a strong real-world asset (RWA) narrative environment.

The Clearing House (TCH), a key US bank-owned payments utility, has chosen Quant’s technology for its new U.S. On-Chain Money Initiative. This initiative aims to allow banks to clear tokenised deposit transactions over existing TCH rails such as RTP and CHIPS, with a rollout targeted for the first half of 2027. Quant had already facilitated tokenised deposit trials with British banks alongside EY and Linklaters, so the TCH selection is seen as an escalation from pilots into large-scale US infrastructure work. Multiple analysts on X attribute the sudden move to this deal, describing it as a “major deal with The Clearing House for tokenized bank money in the U.S.” and noting that QNT “jumped from $70 to $90+” on the news, pushing toward the psychological 100 dollar level as a short-term target. X summary of the TCH deal.

The market is repricing QNT as the token most directly linked to a real-world tokenised deposits system for large US banks, which is a significant narrative and fundamentals upgrade compared with a typical altcoin.

At almost the same time, a UK initiative went from theory to live customer transactions using Quant’s stack. Major UK banks completed the first live customer payments using tokenized sterling deposits over shared infrastructure developed by Quant. The participants include Barclays, HSBC UK, Lloyds Banking Group, Monzo, Nationwide, NatWest, and Santander. The pilot represents tokenised versions of existing commercial bank deposits, preserving current legal protections while adding programmability and faster settlement. EY manages the project, and Linklaters wrote the legal rulebooks, but the underlying orchestration platform for interoperability and tokenised payments is Quant. The article emphasises that this is not just a demo. Real customers have already completed real sterling payments using tokenised deposits over Quant’s infrastructure. Report on UK banks' tokenised sterling pilot.

Traders see two of the most conservative parts of global finance, UK high street banks and US clearing infrastructure, both choosing Quant’s stack for tokenised deposits at roughly the same time. That is exactly the kind of convergence that can justify a rapid repricing.

These banking headlines landed in a backdrop that made the move easier to accelerate. Real world assets (RWA) and tokenisation are already a hot narrative. QNT has been under long-term compression. Multiple traders on X point out that QNT is testing a multi-year descending resistance trendline, describing a “macro breakout setup” and calling for significantly higher levels if weekly closes reclaim key zones such as 96 dollars and 115 dollars. At the time of writing, Quant (QNT) is trading around 110.68 dollars, up about 16.35% over 24 hours with roughly 55.23 million dollars in daily volume, and recent coverage repeatedly lists QNT as the top gainer in the top 100 coins over the past week, with a 61.8% weekly rise tied to these banking announcements. This confirms that the move is not just intraday noise but a sustained rerating over several sessions. Social chatter reflects aggressive trading sentiment. Numerous posts share trade setups around the 90 to 100 dollar region, present levered long targets above 100 dollars, and talk about “five years of compression” and “monthly momentum turning” with “tokens leaving exchanges while price rises,” which fuels FOMO and speculative buying on top of the fundamental news.

The banking news did not hit a flat technical backdrop. It arrived exactly as QNT was breaking a long-running resistance and as traders were hungry for RWA-themed plays while the broader crypto market trends higher. That combination makes large percentage moves much more likely.

There are clear, named catalysts behind QNT’s move in the last 24 to 25 hours. Fundamentally, Quant’s technology has just been publicly tied to The Clearing House’s U.S. On-Chain Money Initiative for tokenised deposits and to a live tokenised sterling pilot with seven major UK banks. Narratively, this places QNT at the center of the real-world asset and tokenised bank money story, which is one of the strongest themes of the current market cycle. Technically and sentiment-wise, those announcements coincided with a breakout from long-term resistance and aggressive trader positioning, so the news translated into a rapid 15 to 20 percent daily repricing rather than a slow grind. Confidence is high, because multiple independent news outlets and widely cited X posts directly link QNT’s recent price surge to the U.S. and UK banking tokenisation announcements and to a long-term technical breakout.
