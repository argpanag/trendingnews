Prime Video brings Sony LIV into its add-on subscription offering

TheTools · 21.10.2026 19:14 · source · permalink

The service also offers live India cricket, UEFA Champions League, French Open, Commonwealth Games and UFC, plus India’s New Zealand tour from October 22.

Prime Video has added Sony LIV as an add-on subscription in India, allowing customers to access the streaming service's movies, shows and live sports through the Prime Video platform.

Sony LIV is available on Prime Video across mobile, web, Fire TV, smart TVs and other supported devices. Customers can choose between monthly and annual plans priced at Rs 399 and Rs 1,499, respectively.

The Sony LIV offering includes more than 450 films, 300 shows, live sports and on-demand content across multiple languages. Its catalogue includes originals such as the Scam franchise and Gullak, along with films such as Sitaare Zameen Par and television shows including Kaun Banega Crorepati, Indian Idol, Taarak Mehta Ka Ooltah Chashmah and Pushpa Impossible.

The service also offers live coverage of bilateral India cricket, UEFA Champions League, French Open, Commonwealth Games and UFC. India's tour of New Zealand, comprising Tests, ODIs and T20Is, is also available on the platform from October 22.

The content spans Hindi, English, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam and Gujarati. New episodes of Sony LIV shows will also be available daily, alongside reality and entertainment titles including KBC Season 18, Family Full House With Rohit Sharma and Indian Idol Season 17.

Gaurav Bhasin, head- AVOD Business and Marketplace, Prime Video India, said: "Our ambition has always been to make Prime Video the ultimate entertainment destination for our customers. With Sony LIV now available as an add-on subscription, we take a significant step forward in that journey.

Along with our extensive library, customers now have the daily viewing cadence of television and a near-continuous calendar of live sports: from bilateral India cricket to the UEFA Champions League — all on one service. This deepens the breadth and choice of multilingual entertainment we offer, and we are excited about what it means for the millions of entertainment and sports fans across India."

Rajesh Kaul, chief revenue officer and business head - Sports and International, SPNI, said: “At Sony LIV, we’re always looking for new ways to make our content available to audiences wherever they choose to watch it. Our partnership with Prime Video is an important step in expanding that reach in India. It gives viewers another convenient way to discover and access Sony LIV's diverse content offering. From some of India’s most popular television properties, such as Kaun Banega Crorepati and Indian Idol, to the upcoming launch of Sony VIZHA in Tamil and the new season of Kon Honaar Crorepati in Marathi, we are further strengthening that offering.

Live sports and our slate of acclaimed Originals remain key to our proposition. Through this association, we look forward to taking the breadth of Sony LIV's entertainment and sports offering to more viewers across India and the world.” 

As part of Prime Video's add-on subscription model, users can manage subscriptions and billing through the Prime Video platform. They can also browse content, search across subscriptions, use a consolidated watchlist and download titles for offline viewing.

Sony LIV joins more than 30 OTT services available through Prime Video's add-on subscription offering.
