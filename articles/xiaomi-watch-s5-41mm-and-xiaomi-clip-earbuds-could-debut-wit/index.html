Xiaomi Watch S5 41mm and Xiaomi Clip Earbuds Could Debut with Xiaomi 18 Pro Series Globally ~ My Mobile India

TheTools · 03.11.2026 13:07 · source · permalink

Highlights

  • Xiaomi Watch S5 41mm and Xiaomi Clip Earbuds could make a global debut with Xiaomi 18 Pro series.
  • The smartwatch features a 1.32-inch 3000-nit AMOLED screen and weighs just 36 grams.
  • Retail listings show the Watch S5 41mm at around €240 and the Clip Earbuds at €137.90.

Xiaomi is expanding its wearable portfolio for the international markets with the Xiaomi Watch S5 41mm and Xiaomi Clip Earbuds, along with its flagship Xiaomi 18 Pro series. Recent retail leaks and regulatory filings show that both devices will soon be hitting international markets after their recent launch in China, with early store listings in Europe hinting at costs of around €240 for the smartwatch and €137.90 for the clip-style open-ear earphones.

Xiaomi Watch S5 41mm: What to Expect

S5 $1mm has an 1.32-inch AMOLED display

The Xiaomi S5 41mm offers a small elegant design for those who want a slim fit without sacrificing on performance. It has a 9.9mm thin stainless steel shell and a 1.32-inch AMOLED display with 3,000 nits of brightness. There’s a 590 mAh battery on board as well. It’s an attractive device with robust health sensors and dual-band GPS navigation.

Xiaomi Clip Earbuds: Features and Design

Xiaomi Clip Earbuds weigh 5.5 grams each bud (image: @adam_kurfurst)

The Xiaomi Clip Earbuds feature an ergonomic clip-on design made for all-day comfort and an active lifestyle. They weigh just 5.5 grams per earbud, and come with IP57 dust and water protection, 11mm dynamic drivers, a 3-mic clear call setup, and up to 38 total hours of battery life.

Xiaomi 18 Pro Series Global Launch: What We Know

Xiaomi has confirmed the Xiaomi 18 Pro and Xiaomi 18 Pro Max will be launched globally in November 2026. The worldwide rollout will begin in Europe, the UK and Southeast Asia after the first launch in China, with India’s launch likely between late November and December. Both variants include a premium triple camera setup with 200MP sensors co-developed with Leica, an enhanced secondary AMOLED back display for previews and alerts, and Qualcomm’s Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 CPU.
