The death has occurred of

Larry Mc Hale, 23rd September 2026, Turlough, Castlebar and late of St. Bridgid’s Cresent, Castlebar and Caherlistrane, Co.Galway, peacefully at his home.

 

Predeceased by his parents Larry and Ellen and twin sister Geraldine.

 

Sadly missed by his heartbroken wife Teresa, daughter Gráinne and her partner Jorge, son Eoin and his partner Muireann, brother Anthony, sisters Mary and Columba, adored grandson Jack, brothers-in-law, sisters-in-law, nephews, nieces, relatives, neighbours and friends.

 

May he rest in peace.

 

Larry will repose in Coady’s Funeral home, Castlebar, F23 XY63, on Sunday (27th September) from 5.30pm with removal at 7pm to the Church of the Holy Rosary, Castlebar.

House strictly private.

 

Mass can be viewed live on https://www.churchtv.ie/castlebar/
