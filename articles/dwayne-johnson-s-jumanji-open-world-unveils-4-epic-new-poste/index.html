Sony Pictures has unveiled four new character posters for Jumanji: Open World. The posters feature Dwayne Johnson, Jack Black, Kevin Hart, and Karen Gillan as their respective video game avatars.

The studio shared the posters on its X handle. The artwork included Dr. Xander "Smolder" Bravestone, Professor Sheldon "Shelly" Oberon, Franklin "Mouse" Finbar, and Ruby Roundhouse.

The posters playfully present the characters' strength as "Coming to our world this Christmas" and their weakness as having to wait until the holiday. The promotional images offer another look at the four returning characters ahead of their latest adventure.

What to know about Dwayne Johnson & Jack Black's role in Jumanji: Open World

Johnson reprises his role as Dr. Xander "Smolder" Bravestone, the group's muscle-bound adventurer. Black returns as Professor Sheldon "Shelly" Oberon, the group's cartographer. Hart plays Franklin "Mouse" Finbar, while Gillan returns as Ruby Roundhouse, the martial arts expert.

Jumanji: Open World follows the events of 2019's Jumanji: The Next Level, the sequel to 2017's Jumanji: Welcome to the Jungle. In the upcoming film, Jumanji breaks free of its video game console and unleashes chaos in the real world. The shift brings the game's dangers into the real world, forcing the characters to face an entirely new challenge.

The ensemble also includes Alex Wolff, Morgan Turner, Ser'Darius Blain, Madison Iseman, Nick Jonas, and Danny DeVito.Their return further connects the film to its predecessors.

Jake Kasdan directs the film and co-wrote the screenplay with Jeff Pinkner and Scott Rosenberg. Kasdan also produces the film alongside Matt Tolmach, Johnson, Dany Garcia, and Hiram Garcia. The executive producers are Barry Waldman, Pinkner, Rosenberg, Melvin Mar, Sky Salem Robinson, Chris Van Allsburg, William Teitler, Ted Field, and Mike Weber.

Jumanji: Open World will hit theaters on December 25, 2026. The upcoming film will serve as the final installment in the modern Jumanji trilogy.
