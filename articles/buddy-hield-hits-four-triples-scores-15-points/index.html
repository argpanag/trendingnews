    Buddy Hield produced 15 points (4-of-7 FGs, 3-of-3 FTs), three rebounds, one assist, two steals and four three-pointers in Sunday’s exhibition against the Nuggets.

    Between Golden State and Atlanta, Hield wasn’t a fantasy basketball factor last season. Could that change in Chicago? He’s played consistent rotation minutes off the bench this preseason, and the veteran guard shot 4-of-5 from beyond the arc. While Hield had a good showing, there’s no need to consider him in drafts. If he is consistently productive during the season, you can pick him up off the waiver wire.
