CORPUS CHRISTI, Texas — It's week 9 of the Friday Night Fever high school football season on KRIS 6! We have district games this week. Below you'll find a list of Coastal Bend highlights and scores.

H.M. King defeated Robstown 41-7 on the road on our Game Night South Texas.

Peyton Hooks scored the first touchdown from 13 yards out on a direct snap. Damarius Montgomery was a force to be reckoned with, finishing with three touchdowns. Julio Pineda-Garcia nabbed an interception in the waning moments.

It all added up to a win that will give King a much better opportunity to make the postseason.

Sinton lost its starting QB, Quincy Bess, but they fought through the adversity to win 22-7 over Orange Grove.

Sophomore Luke Villarreall stepped in at quarterback, senior running back Marcos Zapata was outstanding, and the Pirates defense pitched a shutout for three quarters. Sinton stays undefeated and in first place in UIL 3A-DI District 15.

The Ray Texans (8-0, 4-0) have not lost a game all 2026 season, after taking down Mercedes 27-13 in a UIL 5A-DII District 15 matchup. The last time Ray even won 8 games in a season was in 2012. Next week they take a road trip to Donna before hosting another Coastal Bend undefeated team, Gregory-Portland week 11.

The Calallen Wildcats (7-1, 4-0) are paving the way in UIL 4A-DI District 15 after taking down Beeville 49-7. Sophomore running back Jimmy Tipton provided a lot of power. The Wildcats are tied for first in district with Miller, who they'll face week 11.

The Miller Buccaneers (6-2, 4-0) are tied for first in UIL 4A-DI District 15 with Calallen as the Bucs continue to stay undefeated in district play. Miller overpowered King 49-6. They'll face Calallen in week 11.

The Mathis Pirates (3-5, 2-1) are now tied for second with Orange Grove in UIL 3A-DI District 15 after holding off Aransas Pass 60-46. Mathis takes a road trip to Goliad next week before hosting Orange Grove week 11 to end the regular season. Mathis bettered their odds of making playoffs.
