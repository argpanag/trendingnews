Katie Price ‘ditched by squad’ after claiming surgery made her look like hamster

TheTools · 08.09.2026 20:57 · source · permalink

Katie Price was apparently dropped by her ‘Glam Squad’ before the NTAs tonight.

The reality star explained that her glam squad passed on helping her get ready for the red carpet after they had a falling out.

So what was the reason for the row?

Well, apparently it was Katie’s high standards when it comes to fashion.

‘So originally I did have a glam squad, but they’ve passed on doing my glam for the NTAs, because I wouldn’t wear the outfits they wanted me to,’ she told The Sun.

‘“They wanted to do something different. But the outfit I’ve chosen myself is still different, because I don’t like to follow what everyone else follows.’

Katie didn’t let being abandoned by the squad get her down, though, explaining they did her a favour as her wet-hair look worked ‘perfectly’ with the outfit she chose.

To be honest, though, Katie probably had other things to worry about as she’s already described herself as ‘looking like a hamster’ before the NTAs.

The former glamour model joked on Instagram that she’d just been to Brussels, where she’d received facial filler that left her looking like a hamster.

According to Katie, she took the trip to fix one of her boob implants when she decided that if she was in for a penny, she was in for a pound.

‘I went to Brussels because one of my boobs was still slightly bigger than the other,’ she said.

‘So, they put a new implant in, and while they were there, I said, “Oh, go on, just put a bit more in both of them”.

‘At the same time, where my face was hollow here, as you get older and lose weight, he put filler in for me, obviously while I’m asleep, and he does it under the muscle, right into the bone.’

Unfortunately, when she woke up on Tuesday morning, some of the swelling had gone; she thought it made her look ‘like a hamster and it’s sore’.

Earlier this month, Katie left her husband Lee Andrews. Apparently she told Lee over WhatsApp, telling him: ‘I have been played and you are not who you say you are. Why have you lied to me?’

According to The Sun, she then told him that the marriage is done and that she won’t be seeing him again.
