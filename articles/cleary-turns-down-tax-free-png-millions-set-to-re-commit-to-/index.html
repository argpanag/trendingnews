Cleary turns down tax-free PNG millions, set to re-commit to Panthers

TheTools · 04.11.2026 10:20 · source · permalink
  • Sport
  • NRL
  • NRL

November 4, 2026 - 10.20am

Nathan Cleary is set to re-commit to Penrith after turning down the tax-free millions dangled in front of him by the PNG Chiefs.

Nine’s Danny Weidler revealed on Wednesday evening that Cleary would sign a two-year contract extension with Penrith, tying the champion halfback to the Panthers until the end of 2029.

Nathan Cleary boots a field goal for Penrith. Getty Images

Weidler expects the contract to include a two-year player option.

He said Cleary, who became a free agent on Sunday, informed the Chiefs on Wednesday evening of his intention to remain at the Panthers.

A host of premiership Panthers became free agents on Sunday, including Isaah Yeo, Moses Leota, Liam Martin and Cleary.

Having failed to land their leading halfback target, the Chiefs will likely turn their attention to Sam Walker.

The Queensland State of Origin halfback is up for grabs from 2028 after billionaire Roosters chairman Nick Politis sensationally pulled a mega offer off the table last week.

Walker may even become available to rival clubs in 2027, so ugly is the situation at the Bondi club, but the Chiefs don’t join the competition until 2028.

Promising halfback Zane Harrison last week re-signed with the Titans until the end of 2029.
