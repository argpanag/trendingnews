iPhone 18 Pro: Use Texture Controls in Photographic Styles

TheTools · 18.09.2026 09:10 · source · permalink

With Apple's latest iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max models, you can adjust not only specific colors in certain parts of your photos, but also their texture and grain, thanks to the latest-generation Photographic Styles 3. Here's how to use them.


Photographic Styles are tone presets that adjust the color and warmth of a photo as it is captured, rather than as a filter applied afterward, while preserving skin tones. The ‌new iPhone 18 Pro‌ models feature a redesigned computational imaging pipeline for the 48-megapixel Fusion Main camera that Apple says produces sharper, more detailed shots. And the new texture and grain controls in Photographic Styles 3 build on that pipeline, allowing you to further dial in your desired appearance for more creative control over your photos.

When you first open the Camera app on your ‌iPhone 18 Pro, you'll be asked if you want to choose a default Photographic Style that changes the overall mood before you take a photo. You can go ahead with this, or you can dismiss the prompt and add Photographic Styles as you see fit for each photo.

Here's how to use the new controls in Photographic Styles on iPhone 18 Pro.

  1. In the Camera app, tap the button with six dots at the bottom-right of the interface.
  2. Select Styles from the control pane.

  3. Swipe to a style that you want to use, then tap the Customize button below it.
  4. Use the square palette to adjust tone and color simultaneously, or use the + and - dial below it to adjust the palette separately.
  5. When you're done, tap Texture.
  6. Select a texture from the options: Soft Skin, Glow, or Film. Drag the slider below to adjust the intensity of each texture, or choose Standard to apply no texture. Tap Grain to add film grain to any texture.
  7. When you're happy with the applied effect, take your shot.

Note that the texture and grain effects are applied after you take the photo.

Remove or Reset a Photographic Style

If you set a default Photographic Style that you no longer want to use, you can remove the effect by following these steps:

  1. Open the Settings app on your ‌iPhone.
  2. Tap Camera.
  3. Tap Photographic Styles.
  4. Select Customize Texture, Use Standard, or choose a different style.
