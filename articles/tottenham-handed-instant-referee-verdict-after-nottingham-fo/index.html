Tottenham handed instant referee verdict after Nottingham Forest VAR decision

TheTools · 05.09.2026 15:37 · source · permalink

Nottingham Forest's opening goal against Tottenham was disallowed following a VAR review with Neco Williams handling a loose ball over the line.

Spurs had failed to clear Morgan Gibbs-White's goal-bound effort that was parried away by Antonin Kinsky with Williams appearing to head home the rebound. However, replays clearly showed that the Wales international had inadvertently handled.

Speaking on Sky Sports, as the incident was being reviewed, former Premier League referee Mike Dean stated: "It looks like it has hit his hand."

Craig Pawson, the referee for the game, later announced: “After review, the ball touches the hand of Nottingham Forest No.3 before the goal is scored. The final decision is a direct free-kick for Spurs”.

Prior to Gibbs-White's chance, there had been just one shot on target in a particularly lacklustre game with Spurs failing to trouble Matz Sels once.

Omar Marmoush had several efforts on goal but all of those were off target. Roberto De Zerbi's side did hit double-figures for corners with Micky van de Ven heading just wide from one of those.

Dominic Solanke replaced Marmoush and had a late header from a Mohammed Kudus cross that went just over. Having conceded five goals in the Premier League this season already, keeping a clean sheet is a step in the right direction but the Lilywhites have yet to score.

That is despite making considerable improvements to their attack with De Zerbi also given the funds to improve the club's midfield and defensive ranks.

Sandro Tonali, Savio, Jan Paul van Hecke and Marmoush were the new arrivals who started at the City Ground with Mateus Fernandes coming off the bench.

Mykhailo Mudryk, who joined on loan from Chelsea late in the window, also made a cameo appearance with it being his first competitive minutes in nearly two years.
