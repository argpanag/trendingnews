Portugal vs Denmark Prediction, 02:45 on November 15th: Beginning a New Journey

TheTools · 14.11.2026 21:17 · source · permalink

TPO - Portugal vs Denmark Football Prediction, UEFA Nations League - Team news, expected lineup, form, head-to-head history. Tonight, the Portuguese national team will begin a new chapter in their history, embarking on an unfamiliar journey where they officially no longer have their star player Cristiano Ronaldo.

Match preview: Portugal vs Denmark

As is well known, after a series of controversies with coach Jorge Jesus, Ronaldo officially retired from the national team, ending 23 years of service. Today marks the first match for the Seleção where Ronaldo is no longer part of the plans.

Portugal continues to demonstrate its status as a top contender for the championship. Despite the departure of Ronaldo, coach Jorge Jesus still possesses a squad of top players in their prime, competing for leading clubs across the continent.

The midfield is Portugal's strongest point, with the excellent partnership between PSG duo Vitinha and Joao Neves, who are responsible for controlling the tempo and recovering the ball, thereby completely freeing up creative space for playmaker Bruno Fernandes up front.

Up front, Portugal promises to be more flexible because, no longer needing to concentrate the ball on a single target as before, players like Joao Felix, Goncalo Ramos, and Pedro Neto are expected to become much more unpredictable and versatile.

On the other side of the pitch, Denmark entered this match as the underdog, relying on a disciplined and physically strong team play. After the unfortunate setback of failing to advance beyond the 2026 World Cup play-offs, Danish football is striving to regain the fans' trust through the Nations League.

Despite lacking the flashy attacking stars of their opponents, the "Danish Dynamite" remain confident thanks to their tenacity and experienced central midfield. In midfield, the central midfield pairing of Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg and Morten Hjulmand provides strong one-on-one tackling ability, ready to disrupt the rhythm of the home team's technically gifted midfielders. Meanwhile, Denmark's greatest hope in attack rests on the shoulders of striker Rasmus Hojlund, who has proven his goal-scoring prowess for the national team with 16 goals in 39 appearances.

Form and head-to-head history of Portugal vs Denmark

Looking at their recent performances, Portugal is showing remarkable consistency in the tournament where they won the title in 2025. In their last five matches across all competitions, coach Jorge Jesus's team has won four and lost only one, scoring nine goals and conceding five.

In the 2026/27 campaign alone, Portugal won all four matches played, demonstrating absolute dominance in their group. Even in the first leg, played in early October 2026 in Denmark, Portugal showed superior skill by convincingly defeating the home team 4-2 in a thrilling back-and-forth match.

In contrast to their rivals' impressive form, Denmark's performance has been a cause for concern. Over the same period, the Nordic team has won 3 and lost 2, scoring 8 goals but also conceding 8. Their defensive uncertainty has cost them their advantage in the race for the top spot, especially after their 2-4 defeat against Portugal in the first leg.

A hard-fought 1-0 victory against Wales in their last match has temporarily given Denmark control of their own destiny, but they still need at least four points in their final two matches against Portugal and Norway to guarantee a place in the quarter-finals. The psychological pressure is further compounded by statistics showing they have lost 100% of their last four away matches against higher-ranked opponents in the FIFA rankings, similar to this game.

The head-to-head history also heavily favors the Iberian Peninsula team. In a total of 19 encounters across all competitions, Portugal has dominated Denmark with 13 wins, 2 draws, and only 4 losses. If we only consider the last 9 clashes, Portugal has won 6 times and lost only 2.

The most recent painful memory for the Danes was in the Nations League quarter-finals in March 2025. Despite surprisingly winning 1-0 in the first leg at home in Parken, they completely collapsed in the return leg in Portugal with a heavy 2-5 defeat, thus watching their opponents go on to win the championship. Statistics from the past and present show that Portugal's home ground has always been a fraught with uncertainty for the Nordic representatives.

Information on the Portugal vs Denmark squad

Coach Jorge Jesus will be unable to field Joao Cancelo and Francisco Conceicao due to suspensions. Furthermore, Portugal's injury list is extensive, including quality players like Nuno Mendes, Rafael Leao, Nuno Tavares, and Tomas Araujo. The absence of several key full-backs forces them to give opportunities to new players, most notably young left-back Flavio Nazinho of Monaco.

On the visitors' side, the squad situation is somewhat more favorable as they have no players suspended for this match. However, the Nordic representatives still suffer significant losses in defense with veteran center-back Andreas Christensen absent due to injury, and young winger Patrick Dorgu's participation uncertain.

Christensen's absence forces the visiting coach to place his trust in the central defensive duo of Joachim Andersen and Oliver Provstgaard. Another point to note for Denmark is that as many as six of their players are at risk of suspension for the crucial match against Norway if they receive another yellow card in Lisbon. This will undoubtedly have a significant impact on their morale.

Predicted lineups for Portugal vs Denmark

Portugal (4-3-3): Costa; Dalot, Dias, Veiga, Nazinho; Vitinha, Neves, Bruno Fernandes, Bernardo Silva, Ramos, Felix.

Denmark (4-2-3-1): Hermansen; Kristensen, Andersen, Provstgaard, Maehle; Hjulmand, Hojbjerg; Isaksen, Damsgaard, Daramy; Hojlund.

Predicted score: Portugal 3-1 Denmark
