Sarah Lancashire's "masterpiece": Police drama that's one of the BBC's best is coming to Netflix

TheTools · 13.11.2026 16:21 · source · permalink

The hit crime drama has a near-perfect Rotten Tomatoes score...

Happy Valley, the beloved British crime drama that just so happens to be Sarah Lancashire's highest-rated TV show, is currently sitting in Netflix's "worth the wait" section, which means it will be arriving to the streaming service soon.

Having just starred in season two of Black Doves, Netflix's hit spy thriller, it's great timing for another of the popular TV star's brilliant performances to become available on the platform – and what better option than the BBC crime series, which still sits with a near-perfect 98% score on Rotten Tomatoes.

Across three seasons, Happy Valley's central Sergeant Catherine Cawood (an award-winning performance from Sarah Lancashire) goes up against ex-con Tommy Lee Royce (played by James Norton), whose despicable past actions are still hanging over those that crossed his path.

The excellent cast also includes Siobhan Finneran, who takes the role of Catherine Cawood's sister Clare, Katherine Kelly (who more recently starred with Finneran in ITV crime drama Protection), Steve Pemberton (Whitechapel), Charlie Murphy (Peaky Blinders), and Rhys Connah.

Written by Sally Wainwright, the British police drama has won numerous accolades – including multiple BAFTAs and a National Television Award for Sarah Lancashire – and is widely credited as being one of the BBC's best crime shows of recent years.

In addition to being a huge hit with audiences, Happy Valley was widely praised by critics too. The binge-worthy drama has been praised as a "masterpiece" (via Forbes), an "instant classic" (via TV Insider), with a "flawless" second season (via Den of Geek).

What to Read Next

Digital Spy also previously named Happy Valley as one of the 100 greatest TV shows of the 21st century, branding it "extraordinary TV", as well as highlighting it as one of the 25 best TV dramas from the UK in the past 25 years.

"In line with Wainwright's trademark of penning strong, complex, women, as well as setting her stories in West Yorkshire, Happy Valley brought us Catherine Cawood – a no-nonsense police sergeant with a dark past," we wrote as part of that list.

We also concluded that the season three climax, which proved to be event television when it first aired, was "one of the most satisfying finales we have seen in quite some time".

Happy Valley seasons 1-3 are streaming now on BBC iPlayer. The show will be added to Netflix soon.
