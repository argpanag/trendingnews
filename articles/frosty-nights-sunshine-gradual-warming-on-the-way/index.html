Frosty nights, sunshine, gradual warming on the way

TheTools · 05.11.2026 12:36 · source · permalink

Temperatures will remain on the cooler side leading to some frosty nights ahead of sunshine and gradual warming on the way. We can expect warmer, more mild temperatures by this weekend, sticking around through next week.

Today, however, will remain rather gloomy with a blanket of clouds continuing to hang over the area. This could lead to some misty conditions and/or sprinkles around the area due to the amount of moisture we have gained over the past few days along with patchy fog from time to time. Temperatures will steadily hover in the mid-40s through the afternoon hours with a breeze between 10 and 20 mph, gusts up to 30 mph possible at times. Winds are projected to slowly die down through this evening and tonight as skies gradually become mostly clear and temperatures drop into the low-30s by Friday morning. Areas of frost are likely overnight into Friday morning.

Friday will then start off on the frosty side with chilly temperatures and mostly sunny skies. We may see some clouds from time to time, but more sunshine than clouds with no rain in the forecast. Temperatures will warm up into the mid-40s by the afternoon hours with winds up to 10 mph mixed in. Friday night will once again be on the chilly side with areas of frost likely as temperatures drop into the upper-20s by Saturday morning.

Saturday will continue with mostly sunny skies and gradual warming following a chilly start to the day. Temperatures are projected to top out in the low-50s for a seasonal afternoon with light winds up to 10 mph across the area. Saturday night will remain quiet with mostly clear skies and areas of frost possible as temperatures drop into the mid-30s by Sunday morning.

Monday through Wednesday of next week will teeter between mostly sunny and partly cloudy at times with no rain in the forecast. Temperatures will be slightly warmer than average for a mild week with highs in the low-60s on Monday and the mid-50s for Tuesday and Wednesday. Winds will remain light, averaging between 5 and 15 mph across the area. Overnight conditions will remain quiet with temperatures slightly warmer, dropping into the upper-30s and low-40s by the following mornings.
