    Inter Miami vs Red Bull New York: Match Preview, Team News, Kickoff Time and Prediction Inter Miami CF face Red Bull New York in an important Major League Soccer matchup on Saturday, October 24, 2026. With the regular season approaching its conclusion, both teams have plenty at stake. Inter Miami are looking to maintain their strong position in the Eastern Conference, while the Red Bulls need valuable points to strengthen their playoff hopes.

    Lionel Messi and Inter Miami arrive in impressive form after winning three consecutive matches. Red Bull New York, meanwhile, will be eager to respond after suffering back-to-back defeats. The contrasting momentum makes this an intriguing contest, with both sides having strong reasons to pursue a positive result.

    Match Details

    Match: Inter Miami CF vs Red Bull New York

    Competition: Major League Soccer (MLS)

    Date: Saturday, October 24, 2026

    Kickoff: 5:30 PM Eastern Time

    Venue: Sports Illustrated Stadium, Harrison, New Jersey

    Broadcast: Apple TV

    The match will take place at the Red Bulls’ home stadium, where the hosts will look to use home advantage against one of the Eastern Conference’s leading teams.

    Inter Miami Match Preview

    Inter Miami enter this fixture with confidence after recording a 3-1 away victory against Atlanta United. Lionel Messi scored twice and provided an assist in that match, while Germán Berterame also contributed to the win.

    Miami have scored 73 goals in 30 regular-season matches, making them the league’s leading attacking team at this stage of the campaign. With 55 points, they sit second in the Eastern Conference and have already secured a place in the MLS Cup playoffs.

    Messi remains the central figure in Miami’s attack. His creativity, close control, passing ability, and finishing can create problems for even the most organized defenses. He has recorded 25 goals and 15 assists in the 2026 regular season, making him one of the most influential players in MLS.

    Germán Berterame has also become an important attacking option, while Luis Suárez offers additional experience and finishing ability. Their movement and understanding could be crucial against a Red Bulls defense that has struggled for consistency in recent matches.

    Inter Miami’s objective will be to control possession, create opportunities in the final third, and take advantage of defensive gaps. Another victory would strengthen their position as the regular season approaches its final stages.

    Red Bull New York Match Preview

    Red Bull New York face a challenging assignment against an in-form Inter Miami side. The hosts enter the match following consecutive defeats, including a 2-0 loss to Toronto FC.

    New York currently have 36 points from 30 matches and occupy ninth place in the Eastern Conference, putting their playoff position under pressure. With several teams competing for postseason places, every remaining match carries considerable importance.

    Forward Julian Hall has been one of the team’s most productive players this season, recording 11 goals and five assists. His pace, movement, and ability to attack open spaces could give the Red Bulls opportunities to trouble Miami’s defense.

    Jorge Ruvalcaba is another player to watch, while midfielder Emil Forsberg can provide creativity and experience. New York will need a disciplined defensive performance and effective transitions if they are to disrupt Miami’s attacking rhythm.

    The home side could look to press aggressively, win possession in midfield, and attack quickly before Miami can organize defensively. However, maintaining that intensity without leaving spaces behind the defensive line will be a major challenge.

    Key Players to Watch

    Lionel Messi

    Messi is the standout player heading into this fixture. His 25 goals and 15 assists underline his importance to Inter Miami’s attacking system. If the Red Bulls allow him too much space around the penalty area, he can change the game with a single pass or finish.

    Germán Berterame

    Berterame provides Miami with another major attacking threat. His movement around the box and ability to combine with Messi and Suárez give the visitors multiple ways to create scoring opportunities.

    Julian Hall

    Hall is one of New York’s most important attacking players. His goal-scoring ability could prove decisive if the Red Bulls can create opportunities through counterattacks and quick transitions.

    Emil Forsberg

    Forsberg’s experience and technical quality could help New York maintain possession and create chances. His delivery and ability to find attacking teammates will be particularly important against Miami’s midfield.

    Head-to-Head Record

    The rivalry between Inter Miami and Red Bull New York has become increasingly competitive. Entering this match, their regular-season series is level at six wins apiece, with one draw.

    Their first meeting of the 2026 regular season ended in a 2-2 draw at Inter Miami’s Nu Stadium in April. That result demonstrated that New York can compete with Miami, even when facing the pressure of a high-quality attacking lineup.

    The latest meeting presents a different challenge. Miami arrive on a winning streak, while New York need to reverse their recent form and protect their position in the playoff race.

    Tactical Battle: How the Match Could Be Decided

    The contest is likely to revolve around three important factors.

    Possession and attacking creativity: Inter Miami will look to control the ball and use their technical quality to create openings. The Red Bulls must prevent Messi and his teammates from receiving the ball freely in dangerous areas.

    Pressing and counterattacks: New York may attempt to pressure Miami high up the pitch and exploit spaces when possession changes hands. Successful counterattacks could provide the hosts with their best opportunities.

    Defensive organization: Miami’s attacking numbers make defensive concentration essential. The Red Bulls must track runners, close down passing lanes, and avoid giving away space around the penalty area.

    If Miami establish control early, the hosts could be forced to take greater risks. Conversely, an early New York goal would make the game more open and potentially create opportunities for both sides.
