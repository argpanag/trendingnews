Pistons vs. Heat Discussion: Game Time, TV, Odds, and More

TheTools · 15.11.2026 00:00 · source · permalink
  • Detroit Pistons Game Day

Cade Cunningham will follow up an NBA-record performance

The Detroit Pistons face the Giannis-led Miami Heat for the second time this season, but this is not the same Heat team Detroit beat 128-111 in the second game of the season. The Heat can get above .500 with a win after winning six of its past eight games, including the previous three in a row. The Heat are starting to understand how best to play around Giannis Antetokounmpo, especially Bam Adebayo. In the team’s most recent win, Bam and Giannis scored 37 points, and Davion Mitchell was able to support the big two with a 22-point, 17-assist effort.

The Pistons also had Jalen Duren the last time the two teams faced each other, and the big man is still out, and Detroit’s big man is still out recovering from a hamstring injury. Detroit’s big men have been an issue since Duren went down, and that could be an issue when going against both Bam and Giannis. Of course, the Pistons do have Cade Cunningham, and he has vaulted himself into a top-10 player in the NBA. Cunningham is coming off a career-high 52 points and an NBA-first 50-point, 10-assist, zero-turnover game. I’m not sure what he’ll be able to do for an encore, but if the Pistons don’t get decent efforts out of John Collins and Paul Reed, it might not matter.

Game Vitals

When: 8 p.m. ET
Where: Kaseya Center, Miami, Florida
Watch: TV: NBA TV, TV-20 | Streaming: DAZN-Det
Odds: Pistons -3.5

Projected Lineup

Detroit Pistons (8-3)

Cade Cunningham, Daniss Jenkins, Duncan Robinson, Ausar Thompson, John Collins

Miami Heat (6-6)

Davion Mitchell, Klay Thompson, Myron Gardner, Giannis Antetokounmpo, Bam Adebayo
