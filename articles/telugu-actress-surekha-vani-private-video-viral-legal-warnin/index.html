Telugu actress Surekha Vani private video viral, legal warning issued

TheTools · 07.11.2026 09:44 · source · permalink

Hyderabad: Popular Telugu actress Surekha Vani has raised serious concerns over the alleged hacking of her Instagram account after her personal content began circulating on social media without her consent. The actress has now broken her silence on the matter, issuing a strong warning to those sharing the content and urging social media users, meme pages and media organisations to respect her privacy.

The controversy gained attention after a video allegedly featuring the actress started doing the rounds online. While several social media users questioned whether the viral video was genuine or AI-generated, Surekha Vani herself addressed the issue through a social media post, claiming that her Instagram account had been hacked and that her private content was being circulated without her permission.

Expressing her anger over the incident, the actress urged people not to share, repost or further circulate the content.

Surekha Vani warns of legal action

In her statement, Surekha Vani made it clear that the matter was serious and that she would not tolerate the unauthorised circulation of her private content.

“My Instagram account has been hacked. My personal/private content is being circulated without my permission. I request meme pages, media and press members not to share it. If anyone shares it, I will take legal action against them. Please do not use this situation for views, reach or publicity. Respect my privacy. This is a very serious matter,” she stated.

View this post on Instagram

The actress’s response has brought attention to the issue of online privacy and the misuse of personal content, particularly when it involves public figures. Sharing someone’s private material without consent can have serious consequences, and Surekha Vani’s warning serves as a reminder that social media engagement should not come at the cost of an individual’s dignity and privacy.

More about the actress

Surekha Vani is a familiar face in Telugu cinema, having appeared in several films in supporting roles. Although she has not been particularly active in movies lately, the actress continues to remain in the spotlight through her social media presence, where she frequently shares photographs and videos.

Her daughter, Supritha, has also followed in her footsteps and is currently pursuing a career in acting. Surekha Vani’s husband passed away in 2019.
