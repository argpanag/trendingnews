Hurts won't play vs. Panthers due to superficial blood clot | theScore

TheTools · 15.10.2026 18:58 · source · permalink

Philadelphia Eagles quarterback Jalen Hurts won't play Sunday against the Carolina Panthers due to a superficial blood clot, the team announced Thursday.

Hurts sought treatment for a calf injury last Thursday after being cleated in an Oct. 4 game against the Los Angeles Rams. He was diagnosed with a blood clot in his calf in London on Saturday after an ultrasound. The quarterback was cleared to play against the Jacksonville Jaguars and scheduled to undergo a follow-up ultrasound. Imaging performed Wednesday "revealed movement in the small blood clot."

The team is still determining the appropriate course of treatment and did not provide a return timeline.

Tanner McKee is currently listed as the Eagles' backup quarterback. Fifth-round rookie Cole Payton is the other signal-caller on the roster after the team released Andy Dalton last week.

McKee has started two games in his four-year career and made six total appearances, posting a 1-1 record while throwing for 597 yards with five touchdowns and one interception.

Hurts' blood clot adds to a long list of health issues plaguing the Eagles this season. He's one of 24 Philadelphia players listed on Wednesday's injury report. Saquon Barkley and DeVonta Smith did not practice, and their status for Sunday's game is unclear.
