Sell outs for Blacks Caps T20s against India

TheTools · 20.10.2026 23:57 · source · permalink

The sold-out sign is officially up for two of the New Zealand T20is against India with Christchurch and Hamilton at their peak and Eden Park game tracking towards a record attendance.

Including the Haley Oval and Seddon Park sell outs More than 110,000 tickets have been snapped up so far for the highly anticipated tour -, 63,000 of those across the T20I series alone.

Attendance for the fourth T20I at Eden Park on Friday October 30 is on track to break NZC’s bi-lateral record at the venue of 36,523 and is expected to exhaust capacity early next week.

NZC’s Chief Marketing and Commercial Officer Glenn Critchley said the groundswell of interest and support for the India tour was remarkable.

“To almost have four out of five T20Is sold-out pre series is fantastic,” said Critchley.

“I think fans are really excited for the summer of cricket to get started, and there’s no better way to kick it off than taking on the ICC T20 World Champions in our backyard.

“There’s already an incredible buzz in Christchurch with the teams now in town and anticipation is building towards tomorrow Wednesday's Black Caps team welcome in Victoria Square, and of course the two sold-out T20Is on Thursday and Saturday night.

The full India tour also includes five OIDs and two test matches.
