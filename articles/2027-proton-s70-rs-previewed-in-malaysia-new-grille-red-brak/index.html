2027 Proton S70 RS previewed in Malaysia: new grille, red brake calipers, dual-tone cabin and column shifter

TheTools · 06.11.2026 22:58 · source · permalink

KUALA LUMPUR: Proton is introducing the 2027 Proton S70 RS as part of the upcoming S70 facelift, adding a sportier flagship variant at the top of the sedan’s range.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

What is the 2027 Proton S70 RS?

What engine does the Proton S70 RS use?

What features have been revealed for the S70 RS?

The first preview reveals revised exterior styling, a reworked cabin layout and a claimed 0–100 km/h time of 7.5 seconds ahead of its Malaysian launch.

The RS badge stands for Refined Sportiness, with the new variant getting a more obvious sporting theme inside and out. Pricing and an official launch date have not been detailed in this announcement.

Also Read: Proton sold 21,624 units in October 2026; YTD reaches 182,510 units

Up front, the S70 RS gets a redesigned grille and bumper, with red accents adding contrast to the detailing. Side skirts, 17-inch alloy wheels and red brake calipers continue the theme along the sides.

The rear gets its own revised bumper, a diffuser and quad exhaust-tip finishers. Together with the red detailing, these give the S70 RS a more overtly sporty look.

Inside, a dual-tone cabin with red accents carries over the exterior’s colour theme, with ambient lighting also on the feature list. The layout changes include a floating centre console, a Commander Dial and an automatic column shifter, moving the gear selector to the steering column.

Under the bonnet, the S70 RS uses the familiar i-GT 1.5-litre turbocharged engine paired with a seven-speed dual-clutch transmission from the turbocharged S70 range. Proton quotes a 7.5-second sprint to 100 km/h.

Proton also mentions refinements to the driving dynamics, but has yet to explain what has changed. We will need those details, and some time behind the wheel, to assess how the updates affect the drive.

“The PROTON S70 has established itself as Malaysia’s Sporty Sedan, and the 2027 PROTON S70 RS represents the next step in that journey. Through Refined Sportiness, we are giving the model a more distinctive identity with purposeful design, turbocharged performance and a sportier, driver-focused cabin. These elements bring our ‘Performance with Style’ theme to life while retaining the everyday usability that customers value,” said Dato’ Ir. Abdul Rashid Musa, Deputy Chief Executive Officer of PROTON.

The preview follows 5,367 sales for the S70 range in October 2026, bringing its total for the first 10 months of the year to 26,076 units.

Of October’s tally, 2,338 units came from the newly introduced S70 i-GT 1.5 Lite and Prime, while the more powerful variants accounted for 3,029 units. Proton reports B-segment sedan sales leadership for Lite and Prime, and C-segment leadership for the more powerful versions.

The next date to watch is 10 November 2026 at 8pm, when Proton will hold an S70 RS line-off reveal alongside a fashion showcase with a local designer. The event will be livestreamed on Proton’s official social media platforms.

The 10 November event is the line-off reveal, with the official launch still to follow.
