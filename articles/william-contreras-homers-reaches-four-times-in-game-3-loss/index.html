    William Contreras went 3-for-4 with a homer in a loss to the Padres in Game 3 of the NLDS on Tuesday.

    Contreras got the Brewers on the board with a shot off Nick Pivetta in the fourth inning. The 28-year-old also drew a walk in the ninth inning, but the Brew Crew were unable to score and picked up their first postseason loss. Contreras has homered twice in this series with a slash of .364/.462/.909 over the first three games.
