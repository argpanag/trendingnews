Best Amazon Black Friday deals

Oura Ring 5

The latest iteration of the Oura Ring, which is currently at its lowest price ever, is slimmer than the previous versions, making it sleeker and much more discrete. The Oura Ring 5 tracks several health metrics, including heart rate, body temperature and respiratory rate, plus it automatically detects and logs exercise and sleep. The battery lasts up to nine days per charge (up from eight for the previous version). Keep in mind the ring requires an Oura membership subscription ($5.99 per month or $69.99 per year).

Apple AirPods Pro 3

A few months ago, I finally upgraded my earbuds to the Apple AirPods Pro 3 and have used them every day to listen to music and take calls. I love that they block out all noise when I want them to, and there’s no loud background sounds when I’m chatting on the phone. They also have built-in heart rate sensing, live language translation, hearing aid and hearing test features. They come with five sets of ear tips in different sizes and a charging case.

Amazon Kindle Paperwhite 16GB (2024 Model)

Amazon’s 2024 version of its Kindle Paperweight has a seven-inch, glare-free screen and a thin, water-resistant design, so you can easily take your novels on the go (including to the beach or pool). The battery lasts up to 12 weeks on a single charge, plus you can adjust the display to read in either bright sunlight or in the dark. The regular Amazon Kindle is also on sale right now.

Hatch Restore 3

One of our favorite sunrise alarm clocks, the Hatch Restore 3 uses gradual light to gently wake you up in the morning, moving from red to orange to white before your alarm sounds. You can customize your sleep and wake up routine, play sound, adjust light and more using the companion Hatch Sleep app, or you can press the buttons directly on the device to control it. “I’m more likely to sleep through the night now since I’m not anticipating being startled awake by my phone’s blaring alarm,” says NBC Selected reporter Zoe Malin. You can use it as a bedside light to wind down, too.

Medicube Toner Pads Zero Pore Pad 2.0

These TikTok-beloved toner pads are a favorite among our editors, who tested them for our Medicube brand guide. The pads are dual-sided: one side is textured to exfoliate, while the other is soft to hydrate and soothe your skin. NBC Selected senior editor Nikki Brown, who uses these pads to treat her hormonal acne, says the textured side feels gentle on her skin, so there’s no burning sensation during or after she uses it. They also come with a pair of small tongs, so you don’t have to use your fingers to separate the pads.

DJI Osmo Pocket 3 Vlog Bundle

The DJI Osmo Pocket 3 has a built-in stabilizer that keeps your footage steady, plus a two-inch touchscreen that you can rotate for both horizontal and vertical shooting. You can get over 2.5 hours of footage on a single charge and it’s small and compact, so you can vlog from anywhere. This bundle comes with a DJI mini mic transmitter to capture clear sound, plus accessories like mic windscreens, a mic charging dock and carrying pouch, a protective cover for the camera and a wrist strap.

Shark ChillPill

This portable fan came in handy during the hotter months, and I always carry it around in my purse or work tote in case I need a boost of cold air. It has 10 speeds, which is more than many other popular handheld fans I’ve tried, and a compartment for water that allows it to both function as a mister and activate the cooling plate on the side. I place the cooling plate directly on my neck to help lower my skin temperature on particularly hot days.

Crest 3D Whitestrips Professional Effects

One of our favorite at-home teeth-whitening products, this pack comes with 20 treatments that gently brighten your teeth over time and keep them white for at least 12 months, according to the brand. Crest recommends using the strips once a day for 30 minutes. The kit also comes with two one-hour express treatments you can use when you’ve got a bit more time.

Ember Mug 3

With this self-heating mug, you’ll never have to microwave your lukewarm coffee again. I’ve been a fan of the Ember Mug since 2021, and after recently trying the brand’s latest release, the Ember Mug 3, I’m newly obsessed. The mug can warm beverages at temperatures up to 145 degrees Fahrenheit, which you can control using the buttons on the mug or via the Ember app. It can keep drinks warm for up to three hours in cordless mode, or all day when on the charging coaster. It’s also dishwasher-safe.

Biodance Bio-Collagen Real Deep Mask

Biodance’s sheet masks have hyaluronic acid to hydrate and moisturize skin. I use these at least once a week and love how it goes from opaque to completely translucent overnight. My skin feels soft, smooth and hydrated as soon as I take it off in the morning.

Skylight Calendar

You can use this digital wall planner to color-code events, sync calendars across apps and add events via the Skylight app. You can also create a chore chart, custom lists, check the weather and more. NBC Selected commerce editor Bethany Heitman, who is a mom of two, uses this calendar to keep her family’s tasks and to-dos together. “Every family member gets assigned their own color, so at a glance you can tell whose dentist appointment is Tuesday and whose dance practice runs late on Thursday, without squinting at tiny text or scrolling through a shared app,” she says.

Nex Playground

This viral active play system that gets kids and families moving. It has a growing library of 60+ games from major brands, including Bluey, Fruit Ninja and Barbie. It connects to your TV and has a built-in camera to track motion and natural body movements. Plus, it supports up to four players at once (which varies by game), so the whole family can play together.

Nodpod Mineralized Silk Gentle Pressure Sleep Mask

The Nodpod is essentially a weighted blanket for your eyes. I have the original Nodpod and loved it so much that I got the silk version, which is much gentler on my sensitive skin. Plus, as a hot sleeper, I love that the silk feels cool. It’s machine-washable and doesn’t use straps, velcro or tension like most sleep masks, which makes it feel much less constricting.

Shark DePuffi

The viral Shark DePuffi has a gua sha-inspired edge and six customizable temperatures that go from cold to hot. I use this device nearly every day and notice it makes me look so much less puffy and more awake in the morning . As you massage it along your face and neck, it depuffs, sculpts and temporarily relieves muscle tension. It comes with a charger and travel case, so you can take it on the go.

Cosrx Snail Mucin 96% Repairing Face Serum

This buzzy ingredient hydrates skin and targets fine lines, according to experts in our guide to snail mucin. This Cosrx serum, one of our favorite facial essences, is suitable for all skin types, lightweight and hypoallergenic, according to the brand.

Stanley Quencher H2.0 Flow State 40-Ounce Tumbler

Our editors agree that the Stanley Quencher withstands the test of time. It keeps drinks cold and ice frozen for hours thanks to its stainless steel construction and double-walled vacuum insulation. The base is cup holder-friendly and you can move the lid to drink, place a straw or completely cover the top. The large handle also makes it comfortable to hold wherever you go.

JBL Charge 6

If you’re in the market for a new portable Bluetooth speaker, this one from JBL has up to 24 hours of playtime on a single charge and is water-resistant, so you can take it with you to the pool or beach. It also has a handle strap to make it easier to carry.

Samsung The Frame 65-Inch Class Smart TV (2025 Model)

Samsung’s Frame Smart TV has a matte screen that makes it less reflective than traditional TV screens, and when it’s turned off, it becomes a digital frame that displays different artwork curated by the brand. You can also upload your own photos to add a personalized touch. (Ed’s note: This product slightly increased in price since Black Friday began, but because it’s 40% off and one of our favorite products, we think it’s a worthwhile deal.)

PicassoTiles 100-Piece Magnetic Tiles

These magnetic tiles are not only a great way to keep children entertained, but they can also unlock their creativity and help them understand color, geometrical shapes and architectural design. The tiles are scalable, so they can add more pieces to build as big as they want. Made for children three years and older, the tiles have smooth edges to avoid any cuts or bumps. This set comes with 100 magnetic tiles.

Owlet Dream Sock Smart Baby Monitor

The Owlet Dream Sock is a popular wearable smart monitor that tracks your baby’s pulse rate, blood oxygen levels and sleep patterns in real time. Made for infants one to18 months old (six to 30 pounds), the Owlet will send alerts directly to your phone if your baby’s readings aren’t within the preset zones. The sock is made from comfortable fabric and wraps gently around your baby’s foot, plus it has up to 16 hours of battery life and connects via Bluetooth.

Life360 Tile Bluetooth Tracker (2 Pack)

This two-pack of Tile Stickers have an adhesive back, so you can stick them to your essentials and keep track of them, like your keys, wallet and remote. You can pair them with the Tile app to track their most recent location and ring the device if needed.

Aura 10-Inch Digital HD Picture Frame

Upload your favorite photos and videos via the Aura app to display them on this Wi-Fi-enabled digital frame. The device will automatically swipe through the images when you walk by. The frame automatically adjusts screen brightness, crops photos, turns off automatically at night and has a built-in speaker for videos. You can invite loved ones to upload their memories, too.
