Update: SaskPower announces planned power outage in Estevan

TheTools · 21.10.2026 19:13 · source · permalink

REDVERS, Sask. - SaskPower has announced details for an upcoming planned power outage in Estevan.

According to a message from the Crown corporation, the outage is slated for Oct. 15 from 9:30 a.m.-12:30 p.m. on Coronation Street. 

SaskPower also announced earlier in the day that crews have been dispatched and are determining the cause and repair time for an outage reported in Redvers. Power has been restored, but now an emergency outage was scheduled to happen in the town at 4:30 p.m. Electricity was restored by 5:30 p.m. 

SaskToday will have updates as they become available.
