Glenn Coldenhoff returns to TeamNL for Motocross of Nations - MX Vice

TheTools · 18.09.2026 13:04 · source · permalink

The Dutch Motocross of Nations (MXoN) team is complete once again. Star rider Glenn Coldenhoff joins TeamNL and will compete in the World Championship for national teams alongside Jeffrey Herlings and Roan van de Moosdijk. This means that from October 2 to 4, the Dutch team will line up behind the starting gate in Ernée, featuring three champions from 2026.

Kay de Wolf was forced to withdraw from the MXoN earlier due to an injury. An injury that initially appeared to be harmless turned out to be more serious than expected, just one day after the team announcement. His place in TeamNL will now be filled by another big name: Glenn Coldenhoff.

Glenn has competed in the Brazilian Championship this season, where he secured the title last weekend. At the end of August, he made his final appearance at the highest level with a one-off appearance at the MXGP of Arnhem. The 35-year-old rider from Brabant has finished runner-up at the MXoN four times and third once in the past. The highlight came in 2019 in Assen, when he helped the Dutch team secure its first-ever MXoN title.

Coldenhoff won both races that year, just as he had done in 2018, and played a crucial role in the Dutch victory.

TeamNL will be aiming for another gold medal in Ernée. With Jeffrey Herlings (MXGP World Champion), Glenn Coldenhoff (Brazilian Champion) and Roan van de Moosdijk (German Champion), the Dutch team will certainly be one of the strong teams in the field.

MX Nationals Heerde

Before the MXoN, the Dutch riders will be in action next Sunday, September 27. This is the perfect opportunity to see newly crowned World Champion Jeffrey Herlings and Glenn Coldenhoff in action! Glenn will compete in the 500cc class on an MX2 bike.

Fans will also have the opportunity to get a photo, poster and autograph during a meet & greet.
