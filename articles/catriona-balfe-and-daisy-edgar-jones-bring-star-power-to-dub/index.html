Catriona Balfe and Daisy Edgar Jones bring star power to Dublin premiere

TheTools · 18.09.2026 09:37 · source · permalink

Daisy Edgar‑Jones and Caitriona Balfe brought serious star power to Dublin as they stepped out for the Irish premiere of Sense and Sensibility at The Stella Cinema Rathmines on Thursday night.

The Golden Globe nominees posed together on the red carpet, marking the latest stop on their promotional tour for the new adaptation.

Daisy, 28, looked stunning in a white silk Stella McCartney gown trimmed with feathered detailing, finishing the look with sparkling Boucheron jewellery.

Caitríona, 46, opted for a chic monochrome ensemble, keeping her signature understated elegance front and centre.

If you haven't already seen the earlier versions of Sense and Sensibility, it follows the fortunes of the three Dashwood sisters in the 18th Century.

Following the death of their father, the sisters and their mother are forced to move out of their family estate and into a cottage with a relative.

Their half-brother, who had promised their father that he would support them, is instead persuaded by his wife to withhold financial support and also sets about blocking a potential match with Elinor and her brother.

Marianne, meanwhile, is attempting to stave off a marriage to the much older but wealthy Colonel Brandon and instead falls in love with the dashing but weak-willed John Willoughby.

As the plot unfolds, the sisters find themselves experiencing contrasting relationship stories.

The cast also features George MacKay, Herbert Nordrum, Bodhi Rae Breathnach, Fiona Shaw and Frank Dillane.

Earlier this week, the new cast attended the world premiere in London before bringing the celebration to Ireland.

Sense and Sensibility opens in cinemas next week.
