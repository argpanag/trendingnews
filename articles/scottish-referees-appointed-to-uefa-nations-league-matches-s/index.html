Scottish referees appointed to UEFA Nations League Matches - Scottish FA

TheTools · 07.10.2026 00:37 · source · permalink

UEFA have announced that two of Scotland's top referees will be taking charge of two crunch Nations League fixtures in League A and League C. Monday evening will see Referee Nick Walsh, Assistant Referees Francis Connor and Daniel McFarlane, 4th Official Don Robertson, Video Assistant Referee Andrew Dallas and Assistant Video Assistant Referee Steven McLean take charge at the Stade de France in St. Denis as first placed League A side France host Belgium. Tuesday sees Referee David Dickinson, Assistant Referees David McGeachie and Jonathan Bell, 4th Official Lloyd Wilson, Video Assistant Referee Kevin Clancy and Assistant Video Assistant Referee Chris Graham take charge of the League C match between Estonia and Iceland in Tallinn.
