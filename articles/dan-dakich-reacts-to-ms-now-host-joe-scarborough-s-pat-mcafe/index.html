Dan shreds cable news host Joe Scarborough after he threw an on-air tantrum claiming he's "had enough of Pat McAfee ruining College GameDay" and suggested ESPN executives step in.

While there are plenty of polarizing figures on television nowadays, there is perhaps no one who is more of a lightning rod than ESPN's very own Pat McAfee.

The former All-Pro punter for the Indianapolis Colts has become somewhat ubiquitous with The Worldwide Leader, appearing all over their many different channels seemingly at all hours of the day.

He is most well known for his work on "College Gameday," one of ESPN's most popular original programs, but lately his notoriety has turned to infamy thanks to the cries of several online "haters."

One of those haters just so happens to be MS NOW commentator (and Matthew Perry lookalike) Joe Scarborough, and although most political pundits were busy making sense of the midterm elections on Tuesday, the "Morning Joe" host took aim at the "Gameday" centerpiece.

Scarborough claims McAfee "ruined College Gameday" and that the supposed rejection of his antics are a rebuke of the "manosphere" that has been cultivated since 2024.

Take it easy, Chandler.

OutKick's Dan Dakich got a hold of this rant from Scarborough and had a few choice words for Mr. Morning Joe himself.

"Four dudes that know absolutely nothing about football are trying to chime in on social events that football people are talking about," Dakich said, referring to Scarborough and his fellow deskmates.

"They look like idiots."

The "Don't @ Me" host notes that this feels painfully similar to Minnesota governor and noted manly man Tim Walz larping as a football dude during Kamala Harris' 2024 presidential campaign.

"This is just like little Timmy Walz trying to be a 'real boy,'" Dakich joked.
