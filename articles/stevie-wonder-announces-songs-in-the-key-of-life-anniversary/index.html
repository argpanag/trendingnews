Stevie Wonder Announces Songs in the Key Of Life Anniversary Tour

TheTools · 08.09.2026 12:40 · source · permalink

Stevie Wonder is celebrating 50 years of his seminal album Songs in the Key of Life with a series of anniversary shows. The tour, which will see Wonder perform the record in full each show, kicks off in October in England and concludes at Los Angeles’ Intuit Dome on December 19. Check out a full list of dates below.

Released in 1976, Songs in the Key of Life gave Wonder his third Grammy Award for Album of the Year, and features modern standards “I Wish,” “Isn’t She Lovely,” “As,” and “Sir Duke.” Although Herbie Hancock, George Benson, Minnie Riperton, and many more contributed to it, Wonder produced, wrote, and arranged nearly every track, and played almost every instrument. Songs in the Key of Life is a part of the National Recording Registry overseen by the Library of Congress.

The run marks Wonder’s first official tour since fall 2024, when he embarked on the 11-city Sing Your Song! As We Fix Our Nation’s Broken Heart tour. Since then, he has made a number of one-off appearances, including at a tribute performance to Quincy Jones at the 2025 Grammys and a celebration of Sly Stone at the 2025 Rock & Roll Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony.
