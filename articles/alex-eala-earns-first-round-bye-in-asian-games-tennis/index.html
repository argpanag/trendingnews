Alex Eala earns first round bye in Asian Games tennis

TheTools · 26.09.2026 19:45 · source · permalink

Alexandra “Alex” Eala earned a first-round bye in the 2026 Asian Games, according to the draw released by the Philippine Tennis Association.

Eala, the highest-ranked player in the tournament, will advance directly to the second round, where she will face the winner of the match between Thailand’s Patcharin Cheapchandej and Pakistan’s Mahin Aftab Qureshi.

World No. 55 Zhang Shuai of China also earned a first-round bye as the tournament’s second seed.

Filipina Tennielle Madis, meanwhile, will take on Japan’s Himeno Sakatsume in the first round.

Eala will also compete in doubles with Shaira Hope Rivera. The pair will face Hong Kong’s Lai Ching Laam and Kallista Liu in the first round.

The other Philippine pair will see Madis team up with Stefie Aludo against Indonesia’s Anjali Kirana Junarto and Reinnamah Meydiana Laviola.

In mixed doubles, Rivera and Francis Casey Alcantara will open their campaign against Iran’s Kasra Rahmani and Meshkatolzahra Safi.

Eala recently absorbed a first-round exit in the Singapore Tennis Open as she fell to Tatiana Prozorova.
