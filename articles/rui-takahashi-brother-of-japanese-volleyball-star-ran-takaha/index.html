Rui Takahashi, brother of Japanese volleyball star Ran Takahashi, comes out as gay

TheTools · 29.09.2026 15:37 · source · permalink

Opening up to a new chapter.

Ran Takahashi's older brother Rui Takahashi has confirmed via a public statement that he is part of the LGBTQIA+ community.

Rui is a volleyball athlete playing the outside hitter position for the Japanese team Suntory Sunbirds Osaka. Meanwhile, his younger brother Ran is part of the Japanese men's national volleyball team.

On his Instagram page, Rui shared a lengthy statement in Japanese detailing his coming-out story.

In a quick English translation, the volleyball star said, “I've been afraid to face my own side since I was a child, and I've been troubled and denied myself. That means I'm gay and a member of the sexual minority.”

He also promised to continue to excel in the sports of volleyball and strive to become better.

“I will continue to do my best in volleyball and strive for personal growth in various situations.

“And for those who struggle with not being able to accept themselves, I would be happy if my words could be a small trigger for them to face themselves and make a change.

“This is a sudden announcement, but thank you for reading until the end.

“I am truly grateful to all the fans and many others who have supported me.”
