Russian president Vladimir Putin will be invited by the US to the G20 Summit in Miami this December, secretary of state Marco Rubio says.

"We think it's an opportunity for (him) to engage not just with the president (Trump) but with other world leaders. We hope that's an invitation he'll accept," Rubio told the media.

Ukrainian leader Volodymyr Zelensky, meanwhile, told leaders in New York that Ukraine was ready for a maritime truce in the Black Sea based on proposals by Egypt, India and Turkey, saying it was now up to Russia to respond.

The president, who is expected to address the UN General Assembly soon, met US president Donald Trump on Tuesday and said they had also discussed a Russia-Ukraine ceasefire on energy-related targets.

The Kremlin has not publicly backed the proposals, but Rubio said both sides had expressed interest in a limited ceasefire involving grain and energy. Moscow said, however, that there were no grounds for wider peace talks yet.

One person killed as Russia strikes Ukrainian petrol stations

Russia slammed jet-powered drones into two petrol stations and other sites in Kyiv early on Wednesday, killing one, hours after President Volodymyr Zelensky met US President Donald Trump.

A further seven people were injured in the morning attack, city authorities said. Explosions were heard and smoke billowed in the city centre, according to Reuters journalists.

Russia has changed tactics and in recent weeks been attacking Kyiv and nearby areas with near constant barrages of the faster and high-flying jet drones that are harder to intercept.

Ukrainian oil company Ukrnafta said Russia hit one of its petrol stations in the capital — the sixth such facility attacked in recent weeks.
