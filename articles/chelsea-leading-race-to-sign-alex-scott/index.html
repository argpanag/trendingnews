Chelsea leading race to sign Alex Scott

TheTools · 04.10.2026 23:02 · source · permalink

Xabi Alonso’s side will push hard to sign Bournemouth midfielder who is also attracting strong interest from Liverpool and Arsenal

Chelsea are at the front of the queue of clubs tracking England’s Alex Scott.

Scott shone on his first England start in the 7-0 drubbing of Croatia on Saturday and earned glowing praise from head coach Thomas Tuchel.

Chelsea had more than one bid, thought to be worth more than £60m, for Scott rejected by Bournemouth in the summer transfer window and remain highly interested in the 23-year-old.

Having sold Enzo Fernández to Manchester City, midfield is an area Chelsea will look to strengthen in January and next summer.

Chelsea almost signed Lamine Camara at the end of the summer transfer window, but their interest in the Monaco player is not thought to preclude another move for Scott.

Bournemouth have been adamant they will not sell Scott in the January transfer window, but that has not yet dimmed Chelsea’s interest – even if they have to wait until the summer.

Scott will have one year remaining on his contract at the end of this season and Chelsea are not the only club tracking him.

Liverpool, managed by Scott’s old Bournemouth head coach, Andoni Iraola, have shown an interest in him as, too, have Arsenal. Liverpool have also been linked with a bid for Camara.

Tottenham Hotspur, the team Scott grew up supporting, Manchester United and Manchester City all spent heavily on midfielders during the past summer, and uncertainty surrounds the outcome of City’s Premier League cheating scandal.

Bournemouth have made attempts to tie Scott to a new contract, but there has been no progress and the club and player have agreed to focus on the season.

Scott played 70 minutes next to City’s Elliot Anderson against Croatia and is in line to win his third England cap and first at Wembley against the Czech Republic on Tuesday night.

The pair starred together in England Under-21s’ European Championship victory last year. The emergence of Scott has given Tuchel a welcome extra option and can lift the load on Declan Rice, who withdrew from this Nations League squad.

Asked if Scott is fighting for Rice’s place in his England team, Tuchel said: “Of course and he does, we need performances like this. This is just the nature of it and we will not survive on two central midfielders. The more the better and it’s always about the guys who are in camp.

“Declan knows about his importance and he’s one of the very best midfielders in Europe. Nothing will change that. But it’s exactly what we need. We need more, more, more, more variety. We need more, more, more options. And it’s always then, in the end, it’s always about the guys who are in camp. And Alex was at the highest level.”

On the relationship between Scott and Anderson from their under-21 days, Tuchel said: “I guess it helps. Even their characteristics and not only as players, but even in their character. So they’re a good mix and match. Alex had a fantastic match [against Croatia] and we need to be strong in the middle of the pitch. And I’m very happy with the level that Alex trained at, and the player is on the highest level.”

Scott said: “We [he and Anderson] work really well together. We saw that last year at the under-21 Euros and we’ve been speaking about it ever since, playing in this midfield together. I love playing with Elliot, he makes life so easy for me as his partner in there. He’s probably one of the best midfielders in the world at the moment.”

Tuchel included Scott in England’s pre-World Cup training camp, but preferred 36-year-old Jordan Henderson and Kobbie Mainoo in his squad for the tournament.

On missing out on a place at the World Cup, Scott said: “It gave me more hunger. But I’ve said there’s so many top players for this country, it’s a tough squad to get into.

“There were no bad feelings about not going to the World Cup. I went to the prep camp, I wanted to do that and learn as much as I can and it helped me coming into this camp and starting to play games. Off the pitch, the stuff that I learnt there, being around the group, it made me feel way more comfortable.”

Manchester City full-back Rico Lewis has been added to Tuchel’s England squad for the Czech Republic game, stepping up from the under-21s. Lewis’s City team-mate Nico O’Reilly was forced to withdraw after the Spain game with an injury.
