Public School Set to Release Its ‘In-Security’ Nike Air Max Goadome Monk Strap Boot — An Update to a Security Guard Favorite

TheTools · 17.09.2026 17:18 · source · permalink

The shoe was first teased during the New York fashion brand's fall 2026 runway show.

Public School’s new collaboration with Nike is here.

The new shoe with the Swoosh — the “In-Security” Nike Air Max Goadome High is set to release today.

First seen on the runway during Public School’s fall/winter 2026 show in February, the new shoe is a reimagined take on the Nike Air Max Goadome by designers Dao-Yi Chow and Maxwell Osborne.

Described as a classic boot style, the shoe features a leather upper with a padded high collar while double monk straps with silver alloy buckles cover the upper. Visible Nike Air units are seen in the heel and forefoot as well as an embossed Swoosh and Public School New York logos.

The shoe is finished with a branded Public School New York silver alloy metal clip, while the rubber sole and unique lug pattern features Nike’s ACG branding.

First introduced in the early 2000s, the Goadome fused traditional boot construction with Air Max technology. Known simply as the “Nike Boot” to generations of wearers, it developed a distinct cultural identity across the East Coast and became particularly embedded in New York’s intersection of footwear, hip-hop, streetwear and everyday city style.

As early and mid-2000s footwear continues to influence fashion today, Public School is revisiting the silhouette through its own New York lens.

“The Goadome is the ultimate NYC boot built for extreme East Coast winters,” Chow told FN. “They’ve been part of our wardrobe since the 2000’s and a staple in the city landscape, from everyday workers to security guards, they’re synonymous with functionality and vigilance. They’re the ‘ready-for-anything’ boot. We just dressed them up a bit so you can wear them out more often.”

The Public School “In-Security” Nike Air Max Goadome High, which retails for $320, will be released at 5 p.m. EST on Sept. 17 alongside an original action-driven short film.

The release comes just days after Chow and Osborne showed their second runway show since returning to New York Fashion Week. Dubbed “Living Proof,” the design duo’s spring 2027 collection was all about moving forward and exploring the “extremes” of the world.

The show saw the debut of Public School’s second tie-up with Allen Edmonds. The two-style collection featured the Denzel laceless derby, available in black and white garment-dyed crust leather, and the Russell Longwing offered in a black washed cordovan leather.

A set of custom Nike Moon Shoes were also used as styling for the show.

This collaborative shoe also marks the first drop between Public School and Nike since their 2018 Air Force 1 release.
