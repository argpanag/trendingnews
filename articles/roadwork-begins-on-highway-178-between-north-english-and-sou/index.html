Roadwork Begins on Highway 178 Between North English and South English Tuesday

  • November 7, 2026 @ 6:30 am
  • Today's Local News
  • Tweet
  • Share
  • Email

The Iowa Department of Transportation will start a new project along Highway 178 between South English and North English on Tuesday.

Traveling north from South English, the work area begins just past 135th Street, about a half-mile from Highway 22. Work then continues north for about a mile, crossing the South English River before concluding around County Road G13, just over two miles south of North English.

The road will remain open during the length of the project, but drivers should expect intermittent lane closures as work is completed.

Work will begin at 7 a.m. on Tuesday, November 10th. The project is expected to take just under one month to complete. The DOT estimates the project will be completed by December 8th.
