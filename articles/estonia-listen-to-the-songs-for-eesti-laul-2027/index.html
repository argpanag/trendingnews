Estonia: Listen to the songs for Eesti Laul 2027

TheTools · 27.11.2026 18:34 · source · permalink

The 12 songs competing in Eesti Laul 2027 have been released. Listen to the songs and vote in our poll: Who should win Eesti Laul 2027?

Estonia's Eesti Laul 2027 songs are here.

The 12 competing entries were unveiled on 27 November in the ETV programme "Eesti Laul 2027. Start", giving listeners their first chance to hear the songs in the running to represent Estonia at Eurovision 2027.

The artists and song titles were announced in October, following selection by a jury of 40 people. The lineup brings together returning Eesti Laul contestants and newcomers.

Eesti Laul 2027

The final of Eesti Laul 2027 will take place on 30 January at Unibet Arena in Tallinn, hosted by Koit Toome and Eda-Ines Etti.

All 12 acts will compete in a single final, with the winner decided over two rounds of voting.

In the first round, an international jury and viewers will select three acts to advance to the superfinal. The winner will then be chosen solely by televoting.

Estonia in Eurovision

Estonia has participated in the Eurovision Song Contest 31 times, first in 1994, competing every year except 1995.

Estonia has managed to qualify for the Grand Final 11 out of 22 times.

Estonia has won just once, in the year 2001, with the song "Everybody," performed by Tanel Padar and Dave Benton and 2XL.
