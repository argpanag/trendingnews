Highs near 70 next two days; dry for trick-or-treat

TheTools · 29.10.2026 07:45 · source · permalink

Our dry stretch continues into early November; rain chances return late next week

Our dry stretch continues into early November; rain chances return late next week

A Dense Fog Advisory is in effect for Mifflin and Juniata Counties until 10 a.m. Watch for visibility dropping to 1/4 mile or less. Low clouds are expanding across the rest of the area, but visibility is still good. We will continue to monitor, to see if fog develops.

By mid-morning, low clouds and fog will decrease. We'll see wall-to-wall sunshine and highs near 70 degrees this afternoon.

Mostly clear skies are in store tonight. As a cold front passes right before sunrise, a stray shower is possible. Expect lows near 50 degrees.

Look for mostly sunny and pleasant conditions Friday. You'll notice a 10-15 mph northwesterly breeze. Highs will reach 70 degrees, which is almost 10 degrees above average for the end of October.

High pressure will build overhead Saturday. We'll see partly sunny skies and highs in the mid-60s. Trick-or-treaters are in for a treat, as temperatures hover in the low 50s between 6 to 8 p.m.

We'll see a mix of clouds and sunshine Sunday, and highs will remain above average, in the low-to-mid 60s.

Early next week looks dry. Look for partly sunny skies and highs in the mid-to-upper 60s, as high pressure dominates.

Clouds will gradually increase toward the end of the week, and a cold front will bring a chance of showers Thursday into Friday. Highs will reach the low-to-mid 60s.
