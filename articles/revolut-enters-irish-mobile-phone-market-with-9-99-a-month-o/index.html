Revolut enters Irish mobile phone market with €9.99 a month offer to first 25,000 customers

TheTools · 09.11.2026 00:00 · source · permalink

Revolut is entering the Irish mobile phone market, with a new eSim 5G plan launched on Monday.

Revolut is to enter the Irish mobile phone market, with a new service starting at €9.99 a month for the first 25,000 customers to sign up.

The launch of Revolut Mobile follows hot on the heels of Lidl Mobile, which saw 100,000 customers sign up to its introductory €8.99 offer in just 10 days.  With 3.4m users in Ireland - the highest penetration per capita in the world - Revolut Mobile is expected to cause a similar stir.

Read More

Revolut Mobile will be priced at €9.99 per month for the first 25,000 customers to sign up, rising to €12.99 per month thereafter. The mobile plan includes unlimited 5G-ready data, calls, and texts, as well as 20GB of data roaming across the UK and European Economic Area. 

Revolut Mobile customers can also redeem their RevPoints earned through everyday spending via their Revolut Bank debit or credit card, for discounts.

“We are bringing the same challenger mindset to telecommunications that helped us transform digital banking," said Revolut's Elyas Sadou, the firm's telecoms general manager. "Mobile contracts in Ireland have – for too long – been burdened by lofty pricing, hidden fees, and rigid long-term commitments. Revolut Mobile changes that by delivering flexible, premium connectivity managed entirely within the Revolut Bank app — allowing customers to utilise the RevPoints they earn on everyday spending towards their mobile plan.”

eSim directly in app

The new service is provided by an eSim, activated directly within the app, instead of a physical sim card. The service is provided for Revolut by Gigs, a US telecoms as a service provider.  "Over 80% of adults in Ireland already bank with Revolut. From today, the same app keeps them connected too," said Gigs chief executive Hermann Frank.

The Revolut plan also includes a 'Messaging Pass', 1GB of additional low-speed data covers more than 120 non-EU countries worldwide and is designed for general-use and text messaging.

Revolut is Europe's most valuable startup, with a funding round last month confirming its valuation at €103bn.
