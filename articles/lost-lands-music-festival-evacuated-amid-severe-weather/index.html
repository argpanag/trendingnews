Lost Lands music festival evacuated amid severe weather

TheTools · 20.09.2026 00:52 · source · permalink

The Lost Lands music festival at Legend Valley was evacuated on Sept. 19 after a band of severe weather rolled through southern Licking County.

The multi-day festival is taking place at the outdoor concert venue and began around 3 p.m. Sept. 19.

The National Weather Service issued a severe thunderstorm warning for Fairfield County due to a line of storms capable of producing strong winds and heavy rainfall.

The festival urged attendees to return to their cars or campsites to wait out the storms. Patrons were allowed back into the festival after the storms cleared the area.
