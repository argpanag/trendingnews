Heidi Klum's Halloween Party 2026: All the Celebrity Costumes on the Red Carpet

TheTools · 01.11.2026 09:13 · source · permalink

Heidi Klum

The model's costume is hands down one of her best, as she dressed as her real left hand—including her wedding ring from husband Tom Kaulitz.

Neil Patrick Harris and David Burtka

The couple was unrecognizable as the twins from The Shining at Heidi Klum’s 25th annual Halloween Party.

Kyle MacLachlan and Desiree Gruber

Attending alongside his wife, the Sex and the City star was dressed as "the evil" Cliff Vandercave from his 1994 movie The Flintstones.

"This is a blast from the past with Flintstones," he told E!. "Original costume. I don't think they know it's gone, so don't tell them."

Lou Samuel, Johan Samuel and Leni Klum

Klum's kids banded together on the red carpet for the party, which was presented by Apothic and Butterfinger at Cathédrale in New York City.

Chase Hudson

The influencer channeled American Psycho by dressing as Patrick Bateman.

“I thought this fit the New York atmosphere,” he told E! News. “A little bit of Wall Street, a little bit of money and a little bit of heartbreak and some pain.”

James Charles

The beauty influencer spent seven and a half hours transforming into Yzma from The Emperor's New Groove .

"I did prosthetics, and I'm not a special effects makeup artist, so that definitely tacked on a little bit more time than I would normally need," he told E!. "It's one of my favorite movies from when I was a kid. It's just a great way to combine glamor, camp, color and a lot of effort for Halloween."

Taylor Momsen

The Gossip Girl alum embraced her rock skills for All Hallows' Eve.

"I've been on tour with AC/DC for the past three years," she told E!. "I'm Angus Young tonight."

Questlove

The musician got in the NFL spirit with a San Francisco 49ers jersey and blinged helmet accessory.

Valentina Sampaio

You'll be green with envy over the model's Poison Ivy look.

Andrew Rannells and Tuc Watkins

The Girls alum and One Life to Live actor appear to have crashlanded on the shores of Heidi Klum's party.

Karen Mulder
