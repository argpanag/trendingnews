7 Years Later, Ben Affleck's David Fincher-Inspired Action Thriller Gets Its Biggest Reveal Yet

TheTools · 17.10.2026 19:49 · source · permalink

Since his feature directorial debut, Gone Baby Gone, Ben Affleck has become one of the most in-demand directors. His resume includes critically acclaimed titles like The Town, Air, and the Best Picture winner Argo. His latest film, Animals, debuted on Netflix on Oct. 9, 2026. Despite negative reviews, Animals still ranked No. 1 on the Netflix charts the week it was released. Affleck has often excelled at adult-oriented action thrillers, both as a director and star, working with directors like Gavin O'Connor, Ridley Scott, and David Fincher.

Affleck worked with Fincher on the hit film Gone Girl in 2014. At the same time, Affleck was filming Batman v. Superman: Dawn of Justice, playing the role of Bruce Wayne / Batman, one that Warner Bros. was hoping Affleck would play for years. The Town director was even set to write and direct a Batman movie for the DCEU, and, according to one of the actors he cast in the film, it would have drawn heavily on David Fincher's work.

In an excerpt from his memoir Bloodlines, via Entertainment Weekly, Joe Manganiello describes Affleck's planned Batman film as "a superhero version of David Fincher’s The Game." Manganiello was cast as Slade Wilson/Deathstroke, the movie's primary villain. Deathstroke would be on a mission to “dismantle Batman’s entire life” after being led to believe The Dark Knight murdered his son. The film would have culminated in a "climactic one-on-one showdown through the streets of Gotham" inspired by the Arkham Asylum video games. Manganiello said:

"It sounded good to me. I told Ben how great I thought his films were, especially The Town, and how I was really looking forward to seeing his Best Picture version of a Batman movie.”

"It sounded good to me. I told Ben how great I thought his films were, especially The Town, and how I was really looking forward to seeing his Best Picture version of a Batman movie.”

In 2021, Manganiello alluded that Affleck's Batman film would have seen Deathstroke kill a key member of Batman's supporting cast but did not specify who. Now the actor reveals Deathstroke would have killed Commissioner Gordon (J.K. Simmons) and taken Alfred Pennyworth (Jeremy Irons) hostage. Manganiello also hints that Commissioner Gordon's death would have inspired his daughter, Barbara Gordon, to take up the mantle of Batgirl in the DCEU. The Magic Mike actor teased that "Deathstroke would nearly kill both Batman and Batgirl before they would both escape via an aboveground train."

Affleck signed on to write and direct a solo Batman film in 2013, after being cast as the Caped Crusader in Batman v. Superman: Dawn of Justice and Justice League. However, after the Justice League reshoots, which Affleck called "the low point" of his career, Affleck dropped out of directing a new Batman movie in January 2017. He ultimately dropped out of starring in the project altogether in 2019, setting the stage for Matt Reeves' The Batman, starring Robert Pattinson. Affleck would later reprise his role as Bruce Wayne / Batman in 2023's The Flash.

Joe Manganiello was cast as Deathstroke in Affleck's Batman, with a post-credit Justice League scene shot to set up that film. When Affleck's Batman project fell through, the post-credit scene was reworked to position Deathstroke as a potential member of the Legion of Doom in a Justice League sequel that never materialized. In his memoir, Manganiello revealed that Warner Bros. considered him for both Aquaman and the Lost Kingdom and a Suicide Squad sequel. Ultimately he didn't appear in either title.

In the end, Manganiello appeared in only one other DC project, Zack Snyder's Justice League. A new Deathstroke project is now being developed for James Gunn's DCU. The movie, reportedly titled Savage Hunters, will be a team-up project between Deathstroke and Bane. Manganiello is not expected to reprise his role in the DCU.
