Heat 2: Christian Bale Explains Why His Role Is Much Deeper Than an Al Pacino Impression - AOL

TheTools · 08.11.2026 00:25 · source · permalink

Christian Bale says he plans to bring his own interpretation to Al Pacino’s iconic character. Speaking about his role in Heat 2, the actor discussed how he plans to approach the role.

AP Entertainment recently interviewed Bale and asked whether he would imitate Al Pacino’s performance. The Dark Knight Rises star begged to differ and said, “No, I’ve got to make it my own. With all due respect, you know, he’s absolutely brilliant in what he did, and I’m watching it obsessively, of course, but I’ve got to figure out my way to do it, to make it my own thing.”

Bale will be playing Vincent Hanna in the movie. Pacino originally portrayed Hanna’s character in 1995’s Heat. Apart from Bale, the project is expected to feature Stephen Graham as Neil McCauley, Leonardo DiCaprio as Chris Shiherlis, Odessa Young as Charlene Shiherlis, Adam Driver as Otis Wardell, and Jason Clarke in an unknown role.

What else do we know about Heat 2 so far?

The narrative for Heat 2 serves as both a prequel and sequel to the 1995 crime film. The prequel plot will feature Neil McCauley, Chris Shiherlis, and Michael Cerrito as they execute high-stakes heists. A plot twist occurs when an ambush fails to capture Otis Wardell’s crew. Hanna resigns from the department after Aaron is dismembered. The sequel portion will reportedly follow the aftermath of Heat.

The summary for Heat 2 reads, “1988, Chicago: McCauley and his gang carry out robberies on the West Coast and the Mexico-USA border. Detective Hanna kills McCauley in a confrontation at LAX. Years later, in LA, Hanna hunts down Chris Shiherlis, the group’s last survivor.”

Michael Mann directs Heat 2. Based on the novel of the same name by Meg Gardiner and Michael Mann, the movie is produced by the director himself. Jan Pance, Jerry Bruckheimer, Art Linson, Nick Nesbitt, and Scott Stuber join Mann as producers. Eric Roth and Shane Salerno are the executive producers for the film.

Heat 2 has not yet received an official release date.
