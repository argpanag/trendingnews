Terriers Look to Continue Strong Start at Northwestern on Monday (BTN)

November 8, 2026 | Men's Basketball


TERRIER TIDBITS

  • Highlighted by a career-high 29 points in Friday's win at Brown, senior Kyrone Alexander is averaging 24.0 points per game in the early stages of the season.
  • The Terriers trailed 52-38 with under 14 minutes remaining in regulation at Brown but outscored the Bears 34-15 for the remainder of the contest to emerge victorious in its 2026-27 road opener.
  • Junior Michelanglo Oberti, a transfer from Penn, posted a double-double against the Bears with 12 points and a career-high 10 rebounds. 
  • BU opened its campaign with a 93-85 home victory over Northeastern on Nov. 2.
  • The Terriers shot over 54 percent from the field while leading by as many as 17 points during the contest.
  • Junior guard Azmar Abdullah paced five Terriers in double digits with 21 points against the Huskies. It marked his fourth career game with 20-plus points.
  • The Terriers are 2-0 to start a season for the first time since the 2013-14 campaign.

SCOUTING NORTHWESTERN

  • The Wildcats opened the season with home wins over Binghamton (86-60) and Stony Brook (78-65).
  • Monday marks the third game of a season-opening five-game homestand.
  • Northwestern was picked 17th in the Big Ten Preseason Media Poll.
  • Chris Collins is in his 15th season as head coach, having guided the Wildcats to three NCAA tournaments (2017, 2023, 2024).
  • This year's roster features 11 newcomers, including eight transfers. 
  • Sophomore Jake West is the lone returning starter from last season and is averaging 21.5 ppg through two games.
  • Senior Jack Karasinski, a transfer from Bellarmine, had 24 points in the opener against Binghamton.
  • The Wildcats are shooting 45.3% from 3-point range over the first two games while limiting opponents to 25.5% shooting from beyond the arc.

THE SERIES

  • Last year's contest - a 76-52 Northwestern win -  was just the second all-time meeting and the first since Dec. 19, 1959.
  • The Wildcats earned a 78-74 home win in the inaugural meeting 67 years ago.
  • The Terriers will return home to face Johnson & Wales on Thursday (Nov. 12).
  • Game time at Case Gym is set for 6 p.m.
