Maurizio Sarri's Atalanta visit Ivan Juric's Monza at the Stadio Brianteo in the 11th round of Serie A fixtures. They arrive in Brianza needing to turn around a run of form that has taken a worrying turn in recent weeks.

Monza have one win, no draws and four defeats from their last five league games. A 2-1 defeat to Bologna followed a heavy 4-1 loss to Napoli. Their one bright spot in that spell was a 4-0 win over Cagliari. Across the five matches, Monza scored 11 goals and conceded 15.

Across all competitions, Atalanta have no wins, two draws and three defeats in their last five outings. A 1-1 draw against Kairat Almaty in the Conference League followed a 3-0 defeat to Parma in Serie A. Atalanta have scored nine goals and conceded 12 in those five matches. Their 4-4 draw against Frosinone and 2-2 draw against Fiorentina show just how vulnerable they have been at the back.

Head-to-head record

The teams last met in May 2025, when Atalanta won 4-0 away at the Stadio Brianteo in Serie A. Across their last five competitive meetings, the record favours the Bergamo side: four Atalanta wins to one for Monza, with Atalanta scoring 12 goals and conceding only two in the league encounters. Monza's only win in the dataset came in a friendly in August 2025, which they won 2-1.
