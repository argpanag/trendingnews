Ukrainian Drones Hunt Down Russian Military Supply Trucks in Occupied Ukraine, Video

TheTools · 06.11.2026 17:57 · source · permalink

Ukrainian drone operators from the Signum Battalion of the 59th Separate Assault Brigade have carried out a series of strikes against Russian military supply trucks operating in occupied areas of eastern and southern Ukraine.

On November 3, the battalion released footage showing Ukrainian drones pursuing and striking Russian cargo vehicles transporting supplies to frontline units.

The video, published on the battalion’s official Telegram channel, documents multiple engagements involving trucks traveling along roads in Russian-occupied territory.

Signum personnel said Russian truck drivers have reportedly become increasingly reluctant to undertake supply missions because of the threat posed by Ukrainian drones. However, Russian forces continue to use road transport to deliver military equipment and supplies.

The battalion emphasized that targeting logistics vehicles is intended to disrupt Russian military operations by reducing the availability of transport and essential resources.

Earlier, on October 27, Signum drone operators destroyed a Russian military convoy consisting of three Ural trucks carrying ammunition and other supplies, along with two fuel tankers.

The convoy was escorted by pickup trucks equipped with mobile fire teams intended to protect the vehicles from Ukrainian drones. However, footage released by the battalion showed Russian personnel abandoning their positions and scattering as FPV drones approached, leaving the supply vehicles exposed to further strikes.
