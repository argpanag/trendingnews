Trump official puts Susan Dell to shame with face that 'doesn't look real'

TheTools · 14.10.2026 14:38 · source · permalink

A new official has been appointed to serve the U.S. government, but audiences online are commenting on more than just her new title

Just days after Susan Dell became the focus of online discourse, another figure linked to the White House is capturing audiences' attention for similar reasons.

The newly appointed State Department anti-trafficking director is the subject of viral social media posts about her appearance after another official posted about her new position.

The State Department's new anti-trafficking director, Barbera Thornhill, is 78 years old, and a post made by Assistant Secretary Riley Barnes with her official government picture drew in comments on her looks.

Newly appointed official

A new government official was appointed this Tuesday (13) as the State Department's anti-trafficking director - a decision that went viral online. But commenters on social media are focusing on her appearance rather than her new position.

Barbera Thornhill was announced as a new White House official in a post by Assistant Secretary Riley Barnes, who noted that she now directs the State Department's Office to Monitor and Combat Trafficking in Persons and holds the title of ambassador-at-large.

The 78-year-old has not commented on the repercussions of her new official picture.

Discourse about her appearance

"Ambassador Thornhill, welcome to the team," Barnes wrote. "I'm proud to have such a dedicated leader join us as Ambassador-at-Large to Monitor and Combat Trafficking in Persons."

He added that human trafficking is "a grave human rights issue and a transnational crime exploiting men, women, and children for profit, and that "we will continue to go after traffickers, protect survivors, and deliver justice for the American people."

Comments online

But comments online focused less on her new role and more on Barbera's appearance in the new official picture.. One of the replies to the original post read: "I had to check and see if this was a satire account."

Other users joked: "Are they planning to scare the traffickers to death?" "Jeez, put a trigger warning next time," and "official government accounts should not be posting obviously AI-generated images."

Another was more sympathetic, saying "bro… Why would y'all do this to her?"

Her career and past experience

Thornhill is a business executive and philanthropist who led the commercial interior design firm Impact Design.

On her nomination hearing for the new role she was appointed for, she told the Senate Foreign Relations Committee that she lived and worked alongside Mother Teresa's Missionaries of Charity in Calcutta in 1994.

Pictures of her from the past

After that, she also served as a secretary of the board of the Children's Institute of Los Angeles, which supports families recovering from trauma and abuse, and has worked with Scouts BSA.

In her new role, she will lead the U.S. global strategy against modern slavery and forced labor. She will also oversee the annual Trafficking in Persons Report and manage victim protection programs as part of her department's responsibilities.

Comparisons to Susan Dell

In her official photo, one of the most popular comments from social media users joked, "Is she related to Susan Dell by any chance?"

That was in reference to 62-year-old Susan Dell, whose appearance was also the main point of viral posts last week, after she joined her husband - Dell Technologies chairman Michael Dell - in a visit to Donald Trump in the Oval Office on October 7, to mark their $6.25 billion contribution to "help 25 million children start building a strong financial foundation."

Comments about Susan Dell

Talking about the impact the donation had on children, Susan told trump they had received "beautiful letters" from parents, children and grandparents.

"It's not just the money that matters... Knowing someone believes in you and your future that matters just as much," she said.

The official response

But the visit went viral online after users started joking about her appearance.

Some users labeled the clip of her talking "one of the weirdest things" and asked what was wrong with her face. Others called her an "alien."

Michael Dell responded by resharing posts defending his wife. One read: "My grandson got 250 dollars in his Trump account from Susan Dell. Please leave that woman alone."

The MAGA look

Both of the women are, according to some online critics, examples of people that sport the 'MAGA look' - or Mar-a-Lago face.

People considered to have the MAGA look are often styled in a hyper-polished, deliberately stylized aesthetic often involving, for women, heavy, dramatic makeup in bold colors, long, wavy hair, and form-fitting dresses. Sometimes, the styling is also paired with noticeable plastic surgery or non-surgical enhancement procedures.

The look is mostly seen among prominent figures and insiders in Trump's political circle, and among supporters.

Neither Susan Dell nor Barbera Thornhill are confirmed to have gone through any cosmetic procedures or surgeries.
