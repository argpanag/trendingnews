iHeartMedia New York Promotes Dellilo to Marketing Director - Radio Ink

TheTools · 11.11.2026 15:55 · source · permalink
  • News
  • People in the News

From Jingle Ball to Skating in Central Park, Danielle Dellilo has spent years behind the events and promotions that put iHeartMedia’s 106.7 Lite FM (WLTW) and 103.5 KTU (WKTU) in front of the public. Now she is taking that work market-wide.

iHeart New York has named Dellilo Director of Marketing and Promotions, overseeing marketing and promotions across the market’s seven stations.

Dellilo has spent more than 10 years at iHeartMedia and most recently served as Promotions Director, leading public relations, artist relations, and promotional initiatives for 106.7 LITE FM (WLTW) and 103.5 KTU (WKTU).

iHeart New York EVP of Programming Thea Mitchem said, “We’re incredibly excited to have Danielle step into this role. Her leadership, infectious enthusiasm and ability to make things happen have made her a go-to for our teams, talent and sales partners. I’m incredibly proud of the leader she has become over the past 10 years. Danielle has earned this opportunity, and we’re thrilled to have her leading marketing and promotions for our legendary New York brands and talent,”

Dellilo added, “I am incredibly honored and proud to step into this new role as Director of Marketing and Promotions for iHeartMedia New York. This moment is especially meaningful to me because it represents years of hard work, dedication, and a genuine love for radio. I’ve been fortunate to build a career doing what I’m passionate about, surrounded by incredibly talented people who inspire me every day. I’m excited to continue to build on our success, embrace new opportunities, and bring even bigger ideas to life.”
