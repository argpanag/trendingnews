Santos aim to end fifth away taboo at Fluminense

Santos visit Fluminense at the Maracanã on Sunday in the Brasileirão’s 31st round, aiming to end a fifth away taboo this season. Kick-off is at 16:00 Brasília time.

Globo.com highlights the backdrop to this fixture. Santos have not beaten Fluminense away since 2018. In six meetings they have four defeats and two draws, scoring two and conceding seven. Their last win was 1-0 in round 12 of 2018, Bruno Henrique on target.

OneFootball Videos

Two players from that game are expected to be involved, Gabigol and centre-back Luan Peres, who then played for Fluminense. The last Maracanã meeting in last year’s league finished 1-0 to Fluminense, Samuel Xavier scoring.

Santos have already snapped four away droughts in this Brasileirão. They beat Corinthians 1-0 at Neo Química Arena, ending a 15-year league wait, Christian Oliva scoring. They then won 3-2 at Internacional’s Beira-Rio, Gabigol netting twice and Oliva adding another, their first win there since 2008.

Next came Remo at the Mangueirão in Belém, a 2-1 victory through Rony and Gabigol. That result secured a fourth straight away win in the league at the time, a feat last managed in 1984. The latest taboo to fall was at São Paulo’s Morumbis, where a comeback 2-1 win ended a run dating to 2020, Gabigol scored both and Luciano replied.
