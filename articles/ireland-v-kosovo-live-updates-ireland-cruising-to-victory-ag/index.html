Here is the under-21s preview from Gavin Cummiskey:

Despite trailing 1-0 after a snow-hit first leg in Tampere last Friday, Ireland have the quality to turn it around in the return leg and reach their first European Under-21 Championship finals.

Jim Crawford has had three full campaigns to qualify for the European Championships since he replaced Stephen Kenny as under-21 manager in 2020. The best chance before this match at Turners Cross came in 2022, when Ireland lost a playoff to Israel on penalties in Tel Aviv.

Qualification for the Euros is not the only method of assessing Crawford’s six years in charge. It would help, of course, but consistently matching nations like Norway, Italy and England before supplying the senior squad with well-rounded professionals is his primary aim.

Hello and welcome to live coverage of a bumper day of Irish football action, with the Republic of Ireland under-21s and senior teams playing in important matches. First of all, it’s the under-21s, who play Finland in a European Championships playoff at 5.45pm at Turners Cross. They are 1-0 down from the second leg, but well capable of doing the business of overturning that at home, with it would be a place in the European championship for the first time in their history.

Then attention will turn to the Aviva Stadium and the Nations League where Ireland play Kosovo at 7.45pm. A win is needed there to finish second in the group and qualify for the promotion playoffs with a League A team next March. It would also lead to them being second seeds when the Euros qualification draw is made on December 6th.

If there is extra time and penalties in the under-21s, there will be some overlap between the two games so bear with us, but it could be a special night if everything goes to plan.
