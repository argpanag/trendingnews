Another NCEA English online exam hit by technical problems

TheTools · 17.11.2026 21:40 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Education

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._v0.KrRTFtoPsaA8nSDloPZ4KejQJr59ANQpD9ULgGwzaM0/education/2638743%2Fanother-ncea-english-online-exam-hit-by-technical-problems

Teachers are reporting further problems with online NCEA exams.

Several teachers have told RNZ some students had difficulty logging into this morning's NCEA level 1 English exam and others abandoned it and completed the paper version instead.

The head of the English teachers association, Pip Tinning, said the glitch, following similar problems with last week's level 2 English exam, was "absolutely and utterly unacceptable".

"We cannot keep doing this to our young people," she said.

She said at her school, Pāpāmoa College, the exam seemed to have the same issues as last week.

"At my school we had students it was glitching for. They managed to refresh and get it back up and running, but I have colleagues in different parts of the country who've had students who've had to switch from digital to paper," she said.

Tinning said exam centre managers were better prepared to cope than last week because NZQA had sent schools paper copies of the exam in case there was another failure.

"However, it sounds like this time as well we had students who ran out of time because they may have been kicked off for like 15 minutes," she said.

The recurring problems would shake teachers' and students' confidence in the online system, she said.

"There'll be a number of schools and a number of teachers who just have no faith in that system and will look at going back to paper."

Tinning said there were problems last year with the level 1 exam, and NZQA had assured schools at the time it would be fixed.

NZQA has been asked for comment.

It said there was extensive testing of the exam system over the weekend and NZQA's IT provider had assured it there would be no repeat of the problems that affected the level 2 English exam last week.
