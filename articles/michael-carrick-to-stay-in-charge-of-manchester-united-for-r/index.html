United 17th in Premier League after Chelsea defeat

No change expected until international break

Michael Carrick is set to remain in charge of Manchester United for Tuesday’s Champions League game against Roma at Old Trafford despite Saturday’s 2-0 loss at Chelsea.

The defeat means United have not won in nine games and have made their poorest start to a season since 1988, but Carrick’s position is expected to be reviewed again during the the next international break after the next round of Premier League matches, United hosting Aston Villa on Sunday.

The 45-year-old came close to losing his job last Monday when Jason Wilcox, and Omar Berrada, met at the training ground to discuss his future. The director of football and the chief executive decided Carrick should be given a chance to turn form and results around.

Carrick’s side are in 17th with seven points, above the relegation only on goal difference.

There remains a view within the hierarchy that Carrick can still transform fortunes, with United’s travelling support still backing him at the end of the defeat at Stamford Bridge on Saturday.

Afterthe game the head coach indicated that he expects to still be in position for the Roma game. He said: “We’ve got to move forward and win matches, it’s as simple as that.”

Eddie Howe is Wilcox’s preferred candidate should Carrick be sacked, with contact between the parties understood to have been made. The former Newcastle ­manager is open to taking up the position, ­subject to negotiations. He would cost around £3m in compensation to Newcastle.

Julian Nagelsmann, the ­former Germany and Bayern Munich head coach, is highly regarded by ­Christopher ­Vivell, United’s director of ­recruitment, who is believed to have sounded out his fellow ­German. The pair previously worked together at RB Leipzig.
