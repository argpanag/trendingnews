What we learned as Brock Purdy. Christian McCaffrey, 49ers move to 6-0 after holding off Commanders

TheTools · 20.10.2026 04:07 · source · permalink

Brock Purdy threw one touchdown pass and Christian McCaffrey ran for two more as the San Francisco 49ers prevailed over the Washington Commanders.

BOX SCORE

The 49ers played with their food and almost made a mess before holding off the Washington Commanders, 23-17, on Monday night at Levi's Stadium.

San Francisco improves to 6-0 and now leads the Seattle Seahawks by three games in the NFC West.

Running back Christian McCaffrey rushed for 64 yards and two touchdowns on 16 carries.

Tight end George Kittle caught five passes for 69 yards and one touchdown.

The news wasn't all good for the 49ers, as star wide receiver Mike Evans left the game late in the first half with an Achilles injury. Before exiting, he caught three passes for 94 yards.

The 49ers take their undefeated record to Atlanta next week to face Bijan Robinson and the Falcons at Mercedes-Benz Stadium.

Here are Matt Maiocco's takeaways from the 49ers' latest win:

Purdy ranks No. 1 all-time

Purdy entered the game with 1,495 pass attempts, just five shy of qualifying for the NFL’s all-time lead in passer rating.

And after six drop-backs on the 49ers’ first drive, Purdy completed five passes for 51 yards, including an 11-yard touchdown pass to Kittle.

Purdy had a 105.4 passer rating when the evening began. He vaulted over Baltimore Ravens quarterback Lamar Jackson, who previously was atop the all-time list with a 102.7 rating.

Purdy had it all working on Monday night, as he continues to take his game to greater heights with his ability to spread the ball around to different targets at all levels of the field.

Rookie defenders make a difference

The Commanders couldn't capitalize on a second-quarter takeaway, and it was a team effort from the 49ers’ rookies that forced a three-and-out.

Defensive tackle Gracen Halton dropped Daniels for a sack on first down. Linebacker Jaden Dugger made a stop after a 6-yard gain on a second-and-22 play. On third down, Height hit Daniels as he was throwing, resulting in only a short gain, setting up a Commanders punt.

First-year player Sebastian Valdez also made a huge contribution as part of the 49ers’ defensive line rotation.

Linebacker Fred Warner is the star in the middle of the 49ers’ defense, and the young supporting cast has continued to play well in defensive coordinator Raheem Morris’ scheme.

McCaffrey gets his touches

The 49ers managed McCaffrey's workload through the first five games of the season, but on Monday, he was the offensive focal point as a runner and receiver.

McCaffrey caught all six of his targets for 38 yards while carrying the ball 10 times for 44 yards and two touchdowns.

And that was in the first half alone, as the 49ers rolled to a 20-3 lead at halftime. San Francisco had 303 yards of total offense in the first 30 minutes.

McCaffrey had one setback in the first half when he lost a fumble, his first in 513 touches, dating back to Week 12 of the 2024 season. But he picked right up where he left off after that giveaway.
