HT: United 2 Brighton 1

Plenty of entertainment for the paying punters at Old Trafford, with Shea Lacey and Mason Mount scoring twice in two minutes to put United in a commanding position. Given the JJ Gabriel story, the home fans enjoyed seeing a 19-year-old kiss the badge after scoring on his full debut.

Brighton have probably been the better team in general play though, and scored a deserved goal on the stroke of half-time.

United’s second-string back four looking very shaky, understandably.

GOOOAALL! Kostoulas steals in for Brighton

Carrick and United wanted a foul on Sesko on halfway, but the referee said nothing doing. Hadjam then floats a diagonal pass over the head of Heaven to find the run of Kostoulas, but goalkeeper Darlow surely had the chance to come off his line and claim? Passive goalkeeping, and Kostoulas slips the ball under Darlow into the far corner. Game on.

43 minutes: United 2 Brighton 0

Chema Andres, signed by Brighton from Stuttgart on deadline day and known by the moniker “Baby Rodri”, has just been booked for a reckless challenge in midfield. Then Rashford shows he still has plenty in the locker with a touch and shimmy to beat Costinha.

40 minutes: United 2 Brighton 0

Tielemans with an important challenge on Kostoulas, after more unconvincing United involving Dalot misjudging a bouncing ball. Then Kostoulas fizzes a cross all the way through the six-yard box and Gross fires over at the back post. Brighton have had the chances to be level.

36 minutes: United 2 Brighton 0

Yalcouye was very dangerous for Brighton against Coventry, and he is proving difficult to contain again. Gross’s through ball releases him into space behind Dalot, but his side-footed shot was shoved wide of the post. He tried to “Henry” one around Darlow with his right instep and failed to find the curl.

33 minutes: United 2 Brighton 0

Clean hit from almost 35 yards from Boscagli, but his left-footed attempt whistles just over the bar. Yoro heads away a temping Gross cross when Brighton had three blue shirts in promising positions. Rashford then puts the ball through the legs of Chema Andres, and United have a corner.

30 minutes: United 2 Brighton 0

Lacey goes down hoping for free-kick, anticipating the contact from Gross but it was not forthcoming. Since 2013-14, United have been 2-0 up at Old Trafford 145 times, winning 143 and drawing just two.

27 minutes: United 2 Brighton 0

Brighton well on top now, and Ayden Heaven goes to ground hoping for a free-kick but the referee is having none of it. O’Riley’s cross is turned behind for a corner, which United scramble away. Feels like a Brighton goal is on the way, despite the comfortable scoreline for United.

25 minutes: United 2 Brighton 0

Brighton’s corner swung in from the right was far too deep and loopy, drifting harmlessly behind for a United goal kick. Brighton are soon back on the front foot, and Gross picks out Kostoulas in the middle but the forward’s touch let him down. Edgy defending from Yoro but United clear their lines eventually.

23 minutes: United 2 Brighton 0

Mazraoui is now back on the pitch but he does not look 100 per cent. Brighton enjoying some long passages of possession now, but you sense United are happy to drop off and let them have the ball. More promising build-up play ends with Mazraoui heading a cross over the bar for a Brighton corner.
