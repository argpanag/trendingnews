Gehlee forgives Calix, clears the air after PBB Collab 3.0 misunderstanding

TheTools · 05.11.2026 01:57 · source · permalink

PBB Collab 3.0 housemates Gehlee and Calix closes issue between them

GMA Network

A heartfelt moment of understanding and forgiveness unfolded inside the Pinoy Big Brother: Collab Edition 3.0 house as housemates Gehlee Dangca and Calix finally addressed the misunderstanding that had drawn attention among viewers.

In an emotional conversation, Calix personally apologized to Gehlee, admitting that he was sorry for what he had done and stressing that he never intended to hurt her.

“Sorry dahil nagawa ko ’yun. Wala akong other intention doon. Sorry talaga, hindi ko sinasadya,” Calix told her.

Gehlee, meanwhile, responded with maturity and grace, assuring him that she understood his apology and was simply happy that he had become aware of his actions.

“I understand. It’s okay. I’m just happy that you’re aware,” she said.

The conversation ended on a touching note when Gehlee reached out to Calix and told him the words that brought their reconciliation full circle: “I forgive you.”

What makes the moment even more meaningful is Gehlee’s clarification that her disappointment was never directed at Calix as a person, but at the particular action that had upset her.

According to the sentiment shared about their reconciliation, the issue was also addressed through conversations with Kuya and Calix rather than being turned into gossip or discussed behind his back.

Gehlee’s response showed that forgiveness does not mean dismissing what happened. Instead, it means recognizing a mistake, allowing someone to acknowledge it, and choosing to move forward.

In a reality show where emotions, misunderstandings and personal differences are part of everyday life, Gehlee and Calix’s reconciliation served as a reminder that honest communication and accountability can make a difference.

More than anything, Gehlee earned praise for showing that one can stand up for what is right while still choosing kindness and forgiveness.
