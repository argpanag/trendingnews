Julianne Hough Dating Peloton Instructor Adrian Williams

TheTools · 23.11.2026 22:58 · source · permalink

Dancing With the Stars co-host Julianne Hough is dating Peloton instructor Adrian Williams, just a few months after teasing on Live From E!: 2026 Emmys that she may not be dancing solo.

Julianne Hough is giving dating another waltz.

The Dancing With the Stars co-host is dating Peloton instructor Adrian Williams, a source confirmed to E! News.

The fitness instructor, 42, has already proved they're a winning pair by sharing a sweet glimpse into their bond on social media, posting a snap of Hough holding her dog Sunny on his Instagram Story Nov. 22, which she reshared.

E! News has reached out to reps for Hough and Williams for comment and has not yet heard back.

Though Hough has kept much of her love life private since she and Brooks Laich finalized their divorce in 2022, she did reveal that a new man might be lacing up his ballroom shoes.

"I might not be single," she teased E!'s Justin Sylvester at the 2026 Emmys in September. "There's a lot to discuss off-camera."

And the 38-year-old—who split from the former Washington Capitals player in 2020 after nearly three years of marriage—had previously spelled out what she's looking for in a partner.

"It's all energy-based," she told E! News in December 2025. "I don't really have a type. It's more connection, and that's it."

The former dance pro also knows that, when it comes to blossoming romances, it takes two to tango. As she put it, "I'm always looking for love, whether it's in a romantic sense or friends. Always."

As Hough cha-cha'd through her single era, she revealed her plan was pretty simple: Freestyle her love life.

"I've been very intentional about what I'm putting out and what I'm excited to attract and hopefully be attractive to," she explained to E!, "but I will say that I kind of just threw that out the window."

The attitude proved successful, as she shared how much she loved rediscovering herself.

"It brought me back to the joyful, playful side, versus being so serious," she reflected. "Sometimes, when you're intentional, you can get a little bit tunnel vision, and so, I kind of was like, 'You know what? I'm just gonna be playful and fun.'"

But she does have one caveat. Simply put, she's looking for "someone super hot."

As Hough and Williams explore this new chapter, keep reading for more Dancing With the Stars celebs and their partners IRL.

Jenna Johnson & Val Chmerkovskiy

Jenna and Val didn't waltz into a relationship right away. 

When she joined Dancing With the Stars in 2014 and they were paired together for a routine, "We had physical chemistry," he recalled on The Squeeze in 2025. "I liked her energy and spirit. And then throughout the season, we kind of entertained that attraction."

After initially trying to resist the sparks, Jenna and Val dated on and off for a few years. They got engaged in 2018 and tied the knot the following year—growing their family with son Rome in 2023.

"As much as we tried to fight it," Jenna said on The Morning After With Kelly Stratford in 2025, "we were drawn to each other always."

Daniella Karagach & Pasha Pashkov

Looking for a new dance partner in 2008, Pasha's coach suggested he team up with Daniella. 

Initially hesitant due to their six-and-a-half-year age gap, he recalled to DancesportInfo in 2017, "<y coach said: 'Doesn't matter, she is a right partner for you,' and that was it."

And she was in every sense of the word. Five years after their 2014 wedding, Pasha joined DWTS with Daniella entering the ballroom the following year.

In 2023, they stepped into parenthood with the birth of daughter Nikita.

"I married my best friend," Daniella told The Knot in 2025 after she and Pasha renewed their vows. "He's someone I can turn to for anything because he's my number one fan, my biggest supporter, and vice versa."

Emma Slater & Alan Bersten

After her four-year marriage to DWTS' Sasha Farber ended in 2022, Emma formed a romance with fellow pro Alan. However, the move wasn't a quickstep. While Emma and Alan sparked romance rumors in 2024, they didn't go public with their relationship until they kissed on stage during the 2025 DWTS Tour.

"We've always been really good friends, and now we have a great relationship," she told E! News in June 2026. "It feels really good."

Britt Stewart & Daniel Durant

Scoring a perfect 10 in their love story, Britt and actor Daniel competed together on DWTS in 2022. After they were eliminated, they wondered if their feelings would remain outside of the show and slowly began dating.

"I wanted to make sure that it was as real as what we were feeling," Britt explained on The Joe Vulpis Podcast in December 2025, "and it was."

The dancer and the CODA star got engaged in 2023 and tied the knot two years later.

"I love you," Britt told Daniel while signing their vows in a video from their wedding day. "I think my soul loved you before I met you."

Adele Zaikman & Allen Genkin

Called the Rolls Royce, Adele cruised away from 2026's DWTS: The Next Pro with the win and a new relationship with fellow contestant Allen. 

"When I got to know Allen on The Next Pro, I immediately felt that he was one of the kindest souls I’d ever met," she told E! News in October 2026. “Having such a strong bond and friendship first makes our relationship now feel so right.”

Brandon Armstrong & Brylee Armstrong

While fans have seen Brandon glide across the ballroom, he met Brylee by sliding into her DMs.

"I said, 'Hey, how are you?' he recalled to Joe Vulpis in 2024, "and she ghosted me."

After her mom convinced her to give him a chance, Brylee and Brandon started dating in 2021 and he proposed the following year with a custom movie trailer. Months later, they tied the knot.

"If y’all knew Bry the way I do," Brandon wrote on Instagram in May 2026, "your life would be 100 times better."

Rylee Arnold & Walker Lyons

Collegiate football player Walker has been Rylee's teammate since 2024.

"His aunt was my Uber driver in L.A., which was crazy," the dancer told It Girl in September 2026. "I got in her car, was talking to her, and she was like, 'I have a nephew who's playing football at USC. You should reach out to him.'"

After Rylee followed the tight-end, later suiting up for Utah's BYU, on Instagram, "He messaged me,” she continued, “and then the rest is history." 

Sharna Burgess & Brian Austin Green

Introduced by their mutual manager, Sharna and Brian met in 2020 following his split from Megan Fox.

And while they didn't nab the mirrorball trophy while competing the following year, they scored a bigger prize, welcoming son Zane in 2022. 

Two years later, Brian and Sharna got engaged.

"I’m so grateful I get to do life with you," she wrote in a 2024 birthday tribute to the Beverly Hills, 90210 alum, also dad to kids Noah, Bodhi and Journey from his former marriage to Megan as well as son Kassius from past relationship with Vanessa Marcil. "Us, our tribe, our adventures past present and future… it’s all everything I’ve ever wanted."

Mark Ballas & BC Jean

In a meet-cute that’s music to fans’ ears, Mark and BC told Entertainment Tonight they met in 2012 at a friend's house, where they were both performing for a live-stream event.

Marrying in 2016, the singer and the dancer collaborate both in life—as parents to son Banksi—and in work, teaming up for their band Alexander Jean.

Calling BC’s love and support "unmatched," Mark wrote in a 2025 Instagram post, "You're the best wife & friend a man could ask for." 

Witney Carson & Carson McAllister

High school sweethearts Witney Carson and Carson McAllister tied the knot in 2016. Their son Leo was born in 2021, and his brother Jet arrived two years later. In 2026, Witney and Carson announced they're expecting their third baby boy.

Calling Carson her "romance novel husband," Witney wrote in a June 2026 Instagram post, "I love you so much."
