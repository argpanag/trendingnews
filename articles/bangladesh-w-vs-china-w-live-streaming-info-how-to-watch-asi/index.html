Bangladesh women’s team comes to this tournament after exiting in the semifinals in the Asia Cup. | Photo Credit: Getty Images

The bronze-medal winner from 2023, Bangladesh, will kick-off its Asian Games 2026 campaign in the quarterfinals against China on Thursday.

The Nigar Sultana-led side made it to the semifinals of the recently-concluded Asia Cup in the UAE, where it lost to eventual champion India.

If Bangladesh wins on Thursday, it will once again face India in the semifinals, should it beat Japan in the last-eight fixture.

Where will the Bangladesh women vs China women Asian Games 2026 match be played?

The Bangladesh women vs China women Asian Games 2026 match will be played at the Korogi Park in Nisshin.

At what time will the Bangladesh women vs China women Asian Games 2026 match start?

The Bangladesh women vs China women Asian Games 2026 match will start at 9:00am local time, 5:30am IST or 6am BST. The toss will take place 30 minutes before the start of the game.

Where to watch the live streaming of the Bangladesh women vs China women Asian Games 2026 match?

The Bangladesh women vs China women Asian Games 2026 match can be live streamed on the SonyLIV mobile app and website in India. The game will be live streamed in Bangladesh on Akash Go.
