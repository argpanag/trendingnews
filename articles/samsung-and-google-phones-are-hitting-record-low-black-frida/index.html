Samsung and Google phones are hitting record-low Black Friday prices

TheTools · 26.11.2026 04:49 · source · permalink

Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra proves to be a worthy follow-up to the successful Fold 7. It adds a faster Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy chipset, brighter displays, and a new 50MP ultrawide lens. It’s the first Samsung foldable with a silicon-carbon battery, and it now matches the Galaxy S26 Ultra's 5,000mAh capacity. Charging speeds are faster, too. Without IP68 durability and magnetic Qi2 support, there are reasons to pick another foldable instead. But if you want a thin, light, and powerful foldable for productivity, there’s no better option than the Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra.
