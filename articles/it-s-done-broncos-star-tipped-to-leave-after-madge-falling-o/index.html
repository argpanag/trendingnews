‘It’s done’: Broncos star tipped to leave after Madge falling out as powerhouse circles — Whispers

TheTools · 21.09.2026 04:43 · source · permalink

The Brisbane Broncos are set to be dealt a massive blow, with the club resigned to losing an international forward due to his complicated relationship with coach Michael Maguire.

One of 12 Broncos players set to hit the open market on November 1 next season is set to mark Jordan Riki’s last at the club as he considers his long-term future.

“The Broncos are growing increasingly sceptical that Jordan Riki will stay at the club beyond next season,” reporter Brent Read said on Triple M’s Sunday Sin Bin.

“He’s a November 1 guy and in the last year of his deal.

“On November 1, he will become available to speak to rival clubs and my understanding is the Broncos are concerned Jordan Riki may leave at the end of next season.”

The fresh reports come as his testy relationship with Maguire has been described as “oil and water”, with the two reportedly not seeing eye to eye.

“I think it’s done. I got told the Jordan Riki and coaching relationship is like oil and water. It doesn’t mix,” Ben Dobbin added.

“They’re resigned to the fact that he will be moving on. They are pretty confident that he will go somewhere else

It is understood that there were whispers of Riki linking up with the Melbourne Storm for the 2028 season, but both camps have denied it.

Meanwhile, the New Zealand Warriors have been floated up as a potential destination.

This would see him replace former teammate Kurt Capewell in the back row once the veteran decides to hang up his boots.

“All the mail I got was the Melbourne Storm, but that has been absolutely categorically denied by people at Melbourne and by the Broncos,” Dobbin said.

“The Broncos said we don’t think he’ll be here, but it won’t be at the Melbourne Storm.

“I think the Warriors are in for Jordan Riki to bring him home.”

LEADING AGENT’S INSIDE WORD ON PANTHERS STARS

One of the game’s leading player agents has good news for Penrith fans.

Steve Gillis, who has been managing NRL stars for more than 30 years, spoke recently on Fanatics TV and was asked about the status of a number of Panthers stars.

While he doesn’t manage them, he’s generally well-informed about the status of most star players in the game.

He was asked about the prospect of Penrith’s championship-winning roster being completely broken up by the tax-free incentives the PNG Chiefs will offer from November 1.

Gillis thinks Penrith will be able to fight off the Chiefs, and Nathan Cleary even recently indicated that he could get a deal done with the club before November 1.

“I think originally the feeling was that PNG was coming for them. They were talking Liam Martin, Mitch Kenny, Isaah Yeo, Nathan Cleary and Brian To’o,” Gillis said.

“Brian’s the only one to have gone thus far.

“The chatter within agent land is that Isaah Yeo’s on board and is staying. Also, I’m hearing that Nathan Cleary is leaning towards staying. It’s just chatter, it might be proved wrong.”

The Panthers have already suffered massively from expansion teams coming into the NRL.

Had the Dolphins not come into the league, Cleary would likely be playing alongside emerging superstar half Isaiya Katoa this weekend against the Knights in the preliminary final.

Instead, Katoa is pulling the strings at the Dolphins and is a good chance of bringing down the Panthers with all his skills on grand final day in a fortnight.

The Panthers have already lost top forwards Liam Henry and Scott Sorenson to the Perth Bears for 2027 as well as their current five-eighth Jack Cole.

But Gillis doesn’t think there will be too many more losses, certainly not significant ones.

“Mitch Kenny is staying. He’s signed on for a long-term deal so I don’t think they’re going to be decimated,” he said.

“I think Liam Martin would like to stay and if they can work a deal I think he will stay, unless someone comes along and has a chomp at him.

“They’re the whispers.”

Meanwhile, Gillis, who manages Roosters star halfback Sam Walker, set the record straight on Walker’s contract negotiations.

He wanted to clear one rumour up in particular.

He said his team didn’t leak the story that Walker’s negotiations had been put on hold while dispelling another rumour regarding Walker’s desire for a 10-year deal at the Roosters.

“That’s never, ever been discussed or requested,” Gillis said.

“I don’t know where that’s come from. It’s never come across our desk, regarding a 10-year tenure.”

Gillis also said Walker’s preference is to stay at the Roosters.

TITANS OWNER CONFIDENT ROOKIE WILL STAY

A part-owner of the Gold Coast Titans, Gorden Tallis is “confident” that rookie halfback Zane Harrison will remain at the club amid reports linking him to the PNG Chiefs.

One of three halfbacks the Chiefs are interested in - the other two being Nathan Cleary and Sam Walker - Harrison is set to test his value on the open market come November 1.

Despite playing only 11 NRL matches, he was extremely impressive throughout this year’s campaign and showed he is in for a long first-grade career.

This has led his management to ask the Titans for a deal worth around $900,000 a season, compared to the $600,000 a season they can currently offer due to salary cap constraints.

However, Tallis remains confident both parties will reach a deal and he will be at the Titans rather than the Chiefs in 2028.

“We will offer him a really good deal,” he said on Triple M’s Sunday Sin Bin.

“I’m confident that he’ll stay there. I think he’s a really good kid.

“We would be super confident that Zane Harrison is really happy at our club and where he is going.”
