Buccaneers' Josiah Trotter: Green light to play against Pittsburgh

TheTools · 18.10.2026 22:53 · source · permalink

Trotter (knee) is active against the Steelers on Sunday.

Trotter popped up on Friday's injury report as a limited participant due to a knee injury, but the rookie second-rounder has been cleared to play Sunday after going through pregame warmups. He has 40 tackles (22 solo), including 1.0 sacks, and a pick-six through four regular-season games.
