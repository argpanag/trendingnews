Brighton will be hoping to close in on the Premier League's top two when they host Keith Andrews' Brentford on Sunday.

Brentford boss Keith Andrews has urged his side to "bring our A game" when they face high-flying Brighton in the Premier League on Sunday.

The Bees ended a three-game winless run in the league (D2 L1) with a 2-0 victory over Nottingham Forest last time out.

Brentford have lost just one of their last 11 Premier League games (W3 D7), and after beating Forest, the Bees are looking to win consecutive league games for the first time since February.

However, Andrews knows his side are in for a tough task at the Amex, with only Arsenal (24) and Manchester City (21) picking up more points than Brighton (19) after nine games this season.

"I think they're playing really, really well," Andrews said of Brighton. "The way they're organised in every facet of the game is very impressive.

"They've clearly got really good spirit, they've recruited well, they've got an honesty around the group and they're clearly playing with a lot of confidence.

"We'll need to bring our A game on Sunday to get the result that we want.

"We've got the tools to cause them problems; we've got the tools to cause any team problems, but we'll need to play very well."

Along with their fine start to their league campaign this season, Brighton continued their unbeaten start in the Conference League with a 1-0 victory over Getafe on Thursday.

Fabian Hurzeler made five changes from the team that beat City in the league for their trip to Spain, and knows the Seagulls must be prepared for a physical battle against Brentford.

"It will be a very physical game and completely different from Getafe," said Hurzeler.

"We know it's a game of long balls. Brentford try to be very physical for the second ball and they have a clear style of play.

"We have to be ready for that physicality. Brentford are doing all these things at a really high level in the Premier League and they know how to win games."

PLAYERS TO WATCH

Brighton – Pascal Gross

Gross has created more chances than any other player in the Premier League this season (24), while only three players have registered more goal involvements than the German (seven – three goals, four assists).

Brentford – Igor Thiago

Igor Thiago has either scored (two) or assisted (two) a goal in each of his last four games in all competitions, his longest run as a Brentford player.

MATCH PREDICTION: BRIGHTON WIN

Brighton have never lost against Brentford in the Premier League at the Amex (W3 D2). The Seagulls have only faced Newcastle more often at home without losing (nine).

Brighton have scored the most goals in the Premier League this season (24), and also rank first for shot conversion rate (16.1%), second for expected goals (17.3) and third for total shots (149).

They have also kept a clean sheet in each of their last nine Premier League victories, including all six this season. The Seagulls are winless in their last seven games when conceding at least once (D2 L5).

And Brentford are the only side to have scored in every Premier League game this season. They have opened the scoring a league-high eight times this term, but have also failed to win the most games having scored first this term (D4 L1).

The Bees have lost their last two league games against Brighton, last losing three in a row between March 2010 and March 2011 in League One.
