After big game vs. 76ers, Paul George ruled out for Celtics-Raptors

TheTools · 11.11.2026 18:09 · source · permalink

Breaking News

Paul George will not play Wednesday as the Celtics visit Toronto on the second night of a back-to-back.

Boston ruled the veteran wing out with an illness ahead of its road matchup with the Raptors (7 p.m.).

George was the star of Tuesday’s statement win over the Philadelphia 76ers, scoring 36 points on 14-of-22 shooting against his former team. Fourteen of his points came in the fourth quarter as the Celtics turned a tie game midway through the third into a 123-101 rout.

It was the latest impressive performance in a terrific start to the season for George, who’s fit seamlessly into the Celtics’ system and locker room since arriving in the controversial Jaylen Brown trade this summer.

The 36-year-old has averaged 22.0 points, 5.2 rebounds and 3.8 assists per game while shooting 44.0% from 3-point range and leading the team in steals (21; seventh-most in the NBA) and deflections (49; third-most).

“I’ve been playing some of my best basketball of late and just been enjoying grinding out with these guys out here,” George said before Tuesday’s win. “It’s just a fun style of play. And again, I can’t talk (enough) about just the thrill and the happiness and excitement and joy that it has brought being here (in Boston).”

Wednesday’s DNP is George’s second of the season. He also sat out the second game of Boston’s previous back-to-back, a road loss to the Houston Rockets last Monday.

No other players were listed on the Celtics’ latest injury report, meaning they should have the rest of their roster available against the Raptors. Boston entered Wednesday with an Eastern Conference-leading 9-2 record and a four-game win streak.
