Chris Pratt’s Intense 157-Minute Political Thriller Is On A Tear On Streaming

TheTools · 14.09.2026 14:28 · source · permalink

Chris Pratt's intense, 157-minute political thriller based on a true story is tearing up streaming, growing into a massive success. Pratt's movies and TV shows are diverse in presentation, from the superhero exploits of the Guardians of the Galaxy trilogy to his iconic role in Parks and Recreation. He's also starred in a number of military-focused projects, including Prime Video's action thriller show The Terminal List. The TV universe is set to return with The Terminal List season 2 on October 21, 2026. But that's not the only military-based project of Pratt's noteworthy on streaming.

Another is Zero Dark Thirty, a political thriller released in 2012. The movie is based on the real manhunt for al-Qaeda leader Osama bin Laden following the September 11, 2001 terrorist attack in New York City. It focuses on the search and eventual killing of bin Laden from the perspective of multiple parties, most notably the CIA and the US Navy. Pratt portrays Justin Lenihan, a SEAL Team Six operator who becomes an integral part of the hunt for bin Laden. Reviews for Zero Dark Thirty were positive, and it earned five Oscar nominations.

Pratt's Zero Dark Thirty Is Back As A Major Streaming Hit

Now, according to data from September 14, 2026, Zero Dark Thirty has returned as a major streaming hit following its arrival on a new platform. The political thriller is Number 4 on Apple TV in the United States, putting it just behind The Dink, Troy, and Mayday on the Top 10 list. Its performance on the platform appears to only be getting stronger, with the film originally being Number 10 on September 13. Now, it's climbing, with renewed interest coming in the days following the 25th anniversary of the September 11 attacks.

The movie provides a balance of analytical and military lenses into the hunt for bin Laden, featuring CIA operatives portrayed by Jessica Chastain and Jason Clarke in the forefront. SEAL Team Six also has a number of noteworthy actors joining Pratt as team members, including Joel Edgerton, Mike Colter, and Frank Grillo. The military movie follows how both teams worked hand-in-hand to find and locate bin Laden, alongside a depiction of the operation itself. However, the movie wasn't without controversy for its subject matter.

The Political Thriller Was A Controversial Hit

Zero Dark Thirty was well-received and award-winning, while also earning $132.8 million against a reported $52.5 million budget. However, the movie was accused of being pro-torture by several US senators, including the late John McCain, who was kidnapped and tortured for six years during the Vietnam War. The Obama Administration also came under fire for their involvement in the film, with opponents saying they improperly allowed director Kathryn Bigelow access to classified intel. It was also criticized for using recordings from victims of the September 11 attacks without permission from surviving family members.

The level of controversy surrounding the movie has led it to become a polarizing film, especially in the modern era. But, despite how much controversial subject matter is attached to the movie, audiences in the US seem to admire Zero Dark Thirty enough to rewatch it during the weekend following the attacks' anniversary. Pratt's political thriller leaves an impression to this day, despite how much controversy still surrounds the way it was handled.
