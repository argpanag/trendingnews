Confirmed Liverpool line-up v AFC Bournemouth - Liverpool FC

TheTools · 20.09.2026 14:38 · source · permalink

Share

Liverpool make three changes from their last Premier League outing for today’s fixture at AFC Bournemouth.

Cody Gakpo, Milos Kerkez and Alexis Mac Allister come back into the Reds’ XI at Vitality Stadium, replacing Ryan Gravenberch, Victor Munoz and Kostas Tsimikas from last weekend’s draw with Fulham.

Liverpool: Alisson, Van Dijk, Jacquet, Kerkez, Wirtz, Szoboszlai, Isak, Mac Allister, Gakpo, Barcola, Araujo.

Subs: Mamardashvili, Gomez, Tsimikas, Munoz, Frimpong, Gravenberch, Nyoni, Koumas, Ngumoha.
