On November 9, Mirra Andreeva and Elina Svitolina will face off at the WTA Finals in Indian Wells. Here is my betting pick for the upcoming match.

Mirra Andreeva

Andreeva has performed at the highest level this season, winning tournaments in Linz and Adelaide. Her biggest achievement, however, was claiming her first Grand Slam title at Roland Garros. The 19-year-old achieved such remarkable success at a very early stage of her career.

The tennis player has been at her best on clay this year. On hard courts, she has recorded 27 wins in 38 matches. Andreeva last played in Beijing, where she reached the final before losing to the experienced Chinese player Zheng.

She will be making her debut at the WTA Finals, arriving as the world No. 3. Andreeva is physically powerful and possesses plenty of skill, but her mental game remains a concern. If an opponent can get under her skin, there will be a chance to beat her.

Elina Svitolina

The Ukrainian has enjoyed a strong season and deservedly earned her place at the WTA Finals. Svitolina’s biggest achievements were her titles in Rome and Auckland, while her best Grand Slam performance came in Australia, where she reached the semifinals.

The Ukrainian is currently ranked sixth in the world. She has never won a Grand Slam title, but she did lift the WTA Finals trophy in 2018. The player recently went through a separation from her husband, fellow tennis star Gaël Monfils.

It will be interesting to see what kind of mood and form the Ukrainian brings to the upcoming tournament.

Head-to-head

The head-to-head record is tied at 1-1. Their most recent meeting took place at the Australian Open earlier this year, when Svitolina won 6-4, 6-2.

Prediction

This will be a clash between two top-level players, with Andreeva a slight favorite. She is ranked higher, is 13 years younger and also holds the edge in power. Svitolina’s main strengths are her consistency from the baseline, experience and mental resilience. The most promising bet here is on the Ukrainian to win with a +4.5-game handicap at odds of 1.55.
