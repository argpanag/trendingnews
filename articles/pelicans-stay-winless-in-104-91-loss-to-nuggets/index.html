Pelicans stay winless in 104-91 loss to Nuggets

TheTools · 29.10.2026 04:55 · source · permalink

NEW ORLEANS (Acadiana News First) — Zion Williamson scored a team-high 25 points on 10-of-14 shooting, but the New Orleans Pelicans couldn’t overcome a slow start in a 104-91 loss to the Denver Nuggets Tuesday night at the Smoothie King Center.

New Orleans trailed by as many as 22 in the first half before cutting the deficit to five in the fourth quarter. Denver pulled away late to sweep the two-game set against the Pelicans.

Derik Queen added 11 points and 12 rebounds off the bench. The Pelicans’ reserves outscored Denver’s 36-19.

New Orleans shot 7-of-30 from 3-point range and committed 19 turnovers. Jordan Poole left in the first half with a left ankle injury and did not return.

Denver’s Nikola Jokic recorded his 200th career regular-season triple-double in the win, per the NBA.

New Orleans (0-4) hosts Sacramento on Thursday.
