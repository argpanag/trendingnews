Inter Miami vs D.C. United line-ups confirmed for MLS Matchday 29

Inter Miami host D.C. United at Nu Stadium tonight on MLS Matchday 29, with kick-off at seven thirty pm ET. Both teams have confirmed their starting XIs.

According to Inter Miami, the hosts line up in a 4-3-3 led by captain Leo Messi with Luis Suárez and Germán Berterame. Dayne St. Clair starts in goal behind Gonzalo Luján, Casemiro, Micael and Sergio Reguilón. Rodrigo De Paul, David Ayala and Matías Galarza make up the midfield.

The substitutes are Rocco Ríos Novo, Ian Fray, Maximiliano Falcón, Cesar Abadia, Telasco Segovia, Alexander Shaw, Preston Plambeck, Daniel Pinter and Riquelme Fillipi. Cristian González is head coach.

D.C. United are set up in a 4-4-2. Sean Johnson starts in goal. The back four is Kimito Nono, captain Lucas Bartlett, Kye Rowles and Keisuke Kurokawa.

Hosei Kijima, Andre Dozzell, Nikola Markovic and João Peglow line up across midfield. Jackson Hopkins partners Tai Baribo up front.

The substitutes are Alex Bono, Silvan Hefti, Sean Nealis, Conner Antley, Matti Peltola, Jared Stroud, Brandon Servania, Oscar Avilez and Nathan Ordaz. René Weiler is head coach.
