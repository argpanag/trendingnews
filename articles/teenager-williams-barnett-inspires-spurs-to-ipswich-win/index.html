    Leeds captain Ethan Ampadu, speaking to:"People talk about turning points and we have to learn about stuff that didn't work and try to better that."

Luca Williams-Barnett celebrates his first goal for Tottenham

Tottenham Hotspur teenager Luca Williams-Barnett starred for Roberto de Zerbi's side as they comfortably beat Ipswich Town.

On his first Premier League start, Williams-Barnett scored one and helped create the other for Dominic Solanke to give Spurs their fourth win of the season.

Tottenham were on the front foot early on and Solanke went close with a couple of headers.

But it took 18-year-old Williams-Barnett to break the deadlock in the 15th minute with his first senior goal.

It was sheer determination from the club's academy graduate as he got ahead of Ipswich defender Abdoul Ouattara to reach Pedro Porro's cross and turn home to become the club's youngest scorer on their Premier League full debut.

Mohammed Kudus then struck the post with a free-kick while Williams-Barnett and James Maddison - back in the team for the first time since 24 October - forced saves from Christian Walton in the Ipswich goal before the break.

Julio Enciso was one of the Tractor Boys' bright sparks but, despite some tidy trickery on the ball, he couldn't create many openings as Antonin Kinsky remained largely untroubled in the Spurs goal.

Tottenham got their second six minutes into the second half thanks to some great wing play down the left from Williams-Barnett.

The teenager crossed for Solanke to turn home just his second leg goal of the season, with manager De Zerbi watching on from the stands as he served a touchline ban.

Williams-Barnett was given a standing ovation when substituted in the 64th minute.

Tottenham cruised to the win as Gary O'Neil's Ipswich suffered their sixth defeat of the season.
