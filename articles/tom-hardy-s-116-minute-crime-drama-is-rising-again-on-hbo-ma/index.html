Tom Hardy’s 116-Minute Crime Drama Is Rising Again on HBO Max

TheTools · 29.09.2026 19:00 · source · permalink

The recent drama surrounding Tom Hardy's on-set behavior on Paramount+'s MobLand seems to have drummed up some interest in his other projects. HBO Max subscribers have recently been streaming Hardy's 2024 crime drama film The Bikeriders, leading it to become one of the most-watched titles on the Warner Bros. Discovery-owned platform.

According to FlixPatrol, The Bikeriders is currently ranked No. 6 on HBO Max's global Top 10 Movies chart. At the top of the list is DC Studios' Supergirl, followed by In the Lost Lands, Beast of War, Leon: The Professional, and Ice Road: Vengeance.

The Bikeriders' current streaming success on HBO Max comes without any help from the United States. At the time of writing, The Bikeriders is not available on any US streaming service. Instead, it is in countries like Croatia, Hungary, Moldova, Poland, and Serbia where The Bikeriders is finding itself within the Top 10 Movies.

The Bikeriders' Cast Includes Tom Hardy, Austin Butler, and More Major Stars

Hardy is just one of the many recognizable actors who are part of the ensemble cast for The Bikeriders. Austin Butler (Dune: Part Two), Jodie Comer (Free Guy), Michael Shannon (Man of Steel), Mike Faist (Challengers), Norman Reedus (The Walking Dead: Daryl Dixon), and Boyd Holbrook (Indiana Jones and the Dial of Destiny) also star in the crime drama film.

The Bikeriders is inspired by Danny Lyon's iconic photo book of the same name. Jeff Nichols wrote and directed the crime drama, which focuses on the rise of the fictional 1960s Midwestern motorcycle club, Vandals. It was Nichols' first directorial effort in eight years.

The official synopsis for The Bikeriders reads, "Kathy (Jodie Comer), a strong-willed member of the Vandals who’s married to a wild, reckless bikerider named Benny (Butler), recounts the Vandals’ evolution over the course of a decade, beginning as a local club of outsiders united by good times, rumbling bikes, and respect for their strong, steady leader Johnny (Hardy)."

"Over the years, Kathy tries her best to navigate her husband’s untamed nature and his allegiance to Johnny, with whom she feels she must compete for Benny’s attention. As life in the Vandals gets more dangerous, and the club threatens to become a more sinister gang, Kathy, Benny and Johnny are forced to make choices about their loyalty to the club and to each other," concludes the synopsis.

Tom Hardy Creating His Own Drama Behind the Scenes on the Crime Series MobLand

A year after The Bikeriders' release, Hardy began starring in a different crime drama — Ronan Bennett's MobLand on Paramount+. The actor leads the show as Harry Da Souza, a fixer working for the Harrigan crime family led by Conrad and Maeve Harrigan. Pierce Brosnan and Helen Mirren co-star as the Harrigans.

In the first season, the Harrigans go to war with a rival London crime family, the Stevensons, for ultimate power. Across 10 episodes, the two families clash in a kill-or-be-killed battle that threatens to topple empires and ruin lives. In the currently airing second season of the crime drama, the Harrigans are on the brink of civil war, and their rivals are starting to notice. As tensions within the Harrigan family intensify, Harry is the only one who can keep them from falling apart.

MobLand has been a major hit for Paramount+, although its success hasn't been drama-free. Ahead of the Season 2 premiere, reports emerged stating that Hardy had repeatedly clashed with showrunner Jez Butterworth on set, among other issues. Puck News even stated that Hardy had been fired from MobLand and would not be returning for Season 3.

Luckily, it sounds like Hardy's behind-the-scenes difficulties were blown out of proportion. Earlier this month, Brosnan addressed the drama, telling the Press Association that it was merely "a storm in a teacup." He went on to praise Hardy, calling himself "a massive fan of the man's work. Truly."

Hardy and Butterworth have since patched things up between them, with both set to return in their respective roles on MobLand Season 3. Filming will begin in November.

The Bikeriders is available to stream in select countries on HBO Max.
