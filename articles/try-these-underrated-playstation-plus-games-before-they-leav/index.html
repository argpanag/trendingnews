Try These Underrated PlayStation Plus Games Before They Leave - AOL

TheTools · 23.10.2026 14:46 · source · permalink

PS Plus members looking to play something different from high-profile PS5 and PS4 games should check out these underrated hidden gems before they leave the catalog. All the games mentioned below are still in the 2026 catalog at the time of this writing, but they can leave at any time with little notice.

These hidden gem PS Plus games are recommended by players

When a Reddit user asked fellow subscribers for “underrated masterpieces” in the catalog that aren’t your usual mainstream recommendations, the community came up with a massive list of awesome games — many of which I’ve played and would personally recommend as well. With the holiday season approaching, now is a good time to try these games if you haven’t already.

  • Immortals Fenyx Rising: Ubisoft’s Zelda-like open-world RPG.

  • Banishers: Ghosts of New Eden: Don’t Nod’s intriguing RPG about ghost hunters on a mission

  • Dungeons of Hinterberg: a fantastic little adventure RPG about mysterious dungeons in a tourist hotspot

  • Pacific Drive: a unique first-person driving survival game in which your car is your companion

  • Hell Is Us: a surprising new action-adventure IP in which you explore a semi-open world while dealing with a mysterious calamity.

  • Blades of Fire: a dark fantasy adventure where your weapon is everything.
