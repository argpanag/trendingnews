Emirates resumes A380 flights to Beijing next month

TheTools · 04.09.2026 16:01 · source · permalink

Emirates will deploy its Airbus A380 on Dubai-Beijing routes starting October 4, replacing Boeing 777s. The double-decker will operate daily for a limited period. This expands the airline’s Asia-Pacific A380 network to 20 airports by year-end.

By InsideFlyer

Sep 4 · 4:01 PM · 1 min

From October 4 through October 31, Emirates will fly the 615-seat A380 on EK306 and EK307 between Dubai and Beijing, covering 3,162 nautical miles. These flights replace 777-300ERs and offer increased passenger capacity but reduced cargo space. The route previously saw A380 service from 2010 until 2020, then briefly in 2023.

This deployment is part of a broader 18% increase in A380 flights across Asia excluding the Middle East. Emirates also begins daily A380 service to Delhi on October 25 and continues operations to Guangzhou and Shanghai. The growth offsets declines in Australia and New Zealand routes during the same period.

Travelers gain access to premium seating and higher capacity on a key China route, though cargo options shrink. Those connecting via Dubai may see more flight choices, especially to African and European destinations where demand remains strong.
