Ahtesham stuns Russian

TheTools · 04.11.2026 19:00 · source · permalink

ISLAMABAD: Ahtesham Humayun produced an excellent performance to defeat Russia’s Daniel Elias in a closely contested three-set encounter in J-60 Brad Foundation Junior World Tennis at the PTF Complex Wednesday.

Ahtesham won 6-3, 4-6, 6-4, while Muhammad Hassan Usmani dominated his match against fellow Pakistani Amir Mazari, securing a convincing 6-1, 6-1 victory. In the boys’ doubles event, five Pakistani players, Ahtesham Humayun, Hamza, M. Hassan Usmani, M. Haziq Aasim and Amir Mazari also secured their places in the semifinals.

A total of 58 players, comprising 36 boys and 22 girls, from 14 countries are participating in the championship. The participating countries include Russia, Hong Kong, China, Thailand, Kazakhstan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Serbia, Australia, Spain, Canada, Belarus, Sweden and Pakistan.

Results:

Boys singles second round:

M.Hassan Usmani bt Amir Mazari 6-1,6-1; Ahtesham Humayun bt Daniel Elias (RUS) 6-3, 4-6, 6-4; Phasin Sinchalong (THA) bt Sanzhar Bolat (KAZ) 6-2, 6-0; Aliaksandr Viarsotski (BLR) bt Aolun Ma(CHN) 7-5, 6-4
