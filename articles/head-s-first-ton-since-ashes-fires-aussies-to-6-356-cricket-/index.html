Head's first ton since Ashes fires Aussies to 6-356 | cricket.com.au

TheTools · 18.09.2026 14:55 · source · permalink

Match Report:

An eighth ODI ton from Travis Head came in a rollicking 130-run opening stand, before Ollie Peake put on the finishing touches

Travis Head's first century since last summer's Ashes fired Australia to their highest ever ODI total against Zimbabwe.

After a sequence of four consecutive chop-on dismissals that begun in last month's Tests against Bangladesh, Head returned to form with a brutal 84-ball 112 featuring 15 fours and three sixes that took his side to 6-356 from their 50 overs.

The left-hander shared in a power-packed 130-run opening stand between with captain Mitch Marsh (53 from 63) before Cooper Connolly (78 off 69) continued his excellent white-ball run with his third straight fifty-plus ODI score.

Ollie Peake then gave another glimpse of his talent. The 20-year-old carted Brad Evans (3-86 from nine overs) for three final-over sixes in a blazing late cameo of 41 not out off 18 to take Australia to a tally they have bettered only once since the last ODI World Cup.

Head had needed just 32 balls to reach fifty and then 75 to bring up his eighth ODI hundred, celebrating with a series of cheeky gestures to the team dugout. He was finally out caught trying to hoist his fourth six, skying a catch off Wessly Madhevere's off-spin.

Zimbabwe were sloppy in the field – Marsh earnt a reprieve first ball of the game by Madhevere, before Newman Nyamhuri dropped a top-edged Matthew Renshaw hook in the death overs.

A dry Harare pitch slowed Australia's scoring somewhat through the middle overs – Wellington Masakadza (2-51 off nine) doing best to put the brakes on – though Connolly and Peake both cleared the fence three times to keep Australia's momentum going.

Josh Inglis popped a simple catch back to Masakadza to go for 21, while Alex Carey's batting struggles continued as he was caught at point for a second consecutive innings. After making 20 here, he has now passed 30 just once in his last 15 international innings.
