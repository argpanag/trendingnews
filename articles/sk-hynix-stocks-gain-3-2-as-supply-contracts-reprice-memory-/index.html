SK Hynix Stocks Gain 3.2% as Supply Contracts Reprice Memory Scarcity

TheTools · 02.10.2026 01:27 · source · permalink

SK Hynix SKHY, the high-bandwidth-memory and semiconductor company, rose 3.2% in the Korea market on October 1, 2026, as optimism over tight AI-memory supply supported the rally. Demand is the easy part of the story. The harder question is how much future growth investors have already paid for.

The company reported record second-quarter results, long-term agreements with around 10 customers and the start of HBM4 mass shipments. It plans to increase HBM4 production in the second half, while HBM4E sample shipments were completed in the first half. Those customer commitments give the expansion a firmer foundation. But contracts alone do not reveal pricing protection, committed volumes or the returns on new capacity.

The valuation snapshot puts the shares 46.31% above the $126.84 GF Value estimate, leaving less room for execution mistakes. Strong AI demand can support a premium. Keeping that premium requires profitable shipments, disciplined spending and cash generation. Investors should watch whether SK Hynix turns its technological lead into durable returns as production expands.
