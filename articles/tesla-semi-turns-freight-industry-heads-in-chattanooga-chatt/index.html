Tesla Semi turns freight industry heads in Chattanooga - Chattanooga Times Free Press

TheTools · 28.10.2026 00:40 · source · permalink

This story is free to read.

Surging quietly from a Chattanooga Choo Choo parking lot, the new Tesla semitruck doesn’t belch clouds of black diesel smoke into the air like a traditional big rig.

The Tesla company offered ride-alongs in the Tesla Semi during the F3: FreightWaves Future of Freight Festival at The Signal on Tuesday, and freight industry attendees lined up all day for a trip around the Choo Choo property.

FreightWaves is owned by Craig Fuller, who also owns the Chattanooga Times Free Press.

Jason Gies, Tesla’s East Coast business development manager, was in the centrally positioned driver seat for demo rides to highlight the truck’s quiet power, maneuverability and regenerative braking system, alongside its technology. The truck featured in Chattanooga doesn’t drive itself, though the technology exists.

The truck wove through the full parking lot without difficulty, slowing enough with its regenerative braking the driver never had to touch the brakes except to come to a complete stop.
