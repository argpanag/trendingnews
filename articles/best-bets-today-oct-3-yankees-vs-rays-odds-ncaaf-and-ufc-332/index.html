Best Bets Today, Oct. 3: Yankees Vs Rays Odds, NCAAF And UFC 332 Picks

TheTools · 03.10.2026 12:11 · source · permalink

The MLB Division Series opens with four Game 1s on Saturday, led by Yankees at Rays and Braves at Dodgers, while No. 7 Alabama visits No. 16 Mississippi State and UFC 332 crowns a new flyweight champion. Here are the best prices on each at FanDuel, Fanatics, BetRivers and LetsBetMD.

Four Division Series openers, a top-20 SEC meeting in Starkville and a vacant-title fight at UFC 332 fill Saturday's board. The most notable number sits on the New York Yankees, who send Gerrit Cole to the mound as +114 underdogs at FanDuel for Game 1 at top-seeded Tampa Bay. The prices below compare FanDuel, Fanatics, BetRivers and LetsBetMD (Maryland only). Odds were taken this morning, and all times are ET.

Today's Best Odds At A Glance

Yankees Vs Rays Odds: Moneyline, Run Line And Total

Tampa Bay won the AL East at 98-64, took the American League's top seed and sat out the first round. New York (93-68) got here by sweeping Boston in the Wild Card Series, 9-0 and 9-2. The Rays edged the season series 7-6, and Game 1 of the best-of-five is at Tropicana Field.

Drew Rasmussen (16-5, 2.67 ERA) starts for the Rays against Cole (10-9, 3.61 ERA). Aaron Judge missed the Wild Card Series with a right calf strain. The books split on the total: FanDuel and LetsBetMD hang 6.5, Fanatics and BetRivers 7.

Bet to consider: Rays moneyline at -130 with Fanatics. Rasmussen has the better regular-season line of the two starters, Tampa Bay is rested and at home, and Fanatics is the cheapest of the four books on the favorite.

Braves Vs Dodgers Odds: Moneyline, Run Line And Total

Two division champions meet at Dodger Stadium. Los Angeles went 100-62 to win the NL West and earn a bye. Atlanta (94-68) won the NL East, then beat Philadelphia two games to one in the Wild Card Series. The Braves took five of the six regular-season meetings.

Tarik Skubal, acquired from Detroit at the trade deadline, starts Game 1 for the Dodgers after going 12-7 with a 2.72 ERA across both clubs. Atlanta is expected to open with a bullpen game. LetsBetMD posts a total of 7.5; the other three books are at 8.5.

Bet to consider: Dodgers -1.5 at -103 with BetRivers. Skubal against a bullpen game is the widest pitching gap on the Game 1 slate, and the run line at near even money beats laying -213 or more on the moneyline. Bettors who trust the 5-1 season series can take Atlanta at +188 with FanDuel.

Alabama Vs Mississippi State Odds: Spread, Moneyline And Total

No. 7 Alabama and No. 16 Mississippi State are both 4-0 overall and 2-0 in the SEC going into the noon kickoff at Davis Wade Stadium. The Bulldogs beat Missouri 31-24 last week, and Alabama arrives off a win over South Carolina. Keelon Russell quarterbacks the Crimson Tide against Kamario Taylor.

Bet to consider: Mississippi State +5.5 at -108 with Fanatics. An unbeaten, ranked home team is getting nearly a touchdown, and Fanatics has the best price on the Bulldogs. Alabama backers get -5.5 at -105 with FanDuel or LetsBetMD.

Ohio State Vs Iowa Odds: Spread, Moneyline And Total

No. 5 Ohio State (3-1) goes to Kinnick Stadium to face No. 14 Iowa (4-0) at 3:30 p.m. The Buckeyes beat Illinois 42-19 last week, while Iowa won 20-19 at Michigan. Julian Sayin starts at quarterback for Ohio State and Hank Brown for the Hawkeyes.

FanDuel, Fanatics and LetsBetMD have Ohio State -14.5, priced at -104, -105 and -109. BetRivers is at 14, with Iowa +14 at -108. On the moneyline Iowa is as long as +490 at FanDuel. The total is 45.5 at three books and 46 at BetRivers.

Bet to consider: Iowa +14.5 at -114 with LetsBetMD. The Hawkeyes are unbeaten and have just won a one-point game on the road, and LetsBetMD has the best price on the bigger number (-115 at Fanatics, -118 at FanDuel).

UFC 332 Odds: Silva Vs Wang

Natalia Silva and Wang Cong fight over five rounds for the vacant women's flyweight title in the UFC 332 main event at Delta Center in Salt Lake City. Silva is 20-5-1 and 8-0 in the UFC, and beat Rose Namajunas by unanimous decision in January. Wang (10-1) outpointed Tracy Cortez in July. Both made weight on Friday.

BetRivers and FanDuel are the two books with the fight. Silva is -204 at BetRivers and -208 at FanDuel; Wang is +176 at FanDuel and +163 at BetRivers.

Bet to consider: Silva moneyline at -204 with BetRivers. She is unbeaten in eight UFC fights and has the stronger recent win.
