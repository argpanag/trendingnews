Ethena Says It Is Aware of a Potential Security Incident Related to Binance

TheTools · 29.10.2026 21:05 · source · permalink

Summary

  • Ethena said it is aware of a potential security incident related to Binance.
  • Ethena stressed that all collateral assets with exchange exposure are being safely held with off-exchange third-party custodians.
  • Ethena added that it will provide further updates through its official X account as soon as specific details about the matter are confirmed.

Forecast Trend Report by Period

Ethena, a cryptocurrency project, said it is aware of a potential security incident related to Binance. It added that all collateral assets with exchange exposure are held with separate off-exchange third-party custodians.

On Oct. 29, Ethena said in a post on its official X account, formerly Twitter, that it was aware of a potential security incident related to Binance.

The project emphasized that all collateral assets with exchange exposure are not held directly on the exchange and are instead stored securely with off-exchange third-party custodians.

Ethena added that it would provide further updates through its official X account once specific details on the matter are confirmed.
