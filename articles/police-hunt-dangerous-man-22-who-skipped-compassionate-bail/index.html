Police hunt 'dangerous' man, 22, who skipped compassionate bail

TheTools · 21.10.2026 03:59 · source · permalink

Police are hunting a 22-year-old man considered dangerous who failed to return to prison after the courts granted him compassionate bail last week.

Cain Te-Kahurangi Hanara was bailed on October 15 and now has a warrant out for his arrest.

Police said Hanara was actively avoiding them.

He has links to Tauranga and the Kāpiti Coast, but police believed he was now in the wider Auckland area.

“Hanara is considered dangerous and should not be approached,” a police spokesperson said.

“If you see [Hanara], please call 111 immediately.”

Information could also be passed to police through 105, quoting file number 261016/8427.

People could also contact Crime Stoppers anonymously on 0800 555 111.
