AFLW R5 team: Roos stick with single change

TheTools · 13.09.2026 05:46 · source · permalink

Club debutant Kristie-Lee Weston-Turner is North Melbourne's only inclusion for Sunday's AFLW SuperClash against Carlton

Presented by

Club debutant Kristie-Lee Weston-Turner is North Melbourne's only inclusion for Sunday's AFLW SuperClash against Carlton at Arden Street Oval.

A former No.1 draft pick, Weston-Turner spent two seasons at the Western Bulldogs before making her way to the Kangaroos following the 2025 campaign.

As confirmed on Thursday, Jenna Bruton (managed) will miss the Round 5 bout.

Tessa Boyd, Abby Favell and Maddison Torpey are North Melbourne's three emergencies.

North Melbourne’s AFLW players reveal three re-signings and our newest Shinboner

Sunday's SuperClash at Arden Street Oval starts at 3.05pm AEST and will be broadcast live on Channel 7, Fox Sports and Kayo.

The day is about more than football. Together, we'll celebrate the true heroes of the Royal Children's Hospital and support the life-changing work of the Good Friday Appeal.

Round 5 •

Arden Street Oval, Melbourne • VIC
