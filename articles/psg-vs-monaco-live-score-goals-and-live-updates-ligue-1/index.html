Folarin Balogun returns to Monaco and could feature in a massive Ligue 1 clash against PSG.

Follow PSG vs. Monaco live on beIN SPORTS

In the final seconds of the summer transfer window, it looked like USMNT striker Folarin Balogun was going to move from Monaco to Everton, swapping the Mediterranean for the Mersey. But suddenly, the deal was off, and the exact reason why is still not clear.

That means the forward could be in contention to make Monaco’s squad for Friday’s crunch clash against PSG, live on beIN SPORTS.


Balogun And Camara Remain In Monaco

Lamine Camara finds himself in a similar situation, although not through his own volition.

The midfielder was on the brink of joining Chelsea before that move was also cancelled on the presumption that Balogun would be leaving. However, Monaco coach Filipe Luís said that the Senegalese player would see action at the Parc des Princes.



Monaco Arrives In Paris With Confidence

Monaco might fancy its chances of pulling off an upset at PSG’s home on Friday.

The reigning champions have started the season slowly, having lost the Trophée des Champions before fighting back from 2-0 down in each of their first two Ligue 1 games to draw 2-2.

Monaco, in contrast, is the only team to have won both matches.

Ironically, the early league leaders have Paris to thank for their most recent 2-0 win against Marseille: Paris Brunner.


Paris Brunner Makes An Instant Impact

The young German striker joined from Borussia Dortmund over the summer and has made an instant impact, scoring four goals in four games for Monaco.

He will hope to keep that record going in his namesake city on Friday.
