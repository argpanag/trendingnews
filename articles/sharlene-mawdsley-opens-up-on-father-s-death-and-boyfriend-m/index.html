Sharlene Mawdsley opens up on father's death and boyfriend Mikey Breen's support

TheTools · 23.10.2026 17:00 · source · permalink

Sharlene Mawdsley faced serious adversity last year when her father Thomas 'Tucker' Mawdsley suddenly passed away.

Her father died in June 2025, leaving Sharlene at a loss coming up to a crucial point in her season.

"It's been a hard year, obviously we are still grieving him and missing him every day," Mawdsley said last year.

The runner disclosed how recollections of her dad cheering her on at races have carried her through the difficult period.

"Those are the good times you have to bring forward. I try to stay strong for me, my family. We are all under that same impression of re-living all the great memories we have with him."

Sharlene also recalled a more light-hearted detail, remarking that her father wasn't just her most devoted fan but Manchester United's as well.

"Very proud of Man United... he still stayed a loyal fan," she commented, alluding to the football club's recent struggles, struggles which continue to this day.

"That would tell you the type of person he was.", reports the Irish Mirror.

She acknowledged both her parents, including her mother Louise, for providing every opportunity for her to flourish, transporting her to training sessions and competitions during her younger years.

The athlete revealed that mourning publicly proved extremely difficult for both herself and her family, explaining she had never anticipated her father's eulogy would be made public.

"The support we received was so so nice, and it was obviously so lovely to hear the lovely things people had to say about him," she told the Oliver Callan show on RTÉ Radio 1 last year.

Sharlene explained that training and competing have provided a welcome distraction for her and her relatives.

"You would go to training and you'd just have a session to complete, that's all you'd really be thinking about at the time. Although you never stop thinking about him, running was a bit of distraction."

Her boyfriend, Tipperary hurler Mikey Breen, has been a pillar of strength throughout this challenging period. She mentioned the couple frequently train alongside one another.

"In case you're wondering why Mikey has gotten so fast this year, it's obviously because of me," she joked.

"He's helped me out in a lot of my sessions this year, even on all the hard days after my dad had passed away, he was at the track every day... he'd be there with the video and stopwatch. The support I had from him was really special."

Looking back on 2024'sParis Olympics, Sharlene described it as a largely positive experience, despite finishing fourth.

"Fourth is always the worst place to come, you get a medal for first second ad third, and a certificate for fourth... when I reflect on it, it is really special. I think the nicest part for me was after the race finished, my parents were both there and I got to go over to them.

"You know when you're having a bad day and your parents are there and everything just feels that little bit easier? I remember my mam saying how proud she was and my dad, they were just so so thrilled that we were there and in the final.

"I'd ran the fastest I'd ever ran that day. My mam was trying to tell me everything was fine and that it was good."
