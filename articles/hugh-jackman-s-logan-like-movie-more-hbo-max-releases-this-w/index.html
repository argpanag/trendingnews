The new movie and TV releases coming to HBO Max from October 12-18, 2026, include The Death of Robin Hood and many others.

Written and directed by Michael Sarnoski, The Death of Robin Hood is a thriller film. It is a dark adaptation of the ballad "Robin Hood's Death." The film focuses on Robin Hood as he confronts his violent past and nurses battle wounds. His life changes while he takes refuge in a priory, where he meets the prioress and a young girl. The Death of Robin Hood received mixed reviews from critics. The star cast includes Hugh Jackman, Jodie Comer, Bill Skarsgård, Murray Bartlett, and Noah Jupe, among others, in key roles.

Also coming to HBO Max is the spy action thriller, The Amateur. Directed by James Hawes, it is based on the 1981 novel of the same name by Robert Littell. It follows a CIA decoder, Charlie Heller, whose life turns upside down after his wife is killed in a terrorist attack in London. As his supervisors are unable to take action, Charlie decides to take matters into his own hands. The film opened to mixed reviews from critics and features Rami Malek, Laurence Fishburne, Rachel Brosnahan, and Caitríona Balfe, among others, in important roles.
