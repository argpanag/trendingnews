Portugal vs Norway: Nations League preview

Erling Haaland has plenty on his plate at the moment but remains the star turn in this fixture. The Manchester City striker will be well aware that Portugal have run away with the group but have conceded three goals in their last two matches.

Lining up against him is a resurgent Joao Felix. The 26-year-old has attracted acclaim for his performances over the last couple of weeks and has scored in all three of Portugal's games.

Cristiano Ronaldo left the camp under a cloud but Jesus' crack at the national team job has got off to a stellar start. Three wins in three means that Portugal are on the cusp of a play-off place.

History's on their side too. Portugal have won their last three games against Norway and have only been beaten once in all their previous meetings.

Ronaldo wasn't involved in that game in 2010 either. Whether that's an omen or not, Portugal seem to be doing just fine without him.
