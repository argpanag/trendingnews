Argentina - Riding a wave of success

Fresh from their 2022 World Cup final appearance, Argentina are in formidable form. In the following sections, we’ll analyze their recent results, tactical setup, and squad adjustments for this friendly at the Monumental.

Recent form

Argentina enter this clash in commanding form under Lionel Scaloni, recording 15 wins from their last 16 outings across all competitions. Their friendly record has been flawless over recent cycles, riding a 20-match winning streak in non-competitive fixtures dating back to 2019.

Wednesday’s 4-0 rout of Bolivia in Córdoba showcased the depth of Argentina’s talent pool, with Cristian Romero, Como playmaker Nico Paz, and Palmeiras forward José Manuel López getting on the scoresheet alongside Lautaro Martínez. Scaloni continues to evaluate the next generation, with five players making their senior international debuts in midweek.

With Lionel Messi slated for his ceremonial national team farewell next week against Benin, the Albiceleste will rotate. Thiago Almada and Nicolás Tagliafico remain sidelined through muscle injuries, while Lautaro Martínez is a doubt after a knock against Bolivia, handing José Manuel López a prime opportunity to partner Julián Álvarez up front and Nico Paz the chance to keep his excellent club and national team playmaking momentum. Franco Mastantuono, starting a revamp season on loan at Fiorentina, could also grab some quality minutes.

Burkina Faso - Looking To Build Momentum

Let’s examine Burkina Faso’s position as they head to Buenos Aires. We will assess their recent defensive displays, qualifying momentum, and standout performers ahead of this challenging road fixture.

Recent form

Burkina Faso enter this contest having recently completed a positive double-header in 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifying. After holding Benin to a 1-1 draw, Amir Abdou’s side secured a vital 1-0 away victory over the Central African Republic in Morocco, courtesy of debutant Aboubacar Camara’s late strike.

The Stallions have developed into an organized, combative unit under Abdou, losing just one of their last six internationals. However, translating that resilience against top-tier opposition on foreign soil remains an immense challenge, especially after friendly defeats against Russia (3-0) earlier in the summer.

Defensive rock Edmond Tapsoba of Bayer Leverkusen anchors the backline alongside Nasser Djiga, while Shakhtar Donetsk forward Lassina Traoré leads the frontline. With Brentford winger Dango Ouattara doubtful through illness, Burkina Faso will rely heavily on disciplined low-block defending and set-piece opportunities.
