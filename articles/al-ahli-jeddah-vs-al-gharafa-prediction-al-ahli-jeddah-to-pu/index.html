Al-Ahli Jeddah vs Al-Gharafa: Prediction for the match on October 26, 2026

Al-Ahli Jeddah, who beat Al Khaleej Saihat (4-1), will be keen to carry that momentum into Monday’s AFC Champions League Elite clash.
Al-Gharafa have points to pursue on matchday three of the West league stage. With Merih Demiral among three confirmed absentees, we expect the hosts’ selection problems to complicate their push for maximum points.

Al-Ahli Jeddah: Form and statistics

Al-Ahli Jeddah’s revival began from a (1-2) defeat at Mamelodi Sundowns in the FIFA Intercontinental Cup. They answered by running riot at Al-Fateh (4-0). The goals kept coming on the road, with Al-Wasl FC beaten (3-1) in the AFC Champions League Elite and Al-Fayha edged (3-2) in the Pro League. With that attacking touch carried into their latest home success, the hosts have good reason to trouble Al-Gharafa, although conceding against both Al-Wasl and Al-Fayha gives the visitors encouragement.

Al-Ahli Jeddah: Lineup

Shoulder rehabilitation keeps Saleh Abu Al-Shamat out, with Nayef Masoud also unavailable through a foot-joint injury, narrowing Al-Ahli’s options for changes against Al-Gharafa.

Expected XI: Abdulrahman Al-Sanbi, Saeed Baattia, Mohammed Sulaiman, Roger Ibañez, Saad Yaslam Balobaid, Valentin Atangana Edoa, Artem Bondarenko, Eduard Spertsyan, Francisco Trincão, Ivan Toney, Firas Al-Buraikan

Al-Gharafa: Form and statistics

On the road, Al-Gharafa’s (2-3) defeat at Al Wakrah offers little reassurance ahead of the trip to Jeddah. They have since settled for a share of the spoils with Al-Rayyan SC (0-0) and, most recently, Al Ahli Doha (2-2), with the latter result exposing defensive concerns again. The guests need a tighter showing against Al-Ahli Jeddah.

Al-Gharafa: Lineup

The projected 4-2-3-1 gives Adam Ounas a supporting attacking role, with Fabricio Díaz providing midfield balance against Al-Ahli Jeddah. Yacine Brahimi is expected behind Alaaeddin Mohamed Hassan, with continuity favoured from the latest XI. The guests can produce plenty of shots, although their inconsistent output means that attacking support offers no assurance of a clinical showing in Jeddah.

Al-Ahli Jeddah vs Al-Gharafa: Head-to-head statistics
