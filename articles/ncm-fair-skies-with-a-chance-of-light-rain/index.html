Conditions are expected to become humid overnight and on Friday morning in some coastal and inland areas, with a possibility of fog or mist formation.

Winds will be light to moderate, freshening at times, shifting from north-easterly to south-easterly, at 10 to 25 km/h, with gusts to 35 km/h.

In the Arabian Gulf, the sea will be slight to moderate at times. The first high tide is expected at 1:14pm and the second at 3:33am, while the first low tide will occur at 8:39pm and the second at 7:34am.

In the Sea of Oman, waves will be slight. The first high tide is expected at 9:39am and the second at 11:37pm, while the first low tide will occur at 4:30pm and the second at 5:26am.
