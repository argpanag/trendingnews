Bruno Mars adds second Auckland show due to 'incredible demand'

TheTools · 03.09.2026 23:10 · source · permalink

American singer-songwriter Bruno Mars has left the door open for more Kiwi fans, adding a second Auckland show due to "incredible demand" for tickets.

The new show would be at Auckland's Eden Park on Sunday, March 14 2027.

Live Nation Aotearoa said the new show had been added due to "incredible demand" for Mars' first Eden Park show, which was announced last week.

Ticket presale for Mars' second show began at 12pm today.

General onsale tickets for both shows would go on sale at 11am on Friday, September 4.

The two Auckland shows were among new stadium shows for Mars' The Romantic Tour announced across Australia, New Zealand and Asia.

He will be joined by Grammy-award winning artist Tyla and Silk Sonic musical partner Anderson .Paak, who will appear as DJ Pee .Wee.

Mars first rose to prominence with songs including Just The Way You Are, The Lazy Song and Grenade, before going on to release hits such as Locked Out Of Heaven, Uptown Funk and 24K Magic to cement himself as one of the world's biggest pop stars.
