Two arrested and man sought over 'altercation' by roadside in Fife

TheTools · 23.11.2026 00:04 · source · permalink

Police were called to reports of the incident on the road between St Monans and Pittenweem

  • Published
    22 November 2026

Two men have been arrested in connection with an "altercation" involving a third man by the side of a road in Fife.

Officers were called to reports of an incident on the A914 between St Monans and Pittenweem at about 20:20 on Saturday evening.

They said three men were involved in the dispute beside a parked red Ford Transit van near the road's junction with the B924.

A Police Scotland spokesperson said the vehicle and two men had been traced and arrested. Officers remain "concerned" for the third man, who has not been found.

They have asked anyone who witnessed the incident to come forward, external and share dashcam footage.

Insp Gordon Miller said: "As a result of the reports made, we are keen to find the third man involved and ensure that he is safe and well."
