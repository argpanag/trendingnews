Mikhail Sergachev notches two helpers Tuesday

TheTools · 05.11.2026 01:59 · source · permalink

by Nicholas Rodriguez | Mammoth Correspondent | Wed, Nov 4th 2026, 8:59pm EST

Mikhail Sergachev recorded two assists in Tuesday's 3-2 win over the Toronto Maple Leafs.

Fantasy Impact:

Both of Sergachev's assists came on the power play, as he set up Dylan Guenther's first-period goal and Nick Schmaltz's third-period tally. He is now riding an eight-game point streak, recording three goals and seven assists during that span.
