Latest news on when the cold front will arrive in Northern Vietnam.

TheTools · 15.11.2026 21:38 · source · permalink

Northern Vietnam is forecast to experience a new cold front from around November 18th, bringing scattered showers and thunderstorms, causing temperatures to drop rapidly, and bringing cold weather to mountainous areas, with some places experiencing freezing temperatures. So, which areas will be the coldest during this cold front?

Northern Vietnam is entering a period of rising temperatures and warm, sunny weather before a new cold front is forecast to arrive around November 18th. This cold front may bring scattered showers and thunderstorms, causing temperatures to drop, with colder weather in mountainous areas. So when will the cold front affect Northern Vietnam, and how low will the temperature drop?

According to weather forecasts, the weather across the country will be volatile over the next 10 days. In the North, there will be scattered showers and thunderstorms, sunny days, and cold nights and early mornings.

Notably, from November 15-17, temperatures in the North continued to rise, with some areas reaching over 30 degrees Celsius. However, around November 18, the region is likely to experience a new cold front, bringing scattered showers and thunderstorms, with some areas experiencing moderate to heavy rain.

Forecasts indicate that from around November 18-19, the weather in Northern Vietnam will turn cold, with some mountainous areas experiencing freezing temperatures.

Weather forecast for Northern Vietnam predicts a rise in temperature before the arrival of a cold front. (Illustrative image.)

Experts predict that this cold front will not be as strong as the one on November 9th, so the wind will not howl for an extended period, and temperatures will not drop significantly. Daytime temperatures will be around 21-25 degrees Celsius, while nights and early mornings will be chilly, dropping to 15-20 degrees Celsius. The temperature difference between day and night will create a noticeable change in weather.

In the Central region, scattered showers and thunderstorms are expected in some areas in the coming days. Specifically, from Thanh Hoa to Ha Tinh , around November 18-19, there will be scattered showers and thunderstorms, with some areas experiencing heavy rain.

From November 19th, the area from Quang Tri to Da Nang City and the eastern parts of the provinces from Quang Ngai to Dak Lak and Khanh Hoa will also experience scattered showers and thunderstorms, with some areas experiencing heavy rain.

In Southern Vietnam, scattered showers are expected in the afternoon and evening, with thunderstorms in some areas. From around November 19-20, scattered showers and thunderstorms will continue in the late afternoon and evening, with heavy rain in some localized areas.

Looking further ahead, from now until around midday on December 10, 2026, the meteorological agency predicts that the average temperature across the country will generally be 1-2 degrees higher than the multi-year average, and even higher in some areas, according to Vietnamnet.

Cold fronts affecting Northern and Central Vietnam are likely to be strong during the first 1-2 weeks of the forecast period, then tend to weaken compared to the average for the same period.
