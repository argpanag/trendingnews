Life Gets A Lot Easier For 4 Chinese Zodiac Signs After Thursday, October 1

TheTools · 30.09.2026 02:00 · source · permalink
  • Aria Gmitter

Written on Sep 30, 2026

October 1, 2026, is an Earth Monkey Close Day, shutting the door to the past. This is part of the reason why four Chinese zodiac signs feel like their lives get a lift. Things just get a lot easier for them all around.

Close Days aren't about fresh starts or beginning new projects. There are no adventures meant to pursue. It's time to ease into the month and give yourself breathing room. Earth energy is about change, and so things feel a bit unstable. It's these four animal signs who slow down and worry less about what to do next.

1. Monkey

Design: YourTango

After Thursday, there's a sense of ease because the hard work you've struggled through is finally behind you. Now, all you need to do is sit and wait. You're watching for the money to roll in or the job offer to find you. You've been chasing a dream, and it requires a ton of work to get where you are right now. 

Even work has its limitations, Monkey. You've finally found yours, and it's better than you thought it would be. No more assignments to submit or rechecking a project. The deadline has passed, and your turn is to be the one patiently waiting. It's both a little scary and refreshing, but the signs around you say to be still and know that good things come to you while waiting. You don't have to do anything else for today. 

2. Rat

Design: YourTango

You are big on having an intelligent mind, and you have been problem-solving since the day you were born. On Thursday, someone swoops in and helps you resolve a situation that honestly made you feel overwhelmed. You didn't have the solution, and even if you wanted to find it, it was above your pay grade. 

Yet, a conversation with a colleague or friend turns into help that you have needed for too long, and you were too tired to even ask. You feel such a sense of relief, Rat. You don't have to keep up with the facade. You can say you need help and then it arrives. Boom.

3. Dragon

Design: YourTango

You finally find out the truth about a person you were unsure of. It's really been bothering you a lot that this one individual who you've called a friend hasn't felt like that lately. You haven't been able to put your finger on the problem. 

But on Thursday, something is revealed. They drop a truth bomb, and you realize you were right all along. The situation changes your perspective on many things. Now all that's left is to wait and see what happens next. They don't call or text, and you decide to let it go. Why worry about it if they aren't?

4. Snake

Design: YourTango

Snake, you've been in a bit of a funk, but your creativity seems to come back on Thursday. You get this brilliant idea after a long period of having nothing new come into your mind. You go for a walk, and when you relax into the day, that's when the moment happens. 

It's as though you just needed to relax and not worry so much. Your mind wasn't filled with the list of things you had to do or anything at all. You just focused on enjoying the moment and being present in the day. Now, you have something you can work on later.  Your groove is back, and now you can finally believe in yourself again.
