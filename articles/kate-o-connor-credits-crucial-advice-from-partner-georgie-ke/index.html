For Kate O'Connor, the 2024 Olympics can perhaps be viewed as a defining moment in her career. She has medalled in some of the biggest events in world athletics over the last two years, but it was arguably her struggles in Paris that have influenced her rise to the top of the sport more than any of her successes.

The Louth woman finished 14th in the women's heptathlon that summer, an underwhelming end considering the expectations she placed on herself heading into the games.

Some athletes may have allowed that disappointment to negatively impact them over the months that followed. It had the opposite effect for O'Connor.

She has since gone on to claim gold in the European, Commonwealth, and World Student competitions, as well as silver in both world indoor and world outdoor levels.

Kate O'Connor Reveals Advice From Partner Georgie Kelly

Kate O'Connor made plenty of changes to her preparation after her disappointing finish at the Olympics, some of which were influenced by boyfriend Georgie Kelly.

Donegal man Kelly is a professional footballer, establishing himself as one of the best strikers in the League of Ireland before moving to English football. He currently plies his trade at Carlisle United.

The pair have been in a relationship for a number of years, and with both being athletes, they have been able to share some valuable advice with each other at times.

Speaking to Balls in her role as SPAR and EUROSPAR Team Ireland ambassador, Kate O'Connor revealed the impact her boyfriend has had in helping reach the pinnacle of her sport.

We've definitely learned a lot of like tips and tricks from each other.

I'm highly competitive, whereas Georgie can switch his competitive side on and off. I would imagine that I've probably instilled a lot more competitiveness in him.

From being together, he's also shown me that it's okay to play a game and not have to win, which is still a struggle for me.

We are, are very different, but it's nice because we've learned a lot from each other.

Georgie had seen how disappointed I was with how I did at the Olympics and how much I had believed in myself at that time. I just didn't know how to navigate it and become what I wanted to become.

He definitely gave me some tips on things that he thought that I should change to make me a better athlete. It was probably the first time that I ever took something on board from somebody else that wasn't to do with technical stuff.

He was a huge contributor to the success and that I had in 2025 by just being there, giving me good advice and being honest with me.

Katie Taylor fight provides further inspiration for O'Connor

The 2026 season was both a hugely busy and successful one for Kate O'Connor.

It started with a bronze medal in the World Indoor Championships in China back in March, before ultimately culminating with her European Championships gold last month. There was also the small matter of gold in the Commonwealth Games in sandwiched in between.

She has had some much needed downtime in recent weeks, admitting that she enjoyed the opportunity to accept invitations to the likes of Electric Picnic and London Fashion Week.

The 25-year old was also in attendance for Katie Taylor's recent bout at Croke Park, a historic occasion for women's sport in this country. Having played a starring role in what has been a brilliant year for Irish sportswomen, she couldn't help but come away even more determined to succeed after taking in the occasion on Jones' Road.

As an athlete sitting watching a fellow female athlete sell out Croke Park, haveing people fighting over tickets, I don't know if there's been the likes of it before.

Just to be a part of that history and to sit and watch it in person, I just felt really inspired by her.

She's a class athlete that's shown so many of us other female athletes that it's possible, that it's okay to work hard, to be aggressive and competitive.

I felt really inspired, let alone all the young boys and girls that are sitting there watching this amazing woman with an incredible amount of fans there to support her.

No doubt it further lit the fire burning in Kate O'Connor ahead of the Olympics in 2028.

After her disappointment in Paris, she very much has redemption in LA on her mind.
