Rockstar Games plans to release a remaster of Grand Theft Auto IV using the sixth installment's engine.

TheTools · 23.11.2026 00:07 · source · permalink

According to recent reports from industry insiders, Rockstar Games is considering a full-fledged remaster of the crime action game Grand Theft Auto IV, built directly on the updated RAGE engine from the sixth installment in the series. Niko Bellic's updated story will reportedly be integrated into the Grand Theft Auto VI ecosystem as a major paid single-player expansion, tentatively scheduled for release in late 2027. This approach will allow players to seamlessly return to the streets of Liberty City with modern lighting, reworked physics, ray tracing, and up-to-date gameplay mechanics, without the need to launch a separate, outdated client for the 2008 game.

In addition to long-term plans to expand the game's map with a classic metropolis, sources have shared details about hidden music content included in the base version of Grand Theft Auto VI. It's claimed that after completing the main story campaign, a special secret nostalgic radio station dedicated to the events of the original 2002 Grand Theft Auto: Vice City will be unlocked in the car stereo. This station will be hosted by classic radio hosts from the 1980s-era game, and the playlist will include over forty licensed tracks familiar to fans from the original soundtrack released twenty-four years ago.

Official representatives of publisher Take-Two Interactive and Rockstar Games are currently refraining from commenting on the existence of the fourth game's remaster and the unlockable rewards for completing the story. Previously, the developers only officially confirmed the basic set of Leonid radio stations, including the updated Flash FM, Cocoteo FM, Back Country Radio, and The Chamber 106.6, as well as the release of a separate music album of 34 original tracks for the game's launch on November 19, 2026. Nevertheless, the practice of integrating classic cities and musical Easter eggs is entirely consistent with the developers' desire to unite the franchise's legacy within a unified technological space.
