Carmel in brief — November 24, 2026 | Current Publishing

TheTools · 21.11.2026 11:00 · source · permalink

Hamilton County leadership fellows selected — The Mitch Daniels Leadership Foundation announced its Class IX Fellowship cohort, which consists of eight Hamilton County residents. The two-year fellowship includes traveling, coursework and Socratic seminars at Liberty Fund. Hamilton County’s Class IX fellows are Melissa Beuc, Chad Bills, Tyler Coward, Matthew Gebbie, Chris Greisl, Dustin Mikesell, Jesse Sherman and Cassandra Williams.

Hamilton County Parks & Recreation awarded — Hamilton County Parks and Recreation received two awards from the Indiana Park & Recreation Association, earning the Outstanding Agency Award for its leadership, innovation and service in one of Indiana’s fastest-growing counties, and the Innovative Program Award for the Strawberry Moon Market, which debuted in 2025 at Strawtown Koteewi Park. 

“If I’m Not Here Tomorrow” — Local author Jessica Willits is celebrating the launch of her workbook, “If I’m Not Here Tomorrow,” from 5 to 7 p.m. Dec. 10 at Event Nest, 40 Executive Drive The workbook is a guide for caregivers wanting to plan for the future of those with special needs in their care. The event will feature refreshments and opportunities to purchase the workbook. Attendees can RSVP at jessicawillits.com/rsvp. 

Fairgrounds kicks off annual light show — Christmas Nights of Lights, an annual drive-through light show at the Indiana State Fairgrounds, is open through Dec. 31, celebrating the holiday season with more than 1 million LED lights synchronized to holiday music, with festive displays for all ages. For more, visit christmasnightsoflights.com.

Pickleball tournament fundraiser — Pickle for Paws’ first annual doubles pickleball tournament has been rescheduled for 10 a.m. Jan. 9, 2027, at Pickle on Penn, 11575 N. Pennsylvania St. Participants can compete in beginner and competitive divisions. Registration is required. Tickets cost $85 for players and $20 for spectators. Proceeds from the tournament will support the nonprofit’s pet therapy work.

Merchants Square infrastructure – Work to improve water and sewer networks in Merchants Square is is under way and will continue in 13 rolling phases through mid-2027. The first phase will upgrade infrastructure under Keystone Way just west of Keystone Parkway. Subsequent phases will include the rest of Keystone Way and move west toward AAA Way and the roundabout at 116th Street. Other impacted roadways are Merchants Square Drive, Medical Drive and Range Line Road. Learn more by calling Carmel Utilities Customer Service at 317-571-2442.

Princess program applications — The 500 Festival is accepting applications for the 2027 Princess Program through Jan. 10, 2027, at 500Festival.com/Princess. Presented by The National Bank of Indianapolis, the program recognizes college-age women statewide for leadership, service and academic excellence, offering mentorship and a $1,500 scholarship. Top applicants will interview Jan. 23 in Indianapolis, with 66 advancing to a second round before 33 are selected as 2027 Princesses. Selected Princesses will serve as ambassadors from February through May, taking part in community outreach and 500 Festival events statewide.

Winter Market —  The Carmel Farmers Market will host its Winter Market every Saturday from 9 a.m. to noon through March 27, 2027. Attendees can shop from more than 60 vendors, including first-time Winter Market vendors Elevation Produce, Happy Little Pineapple Shop, FDG!, Moore’s Pie Shop, The Dirt Bar and Skillington Elderberries. The Winter Market is held inside the 611 building at 611 3rd Ave. SW in Carmel. For more information, visit carmelfarmersmarket.com/wm-home.       

MCL Breakfast with Santa — MCL Restaurant & Bakery, 1390 Keystone Way E., is hosting Breakfast with Santa from 9 to 11 a.m. Dec. 5. The event includes an all-you-care-to-eat breakfast, a visit with Santa Claus and sugar cookie decorating. Tickets are $24 for adults, $16 for children and children 2 and younger are free. For more, visit mclhomemade.com. 

Green Gift Shop — Carmel Green Initiative is hosting its annual Green Gift Shop from 1:30 to 7 p.m. Dec. 3 at the Carmel Clay Public Library, 425 E. Main St. Attendees can shop for handcrafted, reworked, organic or natural gifts sold by local businesses. There also will be crafts for teens and children to make starting at 4:30 p.m. For more, visit carmelgreen.org. 

Donut 5K Holiday Run/Walk — Donut 5K is hosting its 20th annual holiday run/walk Saturday, Dec. 19, from 9 to 10 a.m. at 14455 Clay Terrace Blvd. Proceeds from the event will go toward local animal rescues. Dogs are eligible to participate in the event and there will be awards for dog costumes. Registration will close Dec. 19 at 8:45 a.m. It is $25 for ages 6 to 12 and $49 for adults. For more, visit donut5krun.com.

The Stratford earns Great Place to Work certification — For the seventh consecutive year, the retirement community The Stratford has been certified by Great Place to Work. The certification is given based on workplace culture, leadership practices and employees’ experience.
