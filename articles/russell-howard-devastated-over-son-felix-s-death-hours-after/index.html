Russell Howard 'devastated' over son Felix's death hours after wife gave birth

TheTools · 04.09.2026 13:18 · source · permalink

Russell Howard has revealed his baby son Felix died hours after his wife Cerys Morgan gave birth to him.

The comedian, 46, shared his "unimaginable heartache" over Felix's death shortly after he was born earlier this year. Howard and his NHS doctor wife had not announced that they were expecting a second child.

The Russell Howard's Good News presenter thanked the NHS for their support in a devastating Instagram post on Friday, saying he and Morgan were able to spend "precious hours" with their newborn.

"Earlier this year, our family changed forever when our beautiful baby boy, Felix, died shortly after birth," the Mock The Week star penned.

"During the most devastating time of our lives, the hospital staff who cared for us showed extraordinary compassion, kindness and humanity."

The comedian, 46, shared his 'unimaginable heartache' (BBC)

He went on: "They helped us navigate an unimaginable heartache and gave us support when we needed it most. We were assigned a bereavement midwife, Lauren, who had experienced a similar tragedy.

"After losing her own baby, Lauren retrained as a midwife so that she could help other families going through what she had been through.

"Her care meant more to us than we can ever put into words. During our time in hospital, Felix was placed in a cool cot, which meant that my wife and I were able to spend precious hours with our beautiful boy.

"We could hold him, talk to him and tell him just how much we loved him. Those hours are among the most precious memories we have of Felix."

Howard explained that he is setting up a charity dedicated to supporting family people affected by baby loss.

"We were overwhelmed by the care we received, and we made a promise to each other: that we would try to help other families who find themselves facing the same devastating circumstances," the TV star wrote.

"And so, in Felix's memory, we are establishing The Felix Foundation. The foundation will aim to relieve the needs of parents, siblings and other close family members affected by miscarriage, premature birth, stillbirth, neonatal death or related bereavement."

Howard - who recently shared his desire to appear on Celebrity Traitors - and Morgan welcomed their first child, a boy, in May 2024.

The comedian shared the news in August 2024 during an interview with The Times, revealing that his son was already three months old.

"Six months ago my evenings would have been spent getting ready to go out and perform in front of a thousand people, but now evenings find me sitting on the sofa watching TV with my son on my chest," he said.

"I'm tired a lot. It's funny, when you become a parent everybody compliments you on your baby, but they will freely turn to you and say that you look like shit, that your face should be on a government watch list or something.

"So I try to get sleep whenever I can. Right now, I need it."

Howard's NHS doctor wife Cerys Morgan with their dog (X)

Howard and Morgan began dating in 2004. He confirmed their engagement in 2018, and they married a year later.

In January 2025, he hit the headlines when he implied he won't be appearing on TV anymore, preferring to focus on stand-up comedy and podcasts.

He later backtracked, saying "never say never" and admitting he'd be open to doing another topical show like Russell Howard's Good News.
