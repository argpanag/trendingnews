Boy (12) pronounced dead following collision between train and car in Co Mayo last night

TheTools · 04.09.2026 08:51 · source · permalink

LAST UPDATE | 53 mins ago

A 12-YEAR-OLD BOY was killed after a train and a car collided at a level crossing in Ballyhaunis, Co Mayo, last night. 

Shortly after 8.45pm, gardaí and emergency services responded to the collision at a level crossing in Kiltybo, near Ballyhaunis.

A passenger in the car, a 12-year-old boy, was pronounced dead at the scene.

The driver of the car, a man in his late 40s, was taken from the scene by ambulance for hospital treatment. His injuries are not life-threatening.

A garda spokesperson said all passengers on the train safely disembarked and onward transport was arranged.

A technical examination of the scene will be carried out by forensic collision investigators and the road remains closed at this time.

Local Fine Gael councillor Alma Gallagher told The Journal the boy who died and the man in hospital were from a very well-known family in the area and that the community of Ballyhaunis was “united in grief” this morning. 

She said the family is “at the heart” of the local GAA club and expressed her condolences to the boy’s family and neighbours.

Minister for Road Safety Seán Canney extended his “deepest sympathies to the families affected by this horrific collision”.

Speaking to RTÉ’s Morning Ireland, Canney said his thoughts are also with the train driver.

There were two collisions on the line yesterday and Canney said it was “frightening” that this could happen.

“Hopefully we will see what caused these accidents, so that we can prevent them in the future,” said Canney.

“Rail is a very safe mode of transport and these incidents are very rare, but they are very tragic.”

Gardaí are appealing for witnesses to this incident to come forward.

Road users and pedestrians with camera footage, including dash-cam, who were in the area of Kiltybo, Ballyhaunis, Co Mayo, between 8.15pm and 9.15pm on Thursday are asked to make this footage available.

Anyone with any information is asked to contact Ballina garda station on (096) 20560, the Garda Confidential Line on 1800 666 111, or any garda station.

In a statement this morning, Irish Rail expressed its “deepest sympathies to the family of the young boy who tragically lost his life”.

Irish Rail said the 6.05pm Heuston to Westport service struck a car in which the boy was travelling at XM160 level crossing at Ballyhaunis, Co. Mayo.

Irish Rail also thanked the emergency services “for the professionalism and care shown to those involved”.

A spokesperson added that Irish Rail “immediately commenced a full investigation into the incident and has advised the relevant authorities”.

Meanwhile, the line will remain closed between Westport and Athlone “until further notice” and replacement bus services are being provided.

Ballina to Manulla services will operate to Claremorris, with onward bus transfer to Athlone.

Knock-on delays are also expected on Galway route services.

Tullamore

The Ballyhaunis collision came after a man was hospitalised earlier on Thursday when a train collided with a tractor in Tullamore, Co Offaly.

Irish Rail said that incident happened at around 3.50pm at a “user worked level crossing” used by the man, who is a farmer, to access the lands either side of the track.

The man was taken to hospital for treatment of injuries believed to be non-life-threatening.
