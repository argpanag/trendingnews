YouTuber and Twitch streamer Reggie Travers is accusing his brother of sexually abusing him when he was younger.

"My brother, he took advantage of me. … I'm not scared no more," Travers said in a tearful YouTube video shared on Sept. 27.

"I knew this day would come," he continued. "They say the truth will set you free, and I've been running from this for so long."

Travers, 21, boasts over 1.1 million followers on the livestreaming platform Twitch and over 569,000 subscribers on YouTube, and he's collaborated with several big names in streaming, including Kai Cenat, who is the seventh-most subscribed person on Twitch and the winner of the Streamer Awards' streamer of the year in both 2022 and 2023.

Cenat is also named in Travers' emotional Sept. 27 video.

Streamer Kai Cenat's career in the spotlight

Popular online streamer Kai Cenat is in the spotlight again. Three years after his controversial "giveaway" gathering in New York led to a riot and multiple arrests, crowds continue to follow the content creator. According to Atlanta News First, Cenat was hosting auditions for his Streamer University event Tuesday, which caused a large crowd to form in Atlanta’s Summerhill neighborhood. Police made multiple arrests and the event was cancelled. According to local news station WXIA-TV, the streamer announced that auditions would move forward at a new location Wednesday.

"Kai, you wrong as hell, 'cause you know somebody took advantage of RaKai," he said, referencing content creator and Twitch streamer QuVonn RaKai Linder, known online as RaKai, who is another frequent collaborator with Cenat.

Linder, 17, is often the youngest person on Cenat's livestreams. He rose to fame after appearing in Cenat's 2025 "Streamer University" series, a weeklong 24/7 livestream with aspiring content creators enrolled as students in a mock university. Linder was also nominated for best breakout streamer at the 2025 Streamer Awards.

"I'mma be real. Y'all need to tell him to speak up," Travers said, calling out Cenat. "And I'm glad I got this off my back. And if you just like me, I'mma speak up for us. So parents, if your kids tell you that somebody is making them uncomfortable, you better listen."

"Y'all gonna ignore me again," Travers added, speaking to Cenat and his associates. "[Then] it's gonna keep getting seriouser and seriouser."

The roughly three-minute-long video already has over 642,000 views on YouTube, and viral reposts on X have amplified its reach – one tweet shared by X user @FearedBuck on the same day has over 8.8 million views as of Sept. 28.

Neither Cenat nor Linder has publicly addressed Travers' allegations.

USA TODAY has reached out to Linder, Cenat and both Travers brothers for comment.

On Sep. 27, Linder was streaming live on Twitch at the Baltimore Ravens vs. Dallas Cowboys NFL game in Brazil, and read aloud a chat message from a viewer, asking him who "took advantage" of him. "What?" Linder exclaimed before moving on.

Who is Twitch streamer Reggie Travers?

Travers, who goes by the username Girlhefunny1 online, began posting on YouTube in 2020. He, Linder and Cenat have appeared on several livestreams together since then. Both Linder and Travers are a part of the Clover BoyZ, a group of male influencers who stream themselves hanging out together online.

One comment on Travers' Sep. 27 YouTube video with over 2,000 likes reads, "Child molestation by a family member in the black community has been swept under the rug for way too long. Keep speaking up for us, king, you not alone."

Other reactions on X are not as sympathetic and believe that Travers is putting Linder in a difficult situation. "Putting an allegation that a 17-year-old got molested just because you went through the same thing [is] wrong," wrote user @641shakur in a viral post.
