Former F1 boss Bernie Ecclestone 'arrested for illegally carrying shotgun at airport'

TheTools · 07.09.2026 16:01 · source · permalink

Former F1 boss Bernie Ecclestone has reportedly been arrested for illegally carrying a shotgun at a Portuguese aerodrome.

Ecclestone, 95, is understood to have been held at Tires Aerodrome, near Lisbon, after landing there in his private jet.

The weapon the former F1 boss was carrying has been reported locally as a shotgun.

The 95-year-old was previously arrested in May 2022 in Brazil for illegally carrying a gun while boarding a private aircraft.

At the time, Ecclestone acknowledged owning the gun but said he was unaware it was in his luggage.

The 95-year-old was freed and allowed to travel to Switzerland after paying bail, later describing the arrest as a "silly, tiny little incident".

Ecclestone denied being formally arrested despite the Sao Paulo state public security office confirming in a statement that an unnamed businessman was arrested for possession of a firearm at Campinas Airport.

Speaking about the incident afterwards, Ecclestone said he had bought the handgun from a former F1 mechanic in Brazil years earlier.
