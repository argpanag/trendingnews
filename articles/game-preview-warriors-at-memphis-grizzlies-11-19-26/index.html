Game Preview: Warriors at Memphis Grizzlies - 11/19/26

TheTools · 20.11.2026 02:08 · source · permalink

WARRIORS FACE GRIZZLIES ON BACK-TO-BACK
The Warriors head to Memphis to face the Grizzlies on the second night of a back-to-back at FedExForum on Thursday. Memphis is also on the second night of a back-to-back, facing the Cleveland Cavaliers on Wednesday. The Dubs are 0-1 against the Grizzlies, losing a double-digit lead in the final minutes at Chase Center on Oct. 23.

COMPELLING NOTE:
Warriors: Yaxel Lendeborg has the second-most points by a rookie this season (291) and is one of two rookies averaging at least 20 points per game (20.8 PPG). Lendeborg was first on NBA.com’s latest Rookie of the Year Ladder.
