September 2026 COE Results 1st Bidding: Hungry ghosts packed up and COE prices were jacked up, with all the car Categories revving to over $130k on the LTA tachometer | Articles | Motorist Singapore

TheTools · 09.09.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

In the first COE bidding exercise of September 2026, Cat A closed at $133,009; Cat B closed at $135,001; Cat C closed at $93,101; Cat D closed at $12,556; while Cat E closed at $137,890.

Cat A (car up to 1600cc & 130bhp, and EV with up to 110kW) received 1,708 bids for 1,195 COEs and its premium climbed by $4,508. Category A COE closed at $133,009.

Cat B (car above 1600cc or 130bhp, and EV with more than 110kW) received 1,333 bids for 934 COEs and its premium climbed by $4,000. Category B COE closed at $135,001.

Cat C (goods vehicle and bus) received 565 bids for 310 COEs and its premium increased by $3,099. Category C COE closed at $93,101.

Cat D (motorcycle) received 657 bids for 514 COEs and its premium increased by $1,255. Category D COE closed at $12,556.

Lastly, Cat E (all vehicles except motorcycle) received 513 bids for 300 COEs and climbed by $2,890. Category E COE closed at $137,890.
