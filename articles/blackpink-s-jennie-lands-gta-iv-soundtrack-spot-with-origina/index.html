Blackpink’s Jennie lands ‘GTA IV’ soundtrack spot with original song ‘Power Trip’

TheTools · 22.11.2026 02:56 · source · permalink

KUALA LUMPUR, Nov 21 — Rockstar Games has confirmed that Blackpink's Jennie features on the official soundtrack album for Grand Theft Auto VI, following earlier reports that she had recorded a song titled Power Trip for the game.

In its album release announcement yesterday, the developer linked to the full tracklist, which names Jennie as the performer of Power Trip, the 21st of 34 original songs.

Her involvement had come to light before the release when photographs of the vinyl sleeve circulated online, according to The Korea Herald.

The game's credits show that Jennie also co-wrote the song with Alexander Guy Cook, Badriia "Bibi" Bourelly and Corey Marlon Lindsay-Keay.

Rockstar partnered with Atlantic Records on Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, which also features new music from Dua Lipa, Lana Del Rey and Burna Boy.

The first six singles came out in September, including Travis Scott's RHYNO and Yung Lean's That's It, featuring Future and Metro Boomin.

The full album is now available to stream, with limited-edition vinyl and CD copies also on sale.

Power Trip is Jennie's latest soundtrack contribution this month. She also recorded Someone to Hold for the Netflix animated film Steps.
