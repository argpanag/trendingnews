'I didn't believe it!': Richmond resident scores $1M lottery win

TheTools · 21.11.2026 22:56 · source · permalink

A Richmond resident had to convince his wife he truly won $1 million in the lottery.

Jiawei W. found he won the $1-million Lotto 6/49 prize from the Nov. 4 draw after scanning his ticket on the BCLC Lotto app.

His wife was the first person he shared the news with.

“She thought I was joking. I didn’t believe it!”

Jiawei said he was "surprised and happy" to have won.

While he has no immediate plans on what to do with his winnings, Jiawei plans to help his family in some way.

The winning ticket was bought at the 7-Eleven on Bridgeport Road and Great Canadian Way, near Costco, in Richmond.
