🎧You have to believe you can win - Clement - AOL

TheTools · 17.09.2026 18:01 · source · permalink

Norwich City boss Philippe Clement tells BBC Radio Norfolk his players "have to believe" they can beat Premier League side Manchester City at the Etihad on Thursday night.

The two sides meet in round three of the Carabao Cup, with the top-flight hosts the holders, having won the competition seven times in the past 13 years.

Premier League Brighton await in round four after coming from behind to knock Manchester United out on Wednesday.

The Canaries are 14th in the Championship with nine points from a possible 21, and welcome 20th placed Bolton on Sunday.

Advertisement

Clement concedes he has to also have one eye on that game.

"The league stays the bread and butter of this club, and for the future of this club also," he said.

"But neither do I want to give away anything. I want a winning mentality, and it's not about not believing in games if you play one of the best teams in the world."
