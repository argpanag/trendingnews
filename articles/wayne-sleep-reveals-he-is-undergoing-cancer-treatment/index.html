Wayne Sleep reveals he is undergoing cancer treatment

TheTools · 02.11.2026 07:29 · source · permalink

Former Royal Ballet dancer says he is ‘on the mend’ after second diagnosis in 11 years

Wayne Sleep has revealed he is undergoing treatment for cancer.

The 78-year-old former Royal Ballet dancer shared the news in an Instagram post on Sunday.

He was previously diagnosed with prostate cancer in 2015 but he has not shared his specific diagnosis this time.

Sleep wrote: “A few people have commented that I haven’t been looking quite my usual self recently, so I thought I would share an update. Since the summer, I have been undergoing treatment for cancer.”

He said his treatment “appears to be working”, adding: “I am on the mend thanks to all at the Royal Marsden and am looking forward to getting back to full strength.

“I’m in good spirits and feeling positive about the future. Thank you to everyone for their kindness and concern.”

The post was accompanied by a photograph of Sleep wearing a jacket and a beanie at Kew Gardens earlier this week.

He previously spoke about his prostate cancer and recovery, telling the Mirror in 2016 that his PSA levels had first been raised in 2010. In July 2015, an MRI and biopsy led to a positive diagnosis.

Sleep opted for brachytherapy, which the NHS describes as a treatment involving tiny radioactive pellets being placed in the prostate to kill cancer cells.

The Telegraph has been campaigning for greater awareness and earlier detection of prostate cancer, calling for a targeted national screening programme to help identify men at risk before the disease advances. The “Testing Saves Lives” campaign aims to prevent men from being diagnosed too late to benefit from treatment.

The campaign highlights the increased risk faced by men over 50, black men and those with a family history of the disease, and calls for more proactive testing to save lives.

Sleep rose to fame as a dancer with the Royal Ballet, later establishing himself as a director, choreographer and actor.

He also enjoyed a career in musical theatre, appearing in productions including Cats and Cabaret, before becoming a familiar face on reality television.

He finished fourth on the ITV reality show I’m A Celebrity… Get Me Out Of Here! in 2003 and appeared on Celebrity Big Brother in 2018, finishing fifth.
