Amazon is creating over 300,000 new US jobs this holiday season with increased pay and expanded benefits

TheTools · 20.10.2026 12:30 · source · permalink

Key takeaways

  • Amazon is hiring over 300,000 people for seasonal, part-time, and full-time roles across its U.S. core operations nationwide this holiday season.
  • Beginning January 1, 2027, seasonal employees (who work at least 30 hours per week) will have access to healthcare coverage on their first day. Plan options start at $5 a week with $5 copays.
  • Full-time operations employees start at a minimum of $20/hour, with average hourly pay reaching nearly $24. With benefits, average total compensation for full-time employees exceeds $32/hour.
  • Seasonal employees earn an average of $20/hour, with access to a range of benefits and the opportunity to transition into full-time roles.
  • New job opportunities will be posted weekly from October through December. Visit amazon.com/localjobs to sign up for job alerts.
