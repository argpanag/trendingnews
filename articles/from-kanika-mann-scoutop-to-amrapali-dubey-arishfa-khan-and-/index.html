From Kanika Mann, ScoutOP to Amrapali Dubey, Arishfa Khan and more: Estimated net worth of popular Bigg Boss 20 contestants

TheTools · 28.10.2026 18:08 · source · permalink

Bigg Boss 20 is winning hearts with its entertaining episodes, unexpected twists, emotional moments and changing equations inside the house. Fans are following their favourite contestants closely, rooting for them on social media. Here is a look at the estimated net worth of popular Bigg Boss 20 contestants:-

Kanika Mann is a television actress and model known for playing the role of Guddan. She has also acted in Punjabi films and has participated in stunt based reality shows like Khatron Ke Khiladi 12. Through her television appearances and digital presence she has managed to make a name for herself in the entertainment industry. It is estimated that her net worth is around Rs 15 crore according to various entertainment and business websites.

Tanmay Singh, popularly known as ScoutOP is one of the prominent esports players and content creators from India. He made headlines for revealing that he earned about Rs 30 lakh in a month from gaming and content creation. According to media reports, his net worth is around Rs 20 crore.

Arishfa Khan started her career as a child artiste and slowly moved into music videos, digital content and social media. With a large following online she has managed to stay relevant outside of television. Some online sources have estimated her net worth to be around Rs 8-12 crore.

Kushal Tanwar popularly known as Gullu entered Bigg Boss 20 after gaining fame from reality TV shows. He gained a fan base through competing in reality shows like Roadies and Splitsvilla. Some online sources have estimated his net worth between Rs 1-6 crore.

Mahhi Vij is a well-known television actress who has appeared in popular shows like Laagi Tujhse Lagan and Balika Vadhu. She has also participated in reality shows like Nach Baliye and Khatron Ke Khiladi. Through her years in television she has become a familiar face in India. It is estimated that her net worth is between Rs 10-15 crore according to various entertainment websites.

Amrapali Dubey is one of the most prominent actors in the Bhojpuri industry. She gained fame through movies like Nirahua Hindustani. With her collaboration with actor Dinesh Lal Yadav popularly known as Nirahua, she gained fame. She is estimated to have a net worth of Rs 14-30 crore according to various entertainment websites.

Love Gill, whose full name is Lovepreet Kaur Gill is a Punjabi actress and model who entered the Bigg Boss 20 house after gaining attention from the voting system. She has worked in the Punjabi entertainment industry and has also been featured in music videos like "Facts" by Karan Aujla. However, unlike other prominent actors in the film industry, her entire financial details are not known publicly. Her net worth is said to be around Rs 10 crore.
