Slavia Prague set a unwanted record after loss to Villarreal

Slavia Prague set an unwanted record as they lost to Villarreal to make it 24 Champions League games without a win.

That takes them past the old European Cup or Champions League record of 23 by AEK Athens (1989 to 2006) and Steaua Bucharest (2006 to 2013).

The Czech club have not won in Europe's premier competition since beating Steaua, during the Romanian side's winless run, in September 2007.

Tajon Buchanan scored the only goal for Villarreal with a first-time finish inside the box finding the bottom corner in the second half.

Three big decisions from the officials all went against Slavia.

Mubarak Suleiman's goal was disallowed for offside when he pounced after Wiktor Nowak's shot was spilled by the goalkeeper.

Villarreal's Renato Veiga was shown a straight red card for elbowing Mojmir Chytil but that was reduced to a yellow card after a video assisant referee (VAR) review.

And then Slavia were awarded a penalty for a Pape Gueye foul on Nowak, which was overturned by the VAR.

Buchanan's winner takes Villarreal up into the qualification places, albeit before the majority of Wednesday's late games.
