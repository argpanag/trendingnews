Sunday Selection from Asia and Europe: Accumulator Tips for October 12th, 2026

TheTools · 12.10.2026 17:34 · source · permalink

Action returns to the AFC Champions League on Monday, with Saudi Pro League Champions Al Nassr facing Uzbekistan Super League Champions Neftchi Fergana. In Spain, Levante battle Sevilla, and Coventry will slug it out against Newcastle in England. Below are analyses and odds for these games.

Al Nassr vs Neftchi Fergana

Cristiano Ronaldo was on the scoresheet for Al Nassr in their last game after that controversial international break with Portugal. The Saudi defending champions won the game 3-0 against Diriyah. They will now return to continental action on Monday, hoping to bounce back to winning ways. They suffered a 4-0 loss to Al-Amin in their AFC Champions League opener. They have been impressive in the league, with six wins and one draw in their eight games so far.

Meanwhile, Neftchi Fergana are back in the AFC Champions League for the first time since 2012. They won the Uzbekistan Super League last season and are currently leading the league on goal difference with 52 points this season. In this competition, they started with a 1-0 win over Al-Jawiya, and will now hope to continue that run. Nevertheless, I will invest my money on the goal market in this game. The hosts have been impressive at home, winning all four games there, and I don’t see them losing here. However, the visitors will not give up without a fight.

Al Nassr vs Neftchi Fergana Trends

Al Nassr have won all four of their home games this season.

Al Nassr have seen at least three goals scored in nine of their last ten games.

Neftchi Fergana have scored at least three goals in five of their last six league games.

Bet Type: Over 2.5 goals

Levante vs Sevilla

Levante have been unconvincing this season in the league, picking up just five points in six games. In their last game, they lost 3-1 to Villarreal and are close to the relegation zone. They were on the verge of relegation last season, and nothing seems to have changed. They have managed one win in six games, and it came with a 5-2 win over Real Betis. Meanwhile, Sevilla seem to be enjoying their season. They are currently fifth on the table with 13 points in seven games. Nevertheless, I think the best option in this game is for both sides to find the back of the net.

Levante vs Sevilla Trends

Levante are winless in five of their last six games.

Levante have seen goals from both sides of the net in six of their last eight games.

Sevilla have seen goals from both sides of the net in five of their last seven games.

Sevilla have won seven of their last ten games against Levante.

Sevilla have scored in their last seven games.

Bet Type: BTTS

Coventry vs Newcastle

Coventry got their first win of the season in a 1-0 win over Nottingham Forest in their last game before the international break. They lost four of their first five games of the season and have scored only one goal so far. Their home form isn’t special, as they’ve lost their last three home games. Newcastle have been impressive since the start of the season, remaining unbeaten in six of their seven games, including four wins. So, while the odds are in favour of the visitors, I still believe the hosts should get their scoring back. So, I see both sides finding the back of the net in this game.

Coventry have lost their last three games.

Newcastle have won four of their last six games.

Coventry have scored only one goal this season.

In four of the last five games between Coventry and Newcastle, both sides have scored.

In five of Newcastle’s seven games this season, both sides have scored.
