Carabao Cup fourth round draw LIVE: Liverpool learn fate as Everton aim for spot

TheTools · 16.09.2026 19:51 · source · permalink

Hello and welcome to the Liverpool ECHO's live blog for the Carabao Cup fourth round draw.

Liverpool are waiting to discover their opponents in the next round of the competition after coming out on top in its third-round clash against Tottenham Hotspur on Tuesday night. Goals from Alexis Mac Allister, Cody Gakpo and Dominik Szoboszlai gave the Reds a 3-1 win and secured their place in the hat.

Tonight, it's Everton's turn to try and do the same. David Moyes' team take on Championship side Wolves at the Hill Dickinson Stadium (7.45pm kick-off).

Should they get through, the Toffees won't have long to wait to discover their opponents in the next round.

The draw will take place live on Sky Sports following the tie between Manchester United and Brighton and Hove Albion. The match kicks off at 8pm, with the draw slated to take place shortly after 10pm.

Stay right here for all of the build-up and, of course, the draw itself.

Andoni Iraola on 'deserved' win

Andoni Iraola said that Liverpool 'deserved' to book their place in the fourth round of the competition, despite him making 10 changes to his starting XI for the game against Spurs.

The Reds came out as 3-1 winners over their Premier League rivals to secure their place in the fourth round draw.

Speaking after the match, he said:

"Yes, it’s true that I made a lot of changes but I think I needed to do it because we could see the other day we lacked a little bit of energy, we needed fresh legs. I’m happy because all the players have given us intensity, have given us aggressiveness. "I think we’ve been good in a lot of parts of the game. And also happy because we’ve deserved the reward to win the game and to have earned the right to be in the next round."

When will the fourth round ties take place?

The ties of the fourth round will take place the week commencing 26 October. All matches will be available to watch live on Sky Sports.

Teams who get through the fourth round will play the fifth round of the week commencing 14 December.

The first leg of the semi-finals is scheduled for the week commencing 11 January, with the second leg on the week commencing 1 February.

The final will take place at Wembley Stadium on Sunday, 21 March.

Who will be doing the draw?

The draw will be hosted by Sky Sports presenter Pien Mulensteen.

It will be conducted by Former Liverpool man Jamie Redknapp and former Everton defender Ashley Young.
