Kim Jong Un's daughter hits ski slopes in rare photos as North Korean dictator tours mountain resort

TheTools · 18.11.2026 12:51 · source · permalink

North Korean leader's snowy resort visit comes as Trump raises possibility of meeting him during high-stakes Asia summit

Kim Jong Un's daughter hits ski slopes in photos as North Korean dictator tours mountain resort

Kim Jong Un watched from an ornate armchair at a snowy North Korean ski resort as his daughter skied in an all-white outfit. 

North Korean leader Kim Jong Un was photographed sitting in an ornate, throne-like armchair in the middle of a snowy ski resort while his daughter, viewed as his likely successor, carved down the slopes during an unusual family outing.

The striking images, released Monday by North Korean state media, show Kim wearing sunglasses and dress shoes while seated in the elaborate upholstered chair, holding a lit cigarette.

Other photographs show his daughter skiing in an all-white outfit as officials navigate the slopes nearby.

The girl, believed to be named Kim Ju Ae, has increasingly appeared alongside her father at high-profile public events. 

South Korea's National Intelligence Service has assessed that she is being positioned as his likely successor, although North Korea has not publicly confirmed a succession plan.

The snowy spectacle unfolded just as President Donald Trump raised the possibility that Kim could make an unexpected appearance during his upcoming high-stakes meetings with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin.

"We might have Kim Jong Un. He may come, I don't know. That's up to President Xi," Trump told reporters Monday.

Secretary of State Marco Rubio said Trump was serious about potentially meeting Kim, though no talks were confirmed.

"If we have a problem with North Korea, the only way we're ever going to solve it is if we engage with them," Rubio told reporters in the Philippines, adding that a meeting "may not happen."

The photographs were taken during Kim's Nov. 15–16 inspection of a tourist zone under development in Samjiyon, a mountainous city in northern North Korea, according to a report by the state-run Pyongyang Times reviewed by Fox News Digital.

Kim visited a ski resort under construction near Mount Pukphothae, where he examined skiing tracks and equipment and instructed officials to prepare the facilities for operation, the outlet reported.

His daughter was photographed skiing at the tourist zone on Nov. 15, according to an Associated Press caption accompanying the government-provided images.

The North Korean leader's visit was part of a broader effort to transform Samjiyon into a year-round mountain tourism destination, with plans for hotels, ski facilities, railway connections and an expanded airport.

The appearance of Kim's daughter added another layer of intrigue to the ski resort visit.

South Korean intelligence officials have pointed to her increasingly prominent public role as evidence that she may eventually inherit power from her father. In an October 2026, assessment reported by KBS World, the National Intelligence Service said her position as a potential successor was strengthening.

The agency has also noted her frequent appearances at military-related events, although the North Korean government has made no formal announcement identifying her as the country's next leader.

The latest photographs offered a different setting for the young girl, showing her navigating a groomed ski slope rather than accompanying her father at an official event.

Meanwhile, the possibility of renewed diplomacy between Trump and Kim remains uncertain.

Trump and Kim last met in 2019, when their summit in Hanoi ended without an agreement on North Korea's nuclear program. Efforts to restart negotiations have since stalled as Pyongyang has continued developing its nuclear and missile capabilities.
