DCU Accidentally Reveals Who Will Play the Next Big Superhero

TheTools · 15.10.2026 13:43 · source · permalink

DC Studios is in the midst of major adjustments following Warner Bros.' merger with Paramount (becoming Skydance), especially in how it develops its overall slate. After Clayface's release in Oct., James Gunn's Man of Tomorrow is the only live-action movie with a confirmed release date (Jul. 9, 2027), although more movies and TV shows are in development. This includes new animated projects, one of which is the new Mister Miracle series on HBO Max.

Mister Miracle was confirmed for development in Jul. 2025, with DC's Tom King writing the show based on his own 12-issue series from 2017. This story, which will inspire the show's plot, opens with Scott Free after a suicide attempt, then dives into a battle between New Genesis (Scott's home) and Apokolips (ruled by Darkseid), with Scott's wife, Big Barda, involved. The story has earned acclaim for its depiction of mundane human struggles amidst massive comic-book action, as Scott eventually embraces his reality as a father, partner, and hero. Now, as the show moves further into development, DC Studios has confirmed who will bring this unique hero into action.

Speaking with IGN, DC Studios co-CEO Peter Safran revealed that the studio officially cast Josh Hutcherson to voice Scott Free in the animated Mister Miracle show on HBO Max. Additionally, Mackenzie Davis will play Big Barda. Safran discussed how much he enjoys the "Mister Miracle" story from author Tom King, recalling times that King has been at conventions and has had "people come up to him and say this book saved my life" and expressing his excitement to "tell that story over eight episodes:"

"Somewhere in early stages. 'Absolute Batman,' just working on the scripts right now. 'Mister Miracle,' I think that Tom King really learned a lot in his season of co-creating and executive producing 'Lanterns' and he adapted his own book 'Mister Miracle'fully on his own, wrote all eight episodes, and I think it's just dynamite. Josh Hutcherson as Scott Free and Mackenzie Davis as Big Barda. And I think it's a book that I've always loved and I remember Tom telling us early on that, every convention he goes to, at least a couple people come up to him and say this book saved my life and I love that we got to tell that story over eight episodes. And it's just a beautiful story about, 'How do we survive in today's world knowing that we're never going to truly know the answers to the big questions in life?' It's a beautiful book, and the adaptation is fantastic, so we're excited about that."

Hutcherson has plenty of experience in massive blockbusters, having played Peeta Mellark in the Hunger Games saga and, more recently, Mike in both Five Nights at Freddy's films. He and Davis (Black Mirror) could also perfectly embody the size disparity between Mister Miracle and Big Barda if they are brought to live action, with Hutcherson standing at 5'5" and Davis at 5'10". For now, however, they will bring their voices to characters who have not gotten much opportunity to shine alongside bigger DC names like Superman and Batman.

Now, the show moves further into development ahead of an expected 2027 release, which will also include the DCU debut for the supervillain Darkseid and the return of Nathan Fillion's Guy Gardner/Green Lantern. How this fits into the greater DC Universe is still a mystery, but it will add a pair of thrilling and powerful heroes to a world that continues to expand to new heights and plot threads.
