The scorching heat is finally leaving, temperatures will drop below 30 degrees next week

TheTools · 13.09.2026 18:17 · source · permalink

The Kosovo Hydrometeorological Institute has announced that during the period 14–18 September, the weather in Kosovo will be mainly sunny with scattered clouds. On Tuesday, rain is expected in some parts of the country, while from Wednesday temperatures will gradually increase. Minimum temperatures will range from 6 to 13 degrees Celsius, while maximums can reach up to 29 degrees Celsius.

According to the IHMK, on ​​Monday the maximum temperatures will be 18–24°C, on Tuesday 19–24°C, on Wednesday 22–28°C, and on Thursday 23–29°C. On Friday the weather will be sunny and partly cloudy, with a chance of rain in limited areas, while the maximum temperatures will range from 23 to 27°C. Winds during the weekdays will blow mainly from the north, northeast, southwest and southeast, at speeds of 1–7 m/s.

IHMK has assessed that biometeorological and agrometeorological conditions will be generally favorable. Lower temperatures in the morning hours will bring coolness, while during the day, especially from Wednesday, warmer conditions are expected and suitable for outdoor activities. The ultraviolet radiation index will be mainly at a moderate level, with higher values ​​around noon on sunny days. /Gazeta Express
