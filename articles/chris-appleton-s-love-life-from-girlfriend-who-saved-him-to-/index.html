Chris Appleton's love life from girlfriend who 'saved' him to famous ex-husband

TheTools · 26.09.2026 15:30 · source · permalink

Chris Appleton has previously spoken candidly about his enduring bond with the mother of his children.

The 43-year-old celebrity hairdresser and author, who is currently competing in Strictly Come Dancing 2026, was in an eight-year relationship with Katie Katon before coming out as gay in 2009.

He began his romance with Kate while the pair were both employed at Georges Hairdressing in Leicester, where he first established himself as a stylist.

The couple later separated having had two children together; Billy Appleton was born in 2002, when Chris was just 19, and Kitty-blu Appleton followed two years later in 2004.

Reflecting on his previous relationship during an appearance on Jamie Laing's Great Company podcast in May, he said: "Me and Kate got together when I was 19. When we broke up when I was 27, it was really dark. When I came out as gay, she saw me, when I couldn't see myself.", reports the Daily Star.

"For a lot of years, I couldn't see myself, even with the shame of me being gay and it was so painful for her. She saw me and still does. We spent Christmas together with the kids this year, it was New Year's Eve, I put my arm round her and I said 'I did it, Kate'."

He went on to add: "Although sometimes me and Kate get upset as our kids didn't have a conventional family but it's loyal and it's solid. So I think, look, we still argue like cat and dog but there was a pact we made when we met. She saved me.

"Having kids and this bond that never left, it never will. Even when she was in pain and shame, and hated me and probably didn't want to talk to me again, she didn't because she could see what I was going through. It takes a lot of compassion to do that."

Chris had previously disclosed that Katie was the first person he confided in about his sexuality because he "didn't want to be gay". Speaking on Jay Shetty's On Purpose podcast last August, he explained: "Because I loved her, I really loved her, and I didn't want to be gay. I didn't want to be different."

Breaking the news to their children proved even more challenging, with Katie's mother ultimately taking on the responsibility of telling Billy and Kitty-blu. "All of a sudden, I just felt like I'd just messed their life up, and I felt like I'd failed as a dad because my job was to protect them."

Chris and Kate have remained close over the years and have even shared holidays together. In 2025, they reunited to mark the Fourth of July with their children.

"So grateful for a weekend with my family, full of laughter, love, and those little moments that stay with you," he wrote in a post marking the occasion. "Always everything I need."

Chris is understood to be single after his marriage to The White Lotus actor Lukas Gage ended. The couple married in Las Vegas in April 2023, with Chris' close friend and client Kim Kardashian officiating the ceremony.

The short-lived marriage endured approximately six months before Chris initiated divorce proceedings in November 2023, stating irreconcilable differences, which was concluded in 2024, with Lukas describing the brief relationship as "a manic episode for six months."
