The Mega Millions winning numbers are in for the Tuesday, Sept. 15 jackpot, which is on another hot climbing streak after no ticket won the big prize.

How to play the Mega Millions

Buy a ticket at a convenience store, gas station, grocery store or, in some states, online.

Pick five white ball numbers (1–70) and one Mega Ball (1–24), or let the computer choose for you with a Quick Pick.

All tickets include a built-in multiplier, boosting non-jackpot prizes by 2X to 10X.
