McConnell, Oubre & DeRozan's status for Pacers-Nuggets: Injuries, Odds & How to Watch

TheTools · 07.11.2026 21:22 · source · permalink

In this story:

Game date, time and location: Saturday, Nov. 7, 5:00 p.m. EST, Arena CDMX, Mexico City, Mexico

TV: DAZN (Indiana), DAZN, Altitude Sports (Denver)

Radio: 93.5 FM, 107.5 FM (Indiana), Altitude Sports Radio 92.5 FM/950 AM (Denver)

VITALS: The Indiana Pacers (3-6) and Denver Nuggets (4-3) meet for the first of two matchups in 2026-2027. Last season, the Nuggets won the season series, 2-0.

The Pacers are 47-57 all-time in the regular season against the Nuggets, including 30-23 at home and 17-34 on the road.

The Pacers were slated to come back with a new and improved team with Tyrese Haliburton back from a torn achilles and Ivica Zubac and Kelly Oubre replacing Myles Turner and Bennedict Mathurin, but have gotten off to a very unhealthy start to the season, injury-wise.

This offseason, the Nuggets signed DeMar DeRozan, Marvin Bagley in free agency, re-signed Tyus Jones and Spencer Jones, traded Peyton Watson for a first round pick and a second round pick, and drafted Trevon Brazile and Bryce Hopkins with the 35th pick and 49th pick in the 2026 NBA Draft, respectively.

Most recently, the Grizzlies are coming off a, 107-103, win against the Memphis Grizzlies.
