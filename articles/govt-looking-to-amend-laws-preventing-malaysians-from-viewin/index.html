Govt looking to amend laws preventing Malaysians from viewing their MyKad through MyGOV app, says Gobind

TheTools · 24.11.2026 19:49 · source · permalink
    bookmark

KUALA LUMPUR: Legal restrictions are preventing Malaysians from viewing their identity cards digitally through the MyGOV app, with the government looking at amending the relevant laws to allow this, says Digital Minister Gobind Singh Deo.

He said this differed from the digital driving licence, which users could already view on their screens through the Road Transport Department's system.

Gobind said the matter was discussed at a meeting with GovTech, MyGOV and MyDigital ID representatives the previous day, following instructions from the Prime Minister.

He said the government was also looking at bringing personal documents, including identity cards, passports, birth certificates and marriage certificates, into a single application.

The initiative would be carried out in stages, with existing government applications being brought together through MyDigital ID and MyGOV serving as a single access point for users.

Gobind said payment facilities would also need to be integrated in a later phase to allow users to complete transactions such as passport renewals through the application.

He said this when winding up the committee-stage debate on the Supply Bill 2027 for the Digital Ministry in the Dewan Rakyat on Tuesday (Nov 24).

Separately, Gobind said the Personal Data Protection Department currently had only 48 officers, which he acknowledged was insufficient. He said a proposal to expand its workforce had been submitted to the Government, with the process under way.
