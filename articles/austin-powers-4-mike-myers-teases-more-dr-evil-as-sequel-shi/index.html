Austin Powers 4: Mike Myers Teases More Dr. Evil as Sequel Shifts to His Point of View

by Phil Sadsad | Oct 30, 2026

Yes. Mike Myers has said he is writing the fourth film. During a June 2026 World Cup watch party, he was asked whether audiences would see Austin Powers 4, and Myers immediately responded, “Yes.” Later, on Hollywood Insiders with Hawk Koch, he gave a clearer update on the script and the direction of the sequel.

Myers says the new story currently gives Dr. Evil more attention. He told Hawk Koch, “There is going to be more Dr. Evil,” and added, “This one is more from Dr. Evil’s point of view so far.” That “so far” matters because the script is still evolving and could change before the fourth Austin Powers movie reaches production.
