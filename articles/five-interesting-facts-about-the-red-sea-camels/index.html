Five interesting facts about the Red Sea Camels

TheTools · 30.09.2026 18:56 · source · permalink

We dive into Bafana’s opponents, Eritrea — nicknamed the Red Sea Camels — ahead of their Africa Cup of Nations (Afcon) qualifier against Bafana at Cairo’s Al-Salam Stadium on Wednesday evening (6pm SA time).

  • Eritrea are ranked 184th in the world, 130 places behind Bafana. The Red Sea Camels are 50th on the continent, 40 slots adrift of SA.
  • Their best Fifa ranking was 121st in August 2007 but extended periods of being unranked due to inactivity and tournament withdrawals saw them fall down the pecking order.
  • Fear of mass desertions has historically forced the national sports authorities to pull the Eritrea national team out of the qualifying phases of major tournaments such as the World Cup and Afcon.
  • Unbearable domestic conditions and indefinite military service are some of the factors forcing many Eritrean players and staff to defect and seek asylum during international away matches. To avoid defections, the local FA has opted for more diaspora players in recent times. Seven players were reported to have gone missing in SA after Eritrea played against Eswatini in the preliminary phase of the current Afcon qualifiers.
  • Striker Ali Sulieman, 26, of Egyptian elite league outfit Al-Masry, and 29-year-old midfielder Tesfaldet Tekie, who’s on the books of Swedish top-flight side Hammarby IF, are two of the Red Sea Camels’ key players.
