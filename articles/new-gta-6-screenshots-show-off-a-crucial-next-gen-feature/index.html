New GTA 6 Screenshots Show Off A Crucial Next-Gen Feature

TheTools · 17.11.2026 16:15 · source · permalink

New GTA 6 gameplay images show fans random world events that impact the entire environment

With release just a couple of days away, Rockstar's marketing for Grand Theft Auto 6 has officially gone into hyperdrive. In addition to collaborations with entire cities, basketball teams, clothing brands, and South Park, the brand has also begun a partnership with something you'd never expect: The Weather Channel. It's unusual, but also a perfect opportunity to showcase GTA 6's enhanced weather system.

We've heard bits and bobs about the weather and how well it reflects Florida's microclimates throughout the year across various previews. Rockstar has emphasized that its new system will feel grander, thanks to events like hurricanes. GTA 6 has a dedicated wind system, and this element will interact with environmental details like blades of grass. Aspects like rainbows and puddles will spontaneously appear throughout the world. When there's a tropical storm, you'll see that reflected even in the buildings.

It all sounds exciting, but we've scarcely seen any of the weather system in action. Rockstar has published dozens of official screenshots on its website and also given publications exclusive images of the game, but these have mostly focused on characters like Jason and Lucia. In a series of posts where The Weather Channel offers a mock forecast for Leonida, GTA 6's setting, the meteorological service also published a handful of screenshots depicting weather phenomena. The leading image in this post appears to be the first time we've really gotten a good look at GTA 6's rain and how it might interact with lighting in the scenery. But there's more!

What do storms, hurricanes, and other weather events look like in GTA 6?

In this screenshot, we get a sneak peek at GTA 6's range of cloud formations and how they'll interact with crepuscular rays.

The next image slyly reveals how the weather can vary across the border between two regions. On one side, you can see a faint rainbow forming in the thick of a mighty storm. The other side still has hints of the downpour, but things have started to clear up.

In this snapshot, the game shows what it looks like when a tropical storm starts to form and lightning begins striking the environment.

The next scene shows what happens when GTA 6 experiences high humidity near a bay area — a wispy mist hovers above the ground.

And finally, here's what it'll look like if you're a law-abiding citizen stuck in traffic when a storm is about to hit. You'll note that a sunset is quietly forming amid foreboding clouds.

GTA 6 releases on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S on Nov. 19.
