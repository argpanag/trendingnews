Sligo Junior Minister calls for fuel allowance threshold to be increased for those living alone | OceanFM

TheTools · 26.09.2026 17:02 · source · permalink

There are calls to increase the fuel allowance threshold for older people living alone in the North West.

 

The scheme is means tested for people over 66, with the current threshold standing at €1,068 per week for a couple and €534 for a person living alone.

 

However it's being argued that the support should not be halved for single-occupant households.
