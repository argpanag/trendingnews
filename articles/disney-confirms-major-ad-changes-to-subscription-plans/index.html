Disney+ Confirms Major Ad Changes To Subscription Plans

TheTools · 19.09.2026 16:01 · source · permalink

Disney+ is introducing a controversial change.

In 2019, The Walt Disney Company launched a flagship direct-to-consumer streaming service called Disney+. The service achieved amazing success by reaching over 10 million subscribers within its first 24 hours. Thanks to the major IPs and strategic acquisitions under the former CEO Bob Iger, Pixar, Marvel Studios, Lucasfilm, and 21st Century Fox all have their content streamed through Disney+. With some of the biggest IPs in the world, Disney+ is a fierce competitor in the streaming world.

As per an email sent out to subscribers, Disney+ has confirmed that ad changes are expected for subscribers on all tiers, including ad-free subscriptions. It appears UK subscribers are receiving the email with some U.S. terms and conditions yet to be updated with the information, although it's unclear when or if these changes will be implemented. The Disney+ support page also states that live events and linear channels will show advertisements regardless of subscription tier. The Disney+ subscriber email states:

"Changes to your Disney+ Subscriber Agreement, include, in summary, the following (though we do encourage you to read the new Subscriber Agreement in full):

We've clarified that all Service Plans may include: (i) promotional content, (ii) sponsorships, and (iii) advertisements before/after playback of Content and in channels, live/as-live, special events, and any third-party services content."

"Changes to your Disney+ Subscriber Agreement, include, in summary, the following (though we do encourage you to read the new Subscriber Agreement in full):

We've clarified that all Service Plans may include: (i) promotional content, (ii) sponsorships, and (iii) advertisements before/after playback of Content and in channels, live/as-live, special events, and any third-party services content."

Junior Mode profiles will be the only exception to the updated subscriber agreement and will have no commercial advertising and sponsor placements. Other changes announced by Disney+ include, "We have updated the wording relating to subscriptions, recurring billing periods, and payment plans. In addition, we have added new wording to cover service plans that offer discounted prices for a set promotional period (e.g. a six-month discounted payment plan) during the ongoing subscription. We’ve updated the notice period for price changes to your Disney+ Subscription from 30 days to 28 days. We have added wording concerning your legal right of withdrawal within 14 days of subscribing."

The email sent out to subscribers states that "all these changes will come into effect in 30 days," so those who use Disney+ have the option to opt out before the next recurring payment if they don't approve of Disney's changes. However, it's unclear which other regions will be impacted by these new changes that once offered ad-free tiers, and now seems to offer tiers with some ads.

Disney+ offers a wide range of tiers including a basic plan with ads for $11.99 per month, Disney+ Premium with no ads for $15.99 to $18.99 or $189.99 to $190 per year. There are also bundles available with access to Hulu and ESPN content.
