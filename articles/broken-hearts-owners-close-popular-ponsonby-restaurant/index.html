‘Broken hearts’: Owners close popular Ponsonby restaurant

TheTools · 16.09.2026 16:31 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

Mekong Baby director Marcin Kulak says the Ponsonby restaurant is no longer financially sustainable. Photo / Michael Craig

A popular Ponsonby Rd eatery has closed its doors after more than a decade in business.

Mekong Baby owners Jackie and Marcin Kulak said in a social media post this evening that operating the restaurant was “no longer viable”.

In a message titled “A very difficult goodbye”, they described the sacrifices they made to keep the restaurant afloat, including selling their family home.

“We have poured our hearts into it. We have worked through long days, late nights, incredibly challenging times and all of the ups and downs that come with running a hospitality business.

“We have always believed that if we worked hard enough, cared enough and kept giving our best, we could keep going. But sometimes, hard work alone isn’t enough.”

The restaurant served Southeast Asian fusion cuisine and received glowing reviews from Herald food reviewers over the years.

It opened in 2013.

The Kulaks, who took over the restaurant in 2019, noted “enormous” changes to the hospitality industry in recent years.

“Sadly, it has never truly returned to what it was before Covid,” they said.

“The economic pressures of the past few years have continued to build, making running Mekong Baby no longer financially sustainable.

“We have fought incredibly hard to keep going. Including selling our family home, and putting every penny to our name back into the business.”

The owners also thanked their staff for the “kindness and love” they had shown, “especially now during our darkest time”.

“To every single customer who has walked through the doors, thank you from the bottom of our hearts.”

The owners signed off their announcement: “With love, gratitude and broken hearts.”
