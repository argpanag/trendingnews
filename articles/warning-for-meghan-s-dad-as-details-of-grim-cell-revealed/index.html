Warning for Meghan’s dad as details of grim cell revealed

TheTools · 16.11.2026 02:13 · source · permalink

Thomas Markle is being held in Florida jail after cutting off his ankle monitor, and is being warned to keep his head down.

Meghan Markle's father charged with child abuse material possession

After cutting off his ankle monitor, Thomas Markle is now being held in at Pinellas County Jail in Florida, with his pre-trial release revoked.

Attorney Nicholas Dorsten spoke to The Sun, and has said the nature of Mr Markle’s allegations, alongside his notoriety means he could “absolutely become a target”.

The estranged father of Meghan Markle was arrested and charged with one count of possessing child sexual abuse material, after 78 items were seized by authorities after the search of Californian storage units.

After his initial court appearance, the judge ordered him released to await trial on the condition that he submitted to home detention and electronic monitoring, but early Tuesday morning he cut off his ankle monitor and was arrested.

Mr Dorsten warned the 82-year-old could face a beating from someone looking to establish a reputation.

“Bad guys don’t like people like him,” Mr Dorsten said, referring to the fact that many of his fellow inmates would have children of their own.

Mr Markle is confined to a wheelchair, and at the time of his arrest was living in a care facility in Florida because of his dementia.

His new sleeping quarters are a big change for the former Hollywood lighting director.

The Pinellas County Jail is described by formed inmates as a grim place with little privacy and prisoners can expect a breakfast of “canned peaches or powdered eggs”.

The small 3.65m by 3.65m cells are shared with another inmate, which are part of larger pods, containing six to eight cells with a communal area and television in the middle.

While prisoners are typically allowed access to a tablet to remain in contact with those outside the prison, local lawyer Samara Ballard said Mr Markle would not be allowed one due to the nature of the charges he faces.

Ms Ballard has warned Mr Markle to keep his head down, “if you are getting into argument they can put you in confinement and then you’re not coming out of your cell”.

If convicted, Mr Markle could face up to 20 years in prison, the Justice Department said.

Meghan and Prince Harry have not commented publicly on the charge.
