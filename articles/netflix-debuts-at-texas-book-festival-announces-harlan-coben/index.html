Netflix Debuts at Texas Book Festival: Announces Harlan Coben's ‘The Innocent’, ‘Virgin River’ Season 9 and ‘3 Body Problem’ Return Date - About Netflix

Reveals New 'Netflix Effect' Data on Book Sales and Heartland Adaptations

Netflix made its first-ever appearance at the Texas Book Festival in Austin November 14-15, bringing together authors, creators, and fans to celebrate the literary origins of our biggest series. As part of this effort, we announced a limited series adaptation of Harlan Coben’s The Innocent, the return of the Emmy-nominated series 3 Body Problem on January 14, 2027, and the Season 9 renewal of Virgin River. The renewal makes Virgin River Netflix’s longest-running original scripted series as it surpasses half a billion views. 

We also unveiled new data on the “Netflix Effect,” demonstrating how book adaptations drive viewers back to the page. Since 2025, book adaptations have generated over 13 billion global views and nearly 20% of total view hours on Netflix, with releases boosting US book sales by 10x on average in the 30 days after premiere. The Global Top 10 lists have featured a book adaptation for 98% of 2026 so far.

Harlan Coben’s The Innocent Comes to Netflix

Harlan Coben is bringing another page-turner to Netflix. The Innocent, the New York Times bestselling novel, is a new limited series from showrunners, writers and executive producers Robert Hull, Bryan Wynbrandt and Steven Lilien, the creative team behind I Will Find You. Alongside the series, Netflix forged a new creative partnership with Hull, Wynbrandt, and Lilien of StoryMath, who will continue to create new series exclusively for Netflix. Harlan Coben serves as executive producer, and Ben Coben produces through Final Twist Productions. 

Robert Hull, Bryan Wynbrandt and Steven Lilien said, “We’re thrilled to call Netflix our home and couldn’t be more grateful to our friends Jinny, Cole, James, Mike, and of course, the incredible Harlan Coben, for their continued partnership. Bringing I Will Find You to life and seeing it connect with audiences around the world was an amazing experience. We’re excited to bring our signature brand of twisty and propulsive storytelling to The Innocent and the many new stories we look forward to telling at Netflix.”

Cole Galvin, Netflix VP of Drama Series, added, “Our partnership with Harlan Coben is one of the greatest creative relationships at Netflix, and The Innocent marks our 15th adaptation together. His addicting storytelling resonates with audiences around the world, and Robert Hull, Bryan Wynbrandt and Steven Lilien proved that with their adaptation of I Will Find You, which became a global hit for us. We’re thrilled to welcome them to the Netflix family and can’t wait for audiences everywhere to experience their take on The Innocent.“ 

This marks Coben’s 15th adaptation with Netflix, and his audience keeps growing. Since the Netflix Top 10 launched in 2021, his shows have made the weekly Global Top 10 41 times, reached the Top 10 in 93 countries and drawn over 450 million global views since 2023.

Fool Me Once became one of our most-viewed titles of 2024, with over 98 million views, and the novel reached Amazon UK’s Best Sellers of 2024 list and The New York Times Best Seller list. I Will Find You rose to #5 on the Most Popular list with 120.8M views, Coben’s second series there and his first US series to make it. Myron Bolitar, his longest-running series at 12 books, is now in production in New York, with David E. Kelley and Kyle Long co-showrunning.

3 Body Problem Returns January 14, 2027

From Emmy Award® winners David Benioff and D.B. Weiss and Emmy-nominated writer-producer Alexander Woo, the Emmy-nominated drama series 3 Body Problem returns on Thursday, January 14, 2027. Based on the international bestselling novels, the series debuted in 2024, spending seven weeks in the Global Top 10, reaching the Top 10 in 93 countries and hitting #1 in 44, with 46.6 million views. Three songs from its soundtrack also reached Billboard’s Top TV Songs chart, including Lana Del Rey’s “Video Games” at #1. This season, the alien invasion nears and humanity prepares to meet them — on Earth and beyond. While a space fleet embarks on a mission that spans centuries, a secret locked inside of one man's mind might be humanity's only hope for survival. Watch the Season 2 teaser HERE.

Virgin River Renewed for Season 9, Hitting Two Milestones

Netflix renewed Virgin River for Season 9, making it Netflix’s longest-running original scripted series. We also announced that the series has surpassed half a billion views. Across all seven seasons, Virgin River has spent 28 weeks in the Global Top 10 and reached the Top 10 in over 80 countries. Season 8 premieres in 2027.

The Hunting Wives Gets a New Book Cover Ahead of Season 2

The Hunting Wives, which was recently renewed for Season 3 ahead of Season 2’s November 26 debut, has drawn over 35 million views since its July 2025 premiere. May Cobb’s bestselling novel has felt the love. Christine Ball, EVP & Publisher at Berkley, said: "Originally published in 2021, May Cobb’s ‘The Hunting Wives’ surged back into the spotlight following its Netflix premiere. In just one week, book publisher Berkley has gone back to press twice on the trade paperback and saw a +5,000% increase across all formats in the same one-week period year over year." Readers can pick up a new series tie-in cover beginning November 17.

The Netflix Effect: Adaptations Send Readers Back to Bookstores

Netflix book adaptations drive significant viewership and fan engagement, accounting for nearly 20% of total view hours and over 13 billion global views since 2025. Publishers also see a direct impact on readership, as titles experience an average 10x increase in US book sales across audio, print, and ebook formats within the first 30 days following the adaptation’s Netflix premiere.

  • The Whisper Man: Sales increased almost 500% (Celadon Books) and Spotify audiobook listening jumped 426% globally. The film hit #1 in 68 countries with 79.5M views.

  • Remarkably Bright Creatures: Sales across all formats rose 560% from the March first-look announcement to peak week in May. (Ecco/HarperCollins)

  • East of Eden: Sales across all formats climbed 200% (Penguin Classics), and the book hit #1 on Amazon’s Best Sellers list 74 years after publication. First-time audiobook listeners on Spotify jumped 156%, and Pinterest searches spiked 869% since last month’s premiere.

  • Little House on the Prairie: Sales for the series across all formats soared 110% month to month. (Storytide/HarperCollins)

  • A Good Girl’s Guide to Murder: Seasons 1 and 2 sent the book back to the NYT bestseller list (Delacorte Press at Penguin Random House), and first-time audiobook listeners on Spotify surged 183%.

And The Netflix Effect is global. Adaptations in this year’s Global Top 10 came from over 25 different  countries, from Nordic crime fiction (The Chestnut Man) to Indian romance (Musafir Cafe) to Mexican historical fiction (Lovesick). According to NielsenIQ, sales of The Leopard (Italy) were up more than 1,180% in the week after launch; ARK bookstore saw sales rise more than 1,000% over Easter vs. the prior year for Detective Hole (Norway); and Museum of Innocence (Türkiye) led to a 406% increase in book sales on Hepsiburada in the weeks following the premiere.

A Look at Netflix’s Texas Book Festival Debut

In celebration of our Texas Book Festival debut, we hosted the panel, “The Netflix Effect: How Book-to-Screen Adaptations Are Capturing the Heartland,” with authors Cobb (The Hunting Wives) and Katherine Center (Guarding Stars) joined Virgin River showrunner Patrick Sean Smith and Netflix Vice President of Drama Cole Galvin, moderated by Cosmopolitan’s Tamara Fuentes. They discussed why stories rooted in the heartland resonate with audiences worldwide and the Netflix Effect that powers bestseller lists. “People come to comfort and heartland stories for connection and optimism—the kind of story you put on to unwind. Those titles have drawn more than a billion views on Netflix since 2023, and they’ve reached viewers in more than 90 countries,” said Galvin. “But what matters most to me are the readers who loved these books first. We work with authors and creators to honor what made the stories special, and then we invite new fans in. From Virgin River to Little House on the Prairie to Guarding Stars, our job is to give audiences more of what they love.”

Festivalgoers also stopped by the Netflix Reading Lounge for free coffee, Netflix tote bags and customizable bookmarks, and met authors at book signings. At a special screening, fans got an early look at Guarding Stars ahead of its November 18 premiere, with special appearances by Center, Jared and Gen Padalecki.
