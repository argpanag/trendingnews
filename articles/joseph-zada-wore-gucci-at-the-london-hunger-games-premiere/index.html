Joseph Zada wore Gucci at the London Hunger Games premiere

TheTools · 17.11.2026 15:54 · source · permalink

Joseph Zada was in New York on Monday for the premiere of Lionsgate's The Hunger Games: Sunrise On The Reaping, a few days before the movie is due in theaters.

A Deadline gallery from November 16 puts him on the carpet as sixteen-year-old Haymitch Abernathy, the character the story follows. The same report sets the theatrical opening for November 20.

At the film's London premiere, he wore a Gucci Spring 2027 suit from the Italian house's The Store show, styled by Jason Bolden, Red Carpet Fashion Awards reported.

Jennifer Lawrence and Josh Hutcherson, the original Hunger Games stars, walked the same carpet, according to Mezha, along with cast members from across the franchise.

The film is scheduled to open in theaters on Friday, November 20.
