Earthquake with preliminary magnitude of 5.7 strikes Japan

TheTools · 08.11.2026 07:05 · source · permalink

A strong earthquake with a preliminary reading of magnitude 5.7 has struck central Japan

TOKYO -- A strong earthquake with a preliminary reading of magnitude 5.7 struck central Japan early Sunday, according to the Japan Meteorological Agency. Japanese media reported dozens of injuries.

A wide area near Aichi Prefecture was affected and smaller aftershocks have followed.
