San Gabriel Valley Girls Athlete of the Week: Isla Terrill, Monrovia

TheTools · 06.10.2026 13:50 · source · permalink

Monrovia cross country standout Isla Terrill turned in a season best at the Woodbridge Invitational

San Gabriel Valley Girls Athlete of the Week

Name: Isla Terrill

School: Monrovia

Sport: Cross Country

Year: Junior

Noteworthy: Isla Terrill turned in a season-best mark at the Woodbridge Invitational at the Great Park in Irvine over the weekend, finishing in 17:11.69 over the three-mile course that brings together some of the top distance runners in the state. A week earlier, Terrill won the 42nd annual Laguna Hills Invitational, clocking a 17:51.1 over the three mile course to win the race. Terrill is just warming up, last season she finished sixth at the CIF-SS Division 3 finals with a personal-best 16:54.50 at Mt. SAC.
