Montgomery's first H-E-B is officially open, bringing a grocery store spanning more than 127,000 square feet and a True Texas BBQ restaurant to Highway 105 and FM 2854.

Nearly 900 people attended the early-morning grand opening, according to H-E-B. Some camped overnight, with the first customer arriving at 10 p.m. the night before.

The store employs more than 400 people and offers curbside pickup, home delivery and a pharmacy with drive-thru service. Its True Texas BBQ restaurant serves meat smoked in-house over Texas oak, along with sides such as brisket beans, creamed corn and potato salad. Customers can eat inside or pick up their orders through the restaurant's drive-thru.

Grocery departments include a bakery that makes fresh tortillas and breads, an in-store sushi counter, an Asian grill and prepared meals. Shoppers will also find produce, meat, seafood, floral arrangements, household goods, outdoor plants, and products from local vendors.

"I'm eager to give back to this community and to foster a store environment that's welcoming to all our neighbors," General Manager Byron O'Bannon said in a statement.

The property also features displays recognizing Montgomery's connection to the Texas flag and Charles B. Stewart, who created it there in 1839. A 60-foot Texas flag flies from a 150-foot pole above a limestone base shaped like the state. A historical marker honors Stewart, while a mural inside highlights his legacy and Montgomery's history.

To mark the opening, H-E-B announced $40,000 in donations, with $10,000 each going to the Montgomery Education Foundation, Montgomery Historical Society, Mighty Oaks Foundation and Hope Resale.

The Mighty Oaks donation supports its work with military veterans and their families. H-E-B also recognized two veterans who received mortgage-free homes through its Homes Built for Heroes program and presented them with gift cards.

The company said it will also work with Montgomery schools to plant trees on their campuses.
