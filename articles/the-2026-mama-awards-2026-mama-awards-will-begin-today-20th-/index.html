The 2026 MAMA AWARDS (2026 MAMA AWARDS) will begin today (20th) in Osaka, Japan.

The 2026 MAMA AWARDS will be held in Osaka, Kyocera Dome, Japan, on the 20th and 21st. Under the concept of "AMUSIC INTERSTELLAR," this year's awards ceremony will illuminate music from various eras beyond time, space, generation, and taste. It will showcase the past, present, and future of K-POP with the "ALL GENERATION" stage, which encompasses newcomers to artists with long career experience.

MAMA AWARDS, which has a history of 27 years this year, has presented a variety of stages, including joint performances by artists and the first release of new songs. Last year, it was viewed in more than 250 regions around the world and recorded more than 2 billion online impressions.

This award ceremony will also feature a collaborative stage for generations. The male duo Panic, which has resumed its activities in about 20 years, will perform with members of ALLDAY PRODUCT, CORTIS, Hearts2Hearts, WOODZ and ZEROBASEONE. CL, who will appear on MAMA AWARDS for the first time in 11 years, will present "STILL THE BADDEST", the first collaborative stage with ALLDAY PRODUCT. Celebrating its 20th anniversary, BIGBANG will perform a collaboration with the animated character "Baby Dinosaur Dooley" for the first time.

Performances that combine music and other cultural and artistic fields were also prepared. Through collaboration with Korean contemporary artist Lee Bae, ENHYPEN will present a stage that combines music and art, and ENHYPEN will hold a cinematic stage that combines the world view with the movie "Dune: Part 3," which is scheduled to be released in December. In addition, in collaboration with Google Gemini, it will also unveil a stage that combines technology and art.

The heat of 'MAMA FAN FESTA', which was held prior to the awards ceremony, also continues. The event, which has been held in downtown Osaka and Mnet Plus since the 9th, was organized as a program involving fans from more than 200 regions around the world. Following Osaka's Hep Five and Midosuji, the event will be held at Namba Square from the 19th, raising the atmosphere of the main ceremony.

The two-day awards ceremony will select artists and works representing K-pop this year. Along with the results of the award, collaborative performances across generations and genres are expected to be a major point to watch.

Meanwhile, "2026 MAMA AWARDS," in which Visa will participate as a title sponsor, will host the CHAPTER 1 red carpet at 3:30 p.m. on the 20th and the main ceremony at 6 p.m. On the 21st, CHAPTER 2 will be followed by the red carpet at 12:30 p.m. and the main award ceremony will be held from 3 p.m. The awards ceremony will be broadcast live around the world through major digital platforms including Mnet Plus.
