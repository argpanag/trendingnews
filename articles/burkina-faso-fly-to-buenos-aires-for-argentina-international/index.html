Burkina Faso Fly to Buenos Aires for Argentina International Friendly

TheTools · 03.10.2026 16:59 · source · permalink

Burkina Faso’s national football team, the Stallions, have departed Casablanca, Morocco, as they begin their journey to Buenos Aires, Argentina, for an international friendly against Argentina.

The Burkinabe delegation left Casablanca on a chartered flight as preparations intensify for the highly anticipated encounter scheduled for Saturday, October 3.

The trip marks the next stage of the Stallions’ preparations as they get set for a major test against the reigning world champions.

Burkina Faso will be looking to use the friendly as an opportunity to assess their squad, build team cohesion and gain valuable experience against one of international football’s strongest sides.

The decision to travel on a chartered flight is expected to provide the team with a more direct and organised journey ahead of the fixture in Buenos Aires.

The Stallions’ arrival in Argentina will be followed by final preparations for the match, with the coaching staff expected to focus on tactics, fitness and team organisation.

For Argentina, the fixture provides another opportunity to test their squad in an international setting, while Burkina Faso will be aiming to produce a competitive performance and make the most of the occasion.

The match is expected to attract significant attention across Africa, particularly among Burkina Faso supporters, as the Stallions take on the South American giants.

The encounter also adds another chapter to Burkina Faso’s international football development, with matches against high-profile opponents providing valuable exposure for the team and its players.

Football fans will be watching closely to see how the Stallions approach the challenge and whether they can trouble Argentina in Buenos Aires.
