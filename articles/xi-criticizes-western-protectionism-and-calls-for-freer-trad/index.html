Chinese leader Xi Jinping sought to position himself as the global champion of free trade in his first speech at the APEC summit. In a statement delivered shortly before US President Donald Trump arrived in Shenzhen, Xi called for greater cooperation on free trade and AI, criticizing Western "protectionism."

While the American delegation appeared divided ahead of the summit — officials are feuding over whether to curb trade with China — some countries have struck deals with Beijing in order to diversify away from a US they see as increasingly unreliable. The transformation is notable: When Washington shepherded Beijing into the World Trade Organization 25 years ago, "the US thought it could change China," Bloomberg noted. "Instead, China changed the US."

— Jeronimo Gonzalez
