The Weeknd at National Stadium 2026: Setlist, last-minute tickets, merch store, and ultimate guide

TheTools · 02.10.2026 15:37 · source · permalink

The Weeknd will perform on two nights – limited tickets are still available for purchase

The Weeknd is bringing his blockbuster After Hours Til Dawn stadium tour to Singapore – and if you’re wondering when to show up, what he’ll sing and how to get home afterwards, here’s everything you need to know.

What is The Weeknd After Hours Til Dawn Tour?

It’s The Weeknd’s massive stadium tour built around his album trilogy: After Hours (2020), Dawn FM (2022) and Hurry Up Tomorrow (2025). The 2026 Asia leg is also the final leg of the tour, so Singapore is your last chance to catch the show before this particular era clocks out.

When is The Weeknd performing in Singapore?

The Weeknd will perform at the National Stadium on Friday, October 2 and Saturday, October 3, 2026.

Are there still tickets?

There are still tickets, but most are restricted seats. If that isn’t an issue for you, you can purchase them here.

What time will the doors open?

Doors open at 6pm.

What time does The Weeknd start?

The show starts at 8pm.

How long will the show last?

Exact timings for the Singapore show haven’t been confirmed, but based on previous concerts at the National Stadium, The Weeknd’s set should run about two hours.

Who is opening for The Weeknd in Singapore?

Japanese hip-hop duo Creepy Nuts will support the Singapore dates.

If you know ‘Bling-Bang-Bang-Born’, you already know the drill. The duo have racked up hundreds of millions of streams with the track and other hits including ‘Otonoke’ and ‘Mirage’, and played their first Tokyo Dome show to around 50,000 people in 2025.

Where can I buy official The Weeknd merchandise?

The Weeknd Singapore Stadium Store is setting up shop at The Kallang from October 1 to 3, ahead of his sold-out After Hours Til Dawn shows at the National Stadium. Expect venue-exclusive merchandise, official tour merch and vinyl records.

It’s open to the public and free to visit at Stadium Riverside Walk, The Kallang, 1 Stadium Drive, Singapore 397629.

October 1: 4pm to 8pm
October 2 to 3: 2pm to midnight

What items cannot be brought into the venue?

Outside food and drinks, including alcohol and bottled water, are not permitted. Bags larger than 35cm by 20cm by 30cm are also prohibited, along with umbrellas (including collapsible ones), chains, studded accessories, carabiners, strollers, noisemakers, chairs, stools, flowers, gifts, hand sanitiser bottles over 60ml, glass perfume bottles (except travel-size perfume atomisers) and fan boards larger than A3.

As for recording equipment, leave the video cameras, monopods, tripods, selfie sticks, audio recording devices and professional cameras with telephoto or zoom lenses at home – bringing any of these to the venue may result in denied entry.

Check the official Singapore event guidelines before heading down. This is one occasion where discovering at the security gate that your bag contains a forbidden item is very much not part of the VIP experience.

Is re-entry allowed?

Once you leave the venue, don’t expect to get back in unless the event organiser says otherwise. Running late? You may have to wait outside until a suitable break in the show before you’re allowed through, and there’ll be no refunds or compensation for missed entry time.

What are the possible modes of transport after the show?

If you’re hoping to beat the post-concert chaos, Stadium MRT (CC6) is the closest station to the National Stadium, but expect it to be extremely crowded immediately after the show. A better bet is to walk a little further to Tanjong Rhu MRT (TE23), around a nine-minute sheltered walk away, or Kallang MRT (EW10) and Mountbatten MRT (CC7), both roughly 13 minutes from the stadium.

Buses are another option, with services 10, 11, 14, 16, 16M, 70, 70A, 70M and 196 running near the stadium. If you’re opting for a taxi or ride-hailing service such as Grab or Gojek, head to the designated pick-up points at Stadium MRT Station Exits A and B – though expect heavier traffic and potentially higher fares around showtime.
