'You want to be appreciated' – Azaz opens up on his performances ahead of West Ham

TheTools · 24.10.2026 14:21 · source · permalink

FINN Azaz believes Saints have a chance to “stamp our authority” on the Championship when they face promotion rivals West Ham on Saturday.

Saints travel to the London Stadium to face a side with one of the strongest squads in the division, sitting second in the table after 11 games.

West Ham have scored a league-leading 30 goals so far, including 6-0 wins against Wrexham and Lincoln, while Saints have found the net 21 times.

Azaz insists Saints have the quality to compete with the division’s leaders, drawing on their performances against top-flight opposition last season.

“It’s a massive game,” the midfielder told the Daily Echo. “I think it’s a really good time for us to show the level that we’re on and rise to that.

Finn Azaz has three goals and six league assists (Image: Stuart Martin)

"They're obviously one of the best teams in the league, one of, if not the best squad on paper, so we need to step up all the things we've been working on.

“It’s a good opportunity to really stamp our authority on the league. We showed it last season, playing against Premier League sides, playing against Cov.

“We know that if we perform to our level, with the players we’ve got and the game plan the manager’s going to provide, we believe we can beat anyone.”

Saints have conceded just 10 goals in the Championship this season, with only league leaders Swansea City boasting a better defensive record.

It would be some effort, but they could record four consecutive league clean sheets for the first time since February 2016, when they kept six in a row.

Saints head to West Ham after a positive week, recording consecutive clean sheets and an away victory before drawing 0-0 with Bolton Wanderers.

Azaz felt there were encouraging signs, although he believes the team still have plenty of room to improve following that disappointing draw last time out.

“The clean sheets, away win, very positive,” he said. “I don’t think we should draw the game on the weekend, but there’s a lot of positives to take.

"I still think there's a lot of room for us to improve in all areas."

Azaz has not scored since the international break, although he has kept his numbers ticking over with the game-winning assist against Portsmouth.

"We knew when we went down to 10 that it would be tough, but I think everyone showed that they were willing to give everything to win," Azaz recalled.

Finn Azaz was booked for a late tackle on Ebou Adams (Image: Robin Jones)

He would have set up two goals against the bitter rivals, had Leo Scienza taken his one-on-one chance right at the start of the second half at St Mary's.

"You might get a bit frustrated sometimes, but I think that's literally part of being a creative player. It's how it goes over the course of a season," he said.

"For example, I got an assist for tapping it to Cyle [Larin], and he put it in the corner, and then I give someone a really good chance, but they don't score.

"All you can do is just keep trying to provide for your teammates. You have to just keep that mindset, and definitely not be too serious about stats."

The 26 year old, who some pundits are claiming is one of the best players in the league, wants there to be more to his game than sheer numbers.

"I've never been too serious about the stats, because although that's what is looked at these days, I'm still a believer in the eye test personally," he said.

Does he care if supporters and pundits think he passes the test?

"With human nature, I think everyone does to an extent," he admitted. "I'm not on anything, so I don't see any comments, but you want to be appreciated."

With an arsenal of cute touches, deliberate feints, and defence-splitting passes at his disposal, Azaz surely passes the eye test for almost every viewer.

But the data also puts him in the Championship's top five players for big chances created, second for key passes, and first for through balls and carries leading to chances.

More surprisingly, no Saints player has won possession more times this season than Azaz, who has recovered the ball for his team in some way 45 times.

Only Flynn Downes and James Bree have made more tackles than Azaz (10), and that work-rate is why Tonda Eckert trusts him in multiple positions.

"I try and look for how I can have the best impact wherever I am, and obviously I always trust the manager's game plan," Azaz insisted.

"Having played multiple positions and obviously trying to use my game intelligence to adapt, I feel like I can be effective in all of those positions.

"I told him that he can play me as a six if he wants," he said laughing, before seriously adding: "I've played as a six for Ireland, so I know I can do it."

Dropping that deep is not the end goal for Azaz, though, even when quizzed on the hypothetical scenario that, well into his 30s, his 'legs have gone'.

"I don't use my speed that much anyway, so maybe I can stay as a 10."
