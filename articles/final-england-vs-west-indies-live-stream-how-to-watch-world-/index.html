The World Championship of Legends Cricket gets a 1st-time winner on Sunday as England takes on West Indies in the final

England stunned unbeaten defending champion South Africa in a semifinal on Friday at the World Championship of Legends Cricket to advance to Sunday’s final against West Indies at Sharjah Cricket Stadium in Sharjah, United Arab Emirates. Both teams are first-time finalists at the event, now in its third year, that features teams of retired stars and active players no longer under national-team contracts. India won the inaugural event in 2024 and South Africa took the title in 2025, with the first two tournaments played at Edgbaston Cricket Ground in England before the event shifted to the UAE for this year.
