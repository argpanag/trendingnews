Sportswear giant sues Aussie fashion house White Fox

TheTools · 09.10.2026 01:22 · source · permalink
  • Australia
  • Court Case

October 9, 2026 - 1.22am

Sporting goods giant Adidas is suing Australian fashion house White Fox alleging its four-stripe clothing infringes the German company’s three-stripe trademarks.

The lawsuit also involves White Fox’s advertising, with Adidas alleging the designs could mislead shoppers into believing the brands are linked.

The White Fox head office in Roseberry, Sydney. The Sydney Morning Herald

The multinational alleges ads for the Australian firm’s four-striped clothes and White Fox-branded socks show people clad in “genuine” Adidas items.

Its evidence includes advertising on the side of Sydney buses.

The first hearing in the Federal Court is due to begin on Friday.

White Fox has been selling and advertising clothes with four parallel stripes of equal width since March, the German company claims.

A White Fox tracksuit and an Adidas outfit. White Fox/Adidas

In court documents, Adidas alleges the designs are “substantially identical with or otherwise deceptively similar” to its trademarks.

In 2025, Adidas AG reported record annual revenue of €24,811 billion ($39.9 billion).
