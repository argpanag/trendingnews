Las Vegas home prices dip in August as fewer homes sell

by Matthew Seeman

LAS VEGAS (KSNV) — Las Vegas home prices saw a slight dip in August as fewer homes sold during the last full month of the summer, according to a local industry group.

The median price of existing single-family homes sold in Southern Nevada last month was $475,000, per Las Vegas Realtors.

That's a 1% drop from August last year and down from the all-time high of $490,000 set this past May and June and the $480,000 median price reported in July.

For condominiums and townhomes, the median price was $299,900, Las Vegas Realtors said. That's a slight increase of 0.6% from August last year, but still well below the record high of $315,000 set in October 2024.

A total of 2,252 existing homes, condos and townhomes were sold in August. Compared to the year-ago month, sales were down 1.7% for homes and down 7.4% for condos and townhomes.

"Local home prices have been pretty stable this year – and really for the past two years or so," Las Vegas Realtors President George Kypreos said in a statement. "It’s not surprising to see sales slowing down a bit, especially considering how mortgage rates have been rising recently and that can be a drag on the housing market."

By the end of the month, there were 7,590 single-family homes listed without any offers, up 5.3% compared to August 2025. There were also 2,714 condos and townhomes listed without offers, up 6%.

The sales pace in August equates to a housing supply of just over four and a half months.
