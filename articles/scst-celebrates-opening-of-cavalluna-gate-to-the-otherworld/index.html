SCST Celebrates Opening of CAVALLUNA - Gate to the Otherworld

Speech by SCST at opening ceremony of CAVALLUNA - Gate to the Otherworld

Following is the speech by the Secretary for Culture, Sports and Tourism, Miss Rosanna Law, at the opening ceremony of CAVALLUNA - Gate to the Otherworld today (September 10):

The Secretary for Culture, Sports and Tourism, Miss Rosanna Law, Photo source: reference image

Deputy Commissioner Hua You (Deputy Commissioner of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region), Deputy Director-General Lin Nan (Deputy Director-General of the Department of Publicity, Cultural and Sports Affairs of the Liaison Office of the Central People's Government in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region), Mr Martin Liao (Chairman of the Hong Kong Jockey Club), Mr Winfried Engelbrecht-Bresges (Chief Executive Officer of the Hong Kong Jockey Club), Mr Johannes Mock- O' Hara (Chief Executive Officer of Apassionata World GmbH), distinguished guests, ladies and gentlemen,

Good evening. It is my great pleasure to join you here at the spectacular Kai Tak Arena to celebrate the opening of CAVALLUNA - Gate to the Otherworld.

CAVALLUNA - Gate to the Otherworld, Photo source: HKJC

This extraordinary equestrian show has already captivated millions across more than 30 cities in Europe, enchanting audiences of all ages with its seamless fusion of world-class horsemanship, theatrical storytelling, music, and dance. Tonight, we are privileged to witness its Asian debut - right here in Hong Kong - featuring 54 magnificent horses and an internationally acclaimed cast of riders and performers. We are truly honoured that the organiser has chosen our city as the first stop in Asia, and the successful staging of this large-scale production at the Kai Tak Arena once again affirms Hong Kong's standing as a premier host of world-class events.

In addition, with the support from our Mega Arts and Cultural Events Fund, we are delighted that the organiser has thoughtfully tailored the show to resonate with local audiences and visitors from the Greater Bay Area. From featuring a local singer in the performance to adding English voice-over with Chinese subtitles, these adaptations reflect a genuine commitment to cultural connection. We trust this collaboration marks a promising beginning - and we look forward to many more spectacular shows and mega events like CAVALLUNA making their way to Hong Kong, enriching the cultural tapestry of our community and our friends from around the globe.

In 2026, the Year of the Horse, this production carries profound symbolism. In Chinese culture, the horse embodies strength, vitality, and perseverance - values that echo the spirit of our city. Under the steadfast framework of "one country, two systems", Hong Kong will continue to harness its unique advantages to foster international connectivity, while deepening the world's appreciation of Chinese culture and heritage.

I would like to extend my heartfelt gratitude to the organiser, the Hong Kong Jockey Club, and all collaborating organisations for their invaluable sponsorship and unwavering support. My sincere thanks also go to every artist and team member who has worked tirelessly to bring CAVALLUNA to our shores.

May the production enjoy every success during its run in Hong Kong, and may each of you have a truly memorable and enchanting evening. Thank you.

The opening ceremony of CAVALLUNA - Gate to the Otherworld, Photo source: HKJC

Hong Kong Customs reminds non-local jewellery exhibitors about statutory requirement to submit cash transaction report under Dealers in Precious Metals and Stones Regulatory Regime

JGW September (Jewellery & Gem World Hong Kong September) will be held from tomorrow (September 14) to September 18 at AsiaWorld-Expo and from September 16 to September 20 at Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre. Hong Kong Customs reminds exhibitors that according to the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance (Cap. 615), unless exempted, any person who is seeking to carry on a business of dealing in precious metals and stones and engage in any transaction(s) (whether making or receiving a payment) with a total value at or above HK$120,000 in Hong Kong is required to register with Hong Kong Customs and fulfil his/her anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing statutory obligations as appropriate. All dealers in precious metals and stones must successfully obtain a relevant registration before they can carry out any cash or non-cash transaction(s) with a total value at or above HK$120,000.

For non-Hong Kong dealers in precious metals and stones fulfilling the prescribed conditions (including those who come to Hong Kong to participate in exhibitions), although they are exempt from registration, they are required to submit to Hong Kong Customs a cash transaction report for any cash transaction(s) (whether making or receiving a payment) with a total value at or above HK$120,000 carried out in Hong Kong within one day after the transaction, or before the dealer or the person acting on behalf of the dealer leaves Hong Kong, whichever is earlier.

Non-Hong Kong dealers can make an online submission of a cash transaction report via the Dealers in Precious Metals and Stones Regulatory Regime's (the Regime) webpage atwww.drs.customs.gov.hkby accessing the Dealers in Precious Metals and Stones Registration System. For details, please refer to relevant procedures.

Non-Hong Kong dealers can also download the related form and then submit the completed report by fax at (852) 3568 4395 or in person at the Dealers in Precious Metals and Stones Supervision Bureau during office hours (Address: Unit 3501, 35/F, Skyline Tower, 39 Wang Kwong Road, Kowloon Bay, Kowloon).

Dealers can visit relevant website for more information about the Regime.
