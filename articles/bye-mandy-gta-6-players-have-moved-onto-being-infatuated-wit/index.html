Bye Mandy, GTA 6 players have moved onto being infatuated with goth girl Vicki

TheTools · 23.11.2026 08:43 · source · permalink

Goth girl Vicki has become the new obsession from Grand Theft Auto 6 players.

Olly Smith

As Grand Theft Auto VI players continue to explore the game's story mode and progress through each chapter, a new fixation has hit the community.

Mere days after fans started obsessing over Mandy, Jason's overly-friendly neighbour who needs help with her kitchen sink, players have now met Vicki, who is having the same effect on the community.

We'll keep spoilers to a minimum here, but here's what folk have been saying about Vicki so far during their GTA VI playthroughs.

Who is Vicki in GTA 6?

Met at the very beginning of Chapter 2 while playing as Lucia, Vicki has become a fan favourite due to her tough attitude and goth girl appearance.

She also works as a dancer at the strip club known as Delights, which I'm sure is also another reason for the sudden interest among fans.

Advert

While playing as Lucia, we meet Vicki in a motel as she has something we want (again, we're keeping spoilers to a low level here), which eventually transpires into a larger mission which opens up more activities to do around the map.

Although she's a minor character throughout the wider GTA VI story, Vicki makes several more rather memorable appearances throughout Chapter 2.

"Oh yeah we on Vicki now, I don’t even know who Mandy is," writes one player on X (via Push Square).

"Bye Lucia, Bye Mandy, Heyyy Vicky," another fan writes.

Both posts feature dozens of comments in the replies simping for the dancer, and for those players who have already reached Chapter 2, she's become a standout icon for this section of the game.

Who plays Vicki in GTA 6?

If Vicki's voice sounds familiar, it's because she's portrayed by Taryn Manning, an American actress best known for playing Tiffany "Pennsatucky" Doggett in Netflix's Orange is the New Black.

She also played Cherry in the AMC series Sons of Anarchy, which shares a lot of DNA with the GTA series, and has had roles in films like 8 Mile and Weirdsville.

GTA VI also features other actors who have starred in films and television shows that are similar to the Grand Theft Auto series. Stephen Root for example, who plays Jason's boss Brian Heder, has had roles in shows like Fargo, Justified, and Barry. Meanwhile, rising star Massi Pregoni who plays Matthias has previously acted in shows like NCIS and Coyotes.

The full cast list was revealed at the launch of Grand Theft Auto VI, in which Manni L. Perez was finally confirmed as co-lead Lucia, while Jason was confirmed to be played by actor and stand-up comedian Dylan Rourke. Perez has also been nominated for Best Performance at The Game Awards 2026. Grand Theft Auto VI is also leading with 8 nominations.
