MGM+ Black Friday streaming deal: One year is just $31

TheTools · 26.11.2026 16:28 · source · permalink

SAVE $31: Through Nov. 30, new and returning subscribers can score an entire year of MGM+ for only $30.99 with Prime Video's Black Friday streaming add-on deal. That's 50% off the usual cost of an annual subscription.

The horror series From has made quite the splash over on Netflix recently. But viewers who want to keep the binge-watch rolling will need an MGM+ subscription in order to watch Season 4, which came out in spring 2026, as well as next year's fifth and final season. Luckily, thanks to Black Friday streaming deals, you can sign up for cheaper than ever.

Through Nov. 30, new and returning subscribers can score an entire year of MGM+ for only $30.99. That's 50% off the usual cost of an annual subscription and breaks down to only $2.59 per month. You can also choose a shorter subscription option and sign up for two months for just $1.99 per month (reg. $7.99 per month). Either way, you're getting a helluva deal.
