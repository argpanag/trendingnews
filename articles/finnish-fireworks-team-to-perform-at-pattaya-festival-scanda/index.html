Finnish fireworks team to perform at Pattaya festival - Scandasia

TheTools · 27.11.2026 07:38 · source · permalink

A Finnish fireworks team will put on two displays over Pattaya Bay at the Pattaya International Fireworks Festival on 27 and 28 November. Both shows are free to watch from Pattaya Beach.

Finland is scheduled to perform at 8:05 p.m. on Friday and 9:45 p.m. on Saturday. The festival also features fireworks teams from Japan, Malaysia, Italy and the Philippines, with live music between displays.

Pattaya Mayor Poramet Ngampichet welcomed the five teams at City Hall on Thursday, Pattaya Mail reported. ScandAsia earlier reported on Finland’s participation in the 2025 festival.

Pattaya Beach Road will close to traffic from 3 p.m. on both festival days.

Sources: Thailand.go.th, Pattaya Mail, Festival schedule
