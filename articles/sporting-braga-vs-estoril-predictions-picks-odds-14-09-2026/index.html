Sporting Braga vs Estoril Preview

Sporting Braga sit eighth in the Liga Portugal table and will be keen to respond after suffering their first defeat of the campaign, a 2-1 reverse at Estrela da Amadora. Prior to that setback, Carlos Carvalhal's side had strung together a promising sequence of two wins and a draw from three outings. The Estádio Municipal de Braga has been a reliable fortress, and a return to home comforts should sharpen their focus on climbing back towards the top three.

Estoril arrive in desperate shape, marooned in seventeenth place and still without a league victory in 2026-27. Their winless run now stretches to thirteen consecutive top-flight fixtures, a burden new head coach Vasco Matos is tasked with lifting. A goalless draw against Arouca offered a glimmer of resilience, with eighteen goal attempts showing attacking intent. Deadline-day arrivals Roko Jurisic, Milan Robin, and Nail Omerovic could feature, though their readiness remains uncertain.
