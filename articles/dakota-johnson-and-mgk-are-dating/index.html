Dakota Johnson and MGK are dating?

TheTools · 13.09.2026 10:23 · source · permalink

Published September 13, 2026

Dakota Johnson and MGK, formerly known as Machine Gun Kelly, sparked romance rumours after “secret date night.”

Reportedly, the two were spotted separately arriving at Taylor Swift’s apartment for what has been described as a private meetup.

According to reports, the Verity actress was the first to reach, entering the building through the garage with assistance from members of her team.

Around 30 minutes later, the American rapper, whose real name is Colson Baker, reportedly arrived, escorted by Johnson’s security.

The two were then said to have spent several hours inside the apartment while the Blank Space hitmaker was reportedly out of town.

Later, the Fifty Shades of Grey star and the Rap Devil maker allegedly left together and headed to Johnson’s nearby hotel.

The outing has fueled speculation that the pair may have enjoyed a low-key date night.

While there has been no confirmation from either Johnson or MGK, both 36, about the nature of their relationship, the reported rendezvous has inevitably caught attention.

Notably, the latest sighting comes after the two were allegedly seen crossing paths at Swift and Travis Kelce’s July wedding.

That earlier encounter has now added another layer of intrigue to the latest reports, with fans and celebrity watchers wondering whether the pair’s connection could be more than casual.
