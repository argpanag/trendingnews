TikTok outage has resolved after thousands reported the app was down in the U.S.

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:09 · source · permalink

If your TikTok app scrolling was interrupted today, you're not alone.

TikTok went down today with what appeared to be a major, but brief, outage. Downdetector, a website that tracks outages for online services, showed tens of thousands of user reports. The reports started spiking around 2 p.m. ET on Tuesday, though they tapered off starting around 3 p.m. ET.

TikTok didn't acknowledge the outage through any of its social media channels. However, it was widely reported by TikTok app users online. It's unclear how widespread it was, but Downdetector showed user reports peaking at around 27,000.

The TikTok app outage follows a rough couple of weeks for outages across the tech world. Starting on Nov. 12, Microsoft Outlook and Teams were down for more than 30 hours. The following day, air traffic in the U.S. had to be grounded due to a technical issue, delaying or canceling thousands of flights.

On Nov. 16, a technical issue with JPMorgan Chase brought banking services to a halt for most of the day, including causing problems at other institutions. On Nov. 18, Apple's online services went down, causing issues with everything from signing into iCloud to using Apple Pay.
