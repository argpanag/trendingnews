WWE Announces Bronson Reed Death Before SmackDown

TheTools · 28.11.2026 00:52 · source · permalink

WWE announced the death of Bronson Reed at age 38 ahead of its Friday SmackDown broadcast, drawing attention to the former NXT North American Champion before Survivor Series WarGames 2026. The announcement was made before the prerecorded episode, which was scheduled to take place at Intrust Bank Arena in Wichita, Kansas. Reed was a former NXT North American Champion and won the 2024 Andre the Giant Memorial Battle Royal, according to WWE's announcement.

The company described his passing as untimely but did not provide further details about the circumstances of his death. The SmackDown broadcast also featured several scheduled matches and appearances as WWE prepared for Survivor Series WarGames 2026.

Kevin Owens and Finn Balor were scheduled to face Sami Zayn and Gunther in a tag team match. The women's division also had a championship contest on the card.

Alexa Bliss and Tatum Paxley were scheduled to challenge reigning Women's Tag Team Champions Michin and B-Fab. Chelsea Green was also set to face Blake Monroe in a singles match. Beyond the scheduled matches, CM Punk was advertised for an appearance, while Danhausen was scheduled to host a Friendsgiving segment. The episode was part of WWE's programming ahead of Survivor Series WarGames 2026.
