Tzolis returns to training - Arteta issues pre-Liverpool fitness update

TheTools · 30.10.2026 14:15 · source · permalink

Christos Tzolis has returned to full training after nearly four weeks out with a hamstring injury sustained on international duty with Greece.

The winger pulled up in the early stages of his country’s 2-2 draw with the Netherlands at the start of the month and returned to London for treatment.

His recovery is well-timed. With Bukayo Saka sidelined by a quad problem, Max Dowman banned for three league games and Noni Madueke nursing a knock picked up against Everton last weekend, Arsenal’s options on the flanks are thin on the ground.

A start at Anfield on Sunday may come too soon for Tzolis, but an appearance off the bench could be on the cards. Madueke is also expected to make the trip, provided he comes through tomorrow’s training session.

“We don’t know yet,” said Arteta when asked about the England international’s availability.

“We have a training session tomorrow. Let’s see if he can complete that full session tomorrow and after that we’ll know whether he’s available or not.”

Declan Rice and Riccardo Calafiori both trained today after sitting out Tuesday’s trip to Fleetwood Town and will be available for selection.

Meanwhile, William Saliba is expected to resume full training in the coming days, with his first appearance of the season pencilled in for after the Interlull.

Pressed on a timeline for Saka’s return, Arteta kept it vague, saying he’d “probably” be out until after the upcoming internationals but “we’ll have to see next week.”

The last we heard, the 25-year-old was waiting to undergo another scan to determine the seriousness of his muscle injury. Judging by today’s update, that’s still the case.

“He needed another scan to see how the amount of fluid that was there was resolving, whether the tendon is touched and how badly it is touched. Afterwards, we’ll know more about it.”

“We want to have him [back as quick as possible. The player is desperate. He’s feeling much better than he was a few days ago, and we need to wait and see how he evolves.”
