Man charged with terror offences after raids across Sydney

TheTools · 19.11.2026 23:58 · source · permalink

  • Topic:Crime

Topic:Crime

Counter-terror raids have taken place across Sydney's south-west. (ABC News)

A 35-year-old man has been charged with terrorism offences after sweeping raids across Sydney's south-west this morning.

The man was arrested at an Ingleburn home about 5am and charged with making a document connected with terrorism and using a carriage service to possess violent extremism material.

Police will allege the material related to religiously motivated extremism, including the Islamic State group.

He has been refused bail to appear at Campbelltown Local Court today.

Officers remain on scene at multiple properties. (ABC News)

A second man, 34, was arrested at a home in Campbelltown in connection with the raids and is expected to be charged with hindering a police investigation, NSW Police said in a statement.

A third man, 33, was arrested at Wolli Creek where he is expected to be charged with firearms offences.

It comes after a large number of officers were seen at a house in Campbelltown, including heavily armed members of the Raptor anti-gang squad.

An explosives-detection dog, forensics teams and the Rescue and Bomb Disposal Unit officers were also at the scene.

It was understood a number of other properties were also being searched.

Police said a number of items had been seized during the searches.
