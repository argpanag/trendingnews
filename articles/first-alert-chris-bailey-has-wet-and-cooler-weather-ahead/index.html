FIRST ALERT| Chris Bailey has wet and cooler weather ahead

TheTools · 01.10.2026 18:23 · source · permalink

LEXINGTON, Ky. (WKYT) - The month is starting out on a very windy and very warm note, but things will change quickly as a cold front drops in on Friday. This boundary likely slows down, allowing a few more showers to continue into a cooler weekend.

Temps today reach the low and mid 80s in the east with near 90 in the west. Southwest winds will be very gusty, especially this evening and may top 30mph at times. This is ahead of a cold front dropping in here on Friday.

Showers and some rumbles of thunder will be possible from northwest to southeast throughout the day. We may very well have a near 25-degree temp spread showing up from one side of this front to the other. Temps drop through the 60s and may hit the upper 50s behind the boundary.

This front then slows down across the Tennessee Valley on Saturday with a couple of areas of low pressure moving along it. The first comes Saturday with the next one due on Sunday. Both of these will bring showers across parts of Kentucky.

As I’ve mentioned, any areas seeing clouds or showers for most of the day on Saturday could stay in the upper 50s to low 60s.

The patchy frost threat remains for Monday night as lows drop into the mid and upper 30s for parts of Kentucky. Next week as a whole looks super nice across our region.
