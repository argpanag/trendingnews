Mark Warner: ‘Election was a huge repudiation of Donald Trump’s agenda’

TheTools · 13.11.2026 00:34 · source · permalink

WASHINGTON, D.C. (WVVA) - Democrats are projected to hold 56 Senate seats and 259 House seats when the 120th Congress is sworn in Jan. 3.

Warner among projected Democratic senators

Virginia Sen. Mark Warner, a Democrat, will be one of those senators. He received his highest percentage of the vote since 2008.

“In many ways, this election is a huge repudiation of Donald Trump’s agenda, his complete disdain for the American, his willingness to spend all of his time focused on vanity projects,” Warner said.

Trump posts on Truth Social

Trump took the opposite position in a Nov. 3 Truth Social post. He said, “I wasn’t on the ballot and if I was, we would have won in a landslide!”

Economy was top issue for voters

Pew Research reports the economy, such as rising costs, was the top issue for midterm voters.

“Rarely has there been a time when a president’s direct actions are so directly attributable to the increase in costs. But for these crazy tariffs costs would be lower. But for this war of choice with Iran prices would be lower,” Warner said.

Vance blames war in X post

Vice President JD Vance also blamed the war in a Nov. 4 post on X.

“If you’d like to argue it was worth it, that losing the Senate for the next six years was worth it, then do so. Argue it was a worthwhile cost to pay. Agree or disagree, that’s at least honest,” Vance wrote.

President Donald Trump, a Republican, said: “It’s largely true because it drove the gasoline up a little bit short-term and that’s, uh, probably true.”
