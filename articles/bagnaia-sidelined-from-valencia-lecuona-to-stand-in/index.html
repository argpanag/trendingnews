Bagnaia sidelined from Valencia, Lecuona to stand in

TheTools · 28.11.2026 10:00 · source · permalink

The #63 concludes his Ducati chapter off-track as it’s confirmed he won’t be taking part in the 2026 finale

Francesco Bagnaia will be sidelined from this weekend’s Motul Grand Prix of Valencia as he continues to recover from his foot injury sustained in Qatar. Sadly, that means he bows out from his chapter with Ducati Lenovo Team off track, concluding his chapter in red as the marque’s most successful rider in terms of wins – and with two MotoGP World Championships.

After a tougher rookie season for Bagnaia with Pramac and then an injury-hit 2020 campaign, nevertheless taking his first podium that season, the #63 landed at Ducati Lenovo Team in 2021. After a slew of podiums he then took his first MotoGP win in a stunner at Aragon, taking two more before the end of the season and ending the year runner-up.

In 2022, he went one better. Making what had been the biggest comeback in history until this season’s exploits from now-teammate Marc Marquez, he took his first premier class World Championship and Ducati’s first riders’ crown since Casey Stoner in 2007. In 2023, he backed it up with another World Championship.

2024 saw him just lose out to Jorge Martin, before a tougher couple of seasons in 2025 and 2026, with some awesome standout moments as well as some difficult weekends. His next chapter sees him move to Aprilia Racing alongside Marco Bezzecchi next year – so that will now be the next time we see the two-time MotoGP World Champion race.

With Lecuona confirmed as stepping in again – after he himself has impressed on super sub duty throughout the year for Bagnaia, Alex Marquez and Fermin Aldeguer – we wait to see if Bagnaia will be able to take part in the test on Tuesday, after the GP weekend.
