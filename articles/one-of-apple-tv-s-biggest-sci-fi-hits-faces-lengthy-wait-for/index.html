One of Apple TV's Biggest Sci-Fi Hits Faces Lengthy Wait for Season 2

TheTools · 03.11.2026 19:26 · source · permalink

The process behind creating our favorite television shows and big screen releases can be far more arduous than it appears from the outside. All that movie magic takes time, and when a project hits a roadblock, it can cause heavy delays. Widening gaps between seasons is becoming an increasing annoyance for viewers across streaming platforms, and it's made worse when a story ends on a cliffhanger.

Unfortunately, when it comes Apple TV favorite Pluribus, that is exactly the situation viewers find themselves in following the Season 1 finale last December. The gripping series ended its debut season on a dizzying cliffhanger, and viewers have been anxiously awaiting updates on Season 2. Those updates officially arrived in early November 2026 via series creator Vince Gilligan, and it's not good news.

First, the positives. Pluribus will return for a second season, and filming for Season 2 has officially begun. The estimated release date is still quite a long ways off, unfortunately, leaving viewers with a lengthy wait before they get answers to their questions.

Filming for Pluribus Season 2 began in New Mexico on Nov. 1, 2026, according to Forbes. Filming for the season is expected to take place over a 225-day shoot, which should give it a vague wrap date of June 2027. Then it will move to post-production, which for Season 1 took more than a year. If Season 2 requires another 14 months of post-production work, Apple TV subscribers are not likely to see Season 2 of Pluribus arrive on screens until the fall of 2028. That's right around three years after the series first debuted.

It is a painfully long wait, particularly since the first season's story concluded with a nail-biter. In another disappointing development, the lengthy gap between seasons won't yield a major uptick in episode count, either. Season 1 of Pluribus had nine episodes, and reports claim that Season 2 will grow by just one episode.

When asked about the unexpectedly long wait between seasons, Gilligan cited the show's "very complex story" as a factor in its slow development. He also poked fun at the timeline back in March 2026. "It is not going quite as fast as I would hope," he said. "We're looking forward to people seeing it in eight or nine years when we get Season 2 finished."

Gilligan was exaggerating with that timeline joke, but three years is still a long gap between seasons. If any show is worth the wait, however, it is Pluribus. Season 1 of the gripping series is currently available on Apple TV.
