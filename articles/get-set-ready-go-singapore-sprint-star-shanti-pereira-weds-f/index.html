Get set, ready, go: Singapore sprint star Shanti Pereira weds former national athlete Tan Zong Yang

TheTools · 23.11.2026 19:27 · source · permalink
    bookmark

Singaporean sprint star Shanti Pereira, 30, married her longtime boyfriend and former national teammate Tan Zong Yang, 32, on Nov 21, two months after claiming gold at the Asian Games.

The wedding took place at the Church of Our Lady of Lourdes in Rochor, where Pereira wore a strapless white gown adorned with floral details, featuring a sweetheart neckline and fitted corset bodice, AsiaOne reported.

Tan, meanwhile, opted for a beige suit paired with a dark bow tie and a pale pink boutonniere.

He reposted several Instagram stories shared by relatives and friends, including a clip from Pereira's sister Seema capturing the newlyweds walking down the aisle.

The ceremony brought together family members, friends and current and former national athletes, including fencer Amita Berthier and badminton player Yeo Jia Min, who attended with her husband, former national swimmer Pang Sheng Jun, according to The Straits Times.

Fellow sprinter Timothee Yap was among Tan's groomsmen. Pereira's bridal party included her sisters Shobi and Valerie, the latter a former national sprinter. A video posted on TikTok showed the bride sharing an emotional embrace with them.

The wedding followed Pereira's successful campaign at the Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, where she defended her women's 200m title and secured silver in the 100m two months earlier.

Pereira has credited Tan, a lawyer and former SEA Games men's 400m bronze medallist, with supporting her athletic career, including making time to train alongside her.

Speaking to Olympics.com, Pereira said getting engaged had brought happiness during a challenging year and helped her recognise the importance of life beyond athletics.

Although she remains fully committed to running, she said gaining that broader outlook had "made me fall in love with my sport."

Tan proposed on June 9, 2024, near Drottningholm Palace in Stockholm, Sweden, after the couple had been together for three years.

They began dating in 2021 but postponed their wedding because of Pereira's sporting commitments, including the 2024 Paris Olympics and the 2025 SEA Games in Thailand, where she successfully defended her women's 100m and 200m titles. -- VnExpress-Vietnam News
