Robert Irwin reveals he’s got his sights on a wild career pivot: ‘I could hold my own’

TheTools · 24.11.2026 00:46 · source · permalink
  • Entertainment
  • Celebrity
  • Aussie stars

November 24, 2026 - 12.46am

Robert Irwin has proven in recent years that he is a man of many talents – from dancing to wrangling crocs – but he’s got an ambitious career move in his sights. 

The 22-year-old admitted he’s not one to shy away from pushing his body to the extreme, and it’s got him looking at starring in an action movie. 

Robert Irwin has got his sights set on an ambitious new career pivot, admitting he’s very keen to star in an action movie.  Instagram/@robertirwinphotography

Opening up about the career pivot, he told Extra that he’s got ambitions to become an action star. 

“If I’m catching a crocodile or I’m dancing on the floor, whatever I’m doing, I like to push my body to the limits while telling a story,” he told the publication.

“And you know, I thought, ‘What’s to do that?’ Action movies. One hundred per cent. I reckon I could hold my own. If I can catch a crocodile, I reckon I could do anything there.”

He’s clearly put some real thought into this already and admitted that a Jurassic Park movie would be right up his alley. 

For Irwin, he feels like there are a lot of similarities between Jurassic Park and his daily life at Australia Zoo.

“I’ve always said if Jurassic Park was real and all the dinosaurs got out, it would be the best day of my life, mate. It’d be awesome. I’d be straight in there. You wouldn’t have to worry. I’d be lassoing the T-Rex. No worries,” he added. 

It’s hard to believe that the young wildlife warrior, who is following in the footsteps of his late father Steve Irwin, would even be able to find the time for a movie. 

Wrangling crocs is just a normal day at Australia Zoo for the wildlife warrior.  Instagram/@robertirwinphotography

Prince William and Robert Irwin arrive on a green double decker bus to promote the Earthshot Prize. Instagram/@princeandprincessofwales

He’s gone from Dancing With the Stars in the US – which he won alongside Witney Carson to his day job at Australia Zoo, hosting the 2026 Logie Awards, attending the Earthshot Prize ceremony in Mumbai alongside royals Prince William and Princess Kate, to television appearances and even the simple stuff like uncle duties and hanging out at home.

To say he’s busy is an understatement, but it’s something he loves doing and believes it helps keep the legacy his dad created alive.

In May during an appearance on The Tonight Show starring Jimmy Fallon he explained that despite everything he has going on, he’s a conservationist at the heart of it all.

Despite being one of the busiest young men in the world right now, Irwin admitted that at the heart of what he does it’s always about conservation.  Todd Owyoung/NBC via Getty Image

Robert and the Irwin family have continued the legacy left behind by the late Steve Irwin.  Instagram/@bindisueirwin

"I'm on so many side quests right now. I never thought I'd be out there dancing but at the core of everything I do, it's about continuing a legacy.

"Our conservation work goes from strength to strength.

"The charity Wildlife Warriors... that's what it's all about for me. Keeping that going."

Wildlife Warriors was established in 2002 by Steve and Terri Irwin, and has been managed by Terri since Steve’s death in September 2006, with Robert and his older sister Bindi also heavily involved.
