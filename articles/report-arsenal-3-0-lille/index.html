Report: Arsenal 3-0 Lille

TheTools · 17.10.2026 19:06 · source · permalink

Three goals in seven devastating second-half minutes did the damage as we made it two wins from two in this season’s Champions League with victory over Lille.

After a goalless first half, we finally saw our dominance reflected on the scoreboard in the second 45, with Jurrien Timber scoring a fine individual goal to break the resistance on 64 minutes, and Ebere Eze finishing off a quick counter-attack three minutes later.

Then Bukayo Saka became our second-highest European goalscorer of all time as he coolly netted minutes after coming off the bench, ensuring we made it 14 successive victories in the league phase of the competition, closing in on next week’s opponents Bayern Munich’s record of 17.

PATIENCE REQUIRED

European nights under the Emirates Stadium lights are always special, and as North London Forever and the Champions League anthem added to the pre-match spectacle, it felt poignant that this game fell on the same day as the passing of life president Ken Friar, who played a significant role in the creation of our iconic home, among his many achievements working for the club across nine decades.

A supporter first and foremost, Mr Friar would have loved our display, especially as the goals flowed in the second half as the home support grew louder and louder. 

But it took the game a while to get going, with neither goal threatened for 20 minutes until Timber created chances for Bruno Guimaraes and Mikel Merino in quick succession, but the Brazilian stabbed wide before the World Cup winner’s header lacked power and direction.

On 21 minutes we had a scare when ex-Tottenham man Nabil Bentaleb’s fierce shot struck Gabriel, hit Hakon Haraldsson and spun into Ayase Ueda’s path unmarked inside the box. The Japanese striker buried the ball past David Raya, but the offside flag curtailed any celebrations, with a VAR check confirming the linesman’s decision.

Otherwise, our goal remained well protected, with all the half’s other opportunities falling our way. Eze’s side-footed attempt was comfortable for Berke Ozer, while just before the break Guimaraes saw a deflected shot cause the keeper issues, who did well to adjust to make the save, then sprang up to scoop the ball off Viktor Gyokeres’ toes.

THREE IN SEVEN

A goalless first half flattered the French side, and after the restart we cranked up the pressure further to try and rectify that. In the opening 15 minutes, we suffocated Lille, not allowing them out of our half, and eventually their resistance broke.

Just like on Saturday, we got in front just as Mikel Arteta was poised to make a triple change. On 64 minutes, Noni Madueke found Timber inside the box, who produced a brilliant touch to drag the ball into space, and then calmly lifted the ball over Ozer to score in style.

That was the cue for Kai Havertz, Martin Odegaard and Saka to enter the fray, and within 90 seconds we doubled our lead. Again, Timber did the damage by pressing high and winning possession, and after Odegaard found Guimaraes, his pass set Saka up to centre for Eze to finish from just inside the area.

And on 71 minutes the trio of subs all combined to allow Saka to put the game to bed after a devastating period of play. Odegaard played a one-two with the winger on the edge of the box before finding Havertz, and he returned the ball to Saka. With time to take a touch, he composed himself before sliding into the bottom corner. That was his 19th European goal for us - only Thierry Henry has more.

With the result in the bag, another great French striker received some N5 adulation when 40-year-old Olivier Giroud was introduced as a sub and given a standing ovation.

However he couldn’t help improve his side’s attacking threat, as Raya celebrated equalling David Seaman’s goalkeeping Champions League appearance record of 38 games with another clean sheet, setting us up perfectly for our heavyweight fixture in Munich next week.

FACTS AND STATS

We have won our last 14 Champions League group stage/league phase games, keeping nine clean sheets in this run.

Bukayo Saka has provided nine assists in the Champions League for Arsenal, with only Thierry Henry (16) and Cesc Fabregas (14) providing more.

Lille have lost their last three Champions League games, and each of their last eight against English sides.

Olivier Giroud (40y 13d) became the first French player aged 40+ to play in the UEFA Champions League.

Lille failed to have a shot on target for just the second time on record (since 2003/04) in a Champions League match, after October 2012 against Bayern Munich.

This was the third time we won a UEFA Champions League match by 3+ goals having been 0-0 at half-time, with all three coming under Mikel Arteta (3-0 v Real Madrid in 2024/25 and 4-0 v Atletico Madrid last season.

There were just seven minutes and 18 seconds between Jurrien Timber giving Arsenal the lead and Bukayo Saka making it 3-0.

WHAT'S NEXT

We return to Premier League action on Sunday afternoon as we head to Nottingham Forest, then head to the continent for another big Champions League night as we tackle Bayern Munich on Wednesday, October 21. Then we're back at Emirates Stadium the following Saturday when we face Everton.
