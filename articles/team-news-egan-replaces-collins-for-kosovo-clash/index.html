Team news: Egan replaces Collins for Kosovo clash

TheTools · 17.11.2026 18:39 · source · permalink

Dara O'Shea will don the captain's armband while John Egan will replace captain Nathan Collins as the Republic of Ireland make one change for their must-win UEFA Nations League clash against Kosovo at Aviva Stadium.

Collins was ruled out of the game after sustaining a knee injury against Austria on Saturday, with Hallgrimsson confirming on Monday that QPR defender Jimmy Dunne would replace him in the matchday '23.

Egan slots into the back three alongside O'Shea, who wins his 50th cap, and Liam Scales, while James Abankwah and Ryan Manning retain their places at wing-back.

Finn Azaz will again take up the deeper midfield role beside Jayson Molumby, behind the front three of Troy Parrott, Jack Moylan and Adam Idah who start together for the fift match in a row.

The Boys in Green must beat Kosovo tonight to finish second in Group B3 which secure a promotion/relegation play-off in March as well as a pot two seeding for the Euro 2028 qualifier draw in December.

Republic of Ireland: Caoimhin Kelleher; Dara O'Shea, John Egan, Liam Scales; James Abankwah, Jayson Molumby, Finn Azaz, Ryan Manning; Jack Moylan, Adam Idah; Troy Parrott.

Kosovo: Arijanet Muric; Ilir Krasniqi, Kreshnik Hajrizi, Lindrit Kamberi; Mergim Vojvoda, Lindon Emerllahu, Leon Avdullahu, Elvis Rexhbecaj, Dion Gallapeni; Fisnik Asslani, Vedat Muriqi.

Referee: Edgars Malcevs (Latvia)
