Larry Poindexter, ‘Days of Our Lives’ and ‘S.W.A.T.’ actor, dies after being hit by car while crossing street

TheTools · 19.11.2026 19:43 · source · permalink

Veteran actor Larry Poindexter — who appeared in soap operas “Days of Our Lives” and “General Hospital” and the movie “S.W.A.T.” — died Wednesday, days after he was hit by a car while crossing the street near his Los Angeles home, officials said Thursday.

He was 66.

Poindexter was walking near the corner of Moorpark Street and Lankershim Boulevard on Friday, about three blocks from the actor’s home, when he was struck by a car at around 6 p.m. PT, his agent and police said.

The investigation is ongoing, an LAPD spokesperson said.

“He was picking up some dinner,” the actor’s agent, Harry Gold, told NBC News on Thursday.

Poindexter suffered broken ribs but his injuries were not believed to be fatal, according to Gold.

“They did not diagnose this as anything serious,” Gold said. “They put him in the hospital for observation and to repair some broken bones and then he passed away.”

Gold praised his client’s work ethic and diverse skill set.

“He had great musical talent, was in rock band and produced musicals,” Gold said. “That was important to him.”

Poindexter’s long career includes 30 episodes on “Days of Our Lives” between 1984 and 2018 and a dozen appearances on “General Hospital” in 2005.

He played by-the-book LAPD Capt. Tom Fuller in the 2003 movie “S.W.A.T.,” supervising Samuel L. Jackson’s Sgt. Dan “Hondo” Harrelson.
