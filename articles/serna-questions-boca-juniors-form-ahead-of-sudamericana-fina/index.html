Serna Questions Boca Juniors Form Ahead of Sudamericana Final

TheTools · 21.11.2026 22:42 · source · permalink

Boca Juniors will face Atlético Mineiro in the 2026 Copa Sudamericana final with questions surrounding the Argentine club's recent performances, as former midfielder Mauricio Chicho Serna evaluates the team's preparations, Leandro Paredes' influence and the challenge posed by their Brazilian opponents.

Speaking ahead of the November 21 final, the former Boca champion discussed several aspects of the team's campaign, including its progress to the decisive match and the importance of Paredes within the squad, according to beIN SPORTS.

Serna highlighted Paredes' role as one of the key subjects in his assessment of Boca's squad. The former midfielder also examined the difficulties Atlético Mineiro could create for the Argentine side in their pursuit of continental silverware.

Another central concern in Serna's comments was Boca's current form. His assessment addressed the team's performances as it approached the final, placing its recent level of play alongside the challenge of competing against Atlético Mineiro.

The match represents an opportunity for Boca Juniors to secure another international trophy, but Serna's assessment focused on the team's condition and the demands of the final rather than its previous achievements alone.

Serna's remarks also reflected on Boca's journey through the competition, with the former champion considering both the team's progress and the quality of the opponent awaiting them in the final.

Atlético Mineiro and Boca Juniors are scheduled to meet on November 21, 2026, in the Copa Sudamericana final in Barranquilla, Colombia. The match will determine the competition's champion.
