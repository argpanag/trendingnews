Willia Yvonne McFall, age 82, peacefully passed away on November 15, 2026, surrounded by her loving family.

A memorial service will be held on Monday November 23, 2026 at 3PM at Heritage Funeral Home with Daniel Williams officiating. A visitation will also be held on Monday November 23, 2026 at Heritage Funeral Home from 2-3PM.

Yvonne was born on February 18, 1944, to the late Mary Rebecca Stephenson and Richard McKissack. She attended Carver Smith High School, where she served as a majorette and was runner-up for Miss Carver Smith. She graduated in 1961. In 1963, she married her high school sweetheart, Charles A. McFall, and embraced the role of a dedicated Navy wife. Together, they raised two children, Anita (Stevie) Frierson and Christopher McFall, whom she loved dearly. Yvonne held various positions at JCPenney, where she dedicated 20 years of service before retiring.

In 1986, Yvonne became a dedicated Jehovah’s Witness. She especially enjoyed going door to door to share her faith. Some of her favorite memories were made attending the annual conventions, with her favorite destination being Alabama.

Affectionately known as “Granny,” Yvonne leaves to cherish her memory four grandchildren: Anietra (Frank) McCoy, Christen Martin, James Frierson, and Sylvia McFall; two great-grandchildren, Aniesha Frierson and Cameron Goff Jr.; and several beloved nieces and nephews to whom she was a devoted aunt.

She was preceded in death by her parents, Richard McKissack and Mary Rebecca Stephenson; her sisters, Delores (William) Holmes, Diane (A.G.) Hibbler, and Toi Stephenson.

The family would like to extend their heartfelt gratitude to everyone for their kindness, love, support, and prayers during this difficult time.

You may share condolences with the family at the Heritage Funeral Home website: www.TnFunerals.com.
