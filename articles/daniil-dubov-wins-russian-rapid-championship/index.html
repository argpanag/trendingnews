Daniil Dubov Wins Russian Rapid Championship

TheTools · 18.10.2026 14:27 · source · permalink

Kateryna Lagno took the women's title.


The Russian Rapid Chess Championships concluded at the Grand Hotel "Zhemchuzhina" in Sochi. 

In the open tournament, GMs Daniil Dubov (Moscow), Dmitry Kokarev (Sverdlovsk Oblast), Arseniy Nesterov (Moscow Oblast), and Dmitry Kryakvin (Republic of Crimea) have scored 8.5 out of 11 points. This is exactly the order in which the additional tiebreakers have sorted them out. 

GM Kateryna Lagno (Moscow) won the Women’s Championship with 9 points out of 11. The winner finished ahead of WFM Anna Zhurova (Voronezh Oblast) due to superior additional tiebreakers. Third place was claimed by WGM Daria Voit, who scored 8 points and finished ahead of GM Valentina Gunina (both from Moscow) on superior additional tiebreakers.

Congratulations to the winners and prize-takers!

Friday, October 16, the Russian Blitz Championships will take place in Sochi. The competitions re the 13-round Swiss tournaments. 

The games start at 2 PM Moscow time.

The individual Russian Open and Women's Rapid and Blitz Chess Championships are organised by the Chess Federation of Russia with the support of the Ministry of Sports of the Russian Federation and the Russian Sports Foundation. 

General partner of the CFR is PhosAgro.

Media partner is the people’s social network Odnoklassniki.
