Liberia and Cape Verde meet again

The 2027 AFCON Qualifiers bring another showdown between Liberia and Cape Verde. Just four days after their previous meeting, both national teams will face each other once again, this time at the Samuel Kanyon Doe Sports Complex in Paynesville, for Matchday 4 of Group K.

The timing could not be more important for either side. While one team looks to prove it can compete for the top spots, the other needs answers before its qualification hopes become increasingly complicated.



Cape Verde holds the advantage after a goal-filled encounter

Last Friday, Cape Verde defeated Liberia 4-2 in a match that exposed the Lone Stars' defensive struggles. Ayoni Santos, Dje Tavares, Dailon Livramento, and Derensili Sanches Fernandes scored for the Blue Sharks, who picked up their first victory in the qualifiers.

The result earned them their first three points after opening the campaign with defeats against Mali and Rwanda. Now, they will have the opportunity to repeat that result, although playing away from home presents a different challenge for a team also looking to find consistency.



Liberia needs to respond to avoid falling further behind

The situation is much more complicated for Liberia, managed by Mohammed Erradi. The national team has suffered three consecutive defeats against Rwanda, Mali, and Cape Verde, leaving them without a single point in Group K. Their latest result also raised questions about their defensive performance, an issue they will need to address quickly.

Playing in front of their home supporters could provide an opportunity to turn things around. Players such as Franklin Nyenetue, who scored in the previous encounter, will be important as Liberia looks for an attacking response. However, the Lone Stars need to show a much stronger performance if they hope to avoid another defeat.



Group K tightens as Cape Verde looks to move closer to qualification

Mali leads the group with nine points, followed by Rwanda with six. Cape Verde sits in third place with three points, while Liberia remains at the bottom without a point. A victory would allow the visitors to temporarily draw level with Rwanda, while the hosts need points to keep their qualification hopes alive.

With only two matchdays remaining after this encounter, the opportunity to recover is becoming increasingly limited. The top two teams will qualify for the 2027 AFCON. Cape Verde is looking to return to the tournament for the first time since 2023, but first, the Blue Sharks must overcome a Liberia side desperate to change its fortunes in these qualifiers.
