Emma Watson resurfaces in US after quitting 'soul-destroying' Hollywood

TheTools · 24.10.2026 00:24 · source · permalink

Harry Potter star Emma Watson has resurfaced in the US after quitting "soul-destroying" Hollywood.

The actress grew up in Oxford and attended the Dragon School before going to Headington School, where she studied for her GCSEs and A levels.

Ms Watson also attended the city's branch of Stagecoach Theatre Arts before finding worldwide fame as Hermione Granger in the Harry Potter films.

The 36-year-old joined Oxford University in 2023 to begin a master's in creative writing and later switched to a DPhil in literature.

She has now been pictured making a rare appearance in Los Angeles after largely stepping away from mainstream Hollywood.

HELLO! magazine said Ms Watson had "resurfaced" as she attended an after-party for the 2026 Academy Museum Gala at Chateau Marmont.

The publication reported that she wore a lace gown featuring a thigh-high split and fringed mini skirt, together with a shawl and platform heels.

Ms Watson has largely remained away from mainstream Hollywood since appearing in Little Women in 2019, instead focusing on other pursuits including her studies.

She previously discussed her decision to step back from the industry in an interview with Hollywood Authentic, saying she was "maybe the happiest and healthiest I’ve ever been".

Speaking about the promotional side of the film industry, Ms Watson said: "The bigger component than the actual job itself is the promotion and selling of that piece of work, this piece of art. The balance of that can get quite thrown off.

"I think I’ll be honest and straightforward, and say: I do not miss selling things. I found that to be quite soul-destroying.

"But I do very much miss using my skill set, and I very much miss the art. I just found I got to do so little of the bit that I actually enjoyed."
