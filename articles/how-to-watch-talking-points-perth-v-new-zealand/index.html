Talking points

They left it late, thanks to some Elijah Pepper heroics, but the Wildcats are on the board for NBL27.

A big part of that was the play of their starters, who all scored in double figures on Thursday night.

It's now important they build on this win, in Saturday's fixture with the Breakers, which will be their third straight home match to open the new campaign.

Sharpshooter Dejan Vasiljevic has settled into his new home in New Zealand perfectly, hitting 10 triples across the first two games.

His pairing in the backcourt with Parker Jackson-Cartwright is already paying dividends for the Breakers, thanks to their inside-outside scoring potency.

The Boomers sharpshooter looms as a key figure in what could be a high-scoring clash at RAC Arena.
