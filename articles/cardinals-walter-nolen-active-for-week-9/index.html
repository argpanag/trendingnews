Cardinals' Walter Nolen: Active for Week 9

TheTools · 08.11.2026 23:30 · source · permalink

Nolen (ankle) is active for Sunday's game against the Seahawks.

Nolen's trio of limited practices during Week 9 prep proves enough to allow the defensive tackle to suit up for Sunday's game. The 23-year-old accumulated 14 tackles (five solo), including 1.0 sacks, in the 2026 regular season before missing consecutive contests with an ankle injury.
