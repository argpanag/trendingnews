Johor allocates over RM5mil for exam kits and meals for SPM candidates

TheTools · 17.11.2026 20:42 · source · permalink
    bookmark

JOHOR BARU: Johor has allocated over RM5mil to provide Sijil Pelajaran Malaysia (SPM) candidates with examination kits and free lunches during the upcoming national examination, says Datuk Onn Hafiz Ghazi.

The Johor Mentri Besar said RM880,000 had been set aside to provide SPM kits to 44,075 candidates from 297 schools across the state.

He said the kits would be distributed by state assemblymen and his official representatives from Nov 16 to 18.

"In addition, RM4.19mil has been allocated for the SPM lunch initiative, which will benefit all 44,075 candidates from the first day of the written examination on Nov 23," he said in a Facebook post here on Tuesday (Nov 17).

The written SPM 2026 examination will run from Nov 23 to Dec 17.

Onn Hafiz added the initiatives reflected the state government's commitment to supporting students and easing their needs throughout the examination period.

He expressed hope that the assistance would allow candidates to concentrate fully on their examinations and achieve their best results.

Onn Hafiz also wished all candidates success, urging them to remain calm and confident while answering their examination questions.

"To parents, guardians and teachers, thank you for your patience, sacrifices and guidance throughout this journey.

"Continue to encourage and support the students so that they can enter the examination halls with peace of mind," he said.

Johor education and information committee chairman Dr Muhammad Naqib Md Ghazali said the two initiatives were aimed at looking after the welfare of candidates while easing the financial burden on parents.

He said providing meals throughout the examination period would help ensure candidates are fed and can concentrate on answering their papers.

The examination kits, meanwhile, are intended to ensure candidates have the necessary stationery.

Dr Muhammad Naqib also expressed appreciation to teachers, parents, guardians and all parties who guided and encouraged the candidates throughout their preparations.
