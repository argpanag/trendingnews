Winter Wonderland: First look at new rides and attractions as festive funfair opens

Winter Wonderland is back for another year, with brand new attractions to mark the start of the 2026 festive season.

The huge funfair and festival, located in London’s Hyde Park, opened its gates on Thursday, November 19 and stays open every day (except for Christmas Day) until January 3, 2027.

At an exclusive preview on Wednesday of the much-loved event, which welcomes a host of celebrities each year, The Standard took an early look around the sprawling site, which is home to more than 150 rides and attractions.

What’s new for 2026?

Aside from old returning favourites, this year’s edition will see several new attractions and packages make their Winter Wonderland debut.

Guests can enjoy The Magical Ice Kingdom's “Neverland”, a new attraction filled with ice sculptures inspired by the iconic and timeless world of Peter Pan.

The attraction, which has been developed in association with Great Ormond Street Hospital Charity, has been brought to life using 500 tonnes of ice and snow.

The adventure begins in the Darling family nursery, where you’ll meet Tinkerbell and Peter Pan, before exploring a range of iconic settings, including the Lost Boys’ Camp and the formidable Jolly Roger ship.

Make sure to wrap up warm, as you can really feel the temperature drop and see your breath in the icy air.

It will take 15 minutes to walk through and enjoy the attraction, and adult tickets range from £10.20 to £15.60.

Child tickets range from £8.00 to £13.40, while family tickets (available for groups of two adults and two children, or one adult and three children) start at £32.00 and go up to £53.60.

Another new attraction, Gandeys K-Pop Dragon Circus, will also be making its Hyde Park debut this winter.

The 55-minute show fuses K-Pop with a circus show, complete with acrobatics, aerial acts and of course, dragons.

“Expect an action-packed performance that blends emotive storytelling with awe-inspiring stunts and unforgettable moments that will delight the whole family,” organisers said of the event, which has already toured across the UK.

It is inspired by 2025’s global phenomenon K-Pop Demon Hunters, which follows a girl group who fight evil entities and rival boy bands when they’re not busy performing sold-out shows.

Adult tickets begin at £13.75 and go up to £22.00, depending on dates and seats, and child prices range from £10.45 to £8.70.

Family tickets begin at £41.80 and go up to £74.80.

Of course, what would Winter Wonderland be without a photo opportunity? This year, the fun fair has installed a brand-new mirrored polo bear, situated right next to the Ice Slide.

A new VIP tour will also be available for guests to purchase, offering an exclusive festive experience.

Lasting 80 minutes, those in the tour will be able to skip queues as they are led around the fun fair with a guide.

Guests on the small-group tours can explore The Magical Ice Kingdom Presents Neverland, enjoy queue-free access to three handpicked rides, take on a fairground game, make use of a dedicated cloakroom and receive £30 in credit to spend on rides and games afterwards.

Stars flock to Winter Wonderland for an exclusive first look

Celebrities arrived en masse for the opening of the beloved park, from Guy Ritchie to Alexandra Burke.

What other rides and attractions are there?

Hyde Park Winter Wonderland boasts an impressive 150 rides, with many returning for the 2026 edition.

Here are some of the favourite attractions returning this year.

  • Christmas Market
  • Bavarian Village
  • Santa Land and Santa’s Grotto
  • Ice rink
  • The Real Ice Slide
  • Ice bar
  • The Giant Wheel – the world’s largest transportable observation wheel
  • Munich Looping – the world’s largest transportable rollercoaster – alongside the Euro Coaster and XXL

How much does it cost to attend?

You need a ticket to enter Winter Wonderland, though these can be booked online in advance.

Here is the price breakdown.

  • Off-peak: £1
  • Standard: £5.50
  • Peak: £8.25

Tickets just includes admission, with rides, food and drink, and experiences all being needed to be purchased separately.
