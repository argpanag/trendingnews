76ers' Dean Wade: Will start Friday

TheTools · 31.10.2026 00:38 · source · permalink

Wade will start Friday's game against the Knicks, Noah Levick of NBC Sports Philadelphia reports.

With Jaylen Brown (knee) sidelined, Wade will get the starting nod for the third time in Philadelphia's last four games. In two starts this season, he's averaged 7.0 points, 3.5 rebounds, 2.5 steals and 1.0 blocks in 32.5 minutes per game.
