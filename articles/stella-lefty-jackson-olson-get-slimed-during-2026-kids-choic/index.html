Stella Lefty, Jackson Olson Get Slimed During 2026 Kids' Choice Awards (Special Look)

TheTools · 15.11.2026 02:04 · source · permalink

Neither the singer-songwriter nor baseball player could escape the Slime.

The tradition of sliming on-stage participants continued during the 2026 Kids’ Choice Awards, with numerous notables finding themselves on the receiving end of the green substance.

Among those to earn a place in Kids’ Choice history were Stella Lefty and Jackson Olson.

The singer-songwriter and baseball player were appearing together in a segment when the slime hit; good sports about the moment, they posed for a fun backstage picture shortly thereafter.

CBS, which is joining Paramount+ to broadcast the Nickelodeon special, shared photos.
