UAE flight status today: Full list of cancellations, delays, and travel advisories for Saudi Arabia

TheTools · 18.10.2026 17:54 · source · permalink

Travel warnings issued amid UAE-Saudi flight cancellations and delays

UAE travellers face another unsettled day of air travel on Sunday, October 18, as cancellations and schedule changes affect flights between the Emirates and Saudi Arabia, alongside delays on other international routes.

Emirates, Etihad Airways and Air Arabia have reported cancellations affecting selected routes, while flydubai’s website shows a mix of arrived, estimated and in-flight services. Flight status information is unavailable for several flydubai routes between Dubai and Saudi Arabia.

Arrivals at Dubai International Airport (DXB) and Zayed International Airport in Abu Dhabi are also facing delays, with services from destinations including Toronto, Washington, Kathmandu, Mumbai and Tel Aviv listed as running behind schedule.

Travellers should check their flight number directly with the operating airline before leaving for the airport, as schedules and operating conditions can change at short notice.

flydubai’s Tel Aviv services remain affected following the security incident involving flight FZ1073 on September 30. Emirates has also suspended codeshare operations with flydubai to and from Tel Aviv until further notice.

Passengers travelling on these routes should contact their airline or travel agent before going to the airport.

The UAE’s suspension of flights operated by Iranian airlines to and from the country remains in effect until further notice, according to the information supplied. Iran Airtour cannot operate flights to or from Dubai under the restriction.

Affected passengers should contact their airline or travel agent for information about alternative arrangements.

The United States, United Kingdom and Australia have issued travel warnings amid reported threats to Saudi airports and continuing aviation disruption.

Riyadh: King Khalid International Airport remains suspended, according to the advisories supplied. This status should be confirmed against the latest official notice before publication.

Jeddah and Dammam: Services continue, but delays and cancellations remain possible.

United Kingdom: Advises against all travel within 10km of the Yemen border and all but essential travel elsewhere in Saudi Arabia.

United States: Warns of potential airport attacks and airspace closures and urges travellers to follow official security alerts.

Australia: Advises travellers to avoid Riyadh airport and consider alternatives.

Travellers should consult the latest official government advisories before changing plans. Alternative routes should be checked for flight availability, transit requirements and entry rules.
