French jokes and Luka Doncic praise headline Victor Wembanyama’s postgame reactions

By Johnny Askounis/ info@eurohoops.net

Victor Wembanyama was in a good mood on NBA on Prime after the San Antonio Spurs beat the Los Angeles Lakers at Frost Bank Center on Friday.

When Udonis Haslem pointed out how often the French center had used ‘we’ while discussing halftime adjustments, Wemby dropped a joke.

“You’re talking about we, we. You speak French now?” he teased, playing off the sound of oui before leading the crew in counting in French.

Later in the TV interview, Dwyane Wade compared him to Shaquille O’Neal. He kept everyone laughing with a quick jab.

“To me, the most dominant player of all time, or he’s up there,” he said of the former four-time champion. “I gotta say I’m just like Shaq free-throw-shooting-wise.”

In a serious approach during the postgame press conference, Wembanyama pointed directly to their early mistakes and how his team stormed back from a 20-point deficit.

“We were turning the ball over a lot. We were letting them get offensive boards, not as much pressure, not as much aggressiveness,” he said. “I feel like we still approached the game with a good mentality, but maybe a little off. We should have been more on the front foot and more with a meaner behavior, just like we had in the second half.”

That shift paid off. The result pushed San Antonio into the knockout round of the Emirates NBA Cup 2026 and capped a matchup between early Most Valuable Player candidates in Wembanyama and Luka Doncic.

“He’s the same that I’ve seen all season. And I was surprised of the quality of the game that they offered and that he offered, collectively,” he went on to credit the Slovenian superstar and the Lakers.

San Antonio improved to 16–4 with the win, passing the Lakers for first place in the West and tying the Philadelphia 76ers for the league’s best record ahead of Sunday’s home game against the Boston Celtics.
