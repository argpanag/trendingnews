1 taken to hospital after crash involving bicyclist on Highway 544

TheTools · 15.10.2026 13:33 · source · permalink

HORRY COUNTY, SC (WMBF) - One person is hurt after a crash on a busy highway in Horry County.

On Thursday, Horry County Fire Rescue said that the crash and involved a person on a bicycle.

One person is being taken to the hospital.

Horry County Fire Rescue was dispatched 7:48 a.m.

South Carolina Highway Patrol is investigating.
