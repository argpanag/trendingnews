David Miller Arrives at TU Cricket Ground for Karkali Yaks Match

TheTools · 06.11.2026 10:40 · source · permalink

Kathmandu. South Africa's famous batter David Miller, who is contracted with Karnali Yaks, has reached the TU Cricket Ground. He started warming up after reaching there before the match started on Friday.

37-year-old left-handed middle-order batter Miller has made his mark in famous franchise leagues around the world as well as in South Africa. He has played in competitions such as India's Indian Premier League (IPL), Australia's Big Bash League, and Pakistan Premier League.

In the IPL, he played for Delhi Capitals, Rajasthan Royals, Kings XI Punjab, and Gujarat Titans. Miller was a key player in the Gujarat Titans team when they won the IPL title in 2022.

Miller has scored 2,804 runs in 140 T20 international matches for South Africa. In this process, he has hit two centuries and nine half-centuries.

In overall T20 cricket, he has played 584 matches and scored 12,435 runs. In this process, he has hit four centuries and 53 half-centuries.

In 2017, he scored a century in 35 balls against Bangladesh. Karnali will play against Sudurpashchim Royals on Friday. Miller will also be on the field in the match which will start at 12:30 PM.
