MATCH PREVIEW: Inter Miami CF in Road Action Against Columbus Crew this Sunday | Inter Miami CF

TheTools · 28.09.2026 00:24 · source · permalink

Inter Miami CF (12W-4L-10D, 46 points) carries on with MLS regular season action on the road this Sunday, Sept. 27, heading to Columbus, Ohio to take on the Columbus Crew (7W-14L-5D, 16 points). Kick off at ScottsMiracle-Gro Field is set for 7 p.m. ET.

Where to Watch

Fans around the world can tune in to the action live on Apple TV.

Past Match

Inter Miami visits Columbus after earning a point last Sunday night with a 2-2 draw at home against San Diego FC in MLS regular season action. Goals from captain Leo Messi and striker Luis Suárez secured the point at Nu Stadium.

Inter Miami CF This Regular Season

Overall, Inter Miami has recorded 12 wins, four losses, and 10 draws so far this MLS regular season for a total 46 points, and sits second in the Eastern Conference standings.

Messi, the reigning MLS Landon Donovan MVP Award and Golden Boot presented by Audi winner, leads both the team and the league in goals with 20. Additionally, Messi is also tied for the lead in assists this MLS regular season with 13 so far.

Potential Debut

Brazilian winger Riquelme Fillipi, 19, has completed the necessary work documentation and is clear to make his Inter Miami debut this weekend after being signed at the start of the month.

Previously Against Columbus Crew

Sunday's meeting presents the 13th meeting between the sides in Club history. Inter Miami leads the series with seven wins to three for Columbus Crew, while two other matchups restulted in a draw. 

Inter Miami aims to extend its current four-match unbeaten run against the Crew. Last time out, the teams faced off in Miami in early August in an MLS regular season match that resulted in a 2-2 draw.

Scouting Columbus Crew

Columbus Crew hosts Inter Miami after earning a 0-2 victory on the road against CF Montréal last weekend. Overall, the Ohio-based side has recorded seven wins, 10 losses, and five draws so far for a total 26 points this regular season and is 13th in the Eastern Conference table.
