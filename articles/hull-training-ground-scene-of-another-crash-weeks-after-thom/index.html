Hull training ground scene of another crash weeks after Thomas rolled car over

TheTools · 01.10.2026 10:33 · source · permalink
  • Josh Fordham
  • Jake Lambourne
  • Published: 11:33, 1 Oct 2026
  • Updated: 13:22, 1 Oct 2026

Another Hull City player has reportedly been involved in a car crash near the club's training ground.

Pictures show a Mercedes on its side on a residential road on Thursday morning.

But in positive news, no one has been harmed by the incident.

A statement from the club read: “Hull City is aware of images circulating on social media following a road traffic accident near the club’s training ground and can confirm that no one was harmed in the incident.”

A statement from Humberside Police read: “Officers and emergency services were called to reports of a single vehicle road traffic collision on Millhouse Woods Lane in Cottingham at around 8.30am this morning (Thursday, 1 October 2026).

"Thankfully, no one was injured as a result of the collision.”

What other Hull players have been involved in car accidents?

This is believed to be the second player to have been involved in a crash near Hull's training complex in the last three weeks.

Sorba Thomas rolled his car over earlier in September, with emergency services quickly arriving on the scene.

The Wales winger's vehicle was pictured upside down on a residential road in Cottingham.

Thomas was lured to the MKM Stadium in the summer as high-flying Hull look to avoid an immediate drop into the Championship.

He arrived in a £10m transfer from Stoke, with the 27-year-old appearing in each of the club's last three Premier League matches.

Thankfully, the forward escaped without any injuries.

Hull said in a statement: "We are relieved to confirm that Sorba Thomas is unharmed following a road traffic accident near to the club’s Cottingham training ground.

"The club is aware of images of Sorba’s car circulating on social media and can reassure supporters that he has sustained no injuries and emerged from his vehicle completely unscathed."

Following Thomas' crash, local councillor Phil Redshaw declared he had raised an issue with Hull City - stressing the importance of driving slowly on their way to the training ground.

He remarked: "I wrote to the Hull City club secretary on Sunday asking them to remind players to be careful driving down the road because of the residents’ concerns.

"I wanted them to remind their staff to drive with extra care, because they get new players in every season.”

Hull currently sit in eighth spot in the Premier League table after five matches, with the Yorkshire outfit to face Everton after the international break.
