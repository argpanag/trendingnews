Channing Tatum is setting the record straight.

Amid speculation that he's the secret owner of an Instagram responsible for leaving shady comments on his ex Zoë Kravitz's account, the Step Up star addressed the rumors, denying being the unnamed user.

"Yo i truly can't believe yall think this is what l'm doing with my free time," Tatum wrote in a statement shared to his Instagram Stories Sept. 21. "F--kin hilarious."

And the 46-year-old also pointed out a notable flaw in the online theories, many of which claimed the mysterious Instagram was only followed by Tatum's verified account.

"Also why would I follow a burner account if I didn't want people to connect me to it?" he continued. "Wtf, come on."

In fact, Tatum—who shares daughter Everly, 12, with ex-wife Jenna Dewan—closed out his message by assuring fans there's only one place they should go if they want to hear directly from him.

"If I really need to say it," the Magic Mike actor added, "l only have one IG account, @channingtatum."

Days before Tatum cleared the air, internet sleuths noticed an account named "Nonya Bidness" was commenting below several Instagram posts about Harry Styles and his fiancée Kravitz, who Tatum was engaged to before calling it quits in 2024. Under one post about a PDA moment between the "Watermelon Sugar" singer and High Fidelity actress shared, the account wrote in a since-deleted comment, "Just here for the hate."

Soon after, theories emerged that the trolling came from Tatum due to the Instagram profile appearing to only be followed by his official account.

Photo by Arturo Holmes/Getty Images

And while Tatum—who is currently dating model Inka Williams—may be staying tight-lipped on his past relationship, Kravitz has previously shared that she has no regrets about their romance, which began in 2021 and fizzled out shortly after the release of their 2024 film Blink Twice.

"I love this thing that we made together," Kravitz told Elle in 2025, "and I care for him very much."

She added, "I'm so happy that all happened. I just feel so grateful to go on that journey together."

To look back on Kravitz and Tatum's complete relationship timeline, keep reading.

Collaborators: June 2021

In a June 2021 interview with Deadline, Channing Tatum shared how honored he was that Zoë Kravitz thought to include him in her directorial debut, Pussy Island, later renamed Blink Twice.

Romance Rumors: August 2021

Amid rumblings of a romance, Tatum and Kravitz appeared to confirm their relationship status in August 2021 with a sweet bike ride around New York City. "There's more than a friendship going on with Channing and Zoe," a source told E! News at the time. "They are spending a lot of time together and having fun. They are more than just close friends or co-stars. Their relationship has grown into more."

Out and About: August 2021

Sightings of the pair became more frequent, and they were spotted again in August enjoying a walk after having lunch in New York.

Met Gala Date Night: September 2021

Though they initially posed separately on the 2021 Met Gala red carpet, the duo was photographed exiting the star-studded event together. Tatum even shared a photo with Kravitz on his Instagram Story from the after-party.

First Halloween: October 2021

For their first Halloween together, the pair dressed up as Robert De Niro and Jodie Foster's characters from the film Taxi Driver.

A "Wonderful" Romance: November 2022

During a chat with GQ, Kravitz said of Tatum, "He's just a wonderful human. He makes me laugh and we both really love art and talking about art and the exploration of why we do what we do. We love to watch a film and break it down and talk about it and challenge each other."

Couples Costume: October 2023

They dressed up as Rosemary Woodhouse and her baby from Rosemary's Baby for Kendall Jenner's 2023 Halloween Party in L.A.

Engaged: October 2023

After over two years of dating, multiple outlets confirmed that Kravitz and Tatum were engaged to be married.

Family Affair: March 2024

Channing showed his support for the Kravitz fam by joining Zoe and her dad Lenny Kravitz at his Hollywood Walk of Fame Star Ceremony.

Big Premiere: August 2024

They looked smitten at the Los Angeles premiere of their movie collaboration, Blink Twice.

Glammed Up: August 2024

The pair continued their press tour together at the European premiere of Blink Twice in London.

Split: October 2024

Tatum and Kravitz broke up after three years together, multiple outlets reported in October 2024.
