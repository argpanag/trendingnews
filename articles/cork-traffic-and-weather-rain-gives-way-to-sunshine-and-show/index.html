Cork traffic and weather: Rain gives way to sunshine and showers as temperatures reach 14C

TheTools · 09.10.2026 06:45 · source · permalink

Motorists are being warned of a reported hazard on the westbound N25 at the off-slip for Junction 1. Picture: Chani Anderson

Cork, and Munster as a whole, are set for a wet and blustery start to Friday, but there will be brighter spells later as rain gives way to sunshine and scattered showers.

Outbreaks of rain and gusty winds will affect the province this morning before conditions improve during the afternoon. 

It will remain a little breezy, with temperatures reaching between 12C and 14C.

Friday night will bring a mixture of clear spells and isolated showers, although much of the east and south should stay dry. 

Temperatures will fall to between 9C and 11C.

Traffic 

Motorists are being warned of a reported hazard on the westbound N25 at the off-slip for Junction 1.

Drivers should approach the area with caution.

National outlook 

Elsewhere, a dull, damp and wet start will see a band of rain move further south across Leinster and Munster.

Brighter and fresher conditions will follow, with a mix of sunshine and scattered showers developing behind the rain. 

A brisk westerly breeze will persist, with afternoon temperatures ranging from 11C to 14C.

Friday night will remain breezy, with clear spells and isolated showers. 

Much of the east and south will stay dry, with overnight lows of 8C to 11C.
