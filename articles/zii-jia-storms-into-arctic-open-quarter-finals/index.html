Zii Jia storms into Arctic Open quarter-finals

TheTools · 08.10.2026 09:32 · source · permalink

The Malaysian world No 56 shuttler defeated a higher-ranked Taiwanese player in straight games in the last-16 match.

The world No 56 men’s singles shuttler beat Su Li Yang, who is ranked 17 slots above him, 21-17, 21-12 in a match lasting 46 minutes at Energia Areena in Vantaa, Finland.

Zii Jia has a tough last-eight match tomorrow against France’s world No 2 shuttler Christo Popov.

Yesterday, Zii Jia scored an impressive opening round win over China’s 40th-ranked Dong Tian Yao in straight games.

The former world No 2 player has had a rough road in his return to competitive badminton since January after a lengthy layoff.

Separately, Malaysian mixed doubles pair Jimmy Wong-Cheng Su Yin lost to Japan’s Akira Koga-Natsu Saito 21-19, 7-21, 11-21 in 61 minutes in a second-round match.
