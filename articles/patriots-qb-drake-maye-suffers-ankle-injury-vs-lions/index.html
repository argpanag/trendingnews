Patriots QB Drake Maye suffers ankle injury vs. Lions

TheTools · 15.11.2026 16:15 · source · permalink

New England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye was ruled out of Sunday's game against the Detroit Lions after suffering an ankle injury in the first quarter.

Maye appeared to suffer the injury on a first-quarter sack by Lions pass rusher Aidan Hutchinson, who also hurt his knee on the play.

Maye walked off the field under his own power and went to the bench. He then went to the locker room with trainers.

Maye was initially deemed questionable to return before being downgraded in the second quarter.

The third-year QB was 3-of-4 passing for 48 yards, with an 11-yard scramble, at the time of his exit.

Tommy DeVito is in at quarterback.
