GTA 6 Players Warned To Turn Off Auto-Uploads Or Risk Getting Banned On Xbox

TheTools · 20.11.2026 11:00 · source · permalink

You could be getting thirst-trapped

Xbox is handing out bans to GTA 6 players for uploading game captures that 'accidentally' breach its family and online safety guidelines.

On by default, game captures automatically uploaded to Xbox Live servers are scanned for offensive imagery, including hate speech, drugs misuse, excessive violence, as well as nudity and sexual content.

According to some Reddit sleuthing, GTA 6 players are being banned for uploading content captured on the beach in Leonida amongst other activities. The original poster claimed to have captured "zero nudity-just standard Vice City beach/coastal gameplay with NPCs in swimsuits/bikinis" before getting slapped with a ToS violation.

If you think about it, it would be odd to walk around a beach with a video camera filming the locals, but it's a bit silly to see Xbox players getting 2-day bans. The rulings appear to be automated and unappealable too.

It's not clear if sexual content or nudity is the cause for the violation, but it's worth noting GTA 6 does contain full-frontal nudity. We'd recommend not spamming the capture button during those sequences. Xbox doesn't like that sort of thirsty behaviour.

If you're worried about getting a 2-day ban in the height of GTA 6 fever, then perhaps you could consider turning off automatic uploads in your Xbox settings. Here's a quick guide on how to do this:

How To Turn Off Automatic Uploads - Game Capture

  1. Go to Settings
  2. Navigate to Preferences > Capture & Share
  3. Toggle Automatically Upload to Don't Upload
