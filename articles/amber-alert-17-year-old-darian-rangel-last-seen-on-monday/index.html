SAN ANTONIO - The Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS) has issued an AMBER ALERT for a missing teenager.

17-year-old Darian Rangel was last seen around 8:30 p.m. on Sept. 7 on the 7200 block of Marbach Rd and Military Drive W.

Rangel stands about 5 feet and 7 inches tall, weighing about 160 pounds, with brown eyes and brown hair.

He was last seen wearing a red Bass Pro Shop hat, black shirt, black jeans, black shoes, and carrying a cross-body Coach bag.

Anyone with any information on his whereabouts is asked to call the San Antonio Police Department Missing Persons Unit at (210)207-7660.

Harboring a runaway child is a Class A Misdemeanor punishable by a fine of up to $4,000 and/or up to one year in jail.
