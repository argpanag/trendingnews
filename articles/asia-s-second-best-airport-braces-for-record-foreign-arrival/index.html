Asia's second best airport braces for record foreign arrivals during China's Golden Week - VnExpress International

TheTools · 25.09.2026 07:20 · source · permalink

The Incheon Airport Immigration Office under the Ministry of Justice said between 198,000 and 220,000 foreign nationals are expected to enter the country through the airport during the four-day period, Korea Times reported.

That amounts to an average of 49,500 to 55,000 arrivals a day, exceeding the daily average of 45,640 recorded from the holiday period during Sept. 30 - Oct. 4 last year.

Incheon International Airport in South Korea. Photo by Skytrax

Foreign arrivals could reach 60,000 on Oct. 2, ahead of the weekend, slightly surpassing this year's single-day record of 59,000, Korea Herald reported.

In response to the overcrowding, airport authorities will implement emergency measures for four days starting Sept. 30, increasing the number of immigration officers assigned to arrival halls to 188 from 128 during normal operations and 166 during special measures periods such as holiday seasons.

Staffing at departure halls will also rise from 38 to 54 officers.

Departure halls will open earlier for 12 days from Sept. 30.

Ranked Asia's second best airport after Singapore Changi in the 2026 Skytrax World Airport Awards, Incheon handled 38.39 million international passengers in the first half of this year, a 6.3% increase from the same period last year.

The airport is set to expand international services linking the country's main gateway with major Chinese cities amid a surge in tourism.

South Korea welcomed 3.99 million tourists from mainland China in the first seven months of 2026, up 27.5% from the same period last year.
