'The End Of Oak Street' Gets HBO Max Release Date

TheTools · 06.11.2026 18:01 · source · permalink

Warner Bros. Pictures’ The End of Oak Street will make its global streaming debut exclusively on HBO Max on Friday, November 13.

The movie will be released on HBO linear the following day, November 14, at 8 p.m. ET.

The film, written and directed by David Robert Mitchell (Under the Silver Lake, It Follows), stars Anne Hathaway, Ewan McGregor, Maisy Stella and Christian Convery in a tale of a 1980s neighborhood transported via cosmic event back to the age of the dinosaurs.

“The Platt family,” the synopis continues, “soon discovers that their very survival depends on them sticking together as they navigate their now unrecognizable surroundings.”

The film is produced by J.J. Abrams, Hannah Minghella, Jon Cohen, David Robert Mitchell, Matt Jackson and Tommy Harper. The executive producers are Chris Bender, Jake Weiner, Joanne Lee and Leeann Stonebreaker.

Watch on Deadline

The film has grossed $122 million worldwide. Critics were generally kind, with Deadline’s Pete Hammond calling it “a blast.”
