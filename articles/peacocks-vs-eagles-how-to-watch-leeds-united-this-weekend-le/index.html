Peacocks vs Eagles: How to watch Leeds United this weekend! - Leeds United

TheTools · 20.09.2026 14:41 · source · permalink

Elland Road hosts another Premier League fixture on Sunday afternoon as Leeds United welcome Crystal Palace, with the Peacocks looking to maintain their place among the early pace-setters.

Daniel Farke’s side head into the contest sitting third in the table, unbeaten in their first four matches after two wins and two draws. That run was enhanced in style on Monday night, when Leeds produced an impressive 4-1 victory over Newcastle United under the Elland Road lights.

The Eagles have collected one league win so far under new head coach Pierre Sage, who took charge during the summer, and arrive in Yorkshire looking to respond after a 3-2 home defeat against Ipswich Town last Saturday.

With the action getting under way at 2pm, here’s how you can watch and follow Sunday’s clash between the two sides.

PRE-MATCH SHOW

Coverage starts from 1:30pm with the official pre-match show, live on LUTV and across the club’s digital platforms.

Leeds United alumni Lucas Radebe, Stuart Dallas and Michael Bridges will be in the studio, previewing the action with their thoughts on team news, tactical set-up and the key battles to watch at Elland Road.

LUTV AUDIO COMMENTARY

Supporters unable to watch live can still stay across all the action via LUTV’s live audio commentary.

Bryn Law will be your guide from Elland Road alongside Leeds United legend Tony Dorigo, offering expert insight and analysis throughout the 90 minutes.

FOLLOW ON SOCIAL MEDIA

Fans can also keep across all the latest via the club’s official social media channels, with live updates, behind-the-scenes access and exclusive matchday content on Facebook, X (Twitter), Instagram, WhatsApp.

From team news through to key moments and post-match reaction, Leeds United’s digital platforms will ensure supporters remain connected to the action at Elland Road wherever they are in the world.
