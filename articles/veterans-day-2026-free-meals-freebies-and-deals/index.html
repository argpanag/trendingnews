TheTools · 10.11.2026 22:14 · source · permalink

A list of restaurant, retail and attraction deals for veterans, servicemembers and their family.

Please note that these offers are subject to change and may not be available in all areas. It is recommended that you verify with the restaurant, retailer or other business you plan to visit.

If you are a business and would like to add your Veterans Day offer to our list, please contact us here.

During the month of November, the Hall of Fame will offer all military veterans free admission to the home of baseball in Cooperstown.

All veterans will be admitted to the museum free of charge in November with proof of veteran status.

The Museum will host special public programs throughout the day on Veterans Day, Wednesday, Nov. 11, including the debut screening of the documentary Trimble: Baseball’s Unknown Hero. The feature-length film by director Pat Kondelis tells the story of amateur star Jimmie Trimble, who enlisted in the Marines after signing a contract with the Washington Senators and was killed in action at Iwo Jima. The film will be screened at 1 p.m. Nov. 11 in the Museum’s Bullpen Theater and is available on a first-come basis to all those with a museum ticket for that day.
