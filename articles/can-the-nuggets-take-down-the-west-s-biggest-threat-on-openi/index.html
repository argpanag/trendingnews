Can the Nuggets take down the west’s biggest threat on opening night? - AOL

TheTools · 23.10.2026 04:03 · source · permalink

DENVER (KDVR) — The Denver Nuggets are preparing to tip off their 2026-27 NBA season Thursday night against the Oklahoma City Thunder in a highly anticipated Western Conference matchup.

The Nuggets will travel to Oklahoma City for their season opener at Paycom Center, with tipoff scheduled for 7:30 p.m. MDT.

What do the Nuggets need to be successful this season?

Denver is looking for a strong start after finishing last season with a 54-28 record. However, the Nuggets will face a tough challenge against a Thunder team that finished 64-18 last season.

Three-time NBA Most Valuable Player Nikola Jokić will once again lead Denver’s offense alongside star guard Jamal Murray and forward Aaron Gordon.

Jokić averaged 27.7 points, 12.9 rebounds and 10.7 assists last season, while Murray averaged 25.4 points per game.

One of the biggest matchups to watch will be Jokić against Oklahoma City star Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, who averaged 31.1 points per game last season.

The Nuggets will also need to find ways to slow down Oklahoma City’s defense, which allowed just 107.9 points per game last season, the second-fewest in the NBA.

Another key factor will be Denver’s bench production. Veteran forward DeMar DeRozan joins the Nuggets this season and is expected to provide additional scoring when the starters are resting.

Denver will be without guard Julian Strawther, who is sidelined with a knee injury.

The Nuggets will also look to improve defensively after allowing an average of 116.9 points per game last season.

Ball movement, rebounding and limiting turnovers could play major roles in Thursday’s matchup.

For Denver, beating one of the Western Conference’s strongest teams could provide an early boost as the Nuggets begin their pursuit of another NBA championship.
