Villarreal - Searching for a first win

Villarreal have had a tough start to the season and are still looking for their first league victory. In the next sections, we will take a closer look at their current form, recent results, and tactical approach for this match against Real Betis. We will also analyse the expected Villarreal vs. Real Betis line-ups.

Recent form

Villarreal’s start to the Spain Primera Division season has been challenging. They currently sit in 17th place with just two points from four matches. The team is yet to register a win in the league, recording two draws and two losses.

Their recent form is a cause for concern, marked by consecutive defeats in the domestic competition against Alaves and Deportivo La Coruna. They conceded four goals in those two games. Despite scoring against Deportivo La Coruna, their inability to secure points highlights a defensive fragility that needs to be addressed if they are to climb the table.

On a positive note, Villarreal can count on a full squad, with no players reported injured for this clash. Offensively, they have players who can make a difference, such as Nicolas Pépé, who has already found the back of the net twice this season. The pressure is mounting at the Estadio de la Cerámica, and the team will be desperate to turn their fortunes around with a much-needed home victory.

Real Betis - Riding a wave of success

Let’s take a look at Real Betis’s current situation. We will analyse the team’s recent form, their latest results, and standout players for this match.

Recent form

Real Betis occupies 5th in the Spain Primera Division, earning nine points from four games. Their league form is mixed, with a 1-0 win over Real Madrid and a 5-2 away loss to Levante. This highlights their inconsistent performances away from home.

Boosted by a 3-2 UEFA Champions League win against Lille and their recent Real Madrid victory, Real Betis carry strong momentum. Rodrigo Riquelme, a standout midfielder, has netted two goals, providing a significant attacking threat.

With no reported injuries, the visitors can deploy their strongest team. Following recent domestic and European victories, Real Betis arrive at the Estadio de la Cerámica full of confidence. Their potent attack and fully-fit squad pose a significant challenge for Villarreal.
