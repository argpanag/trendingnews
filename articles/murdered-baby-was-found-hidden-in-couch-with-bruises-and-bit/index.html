Murdered baby was found hidden in couch with bruises and bite marks, citation shows

TheTools · 18.09.2026 16:54 · source · permalink

MUHLENBERG CO., Ky., Ind. (WFIE) - A criminal citation obtained by 14 News reveals new disturbing details surrounding the murder of an 11-month-old boy in Muhlenberg County.

As we reported, 11-month-old Grayson Walton was found dead inside a home on Hillview Street in the small community of Cleaton on Sept. 13.

Authorities said 19-year-old Elijah Mayes was babysitting Grayson while Grayson’s father was at work.

Mayes claimed a family friend had picked the child up on Friday night, but investigators say they learned that never happened. Mayes was later arrested and charged with the child’s murder, in addition to criminal abuse, tampering with evidence, abuse of a corpse, and possession of meth.

Court documents obtained on Friday detail the disturbing scene that was uncovered as authorities searched the home on Hillview Street.

During their search of the home, police say they found baby Grayson dead inside a couch.

They say Grayson was covered in bruises and appeared to have teeth marks on his stomach.

According to police, Mayes also made a fake phone number and texted himself, pretending to be the person he gave the baby to, in an attempt to convince them of his alibi.

The citation says Mayes’ meth charge stems from the separate search of a home on Harrison Avenue. Authorities said they found a shard of meth in a dresser drawer belonging to Mayes at that home.

As we’ve reported, Grayson’s step-grandfather has also been arrested in connection to the case. He’s charged with tampering with physical evidence.

Mayes was in court for the first time Thursday.

14 News will continue to follow this developing story.
