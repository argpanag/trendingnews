Missing 14-year-old girl last seen in St Wilfred Road area; police appeal for information

TheTools · 02.10.2026 13:39 · source · permalink

The police are appealing for information on the whereabouts of a 14-year-old schoolgirl, Tianyi Chanel Maglio, who was last seen in the Bendemeer area.

Police said in a news release on Friday (Oct 2) night that Tianyi was last seen at about 9.30am on Sept 25 near 1 Saint Wilfred Road, wearing a white polo tee with a dark collar and a greyish-blue skirt.

The said address is the location of Bendemeer Secondary School, and the clothing description suggests she may have been wearing the school's uniform when she was last seen.

Anyone with information about her whereabouts is urged to contact the police hotline at 1800-255-0000 or submit information online at www.police.gov.sg/i-witness.

All information will be kept strictly confidential.
