Apes Hill Barbados Wins Three Titles at World Golf Awards

TheTools · 10.11.2026 08:09 · source · permalink

Apes Hill Barbados has won three honours at the 2026 World Golf Awards, held on November 7 at Madinaty Golf Club in Egypt. The resort claimed the titles of Caribbean's Best Golf Course, Caribbean's Best Golf Clubhouse, and secured a second consecutive award for Little Apes as the Caribbean's Best 9 Hole Golf Course.

The awards recognise the breadth of experiences available at Apes Hill, from the championship course to the family-friendly Little Apes and the hospitality provided at the clubhouse. Sunil Chatrani, Executive Chairman of Apes Hill Barbados, reflected on the recognition.

„To receive awards for our championship course and clubhouse as well is a source of real pride for the team. They put a great deal of care into the courses and into looking after our members and guests.“

Little Apes: Golf for All

Set within the resort's 475-acre estate, Little Apes is a nine-hole, par-three walking course designed for golfers of all ages and abilities. The compact layout allows families and friends to complete a round in approximately one hour, offering beginners the chance to learn the game without the demands of a full championship course.

The course draws inspiration from well-known golf holes around the world and provides views across the landscape toward the Caribbean Sea. Experienced players can use it to develop their short game, making it a versatile facility within the resort community.

The Championship Course and Clubhouse

Apes Hill's 18-hole championship course sits at approximately 1,000 feet above sea level, commanding views of both the Atlantic Ocean and the Caribbean Sea. The elevated setting allows the course to make dramatic use of indigenous gullies and tropical vegetation in its design. An island-green 19th hole adds a distinctive feature, while the Golf Performance Centre provides technology-supported coaching and practice facilities.

The clubhouse, which overlooks the 19th hole, houses the Noisy Cricket and 20th Hole restaurants, functioning as a gathering place for members and guests before and after their rounds. The facilities contribute to the social life of the wider resort and community.

The World Golf Awards recognition follows Apes Hill's inclusion in GOLF Magazine's Top 100 Golf Resorts in the World, which also recognised the resort for „Best Lodging“ among that list. The accolades celebrate Apes Hill's investment in creating a Caribbean luxury destination where championship golf, wellness, nature, sustainability and hospitality converge within a single community.
