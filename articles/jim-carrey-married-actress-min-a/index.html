Jim Carrey married actress Min A

TheTools · 01.10.2026 09:55 · source · permalink

Hollywood actor Jim Carrey married his girlfriend, artist and actress Min A, also known as Minzi. 

The wedding ceremony took place in Los Angeles in an intimate, private setting. Jim Carrey and Min A have been in a relationship since 2022.

The couple made their first official public appearance together seven months ago. At that time, Jim Carrey attended the César Awards ceremony, where he received an award for outstanding achievements in cinema.

For the star of the films *Bruce Almighty* and *How the Grinch Stole Christmas*, this is his third marriage.

Jim Carrey first married in 1987. His wife was Melissa Womer. That same year, the couple had a daughter, Jane.

Carrie and Womer’s marriage ended in divorce in 1995.

In 1996, the actor married for the second time—to Lauren Holly, his co-star in the cult comedy *Dumb and Dumber*. However, this marriage was short-lived: Carrey and Holly separated as early as 1997.

After that, Jim Carrey had several high-profile romantic relationships.

From 1999 to 2000, the actor dated actress Renée Zellweger, whom he met while working on a film.

After breaking up with Zellweger, Carrey began a relationship with actress and TV host Jenny McCarthy. Their romance lasted several years, but the couple split up in 2010.

In 2015, news broke of the tragic death of the actor’s former girlfriend, Catriona White, who took her own life.

Meanwhile, Jim Carrey’s fans can look forward to seeing him in the sequel to one of the most famous films of his career.

Universal Studios and Imagine Entertainment are working on a sequel to the 2000 family comedy *How the Grinch Stole Christmas*, in which Jim Carrey played the lead role.

The film, directed by Ron Howard, became one of the most famous Christmas comedies starring Carrey, and his portrayal of the Grinch gained widespread popularity among audiences.

A representative for the 64-year-old actor confirmed this information to Just Jared and People.
