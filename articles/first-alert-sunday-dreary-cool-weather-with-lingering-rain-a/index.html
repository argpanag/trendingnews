First Alert Sunday: Dreary, cool weather with lingering rain across the Carolinas

TheTools · 04.10.2026 11:54 · source · permalink

CHARLOTTE, N.C. (WBTV) - After rounds of rain, heavy at times, dreary and cool conditions are holding strong across the Carolinas.

Expect widespread cloud cover Sunday with fog, mist and periods of light rain and drizzle. Highs will manage only the upper 60s in most locations. Localized flooding could become an issue in a few spots, especially this morning.

Rain coverage and intensity have decreased compared to Saturday, but conditions will stay gray and damp through the day. Another round of light and steady rainfall will be possible this evening, with the best chance for impacts in central and southeastern counties.

Clouds and fog will linger through the Monday morning commute before gradual clearing takes place. By Monday afternoon, sunshine returns with highs back in the upper 70s.

Dry fall weather follows for the middle of the week. Another cooldown arrives Tuesday and Wednesday, with morning lows in the 40s and 50s and afternoon highs in the low to mid-70s under sunshine.

• Today: First Alert - Fog, mist, areas of light rain, cooler temperatures

• Monday: AM clouds/fog, PM sunshine, warmer

• This Week: Dry, cool mornings, afternoon fall sunshine!
