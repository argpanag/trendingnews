How to Watch All Six Hunger Games Movies in the Correct Order

TheTools · 19.11.2026 15:00 · source · permalink

There's finally enough Hunger Games movie adaptations to debate the order to watch them! This Friday, November 20, marks the release of Sunrise on the Reaping, the official depiction of Haymitch Abernathy's turn in the 50th annual Hunger Games. The second Quarter Quell—featuring double the amount of tributes—occurs a full 24 years before Katniss Everdeen enters the arena to protect her baby sister, becoming a symbol of resistance in the process.

So, does one have to watch all four of the Jennifer Lawrence-helmed films based on author Suzanne Collins' original dystopian trilogy before SOTR? Or should you start your inevitable Hunger Games binge with the Coriolanus Snow-centric prequel, The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes, and go from there?

The answer, I'd hope, is obvious. Sure, it might be fun to watch the films in chronological order for your second or third re-watch but the full impact of both President Snow's villain arc and Haymitch Abernathy's tragic victory can only be truly be felt when you know where everything leads. And no, it's not a spoiler to reveal that Haymitch won his games, considering he is District 12's only living victor when we meet him in the original saga. Woody Harrelson didn't wear his pajamas to the Paris premiere of The Hunger Games: Mockingjay–Part 2 to be disrespected like this.

Of course, I'm not the ruthless dictator here. Whether you're going to view the series correctly or go rogue and binge them chronologically, here's how to watch each Hunger Games film in the order God Suzanne Collins intended. (Only the obvious spoilers beyond this point.)

The Hunger Games (2012)

Elizabeth Banks and Jennifer Lawrence in The Hunger Games.

The first movie based on the original Hunger Games series introduces us to the dystopian world of Panem through the eyes of District 12 tribute Katniss Everdeen (Lawrence). As punishment for a 74-year-old rebellion against the Capitol and its leaders, two young members of each of Panem's impoverished districts are forced compete in an annual game of survival, which doubles as entertainment for the wealthy members of the Capitol. Only one of the 24 tributes can make it out of the Hunger Games alive.

How to watch: Stream The Hunger Games on Peacock or rent/purchase on Amazon Prime Video. You can also purchase Prime's five film bundle (including the original four films and The Ballad of Songbirds and Saints), which is currently on sale for $19.99.

Catching Fire (2013)

Josh Hutcherson and Lawrence in The Hunger Games: Catching Fire.

After defying President Snow to save her fellow tribute Peeta Mellark (Josh Hutcherson) at the end of The Hunger Games, the pair are sent back into the arena for the third Quarter Quell, which offers a twist on games every 25 years. President Snow (Donald Sutherland) hopes that sending the so-called lovers—and surviving victors from the rest of the districts—back into the games, he can stamp out the seeds of revolution sown by the Girl on Fire.

How to watch: Stream Catching Fire on Peacock or rent/purchase on Amazon Prime Video.

Mockingjay—Part 1 (2014)

Lawrence in The Hunger Games: Mockingjay—Part 1

Having been rescued from the 75th annual Hunger Games by District 13 resistance fighters, Katniss must save Peeta from the hands of the Capitol while living up to her title as a symbol of revolution.

How to watch: Stream Mockingjay—Part 1 on Peacock or rent/purchase on Amazon Prime Video.

Mockingjay—Part 2 (2015)

Banks and Lawrence in The Hunger Games: Mockingjay—Part 2.

Now that a hijacked and traumatized version of Peeta has been rescued, Katniss (a.k.a. the Mockingjay) must take down President Snow to end the war for good.

How to watch: Stream Mockingjay—Part 2 on Peacock or rent/purchase on Amazon Prime Video.

The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes (2023)

Tom Blyth and Rachel Zegler in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes.

The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes takes us back to the 10th annual Hunger Games, years before Coriolanus Snow was President of Panem. At 18 years old, Snow (Tom Blyth) is just a mentor in the games, hoping to lead tribute Lucy Gray Baird (Rachel Zegler) to victory and restore his family name to its previous position of power.

How to watch: Stream The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes on Peacock or rent/purchase on Amazon Prime Video.

Sunrise on the Reaping (2026)

Joseph Zada and Mckenna Grace in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping.

Before he was Katniss and Peeta's alcoholic mentor, Haymitch Abernathy was the golden boy of District 12 and in love with a girl named Lenore Dove Baird (Whitney Peak). But when he's forced to enter the 50th annual Hunger Games against double the amount of tributes, he must decide between killing his way home to her or take down the games from the inside.

How to watch: Sunrise on the Reaping hits theaters on November 20, 2026.
