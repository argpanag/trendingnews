Uganda v Botswana: official line-ups

TheTools · 14.11.2026 15:52 · source · permalink

The two teams have named their starting XIs for matchday 3 of the AFCON 2027 qualifiers. Uganda line up in a 4-3-3, Botswana in a 4-4-2.

The essentials

  • The match: Uganda – Botswana, 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers, matchday 3 of the group stage, kick-off at 16:00 GMT
  • Uganda: 4-3-3 formation, 6 changes from the previous match
  • Botswana: 4-4-2 formation, 9 changes from the previous match

Uganda and Botswana meet this Saturday 14 November 2026 in Hoima, at Hoima City Stadium, for matchday 3 of the group stage in the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers. Kick-off is set for 16:00 GMT.

For Uganda, coach P. Put opts for a 4-3-3 and changes six positions from the friendly on 5 October 2026 against DR Congo (1-1). Denis Onyango, Nathan Asiimwe, Rogers Torach Ochaki, Aziz Kayondo, Bobosi Byaruhanga and J. Bogere come into the starting XI. Up front, Bogere joins A. Vula and Kelvin Boateng in the attacking trio.

Thaloba Nthaga's Botswana are set up in a 4-4-2 and make nine changes to the team from the 28 September 2026 match against Tunisia (2-2, Africa Cup of Nations qualifiers). Alford Velaphi, Tebogo Kopelang, Mosha Gaolaolwe, Chicco Molefe, Losika Ratshukudu, Godiraone Modingwane, Thero Setsile, Tumisang Orebonye and Gilbert Baruti are the new starters. In attack, Orebonye and Baruti form the starting pair.

The two full line-ups, including substitutes, are just below.

Line-up: Uganda (4-3-3)

  • Goalkeeper: Denis Onyango
  • Defence: Nathan Asiimwe, Rogers Torach Ochaki, Toby Humphrey Sibbick, Aziz Kayondo
  • Midfield: Baba Alhassan, Kenneth Semakula, Bobosi Byaruhanga
  • Attack: J. Bogere, A. Vula, Kelvin Boateng
  • Substitutes: David Lukwago, Khalid Aucho, T. B. Majub, Sadam Masereka, Hilary Mukundane, Travis Mutyaba, A. Okello, Kingdom Osayi, Allan Oyirwoth, Frank Ssebufu, Denis Omedi, Herbert Bockhorn
  • Coach: P. Put

Line-up: Botswana (4-4-2)

  • Goalkeeper: Goitseone Phoko
  • Defence: Alford Velaphi, Tebogo Kopelang, Mosha Gaolaolwe, Chicco Molefe
  • Midfield: Losika Ratshukudu, Godiraone Modingwane, Gape Mohutsiwa, Thero Setsile
  • Attack: Tumisang Orebonye, Gilbert Baruti
  • Substitutes: Mbatshi elias, Victor James, Thatayaone Kgamanyane, Thabo Maponda, Kenneth Mmoko, Larona Modisaemang, Kutlwelo Mpolokang, Godfrey Nonko Tauyatswala, Omphile Ramoagi, Amogelang Reetsang, B. Bwire, Lebogang Ditsele
  • Coach: Thaloba Nthaga
