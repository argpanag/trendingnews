Dunkin' to give away Chicago Bears-themed dad hat at Chicago-area locations

TheTools · 16.09.2026 22:49 · source · permalink

CHICAGO (WLS) -- Bear down at Dunkin'!

Customers could score free Chicago Bears gear Thursday while getting an afternoon pick-me-up cup of joe.

People who swing by participating Dunkin' locations across the Chicago area have a chance to get a free Dunkin' x Chicago Bears dad hat with a purchase of a medium or larger beverage.

The giveaway begins at 1 p.m. Thursday, Sept. 17.

Dunkin' is also bringing back Chicago Bears treats "Da Donut" and the new "Da Brew." They're available through Nov. 3.

More information can be found on the Dunkin' website.
