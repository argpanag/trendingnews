A 23-year-old man has died after he received critical injuries from a crash involving a vehicle on Highway 544 Wednesday night.

Part of the highway also was blocked Thursday morning after a bicyclist was struck by a vehicle.

The collision is the third time this week that local first response agencies have responded to a crash on Highway 544.

Horry County Fire Rescue responded to the area of Singleton Ridge Road and Highway 544 in Conway around 9:27 p.m. on Wednesday to reports of a crash.

Jarien Martin, who lived in the area near where the crash happened, died just before 10 p.m. while attempting to cross the roadway near Sweetwater Branch Baptist Church, according to an email from the Horry County Coroner's Office.

South Carolina Highway Patrol is investigating Wednesday's crash with assistance from Coastal Carolina University Public Safety, Conway Police Department and Horry County Police Department.

Horry County Fire Rescue reported Thursday morning that several lanes of traffic in the area of Highway 544 and West Cox Ferry Road were blocked due to a crash incident involving a bicyclist.

One person was transported to the hospital with injuries, according to Horry County Fire Rescue's post.

On Oct. 12, Horry County Police closed down several northbound lanes of the roadway due to a fatal collision involving a motorcycle.

The motorcyclist, 64-year-old Kevin Lawrimore of Bucksport, died just after 11:30 a.m. on Monday at Conway Medical Center. Lawrimore had been riding in the area of Nottingham Lakes Road and Kingswood Drive when he collided with a vehicle, according to an email from the Horry County Coroner's Office.
