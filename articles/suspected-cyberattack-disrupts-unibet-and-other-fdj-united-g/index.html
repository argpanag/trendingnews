Suspected cyberattack disrupts Unibet and other FDJ United gambling platforms across Europe

TheTools · 23.11.2026 17:40 · source · permalink

A suspected cyberattack has disrupted several online gambling platforms operated by FDJ United across Europe, leaving customers unable to access betting websites and, in some cases, customer support services, though it was later confirmed operations were restored. 

The disruption has affected Unibet and its Swedish operation, Unibet Sverige, alongside sister brands Maria Casino, Otto Casino and Storspelare. The full extent of the incident remains unclear, including whether it is confined to FDJ United's operations or a bigger cybersecurity issue.

Customers attempting to access Unibet have encountered a maintenance notice stating: “We are currently performing some maintenance and will be back online shortly.”

The outage has also affected customer support services in the UK, where Unibet's telephone support line was temporarily unavailable. Callers were directed to alternative assistance through a recorded message stating: “Our phone line is currently closed, please go to our online help centre where you can find alternative support.”

However, the operator's online help center is also inaccessible, further limiting customers' ability to obtain assistance during the disruption.

Unibet Sverige acknowledged the outage on November 20, although its responses to customers on X indicate that the Swedish platform had been experiencing problems since approximately 10 p.m. on November 19. The timeline suggests the disruption had continued for at least 16 hours, while one Reddit user reported being unable to access Unibet for 14 hours.

Before identifying the incident as a cyberattack, the operator had described the disruption as a “technical problem.” Responding to a customer who questioned whether other countries were experiencing similar difficulties, Unibet Sverige stated: “Same for all.”

Unibet subsequently confirmed the disruption through its official X account, stating: "Our team is working to restore access safely. Thank you for your patience – we’ll share further updates as soon as we can.”

Following a cyberattack we’ve temporarily taken Unibet offline as a safety precaution. Our team is working to restore access safely. Thank you for your patience — we’ll share further updates as soon as we can.

The incident follows a separate cybersecurity breach involving Optimizely, an artificial intelligence-powered digital experience platform used by marketing and product teams to develop, test and personalize websites and digital products.

Reports emerging days before the Unibet disruption indicated that malicious code had penetrated Optimizely's platform, which is used by major international organizations, including the BBC, CNN and Zoom, as well as gambling companies.

Betsson, Tombola, PokerStars and Betfred previously confirmed that they had been affected by the Optimizely incident. The technology provider subsequently stated that it had closed the vulnerability. No connection between that breach and the latest disruption affecting FDJ United has been established.

The gambling industry has also experienced other recent cybersecurity incidents, including attacks targeting prediction market operator Polymarket. Last month, more than 700 Polymarket users reported unauthorized withdrawals totaling at least $2.7 million following a third-party security breach. The operator had previously suffered another attack in June, which resulted in $3 million in customer refunds.
