Football fans in India will be able to see a select number of European football matches for free in the next season, Real Madrid’s president Florentino Pérez said on Saturday.

Speaking at the club’s general assembly in Valdebebas, Real Madrid president Pérez discussed a pilot project from the UEFA to offer the best continental matches for free via a new platform in India.

“Starting next season, the best matches will be available to watch for free in India, one of the most populous and youthful countries in the world, thanks to a specific platform,” Pérez said.

“Just imagine a 15-year-old in Mumbai who will be able to watch matches for free, whenever he wants, unlike the current situation, where he has to rely on low-quality, pirated websites. For the first time, he will truly realise that his club exists.”

Florentino Pérez unveiled a pilot project to offer the best UEFA competition matches for free via a new platform in India starting next season. | Photo Credit: REUTERS

Sony Sports Network has broadcast and streamed all UEFA competition matches in India since 2017. In 2024, the group renewed the deal until the 2026-27 season for reportedly USD 40 million.

Perez’s announcement means the matches will now be accessible to a larger audience since Sony’s broadcast followed a paid subscription model.

“For UEFA competitions, such as the Champions League ‌or the Europa League, a ​pilot test will be conducted in India,” Real Madrid’s general manager Jose ​Angel Sanchez clarified.

“In ⁠this model, users can access content for free, supported by advertising, or ‌opt for a premium, paid version that is ad-free. However, the initiative in India is strictly a pilot test being run by UEFA. It cannot be applied to ‌Real Madrid’s matches, nor is it ​something that this institution can control.”
