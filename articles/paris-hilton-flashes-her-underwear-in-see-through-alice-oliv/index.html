Paris Hilton Flashes Her Underwear in See-Through Alice + Olivia Dress

TheTools · 12.09.2026 10:22 · source · permalink

Paris Hilton has long been known for her fashion choices, and her latest see-through dress left her underwear on display. She has made bold fashion choices since her early-2000s days, often using her outfits to make a statement. Her latest New York Fashion Week appearance stayed true to that style, with one revealing detail taking center stage.

Paris Hilton’s see-through Alice + Olivia dress leaves her underwear on full display at NYFW show

View this post on Instagram

Paris Hilton attended New York Fashion Week in a sheer black dress from Alice + Olivia. The lace design was fitted through the bodice, making her underwear visible underneath. The dress featured intricate black lace and a long, flowing skirt. The fitted upper half contrasted with the looser silhouette below, giving the look a defined shape.

Hilton paired the dress with black sunglasses and a sparkling silver handbag. The Gucci silver crystal medium bag is worth around $7700. Her long, blonde, straight hair fell loosely over her shoulders, while minimal jewelry kept the focus on the dress.

She shared photos from the occasion on Instagram, writing, “Sliving in Wonderland with @AliceAndOlivia and @ParivieBeauty for #NYFW.” The post showed Hilton posing in the look while highlighting her collaboration with the brands.

The look also continues Hilton’s long-standing affinity for sheer and embellished fashion. She recently wore a crystal-covered Gucci gown with a similarly transparent effect at the Kering Foundation’s Caring for Women Dinner in New York, showing that the see-through aesthetic remains a recurring element of her red-carpet wardrobe.

At the Kering Foundation’s Caring for Women Dinner earlier during New York Fashion Week, Hilton also opted for a crystal-covered Gucci look, pairing a custom gown with a matching crystal shoulder bag. Her latest Alice + Olivia appearance offered a different take on the same high-shine aesthetic, using sheer black lace and sparkling accessories instead.
