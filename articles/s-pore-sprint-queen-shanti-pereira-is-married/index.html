S'pore sprint queen Shanti Pereira is married

TheTools · 21.11.2026 17:17 · source · permalink

Congratulations Queen and King!

November 21, 2026, 04:45 PM

Photo via Tan Zong Yang/Instagram & sohng.jam/Instagram

Singapore's sprint queen Shanti Pereira has officially tied the knot with ex-national sprinter Tan Zong Yang.

In an Instagram story on Nov. 21, Tan shared a sweet photo of himself and Pereira in their wedding attire in a car.

Also in attendance is Olympic sprinter Timothee Yap.

Yap captured the happy occasion and shared it on his Instagram stories.

The pair has been together for five years and got engaged on Jun. 9, 2024, in Sweden, when Pereira was preparing for the 2024 Paris Olympics.
