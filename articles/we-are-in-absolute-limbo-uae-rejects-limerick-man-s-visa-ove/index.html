'We are in absolute limbo': UAE rejects Limerick man's visa over newly updated passport

TheTools · 19.11.2026 14:31 · source · permalink

It took the family two weeks to be told that the issue with the residency visa rejection was that 'the UAE authorities have not been given a specimen of the new style Irish passport'. File picture

An Irish citizen has been left in limbo after his residency visa was rejected over claims that a specimen of the newly launched Irish passport was not given to UAE authorities.

Mark, originally from Limerick, was offered a job in the state and had arrived in the UAE a few weeks ago, with the hopes that his whole family would then emigrate with him from the United Kingdom.

His wife, Laura Vallance, said her husband had renewed his passport in September, before travelling over. He was issued with the new Irish passport, which was launched earlier in June.

After arriving, his residency visa, which was applied for by his employer, was rejected after it was put through the system, and “nobody could work out why". 

“It’s obviously a brand new passport; he had updated it before he left. He had eight months on [the previous one], and we thought it would just be easier to update it here,” Ms Vallance told the Irish Examiner.

She added that it took her family two weeks to be told that the issue with the residency visa rejection was that “the UAE authorities have not been given a specimen of the new style Irish passport”.

“So, according to the UAE authorities and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, his passport doesn’t exist. His passport is completely invalid. “And what he’s been told, and what we can gather, is that this is not the first time this has happened,” Ms Vallance claimed.

She added that the Irish embassy in Abu Dhabi “had given him a letter to give back to the UAE authorities to say this is a valid passport". “But the point is this is something that should have been done,” Ms Vallance said.

“This is a massive oversight on their part because you do not bring a new passport into circulation without sharing it with authorities across the globe. Otherwise, your citizens are clearly going to face issues like this.

“So at the moment, we have got no resolution, no timeframe for a fix,” she added.

We are in absolute limbo.

She said if the matter is not sorted in the next few days, her husband's tourist visa will run out, and he will have to leave the country.

“We cannot progress with our move until this happens. We’ve paid quite a large deposit for a rental property over there; we will lose it if they don’t get this fixed,” she added.

Ms Vallance said if you have a valid passport, “you expect to be able to travel, move freely and be able to apply for other residency visas without issue". 

“You don’t expect to find yourself in a situation where a passport issued by your own Government isn’t recognised by another country because of a diplomatic and administrative failure,” she added.

When asked by the Irish Examiner, the Department of Foreign Affairs said it was “aware of a very small number of citizens who have been affected by the issue". 

“The new Irish passport was launched in June 2026. At that time, the department undertook extensive engagement with national governments worldwide to highlight the new passport design and its security features,” a spokesperson said.

The spokesperson added: "The Embassy of Ireland in Abu Dhabi is engaging directly with these citizens and with the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs. It is expected that this issue will be resolved in the coming days.”
