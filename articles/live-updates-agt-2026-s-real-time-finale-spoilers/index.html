Warning: Spoilers for the AGT 21 Live Finale below!

Key Takeaways

  • America's Got Talent Season 21 concludes with the Live Finale on September 23 at 9 p.m. ET, where the winner of the $1 million grand prize will be announced.
  • The Top 10 finalists include Acro Canine Crew, Come Here, Geno Ploeger, Hundred Fingers, Lai Noelle, Nene Royal, Olivia Befus, Royal Lasers, Unitree, and Veronika Goroshkova.
  • The finale features judges Howie Mandel, Sofía Vergara, Mel B, and guest judge KSI, along with appearances and performances from Lele Pons, Trisha Paytas, Taylen Biggs, Jessica Sanchez, Mat Franco, Josh Groban, Linkin Park, REI AMI, Chloe Flower, and Steve Aoki.

After a whirlwind season filled with Golden Buzzers, Live Golden Buzzers, TikTok Voting, and special guest Judges, Season 21 of America's Got Talent is officially wrapping up.

With summer behind us, AGT will announce its 2026 winner on Wednesday, September 23 during its Live Finale Show, which begins at 9 p.m. ET. Howie Mandel, Sofía Vergara, Mel B, and guest Judge KSI will gather as the Finalists learn who's taking home the $1 million grand prize.

To recap, this year's Top 10 are:

  • Acro Canine Crew
  • Come Here
  • Geno Ploeger
  • Hundred Fingers
  • Lai Noelle
  • Nene Royal
  • Olivia Befus
  • Royal Lasers
  • Unitree
  • Veronika Goroshkova

By the end of the night, Host Terry Crews will reveal the results of America's Vote. However, before the big news, fans should expect some exciting guest appearances. Lele Pons will take over as the night's TikTok host, while Trisha Paytas and Taylen Biggs will be on call as correspondents. There will also be performances from previous AGT winners Jessica Sanchez and Mat Franco, in addition to the likes of Josh Groban, Linkin Park, REI AMI, Chloe Flower, and Steve Aoki.

Acro Canine Crew vs. Geno Ploeger

The Act that took the final spot in the Top 5 was Geno Ploeger. This ended Acro Canine Crew's time on AGT.

Nene Royal vs. Unitree

Nene Royal became the fourth Act to make the Top 5, meaning Unitree was sent home.

Lai Noelle vs. Veronika Goroshkova

America's Vote sent Veronika Goroshkova to the Top 5, while Lai Noelle was eliminated

Hundred Fingers vs. Olivia Befus

When it came time for these two Acts to face-off, Hundred Fingers advanced to the Top 5, while Olivia Befus was sent home.

Terry Crews kicks of the results of AGT 21 with the Top 5

First up were Come Here vs. Royal Lasers, with Crews revealing that...

Royal Lasers advanced to the Top 5, with Come Here eliminated.

An AGT winner returns to the stage

14-year-old Golden Buzzer, Lai Noelle, belted Sienna Spiro's "Die on This Hill" with last year's AGT winner Jessica Sanchez. Noelle then let Sanchez take over for a solo performance of her original single "Beggin."

What rock n' roll dreams are made of

A collaboration only made possible by AGT, 16-year-old Nene Royal dueted "The Emptiness Machine" with Linkin Park's own Emily Armstrong.

Hundred Fingers collaborates with a world renowned composer

Classical pianist Chloe Flower played her own flawless version of Vivaldi's "Winter" as Hundred Fingers worked their (literal) magic on the stage.

A traitor and an Olympian meet two magicians

The Traitors Season 4 Faithful-turned-Traitor, Eric Nam, and Olympic gold medalist hurdler and sprinter ,Sydney Levrone, partook in an unbelievable card trick with Geno Ploeger and Season 9 winner, Matt Franco — who just happens to be Ploeger's idol and mentor.

Josh Groban sings a stunning cover

Aerialist Veronika Goroshkova soared above the stage as Groban sang an amazing cover of Adele's "Skyfall," which the Grammy-winner wrote for the 2012 James Bond film of the same name. 

The dogs take over!

For the first time ever, two dog Acts advanced to the Top 10 of AGT. To celebrate, both Acro Canine Crew and Olivia Befus came together for a delightful performance full of adorable furry friends. TikTok host Lele Pons even got in on the fun at the end of the spectacle.

Taylen Biggs takes on AGT

13-year-old media personality Taylen Biggs arrived at AGT to interview the Top 10, the Judges, and Host Terry Crews, getting them to open up and impressing them with her maturity.

Two finalists join forces

For the second act of the night, Unitree and Royal Lasers teamed up with Steve Aoki for an epic performance of the songs "Closer to God," "Just Hold On," and "Boneless."

The first performance of the night!

Crews introduced the first performance of the night, with finalist Come Here taking the stage with REI AMI to perform the song "2Loud."

The AGT Season 21 Finale begins

Terry Crews kicks off the show by introducing the Top 10 Acts and the Judges: Howie Mandel, Mel B, Sofía Vergara, and guest Judge KSI.
