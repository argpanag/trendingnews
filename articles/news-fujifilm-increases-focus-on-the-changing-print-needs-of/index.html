NEWS: Fujifilm Increases Focus on the Changing Print Needs of UK Schools | Edexec

TheTools · 06.10.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

As schools wrestle with static or declining budgets, a new campaign from Fujifilm highlights how its Apeos multifunction printer range can help schools manage print costs, reduce waste, protect sensitive information and simplify everyday document tasks

In its new “Born Ready” campaign, Fujifilm highlights four value pillars: Smart, Secure, Sustainable and Adaptive, which map directly onto the practical considerations schools face when reviewing and renewing their print technology.

Schools manage a wide range of printing needs, from SEND learning plans, letters, and reports to high-volume GCSE and A-Level exam papers. Print demand may also rise during exams, admissions, and reporting periods.

For school business managers, the purchase price of a multifunction printer is only a small part of the overall cost, as toner, energy use and servicing need to be factored in over time. Schools need to look at where devices will be used, how much work each will handle and what they will cost to run.

Fujifilm is encouraging schools to look at these ongoing costs when reviewing their print provision. Features such as default double-sided printing can reduce paper use, while secure print release can prevent jobs being printed and left uncollected. Energy-saving functions and lower-temperature toner technology can also help reduce power consumption.

Security is equally important when the same device handles pupil, staff, financial and safeguarding information. Schools need to consider UK GDPR and wider data protection requirements, including who can access a device and how confidential documents are printed, scanned and collected. User authentication, encryption and secure print release can help reduce the risk of sensitive information being accessed by the wrong person.

Fujifilm’s Apeos portfolio includes A3 and A4 colour and monochrome multifunction printers, drawing on decades of experience in toner, imaging and workplace technology and available to UK schools through Fujifilm’s partner network.
