Texas' travel to Baton Rouge is questionable. So is one Longhorn on the injury report.

TheTools · 14.11.2026 01:16 · source · permalink

Inside Texas Football

Questionable: Texas getting to Baton Rouge by a reasonable bed time Friday night. Not questionable: Texas defensive tackle Hero Kanu. If Saturday’s game between the No. 6 Longhorns (7-2) and No. 21 LSU (6-3) goes off as scheduled in the face of a nationwide Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)-mandated ground stop, it appears Kanu should be good to go for it.

Kanu was injured in the second half of Texas’ 34-20 win at Missouri last week. He was down on the field for a while and was eventually helped to the sideline by some team trainers. True to his all-for-the-team form, he returned to the field and finished out the game.

After appearing on the injury report as questionable on Wednesday and probable on Thursday, Kanu was also listed as probable on Friday. Reserve offensive lineman Cole Hutson is listed as questionable, as he was on Thursday. It should be inconsequential for Texas whether Hutson is available or not. Kanu is a starter, and his services for a stout Longhorn defensive line are always appreciated — even if he’s not 100 percent.

Same as Wednesday and Thursday, four Texas players are OUT for the LSU matchup; left tackle Trevor Goosby, running back Hollywood Smothers, tight end Nick Townsend and defensive end Richard Wesley. Goosby and Smothers are out for the season. Townsend and Wesley are expected to make returns at some point.

Wide receiver Cam Coleman did not play against Missouri and defensive end Colin Simmons was on a pitch count, but neither of them have been on the availability report all week. They’re good to go for Baton Rouge — whenever it is and however it is Texas gets there.

As for LSU, linebacker Whit Weeks is still questionable. Offensive tackle Weston Davis is probable. The Tigers have four players tabbed as OUT, just like the Longhorns. Here’s the full availability report:
