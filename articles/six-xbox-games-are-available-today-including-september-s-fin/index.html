Six Xbox Games Are Available Today, Including September's Final Game Pass Addition (September 29)

TheTools · 29.09.2026 10:00 · source · permalink

Wind's howling

We're in the final days of September, but before we get ahead of ourselves with all the big October releases, let's get stuck into your latest daily fix of all the new games launching on Xbox today.

Today, players can return to Kaer Morhen with a fresh-faced White Wolf in The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Remastered! I can't tell you how excited I've been for this revamped beauty to hit full release. Oh, and If you own a copy of the game, then this update is free to you!

Joining grumpy boy Geralt is September's final Xbox Game Pass addition, Minecraft Dungeons 2. We played a bit of it this weekend at Xbox FanFest in London and thought it was pretty darned great.

So, here's what launching on Xbox today (September 29):

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt - Remastered - (Series X|S, PC, Cloud)

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Remastered brings an array of improvements and modernizations to visuals, gameplay, character progression, and much more. Remastered also includes the acclaimed Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine expansions.
