Nexon has officially released "Azur Promilia," a new product of Manju Games that developed the popular subculture game "Byeokram Pass." Azur Promilia is a new work that Nexon, which confirmed its possibility with "Blue Archive," has ambitiously prepared to expand the subculture field.

Nexon announced on the 26th that it has officially released Azur Promilia, a new fantasy world role-playing game (RPG) developed by Manju Games, on PC and mobile in Korea.

Azur Promilia is a fantasy world RPG set in Promilia, a fantasy continent where the mysterious creature "Kibo" lives.

It is characterized by combining monster capture and fostering content with the character collection and action battle of existing subculture open world games. Users can capture kibos directly from all over the continent and use them for combat and exploration, and they can also secure resources by deploying them in production facilities.

Users can become "adults" who have lost their memories, venture around the continent with their charming fellow characters and kibo, and enjoy high-freedom content such as combat, construction, and life.

It also tried to differentiate itself in the probability-based item system. In the process of selecting a limited character, the so-called "pick-piercing" in which other characters of the highest grade appear instead was eliminated. Pickup characters can be definitively obtained within a maximum of 90 picks.

Azur Promilia is attracting attention as a subculture anticipated work, surpassing 30 million global pre-registration prior to its launch. Since the pre-download, which began on the 24th, it has also ranked first in the popularity rankings of the App Store and Galaxy Store.

Nexon will participate in the "AGF 2026" held at KINTEX in Goyang, Gyeonggi-do from the 4th to the 6th of next month and operate the Azure Promilia booth.

Choi Sung-wook, head of Nexon's publishing live division, said, "We are happy to officially introduce Azur Promilia to domestic users. We will do our best to make every moment in the Promilia continent a pleasant adventure."
