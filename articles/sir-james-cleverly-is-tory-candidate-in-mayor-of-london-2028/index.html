Sir James Cleverly is Tory candidate in mayor of London 2028 race

TheTools · 19.11.2026 11:28 · source · permalink

Sir James Cleverly represented the Conservatives on the London Assembly before he became an MP in 2015

  • Published
    48 minutes ago

Former cabinet minister Sir James Cleverly has been selected as the Conservative candidate for mayor of London.

The MP for Braintree in Essex was chosen in a poll of party members in London - the next mayoral election will be in May 2028.

A former home secretary and foreign secretary, Sir James saw off a challenge from Westminster City councillor Festus Akinbusoye and Richmond Park parliamentary candidate Sara Gezdari.

Sir James, a former head of the London Fire Authority, was widely expected to win the nomination after he stood down from the shadow cabinet in August.

Labour incumbent Lord Khan is yet to confirm whether he will seek an unprecedented fourth term.

'New sheriff'

Sir James, an Army reservist, represented the Tories on the London Assembly before his election as MP for Braintree in 2015.

After rising through the backbenches, he unsuccessfully ran for Conservative Party leader in 2024, losing out to Kemi Badenoch.

The only other confirmed party choice for the mayoral race is Laila Cunningham from Reform UK, whose candidacy was announced in January.

A former Tory councillor in Westminster, she said at the press conference announcing her selection: "There will be a new sheriff in town, and I'll be launching an all-out war on crime."

Sarah Olney and Rob Blackie are both candidates for the Liberal Democrats' nomination.

Should Lord Khan decide not to run again, there are a number of London Labour figures with ambitions to be the next mayor, including MPs Dawn Butler and Rosena Allin-Khan.

The Green Party, which won control of three London boroughs at the local elections in May, has yet to begin the process of picking a candidate for the 2028 mayoral race.
