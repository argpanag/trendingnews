Capital Humane Society offers half-off cat adoptions through Nov. 29

TheTools · 10.11.2026 17:11 · source · permalink

LINCOLN, Neb. (KOLN) - The Capital Humane Society is offering reduced adoption fees for cats five months and older as part of a special promotion honoring the memory of Gary Reiners.

The promotion runs through Sunday, Nov. 29, at the Pieloch Pet Adoption Center, located at 6500 S 70th St in Lincoln.

During the promotion, adoption fees for all eligible cats will be reduced by 50%. License and rabies fees may still apply.

The humane society says many cats are currently waiting to find permanent homes and hopes the promotion will help more of them get adopted.

Hours at the Pieloch Pet Adoption Center are:

  • Monday: Closed
  • Tuesday-Wednesday: 11:30 a.m. to 7 p.m.
  • Thursday-Friday: 11:30 a.m. to 7 p.m.
  • Saturday-Sunday: Noon to 5 p.m.

For more information about available cats and adoption, visit the Capital Humane Society’s website.
