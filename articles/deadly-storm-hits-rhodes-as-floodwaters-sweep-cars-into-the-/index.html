Deadly Storm Hits Rhodes as Floodwaters Sweep Cars Into the Sea - tovima.com

TheTools · 14.10.2026 11:55 · source · permalink

A 25-year-old man died in Rhodes after floodwaters swept his car into the sea at Stegna, as a severe storm batters the island and much of Greece.

A 25-year-old man died in Rhodes after floodwaters swept his car into the sea at Stegna, as a severe storm batters the island and much of Greece.

The severe weather that struck Rhodes on Wednesday, October 14, has turned deadly, with a man found dead in the Stegna area of Archangelos, according to local authorities. He was discovered unconscious on the shoreline, and his identity has not yet been confirmed.

According to the Athens-Macedonian News Agency (ANA-MPA), the 25-year-old was recovered by Hellenic Coast Guard personnel after the car he was in was swept into the sea at Stegna beach in Archangelos. The same sources say four vehicles in total were carried into the water. The young man, who was inside one of them, was unable to get out of the car in time. Severe weather continues across the island, causing serious problems.

Since the early hours of the morning, the storm has severely disrupted the island’s road network and transportation. Civil Protection teams from the Municipality of Rhodes and the South Aegean Region have been fully mobilized, responding to incidents at multiple locations.

Earlier, residents received a 112 emergency alert urging them to limit travel and follow the instructions of the authorities. Rhodes Mayor Alexandros Koliadis issued a similar appeal shortly afterward.

Torrential rain has turned roads into rivers from Ialysos to the town of Rhodes, as well as in Afantou, Faliraki and Archangelos, leaving dozens of vehicles stranded. In Archangelos, an unoccupied car was swept away by a raging torrent.

The storm is affecting much of the country, putting local authorities on high alert and causing serious problems in several regions. Wednesday is expected to be the worst day of the bad weather, with flooding, intense thunderstorms, localized hail and gale-force winds reported in many areas.

In northern Evia, the situation is described as critical, as heavy rainfall has caused flooding and the Kireas River to overflow its banks. Sections of the road network have been closed to traffic. Earlier, a 112 alert ordered the evacuation of the Makrykapa area in the regional unit of Evia, directing residents to move toward Psachna due to the overflowing river.

Rhodes Mayor Alexandros Koliadis Requests State of Emergency Declaration

Rhodes Mayor Alexandros Koliadis submitted a request, to the Ministry of Climate Crisis and Civil Protection, to declare the island a state of emergency

The request has been approved, with an official decision expected shortly, given the conditions on the island.

Earlier, the mayor took to social media urging citizens in light of the extreme weather conditions to:

  • Avoid all unnecessary travel while the severe weather persists.
  • Do not attempt to cross flooded roads or closed-off areas, either by vehicle or on foot.
  • Move vehicles away from streams and low-lying areas.
  • Follow only official announcements from Civil Protection and the Municipality.

He also added that “Our services are on full alert across the island and are responding wherever necessary. In an emergency, call 112.”
