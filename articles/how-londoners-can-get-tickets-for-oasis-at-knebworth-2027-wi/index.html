How Londoners can get tickets for Oasis at Knebworth 2027 (with new dates)

TheTools · 26.09.2026 12:47 · source · permalink

Tickets for the venue near London are now live. Here’s everything you need to know about getting tickets, including camping options and added dates

Tickets for Oasis’s 2027 reunion tour are now on sale. Pre-registered punters will be able to buy tickets this weekend (September 25-27) in specific timed slots. 

While there are no shows at Wembley this time around (in summer 2025 the Gallaghers played seven shows in northwest London), Londoners won’t have to trek all the way up to Glasgow or Manchester to see the band next summer. The ‘Wonderwall’ legends are making a grand return to Knebworth, which is a very short distance from London. After the initial announcement, the band added more dates. The band are in Hertfordshire for six dates, triple the number of their iconic 1996 run. Richard Ashcroft has been confirmed on support.

Knebworth has a special place in Oasis lore. In August 1996 the band headlined two days of music in the grounds of the Hertfordshire country estate. A whopping 125,000 people attended each night, with support from Charlatans, the Prodigy, Manic Street Preachers, the Chemical Brothers, Ocean Colour Scene and more.

Oasis’ 2027 Knebworth gigs won’t just be special because of the location. The gigs will also mark the first time that Knebworth has had a ‘camping village’, while Great British Railways will provide late-night trains to London King’s Cross until 1.30am each night. Another first will see the introduction of grandstand hospitality seating and the venue’s biggest accessible viewing platform ever. 

Keen to get tickets to see Oasis at Knebworth in September 2027? Here’s everything you need to know, from when tickets go on sale to transport arrangements.

Oasis Knebworth 2027 tour dates

The ‘Don’t Look Back in Anger’ icons are in Hertfordshire on the following dates:

  • September 11 2027
  • September 12 2027
  • September 18 2027
  • September 19 2027
  • September 25 2027 (new date)
  • September 26 2027 (new date)

It’s worth noting that there was no new registration process for the new dates. You’ll still need to have registered before the deadline last Thursday September 17.

When do Oasis Knebworth tickets go on sale?

Tickets for Oasis’ grand return to Knebworth have started to go on sale. Sales go live at staggered times on the following dates:

  • Friday September 25
  • Saturday September 26
  • Sunday September 27

Here are the time slots for buying tickets

  • Friday September 25 – 9am to 12pm, 1pm-3pm, 5pm-8pm
  • Saturday September 26 – 9am to 12pm, 1pm-3pm, 5pm-8pm
  • Sunday September 27 – 9am to 12pm, 1pm-3pm, 5pm-8pm

Oasis Knebworth 2027 ticket prices

Here’s the pricing breakdown for the Knebworth dates:

  • General admission – £170.50
  • Front standing – £203.50
  • Coach packages – £239.50, £249.50, £259.50, £272.50, £282.50 and £292.50
  • VIP packages – £317.50, £508.50 and £513.50

All prices include booking fees.

You can find a full breakdown of ticket prices for all UK dates, including VIP, coach and hotel packages, here.

How to get to Knebworth from London

There will be a few ways for Londoners to get to the Hertfordshire venue for the Oasis gigs:

Train

Knebworth Estate is just 30 miles north of London and takes just 20 minutes to get to by train from King’s Cross.

On Oasis gig days, two hours of late-night trains will also run between Stevenage and King’s Cross (until 1.30am – the first time this has been done for concerts at Knebworth). Stevenage is just under an hour’s walk from Knebworth. 

Train tickets will be capped at £10 for concert-goers. 

Coach

As indicated in the ticket prices above, you’ll also be able to get coaches to and from Knebworth. Operated by Big Green Coach, ticket-buyers can pick from 124 pick-up points across England and Wales.

Car

Ticketmaster encourages Oasis fans not to drive to the event. However, there will be parking available – these passes will go on sale in the coming weeks.

Camping at Knebworth

Camping will only be available for the Oasis shows on Saturdays September 11, 18 and 25. Fans who are successful in getting tickets for these dates will have the option to add a ‘Campsite Village’ ticket for £60. 

Campers will be allowed to arrive the day before the gig and the ticket includes car parking. 

Oasis Knebworth age restrictions and rules

Oasis’ Knebworth shows will be strictly 14+. All under-16s will have to be accompanied by an adult aged 18 or over.
