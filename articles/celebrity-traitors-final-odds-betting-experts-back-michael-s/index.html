Acaster Confesses As The Traitors Lose Another Member

The penultimate episode of Celebrity Traitors delivered one of the most dramatic round tables of the series, and betting experts have reshaped their market ahead of the final. 

Michael Sheen is now the 1/2 favourite to be among the winners, an implied 66.7% chance. The night belonged to James Acaster, though, for all the wrong reasons. 

41-year-old comedian Acaster came under pressure during a discussion about Ross Kemp’s murder in plain sight, struggled to defend himself, and then admitted to the group that he was a Traitor.

The key moment came when Sheen pointed out that Acaster was the only one who had not left the library during Kemp’s murder. From there, the comedian’s fate was sealed, and he became the second Traitor to be banished from the castle, after Hannah earlier in the series.

Sheen Leads As The Faithful Close In

Sheen did not have it all his own way. James Blunt described him as the “father” of the group, claiming he had been responsible for the Faithfuls being “guided in the wrong direction”, but the actor came through the round table with his reputation intact.

Romesh Ranganathan is next at 6/4. The comedian put Blunt under suspicion during the discussion, and Blunt hit back by suggesting the group should be “looking at the quieter ones”, naming Acaster and Maya Jama. 

Sheen added that the pair had “never had any heat”, and within minutes Acaster had cracked.

Leigh-Anne Pinnock is 2/1 despite some suspicion coming her way, while Sebastian Croft is 9/4. Blunt, who found himself at the centre of several arguments, is the outsider of the seven at 5/1.

Can Maya And King Kenny Survive The Final?

The most fascinating part of the market is the gap between the two remaining Traitors. King Kenny, who was recruited to the turret after Hannah’s banishment, is 2/1, but Maya Jama has drifted out to 9/2.

That is a dramatic change for Maya, who led the betting at 6/4 when Kenny was recruited.

Sheen will be all out for Maya and Kenny in the final and it may well end up being a coin toss as to who wins.
