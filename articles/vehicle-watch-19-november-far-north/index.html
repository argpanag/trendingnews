Vehicle Watch, 19 November - Far North

TheTools · 18.11.2026 21:30 · source · permalink

Senior Constable Heidi Marek on Nov 19, 2026 at 7:30am

Police are seeking public assistance to help locate a number of vehicles stolen across the Far North in the past 28 days.

Now available – Active Stolen Vehicle Public portal

Vehicles Stolen this month:  35

Vehicles Stolen this year:  768

Vehicles stolen in 2025: 808

Tips you can take to help prevent vehicle theft;

  • Remove all valuables from your vehicle
  • Ensure your vehicle is locked
  • Do not leave spare keys in your car
  • Ensure your keys are secured in a safe place out of plain view
  • Ensure that your home is secure
