Etihad Airways will change the aircraft type operating its Tokyo/Narita–Abu Dhabi route.

The route is currently operated with an Airbus A380. From December 15 through March 27, 2027 (the following day for departures from Tokyo/Narita), it will be operated with an Airbus A350-1000. The airline is expected to revert to the Airbus A380 from the following day onward. The Airbus A380 has been deployed on the route since June 16.

In conjunction with this change, Airbus A380 aircraft will be deployed on the Abu Dhabi–Singapore route from December 16.
