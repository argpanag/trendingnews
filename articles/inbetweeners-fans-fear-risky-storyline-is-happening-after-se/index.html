Inbetweeners fans fear 'risky' storyline is happening after seeing set photos

TheTools · 03.11.2026 10:35 · source · permalink

It’s a day ending in ‘Y’ so you know what that means? There are new set photos from The Inbetweeners 3.

But before you read this piece, be warned: you may want to bring your wellies because we’re going to be up to our knees in spoilers.

Still, here? Good stuff.

Right, the new photos don’t appear all that special at first glance.

They show Simon (James Thomas) and Will (Simon Bird) getting off a boat and walking along a beach in Magaluf (Or as Simon’s dad would call it, Shagaluf… and hard).

Yet there’s a third actor who’s been snapped filming with the boys, and it’s that person who’s got fans talking.

The actor in question is none other than Tamla Kari, who played Lucy in the first and second Inbetweeners movies.

Lucy, of course, seemed like the perfect partner for Simon, but she was later revealed in The Inbetweeners 2 to be cheating on him with his university pal, Pete.

Lucy ultimately dumped Simon (Yessssssssss f****** brilliant!), so the question is: what is she doing in this new film and why’s she wearing an outfit from the first movie?

Well, the current theory is that this may be a flashback to the events of The Inbetweeners 1 that we didn’t see.

One user on Reddit wrote: ‘In one of the photos it looks like Lucy is back, which to me confirms this must be a flashback.’

‘I’m genuinely confused about the true plot of this film?’ asked ElSenorOwl. ‘Is Tamla Kari reprising her role as Lucy?’

‘So are we seeing flashbacks to the series and the other movies? I mean I’m certainly intrigued, but they’re gonna look weirdly old in these scenes,’ added Dial-Appreciator.

‘A flashback to the first film is risky imo,’ wrote KezzaJones.

‘Appreciate the lads haven’t aged horrendously, but I think it’ll be pretty damn clear they aren’t the same age as they were in 2010. I hope I’m wrong.’

Still, Kezza had an alternate theory. They said these pics could be from Simon’s stag do.

‘This suggests to me they’re on a stag and the film is them acting like teenagers again, sadly,’ they added.
