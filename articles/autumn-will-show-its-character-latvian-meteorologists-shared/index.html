Autumn will show its character: Latvian meteorologists shared the weather forecast for Sunday

TheTools · 10.10.2026 14:33 · source · permalink

The air temperature during the day will not exceed +14 degrees, predicts the Latvian Environment, Geology and Meteorology Centre (LVĢMC). On the night of Sunday, the sky over Latvia will remain mostly cloudy. Rain will occur at times over most of the country. A weak to moderate wind from the south is expected. Early in the night, gusts of south-western and western winds along the coast may still reach 15 meters per second. In some places, mist will form, and the air temperature will drop to +6…+11 degrees. On Sunday, no significant changes in the weather are expected. The sky will remain mostly overcast, with periodic rain over most of the country, and heavy downpours possible in some areas. The wind will be weak to moderate, predominantly from the south. During the day, the air will warm up to +11…+14 degrees. **What weather is expected in Riga?** In the capital, the night of Sunday will be cloudy. Light rain is possible in the morning hours. The air temperature will be +9…+10 degrees. During the day, Riga will remain predominantly cloudy, with brief rain expected. A weak to moderate southern wind will blow. Thermometer readings will rise to +12…+13 degrees.
