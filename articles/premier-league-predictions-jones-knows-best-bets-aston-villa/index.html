This could be a very fun game involving two managers who like to play fast, vertical football. Anthony Elanga looks a different player under Matthias Jaissle playing this style. His confidence is sky high and when he's in full flow he is such a weapon for a team that like to play direct balls - as seen by his goals against Liverpool and Spurs.

He's not always reliable in front of goal but is capable of these types of goalscoring streaks, so the 100/30 with Sky Bet on him to score again has potential.

They created the most shots (15) and touches in the opposition box (31) any away team at Elland Road has produced in the opening 60 minutes since the start of last season.

Yankuba Minteh, Jack Hinshelwood, Kaoru Mitoma, Georginio Rutter and Mats Wieffer all could be missing for Brighton. It's why they've been weak in the betting in the build-up to this one.

Since Daniel Farke switched to a back three in December, Leeds have been very comfortable defending away from home and taking a very conservative approach to first halves.

Not many goals are being scored to the extent that five of their last six away games have been 0-0 at half time. The 7/4 with Sky Bet for another 0-0 at the break is a runner.

Fulham vs Crystal Palace, Saturday 3pm

There is lots of volatility from a prediction point of view for this encounter which has become a big match for both managers as they look to get their campaigns moving in the right direction.

Palace have lost the defensive identity that made them so successful under Oliver Glasner, while the attacking improvements have not yet materialised consistently. Fulham fans are already frustrated with Alvaro Arbeola after their timid showing at Sunderland. A low-scoring draw it is.

SCORE PREDICTION: 1-1

Manchester City vs Coventry, Saturday 3pm

Manchester City were like clockwork in these types of spots under Pep Guardiola. Normally victories over newly-promoted teams are achieved with minimal fuss, meaning those looking to add a City win to their weekend accumulators shouldn't have too many sweaty moments.

Rayan Cherki at 5/6 with Sky Bet for another assist will be popular. The French maestro is averaging 0.45 assists per 90 since the start of last season - a ratio hotter than Bruno Fernandes. Nottingham Forest vs Tottenham, Saturday 3pm

It must be so stressful being a Spurs fan. Seriously, how can they be this bad still after spending so much this summer?

They were more competitive last weekend in the defeat to Newcastle but they were so easy to score against and there is still a feeling that the players are weighed down by past failures.

Tottenham have attempted an enormous tactical and personnel reset whilst Forest do have more continuity and have shown signs of implementing Glasner's style. They were more like a Glasner team at Anfield, secure and organised without the ball but also so dangerous to stop when they decided to press forward almost as a front five with the full-backs pushing on.

There is an expectation that Morgan Gibbs-White's output is going to grow under this manager and he got off the mark at Anfield. He's had at least a shot on target in his last four starts against Tottenham and averages 1.05 per game since the start of last season. Combining him to have a shot on target and Forest to avoid defeat rates as a decent wager at 10/11 with Sky Bet.
