These are the 7 women's trends you're about to see everywhere this winter, according to style experts

TheTools · 21.11.2026 17:17 · source · permalink

From eye-catching outerwear to faux fur and rich chocolate brown, here's your stylist-approved guide to spicing up your cold-weather wardrobe.

The temps may be falling, but your style game doesn't have to. Winter is prime time to have a little fun with fashion. Think cozy layers, statement-making outerwear, stylish boots and outfits so chic they make braving the cold worth it. To pinpoint which styles are about to be everywhere and what's actually worth adding to your cart, we tapped a team of stylists to break down the top trends shaping women's style this season. 

From luxe cashmere sweaters to eye-catching outerwear and accessories with character, consider this your insider roadmap for the months ahead. And of course, we rounded up the budget-friendly pieces that will bring each trend to life. 

Cozy cashmere

Cashmere is a winter staple that never really goes out of style. "I always reach for soft cashmere in the winter," says stylist Tara West. "It's what I wear when I want to feel cozy and still look pulled together. A relaxed cashmere crewneck with straight-leg jeans and a great belt is one of my favorite combinations."

"Cashmere and luxurious knitwear are winter staples, but this season is all about polished silhouettes and intentional layering," adds personal stylist and wardrobe consultant Laura Koch. "Think oversized turtlenecks, sculptural cardigans and matching knit sets. I always encourage clients to invest in quality knitwear (particularly neutrals) because these are pieces they'll reach for year after year. The key is choosing timeless fabrics with modern proportions." And no matter which silhouette you choose, you'll be happy you have it every time the temperature drops.

Eye-catching outerwear

Your coat deserves just as much consideration as everything underneath it. It's "the first thing people see in winter, so have a little fun with it," West says. "A bold color, a rich texture or an oversized collar can make even jeans and a sweater feel like a whole look." Koch agrees, telling us that if you're going to invest in one piece this winter, make it a great coat. "A statement coat can instantly elevate even the simplest combination of jeans and a sweater. It's also one of the easiest ways to make your winter wardrobe feel refreshed without buying an entirely new wardrobe." 

The best part of a great coat is that it can hide what's underneath. No one will ever guess that you're rocking comfy sweats underneath a stylish floor-length topper. "I am very excited about statement coats this winter!" says fashion stylist and personal shopper Zoë Hennessey. "I still love my oversized trench, but even living in L.A., I have a truly ridiculous coat obsession, and I am very ready for a coat that can basically be the outfit."

Chocolate brown 

Move over black: There's a new classic hue. "Chocolate brown is quickly becoming the new neutral, and I expect it to dominate winter wardrobes. It's softer than black but equally versatile," says Koch. Mix and match it with other seasonal colors like cream, camel, burgundy or navy, or go all in on the trend and rock a head-to-toe brown look.

You can also have a little fun with your color combos. "We're also seeing a huge tonal shift to pairing with more fun colors like royal purple and red. Tonal dressing in varying shades of brown creates an incredibly sophisticated look without requiring much effort," Koch explains.

Faux fur

While it's hardly a groundbreaking winter trend, faux fur is showing up in some unexpected places this season. "I like that not just coats have fur and faux fur," says fashion stylist Yana Echko. "We have faux fur collars and cuffs and scarves and bags and even shoes. I like the idea of just one beautiful furry thing on a simple wool coat or leather jacket more than fur everywhere. It's more interesting that way."

Of course we still love a full-on faux fur coat, but if you're not ready to commit, we found some styles that add just a touch of texture to your look.

Slouchy boots 

This winter's coolest boots are loosening up as slouchy styles step back into the spotlight. "A slouchy boot softens a tailored outfit and gives it a little boho edge. I love pairing one with a straight midi skirt that hits just high enough to show off the folds in the leather. That's what makes the outfit feel current," West says.

Hennessey is also a fan. "I am really excited about slouchy boots! They have been trying to come back for a few seasons, and I feel like maybe this is finally the year. I always think of Isabel Marant or even the '80s when I see them. I love them with skirts, but I also think they look so cool with pants tucked in, whether that is a skinny pant or even something with a little more volume."

Purple

If you prefer more color in your wardrobe than the usual winter neutrals, you're in luck! Purple is taking the throne. From rich plum and deep wine to soft lilac, there's a shade for every outfit and mood.

"I've been talking about purple for a while now. And purple is definitely not going anywhere this winter," says Echko. "I'm so glad to see everyone trying it out. I love the combo of purple and chocolate brown. But I also love purple with olive green or red. You don't have to be head to toe purple. Just something like a sweater, a bag or a pair of shoes can make a difference to spice up your look."

Accessories with character

Sometimes it's the little things that make an outfit feel like an outfit. This winter, accessories are getting a personality of their own with bright colors, oversized proportions and playful details taking center stage. Instead of treating them like an afterthought, stylist Marlene Anzaldua suggests thinking of these finishing touches as the main event. 

"Try accessories with main-character energy," she tells us, recommending giant scarves, faux fur collars, unusual hats, patterned tights and statement socks to "make the look."
