Heavy rainfall likely over Andhra Pradesh, Coastal Karnataka, Kerala and Mahe

The India Meteorological Department has forecast heavy to very heavy rainfall over South Interior Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Karaikal today. Heavy rainfall is also expected over Andhra Pradesh, Coastal Karnataka, Kerala and Mahe. The weather agency has predicted thunderstorm accompanied with lightning very likely at isolated places over Chhattisgarh, Coastal Karnataka, Goa, Madhya Pradesh, Marathwada and Odisha today.
