Watch On TV: CBS

Seahawks vs. Los Angeles Chargers will be carried on CBS. Andrew Catalon will call the play-by-play while Logan Ryan provides color analysis with AJ Ross reporting from the sidelines.

Listen On Radio: Seattle Sports 710AM, KIRO Newsradio 97.3 FM, SiriusXM (channel 83 or 193) Compass Media Networks and Seahawks Mobile App

On the radio, you can hear the Seahawks on Seattle Sports 710AM, KIRO Newsradio 97.3 FM and in the Seahawks Mobile App. Steve Raible and Dave Wyman will call the action with Jen Mueller as the sideline reporter.

The game will be broadcasted nationally on Compass Media Networks, with Troy Clardy calling the play-by-play and Brian Baldinger providing color analysis.

Listen Online: Seahawks Mobile App

For fans in the local Seattle market and international fans in Canada, Germany, Switzerland, Austria, Australia and New Zealand, the game will be available to watch on mobile devices via the Seahawks Mobile App and Seahawks.com.
