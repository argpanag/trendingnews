Euromillions Jackpot Hits 130 Million Euros for Draw

TheTools · 25.09.2026 19:42 · source · permalink

A massive jackpot of 130 million euros is up for grabs in the Euromillones lottery draw on Friday, September 25, 2026. Millions of players in participating European nations are competing for the top prize alongside secondary regional draws such as Spain's El Millón, according to official reports.

Official operations, ticket marketing, and prize distributions within Spain are managed by the State Society Lotteries and Gambling (SELAE). Operating as a public company under the Ministry of Finance, the organization regulates both physical vendor networks and digital sales channels pursuant to state gaming legislation under Law 13/2011.

The current draw follows a separate lottery held on Tuesday, September 22, which offered a 32 million pound jackpot with tickets priced at 2.50 pounds. During that earlier draw, secondary tier payouts reached 130,554 pounds for matching five main numbers and one lucky star, while players matching five main numbers received 13,561 pounds.

Winners must claim their cash prizes within designated expiration timeframes set by official regulations. For games overseen by SELAE, the claim period is typically three months from the draw date.

Additionally, SELAE enforces strict identity verification measures and anti-money laundering controls across all online platforms and physical points of sale for large prize distributions.
