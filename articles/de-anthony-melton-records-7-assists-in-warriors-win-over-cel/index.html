In the Warriors' 88-87 win against the Celtics, De'Anthony Melton played 27 minutes, contributing 7 assists, 2 steals, 3 rebounds, 2 points on 0-7 shooting from the field, including 0-5 from three-point range, and making 2-2 free throws with 2 personal fouls and 2 turnovers.

Insight

Melton recorded 7 assists in 27 minutes, significantly surpassing his season average of 2.7 assists per game. He also contributed 2 steals and 3 rebounds while scoring 2 points on 0-7 shooting from the field. Despite the shooting struggles, his passing and defensive contributions were notable in the team's 88-87 win against the Celtics.
