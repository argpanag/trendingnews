Marvel to Air Exclusive 'Avengers: Doomsday' Preview During World Series Game 1 - WDWNT

TheTools · 22.10.2026 18:13 · source · permalink

An exclusive preview of Marvel’s upcoming film, Avengers: Doomsday, will be shown during Game 1 of the World Series.

‘Avengers: Doomsday’ Preview During World Series Game 1

MLB and Marvel made the announcement via an Instagram post on Thursday, October 22. In the video, the backs of players from different baseball teams—Bryce Harper, Jacob Misiorowski, Freddie Freeman, Randy Arozarena, and Junior Caminero—are seen hanging out on the baseball field when the sky turns green and the Avengers logo appears.

The video then cuts to a text screen explaining that a special look will be shown during the game, which will air on Friday, October 23, 2026, at 8:00 p.m. ET on FOX. The game is between the Tampa Bay Rays and the Milwaukee Brewers.

‘Avengers: Doomsday’

Anthony and Joe Russo, who previously directed Captain America: The Winter Soldier, Captain America: Civil War, Avengers: Infinity War, and Avengers: Endgame, return to direct the film. Marvel Studios President Kevin Feige is among the film’s producers. The runtime for Avengers: Doomsday is 165 minutes, or two hours and 45 minutes.

The film starring Chris Evans, Pedro Pascal, Florence Pugh, and more releases in theaters on December 18, 2026. The movie will be followed by Avengers: Secret Wars, currently scheduled for December 17, 2027.

Last month, we reported that Avengers: Doomsday crossed $40 million in domestic pre-sales.
