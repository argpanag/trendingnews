FIRST ALERT| Chris Bailey looks for some heat relief

TheTools · 16.09.2026 18:29 · source · permalink

LEXINGTON, Ky. (WKYT) - The heat is back in the Bluegrass State as summer just refuses to go quietly into the night. This hot pattern may take a punch from a cold front on Friday with a bigger punch arriving early next week.

Temps today reach the 90-95 degree range across central and eastern Kentucky with mid and upper 90s in the west. Humidity levels make it feel hotter than that, especially in the west.

The chance for a storm or two is there today as some moisture pushes in. This is ahead of a cold front dropping in from the northwest late Thursday into Friday. The models are pushing this boundary much farther south now, bringing the potential for a few showers and storms and much cooler air across central and eastern Kentucky.

The heat attempts to fight back to the northeast, but a few showers and storms cut it off at the pass Saturday and Sunday.

Temps still run above normal into the first half of next week, but nothing like right now. Several systems dropping in from the northwest will team up with some tropical moisture streaming northward to create showers and storms into much of next week.
