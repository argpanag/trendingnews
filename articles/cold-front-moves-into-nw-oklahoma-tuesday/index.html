Cold front moves into NW Oklahoma Tuesday

OKLAHOMA CITY (KOKH) — Temperatures were again able to reach into the triple digits Monday afternoon. This would mark the 43rd day this year where Oklahoma City was able to get into the 100s! This year is now tied with 1936 for 4th most on record!

The ongoing heat and drought have taken its toll on the land. We have the Gracemont Fire near Binger in Caddo and Canadian Counties still burning from Sunday. At last check, it has burned 3,000 acres and is 0% contained. We also have a newer fire east of Roll in Roger Mills County named the Black Kettle Fire. It has formed quickly due to the strong winds. Then on the water front, we are still expecting the 16,000-acre-foot water release from Canton Lake to help replenish Lake Hefner in Oklahoma City. This water release should begin on Thursday.

The heat dome will continue to make for a hot week this week with more 90s and even 100s. The cold front we are tracking will shift into northwest Oklahoma Tuesday, providing that part of the state with the chance for storms, but it lifts back north by Wednesday and stays north of the area until the weekend when it finally looks to pass through. When it does, it will bring in rain and help to drop our temperatures.

TUESDAY: Mostly sunny with a high of 101. Winds will be out of the southwest at 10 to 20 mph. Northwest Oklahoma is under a Marginal Risk for severe weather due to the chance some storms along the cold front produce winds up to 70 mph. These storms will be isolated, so most will remain dry, but the northern part of the state could see some beneficial rain with these storms.

WEDNESDAY: That cold front lifts back north, making all of us dry again with highs still near 100. Winds will be light out of the south at 5 to 10 mph.

80s will finally arrive by the weekend when our cold front finally pushes through the state. Rainfall and storm chances look to occur Saturday and Sunday and should give all corners of the state an equal chance. While you may want to watch the radar for any outdoor plans, it's so nice to at least be talking about the chance for rain!

Even after this front, temperatures will likely remain above normal and chances for rain going into the end of the month may be a little hard to come by.
