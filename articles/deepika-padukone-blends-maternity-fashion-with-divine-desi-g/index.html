Deepika Padukone blends maternity fashion with divine desi glam at Siddhivinayak in a colourful custom Mayyur Girotra couture

TheTools · 19.09.2026 13:48 · source · permalink

Every time Deepika Padukone slips into traditional Indian attire, it's never less than spectacular. The diva made a rare public appearance in Mumbai and stepped out to seek blessings at the Siddhivinayak Temple, where she was accompanied by her husband, Ranveer Singh, and looked pretty amazing in a custom Mayyur Girotra Couture number, which came in rich colours and exquisite tailoring, speaking volumes about the designer's work.

The ensemble featured intricately crafted decorative elements in the shade of orange and purple hues with a touch of earthy appeal, which sat perfectly with the golden thread shades and muted heritage colours. The traditional silhouette in the dress added a royal appeal, and the detailing of the outer surface enhanced the overall appeal of the outfit. Deepika complemented the attire with a pair of purple Patiala pants with a bold border design, reflecting a festive-rooted aura on the overall look.

(Image Credits: Instagram)

The elaborate detail of the ensemble was enhanced by the traditional ornaments from the label Umrao Jewels, which enriched the outfit further with ancient splendour. The ornaments helped to create the overall majestic image of the outfit, rather than overwhelming it with their splendour. She simply wore a dangling pair of golden jhumkas and completed the look with a pair of golden juttis.

(Image Credits: Instagram)

Furthermore, Deepika went with her signature sleek bun look with gajra and opted for a glowing base with dark kohl-defined eyes, nude lipstick, and a soft blush edge. It seemed that she didn’t have to work hard to produce a sophisticated image, as it comes with her personality, but she made sure that the wealth of the colours, splendour of design, and age-old tradition remained the centre of attention. Well, the result was an image that is ideal for visiting temples and is at the same time festive and luxurious in character.
