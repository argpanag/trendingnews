Match Facts

  • Hibernian have lost both of their last two league games against Hearts, but haven’t lost three in a row to their Edinburgh rivals in the top-flight since March 2012.

  • After their 2-1 win in April, Hearts could enjoy back-to-back away league wins over Hibernian for the first time since March 2020.

  • Hibernian have lost each of their last four home league games, their longest run in the top-flight since August 2011 (also 4), while they last lost five in a row in August 2010.

  • Hearts have only won one of their last eight away league outings (D2 L5), although that was at rivals Hibernian in April (2-1).

  • Last season, all six of Heart of Midlothian’s goals against Hibernian came in the second half of games, with four of those coming in the 86th minute or later. Hibs, by contrast, scored three of their four goals in this fixture in first halves.
