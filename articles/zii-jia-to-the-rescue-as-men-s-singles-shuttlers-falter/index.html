Zii Jia to the rescue as men’s singles shuttlers falter

TheTools · 25.11.2026 19:08 · source · permalink
    bookmark

PETALING JAYA: Lee Zii Jia saved the day for Malaysia by reaching the second round of the Syed Modi International as several other men's singles shuttlers bowed out early on Wednesday (Nov 25).

In the first-round action at the Babu Banarasi Das Uttar Pradesh Badminton Academy, Zii Jia dominated from the start to beat home player Sankar Muthusamy 21-17, 21-12.

The victory extended Zii Jia's winning record against the world No. 98, having also beaten him at the Australian Open in 2024 and the Orleans Masters in March.

However, it was a different story for Justin Hoh, who continued to struggle for form, losing 11-21, 17-21 to home player Tushar Suveer despite being the fifth seed.

Aidil Sholeh Ali Sadikin also suffered an early exit, going down 14-21, 15-21 to Indonesia's Pradishka Bagas.

Cheam June Wei was another casualty, losing 16-21, 13-21 to Indonesia's Chico Aura.

Eogene Ewe, however, fought hard to secure his place in the next round, beating Japan's Riku Hatano 21-12, 19-21, 21-14.

In the women's singles, Goh Jin Wei was forced to retire after taking a 2-1 lead against Tanvi Sharma.

Wong Ling Ching, meanwhile, advanced after beating Aakarshi Kashyap 21-11, 21-10.
