FC Barcelona beat Sporting CP to win its fifth consecutive Iberian Super Cup.

Barcelona defeated Sporting CP 39-34 in the final of the 2026 Iberian Super Cup at the Instituto Feiral de Vigo in Vigo, Spain on the 5th.

Barcelona continued its overwhelming record in the tournament by winning all of the tournaments from the 1st to the 5th. Barcelona also won the Catalan Super Cup earlier, lifting its second trophy this season.

Since the beginning of the game, the two teams have been in a fierce battle. With no team easily ahead of them, Barcelona focused on Dani Fernández and effectively kept Sporting's attacks in check in defense.

Barcelona led 13-10 in the 20th minute to create a three-goal lead for the first time. However, Sporting CP immediately fought back and narrowed the gap, and the battle between the two teams became tense again.

At the end of the first half, Sporting CP pressed Barcelona hard. Barcelona's young goalkeeper Albert Quesada saved the team at an important moment by blocking a 7m draw, and Barcelona finished the first half with a slight 19-18 lead.

In the second half, Barcelona began to take the lead in the game. Alex Gómez finished the counterattack to make it 23-20, and later scored again to widen the gap to 28-24 in the 10th minute of the second half.

Barcelona's attack continued to be powerful midway through the second half. Shaking Sporting CP's defense, he led by 30-25 and five goals to win the game. Sporting CP followed with three consecutive points at the end of the game to 30-28, seeking the final turnaround.

However, Barcelona cut Sporting's rise in the crisis when Dika Mem scored with a long-range shot. Since then, Carlos Ortega's team has maintained its lead by focusing on game management.

In the end, Barcelona secured a 39-34 victory with a stable performance until the end of the match. Barcelona once again confirmed its position as the strongest player in the tournament, setting a record of winning all five Iberian Super Cups.

[Kim Yongpil, MK Sports Reporter]
