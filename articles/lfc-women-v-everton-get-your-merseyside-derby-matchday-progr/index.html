LFC Women v Everton: Get your Merseyside derby matchday programme at Anfield - Liverpool FC

TheTools · 27.09.2026 13:40 · source · permalink

Share

The official matchday programme is the perfect souvenir for Liverpool’s Merseyside derby with Everton at Anfield in the Barclays Women’s Super League.

Inside Sunday’s issue, Vivien Endemann reflects on her fine start to life at the club and explains why she believes the Reds will only get better as the season progresses.

We also put the focus on the poignant permanent memorials to former LFC Women manager Matt Beard, which have been unveiled at Anfield and the AXA Melwood Training Centre.

Elsewhere, Alice Bergstrom tackles our quickfire questions, while captain and cover star Grace Fisk and head coach Gareth Taylor share the latest thoughts from the camp in their regular columns.

We shine the spotlight on our visitors from across Stanley Park, put you to the test in our fun quiz and round up all the latest match action, plus present all the facts and stats you need to know.

With all this alongside a picture feature focusing on centre-back Jenna Clark and a pull-out team poster, make sure you pick up your copy of the programme at Anfield, priced £3.
