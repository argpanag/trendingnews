Deshaun Watson's wife is a singer and SI model with bigger IG following than him

TheTools · 01.10.2026 14:50 · source · permalink
  • Alex Worth, US Sports Writer
  • Published: 15:50, 1 Oct 2026
  • Updated: 15:50, 1 Oct 2026

If there's one thing that has remained consistent in the ups and downs of Deshaun Watson's NFL career, it's his wife.

Jilly Anais and Watson began dating in July of 2019.

Six years later, in March 2025, the Cleveland Browns quarterback popped the question and received a resounding yes.

"Mrs. Watson Loading…," Anais posted on her Instagram story.

Just four months later, the couple exchanged vows in Miami, on the same date that their reported first date took place, July 3.

Who is Jilly Anais?

Born in Houston on January 2, 1996, Anais is the daughter of former basketball player Jules K. Moor and sales executive Theresa Moor.

She won Miss Teen Houston in 2011 before moving to Los Angeles after turning 18 to pursue entertainment.

Since moving to LA, she has worked in music, modeling, acting and content creation.

She boasts 2.3 million followers combined on Instagram and TikTok, almost double the number of followers her husband has.

In 2023, Anais was a member of Nick Cannon's Future Superstar Tour, appearing on multiple TV shows including 'Wild 'N Out', 'Rosewood' and 'Real Husbands of Hollywood'.

She was one of six finalists in Sports Illustrated's Swimsuit Swim Search in 2025, also appearing in the brand's runway show during Miami Swim Week.

Anais has also launched multiple business ventures, including Boss Babe fragrance, and Boss Babe cookbooks.

Anais shows support to Watson after strong start

Anais is a regular attendee at Browns games, cheering on her husband who was named the starting quarterback after an offseason battle with Shedeur Sanders.

Cleveland has started the season 2-1 under new head coach Todd Monken, and Watson has exceeded expectations.

After their 21-18 victory over the Carolina Panthers, Anais took to Instagram to share a message of support to her husband.

"Another Sunday, Same Winner," Anais wrote in her Instagram story.

Since joining the Browns from the Houston Texans, Watson has struggled to replicate his previous success - largely thanks to injury.

However, his start to the 2026 campaign has given Cleveland reason to hope again.

Watson has thrown for 587 yards with five touchdowns to one interception through three weeks.

Up next is a Thursday Night Football matchup against Aaron Rodgers and the Pittsburgh Steelers.
