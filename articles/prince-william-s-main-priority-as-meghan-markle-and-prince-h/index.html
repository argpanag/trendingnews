Prince William's main priority as Meghan Markle and Prince Harry return to UK

TheTools · 19.09.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

Prince William is focused on safeguarding his family after Prince Harry and Meghan Markle relocated to the UK, according to a royal expert.

The Duke and Duchess of Sussex left the UK after stepping down as senior royals in 2020, relocating to Montecito, California. However, the couple returned Harry's homeland at the end of August, with their children, Prince Archie and Princess Lilibet, now attending school in Britain.

The UK relocation follows years of strain between the Sussexes and the broader royal family. Harry has been distanced from his brother for years and previously indicated William was "trapped" in the monarchy. Since returning to the UK, Harry and Meghan have faced backlash following reports that they transferred their children to a different school due to security concerns.

Royal expert Lynn Carratt discussed with The Mirror US how Harry's homecoming to the UK might be viewed by his brother, William. She cautioned the Duke of Sussex not to "expect an overnight reconciliation."

"There has been an enormous amount of hurt between William and Harry, and trust is probably the biggest hurdle they would need to overcome," the expert noted.

According to Carratt, Harry and Meghan are not William's primary concern following their relocation to the UK. "William is also likely to be incredibly protective of Catherine and their children, particularly after everything the family has been through," she stated, indicating his family will take precedence.

The expert continued, "Any relationship would need to happen slowly and, crucially, privately." Harry has been developing a connection with his father, King Charles, with the two meeting privately alongside Meghan and Queen Camilla last month.

Carratt highlighted how William's wife, Kate Middleton, has frequently been seen as "something of a peacemaker between the brothers." She warned, however, "I don't think the responsibility for bringing William and Harry back together should fall on her."

While a reconciliation between the Sussexes and the Wales family appears improbable, Carratt did propose there's one factor that might unite them. "The children could inadvertently become a bridge between the two families," she said.

The Duke and Duchess of Sussex are parents to seven-year-old Prince Archie and five-year-old Princess Lilibet. William and Kate have three children: Prince George, 13, Princess Charlotte, 11, and Prince Louis, eight.

Carratt noted, "If Archie and Lilibet begin spending more time in Britain, it would be perfectly natural for Harry to want them to see George, Charlotte and Louis. Sometimes relationships between adults remain complicated while children simply want to know their cousins."

She wrapped up by saying, "In fact, the first really significant photograph might not be the Fab Four at all. It could simply be George, Charlotte, Louis, Archie and Lilibet together. Seeing all five cousins in the same photograph would send a very powerful message about how far the family had come."
