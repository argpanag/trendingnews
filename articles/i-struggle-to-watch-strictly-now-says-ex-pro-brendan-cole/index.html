I struggle to watch Strictly now, says ex-pro Brendan Cole

TheTools · 07.10.2026 20:30 · source · permalink

Former star of show questions choice of presenting trio and says programme running time is too long

A former Strictly Come Dancing professional says he “struggles” to watch the new version of the show.

Brendan Cole, who danced on the programme from 2004 until 2017, has criticised the show’s new hosts, as well as the BBC’s decision to overhaul its professional dancer lineup.

Appearing on Jeremy Vine on 5, the dancer also said the show’s run time – which stretches past two hours 20 minutes on a Saturday – was too long.

Cole, 50, told Vine he was “not enjoying” the presenting trio of Emma Willis, Josh Widdicombe and Johannes Radebe, previously one of the show’s pro dancers, who replaced long-standing hosts Claudia Winkleman and Tess Daly.

“It’s not what people are used to,” he said. “I think the signings are interesting, let’s say. I’m not enjoying it so far.”

He told the programme that Willis had “been great”, but questioned whether comedian Widdicombe was the right “fit”.

“I think Emma’s been great. I think Josh is trying to be great, and I don’t think he’s a bad host, but does he fit Strictly? I’m not sure.

“Johannes obviously fits Strictly, but I don’t want to hear, ‘my darling, my darling, my darling’ every time someone comes up the stairs.

“So I think the BBC are probably looking at themselves now and going, ‘Eek, have we made the right decision?’”

Cole described the changes as a “massive risk”, and said he “struggled” to get through the full programme.

“I don’t disagree with the moving on,” he said. “I think you’ve got to change your show.

“I think making those changes – the contestants and then the hosts – it’s a massive change for people and I don’t think people are loving it.”

He added: “People are watching TV in a different way these days.

“I struggle to get through two and a half hours on the main show on a Saturday night. It’s long. It’s a big watch.”

The hit BBC dancing competition returned for its 24th series last month with its three new presenters.

Chris Appleton, a celebrity hairstylist, became the first celebrity to be sent home during Sunday’s show. He was eliminated following a dance-off with actress Jaime Winstone.
