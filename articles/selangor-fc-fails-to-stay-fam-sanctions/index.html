Selangor FC fails to stay FAM sanctions

TheTools · 08.09.2026 03:36 · source · permalink

FAM says the football club’s application was rejected by the Court of Arbitration’s emergency sole arbitrator yesterday.

In a statement, FAM said the application was rejected by the Court of Arbitration’s emergency sole arbitrator yesterday.

“Following the decision, the sanction against Selangor FC to play without spectators for its 2026/2027 FA Cup match against Sabah on Sept 9 on home ground remains in force,” FAM said.

FAM said the full hearing of Selangor FC’s appeal against the sanctions would continue to be heard for a final decision, in accordance with the prescribed arbitration process.

The emergency application was filed by Selangor FC under Rule 23 of the Asian Sports Arbitration Rules 2026.

On Nov 15 last year, the Red Giants were slapped with a RM100,000 fine and an order to play in empty stadiums for the second leg of the season’s FA Cup semi-final against Sabah FC on Nov 30, and their first home match for the same competition the next season.

Two Negeri Sembilan FC supporters were injured after firecrackers and benches were thrown at them, with a VVIP throwing a water-filled container at families of the opposing team.

FAM’s disciplinary committee found Selangor FC guilty of five charges under the association’s disciplinary code.

The disciplinary committee’s decision was upheld by the association’s appeals committee on Nov 26 last year.

In the match on Oct 29 last year, the second leg of the FA Cup quarter-finals, Selangor FC were defeated 3-2 by Negeri Sembilan FC but qualified for the semi-finals on a 6-3 aggregate.
