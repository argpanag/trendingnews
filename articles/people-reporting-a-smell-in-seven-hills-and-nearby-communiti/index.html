People reporting a ‘smell’ in Seven Hills and nearby communities

TheTools · 17.09.2026 00:22 · source · permalink

SEVEN HILLS, Ohio (WOIO) - Seven Hills Fire Department said it is aware of multiple reports of an odor in the area.

Neighboring fire departments are also responding to similar reports in their communities, according to a social media release from Seven Hills Fire.

The source of the odor has not been identified.
