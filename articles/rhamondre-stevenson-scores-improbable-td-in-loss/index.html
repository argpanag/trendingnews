Rhamondre Stevenson scores improbable TD in loss

TheTools · 23.10.2026 03:59 · source · permalink

    Rhamondre Stevenson scores improbable TD in loss

    Rhamondre Stevenson

    NE Running Back #38
    Player Stats

    Rhamondre Stevenson rushed six times for 22 yards in the Patriots’ Week 7 loss to the Bears, adding 93 yards and a touchdown on five receptions.

    Stevenson scored one of the more improbable touchdowns you will see late in the second quarter. After taking possession with just 14 second remaining on the clock, Drake Maye completed an 18-yard pass to A.J. Brown to place the ball near midfield with three seconds remaining before the half. Instead of heaving the ball deep on a Hail Mary, Maye dumped the ball off to Stevenson in the flat with blockers out front. Stevenson would do the rest on his way to a 48-yard score as time expired in the second frame on a score that never really should have happened. The six carries go down as a season-low for the veteran back, although 11 opportunities handily led the team. Stevenson has not seen more than 14 carries since Week 1 and is not finding paint frequently enough to remain a consistent RB2, making him a shaky weekly start moving forward. That holds true ahead of a Week 8 meeting with the Dolphins.
    - Mark Garcia
