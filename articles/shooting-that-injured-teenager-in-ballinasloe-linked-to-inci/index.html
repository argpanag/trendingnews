Shooting that injured teenager in Ballinasloe linked to incident at Halloween

TheTools · 06.11.2026 17:57 · source · permalink

Gardaí suspect a shooting in Ballinasloe, Co Galway, in which a teenage boy was injured, is linked to an incident in the area on Halloween night.

It is believed that at least one shot was fired in the direction of caravans in Curragh Park, resulting in the boy sustaining a non-life threatening injury.

A major Garda investigation is under way into the incident, with arrests made and searches carried out in Ballinasloe and Athlone, about 30km from the crime scene.

The attack, which happened on a green area at around 2am on Tuesday, is suspected to be linked to a long-standing family-based feud that has continued, in various guises, for several decades.

The latest incident appears to have been provoked by events during Halloween in Ballinasloe.

A group of people based in the town believe a number of caravans were deliberately targeted by antisocial behaviour, with a bonfire lit or fireworks set off close to them. However, it was not clear if this aimed to target anyone or was in any way deliberate.

Gardaí suspect the shooting was effectively a revenge attack and may have occurred as a result of misinformation.

The injured teenager was taken University Hospital Galway to be treated for non life-threatening injuries. He has since been discharged and is expected to make a full recovery.

Sources said there was concern that a shotgun appeared to have been fired indiscriminately in the direction of caravans where a number of people live.

“An incident room has been established at Ballinasloe Garda station and a senior investigating officer has been appointed,” a Garda statement added.

“Investigating gardaí carried out a number of searches of properties in Ballinasloe and Athlone (on Thursday) and seized a vehicle and a number of electronic devices.”

One of those arrested is an adult teenager, who was detained in Athlone and questioned at a Garda station in the northwest under Section 50 of the Criminal Justice Act, which is used in cases of suspected organised crime.

A second man, in his 20s, was arrested in Athlone during searches linked to the investigation on foot of outstanding bench warrants.

An Garda Síochána appealed to anyone who was in the Curragh Park area from midnight to 3am on Thursday to come forward. They are especially keen to secure CCTV recordings or dashcam footage that may have been captured around the time of the attack.

Anyone with information is asked to contact Ballinasloe Garda station on 090 063 1890, the Garda Confidential Line on 1800 666 111, or any Garda station.
