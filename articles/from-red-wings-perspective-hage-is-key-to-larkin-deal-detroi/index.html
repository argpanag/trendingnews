From Red Wings' Perspective, Hage is Key to Larkin Deal | Detroit Hockey Now

TheTools · 23.11.2026 03:20 · source · permalink

Detroit Red Wings

There was no way the Detroit Red Wings were going to be able to deal Dylan Larkin and get a center in return who could replace his goal output.

With Michael Hage, they’ve got themselves the next-best thing. Hage is a more traditional center. He’s a playmaker. He sets up others to score.

Early consensus around NHL is Detroit got a fantastic return.

This is an element that’s never been part of Larkin’s game. He’s always been first and foremost a goal scorer. He’s scored at least 30 times in each of the past five seasons.

Make no mistake: Among the multitude of pieces coming Detroit’s way in this blockbuster deal, Hage is the true gem. His development as an NHLer will determine whether this trade is a success for the Red Wings.

Montreal’s top pick (21st overall) in the 2024 NHL Entry Draft, the right-shot Hage is an elite presence in college hockey with the Michigan Wolverines. He is currently leading the nation in scoring with 5-21-26 totals through 13 games.

Hage is the NCAA leader in points per game (2.00) and assists per game (1.62).

Red Wings Are Getting a Dominant Playmaker

“He’s finding ways to take over games,” Michigan coach Brandon Naurato said in a recent interview with Sportsnet. “The production’s going to be there.”

In Hage, Naurato also sees a natural leader.

“Being a leader isn’t just wearing a letter on your jersey,” Naurato said. “He’s having hard conversations with his peers. He’s held to a higher standard to perform on and off the ice in a lifestyle that is what Michigan wants and how he wants to represent the Hage name.

“I think there’s more accountability to that. Expectations are higher.”

Playing for Canada at last year’s World Juniors, Hage was the tournament scoring leader with 15 points. He’s already got NHL size at 6-foot-1 and 198 pounds.

The scouting report is that Hage is an elite skater with a quick and powerful stride. While known for his playmaking skills, he’s also equipped with a high-velocity shot. Hage also gets kudos for his compete level and willingness to battle to win pucks.

Red Wings GM Shawn Horcoff wouldn’t have to look far to get further intel on Hage. His son, Will Horcoff, is Hage’s Michigan teammate.
