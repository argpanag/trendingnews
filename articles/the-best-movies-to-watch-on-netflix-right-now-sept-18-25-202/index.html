The Best Movies to Watch on Netflix Right Now (Sept. 18-25, 2026)

TheTools · 18.09.2026 19:30 · source · permalink

Check out 'Those Who Wish Me Dead' and a trio of other thrilling survival movies.

The No. 1 ranked movie on Netflix this week is Tyler Perry's Why Did I Get Married Again? And while we're totally fine with Perry's vast output, it's not really our thing. So let's look at the second-place flick, which is 2021's Those Who Wish Me Dead, a survival thriller from another entertainment mogul, Yellowstone's Tyler Sheridan. Once you've watched it, I have a trio of similar recommendations for you.

Those Who Wish Me Dead

When a young boy witnesses the murder of his father, he takes off into the Montana wilderness with evidence that the assassins would really like to have. Lost and alone, he meets a struggling smokejumper who takes him back to her fire tower to get help. When the killers start a forest fire to distract police, our duo must survive dangers both natural and man-made to bring them to justice. It's a fun '90s-style thriller that doesn't push any envelopes but guarantees a good time.

Don't Move

Staying mobile is one of the most essential keys to survival, so what happens when that's taken away from you? That's the premise of 2024's effective Don't Move, which stars Kelsey Asbille as a grieving mother out for a walk in a state park. She encounters a mysterious man who talks her out of committing suicide only to knock her unconscious and inject her with a paralytic chemical. As she slowly loses her mobility, she must elude her pursuer and stay alive.

The Lost Children

As compelling as the fictional wilderness survival stories in movies are, they pale in comparison to the real thing. Netflix documentary The Lost Children tells the almost unbelievable tale of four children stranded after a plane crash in the jungles of Colombia. Taught to hunt, fish, and forage from an early age, the kids managed to hold out in the deadly environment for 40 days before being rescued.

Nowhere

Finally, we have the 2023 Netflix hit Nowhere. Set amid a societal collapse in the near future, Nico and his pregnant wife Mia smuggle themselves out of Spain on a cargo ship, only for a storm to wash the container she is hiding in out to sea. Now Mia needs to not only survive but also safely give birth to her child and find a way to get rescued. It's a tight little movie with a bravura performance by Anna Castillo as Mia.
