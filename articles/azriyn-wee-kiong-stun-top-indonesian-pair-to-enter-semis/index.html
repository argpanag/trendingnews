Azriyn-Wee Kiong stun top Indonesian pair to enter semis

TheTools · 04.09.2026 14:56 · source · permalink

The world No 29 Malaysian duo beat 7th ranked Indonesians in three games to book a semi-final slot at the China Masters.

In a thrilling three-game match lasting 58 minutes, world No 29 pair Azriyn-Wee Kiong beat the sixth seeds 21-17, 16-21, 21-19 at the Sports Centre Gymnasium in Shenzen China. Both pairs have won twice each in their four encounters so far.

Also in the semi-finals are compatriots Tee Kai Wun-Yap Roy King in the men’s doubles, and mixed doubles pairs Goh Soon Huat-Shevon Jemie Lai and Hoo Pang Ron-Lai Pei Jing.

Azriyn-Wee Kiong will face India’s SatwiksaiRaj Rankireddy-Chirag Shetty tomorrow.
