Christian Pulisic scores again vs Inter in Derby della Madonnina

TheTools · 01.11.2026 02:03 · source · permalink

Christian Pulisic loves playing in the Milan derby.

The AC Milan star scored in a 3-1 win against Inter on Saturday, Oct. 31, making it four goals in his past eight games against Milan's biggest rival.

Pulisic opened the scoring just four minutes in at San Siro, converting a header from a great Diego Moreira cross.

It was the American's third goal of the season, all of which have come in his past six matches.

Adrien Rabiot doubled Milan's advantage just six minutes later, before Alessandro Bastoni pulled a goal back for Inter in the 22nd minute.

But Moreira restored Milan's two-goal lead with a sensational free kick just before halftime, making it 3-1.

The win took Milan up to fourth in the Serie A table, four points back of first-place Lazio.

Pulisic's goal came just one day after he and AC Milan officially agreed to a contract extension through 2031.

The 28-year-old has made a habit of scoring against Inter, also finding the net in the November 2025 derby, as well as twice during the 2024-25 season.
