PCSO Lotto Results: No winners of major jackpot draws on September 23, 2026

TheTools · 23.09.2026 23:34 · source · permalink

There were no winners of either of the major lotto draws on September 23, 2026, as the Grand Lotto 6/55 jackpot reached over P219 million, the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO) said Wednesday.

No one chose chose the winning numbers of 29-47-22-32-50-46 for the Grand Lotto 6/55 prize of P219,836,261.65.

According to PCSO, the odds of winning the jackpot for the Grand Lotto 6/55 are 1 in 28,989,675. To win, bettors must match all six numbers chosen from a field of 1 to 55.

For Megalotto 6/45,there were also no bettors who picked the combination of 44-05-04-40-45-07 that would have unlocked the P71,193,623.60 jackpot.
