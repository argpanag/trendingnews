Roberto de Zerbi urges Tottenham Hotspur to use Stamford Bridge record as fuel against Chelsea

TheTools · 24.10.2026 08:20 · source · permalink

English Premier League

Roberto de Zerbi said Tottenham Hotspur will use their woeful record at Chelsea as motivation when they make the trip across London on Saturday.

Spurs have won just once at Stamford Bridge in the English Premier League era and have lost 10 of the last 12 meetings between the capital clubs. However, they go into the match buoyed by Monday's 3-1 win at home to Coventry — their first Premier League victory this season.

De Zerbi, asked during a pre-match press conference on Friday if he sensed any fear in his squad, replied: "No. I think no because I played just one game with Chelsea as Tottenham coach.

"Anyway, it has to be one motivation more. Not one more problem for us.

"What is the problem?

"We didn't play in the past for Tottenham, and I was not the Tottenham coach in the last seasons. It is not a problem. We have already many problems, and this is not a problem.

"I think it's not just this negative record. There are many, many negative records, but for me, it's one motivation more. For the players, I want this negative record to give us more motivation, more mentality, more energy to win a game, to play a good game, because we have enough quality to win."

Tottenham's bid for a first win at Stamford Bridge since 2018 is not helped by the absence of Destiny Udogie and potentially James Maddison, who marked his first start in 17 months with a goal against Coventry, but has since missed three days of training.

"In the first three days, Maddison didn't work with us; I don't know if he's ready for tomorrow," said De Zerbi.

"Like I said many times, I don't want to take risks, because if you analyse this period of European football, you can see many injuries — not just for Tottenham, for everyone. So, if I can, I don't want to take risks to lose many players for many games."  |  AFP
