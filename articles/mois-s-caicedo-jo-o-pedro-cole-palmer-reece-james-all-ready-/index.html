Moisés Caicedo, João Pedro, Cole Palmer, Reece James all ‘ready to go’ against Bournemouth

TheTools · 09.10.2026 21:48 · source · permalink
  • Chelsea FC: Injury News

Oh baby

Xabi Alonso had some excellent news in his press conference earlier today, revealing that Moisés Caicedo, João Pedro, Cole Palmer, and Reece James are all ready to play this weekend after being able to return to training this week. This quartet of key players have all been dealing with injuries over the past few weeks, causing them to miss international duty, in addition to any missed time with Chelsea prior to the break.

The news is especially welcome in the case of Caicedo, who’s missed practically the entire season thus far. He tried to come back a few weeks ago, but that 14-minute spell on the pitch only served to re-aggravate things. Hopefully he’s now fully healed and truly good to come back.

“Yes, [Caicedo] is ready to go. [We] have used the time with Moises to [get him] back fit. He’s been training good and he’s okay to go. […] We have missed him for sure and we are happy to have him back. He feels better. We did not want to take risks for Brentford for example because we did against Brighton, I did [take risks] myself and I said ‘Xabi you did it wrong’ but now he feels much better.”

“[JP, James, and Palmer] have been able to train [as well]. Some of them earlier than others. Marco Palestra as well. So, we have used the time and that was the plan. We knew that against Brentford we were with some players struggling but now they have trained well and they are ready to go.

“[For Cole], the most important thing […] is to be fully fit, that he enjoys his game, that he is feeling okay. Whenever he is not, like in the Brentford game, we could see that he was struggling and for the next part we have a good feeling with him and that he is feeling better.”

-Xabi Alonso; source: Football.London

Let’s go!

(Alonso did confirm that Jamie Gittens is out with the ankle injury he picked up with the England U21s.)
