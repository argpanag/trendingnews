Pleasant weather Wednesday before rain and winds ramp up this weekend. Here’s the latest

TheTools · 14.10.2026 10:15 · source · permalink

Weather

Highs Wednesday nearing 90 degrees

ORLANDO, Fla. – Central Florida will see another warm, mostly dry day before rain chances increase late this week. A cold front is expected to move through Friday night into Saturday, bringing gusty winds, rough surf, and a shift to slightly cooler temperatures heading into the weekend.

Wednesday & Thursday

Expect mostly dry conditions, with just a few isolated showers possible as the east coast sea breeze develops. Highs will reach the mid to upper 80s, with east-northeast winds picking up to 10 to 15 mph. Overnight temperatures will fall into the 70s, with a few cooler spots northwest of I-4 dipping into the upper 60s.

Friday

Rain chances will gradually increase as more moisture moves into Central Florida. Showers and a few storms will be most likely during the afternoon and early evening, although a few coastal showers could develop overnight or during the morning.

A few storms could produce frequent lightning, gusty winds, and locally heavy rain, but the overall threat for stronger storms remains low. Highs will climb into the upper 80s, with some inland spots nearing 90 degrees.

Weekend outlook

A cold front is expected to move through late Friday night into Saturday, bringing a shift in temperatures and stronger winds. While highs will trend cooler, most areas will still reach the low to mid 80s.

The bigger change will be the wind. Strong northeast winds could produce gusts of 35 to 40 mph, especially along the coast, with breezy conditions continuing into early next week.

Onshore winds will also bring rounds of showers and a few storms, particularly along the coast.

Beachgoers and boaters should prepare for rough surf and hazardous marine conditions this weekend into early next week. Strong, persistent onshore winds will also increase the risk of beach erosion.
