Singapore Airlines, Scoot to offer over 380,000 discounted tickets at Time to Fly travel fair

TheTools · 13.10.2026 07:07 · source · permalink

Singapore Airlines (SIA) and its low-cost subsidiary Scoot are offering customers more than 380,000 discounted tickets to 125 destinations across their networks at the upcoming Time to Fly travel fair.

The promotion will be available online from Oct 23 to 30 through the mobile apps and websites of SIA and Scoot, and their appointed travel agents.

During the promotion period, SIA will offer over 200,000 discounted return tickets across its Business, Premium Economy and Economy Classes to 78 destinations, including Bangkok, Hangzhou, London, Los Angeles, Madrid, Seoul and Sydney.

The promotional fares will apply for travels between Jan 1 and Sept 30, 2027.

Meanwhile, Scoot will offer more than 180,000 discounted tickets in ScootPlus and Economy Class to 70 destinations, including new routes such as Belitung, Chiang Rai, Guiyang and Okinawa. 

Scoot's promotional travel period runs from Nov 9 this year to Oct 15, 2027.

A three-day physical fair will also take place between Oct 23 and 25 at the Suntec Singapore Convention and Exhibition Centre, with more than 30 participating travel agents and partners.

There will be exclusive giveaways, lucky draws and promotions taking place at the event, with the grand lucky draw's top prize being a pair of SIA Business Class return tickets to Cebu, Hangzhou, and Sydney with hotel accommodation. 

Other daily draw prizes include SIA Business Class return tickets to London Gatwick, Madrid, and Male with hotel accommodation, as well as Scoot vouchers worth up to $2,000, and $800 worth of KrisShop vouchers. 

KrisShop will also offer its customers 30 per cent off selected Batik Label items and exclusive access to its new Destination Series: Korea Edition merchandise to be launched at the event.

Those who spend a minimum of $5,000 using a Mastercard credit or debit card will be able to redeem $100 worth of Pelago or KrisShop vouchers, and enter a sure-win lucky dip, with prizes including a pair of SIA Economy Class return tickets. 

SIA's senior vice president for sales and marketing Vinod Kannan said its annual Time to Fly promotion gives Singapore-based customers more choice and value as they plan for their holidays, reconnect with loved ones, or discover a new destination.

Scoot's chief commercial officer Calvin Chan added that Scoot has always been committed to providing its customers with quality service and differentiated products at great value.

More information on SIA's Time to Fly promotion and trade fair is available on its website.
