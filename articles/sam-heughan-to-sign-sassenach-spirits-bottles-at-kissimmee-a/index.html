Sam Heughan to sign Sassenach Spirits bottles at Kissimmee ABC Fine Wine & Spirits, Nov. 10 - Orlando Shine

TheTools · 09.11.2026 01:25 · source · permalink

ONE OF THE BEST PARTS OF 'OUTLANDER'

Outlander star Sam Heughan (Instagram) will sign bottles of his Sassenach Spirits at ABC Fine Wine & Spirits in Kissimmee on Tuesday, November 10, from 1 p.m. to 3 p.m. The signing is at the ABC store at 3187 Black Lake Road [GMap] in Kissimmee, just south of Orlando. It’s free, open to the public, and first come, first served. No registration is required, but space is limited. And it’s going to be busy. Because Outlander pervs.

Signed bottles of Sassenach Spirits can be bought in the store only, during the event and while supplies last, ABC said. Heughan will sign only bottles, not other merchandise. A meet-and-greet is not guaranteed because of the limited time and expected crowd. Parking is limited, so the store recommends ride-sharing. The line will extend outside, so plan for Florida weather.

The Kissimmee stop is one of five on Heughan’s Fall Tour 2026. Other stops include Los Angeles on November 5, Dallas on November 6, Austin on November 7, and River Edge, New Jersey, on November 12.
