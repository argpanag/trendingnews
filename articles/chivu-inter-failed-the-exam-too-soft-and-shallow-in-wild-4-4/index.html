Chivu: 'Inter failed the exam, too soft and shallow' in wild 4-4 with Atalanta

TheTools · 22.11.2026 22:44 · source · permalink

BySusy Campanale

Cristian Chivu warns Inter ‘failed the exam’ in their 4-4 draw with Atalanta, criticising some of his players. ‘We were too soft, need to be more determined in the duels.’

The Nerazzurri performed several remarkable comebacks this season, but they were on the wrong end of it tonight in Bergamo after fumbling a two-goal advantage three times.

Pio Esposito scored his first ever Serie A hat-trick, carrying on the remarkable form seen for Italy in the Nations League, and the Petar Sucic penalty had seemingly put the game to bed at 4-2.

However, Charles De Ketelaere’s second of the game and a Franck spot-kick in stoppages allowed Atalanta to claw their way back for 4-4.

Chivu blames Inter for collapse

“We were leading 2-0, 3-1 and 4-2, so this is the regret,” Chivu told DAZN Italia.

“We had an idea of what we should’ve done and did not do, we should’ve controlled the advantage, then when we felt them snapping at our heels, we immediately restored the two-goal cushion.

“I think we failed the exam in the final minutes. It’s hard to say why we were unable to control the lead. We have to congratulate Atalanta, as they sensed that we were a bit shallow, and pumped crosses into the box, put bodies in there, while we were a little soft compared to our usual approach.

“I’m sorry, I see this as two gifted points, but we will learn from it and know now to let the intensity and mentality drop in the next games.”

Inter were angry at the Davide Zappacosta penalty for clearing off the line with his elbow because it was only a yellow card and not a red.

Alessandro Bastoni also felt the decisive spot-kick was harsh, but while his handball was unintentional, the arms were raised above his shoulder and therefore in a punishable position.

“The second Atalanta goal came from a corner, identical to one where Pepo had just made a great save moments earlier, so we did not learn from it,” continued Chivu.

“When we got to 4-2, our defence was then static in a chaotic situation. We need a certain determination and focus in these situations, and we were negligent in that area today.”

There was another serious blow today along with the dropped points, because Hakan Calhanoglu limped off with a suspected flexor strain in the first half.

At least Curtis Jones and Lautaro Martinez made their comebacks after more than a month out, two in the case of the former Liverpool midfielder.

“I felt we were suffering on the wings, partly because we were too soft, we were allowing the space to put the crosses in too easily. I decided to put Marcus Thuram wide to help out Djed Spence in the final stages,” explained Chivu.

“It’s a matter of desire to go into the duels, to be more determined, not just put bodies behind the ball, because if you don’t have the right intensity then a single error can be costly.”

Inter have conceded 17 goals in 12 Serie A games, more than any other side in the top six, but this is their first draw of the entire season after 11 wins and three defeats in all competition.

Is Chivu more angry at the dropped points, or worried at how many goals Inter are conceding this season?

“We are conceding too many goals for a club that has high ambitions, but we must do like we did in Rotterdam and with Como, respond to a setback, not look over our shoulders to the past. We must learn that we cannot just control a situation, we’ve got to keep giving 100 per cent until the final whistle,” concluded the Inter coach.
