27-year-old construction worker killed in I-275 crash, sheriff's office says

TheTools · 19.09.2026 04:31 · source · permalink

A 27-year-old construction working was struck and killed in a crash on I-275 early Friday in Springdale.

Joshua Christopher Holland "entered the single open lane of traffic and was struck by a 2015 Ford Fusion," the Hamilton County Sheriff's Office said in a news release.

Holland, of Adams County, was taken to Bethesda North Hospital, where he died from his injuries, according to Springdale police

The crash happened a little before 4 a.m. Sept. 18 on I-275 near Ohio 747.

Speed and impairment are not factors in this crash and no charges are pending, according to the sheriff's office.
