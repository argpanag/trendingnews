Detroit Lions Vs Miami Dolphins: Game Information & Live Chat

TheTools · 08.11.2026 17:00 · source · permalink

Talk about Week 9’s matchup against the Lions with your fellow Dolphins fans!

Our Miami Dolphins will be at home again this week as they host the Detroit Lions. The 1-6 Dolphins come into this game following a loss at home last week to the New England Patriots, 19-24. On the other side of the ball, the 5-2 Lions enter the week coming off a 3-game win streak, including last weekend’s victory over their divisional rivals, the previously undefeated Minnesota Vikings, 19-15.

Please use this live thread to discuss this afternoon’s game vs. the Lions, the latest NFL news from the past week, and all things Miami Dolphins.
