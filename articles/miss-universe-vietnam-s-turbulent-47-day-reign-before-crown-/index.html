Miss Universe Vietnam's turbulent 47-day reign before crown revocation - VnExpress International

TheTools · 22.11.2026 10:58 · source · permalink

Miss Universe Vietnam (MUV) announced Wednesday evening that it had revoked the 27-year-old's crown, citing violations of its rules and her failure to meet responsibilities as the titleholder.

The decision marks an unprecedented move among Vietnam's major beauty pageants. The organizers have not announced a replacement.

Anh, who was crowned on Oct. 2, has not publicly commented on the decision but has removed the title from her social media accounts.

Despite winning the competition, she had not signed a formal management contract with MUV.

Ho Chi Minh City-based entertainment company SIGNE announced Wednesday that it had taken over management of Anh's professional activities. The company said it did not support her past behavior and acknowledged differing public views surrounding the controversies.

Despite winning the competition, she had not signed a formal management contract with MUV.

A SIGNE representative expressed surprise and regret Thursday, calling the crown a significant achievement in Anh's career. However, the company said it respected MUV's decision and hoped the disputes would be resolved through transparent and objective discussions in line with relevant regulations.

Nguyen Quynh Anh poses for a photoshoot after winning Miss Universe Vietnam 2026. Photo courtesy of Anh's Instagram

A series of controversies

Anh's troubles began about 10 days after her coronation in Hai Phong, when screenshots of an alleged private group conversation involving her spread across social media.

The messages reportedly included insulting remarks about several people connected to the pageant, including fourth runner-up Trinh Hong Dang.

On Oct. 13, Anh admitted wrongdoing and apologized to those affected and the public. She met Dang two days later to reconcile, but a video of their conversation drew further criticism, with some social media users questioning whether her apology was sincere.

Another controversy emerged during filming of the reality show "Model Kid Vietnam," when Anh and fellow coach Bui Quynh Hoa left the set following a disagreement with the judges over a challenge's results.

The production team criticized her behavior as unprofessional. Anh withdrew from her coaching position on Oct. 18 and was absent from the show's finale.

As criticism intensified, MUV announced disciplinary action on Oct. 25, suspending Anh from stage performances for six months and requiring her to cover any fines issued by relevant authorities.

However, she continued appearing at public events in the following weeks. MUV said it had identified multiple occasions when Anh attended events without authorization, which ultimately led to the revocation of her title.

During one appearance, she was also mistakenly introduced using a Vietnamese beauty queen title whose intellectual property rights belonged to another company.

Separately, Anh lost the chance to represent Vietnam at Miss Universe 2026 in Puerto Rico later this month because of a dispute over participation rights between the organizations involved.

Born in Hanoi, Anh gained recognition as the runner-up on "The Face Vietnam" in 2018. She studied human resource management at Trade Union University in Hanoi and Saigon Tourism College before winning the international modeling competition "Supermodel Me" in December 2021.
