All four quarter-finals abandoned in Nagoya as rain denies any action at Asian Games.

Monday and Tuesday quarter-final action of the Men's Asian Games cricket competition has been curtailed by persistant rain in Nagoya

On Monday, both Pakistan's match with Hong Kong, China and India's meeting with Afghanistan were abandoned. A day later, the Nepal-Sri Lanka meeting and Bangladesh's match with Malaysia had a similar fate.

As the higher-seeded teams in their respective quarter-finals, Bangladesh, India, Pakistan and Sri Lanka move forward to the semi-finals.
