    England

    England captain Harry Kane, speaking to BBC Radio 5 Live: "Almost the perfect night to finish the camp for me. A big night to equal the record and an extremely proud achievement to reach. I enjoy being out there every time with the boys and feeling as good as I have ever felt. Let's keep them coming, I never put a ceiling on anything and right now I'm in a good place."

  4. Kane scores twice in impressive England winpublished at 22:25 BST 6 October
    22:25 BST 6 October

    FT: England 3-0 Czech Republic

    Phil McNulty
    BBC Sport chief football writer at Wembley

    Harry Kane celebrated his record-equalling 125th England cap with two goals in a comfortable Uefa Nations League victory against the Czech Republic at Wembley.

    Kane made it a remarkable tally of 91 goals for England on the night he drew level with Peter Shilton - and is now firmly on course to become the first player to reach a century for his country.

    England built on the win against the Czech Republic in Prague and the outstanding 7-0 victory in Croatia with another convincing display to delight head coach Thomas Tuchel and the home support.

    Kane had a hand in England's opener after 27 minutes, when his cross was turned into his own net by former West Ham United defender Vladimir Coufal.

    The goal Kane and Wembley craved came five minutes before the break, England's captain applying a simple close-range finish to Morgan Rogers' excellent first-time delivery.

    And Kane was on target again after 57 minutes, rising at the far post to head home Lewis Hall's corner.

    Kane departed to a well-deserved standing ovation when he was replaced by Dominic Calvert-Lewin in the closing minutes.

    22:22 BST 6 October

    FT: England 3-0 Czech Republic

    England

    England manager Thomas Tuchel speaking to BBC Radio 5 Live: "I am very satisfied. We had a very good camp and again a very good performance. We were totally dominant, created good moments, scored nice goals and deserved the win. The effort was there, the quality was there. It was an excellent two weeks."

    On Harry Kane: "He delivers all the time, it makes no difference. It's of course special that he did it today when he equals the record with Peter Shilton. If it needed any proof, it doesn't of course, he shows it again and again. He has again a very good performance. He played all four matches for us, he was eager to play and start all four matches. Top captain and hopefully next camp he gets the next two camps."

    On if Kane is the best England has ever had: "I don't know about the best and stuff but he is a legend of the English game and hopefully he stays fit and gets the record for himself. He will inspire loads and loads of young people to play football and encourage people to have a career like him and try their luck. He's a fantastic role model and he deserves the praise.

    "Next camp is the most important to secure our place in the quarter-finals but we have high ambitions, we want to go to the final four and try to make it 10 matches in the Nations League. We had a very good camp, very good four matches. The performance against Spain was also on a high level.We were very close to beat them, didn't beat them but the reaction is always what matters. To win three matches with three clean sheets in the way we did is very impressive and the team deserves the praise."

England show signs of progress

England's Uefa Nations League campaign may have started with defeat against World Cup holders Spain at Wembley but three subsequent victories, all achieved in convincing fashion, hinted at progress and optimism.

Indeed, it could have been four wins from four had Kane scored the penalty that would have put England 3-1 up against Spain before the lost 3-2, but head coach Tuchel will have been highly satisfied with what he has seen since.

England, after winning 7-0 in Croatia, were a threat again against the Czechs at Wembley, although Eberechi Eze failed to make the most of his opportunity with in-form Anthony Gordon rested.

Tuchel's attacking force is now set in stone, led by captain Harry Kane, with Gordon and Bukayo Saka on either side and Jude Bellingham stamping his world-class in the number 10 position.

Trent Alexander-Arnold took his chance at right-back after being recalled by Tuchel following his World Cup exclusion, looking solid in defence - albeit not under huge pressure - while offering an extra dimension in attack.

Newcastle United's Hall now has strong claims to be first-choice left-back, while Jarell Quansah impressed with his versatility at right-back then in central defence against the Czechs, cementing his place as a vital part of Tuchel's squad.

England face Croatia next in November before their biggest test, away to Spain, but they have foundations to build on.

And it was fitting that Kane marked the night when he equalled that caps record with two goals, capping a pleasing international break for Tuchel and England.
