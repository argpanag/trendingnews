Payton Pritchard sinks four triples, scores 18

TheTools · 12.11.2026 01:28 · source · permalink

    Payton Pritchard sinks four triples, scores 18

    Payton Pritchard

    BOS Guard #11
    Player Stats

    Payton Pritchard amassed 18 points (7-of-15 FGs), six rebounds, five assists, one steal and four three-pointers in Tuesday’s win over the 76ers.

    After totaling 11 points in Boston’s first two games this month, Pritchard has scored in double figures in four straight games. Against the 76ers, the Celtics guard shot 4-of-8 from beyond the arc, the third straight game in which he’s made four three-pointers. Add in the rebounds and assists, and this was a good start to the week for Pritchard. He’ll look to extend his double-digit points streak to five when the Celtics visit the Raptors on Wednesday.
    - Raphielle Johnson
