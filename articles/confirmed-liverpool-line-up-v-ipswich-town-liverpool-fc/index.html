Confirmed Liverpool line-up v Ipswich Town - Liverpool FC

TheTools · 04.09.2026 20:40 · source · permalink

Share

Ronald Araujo makes his first start for Liverpool in tonight's Premier League clash at Ipswich Town.

The Uruguay defender replaces Jeremie Frimpong in the only change from last weekend's encounter with Nottingham Forest.

Bradley Barcola is included in the squad following his transfer from Paris Saint-Germain and is named on the bench.

Liverpool: Alisson, Van Dijk, Jacquet, Kerkez, Wirtz, Szoboszlai, Isak, Mac Allister, Gakpo, Munoz, Araujo.

Subs: Mamardashvili, Endo, Tsimikas, Barcola, Frimpong, Gravenberch, Nyoni, Koumas, Ngumoha.
