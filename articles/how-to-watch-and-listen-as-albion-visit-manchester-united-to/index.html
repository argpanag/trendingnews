How to watch and listen as Albion visit Manchester United tonight

Albion’s trip to Manchester United is live on Sky Sports tonight, with radio coverage also available. According to TheArgus.co.uk, viewers can tune in on Sky Sports Football and Main Event from seven pm for an eight pm kick-off at Old Trafford.

Sky selected the third-round tie as one of the standout fixtures. Build-up is expected to include analysis of Albion as well as scrutiny of United’s form.

Bill Leslie is on commentary with Chris Sutton, while Jamie Redknapp and Ashley Young are in the studio.

BBC Radio 5 Live and talkSPORT plan live commentary, with build-up following their seven pm news bulletins.

Many Albion supporters may opt for BBC Radio Sussex, where Johnny Cantor and Warren Aspinall are on duty for the game. The programme is also available online via the club website.

Their build-up starts at seven thirty pm, with a post-match show from 10pm featuring reaction and interviews for 30 minutes.

A live blog will provide updates, pictures and a space for fan comments, running in time for confirmed team line-ups.

Albion will aim to knock United out of the Carabao Cup, having done likewise in the FA Cup in January, a result that hastened a change in charge. Darren Fletcher was replaced in interim command by Michael Carrick, who later took the role more permanently.
