Upgraded ZIRKA Interceptor Drone Destroys Geran-5 at 400 km/h

TheTools · 01.11.2026 08:30 · source · permalink

The upgraded ZIRKA interceptor drone successfully chased down and destroyed a jet-powered Geran-5 drone while traveling at approximately 400 km/hand flying at an altitude of 3,500 meters.

ZRK.tech, the technology project behind the interceptor’s development, announced the achievement on social media.

The Retro crew of the Darknode Battalion, part of the 412th Nemesis Brigade, carried out the interception. The brigade’s personnel have been involved in the interceptor’s development since the very beginning.

The ZIRKA interceptor drone, developed by Ukrainian company NOCTIS, was first unveiled in June 2026. Vyriy Industries provided investment support and technical expertise for the new system.

The first version of the interceptor reportedly had a maximum speed exceeding 340 km/h and an operational altitude of up to 6 km. Its stated operational range was 30 km, with a flight endurance of 20 minutes. The specifications of the upgraded version have not been disclosed.

The ZIRKA interceptor drone is reportedly equipped with an onboard computer based on the Raspberry Pi 5, running a trained computer vision model. The model analyzes each video frame in real time, searching for visual characteristics associated with Shahed-type UAVs. Judging by the footage, the upgraded version is also equipped with this system.

The system automatically detects targets up to 1 km away. Once the operator confirms the target, the system autonomously tracks it, leaving the pilot only to adjust closing speed for a more precise intercept.
