Innovation by injury: Victor Wembanyama debuts the 'shunk'

TheTools · 22.11.2026 02:54 · source · permalink

Top Stories

After dunking his way to an NBA-leading total & a blister, the Spurs center finds a way to finesse his finish around the rim.

From NBA.com News Services

Victor Wembanyama scores on a 'shot dunk' to avoid dunking on painful blister.

The Spurs won Thursday. Victor Wembanyama dominated.

In a season that has opened with San Antonio racing to a 13-3 share of the league lead and their 7-foot-4 center averaging 28.6 points, 11.9 rebounds and 3.9 blocks, neither is surprising.

And yet, in the Spurs’ 124-112 win over the Clippers, Wembanyama found a way to expand the boundaries of possible NBA action once again. This time, it was debuting the “shunk” — a combination jump shot and dunk that helped him avoid re-injuring a painful blister in the middle finger of his right hand.

“I’m not gonna lie, I’ve had this blister forever, and it’s killing me,” Wembanyama said. “Every time I dunk, it kills me. I’ve been dragging this blister for I don’t know how long because of dunking, so if I can avoid it, I’ll avoid it.”

The league leader in dunks at 72, Wembanyama was streaking ahead on the break late in the fourth quarter when he caught a pass from Stephon Castle in stride at the rim.

With everyone anticipating an impactful throwdown, Wembanyama rose and … softly dropped it in. Textbook execution on his ever-accurate jump shot, just right at the rim.

It was paradoxically his most underwhelming and mind-blowing pair of points, two of 32 on the night and one of 13 field goals (26 attempts). Wembanyama added 11 rebounds, 3 assists, a steal and a block.

This marked the seventh time this season Wembanyama reached 30 points.
