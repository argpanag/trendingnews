Pet supplements sold on Chewy, Amazon recalled. See affected products

TheTools · 11.09.2026 01:02 · source · permalink

A pet company is recalling two lots of pet supplements that could make dogs and their owners sick.

The company, Fi, issued a voluntary recall for two of its dietary supplements for dogs due to possible salmonella contamination, according to a Sept. 8 notice from the Food and Drug Administration. So far, no illnesses have been reported, but ingredients in the recalled supplements may contain the pathogen. An investigation is ongoing.

Serious illness could occur not only in pets, but in humans who may have come in contact with the supplements, according to the FDA.

Here's what to know about the ongoing pet supplement recall.

Which pet supplement products were recalled?

The affected products were sold directly to consumers through Fi, Amazon and Chewy.com.

  • Fi Calming supplement for Dogs 180 grams; Lot number 26118; UPC 8 50064 80011 1
  • Fi 8-in-1 Formula supplement for Dogs 180 grams; Lot number 26159; UPC 8 50055 11168 4

What to do with recalled dog supplements

Pet owners should stop using the product immediately, per the company and FDA. Before throwing the supplements away, as recommended by the FDA, purchasers should make sure the container is sealed tightly.

Consumers with questions may contact the company by calling 1-888-434-3647 or by emailing the Fi support team at support@tryfi.com.

Adverse effects may be reported to FDA’s Center for Veterinary Medicine Adverse Event Reporting program by completing an online form, FDA 1932a, available at www.fda.gov/reportanimalae.

Symptoms of salmonella poisoning in people and pets

While humans likely aren't consuming the supplements directly, salmonella can still spread through contact, especially if you fail to thoroughly wash your hands after touching contaminated products or surfaces they have come into contact with. In humans, signs of salmonella poisoning include:

  • Bloody diarrhea or diarrhea that lasts more than three days without improving
  • Diarrhea and a fever over 102 degrees Fahrenheit
  • Excessive vomiting, especially if it prevents you from keeping liquids down
  • Signs of dehydration, including dry mouth and throat, infrequent peeing and feeling dizzy when standing up
  • Stomach cramps

These symptoms most commonly occur between six hours and six days after exposure. Though most people recover in four to seven days, those with weakened immune systems, including children younger than 5 and adults over 65, may experience more severe symptoms that require medical treatment or hospitalization.  

Symptoms can vary in infected pets, who can be carriers even when they appear healthy and infect other animals or humans. Because of this, the FDA suggests pet owners contact their veterinarian as soon as possible if their dog consumed these products, even if they aren't showing signs of illness. Some salmonella symptoms to look for in your dog include:

  • Lethargy
  • Diarrhea or bloody diarrhea
  • Fever
  • Vomiting
  • Decreased appetite
  • Abdominal pain
