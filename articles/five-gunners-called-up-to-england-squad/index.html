Five members of our men’s first time have been named in Thomas Tuchel’s squad for the upcoming international break.

Ben White, Ezri Konsa, Myles Lewis-Skelly, Declan Rice, and Eberechi Eze have all got the nod to represent England in November.

The Three Lions host Croatia at 7.45pm on Thursday, November 12 at Wembley Stadium in the UEFA Nations League, before travelling to Spain for a 7.45pm kick-off on Sunday, November 15.

England are currently second in the group with three wins from four matches, while Spain are top on maximum points and Croatia sit third with one win and three defeats.

The quarter-finals will then take place in March, followed by the semi-finals and final in June.

Konsa has made 30 appearances for England since his debut in 2024, while Lewis-Skelly has earned 10 international caps under Tuchel.

Rice has 80 England games under his belt, White has six and Eze is on 25.
