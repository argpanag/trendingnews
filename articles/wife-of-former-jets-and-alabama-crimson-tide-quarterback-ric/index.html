Richard Todd was a three-year starting quarterback at Alabama from 1972 through 1975 under coach Paul "Bear" Bryant, winning four SEC championships and the 1973 co-national title before being named first-team All-SEC as a senior.

The Jets selected him sixth overall in the 1976 NFL Draft, and in his 10 seasons he led New York to playoff appearances in 1981 and 1982. In 1982, Todd led the Jets to the AFC Championship game against the Miami Dolphins.

Todd finished his career with 20,000 passing yards and 124 touchdown passes.
