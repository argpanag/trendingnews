Enthusiasts have launched Marvel Wolverine on PS5 Pro with DLSS 5 technology using the Nitlink utility.

TheTools · 12.11.2026 18:00 · source · permalink

Enthusiasts demonstrated a way to integrate Nvidia image scaling and frame generation technologies on the PlayStation 5 Pro console. The benchmark project was the action game Marvel Wolverine, running on the console and then transferred to an external PC for post-processing of the video stream. The implementation required an Elgato 4K X video capture card with HDR support, as well as a laptop equipped with a GeForce RTX 5070 Ti mobile graphics card and 16 GB of RAM.

The incoming video signal was processed in real time using specialized Nitlink software in conjunction with the DLSS 5 Manager utility. The user applied the neural network model version 310.8.3.0 in the Performance preset, activating the Live Flow Frame Gen feature. This algorithm enabled on-the-fly image upscaling and insertion of intermediate frames into the video captured from the console, ensuring a stable 60 FPS in full 4K resolution with HDR.

Despite the complexity of the connection chain, input lag remained between 4 and 6 ms, although some community members reported values ​​around 33 ms with other parameters. The main requirement for signal capture was disabling HDCP protection in the set-top box settings. The experiment demonstrated significant improvements in color correction, the stability of fine details, and scene depth. However, geometric and lighting artifacts typical of end-to-end external video processing are possible in dynamic scenes.
