FC 27 FUT Champs: Start time, end time, rewards and Champs Token Store explained

TheTools · 18.09.2026 00:07 · source · permalink

Get your Tokens here!

  • Alex Raisbeck

Weekend League is back, and we've got the lowdown for you on all things FUT Champs in FC 27.

This year's rewards have been completely redesigned, so there's plenty to brush up on!

Here's what you need to know about FC 27 FUT Champs including the weekly start time, what time it ends, and all the rewards for every rank.

What time does FUT Champs start and end in FC 27?

FUT Champs begins every week at 8am on Thursday morning and runs until 8am on Tuesday morning.

The only exception to this is the very first one of the year, which runs from Friday, so players have a bit longer to qualify.

How to qualify for FUT Champs in FC 27

To qualify for FUT Champs in FC 27, you must reach Division 6 in FUT Rivals and earn at least 1,000 Champions Qualification Points.

You can earn Qualification Points by playing matches in Rivals, with wins earning you bonus points.

That means, assuming you start off in a lower division, you're probably going to have more than enough Qualification Points to enter by the time you hit Division 6.

Once you've fulfilled the criteria, simply spend your points to enter the Weekend League – just remember, you'll need another 1,000 points next week to qualify again!

When are FUT Champs rewards released in FC 27?

FUT Champs rewards are available to you as soon as you finish all 15 of your matches.

If you don't finish them all, then they'll become available once that weekend's event comes to an end on Tuesday at 8am.

This is unlike the Division Rivals system, which requires you to wait for a specific time each week to receive your rewards.

FC 27 FUT Champs ranks and rewards

In FUT Champs, you'll have 15 matches to play, with each win netting you one point.

Unlike in previous years, where higher ranks receive special rewards like Packs, the system has been overhauled to reward players with Coins and Champions Tokens.

Champs Tokens can be spent in the Token Store for rewards like Packs, Evolutions and cosmetics, meaning even if you aren't finishing in Elite I every week, you can save up for the best rewards.

Here's a full list of all the ranks, their points requirements, and their respective rewards:

  • Elite I (15 Points): 450 Champions Tokens, 250,000 Coins
  • Elite II (14 Points): 350 Champions Tokens, 145,000 Coins
  • Elite III (13 Points): 275 Champions Tokens, 105,000 Coins
  • Elite IV (12 Points): 215 Champions Tokens, 75,000 Coins
  • Elite V (11 Points): 165 Champions Tokens, 50,000 Coins
  • Champion I (10 Points): 135 Champions Tokens, 35,000 Coins
  • Champion II (9 Points): 110 Champions Tokens, 25,000 Coins
  • Champion III (8 Points): 90 Champions Tokens, 15,000 Coins
  • Champion IV (7 Points): 75 Champions Tokens, 10,000 Coins
  • Champion V (6 Points): 60 Champions Tokens, 5,000 Coins
  • Contender I (5 Points): 50 Champions Tokens, 2,500 Coins
  • Contender II (4 Points): 40 Champions Tokens
  • Contender III (3 Points): 30 Champions Tokens
  • Contender IV (2 Points): 20 Champions Tokens
  • Contender V (1 Point): 15 Champions Tokens
