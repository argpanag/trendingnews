Cleveland Cavaliers vs. New York Knicks Live NBA Updates, Scores and Highlights

The New York Knicks play host to the Cleveland Cavaliers on Wednesday night in what will be the second Eastern Conference Finals rematch of the season.

Last time, the reigning NBA champs took care of business rather easily. And they are rolling overall. The Knicks enter having won four straight and six of their last seven.

Cleveland, meanwhile, is struggling to find its footing. The Cavs have lost four of their last five, the most recent of which was a meltdown against the reeling Charlotte Hornets.

Will the Knicks stay hot or will the Cavs climb out of this mini rut? Follow all the latest updates, highlights, analysis and takeaways from this one below!

Yeah, the Cavs Have Problems

Here's more evidence that Charles Barkley is right.

You can make the case that Evan Mobley spends a lot of time playing away from the basket. But it doesn't really excuse the returns when you look at how other teams with perimeter-oriented bigs still crash the offensive glass.

Cleveland, in fact, is playing one of them tonight. The Knicks remain the NBA's best offensive rebounding squad despite losing Mitchell Robinson to the Boston Celtics and having Karl-Anthony Towns run offensive sets from above the free-throw line.

Charles Barkley Has a Point About the Cavs

Despite having both Evan Mobley and Jarrett Allen, the Cavs rank in the bottom 10 of both offensive and defensive rebounding rate.

Granted, Allen's absence has skewed these returns. But Cleveland is still a below-average team on the defensive glass with both bigs on the floor and rates in the 3rd percentile of offensive rebounding to boot.
