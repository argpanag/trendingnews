Oasis to make huge announcement at 4pm today - live updates

TheTools · 14.09.2026 08:57 · source · permalink

Fans of the Manchester rock band are poised to find out whether tickets will be going on sale for a fresh round of shows, after their massive 2025 reunion tour

Oasis are widely expected to be announcing a new tour today, with fans poised for 4pm BST when the band will be sharing their news.

The Manchester rock legends teased the announcement last week with a spectacular drone display in Manchester, with the band’s logo showing up in lights along with the date 14/09/26 and the time 4pm BST.

Oasis have also been dropping hints about potential tour venues over the past few weeks on their social media channels, including the Etihad Stadium in Manchester, Celtic Park in Glasgow, and Knebworth in Hertfordshire.

Even before the band’s 2025 reunion concerts had concluded, fans and the media were speculating that once-warring brothers Noel and Liam Gallagher would be back on stage together again in the near future, such was the success of last year’s tour.

The announcement comes a week after Noel and Liam attended the UK premiere of the documentary film Oasis: Don’t Look Back in Anger from Steven Knight and co-directors Dylan Southern and Will Lovelace, showing behind-the-scenes footage and concert clips from the 2025 reunion.

Tickets to the Oasis Live ‘25 shows sold out within minutes of going on sale, with the tour estimated by Forbes to have generated over $400m (£293m) in revenue, and Noel and Liam each pocketing between $50m-$100m. As a result, the pair cracked the Sunday Times Rich List this year with a combined net worth of $375m.
