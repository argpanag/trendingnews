Xabi Alonso convinces £90m star to join Chelsea in January as verbal agreement struck

TheTools · 26.10.2026 13:53 · source · permalink

Chelsea have a verbal deal in place over personal terms with Alex Scott and plan to bring the Bournemouth midfielder to Stamford Bridge in the January transfer window for £90million, according to a journalist.

The Blues had a bid for Scott rejected by Bournemouth in the summer transfer window.

However, Chelsea, who appointed Xabi Alonso as the manager in the summer of 2026, are keen on a deal for the England international midfielder in January 2027.

Chelsea FC Journalist Simon Phillips, who has over 497,000 followers on X, said on October 19 that Alonso has spoken to Scott and has convinced him to join him at Stamford Bridge.

Xabi Alonso wants Alex Scott to join Chelsea

Phillips said: “Another player who will love Alonso is Alex Scott, and Alonso loves him as well.

“I have spoken about Scott so many times, and I will continue to put anything out that I hear on this situation because it is one of those where, as I keep saying, it’s going an, it’s inevitable that I feel that he will end up at Chelsea.

Phillips has now claimed that Chelsea plan to get a £90m deal done for Scott before the start of the January transfer window so that they can add him to Alonso’s squad.

The journalist has added that there is a verbal agreement in place between Chelsea and Scott over personal terms.

Alex Scott has verbal agreement with Chelsea

Phillips said on his YouTube channel: “Basically, I heard late last week that Chelsea now want to get the deal done before the transfer window opens.

“So, by the end of December, they want it all agreed, in place, so they can get him in at the start of the window, get him to help as soon as possible.

“You certainly need him. He keeps the ball well, keeps the ball very well, good passer of the ball, progressive with his passing and his running. So, yeah, he would certainly help in that sense.

“Yeah, Chelsea are looking to agree a fee with Bournemouth, believed to be about £90-£100million. I think we might see a £90m base fee.

“Everything on the player side is provisionally done or verbally done because Chelsea did all that in the summer.

“Alonso spoke to Scott himself and convinced him to come to Chelsea.

“It’s not done yet. It’s important to say it’s not done yet because other clubs are interested.

“Scott could easily change his mind, Chelsea have not agreed a fee with Bournemouth yet, so, it’s not done yet.

“Important to say that. Unfortunately, I always have to make sure I state that.

“But yeah, the terms are there on the table. Scott’s just got to sign the terms. He has agreed to them verbally.

“They fit within Chelsea’s wage structure. He will get a nice bonus for Champions League qualification this season, which most of the players are on actually.

“So, all of that side is good, all of that side is ready to go, and it’s just a case of Chelsea agreeing a fee with Bournemouth.

“But again, as I keep saying, it’s got that feel of inevitability about it.

“Scott has spoken with a number of the Chelsea players that are there already, so there’s already that, you know, setting up for him to become a Chelsea player in January.

“So, yeah, big, big progression there with that one. We just have got to wait now a few weeks to see if we can get that done.

“Hopefully we can get it over the line for the winter window.”
