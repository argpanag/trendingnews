Gasperini calls up 4 Primavera players vs Genoa

TheTools · 17.10.2026 11:38 · source · permalink

Roma enters the match against Genoa with five key absences and four young players from the Primavera called upon to lend a helping hand.

Gian Piero Gasperini has announced the squad for the seventh matchday of Serie A, which will see the Giallorossi face Daniele De Rossi’s side at the Olimpico.

As expected, Dybala, Koné, Cristante, and Svilar are not included in the squad, all struggling with their respective injuries. Malen joins them, having been forced to miss the match due to suspension.

These five unavailable players, especially, reduce the options available in midfield and attack.

To complete the group, Gasperini decided to draw from the Primavera, calling up Di Nunzio, Paratici, Arena and Camara.
