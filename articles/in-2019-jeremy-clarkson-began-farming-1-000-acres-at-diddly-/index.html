In 2019, Jeremy Clarkson began farming 1,000 acres at Diddly Squat, adding ponds and widening field margins. 6 years later, 45 bird species were recorded there

TheTools · 23.09.2026 00:12 · source · permalink

    At Jeremy Clarkson's Diddly Squat Farm, a survey unveiled forty-five different bird species, a testament to Clarkson's commitment to enhancing wildlife habitats. By introducing wider field margins and digging ponds, he supported both the farm’s ecosystem and local biodiversity.
