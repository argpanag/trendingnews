Severe delays as major route FLOODS in storm

TheTools · 23.10.2026 09:16 · source · permalink

Motorists are facing long delays after flooding forced the closure of two lanes on a busy stretch of the M25.

The M25 anticlockwise is affected between Junction 25 for the A10 at Enfield and Junction 24 for the A111 at Potters Bar, with lanes three and four closed due to flooding.

Congestion is stretching back to Junction 26 at Waltham Abbey, with traffic building on the approach to the affected section.

Drivers are currently facing travel times of around 35 minutes as a result of the lane closures.
