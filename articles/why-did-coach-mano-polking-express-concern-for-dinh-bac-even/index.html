Why did coach Mano Polking express concern for Dinh Bac even though he had just scored a spectacular goal?

TheTools · 18.11.2026 12:53 · source · permalink

HHTO - Having just scored a spectacular goal against the Kyrgyzstan national team, Nguyen Dinh Bac continues to be the center of attention. However, instead of being happy with his player's performance, coach Mano Polking expressed concern about the young striker.

On the evening of November 17th, Nguyen Dinh Bac made his mark by scoring the opening goal for the Vietnamese national team in a friendly match against Kyrgyzstan. The striker, born in 2004, executed a spectacular long-range shot, sending the ball into the opponent's net and receiving much praise from fans.

However, just one day later, Dinh Bac's name was mentioned again in a different story. At the press conference before the match between Hanoi Police FC and Johor Darul Ta'zim FC in the group stage of the Shopee Cup 2026/27, coach Mano Polking spent a lot of time sharing about the young striker's physical condition.

The coach admitted his concern that Dinh Bac could become overloaded if he continues to play at such a high frequency at both club and national team levels.

"I knew this question would come up, and it's a rather sensitive issue for me as a coach. First of all, more important than anything else is that all of us in this country love Dinh Bac. He's a very good guy and a great player," Coach Polking shared.

Why is coach Polking worried about Dinh Bac being overloaded?

The first reason lies in the 2004-born striker's demanding schedule. Dinh Bac is currently one of the key players for Hanoi Police FC and is regularly called up to the Vietnamese national team. Having to travel, train, and compete at two levels significantly reduces his recovery time.

Notably, Dinh Bac played the full 90 minutes in the Vietnam national team's match against Kyrgyzstan on the evening of November 17th. Meanwhile, Hanoi Police FC will host Johor Darul Ta'zim at 7:30 PM on November 19th in the group stage of the Shopee Cup 2026/27. Therefore, the young striker will only have a short time to recover before the club's next match.

Coach Polking argued that it's impossible to demand a player constantly play without rest. He emphasized that Dinh Bac is only 22 years old and would face health risks if he had to play around 50-55 matches per season.

"We can't play 50 consecutive matches. Nobody is a robot. Even Dinh Bac, despite being very fit, isn't a machine. He also needs rest," the head coach of Hanoi Police FC emphasized.

Earlier in November, coach Polking also expressed his wish that Dinh Bac not have to join the Vietnamese national team during the FIFA Days so that he could have more time to rest. However, he understood that the striker was an important player for the team and always longed to wear the national jersey.

How will coach Polking handle Dinh Bac?

A noteworthy detail in Coach Polking's statement is that he doesn't prioritize the club's interests over the national team. When asked which of the two levels is more important, the coach affirmed that the national team always comes first.

However, that doesn't mean Dinh Bac has to play in every match regardless of his physical condition. Coach Polking believes that a reasonable approach is needed to protect the player while ensuring the professional goals of Hanoi Police FC.

"I will carefully consider using him. The club and I will work together to develop the most reasonable plan, ensuring a balance of interests between the national team and the club," he said.

According to the schedule, after hosting Johor Darul Ta'zim, Hanoi Police FC still has to prepare for important matches against Vissel Kobe in the AFC Champions League Elite and Nam Dinh in the domestic league. The packed schedule means the coaching staff must be even more cautious in allocating playing time for key players.
