Talking points

Walking wounded

Nobody wants to see the FIBA Break more than Brisbane and Tasmania, with both teams patiently waiting for reinforcements to arrive.

While both rosters are missing some superstars, it’s been Tasmania that continues to find a way to fight, scrape and claw through adversity and stay competitive.

That trademark of JackJumpers basketball is exactly why this team is still sitting pretty towards the top end of the table.

Maestros at work

It’s two point guards at two very different points in their careers when Taran Armstrong and Raul Neto go head-to-head.

For the purists, their games share plenty of similarities, with both thriving off an extremely high basketball IQ.

The pass-first guards are key to their teams’ success, but who gets the upper hand in this one?
