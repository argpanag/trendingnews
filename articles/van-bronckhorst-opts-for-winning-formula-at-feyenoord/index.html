Van Bronckhorst opts for winning formula at Feyenoord

TheTools · 21.10.2026 19:16 · source · permalink

Feyenoord face Real Betis in the Champions League league phase on Wednesday evening. Coach Giovanni van Bronckhorst has settled on his starting XI for the 21:00 kick-off, sticking with the side that beat Como 3-2.

In goal, Tjark Ernst starts for Feyenoord, with Givairo Read at right-back and Javi López at left-back. Jeremiah St. Juste returns to the heart of the defence alongside Mike Marmol.

Oussama Targhalline takes Steijn's place in midfield after Steijn scored twice against Telstar. Charles Vanhoutte and Gjivai Zechiël complete Feyenoord's midfield in Spain.

On the right wing, Anis Hadj Moussa starts on Wednesday, while Luciano Valente returns on the left. Nacho Ferri leads the line in the visitors' three-forward system.

Real Betis line-up: not yet known

Feyenoord line-up: Ernst; Read, St. Juste, Mármol, López; Vanhoutte, Targhalline, Zechiël; Hadj Moussa, Ferri, Valente.
