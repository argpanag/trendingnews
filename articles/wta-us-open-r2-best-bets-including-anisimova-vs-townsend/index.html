WTA US Open R2 Best Bets Including Anisimova vs Townsend

TheTools · 02.09.2026 04:34 · source · permalink

The second round begins at the 2026 US Open. The Billie Jean King USTA National Tennis Center was pounded with rain on Tuesday, so while the second round will start on Wednesday, some first-round matches remain to be played. They will fill the earlier hours of the day. Then the second-round matches will be played on the outdoor courts. We are all waiting to see which women’s tennis stars will rise to the occasion over the next two weeks. So much is on the line in New York: the World No. 1 ranking, the last Major title of the year, spots in the top five, the race to the WTA Finals being held in Indian Wells this fall, and a lot more. Three different players have won Major titles this year, and neither of them is Aryna Sabalenka or Coco Gauff. Both desperately want to win a big title in 2026, and this is their last chance. Other players such as Naomi Osaka, who haven’t played in a Major final in quite some time, are hoping to get back to a championship level of play. The early rounds of the US Open will not tell us who will win the tournament, but they can eliminate some big-name players if they aren’t careful. We will see who avoids the big upset and who falls into the trap. LWOS has our best bets for three matches. Post your best bets and thoughts in the comments. 

WTA US Open

Tagger – Anisimova: TBA

H2H: 0-0

Amanda Anisimova made the final of the WTA US Open last year. She struggled earlier this season but is showing signs of picking up steam. She made the Cincinnati quarterfinals and lost to Jessica Pegula, who reached the final of that tournament. She is 18-8 on hardcourt this year. Her opponent, Lilli Tagger, is 24-8 on hardcourt this season, but part of that total is a number of wins in smaller tournaments. Tagger is 3-2 in her last five matches. Anisimova is 4-1.

Best Bet to Make

Tagger is playing well, but Anisimova is playing better. Anisimova should be able to win this WTA US Open match in straight sets, and the price is just barely good enough for us to make it our recommended standalone wager.

That being said, betting on Anisimova winning 2:0 is a value bet for sure.

Value bet/ the best odds: Anisimova winning 2:0 at 1.61 @1xBet  

Townsend – Preston: TBA

H2H: 0-0

Taylor Townsend is 4-3 in her last seven matches. Taylah Preston is 5-2. However, look at the three players who have recently beaten Townsend: Elena Rybakina, Belinda Bencic, and Iga Swiatek. Townsend is playing better than her record indicates. She is 14-7 on hardcourt this year. Taylah Preston is 29-13, the product of playing smaller tournaments such as the Vancouver WTA 125, which she won this summer. Townsend represents a tougher opponent than what she has recently faced.

Best Bet to Make

This WTA US Open match is another example of one player having a worse record based on playing tougher competition in bigger tournaments, compared to another player who racked up a lot of wins playing more small tournaments against weaker competition. We usually prefer the player who has played stronger opponents, even if the overall win-loss record isn’t as good. We are going with Townsend in straight sets as our recommended standalone wager.

That being said, betting on Townsend winning 2:0 is a value bet for sure.

Value bet/ the best odds: Townsend winning 2:0 at 1.73 @1xBet

Wang – Kalinskaya: TBA

H2H: 1-2

The 2026 WTA US Open second round includes this matchup of top-50 players. Xinyu Wang is 13-10 on hardcourt this year. Anna Kalinskaya is 14-10. Both players are 3-2 in their last five matches.

Best Bet to Make

This 2026 WTA US Open match is not easy to call. We ultimately think that since there’s no clear difference in form between the two players, we’re going to side with the favorite and the higher-ranked player. Kalinskaya will win in straight sets because we’re not convinced Wang will bring something special to this match.

That being said, betting on Kalinskaya winning 2:0 is a value bet for sure.

Value bet/ the best odds: Kalinskaya winning 2:0 at 2.10 @1xBet
