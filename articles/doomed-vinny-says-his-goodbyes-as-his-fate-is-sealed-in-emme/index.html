TheTools · 06.10.2026 22:00 · source · permalink

Vinny Dingle (Bradley Johnson) has been gearing himself up for this moment in Emmerdale – his verdict.

The meek and mildest of the Dingles, who’d just been about to get engaged and settle down with his boyfriend Lewis Barton (Bradley Riches,) turned his whole life upside down that fateful day he answered his phone while driving.

That seemingly innocent call set off a horrible chain of events, that distracted him from driving, forced Jimmy off the road, and left six kids without a parent after Jimmy (Nick Miles) and Dawn (Olivia Bromley) died as a result of Vinny’s actions.

Vinny didn’t even stop to see if Jimmy was ok.

He and Gabby Thomas (Rosie Bentham) – the person at the other end of the call – then decided to keep it all a secret, meaning when it all eventually came out, the blowback was 10 times worse than it could have been. Nicola (Nicola Wheeler) and Joe Tate (Ned Porteous) are gunning for him, and Lewis dumped him.
