LoL Worlds 2026 — Gen.G and Hanwha Life Draw LPL Rivals in Swiss Stage 1

TheTools · 21.10.2026 23:10 · source · permalink

The LoL Worlds 2026 Play-In Stage has concluded, setting the final 16 teams for the Swiss Stage. The official 10th-anniversary style draw has sparked intense interest across global esports communities as the first-round matchups for LCK teams reveal distinct contrasting paths.

LCK Top Seeds Face LPL Giants in Opening Round

While top-seeded LCK championship contenders are slated for brutal head-to-head battles against powerhouse teams from China’s LPL, T1—entering as the LCK 3rd seed—will kick off the Swiss Stage against a representative from a different region.

Immediately following the conclusion of the Play-In stage on the 19th, the Swiss Stage Round 1 draw pitted LCK 1st and 2nd seeds Gen.G and Hanwha Life Esports directly against LPL squads. This early clash between the historic regional rivals will serve as a crucial barometer for the 2026 competitive landscape.

Gen.G will square off against LPL 4th seed Invictus Gaming (IG), while Hanwha Life Esports will face LPL 3rd seed Top Esports (TES). Both matches promise unrelenting action where early-game momentum will dictate the victors.

T1 and DK Matchups Set for Swiss Stage Opening

T1, competing under the LCK 3rd seed banner, will open their campaign against CBLOL 1st seed LOS. Unlike other groups featuring heavy regional collisions, T1 enters a calmer initial phase. Meanwhile, Dplus KIA (DK) faces a formidable test against European powerhouse G2 Esports.

Meanwhile, LEC’s Karmine Corp surged into the Swiss Stage after sweeping the Play-In bracket 3-0. Korean standouts ‘Yeon’ Kang Ye-hou, ‘Canna’ Chang-dong Kim, and ‘Caliste’ dominated the series to sweep Player of the Series honors. Karmine Corp will now face LPL 1st seed Anyone’s Legend (AL) in the Swiss Stage.

Swiss Stage Format and Schedule

Featuring 16 teams vying for eight Knockout Stage berths, the Swiss Stage kicks off on October 24 at the Credit Union of Texas Event Center with the opening match between Gen.G and IG. Teams with identical records will face off in mostly single-game matches, while advancement and elimination matches will be contested in best-of-three series.

With high-stakes regional showdowns and unexpected cross-region matchups, fans eagerly anticipate how LCK teams will perform in their quest for global supremacy.
