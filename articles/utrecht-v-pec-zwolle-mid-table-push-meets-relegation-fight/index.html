Utrecht v PEC Zwolle: mid-table push meets relegation fight

Utrecht host PEC Zwolle in the Eredivisie on Saturday, 24 October 2026 at Stadion Galgenwaard, with the hosts eyeing a climb from the lower half and the visitors looking to put daylight between themselves and the bottom three.

Utrecht's recent uptick includes wins over FC Twente and Willem II after heavy defeats earlier in the campaign, and they drew 1-1 the last time these sides met. Zwolle halted a three-game losing run with a 4-1 victory against Go Ahead Eagles but remain in the bottom five.

OneFootball Videos

Availability could shape the contest. Utrecht are without Vasilis Barkas, Victor Jensen, Kevin Paredes, Marius Broholm, Alonzo Engwanda and Artem Stepanov. Stepanov is among this season's leading scorers for the hosts, so Ángel Alarcón and Yoann Cathline may carry more of the attacking load. Zwolle are missing Younes Namli and right back Sherel Floranus.

The numbers point to chances. Utrecht have scored 16 league goals but are yet to keep a clean sheet, conceding 26 across nine games. They deliver accurate service from wide areas, completing 23.81% of 105 open-play crosses, and all their goals have come from inside the box, which could test a Zwolle defence that has shipped 22. For Zwolle, Koen Kostons has four league goals and Dylan Mbayo leads their creativity with four assists. Zwolle's shooting accuracy of 50.65% suggests they can punish lapses even with less of the ball.

Utrecht sit 11th on 11 points and a win would move them to 14, potentially nudging them towards the top half. Zwolle are 15th on seven points and victory would lift them to 10, strengthening their cushion above the relegation places.
