The SENSEX Index Closes 0.16% Lower

TheTools · 11.09.2026 21:40 · source · permalink

The SENSEX Index decreased 121 points or 0.16 percent on Friday to close at 74782 points.

The decline was led by Tata Steel (-2.25%), Indusind Bank (-1.52%) and Maruti Suzuki (-1.25%).

On the upside, the strongest performers were HDFC Bank (2.12%), Tech Mahindra (0.86%) and ITC (0.83%).
