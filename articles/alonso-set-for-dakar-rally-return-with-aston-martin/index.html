Alonso set for Dakar Rally return with Aston Martin

TheTools · 17.11.2026 21:38 · source · permalink

Fernando Alonso will compete in the 2027 edition of the Dakar Rally for Aston Martin.

Fernando Alonso will compete in the Dakar Rally for Aston Martin in January 2027, marking his return to the event after seven years.

Alonso is set to drive the Aston Martin Dreadnought as part of a three-car challenge alongside nine-time World Rally Champion Sebastien Loeb and reigning World Rally Raid Champion Lucas Moraes.

The Spaniard will be joined by co-driver Marc Coma, a five-time Dakar motorcycle category winner who sat alongside Alonso during his 2020 debut in the event – in which he finished 13th overall.

With the 2027 Dakar taking place in the Sahara Desert between January 9 and 23 – beginning in Portugal and then racing through Morocco – Alonso will then turn his focus to the 2027 F1 season, with pre-season testing scheduled for February before the campaign gets underway in March.

"After deciding to continue racing in Formula 1 with the Aston Martin Aramco, I thought carefully about Dakar," said Alonso. "In the end, the decision became simple. I wanted to embrace the challenge completely and take on both Formula 1 and the Dakar Rally in the same year.

"In 2020, Dakar was one of the most challenging and enjoyable experiences of my career. I have enormous respect for this race and the specialists who have mastered it. Having Marc alongside me again is very important, and with Aston Martin, Prodrive, Sebastien and Lucas we have an exceptional team.

"Formula 1 remains my priority. But I am a racing driver, and challenges like this are why I love competing. I’m looking forward to seeing what we can achieve."

Alonso’s previous participation in the Dakar Rally occurred during his two-year hiatus from Formula 1 between 2019 and 2020, with the Spaniard also taking part in other feats such as the Indianapolis 500 as well as winning the World Endurance Championship.
