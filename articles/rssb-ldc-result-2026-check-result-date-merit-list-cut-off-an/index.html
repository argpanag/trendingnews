RSSB LDC Result 2026 - Check Result Date, Merit List, Cut Off and Next Stage

TheTools · 28.09.2026 15:09 · source · permalink

Share

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) is scheduled to announce the RSSB LDC Result 2026 for the written examination on 28 September 2026. The recruitment examination was conducted for 10,644 vacancies for Lower Division Clerk (LDC), also known as Clerk Grade-II, and Junior Assistant posts. The written exam was held on 5 July 2026 in offline OMR-based mode.

Candidates will be able to check their results through the official RSSB website. The result will be published as a PDF containing roll numbers of the candidates shortlisted for the next stage.

Candidates should keep the following recruitment and result-related dates in mind. The written examination has already been conducted, while the result is scheduled for release on 28 September 2026.

The RSSB LDC Result 2026 PDF is scheduled to be activated on 28 September 2026. Once available, candidates can access it through the official RSSB website.

The result PDF will contain the roll numbers of candidates shortlisted after the written examination. Candidates can use the Ctrl+F function in the PDF to quickly locate their roll number.

RSSB LDC Official Website Link

Candidates can follow these steps to check their result after the result link becomes active:

Visit the official RSSB website at rssb.rajasthan.gov.in.

Go to the Results or Notice Board section.

Find and select the link for RSSB LDC Clerk Grade-II/Junior Assistant Result 2026.

The result PDF will appear on the screen.

Use Ctrl+F and enter your roll number to check whether your roll number is listed.

Save the result PDF or take a screenshot for future reference.

Candidates who qualify the written examination will move to the next stage of the selection process. The next stage is the Hindi and English Typing Test, followed by document verification.

The exact date of the typing test has not been confirmed in the provided information. Therefore, candidates should continue checking the official RSSB website for further updates regarding the schedule.
