Experts release Bills vs. Lions prediction as two new Lions injuries shift the market

TheTools · 17.09.2026 18:59 · source · permalink

The Buffalo Bills host the Detroit Lions on Thursday, September 17, 2026, with kickoff set for 8:15 PM EDT at Highmark Stadium. Both teams enter 1-0 after Buffalo’s 36-31 win over the Texans and Detroit’s overtime escape against the Saints, but the prediction market has moved firmly toward the Bills, with Polymarket pricing Buffalo at 67¢ to win compared to 34¢ for Detroit.

Two Lions starters carry questionable tags into Thursday night

Guard Christian Mahogany (knee) and cornerback D.J. Reed (foot) are both listed as questionable heading into the short week. Mahogany underwent imaging that came back negative following Sunday’s overtime win over the Saints — the same game in which he picked up the injury — according to Nolan Bianchi of The Detroit News. A clean scan is the best-case scenario for a quick turnaround, though the questionable tag means his availability may not be settled until close to kickoff. The 25-year-old, a 2024 sixth-round pick out of Boston College, starts on an interior offensive line that will be facing a hostile road environment in Orchard Park.

Reed was limited at Tuesday’s practice, per Tim Twentyman of the Lions’ official site. The 29-year-old opened his 2026 season with seven solo tackles and a pass defensed in Week 1, and on a short week, that limited session is about the only real data point available before game day — notable given how much a secondary absence could matter against a Buffalo offense that put up 36 points in its opener.

How the market has moved

Dimers’ Sina Torki writes that Buffalo’s line sat in a tight band around 60-62% through late August and into early September before a sharp move in recent days pushed the Bills up to their current 67% on Polymarket — a shift that tracks closely with Detroit’s injury news becoming public. The moneyline market alone has traded $18.3K in volume.

The spread tells a different story than the moneyline, though. At Detroit +4.5 (50¢) against Buffalo -4.5 (51¢), it’s the deepest market of the three at $53.3K in volume and priced about as close to a coin flip as it gets. As Torki puts it, “traders are confident Buffalo wins, but far less confident they cover.” The total sits at 54.5, split between 50¢ on the over and 52¢ on the under, a nod to two offenses that combined for 67 points in their Week 1 wins.

Dimers’ own win-probability model lands close to the market, giving Buffalo a 65.9% chance to win against 34.1% for Detroit.

What’s driving the line

A few factors are shaping how traders are pricing the game:

  • Injury designations and inactives — Questionable tags like Mahogany’s and Reed’s are among the fastest-moving inputs, and Thursday’s official inactives list, released roughly 90 minutes before kickoff, often triggers the sharpest repricing of the day.
  • Practice participation — On a short week, full, limited, or did-not-participate designations are the best signal available, which is part of why Reed’s limited Tuesday session matters to traders.
  • Home-field and travel — A Thursday night road game on a short week is consistently priced as a meaningful disadvantage, and Detroit is walking into exactly that in Orchard Park.
  • Week 1 performance — Buffalo won comfortably while Detroit needed overtime, and traders are factoring that difference into how they read each team’s underlying strength.
  • Weather — Conditions in Western New York can move the total market quickly, particularly as the season goes on.
