ATP Tokyo Day 4 Predictions Including Alcaraz vs Arnaldi

TheTools · 02.10.2026 19:22 · source · permalink

Day 4 at ATP Tokyo will feature the conclusion of the round of 16, with #1 seed Carlos Alcaraz as the main headline, but a few other very interesting matches in prospect. It will surely be another fantastic day of tennis in the Japanese capital as we head into the decisive stages of the tournament.

ATP Tokyo Day 4 Predictions

Denis Shapovalov vs Alejandro Tabilo

Head-to-head: first meeting

Shapovalov is an extremely inconsistent player overall, but he has been playing decently recently. He has far bigger weapons than Tabilo, and the outcome will mostly depend on what level he brings to the court. That is always hard to predict, but given how he’s been playing lately, Shapovalov should have the slight edge here.

Prediction: Shapovalov in 3

Matteo Berrettini vs Adolfo Daniel Vallejo

Head-to-head: first meeting

While Berrettini is far from his best, on a fast hard court his serve should overwhelm Vallejo. The latter has been playing well so far at ATP Tokyo, coming all the way from qualifying and scoring a fantastic first-round win over Rafael Jodar. Still, under normal circumstances this should be the end of the line for him.

Prediction: Berrettini in 2

Ugo Humbert vs Jiri Lehecka

Head-to-head: Humbert 1-0 Lehecka

Humbert is a former finalist (who had championship point) at this event. Still, he is far from the level he was then, while Lehecka is arguably having the best year of his career so far, despite remaining very inconsistent. It should be a very close match decided by small details, but at this point Lehecka should have a slight edge.

Prediction: Lehecka in 3

Carlos Alcaraz vs Matteo Arnaldi

Head-to-head: Alcaraz 2-0 Arnaldi

Arnaldi’s heroics at Roland Garros were incredible, but he hasn’t really done much since and has generally struggled to get strong results on clay. While Alcaraz seems more vulnerable than usual at the moment, having only recently returned from injury and not playing his best tennis yet, it is highly unlikely that Arnaldi will be the one to stop him at ATP Tokyo.

Prediction: Alcaraz in 2
