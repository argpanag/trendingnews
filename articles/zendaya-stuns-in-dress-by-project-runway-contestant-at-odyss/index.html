Before fully stepping into "Dune" mode, Zendaya revisited "The Odyssey" with one last Athena-coded look.

Ahead of the Governors Awards Sunday, Nov. 15, in Los Angeles, Zendaya attended a special For Your Consideration screening of Christopher Nolan's "The Odyssey" at the Academy Museum's David Geffen Theater on Saturday, Nov 14. The screening event also featured a Q+A panel with the cast, where the actress was joined by husband Tom Holland and their co-stars Matt Damon, John Leguizamo, Charlize Theron, Elliot Page and Himesh Patel.

For the event, the star made an ethereal but elegant statement in a cream-and-ivory lace off-the-shoulder corset dress with a high-low skirt and asymmetrical hem. In an Instagram Story over the week, Law Roach, Zendaya's longtime stylist, revealed that "Project Runway" contestant Jeffrey Kelly Abess designed the custom look. And while the recent Season 22 contestant was eliminated before the finale, he shared a touching goodbye with Roach, who said at the time that he hoped to work with him in the future.

"I want to work with you," Roach told Kelly on the competition series following the designer's elimination. "I'll be calling you. Trust me."

And call he did. Sharing photos on his Instagram, Kelly wrote, "@zendaya in custom @jeffreykellydesigns couture, designed and handmade by me...Thank you so much to Law for trusting me with this one!! And thank you, Z, for making this dress even prettier."

Whites and neutrals reigned supreme in the star's "Odyssey" press tour wardrobe over the summer. The star channeled her inner Greek goddess in a series of whimsical looks that were worthy of Athena (not to mention a nod to the star's bridal era − as the actress and Holland confirmed their marriage this summer). Zendaya's look at the weekend screening was a nod back to her Athena era and seemingly her last goddess-inspired look before stepping into the desert sands of "Dune: Part Three."

Zendaya shows off her first 'Dune: Part Three' look

At the 2026 Governors Awards, where the Academy's honorary Oscars are presented, on Nov. 15 in Los Angeles, Zendaya used the navy carpet to showcase her first look of her "Dune" press tour.

The star dazzled in a dusty-gold archival Giles Deacon gown from the designer's fall-winter 2007 collection. The vintage look featured a strapless corset top with structural bustles on either side of the hip and a column skirt adorned with a glittering fringe overlay. She styled the look with a vintage Rolex watch and dangling Chopard earrings that matched the gown's golden hue.

"Dune" will close out the star's epic run of press tours from this year, which included "The Drama," "Spider-Man: Brand New Day" and "The Odyssey." Zendaya stars in the film with another A-list-filled cast, including Timothée Chalamet, Florence Pugh, Javier Bardem, Anya Taylor-Joy and the newly named James Bond, Robert Pattinson.

Over the years, Zendaya's "Dune" press tour looks have been praised as some of fashion's most iconic moments, from the revival of the silver robot-inspired Mugler armor to her custom nude-colored Balmain wet-look gown. With the highly anticipated film premiering on Dec. 18, be sure to expect another parade of Roach and Zendaya fashions for the ages.
