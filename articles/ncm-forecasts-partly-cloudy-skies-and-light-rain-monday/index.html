NCM forecasts partly cloudy skies and light rain Monday

TheTools · 08.11.2026 23:38 · source · permalink
  • FAJAR
  • Duhur
  • Asr
  • Maghrib
  • Isha

Winds will blow from the southeast to the northwest at light to moderate speeds of 10 to 25 km/h, occasionally freshening to reach 35 km/h.

Sea conditions in the Arabian Gulf will be slight to moderate, becoming rough at times by Tuesday morning. High tides are expected at 12:39 and 01:50, while low tides will occur at 19:32 and 06:40.

Meanwhile, the Sea of Oman will remain slight throughout the day, with high tides at 08:43 and 22:35, and low tides at 15:32 and 04:13.
