Russian drone flies into Poland during overnight strikes on Ukraine, Warsaw says

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:08 · source · permalink

The drone was not shot down as it "changed course to head east," Warsaw said.

LONDON -- A Russian drone violated Polish airspace late on Monday night during Moscow's latest wave of long-range strikes on Ukraine, the Polish military said, in a week that has seen multiple incidents related to the four-year-old conflict reported in theaters far from the frontline.

Ukraine's air force said Russia launched 96 drones, of which 64 were jet-powered, into the country overnight. Air defenders downed or suppressed 73 drones, of which 45 were jet-powered, the air force said. Impacts were reported across 13 locations.

Poland's Armed Forces Operational Command said in a post to X that "a Russian unmanned aerial vehicle briefly violated Polish airspace" during the overnight attacks while targeting western Ukraine.

The command said it "activated the necessary air defense forces and assets" and gave permission for the drone to be shot down. "The object was continuously tracked by military radar systems throughout its flight path," the command said.

"After the unmanned aerial vehicle changed course to head east, taking into account the potential threat to civilians and the possible consequences of shooting down the object, a decision was made to refrain from using weapons due to the continuing risk of causing damage," the command said.

Russian missiles and drones have violated Polish airspace multiple times since the start of Moscow's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022.

Elsewhere, NATO allies have reported confirmed or suspected Russian drone violations in Romania, Lithuania and Latvia. Russian drones have also been discovered in Moldova, which is not a NATO member. Russian officials have denied responsibility for all such incursions.

The most notable recent example saw three Russian cruise missiles land on Polish territory during attacks on Ukraine earlier this month. The incident prompted an emergency meeting of NATO members on Monday, with the alliance agreeing to deploy an AWACS early-warning aircraft deployment to Poland.

On Tuesday, Polish Defense Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz said Polish officials were due to meet on several security issues including "protecting Polish airspace, violations of our airspace, NATO support -- early warning aircraft and a permanent U.S. military base in Poland."

As the war approaches its five-year anniversary, its ripples have spread far beyond southern and eastern Ukraine, where the fighting is concentrated.

This week, for example, Ukraine claimed naval drone attacks on a Russian warship and a cargo vessel in the Libyan port of Benghazi.

Also this week, authorities and analysts reported two drone attacks on Russian cargo ships in the Baltic Sea.
