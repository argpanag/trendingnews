These 5 singers just made the top 10 on ‘The Voice’

TheTools · 13.11.2026 05:12 · source · permalink

“The Voice” has reached the live portion of the competition — and it’s time for viewers to weigh in on their favorite singers.

Currently, the show is narrowing the Season 30 competition down to a top 10. Here’s a look at the five singers viewers who have already secured their spot.

Team Queen Latifah

Mia Knight

Mia Knight got three coaches to turn around for her during her blind audition. But first-time “Voice” coach Queen Latifah jumped out of her seat when she learned Knight’s age.

“I cannot believe you’re 16 years old,” Latifah said following the New Jersey teen’s performance of Sienna Spiro’s “You Stole the Show.” “You own this stage like nobody’s business.”

In her coaching pitch, Kelly Clarkson noted that she’s won “The Voice” twice with teenage singers.

“I love working with really young artists because there’s no expectation, there’s just joy, there’s just excitement,” she said. “It’s almost like y’all don’t limit yourselves. It makes it really fun and inspiring for me as well.”

Initially, Knight had her pick of Latifah, Clarkson and country singer Riley Green. But Latifah ultimately took the choice away, taking advantage of a new feature this season called the “first-in button” that allows a coach to automatically snag a singer for their team if they’re the first to turn around.

Since the audition, Knight has continued to display powerhouse vocals. Green called her Battle performance of the hit song “Never Enough” from “The Greatest Showman” “shocking.”

“I didn’t know people could sing like that in real life,” he joked.

Knight survived the competition’s Cut Week, and was one of two singers Latifah chose to automatically advance to the live shows, where she took on a performance of Clarkson’s “Miss Independent” — and got the approval of Clarkson herself.

“That was awesome,” Clarkson said.

BigCountry

BigCountry, a 37-year-old U.S. Army veteran from Tacoma, Washington, got instant chair turns from Latifah and Adam Levine, and then a third chair turn from Green, with his high-energy performance of “I Put a Spell On You” during his blind audition.

“I know when I turn around and see somebody that can win this thing,” Levine said after the performance, which has over 1 million views on the official “Voice” YouTube channel.

“I wanna see you win this,” Latifah added.

After selecting Latifah to be his coach, BigCountry moved forward on the show with a performance of “Tennessee Whiskey” during the Battle round.

“Your voice is big, your performance is big, your heart is big,” Latifah said as she declared him the winner of the round.

BigCountry went on to survive Cut Week, where he was one of two singers Latifah immediately sent through to the live shows. His rendition of Bon Jovi’s “It’s My Life” during the first live show secured him a spot in the top 10.

Team Adam Levine

Dakota Cohen

Over a year ago, Dakota Cohen sang Maroon 5’s “Sunday Morning” during a set at a restaurant in Montecito, California — and Levine, the band’s frontman, happened to be in attendance.

Levine gave her his approval in the form of a thumbs-up.

Now, Cohen has joined Levine’s team on “The Voice.”

The 25-year-old singer from Los Angeles instantly caught Levine’s attention with her unique song selection: Black Sabbath’s “Changes.”

“I’ve never heard anybody cover that song,” the Maroon 5 singer said. “That’s instantly something out the gate, before I hear you sing a note, that I want to be a part of.”

During every round of the competition so far, Levine has compared Cohen’s vocal style to Miley Cyrus — who he called “one of the best vocalists on the planet.”

Performances from Cohen have included Evanescence’s “Bring Me to Life” during the Battle round and Fuel’s “Hemorrhage (In My Hands)” — which earned her a spot in the first live show.

“There’s nobody on my team like Dakota,” Levine said following that performance. “She’s just carrying the rock torch for me and has such a cool, interesting voice.”

Most recently, Cohen earned a spot in the top 10 with a performance of Aerosmith’s “I Don’t Want to Miss a Thing.”

Connor Closs

Connor Closs has been singing his whole life, including in a local band and on a cruise ship. Now, he’s made it to the top 10 of “The Voice.”

Closs, a 23-year-old singer from Middletown, New Jersey, caught the attention of Levine and Green with his rendition of Hozier’s “Too Sweet” during the blind audition round.

“That’s a tough song, and you did it justice,” Green said.

Levine, in particular, was drawn to Closs because he said he could hear some of his own style and artistry in him.

Closs chose to team up with Levine, who described his new team member as having “one of the most unique” voices on the show.

During the Battle round, Closs went up against Utah singer Cal Cook. Their duet of sombr’s “Homewrecker” got a standing ovation from both Clarkson and Levine, with their coach calling the performance “dead even.”

After some deliberation, though, Levine chose to send Closs through to the next round.

During Cut Week, Closs was one of two singers Levine chose to automatically advance to the live shows.

Closs secured his spot in the top 10 with a performance of NSYNC’s “This I Promise You.”

“You are a superstar,” Levine said.

Lexi Stephens

Viewers didn’t see much of Lexi Stephens when she tried out for Season 21 of “American Idol” back in 2023. Her audition didn’t air, and she didn’t advance after the following round.

Now, the 23-year-old singer from Albany, New York, has made it to the top 10 on “The Voice.”

Stephens got a four-chair turn — and a standing ovation from all of the coaches — with her rendition of Sienna Spiro’s “Die on this Hill” during her blind audition.

“That was by far the best blind audition,” Levine said. “I think you’re going to probably win ‘The Voice.’ You are the one.”

Although Stephens admitted she had gone on the show with Clarkson in mind as her coach, Levine’s pitch and bold declaration swayed her.

But Stephens still got high praise from Clarkson after her performance of Paramore’s “All I Wanted” during the Battle round.

“You are like the mountain lions I see on my property,” Clarkson said. “You are ferocious.”

Stephens survived Cut Week, where she was one of two singers Levine automatically sent through to the live portion of the competition.

She has now secured a spot in the top 10 with a performance of Avril Lavigne’s “I’m With You.”

What happens next on ‘The Voice’?

On Monday, Nov. 16, the seven singers from Clarkson and Green’s teams (four on Clarkson’s team and three on Green’s team) will compete for a spot in the top 10.

Voting from viewers determines the five singers who will round out the top 10.

The one-hour episode airs 8 p.m. MST on NBC.
