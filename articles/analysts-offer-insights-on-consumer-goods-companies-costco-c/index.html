Analysts Offer Insights on Consumer Goods Companies: Costco (COST) and Sheng Siong Group Ltd. (SG:OV8)

TheTools · 12.10.2026 07:09 · source · permalink

Analysts fell to the sidelines weighing in on Costco (COST) and Sheng Siong Group Ltd. (SG:OV8) with neutral ratings, indicating that the experts are neither bullish nor bearish on the stocks.

Sheng Siong Group Ltd. (SG:OV8)

Maybank analyst Maybank Research maintained a Hold rating on Sheng Siong Group Ltd. yesterday and set a price target of S$3.12. The company’s shares closed last Friday at S$2.92.

Currently, the analyst consensus on Sheng Siong Group Ltd. is a Moderate Buy with an average price target of S$3.35.
