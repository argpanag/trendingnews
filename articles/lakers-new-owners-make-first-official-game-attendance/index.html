Lakers' new owners Josh Kushner and Bob Iger are in attendance for the Lakers' Monday home game against the Jazz, marking their first time watching the team in person.

Hosting the Utah Jazz on Monday night, the Los Angeles Lakers will enjoy special support for themselves from their new ownership.

According to ESPN's Ramona Shelburne, Josh Kushner and Bob Iger are both in attendance for the Lakers' home matchup against the Jazz, marking their first time watching the team in person since their historic purchase.

Kushner was also seen walking through the Lakers' behind-the-scenes areas with Rob Pelinka, according to ESPN's Dave McMenamin.

Thrive Capital founder Josh Kushner and former Disney CEO Bob Iger have teamed up to acquire the Los Angeles Lakers' majority stake from Mark Walter for a record-breaking $12.5 billion last August.

Although the terms of the deal have already been finalized, the sale is still awaiting formal approval from the NBA Board of Governors before Kushner and Iger can officially take over as the franchise's new owners.

While awaiting approval for their historic purchase, Kushner and Iger will get their first taste of the LA crowd this Monday as the Lakers continue their strong start to the season.

Entering their home game against the Jazz, the Lakers are tied for the best record in the NBA at 14-3, with Luka Doncic leading the way with MVP-caliber averages of 37.2 points, 6.8 rebounds, and 7.7 assists per game.
