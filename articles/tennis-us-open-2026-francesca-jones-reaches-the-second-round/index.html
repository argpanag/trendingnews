Francesca Jones v Iva Jovic | H2H & Match Fundamentals

Francesca Jones will face Iva Jovic in the second round, her best showing in New York so far.

 

Tale of the Tape – Key Stats (2026)

Jones is definitely the underdog here, but she has proved her worth to make it this far, so Jovic should not take her edge for granted.

 

 

Playing Styles & Prediction

Jovi. Started the year really well in Australia, reaching the final in Hobart and the quarter-finals at the Australian Open, and was fairly steady over the clay court season and notched up some good results on grass. All of that has lifted her into the top 15.

Jones has all the grit and determination, but the key question has always been her stamina the further she goes. So often she has had some landmark wins, only to have to retire or for her fitness to fail her.

Jones certainly has the capability to upset the apple cart here, but it might be another three setter.

Prediction: Jones in three sets.
