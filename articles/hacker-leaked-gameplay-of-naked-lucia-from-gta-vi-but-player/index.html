Hacker leaked gameplay of naked Lucia from GTA VI, but players' attention was not drawn to the heroine's body

TheTools · 12.10.2026 02:46 · source · permalink

Jason and Lucia use different fighting styles.

A hacker (or group) under the nickname Cyberleek convinced players to inflate the value of his memecoin. The capitalization of $CYBERLEEK has already exceeded first 4, and then 6 million dollars — for this, the hacker promised to reward them by publishing gameplay of naked Lucia, as well as presenting materials with the heroine in 4K resolution and with close-ups.

Due to copyright, we cannot present the recording here, but copies have already spread across social networks. Take-Two failed to remove everything, and therefore players have already managed to examine Lucia's model from different angles.

The leak also revealed that body hair was implemented for the model (there is no information yet about the possibility of an “intimate trim”).

But players were looking at more than just Lucia's body. For example, ZamnBoii noted that the heroine “hits the enemy with the back of her hand and uses wrestling moves” — her fighting style differs from the one Jason used.

During close combat, the heroine uses the environment: near an ATM, she hit an NPC against a wall.

Cyberleek also showed a mini-game with parachute flying: players will have to fly through “rings”. If you miss and crash into a building, the character falls down and hits the ground, and then the familiar [WASTED] message appears.

Discussion participants noted that the characters do not react to naked Lucia in any way, so they express the opinion that Rockstar Games will remove the ability to completely undress characters. It is assumed that such “naked models” are part of the developers' build for debugging the game.

Grand Theft Auto VI is scheduled for release on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S consoles.
