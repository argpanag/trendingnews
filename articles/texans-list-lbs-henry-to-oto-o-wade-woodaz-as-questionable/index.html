Texans list LBs Henry To'oTo'o, Wade Woodaz as questionable

TheTools · 16.10.2026 19:14 · source · permalink

The Texans already ruled out three players who didn’t make the trip to London.

Wide receiver Tank Dell (knee), running back David Montgomery (groin) and safety Jalen Pitre (shoulder) remain in Houston to rehab.

Linebacker Henry To’oTo’o (shoulder) and linebacker Wade Woodaz (thumb) are questionable.

Defensive end Jadeveon Clowney (knee/rest) did not practice Friday but has no injury designation.

Linebacker Azeel Al-Shaair (groin) and linebacker Jake Hansen (illness) returned to full participation on Friday, and they also do not have a status for Sunday’s game against the Jaguars.
