Rain returns to Philly region after 17 days

TheTools · 06.11.2026 10:34 · source · permalink

The rain will finally return to the Philadelphia region on Thursday with lingering showers on Friday before a sunny but cooler weekend.

Wednesday is unseasonably warm and mostly sunny with highs in the mid-70s, which is 15 degrees above normal.

Wednesday night, clouds move in and rain arrives in the Lehigh Valley around 4 a.m. Lows will be in the lower 60s.

Thursday will feature on and off again showers with a break from the rain during the early afternoon and highs in the low 70s.

Early Friday, we'll see a passing shower and possible thunderstorms. The clouds will then slowly clear during the afternoon from west to east. Highs will be around 70 degrees.

During the weekend, conditions will be cooler but dry. Saturday will be mostly sunny and breezy with highs around 60. Sunday, conditions will be pleasant for the Eagles game with highs around 60 and mostly sunny skies.

Monday will be mostly sunny and dry with highs around 60 degrees while Tuesday will be mild as a cold front approaches with highs in the mid to upper 60s and sunny skies.
