Cyberpunk 2077 Update 2.5 Leaks Early, Brings Console Mods and Dual-Wielding Pistols

TheTools · 21.10.2026 09:20 · source · permalink

Tuesday, October 20th 2026 Cyberpunk 2077 Update 2.5 Leaks Early, Brings Console Mods and Dual-Wielding Pistols CD Projekt RED's Cyberpunk 2077 Update 2.5 isn't out until October 22, but most of its content has already leaked. YouTuber WesNemo apparently published a video covering the update too early, and while it has since been pulled, players on r/GamingLeaksAndRumours had already summarized it. According to WesNemo, CD Projekt gave him and other creators early access to the update ahead of its official presentation. The studio itself has only confirmed dual-wielding and cyberware customization so far, so treat the rest as unofficial until the patch notes go live. Apparently, dual-wielding is limited to pistols and comes through a new "Double Trouble" perk in the Cool skill tree. The leak also lists a new Edgerunners 2 gig called "Clampdown," which reportedly acts as a prequel to the anime that premiered on Netflix on October 20, plus a mystery Night City tour sidequest. Glamdoc shops will let players change the look of arm cyberware like Mantis Blades and Gorilla Arms, and there are five new clothing sets, new hairstyles for both male and female V, Quadra skins through Twitch Drops, and a radio station playing Edgerunners music. The bigger news for console players is integrated mod.io support on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, along with a long-requested FOV slider. The Switch 2 is reportedly left out of mod support, although it's not clear if that changes later. CD Projekt will share full details during its livestream on October 21 at 5 PM CEST, so it's reasonable to expect a complete list of changes then. Sources: WesNemo on YouTube, r/GamingLeaksAndRumours, via Notebookcheck
