GTA 6 review suggests base PS5 suffers frame rate drops during intense driving and prolonged explosive encounters

TheTools · 18.11.2026 12:49 · source · permalink

GTA 6 reviews are finally out, and Rockstar Games' highly anticipated title has managed to achieve an impressive score of 99 on Metacritic. However, one question that almost every PlayStation 5 owner has had in mind is how well the game runs on the base PS5. Well, a review from Digital Foundry has finally provided some answers.

According to Digital Foundry, GTA 6 generally runs smoothly on the base PS5, maintaining its target of 30 FPS during normal gameplay. This means players should be able to explore the open world and complete most activities without experiencing major performance issues.

However, things aren't perfect. The review points out that the base PS5 experiences minor frame rate drops during intense driving sequences. More demanding situations, particularly prolonged encounters involving explosions, can cause the frame rate to drop further to the mid-20s.

The good news is that these issues don't appear to affect regular gameplay significantly. However, players should expect occasional performance dips when the action becomes particularly demanding.

On the other hand, the PS5 Pro delivers a much more consistent experience, maintaining a stable 30 FPS with proper frame pacing even during demanding gameplay.

That said, both consoles render GTA 6 at a base resolution of 1440p. However, the PS5 Pro uses Sony's PSSR upscaling technology to produce a sharper image that comes close to native 4K. The standard PS5 uses conventional upscaling, resulting in a softer image.
