Michigan U.S. Senate Election 2026 Live Results: Abdul El-Sayed vs. Mike Rogers

TheTools · 04.11.2026 02:03 · source · permalink

Democrat Abdul El-Sayed and former Republican Rep. Mike Rogers face off to replace retiring Democratic Sen. Gary Peters. El-Sayed has been endorsed by high-profile progressives, including Sen. Bernie Sanders. Meanwhile Rogers is looking for a comeback after losing a Senate race in 2024, and has been endorsed by President Donald Trump, though Republicans haven’t won a Senate race in the state since 1994.

Race Results

About the Remaining Vote

Mike Rogers would need to win 50.1% of the remaining vote to catch or overtake Abdul El-Sayed, who leads.

Inside the Election

Election Day voter turnout in Detroit is surpassing turnout from the 2022 midterm elections. By 5 p.m. ET in 2022, Detroit had around 66,000 people vote on Election Day. Today, at 5 p.m. ET, Detroit had around 75,000 votes, per the Detroit City clerk. Polls close in Michigan at 8 p.m.

Elections are administered by towns and municipalities, and counting usually takes until early morning or even midday Wednesday. How quickly the winner of this competitive Senate race can be called depends upon the margin. For that, all eyes will be on Wayne County, home of Detroit.

Some states and some individual counties provide results broken down by type of ballot cast, which can include Election Day votes, or those cast early, either in person or by mail. Whenever that information is widely available in a state on election night, NBC News will display it.
