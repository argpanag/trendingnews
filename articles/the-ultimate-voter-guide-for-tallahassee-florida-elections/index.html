The ultimate voter guide for Tallahassee, Florida elections

TheTools · 03.11.2026 07:44 · source · permalink

Tomorrow, Nov. 3, is election day. For those in the Tallahassee community who are looking to vote, here are some voting resources and candidate profiles to learn more about who might show up on your ballot.

Florida governor's race

Democratic nominee David Jolly will face off against Republican nominee Byron Donalds.

Florida's US Senate race

Democratic nominee Angie Nixon will face off against Republican nominee Ashley Moody.

Florida's US House race, District 2

Democratic nominee Amanda Marie Green will face off against Republican nominee Austin Rogers.

Tallahassee's mayoral race

This is a nonpartisan race. Loranne Ausley will face off against Jeremy Matlow.

Tallahassee City Commissioner races

These are nonpartisan races. PJ Perez will face off against Tom Derzypolski for City Commissioner Seat 3 and Dianne Williams-Cox will face off against Dot Inman-Johnson for Seat 5.
