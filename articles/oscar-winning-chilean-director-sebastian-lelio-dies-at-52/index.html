Oscar-winning Chilean director Sebastian Lelio dies at 52

TheTools · 06.11.2026 18:45 · source · permalink

Chilean director and screenwriter Sebastian Lelio, who won the Oscar for Best International Feature Film for 'A Fantastic Woman' (2017), has died at the age of 52. According to reports in Chilean media, Lelio was found dead in his home in the Las Condes neighborhood of Santiago. His family confirmed his death in a statement, saying he died 'due to health reasons,' without providing further details. 'We are going through a moment of great pain, and his memory will remain with us forever. He was a wonderful person who contributed greatly to Chilean culture at significant moments in its history,' the family said in the statement.
