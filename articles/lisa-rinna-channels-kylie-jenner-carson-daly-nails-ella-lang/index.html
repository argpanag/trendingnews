From pop star tributes and family costumes to a wearable seating chart, celebrities are getting into the Halloween spirit.

Is it really Halloween if celebrities don't go all out with their costumes? The spookiest night of the year is just around the corner — which means everyone from actors and artists to athletes and public figures is showcasing their latest looks on social media. Whether it's an obscure, avant-garde take on a larger concept or a faithful — and playful — transformation into a fellow celebrity, the stars have a knack for dressing up. 

Below, Yahoo has rounded up the best Halloween costumes of 2026 — so far.

Paris Hilton and her family

 Paris Hilton continued her tradition of dressing up as Britney Spears, this time recreating the pop star's look in the "I'm a Slave 4 U" music video. Hilton also included her family in this year's costumes. The Simple Life star, her husband Carter Reum and their two children brought characters from Beauty and the Beast to life. Reum was the Beast, son Phoenix was Lumiere, daughter London was Chip and Hilton was Belle — a decision that garnered backlash online. Those weren't their only costumes, though: Hilton also dressed up as goth matriarch Morticia Addams, while her hubby and kiddos rounded out the Addams family. 

Lily Allen 

Ticketmaster… but make it wearable? Lily Allen dressed up as the ticketing platform's infamous seating map, wearing a matching white puffer and leotard covered with little blue dots. (We can't get over the "Stage" detail across the collar.) Allen shared her costume on Instagram with the caption, "Never beating the allegations. #bluedotfever." One fan explained the West End Girl singer's costume in the comments. 

"For anyone who is missing the joke - Lily canceled her Auckland concert due to being sick," she wrote. "People online said it was because she didn't sell enough tickets. Geddit now? 🫩"

Lily Allen.

Carson Daly and the 'Today' show hosts

During Friday's broadcast, the Today show hosts debuted their transformations for this year's festivities. Standout costumes included Savannah Guthrie as an old-school news anchor, Al Roker as Gumby and Jenna Bush Hager as Phoebe Buffay from Friends. Our personal favorite? Carson Daly, who dressed up as "Choosin' Texas" hitmaker Ella Langley. Daly hilariously brought the country darling to life — complete with her signature fringe. 

Kim Kardashian 

Kim Kardashian doesn't play when it comes to Halloween. This year, the Skims founder dressed up as someone near and dear to her heart: her daughter North West! From her long, electric-blue hair and piercings to her signature hand-covering-mouth pose, Kardashian was the spitting image of North. 

Keke Palmer and her son 

Keke Palmer and her son, Leodis, channeled 1995-era Janet Jackson and Michael Jackson from their acclaimed "Scream" music video. Palmer transformed into the "All for You" singer, while 3-year-old Leodis made an adorable King of Pop. 

"Another memory with mommy and son! I LOVE YOU SO MUCH BUDDY. ITS SO MUCH FUN PLAYING WITH YOU! You will take over the family business, your way, someday," Palmer wrote on Instagram. "My SCREAM come true. 🥹🖤" 

Owen Thiele and Benito Skinner 

They're going stateside! Overcompensating stars Owen Thiele and Benito Skinner dressed up as PinkPantheress and Zara Larsson from their "Stateside" music video. Thiele transformed into the British R&B singer, while Skinner channeled the Swedish pop girlie. The costars pulled out all the stops — from their impeccable glam to their bedazzled "Stateside" crop tops. Their costume even caught Larsson's attention: The "Midnight Sun" singer commented "Ateeeee" on the duo's joint Instagram post. 

Lisa Rinna

Kylie Jenner, is that you?! Lisa Rinna transformed into the Khy founder for this year's festivities. Rinna recreated Jenner's look from the Marty Supreme premiere in December 2025: a bright orange Chrome Hearts column gown paired with her signature long black locks. 

Alex Consani

Calling all Spy Kids fans! This year, model Alex Consani transformed into a Thumb-Thumb, one of the creatures from the 2001 flick starring Daryl Sabara and Alexa PenaVega. In addition to having human-sized thumbs as heads, arms and legs — the memorable henchmen also wore red suits.

Cara Delevingne

Leeloo, is that you? Model Cara Delevingne transformed into Leeloo, the powerful being sent to Earth in the film The Fifth Element. Actress Milla Jovovich famously portrayed the character in the 1997 sci-fi flick.

Liz Gillies 

Count on Liz Gillies to make a stellar pop culture reference. The former Nickelodeon star, who's known for debuting elaborate costumes alongside bestie Ariana Grande, transformed into Luke from Halloweentown.  

Serena Page

Love Island USA star Serena Page made the ultimate 2000s Disney Channel reference. Page transformed into Raven-Symoné circa her That's So Raven era — and even recreated one of the show's promotional photoshoots. 

Troye Sivan

Troye Sivan brought a beloved Disney character to life. The "Party" singer stepped out as Woody from the Toy Story franchise.

Janelle Monae

For their annual Wondaween Festival in Los Angeles, Janelle Monae transformed into "Phish Boy," a fishy sea creature.

"I'm choosing to be courageous and transform," they said during the event. "How do I get to the next version of Janelle?"

McCall DaPron

McCall DaPron channeled her inner Jackie Kennedy. The star of Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County, which premieres on Nov. 12, her husband, Kirk DaPron and their four children dressed up as the Kennedy family.
