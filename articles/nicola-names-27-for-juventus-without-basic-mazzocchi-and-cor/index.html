Nicola names 27 for Juventus without Basic, Mazzocchi and Correia

TheTools · 21.11.2026 17:55 · source · permalink

Basic is out with last-minute fatigue, while Mazzocchi and Correia carry minor hamstring discomfort. The match is this Sunday at 12:30.

By TuFutbolero Newsroom·November 21, 2026 at 12:55 PM

Venezia have released their squad for the trip to Juventus, the Serie A round 12 game on Sunday 22 November at 12:30. Davide Nicola has 27 players available, according to TuttoMercatoWeb.

There are three notable absences:

  • Toma Basic, ruled out by fatigue detected at the last moment.
  • Pasquale Mazzocchi, with a slight hamstring complaint.
  • Thierry Correia, with the same hamstring issue, which keeps him out of this match at least.

As we reported in the preview, the match arrives with the list already closed, while Juventus have their whole group back.
