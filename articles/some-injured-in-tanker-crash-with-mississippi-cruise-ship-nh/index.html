Some injured in tanker crash with Mississippi cruise ship | NHK WORLD-JAPAN News

TheTools · 14.11.2026 09:56 · source · permalink

A tanker collided with a docked river cruise ship in the southern US state of Louisiana on Friday. All passengers and crew were rescued, but some were injured.

The collision occurred on the Mississippi River in New Orleans at around 12:30 p.m., local time. Footage from the scene shows the riverboat listing heavily before rolling onto its side.

Many people were thrown into the water and 32 were taken to the hospital. All are reported to be in stable condition.
