Mourinho experiments with a more advanced and aggressive defensive line, with full-backs pressing high and encouraging numbers.

Mourinho and his tactical gamble


Real Madrid is going through a period of adjustments under the direction of José Mourinho, who is beginning to outline an unprecedented defense for the most demanding matches. The chosen defense is composed of Dumfries, Konaté, Huijsen, and Cucurella, four footballers who represent the bet on a more dynamic and advanced line.

With Dumfries and Cucurella on the flanks, the team gains width and aggression. The Dutchman, accustomed to playing as a wing-back in a five-man defense, fits into the proposal of greater intensity. Cucurella brings versatility and constant movement, which allows maintaining pressure in the opponent's field.



First encouraging results


The initial trials left numbers that support the bet. Against Real Sociedad, the defense recovered 15 balls, seven of them in the opponent's field, reflecting the intention to disrupt the rival from the start. Huijsen stood out as the best passer, with 69 successful passes out of 72 attempts, representing a 95.8% success rate.

Dumfries also had an offensive role, with 15 passes in the final third, the fifth-best record of the match. These statistics reinforce the idea that the new defensive line not only aims to protect the goal but also to be a driving force for pressure and quick transitions into attack.



The enigma of solidity


Beyond the good signs, Mourinho is not left without alternatives. Rüdiger remains a solid option and Militao's return is on the horizon. The big question is whether this defense can maintain the characteristic solidity of the Portuguese coach's teams without sacrificing the aggressiveness that this new line proposes.

The challenge is set, Real Madrid seeks to evolve its defensive identity, combining security with higher pressure and a more daring style of play. The answer will come in the big matches, where this defense will have to prove if it lives up to expectations.
