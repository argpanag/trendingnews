EAST PROVIDENCE, R.I. (WJAR) — East Providence and Rehoboth firefighters marked the 25 years since the Sept. 11 attacks Friday by taking on a 110-story memorial stair climb at City View Manor in East Providence.

Firefighters climbed 11 flights of stairs 10 times while wearing their gear, recreating the physical challenge faced by first responders who climbed the World Trade Center towers on 9/11.

NBC 10's Martha Konstandinidis reports that firefighters from East Providence and Rehoboth took on a 110-story memorial stair climb at City View Manor in East Providence.

East Providence firefighter Davis Roast said the climb became increasingly difficult as it went on.

"You start fast and you feel great, and then just as you get into the second half, it just wears on you more and more," said Roast.

Firefighters say the heat and nearly 60 pounds of added weight of the gear made the challenge more difficult.

Some said they believe the difficulty was an important part of the tribute.

"For a day like today, on the 25th anniversary, I think it's important to kind of struggle as much as we can," said Roast. "It's nothing compared to what they did, but at least it's an homage to them."

For East Providence Lt. Zachary Goodheart, the memorial has a different meaning now that he is a firefighter himself.

Goodheart was 9 on Sept. 11, 2001. Now, as a firefighter himself, he says he sees their sacrifice differently.

"They charged into that building and started going up the stairs," said Goodheart. "I can't imagine what they were thinking, but they had a call, they went to it."

Family members and community members also watched the firefighters take on the challenge.

This was the first year East Providence and Rehoboth firefighters joined together for the memorial climb.

Organizers hope to make it an annual tradition, with donations supporting the Tunnel to Towers Foundation.
