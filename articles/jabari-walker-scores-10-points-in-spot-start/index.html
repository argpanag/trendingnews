Jabari Walker scores 10 points in spot start

TheTools · 24.11.2026 05:20 · source · permalink

    Jabari Walker scores 10 points in spot start

    Jabari Walker

    PHI Forward #33
    Player Stats

    Jabari Walker produced 10 points (3-of-8 FGs, 4-of-5 FTs), five rebounds, two assists and one steal in Monday’s loss to the Trail Blazers.

    Walker filled the void left by Joel Embiid, who was held out of Monday’s game with an illness. Walker scored in double figures for the fourth time this season and the third time in his last five appearances, while also grabbing five rebounds. The production wasn’t enough to make Walker someone worth holding onto once Embiid returns, but the few who streamed him did receive some value.
    - Raphielle Johnson
