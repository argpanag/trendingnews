Nepal Loses Toss Against Japan in Asian Games Cricket Match

TheTools · 26.09.2026 01:18 · source · permalink

Kathmandu. In the ongoing 20th Asian Games under way in Japan, Nepal has lost the toss in today's Saturday match against host Japan. In Aichi, Nishin, Korogi Sports Park, Japan won the toss and decided to bat first.

However, even though there was a toss, the game has not started on time due to rain.

This is Nepal's second match in this competition. Earlier, in the first match completed on Friday, Nepal started with a victory by defeating Afghanistan by 5 wickets. On the other hand, Japan was defeated by Afghanistan in its first match.

Today's match is very important for Nepal to become the group winner. If Nepal defeats Japan, it will become the group winner and enter the quarter-finals of the competition.

According to the equation, if Nepal becomes the group winner, it will face Sri Lanka in the quarter-finals, and if it becomes the group runner-up, it will have to play against India.
