Go Inside Training ahead of United showdown

TheTools · 19.09.2026 17:07 · source · permalink

We welcome Manchester United to Emirates Stadium on Saturday evening (5.30pm UK) looking for our first home win of the season, and you can see how we've been gearing up for the contest with Inside Training. 

Our Gunners have taken four points from their opening two games of the season, beating Brighton 1-0 on the opening day of the campaign before being held by Crystal Palace in north London last weekend. 

Renee Slegers' side will be looking to get back to winning ways when the Red Devils visit, as we start an enticing run of fixtures which sees us play United, Chelsea and Manchester City, with Champions League matches against HB Koge Women and Paris FC in between.
