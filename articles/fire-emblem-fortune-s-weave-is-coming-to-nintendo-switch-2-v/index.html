Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave is coming to Nintendo Switch 2. – Virus

TheTools · 14.09.2026 09:16 · source · permalink

The popular series is getting a new full-fledged sequel.

Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave is coming out on September 17, 2026, exclusively for Nintendo Switch 2, bringing a new story about four heroes whose destinies intertwine through Heroic Games and the fight to save the future.

Players will be able to simultaneously follow the stories of Caij, Dietrich, Theodore, and Lede, recruit allies, explore the city of Dagsion and the wider continent of Dagda, and develop relationships and abilities of their party between tactical battles.

A new feature is Blaze Arts, special abilities that consume health points, as well as various rules and terrain elements during battles. Fortune’s Weave is also the first Fire Emblem to support mouse control via Joy-Con 2 controllers.
