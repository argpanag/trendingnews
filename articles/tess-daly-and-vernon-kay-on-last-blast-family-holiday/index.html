Tess Daly and Vernon Kay on 'last blast' family holiday

TheTools · 02.09.2026 17:16 · source · permalink

Tess Daly and Vernon Kay have proved it's all amicable between them after their unexpected split announcement earlier this year.

The couple had been married for 22 years when they announced they parted ways in May. They share two daughters — Phoebe and Amber.

Today's top videos

Despite their no longer being together romantically, Tess, Vernon, Phoebe, and Amber are still a family unit, and they're keen to prove this statement true.

On Wednesday, Tess shared a photo with her estranged husband and daughters as they soaked up the sun in the south of France.

'Last blast of South of France summer with the fam,' she wrote, as people praised the show of affection, despite their situation.

Georgia Toffolo commented: 'Love that you all stick together. So cool and inspirational,' as someone else commented: 'Gorgeous photo and so lovely to see you all together, so important for the girls. Well done on remaining great friends. X.'

It comes after reports that Vernon bought a € 3.5m pad 'just 15 minutes away from their family home'.

Tess and Vernon shocked fans in May when they announced the end of their 22-year marriage.

At the time, they released a statement, which read: 'After much consideration, and with a deep sense of care and respect for one another, we have decided to separate amicably.'

'This has not been an easy choice, but it comes from a place of mutual understanding and a shared desire for what is best for both of us.

'We remain great friends and, most importantly, fully committed to our roles as loving and supportive parents,' they continued at the time. 'There are no other parties involved in this decision. We kindly ask for privacy during this time.'
