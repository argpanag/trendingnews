Crown Prince Christian of Denmark Looks Dashing in New Portraits for His 21st Birthday

TheTools · 15.10.2026 15:57 · source · permalink

The heir to the Danish throne marks his milestone birthday today.

It’s a big day for Denmark’s Crown Prince Christian. The heir to the Danish throne marks his 21st birthday today, and to celebrate the occasion, the palace has released new portraits of the future king—who looks particularly dashing in military dress.

In the portraits, which were taken in the Vestibule of Christian VII's Palace at Amalienborg, the prince sports the red and blue gala uniform of the Royal Life Guards—the first time he has appeared in the style.

The uniform is festooned with honors, including the Order of the Elephant (which was awarded to him on his 19th birthday), the Order of the Dannebrog, and the Grand Commander’s Cross of the Order of the Dannebrog, which was awarded to Christian by his father, King Frederik, ahead of his milestone birthday. According to the palace’s official Instagram, “Grand Commander is the highest degree of the Order of the Dannebrog, which alone is awarded to princely (royal) persons, who are foremost elephant knights.”


Christian, who joined the military in 2025, currently serves as a second lieutenant and platoon commander with the Royal Life Guards at Høvelte Barracks, according to the Copenhagen Post. He recently told the Danish newspaper Politiken that he would consider deployment after completing his lieutenant training.

As for his future as monarch, Christian told Politiken, “It’s surreal for me to think about it.” He added, “I’m about to turn 21, and I know that this is what my future holds, among other things. I’ve known it from a young age and have been prepared for the task by my parents. So it’s something I’m obviously both excited about and nervous about.”

Christian became heir to the throne in January 2024, after his grandmother, Queen Margrethe, abdicated, becoming the first Danish monarch in centuries to do so.
