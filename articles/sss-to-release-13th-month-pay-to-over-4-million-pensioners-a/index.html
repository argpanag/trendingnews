MANILA — Over 4.1 million pensioners are set to receive a holiday boost as the Social Security System (SSS) disburses P45.26 billion worth of 13th month and December pensions starting Dec. 1.

SSS President and CEO Victor Alfonso Limlingan said the funds will be credited in two tranches during the first week of December.

The first batch on Dec. 1 will cover around 2.39 million SSS and Employees’ Compensation (EC) pensioners, totaling P25.33 billion. The second batch on Dec. 4 will disburse P19.88 billion to roughly 1.7 million recipients.

Meanwhile, around P28.81 million will be released to over 3,798 pensioners via checks and non-PESONet banks on Dec. 4, with the Philippine Postal Corporation tapped to expedite check deliveries.

ADVERTISEMENT

“This is our way of giving back to our pensioners, who contributed to the System during their productive years. We hope this additional benefit brings joy and relief to them and their families this Christmas,” Limlingan said.

This year’s total payout is higher than in 2025 following the second tranche of increases under the Pension Reform Program, which grants annual hikes every September from 2025 to 2027—10 percent for retirement and disability pensioners, and 5 percent for death and survivor beneficiaries.

A tradition since 1988, the 13th month pension matches a beneficiary’s regular monthly payout and automatically covers retirement, total disability, survivorship, and qualified dependent pensioners, as well as those who availed of the advance 18-month initial benefit.
