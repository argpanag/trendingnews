Powerball unstruck in Wednesday draw, jackpot climbs to $25m

TheTools · 25.11.2026 12:24 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

Powerball will roll over to a massive $25 million for Saturday’s draw after going unstruck tonight.

However, three players take away $333,333 tonight, sharing the Lotto First Division prize.

The lucky numbers for Lotto draw 2641 were 26, 39, 18, 40, 3 and 11. The Bonus Ball was 28, and Powerball was 3.

Two punters also bagged $100,000 each with Strike Four.

A further 15 players won $18,911 each with Lotto Second Division.

One of those players correctly picked the Powerball number, taking their total winnings to $38,280.

Previous winners

Five lucky Kiwis bagged a whopping $200,000 each in Saturday’s draw, sharing the $1 million Lotto First Division prize.

One Bay of Plenty punter bought their ticket through MyLotto, while the remaining four tickets were purchased at Kaitāia’s Marston Moor, Wellington’s Pak’nSave Kilbirnie, Christchurch’s Pak’nSave Wainoni and New World Ōamaru.

A Northland player who bought their ticket through MyLotto scooped the entire $400,000 Strike Four prize.

Luck was on the side of a further 22 players who won $14,500 each with Lotto Second Division.

What to do if you win

Ticket holders can see immediately if they have won if they purchased a ticket through MyLotto. Prizes of $1000 or less are automatically credited to the account.

For prizes exceeding $1000, winners must complete an online prize claim form.

Winners with physical tickets must still visit a Lotto retailer to claim their prize.
