Desert Vipers (DV) will take on MI Emirates (MIE) in the ninth match of the International League T20 (ILT20) 2026 at the Zayed Cricket Stadium in Abu Dhabi on Saturday, November 28.

Desert Vipers, led by Shadab Khan, have endured an inconsistent start to their campaign, winning just one of their three matches. They are currently placed fifth in the points table with two points and a net run rate of -1.304. The Vipers will be keen to turn things around and get their campaign back on track. 

Meanwhile, MI Emirates have enjoyed a relatively better start to the tournament, winning two of their three matches. Led by Kieron Pollard, they occupy the second position in the standings with four points and a net run rate of +0.696.
