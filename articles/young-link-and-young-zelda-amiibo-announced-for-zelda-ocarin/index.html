Young Link and Young Zelda amiibo announced for Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake

TheTools · 08.09.2026 14:29 · source · permalink

Submit a news tip

☰

New amiibo for Young Link and Young Zelda are on the way for The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time’s remake. Both are coming this year.

The Young Link and Young Zelda amiibo will have special functionality in the upcoming Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake. By scanning them, players can receive special in-game masks.
