Lupin Season 4 Episode 5 Recap: Is Assane In Grave Danger? | Film Fugitives

TheTools · 25.10.2026 16:35 · source · permalink

Audio By Carbonatix

In Episode 5 of the fourth season of “Lupin,” the car explosion in Juliette’s garage has left Assane and Adele dumbfounded. Assane manages to stay composed, swallowing the grief of losing Juliette. Adele finds it hard to see her mother die, right in front of her eyes. She feels helpless about the situation and cannot do anything to bring Juliette back. Before Adele and Assane can react, the cops arrive at the spot. As the chase continues where they try to get far away from the cops, Diop tries to realize who might have called the cops so fast. Meanwhile, Raoul teams up with Esteban and robs a luxury car showroom, getting away with millions. This is the first time Raoul has taken such a big risk, unbeknownst of the dangers that might be waiting for him.

Spoilers Ahead

Why Does Assane Agree To Work With Adele?

Assane and Juliette go way back; they have secretly been lovers at some point and even though life drifted them apart, they have never stopped caring for each other. Once Assane gets aware of the fact that Juliette was killed mercilessly by The Guardians, he does not let it sit. He has never expected the stakes to be so high and it’s quite shocking that Juliette had to lose her life in this fight for the King’s Treasure. If Assane walks away, he loses the chance to hit back at The Guardians. Since the cops are all on their payroll, the police will most likely never open the case or document this death. Assane knows for a fact that the cops are involved because the moment Juliette’s garage went up in flames, the police instantly reached her location. Even though Assane walks out, Adele will be in great danger because The Guardians are not going to give up. That’s why Assane hurries to help Adele in avenging Juliette’s death as soon as possible.

Why Is The Search For The Crown So Hard?

Adele knows that the crown of King Henry XVI is the final piece of the puzzle. Once she can keep the ruby, sapphire and other gemstones on the crown, (theoretically) it will give the map to a secret treasure. However, finding this crown is far from being easy because it has been decades since the crown of King Henry XVI was auctioned off. The main problem is the records, which are not available on the internet and cannot be taken out that easily. These auctions were mostly kept off the grid because the auctioneers probably got jittery by the fear of getting robbed. After a lot of hard work, Adele and Assane fool a heritage auction palace to pull the offline records of the crown sale. It turns out that the crown was purchased by one of The Guardians’s families and it was kept inside a hotel to hide it. Assane realizes that the hotel is most likely a new establishment now because The Guardians always work like shadows, measuring each one of their moves.

Who Is Commissioner Korvin Working For?

Korvin comes from the long line of cops who are bought by The Guardians. These cops are no longer the protectors of the law; they carry out missions on behalf of the protectors of the King’s Treasures, as if they are their boss. Korvin may act like the Police Commissioner who does not prefer the sloppy work of her juniors but in reality, she is a liar. Her loyalty lies entirely with The Guardians. As she is the Police Commissioner, Korvin has control over the entire police force and can make her officers act according to her will. It is highly possible that Korvin has known all along about the new Lupin and could have let Assane escape from prison a lot sooner. Just because Korvin works for The Guardians, she deliberately removes Guedira from the investigation despite being aware of his expertise. Korvin knows how much Guedira cares for Sophia but she still removes him to recruit one of The Guardians’ own, Georges. This automatically puts Korvin in a much higher position as she is now free to do anything she wants in order to serve The Guardians better. With men like Georges on her side, Korvin is basically invincible, given the degree of power The Guardians have given her.

What Triggers Raoul’s Anger?

Raoul is not doing well lately and that’s mainly because of his struggles with mental health. Although he has earned loads following a successful heist with Esteban and his friends, he is not ready to lead that kind of a life that has no respect in it. Amidst his moral dilemmas regarding the car dealership robbery, Raoul cannot even catch a breath at home because of Claire and her intrusive nature. Claire is not at all ready to give Raoul some space to relax; she is always impulsive around him and questions him about almost everything she can think of. This frustrates Raoul even more because he has still not moved on from the fact that Assane lied to him and forged his own passport. Raoul is not ready to accept advice from Assane when he himself is still on his way to steal. When Claire confronts Raoul, she makes matters further worse because the last thing he needs in his life is some discipline. Raoul needs a bit more love and he needs to be understood by both his parents. Even though Raoul seems extremely distressed, fortunately, Assane saves the day by comforting him. 

Is Assane In Grave Danger?

When Assane and Adele decide to go back to Juliette’s house, they have only one mission in mind; the crown requires all the gemstones to be assembled on it for viewing the real treasure map. However, they find some henchmen stealing the gems from Juliette’s library, right in front of their eyes, as if they have known their location all along. While this confuses both Assane and Adele, the former wastes no time in sending his daughter far away from the spot because of the impending danger. While Assane goes in confidently and defeats a few of those men, he gets subdued by a stun gun. It turns out that they have already come prepared and although Adele is temporarily out of danger, Assane is going to face a lot going forward. He is kidnapped by some of the most powerful men and unless he finds out who is behind this, Assane will not be able to escape.
