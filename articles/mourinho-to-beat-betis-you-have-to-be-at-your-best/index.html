Mourinho: "To beat Betis, you have to be at your best"

TheTools · 03.09.2026 08:15 · source · permalink

“Sergio Martínez is a boy with potential, and the plan is for him to be on the bench tomorrow,” explained the coach.

José Mourinho appeared in the press room at Ciudad Real Madrid ahead of the game against Betis, corresponding to Matchday 4 of LaLiga (Friday, 9:00 pm CEST; Orange TV and Movistar LaLiga): “Real Madrid needs to be great; otherwise, it won't win. Betis is a very good team. Real Madrid's recent results there are very telling. It's difficult to win there, and to do so, you have to be at your best; there’s no other way.”

Comments on Messi's announcement about leaving the national team
“Talking about Messi is complicated. There’s not much more to say; there are no words to describe his quality as a player. Even now, at this upcoming World Cup at 38 or 39 years old, he’s shown us what he’s capable of. Sometimes there are players you’re going to miss. We’ll have to watch some MLS matches, which is something I don’t particularly enjoy, but we’ll have to watch some to make the most of these last years or seasons, I don’t know, to see him because he’s one of those players you always miss. In our generation, over the last two decades, we all know what he means.”

On the academy players and their role
“Many work with me regularly, every day; others come occasionally, and for those who don’t come, we keep a close eye on them. They’ve done a great job in recent years, and the result of this work is that Thiago has definitively moved up to the first team, and it has also resulted in the many millions that Real Madrid has earned from the transfers of these young players. In the end, they aren’t really transfers because Real Madrid brings back the players whenever it wants and thinks they should be part of the main squad. Over the course of the season, with suspensions and injuries, opportunities will obviously arise for them, and I’m getting to know them better every day. We maintain daily contact with Manu and La Fábrica, and they can be excited knowing their time with the first team will come.”

About Sergio Martínez
“The plan is for him to be on the bench tomorrow, so from there you can more or less see the plan. He has trained with the first team for two days, nothing more. Before the club signed him, it was clear he wouldn’t be directly part of the first team. We have also analyzed and given our opinion that he could potentially make it here one day. He is a boy with potential, who has some experience in the Second Division and at the start of the league with Racing. He has potential, he needs to work, and only he can determine his path. The quality and intensity here are different, but he is here. Tomorrow, he will be there, along with Rivas, who has been with us since the first match, and also Cestero. We'll see if he plays or not, but being here with us is already a step forward.”
