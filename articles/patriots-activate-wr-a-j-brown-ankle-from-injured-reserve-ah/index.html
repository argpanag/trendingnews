Patriots activate WR A.J. Brown (ankle) from injured reserve ahead of Sunday's game vs. Jets

TheTools · 18.10.2026 17:53 · source · permalink

He's back. 

The New England Patriots activated A.J. Brown from injured reserve ahead of Sunday's AFC East showdown with the New York Jets, setting the wide receiver up to make his first appearance since Week 1.

Brown, who suffered a high ankle sprain in New England's season-opening loss to the Seattle Seahawks, was a limited participant in practice all week after missing the past four games. He's officially listed as questionable, but ESPN's Adam Schefter reported he is expected to play.

RELATED CONTENT

A three-time Pro Bowler, Brown was a highly anticipated addition by the reigning AFC champions, but he played only 30 snaps for the team, with three catches for 26 yards, before sustaining his injury.

The Pats had a lot to figure out with Brown going on IR, as quarterback Drake Maye seemed stuck in a funk dating back to last season's playoffs, and new weapons needed to emerge, but the offense appears to have figured things out just as the unit's best player returns to the fold. 

After throwing for 585 yards, one touchdown and six interceptions for a 58.6 passer rating over the first three weeks of the campaign, Maye has rebounded with 643 yards, seven TDs and a single pick across his last two outings. 

He figures to take another step with his WR1 healthy again.

New England (3-2) hosts New York (2-3) at 1 p.m. ET on Sunday.
