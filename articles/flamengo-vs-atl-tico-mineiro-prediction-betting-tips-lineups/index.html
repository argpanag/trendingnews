Flamengo vs Atlético Mineiro Preview

Flamengo square off against Atlético Mineiro, with kick-off set for 18:00 on Monday 26 October.

Flamengo have enjoyed six home victories on the spin. Most recently, a 3-0 win occurred away from home when playing Bahia. The Rio de Janeiro outfit enjoyed 63% possession and had fifteen shots on target, while Jorginho, Gonzalo Plata and Lucas Paqueta got among the goals.

Four days ago, Flamengo were beaten on penalties (0-0 AET) when hosting Estudiantes La Plata in the Copa Libertadores.

Atlético Mineiro have won two Série A games on the spin after recently recording a 1-0 home victory over Coritiba. Galo boasted 59% possession and had ten shots on goal, with Igor Gomes finding the net in the 78th minute.

On Wednesday night, Atlético lost 2-0 on the road against Montevideo City Torque in the Copa Sudamericana.

H2H: Flamengo Too Strong for Atlético in Last Head-to-Head

Flamengo boast an unbeaten record in the past three games with Atlético. In their last clash, Flamengo bagged a 4-0 away win. The past 10 meetings between these sides have resulted in five Flamengo victories and two Atlético wins. There have also been three draws.

8 wins, 1 loss and 1 draw, averaging 2.2 goals from 6.9 shots on goal and 17.4 attempts. Flamengo have had 58.3% possession and 7.2 corners per game. On average, they have conceded 0.7 goals from 3.4 shots on goal and 12.2 attempts, while the opposition have been awarded 4.0 corners.

The four top scorers are Lucas Paqueta, Jorge Carrascal, Pedro and Samuel Dias Lino, with these players all scoring 4 goals. Leading assistor is Jorge Carrascal with 6.

Atlético have won 6, lost 1 and drawn 3, with an average of 1.5 goals from 6.3 shots on goal and 15.2 attempts. The Belo Horizonte team have averaged 52.2% possession, 6.1 corners taken and 6.4 corners against, while conceding 0.8 goals from 3.8 shots on goal and 15.6 attempts.

Tomas Cuello has scored 3 goals, followed by Vitor Hugo, Thiago Borbas, Bernard and Reinier on 2. Natanael, Maycon Barberan and Thiago Borbas have managed 1 assists in the last 10 games.
