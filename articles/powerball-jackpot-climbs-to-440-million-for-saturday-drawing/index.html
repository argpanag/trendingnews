Powerball Jackpot Climbs to $440 Million for Saturday Drawing

TheTools · 02.10.2026 14:14 · source · permalink
  • Tennessee Lottery Results

No one matched all six numbers in Wednesday’s Powerball drawing, pushing the jackpot to an estimated $440 million for Saturday night’s drawing. The cash value option now stands at $183.1 million. More Tennessee Lottery Results!

Recent Winning Numbers

The winning numbers drawn on Wednesday, September 30, 2026, were 4, 6, 23, 33, 44, and Powerball 13. The Power Play multiplier was 4x.

There were no jackpot winners and no Match 5 + Power Play $2 million winners. One Match 5 ticket worth $1 million was sold in Texas.

Current Powerball Jackpot

The jackpot grew from an estimated $411 million (cash value $171.0 million) for Wednesday’s drawing to $440 million for Saturday’s drawing. The cash value option is $183.1 million.

Next Drawing

The next Powerball drawing is Saturday, October 3, 2026, at 10:59 p.m. ET.

How to Play Powerball

Powerball tickets cost $2 per play. Players select five numbers from 1 to 69 for the white balls, then choose one number from 1 to 26 for the red Powerball. You can pick your own numbers or choose a Quick Pick and let the terminal select them for you.

Drawings are held every Monday, Wednesday, and Saturday at 10:59 p.m. ET from the Florida Lottery draw studio in Tallahassee.
