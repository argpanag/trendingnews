All New iPhone 18 Pro and 'iPhone Duo' Colors Leaked

TheTools · 09.09.2026 17:41 · source · permalink

Ahead of Apple's annual iPhone event at 10 a.m. Pacific Time today, Bloomberg's Mark Gurman has leaked all of the color options for the iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, and the first-ever foldable iPhone named the iPhone Duo.


iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max color options:

  • Black
  • Light Blue
  • Burgundy
  • Silver

Burgundy has also been described as Dark Red or Dark Cherry.

iPhone Duo color options:

  • White
  • Dark Blue

Dark Blue has also been described as Indigo.
