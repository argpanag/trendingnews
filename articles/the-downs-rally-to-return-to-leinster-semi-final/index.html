The Downs rally to return to Leinster semi-final

TheTools · 15.11.2026 15:32 · source · permalink

The Downs 3-13 Kilanerin-Ballyfad 0-15

Westmeath champions, The Downs, put a poor first-half display behind them to ultimately advance to the Leinster football semi-finals with a bit to spare against their counterparts from Wexford, Kilanerin-Ballyfad, in cold conditions with a tricky cross-field wind blowing in Mullingar.

The Wexford champions started by far the stronger and they should have led by more than Cian Hughes' fourth-minute point when Michael Moloney equalised in the eighth minute.

Two minutes later, good work by Luke Loughlin set up Niall Mitchell for a slapped goal from point-blank range against the run of play. The same player added a point under pressure in the 12th minute, with Loughlin again the provider, to put Dermot Power’s charges ahead by 1-02 to 0-01.

However, Kilanerin-Ballyfad, who were dominating the midfield battle, went on to control the remainder of the first moiety, outscoring the home team by 0-09 to 0-01. The Downs’ only score came from Loughlin in the 23rd minute, when a goal beckoned.

The visitors’ points came courtesy of Dean O’Toole (six, including two frees and the only two-pointer of the half from a 25th-minute free, after dissent by The Downs), and one each from play by the Hughes brothers, Padraic, Eoin and Cian.

Ian Martin was very narrowly wide from a goal opportunity in the last action of the half, but Kilanerin-Ballyfad deservedly led by 0-10 to 1-03 at half-time.

Martin halved the deficit with a fine two-pointer from play less than three minutes after play resumed, but Padraig Murphy’s troops rallied and were 0-13 to 1-06 ahead after 38 minutes.

However, the complexion of the game changed in the 42nd minute when the hard-working Joe Moran found the net despite stumbling at the end of a good move, to leave the sides tied at 2-07 to 0-13.

With Westmeath players Loughlin and Kevin O’Sullivan by now more prominent in attack, The Downs edged ahead by 2-10 to 0-15 with 50 minutes on the clock.

A minute later, Mitchell and O’Sullivan combined in style for the latter to score a decisive third goal from close range, and the home side always looked the likely winners from then on.

Niall Hughes was narrowly wide from a goal opportunity in the 58th minute, and with that the chance of an away victory ended.

The Downs: Trevor Martin; Eanna Burke, Charlie Drumm, Josh Murtagh; Michael Moloney (0-01), Ciaran Nolan, Joe Moran (1-00); Lorcan Mullen, Jonathan Lynam (0-01); Kevin O’Sullivan (1-02), Ian Martin (0-02, 2pt), Billy Moffatt; Luke Loughlin (0-04, 0-01f), Niall Mitchell (1-01), Tom Tuite (0-01).

Subs: Dean Egerton for Moffatt (38), Philip Martin (0-01) for Tuite (59), Oisín Murphy for Moloney (60+1), Tadhg Nolan for I Martin (60+3).

Kilanerin-Ballyfad: Conor Hughes; Andrew Smyth, Conor Jones, Kian Stedmond; Padraic Hughes (0-01), Eoin Hughes (0-01), Padraig Jones; Tom Modraunt, Mikey Hughes; Niall Hughes (0-01), Vincent Higgins, Conor Devitt; Cian Hughes (0-02), Conor Kinsella (0-02), Dean O’Toole (0-08, 1 2ptf, 0-04f).

Subs: Sean Hughes for M Hughes (h-t), Sean Forde for S Hughes (blood, 39 - 42), Jason Bolger for Higgins (54), Sean Forde for Mordaunt (54), Jamie Hughes for Kinsella (59).

Referee: Anthony Nolan (Wicklow).
