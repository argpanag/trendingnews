Sir Tom Jones 'truly sorry' as he cancels concerts due to illness

TheTools · 31.10.2026 22:20 · source · permalink

"Following doctor's advice", said Sir Tom, he "unfortunately" has had to reschedule two forthcoming shows

  • Published
    27 October 2026

Sir Tom Jones said he was "truly sorry" after rescheduling or cancelling forthcoming concerts in the United States due to "a bad case of the flu".

The 86-year-old singer from Pontypridd, Rhondda Cynon Taf, is currently on his Come Gather Round world tour.

He announced the decision to push back his Monday show in Los Angeles, and Tuesday's concert in San Diego, following doctor's advice.

A later post said that a show in Sacramento on Saturday, and San Francisco on Sunday, would also be postponed "following further advice from my doctor".

"I know this will be disappointing for those of you who were planning to attend, and I thank you for your continued support and understanding," it said.

"Further updates regarding the postponed dates will be shared as soon as possible."

The San Diego concert is due to take place at The Rady Shell, at Jacobs Park, on 3 November, while his Los Angeles show is set for 4 November.

Another gig will be rescheduled to 6 November, taking place at Yaamava' Resort & Casino in Highland, east of Los Angeles.

On Instagram, he wrote: "I've had a great time these last few weeks, completing 20 shows and singing for wonderful audiences across the USA, but unexpectedly I've been hit with a bad case of the flu.

"Following doctor's advice, I unfortunately have to reschedule tonight's show in Los Angeles and tomorrow's show in San Diego."

He added: "I'm truly sorry for any inconvenience caused. I look forward to seeing you all soon.

"All tickets for the original dates will remain valid. However, if customers are unable to attend the rescheduled dates, they are advised to contact their original point of purchase for a refund."

The post was shared just hours before he was expected to perform at the Greek Theatre, with the Los Angeles venue among those wishing Sir Tom well.

Sir Tom posted on Facebook on Thursday, saying: "Following doctor's advice for another day of rest, I unfortunately must reschedule tomorrow night's show in Yaamava Resort & Casino, Highland, CA. to Friday November 6th 2026."

He added he was "truly sorry for any inconvenience caused".

The It's Not Unusual and What's New Pussycat? singer recently made headlines after revealing ITV had "made the decision" to "fire" him from The Voice UK coaching panel.

Sir Tom, who has served as an on-off coach on the popular singing competition since its 2012 debut, shared the news on Instagram last month, amid speculation he had been "dropped" from its 2027 series.

The Delilah and She's A Lady singer has achieved three UK number one singles and four chart-topping albums during a music career spanning more than six decades.

His awards include one Grammy, two Brits and an Ivor Novello; Sir Tom also received a knighthood from the late Queen Elizabeth II in 2006 for his services to music.
