San Antonio police are asking for the public's help finding an endangered missing juvenile who was last seen over the weekend.

Darian Alexandre Rangel was last seen Sunday, Sept. 7, in the 7200 block of Marbach Road near W. Military Drive, according to an SAPD missing person bulletin.

Police describe Rangel as 5'7" tall and weighs about 160 pounds, with brown eyes, brown curly hair and a light brown complexion.

He was last seen wearing a red Bass Pro Shops hat, black shirt, black jeans and black shoes. Police said he was also carrying a crossover Coach bag.

Anyone who has seen Rangel or knows where he may be is asked to contact the SAPD Missing Person Unit at (210) 207-7660.
