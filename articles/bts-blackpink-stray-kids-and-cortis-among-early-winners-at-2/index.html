BTS, BLACKPINK, Stray Kids and CORTIS among early winners at 2026 KGMA

BTS, BLACKPINK, Stray Kids and CORTIS among early winners at 2026 KGMA

As the first day of the 3rd Korea Grand Music Awards (“2026 KGMA”), “Artist Day,” takes place on the 7th, some category winners have been announced ahead of the main ceremony.

According to KGMA’s official social media accounts, BTS was named the winner of “Best Artist” at the “2026 KGMA.” The group’s fifth full-length album, “ARIRANG,” was also named “Best Selling Album,” earning BTS two awards.

Along with BTS, BLACKPINK, Stray Kids and CORTIS each won “Best Artist.” TREASURE won “Best Hip-Hop,” while LUCY took “Best Band,” demonstrating their strength in their respective genres.

DAY6’s Young K won “Best O.S.T” for “The Season Has Returned,” an original soundtrack from the SBS drama “My Royal Nemesis.” The distinctive solo artist Dayoung also celebrated winning “Style Icon.”

In the Kugung fan-voted award categories, NCT Wish’s Yushi received the “Hallyu Popularity Award,” backed by support from global fans, while Flare U won in the “Trend of the Year Rookie” category.

The “2026 KGMA,” a popular music awards ceremony established in 2024, will be held this year on November 7 and 8 at Gocheok Sky Dome in Seoul’s Guro District. RESCENE’s Woni and actor Nam Ji-hyun will host the first day, while IVE’s Liz and actor Kim Jae-won will host the second.

[Lee Da-gyeom, Star Today reporter]
