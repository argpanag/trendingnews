TikTok 'technical issue' resolved after users report issues with platform

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:09 · source · permalink

The TikTok logo is displayed on the screen of a smartphone placed on a reflective surface onto which an abstract illustration in blue and pink is projected. (Photo by Samuel Boivin/NurPhoto via Getty Images)

Thousands of users were reporting issues with the social media platform TikTok on Tuesday.

TikTok users report issues

At 3:00 p.m. Eastern Time, Downdector showed more than 25,000 outage reports with 71% of users citing issues with the app.

The outage was brief, lasting no longer than two hours, according to Downdetector. 

What they're saying:

FOX Local reached out to TikTok, who confirmed the issue and that it had been resolved. 

"We experienced a brief technical issue that impacted access to TikTok," a spokesperson said. "The issue has been resolved. We apologize for the inconvenience and thank you for your patience."

The backstory:

The global social media platform enables its users to create short-form videos with music integration.
