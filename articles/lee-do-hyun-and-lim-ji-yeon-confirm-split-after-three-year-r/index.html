Lee Do-hyun and Lim Ji-yeon confirm split after three-year romance

TheTools · 24.11.2026 17:17 · source · permalink

AI-translated article with transliterated names. Read original Chinese article here.

(Seoul) South Korean actors Lee Do-hyun, 31, and Lim Ji-yeon, 36, fell in love while working on the Netflix Korean drama The Glory (《黑暗荣耀》). Their relationship had appeared steady since they publicly confirmed it in April 2023. As recently as July, they were spotted sharing affectionate moments at a golf course, but news of their split emerged on Tuesday (24 November).

Responding to the break-up rumours, their agencies issued statements on 24 November confirming that the relationship had ended. Lee’s agency said: “The two have indeed broken up and have decided to return to being senior and junior colleagues on good terms. They will continue to support each other in the entertainment industry.” The news left many fans who had supported the couple disappointed.

Reports suggest that their packed work schedules were the main reason for the split. Spending more time apart than together caused them to grow distant, and they decided to part ways after careful consideration.

Both are enjoying successful careers. Lim, who has been acting for many years, appeared in works including Obsessed (《人间中毒》) and High Society (《上流社会》) before reaching a career high with her villainous role in The Glory. Lee built up his acting experience in dramas such as Hotel del Luna (《德鲁纳酒店》) and 18 Again (《重返18岁》), and won Best New Actor in the film category at the 60th Baeksang Arts Awards for Exhuma (《破墓》).
