U.S. Treasury Secretary Bessent said Friday that core inflation remains under control, Iran caused an energy shock and excessive rate hikes would be inappropriate as the shock nears an end.

Bessent said the United States would continue to pressure Iran and would not hesitate to sanction Iranian banks.

The comments echo Bessent’s earlier remarks on core inflation, when he said the Federal Reserve Board should remain open-minded.
