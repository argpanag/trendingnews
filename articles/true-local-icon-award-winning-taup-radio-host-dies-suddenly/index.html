'True local icon': Award-winning Taupō radio host dies suddenly

TheTools · 12.10.2026 00:23 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

More FM Taupō has paid tribute to long-serving breakfast host Andrew Leiataua after the “true local icon” died suddenly.

The station announced the news on Facebook this afternoon.

“It is with great sadness and shock that we need to let you know of the sudden passing of Andrew Leiataua yesterday due to an unexpected medical condition,” the post read.

“Andrew has been with us for 30 years and our More FM breakfast host here in Taupō for 20 years. He was a huge part of our community, and our thoughts are with Andrew’s family at this incredibly difficult time.”

The station said it would share details for those wanting to pay their respects once they became available.

“Sean will be on air tomorrow morning from 6am and would love to hear from you as we grieve and remember our true local icon.”

Leiataua won Best Music Host – Provincial at the 2014 New Zealand Radio Awards for The Andrew Leiataua Breakfast Show.

He was a finalist in the same category in 2011.

Leiataua was also recognised for services to broadcasting at the 2025 NZ Radio and Podcast Awards.

He also appeared on Shortland Street in 1995.

Out of the studio, Leiataua was involved in Taupō community events and fundraisers, including Dancing for Life Education’s inaugural fundraiser in 2019.

He later returned to the event as a judge.
