Crews remove plane from Lake Wausau

TheTools · 13.09.2026 23:57 · source · permalink

WAUSAU, Wis, (WSAW) - Removing the Beechcraft K-35 plane involved in U.S. Rep. Tom Tiffany’s emergency water landing Saturday is a slow and delicate process.

Jerry’s Towing Company is surveying the area around Everest Park in Wausau to plan how to remove the plane.

Recovery plan

The company will have three to four people working to pull out pieces of the aircraft. A pontoon barge will be set up, with 200-foot-long cables pieced together to pull the plane to shore.

The Wisconsin DNR is urging boats to stay away from the crash site.

“We’ll start probably tomorrow morning, do the recovery. We’re getting our divers lined up and everything lined up, and then we’ll notify the DNR, let them be aware that we’re out on the scene, and we’ll run out cable and start pulling it in,” said Mark Bradfish, terminal manager for Jerry’s Towing.

Timeline uncertain

Bradfish said the time it takes to get the plane out could vary based on the weather, water current and how intact the aircraft is underneath the water.

The process will also be slow to prevent the plane’s fuel from spilling into Lake Wausau.
