Alexander Zverev is ready to face one of those matches that can define his career for a while. He has nothing to prove or to assert his position as the number 1 in the world, but the chance to end the year by beating Carlos Alcaraz at the ATP Finals 2026 is motivating for the German. He expressed this in a press conference.

The dream final is here. Since the beginning of these ATP Finals 2026, the vast majority of tennis fans have been hoping to witness a duel between the number 1 in the ATP ranking and the best player on the planet. That’s how the match could be presented, as stated by Alexander Zverev in a surprising press conference, where he spoke of Carlos Alcaraz in glowing terms.

Zverev calls Alcaraz the best returner in the world
