LFC fitness update: Alexander Isak, Cody Gakpo, Federico Chiesa and Jeremy Jacquet - Liverpool FC

TheTools · 10.10.2026 13:31 · source · permalink

Alexander Isak and Cody Gakpo have been ruled out of Liverpool's Premier League meeting with Manchester City.

By Glenn Price

The attacking pair will miss Sunday's game at Anfield due to the issues that forced them to withdraw from their national teams in the recent round of fixtures. 

Speaking at his pre-match press conference on Friday, Iraola said: "Alex Isak and Cody Gakpo will not play the game. They are not ready. 

"Unfortunately for us, both got injuries during the international break. As a club manager, you are always waiting until the games end, that the doctor doesn't call you and then gives you bad news.

"But in this case we've been unlucky with Alex and Cody – both of them are injured and they are not going to be involved on Sunday."

Iraola continued: "Cody has an ankle injury. He has had some before in his career and it's a matter of him dealing with the pain because when you twist your ankle... it was a really bad tackle, I think. 

"He is doing things individually but [he is] not ready for Sunday. 

"And Alex has a thigh injury. It's a small injury but it's a muscle injury. The same [as Gakpo], whenever he feels ready. 

"They both don't look like long term – we will be analysing day to day. But they are not going to arrive on Sunday."

Press Conference: Liverpool vs Man City | Andoni Iraola

However, Jeremy Jacquet is set to be available for the encounter with the current league leaders. 

The 21-year-old made his first two appearances for France's senior side during the break but then sat out their UEFA Nations League tie with Belgium at the beginning of this week.

"Yeah, he's fit, he's ready to go," Iraola said of the defender. "I think he didn't play with the national team [in the] last game because of protection. 

"But he should be fine. We still need to train tomorrow but he's fine, yes."

Federico Chiesa, meanwhile, is back in training after the injury he sustained in pre-season.

Iraola said: "We will maybe recover Fede Chiesa but he hasn't played any minutes. 

"He's recovering from an injury but he has been doing a good job in the international break and has been training with the small group we had."
