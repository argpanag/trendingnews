Spalletti: Victory will restore confidence and we mus… | YSscores

TheTools · 17.09.2026 21:24 · source · permalink

Luciano Spalletti, the manager of Juventus, has stressed the importance of securing a victory at the beginning of the team's UEFA Europa League journey, highlighting the need to overcome the recent loss against US Sassuolo and quickly return to the path of victories.

Luciano Spalletti has stated ahead of the match against Nijmegen: "We need to reset things and move past the loss to US Sassuolo, as carrying its effects with us will not benefit us. We have analyzed what happened, and we need to act differently in those moments."

The Italian coach added: "We have an urgent need to win this match and show that we are Juventus. Victory will restore our confidence, and this confidence can give us a future at a high level."

Luciano Spalletti's comments came before Juventus achieved a comprehensive victory over Nijmegen, winning 5-0, with goals from Nicolo Gonzalez, Aljabaovic, Nick Woltemade from a penalty, Zeki Celik, and Randal Kolo Muani.
