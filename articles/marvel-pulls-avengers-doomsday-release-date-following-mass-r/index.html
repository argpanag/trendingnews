Marvel Pulls ‘Avengers: Doomsday’ Release Date Following Mass Reshoots

in Marvel

Credit: Marvel Studios

Marvel Studios has fixed Avengers: Doomsday for December 18 – but there are signs that this release date may change.

Amid reports of reshoots completed just three months before the film’s release, certain movie theaters have started listing a different date for audiences to first see the upcoming superhero epic on the big screen.

What’s Happening With ‘Avengers: Doomsday’?

Principal photography for Avengers: Doomsday wrapped in September 2025, with The Hollywood Reporter reporting that Marvel had a “healthy” amount of additional photography planned. By June, David Harbour (Red Guardian) confirmed he was back at Pinewood Studios shooting alongside other Avengers.

The work continued deep into the summer. Joe Russo said in June that the team expected to keep working on Doomsday until November, while Winston Duke later confirmed that additional photography was still underway as the film moved through post-production (via CNA).

By late August, insider Daniel Richtman claimed cameras were still rolling and that Marvel was adding “a lot” of cameos. He later reported that new material included Scarlet Witch alongside Sue Storm, although it remained unclear whether Elizabeth Olsen herself or a stunt double filmed Wanda’s scenes.

The latest update suggests that this phase is finally over. Richtman said on September 6 that the reshoots were “officially done,” adding that Marvel had filmed one new scene in particular that “will make a lot of people happy.” What that scene contains remains under wraps – although fans may get to see it on the big screen sooner than expected.

Possible New Release Date for ‘Avengers: Doomsday’

Judging by recent reports, at least one theater is getting Avengers: Doomsday early.

A Reddit user shared a booking for a “special advance screening” at Regency Granada Hills 9 in California at 7:30 p.m. on December 16 — two days before Disney’s currently advertised December 18 release date.

December 17 screenings are much more widespread. The Disney-owned El Capitan Theatre in Hollywood starts showing Doomsday at 1 p.m. that day, while Phoenix Theatres locations list screenings from as early as 1 p.m., and Terrace Theater has showtimes beginning at 2 p.m.

For now, December 16 appears to be the outlier. GamesRadar+ found the Regency screening to be the earliest available, while the official Doomsday ticketing site reportedly begins its listings on December 17. Disney’s own movie page, meanwhile, continues to describe December 18 as the film’s release date.
