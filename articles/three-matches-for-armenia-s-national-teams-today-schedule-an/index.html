Three matches for Armenia's national teams today: schedule and where to watch

TheTools · 02.10.2026 20:32 · source · permalink
  • Oct. 2, 2026, 1:35 p.m.
  • Author: Vahe Hakobyan
  • Sportaran

Today, October 2, three Armenian national football teams will be in action. The U19 and U20 sides will take the field in the afternoon, while the senior national team will conclude the day with a UEFA Nations League match against Cyprus.

All times are listed in Yerevan time.

16:00 — Armenia U19 vs Estonia U19

Armenia U19 will play a friendly match against Estonia U19.

Kick-off: 16:00. There is currently no information available regarding a live broadcast of the match.

16:00 — Armenia U20 vs Uzbekistan U20

Armenia U20 will face Uzbekistan in the Mirabor Usmanov Cup.

Kick-off: 16:00. The match will be available to watch live on YouTube.

Live stream: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VzcDjKl3LDE

20:00 — Cyprus vs Armenia

The main match of the day for Armenian football fans will take place in Nicosia, where the Armenia national team will face Cyprus in the UEFA Nations League.

Kick-off: 20:00.

In Armenia, the match will be broadcast live on H1 and will also be available on the OKKO TV platform.

Armenian football fans will therefore have a busy day, with two youth national team matches starting simultaneously at 16:00 before the senior national team takes centre stage in the evening.
