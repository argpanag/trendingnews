Mega Millions jackpot climbs to $673 million just in time for Black Friday’s drawing

TheTools · 27.11.2026 01:40 · source · permalink

(Gray News) - The Mega Millions jackpot is giving lottery players another reason to dream big this holiday weekend.

There was no winner during the last drawing Tuesday. The winning numbers were 38, 42, 44, 45, 62 and the gold Mega Ball 16.

Now, the top prize has climbed to $673 million.

The winner could choose to receive the full jackpot in 30 annual payments, or they could take a lump sum of about $283 million.

That cash prize could shrink to about $178 million after federal taxes. State taxes could reduce the winnings even further.

The odds of winning the jackpot are about 1 in 290 million.

This would be the fourth Mega Millions jackpot won this year. The last Mega Millions jackpot was won on July 28 by a single ticket bought in Bradenton, Florida.

The next Mega Millions drawing is Friday at 11 p.m.
