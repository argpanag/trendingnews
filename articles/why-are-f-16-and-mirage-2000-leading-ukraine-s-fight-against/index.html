​Why Are F-16 and Mirage 2000 Leading Ukraine's Fight Against Jet-Powered Shaheds, What About MiG-29, Su-27? | Defense Express

TheTools · 21.10.2026 15:04 · source · permalink

Ukraine employs a comprehensive, multi-layered air defense approach against Shahed drones, making use of the full range of available air defense assets. The aviation component plays a particularly important role in this effort, with Western-made F-16 and Mirage 2000 fighter jets taking centre stage. Two priorities are crucial to maximising their operational effectiveness: maintaining ammunition stockpiles sufficient for several months of operations and addressing bottlenecks in aircraft maintenance to increase fleet availability.

These remarks were reported by Ukrainian media following a briefing by the Minister of Defense of Ukraine Evgeniy Khmara. This raises the question: what about the MiG-29s and Su-27s?

From Defense Express we would like to point out that, taken literally, the above could give the impression that the Ukrainian Armed Forces rely exclusively on Western-made F-16s and Mirage 2000s to counter jet-powered UAVs.

However, this perception does not reflect reality. This is evident from the video footage regularly shared online showing MiG-29s shooting down russian Shahed drones.

Nevertheless, it is important to consider the broader context of the discussion surrounding the F-16s and Mirage 2000s. Ukraine's Minister of Defense was speaking to Ukrainian journalists, so it made sense to provide a general overview that would be accessible to a broad audience rather than a highly specialized one.

The key takeaway is that, while the MiG-29 and Su-27 remain important components of the Ukrainian Air Force, Western-made F-16s and Mirage 2000s are increasingly taking on a more prominent role, alongside plans to transition to the Swedish JAS 39 Gripen and French Rafale. The British think tank RUSI addressed this issue in a report published in August 2026.

This, in turn, influences perceptions of the Ukrainian Air Force, which is increasingly seen as a force operating primarily Western-made aircraft.

It is reasonable to assume that, of the 1,300 sorties flown on fighter air defense missions and the more than 250 sorties conducted in strikes against ground targets in September 2026, the majority involved F-16s and Mirage 2000s, while MiG-29s and Su-27s continued to play an important role.

That said, the full list of aircraft deployed to combat russian jet-powered UAVs is much longer. In one of its articles, Defense Express, citing RUSI, reported that the Ukrainian Armed Forces are using PC-9s equipped with miniguns against jet-powered Shaheds.

Ivan Kyrychevskyi, serviceman of the 413th Raid Regiment of the Unmanned Systems Forces of Ukraine and weapons expert at Defense Express.
