The best early Black Friday clothing deals for shoring up your winter fits

TheTools · 23.11.2021 12:00 · source · permalink

The best Black Friday clothing deals are short-lived, but unmissable. So much so that GQ's sale pros now swear that the best Black Friday deals beat even those in January. That makes the end of November peak time to shore up your wardrobe — because the menswear sales are just as important as the fusty old mattress and vacuum cleaner ones.

GQ's best Black Friday clothing deals at a glance…

Abercrombie & Fitch

If your wardrobe is particularly ill-suited to the tempestuous weather now, then the good news is that the likes of Uniqlo, Levi's and Mr Porter have already kick-started their mid-season sales. So you're in dire need of menswear deals; we not only empathise, but are uniquely positioned to help. Senior commerce writer Jessie Atkinson and style writer Marcus Mitropoulos both have boots on the ground in the online rails from Monday to Friday, sorting out what's cooking in new collections and in the sales. So we already know what's worth picking up — even though we're still a few weeks out from Black Friday on 27th November. Here are some of the best deals we've found so far…
