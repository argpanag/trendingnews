The Lakers and the Warriors have finally unveiled their respective starters for Wednesday's opening night showdown.

The Los Angeles Lakers and the Golden State Warriors are set to collide for their first game of the 2026-27 NBA season.

Luka Doncic will officially commence a new era in Los Angeles, facing Stephen Curry who has fully recovered from his ankle injury to keep himself ready to lead the Warriors.

Ahead of tip-off for their regular-season opener this Wednesday at 10:00 PM ET in Crypto.Com Arena, the Lakers and the Warriors have finally revealed their official starters.

The Los Angeles Lakers will be headlined by Luka Doncic and Austin Reaves to open their new NBA season.

Joining the franchise's star duo against the Golden State Warriors are Matisse Thybulle, Ziaire Williams, and Walker Kessler.

This five-man lineup led by Doncic already made their key debut in the Lakers' final preseason game against the Denver Nuggets. Despite losing that exhbition finale, LA is eager to secure a much different result on Wednesday against the Dubs.

On the side of Golden State, Stephen Curry will be joined by De'Anthony Melton, Gui Santos, Yaxel Lendeborg, and Draymond Green. 

As stated, Curry is fully available to play after shedding his probable tag from a sprained right ankle.

Melton earns the starting gig in Golden State with Brandin Podziemski out due to a back injury. Green will further anchor the Warriors at the five with Al Horford coming off the bench.
