Marc-André Barriault’s Durability Meets Kyle Daukaus’ Grappling Advantage

Marc-André Barriault has won one of his last six fights, but his last two appearances earned Fight of the Night honors. Despite his recent record, his entertaining style remains a significant part of his appeal.

Barriault faces Kyle Daukaus, who was initially listed as a -225 favorite. Daukaus lost his previous fight to Bo Nickal after being outstruck and finished on the feet.

Daukaus appears to have the grappling advantage and could use it to control the matchup. However, Barriault still has enough upside to hurt him early. Barriault was once known for his chin, although multiple knockout losses had raised concerns about his durability.

Daukaus is the preferred pick, but the fight is difficult to bet because either man could win by finish. The total is set at 1.5 rounds, with over 1.5 priced at -135. Daukaus was most recently listed as a -185 favorite.
