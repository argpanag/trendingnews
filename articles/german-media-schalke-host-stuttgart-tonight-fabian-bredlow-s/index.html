According to Sport1, Schalke 04 host Stuttgart today at the Veltins-Arena, with kick-off at 18:30 local time. Sport1 explains how to watch the match on TV, live stream, and live ticker.

Schalke 04 have yet to score a goal at the Veltins-Arena in the Bundesliga this season. Tonight, the Royal Blues host Stuttgart, with kick-off at 18:30.

Schalke vs Stuttgart: TV, Live Stream, and Live Ticker

Ahead of the match, Schalke's squad situation has improved: Gosens returned to training this week. The full-back came off in the 1-2 away defeat at Hoffenheim after a knee injury sustained in a collision with an opponent. M.Sylla, Adrian Gantenbein, and Janik Bachmann have also returned to team training; M.Sylla and Adrian Gantenbein had already shown progress in their recovery in team sessions last week. Midfielder Luca Vozar is out with a thigh injury, so coach Miron Muslic cannot call on him.

Stuttgart's contract with goalkeeper Fabian Bredlow extended

For Stuttgart, Fabian Bredlow's future is settled: the goalkeeper's contract has been automatically extended by one year, until 2028. The extension was triggered by a clause tied to a set number of official appearances. This season, Bredlow has made four Bundesliga appearances, plus five more in the DFB-Pokal and Champions League. In the league, Dennis Seimen is currently ahead of him in Stuttgart's goalkeeper pecking order.

Schalke won the last meeting with Stuttgart

The two clubs meet again after more than three and a half years. In their last Bundesliga meeting, Schalke won 2-1 at home. In their last twelve matches against Stuttgart, the Royal Blues have lost only once, a 1-5 defeat in Stuttgart.

Is Schalke 04 vs Stuttgart on free-to-air TV today?

No, this match will not be broadcast on free-to-air TV.

Bundesliga today: Which TV channel and streaming service will broadcast Schalke 04 vs Stuttgart?

The match can be watched live on Sky via TV and streaming.
