Last One Laughing is gloriously inconsequential Halloween hokum

TheTools · 25.10.2026 20:35 · source · permalink

Jimmy Carr unites another troupe of comics, but this time the challenge is not to laugh… or scream

4/5

While half the comedians in Britain are busy being useless on The Celebrity Traitors, the remainder have been rounded up for an agreeably giggle-filled, Halloween-themed mini-season of surprise Prime hit Last One Laughing. Jimmy Carr is back as host, looking more than ever like an evil ventriloquist’s dummy (his joke), while the assembled comics run from the suited and booted Tom Allen to the muted and inscrutable Jack Dee.

As with The Traitors, Last One Laughing had its origins in an international TV show – in this case a Japanese Amazon series in which comedians try to keep a straight face while locked in a room for six hours with other chortle-providers.

With Halloween fast approaching, the format has had a ghoulish makeover: the chamber where the comedians are entombed for the duration is spruced up with scary dolls and fake headstones, and actors dressed as zombies are on hand for bonus chills. The producers have also been clever in bringing in Reece Shearsmith, who, as co-creator of The League of Gentlemen, has a history of walking the line between comedy and horror.

He is joined by the aforementioned Dee and Allen, as well as by, among others, a phlegmatic Lou Sanders and a deadpan Daisy May Cooper. In an adjoining room, Carr and co-presenter Róisín Conaty are on the lookout for the stray guffaw or scream that will earn the contestant a yellow card (two and they’re out).

Comedians do not always play well with others. But there is no bad blood in this gory chucklefest and the comics all get into the spooky spirit from the outset. They are also encouraged to use their imagination. Each participant has a “joker” segment where they take to the stage for a pre-rehearsed routine, invariably involving bizarre costumes and, in one case, some semi-naked wriggling around on the floor.

But it’s the scary stuff that invariably drives them over the edge (remember, under the Halloween rules, screams and laughs are equally verboten). The appearance of a zombie hand in an unusual setting causes one of the bunch to lose it completely. There is also a clever gag involving a prosthetic limb and a cleaver. Fellow comedians pop by, too, for engaging – and often unhinged – cameos.

Last One Laughing is just a few rungs above some YouTube rubbish you might waste 20 minutes on. Yet that disposability is also part of the charm. It’s one of the least consequential half-hours of TV ever created, but it passes the time and, as anyone who has ever got the giggles in an inappropriate setting will attest, it’s always amusing watching someone trying not to laugh.

Yes, four half-hour episodes all devoted to the season of trick-or-treating do over-egg the pumpkin pie slightly. But with the nights drawing in and hordes of children shortly to descend on our doorsteps demanding free sweets, there is surely room in all our lives for some Halloween hokum. And that is exactly what Last One Laughing delivers.

The first three episodes are on Amazon Prime Video now
