Ducks To Recall Roger McQueen From AHL San Diego

TheTools · 13.10.2026 15:43 · source · permalink

According to Elliotte Friedman, the Anaheim Ducks are calling up forward Roger McQueen.

The 20-year-old is entering year one of his three-year entry-level contract, signed back in April out of his freshman campaign at Providence College, scoring 27 points in 36 NCAA games. Further to that, on his amateur tryout agreement, which slid the ELC to this being his first, he added three points in seven games to cap off 2025-26. His AAV is $1.075MM, so with Anaheim sending down defenseman Travis Mitchell earlier, this offsets the move monetarily, allowing the Ducks to hold their current cap space with injured players at around $3.2MM.

The 6-foot-6 Saskatoon native is well-known for his skilled offensive approach that includes constant elusiveness as well as intriguing passes to find opportunities for his teammates. His physicality can also impact the game when he misses the score sheet, too. Despite his injury from his draft year in Brandon, the Ducks still liked taking him at 10th overall in the 2025 NHL Draft.

If he plays tonight, a 9:45 p.m. EST puck drop against the Calgary Flames, it would mark his NHL debut.

This comes after the Ducks suffered two injuries to their forward group in Cutter Gauthier and Ryan Poehling. Both are currently listed as day-to-day according to Anaheim, but there should be more clarity on their situations soon. The Ducks managed to get through the last 14:00 of their game at Calgary with 11 forwards. Playing in 75 and 76 games last year, respectively, Gauthier and Poehling highlight as both steady presences in the lineup. Besides the obvious 40-goal prowess of Gauthier on the wing, the play having Poehling in the mix shows his impact as a third-line center who excels on the penalty kill while offering short-handed offense with his speed.

These losses from the forward group, which includes Troy Terry on long-term injured reserve, require the team to insert a few more younger players on two-way deals into the fold. Potentially, with all the roster turmoil the team endured over the offseason, perhaps this has become a blessing in disguise to see a youthful jolt of energy in Orange County. If McQueen provides help through his strengths, it’ll make this Ducks team a dynamic one in their hope to continue playoff contention.

The Ducks sit in third place in the Pacific Division with 4 wins for 8 points so far. Their lone loss has come against a division opponent, Edmonton, who is a spot above them in the wild card standings.
