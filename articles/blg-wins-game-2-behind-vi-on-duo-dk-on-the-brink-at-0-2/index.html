BLG Wins Game 2 Behind 'Vi-ON' Duo... DK on the Brink at 0-2

Dplus KIA dropped Game 2 following their Game 1 defeat, pushing them to the brink of elimination.

BLG got off to a good start in Game 2 as well. Park 'Viper' Do-hyeon's Ezreal showed quick wit in a 1v3 situation to take down Shin 'Smash' Geum-jae's Yunara, while 'knight' on Orianna initiated with a clean ultimate to catch out Oh 'Career' Hyeong-seok's Lulu. BLG also scored points in the Scuttle Crab fight led by Ezreal and Orianna.

Dplus KIA grouped up to catch 'ON''s Leona, catching a brief breather. Although they unilaterally surrendered dragons and the Rift Herald due to a lack of priority, they ambushed BLG as they retreated after securing the Herald, trading kills 3-2. However, at the crucial timing of the opponent's third dragon, Yunara was picked off, shifting the momentum back to BLG, who then decisively took control with a massive win in the Cloud Soul teamfight.

Around the 30-minute mark, BLG started taking down the Elder Dragon. Jeon 'Siwoo' Si-woo's Kennen urgently joined using Teleport as Dplus KIA attempted to defend, but the Elder Dragon had already been secured by BLG. Dplus KIA had no choice but to retreat, and pushing right through, BLG destroyed Dplus KIA's Nexus for the second time.
