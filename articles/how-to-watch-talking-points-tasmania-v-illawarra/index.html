Talking points

Stattmann seizing his chance

Injuries have tested Tasmania’s depth all season, but the JackJumpers keep finding players ready to step up, and right now it’s Kody Stattmann.

A few years ago it was Sean Macdonald, and this year it may be Kody Stattmann, who is coming off his career-best game on Sunday.

Back home in Hobart and sitting near the top of the ladder, can Stattmann back it up and prove he deserves a permanent spot in this rotation?

Hawks flying high

Illawarra has turned its season around since sitting last, with the Hawks storming home against Adelaide on Thursday night for another big win.

The challenge now is the quick turnaround, with a trip to Hobart less than 48 hours later to face a JackJumpers side that handled them comfortably earlier in the season.

Can the Hawks carry that momentum into one of the toughest road trips in the league, or will the short break catch up with them?
