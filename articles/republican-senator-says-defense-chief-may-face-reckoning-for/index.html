Republican senator says defense chief may face ‘reckoning’ for Pentagon funding

TheTools · 04.11.2026 19:05 · source · permalink

Reporting the Changing World

Anadolu Ajansı © 2026

US Republican Sen. Thom Tillis said Wednesday that Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth could face serious trouble in Congress after the midterm elections, questioning whether the Pentagon can secure the funding it needs under his leadership.

“Hegseth has a reckoning,” Tillis told CBS News. “He is one of the reasons why we don’t have the funding or guarantee for defense that we need.

Tillis said Hegseth has become an obstacle to defense funding and urged lawmakers to reconsider whether he is the right person to lead the Pentagon.

Democrats have threatened to block $70 billion in emergency military funding and the annual defense spending bill, as the US remains involved in a war with Iran.

Hegseth could also face an impeachment vote in the House of Representatives, with Republican Rep. Thomas Massie pushing for action later this month or in December.

Tillis, who voted to confirm Hegseth in January 2025, has become one of his sharpest Republican critics.

He called the vote to confirm Hegseth “the biggest regret” of his career, accusing the defense chief of poor leadership and pushing out experienced military officials.

Last month, Tillis went further, urging President Donald Trump to replace Hegseth with someone who can strengthen the military and earn bipartisan support in Congress.

“I have never witnessed more inept management of the brave men and women who serve our country. He is intimidated by competence and retreats to ginning up culture wars instead of soberly attending to the vital work of our national defense and the health and well-being of our fighting force,” Tillis posted last month on US social media company X.

“I urge the president to find a new leader at the Pentagon who will retain and empower our military talent rather than diminish it,” he added.
