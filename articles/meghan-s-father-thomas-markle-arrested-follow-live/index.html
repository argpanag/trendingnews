Meghan's father Thomas Markle arrested - follow live

TheTools · 06.11.2026 17:53 · source · permalink

Summary

  • Thomas Markle, the father of Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, has been charged over the possession of child sex abuse material

  • He is due to appear in court in Tampa, Florida, later at about 18:30 GMT (13:30 ET)

  • Markle was arrested after the FBI searched two Los Angeles-area storage units he rented for almost 20 years - here's more from the Department of Justice statement

  • Images published by news outlets alongside reports of the 82-year-old's arrest appear to show him in handcuffs surrounded by FBI personnel

  • Markle has previously denied allegations of possessing images of child sex abuse, calling the claims "sick lies"

  • The duchess has been estranged from her father since 2018 - in a statement last month, the wife of Prince Harry said: "I am deeply distressed... my thoughts are with any children who may have been harmed"

  • She added that she hoped it could be understood why she had largely cut off contact from her father

    The Duke and Duchess of Sussex are so far not commenting on the arrest of Meghan’s father, according to a spokesman for the couple.

    But on Tuesday, Prince Harry and Meghan are due to appear in Brussels, opening the so-called Lost Screen Memorial, which remembers children who lost their lives because of the harms of social media. That’s likely to include a speech about keeping children safe online.

  5. Markle 'allegedly targeted innocent children', says FBI directorpublished at 16:30 GMT
    16:30 GMT

    FBI director Kash Patel says authorities "moved quickly" in the arrest of Thomas Markle.

    In a statement, Patel says: "Mr. Markle allegedly targeted innocent children and sought to steal their innocence through his criminal actions."

    FBI agents worked with the US Department of Justice and "moved quickly, identified and located the explicit material, and arrested Mr. Markle in Florida", he says.

    Patel adds: "This FBI will use every single resource at our disposal to protect innocent kids – any predator who targets children will have zero safe haven in this country or anywhere they try to hide."

  6. Markle due to appear in court in Florida on Fridaypublished at 16:14 GMT
    16:14 GMT

    We can now bring you more from the statement released just now by the US Department of Justice.

    Thomas Markle, the statement says, has been "charged with one count of possession of child pornography".

    The statement alleges Markle "kept child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including sexually explicit photographs of himself with prepubescent girls, in two Los Angeles-area storage units he rented for nearly two decades".

    Giving detail about the searches leading to Markle's arrest, the statement describes searches of Los Angeles storage units rented by Markle since 2008.

    Inside, it says, "agents seized approximately 78 items, including slides, film negatives, and books that contained suspected CSAM (child sexual abuse material)".

    Markle is expected to appear later today in United States District Court in the Middle District of Florida in Tampa, the statement adds.
