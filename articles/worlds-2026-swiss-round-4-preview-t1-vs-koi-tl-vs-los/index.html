Worlds 2026 Swiss Round 4 Preview: T1 vs. KOI, TL vs. LOS

TheTools · 27.10.2026 15:31 · source · permalink

Team Liquid Alienware and LOS swept elimination series Monday to stay alive at the League of Legends World Championship, setting up a Swiss Stage Round 4 that opens Wednesday with T1 vs. Movistar KOI in Allen, Texas.

(Riot Games)

Team Liquid Alienware and LOS swept elimination series Monday to stay alive at the League of Legends World Championship, setting up a Swiss Stage Round 4 that opens Wednesday with T1 vs. Movistar KOI in Allen, Texas.

Dplus KIA and Gen.G already reached the quarterfinals with 3-0 records Sunday, as RG.org reported, after Dplus KIA beat T1 in Round 2, as RG.org reported. Both South Korean teams had won the Round 3 best-of-threes RG.org outlined in its Round 3 preview. Monday's results eliminated Anyone's Legend and Team Secret Whales and settled the Round 4 bracket, which Riot has drawn with every series a best-of-three.

Team Liquid Knock Out Anyone's Legend

Team Liquid swept Anyone's Legend 2-0 in a series that started about an hour early, at 5 p.m. ET, Pogwire reported. Team Liquid won Game 1 in about 33 minutes with a 16-5 kill edge, then took Game 2 in about 47 minutes, when kills finished level at 18 but Team Liquid took both Barons and 11 towers to three. Anyone's Legend, the LPL champion, is out at 0-3.

LOS, Brazil's only team in the Swiss Stage, earned its first win by sweeping Team Secret Whales 2-0. LOS won Game 1 in about 40 minutes with two Barons and Game 2 in about 37 minutes, 22 kills to 13. Team Secret Whales is out at 0-3.

In Monday's best-of-one matches, T1 beat CTBC Flying Oyster in about 27 minutes, 14 kills to 6, and Bilibili Gaming beat Karmine Corp in about 36 minutes, 14 kills to 11, with the game's only Baron.

Round 4 Schedule and Stakes

Six teams sit at 2-1 and will play for quarterfinal spots: Movistar KOI, LYON, Invictus Gaming, Top Esports, T1 and Bilibili Gaming. Hanwha Life Esports, G2 Esports, CTBC Flying Oyster, Karmine Corp, LOS and Team Liquid are 1-2 and face elimination with another loss.

Round 4 schedule (all times ET, all best-of-three):

  • Wednesday, Oct. 28: Movistar KOI vs. T1, 4 p.m.; LYON vs. Invictus Gaming, 7 p.m.
  • Thursday, Oct. 29: CTBC Flying Oyster vs. Karmine Corp, 4 p.m.; Bilibili Gaming vs. Top Esports, 7 p.m.
  • Friday, Oct. 30: Team Liquid vs. LOS, 4 p.m.; G2 Esports vs. Hanwha Life Esports, 7 p.m.

T1, the three-time defending champion, can reach the quarterfinals with a win over Movistar KOI, which lost its Round 3 series to Dplus KIA. Team Liquid, one of two LCS teams still alive along with LYON, gets LOS on Friday. Round 5 follows Saturday, Oct. 31, from 1 p.m. ET.

On Polymarket, Gen.G was the favorite to win the title at about 43% as of 3 a.m. ET Tuesday, followed by T1 at about 15%. Team Liquid was priced below 1%.

The Swiss Stage runs through Oct. 31 at the Credit Union of Texas Event Center in Allen. The quarterfinals (Nov. 3-6) and semifinals (Nov. 7-8) also stay in Allen before the grand final moves to Barclays Center in Brooklyn on Nov. 14.
