Pakistan announce new men's central contracts list | ICC

TheTools · 21.11.2026 06:44 · source · permalink

The Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) announced a new central contracts structure for 38 men’s cricketers, which will conclude on 30 June, 2027.

Babar Azam and Salman Ali Agha are the only players in the men's Test and ODI category of the men's central contracts announced by the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB). 

The contracts are due to run midway till 2027, and have the most number of players under the development category.

Babar Azam was re-appointed as the Test skipper recently, and led the side to their first-ever Test series win in two years after the side defeated Sri Lanka in Rawalpindi on 20 November.

He has been offered both Test and ODI contracts, with experienced all-rounder Salman Ali Agha, also being offered the same. Salman, who was Pakistan's T20I captain a few months back before being replaced by Sahibzada Farhan, does not have a T20I contract with him.

Youngster Azan Awais, middle-order batter Saud Shakeel, and experienced bowlers Mohammad Abbas and Sajid Khan received Test contracts.

Abrar Ahmed, Arafat Minhas, Fakhar Zaman, Faheem Ashraf, Haris Rauf and Shaheen Afridi have been zeroed in on ODI and T20I contracts.

Pakistan's T20I skipper Sahibzada along with Hasan Nawaz, Maaz Sadaqat, Mohammad Salman Mirza, Naseem Shah, Shadab Khan and Usman Tariq hold exclusive T20I contracts.

Pakistan Men's Central Contracts

Contract (Test and ODI):

Babar Azam and Salman Ali Agha

Contract (Test):

Azan Awais, Mohammad Abbas, Sajid Khan and Saud Shakeel

Contract (ODI and T20I):

Abrar Ahmed, Arafat Minhas, Fakhar Zaman, Faheem Ashraf, Haris Rauf and Shaheen Afridi

Contract (T20I):

Hasan Nawaz, Maaz Sadaqat, Mohammad Salman Mirza, Naseem Shah, Sahibzada Farhan, Shadab Khan and Usman Tariq

Contract (Development):

Abdul Samad, Abdullah Fazal, Ahmed Daniyal, Ali Raza, Farhan Yousaf, Hunain Shah, Mohammad Taha, Mohammad Tayyab Arif, Muhammad Ghazi Ghori, Muhammad Imran Randhawa, Razaullah, Saad Baig, Saad Masood, Sameer Minhas, Saqib Khan, Shahzaib Khan, Shamyl Hussain, Sufyan Moqim and Ubaid Shah

With 19 players accounted for various contract categories already mentioned, the remaining players have been offered development contract.

These include up-and-coming youngsters like Tayyab Arif and Ghazi Ghori, both of whom featured in Pakistan's win over Sri Lanka in Rawalpindi. It also includes Naseem Shah's brothers Ubaid Shah, who featured in a Test earlier this year, and Hunain Shah.
