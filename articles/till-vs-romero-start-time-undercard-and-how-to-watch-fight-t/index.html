Darren Till and Yoel Romero will meet in a long-awaited fight, and they will do so under bare-knuckle rules this evening.

The former UFC title challengers will headline a Bare Knuckle Fighting Championship (BKFC) card in Manchester, in a sport where Romero has the experience advantage.

Still, Till debuted successfully in BKFC in May, following a fine run of form in the boxing ring since his UFC exit in 2022.

Now, the Liverpudlian star tests himself against Cuba’s Romero, who is still dangerous at 49 years old.

Here’s all you need to know.

When is the fight?

Till vs Romero will take place at Manchester’s AO Arena on Saturday 26 September. The main card is due to begin at 7pm BST (11am PT / 1pm CT / 2pm ET), with main-event ring walks expected at 10pm BST (2pm PT / 4pm CT / 5pm ET).

How can I watch it?

The event will stream live exclusively on DAZN pay-per-view, at a cost of £24.99. You can purchase the fight on DAZN here.
