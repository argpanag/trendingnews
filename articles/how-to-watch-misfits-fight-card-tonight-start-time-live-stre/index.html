How to watch Misfits fight card tonight - start time, live stream and full card

TheTools · 12.09.2026 16:32 · source · permalink

Two-time Olympic gold medallist Jade Jones continues her boxing journey this weekend as she takes on Hungarian MMA fighter Sheena Bathory for the Misfits super-middleweight title.

The 33-year-old from Flint quit taekwondo for boxing 18 months ago and now has a professional career in the ring in her sights.

But first she must get past Bathory as part of an eye-catching fight card in Newcastle on Saturday night. Here's everything you need to know ahead of the big fight night.

Other big fights on the card include Ibiza Final Boss vs GK Kelly (approx 8.30pm) and Jordan McCann vs Big Stacks (approx 10pm).

What time is Jade Jones vs Sheena Bathory?

The main card is scheduled to begin at 5.30pm BST on Saturday, September 12 for viewers in the UK.

The ringwalks for Jones and Bathory are expected to happen just before 9pm.

However, those timings are subject to change depending on how long the earlier fights last.

Jordan McCann vs Big Stacks is included with a DAZN subscription from £9.99 in a limited-time deal on the broadcaster's monthly-rolling plan.

How to watch Jade Jones vs Sheena Bathory

The Misfits Boxing event will be shown live on DAZN.

The card can be streamed through the DAZN app or website, with the main card scheduled to begin at 7pm BST.

A standard DAZN subscription is listed at £9.99 per month, while DAZN Ultimate costs £24.99 per month and includes a minimum of 12 pay-per-view events at no additional cost.

Jordan McCann vs Big Stacks is included with a DAZN subscription from £9.99 in a limited-time deal on the broadcaster's monthly-rolling plan.

Can I watch the fight through Amazon?

Yes, DAZN is available through compatible Amazon Fire TV devices.

Viewers can download the DAZN app through the Amazon Fire TV app store and then sign in to their DAZN account.

The fight is being broadcast by DAZN rather than as a separate Amazon Prime Video event.
