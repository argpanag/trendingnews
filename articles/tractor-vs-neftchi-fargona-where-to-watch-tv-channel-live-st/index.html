Tractor and Neftchi Fargona meet in the AFC Champions League Elite in what represents a significant test for both clubs as they look to strengthen their positions in the West group.

Neftchi Fargona, coached by Islom Ismoilov, arrive having lost 1-2 to Al Ittihad in their last AFC Champions League Elite match. The Uzbek side have been active domestically, picking up back-to-back wins in the Uzbek League ahead of this trip.

Both clubs sit in the lower half of the West group standings, meaning points here carry real weight for their continental ambitions. A defeat for either side would make the path to progression considerably harder.
