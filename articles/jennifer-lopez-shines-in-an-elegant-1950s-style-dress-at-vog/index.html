Jennifer Lopez Shines in An Elegant 1950s-Style Dress at Vogue World 2026

TheTools · 22.09.2026 17:59 · source · permalink

At Vogue World 2026, Jennifer Lopez is one of the must-see celebrities celebrating the convergence of fashion, culture, and philanthropy. As expected, the singer made a triumphant entrance at the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, the iconic glass-roofed shopping arcade located in the heart of the city, where the fifth edition of the Vogue event – which reinvents the traditional runway – is taking place today. The significance of this major fashion celebration is such that it took place on the first day of Milan Fashion Week, which is why the most prominent figures from the creative industries are now gathered in one place.

Experience the magic of Vogue World 2026: Milano for yourself. Watch the show live on Tuesday, September 22, at 2 p.m. EDT / 11 a.m. PDT

While all the celebrities are shining brightly at Vogue World 2026, JLo has once again stolen the show. During the event, the actress wore an elegant A-line dress reminiscent of 1950s fashion. The dress in question is a black tulle design with sequin embellishments from the bodice to the skirt and is by Dolce & Gabbana. Jennifer Lopez paired it with matching sheer opera gloves, as well as a set of turquoise-toned diamond jewelry.

As for her hairstyle and makeup, the actress opted for a pink-toned lip look that matched her blush and eyeshadow. She wore her hair loose and wavy, a choice that made her look classic yet modern.

Vogue World 2026 in Milan is dedicated to honoring the craftsmanship and the human side of fashion that have always characterised “Made in Italy.” Of course, Jennifer Lopez has become a spokesperson for these values on more than one occasion – whenever she has worn Italian designs, whether as a front-row guest at Dolce & Gabbana fashion shows or when she has donned looks by Gucci, Etro, and Ferragamo.
