Two taken to hospital after Porsche EV battery catches fire at car workshop

TheTools · 20.11.2026 02:12 · source · permalink

AI-translated article with transliterated names. Read original Chinese article here.

A fire broke out at a car workshop on Sin Ming Drive when the battery of a Porsche electric vehicle suddenly caught fire as workers were removing it. Repeated explosions, thick smoke and flying flames created a frightening scene.

The fire broke out at around 1.15pm on Thursday (19 November) at Koh & Lim Motoring Services, a workshop on the fifth floor of Sin Ming AutoCity at 160 Sin Ming Drive.

Video footage provided by a Lianhe Zaobao reader showed thick smoke billowing from the workshop, accompanied by loud explosions.

When a reporter arrived at around 4pm, the fire had been brought under control, but firefighters were still damping down the affected area. At least two fire engines were parked at the scene, with several firefighters and police officers on standby.

Mr Yang, 44, an employee at a nearby car workshop, said he heard an explosion and rushed out to investigate, only to see thick smoke pouring from the affected unit.

Footage provided by Mr Yang showed a Porsche Taycan electric vehicle raised off the ground, with flames repeatedly flaring beneath it and a series of explosions audible.

Mr Yang said the burning object was believed to be the Porsche electric vehicle’s battery, which had caught fire for unknown reasons after being removed.

The footage showed at least four employees at the affected workshop hurriedly moving nearby items away after the battery developed a problem. One also fetched a fire extinguisher and tried to put out the flames.

Mr Yang said: “The workers later came over to our unit to borrow a fire extinguisher. There were three explosions.”

The reporter saw at least three fire extinguishers left on the floor of the affected unit after the incident.

Another mechanic, 62, who declined to be named, said employees at the affected unit initially put out the fire, but it soon reignited and explosions were heard. “This is the first time I have encountered such a serious fire in my nine years working here.”

In response to queries from Lianhe Zaobao, the Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF) confirmed that it was alerted at around 1.35pm to a fire involving an electric vehicle battery at a car workshop on the fifth floor of an industrial building.

SCDF personnel extinguished the fire using two water jets and took two people who had suffered smoke inhalation to Singapore General Hospital for treatment.

The cause of the fire is still under investigation.
