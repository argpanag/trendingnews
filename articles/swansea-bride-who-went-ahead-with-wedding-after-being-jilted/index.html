Swansea bride who went ahead with wedding after being jilted gets married

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:01 · source · permalink

A bride who became a social media sensation after being jilted at the altar has described the "pure magic" of finally walking down the aisle.

Kayley Stead's fiance didn't turn up for her big day in 2022, but she surprised everyone by letting the celebration continue with many agreeing to "just go with this madness".

Four years on and she's found love again, reconnecting with an old university friend who did turn up for their ceremony on Friday.

Stead walked down the aisle in a bespoke wedding dress to applause, later saying she felt everyone was relieved the wedding was actually happening this time.

Marrying Richard Perrott was everything she had imagined, Stead told Radio Wales Breakfast.

"As soon as I started walking down the aisle, everyone just started clapping, because I think everyone was relieved that this is actually happening," she said.

She described exchanging vows with Perrott as a moment when "everyone else just kind of disappears and it's just you two in the room".

"It just felt magical," she added.

Her new chapter in life was also marked by a change in style.

Stead described her dress in 2022 as being "kind of like an armour, rather than a bridal dress".

Her new dress, from designer April Brambury, made her feel "a million bucks".

"I wanted a dress that celebrated my body, who I am as a woman, and I didn't want to shy away from that."

She said being able to hold his hands and share vows "was everything I pictured and more".
