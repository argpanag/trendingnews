David Jones misses 2026 accounts deadline; five loss-making stores in focus

TheTools · 02.11.2026 07:33 · source · permalink

David Jones has failed to meet a deadline to file its 2026 accounts with the corporate regulator, while the department store group pursues the closure of its loss-making stores, including five in Westfield shopping centres.

The upmarket department’s auditor, PwC, has not yet signed off on the accounts, which it was required to file with the Australian Securities and Investments Commission by October 30.
