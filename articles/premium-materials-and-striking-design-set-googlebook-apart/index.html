Google just launched five new premium laptops called Googlebooks that are built to be super fast and durable. They feature high-quality screens, great sound, and powerful chips that handle AI tasks without getting loud or hot. Each model comes with extra perks like cloud storage and creative software subscriptions. You can pre-order them now if you're looking for a high-end upgrade.

Beautiful on the outside and powerhouses on the inside, Googlebook sets a new standard for hardware. Here’s what to expect from our new category of premium laptops.

Five distinctive models

Premium hardware shouldn’t be one-size-fits-all, so we’re launching five flagship models built with the world's best laptop makers, starting at $899.

Acer: The sole convertible in the lineup, it shifts from laptop to tablet with ease.

ASUS: Feather-light at just 2.2 pounds, it’s the ultimate travel companion.

Dell: Bold and flashy, its height, weight, and display are perfectly balanced.

HP: Elegance defined, it’s as beautiful closed as it is open.

Lenovo: With a spacious 15-inch display built for productivity, it’s the ultimate workhorse.

Master craftsmanship

Every Googlebook is made from premium materials like aluminum, magnesium alloy, and carbon fiber, making them exceptionally light, sleek, and durable. They feature touchscreens with up to 2.8K resolution that are bright enough to view outdoors in direct sunlight without a glare.

Our partners spent months fine-tuning the features that you engage with most.

  • Custom keyboards: backlit layouts designed for fast, clean typing
  • Haptic trackpads: large, glass-feel surfaces with precise haptic feedback and native OS gesture support
  • Cinematic audio: up to six speakers with Dolby Atmos surround sound, paired with studio-quality, background noise-canceling microphones

Interactive Glowbar design

We’re bringing back the Glowbar, which dances when you boot up, sweeps to show battery level, and supports other playful patterns. We’re also opening Glowbar access to developers to build custom, interactive animations.

Silent, next-gen power

We teamed up with Intel, Qualcomm, and MediaTek to ensure Googlebook keeps up with developers and creators alike.

At launch, you can choose between Intel Core Ultra Series 3 and Snapdragon X Elite processors, which are paired with advanced NPUs running at over 45 TOPS of on-device AI power. This lets you run complex AI workflows without spinning loud and disruptive fans.

  • Memory: a baseline of 16GB of high-speed RAM across the entire portfolio, and some models go up to 32GB
  • Battery: up to 14 hours of video playback and 16 hours of web browsing

Pricing, availability, and pre-order details

Starting at $899, every Googlebook includes 12 months of Google AI Pro providing 5TB of cloud storage and higher usage limits in Gemini, plus 3 months of YouTube Premium, Adobe Photoshop, and more. Googlebook OS also comes with regular feature drops and updates for up to 10 years.

Pre-orders are now open on the Google Store, Best Buy, and other select retailers. Devices hit shelves on October 4 in the U.S., and on October 5 in Canada, the U.K., Ireland, France, Germany, and Australia.
