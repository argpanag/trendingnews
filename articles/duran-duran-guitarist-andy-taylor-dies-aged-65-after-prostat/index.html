Duran Duran guitarist Andy Taylor dies aged 65 after prostate cancer battle

TheTools · 25.11.2026 22:15 · source · permalink

Former Duran Duran guitarist Andy Taylor has died aged 65 “following a long and courageous battle with prostate cancer”, the band has announced.

Taylor rose to fame as a member of the award-winning rock band in the 1980s, famous for tracks such as Hungry Like the Wolf and Rio.

In a statement, the band said: “Andy Taylor, beloved husband, father and grandfather, has died following a long and courageous battle with prostate cancer.

He is survived by his wife of more than 40 years Tracey Wilson, who praised him as “the most wonderful husband, a loving dad and the proudest grandfather”.

“He was the rock of our family,” she added.

Lead singer Simon Le Bon said: “There’s a massive Andy Taylor shaped hole in my heart. It’ll never close up; I won’t let it. I will miss him & love him for all my days.”
