MPL S18 Weekly Watch: Key Matches For Week 3 - Hotspawn

MPL S18 of all franchise leagues are entering the midpoint with big organizations found in unfamiliar positions. While some teams have come out of the gates strong, others are struggling to replicate the form that made them international contenders.

As we progress the story of all 3 regions, here are the key matches to watch across MPL Philippines, Indonesia, and Malaysia.

Two teams desperate to end their win drought will meet in the opening match of Week 3. Aurora PH have yet to escape the slump that followed their historic M7 World Championship run. After lifting the world championship last season, Aurora now find themselves in danger of missing the playoffs entirely.

Failing to qualify would also mean losing their opportunity to defend their M7 title later this year. With every match becoming more important, Aurora Gaming PH needs to find their form sooner.

Twisted Minds PH are facing a similar problem. Despite rebuilding their roster ahead of the new season, the team has yet to show any improvement compared to their previous season run. They managed to reach the playoffs last season, but their current performance would not even land them a chance to contest for this season’s crown.

Can Aurora finally end their drought, or will Twisted Minds turn their own struggles around first?

Prediction: Aurora Gaming PH 2-1 Twisted Minds PH

Another clash between two of the Philippines’ biggest names awaits in Week 3. ONIC PH remains undefeated in the new season, quickly establishing themselves as the team to beat while the region’s previous kings have struggled to maintain their momentum.

ONIC appear to be returning to the form that made them one of the Philippines’ most praised organizations, setting themselves up for another rerun of their previous M6 form. Team Falcons PH, however, have already shown that they can take down the reigning champions. They defeated Team Liquid PH during the opening week, but their momentum was halted by an unexpected loss to Smart Omega.

A victory for Falcons could immediately put them back on track, while a win for ONIC would further solidify their position at the top. Can ONIC maintain their perfect run, or will Team Falcons become the first team to finally bring them down?

Prediction: ONIC PH 2-1 Team Falcons PH

Geek Fam ID desperately need to wake up as they are still searching for their first victory after releasing their long time captain, Baloyskie. Their chances of making the playoffs are dwindling down with every series they drop.

Standing in their way is Alter Ego for their first win in the league. After reaching the Grand Finals of M7 and taking Aurora Gaming PH all the way to the Grand Finals, Alter Ego have struggled to attain that level of performance in the new season. Their current slump is very similar to Aurora PH’s situation in the Philippines. Both teams reached the peak of the world stage and are now struggling to find the same consistency domestically.

Can Geek Fam finally put a win on the board, or will Alter Ego push them even closer to elimination?

Prediction: Alter Ego 2-0 Geek Fam ID

Two of MPL Indonesia’s biggest organizations find themselves in a surprisingly precarious position. ONIC and EVOS are both stuck in the middle of the standings, far from where fans would traditionally expect to see them, especially ONIC.

ONIC’s struggles have been particularly unexpected. The Indonesian powerhouse suffered three consecutive losses against teams they would normally be expected to defeat. After finishing 3rd in MSC 2026, they now find themselves struggling to be consistent.

EVOS are experiencing a similar case with ONIC. After their run last regular season and returning to the playoffs, they now find themselves fighting to avoid getting lost in the middle of the standings.

Can ONIC finally rediscover their winning form, or will EVOS emerge from the pack?

Prediction: ONIC 2-1 EVOS

Two teams still in their rebuild phase will match up as AC Esports take on Team Flash. Both organizations are still adjusting to their new line ups where the victor will be determine as which squad has adapted faster.

With neither team fully settled yet, early-season chemistry could prove to be the deciding factor. Small improvements in communication and coordination can make the difference between victory and defeat when rebuilt rosters meet.

Which team will adjust to the new MPL Malaysia environment first?

Prediction: AC Esports 2-1 Team Flash

Selangor Red Giants remain the benchmark that every team in MPL Malaysia must eventually overcome. Their consistency and dominance remain to be the team to beat in their region, but Team Vamos have the experience and talent to at least challenge the reigning giants.

For Team Vamos, this is an opportunity to prove that they can compete with Malaysia’s strongest team. For SRG, another victory would only strengthen their positioning in the league.

Will Team Vamos finally best the giants, or will Selangor Red Giants continue their reign?

Prediction: Selangor Red Giants 2-1 Team Vamos

As the franchise leagues of MPL Season 18 progresses, the early struggles of several former contenders are becoming more prominent for their fans to take concern. With playoff races beginning to take shape, Week 3 and Week 4 could be a turning point for teams looking to recover before the gap becomes too difficult to close.
