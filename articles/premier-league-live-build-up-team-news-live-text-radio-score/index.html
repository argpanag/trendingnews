  3. ‘I believe in football’published at 20:15 BST
    20:15 BST

    FT: Man Utd 1-1 Tottenham

    Tottenham Hotspur

    Tottenham boss Roberto de Zerbi, speaking to BBC MOTD: “It’s not the first time. I think we are playing very well. We can’t forget the opponent. Big players. But I think in the first half we deserved to be two goals up. The first part of the second half, we suffered. The red card was the switch. It was the right reaction after the red card because we deserved to score.

    “Bentancur is a good guy, top player, great character. He made mistake but in this moment, this club needs a reaction. Sometimes it’s not nice, it’s not right.

    “I want to see the same character, the same personality, the same personality we did this afternoon.

    “I believe in football if we work well and if we are serious people and have qualities, you will make results.

    “It’s difficult to understand but if we are on the bottom of the table, we have to stay there. We have to be a team and then we find the results.”

  5. 'Boos hurt a lot'published at 20:08 BST
    20:08 BST

    FT: Man Utd 1-1 Tottenham

    Manchester United

    Manchester United manager Michael Carrick, speaking to BBC Match of the Day: "It was a mixed game. We found it tough to start with. For a number of reasons, we were not quite there. You could see it was a challenging time.

    "But the lads grew into the game, in the second half I thought we were better in everything. To get the game as we wanted it, in such a good position - we should see it through. We have to manage those situations. That's two late goals which have hurt us, and things look very different. That's the reality."

    On starting slow: "I don't think it is lack of confidence, but a game can get away from you very quickly. We found a way right back into the game, and got ourselves in a great position - that is one of the most disappointing things."

    On the boos at half-time and full-time: "It's not nice, it hurts a lot because I care about this club. But I understand the disappointment, the reality is what it is, we need to get back to winning games."

  7. ‘I wouldn’t say it feels like a win’published at 20:05 BST
    20:05 BST

    FT: Man Utd 1-1 Tottenham

    Tottenham Hotspur

    Tottenham striker Dominic Solanke, speaking to BBC MOTD: “I wouldn’t say it feels like a win. In the first half, we played so well. Dominated the majority and should have been a couple of goals ahead. In the second half, we go and man down and still managed to get something from the game. It shows the mentality and the character of the team.

    “We’ve been a goal down and not reacted in the right way, we wanted to put that right. Goal down, man down, it would have been easy for us to not go for the win.

    “I probably should’ve had three goals today. I wanted to make sure I kept getting as many as I can. I put one away so I’m thankful for that.

    On the red card: “It all happened so quickly so I haven’t seen it back yet. It shows the fight in the team. Everyone is so hungry to do the right thing and show the right passion.

    “We’ve got so much to work on. The mentality and character was great. We’re going to put things right eventually and we’ll be flying sooner rather than later.”

  13. 'I have seen the right spirit and character'published at 19:54 BST
    19:54 BST

    Tottenham Hotspur

    Tottenham boss Roberto de Zerbi, speaking to Sky Sports: "I think we played very well against a great team. We had many chances to score in the first half. Three or four.
