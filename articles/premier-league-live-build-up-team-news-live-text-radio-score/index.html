  3. 'We are able to do more and better'published at 16:56 GMT
    16:56 GMT

    FT: Crystal Palace 1-3 Liverpool

    Crystal Palace

    Crystal Palace boss Pierre Sage, speaking to Sky Sports: "I always say the same thing about what we deserve and what we have.

    "We started really well, created a lot of chances but without scoring. They score a goal from a set piece which is the result of a transition.

    "It's really difficult against this team because they have a lot of speed and are really efficient in transition. That's why we struggled in the second half. We have to come back in the play because of the score to make it 1-1 but we left too much space and they took it easily."

    On differences between first and second half: "Football is about efficiency. If we create these chances it's a good step but the next step is to score because if not, it's as if you did nothing.

    "I'm really frustrated for the fans and the players because I think we are able to do more and better."

    On goals conceded: "The first one is a mistake, we lose the ball high and we fall on the side and they score the set piece. The second and the third, and another one before that Dean [Henderson] saves, it is about the transition and they are quicker than us.

    "If we leave too much space as we did, the scenario was for them. To concede the first goal was a big problem for us and a tactical problem second half."

    On his confidence results will improve: "It's always the same thing. We'll continue our work, we have to be more efficient in what we do, I have to be really close to the players to create the atmosphere for the confidence and togetherness when we defend.

    "We are not too close but, for the moment, we don't have the results expected."

    On if he has support of the board: "You'd have to ask them but, in my position, I am only focused on what I have to do. I analyse what's happened and try to change things. All the other decisions are not for me."

  12. 'I'm very upset with the penalty'published at 16:27 GMT
    16:27 GMT

    FT: Crystal Palace 1-3 Liverpool

    Liverpool

    Liverpool head coach Andoni Iraola, speaking to Sky Sports: "It was an important win for us going into the international break after the week we've had.

    "Also, we arrived very late Thursday night from Turkey so - I know they also played - it was important to finish with a win.

    On Alisson: "Especially in the first half at 0-0, he had a brilliant intervention. It was massive because, in these types of games, whoever scores first is important. In that moment, he did very well."

    On Palace's penalty: "I am very upset because the ones in our box they are calling penalties and in the other box they are now. I know we are 3-0 and it's the 95th minute but I'm very upset.

    "It's a head to head but even then it's Osario to Milos [Kerkez]. It's a clash of heads. You can say it's 3-0 but, for me, there is more because it is not the first time this season and you compare... I'm really upset.

    "The VAR took a lot of time. When you call a penalty and they are two minutes. Two repetitions, three repetitions, they are finding a way not to be called by the VAR. A clean sheet is not cheap in the Premier League and we wanted to win 3-0.

    "It is not just because of this penalty, it's because of a lot of things."

    On confidence taken from the win: "You want to finish with a win. It puts us in a better position but we need to keep improving.

    "Now we are all praying because the last international break we lost three players and now, it is the last thing we need. I hope everything goes well and we don't lose anyone this time."
