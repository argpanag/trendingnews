  1. 'We could've won the game'published at 22:22 GMT
    22:22 GMT

    FT: Aston Villa 1-1 Fulham

    Fulham

    Fulham midfielder Shea Charles speaking to Sky Sports: "Definitely. You could feel a shift in the second half. The way the goal came wasn't how we deserved it really, but positive signs. We could've won the game in the end, but a really good away performance."

    On the message from head coach Alvaro Arbeloa: "It was just to play how we can play. I thought we started quite slow. Giving the ball away in areas we didn't want to. I feel like them right where we wanted them on the press, but then they'd win a duel and be in on goal. I think when we tightened up and recycled the ball and started to play through them, we played really well."

    On teammate Calvin Bassey: "Top player. I don't want to big him up too much, but he's a really good leader. Especially for me coming into the league in my first season. There is no one better to have at the back."

  2. 'We will support him'published at 22:17 GMT
    22:17 GMT

    FT: Aston Villa 1-1 Fulham

    Aston Villa

    Aston Villa defender Ian Maatsen speaking to Sky Sports: "We started the game well. Fulham is a good side, not easy to win [against] but we came in front. We done well but in the second half we gave them more time on the ball. They have good players and they can make chances.

    "We were unlucky with the goal but we tried to keep fighting. It’s important not to lose these games.

    On Suzuki's mistake for the goal: "It’s frustrating because don’t want to concede goals but it can happen to everyone.

    "Zion is a great guy, great goalkeeper. He will learn from it. We will support him and go again."

  3. 'A good away performance'published at 22:13 GMT
    22:13 GMT

    FT: Aston Villa 1-1 Fulham

    Fulham

    Fulham centre-back Calvin Bassey speaking to Sky Sports: "I think it was a good away performance. We know how hard it is to come here. It is a difficult place to come, a team with a lot of quality. We wanted a clean sheet, but proud of the boys. They did well."

    On whether they could have got three points instead of one: "I think we should have got more. We missed two chances inside the six-yard box. I know the boys are out there working their socks off to get in the right positions and finishing, we've just got to stay patient and keep trusting in them because we know the quality they have. I just think we could have took all three. A bit unlucky, but we've got to keep positive. Games like this, we've seen in the past where we've been drawing and we've lost. Ipswich, for example.If we can't win, it's very important we don't lose and just happy that we've progressed."

    On Fulham's current form: "100%. Since the start of the season, we've been improving each week and everyone all working hard. Everyone has bought into the way the manager wants to play. We're starting to learn and understand each other. Hopefully, things are going up from now."
