  1. 'Biggest difference was physicality'published at 19:39 BST 10 October
    19:39 BST 10 October

    FT: Man Utd 1-1 Tottenham

    Danny Murphy
    Former Liverpool midfielder and Match of the Day pundit

    I was surprised at United. I thought they'd be better than that. And they lost a bit of confidence as the first half went on because Tottenham were good. Spurs had more energy, more quality and more urgency.

    The biggest difference I saw in the teams was the physicality, United looked lesser in that department. They didn't have the same power and the same pace as Tottenham. In midfield, especially. Tielemans and Mainoo - the gaps between them and the wide midfielders was too big.

    Tottenham were getting in all sorts of good positions and on another day, United could have been a couple down by half-time.

    United have got to find a system and the personnel to be more difficult to play against. Otherwise it's going to be really difficult to win matches.
