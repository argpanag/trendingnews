  4. 'Offensively we need to do much better'published at 17:34 BST
    17:34 BST

    FT: Liverpool 0-0 Fulham

    Liverpool

    Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola, speaking to BBC Match of the Day: "When you draw, especially at home, you can't be happy. Today we lacked a bit of freshness and we did not do enough offensively to make the difference.

    "We have been good enough to keep the clean sheet. It was slightly better in the second half. In the first half we started the game slow, we lacked energy and it was not enough, we have to be offensively better.

    "It is less than three days, 60 hours, [since they played Atletico Madrid in the Champions League] and a lot of players had to adapt. Most of the changes were for physical reasons because the players were tired, but that is the season and in less than three days we will be here against Spurs [in the Carabao Cup on Tuesday].

    "We have to adapt and find ways to win."

    On Bradley Barcola: "Bradley is one we are building up physically with games and the games will give him the condition we want. But it is not just Bradley, we lack the freshness. I only remember one clear chance [for Fulham] and we kept the structure but offensively we need to do much better."

  9. Richarlison saga continuespublished at 17:25 BST
    17:25 BST

    Tottenham v Everton (17:30 BST)

    As if things were not bad enough on the pitch for Tottenham, off it the saga with Brazilian forward Richarlison continues.

    His proposed move to Vasco da Gama fell through yesterday and personal terms over the deal are understood to have emerged as an issue.

    The Brazilian transfer window closed on Friday night.

    Richarlison was left out of Tottenham's Premier League squad after his proposed £35m deadline-day move to former club Everton broke down, also over a disagreement on personal terms.

    On Thursday, BBC Sport reported that Richarlison and Spurs were in a dispute over the remaining nine months on his contract.

    Spurs boss Roberto de Zerbi did not provide any update on Richarlison's future at a news conference on Friday but the player is understood to be exploring his options.

    For now, he is training with the under-21's.

    FT: Bournemouth 2-2 Brentford

    Bournemouth

    Bournemouth manager Marco Rose speaking to BBC Match of the Day: "We were behind for the first time this season, we reacted well, and lucky me, we didn't concede a late goal. We had more chances, more possession, we were the best team.

Match Facts

  • Tottenham Hotspur have only lost one of their last 17 Premier League home games against Everton (W12 D4), winning their last five since a 0-1 defeat in September 2020.

  • Everton lost both Premier League meetings with Tottenham Hotspur last season by an aggregate score of 4-0, but have never lost three in a row to Spurs without scoring in their league history.

  • Tottenham are one of three teams yet to score in the Premier League this season along with Aston Villa and Coventry City. Spurs have never failed to score in any of their first four league games in a single campaign before.

  • Tottenham Hotspur have failed to score in any of their three Premier League games this season. They last went four without a goal in August/September 2006, a run which included a 2-0 home loss to Everton.

  • Everton remain unbeaten in the Premier League so far this season (W1 D2), netting stoppage time equalisers in their last two games. However, the Toffees are winless in their last six away league games, alternating between defeat (3) and a draw (3) each time.

  • Only Brighton (34) and Crystal Palace (32) have forced more high turnovers than Everton (28) in the Premier League this season, with the Toffees’ seven shots following a high turnover the joint-most so far.

  • Tottenham’s only win and clean sheet in their last 12 Premier League home games came against Everton on the final day of 2025-26 (D4 L7). Their next loss will be their 50th at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium in the competition.

  • Tottenham Hotspur have made eight changes to their starting XI in the Premier League this season, the most of any side and their joint-most in the first three games of a campaign in the competition (also 8 in 2011-12).

  • Spurs’ Mathys Tel has completed more dribbles than any other player in the Premier League this season (10).

  • Ainsley Maitland-Niles scored on his Premier League debut for Everton last time out, having netted in just one of his previous 109 appearances in the competition.
