  2. 'Cost us energy to chase game'published at 17:13 BST
    17:13 BST

    FT: Brighton 1-1 Leeds

    Brighton & Hove Albion

    Brighton boss Fabian Hurzeler, speaking to BBC Match of the Day: "Today we had a lack of energy, lack of intensity, but showed another face in the second half and could have won the game. It has cost us energy to chase the game. We need to be at the highest level from the start and need to find solutions for that.

    "Every second phase, every second ball landed for a Leeds player. That's what we changed in the second half and we were a better team. We don't need long to change a game. We had plenty of chances, but it's all about fighting for the clean sheet and being better.

    "Our start to the season can be better, can be worse. There were positives today but also things to improve."

    On Luka Vuskovic's performance: "I think he needs this time to adapt but he has the physicality to do this. He is young and needs time to improve and then he can be a top player."

Match Facts

  • This is the first meeting between Hull and Aston Villa in any competition since a 2-2 draw in the Championship in January 2019 – the Tigers have won just one of their last 16 games against Villa (D4 L11).

  • Aston Villa have won six of their eight Premier League matches against Hull City (D1 L1), with this the first meeting since February 2015 in what was Hull’s only win – the Tigers were victorious 2-0 with goals from Nikica Jelavic and Dame N’Doye.

  • Hull City are looking to become only the second newly promoted side to win each of their opening three games of a Premier League season, after Bolton Wanderers in 2001-02. The Tigers themselves have never won their first three matches of any top-flight campaign.

  • Aston Villa are the only side yet to have a shot on target in the Premier League this season – since 2003-04, no side has ever failed to have a shot on target in their opening three games of a campaign.

  • Hull have faced the most crosses of any Premier League side this season (73 – 21 more than any other side). Central defender John Egan has made the most clearances (27) and headed clearances (15), while Nobel Mendy ranks joint-third for clearances (17).

  • Aston Villa are yet to score in the Premier League this season, while they also drew a blank in their first three games of last season. In top-flight history, the only team to fail to score in their first three games in consecutive seasons is Ipswich Town in 1969-70 and 1970-71.

  • Aston Villa could become the first team to finish in the top-four of the top-flight (4th in 2025-26) and then lose their first three games of the next season since Man Utd in 1986-87, while the only top-four side to fail to score in their first three games of the following season was Arsenal in 1953-54.

  • Aston Villa manager Unai Emery has never faced Hull City before in the Premier League – he has beaten 27 of the 28 teams he has faced in the competition, failing only against Ipswich (D2). Emery has never lost a game with Villa against a promoted side (W15 D6).

  • Hull boss Sergej Jakirovic is looking to become just the fourth manager to win each of his first three Premier League games with a clean sheet after José Mourinho in 2004, Sven-Goran Eriksson in 2007 and Arne Slot in 2024.

  • Aston Villa have lost their last four Premier League matches that haven't featured Ollie Watkins by an aggregate score of 10-1. Their last victory without Watkins dates back to 13 November 2022, a 2-1 win at Brighton in Unai Emery's second game in charge.

This is the first meeting between Hull and Aston Villa in any competition since a 2-2 draw in the Championship in January 2019 – the Tigers have won just one of their last 16 games against Villa (D4 L11).

Aston Villa have won six of their eight Premier League matches against Hull City (D1 L1), with this the first meeting since February 2015 in what was Hull’s only win – the Tigers were victorious 2-0 with goals from Nikica Jelavic and Dame N’Doye.

Hull City are looking to become only the second newly promoted side to win each of their opening three games of a Premier League season, after Bolton Wanderers in 2001-02. The Tigers themselves have never won their first three matches of any top-flight campaign.

Aston Villa are the only side yet to have a shot on target in the Premier League this season – since 2003-04, no side has ever failed to have a shot on target in their opening three games of a campaign.

Hull have faced the most crosses of any Premier League side this season (73 – 21 more than any other side). Central defender John Egan has made the most clearances (27) and headed clearances (15), while Nobel Mendy ranks joint-third for clearances (17).

Aston Villa are yet to score in the Premier League this season, while they also drew a blank in their first three games of last season. In top-flight history, the only team to fail to score in their first three games in consecutive seasons is Ipswich Town in 1969-70 and 1970-71.

Aston Villa could become the first team to finish in the top-four of the top-flight (4th in 2025-26) and then lose their first three games of the next season since Man Utd in 1986-87, while the only top-four side to fail to score in their first three games of the following season was Arsenal in 1953-54.

Aston Villa manager Unai Emery has never faced Hull City before in the Premier League – he has beaten 27 of the 28 teams he has faced in the competition, failing only against Ipswich (D2). Emery has never lost a game with Villa against a promoted side (W15 D6).

Hull boss Sergej Jakirovic is looking to become just the fourth manager to win each of his first three Premier League games with a clean sheet after José Mourinho in 2004, Sven-Goran Eriksson in 2007 and Arne Slot in 2024.
