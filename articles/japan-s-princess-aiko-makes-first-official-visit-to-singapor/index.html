Japan’s Princess Aiko makes first official visit to Singapore

TheTools · 18.11.2026 21:15 · source · permalink

Japan’s Princess Aiko is making her first official visit to Singapore, marking 60 years of diplomatic relations between the two countries. President Tharman Shanmugaratnam says the Singapore-Japan Strategic Partnership will strengthen cooperation in areas such as AI, sustainability and security amid global uncertainty. Princess Aiko also expressed hope that future generations will deepen bilateral ties. During her five-day visit, she will explore Singapore’s heritage, visit local landmarks and engage the Japanese community. Noah Kong reports.
