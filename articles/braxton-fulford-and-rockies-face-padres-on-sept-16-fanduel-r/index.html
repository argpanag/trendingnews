Braxton Fulford And Rockies Face Padres On Sept. 16 | FanDuel Research

TheTools · 16.09.2026 22:24 · source · permalink

Braxton Fulford

Colorado Rockies • #37 • C

Braxton Fulford and the Colorado Rockies will take on the San Diego Padres at Coors Field, on Wednesday, Sept. 16 at 8:40 p.m. ET. Fulford has +520 odds to hit a home run as of Wednesday evening.

What It Means

Fulford is hitting for a .174 BA, .253 OBP and .275 SLG with a 39.2% strikeout rate and an 8.9% walk rate. His OPS is .529 and he has scored nine runs. In 79 plate appearances, he has hit one home run and driven in seven runs. Fulford has recorded four steals on four attempts. He is back in action for the first time since Aug. 24, when he went 0 for 1 against the Nationals.

The Padres are sending Robbie Ray (12-8) to the mound to make his 29th start of the season. He is 12-8 with a 3.57 ERA and 132 strikeouts through 156 1/3 innings pitched.
