Recent form

Bulgaria enters this UEFA Nations League campaign with a very inconsistent run of form. Their overall record in the last 21 matches reveals five wins, six draws, and ten losses. This paints a picture of a team that has struggled to find a steady rhythm, often alternating between positive results and disappointing defeats.

Their recent games further illustrate this inconsistency. They secured impressive wins in the FIFA Series, such as a dominant 10-2 victory against the Solomon Islands. However, they also faced significant challenges in the World Cup Qualifiers, suffering heavy losses against strong teams like Spain and Turkey.

More recently, their friendly matches have yielded mixed results, including a 2-2 draw with Moldova and a 1-0 defeat to Montenegro. As this is the second round of their UEFA Nations League campaign, Bulgaria will be eager to establish a positive momentum at home. Fortunately, the manager has a full squad available, with no reported injuries for this important fixture.

Estonia - Unbeaten in three matches

Let’s now take a look at Estonia’s current situation. We will analyse the team’s form and their recent results for this match.

Recent form

Estonia enters this match on a positive note, currently unbeaten in their last three games. They kicked off their UEFA Nations League campaign with a respectable 1-1 draw away to Iceland. However, looking at the bigger picture, their form has been patchy, with just five wins in their last 21 matches, alongside five draws and eleven defeats.

Their recent success includes winning the Baltic Cup, where they secured narrow 1-0 victories against both Lithuania and the Faroe Islands. This shows they can grind out results against similarly-ranked opposition. In contrast, their World Cup qualification journey was much tougher, featuring heavy defeats against European powerhouses like Italy and Norway.

This mixed bag of results demonstrates a team that can be competitive but lacks consistency against top-level opponents. The recent unbeaten streak will certainly boost their confidence heading into this fixture. A significant advantage for the visitors is that they have a fully available squad to choose from, with no reported injuries.
