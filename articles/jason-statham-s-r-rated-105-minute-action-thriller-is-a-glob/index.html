Jason Statham's R-Rated 105-Minute Action Thriller Is a Global Streaming Hit Ahead of 2027 Sequel

TheTools · 18.11.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

Hollywood action megastar Jason Statham might be going through a rough year, with Shelter and Mutiny failing to make a dent at the box office. However, as his 2026 movies keep finding new viewers on streaming, one of Statham's other recent movies has resurfaced. Not only that, it has officially become the No. 1 movie on streaming worldwide. Never underestimate the king of streaming.

'The Beekeeper' Is B-Movie Action Made Right

Directed by David Ayer (Suicide Squad, Fury, Statham's A Working Man), The Beekeeper sees Statham playing a classic Statham loner with a thirst for justice and revenge. He's Adam Clay, a former government assassin taking care of bees on a small farm in Massachusetts. His landlady Eloise (Phylicia Rashad) doesn't ask many questions about his past. Instead, the two develop a genuine bond.

Eloise becomes the victim of a phishing scam, and after losing everything, she takes her life. Her death hits Clay hard, and he swears revenge, going on the run to find those responsible. As it turns out, his responsibilities as a beekeeper went far beyond tending to his hives. He belonged to a secret organization tracking down corrupt companies and restoring order. They should never have messed with the retired beekeeper.

The 105-minute nonstop thriller is one of the biggest hits of Statham's recent career. It grossed more than $162 million worldwide against a $40 million production budget. Its appeal is also reflected in the film's 92% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes, and the subsequent success the film has had on streaming since its digital premiere.

The film has B-movie charm, especially in its depiction of the villains, with Josh Hutcherson playing an obnoxious but funny antagonist, and Taylor James delivering great one-liners while swearing vengeance on Clay. Despite some schlocky elements, critics were largely positive, and it eventually earned a 71% critics' score on the review aggregator.

As the sequel approaches, The Beekeeper has found momentum on streaming again.It has officially become the No. 1 movie on Starz worldwide, topping the platform's global chart. Not only that, Wrath of Man, Statham and Guy Ritchie's 2021 action collaboration, is also trending on Starz and now sits at No. 4 on the platform. Clearly, audiences are revisiting Statham's action catalog ahead of his next outing as Adam Clay.

'The Beekeeper 2': This Is What We Know

Less than three years after The Beekeeper premiered, a sequel was announced. The Beekeeper 2 is officially coming to theaters on January 15, 2027, featuring Jason Statham as Clay again, as well as Emmy Raver-Lampman and Jeremy Irons reprising their roles from the original. Ayer is not returning to direct, and instead, Timo Tjahjanto (The Night Comes for Us, Nobody 2) will take over as director.

Other details have been kept under wraps. However, Clay will have an even bigger fight on his hands this time around. His mission involves protecting the President of the United States (played by Jemma Redgrave), while the other Beekeepers have gone rogue and are now after the head of the U.S. government. Sounds like Statham will be facing his toughest challenge yet in 2027.
