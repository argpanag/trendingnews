Rashid Shaheed always does his best on Thursdays

TheTools · 15.10.2026 22:07 · source · permalink

Seahawks vs. Broncos Week 6 expectations

The last time Rashid Shaheed played on a Thursday, he returned a punt for 58 yards against the Rams and helped the Seahawks cut L.A.’s lead to eight points. On Seattle’s very next offensive play, Shaheed took an end around 31 yards to set the Seahawks up for the game-tying score to A.J. Barner.

Now can you believe that Shaheed’s next most recent Thursday night game was also against the Rams in 2023? And in that game he caught a 45-yard touchdown from Derek Carr to get the Saints on the board:

This is the side to Shaheed that the Seahawks have not seen enough of after signing him to a three-year, $51 million contract in March. But it is a side that — like Thanksgiving — always seems to show up on a Thursday.

In Shaheed’s first career Thursday night game in 2022, he caught a 53-yard touchdown pass from Andy Dalton.

In four career Thursday night games, Shaheed has a 53-yard touchdown catch, a 45-yard touchdown catch, a 58-yard punt return touchdown, and a 31-yard carry, making a grand total of 10 catches for 151 yards, 39 rushing yards, and over 250 scrimmage yards.

That’s the type of night that the Seahawks are looking for from Shaheed against the Broncos.

In 14 career regular season games with Seattle, Shaheed has 284 receiving yards and zero receiving touchdowns. He scored his first and only rushing touchdown with the Seahawks against the Chargers in Week 4, but he’s averaging 35 yards per game below his receiving numbers with New Orleans prior to the trade.

It wouldn’t be the first time Seattle invested money and resources into a pass catcher and didn’t get the desired production from him:

Can Brian Fleury do a better job? Can Jay Harbaugh do a better job? Can Sam Darnold do a better job?

All roads points to YES.

But they are not the ones who have to worry about his money, so Shaheed’s going to have to take it into his own hands to make sure he continues to be paid: If Shaheed is on the roster five days after the Super Bowl, his $11.7 million salary becomes fully guaranteed. Meaning that if he is cut before then, the Seahawks will save over $11 million.

Shaheed has nine catches for 96 yards and zero touchdowns and he hasn’t had a notable return yet, plus he has fumbled twice, so I’d be getting pretty anxious about my future right about now if I were him.

Thursday would not be too soon for Shaheed to remind everyone why the Seahawks felt he was too special to lose.
