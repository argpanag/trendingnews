Apple CEO John Ternus Teases ‘Huge Launch’ Next Week: What to Expect

TheTools · 03.09.2026 08:41 · source · permalink

By Indrani Priyadarshini

on September 3, 2026

Apple’s new CEO John Ternus has wasted little time setting the tone for his tenure. In his first memo to employees after taking over from Tim Cook, Ternus hinted at a major product launch planned for next week and said he was excited about what lies ahead for the company.

Read More | Who is John Ternus? Apple’s new CEO takes charge ahead of iPhone 18 launch

“We have a huge launch next week that’s going to be phenomenal,” Ternus wrote in the memo. He also pointed to products already in development, as well as ideas that Apple has yet to imagine.

Ternus officially became Apple’s CEO on 1 September, replacing Cook after his 15 years at the helm. Cook has moved into the role of executive chairman, allowing the company to maintain some continuity during the leadership transition. The timing of Ternus’ arrival is significant. His first major event as CEO will take place just days after he assumed the position, giving him an immediate opportunity to introduce Apple’s latest products to the world.

Apple has confirmed an iPhone launch event for 9 September. The company is expected to unveil its next-generation iPhone range at the event.

One of the biggest rumours surrounding the September event is Apple’s first foldable iPhone. Reports suggest the device could mark a major change to the iPhone line-up and give Apple a new category in the premium smartphone market.

The launch is also expected to include new iPhone models, alongside updates to products such as the Apple Watch and AirPods. However, Apple has not confirmed the full product line-up. The foldable iPhone would be particularly significant because it would represent Apple’s entry into a smartphone category that rivals such as Samsung have already been developing for several years.

While Ternus focused on the upcoming launch, his memo also suggested that Apple’s product pipeline extends well beyond the devices expected next week.

Apple is reportedly exploring products including camera-equipped AirPods, smart glasses and a touchscreen MacBook. Ternus said he was equally excited about products already in the works and those that Apple has yet to create.
