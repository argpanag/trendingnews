Following a crushing win over Golden State, Victor Wembanyama lauded San Antonio's respectful culture and hinted at spending his entire career with the Spurs. The French phenom called the franchise an ideal environment for the next 15 to 20 years amid San Antonio's impressive 11-2 start to the season.

Victor Wembanyama praised the culture around the San Antonio Spurs after a win crushing 123-85 win over the Golden State Warriors on Friday.

The center talked to media after the game answering a question in French about the culture in San Antonio.

"What I like most is the respect they show us. Sometimes I fell a little uncomfortable being placed on such a pedestal," he said.

"I think this is really the ideal environment because even in other cities, where we're not the home team, people aren't as respectful. People can be very persistent or intrusive sometimes, but never here."

Then he hinted at possibly spending his entire career with the Spurs as he has done before.

"I'd say it's the ideal environment to spend 15 or 20 years," Wembanyama said about San Antonio.

Although the French superstar had a rather quiet game against the Warriors, scoring 12 points, grabbing 12 rebounds and dishing out six assists, alongside two blocks, his season start has been spectacular.

Wembanyama is averaging 28.9 points, 11.7 rebounds, 3.1 assists and 4.2 blocks. He is currently shooting a career-high 63% from inside the arc as the Spurs have started the season with a 11-2 record.
