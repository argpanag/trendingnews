Football games today: 14 NFL games on schedule for Sunday, Sept. 20

TheTools · 20.09.2026 15:41 · source · permalink

It's that exciting time of year when the NFL is just getting underway, and every team believes they have a chance to go all the way. Teams will start separating themselves from the pack soon. In Week 2, hopes are still high for every team, and there's lots of time to turn things around.

So, what NFL games are on TV on Sunday, Sept. 20? Let's get into the tally of 14 games.

The Indianapolis Colts and Kansas City Chiefs will go up against each other in a prime-time "Sunday Night Football" matchup, and it's one worth watching. 

Sure, the Colts aren't considered the very best team right now. But they have quarterback Daniel Jones healthy again, and they're coming off a loss to the Baltimore Ravens, so they're hungry for a win. 

Meanwhile, the Chiefs are coming off a win over the Denver Broncos and trying to prove that they're a stronger team than they were last season.
