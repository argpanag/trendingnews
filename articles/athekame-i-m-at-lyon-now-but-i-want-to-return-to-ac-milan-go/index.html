Athekame: "I’m at Lyon now, but I want to return to AC Milan" | Goal.com

TheTools · 29.09.2026 20:35 · source · permalink

Zachary Athekame left AC Milan on loan this summer. The Swiss full-back joined Olympique Lyon on a straight loan under Paulo Fonseca and has made a big enough impression to earn a call-up from head coach Murat Yakin to the senior Switzerland national team. The structure of the deal also suggests AC Milan could still look to rely on him again in the near future.

I settled in well, it was a new league, more tactical. Then there was a change in the dugout, I spoke with the new manager and decided to change shirts.
