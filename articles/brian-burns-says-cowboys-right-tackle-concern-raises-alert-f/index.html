Brian Burns says Cowboys right tackle concern raises ‘alert’ for Giants pass rush

TheTools · 11.09.2026 21:46 · source · permalink

The Dallas Cowboys’ lack of faith in starting right tackle Terence Steele portends a potentially big game Sunday night for Giants top pass rusher Brian Burns.

The Cowboys traded for Pittsburgh Steelers tackle Broderick Jones two weeks ago and told Steele he would compete for his job once Jones got up to speed with the playbook.

No disrespect to the players themselves, Burns said, but Dallas showing that level of concern this close to the start of the season is noteworthy.

“I mean, opportunity lies within the game,” Burns said. “They’re both pros. Not to disrespect anybody’s craft or anything like that, but the fact that they are so concerned in that area does, I guess, raise some form of an alert in our minds, as far as like just knowing that they’re having struggles.”

“So we can definitely see that as an opportunity to exploit,” he said.

Burns, 28, plays on both sides of the defense, but he lines up more often on the left side across from the right tackle. And this is a matchup the Giants undoubtedly will hunt.

Burns finished second in the NFL with a career high 16.5 sacks last season and third in the league with 22 tackles for loss. He is so highly regarded that the NFL used Burns as the face of the Giants’ team on its annual Super Bowl graphic.

Offensive players represent all three of the Giants’ division rivals in the illustration posted to the league’s official social media accounts: Quarterbacks Dak Prescott (Cowboys) and Jayden Daniels (Commanders) and running back Saquon Barkley (Eagles).

But Burns in his blue No. 0 jersey is now the Giants’ standard bearer, staring at Los Angeles’ SoFi Stadium in the front row.

Cowboys owner Jerry Jones said on Dallas radio Friday that the organization is “hopeful” that Steele’s “experience will prevail,” and that he’ll be steady enough for the time being at right tackle.

The Giants’ pass rush of Burns, Abdul Carter, Kayvon Thibodeaux and rookie linebacker Arvell Reese, however, will test a Cowboys offensive line that also will be playing without injured left guard Tyler Smith. Backup T.J. Bass will start in Smith’s place.

COWBOYS’ DAVIS CARTED OFF

Dallas backup running back Malik Davis was carted off Friday’s practice field with a hip injury. He is out for Sunday’s game, and the Cowboys fear it’s serious.

Javonte Williams will be their lead back Sunday. Their other two running backs are Emari Demercado on the active roster and Israel Abanikanda on the practice squad.

HELLO, JERRY …

Jones, the Cowboys’ owner, said on 105.3 The Fan in Dallas that he gets a “tingle” every time the Giants and Cowboys play.
