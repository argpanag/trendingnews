Three men killed in separate road crashes

TheTools · 20.09.2026 08:16 · source · permalink

Three men have died in separate road crashes in counties Limerick, Wexford and Meath.

In Co Limerick, a man in his 20s died and two people were injured, one critically, in a single-vehicle crash on the N21 at Cummeen, Adare at around 2.40am yesterday.

Gardaí said the car struck a tree.

The driver died at the scene and his body was removed to University Hospital Limerick, where a post-mortem examination will be carried out.

Two passengers were taken to UHL, where a man in his 20s is in a critical condition.

The second passenger, a man in his late teens, was treated for non-life-threatening injuries.

Gardaí are appealing to any witnesses, particularly anyone travelling on the N21 at Cummeen, between 2.15am and 2.45am, and who may have camera footage (including dash-cam), to contact Henry Street Garda Station on 061 212 400, the Garda Confidential Line on 1800 666 111, or any garda station.

Man, 50s, dies after car crashes into premises in Meath

In Co Meath, a man in his 50s died after his car struck a premises on Connaught Street in Athboy at around 2am.

The man, who was travelling alone, died at the scene.

His body was removed to Our Lady's Hospital, Navan, where a post-mortem examination will take place.

Gardaí are appealing to road users who were in the Connaught Street area between 1.45am and 2.15am, and who may have camera footage (including dash-cam), to contact Kells Garda Station on 046 928 0820.

Motorcyclist dies in Wexford collision

In Co Wexford, a man in his 50s died in a collision involving a motorcycle and a van.

The collision occurred on the R736 near Wellington Bridge shortly before 3.15pm yesterday.

The motorcyclist was pronounced dead at the scene.

The van driver did not require hospital treatment.

Gardaí have appealed for witnesses, particularly any road user who may have camera footage (including dash-cam), to contact New Ross Garda Station on 051 426030, the Garda Confidential Line on 1800 666 111, or any Garda Station.

Emergency services at scene of 'serious' collision in Waterford

Separately, a serious collision involving a bus was reported in Co Waterford yesterday evening.

The incident occurred on the N25 at Pilltown between Dungarvan and Youghal in Co Cork.

A garda spokesperson said: "Gardaí and emergency services are currently at the scene of a serious road traffic collision on the N25 near Pilltown, Kinsalebeg, Co Waterford this evening Sunday 20 September 2026."
