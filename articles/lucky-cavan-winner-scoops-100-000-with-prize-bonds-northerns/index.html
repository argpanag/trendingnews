A lucky Cavan winner has scooped €100,000 with Prize Bonds.

The weekly prize of €100,000 goes to Prize Bond number ABL243499 held in County Cavan.

The bond was purchased in 2015.

In addition to a €500,000 prize awarded on the last weekly draw of every calendar month, there are weekly draws where the top prize is €100,000. Other cash prizes awarded each week include 50 of €1,000 and the remainder of €100.

Prize Bonds are an Ireland State Savings product which, instead of paying interest, offers bond holders the chance to win cash prizes every week.

All winnings are tax-free, and Prize Bonds can be cashed in at any time after the minimum holding period of three months.
