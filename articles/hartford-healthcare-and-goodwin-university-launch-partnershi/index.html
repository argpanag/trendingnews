NEW HAVEN, Conn. (WTNH) — Leaders from Hartford HealthCare and Goodwin University announced a new partnership Monday aimed at connecting students with career opportunities while helping meet Connecticut's growing demand for healthcare workers.

Mark Scheinberg, president of Goodwin University, said the partnership is designed to create additional opportunities for students to enter healthcare professions and gain access to in-demand careers.

For more information about the partnership and healthcare programs offered through Goodwin University, visit Goodwin University and CT River Academy.
