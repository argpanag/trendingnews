Strictly exit sealed as star dealt crushing news hours before Icons Week

TheTools · 16.10.2026 10:29 · source · permalink

Could Shaun miss out on the Glitterball Trophy?(Image: CREDIT LINE:BBC/Guy Levy)

Strictly Come Dancing star Shaun Wright-Phillips has been dealt another crushing blow hours before Icons Week. Ever since Strictly began, Shaun has consistently been the favourite to leave the competition; however, each week, he has been saved by the public vote.

Last week, Melanie Walters left the competition after landing in the bottom two alongside Shaun and his dance partner, Alexis Warr. However, the judges decided to save him, meaning the Gavin and Stacey star and her dance partner, Aljaz, were sent home.

The latest odds predict that Shaun and Alexis will finally leave the competition this weekend. Shaun has been given odds of 5/6 of leaving the competition. Sarah Storey is close behind him with odds of 15/8.

In third place is Jaime Winstone with odds of 15/1 of leaving, while Bethany Antonia has odds of 12/1 of being voted off. On the other end of the scale, Lacey Turner is still the frontrunner to win the competition with odds of 100/1.

Strictly Come Dancing's Alexis Warr and Shaun Wright-Phillips could leave the competition this weekend(Image: BBC)

Tabby Stoecker is a close second with odds of 66/1, while John Nellis comes in third place with odds of 50/1. However, Dani Dyer could miss out on a place in the final as she is in fourth place with odds of 25/1.

Speaking on the latest market, Betway’s Lewis Knowles told Daily Star: “Shaun Wright-Phillips is the favourite to be eliminated once again at 5/6, but he continues to defy his odds and dodge the exit.

“Sarah Storey is currently second favourite to leave at 15/8, so if Shaun manages to survive yet again, she could find herself in trouble. Jaime Winstone is next in the betting at 15/2, but there’s still a sizeable gap between her and the top two so it would be a shock to see her go, but it's not impossible.

“Shaun has become one to watch, with his repeated escapes keeping everyone guessing. If he manages to pull it off again, Sarah could be the one who finds herself in the firing line next.”

Will Shaun avoid elimination this weekend, or will his time in the competition be up?

Shaun landed in the bottom two last weekend against Melanie Walters(Image: BBC/Guy Levy)

Elsewhere, Delta Goodrem will not be performing this weekend after injuring her rib, meaning her dance partner, Kai Widdrington, will have to sit out.

Earlier this week, a Strictly spokesperson confirmed the news that Delta had pulled out of Icons Week.

They said, “Due to injury, Delta Goodrem has been advised by doctors to rest and, as a result, will not dance on this weekend's Strictly Come Dancing.

“In line with the rules of the show, Delta and Kai will receive a bye through to next week, when it is hoped they will be able to dance again. We wish Delta the very best as she continues her recovery."

“I’m absolutely gutted that a rib injury means I won’t be able to dance on Strictly this week. I’ve been loving every second of this experience, so having to sit this one out is heartbreaking - especially after the high of Saturday night,” Delta went on.

“I cannot tell you how much I love being a part of this magical show. I’m going to focus on resting and recovering, and hopefully I’ll be back on that dancefloor very soon. Thank you so much to everyone for all the love and support. It’s so appreciated. I’ll be cheering everyone on from the sidelines this week! Sending all my love, Delta xx”

Strictly Come Dancing airs on Saturday at 6.15pm on BBC One.
