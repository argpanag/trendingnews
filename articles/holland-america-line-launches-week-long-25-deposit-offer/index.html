Holland America Line launches week-long £25 deposit offer

TheTools · 17.09.2026 13:20 · source · permalink

 

You are viewing 1 of your 2 free articles

Holland America Line has launched a £25 per person deposit offer to tie in with Clia Cruise Week.

The new promotion is available on new bookings made between September 15 and 22 for selected 2026-2028 cruises and cruisetours.

The lower deposit applies to a variety of itineraries in Alaska, Europe and the Caribbean

Highlighted sailings departing in 2027 include the seven-day Alaska Inside Passage on Koningsdam on May 1, the 14-day Canary Island Enchantment on Nieuw Statendam on April 16 and the 11-day Caribbean Cruise on Rotterdam on March 16.

The £25 deposit is combinable to with Have It All Early Booking Bonus and a Caribbean Fly & Cruise offer.

The Have It All Early Booking Bonus includes, premium Wi-Fi, a beverage package, up to $300 shore excursion credit, specialty dining and up to $300 onboard credit.

The Caribbean Fly & Cruise offer provides a £600 per person air credit, one-night pre-cruise hotel stay and return airport-to-ship transfers.

Karen Farndell, associate vice-president of sales and marketing for UK & Ireland, said: “Clia Cruise Week is an ideal opportunity for travel agents to engage their customers who are considering a future holiday but may not be ready to make a significant upfront commitment.

“By reducing the deposit to just £25 per person for one week only, we’re making it easier for guests to secure their preferred cruise or cruisetour, while giving agents another strong selling tool to support booking conversion during one of the most important weeks in the cruise calendar.”
