SHARONVILLE, Ohio (WKRC) - School bus drivers on strike and their employer are set to return to the bargaining table as a labor dispute continues to disrupt transportation for students in the Princeton City Schools District.

The union representing the striking drivers has agreed to negotiations with First Student, which says it filed a labor complaint against the union. The company says its latest offer would raise wages more than 27 percent over four years, with top pay reaching about $40 an hour.

First Student also accuses the union of not bargaining in good faith. The union is now scheduled to meet with the company on Friday morning.

Princeton Schools said it is not part of the negotiations but is pressing First Student to restore reliable service. The district said the strike led to significant absences, even as some families found other ways to get students to school.
