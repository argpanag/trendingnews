11 PS Plus Extra Game Removals for November 2026 Confirmed

TheTools · 20.10.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

Rockstar drops PS Plus support again

This month's PS Plus Extra and PS Plus Premium game update is now live for PS5, PS4 players (or some of it is, if you are in the UK, USA, or Japan), which means it's time to learn what titles will be taken off the service in November 2026.

We already know that GTA 5 is leaving PS Plus Extra, as per a message from Sony, but the full list has now been shared in the Last Chance to Play section.

The following 11 PS5, PS4 games are leaving PS Plus Extra on 17th November 2026:

  • Red Dead Redemption 2 (PS4) | Review
  • Football Manager 26 (PS5)
  • GTA 5 (PS5, PS4) | Review
  • The Talos Principle 2 (PS5) | Review
  • Cricket 24 (PS5, PS4) | Review
  • Monster Boy and the Cursed Kingdom (PS5, PS4)
  • Hotshot Racing (PS4) | Review
  • Rock of Ages 3: Make & Break (PS4) | Review
  • Earth Defense Force 4.1: The Shadow of New Despair (PS4) | Review
  • Rapala Fishing Pro Series (PS4)
  • Yet Another Zombie Defense HD (PS4)

Unlike the PS Plus Essential tier, in which you gain access to the games for as long as you subscribe to the service after redeeming them, the PS Plus Extra tier revokes access once the titles depart. If you want to continue playing any of the above, you'll have to buy them after 17th November 2026.

Red Dead Redemption 2 and GTA 5 are the two obvious standouts, but since you'll all be playing GTA 6 next month, I don't advise you play something so similar in the lead-up to the next Rockstar game. You wouldn't want to burn yourself out!

Instead, take a look at Earth Defense Force 4.1 and The Talos Principle 2. They offer a different kind of experience from the norm, and they're both of high quality.

Earth Defense Force 4.1 offers tons of dumb action-based fun, as our PS4 review stated: "A game that knows exactly what it's doing and sticks wholeheartedly to its stupidity, it's a refreshing experience that's bolstered by an admittedly addictive gameplay loop."
