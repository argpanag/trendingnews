When was the last time Florida elected a Democratic governor?

TheTools · 04.11.2026 02:07 · source · permalink
  • Florida has not elected a Democratic governor in 27 years, with the last being Lawton Chiles in 1991.
  • Despite recent trends, the majority of Florida's governors since 1845 have been Democrats.
  • As of November 2026, registered Republican voters in Florida outnumber registered Democrats.

The polls close in Florida in less than an hour. Will Florida voters put a Democratic governor in office for the first time in 27 years?

Despite recent history, Florida is no stranger to a Democratic governor. Since Florida became a state in 1845, the huge majority of governors have been Democrats.

Here's the last Democratic governor Florida had and a look back at Florida governors from when the Sunshine State became a state through present.

What time do the polls close? Voting times in Florida

The polls are open on Election Day in Florida, from 7 a.m. until 7 p.m. (local time). If you're waiting in line at 7 p.m., don't go anywhere!

You will have the opportunity to cast a ballot as long as you are in line by the time the polls close.

When was the last time Florida elected a Democratic governor?

The last Democratic governor in Florida was Gov. Kenneth Hood MacKay, who served less than a month after the death of Gov. Lawton Chiles.

The last elected Democratic governor was Chiles, who served from January 1991 until his death on Dec. 12, 1998. MacKay served from Dec. 12 until Jan. 5, 1999, when newly elected Republican Gov. Jeb Bush was sworn into office.

There has been a Republican governor in Florida for the past 27 years, since January 1999, including Gov. Ron DeSantis.

How many Democratic governors has Florida had? See breakdown by party

Of Florida's 45 past governors, the breakdown by party is as follows:

  • Democrats: 34
  • Republicans: 9
  • Whig: 1
  • Prohibition: 1
  • Provisional: 1

The breakdown by party is off by one number. In 1916, Sidney Johnston Catts ran and won the Democratic gubernatorial nomination, but the Florida Supreme Court ordered an election recount, rescinding his nomination.

Catts then left the Democratic Party and was elected Florida’s governor as a Prohibition candidate.

How many people are registered to vote in Florida? Is Florida a red or blue state?

According to the Florida Division of Elections, there are 13,672,122 active registered voters in the state as of Nov. 3.

The breakdown by party is as follows:

  • Republicans: 5,678,755
  • Democrats: 4,133,397
  • No party affiliation: 3,345,188
  • Minor parties: 514,782

Republican voters have outnumbered Democrats in the state since 2021.
