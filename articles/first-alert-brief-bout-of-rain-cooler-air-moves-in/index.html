FIRST ALERT: Brief bout of rain, cooler air moves in

TheTools · 28.09.2026 20:03 · source · permalink

MARQUETTE, Mich. (WLUC) - The dry stretch looks to end with rain on the way early this week.

A small system looks to move in Tuesday evening; this could bring around half an inch of rainfall. However, drought conditions will linger, and we should see more rain. After this rain moves out, there won’t be much more.

But as the week progresses, cooler air settles in with seasonal air in the 60s. Once we reach the weekend, there are chances for showers, which could help the U.P.

Tuesday: Cloudy skies; rain later in the evening

>Highs: High 60s to Low 70s

Wednesday: Partly cloudy skies; showers in the morning

>Highs: Mid to High 60s

Thursday: Breezy and cloudy; seasonal air

>Highs: Low to Mid 60s

Friday: Mostly sunny skies; cooler air

>Highs: Low 50s west; Mid to High 50s east

Saturday: Partly cloudy skies; scattered showers

>Highs: Low 50s west; Mid to High 50s east

Sunday: Partly cloudy; isolated showers

>Highs: Low 50s west; Mid to High 50s east
