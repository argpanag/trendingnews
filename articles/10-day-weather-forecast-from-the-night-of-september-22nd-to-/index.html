10-day weather forecast from the night of September 22nd to October 2nd nationwide

TheTools · 22.09.2026 15:00 · source · permalink

The National Center for Hydro-Meteorological Forecasting forecasts the weather for the next 10 days (from the night of September 22 to October 2) in regions across the country.

Weather forecast from the night of September 22 to September 24

On the afternoon of September 22, the National Center for Hydro-Meteorological Forecasting updated the weather forecast for the coming days in regions across the country.

The Northern and North Central regions have showers and thunderstorms in some places; especially the North Central region on September 24 has rain, scattered showers and thunderstorms in some places.

The area from Hue city to Da Nang city, the eastern provinces from Quang Ngai to Dak Lak have heavy to very heavy rain and thunderstorms.

Lam Dong, Khanh Hoa provinces and the Southern region have moderate rain, heavy rain and thunderstorms, with locally very heavy rain.

Other areas have rain, moderate rain and thunderstorms, locally heavy rain.

The meteorological agency warns that thunderstorms are likely to occur with tornadoes, lightning, hail and strong gusts of wind.

Weather trend forecast from the night of September 24 to October 2.

The National Center for Hydro-Meteorological Forecasting also gave a noteworthy weather trend in some areas from the night of September 24 to October 2.

The Northern region from the night of September 24 to October 2, will have showers and thunderstorms in some places.

The North Central region from the night of September 24 to October 2, will have rain, scattered showers and thunderstorms in some places.

The area from Ha Tinh to Da Nang city has moderate rain, heavy rain and thunderstorms, locally very heavy rain; from the night of September 25th, rain tends to gradually decrease.

Other places in the Central and Southern regions have rain, moderate rain and thunderstorms, locally heavy rain until about September 26; then scattered showers and thunderstorms. Rain concentrates in the afternoon and evening.

The meteorological agency warns that thunderstorms are likely to occur with tornadoes, lightning, hail and strong gusts of wind.

Forecast heavy rain in the area, with over 250mm in some places from this evening to tomorrow

Warning of heavy rain in 5 provinces and cities, risk of bad weather in the next 6 hours
