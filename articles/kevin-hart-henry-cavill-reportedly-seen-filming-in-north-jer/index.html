Henry Cavill and Kevin Hart were recently spotted filming their new Netflix movie in multiple locations around the Garden State, according to reports.

The actors are set to play a pair of spies in the new comedy, which remains untitled, according to Netflix's Tudum. Joseph McGinty Nichol, widely known as McG, is directing the film.

"When two rival spies - played by Hart and Cavill - cross paths in a Lamaze class, and their wives become fast friends, their double lives collide in unexpectedly hilarious and dangerous ways. The two men are forced to become reluctant confidants and partners on the road to fatherhood," reads the description from Netflix.

Jason Isaacs, Justine Lupe, and Aja Naomi King will also be joining the cast, according to Tudum. A release date has yet to be announced.

The cast and crew were seen filming outside a private home in Glen Ridge on the morning of Sept. 9, according to a report from Essex Local. Chairs on the film set read the words "The Serpent and the Snake," along with a graphic showing the outline of two spies with guns drawn. It's unclear if that is the production's code name or an actual film title, Essex Local reports.

Hart and Cavill were both reportedly on set in the Essex County borough.

Glen Ridge Mayor Deborah Mans confirmed to NorthJersey.com that Netflix was filming in the borough.

"While there is some disruption for folks with the filming, we appreciate Netflix choosing to film in Glen Ridge," Mans said in an emailed statement. "The Borough uses the film fees we receive from the companies to provide grants to community-based organizations that serve our residents. Plus, it has been exciting to see celebrities around town!"

Filming activity was set to wrap up in Glen Ridge Sept. 11, Mans said.

Shortly before they came to North Jersey, the cast and crew were reportedly filming at Delicious Orchards in Colts Neck.

The market was closed Sept. 1 through Sept. 3, though it did not reveal why, according to the Asbury Park Press. However, on Sept. 1, McG posted a photo of himself on Instagram standing next to a display cart marked with an altered version of the Delicious Orchards Market logo.

On Sept. 2, Hart posted an Instagram story in front of what looked like Delicious Orchards' outdoor pick-up window, Asbury Park Press reported. Facebook users reported seeing Hart at Delicious Orchards.

In a Sept. 4 Facebook post, Delicious Orchards revealed that a movie had been filmed there during the closure and said that it had officially reopened for normal operating hours.

This is just the latest example of New Jersey's ongoing endeavor to become "Hollywood East."

Most notably, over the last few years, films such as "Happy Gilmore 2," "A Complete Unknown" and "Springsteen: Deliver Me From Nowhere" have been shot on location in New Jersey.

Adam Sandler was in North Jersey in August for the filming of "Grown Ups 3."

In 2023, film and TV production brought $592 million into the state. That figure grew by a third, to $833 million, in 2024, NorthJersey.com previously reported. Four enormous studios are under construction along with Filmology Labs, a $250 million "studio campus" set to open in Paterson by 2028.
