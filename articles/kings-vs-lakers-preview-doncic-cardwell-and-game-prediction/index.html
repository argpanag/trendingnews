Kings vs. Lakers Preview: Doncic, Cardwell and Game Prediction

TheTools · 04.11.2026 19:52 · source · permalink

Main Story: Sacramento hosts the surging Lakers as a heavy underdog, with Luka Dončić’s elite scoring set against the Kings’ struggling defense. The Kings are coming off a win over Dallas, where Malik Monk and Leaky Black sparked the bench. Rookie center Dylan Cardwell is expected to make his season debut, while Sacramento remains short-handed.

  • Kings: 1-5; Lakers: 6-2.
  • Dončić is averaging 38.3 points, 8 rebounds and 8.1 assists per game.
  • Sacramento allows the NBA’s second-most points per game, has its third-worst defensive rating and commits the league’s fifth-most fouls.
  • Monk scored 24 points in the Kings’ win over Dallas; Leaky Black made 4 of 6 3-pointers and recorded 4 steals.

The Lakers have the healthier, more talented roster, and Sacramento’s defensive numbers make containing Dončić a daunting challenge. Still, the Kings showed against Dallas that stronger bench play could help them stay competitive.

  • Keegan Murray and Maxime Raynaud are out; Precious Achiuwa is probable with an ankle issue.
  • Cardwell is off the injury list and expected to make his debut, potentially taking on center minutes.
  • Los Angeles is largely healthy; Adou Thiero is expected to sit with a knee injury.

The Kings’ chances hinge on whether their bench can sustain its recent production and whether Cardwell can handle the backup-center role. The preview predicts a narrow Sacramento win, 131-128.

Sacramento has little margin for error against a Lakers team led by a red-hot Dončić. A competitive game will require the Kings to defend far better and get meaningful contributions beyond their starters.
