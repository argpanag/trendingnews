Dolphins’ $1.4m battle to retain superstar flyer emerges in 15-player free agency headache

TheTools · 12.09.2026 09:07 · source · permalink

The Dolphins have a monumental battle on their hands to re-sign superstar fullback Hamiso Tabuai-Fidow.

According to Code Sports, the superstar fullback is seeking a mammoth contract worth $1.4 million per-season to remain at the 17th franchise.

Tabuai-Fidow has emerged into one of the NRL’s dynamic attacking threats, having joined the Dolphins ahead of their inaugural season from the Cowboys.

The superstar fullback is set to hit the open market come November 1, alongside 14 of his teammates, who will also be free to negotiate with rival clubs.

Dolphins powerbrokers have reportedly began contract discussions which could see Tabuai-Fidow sign the richest deal in the club’s short first grade history.

However, if a deal can’t be struck, rival clubs will be on notice to poach the Origin gun.

“I do want to stay here,” Tabuai-Fidow said to News Corp.

“I’ll leave all contract stuff to my management and what they want to do.

“I just want to focus on this finals series and hopefully get wins. But this club has been good to me.

“I love the club, love the boys. It’s a pretty good club to be at, so hopefully it all works out.”

Tabuai-Fidow is currently earning a reported $1 million per-season, but is set for a healthy pay rise which could coincide with an increase in club’s salary caps.

According to Dolphins chief executive Terry Reader, Tabuai-Fidow is a key retention target for his club who are preparing for their maiden finals appearance.

“It (their top-four finish) is a great sign of the strength of our squad, but also it means the value of that player does grow, and there’s some market conditions that go around that,” he said

“We’re keen to lock up our key players.

“We’ve got a number of players coming off-contract at the end of 2027, and our focus at the moment is going through those negotiations to make sure we can keep them at Dolphins.

“People love superstars, and what Hammer does on the field is amazing.”

In his 76 games for the Dolphins, Tabuai-Fidow has scored an impressive 65 tries — including three hattrick and a four-try performance.

As aforementioned, Tabuai-Fidow won’t be the only Dolphins player that will be making a decision on their futures in the coming months.

A total of 15 players from the Dolphins squad could hit the open market, including the likes of Herbie Farnworth, Connelly Lemuelu, Kulikefu Finefeuiaki, Selwyn Cobbo and Tom Flegler.

Farnworth, meanwhile, reportedly informed the Dolphins he will be putting off contract talks until after his time on the field in 2026 is over.

Speaking to AAP, the English international insisted he has a desire to remain at the 17th franchise — but he wants to focus on on-field results instead of contract distractions.

“All my energy and focus is on the finals series and when the time is right we will talk,” Farnworth said.

“I have told the Dolphins that I want to put it off until after we are done playing, hopefully in the grand final ... and even better if it’s after the World Cup final.

“I love the boys here. We have got a great crew. Woolfy has had a great year and all the boys look up to him. It’s all about really high standards and you have to toe the line.

“We really want to go after this finals series as a team and we feel like we have got the players and roster to win it.”

Farnworth has consistently been linked with a move to the Roosters, but the gun centre has previously poured cold water on a move to the glamour club.

Regardless, Farnworth’s reveal could also open him up to potential raids from rival clubs alongside Tabuai-Fidow.

Interestingly though, the English star’s last contract negotiations also came down to the wire before the last Ashes series, signing only hours before getting on his flight.

BRONCOS ENFORCER’S SHOCK LINKS

Broncos enforcer Xavier Willison has been linked with a potential switch to South Sydney.

Considered an essential part of the Brisbane pack after the departure of Payne Haas, Willison could join the Rabbitohs from 2028 onwards, according to the Rugby League Insider podcast.

Willison is still just 24 and highly rated, able to play either prop of back-row. He is contracted with the Broncos until the end of next season and the club wanted to keep him, but may have a fight on their hands with Wayne Bennett’s Bunnies.

In a twist, Willision is reportedly transitioning to a new manager, Ahmad Merhi — the man who sealed Haas’ $3.6 million, three-year move to Souths. Willison reportedly knocked back a Broncos offer in July, worth a hefty $900,000 per season through to 2030.

Souths are losing Kangaroos Test prop Keaon Koloamatangi to the Dragons, but adding Haas then Willison — who has represented New Zealand and Cook Islands at Test level — would bolster their pack. The Rabbitohs have put virtually all their contract talks on hold to prioritise another young star, lock Tallis Duncan, with the aim of signing him through to 2030 for $700,000-$800,000 per season.

South Sydney have a number of outstanding contract situations to resolve, including those of veteran stars Latrell Mitchell and Cody Walker; with the former touted to come in at up to $1.4 million per season.

DOGS POACH RISING FULLBACK

The Bulldogs have added to their fullback stocks, signing up-and-coming Knights gun Connor Votano.

The 20-year-old’s management, Titans Sports Management, unveiled the news with a post on Instagra, revealing the young gun’s two-year deal starting in 2027.

Votano joined the Knights’ first grade squad in 2025 and went on to make his NRL debut in Round 24 last year.

However, with Kalyn Ponga, Fletcher Sharpe and Fletcher Hunt in Newcastle’s fullback ranks — the rising star has made the decision to depart for a better opportunity.

Votano has been a regular fixture for the Knights’ NSW Cup side, scoring six tries despite sitting out for a short period due to a hamstring injury.

Now he will compete with the likes of Connor Tracey and Kade Dykes, if the latter is handed a contract extension for the 2027 season.

EXILED DRAGONS’ SWITCH LOOMS

Moses Suli has reportedly landed on the radar of Super League clubs after being informed he was free to depart the Dragons.

According to Love Rugby League, multiple clubs in England’s top flight have been alerted of Suli’a availability, with a group showing interesting in his services.

The 28-year-old has slipped down the centre pecking order under new coach Dean Young, having made 93 appearances across five seasons at the club.

Prior to that, Suli spent time at the Tigers and Sea Eagles.

Tyrell Sloan, Damien Cook and Mat Fegai, Suli’s Dragons teammates, are also confirmed to be heading for the Super League, signing with Castleford Tigers in 2027.

ROOSTERS VETERAN’S BIG REVEAL

Veteran winger Daniel Tupou has confirmed that he will sort out his future by “the end of the week” as he gears up for another finals series with the Sydney Roosters.

One of only two Roosters players who are off-contract at the end of the season - the other being Tommy Talau - the 35-year-old is coming to the end of his career but has shown this year that he can still be a regular in first grade.

Amid reports from CODE Sports on Wednesday that he is expected to remain at the Roosters on a one-year contract until the end of 2027, the three-time premiership winner has revealed that he has multiple offers on the table.

“I’m still sorting it out. I’m going to sort it out by the end of this week,” Tupou told foxsports.com.au.

“Just got to catch up with my manager and there’s a couple of options there.

“I know what he’s got for me and we’ll go from there.”

Although remaining at the Tricolours is the likely destination, it is understood that the Super League is another option.

Currently in his 16th season with the club, he remains open to playing in the overseas competition one day in the future.

“Whatever’s (the) best fit for myself and my family,” he added.

“Yeah (the dream is to stay at the Roosters but to go over there (to the Super League) and experience that would be an option and something to look forward to.”

He also made it clear that previous links to the South of France to link up with the Catalans Dragons was something he didn’t expect.

It is understood they have been interested in Tupou ever since 2024, when they identified him and New Zealand Warriors star Dallin Watene-Zelezniak as possible recruits - the duo both remain in the NRL.

“Definitely rumours. It caught me by surprise,” he continued.

SHOCK LENIU REVEAL

The Roosters are on the verge of letting Spencer Leniu go, amid reports they have already lined up a replacement.

The Tricolours prop has been linked to a number of clubs, including the Parramatta Eels, for the 2027 season despite a year to run on his current deal, amid mounting frustration with his on-field temperament and ill discipline.

He was hooked in the 31st minute of the team’s thumping loss to the Rabbitohs to end the home-and-away season after giving away a penalty during a confrontation with Keaon Koloamatangi.

CODE Sports reporter David Riccio told Fox League’s NRL 360 on Tuesday night that Leniu’s future at the club is on a knife’s edge.

“It (this finals series) could be contract-defining, and the Roosters would let him go if he doesn’t learn from that,” Riccio said.

The Daily Telegraph reports that the Roosters won’t stop him from joining the Eels at the end of their NRL finals campaign, where he would link up with former Panthers premiership teammate Jarome Luai on his one-year stop-off before his move to Papua New Guinea.

The report also revealed Braden Uele has been touted as his prop replacement on a one-year deal.

The unsigned Sharks veteran, who has been at the club since 2018, has been on the outer for much of the season, and hasn’t been picked for their elimination final against the Cowboys.

He has managed just four first-grade matches in 2026, including just two since Round 4.

‘TOO GOOD TO LEAVE’: TWIST IN DRAGON’S SUPER LEAGUE MOVE

Outgoing Dragons fan favourite Tyrell Sloan has vowed he will “definitely be back” in the NRL one day.

And it has been revealed that return might come sooner than anyone thinks.

Sloan ended his time at St George Illawarra in a blaze of glory with a stunning two-point field goal to secure a 24-22 win over the Eels.

He, veteran Damien Cook and Mat Feagai are all set to join Castleford in the Super League, with Sloan not offered a new deal despite being keen to stay.

“I don’t reckon it’ll be the last time you see me in the NRL,” the 24-year old told ESPN.

“I’ll definitely be back.

“Regardless of what jersey I put on, I’ll always have the Red V at heart.”

Sloan, who progressed through the Dragons’ junior pathway before breaking through at NRL level, was set link up with ex-Dragons assistant and interim head coach Ryan Carr, who is at the helm at Castleford.

But Carr’s sacking in August has opened the door for a change of heart, according to NRL journalist David Riccio.

“Tyrell was going to sleep on his couch to start his career over there,” Riccio told Fox League’s NRL 360.

“At this stage he’s on the plan because the Dragons don’t have a roster spot for him.

“Now, if there’s wriggle room, and maybe a player goes, then suddenly it’s back on.”

“They’d keep him in a heartbeat.”

Co-host and former great Gorden Tallis said Sloan is “too good to leave the NRL”.

“He would have had so much natural talent and relied on it, and now he’s learned to work hard with it,” he said.

“He’s only 24 – and I was thinking on the weekend, a guy like Cody Walker didn’t reach his best until 27-28,” journalist Paul Crawley added.

‘DON’T THINK HE’LL GO’: RAIDERS CEO’S CALL ON YOUNG’S FUTURE

Canberra CEO Don Furner has expressed his confidence that Hudson Young will remain at the club, as the Raiders star weighs up his options.

Young has been at the centre of a bidding war between the Raiders and the PNG Chiefs, having been given permission to negotiate with the new NRL franchise despite having another year to run on his current contract.

While the Raiders’ reported offer of $1.1 million a season for the forward exceeds the Chiefs’ $900,000, the latter deal would be tax-free.

However, complicating the deal is the NRL’s view the Chiefs’ deal is below the 28-year old’s market value, causing the club to back down amid concerns matching the Raiders’ offer could stretch their salary cap to breaking point.

Speaking on SEN’s Kick Off, Furner said he was confident Young would remain in green even without the NRL intervention – and said the Chiefs remain their only realistic rivals for his signature.

“We’re quietly confident, but you never know – I mean, Papua New Guinea could increase their offer,” he said.

“I don’t think he’ll go anywhere else.

“He’s told us that it’ll be either us or there, so I guess that gives us a 50-50 chance.”

Young has previously said he would not make any decisions on his future until he was able to fly to Port Moresby and learn more about PNG and the new franchise in person.

Raiders coach Ricky Stuart told The Canberra Times last week that Young “doesn’t want to play for any other NRL club”, and said the move became “out of the question” for the NSW star when he needed to make a decision sight unseen.

“Obviously, he wanted to explore his options,” Furner added.

“We’d rather him get it over and done within the next couple of weeks rather than go to the 1st of November.

“Gor him to make the right decision hopefully and stay with us, I think he wanted to feel comfortable that he had checked everything else out.

“Not knowing that, he might have regretted that decision.

“He certainly wanted to see what it would have been like over there and hopefully make an informed decision, but we’re hoping that he stays.”

Furner confirmed the club will begin talks with Young on his future this week.

KEVVIE COUP AS SUPER LEAGUE CLUB SIGN DOG

Sean O’Sullivan has become Kevvie Walters’ first major signing as St Helens coach.

As reported by loverugbyleague.com, the NRL journeyman, who played six games for the Bulldogs in 2026, will head to the Super League giants on an initial one-year contract.

The move comes out of left field, with O’Sullivan heavily linked to Super League rivals the Bradford Bulls, who he reportedly backflipped on when the Saints expressed their interest.

The halfback has been on the outer in his first season at the Dogs, with the inaugural Dolphin’s half-dozen games taking his career NRL tally to 66.

He served as the 18th man for the Panthers’ 2022 grand final win over the Eels, and has served at six NRL clubs since beginning his career with a single game for the Roosters in 2018.
