New Honda ADV125 Scooter Debuts - 12.5 PS, 5-Inch TFT, RoadSync

TheTools · 03.11.2026 03:39 · source · permalink

Honda’s youngest ADV scooter, the ADV125, suits both urban explorations and destinations beyond city limits

At EICMA 2026, Honda has introduced the newest member of its adventure scooter lineup – the ADV125. While inheriting the same rugged character as its larger siblings, the ADV125 will be more accessible in terms of its pricing. It will primarily target urban riders and A1 licence holders in Europe.

Honda ADV125 – Performance

Powering the ADV125 is a 125 cc, water-cooled eSP+ SOHC four-valve engine that generates 12.5 PS at 8,750 rpm and 11.7 Nm of torque at 6,500 rpm. This is the same engine as used with the PCX125. Honda ADV125 has fast acceleration and it is designed to cruise effortlessly at 90 km/h. Top speed is rated at 97 km/h.

While delivering robust performance, ADV125 is also quite fuel efficient, offering a mileage of approximately 47.6 km/l (WMTC mode). With an 8.1 litre fuel tank, the overall range is around 385 km. ADV125 gets various performance boosting technologies such as Honda Selectable Torque Control (HSTC), eSP+, PGM-FI fuel injection, brushless ACG starter and Idling Stop System.

SUV-inspired styling

ADV125 features sharp, muscular bodywork, aligned with the design approach adopted across Honda’s wider ADV family. Key features include a large windscreen, aggressive dual headlamps with integrated DRLs, step-up seat and an upswept exhaust. Honda is offering four colour options with ADV125 – Pearl Falcon Grey, Pearl Cool White, Mat Cynos Gray Metallic and Mat Pearl Diaspro Red.

Specifications

Honda ADV125 uses a steel tubular underbone frame, integrated with telescopic front forks and dual rear shock absorbers. Wheel combo is 14-inch front and 13-inch rear, paired with 110/80 and 130/70 Pirelli/Tourance tyres, respectively. Braking setup comprises 240 mm and 220 mm discs at the front and rear, respectively. The ADV uses Nissin brake callipers at both ends. Single channel ABS is offered as standard.

Block-pattern tubeless tyres have been used, which can easily manage rough patches and mild off-road tracks. The go-anywhere attitude also comes from a generous ground clearance of 165 mm. Seat height of 780 mm makes agile handling as well as slow riding a lot easier to manage. ADV125 meets practical needs with its 29-litre underseat storage and 2-litre glove box.

Tech package, accessories

Honda ADV125 is equipped with a 5-inch TFT display, presenting all the information in a clear, crisp format. The scooter also gets Honda RoadSync connectivity suite. When paired with their smartphone, riders can access messages, navigation and music controls. While enhancing convenience, Honda RoadSync also improves safety by allowing the rider to stay focused on the road ahead.

Other key highlights include Honda SMART key, which allows keyless ignition. Users can also open the seat and fuel filler cap without using a physical key. The SMART key also integrates anti-theft and answer-back functions. For added convenience, Honda ADV125 is equipped with a USB Type-C charging port. Users can further improve overall experience with accessories such as heated grips, security buzzer, body cover, rear carrier and a 40-litre top box.
