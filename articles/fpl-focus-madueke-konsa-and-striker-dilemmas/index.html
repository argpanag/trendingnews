Two weeks have passed since the last taste of Premier League action, giving you plenty of time to mull over your Fantasy Premier League side!

Gameweek 10 saw us defeat Hull City to make it nine wins and sit top of the league table - has your FPL team been in similar form?

Whether your team is top of the charts or struggling to get going, fear not, the FPL Scout has given an insight into how to shape your team for Gameweek 11!

BEST OF ARSENAL

Martin Odegaard (£6.7m) stole the show in the Gunners’ 1-0 victory against Hull City in Gameweek 10 with an 11-point Fantasy return. The Norwegian continued his impressive home form, notching his third double-digit haul in five league matches at the Emirates. No Arsenal player has scored more points in home matches than Odegaard (36). As well as scoring the winning goal, the Gunners captain claimed maximum bonus matches, largely because of his five chances created. 

In defence, the Gunners made it seven clean sheets in 10 league matches. Consequently, David Raya (£6.2m) extended his lead at the top of the goalkeeper overall points standings. Over 60,000 managers have bought in the Spanish shot-stopper, who could rise in price before the Gameweek 11 deadline. Both Ezri Konsa (£4.9m) and Gabriel (£8.1m) added defensive contribution points to their clean sheet, returning eight points in total. Over the last four Gameweeks, no defender has scored more points than Konsa (41) with Gabriel in second place on (39). 

Elsewhere, Noni Madueke (£6.2m) put in another impressive performance, assisting for the first time in FPL this season on his way to an eight-point return. The Englishman had four shots in the box, created four chances and had 15 penalty box touches against the Tigers, the most of any Arsenal player. Over the last five Gameweeks, Madueke ranks second behind Odegaard for expected goal involvement (xGI) among all Gunners players. 

TOP TRANSFERS

Outside of Arsenal, forwards are very much in demand ahead of Saturday's deadline with Nicolas Jackson (£7.1m), Evanilson (£6.5m) and Thierno Barry (£5.7m) the three most-bought players. All three players scored in GW10. The latter was the FPL Player of the Week last time out, notching a 16-point haul against Coventry after scoring twice and assisting once. No player has recorded more big chances (22) or more xG (7.28) than Barry this season. 

Both Evanilson and Jackson have proven to be consistent in recent weeks. The former has scored in five consecutive Gameweeks, registering 43 points in that time ahead of Bournemouth’s home fixture against Nottingham Forest. The Tricky Trees are without a clean sheet since Gameweek 3. Jackson has notched two double-digit hauls in his last three matches, risen in price six times since Gameweek 6 and has over 400,000 new owners.

Luka Vuskovic (£5.3m) is a defender proving popular following 30 points in the last four Gameweeks, including two double-digit hauls. The Croatian international has risen in price over the international break and now sits just five points behind his out-of-position teammate Maxim De Cuyper (£5.5m) in the overall standings. Brighton are about to embark on a very favourable run of fixtures. 

GAMEWEEK 11 TIPS

Make sure you don’t miss the deadline! Finalise your team by 11am GMT on Saturday 21st November ahead of Man City versus Fulham at the Etihad. 

With plenty of uncertainties swirling around key FPL assets, it makes sense to roll a transfer if you can this week. A change of manager at Manchester United could make Bruno Fernandes (£11.6m) an option for the Red Devils’ favourable upcoming fixture run, while we await a fitness update on Joao Pedro (£7.6m). William Saliba (£5.9m) could disrupt Arsenal’s backline and make Konsa an uncertain pick going forward.
