De Zerbi's Tottenham Sink Further as Arsenal Suffer Shock Brighton Defeat

TheTools · 19.09.2026 22:49 · source · permalink

Premier League

Tottenham's James Maddison in despair after the defeat to Villa. Epa

After a €400 million transfer window, Spurs have just two points from five matches. Ruggeri made his first start for the Villans. The reigning champions, the Gunners, lost by a clear 3-0 margin.

Shock in Brighton: Arsenal slumped to a dismal defeat in Brighton, losing their first match of the year. Tottenham also struggled, falling at home to Aston Villa, meaning De Zerbi heads into the international break still without a win. Tomorrow, Maresca's Manchester City, hosting Sunderland, could go clear at the top of the Premier League. Sunday's fifth matchday also features Iraola's return to Bournemouth with Liverpool, and a struggling Manchester United visiting Fulham.

TOTTENHAM-ASTON VILLA 2-3

Delayed until October. Tottenham head into the international break without a win and with growing concerns, after a heavy 3-2 home defeat to Aston Villa, with two goals – their first of the season – coming as mere consolation. De Zerbi goes into the break with just two points from five matches, despite nearly €400 million spent in the transfer market, with a team that has failed to ignite and ongoing work falling worryingly behind schedule. Aston Villa were in the same boat as Spurs before this match, also without a Premier League win, but they abandoned the sinking ship with a splendid second-half performance, initially kept afloat by Zion Suzuki's saves and then propelled by goals from Johan Manzambi and Nicolas Jackson, two of the six new signings Unai Emery started (including Matteo Ruggeri, making his first Premier League start).

Emi Buendia applied the finishing touch with a wonderful shot from outside the box into the corner in the 79th minute, prompting Spurs fans to begin their exodus towards the exits. De Zerbi's half-full glass is the reaction, however belated: Gallagher in the 86th minute and Van Hecke in the 98th at least got them off the mark for goals scored in the Premier League. Up until that point, despite their usual dominant opening 25 minutes which created chances superbly thwarted by Zion Suzuki (but no goals), Tottenham had taken a worrying step backwards in terms of performance.

 Villa deservedly ended their winless run and can now begin phase two of their summer revolution, which saw them bid farewell to the heroes of their Europa League triumph and usher in a new era. Tottenham are still chasing their first victory, waiting for the spark that can unlock their potential. De Zerbi now has three weeks to figure out how to get his Tottenham side firing, but the regression shown in this defeat is too significant not to cause concern.

BRIGHTON-ARSENAL 3-0 

“Are you Tottenham in disguise?” Brighton fans taunted Arsenal, who were sensationally torn apart at the Amex by the Seagulls. Brighton dominated the match and were deservedly rewarded with an undisputed 3-0 victory, a fitting climax to their 125th-anniversary celebrations. Arteta's side looked a shadow of the team that had dominated their first seven matches of the season across all competitions. Instead, they displayed the weak, uninspired side in Brighton that their Premier League title win last year and the start of this season had suggested was a thing of the past. Nothing worked for the Gunners; neither their usually formidable defence, where even stalwarts like Gabriel and Raya had moments to regret, nor their previously impeccable attack, which fired a blank for the first time this season. Brighton struck in the first half with two unstoppable long-range efforts: Pascal Gross in the 31st minute, once again among the best performers, and Kostoulas in the 45th, immediately after Saka had squandered a golden opportunity to equalise. Chema's header in the 57th minute definitively sealed the match, even with over half an hour remaining, during which the Seagulls came closer to a fourth goal than the Gunners did to initiating a comeback. Arsenal had not conceded three away goals in the Premier League since 5th December 2023, when they won 4-3 at Luton, and generally hadn't shipped so many since 25th January, in a 3-2 home defeat to Manchester United. The Gunners could not have picked a worse time to falter, with three weeks until their next match (1st October at home to Leeds) during which the question of whether this Brighton debacle is a mere blip or a worrying sign that something is amiss will dominate discussions. For Huerzeler's Seagulls, however, everything is working perfectly: they now have 10 points in the table, trailing only Arsenal and Manchester City, boast the Premier League's best attack with 16 goals, and have confirmed their ability to soar.

Other Results 

Newcastle put their disastrous Leeds defeat behind them, beating Hull City 2-1, handing the surprising newly-promoted side their first loss of the season. The Magpies climb to 8 points in the table, one point behind Everton, who defeated Ipswich 1-0 at home (a third defeat in four matches for the Tractor Boys) and remain unbeaten on 9 points. Saturday's fifth matchday concluded with the clash between Nottingham Forest and Coventry, won 0-1 by the visiting side thanks to Dasilva's goal. This marks the first victory and first points secured in the 2026/27 Premier League season for Frank Lampard's team.
