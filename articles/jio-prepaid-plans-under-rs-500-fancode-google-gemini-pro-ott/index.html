Jio Prepaid Plans Under Rs 500: FanCode, Google Gemini Pro, OTT Apps and 5G Benefits

TheTools · 18.09.2026 06:17 · source · permalink

Jio users looking for a prepaid recharge with sports streaming benefits have a few options under Rs 500. The operator currently lists FanCode access with select 28-day prepaid plans, along with data, unlimited calls and bundled digital services. The plans are priced at Rs 399, Rs 459, Rs 495 and Rs 500.

The main difference is not just the daily data allowance. Jio has packed these recharges with different add-ons, including unlimited 5G data for eligible users, Google Gemini Pro, Canva Pro Lite, JioHotstar Mobile, JioGames and OTT subscriptions. This makes the choice less about the lowest price and more about which bundle a user will actually use.

Jio Prepaid Plans with FanCode Under Rs 500

The Rs 399 plan is the lowest-priced option in this set. It offers 28 days of validity, 2GB data per day, unlimited voice calls and 100 SMS per day. Eligible subscribers also get unlimited True 5G data, which can be useful for users in Jio’s 5G coverage areas with compatible phones.

This recharge also includes FanCode, JioHotstar Mobile, JioGames Pass, JioTV and JioAICloud. Jio is also bundling an 18-month Google Gemini Pro subscription with the plan. The company lists the value of this offer at Rs 35,100 and says it includes 5TB cloud storage, Gemini Live, Nano Banana and other AI features.

The Rs 459 prepaid plan offers the same 28-day validity, 2GB daily data, unlimited calls and 100 SMS per day. It also supports unlimited 5G data for eligible users. The larger appeal here is the wider set of app-linked benefits compared with the Rs 399 recharge.

With the Rs 459 plan, users get FanCode, JioGames Mobile, Snapchat+, JioHotstar Mobile, JioTV and JioAICloud. Jio is also offering 18 months of Google Gemini Pro and a 12-month Canva Pro Lite subscription. The operator notes that users may need to remain on an eligible unlimited 5G plan of Rs 349 or above to continue using some bundled benefits.

How the Rs 495 and Rs 500 Jio Plans Differ

The Rs 495 plan is slightly different because it offers 1.5GB data per day instead of 2GB. It also includes an extra 5GB of data, unlimited calls and 100 SMS per day. The validity remains 28 days, making it suitable for users who want FanCode but do not need 2GB daily data.

This plan includes FanCode and JioGames Cloud for 28 days. JioTV and JioAICloud are also part of the recharge. As a limited-period offer, Jio is adding an 18-month Google Gemini Pro subscription, including 5TB cloud storage and other AI features listed by the company.

The Rs 500 recharge is the most entertainment-heavy option in this group. It includes 28 days of validity, 2GB data per day, unlimited voice calls, 100 SMS per day and unlimited 5G data for eligible subscribers. It also includes FanCode and JioGames Mobile.

Its biggest addition is access to 15 premium OTT apps and services. Jio lists YouTube Premium, Amazon Prime Video Mobile Edition, JioHotstar Mobile, SonyLIV, ZEE5, Discovery+, SunNXT, ETV Win and Hoichoi among the included platforms. Users also get live TV channels through JioTV.

Which Jio Recharge Makes More Sense?

For most users, the Rs 399 plan is the straightforward pick if the priority is daily data, calls, FanCode and some entertainment benefits. The Rs 459 plan makes more sense for users who want Canva Pro Lite and Snapchat+ along with the AI and streaming bundle.

The Rs 495 recharge is useful only if the lower 1.5GB daily data allowance is not a concern. The Rs 500 plan offers the broadest entertainment package and is better suited for users who will actively use multiple OTT apps. Before recharging, users should check benefit activation rules in the MyJio app, as bundled subscriptions may have eligibility conditions.
