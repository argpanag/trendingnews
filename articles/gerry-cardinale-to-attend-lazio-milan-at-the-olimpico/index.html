Gerry Cardinale to attend Lazio-Milan at the Olimpico

TheTools · 12.09.2026 10:12 · source · permalink

Gerry Cardinale will be in attendance at the Stadio Olimpico in Rome tonight for his Milan’s match against Lazio. It will be the Rossoneri owner’s fourth appearance at the stadium this season, having already watched all three of the team’s previous matches against Torino, Venezia and Juventus from the stands.

The RedBird founder was not at Milanello on the eve of the match, but will travel directly to Rome today to be close to the Milan squad and Ruben Amorim. La Gazzetta dello Sport reported this in its morning's print edition.
