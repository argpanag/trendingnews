    Tyrese Maxey led the 76ers with 28 points (9-of-19 FGs, 8-of-9 FTs), four rebounds, five assists, three steals, three blocks and two three-pointers in a 106-100 win over the Knicks on Tuesday.

    Even with all the roster changes, this is still going to be Maxey’s team. While spoiling the Knicks’ ring night, he was assertive on both ends of the floor and truly stuffed the stat sheet. Having more playmaking and scoring threats around him will only make things easier on him, and it’s clear he’s not going to have to sacrifice much volume. Those who took Maxey at the end of the first round in fantasy drafts may end up reaping some major rewards.
    - Noah Rubin
