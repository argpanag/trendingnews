Queen Mette-Marit Is Spotted in Public for the First Time in Months

TheTools · 17.11.2026 15:27 · source · permalink

The Norwegian queen is currently on medical leave.

King Haakon and Queen Mette-Marit made a surprise appearance in Trondheim today, visiting the Nidaros Cathedral. Next summer, the two will be consecrated at the Cathedral (Norway does not have a coronation). The palace also confirmed the couple spent time at Stiftsgården, the royal residence in Trondheim, and visited the Archbishop’s Palace, where the Norwegian crown jewels are on display.

“We have high expectations for that. I think it will be very nice,” King Haakon told media about the upcoming consecration, according to NRK. The outing marked a rare appearance for Queen Mette-Marit, who is recovering from her lung transplant. The Norwegian queen is not expected to return to royal duties until summer 2027; her last appearance was at King Harald’s funeral in early September.

“The Crown Princess’s condition is as good as can be expected under the circumstances,” Are Holm, Head of the Department of Respiratory Medicine at Oslo University Hospital Rikshospitalet, said in July. “Over the next six months, the Crown Princess will undergo physical rehabilitation and be closely monitored for possible complications, including rejection and infections.”

Trondheim’s mayor, Kent Ranum, said of the royal couple’s visit today, “We welcomed them to the city and talked about the big event for next year.” The consecration, he added, is “still in the very early stages. But it’s great that they’re here and to know the outlines of the big event for next year.”

Ranum added, “Then it is important that we do what we can to make it the best possible weekend, both for the royal couple, the royal family and the nation. The royal couple are looking forward to it too. This will be very good.”
