Trump’s new press secretary shared ‘pro-Putin’ post

TheTools · 13.10.2026 15:46 · source · permalink

Katie Zacharia promoted message claiming Russian leader was fighting against Left-wing cabal

Donald Trump’s new press secretary shared a social media post seemingly in support of Vladimir Putin weeks after the Russian invasion of Ukraine in 2022.

Katie Zacharia, appointed to replace Karoline Leavitt as White House press secretary following her departure in August, held her first briefing on Tuesday.

In a media conference that was noticeably more stilted than Ms Leavitt’s sparring sessions, Ms Zacharia revealed that the US president had asked Kimberley Guilfoyle, his under-fire Greek ambassador, to resign.

Ms Zacharia also praised the economy and thanked Mr Trump for the new role.

Before her first briefing, a report by CNN revealed that Ms Zacharia had reshared a Truth Social post in 2022 that claimed Putin was fighting a secret network of powerful Left-wing elites.

“Putin is at war with the CABAL – and the Ukraine is in bed with them,” read the post, shared by an account named Richard Tomas.

It featured images of prominent figures including Hillary Clinton, Barack Obama, Joe Biden and George Soros.

Ms Zacharia was previously a senior communications adviser to Trump Media and Technology Group, the president’s US media company, which owns his Truth Social platform.

She would often appear on Fox News as an expert on the Trump administration, and had a brief stint working as a spokesman for Kristi Noem, then secretary at the department of homeland security.

When announcing her appointment, Mr Trump praised her backing for his administration on Truth Social, saying she had an “unblemished record of support” and had been a “loyal and brilliant supporter, right from the beginning”.

However, in a post on X, which has been deleted, Ms Zacharia had criticised the Trump administration with a warning about the upcoming midterms.

Responding to a post on the platform by Mike Cernovich, a Right-wing American commentator, Ms Zacharia wrote: “2026 Midterms will be a bloodbath if we don’t shift course now.”

Pizzagate conspiracy

Other posts from 2022 show that Ms Zacharia often shared “Pizzagate” conspiracy theories, which claimed a cabal of powerful people was sexually abusing children.

“Pedo Podesta. #pedoPodesta,” Ms Zacharia wrote in September 2022, according to CNN, referring to John Podesta, Bill Clinton’s White House chief of staff, who had been subject to a conspiracy theory campaign.

It was claimed that his emails, released by Russian-linked hackers, contained coded references to child sex trafficking and that Mrs Clinton, whose presidential campaign he was managing at the time, had operated a child sex ring out of the basement of a pizzeria in Washington.

Mr Podesta has never been formally accused or charged with a crime. He denies the allegations and has dismissed them as alt-Right fake news, describing it as “this Pizzagate bulls---”.

Mrs Clinton said the Pizzagate conspiracy was “totally made up” during a congressional deposition on the Jeffrey Epstein files in February this year.

When approached for comment, Katie Zacharia said she was “grateful to serve President Trump as Press Secretary, and I will diligently and effectively amplify his successful agenda”. 

Steven Cung, the White House communications director, added: “Katie is a fabulous and wonderful addition to the team. She is uniquely qualified and whip smart to help inform the American people about all the great things President Trump and his Administration have been doing, and she will do an incredible job as Press Secretary.”
