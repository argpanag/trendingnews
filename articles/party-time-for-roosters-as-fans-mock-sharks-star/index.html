Marky Mark on that banner

As expected, Mark Nawaqanitawase was a little embarrassed by that banner portraying him as Ronaldo Mulitalo’s father.

“It’s a bit slack, he’s a wonderful competitor,” he said on Nine.

“We didn’t have too much going on against each other tonight. I’ve got to thank him a lot, it’s been really fun to go out there and play against him.”

DCE reflects on Panther loss

After failing to produce the clutch moment against Penrith, Daly Cherry-Evans says “there was no one more disappointed in missed moments than myself”.

“I was really hard on myself but the thing is at this time of the year you can’t stay hard on yourself for the whole week,” he told Nine.

“Otherwise you disrupt the team’s prep and my prep.”

Teddy backs Walker to return

James Tedesco has given a big hint on the fitness of Sam Walker.

Walker could make a miraculous return from injury with Tedesco saying “Sammy’s a chance”.

“He was training this week, running and running his plays well,” Tedesco said on Nine.

“He’ll be a real chance this week.”

That’s a statement from the Chooks tonight!

The final score is 46-10 as the Roosters bounce back successfully from that loss to the Panthers.

Will they have Sam Walker back next week? That could be a massive inclusion.

The Roosters have another try through Mark Nawaqanitawase.

Robert Toia put a grubber in and it bobbled around before the cross-code star picked it up and swan-dived over the line.

Roosters fans mock Mulitalo

Roosters fans have shown a poster showing Mark Nawaqanitawase holding Ronaldo Mulitalo like a baby.

Both men have had a rivalry since the last time these sides met.

“I think Ronaldo would even like that,” Brad Fittler said on Nine.
