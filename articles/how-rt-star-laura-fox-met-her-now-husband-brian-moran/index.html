How RTÉ star Laura Fox met her now-husband Brian Moran

TheTools · 23.10.2026 17:13 · source · permalink

RTÉ star Laura Fox has tied the knot with her partner of over a decade, Brian Moran.

Laura and Brian got engaged whilst on holiday in August 2024, saying at the time: 'It was always forever B Balls, but now, it’s forever with a ring. I love you so much I could burst!'

So how did the now-spouses meet? Laura quite literally danced her way into Brian's heart when they first saw each other in a nightclub all those years ago. Here's everything we know about their love story...

Laura and Brian, whom she affectionately calls 'B Balls', first met in a nightclub.

Speaking to RSVP magazine, the Ireland's Fittest Family host said: 'Like every Galway person, I met him in a nightclub, and he was in college with my friends. We were at parties together, and then we became pals. We ended up together then.'

'He was in college with my friends, and I knew his friends; we were never supposed to be together. He was just one of the lads, and I was one of the girls. We ended up hanging out more, and he never left.'

Laura and Brian officially started dating in July 2015, and the rest -as they say- is history!

Laura and Brian took a major step less than one year into their relationship, deciding to move in together upon finding themselves having to move up to the Big Smoke.

Speaking about living with her beau, the RTÉ 2FM presenter told the publication: 'It’s great, but I’m not a young one; it would be a bit weird if I wasn’t living with my partner!'

Laura went on to explain that their move to Dublin worked out timing-wise, as she was thinking about relocating when Brian was offered a job in the city.

'I knew that if I was to make my career work, I had to be in Dublin. He ended up moving before me, I think it was out of spite, to be honest,' Laura quipped.

Upon learning that she was set to take part in the 2024 series of Dancing with the Stars, Laura and Brian had a very 'frank' conversation because she knew it was going to be such a huge commitment.

The Galway girl realised she would need 'to give every waking second' because 'this is something you can't fake' if she wanted to reach the grand finale.

Laura, who is partnered with pro-Denys Samson, explained: '[Brian] was like, "I've got you. I will make sure that the washing is done." That was my main thing, I was like how am I going to get my washing done for the week because I don't have time?'

He told her: '"I'll make sure the washing's done, I'll stay on dinners, I'll walk the dog",' as Laura added: 'He's happy to be there to support me and just push me along throughout all of this.'

Laura said: 'I was asking him the other day what he was going to wear because I knew he was going to be in Karen Byrne and Jason Smyth's dance, so he was in the booth, but I didn't tell him. His response was: '"Well I have to wear my cream chinos because they're my lucky pants. I've worn them for every show, and you've done really well so far".'

Having been together almost nine years, Laura and Brian have been saving for a mortgage, with the DWTS contestant saying in the past that a wedding 'wasn't a priority for [her].'

'I never had the money to put into a wedding because it wasn't a priority for me,' she told Women's Way. 'I mean that in the nicest possible way, as Brian and I are set for life, so we'll have the party at some point.'

But things changed, and Brian got down on one knee while he and Laura were enjoying a romantic holiday in August 2024.

At the time, she showed off her new bling in a sweet selfie, writing: 'It was always forever B Balls, but now, it’s forever with a ring. I love you so much I could burst!'

After blowing off some steam with a Vegas hen do, Laura and Brian tied the knot in a legal ceremony in Dublin's City Hall on Tuesday, April 21.

The 2FM star opted to wear a white mini dress on her special day, showing off her long legs.

Short, chic dresses have been growing in popularity in recent years, with a string of famous faces choosing a mini dress for their civil ceremony.

'Mr and Mrs FoMo. Fox, Moran crew! We’re MARRIED!!!!,' Laura penned as she shared their first official wedding day snaps.

The couple celebrated with friends and family at Café en Seine, and are expected to celebrate their nuptials once more with a lavish second wedding in Italy later this year.

In August, Laura and Brian said I Do for the second time at their destination wedding in Italy.

A host of the couple's showbiz pals flew out to be in attendance, including Arthur Gourounlian and Brian Dowling, Katja Mia, Tracy Clifford, and Jennifer Zamparelli.

Sharing the first official photo from her wedding day, Laura wrote: ''We’re married!!! The FoMos, Fox Morans hehe! We’re so lucky to have all of the people in the world who care about us here for a GREAT TIME!! Sorry for the delay, I’ve been in the pool for eight hours.'
