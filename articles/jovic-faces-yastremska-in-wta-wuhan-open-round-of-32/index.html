Jovic Faces Yastremska in WTA Wuhan Open Round of 32

TheTools · 14.10.2026 05:47 · source · permalink

Iva Jovic will face Dayana Yastremska in the round of 32 at the WTA Wuhan event in China, with Dimers' model projecting Jovic as the most likely winner. The matchup is part of the women's singles tournament and is based on statistical simulations.

Dimers reported that its advanced tennis model identified Jovic as the projected winner for the upcoming Jovic-Yastremska match. The outlet said the analysis was generated from its tennis prediction model.

The match follows Jovic's earlier WTA Wuhan Open appearance against Mia Pohankova in the round of 64. Dimers reported that its model gave Jovic an 81% chance of winning that match and a 76% chance of taking the first set.

According to Dimers, the previous Jovic-Pohankova simulation also showed Pohankova had a 53% chance of staying within the model's projected games margin, while the total games projection reached a 70% probability in the model's analysis.

Dimers reported that Jovic's previous match against Pohankova was scheduled for Monday, October 12, 2026, at approximately 12:10 AM ET, as part of the WTA Wuhan, China Women's Singles 2026 tournament.

The Jovic-Yastremska matchup is listed by Dimers as a round of 32 contest at the WTA Wuhan event, with the outlet providing a pre-match projection based on its simulation system.
