PSG x Nike KD 6 "Metallic Silver": Kevin Durant's sneaker is available - SELECTA BISSO

TheTools · 19.09.2026 20:48 · source · permalink

The collaboration between Kevin Durant, Nike, and Paris Saint-Germain has reached a new milestone with the arrival of a KD 6 inspired by the Parisian club. Dubbed "Metallic Silver," this special edition has been available since September 18, 2026.

Priced at $135 , the pair is available in men's sizes. It also marks a significant release for the model, as it's the first completely new colorway of the Nike KD 6 since the silhouette's return to the brand's retro collections.

A KD 6 in PSG colors

The shoe features a silver base that covers most of the upper. The Metallic Silver colorway gives the sneaker a particularly bright and technical look, almost comparable to that of a football boot. Despite this visual inspiration, the shoe retains the original construction and features designed for basketball.

The traditional colors of Paris Saint-Germain are incorporated through several navy blue and red details. These contrasting touches appear particularly on the various elements of the upper, the tongue, and the sole, allowing for quick identification of the Parisian club's identity.

A circular badge displaying the number 35 is also placed on the outer side of the shoe. This detail references the number worn by Kevin Durant throughout his career and reinforces the connection between the NBA player and PSG.

Kevin Durant, new partner of the Parisian club

This announcement comes after Kevin Durant's increasing involvement with Paris Saint-Germain. The basketball player recently acquired a minority stake in the club, which has led to several joint projects with Nike.

Prior to the KD 6, the partnership had already manifested itself through a KD 18 PE in PSG colors, as well as a clothing collection called "DC to Paris ." This new sneaker thus continues a relationship that has become increasingly visible between the American star, the Swoosh brand, and the Parisian team.

With its silver design, navy and red accents, and "35" emblem, the PSG x Nike KD 6 "Metallic Silver" appeals to both basketball fans and Paris Saint-Germain supporters. Available for $135, it could quickly become a sought-after item for collectors in both worlds.
