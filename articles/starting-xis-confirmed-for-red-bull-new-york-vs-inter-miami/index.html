Starting XIs confirmed for Red Bull New York vs Inter Miami

Inter Miami face Red Bull New York tonight at Sports Illustrated Stadium on MLS Matchday 32. According to Inter Miami, kick-off is 5:30 p.m. ET and the starting XIs are confirmed.

Inter Miami set up 4-3-3, with Dayne St. Clair in goal. The back four is Gonzalo Luján, Casemiro, Micael and Sergio Reguilón. The midfield three is Rodrigo De Paul, David Ayala and Matías Galarza. The front line is Riquelme Fillipi, captain Luis Suárez and Germán Berterame.

The bench features Rocco Ríos Novo, Ian Fray, Facundo Mura, Telasco Segovia, Yannick Bright, Santiago Morales, Alexander Shaw, Leo Messi and Daniel Pinter. Head coach is Cristian González.

Red Bull New York also go 4-3-3, with Ethan Horvath in goal. The defence is Dylan Nealis, Julian Bazan, Tim Parker and Devin Padelford. The midfield is Mohammed Sofo, Adri Mehmeti and Ronald Donkor. The attack is Cade Cowell, Julian Hall and Jorge Ruvalcaba.

Nealis captains the side. The bench includes John McCarthy, Jahkeele Marshall-Rutty, Matthew Dos Santos, Sekou Kone, David Ruiz, Emil Forsberg, Eric Choupo-Moting, Cameron Harper and Rafael Mosquera. Head coach is Michael Bradley.
