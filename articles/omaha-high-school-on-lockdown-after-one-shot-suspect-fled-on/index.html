Omaha high school on lockdown after one shot, suspect fled on foot

TheTools · 30.09.2026 13:01 · source · permalink

At least one person was shot at Burke High School in Omaha, Nebraska, on Wednesday, prompting a lockdown as police scour the area for the suspect — who fled on foot.

Local authorities received a call that a shooting was in progress at approximately 7:30 a.m. local time and raced to the scene.

The shooting itself didn't take place at the school but rather at an intersection just outside it. Local authorities told WOWT that shots were fired at the intersection of S. 120th Street and W. Dodge Road.

Students were rushed into the building, which was placed on lockdown. The incident occurred as they arrived for classes that morning.

According to the X account Omaha Scanner, which follows scanner activity in the Omaha area and reports on it, a 17-year-old boy was shot in the arm.

A handgun was allegedly found on his person, but it remains unclear whether he shot himself or if someone else shot him. He was transported to a local trauma center.

The police have not yet confirmed any of the details, however — just that there was a shooting and that the scene is secure. An investigation is underway.
