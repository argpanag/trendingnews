Gerard Butler has been in a number of action thrillers in his career stretching as far back as Reign of Fire in 2002. However, it was his starring role as King Leonidas in Zack Snyder's 300 that really put him on the map. In the time since, he's become a bona fide star thanks to movies like Law Abiding Citizen and the Has Fallen series, but now, his best action thriller in years has officially landed on a new free streaming home.

Gerard Butler Stars in a Near-Perfect Action Thriller Throwback

Released in 2023, Plane is Butler's superb action thriller directed by Jean-François Richet (Jason Statham's Mutiny) that sees the Scottish sensation take on the role of Brodie Torrance, a former RAF pilot who now flies commercial airlines. When a routine flight from Singapore to Tokyo is interrupted by a violent electrical storm, Brodie is forced to make an emergency landing on a remote island in the Philippines.

That spells bad news for the pilot and his surviving passengers, as they quickly find themselves in hostile territory, hunted by an armed militia. Luckily for Brody (and us), one of those passengers just happens to be Louis Gaspare (Mike Colter), a former French Foreign Legionnaire accused of a crime he didn't commit. Putting their combined skills to good use, Brodie and Louis truck through the jungle to rescue everyone taken hostage by the militia.

At just 107-minutes long, Plane is an old-school throwback to action thrillers of yesteryear that many believed was based on a true story. It wastes no time on heavy exposition, is straightforward, violent, and by the time it's over, you know you've been taken for a wild ride. Think Die Hard in the jungle, and you pretty much have a general idea of what Plane is like.

That being said, it was that straightforward formula that made Plane such a hit with audiences. Grossing $74 million at the box office against a production budget of just $25 million, the film went on to become Butler's highest rated action thriller in years. It currently holds a 94% on Rotten Tomatoes from viewers, placing it number one on the list of Butler's many action outings over the likes of Angel Has Fallen (93%), 300 (89%), and Kandahar (82%).

'Plane' Soars Onto Free Streaming

While it's bounced around more than a few streaming services since it debuted in 2023, Plane has officially touched down on Tubi this month. That now makes it free to watch in addition to its availability on Prime Video, which is definitely beneficial for those without a subscription to Amazon. Sure, you might have to sit through a few ad breaks, but it's a small price to pay to see Butler being Butler in a top-notch action thriller that some critics called an "adrenaline-pumping banger."

Next up for Butler is a return to the How to Train Your Dragon franchise. He'll reprise his role of Stoick the Vast in the upcoming sequel to the 2025 live-action hit, which is slated to hit theaters on June 11, 2027.
