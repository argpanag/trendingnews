GTA VI’s Jason Actor Revealed: Dylan Rourke and Lucia Star Manni L. Perez Pose Together for the First Time

With "Grand Theft Auto VI" officially out, Rockstar Games has finally revealed the main cast it kept under wraps for years. Dylan Rourke plays Jason Duval, while Manni L. Perez portrays Lucia Caminos. The two leads recently appeared together in their first public photo, officially ending years of fan speculation over the identities of the performers behind the characters.

▼Dylan Rourke 

▼Manni L. Perez plays LuciaThe photo was shared by Lucia stunt double Victoria Lee on  Instagram and shows the trio embracing in front of a "GTA VI" billboard. Lee said she had kept her involvement secret for years and could finally reveal that she not only doubled for Lucia, but also performed stunts for several other characters.

Looking back on the production, Lee said the team poured thousands of hours and an immeasurable amount of effort into the game, adding that the physical impact endured during filming was very real. She praised both leads as incredible people inside and out, thanked everyone involved in development and sent a message to players heading into Vice City.

Perez’s role surfaced earlier when her work on "GTA VI" earned her a Best Performance nomination at The Game Awards 2026, indirectly confirming her involvement. Rourke, meanwhile, had long been a popular fan pick because of his resemblance to Jason and the similarity of their voices, but his casting was not officially confirmed until Rockstar released the credits.

The two stars later shared an Instagram post thanking the development team for years of hard work and saying they were honored to contribute to a project that showcases human creativity, ingenuity and perseverance. They signed off with “See y’all in Leonida” and “Jason ♥ Lucia,” nodding to the protagonists’ romantic relationship.

Rourke previously made guest appearances on series including "Modern Family" and "Grey's Anatomy," though he had not landed a widely recognized leading role before "GTA VI." Rockstar’s cast list also includes Bobby Moynihan as Cal and Stephen Root as Brian Heder. Root was one of the few actors to publicly confirm his involvement before the game’s release.
