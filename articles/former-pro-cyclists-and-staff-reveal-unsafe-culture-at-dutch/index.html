Former pro-cyclists and staff reveal unsafe culture at Dutch team PicnicPostNL

TheTools · 15.10.2026 10:50 · source · permalink

There is a culture of fear powered by an unsafe, rigid, top-down environment at the Dutch WorldTour cycling team Picninc-PostNL, previously known as Team Sunweb. Many left the team traumatized, NOS and In De Waaier found based on confidential conversations with 71 people who work or have worked at the WorldTour team.

The issues reportedly date back years. On the evening of October 22, 2020, after the eighteenth stage of the Giro d’Italia, then 26-year-old rider Martijn Tusveld was asked by sports director Rudi Kemna what he thought of the day’s tactics. The rider answered that the team “could also have done it differently.”

By the end of that season, Tusveld was told he was creating a “negative atmosphere,” after which major races disappeared from his 2021 schedule. His contract was also not renewed until two other riders did not return to the team at the last moment. Tusveld was only allowed to stay temporarily until the end of 2024, after which he was required to leave.

In an email to Kemna, reviewed by NOS, Tusveld later wrote that after the meeting “everything changed.” “I became fearful. I thought, "I'll keep my mouth shut and say 'amen.'" And that’s what I did for the rest of the year with the team.” The rider struggled with depressive symptoms and wrote that he “also thought about doing something to [himself].

Similar stories came in from other riders. One former staff member who asked for a mental coach was told by Kemna that he does not “believe in psychologists.” Others spoke of an atmosphere of tyranny and a lack of understanding when riders were nearing burnout.

"They see feedback as an attack on the philosophy," a former staff member said. “And that makes you an enemy of the philosophy.”

After the 2017 Giro wins from Marcel Kittel and Tom Dumoulin, management had reportedly imposed tighter protocols and a stricter top-down structure. From then on, everything had to go through management, meaning that the trainers, for example, could not speak openly about certain topics to their teams and with one another.

Adriaan Helmante, form trainer for Tom Dumoulin, later said that the environment within the team “became a dictatorship.” Riders were allegedly described by some as “the team’s puppets."

The Association of Professional Cyclists confirmed to NOS that it has received multiple complaints over the past year and a half, with riders sometimes crying on the phone. Picnic-PostNL was contacted for a comment by the news outlet but declined.
