Sharma leads in race for Saanich mayor - Saanich News

TheTools · 18.10.2026 04:17 · source · permalink

Published 9:17 pm Saturday, October 17, 2026

By Olivier Laurin

After weeks of campaigning, the wait is nearly over. Polls have officially closed in the 2026 Saanich municipal election.

Voters had four mayoral candidates to choose from: Zac de Vries, Karen Harper, Rishi Sharma and Robin Morton Stanbridge.

“We had heartfelt, honest, empathetic conversations and what I would like to do is somehow to continue the campaign after the election,” Sharma told the Saanich News as the final ballots were counted Saturday (Oct. 17) night. “If we are successful, I want to immediately have a large open house (and) bring in all of the councillors so they can experience… real, honest conversations with individuals and then take that feedback to move forward.”

Despite leading with 47 per cent of the vote to Zac de Vries’ 37 per cent as early results trickled in, Sharma takes nothing for granted.

“I’ve seen the endorsements coming in from former Mayor Dean Murdoch endorsing Zac,” he said. “Those put you in a real state of nervousness and anxiety.

“But I don’t think we could have worked any harder. We have run an amazing campaign… and we’ve attracted a lot of attention and I think it’s because of the work that we’ve done that more people were out in advance voting.”

A few kilometres away, Zac de Vries held his election watch party at Junction Orchard & Cidery. Despite trailing by eight percentage points, the two-term councillor remained positive.

“The energy is great,” de Vries said. “We’re seeing results roll in and we’re looking forward to just seeing how things play out because. We ran a very positive campaign and we’ll see how it goes.”

Having hit the campaign trail well in advance, de Vries said this year was about doubling down on efforts to connect with Saanich residents and hear their concerns.

With the final results awaiting to come in, de Vries mentioned staying optimistic.

“It’s gonna be close so we’re watching carefully,” he said. “We know that we put our best foot forward and ran a very positive campaign, so we’re gonna see how this turns out.”

As of 10:20 p.m., all five of SOS’s council candidates were leading the polls. Lorne DeLarge sits atop the polls with 10,534 votes, followed by Nancy DiCastri, incumbent Colin Plant, Laurie Lidstone, Graham Wright, Matt McGeachie, followed by incumbents Nathalie Chambers and Teale Phelps Bondaroff. Newcomer Mauricio Curbelo trailed Phelps Bondaroff 32 votes for the final council seat.

We will update this page throughout the evening as election results come in.
