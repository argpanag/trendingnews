Who Is Grace Reiter? You Might've Seen SNL's New Cast Member on TV or TikTok

TheTools · 02.10.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

The comedian, writer, and director debuted in the September 26 premiere in the "Dream Academy" sketch.

Key Takeaways

  • Grace Reiter joined the cast of Saturday Night Live Season 52 as a Featured Player after building a large online comedy following.
  • Her viral 2025 parody film “The Hunger Games (but Better)” became a breakout success, earning more than 14 million YouTube views and helping her surpass four million TikTok followers.
  • Before SNL, Reiter performed with sketch groups like Stapleview and American High, and she has acting, writing, and directing credits on projects including The Chair Company.

Season 52 of Saturday Night Live is underway, and viewers were introduced to new Featured Player Grace Reiter, alongside fellow newbie Saidah Belo-Osagie. Reiter made her SNL sketch debut as Margl in the "Dream Academy" sketch, followed by "Old Friend" later in the show. But Reiter had already gained plenty of online fans ahead of her debut.

How to Watch

Watch Saturday Night Live Saturdays at 11:30 p.m. ET on NBC and next day on Peacock.

Before joining SNL, the Chippewa Falls, Wisconsin native honed her comedy skills with videos posted on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. Reiter even co-starred in videos with current SNL Repertory Player Jane Wickline, years before either joined the show. 

If Reiter hasn't already popped up on your feed, you might've seen her in your TV screen — including on a series starring an SNL cast alum.

Keep reading for more details about SNL's new cast member Grace Reiter.

Grace Reiter's Hunger Games (but Better) parody went viral in 2025

Grace Reiter joins the Saturday Night Live Season 52 cast.

Gaining traction with her self-produced comedy videos online, Reiter made the "The Hunger Games (but Better)" trailer, released on TikTok in November 2025. 

Just three days after the trailer dropped, fans were treated to the official release of "The Hunger Games (but Better)" in its entirety. The final product was hit YouTube on November 18, 2025, and begins with a title card that says, "For my 24th birthday, my friends and I spent 3 days in Pennsylvania remaking The Hunger Games from memory."

The 42-minute video has amassed over 14 million views as of October 2026.

As for Reiter's next pop culture opus, her TikTok bio yields clues: "Twilight (but better) coming soon."

Here Are The Stars Who Have Hosted SNL The Most Times

Chevy Chase has hosted SNL 8 times in total. He hosted his first episode back in 1978. 

Buck Henry has hosted SNL 10 times in total. He also became the very first SNL five-timer. He hosted his first episode back in 1976. 

Tom Hanks has also hosted SNL 10 times in total. Hanks's fifth episode famously introduced him—and viewers—to the Five-Timer's Club. He hosted his first episode back in 1985.

John Goodman has hosted SNL 13 times in total. Goodman has also made a dozen cameos over the years. He hosted his first episode back in 1989.

Steve Martin has hosted SNL 16 times in total. However, Martin has never appeared as a cast member. He hosted his first episode back in 1977.

Alec Baldwin has hosted SNL a whopping 17 times! He holds the title for hosting the most times ever. He hosted his first episode back in 1990. 

Grace Reiter has over X million followers on TikTok

Grace Reiter, Jane Wickline, and Veronika Slowikowska during the Dream Academy sketch in Saturday Night Live Season 52 Episode 1.

After making her SNL debut in the Season 52 premiere, Reiter passed 4 million followers on TikTok.

In a 2025 interview with Elite Daily, Reiter explained that sharing comedy videos on TikTok was a natural next step for her, since she'd long shared her work within her own circle.

"I had been making videos on Instagram for my friends, so I decided to post them on TikTok as well to see what it could do," she said. And she knows her Hunger Games parody had a big impact on her already-growing online presence.

"That got me maybe a million likes and then a million followers," Reiter told the outlet. "When it was happening, I was like, 'Yeah, kind of about damn time.'"

Grace Reiter's movie and film roles include The Chair Company

In early 2022, Reiter debuted in the TikTok live sketch comedy show Stapleview as one of its original cast members. That's how she wound up working with Wickline.

One year later, Reiter joined the Syracuse, New York-area sketch comedy group American High.

Reiter also played Tara in six episodes of comedy series The Chair Company starring SNL alum Tim Robinson.

The freshman SNL cast member also stars, writes, and directs in the upcoming workplace comedy show Minimum Wage, set for a streaming release in October 2026 after first premiering as a web series in 2024.
