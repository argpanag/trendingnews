W.A.S.P. Take Their First Four Albums Back on the Road With 1984 To Headless - That Eric Alper

TheTools · 05.09.2026 18:00 · source · permalink
  • Music

W.A.S.P. are turning their first four albums into one giant theatrical metal show with the 1984 To Headless Tour, a career-spanning production built around the records that established the band as one of the loudest, most provocative forces of 1980s heavy metal. The North American leg begins September 10 in El Cajon, California, before running through Halloween, with another European stretch waiting in December.

The concept reaches from W.A.S.P.’s self-titled 1984 debut through ‘The Headless Children’, concentrating an entire show around the band’s first four albums. That period also encompasses ‘The Last Command’ and ‘Inside the Electric Circus’, giving W.A.S.P. a deep catalogue of early material to pull from as the tour revisits the years that defined their original sound and visual identity.

Blackie Lawless has made clear that the production goes far beyond assembling songs from those records into a setlist.

“It’s hard to express the genuine excitement I’m feeling in describing the ‘1984 To Headless’ U.S. Tour,” Lawless said when outlining the production. “Playing those songs from those 1st Four Albums in a combined package like this is something we’ve never attempted before.”

The albums themselves are also becoming part of the scenery. Lawless said the stage show was designed to make elements from the record covers appear to come alive in front of the audience, drawing on the bombastic presentation and theatricality that have been central to W.A.S.P.’s live reputation.

That makes 1984 To Headless an unusually focused retrospective. Instead of surveying the band’s entire career evenly, W.A.S.P. are drilling into the run that introduced their mix of heavy riffs, spectacle and confrontation, then building a modern production around those records. For longtime fans, it creates an opportunity to hear songs from that period within a show specifically designed around their original imagery. For anyone who discovered the band later, it amounts to a live trip through the foundations of W.A.S.P.

The remaining schedule is extensive. The North American run starts with a cluster of California dates before heading through Arizona, New Mexico and Texas, then moves east through Florida and the Northeast. October takes the tour into Canada for shows in Montreal, Ottawa and Pickering before a swing through the Midwest and another Canadian stretch covering Winnipeg, Edmonton, Calgary and two nights in Coquitlam. The final week returns W.A.S.P. to the U.S. West Coast, finishing October 31 at The Grove in Anaheim.

One of those dates has already hit a milestone: the October 23 show at Great Canadian Casino Vancouver in Coquitlam is sold out, with a second performance scheduled there the following night.

Then the production heads back to Europe in December. Festival appearances in Germany are mixed with headline dates in Belgrade, Bucharest and Sofia, extending the 1984 To Headless concept through the end of the year. Earlier European festival appearances in June and July have already taken place and are omitted from the upcoming schedule below.

Four albums. More than four decades of history. And a stage designed to make the artwork surrounding those records part of the performance itself.
