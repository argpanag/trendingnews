The confirmed Newcastle United team v Fulham has now been announced.

Saturday afternoon seeing the thirteenth match of the season for NUFC.

A record so far of five wins, two draws and five defeats.

A 3pm kick-off at Craven Cottage.

Matthias Jaissle and his players looking to bounce back from the Carabao Cup and Premier League defeats to Everton.

Newcastle United team v Fulham:

Hornicek, Dedic, Thiaw, Burn, Hall, Gonzalez, Steur, Barnes, Wissa, Murphy, Osula

So it is four changes from the team that started in Monday night’s defeat to Everton.

IN:

Thiaw, Steur, Wissa, Murphy

OUT:

Joelinton, Schar, Willock, Elanga

Subs:

Pope, Livramento, Botman, Schar, Toure, Elanga, Fernandez-Pardo, Lewis Miley, Willock

Great to see Tino Livramento, Lewis Miley and Matias Fernandez-Pardo now able to return to the matchday squad after injury and illness.
