Where to watch LA Lakers vs Portland Trail Blazers? Schedule for tonight's game and TV Channel

Undefeated Lakers host the surprising Trail Blazers at Crypto.com Arena

The start of the NBA season has left the West with two undefeated teams that face off this Tuesday night at Crypto.com Arena. The Los Angeles Lakers, with a perfect 3-0 record, open the doors of their home to the Portland Trail Blazers who, with just two games played, also boast a spotless 2-0 mark. Two unbeaten teams, only one streak will survive the night.

How are Los Angeles and Portland coming into this Tuesday's game?

The Lakers are the hottest team in the city. They are coming off a trio of convincing victories: 127-105 over the Golden State Warriors at home, 125-117 against the Los Angeles Clippers in the local derby, and a 132-123 road win over the Utah Jazz.

Three games, 384 points scored and a +39 differential that places them first in the Pacific Division and fourth in the Western Conference, just half a game behind the group of undefeated teams that lead the overall standings.

Portland, for its part, has been one of the pleasant surprises at the start. The Blazers crushed the Phoenix Suns by a commanding 124-83 in their home debut and then defeated the Sacramento Kings 128-120 on the road.

With only two games played, they boast the best point differential in the entire league: 49, the result of 252 scored and just 203 allowed. They are second in the Northwest Division and sixth in the West, with the caveat that they have played two fewer games than many of their rivals.

The star showdown at Crypto.com Arena

The biggest appeal is in the backcourts. The Lakers revolve around Luka Dončić, the 27-year-old Slovenian who leads the Los Angeles offense, accompanied by Austin Reaves at shooting guard.

Also, Los Angeles has Walker Kessler and the experience of Kevon Looney, while pieces like Collin Sexton, Quentin Grimes, and Australian defender Matisse Thybulle give the roster depth and versatility.

Across from them, Portland features a backcourt of the very highest pedigree. Ja Morant and veteran Damian Lillard form an explosive duo in point production, with Jrue Holiday, also 36, providing savvy and perimeter defense.

Canadian Shaedon Sharpe and young Scoot Henderson bring athleticism and upside. Inside, the Blazers are betting on youth with Donovan Clingan and Chinese center Yang Hansen, along with Israeli Deni Avdija and Belgian Toumani Camara on the wings, giving Portland one of the most international rosters in the league.

What’s at stake in the overall standings

Although we are in the very earliest weeks of the season, every victory carries weight in a tight Western Conference. The Lakers want to solidify their lead in the Pacific Division, where they are comfortably in command: their pursuers, the Clippers, Suns, and Warriors, carry 1-2 records or worse. A fourth victory would put them squarely in the conversation for the top spots in the conference.

For Portland, the game is a test of authenticity. Blowing out Phoenix and beating Sacramento is a great start, but facing an undefeated team with elite stars on the road will say much more about the real ceiling of these Blazers. Staying unbeaten against the Lakers would be a statement of intent to the entire league.

Where to watch the Lakers vs Trail Blazers and what time does it start?

The matchup will be played this Tuesday, October 27, 2026 at 11:00 p.m. Eastern Time (10:00 p.m. Central Time and 8:00 p.m. Pacific Time), at the Los Angeles venue with a capacity of 20,000 fans.

The matchup will have national broadcast coverage on NBC, with streaming available on Peacock. In the local Los Angeles market, the game can be watched on Spectrum Sports Net and its streaming platform Spectrum Sports Net+.

What is the prediction for the LA Lakers vs Portland Trail Blazers game?

The Lakers come in with more game time under their belts, home-court advantage, and a flawless recent record against rivals from their conference. Portland, however, has shown that it is not just here for the ride: its defense has been the best in the league in its two road games, and its point differential is the highest among the 30 franchises.

Fans can expect a back-and-forth battle, with lots of points and the outcome decided in the fourth quarter. The support of their fans and their offensive firepower tip the scales slightly toward Los Angeles, but don’t be surprised if one of these two unbeaten teams leaves Crypto.com Arena with its perfect record barely blemished.
