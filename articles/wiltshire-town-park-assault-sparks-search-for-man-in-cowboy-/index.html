Wiltshire town park assault sparks search for man in cowboy hat

TheTools · 03.09.2026 14:47 · source · permalink

An assault in a Wiltshire town park has sparked a search for a man in a cowboy hat.

The incident took place at around 6.20pm on August 18, when a man assaulted another man on The Green in Devizes.

Wiltshire Police have now released an image of a man they would like to speak to in connection.

In the image, the man appears to be wearing a cowboy hat, a dark T-shirt, dark tracksuit bottoms, and dark trainers.

The force has urged anyone with information to get in touch via 101.

Police want to speak to this man in connection with the assault on August 18. (Image: Wiltshire Police)
