Crooked Stick’s 2028 Senior Open ‘is going to be magnificent,’ says USGA’s John Bodenhamer

TheTools · 15.10.2026 21:13 · source · permalink

CARMEL, Ind. (WRTV) — Crooked Stick Golf Club will host the U.S. Senior Open Championship in 2028, marking the event’s return to Carmel for the first time since 2009. It will be the seventh USGA championship held at the club.

USGA Chief Championships Officer John Bodenhamer spoke with Sports Director Anthony Calhoun in Carmel about why the course is a fit for the championship, which is two years away.

“Crooked Stick is a prestigious place,” Bodenhamer said. “We like to think at the USGA that we take all of our championships, especially the Senior Open, to America’s greatest venues. Crooked Stick is certainly one of those. This is a Pete Dye masterpiece, and to see it now, I think the players are just going to love the U.S. Senior Open in two years. It’s going to be magnificent.”

Bodenhamer said the course’s architecture, membership and history of hosting major events will appeal to players and fans. He cited John Daly’s victory at the 1991 PGA Championship at Crooked Stick and said winning a Senior Open at the club would carry special meaning for players.

“When they can say, when they’re asked, ‘Where did you win?’ and you can say a place like Crooked Stick, and all those great champions that have come through here, it just means more,” he said.

Bodenhamer said he expects the Indiana community to embrace the championship again. More than 150,000 people attended during the week of the 2009 event, Bodenhamer said.

“We look forward to that again,” he said. “That excitement makes the championship better for the players, for everybody. This great club embraces the game.”

Bodenhamer said Crooked Stick will test players with its variety of angles and strategic choices, while still offering a fair challenge.

“That’s our DNA, and I think Crooked Stick allows us to do that in a tough but fair way,” Bodenhamer said. “Crooked Stick is the type of venue, and Mr. Dye was brilliant with this: He gave you a lot of variety, a lot of different angles, a lot of strategy. The great players rise to the top when you put that in front of them.”

He said the course’s challenges can make the championship more exciting for fans and give players a greater sense of accomplishment.

“For us at the USGA, we’ve got a longstanding relationship with this great club, and we look forward to continuing it,” Bodenhamer said.

The 48th U.S. Senior Open will be held at Crooked Stick from June 29 to July 2, 2028.
