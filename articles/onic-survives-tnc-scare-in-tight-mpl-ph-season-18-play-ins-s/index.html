Onic survives TNC scare in tight MPL PH Season 18 play-ins showdown

TheTools · 22.10.2026 09:23 · source · permalink

Onic Philippines survived a major scare from TNC Pro Team, escaping with a nail-biting 3-2 victory in the MPL Philippines Season 18 play-ins on Wednesday at the Philsports Arena.

By fending off TNC, Onic advanced to the upper bracket semifinals against Team Falcons, keeping its hopes of returning to the championship round after missing out in the last two seasons.

Onic also extended its streak to four consecutive Top 4 finishes in the MPL Philippines.

It was a seesaw battle for either team with TNC, which finished sixth after the regular season, turning to Jomari "Jowm" Pingol's (Obsidia) double kill that allowed the Phoenix Army to draw first blood against third-seeded Onic.

However, Onic quickly hit back to even the series as Cyric "K1NGKONG" Perez took charge of the jungle with his Aulus to lead the response.

Borris "Brusko" Parro sparked a crucial play at the 13:39 mark on a set-up from his Atlas, taking down three TNC heroes to pave the way for Onic's siege and put the squad on match point, 2-1.

TNC Pro Team refused to go down without a fight, securing an Atlas pick in Game 4 that the Phoenix Army used to catch three Onic heroes late in the game and force a deciding fifth game.

Onic raced to a 2-0 lead in the winner-take-all clash, but TNC kept pace with the powerhouse squad and dragged the game beyond the 20-minute mark. The turning point came when Cyric "K1NGKONG" Perez's Hirara secured the third Lord uncontested after Onic zoned out Justine "Zaida" Palma's Aulus.

With TNC left undermanned, Onic seized the opportunity to storm the base and seal the hard-fought series win.

—JKC, GMA News
