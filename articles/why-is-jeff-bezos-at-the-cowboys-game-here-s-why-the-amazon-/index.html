Why is Jeff Bezos at the Cowboys game? Here’s why the Amazon founder is in Dallas

TheTools · 20.09.2026 21:31 · source · permalink

Amazon founder Jeff Bezos joined Jerry Jones on the Cowboys sideline before Sunday’s home opener against Washington.

Jeff Bezos was at AT&T Stadium on Sunday, but the Amazon founder wasn’t there to deliver packages. Bezos made a surprise appearance at the Dallas Cowboys’ home opener against the Washington Commanders, joining Cowboys owner and general manager Jerry Jones on the field before kickoff.

The billionaire businessman and his wife, Lauren Sánchez Bezos, posed for photos with members of the Jones family before the NFC East matchup.

Why is Jeff Bezos at the Cowboys game?

Jones told 105.3 The Fan before the game that he had “pulled out all the stops” to bring Bezos to AT&T Stadium as his guest for Dallas’ Week 2 matchup with Washington. The Cowboys were opening the season at home after losing 28-20 to the New York Giants in Week 1, and Jones wanted to create some additional energy around the occasion. Bezos said he was happy to accept the invitation.

“One, I really want the Cowboys to win today. Second is field energy here. It’s such great energy here,” Bezos told 105.3 The Fan. “There’s something special about this stadium... about this team, and of course, something special about Jerry. It’s great to be his guest.”

Bezos spent several minutes with Jones before kickoff as the Cowboys prepared for their first home game of the 2026 season.

Jeff Bezos also talked about Cowboys’ Brazil game

Bezos was also asked about another part of the Cowboys’ 2026 schedule - their upcoming trip to Brazil. Dallas is scheduled to face the Baltimore Ravens in Week 3 at Maracanã Stadium in Rio de Janeiro, continuing the NFL’s international expansion.

“I think it’s a great opportunity for the league to expose more people to American football,” Bezos said. “That kind of exposure is a great deal.”

The Cowboys will travel to Brazil next weekend before returning to the United States for their Week 4 matchup against the Houston Texans.
