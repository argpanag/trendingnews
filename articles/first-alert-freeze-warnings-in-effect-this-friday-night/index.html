FIRST ALERT: Freeze Warnings in effect this Friday night

TheTools · 16.10.2026 19:37 · source · permalink

THE BIG CHANGE: As low-pressure works its way out of NE WI, winds have kicked up from the north at 20 to 30 mph alongside some lingering showers. Rain should wrap up area-wide by 4:30pm with skies quickly clearing thereafter. Blustery conditions will remain through sunset, then winds will weaken overnight as high-pressure builds in from the Great Plains.

With decreasing winds and clear skies overnight, radiational cooling will take effect, allowing our daytime heating to easily escape back into the atmosphere. This will bring freezing temperatures to much of NE WI and the Fox Valley. With that said, the National Weather Service has issued a Freeze Warning for many spots to run from 1am through 9am Saturday. Door County is under a Frost Advisory from 3am through 9am Saturday. While northern Oconto and northern Marinette Counties are not under the Freeze Warning, given they already had one earlier in the season, temperatures will fall to the upper 20s in the north.

WHAT THIS MEANS:

Bring the outdoor plants inside to prevent frost or freeze damage before nightfall this Friday evening.

THIS WEEKEND: We’ll get to enjoy radiant sunshine with high-pressure nearby and just south of us. Conditions will be seasonable, in the mid 50s. Breezy conditions will arrive later on as the high runs south of us, pulling breezes from our southwest. This will lead into an overnight that is seasonably chilly with lows largely in the upper 30s, not freezing cold.

Sunday will showcase more of the same: Mostly sunny skies and breezy conditions on the backside of high-pressure. Temperatures will be milder with southwest winds, in the low and mid 60s. Aside from some breezy conditions around kickoff of the home game with the Packers vs the Cowboys, the forecast looks great! Expect a mostly clear sky with temperatures gradually sliding from the upper 50s to the low and mid 50s by the 4th quarter. A light jacket is all you may need.

NEXT WEEK: Dry weather continues through the middle of next week with highs running about 10° above average. There is the chance we see a few showers roll into our neck of the woods by Wednesday night, but any will be scattered and should be out of the way by daybreak on Thursday.
