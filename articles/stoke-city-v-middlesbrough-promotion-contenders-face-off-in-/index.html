Stoke City v Middlesbrough: promotion contenders face off in top-six clash

Stoke City host Middlesbrough in the Championship on Tuesday, 13 October 2026 in Stoke-on-Trent, with second visiting fourth in a meeting that could shape the early promotion picture.

Middlesbrough sit second on 18 points, with Stoke City fourth on 16. The visitors have edged this fixture more often across recent meetings and won 2-1 here in January 2026.

OneFootball Videos

Stoke City arrive in form after four wins in their last five league games. Boro are unbeaten in four in the league and beat Wolverhampton Wanderers last time out.

Team news could matter. For Stoke City, Aaron Cresswell, Sam Gallagher, Svante Ingelsson and Ato Ampah are out, and Maksym Talovierov is suspended. Boro list Alfie Jones, David Strelec and Rokas Pukštas as sidelined.

On the pitch, Boro's control game stands out: 58% possession and 87.34% passing accuracy, plus 165 open-play crosses, point to steady supply for leading scorer Will Lankshear, who has seven league goals. Stoke City average 45% of the ball and have scored five times from outside the box. Ethan Galbraith has three goals and two assists, with Eric Junior Bocat adding two assists. If the game opens up, Stoke City's threat from range could test Boro's back line.

A home win would lift Stoke City to 19 points and could move them into the top two. Victory for Boro would take them to 21 points, strengthening their promotion position and potentially claiming top spot, while a defeat may drag them back towards the play-off pack.
