David Corenswet Done As Superman as Skydance Braces for $100M Man of Tomorrow Loss (Exclusive) - Cosmic Book News

TheTools · 10.11.2026 17:28 · source · permalink

David Corenswet is out as Superman.

Our Skydance Paramount insiders tell us Corenswet has finished his DC contract, the same situation as Milly Alcock, who fulfilled her deal and has already moved on from Supergirl.

“David’s contract ends with MOT,” the insider says.

MOT is Man of Tomorrow, the Superman sequel opening July 9, 2027. And inside the studio, we’re told, nobody is counting on it to make money. The question is how much it loses.

“ParaBros already know MOT will lose money, they’re just hoping [it’s] $100M or less.”

ParaBros is the insider’s running name for the Paramount Skydance brass.

‘He’s The Face Of Gunn’s Failure’

A new Corenswet deal is not impossible. It just comes with a condition our insiders don’t see being met.

“They might renew it but not if MOT is a bust.”

Back on October 2, the same sources told us Corenswet keeps Superman if Man of Tomorrow makes money. Now we’re told the studio already knows it won’t.

The insider does feel for the actor wearing the cape.

“But seriously I feel bad for David. He’s the face of Gunn’s failure. His agents better stop any talk of new contract asap.”

When James Gunn was fired over Man of Tomorrow on Monday, the same insiders said “people already knew,” pointing at Alcock deleting her posts and “Corenswet and Hoult auditions.” Nicholas Hoult just screen tested for James Bond.

‘MOT Is A Total Bust And Ellison Had To Pivot’

Why fire Gunn now instead of letting his contract run out in April? Our insider’s answer:

“I’m convinced MOT is a total bust and Ellison had to pivot faster than expected. They simply can’t wait anymore.”

The pivot is already visible. Gunn and Peter Safran are out, and the new regime now oversees the movie’s final cut.

It is also a timing problem, because the cupboard is bare.

“As it stands the new guy’s movies won’t be ready until mid 2028 at the earliest.”

The new guy is Jeremy Latcham, who takes over DC Studios on December 1. Until his slate exists, DC’s calendar holds two movies: Man of Tomorrow and Matt Reeves’ The Batman Part II on February 18, 2028.

How did it get this bad? The insider points at the fan revolt.

“People boycotting diaperman caused real damage and totally destroyed Supergirl.”

Diaperman is the insider’s nickname for Gunn’s Superman. Supergirl finished as the lowest-grossing DC movie ever at $126.4 million worldwide.

What Puck And TheWrap Are Reporting

The reports landing this week line up.

Puck’s Matthew Belloni says Skydance is already dismantling Gunn’s DCU slate, with DC headed back into “reboot mode.”

TheWrap reports David Ellison is mandating Justice League movies ASAP, with a producer saying audiences did not want the “cornball” approach. Corenswet also happens to have the nickname “David Corn Sweat.”

Shown the Puck report, our insider laughed it off.

“Lol, we’ve known that for months! Told you David and Milly’s contracts are fulfilled.”

On October 21, these sources told us Alcock was done as Supergirl with her three-movie deal fulfilled. Today, Corenswet joins her.

So where does Superman go from here? Our sources say that is up to one man: Henry Cavill.

And behind that choice sits the rest of the rebuild: the Zack Snyder talks that keep happening, a Batman multiverse, and more Matt Reeves.
