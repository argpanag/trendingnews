The French forward unleashes another powerful left-footed effort across goal toward the far side, but AS Roma goalkeeper Pierluigi Gollini stays alert and makes another important save.

Score: 2H: AS Roma 3-1 Real Madrid


63' ENDRICK GOES DOWN — PENALTY APPEAL!

Endrick combines with Yan Diomandé and drives into the penalty area, but goes to ground and appeals for a penalty. However, the referee waves play on, as there is no contact from goalkeeper Pierluigi Gollini.

61' KYLIAN MBAPPÉ'S SHOT SAVED BY PIERLUIGI GOLLINI!

The French forward fires a shot across goal, but AS Roma goalkeeper Pierluigi Gollini is alert and makes the save to deny Real Madrid another goal.

58' JOSÉ MOURINHO TAKES OFF VINÍCIUS JR.

Real Madrid manager José Mourinho substitutes Vinícius Jr., bringing on Endrick in his place. He also introduces Arda Güler, while Denzel Dumfries comes off.

Score: 2H: AS Roma 3-1 Real Madrid


57' GOAL AS ROMA! DONYELL MALEN SCORES HIS SECOND!

The Dutch striker is becoming the star of the match after receiving a through ball from Rodrigo Mora near midfield. Malen races toward Thibaut Courtois and beats the Real Madrid goalkeeper with a clinical low shot across goal in a one-on-one situation.

The goal takes time to be confirmed as VAR checks for a possible foul on Jude Bellingham and a potential offside. After the review, the goal stands!

Score: 2H: AS Roma 3-1 Real Madrid


50' GOAL AS ROMA! DONYELL MALEN SCORES! VINÍCIUS JR. UNDER FIRE!

The Dutch striker puts AS Roma back in front after beating Federico Valverde before slotting the ball past Thibaut Courtois. However, Vinícius Jr. is at the center of the blame after attempting a backheel pass in midfield, losing possession and triggering the attacking move that led to the goal.

Score: 2H: AS Roma 2-1 Real Madrid


THE SECOND HALF IS UNDERWAY!

In a surprising move, Argentine star Paulo Dybala comes off for Rodrigo Mora. Could Dybala be dealing with an injury? Real Madrid stick with the same lineup as both teams push for the winning goal.

Score: 2H: AS Roma 1-1 Real Madrid


HALF-TIME!

Following a long-range effort from Jude Bellingham of Real Madrid, both teams head into the break level. Los Blancos continue to push forward with urgency, but AS Roma refuse to back down, inspired by a determined Paulo Dybala.

Score: HT: AS Roma 1-1 Real Madrid


31' Marc Cucurella Wastes a Golden Chance for Real Madrid!

Real Madrid's Marc Cucurella misses a great opportunity to put Los Blancos ahead. The Spanish World Cup winner receives the ball down the left flank after Matías Soulé fails to mark him properly and drives into the penalty area, but his shot goes narrowly wide of the goal.

Score: 1st Half: AS Roma 1-1 Real Madrid


25' GOAL! KYLIAN MBAPPÉ EQUALIZES FOR REAL MADRID!

Real Madrid level the score through Kylian Mbappé! AS Roma defender Mario Hermoso fails to control the ball, allowing the French superstar to pounce and fire a shot from the edge of the penalty area. The effort takes a slight deflection but still beats goalkeeper Pierluigi Gollini.

The goal comes in a special milestone for Mbappé, who scores in his 100th UEFA Champions League appearance to bring Los Blancos back into the game.

Score: 1st Half: AS Roma 1-1 Real Madrid


23' Real Madrid Survive a Scare as Thibaut Courtois Makes a Goal-Line Save

Thibaut Courtois comes to Real Madrid's rescue with a crucial goal-line save after Donyell Malen fires a close-range shot from the right side of the penalty area following a pass from Paulo Dybala.

The ball slips between the legs of the Belgian goalkeeper, but Courtois manages to keep it out just in time, preventing AS Roma from doubling their lead and making it 2-0.

Score: 1st Half: AS Roma 1-0 Real Madrid


20' GOAL! MARTEN DE ROON PUTS AS ROMA AHEAD!

AS Roma take the lead with a powerful right-footed strike from Marten de Roon just outside the penalty area, near the edge of the D. The Dutch midfielder catches Thibaut Courtois off guard, with the ball slipping past the Real Madrid goalkeeper in a costly mistake.

Real Madrid pay the price for defensive errors by Ibrahima Konaté and Denzel Dumfries, while Paulo Dybala sparks the move by winning possession and distributing the ball to set up the attack.

Score: 1st Half: AS Roma 1-0 Real Madrid


16' AS Roma's Manu Koné Leaves the Pitch in Tears After Injury

AS Roma midfielder Manu Koné is forced off with an injury, leaving the pitch in tears. The Frenchman is replaced by Niccolò Pisilli in a major setback for the Italian side.

Koné appears to suffer a muscle injury while trying to recover the ball after Matías Soulé loses possession. He overexerts himself in the challenge and is unable to continue.

4' AS Roma Threaten, but Thibaut Courtois Makes the Save

Real Madrid goalkeeper Thibaut Courtois comes up big to deny AS Roma the opening goal after a shot from Spanish defender Mario Hermoso. A foul by Vinícius Júnior is also claimed.

THE STARTING LINEUPS ARE IN!






PREVIEW


When and where is AS Roma vs Real Madrid?

AS Roma host Real Madrid on Wednesday, October 14 at 3:00 PM ET at the Stadio Olimpico in Rome, with French referee François Letexier in charge.


Roma come into the game full of confidence, still unbeaten in Serie A and looking for their first victory of this Champions League campaign in front of their home crowd. Madrid, meanwhile, opened with a win but are still trying to find greater consistency.

There is also an emotional subplot: José Mourinho returns to Rome, a city where he left a lasting mark, this time trying to prevent his Real Madrid side from being dragged into a difficult night against one of Italy’s most in-form teams.

UEFA Champions League

How do AS Roma arrive?

Gian Piero Gasperini’s side are enjoying an excellent start to the season. After six Serie A matches, Roma remain unbeaten with four wins and two draws, leaving them third in the table.

Their latest test ended in a goalless draw away to Como. Roma played much of the second half with ten men following Donyell Malen’s dismissal, but held firm and once again showed the resilience that has defined their strong start.

Their European campaign began differently, with a 1-1 draw against Fenerbahçe in Istanbul. Back at home and facing one of the tournament’s biggest names, the Giallorossi will now look to make a much bigger statement.


How do Real Madrid arrive?

Real Madrid travel to the Italian capital after beating Villarreal 1-0, although the result did not completely erase the doubts surrounding their performances. José Mourinho’s side have shown flashes of defensive solidity, but still struggle to control games for long stretches.

Los Blancos sit third in LaLiga, six points behind the leaders, following an uneven start to the campaign. Their biggest concern has been chance creation: Madrid have plenty of attacking talent, but have not always managed to bring those players into games often enough.

The Champions League, however, started on a much stronger note. Real Madrid beat Inter Milan 2-1 at the Santiago Bernabéu, with goals from Kylian Mbappé and Federico Valverde, and will now look to make it two wins from two.


Players to watch

Donyell Malen

The Dutch forward has been one of the driving forces behind Roma’s impressive start, scoring six goals in six Serie A matches. His pace and ability to attack space could cause serious problems for a Real Madrid defense that will need to stay alert whenever Roma win the ball back.


Kylian Mbappé

Kylian Mbappé started the season strongly and was already decisive against Inter, even if his form has cooled slightly in recent weeks. He remains Madrid’s biggest game-changing threat, particularly if Roma leave space behind their wing-backs.

The keys to AS Roma vs Real Madrid

Roma’s pressing could shape much of the game. Gasperini will want his team to push Madrid backward, close passing lanes through midfield and force Mourinho’s side into playing faster and more directly than they would prefer.

That aggression could also create Madrid’s biggest opportunity. If they break through the first line of pressure, Mbappé could find plenty of room behind Roma’s wing-backs, an area that could become decisive if the visitors move the ball quickly.

Patience will matter as well. Roma are at home, full of confidence and capable of turning the Olimpico into a difficult environment if they score first. Madrid will need to manage the tempo and avoid allowing the game to become the physical, scrappy contest that could suit the hosts.

UEFA Champions League

Final outlook

Real Madrid enter as the betting favorites, but Roma’s current form makes this a far more dangerous matchup than the odds might suggest. The Giallorossi are unbeaten, playing at home and have one of the most productive attackers of the early season.

Madrid have greater experience in nights like this and enough individual quality to decide the game out of nowhere, but their inconsistency leaves the door open to a much tighter contest.

If Roma can impose their intensity from the opening whistle, Mourinho could be in for a very uncomfortable return to the Stadio Olimpico.
