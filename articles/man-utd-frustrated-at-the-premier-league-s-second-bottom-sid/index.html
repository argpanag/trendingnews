Man Utd frustrated at the Premier League's second-bottom side!

What is happening at Manchester United?

A terrible week for the Red Devils. After the 11-v-10 defeat to Manchester City and the loss after leading 2-0 against Brighton in the League Cup, the Mancunians have dropped points once again.

OneFootball Videos

Away at Fulham, Manchester United were put under pressure from the outset by the Cottagers, but they also managed to look dangerous in the first half.

However, everything fell apart in the second half after a comical own goal by Lisandro Martinez in the 63rd minute.

For a long time, it looked like the Red Devils were heading for another embarrassing defeat, but a deflected strike from Matheus Cunha allowed them to equalise in the 89th minute.

As a result, Manchester United were held to a 1-1 draw, failing to make any progress and remaining 12th in the Premier League. Fulham, for their part, stay second from bottom, ahead of Tottenham on goal difference.
