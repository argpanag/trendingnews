Lakers' Jake LaRavia: Starting Monday

TheTools · 24.11.2026 05:20 · source · permalink

LaRavia will start Monday against the Jazz, Daniel Starkand of LakersNation.com reports.

LaRavia will replace Ziare Williams in the first unit for Monday's game. As a starter this season, LaRavia is averaging 6.2 points, 4.5 rebounds, 2.5 assists and 1.0 triples per contest.
