Supermodel Candice Swanepoel is a goddess in envy-inducing new bikini photos

TheTools · 17.10.2026 19:00 · source · permalink

Former Victoria's Secret Angel Candice Swanepoel is her own best advertisement in her latest modelling photos

Candice Swanepoel just proved why she's one of the most successful supermodels in history. The former Victoria's Secret Angel took to Instagram to share some incredible modelling photos – and she's never looked better.

The 37-year-old beauty looked incredible as she posed for a poolside shoot, wearing her very own Tropic of C bikini.

The model-turned swimwear entrepreneur, who founded her business in 2018, was seen stretched out on her stomach by the pool, running her hand through her long golden hair.

Rocking a beige bikini with boho glass bead detailing on the straps, Candice showcased her sun-kissed tan to perfection as she modelled her brand's latest drop. The verdict from her fans was unanimous: "She is a goddess."

The statuesque model developed her eco-friendly swimwear brand while pregnant with her second child, putting the focus on sustainable fabrics and female empowerment.

Hanging up her Victoria's Secret wings

While she might have hung up her wings five years ago, Candace has returned to the Victoria's Secret runway since, walking in their rebooted shows in both 2024 and 2025 alongside her fellow OG Angels.

Facing declining TV ratings and public criticism over lack of inclusivity, Victoria's Secret underwent a complete brand overhaul in 2021. They dissolved the 'Angels' contract – which Candice had been under since 2010 – and replaced it with the VS Collective.

The star has frequently emphasized how important the brand was to her global success. "I wouldn't be anywhere without Victoria's Secret," she told News24.

"Being one of the Angels is one of the few jobs that can totally change someone's life. For example, clients and designers who would have never given you a second look now sit up and take notice."

Candice's newfound focus on family life

While she continues to model actively, Candice has scaled back the jobs she takes in order to focus on parenting her two young children, Anacan and Ariel.

The South African model and her husband Hermann Nicoli welcomed their firstborn, Anacan, in October 2016, followed by their second son, Ariel, in June 2018.

"What a gift and precious responsibility it is to bring a new soul into the world. To love and teach to love just the same. My life definitely started over on the day I met my baby," she previously penned in a sweet Instagram caption marking Mother’s Day.

Her eldest, now 10, is already following in his famous mother’s footsteps: Anacan appeared in a 2017 Gap campaign alongside Candice in support of Every Mother Counts.

The supermodel said it has been incredible to see the love blossoming between her two children since Ariel, seven, arrived on the scene. 

"To see the love between them is amazing. It’s a lot, I’m not gonna lie. The second one is a lot easier, but the situation of having two makes it harder," she previously told People. "My second one is an angel. 'He's like, 'Mommy, I know you don’t need more stress.'"
