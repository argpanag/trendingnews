  12. Laimer doubles Bayern's leadpublished at 21:36 GMT
    21:36 GMT

    Atletico 0-2 Bayern Munich

    Bayern Munich double their lead!

    Moments after Atletico Madrid goalkeeper Jan Oblak makes a fingertip save to push Dayot Upamecano's deflected shot onto the bar, Konrad Laimer rifles in off the woodwork.

    He's picked out nicely by Harry Kane, takes a touch to steady himself and then hammers a shot in off the bar. That was about the only place he could put it to beat Oblak.

    Meanwhile, Kane has a little under 15 minutes plus stoppage time to score and equal Cristiano Ronaldo's record of scoring in 11 straight Champions League matches.
