What We Witnessed

TheTools · 08.09.2026 23:57 · source · permalink

On Sept. 10, 2025, Deseret News reporters Emma Pitts and Eva Terry were on the ground at Utah Valley University covering a Turning Point USA event when founder Charlie Kirk was fatally shot. One year later, they reflect on what they saw, the prayer they said while crouched under a desk and what the experience taught them about political violence, faith and the work of journalism. This is an excerpt from a longer roundtable that also includes reporter Brigham Tomco, who published an in-depth profile of Kirk weeks before his death.
