Comfortable passage for cup holders

Enzo Maresca is likely to use this EFL Cup third-round tie against Championship side Norwich to make major changes to Manchester City’s line-up, but I am still expecting a comfortable home win. 

The holders have real strength in depth and the likes of Geronimo Rulli, Vitor Reis, Rico Lewis, Mateo Kovacic, Ayyoub Bouaddi and Iliman Ndiaye should all be given starts. 

Such a side, supplemented by a few players who started in the 1-0 win at Manchester United on Sunday, should be capable of securing a place in the last 16.

After conceding in their first three games, starting with a 3-0 defeat by Arsenal in the Community Shield, Maresca’s side have now kept three consecutive clean sheets. 

That makes me confident  the hosts can overcome a handicap of -2.5 goals against a Norwich side who have been conceding with alarming regularity in the Championship. 

The Canaries lost 4-3 at Middlesbrough on Saturday and have yet to keep a clean sheet in nine games across all competitions. They have let in 13 goals in the Championship this season, with only bottom club Burnley conceding more. 

Odds of 10/11 for Manchester City with -2.5 goals in the Asian handicap market would seem the best way to back a home win.

Semenyo to get off the mark at last

I think Antoine Semenyo is a strong candidate to be among the Manchester City goalscorers against this porous Norwich defence.

Semenyo may be used as a central striker in this match, which would allow Maresca to rest Erling Haaland. 

The 26-year-old has yet to score this season but he has been in decent form, providing two assists in his four Premier League appearances. He scored 21 goals last season – 10 for Bournemouth and 11 following his move to the Etihad. 

Semenyo is overdue a goal and a price of 1/1 for him to score appeals. It will also be worth keeping an eye on whether Nico O’Reilly starts. He is 11/4 to score and has the versatility to be used in an attacking role.

Canary Cordoba always seeing yellow

It looks likely to be a difficult night for the Norwich defenders and one in particular stands out as likely to get himself in trouble with the referee. 

Jose Cordoba has picked up four yellow cards in his eight appearances for the Canaries this season. 

Three of those bookings have come in the Championship, while the other was issued in Norwich’s 4-1 win over MK Dons in the first round of this competition. 

The centre-back now looks set for his biggest test of the season and you can back Cordoba to receive another card at 5/2.
