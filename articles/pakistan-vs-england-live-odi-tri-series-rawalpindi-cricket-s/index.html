  2. 'Kept my plans simple'published at 16:59 BST
    16:59 BST

    Player of the match Arafat Minhas, who made 59 and took 6-40, speaking at the presentation: "I kept my plans simple. There was not much help for the spinners in the first ODI. The pitch was on the slower side today, which helped me.

    "I think variations are very important for a spinner as they help in deceiving the opposition batters. I have worked on my variations. We needed an important partnership when I walked in the middle.

    "Hasan [Nawaz] and I discussed that we need to bat deep. I took the charge after Hasan got out and I tried to bat until the end of the innings, which helped in taking the score beyond 300."

  3. 'Want to build this team'published at 16:56 BST
    16:56 BST

    Pakistan captain Shaheen Afridi: "Yes [I am happy with the batting performance]. It was really good, the way Hasan Nawaz and Arafat Minhas came in. It's a good sign for us but we need the top [order] to make a good score and fast.

    "As a team we played some good things but we need to improve more because we are preparing for the World Cup. We want to build this team and the way the fast bowlers bowled was a good sign for us. We used some bouncers and some thoughts."

    On Arafat Minhas' impact: "He's a very good talent. He's not just a bowler but a proper all-rounder. The way he started his innings with the bat and bowled is good for us, for Pakistan team and hopefully do it again in the future."

  4. 'We couldn't string the partnerships together'published at 16:54 BST
    16:54 BST

    England captain Harry Brook: "We were quite happy with 300. We thought they were slightly under par. It got dewy as we thought it would do. We were pretty happy at halfway."

    On not pushing on after a strong opening partnership: "That was the story of the innings. We thought [Tom] Banton, Ducky [Ben Duckett] set the tone to get a 100 partnership and then it was a case of losing too many wickets in clumps, we couldn't string the partnerships together and we ended up on the wrong side."

    On where they look to do better next time: "You always want to win, don't you? We've spoken a lot about rotation of strike through the middle but I think we were a little too careful against the spinners especially, so we can probably put them under a little bit more pressure in stages and we've got to tonight to talk about it and tomorrow and bounce back better on Thursday."
