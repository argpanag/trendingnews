Suspected intoxicated driver arrested after video shows car doing donuts in front of courthouse

TheTools · 08.10.2026 13:06 · source · permalink

A suspected intoxicated driver was arrested in Indiana on Monday after she was caught on video doing donuts in front of a courthouse.

See the video in the player above.

The incident happened in Vernon, Indiana, on Monday afternoon, according to the Jennings County Sheriff's Office.

"A 67-year-old woman was arrested for Operating a Motor Vehicle While Intoxicated, along with numerous other charges, following an incident at the courthouse," the sheriff's office said in a social media post.

The video shows the driver speeding onto the grass, putting the vehicle into reverse, and going in circles while kicking up dirt and grass.

The sheriff's office posted the video as a reminder for those drinking to make arrangements to get home if needed.

"If you've had a few too many margaritas with dinner, please don't get behind the wheel, and definitely don’t do donuts on the courthouse lawn," the sheriff's office said.
