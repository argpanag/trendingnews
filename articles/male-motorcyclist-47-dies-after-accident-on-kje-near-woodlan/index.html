Male motorcyclist, 47, dies after accident on KJE near Woodlands exit, involved trailer truck

TheTools · 27.10.2026 20:01 · source · permalink

The accident occurred on the morning of Oct. 27.

October 27, 2026, 04:49 PM

Photos via chrisengkh/TikTok

A 47-year-old male motorcyclist died following an Oct. 27 accident on the Kranji Expressway (KJE) near the Woodlands exit involving a car, a motorcycle, and a trailer truck.

Based on the video by user @chrisengkh on TikTok, there was no moving traffic on two lanes of a road, with the area cordoned off by traffic cones.

Accident scene

A motorcycle could be seen lying on its side under the trailer, with a white cloth and blue police tent nearby, according to a separate Facebook post.

There was a red car a short distance in front of the trailer, with an ambulance and a fire truck further in front.

Pronounced dead

The police told Mothership that they were alerted at about 7:20am on Oct. 27 to an accident involving a car, a motorcycle, and a trailer truck, along KJE towards the Bukit Timah Expressway (BKE).

Responding to Mothership's queries, the Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF) said SCDF was alerted at the same time to a road traffic accident along KJE towards BKE near the Woodlands exit.

An SCDF paramedic pronounced a 47-year-old male motorcyclist dead at the scene and police investigations are ongoing.
