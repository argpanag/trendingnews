Icon Judi Dench Accuses Petco of Buying Birds From Breeder Who Kept Them in ‘Cramped, Filthy Cages’

TheTools · 04.10.2026 16:39 · source · permalink

Celebrity News

Hollywood icon Judi Dench is speaking out against retail giant Petco over alleged mistreatment of birds.

The 91-year-old Oscar winner sprang into action after watching a PETA investigation depicting disturbing conditions at a “mass breeding factory in Petco’s supply chain.”

Page Six Hollywood reported Friday, October 2, that Dench did not mince words in a letter to Petco CEO Joel Anderson.

“The footage revealed that thousands of parakeets were confined to cramped, filthy cages with no opportunity to fly, explore, or engage in the natural behaviors that make them who they are,” Dench wrote in part, per the outlet. “Some birds suffered severe injuries, including a bird who had lost an eye and a chick whose wing had shriveled, blackened, and fallen off.”

Dench added that instead of providing veterinary care to sick and injured parakeets, “an owner and worker killed them by hand, including by crushing their necks, slamming them to the ground, or ripping off their heads.”

PETA managed to get cameras inside a parakeet breeder said to be in Petco’s supply chain, where staff were allegedly recorded mistreating birds.

“Anyone who has ever shared their home with a bird knows they are curious, emotional, intelligent individuals with their own personalities and preferences. They feel joy, fear, loneliness, and excitement just like dogs, cats, and humans,” Dench continued. “Please, take a hard look at what PETA uncovered and end the sale of birds in your stores to prevent future suffering for birds.”

In May, the U.S. Department of Agriculture said in an inspection report, posted online by PETA, that it found “unsanitary conditions” at the same facility and gave the supplier a deadline to address them.

In a statement to First for Women, Petco responded: “Creekside Birds is not and has never been a Petco vendor. At Petco, the health, safety and overall well-being of pets is always our top priority. Our companion animal care standards were developed under veterinary supervision, with external counsel from experts in animal care, behavior, ethics and welfare. They are continuously reviewed and always extend to our vendors.”
