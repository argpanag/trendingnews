Prime Video’s Long-Awaited Fantasy Epic Confirms the Start of a New Era Bigger Than Peak 'Game of Thrones'

TheTools · 30.10.2026 13:00 · source · permalink

Prime Video has released a number of epic fantasy series in the wake of Game of Thrones, from high-profile titles like The Lord of the Rings: The Rings of Power and The Wheel of Time to more niche hits like The Legend of Vox Machina. It has more exciting additions to the category coming down the pike, and one long-awaited adaptation marks the start of a new era for fantasy TV.

It could even surpass Game of Thrones's 2010s run in terms of hype, as there's already a massive audience for the source material. That would be Fourth Wing, Prime Video's upcoming take on Rebecca Yarros's Empyrean series. Fourth Wing and its sequels have exploded in popularity since the first book's 2023 debut. Given the enthusiasm surrounding Threshing Day's release this September, that shows no sign of slowing down.

Of course, Fourth Wing's success is part of a bigger trend that's dominated the 2020s: the "romantasy" boom. Thanks to the virality of series like A Court of Thorns and Roses and The Empyrean — and the rise of BookTok — fantasy books with a heavy helping of romance are increasingly common. Many are getting picked up for adaptations, too, but few have made visible progress. Fourth Wing could change that.

'Fourth Wing' Is the First Adaptation From the 2020s' Romantasy Boom to Make Real Progress

Fantasy romance is nothing new, and shows like Outlander have already brought this blend of genres to the screen. Its presence isn't as prominent as straightforward high fantasy, but it does exist on television. However, few of the books to emerge from the 2020s' romantasy surge have made it to the small screen. Even series like Shadow and Bone and My Lady Jane adapted novels from prior to this era.

This decade's viral romantasy titles are getting picked up, though. Adaptation announcements for books like Divine Rivals by Rebecca Ross, Quicksilver by Callie Hart, and Powerless by Lauren Roberts all offer hope that the current romantasy trend will eventually make the jump to TV. Yet while most appear to be stalling in development, Fourth Wing is among the first to make notable gains.

On This Day...

Prime Video's adaptation is gaining traction, with the casting of Esther McGregor and Emilio Sakraya marking a huge leap in the right direction. There's still plenty left to do before Fourth Wing graces our screens, but with casting and writing underway, it's already more promising than so many of the adaptations sitting in development.

It certainly has more movement than Hulu's axed A Court of Thorns and Roses series, which could have been the first viral romantasy project to get a screen counterpart. Unfortunately, it didn't go anywhere, so it looks like it's up to Fourth Wing to prove there's demand for more TV series like it. If it's as successful as the books, it could kickstart a new race to adapt fantasy, just like Game of Thrones.

Prime Video's 'Fourth Wing' Show Could Be the 'Game of Thrones' of Romantasy

Fourth Wing could follow in Game of Thrones's footsteps, becoming a fantasy show with massive mainstream appeal. The books already have that going for them, serving as a solid entry to the genre with an easy-to-follow yet gripping narrative. Their unique blend of romantic subplots and dragon storylines is a clear selling point. House of the Dragon is already proving there's demand for the latter.

If Fourth Wing grows a similarly large audience on-screen, there's a good chance Hollywood will seek to replicate its success. Instead of the "next Game of Thrones," we could start seeing "the next Fourth Wing" touted.

That would certainly push studios and streamers to prioritize any romantasy adaptations they have sitting in the hopper. It could also see new series being green-lit for TV. The interest on the publishing end certainly suggests there's money to be made from such stories. Now it will just take one successful adaptation to prove it.
