GTA 6 Fans Are Buying Handmade Statues on eBay - Resell Calendar

TheTools · 12.10.2026 00:21 · source · permalink

After the Collector's Bundle disappointed, fans are turning elsewhere

By RC Staff

07 October 2026

Key Points

  • Rockstar’s $400 collector’s box includes a vinyl Macca figure but no statue of Jason or Lucia

  • Handpainted statues of Lucia and the GTA 6 cover girl have sold for as much as $650

  • Fan-made 3D printed Macca the Gator figures have sold on eBay for between $25 and $100

Rockstar’s $400 collector’s box includes a vinyl Macca figure but no statue of Jason or Lucia

Handpainted statues of Lucia and the GTA 6 cover girl have sold for as much as $650

Fan-made 3D printed Macca the Gator figures have sold on eBay for between $25 and $100

When Rockstar finally pulled back the curtain on its GTA 6 collector’s box, fans were hoping for a centerpiece statue of Jason and Lucia. What they got was a cartoon alligator sipping a martini, along with a price tag that seemed downright disrespectful. A growing number of fans are choosing to give their money to independent creators rather than Rockstar, and what they’re buying says a lot about where demand lies.

Didn't GTA 6 Collector's Editions Include a Statue?

Rockstar opened preorders for the Goodtime State Vice City Collection on September 24, and the reaction was split from the jump. The box is built around Macca the Gator, a fictional TV character inside the game who has become the unofficial mascot of GTA 6’s marketing push.

The centerpiece is a 6 inch vinyl figure of Macca enjoying a smoke and a flamingo blood martini, surrounded by Oakley Frogskins, a New Era snapback, a shot glass, a poster map of Leonida, and a pile of pins and stickers.

As we noted in our collector’s edition breakdown, $400 is a steep ask for any game’s collector’s edition, and this one doesn’t even include the game. Plenty of fans said they’d happily pay for one or two pieces, the figure especially, but not the whole box.

The other complaint was about the figure itself. Collector’s editions from AAA studios usually anchor on a statue of the main character, and GTA 6 has two of them in Jason and Lucia. A gator with a stash compartment in its base has its charm, but it wasn’t what most fans pictured.

Handcrafted GTA 6 Statues Appear Online

In the weeks since the reveal, a handful of eBay sellers have started listing their own answers to both complaints. The cheapest entry point is a 3D printed Macca, which gives fans the box’s headline collectible without the rest of the party supplies attached. Those have sold for anywhere from $25 to $100.

Other sellers went further and built what Rockstar didn’t. Handcrafted, handpainted statues of Lucia and the game’s unnamed Vice City cover girl have started showing up, and some of these bespoke figures have sold for as much as $650. Sales volume is modest, but these aren’t listings sitting untouched.

Unauthorized statues of video game characters are nothing new, but those are usually scantily clad, sexy-fied versions of female protagonists that we’d describe as little more than masturbation idols. There’s some of that in these GTA 6 statues, but a lot of what we’re seeing looks closer to a traditional collector’s edition statue than a novelty item. In other words: the exact product fans have been asking Rockstar for and haven’t received.

Why GTA 6 Merch Keeps Selling

None of this demand is surprising if you’ve followed the GTA 6 marketing machine. Almost anything with Vice City printed on it gets eyeballs. Rockstar teamed up with Atlantic Records on GTA VI: The Album, which spun off a liquid-filled limited edition vinyl plus a spread of retailer-exclusive variants at Best Buy, Target, Walmart, and others. The Miami Heat will play as Vice City on November 18, one night before the game launches, in a one-night-only jersey the team still hasn’t revealed.

And Rockstar isn’t done yet. Dataminers spotted 25 unannounced SKUs on Rockstar’s store before the collector’s box went live, and as of today that bundle was the only one to appear publicly. With GTA 6 arriving November 19, there’s still about six weeks of runway for new merch.

Will Rockstar Drop Official GTA 6 Statues?

This is speculation, but it’s not a stretch. The demand for a Jason or Lucia figure is clearly illustrated on eBay and Etsy, Rockstar has two dozen products waiting in the wings, and fans have been loud about what the collector’s box was missing. An official statue line would be an easy win.

Rockstar has been dropping new GTA 6 merch on Thursdays for weeks now, usually between 7 and 8 AM PT. Nearly everything Rockstar has sold for GTA 6 so far has been made in large numbers with no hard cap, which keeps flips modest. Refund policies have also varied wildly between drops: the vinyl allows returns within 30 days of delivery, while US buyers can’t return the collector’s box once it ships. Read the fine print on anything new before you buy.

To close out, we’ll repeat something obvious that we’ve been saying for months: GTA 6 is one of the biggest opportunities for resellers that we’ve seen in years. Demand for this game is impossible to overestimate, and that applies to the host of physical merch Rockstar is rolling out.

But we still haven’t seen anything truly limited that resellers can feast on. All of Rockstar’s previous releases have been produced at scale to cash in on overwhelming demand. We still think there will be one or two truly limited releases in the coming weeks, and those could sell for hundreds if not thousands of dollars come launch day.

In short, keep your eyes open, and be ready for new drops on Thursday mornings (like tomorrow).
