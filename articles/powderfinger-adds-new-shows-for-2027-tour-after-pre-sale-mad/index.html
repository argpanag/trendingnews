Powderfinger adds new shows for 2027 tour after pre-sale madness

TheTools · 14.10.2026 05:35 · source · permalink
  • N

    By Ned Hammond

  • Topic:Music

By Ned Hammond

Topic:Music

Powderfinger is heading back out on the road in 2027, playing 24 shows across Brisbane, Melbourne, Adelaide, Perth, Sydney and Cairns. (Sophie Howarth)

Powderfinger has added additional capital city shows to their 2027 tour, after the first ticket pre-sale saw massive demand and long queues.

The band announced their return last week, ten years after their final live show in 2010, revealing they will take to the road for shows across Brisbane, Melbourne, Sydney, Adelaide and Cairns in late 2027.

Powderfinger formed in 1989, and released five ARIA number one albums and won 18 ARIA Awards before their split.

Fight for pre-sale tickets

Fans signed up for the artist and Telstra pre-sales this morning reported long waits as thousands of people attempted to score tickets to the hotly-anticipated tour.

The tour's popularity has prompted the band and partner Live Nation to add additional shows across four capital cities.

Extra shows have been announced at the Brisbane Showgrounds for Monday, September 6 and Wednesday, September 8.

The band will perform two extra shows at Melbourne Park Oval on Monday, September 25 and Wednesday, September 27.

Sydney's Domain will get one extra show on Wednesday, October 6, and the band will play an extra show at Perth's Wellington Square on Monday, September 27.

The band teased their return in a social media campaign. (Supplied: Giulia Giannini McGauran/Live Nation)

More chances for tickets

The band stressed that those who did not get tickets this morning will have other chances.

Ticket sales have been unfolding in stages, with two pre-sales happening this morning and a Live Nation pre-sale on Friday.

General on-sale tickets will be released on Monday, 10am AEST.
