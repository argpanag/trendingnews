Kim Ji-won has been spotted bloodied in an emergency room, prompting questions about what happened.

On the 16th, the team behind SBS’s new Friday-Saturday drama ‘Doctor X: Age of the White Mafia’ (produced by Studio S, Studio Dragon and HighZium Studio; hereafter ‘Doctor X’) released stills from a tense emergency room scene, capturing viewers’ attention.

In the released stills, Gye Su-jeong (played by Kim Ji-won) lies motionless on an emergency room bed, covered in blood. Her clothes soaked through with blood hint at a serious situation. Beside her, Kim Seong-jin (played by Heo Jun-seok) of the emergency medicine department urgently administers emergency treatment, while the panicked expression of intern Park Tae-gyeong (played by Kim Woo-seok) heightens the tension.

The stills also capture hospital director Bu Seung-gwon (played by Son Hyun-joo) arriving to see Gye Su-jeong, prompting surprise. Why was Gye Su-jeong brought to the emergency room covered in blood? The full story behind the ultra-urgent situation that brought even the hospital director to the scene will be revealed in the broadcast.

‘Doctor X’ is a cathartic medical noir about the exploits of Doctor X Gye Su-jeong, the ‘mad Gye of the operating room,’ who knows neither failure nor compromise and uses her genius surgical skills to smash massive injustices.

Episode 3 of ‘Doctor X’ will air at 9:50 p.m. on the 16th.
