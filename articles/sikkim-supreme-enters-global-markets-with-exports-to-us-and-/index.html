Sikkim Supreme enters global markets with exports to US and Australia - Sikkimexpress

Tuesday, Nov 17, 2026 00:30 [IST]

GANGTOK,: Sikkim Supreme, a well-known local brand of the Government Fruit Preservation Factory (GFPF), a Public Sector Undertaking of the Government of Sikkim, has made its international debut with its first consignment of products being exported to the United States and Australia.

Chief Minister Prem Singh Tamang-Golay flagged off the first international consignment of Sikkim Supreme at a ceremony held at Samman Bhawan gate here on Monday morning. The event was attended by Secretary, Commerce and Industries Department, Karma D. Youtso, and Managing Director, GFPF, Chanda Rani Rai, among other officials.

In a post on his official Facebook page, the Chief Minister described the occasion as a proud moment for Sikkim, noting that the export initiative would take the State's flavours and legacy to the international market. A proud moment for Sikkim as Sikkim Supreme goes global, with its first international consignment to the USA and Australia, taking the flavours and legacy of Sikkim to the world, he said.

He also extended his best wishes to the officials and staff of the Commerce and Industries Department and GFPF, expressing hope that the milestone would pave the way for further global achievements.

Speaking to the media, Secretary, Commerce and Industries Department, Karma D. Youtso, termed the development a historic milestone for the state, as it marks the first time that Sikkim Supreme products are entering the international market. The initial consignment comprises 12 products for export.

Youtso also highlighted the department's support through the Raising and Accelerating MSME Performance (RAMP) Scheme, under which a 50 per cent subsidy is provided on transportation costs for exported goods. She said the department was pleased to extend the benefits of the scheme to GFPF and expressed confidence that this would mark the beginning of more locally manufactured products reaching international markets, bringing recognition and economic opportunities to the state.

Managing Director Chanda Rani Rai expressed her gratitude to the Chief Minister for taking the time to flag off the historic consignment. She said the development was particularly significant as it coincides with the celebration of 70 years of GFPF, which was established in 1956 through the erstwhile Durbar.

Describing the factory's seven-decade journey as one built on commitment and quality, Rai said the international recognition of Sikkim Supreme reflected the standards maintained by the institution over the years. She noted that entering the global market was not merely a business opportunity but also an affirmation that the factory's products meet international quality benchmarks and food safety requirements.

Rai also acknowledged the support extended by the Commerce and Industries Department, particularly its initiatives under the RAMP Scheme. She recalled that the department had organised a programme titled Sikkim Utsav in Siliguri last year, which provided GFPF an opportunity to interact with exporters and explore avenues for international trade. The initiative eventually paved the way for the present export consignment.

Emphasising the wider economic significance of the export initiative, Rai said the development was not limited to the growth of Sikkim Supreme but represented an opportunity for the entire agricultural ecosystem of the state. She pointed out that the factory procures its raw materials locally from farmers, adding that the brand's expansion into international markets could directly benefit farming communities while strengthening the state's agriculture and horticulture sectors.

She further observed that the growth of the brand would have a positive impact on allied sectors, including supporting institutions, employment generation and skill development, ultimately contributing to rural livelihoods and the overall economy of the state.

Expressing optimism about the future, Rai said GFPF intended to expand its reach in international markets while ensuring that the benefits of growth were shared across the farming and allied sectors. She reiterated the factory's vision of promoting the entire harvest of Sikkim under a unified brand identity, with the objective of taking the state's locally produced goods to the global market.

The first export consignment is destined for New York in the United States and Perth in Australia. The consignment to Perth comprises four varieties of pickles: Dalle Vinegar, Dalle Bamboo Pickle, Dalle Chilli Pickle and Mixed Pickle.

The consignment to New York includes 12 products: Dalle Chilli Pickle, Dalle Bamboo Pickle, Mixed Pickle, Dalle Vinegar, Dalle Paste, Dalle Chilli Olive Pickle, Dalle Bamboo Olive Pickle, Dalle Turmeric Pickle, Passion Squash, Pineapple Squash, Kiwi Jam and Mixed Fruit Jam.

The diverse range of products being exported reflects GFPF's efforts to take multiple locally produced varieties to international markets, demonstrating the brand's potential to expand beyond individual products while meeting global quality and food safety standards.
