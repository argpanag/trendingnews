Rosanne Cash Postpones Upcoming Shows Due to 'Unforeseen Medical Emergency'

TheTools · 07.09.2026 18:42 · source · permalink

Rosanne Cash has postponed her next three shows due to an “unforeseen medical emergency,” her management wrote on social media Monday.

“She is recovering under her doctors’ supervision, who have recommended a temporary restriction on travel and performing,” Cash’s team said. “The restriction is impacting her upcoming Wyoming and Montana shows. Rosanne deeply regrets the cancellation and looks forward to rescheduling appearances.”

View this post on Instagram

While Cash’s management did not elaborate what the medical emergency was, it won’t keep the singer off the road too long: While her three shows in Wyoming and Montana this week were postponed, an updated touring itinerary anticipates Cash returning to the road on September 19 for a gig in Jonesboro, Arkansas.
