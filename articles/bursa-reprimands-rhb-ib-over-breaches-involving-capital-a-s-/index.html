Bursa reprimands RHB IB over breaches involving Capital A’s distribution of AirAsia X shares

TheTools · 25.11.2026 10:41 · source · permalink

KUALA LUMPUR (Nov 25): Bursa Malaysia Securities has publicly reprimanded RHB Investment Bank Bhd for mishandling two regulatory deadlines related to Capital A Bhd's (KL:CAPITALA) distribution of AirAsia X Bhd (KL:AAX) shares to shareholders.

In a statement on Wednesday, Bursa said the breaches involved insufficient notice of adjustments to Capital A’s warrants and convertible debt securities, as well as a delay in completing the share distribution.

The breaches left Capital A shareholders waiting nearly seven weeks for their AirAsia X shares after the company's market capitalisation had been adjusted downwards by RM2.65 billion or 71.16% to reflect the distribution, while holders of its warrants and convertible debt securities traded without timely notice of the relevant adjustments.

RHB IB was the principal adviser for the corporate exercise, which was first announced on April 25, 2024, involving the distribution of 1.69 billion new AirAsia X shares to entitled Capital A shareholders.

In an immediate response, RHB IB said it acknowledged and respected Bursa’ decision, having cooperated with the regulator throughout the proceedings and exercised its right to appeal.

The investment bank described the corporate restructuring and share distribution undertaken as part of Capital A’s Practice Note 17 (PN17) regularisation plan as highly complex. “The outcome does not affect our ability to continue acting as a recognised principal adviser or providing corporate finance and advisory services to our clients,” RHB IB said in a separate statement.

On Nov 19, 2025, RHB IB announced on behalf of Capital A that the books closing date for the distribution had been fixed for Dec 3, but did not simultaneously disclose the cut-off date for adjustments to the warrants and convertible debt securities due to the exercise.

The latter was only announced on Nov 27 following Bursa’s engagement with RHB IB, which gave investors only four market days’ notice instead of the required minimum of 10.

Due to the late announcement, investors had traded the affected securities during the intervening period without timely information necessary to assess the implications of the adjustments, Bursa noted.

Furthermore, the share distribution was only completed on Jan 19, 2026 — 11 market days after the regulatory deadline of Jan 2.

Bursa noted that RHB IB had proceeded to fix and announce the books closing date despite uncertainty over whether the distribution could be completed within the stipulated period.

Capital A’s securities had begun trading ex-distribution on Dec 2, 2025, at an adjusted reference price of 24.5 sen, although shareholders had yet to receive their AirAsia X shares.

In deciding on the reprimand, which does not carry a monetary penalty, Bursa took into account the significance of the breaches, RHB IB’s responsibilities as principal adviser and its previous breaches of listing requirements.

The regulator said compliance with disclosure and implementation deadlines was essential to ensuring an orderly and fair market and maintaining investor confidence.

“The steps subsequently taken by RHB IB after it became aware of the delay in completing the distribution did not absolve its failure to ensure that the distribution was completed within the prescribed timeframe,” Bursa said.
