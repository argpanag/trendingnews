The Palm Coast City Council meets regularly to discuss and vote on official City business. These meetings are open to the public, and Palm Coast residents are strongly encouraged to attend. An increase in public engagement helps to build a stronger sense of community, increase valuable discussions, and decrease division.

The agenda for the City Council Business Meeting on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. will be as follows:

Minutes

The following minutes will be confirmed:

  1. October 20, 2026, Business Meeting

Resolutions

The following resolutions will be presented:

  1. The Finance Department will present a resolution to approve a Budget Amendment for the 2025-2026 Budget relating to the Appropriations of Funds for the Fire Impact Fee Fund. The Fire Station 22 project costs were higher than expected, so the City Council will look to amend the budget to cover the extra $230,000.
  2. The Finance Department will present a resolution to approve a Budget Amendment for the 2025-2026 Budget relating to the Appropriation of Funds for the Transportation Impact Fee Fund and the Old Kings Road Special Assessment Fund. The City of Palm Coast recorded a $20,000 higher transfer than expected between funds, so City Council will look to amend the budget to match the actual amount.
  3. City Administration will present a resolution to certify the Final 2026 General Election Results. City Council Districts 2, 3, and 4, and City Charter Amendments 1, 2, and 3 were on the November 3, 2026, General Election ballot. As stated in the City Charter, the City Clerk will present the results to the Council to certify and file the results.

Oath of Office for Newly Elected Council Members

The following will be made:

  1. The City Administration will swear-in the newly elected Council Members. The voters elected Antonio Amaral, Jr. for Seat 2, Ray Stevens for Seat 3, and Darlene Shelley for Seat 4. The City Clerk will conduct the swearing-in ceremony.

Organizational Items

The following will be made:

  1. The City Council will appoint a Vice Mayor for Fiscal Year 2026-2027. The City Charter states there will be an annual election for Vice Mayor, and the election will take place at the first meeting after the General Election or at the organizational meeting during years with no election.
  2. The City Council will appoint Council Liaisons for Fiscal Year 2026-2027. Members of the Palm Coast City Council serve on various regional boards and committees. The appointments are reviewed annually.

Public Comments will be opened at the beginning and end of the workshop in accordance with Section 286.0114, Florida Statutes, and pursuant to the City Council’s Meeting Policies and Procedures. Each speaker will approach the podium, provide his/her name, and may speak for up to 3 minutes.

Please note that proclamations are not scheduled for this specific meeting. Additionally, public comments on non-agenda items will be limited to 30 minutes at the beginning and end of each meeting. This schedule is subject to change due to holidays or other unforeseen circumstances. Residents are encouraged to visit the Palm Coast City Council meeting webpage for the latest schedule and agenda updates.

In addition to City Council meetings and workshops, residents are encouraged to participate in a variety of public meetings—including advisory boards, committees, and special sessions—taking place over the next week. Attending in person, tuning in live on the City’s YouTube channel, or reviewing meeting materials online are all great ways to stay informed and engaged in local government.

Upcoming Meetings:

  • Palm Coast Firefighter Pension Board Meeting, 11/16/2026, 8:30 a.m.
  • Planning & Land Development Regulation Board, 11/18/2026, 5:30 p.m.

Business meetings and workshops are open to the public and are streamed live on the City’s YouTube channel. Meeting agendas for all public meetings are posted in advance of each meeting on the City’s website. All agendas can be viewed here.

If you wish to obtain more information regarding the City Council’s agenda, please contact the City Clerk's Office at 386-986-3713. In accordance with the Americans with Disabilities Act, persons needing assistance to participate in any of these proceedings should contact the City Clerk at 386-986-3713 at least 48 hours before the meeting.
