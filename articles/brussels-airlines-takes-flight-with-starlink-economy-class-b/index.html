Brussels Airlines takes flight with Starlink - Economy Class & Beyond

TheTools · 09.11.2026 11:25 · source · permalink

09/11/2026 by Kevincm

Brussels Airlines is the latest airline to take flight with Starlink in-flight connectivity, with the first flight with the product being conducted.

Flight SN2257 to Copenhagen on 9th November was the first flight of Brussels Airlines featuring high-speed Wi-Fi on-board.  OO-SBA, an Airbus A320neo, is the first Brussels Airlines aircraft equipped with the connectivity solution.

The new Lufthansa Group Wi-Fi powered by Starlink will be rolled out across the entire fleet for all passengers.

Brussels Airlines plans to have 15 aircraft equipped with high-speed internet by the first half of 2027.


Installing Starlink aboard OO-SBA – Image, Brussels Airlines.

Manners in the Sky

To ensure a comfortable flight for everyone, guidelines have been drawn up to ensure the best possible internet experience whilst also setting out how this can be achieved without causing disruption to any passengers.

This is intended to ensure an appropriate, relaxed and undisturbed travel experience for everyone on board. Passengers are required to always wear headphones when playing audio and video content, to refrain from making voice and video calls, and not to broadcast live streams from on board.

Accessing Wi-Fi

All passengers wishing to use the new Lufthansa Group Wi-Fi must agree to these terms of use. The high-speed internet will be free for all passengers across all booking classes who have a Lufthansa Group Travel ID or Miles & More account.

In Quotes

Dieter Vranckx, chairman of Brussels Airlines and Chief Commercial Officer of Lufthansa Group, said:

“I’m excited to see Lufthansa Group Wi-Fi being rolled out at Brussels Airlines. With it, we’re making high-speed internet an integral part of the travel experience – across all travel classes. Our product promise doesn’t end with the seats or the menu – today, seamless connectivity is part of what makes the onboard experience truly ​ exceptional.”
