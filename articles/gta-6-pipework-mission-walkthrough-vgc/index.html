GTA 6 Pipework Mission Walkthrough

Pipework begins by driving to Mandy’s house. Mandy will flirt with Jason as you attempt to fix her sink. Go inside Mandy’s house, and collect the plunger from her bathroom. Once you’ve done this, head back to the sink, and begin the plunging.

Then, unblock the sink with R3, before moving under the sink and unblocking the pipes. During this Mandy will continue to flirt with Jason, but all you have to do is complete the short unblocking minigame.

Once this is finished, collect the money from Mandy, and head outside. A short interaction will play, and then Brian will call as you leave the area. This is the end of the Pipework mission, relationship still intact.

GTA 6 Pipework: Can you romance Mandy?

  • No, you can’t romance Mandy.

As much as she may want it, Jason can’t romance Mandy, no matter how you approach  the mission. Once you’re finished with your handywork (behave) move on.
