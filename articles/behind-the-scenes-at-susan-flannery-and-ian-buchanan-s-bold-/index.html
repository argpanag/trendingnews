Behind the Scenes at Susan Flannery and Ian Buchanan’s ‘Bold and the Beautiful’ Visit

TheTools · 24.10.2026 10:38 · source · permalink

Soap Opera

Susan Flannery and Ian Buchanan visit ‘The Bold and the Beautiful’ set, reuniting with former co-stars and reflecting on their time on the beloved soap opera.

The Bold and the Beautiful had some big visitors on set this week when veteran soap stars and fan favorites Susan Flannery and Ian Buchanan paid a visit and reflected on what it felt like to be back on some old sets in a brand-new studio.

On Tuesday, B&B executive producer Casey Kasprzyk posted a short video of Flannery and Buchanan riding a golf cart through the studio lot, indicating they were back for a visit. Now, the official B&B TV account has posted a behind-the-scenes video of the visit, with Buchanan and Flannery reuniting with old friends.

“It’s almost like a mind trick,” Flannery said in the video. “I was looking at the main Forrester set. It was like stepping back in time.”

In the video, you can see Flannery, who played Stephanie Forrester from the soap’s premiere in 1987 to 2012, reuniting with various former co-stars, including Katherine Kelly Lang, who plays Stephanie’s longtime nemesis, Brooke Logan. You can also see her talking with Jennifer Gareis (Donna Logan) and Thorsten Kaye, who plays Stephanie’s son Ridge. Flannery also met Brayan Nicoletti, who began bringing Stephanie’s grandson, RJ, to life last year.

“It’s definitely like coming home in a way, even though it’s not the same studio,” Buchanan said. “But it’s lovely to see old faces.”

Flannery, who also played Laura Horton on Days of our Lives from 1966 to 1975, according to IMDb, moved to Australia last year, according to former DAYS co-star Susan Seaforth Hayes, who posted the news on Instagram.

Buchanan portrayed Dr. James Warwick on B&B on and off from 1999 to 2017, per IMDb. The actor got his soap start on General Hospital as Duke Lavery in 1986 and made his last appearance on the ABC soap as Duke’s ghost in 2020.
