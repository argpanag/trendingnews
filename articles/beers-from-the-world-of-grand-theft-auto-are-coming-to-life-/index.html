Forget stealing cars for a moment. The next big thing from Grand Theft Auto could be you going to the fridge for a GTA-inspired beer. Three beers linked to the popular video game series have appeared in an official U.S. government database, and fans might soon get to see them outside the game. The lineup includes Pißwasser and Patriot, two familiar names from the GTA universe, plus a new Vice City-themed beer called No Mames Vice City.

Videos by Sporked

Three GTA Beers Could Finally Become Real

Longtime GTA fans will recognize these two names. Pißwasser and Patriot have appeared in previous games, giving players a taste of the fictional brands that fill Rockstar’s over-the-top world.

Pißwasser is probably the more memorable of the pair. The fictional beer has appeared in several GTA games, including Grand Theft Auto IV and Grand Theft Auto V. Its deliberately ridiculous name and familiar branding fit right in with the series’ habit of poking fun at real-world products and advertising.

The third beer might be less familiar to GTA fans, but it could have the most eye-catching packaging of the bunch. Called No Mames Vice City, the beer appears to connect the franchise’s fictional Florida-inspired setting with an existing product from Tripping Animals Brewing. The brewery already makes a beer called No Mames, and the new label suggests that the drink could receive a Vice City-themed makeover.

According to the public records reported by GTAVice.net, Pißwasser’s label was approved on September 29, 2026, while Patriot’s was approved on October 15th. Both are listed at 4.2% alcohol by volume (ABV), although their final availability has not been confirmed.

The records come from the U.S. Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau, which reviews alcohol labels. That gives the discovery some solid backing, even if it doesn’t guarantee that the drinks will reach stores.

A Florida Brewery Is Behind All Three Labels

All three beers are listed under Tripping Animals Brewing, a brewery based in Doral, Florida.

The location is a particularly good fit for a GTA VI-themed release. The upcoming game returns to Vice City, Rockstar’s fictional take on Miami, and its surrounding state of Leonida draws heavily on Florida’s scenery and culture.

A brewery based near Miami could make the collection feel right at home, especially if the drinks are released locally around the game’s launch.

The label records also reveal a little about the possible lineup. Pißwasser is identified as a pilsner, Patriot is listed as an American lager, and No Mames Vice City is another lager with a higher alcohol content.

Will The GTA Beers Be Available Before GTA VI Launches?

The timing certainly makes a release around the game launch seem possible. Grand Theft Auto VI is scheduled to arrive on November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. With less than a month to go, the discovery of these labels has arrived at an interesting moment.

A GTA-themed beer collection could give fans another way to celebrate the launch, especially those who enjoy collecting merchandise tied to their favorite games.

But there are still plenty of unanswered questions. No official prices have been announced and there is no confirmed release date or distribution plan for the three beers. It is also unclear whether they will be available outside Florida.
