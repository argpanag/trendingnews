Young Lions name 15-player squad for Pakistan U19 ODIs

TheTools · 14.09.2026 10:26 · source · permalink

Ralphie Albert will captain England Men U19s in four Youth ODIs against Pakistan this month

A 15-player squad has been selected for the England Men U19s’s four-match Youth ODI series against Pakistan Men U19s.

Surrey’s Ralphie Albert will again captain the squad that includes a further nine players from the Test squad that defeated Pakistan by 10 runs at Arundel last week - Jack Auld, Thomas Edwards, Luke Hands, Isaac Mohammed, Joe Moores, Jack Nelson, Luke Symington, Charlie Taylor and Harry Wallace. Spin duo Albert and Nelson combined for all 10 wickets in the fourth innings to bowl the Young Lions to victory.

Joe Moores is included for the opening match of the series before joining up with England Lions.

Neil Killeen will stand in as Head Coach for the series as Mike Yardy is with the England Men for the third Rothesay Test against Pakistan. Sam Billings will also join the coaching team for the series.

Ralphie Albert (Surrey - captain)
Subhaan Ali (Yorkshire)
Jack Auld (Essex)
Robbie Bowman (Durham)
Tom Bradley (Yorkshire)
Tom Edwards (Glamorgan)
Luke Hands (Lancashire)
George Harris (Lancashire)
Theo Lamey (Somerset)
Isaac Mohammed (Worcestershire)
Joe Moores (Lancashire)
Jack Nelson (Middlesex)
Luke Symington (Durham)
Charlie Taylor (Warwickshire)
Harry Wallace (Glamorgan)

1st Youth ODI: Wednesday 9 September, Arundel Castle Cricket Club, Arundel, 10.30am start
2nd Youth ODI: Saturday 12 September, Woodbridge Road, Guildford, 10.30am start
3rd Youth ODI: Monday 14 September, Sir Paul Getty’s Ground, Wormsley, 10.30am start
4th Youth ODI: Wednesday 16 September, Sir Paul Getty’s Ground, Wormsley, 10.30am start
