Hong Kong Open: Race to Finals Down to the Wire

TheTools · 17.11.2026 13:00 · source · permalink

Tuesday, November 17, 2026
TEXT BY BWF STAFF | BADMINTONPHOTO

The LI-NING Hong Kong Open 2026 marks the final stop on the HSBC Race to Finals, with the last available points on offer before the HSBC BWF World Tour Finals in Hangzhou next month.

While several berths have already been secured, a handful of coveted spots remain up for grabs across the disciplines, setting the stage for a high-stakes week in Hong Kong.

Among the biggest beneficiaries of late developments is Anders Antonsen. The Dane received a timely boost from the Seed Withdrawal Movement, which elevated him to the No.1 position in the draw in place of top seed Christo Popov. The promotion comes with a first-round bye and, crucially, enough ranking points to mathematically secure his place in Hangzhou.

Not everyone has enjoyed such a fortunate turn; Chinese Taipei’s Chou Tien Chen faces a sterner challenge than originally expected. Scheduled to meet Nhat Nguyen, Chou will instead open against fast-rising Indonesian Moh. Zaki Ubaidillah.

The race could also be influenced by events elsewhere. Following the withdrawal of his original opponent Alwi Farhan, India’s Kidambi Srikanth has an opportunity to help compatriot Lakshya Sen’s hopes when he takes on Koki Watanabe. A Watanabe defeat would secure qualification for Sen, Chou and Kunlavut Vitidsarn, making the first round clash one of the most significant of the week.

The drama is equally intense in the doubles disciplines. In both men’s and women’s doubles, six pairs are vying for the remaining spots, with all contenders present in Hong Kong.

Among those hoping to make history are Lauren Lam and Allison Lee. The duo are chasing a breakthrough qualification that would see them become the first Americans in any doubles discipline to reach a season finale.

With Hangzhou beckoning, the final chapter of the HSBC Race to Finals promises tension, twists and no shortage of drama.
