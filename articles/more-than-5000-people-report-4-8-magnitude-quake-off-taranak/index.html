More than 5000 people report 4.8 magnitude quake off Taranaki coast

TheTools · 15.09.2026 09:55 · source · permalink
  • News
  • National

A small earthquake off Taranaki has been felt across central New Zealand.

The 4.8 magnitude quake, categorised as “weak” by GeoNet, struck in the Tasman Sea, 70km southwest of the small town of Waverley, at 10.44am.

The shake was 82km deep.

More than 5000 people reported feeling it, with most clustered at the top of the South Island and lower half of the North Island.

One man in Nelson described the earthquake as “pretty gentle”.

A social media user said it was “short and sharp”.
