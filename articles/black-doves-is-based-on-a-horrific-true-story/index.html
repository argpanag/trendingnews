Keira Knightley and Ben Whishaw star in Black Doves, a thrilling and outrageous spy drama on Netflix, and according to creator Joe Barton, it is inspired by true stories.

"I had been reading as well about those spy cops, those guys, and they infiltrated that environmental group and had ended up having children with it," he told Radio Times. "I mean, a really horrific story, much darker than this."

Barton continued, "But I was like, OK, that idea, that duplicitousness of having a pretend marriage lasting years and years and years and then it's disappeared. That was really part of, I think, the inspiration for it as well."

The shadowy and mysterious Black Doves organization employs Sam (Ben Whishaw) and Helen (Keira Knightley).Ludovic Robert/Netflix

The "spy cops" scandal emerged in the UK in 2010, and a public inquiry took place in the matter in 2020. Per the Guardian, "The existence of a squad of police officers sent deep undercover in political groups was so top secret that many of the UK's most senior officers were completely oblivious." For more than four decades, at least 139 police officers were given fake identities to infiltrate political groups, according to the paper, and had intimate relationships with women in the groups.

In 2015, the Metropolitan Police apologized in a lengthy statement, with the assistant commissioner Martin Hewitt writing, "Thanks in large part to the courage and tenacity of these women in bringing these matters to light it has become apparent that some officers, acting undercover whilst seeking to infiltrate protest groups, entered into long-term intimate sexual relationships with women which were abusive, deceitful, manipulative and wrong."

He added, "Firstly, none of the women with whom the undercover officers had a relationship brought it on themselves. They were deceived pure and simple. I want to make it clear that the Metropolitan Police does not suggest that any of these women could be in any way criticized for the way in which these relationships developed."

Keira Knightley's Helen goes on a quest for revenge after her lover is murdered.Ludovic Robert/Netflix

Barton was also inspired to write Black Doves by the recent drama A Spy Among Friends, which was about the real British spy Kim Philby and his defection to the Soviet Union. "I like working within established genres but trying to put a twist on them or place 'trojan horse human character ideas' into them. I've done sci-fi, horror and crime before but had never done [spies]," he told Deadline.

Plus, setting the series during the holidays appealed to him. "I always wanted to set something at Christmas, I haven't managed to do it really before," Barton told Radio Times. "It's so sugary and cheerful, but actually quite a dark time of year for many people, and literally a dark time of year. It's all these contrasting ideas around it so it seemed like quite a natural time to set a thriller."
