Intel shares sink more than 15% as PC slowdown stretches into 2027

TheTools · 29.10.2026 21:45 · source · permalink

Weak outlook overshadows 21% revenue growth driven by data centers and AI

Intel issued weaker-than-expected guidance for the fourth quarter, with revenue expected to be in the range of $15 billion to $16 billion. (Photo by Suzu Takahashi)

PALO ALTO, California -- Shares of U.S. chip giant Intel plunged more than 15% during extended trading Thursday after the company released third-quarter results while cautioning investors the PC market slowdown will continue to weigh on future earnings.
