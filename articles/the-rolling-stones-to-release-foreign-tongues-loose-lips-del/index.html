The Rolling Stones to release ‘Foreign Tongues, Loose Lips’ deluxe edition

TheTools · 27.11.2026 07:29 · source · permalink

The Rolling Stones will expand their latest studio album with Foreign Tongues, Loose Lips, a deluxe edition arriving Nov. 27 through Capitol Records in the United States and Polydor/Universal Music internationally. The deluxe release adds five recordings from the sessions surrounding Foreign Tongues, giving fans more material from the Stones’ latest project.

Foreign Tongues topped charts in 14 countries and received positive reviews from critics. The album followed Hackney Diamonds, the band’s Grammy-winning 2023 release, which achieved multi-platinum success and topped charts internationally.

The expanded collection features “Where There’s A Will,” “Who You Callin’ Crazy,” featuring jazz saxophonist Courtney Pine, “Human Tide,” “Bad Luck Hideaway” and “She Still Comes Around.” “Who You Callin’ Crazy” is currently available to stream as a preview the deluxe edition, while “Bad Luck Hideaway,” previously available as a bonus track on the iTunes-exclusive album edition, has reached wider digital audiences as a B-side to “Mr. Charm.”

Foreign Tongues, Loose Lips will be available in digital formats, as a deluxe two-CD set and as a special vinyl edition packaged with a CD of the original album. The two-CD version includes all 14 tracks from Foreign Tongues on the first disc and the five additional recordings on the second. The vinyl package features the bonus songs on heavyweight red vinyl, along with the original album on CD.
