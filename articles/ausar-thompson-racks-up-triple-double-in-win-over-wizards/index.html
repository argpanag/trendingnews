Ausar Thompson racks up triple-double in win over Wizards

TheTools · 13.11.2026 03:34 · source · permalink

by Garrett Ball | Pistons Correspondent | Thu, Nov 12th 2026, 10:34pm EST

Ausar Thompson scored 17 points (8-15 FG, 0-1 3P, 1-2 FT) with 11 rebounds, 11 assists, three steals, and three blocks in the Pistons' 144-140 2OT win over the Wizards.

Fantasy Impact:

Thompson has a fantastic triple-double in this game, contributing not just on offense but also accumulating a combined six stocks in the big win. He now has four double-doubles this season, but his 11 assists were a season high. He isn't putting up much on the scoreboard, but is worth starting thanks to his playmaking skills.
