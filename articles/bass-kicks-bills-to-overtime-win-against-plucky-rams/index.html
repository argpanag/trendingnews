Bass kicks Bills to overtime win against plucky Rams

TheTools · 13.10.2026 06:36 · source · permalink

Getty

Buffalo Bills 37-34 Los Angeles Rams

The Buffalo Bills claimed a dramatic 37-34 victory over Los Angeles Rams on Monday night.

The Rams forced the game into overtime when Kyren Williams scored a touchdown with four seconds remaining, cancelling out James Cook's second touchdown for Buffalo.

But the Bills emerged victorious after Greg Rousseau blocked a Rams field goal attempt and Tyler Bass kicked the winning 45-yard field goal with one minute and 55 seconds left on the clock.

Bills quarterback Josh Allen threw for 210 yards and rushed for 121 and was involved in two touchdowns as Buffalo improved to 4-1.
