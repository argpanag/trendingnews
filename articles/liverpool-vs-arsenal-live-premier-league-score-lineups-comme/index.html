    Such a journey for Trey Nyoni. He made his Liverpool debut as a 16-year-old under Jurgen Klopp in 2024 and after the departure of Curtis Jones in the summer, Nyoni has seized his opportunity in style.

    He was arguably the standout story of Liverpool's pre-season tour and Andoni Iraola has constantly been full of praise for the 19-year-old since.

    Against Chelsea in midweek, Nyoni had more touches than anyone else on the pitch. Now it's time see how he fares in his first Premier League start against none other than the reigning champions.
