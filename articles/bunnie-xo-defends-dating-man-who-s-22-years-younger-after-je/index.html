Bunnie Xo Defends Dating Man Who’s 22 Years Younger After Jelly Roll Split: ‘He Gave Me My Smile Back’

TheTools · 22.09.2026 16:40 · source · permalink

"Ya girl is living her best life," the podcaster said.

Trending on Billboard

Bunnie Xo isn’t walking a lonely road following her split from Jelly Roll, with the podcaster giving fans a glimpse at her new romance with 24-year-old actor Dylan Wolf on Sunday (Sept. 20).

In the clip, Bunnie takes Wolf on his first excursion to Waffle House, where he lovingly feeds her bites of syrupy waffles as she giggles. In response to comments on the clip calling attention to their 22-year age gap, the Dumb Blonde host wrote, “Hey guys, I’m having fun & he gave me my smile back.”

“Ya girl is living her best life – let meeeee k thanks byeeeee,” Bunnie added in the comments.

Getting Bunnie’s “smile back” is no small feat. The influencer recently shared that she was so despondent in the aftermath of her high-profile divorce from Jelly, she needed IVs and lived off of baby food because she struggled to eat.

“There [were] moments when my team had to pick me up off the floor,” Bunnie recalled on Open Book With Jenna earlier in September. “I lost 20 pounds. Heartbreak, man. It’s the best freaking diet.”

But Bunnie also said at the time: “My heart was shattered, but guess what? It’s healing, and it’s coming back even stronger.”

News of Jelly and Bunnie’s split first went public in June, about a month after the country star filed for divorce in May. Shortly afterward, Bunnie revealed on her podcast that they broke up after having an explosive fight on Mother’s Day, but emphasized that she and Jelly are on good terms with plan to continue their IVF journey in the hopes of welcoming a baby together as friends.

She’s also not the only one who’s exploring new connections. On Bunnie’s now-deleted podcast about the divorce, she revealed that “Daddy Roll is probably in his finest season. … He looks so good and he’s healthier than he’s ever been. He’s started dating, which is great. I love that.”
