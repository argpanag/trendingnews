Confirmed Lineups: SC Freiburg vs. FK Jablonec

TheTools · 15.10.2026 19:06 · source · permalink

Bundesliga leaders Freiburg host Czechia side Jablonec in their opening UEFA Conference League league phase fixture. Julian Schuster's men recorded a comfortable victory over Motherwell in the qualifiers to secure their first participation in Europe's third-tier competition.

Including bringing back the captains Christian Günter and Vincenzo Grifo, Schuster rotated his side heavily with Sunday's trip to Leverkusen in mind. Yuito Suzuki will be suspended for that one after his red card against Schalke, but he starts tonight in the attacking midfielder role.

Here are the lineups for the Europa-Park Stadion duel.

Confirmed Freiburg vs. Jablonec Lineups

Freiburg: Backhaus; Kübler, Ginter. Rosenfelder, Günter ©; Eggestein, Osterhage; Irié, Suzuki, Grifo; Höler

Jablonec: Hanus; Cedidla, Tekijaski ©, Pavlovic, Slama; Sedlacek, Okeke; Chanturishvili, Alioum, Polidar; Rusek
