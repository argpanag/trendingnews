SAN ANTONIO — The UTSA Roadrunners will look to close out their nonconference schedule with a home victory Saturday when they host the Colorado State Rams at the Alamodome.

UTSA enters the matchup with a 2-1 record following last week’s 30-6 loss at No. 1 Texas. Before that, the Roadrunners opened the season with a 45-16 win over UTRGV at the Alamodome and a 31-26 road victory over Texas State.

Colorado State also enters the game at 2-1.

Saturday’s contest will mark the fourth meeting between the programs. Colorado State leads the all-time series 2-1, though UTSA earned a 17-16 win over the Rams in Fort Collins last season. The Rams won the only previous matchup in San Antonio, a 33-31 decision in 2015.

The game is UTSA’s final nonconference contest before the Roadrunners begin American Conference play next week at Rice.

Kickoff is set for 11 a.m. at the Alamodome.
