There's no question that this Australia squad is the deepest at the Rugby League World Cup, but all of that talent has left Kevin Walters with multiple selection dilemmas and he's not yet managed to strike the right notes. To their credit, Reece Walsh and Bradman Best have performed well, given that James Tedesco and Latrell Mitchell would be most fans' preferred starters.

The Kangaroos' key issue has been their forward pack. They've been bullied in every game, and as those big hits from Junior Paulo and Jarome Luai – two of the biggest we've seen at the tournament – demonstrated, Samoa have the firepower to take full advantage.

The Aussies know all about Brian To'o and Stephen Crichton, respectively one of the best wings and one of the standout centres in the NRL, but starting five-eighth Chanel Harris-Tavita has been ruled out with a hamstring injury. Ben Gardiner, however, has a ready-made replacement in Ronald Volkman, who's also been far tidier than Harris-Tavita off the tee.

Toa Samoa outscored Tonga by four tries to three last Sunday, but lost because the Warriors man missed both of his conversion attempts.
