The newest season of Big Brother has been one of the most dramatic to date.

On the series so far, we’ve had fights, rows, and at least one snog on the show, but that’s nothing compared to the argument that explodes in tonight’s episode.

So what causes the bust-up? Well, Rochelle and Johanne have an argument about chicken.

So how did we get here?

Well, according to The Sun, tonight’s episode will see Rochelle and Rob cooking as part of this week’s challenge, and it all leads to what they’ve branded ‘Chickengate’.

However, after serving the delicious dish to their housemates, Johanne panics and accuses the pair of not having properly cooked the meal.

‘Rochelle cooked chicken for everyone and it wasn’t to Johanne’s liking, and she kicked off,’ a source told the paper.

‘She accused Rochelle of undercooking it and, as a proud mum and housewife, Rochelle wasn’t having the criticism.

‘But Johanne is a food blogger, so she was adamant she knew what she was talking about. These two are constantly at it.’

And in a sneak peek released by the official Big Brother X account, Rochelle can be seen reacting to the criticism.

In the clip, the housemates sit down for dinner and complain about the chicken being half-raw.

Johanne then says: ‘Rochelle, I want to cook my chicken for…’

Before Johanne can finish her sentence, however, Rochelle cuts her off, shouting that they’ll fail the task if she cooks.

‘Who are you talking to?’ responds Johanne, to which Rochelle shouts, ‘You! It’s cooked!’

Johanne then says she won’t eat the meal, adding: ‘It’s not my fault you never took cooking classes.’

At this point, the pair start bickering until Rochelle yells: ‘She has got a screw loose. ‘You lazy f****** cow.’

Johanne then brands Rochelle a ‘old dirty hag’

Oh, and did we mention they had this entire argument while dressed as zoo animals.

It’s all very intense, but fans are loving it. ‘This season delivers every day,’wrote @realityteas on X.
