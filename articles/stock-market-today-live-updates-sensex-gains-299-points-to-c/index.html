Stock Market Today Live Updates: Sensex gains 299 points to close at 74,828, Nifty gains 118 points - The Times of India

TheTools · 23.09.2026 11:05 · source · permalink

Small-cap stocks are trading at nearly twice the valuation of the Nifty 50, with trailing PE multiples of 34.4 versus 19.7 for the benchmark, Geojit Investments’ VK Vijayakumar said. He called the valuation gap unusual and said a sustained return of FIIs could help narrow it, as large-cap earnings growth remains strong.

“There is a huge dichotomy in valuations,” Vijayakumar said, noting that the current pattern is the reverse of the usual market hierarchy, where large caps command higher valuations than mid- and small-caps.

Stock market today: Top stocks to buy on September 23

BSE Sensex is trading in green in opening trade, with Bajaj Finance rising 2.03% to Rs 1,029.50 and Bajaj Finserv gaining 1.53% to Rs 1,853. Tata Steel advanced 1.25% to Rs 187, while UltraTech Cement and Larsen & Toubro both climbed 0.89% to Rs 11,095 and Rs 3,904.55, respectively.

Asian Paints rose 0.82% to Rs 2,464.35, followed by Kotak Bank at 0.63% to Rs 415.10 and Adani Ports at 0.61% to Rs 1,801. Titan gained 0.48% to Rs 4,933.50, while SBI and Power Grid rose 0.46% and 0.45% to Rs 990.70 and Rs 267.20, respectively.

ITC gained 0.42% to Rs 266.15, while Indigo rose 0.38% to Rs 5,038.90. Hindustan Unilever and NTPC added 0.33% and 0.31% to Rs 1,941.45 and Rs 328, respectively. Bharti Airtel also advanced 0.31% to Rs 1,822.65.

Reliance Industries rose 0.14% to Rs 1,243.80, while Sun Pharma gained 0.12% to Rs 1,846.30. Maruti edged up 0.07% to Rs 12,199, HCL Tech gained 0.04% to Rs 1,270.45 and Trent was almost flat, rising 0.01% to Rs 2,773.90.

Rupee strengthened 16 paise to close at 95.62 against the US dollar in the previous trading session, supported by a sharp decline in crude oil prices, improved global risk sentiment and hopes of diplomatic talks to resolve the US-Iran war. The Indian currency opened at 95.75 and gained ground during the session to touch 95.55 against the dollar before settling at 95.62.

The currency had closed at 95.78 in the previous session after appreciating 18 paise. The dollar index was trading at 100.34, down 0.08%, while US Treasury yields also declined after reports around possible diplomatic developments. Anuj Choudhary of Mirae Asset Sharekhan said the rupee was expected to trade with a positive bias, with USD-INR spot price seen in a 95.30-95.80 range.

Benchmark indices ended the previous session in red, with BSE Sensex declining 329.91 points, or 0.44%, to settle at 74,529.08, while NSE Nifty fell 85.30 points, or 0.36%, to end at 23,329. Sensex had lost as much as 435.28 points, or 0.58%, during the session, touching 74,423.71.

Bajaj Finserv, Trent, Bajaj Finance, Sun Pharma, Tata Consultancy Services and UltraTech Cement were among the major laggards on the 30-share index. Eternal, InterGlobe Aviation, Titan and Tata Steel were among the gainers.

The domestic benchmarks declined despite a positive global market trend and lower crude oil prices, with IT, financials and capital goods stocks weighing on the indices. Foreign institutional investors also remained sellers, offloading equities worth Rs 3,809.99 crore on a net basis.
