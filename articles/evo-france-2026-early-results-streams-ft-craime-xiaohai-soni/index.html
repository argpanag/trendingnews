Evo France 2026 early results, streams ft. Craime, Xiaohai, SonicFox, Mulgold, Hinao, Higuchi, Hikari, Leffen, Supernoon, Kobayan, Punk and more

TheTools · 09.10.2026 08:00 · source · permalink

Update: This story is being updated live with day 2 results.

Evo France is back for its second year here in 2026 and once again it will be taking place in Nice, France.

There are way too many notable players to even list, and as usual for Evo events the vast majority of top players will be attending (although we previously reported on a few notable omissions).

Of the main titles this year, Street Fighter 6 is as expected the biggest one with over 1,075 entrants to its name. Next in line is Tekken 8 with almost 1,000 participants followed by the recently released Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls netting almost 500 signups. These three titles will be the Sunday finals games.

As for the rest of the main lineup games, 2XKO is after that with over 300 participants followed by Avatar Legends at 260 players and then Guilty Gear Strive just behind with over 250 people joining. Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves landed at over 230 signups and finally Granblue Fantasy Versus Rising netted over 200 entrants.

Unsurprisingly, there's a lot of Pro Tour status going on for Evo France 2026 as well. It's the last Premier for Street Fighter 6 in the Capcom Pro Tour and it's also a Master+ event for Tekken 8 on the Tekken World Tour and a Platinum event for Guilty Gear Strive on the Arc World Tour.

It's also a SNK World Championship qualifier fot Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves and a Five Star Event for Granblue Fantasy Versus Rising, so the stakes are high across the board.

Speaking of stakes, there's a total prize pool of €50,000 ($55,994) for for Street Fighter 6, €38,250 ($42,836) for Tekken 8, €34,873 ($39,054) for Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls, a massive €95,028 ($106,420) for 2XKO, €28,440 ($31,849) for Avatar Legends, €18,000 ($20,158) for Guilty Gear Strive, €35,373 ($39,513) for Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves and €13,501 ($15,120) for Granblue Fantasy Versus Rising.

In addition, there will of course be a good amount of side tournaments played as well.

Streaming is being done at Evo, Evo France, Evo France 2, Evo France 3, Evo France 4, Evo France 5 and Evo France 6.
