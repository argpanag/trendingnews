Lisandro Martínez scores for Manchester United in 4-0 win vs. Sabah in Champions League | Mundo Albiceleste

TheTools · 11.09.2026 05:54 · source · permalink

Lisandro Martínez scored for Manchester United in their 4-0 win against Sabah in the Champions League.

Martínez got his first goal in the Champions League, scoring for Manchester United in their win. With Manchester United leading 3-0, a free kick into the penalty area fell to Lisandro and the Argentine got to the loose ball to score.
