Monday Night Football: Bills WR DJ Moore, CB Dee Alford are inactive

TheTools · 12.10.2026 23:03 · source · permalink

The Bills will not have wide receiver DJ Moore for Monday Night Football.

Moore is among the team’s inactives, sitting out after aggravating an AC sprain in his left shoulder.

He has 12 catches for 178 yards and a touchdown this season.

Cornerback Dee Alford (ankle) also will not play.

Rookie wide receiver Skyler Bell and rookie cornerback Jalon Kilgore are active for the first time. Kilgore could join Damar Hamlin in helping replace Alford in the nickel.

The Bills’ other inactives are wide receiver Joshua Palmer (shoulder), offensive lineman Jude Bowry, offensive lineman Ar’maj Reed-Adams, defensive lineman Ed Oliver (knee) and defensive lineman Landon Jackson (knee).

The Rams will have safety Quentin Lake, who was questionable with a hip injury.

Their inactives are wide receiver C.J. Daniels, quarterback Ty Simpson, defensive end Larrell Murchison, offensive lineman Bill Murray and tight end Max Klare.
