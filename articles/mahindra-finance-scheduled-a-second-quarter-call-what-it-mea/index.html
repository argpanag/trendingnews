Mahindra Finance scheduled a second-quarter call: what it means for Mahindra Finance stock

TheTools · 21.10.2026 03:56 · source · permalink

Upcoming results and operating scale

  • Mahindra Finance scheduled its second-quarter conference call for October 21, 2026.
  • Second-quarter disbursements reached INR 164.90 billion, up 22.00 percent year over year.
  • Business assets stood at INR 146.30 billion in September 2026, up 15.00 percent year over year.

Mahindra Finance (ISIN INE774D01024) had estimated second-quarter fiscal 2027 disbursements of INR 164.90 billion, up 22.00 percent year over year. The company scheduled its results conference call for October 21, 2026, according to StockFin.

Three dates shape the setup

On September 24, 2026, Mahindra Finance approved a secured non-convertible debenture issue of up to INR 20.00 billion, with a base size of INR 10.00 billion and a 7.95 percent coupon, CNBC TV18 reported. On October 2, 2026, the company reported second-quarter disbursements of INR 164.90 billion, up 22.00 percent year over year. On October 13, 2026, it announced the October 21 conference call for the results and outlook, according to StockFin.

The next two scheduled dates are October 21, 2026, for the board meeting to approve the second-quarter and first-half results, and October 21, 2026, at 6:30 p.m. India Standard Time for the earnings conference call. Both dates appear in the company investor-relations calendar, Mahindra Finance states.

What does the second-quarter call mean?

The call will test whether the lending expansion reported in the operating update translated into reported earnings. Business assets reached INR 1.46 trillion as of September 2026, up 15.00 percent from September 2025, while collection efficiency was estimated at 97.00 percent against 96.00 percent a year earlier, CNBC TV18 reported. The figures exclude finance leases.

The company also said first-half fiscal 2027 disbursements reached INR 320.50 billion, up 22.00 percent year over year. That gives the October 21 disclosure a clear numerical benchmark: management must explain whether volume growth is being maintained alongside credit discipline.

Credit quality remains central

Stage-3 assets were estimated at 3.35 percent to 3.45 percent as of September 30, 2026, compared with 3.94 percent a year earlier. Mahindra Finance operates as a non-bank lender focused on vehicle and other asset financing in rural and semi-urban markets, while its shares are listed on the NSE under ticker M&MFIN, according to ATS.
