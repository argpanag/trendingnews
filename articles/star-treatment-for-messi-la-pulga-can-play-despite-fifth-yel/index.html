👀 Star treatment for Messi? La Pulga can play despite fifth yellow

Inter Miami face New England Revolution late on Sunday night in their penultimate regular-season game. Superstar Lionel Messi will also be in action, despite picking up his fifth yellow card in Thursday's 2-2 draw against Cincinnati. Is La Pulga getting special treatment?

Not actually. The Argentine benefits from a very specific MLS rule. Players who go five consecutive matches without receiving a yellow card have one yellow card deducted from their tally, if they have any.

Messi had completed that five-game run just before the match, so he could afford to pick up another booking without worrying. Looking back over the past few months, though, an exception for the star would hardly have been surprising.
