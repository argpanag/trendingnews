Scattered rain Thursday; Coldest air of the season this weekend

TheTools · 12.11.2026 09:59 · source · permalink

Ahead of a strong cold front, expect mostly cloudy skies and scattered rain Thursday.

Ahead of a strong cold front, expect mostly cloudy skies and scattered rain Thursday.

Thursday will be the wettest day of the week as scattered rain filters through the region.

Your Gulf Coast Weather Most Accurate Forecast says the rain is due to an increase in moisture ahead of our next cold front. Expect more clouds than sun Thursday with highs still reaching the low 80s.

The front will pull through Friday morning allowing cooler, drier air to filter in throughout the day. You may want the light jacket by Friday night as lows tumble into the 50s. For most Gulf Coast communities, lows haven't dipped into the 50s since April!

This weekend will feature the coldest air of the season with highs only in the 60s Saturday. By Sunday, our air mass will already begin to modify with highs returning to the 70s.
