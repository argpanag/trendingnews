Valve Reveals Steam Frame VR Headset Price and Introduces Accessories

TheTools · 14.09.2026 17:43 · source · permalink

In mid-September, Valve announced the prices for different versions of the Steam Frame VR headset. The package includes controllers, as well as a copy of the game Half-Life: Alyx.

Buyers will be offered two versions, which will differ in terms of built-in memory:

  • Steam Frame with 256 GB is priced at $1059 (around 90 thousand rubles).
  • Steam Frame with 1 TB is priced at $1299 (around 110 thousand rubles).

Valve noted that Steam Frame is designed for both VR and regular games: “It’s designed for streaming games and works with your entire Steam library. And it also supports a standalone mode: over 100 games (and counting) are fully compatible with Steam Frame in this mode.”

The device is equipped with a 4 nm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 processor and 16 GB of RAM. It features 2160 x 2160 pixel LCD displays with a refresh rate from 72 to 144 GHz.

The company also introduced accessories:

  • Power supply (45W, with 2.5m cable) — $29
  • Ergonomic accessory kit (2 controller straps + battery covers, 1 head strap, 1 enlarged light blocker) — $59
  • Replacement accessory kit (1 face cushion, 1 soft head cushion, 1 standard light blocker) — $49

The Steam Frame page in the Steam store is available at the following link. You can sign up for the queue until September 17, and selected users will be able to place an order starting September 18.
