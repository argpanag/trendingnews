Who is the SNL Host for the November 14 episode?

John Mulaney.

(Ego Nwodim) and Daniel (John Mulaney) appear on Saturday Night Live Season 44 Episode 14 during the "Cha Cha Slide" sketch on Saturday, March 2, 2019.

Mulaney was an SNL writer from 2008 to 2013. During that time, he created the iconic Stefon character, played by co-creator Bill Hader. Stefon made many appearances on "Weekend Update," with Hader often breaking character due to Mulaney constantly changing the cast members' cue cards in between dress rehearsal and the live show.

Making his hosting debut in 2018, Mulaney entered the Five-Timers Club in 2022. He enjoys a successful career in stand-up comed, has starred in shows like Big Mouth, and hosted the streaming talk show series Everybody's Live with John Mulaney in 2025. Mulaney is in the cast of the 2026 biopic Madden starring Nicolas Cage.

Who is the Musical Guest for tonight's November 14 SNL episode?

Phoebe Bridgers.

Phoebe Bridgers during The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon Season 13 Episode 128.

Bridgers is a four-time Grammy winner currently touring in support of her latest album, Lost Weekend, and recently announced more tour dates across the United States through May 2027. Lost Weekend's lead single, "Lost Boys," climbed to the number-one spot on Billboard's Adult Alternative Airplay chart on August 29, 2026.

You may have seen the star on the silver screen lately, too: Bridgers co-stars alongside Robert Pattinson and Skyler Gizondo in the 2026 Chris Hansen biopic Primetime.

Bridgers debuted as an SNL Musical Guest in Season 46 on February 6, 2021, performing her hits "Kyoto" and "I Know The End." The singer-songwriter capped her second performance of the evening by smashing her guitar to pieces.

She subsequently appeared as part of indie supergroup Boygenius in Season 49 on November 11, 2023, performing alongside Julien Baker and Lucy Dacus.
