Taylor Swift flashes wedding ring after Travis Kelce's touchdown

TheTools · 22.09.2026 04:00 · source · permalink

Taylor Swift is back at Arrowhead Stadium six days after cheering on the Kansas City Chiefs in their season opener.

The singer returned Sept. 20 as the Chiefs faced the Indianapolis Colts, watching from a suite with her parents, Andrea and Scott Swift.

Swift wore a Chiefs camisole for the special occasion, and cameras captured the superstar pointing to her massive wedding ring while celebrating husband Travis Kelce scoring a touchdown in the first quarter.

Swift attended Monday Night Football for the Sept. 14 game against the Denver Broncos, when cameras caught her watching alongside actor Tom Cruise, her parents, mother-in-law Donna Kelce and brother-in-law Jason Kelce.

'She's a genius'

Cruise later revealed Swift spent part of the game explaining football to him.

"She was schooling me on football, for real," Cruise told Jimmy Fallon during a Sept. 17 appearance on "The Tonight Show." "She knows her stuff."

Cruise, who attended Swift and Travis Kelce’s July wedding at Madison Square Garden, also praised the singer as a "brilliant musician and director" and said he had followed her career since she was young.

"She's a genius," Cruise said. "She's got such a great sense of humor."

How to watch next Chiefs games

The Kansas City Chiefs host the Indianapolis Colts at Arrowhead Stadium on Sept. 20 for “Sunday Night Football.” Kickoff is scheduled for 8:20 p.m. ET / 7:20 p.m. CT.

Swift has largely limited her regular-season appearances to Chiefs home games since her first season following the team. She traveled frequently during the 2023 season, but has attended only a handful of regular-season road games since then.

The Chiefs have two games on the road — facing the Miami Dolphins on Sept. 27 and the Las Vegas Raiders on Oct. 4 — followed by a bye week. Their next game at Arrowhead Stadium is Oct. 18 against the Los Angeles Chargers.
