Inter Miami v FC Cincinnati line-ups confirmed for MLS Matchday 33

Inter Miami return to Nu Stadium tonight to host FC Cincinnati on MLS Matchday 33 after consecutive away matches. Kick-off is at 7.30pm ET. According to Inter Miami, the starting XIs are set.

Inter Miami start in a 4-3-3. Dayne St. Clair is in goal, behind Gonzalo Luján, Casemiro, Micael and Sergio Reguilón. Rodrigo De Paul, David Ayala and Matías Galarza are in midfield, with Leo Messi, the captain, Luis Suárez and Germán Berterame up front.

OneFootball Videos

The bench features goalkeeper Rocco Ríos Novo, defenders Maximiliano Falcón, Ian Fray and Facundo Mura, midfielders Telasco Segovia, Yannick Bright and Santiago Morales, and forwards Riquelme Fillipi and Daniel Pinter. Head coach is Cristian González.

FC Cincinnati are listed in a 4-3-3. Roman Celentano starts in goal, with Samuel Gidi, Nick Hagglund and Teenage Hadebe in defence. David Douděra, Obinna Nwobodo, Evander, Gerardo Valenzuela and Bryan Ramírez are named in midfield, while Kenji Mboma Dem and Kevin Denkey lead the line.

Evan Louro is on the bench alongside Alvas Powell, Andrei Chirila and Kyle Smith, Pavel Bucha, Brian Anunga and José Martínez, and Ayoub Jabbari and Tom Barlow. Pat Noonan is head coach.
