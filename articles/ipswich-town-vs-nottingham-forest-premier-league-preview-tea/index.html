    Ipswich v Nottm Forest (20:00 BST)

    Ipswich Town

    Ipswich boss Gary O'Neil speaking to Sky Sports about the switch to a back four: "The team today allows us to get more attacking players on the pitch. I don't think we'll always look like a back four tonight, at times we'll look like a back five. We want as many attacking players on the pitch as we can."

    On dealing with Nottingham Forest's transitions and how they do that: "Lots of things. We need to be really secure with the ball. If you lose the ball in central, dangerous areas they will get to the goal really quickly and then how well we deal with it because we are going to lose the ball at times. That will be a test, as will be their direct play. They are a very direct side so their set-pieces will be key too.

    "It is going to be a big test for us. It will be a different test probably to what we've faced so far so we'll learn about ourselves again today and hopefully one we can come out on top in."

  7. 'Gibbs-White is our key attacking player'published at 19:49 BST
    19:49 BST

    Ipswich v Nottm Forest (20:00 BST)

    Nottingham Forest

    Nottingham Forest boss Oliver Glasner talking to Sky Sports about whether you can overthink when results aren't going your way: "Yes, very often. You think about whether we should change here or there. We watch our performances back and we feel like with our performances we could have four, five or six points more. We're not overthinking. Today we will do everything necessary to get the win.

    "It is proper English weather tonight so I expect a very intense game. This is what we have to match."

    On Morgan Gibbs-White's return: "Morgan is maybe our key player in our attack. He scored 15 goals last season and he's been unlucky this season - I think he could have five, six or seven assists already. For an assist you always need someone who takes the chance! With his creativity, his understanding of the game and his personal goal threat, he is very important for our attack."

  12. Glasner has spoken to Marinakis about slow startspublished at 19:36 BST
    19:36 BST

    Ipswich v Nottm Forest (20:00 BST)

    Nick Mashiter
    BBC Sport football news reporter

    There is confidence internally Forest are still on the right track under Oliver Glasner, who has impressed with his methods and personality.

    He was brought in to take Forest to the next level after lifting the FA Cup, Community Shield and Conference League with Palace.

    Glasner is impatient to repeat that success, detailing how owner Evangelos Marinakis is the only person he has met with more ambition than him.

    The 52-year-old has also said he is not a magician and has already spoken to Marinakis about previous slow starts.

    He won just one of his opening six games at Palace after replacing Roy Hodgson in February 2024 and then did not win any of his first eight games in his first full season with the Eagles in 2024-25.

    That campaign finished with Palace beating Manchester City in the FA Cup final to end a 119-year wait for a first major trophy.

  17. Faltering Forest need a winpublished at 19:20 BST
    19:20 BST

    Ipswich v Nottm Forest (20:00 BST)

    Chris Adams
    BBC Sport journalist

    Nottingham Forest head coach Oliver Glasner is one of several Premier League bosses in dire need of a win to stave off the threat of losing his job.

    The Austrian, who moved to the City Ground just 40 days after guiding Crystal Palace to Conference League glory, is already under intense scrutiny after only one win in seven league games.

    Forest have scored just five league goals, fewer than any other team in the division, and sit second-bottom with a tricky run before the November international window. Their xG underperformance of -6.2 is the worst in the division, with marquee summer signing Liam Delap netting just once.

    Forest have lost their past three league games, more defeats than in their previous 14. With last season's top scorer Morgan Gibbs-White fit again for the trip to Suffolk, Glasner will be hoping his men can arrest this worrying decline in form.
