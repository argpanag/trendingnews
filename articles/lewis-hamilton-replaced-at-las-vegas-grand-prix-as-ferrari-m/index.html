Lewis Hamilton replaced at Las Vegas Grand Prix as Ferrari make announcement

TheTools · 17.11.2026 10:55 · source · permalink

Ferrari have announced a major change as they prepare for the Las Vegas Grand Prix this weekend

Lewis Hamilton will not take part in Ferrari's opening practice session ahead of this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix. After a two-week lay-off, Formula 1 action returns on Sunday, November 22, at the Las Vegas Street Circuit.

However, Hamilton will not be afforded all three practice sessions to get to grips with the trials and tribulations of the track ahead of the big race, with the Prancing Horse confirming that Dino Beganovic will replace him during Friday's opener. The move comes as each F1 team is required to fill four practice sessions per season with rookie drivers.

Beganovic has already enjoyed a place in Hamilton's car at the Spanish Grand Prix, and has been at the wheel of Charles Leclerc's motor in Austria and Brazil. As a result, Las Vegas will see Ferrari comply with F1's strict requirement regarding rookie drivers.

As per F1 rules, each team must hand over FP1 duties to rookies with no more than two Grand Prix starts, across two sessions per car per season. That equates to four mandatory appearances for rookies for each team on the calendar.

Beganovic will not be the only rookie on the track for Friday's opening session, which runs between 12.30am and 1.30am GMT, however. Haas have also opted to deploy Ryo Hirakawa as a stand-in for Oliver Bearman to comply with the rules.

While the Las Vegas Street Circuit is not the easiest track for rookies to navigate, Ferrari had little choice in their running of Beganovic as a result of time constraints. Beganovic, 22, has been a member of the Ferrari Academy since 2020, and was recently confirmed to be stepping into a full-time reserve role with the team as of 2027.

There, he will continue to develop under the tutelage of some of the best in the business. Speaking after Brazil's practice session, Beganovic took to social media to share his thanks to Ferrari for their ongoing trust.

He wrote: "Amazing feeling to finally be back in the SF-26 and in such a special place like São Paulo. We collected good data for the team, even though our running was unfortunately cut short due to an issue while we were on the soft tyres.

"Nevertheless, it felt great to be back in the car and feeling in good form. Can’t wait to be back behind the wheel again. As always, a big thanks to my second family, @scuderiaferrari , for the trust and support. Obrigado Brasil."

The Las Vegas Grand Prix gets underway at 4am GMT on Sunday, November 22.
