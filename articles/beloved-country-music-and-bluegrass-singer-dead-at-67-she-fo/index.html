Jeanie Poling, beloved for her work as a country and bluegrass musician, has died at 67. The San Francisco Chronicle reported that Poling died on Aug. 26 after battling two rare forms of cancer for the past three years. She performed alongside her husband, Chuck Poling, and the Chronicle said she was "at the heart of San Francisco's bluegrass scene." Chuck Poling said she passed away on August 26 after a three-and-a-half year battle with cancer.
