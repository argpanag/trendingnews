Police chief gives update on Road of Remembrance hanging investigation

TheTools · 08.09.2026 18:21 · source · permalink

Brackney said JPD has been in contact with Tasia Fortune's mother as well as medical examiner

Brackney said JPD has been in contact with Tasia Fortune's mother as well as medical examiner

The Jackson police chief gave an update in the case of a woman who was found dead, hanging from a tree behind a vacant house.

The case has drawn many questions from the community as to the circumstances that led to the death of Tasia Fortune. She was found hanging behind a home on Road of Remembrance in early August.

Her parents have said the 29-year-old was not suicidal.

Chief RaShall Brackney said they have not yet received results from the medical examiner's office, but they had performed an autopsy about four weeks ago.

"Jackson police have been investigating this case since day one as a homicide until we hear otherwise from the medical examiner's office. I have been in contact with the commissioner who oversees the medical examiners several times," Brackney said.

During the Jackson City Council Meeting Tuesday, Ward 3 Councilman Kenneth Stokes brought a letter from Fortune's mother, Christy Spivey, that was read during the meeting.

"I have no more answers from one day of this investigation. The detectives are like they are on a prerecorded message just saying the same thing over and over. Now it's gone to silence," the letter read.

Chief Brackney said, "There is a lot of misinformation out there about this case and other ones. In addition, if you want to talk about the letter, we have been in constant contact with Miss Christy Spivey."

"Hanging is a ugly, ugly part of our past that we never, ever want to come back again," said Stokes.

The chief addressed other hangings in the south and how there was a lot of misinformation and speculation about them possibly being connected.
