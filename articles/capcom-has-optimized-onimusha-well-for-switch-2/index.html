Capcom has optimized Onimusha well for Switch 2

TheTools · 04.09.2026 07:29 · source · permalink

Onimusha: Way of the Sword on Switch 2 runs at approximately 45 FPS and in quality mode performs smoother than the PS5 version.

On Switch 2, the game received only one graphics mode and maintains around 40–50 FPS, averaging approximately 45 frames. On PS5, in quality mode, the figure is around 35–45 FPS. However, the Sony console has a performance mode with a full 60 FPS, but with a lower internal resolution.

The Switch 2 version received a number of simplifications, especially in portable mode: the developers reduced geometry detail and resolution. However, on a small screen, performance of around 45 FPS should provide comfortable gameplay.

The PlayStation 5 also has a performance mode, providing 60 frames per second. This is accompanied by some graphical degradations and a lower internal rendering resolution. Here, Sony's hardware truly demonstrates its advantages.

Capcom had to make compromises on Switch 2. In portable mode, the terrain geometry is simplified and the resolution is reduced, but on a small screen, around 45 frames per second should ensure an enjoyable gameplay experience.
