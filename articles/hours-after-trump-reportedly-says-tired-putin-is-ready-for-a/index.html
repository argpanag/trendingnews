Hours after Trump reportedly says ‘tired’ Putin is ready for a deal, Ukraine hit with massive strikes | CNN

TheTools · 20.11.2026 07:40 · source · permalink

Moscow launched a deadly missile and drone attack on Ukraine overnight into Friday, just hours after Russian President Vladimir Putin met his US and Chinese counterparts for historic trilateral talks in which the three powers billed themselves as a force for peace in the world.

Trump spoke to European leaders after the call, reportedly describing Putin as “tired” and ready for peace. But Kyiv said Russia fired at least 100 missiles overnight - an unusually large number even amid Russia’s frequent aerial assaults.

Eight people have been killed and at least 28 injured during the massive attack which has so far lasted more than eight hours, according to Ukrainian authorities.

Some 400 drones, including 120 jet-powered drones which are essentially compact cruise missiles, were also part of the attack.

The Polish government said on Friday that fighter jets were scrambled and Patriot air defense systems activated early on Friday after Poland’s airspace was violated during the attack, with “objects” flying in and falling inside the country’s territory.

The deadly assault came hours after Putin left China following a day of meetings in Shenzhen with US President Donald Trump and Chinese leader Xi Jinping.

The talks failed to produce any breakthrough on Moscow’s more than four-year war in Ukraine, and appeared to be used by Putin as an opportunity to repeat his maximalist demands and false narratives about the conflict in front of a global audience.

The Russian leader hailed the summit with Xi and Trump as historic and told reporters the discussions took place in a “relaxed and very intimate atmosphere.”

In his opening remarks, Putin said Russia, China and the US have “shared interests” in settling the Ukraine war.

No official declaration or a readout were released after the talks, but Trump did speak afterwards to the so-called Coalition of the Willing, a group of Ukraine’s key allies which was convening with US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner in Berlin.

According to a senior European official with knowledge of the call’s contents, the president’s main message was that “Putin is tired, wants to end this, ready for a deal.”

The official said Trump’s suggestion was “to reach out and grasp at the opportunity.”

He said the Europeans were “constructive in response but want proof.”

Poland’s Prime Minister Donald Tusk, who was part of the call, said that Trump sounded “fairly optimistic.”

“Although I have to say (his words) were received very cautiously in the room, when it comes to Putin’s and Moscow’s intentions regarding peace talks.”

“But I don’t hide the fact that I’ve heard this very often from President Trump. His belief in Putin’s goodwill seems unshakable, even though so far there are no facts to support it, he said, adding that it “would be good, if it really turned out that Russia was genuinely ready to engage in peace talks.”

Kushner said Trump had conveyed “a very strong message to both of them [Xi and Putin] on the desire for peace and would like to engage both of them very aggressively to get there.”

Strikes target key bridges

Russia’s defense ministry said in a post to Telegram it launched a “massive strike using high-precision weapons from land, sea, and air,” as well as drones, targeting Ukrainian military and energy facilities, port infrastructure in the Odesa region, and transport infrastructure in Kyiv.

But Kyiv said Moscow’s targets included a hospital in Kharkiv and residential buildings in the Dnipropetrovsk, Lviv, Vinnytsia, Sumy, and Chernihiv regions.

Three of Kyiv’s bridges were damaged in the overnight strikes, according to mayor Vitaly Klitschko, halting transport links connecting the two sides of the capital.

“All emergency services have done and will continue to do everything possible to mitigate the consequences of the attack as quickly as possible,” Klitschko said, adding that people should avoid traveling if possible.

The deadly attack came after Putin vowed revenge for Ukraine’s recent attacks on prized project, the strategic multibillion-dollar bridge linking Russia with the occupied Crimea.

“There will be no negotiations,” he said. “I believe our response, as I have just said, will be far more painful than what the Kyiv regime did to the bridge.”

The attacks have also knocked down power in several regions across Ukraine, just as temperatures are beginning to drop to bellow zero.

Several districts in Kyiv were without power due to the attack, according to energy company DTEK. Emergency power outages have also been implemented in the Lviv, Zakarpattia, and Ivano-Frankivsk regions, according to Ukrenergo, the country’s state-owned electricity operator, which called the situation in the power grid “extremely critical.”

CNN’s Nick Paton Walsh and Victoria Butenko contributed reporting.
