Best Amazon device deal

The 2026 Amazon Fire TV Stick HD is an easy, clutter-free way to bring smart streaming to an older 1080p TV or pack your favorite apps for a hotel stay. Its biggest upgrade is Direct Power, which lets you run the stick straight off your TV's USB port with the included USB-C cable instead of hunting for an open wall outlet, while its slimmer profile fits into an HDMI port without blocking the inputs next to it.

Once plugged in, you get WiFi 6 support for smoother Full HD streaming and Amazon's redesigned 2026 interface, letting you pin more apps and use Alexa+ on the voice remote to search naturally across services like Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, and free live TV. Just keep in mind that this entry-level model maxes out at 1080p resolution rather than 4K, and its latest software restricts app installs to the official Amazon Appstore.
