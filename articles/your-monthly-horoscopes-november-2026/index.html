Your Monthly Horoscopes: November 2026

TheTools · 01.11.2026 02:04 · source · permalink

November is the month where urge becomes action. It is the story of how the internal changes of October shape our future plans. We decide who we are and what parts of our lives need our attention and effort. November is where Venus and Mercury pass the baton to Mars.

The month begins in the shadow of two retrogrades: Venus and Mercury. Venus retrograde changes the source of meaning in our lives, while Mercury retrograde changes the tone of the narration. Both of these shifts occur in Scorpio, and so likely have to do with confronting some kind of shadow. Whether that is something we try to avoid, a part of ourselves or our relationships we have trouble owning or a yuckier part of reality we try to pretend doesn’t exist, we’ve likely had to spend some part of the last month with biohazard suits on, digging through topics we would usually prefer not to.

The first week of November gives some insight as to where to go from here. On November 4th, Venus harmonizes with Jupiter as Mercury lines up with the sun. The first of these alignments offers optimism and helps articulate a vision for a hopeful ‘best-case scenario.’ The second provides an ‘aha’ moment where the path to that moment becomes clearer, and sketches for a plan can be made.

On November 5th, Mars enters the pre-shadow of its upcoming retrograde that will define the beginning of 2027. The warrior (Mars) is taking cues from the head (Mercury) and the heart (Venus) as to what changes must be wrought in our lives, and begins foreshadowing the battles we will fight or negotiate through in the early parts of 2027. Pay attention. Little stuff now likely signals larger themes that are not going away.

The Scorpio new moon on the 8th gives a view of the next lunar month ahead and resets the Scorpio parts of our lives. Under the auspices of this moon, Mars and Venus join hands on the 9th, the lovers finally on the same team, sowing seeds of purpose together, so starkly different from their quarrel during October’s Libra new moon. It bodes well for collaboration during difficult circumstances.

On Friday the 13th, both Venus and Mercury station direct and end their retrogrades. Their synchronicity is significant, as it winds down related storylines and divides the month in two. Venus moves forward from her home sign of Libra, while Mercury integrates the secret workings of Scorpio. Their purposes renewed, communication, values and relationships should begin to move forward with increasing efficiency.

The drama shifts to the pileup of Mars, Jupiter and the South Node. Mars is the personal will, Jupiter expands and supports whatever is in contact with it and the South Node siphons old material away. Mars in Leo is easy to interpret—the brazen hero—while Jupiter and the South Node are tricky to guess at because they represent fundamentally opposite processes. Jupiter creates more, while the South Node reduces whatever it touches. It’s like the dentist pouring water in and sucking it out of your mouth at the same time. Whatever Mars is doing, it is likely to change things fast. Expect these shifts from the 11th–16th.

The sun wrestles with the Mars-Jupiter-South Node story from the 17th–19th, a week where we integrate that experience into our overall purpose and life focus.

On the 21st, the sun shifts gears into Sagittarius, and Uranus interrupts the party. There’s an exciting full moon on the 24th that highlights Uranus in Gemini, zooming in on the themes of liberation and rebellion it’s brought to that sector over the last seven months. It’s anything but stable. The following day, Mars enters Virgo. Mars in Virgo is less performative than Mars in Leo, but what he lacks in dramatic flair is made up for in an absolute focus on perfection and productivity. Mars in Virgo is the project manager and skilled artisan, relentless in pursuit of his craft or deadline.

The month ends sweetly with another harmonious Venus-Jupiter aspect, a steadying touchstone between the sun and Saturn, and Mercury leaving the echoes of its retrograde. Enjoy the Thanksgiving calm, as December gets rowdy again quickly.
