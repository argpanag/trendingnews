Barron County turkey farm HPAI positive

TheTools · 21.10.2026 09:19 · source · permalink

Oct 20, 2026

By Nicole Heslip

Read

A second detection of highly pathogenic avian influenza has been confirmed in a commercial poultry flock in northwest Wisconsin.

The Wisconsin Department of Agriculture, Trade and Consumer Protection tells Brownfield the commercial turkey flock in Barron County is a separate case from one earlier in September in the same county.

The agency is reminding livestock owners to register where animals are kept to help officials communicate with flock and herd owners during disease outbreaks.

The USDA says more than 220,000 birds have been impacted by the virus in the past 30 days, including in Minnesota, Iowa, and Indiana.
