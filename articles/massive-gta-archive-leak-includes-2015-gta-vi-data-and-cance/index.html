Massive GTA archive leak includes 2015 GTA VI data and cancelled DLC - VideoCardz.com

TheTools · 18.09.2026 18:19 · source · permalink

Rockstar’s 2022 breach reportedly included GTA VI prototypes dating back to 2015

A large Rockstar Games development archive containing GTA V source files, development tools and early GTA VI assets is spreading publicly. However, the files are not from a new Rockstar breach and were actually extracted during the 2022 hack.

The archive is around 190GB and contains the GTA V source code and tools that previously surfaced, along with a development build of GTA V for PS4 and Xbox One. It also contains cut single-player content, development tests and early GTA VI prototype assets.

Metadata from the included .ymap files reportedly dates the blockout assets to 2015. These are therefore early experiments created years before the current version of GTA VI.

The leaked GTA V build is also old. Rockstar’s active GTA V development moved to the “gta5/titleupdate” branch, which was not included. The leaked “gta5/dev_ng” development tree was effectively frozen around 2015.

Archive also contains cancelled GTA V content

The files include development material for Rockstar’s cancelled Liberty City project and assets connected to planned GTA V single-player DLC. Other discoveries include cut missions, vehicles, dialogue, development maps and content associated with the Agent Trevor DLC.
