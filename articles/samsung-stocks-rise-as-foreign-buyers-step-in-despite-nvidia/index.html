Samsung Electronics Co Ltd (SSNLF) shares rose 0.97% to 208,000 won at the November 12 regular-session close in Seoul, after reports on NVIDIA's leadership turned a 3.88% morning gain into a modest finish. The Kospi fell 0.61% to 5,342.73, ending a two-day rally that had added 7.1%.

Flows told the story. Foreign investors bought a net 744.9 billion won of Samsung while selling a net 872 billion won of SK hynix, according to FinanceFeeds, and options expiry added selling pressure as institutions sold a net 691.1 billion won across the Kospi. Samsung had jumped 3.57% to 206,000 won on Wednesday, the Korea JoongAng Daily reported.

Holding a gain on a down day for Korean chips suggests investors see Samsung as less tied to NVIDIA's memory orders than its rival. GuruFocus's Korean-listing page gives Samsung a GF Score of 91/100, with a perfect 10/10 financial-strength rank and 9/10 for profitability and momentum. At 208,000 won, the shares trade 10.0% above the GF Value of 189,070.42 won, a "Fairly Valued" reading, though the GF Value Rank is only 3/10. The next test is NVIDIA's November 17 results and whether its new leadership broadens its memory supplier base.
