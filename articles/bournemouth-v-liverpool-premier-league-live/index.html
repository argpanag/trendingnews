79 min Truffert’s cross has too much on it for Rayan, who puts the ball back into the box to no avail, Nyoni in the right place to clear it. Given how poor Liverpool’s midfield have been tonight, he can’t be far off a start; him Szoboszlai and an attacker has a much better balance than anything else we’ve seen so far this season.

77 min Bournemouth are dominating possession now as Liverpool protect the lead, and must get Brooks on the ball on the edge of the box. Of the players they have, he’s the one with the guile to make something happen, but they also need to get more men into the box so their crosses at least have men in position to attack them.

75 min Changes for Bournemouth, Cook, Brooks and Jebbison on for Smith, Christie and Tavernier.

74 min Bournemouth try another hopefully cross to no avail; they’ve lacked guile and quality today, as well as a bit of composure.

73 min Another cross into the box and again Jacquet does well, flicking an important header away. If Liverpool win, he’ll be player of the match.

72 min Changes for Liverpool, Nyoni and Munoz for Wirtz and Barcola as Iraola looks to protect what he has while retaining a threat on the counter.

70 min Gakpo prompts, sliding into the box for Isak, where Hill jumps into a terrific challenge, winning the ball when he might’ve conceded a penalty or goal and celebrating with zeal.

69 min “Florian Wirtz plays with such obvious joy that it is impossible not to warm to him, reckons Tony Hughes. “It remains possible that he is not quite good enough at the highest level but puts everything into his play on the field and is worth persevering with. Thoughts?”

I think he’s a luxury player and the game doesn’t really allow for that now at elite level.

67 min Scott, out on the left, sees Mac Allister coming and nips the ball down the line, wearing the inevitable foul; Alisson punches the free-kick away. Then, when the ball comes back in, Christie robbing MacAllister, performing the Zidane turn and finding Adams, he then moves to Evanilson, who shoots low … and Alisson saves.

66 min I meant to say, Isak was yellow-carded for celebrating his goal; back in Manchester, Semenyo put City 4-2 up on Sunderland, by Brobbey has just completed his hat-trick and pulled a goal back.

64 min Jacquet is quickly into a challenge, sending the ball up and away, picking up Isak, who turns, shoots low … and wide. As it turns out, he was offside, but the intervention by Jacquet was again excellent – he’s already better than Ibrahima Konate, an accident waiting to happen despite his physical gifts, and the player Man United thought they were getting with Leny Yoro.

62 min “Your sympathy for Wirtz and his dilemma exceeds that of Stephen Warnock,” advises Eric Peterson, “who suggested on the USA TV broadcast during the first half that, were Wirtz an academy player, he would’ve been withdrawn at half-time.”

That comment sounds like it comes with a pint of bitter. But yes, Wirtz has been granted a lot a opportunities – though, in fairness, his contribution to the goal ought not to be forgotten or underestimated.

61 min Gakpo flicks over his head – he’s feeling himself now – to Wirtz, who feeds inside to Kerkez. With Isak free and waiting, he opts to hammer a hopeful shot into the nearest available shins; displeasure is soon shared.

GOAL! AFC Bournemouth 0-1 Liverpool (Isak 58)

Gakpo does really well, bundling through two challenges down the right, for once going on the outside and his cross is a decent one, towards the near post, where Wirtz’s challenge on Silva sends the ball squirting over to Isak. Unmarked in the middle, he coolly passes into the far corner, and out of nothing Liverpool lead.

56 min Ahahaha, you’ve got to laugh: Jamie Carragher didn’t think the match would go like this. Or, in other words, he thinks Liverpool are better than they are.

They could well win, but there are various reasons they might not.

55 min Scott again, moving through midfield, and this time he goes left, to Christie, whose low shot scuds wide of the far post.

55 min “As an object,” says Ian Copestake, “the side picked is Liverpool’s most unreasonably priced receptacle. But perhaps a better narrative than judging Gakpo is whether Wirtz and Isak have to be picked until it is obvious that the cheaper lads are better suited. Or maybe Rio and Louis come on right now.”

I’d definitely be thinking about Ngumoha, and I’d also be wanting to see if Nyoni, excellent in pre-season, can solve the issue of midfield balanace. If he can take the ball off the keeper or defence and get Liverpool going, they’ll be a different team.

53 min Better from Bournemouth, Scott bursting through rhe middle and finding Rayan, who stands Kerkez up, slows him down, then dashes down the outside, drilling a low cross that Christie, on the slide, pokes wide. Rose’s men need try and create that matchup more often, because I don’t think Kerkez has the smarts or power to cope.

50 min Isak leathers an I-dont’t-know-what somewhere off towards Southbourne. I’m surprised he’s taking so long to get going in a Liverpool shirt – he, you thought, would be plug-and-play. But he’s not getting the ball early enough, and the runners aren’t helping him either, nor is he helping himself.

48 min “Based only on the evidence of this match,” says Alexander Whitney, “I see nothing about Wirtz to suggest he’s finally coming good this season. He still doesn’t seem to have learned where his teammates are going to be. Unable to acclimate to the new league/country? Wrong mentality? I’m sure Liverpool fans have more informed opinions.”

It’s hard playing as a no 10 because there’s contact coming from everywhere and so little space. Wirtz’s brain works really quickly, but his feet are a bit slower, and he doesn’t have the physicality to protect the ball in the meantime. It doesn’t look good, I’m afraid, but he’s still a lovely footballer – he just needs a less intense environment.

47 min Bournemouth win a free-kick out on the left, Tavernier mishitting one that sings out and has Alisson briefly swallowing his insides as he backpeddles, wondering if it’s hitting the far top corner … but there’s too much on it.

46 min We go again, no changes to either side.

HALF-TIME: Bournemouth 0-0 Liverpool

The ref adds on no time and I’m not surprised; no one needs any more of that. A drab 45, Alisson making one fine save to deny Evanilson; Iraola, in particular, has work to do, because his side lack coherence and intensity.

43 min At Elland Road, it’s Leeds 0-0 Palace, but Antoine Semenyo has just put City 3-2 up against Sunderland. Back with our game, a Szoboszlai corner is headed clear easily enough.

41 min “Harsh on Gakpo,” reckons Niall Mullen, “who has been Liverpool’s most creative player this season; not a strongly contested competition, but still.”

He’s not useless, obviously, but I bet they were dead excited when they thought City or Spurs would cough for him. The crossing and shooting into bodies is especially infuriating.

39 min Bournemouth look to up it again, but there’s been precious little quality in this half; in a way, Liverpool have got away with one, because they’ve been sluggish through midfield and attack, but Alisson and Jacquet have made sure they’re yet to be punished.

38 min Brobbey, Brian Brobbey, has scored again for Sunderland, finishing a lovely team goal that survives a VAR check; they and City are now level at 2-2.

36 min A nodded header through the centre and Christie is on to it – that was far too easy for Bournemouth – but Jacquet glides over to block the shot. He’s made an excellent start to his Liverpool career – he looks a player.

34 min This time he tries the knuckle-ball, the shot scooting low past the near post.

33 min Liverpool find Barcola down the left and he cuts inside to open a shooting lane, Petkovic diving by and having to save with his legs, then Wirtz and Gakpo both shoot in the follow-up, various bodies hurled in front to block. But Liverpool maintain pressure and win a free-kick 30 yards out, Szoboszlai again applying hands to lips.

32 min Back at City, Rayan Cherki shifts it quickly, gets a shot away, and his team lead Sunderland 2-1. I think he might be the most technical, natural footballer in the league, and is one of the game’s last mavericks.

31 min Bournemouth win a free-kick down the right and Tavernier sends in an outswinger … easily cleared.

30 min I’d much rather see Rio Ngumoha than Gakpo. He was fortunate, I think, to make the England squad, having done not much, a pick on talent not form, but he’s always asking questions of defenders. Gakpo asks questions of people’s patience.

28 min On Barcola, though, I like him, but I wonder if he’s really a £120m player. I don’t love his finishing, and though he can be lethal against a high line, how many of those will Liverpool face? He’s got room to improve, but I bet PSG were delighted to accept a fee of that magnitude for a player not a first choice.

27 min Ball slide in behind for Barcola and up goes the flag – though I think he was on.

26 min But Liverpool do look to build pressure, knocking the ball about in the Bournemouth half but without tempo. Then, when Kerkez loses patience, he boots into Adams and his team do as Michael Finnegan does.

24 min Isak’s barely had a kick so far; there’s not much balance in Liverpool’s midfield in terms of a pass-first player, and of the wingers, Gakpo is looking to score not provide. When Iraola finally settles on a first XI, I’m pretty sure this won’t be it.

22 min What’s going on with Liverpool’s shorts. I’m not sure why the 1986 away kit is one that needed recreating – the 1985 classic, sure – but the shorts need to red, not cerise.

19 min The resultant corner comes to nowt, but Bournemouth are inching their way on top. Oh, and Kerkez nails Wirtz with a block-tackle as they contest the same ball; that looked a sair yin for the now-limping German.

18 min Bournemouth are coming, Scott sliding a ball between Araujo and Jacquet for Tavernier, who dashes to the line and cuts back to the near post, where Evanilson flicks; Alisson, moving towards the far, does really well to halt momentum and tip around the post with a strong hand.

17 min First, Jacquet follows Evanilaon out wide and fouls him unnecessarily then, when the free-kick comes to nowt, Scott sidewinds towards the box but his pass into it is kicked away by Van Dijk. That’s the first time we’ve seen him in a dangerous area.

16 min Mac Allister slips to Wirtz down the line … and he allows the ball to run away from him. He’s not made much of a start here, careless in his work; generally, the players who translate best to the Prem come from Ligue 1 because the physicality is most comparable; the Bundesliga is very hit and miss.

14 min “Did Iraola just do a Ron Atkinson and sit down in the wrong dugout?” writes Dave Estherby. “Wasn’t paying full attention but it sure looked like it.”

I missed this, but sounds like a hilarious moment for tonight’s MOTD. Who says there are no characters in the game any more?
