Your Complete UK Release Schedule for 'MobLand' Season 2 on Paramount+

TheTools · 09.09.2026 14:44 · source · permalink

MobLand finally returns this month – here's the full release schedule for the second season

MobLand was renewed for a third season last week – which is some show of faith from Paramount+, seeing as the second hasn't even dropped yet.

Last spring, it all wrapped up with a lot of wit, a lot of stabbing and (spoiler alert) one big boss leaving prison. It's going to get even more chaotic from here on out – and we're very much seated.

Read on to learn everything you need to about MobLand season 2, including the release schedule.

What is MobLand about?

MobLand is a crime drama following a large crime family, based in London, who find themselves in an escalating conflict with a rival gang. Each family member is as violent, messy and volatile as the other – and it's the family's street-smart fixer who has to clean up their mess before the rivalry escalates into a full-on war.

Who is in the cast of MobLand?

The Paramount+ series boasts a gargantuan ensemble cast, including Tom Hardy as Harry (the family's fixer), Joanne Froggatt as his wife, Pierce Brosnan, Paddy Considine, Lara Pulver, Anson Boon, Jasmine Jobson and Helen Mirren as members of the criminal Harrigan family.

How Can I Watch MobLand Season 2?

The first episode of MobLand season two will arrive next Friday, 18 September, with episodes dropping weekly after that until Friday, 20 November. The first series is available to stream now in full on Paramount+, and the second will land on the same platform.
