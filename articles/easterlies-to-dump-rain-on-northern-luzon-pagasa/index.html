Easterlies to dump rain on Northern Luzon — PAGASA

TheTools · 13.09.2026 11:27 · source · permalink

The easterlies are expected to bring rains over extreme Northern Luzon on Monday, September 14, 2026, state weather bureau PAGASA said Sunday.

In its 4 p.m. bulletin, PAGASA said the easterlies are forecast to bring partly cloudy to cloudy skies with isolated rainshowers or thunderstorms over Batanes and Babuyan Islands.

Meanwhile, localized thunderstorms are expected to affect Metro Manila and the rest of the country, bringing partly cloudy to cloudy skies with isolated rainshowers or thunderstorms.

Light to moderate winds and slight to moderate coastal waters are expected in parts of the country, with waves running from 0.6 meters to 2.1 meters.

At the PAGASA Science Garden weather station in Diliman, Quezon City, the highest temperature recorded on Sunday reached 31.6 degrees Celsius at 2 p.m., while the lowest temperature was logged at 25.2 degrees Celsius at 5 a.m.

Sunrise in Metro Manila on Monday is at 5:45 a.m. —Lyjah Tiffany Bonzo/RF, GMA News
