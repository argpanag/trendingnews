Léa Seydoux Video Interview On 'Gentle Monster,' The Oscars & Her Career

TheTools · 18.11.2026 19:00 · source · permalink

Breaking News

Léa Seydoux is truly an international star.

The French icon made history in Cannes winning, along with her director and co-star, the Palme d’Or in 2013’s Blue Is The Warmest Color, not as the filmmaker but as an actress. That was unprecedented, but then again Seydoux has had a pretty unprecedented career.

She is of course a major star in France, but moviegoers around the world know her from a series of high profile global blockbusters including a couple of Bond films, Spectre and No Time To Die; opposite Tom Cruise in Mission Impossible: Ghost Protocol; Quentin Tarantino’s Inglourious Basterds; Ridley Scott’s Robin Hood; Woody Allen’s Midnight In Paris; Dune Part Two; and countless others including a few with Wes Anderson.

This year alone she has been seen on the festival circuit starting with Cannes in both Gentle Monster and The Unknown; and at Telluride in the wild Alpha Gang. It is her performance in the harrowing drama, Gentle Monster, a movie picked up by Netflix in Cannes that has been sparking talk about a first possible Oscar nomination for the long overdue star.

Seydoux joins me today for this edition of my Deadline video series, The Actor’s Side and we talk about all of the above and much more. To watch our conversation just click on the link above.
