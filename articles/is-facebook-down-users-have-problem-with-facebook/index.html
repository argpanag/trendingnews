Is Facebook down? Users have problem with Facebook

TheTools · 21.09.2026 04:46 · source · permalink

If you're unable to get on Facebook, you're not alone.

According to Downdetector.com there are more than 5,000 reports of issues on Facebook on Sunday night, and is Facebook down is trending on Google.

The reports poppe up just before 9 p.m. EST.

There is no word on when it will be fixed. The biggest problems are with the website, although a few others are reporting problems with the app.
