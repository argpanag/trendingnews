62 min: Bournemouth cut through Newcastle easily and Scott’s shot from distance is blocked, all the pace taken out of it before Hornicek picks up.

61 min: Looks like we’re about to see the new exciting summer signing Fernandez-Pardo.

60 min: From the corner, Newcastle work it to Hall who tries an ambitious curler from 30 yards but it’s way off target. The hosts are preparing a double substitution.

59 min: Adams exacts some revenge on Wissa and bundles the DR Congo striker to the ground, giving away a free-kick. Barnes teases in a cross from a central/right area and it’s headed behind for a corner.

57 min: Wissa blocks off Adams and becomes the second player to be booked. A frustrated Jaissle pounds the floor in frustration. Bournemouth attack and Truffert’s cross goes straight out.

56 min: Silva tries a ball forward with the outside of his boot but it comes off a different part of his foot and goes out tamely for a throw. Hopefully he doesn’t play golf.

55 min: Truffert wins a corner down the left which Scott wanders over to take. He takes it short to Truffert whose ball in is headed clear by Hall. A waste.

54 min: Newcastle trying to build up a head of steam now as Bournemouth rather feel their way into the second half.

52 min: So close for Newcastle. Wissa burrows forwards down the right and crosses, Elanga flicks it on and Willock’s angled header from close range just drops the wrong side of the Bournemouth post from a Newcastle point of view.

51 min: Hall shields his eyes from the sun after a high ball drops from the skies. Rayan runs into him and the Newcastle left-back wins a free-kick.

50 min: Evanilson, who had a strong first 45, is back on his feet again. Play resumes with Newcastle in possession.

48 min: A break in play as Evanilson goes down clutching his face.

47 min: Willock is flagged offside after a sprinting into the box and feeding Barnes. Fairly clear decision.

47 min: Jaissle has removed his smart jacket, like a man pacing with intent, ready to hit the dancefloor at a wedding.

46 min: Newcastle get the second half in play. No changes. The xG in that first half was Newcastle 0.13 Bournemouth 1.38. Newcastle have faced 57 shots so far this season, 13 coming from Bournemouth in the first half. A tad alarming!

HALF TIME: Newcastle 1-2 Bournemouth

Bournemouth lead at the break and deservedly so. Jaissle will have plenty to say although that Barnes curler has given the hosts fresh optimism.

45 min +3: Bournemouth’s press continues to stifle Newcastle and Thiaw flaps, giving away possession. A cross from Truffert is well dealt with by Miley as Rayan sniffs an opportunity.

45 min +2: Barnes tries to pick out Elanga with a cross but Petrovic plucks that one from the skies.

45 min +1: Can Newcastle find something before the break? They’re hogging possession now but can’t really get Wissa into the game.

44 min: Hall launches a free-kick to the edge of the box but Silva heads away. Kluivert is fouled by Gonzalez as he tries to break. The visitors will be glad to see this through to half-time now. Three minutes to add on.

42 min: Much more intent from Newcastle now. That second Bournemouth goal has really sparked them into action and, of course, they’ve already halved the deficit.

40 min: Rayan’s cross hits Hall in the chest inside the six-yard box and Hornicek has to be alert. The ball had probably gone out before the Brazilian played it in though.

39 min: Blimey, that was all a bit crazy. Glenn Hoddle’s 3-3 prediction could be spot on.

GOAL! Newcastle 1-2 Bournemouth (Barnes 37)

Before the mood really drops, Newcastle hit back straight away. Just 27 seconds from the kick off, Barnes gets the ball down the left from a Hall pass, cuts inside and curls a beauty into the top corner past Petrovic. What a response!

GOAL! Newcastle 0-2 Bournemouth (Thiaw o.g. 35)

It had been coming! Bournemouth break and Kluivert’s thumping effort from distance hits the post and rebounds off the goalkeeper. Scott follows up and his cross/shot off Thiaw deflects up and over Hornicek. But….

33 min: Both sides cough up possession before a cross from Dedic on the right is easy for Petrovic to pluck out of the air.

32 min: The near post corner is sliced behind by Elanga for another. Tavernier has another go and this time goes short. Bournemouth work the ball across to Truffert but his left footer from distance is charged down by Willock. But… the ball comes back to Bournemouth. That’s the story of his half.

31 min: Kluivert bursts into space and pings one from 25 yards. It deflects off Botman for a corner but that could easily have wrong-footed Hornicek and gone in.

30 min: Silva looks a little unshaken as he runs his fingers through his immaculate hair. On we go.

28 min: Ouch! Silva goes up for a header and is clattered into by Elanga. It’s a booking. Shoulder on head. Elanga not happy but it was a bit clumsy.
