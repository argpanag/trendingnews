Flames loan Jonathan Huberdeau to AHL for conditioning stint

TheTools · 17.11.2026 21:37 · source · permalink

Calgary Flames forward Jonathan Huberdeau is making his way back to competitive hockey, but will begin his comeback with a conditioning stint with the AHL’s Calgary Wranglers, who share the Scotiabank Saddledome with the NHL club. 

Under the terms of the CBA, a player who has been out with a long-term injury, defined as missing at least 24 days and 10 games, can be sent to the AHL on a conditioning loan. The loan is limited to three games or six games, whichever is longer, but can be extended by two additional games at request. 

While he is with the Wranglers, Huberdeau won’t count towards Calgary’s active NHL roster, but will count towards the NHL salary cap. 

The 33-year-old Huberdeau underwent hip surgery in the spring and has not played a competitive game since Feb. 4, when he scored in a 4-3 Flames win over the Edmonton Oilers. Before his injury, he posted 10 goals and 15 assists in 50 games with the Flames, bringing his NHL career totals to 263 goals, 544 assists and 807 points in 962 games since making his debut with the Florida Panthers in the 2012-13 season. 

“I’m feeling pretty good. It’s a big surgery,” Huberdeau said in September. “There’s no pain anymore, which is nice to deal with. Just now, the goal is to come back and be 100%.”

Now with the Wranglers, he could return at home on Tuesday and Wednesday night against the Coachella Valley Firebirds, before the team heads on the road to take on the Laval Rocket on Saturday and Sunday. 

If his recovery and form-building remain on track, he could be with the Flames as soon as next week, as they enjoy a mediocre start to the 2026-27 season, with 19 points in their first 21 games and four short of the final Wild Card spot in the Western Conference.
