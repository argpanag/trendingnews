Isaiah Stewart (knee) ruled out for Friday

Isaiah Stewart (knee) has been ruled out for Friday night's game against the Oklahoma City Thunder. (NBA Injury Report)

Fantasy Impact:

The big man suffered a knee injury on Wednesday and will not be available to play on Friday night as a result. It remains to be seen how long he is going to be sidelined as he recovers, and fantasy managers should expect more work for Quinten Post moving forward.
