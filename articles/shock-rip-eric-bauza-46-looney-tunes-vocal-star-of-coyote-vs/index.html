Shock RIP Eric Bauza, 46, Looney Tunes Vocal Star of "Coyote vs Acme" Dead from Heart Attack at Top of Career Animation Legend - Showbiz411

TheTools · 26.10.2026 03:49 · source · permalink

Share

This is tragic.

Eric Bauza, the modern Mel Blanc, has died at age 46.

Bauza was the voice of the animated characters in “Coyote vs. Acme,” but his career went well beyond that. I mentioned him when the trailer was released. He’s the real star of the movie.

I’m told he died of a heart attack. Condolences to family, friends, and legions of fans.

Bauza’s many accolades included five Daytime Emmy nominations. He was beloved in the animation world, and worked literally non stop. His IMDB credit list is stunningly long.
