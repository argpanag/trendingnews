GTA 6 cheat codes: There are cheats, but there's a catch

TheTools · 18.11.2026 17:07 · source · permalink

Throughout their storied history, the Grand Theft Auto games have usually allowed players to skip the stuff they don't like by using cheat codes. There have been codes to inflate in-game wallets, unlock every gun, and even spawn the best vehicles out of thin air. This time, GTA 6 cheat codes take the franchise in a new direction.

Developer Rockstar Games has, by all accounts, created a GTA game that's at least trying to be a little more serious, sincere, and heartfelt than its predecessors. As such, you cannot simply bust out the "all guns" cheat from the start of the game and go on a rampage. According to PC Gamer, the game does have cheats, but they are only accessible after completing all 91 main story missions.

6 Yes, you have to roll credits on the story to use the GTA 6 cheats. Don't get mad at us, get mad at Rockstar.

PC Gamer confirmed that these cheats can't be used in an early-game save, and activating them will prevent the player from saving from that point on.

What's even more interesting: Beating the game doesn't immediately let you use the cheats in question; it merely lets you see a list of them, each with a corresponding in-game challenge that needs to be completed before they can be used. Once a cheat is unlocked, it can then be activated by opening the in-game phone, holding L1 and pressing R1 (or LB and RB on Xbox), and then inputting specific button combos.

For example, the "max ammo" cheat (which presumably just fills up all of your guns) requires the player to win 20 bronze medals or better at shooting ranges. If you want infinite ammo, you'll have to win 25 silver medals or better. To access every explosive weapon, you need to throw 100 grenades.

Each cheat's corresponding in-game challenge is related to what the cheat does, which is a very different approach from how they've worked in the past. You can't say Rockstar isn't trying new things.

So, while you can find lists of cheat codes online, they won't do you any good until you beat the game — at which point you'll get the full list.
