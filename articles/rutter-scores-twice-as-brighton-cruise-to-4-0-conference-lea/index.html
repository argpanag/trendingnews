Rutter scores twice as Brighton cruise to 4-0 Conference League win

TheTools · 15.10.2026 22:16 · source · permalink

Georginio Rutter took his chance to shine – and thanked the man who teed him up.

The Albion striker started and scored twice in a 4-0 win over Kauno Zalgiris in the opening round of the Conference League phase.

Rutter has had a frustrating start to the campaign, having looked more motivated than ever in pre-season and on the opening day against Aston Villa.

But he made the breakthrough here on 41 minutes, just as a few fans might have been getting a little nervous.

Their side had struggled to play through a congested zone around the edge of the box.

They had also been denied by keeper Tomas Svedkauskas from a Pascal Gross free-kick and Jack Hinshelwood header.

When Rutter tucked away a very composed finish from an Ibrahim Osman cutback, the flag went up to indicate the ball had gone over the byline.

But a VAR check found differently and Albion had their opening goal.

Rutter’s fancy footwork and low cross allowed Hinshelwood to add the insurance of a second goal in added time.

It might also have made it easier for Fabian Hurzeler to withdraw Lewis Dunk and Olivier Boscagli at the break.

The second half was all about how many as Albion attacked the North Stand.

There were two nice moments as they made it three, then four.

Rutter pointed to Osman after his unselfish ball inside set up the Frenchman’s second on 53 minutes following a superb through pass by Malick Yalcouye.

Then debutant Younes Ibrahim, who had only just gone on, crossed with precision for Matt O’Riley to head the fourth midway through the half.

Svedkauskas made some decent saves and there was no goal for Promise David, who was denied by a good block.

But otherwise it was very much job done for the Seagulls.

Albion: Verbruggen; Costinha, Svoboda, Dunk (Vuskovic 46), Boscagli (De Cuyper 46); Gross (Ayari 57), Yalcouye; O’Riley, Hinshelwood (Promise 57), Osman (Ibrahim 67); Georginio. Unusud subs: Steele, McGill, Chema, Kostoulas, Kadioglu, Gomez, Nti.
