Jailer 2 OTT Release: Where to Watch Rajinikanth's Movie After Its Theatrical Run

TheTools · 15.10.2026 14:15 · source · permalink

Rajinikanth is back as Tiger Muthuvel Pandian in Jailer 2, the sequel to his 2023 blockbuster directed by Nelson Dilipkumar. With the film arriving in cinemas on October 15, 2026, attention is already turning to when audiences will be able to watch it at home. According to reports, the sequel could make its way to OTT sooner than some viewers might expect, although its digital release plans are yet to be officially confirmed.

According to reports, Jailer 2 is reportedly headed to Amazon Prime Video following its theatrical run. The film is said to have secured a digital deal, though the streaming platform and the terms of the agreement have not been officially confirmed by the makers.

The report suggests that the film could arrive on OTT roughly four weeks after its theatrical release. If that timeline holds, viewers may be able to stream Rajinikanth's action thriller around mid-November 2026. However, the actual premiere date will depend on the final release arrangement, and audiences should wait for an official announcement before marking their calendars.

The movie has made a promising start at the box office, backed by strong advance bookings ahead of its October 15 release. Early estimates reported by The Times of India put its India net collection at ₹24.09 crore during the initial reporting period on opening day, with the Tamil version driving much of the early business.

The reported four-week gap between the theatrical and digital releases has drawn attention due to its potential implications for the film's Hindi-dubbed version. According to reports, multiplex chains such as PVR INOX and Cinepolis typically require an eight-week window for Hindi films before they can be released on streaming platforms. Reports state that these chains have decided not to screen the film in their theatres outside the southern states.

Produced by Sun Pictures, Jailer 2 reunites Rajinikanth with director Nelson Dilipkumar and composer Anirudh Ravichander. Rajinikanth reprises his role as Muthuvel Pandian, the retired police officer whose quiet life conceals a much more formidable side.

The ensemble cast includes Ramya Krishnan, Yogi Babu, SJ Suryah and Vidya Balan. The sequel follows the success of the first film, which combined action, comedy and a star-studded cast to become a major draw for audiences.

For now, the reported Amazon Prime Video release and mid-November streaming window should be treated as provisional. An official announcement will clarify when and where viewers can watch Jailer 2 online.
