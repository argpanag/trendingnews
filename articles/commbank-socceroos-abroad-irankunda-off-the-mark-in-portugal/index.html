CommBank Socceroos Abroad: Irankunda off the mark in Portugal with Cup brace

TheTools · 23.11.2026 12:00 · source · permalink

7 min read 23 November 2026

Nestory Irankunda scored his first goals for Sporting CP on the weekend, putting away a second-half brace to help them to a 4-0 win in the Taça de Portugal.

Elsewhere, Paul Okon-Engstler played a key role in helping FC Köln secure an away draw with Bayern Munich, while Harry Souttar put in a massive shift for Leicester City.

The Headliners

Irankunda off the mark in style

Nestory Irankunda showed off his famous backflip celebration for the first time in Portugal, scoring a brace for Sporting CP in their 4-0 victory over Uniao Da Serra in the Taça de Portugal.

His first goal was a driving strike from the edge of the penalty area that clipped the inside of the post to roll in. The second was cool, calm and composed, carefully playing it around the goalkeeper after being played into space behind the defence and passing it into the back of the net for his second.

It capped off a player-of-the-match performance from the 20-year-old after creating a number of opportunities in the first half that were not converted by his teammates.

The goals are set to be a confidence booster to the young forward in a season where he is establishing himself as a regular first team starter at one of the biggest clubs in Portugal.

Result: Sporting CP 4 - 0 Uniao Da Serra

Okon-Engstler leaves Allianz Arena with point

Paul Okon-Engstler started and played 77 minutes in FC Köln's midfield as they frustrated the German champions, leaving Bayern Munich's home ground with a point.

Okon-Engstler had 83% passing accuracy, made three tackles and three interceptions, and won four out of six duels in the draw.

It's a big result for a club like Köln, who are three points above the relegation zone. It is also another feather in the cap of Okon-Engstler, with the 21-year-old improving every game and becoming a consistent starter in Germany's top flight.

Result: Bayern Munich 1 - 1 FC Köln

Souttar puts in shift for Leicester

Harry Souttar put in another tremendous performance for Leicester City, playing a key role in their 1-0 victory over Huddersfield Town.

The big defender had 120 touches, completed 91/99 passes, made 17 defensive contributions including 11 headed clearances, and won 10/13 of his duels.

The win moves Leicester up to tenth. They are beginning to recover after a tough start to the season, and looking ahead to push for promotion spots back to the EFL Championship as the season progresses.

Result: Huddersfield Town 0 - 1 Leicester City

Other Headlines

Riley McGree's Middlesbrough finished the weekend top of the EFL Championship table after a 2-0 victory over Bristol City. McGree started and played 59 minutes in the confidence boosting win. It is still tight at the top of the table, and every point will count as they look to hold onto that spot throughout the campaign.

Result: Middlesbrough 2 - 0 Bristol City

Alessandro Circati continues to establish himself at SL Benfica, playing the full game in the team's 3-1 win against Torreense in the Taça de Portugal. The 23-year-old is a regular starter in cup and continental competitions for Benfica and is on track to force his way into the first choice starting eleven in the future.

Result: Torreense 1 - 3 SL Benfica

Alex Robertson's good form continues at Cardiff City, playing 85 minutes and picking up an assist against Norwich City. It came in a losing side, however, with Norwich coming from behind to defeat them 2-1.

Result: Norwich City 2 - 1 Cardiff City
