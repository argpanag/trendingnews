Bompastor issues Melvine Malard injury update plus news on trio

TheTools · 13.09.2026 11:26 · source · permalink

Sonia Bompastor has confirmed Melvine Malard will not be fit to face her former club Manchester United this weekend.

The attacker picked up a knock in training last week, missing the 1-1 draw with Aston Villa that got our WSL season up and running.

And Bompastor has confirmed Sunday’s trip to the North-West will come too soon for the French international.

‘Unfortunately she won’t be able to return to Man United and play the game,’ said Bompastor.

‘She’s close to returning, but probably next weekend.’

Mayra Ramirez, Manaka Matsukubo and Nathalie Bjorn also missed our opening league fixture, and Bompastor provided the latest on that trio, with the most positive news regarding the Swedish defender.

‘All of them won’t be available to play this game,’ said Bompastor.

‘Nat Bjorn is starting to train fully with the team so she will be travelling with us. We will see if she has minutes or not.’


Despite their injuries, it was an exciting week for Matsukubo and Malard, both of whom were nominated for the Ballon d’Or award. Bompastor was thrilled about that achievement and can’t wait to welcome them both back to the pitch.

‘It’s a good look on us in terms of the transfer window this summer! I’m proud of them. It’s really good to be nominees in this list. The Ballon d’Or recognisation is huge in the women’s game.

‘We celebrated them for those nominations. Two of them are new signings, for now they are not on the pitch but when they join the team it will be a big help for us.’
