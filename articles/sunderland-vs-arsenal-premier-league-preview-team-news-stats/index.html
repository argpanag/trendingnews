  16. 'We must make Stadium of Light a fortress'published at 19:48 BST
    19:48 BST

    Sunderland v Arsenal (20:00 BST)

    Alex Brotherton
    BBC Sport journalist at Stadium of Light

    Sunderland fans have a reputation for being loud at the Stadium of Light, and Black Cats boss Regis Le Bris has used his programme notes to call on fans to bring the noise for the visit of the Premier League champions.

    "A key challenge for us this season is to make the Stadium of Light a fortress once again," Le Bris said.

    "We have seen time after time how powerful we can be when the team and supporters are fully aligned and pulling in the same direction.

    "Your energy, belief and support can make a real difference. We will need that connection today from the first whistle to the last."

  17. 'We have to stay true to our ideas'published at 19:47 BST
    19:47 BST

    Sunderland v Arsenal (20:00 BST)

    Sunderland boss Regis Le Bris talking to BBC Radio Newcastle about Arsenal:

    "This season, they are flying. They are one of the best teams in Europe. Five wins in a row so the run is really impressive. We learned that to be competitive in this kind of game you have to be yourself. The more you have this underdog mentality, togetherness, connection with the fans, intensity until the end - it is very important. The flexibility and clarity of the game model [is important] because they are so adaptive. We have to adapt in real time."

    "It is not possible to be perfect but we have to stay true to our ideas. When you face a really strong opponent you can be a bit shy. The best learning from last season was that if we are ourselves - no regret. They will find solutions but we are getting better with flexibility and how we adapt when something unexpected happens. It will be challenging but I am very excited to experience this situation again."

    Sunderland v Arsenal (20:00 BST)

    Sunderland

    In September 2021 Sunderland hosted Accrington Stanley in League One. Five years on not only are the Black Cats getting ready to play at home to Arsenal, they're also about return to European football for the first time since 1973.

    These are exciting times for Regis Le Bris' side, who host Dutch side AZ Alkmaar in the Europa League on Wednesday (20:00 BST).

    It will be their first taste of competitive European action for 53 years when Sunderland played at their old Roker Park home.

Match Facts

  • Sunderland have won just one of their last 29 Premier League meetings with Arsenal (D10 L18), and are winless in 17 (D6 L11) since a 1-0 victory in November 2009 after Darren Bent’s goal.
