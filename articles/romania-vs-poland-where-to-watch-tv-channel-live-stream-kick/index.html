Match Preview

Romania host Poland at Arena Națională in Bucharest in UEFA Nations League B Group 4, with both sides in desperate need of points after difficult starts to the campaign.

Gheorghe Hagi's Romania have endured a brutal run of form in the Nations League, losing their last four matches in the competition. Their most recent outing ended in a 1-0 defeat to Sweden, and they were thrashed 6-0 by Poland just days before that. With Romania sitting bottom of the group, this home fixture carries serious weight.

Poland arrive in Bucharest in a stronger position but far from comfortable. Jan Urban's side beat Romania 6-0 in their earlier meeting this campaign, yet they have also lost twice in the group, including a 1-0 defeat to Bosnia and Herzegovina in their last Nations League outing. They sit third in Group 4.

Poland's recent results outside the Nations League show a squad still searching for consistency. A 2-2 draw with Nigeria and a loss to Ukraine in friendlies earlier this year pointed to defensive fragility, and Sweden beat them 3-2 in World Cup qualification. Urban will want a response on the road.

Romania's only win in their last five matches across all competitions came against Wales in June, a 2-1 friendly victory. Hagi's side have conceded 11 goals in their four Nations League matches alone, a record that demands urgent attention before this fixture.

With Romania's Nations League survival at stake and Poland looking to consolidate their standing, this Bucharest fixture has the feel of a match that could define both teams' campaigns. Read on for everything you need to know about where to watch and how to follow the action live.
