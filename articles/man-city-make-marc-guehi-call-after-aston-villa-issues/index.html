Man City make Marc Guehi call after Aston Villa issues

TheTools · 27.10.2026 11:44 · source · permalink

Enzo Maresca does not think that Marc Guehi needs a break despite the Manchester City defender making a number of errors in recent weeks. Guehi has been culpable for four goals conceded in matches with PSG, Ipswich and Aston Villa in the last fortnight but could still start against Brighton in the Carabao Cup on Wednesday.

City have had concerns over Guehi's fitness given his long season last year for Crystal Palace, City and then England. Going all the way to the third-place play-off in the World Cup meant that the centre-back barely had any pre-season with City before being thrown straight into games.

Guehi had started the season very well for Enzo Maresca, including a game being trusted in defensive midfield, but errors have crept into the last few matches and he was withdrawn at half-time at Villa Park on Saturday. However, Maresca did not include Guehi in a list of five players that have been allowed to miss the Carabao Cup game.

The fact that Ruben Dias and Josko Gvardiol are in that handful of players leaves City short of options in defence so Guehi could play again. Maresca is confident that the player has the legs and the mindset to get straight back into action.

"Especially in this moment, I am completely happy with Marc, because in the way he reacts it is fantastic, he is a top guy. He is one of the captains, he is a leader and I love Marc, to be honest," said Maresca.

"Same as Gigio, when they make some mistakes it is recognised. Marc is aware now but he has played all the minutes since the start of the season with us and the international team. In the last two weeks he didn't play the Champions League, 45 minutes the other day and so he is completely OK."
