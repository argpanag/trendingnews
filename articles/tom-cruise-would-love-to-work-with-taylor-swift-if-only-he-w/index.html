Tom Cruise Would Love To Work With Taylor Swift … if Only He Were Capable of Working With Female Directors

TheTools · 26.09.2026 19:45 · source · permalink

“It would be amazing to work with her on anything,” Cruise said. Oh really?… Even a movie?

Tom Cruise, new pal of newlyweds Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce, implied that he would love to be in a film directed by Swift in the future. However … I can’t help but notice a funny little pattern in his decades-long career in cinema: He has never once been in a feature film directed by a woman.

The story of his newfound friendship with the Swift-Kelces goes that last week, he attended one of Kelce’s games and sat in the coveted family-and-friends box next to Swift, where she allegedly taught him the rules of football. Later that week, Cruise appeared on Kelce’s podcast, New Heights, and explained his 6-hour prosthetic process. They were all becoming fast friends. Then on Tuesday, at the UK premiere of his upcoming film Digger, Cruise was asked by a reporter whether he would ever star in one of Swift’s music videos, which often feature A-list actors—the most recent example being Dakota Johnson and Collin Farrell, who will star in a Swift-directed video for “Patient Zero.” Hey, maybe he would star in a Taylor Swift joint! Although she doesn’t have any songs about parachuting off the Burj Khalifa. (Maybe in The Vault…).

“I make movies. Let’s go make a movie,” he replied to the reporter. Cruise does not stoop to the level of music videos. Got it. But then, he provides a more diplomatic soundbite: “I think she’s such a talent in anything she does. She’s absolutely brilliant. She’s a brilliant writer. Brilliant.”

Then he says, “If she ever decided to direct, she could be a director of feature films. I know she does her videos and she does a beautiful job, so it would be amazing to work with her on anything.” Oh really? Work with her on anything?…even…a movie?

Swift’s directorial ambitions are also top of mind as she is set to receive the Inaugural MTV VMA Artist Director Honors award this Sunday at the VMAs. So would Cruise, who has worked exclusively with male directors for all 50+ of his films, change everything for Tay?

“I respect her, her family, and her husband. They’re very magical,” he concludes—a Swiftie-approved and PR-approved response.

While Cruise has never worked with a female director, it’s also worth noting that he has never been cast in a movie directed by a woman … maybe because they don’t want him or his infamous intensity on their set? Something to consider.
