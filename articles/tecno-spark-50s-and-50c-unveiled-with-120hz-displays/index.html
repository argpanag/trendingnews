Tecno Spark 50s and 50C unveiled with 120Hz displays

TheTools · 16.10.2026 23:22 · source · permalink

Login

Sagar 16 October 2026

Tecno Android Announcement

The Tecno Spark 50 series includes three smartphones - the Spark 50 4G, the Spark 50 5G, and the Spark 50 Pro. As promised, the Chinese brand unveiled the Spark 50s today as the newest member of the Spark 50 lineup. But alongside, it also did a surprise unveiling of the Spark 50C.

Let's talk about the Tecno Spark 50s first. It is powered by the Dimensity 6400 SoC, runs Android 16-based HiOS 16 out of the box, and comes with up to 8GB RAM and 128GB of storage, expandable by up to 2TB via a microSD card.

The smartphone features a 6.78" HD+ 120Hz LCD on the front with a centered punch-hole for the 8MP selfie camera, and around the back is a single 50MP primary camera joined by an LED flash.

Keeping the entire package up and running is a 6,500 mAh battery with 45W fast charging support. It also supports reverse wired charging and bypass charging.

Other highlights of the Tecno Spark 50s include an IP64 Pro rating, MIL-STD-810H certification, a side-mounted fingerprint scanner, a dedicated microSD card slot, all-scenario noise cancellation, and a feature called No Network Communication, which allows users to make calls and send messages without cellular coverage or Wi-Fi connectivity within a distance of up to 1.5 km.

The Spark 50s also comes with UPS 3.0 technology, enabling up to 2x faster UPI payments.

The Spark 50s is offered in Breeze Blue and Burgundy Red colors, with two memory options - 6GB/128GB and 8GB/128GB, priced at INR19,999 and INR22,999, respectively. It will go on sale in India starting October 20 through Amazon.in and offline retail stores.

Let's talk about the Tecno Spark 50C now. It's a 4G smartphone having the Helio G81 SoC under the hood, paired with 4GB RAM. The smartphone comes with up to 256GB of storage and runs Android 16.

The Spark 50C is built around a 6.78" HD+ 120Hz LCD and can be operated even with wet or oily hands. For photography, the smartphone has two cameras - a 13MP camera on the rear and an 8MP camera on the front.

Fueling the Tecno Spark 50C is a 5,200 mAh battery with 15W fast charging support. Other features of the Spark 50C include dual stereo speakers, IP68 and IP69 ratings, AI call Noise Cancellation, and an IR blaster. It also comes with FreeLink 2.0, which allows users to communicate through calls and text messages without a network within a distance of up to 1.5 km.


Tecno Spark 50C's color options

The Tecno Spark 50C comes in Burgundy Red, Forge Orange, Basalt Titanium, and Eclipse Black colors, with three memory options - 4GB/64GB, 4GB/128GB, and 4GB/256GB. Tecno is yet to reveal its pricing and availability details.
