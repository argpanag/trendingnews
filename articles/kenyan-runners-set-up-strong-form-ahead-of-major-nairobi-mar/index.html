Kenyan runners set up strong form ahead of major Nairobi marathon – Channel Africa

TheTools · 11.09.2026 05:53 · source · permalink

Kenyan runners Victor Kiplagat and Millicent Kemunto expressed strong confidence in their preparations for the upcoming Standard Chartered Nairobi Marathon after sweeping the half-marathon titles at the Parklands Sports Club warm-up event.

 

Kiplagat took the men’s 21km title in 1 hour, 10 minutes, and 10 seconds, while Kemunto claimed the women’s crown in 1 hour, 22 minutes, and 46 seconds.

 

The warm-up race, which featured events across 6km, 10km, 15km, and 21km distances, drew over 2 000 athletes testing their fitness ahead of the main flagship event scheduled for October 25, 2026.

 

As part of the nationwide build-up towards the World Athletics Label Road race, organisers will also host a satellite run in Mombasa on October 18.
