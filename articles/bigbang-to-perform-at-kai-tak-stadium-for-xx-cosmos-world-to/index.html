BIGBANG to Perform at Kai Tak Stadium for XX: COSMOS World Tour in November - GeneOnline News

Organizers officially announced the Hong Kong stop of the BIGBANG 2026-2027 world tour, titled “XX: COSMOS,” on September 8, 2026. The concert will take place at the Kai Tak Stadium this November, marking a return to the city for the group as part of their international tour schedule.

TME live and ELF ASIA serve as the joint organizers for the event. This performance represents a segment of the broader “XX: COSMOS” world tour, which spans the 2026 and 2027 calendar years. The announcement confirms the venue and the partnership between the two production entities, though further details regarding specific dates and ticketing information remain forthcoming.
