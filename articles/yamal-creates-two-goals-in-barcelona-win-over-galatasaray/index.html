Yamal Creates Two Goals in Barcelona Win Over Galatasaray

TheTools · 13.10.2026 20:50 · source · permalink

A decisive attacking display from Lamine Yamal helped Barcelona secure a 3-1 advantage against Galatasaray at Nef Stadium on October 13, 2026, as the Spanish winger created two second-half goals with individual skill and precise passing.

According to 365Scores, Yamal changed the match after halftime by assisting Fermín López and Dani Olmo, helping Hansi Flick's team build a two-goal lead in the Champions League fixture.

According to Okdiario.com, Yamal's contribution to the third goal came after he moved toward the byline, escaped his marker with a rabona dribble, recovered the ball near the line and delivered a pass for Anthony Gordon.

The assist for Fermín López arrived earlier in the second half, with 365Scores reporting that the midfielder headed in Yamal's accurate delivery to give Barcelona the advantage after Galatasaray had pushed the visitors.

Yamal then produced another attacking moment when he created the opportunity finished by Dani Olmo, according to 365Scores. The performance reinforced his influence in Barcelona's attack during the match in Istanbul.

Okdiario.com reported that Barcelona faced a difficult opening period at Nef Stadium, where Galatasaray scored first and later had another goal ruled out for a narrow offside decision before the Spanish side responded.

The match was played at Nef Stadium, which has a listed capacity of 53,000 spectators, according to 365Scores. The result came in the Champions League meeting between Galatasaray and Barcelona on Tuesday, October 13, 2026.
