MATCH PREVIEW: Inter Miami CF Takes on San Diego FC for the First Time on Sunday | Inter Miami CF

TheTools · 20.09.2026 23:57 · source · permalink

Clear Bag Policy

To provide a safer environment for the public and to expedite guest entry into the stadium, Nu Stadium policy limits and restricts the size and type of bags that are permitted into the stadium during any events taking place. We encourage guests NOT to bring any bags, however, the following will be permitted after proper screening:

  • One bag that is clear plastic, vinyl or PVC and do not exceed 12” x 6” 12” or a one-gallon clear plastic freezer bag (Ziplock bag or similar).
  • Small clutch bags, approximately the size of a hand (no larger than 4.5” x 6.5”), with or without a handle or strap, may be carried into the stadium along with one of the clear bag options.
  • Any medically necessary items must be pre-approved by Stadium management. Please contact guestservices@intermiamicf.com to be considered for approval. All items will be subject to proper inspection at entry gate.
  • Diapers and wipes can be carried in a clear bag. Each member of a family including children will be able to carry an approved clear bag, providing ample storage capacity.

Consult Nu Stadium's complete Clear Bag Policy HERE.

Past Match

Inter Miami closes out a three-match week with San Diego's visit after being crowned 2026 Michelob ULTRA Campeones Cup Champions on Wednesday.

Inter Miami secured a 2-0 victory over LIGA MX side Cruz Azul on Wednesday evening in the 2026 Michelob ULTRA Campeones Cup to clinch a fifth title in Club history. Goals from captain Leo Messi, who made history in the process by becoming the first Inter Miami player to reach 100 goals, and midfielder Casemiro secured the trophy for the team this evening at Nu Stadium. Additionally, the championship match also presented a winning debut for new head coach Cristian “Kily” González in his first match at the helm.

Inter Miami CF This Regular Season

Overall, Inter Miami has recorded 12 wins, four losses, and nine draws so far this MLS regular season for a total 45 points, and sits second in the Eastern Conference standings.

Messi, the reigning MLS Landon Donovan MVP Award and Golden Boot presented by Audi winner, leads both the team amd the league in goals with 19. Messi also leads MLS in assists this regular season with 13.

Scouting San Diego FC

San Diego FC visits Nu Stadium to face Inter Miami for the first time after falling 0-5 at home to Philadelphia Union in its past match. Overall, the California side has recorded eight wins, 11 losses and six draws for a total 30 points and is ninth in the Western Conference table.

Forward Marcus Ingvartsen has been the team's main threat in attack so far this regular season, tallying 11 goals and five assists in 21 appearances.
