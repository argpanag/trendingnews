NFL Week 3 injuries to monitor for Sunday's games

TheTools · 28.09.2026 06:27 · source · permalink

NFL.com keeps you up to date with all of the latest injury updates ahead of Week 2's Sunday games.

  • READ: Week 3 injury report

INJURIES

  • WR Zay Flowers (questionable; hamstring) is planning to work out pregame and push to play against Dallas, NFL Network Insider Ian Rapoport reported. Flowers is viewed as a true game-time decision, but his participation is not something the Ravens are counting on, per Rapoport.

INJURIES

  • WR DJ Moore (shoulder; questionable) is expected to play against the Chargers, ESPN's Adam Schefter reported. Moore is officially active.

INJURIES

  • WR Jalen Coker (ankle) should play Sunday against the Browns, NFL Network Insider Ian Rapoport reported. Coker is officially active.
  • WR Xavier Legette (knee) is a game-time decision against Cleveland, per Rapoport. Legette is officially inactive.

INJURIES

  • CB Charvarius Ward (questionable; groin) is expected to play against Houston, ESPN's Jeremy Fowler reported. Ward is officially active.

INJURIES

  • TE Brock Bowers (questionable; knee) is expected to play Sunday against the Saints barring a surprise, per ESPN's Jeremy Fowler.

INJURIES

  • WR Adonai Mitchell (questionable; finger) is not expected to play against Detroit, ESPN's Jeremy Fowler reported. Mitchell is officially inactive.

INJURIES

  • RB Jaylen Warren (questionable; shoulder) is expected to play against Cincinnati, ESPN's Adam Schefter reported. Warren is officially active.
  • WR Michael Pittman (questionable; foot) is expected to play vs. the Bengals, per Schefter. Pittman is officially active.

INJURIES

  • WR Mike Evans (questionable; hip) plans to play Sunday against Arizona, ESPN's Jeremy Fowler reported.
