Walsh’s shock NFL deal revealed; Moses makes big Kangaroos call in Cleary twist — NRL Daily

TheTools · 17.09.2026 07:22 · source · permalink

Broncos superstar Reece Walsh has signed an ambassadorial deal with Super Bowl champions the Seattle Seahawks.

The 24-year-old, who has endured a nightmare season with Brisbane, was in attendance for the inaugural NFL Melbourne game between the San Francisco 49ers and Los Angeles Rams at the MCG last week.

The Seahawks confirmed their partnership with Walsh — whose manager Nash Dawson’s nine-month suspension from communicating with NRL clubs will be lifted on Friday after he was fined and banned for a physical altercation with fellow agent Chris Orr.

Dawson’s management agency has continued to work on Walsh’s behalf during the agent’s suspension — and the deal with the Seahawks is one that has been struck during that period.

It is hoped the link-up will be mutually beneficial, enhancing Walsh’s profile in the US, while increasing the Seahawks’ reach in Australia.

Walsh is currently recovering from ankle surgery after suffering a high-grade syndesmosis injury during Brisbane’s Round 24 game against the Warriors.

The Broncos, who are the reigning premiers, endured a dismal season as they failed to make the finals.

Last year’s Clive Churchill Medallist Walsh scored just six tries in 2026, but Brisbane coach Michael Maguire has backed him to turn things around next season.

“It’s been a different journey for Reece this year because of circumstance,” Maguire said.

“He’s had to go through injuries, but you also learn a lot when you become the premiers. People come at you in a different way.

“Reece has been the hunted rather than the hunter, but it’s given him more knowledge moving forward.

“He’s one of those players who is very curious about how we can keep evolving our game.

“Sometimes he’s in a rush, so it’s about making sure we’re all on the same page and moving forward.

“People forget how young Reece is, he still has so much upside and I love his attitude towards getting better.”

Dylan Terry — News.com.au.

MOSES MAKES WC CALL

Eels star Mitch Moses has reportedly committed his allegiance to the Kangaroos over Lebanon for next month’s World Cup if picked in Australia’s 23-man squad.

Moses has represented Lebanon at the previous two World Cups. However, according to AAP, the Eels star wishes to wear the green and gold in the global showpiece.

There’s no shortage of halfback options for coach Kevin Walters and Moses is no sure thing to make the World Cup squad, but the injury cloud over Nathan Cleary has opened the door for Moses to potentially start in the No. 7 jersey.

Cleary suffered a foot injury in the qualifying final win over the Roosters last Sunday and while he’s expected to play in next week’s prelim, the injury could force the Panthers star to forego the World Cup.

If Cleary does, Moses is almost a guarantee to earn a spot in Walters’ squad.

In another boost for the Kangaroos, gun prop Tino Fa’asuamaleaui is set to pick Australia over Samoa for the ten-team tournament.

Fa’asuamaleaui played for Australia in last year’s UK tour, but there had been some rumblings he would switch allegiances to Samoa, a la Payne Haas.

In other international news, Samoa will have the services of gun NQ prop Tom Mikaele and promising Knights edge forward Francis Manuleleua.

Recently retired NRL prop Josh Papalii could still feature at the World Cup either for Samoa or the Cook Islands although no decision has been made on whether he’ll play on.

STORM’S HUGE RETENTION PUSH

The Storm have announced they’ve signed five players to extensions, including promising young gun Hugo Peel and rugby union convert Moses Leo.

The club have also inked new deals with forward Jack Hetherington, the versatile Trent Toelau and young playmaker Stanley Huen.

Leo is now contracted until the end of 2029 as is Peel.

The seventh Victorian junior to play for the Storm, Peel played in three games at the top level this season.

Leo’s number was called often this season as the Storm dealt with a few injuries in the backline.

Huen, who featuired in four NRL games off the bench this season, has re-upped for two more seasons until the end of 2028.

Meanwhile, Toelau and Hetherington have signed one-year deals for 2027.
