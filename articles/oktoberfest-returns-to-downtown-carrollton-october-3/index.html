Oktoberfest Returns to Downtown Carrollton October 3

TheTools · 18.09.2026 18:23 · source · permalink

Local businesses and the City of Carrollton will celebrate the arrival of fall, Saturday, October 3, with the return of Oktoberfest to downtown. 

Starting at 10am, attendees can make a day of it downtown with live music, food and drinks, family-friendly activities and plenty of ways to enjoy the season. Highlights include Brown Dog Eatery’s “Brown Dog of the Year” award and costume contest, kids’ activities and crafts at Dream City Brewing, football and cornhole at the Amp, live music at Local Ties and Dream City Brewing and German-inspired food during Brown Dog’s Dogtoberfest.

Oktoberfest Schedule

Downtown Events

10am – “Brown Dog of the Year” award and costume contests

11am – Dogtoberfest food begins at Brown Dog Eatery

12pm – Football and cornhole at the Amp

3pm – Stein Hoist Competition at Dream City Brewing

Live Music at Local Ties

12–3pm – Brian Crews and Julie Anne Steed

4–7pm – Far From Perfect

7:30pm – Shell Echo Arrangement and Ociffer

Live Music at Dream City Brewing

6pm – Joel and the House Band

With activities and entertainment taking place throughout downtown, Oktoberfest offers an opportunity to enjoy local businesses while celebrating the beginning of the fall season.

For more information and the latest event details, visit carrolltonga.com/events.

What: Oktoberfest 

When: October 3, 2026 / 10am

Where: Downtown Carrollton
