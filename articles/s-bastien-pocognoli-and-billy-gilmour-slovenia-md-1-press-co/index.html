Sébastien Pocognoli and Billy Gilmour: Slovenia MD-1 Press Conference - Scottish FA

TheTools · 06.10.2026 20:10 · source · permalink

Billy Gilmour joined Head Coach Sébastien Pocognoli to conduct the MD-1 media duties on Monday, with the midfielder in line to win his 50th Scotland cap against Slovenia on Tuesday evening.

Scotland got their first win of the Pocognoli era on Saturday night in Skopje and will be hoping to round off this UEFA Nations League window with consecutive victories as Slovenia now visit Barclays Hampden.

Sébastien Pocognoli was pleased with the performance on Saturday but knows his side will face a tougher test in Slovenia - who drew 0-0 with the Scots in match day one at the start of this camp.

He said: "It was important because it was the first win. We were happy after the game, but we knew that we have to show it again in three days at home, in a different context with a different opponent - and we have to start again.

"Tomorrow is an important game for the ranking. If you draw away, you have to deliver something at home.

"We create more chances, so that's a positive with the new group of players with totally new principle.

"Tomorrow I just hope we keep creating chances. The reality is we have to win tomorrow and score goals.

"We face a team with a clear structure. They have good movement and good connection between the players and they did well against Switzerland."

Scotland midfielder Gilmour, meanwhile, spoke of his pride at closing in on the milestone of 50 caps.

“Yeah, 50 caps, hopefully (on Tuesday)," he said. "So excitement but overall it is another game, hopefully a good game and a win.

“I’m always so proud to put on my national-team jersey and go out there. We’re very lucky as well, not everyone gets to represent their country at sport.

“I’m always very proud to put it on and I give my all and try my best.

“I’ve matured a lot, ups and downs. But for me, I think I’m still kind of the same player, I still want to get on the ball, play football technically.

“I’m not the strongest or the biggest so I try and stay away from all that. But I come away and try to do my best, whatever I get told to do.”
