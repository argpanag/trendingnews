Looney Tunes Star Eric Bauza, the Voice of Bugs Bunny, Daffy Duck & More, Dies at 46

TheTools · 26.10.2026 03:49 · source · permalink

Prolific Looney Tunes voice actor Eric Bauza, who was just recently heard in the hit film Coyote vs. Acme, has passed away. He was just 46 years old.

The news of Bauza's passing was shared by his family with a post on Instagram. No specific cause of death was named, though the family noted that it happened suddenly. It was stated that more information would be released soon. For now, the comments section of the post is filling up with fans expressing their shock and sadness about the news, with many sharing memories of how pleasant he had been to everyone.

As the statement reads, "Eric Bauza, Emmy Award-winning voice actor and the modern voice of iconic Looney Tunes characters Bugs Bunny and Daffy Duck, along with dozens of other beloved cartoon characters, has passed away suddenly. More details to come."
