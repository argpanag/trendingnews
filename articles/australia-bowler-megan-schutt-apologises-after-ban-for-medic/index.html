Bowler handed two-match ban over ‘single incidence’

Final T20 of series with New Zealand succumbs to rain

Australian women’s cricket stalwart Megan Schutt has been banned for two matches for a prescription medication breach. Schutt has apologised in the wake of her ban, announced on Thursday, which is not related to performance-enhancements.

The 33-year-old strike bowler served the first match of her suspension on Thursday night when Australia’s T20 international with New Zealand was washed out in Sydney. Schutt failed to adhere to requirements under Sports Science and Sports Medicine guidelines with regard to prescription medication, Cricket Australia (CA) said.

“I fully accept the penalty handed down and apologise for failing to meet the standards expected of me,” Schutt, a mainstay of Australia’s women’s team since her debut in 2012, said in a statement. “I’m sorry for the attention this has caused and appreciate the support I’ve received.

“While this occurred out of competition and was not performance related, I understand that I am responsible for following the Sports Science and Sports Medicine Guidelines, which players receive regular education on and I should have adhered to these guidelines.”

Cricket Australia said Schutt’s breach related to “a single incidence which occurred while out of competition and was not for performance enhancing purposes”. It added in a statement: “The two-match ban includes all international and domestic cricket. We will continue to provide appropriate support to Megan and reinforce the standards and responsibilities expected of everyone representing our game.”

Schutt is among the top-10 all-time wicket-takers in women’s cricket. The South Australian has taking 319 wickets in her 244 internationals – four Tests, 114 ODIs and 126 T20s. The bowler most recently played for Australia in the three-match ODI series against Bangladesh, when she starred with 10 wickets in the series.
