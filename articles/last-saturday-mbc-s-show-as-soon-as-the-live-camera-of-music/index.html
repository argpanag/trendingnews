Last Saturday, MBC's "Show!" As soon as the live camera of "Music Core" shone on the four-member girl group bestie (Hye-yeon, Yu-ji, Da-hye, Haeryeong), the music industry and K-pop fans felt a thrill beyond mere welcome.

It has been about 4,000 days (11 years) since the four members took the stage in a music program as a whole named "Bestie." The "Show! Music Core" video is proving its popularity by easily exceeding 1 million views in a few hours immediately after its release.

Bestie, who debuted with "DKDK" in 2013, drew attention for her explosive live skills and healthy performances, but with the expiration of her exclusive contract in 2018, she effectively ended her team activities and took the path to official dissolution. Since then, the members have silently built up their work as trot singers (Hye-yeon), musical actors (Yoo Ji), entertainment and creators (Dahye), and actors (Haeryeong).

It was a stage that rose again after a long gap of eight years after the dissolution, but there was no awkwardness. The mature aura, constant live, and shrewd teamwork captivated viewers at once. In other words, it was not a simple "recall of memories" that stimulates nostalgia of the past, but a fierce "real battle" of artists who have honed in their respective fields.

The starting point of this miracle was the second single, "Love Options," which was released in 2013. As the video of the song being added to the stage of a new girl group went viral, the melody and honest lyrics spread explosively on the short-form platform.

The buzz immediately led to an upheaval in the music charts. After ranking 47th to 48th in the Melon TOP100 and 58th to 61st in the daily chart, it shook domestic and foreign platforms evenly, including 12th place in YouTube Music Korea, 1st place in major Shazam charts, and 9th place in Japan's Oricon Shorts chart. On top of that, the members announced a surprise reunion on October 17, the 13th anniversary of the release, and the 2026 version of the high-heel choreography video exceeded 270,000 views in a day, raising expectations for a return stage to its peak.

From a pop culture standpoint, Vesti's success poses a very big topic for today's K-pop industry.

Above all, amid the trend of rapidly disappearing after numerous backtracking songs were consumed by the sparkling trend of the short-form challenge, Vesti directly received the opportunities opened by the algorithm with bleeding practice and a substantive live stage. It is a rare twist drama completed by an artist only responding to the public's voluntary call without the capital or artificial promotion of a large agency.

At the same time, this stage strongly imprinted the healthy 'weight class' unique to the 2nd and 3rd generations of K-pop. If the K-pop scene recently tends to focus on comfortable easy listening and cool sensibilities tailored to short-form grammar, Vesti proved the solid basics unique to girl groups in the 2010s by performing cool singing, solid physicality, and group dance without shaking even in high heels. The nature of the "real adult women are here" response, which netizens were enthusiastic about, is in contact with the solid inner workings they have built up.

Finally, their return shows that the vitality of artists and music is never trapped in time. After eight years of separating the team, members who had walked their own paths regrouped only for the stage and fans, and music 13 years ago gave a new generation of listeners the inspiration to live and breathe again. They proved themselves that there is no expiration date for a good stage and good music.

"Show" in about 4,000 days! With the appearance of "Music Core," Vesti completed the most brilliant highlight of this reverse narrative. In addition to "Dating Conditions," Bestie is still full of masterpieces with both singing and popularity, such as "THANK U VERY MUCH," "Hot Baby," and "Excuse Me."

It is hoped that this unexpected miracle, which came after 13 years, will not end up as a flash event, but will lead to new music releases and continuous activities, remaining as a long-running model that will be a model for K-pop scene for a long time.
