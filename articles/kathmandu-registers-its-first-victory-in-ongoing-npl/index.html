Kathmandu registers its first victory in ongoing NPL

TheTools · 02.11.2026 18:01 · source · permalink

Kathmandu: Kathmandu Gorkhas has registered its first victory by defeating Karnali Yaks in the match played under the Nepal Premier League (NPL) today.

In the match held at the Kirtipur-based Tribhuvan University International Cricket Ground, Kathmandu Gorkhas defeated Karnali Yaks by 4 wickets. Kathmandu Gorkhas chased down the 181-run victory target posted by Karnali Yaks in 19.4 overs at the loss of 6 wickets.

For Kathmandu, Rashid Khan scored the highest score with 50 runs off 33 balls, followed by Captain Karan KC, who contributed 44 runs off 20 balls, Jahandad Khan gathered 22 runs (not out), and Mohammad Aadil Alam made 20 runs. For the bowling side, Sompal Kami and Hussien Talat took two wickets each. Mark Watt and Nandan Yadav took one wicket each.

Earlier, invited to bat first after losing the toss, Karnali made 180 runs in the stipulated 20 overs at the loss of six wickets. Ariful Islam gathered the highest score of 44 runs off 22 balls, while Hussain Talat made 40 runs off 30 balls and Pawan Saraf scored 30 runs (not out). Gulsan Jha and Mark Watt contributed 20 runs and 17 runs, respectively.

For Kathmandu, Karan KC took two wickets, and Sahab Alam, Jahandad Khan, Dev Shah and Santosh Yadav took one wicket each.
