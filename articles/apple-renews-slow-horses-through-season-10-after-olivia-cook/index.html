Apple Renews Slow Horses Through Season 10 After Olivia Cooke Hints the Series May Be Ending

TheTools · 16.11.2026 16:43 · source · permalink

Season eight is currently in production.

Jackson Lamb and Slough House aren’t going away any time soon. Fresh off the heels of season six airing this fall, Apple TV announced an unprecedented early renewal for seasons nine and ten for Slow Horses. (Season seven has already filmed, and season eight is currently in production.)

The announcement came shortly after an interview with Olivia Cooke, who plays Sid Baker in the show. “I am shooting the last season!” she told The Times of London yesterday. “Never say never. But I think that’s what we’ve been told and it definitely feels like we are ending. We’re all melancholy, distracted. Yesterday, I got in the make-up chair and cried.” It’s unclear if Cooke was unaware about more seasons, or if it is the end for her character, Sid.

The spy thriller is based on Mick Herron’s series. Season six drew from two of Herron’s novels, Joe Country and Slough House, and season seven is likely based on book eight, Bad Actors. It follows that season eight would draw from book nine, Clown Town; the final two seasons, then, would either be in uncharted territory with no Herron source material. However, new books may still be coming. “Mick Herron hasn’t stopped writing. I can’t speak for Mick, but you never know, more books might be coming,” executive producer Douglas Urbanski told the Hollywood Reporter.

The critically acclaimed espionage drama stars Gary Oldman as Jackson Lamb, Jack Lowden as River Cartwright, Olivia Cooke as Sid Baker, Kristin Scott Thomas as Diana Taverner, Saskia Reeves as Catherine Standish, Christopher Chung as Roddy Ho, Rosalind Eleazar as Louisa Guy, and Aimee-Ffion Edwards as Shirley Dander.
