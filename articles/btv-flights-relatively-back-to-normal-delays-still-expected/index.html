BTV flights relatively back to normal, delays still expected

TheTools · 15.11.2026 00:53 · source · permalink

SOUTH BURLINGTON, Vt. (WCAX) - Flights at Burlington International Airport are relatively back to normal, but some delays are still expected, according to Aviation Director, Nic Longo.

Longo said six flights were affected right away Friday evening. A few more inbound flights were affected later.

Longo praised the local communication between the airport, air traffic control and the airlines. He also credited car rental companies and local hotels for helping affected travelers.

Ian Cowley of Cincinnati is visiting Vermont with friends. They were supposed to land in Burlington on Friday night but got stuck at LaGuardia Airport in New York City just before takeoff, so they drove to Burlington instead.

“We ended up spending probably a grand total of seven hours on the tarmac yesterday. I was able to, while sitting on the tarmac, book a rental car, and I think I got pretty much the last rental car in Laguardia it seems, and we drove here, we got here around 2:00 am,” Cowley said.

The FAA said the U.S. air travel schedule should be back to normal by the end of Sunday.
