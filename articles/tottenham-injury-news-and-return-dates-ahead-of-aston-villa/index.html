Tottenham injury news and return dates ahead of Aston Villa

TheTools · 19.09.2026 07:33 · source · permalink

Tottenham Hotspur return to action on Saturday as they seek their first win - and goals - of the Premier League season. The Lilywhites host fellow top-flight strugglers Aston Villa (12:30pm).

Roberto De Zerbi's team were poor again in Tuesday night's 3-1 defeat at Liverpool in the League Cup. That loss followed a 0-0 draw at home to Everton last weekend, meaning Spurs have gone 407 consecutive minutes without scoring in the league.

It is the first time in the club's entire 144-year history that they have started a league campaign with four straight fixtures without netting a single goal.

With all of that being said, football.london has rounded up the latest updates on Tottenham's injured players and when they could return...

Sandro Tonali

Tonali was ruled out of featuring against Liverpool by De Zerbi despite only suffering a knock. However, the Italian will be back for Saturday's game.

What De Zerbi has said: "Porro, Tonali and Maddison all available to play. As normal, we have to understand them in terms of physical condition."

Return date: Aston Villa (H) - Saturday, September 19.

James Maddison

Maddison featured off the bench at Anfield on Tuesday night, replacing Conor Gallagher in the final quarter of an hour. It came after he fractured his shoulder.

Dejan Kulusevski

Kulusevski has not played for Tottenham in 16 months because of a knee injury. He was captured in parts of Tottenham team training earlier this month but has since suffered an unfortunate setback.

What De Zerbi has said: "We have to manage Deki in the best way we can, but this is a great thing for us because he is a good player and a good guy.

"He deserves to play again, and we have to work day by day. Also for Deki, it is important the national break because when he is at top condition and over the injury, he will be like another new signing."

What Sweden boss Graham Potter has said: "He's very close actually. I'm so impressed with him, how he's handled his injury. Of course we have to be careful with him. I hope he comes with us, but we have to talk to Dejan and Tottenham first.”

Possible return date: Unknown.

Pedro Porro

Porro suffered a 'small muscular problem' last week, sidelining him from the goalless draw to Everton in the Premier League, as well as the defeat to Liverpool in the Carabao Cup third round. He will be back on Saturday though.

Return date: Aston Villa (H) - Saturday, September 19.

Mykhailo Mudryk

Summer loan signing Mudryk has been ruled out until after the international break because of an ankle injury. football.london understands he will be out of action for anywhere between four and eight weeks.

What De Zerbi has said: "It was a stupid injury to Mudryk, and we hope he comes back as soon as possible. Playing without tackle, without different movement, he felt pain in the ankle, and then after two days, he made the medical check. We already knew it could be difficult to come back [immediately] as a normal player."

Possible return date: Late October/early November.

Wilson Odobert

Odobert ruptured his ACL back in February and is therefore on track to return later this year, barring any setbacks.

Possible return date: November/December.

Xavi Simons

Simons is also recovering from a major knee injury that he suffered in April which has him out of action until 2027 at the earliest.

Possible return date: Spring 2027.
