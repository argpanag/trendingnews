🚨Confirmed line-ups: Demichelis makes his grand return to Munich

The Allianz Arena hosts this Saturday’s (18:30 CEST) Bundesliga showdown between FC Bayern Munich and RB Leipzig. Vincent Kompany’s side are looking to return to winning ways after consecutive draws against Augsburg (2-2) and Viking Stavanger (3-3). With Jamal Musiala ruled out and Manuel Neuer’s fitness in doubt, the Bavarians will rely on their attacking firepower to bolster their title credentials.

RB Leipzig, meanwhile, arrive in fine form after thrashing Eintracht Frankfurt 6-0 and beating PSV 1-0 in the Champions League. The clash marks an emotional return to Munich for Martín Demichelis, this time as Leipzig’s head coach, as he looks to improve his team’s away form this season. Without Marc Guiu or Assan Ouédraogo, the visitors are confident they can spring a surprise.
