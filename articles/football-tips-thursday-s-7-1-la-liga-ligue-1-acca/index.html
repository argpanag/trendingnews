Toulouse v Lille – Lille to win & both teams to score

WHEN: Thursday, 3rd of September, 7:45pm
WHERE: Stadium de Toulouse, Toulouse
TV: Ligue 1+

The consistently inconsistent Toulouse have begun the new season in standard fashion, drawing one and losing one. On home soil last season they won six, drew six and lost five, scoring 25 goals over those 17 games, conceding 24.

Lille got their campaign off to a good start, winning 2-0 in their opening fixture away to Angers, but over the weekend, they threw away a 2-0 lead when facing champions PSG on home turf, thanks to two injury-time goals from Vitinha and Marquinhos. In fairness to PSG, they created the most chances, and it would’ve been harsh on them to lose.

Lille’s collapse shows they haven’t yet cast off last season’s problems, but that said, they’ve won this fixture in three of their last four visits and certainly have superior firepower, along with a far stronger midfield.

The hosts should at least make a dent, however, after averaging more than a goal per game at home last season. Lille also conceded 20 times in their 17 away games last season, so we’re happy to add a BTTS: Yes selection to the Lille pick.

Real Sociedad v Celta Vigo – Real Sociedad to win

WHEN: Thursday, 3rd of September, 8pm
WHERE: Anoeta, San Sebastian
TV: Premier Sports 1

Neither side can be too happy with how things have started for them this season. Real Sociedad have won one and lost two, albeit one of those losses was a 4-1 thrashing at the hands of Real Madrid, where Kylian Mbappe bagged a hat-trick.

Celta Vigo have been pretty dire, though, taking just a point from their three La Liga games so far, following a dour 0-0 opening draw away to Valencia. Since then, Claudio Giraldez’s side have lost 2-1 to Osasuna and 2-0 to Athletic Bilbao, both of which were at home.

Curiously, though, Celta were good travellers last season, losing just four times. But one of those defeats was a 3-1 reverse to Sociedad, and it’s difficult to see them being such a force on the road this term, considering how many defenders and midfielders departed over the summer.

We feel that the hosts can heap more misery on a side that already looks low on confidence here.
