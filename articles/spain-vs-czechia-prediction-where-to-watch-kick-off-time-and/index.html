The hosts are in formidable form, currently enjoying a long unbeaten streak of 40 games in the regular time of 90 minutes. The visitors are hoping to bounce back from a series of recent defeats, making for a challenging game for the away side, who have historically struggled in this fixture.

Spain vs. Czechia - Head-to-head record

The head-to-head history between Spain and Czechia, though relatively recent, clearly shows Spanish dominance. In their three previous encounters, Spain has remained unbeaten, securing two victories and one draw. Czechia, conversely, is still seeking its inaugural win against this opponent.

Their most recent meetings occurred in the 2022 UEFA Nations League, presenting two distinct outcomes. Spain achieved a decisive 2-0 victory at home in Málaga. Yet, the fixture in Prague saw Czechia earn a hard-fought 2-2 draw, showcasing their resilience when playing in front of their own fans.

This historical record provides Spain with a significant psychological edge in the match fixture. Czechia"s capacity to secure a point at home suggests they are a more formidable side on their own ground. Ultimately, Spain"s 40 game unbeaten streak and superior results make them the clear historical favourites for this upcoming game.

Spain - Riding a wave of success

Here is a closer look at Spain’s current situation. We will analyse the team’s momentum and their latest displays, before considering how they might approach this new test against Czechia. We will also provide the expected line-ups for both national teams.

Recent form

Spain enters this match in superb form, boasting an impressive unbeaten streak of 40 games in the regular time of 90 minutes. The only loss in this period was decided in a penalty shootout against Portugal, when they faced each other in the UEFA Nations League 2024/25 final.

The spanish team is now the current World Cup champions and have carried this momentum into this UEFA Nations League edition, securing maximum points from their two fixtures. With six points, they currently lead their group, showcasing their strong start to the competition.

Their recent UEFA Nations League results highlight their dominance. Spain earned a thrilling 3-2 victory away against England, followed by a commanding 4-1 win over Croatia at home. These performances saw them score seven goals while conceding only three, demonstrating both their attacking power and solid defence.

Young attacker Lamine Yamal has been a standout performer, already finding the back of the net three times in the UEFA Nations League.

Spain might be without some important players for this match: Nico Williams and Ferran Torres could join Pedro Porro and Eric Garcia in the injury list. La Roja is still the clear favourites for the upcoming encounter against Czechia.

Czechia - Struggling to find form

Let’s review Czechia’s current situation. We will analyse the team’s momentum, their expectations and standout players for the trip to Oviedo.

Recent form

Czechia enters this fixture in poor form, having lost four and drawn one of their last five matches across UEFA Nations League and the 2026 World Cup. Their  campaign in the european tournment has started poorly, with defeats in both opening home games against Croatia (2-1) and England (2-0).

These results leave them with zero points in the group, scoring just once and conceding four goals. This upcoming match is their first away game in the current Nations League, adding to the challenge.

Czechia will be playing without midfielder Sulc, suspended for a red card, and could be without Karabec due to an injury. Attacker Hlozek is expected to provide creativity with the absences. However, their current form suggests a difficult encounter awaits.
