Warm afternoons continue through the weekend

TheTools · 08.10.2026 12:17 · source · permalink

LINCOLN, Neb. (KOLN) - Well above average temperatures will remain through at least Sunday. For part of the area, cooler temperatures don’t look to return until Tuesday due to the timing of a cold front. Some locations may tie or possibly set new record high temperatures Friday and Saturday. It should remain dry for much of, maybe even the entire area through Monday.

Partly cloudy this morning with areas of patchy fog possible. This afternoon looks to be mostly sunny and warm with south-southeast winds at 5 to 15 mph. High temperatures will be in the upper 70s to upper 80s.

Friday should be mainly sunny, warmer and breezy. High temperatures will be in the mid 80s to low 90s. For several locations, including Lincoln, this is near record high territory for early October. Winds look to be south-southwest at 10 to 25 mph with gusts to 35 mph.

Saturday is going to be similar to Friday with high temperatures a few degrees hotter and winds a little stronger. High temperatures should range from the mid 80s to low 90s which is still near record high territory for several locations. Winds will be south-southwest at 20 to 30 mph with gusts to 40 mph.

Cold front will move into the area Sunday, but not move completely through until sometime on Monday. This will lead to wide range of high temperatures across Nebraska. Highs should be in the low 60s to around 90 Sunday and the upper 50s to around 90 on Monday.

Rain chances return Tuesday and continue into Wednesday. Temperatures should be much cooler too.
