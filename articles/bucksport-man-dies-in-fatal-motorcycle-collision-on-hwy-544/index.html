CONWAY, S.C. (WPDE) — A motorcyclist has died in a fatal vehicle collision on Highway 544 on Monday, according to the Horry County coroner's office.

Kevin Lawrimore, 64, died when colliding with another vehicle on Highway 544 at Kingswood Drive on Monday, shortly before noon, according to Horry County Chief Deputy Coroner Tamara Willard.

Lawrimore was taken to Conway Medical Center for his injuries, where he died. He was from Bucksport, South Carolina, Willard said.

Willard said the South Carolina Highway Patrol is investigating.
