ICL Q3 2026 sales rose to $1.91B from $1.85B, Potash up on volume, Industrial Products segment EBITDA down to $67M from $100M.

What this filing says

ICL Group filed its Q3 2026 investor presentation under Form 6-K, reporting third-quarter 2026 sales of $1,910 million against $1,853 million a year earlier. Segment results were mixed, with Potash and Phosphate Solutions sales higher and segment EBITDA lower for several units. The presentation includes non-GAAP measures, so shareholders should check the reconciliation tables against the reported figures.

Key figures

Positive for shareholders

  • Third-quarter sales rose by about $57 million year on year.
  • Potash segment sales increased to $469 million from $453 million.

Negative for shareholders

  • Free cash flow fell to $66 million from $129 million a year earlier.
  • Phosphate Solutions segment operating income dropped to $58 million from $85 million.
  • Industrial Products segment EBITDA fell to $67 million from $100 million.
