Larry Poindexter Dead: 'S.W.A.T.', 'JAG' Actor Was 66

TheTools · 19.11.2026 02:32 · source · permalink

Larry Poindexter, a veteran character actor who has appeared in almost 200 movies and TV series, passed away today, Nov. 18, four and a half days after being hit by a car. He was 66.

Poindexter was struck while crossing the street to pick up food in his Studio City neighborhood Friday night. He had been at the hospital and scheduled to have a surgery for broken ribs today when he died suddenly this morning.

Poindexter, a journeyman actor whose career spanned more than four decades, is known for his role in the 2003 film S.W.A.T. in which he played by-the-book LAPD Captain Tom Fuller, who went head-to-head with Samuel Jackson’s Hondo.

On TV, Poindexter appeared in 30 episodes of daytime drama Days Of Our Lives, playing two different characters, first in 1984 as Ben Welch, and as Father Louis from 2014-2018. He also did a 23-episode stint on Santa Barbara and 12 episodes of General Hospital.

In primetime, Poindexter was a series regular on the MTV comedy series The Hard Times of RJ Berger and had a major recurring role on CBS’ JAG. He was a guest star on dozens of shows over the years, including playing Sergeant Boyer in the S.W.A.T. TV series and appearing in episodes of Quantum Leap, NYPD Blue, How I Met Your Mother, Friends, NCIS, Will & Grace, Scandal, CSI, CSI: Miami, CSI: NY, Party of Five, Nip/Tuck, Melrose Place, Weeds, Californication, The Mentalist, Hawaii Five-0, Castle, Curb Your Enthusiasm, Supernatural, Bones, Criminal Minds, Rizzoli & Isles, All Rise, Station 19, The Rookie, and 9-1-1. His most recent guest appearance was in Season 4 of The Lincoln Lawyer earlier this year.

Also a musician, Poindexter was lead singer for the alternative country band The High Lonesome in the 1990s. Their album Feel Free To Do So was released in 1995.

Poindexter is survived by his wife, leading casting director Carol Kritzer, and their two sons.
