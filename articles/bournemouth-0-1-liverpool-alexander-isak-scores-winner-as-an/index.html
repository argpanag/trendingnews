Alexander Isak scored the winning goal as Liverpool beat Bournemouth 1-0 on Andoni Iraola's return to the Vitality Stadium.

A tense game was settled just before the hour mark as Isak finished from Cody Gakpo's cross - via a slight nick off Florian Wirtz.

Iraola unhappy with how Liverpool started game

Liverpool head coach Andoni Iraola to Sky Sports:

"I'm obviously happy with the result. I know it's a difficult place to come, very difficult place to get three points. We could see in the game, at the beginning in the first 20 minutes, we weren't at the level at the game, and it has cost us until afterwards, where we finished the first half better.

"The second half we played better. But that beginning of the game kind of - not surprised us because we knew where we were coming to - but it was difficult for us.

"We could see a bit later when Bournemouth started to get tired that in the second half we controlled the game much better. We even had chances to finish but didn't score the second one.

"But that first part of the game, we were not winning duels, second balls and when they play with this intensity, you have to be at their level."

Analysis: Where would Liverpool be without Cody Gakpo?

Sky Sports' Sam Blitz:

In every single Liverpool game he's started this season, Cody Gakpo has produced a golden moment in front of goal.

A goal at Newcastle on the opening weekend, a silky against Nottingham Forest, assists for both goals in the win over Ipswich, another assist and a goal against Tottenham in the Carabao Cup - but this intervention at Bournemouth was the best of the lot.

With Liverpool struggling for attacking influence, Gakpo's hard-fought run and cross down the right wing was vital in the build-up to Alexader Isak's winner. "The difficult part of the goal is from him," said Iraola about Gakpo, who is unfortunate not to be accredited with the assist.

But Gakpo also starred defensively at Bournemouth, winning more duels than any other player on the pitch. He shut down one of the Cherries' biggest threats. "I know Adrien Truffert very well and he demands a lot from you defensively. Cody today has been very important in both ways."

Looking at this form, it is absolutely staggering that Liverpool considered sanctioning the sale of Gakpo this summer. Where would they be without him?

Rose: Bournemouth panicked in tight situations

Bournemouth head coach Marco Rose to Sky Sports:

"We couldn't see that one team deserved to win. We played really good in the first 25 minutes and then it got more open.

"We had to work really hard to get chances but we conceded an easy goal.

"In both boxes, you had to be ready and be present. In the end, they scored the goal with one situation and we put a lot of effort into creating chances.

"We panicked a little bit in tight situations. We were fit and I thought we could bring more energy into the game. It is disappointing.

"It was an opportunity to win, right now we have three points from five games and it is disappointing, I think we missed details."
