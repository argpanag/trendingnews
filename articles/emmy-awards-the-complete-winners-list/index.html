Emmy Awards: The Complete Winners List

TheTools · 15.09.2026 02:57 · source · permalink

'Widow's Bay,' 'The Pitt,' 'The Late Show,' 'DTF St. Louis' and 'The Traitors' were among the shows honored. Acting awards went to Matthew Rhys (twice!), Jean Smart, Kate O'Flynn, Allison Janney, Rhea Seehorn, Noah Wyle, Stephen Root, Tom Pelphrey and Sally Field.

The 2026 Emmy Awards were handed out Monday night.

Widow’s Bay was named best comedy series (setting a record), The Pitt won best drama series, and DTF St. Louis was named best limited or anthology series. The Late Show With Stephen Colbert won best variety series, and The Traitors was named best reality competition program.

Among the acting winners are Jean Smart for Hacks, Kate O’Flynn for Widow’s Bay, Matthew Rhys (twice! which marks a record) for The Beast in Me and Widow’s Bay, Allison Janney for The Diplomat, Rhea Seehorn for Pluribus, Noah Wyle for The Pitt, Stephen Root for Widow’s Bay, Tom Pelphrey for Task and Sally Field for Remarkably Bright Creatures.

Katie Dippold and Vince Gilligan won best writing for Widow’s Bay and Pluribus, respectively. Hiro Murai and Saul Metzstein scooped up directing trophies for Widow’s Bay and Slow Horses, respectively.

Awards in 19 categories — including best drama and comedy series — were presented at the Peacock Theater in downtown Los Angeles. (See the red carpet arrivals.)

This year, The Pitt led the pack with 25 total nominations, followed by Hacks (with 24 nominations, a comedy series record), Widow’s Bay (19), Pluribus (18), and Beef (16). The Creative Arts ceremonies were held last weekend over two nights; click here and here to see the winners.

Mariska Hargitay hosted the Emmys (which also featured a surprise appearance by Taylor Swift during a pretaped segment). Hargitay picked up two wins last week to her documentary My Mom Jayne.

The ceremony aired live on NBC and Peacock.
