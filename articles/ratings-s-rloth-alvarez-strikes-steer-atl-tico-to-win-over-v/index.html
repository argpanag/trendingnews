Ratings: Sørloth, Alvarez strikes steer Atlético to win over Viking

TheTools · 25.11.2026 22:18 · source · permalink
  • Atlético Madrid Player Ratings

Atlético de Madrid claimed a Champions League victory over Viking on Wednesday night, as first-half goals from Alexander Sørloth and Julián Alvarez provided all the scoring that the Colchoneros would need.

Sørloth’s goal was a product of wonderful build up play from Dávid Hancko and Alejandro Grimaldo, and Alvarez added another free kick golazo to his résumé.

The second half was a bit of a snooze unless you were an Atlético de Madrid supporter. The most notable moment came in the dying stages of the match, when Giuliano Simeone drew a red card in the 92nd minute.

Atlético didn’t play particularly inspiring football, but they did enough to secure all three points. It was a fair result and a much-needed victory for a side that struggled throughout the first half of its Champions League campaign.

Here are Into the Calderon’s player ratings from tonight’s result against Viking:

Starting XI

Jan Oblak

Oblak wasn’t called upon to make his first save of the night until the 81st minute. Another clean sheet to add to the Slovenian’s illustrious résumé, though! 7

Alejandro Grimaldo

What a performance from Grimaldo. He assisted Sørloth with a pinpoint cross for his eighth goal contribution already, and he produced a key block to prevent Viking from halving the score before halftime. 8.25

Dávid Hancko

Played a great ball to find Grimaldo, who laid it off for Sørloth to hammer home the opening goal. His leadership and defensive coordination with Grimaldo and Cardoso were key to Atlético’s defensive solidity. 8

Robin Le Normand

Great(!) performance from Le Normand, who was arguably Atlético’s best defender. Hopefully, the Spain international is beginning to rediscover his form after consecutive solid performances against Getafe over the weekend and Viking tonight. 7.5

Marcos Llorente

Another solid performance from Llorente, who was reliable defensively down the right flank without making much of an impact in attack. 7.5

Johnny Cardoso

When healthy, a pillar of the midfield and out of possession structure. Is taking on a greater role in the attack and finding more opportunity to push forward in transition. A completely different player since his ankle surgery. 7.75

Koke

Koke is timeless, with yet another vintage performance tonight. Prior to being subbed off at the 69th minute, he led the match in touches and kept things ticking as always. 8

Lee Kang-in

A relatively quiet evening for Lee, who created just two chances and saw his effort in the 32nd minute saved by Lubomír Belko. Seemingly shifted into more of a right wing-back role to begin the second half and, despite playing outside his natural position, made some useful defensive contributions. 6.5

Álex Baena

Not the best performance from Baena, though he registered the joint-most shots before being substituted at halftime. Got rest ahead of the double-header at Elche and at Barcelona. 6.5

Alexander Sørloth

You’d be hard-pressed to find anyone within the Atlético Madrid social media community who was happy to see Sørloth in the starting XI, but the Norwegian made us all eat our words with his first goal of the season. He came off in the second half in visible discomfort, so his status for Elche on Saturday is up in the air. 7.75

Julián Alvarez

Scored his first goal for Atlético in over a month with a spectacular free kick that, at first glance, didn’t appear to have crossed the goal line. After a slow start to the season, the Argentine appears to be rediscovering his form. My man of the match. 8.5

Substitutes

Pablo Barrios

A nice shift from Barrios after he was brought on after halftime for Baena. 6.5

Giuliano Simeone

Came on for Alvarez in the 57th minute and provided Llorente with some much-needed defensive support down the right flank. Did what he does best by getting in behind the opposition’s defense and drawing a red card out of Henrik Falchener to effectively put the match on ice. 6.5

Jonathan David

Entered the action in the 57th minute for Lee Kang-in. Made a great run in the dying moments of the match, but unfortunately sent his effort wide of the goal. 6.5

Morten Hjulmand

Solid minutes from Hjulmand once he replaced Koke in the 68th minute. 6

Rodri Mendoza

Good minutes for Mendoza once he was swapped in for Sørloth in the 71st minute. 6
