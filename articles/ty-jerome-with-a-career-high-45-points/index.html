    Ty Jerome leads the charge with 45 points (17-of-25 FGs, 6-of-7 FTs), six rebounds, five assists, a block, and five 3-pointers in win against the Jazz.

    Ty Jerome led this team in shot attempts and was very efficient. The Jazz had no way to stop him, and he was the reason that this game got out of hand so early in the second quarter. He got into the paint whenever he wanted and took advantage of Jaren Jackson being in foul trouble. While he likely won’t continue to lead the team in shot attempts, he could be in line for a big year that could result in him having a top 50 fantasy season.
