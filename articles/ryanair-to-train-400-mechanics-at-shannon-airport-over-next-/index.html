Ryanair To Train 400 Mechanics At Shannon Airport Over Next Five Years - Clare FM

TheTools · 23.10.2026 11:41 · source · permalink

Ryanair is to train over 400 aircraft mechanics at Shannon Airport over the next five years.

It comes as the airline has been granted planning permission for a four-bay maintenance, overhaul and repair hangar and engineering academy.

The company says the €50m development will create 300 highly skilled jobs while the facility will take 80 apprentices a year from 2027 to 2031.

Shannon Chamber CEO Helen Downes has been telling Clare FM’s Daragh Dolan it will be a gamechanger for the region.
