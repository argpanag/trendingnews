Frencken Group’s third-quarter net profit rises 31.3% to S$13 million in 2026

TheTools · 19.11.2026 03:04 · source · permalink

AI-translated article with transliterated names. Read original Chinese article here.

Frencken Group’s net profit rose 31.3% year on year to S$13 million in the third quarter of 2026, while revenue grew 12.8% to S$238.5 million, driven mainly by a significant increase in mechatronics revenue amid strong demand in the semiconductor and medical segments.

In its latest results released after the market closed on Wednesday (18 November), Frencken Group reported revenue of S$642.8 million for the first nine months of this year, up 3.6% year on year. Net profit for the period rose 8.1% to S$29.8 million.

The group said in a statement that its third-quarter growth was driven mainly by a 13.4% year-on-year increase in revenue from its Mechatronics Division to S$214.3 million, largely supported by contributions from the semiconductor and medical segments.

Revenue from the semiconductor segment rose 25.9% year on year to S$124.8 million, driven by its semiconductor business in Asia. The medical segment recorded a 26.1% increase in revenue to S$39.9 million, helped by higher order volumes from a major European customer.

Frencken Group said it maintained its expectation that revenue and net profit for the full 2026 financial year would exceed those of the 2025 financial year, based on current business visibility and the positive business outlook for its key customers. However, it will closely monitor changes in the operating environment, given geopolitical conflicts and heightened uncertainty following the US midterm elections.

The company said: “Backed by solid fundamentals and sound execution capabilities, the Group believes it is well-positioned to capitalise on business opportunities and navigate the volatile macroenvironment.”

Frencken Group shares closed at S$1.71 on Wednesday, up 0.59%.
