Former Clare GAA Joint President Passes Away - Clare FM

TheTools · 18.09.2026 07:33 · source · permalink

Former Clare GAA joint President Naoise Jordan has passed away, at the age of 88.

The Whitegate native, who later lived in Parteen, was elected joint President of Clare GAA in 2020, having given more than seven decades of service to the association.

He played senior hurling for Clare during the 1960s and was also involved in preserving and recording the history of Clare hurling.

Naoise Jordan will be laid to rest in Ballycannon Cemetery, Meelick, on Sunday, following funeral Mass at St Patrick’s Church in Parteen.

He is survived by his daughter Ruth, sons Tony and Raymond, grandchildren and extended family.
