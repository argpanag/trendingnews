Axar Patel Net Worth 2026: IPL Salary, BCCI & Assets

Axar Patel has developed into one of India's most dependable all-rounders across formats. His left-arm orthodox spin, increasingly effective lower-order batting and sharp fielding have made him a valuable international cricketer and a sought-after player in domestic T20 leagues.

That rise has also translated into significant financial growth. Axar's wealth comes from several sources, including Indian Premier League (IPL) contracts, BCCI retainers and match fees, brand endorsements, startup equity and property investments.

In 2026, Axar Patel's estimated net worth is ₹49 crore to ₹65 crore (approximately $6.0 million to $7.8 million). His latest IPL retention, worth ₹16.5 crore per season with Delhi Capitals, represents a major step up from the contracts he commanded earlier in his career.

The IPL has been the biggest contributor to Axar Patel's playing income. His career illustrates how a player's market value can change as performance, experience and versatility increase.

Axar entered the league with Mumbai Indians in 2013 before moving to Kings XI Punjab, where he established himself as a reliable spin-bowling all-rounder. His valuation increased sharply in 2018, when his salary reached ₹12.5 crore.

Delhi Capitals then became a major part of his financial journey. His salary increased from ₹5 crore per season between 2019 and 2021 to ₹9 crore per season from 2022 through 2024. For the 2025 and 2026 seasons, he moved into the franchise's top retention bracket at ₹16.5 crore per year.

His progression from a ₹75 lakh contract to a ₹16.5 crore annual retention reflects the financial premium placed on players who can contribute in multiple areas of the game.

IPL income is only one part of Axar Patel's earnings. As an India international, he also receives compensation through the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI).

For the 2025–26 central contract cycle, Axar is listed in Grade C, which carries a base annual retainer of ₹1 crore.

Players also receive match fees depending on the format:

Test: ₹15 lakh per match

ODI: ₹6 lakh per match

T20I: ₹3 lakh per match

The article estimates that Axar's match-fee income can add approximately ₹1.5 crore to ₹3 crore annually, depending on his international workload.

This distinction is important when assessing a cricketer's annual earnings. The central contract provides a fixed retainer, while match fees vary according to how often the player is selected and appears for India.

Axar's commercial profile has expanded alongside his cricketing reputation. His endorsement portfolio spans consumer electronics, health and wellness, energy solutions, cricket equipment and digital platforms.

His reported brand and business associations include:

FitFeast: Investor and brand ambassador in the health and nutrition sector

Wybor: Consumer electronics and smart-home appliances

Hestur Energy: Electric-vehicle ecosystem

SG Cricket: Professional cricket equipment and gear

Apollo Healthcare: Health and wellness

Fixderma: Personal care

Vision11: Gaming and fantasy sports

GamToss: Digital fan interaction

This also highlights an equity-oriented approach to commercial partnerships. Instead of relying exclusively on traditional endorsement fees, Axar has reportedly taken equity exposure through business relationships.

His reported investment activities include an early equity stake in FitFeast, along with participation in Web3 collectibles through Rario and hardware partnerships involving Nothing Phone.

The reported average endorsement fee is ₹50 lakh to ₹1 crore per deal, although actual commercial agreements can vary significantly based on campaign scope, exclusivity and contract duration.

Property forms another part of Axar Patel's reported asset portfolio.

He owns a designer bungalow in Nadiad, Gujarat, his hometown. The property is described as featuring modern architecture, customized training facilities and a private gym.

Axar has also made reported real estate investments in Ahmedabad and other expanding commercial areas of Gujarat.

Axar's reported garage includes luxury SUVs and premium sedans.
