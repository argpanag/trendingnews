'Press Play' — Amazon Reveals Full PlayStation x Lisa Merch Collection as the Blackpink Star Drops New EP

TheTools · 23.10.2026 15:59 · source · permalink

Lalisa la diva.

Following PlayStation's announcement of a Lisa Edition DualSense controller, Amazon has dropped a full collection based on the new partnership. The full merch drop arrives just as Lisa releases her new EP ‘Press Play.'

The Lisa x PlayStation collection includes new apparel, accessories, and a restock of the Lisa-themed DualSense controller. Amazon has a full page dedicated to the collaboration, with several additional apparel items available exclusively from PlayStation Direct.

Lisa x PlayStation Collection Now Available at Amazon

Shop the Full Collection

The Lisa Edition DualSense controller is now in stock at Amazon at the same price as PS Direct: $84.99. The controller joins an ever-growing list of limited edition PS5 controllers that very rarely get restocks after selling out.

The DualSense controller is still up for preorder with a release date of October 30. The other drop in the collection that’s not available yet is a matching “Lisa” variant of PowerA’s DualSense protection case, releasing November 10.

The rest of the collection is available now. The merchandise features different iterations on the controller’s design: an artistic rendering of Lisa’s eyes, her “LL” logo, and the classic PlayStation button quartet.

Apparel makes up the bulk of the collection. Graphic t-shirts start at $40, with longer sleeve shirts and hoodies reaching up to $65. A “Dad” and “Trucker” hat are both $40, and two very similar tote bag designs are $35. For under $20, you'll find three different keychain designs as well as a metallic sticker sheet.

Several pricier items have also popped up on PlayStation Direct, including a knit beanie design and even more hoodies.

Lisa rose to fame as the leading rapper and dancer of the record-breaking girl group Blackpink. She made her solo debut with the “Lalisa” single album in 2021. Her new EP was released alongside a new music video for the single 'Tarantino.'
