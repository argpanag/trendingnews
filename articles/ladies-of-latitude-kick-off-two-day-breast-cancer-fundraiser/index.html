Ladies of Latitude kick off two-day breast cancer fundraiser

TheTools · 17.10.2026 01:14 · source · permalink

BAY COUNTY, Fla. (WJHG/WECP) - The Ladies of Latitude kicked off a two-day event celebrating breast cancer survivors and those battling the disease, starting with a golf cart parade through the Margaritaville Latitude Watersound neighborhood.

For the entire month of October, the group is raising money for three charities: Fore Her, Breast Cancer Recovery in Action, and METAvivor. The goal is to raise $50,000 for the charities.

After the parade wrapped up, the group started a celebration for breast cancer survivors.

“Last year it was pretty magical. We had the band sing ‘Hallelujah,’ and we all walked down with candelabras, and everyone was just bawling. So, I do not know what they have in store this year. But I am looking forward to being there, and not being in charge, and just being present. I am just happy to be here and have another year of raising money,” said Debbie Olson, founder of Ladies of Latitude.

The Ladies of Latitude will host their third annual Pink-A-Rita Breast Cancer 5K run and walk tomorrow. The event starts at 8 a.m.
