TOP SCHOOLS: September 2026 Civil Engineering Board Exam Results

TheTools · 02.10.2026 10:47 · source · permalink

The Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) announces the summary performance of schools and the list of top-performing schools in the September 2026 Civil Engineer Licensure Exam (CELE) five (5) working days after the exams.

The Civil Engineering board exams were conducted on September 26-27, 2026, at PRC testing centers located in NCR, Baguio, Butuan, Cagayan de Oro, Calapan, Cebu, Davao, Iloilo, Koronadal, Legazpi, Lucena, Pagadian, Palawan, Pampanga, Rosales, Tacloban, Tuguegarao, Zamboanga, and Ilocos Sur.

The Board of Civil Engineering is headed by its Chairperson, Engr. Praxedes P. Bernardo and members, Engr. Pericles P. Dakay and Engr. Romeo A. Estañero.

Release of Results

According to PRC Resolution No. 2113 series of 2025, the September 2026 Civil Engineering Licensure Exam results are targeted for release on October 2, 2026.

However, the release of Civil Engineering Board Exam results may vary, occurring earlier or later than the target date. During the March 2026 CELE, results were released after five (5) working days.

Schedule of Registration

The schedule for online appointments to obtain the new RCEs’ Professional ID and Certificate of Registration will be announced after the release of the September 2026 CELE results.

Date of Registration: Starting November 11, 2026

PRC advises successful examinees to register online through the official website at https://online.prc.gov.ph/. Based on PRC’s requirements for recent Civil Engineer Licensure Examinations, successful examinees should prepare the following:

  1. Notice of Admission (NOA) – for identification purposes only
  2. Completed Oath Form (Panunumpa ng Propesyonal)
  3. Two (2) passport-sized ID photos on a white background, each with a complete name tag
  4. Two (2) sets of documentary stamps
  5. One (1) short brown envelope

PRC emphasizes that successful examinees must complete their registration in person and sign the Roster of Registered Professionals.

The exact registration schedule and requirements will be updated once officially announced by PRC.

Verification of Rating

The Civil Engineer Board exam ratings and test results will be verified online a few days after they are posted.

Successful candidates can visit the official PRC website and utilize the Verification Page to check their ratings in the board examination.

As outlined, a passing candidate should provide the exam name, examination date, application number, first name, last name, and birthdate.

Oath-Taking Ceremonies

The schedule of the oath-taking ceremonies for the new Civil Engineers will be announced after the release of the September 2026 CELE results.

All passers interested in attending the face-to-face mass oath-taking shall register no later than 12:00 PM of the day before the date of oath-taking. Register at http://online.prc.gov.ph to confirm attendance.

Those who cannot attend the mass oath-taking may opt to attend the virtual oath-taking or request a special oath-taking. Once confirmed, the schedule will be posted on the official website.

Passers may schedule an online appointment at http://online.prc.gov.ph and select “e-OATH” as the transaction.

The online registration will close five (5) calendar days before the online oathtaking day. Inductees must print the “OATH OF PROFESSIONAL” form, downloadable from the PRC official website under PRC Online Services.

After the oath-taking, the inductees shall proceed with the Initial Registration by securing the online appointment at http://online.prc.gov.ph.
