21-Man Manchester City Squad Spotted in Training Ahead of AEK Athens Clash - Five Names Missing

TheTools · 19.10.2026 14:33 · source · permalink

Enzo Maresca and his coaching staff have overseen Manchester City's final training session ahead of the club's UEFA Champions League meeting with AEK Athens on Tuesday night with a 21-man squad present at the City Football Academy.

The Premier League leaders return to European action after bouncing back from defeat to Paris Saint-Germain with a narrow victory over English top-flight newcomers Ipswich Town on Saturday afternoon.

Phil Foden's opener - his first goal for Manchester City in 307 days - was accompanied by an Erling Haaland brace, while a 90th minute penalty from Zian Flemming gave Ipswich hope going into 12 minutes of additional time at the Etihad Stadium.

There would be no such late drama however, as City held out to record their seventh consecutive victory in the Premier League whilst maintaining their three-point lead at the top of the division.

Manchester City's last outing in the Champions League saw Paris Saint-Germain end an eight-game winning run across competitions with a 2-4 win at the Etihad Stadium, despite the host's best efforts in a strong second-half performance.

A blistering first-half from the Parisians saw them race into a half-time lead of 0-3 thanks to goals from Desire Doue, Ousmane Dembele, and Vitinha between the 13th and 32nd minute of the game.

Erling Haaland pulled a goal back on 53 minutes and scored a second on 75 minutes, although it was Maghnes Akliouche's 62nd minute strike that largely put the overall outcome beyond any doubt despite City's strong showing.

Now, as AEK Athens prepare to become Manchester City's third opponents in this season's UEFA Champions League campaign, Enzo Maresca has finalised his preparations for the meeting with the Greek side.

Goalkeepers: Gianluigi Donnarumma, Geronimo Rulli, Marcus Bettinelli

Defenders: Rico Lewis, Abdukodir Khusanov, Ruben Dias, Marc Guehi, Vitor Reis, Kaden Braithwaite, Josko Gvardiol, Rayan Ait-Nouri

Midfielders: Ayyoub Bouaddi, Elliot Anderson, Enzo Fernandez, Rayan Cherki, Phil Foden

Forwards: Ryan McAidoo, Allan, Iliman Ndiaye, Jeremy Doku, Floyd Samba

The most notable absentee from Manchester City's opening 15 minutes of training ahead of Tuesday night's clash with AEK Athens was star striker Erling Haaland, who appeared to be struggling with a fitness issue in the final moments of Saturday's 3-2 win over Ipswich.

Enzo Maresca told reporters after the game and during his pre-match press conference on Monday that Haaland was "OK", but it seems as though no risks are being taken concerning the Norwegian superstar ahead of their clash with the Greek side.

Also absent from the session were the other names who appeared to have sustained issues against the Tractor Boys, with all of Matheus Nunes, Nico O'Reilly, and Mateo Kovacic missing training.

As confirmed earlier on Monday, Antoine Semenyo missed the session as the Ghanaian winger starts his eight-week injury lay-off following an issue also sustained during the weekend's victory in the Premier League.
