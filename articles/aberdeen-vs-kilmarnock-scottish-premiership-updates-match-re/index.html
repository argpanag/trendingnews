    14:44 BST

    Scottish League 1

    Unbeaten leaders Hamilton Academical visit fourth-placed East Kilbride in the game of the day in the third tier, with last season's League 2 champions having won all five of their home games this season, scoring 21 goals in the process.

    Ross County, one point behind with a game in hand, remain unbeaten in 11 outings this season and are eyeing a ninth consecutive win as they visit East Fife, who are without a win in five.

    Third-top Alloa Athletic are unbeaten in five as they visit Cove Rangers, while Airdrieonians are seeking a first league win as they head to Dumfries to face a Queen of the South side still without a point.

  2. McCann ignoring 'outside noise'published at 14:42 BST
    14:42 BST

    Aberdeen v Kilmarnock (15:00)

    Kilmarnock manager Neil McCann tells BBC Scotland: "I was pleased with the second-half performance the other night, without getting what we deserved. I've given them another opportunity to go out there and tell everybody they're a real good side.

    "There's no problem keeping the belief high, that's a constant from me and the staff. They know it's early, they know there's been a lot of changes and at times they've underperformed. But there has been signs in most of the games where we've shown our true selves.

    "We were really strong in training yesterday, we've travelled well and prepared well. Four league games in, we're disappointed with how we've started.

    "The modern way is about understanding where we are. The outside noise is very short-termism. I've not applied any extra stuff to the players. They understand we've not got the points we're capable of getting."

  3. Kelsy 'a bit different to other Rangers strikers'published at 14:40 BST
    14:40 BST

    Rangers v Motherwell (15:00)

    Rangers assistant manager Alan Archibald tells BBC Scotland: "It's been a bit manic over the last couple of weeks, a lot of comings and goings.

    "A good strong squad to pick from. You see that today with some guys missing out.

    "[Kevin Kelsy is] a wee bit different to the other strikers. He's got a physicality, he'll run in behind, he's a real athlete and hopefully he'll hit the ground running and hopefully get some minutes today.

    "[Badredine Bouanani arrived] a bit later. He's not done any pre-season with us. He just got there yesterday morning for a training session. He'll get a couple of days with the fitness guys. He'll be ready for Wednesday night [against St Mirren].

    "[Motherwell are a] good footballing team. We need to make sure we stop them. At the same time, we've got to really play our game. Our front four players have been really good recently. We need to get the ball as much as we can and obviously exploit the gaps that Motherwell leave".

  8. New signing Kelsy on bench for Rangerspublished at 14:28 BST
    14:28 BST

    Rangers v Motherwell (15:00)

    There's no place in Rangers' starting XI for new signing Kevin Kelsy but the Venezuela striker signed from Portland Timbers is on the bench.

    14:24 BST

    Aberdeen v Kilmarnock (15:00)

    Kilmarnock manager Neil McCann changes three from the disappointing 1-0 loss to St Mirren on Wednesday night - and he reverts to a back-four.

  10. Happe, Yogane, Smith and Ntelo in for Donspublished at 14:19 BST
    14:19 BST

    Aberdeen v Kilmarnock (15:00)

    Aberdeen head coach Stephen Robinson changes four for the visit of Kilmarnock, with centre-back Dan Happe passing a fitness test to return in defence.

    On the wings, Tony Yogane and Lewis Smith are welcomed back. That's despite Robinson saying Smith's "fitness levels need to be much, much better" after the midweek defeat to Celtic.

    There's also a start for forward Mitchy Ntelo in place of Ayoub Mouloua, who is on the bench along with Mats Knoester, Gavin Molloy and Alexander Briedl.

    New goalkeeper signing Daniel Klein is on the bench.

    Given all the chat today about this SFA proposal, it's worth nothing there are five Scots in the starting line-up and another two on the bench. Of the seven, midfielder Findlay Marshall is the only player under the age of 21.
