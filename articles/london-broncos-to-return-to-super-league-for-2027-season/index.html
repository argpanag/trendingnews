London Broncos to return to Super League for 2027 season

TheTools · 27.11.2026 17:56 · source · permalink

London Broncos have played at the Cherry Red Records Stadium since 2022

  • Published
    25 November 2026

London Broncos will return to Super League in 2027 after being confirmed as the competition's 15th team.

The club will return to the top flight for the first time since 2024 after being selected following a decision to expand the competition from 14 to 15 teams.

The Broncos enjoyed a dominant 2026 campaign in the Championship, winning the League Leaders' Shield, Grand Final and 1895 Cup to complete a historic treble.

Super League clubs voted in favour of expansion earlier this month, with the Rugby Football League (RFL) then tasked with identifying the additional team.

London finished 16th in the 2026 IMG club gradings, below Championship rivals Barrow Raiders, who were initially considered for the available place.

However, Barrow and the RFL recognised the Cumbrian club would not be ready to compete in Super League in 2027, leaving London as the next candidate.

Super League chair Peter Hutton said: "We are delighted to welcome London Broncos back to Super League for 2027.

"This is an exciting moment for the club and its supporters, and an opportunity to build a lasting presence for our competition in the capital."

The fixtures for the new season will be announced on Thursday, 3 December at 08:00 GMT.
