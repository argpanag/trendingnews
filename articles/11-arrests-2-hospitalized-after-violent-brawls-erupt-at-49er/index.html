11 arrests, 2 hospitalized after violent brawls erupt at 49ers-Raiders game

TheTools · 10.11.2026 16:52 · source · permalink

Santa Clara police promise further arrests after brutal fights spread from Levi's Stadium stands to parking lots, prompting heavy criticism of venue security and crowd control.

Santa Clara police are asking for anyone who witnessed a caught-on-camera brawl outside Levi’s Stadium during Sunday’s San Francisco 49ers-Las Vegas Raiders game to come forward. Marianne Favro reports.

Santa Clara police reported 11 arrests, 45 ejections and two hospitalizations following a series of violent altercations during Sunday’s 49ers-Raiders game at Levi’s Stadium.

The chaos spilled from the stands into the parking lot despite a massive security presence involving some 30 outside agencies. Both hospitalized individuals have since been released, but investigators are actively working to identify and arrest others involved in the violence.

Videos circulating on social media captured fans trading blows, people knocked to the ground and others left motionless in both the upper deck and outside the stadium. Law enforcement analyst Michael Leininger warned that the situation could have had fatal consequences.

"There could have been deaths associated with these savage beatings," Leininger said. "You saw the one individual's knees buckle, his arms swing down, his head struck the pavement. He’s lucky to be alive."

Leininger suggested that officials reevaluate staffing levels and alcohol sales at the venue.

"I believe the need to increase staffing, sadly, that needs to happen," Leininger said. "I do believe they need to look after their alcohol sales and perhaps pick a different angle."

Fans expressed shock over the hostility. Thomas Vincent, a 49ers fan from Sweden, said, "I’m sad to see it. What are people doing? It’s just a game. I don’t know why people need to be hostile. It's crazy."

The 49ers condemned the violence in a statement, expressing a zero-tolerance policy for fighting at Levi’s Stadium and praising police and security teams for their swift response.

Investigators are asking anyone with video footage of the fights to come forward as they continue the search for suspects.
