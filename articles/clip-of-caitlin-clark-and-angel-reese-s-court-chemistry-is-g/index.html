Clip of Caitlin Clark and Angel Reese's 'court chemistry' is getting a lot of attention from fans

TheTools · 03.09.2026 11:43 · source · permalink

Caitlin Clark and Angel Reese have been rivals for a number of years, but they will once again be working as teammates at this month’s FIBA Women’s Basketball World Cup.

Team USA will kick off their tournament this coming Friday with a group game against China.

They then face Italy on Sunday before clashing with the Czech Republic on Monday, September 7.

Ahead of their group campaign getting underway, TNT Sports U.S. shared an old clip of Clark and Reese playing together for the USA and mentioned their ‘court chemistry’.

They stated: “That CC and Angel Reese court chemistry. Their connection in Berlin is about to be DIFFERENT.”

Fans react to Caitlin Clark and Angel Reese’s ‘chemistry’

The clip has gone pretty viral and is close to reaching 5,000 likes at the time of writing.

Many fans have also commented on the post to express their opinions about the pair’s chemistry.

One fan claimed: “The chemistry is giving bestie energy and everyone noticed.”

But another suggested: “They hate each other you can tell.”

A third echoed: “They hate each other so much too.”

Another stated: “They really hate each other —- what kinda dap up was that.”

“I do find it pretty funny they are perfect teammates for each other’s game,” tweeted a fifth.

Still, regardless of what some fans think, it will be interesting to see how they fare together for the USA in the coming tournament.
