We’re continuing a multistep process to ensure Operator hair quality looks consistent across the wide range of platforms our players use to play Call of Duty. To address the root of the issue, the weekend 2 update ensured temporal dithering always remains on, which improves the way nearby colors and brightness levels blend across successive frames. Today, we’re refining streaming logic so that Operator models on lower-end hardware only ever render at their intended quality. We’re also working on making hair look better at long distances by launch. The end result: a nourished, fuller-looking hairline for Price however you play.

Friday AUGUST 28

 

The Modern Warfare 4 Open Beta arrives at 10 AM PT today. Thank you for sharing your experiences, best plays, and feedback with us during Weekend 1. Your input is helping shape the experience as we continue to make improvements.

 

We’ve made some sweeping changes to the game with focus on a few key areas: TTK, Spawns, Movement, Killstreaks and Aim Assist.

 

TIME-TO-KILL

After some updates last week, we still weren’t where we wanted to be with time-to-kill. We’re aiming for a slight increase to TTK so instead of changing base health, we focused on weapon tuning to improve durability without making weapons feel too weak at range. Every single weapon in our Beta has received an update for Weekend 2. These changes create more survivability and room for skill expression in combat.There’s more work to do here and we’re looking forward to hearing your feedback.

 

SPAWNS

In the final hours of Weekend 1, we adjusted spawns and spawn logic across all respawn modes to better separate players. Additionally, we greatly reduced spawning on squad members. 

 

For Weekend 2, we wanted to build on these changes with adjustments to Hardpoint specifically. We feel the mode plays best when spawns are consistent and predictable. The prototype in the Beta today is a step toward that vision, but it is far from perfect. You may still see some bad spawns on smaller maps, and on larger maps you may spawn too far from the action. We are excited about the direction and will be improving things with your feedback.

 

Finally, we added Battle Hardened to the temporary spawn protection, reducing the effects of flash and concussion grenades immediately after spawning.

 

  • Updated spawn logic to better separate players [added at the end of Beta Weekend 1]
  • Deprioritized spawning near teammates [added at the end of Beta Weekend 1]
  • Partially improved Hardpoint spawn system to improve predictability
  • Added Battle Hardened to temporary spawn protection
  • Temporarily disabled Hardpoint on Rooftops because of a spawn bug

 

MOVEMENT

We saw a lot of feedback about Tactical Sprint over Weekend 1, and we know there are a range of opinions about it. We have removed Tactical Sprint by default, which we think improves the pace of combat and gives the game a more classic feel. For players who prefer a faster playstyle, we moved it to a Perk and enhanced it by reducing its sprint-to-fire speed penalty. We have also adjusted Tac Sprint and slide speeds to rebalance them against each other.

 

This is an experiment for Beta which we hope improves the pace of the game and enhances player choice. If we decide to stick with this change, we need to consider its place in the Perk table and which other adjustments to Perks go along with it.

INTRODUCING ZODIAC

Zodiac is the newest Resurgence map coming to Warzone. Located in South Korea within the broader setting of MW4, the map drops players into a weapons manufacturing facility where weapons are built and shipped around the world. The setting gives the map plenty of personality, with 10 unique and varied points of interest such as the Old Mine, Solar, Trackline, and the sewers. Together, they offer a mix of striking visuals, combat scenarios, different elevations, and plenty to explore.

Across the map, players will encounter moving solar panels, furnace conveyor belts, dynamic signage warning them about incoming trains, and intense fights across multiple levels, including sniper battles from the burning Admin Tower. Meanwhile, the incoming gas storm makes sure no one can stay comfortable for too long. Built by the team behind Rebirth Island and Vondel, Zodiac brings years of Warzone experience together with new mechanics, memorable moments, and plenty for players to discover.


THE PLAYLIST

Tomorrow, we’ll kick things off with Resurgence Quads, and will rotate in Casual Resurgence Quads across the duration of the Open Beta. As we focus on Beta, Resurgence Quads and Casual Resurgence Quads will be temporarily disabled in the live game. We want as many Resurgence players as possible to jump into Beta for the first time and share their feedback so we can make improvements ahead of launch later this year.


GAMEPLAY

The movement you’ll experience tomorrow in Resurgence will be closely aligned with MW4 Multiplayer. You’ll find that you’ll be able to navigate Zodiac in a more grounded and deliberate  way, without sacrificing the fluidity and momentum that larger maps in Warzone call for.

 

This Open Beta features MW4’s arsenal of weapons, with Default Loadouts and ground loot weapons hand-crafted by our team. While Custom Loadouts will not be available, we’ve ensured that tomorrow’s Default Loadouts offer a solid variety of weapon and equipment combinations. As for where things deviate from Multiplayer, we’ve tuned weapon damage ranges, bullet velocity, ADS speed, sprint-to-fire speed, and mobility to meet Warzone’s specific needs - with weapon time-to-kill designed to feel familiar to players coming from the live game.

 

We’re also introducing gameplay elements to Resurgence designed to create more emergent moments throughout a match. Fuel barrels will be the first taste of this, giving you a dynamic way to interact with the environment and another reason to pay attention to where you’re taking cover. 

 

Killstreaks will include a broader selection from Multiplayer,, with the most powerful ones remaining intentionally rare. You’ll also find new ways to defend yourself, like the SAM Turret Field Upgrade which lets you counter aerial threats.

 

Among other changes, Recon Contracts now reward a single-use Insight Killstreak, which will give you the freedom to choose when to activate your earned Circle Peek - providing a massive intel boost when timed correctly. You’ll notice that Supply Run Contracts have been updated to provide a one-time discount and an upgrade to Buy Stations with exclusive items.

 

As this is still a Beta build, some systems and features are not available in the Open Beta but are planned for launch later this year, including Daily Challenges, as well as some Killstreaks, Tactical Equipment, and Field Upgrades. We’ll share information about the full slate of these systems, along with any future content, as we get closer to launch.
