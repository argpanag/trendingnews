Who Is The Voice Australia 2026 Winner?

TheTools · 27.09.2026 07:38 · source · permalink

After weeks upon weeks of knockout rounds, belts and riffs, the winner of The Voice Australia 2026 has been crowned. Here's who won The Voice Australia 2026. 

 

This article contains The Voice Australia 2026 spoilers. You've been warned. 

It's without a doubt that this year's shebang has featured a bunch of twists — and I'm not talking about the movement on those giant red chairs. 

In the 2026 season, The Voice Australia implemented a first-in-its-history plot twist where Tones And I was recruited as a secret fifth judge who sneakily stole contestants who failed to pass blind auditions and trained them up for a second chance. 

But now, with blind auditions, knockouts, quarterfinals and semifinals done, we've got our final four.

From Kate Miller-Heidke, we've got Daisha Auda; from Team Mel C, we've got Yung Milla, Tommy Novak from Richard Marx's team, and The 60Four from Ronan Keating's team. 

While we want all of these talented stars to win that prize, unfortunately, can only be one winner of The Voice Australia 2026. 

After a phenomenal final performance and a duet with coach Mel C, Yung Milla has become the winner of The Voice 2026. 

“I just can't believe it, I'm just so grateful,” he said before thanking Sporty Spice. 

In this year's shebang, The Voice Australia winner will not only get bragging rights, but they will also cop a $100,000 cash prize and an all-inclusive Hive Sound Studios recording and development package.
