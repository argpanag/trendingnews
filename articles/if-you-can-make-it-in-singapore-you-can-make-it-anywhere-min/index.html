    [SINGAPORE] Singapore’s F&B sector has a reputation for being extremely cut-throat and unforgiving.

    High costs, discerning consumers and an influx of international brands, particularly from mainland China, combine to create a tough operating environment for established players and new entrants alike.

    Yet, for the CEO and chairman of casual-dining restaurant operator Minor Food, it is exactly the reason to have a footprint in the city-state.
