New details released after death of Chad Lowe's teenage daughter

TheTools · 02.10.2026 15:36 · source · permalink

The family called Fiona 'the light of our lives' and encouraged those struggling to contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Chad Lowe's daughter dead at 13

Actor Chad Lowe and his family are mourning the death of his 13-year-old daughter, Fiona, remembering the "smart, creative" teen who loved to dance as the "light of our lives."

This story discusses suicide. If you or someone you know is having thoughts of suicide, please contact the National Suicide Prevention Lifeline at 988 or 1-800-273-TALK (8255).

Chad Lowe's 13-year-old daughter, Fiona, died at a Los Angeles hospital on Sept. 29, according to the County of Los Angeles Medical Examiner's office. 

Her official cause of death was ruled a suicide, per the County of Los Angeles Medical Examiner's office.

"We are deeply heartbroken by the loss of our beloved Fiona," the family said in a statement provided to Page Six on Tuesday.

"She was a loving young girl who was smart, creative and loved to dance," the statement continued. "She was adored by her sisters and she was deeply loved by her parents. In fact, she was the light of our lives."

"Her passing is devastating for our entire family and we ask for prayers and privacy at this time."

The family concluded its statement by encouraging anyone struggling with mental health issues to contact the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline by calling or texting 988.
