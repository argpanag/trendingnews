Emily Blunt and John Krasinski Sold One of their Brooklyn Heights Apartments

TheTools · 15.10.2026 21:01 · source · permalink

John Krasinski and Emily Blunt appear to have downsized their Brooklyn Heights suite of apartments. A trust tied to the Hollywood power couple unloaded a ninth-floor apartment in the Standish for $6.5 million in an off-market deal this past June, according to property records that were made public on Wednesday. As you might expect with the Beaux-Arts tower known for both its celebrity residents and reputation for privacy, the apartment’s buyer was masked by an LLC.

Krasinski and Blunt first bought in the converted 1903 limestone-and-brick hotel in 2018, paying $11 million for a pair of units that covered the entirety of the eighth floor and added up to a potentially Goliath combination. (The couple’s tween daughters, Hazel and Violet, probably enjoyed running around the space instead of using the building’s communal playroom.) The couple’s presence helped cement the Standish as a magnet for the boldface crowd, following Matt Damon, who paid $16.7 million for a 6,000-square-foot penthouse in 2017 (and subsequently craned in his family’s furniture). Earlier this year, Adam Driver paid $11.5 million for three sixth-floor units totaling 3,500 square feet. (All, of course, come with access to an amenities package that includes a 24/7 doorman, a fitness center, and a rooftop terrace with views of the harbor.)

Krasinski and Blunt’s brief ownership of the ninth-floor apartment is a bit of a mystery. The same trust that the couple used to buy the eighth-floor units paid $5 million for the apartment upstairs in January 2024. According to records, 9B is nearly 1,600 square feet, and similar apartments in the building’s B-line are three-bed, two-baths. Permits filed with the city indicate that they renovated after buying it. Although 9B is located right above the Krasinski-Blunts’ full-floor spread and could have been part of an ambitious plan for expanding their Frankenmansion-in-the-sky, the timing of the purchase also came right around their 2024 Golden Globes appearance that sparked rumors the couple was cruising for a divorce. (The couple subsequently went into damage control, with an “insider” telling Us Weekly that they were totally fine and chuckling over the speculation.) Was this apartment an office, a generously sized place for a nanny, a guest apartment, part of a duplex plan that didn’t shake out? We’d ask Emily Blunt–as–Emily Charlton, but you already know what she’d say: “You went upstairs? You went upstairs. Oh my God.”
