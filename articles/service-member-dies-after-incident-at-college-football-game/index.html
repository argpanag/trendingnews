SAN ANTONIO, Texas -- A service member was injured and later died after an incident at the University of Texas at San Antonio football game on Saturday.

Nellis Air Force Base Public Affairs issued a statement saying that the service member — assigned to the 6th Combat Training Squadron, Detachment 2 — was injured at the stadium and died at a local hospital.

UTSA was playing Navy at the Alamodome in San Antonio, Texas.
