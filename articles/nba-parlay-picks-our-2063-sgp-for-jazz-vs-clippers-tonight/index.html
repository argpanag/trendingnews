NBA Parlay Picks for Jazz vs Clippers

The Angle: Guards Shine in Los Angeles Tonight

Darryn Peterson 20+ Points

Of the top teams that drafted in the top five last season, the Jazz have the worst record at 1-13. As a result, some have likely already stopped paying attention to them. However, Darryn Peterson has already proven to be a hit individually as the No. 2 overall pick.

In November, the 19-year-old is averaging 20 points, 4.3 rebounds, and 4.5 assists on 43/35/89 shooting splits. He had scored 20 points in five straight games before 17 against the Spurs. Overall, he has averaged 23.1 points over his last six games.

Tonight's matchup is much better as the Clippers are 22nd in points allowed and 18th to shooting guards. Peterson should be able to capitalize as he has had at least a 24% usage rate in each of his last five games. Jazz fans would like that number to be even higher.

Pick: Darryn Peterson 20+ Points

Keyonte George Over 6.5 Assists

In addition to the losing, some fraction of Jazz fans have been frustrated by the team's unwillingness to run the offense through Peterson and develop him as the lead guard.

However, Keyonte George had a breakout season last year, averaging 23.6 points and 6.1 assists and earning the trust of head coach Will Hardy. George had a 27.9% usage rate and is actually at 28.8% through 13 games despite more talent being added to the roster.

After averaging over six assists per game for the first time in his career last season, George is averaging 6.6 per game this season. He has had seven or more in each of his last six games and is averaging eight assists per game over that span.

George also has seven assists in three of his last five games against the Clippers, including nine in his last two.

The Clippers are allowing the fourth-fewest assists to point guards, but also gave eight to Collin Gillespie and 10 to Davion Mitchell recently. If George's recent numbers against them continue, he will have similar success.

Pick: Keyonte George Over 6.5 Assists

Darius Garland 20+ Points

Speaking of matchup history, Darius Garland has had 23 points and eight assists in two of his last three games with Cleveland.

Now in Los Angeles, Garland has more volume without Donovan Mitchell and, more importantly, is healthy. He is averaging 21.3 points per game, which would be his highest in four years.

He enters tonight's game with 24 points in four of his last five games. In that span, he has a 28.3% usage and is shooting a blistering 54.2% from the field and 42.6% from three. Tonight might be his best matchup of the bunch as the Jazz are 23rd in defensive rating and 24th in points allowed.

Utah is 25th in points allowed to point guards. LaMelo Ball, Anthony Black, Dylan Harper, and Castle combined for 20 points recently against the Jazz, and Fred VanVleet cleared his line as well. Garland should hit 20 points again tonight and potentially 25 as well.

Pick: Darius Garland 20+ Points

Keaton Wagler 15+ Points, Rebounds & Assists

Of these four guards, Keaton Wagler is the only one that comes off the bench. Despite that (and shooting 35% from the field), the fifth overall pick has scored 10 points in 10 of his first 12 NBA games while averaging 12.3 points per game.

Additionally, he has had two rebounds in 11 of 12 games and two assists in 10 of 12 games. He is averaging over three per game in each category and 19.1 points, rebounds, and assists combined this season.

He has recorded 15 PRA in nine of 12 games this season. Like Garland, he will benefit from the Jazz's shoddy perimeter defense. However, they are also last in the league in PRA allowed to shooting guards. Wagler should take advantage of that when he enters the game tonight.

Pick: Keaton Wagler 15+ Points, Rebounds & Assists

NBA Parlay Picks for Pistons vs Spurs

The Angle: Wemby & Spurs Dominate Detroit

Spurs -9.5

The Pistons and Spurs have been on similar rebuilding timelines over the last couple of years after spending multiple years drafting in the top five of the NBA Draft.

Each team has now developed into playoff contenders with the Spurs advancing to the NBA Finals last season, perhaps a year ahead of schedule.

Over the last two years, each team has recorded a sweep in the season series. The Spurs took both meetings by double digits last season — 114-103 and 121-106 — and tonight may result in a similar outcome.

The Pistons got off to a 6-1 start and still sit in third place in the East. However, they are 3-4 against teams above .500 and three of the losses have come in their last five games. They lost to the 76ers by six, to the Lakers by five without Luka Doncic, and to the Heat by 20 on Saturday.

Meanwhile, the Spurs have won 12 straight games since a 0-2 start, and eight of them have been by double digits. Tonight, they host a shorthanded Pistons team.

The Pistons have already been playing without Jalen Duren since the second game of the season. Tonight, they will also be without Duncan Robinson, Ron Holland, Tauren Prince, and likely Isaiah Joe. Tonight, however, Duren's absence will be the most problematic.

Pick: Spurs -9.5

Victor Wembanyama 40+ Points & Rebounds

As a Pistons fan, you won't see me ranking Duren among the league's best rim protectors. However, with Isaiah Stewart now in Memphis, Duren's absence and, more importantly, size have been seriously missed.

Initially, the Pistons started Paul Reed and John Collins together, but lately Collins has slid to center in a smaller lineup.

As a result, the Pistons have been getting ambushed in the paint. They have fallen from third in points in the paint allowed per game to 10th and are allowing 52.8 points in the paint over their last five games.

Notably, Anthony Davis had two games against the Pistons with 44 points and 13 rebounds, followed by 30 points and 15 rebounds. Joel Embiid had 26 points and 15 rebounds while Giannis Antetokoumpo had 34 points and 14 rebounds on Saturday.

Each of those All-Stars had 40 PR, and now the Pistons have to deal with Wembanyama. Last season, Wembanyama had 21 points and 17 rebounds and 38 points and 16 rebounds in two games against the Pistons.

Wemby has shown more of a willingness to play to his size this season. He leads the NBA in dunks and is shooting 75.3% on 9.6 two-point attempts inside of 10 feet per game.

As a result, Wembanyama is nearly averaging a point per minute this season- 28.6 points in 29.8 minutes. He has scored 25 points in 10 of 14 games and had 29 points in eight of them. Additionally, he has 10 double-doubles this season. He has recorded 40 PR in six of his last 10 games, averaging 43.1 in that span.

He has shown he does not need many minutes to pile up his gaudy numbers, so if this game gets out of hand, he could still clear this line in three quarters.

Pick: Victor Wembanyama 40+ Points & Rebounds

Cade Cunningham Under 25.5 Points

Wembanyama is enjoying his best offensive season, but what truly makes him special is his ability to wreck a game with his defense. With Collins at center, it will be easier for Mitch Johnson to have Ausar Thompson as Wembanyama's defensive "assignment" tonight.

However, because Thompson still has not been a willing threat to shoot the ball, Wembanyama will be able to sag off him and protect the rim. In turn, that will make things more difficult for Cunningham.

After scoring 25 points in just four of his first 10 games, Cunningham had 52 points without a turnover against the Wizards before dropping 33 against the Heat. He is now averaging 25.7 points per game. However, he scored 16 and 26 points in two games against the Spurs, shooting 5-for-26 and 10-for-26 in those meetings.

Cunningham will have a difficult matchup with Stephon Castle guarding him initially, and Castle frustrated him in Detroit last season. Castle is emerging as an elite defender in his own right, but his job is easier with Wembanyama backing him up if he gets beat.

Additionally, Detroit's spacing could be even more problematic without Robinson, the team's best shooter. That will make it easier for the Spurs to focus on slowing down Cunningham.

Pick: Cade Cunningham Under 25.5 Points
