ACC Premier Cup: Nepal take on Oman today in decisive semifinal

TheTools · 12.09.2026 04:30 · source · permalink

Subscribe Now

KATHMANDU, Sept 10: Nepal will face Oman in the semifinal of the ACC Men’s Premier Cup today. The match at Bayuemas Oval in Kuala Lumpur will begin at 7:15 am Nepal time.

Nepal reached the last four with an impressive performance in the group stage and will now look to secure a place in the final. The team advanced from Group A after a consistent run. A victory over Oman will send Nepal into the final, where they will compete for the title.

Oman will pose a tough challenge for Nepal in the semifinal. Nepal have performed well with both bat and ball, but will need to maintain their form in this crucial match. A strong start in the opening overs and sustained pressure with the ball could help Nepal secure a place in the final.

Oman will also enter the match with the same goal of reaching the final. The contest at Bayuemas Oval is therefore expected to be closely fought. The match will be decisive for both teams in their bid for the title. If Nepal beat Oman and reach the final, their chances of winning the tournament will strengthen further, making today’s game a much-awaited contest for Nepali cricket fans.

The other semifinal, between the UAE and Hong Kong, will also be played today, starting at 7:15 am Nepal time. The winners of the two semifinals will meet in the final for the title.

The tournament winner will earn the opportunity to play in the Asia Cup.
