Pelicans' Derik Queen: Starting Saturday

TheTools · 22.11.2026 03:00 · source · permalink

Queen will start Saturday against the Spurs.

Queen will earn his first start of the season with Zion Williamson (thigh) out, and he'll be joined in the first unit by Jeremiah Fears, Herbert Jones, Saddiq Bey and Yves Missi. Per 36 minutes this season, Queen is averaging 20.1 points, 10.8 rebounds, 3.7 assists, 1.0 three-pointers, 1.5 steals and 1.4 swats.
