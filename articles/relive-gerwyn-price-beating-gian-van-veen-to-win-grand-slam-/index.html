Relive Gerwyn Price beating Gian van Veen to win Grand Slam of Darts title

Summary

  • Gerwyn Price beats Gian van Veen 16-11 to win Grand Slam of Darts title

  • Success sees Price end four-year wait for a major PDC title

  • Semi-finals results: Doets 13-16 Price & Van Gerwen 11-16 Van Veen

  • Defending champion Luke Littler knocked out in last 16

  1. See you next time!published at 21:23 GMT 22 November
    21:23 GMT 22 November

    So then, after losing in three major finals this year Gerwyn Price finally gets his title in an event he's loved over the years as he claims a fourth Grand Slam of Darts title in Wolverhampton.

    Along with the Eric Bristow Trophy, £200,000 first prize and first major title since 2022, the Welshman moves up to number three in the world ahead of the big one next month - the World Darts Championship.

    And given the record Grand Slam winners have at Alexandra Palace recently he'll head to the sport's biggest event full of confidence to go along with the wonderful consistency he's shown all year.

    Read our full report on the final and make sure you join us again next time for more darting drama.

  2. Price eyes world title nextpublished at 21:15 GMT 22 November
    21:15 GMT 22 November

    The last four winners of the Grand Slam of Darts have doubled up and gone on to win the World Darts Championship at Alexandra Palace.

    That's a trend Gerwyn Price is fully aware of.

    "The sweet thing about this is that the last few years whoever's won it has gone on to win the worlds, so come on!" he said on Sky Sports.

    "I'm chuffed to win this one, but I want the big one at the end of the year."

  3. Van Veen 'disappointed'published at 21:11 GMT 22 November
    21:11 GMT 22 November

    Gian van Veen on Sky Sports: "It's been a very positive week but very disappointed not to get it over the line.

    "It was a very close game and he took out a lot of good finishes with me waiting and I missed a couple of chances. I think the 16-11 scoreline flattered him a bit.

    "That's what Gezzy does he always puts everyone under pressure and he took his chances and that's why he deserved to win it today."

    21:07 GMT 22 November

    Price 16-11 Van Veen

    Gerwyn Price on Sky Sports: "It's amazing, I've had some close calls this year when I probably should have won games and tournaments but when it finally comes around it's nice.

    "I was probably more nervous in this game than ever - I've lost so many finals. Just to keep the media off my back, I mean I do mind and I don't mind, but just to keep them off my back.

    "Now they can leave me alone!"

    There wasn't too much between them, but Gerwyn Price was just mustard with the big finishes - with five ton-plus outshots and all at crucial times.

    Price took out 145, 150, 132, 100 and then 118 to finish it off.
