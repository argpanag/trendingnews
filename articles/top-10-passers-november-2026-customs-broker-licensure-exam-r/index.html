Top 10 Passers: November 2026 Customs Broker Licensure Exam Results

TheTools · 23.11.2026 08:50 · source · permalink

MANILA, Philippines — The Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) releases the results of the November 2026 Customs Brokers Licensure Exam (CuBLE), including the list of topnotchers or top 10 passers.

PRC administered the CuBLE in Manila, Butuan, Cebu, Lucena, Davao, and Koronadal on November 17 and 18, 2026.

Previous Examinations

In the November 2025 Customs Brokers Licensure Examination (CuBLE), Carlos Jophet Dicdican Enopia of Philippine Christian University (PCU)–Manila topped the board with a rating of 95.00%. Ram Christian Almeron Gimeno of Holy Cross of Davao College followed in second place with 94.25%.

The November 2024 examination was led by Adrian Llemit Gacos of DMMA College of Southern Philippines, who earned 91.75%. Bianca Louise Villar Rabino of Lyceum of the Philippines University (LPU)–Manila ranked second with 91.50%.

For the November 2023 CuBLE, Trisha Marie Veneracion Siy of Holy Cross of Davao College secured the top spot with 91.25%, while Ivy Claire Domingo Tan placed second with 91.00%.

In the November 2022 examination, Keith Ann Maramot Casa of LPU–Batangas City and John Ro Aaron Hintay Ramos of Batangas State University (BatSU)–Batangas City shared the top spot with identical ratings of 91.50%. Christine Veronica Gonzales Calingasan, also from BatSU–Batangas City, placed second with 91.00%.
