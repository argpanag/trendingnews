Tennessee basketball leads Tennessee State 42-37 at halftime, live updates

TheTools · 13.11.2026 10:01 · source · permalink

Tennessee basketball has one more tune-up game against a mid-major opponent before welcoming a top-five team to Knoxville.

The No. 9 Vols (2-0) play Tennessee State (1-2) on Nov. 12 (7 p.m. ET, SEC Network+) at Food City Center. They've won their first two games of the season in blowout fashion and will look not notch one more victory ahead of a Nov. 17 showdown with No. 5 Michigan State.

Tennessee has come out slow, then rallied for strong second halves in wins over Wofford and Radford. Despite beating the Highlanders by 23 on Nov. 9, coach Rick Barnes called the Vols' offense "atrocious," so getting off to a faster start will be a priority vs. Tennessee State.

The Tigers bring a number of experienced transfers like Aaron Bradshaw, who started his career at Kentucky, and Ty-Laur Johnson, who has played at Louisville and Wake Forest.

What to know about Tennessee State

Stephen Thompson

The Tigers play at one of the fastest tempo in Division I basketball, averaging 74.3 possessions across three games played this season. They do it by creating turnovers themselves (99th nationally in defensive turnover rate) but the pace also leads to a lot of turnovers on the end (362nd in offensive turnover rate).

Tennessee State scores 39% of its total points off free throws, the second highest rate in the country, and are averaging 104.7 points per game, which ranks fifth nationally. The caveat is that two of its first three games, ones in which they've combined to score 250 total points, have been against non-Division I opponents. In their lone contest vs. another Division I squad, the Tigers took a 94-64 loss to Murray State on a neutral court.

They are lead in scoring by former Kentucky center Aaron Bradshaw, who is averaging 14.3 points, 6.7 rebounds and 3 blocks per game. Arterio Morris, averaging 11.7 points and 6 assists per game, started his career at Texas. And Ty-Laur Johnson, averaging 11.7 points, 6.3 assists and 3 steals per game, played his first two seasons of college basketball at Louisville and Wake Forest.

How Terrence Hill Jr. is changing styles but becoming more efficient

Stephen Thompson

Through two games, only 45.4% of Hill's total shots have come from beyond the arc. It’s a stark change in play style after making 59.1% of his total field goal attempts from 3-point territory through two seasons at VCU.

Now Hill is shooting 83% from 2-point range while leading Tennessee in scoring at 17.5 points per game.

Tennessee players dive into labor negotiations with mock CBA

Stephen Thompson

Tennessee players, led by Tyler Lundblade, Campbell Duncan and DeWayne Brown II, have proposed a model for a collective bargaining agreement to the university. The proposed CBA addresses issues of compensation, player movement and more and has been shared with university leadership, including athletic director Danny White.

“I think it's kind of showcasing what can be done in college sports,” Lundblade said during a press conference on Nov. 10. “Hopefully this snowballs into an effect where other schools are like, ‘We can jump on with this, this is what a CBA can look like and … eventually taking this to the SEC and trying to build it.”

Why Vols don't add new recruits on National Signing Day

Stephen Thompson

Tennessee doesn't have any high school players committed to its 2027 recruiting class, and didn't make any official additions on National Signing Day.

But it's not any reason to panic about the future of the Vols under coach Rick Barnes. The introduction of the transfer portal and a changing calendar has meant that the traditional signing days, once landmark moments on the college sports calendar, don't carry the same weight.
