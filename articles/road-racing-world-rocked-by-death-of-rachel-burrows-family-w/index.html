Road racing world rocked by death of Rachel Burrows: Family well-known in Northern Ireland motorcycle competitions

TheTools · 16.11.2026 16:39 · source · permalink

The road racing world has been plunged into grief by the death of Rachel Burrows, who was a member of a prominent family within the scene.

Her husband John Burrows used to be a top motorcycle competitor, and after his retirement from the track became renowned for running one of Irish national road racing’s most successful teams.

Teenage son Jack followed in his father’s footsteps, gaining a solid grounding in motorsport with several successful seasons in junior kart racing, including winning the Ulster title twice and clinching an Irish crown, before making his two-wheeled debut as a motorbike racer five years ago at the tender age of 11. He recently took part in his first race at Brands Hatch.

A familiar and much-loved face in racing throughout Northern Ireland, Rachel was pictured at her husband’s side with an infant Jack when John retired from the track in 2012.

The entire scene has been stunned by the double family tragedy that saw Rachel pass away on Sunday, followed by her mother Lady Jennifer Morrow just hours later.

Mervyn Whyte, events director of the Ulster’s biggest road racing event the North West 200, said the organisation “expressed their deepest sympathy and sadness” in the wake of Rachel’s death.

“Our sincere condolences to John and the family circle,” he stated in a post on North West 200’s social media.

Lisburn-based Temple Motorcycle Club marked their “deep sadness” at Rachel's death, sending their condolences to her husband and their two children.

“Everyone at the club extends their deepest and most heartfelt sympathies to John, Jack, and Isla, as well as to the wider family circle, their friends, their team, and all who had the privilege of knowing Rachel,” they stated.

“Our thoughts and prayers are with you all at this difficult time. May she rest in eternal peace.”

Similar words came from club and promoters Classic Bike Racing Ireland, who stated: “We here at our club pass on our deepest sympathy to John and family, their friends, team and all who knew her. May she rest in eternal peace.”

Jack’s previous karting team, the Ulster Karting Club, described Rachel as “a lovely lady who supported Jack both on four wheels and now on two”.
