Nice weather for the first half of the week.

TheTools · 09.11.2026 04:04 · source · permalink

LAS VEGAS (FOX5) — High pressure is going to deliver quiet weather for most of the next seven days.

Daytime highs will hover in the low to mid-70’s while wind will be light.

That ridge opens the door for great conditions for the Veterans Day parade Wednesday.

The temperature will be in the low 70’s when the parade begins around 10 AM and will be in the mid 70’s by noon.

Once we get past Wednesday the weather pattern will change as a closed low approaches southern California while an open trough slides into the southwest from Canada.

Bottom line another wet weather pattern has us in its crosshairs.

Latest weather models suggest Clark, Mohave and southern San Bernardino have the best rain chances next weekend.

Once those two systems pass expect daytime temperatures to dip into the 60’s.
