Police freeze HK$30 million in account of man who refused to share Mark Six prize

TheTools · 12.09.2026 06:34 · source · permalink

Hong Kong police chief says investigation not directly connected to Food and Environmental Hygiene Department, although suspect is member of staff

Hong Kong police have frozen HK$30 million (US$3.8 million) held in the bank account of a 36-year-old man who was arrested on suspicion of theft after he allegedly refused to split a winning Mark Six jackpot with his colleagues.

The group reportedly included some staff members of the Food and Environmental Hygiene Department (FEHD).

“The case is being investigated by the Western district crime support team. A 36-year-old man was arrested yesterday, and the relevant funds in his bank account have been frozen,” said Commissioner of Police Joe Chow Yat-ming on Saturday.
