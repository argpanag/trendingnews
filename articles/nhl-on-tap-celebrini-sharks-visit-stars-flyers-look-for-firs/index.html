NHL On Tap: Celebrini, Sharks visit Stars; Flyers look for first win | NHL.com

TheTools · 06.10.2026 00:41 · source · permalink

Penguins host Jets with 13 goals in first two games

© Glenn James/NHLI via Getty Images

There are four games on the NHL schedule for Monday, one of which is nationally televised in the United States and one in Canada. Here are five things to watch today, along with the complete game schedule.

Shark attack

There's nothing like starting off the season with a winning streak. The San Jose Sharks will look to go 3-0-0 when they visit the Dallas Stars at American Airlines Center (8 p.m. ET; Prime Video, NBCSCA). The Sharks won their first two games of a season for the first time and forward Macklin Celebrini already has five assists, tied with Joe Thornton (2012-13) and Vincent Damphousse (1999-00) for the most after the first two games since San Jose joined the NHL for the 1991-92 season. The Stars (0-2-0) have scored one goal in two games after averaging 3.33 goals per game last season, ninth in the NHL.

LAK@SJS: Celebrini sets up Chernyshov to win it in overtime

Light 'em up

The Pittsburgh Penguins (2-0-0) have scored 13 goals in their first two games and their longtime stars, Sidney Crosby and Evgeni Malkin, are leading the way with the Winnipeg Jets (1-0-1) coming to PPG Paints Arena (7:30 p.m. ET; SN-PIT, SN+). Crosby is one primary assist from tying Jaromir Jagr (684) for fifth in NHL history and two assists from tying Ray Bourque (1,111 for the Boston Bruins) for the most with one team. Malkin is the second 40-year-old to score in each of his team's first two games of a season after Gordie Howe did so in 1968-69.

MTL@PIT: Malkin buries rebound for PPG in the 1st

Finding answers

The Philadelphia Flyers (0-1-2) are looking for their first win when they visit the Tampa Bay Lightning at Benchmark International Arena (7 p.m. ET; The Spot, NHLN, NBCSP). Philadelphia has been outscored 13-4 in its first three games and may be without Rasmus Ristolainen, a defenseman who's a game-time decision after leaving in the third period of a 3-2 overtime loss to the Carolina Hurricanes on Saturday because of an undisclosed injury. The Lightning (1-1-0) play the second of a three-game homestand after defeating the Washington Capitals 3-1 on Saturday. Jake Guentzel had a goal and two assists for his first points of the season.

CAR@PHI: Michkov and Konecny team up for a 2-1 lead

Back home again

The Bruins (2-1-0) begin a three-game homestand against the Ottawa Senators (1-0-0) at TD Garden (7:30 p.m. ET; NESN, SN+, TVAS). Boston goalie Jeremy Swayman is 2-0-0 with a 1.47 goals-against average, .947 save percentage and one shutout in two starts. This is the second of a three-game road trip for the Senators, who defeated the Toronto Maple Leafs 3-2 on Saturday. It's also the first of a back-to-back set for Ottawa, which visits the Detroit Red Wings on Tuesday. The Senators will be without Jake Sanderson for the rest of the trip, coach Travis Green said, after the defenseman reaggravated an undisclosed injury and left at 12:45 of the first period Saturday.

BOS@WPG: Peterka goes five-hole on the rush to win it in overtime

Jet setting

Kyle Connor is picking up where he left off last season, when he had 92 points (39 goals, 53 assists) that ranked second on the Jets behind Mark Scheifele (103 points; 36 goals, 67 assists). He scored two power-play goals in Winnipeg's 3-2 win against the Red Wings on Sunday and is fifth in Jets history with 75 power-play goals. Scheifele (96) is fourth.
