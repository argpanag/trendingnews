Int'l analysts hail China's key global role as country gears up for APEC meeting

Experts from various countries have highlighted China's important role as a force for economic growth and stability as leaders gather for the upcoming Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit in Shenzhen of south China's Guangdong Province.

The 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting will open in Shenzhen on Wednesday, with the two-day event being held under the theme "Building an Asia-Pacific Community to Prosper Together". It will also be preceded by the APEC CEO Summit, which brings together representatives from the 21 APEC economies as well as invitees from other regions.

This week's meeting marks China's third time hosting APEC following past editions in Shanghai in 2001 and in Beijing in 2014.

Global experts and scholars hope the upcoming gatherings will help build consensus on cooperation, strengthen mutual trust, and open up new opportunities for a peaceful, stable, and prosperous Asia-Pacific region.

Many say the Shenzhen meeting will push the implementation of the long-planned 'Free Trade Area of the Asia-Pacific'.

"China has invested significant resources and funds to create tangible economic benefits based on openness and cooperation. For this reason, we attach great importance to the Free Trade Area of the Asia-Pacific, as well as to engaging in dialogue and reaching consensus," said Boris Titov, chairman of the Russian side of the China-Russia Committee for Friendship, Peace and Development.

The significance of the event in Shenzhen extends far beyond the Asia-Pacific region, with observers in Europe and Latin America keeping a close eye on proceedings and also highlighting the major influence China has in advancing global development.

"We are following developments in the Asia-Pacific region closely and look forward to the upcoming APEC meetings. As the world's second-largest economy and a major industrial economy, China plays an important stabilizing role amid today's uncertainty," said Janusz Piechocinski, former Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Economy of Poland.

"The biggest opportunity of APEC is its huge economic and technological potential. And I think in this, China can play a vital role. For example, the Global Development Initiative, it focuses on poverty reduction and on human development. I think China has very much tools to help [in this regard] and [also] because of its huge economic and technological potential," said Marc Vandepitte, a Belgian economist.

"I believe this meeting can promote shared development and mutual understanding. Cooperation among the economies will help narrow development gaps across the region and improve people's living standards," said Professor Luis Antonio Paulino with Sao Paulo State University in Brazil.
