Spurs Adjust Rotations as Harper Misses Pelicans Matchup

TheTools · 22.11.2026 00:19 · source · permalink

San Antonio will face New Orleans at Frost Bank Center on Saturday, November 21, as the Spurs navigate injuries and adjust their rotations during a strong opening stretch of the 2026-27 NBA season. The matchup comes with San Antonio chasing the Western Conference's top position and the Pelicans attempting to recover from a difficult run.

The Spurs enter the game at 13-3 after winning 13 of their last 14 contests, according to Miami Herald. Their latest victory was a 124-112 result against the Los Angeles Clippers on Thursday, despite the absence of guard Dylan Harper.

Harper is expected to miss one to three weeks because of a left hip external rotator strain sustained against the Detroit Pistons. According to the injury report published Friday by Sports Yahoo, his absence marks his second consecutive missed game.

San Antonio also listed Tobias Harris and rookie Jayden Quaintance as unavailable. Harris continues recovering from a strained left calf suffered during training camp, while Quaintance is rehabilitating after offseason right knee surgery. Neither has made an NBA appearance for the Spurs this season.

Ja'Kobi Gillespie and Maliq Brown were also unavailable because they had been assigned to the Austin Spurs, San Antonio's G League affiliate, under their two-way contracts.

Coach Mitch Johnson acknowledged that the injury situation requires changes to the team's lineup combinations and player responsibilities.

"We will have to think a little differently than maybe we have so far this year in terms of lineups, rotations, different combinations," Spurs coach Mitch Johnson said.

Against the Clippers, Victor Wembanyama delivered 32 points and 11 rebounds. Stephon Castle moved into the primary point guard role and contributed 21 points and 12 assists, while Julian Champagnie scored 20 and Devin Vassell added 15.

Castle also committed a season-high eight turnovers as San Antonio recorded 21 turnovers overall. He acknowledged that adjusting to a different ball-handling responsibility would require additional time.

"I was getting probably just one dribble too deep into the paint at times. And by the time I tried to make my read, they were already pretty much reacting out. It will take a little time for me to get back into that groove," Castle said.

Wembanyama is averaging 28.6 points, 11.9 rebounds, 3.3 assists, 1.1 steals and 3.9 blocks per game, according to Sports Yahoo. The Spurs are scoring 117.5 points per game, the sixth-highest average in the league, while allowing 106.3, the NBA's fourth-best defensive figure.

New Orleans arrives after a 126-120 defeat against the Oklahoma City Thunder on Friday in an NBA Cup West Group B game. The Pelicans led 64-56 at halftime and built a 15-point advantage early in the third quarter before Oklahoma City rallied to tie the contest at 102-102 early in the fourth.

Zion Williamson and Jeremiah Fears each scored 22 points for New Orleans. Saddiq Bey added 21, Trey Murphy III recorded 17, Herbert Jones contributed 14 and Derik Queen finished with 11. The Pelicans made 17 three-pointers despite the loss.

The defeat extended New Orleans' losing streak to two games and left the team with eight losses in its last nine contests. The Pelicans also dropped to 0-3 in NBA Cup competition.

Coach Jamahl Mosley emphasized the importance of transition offense and improved shot selection ahead of another challenging road game.

"We've got to do us, but we've got to be smart about how we do it. We've got to get out in transition, allow our stops to get out and run, and get us some easy baskets, and then you know we can't force it. Guys got to be ready to knock shots down," Pelicans coach Jamahl Mosley said.

According to the New York Times game guide, New Orleans entered the matchup with a 5-12 record, while San Antonio stood at 13-3. The Spurs had won six of their previous 10 meetings with the Pelicans dating back to February 2, 2024.
