Is Spotify Down? Streaming Service Reports Issues - CNET

TheTools · 29.09.2026 15:25 · source · permalink

Almost 20,000 people reported issues with the music streaming service on Downdetector Tuesday morning.

Managing Editor

Jon Reed

Managing Editor

September 29, 2026, 11:25 am ET

Bust out your old CD collection. Spotify users reported problems accessing the streaming service Tuesday morning, with nearly 20,000 issues reported to Downdetector as of 10:30 a.m. ET.

Spotify reported in a post on its Spotify Status X account that it is aware of concerns: “We’re aware of some issues right now and are checking them out!”

CNET has reached out to Spotify for more information on the outage.

This is a developing story and will be updated.

Problems are widespread

The latest update on Downdetector shows outage reports down slightly, but still above 17,000. That’s a big number, and the sign of a significant outage. The map showing user reports across the US looks pretty widespread, with significant reports in cities from coast to coast.

NordVPN, which also tracks site outages across the internet, also shows a significant number of Spotify problems, starting before 10 a.m. ET.
