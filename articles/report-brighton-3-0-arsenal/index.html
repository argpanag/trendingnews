Report: Brighton 3-0 Arsenal

TheTools · 19.09.2026 17:08 · source · permalink

We succumbed to our first defeat of the season against a rampant Brighton side on the south coast.

The hosts created the better chances all afternoon, and went ahead when Pascal Gross scored in off the post from the edge of the box.

Another goal from long-range, scored by Charalampos Kostoulas, doubled their advantage on the stroke of half-time and they made it 3-0 through Chema Andres from an inswinging corner.

Our best chance came when Kai Havertz was denied one on one with the keeper, but our seven-game winning streak at the start of the season was brought to a sudden end.

On a day when the Brighton players were sporting a commemorative shirt to mark the club’s 125th anniversary, the hosts started strongly, penning us in our own half and pressing very high up in the opening 10 minutes.

A loose Riccardo Calafiori clearance early on fell to Ferdi Kadiolgu outside the area and he flashed his shot over, before another turn over in our own half led to a better chance for the home side. This time Kostoulas’s low strike was just tipped round the post by a full stretch David Raya.

It was at least 15 minutes until we could make any headway into Brighton territory, when a long Raya pass released Bukayo Saka, but his shot was charged down as he burst into the box.  Calafiori then had a speculative header blocked in the box.

We had started to settle down midway through the half, and created a few chances. Christos Tzolis raced onto a Saka throughball on the break, before he was dispossessed outside the area as Brighton retreated.

But then the home side broke the deadlock. There didn’t seem to be too much danger as Gross took possession outside the area, but his low right-footed shot struck the inside of Raya’s near post and flew in.

The goal injected new confidence into the Brighton attack, and on the stroke of half-time they doubled their lead.

It came just after our first effort on target of the game. Setting off on a quick break, Odegaard strode into the box, where his effort was diverted into the path of Saka, but off balance, he couldn’t get enough on his shot, and Bart Verbruggen gathered.

The keeper launched a counter attack, Charalampos Kostoulas strode forward before picking his spot low into the corner from 25 yards out.

We needed a response in the second half, but it was Brighton who extended their lead on the hour mark. A corner from the left was perfectly delivered for Chema Andres to head home at the far post.

Brighton were firmly in the driving seat now, and Mikel Arteta turned to his bench to try to breathe life into our performance.

On came Viktor Gyokeres, and he released Havertz with a great ball over the top, but the German struck his shot straight at Verbruggen when one on one. 

In the end that was as close as we came to registering on a disappointing day down south. So our winning start to the season comes to an end, falling to defeat in the league for the first time since April, after what was our fourth consecutive away game in less than two weeks.

WHAT'S NEXT

Most of the players are now set for an extended international break, which covers the next two weekends. We return to action on Saturday, October 10 at home to Leeds United in the Premier League.
