Perikatan challenges other DAP ministers to resign over Najib's house arrest order

TheTools · 12.11.2026 18:59 · source · permalink
    bookmark

KUALA LUMPUR: Perikatan Nasional has challenged several DAP ministers who questioned the royal decree in relation to the conditional pardon granted to former prime minister Datuk Seri Najib Razak to resign.

Its secretary-general Datuk Seri Takiyuddin Hassan also challenged Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim to sack the ministers.

“We are not disputing the court’s decision, let alone the royal pardon granted by the Yang di-Pertuan Agong unlike several DAP ministers who have indirectly questioned the decision by saying, ‘If Najib gets to go home, I will resign’.

“To the DAP ministers, all of you should resign. Or we challenge the Prime Minister to advise His Majesty Sultan Ibrahim, (King of Malaysia) to sack all DAP ministers who have questioned the royal decree on the pardon,” he said at a press conference in Parliament on Thursday (Nov 12).

DAP secretary-general Anthony Loke resigned as Transport Minister after Najib's house arrest order was implemented.
