President Trump’s visit to Mobile draws crowds of supporters and protestors

TheTools · 03.10.2026 04:14 · source · permalink

MOBILE, Ala. (WALA) - Hundreds of people on both sides of the political aisle lined up for different reasons as President Donald Trump took the stage in Mobile.

The University of South Alabama was on high alert as multiple law enforcement agencies were spread across campus ahead of President Trump’s visit.

Before noon, a line of supporters waiting to go inside the Mitchell Center to see President Trump stretched half a mile across campus.

Supporters line up for hours

Caroline Burroughs, a Trump supporter and South Alabama student, said, “We’ve been around here since about 10 a.m. It’s absolutely crazy to think we can come walk to see the president from my dorm and we’re so excited to keep sticking it out and see him in person.”

Francisco Salazar, who is also a Trump supporter and South Alabama student, told FOX10 News, “I think he can kind of pitch in on what he’s gonna do to kinda of lower the prices on kind of everything. I think that would help the Americans feel more confident and what he’s doing.”

Protesters gather off campus

Off campus on Old Shell Road, a line of protesters stood holding signs and chanting.

“Hey if he wants to spew his rhetoric wherever, he’s got people that are loyal. He will probably draw big crowd, but hopefully people are waking up to what he’s spewing,” Jeff Fritz said.

“Why come down here to try to show up for your friends, your people that are suffering. Do something for the people that are out here; do something with the people that are in there with you. What have you done for them? He hasn’t done a thing for them right now, but make their life worse,” Thomas Warner said.

President arrives on campus

After hours of waiting, President Trump arrived on campus, with law enforcement vehicles following in front and behind.
