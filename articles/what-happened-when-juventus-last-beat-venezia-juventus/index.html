What happened when Juventus last beat Venezia? - Juventus

TheTools · 22.11.2026 10:55 · source · permalink

21 November 2026

The Men’s First Team are back in Serie A action after the international break, this time taking on Venezia at the Allianz Stadium in the 12:30 CET slot on Sunday, November 22.

The Bianconeri are currently pushing to get into the European places, whilst Venezia sit in the relegation zone and will be hoping to cause an upset.

Let’s take a look at the last time Juventus beat Venezia.

Champions League football secured

Venezia have bounced between Serie A and Serie B frequently in recent years, meaning the last clash between the Arancioneroverdi and the Bianconeri came in May 2025. It proved to be an incredibly important one for both teams, with Juventus clinching a Champions League place and Venezia being relegated.

The match at the Stadio Pier Luigi Penzo began in dramatic fashion, with Daniel Fila putting Venezia ahead after just two minutes. It seemed that just two minutes later, Juventus had equalised, but Alberto Costa’s excellent strike was ruled out for a handball.

It took another 20 minutes before the equaliser did come. Kenan Yildiz charged at the Venezia defence and they gave him enough space to get a shot on target, which went underneath Andrei Radu.

In a flash, Juventus had turned the game around. The ball broke on the edge of the box for Randal Kolo Muani, who charged into the box and fired past Radu, making it 2-1 to the Bianconeri.

Despite falling behind, Eusebio Di Francesco’s side rallied in the second half and soon found an equaliser, with Ridgeciano Haps scoring and making it 2-2. 

Just after Michele Di Gregorio produced a fine save to keep the score level, Francisco Conceicao won a penalty thanks to his quick feet. Manuel Locatelli stepped up and scored, putting Juventus back in control.

There were contrasting scenes at full-time, as Igor Tudor’s side celebrated Champions League qualification, and Venezia’s players and fans came to terms with relegation.
