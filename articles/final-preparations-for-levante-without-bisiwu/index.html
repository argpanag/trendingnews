Final preparations for Levante without Bisiwu

TheTools · 12.09.2026 13:48 · source · permalink

FC BARCELONA

Barça trained on Saturday to complete preparations for their trip to the Ciutat de València, with the young Belgian absent as he is set to be called up by Barça Atlètic

Eric Garcia will return to the squad and play wearing a protective mask

    Journalist

Actualizado el 12/09/2026 09:48 EDT

FC Barcelona held its final training session ahead of Sunday’s visit to Levante at the Ciutat de València (10:15 a.m.). Head coach Hansi Flick led the session with all available first team players, with Jesse Bisiwu the only notable absentee. The youngster is set to be called up for Barça Atlètic’s match against UD Logroñés Promesas tomorrow at noon.

Striker Hamza Abdelkarim, meanwhile, remained with the first team after signing a contract extension with Barcelona through 2030 on Friday.

Eric Garcia’s return will be the main addition to the squad after the defender missed the Champions League match against Feyenoord through suspension. Garcia will have to wear a protective mask due to the fracture he suffered in the previous LaLiga game against Valencia.

That leaves injured midfielders Frenkie de Jong and Roony Bardghji as Barcelona’s only absentees for the trip to face Levante.

The Barça squad will travel to Valencia this afternoon and spend the night there ahead of the match, allowing the players to rest properly before Sunday’s encounter. Flick’s side will be looking for another victory as it aims to maintain its position at the top of the LaLiga standings.
