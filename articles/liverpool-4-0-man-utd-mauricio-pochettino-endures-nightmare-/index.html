Liverpool 4-0 Man Utd: Mauricio Pochettino endures nightmare start as Leny Yoro's early red card sparks collapse

TheTools · 23.11.2026 08:52 · source · permalink

Report and free match highlights as Manchester United are routed by Liverpool at Anfield in Mauricio Pochettino's first game; Leny Yoro received early red card before Liverpool ran riot; defeat keeps Man Utd in relegation zone, one point off bottom

Football Features Writer @nicholaspwright

Sunday 22 November 2026 18:30, UK

Please use Chrome browser for a more accessible video player

Mauricio Pochettino endured a horror start to his Manchester United tenure as his 10-man side were thrashed 4-0 by Liverpool at Anfield following Leny Yoro's early sending off.

Yoro saw red in the eighth minute when he was adjudged to have made a last-man foul on Alexander Isak, with Dominik Szoboszlai slamming home the resulting free-kick.

The decision was contentious, with Isak having seemingly lost control of the ball when he was fouled, but the double blow left the visitors in disarray and Florian Wirtz added an acrobatic second only two minutes and 46 seconds after the opener.

Liverpool fans taunted Pochettino with chants of 'you're getting sacked in the morning' following the second goal.

The Argentine's decision to hand Matthijs de Ligt a first Premier League start in 357 days backfired spectacularly, with the 27-year-old misjudging a high ball in the lead-up to Yoro's red card before the hosts punished more shambolic defending after the break.

Rio Ngumoha added the third when he converted Bradley Barcola's low cross following a rapid counter-attack, becoming the youngest scorer in the history of the fixture, with substitute Victor Munoz netting the fourth from a through ball by the excellent Wirtz.

The four-goal loss showed Pochettino the size of the task ahead and keeps United in the Premier League relegation zone, only a point off the bottom. They have conceded more goals than any other side in Europe's major leagues in all competitions, with 34.

The scoreline could easily have been worse for them, with Barcola and Isak among those to miss chances as Liverpool racked up 28 shots, 10 of which came from Wirtz passes.

The defeat was the heaviest in a Manchester United manager's first game since Lal Hilditch was beaten by the same scoreline a century ago, in 1926. It is the first time in 40 years that United have been in the bottom two at this stage of a season.

For Pochettino's counterpart Andoni Iraola, the result represents his first statement win since taking charge. Liverpool move back into fourth place, only a point behind Manchester City in third and seven points behind leaders Arsenal.

Man Utd's misery in stats

  • Since the start of the 2021/22 season, Liverpool's four wins by four goals or more against Manchester United are the most any Premier League team has achieved against another across all competitions.
  • Adjusting for three points for a win, Manchester United's eight points this season are their fewest after 11 games of a league campaign since the 1972/73 season.
  • That is also the previous season in which the Red Devils only managed one win through their first 11 league matches.
  • Mauricio Pochettino suffered the joint-heaviest defeat by a Manchester United manager in his first game, along with a defeat by the same scoreline for Lal Hilditch in October 1926.
  • This was the quickest Liverpool had gone 2-0 up in a Premier League match against Manchester United, going two ahead after just 12 minutes and 40 seconds.
  • Pochettino is the first manager in Premier League history to see his team concede twice and receive a red card in the first half of his first match in charge of a club.
  • Manchester United will end the day in 19th position in the Premier League, with this the first time since November 1986 that they have been within the bottom two places of the league table having played at least 10 matches.
  • Leny Yoro's eighth-minute red card was the earliest shown to a Manchester United player in a Premier League match.
