Caitlin Clark, Sophie Cunningham, A'ja Wilson's Box Score Stats, Highlights as Aces Eliminate Fever

TheTools · 02.10.2026 08:09 · source · permalink

Las Vegas Aces center A'ja Wilson's dominant performance propelled the defending WNBA champions to a 94-83 home win over the No. 6 seed Indiana Fever in Game 3 of their first round playoff series.

The No. 3 seed Aces, who were 4.5-point favorites (per FanDuel Sportsbook), featured four players scoring in double digits.

The Aces were up by as many as 10 points in the fourth quarter and then 82-73 with 4:23 left in regulation, but Indiana superstar guard Kelsey Mitchell went on a personal 7-0 run capped by a stepback three with 3:00 remaining to cut the lead to 82-80.

However, the Aces were too strong down the stretch and finished the game on a 12-3 run. Ten of those points were via free throws.

Wilson was a superstar as usual and showcase why she's a lock to win her fifth WNBA MVP award. The Aces were plus-12 with her on the floor, and she also made 12-of-15 free throws. Wilson ended up playing 37 minutes.

Chelsea Gray and Jackie Young played all 40 minutes in this elimination game. Gray, one of the most clutch players in WNBA history, scored 11 points in the fourth quarter. Young capped a fantastic three-game series, averaging 23.3 points, 7.3 assists and 4.7 rebounds.

Las Vegas shot 45 percent from the field, made 8-of-20 threes and hit 28-of-35 free throws. Indiana went to the line 20 times and made 14 free throws.

Indiana guard Caitlin Clark had an up-and-down game. On the plus side, she made a big defensive impact (four blocks, two steals) and scored 18 points. However, she also committed a game-high six turnovers and shot 6-of-18 (3-of-12 from three).

Clark also took an inadvertent elbow to the face from Aces guard Jewell Loyd on a rebound attempt in the fourth quarter. No foul was called.

Overall, Indiana shot just 38 percent from the field and 9-of-32 (28 percent) from three.

Mitchell had a tough shooting night (7-of-23, 3-of-11 from three), but her efforts down the stretch kept Indiana in the game.

Aliyah Boston was arguably Indiana's best player thanks to her 19-point, 10-rebound double-double. Myisha Hines-Allen posted her 12-point, 12-rebound double-double off the bench in just 20 minutes.

Las Vegas will now play the No. 2 seed Golden State Valkyries or the No. 7 seed Dallas Wings in the best-of-five WNBA semifinals, which begin on Sunday.

Golden State will host Dallas in Game 3 of their first-round series on Friday to determine Vegas' opponent.
