Warner calls election a 'huge repudiation' of Trump, says Northern Virginia felt it most

ALEXANDRIA, Va. — Sen. Mark Warner, D-Va., said Tuesday that last week's election was "a huge repudiation of Donald Trump's agenda," and that Northern Virginia has felt that agenda more than most places.

"We feel that disproportionately in Virginia, and particularly in Northern Virginia, as he tries to remake our capital in his image," Warner told reporters in a remote media availability from the U.S. Capitol, a week after he won a fourth term.

Warner, an Alexandria resident, said voters were frustrated with rising costs, which he blamed on Trump's tariffs and the war with Iran. "But for these crazy tariffs, costs would be lower. But for this war of choice with Iran, costs would be lower," he said.
