It is with profound sadness that we announce the death of our beloved Danielle Mulcahy, Rathbawn Grove, Castlebar on Sunday, 11th October 2026 suddenly and unexpectedly at Mayo University Hospital, Castlebar.

Predeceased by her grandparents Marie and Joey Mulcahy.

Dearly loved daughter of Kathleen and Daniel and much loved sister of Nadian, Emma and J.P.

She will be sadly missed by her heartbroken parents, sisters, brother,  grandparents Ethna and P.J. O’Gara (Ballindine), aunts Anne and Joanne and uncles Darren, Carlos, Barry and Patrick, her niece Maleeah, nephews A.J., Leo, Jayden and Mylo and all her family,  friends and her boyfriend Wello and her work colleagues in Café Rua.

May Danielle rest in peace…forever 27 years.

Danielle will repose at Kilcoyne’s Funeral Home, Thomas Street, Castlebar on Wednesday, 14th October from 6.30pm to 8.30pm.

The Funeral will arrive at the Church of the Holy Rosary, Castlebar on Thursday, 15th October for Funeral Mass at 2.30pm.

Afterwards Danielle will be laid to rest in Castlebar New Cemetery.

The Mass can be viewed on www.churchtv.ie/castlebar or it can be listened to in Castlebar on the Castlebar Parish Radio 105.3fm

If you wish to leave a message of condolence for the family please use the condolence link below or you can write to the family c/o Kilcoyne Funeral Directors, Thomas Street, Castlebar.

All enquiries to Kilcoyne’s Funeral Home, Thomas Street, Castlebar 0872648869   www.kilcoynefuneralhome.com
