Jackson police provide major update in death of Tasia Fortune

TheTools · 11.09.2026 20:30 · source · permalink

JACKSON, Miss. (WLBT) - Jackson Police Chief RaShall Brackney provided a major update on the death of Tasia Fortune.

The city’s top cop said during a press conference on Friday that JPD secured two search warrants and an arrest warrant for a man identified as 51-year-old Jarques Ratliff aka “Jock”.

Brackney said they executed both search warrants. Ratliff was not at the location when officers arrived.

He was later found walking on Capitol Street, where he was arrested and taken into custody.

Thursday night, medical examiners officially classified Tasia Fortune’s death as a homicide.

Brackney said JPD investigators interviewed nearly 15 individuals in this case.

Fortune, 29, was found hanging to death from a tree behind a home off Road of Remembrance in Jackson on Aug. 3.

Multiple local leaders and activists across the country have called for answers in connection with her death.

More arrests are expected. The family of Fortune has been notified of the arrest.
