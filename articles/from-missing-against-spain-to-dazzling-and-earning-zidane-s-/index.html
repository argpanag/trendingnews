From “missing” against Spain to dazzling and earning Zidane’s admiration

FRANCE

Michael Olise, who was one of the players most heavily criticized after France’s World Cup elimination, has been outstanding this season

Actualizado el 29/09/2026 06:20 EDT

“Michael Olise? I love him.” Those were Zinedine Zidane’s words to TF1 after France’s 1-0 victory over Belgium, sealed by a spectacular goal from the Bayern Munich winger that sent the current France coach wild on the sideline. A top target for Real Madrid this past summer, Olise ultimately stayed in Germany after Bayern refused to sell him. He is now dazzling once again, although he is coming off a year that included a date he will never forget: July 14.

That was the day Olise “disappeared” in the semifinal against Spain, becoming one of the players most heavily criticized by French fans and media following France’s 2-0 defeat to the eventual world champions. Luis de la Fuente’s defensive setup completely neutralized the Bayern star.

Despite Olise’s disappointing performance against La Roja, the former Crystal Palace player, who had previously been released from the youth systems of Chelsea, Manchester City and Arsenal, broke a Pelé record at the World Cup for assists, finishing the tournament with seven.

Olise has been outstanding again this season. Zidane was full of praise for the winger, who represents France despite being born in England. He was eligible to play for England, Nigeria, Algeria or France because of his family background, but ultimately chose Les Bleus.

Signing Olise became almost an obsession for Real Madrid president Florentino Pérez this summer. There was speculation that the star had told his France teammates during the World Cup, including Kylian Mbappé, that he wanted to join the Spanish giants. However, he remained at Bayern Munich, with the Bavarian club adamant that the France international would not be allowed to leave.

Olise provided the assist for Kylian Mbappé’s goal against Turkey. The France captain and Real Madrid striker suffered an injury while scoring. At club level this season, the Bayern winger has already recorded eight goals and four assists across all competitions. In 2025-26, he finished with 22 goals and 31 assists.
