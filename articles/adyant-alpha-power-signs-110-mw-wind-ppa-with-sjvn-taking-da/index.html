Adyant Alpha Power Signs 110 MW Wind PPA With SJVN, Taking Datta Infra Group Portfolio Beyond 600 MW

TheTools · 31.10.2026 07:36 · source · permalink

Adyant Alpha Power Private Limited, a company belonging to the Datta Infra Group, has signed a 110 MW Wind Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) with SJVN Limited for a wind power project in Davanagere, Karnataka. The agreement marks another step in the group’s expansion in India’s renewable energy sector and strengthens its presence in the wind power segment.

With the signing of this agreement, Datta Infra Group has crossed 600 MW of cumulative signed power purchase agreements with reputed power offtakers. The latest agreement with SJVN adds to the group’s growing portfolio of wind energy projects and reflects its focus on developing renewable energy capacity through long-term power procurement arrangements.

The group stated that its growth is supported by project execution and commissioning activities, rather than relying solely on signed agreements. So far, it has commissioned 50 MW of renewable energy capacity, while more than 300 MW of wind power projects are currently under construction.

The 110 MW project in Davanagere is expected to contribute to Karnataka’s renewable energy capacity and support the state’s efforts to increase clean electricity generation. The partnership with SJVN, a public sector renewable energy developer, also highlights the growing role of independent power producers in India’s clean energy transition.

Datta Infra Group acknowledged SJVN Limited for its confidence and expressed appreciation to the Adyant Alpha Power and Datta Infra teams for their contribution to achieving the milestone.

The group aims to strengthen its renewable energy portfolio further, building on its 600 MW-plus signed PPAs, 50 MW commissioned capacity and more than 300 MW of wind projects under construction.
