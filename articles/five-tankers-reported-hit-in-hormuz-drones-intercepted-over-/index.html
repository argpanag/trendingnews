Five tankers reported hit in Hormuz, drones intercepted over Riyadh

TheTools · 17.10.2026 22:09 · source · permalink

Iran’s IRGC says one tanker, with two million barrels of crude on board, was taking ‘unauthorised’ route.

  • The navy of Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps warns the Strait of Hormuz is “closed”, with four tankers reported hit since Friday. One of the tankers was hit by an RPG fired from a small boat, the United Kingdom Maritime Trade Operations says. Gunmen fired on a fifth tanker off the coast of Yemen’s Al Mukalla, the maritime agency added.
  • The Saudi-led coalition says it has intercepted and destroyed four drones over the Riyadh, Eastern and Southern provinces.
