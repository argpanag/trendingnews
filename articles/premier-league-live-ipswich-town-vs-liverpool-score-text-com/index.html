Match Facts

  • Ipswich Town have lost their last four Premier League games against Liverpool across the 2001-02 and 2024-25 seasons, conceding 17 goals and scoring just once.

  • Liverpool are unbeaten in all six away Premier League matches against Ipswich Town at Portman Road (W4 D2). It’s the most games they’ve played at one stadium without losing in the competition.

  • Liverpool are unbeaten in their last 20 Premier League matches against newly promoted sides (W15 D5) but the last side to beat them were a Bournemouth side managed by current Ipswich boss Gary O’Neil in March 2023.

  • Ipswich are unbeaten in their last 18 home league games, 17 in the Championship last season and a 2-1 win against Sunderland this term. They’ve not won consecutive Premier League home games since a run of four in December 2001/January 2002.

  • Liverpool are winless in their last six Premier League games (D4 L2). Only once before have they gone 7+ matches without winning in the competition, a run of 11 from November 2002 to January 2003 under Gérard Houllier.

  • Ipswich Town have kept one clean sheet in their last 36 Premier League games and none in their last 21 since a 2-0 win over Chelsea in December 2024. Meanwhile, manager Gary O’Neil has two clean sheets in his last 35 Premier League games, both against sides who finished 20th that season – Sheffield United in February 2024 and Southampton in November 2024 with Wolves.

  • Liverpool have drawn 2-2 in their first two Premier League matches in 2026-27 – the Reds have only conceded 2+ goals in their first three games once before in the competition, doing so in 2012-13 in Brendan Rodgers’ first three games in charge.

  • Across their opening two Premier League games, Abdul Fatawu has created more chances (5), played more passes into the box (9) and won more duels (14) than any other Ipswich player, while only Julio Enciso (178) has applied more pressures than the Ghanaian (120).

  • Although he has only created two chances in two Premier League games this season, Liverpool’s Florian Wirtz has made 11 passes that have broken the defensive line, with only Bruno Fernandes making more (13).

  • Liverpool striker Alexander Isak has four goals in just two Premier League appearances against Ipswich Town, all for Newcastle in 2024-25. Three of those were as a hat-trick at Portman Road, making him the joint-second top scorer there that season, behind only Liam Delap (6).
