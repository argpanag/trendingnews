Alexander the Great smashed a 20-game undefeated challenger.

Alexander Volkanovski won the second round TKO in the main UFC 333 event featherweight title match against Mobsar Yevloyev at the Etihad Arena in Yasum, Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates on the 25th (Korea time).

As a result, Volkanovski succeeded in defending the second featherweight title, proving himself once again to be the best.

Yevloyev was not considered an easy opponent. He was an unbeaten in 20 games and won every game with strong wrestling after entering the UFC. One, Volkanovski showed a featherweight dominant performance, leading in both batting and wrestling.

A batting battle that started at the beginning of the first round. Volkanovski was not behind Yevloyev's long-range strike and defended his wrestling attempts well. Rather, the variables were set to the extent that Volkanovski tried to wrestle.

The first round ended like a quest. Volkanovski allowed Yevloyev to strike sharply early in the second round, but there were no major problems. Rather, Yevloyev's attempt to wrestle was reversed and he continued the good trend by taking the upper position.

Volkanovski's powerful right-hand punch shook Yevloyev. Since then, he has overwhelmed the batting battle, and a series of spectacular punches exploded and destroyed Yevloyev. Volkanovski did not miss an opportunity. Finished with a Founding, TKO victory after taking the top position.
