TSMC Stocks Jump 3.1% as High-NA Road Map Targets 2030

TheTools · 03.10.2026 00:21 · source · permalink

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing, the advanced-chip foundry TSM, outlined plans to bring High-NA lithography into advanced-node volume production starting in 2030, while its U.S. shares traded around $473.41, up approximately 3.1%, around 12:34 p.m. ET on October 2. The technology roadmap stretches years ahead. The valuation already demands execution: the stock sits 27.49% above its $371.32 GF Value, leaving less room for disappointment.

The rollout has two stages. TSMC plans to start with existing six-inch masks, while its joint initiative with ASML targets a 12-inch-mask pilot line in 2031 and production-ready lithography systems in 2033. Larger masks could let scanners process chips more efficiently and remove constraints from stitching separate exposures together. Those improvements could lower manufacturing costs as AI chips become increasingly complex.

That timetable gives TSMC a way to introduce High-NA before the larger-mask infrastructure is ready. But better technology still has to earn its keep. Investors need proof that manufacturing savings and stronger output can cover the cost of equipment and qualification. With shares carrying a hefty premium to GF Value, successful deployment alone may not be enough. The economics need to deliver, too.
