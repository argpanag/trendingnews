Bella Hadid left little to the imagination as she went topless while lifting weights during a racy wellness session on Saturday.

'I've officially moved in @alo,' she wrote. 

In August, Bella, who is a brand ambassador of Alo, said she was 'blessed' to be the face of an athleisure clothing company she's been 'loving, living and styling' her looks with for over a decade.

'Being comfortable in your body, being comfortable in stillness, in silence, with only your breath and your heartbeat, and this moment,' Bella mused in the commercial.

'I value the feeling of being fully myself. The freedom to move through life with an open heart, guided by intuition, surrounded by love, giving myself grace to grow. When my mind is clear and calm, everything seems to fall right into place.'

Bella's new gig came five weeks after she suffered a flare up amid her 14-year battle with chronic neurological Lyme disease when she complained of brain fog and exhaustion so bad she couldn't take a shower 'without fainting'.

'Success means something completely different to me now than it did a few years ago. I used to think it was about always working, always saying yes, always chasing the next thing,' Hadid told ELLE.

'Now it feels a lot more simple. It's having the space to wake up and actually enjoy my life, take care of myself, spend time with the people I love, and feel present in the moments that matter. The more I listen to myself and live in a way that feels honest, the more grounded and confident I become.'
