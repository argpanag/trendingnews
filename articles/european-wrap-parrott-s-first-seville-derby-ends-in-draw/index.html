European wrap: Parrott's first Seville derby ends in draw

TheTools · 21.11.2026 19:36 · source · permalink

Getty

Troy Parrott was a second-half substitute for Real Betis as his first Seville derby ended in a 1-1 draw after a late equaliser by Sevilla at the Ramon Sanchez Pizjuan Stadium on Saturday evening.

The Republic of Ireland international, who scored two penalties for the Boys in Green in Tuesday's 3-0 victory over Kosovo in the UEFA Nations League, was brought on in the 63rd minute by Betis manager Manuel Pellegrini.

The visitors were leading 1-0 at that point through a 50th minute goal from Brazilian winger Antony.

But Sevilla levelled in the first minute of stoppage time when Betis goalkeeper Alvaro Valles failed to get a strong hand on a long-distance snapshot from Kike Salas as Betis missed the chance to go third.

The Seville club and Parrott will return to action in the Champions League on Tuesday away at a Slovan Bratislava side managed by Yaya Toure.

Parrott has started all four of Real Betis' games in the competition thus far this season.

Elsewhere, Real Madrid came from behind to defeat Celta Vigo 2-1 and draw level on points with Barcelona at the top of La Liga.

Pablo Duran shocked Real with the opener after only six minutes but Jude Bellingham equalised midway through the second half and Carlos Espi scored a late winner.

Barca will have the chance to pull ahead again when they take on Villarreal on Sunday.

Valencia moved out of the bottom three after a 1-0 win at out-of-form Rayo Vallecano.

Borussia Dortmund edged two points clear at the top of the Bundesliga with a 1-0 win at bottom side Borussia Monchengladbach.

Dortmund took advantage of Bayern Munich's failure to beat Cologne on Friday night as Felix Nmecha struck with 10 minutes left.

Freiburg went level with Bayern on 22 points after a 1-0 win at Elversberg.

RB Leipzig are a point behind after coasting to a 5-1 win at strugglers Union Berlin.

Bayer Leverkusen kept themselves in touch after beating Paderborn 4-0, with Afonso Moreira scoring twice.

In Serie A, Lazio consolidated their place at the top with a 3-0 win over Lecce at Stadio Olimpico, where Tijjani Noslin scored twice.

Donyell Malen scored a hat-trick as Roma coasted to a 5-1 win at Parma to stay within two points of the leaders.

Como are fourth after beating Cagliari 2-0 at Stadio Giuseppe Sinigaglia.

Scott McTominay’s second-half strike gave Napoli a hard-earned 1-0 win over Torino to ease the pressure on manager Massimiliano Allegri.

Ligue 1 leaders Paris Saint-Germain came from behind to win 3-2 at Nice, where Desire Doue suffered a serious-looking knee injury.

Lille lost ground after they were beaten 1-0 at home by Lyon, who are now just a point behind in fourth.

New Strasbourg boss Vitor Matos, who left Swansea earlier this week, saw his first game end in a 1-0 home defeat by Brest.
