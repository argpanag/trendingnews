Inside I'm A Celebrity star Ricky Wilson's life with wife he met on ITV show

TheTools · 19.11.2026 07:33 · source · permalink

Ricky Wilson is currently appearing on the latest series of I'm A Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here!. He was one of 11 famous faces who entered the Australian jungle for the first time earlier this week.

As he touched down in Brisbane Airport last week, Ricky said he was doing a "top secret TV show that I can't talk about." He added: "My flight was cancelled but there are lots of trials. I am here to do a top secret TV show I can't talk about. Are my bandmates not here? I thought I was meeting them here. I am not a great liar am I?

"I have never been camping. I've been to festivals playing them, never camping." As Ricky continues to live out his I'm A Celebrity dreams on screen, here's everything you need to know about the star's life, including his celebrity stylist wife.

Ricky Wilson's early life and career

Ricky is known for being the frontman of the Kaiser Chiefs(Image: Bonnie Britain/SOPA Images/LightRocket via Getty Images)

Ricky, whose real name is Charles Richard Wilson, was born in Keighley, West Yorkshire, to Glynne Wilson and TV producer Geoff Wilson, who worked as a director on the 1970s game show It's a Knockout. Glynne and Geoff notably met through their work in the TV industry.

Before rising to fame, Ricky attended Leeds Grammar School after Ghyll Royd primary school, and later attended Leeds Metropolitan University and completed his degree in Graphic Arts and Design. After earning his master's, Ricky taught at the Leeds College of Art and Design for a year, before his band's big break.

Ricky first formed the band Runston Parva in 1996, before they became the Kaiser Chiefs in 2000. The group had numerous UK hit singles, including I Predict a Riot and Ruby. I Predict a Riot is still a big deal in Leeds, as the song is notably played at the end of every Leeds United home game at Elland Road - the team that Ricky supports.

Ricky entered the Australian jungle earlier this week(Image: ITV)

Back in 2006, the Kaiser Chief frontman was injured in a hit-and-run while he was using a pedestrian crossing. "I wanted to get home to watch Phone Booth, which was on at 10pm. I was crossing the road; it was a green man. I walked past one car that had stopped, and when I got halfway across, a car came out of nowhere," Ricky previously told NME.

"You know how they say you see it coming in really slow motion? Well, it did. I jumped in the air, and it clipped my shins and flipped me over, and I hit the windscreen."

Shortly after the accident, the Kaiser Chiefs shared an update on their website, which read: "He would like all of you to know he's OK. He's the luckiest man alive, because he is still alive. It sounds daft, but a 'Ricky trademark jump' has probably saved his life. The jump meant he was flipped over the top of the car, hitting the windscreen, rather than getting trapped under it."

Who is Ricky Wilson's wife?

The musician is married to celebrity stylist Grace Zito(Image: David M. Benett/Dave Benett/Getty Images for GREY GOOSE)

Ricky is married to celebrity stylist and costume designer Grace Zito, who has worked on several TV shows, adverts and music videos. The couple met on the set of ITV's hit competition The Voice. Ricky was notably a coach on the programme, while Grace worked as a stylist behind the scenes.

Ricky proposed to Grace in 2018, but their wedding plans weren't as smooth sailing. The couple originally planned to marry in 2020, but were forced to postpone their ceremony four times due to the COVID-19 pandemic. They eventually exchanged vows in June 2021, before welcoming their twins, Knox and Rocco, the following year.

Ricky's "good friend" Leigh Francis entered the jungle via helicopter on Wednesday (November 18) night. Ahead of joining the show, Leigh said: "I thought to myself, if I don't do it this year, my mate Ricky's doing it, and I'm sat at home watching it on the sofa. I'll be really envious watching a slow-mo of him laughing and having a great time. So, that's why I'm here."

I'm A Celebrity…Get Me Out of Here! airs every night at 9pm on ITV1 and ITVX
