As rain continues to pound Japan, weather officials are urging people to remain on high alert for landslides and other disasters.

The Japan Meteorological Agency issued a Level 5 Emergency Warning for landslides in three municipalities in Tokyo. This is the agency's highest level alert. The agency issued Level 5 Emergency Warnings for landslides in Oshima Town, Niijima Village, and Toshima Village in the Izu Islands. The islands are off central Tokyo.

In the 48 hours through 3 p.m., 725 millimeters of rain fell on Oshima Island. That's about twice the average amount for September.

Weather officials say the heavy rain could continue in many parts of the country over the next few days. They are urging people to remain on high alert for landslides and swollen or overflowing rivers, and for flooding in low-lying areas.

In Iwaki City, Fukushima Prefecture, two cars were found in a flooded underpass. A woman in her 60s was rescued from one of the vehicles and taken to a hospital, but she was later confirmed dead.
