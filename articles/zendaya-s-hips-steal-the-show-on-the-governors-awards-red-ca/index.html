Zendaya’s hips steal the show on the Governors Awards red carpet

  • Lifestyle

November 16, 2026 - 4.14am

Suffering from awards season withdrawals? You’re in luck: the 2026 Governors Awards have arrived with a delicious hit of Hollywood red carpet fashion in tow.

The event, hosted by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, signals the start of the awards circuit, honouring lifetime achievements and extraordinary contributions to the motion picture industry.

Some of Hollywood’s finest have hit the red carpet at the 17th Governors Awards. AP

The stars of some of the year’s biggest movies, including The Odyssey, have hit the red carpet in Los Angeles, and there’s one guest we can’t take our eyes off: Zendaya (and her hips).

It probably sounds weird to declare a celebrity’s hips the star of an event, and the photo above doesn’t really illustrate our claim.

But this one does:

Zendaya’s dress had a striking silhouette, thanks to dramatic padding at the hips. AP

Read more

Fashion, baby! All the glamorous celebrity looks at the Baby2Baby gala

The actress wore an archival Giles Deacon gown from the designer’s fall/winter 2006 collection, featuring a glittering netted overlay and sculptural padding at the hips.

Stylist Law Roach revealed via Instagram that the look heralds the beginning of Zendaya’s Dune 3 press tour fashion – which, if it’s anything like her previous runs, will be nothing short of jaw-dropping.

Fellow Dune co-star Florence Pugh also pulled out all the style stops for her big night out.

The actress wore a pale pink gown with dropped shoulders, a sparkling bodice and a ruffled skirt – but can we talk about her hair for a moment?

Pugh’s hair flowed right down her back in perfectly beachy curls.

Florence Pugh looked ethereal in this sparkling pale pink gown. AP

A moment for the HAIR! AP

The event was dotted with beautiful gowns (more on those in a moment), but we need to give a special shoutout to one of the male attendees.

We’re calling it: no man is having more fun with his red carpet fashion right now than Alexander Skarsgård.

Alexander Skarsgård in the “nice top” of our dreams. AP

The Swedish actor has well and truly kept us guessing with his style choices during the press tour for his new movie, Wicker.

From freaky transparent shoes to kilts and graphic tees, he’s been turning heads left right and centre, and his outfit for the Governors Awards also stands out in a sea of tuxes.

Skarsgård teamed a black suit with a silver sequinned low-cut tank – truly, the stuff of our ‘jeans and a nice top’ dreams – and what appears to be a matching vest over the top. Obsessed!
