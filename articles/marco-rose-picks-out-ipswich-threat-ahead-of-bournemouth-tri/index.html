Marco Rose picks out Ipswich threat ahead of Bournemouth trip

TheTools · 08.11.2026 14:16 · source · permalink

IPSWICH have 'quality in possession' as Marco Rose anticipates a 'really tight game' when Cherries visit Portman Road.

The German boss picked out Julio Enciso as a key part of the Tractor Boys' side and a threat for Bournemouth as Rose looks to guide his side to an eighth consecutive victory in all competitions.

Newly promoted Ipswich are coached by former Cherries boss Gary O'Neil.

They have made a solid start to their first season back in the top flight and sit one point behind Rose's side on 11 from their nine matches so far.

Looking ahead to the game, Rose said: "You never can switch off.

"You have to be always alert, always ready for 94, 95 minutes and Ipswich, it's the next tough challenge for us away.

"A good team. They have quality in possession. They defend together, they score goals, as you said, in every game.

"Even City away, it was a really tight game. So we have to be ready and it's getting difficult."

Casting his eye over the Ipswich side and where they might threaten Bournemouth, Rose said: "They have tall guys, we have to take care of set pieces, I think, and Enciso is a really top player.

"They have two really good wingers. They can create goal threat and it's a Premier League physical team that's working hard in every moment.

"You need to find solutions in possession against them.

"As I said, it's a tough challenge and even the bench, if they change players, they always have impact."
