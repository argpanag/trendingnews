Black Clover season 2 release schedule: When are new episodes out on Crunchyroll?

TheTools · 03.10.2026 17:11 · source · permalink

We've waited five long years but Black Clover is finally back.

  • Trent Cannon

At long last, we have a Black Clover season 2 release schedule, meaning we’ll finally be able to continue the adventures of Asta and Yuna. More than five years after the first season ended, Black Clover is back.

It seems a little insincere to call the first 170 episodes of Black Clover a single season, but that’s how the series classifies them. After a long break, presumably to avoid catching up to the manga, the anime is back for more episodes in the autumn 2026 anime season.

Black Clover season 2 release schedule: When is the next episode out?

The next (and first) episode of Black Clover season 2 lands on 3 October on Crunchyroll.

Subsequent episodes will follow weekly following the first installment's release.

Black Clover season 2 release schedule

The Black Clover season 2 release schedule starts on Saturday 3 October 2026. This will mark the first time since the 2023 film, Black Clover: Sword of the Wizard King, that we’ve had new animated content of the series. An official episode count for Black Clover season 3 hasn’t been confirmed, but we expect it will run for at least 12 episodes, through December 2026.

What time are new episodes of Black Clover released?

New episodes of Black Clover air at 11pm on Saturdays in Japan. While Crunchyroll hasn’t confirmed the exact time that new episodes will appear online, it seems likely that they will be either at the same time or shortly after the episode airs on Japanese TV.

The Black Clover season 2 release time in other regions is:

Where can I watch Black Clover?

The entire first season of Black Clover (all 170 episodes!) is available to stream on Crunchyroll for most of the world. Crunchyroll will also stream new episodes of Black Clover season 2 as they air in Japan. However, if you want to watch the movie, Black Clover: Sword of the Wizard King, you’ll need a Netflix subscription. Netflix also has Black Clover season 1 episodes available to stream.

Is it Black Clover season 2 or season 5?

There is some confusion between the season numbering for Black Clover due to how the first 170 episodes aired. The first season aired continuously from October 2017 to March 2021 before going on an extended hiatus. This meant that the series aired for over three years before it ended. Many sites break up this block of episodes into "seasons" because the series was marketed in English as being part of four blocks for its physical release.

Is the Black Clover manga over?

The Black Clover manga ended on 1 May 2026, after running since February 2015. Originally, the series was published in Weekly Shonen Jump, but in December 2023, it switched to one of Shueisha’s monthly manga magazines, Jump Giga, to give series creator Yuki Tabata more time to complete each chapter.
