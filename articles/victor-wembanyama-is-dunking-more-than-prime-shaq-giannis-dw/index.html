Victor Wembanyama is dunking more than prime Shaq, Giannis, Dwight Howard, or anyone else in the modern era | Sporting News

TheTools · 17.11.2026 12:49 · source · permalink

Stephen Noh

SN

NBA offenses have changed drastically since the 3-point revolution. Teams are attempting more shots from deep than ever. Despite that shift, one constant has remained the same. A dunk is the most efficient shot an offense can get. And the Spurs have the most of them in the league, led by a historic effort from Victor Wembanyama. 

Wemby's 65 dunks through the Spurs' first 14 games puts him at a higher pace than Shaquille O'Neal, Dwight Howard, Giannis Antetokounmpo, or any of the league's most dominant dunkers. He is putting together the most prolific dunking season since Basketball-Reference began tracking the stat in 1997, and he is on pace to set records that might never be broken by another player.

NBA POWER RANKINGS: Spurs sit at the top of the league

Victor Wembanyama will smash the single-season record for dunks

In a typical NBA season, two or three players might break past the 200-dunk mark. Rudy Gobert is the only player to ever pass 300, hitting 306 in a record-breaking 2019 campaign. 

Wembayama is going to fly past Gobert's record. At his current 4.6 dunk-per-game average, he's on pace for 381 on the season. He should set a new NBA high score some time in the middle of March if he remains healthy. 

SN

To put into context how remarkable Wembanyama's dunk rate is, consider that his 65 dunks on the year are more than 21 entire teams.

Throughout his first three years in the league, Wembanyama was criticized by some of the greats in the game for settling too often for 3-pointers. One of the most dominant paint scorers of all time, Shaquille O'Neal, said as much over the summer. 

"Yes, the step-back three and all that looks good, but it’s not a high-percentage shot. If my guy is 7-foot-5 and can shoot, I want him as close as possible taking the high-percentage shot," Shaq told The Old Man and the Three podcast back in July. 

Wembanyama seems to have taken Shaq's advice. He's always been a good dunker for his career, finishing fifth in total dunks last season with 178. But this year, his dunking has literally and figuratively exploded.

Wembanyama is still taking roughly the same percentage of his shots as 3-pointers, where he is shooting a career-low 31.8 percent. He has exploded offensively despite that shooting slump, averaging a career-high 28.6 points per game, in large part because of those dunks.

When Wemby gets positioning near the rim, he'll point his finger upward to set up what he told 60 Minutes was his favorite play in basketball.  

"I think alley-oops in particular. Alley-oop is a team play and also a dunk." 

The Spurs have finally figured out that it's virtually impossible to throw a lob that Wemby can't get to with his ridiculous catch radius.

There are several different reasons for Wemby's dunk explosion. The Spurs have had better spacing and execution in their offense. Wembanyama has been better about establishing his positioning. And his teammates, particularly Stephon Castle, have gotten excellent at throwing him those passes. 

All of that has led to a season of offensive growth for Wemby. His offense is catching up to his GOAT-level defense. He's the fourth-leading scorer in the NBA, and he's not even shooting the ball well yet. Wembanyama is going to shatter tons of records throughout his career. Add the dunking ones to the list.
