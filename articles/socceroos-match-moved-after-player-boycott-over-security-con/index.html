Socceroos match moved after player boycott over security concerns

TheTools · 12.11.2026 20:22 · source · permalink
  • Sport
  • Football
  • Football Australia

November 12, 2026 - 8.22pm

Football Australia has been forced to move the Socceroos′ friendly against Jordan next week to a neutral venue after nearly the entire squad refused to travel to the country over security concerns.

As many as 21 members of the 24-man squad reportedly told FA they did not want to travel to the Jordan capital Amman, about 100km east of Tel Aviv.

Socceroo Tete Yengi and Brazil’s Gabriel dos Santos Magalhaes. The Socceroos’ friendly against Jordan has been moved to Doha. Getty Images

Despite initial resistance from the federation, coach Tony Popovic backed his players' decisions.

The team is currently in Doha, preparing to take on Qatar on Saturday morning. They were joined by Professional Footballers Association boss Beau Busch, who had been helping their fight.

After meetings between the Jordani federation's acting general secretary Farah Badarneh and FA chief executive Martin Kugeler, it was agreed the match would me moved to Doha on Wednesday (AEDT).

“We are ⁠very grateful to the Jordan Football Association for accepting to relocate the match," Kugeler said in a statement.

"We know how much the Jordan team and fans were looking forward to welcoming the Socceroos to Amman.

“The dialogue with the JFA has been nothing but open, respectful and collaborative. They were wonderful hosts in Amman this week, and I thank them for their professionalism and flexibility. We look forward to welcoming Jordan to Australia in the future.

“I would also like to thank the Australian Embassies in Jordan and Qatar for their support, and the Qatar Football Association, for helping us facilitate the switch.”

Popovic said he was "always confident a positive outcome would be reached".

"From the outset, our focus has been on playing two matches, and the players are ready for both Qatar and Jordan," he said.

“These are crucial games, as we have limited time together, especially now that the AFC Asian Cup has been rescheduled. This will be our last camp for four months.”
