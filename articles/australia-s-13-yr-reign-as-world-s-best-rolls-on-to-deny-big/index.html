Australia’s 13-yr reign as world’s best rolls on to deny big Kiwi dream

TheTools · 28.10.2026 11:13 · source · permalink

Australia’s 13-year reign as the world’s best will continue after the Diamonds claimed a hard-fought 68-58 win over New Zealand on Wednesday night.

While the match itself was a Constellation Cup dead rubber given the Kiwis sealed the series with victory last Sunday, there was more than pride on the line at John Cain Arena.

The world number one ranking was on offer for the winner - which could prove all the difference at next year’s Netball World Cup in Sydney.

And it was the Diamonds who finally found their rhythm after a tough first three losses this series.

“‘I think we’re obviously really happy with that game but a bit of a bittersweet feeling,” MVP Georgie Horjus said.

“It was lots of fun out there tonight.”

The new-look attack of Sophie Garbin (42/47) and Horjus (26/28) was unstoppable after a clunky start.

Horjus found her range – despite her short stature in a shooting circle occupied by 192cm Kiwi defender Kelly Jackson, while Garbin played her role to perfection.

And as one partnership grew, another blossomed down the other end as Jessie Grenvold (team-high four gains) and Courtney Bruce starred in defence.

“This Diamonds defence line has smothered them and suffocated them,” triple international Cat Tuivaiti said.

While Bruce did draw a verbal scolding for her tactics, and wing defence Amy Parmenter copped an official warning in the third term, there were plenty of gains for the Aussies in promising signs for what’s to come for the Diamonds in 2027.

Australia had headed into half time trailing by one before turning it on in a third quarter blitz.

They nailed 20 goals to just 13 for the Kiwis – their worst quarter for the entire series, as Silver Ferns coach Dame Noeline Taurua changed her entire attack end.

Grace Nweke (42 goals at 93%) headed to the bench as the Aussies continued to pull away in the final term.

And despite the match’s standing as Australia’s final international before the Netball World Cup, coach Stacey Marinkovich opted not to use her bench to give players like Donnell Wallam and Cara Koenen minutes on court.

She used the same starting seven for the entire 60 minutes to run out 10-goal winners in a real team win.

While there were celebrations for the Aussies post-match, the final presentations went to New Zealand as series winners 3-1.

“We wanted to go 4-0 which is something we haven’t done before,” Kiwi Te Paea Selby-Rickit said post-match.

“Disappointing but we’ll go away and look at that.”

The 10-goal defeat ensures New Zealand’s woeful Melbourne remains - the nation hasn’t won a netball test in the city since 2015.
