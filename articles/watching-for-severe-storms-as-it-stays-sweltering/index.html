Where it remains dry, temperatures are pushing into the 90s, with a heat index in the triple digits. Be sure to do what you can to stay cool and hydrated, as this same heat and humidity will impact us tomorrow and part of Friday before it finally starts to cool down.

Before we can get to that relief, we'll have a few more rounds of storms to deal with. While it likely quiets down this evening, there could be additional storms developing tonight, Thursday, and Thursday night. It really will be a wait-and-see kind of game as to when and where storms show up through tomorrow night, but it's likely additional waves will show up. There is good agreement that it will be active again tomorrow afternoon and Thursday night, with some lulls in between, but that timing could shift a bit. Damaging wind gusts will be the primary concern out of any severe storms that develop, but some heavy rain and small hail can't be ruled out.

Most of the severe weather will drop south of the area on Friday with a frontal boundary pushing through, but some isolated showers and storms can't be ruled out with a bit of heat and humidity lingering around. Everything will be trending in the right direction on Saturday, with drier conditions and temperatures cooling off. The humidity will also drop off significantly as we head through the holiday weekend, with a comfortable feel and sunny skies on Sunday and Labor Day. High pressure will stick around and keep it quiet and seasonal through the middle of next week.

Stay safe and cool out there, and have a great rest of your Wednesday!
