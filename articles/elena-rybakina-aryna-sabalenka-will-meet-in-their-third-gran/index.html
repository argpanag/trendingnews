Elena Rybakina, Aryna Sabalenka will meet in their third Grand Slam final at 2026 US Open

TheTools · 11.09.2026 03:55 · source · permalink

Despite an intense tennis rivalry between Elena Rybakina and Aryna Sabalenka that involves 17 matches, it is remarkable that only two of those matches were in Grand Slam finals.

Both were at the Australian Open. Sabalenka won in 2023, and Rybakina won in 2026. The 2026 US Open will serve as the Grand Slam tiebreaker between them.

Sabalenka owns the overall head-to-head record 10-7.

Rybakina is achieving career bests at this year’s US Open

For a variety of reasons, Rybakina has not played well at the US Open. She came into this event hampered with an injury that forced her to retire from the Cincinnati Open.

Expectations were low, but the World No. 1 ranking was within her view. She achieved it and went on to defeat Coco Gauff in the US Open semifinal the next day.

Since the 2025 WTA Finals, which she won, Rybakina has had an amazing run.

Sabalenka wants the three-peat

Sabalenka always plays well in New York. This is her fourth consecutive final, a mind-boggling feat that Serena Williams accomplished in 2014.

She is a tough competitor with an extra chip on her shoulder now that the World No. 1 ranking is gone, for the moment.

Sabalenka played her best match of 2026 in the semifinal win over Jessica Pegula.

This is the battle of two heavyweight boxers. Both have big serves, and it will be great to bookend the 2026 Grand Slam season with another final between them.

Watch the 2026 US Open final on September 12 at 4 PM EDT on ESPN.
