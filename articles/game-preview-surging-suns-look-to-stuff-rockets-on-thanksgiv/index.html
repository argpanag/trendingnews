Game Preview: Surging Suns look to stuff Rockets on Thanksgiving Eve

TheTools · 25.11.2026 19:00 · source · permalink
  • Suns Game Previews

﻿The Suns have won four straight on the road, but Houston’s size and rebounding could provide their toughest test yet.

Who: Phoenix Suns (9-7) @ Houston Rockets (13-5)

When: 6:30 pm Arizona Time

Where: Toyota Center — Houston, Texas

Watch: Suns+, 3TV/AZFS

Listen: KMVP 98.7

It appears the Phoenix Suns may be figuring things out. The chemistry, camaraderie, and tenacity expected of this team at the start of the season might be materializing after a slow start. The Suns have won all four games on their current road trip and eight of their last ten overall.

After a big win against the Denver Nuggets to kick off the five-game excursion, they took care of business against the Sacramento Kings, Utah Jazz and New Orleans Pelicans, who are a combined 10-45.

Even with the current string of wins, the Suns still sit in 9th place, one spot ahead of the Oklahoma City Thunder, but they are just two losses out of third.

If the Suns can pull off an upset on the road, they might be able to put together an impressive winning streak. After the Rockets game and a home match against the Dallas Mavericks on Friday, the Suns will face the Jazz, Los Angeles Clippers and Kings. Substituting the Clippers for the Pelicans makes this group’s cumulative record 9-43. Even if the Suns just split the next two matches, then continue to punish the dregs of the Western Conference, they will stand at 13-8 after a 1-5 start.

They will have their hands full tonight against the Houston Rockets, a matchup that garners extra attention since Durant was traded in a deal that brought back Jalen Green, Dillon Brooks, and a draft pick used to select Khaman Maluach.

Houston is coming off a 116-104 win at home against the Clippers on Monday night. Prior to that, they split a four-game road trip. The Rockets are 8-1 at home this season.

Setting the Stage

Miles Bridges has been solid for the Suns so far this season but has looked more comfortable in the flow of the game recently. Among the ten rotation players this season, Bridges is first in rebounding, second in free-throw percentage, second in minutes, third in assists, and third in scoring. Looking at advanced statistics, he is first in VORP and second in BPM.

Collin Gillespie leads the team in made three-pointers (37) and has hit at least two in each of his last eight games. It is becoming more apparent that the Suns have their answer at point guard and that coach Jordan Ott needs to hand those reins to the smart, gritty kid from Villanova.

Those two helped spearhead a small-ball lineup that could become a great situational weapon. Along with Booker, Green, and Brooks, this group has shown incredible shooting and playmaking. This lineup still struggles with rebounding but has offset that with effort and basketball IQ.

Those rebounding woes will be put to the test tonight. The Rockets allow the fewest opponent rebounds in the league. The rebounding disparity between the teams is somewhat staggering. The Rockets are +6.0, and the Suns are -4.8 rebounds per game. The team is aware of this glaring problem. Center Khaman Maluach told Arizona Sports 98.7 FM that it has been an area of emphasis in practices and something that he specifically needs to work on. Maluach currently leads the team in rebounds per possession.

At age 38, Kevin Durant is scoring the fewest points per game (21.6) since his rookie season…19 years ago. For comparison, Suns center Maluach is just 20 years old. While he may be slowing down, he is still a deadly shooter. He currently boasts a TS% of .654, which would be the third-highest of his career. The Rockets are led by center Alperen Sengun, who is averaging 21.9 points and 9.2 rebounds per game.

This season’s previous matchup on November 7 saw the Suns topple the Rockets 102-99 at Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix. Booker led all scorers with 24 points, including the last nine points for the Suns in the game, and the Suns’ bench outscored the Rockets’ reserves 34-15.

The Suns have a different vibe now than they did then, when the victory pushed the team’s record to 3-5. My feelings of “this can’t work” have changed to “let’s see where this is going.” Some of the missing chemistry and effort we had expected right out of the gate has seemed to percolate into a style of basketball that has been much more entertaining after the miserable start. The Suns may not be world beaters, but they are stringing together wins while they appear to be figuring some things out.

What to Watch For

I’m really hoping this game goes down to the wire, giving us an opportunity to see which group of players closes it out. I think at this point Gillespie has proven he should be one of those five. The two players that might be the fulcrum for this season are Dillon Brooks and Jalen Green. If the team can get those players clicking in the lineup, they might end up being really good. They should take extra pleasure in doing so against their former team.

The Suns can’t get obliterated on the glass. It would be surprising for the Suns to win this battle, but minimizing the disparity is probably necessary to win. I think Booker likes sticking it to KD…and so do I. We all know a win tonight means more than last Wednesday against the Kings.

Prediction

The Suns will win a close one after trailing late. Then tomorrow they will eat turkey.

Suns 106, Rockets 101
