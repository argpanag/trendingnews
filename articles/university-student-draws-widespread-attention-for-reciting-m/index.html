SAN ANTONIO (WKRC) - A university student is drawing widespread attention for reciting a morbid "Kirk-aversary" poem.

According to WOAI, a University of Texas at San Antonio student drew widespread attention after reciting a poem marking the anniversary of Charlie Kirk’s killing at a Utah university in 2025, according to WOAI.

The student, who introduced herself as Pamper Baby, recited the poem during Turning Point USA’s Pick Up the Mic event last month. The event encourages students to debate controversial topics. Christian Collins, a conservative activist and founder of the Texas Youth Summit, said he came to UTSA to talk about Christ, abortion and feminism.

The poem mimicked Clement Clarke Moore’s “’Twas the Night Before Christmas.”

“’Twas the night before the kirk-aversary and all through the house not a creature was stirring not even a mouse,” Pamper Baby read. “The flags were hung proudly. The memes were all on deck as we solemnly remembered a shot to the neck. The podcast grew silent. The hot-takes grew weary. For tomorrow dear friends is the great Kirk-aversary.”

Collins told WOAI the poem caught him off guard.

“I felt surprised,” Collins said. “I knew that there were people like this. I knew that the left felt this way. There were so many people singing songs, mocking Charlie Kirk. I’ve seen it online, but to experience it first-hand.”

After reciting the poem, Pamper Baby called Collins brave for coming to UTSA.

“You’re brave for being on this campus with no neck brace,” she said.

“I’m not afraid because I know where I’m going,” Collins said. “I’m going to heaven. But it did feel like a threat. It was an indirect threat. The goal of this is to scare people from doing what Charlie Kirk did because they will be intimidated by these conversations.”

The student has not responded to WOAI’s request for an interview, the station reported.

UTSA said the student’s comments do not reflect the university’s values and that it is reviewing the circumstances. Collins said he does not plan to press charges but hopes the student learns from the experience.
