Why Wake Forest's Jake Dickert is not a candidate for Florida State job

TheTools · 10.11.2026 16:07 · source · permalink

The Florida State Seminoles football program fired head coach Mike Norvell on Nov. 1 after a 3-5 start to the season. 

On Monday, Florida State officially hired a new athletic director, with Learfield executive vice president Jeff Purinton being tabbed as the new GM in Tallahassee. As a result, the coaching search can now begin in full force, and several names have been tied to the opening. 

According to John Brice of USA Today, some of the candidates include Jimbo Fisher, the ex-Florida State coach, and Georgia defensive coordinator Glenn Schumann. 

Another up-and-coming coach is Jake Dickert, who has turned things around quickly at Wake Forest after he came over from Washington State. However, according to Brice, Dickert is not a candidate for the job.

"Two coaching agents who represent current Wake Forest assistants or have represented Demon Deacons staffers in recent years told USA TODAY Sports that Wake Forest does, in fact, have “non-compete” clauses in its coaches’ contracts involving fellow ACC schools."

One source said it's a "real thing" that is costing some coaches big jobs, and that it seems to be holding back interest in Dickert for another job. 

Dickert went 23-20 in four years at Washington State, and he has now gone 17-5 in two seasons at Wake Forest. The Demon Deacons are currently 8-1 and in the running for the ACC title, so Dickert could get a promotion soon. However, don't expect it to be at Florida State or another ACC program.
