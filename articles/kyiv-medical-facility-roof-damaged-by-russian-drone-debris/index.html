Debris from a Russian drone has fallen onto the building of a medical facility in Kyiv's Solomianskyi district.

Source: Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko

Quote: "A UAV hit the top floor of a medical facility building in the Solomianskyi district. The roof is partially destroyed. Emergency services working on site."

Details: Klitschko added that drone debris crashing on open territory near residential buildings was recorded in the Holosiivskyi district.

Background: Since the morning of 20 October, Russian forces continued drone attacks targeting the capital of Ukraine. One person was killed and six injured as a result of a drone crashing near a petrol station in Kyiv's Holosiivskyi district.
