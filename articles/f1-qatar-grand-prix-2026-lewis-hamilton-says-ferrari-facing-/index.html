F1 Qatar Grand Prix 2026: Lewis Hamilton says Ferrari facing 'very challenging times'

TheTools · 28.11.2026 10:05 · source · permalink

Lewis Hamilton is third in the drivers' championship

  • Published
    26 November 2026

Lewis Hamilton says Ferrari are facing "very, very challenging times" as a result of the series of on-track incidents between himself and team-mate Charles Leclerc.

The two drivers had talks on Thursday after arriving at the Qatar Grand Prix to discuss the fall-out from their collision in the race in Las Vegas last weekend, which team boss Frederic Vasseur said was "completely unacceptable".

Both said the conclusion of those discussions would "stay internal" but admitted a solution needed to be found.

Hamilton said: "I like to think that we're stronger from these difficult...

"If everything was just great all the time, I don't think you learn anything when you're winning all the time. You learn really through the hardest times.

"These are definitely very, very challenging times for Fred and Charles and I."

Leclerc admitted that "at the moment it's not working" and said there was "tension around that we need to dissipate".

The incident in Las Vegas was the second in three races between Hamilton and Leclerc.

In Mexico City last month, their battle on the first lap led to them handing victory to Red Bull's Max Verstappen in a race that Ferrari should have finished one-two.

After that event. Hamilton said Ferrari "had to have some serious talks behind closed doors" and that the team had "big, big problems".

They had those talks in the next race in Brazil, brokered by Vasseur, only for there to be two flashpoints in Las Vegas.

Hamilton was first penalised 10 seconds for gaining an advantage by cutting the track while racing with McLaren's Lando Norris, in an incident to which Leclerc was also in close proximity.

And then Leclerc received a 10-second penalty for pitching Hamilton into a spin.

Leclerc said: "We have a very good relationship outside the car with Lewis.

"I wouldn't say the atmosphere was good because we are disappointed by the situation we are in.

"But we are all grown up and responsible and we have to find a solution and there is also a big willingness to find a solution."

Leclerc said the incidents kept happening because "we are two very strong drivers who want to finish one ahead of the other".

He added: "I do believe some level of tension is always there in top teams when you have two drivers.

"If you look this year there have been more than 10 times we have started side by side. We are always together. So a certain amount of tension is obviously always there, but it doesn't necessarily have to escalate and that is why we need to find a solution and fix that."

Hamilton added: "I have no doubts that next year we'll come back working hard together and in a better place. I think we'll quickly get better processes in place so that we can really get a right team."
