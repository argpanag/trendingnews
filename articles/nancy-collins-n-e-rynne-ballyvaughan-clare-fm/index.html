Nancy Collins (née Rynne)

Acres, Ballyvaughan, Co. Clare

Passed peacefully on October 10 2026 surrounded by her loving family at Ennistymon Community Hospital where she received wonderful care.

Predeceased by her husband Gus.

Sadly missed by her loving family Patrick, John, Mary, Theresa, Gerard and Bernadette, sons in law, daughters in law, her cherished 16 grandchildren, great grandson, sister Eileen (USA), nieces, nephews, relatives, neighbours and friends.

May she rest in peace.

Reposing at St. John the Baptist Church Ballyvaughan on Monday evening from 6 pm to 8 pm. Requiem Mass on Tuesday at 11 am followed by burial in Bishops Quarters cemetery.

Family flowers only please, donations if desired to Ennistymon Community Hospital.
