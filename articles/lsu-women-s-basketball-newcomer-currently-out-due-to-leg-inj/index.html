LSU women's basketball newcomer currently out due to leg injury

TheTools · 22.09.2026 14:31 · source · permalink

BATON ROUGE – The 2026-27 season hasn't started yet, but the hits are already coming for Kim Mulkey and the LSU women's basketball team.

Senior forward and newcomer Noa Morro suffered a foot injury and will miss some time at the beginning of the season. Mulkey did not specify how long Morro will be out but, the LSU coach theorized it could be by the end of November.

"Noa had a rod put in her shin at the beginning of September," Mulkey told reporters Monday, Sept. 21, before her team's first practice of the season. "So we won't have her. She has a stress fracture in that shin bone. I think Angel Reese had that in her career here as well. So she'll be on the sideline."

Mulkey said Morro came to LSU with the injury and must have been playing on it in Spain's national league before arriving in Baton Rouge this summer.

"She came here with it and you try to let it heal on its own," Mulkey said. "For whatever reason, it doesn't heal in the foot or something. She's frustrated because she wants to be out there right now. She was not expecting this. She obviously must've played through some tremendous pain because she played all summer for her team back home."

Depth inside for the Tigers took its first hit over the offseason when junior forward Kate Koval, a native of Ukraine, decided to leave the team for personal reasons after the team added a Russian freshman and 17-year-old Anna Minaeva this summer.

Recently, Koval did get a waiver from the NCAA to allow her to transfer outside of the offseason transfer portal window and signed at Baylor.

LSU is not void of experience in the low block as sophomore Grace Knox is back after getting ample playing time last season as a freshman. But the rest of what Mulkey has in the post is question marks with freshman Lola Lampley as well as Meghan Yarnevihc.
