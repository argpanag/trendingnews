With top spot still in reach, Chivas Guadalajara will bid to extend its four-game unbeaten streak in the Apertura 2026 regular season, when Gabriel Milito’s men host Atlante at Estadio Akron on Saturday.

Meanwhile, Atlante sits outside of the Apertura playoff picture, with matchday 14’s 1-0 defeat to Atlético San Luis leaving Los Potros de Hierro down in 16th.
