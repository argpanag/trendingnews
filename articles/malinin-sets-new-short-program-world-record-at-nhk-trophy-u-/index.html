Malinin Sets New Short Program World Record at NHK Trophy - U.S. Figure Skating

TheTools · 27.11.2026 18:00 · source · permalink

Zingas and Kolesnik, Carreira and Ponomarenko stand first and second after rhythm dance

11/27/2026 9:23:00 AM

Ilia Malinin delivered a record-breaking performance on the opening day of 2026 NHK Trophy, scoring 114.81 points in the men's short program. 

With it, Malinin adds a second world record under his belt in just his second competition of the season. The 2026 Olympic gold medalist previously set the men's free skate world record at the 2025 Grand Prix Final.  

Malinin opened his short program, Cantata Memoria, with a quad flip worth over 15 points. The highest-scoring element of his performance and the competition at 20.88 points was his quad Lutz-triple toe loop combination. His three spins and step sequence also received Level 4 marks.  

"I feel really good tonight," he said at the post-short program press conference. "I was so surprised by my score; I was not expecting to get such a high score. But I really went out there and just enjoyed the beauty of figure skating, and I think that's what helped get me through my program and deliver the best I ever could." 

Jason Brown, fresh off of his season debut at Skate America, finished eighth overall with a season's best score of 85.89. His short program, a Traitors themed piece set to "His Body Lies" and "Toxic," remained a crowd favorite throughout the entire event.  

The Olympic bronze medalist opened strong with a clean triple Axel. Although Brown fell on his triple flip-triple toe loop combination, he rallied to earn Level 4s on his three spins and step sequence.  

"Coming here to Japan, I think there was a little bit of a monkey off my back [compared to Skate America]," said Brown. "There was the feeling of something that was holding me down, a weight on me, and that kind of felt like it was lifted."  

The U.S. heads into the free dance holding the top two spots in ice dance. Emilea Zingas and Vadym Kolesnik, who are building off of capturing their first Grand Prix gold at 2026 Saatva Skate America, notched a season-best score after the rhythm dance.  

The team was awarded Level 4 distinctions for their sequential twizzles, stationary lift and creative dance element. Their element score of 46.63 points was the highest of the competition.  

Despite two highly successful executions of their Carmen program, the Michigan-based team remains focused on what they can improve going forward this season.  

"As far as our performance of the program, we think there's always room for growth," said Zingas. "From Skate America until now, I think we made some adjustments and improvements on the program. But we definitely want to keep going in that direction [...] we hope that the next time we get to skate it, it will be even better." 

Christina Carreira and Anthony Ponomarenko also set a season-best score of 80.82  for their rhythm dance. They showed mastery of the Golden Waltz, earning their first Level 4 on the pattern this season. The 11.85 earned points on the pattern is the second-highest earned by an American ice dance team this Grand Prix series.  

At 46.63 points, their element score was also the highest of the event. The ice dancers, who earned a bronze medal at their first Grand Prix assignment in France, hope to take this success into Saturday.  

"We're really happy with our skate today," said Carreira. "We've been working really hard since France to make quite a few adjustments, and I think the improvement showed today. There were still a few spots where we're a bit tight that we can improve on, but overall we're quite happy. The crowd was absolutely amazing. They cheered us on the entire time, and I love it here." 

Chelsea Liu and Ryan Bedard, who are making their Grand Prix Series debut this season, posted the top results for U.S. Figure Skating in the pairs event.  

Their short program, set to "Still Loving You" by The Scorpions, finished in fifth place. The star of their performance was a Level 4 lift.  

Naomi Williams and Lachlan Lewer head into Saturday's free skate with a score of 49.50, good for eighth in the segment.  

Sarah Everhardt finished 12th in the women's short program with a score of 59.05
