Bulgaria's PM Calls for Black Sea to Become Zone of Shared Security - Novinite.com - Sofia News Agency

TheTools · 20.11.2026 11:10 · source · permalink

Prime Minister Rumen Radev said at a conference organized by the Institute for Economics and International Relations and the Friedrich Ebert Foundation - Bulgaria that the Black Sea should be turned from a zone of conflict into one of shared security.

The joint event, titled "Black Sea 2026: The 'Nearer' East," addressed shared security, changing military balances in the Black Sea, the socio-economic aspects of the conflict and opportunities to ease regional tensions.

Radev said the Black Sea should be viewed not only as an important transport, energy and digital corridor for Bulgaria's economy, but also as a crossroads of geopolitical interests. He argued that transforming the region into an area of shared security was essential to achieving Bulgaria's economic and social development goals.

He said diplomatic initiatives alone would not be enough, adding that developments in the war in Ukraine directly affect security across the Black Sea region. Radev called for a realistic evaluation of the conflict, including its progress, evolution and possible outcome.

The prime minister distinguished between two ways of assessing the war: a political approach shaped by ideology and wishful thinking, and an approach based on realism and military-strategic and military-operational assessments.

Radev also pointed to technological progress as a major challenge when evaluating the war and its consequences. He said it was important to anticipate how advances in battlefield technology were changing military strategies and the political objectives being pursued.

“A major challenge remains our ability to forecast how technological development influences the transformation of the political goals being set. It is about how advancements in battlefield technology alter strategies,” the prime minister said.
