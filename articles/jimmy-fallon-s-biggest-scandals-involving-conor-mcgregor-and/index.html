Jimmy Fallon's biggest scandals involving Conor McGregor and Addison Rae

TheTools · 19.09.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

Jimmy Fallon has made a career out of keeping things light. But from Conor McGregor to Addison Rae, some of The Tonight Show host's celebrity encounters have landed him in controversy. Earlier this year, Jimmy Fallon faced intense backlash and was even branded as "a disgrace" for giving Conor McGregor a platform on his talk show.

Conor, 37, made an appearance on The Tonight Show to chat about his upcoming fight with Max Holloway, his first in five years, at UFC 329 on July 11, in an attempt to make his way back to the spotlight and recover his reputation.

The Irishman was under fire due to his civil case at the end of 2024, in which he was found liable for sexual assault of a woman at a Dublin hotel in 2018. Nikita Hand alleged that Conor beat and raped her in Dublin in December 2018, and six years later, the MMA star was found civilly liable for assault.

Nikita was then awarded €248,603 (around $285,000) in damages following the jury's ruling in the two-week-long case. With this context, The Tonight Show viewers were appalled to see Jimmy platform the MMA star.

"This is disgusting. Shame on @jimmyfallon," one person furiously posted in response to a clip of the interview on X. "You should be ashamed of yourself!!!" commented another.

"No one with an ounce of moral credibility would platform this human piece of trash," added a third, while a fourth person wrote, "You're a disgrace @jimmyfallon, for having this disgusting man on your show. You have daughters. Shame on you [sic]."

A fifth then added, "Whatever respect [they] had for @FallonTonight is now officially gone", before a sixth declared, "I don't ever want to hear how rape accusations ruin men's careers."

Viewers were especially astounded when Jimmy referred to Conor as "my man" and reiterated that he is "considered one of the best MMA fighters in the world." The comedian even recalled going out drinking together the last time they filmed.

This isn't the only time Jimmy has faced intense backlash.

The Tonight Show host was also slammed in 2021 after bringing on TikTok star Addison Rae to perform a medley of viral internet dances.

The host later made up for this mistake by inviting several of the original creators onto the show via video call to perform their dances and get substantial recognition.

Another major Jimmy Fallon scandal occurred in May 2020, when a clip from a 2000 episode of Saturday Night Live resurfaced showing the anchor wearing dark makeup to impersonate Chris Rock. The hashtag #JimmyFallonIsOverParty was trending online as many called out the racist skit.
