"Family Guy 2026 Holiday Special" Trailer Released - What's On Disney Plus

TheTools · 16.11.2026 21:21 · source · permalink

Disney has released details on this year’s “Family Guy” holiday special, which is called “The Peter Clause” and it will be coming to Hulu and Disney+ later this month.

This animated series features the adventures of the Griffin family. Endearingly ignorant Peter and his stay-at-home wife, Lois, reside in Quahog, R.I., and have three kids. Meg, the eldest child, is a social outcast, and teenage Chris is awkward and clueless about the opposite sex. The youngest, Stewie, is a genius baby who is bent on killing his mother and destroying the world. Brian, the talking dog, keeps Stewie in check while sipping martinis and sorting through his own issues.

In “The Peter Clause”, it’s Christmas in Quahog, and Peter and the guys compete to become the town’s official Santa. Peter loses, and he can either process his disappointment in a mature manner, or give fans a memorable episode of “Family Guy.” Thankfully, he chooses option number two.

“Family Guy” is a 20th Television Animation production. Seth MacFarlane is the creator and executive producer. Rich Appel and Alec Sulkin serve as executive producers and showrunners, while Steve Callaghan, Danny Smith, Kara Vallow, Mark Hentemann, Patrick Meighan and Alex Carter are executive producers.

The new “Family Guy” holiday special premieres on Monday, 30th November 2026 on Hulu and Hulu on Disney+ for bundle subscribers in the United States, and it will also be available internationally on Disney+ in most markets.    Previous seasons of “Family Guy” are also available to stream on Hulu and Hulu On Disney+ in the US and internationally on Disney+.
