The 2026 NBA Preseason warm-up campaign brings a heavyweight clash in the Eastern Conference: the Philadelphia 76ers host the New York Knicks in a matchup between two franchises aiming to battle for the top spots in the league once the regular season arrives.

Although October’s results do not count in the standings, these matchups serve to gauge rotations, fine-tune systems, and give minutes to young players looking to earn a spot on the roster. And few opponents offer as demanding a measuring stick as an Eastern Conference rivalry game.

Philadelphia 76ers vs New York Knicks: Time and where to watch it in the United States

The preseason matchup between Philadelphia and New York is scheduled for this Monday, October 5, 2026 at 7:00 p.m. (6:00 p.m. Central Time and 4:00 p.m. Pacific Time).

For those who enjoy the NBA starting from the preseason, NBA game broadcasts are handled by various networks and streaming services, in addition to the comprehensive NBA League Pass option for those who want to follow the entire preseason via streaming.

The tactical keys to follow in the NBA preseason game

In a warm-up matchup, attention shifts from the scoreboard to the details. It is worth paying attention to how Philadelphia distributes the minutes among its veteran pieces and the young group looking to establish itself: the three-point shooting percentage, the pace at which the team wants to run, and the defensive organization in the first quarters will say more than the final result.

On the Knicks’ side, the priority will be cohesion. A team that aspires to compete at the top of the East needs its half-court defense—traditionally one of its trademarks—to look sharp from the very first beat of October. Rebounding and controlling turnovers will be early indicators of where the New York roster stands.

Prediction for the 76ers vs Knicks: The favorite according to the market

Although the preseason usually blurs predictions, the betting market offers a clear snapshot of the relative weight of these two teams heading into the 2026-27 season. On the moneyline, the New York Knicks appear as favorites with an implied probability of 63.5% (odds of 1.53), compared to 36.5% for the Philadelphia 76ers (2.78).

The spread (points handicap) reinforces that hierarchy: New York starts with a margin ranging from -4.5 to -5.5 points, while Philadelphia gets those same points as an advantage by taking +4.5, +5, or +5.5.

As for the total points, sportsbooks have set the line around 231.5 points, with virtually identical odds for the over and the under (1.93 on both sides at that line), a sign of a game that bookmakers expect to be balanced in offensive pace.

It is worth remembering, however, that no preseason figure should be taken at face value: the absence of stars, minute agreements, and tactical experiments distort both the scoreboard and the winner. The Knicks’ favoritism reflects the stature of their roster, not a guaranteed result in an October game.

With the favorites tag and an implied probability close to 64%, the New York Knicks come in as the benchmark of the matchup according to the market. That said, the preseason is the realm of controlled randomness: whoever gives more minutes to their starters and finds offensive rhythm first will have the advantage.

The adjusted total of 231.5 suggests a closely matched scoring game, so a tight finish decided in the final minutes would not be surprising, with Philadelphia using its home-court advantage to fight the point margin. The most sensible read: Knicks slightly ahead, but a 76ers team capable of covering the spread at home.

Beyond the scoreboard, what matters in October is getting into shape. And for two franchises with ambitions in the East, there are few tests by fire as useful as facing each other before what really counts begins.
