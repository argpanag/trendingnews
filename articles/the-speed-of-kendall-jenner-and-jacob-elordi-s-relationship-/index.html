The Speed of Kendall Jenner and Jacob Elordi’s Relationship Is “Bothering” Kylie, Per a Source: “It’s Causing Some Jealousy and Frustration”

TheTools · 22.10.2026 13:31 · source · permalink

Timothée is “just not moving at the same pace.”

Take this with a laaaaaarge grain of salt, but a source is out here claiming that there’s drama between Kendall Jenner and Kylie Jenner over how fast Kendall’s relationship with Jacob Elordi is moving. Specifically compared to Kylie’s relationship with Timothée Chalamet.

“Even though Kylie and Timothée have been together much longer, people in their inner circle actually see Kendall and Jacob getting married first, and it’s causing some jealousy and frustration for Kylie,” an insider tells Page Six. “Kylie is ready to get engaged and has made it pretty clear to Timothée that she sees marriage in their future. She’s not giving him ultimatums or demanding a ring, but she’s definitely making her feelings known and hoping he’ll get there sooner rather than later. It’s putting some pressure on Timothée to take that next step.”

This anon person also spilled that “Timothée loves Kylie and is very much invested in their relationship, but he’s simply not there yet.” Oh, and apparently, “There’s a feeling that if he were to propose anytime soon, it would be more because of the pressure he’s feeling rather than because he’s genuinely ready.”

Cut to Jacob, who’s ready to commit and “genuinely feels like he’s found the one.” Per the source, who had a LOT to say, “While Kylie and Timothée still have a more fun and carefree dynamic, Kendall and Jacob are getting much more serious about their future together.”

Meanwhile, what’s “bothering” Kylie (again, allegedly!) is the “possibility of Kendall getting engaged before her, despite being in a much newer relationship. Kylie is ready for that next chapter with Timothée, and he’s just not moving at the same pace. Seeing Kendall and Jacob get serious so quickly is only adding to the pressure.”

On the one hand, very seated for the insider info, on the other hand, the grain of salt is...growing!
