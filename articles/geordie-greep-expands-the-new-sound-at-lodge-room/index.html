Geordie Greep Expands The New Sound at Lodge Room

TheTools · 09.10.2026 08:01 · source · permalink

British multi-instrumentalist Geordie Greep brought his expansive solo repertoire to the Lodge Room on September 28, delivering a two-hour performance built around intricate arrangements, theatrical vocals, and extended improvisations. The former black midi frontman and his six-piece ensemble reworked songs into ambitious musical excursions that moved across genres while maintaining tight coordination.

According to Hollywood Times, the performance showcased Greep's command of the material from his 2024 solo debut, The New Sound. The musicians combined keyboards, percussion, drums, bass, and rhythm guitar to create a dense sound that shifted between unconventional time signatures, flowing grooves, noisy passages, and restrained emotional moments.

Greep described his approach to making the album as an opportunity to work without the creative constraints he associated with performing in a band.

"Music can be so much more than learning to play the same as everybody else. It can be anything you want. With recording The New Sound, it was the first time I have had no one to answer to.

Being in a band (black midi), we often have this ‘we can do everything’ feeling, but you are also kind of limited in that approach, and sometimes it’s good to do something else, to let go of things." Greep said.

At the Lodge Room, that independence translated into arrangements that frequently moved far beyond their recorded versions. Greep wore sunglasses throughout the performance, dividing his attention between the audience and his fellow musicians as the ensemble followed his cues with precision.

The title track from The New Sound emerged as one of the night's most energetic performances. The band repeatedly departed from the song's original structure, extending its instrumental sections before returning to the central musical motif with forceful ensemble passages.

Greep's theatrical delivery and eccentric vocal style complemented the music's elaborate arrangements. His guitar solos highlighted the technical demands of the performance, balancing instrumental complexity with the dramatic presentation that characterized his solo material.

"The Magician" provided a contrasting highlight, with its live guitar passages taking on a shimmering quality. The arrangement emphasized the song's rhythmic momentum and gave the extended set one of its more emotionally resonant moments.

The ensemble's improvisational sections sometimes extended to approximately half an hour, according to Hollywood Times. Sustaining these lengthy passages required the musicians to balance technical precision with the freedom to explore changing musical directions.

Audience attention also stood out during the concert. Raised phones were rarely visible throughout most of the two-hour performance, leaving the musicians to perform before a crowd that remained focused on the stage.

Hollywood Times reported that Greep's six-piece ensemble maintained control across the performance's elaborate transitions, allowing the songs to expand without losing their defining musical themes.
