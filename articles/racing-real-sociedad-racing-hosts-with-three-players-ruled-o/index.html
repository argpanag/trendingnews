Racing Santander host Real Sociedad on Sunday 22 November at El Sardinero in LaLiga matchday 13, with Racing 12th and Real Sociedad 5th.

By TuFutbolero Newsroom·November 21, 2026 at 11:26 AM

LaLiga matchday 13 brings Racing Santander against Real Sociedad on Sunday 22 November at El Sardinero, where Racing are the home side.

Racing sit 12th with 14 points from 12 matches, and their last five results are two wins, two defeats and a draw. Real Sociedad are 5th with 22 points from 12 matches, after four wins and one defeat in their last five.
