Fourth Wing Live-Action Series Casts Its Xadan and Violet

TheTools · 15.10.2026 21:16 · source · permalink

Prime Video's Fourth Wing series is one of the most anticipated fantasy shows in development, and it's just gotten a major update on its progress. Both Xaden and Violet have been officially cast as the project gears up to start filming, putting faces to the story's two most iconic characters.

Per Variety, it's been revealed that Emilio Sakraya (Warrior Nun) has been cast as Xaden Riorson. He joins Esther McGregor (We Were Liars) as the live-action Violet Sorrengail. The casting comes several months after Fourth Wing was officially ordered to series by Amazon in May. It is being developed in collaboration with original author Rebecca Yarros, and there has been a lot of attention in the fandom on who will end up playing the TV show's leading roles.

Emilio Sakraya Is the Live-Action Xaden Riorson

A German actor and former child star, Sakraya is also known for his work as a musician under the name Emilio. One of his most recognizable roles is as JC in the Netflix series Warrior Nun, with other credits including Rheingold, Tribes of Europa, Sixty Minutes, and Those About to Die. He is also set to appear in the upcoming third season of Netflix's One Piece series, playing the role of Koza.

McGregor, the daughter of Ewan McGregor, appeared as Tetha Grig in her father's Star Wars series Obi-Wan Kenobi. She is also known for her role as Mirren Sinclair Sheffield in Prime Video's We Were Liars series. Her other notable credits include High School, Babygirl, The Room Next Door, and Clean Hands. She is also set for a role in Prime Video's upcoming Life Is Strange adaptation, based on the popular video game.

Esther McGregor Is the Live-Action Violet Sorrengail

The original Fourth Wing novel is the first of Yarros' Empyrean series. It tells the story of Violet Sorrengail, a woman forced by her mother to attend a military academy where students train to become dragon riders. While she's physically smaller and weaker than her classmates, Violet must survive dangerous training and deadly rivalries while forming a bond with dragons. She develops a complicated romance with Xaden Riorson along the way, a fellow student whose family has a troubled history with Violet's.

Michael B. Jordan is serving as an executive producer, joined by Lisa Joy and showrunner Meredith Averill. Yarros is also executive producing. Jordan will produce for Outlier Society alongside Joy and Jonathan Nolan's Kilter Films and Premeditated Productions.

"These are the people who understand that a story means to its readers, and they’re passionate about the book, which is why I trust them completely," Yarros said of the adaptation when it was announced in May.

Prime Video has not yet set a premiere date for the Fourth Wing series.
