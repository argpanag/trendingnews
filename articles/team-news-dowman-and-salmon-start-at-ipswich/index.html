Team news: Dowman and Salmon start at Ipswich

TheTools · 15.09.2026 22:06 · source · permalink

Mikel Arteta has made nine changes to the side that beat Sunderland at the Stadium of Light for our Carabao Cup third round trip to Ipswich Town.

Teenagers Max Dowman and Marli Salmon make their first competitive starts of the season. Spanish duo Martin Zubimendi and Kepa Arrizabalaga are also starting for the first time this campaign.

It's just the second time Marli has started a match for us, with his first coming against Mansfield Town in the FA Cup last season.

Bruno Guimaraes, who netted his first goal for us in stunning fashion at the weekend, comes in from the get-go. Mikel Merino, who is captaining the side tonight, Noni Madueke, Ebere Eze and Viktor Gyokeres are also coming off the bench to start tonight's clash at Portman Road.

Myles Lewis-Skelly and Gabriel are the only two players to keep their places from the victory at the Stadium of Light on Saturday evening.

Academy products Ife Ibrahim and Theo Julienne are among the substitutes. They're joined by Illan Meslier, Ezri Konsa, Riccardo Calafiori, Christos Tzolis, Bukayo Saka, Martin Odegaard and Kai Havertz.

As for our hosts, Gary O'Neil makes 10 changes from their victory over Crystal Palace last weekend.

Christian Walton starts in goal, with Darnell Furlong joining Cedric Kipre, Jacob Greaves and Abdoul Ouattara at the back.

Florentino Luis and Marcelino Nunez are in midfield. Kasey McAteer, Anis Mehmeti and Jaden Philogene are in attack and striker Zian Flemming, who scored the winner at Selhurst Park, leads the line.

LINE-UPS

Arsenal: Arrizabalaga, Gabriel, Salmon, Lewis-Skelly, Zubimendi, Merino, Guimaraes, Madueke, Dowman, Eze, Gyokeres

Subs: Meslier, Konsa, Calafiori, Ibrahim, Julienne, Tzolis, Saka, Odegaard, Havertz

Ipswich Town: Walton, Furlong, Kipre, Greaves, Ouattara, Florentino, Nunez, McAteer, Mehmeti, Philogene, Flemming.

Subs: Palmer, Davis, O'Shea, Lukic, Walle Egeli, Enciso, Maeda, Clarke, Akpom.
