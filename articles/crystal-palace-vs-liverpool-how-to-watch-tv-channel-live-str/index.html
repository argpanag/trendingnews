Crystal Palace have shipped 21 goals in nine Premier League games this season - the most the club have conceded at this stage of a top-flight campaign. Pierre Sage has inherited a defensive problem that doesn't look particularly easy to fix and that makes the Liverpool goalscorer market an interesting place to look for value.

Alexis Mac Allister stands out at 7/2 with Sky Bet to score anytime. His Premier League goal drought stretches back to February 28 against West Ham, but I'm not convinced that's a reason to swerve him. In fact, the signs suggest a goal is getting closer.

Mac Allister has been making increasingly dangerous runs into the box and getting himself into positions where chances are falling his way. He's also scored four times in cup competitions this season, while his recent shot volume is particularly encouraging - 12 attempts across his last four matches.

He's getting into the right areas. Against a Palace defence that has been leaking goals at an alarming rate, 7/2 looks a generous price to back to see that drought finally come to an end.
