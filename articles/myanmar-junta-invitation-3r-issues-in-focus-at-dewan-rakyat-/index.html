Myanmar junta invitation, 3R issues in focus at Dewan Rakyat today

TheTools · 24.11.2026 08:19 · source · permalink
    bookmark

KUALA LUMPUR: Malaysia’s recent invitation to the head of Myanmar’s junta and efforts to address race, religion and royalty (3R) issues are key matters to be raised at the Dewan Rakyat sitting on Tuesday (Nov 24).

According to the Order Paper on Parliament’s official website, Hassan Abdul Karim (PH-Pasir Gudang) will ask the Prime Minister during Ministers' Question Time (MQT) whether Malaysia regrets inviting the head of Myanmar’s junta to the country.

This is in relation to the International Criminal Court’s (ICC) ruling on Nov 19 regarding the killing of thousands of Rohingya civilians by Myanmar security forces.

Datuk Rosol Wahid (PN-Hulu Terengganu) will ask the Prime Minister to state the government’s position on measures to ensure 3R issues, especially those involving religion, are not politicised or raised irresponsibly to cause public unease.

During the oral question-and-answer session, Datuk Dr Ahmad Marzuk Shaary (PN-Pengkalan Chepa) will ask the Defence Minister to explain the priority given to locally developed defence technologies and systems in procurement by the Ministry of Defence (Mindef) and Malaysian Armed Forces (MAF).

The technologies highlighted include radars, sensors, surveillance systems, autonomous systems and electronic warfare. These align with defence self-reliance aspirations under the National Defence Industry Policy (DIPN).

Mohd Kurniawan Naim Moktar (BN-Kinabatangan) will ask the Digital Minister to state the government’s position on the risk of children developing emotional dependency on artificial intelligence (AI) companions.

The Dewan Rakyat is scheduled to resume the committee-stage debate on the Supply (Budget) Bill 027 after the question-and-answer session.

According to the DewanRakyat calendar, the debate is scheduled to run until Nov 26. - Bernama
