Get a Sneak Peek at Pokémon Day 2027 with this Newly Revealed Collection — Joseph Writer Anderson

TheTools · 12.11.2026 23:31 · source · permalink

Want to celebrate Pokémon Day 2027 early? Well, now you can thanks to an early reveal of the Pokémon TCG: Pokémon Day 2027 Collection!

While we may not be done with Pokémon’s 30th anniversary yet, Pokémon is not missing a beat with this newly revealed product that gives us a sneak peek of the fun we can expect to celebrate Pokémon’s big day next year.

Pokémon Day 2027 doesn’t officially kick off until February 27, 2027, but you can get this special collection a bit earlier. It’s dropping on January 29, 2027. It will retail at $14.99, and will once again feature a Pikachu promo card with the Pokémon Day stamp, along with three booster packs from the latest expansions (with Delta Reign featured prominently in the packaging) and a coin.

While this product will likely be popular, as all Pokémon TCG products are these days, you don’t necessarily have to rush out the door to get it. Looking at last year’s Pokémon Day Collection shows that price hikes on the secondary market are relatively modest. And that price is being driven mostly by the packs included in the box as last year’s promo isn’t particularly valuable.
