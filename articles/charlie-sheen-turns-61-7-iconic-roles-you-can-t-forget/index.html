From Platoon to Anger Management, Charlie Sheen has played characters across genres. Here are seven iconic roles that showcase his versatility, comic timing and lasting impact on film and television.

Updated Sep 3, 2026, 02:31 PM IST

Charlie Sheen has built an impressive career for himself, with memorable roles, hit shows and films that audiences still talk about. From intense dramas to effortless dialogue delivery, Charlie has explored a variety of roles throughout his decade-long career. Whether it be a troubled young man or a fictional character of himself, the actor has left his mark on Hollywood. As the actor celebrates his 61st birthday, let’s revisit seven of his most iconic roles that remain unforgettable.
