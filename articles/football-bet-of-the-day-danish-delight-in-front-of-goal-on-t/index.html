Football Bet of the Day: Danish delight in front of goal on Tuesday

TheTools · 17.11.2026 09:11 · source · permalink

Tuesday’s Football Bet of the Day sees me continue to focus on the Nations League, and I’ve highlighted a goals-based selection in one of the fixtures.

⚽️ Denmark vs Norway

🇪🇺 Nations League

⏰ Tuesday 17 November, 19:45

Goals look guaranteed in Copenhagen on Tuesday night as Scandinavian rivals Denmark and Norway battle it out for second spot in Nations League A Group 4.

Denmark endured a slow start to their Nations League campaign, as a 2-0 win at home to Wales was sandwiched between 3-2 and 2-4 defeats away to Norway and at home to Portugal.

However, Brian Riemer’s hosts have gone on to win consecutive matches on their travels, winning 0-1 in Cardiff against Wales before thrashing Portugal 1-4 in Braga on Saturday night.

Norway opened up their Nations League campaign by edging a five-goal thriller at home to Denmark in the reverse fixture, but Stale Solbakken’s men lost each of their next three against Portugal (twice) and Wales.

However, the Lions defeated Wales in Oslo 3-1 on Saturday night meaning they travel to Copenhagen three points behind their hosts.

Bet of the Day

Denmark have been in fine goal-scoring form in the Nations League this term, firing in 11 goals across their opening five matches.

Brian Riemer’s hosts have scored 2+ goals in four (80%) of glad clashes – only failing to do so in a 0-1 victory in Cardiff against Wales – and I’m backing them to bag another double against a Norway backline that has failed to keep a clean sheet in each of their previous 13 competitive matches, shipping multiple goals in five of their past six.
