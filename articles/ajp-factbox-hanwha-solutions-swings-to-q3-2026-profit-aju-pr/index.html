AJP Factbox: Hanwha Solutions swings to Q3 2026 profit | Aju Press

TheTools · 29.10.2026 05:52 · source · permalink

Hanwha Solutions on Oct. 29 posted in its preliminary earnings a third-quarter operating profit of 90.5 billion won ($67.6 million), reversing a 7.4 billion won loss a year earlier, as U.S. module sales lifted its renewable energy division. Revenue rose 17.4 percent to 3.95 trillion won.
 

Operating profit for the first nine months reached 489.6 billion won, up 291.8 percent from 125.0 billion won in the same period of 2025. The chemical division’s operating loss widened to 44.7 billion won from 9.0 billion won a year earlier.
