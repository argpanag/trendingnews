GTA 6 soundtrack: Travis Scott, other artists share cryptic 'GTA 6' messages on Instagram

TheTools · 15.09.2026 17:45 · source · permalink

During the big Grand Theft Auto VI extended look on Netflix in August, we got a small idea of what to expect from the hotly anticipated game's soundtrack. On Tuesday, several popular artists gave us an even bigger idea.

Travis Scott, Morgan Wallen, Future, and others all took to Instagram on Tuesday to share images that appeared to be small parts of a larger piece of artwork related to Grand Theft Auto VI, per IGN. Scott's post showed a pair of high-heeled shoes on a stripper pole, Future's showed the rear end of a nice car with a Vice City license plate, and Wallen's showed part of a swimming pool drawn in Rockstar's signature style.

While many of these posts went without captions, Scott's caption tagged Rockstar Games, and they're all clearly related to GTA VI in one way or another. The most obvious place to go here is that all of these artists might be on the game's soundtrack. All of them would fit with the vibe Rockstar usually goes with, and they're all sufficiently popular and/or trendy enough to be on a GTA soundtrack.

Of course, there's also the possibility that some or all of them are in the game as characters or radio DJs. Rockstar has done that in the past with the likes of Dr. Dre, Phil Collins, and Axl Rose. Either way, if you like any or all of these artists, and weren't already excited for GTA VI, now you have a reason to be.
