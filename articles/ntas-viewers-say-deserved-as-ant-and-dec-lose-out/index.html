NTAs viewers say 'deserved' as Ant and Dec lose out

TheTools · 08.09.2026 20:48 · source · permalink

Ant and Dec were beaten to the Best Presenter award at the NTAs(Image: PA)

ITV viewers all said the same thing following a historic moment during the National Television Awards.

Celebrating the best in British television, the glamorous awards ceremony returned to our screens once again. Joel Dommett was on hosting duties once again live and direct from the O2 Arena in London.

Some of the biggest stars were also on hand to hand out accolades for various categories. These included factual entertainment, best new drama, daytime, talent show, returning drama and more.

However one of the most anticipated award to be handed was for Best Presenter. Last year's ceremony produced one of the biggest surprises in NTAs history as Ant and Dec’s winning streak came to an end.

After winning the Best Presenter award 23 times in a row, the I’m A Celebrity and Britain’s Got Talent hosts were beaten by former Match of the Day presenter Gary Lineker. Mastermind host Clive Myrie was on hand to read out the nominations and winner this time around.

In the running were Alison Hammond, Ant and Dec, Claudia Winkleman, Sir David Attenborough and Stacey Solomon. Myrie revealed that the National Television Award was won by Sir David Attenborough for Secret Garden.

Sir David Attenborough was named best presenter(Image: ITV)

While he was not actually present to accept the award on stage in person, executive producer Mark Brownlow went up on his behalf. He addressed the audience and confessed: "Sorry I'm not David. I've worked closely with him on a number of different shows. Here are some words from the most trusted broadcaster who is in the 101st year of his life."

The speech from Attenborough thanked everyone for their compliments with the award but also called for more protection of the wildlife and conserving it for future generations. He said: "It's possible to make small differences. If enough small differences are made, they do something significant."

Those watching along at home wasted no time in sharing what they thought of the moment. Most of them admitted that Sir David was more than a worthy winner.

Ant and Dec missed out(Image: ITV)

One simply said: "Sir David Attenborough. Legend." Another added: "There will never be another like Sir David Attenborough and I’m sure the other presenters that lost also agree - he deserves all the awards he can get."

Someone else confessed: "Massively wanted Ant and Dec to win tonight but how can you win against a National Treasure like Mr David Attenborough! The man is a legend and deserves it 100%."

While one person noted: "It is the grandest of congratulations to Sir David Attenborough, who has scooped the TV Presenter award & at the age of 100 years old, I think he is the oldest ever winner of this particular award."

Viewers can catch up with The National Television Awards 2026 on ITVX.
