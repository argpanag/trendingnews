West Coast Eagles list update

TheTools · 16.10.2026 20:43 · source · permalink

The West Coast Eagles have today informed Clay Hall, Harry Schoenberg and Jack Williams that they will not be offered contracts for 2027.

Tall forward Williams played 54 games in blue and gold and kicked 46 goals after being drafted from East Fremantle with pick 57 in 2021.

Hall was selected from Peel via pick 38 in 2023 and represented the Eagles on 16 occasions at AFL level, including in 13 games during 2025.

Schoenberg featured in five matches last season following his recruitment via the Supplemental Selection Period.

Head of List Management and Recruiting Matt Clarke thanked the players for their contributions during their time at West Coast.

“It’s always a challenging time when list decisions are made and today we have informed Clay, Harry and Jack they will not be offered contracts for next season,” Clarke said.

“We would like to thank each of them for their commitment to West Coast and for the contributions they have made on and off the field.

“We wish them every success in their future endeavours.”
