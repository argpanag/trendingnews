Supercars grants Holden Commodore parity change

TheTools · 30.10.2026 00:21 · source · permalink

SUPERCARS has passed a Dunlop Super2 Series parity adjustment ahead of the penultimate round of the season next week at Sandown.

The official Supercars website has today revealed that an investigation has prompted a “small change to the rear boot lid extension gurney of the Commodore”.

It is the first tweak to the Gen2 specification of cars since they finished competing at main game level at the end of 2022.

Holdens won the Super2 title in 2023 and 2024 through Kai Allen and Zach Bates respectively, before Rylan Gray got the job done last year in a Ford.

Mustangs have largely ruled the roost this season, winning six of eight races thus far – four of which series leader Lochie Dalton is responsible for.

There have been glimmers for the ‘red side’ the last two rounds, however, with Eggleston Motorsport stand-in Aaron Love grabbing both poles in Perth, and Brad Vaughan winning the most recent race at Bathurst.

According to Supercars communications, “the change has been determined after the analysis of data, runway test results and a physical test this week”.

Two rounds remain in the Super2 season, with Sandown and Adelaide to come in November.
