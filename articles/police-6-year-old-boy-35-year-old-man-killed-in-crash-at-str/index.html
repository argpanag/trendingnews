Police: 6-year-old boy, 35-year-old man killed in crash at Stryker Sports Complex

TheTools · 17.10.2026 20:58 · source · permalink

WICHITA, Kan. (KWCH) - 8:12 p.m. Update: Wichita police said a 6-year-old boy and a 35-year-old man, both from McPherson, died Saturday afternoon after a truck plowed onto a field during a youth soccer game at the Stryker Sports Complex in east Wichita.

The two were not related, police said.

Eleven other people were taken to the hospital with traumatic injuries. Police released their ages. They include a 5-year-old girl, a 5-year-old boy, two 8-year-old boys, a 9-year-old girl, a 28-year-old woman, a 32-year-old man, a 59-year-old woman, a 65-year-old woman and a 68-year-old man. A 61-year-old woman was taken to the hospital for medical, non-traumatic treatment.

Wichita Police Capt. Aaron Moses said the driver of the truck is a 34-year-old woman from Garden City. She is currently police custody. Two girls were in the car with her at the time of the deadly incident. He would not say what happened to the children or where they are now.

Moses said the woman had a child playing soccer at Stryker but not on the field where the deadly crash occurred. He also said she had a valid driver’s license. Now, police are working to determine how and why the crash occurred.

Anyone with information is asked to call WPD Investigations at 316-268-4407, Crime Stoppers at 316-267-2111 or 911.

6:20 p.m. Update: Wichita police are asking who may have video from incident at Stryker Sports Complex that resulted in the deaths of a child and an adult to submit it using the QR code below or the following link: https://wichitapd.evidence.com/axon/community-request/public/stryker

5:00 p.m. Update: Wichita police said a child and an adult have died following an incident at the Stryker Sports Complex, near 29th and Greenwich.

Wichita Police Capt. Aaron Moses said multiple other casualties have been taken to the hospital. Their conditions were not given.

Around 3:25 p.m., Sedgwick County 911 received multiple calls that a vehicle had driven through at soccer game.

Wichita police officers, other local law enforcement, the Wichita Fire Department and Sedgwick County EMS all responded.

Moses said the driver of the vehicle is in custody. He said there were also two juveniles inside the vehicle, a black truck, when the incident occurred.

Moses said there is no additional ongoing threat from the driver, calling today’s incident a “tragedy.”

The names and ages of the two fatalities have not been released. Moses said he did not know how fast the truck was going or how it got on to the field. Those details remain under investigation.

Police are expected to give another update this evening.

Update: Wichita police say they are responding to a mass casualty incident involving a vehicle at Stryker Sports Complex

“There is no further ongoing threat at this time. We will provide further updates when we have additional information,” said police.

Drivers are asked to stay away from the area of 29th and Greenwich while the investigation continues.
