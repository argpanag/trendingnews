Oxfordshire-born Netflix Bridgerton star emerges as James Bond favourite

TheTools · 15.10.2026 03:57 · source · permalink

Netflix's Bridgerton star and Wallingford man Jonathan Bailey has emerged as the top pick to be the next James Bond.

The actor has become the most-backed contender to be the next James Bond, with bookmakers slashing his odds after punters surged in support.

The 38-year-old Bridgerton star, who grew up near Wallingford, has seen his odds of becoming the new 007 cut dramatically from 40/1 to 16/1, according to betting data from Oddschecker.

Although Mr Bailey is not among the favourites in the overall market, he has attracted more bets than any other contender this week, accounting for 22 per cent of all wagers placed on the race to replace Daniel Craig.

The actor, best known for playing Viscount Anthony Bridgerton in Netflix hit Bridgerton, has climbed into the bookmakers' top six contenders as speculation continues over who will lead the world-famous film series.

Despite Bailey's rise, Scottish actor Jack Lowden remains the odds-on favourite at 4/6, ahead of Callum Turner at 5/4.

Other names in the running include Jack O'Connell, Robert Pattinson, Harris Dickinson, Henry Cavill and Aaron Taylor-Johnson.

Mr Bailey spent his childhood in Oxfordshire before launching a stage and screen career that has made him one of Britain's most recognisable actors.

In addition to Bridgerton, he has starred in Fellow Travelers and appeared in the blockbuster adaptation of Wicked alongside Cynthia Erivo and Ariana Grande.

With Bond fans eagerly awaiting news on who will take over the famous spy franchise, Bailey's growing popularity appears to have put him firmly in the conversation.
