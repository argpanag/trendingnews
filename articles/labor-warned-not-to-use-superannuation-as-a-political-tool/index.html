Labor warned not to use superannuation as a ‘political’ tool

TheTools · 04.10.2026 03:30 · source · permalink

The Albanese government has made a big mistake by repeatedly using superannuation funds as a political tool to achieve diplomatic goals, a superannuation adviser to former Labor prime minister Kevin Rudd has warned.

Treasurer Jim Chalmers on Sunday distanced himself from a call by Trade Minister Don Farrell for superannuation funds to act in the national interest by considering investing in the American lamb industry, to help persuade US President Donald Trump not to impose tariffs on Australian farmers.
