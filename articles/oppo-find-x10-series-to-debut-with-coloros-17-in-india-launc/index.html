OPPO Find X10 Series to Debut With ColorOS 17 in India, Launch Imminent - Smartprix

TheTools · 09.10.2026 10:26 · source · permalink
  • Like
  • Comment
  • Share
  • OPPO has confirmed that the Find X10 series will be the first smartphone lineup to launch in India with ColorOS 17. The global unveiling of ColorOS 17 is scheduled for October 13 at 2:30 PM IST.
  • The Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max have already launched in China, with the Pro Max featuring the 2nm MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro, three 200MP rear cameras, and an 8,000mAh battery.
  • The Find X10 series is expected to reach India around October 22. OPPO has also highlighted its continued collaboration with Hasselblad and the LUMO Image Engine for smartphone photography.

OPPO is preparing to bring its Find X10 series to India, with ColorOS 17 set to be a major part of the launch. The company has confirmed that the new lineup will be the first Find X series generation to debut in India with ColorOS 17 out of the box.

The official unveiling of ColorOS 17 is scheduled for October 13 at 2:30 PM IST. OPPO has yet to confirm the Indian launch date for the Find X10 series, although October 22 is the expected date based on current reports.

OPPO Find X10 Series to Launch With ColorOS 17 in India

ColorOS 17 will be the software highlight of OPPO’s upcoming flagship launch. The company has confirmed that the Find X10 series will be the first lineup to launch in India with the new software pre-installed.

OPPO’s Find X series has also focused heavily on smartphone photography. The company continues to work with Hasselblad on its camera system, while its LUMO Image Engine handles computational photography, including image detail, colour, exposure, and noise reduction.

The Find X9 series arrived in India in November 2025. OPPO subsequently expanded the lineup with the Find X9 Ultra and Find X9s in May 2026. The Find X10 series will continue that flagship push with newer MediaTek chipsets and updated camera hardware.

OPPO Find X10 Pro Max Specifications

The OPPO Find X10 Pro Max runs on the MediaTek Dimensity 9600 Pro, built on a 2nm process. The chipset uses an octa-core CPU with two C2-Ultra cores clocked at up to 4.55GHz, three C2-Pro cores at 4.35GHz, and three additional C2-Pro cores at 3.1GHz. Graphics are handled by the G2-Ultra NX GPU, while the NPU 1090 handles on-device AI workloads. Its Imagiq 1290 ISP supports video recording at up to 4K and 240fps.

The phone has a 6.78-inch display with a 2,772 x 1,272-pixel resolution. The refresh rate reaches 144Hz in supported games and 120Hz across the system, while peak brightness is rated at 2,000 nits.

Camera hardware is a major part of the package. The rear setup includes three 200MP cameras. The main camera uses a 1/1.3-inch sensor with an f/1.5 aperture and dual-axis OIS. The ultrawide camera uses a 1/1.56-inch sensor, while the telephoto camera is a 1/1.56-inch f/2.1 setup that supports 3X optical zoom and macro photography.

The Find X10 Pro Max packs an 8,000mAh battery with support for 80W wired SuperVOOC charging and 50W wireless charging. It also has IP66, IP68, and IP69 ratings, an ultrasonic fingerprint sensor, and Wi-Fi 7 connectivity.

OPPO Find X10 Specifications

The standard OPPO Find X10 uses the MediaTek Dimensity 9600M, based on the Dimensity 9500 platform and manufactured using a 3nm process. Its octa-core CPU has one high-performance core, three performance cores, and four efficiency cores. The phone also uses a Mali-G1 Ultra MC12 GPU, LPDDR5X RAM, and UFS 4.1 storage.

On the front, the Find X10 has a 6.59-inch AMOLED display with a 2,760 x 1,256-pixel resolution and an adaptive refresh rate ranging from 1Hz to 144Hz. The panel also supports Dolby Vision.

The rear camera system includes a 200MP main camera with a 1/1.4-inch sensor and optical image stabilization, a 50MP ultrawide camera, and a 200MP periscope telephoto camera with a 1/1.56-inch sensor and 2.6X zoom. A 50MP 1/2.76-inch 18mm camera handles selfies.

Power comes from an 8,000mAh silicon-carbon battery, with 80W wired and 50W wireless charging. The phone also has IP66, IP68, and IP69 ratings, Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6.2, and an ultrasonic fingerprint sensor.
