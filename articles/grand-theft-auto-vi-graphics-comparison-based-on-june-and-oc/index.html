Grand Theft Auto VI graphics comparison based on June and October 2026 footage

TheTools · 18.10.2026 14:26 · source · permalink

A Reddit community has been actively discussing the visual changes in Rockstar Games' upcoming action game, Grand Theft Auto VI. Users compared promotional materials from June 2026 with more recent footage from October of the same year featuring the main characters, Jason and Lucia. Players noted noticeable differences in the overall presentation of the scene, the color palette, as well as the refinement of the character models, their facial hair, hairstyles, clothing, and surrounding objects, including vehicles.

Discussion arose among participants regarding the reasons for such a noticeable visual difference between the presented materials. Some users suggested that the developers had done significant work on the graphics and shaders during the past four months of polishing the project. However, many commentators with technical experience pointed out that the key factor in perception was the fundamentally different lighting: direct daylight in the early frame versus the backlit pre-sunset light in the late frame, which traditionally makes the scene more three-dimensional and cinematic.

Players also took note of the demonstration of the deep protagonist customization system, noting changes in hairstyles, tattoos, and clothing, including Jason's T-shirt with military insignia. This last detail once again sparked theories about his possible military past and flashbacks in the vein of the publisher's past projects. Most participants agreed that the game is in the final stages of polishing before its planned fall release, and the visual contrast between the materials is due solely to the artistic direction of the scenes and camera angles.
