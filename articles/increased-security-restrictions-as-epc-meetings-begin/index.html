Increased security restrictions as EPC meetings begin

TheTools · 12.11.2026 00:00 · source · permalink

Increased security restrictions are in operation in Dublin city centre as the largest diplomatic undertaking in Irish history gets under way.

The ninth EPC summit is taking place at at the Convention Centre.

The public is being advised to avoid moving between the Convention Centre in the north city and Merrion Square in the south city, in particular this afternoon and evening.

Since 8pm last night, the security cordon around the Convention Centre has been increased, resulting in the closure of the Samuel Beckett Bridge until Friday.

Merrion Square is also under a larger lockdown since 7am. This will remain in place until midnight, as the political leaders are due to attend a dinner in the National Gallery.

Traffic and parking restrictions are continuing around hotels in the north and south city associated with the event until late tomorrow evening.

Additional traffic and parking restrictions are also in place in the vicinity of Merrion Square and the Docklands until late tonight, with the most significant road closures expected between 3pm and 7pm.

Motorcades transporting political leaders will also continue between both Dublin Airport and Casement Aerodrome and the city centre until 11am.

Motorcades travelling around the city are expected to result in rolling road closures between 3pm and 11pm.

People attending the Niall Horan concert in the 3Arena will have no pedestrian access to North Wall Quay and no LUAS service to the venue.

They will have to approach the venue via Sherriff Street, the East Wall Road and Tom Clarke Toll Bridge and a limited number of LUAS services will run from the venue after the concert.

An informal meeting of political leaders also takes place at the Convention Centre tomorrow and until delegates have left Ireland, increased security measures and many traffic restrictions will remain in place.
