Daniel O'Donnell pays tribute to late Irish music legend Johnny McEvoy - Donegal Daily

TheTools · 10.11.2026 11:06 · source · permalink

Daniel O'Donnell and inset, the late Johnny McEvoy.

Daniel O'Donnell has paid tribute to Irish singer-songwriter Johnny McEvoy, whose death was announced yesterday.

Daniel said he was 'very sorry' to hear of the 'Muirsheen Durkin' singer's passing, which was announced by his children, Jonathan and Alice.

Daniel added: "He gave so much pleasure to so many through his singing and songwriting. His memory will live on in the songs he wrote and sang. Deepest sympathy to all his family at this sad time.

"May he Rest In peace."

Numerous tributes have been paid to Johnny McEvoy since yesterday, from across the world of music and beyond.

Donegal singer David James was one of them and described him as a 'true icon of Irish music'.

He added: "Thank you for the songs. Johnny’s ability to tell a story through song was second to none.

"Sending deepest sympathy’s to Johnny’s family at this time."
