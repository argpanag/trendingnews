A cool and partly cloudy weekend ahead

TheTools · 23.09.2026 20:09 · source · permalink

The commonwealth mostly keeps feeling like fall through this weekend

What You Need To Know

  • Partly cloudy skies repeat
  • Rain chances are close to zero 
  • Breezy weather is likely

Kentucky will experience the effects of a heightening ridge this weekend, producing generally quiet weather with cooler than normal temperatures on both Saturday and Sunday. While a spotty shower embedded within low-level stratus or cumulus decks cannot be totally ruled out, the likelihood of rain in Kentucky is low both days.

Partly cloudy skies take over the state through the weekend. 

A warm-up is looking more likely in the middle of next week.
