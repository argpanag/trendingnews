What channels are NFL games on Sunday? How to watch NFL Week 1 on TV

TheTools · 13.09.2026 18:43 · source · permalink

There are 13 games on the NFL Week 1 schedule on Sept. 13, with several intriguing matchups.

There are eight games in the early NFL game window, including the Buffalo Bills at Houston Texans and Baltimore Ravens at Indianapolis Colts.

There are four games in the late window, including the Green Bay Packers at Minnesota Vikings and Washington Commanders at Philadelphia Eagles.

The day concludes with the Dallas Cowboys at New York Giants on Sunday Night Football.

Here's what you need to know about how to watch the NFL Week 1 games on Sunday, including times, TV channels and streaming information.
