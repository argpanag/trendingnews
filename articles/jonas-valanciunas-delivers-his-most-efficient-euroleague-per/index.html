Powered by Jonas Valanciunas' 16 points and eight rebounds, Zalgiris Kaunas held off Partizan Belgrade for an 87-80 victory. Valanciunas logged a season-high 25 PIR in 28 minutes of action. The result pushes Zalgiris' winning run to three straight games.

Jonas Valanciunas played his best game after returning to Europe as Zalgiris Kaunas took down Partizan Belgrade at home 87-80 in their Round 9 matchup.

Zalgiris faced a tough battle against the Serbian club, as Partizan returned to the game a few times after the Lithuanian club tried to get away.

Valanciunas recorded the highest Performance Index Rating in his stint with Zalgiris at 25. He was also near his season points record, as he scored 16, while his season-high was 17.

The Lithuanian big man was close to a double-double as he also had eight rebounds in the game with efficient shooting of 5-of-7 from the field. Valanciunas played the most minutes as well with 28.

Zalgiris have bounced back after a tough start to the season recording three wins in a row after starting the season 2-4.

Zalgiris also had a solid performance from Keenan Evans, who played his first home game back with Zalgiris scoring six points and dishing out six assists with 13 PIR. Carsen Edwards added 17 points, four assists and 13 PIR, while Azuolas Tubelis contributed 11 points, six assists and 15 PIR.

Partizan were led by Nikola Tanaskovic, who had 19 points and five rebounds with 19 PIR. Ethan Thompson added 15 points, six rebounds and four assists with 19 PIR, while Luca Vildoza had 14 points, three rebounds and 17 PIR.
