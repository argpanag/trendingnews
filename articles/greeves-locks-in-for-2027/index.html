Greeves locks in for 2027

TheTools · 14.09.2026 10:25 · source · permalink

Ollie Greeves has extended his time at the Hawks!

Hawthorn youngster Ollie Greeves has signed a one-year contract extension, keeping him at the Hawks until at least the end of 2027.

After strong signs of development and progression at VFL level for Box Hill, the 19-year-old broke through for his AFL debut against Carlton in Round 18 earlier this year.

Greeves went on to feature a further three times at the senior level during his debut season, racking up double-digit disposals in three matches and kicking a memorable first goal against Richmond in Round 19.

The powerful 191cm midfielder/ forward was picked up in last year’s rookie draft after representing Eastern Ranges and Vic Metro during his junior career, which saw him earn U18 All-Australian selection and Coates Talent League Team of the Year honours.

Hawthorn National List & Recruiting Manager Mark McKenzie said:

“We’re thrilled to have Ollie continue his development at Hawthorn, he continues to demonstrate an appetite for hard work, and this extension is well earned.

“After breaking through for his AFL debut earlier this year, Ollie has shown his capacity at the highest level, and we are excited about what his future looks like in the brown and gold."
