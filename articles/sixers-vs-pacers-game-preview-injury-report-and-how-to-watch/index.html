Sixers vs. Pacers: Game Preview, Injury Report and How to Watch 11/15/2026 | Philadelphia Sixers

TheTools · 15.11.2026 21:37 · source · permalink

The Philadelphia 76ers (10-3) host the Indiana Pacers (5-8) on Sunday afternoon before visiting Brooklyn on Monday. Tipoff is set for 1 p.m. EST at Xfinity Mobile Arena.

The Pacers are coming off a 106-104 home win over the New York Knicks on Friday in Emirates NBA Cup play despite 33 points and 11 assists from Jalen Brunson. All five Indiana starters scored at least 12 points, including former Sixer Kelly Oubre Jr., who finished with 16.

The Sixers recovered from a 17-point first-quarter deficit Thursday against the Toronto Raptors to win 123-112. Tyrese Maxey recorded 27 points and eight assists, while Joel Embiid added 20 points. Embiid is out Sunday because of right foot plantar fascia injury management.

Here are the RAV4 Things to Know ahead of Sunday’s game:

  1. First Meeting

Philadelphia trailed 88-75 entering the fourth quarter on Oct. 28 at Indiana before outscoring the Pacers 39-21 in the final period to secure a 114-109 victory.

  1. Twenty x Four

The Sixers had four players score at least 20 points in the first meeting: Maxey, Embiid, Brown, Simons. LeBron James recorded a season-high 10 assists.

  1. LeBron Chasing More History

With 12,081 career assists, LeBron James is 11 shy of passing Jason Kidd for third place on the NBA’s all-time assists list.

  1. Tonight’s Uniforms

The Sixers are scheduled to wear their white Association Edition uniforms, while the Pacers will wear their navy Icon Edition uniforms.
