Teamwork makes the dream work for Timmy and Co

TheTools · 19.11.2026 18:37 · source · permalink
    bookmark

PETALING JAYA: Trust, patience and teamwork proved to be a winning combination for national bowler Timmy Tan and his teammates as they held their nerve under pressure to strike gold in the men’s trios event at the Asian Championships in Doha.

The trio of Timmy, Syafiq Ridhwan Abdul Malek and Izz Naqiuddin Putera Islahuddin emerged triumphant on Wednesday (Nov 18) with a total pinfall of 4,193 at the Qatar Bowling Centre.

It was a memorable day for Malaysian bowling as the country secured a one-two finish, with Mohd Hazim Alihanafiah, Rafiq Ismail and Tun Ameerul Hakim Tun Hasnul Azam taking silver with 4,114 pins.

For Timmy, who last stood on the top step of the podium at the Asian Championships three years ago, the latest triumph was particularly special.

"It was quite an emotional win for me because my last medal at the Asian Championships was three years ago," said Timmy.

"We had a good start on Tuesday (Nov 17), and from there it was about staying patient and picking up all the spares.

"The other countries did put a lot of pressure on us because they bowled a big game. But I have to thank my partners for getting the job done."

The two Malaysian trios finished ahead of South Korea's Ryou Ho-jun, Han Jae-hyeon and Kang Myung-jin, who settled for bronze with 4,083 pins.

Malaysia's celebrations were not confined to the men's event, as they also secured two podium finishes in the women's competition.

Sin Li Jane, Faten Najiha Ahmad Faik and Gillian Lim narrowly missed out on gold, finishing just one pin behind the champions with 3,930.

South Korea's Baek Seung-ja, Paek Ye-dam and Nam Hye-bin edged them out with 3,931 pins to claim gold.

Malaysia's second women's trio of Adelia Nur Irwan Syazalee, Nur Syazwani Sahar and Nora Lyana Nastasia Norkamal completed the podium, taking bronze with 3,862 pins.

Malaysia now have three gold, four silver and one bronze medal from the championships and will be looking to add to their tally in the team event, which concludes on Friday (Nov 20).
