Preview: Utah Jazz Begin Back-to-Back Against LA Clippers  | Utah Jazz

TheTools · 18.11.2026 03:30 · source · permalink

The Utah Jazz continue to search for consistency.

A shorthanded Jazz squad battled until the final minute against the San Antonio Spurs Sunday afternoon, with untimely mistakes sinking Will Hardy’s squad. Despite 17 made 3-pointers (including six from Mo Bamba, who scored 26 points in 36 minutes), Utah turned it over five times in the fourth quarter and surrendered 29 fast break points to the visiting Spurs.

San Antonio was led by 57 combined points from Victor Wembanyama and Dylan Harper.

The Jazz now head to Los Angeles for a back-to-back set of games against the Clippers and Lakers.

The Clippers (4-8) have won two in a row and enter Tuesday’s game with momentum. After an eventful offseason that saw Kawhi Leonard depart in a trade for the Toronto Raptors, LA is led by a group of veterans fighting for Play-In positioning in the Western Conference. Darius Garland leads the Clippers offense (21.3 points and 6.9 assists per game), but LA has a balanced scoring attack that features five players averaging double-figures.

LA won two of the first three meetings between the two teams last season.

Jazz injury report:

OUT - Kobe Bufkin (G League - Two-Way), Jaxson Hayes (Left Knee Strain), Harrison Ingram (G League - Two-Way), Jaren Jackson (Right Knee; MCL Tear), Lauri Markkanen (Right Hand Fracture), Jusuf Nurkić (Right Foot; 2nd Plantar Plate Tear)

Clippers injury report:

OUT - Bradley Beal (Right Knee Injury Management), Brandon Ingram (Right Heel Surgery), Jordan Miller (Left Rotator Cuff Surgery), Yanic Konan Niederhauser (Right Lisfranc Surgery)

Here are three keys to Tuesday’s game.

The turnover battle 

Tuesday’s battle pits two teams against each other that can struggle in the possession game, specifically as it relates to the turnover battle. 

For LA, a high turnover percentage (17.6 percent, 28th in the league) has held the team’s offense back, despite ranking eighth in 55.5 effective field-goal percentage. On the defensive end, the Clippers have had success in creating turnovers from opponents, ranking 11th in opponent turnover percentage. 

The Jazz did well to limit their mistakes against San Antonio up until the fourth quarter, when five turnovers hurt down the stretch of a close game. On the season, Utah ranks 22nd in turnover percentage and 26th in opponent turnover percentage, a tough recipe for success without elite rebounding.

If either team can gain a clear advantage in the turnover battle Tuesday night, it will go a long way towards securing a win.

Attacking the glass 

The other component of the overall possession game comes on the boards.

Neither team has consistently relied on offensive rebounding to begin the season, as Utah ranks 28th in offensive rebound percentage while LA ranks 23rd. 

However, the clear difference between the two squads comes on the defensive end. Even without most of their frontcourt rotation (including an elite rebounder in Jusuf Nurkić), the Jazz have remained above the league average in defensive rebound percentage. On the other side, the Clippers have struggled to end defensive possessions with a rebound, ranking 25th in defensive rebound percentage.

If Utah can carve out an edge on the boards Tuesday night, it will go a long way in attacking LA’s defense. 

Defending the 3-point line 

As mentioned earlier, LA has thrived when it comes to putting the ball in the basket. 

The Clippers rank eighth in effective field-goal percentage, with much of their success coming from beyond the arc. Although LA only ranks around the league average in 3-point attempt frequency, Ty Lue’s squad has connected on 39.8 percent of those looks, the highest efficiency in the league.

It sets up an interesting clash against a Jazz defense that has prioritized limiting 3-point attempts. Just 34.7 percent of opponent shots have come from downtown against Utah this season, the lowest frequency in the league. The Jazz will certainly need to be dialed in defensively on the perimeter against LA on Tuesday night. 

Tuesday’s game tips off at 8:30 p.m. MT. Tune in to the pregame show with Alema Harrington and Mike Smith beginning at 8 p.m. MT on Jazz+!
