We picked up a point in the Premier League on Saturday evening with a 2-2 draw at Newcastle United.

Ebere Eze emphatically converted us a half-time lead, but we fell behind to a quick-fire double from the home side before Noni Madueke lashed in an equaliser for a share of the spoils.

Mikel Arteta addressed the media after the contest at St James' Park, and here's what the boss had to say.

on his feelings post-match:

My feeling is that we have lost two points today. I say ‘we’, because we were exceptional in the first half. The level of aggression and intensity we put into the defensive aspect, the quality we showed with the clarity to understand the jumps and the way they were making every adjustment man-to-man, the aggression to attack the right spaces, and we should have been three or four up. When you don’t do that, then in the second half, you know the opponents will try to react, and that’s what they did to us. 

You have to increase the level to cope with that, rather than stay at the same level. Right from the beginning, we’re not at the races - we start to lose duels, start to lose second balls, we give the ball away and have really bad circulation balls against their press, and we give them belief. It’s one thing to give them belief, and another to give them a goal. 

We did both, giving them a goal from a throw-in that was very disappointing, and then we give them a corner, and leave their best set-piece player free - we get punished. After that, we came back to be that exceptional team; we could break the press, run, create infinite situations where we could score a goal, we scored the second one, and we had infinite situations with overloads to go and kill the game and win it. We lacked that moment of magic to win the game in the end.

on if this result serves as a reminder of the league’s difficulty:

I think the reminder comes in every action in the Premier League, every day. You see how tough it is for everybody, and you have to give credit to Newcastle for their quality, conviction and aggression during that period. The same with the stadium - we know when it comes to these evenings and they believe they can do it, it’s another push.

on David Raya’s injury:

Unfortunately, he had to come off, which is never good news, and he will get assessed now. He will probably get an MRI scan tomorrow, to see what exactly it is. At the moment, I don’t know.

on how it happened:

It was a knock, I don’t know how it happened or what the injury is - we’ll have to wait and see. We know how important David is for us, but tomorrow we’ll have more news.

on not killing games off:

We have to generate more volume - that’s it. The more volume we create, the more efficient we will be. I’ll never criticise that aspect of the team, especially with the will they have. It’s a pity, again, because the margin should have been much better for the level we showed - I can’t ever remember playing at that level here, and we’ve won matches here while playing much worse than we have done today. We didn’t treat the throw-in as the most important action of the game, and anything can happen - we got punished today for that.

on Guimaraes’ performance on his return to St James’ Park:

Very good - we spoke about the emotional aspect before the game.He feels connected to this club, he was the captain, and they did a lot together. But he was very composed and focused during the game, and put in a very good performance during his time on the pitch.

on if he’s reaching his best condition:

He’s getting there - he’s understanding what the game requires, the relationship that we want, and we want to build a connection and play to his strengths as well to continue to develop. But he was really good today.
