Body recovered from Waiwhakaiho River in New Plymouth

TheTools · 09.11.2026 05:28 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

Police are making inquiries after a body was recovered from Waiwhakaiho River in New Plymouth today. Photo / NZME

A body has been recovered from Waiwhakaiho River in New Plymouth this afternoon.

Emergency services were called to Smart Rd about 12.30pm after the body was discovered in the water by members of the public.

“Formal identification procedures are underway and the death will be referred to the coroner,” a police spokesperson said.

“Inquiries regarding the circumstances are ongoing.”

Anyone with information in relation to the matter is asked to contact police online at 105.police.govt.nz or call 105, referencing P068186097.
