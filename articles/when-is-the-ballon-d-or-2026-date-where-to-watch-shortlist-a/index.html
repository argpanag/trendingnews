The 2026 Ballon d’Or ceremony is almost here, with the world’s most prestigious individual football award up for grabs in London.

Read More

The nominees for the latest edition of the golden annual prize presented by French magazine France Football were officially announced last month, with England captain Harry Kane leading the charge.

World Cup winner Lamine Yamal and Real Madrid superstar Kylian Mbappe are also expected to feature prominently in the running for this year’s Ballon d’Or, with the likes of Khvicha Kvaratskhelia, Rodri and Lionel Messi also potential options.

Paris Saint-Germain and France forward Ousmane Dembele is the current holder of the men’s Ballon d’Or award, taking over from Rodri last year. Barcelona and Spain superstar Aitana Bonmati, meanwhile, has won the last three editions of the Ballon d'Or Feminin in a row.

Here is all you need to know about the Ballon d’Or 2026...

When is the Ballon d’Or 2026 ceremony?

The 2026 Ballon d’Or awards ceremony takes place on Monday, October 26. Players are expected to arrive from around 6pm BST, with the ceremony itself getting under way at approximately 8pm. Those exact timings are subject to change.

Where is the Ballon d’Or 2026 ceremony?

This year’s event is being held in London for the very first time, at the London Palladium in Soho.

Why is the Ballon d’Or 2026 ceremony in London?

The Ballon d’Or ceremony usually takes place at the Theatre du Chatelet in Paris, but this year it is temporarily moving to England to pay tribute to the 70th anniversary of Sir Stanley Matthews’ victory in the inaugural Ballon d’Or race back in 1956.

Ballon d’Or 2026 tickets

Tickets for the ceremony in London were available through the UEFA website, but have now all sold out. As have the hospitality packages.

Who is the favourite for the Ballon d’Or 2026 award?

Kane is the current bookmakers’ favourite to claim his first Ballon d’Or award in 2026, following a phenomenal season with Bayern Munich in which his remarkable 61 goals in 51 appearances across all competitions powered Vincent Kompany’s side to a memorable domestic treble and also earned him a second European Golden Shoe.

He also played a vital role in England reaching the semi-finals of the World Cup in North America earlier this summer, with the Three Lions eventually claiming third place ahead of favourites France.

Kane would be the first English winner of the Ballon d’Or since Michael Owen in 2001 and only the fourth overall, adding to a list that also includes Sir Bobby Charlton, Sir Stanley Matthews and the late Kevin Keegan, who did it twice in back-to-back years in 1978 and 1979 while playing in Germany with Hamburg.

Who is hosting the Ballon d’Or 2026 ceremony?

This year’s ceremony will be hosted by journalist and presenter Kate Scott and Arsenal icon Thierry Henry, who finished as runner-up to Juventus legend Pavel Nedved for the Ballon d’Or award in 2003.

Who won the Ballon d’Or last year?

The trophy is currently held by Dembele, with the PSG forward having beaten Barcelona’s Yamal - who was runner-up and also won Young Player of the Year in 2025.

Who has won the most Ballon d’Or awards?

Messi comfortably leads the all-time list of Ballon d’Or wins with eight - three more than great rival Cristiano Ronaldo, who has five. Michel Platini, Johan Cruyff and Franz Beckenbauer all won three each during their illustrious careers.
