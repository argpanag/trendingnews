Lugano vs Servette Preview

Lugano meet Servette at AIL Arena, with kick-off set for 14:30 on Thursday 3 September.

The Black and Whites managed four shots on target and had 42% possession, with Dereck Moncada and Beckham Castro each scoring one goal.

Servette have taken four points from their opening four Super League matches. Last time out, they played out a 1-1 home draw against FC Luzern at Stade de Geneve. The Geneva side enjoyed 64% possession and had four shots on goal, with Miroslav Stevanovic on the scoresheet.
