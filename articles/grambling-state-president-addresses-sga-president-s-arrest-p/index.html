Grambling State president addresses SGA president’s arrest, police resignations

TheTools · 12.10.2026 15:44 · source · permalink

GRAMBLING, La. (KSLA) - Grambling State University President Dr. Martin Lemele, Jr., has released a statement following the arrest of former SGA President Life Makarudze on third-degree rape charges and the resignation of GSU Chief of Police Rodney L. Demery and Assistant Chief Joshua Feliciano.

The statement was released directly to GSU’s news platform on Oct. 10 and was addressed directly to the GSU community.
