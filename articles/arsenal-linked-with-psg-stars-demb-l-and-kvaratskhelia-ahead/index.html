Arsenal Linked With PSG Stars Dembélé And Kvaratskhelia Ahead Of January 2027 Window | Signs Tv

TheTools · 04.09.2026 20:32 · source · permalink

Photo courtesy

Arsenal are reportedly considering a major attacking addition during the January 2027 transfer window, with Paris Saint-Germain forwards Ousmane Dembélé and Khvicha Kvaratskhelia reportedly on the club’s radar.

According to Spanish outlet Fichajes, Arsenal are monitoring the situation of both PSG stars as they assess options for strengthening their attack midway through the 2026/27 season.

Dembélé, 29, remains an important member of the PSG squad after joining the French champions from Barcelona in August 2023. He signed a five-year contract with the Parisian club and has established himself as one of their leading attacking players.

Kvaratskhelia, 25, joined PSG from Napoli in January 2025 and is contracted to the French club until 2029. The Georgia international has also become an important figure in PSG’s attack since moving to France.

The reported interest in the two players comes after Arsenal strengthened several areas of their squad but remain linked with further attacking reinforcements.

Fichajes claims Arsenal could consider either Dembélé or Kvaratskhelia for a potential January move, although there is currently no confirmation that the Gunners have opened formal negotiations with PSG.

Kvaratskhelia would be a particularly ambitious target given his contract situation and importance to PSG. The winger has continued to feature prominently for the French champions since his arrival from Napoli.

Dembélé would similarly represent a high-profile signing for Arsenal, having developed into an established star at PSG and one of the most experienced attacking players in the French side.

Bournemouth forward Eli Junior Kroupi is reportedly being considered as an alternative should Arsenal fail to secure either PSG player.

Kroupi, 20, joined Bournemouth on a long-term contract and has previously been linked with interest from several major European clubs, including Arsenal.

The French forward's contractual situation means Bournemouth would have significant negotiating leverage if Arsenal return with an offer in January.
