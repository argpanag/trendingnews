A few more storms this evening with better chances ahead

TheTools · 23.09.2026 23:18 · source · permalink

WICHITA FALLS, Texas (KAUZ) - We’ve got a few showers moving through Wichita Falls over the next few hours, but for the most part, we’ll stay dry and mostly cloudy through the rest of the evening. Temperatures cool into the 70s overnight.

Tomorrow, expect another round of isolated showers and storms in the afternoon, with highs climbing back into the 90s. That pattern holds into Friday, with things trending a touch warmer as we move into the weekend.

Part of what’s fueling this activity is moisture streaming up from Hurricane Polo out in the Pacific. That tropical moisture is helping boost our cloud cover and giving storms something to work with each afternoon, even though we’re not dealing with any direct tropical impacts here in Texoma.

Looking further out, better rain chances arrive by early next week as a much-needed cold front finally works its way into the region — a pattern change we’ll be watching closely.
