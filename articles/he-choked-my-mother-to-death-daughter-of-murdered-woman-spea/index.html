‘He choked my mother to death’: Daughter of murdered woman speaks out

TheTools · 23.11.2026 05:00 · source · permalink

The daughter of the woman murdered by an American now living in Ireland has accused him of being a narcissist - and of showing no real remorse for the crime.

“He can soften it or gloss over it, but that rage is still in him. He is not a benign little puppy dog like he claims to be,” a furious Thora Collard said of Michael Glacken - who threw her mother down the stairs of their California home before strangling her to death when she complained about his drinking.

And we have also learned that Glacken, who served 18 years in prison for the 2002 murder of his wife and Thora’s mother Kimberly, was able to get into Ireland in 2024 despite his conviction - because he has an Irish passport.

He would normally not have been allowed into Ireland because of his 2003 conviction for second degree murder - but he has an Irish passport, which means he has every right to live here.

Glacken, who used the name DeLongis, denied murdering Kimberly, but admitted manslaughter. But a jury did not believe him and convicted him of second-degree murder.

News of Mr Glacken, 63, living in Ireland came to light when we revealed on Saturday that he had set up home just outside the village of Glinsk on the Roscommon/Galway border.

He insisted in our exclusive interview that people in the area had no reason to be afraid of him - and he was filled with remorse for his crime.

He told us: “You gotta tell the people I am no danger to anyone.

“I couldn’t kill a fly after what I had done. I am a different man than a long time ago. I hope people can understand that.”

And he added: “I went to prison for 18 years. And I was so, so f***ing remorseful and I strove only every day at that place to better myself and learn why I had lashed out.”

But, speaking to us from her home in the United States, Mrs Collard - whom Glacken walked down the aisle for her first marriage - said she did not accept his claim that he was remorseful, despite apologies he has made in public and in letters to her.

And that, she said, meant she could not forgive him.

She told us: “For forgiveness he’d actually have to have remorse for his actions, not what he lost… He has apologised in court, but never fully been remorseful.

“He always blamed the alcohol.

“He pushed her down the stairs from behind, her head smashed into the sheetrock midway down, and then as she laid there disoriented, but conscious, he climbed on top of her, put his hands around her neck and choked the life out of my 45 year old mother.”

Mrs Collard also said she believed Glacken was a narcissist who was playing the victim.

She said: “You can’t be forgiven if you don’t show true remorse

“He is still playing victim… that’s clear in your article.

“Michael has been a victim his whole life.

“His dad left him, his mom didn’t love him enough, his stepfather was mean to him, my mom expected him to work. It’s always been about him. Even the parole officer told him he was a narcissist.

“All of his apologies have been regurgitated (Alcoholics Anonymous) responses. Never any true accountability. He is a narcissist.

“He would send me letters from prison and he always blamed the alcohol for his actions.”

Glacken moved to Ireland in late 2024 with his second wife, Deborah.

They were long term partners and married each other when he was serving his sentence of 23 years to life at the Ironwood State Prison in California, from which he was released in mid 2021 after serving 18 years.

He and Deborah even set up a coffee shop here together - but she became ill with pancreatic cancer and returned to America, where she died at the end of October.

He told us he intends to stay in Ireland - and people who know his past here have accepted him.

But, speaking to us after our article appeared, Mrs Collard alleged that Glacken had a serious drinking problem when he killed Kimberly, and that he was having what she said was an “emotional affair” with Deborah before the murder. We put that to Mr Glacken, who claimed he first met Deborah when he went to buy then wife, Kimberly flowers 1994.

"The first time I walked in that flower shop Deborah and I saw each other, and it was over. Done. Can I stress that enough?

“Emotional relationship. Well, I can tell you, we never cheated. We never slept together. We never did anything like that, the only problem was when we looked at each other. We knew it was gonna be Bonnie and Clyde. That was the problem.

“I never ever, cheated on Kim. That thought it never crossed my mind. But perhaps I spent too long at the flower shop with Deborah. I’m not entirely sure what an emotional relationship is, but Deborah and I were instantly close. But we had our duties and we left it at that. Wasn’t gonna be any fooling around or horsing around or sleeping around that was not even in the picture. But we did confide in one another, and that is the emotional relationship. And I ended it when Kim said end it.”

When asked if Kimberly had any problems with him before the murder, Thora replied: “Not physical. Just verbal. He was a very difficult man and his drinking was bad.

“He had an emotional affair with Deborah before he killed my mom, his wife who moved over there with him.”

And she spoke of her anger at Glacken’s new life in Ireland.

She said: “It pretty much rubs everyone the wrong way.

“You don’t get to murder your wife, marry your mistress, then change your name and move to Ireland to live a happy little life in a cottage by a lake.

“He went from my mom supporting him, to the prison supporting him, to the homewrecker supporting him.

“Why does he get to live peacefully in a cottage and retire?

“The way he murdered my mother was brutal and personal…. No hesitation. That is why it’s murder, not manslaughter.”

Meanwhile, Mrs Collard also sent us a letter she wrote to the parole board in an unsuccessful bid to stop Glacken’s release in 2021.

In it she said: “My name is Thora Collard. I am the daughter of Kim DeLongis, the woman murdered by Michael DeLongis.

“I have known Mr. DeLongis since I was approximately ten years old. Mr. DeLongis is a very intelligent man.

“I have always known him to be an avid reader and seeker of knowledge. He was a skilled chameleon who could adapt to his situation and manipulate those around him. He also had a mean streak and liked to pick on people he deemed “weak” and make them feel small. As an adult I can now see he was a narcissist. Michael DeLongis was a jealous and manipulative man.

“His control and need to be wanted was so great he even told my mother he was jealous of her love for her dogs, and of course her children.”

We contacted Mr Glacken last night. He confirmed he had an Irish passport. And he also repeated his remorse.

He said: “Kim is always on my mind every day. Every single day. Because of what I did. I remember every morning, I did a terrible thing, the worst thing, and every day since that day I try to make things better; help, be the better person, even if it now means being a smaller person.

“I remember Kim every single morning when I wake and what I took from her.

“Once again, since my crime, I have done nothing, nothing but try to better myself because that is what is required. You can’t change the past. You can only change yourself. Which I’ve done. And rage? Only had rage once. It was terrible. Cost us all everything.

“My whole life I had kept everything bottled up, which I should never have done.

“My fighting days are over. I just wish there was more forgiveness and kindness in the world. That is what I’m working towards.

“I really love my little tiny corner in the west of Ireland. I just want to live out my days quietly. Lonely now, but still quietly.

“In the past it took me a long time to forgive myself for my actions, and I would never ask anyone to forgive me.

“But please don’t call yourself a Christian or a Catholic, and preach forgiveness and then be vindictive on me.

“I’m just tired. I’m beaten.

“You win. You all win, and I’m so happy for you. I’m so tired and sad, you guys win.

“You have beaten me. And I am happy for your win. What more do you want from me? You guys got your gossip and your target.

“You’re hurting me now. Please let me grieve my wife. I am so sad. Please stop the pain, you guys win. I give it to you. I want you to be happy with your win.

“You have destroyed me, you won.”
