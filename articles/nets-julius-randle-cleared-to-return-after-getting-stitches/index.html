Nets' Julius Randle: Cleared to return after getting stitches

TheTools · 17.11.2026 03:32 · source · permalink

Randle exited Monday's game against the 76ers during the fourth quarter to get stitches in his mouth but returned with 5:01 remaining in the period, Chris Hine of The Philly Inquirer reports.

Randle appeared to suffer the injury on a collision with Jaylen Brown, who was also spotted receiving medical attention on the bench before returning to the game. The Nets' training staff worked quickly to get Randle back into the game for the final few minutes of Monday's clash.
