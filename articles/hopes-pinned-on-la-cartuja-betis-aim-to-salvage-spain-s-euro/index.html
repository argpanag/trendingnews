Hopes pinned on La Cartuja: Betis aim to salvage Spain's European week

The Champions League has delivered a difficult night for Spanish football. Barcelona fell to PSG (4-2), Villarreal lost to Liverpool (4-3), and Atlético de Madrid could only manage a draw in Stuttgart (1-1). Three matches, no wins, and a chance for Betis to put a more positive spin on the European matchday. The Green and Whites host Feyenoord at La Cartuja this Wednesday with the chance to maintain their perfect winning record in the competition.

Manuel Pellegrini’s side head into the match with six points after beating Lille and Porto. Two narrow wins, but enough to show that Betis are not in the Champions League simply to enjoy the experience. A third victory would strengthen their qualification hopes before a run of fixtures that includes trips to Borussia Dortmund and Bayern Munich.

OneFootball Videos

The match comes after the heavy 5-2 defeat to Barcelona in LaLiga last weekend. Curiously, that loss gives the Green and Whites something in common with tonight’s opponents: Feyenoord also know what it is like to be thrashed by Barça, who beat them 5-1 in the Champions League in September. Betis and Feyenoord therefore arrive at La Cartuja nursing similar wounds and needing to prove that those setbacks do not reflect their true quality. The Dutch side do, however, come into the game buoyed by their recent victory over Como, while Pellegrini is looking to restore defensive solidity without sacrificing the attacking identity that has allowed his team to compete successfully in Europe.

The Chilean coach will also be without the injured Cucho Hernández. His absence once again puts the spotlight on Troy Parrott, the Irish striker who knows Dutch football inside out after spells at Excelsior and AZ Alkmaar. The forward also has unfinished business with Feyenoord: he scored against them during his time in the Netherlands but never beat them. The good news is Aitor Ruibal’s return to the squad after five months out. His versatility gives Pellegrini more options in a week of packed fixtures.
