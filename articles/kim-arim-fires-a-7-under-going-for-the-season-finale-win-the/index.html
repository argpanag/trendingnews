Tied for the lead with Thitikul in the first round of the CME Group Tour Championship

Haeran Ryu, Hyejin Choi and Somi Lee tied for fourth, one shot back

Korda, Ina Yoon and Yamashita tied for 20th, chasing the leaders

Kim Arim got off to a flying start in the season-ending event.

On Nov. 20 (Korea time), Kim Arim made nine birdies and two bogeys in the first round of the LPGA Tour's CME Group Tour Championship, which was held at Tiburon Golf Club (par 72) in Naples, Florida, with a total purse of $11 million. Her 7-under-par 65 put her in a tie for the lead with world No. 1 Jeeno Thitikul of Thailand and Carys Davidson of Australia.

Kim A-rim (left) and Arpichaya Yubol embrace after finishing the first round of the CME Group Tour Championship. Naples=AFP Yonhap News

Kim made three birdies over the first nine holes but had an up-and-down round, recording two bogeys as well. She showed her strength on the back nine, making six birdies without a bogey. Kim won the 2020 U.S. Women's Open, a major championship, and made her LPGA Tour debut the following year. She then claimed her second and third career victories at the 2024 Lotte Championship and the Hilton Grand Vacations Tournament of Champions in January 2025, respectively.

In an official interview after the first round, Kim said, "I've played here six times, so I feel comfortable here. My putts were dropping, and my shots were good. I was able to get into a good rhythm."

Ryu Haeran, Choi Hyejin and Lee Somi are chasing the leaders, tied for fourth at 6-under 66, one shot back. "When I was a rookie, I didn't play well on courses in Florida," Ryu said with a smile. "But I've been doing better than before lately, and I think today is making me a fan of Florida courses."
