Anticipated GTA VI game sees record pre-orders in the Netherlands, drives PS5 sales

TheTools · 17.11.2026 13:30 · source · permalink

Game retailers in the Netherlands are also benefiting from the launch of the highly anticipated video game Gran Theft Auto (GTA) VI. Electronics retailers Coolblue and Media Markt, as well as gaming retailer Nedgame, report that they have already sold large numbers of game cases containing download codes for the game, known as “Code in a Box,” ahead of its launch overnight from Wednesday into Thursday. There appears to be particularly strong interest in playing GTA VI on the PlayStation 5.

Coolblue reports having received thousands of orders for download codes, most of which are for the PS5. “GTA VI is the biggest game launch ever for Coolblue,” a company spokesperson stated. In the lead-up to the launch, Coolblue has also noticed increased interest in additional storage for the PS5 "so that there is enough space for the game.”

The number of orders for GTA VI is also “extremely high” at MediaMarkt, according to a spokesperson for the retailer. MediaMarkt is unwilling to disclose how many copies of the game have already been sold but does state that its number of pre-orders is the highest in the Netherlands “of any game in history."

According to the spokesperson, the retailer’s promotional efforts have contributed to his success. In the lead-up to the release, MediaMarkt is organizing special events at several locations throughout the Netherlands, as well as online, to promote the game.

Additionally, more people are purchasing a PS5 specifically to play GTA VI, the spokesperson reports. Other gaming consoles, as well as products such as memory cards and storage devices, are also selling well. GTA VI will also be available to play on the Xbox Series consoles.

Niels Thomassen, director of Nedgame, says that his gaming retailer has sold a substantial number of download codes. However, he adds that the number of orders might have been as much as five times higher “if it had been a traditional CD release." Nevertheless, he expects GTA VI to become the best-selling video game of all time, as fans have been waiting 14 years for it. Its predecessor, GTA V, was released in 2013.

Thomassen further estimates that Nedgame has sold approximately 20 to 30 percent more PS5 consoles as a result of GTA VI. However, the same cannot be said for Microsoft’s Xbox consoles. "Microsoft withdrew from the Dutch console market some time ago; its consoles have not been available for purchase for quite a while.”

People who have a download code will be able to play the game starting Thursday. According to the latest figures from research firm Sensor Tower, an estimated 12.1 million copies of the game have already been sold worldwide.

Reporting by ANP
