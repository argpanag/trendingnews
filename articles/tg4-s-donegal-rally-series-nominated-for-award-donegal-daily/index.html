TG4's Donegal Rally series nominated for award - Donegal Daily

TheTools · 24.10.2026 09:20 · source · permalink

Kris Meeke sending it over a crest on day 1 of the 2026 Donegal International Rally. Credit - Donegal International Rally/Facebook.

A TG4 documentary series following the Donegal Rally and produced in the Donegal Gaeltacht has been nominated for an award.

Taobh Thiar den Roth has been shortlisted for this year's Gradaim Chumarsáide an Oireachtais awards, in the Sraith Teilifíse na Bliana (Best Series of the Year) category.

The series went behind the scenes during the 2025 edition of the Donegal International Rally, following several local drivers and the organisers of the event.

It followed the stories of Jordan Greene McGee and PJ Mór and PJ Óg McDermott as they took on their first Donegal Rally, as well as veteran driver Kieran Ó Rabhartaigh's 31st participation in the event.

They also followed Derry's Callum Devine as he completed his quest to win three Donegal Rallies in a row, with inside access also granted to follow Clerk of the Course Eamonn McGee and one of the organisers, Suzanne McCafferty, in their roles.

Its creators, the Gaoth Dobhair-based production company LIGID Léiriúcháin, also say it is the first programme created entirely within the Donegal Gaeltacht.

Each stage of production before the programme's delivery to TG4 took place entirely within Gaoth Dobhair, with most of its production crew also being based in Donegal.

Sonia Nic Giolla Easbuig, who founded LIGID with her husband Peter when they returned home to Gaoth Dobhair five years ago, said the nomination was recognition for everyone involved.

“We wanted to prove that programmes didn't just have to be filmed here. They could be made here from beginning to end,” she said.

The team thanked the rally drivers, co-drivers and their families for welcoming them into their lives, as well as TG4, commissioning editor Donncha Mac Con Iomaire, Motorsport Ireland and the organisers of the Donegal International Rally.”

“To the people of Donegal and everyone who shared their stories with us, you are the heart of Taobh Thiar den Roth,” Sonia said.

“Thank you to the LIGID team Peter, Ruairí, Martin, Conor and Hannah for their work on the series, especially Martin O’ Donnell, our main Editor on the series.”

The winners of this year's Gradaim Chumarsáide an Oireachtais will be announced in Killarney on Wednesday the 28th of October.

Taobh Thiar den Roth is still available to watch on streaming via the TG4 Player.
