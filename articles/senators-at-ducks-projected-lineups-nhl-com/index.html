Status report

Neither team held a morning skate. ... Forwards Burakovsky and Foegele will each be out 3-4 weeks. … Hodgson, a forward, will serve the last of a two-game suspension for boarding Los Angeles Kings defenseman Brandt Clarke on Thursday. … The Ducks recalled Brossoit from San Diego of the American Hockey League on Saturday. ... Mintyukov was activated from injured reserve and will return after missing four games with a lower-body injury; Hinds, a defenseman, will be a healthy scratch for the first time this season. … Terry, a forward, returned to practice Saturday for the first time since undergoing surgery on June 9 for a hip impingement and labral tear. Ducks coach Joel Quenneville said Terry will return "definitely in the next week or so." ... Moore also practiced for the first time this season Saturday, but the defenseman is further away from returning. He was injured on the first day of training camp. ... Anaheim sent defenseman Nick Jensen to San Diego on Sunday.
