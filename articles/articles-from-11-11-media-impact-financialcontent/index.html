Articles from 11:11 Media ImpactParis Hilton Unites 200+ Neurodivergent Creators for UNSTOPPABLE, a First-of-Its-Kind ADHD Creator Summit Paris Hilton, through her nonprofit 11:11 Media Impact and presented by Loop Earplugs, brought together more than 200 neurodivergent creators with a combined audience reach of 500 million+, along with advocates and experts, for UNSTOPPABLE, a first-of-its-kind ADHD creator summit held at Slivington Manor in Los Angeles on October 15. The daylong event featured interactive activations, educational sessions, and candid conversations designed to build community and equip creators with tools to help their audiences better understand ADHD.
