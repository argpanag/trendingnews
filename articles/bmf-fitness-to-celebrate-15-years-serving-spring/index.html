BMF Fitness to celebrate 15 years serving Spring

TheTools · 21.09.2026 20:00 · source · permalink

Co-owner Amy Ortmann said BMF Fitness, the local fitness center, is celebrating 15 years of serving the Spring area in November.

“BMF has never been just about the workout. It’s about the people,” Ortmann said. “Whether you’re brand new to fitness, getting back into a routine, looking to build muscle or lose weight, training for something specific, or simply searching for a community that makes showing up a little more fun — there’s a place for you at BMF.”

The details

To commemorate the anniversary, Ortmann said BMF Fitness invites guests to experience its services free of charge during the month of November. In addition, new participants can get started anytime with a free two-week pass.

“After 15 years, we’re incredibly grateful to still be doing what we love in the community that helped us build it,” Ortmann said.

In case you missed it

Founded in 2011, BMF Fitness is owned and operated by Kelli Barrett and Amy Ortmann and is known for its coach-led group fitness classes.

The fitness center also offers personal training, strength and conditioning, nutrition coaching, specialty programs, and individualized fitness solutions for all ages and fitness levels, per officials.

With two studios under one roof, members can choose from a variety of workouts including:

  • Strength training

  • High-Intensity Interval Training

  • Spin

  • Barre

  • Core

  • Mobility
