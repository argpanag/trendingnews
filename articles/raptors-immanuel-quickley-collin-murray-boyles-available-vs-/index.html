Raptors' Immanuel Quickley, Collin Murray-Boyles available vs. Celtics

TheTools · 11.11.2026 17:35 · source · permalink

The Toronto Raptors are getting two key pieces back.

Immanuel Quickley and Collin Murray-Boyles are both available for the Raptors as they take on the Boston Celtics, Sportsnet's Michael Grange reported on Wednesday.

Quickley has missed the past two games with right hamstring tightness, while Murray-Boyles has been fighting an illness.

Quickley, 27, is averaging 17.4 points, 3.5 rebounds, 5.8 assists and 31.8 minutes over eight games.

Meanwhile, Murray-Boyles is averaging 10.7 points, 5.8 rebounds and 1.3 assists across nine games this season.

The 21-year-old has started the Raptors' last three games as they continue to deal with injury concerns. He broke out for 19 points, seven rebounds, two assists, four steals and a block in Friday's 121-107 win over the Milwaukee Bucks.
