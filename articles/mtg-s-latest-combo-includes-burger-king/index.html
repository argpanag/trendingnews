MTG's Latest Combo Includes Burger King

TheTools · 10.11.2026 15:33 · source · permalink

Fast food chain offering BK sleeves and deckbox in Italy

The home of the Whopper will also be home to an exclusive Magic: The Gathering combo in Italy this month.

Until November 30, hungry Magic fans can get their hands on a Burger King branded deckbox and sleeves for the low price of 20 Euros while also grabbing a full meal from the fast food giant. The Great Gathering Bundle has taken Magic social media by storm, with pictures of the accessories popping up on X and Reddit.

According to the Burger King website, the combo comes with your choice of Chicken Krispper, Double Cheese King, or Whopper, fries, and a drink.

This crossover continues Magic’s promotions with restaurants and snacks as earlier this month we saw the Mythical Meats beef sticks make the news.
