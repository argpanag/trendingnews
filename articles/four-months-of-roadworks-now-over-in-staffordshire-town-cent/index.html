Four months of roadworks now over in Staffordshire town centre

TheTools · 01.11.2026 15:47 · source · permalink

A scheme to improve main roads in a town centre has now been completed. Road repairs have been carried out in Stone since July - including on Crown Street, Newcastle Street, Station Road, Granville Square, Christchurch Way, and Radford Street.

Improvements have included repairs to kerbs, footways, and drains and the installation of tactile crossing points. Final resurfacing has now been completed.

Councillor Patrick Allen, Staffordshire County Council cabinet support member for highways, said: "This has been a significant scheme and an important one for Stone as it will bring huge improvements for motorists in a thriving town centre. Our project team has worked hard to complete the scheme on schedule, coordinating the start with the school summer holidays to minimise disruption as well as carrying out the recent overnight resurfacing.

"We do appreciate that any major scheme, particularly in town centres, will be disruptive, so we’d again like to thank people for their patience and understanding while work has taken place. This demonstrates our commitment to ensuring we have good quality roads across the county and it’s great to see the scheme completed.”
