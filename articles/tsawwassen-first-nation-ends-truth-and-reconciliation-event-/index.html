Tsawwassen First Nation ends Truth and Reconciliation event early; police say 4 people hit in 'collision' | CBC

TheTools · 30.09.2026 18:56 · source · permalink

The Latest

  • The Tsawwassen First Nation has ended its event marking the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation early due to an ongoing incident, according to an alert.
  • Police say four pedestrians were hit in a "collision," describing the events as a "medical incident."
  • The City of Delta, B.C., says the public should avoid the Tsawwassen Mills area, south of Vancouver.

Updates

  • September 30

    11 minutes ago

    What event was happening in the area?

    Lauren Vanderdeen

    The Tsawwassen First Nation (TFN) was hosting a community walk starting at the TFN Recreation Centre on National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, according to the nation's Facebook page.

    "This day is an opportunity for our community to come together to honour and remember the children," said the post.

    A house post unveiling was scheduled for 10:15 a.m. PT at an entrance to the nearby Tsawwassen Mills mall.

    Guests were encouraged to wear orange to support and commit to reconciliation.

    A statement said officers are on scene investigating what police are calling a "medical incident." They are also asking people to avoid the area. 

  • 28 minutes ago

    First Nation urges people to avoid Tsawwassen Mills area due to 'civil emergency'

    Michelle Gomez

    People are being asked to avoid the Tsawwassen Mills area in Metro Vancouver due to what the Tsawwassen First Nation is calling a "civil emergency."

    "An incident is currently in progress that has ended our Truth & Reconciliation event. Participants are asked to walk back the way we came and stay out of the area," says an alert posted just before 11 a.m. PT Wednesday.

    The City of Delta posted on social media asking people in the area to follow the direction of local authorities. The provincial government has also posted an alert.

    CBC News has contacted police for more information.
