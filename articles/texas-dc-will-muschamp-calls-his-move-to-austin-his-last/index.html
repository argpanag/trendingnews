Texas DC Will Muschamp calls his move to Austin his last

TheTools · 24.11.2026 17:21 · source · permalink
  • Texas Longhorns Football

Georgia defensive coordinator Glenn Schumann’s departure for Tallahassee may challenge that statement by Muschamp.

During the spring, Texas Longhorns defensive coordinator Will Muschamp was asked in his lone media availability since his hire last December whether he wants to be a head coach again.

“No,” Muschamp responded.

With head coaching positions coming open across college football, whether Muschamp wants another chance at the role he held at Florida from 2011 to 2014 and at South Carolina from 2016 to 2020 started to surface again in recent days.

And then a truly seismic shift happened on Monday when Florida State hired longtime Georgia assistant Glenn Schumann to replace Mike Norvell as the head coach of the Seminoles.

Since Mel Tucker left to take over the Michigan program after the 2018 season, Schumann has been a staple on Kirby Smart’s staff, sharing the co-defensive coordinator role with current Oregon head coach Dan Lanning and Muschamp before Smart named Schumann the defensive coordinator in 2024.

Current co-defensive coordinator Travaris Robinson called plays for Muschamp at South Carolina, but because he’s regarded as more of a position coach who shares enough responsibility with game planning to merit a co-coordinator designation, the expectation is that Smart will now make a hire to lead the Georgia defense.

And Muschamp has deep ties to the program and to Smart.

A former walk on at Georgia, Muschamp played in the same defensive backfield with Smart, the start of a friendship that has lasted more than three decades and precipitated Muschamp’s return to Athens after he was fired at South Carolina, holding multiple different roles as he tried to take a step back from full defensive coordinator responsibilities for family reasons.

If there’s one defensive coordinator job that would cause Muschamp to leave Texas, it’s the Georgia job.

But Muschamp and his wife also have deep ties to Austin after his previous stint a the Longhorns defensive coordinator from 2008 to 2010. During that time, Muschamp’s wife Carol built such long-lasting friendships that she would return to Austin for about 10 days every spring.

“We love it. We loved living here before.. It was a very easy transition for us — our 14th move, and our last move, I can tell ya that,” Muschamp said on the Lawyers and Longhorns podcast two weeks ago.

Last week, Texas was already reportedly in discussions about extending Muschamp’s initial three-year contract, which could now also necessitate a raise as well.

But only time will tell if Muschamp’s statement about his move to Austin is actually his last with the position on Smart’s staff now open.
