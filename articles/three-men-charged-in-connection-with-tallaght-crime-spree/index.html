Three men charged in connection with Tallaght crime spree

TheTools · 21.09.2026 07:58 · source · permalink

Three men have been charged in connection with robberies in Tallaght after gardai spotted a man trying to run from a stolen car.

Officers from the Tallaght Community Engagement and the Serious Crime Unit, who have been probing a series of car thefts and other burglaries and associated crimes in the Dublin 24 area, spotted a man getting out of a car and trying to run from them on Saturday in the Cloonmore area of Jobstown. The man, aged in his 40s, was subsequently caught and arrested.

The car he was running from had recently been reported stolen after a burglary in the area. Gardai also arrested two more men, aged in their 20s and 40s, in connection with the crime spree.

All three are set to appear before Tallaght District Court this morning. Gardai said: "Three males have been arrested and charged following operations conducted on Saturday, 19th September 2026 by Gardaí from the Tallaght Community Engagement and the Serious Crime Unit as part of ongoing investigations into burglary, car theft and associated crimes in the Tallaght area. While on active patrol in the Cloonmore Park area, Gardaí observed a man exiting a vehicle and subsequently attempting to flee on foot.

"The male, aged in his 40s, was apprehended and arrested in connection with the investigations. He was detained under Section 4 of the Criminal Justice Act, 1984 at a Garda Station within the Dublin Metropolitan Region.

"The vehicle recovered had been reported stolen following a recent burglary in the area. In the course of the operation Gardaí arrested two additional males, aged in their 20s and 40s.

"Both were also detained under Section 4 of the Criminal Justice Act 1984 at a Garda Station in the Dublin Metropolitan Region. All three men have been charged with a number of offences and are scheduled to appear before Tallaght District Court on Monday, 21st September 2026, at 10:30am.

"Investigations remain ongoing."
