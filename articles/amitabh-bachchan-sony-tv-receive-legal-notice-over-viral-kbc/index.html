Veteran Bollywood actor Amitabh Bachchan hosted Kaun Banega Crorepati 18, has reportedly landed in legal trouble.

A lawyer based in Sangrur, Punjab, has sent a legal notice to megastar Amitabh Bachchan and Sony TV regarding a viral video that allegedly shows the actor posing a question about bomb attacks on police stations in Punjab on the quiz show KBC 18.

Advocate Indrajeet Arora raised an objection to the question, asserting that it casts Punjab in a negative light and seeks to harm the state's reputation.

According to reports, he demanded that the video be removed from national media immediately and that Bachchan and Sony TV issue an unconditional apology if the footage is confirmed to be from an actual episode of the show.

According to a report in The Statesman, Arora stated that he encountered the video three to four days earlier, after one of his clients came to him with screenshots and a clip that allegedly showed Bachchan questioning a contestant about the state where criminals had recently launched bomb attacks on police stations without any fear.

The advocate stated, "Three to four days ago, one of my clients approached me and showed me a screenshot and a video from KBC, the quiz show that airs on Sony TV, where general knowledge questions are asked to contestants and they win prize money as they give correct answers."

He went on to question how relevant the question was, saying it seemed aimed at the image of a particular state instead of testing a person's general knowledge.

"I did not feel that this was a general knowledge question; this is a direct attempt to defame a state," he said.

According to the video, Amitabh Bachchan is allegedly seen posing a question for Rs 12.5 lakh about which Indian state had lately seen criminals boldly carry out bomb attacks on police stations. The four options shown are Uttar Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, West Bengal and Punjab.

The contestant is said to have used the 50:50 lifeline, after which Uttar Pradesh and West Bengal were removed. Amitabh Bachchan is then allegedly heard declaring Punjab to be the correct answer.

Arora said he had sent legal notices to both Amitabh Bachchan and Sony TV, seeking clarification on whether the footage was actually part of the show.

"For this reason, I have sent a legal notice to Sony TV and Amitabh Bachchan, demanding that it be immediately removed from national media, and if this video was made from their show, they should issue an unconditional apology," he reportedly said.

The advocate further stated that he did not wish for Punjab or any other state to be depicted in a manner that might damage its reputation. He also expressed worries regarding the spread of such content before the forthcoming elections in Punjab, a time when political activity is anticipated to rise.

Asked if the video might have been created or altered using artificial intelligence (AI), Arora said its authenticity would be established once he receives a reply from Amitabh Bachchan and the channel. He further said that legal action could be contemplated against those responsible if the footage proved to be fabricated.

The clip, roughly two minutes long, has been extensively circulated on social media, with numerous questions being raised about whether Amitabh Bachchan actually posed the question on KBC.
