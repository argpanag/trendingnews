Shares of Take-Two Interactive Software (TTWO) fell 6.3% on Monday, November 16, 2026. The S&P 500 lost 1.9% and the Nasdaq Composite fell 2.5% the same day. The company issued no announcement, earnings report or filing around the move. So what else was happening in the market that Monday, and what was on Take-Two's own calendar?

A Weak Market Day Before Take-Two's Big Week

Chip stocks declined that Monday. AMD and Intel slid ahead of two big tests for the AI trade: Nvidia's results on Tuesday and Anthropic's expected trading debut on Thursday.

Take-Two's own big date was later the same week. Grand Theft Auto VI comes out on November 19, three days after the fall. Net bookings are the sales measure management guides on. For fiscal Q3 2027, the quarter of the launch, it expects $3.35 billion to $3.5 billion. This compares with $1.76 billion a year earlier. On its November 9 call, management kept its full-year outlook. It said it tends to avoid claiming success before it occurs.

Did Take-Two Fall Alone That Monday?

No. All four companies on Take-Two's peer list fell during the same session. The declines ranged from 1.1% for Microsoft to 7.6% for Roblox. Two other companies worth $2 billion or more share its GICS sub-industry, interactive home entertainment. Both fell, with a median drop of 5.7%.

The wider sector held up better. XLC, the communication services sector fund, lost 1.7%. That was slightly less than the S&P 500's 1.9% drop. While Take-Two fell alongside its peers, its losses went much further than its sector and the market.

Where Does Take-Two Stock Stand Now?

A fall this large is rare for Take-Two. The stock had dropped this much on only three sessions in the prior 365 days. Its last move at least this large, up or down, was an 8.3% gain on November 10, the day after the earnings call.

Over the past twelve months the shares lost 10.1%, trailing the 11.4% return of the S&P 500. The past month proved kinder to the stock, which gained 4.0% against a 3.1% loss for the index. Monday's close of $213.47 was below the year's highest close, $258.41 on July 6. The year's lowest close was $189.69 on March 27.

Take-Two trades at 6.2 times its sales over the past twelve months. The S&P 500 trades at 3.2 times. The company is expected to report fiscal Q3 2027 results in February 2027. Net bookings for that quarter, the first with Grand Theft Auto VI on sale, are the figure to watch.

That is the trouble with holding a single stock. No one can see moves like these coming.
