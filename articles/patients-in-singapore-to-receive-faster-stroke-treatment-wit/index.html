Patients in Singapore to receive faster stroke treatment with new SGH facility

TheTools · 30.10.2026 15:48 · source · permalink

Stroke patients at Singapore's largest hospital can now receive life-saving treatment more quickly, with the country's first integrated angiography suite. It's part of Singapore General Hospital's new 12-storey emergency and neuroscience building, which aims to improve patient care and coordination among teams. Natalie Ong reports.
