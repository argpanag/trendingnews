Sophie Cunningham laughs at Stephen A Smith's belittling claim about White NBA players

TheTools · 14.11.2026 02:02 · source · permalink

The WNBA star responded to Smith's claim that the Lakers cannot win a championship led by 'three White dudes'

Skip Bayless DEFENDS Lakers' New Trio After Stephen A. Smith's Brutal Insult | Don't @ Me w/ Dan Dakich

On this episode of Don't @ Me with Dan Dakich, we dive into the MASSIVE media feud over the Los Angeles Lakers' newly formed trio. Skip Bayless completely shut down Stephen A. Smith's viral and racially motivated criticism of the new trio

Luka Doncic is on pace for one of the greatest seasons in NBA history, averaging just under 40 points per game. WNBA star Sophie Cunningham loves watching him set these records, especially when he makes Stephen A. Smith look like a clown in the process.

This week, Smith again said the Los Angeles Lakers cannot win an NBA championship led by "three White dudes": Doncic, Austin Reaves and Walker Kessler. He said they need more "brothers" to help them.

Cunningham disagrees.

"He’s killing it," she said on her "No Hesitation" podcast about Doncic’s historic start to the season. "He has been so much fun to watch."

"Stephen A. was, like, ‘Why would the Lakers ever build a team around three White boys? Like, look at the NBA history. That’s never going to happen. That’s never going to be successful.’ And he (Doncic) is out there freaking killing it. I think it’s so funny."

Also funny: In the very season Smith is belittling the idea of building around three White players, the Lakers’ trio ranks as the NBA’s best, according to ESPN’s own advanced analytics.

As OutKick explained this week, Doncic, Reaves and Kessler are not the reason the Lakers have only the 10th-best championship odds. Based on their play alone, Los Angeles would be near the top. The concern is the weak supporting cast around them.

Here’s the catch: Those role players are almost entirely Black.

If Stephen A. insists on blaming the Lakers’ championship limitations on a particular race, he’s blaming the wrong one.
