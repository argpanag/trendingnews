CSKA Sofia performance check

CSKA arrive in first place with 30 points from 12 league matches. Their record of nine wins, three draws and no defeats is the strongest in the division, and the goal difference of 29:9 highlights their effectiveness at both ends of the pitch. They have been particularly commanding at Bulgarska Armia, winning all six home league games without dropping a point.

The hosts’ latest result was a 2-0 home win over Ludogorez Rasgrad, a result that underlines their ability to combine defensive control with enough attacking threat to decide important matches. Across their last five games in all competitions, CSKA have recorded three wins, one draw and one defeat. That sequence is not flawless, but their broader league consistency remains impressive. Coach Markus Gisdol will want his side to set the tempo early and use their home advantage to put Slavia under pressure.

Slavia Sofia’s current form

Slavia Sofia sit eighth with 12 points, having won twice, drawn six times and lost four of their 12 league fixtures. Their goal record is unusually balanced at 9:9: they have conceded at a respectable rate, but have also struggled to produce goals consistently. Six draws suggest they can be difficult to put away, though that tendency has also limited their ability to turn competitive performances into victories.

Away from home, Slavia have two wins, two draws and two defeats. Their latest match was a 0-0 draw away to Lokomotiv Plovdiv, extending a recent run in which results have been mixed. Over their last five games across all competitions, they have claimed one win, drawn twice and lost twice. Coach Ratko Dostanić will likely prioritise defensive organisation against the league leaders, while seeking opportunities to threaten on the counter. Slavia’s cautious approach could keep the score close, but their modest scoring return makes an upset a demanding prospect.

The last direct duels

The most recent meeting between CSKA and Slavia ended 0-0, both in all competitions and in the Parva Liga. That result will give the visitors some encouragement, particularly if they can again restrict CSKA’s space and keep the match tight. However, the broader record across the last five meetings is close rather than decisive: CSKA have won once, two games have been drawn, and Slavia have won twice.

Those head-to-head figures advise against treating this as an automatic home victory. Still, past meetings need to be weighed against the current league picture. CSKA are unbeaten and have a perfect home record this season, whereas Slavia have collected only 12 points and have scored nine league goals. The latest stalemate is relevant, but the hosts’ present form gives them a stronger case.

Match conclusion and prediction

CSKA Sofia have the clearest advantages going into this Matchday 14 fixture: they lead the table, have yet to lose in the league, and have won every home match so far. Their 29 goals scored also compare favourably with Slavia’s total of nine, while CSKA’s defence has conceded only nine times. Slavia’s away record is respectable enough to make them competitive, and their recent 0-0 draw against CSKA shows they may be capable of frustrating the hosts. Even so, the overall evidence points to the home side having more quality and momentum.

Our primary prediction is a CSKA Sofia win, with the hosts favoured to make their home advantage count.
