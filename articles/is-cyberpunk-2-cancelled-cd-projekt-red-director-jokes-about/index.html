Is Cyberpunk 2 cancelled? CD Projekt Red director jokes about scrapping sequel after GTA 6, but there's a bigger story

TheTools · 24.11.2026 05:16 · source · permalink

    Igor Sarzyński, the imaginative mind behind CD Projekt Red, offered a lighthearted comment regarding Cyberpunk 2. After experiencing the robust world of Grand Theft Auto VI, he humorously suggested that the project felt like it was cancelled. Nevertheless, he assured fans that Cyberpunk 2 is very much alive, having moved past ideation and into pre-production.
