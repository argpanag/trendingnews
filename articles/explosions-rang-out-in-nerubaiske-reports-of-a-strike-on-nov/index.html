Explosions rang out in Nerubaiske: reports of a strike on Nova Poshta warehouses

TheTools · 13.09.2026 00:46 · source · permalink

In the village of Nerubaiske, Odesa region, a strike on the warehouse facilities of “Nova Poshta” is reported. According to preliminary data, 2 people were injured as a result of the incident.

There is currently no official confirmation of this information.

Details regarding the circumstances of the strike and the condition of the injured are being clarified.
