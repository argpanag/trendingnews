EasyJet flight makes emergency landing at Bristol Airport

TheTools · 18.11.2026 12:47 · source · permalink

Flight EZY213 made an emergency landing at Bristol Airport after it was struck by a bird while taking off

  • Published
    1 hour ago

A passenger plane was forced to make an emergency landing after it was struck by a bird.

Flight EZY213 departed Bristol Airport for Edinburgh at approximately 09:24 GMT, but returned to the airport shortly after 10:00 due to suffering a bird strike during take-off.

A spokesperson for EasyJet said nobody was hurt in the incident and the plane landed back in Bristol safely, adding the aircraft was met by the emergency services as a precaution in line with standard procedures.

The spokesperson also said: "While this was outside of our control, we would like to apologise for the inconvenience caused and we have arranged for a replacement aircraft to continue the flight to Edinburgh this morning."
