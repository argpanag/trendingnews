Premada Oorali Box Office Colections: Kannada film fetches STRONG Rs 9.50 crore in 5th weekend, grosses Rs 110 crore

TheTools · 26.10.2026 07:49 · source · permalink

Premada Oorali continues to gain solid traction at the box office. The Kannada film is heading to close its entire run around Rs. 125 crore gross or so.

Premada Oorali collected Rs. 4 crore on its 5th Sunday, registering a 15 per cent spike from the previous day. The Kannada film closed its 5th weekend at Rs. 9.50 crore gross, bringing its entire theatrical cume to Rs. 110.75 crore. 

The movie is performing extremely well at the box office. Based on current trends, it is heading towards a finish around the Rs. 125 crore mark. That will put it among the top grossers of Kannada cinema like Kantara, Kantara Chapter, and KGF Chapter 2.

Premada Oorali joins the list of movies like Laalo: Krishna Sada Sahayaate and Hanuman Ansh, which remained silent for the initial few days and then exploded at the box office. What makes the Kannada film different from the other two is its genre. It is a regular romantic comedy drama, while the other two have devotional themes. It will be interesting to see how far the movie can go at the box office.
