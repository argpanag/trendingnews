What to know about the Sunnyside Yards project and its signficance

Mayor Zohran Mamdani has previously pitched President Donald Trump on a massive housing project that would require billions of dollars in federal funding.

The massive development plan would create thousands of housing units atop the Sunnyside Yard in Queens — a 180 acre railyard where the city has long sought to build housing.

During Mamdani’s last meeting with Trump in February, he presented Trump with a mock version of a Daily News front page with his photo and a headline reading “Trump to City: Let’s Build.” The prop was a riff on a famous Daily News cover from the 1970’s which read “Ford to City: Drop Dead” after President Gerald Ford denied a federal bailout for New York City, which was on the brink of bankruptcy at the time.

The exchange represents what Mamdani’s circle refers to as “speaking Trump,” appealing to the president’s love of buildings and his identity as a developer in the hopes of enticing him to commit federal funding for what could be the most significant federal housing project in generations

Mamdani meeting is an “opportunity moment” for Trump, former New York mayor says

Former New York City Mayor Bill de Blasio told CNN that today’s meeting with Mayor Zohran Mamdani gives President Donald Trump an opportunity to demonstrate that he understands Americans’ concerns about affordability.

De Blasio spoke of Mamdani’s pitch to Trump earlier this year about affordable housing.

He added that addressing topics like affordable housing “would be the kind of thing you’d like to see Republicans do more of, not just as a matter of governance, but if they … want to win elections, they better start showing they understand the affordability issue.”

“So, there’s an opportunity moment here for Trump. Not sure he’s going to take it, but it’s there for him,” de Blasio said.

A US president hasn't visited the NYC mayor's home since the '80s

Presidential visits to New York City’s mayoral residence are rare. A sitting president has not made an official visit to the mansion since the 1980’s.

Presidential records show President Ronald Reagan visited Gracie Mansion and presented then-Mayor Ed Koch with a prop check as a sign of the Reagan administration’s commitment to an infrastructure project in Manhattan.

President John F. Kennedy also visited Gracie Mansion in 1962 to deliver a speech on medical care before a group of US mayors. President Jimmy Carter and first lady Rosalynn Carter were overnight guests at the mansion in 1978. Carter made the visit during an official trip to New York City and a bill-signing ceremony at City Hall alongside Koch.

Trump and Mamdani have met twice before. Here’s what they discussed

Despite the negative rhetoric, the president said he was “very comfortable” with the mayor running New York City following their first Oval Office meeting.

In his meetings with Trump, Mamdani has also taken a more diplomatic approach, saying his primary focus is New York City’s needs.

Immigration likely to come up during meeting between Trump and Mamdani

While aides for the mayor declined to share details about what President Donald Trump and New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani plan to discuss, immigration is likely to be a key topic.

Although New York City has been spared from the large-scale immigration enforcement that Trump threatened during Mamdani’s campaign, Trump’s immigration and deportation agenda has severely impacted the city’s large immigrant population.

Last week, Mamdani joined New York State Attorney General Letitia James in a lawsuit against the Trump administration’s changes to the “public charge” rule, which gives immigration officers wide discretion to deny green cards and visas to applicants they deem likely to become dependent on government assistance like food stamps and housing vouchers.

Mamdani warned the changes will likely shift costs to the city and put thousands of people at risk of losing crucial health and nutrition benefits.

What to know about Gracie Mansion, the New York City mayor's official residence

Nestled in a green park with a riverside garden, just a street away from the bustling Upper East Side, Gracie Mansion has been New York City’s mayoral residence since 1942.

It is also one of the oldest wooden structures in Manhattan, according to the Gracie Mansion Conservancy, and is part of the city’s Historic House Trust.

With its yellow siding, green shutters and white wrap-around porch, the house was originally built by a merchant in 1799, according to the Historic House Trust. It changed hands several times and had several uses, before Fiorello H. La Guardia and his family moved in after it was officially designated as the house of the mayor.

Now, Mayor Zohran Mamdani and his wife, Rama Duwaji, are living at the property where the mayor will host President Donald Trump today. They moved from Queens to the mansion in January.

Mamdani said at the time that he was looking to make a few updates to the historic property, including installing some bidets and getting a cat, CNN affiliate Spectrum News 1 New York reported.

He said he hadn’t yet “heard from the ghosts” that are rumored to also take up residence in Gracie Mansion, the affiliate reported.
