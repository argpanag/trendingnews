Croatia return to Wembley six weeks after their worst-ever defeat, with Harry Kane chasing history - News - Nogomania

TheTools · 12.11.2026 23:37 · source · permalink

Harry Kane fires England past Croatia and into Nations League quarter-finals

Harry Kane ends Peter Shilton's 36-year reign as England's record appearance holder

Croatia return to Wembley on Thursday evening looking to restore their reputation after suffering the heaviest defeat in their history against England just six weeks ago. The 2026/27 UEFA Nations League resumes with three major fixtures, including Italy against France and Spain's visit to Czechia.

All three matches kick off at 20:45 CET, with qualification places, individual records and national pride on the line.

For Croatia, the return to Wembley brings contrasting memories. In 2007, Slaven Bilić guided his country to a famous 3-2 victory that ended England's hopes of qualifying for Euro 2008.

Now back in charge of the national team, Bilić faces a far more difficult challenge after England humiliated Croatia 7-0 in Rijeka on 3 October.

Harry Kane and Bukayo Saka scored twice each, while Jude Bellingham, Anthony Gordon and Morgan Rogers also found the net. England were already four goals ahead at half-time.

Croatia subsequently showed improvement in a 2-1 defeat against Spain, but their defensive problems remain a concern. Captain Luka Modrić and young defender Luka Vušković face another demanding evening, while the suspended Ivan Perišić is unavailable.

For England, the occasion has additional significance. Harry Kane is set to make his 126th international appearance, surpassing Peter Shilton to become England's most-capped men's player.

The Bayern Munich striker, already England's record goalscorer with 91 goals, is one appearance away from another historic milestone.

Thomas Tuchel's side need only a point to secure their place in the Nations League quarter-finals.

France travel to Milan defending their lead in Nations League Group A1, having collected ten points from their opening four matches. Italy sit three points behind and have an opportunity to reopen the race for first place.

Under returning coach Roberto Mancini, the Azzurri have recovered from an opening defeat against Belgium, beating Turkey twice and earning a 1-1 draw in France.

France coach Zinedine Zidane faces several selection problems. Captain Kylian Mbappé is struggling with fitness concerns, while Ousmane Dembélé and other senior players are unavailable.

As Nogomania English reported on Wednesday, Mbappé missed France's final training session before the trip to Italy.

Italy will look to Sandro Tonali, Nicolò Barella and young striker Francesco Pio Esposito to provide inspiration.

The hosts also have an unwanted San Siro record to address. After going 96 years without losing an international match in Milan, Italy have suffered three consecutive defeats in the city.

Spain travel to Prague having won all four of their Nations League matches and already secured a quarter-final place.

Luis de la Fuente's side lead Group A3 by three points ahead of England, with the two nations scheduled to meet in Sunday's final group fixture.

Czechia, meanwhile, have lost all four matches and are already out of contention for the quarter-finals. Their previous meeting with Spain ended in a 3-1 defeat.

The world champions will be without Barcelona winger Lamine Yamal, who has been dealing with a minor knee problem. Spain hope to have him available for Sunday's meeting with England.

Despite the absence of their teenage star, Spain will enter the match as strong favourites against opponents still searching for their first points of the campaign.
