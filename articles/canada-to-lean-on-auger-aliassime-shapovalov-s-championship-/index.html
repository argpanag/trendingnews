Canada to lean on Auger-Aliassime, Shapovalov's championship experience at Davis Cup Final 8 | CBC Sports

TheTools · 26.10.2026 21:30 · source · permalink

Team advanced to final stage with 3-1 win over France last month in Quebec City

World No. 7 Félix Auger-Aliassime and veteran Denis Shapovalov will lead Canada at the Davis Cup Final 8 international men's tennis tournament next month in Bologna, Italy.

Auger-Aliassime, from Montreal, and Shapovalov, from Richmond Hill, Ont., were key contributors to Canada's lone Davis Cup title in 2022.

Montreal's Gabriel Diallo, Liam Draxl of Newmarket, Ont., and Duncan Chan of Markham, Ont., were also named to Canada's roster Monday for the event, which runs Nov. 24-29.

Canada advanced to the final stage with a 3-1 win over France last month in Quebec City. Shapovalov was not on Canada's roster for that tie, but Auger-Aliassime picked up two singles wins while Draxl opened the competition with an upset of then-No. 32 Arthur Rinderknech.

Canada, ranked eighth, will face fifth-ranked Czechia in the quarterfinals on Nov. 24, with the winner advancing to face either Italy or South Korea in the semifinals.

The tie in Bologna will mark the first Davis Cup meeting between Canada and Czechia, a three-time Davis Cup champion.

Alcaraz and Zverev lead Spain, Germany

Carlos Alcaraz was in Spain's team for the Davis Cup Final 8, meaning he's slated to play in the competition for the first time in two years.

Alexander Zverev, who is guaranteed to take over the No. 1 ranking before the tournament, was named to Germany's team in Monday's squad announcements.

Three-time defending champion Italy, which will be without the injured Jannik Sinner, will be led by French Open runner-up Flavio Cobolli.

Alcaraz hasn't played in the Davis Cup since the 2024 Final 8.

After becoming the youngest man to complete a career Grand Slam by adding an Australian Open title to his resume at the start of this year, the Davis Cup is one of the few big titles missing from Alcaraz's career.

Spain features two other top-15 players, with No. 11 Rafael Jodar and No. 15 Alejandro Davidovich Fokina also selected by captain David Ferrer.

Spain opens against Austria in the quarterfinals.

Zverev's Germany opens against Britain, which is led by Wimbledon semifinalist Arthur Fery.

Davis Cup Final 8 rosters

Czechia: Jakub Mensik, Jiri Lehecka, Patrick Rikl, Adam Pavlasek; Tomas Berdych (captain)

Canada: Felix Auger-Aliassime, Denis Shapovalov, Gabriel Diallo, Liam Draxl, Duncan Chan; Frank Dancevic (captain)

Italy: Flavio Cobolli, Luciano Darderi, Lorenzo Musetti, Andrea Vavassori, Simone Bolelli; Filippo Volandri (captain)

South Korea: Kwon Soonwoo, Chung Hyeon, Park Uisung, Nam Jisung, Hong Seongchan; Chung Jong-sam (captain)

Britain: Arthur Fery, Cameron Norrie, Henry Patten, Neal Skupski; Leon Smith (captain)

Germany: Alexander Zverev, Jan-Lennard Struff, Kevin Krawietz, Tim Puetz; Michael Kohlmann (captain)

Austria: Jurij Rodionov, Lukas Neumayer, Joel Schwaerzler, Neil Oberleitner, Lucas Miedler, Jurgen Melzer (captain)
