FIBA World Cup Qualifiers Resume Across Americas and Asia

TheTools · 24.11.2026 20:37 · source · permalink

National basketball teams across the Americas and Asia will resume their campaigns for the 2027 FIBA Basketball World Cup when the fifth qualifying window begins November 26. The upcoming games will determine crucial standings as countries compete for seven available places in Qatar, with Canada, the United States and Australia entering the latest round among the leading teams.

According to DAZN's November 24 tournament previews, Window 5 features 12 games in each qualifying region. All matches are scheduled to be available live or on demand through BasketballTV, formerly known as Courtside 1891.

In the Americas, Canada leads Group F with an unbeaten 8-0 record and 16 points. The United States tops Group E with seven victories, one defeat and 15 points. Canada needs one additional victory to secure qualification, according to DAZN.

The qualification system carries points accumulated during the first three windows into the remaining rounds. The three highest-ranked teams from each group, together with the best fourth-placed team, will advance to the tournament.

Group E features the United States, Dominican Republic, Brazil, Mexico, Colombia and Chile. Group F consists of Canada, Uruguay, Argentina, Bahamas, Puerto Rico and Panama.

The Americas schedule opens November 26 with Uruguay facing Canada, while Argentina meets Bahamas and Panama takes on Puerto Rico. The United States begins its Window 5 schedule against Brazil on November 27, followed by a meeting with Colombia on December 1.

Asia also enters a decisive stage, with Australia leading Group E after winning all eight of its games. New Zealand follows with six victories and two defeats, while Iraq and Jordan each have five wins.

Japan and Lebanon share the top of Group F with identical 6-2 records and 14 points. China occupies third place with 13 points, followed by Korea and host nation Qatar, both with 12 points.

Qatar has already secured automatic qualification as the tournament host. However, its results remain part of the qualifying competition even though the country will be excluded from the final group standings. The remaining teams are competing for the seven available Asian qualification positions.

Asian Group E includes Australia, New Zealand, Iraq, Jordan, Philippines and Syria. Group F features Japan, Lebanon, China, Korea, Qatar and Saudi Arabia.

The Asian schedule begins November 26 with Saudi Arabia playing China, Qatar facing Korea and Lebanon meeting Japan. On November 27, Jordan takes on New Zealand, Iran faces Australia and Syria plays the Philippines. Additional Group E fixtures on November 29 include Syria against New Zealand, Iraq against the Philippines and Jordan against Australia.
