DEA Meets With Late ‘Nashville’ Star’s Boyfriend

TheTools · 11.09.2026 13:47 · source · permalink

There has been a major update in the death of Hayden Panettiere

There has been a major update in the death of Hayden Panettiere. According to a Thursday (September 10) report from TMZ, the Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) has spoken to her on-again, off-again boyfriend Brian Hickerson.

Videos by Wide Open Country

DEA Speaks To Hayden's Boyfriend Amid Investigation 

The cause of death has yet to be confirmed. However, Investigators do believe that drugs played a role in her untimely death. One source told the outlet that the DEA is "zeroing in on a potential drug dealer."

However, no arrests have been made in connection with the actress's death.

Panettiere was found unresponsive in a Greenville, South Carolina apartment complex back in August.

No cause of death for the actress has been confirmed. However, in a 911 call obtained by TMZ, the Emergency Medical Services dispatch team responds to an "unresponsive female." Who is suffering from an apparent "cardiac arrest." Another worker confirmed that the female had suffered from an overdose. And that they were administering CPR in an attempt to revive her, which was unsuccessful.

It was later discovered that it was Hickerson's brother who found the actress unresponsive. They tried to give her Narcan to revive her. But the attempts were unsuccessful.

Star's Mother Gets Honest About Her Boyfriend

When reacting to the news that Brian was with her daughter at the time of her death, Panettiere's estranged mother, Lesley Vogel, referred to him as a "person in her life that we have been trying to get rid of for quite some time."

"There is a lot of history in the past that Brian has enabled Hayden many times. And this is why her father and I had been concerned for a long time about him," Vogel said in a statement to NBC News back in August.

Additionally, a rep for the actress said that there is "more to the story," referring to the details surrounding Hayden's death.

"There is much more to this story than what is currently being discussed publicly. And those closest to Hayden are actively cooperating with the ongoing investigation. To help ensure that the truth comes to light. And that justice is served," the rep said to US Weekly back in August.
