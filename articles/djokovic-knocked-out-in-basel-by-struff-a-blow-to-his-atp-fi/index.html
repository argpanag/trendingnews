Djokovic Knocked Out in Basel by Struff, a Blow to His ATP Finals Hopes

TheTools · 29.10.2026 21:09 · source · permalink

Back in Basel fifteen years after his last appearance, the Serb was stunned by Jan-Lennard Struff after a match lasting more than two and a half hours. A defeat that also complicates his ATP Finals race.

Novak Djokovic will not play the quarterfinals in Basel.

Returning to the Swiss tournament fifteen years after his last appearance, the 39-year-old Serb was eliminated in the second round by Jan-Lennard Struff after a battle lasting more than two and a half hours (6-7, 7-5, 7-6).

Struff ends his losing streak against Djokovic

Already pushed to the limit the day before by Ugo Humbert, Djokovic this time faced another dangerous opponent indoors.

At 36 years old, Struff was coming off a final in Brussels played just last Sunday. The German therefore had strong credentials to trouble the Serb.

Until now, however, he had never managed to beat Djokovic in eight meetings on tour. This time, he turned the tide thanks to 66 winners, 27 aces, and great resilience in the baseline rallies.

Djokovic lets his chance slip away

After winning the first set in a tiebreak, the Serb had several opportunities to gain the upper hand in the second set. However, he let four break points slip away, before Struff converted his first opportunity to level the match at one set apiece.

Everything was ultimately decided in another tiebreak, which the German handled better. Djokovic did well to fend off two match points with two big serves, but Struff eventually closed it out with an ace.

A blow to his ATP Finals hopes

This defeat is a bad result for Djokovic in the race to the ATP Finals. Still 11th in the Race, he remains behind Rafael Jodar and Alex de Minaur and will have to try to make up ground at the Paris Masters 1000 next week.

The Serb will have a new opportunity there to move closer to a qualification for the Turin event, in a tournament he has won seven times.

For his part, Struff reaches his second quarterfinal in less than a week. He will now face Nuno Borges as he looks to extend his run in Basel.
