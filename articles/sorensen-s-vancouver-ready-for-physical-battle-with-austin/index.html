The Vancouver Whitecaps will be looking to extend their lead at the top of MLS' Western Conference when they host Austin FC.

Jesper Sorensen insisted his Vancouver Whitecaps side are ready to face a "very aggressive" and "physically strong" Austin FC side on Sunday.

The Whitecaps are top of the Western Conference heading into this clash at BC Place, three points ahead of the Houston Dynamo with two games in hand.

They trail Nashville SC by eight points at the top of the Supporters' Shield race, having also played two fewer games than B.J. Callaghan's men.

After a poor performance against St. Louis City, Vancouver bounced back with a 3-0 win over the LA Galaxy last time out, with Brian White, Ryan Gauld and Bruno Caicedo getting on the scoresheet.

Austin, meanwhile, are on a five-game unbeaten run in MLS (W2 D3), but have drawn each of their last two games.

A 1-1 draw with the Colorado Rapids on Wednesday saw Austin remain 14th in the West, but they are just four points off the playoff places ahead of their trip to Canada.

And Sorensen anticipates a physical encounter for his Whitecaps side against an Austin outfit beaming with confidence.

"They have been unbeaten and been on quite a run," said Jesper Sorensen.

"It's a team that has some good players and also have changed a little bit in the way they play.

"I think it's a team that's very aggressive and wants to get up and press man-to-man. We know that we're going to face a team that's physically strong and also is quite capable of being dangerous against you.

"We have to be ready and on the front foot when we play here."

PLAYERS TO WATCH

Vancouver Whitecaps – Brian White

With a goal on Wednesday, White has now scored 14 regular-season goals in four consecutive seasons.

White is the fourth player in MLS history to have 14+ goals in four straight regular seasons, after Bradley Wright-Phillips (five), David Villa and Robbie Keane.

Austin FC – Facundo Torres

Torres scored Austin's goal in their 1-1 draw with Colorado, marking his third goal in his last five regular-season games.

Torres had managed to score only one goal in his first 19 regular-season matches for Austin.

MATCH PREDICTION: VANCOUVER WHITECAPS WIN

Vancouver have lost only one of 10 all-time meetings with Austin (W6 D3), though Austin did manage a goalless draw at home in the last meeting in May 2025.

The Whitecaps have won four of their five home matches against the Verde, including a 5–1 win in the last meeting between the sides at BC Place in 2025.

Vancouver’s 3–0 win over LA Galaxy was their 12th time scoring three or more goals in an MLS match this season. That is the most by any MLS team in 2026 and the most by Vancouver in a single MLS campaign, besting their 11 such games in 2025.

Austin have won each of their last two regular-season away matches, with both wins coming in Cascadia over the Seattle Sounders and Portland Timbers. Austin have posted a record of two wins, four draws and nine losses in their first 15 all-time visits to Cascadia.
