Laneway Festival is returning in 2027 with a stacked lineup of international and local talent set to take over stages across Australia and New Zealand next February.

The festival kicks off at Sydney’s Centennial Park on 12 February, before heading to the Brisbane Showgrounds on 13 February, Flemington Park in Melbourne on 19 February, and Western Springs in Auckland on 21 February.

Leading the charge is acclaimed Irish post-punk outfit Fontaines D.C., is prime position with The Hayley Williams Show, as Australian fans rejoice over the fact that they can still see the Ego Death At A Bachelorette Party superstar and frontwoman of Paramore, with her two intimate headline shows in Melbourne and Sydney selling out.

12 February – Centennial Park, Sydney

13 February – Brisbane Showgrounds Brisbane

19 February – Flemington Park, Melbourne

21 February – Western Springs, Auckland

Adding to the two superstars appeal is Slayyyter, global icon Adela, Kim Petras, Australia’s electronic darling Ninajirachi, Balu Brigada, DIIV, the viral sensation Horsegiirl, Jane Remover, MUNA, Rachel Chinouriri and Rico Nasty.

The lineup also features a substantial crop of emerging and established artists, including 1TBSP, Automatic, Bleech 9:3, Daine, Danny L Harle, DJ Dave, Ecca Vandal, Florence Road, Gurriers, Rommulas, Tiffancy Day, Violet Grohl, GREATSOUTH, O & The Mo, Office Dog, Public Figures and Swapmeet Trials.

With a mix of indie, punk, electronic, pop, hip-hop and experimental sounds across the bill, Laneway 2027 looks set to continue the festival’s tradition of spotlighting some of the most exciting names in contemporary music alongside major international acts.

The Laneway Festival pre-sale, presented by Afterpay is the online chance to buy tickets at 1st release price. Presale starts Wednesday 7 October.
