Kings of Leon are heading back to Australia, with the Nashville rock icons announcing a three-date tour that will bring their formidable catalogue and new material to stages across the country in early 2027.

The Followill family will kick off the run at Sydney’s Centennial Park on 28 February before travelling to Brisbane Showgrounds on 3 March and Melbourne’s Flemington Racecourse on 5 March. The Melbourne performance will be the band’s only Victorian show, with Australian psychedelic rock favourites Ocean Alley joining the lineup at every date.

28 February 2027 – Centennial Park, Sydney

3 March 2027 – Brisbane Showgrounds, Brisbane

5 March 2027 – Flemington Racecourse, Melbourne

Special guests: Ocean Alley

The tour arrives on the eve of a new chapter for Kings of Leon, with their 10th studio album, O My Beloved, scheduled for release on 6 November 2026. The record follows 2024’s Can We Please Have Fun and signals the next instalment in a career defined by an evolving sonic palette, Southern rock sensibilities and arena-ready anthems.

The band remains anchored by its original lineup; brothers Caleb, Nathan and Jared Followill, alongside their cousin Matthew. Since emerging from Nashville in 1999, Kings of Leon have transformed from Southern rock outsiders into one of the most recognisable acts of their generation, cultivating an expansive international following through their distinctive blend of gritty guitars, brooding melodies and widescreen choruses.

Their breakthrough gathered momentum with debut album Youth & Young Manhood in 2003 backed by the shimmy-shaking Aha Shake Heartbreak and it’s ever dazzling ‘Taper Jean Girl’, ‘Razz’, ‘Four Kicks’, and of course ‘The Bucket’, which first captured significant attention in the UK. Because of the Times took off in Australia with tracks like ‘On Call’ and ‘Knocked Up’ and ‘Charmer’ dominating the airwaves. It was 2008’s Only by the Night, however, that propelled KOL into the global mainstream. Featuring enduring hits ‘Sex on Fire’ and ‘Use Somebody’, along with fan favourites ‘Manhattan’ and ‘Revelry’, the album became a defining release of the era, with the latter earning the band the GRAMMY Award for Record of the Year in 2010. Their commercial ascent continued with Walls, which delivered their first US number-one album in 2016.

Joining them across the Australian dates, Ocean Alley bring their own formidable live reputation. The Northern Beaches outfit claimed the Triple J Hottest 100 crown in 2018 with ‘Confidence’, cementing their place among Australia’s most prominent contemporary rock exports. Their sun-soaked psychedelia and expansive guitar work make them a compelling addition to a tour built around two generations of modern rock success.

With a fresh album on the horizon and a catalogue stacked with internationally recognised hits, Kings of Leon’s Australian return will be gigantic – three major outdoor shows for fans eager to experience the band’s unmistakable sound live.

Fans can access the artist pre-sale by pre-ordering O My Beloved through Artist First by 4pm AEDT on 21 October. The artist pre-sale opens at 12pm local time on 22 October, alongside dedicated pre-sales for eligible Mastercard and Vodafone customers. Live Nation’s pre-sale begins at 12pm local time on 23 October, with registration required. General tickets go on sale at 1pm local time on 27 October.

All ticketing times are local to each venue. Queensland does not observe daylight saving, meaning the Brisbane pre-sale begins at noon in Brisbane and 1pm in Melbourne.

For registration and further details, visit Live Nation.
