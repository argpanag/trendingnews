Canadian rock heavyweights Nickelback are heading to Geelong’s Mount Duneed Estate on Saturday 9 October 2027, bringing their massive Everything Under The Sun Tour to the region as part of Lookout Festival, presented by MG Live and Lookout Festival, with special guests Don Broco and more acts to be announced.

The Geelong show forms part of Nickelback’s huge 2027 global arena tour, celebrating the release of their highly anticipated eleventh studio album, Everything Under The Sun, out 30 October via Virgin Music Group. The Australian leg kicks off in Perth on 2 October before travelling to Adelaide, Melbourne, Geelong, Brisbane and Sydney, wrapping up in Hunter Valley on 16 October.

Presented by Live Nation and Triple M. The Geelong show is presented by MG Live and Lookout Festival.

Keep connected to your live music scene here.

With nearly 65 million albums sold worldwide, billions of streams and a catalogue packed with generation-defining rock anthems, Nickelback remain one of the most successful rock bands on the planet. Following the phenomenal success of 2022’s Get Rollin’ and a record-breaking world tour, the band are set to deliver another unforgettable live experience, bringing their signature arena-sized sound to one of Australia’s premier outdoor concert locations.

Joining Nickelback as direct support are Don Broco, the English four-piece renowned for their boundary-pushing blend of metal, post-hardcore and electronica. With a reputation for explosive live performances and sold-out shows at major UK venues, including Wembley Arena and Alexandra Palace, Don Broco will help kick off an epic day of rock at Mount Duneed Estate.

Making its debut at Mount Duneed Estate in 2027, Lookout Festival celebrates alternative rock, bringing together the music that has stood the test of time alongside fresh sounds from across the scene. Set against the spectacular backdrop of the Geelong region, the festival promises a unique outdoor concert experience, with further artists to be announced.

Tickets go on sale Friday 16 October at 10.00am local time from Ticketmaster with MG Live, Face To Face , Lookout Festival and Live Nation member presales beginning Thursday 15 October at 10.00am local time.

Mastercard cardholders have special access to presale tickets in Australia. Mastercard presale starts Wednesday 14 October at 10.00am local time.
