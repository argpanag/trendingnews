Zii Jia beats Chinese shuttler in Arctic Open 1st round

TheTools · 07.10.2026 12:33 · source · permalink

The world No 56 player defeats the much younger 40th ranked Dong Tian Yao in straight games.

Zii Jia, 28, who had little trouble clearing the qualifying round yesterday, defeated China’s 40th ranked Dong Tian Yao 21-18, 21-14 in 42 minutes at the Energia Areena in Vantaa, Finland.

The victory over the 23-year-old Chinese at the World Tour Super 500 event is a major boost as the former world No 2 shuttler has faced a long and difficult journey following a lengthy injury layoff.

His best performance this season was at the World Tour Super 300 Canada Open where he managed to enter the semi-finals.

In the last-16 match tomorrow, he will take on Taiwan’s Su Li Yang.
