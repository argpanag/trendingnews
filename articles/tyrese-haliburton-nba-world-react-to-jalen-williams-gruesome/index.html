Stars and fans across the NBA world offered their well wishes for OKC Thunder star Jalen Williams following his unfortunate leg injury.

Jalen Williams suffered a significant, gruesome lower-leg injury in the fourth quarter of the Oklahoma City Thunder's Sunday night 108-101 win over the LA Clippers.

Williams landed badly after a dunk attempt, causing his left foot to twist sideways at an unnatural angle. The injury was so devastating that OKC medical staff immediately covered his leg with a towel and carried him off the court on a stretcher.

Both teams were completely stunned along with every fan watching at the Paycom Center, as this 2026-27 season might be unfortunately over for the Thunder star with his gruesome injury.

Following Jalen Williams' ugly leg injury, several stars and the entire NBA world offered their well-wishes for the OKC Thunder wing.
