Fashion Sakala and Albert Kangwanda to lead Chipolopolo Boys attack - ZamFoot

TheTools · 17.11.2026 10:36 · source · permalink

……Could be joined by Kennedy Musonda 

Saudi Arabia based Fashion Sakala and YANGA SC’s Albert Kangwanda are expected to again lead the Chipolopolo Boys attack against Burundi this afternoon. 

Sakala is the second highest top goalscorer for Chipolopolo Boys among active players behind Patson Daka who is out injured. Despite not having a good game last Friday, Lwandamina is expected to keep faith in his captain. 

The ‘Showman’ has 17 goal contributions (12 goals and 5 assists) in 41 appearances since making his senior debut back in 2017. 

With in-form Kingston Mutandwa and Daka both out, Mafasho will provide the experience and the explosive outlet for the George ‘Chicken’ Lwandamina coached side. 

 Kangwanda has also been in fine form and scored a brilliant volley in the win over Togo. He also gave Burundi a tough time at Levy Mwanawasa Stadium and also set up Edward Chilufya for the goal. 

ZESCO United striker Kennedy Musonda has recovered from the knock that kept him out of Friday’s match and could start this afternoon. 

Lwandamina could however keep faith in Israeli based Edward Chilufya who netted the goal on Friday. 

Portuguese based Chanka Zimba could also start upfront should Lwandamina need a target man. Derrick Bulaya of Green Eagles who started at Levy Mwanawasa Stadium and Kelvin Mwanza of FC MUZA are also available for selection. 

Zambia face Burundi this afternoon in a crucial Group I qualifier at Stade Intwari, with kick-off scheduled for 15:00 CAT.

The Chipolopolo head into the encounter knowing that a positive result would strengthen their qualification hopes, with Algeria currently leading the group on seven points.

Zambia sit second on four points, ahead of Togo on three, while Burundi are bottom with two points.

The top two teams in each qualifying group will book their places at the 2027 AFCON, which will be jointly hosted by Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda.
