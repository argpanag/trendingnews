Simeone on Sorloth: "His future is for him to see"

TheTools · 24.11.2026 14:09 · source · permalink

Just 170 minutes and no goals in seven official games this season, ahead of the Champions League clash with Viking on Wednesday 25 November.

By TuFutbolero Newsroom·November 24, 2026 at 09:09 AM

Diego Pablo Simeone has cast doubt on the future of Alexander Sorloth at Atlético de Madrid. According to Mundo Deportivo, the Argentine coach left the decision in the striker's hands: "I can't see his future, he has to see it himself". Simeone considers him an important player with no equivalent in the squad, even after the arrival of Jonathan David.

The Norwegian has lost influence: he has only 170 minutes across seven official games, five in La Liga and two in the Champions League, without a goal or an assist. His only start came on 3 November against Bayern Munich. The contrast is huge with his first season at the club, when he scored 24 goals, twelve of them coming off the bench.

The coach was clear about what he can offer him: "I don't mind if he plays 20 minutes or 70", though there are no promises of a starting place.

Sorloth, 30, is also a factor. Juventus showed interest last summer, but the deal fell through due to uncertainty over Julián Álvarez's future.
