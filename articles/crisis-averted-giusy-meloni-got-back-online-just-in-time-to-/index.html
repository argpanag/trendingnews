I know people got sick of Christian Pulisic because of what happened at the World Cup. I'll be brave enough to say it. Christian Pulisic can still be good at soccer. He doesn't have to be the face of the US Soccer team anymore. We have guys like Balogun and Sullivan and this young group coming up that can take some pressure off of him. He doesn't have to do it all. He can just play and be effective and that's massive for the US team. 

So him staying at AC Milan is big news. It was the club that sort of saved him (and vice versa) after the ending at Chelsea. Then he got hurt, stunk for a little, but figured it out again under Ruben Amorim. If Pulisic can help us at the 2030 World Cup, then him being at AC Milan until 2031 is important to me. 

At the same time, I was wondering what Milan people thought about it. And having the blogger memory, I went to Giusy Meloni, who also burst onto our stage because of AC Milan 

When I think AC Milan I think San Siro, the Ruud Guilt era squad, Paolo Maldini, Kaka, Christian Pulisic and Giusy Meloni. Not necessarily in that order either. So naturally, like any blogger would, I went to her Instagram to see if she had an update on the news. That's when I realized she caught the break of a lifetime. She posted nearly at the same time:
