A$AP Rocky is coming to New Zealand

TheTools · 13.10.2026 00:25 · source · permalink

RNZ / Life / Music

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/life/music/ausdap-rocky-is-coming-to-new-zealand

The rapper will play at Auckland’s Spark Arena on 12 December.

Rapper A$AP Rocky has announced one New Zealand show as part of his Don’t Be Dumb World Tour.

The musician turned fashion icon will make his first return to NZ in eight years, playing Auckland’s Spark Arena on 12 December.

A$AP Rocky dropped his fourth album Don’t Be Dumb at the start of this year.

The album featured cover art by filmmaker and illustrator Tim Burton and included tracks with BossMan Dlow, Brent Faiyaz, Danny Elfman, Doechii, Gorillaz, Jessica Pratt, Jon Batiste, Slay Squad, Thundercat, Tyler, The Creator, Westside Gunn and will.i.am.

Rocky co-chaired the 2025 Met Gala with the theme of Superfine: Tailoring Black Style.

He last played in New Zealand at Auckland’s Listen In Festival in 2018. Tickets to this year’s show go on sale 1pm on 16 October.
