Former Belgium striker Toni Brogno dies aged 53 after liver cancer

Former Belgium international and ex-Sedan forward Toni Brogno has died aged 53 after liver cancer. His daughter Olivia confirmed the news in a Facebook post.

Belgium’s national team later offered condolences to his family and friends. Brogno won seven caps for his country as a striker.

OneFootball Videos

His club career took him to Charleroi, Westerlo and Louvain. He also spent two seasons in France with Sedan from 2000 to 2002, making 32 appearances and scoring 10 goals. Sedan marked his passing on social media.

After retiring from football, he worked as a salesperson at Decathlon.

His brother, coach Dante Brogno, put his career on hold to support him during his illness.
