Cincinnati Bengals-Atlanta Falcons: the NFL returns to the Bernabéu

Our stadium hosts the second NFL game in Spain’s history (Sunday, 3:30 pm CEST; Cuatro and Dazn).

The NFL returns to the Bernabéu, hosting the second game in the history of this competition in Spain (3:30 pm CEST; Cuatro and Dazn). The Cincinnati Bengals and the Atlanta Falcons face off in a matchup that, like last year’s game between the Miami Dolphins and the Washington Commanders, now joins the list of major events hosted by our stadium during its nearly 79 years of history.

Fans attending the Bernabéu will enjoy an unforgettable sports event. The Atlanta Falcons and the Cincinnati Bengals come to this game, corresponding to the ninth week of the regular season, with opposite records. The Falcons have won three games and lost five, while the Bengals, having played one game fewer, boast five wins and two losses. In their last game, the Falcons were defeated by Tampa Bay (16-0). Their opponent this afternoon defeated the Tennessee Titans (43-36).

Another historic event at the Bernabéu
Four European Cup finals, the final of the 1982 World Cup and the 1964 Euro Cup, the 2018 Copa Libertadores, visits from Popes Leo XIV and John Paul II, and concerts by global stars like Taylor Swift, Bruce Springsteen, and the Rolling Stones are just some of the unforgettable events held at the Bernabéu. Today, the NFL spectacle returns to our stadium.
