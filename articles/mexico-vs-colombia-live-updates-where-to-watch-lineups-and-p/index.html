Mexico vs Colombia LIVE updates: Where to watch, lineups and predictions

TheTools · 27.09.2026 01:12 · source · permalink

BALTIMORE — Mexico hosts Colombia in an international friendly at M&T Bank Stadium in Baltimore on Saturday night, the beginning of a new era for El Tri with Rafa Márquez makes his managerial debut.

El Tri fell 3-2 to England in the round of 16 at the 2026 World Cup on home soil and enters the September game ranked 10th in the FIFA rankings.

Colombia arrives after a penalty-shootout loss to Switzerland in the round of 16 at the World Cup.

Los Cafeteros have won six of their last eight friendlies with six clean sheets and have beaten Mexico in their last four meetings but are without Bayern Munich star Luis Diaz, who was left off the roster for this window for rest and recovery.

Saturday marks the first-ever MexTour stop in Baltimore during the tour's 23-year history.

Where to watch Mexico vs. Colombia: TV channel, streaming

  • When: Saturday, September 26
  • Where: M&T Bank Stadium (Baltimore, MD)
  • Time: 9 p.m. ET
  • Channel/streaming: Univision (Watch LIVE on Fubo), TUDN USA, ViX (Watch on Prime Video)

Mexico vs Colombia underway in Baltimore

The teams have kicked off at M&T Bank Stadium on this Saturday night with a strong crowd on hand to watch the post-World Cup friendly.

Over 60,000 tickets were reportedly sold for the match, but it rained all day in the area and there are plenty of empty seats in the upper levels.

How many World Cups did Rafa Marquez play in?

Mexico manager Rafael Marquez captained Mexico in five World Cups as a player: 2002, 2006, 2010, 2014 and 2018.

Has Mexico won the World Cup?

Mexico has never won the World Cup and its best-ever finish is two appearances in the quarterfinals.
