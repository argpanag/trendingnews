Nextalia Investment – an asset management company and one of Italia’s leading alternative investment platforms – announces that it has entered into a binding agreement to acquire a majority stake in Di Bartolo, a Sicilian manufacturer of high-quality ingredients for the confectionery and ice-cream industry. The transaction consolidates Nextalia’s presence in the sector of semi-finished and finished ingredients with high added value for the industry, a market experiencing structural growth. Last January, Nextalia acquired Di Sano and DiGel, which specialise in pistachio-based products for ice-cream parlours and patisseries (their portfolio also includes ice-cream bases (Meucci brand) and candied fruit (Romeo brand). The company states that the addition of Di Bartolo brings to the platform a production capacity rooted in the Sicilian pistachio supply chain, which is complementary in terms of geography, expertise, know-how and customer base.

Almonds and hazelnuts for the confectionery industry

Founded in 1988 in Calatabiano (Catania) by Rosario Di Bartolo, the Di Bartolo company has grown significantly in recent years, exceeding 25 million euros in turnover. Its business centres on the promotion and processing of local raw materials – in particular fresh and dried fruit – to offer tailor-made ingredient solutions for professionals and industries within the food sector. Over the years, it has gradually expanded its product range, which is now centred on pistachios, accounting for around 70 per cent of sales, alongside almonds, hazelnuts and citrus extracts.

Ice cream, juices, baked goods and desserts in your wallet

Di Bartolo’s product range is organised around four key sectors of the food industry and the hospitality sector: ice cream (supply of bases, mixes and solutions – both standard and bespoke – for the industrial and artisan ice cream sectors; beverages (production of natural juices and frozen products, Sicilian citrus concentrates and essential oils); bakery (development of innovative ingredients designed to impart flavour and character to baked goods); confectionery (production of semi-finished products with intense flavour for the art of confectionery and pastry-making). Among the flagship products are items deeply rooted in Sicilian identity, such as the 100% Bronte Green Pistachio Paste PDO, the Val di Noto Almond Milk Paste and the range dedicated to the Avola Almond. The pastes, the flagship product accounting for around 60 per cent of turnover, are complemented by chopped nuts, pralines, creams and variegated products – including vegan versions – as well as citrus juices, flavourings and essential oils.

Abroad: France, Poland and Germany

The company operates primarily in the industrial sector and supplies leading Italian and international groups active in the production of ice cream, the confectionery industry and chocolate. Most sales are made in Italia, but Di Bartolo’s products also reach international markets, both indirectly, through its customers, and directly, particularly in France, Poland and Germany.
