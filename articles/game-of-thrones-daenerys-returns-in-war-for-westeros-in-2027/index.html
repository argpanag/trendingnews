Game of Thrones' Daenerys Returns in War for Westeros in 2027

TheTools · 15.10.2026 23:00 · source · permalink

Game of Thrones' Daenerys Targaryen is set for a return to the franchise when Game of Thrones: War for Westeros releases in 2027. It's an exciting return for one of the most popular characters in the franchise, and the upcoming game is filled to the brim with the most notable names in Westeros.

The real-time strategy game will include characters like Jon Snow, Jaime Lannister, and more fan favorites. It's an exciting time to be a Game of Thrones fan, as even if the main series is over, there's more of Westeros to explore. There could be more characters coming to the game as well, but the Mother of Dragons might be the best one of them all.

The Mother of Dragons Is Coming Back to the Franchise

Game of Thrones: War for Westeros is an upcoming RTS developed and published by PlaySide Studios, the same publishers behind Mouse: P.I. for Hire and Sid Meier's Civilization VII VR. Mouse: P.I. for Hire is a refreshing, hilarious take on the private investigator noir genre, and there isn't a turn-based strategy game fan who hasn't played one of the Civilization series games.

A real-time strategy game is an interesting choice for the Game of Thrones franchise, and it feels like a great idea. Game of Thrones is one of the greatest television series of all time, and it's a world almost overflowing with lore. Fans can only hope that the game will expand on some of the untouched pieces of the story that the series didn't cover, and Daenerys' inclusion might be part of that.

Game of Thrones: War for Westeros Comes Out in 2027

Game of Thrones: Westeros will allow players to take control of the major houses in the series, including House Stark, House Lannister, and House Targaryen. Players can also take control of the Night King himself, using a litany of undead monsters and men to take down their enemies with brutal precision.

The game will include both a campaign mode players can enjoy and a skirmish mode that will see players get straight into battle. Game of Thrones: War for Westeros doesn't have a definitive release date yet, but PlaySide Studios has announced that it will be released in early 2027, meaning players won't have to wait much longer before diving back into Westeros.
