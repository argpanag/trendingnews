Opposition leads Netanyahu camp in Israel vote, Arab parties up: Exit polls

TheTools · 27.10.2026 23:42 · source · permalink

High turnout in vote widely described as the most consequential in Israel’s history.

Israelis begin voting as Netanyahu fights for political survival

  • Exit polls show no outright winner in Israel’s election but suggest the main Jewish-led opposition alliance is slightly ahead of Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s coalition, though neither side can secure a majority.
  • According to the projections, the opposition bloc would win 52-53 seats in the 120-seat Knesset, while the Netanyahu camp would get 50 seats. Arab parties are projected to win 17-18 seats collectively, improving on their performance four years ago.
