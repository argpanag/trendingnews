Round-up: Weekend's Senior Club Camogie championship action

TheTools · 13.10.2026 08:58 · source · permalink

Sorcha McCartan of Cork champions St Finbarr’s. Photo by Seb Daly/Sportsfile

There were no shortage of county camogie finals down for decision over the weekend, as the AIB Provincial Championships begin to take shape.

St Finbarr’s made it three-in-a-row in Cork, with a dominant 3-14 to 0-4 win over Blackrock in Sunday’s final. Sorcha McCartan led the charge for the Barrs, with 1-9, as they saw off their southside rivals to capture the title. They will now look ahead to a Munster opener against Truagh-Clonlara of Clare.

It will be Thurles Sarsfields vs Clonoulty Rossmore in the 2026 FBD Insurance Tipperary Senior Championship final. Sars were too strong for Drom-Inch, prevailing 5-12 to 1-13. Clonoulty Rossmore defeated Éire Óg Annacarty, 1-16 to 0-14.

2025 Munster finalists De La Salle fell in the Clannad Care Waterford Senior Championship finale, as Gailltír returned to the Déise summit, winning 0-15 to 0-12.

With Loughgiel Shamrocks out of the picture, after they were knocked out in their quest for a 13th consecutive IN2 Engineering Antrim Senior Championship crown, opportunity knocked for Dunloy Cúchullain and Ruairí Óg Cushendall in Sunday’s final. It was Dunloy who triumphed 0-16 to 0-15, ending a 23-year wait, as Eobha McAllister continued her dream year with a late winner.

St Martin’s were impressive winners in Clearwater Construction Wexford Senior Championship, winning 1-17 to 0-9 against Glynn-Barntown, and now look ahead to the Leinster Championship.

St Vincent’s and Naomh Jude will contest the PTSB Dublin Senior Championship final, after semi-final wins over Kilmacud Crokes and Ballyboden St Enda’s on Saturday.

Shinrone are HQPhysio Offaly Senior Championship winners, after a 2-13 to 1-11 win over St Cillian’s.

Mount Leinster Rangers overcame Naomh Bríd, 2-10 to 1-8 in the AZAP Print Carlow Senior Championship on Saturday.

Clonkill defended their title in the David Smyth Catering Westmeath Senior Championship, seeing off Raharney.

Naas were another team to retain their county title, as they saw off Celbridge in a tight Cúl Sliotars Kildare Senior Championship final.

In the Bective Stud Meath Senior Championship semi-finals, Na Fianna and Kilmessan progressed, with victories over Ratoath and Killyon respectively.

Cillard made it six-in-a-row in the Aspen Grove Kerry Junior Championship final on Friday, winning 1-7 to 0-6 against Tralee Parnells.

Elsewhere, St Kevin’s (Louth), Crosserlough (Cavan), Granemore (Armagh) and Clontibret (Monaghan) all claimed senior county honours.
