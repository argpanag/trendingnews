Wild winds cause small aircraft to overturn at John Wayne Airport

TheTools · 10.11.2026 08:10 · source · permalink

The two occupants who were in the airplane at the time were not injured, airport officials said.

Santa Ana, California April 13, 2019: Santa Ana John Wayne airport SNA in California.

Two people were inside a small airplane that flipped upside down due to strong winds at John Wayne Airport, authorities said.

The incident was reported just after 2:30 p.m. Saturday after a Cessna 172 overturned while on the ACI Jet ramp due to the wind, according to airport officials. At the time, two people were in the aircraft but they walked away with no injuries.
