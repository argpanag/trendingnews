Bigg Boss 20: Aman Gandhi gives a clear perspective on Mahi Vij conversation

TheTools · 05.10.2026 14:23 · source · permalink

Posted on October 5, 2026 at 10:23 am

Featured Indian TV

The latest episode of Weekend Ka Vaar saw a detailed conversation around Mahi Vij and the discussions that Aman Gandhi, along with the other housemates, had about her.

With a particular narrative being discussed around the housemates allegedly waiting for Mahi to sleep and then sitting together to discuss how she was wrong, Aman Gandhi came forward and straightforwardly clarified what the actual conversation had been about.

During the Weekend Ka Vaar discussion, Salman Khan said, “Log wait karte hai ki raat ko Mahhi so jaye aur aap teeno baith kar ye discuss kare ki Mahhi aaj galat thi.”

Aman did not deny that observations about Mahi had been discussed. Instead, he clearly explained the context behind them. He said, “Observations jo thi haan negative thi, hamne discuss ki thi vo observations ki mereko aisa lag raha hai, par maine kaha jo unki achi sides hai vo dominant side ho to uspar thodi dekhte hai aur baat karte hai. Varna ham unse baat karenge, hamne ye baat ki thi.”

Aman’s clarification made it clear that the conversation was not simply about criticising Mahi or deciding that she was wrong. He acknowledged that there were certain negative observations but also pointed out that they had discussed focusing on Mahi’s positive sides and even considered speaking to her directly.

When Salman Khan said that he had not heard this part of the conversation and asked if anyone had heard it in passing, Aman confidently stood by his statement and said, “Qazi Bhai Sir, inko puchiye, inko bhi yahi same baat boli thi.”

The conversation then moved towards the specific incident that had initially prompted Aman and the others to discuss Mahi’s actions. When Aasif Khan said it appeared that Aman was refusing something when he was asked about it, Salman asked Aman to explain the simple point behind his observation.

Aman then gave a direct explanation. He said, “Sir vo ye point tha ki Mahhi Maam ko maine hamesha bolte wakt suna hai ki apni jaan koi kaise de sakta hai, apne aap ko koi kaise nominate kar sakta hai, vo us angle me bahot jyada strong feel karti hai. To jab unhone vo kiya to fir ek ulterior motive dikh ke aaya, to us motive par hamne discuss kiya tha, jaha se ye saari baat shuru hui thi.”

With this, Aman put the entire context of the conversation forward. His point was based on an observation of Mahi’s earlier statements and the contrast he felt when her actions appeared different from those views. Rather than making a personal allegation, Aman explained exactly what had made him question the situation and why that discussion had started.

What stood out during the Weekend Ka Vaar conversation was Aman’s straightforwardness. He neither denied a conversation that had actually taken place nor allowed it to be presented without context. He acknowledged the negative observations, explained that Mahi’s positive sides had also been discussed and clearly stated the specific incident that had led to the discussion in the first place.

Aman Gandhi’s composed clarification therefore brought the focus back to what was actually said, allowing the conversation to be understood in its complete context rather than through a one-sided narrative.
