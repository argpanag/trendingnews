Weather forecast for tomorrow, September 23rd, nationwide

TheTools · 22.09.2026 13:00 · source · permalink

Weather forecast for tomorrow, September 23, Hanoi will have intermittent sunshine with the highest temperature of 31-33 degrees C. Ho Chi Minh City will have moderate rain, heavy rain and thunderstorms, locally very heavy rain.

The National Center for Hydro-Meteorological Forecasting has updated the weather forecast for tonight and tomorrow (September 23) in regions across the country.

The Northern region is sunny with the highest temperature around 31 - 34 degrees Celsius.

The area from Hue to Da Nang, the eastern provinces from Quang Ngai to Dak Lak have heavy to very heavy rain and thunderstorms.

Lam Dong and Khanh Hoa provinces, Southern region have moderate rain, heavy rain and thunderstorms, locally very heavy rain in some places.

The southern part of Quang Tri province, the western part of provinces from Quang Ngai to Dak Lak will have rain, moderate rain and thunderstorms, with locally heavy rain.

Weather forecast tonight and tomorrow September 23 for regions:

Hanoi City

Lowest temperature: 24-26 degrees C.

Highest temperature: 31-33 degrees C.

Cloudy, sometimes showers and thunderstorms at night, intermittent sunshine during the day. Light wind.

Northwest

Lowest temperature: 21-24 degrees C, in some places below 21 degrees C.

Highest temperature: 31-34 degrees C.

Cloudy, showers and thunderstorms in some places at night, sunny during the day. Light wind.

Northeast

Lowest temperature: 23-26 degrees C, mountainous areas in some places below 22 degrees C.

Highest temperature: 31-34 degrees C.

Cloudy, showers and thunderstorms in some places at night, sunny during the day. Light wind.

Thanh Hoa to Hue

Lowest temperature: 23-26 degrees C.

Highest temperature: North 29-32 degrees C, South 27-30 degrees C.

Cloudy, showers and thunderstorms in some places at night, sunny days; especially Hue City is cloudy, with heavy to very heavy rain and thunderstorms. Light wind. In thunderstorms, there is a possibility of tornadoes, lightning and strong gusts of wind.

South Central Coast

Lowest temperature: 22-25 degrees C.

Highest temperature: North 26-29 degrees C, South 29-31 degrees C.

Cloudy, heavy to very heavy rain and thunderstorms. Light wind. In thunderstorms, there is a possibility of tornadoes, lightning and strong gusts of wind.

Central Highlands

Lowest temperature: 20-23 degrees C.

Highest temperature: 27-30 degrees C.

Cloudy, with rain, moderate rain and thunderstorms, locally heavy rain. Light wind. In thunderstorms, there is a possibility of tornadoes, lightning and strong gusts of wind.

Southern region

Lowest temperature: 23-26 degrees C.

Highest temperature: 29-32 degrees C.

Cloudy, with moderate rain, heavy rain and thunderstorms, locally very heavy rain. Light wind. In thunderstorms, there is a possibility of tornadoes, lightning and strong gusts of wind.

Ho Chi Minh City

Lowest temperature: 24-26 degrees C.

Highest temperature: 30-32 degrees C.

Cloudy, with moderate rain, heavy rain and thunderstorms, locally very heavy rain. Light wind. In thunderstorms, there is a possibility of tornadoes, lightning and strong gusts of wind.

Forecast of heavy rain in provinces and cities from now until September 24, with days of rain over 200mm
