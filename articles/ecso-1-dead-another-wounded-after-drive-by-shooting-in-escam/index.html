ECSO: 1 dead, another wounded after drive-by shooting in Escambia County

TheTools · 21.09.2026 17:50 · source · permalink

ESCAMBIA COUNTY, Fla. (WALA) - One victim is dead and another is wounded following an apparent drive-by shooting in Florida’s Escambia County, officials said.

The Escambia County Sheriff’s Office said deputies responded to the shooting on Baisan Street, where both victims were found inside a home.

The ECSO said investigators believe this was a drive-by shooting and that someone fired shots into the home.

A male victim is deceased, and a female victim was transported to a local hospital, the sheriff’s office said.

There is no suspect information at this time, an official told FOX10 News.
