SBI Clerk Mains Exam Date 2026 announced; exam on November 26 and 27, check admit card, pattern and shift timings

TheTools · 17.11.2026 12:55 · source · permalink

The State Bank of India (SBI) has announced the SBI Clerk Mains Exam Date 2026 for candidates shortlisted in the preliminary examination. The Mains examination will be held on November 26 and 27, 2026, as part of the recruitment process for Junior Associates (Customer Support & Sales). Candidates who have cleared the preliminary stage can now plan their final preparation according to the examination schedule.

The Mains examination will be conducted online and will assess candidates across four sections: General/Financial Awareness, General English, Quantitative Aptitude, and Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude. With the examination dates approaching, candidates should focus on revision, practise full-length mock tests and work on improving their speed and accuracy.

The SBI Clerk Mains admit card 2026 will be made available on the bank's official website. Candidates will need their registration number or roll number and password or date of birth to access their call letters. The admit card will contain details about the examination date, shift, reporting time and allotted examination centre.

SBI Clerk Mains Admit Card 2026: Details and download process

SBI will release the Clerk Mains admit card 2026 through its official recruitment portal. Candidates who have qualified in the preliminary examination will be able to download their call letters using their login credentials.

The admit card will carry important information required for appearing in the examination, including the candidate's name, roll number, examination date, shift, reporting time and examination centre address. It will also contain the candidate's photograph, signature and instructions that must be followed on the examination day.

How to download SBI Clerk Mains Admit Card 2026?

Candidates can follow these steps once the download link is activated:

  • Visit the official SBI website at sbi.co.in.
  • Navigate to the 'Careers' section.
  • Select the 'Current Openings' option.
  • Find the recruitment link for Junior Associates (Customer Support & Sales) 2026.
  • Click on the SBI Clerk Mains Admit Card 2026 download link.
  • Enter the registration number or roll number along with the password or date of birth.
  • Submit the login details.
  • The admit card will appear on the screen.
  • Download the call letter and take a printout for future reference.

Candidates should check all the details printed on the admit card carefully. Any discrepancy should be brought to the attention of the concerned authorities before the examination.

SBI Clerk Mains Shift Timings 2026

SBI will conduct the Mains examination across the announced dates, November 26 and 27. The individual shift timings, reporting schedule and examination details will be mentioned on candidates' admit cards.

Candidates should check their respective call letters to confirm the examination date, allotted shift and reporting time. They should reach the examination centre well before the reporting deadline and follow all instructions provided by the bank.

SBI Clerk Mains Preparation Tips 2026

With the examination dates approaching, candidates should use their remaining preparation time effectively. Regular revision and mock test practice can help improve performance across all four sections.

Some important preparation strategies include:

  • Revise General and Financial Awareness: Focus on recent current affairs, banking developments, financial news, Reserve Bank of India announcements and important government schemes.
  • Practise Quantitative Aptitude: Give attention to Data Interpretation, Arithmetic, Simplification and other important topics. Solve questions under timed conditions to improve calculation speed.
  • Strengthen Reasoning Ability: Practise puzzles, seating arrangements, input-output questions and logical reasoning regularly.
  • Improve English Language Skills: Revise Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Error Detection, Para Jumbles and vocabulary-based questions.
  • Attempt full-length mock tests: Take regular SBI Clerk Mains mock tests to understand the examination structure, improve time management and identify topics that require additional preparation.
  • Analyse mock test performance: Review incorrect answers and identify questions that consume excessive time. Work on weaker areas and avoid repeating the same mistakes.

Candidates who have qualified in the preliminary examination should also keep their login credentials ready for downloading the Mains admit card. Checking the official SBI website regularly will help them stay updated on the call letter release and other examination-related instructions.
