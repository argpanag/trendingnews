Stegra selects leading partners for maritime transport | Freight news | Shipping Telegraph

TheTools · 15.09.2026 00:39 · source · permalink

Sweden’s green steel producer Stegra has selected sea transport companies for the management of its shipping operations.

Sea transport is an important part of the full supply chain and logistics solutions for Stegra, and when designing the logistics network the steelmaker has selected leading sea transport companies for the management of its shipping operations.

These partners will support Stegra’s inbound and outbound goods flow, not the least, in bringing scrap steel and iron to Boden and steel products from Boden to service centers or direct customers in different parts of Europe.

Finland’s ESL Shipping will operate Stegra’s European shipping network, transporting both input materials and finished products.

Netherlands based Royal Wagenborg will also operate Stegra’s European shipping network by transporting both input materials and finished products.

US-headquartered Pangaea Logistics Solutions will secure iron ore deliveries and brings experience and expertise in dry bulk shipping, including ice-class and Arctic operations.

UK-headquartered shipbroker Clarksons has worked with Stegra as a strategic advisor, providing shipping and logistics expertise to design and implement efficient, scalable and lower-carbon ocean freight solutions that support its global supply chain.

Jenny Marin, Stegra’s head of logistics, said “With these four experienced players with complementing areas of expertise, we get robust, scalable and efficient handling of the seagoing supply chains needed for our operations in Boden and we look forward to a fruitful collaboration.”
