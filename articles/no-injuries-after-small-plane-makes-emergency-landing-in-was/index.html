No injuries after small plane makes emergency landing in Washington County

TheTools · 14.11.2026 00:55 · source · permalink

The pilot of a small plane made an emergency landing around 4 p.m. Friday in a field in rural Washington County. The plane landed safely and no injuries were reported, according to a statement from the Washington County Sheriff’s Office.

Members of the sheriff’s office and Cottage Grove Police Department responded to the scene just east of 100th Street South and Manning Avenue South in Denmark Township, where they found the aircraft occupied by two adults.

Both the National Transportation Safety Board and Federal Aviation Administration were notified. The aircraft, which suffered no apparent damage, has been released back to the owner, according to the sheriff’s office.

The reason for the emergency landing was not immediately clear, but it was not related to the FAA’s decision to ground flights nationwide around 5 p.m. Friday, said Sgt. Matt O’Hara of the Washington County Sheriff’s Office.

“They were working with the FAA on possible issues, but they were not able to determine them on site,” said O’Hara, on Friday evening. “They’re working with the property owner right now to come up with a plan to determine how to get the aircraft out of there.”
