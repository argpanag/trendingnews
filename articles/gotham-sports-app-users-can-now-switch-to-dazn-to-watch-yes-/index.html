Gotham Sports App users can now switch to DAZN to watch YES, MSG Networks

TheTools · 20.09.2026 18:09 · source · permalink

The loading screen of the Gotham Sports App.

Gotham Sports App users can now switch to watch YES and MSG Networks on DAZN in preparation for the end of the Gotham Sports App at the conclusion of the Yankees season.

The companies announced in July that the Gotham Sports App would be discontinued after the Yankees season and replaced by DAZN. Pricing for the new app has yet to be announced.

According to an email sent on Thursday to Gotham Sports App customers, viewers can use their existing credentials to log in to the DAZN app through their TV provider.

The Gotham Sports App was launched in 2024 and shows games and programming for the Yankees, Nets, Knicks, Rangers, Islanders, Devils and Buffalo Sabres in some markets.

The app has been a business success with constant growth, but has been plagued by technical issues that frustrated many customers.
