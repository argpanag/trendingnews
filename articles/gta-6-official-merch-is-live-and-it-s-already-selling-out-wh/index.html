'GTA 6' official merch is live, and it's already selling out: Where to buy

TheTools · 12.10.2026 00:21 · source · permalink

While we all count down the days until we can finally hit the streets of Vice City, Rockstar Games is letting us dress the part right now. The official Grand Theft Auto VI merch collection is live on the Rockstar Store, packing the iconic neon pink, ocean blue, and palm-tree aesthetic of Leonida into apparel, accessories, and heavy-duty collectibles.

Whether you want to rep the game subtly with low-key Vice City logos or go all-in with a $400 collector's box, Rockstar dropped something for every budget level. To help you skip the fluff and grab the best pieces before they sell out, we rounded up the standout items from the new collection.

The White Grand Theft Auto VI Macca the Gator Tee leans right into Leonida's absurd pop-culture lore, plastering the hit in-universe TV show's character, Macca the Gator, across a heavyweight, 100% white cotton shirt inspired by the fits worn by Jason and Lucia.

If you prefer keeping the sun out of your eyes in pure Vice City style, the Grand Theft Auto VI Baseball Cap features a bright blue stretch-fitted cotton build accented by an embroidered pink VI palm tree logo up front and a matching Rockstar logo along the side.

Moving over to carry gear, the Pink and Blue Tote features heavy-duty canvas in custom Vice City pink, pairing iconic franchise logos with built-in interior organization, including a laptop sleeve and three dedicated bottle slots. For a smaller accessory that packs pure retro crime-boss energy, the Vice City Zippo Lighter delivers a classic, refillable, polished-chrome body laser-etched with Vice City and Rockstar logos on opposing sides.

Audiophiles looking to soundtrack their room setup can lock in the Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album – Exclusive Vinyl, a two-disc set pressed on transparent magenta vinyl with blue splatter, featuring 34 original tracks from heavy hitters like Travis Scott, Yung Lean, and Morgan Wallen.

And finally, for the hardcore fans wanting to go all out, the Grand Theft Auto VI: The Goodtime State – Vice City Collection serves up the ultimate physical box set loaded with an official Macca the Gator figure, custom Oakley Frogskins, a New Era snapback, a double-sided souvenir map poster, and a mountain of exclusive Vice City display pieces.
