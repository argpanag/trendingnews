Nigeria 2027 mock election exercise: Inec conduct accreditation exercise for Nigeria 2027 elections - BBC News Pidgin

TheTools · 28.11.2026 10:02 · source · permalink

Inec dey conduct mock accreditation and result upload exercise across polling for Nigeria for di 2027 general elections.

Summary

  • INEC dey conduct nationwide mock accreditation and result upload exercise on Saturday, 28 November, ahead of di 2027 general elections
  • Mock accreditation na exercise wey Inec dey use test dia election equipment and procedures bifor di actual election
  • Di exercise go happun for 17,656 polling units across Nigeria, including di FCT, between 8:30am for morning and 2:30pm for afternoon

Live Coverage

Adesola Ikulajolu, Sarah Ayeku, Atsahyel Aaron, Kingsley Onwuka, Abubakar Maccido

  1. 17 minutes wey don passKano pipo complain of slow BVAS process

    For Kano, di INEC mock exercis dey also happun as pipo come out to test di machines.

    BBC Pidgin monitor polling units 19, 18, 22 and 23, Kabuga Primary School I & II, Kabuga, Gwale local govment area of Kano state.

    We see say women come out well well as dem take turn to do accreditation.

    Meanwhile, some of dem complain say di machine now really fast. Dem say di process too slow.

  2. 51 minutes wey don passMock accreditation begin for Lagos state

    Inec mock election exercise wey dem wan use test dia machine and equipment ahead of di 2027 general elections don start for Lagos state.

    For 002 polling unit Holy Cross, Ward D 3 Popoaguda, Lagos Island LGA, Inec pipo start late becos pipo wey suppose participate for di exercise first dem come.

    Wetin BBC witness na say some pipo neva collect dia PVC becos dey register dis year.

    Meanwhile, di machine dey capture dose wey get PVC and dey participate for di exercise.

    One observer for today exercise say if Inec fit maintain dis pattern dey bring today for di mock election, e mean say 2027 elections go dey smooth.

    E say, although INEC pipo come late, but as dem arrive pipo wey suppose come participate start dey come out.

  3. one hour wey don passInec begin nationwide mock election exercise for 2027

    Di Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) don begin nationwide mock accreditation and result upload exercise wey dey scheduled for Saturday, 28 November.

    Inec list 17,656 polling units across di 36 states and di Federal Capital Territory wia di mock exercise dey sup.

    Di exercise, wey go take place between 8:30am and 2:30pm, na part of di electoral body preparations for di 2027 general elections.

    According to Inec, dem say di exercise go hold for two polling units for each of di 8,838 Registration Areas nationwide.
