Taylor Swift’s “Babylon” Lyrics Will Leave You With Chills—Here’s What the Haunting Song Is About

TheTools · 25.09.2026 04:26 · source · permalink

WHAT DOES IT MEAN?!

Swifties have busily spent the past 24 hours reading into the meaning of Taylor Swift’s four new songs from The Life of a Showgirl: The Encore, and while we deciphered the meaning of “Pink Clouding,” figured out what “Patient Zero” is a reference to, and even zoomed in on / enhanced some blurry gravestones in a music video snippet, “Babylon” has remained a total mystery. Until now!

The song is a narrative journey about a person who left their small town in Virginia and is seemingly going through a lot of regrets at a casino and on the train. The lyrics are Taylor at her best, with moments like, “You're using nostalgia as a weapon to hurt yourself / But darling, you end up hurting everybody else” and my personal favorite, “No elbows on the table, and the neighbors lawn always looked better
Your hand-me-down sweater should've been from the mall.”

Pretty sure fans will be debating this one for days to come, so stay tuned, and in the meantime, check out the full lyrics, via Genius.
