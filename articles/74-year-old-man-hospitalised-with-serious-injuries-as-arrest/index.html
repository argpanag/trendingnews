74-year-old man hospitalised with serious injuries as arrest made

TheTools · 14.11.2026 10:01 · source · permalink

A 74-year-old man has been seriously injured after being hit by a car in Scarborough while a motorist has been arrested in connection with the incident.

The collision happened at around 4.30pm on Wednesday, November 11, on Scalby Road near the Scalby and Newby library.

The man, a pedestrian, was struck by a white Suzuki and remains in hospital with serious injuries.

A police spokesperson said: "We are appealing to anyone who was in the area at the time, anyone who witnessed the collision along, with anyone with dashcam footage of the incident, to get in touch if they have not already spoken to the police."

The driver of the Suzuki, a man also in his 70s, was arrested in connection with the incident and has been released under investigation.

Anyone with information is asked to email Nicholas.Simpson@northyorkshire.police.uk or call 101, quoting reference number 12260222146.

Enquiries remain ongoing.
