Key Takeaways:

  • Spectrum is saying ‘thank you’ with a free Gig Speed Lift November 9 to 30 for eligible customers* that activate the speed upgrade in the My Spectrum App. 
  • Don’t let a 5G Home Internet connection offered by the competition eat into your playtime. Download Grand Theft Auto VI in less than 30 minutes* with Spectrum Internet Gig.

STAMFORD, Conn. — The most anticipated game in the world is almost ready to download, and Spectrum is treating eligible customers to a free Gig Speed Lift, November 9 to 30, that will have them cruising Vice City before the rest of the block finishes downloading. No cost, and no strings attached. With Spectrum Internet Gig, customers can start playing Grand Theft Auto VI in less than 30 minutes, so game day is for cruising, not downloading. 

The free Gig Speed Lift offer runs through November 30, which means when Thanksgiving guests show up with all their devices, the house keeps up. Football looks sharp on the 4K TV, FaceTime calls with out-of-town family stay smooth and the Black Friday deals are a quick checkout away through Cyber Monday. 

How Spectrum's Gig Speed Lift works:

  • Now through Nov. 30, opt in: Customers with qualifying Internet plans and compatible equipment can activate the speed upgrade in one tap on the My Spectrum App.
  • Nov. 9, level up: The Gig Speed Lift starts. Customers get boosted to Spectrum Internet 1 Gig. In select areas, if customers already have 1 Gig, they move up to 2 Gig.
  • Nov. 10, Grand Theft Auto VI pre-load opens: Pre-ordered copies of Grand Theft Auto VI can download early, skipping the launch day rush.
  • Nov. 19, game on: Grand Theft Auto VI launches.
  • Nov. 26, Thanksgiving: A full house, a lot of devices and plenty of bandwidth to go around.
  • Nov. 27 to 30, Black Friday through Cyber Monday: Browse and check out faster while the lift is still running.
  • Nov. 30, last lap: The Gig Speed Lift ends. No changes to your bill, nothing for you to do.

Go deeper: Spectrum Internet Gig for the win: Spectrum Internet delivers speeds up to 1 Gbps across the Company’s 45-state service area and in select markets, offers speeds up to 2 Gbps. Spectrum’s network powers the nation’s most reliable internet**, keeping speeds fast even when the whole neighborhood is online.

Customers can learn more about Spectrum’s free Gig Speed Lift at spectrum.net/thank-you.
