Robbie Williams sang with fans in Lima after concert cancellation

TheTools · 25.09.2026 12:33 · source · permalink

Robbie Williams gave an impromptu performance outside a hotel in Lima, the capital of Peru, after a planned concert in the city was cancelled. As BBC News reports, the musician told fans gathered outside the hotel that he was heartbroken and joined them for a brief singalong.

Concert cancellation

Williams' concert was planned to take place at Arena 1 Park. Authorities announced that the event could not be held because the venue did not comply with safety requirements.

More current news is available on the UA.News Telegram channel Telegram.

Performance outside the hotel

After the performance was cancelled, a crowd of fans gathered outside the hotel where the artist was staying. They welcomed Williams, and he joined them for a brief singalong.

Thus, the planned concert at Arena 1 Park did not take place, but the singer briefly came out to meet people outside the concert venue. The BBC describes the performance as impromptu.
