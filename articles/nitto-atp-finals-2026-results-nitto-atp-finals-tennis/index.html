Nitto ATP Finals 2026 Results | Nitto ATP Finals | Tennis

TheTools · 19.11.2026 20:13 · source · permalink

The Nitto ATP Finals continued Wednesday, when second seed Carlos Alcaraz and debutant Frances Tiafoe won their matches at the prestigious season-ending championships. Alcaraz defeated former champion Daniil Medvedev and later qualified for the semi-finals, while Tiafoe overcame home hope Flavio Cobolli to notch his first win at the event.

Earlier in the day, former doubles champions Kevin Krawietz and Tim Puetz sealed their return to the semi-finals. View below the full Wednesday Nitto ATP Finals results.
