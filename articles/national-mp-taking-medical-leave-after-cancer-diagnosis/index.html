National MP taking medical leave after cancer diagnosis

TheTools · 22.11.2026 02:58 · source · permalink

Minister for Women and National MP Nicola Grigg is taking medical leave to deal with a cancer diagnosis, the Prime Minister says.

In a statement, Christopher Luxon said Grigg recently underwent surgery and is scheduled for another in a fortnight, before beginning further treatment.

"Nicola has a positive prognosis and is hopeful of a successful outcome."

"I want Nicola to take the time she needs for her treatment and recovery. The whole National team backs her as she focuses on getting well."

Luxon confirmed National MP Penny Simmonds would take over Grigg's portfolio's of Minister for the Environment and Women while she was on medical leave.

Grigg went to Parliament in 2020 after winning the safe National seat of Selwyn in the 2020 election. She worked as a journalist before shifting to politics, working for former National leaders Bill English and Simon Bridges.

Luxon said Grigg is a very committed MP.
