Following the past when actor Kim Ji-won played the piano on Younha's stage when she was 16, she even showed off her hidden talent by performing Girls' Generation dances at her fan meeting.

Broadcaster Park Seul-ki revealed the scene and backstory of Kim Ji-won's fan meeting "WONEDERLAND" held on the 24th and 25th on his SNS on the 26th.

In the released video, Kim Ji-won performed Girls' Generation's "Tell Me Your Wish" with dancers in navy uniform-style costumes and black long boots. Standing among the dancers in white costumes, he drew cheers from fans by naturally performing movements that saved the fingertips and choreography to the rhythm.

In particular, Kim Ji-won, who stood in the center of the stage in uniform unlike her usual appearance in dramas, caught the attention with her dance lines and stage manners as well as idols.

Kim Ji-won's dance skills are even more eye-catching because she has a history of preparing for her actual singer debut. Kim Ji-won recently appeared on tvN's "You Quiz on the Block" and said she stepped into the entertainment industry through street casting when she was a junior in middle school.

At the time, Kim Ji-won said, "I practiced for two or three years for the first time," adding, "At the time, singer Yoon-ha was in the same agency. I have a little experience playing the piano on stage to foster that experience," he recalled.

In fact, Kim Ji-won, who played the piano on Younha's stage around the age of 16, became an actor and performed a cover dance of Girls' Generation at a fan meeting named after her. It was a moment that reminded me of the stage experience I had while preparing for a singer in the past.

Kim Ji-won is currently playing the role of genius surgeon Ko Soo-jung in SBS's Friday-Saturday drama "Doctor X: The Age of the White Mafia." The sixth episode, which aired on the 24th, broke its own highest ratings, recording 19.5% of household viewers nationwide and 21.3% of instantaneous viewership.

Another image was revealed behind the stage. Park Seul-ki, who served as the MC for the fan meeting for two days, posted a picture of Kim Ji-won hugging each other and expressed her affection, saying, "Our Ji-won, who laughs a lot just by facing each other."

"He hugs me after the fan meeting," he said, "I'm running with my arms open to thank him with that face, and I'm glad he didn't do a piggyback, a lantern, and a rinse."

Kim Ji-won, who cheered fans with Girls' Generation dances, also ran to Park Seul-ki with open arms after the stage and thanked her. Park Seul-ki, who wanted to carry Kim Ji-won on his back and even burn a lantern, burst into laughter, saying, "I really held it in."
