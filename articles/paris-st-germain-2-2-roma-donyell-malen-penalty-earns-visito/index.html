Paris St-Germain 2-2 Roma: Donyell Malen penalty earns visitors a point at Parc des Princes

PSG have to settle for a point at home against Roma

Donyell Malen scored for the fourth Champions League game running as Roma ended Paris St-Germain's 100% start to the competition.

The defending champions looked set for a fifth straight win when Ousmane Dembele, less than three minutes after coming on, scored from the penalty spot to put PSG in front.

But Roma, who had led early through Mario Hermoso, fought back and won a penalty of their own when referee Glenn Nyberg was sent to the screen and confirmed a handball by Vitinha.

Malen, who was also introduced as a substitute, duly converted from 12 yards and while both sides pushed for a winner, it finished all square at the Parc des Princes.

Luis Enrique's side remain unbeaten and are second in the standings, two points behind Real Madrid, while Roma are just outside the play-off places in 25th with five points from five games.

The visitors began the game brightly with centre-back Hermoso winning the ball back high up the pitch and continuing his run into the box to finish from Matias Soule's cutback.

PSG gradually grew into the game and levelled when Roma switched off from a corner, allowing former Bournemouth defender Illia Zabarnyi to head in at the near post.

After that setback, the Italians regrouped and started the second half well but, having failed to take their chances, were punished when PSG captain Achraf Hakimi burst into the box and was hauled down by Wesley.

Dembele coolly slotted for his fourth Champions League goal of the campaign and Malen was equally composed with his spot-kick to earn Gian Piero Gasperini's side a point.
