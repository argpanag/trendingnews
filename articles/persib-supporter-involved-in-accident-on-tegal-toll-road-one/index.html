Persib Supporter Involved in Accident on Tegal Toll Road, One Dead and Three Injured

TheTools · 08.11.2026 13:28 · source · permalink

A supporter of Persib Bandung died in an accident in Tegal, Central Java, while three others were injured. They were on their way to watch a match in Semarang.

08 Nov 2026 20:28 WIB · Nasional

ARSIP DITLANTAS POLDA JATENG

Share

Copy Link

By Kristi Dwi Utami, Fabio Maria Lopes Costa

SEMARANG, KOMPAS — A minibus carrying supporters of the Persib Bandung football club was involved in a traffic accident on the Lebeteng toll road in Tarub District, Tegal Regency, Central Java, on Sunday (8/11/2026). The incident resulted in one fatality and three injuries.

The accident occurred on Sunday at approximately 6:47 AM WIB on the Pejagan-Pemalang toll road at Kilometer 282, in Lebeteng Village. At the time of the incident, a minibus with license plate A-7022-S, driven by Anjar Supandi (38), was traveling eastward from West Java towards Semarang, Central Java.

Upon arriving in Lebeteng Village, the minibus collided with the rear of a Hino truck with the license plate BE-8163-CU. The truck, which was traveling in the same direction as the minibus, was driven by Winarto, 51, a resident of Bekri District in Central Lampung, Lampung.

"The microbus driver is suspected of being careless and lacking concentration while operating the vehicle, which led to a collision with the rear of a Hino truck," said Central Java Police Traffic Director, Commissioner Muhammad Aldian, on Sunday.

As a result of the incident, three individuals sustained lacerations and eye injuries: Dian Hardiansyah (23), Faoziah Oktafianti (26), and Indah Nuriah (25). All of them are residents of Bogor Regency, West Java.

One person has been reported dead after receiving treatment at the hospital, identified as Roby Kurniawan, 34. Roby, who suffered a head injury, was also a resident of Bogor Regency.

"The minibus was carrying supporters of Persib Bandung. It was not full, with just one driver and four passengers," said Aldian.

According to Aldian, the victims were promptly evacuated shortly after the accident. They were then taken to the PKU Muhammadiyah Adiwerna Islamic Hospital in Tegal for further treatment.

Meanwhile, the management of Persib Bandung expressed deep condolences over the passing of Roby, a supporter from Bogor, in the traffic accident.

The deceased was reportedly traveling with several fellow Bobotoh from Bogor to Semarang to support Persib Bandung in their match against Java United FC at Jatidiri Stadium on Sunday at 7:00 PM WIB.

Adhitia Putra Herawan, Deputy CEO of PT Persib Bandung Bermartabat, expressed his condolences to the family of the deceased and to all those who share in the grief over this tragedy.

Persib also prays that the deceased finds the best place beside Allah SWT, and that the family left behind is granted strength, resilience, and sincerity.

"On behalf of the management, players, team, and the entire Persib family, we express our deepest condolences on the passing of the late Roby Kurniawan. We share in the profound sadness and extend our sympathies to the bereaved family," said Adhitia.

In a tribute to the late individual, all players and the Persib team will wear black armbands during their match against Java United FC at Jatidiri Stadium in Semarang.

A representative from Persib will soon visit the mourning family of the victim in the Parung area of Bogor to express condolences and offer support to the bereaved family.

"We want to be present and express our condolences directly to the family of the deceased. The support and love from Bobotoh have always been an important part of Persib's journey," said Adhitia.

We share our deep sadness and express our condolences to the bereaved family

Aditya added that Persib is also praying for fellow Bobotoh members affected by the incident. The victims who were injured are hoped to receive the best care and to have a smooth recovery process.

"We also remind all Bobotoh who are traveling to prioritize safety at all times. Support and love for Persib are certainly very important, but safety remains our top priority," he stated.
