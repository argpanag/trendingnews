Rohit Sharma sets new personal best for ODI catches in 2026

TheTools · 13.11.2026 05:15 · source · permalink

Rohit Sharma's 12th catch of 2026 at Bay Oval surpasses his previous personal best of 11 set in 2018, making him India's leading fielder in ODIs this calendar year.

Published - 13 Nov 2026, 10:45 IST

8 Min Read

Rohit Sharma set a new personal best for the most catches taken in a single ODI calendar year when he pouched his 12th of 2026 during the fourth ODI against New Zealand at Bay Oval, Mount Maunganui, on November 13, 2026. The milestone arrived at short cover, where Rohit held on to dismiss Finn Allen off the bowling of Axar Patel. Allen had scored 41 runs before that catch ended his innings, and the dismissal represented one of the key breakthroughs in the New Zealand innings.

The effort moves Rohit past his own previous best of 11 catches in a calendar year, a mark he had set in 2018. His career tally of ODI catches now stands at 112, and he leads India's fielding chart in the format for 2026 by a clear margin. Will Young produced a commanding innings for New Zealand in the same match, scoring 125 runs off 108 balls, underlining the competitive nature of the contest at Bay Oval on that day.

How does Rohit's 2026 tally compare with the rest of the Indian fielders?

KL Rahul is the closest challenger to Rohit among the Indian outfield, sitting on 11 ODI catches in 2026, one behind the former captain's new personal best tally of 12. Shreyas Iyer and Naman Dhir have each taken 6 catches in the format this year, placing them well behind the top two in India's fielding standings.
