Sienna Miller Makes Rare Red Carpet Appearance with Daughter Marlowe, 14, at 'War' World Premiere

TheTools · 17.09.2026 01:58 · source · permalink

Sienna Miller is hitting the red carpet with her daughter!

The 44-year-old Golden Globe-nominated actress made a rare red carpet appearance with 14-year-old daughter Marlowe at the world premiere of her new series War on Wednesday (September 16) held at Odeon Luxe Leicester Square in London, England.

If you didn’t know, Sienna shares Marlowe with ex-fiancé Tom Sturridge. She also shares a daughter, born in December 2023, and a son, born in April 2026, with partner Oli Green.

Also joining Sienna at the War premiere was co-star Dominic West.

Fellow cast members in attendance included Nick Mohammed, Pip Torrens, Nina Sosanya, Archie Renaux, Phoebe Fox, James McArdle and Emma Laird.

What is War about?

Here’s the synopsis:

WAR is a high-stakes, legal drama following the lives of London’s most powerful lawyers as firms Cathcarts & Sons and Taylor & Byrne clash over a headline-grabbing legal battle, drawing ambitious young lawyer Jonathan “Johnny” Warren (Renaux) into a ruthless world where ambition is currency, betrayal is strategy, and winning is everything. Season one follows the explosive divorce of British tech entrepreneur Morgan Henderson (West) and international film star Carla Duval (Miller), as Cathcarts & Sons and Taylor & Byrne stop at nothing to secure victory for their clients.
