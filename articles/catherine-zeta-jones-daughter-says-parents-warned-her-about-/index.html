Catherine Zeta-Jones' daughter says parents warned her about 'nepo baby' label

TheTools · 21.11.2026 01:16 · source · permalink

Catherine Zeta-Jones's daughter has said her parents warned her people might have "preconceived notions" about her embarking on an acting career, as she shared her views on the "nepo baby" label.

Carys Douglas, 23, made her stage debut this year, playing the lead role of Nina in Anton Chekhov's The Seagull in Brooklyn, New York.

Zeta-Jones, who is married to Michael Douglas, son of Hollywood star Kirk, previously said the "nepo baby" term was "horrific". It refers to children of famous parents perceived to have achieved riches and success off their parents' backs.

But the young actor said her parents taught her to build her "own identity" and she has learned how to deal with the "pressure".

During an interview with Elle Spain, Carys Douglas said her parents "do not hide" from her that people would have preconceived ideas about her.

"They taught me that building my own identity depended on me and no one else. It was my responsibility, and it still is.

"As time has gone on, I've come to understand that there are different ways to deal with that kind of pressure," she said.

Her father, star of hit 1980s films such as Wall Street and Fatal Attraction, recently stated that he is not a fan of the "nepo baby" label, calling it an "unfortunate title".

"I guess you could say that about any business. The same thing with Hollywood royalty. My father's father was a junkman," Douglas, 82, said, referring to Kirk, who was considered a Hollywood Golden Age icon.

Zeta-Jones, 57, from Mumbles, Swansea, also criticised the phrase, saying it was "horrible because these kids didn't ask to be born into this, they're finding their own way in life".

"You have to go with your dreams, your passion," Zeta-Jones said.

She added: "But they love it, and they're good. Otherwise, Michael and I would be the first to go, 'you know what? Maybe law school looks really good'."

Zeta-Jones' big break came when she landed a West End role in 42nd Street in 1987, before she became a household name in the UK in 1991 after starring in the ITV hit The Darling Buds Of May.

She has since become a global superstar, with roles in The Mask Of Zorro, Entrapment, Traffic and 2002's adaptation of Chicago, for which she won an Oscar for Best Supporting Actress.

The couple's children, Dylan and Carys, have both followed in their footsteps into acting.

Michael Douglas said his children have been performing "since they were in kindergarten".

"Every play, every show, summer theater camps, graduating... I mean, they've done so much work. It's unbelievable," he said.

Douglas and Zeta-Jones' children predominantly grew up in the US, but the family regularly travels to Wales, a place they are fond of.

Speaking to British Vogue last November, Carys Douglas said her summers were spent reading on her grandmother's sofa and travelling around the Gower Peninsula in Swansea.

"I feel so lucky that this is where I'm from," she told the magazine, reflecting on the "weird but magical places" scattered across the peninsula.

"Those beaches are some of the most beautiful in the world," she said.

"The Gower Peninsula is breathtaking."

She also spoke about her "theatrical" family's traditions, recalling how "there'd be lots of little competitions for singing and pantomime shows all summer".

"It's true, the Welsh love to sing," she added.
