The GTA Leaker Who Revealed Lucia Pleads Guilty to Hacking Rockstar, Biting People

TheTools · 16.11.2026 16:49 · source · permalink

No, not Cyberleek.

Arion Kurtaj, the Grand Theft Auto 6 leaker who first revealed Lucia to the world, has pleaded guilty to cybercrimes in relation to his 2022 hack of Rockstar. He also pleaded guilty to attempting to extort Rockstar, and to attacking workers at a facility where he was being detained after the hack.

Kurtaj, who is now 21, was 17 when he was initially taken into custody over the 2022 Rockstar hack. As he is from the U.K., he could not initially be named as a minor. He was also deemed unable to stand trial due to the severity of his autism, and was instead sent to be held indefinitely in a secure psychiatric facility. Now, as reported by the BBC, he has been deemed able to stand trial and has entered a guilty plea.

GTA 6 Launch Screenshots

The 2022 hack resulted in the first major GTA 6 leak, as early gameplay footage was shared online. Here, we saw Jason and Lucia for the first time, as they held victims at gunpoint. These clips were shared under the online name TeaPotUberHacker. Rockstar refused to pay Kurtaj's ransom.

Crucially, this was our first confirmation that GTA 6 would star a female protagonist, a first in modern GTA games.

It would be over a year before Rockstar finally confirmed this itself, and even longer until we got an official gameplay reveal, which was only shared in August of this year.

It wasn't just the leaked material that brought attention to this hack in 2022, but also the method in which Kurtaj pulled it off. At the time, police were housing him in a hotel after he was apprehended for previous hacks, fearing he would be targeted by rival hackers. Using his hotel TV, an Amazon Fire Stick, and a Bluetooth mouse and keyboard, Kurtaj was able to breach Rockstar, taking gameplay clips and files.

Rockstar claims that the hack cost them $5 million to recover from. It further claimed that the Lucia reveal was being kept underwraps for the marketing campaign, something that Kurtaj's leaks spoiled.

However, it isn't just these crimes related to the hack that Kurtaj was facing. The BBC also notes that the 21 year old pleaded guilty to physical assaults, in relation to biting workers at the facility where he was being kept.

Kurtaj is awaiting his sentencing. It is unclear if his time at the secure facility will be taken into account should he recieve a custodial sentence.
