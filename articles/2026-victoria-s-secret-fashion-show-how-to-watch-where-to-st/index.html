Who's ready for Supermodel Sunday? Here's how to tune in.

The Victoria's Secret Fashion Show returns this weekend for what VS has dubbed "Supermodel Sunday." Kicking off at 7:30 p.m. ET/4:30 p.m. PT on Sunday, this year's event will stream live for free on YouTube and across the brand's socials. Katseye, Megan Moroney, Megan Thee Stallion and Tate McRae are slated to perform. Candice Swanepoel, Adriana Lima, Gigi Hadid, Ashley Graham, Alex Consani and more models will appear on the catwalk.

The 2026 Victoria's Secret Fashion Show will also feature the debut walk from the winner of Angels Among Us, Victoria's Secret's docuseries charting the search for the newest Angel. Here's what you need to know about tuning in this weekend. 

When is the 2026 Victoria's Secret Fashion Show?
