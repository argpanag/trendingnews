Earthquake strikes near Iredell-Rowan County line, data shows

TheTools · 18.11.2026 11:52 · source · permalink

CLEVELAND, N.C. (WBTV) - An earthquake rattled the Iredell and Rowan County areas late Tuesday night.

The 2.4-magnitude earthquake struck just after 11 p.m. on Nov. 17, and had an epicenter just east of Knox Road along Third Creek, right at the Iredell-Rowan County line.

More than 120 people reported feeling the quake, according to U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) data. Most of those reports came from the Troutman, Mooresville, Statesville and Cleveland communities.

Of those who felt the late-night earthquake, none reported feeling more than weak to light shaking.

Earthquakes of such low magnitude usually do not cause any damage.

Anyone who felt the earthquake can report it to the USGS here.
