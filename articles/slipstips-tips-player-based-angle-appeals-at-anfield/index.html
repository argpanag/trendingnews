SlipsTips’ Tips: Player-based angle appeals at Anfield

TheTools · 21.11.2026 08:26 · source · permalink

SlipsTips shares a selection ahead of Liverpool’s showdown with Manchester United in the Premier League on Sunday.

Liverpool vs Manchester United (Sunday, 16:30)

A blockbuster Premier League showdown awaits at Anfield on Sunday as Mauricio Pochettino begins his Manchester United reign against Liverpool.

United went into the international break in the relegation zone after winning just one of their opening 10 Premier League matches of the season, prompting the Red Devils hierarchy to part company with Michael Carrick.

Liverpool, meanwhile, sit fourth in the table after winning three of their last four league matches.

The most recent of those was a 3-1 success away at Crystal Palace a fortnight ago.

As such, it is no surprise to see Andoni Iraola’s side come into this clash as odds-on favourites at 8/11 with bet365.

However, I’m going to turn my attention to the player-fouls market for a selection that looks terrific value at the odds on offer.

I’m backing Matheus Cunha to be fouled 2+ times at evens with bet365.

The Brazilian forward is one of Manchester United’s biggest attacking threats, and that regularly sees him come in for close attention from opponents.

Cunha has drawn an average of 1.91 fouls per game in the Premier League this season.

A closer look shows that figure has risen in recent weeks, with the Red Devils forward fouled at least twice in four of his last five Premier League outings, including each of the previous three.

He was also brought down three times in a recent Champions League home win over Roma, while he won United multiple free kicks in their Old Trafford victory over Sabah.

It remains to be seen what role Pochettino will give Cunha, but if he is deployed in his usual inverted role on the left of the front three, he could come up against Ronald Araujo, who committed four fouls against Palace last time out.

Notably, Cunha was fouled exactly twice in last season’s corresponding fixture, adding weight to the selection.
