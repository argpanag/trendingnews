Fiona, famed Cincinnati Zoo hippo, dies after swallowing child’s hat

TheTools · 19.11.2026 20:49 · source · permalink

Fiona the hippo, a beloved figure at the Cincinnati Zoo and internet sensation, has died after suffering health complications from swallowing a child’s hat. She was 9 years old.

She had been receiving around-the-clock care as she tried to pass the knit hat she swallowed earlier this month after it was accidentally tossed into her enclosure by a visitor.

The zoo confirmed Fiona did not make it after "multiple attempts from our amazing vets and keeper team to help her through what ended up being a medical emergency."

"The vet staff tried everything they could to help her after she ingested a hat that ended up in her habitat by accident last week. Despite multiple attempts to move the hat out of her system, it was ultimately too much to overcome. The team performed necessary supportive care today in an attempt to save her, but it was not successful and she did not recover. We know everyone in the community will be crushed by this news, as we all are. Please keep her keepers and everyone who tried to help her in your thoughts as they try to navigate and come to terms with this shocking news. They are also, understandably, asking for space at this time," the zoo shared.

The beloved hippo was set to turn 10 in January, a milestone in her journey from preemie to growing hippo.

The complications began earlier this month after a child tossed a knit cap, which got caught on a fan and was catapulted through a one-foot gap into Fiona’s habitat, where she ate it.

"We probably couldn't make that happen again if we tried. It was clearly an accident," the zoo shared.

Fiona had undergone multiple tests and treatments in attempts to help pass the hat. The zoo even brought in a nine-foot scope from the Columbus Zoo to attempt a colonoscopy, but they were unable to reach the hat. Most recently, the zoo attempted vibration plate therapy to help pass the hat.

Fiona’s story

Her story captured the hearts of millions across the world.

Born six weeks premature on Jan. 24, 2017, Fiona weighed only 29 pounds at birth — 25 pounds less than the lowest recorded birth weight for her species.

Since then, her journey inspired many not just in Cincinnati, but the entire world.

Millions watched as she began enrichment programs in the pool and eventually acclimated to the deep waters of her enclosure and when she eventually reunited with her mom, Bibi, and her father, Henry.

Known for her perseverance and spunky hippo personality, she gained national stardom, inspiring children’s books, T-Shirts and memes all over the internet.

She became the feature of national outlet coverage, with her birthday becoming an unofficial holiday in Cincinnati and beyond.

She became a big sister in 2022 when her mother, Bibi, gave birth to a healthy boy, Fritz.
