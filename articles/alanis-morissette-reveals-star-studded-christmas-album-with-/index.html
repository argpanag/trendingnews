Alanis Morissette Reveals Star-Studded Christmas Album With Steve Martin and Martin Short - AOL

TheTools · 10.11.2026 00:07 · source · permalink

Alanis Morissette is getting ready for Christmas with her first full-length holiday album, Good Weather for Immaculate Conception. The “You Oughta Know” singer was a guest on Amy Poehler’s Good Hang podcast on November 3 and shared details about the 14-track collection, which blends traditional Christmas songs, familiar holiday favorites and two original songs, along with some surprise collaborators, including Brandi Carlile, Steve Martin and Martin Short. Morissette shared the inspiration for her upcoming Christmas album and teased a few of its special guests, helping Poehler and her live Chicago audience get into the Christmas spirit.

What Inspired Alanis Morissette to Make a Christmas Album?

The singer who took the ’90s by storm with her emotional (we won’t call it angry) girl-power rock might not be the most likely artist to release a Christmas album. She admitted that she was never really a big Christmas celebrator until her first son, Ever Imre Morissette-Treadway, was born on December 25, 2010.

Over the years, she began recording a holiday tune each season before eventually deciding to bring those recordings together and create a full album.

Who Is Collaborating With Alanis Morissette on the Album?

Making Christmas come early, Alanis gave listeners a taste of what’s to come. The album features several notable guests. Brandi Carlile and Steve Martin join her on “Beautiful Star of Bethlehem,” with Martin also playing banjo. Amythyst Kiah joins Alanis on “Momma Mary,” Joy Oladokun is featured on “Silent Night,” and Trans Voices, the U.K.’s first professional trans and nonbinary vocal ensemble, accompanies Alanis on “O Tannenbaum.” Martin Short appears on “Merry Christmas,” which closes the album.

What New Songs Are on Good Weather for Immaculate Conception?

The album also features two original songs, “To an Empath at Christmas Time” and “Christmas on the Dancefloor.” It includes Morissette’s interpretations of songs by John Lennon and Yoko Ono, Joni Mitchell and Wham!

When Does Alanis Morissette’s Christmas Album Come Out?

Good Weather for Immaculate Conception is scheduled for release on Friday, November 6, 2026, through Epiphany Music and Virgin Music. The first single, “Beautiful Star of Bethlehem,” featuring Brandi Carlile and Steve Martin, has already been released.
