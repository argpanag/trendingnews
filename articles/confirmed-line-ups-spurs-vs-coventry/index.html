Confirmed line-ups: Spurs vs Coventry

TheTools · 19.10.2026 19:12 · source · permalink
  • Spurs
  • Tottenham Hotspur Stadium

We can now confirm our starting line-up as we take Coventry City in the Premier League - kick-off 8pm.

Roberto De Zerbi has made five changes to the side that started our Premier League draw at Manchester United last Saturday.

James Maddison is set to make his first competitive start for us since May, 2025 while Dejan Kulusevski returns to the matchday squad for the first time in 17 months.

Andy Robertson, Micky van de Ven and Omar Marmoush return to the starting XI while Tosin Adarabaioyo makes his first start for us in the league.

Starting XI: Kinsky, Robertson, Tosin, van Hecke, Maddison, Gray, Tonali, Solanke, Kudus, Marmoush, van de ven (c).

Substitutes: Dubravka, Senesi, Gallagher, Tel, Fernandes, Kulusevski, Davies, Olusesi, Williams-Barnett.

Our visitors line up as follows...

Starting XI: Rushworth, Pinnock, Dasilva, Thomas, Rudoni, Grimes (c), Sakamoto, Yirenkyi, Thomas-Asante, van Ewijk, Cherif.

Substitutes: Bentley, Simms, Mason-Clark, Mfuni, Awoniyi, Onyeka, Kesleer-Hayden, Torp, Hamer.
