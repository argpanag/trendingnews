Results of Darby Allin and Steven Borden vs. Kevin Knight and Kyle Fletcher at AEW All Out 2026 - NoDQ.com: WWE and AEW Coverage

TheTools · 27.09.2026 00:41 · source · permalink

Darby Allin and Steven Borden vs. The Don Callis Family (Kevin Knight and Kyle Fletcher) took place at AEW All Out 2026. Here are the highlights…

Borden started the match and held his own against Fletcher and Knight. Darby tagged in and continued the momentum. Fletcher struck Darby from behind to gain the advantage for his team. Fletcher followed it up by powerbombing Darby onto the steel steps outside of the ring.

Darby came back with a Scorpion Death Drop to Fletcher. Darby made the tag to Steven, but the referee didn’t see it. Despite this, Steven was eventually able to make the hot tag. However, Borden went for a Stinger Splash but missed and went over the top rope. Knight climbed to the top for a Coast-to-Coast on Darby, but Borden went to the other side, and both men collided in mid-air. After checking on Borden, the referee stopped the match and declared Fletcher and Knight the winners.
