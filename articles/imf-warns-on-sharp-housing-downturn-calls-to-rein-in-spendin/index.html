IMF warns on sharp housing downturn, calls to rein in spending

TheTools · 27.11.2026 04:26 · source · permalink

The International Monetary Fund has warned that a sharp downturn in the housing market could further hurt an already weakening economy, as it again urged Treasurer Jim Chalmers to curtail government spending to help the Reserve Bank reduce inflation.

In a review of Australia’s economy and financial system, the IMF pointed to risks from high household debt and the high exposure of the big banks to home loans.
