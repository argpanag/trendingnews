Best early October Prime Day deals

Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K Max

Turn any TV into a smart TV with Amazon’s Fire Stick, which you simply plug into an HDMI port to use. It lets you stream entertainment from your favorite platforms and play games without a console — plus, when you’re not using the TV, you can choose from over 2,000 pieces of art and photography to display. The Fire Stick comes with a remote that lets you control the TV via buttons or voice commands.

Colgate Optic White Pro Series High Impact White Toothpaste (3 Pack)

Colgate’s toothpaste is made with 5 percent hydrogen peroxide, a bleaching ingredient that helps remove stains from teeth and make your smile less yellow. There’s also cavity-fighting fluoride in its formula. The minty whitening toothpaste is safe for daily use and doesn’t cause tooth sensitivity, according to the brand.

Aesop Reverence Aromatique Hand Wash

We’re all about a practical gift at NBC Selected, and this “fancy” hand soap definitely falls into that category if you already need some holiday shopping inspiration. NBC Selected commerce editor Bethany Heitman says Aēsop Reverence is her favorite option right now, and a little luxury she allows herself. “The scent is a combination of citrus and wood, and the formula leaves my hands soft after washing,” she says. “I also like how the sleek bottle looks sitting on my bathroom sink.”

Lego Botanicals Flower Arrangement

Once built, this Lego flower arrangement can be a table centerpiece or shelf decor in your home. It comes with over 1,000 pieces to make a pot and 14 blooms, including camellias, peonies, hydradras and lilies, plus there’s a detailed instruction booklet that walks you through every step. The brand recommends this set for adults because it’s a complex build, but it’s a great challenge for younger Lego masters, too.

Blink Battery Doorbell 2K+

With Blink’s doorbell, you can monitor visitors and deliveries at all times. It connects to the brand’s app, letting you livestream head-to-toe video (including in night vision) and audio, as well as speak to people. The doorbell runs on two AA batteries, comes with a mounting kit and you can install it wire-free. There’s also a Blink subscription available for an additional fee, which gives you access to features like in-app person detection and motion-activated notifications.

Casper Sleep Original Pillow

The Casper Sleep Original Pillow is a NBC Selected staff-favorite product, and many of us rest our heads on it every night. It’s stuffed with a down alternative material, has a breathable cotton cover and gets its medium-soft feel from a two-layer design: the inner layer is firmer to offer support, while outer layer is plush for extra comfort. The pillow is machine-washable and clump-resistant.

Ninja Pods & Grounds Coffee Maker

You can use pods or grounds to make three types of coffee with this appliance: classic, rich and iced. It also lets you brew cups between 6 and 24 ounces, and it has a removable water reservoir for easy refills. To save space on your counter, the compact machine has a storage drawer inside so you can put away the brew basket or permanent filter when they’re not in use.

iRobot Roomba 115X Combo Robot Vacuum & Mop

This robot vacuum suctions up dirt and debris, and it has a built-in mop that scrubs floors to leave them shiny. Philips Sonicare 5950 Electric Toothbrush

Philips Sonicare’s 5950 Electric Toothbrush has all the features dentists told us to look for while shopping: a pressure sensor, a timer, a brush head replacement reminder and adjustable brushing settings. Its battery lasts for about 21 days before you need to recharge it, and it comes with a travel case and charging stand.

Byoma Moisturizing Gel-Cream

I prefer using a gel-cream moisturizer like this one in the morning because it’s lightweight and absorbs into my skin quickly, letting me layer sunscreen and makeup on top almost immediately. Byoma’s fragrance- and oil-free cream is formulated with hydrating ingredients like ceramides and niacinamide, as well as soothing green tea to reduce redness. Iris USA 6-Pack 54-Quart Storage Bins with Lids

Organize your home, dorm, garage or shed with these clear plastic storage bins, each of which comes with a lid that has locking latches for an extra secure seal. The bins come in a pack of six, have a roomy 54-quart capacity and have pull handles, making them convenient to pick up, pull out and move around.

I bought this blender in 2021 and still swear by it today. Its sharp blades and powerful motor quickly break down frozen and room temperature ingredients, as well as crushes ice almost instantly. Why are these early October Prime Day deals worth it?

October Amazon Prime Day historically offers Prime members some of the lowest prices of the year on products across categories. These limited-time early deals give you the opportunity to shop when inventory is the highest, before the sale officially begins on October 6.

Frequently asked questions

The best deals on October Prime Day usually center around Amazon-owned technology like Echo, Fire, Blink and Ring devices, small home and kitchen appliances, beauty products, household basics and groceries. Additionally, because this sale is in the fall, it unofficially kicks off the holiday shopping season, so brands discount popular gifts like gift cards and toys, as well as Advent calendars and themed decor. You’ll also be able to shop Amazon’s Lightning Deals during the event: They’re lucrative flash sales that give shoppers a short window to purchase limited quantities of specific products.

Products across all categories are generally cheaper on October Prime Day, with many brands discounting products by over 20 percent. Prices on tech, household essentials and groceries, small home and kitchen appliances, beauty products, giftable items and holiday decor tend to drop the most overall.

October Prime Day 2026 is from October 6 to 7. It starts at 12 a.m. PT on October 6 — that’s 3 a.m. ET, 2 a.m. CT and 1 a.m. MT. It ends at 11:59 p.m. PT on October 7 — that’s October 8 at 2:59 a.m. ET, 1:59 a.m. CT and 12:59 a.m. MT.

Yes, October Prime Day is actually worth it, so long as you shop strategically. The sale offers some of the lowest prices of the year on products across categories, giving you a chance to start shopping for holiday gifts, upgrade outdated or broken items and restock your favorite basics, like skin care, cleaning supplies and groceries. But ultimately, deals are only worth it if you actually need something, so plan your spending to prevent making impulse purchases.
