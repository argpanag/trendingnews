The Final Fantasy 7 Remake series is getting a $500 Japan-only collector’s edition | VGC

TheTools · 03.09.2026 16:24 · source · permalink

The FF7 Remake Series Complete Edition includes displays for all three games

Square Enix has announced a $500 Final Fantasy 7 Remake series collector’s edition, featuring display stands for all three games in the series.

Announced on Thursday alongside a new FF7 Revelation trailer and confirmation of a release date, the 77,777 yen (£380) ‘Complete Edition’ includes the Cloud, Zack, and Sephiroth statues included with the Collector’s Edition available in the West, plus a plethora of extra goods.

Notably, it includes all three games in the remake trilogy – Remake, Rebirth, and Revelation – plus Steelcases for each game, and acrylic display stands featuring dummy discs to display.

The stand for Final Fantasy VII Revelations will have a serial number printed on it.

The Japan-only edition will also include a Polygon Cloud keychain and the goods included in the Western Collector’s Edition (art book, soundtrack, Magic: The Gathering card, digital goods, and expansion pass).

The Complete Edition is now available for pre-order on the Japan Square Enix store in limited quantities.

In the West, Final Fantasy Revelation is getting digital Premium Plus and Premium Editions, as well as a physical Deluxe Edition.

Square Enix confirmed Final Fantasy 7 Revelation’s release date during Thursday’s PlayStation State of Play live stream.

A new trailer confirmed that the final entry will release on April 8, 2027. As previously announced, FF7 Revelation will release for PS5, Xbox, Nintendo Switch 2, and PC simultaneously.

Final Fantasy VII Revelation will introduce a new dynamic character-switching feature that allows players to instantly swap between party members while exploring the world, Square Enix said. Traversal has also been upgraded through the addition of a grappling gun that will allow players to quickly scale rooftops.

“Set in a vast open world realized on an unprecedented scale, Final Fantasy VII Revelation gives players new opportunities to explore the planet via the iconic Highwind airship,” Square Enix said.

“Exploration extends beyond the Highwind, as players can once again rely on their trusty chocobo companion Piko, who this time serves as both a mount and combat ally.”

Following the State of Play live stream, director Naoki Hamaguchi has announced that Final Fantasy 7 Revelation will get story DLC focusing on Sephiroth and Vincent.
