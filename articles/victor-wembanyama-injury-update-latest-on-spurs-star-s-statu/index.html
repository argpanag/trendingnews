Victor Wembanyama Injury Update, Latest on Spurs Star's Status and Earliest Return Date Possible

TheTools · 24.11.2026 05:15 · source · permalink

San Antonio Spurs star Victor Wembanyama missed Monday's game against the Sacramento Kings because of back tightness.

Per ESPN's Anthony Slater, Wembanyama is expected to be back for Wednesday's matchup against the Portland Trail Blazers. De'Aaron Fox also missed Monday's game because of a right shin contusion, but he is expected to be back Wednesday as well.

The injury comes as Wembanyama is putting up 29.9 points, 12.3 rebounds, 3.2 assists and 3.9 blocks per game, and the Spurs are sitting at 14-3 on the season.

The 22-year-old Wembanyama is coming off the best season of his young career, putting up 25.0 points, 11.5 rebounds, 3.1 blocks and a steal per game on his way to becoming the NBA's first unanimous Defensive Player of the Year.

He helped lead San Antonio to its best record since 2015-16, and the Spurs won the Western Conference Finals before losing to the New York Knicks in the NBA Finals.

The Spurs have every intention of getting back to the Finals this year, but their success depends almost entirely on Wembanyama staying healthy.

Wembanyama played 71 games as a rookie before missing the second half of the 2024-25 campaign after he developed deep vein thrombosis in his right shoulder. Luckily, that condition didn't follow him into the 2025-26 campaign, and he was able to play 64 games.

Wembanyama is already on his way to becoming an all-time great, and injuries are perhaps the only thing standing between him and the Hall of Fame. He'll look to bounce back from his recent soreness swiftly as he tries to lead the Spurs to more success.
