Rubber Tapper Finds Missing Toddler Safe After Overnight Search in Kuala Krai - MCI Group

TheTools · 23.11.2026 09:02 · source · permalink

KUALA KRAI, Nov 23 — The parents of a 20-month-old boy who was found safe this morning after going missing since Sunday afternoon broke down in tears of relief and gratitude, thanking Malaysians for their prayers and support throughout the search.

According to Sinar Harian (SH), Muhammad Irsyad Hakim Mohammad Khairul Najmi was found at about 10.20am in a hilly rubber plantation near Kampung Bechah Salak here.

His father, Mohammad Khairul Najmi Mohd Rosli, 26, said he had barely slept since learning of his son’s disappearance, having rushed back from Bandar Jengka, Pahang, and joined the search effort through the night.

“Since yesterday, I’ve been searching for my son and haven’t been able to sleep. Alhamdulillah, a rubber tapper found him safe.

“We are so relieved. It is through everyone’s prayers that our son was found,” he told SH at the family’s home, visibly emotional.

Mohammad Khairul Najmi, a lorry driver, said his son was taken to hospital for precautionary checks following his ordeal in the forested rubber plantation.

The family reside in Bandar Jengka, Pahang, but had travelled to Kampung Bechah Salak, Nur Fatihah’s hometown, for a week-long visit.

Nur Fatihah, 24, said she had arrived at the village at about 1pm on Sunday after a nine-hour drive. Tired from the journey, she bathed Muhammad Irsyad Hakim before letting him play in the yard with his 16-year-old aunt while she took a rest, SH reported.

When she woke up, the toddler was nowhere to be found, she said, adding that the teenager had also dozed off. The family later found only the child’s pacifier outside the house and were unable to locate him despite searching the surrounding area.

“He’s still not able to speak properly and only says ‘mama’ and ‘papa’. He’s not familiar with this area and would usually become frightened and cry if he wandered too far from us.

“I don’t know how he managed to go so far this time, but thankfully he was found safe,” she said.

Nur Fatihah said the family was immensely grateful that the youngest of their two children had been found alive after an anxious search that drew widespread public attention and support.
