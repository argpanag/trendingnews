Hooker Codie Taylor will captain the All Blacks in the first Test of the Bledisloe Cup series and is joined in the front row by props Ethan de Groot and Fletcher Newell.

Lock Scott Barrett returns to the starting line-up for the first time since November 2025, joined by Josh Lord in the second row. Tupou Vaa'i, Peter Lakai and Wallace Sititi make up the loose forward trio.

In the backs, Cam Roigard will start at halfback, while Damian McKenzie starts at first five-eighth for the first time this season. Quinn Tupaea returns from injury to join Jordie Barrett in the midfield. Leroy Carter and Will Jordan will play on the wings, while  Beauden Barrett starts at fullback for his 150th match for the All Blacks (he has played 145 Tests and four games so far).

Hooker Asafo Aumua and props George Bower and Pasilio Tosi provide front row cover from the bench, alongside loose forwards Shannon Frizell and Anton Segner, halfback Kyle Preston, and outside backs Rieko Ioane and Josh Moorby.

MATCH FACTS

  • The All Blacks and Australia have met 181 times, with New Zealand winning 128, Australia 45 and eight drawn. The All Blacks have won the last 11 Tests between the sides, with Australia's most recent win coming in 2020.
  • New Zealand has held the Bledisloe Cup since 2003, and has won 51 Bledisloe Cup series to Australia's 12, with one drawn.
  • The two sides have met 34 times at Eden Park, with the All Blacks winning 30 and Australia four. Australia's most recent win at the ground was 22-9 on 6 September 1986.
