Hallman Elementary School closed after 2-alarm fire in north Salem

TheTools · 18.09.2026 21:36 · source · permalink

Hallman Elementary School is closed after a 2-alarm fire late on the evening of Sept. 17 damaged the building in north Salem.

According to PulsePoint, at last 14 units responded to the blaze, which was reported at 10:06 p.m.

Salem-Keizer Public Schools spokesperson Aaron Harada said in an email district safety and maintenance crews also responded.

It was not immediately clear what caused the fire or the extent of the damage.

Harada said the district will continue to evaluate the condition of the building and inform families about school on Sept. 21.

The school covers grades K-5 and has approximately 328 students, Harada said.
