Antonio Ferreira left off OL teamsheet after flashpoint with Matteo Guendouzi

Antonio Ferreira was left off OL's teamsheet against Le Havre on Saturday.

L'Équipe reports that the club sanctioned the goalkeeping coach after a midweek flashpoint with Matteo Guendouzi.

OneFootball Videos

The incident followed the Champions League play-off second leg against Fenerbahçe, which ended 1-2. Ferreira tried to rush at the France international after multiple provocations towards the crowd as the Turkish club sealed qualification.

Rui Faria, Ferreira's assistant who arrived this summer to strengthen the goalkeeping staff, led the goalkeepers' warm-up and was listed on the teamsheet.

The Le Havre match formed the second round of Ligue 1.
