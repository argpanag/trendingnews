William Dale "Bill" Thomas, age 70, passed away at CHI Mery Hospital on September 19, 2026.

He was born August 2, 1956, to the late Donald and Beverly (Rose) Thomas in Omaha, NE. Bill had proudly served his country in the United States Army and was a career security officer. He also served on the Carter Lake Volunteer Fire Department.

In addition to his parents, he was preceded in death by his siblings, Joey Thomas, Donald Thomas, Cindy Robles, and Jimmy Thomas.

Bill is survived by his wife of many years, Anna Thomas; daughters, Lisa Marie Thomas and Melissa Jean Thomas; sister, Violet Garcia; grandchildren, Natalya Trovato, Kaiden Trovato, and Savannah Trovato; nieces, nephews, and cousins; a host of other family and friends.

Funeral Service will be held at 3:00 PM at Hoy Kilnoski Funeral Home on Tuesday, September 29, 2026. Visitation will be during the hour prior to the service. The family will direct memorials.
