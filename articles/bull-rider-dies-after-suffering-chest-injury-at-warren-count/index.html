Bull rider dies after suffering chest injury at Warren County stables, police say

TheTools · 25.10.2026 16:18 · source · permalink

WARREN COUNTY, Ohio (WXIX) -A 35-year-old man died Saturday night after being injured by a bull during a rodeo event in Warren County, according to police.

Clearcreek Township police and EMS responded around 7:41 p.m. to Fox Hollow Stables, located at 1909 E. State Route 73, for a report of a rider injured during the venue’s Saturday night rodeo.

Authorities identified the rider as Jason Blake Saylor. Police say Saylor suffered a traumatic chest injury after being kicked or stepped on by a bull. Although he was wearing chest protection, he still sustained severe injuries to his chest, officers said.

Medics treated Saylor at the scene before taking him to Atrium Medical Center, where he was pronounced dead, police said.

The Fox Hollow Stable released the following statement:

Clearcreek Township police are investigating the incident as an accidental death.

Investigators are asking anyone with cell phone video, recordings, or additional information about the incident to call police dispatch at 513-695-2525, or contact Detective Sgt. Kevin Barton at 937-748-1267 during regular business hours.
