Big Brother's Philip and Rochelle clash with Tiffany Pollard: "She seems absolutely vile"

TheTools · 28.10.2026 15:31 · source · permalink

"Nasty through and through."

Big Brother has previewed Tiffany Pollard's first scenes as she re-enters the house – and it seems she's already causing plenty of drama. The show legend was confirmed to have joined the ongoing series as a special guest yesterday.

The show has now released a preview of her first scenes, as she declares to the assembled: "New York is in the motherf**king House!"

While things start lightheartedly, even jokingly referring to Samuel as "Harry Potter", tensions start to rise, as over dinner she declares: "I have the floor right now."

"Let me shut the f**k up," Yasmin sarcastically responds.

"You've barely seen the lady," Lynx notes, as Rochelle is adamant: "Don't want to."

There's also issues between Tiffany and Philip, as the latter declares that he's been left "very upset" by Tiffany, huffing off as he calls her "f**king nasty through and through".

"That was some bullshit and I'm the House Guest, I just got here!" Tiffany then states.

Fans had previously expressed their excitement to see Tiffany in the house, one correctly predicting: "I think she will have a massive fight with Philip. I can’t wait for the chaos. House of horror indeed."

"Tiffany is gonna shake that house up & hopefully humble some people & put them in their place especially Rochelle & Philip & Rhianna & Omar the vile gossip group! I can't wait!" another added, while a third suggested she would bring some "madness" to the show.

Elsewhere, the show has confirmed its latest nominations results, with Rochelle, Rhianna, Samuel, Philip, and Twinks at risk of eviction.
