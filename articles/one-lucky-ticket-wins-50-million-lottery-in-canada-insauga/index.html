One lucky ticket wins $50 million lottery in Canada | insauga

TheTools · 07.11.2026 11:53 · source · permalink

By insauga Staff

Published November 7, 2026 at 6:53 am

A Lotto Max ticket holder is waking up $50 million richer after Friday night’s draw (Nov. 6), while thousands of other players across Canada are celebrating smaller lottery wins.

RELATED: The city where the $50 million lottery ticket was sold in Canada

According to the lottery results, one ticket matched all seven winning numbers to claim the massive $50 million jackpot.

The winning Lotto Max numbers for Friday, Nov. 6, 2026, were:

10, 23, 32, 43, 46, 48 and 51

The bonus number was 30.

A total of 143,069 winning selections were recorded in the main Lotto Max draw, with one lucky ticket matching all seven numbers to take home the grand prize.

There were no winners in the Maxmillions draws.

However, six players each won $100,000 through the Maxplus draws, bringing the total Maxplus prize money awarded to $600,000.

01, 03, 14, 20, 34, 38, 40

02, 12, 20, 29, 40, 45, 46

02, 13, 22, 24, 33, 39, 47

03, 08, 13, 34, 37, 43, 49

04, 15, 33, 40, 41, 43, 52

There was also excitement in the ENCORE draw, where two ticket holders each won $100,000.

The winning ENCORE number was 8833349.

No ticket matched all seven ENCORE digits to claim the $1 million top prize. However, two tickets matched the last six digits, 833349, to collect $100,000 each.

The location where the $50 million jackpot-winning ticket was purchased was not included in the available results.

The next Lotto Max draw takes place Tuesday, Nov. 10, with an estimated $10 million jackpot up for grabs.

The upcoming draw will also feature 10 additional prizes of $100,000 each, giving players more opportunities to win significant cash prizes.

Lotto Max is one of Canada’s largest national lottery games, offering multimillion-dollar jackpots and additional opportunities to win through supplementary draws.

Draws take place every Tuesday and Friday, giving players two chances each week to win major cash prizes.

In Ontario, Lotto Max is administered by the Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG), which also offers ENCORE as an optional add-on to eligible lottery purchases.

Players can check their tickets using the official OLG mobile app, at authorized lottery retailers or through OLG.ca.

OLG reminds players that lottery tickets should be signed immediately after purchase and kept in a safe place.

Lottery prizes in Ontario generally must be claimed within one year of the draw date.
