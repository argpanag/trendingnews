Is that sarcasm? Jürgen Klopp says it was ‘incredibly difficult’ to humiliate the Netherlands

TheTools · 16.11.2026 22:43 · source · permalink

    The former Liverpool FC boss might have picked up a trick from Pep Guardiola.

    Despite his country’s reputation, Germany coach Jürgen Klopp is known for a sense of humor. At both Borussia Dortmund and Liverpool FC, he would amuse fans and journalists with witty remarks and biting sarcasm. However, there are moments where Klopp seems like he is joking but is actually being serious. When he calls the 5-0 win over the Netherlands a difficult game, he means it.

    “What we’ve just witnessed was truly extraordinary,” the former LFC boss told reporters at full time (via @iMiaSanMia).“ The game was extraordinary, and incredibly difficult.”

    “Against a really good opponent, you have to find a way to win,” he added. “We had to adapt and make a few adjustments, some fairly significant ones, but we made them. We took away a lot of their strengths. And then we scored some incredible goals. That was unbelievable.”

    “It was a fantastic football match and a wonderful experience. To be able to do this here in Berlin, in front of our home crowd, in the final international game of the year, is amazing.”

    This may be a trick he picked up from Pep Guardiola, back when the legendary pair were coaching against each other in the Premier League. Pep had a tendency to hype up his opponent to make a big win seem sweeter. Whether or not Klopp also did it with Liverpool, he’s probably sincere about his feelings regarding this game. This was the Netherlands worst defeat since 1959 (ironically, a 7-0 loss at the hands of the Germans) that capped off an otherwise disappointing year for Germany with a bit of a flourish.
