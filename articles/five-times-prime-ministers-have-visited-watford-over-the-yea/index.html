Five times Prime Ministers have visited Watford over the years

TheTools · 15.11.2026 16:17 · source · permalink

Andy Burnham's visit to Watford this week was not the first time the holder of the office has come to the town in recent years.

This weekend, the Watford Observer is delving into the archives to remember several other visits you may remember.

Andy Burnham, 2026

Andy Burnham visited Watford this morning (Image: Leon Neal/PA Wire)

Starting with the current Prime Minister, who visited Watford General Hospital on Monday (November 9) and gave residents a "guarantee" that there will be no further delays to the planned rebuild of the hospital.

Burnham said that Watford has "waited long enough for its hospital" and that he wants to make sure commitments made many years ago are "finally followed through".

Work to build the new hospital - first promised by 2030 by Boris Johnson in 2019 as part of the New Hospital Programme - is currently scheduled to begin in 2032.

Rishi Sunak, 2023

Three years ago it was the turn of Britain's most recent Conservative leader to visit the hospital.

Sunak worked a night shift at Watford General but faced questions during his visit about the 'Partygate' scandal that brought down predecessor Boris Johnson.

He also visited Watford during his time as Chancellor, announcing a cost of living support package at the town's B&Q store.

Boris Johnson, 2020

Boris Johnson at Beechfieldd School in 2020

Johnson made several visits to the town during his tumultuous premiership, including this trip to Beechfield School in September 2020.

He accompanied then Watford MP Dean Russell to the Gammons Lane primary as part of his campaign to get children back into the classroom after months of pandemic-enforced online learning.

His other visits included an appearance at Watford General in 2019 and a visit to charity Watford Workshop in 2022.

David Cameron, 2012

David Cameron in Watford in 2012

Going back to the coalition era now for Cameron's visit in which he talked up the government's funding of an extension to the Metropolitan Line as examples of its support for Watford.

He made the remarks on a visit to the headquarters of the parenting website Netmums in Otterspool Way.

Gordon Brown, 2008

Prime Minister Gordon Brown. PICTURE: Simon Jacobs.

Brown visited the town early in his ill-fated premiership, accompanying then MP Claire Ward to the opening of a new Sure Start Children's Centre in Gammons Lane.

After unveiling a gold plaque, he chatted with mothers, cuddled babies and met parents-to-be inside the centre.

He said: “I have come to visit Watford today to meet mothers and children and to see one of our Sure Start programmes.

“I was able to talk to some mothers while I was there and then I have been able to come here to make sure people can see we are doing everything we can tackle the global economic problems we are facing at the moment.”
