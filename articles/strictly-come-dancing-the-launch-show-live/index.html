We will always love you, Dolly

TV viewers can now flip to BBC2 for Dolly Parton Night, head to BBC4 for French crime drama Paris Police 1910 or stay on BBC1 for new John Bishop-hosted gameshow Wisdom Of The Crowd. At 10pm, the mighty Jamie Demetriou of Stath Lets Flats fame is guest host of Saturday Night Live UK on Sky One

Film-wise, Braveheart just got underway on Film4, as did Total Recall on Sky Mix and Kind Hearts and Coronets on Great TV. There’s Shang-Chi & The Legend Of The Ten Rings (9.15pm on Channel 4) or Made In Dagenham, starring Strictly’s very own Jaime Winstone (11.40pm on BBC1). Altogether now: “They may take our lives but they’ll never take our freedooooom!”

Jojo Radebe? FOMO Radebe, more like

The pro-turned-presenter is clearly missing being out there on the dancefloor but Johannes promises we’ll get to see him in group numbers and the like as the series progresses. Good, because he’s a helluva hoofer to watch.

Did it take three to tango?

The biggest talking point this year isn’t on the dancefloor, of course, it’s behind the mic. So what did we make of our e all-new presenting trio?

It will take some getting used to, of course, but I thought it worked pretty well. Emma Willis seemed completely at home and smoothly steered the ship. Johannes Radebe was a legend and his understanding of the contest from a pro’s perspective will prove invalauble. Josh Widdicombe was enthusiastic and entertaining, although the loud laughing into his mic might need to be turned down.

At least that’s their first-night nerves out of the way. An even sterner test will come next weekend, of course, as we go live for the first time. No pressure, you three. But a promising start.

Ginger Neil left on the shelf

No celebrity partner for Neil Jones again. Sadface. The Ginger Jiggler will be on the subs’ bench alongside three of the new pros. They’ll be ensemble dancers and provide injury cover. And in Neil’s case, of course, elbow his way to the front every time there’s a camera nearby. Don’t go changing, my sweet auburn prince.

Bethany Antonio’s partnership with Nancy Xu is an intriguing one.

Melanie Walters, Shaun Wright-Phillips, Sarah Storey and Graeme Hall should also be fun to watch.

Sparkly scouting report

Early days, of course but on tonight’s evidence, who do we predict might dance deep into the contest? I rather liked the look of Lacey Turner, Tabby Stoecker, Cach Mercer, Dani Dyer, Will Best, Jaime Winstone and Lawrence Robb.

As the sparkly confetti settles and the newly formed couples bond over a carton of Ribena Toothkind backstage, stay with us for analysis, reaction and a round-up of your comments.

Credits roll

We wondered how the traditional sign-off of “Staaaaart/Keeeeep dancing” would work with three presenters, rather than two. Well, now we have our answer. They cuddled up together in a throuple. Sweet. I’m just glad they kept it.

Well, that was short-lived. Not so much of a crowd scene as a brief bit of frugging.

Class of 2026 hit the floor for first time

As per launch show tradition, proceedings climax with all 15 couples cramming onto the dancefloor for a slightly chaotic group routine.

Your most eagle-eyed analytical stares are required. We’re about to get our first glimpse of the new crop’s hoofing abilities.

Same Strictly but different

Don’t fret, though, not everything is a-changing this series. We might have new presenters, new pros, a new logo and a new-look spin-off show, but there are no new themed weeks.

The BBC has confirmed the return of Movies, Icons, Hallowe’en, Blackpool and Musicals, as well as Instant Dance Challenge, which debuted to a thumbs-up last year. The set and judging panel are also the same. A few things will be reassuringly familiar, at least.

It Takes Two gets a revamp too

It’s not just the main show that’s had a spruce-up and new hosts. Sister show Strictly: It Takes Two has also had a glow-up.

This year it will be broadcast from Strictly’s home at Elstree Studios with Ellie Taylor - comedian and 2022 contestant (partnered by Johannes, no less) - taking over as presenter. It starts a week on Monday 28th September at 6.30pm on BBC2.

It was announced today that Ellie will also take over Emma Willis’ Saturday lunchtime show on Radio 2 this autumn, while Emma is on Strictly duty. Clear as mud? Goodo.

Honk the Dave Arch horn

The stalwart Strictly bandleader - black tie on, baton in hand, Winkleman restraining order expired – finally gets a shout-out. Phew. The #justicefordavearch campaign can be retired.

Couples on the couch

Another batch of pair-grillings in the choreographic crow’s nest. Chris Appleton has promised Amy Dowden her hair will get bigger every week.

All 15 couples are now complete

So our full Strictly 2026 line-up is confirmed. Which pro-celebrity pairings do you like the look of? And who are the early contenders for the sequin-handled wooden spoon?

Fun fact: Will Best won Celebrity Mastermind last year with the specialist subject of… Danny Dyer. Wonder if this will make things awkward backstage with his daughter Dani?

It’s Will and Dianne!

Strictly studio, this is TV presenter Will Best. You’re not-quite-live on BBC1, please do not swear. Who will be his Big Ballroom housemate?

By process of elimination, the co-host of Big Brother and the Hits Radio breakfast show is coupled with Australian redhead Dianne Buswell. Three-time finalist Di was out early with compatriot Stefan Dennis last year. She might fare better with Will, who looks like he could be a decent dancer. Simply the Best, etc.
