Walang Pasok: Class suspensions on Thursday, Sept. 3

TheTools · 02.09.2026 23:42 · source · permalink

[Updated 10:43 p.m., Sept. 2, 2026]

MANILA, Philippines — In-person classes were suspended on Thursday, Sept. 3, in some areas due to continuous rains brought by the southwest monsoon, or habagat.

Below are the following areas with class suspensions:

Benguet

  • Atok – no face-to-face classes (preschool to senior high school); public and private schools; shift to alternative modes of learning; until Sept. 4

Pangasinan

  • Bugallon- no face-to-face classes; public and private schools
  • Mangaldan – no face-to-face classes, all levels, public and private, shift to alternative modes of learning
  • San Carlos City – no face-to-face classes, all levels, public and private, shift to alternative modes of learning
  • Aguilar – no face-to-face classes; public and private schools
  • Binmaley – no face-to-face classes; public and private schools

Bulacan

  • Calumpit – no face-to-face classes; public and private schools

Pampanga

  • Apalit – no face-to-face classes; public and private schools
  • San Simon – no face-to-face classes; public and private schools; until Sept. 4
  • Sta. Ana – no face-to-face classes; public and private schools; until Sept. 5
  • San Fernando City – no face-to-face classes; public and private schools
  • Minalin – no face-to-face classes; all levels, public and private; shift to alternative modes of learning
  • Guagua – no face-to-face classes; all levels, public and private schools
  • Sto. Tomas – no face-to-face classes; public and private schools

The Philippine Atmospheric, Geophysical and Astronomical Services Administration reported in its 11 a.m. weather advisory that the southwest monsoon will continue to bring cloudy skies and rain over Metro Manila, the rest of Luzon and Visayas until Thursday afternoon. /apl /das
