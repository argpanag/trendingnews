Google Birthday 2026: 10 Things You Probably Didn’t Know About Google, From Its Original Name To The First Google Doodle

TheTools · 27.09.2026 05:13 · source · permalink

Google has become one of the most recognisable names on the internet, but the company has a fascinating history that goes well beyond its familiar search page. From its original name to the story behind Google Doodles, several details about the technology giant may surprise users.

As Google Birthday 2026 is observed on September 27, here are 10 interesting facts about the company and its early journey.

1. Was Google Originally Called BackRub?

Yes. Google’s origins can be traced to a Stanford University research project called BackRub. Larry Page and Sergey Brin developed the project while working on ways to analyse relationships between webpages.

2. Who Founded Google?

Google was founded by Larry Page and Sergey Brin. The pair met at Stanford University and eventually collaborated on the search technology that became the foundation of Google.

3. Why Was It Named Google?

The name Google is linked to “googol,” a mathematical term representing the number 1 followed by 100 zeros. The name reflected the founders’ ambition to organise an enormous amount of information.

4. When Did Google Become A Company?

Google was incorporated as a company in 1998. Its development had already begun several years earlier through Page and Brin’s Stanford research.

5. Where Did Google Begin?

Google’s story began at Stanford University in California. The university environment played an important role in the development of the search technology that eventually became Google’s core product.

6. When Was The First Google Doodle Created?

The first Google Doodle appeared in 1998. It was created by Page and Brin to indicate that they were attending the Burning Man festival. The idea eventually evolved into the elaborate Google Doodles users see today.

7. What Is A Google Doodle?

A Google Doodle is a special alteration of Google’s homepage logo used to celebrate or highlight notable people, events, anniversaries, cultural occasions and achievements. Over the years, Doodles have featured subjects from science and history to art, sport and major cultural events.

8. Did Google Always Have Its Familiar Logo?

No. Google’s visual identity has changed several times since the company began. The logo has been redesigned over the years while retaining the company’s recognisable lettering and colour combination.

9. Was Search Always Google’s Only Product?

No. Google eventually expanded far beyond its original search engine. Its broader ecosystem has included products and services such as Gmail, Google Maps, Chrome, Android and YouTube. This expansion helped transform Google from a search company into a major technology ecosystem.

10. Why Is Google Birthday Celebrated On September 27?

September 27 is widely recognised as Google’s birthday. However, the company has used different dates for birthday celebrations during its history. Google has also marked the occasion with special Doodles and other homepage features.

Why Is Google’s History Still Interesting?

From a Stanford research project called BackRub to a technology company with products used around the world, Google’s evolution has been substantial. Its story also shows how an idea developed by two university researchers eventually became one of the defining internet companies of the modern era.
