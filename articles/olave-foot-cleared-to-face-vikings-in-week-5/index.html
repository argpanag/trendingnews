    Olave (foot) cleared to face Vikings in Week 5

    Chris Olave

    NO Wide Receiver #12
    Player Stats

    Chris Olave (foot) is not on the final injury report for Week 5 against the Vikings.

    The Saints gave Olave a rest day on Wednesday. He then logged a limited practice on Thursday, listed with a new foot injury, and was reportedly seen sporting a walking boot after practice. ESPN’s Kat Terrell notes, however, that “walking boots are often a precautionary measure.” The fact that the team cleared him right away is reassuring. He can be treated as a low-end WR1, as usual. Olave later told Terrell that his “foot got a little bruised in Thursday’s practice, but he’s good.”
