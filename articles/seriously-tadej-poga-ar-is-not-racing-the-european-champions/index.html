Seriously, Tadej Pogačar is not racing the European championships - Canadian Cycling Magazine

TheTools · 23.09.2026 10:40 · source · permalink

Rumours about some sort of miracle comeback probably due to outdated paperwork

We get it, cycling fans—it sucks that Tadej Pogačar crashed out of the Vuelta a España—which would have most likely been a dominant win—and then in fine form to defend his world championships crown, his Euro win and Il Lombardia.

After the crash, it was unclear the extent of his injuries. It was enough for him to abandon the Vuelta, but with a month before the worlds, was there still hope?

The crash was one of those fluke things that happens on a relatively standard day. At the time, the current (but not for long) world champ wore maillot rojo, with almost four minutes on Enric Mas. Even though it was only Stage 8, it seemed he already had the Spanish Grand Tour in the bag.

The medical update

But after the crash, then came the news from his UAE Team. A broken collarbone and a fracture to his C5 vertebra, and a bunch of bruises. The collarbone wasn’t the most concerning, but the C5 business was serious stuff. If you reinjure that, you can legit do some long-term damage to your body. And sure enough, on Sept. 5 the team announced the news, along with a contract extension, all the way to 2032.

“Following his crash at La Vuelta, Tadej will not return to racing this season as he focuses fully on his recovery and rehabilitation. Right now, the most important thing is to recover properly and give my body the time it needs,” it read.

The rumors based on…not much

But then Cycling Twitter™ revved up its motor when his name was still on the Euro championships—which take place after the worlds—in Slovenia. That was the double bummer of his crash—not only was he forced to skip Montreal, but also a race in his home country. Plus, his partner, fellow pro Urška Žigart, also crashed a few weeks later, re-injuring her jaw and was also sidelined. Suddenly, the season for cycling’s royal couple was totally sidelined.

Although it still seemed totally implausible that Pogi would race the Euros, people wondered. That’s why a UAE press officer had to step in and say, “c’mon now.”

“This is probably an old list. Although he is training slightly indoors again, he is not supposed to race this year,” was the word to Wielerflits.

Pogačar did say he was planning to come to Montreal to support Žigart and his Slovenian team. With Žigart out, it’s unclear if he will—but if you’re in Quebec and see a dude with a bucket hat, Scicon glasses and a Slovenian flag…maybe take a second look. You never know.
