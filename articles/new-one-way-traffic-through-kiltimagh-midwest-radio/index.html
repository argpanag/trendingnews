A new one way temporary traffic system is being introduced through the centre of Kiltimagh this morning.

The new system comes into effect at 10am and will run until the end of this month.

The main streets that will be impacted are  the R322 James Street – it will flow in a west direction only and  the R323 Thomas Street will flow in an east direction only.

Clear directional signage will be in place.

Motorists are asked to obey all traffic management signs and drive with care as road users adapt to the temporary one way system.
