Southampton flight forced to turn back after drone activity near runway

TheTools · 02.11.2026 07:33 · source · permalink

A flight from Southampton was forced to turn back after drones disrupted Jersey Airport.

The Loganair service, LM558, was heading to Jersey on Friday evening when unauthorised drone activity prevented flights from landing.

The plane circled over the Channel before heading back towards Southampton at about 9.05pm.

FlightAware records show it landed back at Southampton Airport at 9.31pm, having taken off at 8.05pm.

Three flights travelling to Jersey from the UK were turned back following drone activity near a runway.

The others were a British Airways flight from Heathrow and an easyJet service from Gatwick.

The easyJet flight eventually reached Jersey shortly before midnight, after the airport stayed open late for passengers.

States of Jersey Police said the first report was received at about 8.20pm, with reported drone activity continuing until about 10.30pm.

Officers and other agencies searched several areas in the west of the island, but police said no drone operator had yet been identified and enquiries were continuing.

The force said the reported activity was within restricted airspace and active flight paths, causing significant disruption to airport operations and passengers.

Jersey Police have appealed for witnesses who may have seen people operating drones near St Peter, La Pulente, Watersplash, El Tico, L'Etacq and Jubilee Hill.

Anyone who saw drones or people who appeared to be operating them, noticed potentially connected people or vehicles in unusual locations, or has dashcam, CCTV, doorbell or mobile phone footage has been asked to contact police.

The force said: "Even if you believe what you saw was insignificant, please let us know."
