White House launches retro games on border security, MAHA and Trump Accounts

WASHINGTON (TNND) — The White House debuted a new “Arcade” page Thursday featuring five retro-style video games that promote priorities of President Donald Trump’s administration.

“Build the Wall,” a “Tetris”-style game, tasks players with stacking blocks to “Protect the border from the coming horde.” In “Rio Run,” modeled after the mobile game “Snake,” a character resembling Trump patrols the Rio Grande while collecting icons representing potential border crossers.

“Supply Line,” which resembles the arcade game “Tapper,” challenges players to sort school lunch items and reject those that do not meet “Make America Healthy Again” standards.

“Flappy Bill” has players guide an eagle carrying a bill across the National Mall. “Trump Savings Tycoon” challenges players to collect money floating through the air to “Fill your kids’ Trump Accounts.”

Trump Accounts are federally backed investment accounts intended to help children build long-term wealth.

In June, the Treasury Department said more than 5.5 million Trump Accounts had been opened, including 1.4 million eligible for a $1,000 federal contribution.

The page also teases a sixth, unidentified game as “Coming Soon.”
