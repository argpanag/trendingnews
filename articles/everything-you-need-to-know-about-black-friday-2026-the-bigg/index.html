All seasoned shoppers know that waiting for a special event is the best way to score some serious savings. And while we've seen some deep discounts throughout the calendar year, we still have one final shopping event in 2026 to gear up for: Black Friday.

Delivering record-low prices and some of the best deals you'll see all year, Black Friday begins the day after Thanksgiving and runs through the whole holiday weekend. What Is Black Friday?

Black Friday marks the first official day of the holiday shopping season, and it always falls on the Friday after Thanksgiving. It began way back in the 1950s, when newspapers started using it as a loathsome term to describe the holiday weekend traffic. But people also started noticing major markdowns at their favorite retailers around that time, so by the 1980s, it was widely associated with prices instead of packed roadways. After that, the "black" in Black Friday referred to the black ink you'd see on a positive book balance.

But once online shopping started to gain real traction, retailers coined "Cyber Monday" to keep the savings going after the long weekend. But now that internet novelty has calmed down over the last decade or so, people don't use it as much. "Black Friday" now typically encompasses the entire extra-long weekend of deals.

When Is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday starts at 12:00 p.m. on November 27, 2026. It's the last Friday of the month this year, which marks the start of the shopping season much later than usual. That means it's closer to the gift-giving holidays than normal, which might light a fire under your shopping urgency.

When you combine Thanksgiving Day, Black Friday, Thanksgiving Weekend, and Cyber Monday, it's often referred to as "The Turkey 5." That means you'll have five days to maximize your savings, whether that's on gifts for your loved ones or splurging for yourself.

When Exactly Do Black Friday Deals Start?

Because Black Friday isn't owned by any one retailer, every business has a different plan for it. Some retailers might go all out starting at midnight on Friday, and others will wait until Monday to dole out some of their best deals. However, one thing you can count on is that they all sprinkle out sales before Thanksgiving is even close. Sometimes, we'll see price drops starting in late October!

Major retailers like Amazon, Best Buy, and Walmart typically roll out their markdowns on Wednesday before Thanksgiving (November 25), but many others start their sales that Monday (November 23). But if you keep tabs on your favorite websites, you'll start seeing banners and teasers for Black Friday deals as early as November 1.

There will be plenty of deals popping up, but don't worry about keeping track of them all to score the steepest savings—we'll be tracking them to only bring you the best of the best.

Where Should I Shop On Black Friday for the Best Deals?

While there's no way to tell (yet) which retailers will have the very best sales, it's safe to assume that larger companies like Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, and Target will have the best deals during the sales event, as well as top tech manufacturers like Apple, Dell, HP, Lenovo, and more.

What Time Do Stores Open on Black Friday?

The internet is never closed, so even if you don't take advantage of early Black Friday deals, you can log on at the stroke of midnight to start your shopping spree (as long as the sales are already live). All this depends on the retailer, of course.

But if you're feeling ambitious after your turkey dinner, you can still run to a brick-and-mortar store to find deals in person. While many retailers set special schedules for Black Friday shopping, there's no guarantee that a specific store will be open. Make sure to call or check ahead of time.

Will Amazon Have Black Friday Sales?

Absolutely. In fact, Black Friday is one of the best times to shop for deals on Amazon—sometimes even better than Prime Day. Black Friday Week usually kicks off at midnight on Thanksgiving and runs through Cyber Monday (Nov. 30), but the deepest discounts will likely be on Thanksgiving and Black Friday.

Just like Prime Day, you'll see up to 50% off a wide selection of Amazon devices like Blink security cameras, Fire TVs, Echo smart speakers, and more. But you can also expect massive markdowns from big brands like Anker, Bose, Canon, Dyson, JBL, Samsung, and dozens more.

You don't have to be a Prime member to shop Amazon's Black Friday sale. However, you can sign up for a free 30-day trial to access the very best prices and cancel later (we won't judge you). But based on our assessment, we actually think the subscription is well worth it (billed at $14.99 per month or $139 per year).

Will Apple Have Black Friday Sales?

Apple may be reluctant to offer sales throughout the calendar year, but it does have its own Black Friday and Cyber Monday shopping event, both online and in-store. It doles out discounts on popular products like iPhones, MacBooks, AirPods, Apple Watches, and more, which can all get pretty pricey without a deal on your side. So if you need a new Apple device and can wait until the Turkey 5, it'd be a very timely time to buy—perhaps even the best time all year.

Note one thing, though: it's unlikely that any of the newest models (like the iPhone 18 Pro or the iPhone Duo) will be on sale during Black Friday.

Will Best Buy Have Black Friday Sales?

It sure will, and it's one of the first places we check for the deepest discounts during the holiday weekend. Best Buy is likely to offer major deals on big-ticket items like laptops, consoles, TVs, and gaming rigs, as well as smaller devices like phones and tablets.

The retailer has already made plans to make this year's Thanksgiving sale "even more memorable" than last year's, so mark your calendars for Nov. 27, when it officially kicks off. But with Best Buy's Doorbuster deals, you'll see great prices on tech dropping all month long leading up to the event.

Will Walmart Have Black Friday Sales?

If there's one thing you can guarantee, it's that Walmart will be locked and loaded for Black Friday sales. Last year, the retailer rolled out its deals in three different phases weeks before Thanksgiving even arrived. Walmart+ owners also usually get sneak peeks and special promotions, so stay tuned for that gossip to roll in as we get closer and closer to the Turkey 5.

Will Target Have Black Friday Sales?

Target has yet to announce its formal Black Friday game plan, but the popular retail giant usually delivers massive discounts across dozens of categories during the Thanksgiving sale. Since we're most concerned with tech deals, we'll keep our eyes peeled for markdowns on small appliances and electronics like robot vacuums, TVs, speakers, headphones, and more.

A special note is Target's Holiday Price Match Guarantee, which matches the lowest price on items purchased between certain dates during the holiday season (usually Nov. 1 and Dec. 24).

Is Everything On Sale On Black Friday Worth It?

Sigh—no. Unfortunately, it takes some sifting to find the sweet spot between prices and products that are actually worth your dollar, especially during sales events like Black Friday, where you're bombarded with red "SALE" signs left and right. Some third parties selling on sites like Amazon and Walmart even resort to shadier tactics, like inflating prices and adding a discount to make it look like a good deal. That's not every one of them, but there's always a little fool's gold hiding among the real gems.
