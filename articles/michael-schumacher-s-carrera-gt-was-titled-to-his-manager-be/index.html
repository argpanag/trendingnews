Michael Schumacher's Carrera GT Was Titled to His Manager Because of His Ferrari Contract, and It's Headed to Mecum Kissimmee

TheTools · 23.11.2026 21:06 · source · permalink

Michael Schumacher was closing in on his seventh Formula 1 world championship in 2004, and he had a problem when it came to buying a Porsche. According to Mecum's listing for this 2004 Porsche Carrera GT, the car was ordered new for Schumacher and titled to his longtime manager, Willi Weber, because Schumacher's Ferrari contract limited him to driving Fiat Group vehicles. It crosses the block at Mecum Kissimmee on Saturday, January 16, 2027, as Lot R808.

The car is a black-on-black Carrera GT, and the contract story is why it is more interesting than its spec sheet.

A Porsche for a Ferrari driver

In 2004, Ferrari was part of the Fiat Group, and Mecum says that is the reason the Carrera GT was put in Weber's name rather than Schumacher's. The same account appears in a separate profile of the car on Elferspot, which quotes Michael Roock of Roock Sportsystem, a dealer who has handled the car. Roock says Weber Management GmbH, the company of Schumacher's manager, bought the Carrera GT and made it available to Schumacher, and that written confirmation of that arrangement is in the vehicle's history. He also says official appearances in the car were off limits.

Elferspot reports the car was delivered on April 29, 2004, by Porsche Centrum Leipzig. It reports roughly 14,200 km on the odometer, with just under half of that attributed to Weber Management's ownership. That lines up with Supercar Nostalgia's note that the car showed just under 9,000 miles when it was catalogued.

What ties it to Schumacher

Mecum lists several items it says connect the car to its famous driver:

  • Photographs documenting Schumacher driving the car

  • A Michael Schumacher "MS" vinyl insignia on the rear decklid

  • An owner's manual bearing an illustration of his racing helmet

Those are the only outward signs of the ownership, according to Elferspot. There is no flashy livery and no celebrity badge on the dash, which fits a car that was never meant to advertise who was driving it.

The Carrera GT underneath

The Carrera GT was Porsche's analog supercar, one of 1,270 built. This one is finished in black over a black leather interior with black factory magnesium center-lock wheels, a red outer accent stripe and red brake calipers. Under the rear deck sits the 5.7-liter V10 making 605 horsepower, paired with a six-speed manual and Porsche's Ceramic Composite Clutch. Porsche Ceramic Composite Brakes, a carbon-fiber monocoque and removable carbon-fiber roof panels are all part of the package. Factory books, manuals, tools and accessories are included. Mecum gives the VIN as WP0ZZZ98Z4L000116.

Elferspot adds that the car has air conditioning, a luggage set and a sound system, and that apart from ABS it has no electronic driver aids.

Why this one stands out

Celebrity ownership is common at auction, and Motorious has covered plenty of it, including Roger Waters' blue BMW M1. What separates this car is the reason it was titled the way it was. The story comes from a contractual restriction on one of the most successful drivers in Formula 1 history, rather than from a famous name on a registration. It also helps that the file reportedly includes photos of him behind the wheel and a written record of the Weber Management purchase.

It is also one of the standout consignments already confirmed for the January sale, which Motorious previewed in Mecum Previews Kissimmee 2027. Another Schumacher-era Ferrari halo car, the Enzo, was the subject of this look at a Classiche-certified 2003 example.

The details

The Carrera GT is Lot R808 at Mecum Kissimmee 2027, crossing the block on Saturday, January 16, 2027, in Kissimmee, Florida. Mecum's lot page does not list an estimate. The Schumacher connection, the Weber title arrangement and the photographs come from Mecum and from Elferspot's reporting, so prospective buyers should review the history file and paperwork in person or through Mecum before bidding.
