EA FC 27 not working on PC: Possible fixes

TheTools · 17.09.2026 23:00 · source · permalink

EA FC 27 is finally here, but many PC players are running into frustrating launch errors, sudden crashes, and black screens right after booting up. The highly anticipated football title is currently in early access, meaning day-one instability, anti-cheat glitches, and server bottlenecks are bound to happen while the developers work through initial optimization.

If your game refuses to start, freezes on the loading screen, or constantly disconnects during matches, you do not need to panic. Most of these startup bugs stem from corrupted installation files, outdated graphics drivers, or third-party software conflicts that you can resolve right at your desk.

Possible fixes for EA FC 27 not working on PC error

Before you jump into complicated troubleshooting, it helps to identify what might be triggering your specific issue. Incomplete updates, server downtime, and aggressive background overlay utilities frequently prevent the game from communicating with EA services properly.

Let's explore some possible fixes that might solve the EA FC 27 not working on PC error:

  1. Verify EA FC 27 files on Steam
  2. Make sure EA FC 27 and GPU drivers are fully updated
  3. Check the EA server status
  4. Shut down background apps
  5. Uninstall and reinstall the game

Here is a detailed explanation of each fix:

1) Verify EA FC 27 files on Steam

Corrupted or missing game files often cause immediate crashes right after you hit play. Steam includes a built-in verification tool that compares your local install with official game servers and replaces damaged data automatically.

  1. Launch your Steam client and head over to your Library.
  2. Locate and right-click on EA FC 27 in your games list, then choose Properties.
  3. Click on the Installed Files tab from the left sidebar.
  4. Select the Verify integrity of game files option and wait for Steam to scan your installation.

2) Make sure GPU drivers are fully updated

Running an outdated display driver is one of the most common causes of black screen errors and low frame rates. Both Nvidia and AMD frequently push dedicated game-ready drivers designed specifically for new football releases.

  1. Open your graphics management software, such as Nvidia GeForce Experience or AMD Software Adrenalin Edition.
  2. Navigate directly to the Drivers tab at the top of the window.
  3. Click Check for updates to scan for the latest driver release.
  4. Download and install the update, then reboot your computer before launching the game again.

3) Check the EA server status

During the busy early access launch window, server traffic spikes can kick you out of Ultimate Team or block your game from validating your account. If the title hangs indefinitely on the main menu, the root issue might be entirely on EA's end.

  1. Visit the official EA Help website to see real-time maintenance announcements.
  2. Check the official EA Sports FC communications channel on social media for outage updates.
  3. Look up third-party outage trackers like DownDetector to see if other players in your region report identical connection drops.
  4. Power cycle your own internet connection if servers are green, but you still cannot connect.

4) Shut down background apps

Background programs that utilize screen overlays or heavy monitoring tools can clash directly with the EA anti-cheat system. Disabling these extra applications frees up system memory and ensures nothing blocks the game executable.

  1. Press Ctrl + Shift + Esc on your keyboard to bring up the Windows Task Manager.
  2. Look through the Processes tab to identify non-essential utilities like Discord, MSI Afterburner, or capture software.
  3. Right-click on each unnecessary program and choose End task.
  4. Temporarily disable third-party antivirus shields that might mistakenly flag the launch files.

5) Uninstall and reinstall EA FC 27

If basic repairs and driver refreshes fail to get you onto the pitch, performing a clean reinstallation usually clears out stubborn registry bugs and corrupted caches. Installing the title on a fast solid-state drive also drastically cuts down on asset load times.

  1. Open Steam or the EA app and head to your game library.
  2. Right-click on EA FC 27, navigate to Manage, and select Uninstall.
  3. Restart your PC to ensure all temporary cache files are cleared out.
  4. Download the game again, choosing an internal SSD as your preferred install directory.

Troubleshooting launch bugs can test your patience, but walking through these simple adjustments will get you back into the match in no time. Because the game is still navigating its early access rollout, keeping your PC drivers updated and monitoring community server alerts will save you plenty of headaches down the road. If the errors persist after trying every step, reach out to official EA Help so the support team can investigate your specific setup.
