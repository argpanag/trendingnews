Latvian airline airBaltic said Monday it has filed for protection under Chapter 11 of the US Bankruptcy Code in New York as it begins a financial restructuring aimed at reducing its debt, according to a Reuters report.

The airline, which is majority-owned by the Latvian government, said flights would continue operating as scheduled throughout the court-supervised process. Existing tickets and reservations will remain valid, and customer service will continue without interruption, the company said.

The airline said it had secured a commitment from creditors for 350 million euros in debtor-in-possession financing to support its operations during the restructuring.

The creditors providing the financing include Strategic Value Partners, Barclays, Hayfin Capital Management, Morgan Stanley and Oaktree Capital Management, according to the airline.

The Chapter 11 process allows companies to continue operating while restructuring their finances under US court supervision.
