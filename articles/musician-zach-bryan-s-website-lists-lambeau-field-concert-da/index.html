GREEN BAY (WLUK) -- Another concert is headed to Lambeau Field in spring.

Country star Zach Bryan's tour lists a Tuesday, May 4 date for the next "With Heaven on Tour" concert series.

No ticket details are listed, although the website has a place for fans to register for information.

The website also lists supporting acts of Kings of Leon, Ryan Bingham and the Texas Gentleman, Alabama Shakes, Gregory Alan Isakov, Trampled by Turtles, Gabriella Rose, and Fey Fili. It does not specify if some or all would perform in Green Bay.

Green Bay is listed as the third stop, after dates at NFL stadiums in Minneapolis on April 28 and Indianapolis on May 1.

Bryan is a singer-songwriter from Oklahoma whose music is known as a blend of folk, Americana and country. He has never performed in the Green Bay area before. His most popular songs include "Something in the Orange" and "I Remember Everything."

Packers President & CEO Ed Policy has expressed the team's desire to bring more non-football events to Lambeau Field.

In May, country star Luke Combs performed at Lambeau on back-to-back nights, drawing 51,981 fans for the second night -- a stadium concert record

Lambeau is also hosting men's and women's Wisconsin Badgers hockey games on Feb. 20.
