Time to move on from LIV, says Oosthuizen

TheTools · 21.10.2026 06:26 · source · permalink

Getty

Former Open champion Louis Oosthuizen is set to become the latest player to leave LIV Golf with the South African golfer saying it was time to move on from the breakaway circuit as it grapples with an uncertain future.

Former world number one Jon Rahm is among those quitting the rebel circuit, which is fighting to emerge from Chapter 11 bankruptcy after Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF) said in April it was cutting funding.

LIV has secured investment from BC Partners Credit and is set to make a decision this month on whether it winds down or moves forward with a 2.0 model.

Oosthuizen, who was among the first golfers to join LIV and played in its inaugural event in June 2022, told the South China Morning Post that the proposed stripped-back schedule would require a lot of international travel for single events.

"I need to arrange my own schedule and look after my body," said the 44-year-old ahead of this week's Hong Kong Open.

"I want to play near one spot for three weeks at a time, not have all the big travelling for one tournament.

"I loved LIV and the team aspect, but it's time to move on," added the 2010 British Open champion.

Oosthuizen is still listed as captain of the Southern Guards GC team on the LIV website. Reuters has contacted LIV for confirmation on his departure.

According to reports in US media, players have a soft deadline of 25 October to make an official decision on whether they will stick with the circuit.
