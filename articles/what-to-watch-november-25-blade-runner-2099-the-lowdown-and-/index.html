The TV Cave Top Pick: Blade Runner 2099

Streaming on Prime Video | All Eight Episodes

Michelle Yeoh and Hunter Schafer are taking us back into the dystopian world of Blade Runner, and that casting alone has our attention. Set five decades after the events of Blade Runner 2049, the limited series follows a fugitive named Cora who assumes the identity of a Blade Runner and crosses paths with Olwen, a replicant facing her own mortality.

With Shining Girls creator Silka Luisa serving as showrunner and Ridley Scott executive producing, the series has some impressive talent behind the camera, too. Prime Video is dropping the entire season at once, so anyone looking for an excuse to avoid helping in the kitchen tomorrow officially has eight of them.

More Wednesday Highlights

The Lowdown: Ethan Hawke's crime drama wraps up its second season tonight on FX. If there's one thing this series understands, it's that digging into other people's secrets rarely ends with everyone walking away happy. We're curious to see what consequences are waiting in the finale.

Mickey's Christmas Carol: ABC is bringing the beloved 1983 animated adaptation back to broadcast television after a 23-year absence. Scrooge McDuck taking on Ebenezer Scrooge remains inspired casting, and this is an excellent excuse to introduce younger viewers to a holiday classic.

NFL Thanksgiving Eve: Netflix is making football an even bigger part of Thanksgiving with the NFL's first-ever Thanksgiving Eve matchup. The Packers face the Rams, with U2 celebrating its 50th anniversary during the halftime show. Apparently, tomorrow's football marathon wasn't enough.
