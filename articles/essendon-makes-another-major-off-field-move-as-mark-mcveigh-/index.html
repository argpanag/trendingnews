Essendon makes another major off-field move as Mark McVeigh's rebuild gathers speed

TheTools · 14.10.2026 01:45 · source · permalink

Essendon has signed Craig Vozzo to be its general manager of football operations going forward.

The role works in tandem with fellow recent off-field signing John Longmire, who was hired as the club's  general manager of performance.

Vozzo fits the billing of a former Bomber coming back to the club under this regeneration by new coach Mark McVeigh.

He was the general manager of West Coast from 2013-2021. During this time the Eagles played in two Grand Finals, winning one.

Vozzo then joined Essendon and was the CEO of the club for three years before moving into recruiting, list management and commercial projects with the Dons.

Speaking to Essendon Media, Vozzo said the excitement surrounding the club's future is palpable.

"I'm very energised and looking forward to jumping back into the football department and working with Mark and John to help create the best environment we can for our players," Vozzo said.

"Walking back into the club, I've already noticed a real sense of excitement across the whole place.

"We have plenty of hard work ahead, but we will roll up our sleeves and get to work."

Essendon CEO Tim Roberts said Vozzo and Longmire will complement each other's strengths as the Bombers start a fresh chapter in their history.

"Craig has worked closely with our list management and recruiting team throughout the season, and he's been a great support to me since I started," Roberts said.

"John's expertise is in coaching, high performance and developing players. Craig brings deep experience in list management, compliance and running a football department. Having the two of them side by side gives us a very strong combination.

"Craig did this job with great success at West Coast, and it's fantastic to have his experience full-time in such an important role."

Essendon has made sweeping changes to its off-field roles in recent weeks.

After McVeigh was hired following stints as an assistant coach at GWS and Sydney, he brought Longmire down to Melbourne to assist with the football program.

The move has been widely regarded as Essendon's best recruitment, on or off the field, in a long time.

McVeigh has looked to bring previous Bombers back into the fold, as is the way out at Tullamarine.

Former Essendon player Paul Thomas has joined the club as an assistant coach. He arrives from South Australia where he was senior coach of Central Districts in the SANFL.

Thomas was at the Bombers for one season in 2005 and played alongside McVeigh, featuring in eight games.

Essendon has also stolen highly regarded assistant coach Daniel Pratt from the Western Bulldogs.

Pratt was awarded the AFLCA Assistant Coach of the Year in 2024 and will be McVeigh's senior assistant for 2027.

Former Dons player and hard man Mark Johnson has also been targeted by the club in a part time role as tackling coach.
