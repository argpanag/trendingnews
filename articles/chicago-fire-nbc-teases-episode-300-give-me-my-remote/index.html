CHICAGO FIRE: NBC Teases Episode 300 - Give Me My Remote

TheTools · 20.10.2026 20:22 · source · permalink

October 20, 2026 by Marisa Roffman  

CHICAGO FIRE — Pictured: “Chicago Fire” Key Art — (Photo by: NBCUniversal)

CHICAGO FIRE is celebrating its 300th episode on Wednesday, November 4.

In the episode, “Three Hundred,” per the show’s official logline, “In the milestone 300th episode of the series, Firehouse 51 responds to a complex hostage situation. Cruz continues to make strides. Chief Tomlin gives Severide an impossible task.”

New CHICAGO FIRE showrunner Victor Teran previously told Give Me My Remote that the milestone was “going to be a Cruz episode.” (Original series star Joe Miñoso will be exiting this season.)

“Rather than bringing back some of our beloved characters, what we really wanted to do was center this big milestone around Cruz, and make it about him,” he said at the time. “So that’s what we did.”

CHICAGO FIRE, Wednesdays, 9/8c, NBC
