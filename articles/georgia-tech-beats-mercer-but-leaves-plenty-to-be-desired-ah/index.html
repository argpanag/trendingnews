Georgia Tech beats Mercer, but leaves plenty to be desired ahead of ACC play

TheTools · 19.09.2026 20:16 · source · permalink

Georgia Tech secured its first victory of the season Saturday against Mercer ahead of ACC play beginning next week.

The Yellow Jackets never trailed and won comfortably 44-11, but they still showed issues that hampered them in losses to Colorado and Tennessee.

Earlier this week, Tech coach Brent Key talked about building confidence after the 0-2 start. The Jackets (1-2) entered Saturday as 38.5-point betting favorites against FCS Mercer (2-2), giving them an opportunity to play more cleanly before traveling to Stanford next week for the ACC opener.

Saturday did little to ease those concerns.

Tech’s offense started with three-and-outs on two of its first three drives. Later, on the same drive, the Jackets had a touchdown overturned by review and another score wiped off the board by a holding penalty. They settled for three points.
