Kevin Can F**k Himself

TheTools · 14.09.2026 20:47 · source · permalink

Annie Murphy from Schitt’s Creek is perfectly cast in this black comedy drama that has a split personality. When we see Murphy’s Allison with her husband Kevin, the show looks and feels like a typical US mainstream sitcom, with bright lighting and canned laughter (think The King Of Queens and Everybody Loves Raymond). When she’s on her own, though, the show is more realistic, a rather bleak drama that shows the impact her ‘lovable idiot’ husband really has on his ‘long-suffering’ wife who’s the butt of all his jokes.
