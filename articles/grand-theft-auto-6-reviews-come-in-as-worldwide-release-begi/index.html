Grand Theft Auto 6: Reviews come in as worldwide release begins

TheTools · 18.11.2026 12:56 · source · permalink

Summary

  • Grand Theft Auto 6 has begun its launch, ending gamers' 13-year wait for a new instalment

  • The game launches at midnight around the world, meaning gamers in New Zealand became the first to play at 11:00 GMT

  • Initial reviews give the game a score of 99, according to Metacritic

  • Many critics are praising it as "exquisitely detailed" and "mature", although some say it has "flaws"

  • The crime game, where players can explore a fictional US state called Leonida, features the series' first playable female protagonist in a 3D setting

  • A New Zealand gamer has already finished the introduction - here are his first thoughts (spoiler in post!)

  • Warning: some posts on this page contain spoilers - they are labelled at the top

Grand Theft Auto 6 has begun its launch, ending gamers' 13-year wait for a new instalment

The game launches at midnight around the world, meaning gamers in New Zealand became the first to play at 11:00 GMT

Initial reviews give the game a score of 99, according to Metacritic

Many critics are praising it as "exquisitely detailed" and "mature", although some say it has "flaws"

The crime game, where players can explore a fictional US state called Leonida, features the series' first playable female protagonist in a 3D setting

A New Zealand gamer has already finished the introduction - here are his first thoughts (spoiler in post!)

  2. 'I stayed up to play GTA 6 - it did not disappoint'published at 12:37 GMT
    12:37 GMT

    Kieran Kelly
    Live reporter

    Spoiler alert: This post contains a few details about the game.

    New Zealander Connor Cattermoul has already completed GTA 6's introductory mission.

    The 27-year-old says it's "amazing" so far and has "beautiful" graphics, adding: "It feels really smooth - there's so many little things you can do."

    Those who have played previous instalments will know one of GTA's major appeals is its realism, which Connor says this version has maintained.

    Shortly after completing the 45-minute introduction, Connor headed straight to his character's in-game apartment and drank two beers before exploring the in-game version of Instagram.

    Connor was speaking to me at around 01:00 local time (12:00 GMT). He's still going to work tomorrow but plans to play the game every night through the weekend.

    Staying up was "pretty worth it", he says, adding that it "did not disappoint".

  3. 'It will be worth the wait,' says UK dad who took annual leave to play GTApublished at 12:30 GMT
    12:30 GMT

    Rozina Sini
    Your Voice

    Jason Waller, 56, from Bedford, has booked two days of annual leave to play Grand Theft Auto 6 this weekend.

    "I love the freedom of GTA," he says. "You can leave the missions and wander off and discover so much."

    He says he and his son have both pre-downloaded the game ahead of its UK release.

    "We are going to put in an hour or two after midnight tonight and then play all day Friday and over the weekend."

    "If they’ve done it right it will be worth the wait. I can't wait," he says.

  4. New Zealand gamer shares first look at game (spoiler-free in this post)published at 12:23 GMT
    12:23 GMT

    Andrew Rogers
    BBC Newsbeat

    Aleana Fernando, who lives in Auckland, New Zealand, gave BBC Newsbeat a first look at the game.

    So, her first impressions? Spoiler-free, of course.

    "The graphics are great. Even from the set-up and credits, it's feeling pretty good," she says.

    The 27-year-old says she’s going to play for a bit before getting some sleep ahead of work.

    She says it’s worth staying up, though, because it means so much to her for New Zealand to get the game first worldwide.

    "People sometimes forget about us, but it's nice to get a little bit of attention, especially with something this big."

  5. 'It's a once in a generation kind of game'published at 12:07 GMT
    12:07 GMT

    Simon Atkinson
    BBC News, reporting from Sydney

    I’m with YouTuber and gaming content creator Andrew Shelley - known to his one million followers and subscribers as Bombastic.

    He watched some of the GTA countdowns from New Zealand but turned off when the game began. “I want to see it fresh”, he says.

    “I'm so excited to play. It's 13 years waiting. So this is the moment. There's literally an hour to go.

    “It's a once in a generation kind of game. There's so much more to it than so many other games. The scale of the world, the density, the satire of reality.

    “I think they're just trying to capture so much in one giant game and have definitely taken the time to do so. So the anticipation of that's tremendous.”

    12:00 GMT

    Joe Coughlan
    Live editor

    More than 2.4 million people are currently watching gamers play GTA 6 on Twitch, a website where people can watch and comment on others playing video games.

    Content creators are trying a variety of ways to attract viewers, including saying they’ll be the first to complete the game, playing it with their partners and giving away merchandise.

    For comparison, the second most popular category on Twitch is creators talking without playing a video game, with 136,000 viewers. The third most popular is World of Warcraft, with about 76,000 viewers.

  7. Trying to avoid spoilers? A twitch streamer has some tipspublished at 11:54 GMT
    11:54 GMT

    Jack Gray
    BBC Newsbeat

    While gamers in New Zealand have started playing GTA 6, many of you in the UK are waiting patiently for it to drop here later tonight.

    Gaming livestreamer Alana Black, who you might know better as Alana CJ on Twitch, says she'll spend the day doing her best to avoid spoilers.

    "I just try to swipe past it, ignore it and don't peek at it,” she says.

    And her advice if you're trying to do the same? "Lock your phone away, turn off all devices, don't go near any social media.”

    The 30-year-old tells BBC Newsbeat she knows gamers who might be trying to use what's called the "New Zealand trick" to access the game early by changing their console's location.

    "You can be banned for it,” she warns.

    “I wouldn't risk it personally. It's been 13 years since the last GTA, I can wait one more day."

  8. 'Exquisitely detailed' and 'mature': What the initial reviews are sayingpublished at 11:46 GMT
    11:46 GMT

    As we’ve just reported, reviews on Metacritic have given the game an average score of 99.

    The New York Times, external has called the instalment a "dense game filled with friction", saying it "matures beyond its memorable car crashes and stick-ups with a game about love, relationships and nurturing your partner".

    Gaming website IGN, external has given the game a score of 10 out of 10, describing it as "exquisitely detailed" and "uncompromisingly engineered".

    The lowest confirmed reviews on the website currently give the game a score of 90.

    Entertainment outlet Slant Magazine, external says there are "flaws" in the game's creative approach in its four-and-a-half-star review, with its fantasy taking "power away from the player" at regular checkpoints.

  9. Pretending to be in New Zealand, booking leave and buying consoles - the lengths fans are going topublished at 11:30 GMT
    11:30 GMT

    Laura Cress
    Technology reporter

    When Victoria Evans filed a leave request at work, the reason she gave was “GTA 6”. She told the BBC her boss "doesn't know what GTA 6 is".

    Anyway, the request was approved, and Victoria will get up at 05:00 GMT on Thursday to play, after its launch at midnight in the UK.

    She’s far from the only one. For the game’s launch day, HR software company BrightHR looked at absence data from more than 1.2 million employees at 70,000 businesses across the UK.

    It found GTA was mentioned in 4.6% of employees’ time-off requests.

    It said an estimated 83,000 days had been booked off for the game throughout November and December.

    Booking leave is only one way fans are making sure they’re ready for the big day.

    Others are buying a new console to play it on or even pretending they’re halfway around the world to get early access.

  10. GTA 6 gets almost perfect marks from reviewerspublished at 11:17 GMT
    11:17 GMTBreaking

    Tom Gerken
    Technology reporter

    The reviews are in, and it appears the hype was for good reason.

    GTA 6 has an average review score of 99 on Metacritic, external, based on 100 critic reviews.

    That means it’s - at least for now - tied for the top spot with the original The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, which also scored 99 when it launched on the N64 in 1998.

    Not all the reviews may be in yet, so the final score could be a bit different.

    Some sites may want to experience more of the game before finalising their scores.

    But whatever the case, with countless reviewers giving GTA 6 perfect marks, this is a remarkable case of a game delivering on its truly monstrous hype.

  11. GTA 5 earned millions for Rockstar Games - can the sequel top it?published at 11:10 GMT
    11:10 GMT

    GTA 6’s predecessor was a record-breaker, and this new title is widely expected to be the biggest entertainment release ever.

    GTA 5 made Rockstar $1bn (£760m) in just three days after its release. Unsurprisingly, it holds the record for the best opening-week sales in video game history.

    The figure is on a par with the cost of developing GTA 6: estimates also put the cost of creating the game at about $1bn.

  14. What is GTA 6 about, where is it set and who are its main characters?published at 10:58 GMT
    10:58 GMT

    Laura Cress and Liv McMahon
    Technology reporters

    GTA 6 features the first ever playable female protagonist in a 3D and HD setting for the series - Lucia - alongside her partner in life and crime, Jason, as a second playable character.

    From what we've seen in two gameplay trailers and Netflix's extended look, the story will follow the pair in a Bonnie and Clyde-esque adventure through the seedy underbelly of America after an "easy score goes wrong".

    Like other GTA games before it, the sixth chapter is set in a fictional US state - this time in Leonida, which is Rockstar's version of Florida.

    Fans of previous games will be excited to hear that Vice City, which is inspired by Miami, will return, featuring as the main city within the state.

    GTA 6's cover art, revealed by Rockstar on 18 June, made several nods to the setting with alligators and flamingos.

    10:51 GMT

    Laura Cress and Liv McMahon
    Technology reporters

    The sixth game in the main series is being released on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X and S.

    There’s no news on when a PC version will be released. It’s also currently unknown whether it’ll come to Nintendo Switch 2.

    The game is priced at £69.99 in the UK and $79.99 in the US. An ultimate edition, featuring extra vehicles and weapons, is priced at £89.99 and $99.99 respectively.

    Physical copies of the game contain a code for a digital download inside the box, rather than a disc.
