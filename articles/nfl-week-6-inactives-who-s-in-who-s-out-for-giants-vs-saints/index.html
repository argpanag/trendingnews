NFL Week 6 Inactives: Who's in, who's out for Giants vs. Saints

TheTools · 18.10.2026 17:56 · source · permalink

The following players will be inactive for the Week 6 matchup between the New York Giants (3-1-1) and New Orleans Saints (1-4) at MetLife Stadium.

GIANTS INACTIVES

  • OL Jermaine Eluemunor
  • CB Colton Hood
  • QB Jake Haener (3rd QB)
  • TE Theo Johnson
  • S Tyler Nubin
  • DL C.J. Okoye

SAINTS INACTIVES

  • QB Zach Wilson (3rd QB)
  • EDGE Anfernee Jennings
  • S Mike Reid
  • LB Kaden Elliss
  • DL Nathan Sheperd
  • DL John Ridgeway III

The Giants had already ruled out cornerback Colton Hood (concussion), tight end Theo Johnson (neck) and safety Tyler Nubin (neck) on the final injury report of the week.

Outside linebacker Abdul Carter (calf), right tackle Jermaine Eluemunor (calf) and left tackle Andrew Thomas (groin) were listed as questionable.

On Saturday, the Giants activated outside linebacker Trace Ford from the practice squad. It was the third consecutive elevation for Ford, who has recorded three special teams tackles in the last two games.

The Saints, meanwhile, had ruled out linebackers Kaden Elliss (calf) and Anfernee Jennings (knee).

Running back Alvin Kamara (back), wide receiver Chris Olave (illness), defensive tackle Nathan Shepherd (illness) and cornerback Lorenzo Styles (knee) were questionable.

New Orleans also activated long snapper Zach Wood (calf) from injured reserve and elevated wide receiver Cedric Tillman to the active roster for Sunday's game.
