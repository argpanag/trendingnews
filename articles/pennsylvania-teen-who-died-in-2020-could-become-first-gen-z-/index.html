Pennsylvania teen who died in 2020 could become first Gen Z saint

TheTools · 24.11.2026 17:15 · source · permalink

Maria Middleton was diagnosed with an inoperable brain tumor when she was 16.

NEW BRITAIN TWP., Pa. -- Six years after her death at age 18, Bucks County, Pennsylvania, native Maria Middleton has taken the first step toward possible sainthood in the Catholic Church.

The U.S. Conference of Catholic Bishops voted last week to advance the cause of beatification and canonization for Middleton following a petition filed by Philadelphia Archbishop Nelson Perez. The petition highlighted Middleton's faith during her battle with an inoperable brain tumor.

Her parents, Susan and Brian Middleton, said they are still trying to process what the development could mean.

"We're sort of living in a space somewhere between heaven and earth," Brian Middleton said.

"Sainthood is attainable. God created us to be saints," Susan Middleton said.

Maria Middleton was born in 2002 and grew up in Bucks County. She loved to dance, listened to Taylor Swift, loved her pets and enjoyed cracking jokes.

Her parents described her as an ordinary teenager whose faith and pursuit of virtue were extraordinary.

"Our favorite Maria quote: '...you don't have to be weird to be holy,'" Brian Middleton said. "She was in every way normal, but she had this extraordinary faith and pursuit of virtue."

Middleton was diagnosed with an inoperable brain tumor when she was 16.

"It was devastating. It was a nightmare. It was a no-way-out situation," Susan Middleton said.

The family turned to prayer, and people around the world soon began praying for Maria. While battling cancer, she was able to take a mission trip to Uganda.

Middleton died in October 2020. Since then, people from around the world have visited her grave at the Shrine of Our Lady of Czestochowa.

Her parents also discovered a collection of her writings detailing her faith.

"We were curious as to what was the last thing physically she was able to write while holding a pen, and she left us with 'Think hope,'" Susan Middleton said.

"Think Hope" is now the name of a book about Maria.

Notices have been posted at churches across the area asking people with information about Maria to come forward as a formal inquiry is prepared.

Advancing Middleton's cause is the first step in the sainthood process. The Vatican must sign off on the process before her writings are examined. After that, two miracles would have to be attributed to her.

If canonized, Middleton would become the fourth saint born in America and the first Gen Z saint.
