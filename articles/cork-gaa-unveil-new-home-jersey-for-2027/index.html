Cork GAA unveil new home jersey for 2027

TheTools · 22.10.2026 15:01 · source · permalink

Thursday, 22 October 2026 at 08:56

By News Team

The traditional blood and bandage is getting a modern update.

The new strip features a return of the red collar, a fresh look for the white stripes and a subtle silhouette of the castles and ship from the Cork GAA crest.

The jersey will be worn by Cork's senior footballers and hurlers for the first time at the start of their 2027 Allianz National League campaigns in January.

Cork GAA CEO Kevin O'Donovan says the design reflects the county's strength, identity and power, while celebrating its rich sporting heritage.

Fans will have the chance to meet some of Cork's senior footballers and hurlers at an in-store launch in Sports Direct at Mahon Point on Thursday, October 29th, from 5pm to 6.30pm.

The jersey is available online through O'Neills and in Sports Direct stores nationwide. Cork's away jersey and training kit are expected to be launched in the coming weeks.
