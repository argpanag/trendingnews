Tennis | US Open 2026 | Arthur Fery defends US Open preparation despite opening round exit to Lorenzo Musetti

TheTools · 02.09.2026 21:24 · source · permalink

By Tony Fairbairn

  • Lorenzo Musetti [13] def Arthur Fery 6-3 6-3 7-5
  • Arthur Fery has defended his decision to play the week before the US Open after his straight sets defeat to Lorenzo Musetti.

 

It was not to be for Arthur Fery as the Wimbledon semi-finalist lost in straight sets to Lorenzo Musetti in the opening round of the US Open.

There was plenty of optimism for Fery who reached his maiden ATP final of his career in Winston-Salem the week prior.

However, he was up against a former Grand Slam semi-finalist in the form of Musetti who was starting to regain his form after a tough year.

It was a positive start for Fery who was proactive and aggressive on return as he kept the games close in the early stages.

Despite being under some pressure early on, Musetti was clinical on serve and fended off any early pressure from the Brit.

That would be a turning point in the opening set as Musetti broke in the last two return games to take the opening set 6-3.

In the second set, Fery made a really good start once again as he was clinical on serve as he used great variety to force errors from Musetti.

However, Musetti once again was able to raise his level when it mattered most and he broke serve for a 4-2 lead.

There was an immediate response from Fery though who was effective on return and he created four break points in the following game.

Once again Fery lacked that clinical touch when needed and Musetti fought off the break points before closing out a two sets to love lead with a hold to love.

It was a mountain to climb for Fery but the Brit remained positive and broke in the second game for a 2-0 lead.

There was a lack of consistency on the Fery serve though and the Brit was broken in two consecutive service games as Musetti took control of the contest.

A brief lifeline for Fery was obtained after he saved match point, the Brit broke Musetti when he was serving for the match.

The lifeline didn’t last long as Musetti won the last two games of the match to secure his place into the second round.

After the match Fery praised his Italian opponent for the way he played as he lost in the opening round.

“Tough match,” Fery was quoted by the LTA website as saying.

“Tough match,” Fery was quoted by the LTA website as saying.

“Obviously a good player that I faced. I didn’t have much time to get used to the site and the conditions coming straight from Winston-Salem, but I knew that coming in. I did what I could with what I had today.

“Not a great performance my end, but I have to give credit to Musetti. He’s tricky to play, has a one-hander, slices a lot and I wasn’t able to do enough. It’s useful for sure (competing against players of that level). I know what I have to work on, I have a lot of ideas coming straight off the court of what I could do better.

“My serve let me down today, which is not usually the case. When you play guys of that level it’s tough and the margins are small.”

 

 

Fery defends Winston-Salem decision after US Open exit

It wasn’t to be for Fery who would have been disappointed to exit the US Open at such an early stage.

Especially as he reached the final at Winston-Salem the week before which could have led to some fatigue for Fery.

However, the British number two has defended his decision to play the week before the US Open.

“The Grand Slams are the biggest tournaments, and everyone has their eye on them, but these past two weeks, would I sign on for that again? Yeah, probably to be honest,” Fery confessed to the LTA website.

“The Grand Slams are the biggest tournaments, and everyone has their eye on them, but these past two weeks, would I sign on for that again? Yeah, probably to be honest,” Fery confessed to the LTA website.

“I got a lot of ranking points, made an ATP final, which is good, even though it meant my preparation for the US Open wasn’t great.”

Now attention turns to the Davis Cup for Fery against Ecuador in London and Fery is excited about representing his country again.

“It’s going to be great, I’m really looking forward to that,” Fery claimed to the LTA website.

“It’s going to be great, I’m really looking forward to that,” Fery claimed to the LTA website.

“I’m looking forward to going back, practicing and getting in good shape to represent my country. Playing as the No.1 Brit at home is going to be special. It’s an honour to be No.1 in Great Britain and following all those names and great players who have had unbelievable careers. I’m new to it, and I’m just going to try and focus on the tennis.

“I’m going to go to Asia after Davis Cup, play tour events there for the first time. Go to Tokyo and then Shanghai, and then unsure yet. Hopefully I’ll play a busy schedule, get a lot of matches in, and hopefully qualify for Davis Cup Finals.”

The Davis Cup tie at the Copper Box Arena takes place on Saturday 19th of September and Sunday 20th of September.
