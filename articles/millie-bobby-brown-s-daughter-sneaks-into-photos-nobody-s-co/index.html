Millie Bobby Brown’s Daughter Sneaks Into Photos & Nobody’s Complaining

TheTools · 02.09.2026 13:44 · source · permalink

Millie Bobby Brown‘s baby daughter sneaked into her new photo, and nobody has any complaints. The “Stranger Things” star took to Instagram on September 2 to share a carousel of photos, summing up her August adventures. Some of those pictures also give a sneak peek at her adorable kid.

Millie Bobby Brown’s baby daughter sneaks into mom’s August photos and it’s adorable

View this post on Instagram

Millie Bobby Brown shared the most adorable photo ever as part of her August dump, as her baby girl sneaked into the snap. On the right, the actress could be seen from behind, wearing a wide-brimmed sun hat. Crafted from a mix of solid bright red fabric and woven, checkered straw, the hat featured a pleated ruffle around the crown and a wide brim that shaded her neck. She was wearing an off-white, open-work crochet top that perfectly matched the summer aesthetic.

To the left sat her little girl wearing a classic bright red paisley bandana as a headscarf. She paired it with a white top featuring a red polka-dot print. The mother-daughter duo sat facing a bright, sun-drenched landscape filled with lush green treetops under a clear, pale blue sky.

Another shot captured Brown’s feet next to her daughter’s little ones on a waterboard floating in a pool. The fifth slide showed her husband, Jake Bongiovi, pushing a brown baby stroller while looking away from the camera.

The “Enola Holmes” star captioned the post, “fresh out of august.” Fans gushed with love and praise in the comments. One user wrote, “Awww this is so cute,” while another commented, “You and your daughter are just the cutest.” Meanwhile, one fan mused, “What a perfect family!”

During her appearance on Jay Shetty’s “On Purpose” podcast in July, Brown opened up about adopting her baby. She had described the moment when she and her husband received the call confirming their daughter’s adoption as “the most surreal feeling.” The couple welcomed their baby girl in summer 2025.
