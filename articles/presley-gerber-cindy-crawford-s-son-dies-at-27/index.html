Presley Gerber, Cindy Crawford's son, dies at 27

TheTools · 21.09.2026 04:45 · source · permalink

Presley Gerber, supermodel Cindy Crawford's only son with businessman Rande Gerber, has died. He was 27.

Presley Gerber, who followed in his mother's modeling footsteps, died Sunday, Sept. 20, at a rehab facility, according to Los Angeles County Medical Examiner records, USA TODAY can confirm. TMZ first reported the news.

Crawford’s publicist confirmed the news to CNN, saying, “The family is asking for privacy during this very difficult and painful time.” People magazine also published the statement from the family's rep.

An autopsy for Presley Gerber is pending completion, according to TMZ.

USA TODAY has reached out to the family's representatives for comment.

Who was Presley Gerber?

Born July 2, 1999, in Beverly Hills, California, Presley Gerber was the only son of supermodel mom Cindy Crawford and Rande Gerber, a businessman and cofounder of the tequila brand Casamigos.
