Ravens' Devontez Walker: Sitting out Sunday

TheTools · 13.09.2026 18:37 · source · permalink

Walker (groin) is inactive for Sunday's clash with the Colts.

The wide receiver logged limited practice sessions throughout the week leading up to the game and entered the weekend with a questionable tag. Ravens coach Jesse Minter described the injury as "minor" Tuesday, per Jonas Shaffer of TheBaltimoreBanner.com, but it's at least serious enough to keep Walker out for Week 1.
