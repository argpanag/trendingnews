Type 23 frigates to retire two years earlier than planned

TheTools · 26.11.2026 09:30 · source · permalink
  • Sea

The Royal Navy’s Type 23 frigate class will leave service in 2033, two years earlier than the 2035 date ministers gave last year, Luke Pollard, Minister for Defence Readiness and Industry, has confirmed, according to an answer given in Parliament.

Conservative MP Ben Obese-Jecty asked for the current out of service date of each remaining ship listed in a Commons debate of 3 July 2009. Pollard said: “While the Ministry of Defence does not comment on the service dates of individual ships for security reasons, I can confirm that the Type 23 frigate class leaves service in 2033 as per the Defence Investment Plan.”

In a separate answer, Pollard told Obese-Jecty that records do not show when the 2009 planned dates were revised, saying: “The available historical records do not identify a specific date on which the planned out of service dates referenced in the Official Report of 3 July 2009 were revised. The dates represented the Department’s planning assumptions at that time and would therefore have been determined on or before 3 July 2009.”

In October last year, the ministry said the Duke class ships were “currently due to exit service in 2035”, as we reported. The Defence Investment Plan published in June set out a phased withdrawal of the class running to 2033 and retired two more ships, after HMS Lancaster left service late last year and HMS Iron Duke this spring. Iron Duke was withdrawn around 16 months after a refit costing more than £100 million.

Pollard said last week that planned out of service dates may change depending on operational requirements, citing the material condition and supportability of the ageing fleet, affordability and the arrival of replacements.

Thirteen new frigates are planned to replace them, eight Type 26 anti-submarine frigates built by BAE Systems on the Clyde and five Type 31 frigates built by Babcock at Rosyth, with HMS Glasgow the first Type 26 and HMS Venturer the first Type 31.
