College Football Corner: Hokies rally, plus a bad day for the Big 12 – WTOP News

TheTools · 25.10.2026 20:21 · source · permalink

College football’s unbeaten pack was trimmed by three more teams this week, beginning with James Madison’s overtime defeat that might deflate the Dukes’ College Football Playoff hopes for good (last year they were a one-loss conference champ whose defeat was at Louisville, a far cry from App State).

Saturday saw two body blows get delivered to the Big 12, which remains in competition with the ACC as the red-haired stepchild in the new Power Four Conference landscape.

Second-ranked BYU — one week after announcing its arrival by nipping previously unbeaten Notre Dame — suffered its first loss of the season in double overtime at UCF.

Previously unbeaten Texas Tech also suffered its first loss of the year, getting outscored at Cincinnati. Both teams had plenty of questions surrounding their semisoft schedules and those defeats pull them back into the pack, where they still might post an outstanding record but come up short on Selection Sunday.

James Madison (7-1, 4-1 Sun Belt) came up short for the first time all season, slipping at App State 34-31 in overtime. The Dukes drove for a touchdown on their first possession but then were held to 17 yards on 12 plays while posting four straight three and outs. A fourth-quarter interception led to the Mountaineers’ first lead of the night and a missed field goal in OT sealed their fate.

Duke Do’s: Camden Coleman threw a touchdown pass while also running for a score. Turner Schmidt tallied a pair of sacks. Patrick Rea averaged 45 yards per punt.

Duke Don’ts: The offense was held to 2-15 on third down while the team committed 10 penalties. Morgan Suarez missed two of three field goal attempts.

Next: Thursday against 3-4 Troy at 7:30 p.m. on ESPN2.

Virginia (4-4, 2-3 ACC) fell to .500 on the season after slipping at home to Duke 19-16. They actually took a lead with three minutes left on a 30-yard field goal by Will Bettridge, but the Blue Devils had a trick play up their sleeves as running back Nate Sheppard hit Brody Keefe for a 28-yard touchdown pass with 36 seconds 1:14 remaining. Beau Pribula wasn’t able to dial up any last-minute magic and his interception sealed the defeat.

Cavalier Congrats: Jekail Middlebrook rushes for 117 yards and a touchdown. Kam Robinson tallied an interception and the defense held the Blue Devils to 2-10 on third down. Daniel Sparks averaged 56.2 yards per punt.

Cavalier Concerns: The offense was held out of the end zone for the final three quarters while a fumble and a missed field goal in the third period kept this one closer than it should have been.

Next: Saturday at 3:45 at No. 17 Wake Forest (6-1) on ESPN2.

Navy (2-5, 1-4 American) actually outgained North Texas by 29 yards but big plays loomed large in a 49-39 loss. The Mean Green posted touchdown passes of 45 and 53 yards, tallied a 59-yard touchdown run, returned a fumble 63 yards for a score, and ran back a punt 76 yards for a touchdown. Even with all of that carnage, the Mids were still in this game when they elected to punt with 6:32 left in the fourth quarter while facing a 4th and nine at their 47. They wouldn’t see the ball again and now need to win the rest of their games to become bowl-eligible.

Midshipmen Medals: Jackson Gutierrez rushes for 228 yards and three touchdowns while completing 6-9 passes for 104 yards and a score. Aiden Lyczek led the defense with nine tackles. The team was penalized just once for five yards after getting flagged 10 times the previous weekend.

Midshipmen Miscues: The defense allowed Tayven Jackson to complete his first nine passes and he finishes with 305 yards on the afternoon, while the Mids also coughed up 5.6 yards per carry.

Next: Saturday at noon against No. 4 Notre Dame (6-1) on ABC.

Virginia Tech (5-3, 3-2 ACC) led Clemson at the half before allowing the toothless (last in the ACC in offense) and paper (zero wins against ranked foes) Tigers to score on their first three possessions of the second half. But instead of a repeat of their losses to Pitt and Cal, Tech dug in and scored twice in the fourth quarter, with Ethan Grunkemeyer finding Luke Reynolds in the end zone with 1:44 left to deliver a 20-16 win that was their first in Death Valley since 2007.

Hokie Highlights: Jaquez White notched 11 tackles with a sack to lead a defense that held Clemson to 3-14 on third down. Luke Reynolds paced the team with seven catches including both touchdowns. John Love connected on both of his field goal attempts.

Hokie Humblings: The running back rotation was held to 38 yards on 16 carries while the offensive line allowed six sacks. The third quarter continues to be a nightmare as they’ve now been outscored 44-10 in that period by ACC foes.

Next: Friday Nov. 6 at No. 24 SMU (6-1) on ESPN.
