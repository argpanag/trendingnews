Excitel becomes Global Sponsor of DP World Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026

TheTools · 20.11.2026 11:08 · source · permalink

The tournament will be held in Doha from November 19 to 29, with eight teams competing across 15 matches.

Excitel Broadband has signed on as the global sponsor of the DP World Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026, which begins in Doha, Qatar, on November 19. The tournament will feature eight teams from across Asia, with the final scheduled for November 29.

This is Excitel's second association with an Asian Cricket Council tournament this year. In September, the broadband provider had an on-ground presence at the DP World Women's Asia Cup 2026 in Dubai, where India defeated Sri Lanka in the final to claim its eighth title.

As part of the sponsorship, Excitel's branding will feature on the pitch mat behind the wicket, perimeter boards around the ground and backdrops used during post-match presentations, press conferences and player interviews. The matches will be broadcast live on Sony Sports Network and Sony LIV.

Commenting on the association, Aditya Jain, chief marketing officer, Excitel Broadband, said, “We are grateful to the Asian Cricket Council for welcoming Excitel back, this time as a global sponsor. Cricket is where Indian fans are at their most passionate, and few platforms let a brand share in that emotion at this scale. We look forward to being a part of this exciting tournament over the next eleven days.”

Varun Pasricha, CEO, Excitel Broadband, added, “Excitel started out as a young challenger with big ambitions, so a tournament built around rising talent feels very close to who we are. These players have worked for years to earn their place on a bigger stage, and we are glad to support them at such an important point in their journey. Cricket has always brought Indian homes together, and we are proud to be part of that as we stand with the next generation of Asian cricket.”

Haider Raza, head of media, communications and operations, Asian Cricket Council, said, “We are pleased to welcome Excitel on board as a global sponsor for the DP World Asia Cup Rising Stars. The players at this tournament reflect how cricket is played today, and Excitel’s energy and ambition align well with the qualities that define modern cricket - intent, speed, and a confident mindset. We look forward to a successful tournament together.”

The sponsorship adds to Excitel's sports associations. In October 2026, the company became the Official Broadband Partner of Sporting Club Delhi in the Indian Super League. In 2024, it partnered with JioCinema for the 'Speed Ka Man of the Match' segment during the Indian Premier League.
