Harry Styles has once again turned the opening act slot into its own kind of musical lottery. The superstar's 2027 stadium extension of his Together, Together tour will bring a remarkably wide-ranging cast of special guests to 12 cities, including Kylie Minogue, LCD Soundsystem, Feist, Tinashe, Lainey Wilson and Tears for Fears.

The 25 newly announced dates in support of Styles' dance-driven Columbia album Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally begin April 2 with two nights at State Farm Stadium in Phoenix, marking Styles' first U.S. and Canadian stadium shows. From there, the tour rolls through Dallas, Los Angeles, Toronto, Atlanta and Chicago before heading to Europe for Berlin, Paris, Madrid, Rome and Dublin.

The tour will conclude with three additional nights at London's Wembley Stadium, where Styles already played a record-breaking 12 shows earlier this summer.

If the other names on the marquee suggest a Spotify playlist assembled by someone with pleasantly unpredictable taste, that's very much in keeping with Styles' touring aesthetic. Besides the aforementioned names, CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, Q Marsden, the Womack Sisters, Getdown Services, former Chairlift frontwoman Caroline Polachek and Baby Rose will each take the stage in select cities. Click here for tickets.

Styles professed his love for James Murphy and LCD Soundsystem in a January BBC interview, recalling seeing the band in Madrid and Brixton, U.K., and coming away feeling "so joyous. And when you're kind of watching them be so like immersed in it … I think the inspiration from them came of like, 'oh, that's how I want to feel when I'm on stage.' And then that kind of led to the kind of music I was making."

The original Together, Together itinerary has already been something of an eclecticist's dream, with Robyn, Shania Twain, Fcukers, Jorja Smith, Jamie xx, Fousheé and Skye Newman among the artists booked to support Styles across his seven-city 2026 residency run. He's currently seven shows into a 30-show stay at New York's Madison Square Garden.

For fans who prefer their stadium pop with a little less financial commitment, the tour will also introduce Unpredictable Fun Tickets, with a limited number of seats available for $20 in North America, £20 in the U.K. and €20 in Europe. Styles will additionally extend his partnership with EARNT, allowing fans to receive tickets in exchange for volunteering their time.
