Updated 2026 WWE Survivor Series Card and Predictions for Match Order

TheTools · 28.11.2026 01:17 · source · permalink

WWE will continue its Thanksgiving Week tradition Saturday when the Survivor Series premium live event descends Saturday evening on Houston.

The show will be headlined by a pair of WarGames matches with The Bloodline taking on 946 and a team led by Charlotte Flair facing a team led by Jade Cargill.

Additionally, the traditional, five-on-five elimination match is making its return in the form of Team USA vs. Team Canada, and Liv Morgan will defend the Women's World Championship against Becky Lynch in the only title bout of the night.

Here is a complete rundown of the Survivor Series 2026 card, along with predictions for the match order.

Where: Daikin Park in Houston

When: Saturday, Nov. 28 at 6 p.m. ET

Watch: ESPN Unlimited

Survivor Series 2026 Card and Projected Match Order

  • WarGames Match: Charlotte Flair, Alexa Bliss, Tatum Paxley, Bayley & Stephanie Vaquer vs. Jade Cargill, Michin, Jacy Jayne, Fallon Henley & Lainey Reid
  • Traditional 5-on-5 Elimination Match: Team USA (Cody Rhodes, CM Punk, Je'Von Evans, LA Knight & Seth Rollins) vs. Team Mexico (Rey Mysterio, Rey Fenix, Penta, Dragon Lee & El Grande Americano)
  • Women's World Championship: Liv Morgan (c) vs. Becky Lynch
  • WarGames Match: The Bloodline (Roman Reigns, Jey Uso, Jimmy Uso, Solo Sikoa & Jacob Fatu) vs. 946 (Royce Keys, Bronco Nima, Lucien Price, Carmelo Hayes & Cruz Montana)

Top Survivor Series Matches to Watch

WarGames: The Bloodline vs. 946

The most heated feud in pro wrestling will finally come to a head Saturday at Survivor Series when The Bloodline and 946 clash in a WarGames match.

For the past few months, The Bloodline has been firmly in 946's crosshairs, and 946 has gotten the upper hand more often than not thanks to constant additions to the group, including Jaida Parker, Carmelo Hayes and Cruz Montana.

The Bloodline received a huge boost a couple of weeks ago, though, when Jacob Fatu returned to the fold after missing several weeks on the heels of Parker hitting him with a car.

As a unit, The Bloodline has a ton of experience in WarGames matches, which potentially gives it the edge heading into Survivor Series.

However, 946 will be hungry to make a statement, especially since a win could potentially position Royce Keys for an eventual World Heavyweight Championship match against Roman Reigns.

Given that it will be 946's first major match together as a group, it likely needs the win more than The Bloodline, but The Bloodline can never be counted out, especially when considering the faction's long history of success.

WarGames: Team Charlotte vs. Team Jade

While not as deeply rooted in hatred as The Bloodline vs. 946, the women's WarGames match at Survivor Series has a chance to be a highly entertaining contest in its own right.

The rivalry between Charlotte Flair and Jade Cargill is the nucleus of the match, but there are plenty of other intriguing elements as well, including Alexa Bliss and Tatum Paxley's recent run-ins with both Fatal Influence and Cargill's group involving Michin and B-Fab.

On top of that, Flair added some major star power to her team in recent weeks with Bayley and Stephanie Vaquer entering the fray, which likely swung the pendulum in Charlotte's favor.

Cargill's team is young and hungry, though, and several of the members will be competing in the biggest match of their lives at Survivor Series.

Although it is difficult to envision such a star-studded team losing, Cargill's team has plenty to gain with a win, so that possibility cannot be completely ruled out.

Traditional 5-on-5 Elimination Match: Team USA vs. Team Mexico

For many years, the traditional, five-on-five elimination match was a Survivor Series staple, but it has gone dormant recently due to WarGames becoming a key part of the event.

However, this year, the five-on-five elimination match is back, and it will be contested alongside the two WarGames matches on the Survivor Series card.

In a unique twist, five of the best WWE Superstars Mexico has to offer will take on five of the United States' best with Rey Mysterio and Cody Rhodes serving as the captains.

A huge wrinkle was added Monday night on Raw when Rhodes revealed Seth Rollins as the last member of Team USA, much to the disapproval of CM Punk, who had been loving his role as co-captain of the team up until that moment.

Punk and Rollins have a checkered history, and their issues with each other figure to play a role in Saturday's bout, which adds another layer of intrigue to the proceedings.
