    The race to finish second behind title winner Kimi Antonelli is the main focus until the end of the season, and with another podium under his belt, Max Verstappen has moved 17 points clear of Mercedes' George Russell, who himself has gained a position tonight and is ahead of now fourth-placed Lewis Hamilton by one point.

  2. 'Taken a few years to be back on top spot' - Wolffpublished at 19:22 GMT
    19:22 GMT

    Mercedes team principal Toto Wolff speaking after securing the constructors' championship: "It's amazing. It was a very strong beginning of the season and that got us over the line, but also these last few races have been challenging. At times today it was difficult, but overall I'm so proud of the organisation. We've taken a few years to be back on the top spot, not many teams have been able to come back from that situation. It just shows the great work, the great people in our organisation and it's fantastic to be among them."

    On what it means to lead Mercedes to nine constructors' titles, equalling the record of Williams' Frank Williams: "Frank is such an unbelievable icon of the sport and to be tied on championships, I never thought that was ever possible. I had a close relationship with Frank and to be there, it's amazing. I can't believe it. There is also some of our people that have won more than nine that have been with Brawn, winning it with Honda. I take it, it's great."

  3. 'I could have avoided a little awkward situation with Max' - Hadjarpublished at 19:17 GMT
    19:17 GMT

    Red Bull's Isack Hadjar who finished the race in third: "It was so, so difficult, so challenging. Especially switching from the inters to the soft tyre, avoiding the traps.

    "It was extremely difficult, especially in the pit lane you saw how tricky it was to drive. And then more rain at the end.

    "Happy to be standing in third today but I need to keep making a bit less mistakes because I could have avoided a little awkward situation with Max, so just want to apologise because on that stint I could have kept it more on the track.

    "It was so hard because [Kimi Antonelli] was behind me and I was testing the grip for him basically. I could see he was challenging me a lot. I was on ice and he was on ice as well. It was well managed, I nearly shunted into my pit crew when boxing. But they did a fantastic job, they kept their calm and the pit stop was fast. Big thanks to the boys."

  4. 'Starting P21 and being on the podium in second is a very good result' - Verstappenpublished at 19:13 GMT
    19:13 GMT

    Red Bull's Max Verstappen speaking after gaining 19 places to finish P2 at the Sao Paulo Grand Prix: "It was a challenging race. I think the first few laps were very important, I overtook a lot of cars.

    "Actually, that first stint on the inters was really strong. Unfortunately after that, I don't know if we picked up some kind of damage because the car felt like completely opposite to anything I've felt the whole weekend, with some really weird balance.

    "Unfortunately, once I got into P2, I couldn't really make any inroads. We have to understand what went wrong there but starting P21 and being on the podium in second is a very good result."

    Share page

  5. 'I kept it cool and did not make any mistakes' - Norrispublished at 19:09 GMT
    19:09 GMT

    McLaren's Lando Norris who has won the Sao Paulo Grand Prix from pole position: "It was a very tough race, very, very tough. Not the best of launches off the line, but a lot of challenges today with the conditions and going on slicks in the wet is always tough. Especially when you are pushing for the lead and trying to catch up a little bit so the main thing today was that I just kept it cool and I did not make any mistakes. I had the pace when I needed to so it was good. Nice to be back on the top step and what a place to do it as well."

    On how confident he was that he could get the lead back in the race: "I'm trying my best. I think this weekend we had an opportunity to get pole yesterday, an opportunity to win and I feel like most times this year when there's been an opportunity, I've went for it and we've been able to achieve it.

    "I have to say a big thanks to the team because they are still working very hard. It's annoying when you can't give them a good result back, like the last few weeks, but certainly this weekend I feel like we have been able to give something back to to them and give them a reward for their hard work. Always a big thanks to them and we will have to keep pushing until the end of the year."
