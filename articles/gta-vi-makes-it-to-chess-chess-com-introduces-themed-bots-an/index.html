GTA VI makes it to chess — Chess.com introduces themed bots and promises a reward for winning

TheTools · 02.11.2026 17:32 · source · permalink

Translated with AI from the Russian original.

Before the release of GTA VI, players can “flex” their brains.

The release of Grand Theft Auto VI is approaching. All eyes are on the project, and some are taking advantage of this. Players recently noticed new bots on Chess.com.

The developers offered players the chance to play several matches against themed bots inspired by GTA. Rewards have been prepared for those who manage to defeat the virtual opponents. The opponents include characters referencing various heroes from the Rockstar Games franchise:

  • Marko — refers to Niko from GTA IV.
  • Jamie — refers to Jason from GTA VI, while Luna refers to Lucia, who is also a protagonist of GTA VI.
  • Troy — fans speculate that this might be a reference to Brian from GTA VI or Trevor from GTA V.
  • TJ — clearly references Carl “CJ” Johnson from GTA: San Andreas.

 

The release of Grand Theft Auto VI is scheduled for November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S consoles. There is currently no information about a PC version of the game.

A millionaire blogger also took advantage of the interest in the new installment of the Rockstar Games franchise. He decided to look at buying GTA VI from the standpoint of financial benefit — the game could potentially help save a lot of money.
