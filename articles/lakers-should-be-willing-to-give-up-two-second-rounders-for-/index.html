Lakers should be willing to give up two second-rounders for Derrick Jones Jr.

TheTools · 20.11.2026 19:30 · source · permalink

The Los Angeles Lakers need another defensive-minded wing (preferably one who can shoot 3s), and it sounds like Derrick Jones Jr. is available on the trade market. Should the Lakers get involved. In my opinion, the answer is a gigantic YES.

Jones Jr. is on an expiring contract with the non-contender Los Angeles Clippers and "very much available," in the words of ESPN's Tim MacMahon. Appearing on The Brian Windhorst & The Hoop Collective pod, MacMahon discussed the Lakers' fit for DJJ, especially since Jones played with Luka Doncic on the Dallas Mavericks. More specifically, MacMahon noted that two second-round picks would be able to seal a Jones deal for Lakers general manager Rob Pelinka.

Tim MacMahon suggests Lakers could acquire Derrick Jones Jr. for two second-round picks

Jones is playing well for the Clippers this season. He holds the distinction of having improved his 3-point percentage in every NBA season of his career, and that's continued in 2026-27, with Jones shooting an astounding 50.0 percent from 3 (entering Friday) on 3.7 attempts per game.

Now 29 years old and armed with 10-plus years of NBA experience across six teams (including a run to the NBA Finals with Luka's Mavs in 2024), Jones is at that sweet spot in his career where he's still in his athletic prime but is now imbued with a veteran's understanding of the game. That's the kind of player who helps teams win championships.

The Lakers should absolutely be willing to trade two second-round picks for Derrick Jones Jr.

LA's been linked to Jones for weeks for a reason. He's as qualified a candidate on the wing as there is out there to come in and solidify LA's depth beside Matisse Thybulle, Ziaire Williams, and Jake LaRavia.

Naysayers to this idea will point to LA's meager trade assets as a reason not to surrender two second-rounders (out of their three tradeable second-rounders) for Jones. But I'd retort, if not Jones, who? Certainly not someone like Jericho Sims.

With Jones' deal expiring, it would give the Lakers an easy out if things didn't go according to plan after trading for him. And if Jones did deliver (as I believe he would), suddenly you'd have another player in the mix to potentially re-sign if you lose any of Thybulle, Williams, or LaRavia to free agency following the season.

I'm with MacMahon here. This is an easy call. Get on the phone, Pelinka!
