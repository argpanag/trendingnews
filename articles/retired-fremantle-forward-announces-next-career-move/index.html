Retired Fremantle forward announces next career move

TheTools · 12.10.2026 22:11 · source · permalink

Retired Fremantle forward Sam Switkowski will join North Melbourne as a development coach in 2027.

Switkowski, who turns 30 next month, announced in August this year that it would be his last at the top level and he was part of Fremantle's Grand Final side.

He made the decision for family reasons, wanting to relocate to Victoria and will reunite with his former assistant coach Jaymie Graham at the Kangaroos.

He played 131 games for the Dockers after being picked up as a mature-ager.

There was consideration given to joining another AFL club in a playing capacity before he decided against it, but he may play some VFL footy for the Roos.

Switkowski is the first new assistant coach to be confirmed under the Graham regime. He'll join premiership Tiger Kane Lambert as a development coach.

In an interview for the Dockers' website released on Monday, he reflected on his time with the club.

“I never thought I would even make it into the AFL and my goal was just to play one game and 131 is pretty awesome, thinking back now,” he said.

“It's hard to put into words; it's such a special journey, and I'm proud of what I've been able to achieve and to have shared it with so many awesome people and this club for so long, and to finish off with a Grand Final is pretty cool.”
