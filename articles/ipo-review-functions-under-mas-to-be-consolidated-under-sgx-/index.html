IPO review functions under MAS to be consolidated under SGX RegCo from Jan 4, 2027

TheTools · 06.11.2026 02:10 · source · permalink

Under the new arrangement, a prospective issuer will only need to submit its prospectus and listing application to SGX RegCo via their electronic submissions portal.

The Singapore Exchange Regulation (SGX RegCo) will be taking over the Monetary Authority of Singapore's (MAS) prospectus review functions for both Mainboard and Global Listing Board (GLB) listing applications from Jan 4, 2027.

"This change allows us to streamline the IPO process and give issuers greater regulatory certainty," says SGX RegCo CEO Tan Boon Gin in a statement on Nov 3. The consolidation was first mooted by the Equities Market Review Group and the MAS conducted a public consultation on the proposal from Oct 29, 2025 to Nov 29, 2025.

Previously, a prospective issuer had to file its prospectus and listing application to MAS and SGX RegCo respectively for review. From Jan 4 onwards, issuers will only have to submit their prospectuses and listing applications to SGX RegCo via their electronic submissions portal.

SGX RegCo says it expects to process all complete listing applications within six to eight weeks under the new arrangement. MAS will continue to oversee SGX RegCo's regulatory responsibilities and retains the power to determine and grant exemptions from prospectus disclosure requirements under the Securities and Futures Act.

Aside from consolidating MAS's IPO review functions, SGX RegCo says it will be standing down the Listings Advisory Committee (LAC) to further reduce regulatory touchpoints. The LAC was created before SGX RegCo and was originally established to address concerns about SGX's independence in the listing policymaking and review process.

Law firms and issuer managers who participated in MAS's public consultation last year expressed their support for the changes. SGX RegCo says it will continue to solicit feedback from market participants as these changes are implemented and will roll out further enhancements when needed.

"Singapore investors have become more sophisticated, and they have access to more information of higher quality," Tan says. "Equity investing has also become much more global in nature. This requires an evolution of our philosophies and roles."

"Moving to a disclosure-based approach allows market forces to take the place of merit-based judgements by regulators. SGX RegCo's role in such a disclosure-based regime will be focused on ensuring proper disclosures so that investment decision-making takes place in the market - where such decisions rightfully reside."
