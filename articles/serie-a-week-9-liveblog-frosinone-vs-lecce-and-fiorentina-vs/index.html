Serie A Week 9 Liveblog: Frosinone vs. Lecce and Fiorentina vs. Atalanta

Football Italia Live Blog

Join us for all the build-up and action as it happens as the Serie A midweek round closes with high-flying Frosinone against Lecce, then an intriguing Fiorentina vs. Atalanta.

We begin at 17.30 GMT (18.30 CET) with newly-promoted Frosinone who could climb into sixth place just one point shy of Milan in fifth if they beat Lecce.

At 19.45 GMT (20.45 CET), Fiorentina in crisis mode host Maurizio Sarri’s stuttering Atalanta.

Obric booked for a foul on Mastantuono.

Mastantuono on the edge of the area, cuts inside, shot deflected for a Fiorentina corner.

Gnonto into the book for a late tackle.

De Ketelaere involved again with the assist down the right, pulls it back, Zalewski cuts inside and shoots through a sea of legs from 15 yards to surprise De Gea at the near bottom corner! He just couldn’t see it until the last second!
