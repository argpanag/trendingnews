Barcelona Hosts Athletic Club in Liga F Matchday 12

TheTools · 22.11.2026 17:43 · source · permalink

Barcelona's pursuit of another Liga F victory faced a significant test against Athletic Club at Estadi Johan Cruyff on Sunday, November 22, 2026. Pere Romeu's side welcomed the fourth-place visitors for a Matchday 12 encounter at 7 p.m. CET, seeking to protect their position at the top of the standings before the international break.

According to match previews published by SPORT and Noticias Yahoo, Barcelona entered the fixture with 28 points from 11 matches. The defending champions had recorded nine wins, one draw and one defeat, establishing themselves as the league leaders. Athletic Club arrived in Barcelona with 19 points, occupying fourth place in the standings.

The Basque side had demonstrated strong form against competitive opposition, making the encounter a demanding assignment for the home team.

The visitors secured an emphatic 4-1 victory over Real Sociedad in their previous league appearance. They had also earned a 1-1 draw against Real Madrid, highlighting their ability to compete against teams near the top of the table.

Barcelona also carried positive momentum from European competition. The Catalan side had defeated Servette 6-0 in the fifth round of the Women's Champions League before returning to domestic action. However, Romeu faced several selection difficulties ahead of the match. Kika Nazareth, Vicky López, Laia Aleixandri and Kerolin Nicoli were unavailable, while Laia Cabetas was also absent from the squad compared with the previous league selection.

Despite those personnel setbacks, Barcelona aimed to maintain its attacking approach and secure another three points at home. The match represented the team's final domestic fixture before the upcoming international break. The encounter took place at Estadi Johan Cruyff as part of the 12th round of Liga F Moeve. Spanish viewers could follow the match through DAZN, Teledeporte, RTVE Play, TV3 and 3Cat, according to the published broadcast information.
