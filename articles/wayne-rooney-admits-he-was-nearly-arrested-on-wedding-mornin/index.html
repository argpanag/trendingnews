Wayne Rooney admits he was nearly arrested on wedding morning

TheTools · 21.11.2026 23:29 · source · permalink

Wayne Rooney has revealed he came close to being arrested on the morning of his wedding after a wild night of partying with his friends in Italy.

The former England captain, who has been married to wife Coleen for 18 and a half years, opened up about the unexpected wedding drama during a candid conversation with pal Chloe Burrows in the I'm A Celebrity jungle.

The couple tied the knot in a romantic ceremony in Italy in 2008, with the big day said to have cost an estimated £5million. It took place in the picturesque seaside town of Portofino, at the same location where singer Rod Stewart had previously married.

Wayne, 41, explained that he had very little involvement in organising the big day, admitting he did not even see the wedding venue until the day of the ceremony.

Despite leaving much of the planning to Coleen, 40, Rooney revealed the celebrations featured some impressive musical guests.

Westlife performed during the couple's first dance, while Stereophonics star Kelly Jones entertained guests the following day.

However, it was the night before the wedding that Wayne remembers for very different reasons.

Recalling how Coleen had left to stay on a boat with her bridesmaids, Wayne said she had given him one important instruction before heading off.

'Don't get too p****d. You're getting married in the morning,' she warned him.

Wayne admitted he assured his soon-to-be wife that he would behave, but things quickly got out of hand.

'Seven o'clock in the morning, had the police at the door,' he recalled.

'A few of the lads ended up in my room and we carried big, massive speakers up. And then a few bottles got thrown over the balcony and the police turned up.

'So, I nearly got arrested!'

His campmate Ade Adepitan quipped: 'Coleen wouldn't have been too pleased with that!'

'All I'm saying is that I'm sure they've paid him a lot of money. But his PR is sat at home biting their fingernails every single time Wayne opens his mouth because he's such an open book in a good way, which is really nice.'

The bill for Coleen and Wayne's lavish wedding celebrations included close to £1million on pre-wedding celebrations, £400,000 to book Coleen's favourite band, Westlife, and £200,000 on her dress.

Five private jets flew 64 guests from the UK to the four-day celebrations, which also included a luxury yacht trip, masked ball and champagne breakfast every morning.

After both a civil and church ceremony the newlyweds celebrated at the Villa Durazzo, for a champagne reception, which continued on until 4am.

A live band played a medley of Frank Sinatra songs and enjoyed a £50,000 firework display.

True to form, the couple reportedly decided against sophisticated fare, opting for a sit-down meal of pizza.

In recent months, the ex-Everton and Manchester United striker has been candid about turning his life around.

He told his fellow contestants last week how his eldest son Kai, 17, found him passed out on his pool table after a drinking session.

Earlier this year, The Match of the Day pundit decided to give up alcohol after Coleen grew tired of his wild partying – and since then he has lost an astonishing three stone, and has won plaudits from viewers for how well he is looking.

As he follows his wife into the jungle during this year’s I’m A Celebrity – Coleen came second in the ITV show two years ago – he told The Mail on Sunday: ‘I have stopped drinking for six months and Coleen is happy because from a point of view of our marriage we are in a much better place and a much happier place, so that obviously makes a difference.

‘She has said I look better, but from a mental point of view I am in a much better place as well. I think I am in a really good place, a really happy place.’
