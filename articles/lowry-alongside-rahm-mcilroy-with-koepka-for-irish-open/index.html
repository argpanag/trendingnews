Lowry alongside Rahm, McIlroy with Koepka for Irish Open

TheTools · 08.09.2026 15:31 · source · permalink

Shane Lowry and Rory McIlroy have been given marquee groupings for rounds one and two of the 2026 Amgen Irish Open at Trump Doonbeg.

The Ryder Cup winning duo have been given a late-early split starting at 1.35pm on Thursday afternoon. McIlroy kicks off the wave alongside Scotland's Robert MacIntyre and five-time major winner Brooks Koepka.

They are followed ten minutes by Lowry, and his playing partners Patrick Reed and Jon Rahm. Both are making welcome returns to the event, with Rahm a two-time past champion, while Reed currently leads the Race to Dubai standings.

Northern Ireland's Tom McKibbin tees off from 1.15pm alongside European Ryder Cup captain Luke Donald, and Alex Fitzpatrick.

Seamus Power and Pádraig Harrington headline the morning wave, as they tee off shortly after 9am. Power is alongside Sepp Straka and Joaquin Niemann while Harrington has been paired with Eugenio Chacarra of Spain and Tyrell Hatton of England.

There are ten Irish players in action this week with Mark Power, Cormac Sharvin, Thomas Higgins, Max Kennedy and Liam Nolan all teeing it up.
