Borussia Dortmund fight back to draw with Arsenal

Arsenal failed to make the most of their first-half dominance as they were held to a frustrating draw by Borussia Dortmund in the Champions League.

Mikel Arteta made six changes to the side that drew with Newcastle at the weekend with Viktor Gyokeres coming into the starting line-up for the league-phase encounter.

And it was the Swedish striker who gave the Gunners the lead when he struck a powerful looping effort from the edge of the box over Gregor Kobel in the Dortmund goal.

Arsenal were dominant after that and went close through Christos Tzolis, Noni Madueke and Declan Rice but only took the single goal lead into half-time.

That proved to be costly as Dortmund were much improved after the break, and levelled when centre-back Joane Gadou carried the ball from inside his own half before teeing up Maximilian Beier, who finished excellently into the corner in the 55th minute.

The Bundesliga leaders thought they had completed the turnaround when Jobe Bellingham tapped in from close range but the flag was up for offside.

Mikel Arteta made a triple change as he pushed for a win and the Gunners had a golden opportunity when Gyokeres got on the end of a cross inside the six-yard box but mistimed his header wide of the goal.

The result leaves Arsenal fifth in the league table on 11 points while Dortmund have a point less and sit seventh.

Kepa Arrizabalaga: Came in for the injured David Raya and did not have too much to do. Not as assured at coming to claim crosses as the Arsenal number one. 6

Ben White: The right-back has been in great form this season and was solid again before he was replaced in the second half. 7

Ezri Konsa: Konsa is becoming a reliable fixture in the Arsenal backline. 7

Gabriel Magalhaes: The Brazilian was beaten easily as Maximilian Beier slotted in the equaliser. 6

Piero Hincapie: The left-back took up some adventurous positions as he was given a lot of attacking freedom by Mikel Arteta. 6

Declan Rice: The usual good performance from Rice, including one crunching tackle which got the crowd off their feet. 7

Myles Lewis-Skelly: Slotted into the midfield with ease as he so often does. Nearly caught in possession in a dangerous area but a decent night. Subbed in second half. 6

Martin Odegaard: Dictated the pace of the attacking play and added energy when Arsenal pressed the ball. 7

Noni Madueke: Continued his recent excellent form. Started with two dangerous crosses and got the assist for the Arsenal goal. 8

Christos Tzolis: A disappointing night going forward and was shrugged off by Joane Gadou as he set up the Dortmund equaliser. 5

Viktor Gyokeres: Scored an excellent opening goal with a powerful effort from the edge of the box. A mixed night after that and missed a huge chance to win the game. 7
