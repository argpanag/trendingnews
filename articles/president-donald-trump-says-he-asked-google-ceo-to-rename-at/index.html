At a South Carolina rally on Wednesday, Oct. 21, Donald Trump said he explored renaming the Atlantic Ocean, according to The Guardian. He said he contacted Google Maps and spoke with CEO Sundar Pichai about changing the Atlantic Ocean's label. Donald Trump, 80, recounted that Pichai declined, saying oceans span many countries. The remarks followed his January 2025 order to label the Gulf of Mexico the Gulf of America for U.S. federal use and a push to rename Lake Ontario, which Google Maps showed for U.S. users. He also floated "Trump Strait" for the Strait of Hormuz and suggested renaming New Mexico to "New America."
