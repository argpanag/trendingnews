AIB Munster Club SFC: Nemo cruise to victory

TheTools · 22.11.2026 14:40 · source · permalink

Conor Horgan, Nemo Rangers, and Culann Quigley, Fr. Casey's, in AIB Munster Club SHC action. Photo by Paul Phelan/Sportsfile

AIB Munster Club SFC semi-final

Nemo Rangers 3-16 Fr Casey’s 0-12

By Stephen Barry at Páirc Uí Rinn

Nemo Rangers became the first-ever club to reach 20 provincial finals after tearing Fr Casey’s to shreds in a 13-point victory at Páirc Uí Rinn.

Nemo led 3-9 to 0-2 at half-time before the Abbeyfeale side clawed back some respectability on the scoreboard, winning the second half by 0-10 to 0-7.

Nemo tallied nine different scorers, led by 1-2 each for Mark Cronin, Luke Horgan, and Ross Corkery.

Starting with the wind, Fr Casey’s pointed inside 40 seconds through Daniel Daly, but had a half-hour wait until adding to their account in first-half stoppage time.

Their lead lasted five minutes until Kevin O’Donovan’s piercing run took out three defenders, despite being fouled, and offloaded to Horgan. Playing with advantage, the centre-forward seized the opportunity to beat another man and lash a shot from the 13 into the far corner.

O’Donovan got back to block Fr Casey’s next shot, which resulted in a Ronan Dalton point. Seconds later, Dalton was in again and chose to sell a dummy before squaring for Corkery to palm home.

In the 11th minute, O’Donovan was tripped by Seán Kilbridge. Referee Eoin Morrissey black-carded the full-back before awarding a penalty, which Cronin tucked into the far corner.

Corner-back Kieran Histon was next to breach the cover, with his drop-kick whizzing over the crossbar.

With Bríain Murphy and Horgan tagging on points, Nemo had returned 3-7 from their first 10 shots for a 15-point lead after 19 minutes.
After Cronin dropped a 13-metre free short, the Cork star and Jack Horgan booted two more.

Sam Quigley earned a late goal chance for the visitors, which Micheál Aodh Martin was alert to save. Eliah Riordan slotted the 45 for 3-9 to 0-2 at half-time.

Riordan added a free on the restart before landing the game’s first two-pointer. Adrian Enright kicked a brace as the Fr Casey’s tally began to look more respectable.

By then, Nemo had withdrawn Cronin as they chose to rest some key players while running their bench. Bryan Hayes, who has been nursing a knock, came on to score back-to-back points.

Dalton won and converted a two-point free before Fr Casey's landed the last four on the trot, including a third for Enright.

Scorers for Nemo Rangers: Mark Cronin (1-0p, 1f), Luke Horgan, Ross Corkery 1-2 each, Ronan Dalton 0-4 (1tpf), Bryan Hayes 0-2, Kieran Histon, Jack Horgan, Bríain Murphy, Conor Horgan 0-1 each.

Scorers for Fr Casey’s: Eliah Riordan 0-4 (1tp, 1 45, 1f), Adrian Enright 0-3, Daniel Daly, Michael Kilbridge, Ronan Quirke (f), Jamie O’Connor (f), Leon Curtin 0-1 each.

Nemo Rangers: Micheál Aodh Martin; Conor Moore, Mark Hill, Kieran Histon; Jack Horgan, Colin Molloy, Stephen Cronin; Alan O’Donovan, Bríain Murphy; Mark Cronin, Luke Horgan, Kevin O’Donovan; Ross Corkery, Ronan Dalton, Conor Horgan.

Subs: Kevin Fulignati for Hill (40), Bryan Hayes for M Cronin (40), Conor Shalloe for J Horgan (46), Barry Cripps for A O’Donovan (46), Dara O’Sullivan for Corkery (50).

Fr Casey's: Shane O’Connell; Danny McCarthy, Seán Kilbridge, Matthew O’Connor; Mark McCarthy, Diarmuid Buckley, Daniel Daly; Culann Quigley, Sam Quigley; Rory O’Brien, Eliah Riordan, Michael Kilbridge; Jamie O’Connor, Ronan Quirke, Adrian Enright.

Subs: Oisín Woulfe for M O’Connor (HT), Cathal Woulfe for Daly (h-t), Eoghan O’Connell for O’Brien (44), Leon Curtin for Quirke (44), John Riordan for C Quigley (51).

Referee: Eoin Morrissey (Waterford).
