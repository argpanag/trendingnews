Tennis star Alexander Bublik destroys racket before smashing drinks container in latest on-court outburst

TheTools · 16.10.2026 12:11 · source · permalink

The world’s most free-thinking newspaper

Tennis star Alexander Bublik destroyed his racket before smashing a drinks container in his latest on-court outburst at the Shanghai Masters.

The world no 22 expressed his anger after losing a close first set to Brandon Nakashima.

Bublik lost the first-set tie-break 9-7 and smashed his racket on the ground five times, crushing it.

He then walked over to his chair, kicking his destroyed racket, before hitting his hand through the lid of a drinks cooler.

His hand then started bleeding as he informed the umpire he would need a physio and a three-minute medical time-out.
