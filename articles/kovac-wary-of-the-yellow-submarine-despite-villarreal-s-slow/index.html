Kovac wary of the 'Yellow Submarine' despite Villarreal's slow start

Situation

Borussia Dortmund have made an excellent start to the season. BVB have recorded two wins from two matches in the Bundesliga, while Schwarzgelb also claimed a routine 5-0 victory away to fifth-tier side HEBC in the DFB-Pokal. After last Saturday’s spectacular comeback at TSG 1899 Hoffenheim, Schwarzgelb now feel ready for their first Champions League night of the season, for which a limited number of tickets are still available in the online ticket shop.

Villarreal CF, meanwhile, are still searching for the form they showed last season, when the club from eastern Spain finished an impressive third in La Liga behind FC Barcelona and Real Madrid, securing Champions League qualification once again ahead of Atletico Madrid and Real Betis. Last Saturday, the “Yellow Submarine” suffered a 3-2 home defeat to promoted side Deportivo A Coruña.

Squad

In the 3-2 away win in Sinsheim, centre-back Filippo Mane picked up an injury in the first half and had to be substituted after just 32 minutes. Head coach Niko Kovac said of his condition: “We expect that nothing will happen before the international break. He will certainly only return after that.”

In addition to the young Italian, the injured Giannis Konstantelias, Emre Can and Nico Schlotterbeck are also still unavailable, with Schlotterbeck suspended anyway following a red card. Ramy Bensebaini is also still serving over a second-yellow red card suspension from last season and will likewise not be included in the squad.

Daniel Svensson and Konstantinos Karetsas missed Monday’s final training session (photo gallery) with the team, but according to Kovac they are “perfectly healthy” and were absent only for workload management reasons. (Squad update)

Opposition

Villarreal CF are still without a win in the Spanish league after four matchdays. The 2-2 draws against Racing Santander and Atletico Madrid were followed by 1-0 and 3-2 defeats to Alavés and A Coruña respectively, dropping last season’s third-placed side down to 16th. Only three teams in Spain’s top flight have conceded more goals than Villarreal so far.

Even so, BVB coach Kovac has enormous respect for their opening opponent in Europe’s premier competition: “They finished third last year and are playing in the Champions League for the second year in a row. Normally, the top three places in Spain are already taken. If you break into that phalanx (alongside FC Barcelona, Real Madrid and Atletico Madrid, editor’s note), that says a lot!” said the 54-year-old about this season’s first Champions League opponents.

He also described Villarreal CF as a “technically strong team” and added: “They have a new coach now, but they still maintain the Spanish style of play with a lot of possession. They are technically very clean and very precise in their execution. When they have the ball, this team makes very few mistakes. We need to make sure we reduce the amount of time they have in possession. When we have the ball, they can’t impose their game and have to defend more, which will hopefully suit us better. It will be a very difficult match.”

Villarreal CF are determined to improve on last season’s Champions League campaign and are targeting a place in the play-offs. In 2025/26, the “Yellow Submarine” suffered six more defeats in Europe in addition to their 4-0 loss at Borussia Dortmund and were eliminated in 35th place, second from bottom in the league phase.

Good memories

In BVB’s first meeting with Villarreal, Schwarzgelb celebrated a ultimately comfortable 4-0 win on 25 November 2025, with the Spaniards seriously weakening themselves in the 50th minute when Juan Foyth was shown a red card. At that point, the score had been only 1-0 from the visitors’ perspective. Serhou Guirassy, Karim Adeyemi and Daniel Svensson then made sure of the emphatic home victory with their goals.
