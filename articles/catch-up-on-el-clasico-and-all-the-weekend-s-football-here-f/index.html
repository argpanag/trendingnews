After another midweek of European football, clubs have returned to domestic action, attempting to juggle a busy period as we near the start of November. Our Football Tracker is the place to be for all the key coverage.

Sunday's highlights

• The headline match of the weekend saw Real Madrid stun Barcelona 2-1 in the first Clasico of the season to blow the LaLiga title race back open, thanks to a clinical brace from Kylian Mbappe

• Man Utd's season went from bad to worse at Old Trafford, where Michael Carrick's side were thrashed 3-0 by Bournemouth. Elsewhere in England, Liverpool saw off high-flying Brighton and Newcastle got the better of Crystal Palace

• Bayern Munich proved too strong for Freiburg to return to the Bundesliga summit, after a clash at the top of Ligue 1 between Monaco and Lille ended all square

• Inter moved joint-top of Serie A with a last-gasp win at home to Fiorentina, before promoted side Frosinone continued their stunning start to the season with a 4-4 draw away to Atalanta. In the evening, Juventus beat Lecce and unbeaten AC Milan took down Udinese
