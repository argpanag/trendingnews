Grand Theft Auto 6: The Festival Revealed by Rockstar Games as Launch Event

TheTools · 09.11.2026 14:22 · source · permalink

Rockstar Games has unveiled Grand Theft Auto 6: The Festival, an in-person event in Miami being hosted to celebrate the launch of GTA 6. It’s set to feature a ‘star-studded lineup’ that represents a fair chunk of the game’s many radio stations, as well as the official soundtrack. It’s hosted by Rockstar Games and PlayStation, and it’s coming on November 21.

Those who want to attend the event can sign up for a pre-sale lottery via DICE. The lottery closes tomorrow at 16:00 ET, and the winning entries will be drawn at 19:00 ET. Once completed, the tickets will go on sale at 12:00 PM ET on Wednesday, November 11.

Enjoy The GTA 6 Festival

The Grand Theft Auto 6: The Festival event is likely going to be the place to be for any GTA fan. It’s set to be hugely exclusive, as attendees can only get through the door after winning a random lottery. I mean, there will be plenty of folks invited, so you’ll likely be rubbing shoulders with the best of them, but the general public can only get in via the lottery.

Rockstar Games revealed the full list of performers when they announced the event:

  • Cactus Jack
  • Travis Scott
  • Don Toliver
  • Kodak Black
  • Sofaygo
  • Cardi B
  • Ca7riel
  • Paco Amoroso
  • Justice
  • Metro Boomin
  • Nick León B2B DJ Python
  • Prospa B2B Skream
  • Rauw & Friends
  • Suero
  • Tainy
  • The Dare B2B Fcukers

In the spiel for the event on the Rockstar Games website, the team wrote:

Experience the music of Vice City and Leonida at Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival, a special live event on November 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami to celebrate the launch of GTAVI, featuring a star-studded lineup of artists from the game and the official soundtrack.

Experience the music of Vice City and Leonida at Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival, a special live event on November 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami to celebrate the launch of GTAVI, featuring a star-studded lineup of artists from the game and the official soundtrack.

To try and get tickets for the GTA 6: The Festival event, you need to head to Rockstar Games’ site to apply for the lottery, but you’ll need to act fast.
