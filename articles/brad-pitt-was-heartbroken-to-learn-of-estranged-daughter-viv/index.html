Brad Pitt may be estranged from daughter Vivienne Jolie, but a source tells In Touch that doesn't mean he's stopped worrying about her, especially following news of the teen's hospitalization for an eating disorder.

"Brad is heartbroken that Viv went through something so awful," shares the source. "He just hopes and prays that she's getting all the support she needs and that she's thriving again. That's all he wants for his kids."

As In Touch previously reported, the actor's youngest daughter with ex-wife Angelina Jolie has revealed that she sought treatment earlier this year after a private struggle that left her feeling "angry, sick and very alone."

Budding artist Vivienne, 18, made the revelation while explaining the inspiration behind a recent exhibition of her artwork in Los Angeles, which featured illustrations alongside journal entries from April and May when she was in the hospital, reported Entertainment Tonight.

"Brad is really proud of her for being brave enough to talk about what she's been through," adds In Touch's source. "He knows that can't have been easy for Viv. It takes real guts to be so vulnerable."

Steven Bergman/AFF-USA.COM / MEGA

Shortly before the young creative's secret battle came to light, a judge granted her request to legally change her name from Vivienne Marcheline Jolie-Pitt to Vivienne Marcheline Jolie on November 2.

She became the fourth of Pitt's children to legally drop his last name, following in the footsteps of older siblings Maddox, 25, Zahara, 21, and Shiloh, 20.

"Viv's made it pretty clear she doesn't want a relationship with Brad," the source notes. "He knows he has to respect her wishes, as much as it hurts. More than anything, Brad just wants to see her happy and healthy."
