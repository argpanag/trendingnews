NBA GAME THREAD: Miami Heat (2-5) look to bounce back against struggling LA Clippers (1-6)

TheTools · 05.11.2026 03:31 · source · permalink

The Miami Heat continue their West Coast road trip tonight as they visit the Los Angeles Clippers at Intuit Dome, looking to bounce back from a disappointing defeat against the Lakers.

Miami enters Wednesday’s matchup with a 2-5 record after suffering a 138-117 loss to the Lakers on Monday night, snapping the Heat’s two-game winning streak.

Giannis Antetokounmpo continued his strong start to the season with 30 points, 12 rebounds and seven assists, while Klay Thompson added 19 points and Bam Adebayo contributed 16 points and nine rebounds. Unfortunately, the Heat couldn’t slow down Los Angeles defensively, allowing the Lakers to shoot over 57 percent from the field.

Defense has been one of Miami’s biggest concerns through the opening seven games, and Erik Spoelstra will be looking for a much stronger effort tonight.

The Clippers have endured an even more difficult start, entering the game at 1-6 and riding a five-game losing streak. Led by Darius Garland, Los Angeles has struggled to find consistency and will also be without several important players due to injuries.

For the Heat, the goal is simple: tighten things up defensively, continue building chemistry between Antetokounmpo and Adebayo, and get back into the win column before facing the Golden State Warriors on Thursday night.

Can Miami take advantage of a struggling Clippers team and get its road trip back on track?
