Renée Slegers has made one change to her starting line-up for this evening’s UEFA Women’s Champions League clash away to Barcelona.

Following Sunday’s 2-1 win away to Liverpool in the Women’s Super League, Frida Maanum comes into the side with Olivia Smith making way, so we expect that to mean Alessia Russo starts up top with Lisa Baum and Caitlin Foord on the wings.

Georgia Stanway and Kim Little keep their places in midfield and the defence is as you were too, with Misa Rodriguez behind a back four of Emily Fox, Leah Williamson, Steph Catley and Taylor Hinds.

Ona Batlle returns from injury for a place on the bench.

LINE-UPS

Barcelona XI: Cata Coll, Camara, Irene Paredes, Van Asten, Brugts, Serrajordi, Patri Guijarro (C), Schertenleib, Graham Hansen, Pajor, Claudia Pina

Substitutes: Gemma Font, McCamey, Marta Torrejon, Aitana Bonmati, Carla Julia, Martina, Julia Torres, Rosalia, Noa Jimenez

Arsenal XI: Misa, Fox, Williamson, Catley, Hinds, Stanway, Little (C), Maanum, Baum, Foord, Russo

Substitutes: Borbe, Damm, Wubben-Moy, De Almeida, Batlle, Holmberg, Cooney-Cross, Reuteler, Smith, Dixon
