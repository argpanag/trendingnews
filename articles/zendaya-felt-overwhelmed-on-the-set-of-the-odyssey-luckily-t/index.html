Zendaya “Felt Overwhelmed” on the Set of ‘The Odyssey.’ Luckily, Tom Holland Came to Support Her

TheTools · 16.11.2026 18:30 · source · permalink

On the set of The Odyssey, in which Zendaya plays the goddess Athena, there were some particularly challenging days, as the actor recalled during a recent special screening attended by the cast at Los Angeles’s Academy Museum of Motion Pictures. While filming certain scenes—especially those with a crowd of extras—the 30-year-old actor felt “incredibly nervous,” she said.

How did she manage to keep the situation under control? Thank her husband, Tom Holland, whom Zendaya says went to set that day to cheer her up. Spider-Man: Brand New Day star Holland also appears in The Odyssey as Telemachus, son of Odysseus and Penelope. And although he wasn’t scheduled to work at that particular moment, he showed up anyway to support his wife.

Matt Damon’s Odysseus consults with Zendaya’s Athena in The Odyssey.

“I felt overwhelmed, to say the least,” Zendaya said at the screening, as reported by the Daily Mail, with the actor looking stunning in her cream-colored one-shoulder dress, pixie cut, and Christian Louboutin stilettos. “It was interesting because I was on a night shoot, and I remember I came in and I was like, This is like, a pinch-me moment…. I’m in front of these people who I’ve admired, but I’m just like, I don’t want to mess this up.”

While she admitted that these emotions might have worked for her character, they were not so good for her state of mind: “I was incredibly nervous, which I guess is helpful for the scene, but there’s so many other background actors as well who are performing their hearts out—so many—stunt performers who are performing and giving the most beautiful performances.”

Tom Holland in a scene from The Odyssey

The turning point came when Holland stepped behind the scenes to encourage her. “It helped that Tom came to set that day, and he was, like, in the corner like, ‘You got this, you’re doing good.’” It was a team effort.

Zendaya and Holland are one of Hollywood’s most admired couples, not only for their discretion (they quietly married last August 4 in Surrey) but also for the care with which they address sensitive issues, such as mental health. Even in the cult series Euphoria, Zendaya’s character, Rue, grapples with psychological issues—in addition to addiction—as a way to raise public awareness and combat the stigma that often surrounds these kinds of situations.
