These Backstreet Boys fans say their 'obstructed view' seats were relocated to worse spots

TheTools · 31.10.2026 00:34 · source · permalink

Some Backstreet Boys fans aren't happy with their new seats for the group's Toronto residency. They say presale tickets they bought were later reclassified as partially obstructed. Live Nation promised to relocate them to better seats, but some say their new seats are actually worse. CBC's Tyler Cheese reports.
