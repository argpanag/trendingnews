Winning Lotto and Thunderball numbers for Saturday's £2m lottery jackpot

TheTools · 03.10.2026 19:17 · source · permalink

Need to know

Two Lotto draw machines(Image: Allwyn/PA)

Everything you need to know about the Lotto and Thunderball draws on Saturday, October 3, including how much was up for grabs and the winning numbers.

  1. The Lotto jackpot was a life-changing £2m.
  2. The Thunderball game has a huge top prize of £500,000.
  3. The National Lottery has introduced a new two-round format for the Lotto game. It gives players two chances to win.
  4. The Lotto draw took place at 8pm.
  5. The winning Lotto numbers in round one are: 15, 37, 39, 42, 46, 53, and the Bonus Ball is 33.
  6. The winning Lotto numbers in round two are: 5, 11, 12, 44, 52, 54, and the Bonus Ball is 1.
  7. The Thunderball draw was at 8.15pm.
  8. The winning Thunderball numbers are: 15, 19, 20, 30, 35, and the Thunderball is 2.
