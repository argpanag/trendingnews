Kings Sign Gabe Vincent | Sacramento Kings

TheTools · 26.11.2026 04:51 · source · permalink

The Sacramento Kings announced today that the team has signed guard Gabe Vincent. Per team policy, terms of the deal were not disclosed.

During the 2025-26 season, Vincent averaged 4.4 points, 1.3 rebounds and 1.4 assists in 16.6 minutes per game over 53 games with the Atlanta Hawks and Los Angeles Lakers. The seven-year veteran has appeared in 331 career regular season games (86 starts), averaging 6.7 points, 1.5 rebounds and 1.9 assists in 20.3 minutes per game with Miami (2019-23), Los Angeles (2023-26) and Atlanta. 

A Modesto, Calif., native, Vincent has made 60 career postseason appearances (30 starts), accruing averages of 7.9 points, 1.4 rebounds and 2.5 assists in 23.2 minutes per contest. He played two seasons for the Stockton Kings, Sacramento’s G League affiliate, from 2018-20.
