AFLW team selection: One change for Roos

TheTools · 13.09.2026 05:46 · source · permalink

The Round 5 team is in.

CARLTON has confirmed its team for Sunday’s Round 5 clash with North Melbourne.

Making the quick trip up to Arden Street Oval, the Blues will head into the AFLW SuperClash with one change to the side that defeated Collingwood last week.

Gab Pound comes back into the line up, replacing Keeley Sherar, who will be unavailable for the clash due to a shoulder injury.

Sherar will aim to be available for selection ahead of the Blues' match against Melbourne in Round 6.

The Blues will be looking to make it five straight wins to begin the 2026 season when they take on the Roos on Sunday.

The game will also mark a special milestone for popular defender Amelia Velardo, who will play her 50th AFLW game.

Carlton's three emergencies are Kiara Bischa, Lily Baxter and Tayla McMillan.

Sunday's game begins at 3:05pm and will be broadcast live on Channel 7, Fox Footy and Kayo Sports.
