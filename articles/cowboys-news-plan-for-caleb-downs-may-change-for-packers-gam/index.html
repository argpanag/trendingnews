Rookie Caleb Downs could be used in a different way on Sunday against the Green Bay Packers than he has been over the last few weeks. Mike Pettine is the new senior defensive assistant for the Dallas Cowboys, and the hope is that his experience brings some hope to a struggling unit.

Cowboys could have new plan for rookie Caleb Downs ahead of Sunday’s showdown vs. Packers - Joseph Hoyt, Dallas Morning News

The Cowboys have adjusted how they’ve used Caleb Downs over the last few games due to players being banged up. However, a change back to his original usage could be coming.

FRISCO — The Cowboys have used rookie first-round pick Caleb Downs mostly at safety over the last two games due to injuries in the team’s secondary.

“That’s not the design for Caleb,” Cowboys coach Brian Schottenheimer said this week. “We want Caleb down near the line of scrimmage.”

Pettine ready to lend support, experience to Cowboys’ defense - Todd Archer, ESPN

Mike Pettine is now in Dallas after he planned on being away from the league for a while. His mission is to give everything he’s got for his close friend Christian Parker as a senior defensive assistant.

The subject of joining the staff was first broached in the winter, but Pettine had recently announced his retirement. Pettine visited the Cowboys in the spring and was with the coaches for a few days.

Pettine will be in the coaches booth during games while Parker will remain on the sideline.

“I’m just obviously trying to hit the ground running here,” Pettine said. “I feel like I’m trying to sip water from a firehose but trying to get caught up on all the film and make observations here and there. But I’m just here to support him, and both him and [head coach Brian Schottenheimer] and the rest of the defensive staff, in any way I can.”
