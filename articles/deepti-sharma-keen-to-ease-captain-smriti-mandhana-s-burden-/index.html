Deepti Sharma keen to ease captain Smriti Mandhana's burden in new vice-captaincy role

TheTools · 22.10.2026 15:55 · source · permalink

Deepti Sharma is ready to shoulder the added responsibility of being India's vice-captain and help Smriti Mandhana settle into her new role as skipper. With India set to face Zimbabwe in a three-match ODI series starting Friday, Deepti said her focus would be on helping the team win matches and supporting Mandhana whenever needed.
