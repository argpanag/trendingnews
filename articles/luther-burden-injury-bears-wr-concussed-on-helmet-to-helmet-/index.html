Luther Burden injury: Bears WR concussed on helmet-to-helmet hit

TheTools · 09.11.2026 04:35 · source · permalink
  • NFL

The Bears WR took an ugly hit and instantly dropped the ball.

Update: Bears head coach Ben Johnson said Burden has a concussion after the game.

The Chicago Bears threw a screen pass to Luther Burden on a critical third down late in the fourth quarter against the Tampa Bay Buccaneers on Sunday Night Football in Week 9 and watched the worst possible scenario play out. Burden fumbled the ball when he tackled short of the sticks, and then exited the game with a possible head injury. Tampa Bay would take over in premium field position, and tie the game a few plays later.

Burden caught a 56-yard touchdown pass earlier in the game by showcasing his signature YAC (yards after catch) ability. It looked like Burden took a direct shot to the head from Tampa Bay rookie defensive lineman Rueben Bain and instantly dropped the ball.
