Salmon and Dowman start; Ibrahim and Julienne named on bench as Arsenal face Ipswich

TheTools · 15.09.2026 17:49 · source · permalink

Marli Salmon and Max Dowman have both been named in the starting lineup as the Arsenal first-team face Ipswich away in the Carabao Cup tonight.

Ife Ibrahim and Theo Julienne are both amongst the substitutes, with the latter receiving his first competitive senior callup.
