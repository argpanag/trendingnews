Florence Pugh May Lead "Moneypenny Diaries" Series - Media

TheTools · 03.09.2026 10:10 · source · permalink

The project will tell the secret life of the main girl in James Bond's life.

The British publication Daily Mail named Marvel star Florence Pugh as a possible actress for the role of Miss Moneypenny in the potential series "Moneypenny Diaries".

This information echoes The Sun's rumor from February 2025 and may indicate that over the past year and a half, Amazon MGM has not abandoned the idea of a "James Bond" spin-off series. According to the source, "Moneypenny Diaries" will be based on Samantha Weinberg's trilogy, published under the pseudonym Kate Westbrook.

The books tell the story of Jane Moneypenny's past and complement the events of Ian Fleming's works. The first novel, "Guardian Angel," is set in 1962 against the backdrop of the Cuban Missile Crisis.

However, it is unclear whether Florence Pugh is being considered for the role of Moneypenny in the new James Bond film, which Denis Villeneuve is working on. Such a decision would allow the two projects to be linked, but Amazon MGM has not yet announced the cast of the film, and the name of the new Bond was promised to be revealed by the end of 2026.
