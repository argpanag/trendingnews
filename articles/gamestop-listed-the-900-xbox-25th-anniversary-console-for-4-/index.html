GameStop listed the $900 Xbox 25th Anniversary console for $4,950, then cut it to $1,995

TheTools · 16.11.2026 20:32 · source · permalink

GameStop listed Microsoft's $899.99 25th Anniversary Xbox Series X for $4,950 before cutting it to $1,995 after backlash over the markup.

What you need to know

GameStop listed Microsoft's $899.99 25th Anniversary Xbox Series X at $4,950, then reduced it to $1,995 after backlash. The retailer has not explained the pricing, and it remains unclear whether anyone paid the original price; even the reduced amount is more than double Microsoft's price.

Microsoft's 25th Anniversary Xbox Series X sold out in seconds on November 13, but the bigger story has turned out to be what GameStop did with it.

The retailer was caught listing the $899.99 console for $4,950, a markup of roughly 450%, and this wasn't some random reseller on a marketplace but GameStop itself. Kotaku's John Walker added one to his basket, where shipping and tax pushed the total to $5,311.25. He also found that the link from Microsoft's own site led to that same page.

  • Read more: XBOX Series X25 announced, the translucent console pays homage to the original XBOX

For anyone who missed the hype, the console is a 1TB Series X in translucent green, styled after the original 2001 Xbox, with a matching see-through controller, "XBOX 25" engravings and a copy of Halo: Campaign Evolved. One report says Microsoft made just 25,000 numbered units worldwide, which would help explain why stock vanished so fast. Walmart, for its part, pulled the listing entirely.

GameStop's price didn't jump overnight. An archived listing from October 26 had the console at $999, Reddit users spotted the pre-order price climbing to around $1,500 before launch, and by launch day it sat at $4,950.

After the backlash, it quietly dropped to $1,995, which is still more than double what Microsoft is asking. GameStop hasn't said anything publicly, so there's no confirmation whether this was automated pricing, a mistake, or a deliberate choice, and nobody has confirmed that anyone actually paid $4,950.
