NHL Rumors: Pittsburgh Penguins Have Several Players of Interest

TheTools · 27.11.2026 07:30 · source · permalink

Brian Metzer, host of Penguins Radio Network, joined TFP Hotstove with Ryan Paton and Dave Pagnotta on SiriusXM NHL Network Radio to discuss players like Erik Karlsson, Rickard Rakell, Bryan Rust, and others as the Pittsburgh Penguins consider trades ahead of the deadline.

 NHLRumors.com Transcriptions

NHL Rumors: Pittsburgh Penguins, and the Ottawa Senators

 

Dave Pagnotta: “Well, as we will probably continue to hear this as the season moves along, the trade deadline, as a quick reminder, is March 1st, but they have other pieces. Kyle Dubas has not been shy in saying they’re listening. The biggest asset, I would imagine, would be Erik Karlsson. You alluded to him. The chances of him moving are, I would imagine. Well, I’m hearing pretty high, but what are you hearing out there in Pittsburgh?”

Brian Metzer: “It’s funny you say that, Dave, because I remember when we talked last year. I’m like, Dave, you’ve talked about it. I forget how I phrased it, but I know that’s a trade you’ve heard about. I mean, when you talk to the contacts around the league, I mean, his name has come up in conversation every year since he joined the Penguins, and right ahead of it, and throughout his Penguins tenure.

So I do think that for sure is a deal we’ll end up seeing. He’s an unrestricted guy. He can bring so much to the table. This is one of the most gifted offensive players in the National Hockey League. And last March, I mean, he was the best player in the league for a month-long span, and he just looked in that role. He looks like he can do that again. So a contender is probably salivating. I do think that will happen.

Things sure have changed for the Pittsburgh Penguins since last year

So I don’t know when. There’s talk that they’ll wait until maybe you get past the holidays and kind of like have the conversations and work that out. But Rickard Rakell might be the next one on the list that is very attractive because he comes at an affordable rate, just $5 million per season. He is signed through next year.

The rumors we heard, obviously, with the Chicago situation, were a prospect, first-round pick, roster player to make the money work. So it shows that at least some team was willing to dangle a first in terms of trying to get him. I think that’s what Kyle Dubas would be looking for. I don’t know that he would trade him just because you have him for next year, and even if you are getting younger and you want a viable team that starts taking steps forward, you need NHL players.

I mean, look no further than Chicago because you can’t just tear it down to the studs and never surround your youth with viable NHL talent. Rakell is that, but I think that he can score from anywhere. He also kills penalties. He can play center a little bit if you need him to, and he can kind of just do so many other roles for a team, which makes him an attractive commodity even at 33 years old.

A Few Pittsburgh Penguins Who Could be Moved, and Where Could Erik Karlsson End Up

The other is Bryan Rust, who’s obviously on the shelf right now, but I think contenders would love that whole Mr. Clutch thing that he did during the Penguins’ Stanley Cup run in the past, especially 2016. He was so good then. So keep your eyes on those two guys.

And then there are other guys that obviously are going to come up in terms of unrestricted, smaller deals. Justin Brazeau, an unrestricted free agent. He’s proven he can score some goals at this level. I might keep an eye there. See if they can also maybe find a taker for Sam Girard. He might come up in conversations because he’s had a bounce-back year here in Pittsburgh. I know that he could still be a part of the future at 28 years old, but the offensive game looks back.

The whole Spinorama thing’s been on display, and he’s healthy. The biggest thing for him is that he has been available more often than not, and he looks like the guy he was maybe when he was helping the Avs in a smaller role win the Stanley Cup.”
