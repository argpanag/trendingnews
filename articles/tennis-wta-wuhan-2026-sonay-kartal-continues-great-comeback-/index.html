Sonay Kartal v Elina Svitolina [5] | H2H & Match Fundamentals

Sonay Kartal continues to make ground on her comeback to the tour after an injury break, taking out two seeds on the way and not dropping a set so far. She faces fifth seed Elina Svitolina.

 

Tale of the Tape – Key Stats (Hard Courts – 2026)

Kartal lags behind in all but one stat – overall service points won. However she does push Svitolina close in terms of first serve return points.

 

 

Playing Styles & Prediction

It feels as though Kartal is picking up from the threads of her breakout year, before her back injury caused her to sit out most of this year. Kartal continues to be a bit of enigma to players who have not yet faced her on the tour – as seen by a couple of statement wins against Belinda Bencic and Jasmine Paolini.

She will face Ukraine’s Svitolina – who has enjoyed a real resurgence on the tour. Svitolina’s forst year at this tournament continues to be her best, but a win could equal that semi-final run.

She will feel confident of knowing Kartal’s game, and has thus far got the better of the Brit in their two outings. It feels like Kartal would have to overcome a lot of Svitolina’s current momentum, but Kartal is coming back to a long tour, relatively fresh while other players are beginning to look a little fatigued.

Prediction: Svitolina in three sets.
