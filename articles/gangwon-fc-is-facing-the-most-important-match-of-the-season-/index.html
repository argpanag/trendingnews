Gangwon FC is facing the most important match of the season.

Gangwon will face Incheon United in the 32nd round of the K League 1 of the 2026 season at 4:30 p.m. on October 18 at Gangneung High1 Arena in Gangwon Province.

Gangwon is ranked third with 46 points by the 31st round this season. If Gangwon rings a winning high school against Incheon, it will confirm its entry into Final A.

Gangwon prepared various events for its fans in the final home game of the regular round.

Gangwon will host this home game as "Jeongseon-gun Day." Jeongseon-gun residents can receive a discount on tickets if they visit the on-site ticket office with their ID card. Twenty-four children from Jeongseon-gun Sports Club will participate as escort kids and enter the ground with the players.

Fans who visited the stadium wearing military uniforms for the "National Army Month" will also have more opportunities to be captured on the electronic display.

Before the start of the game, "Welcome Kangwon with Sun Kim" will be held. Fans captured on the electronic display will be given a pass to use the authentic American hot dog restaurant "Sun Kim."

In "Supporting Cam with Sun Kim," fans shout slogans and sing cheering songs with Gangwon supporters Narsha. Fans who showed passionate support will be given a "Sun Kim" voucher.

At halftime, a "kiss time" will be held to capture sweet moments of couple fans. The couple, captured on the electronic display, convey their feelings toward each other with a friendly kiss and leave special memories of the home game.

Couples who participated in the event will be given a two-person "High1" ski lift pass, a two-person "High1 Water World" pass, and a one-night stay at Sky Bay Hotel Gyeongpo.

At the outdoor venue, "Challenge!" There will be a "Korean Navy" booth and a "No Impossible" Navy Special Warfare (UDT/SEAL) experience booth. Fans can receive recruitment guidance and experience special warfare equipment at the experience booth.

A celebratory performance will also be held to pray for the successful hosting of the 2026 Gangneung ITS World Congress, which opens on the 19th. There will also be a promotional booth for High1 Resort and a fan signing event. In the mascot photo zone, you can take a picture of the home game with your family and friends.

Gangwon also paid attention to food.

You can find Gangneung Gilgamja, a famous restaurant in Gangneung, at the stadium. Gangneung Cafe Check East 346 Coffee Story sells coffee, drinks and desserts.

Makgeolli at the Gangneung local brewery "Juruk Juruk Brewery" and beer and potato surprise at "Potato Island" were also prepared.

Six food trucks that sell a variety of menus such as chicken gangjeong, chicken skewers, tteokbokki, fish cakes, New York hot dogs, old-fashioned hot dogs, sotteok sotteok, and whirlwind potatoes also welcome fans.

The food store is open from 1 p.m. to 7 p.m.

The MD Store and Marking Booth are open from 2:30 p.m. to 7 p.m. Four ACL2 jerseys in the 2026-27 season and four K-League jerseys in the 2026 season will be available. Gangwon will sell some items at up to 30% off to mark the last home game of the regular round.

Gangwon will introduce new winter gloves so that they can continue to cheer warmly even in the cold weather.

At the capsule drawing booth, you can see players' English straps and various MD products. The player's actual uniform and the "Clepton" pouch are also prepared as prizes, adding to the fun of picking them out.

Gangwon will also hold an event to match the set time with a stopwatch. Fans who succeed in the challenge will be given a variety of prizes, including coffee bean bread, players' misplaced uniforms, and "Clepton" pouches.

Tickets for the last home game of the regular round between Gangwon and Incheon can be booked at NOL (nol.yanolja.com/ticket) ).

[Lee Geunseung, MK Sports Reporter]
