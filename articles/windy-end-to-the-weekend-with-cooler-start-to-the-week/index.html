SALT LAKE CITY (ABC4) — Happy Sunday, Utah! A warm and windy day across most of the state today with chances of a few rain showers and a cold front working through.

Weather today

Moisture will continue to linger across southeast Utah and for areas east of I-15, leading to a mix of clouds and sunshine for the Wasatch Front and St. George with slight chances of an afternoon rain shower.

The flood risk will remain low but not zero with flash flood potential "Possible" for all of our National Parks with "Probable" for Grand Gulch, Natural Bridges, and Canyonlands. Otherwise expect windy conditions through the day with gusty southwest winds up to 30-35 mph across most of Utah.

Gusty winds Sunday

Daytime highs will warm into the mid and upper 80's for northern Utah with 90's across southern Utah. A cold front will also begin to work its way across northern and central Utah by the evening and overnight hours, which will bring a shift in the winds to a gusty northwesterly flow.

This cold front will bring a much cooler fall-like feel to these areas with overnight lows dipping into the 40's and low 50's for many of our central and northern Utah locations.

Much cooler temperatures Monday

Daytime highs on Monday will run 5-10° below average and a slight crisp in the air with low and mid 70's for northern Utah. Southern Utah will not feel much of the impacts with the associated cold front, as such highs will hover right around average with 90's sticking around.

Moisture will hang around across southeast Utah with chances of a few showers and thunderstorms likely each day during the afternoon hours. Dry conditions will remain in place for the rest of us through Tuesday with sunshine and just a few clouds.

Weather this week

Those looking to head to the state fair Monday and Tuesday should have no problems with the weather aside from cooler temperatures Monday and then a return to 80's by Tuesday.

The drier conditions will come to a bit of end for Wednesday and Thursday as a shortwave trough will dig through the northern portions of Utah and try to pull in moisture. While chances of showers and thunderstorms appear more likely across eastern Utah, the chances still remain fairly low for the Wasatch Front with some uncertainty regarding if any precipitation chances come about.

Though expect an increase in cloud coverage Wednesday and Thursday with slight chances of a shower or thunderstorm for the Wasatch Front. Temperatures will remain right around average with upper 70's and low 80's for northern Utah and low to mid 90's for southern Utah.

Conditions will remain quiet and dry heading into the end of the week and next weekend with temperatures appearing to warm by a few degrees.
