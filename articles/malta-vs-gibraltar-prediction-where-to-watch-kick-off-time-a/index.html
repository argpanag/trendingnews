Malta - On a winning streak

Let’s take a closer look at Malta’s current situation. In the next sections, we will analyse their recent form and tactical approach. We will also show you the expected Malta vs. Gibraltar line-ups for this UEFA Nations League match.

Recent form

Malta enters this UEFA Nations League fixture with strong momentum, having won their last three matches. This run includes friendly victories against Liechtenstein and Azerbaijan, both with clean sheets. They also began their UEFA Nations League campaign with a hard-fought 2-1 away win over Andorra.

Their key attackers powered that crucial opening group win. Midfielder Teuma and forward Cardona were instrumental, both scoring and assisting. This creative spark will be vital as Malta looks to build on their positive start in their first home game to establish a strong group standing.

The home side benefits from no new reported injuries or suspensions. Paul Mbong is set to be among the players to return, while James Carragher is still out.

Gibraltar - Willing to score

Let’s take a look at Gibraltar’s current situation. We will analyse the team’s momentum, their recent results, and key players for this upcoming match.

Recent form

Gibraltar enters this match on a four-match unbeaten streak, which has undoubtedly lifted team morale. This run includes two high-scoring friendly wins against the Cayman Islands and the British Virgin Islands.

However, they began their UEFA Nations League campaign with a 0-0 draw at home against Andorra. Their recent performances have been built on a solid defensive foundation, securing clean sheets in their last two matches.

Despite their defensive stability, Gibraltar’s attack has been less effective, failing to score in their last two games. Goalkeeper Bradley James Banda and the defence will be key to keeping them in the contest, while ex-Manchester United attacker James Scanlon will try to impress upfront.

Malta"s recent winning streak makes them clear favourites for this encounter against Gibraltar. The home side will likely look to assert control from the start, making a lead at half-time a strong possibility.
