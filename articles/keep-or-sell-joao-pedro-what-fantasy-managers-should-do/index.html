Keep or sell Joao Pedro? What Fantasy managers should do

TheTools · 18.09.2026 18:15 · source · permalink

The Scout assesses the options following the forward's withdrawal from Brazil squad

More than 74 per cent of Fantasy managers have a decision to make over Chelsea forward Joao Pedro (£7.8m), who is an injury doubt for the Blues' Gameweek 5 visit to Brentford following his withdrawal from the Brazil squad.

Joao Pedro will take no part in his country's upcoming international fixtures. However, it is unclear what, if any, injury Joao Pedro has, after Xabi Alonso suggested it is a "possibility" that he features for Chelsea against the Bees on Friday night.

The striker is the most-owned player in Fantasy due to his kind price and excellent form under Alonso, scoring three goals and supplying three assists to collect 33 points, placing him joint-second among all players in the first four Gameweeks of 2026/27 Fantasy.

As well as his fine form, there are two other reasons why Joao Pedro's owners should simply bench him for Gameweek 5.

Firstly, there is now a three-week break before Chelsea's Gameweek 6 home match against AFC Bournemouth on Saturday 10 October, which should give their frontman plenty of time to recover from any potential knock or injury.

Furthermore, Alonso's side also have one of the very best long-term schedules to invest in.

According to the Fixture Difficulty Ratings (FDR), just one of Chelsea's eight matches between Gameweeks 6-13 score more than three, which should gives Joao Pedro every chance to continue his great early output.

If you're now struggling to field a starting XI or are simply looking to bring in another forward into your squad in order to bench Joao Pedro, there are a number of budget-friendly forwards who have favourable short-term schedules who can go alongside Erling Haaland (£15.5m) in your line-up.

Everton's Thierno Barry (£5.6m), Newcastle United's Yoane Wissa (£6.2m) and Nottingham Forest's Liam Delap (£5.5m) will each play a promoted club twice in the next four Gameweeks, while Fulham's Gonzalo Garcia (£6.0m) faces all three of them in the same period.

The table below shows that Barry and Wissa face a promoted club in each of the next two Gameweeks and can immediately reward your investment.

Barry hosts Ipswich Town this weekend then visits Hull City. Wissa, meanwhile, hosts Hull before visiting Coventry City.

Similarly to Barry and Wissa, Delap is at home to a promoted club in Gameweek 5, facing Coventry. He will visit his former club Ipswich in Gameweek 8.

Garcia hosts Manchester United this weekend before a successive run of matches against Ipswich, Hull and Coventry in Gameweeks 6-8.

Although all four of those forwards have scored only once in the first four Gameweeks, their underlying numbers have been encouraging.

Barry's 13 shots in the box and five big chances (situations where a player is expected to score) are both only second only to Haaland this season up front.

Wissa, meanwhile, has had four big chances and created another one of them, which means he's been involved in a total of five big chances. His average of 50.3 minutes per shot, however, is far slower than his rivals.

Similarly to Barry, Garcia is all about the goal threat - 10 of his 12 shots have come from inside the box, while he has created only two chances.

Having only played 188 minutes - at least 140 fewer than the other three - Delap is showing plenty of all-round threat.

His 23.5 minutes per shot is just behind Haaland's 22.5 and his goal conversion rate of 12.5 per cent is the second-best here. He's also created four chances, joint-top with Barry and Wissa.
