Jones returns on one-year deal | Southampton FC | News

TheTools · 04.09.2026 13:28 · source · permalink

Goalkeeper Jamie Jones has re-signed for the club on a contract through until next summer.

Spending the last three months of the 2025/26 season providing support for the goalkeeping department, the experienced shot-stopper returns for a second spell as a Saint.

Amassing over 350 EFL appearances throughout his career, the 37-year-old will once again join the goalkeeping unit for the remainder of the campaign.
