Heat returns briefly before tropical moisture enters the region

TheTools · 30.09.2026 11:22 · source · permalink

BOWLING GREEN, Ky. (WBKO) - We’re starting to see more moisture in the region from southerly air flow. Temperatures will be up in the 90s for today and tomorrow, and humid conditions will return as well.

Tropical moisture from remnants of Hurricane Polo is moving across the Southern Plains, propelled by a slow-moving cold front.

Looking towards Friday, we will start to see that moisture in our region. It will bring rainfall and storms throughout Friday and into the weekend.

As the front slowly pushes eastward, we’ll see skies begin to clear through Sunday and cool conditions return for next week. We’ll see temperatures dip down into the 40s for those morning lows, and only heat up slightly for the afternoons into the low 70s.
