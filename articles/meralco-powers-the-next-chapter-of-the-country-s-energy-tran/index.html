Meralco Powers the Next Chapter of the Country’s Energy Transition - BusinessWorld Online

Through Innovative and Sustained Investments

COMMITTED TO ENHANCING SYSTEM CAPACITY AND RELIABILITY. Meralco remains committed to expanding and upgrading its distribution network to support economic growth, strengthen system redundancy, and deliver high-quality service across its franchise area. In the photo is the upgraded North Port substation which boosted system capacity and reliability for customers in Manila.

The global energy landscape continues to evolve, driven by geopolitical uncertainties, rapid technological advancements, and the growing urgency for climate action. These shifts have been reshaping the way electricity is generated, delivered, and consumed.

For countries like the Philippines that remain exposed to external market dynamics, these developments underscore the importance of ensuring that the power industry remains not only reliable and affordable, but also more resilient and sustainable.

As one of the country’s key energy players, Manuel V. Pangilinan-led Manila Electric Company (Meralco) has been steadfast in investing in the country’s energy future. Meralco’s investments across its various businesses—from power distribution and generation to retail electricity supply and sustainability initiatives—reflect its long-term strategy of building a more resilient energy ecosystem that can withstand evolving market conditions, support the country’s growing electricity demand, and enable the transition toward a cleaner and more decentralized future.

“Meralco recognizes that the persistent conflict in the Middle East continues to exert pressure on global fuel supply and on prices and, consequently, electricity rates. While many of these are beyond our control, we remained focused on what we could manage—working closely with our fuel suppliers and exercising prudent sourcing strategies, strengthening our network, and pursuing efficiencies to contain costs,” Chairman Manuel V. Pangilinan said.

In the first half of 2026, Meralco maintained a stable operational and financial performance – owing largely to its diversified business portfolio that enables it to continue delivering value to its stakeholders.

Building a Smarter, More Resilient Grid
The role of Meralco’s core distribution utility business has been continuously transforming amid growing electricity demand, rapid urbanization, digital technologies, and the increasing adoption of distributed energy resources.

That is why Meralco believes in pouring in capital for strategic investments that modernize, strengthen and expand its distribution network, while making it more resilient, intelligent, and adaptable. In the first half alone, Meralco spent P12.9 billion to fund network modernization, new connections, asset renewals, and other infrastructure projects.

These investments translated into improved operational performance and enabled its millions of customers to experience fewer and shorter power interruptions, recording a System Average Interruption Frequency Index (SAIFI) — or the number of power interruptions each customer experiences — of only 0.426 time; and a System Average Interruption Duration Index (SAIDI) — or average duration of outages per customer — of only 46.575 minutes by end-June.

Further, Meralco continued with its proactive initiatives to minimize both technical losses that are inherent in electricity distribution, as well as non-technical losses through network modernization, operational efficiencies, and anti-pilferage initiatives – helping the company keep its system loss level well below the cap set by the Energy Regulatory Commission (ERC).

“We will carry on with our strategic efforts to harden critical infrastructure, enhance network preparedness, and strengthen emergency response and restoration capabilities to fulfill our mandate of delivering safe, stable, and dependable electricity service to our customers under all operating conditions,” Executive Vice President and Chief Operating Officer Ronnie L. Aperocho said.

Driving the Country’s Clean Energy Transition
As the Philippines accelerates its transition toward a cleaner and more diversified energy mix, Meralco—through its power generation arm Meralco Powergen Corporation (MGEN)—continues to mark progress in the development of renewable energy and emerging technologies that strengthen energy security while supporting the country’s decarbonization goals.

Central to these efforts is MTerra Solar, MGEN’s flagship renewable energy project. In July, President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. inaugurated MTerra Solar Phase 1, just 600 days after groundbreaking—a milestone that reflects the scale and speed at which the project has been delivered.

With 1,373 megawatts (MW) of solar photovoltaic (PV) capacity and 825 MW of battery energy storage already energized, MTerra Solar is now the world’s largest operational integrated solar PV and battery energy storage system facility on a single site. Since its commissioning in March, the project has begun supplying clean energy to the Luzon Grid, contributing to the country’s growing renewable energy capacity.

As MGEN continues to expand its renewable energy footprint, it energized the first phase of its Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) project in Toledo, Cebu last May, enhancing grid reliability and energy security in the Visayas. The company is also exploring renewable energy solutions with Vietnam-based VinEnergo Energy Joint Stock Company that could deliver up to 5 GW of baseload-equivalent capacity, reinforcing its commitment to developing cleaner, more reliable energy solutions for the country.

“For us at MGEN, resilience is measured not only by how we respond to challenges, but by how we continue creating value despite them. The first half of 2026 underscored our commitment, from restoring stability following the Mindanao earthquake and supporting the Visayas grid through periods of supply tightness, to advancing landmark projects like MTerra Solar and strengthening our partnerships with communities across the country,” MGEN President and CEO Emmanuel V. Rubio said.

Creating Shared Value Beyond the Grid
Beyond delivering electricity, Meralco continues to create meaningful and lasting impact through programs that advance sustainability, strengthen communities, and improve the lives of the people it serves.

The company continues to make significant strides in environmental stewardship through initiatives that promote circularity and responsible resource management. In the first half of the year, Meralco’s Race to Zero Waste program achieved a record 98.7% waste diversion rate—the highest since the initiative began in 2019 with only 17%.

Building on this drive to minimize environmental impact, Meralco also expanded its PowerPlants Urban Farm program to 20 farms across its headquarters, sector offices, and business centers – with seven (7) more scheduled to open within the year. By turning open spaces into productive farms, and with the support of nearly 200 employee volunteers and local government partners, the program provides regular harvests of fresh vegetables benefitting more than 700 employees and community members.

Through One Meralco Foundation (OMF), the company energized 761 low-income households in the first half of 2026 under its Household Electrification Program, bringing safe and reliable electricity to underserved communities across Bulacan, Laguna, Pampanga, Pasig City, and Valenzuela City.

OMF likewise continued to harness solar energy to improve access to essential services by energizing four public schools benefiting 426 students and 26 teachers, a rural health station in Negros Occidental serving more than 2,500 residents, an e-tricycle charging station supporting 117 drivers and operators in Valenzuela City, and a solar-powered irrigation system that helps improve the livelihoods of 412 farmers in Nueva Ecija. It also donated multimedia learning equipment to two island schools in Camarines Norte previously electrified through solar power.

Meralco also remained committed to promoting environmental sustainability and community resilience. Through OMF’s One for Trees program, the company partnered with Aklan State University to rehabilitate abandoned fishponds and restore mangrove ecosystems in the Batan Estuary by planting 50,000 mangroves.

Together, these initiatives reflect One Meralco’s commitment to creating value that extends beyond powering homes and businesses—empowering communities, advancing sustainability, and helping build a more resilient future for Filipinos.

“Meralco will continue supporting programs and initiatives that will be beneficial to consumers both in the near-term and in the long run. MTerra Solar, which we inaugurated earlier this month, serves as a tangible demonstration of our commitment to be part of the solution, advance the country’s clean energy transition, and contribute to energy security,” Pangilinan concluded.
