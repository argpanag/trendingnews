Ligue 1 Matchweek 5 Odds, Picks and Best Bets: All 9 Games

TheTools · 17.09.2026 10:40 · source · permalink

Ligue 1 Matchweek 5 runs from Friday, Sept. 18, through Sunday, Sept. 20, and it features early leader AS Monaco hosting a Lens side that just fired its manager, table-topping Lille traveling to a winless Nice, and PSG heading to Marseille as sizable road favorites. Below are the latest Matchweek 5 odds from three US sportsbooks, betting analysis, best bets and score predictions for all nine fixtures.

Four matchweeks into the new Ligue 1 season, the table has already split into recognizable tiers. LOSC Lille, AS Monaco and Stade Rennais share the lead on 10 points apiece, all unbeaten, while Nice, Toulouse and Le Havre are stuck at the bottom with two points each and zero wins between them. Matchweek 5 puts several of those threads on a collision course: Monaco host a Lens side that sacked its manager after four matches, Lille travel to bottom-placed Nice looking to stay perfect, and PSG head to Stade Vélodrome to face a Marseille team that has managed only one win all season while also juggling a Europa League opener against Beşiktaş.

Nine matches across three days means nine different pricing puzzles. FanDuel, BetRivers and LetsBetMD generally agree on the shape of the board's biggest favorites but split by real money on the underdogs and totals, which is where a slate this size tends to hide its value.

Friday Opener: AS Monaco vs Lens

AS Monaco vs Lens

AS Monaco vs Lens (+100 to +110 on Monaco): Monaco sit level on points with the league lead and are unbeaten through four matches (three wins, one draw), with Paris Brunner already up to three Ligue 1 goals this season. The hosts are missing Takumi Minamino to a torn cruciate ligament and carry a lengthy list of doubts, including Folarin Balogun, Ansu Fati and Jordan Teze, but the depth has held up so far. Lens arrive in disarray, having sacked manager Dino Toppmöller after just four matches and handed the job to Yannick Cahuzac on an interim basis; they're also missing centre-back Maik Nawrocki to a cruciate injury. The head-to-head actually favors the visitors, who won 4-1 the last time these two met at Stade Louis II, but a squad in managerial transition rarely travels well. FanDuel's +100 is the shortest price on the hosts of the three books. Prediction: AS Monaco 2-1 Lens.

Saturday's Five-Match Slate

Paris FC vs Strasbourg

Paris FC vs Strasbourg (+100 to +105 on Paris FC): Both sides are unbeaten in their last few outings and sit inside the top six. Paris FC sit fifth with eight points from four matches, and goalkeeper Kevin Trapp already leads the entire league with three clean sheets. Strasbourg sit just a point back in sixth after a 2-1-1 start. Neither club has separated itself defensively from the other, and with two of the league's steadier backlines on the pitch, the tight three-way price makes sense. LetsBetMD and BetRivers share the shortest number on the hosts at +100. Prediction: Paris FC 1-1 Strasbourg.

Toulouse vs Le Havre

Toulouse vs Le Havre (-129 to -140 on Toulouse): This is the day's clearest relegation-zone six-pointer. Toulouse are 17th with two points and are already the league's most heavily carded team, averaging 16.8 fouls per match, while dealing with injuries to Aron Donnum and Rasmus Nicolaisen. Le Havre sit one place above them on the same points total and have won only once in their last 16 top-flight matches, with Félix Mambimbi, Rassoul Ndiaye and Lionel Mpasi all sidelined. Toulouse are unbeaten in their last two home meetings with Le Havre without conceding a goal, which is the strongest angle on the board given how little either side has offered going forward. BetRivers' -129 is the cheapest route onto the home side of the three books, and the Under looks like the more interesting market than the moneyline. Prediction: Toulouse 1-0 Le Havre.

Lyon vs Rennes

Lyon vs Rennes (+106 to +110 on Lyon): Rennes arrive third in the table with 10 points from four matches, powered by striker Esteban Lepaul, who already has three league goals and is drawing France call-up chatter. Lyon sit fourth with eight points of their own and have won their last two home meetings with Rennes, including a 4-2 win in May. Left-back Nicolas Tagliafico is expected to start for Lyon with Abner Vinicius unlikely to feature, while Rennes could be without Djaoui Cissé due to a groin issue. With the head-to-head trend at Groupama Stadium running clearly in the hosts' favor and both teams already inside the top four, this reads as one of the more competitive matches of the weekend. FanDuel and LetsBetMD share the top number on Lyon at +110. Prediction: Lyon 2-1 Rennes.

Le Mans vs Lorient

Le Mans vs Lorient (+148 to +160 on Lorient): Le Mans are still searching for their first win of the season, sitting 14th with three points from three draws and a loss, and the market makes them the road underdog in their own building. Lorient are five places and two points better off in ninth, and midfielder Nathaniel Adjei has played every available minute (360) for the visitors so far. A winless home side facing a team that hasn't missed a minute defensively is exactly the kind of spot where the value sits with the favorite even on the road. LetsBetMD's +160 gives the longest price on Lorient of the three books if you're comfortable laying the extra juice. Prediction: Le Mans 1-2 Lorient.

Angers vs Troyes

Angers vs Troyes (+114 to +125 on Angers): Angers are still looking for their first home points of the season and sit 11th with four points, while Troyes are level on points in 12th and trying to avoid a third straight league loss for the first time since 2023. Angers are without Haris Belkebla through a leg injury, and Troyes will likely be missing Yvann Titi while Ismaël Boura is a doubt. The number that stands out here is history: Angers have won six straight meetings with Troyes across all competitions, which is almost certainly why the hosts are favored despite their own poor home form. LetsBetMD's +125 is the best number on the home side of the three books. Prediction: Angers 1-0 Troyes.

Sunday Closes With Le Classique Watch

Auxerre vs Brest

Auxerre vs Brest (+135 to +148 on Brest): The moneyline favors the road team here, and the table backs it up: Brest sit seventh with five points, three places and two points clear of 15th-placed Auxerre. The underlying numbers explain the gap even further: Brest rank second in the entire league for expected goals this season, and striker Kamory Doumia is tied for the Ligue 1 goal-scoring lead with four while also ranking second in individual xG. Auxerre's Lassine Sinayoko is tied at the top of that same scoring chart, but the club has won just once in four matches. BetRivers' +148 is the longest price on Brest of the three books. Prediction: Auxerre 1-2 Brest.

Nice vs Lille

Nice vs Lille (+102 to +107 on Lille): Lille arrive as the only unbeaten team left in Ligue 1 with a share of the league lead, and goalkeeper Berke Özer is tied with Kevin Trapp for the most clean sheets in the division at three. Nice, by contrast, are rock bottom with two points and zero wins in four matches. This is the most one-sided price on the board outside of Sunday's late kickoff, and with Lille both unbeaten and defensively sound on the road, there isn't much reason to fade the favorite. LetsBetMD's +107 is the best number on the away side of the three books. Prediction: Nice 0-2 Lille.

Marseille vs PSG

Marseille vs PSG (-245 to -280 on PSG): Marseille have managed just one win in four league matches and sit 13th, and they're also juggling a Europa League opener against Beşiktaş this week, a scheduling squeeze manager Bruno Genesio has openly acknowledged. PSG are eighth on the table but priced as the heaviest favorite of the weekend regardless of venue, reflecting the gap in squad quality between the two clubs right now. Forward Amine Gouiri is tied for the Ligue 1 scoring lead with four goals for Marseille, and he's the biggest reason the hosts aren't a complete write-off at home, but backing a team that's split its focus across two competitions against the league's deepest roster is a tough ask. BetRivers' +525 is the longest price on the home longshot of the three books, and Marseille's spread of alternate lines is worth a look for anyone who thinks Vélodrome's atmosphere keeps this closer than the moneyline suggests. Prediction: Marseille 1-2 PSG.

  2. Toulouse to beat Le Havre (-129 at BetRivers) — Toulouse are unbeaten in their last two home meetings with Le Havre without conceding, and Le Havre have won only once in 16 top-flight matches. Eleven cents cheaper than FanDuel on the same outcome.
  3. Under on Toulouse vs Le Havre's goal total — Neither team has scored freely this season, and the head-to-head trend at this venue backs a low-event match over either side finding the net twice.
  4. Longshot flier — Marseille to beat PSG (+550 at FanDuel or LetsBetMD) — PSG are the deepest squad in the league, but Marseille get a full price at home with a Europa League fixture on PSG's midweek radar as well, and Amine Gouiri gives the hosts a puncher's chance.
