THREE IN A ROW | Heat Stars Shine | Brisbane Heat

TheTools · 12.11.2026 09:31 · source · permalink

The Brisbane Heat have turned the tables on the Melbourne Stars in Melbourne today, powering to their third WBBL win in a row.

The Heat held firm with the ball against a confident Melbourne Stars line-up, restricting the home side to 165 on an ideal batting track.

Brisbane remained composed, despite losing top order trumps Grace Harris (2) and the in-form Charli Knott (30).

Captain Georgia Redmanyne and Irish international Orla Prendergast were untroubled as they kept the run-rate in check with regular boundaries, before combining for a mighty 32-run Power Surge return.

Redmayne finished with a superb 74 not out from 50 balls, while Prendergast, in her first season with the Heat, was outstanding as she hit 52 not out from 36 balls.

Brisbane finished 2-166 with an over to spare to make in three wins in succession.

The result lifted them into third spot on the WBBL ladder.

Earlier, the Heat attack rebuilt their innings in the field with leg-spinner Sarah Glenn producing a crucial spell of 1-25 from four overs that included the big scalp of Meg Lanning for 52.

Allrtounder Sianna Ginger was also impressive, picking up 2-22 from three overs including top-scorer Amy Jones (74) via a sharp stumping from Redmayne.
