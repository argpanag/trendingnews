BLAST SLAM IX's action-packed Group Stage concluded on Tuesday (24 November) with eight out of the 16 participating teams surviving the GSL-style preliminary stage to advance to the next stage of the tournament. Taking place shortly after the release of the massive 7.42 update, BLAST SLAM IX serves as an exciting preview for how the Dota 2 metagame will look like as the season heads into 2027. 

European powerhouses Team Yandex, PARIVISION, and 1W Team, as well as rising North American squad GamerLegion, topped their respective groups and have all secured a spot in the Playoffs. Those four squads also advance to the Seeding Matches, where two direct berths to the upper bracket semifinals in the Playoffs will be up for grabs.

OG, Xtreme Gaming (XG), Team Liquid, and Aurora Gaming finished second in their respective groups to advance to the Last Chance stage. There, they will be fighting for the last two spots in the Playoffs.

Meanwhile, Natus Vincere (NAVI), Team Nemesis, DO KONCA, LGD Gaming, PlayTime, Team Spirit, LEGION, and Astra Invicta have been eliminated from the tournament.

Here's how all the action in the BLAST SLAM IX Group Stage went down:

The BLAST SLAM IX Group Stage split the 16 participating teams into four GSL-style groups of four teams each, with all matches in this stage being a best-of-three. The first placed team in each group will secure a spot in the Playoffs and advance to the Seeding Matches, while the second placed teams advanced to the Last Chance stage. Meanwhile, the third and fourth placed teams were eliminated.

Group A featured BLAST SLAM VIII champions Yandex as the clear favourites to secure the top seed, with the rest of the group being a closely-matched field of OG, NAVI, and PlayTime. 

Group A's Opening Matches saw Yandex sweep PlayTime while NAVI took down OG in three games. In the Winner Match, NAVI put up a commendable effort in two games that went past over the hour-mark despite 7.42 being designed to make games faster, though Yandex still emerged victorious to secure the top seed of the group.

The Loser Match then saw OG defeat PlayTime in three games to set up a rematch with NAVI in the Decider Match. While their previous meeting was a relatively close affair, OG made it look easy this time as they swept NAVI to advance to the Last Chance stage.

Group B produced by far the most surprising result of the Group Stage. Reigning The International (TI( champions Spirit finished last in a group that also featured GamerLegion, XG, and Nemesis–three teams that were supposed to be well below the level of the current holders of the Aegis of Champions.

Group B's Opening Matches saw GamerLegion make quick work of XG in two games. Meanwhile, Nemesis pulled off a surprising 2-0 upset over a Spirit squad that seemed to have struggled to adapt to the new metagame brought about by the 7.42 update. With the path to the Playoffs left wide open by Spirit's slow start, GamerLegion swept Nemesis in the Winner Match to secure their spot in the Playoffs.

The Group B Loser Match saw Spirit fall victim to another upset, this time in a 2-1 reverse sweep at the hands of XG. The match notably ended with a 97-minute marathon–further extended by a long pause due to technical issues and a subsequent remake–that saw XG pull off a massive comeback in a very chaotic ultra late game.

XG then carried the momentum from their victory over Spirit to the Group B Decider Match, where they swept Nemesis to secure the group's second seed and a spot in the Last Chance stage.

Group C hosted a competitive field featuring TI runners-up and 1win Essence III winners PARIVISION as well as regional powerhouses Liquid and DO KONCA (former BetBoom Team roster). Rounding out the group were LEGION, a respectable squad that had the misfortune to be grouped alongside three heavyweight teams.

Group C's Opening Matches both ended in 2-0 sweeps, with PARIVISION cruising through LEGION and Liquid routing DO KONCA. The Winner Match then saw PARIVISION and Liquid go blow for blow in a three-game slugfest, with PARIVISION emerging victorious to secure the top seed of Group C.

LEGION gave a good account of themselves in the Loser Match, but DO KONCA still came away with the victory after three games to set up a showdown with Liquid in the Decider Match. Liquid then took down DO KONCA to secure Group C's second seed, notably outlasting their opponents in an 85-minute thriller.

Group D had arguably the closest field of the Group Stage, featuring two struggling but still dangerous powerhouses in 1w and Aurora, as well as two squads formed from the remnants of Nigma Galaxy in LGD and Astra Invicta. 

The action in Group D kicked off with 1w sweeping LGD and Aurora taking down Astra Invicta in three games. 1w then carried their momentum into the group's Winner Match, where they soundly swept Aurora to secure their spot in the Playoffs and join Yandex and GamerLegion in finishing the Group Stage with a perfect 4-0 record.

The Group D Loser Match then saw former Nigma Galaxy teammates clash as LGD and Astra Invicta fought for their tournament lives. LGD emerged victorious with a 2-1 reverse sweep to advance to the Decider Match against Aurora. LGD were in position to claim the first game of the Decider Match, only for Aurora to steal that game one win away before eventually sweeping the series.

With the conclusion of the Group Stage, the Dota 2 action in BLAST SLAM IX will continue in the Seeding Matches and Last Chance stage. 

The Seeding Matches will feature the top seeds of the Group Stage fight for better seeding in the Playoffs, with the winners securing a direct berth to the upper bracket semifinals while the losers will have to settle for a spot in the upper bracket quarterfinals. Meanwhile, the Last Chance stage will feature the second seeds from the Group Stage, with the winners securing a spot in the upper bracket quarterfinals while the losers will be eliminated.
