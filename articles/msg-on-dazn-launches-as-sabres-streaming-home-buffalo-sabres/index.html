MSG+ on DAZN launches as Sabres' streaming home | Buffalo Sabres

TheTools · 20.09.2026 18:09 · source · permalink

Fans can sign up for DAZN today.

Buffalo Sabres fans can now sign up for DAZN, the exclusive direct-to-consumer streaming home of MSG Networks for the 2026-27 NHL season.

Subscribers in the Sabres’ broadcast territory will receive access to stream live Sabres games as well as other live and on-demand Sabres content.

DAZN, the world’s leading sports entertainment platform, has replaced the Gotham Sports App. All existing Gotham subscribers have been migrated to DAZN or are in the process of being migrated.

Fans with eligible cable providers can continue to watch games on MSG through their cable providers as well as on the DAZN app.

**Sign up for DAZN here**.

Pricing options for MSG+ on DAZN’s special launch promotion include:

  • $19.99 for the first month, then $29.99 a month after that
  • $249.99 for the year
  • $9.99 per Sabres game
  • Options to upgrade to a bundle for Yankees, Knicks and Nets games for Buffalo users

Note – The Sabres’ broadcast territory includes Buffalo, Rochester and Syracuse among other markets. See which teams are available in your area by entering your zip code ***here***.

MSG+ on DAZN includes streaming access for up to three users and locations.

 Out-of-market fans can continue to stream games on ESPN+.
