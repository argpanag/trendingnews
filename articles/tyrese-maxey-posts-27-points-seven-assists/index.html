Tyrese Maxey posts 27 points, seven assists

TheTools · 08.11.2026 23:35 · source · permalink

    Tyrese Maxey posts 27 points, seven assists

    Tyrese Maxey

    PHI Guard #0
    Player Stats

    Tyrese Maxey amassed 27 points (9-of-16 FGs, 6-of-6 FTs), four rebounds, seven assists and three three-pointers in Sunday’s win over the Bulls.

    After scoring at least 26 points in his first eight games, Maxey hit a rough patch in the two prior to Sunday, totaling 36 points on 10-of-31 shooting. He got back on track in Chicago, shooting high percentages from the field and the foul line while also dishing out seven assists. Maxey didn’t record any defensive stats against the Bulls, but it mattered more that he got back into an offensive groove.
    - Raphielle Johnson
