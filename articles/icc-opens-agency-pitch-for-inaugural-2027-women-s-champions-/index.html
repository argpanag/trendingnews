ICC opens agency pitch for inaugural 2027 Women’s Champions Trophy

TheTools · 22.10.2026 05:34 · source · permalink

The International Cricket Council (ICC) issued a Request for Proposals (RFP) on October 21, 2026, inviting marketing, creative, digital, and public relations agencies to pitch for the inaugural ICC Women’s Champions Trophy 2027. Scheduled to take place in Mumbai and Vadodara from February 14 to 28, 2027, the six-team, 16-match T20 tournament will require the appointed agency—or agencies—to oversee end-to-end strategic positioning, hero creative assets, influencer collaborations, earned media, and experiential fan activations.

The RFP highlights strategic demographic data aimed at addressing the ICC's male-skewed digital audience base. While the 18–24 age group dominated digital consumption during recent tournaments (representing 52% of app users during the Women's World Cup 2025), female user participation remained low at 12% to 24% across web and app platforms. Leveraging audience insights that demonstrate player-led storytelling and multilingual content consistently outperforming generic tournament messaging, the ICC is tasking pitching agencies with executing a comprehensive strategy to drive female fan acquisition and broaden cricket's cultural footprint beyond traditional sports media.
