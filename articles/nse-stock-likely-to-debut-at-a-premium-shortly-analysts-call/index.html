NSE shares are likely to make a decent debut at a premium on the BSE at 10 am on Thursday, according to market analysts tracking the stock.

The Rs 22,569-crore initial public offering of the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE), the country's second-largest IPO, was subscribed 5.71 times on the final day of bidding on Monday, driven by strong demand from institutional investors.

Sarvam Goel, Founder, Pocketful, expects the stock to list with a flat to 10 percent gain, while describing NSE as a long-term story rather than a listing-day opportunity.

"Cautious investors can start with a small position and add gradually as the supply gets absorbed and earnings stabilise," Goel said.

However, he cautioned that the issue comes at a difficult time, with short-term earnings headwinds from regulatory changes in the options framework and a meaningful supply overhang from AIF holdings without a lock-in.

NSE is the crown jewel of India's capital markets and has a dominant presence across cash, derivatives and data services. Its registered investor base has grown at roughly 26 percent CAGR.

The NSE IPO was India's second-largest public issue after Hyundai Motor India's Rs 27,870-crore IPO in 2024. It surpassed LIC's Rs 21,000-crore offering in 2022 but remains below Hyundai Motor India's record public issue.

Shivani Nyati, Head of Wealth at Swastika Investmart, said NSE's earnings are sensitive to market volumes and regulatory changes.

"At Rs 1,700-1,785, the IPO is valued at around 40.9x-42.9x FY26 diluted EPS, at a discount to BSE's 54.28x, offering valuation support. However, around 79 percent of revenue is linked to trading activity, making earnings sensitive to market volumes and regulatory changes," she said.

"We expect minor listing gains, but the IPO remains suitable for a long-term investment play, supported by NSE's strong market position, scale and relative valuation advantage," Nyati added.

Mahesh M Ojha, Vice President, Research & Business Development at Kantilal Chhaganlal Securities, said NSE's integrated ecosystem spanning trading, clearing, listing, indices and data enables multiple revenue streams, while its proprietary technology and strong clearing infrastructure create significant entry barriers.

"At Rs 1,785, NSE is valued at around 42.9x FY26 earnings, below BSE's around 52.7x, while its strong financial profile, including Rs 23,836 crore FY26 operating cash flow, Rs 32,312 crore cash and negligible conventional debt, provides balance-sheet strength," he said.

Ojha said rising capital-market participation and financialisation of household savings offer a favourable long-term growth opportunity, although regulatory changes and lower index-options market share remain key risks.

"Allotted investors with a long-term view may continue to hold, with an expected listing gain of around 3-5 percent. Fresh investors may consider entering after the stock stabilises post listing, allowing the market to establish a more sustainable valuation," he said.

The NSE IPO received bids for 50.58 crore shares against 8.86 crore shares on offer, translating into a 5.71-times subscription, according to BSE data.

The mega IPO generated nearly Rs 90,300 crore of demand against the Rs 22,568-crore issue size. The IPO was fully subscribed on the second day of bidding in the primary market.
