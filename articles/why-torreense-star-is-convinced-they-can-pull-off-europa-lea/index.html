Why Torreense star is convinced they can pull off Europa League shock

TheTools · 23.11.2026 00:06 · source · permalink

Torreense's World Cup star Stopira reckons the Portuguese minnows can pull off a stunning double this week by beating Benfica and Celtic.

The Hoops travel to Leiria this week for their next game in the Europa League, after giving themselves renewed hope with a win against Besiktas in their last game.

They face the Portuguese Cup holders, who became the first-ever team from the second tier of the country to lift the trophy.

They start their defence today against Benfica in a match that has been switched to the scene of Celtic's greatest-ever triumph at Estadio Nacional do Jamor, where the Parkhead side won the European Cup in 1967.

They have also had to switch all of their home European games because they tiny ground doesn't meet requirements.

But Cape Verde international Stopira is convinced they can triumph in both of their games against huge rivals.

He said: "We are absolutely aiming to win both games this week against Benfica and Celtic.
