Roosters bound for NRL grand final after 36-20 win over Dolphins

TheTools · 25.09.2026 18:26 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Sport

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._g.pKhUMkRnoOZwX4iq2fhLAqba5Rz0jb3mINKRqASmxf0/sport/1576452%2Froosters-bound-for-nrl-grand-final-after-36-20-win-over-dolphins

The Sydney Roosters are the first team through to the NRL grand final courtesy of a 36-20 win over the Dolphins.

Sam Walker starred on return from a syndesmosis injury, scoring a try and leading the Roosters around the park with aplomb.

Dual international Mark Nawaqanitawase opened the scoring for the Roosters with a length of the field intercept and finished with a double and a try assist in a sensational display.

The Tricolours will play either Penrith or Newcastle in their first grand final since 2019. - ABC
