OCD sets nat’l earthquake, tsunami awareness drills Nov. 5 - balita.com

TheTools · 03.11.2026 09:52 · source · permalink

Posted by News Service | Nov 3, 2026 | News | |

By Priam F. Nepomuceno

MANILA – The Office of Civil Defense (OCD) on Monday said it will conduct the 4th Quarter National Simultaneous Earthquake Drill (NSED) in Sta. Ana, Cagayan on Nov. 5, with a magnitude 8.5 earthquake and tsunami as the disaster scenarios.

Coinciding with World Tsunami Awareness Day, the drill aims to boost public awareness of tsunamis alongside standard earthquake preparedness.

The Philippine Institute of Volcanology and Seismology (Phivolcs) defines a tsunami as a series of sea waves, often exceeding five meters, “generated by a shallow, strong undersea earthquake that displaces the seabed and the water mass above it.”

The OCD said the exercise will begin at 8 a.m. at the Country Inn in Sta. Ana, with the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council participating.

“The event will feature a ceremonial pressing of the button, followed by the ‘Over Coffee Dialogue: Earthquake and Tsunami Preparedness’ forum, where experts and government officials will discuss earthquake and tsunami risks and preparedness measures,” it said.

The full-scale simulation will simulate a major quake triggered by the movement of the East Luzon Trough.

The exercise will bring together government agencies, emergency responders, local government units, and communities to test and strengthen disaster preparedness and response capabilities.

“The public is encouraged to learn earthquake and tsunami hazards, identify evacuation routes and safe areas, and prepare family emergency plans to help protect lives during emergencies and disasters,” the OCD said. (PNA)
