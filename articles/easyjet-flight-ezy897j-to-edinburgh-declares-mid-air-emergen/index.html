EasyJet flight EZY897J to Edinburgh declares mid-air emergency over UK - AOL

TheTools · 18.11.2026 12:47 · source · permalink

An easyJet flight declared a mid-air emergency above the south-west of England.

Flight EZY897J took off from Bristol this morning and was scheduled to arrive at Edinburgh at 10.35am.

The plane made a U-turn above Brecon Beacons in South Wales before circling over the Bristol Channel and returning to Bristol airport, according to flight tracking data.

The Airbus A320 began squawking 7700 - the universal aviation code for a general emergency - shortly after it departed.

According to easyJet Flight Status, the emergency was due to a bird strike.

A message from the airline reads: "We're very sorry that your flight has now been diverted to Bristol. This is due to a bird strike on your aircraft.
