Besiktas take another step toward signing Nicolas Raskin as fans bombard Rangers star online

TheTools · 15.11.2026 15:56 · source · permalink

Dakir Thanveer

Besiktas have demonstrated they are entirely serious about making another attempt to sign Nicolas Raskin in the upcoming January transfer window, with fresh details emerging from Turkiye regarding their pursuit of the Rangers midfielder.

The Turkish giants came agonisingly close to securing the Belgian’s signature during the summer window. Rangers had successfully reached an agreement with Besiktas over a structured loan deal featuring an option-to-buy clause, but the transfer dramatically collapsed at the eleventh hour when Raskin himself opted against making the move to Istanbul.

Despite that summer setback, talk of an eventual move to the Super Lig has steadily lingered in the background, with the midfielder continually linked with a departure from Ibrox. Rumours intensified in recent weeks with claims that fellow countryman Leandro Trossard had been nudged to speak with Raskin about life in Turkey, while head coach Vincenzo Italiano has demanded the addition of a dynamic new central midfielder when the market reopens in January.

Besiktas officials watch Rangers midfielder Nicolas Raskin in Turkiye vs Belgium

Now, Besiktas have taken a significant concrete step toward turning those ambitions into reality. According to Turkish tabloid newspaper Posta, club board members and talent spotters watched the 25-year-old closely in action during Belgium’s tense 2-1 UEFA Nations League victory over Turkiye at the Gürsel Aksel Stadium in İzmir on Thursday, 12 November.

Raskin anchored the Belgian midfield from the start, putting in a gritty 90-minute shift before being replaced in stoppage time, leaving a powerful impression on the watching hierarchy as they accelerate plans for a winter swoop.

With only seven weeks to go until the winter transfer window, Besiktas are now prepared to re-open talks with Rangers and Raskin over a move.

Besiktas fans storm Raskin’s social media

That high-profile scouting mission on Turkish soil has instantly spilt over into a digital frenzy among the club’s fan base, who are fully convinced by the midfielder’s credentials.

Following the final whistle, passionate Besiktas supporters flooded the comment section of Raskin’s latest Instagram post with an overwhelming wave of “Come to Besiktas” messages, accompanied by black-and-white heart symbols and eagle emojis.

This remarkable online mobilisation highlights just how desperate the supporters are to see the former Standard Liege man spearhead their midfield in the second half of the season, adding massive pressure on the club’s board to test Rangers‘ resolve once the winter window officially opens.

Losing Raskin midway through the season will be a huge blow to Derek McInnes‘ hopes of winning the Scottish Premiership. The Belgian has made 15 appearances across all competitions for the Gers this season, starting in 14 of them.
