Basel tennis picks: Djokovic vs. Humbert, Hurkacz vs. Rune

TheTools · 28.10.2026 01:31 · source · permalink

October 27, 2026 Ricky Match previews

Novak Djokovic will kick off his Basel campaign against a tough indoor hard-court opponent in Ugo Humbert on Wednesday. Hubert Hurkacz tries to maintain his recent momentum at the expense of Holger Rune.

(4) Novak Djokovic vs. Ugo Humbert

Djokovic recently indicated that he plans on finishing 2026 in Turin and beginning 2027 in Australia. Beyond that, all bets are off. However, the 39-year-old Serb still has some work to do if he wants to clinch his spot in the Nitto ATP Finals. He is currently No. 12 in the world and No. 11 in the race (No. 10 if you eliminate Jannik Sinner, who is out for the remainder of the season). Djokovic looked like his own season might as well have been over during the summer hard-court swing, but he bounced back in style to capture yet another Beijing title earlier this month.

Up first at the Swiss Indoors Basel for the 24-time Grand Slam champion on Wednesday is Humbert, who lost their only previous meeting 6-3, 6-2, 6-3 seven years ago in the Wimbledon fourth round. Humbert obviously has a much better chance against the current version of Djokovic, but the 38th-ranked Frenchman probably isn’t playing well enough to capitalize. He is a horrendous 2-8 in his last 10 matches dating back to the grass-court swing in June. It would be quite alarming for Djokovic if he does not win this match with relative ease.

Pick: Djokovic in 2


Wednesday also marks the second encounter between Rune and Hurkacz, with the Dane getting the job done 7-5, 6-1 on his way to the 2022 Paris Masters title. The rematch should favor Hurkacz given the Pole’s current form. He compiled a 10-3 record on the Asian swing with a runner-up effort in Chengdu, a semifinal performance in Beijing, and a fourth-round result in Shanghai. Hurkacz added another win over Zizou Bergs on Monday, when he cracked 13 aces and won 85 percent of his first-serve points. On an indoor hard court, the No. 10 seed’s serve will likely be too much for an opponent who is not yet close to regaining his best level.

Pick: Hurkacz in 3
