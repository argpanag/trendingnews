RLCS Worlds Group Stage Recap - NA Topples France

TheTools · 18.09.2026 01:11 · source · permalink

Jack Marsh

Share:

Jack Marsh

Share:

For the last few years, including the two Majors this season, France has proven to be the superior breeding ground of Rocket League talent. But as the RLCS Worlds Group Stage heats up, it’s clear that North America is finally stacking the hierarchy in its favour.

Karmine Corp, Gentle Mates, Team Vitality, and Evan “M0nkey M00n” Rogez (the house Frenchman playing in the Middle East) have monopolised the RLCS 2026 season so far. Locking out the rest of the world from Major Grand Finals. Only the Esports World Cup proved different, as Team Falcons upped their efforts in the third-party tournament.

But with RLCS returning to Texas and the Dickies Arena, the NA hosts have locked in like never before. For the first time in almost a decade of Rocket League esports, the NA sides have taken the lion’s share of the Playoffs stage, donning a stunning record over their previously superior counterparts and laying the law in the RLCS Worlds Group Stage.

In fact, all five seeds of the North American representatives have ridden the wave of home comforts to fill out nearly half of the 12 playoff spots, as Virtus Pro and FUT Esports especially have stunned their opponents with longshot wins.

In a two-day stint of dominance, here’s how the RLCS Worlds Group Stage unfolded.

Quickly dubbed the group of death after EWC winners Falcons, whose stunted start to the season saw them have to qualify through the Play-In stage, were seeded alongside Karmine Corp. Both teams are joint favourites to lift the trophy after winning the last two tournaments respectively, yet Spacestation Gaming is also a top contender, and they didn’t lie down without a fight.

There were very few shocks in Group A, as both Karmine Corp, Falcons, and SSG made light work of MIBR. While the food chain also swallowed the American hosts, who lost both matches against the favourites by a game-five decider.

The top spot in Group A was decided in what could easily be the warm-up scrim for the grand final, as Karmine Corp made a huge statement with defensive brilliance to nullify Falcons and clinch a spot in the upper bracket. Spacestation Gaming also cemented a Playoff spot in a shoot out against MIBR.

Arguably the hardest group to tear apart, all boasting similarly high peaks but varied consistency, Group B proved to be as confusing as it promised.

The easiest place to start here is Ninjas in Pyjamas, who stayed in bed all tournament, snoring their way to three losses and an early exit from Worlds.

FUT Esports stole the show, though, having stepped down from the Play-Ins and used that momentum to shock FURIA and soar through to win the group in a ferocious manner. From nowhere, FUT Esports proved that there is life in the experienced dog yet as Justin “Jstn” Morales booked himself a top six finish,

The only cracks in the armour of the NA teams were in Group C. Yet, that’s only because they played each other.

Most eyes were on NRG in this lineup, as the reigning world champions claimed to be in better form than in their previous tournaments, but an embarrassing exit was still possible. Thankfully for the home crowd attending the Playoffs, NRG has shown up.

Wins against Manchester City and Bigodes were good enough to set them up with a top-six finish by topping Group C, despite suffering defeat to a game-five dagger against Shopify Rebellion in the series of the day.

Play-In alumni Bigodes were on the back end of some heavy losses as Manchester City and Shopify slipped into second and third place, respectively.

And here we have it: the winners of the RLCS Worlds Group Stage. Virtus Pro keep defying the odds at the World Championships, finally living up to the hype that we were sold on in the opening rounds of the season.

Stunning both EU second and third seeds in Vitality and Gentle Mates with brutal wins, the USA’s bottom-ranked team proved that they are to be taken seriously as championship contenders, no matter where their deep run started.

Vitality and Gentle Mates both showed rocky form throughout the RLCS Worlds Group Stage, even struggling against each other, but wins over Wildcard were enough to steady the ship and regain the confidence that was drilled out of them by Virtus Pro. Unfortunately for Wildcard, there was no fairytale story this year and they faced elimination without a round win.
