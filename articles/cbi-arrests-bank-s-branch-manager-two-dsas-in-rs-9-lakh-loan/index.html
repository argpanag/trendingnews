CBI arrests bank’s branch manager, two DSAs in Rs 9 lakh loan bribe case in Dehradun - Pioneer Edge | Uttarakhand News in English | Dehradun News Today| News Uttarakhand | Uttarakhand latest news

TheTools · 19.11.2026 14:38 · source · permalink

PIONEER EDGE NEWS SERVICE /Dehradun 

The Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) has arrested a Bank of Baroda branch manager and two Direct Selling Agents (DSAs) for allegedly demanding a bribe of Rs nine lakh to facilitate the sanction and disbursement of a Rs 1.8 crore loan in Dehradun. One of the agents was caught red-handed while accepting Rs 50,000, following which the branch manager and another agent were also arrested.

The CBI’s Anti-Corruption Branch (ACB), Dehradun, registered a case on November 18 against Bank of Baroda Indiranagar branch manager Prem Ranjan and three private persons identified as Narendra, Neeraj and Ashraf, who worked as DSAs.

The case was registered following a complaint by a Clement Town resident, who had applied for a loan against property worth Rs 1.8 crore at the bank. The accused allegedly demanded five per cent of the loan amount, amounting to Rs nine lakh, as a bribe for approving and disbursing the loan. According to the CBI, the accused had already received Rs 2.5 lakh in cash from the complainant. They later demanded the remaining Rs 6.5 lakh. The complainant handed over two undated cheques worth Rs 3.8 lakh and Rs 2.2 lakh and agreed to pay another Rs 50,000 in cash.

Officials informed that on the evening of November 18, DSA Narendra Arya was caught accepting the Rs 50,000 bribe. The branch manager and another DSA, Neeraj Rai, were subsequently arrested for their alleged involvement.

The CBI also searched the residential premises of the three arrested accused and seized cash and documents. All three were produced before the court on November 19.
