Missing Springfield teen found alive 263 days later in former teacher's home

SPRINGFIELD, Ohio (WSYX) — An Ohio teenager who was reported missing after she was last seen in Columbus was found 263 days later inside the Springfield home of her former high school science teacher, according to police and court records.

The 16-year-old was reported missing after she ran away Dec. 2, 2025, from Huckleberry House in Columbus. She was last seen wearing a gray hoodie and black pants and was headed south, according to a missing person's report from Columbus Police.

Her mother told CPD the teenager was from Springfield and was unfamiliar with the Columbus area.

ABC 6 is choosing not to name the teenager to protect her privacy.

For months, the teenager's family and police searched for answers, as did neighbors in Springfield. Danielle Flint, who lives two doors down from the home where the teenager was eventually found, said she had seen Facebook posts suggesting the girl could be staying there.

Flint said she tried to catch a glimpse of the teenager while she was outside or in her backyard, but never saw her.

"I've tried to peek around here and see if I can catch her, you know, in the back or maybe out of the house at night but I had never seen her," Flint said.

On August 21— 263 days after the teenager was last seen — Springfield police and an officer with the US Marshals' special taskforce went to the home of Jobe Binkley on West North Street and found the teenager alive inside. Court records obtained by ABC 6 said officers had previously gone to Binkley’s home twice and asked to search for the girl.

At those times, Binkley refused officers to enter his home and stated that the missing person was not inside. The records also say Binkley knew the teenager had been reported missing.

“For nine months not being able to find your baby, is she dead, is she alive, is she okay. I, I cannot imagine,” Flint said. "I couldn't imagine how her mother felt."

Binkley is a former teacher with the Springfield City School District.

According to Binkley’s personnel file obtained by ABC 6 through a public records request, he was placed on administrative leave in November 2024 after the district became aware of concerns regarding his professional interactions with students. The district said those allegations were unsubstantiated.

Binkley was placed on administrative leave again in January 2025 and was ultimately fired the following month for violating district policy regarding the use of technology, according to the district.

Springfield City Schools said it was never made aware of allegations involving this specific teenager until after Binkley was no longer employed by the district.

“If he was her teacher, that's absolutely beyond disturbing,” Flint said. "Are our kids safe in our schools now?"

Binkley has been charged with interference with custody and is out on bond.

Former Franklin County Prosecutor Ron O’Brien said additional charges could follow as the case moves to a grand jury, depending on what investigators find. He said investigators could examine evidence from Binkley’s phone and the teenager’s phone, as well as what the teenager tells investigators about what happened inside Binkley’s home and during the time she was missing.

“If you have an adult trying to take advantage of a minor, oftentimes the only charge you might be able to lodge at the outset is an interference with custody,” O’Brien said. “Oftentimes, you really need the cooperation of the minor to do that. Sometimes they're willing to provide that information and other times not.”

For Flint, the fact that the teenager was so close to home after being missing for 263 days remains difficult to comprehend.

“I would literally lose every piece of me if that was one of my girls,” Flint said.

Binkley's next date in court is September 28.
