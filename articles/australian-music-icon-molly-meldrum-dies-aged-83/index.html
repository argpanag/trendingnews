Australian music icon Molly Meldrum dies aged 83

TheTools · 23.11.2026 00:09 · source · permalink

Legendary journalist Ian "Molly" Meldrum has died at the age of 83 after making and breaking music careers for decades.

He died early on Monday morning at Epworth Hospital in Richmond, Melbourne, after a long illness, his family said.

"He was deeply loved by his long-term partner, Yan Wongngam, his family and all who knew him," the statement said.

His longtime friend and manager, Mark Klemens of Profile Talent Management, said Australia had lost a "true icon".

"A man who opened doors for so many and entertained millions of people here and around the world," Klemens said on Monday.

"He was unconventional and wonderfully unpredictable, but always caring, always funny and always down to earth, with a passion the rest of us could only envy.

"He made the most of his life, and boy, was it a full one. Rest in peace, Molly."

Meldrum's family have requested privacy as they grieve.

Born in regional Victoria in 1943, the eldest of three boys, Meldrum forged a career in the music industry, becoming the face of ABC's wildly popular Countdown program in the 1970s and 80s.

His influence was so great he could either make careers or break them.

Meldrum's catchcry of "do yourself a favour" for bands or artists he thought made the cut sparked record sales and ticket sales.

It wasn't just famous musicians but also the now King Charles who provided fodder for Countdown.

In a 1977 interview with the then prince, he found himself in trouble for referring to the Queen as "your mum".

An interview with Dolly Parton replayed on her recent death revealed he was starstruck in her presence.

"I've never been more nervous doing a show in my life," Meldrum told her.

Meldrum was also a music producer and an avid St Kilda Football Club fan.

He was inducted into the ARIA Hall of Fame in 2014.
