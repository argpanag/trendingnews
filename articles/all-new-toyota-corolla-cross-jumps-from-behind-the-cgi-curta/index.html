All-New Toyota Corolla Cross Jumps From Behind the CGI Curtain, Also Gets Compared to Current One

TheTools · 22.11.2026 11:58 · source · permalink

While average new prices reach new heights – they crossed the $51k threshold back in October in the United States, Toyota is fighting against the trend with a revival of the Scion moniker for a four-wheel drive kei truck built in the US. The catch is that it’s limited to 25 mph, so it won’t bring you too many bragging rights, even though it might be cheaper than a Ford Maverick.

The company is also playing with the hearts of sports car enthusiasts because it’s unwilling to say whether or not a GR Celica revival is also coming to the streets after it has only confirmed the nameplate’s return for the 2027 WRC season. In other news, Toyota recently launched the new Corolla Deck model for the Latin American and Caribbean regions.

It’s a very interesting model – the often-reported unibody compact pickup truck is now live, complete with hybrid power, though it’s not coming to North America to fight the aforementioned Ford Maverick. But while we’re on the subject of the Toyota Corolla Cross, let’s just say that it’s a very dear model to many folks – including the members of the imaginative guild of digital car content creators dwelling across the parallel universes of vehicular CGI.

For example, Nikita Chuicko, the virtual artist better known as "kelsonik" on social media, thinks that now is the right CGI time to work on the compact crossover SUV that launched based on the same TNGA-C platform as the E210 series Corolla during the summer of 2020. Now, following a recent mid-cycle refresh procedure also available in North America and the recent Corolla Deck launch for other regions, the pixel master thinks it’s time to start work on the second generation.

His unofficial, hypothetical design project is further speculation upon the Toyota Corolla Concept presented at the Japan Mobility Show in 2025. Now, everyone and their dog expects the Japanese automaker to reveal the 13th generation of the Corolla series sometime next year for the 2028MY, with influences taken directly from the multi-energy prototype.

Further extrapolating, the CGI expert now unifies the design of the regular, upcoming Corolla passenger car with the Corolla Cross, deploying the new front style combined with a dual-tone paintjob and the high-riding compact crossover SUV profile. This model is also all-electric or a plug-in hybrid, as it has a front charging port located next to the driver’s door.

Last but not least, we also have a quick comparison between the current SUV and the new CGI one. So, what do you think – yay or nay?
