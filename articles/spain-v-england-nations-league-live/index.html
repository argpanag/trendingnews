    Preamble

86 min This, my mates, has been a proper game of association football. Lamine picks a dink into the box, chested off by Fermin – who, like Pedri, has made a difference – but Porro can only drill wide. If De la Fuente had gone with those three subs from the start, we might’ve seen a different contest.

85 min England are deeper than they’d want to be but Konsa brings them up to the edge then, when Williams skates around the outside, beating Rogers, his cross is nasty … but Porro dives to head wide.

84 min A brilliant pass from Pedri sets Lamine free art inside-left! Is this his moment?! It is not because, out of nowhere, Benjamin White launches into a precision challenge, taking all of the ball and none of the man. That could be crucial.

82 min Spain pass and England drop, but the supine resignation we saw against Argentina isn’t quite there. Changes for England: Spence and Calvert-Lewin for Bellingham and Gordon, which the Madrid man loves just as much as you’d expect.

80 min England are pinned back, too deep – we may have seen this one before. This time, instead of looking to commit a defeder, Lamine waves a low curler from edge, which Trafford dives to fist away – back into play, not around the post. But when the ball comes back, he gets it behind, and the corner comes to nowt.

79 min Spain are getting Lamine on the ball now and he teases Hall, who does really well not to buy what’s being sold, eventually blocking his man off with help from Trafford, and England clear.

78 min There’s such tempo and intensity to this game, and Tuchel acts, taking off Kane and introducing Lewis-Skelly. I can see why he’s done that but, on this occasion, I might’ve kept Kane on as a target man to hold the ball; who is the striker now?

77 min A bit of England possession, Gordon and Bellingham doing well in the inside-left channel, but this time when Bellingham crosses the ball soars behind for a goalkick.

GOAL! Spain 2-3 England (Williams 75)

How will England eff it up? Like this. Gordon tarries in possession, Pedri robbing him, then a fantastic first-time pass from Fermin sends Lamine away down the left. His low cross is a delight, Williams arriving on to it without breaking stride, and he swishes a finish in off the near post. It’s a different game now!

73 min Spain win a corner down the right which yields another from the left. This time it goes short and, when the ball is clipped to the far post, Belling ham opts to let it go but Oyarzabal is behind him, and when he forces the cut-back, Fermin shoots … and Trafford parries. Pressure is building…

72 min A further change for Spain, Williams replacing Zubimendi as De la Fuente goes for it.

70 min Spain find Lamine on the right side of the box, but his attempted curler is blocked at source. England have defended superbly so far, the penalty Spain’s first shot on target.

69 min This is a proper test for England; not one Tuchel will have wanted but perhaps one the team needs. If they can make it through this, so wind up dominating Spain with 11, then seeing them away with 10, they’ll end the evening a different side to the one which started it. Lose and – though it’ll hurt – they can blame individual error.

67 min Someone on the England bench is booked, and I wonder if Tuchel contemplates a change. He needs keep an out-ball, but perhaps midfield control means he remove Rogers for, perhaps, Lewis-Skelly.

GOAL! Spain 1-3 England (Oyarzabal pen 65)

Oyarzabal ambles up, skips, and when Trafford dives left, he sweeps right. Hold on to your toupees, this is going to be a serious 25 minutes.

PENALTY TO SPAIN AND A RED CARD FOR ELLIOT ANDERSON!

64 min That question, eh? How will England eff it up? Like thus: Quansah is mugged by Lamine, the first time England have been so caught, and when Baena sweeps him in, Anderson hauls him back, a rash call, and Spain have a route back into the game!

62 min “Are you Scotland in disguise?” sing the away end, a disbelieving Philip Cornwall back in touch BUT EXCUSE ME WHILE I INTERRUPT MYSELF ONCE MORE!

61 min Gordon, who’s having a fine night, win another corner, and England will fancy another here, but this time Rice’s floaty delivery is easily fielded by Simon.

60 min And what a moment of redemption that is for White. Jeered by his own fans after leaving the World Cp squad in 2022, he’s nicely reintegrated now, and that was the kind of finish you’d expect to see from Pedro Porro. That is some serious praise.

GOAL! Spain 0-3 England (White 58)

Kane across the face of the box to Bellingham, who caresses in behind for White! He takes a touch, narrowing the angle but forcing the keeper to move then, when Simon spreads himself, he picks a very deliberate finish through the keeper’s legs! This is a hiding!

57 min Spain haven’t lost for 43 games …and BUT EXCUSE ME WHILE I INTERRUPT MYSELF … THEY’RE GOING TO LOSE TONIGHT!

56 min Bellingham, whose good work won the corner from which England scored, wins another ion similar style, this time down the left, as we see footgge of the England goal, Tuchel on the touchline also applying brow to imaginary ball – that’s how much time and space Konsa had. This time, the corner comes to nowt.

54 min Minor Spain protests for a free-kick of some sort, but VAR isn’t interested. Konsa couldn’t have been freer, the question on everybody’s lips a familiar one: how will they eff it up from here?

GOAL! Spain 0-2 England (Konsa 53)

A Rice outswinger and, in the middle of the box, Konsa has maybe five yards of space all around him to run in and guide home from seven yards. England are all over this!

52 min Zubimendi picks up a poor pass and punches between Anderosn and Rice, finding Lamine … whose trivela cross is straight at Trafford. Then England find Bellingham down the right of the box and he delays well before winning a corner off Pedri, gesticulating to where he thinks the away end is, but isn’t.

51 min Down the right, 30 yards from goal, Rice will swing the free-kick out … and Huijsen heads away well, facing his own goal.

49 min Lamine spreads wide to Cucurella, then Spain work it back to him, Hall delaying before Gordon confiscates possession. Those two have been brilliant so far and, when England counter, Rogers cuts inside Cucurella, who trips him and is booked.

48 min England win it high and win a throw, Tuchel’s alacrity in returning the ball evidencing their intent to speed up the game and impose their will. Spain soon have possession back, but it’s a message not to retreat.

48 min Already, Spain look calmer, Pedri linking play and finding pockets. England will, as Tuchel warned, have to be patient.

47 min “As I’m sure 347 other people are also pointing out,” chortles Bob O’Hara, “Pochettino made a pretty big managerial eff-up this week.”

I fear that lush mane is on borrowed time – on which point is it just me who sees Stan Rizzo from Mad Men?

46 min We go again, those Spain changes reflecting England’s superiority.

A triple Spain change: Huijsen, Pedri and Fermin on; Ruiz, Cubarsi and Merino off.

Back come our teams…

Elsewhere, France are level at 0-0 playing Turkey in Bordeaux; Belgium have come from 1-0 down to lead Italy 3-1. As it stands, it’ll be France and Belgium moving on, Italy’s renaissance curtailed.

“Benitez, Robbie Fowler’s last game for Liverpool,” writes Paul Oakes. “Takes him off with five minutes to go. Liverpool get a penalty one minute later.”

I guess the idea was right, though – a proper reception.

“What did Ferguson do against Chelsea in 2010?” asks Joshua Keeling. In a game United needed to not lose, he picked Nevlle, Giggs and Scholes, though younger, harder-running players had excelled against Bayern the previous midweek, United lost the game, lost the title, and lost the chance of four in a row.

I wonder if De la Fuente will move to Pedri; the control we expect from Spain isn’t there tonight, and his ability to tempo-set might change this game. So far, Merino has been a cone, and it’s no great surprise; he’s a late-doors player, one who finds a goal when you’re desperate for one, rather than someone who gets involved in general play.
