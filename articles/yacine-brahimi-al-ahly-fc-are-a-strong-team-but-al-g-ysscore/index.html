Yacine Brahimi: Al Ahly FC are a strong team but Al-G… | YSscores

TheTools · 25.10.2026 17:07 · source · permalink

Al-Gharafa visit Al Ahly FC on Monday at the Al-Inma Stadium in Jeddah for the third round of the league stage in the AFC Champions League Elite, in a match where the Qatari team aims to secure a positive result.

Ibrahimovic stated during the press conference that Al Ahly FC's strength will not prevent Al-Gharafa from pursuing victory, explaining that his team includes outstanding players and aims to please its fans with a win.

Al-Gharafa player added that he constantly strives to assist his teammates within the team, considering that teamwork is an important element in achieving goals during various competitions.

Ibrahimi has confirmed that he has always maintained a positive attitude throughout his football career, pointing out his interest in supporting young players and helping them in every experience they go through, which aids their development within the team.

Yacine Brahimi, the Al-Gharafa player, has emphasized the difficulty of facing Al Ahly FC, stating that their opponent has a strong team with outstanding players, which is reflected in their dominance in the last two editions of the AFC Champions League Elite.
