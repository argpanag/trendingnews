AIIMS NORCET 11 Prelims Result 2026 OUT today at aiimsexams.ac.in, check direct link here

The All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi, is scheduled to announce the AIIMS NORCET 11 Prelims Result 2026 today, September 18, for candidates who appeared in the Stage 1 examination. The Nursing Officer Recruitment Common Eligibility Test (NORCET) 11 Stage 1 exam was conducted on September 12, and candidates who qualify will be eligible to appear for the next stage of the selection process.

The NORCET 11 prelims result will be made available through the official AIIMS examination website, aiimsexams.ac.in. The result is expected to be issued in PDF format, allowing candidates to search for their roll number and check their qualification status. The exact release time has not been announced.

Stage 1 is a qualifying examination and is used to shortlist candidates for Stage 2. The performance in the prelims does not form part of the final result. Candidates whose roll numbers appear in the qualifying result will be able to participate in the Stage 2 examination scheduled for September 30, 2026. The Stage 2 result is scheduled to be announced on October 10.

Candidates whose roll numbers are included in the qualifying list will become eligible for Stage 2, subject to the prescribed conditions. Since Stage 1 is only a qualifying examination, candidates should not treat the prelims percentile as their final selection score.

The official AIIMS examination portal is the designated source for results and recruitment updates. Candidates should rely on the official website for the result PDF and subsequent instructions rather than third-party links. The AIIMS examination website currently provides dedicated sections for recruitments and results.
