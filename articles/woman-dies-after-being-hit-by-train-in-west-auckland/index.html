Woman dies after being hit by train in West Auckland

TheTools · 23.10.2026 04:02 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Emergencies / Local

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._f0.YIZs2-J2FpwgcBcJJb29s1vXmFAWHLh_p1oUyv-l-RA/emergencies_local/2103241%2Fwoman-dies-after-being-hit-by-train-in-west-auckland

A person is dead after being hit by a train in Henderson.

A police spokesperson said the death happened at the rail crossing near Sturges Road shortly before 3pm.

A Hato Hone Saint John ambulance attended but a spokesperson referred RNZ to police.

"Sadly, I can confirm a woman was pronounced deceased at the scene," acting inspector Warwick Stainton, Auckland shift commander, said.

"This is understandably a terrible incident for everyone involved and we are working to notify the next of kin."

Police are asking for anyone who witnessed the death to come forward.
