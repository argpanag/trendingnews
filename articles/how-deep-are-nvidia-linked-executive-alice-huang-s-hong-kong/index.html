How deep are Nvidia-linked executive Alice Huang’s Hong Kong business ties?

TheTools · 11.11.2026 23:30 · source · permalink

Described as a “kittenish” and “pure” student, businesswoman Alice Huang built a quiet corporate web before drawing global scrutiny

Huang, also known as Huang Le, first set foot in the city 20 years ago to study. Two decades on, company records show she continues to have links to the city, including as a director of a firm that shares an address with a Russian-linked robotics company sanctioned by the United States.

Described by a former classmate as “pure” and “kittenish” during her time as a student, Huang studied in Hong Kong for a master’s degree before rising to become an executive at Singapore-based cloud computing firm Megaspeed International in 2023 – a company that has since faced US scrutiny over suspected attempts to circumvent export controls on advanced semiconductors.

According to sources, Huang was among the first batch of graduates from Baptist University’s master of social sciences in media management programme in 2007.
