How Karmine Corp and T1 got here

Worlds 2026 is being played in the United States from 15 October to 14 November. Every quarterfinal is a best-of-five with Fearless Draft, which means a champion played in one game is off limits for the rest of the series. The winner of this match meets either Dplus KIA or Bilibili Gaming in the semifinals.

T1 arrive as the defending world champions after a 3-1 Swiss stage and a 2-0 win over Movistar KOI in their last Swiss match. Karmine Corp are in their first ever Worlds main event and are the only European team left in the bracket.

Karmine Corp vs T1 head to head in 2026

The two met twice this year, and the results point in opposite directions.

At MSI, T1 swept the French side. At the Esports World Cup, Karmine Corp lost game one, then won games two and three to reach the grand final. That made them the second Western team to win a series against T1, according to Win.gg.

The catch is format. The Esports World Cup match was a best-of-three. Tonight is a best-of-five, which gives T1's depth and experience more games to show up.

Karmine Corp vs T1 player tier list

We ranked all ten starters from S to D on career resume and what each brings to this series. The tiers are relative to this match: D means least proven at this level, not weak.

Red is T1, blue is Karmine Corp.

T1 hold every spot in S and A, and Karmine Corp's best are kyeahoo and Caliste in B. That gap is a big part of why we pick T1 to win 3-1.

Karmine Corp vs T1 lane by lane

Both teams are playing the same five they used at the Esports World Cup. Karmine Corp line up Canna in the top lane, Yike in the jungle, kyeahoo in the middle, Caliste at bot and Busio on support. T1 answer with Doran, Oner, Faker, Peyz and Keria.

The mid lane is the matchup most viewers will track, with Faker against kyeahoo. Karmine Corp will want an early lead in the bot lane, where Caliste and Busio face Peyz and Keria.

Karmine Corp vs T1 odds

The markets agree on the favourite and disagree on the price. Polymarket has T1 at 82% and Karmine Corp at 19%. Sportslyx lists T1 at -650 and Karmine Corp at +400, and EGamersWorld shows 1.15 and 4.77 in decimal odds. All three were read on 6 November and will move before the match.

Our prediction on Karmine Corp vs T1: T1 win 3-1

We expect T1 to win 3-1. Three things point that way:

  • Format. Karmine Corp's win came in a best-of-three. Over five games with Fearless Draft, T1 have more room to adapt.
  • Form. T1 went 3-1 in the Swiss stage and swept Karmine Corp 3-0 at MSI.
  • The markets. All three sources price T1 as a heavy favourite.

We still give Karmine Corp a game. They have beaten T1 once this year, and T1 won only one game of that series against them at the Esports World Cup. A 3-1 leaves room for one more good night from the French side without handing them the series.

When and where to watch Karmine Corp vs T1

The match starts at 22:00 UTC on Friday 6 November. That is 5:00 pm US East, 2:00 pm US West, 10:00 pm in the UK and 11:00 pm in Central Europe. The series streams live on the official LoL Esports channels.
