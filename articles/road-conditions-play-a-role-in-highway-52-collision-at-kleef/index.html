Road conditions play a role in Highway 52 collision at Kleefeld corner

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:02 · source · permalink

The Hanover Fire Department was called to a two vehicle collision at the intersection of Highway 52 and PR 216 South Sunday morning. 

Fire Chief Paul Wiebe says the collision occurred around 10:50 a.m. 

He says a vehicle merging onto Highway 52 from PR 216, collided with a westbound vehicle wanting to turn south onto the 216. He notes it was a low-speed collision and everyone involved was looked over by EMS and released at the scene. 

Wiebe says snowy road conditions played a role in the crash. 

Emergency crews were on scene, allowing traffic through slowly while the collision was cleaned up. The highway has been fully reopened.
