‘Wants to be stuck with me’: Ellidy Pullin announces engagement

Ellidy Pullin has shared the news of her engagement to Brock Wadsworth.

Pullin, 38, confirmed the development via an Instagram post captioned, “HE WANTS TO BE STUCK WITH ME FOREVER” while holidaying in Europe with Wadsworth.

The internet personality and podcast co-host of Darling, Shine! went public with the relationship in late 2025.

Just hours after announcing the news, the couple’s post was flooded with well-wishes for the happy couple.

The engagement comes six years after the sudden death of Pullin’s partner, Olympic snowboarder Alex “Chumpy” Pullin.

Pullin met Wadsworth through mutual friends and knew each other for years before it is believed they began dating, about February last year.

In November, they made the relationship Instagram-official while on holiday in Bali.

Pullin later confirmed the details of their courtship in a December podcast episode, where she also spoke about navigating feelings of grief while falling in love.

“Of course, I love Chumpy, I still love him so much. I’ll never not love him,” she confessed. “Grief is absolutely f---ed.”

Pullin admitted that she felt guilty about how happy she felt: “I was making things up in my head, now I’m just like all of those things were in my head ... I was just scared of my feelings.”

In 2021, 15-months after Alex’s death, Pullin gave birth to their daughter, Minnie, who was conceived via IVF after she chose to undergo a post-mortem sperm retrieval.

“The idea of having a baby on my own, a constant reminder of what I’d lost, was hard enough without having to deal with other people’s judgment,” Pullin wrote in her 2022 memoir, Heartstrong.

“Until someone has been in my shoes, I don’t think they’d ever be able to understand what I went through.”
