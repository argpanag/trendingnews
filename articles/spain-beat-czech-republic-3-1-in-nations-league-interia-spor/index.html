Spain beat Czech Republic 3-1 in Nations League — Interia Sport

TheTools · 03.10.2026 23:50 · source · permalink

Spain’s national team defeated the Czech Republic 3-1 in Oviedo in a Nations League Division A match. The hosts scored twice before halftime, but after the Czechs scored in the second half, Spain’s lead became minimal. Mikel Merino set the final score late in the match.

As Interia Sport reports, Lamine Yamal opened the scoring in the sixth minute. He received a pass from Dani Olmo, beat the defenders and struck into the top corner. In the 35th minute, Rodri took advantage of confusion in the penalty area and scored from close range.

The Czech Republic reduced the deficit

After the break, the Spaniards initially controlled the game, but in the 69th minute the Czech Republic reduced the deficit. Adam Hložek passed to the left to Jiří Sláma, who scored with a first-time shot — 1-2.

More current news is available on the UA.News Telegram channel Telegram.

In the 79th minute, the ball ended up in Spain’s net after an error by David Raya, but the referee disallowed Ryan Naderi’s goal due to an attacking foul. Soon afterward, the Czechs were reduced to ten men: Ladislav Krejčí received his second yellow card.

Third consecutive victory

In the 86th minute, Víctor Muñoz failed to convert a scoring chance, mishitting the ball. Later, Mikel Merino followed up after Muñoz’s shot and set the final score at 3-1.

According to the outlet, the Spanish team had 69% possession, took 18 shots compared with five by their opponents, and hit the target ten times. After three rounds of the Nations League, Spain have the maximum number of points.
