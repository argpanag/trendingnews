Here’s who Georgia could hire to replace Glenn Schumann as defensive coordinator

TheTools · 28.11.2026 01:16 · source · permalink

ATHENS — Georgia is in the market for a new defensive coordinator after Glenn Schumann took the Florida State head coaching job.

Schumann has served as the defensive coordinator and primary defensive play caller since the 2022 season. He’s been Georgia’s inside linebackers coach since 2016, as Schumann was the last hire from Smart’s original staff.

Schumann is expected to remain on staff through the end of Georgia’s postseason run, as has been the case throughout Smart’s tenure when an assistant takes a head coaching job. Smart did the same when he was an assistant with Alabama in 2015, and Dan Lanning did the same in 2021. Both those seasons ended in national championship wins for the departing coach.
