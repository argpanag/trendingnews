The Grateful Dead reveals 'On A Back Porch Vol 4' with Dogfish Head

TheTools · 05.11.2026 14:00 · source · permalink
  • Buddy Iahn
  • November 5, 2026
  • Notable Releases, Rock

The vinyl will be available on Record Store Day’s Black Friday

Continuing their partnership of more than a decade, Dogfish Head and the Grateful Dead will release a new collaborative vinyl album, On A Back Porch, Vol. 4, on Record Store Day’s RSD Black Friday on November 27th. Curated by Grateful Dead Archivist & Legacy Manager, David Lemieux, and Dogfish Head Brewer & Founder, Sam Calagione, it features six live recordings from the Grateful Dead archive and will be sold in limited quantities at participating independently owned record stores while supplies last.

Like its predecessors, On A Back Porch, Vol. 4 brings together songs selected to enjoy alongside a beer on the back porch.

Sam Calagione recommends Grateful Dead Juicy Pale Ale with “Blow Away.” Dogfish Head’s Grateful Dead Juicy Pale Ale (5.3% AV) is a refreshing pale ale brewed with Kernza grains, granola, and heaps of good karma. Calagione says, “It’s a song all about life’s peaks and valleys, and learning to go with the flow, which is nicely complemented by the easy-drinking nature of Juicy Pale Ale.”

David Lemieux recommends Grateful Dead Citrus Daydream Lager with “Doin’ That Rag.” Dogfish Head’s Grateful Dead Citrus Daydream Lager (5.3% ABV) is a crisp and clean lager brewed with rice, Fonio grain, lemongrass, lime and lemon peels, and a mix of Lemondrop and Contessa hops. Lemieux says, “Like Citrus Daydream’s interesting mix of ingredients, the song’s lively, playful, and sometimes wandering lyrics may seem a bit nonsensical when examined in isolation, but when they come together, it’s a rather magical experience.”

Dogfish Head has served as The Official Beer of Record Store Day for more than a decade and proudly supports the organization’s mission to celebrate the culture of independently owned record stores. As an official RSD Black Friday release, On A Back Porch, Vol. 4 will be available at participating independently owned record stores nationwide, in limited quantities, while supplies last.

Since opening as the first brewpub in the First State more than 31 years ago, Dogfish Head has focused on original craft beverages, original food and original music. Throughout its history, Dogfish Head’s live music stage has hosted acts including The Strokes, Black Pumas, The Mountain Goats and Guided by Voices. Alongside its weekly calendar of live music shows, Dogfish Head manifests its music heritage through collaborations with artists and estates. The brewery has created products and compiled vinyl records with The Flaming Lips, the Miles Davis estate, Deltron 3030, the Grateful Dead and many more.

A1. Blow Away – July 7, 1989, at John F. Kennedy Stadium in Philadelphia, PA
A2. Mr. Charlie – May 7, 1972, at Bickershaw Festival in London, England
A3. The Last Time – March 22, 1990, at Copps Coliseum in Hamilton, Ontario, Canada

B1. Loser – March 24, 1990, at Knickerbocker Arena in Albany, NY
B2. Doin’ That Rag – February 28, 1969, at Fillmore West in San Francisco, CA
B3. Ballad of a Thin Man – April 1, 1988, at Brendan Byrne Arena in East Rutherford, N.J.
