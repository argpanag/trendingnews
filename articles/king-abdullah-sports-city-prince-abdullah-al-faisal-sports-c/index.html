The Local Organizing Committee for the 27th Arabian Gulf Cup "Khaleeji 27" has completed preparations at King Abdullah Sports City Stadium and Prince Abdullah Al-Faisal Sports City Stadium to host the tournament's matches in Jeddah, the Saudi Press Agency said on Wednesday.

The preparations included equipping the stadium pitches, facilities, and supporting services, completing arrangements for the entry and exit of spectators, and preparing designated areas for national teams, delegations, and media representatives, in accordance with the tournament's approved operational plans.

The preparations also included reviewing operational, crowd-management, security and safety, and medical service plans, as well as organizing spectator movement inside and around the two stadiums to facilitate entry and exit and ensure services are available throughout the match days.
