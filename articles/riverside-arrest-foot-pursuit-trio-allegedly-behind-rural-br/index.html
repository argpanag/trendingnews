Riverside arrest, foot pursuit: trio allegedly behind rural break-in charged after week-long inquiry

TheTools · 11.09.2026 23:15 · source · permalink

Your digital subscription includes access to content from all our websites in your region. Access unlimited content and the digital versions of our print editions - Today's Paper.

Central West police have arrested three people following a week-long investigation into a break and enter and vehicle theft on a rural property in the state's west.

Between 5pm on Wednesday, August 26, 2026 and 5.40am Thursday, August 27, NSW Police were told a property on Corcoran Road, Parkes, was broken into and a white utility vehicle was stolen.

Following investigations, about 5.30am on Friday, August 28, 2026, detectives arrested a 20-year-old woman at a home on McGregor Street, Condobolin. A mobile phone was also seized at the home.

The woman was taken to Condobolin Police Station, where she was charged.

About 7pm the same day, police located the utility vehicle in bushland near Springvale Road, near Condobolin. The vehicle was seized and examined by police.

About 7.30pm on Friday, September 4, 2026, police arrested a 19-year-old man near the Lachlan River at Condobolin. He was taken to Parkes Police Station, where he was charged.

Following further investigation, about 7.30am on Tuesday, September 8, 2026, officers attended a home on Thornsbury Street, Parkes

When police entered the premises, a man allegedly fled the house on foot.

After a foot pursuit, police arrested a 32-year-old man nearby. He was searched and, allegedly, small amounts of methylamphetamine and cannabis were found in his possession.

A search warrant was executed at the home, and police allegedly located and seized an amount of cannabis, and electronic devices, during the search.

The 32-year-old man was taken to Parkes Police Station, where he was charged.

The 20-year-old woman was charged with: dishonestly obtain financial advantage etc by deception, be carried in conveyance taken without consent of owner, and passenger not disclose driver's/other passenger's identity. She was granted conditional bail to appear at Condobolin Local Court on Thursday, October 15, 2026.

The 19-year-old man was charged with: be carried in conveyance taken without consent of owner, goods in personal custody suspected being stolen, possess prohibited drug, hinder or resist police officer in the execution of duty, passenger not disclose driver's/other passenger's identity, possession of equipment for administering prohibited drugs.

The 19-year-old man was also charged with an outstanding warrant.

The 19-year-old man was refused bail to appear at Bail Division Court 4 on Saturday, September 5, 2026, where he was refused bail to appear at Parkes Local Court on Thursday, September 17, 2026.

The 32-year-old man was charged with two counts of possess prohibited drug. He was also charged with an outstanding warrant.

The 32-year-old was refused bail to appear at Bail Division Court 4 on Wednesday September 9, 2026, where he was refused bail to appear at Parkes Local Court on Thursday, September 24, 2026.
