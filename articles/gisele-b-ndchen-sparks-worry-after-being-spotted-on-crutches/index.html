Gisele Bündchen Sparks Worry After Being Spotted On Crutches And In Medical Boot In Miami

TheTools · 19.10.2026 00:56 · source · permalink

Gisele Bündchen has raised concerns after being spotted using crutches and wearing a medical boot during an unexpected outing in Miami Beach. The 46-year-old supermodel, who is known for her active lifestyle and dedication to fitness, appeared to be nursing an injury to her right foot as she made her way out of a luxury hotel. The sighting comes as a surprise for fans accustomed to seeing the Brazilian beauty practicing yoga, working out, and maintaining a demanding wellness routine. While photographs have revealed her temporary reliance on crutches, Bündchen has yet to explain what happened or how long her recovery might take.

Gisele Bündchen Seen Using Crutches Outside Miami Beach Hotel

MEGA

Bündchen was photographed leaving the Delano Hotel in Miami Beach on Friday, October 16, with a medical boot covering her right foot. According to footage obtained by TMZ, the former Victoria's Secret Angel relied on a pair of crutches as she made her way toward a waiting vehicle. She appeared to be taking care not to put too much pressure on her right foot while navigating the area outside the hotel.

Despite the circumstances, Bündchen kept her outfit casual, wearing a fitted black tank top, denim shorts, and dark sunglasses. Her long blonde hair fell loosely around her shoulders as she made her way toward the vehicle. The model did not appear to address the cameras or offer any explanation for the medical boot. The outlet reported that it was unclear how Bündchen sustained the apparent injury and that the outlet had contacted her representative for comment. No further details about her condition were immediately available.

Gisele Bündchen Has Yet To Address Her Apparent Foot Injury

MEGA

The photographs have left several questions unanswered, particularly about what happened to Bündchen and whether the injury could affect her upcoming plans. Although the medical boot and crutches suggest that she is taking precautions with her right foot, the exact nature of the problem remains unknown. Bündchen has not publicly confirmed whether she suffered a fracture, sprain, or another type of injury. There is also no indication of how long she has been using the mobility aids or whether she has been advised to limit her physical activities.

The sighting is especially unexpected given how frequently the supermodel shares glimpses of her active lifestyle. From martial arts training to yoga sessions, Bündchen has long made physical fitness a major part of her daily routine. However, neither her workouts nor any other specific activity has been linked to the apparent injury. Until the model or her representatives provide an update, the circumstances surrounding the Miami outing remain unclear.

Gisele Bündchen Has Long Prioritized Fitness And Wellness

MEGA

Bündchen has never been shy about discussing the role exercise plays in her life. Throughout her modeling career, she has spoken about the importance of staying active, maintaining balance, and taking care of both her physical and emotional health. Her workouts have included strength training, yoga, Pilates, and jiu-jitsu, a martial art she has practiced for several years.

The supermodel also made headlines in September when she appeared in i-D magazine's Mother Issue, showing off her strength in a series of photographs that included weightlifting. As Page Six reported at the time, the photoshoot offered another glimpse at the model's commitment to staying fit. However, Bündchen has also emphasized that her priorities have changed since becoming a mother of three.

Rather than maintaining the nonstop professional schedule that once defined her career, she has become more selective about her projects and how she spends her time. Her recent Miami appearance offers a very different image from the athletic moments fans are accustomed to seeing. Still, the photographs alone do not indicate whether her apparent injury will have any lasting impact on her fitness routine.

Gisele Bündchen's Victoria's Secret History Remains Iconic

MEGA

The unexpected sighting also comes during a busy period for the fashion industry, with the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show returning to the spotlight. Bündchen remains one of the most recognizable former Angels, having helped define the lingerie brand's runway shows during the early 2000s. She joined Victoria's Secret in 1999 and went on to become one of its biggest stars before ending her association with the company in 2007. Among her most memorable appearances was the 2000 runway show, when she wore the extravagant Red Hot Fantasy Bra, valued at approximately $15 million. The model's history with the company has fueled occasional speculation about whether she might one day return to its famous runway.

Ahead of the October 18 show, Brazilian entertainment coverage discussed the possibility of a Bündchen comeback, although no official appearance had been announced. Her recent use of crutches adds another layer of uncertainty to that speculation. However, there is no evidence that Bündchen had committed to participating in the show or that her apparent injury caused her to cancel any plans. For now, her connection to Victoria's Secret remains a reminder of the runway career that made her an international fashion icon.

Gisele Bündchen Embraces A Different Chapter Away From The Runway

MEGA

Bündchen's latest appearance comes during a period when the former Victoria's Secret Angel has been enjoying a more private lifestyle after years in the fashion spotlight. Since stepping away from full-time runway modeling, she has focused on raising her children, maintaining her wellness routine, and taking on projects that fit around her family life. The Brazilian beauty welcomed her third child, a son, with boyfriend Joaquim Valente in February 2025. She also shares two children, Benjamin and Vivian, with her ex-husband, NFL legend Tom Brady.

Although Bündchen continues to work with major fashion and beauty brands, she has made it clear that being present for her children takes priority over maintaining the demanding schedule she once had. Her relationship with Valente, a jiu-jitsu instructor, has also brought renewed attention to her passion for martial arts and healthy living. For someone whose public image has long been associated with strength and an active lifestyle, the recent Miami photographs certainly marked an unusual sight.

Whether the medical boot will affect her upcoming activities remains to be seen, but Bündchen has yet to share any further details about the apparent injury.
