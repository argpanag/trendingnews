Samsung releases November 2026 update for Galaxy Fold 8 and Fold 8 Ultra

Samsung has begun rolling out the November 2026 software update for the Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra in South Korea. After releasing the November update for the Galaxy S26 series, Samsung is now bringing it to its latest foldable phones.

The new update comes with a software build number ending in AZK1. Users can check this code in their phone’s software information to confirm whether they have received the latest update.

The update package is about 600 MB. Users are advised to download it using a Wi-Fi connection to save mobile data. Samsung has not shared a detailed changelog for this update, but it is expected to include the latest security improvements and software fixes.

These improvements will help keep both foldable phones safe and running smoothly. Users in South Korea can check for the update by going to Settings, selecting Software update, and tapping Download and install.

For users in other countries, the update is not available yet. However, Samsung is expected to expand the rollout to more regions in the coming days. The update may arrive at different times depending on the country and carrier.

Galaxy Z Fold 8 and Fold 8 Ultra owners outside Korea will need to wait a little longer to receive the latest November update. Users can check the Software update section on their devices regularly to see when it becomes available.

Samsung Galaxy Watch 7 and Watch Ultra started getting the One UI 9 Watch Beta 6 update in South Korea. The company has recently begun releasing the stable Wear OS 7-based One UI 9 Watch update for the Watch 8 series.

Galaxy Watch 7 and Watch Ultra first-generation appear to be getting the last One UI 9 Watch Beta update as Beta 6 build. The latest beta arrives with firmware version ZZK1 and a download size of approximately 208.39MB. The update changelog lists three main changes, including stability improvements and bug fixes.

The arrival of Beta 6 could indicate that Samsung is approaching the final stages of One UI 9 Watch testing. However, the company hasn’t officially confirmed whether this is the final Beta release.

What’s new in One UI 9 Watch Beta 6

According to the changelog, Samsung has included three notable improvements in this release:

  • Applied stabilization code for device operation
  • Fixed battery drain issues
  • Fixed Alarm app errors

Battery consumption is particularly important for smartwatches, where users expect consistent endurance alongside health tracking, notifications, and background features. Fixing the Alarm app errors should also improve the reliability of an everyday feature.

Samsung’s priority with this release appears straightforward: iron out remaining bugs, improve battery performance, and prepare its previous-generation watches for the next major software experience.

Galaxy Watch 7 and Watch Ultra owners enrolled in the Beta Program can check for the ZZK1 update through the Galaxy Wearable app. Users outside South Korea may have to wait for availability in their respective markets.

More Galaxy devices are next

The September 28 rollout does not mean every eligible Galaxy device will receive One UI 9.0 immediately. Samsung is following a phased approach, with availability depending on the country, carrier, and specific model.

For now, owners of the listed devices can check Settings > Software update > Download and install. If the update is not available yet, it likely means Samsung has not opened the rollout for that particular region.

With the 2025 flagships now joining the stable release, Samsung is expected to continue expanding One UI 9.0 to more Galaxy smartphones and tablets in the coming weeks.

Older flagship models are also part of the broader roadmap, with some expected to receive the Android 17-based software during October.

Samsung is working on a new feature for its Contacts app that will help users remember birthdays. The feature could arrive with One UI 9.5 and make it easier to keep track of friends’ and family members’ special days.

Currently, users can add birthdays to contact details, but there is no separate section to see upcoming birthdays or whose birthday is today. Users have to check individual contacts or use the calendar to find this information.

According to a recent APK teardown by AssembleDebug, Samsung is planning to add a dedicated Birthdays section to the Contacts app. It will show birthdays in three categories: Past, Today, and Upcoming. This will help users quickly see missed birthdays, today’s celebrations, and birthdays coming soon.

Samsung may also add shortcuts for sending birthday cards and gifts. This will make it easier to celebrate special days without searching for other apps. Samsung has already introduced AI-generated birthday cards through Now Brief in One UI 8.

Image via Android Authority

Another useful addition could be birthday sharing. Users will be able to add their birthday to their profile and share it with their contacts. Once enabled, other people can see their birthday while viewing their contact details. Users can also see birthdays shared by their contacts.

Screenshots found during the investigation show a new birthday settings page with options to turn on birthday sharing and show contacts’ birthdays. Another screenshot gives a look at the new Birthdays page.

The feature is still under development, and Samsung has not officially confirmed its release date. However, it could make managing birthdays much easier for Galaxy users.
