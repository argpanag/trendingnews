  8. What happened in the sprint?published at 14:05 BST
    14:05 BST

    Just joining us? No worries, here is a very quick recap of the sprint race:

    • The race start was delayed by 30 minutes because of heavy rain
    • Mercedes' George Russell was leading the sprint on the half dry, half wet track. He lost control of his car and went into the wall at the end of the first lap.
    • Lewis Hamilton makes up positions at the start and moves into second, with Ferrari team-mate Charles Leclerc in third.
    • Also retiring in the early laps were Red Bull's Isack Hadjar, who was told to stop his car and the Alpine of Franco Colapinto.
    • Various incidents around the track resulted in DNFs across the grid.
    • On the final lap the two McLarens of Oscar Piastri and Lando Norris were in position to finish fourth and fifth but Norris attempted an overtake out of Turn Five, the cars touched and Piastri ended up in the barriers and out of the race.
    • Max Verstappen won a race for the first time in Singapore.
    • There were nine DNFs in total: Russell, Hadjar, Colapinto, Lance Stroll, Sergio Perez, Valtteri Bottas, Gabriel Bortoleto, Alex Albon and Piastri.

  17. Leclerc - 'We just didn't have the pace at the end'published at 11:52 BST
    11:52 BST

    Charles Leclerc speaking to David Coulthard after finishing third in the sprint race: "I struggled a lot from mid-race onwards. I went towards a quite aggressive balance on the other side of what I normally do with quite a weak front. It was quite good for the first part of the race but when it dried up I was nowhere."

    On whether he saw what happened with the McLarens: "I knew I had to have a good exit out of five because I was very weak in all right-hand corners, particularly with the front left. I looked in my mirrors and saw the red lights of the back of the cars so I knew something had happened. Kimi was also strong, we just didn't have the pace at the end."

  18. Hamilton 'had a mega start'published at 11:47 BST
    11:47 BST

    Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton speaking after finishing P2 in the sprint: "I had a mega start which is awesome. I love driving this track in the rain in those conditions so as soon as the heavens opened I was like 'this is a great day'. I was trying to get close to Max without killing my tyres, I wasn't really pushing that much but I was bringing him in and then I just realised at this point we've got a long way to go, I don't think we'll be able to overtake in these conditions, there's only one line. So I backed off and then the front right started to overheat a little bit but then I got the controls back and towards the end I started to push and close the gap. It's a tough track to overtake.

    "We showed that we have a good car, if we can do slightly better in qualifying, get both cars up there then I think we can apply pressure to the Mercedes' and Red Bulls. They are quick of course but we can be there hounding them so hopefully we can position the car in a slightly better position in this qualifying coming up."

  19. 'I want to win tomorrow' - Verstappenpublished at 11:44 BST
    11:44 BST

    Red Bull's Max Verstappen who won the sprint race at the Singapore Grand Prix: "The start was horrendous again, that is definitely something we need to improve on, because if we have something like that tomorrow, that is your race over if anything crazy happens. Not ideal and then we got lucky with what happened with George out of the last corner.

    "After that you just want to keep it calm and under control because you know you can't pass around here, especially in a condition like this, with the intermediates. We just try to bring it home, of course happy to win here in the sprint but I also know that I want to win tomorrow, not today."

    On winning at every track he has raced in: "I want to win tomorrow. I don't see this as a win, in terms of actually winning a grand prix. We need to have a good qualifying. It will be very close, with a lot of cars I think. We just have to try and make sure we are the best one out of them. But it will hopefully be a good battle."
