Jane Seymour on Joe Lando: "We really fell in love"

TheTools · 18.10.2026 10:40 · source · permalink

The love between Dr Quinn and Sully wasn't just TV magic! When Jane Seymour and Joe Lando reunited on stage, she revealed that they truly fell for each other – after fleeing a flood together just days into filming.

For six seasons, television viewers around the world followed the growing love between the brave doctor Michaela Quinn and the quiet outdoorsman Byron Sully. Now, more than thirty years later, Jane Seymour confirms what many fans had long suspected: the chemistry between her and co-star Joe Lando was not just acting.

Last Saturday, 17 October, the two were reunited on stage for a panel discussion about Dr. Quinn at the nostalgia convention 90s Con in Hartford in the American state of Connecticut. There, they talked about their first meeting – and about the moment they realised they had fallen in love. The conversation was led by one of People's editors, who was on site.

"I have never seen a man who looks so good"

Jane Seymour, today 75 years old, did not hold back from being honest about her first impression of the then young Joe Lando, who is today 64.

"I saw him and thought: 'My God, I haven't seen a man who looks that good for as long as I can remember', except maybe in some made-up love song," she said, and continued:

– We really fell in love. It's a secret. Or it's probably not a secret any more?

Fled from a flood together

What brought them together was something as dramatic as a natural disaster. Just a few days into filming, the filming location was hit by a sudden flood. Joe Lando urged Jane Seymour to jump into his pickup truck, and together they managed to get away from there.

"We came from there, and it was the craziest thing that has happened," Seymour said.

Afterwards, they went and had Mexican food and drank margaritas – and somewhere there, at a cheap dinner table, they understood what had happened.

"It cost about five dollars, and then we realised we had actually fallen in love. So everything you saw in Dr. Quinn, at least in the pilot episode, was real," she said.

Why the romance ended

But the love story off camera was short-lived. According to Seymour, they broke up fairly soon, as they wanted different things in life.

– We realised that Joe really needed … he had a plan. Was it four children and a white picket fence? And a younger wife, she said, explaining that she already had children, was older, and didn't think she would be able to have more.

However, life had other plans. Seymour later became pregnant again – and couldn't resist joking about it on stage.

– But I showed him – I had twins!

“Like an old married couple”

Today, they describe their relationship as a deep friendship. When they spoke to People in June, Lando said they are “like an old married couple”, and Seymour agreed: they can finish each other's sentences and know exactly what will upset the other. When Lando's home was destroyed in a fire, he even moved in with her.

Both have other partners today. Joe Lando has been married to his wife Kristen since 1997, and Jane Seymour revealed in June that she has got engaged to A&E doctor John Zambetti.

Reunited in Harry Wild

Den som saknar paret på tv-skärmen har ändå goda nyheter att se fram emot. Joe Lando guest stars in the fifth season of Acorn TV's cosy crime drama Harry Wild, where Seymour plays the title role as a retired literature professor who solves murders in Dublin. Lando plays a charming American pathologist – and there's no mistaking the old chemistry.

The role of Harry Wild means a great deal to Seymour. Last summer she revealed that she wants to be a poster name for all women over 50 – read more in our article Jane Seymour, 75: "Life isn't over after 50".

The role that saved her

Dr. Quinn, Medicine Woman aired on American CBS for six seasons between 1993 and 1998 and became a huge success in Sweden as well. The cast also featured, among others, Chad Allen, Erika Flores, Jessica Bowman and Shawn Toovey, and the ensemble later reunited for TV films in 1999 and 2001.

For Jane Seymour, the role came at exactly the right time. She has said that she was at the lowest point of her life, essentially broke and on the verge of losing her home, when the offer came. She called her agent and said she’d consider doing anything – and that very same day, Dr. Quinn came along. The following morning she said yes, by lunchtime she was trying on costumes, and the day after that she was standing in front of the camera. The rest is TV history.
