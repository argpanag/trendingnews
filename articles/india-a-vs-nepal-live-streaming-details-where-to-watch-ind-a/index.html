India A vs Nepal Live Streaming Details: Where To Watch IND A vs NEP Rising Asian Stars Fixture?

TheTools · 20.11.2026 04:46 · source · permalink

ETV Bharat / sports

India A will square off against Nepal in their first game of the 2026 Emerging Asia Cup, where Tilak Varma will lead the Indian team.

By ETV Bharat Sports Team

Doha (Qatar): India A will kick off their Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026 campaign against Nepal on November 20 in Doha. The squad, led by Tilak Varma, will lock horns with Nepal at 3:30 PM IST. The BCCI have named a strong India A squad which includes the likes of Abhishek Sharma, Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, Rinku Singh, Shahbaz Ahmed, Suryansh Shedge, Harshit Rana and Prince Yadav. Abhishek and Sooryavanshi are regulars in the T20I team, while Prince Yadav earned his place in the shortest format recently.

Rinku was a member of India's 2026 T20 World Cup-winning squad, while Harshit Rana was part of the team in their 2025 Champions Trophy title win. Tillak Varma, who is currently vice-captain of the T20I squad, will lead the India A team.
