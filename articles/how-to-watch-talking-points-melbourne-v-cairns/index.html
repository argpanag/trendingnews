Talking points

Big response incoming

Melbourne United slumped to a 1-2 record on Thursday night, losing a gritty game to Tasmania, with a number of their stars fouling out.

Expect Jacob Chance's side to bounce back in a big way on Saturday, as they return to John Cain Arena.

The hosts will also be buoyed by the return of defensive linchpin Shea Ili, who will make his season debut after recovering from a hamstring injury.

Pressure cooker

Through the opening three rounds of the Hungry Jack's NBL27 Season, arguably no coach is under more pressure than Cairns' Adam Forde.

The Taipans currently sit in 10th with a 1-3 record, which includes a double-digit loss to the Breakers in New Zealand.

The other Snake under pressure is Million Dollar Man Jack McVeigh, who is averaging 15.3 points a game this season, significantly down on last season's 21.2.
