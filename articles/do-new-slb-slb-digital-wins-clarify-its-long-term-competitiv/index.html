SLB Investment Narrative Recap

To own SLB, you generally need to believe its core oilfield services, digital platforms and newer low carbon work can all contribute to steady, if cyclical, earnings. The Equinor and Invictus awards support this multi basin, technology led narrative, but they do not materially change the near term focus on global upstream spending in 2025 or the key risk around integrating ChampionX and realizing the expected cost and revenue synergies.

Among recent developments, the expanded NVIDIA collaboration to build an "AI Factory for Energy" looks especially relevant beside the Johan Sverdrup digital contract. Both underline SLB's push to scale higher margin digital workflows across production and reservoir management, which could be an important counterweight if traditional short cycle spending weakens or macro volatility pressures earnings over the next couple of years.
