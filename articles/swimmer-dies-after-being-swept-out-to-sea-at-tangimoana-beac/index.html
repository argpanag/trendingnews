Swimmer dies after being swept out to sea at Tangimoana Beach

TheTools · 12.10.2026 07:09 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

One person has died after a water-related swimming incident in Tangimoana this afternoon, making it the third death at the Rangitīkei River mouth within a month.

About 2.30pm, police said, they were called to Tangimoana Beach Rd after reports that two people were swept out to sea while swimming at the Manawatu-Whanganui beach.

One person managed to “self-rescue” and reach the shore safely, a police spokesperson said.

A second person was found dead about 3.50pm.

Police said they worked at the scene alongside Hato Hone St John and the New Zealand Defence Force.

Coastguard Manawatū and Coastguard Whanganui, Surf Life Saving New Zealand, and members of the public also helped with rescue efforts.

Police are investigating the circumstances of this latest death.

Last month, two people died near the rivermouth at Tangimoana after their boat capsized.

The Manawatū area commander, Inspector Matenga Gray, said at the time: “Sadly, two people died, while another sustained injuries and has been taken to hospital”.
