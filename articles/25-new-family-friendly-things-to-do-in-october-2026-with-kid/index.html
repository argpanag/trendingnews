25 New Family-Friendly Things To Do In October 2026 With Kids In Singapore

TheTools · 30.09.2026 07:00 · source · permalink

October brings along with it Children’s Day and the F1 season. Wondering what family-friendly activities or what are the new places to visit in October in Singapore? There are variety of activities and things to do this October 2026 in Singapore.

What’s New: Family-friendly and Kid-friendly Things to Do in October in Singapore

BuzzAttack: The Live Game Show Experience

Till 1 January 2027, Clarke Quay Central

Ever wondered what it would be like to take part in a TV game show rather than simply watch one? At BuzzAttack Singapore, you get to step up to your own game station, compete across a series of challenges and see how your score stacks up against the rest of your group. Read more about BuzzAttack Singapore.

Sesame Street Magical Art World Chapter 2

1 October to 31 December 2026, Marina Square Singapore

Celebrate art and creativity with the beloved characters of Sesame Street at Marina Square Singapore’s Magical Art World Chapter 2. Enter into an immersive, multi-sensory space where kids and adults can see, touch and interact with art in playful ways – together with the help of global artists. Find out more about Sesame Street Magical Art World Chapter 2. 

SJ60 Event

Till 11 October, Takashimaya Square B2

Join a celebration of 60 years of Singapore-Japan friendship with a lineup of activities that include complimentary ice-cream redemption, a Digital Stamp Rally and live performances. Find out more about the SJ60 Event at Takashimaya Square.

 

SSO Babies’ Proms – Make Some Noise

2 and 3 October 2026, Victoria Concert Hall

Move along to the music with energetic William “Uncle Bill” Ledbetter as the magical world of orchestra unfolds. The relaxed and interactive format allows children to move, dance and respond naturally to the music, providing a welcoming introduction to live orchestral performance.

Burghley Children’s Day Carnival

3 October, 45 Burgley Drive

Head to Serangoon Gardens for the Burghley Centre Kids Carnival. Grab a goody bag, chase down a claw machine win, and spend an afternoon getting delightfully sticky with candyfloss. Get more details about the Burghley Children’s Day Carnival.

 

LEGO F1® Experience the Thrill

2 October onwards, Suntec City Atrium (Tower 3 & 4)

Expect plenty of racing-inspired fun, from interactive challenges and a life-sized VCARB F1® LEGO Big Build to photo opportunities and exclusive rewards at Suntec City Atrium.

Octoburst 2026

2 to 4 October, Esplanade

Held in conjunction with Children’s Day, Esplanade Presents Octoburst! – A Children’s Festival is a festival that encourages artful play and fun for all. There a variety of free and ticketed activities available over the Children’s Day weekend in Singapore.

Glow-in-The-Dark Activities at KidsSTOP

2 to 4 October, KidsSTOP

Head to KidsSTOP and discover light at the glow-in-the-dark activities. Create a star-loom craft, decorate a light stick, join in a glow-in-the-dark dance party!

Ngee Ann Teochew Cultural & Food Festival 

2 to 4 October, Ngee Ann City

The three-day Ngee Ann Teochew Cultural & Food Festival once again brings Teochew heritage, cuisine and performing arts to the heart of Orchard Road. The festival is a free but ticketed event presented by the Ngee Ann Cultural Centre and co-organised by Teochew Federation (Singapore). Find out more about the Ngee Ann Teochew Cultural & Food Festival.

 

Jazz for Kids with Miss Lou

3 October, Our Tampines Hub

Groove along with your little ones as Miss Lou and the band introduce jazz, blues, funk and improvisation through interactive music‑making. Be guided by Lindy Hop dancers and come ready to join in — a fun, family‑friendly session for all ages.

Joo Chiat Car-Free Day

3 October, Joo Chiat Road

One of Singapore’s most distinctive neighbourhoods goes car-free with its streets coming alive with activities, culture and community. Joo Chiat Car-Free Day is held in conjunction with World Car Free Day. Find out more details.

 

Once Upon a Sandscape

3 & 4 October, Alliance Française, Théâtre

Playful, poetic and full of surprises, Once Upon a Sandscape brings traditional storytelling and live sand art together in a performance where children are not simply watching the story — they become part of it! Get more details about Once Upon a Sandscape.

IKEA Play Festival

Various dates in October, IKEA stores

In conjunction with the launch of two new collections – YXSTABY and ALPTALL, IKEA is holding a month-long line-up of family-friendly activities and gaming challenges. In particular, on 3 and 4 October, IKEA Alexandra is set to become a playground for all gamers and cosplayers with gaming challenges and more. Find out more about IKEA’s Play Festival.

NAVArt

3 & 4 October, Singapore Navy Museum

Make your own zine, leave your mark at our Graffiti Wall Jam, catch live DJ sets, discover the craft of glass neon, try your hand at silkscreen printing, step into an interactive theatre experience, and capture the night with SNAP at the Singapore Navy Museum. 

Sacred Caves: Dunhuang, Buddhism, and the Silk Road

Till 7 March 2027, Asian Civilisations Museum

The Sacred Caves: Dunhuang, Buddhism, and the Silk Road exhibition at Asian Civilisations Museum features life-size grotto reproductions and original works of art from the Mogao Caves for the first time in Southeast Asia. The exhibition also features original paintings, sculptures, manuscripts, textiles and devotional objects, as well as has a stamp rally and digital trail. Get more details about Sacred Caves: Dunhuang, Buddhism, and the Silk Road.

Hans Zimmer Live – The Next Level World Tour 2026

7 October, Singapore Indoor Stadium

The legendary film composer returns with an all-new production that pushes the boundaries of live music through electronic sound design, powerful orchestration, spectacular lighting, and a striking new stage concept. Through fresh arrangements of music from Pirates of the Caribbean, The Lion King, The Dark Knight, Interstellar and Dune, alongside new selections from Dune: Part Two and F1®: The Movie, The Next Level brings Zimmer’s cinematic worlds to life in a completely new way. Get more details about Hans Zimmer Live – The Next Level World Tour.

 

Orchard Paddock

8 to 10 October, Ngee Ann City Civic Plaza

Orchard Paddock offers visitors a chance to experience the excitement of race weekend beyond the circuit, bringing together car showcases, interactive experiences, live entertainment, food and games at Ngee Ann City Civic Plaza from 8 to 10 October. The Circuit Card Stamp Rally invites visitors to complete activities around Orchard Paddock to collect stamps, with each stamp earning a chance to win a Dongfeng 007 Performance worth over $218,000. Beyond race activities, there will also be live performances, racing-themed movie screenings and trivia nights, with line-ups including YAØ, Alyph, 53A, Reverie and Dreamsound, alongside a screening of The Fast and the Furious: Tokyo Drift.

World Post Day

8 to 11 October, SingPost Centre

From 8 to 11 October, visit SingPost Centre for four days of launches, exhibitions and hands-on activities. Highlights include Singapore Then & Now Lenticular Stamps, a Singapore first, in collaboration with The Straits Times. Tilt the stamp and watch the Fullerton Building and Postman shift between past and present. Other activities include Stamp rally, custom keychain making, daily datestamps, philately talks and ice cream giveaways. Get more details about World Post Day.

Sunflower Surprise

9 Oct to 9 Nov, Flower Dome, Gardens by the Bay

The sights and stories of Portugal make port at Gardens by the Bay for its first Portuguese-themed floral display, Sunflower Surprise: Garden by the Ocean in Flower Dome. Shaped by centuries of seafaring and known for its sun-soaked coastal towns, the display captures the country’s character through its maritime history, everyday traditions and agricultural heritage. Find out more about Sunflower Surprise.

Harry Potter and the Philosopher’s Stone in Concert

9 October, The Star Performing Arts Centre

Music and Film fans can now unite together, reliving the magical world of Potter on big screen, while soaking in the live orchestra music conducted by internationally acclaimed conductor Benjamin Pope.

 

Reopening of National Museum of Singapore’s History Gallery

From 10 October, National Museum of Singapore

Singapore History Gallery now boasts of a highly immersive experience, with multimedia experiences and hands-on touchpoints awaiting visitors along with artefacts and storytelling. It has been updated to reflect new research and presents rich narratives that in my opinion make the gallery experience even more family-friendly. Find out more about what to expect at the refreshed National Museum of Singapore’s Singapore History Gallery. 

The Living Archive

10 to 18 October, National Museum of Singapore

The Living Archive, a curated market of design, craft and food taking place from 10 to 18 October 2026 at The Concourse on Level 1. The market forms part of the Museum’s 139th anniversary celebrations. Look forward to more than 30 participating brands across the event, with the line-up refreshing from 14 October. The curation spans accessories, art and prints, one-of-a-kind objects, homeware, food and drink. Selected makers will also bring their processes into the Museum through live demonstrations, customisation stations and hands-on workshops. 

Japan Rail Fair 2026

15 to 17 October, Urban Park, Guoco Tower

From regional destinations and tourism organisations to food, travel experiences, railway-related attractions and more, there will be plenty to discover across the Japan Rail Fair. 

 

Klook Travel Fest: Flee Market Edition

17 to 18 October, Suntec City, Atrium (Tower 1 & 2)

Inspired by the lively local markets and bazaars travellers often stumble upon while exploring a new destination, this year’s Klook Travel Fest brings that same sense of wandering and discovery to the heart of the event. Visitors can explore destination showcases, uncover hidden gems and take part in hands-on activities to get a taste of their next adventure before even leaving Singapore.

Koocester’s Car Meet

18 October, Sentosa Cove Village

Titled Beasts vs Beauties, the event brings a heavy lineup of high-performance engines and aesthetic builds to Sentosa Cove Village. It is an early morning takeover for the local automotive community, featuring car clubs, product try-outs, and a mix of Singapore’s most notable machines. Get more details here.

 

POSB PAssion Run for Kids 2026

25 October, The Promontory @ Marina Bay

Get ready for another exciting edition filled with meaningful moments, family fun, and unforgettable experiences. From the race routes to the vibrant event village, there’s plenty to look forward to as we come together once again to support children in our community through the POSB PAssion Kids Fund. Get more details about POSB PAssion Run for Kids 2026.
