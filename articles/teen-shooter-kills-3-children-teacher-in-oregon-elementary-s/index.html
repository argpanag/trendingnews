Teen shooter kills 3 children, teacher in Oregon elementary school before being shot by bystander | CNN

TheTools · 16.10.2026 05:54 · source · permalink

What we know

• The victims: Three children, aged 5, 8 and 11, and a teacher were killed in a shooting at the Silver Rail Elementary School in Bend, Oregon, on Thursday. Another child and teacher were rushed to hospital with critical injuries.

• The shooter: The 16-year-old Bend resident was shot dead by a “community member” who pursued him into the school, Police Chief Mike Krantz said. The shooter’s motive is currently unclear.

• School shooting surge: This is the 36th shooting on K-12 school grounds across the United States this year, according to a CNN data analysis.

Updates

Recess laughter was replaced by sirens, neighbor says

Sierra Jordan, who works at a business less than half a mile from the school, told CNN the typical laughter of children playing outside for recess was replaced by a swarm of ambulances, police officers and other first responders rushing to the scene.

Jordan had never seen so many police officers in her life, she said, adding one of her coworkers was on a walk outside when she heard gunfire around 1 p.m. That coworker was not sure exactly how many shots she heard.

The situation is “absolutely heartbreaking,” Jordan said.

Bend is described as a scenic and safe city

Bend, a picturesque city of about 100,000 people nestled on the Deschutes River in Oregon, is better known for its outdoor activities and easy access to hiking and biking trails.

The town was named in the top 75 safest midsize cities in the country, according to a SmartAsset study earlier this year.

But Thursday’s deadly shooting isn’t the first in Bend. In 2022, a 20-year-old gunman, a Bend resident, killed two people after walking into a grocery store with an AR-15-style rifle and opening fire.

Oregon state has also seen a handful of school shootings over the past 30 years.

In 2015, a 26-year-old student killed nine people in a shooting rampage at Umpqua Community College in Roseburg. The year before, a 15-year-old student opened fire at Reynolds High School in Troutdale, killing one student before turning the gun on himself.

In 1998, CNN reported on a shooting at Thurston High School in Springfield, where a 15-year-old killed two students and wounded 25 others before he was subdued by fellow students.

Here's what we heard in emergency dispatch audio

Police characterized the person who killed the shooter as “a community member,” and an initial Emergency Medical Services radio dispatch indicates it may have been a pupil’s father.

“It looks like the shooter has been neutralized, possibly shot by a father at the school,” the dispatch audio said.

Officials have not specified the bystander’s connection to the school.

Bend-La Pine Schools had "secure vestibules" and upgraded locking system, district says

All schools in the Bend-La Pine Schools district are equipped with “secure vestibules” at the entrance of each of its buildings and a classroom door locking system, according to the school district’s website.

A photo ID is needed to access the main campus, and the system allows schools to screen and track all visitors. The school district said it has security cameras installed both inside and outside of all buildings.

In 2022, schools in the district began receiving new door locks as part of a multi-million dollar upgrade that allow “anyone inside the classroom to turn the lock and secure the classroom” and is designed to “increase the timeliness and ease of a classroom lockdown.”

Police said the Silver Rail Elementary School gunman forced his way into the school by shooting at locked doors, but it’s unclear how far he made it into the building, according to Chief Mike Krantz of Bend Police Department.

Bend community holds emotional candlelit prayer vigil

Members of the Bend community gathered in a local downtown park Thursday evening for a prayer vigil to support and grieve for those impacted by the school shooting.

A large crowd of mourners held candles and hugged one another as they listened to prayers, music and community leaders in an emotional vigil.

This is the 36th shooting on K-12 school grounds this year

The Silver Rail Elementary tragedy is the 36th shooting on K-12 school grounds this year in the United States, according to CNN’s analysis of multiple databases, including the Gun Violence Archive.

There have been several shootings at elementary and middle schools in recent years, including the deadly mass shooting at Annunciation Catholic School last year and the 2022 shooting at Robb Elementary School in Uvalde, Texas.

As school shootings have increased nationwide in recent years, they have become more common at homecoming and football-related events, too, according to a CNN analysis.

“As a country, we, we still lack the courage to do anything to try to prevent these things from occurring. And it’s going to happen again,” CNN’s Senior Law Enforcement Analyst Charles Ramsey said.

A timeline of how the shooting unfolded

Police are investigating why a 16-year-old entered an elementary school in Bend, Oregon on Thursday with a long gun and began shooting, killing three children and one teacher.

Here’s a timeline of how the attack unfolded, according to Bend Police Department chief Mike Krantz:

  • 12:59 p.m.: The first 911 call is received. It’s from someone in a vehicle reporting being shot at multiple times by an individual with a long gun. The call indicated an “unknown man had shot into a car.” Moments later, dispatch receive dozens of calls reporting that a person had entered the Silver Rail Elementary School and started shooting.
  • 1 p.m.: Dispatch use a tone to alert all law enforcement.
  • 1:01 p.m.: Police direct the school to go into lockdown.
  • 1:02 p.m.: Police arrive on the scene and report that the suspect has been shot by a community member. The bystander saw the shooter enter the school and followed him in before engaging him in gunfire and killing him. Police entered the building and confirmed the suspect is dead. Officers then clear the school and give emergency aid to the casualties. Police confirm four dead, including three children and one teacher.

All students and staff were then transported to the Journey Church for reunification with families.

Every Bend police officer went through a full day of active threat training at a local high school in August, Bend Police Department chief Mike Krantz said Thursday.

“This is going to be a long investigation,” he added.

A 5-year-old is among the children killed in an Oregon school shooting. Here's what we know

Three children, aged 5, 8 and 11, and one teacher were killed when a 16-year-old entered an elementary school in Bend, Oregon, and started shooting, police said Thursday.

Here’s what we know about the shooting:

What happened: The first 911 call indicated an “unknown man had shot into a car,” moments before calls to police reported a person had entered the school and started shooting. The shooter, armed with a long gun, forced his way into the school by shooting at locked doors, but it’s unclear how far he made it into the building, said Chief Mike Krantz of Bend Police Department. A “community member” who saw the teenager entering Silver Rail Elementary School at about 1 p.m. followed him in before engaging him in gunfire, killing him before responding officers arrived, Krantz said.

What we know about the casualties: Police found five people dead at the scene, Krantz said. A 7-year-old child and another teacher were also injured and transferred to a hospital, according to officials.

What we know about the shooter: Police have not yet identified the shooter and it is not yet clear whether he had any connection to the school, what specific long gun was used, or how many rounds were fired. Police have not yet weighed in on a potential motive. The FBI is assisting in the investigation, according to FBI Director Kash Patel.
