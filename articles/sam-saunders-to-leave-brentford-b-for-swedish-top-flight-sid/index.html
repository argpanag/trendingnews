Sam Saunders to leave Brentford B for Swedish top-flight side Djurgårdens IF | Brentford FC

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:08 · source · permalink

Sam Saunders is set to leave Brentford to take on the role of head coach at Djurgårdens IF.

Saunders will leave the Bees next month to take the position at the Stockholm club, who were Conference League semi-finalists in 2024/25.

His last game in charge will be Brentford B’s Premier League 2 fixture against Crystal Palace on 20 December.

Chris Burke, currently Saunders’ assistant, will take over the head coach role on an interim basis. He will be supported by senior PDP coach Jon-Paul Pittman, head of football development Jamie Greenwood and other members of the Academy coaching team.

The move to Sweden will bring to an end a long association with Brentford for Saunders. He spent almost eight years at the club as a player and made more than 200 appearances after joining from Dagenham and Redbridge in the summer of 2009.

He left in January 2017 to join Wycombe Wanderers but returned in 2019 to coach Brentford B, first as assistant and, from July 2025, as head coach after Neil MacFarlane moved up to become a first-team coach.

Brentford academy director David Rainford said: “Having played alongside Sam before he joined Brentford, it has been a real pleasure for me to see his career flourish at the club, first as a player and then through his transition into coaching.

“We are naturally sad to see him leave, but equally delighted that he has earned this exciting opportunity with Djurgårdens IF.

“Sam has made tremendous progress as a coach within the Brentford environment. His commitment and expertise as a player developer have had a huge impact on our young players and on our progression as a Category One Academy.

“He has helped young professionals make a brilliant start to their careers, supporting them to understand the standards required to progress into senior football while also nurturing their development as young men.

“Sam will rightly be forever associated with Brentford and leaves with our sincere thanks and very best wishes.

“Chris Burke knows the players and our programme extremely well, and we have every confidence in Chris and the wider Academy coaching team as we continue to build and improve what we do. This is an exciting time for our players and staff and, as is our way at Brentford, we will keep looking forward and evolving.”

Saunders’ first season in charge of Brentford B ended with the Professional Development League regular-season title.

After the Academy was awarded Category One status ahead of the 2026/27 season, Saunders led the Under-21s into Premier League 2 for the first time, as well as Brentford Academy’s first Jersey Mike’s Trophy campaign.

Wins away at League One side Bromley and League Two outfit Crawley Town saw his side progress through to the knockout stages at the first attempt.

Saunders played 206 times and scored 30 goals for Brentford during his playing career, with 170 of those appearances coming in the EFL.

He was also part of the team that reached the Football League Trophy final in 2011 and helped the Bees win promotion from League One in 2013/14.

The former winger was inducted into the club’s Hall of Fame in 2017.
