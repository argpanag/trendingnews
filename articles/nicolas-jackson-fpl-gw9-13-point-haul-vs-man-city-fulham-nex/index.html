Nicolas Jackson FPL GW9: 13-Point Haul vs Man City, Fulham Next

TheTools · 24.10.2026 14:26 · source · permalink

Nicolas Jackson scored twice in Aston Villa's 2-1 win over Man City for 13 FPL points, and Villa host Fulham next. We make the case for buying the £6.6m striker for Gameweek 9, and the risks.

Nicolas Jackson scored twice at Villa Park today, in the 6th and 60th minutes of a 12:30 kick-off (all times UK), as Aston Villa beat Man City 2-1. It was City’s first Premier League defeat of the season, and it earned Jackson 13 FPL points: eight for the goals, three bonus and two for playing more than an hour, according to official FPL data.

John McGinn set up the first goal. Mbaye, on for Alysson in the 59th minute, made the second a minute later. Jackson came off in the 82nd minute. It was Villa’s first Premier League home win of the season, and City had won their first seven league games. Our Aston Villa vs Man City player ratings cover every player from the game.

Price, ownership and form

Jackson costs £6.6m and is owned by 4.6% of managers as we write on Saturday afternoon. He’s the most-bought player since the Gameweek 8 deadline, with more than 56,000 transfers in, and his price is already up £0.1m since the season started. He has 34 points, four goals and seven bonus points in 547 minutes, with a form figure of 8.0. Today’s 13 points are provisional until the gameweek finishes.

The underlying numbers are less flattering. Official FPL data gives him 1.82 expected goals this season against four scored, so he’s running slightly hot. Today fits that. Villa had just 2 shots on target, and Sky Sports put their expected goals at 0.61 to City’s 1.79. Jackson scored from both, and that conversion rate won’t keep happening.

Jackson against the other forwards

Official FPL data, Saturday 24 October. Difficulty: 1 easiest, 5 hardest. xG is season expected goals.

Evanilson is cheaper at £6.2m and his 2.59 expected goals beat Jackson’s 1.82, so the case for him is real. We’d still pick Jackson for Gameweek 9, because Fulham at home is the easiest fixture of the four: difficulty 2, against 3 for Evanilson and Calvert-Lewin and 5 for Kostoulas, who visits Man City. Calvert-Lewin has the best expected goals and is owned by 22.5%, but the FPL app lists him as a 50% chance of playing with a foot injury. Kostoulas has the most points of the four, 41, and is the cheapest at £5.8m, and Brighton’s trip to City is what holds us back.

Villa’s next five

  • Fulham (H), Sat 31 Oct, 20:00 UK, difficulty 2
  • Man Utd (A), Sun 8 Nov, 16:30, difficulty 4
  • Sunderland (H), Sat 21 Nov, 15:00, difficulty 3
  • Ipswich (A), Sat 28 Nov, 17:30, difficulty 2
  • Everton (H), Wed 2 Dec, 20:15, difficulty 3

Only Man Utd away is rated above 3, and our Gameweek 9 wildcard XI covers the same five-week run if you’re rebuilding the whole squad. Villa teammate Manzambi, who also started today, has his own FPL piece, and we previewed the match in our predicted lineups.

The risk

Minutes are the first concern. Jackson was off in the 82nd minute today, and Villa’s attack was thin on chances: 8 shots to City’s 15 and 36.8% of the ball, according to Sky Sports. A striker who needs both of his team’s shots on target to score twice can’t be relied on to do it weekly, and 547 minutes is a small sample behind a form figure of 8.0.

The Gameweek 9 deadline is 11am UK time on Saturday 31 October. Villa kick off against Fulham at 20:00 UK the same day, so you’ll be buying before the team sheet is out. His price could change before then, and we don’t have a forecast for it.
