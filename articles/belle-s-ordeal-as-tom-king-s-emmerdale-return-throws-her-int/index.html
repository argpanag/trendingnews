Belle's ordeal as Tom King's Emmerdale return throws her into terror

TheTools · 12.10.2026 23:59 · source · permalink

Emmerdale’s Tom King (James Chase) is being released early from his three-year stint inside for assault and coercive and controlling behaviour.

Belle Dingle (Eden Taylor-Draper) has been working hard to rebuild her life since Tom nearly destroyed it with his abuse. She’s back on track with her business and her relationship with Kammy Hadiq (Shebz Miah) is going great guns – perfect time for Tom to rock back up and destroy it all.

Belle’s devastated and terrified when Victim Liaison Office arrives to deliver the bad news that Tom will be released soon. Belle’s somewhat reassured that he legally can’t come anywhere near her, but Kammy isn’t convinced she’s ok.

It doesn’t help when the local vicar pokes his nose in – Charles Anderson (Kevin Mathurin) overhears Belle chatting with Nicola King (Nicola Wheeler), Tom’s aunt, about it all, and Charles steps in to say Tom is a changed man after forgiveness. He’s been lending his ear to the criminal as the prison chaplain. His comment hints that Tom is keen to see her again.

Belle feels disgusted that Tom is already trying to worm his way back in via the vicar. There’s only one thing for it – to see him for herself.

It’s a balsy, if not brash move, and thankfully she has the sensible heads of Cain Dingle (Jeff Hordley) and Kammy to talk her down. But she’s terrified Tom will come and find her anyway – perhaps meeting him face to face would have given her some control rather than him pop out of the shadows at the Homestead like he’s done before.

It’s Rhona Goskirk (Zoe Henry) who comes to her rescue, chatting about her own experiences with Pierce and it helps Belle to see that she hasn’t lost her control at all. She still holds the power, and she walks away feeling miles better.

But Rhona holds a dark truth. She secretly warns Cain that if Tom comes back, he’ll be more dangerous than ever. Her reassurances to Belle were just platitudes.

Producer Laura Shaw has already confirmed that there are dark times ahead for Belle, having said that the storyline will be ‘reigniting a complex and challenging story’, suggesting Belle’s efforts to keep him away will likely be unsuccessful and that the danger Rhona refers to could quickly become real.
