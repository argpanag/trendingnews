Man City 5-3 Sunderland: Antoine Semenyo says Black Cats 'made it very, very tough'

TheTools · 21.09.2026 16:53 · source · permalink
  • Published
    8 hours ago

Former Sunderland and current Manchester City winger Antoine Semenyo, speaking to BBC Radio Newcastle after Sunday's 5-3 win over the Black Cats: "We knew if we controlled them as much as we can we could get the three points.

"It wasn't easy. They made it very, very tough for us.

"We're building a culture and still adjusting, but we're all happy. It's togetherness and sticking together.

"The message from the manager is to win and be at the top of the table by the end of the game."

On netting twice against his former side, he added: "It's crazy. It's so long since I played for them, but happy to win."
