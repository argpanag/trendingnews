As people across the country look for ways to honor Dolly Parton and her impact, U.S. Sens. Marsha Blackburn, R-Tenn., Bill Hagerty, R-Tenn., and Catherine Cortez Masto, D-Nev., are leading efforts to pass a resolution honoring the songbird.

Rep. Diana Harshbarger, R-Tenn., who represents the Tri-Cities area, also introduced a companion resolution in the U.S. House.

"Dolly Parton was a Tennessee treasure who lived out the Volunteer Spirit, loved our nation, and opened doors of opportunity for countless people," Blackburn said. "While there are no words to fully express the sorrow all Tennesseans feel in her loss, I hope this resolution plays at least a small part in ensuring that her legacy is never forgotten, and I am honored to lead it in the Senate."

The resolution focuses on honoring Parton for her tremendous impact through music, philanthropy, literacy, education, health, conservation and how she enriched the lives of countless Americans.

"Dolly is truly a Tennessee legend," Hagerty said. "From her humble beginnings in the foothills of the Great Smoky Mountains to worldwide fame, Dolly exemplified the American Dream. Whether she was performing on stages across the globe, creating opportunities for families to make memories in Dollywood, or fostering a love of reading through her Imagination Library, Dolly lived a life dedicated to encouraging others to dream just as boldly as she did. She never forgot where she came from and left an indelible cultural and economic impact on our state. She will be very dearly missed."
