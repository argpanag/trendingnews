TL Josedeodo On The Struggles Vs BLG: "We Never Forced The Situations." - Hotspawn

TheTools · 25.10.2026 03:11 · source · permalink

Davide "Dovi" Xu

Share:

Davide "Dovi" Xu

Following the end of their match against BiliBili Gaming, we spoke to TL Josedeodo about what went wrong today, as they will be facing the LPL #1 seed, Anyone’s Legend, to stay alive at Worlds 2026.

Davide: Man, [it was] another rough game. What do we make of this? I talked to Spawn the other day and he looked very, very confident, but the results aren’t really coming.

Josedeodo: I would say that in most of our games, I feel like we’re not progressing the games, or we don’t have a clear plan for what’s happening in the game. I feel like they’re just doing the basics and we’re just losing to it.

I don’t think CFO was a normal case, because of what happened at level 1, but I do think that today our confidence went down a bit after what happened yesterday, and we didn’t play our normal game.

Even yesterday, with everything that happened in the early game, we were still playing quite normally. We were making our plays. But today it felt like, I don’t know, they just respected us once, twice, and then we died three times and lost the game. It was quite disappointing to play this game.

Davide: Are you guys scared of making plays or something like that? The general feeling we got from watching was that the game was quite slow.

Josedeodo: Well, I was thinking to myself during the game that my matchup isn’t a great one. Whenever I see Lee Sin, he can just kick me out of the place if he wants until the first item. Then we’re even.

The early game, for example, felt quite normal. Nobody got Dragons, which was quite nice for us. I think overall that part was pretty normal. I do think we didn’t have enough timings to punish the Orianna, so she was playing quite safe, which is not something we want because we’re playing Locke.

And then I just feel like we never got a moment to fight with our champs. We never had the situations. We never forced the situations. And whenever we did force something, like the J4 flash on me, it felt like it wasn’t good enough.

The plays weren’t clicking, or when we went to Herald, we just got hit by Nautilus and died. It was either going to be me or Yeon who died, so it just wasn’t a good play. So, I don’t know. The games were quite heavy. Today we didn’t play our normal game, and that’s why we lost.

Davide: I’m not sure about the rest of the team, but you said the confidence went down a little. How do you feel right now?

Josedeodo: For me, it’s quite disappointing that we’re losing, because of how we’re losing.

Yesterday, for example, we were in a spot to win the game and we didn’t close it out properly. And today it felt like we were just bleeding slowly, dropping waves, and they started taking our towers and stuff. So, for me, it’s quite sad to lose like this.

If we were losing games where we were fighting, and we lost one fight and then the game, that would be fine, but the way we’re losing is quite sad, honestly. But I don’t think it’s affecting me that much. I just want to bounce back and show what we can do, because we all worked up to this, and hopefully we don’t waste it.

[The interview was being done during the Draw Show and TL drew AL while Josedeodo was answering…]

Davide: Luck doesn’t seem to be on your side, because it’s you guys against AL. They are struggling, right? They were supposed to be one of the stronger teams and haven’t really lived up to expectations.

Josedeodo: I think best-of-threes are different from best-of-ones. Drafting in a best-of-one isn’t the same. So hopefully we have good preparation here in the end.

I know with these draws it’s bad to face the good teams early, but in reality, if you want to win the whole thing, you have to face them at some point. So if we lose, we didn’t deserve to make it, and that’s just how it is.

I will say that when we lost to CFO, we lost our chance at an easy run. I’m not saying KOI is an easy team, because they beat BLG, but I think we had everything in our hands to make it easier, and now we’re in a spot where we have to prove that we’re good enough to make it out.

Davide: I saw that Steve (Team Liquid CEO) was here today.

Josedeodo: Having Steve is quite nice. He’s always a very happy guy, so having him here is helpful. I will say right now isn’t the best moment to have him in the best mood, but he’s always trying to cheer us up.

I remember last split we were doing quite badly. He came into the room, gave us a speech, and tried to cheer everyone up, and it gave us some momentum. I think it’s quite nice to have him, even if he’s not in the room every time. He’s there to help us from time to time.

Davide: Although results aren’t coming together,

Josedeodo: It’s nice. I will say that the last time I was at Worlds, I also started quite badly, and then we came back.

Right now we just have to talk to each other, figure out what we want to do next time, and just do our best. People will say, “Oh, it’s good because you’re playing AL.” But they also haven’t shown that they’re at their best, because they lost to TSW, and they have 369 and stuff, you know.

So I would say it’s up to us to come back. Maybe they’re in the same situation as us, mentally speaking, and hopefully we can come back, grab some momentum, and maybe make it back.

Davide: I have one last question for you, regarding the meta. Obviously, in the Bo1s, it was more or less the same picks, so teams just found a comfortable comp.

But today, for example, T1 picked up Caitlyn and Neeko, something a little bit more unconventional to pick (when everything is open).

Josedeodo: I will say game 1 is going to be almost the same. In game 2, you might start seeing some regular junglers, and maybe something more by game three, but I don’t think anything in the jungle will be unexpected. What you’ll see the most is bot lane picks that change.

Either you get pushing lanes, or you have hyper-carry lanes like Kog’Maw and Lulu, that kind of stuff, or Lulu and Yunara, and those come up more. Even if they show up in game one, I think they’re more on the spot in later games. Right now it feels like teams play for Lee Sin or Qiyana in game 1, and then you go from there.

Josedeodo: I think so, or in the bot lane still. Once you’re up against certain teams, it’s quite good to target certain players. But I will say most of the teams at Worlds have huge pools. After Fearless was adopted, it’s kind of normal that everyone plays everything.
