Inter Miami - Looking to finish strong

Here’s a deep dive into Inter Miami’s current run of form. We will explore their latest performances and how they might set up tactically for this crucial final match of the regular season. Towards the end, we will also reveal the probable Inter Miami vs. Charlotte line-ups.

Recent form

Inter Miami have already secured a playoff place, but their second position in the Eastern Conference is not entirely safe. They are only two points ahead of New England Revolution and will want to avoid slipping down the standings before the postseason begins.

Kily Gonzalez’s side have won only once in their last three matches, although that victory came in their most recent outing, a 2-1 win away to New England Revolution. Before that, they drew 2-2 with FC Cincinnati and lost 2-0 to New York Red Bulls.

Lionel Messi remains central to Miami’s attacking threat. The Argentine has registered 39 goal contributions in 2026, while German Berterame and Luis Suarez have also provided important goals.

Inter Miami have a relatively healthy squad, although Tadeo Allende, Ericio Caicedo and Mateo Silvetti are unavailable. The hosts should still have enough attacking quality to cause problems for Charlotte.

Charlotte - Coming off big wins

Now we will turn our attention to Charlotte. We will look at their current form, recent results, and key players for this upcoming fixture.

Recent form

Charlotte FC are fifth in the Eastern Conference and have put themselves in a strong position heading into the postseason. Dean Smith’s side have bounced back from a three-match winless run with consecutive 4-0 victories over CF Montreal and Orlando City.

Those results have restored confidence in their attack, with Idan Toklomati scoring four times in his last five MLS appearances to reach 15 league goals. Pep Biel has also been an important contributor, with 13 goals this season.

However, Charlotte will be without Allan Saint-Maximin, who was sent off against Orlando City. Nimfasha Berchimas, Will Cleary, Archie Goodwin, Aron John and Tim Ream are also sidelined.

David Schnegg and Ashley Westwood are one booking away from suspension, which could be a consideration as the visitors prepare for the playoffs.

Predictions for Inter Miami vs. Charlotte - A high-scoring affair

Inter Miami’s attack is formidable at home, and with Lionel Messi in top form, they will likely dominate early. This offensive pressure makes a halftime lead for the hosts a strong possibility.

Charlotte also brings attacking momentum, having scored freely in their last two matches. Their porous away defence against Miami’s potent attack suggests an open game, possibly with over 1.5 goals before the break.

Given both teams’ attacking form and defensive vulnerabilities, a goal-filled match is anticipated where a winner emerges. Our main prediction is for the game to have over 2.5 goals without ending in a draw.
