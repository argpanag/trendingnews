Philadelphia 76ers vs Boston Celtics: Where to watch the Sixers today and what time the game starts at Wells Fargo

Sixers and Celtics clash for the Eastern Conference throne

There are games that mean more than just a simple mark in the win column, and the one coming up at the Wells Fargo Center is one of them. The Philadelphia 76ers host the Boston Celtics in a clash that, in addition to the historic spice between the two franchises, has the Atlantic Division lead and a large part of the battle for supremacy in the Eastern Conference on the line.

Two teams started the 2026/27 season on the right foot, and only half a game separates them in the standings. The stage is set for a heavyweight battle.

Philadelphia 76ers vs Boston Celtics: What time and where to watch the game

The game will be played this Tuesday, November 10, 2026, with tip-off officially scheduled for 8:00 p.m. Eastern Time (7:00 p.m. Central Time and 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time), at the Wells Fargo Center in Philadelphia, a venue with a capacity of 20,478 fans.

In the United States, the national broadcast of this matchup will be carried by NBC, as well as on NBC Sports local channels for Philadelphia and Boston; in addition, you can stream it on Peacock and Fubo. For international audiences, the NBA maintains its usual offering through NBA League Pass.

How do the Sixers and Celtics come into today's game? Standings and latest results

The image of the standings leaves no room for doubt about the magnitude of this matchup. Philadelphia leads the Atlantic Division with a record of 9 wins and 2 losses (.818), a mark that also leaves it tied at the very top of the entire league in winning percentage.

Boston is right on its heels: 8 wins and 2 losses (.800), just half a game back, in second place in the division.

The stat that should concern the home fans is the defensive one. The 76ers have allowed 1,187 points in their eleven games, a high figure that contrasts with Boston's solidity. The Celtics boast the best point differential in the East, the result of a defense that has conceded just 1,043 points in ten games.

In short: Philadelphia wins with offense and determination; Boston does it by locking things down. That difference in styles is exactly what makes the matchup appealing.

Philadelphia 76ers vs Boston Celtics Lineups: plenty of stars on the court

Few early-season games bring together so much recognizable talent on the court. In Philadelphia’s ranks appears a roster with big-name talent: Joel Embiid commanding the paint, Tyrese Maxey and Anfernee Simons providing dynamite on the perimeter, veteran LeBron James adding experience and stature, and Jaylen Brown reinforcing the wing.

It is a roster built to score in bursts, and the 1,221 points scored — the second-best offense in the East — confirm it.

On the other side, Boston leans on its longtime backbone. Jayson Tatum leads the project as the franchise standard-bearer, with Derrick White and Payton Pritchard handling the ball and punishing from beyond the arc.

The arrival of a shooter and defender of Paul George's caliber adds another layer of danger on the wing, while Mitchell Robinson and the Portuguese Neemias Queta bring muscle and rim protection in the paint.

Tactical chess is played in the paint: Embiid against the defensive front of Robinson and Queta will be one of the silent keys to the game. And on the perimeter, if Maxey and Simons find their rhythm from three, Boston’s defense will have to stretch like rarely before this season.

What is at stake in the Eastern Conference standings

This game is, in effect, an early tiebreaker for the top spot in the Atlantic. A Philadelphia victory would extend its lead over Boston and solidify its position among the teams with the best record in the entire NBA, alongside the Los Angeles Lakers at the top of the league standings.

A Celtics win, on the other hand, would allow them to seize the divisional lead and move ahead of their rival in the overall standings, where they are currently in third place, half a game behind.

Beyond the mathematics of November, these early-season matchups establish hierarchies. In a conference where Detroit, Atlanta, and the Knicks also want to force their way into the conversation, making it clear who is in charge in the Atlantic has a psychological value that transcends the calendar.

Prediction for the Philadelphia 76ers vs Boston Celtics: Who will win today's game?

It is an extremely even matchup, the kind that is usually decided in the final minutes. The home-court advantage and offensive depth tilt the balance slightly toward Philadelphia, but Boston’s defensive solidity —the best in the East by point differential— is a strong argument for stealing a road win.

If the game turns into a scoring race, the 76ers have the arsenal to win it at home; if Boston manages to slow the pace and defend as it has throughout the campaign, the result could go the green side's way by a narrow margin. We’re betting on a nail-biting finish with a slight edge for the home team.
