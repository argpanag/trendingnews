Salesforce Outage Today: Is Salesforce Down Today in the US? Users Report Website, App & Login Issues | Salesforce Downdetector Status

TheTools · 16.09.2026 10:31 · source · permalink

Salesforce Outage Today: Salesforce is down for users in the US, with reports pointing to problems accessing the website, app and login services. The disruption comes as Salesforce hosts its annual Dreamforce event in San Francisco, adding attention to the service issues affecting customers trying to access the platform.

According to the outage data provided, 69% of reported problems are related to the Website, making it the biggest issue by a wide margin. App problems account for 16%, while 15% of reports involve Login.

The reported disruption also comes amid a confirmed Salesforce service incident. The company’s status information says a service disruption began on September 16, with customers unable to access core services.

Salesforce Outage Today: Is Salesforce Down Today

Salesforce is experiencing a reported outage in the US, with website problems accounting for most of the complaints.

The latest reported problem breakdown is:

  • 69%: Website
  • 16%: App
  • 15%: Login

The figures show that the website is the main source of trouble for affected users. People trying to access Salesforce through a browser may find that pages fail to load or that the service becomes unavailable.

The company’s status information also shows a current service disruption affecting its core service, indicating that the problems are not limited to individual users.

Salesforce Outage Today: Downdetector Shows User Reports

The latest outage data shows that website issues account for 69% of reports, followed by app and login problems.

This means customers can experience the outage in different ways. Some may be unable to open Salesforce in their browser, while others could encounter problems when using the mobile app or attempting to sign into their accounts.

Salesforce advises customers to check its Trust Status page for incidents affecting their particular Salesforce instance.

Salesforce Outage Today: What Problems Are Users Reporting?

The biggest problem currently being reported is access to the Salesforce website. With nearly seven out of every 10 reports linked to the website, browser-based access appears to be the main pain point.

App and login problems are also being reported. For businesses that rely on Salesforce for customer records, sales operations and other daily workflows, even a temporary access problem can interrupt normal work.

Salesforce’s status information says that during a service disruption, end users may be unable to access the affected service.

Salesforce Outage Today: Is Salesforce Not Working?

For affected customers, Salesforce is not working normally. The current service disruption can prevent users from accessing core Salesforce services.

The situation is different from the separate browser-freezing issue Salesforce has been investigating since earlier in September. Salesforce said that issue affected some customers using Chrome and Microsoft Edge version 153 and was linked to an external browser environment.

The latest service disruption is therefore a separate and broader access issue.

Salesforce Outage Today: Which Areas Are Affected?

The latest reports indicate that users in the US are experiencing problems with Salesforce.

The disruption may affect customers differently depending on their Salesforce instance and the service they are trying to access. Salesforce’s Trust Status system allows customers to search for their specific instance and see current incidents, service status and maintenance information.

Reports of the wider disruption have also emerged while Salesforce’s Dreamforce conference is taking place in San Francisco.

Salesforce Outage Today: Has Salesforce Confirmed the Issue?

Yes. Salesforce’s status information has confirmed a service disruption affecting its Core Service. Reports on the incident say it began at around 07:50 UTC on September 16 and was ongoing when the reports were published.

The Register reported that Salesforce customers were experiencing severe delays, intermittent errors and an inability to access services during the disruption.

This confirmation means the current reports go beyond isolated customer-side problems.

Salesforce Outage Today: What Should Users Do?

Users who cannot access Salesforce should first check the company’s Trust Status page to determine whether their specific Salesforce instance is affected.

If Salesforce is unavailable because of the current service disruption, repeatedly changing passwords or reinstalling the app is unlikely to solve the problem. Users can instead wait for the company’s engineers to restore the affected services.

For users experiencing browser-specific freezing rather than the broader outage, Salesforce has previously suggested using another supported browser or reverting from Chrome or Edge version 153 where company device policies allow it.

Salesforce Outage Today: When Will Services Restore to Normal?

There is no confirmed restoration time yet for the current Salesforce service disruption.

Salesforce is investigating the issue and providing updates through its Trust Status system. Customers should continue checking their specific instance status because restoration can happen at different times across affected services or instances.

For now, the biggest reported issue is the Salesforce website, which accounts for 69% of user reports, followed by app and login problems.
