Most Valuable Basketball Cards: Top 10 Rankings This September

TheTools · 20.09.2026 21:17 · source · permalink

Basketball trading cards first started as a simple schoolyard hobby. But believe it or not, the most valuable basketball cards today sometimes sell for multi-million dollar price tags - with the right condition and grading meaning everything.

Today, we’ll rank and explore the five most high-profile basketball card sales of all time, and the stories behind each. From autographed, one of a kind Stephen Curry cards to Michael Jordan classics, we’ll review how and why these cards broke records. Card values of course fluctuate over time. But we’ll also share some tips for today’s collectors, too.

Those are some pretty crazy values, eh? Well, here’s a closer look at the stories behind the cards we’ve ranked in our table – including how each came to become so darn desirable, and pricey.

Upper Deck’s Exquisite Collection has long been one of the most sought-after basketball card sets ever produced, thanks to its premium design, limited print runs and the prevalence of on-card autographs and Logoman patches in this particular series.

In August 2025, history was made when a one of a kind Dual Logoman card from the set featuring superstars Michael Jordan and Kobe Bryant side by side, signed by each, and graded PSA 8, sold for an astonishing $12.932 million.

To this very day, the card remains not just the most expensive basketball card ever sold, but the most expensive sports card ever sold. What sent its value skyrocketing? Well, records indicate it’s the only card in existence to pair Jordan and Bryant together with both dual game-worn Logoman patches and official on-card autographs.

Unsurprisingly, the second card to feature on our ranking of the most expensive basketball cards ever purchased is yet another from Upper Deck’s Exquisite Collection. This time, the much-coveted card pairs Jordan with Cleveland Cavaliers legend LeBron James, joining together the NBA’s greatest classic icon with the modern-day player that many fans consider to be his unofficial successor.

Featuring match-worn Logoman patches from both players and autographs from each to boot, this much hyped-up card sold for a shocking $10 million in September 2025, with collectors drawn to its one of a kind status, and symbolic passing of the torch theme.

Panini’s 2009/10 National Treasures series is another of the most desirable basketball trading card sets in the biz. And in July 2021, history was made when a 51% stake in a PSA 8-graded, autographed Stephen Curry card from the collection was purchased for a record-breaking $5.9 million. That’s right, folks – the investment fund doing the purchasing here didn’t even buy out the entire card.

The one of a kind card in question featured Curry’s rookie-year photograph, as well as his official signature graded as Gem Mint 10, the highest grade possible, by PSA. It first rose to prominence around 2015, after Curry led the Golden State Warriors to their first NBA title in 40 years. Around that time, there was a huge buzz around Curry’s approach to three-point shots – which some say redefined basketball as we know it.

Fun fact: before this card, multi-million dollar basketball card sales were generally more commonly associated with vintage cards depicting legends of bygone eras. Not anymore, eh?

Any true collector knows that LeBron James’ rookie cards are among the best basketball trading cards around these days. In recent years, they’ve gained insane popularity and prestige as LeBron’s career and rep have evolved from promising teenager to one of the NBA’s all-time greats.

With LeBron’s legacy truly set in stone by the early 2020s, 2021 was the peak of much of the LeBron rookie card buzz. And the absolute pinnacle was the sale of this autographed, 2003/04 edition Upper Deck Exquisite Collection LeBron card complete with Rookie Patch, and a Gem Mint 10 BGS autograph grade, in October of that year.

At the time, the $5.2 million sale set a new record as the most expensive LeBron James card ever sold – bettered only by the Michael Jordan collab card we ranked as number two above.

Luka Dončić cards rose dramatically in both popularity and value after the Slovenian superstar won Rookie of the Year in 2019 and quickly established himself as one of the NBA’s brightest young talents.

With serious card traders and investors hotly anticipating Dončić’s name on future all-time great lists, cards such as this rare 2018/19 Panini National Treasures Luka Dončić Rookie Patch Autograph Logoman quickly became dream collectibles. This culminated in a 2021 private sale of this true one of a kind card, featuring a BGS 9.2 grade and a BGS 10 autograph grade, and complete with game-worn Logoman patch – which went for a phenomenal $4.6 million. Wowzers.
