Tottenham Targets Endrick as De Zerbi Seeks Striker

TheTools · 18.10.2026 19:50 · source · permalink

Tottenham Hotspur is assessing five striker targets ahead of the January transfer window as manager Roberto De Zerbi seeks to improve the team's attacking output. According to reports published by TEAMtalk on October 15 and 18, the club is considering Real Madrid forward Endrick alongside Vangelis Pavlidis, Roberto Fernández, Igor Matanović and Jean-Philippe Mateta.

Tottenham has collected just three points from six Premier League matches, with no victories and only three goals scored, according to TEAMtalk. The poor start has increased the focus on strengthening the forward line despite substantial summer investment.

TEAMtalk reported on October 18 that De Zerbi wants additional attacking options and has requested changes to the club's sporting leadership. Former Inter Milan executive Piero Ausilio has emerged as a potential candidate for the sporting director position, although his appointment has not been confirmed.

Endrick remains a potential target, but Tottenham faces resistance from Real Madrid manager Jose Mourinho. The Brazilian forward has made two appearances totaling 35 minutes this season, according to Roundtable.io, and scored against Roma in a 4-3 Champions League victory.

Mourinho has publicly emphasized the 20-year-old's importance to Real Madrid, making a January departure uncertain. TEAMtalk reported that Tottenham could explore a loan if the Spanish club becomes willing to consider an arrangement, while Football Transfer Tavern also highlighted Mourinho's strong support for the forward.

Addressing interest from other clubs, Mourinho said:

"Forget it, forget it. Forget the clubs that are interested. Better to sit down and wait; otherwise, you will get tired.

He is a player I love, he has incredible potential. He is very important to us." Mourinho's comments underline Real Madrid's reluctance to lose the Brazilian during the season.

Tottenham is also monitoring Benfica striker Vangelis Pavlidis, who has scored 14 goals this season, according to Roundtable.io and TEAMtalk. The reports differ on the number of appearances behind that tally, listing 14 and 13 respectively.

Benfica has set a €100 million release clause for the Greece international, equivalent to approximately £84.5 million, according to TEAMtalk. The club's reluctance to sell midway through the campaign could make a January transfer difficult, with a move in summer 2027 presented as a more realistic possibility.

Espanyol's Roberto Fernández is another candidate. Roundtable.io reported that the 24-year-old had scored six goals in eight La Liga appearances, while TEAMtalk said Tottenham's recruitment staff had increased their assessment of the Spaniard and viewed him as a potential option for 2027.

Freiburg forward Igor Matanović has also attracted interest after scoring three goals and providing one assist in his first five Bundesliga matches, according to Roundtable.io. However, TEAMtalk reported that securing his departure during the winter window could prove difficult.

Crystal Palace striker Jean-Philippe Mateta represents another possible route. TEAMtalk described the French forward as a more accessible Premier League option, reporting that both the player and his club were open to exploring an exit.

Tottenham's existing forwards could still influence the club's decision. Dominic Solanke scored a late equalizer against Manchester United, according to Roundtable.io, while summer signing Omar Marmoush has yet to deliver consistent goals during the team's winless league start.

Mathys Tel is also under consideration internally after scoring five goals in four appearances for France Under-21s during the international break, including a hat trick against Switzerland, according to Roundtable.io. His form could strengthen the case for giving Tottenham's current attacking options more time before committing to another signing.
