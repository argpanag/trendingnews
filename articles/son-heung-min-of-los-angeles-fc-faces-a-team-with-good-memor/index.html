Son Heung-min of Los Angeles FC faces a team with good memories again.

Son Heung-min was expected to start the home game against LA Galaxy at BMO Stadium in Los Angeles, California on the 26th (Korea time).

He was in the second line of attack in the 4-2-3-1 formation. Along with Leo Walta and Armindo Siev, they will help forward striker Denny Buanga to score a goal.

Son Heung-min has a good memory against the Galaxy. He contributed to a 3-0 complete victory by scoring one goal in an away game against them, the first game since North American World Cup in July.

He will play for his eighth goal of the season and his team's victory. LAFC will confirm its advance to the playoffs if it draws more than a draw in the match.

In LAFC, Hugo Lloris will defend the goal, Yevhen Severko, Aaron Long, Ryan Porteous and Sergi Palencia will be in the four-back line, while Igor Jesus and Mark Delgado will be in the midfield.

In response, the Galaxy will have JT Marcinkowski defending the goal, with Harbour Miller, Justin Haak, Emiro Garces and John Nelson forming a four-back line, while Pablo Luiz, Sergi Roberto and Eric Tommy will be in the midfield, and Robert Taylor, Joao Klaus and Joseph Paintroom will be in the front.

[Los Angeles (USA) = KIM JAE HO, MK Sports Correspondent]
