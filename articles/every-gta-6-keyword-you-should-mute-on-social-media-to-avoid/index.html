Every GTA 6 Keyword You Should Mute on Social Media To Avoid Spoilers

TheTools · 08.11.2026 20:05 · source · permalink

Grand Theft Auto 6 is easily one of the most anticipated games of all time, so avoiding spoilers for it is going to be extremely difficult. Social media has made dodging these spoilers a challenge, but there are certain key words that would absolutely ruin any surprise Rockstar Games has cooked up. Online algorithms recommend based on previous searches and viewing habits, so avoiding certain phrases and words can help prevent spoilers. Whether it’s a leaked cutscene, a character’s fate, or the game’s ending, one careless scroll could reveal something players would rather discover themselves. To avoid this, you’ll need to take some precautions.

Videos by ComicBook.com

Avoiding game websites, Reddit discussions, YouTube thumbnails, and more is the best way to prevent GTA 6 spoilers from coming across your dash. GTA 6 presents an especially difficult challenge because its popularity extends far beyond your average gaming communities. Even people who rarely discuss video games will likely encounter posts about how huge this game is. Players hoping to experience Jason and Lucia’s story without knowing what happens should start preparing their social media accounts now, rather than waiting until spoilers begin circulating online. This is easier said than done, but it can be done.

Important Grand Theft Auto 6 Keywords to Mute and Other Ways to Avoid Spoilers

The easiest way to avoid GTA 6 story spoilers is to mute keywords associated with the game across social media platforms. On X, users can mute specific words, phrases, and hashtags, preventing matching posts from appearing in supported areas of their feeds. However, simply blocking the game’s title won’t catch everything, particularly when users deliberately avoid mentioning it.

Muting these terms can significantly reduce the chance of encountering spoilers for GTA 6. Keep in mind that no filter can guarantee this, and you should also do the same for variants using GTA 6 instead of GTA VI to cover your bases. This will help with social media platforms that filter based on keywords and hashtags.

Platforms like YouTube provide a different challenge because of thumbnails, specifically creators who use spoilery words and images. Avoid searching for GTA 6 gameplay, select “Not interested” or “Don’t recommend channel” on potentially revealing videos, and avoid gaming channels. The same goes for Reddit, where you should unsubscribe from communities surrounding GTA 6.

The only true way to avoid GTA 6 spoilers is to stay offline altogether, or significantly reduce how online you are and where you go. This is very difficult in our day and age, but will most likely prevent major spoilers. GTA 6 is set to be one of the biggest launches in gaming history, and creators will likely be competing heavily for views, which likely includes spoiling the game.
