Notification Issued on Changes to Petroleum Prices

TheTools · 20.11.2026 17:51 · source · permalink

Petrol has been reduced by Rs1 per litre, while diesel has increased by Rs2 per litre.

ISLAMABAD, (Pakistan Point News - 20th Nov, 2026) The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA) has announced changes to petroleum prices. According to a notification issued by the Petroleum Division, petrol has been reduced by Rs1 per litre, while diesel has been increased by Rs2 per litre.

The new price of petrol has been set at Rs322 per litre. The price of high-speed diesel has been kept unchanged at Rs357 per litre.

The new petroleum prices will take effect from midnight tonight and remain in force until November 23.

According to a media report, the Karachi Port Trust warmly welcomed Türkiye’s advanced seismic research vessel, Oruc Reis.

The vessel arrived at Karachi Port on November 18, 2026, and will begin a research mission to conduct offshore seismic surveys in Pakistan’s maritime territory and search for hidden oil and gas reserves.

Equipped with advanced seismic technology and research instruments, the vessel’s arrival is being described as an important step towards strengthening maritime cooperation between Pakistan and Türkiye.

The research mission is expected to help identify potential oil and gas reserves in Pakistan’s maritime areas and explore new energy resources.

According to the Karachi Port Trust, the vessel’s arrival is an important milestone for promoting international maritime cooperation and Pakistan’s energy future.
