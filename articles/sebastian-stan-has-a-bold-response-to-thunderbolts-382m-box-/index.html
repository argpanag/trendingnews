Sebastian Stan Has a Bold Response to Thunderbolts’ $382M Box Office Run - AOL

TheTools · 27.11.2026 17:58 · source · permalink

Sebastian Stan considers Thunderbolts* a success despite its modest box office performance. Released in 2025, the movie grossed $382 million worldwide. While the figure fell short of the billion-dollar grosses achieved by some Marvel releases, Stan believes the film was successful.

Stan, known for his portrayal of Bucky Barnes in the MCU, noted that the feature’s financial numbers shouldn’t overshadow the effort and care the creative team put into it. Appearing in an interview with Matt Neglia of Next Best Picture, he said, “I think it’s just when you’ve had a studio, and you’ve had Marvel, and it’s been so many years where they’ve just been knocking it out of the park.”

He added, “A $350 million global, I think it was, or something like that number, it just suddenly doesn’t seem that much, but it’s insane how much money that is, you know. I don’t know. I still felt like for us it felt like a success.”

The deeper meaning behind Thunderbolts’ approach to mental health and depression

Florence Pugh’s character, Yelena Belova, constantly tries to cope with grief, emptiness, and detachment by staying busy. Yelena tries to bottle up her pain and emotions instead of facing it. Bob Reynolds’ dark alter ego, The Void, acts as a literal manifestation of depression, shame, and unaddressed trauma. The dynamic between Sentry and his fragile internal state constantly reflects emotional mood swings and psychological issues. Bucky Barnes, Stan’s character, also reflects on his past as he constantly struggles with an identity crisis.

During his candid conversation with Matt Neglia, Stan praised the film for thoughtfully portraying real-life problems. The MCU actor stated, “It [Thunderbolts*] had more nuance to it, in terms of, I guess, than some of the recent ones.” He further added, “I don’t know, from my point of view at least, like dealing with real issues like mental health and depression and so on. And there’s just a good group of people.”

Thunderbolts* arrived in theaters on May 2, 2025.
