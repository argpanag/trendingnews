Former women's tennis star Carolina Wozniacki traded in her tennis racket for a microphone this Halloween.

Wozniacki, the former No. 1 player on the WTA Tour, had an incredible career that included her winning the Australian Open in a three-set thriller over Simona Halep. After retiring from professional tennis, she became a broadcaster for ESPN. Fans enjoyed her analysis during the Wimbledon Championships and US Open.

Even though Wozniacki won't be on commentary until the Australian Open in January, she went viral this week for sharing her Halloween costume with the rest of the world.

Wozniacki and her husband, former NBA star David Lee, decided to dress up as Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce. Their costumes weren't exactly unique, but they certainly received plenty of support for committing to the bit.

"Crushed it!!! Mic drop! Complete touchdown," one person commented.

"You aced it! Epic," another person wrote.

"Amazing costume and that DRESS," a fan said.

"Outstanding!!! Love this," an Instagram user replied.

Wozniacki is no stranger to taking bold swings.

In 2015, Wozniacki made her Sports Illustrated Swimsuit debut. Her rookie photoshoot was such a success that she participated in the "Body Paint" series one year later.

"I just couldn't say no," Wozniacki said. "It was an unbelievable opportunity for me and something I've never done before. I feel like a piece of art walking around right now. It's kind of a surreal experience." 

Who knows? Maybe Wozniacki will return for the 2027 issue of SI Swimsuit.
