Philadelphia opens the curtain at home with the weight of the favorite

The 2026-27 NBA season kicks off with a high-voltage matchup at the Wells Fargo Center. The Philadelphia 76ers host the Cleveland Cavaliers in a clash featuring veteran stars, young prospects, and the pressure of starting strong.

With both teams still without significant records in the overall standings, every possession on Wednesday night is worth double in terms of confidence and narrative.

Philadelphia 76ers vs Cleveland Cavaliers: Schedule, venue and where to watch live

The game between Philadelphia and Cleveland will be played at the Wells Fargo Center in Philadelphia, an arena with a capacity of 20,478 fans. Tip-off is scheduled for Thursday, October 22, 2026, at 7:00 pm ET (6:00 pm CT and 4:00 pm PT).

The stars of the Philadelphia 76ers

The Sixers' roster is an interesting mix. At point guard is Tyrese Maxey, the 25-year-old shooting guard expected to carry the offensive load and set the team's pace. Alongside him are highly experienced veterans like LeBron James and Jaylen Brown, two forwards capable of turning the tide at any moment of the game.

The team's star player remains Joel Embiid, the 32-year-old center whose presence completely dictates the opposing defense and opens up space for shooters. The shooting guard corps is rounded out by names like Anfernee Simons and the experienced Kentavious Caldwell-Pope, a defensive specialist and outside shooter.

Youth is also present with VJ Edgecombe, a Bahamian shooting guard of just 21 years old, one of the franchise's future bets.

The weapons of the Cleveland Cavaliers

The Cavaliers respond with a commanding backcourt. Donovan Mitchell, the 30-year-old shooting guard, is the team's scoring engine and one of the most feared players in clutch situations. He's joined by James Harden, the 37-year-old veteran point guard whose court vision and ball-handling skills continue to be a nightmare for any defense.

Cleveland's heart, however, beats in its frontcourt. Evan Mobley, a 25-year-old power forward, is a prodigy of defensive versatility, and alongside center Jarrett Allen, 28, he forms an interior wall that protects the rim and dominates the boards.

On the flanks, there are rotation pieces such as the Croatian Mario Hezonja, 31, the forward Peyton Watson, 24, and the shooter Sam Merrill, 30, who provides firepower from the perimeter.
