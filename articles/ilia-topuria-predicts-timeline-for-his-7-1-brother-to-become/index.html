Ilia Topuria predicts timeline for his 7-1 brother to become UFC bantamweight champion

TheTools · 22.11.2026 10:55 · source · permalink

Ilia Topuria is expecting big things from his brother inside the Octagon.

The younger Topuria sibling, Aleksandre, will return to action on Saturday’s UFC Qatar card.

Before Jiri Prochazka battles Navajo Stirling in the main event, the bantamweight Topuria will have his first fight in a year on the undercard at ABHA Arena.

Beyond a victory over Santiago Luna in Doha, Ilia is expecting his brother to eventually have UFC gold wrapped around his waist.

Islam Makhachev, Ilia Topuria, and Alex Pereira have all spoken about becoming triple champs — but who are you backing to make history as the very first? 👑

Ilia Topuria predicts brother Aleksandre will be UFC champion in 2028 or 2029

Aleksandre Topuria has gone undefeated through two fights under the UFC banner so far.

After Ilia’s brother beat Alexander Volkanovski’s teammate, Colby Thicknesse, the bantamweight prospect defeated the highly regarded Bekzat Almakhan at the November 2025 UFC Qatar event.

The 30-year-old will now hope to make it back-to-back wins in the Middle Eastern nation and begin a period of increased activity as he looks to rise through the ranks at 135 pounds.

While the jury is still out among fans when it comes to Aleksandre’s championship potential, Ilia is firmly expecting his brother to sit on the throne by early 2029.

“I’ve trained with a lot of people, but I’ve never trained with anyone as technical as my brother or anyone with the same level of knowledge and understanding of the sport,” Ilia said while speaking to La Gazzetta Georgia.

“Maybe it’s because we are brothers and he knows me so well. He knows all my strengths and my weaknesses, and of course he used that knowledge during our training sessions and sparring.

“I have absolutely no doubt he’ll become a world champion…in 2028 or definitely by the beginning of 2029,” he added.

Much of the discussion surrounding Aleksandre this year has centered around his work on Ilia’s title fight loss to Justin Gaethje at the White House.

The bantamweight fighter cornered his brother in Washington, DC, and made the decision to end the fight before the start of round five. ‘El Matador’ believes that decision saved his UFC career.

It is now Ilia’s turn to support Aleksandre inside the cage, as the rising prospect looks to move to 8-1 against an opponent who has also only tasted defeat once in professional MMA.
