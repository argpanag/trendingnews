Jodie Foster, Hailee Steinfeld, Rashida Jones talk 'Hexed,' motherhood and more

TheTools · 20.11.2026 19:51 · source · permalink
  • Culture

Actresses Jodie Foster, Hailee Steinfeld and Rashida Jones are opening up about motherhood, family relationships and finding their power through their roles in Disney's new animated movie "Hexed."

"Hexed" follows Billie Doe, voiced by Steinfeld, and her mother, Alice, voiced by Jones, who tries to protect her daughter by keeping her magical abilities a secret. Their lives take an unexpected turn when they enter a magical world.

Foster, who voices the villainous Queen Celeste, said the role fulfilled a longtime dream of playing a Disney villain.

"Something I've always wanted to do and never got to do," the two-time Oscar winner told ABC News' Chris Connelly in an interview that aired Wednesday on "Good Morning America."

Foster recalled admiring Disney villains such as Cruella de Vil and Ursula growing up, explaining that she was drawn to them "because they were powerful."

"I get to be the villain so that we can have an amazing story about a mother and a daughter between these two," she said, referring to Jones and Steinfeld.

For Jones, the movie's emotional themes struck a personal chord.

"This movie is weirdly very deep," Jones said, explaining that the story explores "generational trauma, and mistakes" as well as forgiveness and learning to see parents and children beyond their family roles.

"There is this message of empathy, really," she said.

Steinfeld, meanwhile, revealed that she was still recording lines for the film toward the end of her pregnancy. The actress and her husband, NFL star Josh Allen, announced in April that they had welcomed their first child, a daughter.

Steinfeld said becoming a mother brought new meaning to the film's message.

"I think my daughter has served as the perfect reminder of that," she said.

Foster said the three actresses connected through their shared experiences as parents.

"What is kind of cool about the three of us is that we are all parents, and we're all thinking a lot about our role as parents and what we can give to our children, but we're at different times," she said.

For Jones, the film also reflects her own journey toward self-confidence.

"I feel like I'm trying to figure out how to step into my power," she said, reflecting on turning 50.

Despite playing rebellious characters, Foster joked that the three actresses have something else in common.

"The three of us are -- we're actually all pretty good girls," she said. "And it's interesting that we have played much more rebellious people than we actually are."

"Hexed" opens in U.S. theaters on Nov. 25.
