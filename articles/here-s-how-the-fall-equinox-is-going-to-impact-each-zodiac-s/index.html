Here’s How the Fall Equinox Is Going to Impact Each Zodiac Sign

TheTools · 22.09.2026 18:05 · source · permalink

Hello, autumn!

It’s the first day of fall, y’all! The Fall Equinox, when day and night are exactly equal, marks the end of summer and the beginning of autumn. This will officially happen tonight at 8:05 p.m. ET. As we move forward in time, the nights will keep getting longer until the winter solstice on December 21.

Astrologically, the Fall Equinox marks the start of Libra season, a time when balance becomes more important. It's an ideal time to nurture a romance or another close relationship, like a best friend. This day also offers us a cosmic check-in: Look back at the metaphorical seeds you planted around the spring equinox in March and see how they've grown. This is a time to recognize the transformational nature of the seasons; one phase is ending, and another one is beginning.

Aries

Romance is in the air, Aries. If you're partnered, try to spend some one-on-one time with your S.O. tonight. A date night would be ideal, but even just a cute cuddle on the couch works, too. The idea is to pour energy into your relationship. If you're single, you can recognize all the forms of non-romantic love in your life, like your best friend. And if you want to try manifesting romantic love into your life, this is a good time to try it.

Taurus

Make your schedule work for you, Taurus. With the day and night in perfect balance, it's time to get out your calendar and start thinking ahead. If you have any big goals you want to accomplish over the next six months, break them down into smaller steps and schedule them. If your day-to-day routine is feeling chaotic—maybe you're not getting enough sleep or are always late to work—now's the time to make some tweaks. Right now, small changes can have big effects.

Gemini

Make art, Gemini. The Fall Equinox brings you an influx of creative energy that you can channel into your artistic medium of choice, from oil painting to writing poetry to making TikTok fancams. If there's a bigger creative project you've been thinking about starting, like writing a novel or making a short film, this is the ideal time to get started. The stars are on your side.

Cancer

Embrace nostalgia, Cancer. You're the sign most closely associated with home, and the fall equinox also lights up the section of your chart that's also associated with home. Whatever "home" means to you—the literal house you lived in as a kid, the family and friends who nurtured you, or your childhood hobbies and interests—spend today revisiting them. This could mean calling a family member to catch up, looking at old photos, or rewatching your favorite movie.

Leo

Use your voice, Leo. You have no problem stepping into the spotlight, but the fall equinox gives your words extra power. Whatever you choose to say today, people will hear you. So choose your words carefully. If you've been wanting to have a tough convo with a loved one, bring up the issue so you can work it out. And if there's something you want to advocate for in your community, now's the time to speak out.

Virgo

Follow the money, Virgo. The Fall Equinox lights up the part of your chart associated with your finances. If you've been having cashflow problems, take a look at your spending patterns and make some adjustments. If you've been wanting to ask for a promotion or apply for a new role, you've got some extra cosmic energy behind you right now.

Libra

It's all about you, Libra. The Fall Equinox marks the start of your season, the best time of year. Today, and for the next month, you're feeling confident, powerful, and more like yourself. Treat today like a mini New Year's, just for Libras. Make some resolutions and write down your goals for the year ahead. This is your year.

Scorpio

Say goodbye, Scorpio. The fall equinox marks the end of summer, and with the Sun in your chart's zone of endings, you're likely saying goodbye to something else, too. Review your life and identify something or someone you want to leave in the past, then let it fall out of your life like so many autumn leaves.

Sagittarius

Call your friends, Sagittarius. In fact, if you can make it work with your schedule, try to schedule a happy hour hangout or even a Fall Equinox party. Today draws you closer to your friends and wider social circle, reminding you just how important they are. Make time for them today and throughout the month ahead. Even a "happy fall equinox!" text'll go a long way.

Capricorn

Work hard, Capricorn. You're one of the most ambitious and hardest-working signs of the zodiac, and your efforts won't go unnoticed right now. The Fall Equinox lights up the area of your birth chart associated with your career, so let your boss know you're looking for a promotion, spruce up your resume, apply for a new role, or start that side gig you've been talking about.

Aquarius

Go explore, Aquarius. The Fall Equinox lights up the area of your birth chart associated with travel and learning. If you can call off work and take a quick day trip to a nearby town, now's a great time to do so, but the wanderlust vibes will continue all month. If you're stuck at your 9 to 5, listen to a podcast, watch a documentary, or do a Wikipedia deep dive instead. Whatever you do, learn something new.

Pisces

The seasons aren't the only thing changing, Pisces. You're changing, too. The Fall Equinox occurs in the area of your chart linked to transformation, and it signals that today—and the upcoming month—is a pivotal moment in your story of growth. Think about the version of yourself you want to become. By mid-October, you'll be saying, "The old Pisces can't come to the phone right now."
