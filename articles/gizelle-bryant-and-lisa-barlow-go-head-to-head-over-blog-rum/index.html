Gizelle Bryant and Lisa Barlow Go Head-to-Head Over "Blog" Rumors | Bravo

TheTools · 14.09.2026 02:01 · source · permalink

On RHUGT: Roaring 20th, Gizelle Bryant called out rumors that Lisa Barlow leaked stories about her castmates.

Key Takeaways

  • Gizelle Bryant confronted Lisa Barlow on RHUGT: Roaring 20th over allegations that she leaks cast information to blogs, warning her to stop if the rumors are true.
  • Lisa Barlow strongly denied the accusations, insisting she speaks publicly for herself and does not hide behind anonymous leaks or third parties.
  • Other cast members agreed that leaking stories would be unacceptable, but several, including Kyle Richards and Teresa Giudice, questioned whether the accusations against Lisa were credible.

Gizelle Bryant called out Lisa Barlow over rumors she had been leaking information to blogs during The Real Housewives Ultimate Girls Trip: Roaring 20th's Miami stop. And, naturally, Lisa had a lot to say on the matter as well. 

How to Watch

The Real Housewives Ultimate Girls Trip: Roaring 20th airs on Sundays at 9 p.m. EST. Episodes stream next day on Peacock.

As seen on RHUGT Episode 6, Gizelle had called out Lisa for not taking accountability for their late departure in Atlanta just before the leaking accusation resurfaced. ICYMI: Some of The Real Housewives of Salt Lake City Season 6 cast had previously claimed to have proof that Lisa was "the source of all the dirt" that had come out about them in the press over the years. Though Lisa denied the accusation, Heather Gay and Angie Katsanevas maintained their position while opening up to Gizelle in Atlanta on RHUGT: Roaring 20th.

"An overwhelming, lingering thought that they had was that you talk to the blogs," Gizelle explained to Lisa during a lunch with their RHUGT castmates. As Lisa interjected to defend herself, Gizelle cut her off and continued, "I know that you guys are getting ready to start filming again. And I'm saying to you, if you are doing it, you need to stop."

Trending Shorts

Clarifying that she was not saying the rumors were true, Gizelle added, "If anything is coming out about this group, I'm only gonna have to look at you."

Lisa Barlow and Gizelle Bryant during The Real Housewives Ultimate Girls Trip Season 5 Episode 6. 

How did Lisa Barlow respond to Gizelle Bryant's warning on RHUGT?

Just as she'd done on RHOSLC, Lisa vehemently denied leaking information about her castmates to blogs, while also questioning Gizelle's motives for putting her on blast. "There's nothing that comes from me on my show," Lisa told Gizelle. "On God, strike me dead right now, if I do."

A bit later, Lisa reiterated, "Gizelle, I'm gonna say it so plainly. If I have something to say, I say it with this mouth or these hands. I don't need to go through anybody else."

When The Real Housewives of Potomac cast member asked Lisa to elaborate on the "hands" part of her denial, she added, "I'll tweet it, I'll message it myself. I don't hide behind things. I don't live my life in the dark. I live my life in the light. I'm not worried."

In a confessional, Lisa also said, "Gizelle and I started this trip having the time of our lives. Like, we hit it off so easily. We could talk about anything. I don't know how you go from that to being like, 'You're a leaker to the blogs.' Like, because I was late getting on a van, like, all of a sudden, my character is s--t? Make this make sense."

How did the RHUGT: Roaring 20th cast react to Gizelle Bryant and Lisa Barlow's drama?

Though the RHUGT: Roaring 20th cast agreed that leaking information was unacceptable, some of the ladies were skeptical of how true the accusations against Lisa actually were.

The cast in The Real Housewives Ultimate Girls Trip: Roaring 20th Episode 4.

Kyle Richards, for one, wanted to know if there had been any "accurate information leaked" to the press about the RHOSLC ladies. "Or is it just lies?" she asked. "Just because people say something, doesn't make it true."

As The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills OG added in a producer interview, "I've never heard this rumor, but that is a big no-no. I mean, you do not do that. So, if that is true, I don't blame Gizelle for calling her out. But if it's not true, that's a pretty harsh accusation."

In a separate confessional, Lisa's RHUGT bestie Teresa Giudice commented, "I mean, I don't think Lisa has any reason to do that. I don't think she would ever do that."

Back at the table, The Real Housewives of New Jersey cast member weighed in more broadly on Lisa's drama with her RHOSLC castmates that sparked in Atlanta. "Heather was, like, so out of line," Teresa said. "And Lisa, she had a feeling she was going to get attacked, and she surely did. And I told her, 'I got your back.'"
