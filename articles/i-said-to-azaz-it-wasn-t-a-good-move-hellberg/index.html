Hellberg: Defeat at Saints 'tough to take'

  • Published
    27 November 2026

Middlesbrough boss Kim Hellberg spoke BBC Radio Tees following their 2-0 defeat at Southampton.

"It's a tough one to take when you [produce] that type of performance and create so many chances, but it also gives us a lot of energy and fuel to work harder," he said. "That's what the players and I love - a challenge in terms of getting disappointed.

"It's always a thing that you have as a footballer and a coach to try to be a role model, be better. I know it's sometimes tough with emotions sometimes. I said to [Azaz that] it wasn't a good move.

"I know players, whatever they are, are young people and sometimes get their emotions into it, which you sometimes have to have respect for. But you can handle it and should handle it in a better way."
