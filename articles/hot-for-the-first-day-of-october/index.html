Hot for the first day of October

TheTools · 01.10.2026 11:48 · source · permalink

BOWLING GREEN, Ky. (WBKO) - You can expect another hot day with highs in the low 90s for the afternoon. Once we get into the heat of the day, we’ll see a moderate breeze with wind gusts from the south.

Clouds will start rolling in this evening as a large, moist air mass heads toward the region. Friday will feature widespread rain showers and occasional thunderstorms. Cloudy conditions and residual moisture will stay in the forecast for Saturday and Sunday morning.

Throughout the weekend, the moisture will slowly be pushed away as a cold front passes through. This will bring clear skies and cool temperatures for next week.
