Scene Review: Scene Stealer

TheTools · 25.11.2026 12:20 · source · permalink

Director: Jithu Madhavan | Cast: Suriya, Naslen, Nazriya Nazim | Tamil

When you combine a Malayalam filmmaker’s edginess with a Tamil superstar’s urge to experiment, you get a caper like Scene where Suriya plays DSP Inbaraj, a blind police officer on the trail of a serial killer, Easwaran, played by Naslen with a nutty nastiness. Inbaraj is irritable, brilliant, with finely attuned senses of smell and hearing. The cat-and-mouse game is delightful, with the candy-loving Easwaran usually escaping by the skin of his teeth. Inbaraj hires an assistant, Maya (Nazriya Nazim), an epileptic, and sets up his home as the ultimate fortress. An assortment of quirky characters around Inbaraj help him solve the crime: his criminologist mother who likes to drink, his forensic surgeon father who suffers from forgetfulness, and his dimwit replacement. They are all misfits and rejects, yet sharp and quick thinking. There are several cameos which add to the drama, from Jyothika to Tovino Thomas. It is witty, surprising, and charming, which is odd given the film is about a serial killer. It closes an outstanding year for Suriya, who has proved with Karuppu, Vishwanath and Sons, and now Scene that he is the most exciting actor working in the country today.
