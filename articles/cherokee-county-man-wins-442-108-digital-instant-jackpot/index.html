Cherokee County man wins $442,108 Digital Instant jackpot

TheTools · 20.11.2026 11:04 · source · permalink

Emeterio Garcia of Marble tried his luck on a Digital Instant game on Sunday and won a $442,108 Digital Instant jackpot.

Garcia played for $20 and won the top level ‘Epic jackpot’ in the Bison Bonanza game. The Digital Instant game features a progressive jackpot that can be won any time. The odds of that win are 1 in 1.5 million.

He claimed his prize Wednesday at lottery headquarters and, after required federal and state tax withholdings, took home $318,371.

In the Bison Bonanza digital instant game, a player can play from 50 cents to $30. After Garcia won the jackpot, it restarted at $50,000 and has grown to over $71,000 as of Wednesday afternoon.

Digital Instant games are played exclusively online on the lottery’s website or on the NC Lottery Official Mobile App. Ninety different games are available to play online now.

Cherokee County received a $15 million grant from lottery funds to help build a new school. For details on how lottery funds have made a difference in all of North Carolina’s 100 counties, visit www.nclottery.com and click on the “Impact” section of the lottery’s website.
