Agriculture Department Floats Rule to Reduce Food Stamp Theft

TheTools · 22.10.2026 15:36 · source · permalink

Stolen food stamp benefits became the focus of the Trump administration’s latest move against government fraud, as Agriculture Department officials on Thursday proposed a rule to transition states toward more secure methods of distributing funds low-income Americans use to buy groceries.

Within two years of a finalized rule, states would have to adopt payment cards with chip technology that are less susceptible to theft than cards with magnetic strips used by most Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program recipients to pay for groceries, according to the proposal.
