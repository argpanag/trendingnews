Here Are The Nominees For The Game Awards 2026

TheTools · 17.11.2026 21:41 · source · permalink

Geoff Keighley has revealed the nominees for The Game Awards. As you might expect, Grand Theft Auto VI appears on the list just a couple of days before players will get to experience it themselves, but there are plenty of other worthy contenders, highlighting the overall strong year of releases.
