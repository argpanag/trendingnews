Miriam Margolyes joins new James Bond cast in 'gender-swapped' role

TheTools · 23.09.2026 19:40 · source · permalink

Miriam Margolyes is making her James Bond debut in a gender-swapped role, it’s been confirmed. 

The Harry Potter actor, 85, joins the cast of Young Bond: SilverFin as Cleek MacSawney, described as ‘a violent henchperson on the Scottish estate of villain Lord Hellebore’. 

She joins other new additions Harry Marcus and Christopher Nathan in the audio drama based on the first novel in Charlie Higson’s Young Bond book series, starring The Crown’s Isaac Rouse as the man himself, 007. 

In Higson’s novel, MacSawney is male but will be switched up especially for Margolyes.

The controversial actor and author – who recently came under fire for using a racist term on This Morning – enthused: ‘Cleek is a violent, probably insane semi-criminal who works for Lord Hellebore on his estate in Scotland. 

‘You wouldn’t want to meet her on a dark night, that’s for sure. I do enjoy playing nasty people!’

Writer and director Barnaby Edwards added: ‘I’ve been fortunate enough to have Miriam in my life for two decades now. 

‘She’s an extraordinary human being and a phenomenal actress. When I began adapting SilverFin, I was determined to get more female characters into it. 

‘Thankfully, IFPL and Charlie Higson gave their blessing to gender-swapping the role of Cleek.’

The announcement comes after Higson wading in on who should replace Daniel Craig as Bond in the movie franchise. 

The author previously admitted he felt Slow Horses star Jack Lowden was an ‘unconvincing choice’ to play 007 despite being the bookie’s favourite. 

He’s now declared that whoever Amazon Studios MGM casts as its first 007 since buying the franchise will be heavily criticised. 

‘Whatever the answer, everyone will be very angry when they find out,’ he told The Telegraph. 

Steven Knight, best known for creating Peaky Blinders, will be writing the next instalment in the Bond franchise, which will be directed by Dune filmmaker Denis Villeneuve.
