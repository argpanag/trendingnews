Inside Mariella Frostrup’s Somerset home, complete with a country-style kitchen and butter-yellow AGA

TheTools · 17.11.2026 16:14 · source · permalink

The Somerset home is the perfect place for Mariella to unwind, recharge and reconnect with the countryside

Mariella Frostrup is currently on our screens as a contestant in this year’s I’m A Celebrity… Get Me Out of Here! – swapping the comfort of her Somerset home for a hammock in the jungle.

As a result, we're taking a look around the eclectic home she’s left behind, which she gave Country Living the low-down on earlier this year.

Mariella's home of the past 13 years, in Bruton, is shared with husband, Jason McCue, and their children, Molly and Dan.

Originally three former gardeners’ cottages, Mariella and Jason restored and joined the buildings before filling them with an varied mix of pieces gathered on their travels. Feathered lampshades by local makers sit beside Islamic enamelled plates and decorative heads from West Africa collected over the years by Mariella and Jason.

The kitchen is the heart of the home, with a buttercream AGA set against dark grey tiles. It’s Mariella’s favourite room, not least because of the vast collection of cookware gathered on the pot rack suspended above the AGA, where everything is conveniently kept close to hand when she’s cooking.

The kitchen also features industrial-style pendant lights and contrasting units, some are dark charcoal and others a light neutral with green undertones. Throughout her home, exposed stone walls feature – giving it a rustic look, which Mariella's complemented with leather armchairs and wooden farmhouse-style furniture.

Her other favourite spot is the cabin at the bottom of the garden, which houses both her and Jason’s offices. From here, there are views across the surrounding fields and a stretch of river where the family love to swim in summer.

Her unwavering commitment to rural life stems from a childhood spent in Norway and Ireland, until she moved to London at 16.

“If you’ve grown up in the country, it’s deeply rooted in you,” she says.

“Just the instinct, when the going gets tough, to get to the countryside. It feels like something that’s embedded, that you can’t really change. You love it in all weathers.”

Daisy Bendall is the Houses Editor at Country Living and House Beautiful, where she spends her days sourcing some of the most inspiring homes across the country. Prior to specialising in real homes, Daisy worked for Good Housekeeping, Red, Elle Decoration and Prima, styling shoots, covering the latest decorating trends and hunting down the best buys for your home.
