LIST OF QUALIFIERS: 2026 JLSS Junior Level Science Scholarship Results

TheTools · 17.09.2026 18:06 · source · permalink

JLSS RESULTS 2026 — The Department of Science and Technology – Science Education Institute (DOST-SEI) has released the list of qualifiers for the 2026 Junior Level Science Scholarship (JLSS) Program.

A total of 11,592 incoming third-year college students in priority science and technology degree programs took the JLSS Examination on June 28 and July 5, 2026, at 109 testing centers nationwide.

Following the evaluation and selection process, 769 examinees were initially named as qualifiers, subject to the Fiscal Year 2026 budget allocation. Of these, 269 qualified under the RA 7687 Scholarship Program, 372 under the Merit Scholarship Program, and 128 under the RA 10612 Scholarship Program.

Meanwhile, 231 examinees were identified as potential qualifiers. Their names are not included in the published list of qualifiers and they will be notified via email.

The official qualification status of the 231 potential qualifiers will be determined after they resubmit the required documentary requirements for further evaluation.

231 Potential Qualifiers

DOST-SEI also identified 231 examinees as potential qualifiers. Their names are not included in the published list of qualifiers.

These potential qualifiers will be notified via email and will be asked to resubmit documentary requirements for further evaluation. Their official qualification status will be determined after the evaluation of the submitted documents.

Notice of Award and Scholarship Orientation

DOST-SEI and the DOST Regional Offices will send the Notice of Award to all qualifiers. The notice will contain the schedule of the scholarship orientation and other pertinent instructions.

Potential qualifiers will likewise receive notification regarding the next steps in the evaluation process.

The 2026 JLSS Program provides scholarship support to qualified incoming third-year college students pursuing priority science and technology degree programs.
