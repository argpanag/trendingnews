Cowboys safety P.J. Locke questionable to return with foot injury

TheTools · 20.09.2026 20:56 · source · permalink

The Cowboys are without starting safety Malik Hooker, who fractured his forearm in the season opener. P.J. Locke started for him, but lasted only three plays.

He was injured with 8:48 remaining in the first half on a non-contact play.

Locke was running toward Rachaad White, who caught a short pass from Jayden Daniels, when he suddenly pulled up.

Locke needed help getting off the field but walked slowly to the X-ray room after an examination in the sideline medical tent.

He is questionable to return with a foot injury.

Markquese Bell has replaced Locke in the lineup.

The Cowboys lead 10-0, and CeeDee Lamb has five catches for 89 yards and a touchdown. He was wide open in the end zone for a 3-yard score. Dak Prescott is 8-of-11 for 101 yards and a touchdown.

The Cowboys settled for Brandon Aubrey’s chip-shot, 25-yard field goal on their second drive after stalling at the Washington 6.
