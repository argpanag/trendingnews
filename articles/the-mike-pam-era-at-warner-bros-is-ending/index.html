The Mike & Pam Era at Warner Bros. Is Ending

TheTools · 02.10.2026 04:33 · source · permalink

David Zaslav threw himself a farewell party last night, because of course he did. Per three sources, it was a more muted affair than past get-togethers at the home of the Warner Bros. Discovery showman. No Margot Robbie or Timothée Chalamet or Michael B. Jordan, just a few top producers gathered around four outdoor tables at the Beverly Hills mansion formerly owned by Bob Evans. Greg Berlanti, John Wells, and Chuck Lorre, among others, plus Zaslav’s executive team, including HBO Max’s Casey Bloys (along with lieutenants), streaming chief JB Perrette, C.M.O. Shauna Spenley, H.R. head Amy Girdwood, comms chief Robert Gibbs, as well as Mike De Luca and Pam Abdy at the film studio (with animation head Bill Damaschke, New Line’s Richard Brener, and the DC guys, Peter Safran and James Gunn). I’d call it a wake if that weren’t so depressing.
