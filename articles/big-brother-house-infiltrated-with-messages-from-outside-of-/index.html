Big Brother house infiltrated with messages from outside of 'secret girlfriend'

  • Sep 22
  • Written by Jack Wetherill

Housemates get a message from the outside in tonight’s Big Brother (ITV)

Tonight’s episode of Big Brother looks set for even more chaos, as tennis balls containing messages are thrown into the garden from outside.

Following reports that the ITV2 house was put into lockdown over the incident, Tuesday night’s (22 September) episode is set to show exactly what went down.

In teaser footage, Lynx Noumey, Frankie Taber, Charlotte Nott, Chelsey Ammari, Rhianna Collins and Omar El Mayer are in the garden when a tennis ball is chucked over the wall.

They discover a message in it, which reads: “There is still a secret mole in there.”

View this post on Instagram

As far as the housemates – and viewers, for that matter – know, the identity of the secret mole was social media star Michael “Tays” Taylor, who also enlisted the help of Philip Regan. Their identities were revealed on Friday, with Tays exiting as his mission was complete.

Another ball is thrown into the garden, which includes the message: “Lynx has a girlfriend.”

  • Nov 29 2023

Chelsey, who has been growing close with Lynx, with the duo even kissing less than two weeks in, reacts in shock.

Lynx says: “Guys, I swear on my life I don’t.”

Later, the apparent secret girlfriend gets brought up again by celebrity house guests Jedward during yet another argument.

As they leave the house, one of the twins shouts to him: “I’ll see your girlfriend.”

Whether or not Lynx has a girlfriend on the outside, it looks like the next episode is set to be explosive either way.

Big Brother continues tonight at 9pm on ITV2.
