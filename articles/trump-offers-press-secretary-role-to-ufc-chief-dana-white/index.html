Trump offers press secretary role to UFC chief Dana White

Dana White and President Donald Trump pose for a photo during a UFC event in 2024. (Getty Images: Sarah Stier)

In short: 

Donald Trump has asked UFC chief executive Dana White to serve as White House press secretary.

Dana White posted an image to social media of him standing behind the White House press briefing room podium.

The White House has been without a press secretary since Katie Zacharia left the position in October.

US President Donald Trump has asked UFC chief executive Dana White to serve as White House press secretary.

"I am pleased to announce that the Great Dana White, a violent Fighter and Warrior, has been offered the job of Press Secretary of the United States," Trump wrote on his Truth Social account. 

"Dana, please take the job!"

The White House has been without a press secretary since October 16 when Katie Zacharia left the position after less than a week on the job.

Mr Trump called on the longtime fight promoter to bring some assistants with him, when he made the announcement on Saturday, local time, "even though some are not from our country".

After Mr Trump's post, Dana White posted an image to his own social media, of him standing behind the podium of the White House press briefing room.

"On to the next chapter,"

The online exchange comes a day after Mr Trump announced he was replacing his chief of staff Susie Wiles with his deputy Stephen Miller.

Mr Trump asked for six current or former UFC titleholders to join Mr White, including two athletes from ‌Russia's Dagestan region, Islam Makhachev and Khabib Nurmagomedov.

"We ‌need these people to ⁠defend us against the Lunatic Left," Mr Trump wrote.

Trump, White are old allies

The Ultimate Fighting Championship chief executive is a long-time ally of Donald Trump.

The President hosted UFC Freedom 250 on the South Lawn of the White House in June as part of the United States' 250 year celebrations, which also coincided with his own 80th birthday.

President Donald Trump and UFC President and CEO Dana White walk out during the UFC Freedom 250 at White House South Lawn. (Reuters: Amber Searls-Imagn Images)

A 600-tonne metal arch dubbed The Claw was built for the event, transforming the iconic DC lawn.

The broadcast commenced with Mr Trump and Mr White walking out of the White House, through the crowd.

The White House said the UFC covered the costs of the event, but federal lawsuits were filed in an attempt to halt it, arguing that a private sporting event on a public property required congressional approval.

A federal judge knocked back the lawsuit.
