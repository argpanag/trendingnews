7 artists you can't miss at Dreamstate SoCal 2026

TheTools · 19.11.2026 16:00 · source · permalink

Dreamstate SoCal 2026 returns to the Queen Mary Waterfront in Long Beach, California, on November 20 and 21, bringing together some of the biggest names in trance and electronic music for two nights of unforgettable performances. Known for its euphoric melodies, high-energy sets, and dedicated community of trance enthusiasts, the festival continues to celebrate the sounds that have shaped the genre while embracing its evolution.

Dreamstate SoCal 2026 returns to Long Beach, bringing two nights of trance, melodic techno, and unforgettable performances to the Queen Mary Waterfront.

This year’s edition features an impressive mix of trance, progressive house, melodic techno, and harder electronic sounds. With 4 stages showcasing different musical styles throughout the weekend, attendees will have plenty of opportunities to discover new artists and revisit familiar favorites. Ahead of Dreamstate SoCal 2026, here are 7 artists you won’t want to miss.

Dash Berlin

Few names in trance music carry the nostalgic appeal of Dash Berlin. Known for timeless anthems such as “Till the Sky Falls Down,” “Waiting,” and “Better Half of Me,” the project has played a significant role in shaping the genre’s emotional and uplifting sound. With soaring melodies, powerful breakdowns, and unforgettable vocals, Dash Berlin’s performance is sure to resonate with longtime trance fans and newcomers alike. Catch Dash Berlin on Friday at The Dream stage.

Enrico Sangiuliano

Italian producer Enrico Sangiuliano has established himself as one of techno’s most respected figures, known for his meticulous sound design and immersive productions. His signature style combines driving basslines, hypnotic rhythms, and atmospheric elements that continuously build tension on the dancefloor. As Dreamstate continues to embrace sounds beyond traditional trance, Sangiuliano’s appearance offers attendees an opportunity to experience a darker, more intense side of the festival. Enrico Sangiuliano will perform on Friday at The Void stage.

Trending

ARTBAT

Ukrainian duo ARTBAT have become one of the most recognizable names in melodic techno, captivating audiences worldwide with emotionally charged productions and powerful performances. Known for tracks such as “Hollow” and “Best of Me,” the duo blends cinematic melodies with driving rhythms to create an immersive musical experience. Their appearance at Dreamstate SoCal brings a different dimension to the festival’s trance-focused programming, making them a must-see act for fans of melodic electronic music. ARTBAT will close out The Dream stage on Friday.

TH;EN

Blending melodic techno and progressive house, TH;EN has steadily gained recognition for productions that balance emotional depth with dancefloor energy. His sound is characterized by intricate melodies, atmospheric textures, and driving percussion, creating an engaging experience from start to finish. With his growing presence in the electronic music scene, TH;EN is an artist worth catching for those looking to explore the intersection of progressive sounds and melodic techno. Catch TH;EN on Saturday at The Dream stage.

JOA

Rising DJ and producer JOA is quickly establishing himself as an exciting name in electronic music. Following his festival debut at Ultra Miami 2025, the Miami-based artist has continued building momentum with a sound that combines melodic techno influences and trance-inspired elements. His productions emphasize captivating melodies and infectious rhythms, making him an artist worth keeping on your radar as he continues to expand his presence on the festival circuit. JOA will take the stage at The Dream on Saturday.

David Forbes + John Askew

For fans seeking relentless energy and harder trance sounds, David Forbes and John Askew are an essential addition to the weekend schedule. Both artists have built reputations for pushing the boundaries of trance with driving basslines, intense buildups, and uncompromising performances. Their collaborative set promises a powerful combination of tech trance and harder-edged electronic music, offering a change of pace from the more melodic performances taking place throughout the festival. Catch David Forbes and John Askew together on Saturday at The Void stage.

Above & Beyond (Anjunabeats Classics)

Few acts have influenced modern trance quite like Above & Beyond. The legendary trio has spent more than two decades creating music that connects audiences through uplifting melodies, emotional storytelling, and unforgettable live experiences. For Dreamstate SoCal 2026, the group will present a special Anjunabeats Classics set, celebrating the music and legacy of their influential record label. Fans can look forward to a nostalgic journey through the sounds that helped define an era of trance music, making this one of the weekend’s most anticipated performances. Above & Beyond will close out The Dream stage on Saturday.

With a lineup that celebrates both the roots and evolution of trance music, Dreamstate SoCal 2026 is shaping up to be another memorable weekend at the Queen Mary Waterfront. Whether you’re looking to revisit timeless classics, discover emerging talent, or explore the harder and more melodic sides of electronic music, this year’s edition offers something for every fan. For tickets, additional festival information, and the complete lineup, visit the official Dreamstate SoCal website. Follow Dreamstate’s official Instagram for more updates.
