(UPDATED) A man died after the car he was driving fell off the fourth-floor parking area of the Citadines Benavidez Makati building on Thursday.

Makati City Police Chief P/Col. Pedro Alagano Jr. confirmed the 55-yerar-old driver’s death after the vehicle plunged from the building at around 11:40 a.m.

Based on the police’s initial investigation, the vehicle was moving down the driveway from the fifth floor toward the fourth-floor parking area when the incident happened.

While navigating the off-ramp, the vehicle’s front-left portion hit the building’s wall, causing the driver to lose control.

ADVERTISEMENT

The car then struck a bollard but continued moving forward until it crash through a wall and fell off the building.

Makati City rescue personnel responded to the scene and found the driver without a pulse and not breathing. Police later confirmed the driver died.

In a statement sent to ABS-CBN News, The Ascott Limited Philippines, which operates Citadines Benavidez Makati, said it was aware of the incident involving a vehicle belonging to a tenant of a private residence at the property.

“Our immediate priority is to support those affected,” the company said.

It added that it is cooperating fully with authorities and remains in contact with the individuals involved as the investigation continues.

“Out of respect for the privacy of those affected as well as the ongoing investigation, we are unable to provide further information at this time,” the company further said.

Meanwhile, debris from the incident, including dislodged steel portions of the building, had been cleared from the area.

The wrecked vehicle was also removed after the driver’s remains were retrieved.
