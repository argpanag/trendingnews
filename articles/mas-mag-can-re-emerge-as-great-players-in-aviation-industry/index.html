MAS, MAG can re-emerge as great players in aviation industry

TheTools · 25.11.2026 17:38 · source · permalink
    bookmark

KUALA LUMPUR: Malaysia Airlines and Malaysia Aviation Group (MAG) can re-emerge as great players in the aviation industry if they continue to demonstrate resilience and maintain a strong position amid global uncertainties, Prime Minister Datuk Seri Anwar Ibrahim said.

Anwar, who is also Finance Minister, said the government, through Khazanah Nasional, remained committed to ensuring the success of both Malaysia Airlines and MAG, describing their growth as important to the aviation industry and the country.

"I need not refer to the increasing fuel prices, which are of course a concern to the airline and the nation at large. I have done my part in ensuring that the price of RON95 for Malaysians is one of the lowest in the world. But I cannot do so for airlines.

"Malaysia Aviation Group, Captain Nasaruddin, you have to pay the commercial price.

"But I believe, InsyaAllah, if you continue to demonstrate resilience, remain well-positioned to manage efficiently, attract investment and strengthen industries, we can always have the capacity to create new opportunities for growth," he said in his speech at the document exchange ceremony for MAG's long-range wide-body aircraft acquisition.

Anwar said Boeing's presence reflected not only its confidence in MAG but also the strength of their long-term partnership, adding that MAG had demonstrated its ability to honour its commitments.

He said Malaysia was playing an increasingly important role in the global aerospace ecosystem, underpinned by the capabilities of its workforce and the confidence of international partners.

Local companies and talent were also making growing contributions to global aviation, supply chains and other high-value industries, he added.

Anwar said countries were not only facing an uncertain period but also a period of heightened uncertainty.

He said governments and industries therefore had to act swiftly to anticipate and address these concerns amid shifting economic realities, changing supply chains and increasing competition.

"So let us be more positive, remain steady and ready to compete, adapt and seize new opportunities," the Prime Minister said.

Anwar also commended Malaysia Airlines for providing "the best services in the world" despite facing difficulties due to rising costs.

"You ask anyone, they say it is still, in terms of service, the best in the world," the Prime Minister said.

Earlier, MAG announced the acquisition of ten Boeing 787 Dreamliner aircraft, with an option for six more, as part of its fleet modernisation strategy.

Also present were Deputy Transport Minister Datuk Hasbi Habibollah, MAG president and group chief executive officer Captain Nasaruddin A. Bakar, and Khazanah Nasional managing director Datuk Amirul Feisal Wan Zahir. - Bernama
