Michael Carrick reaction: How did Manchester United boss explain wild draw with Aston Villa?

TheTools · 08.11.2026 19:01 · source · permalink

Manchester United’s wayward defense complicated things again on Sunday as Michael Carrick’s Red Devils twice came back from two-goal deficits in a 4-4 draw with Aston Villa at Old Trafford.

Goalkeeper Senne Lammens was poor on the first two goals goals but the defenders did little to help him and then really put him in a bind when Diogo Dalot conceded a penalty early in the second half once United had rallied for a 2-2 halftime score line.

The draw leaves United in the bottom three heading into the international break and completes a complicated week which already saw a wild comeback win over AS Roma in the UEFA Champions League at midweek.

Is this it for Carrick at Man United?

Another stressful game at Old Trafford? “We’re making it hard for ourselves, really. A lot of the performance was very, very good. Start of both halves made it challenging forourselves. Mistakes that we know and the boys know shouldn’t happen. We probably should’ve scored more than we did today but if we keep conceding the types of goals that are coming from nowhere... we need to tighten things up.”

“I thought we were by far the better team. They started and scored and we played some very good football, got shots. We kept on going. We could’ve even won it at the death.”

More thoughts on the game: “Disappointed that we haven’t won the game. I thought we were the better team for large parts of the game. We made it difficult for ourselves giving away the goals we did. We did it the other night and did it again today. We came back and had a lot of the game. I’m disappointed that we didn’t win.”

What were you thinking down 4-2 in the 90th minute? “That if we get one anything can happen. I was saying to the boys on the pitch we just need one. We never give up. We keep believing. That’s why.”

Will you be in charge after the break with the club now in 18th? “We need to improve the position we’re in. Of course we do. I firmly believe the big moments, if we tighten up those, there are a lot of things we can take forward from the last three games.”

Were all four goals conceded poor ones? “No goal is a good goal to concede but there are ones you can do a bit more about and there are the ones we gave away today. We’ve gotta tighten them up. We’ve given ourselves too much to do.”

Is this a relegation fight or can you still make something bigger out of this season? “We’re in November. I don’t like the situation we’re in or the results we’ve had but we can definitely do something about it.”
