If you already love UGG for its cloud-soft boots and slippers, there's a good chance you'll love this cozy fleece blanket from the brand that brings the same plush feel to your couch or bed. Right now, the UGG Nita Throw Blanket is on sale at Amazon for as low as $48, down from $72 during its Black Friday sale. With the holiday shopping season now in full swing, it's a strong deal for this shopper-favorite throw.

It features a reversible design with a quilted, box-stitch on one side and fluffy fleece and UGG's brand label on the other. It's made for year-round comfort — perfect for movie nights, lazy naps, or curling up with a good book.

Made from polyester, the throw comes in a roomy 50-inch by-70-inch size that's big enough to share. As one shopper put it, "The size is perfect for two people cuddling up to watch a show!" It's available in four colors, with pricing that varies by shade: white is the lowest at $48, gray is $50, coffee is $60, and blue is full price at $72. Whichever option you choose, it's still a solid deal.

The UGG Nita Throw Blanket is also hypoallergenic and OEKO-TEX certified, meaning it's made without harmful chemicals. It's easy to care for, too—machine washable and safe to tumble dry on low. All it takes is a quick shake afterward to help restore its fluffy, plush look.

Shoppers consistently highlight how soft and cozy it feels. One reviewer called it "So soft and comfy!!" Another shared, "This was the perfect gift for my friend who likes cozying up and reading a book." While a third shopper added, "Best UGG blanket ever. I own two." One more simply said it's "perfect for all weather."

For those wanting larger options, the blanket also comes in twin/twin XL, full/queen and king/California king sizes that are all currently included in the sale.
