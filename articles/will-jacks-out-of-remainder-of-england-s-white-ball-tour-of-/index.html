Will Jacks out of remainder of England's white-ball tour of Australia

TheTools · 22.11.2026 10:55 · source · permalink

Will Jacks has been out of the remainder of England’s white-ball tour of Australia after breaking his hand during the first T20I in Melbourne.

ECB confirmed that the all-rounder will take no further part in the series. An update on his replacement will be announced in due course.

Jacks hurt his hand while bowling in England’s three-wicket loss to Australia in the series opener on Saturday (November 21), copping a blow from Ben Dwarshuis in his follow-through while attempting a return catch.

Bowling his third over, Jacks was struck on his bowling hand, with visuals suggesting a possible split webbing. The physio came to check up on him, and the all-rounder, visibly in pain, was forced to walk off.

The injury occurred at a crucial juncture of the game: at that point, Australia needed 12 off 8. Henry Crocombe bowled the final two deliveries of his over. But Sam Curran couldn’t defend 11 off the final one, with Jack Edwards hitting a boundary off the penultimate ball.

It completed Australia’s highest successful men’s T20I chase (188) against England. The second T20I of the five-match series will be played on Tuesday (November 24) in Queensland.
