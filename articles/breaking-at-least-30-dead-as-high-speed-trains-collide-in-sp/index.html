Breaking: At least 30 dead as high-speed trains collide in Spain

TheTools · 28.10.2026 10:11 · source · permalink

  • Topic:Rail Accidents and Incidents

Topic:Rail Accidents and Incidents

Emergency services attend to the victims at the scene of a collision between two trains in near Toledo in Spain. (AP: Juanma Jiménez/Europa Press)

At least 30 people have died in a crash between two trains in central Spain, officials say.

The high-speed trains collided near the city of Toledo in the La Castilla Mancha region on Wednesday morning, shutting down the line connecting ‌Madrid to the southern Andalusia ‌region, rail operator ‌Adif said.

"Traffic ‌on the Madrid-Andalusia ‌high-speed rail line ‌has been suspended following ‌a rear-end ‌collision between two trains," it said on X.

Two cars were derailed in the crash. (AP: Juanma Jimenez/Europa Press)

Regional spokesperson Esther Padilla said the death toll was "very likely" to increase.

She said 147 people were on board the Iryo train and 397 were on the Renfe train, and that several carriages had plunged down an embankment.

Ms Padilla told state broadcaster TVE that one train ran into the tail of the other, which was stationary, knocking two of its carriages off the track.

She said it was not known what had caused the crash, which occurred on a flat stretch between the towns of Urda and Los Yébenes.

In January this year 45 people were killed when an Iryo train derailed at high speed into the path of an oncoming train run by Renfe AVE, near Adamuz in southern Spain.
