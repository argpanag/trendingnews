Will Lamine Yamal play for Spain against England today? Barça star gives up number 19

TheTools · 15.11.2026 09:58 · source · permalink

Spain’s coach has finalized his squad for tonight’s clash with England, with two players still waiting for their international debuts.

Luis de la Fuente has decided on the four players who will miss tonight’s game against England. With Alejandro Grimaldo already ruled out after leaving the Spain camp with the flu, the head coach had four further players to cut from his squad.

The players left out are Álvaro Valles, Jacobo Ramón, Jorge de Frutos and Roberto Fernández, meaning the first two will have to wait a little longer to make their senior Spain debuts.

Valles was also omitted from De la Fuente’s matchday squad against the Czech Republic last Thursday. Ramón, meanwhile, was on the bench but did not get any playing time in Spain’s victory in Prague.

Why is Lamine Yamal wearing No. 10 for Spain against England?

Aside from the players left out by the coach, the most eye-catching detail in the squad list is Lamine Yamal’s shirt number. The Barcelona youngster will once again wear No. 10 for Spain instead of his usual No. 19.

Yamal has worn the iconic number for La Roja before, including against Denmark in 2024, a game in which Dani Olmo was also unavailable, just as he is tonight.
