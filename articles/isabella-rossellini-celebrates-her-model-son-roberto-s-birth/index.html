Isabella Rossellini is wishing her model son, Roberto Rossellini, a happy birthday.

The legendary model and film actress, 74, who made waves with her incredible role in Conclave, is the daughter of Ingrid Bergman and Roberto Rossellini. But she's also a proud mom, and her handsome son is following in her fashion footsteps with a modeling career. In a new photo honoring Roberto on his birthday, Rossellini spoke poignantly about her son.

"Happy birthday to my darling son Roberto. In the photo last birthday together but this year I away working in Poland @robertorossellini," she captioned the shot. There was also a video included taken in Warsaw, where the actress is currently working. However, she noted that she misses her son very much.

Roberto looked like he had a great time celebrating his birthday. On social media, he shared a delightful array of photos.

She's Still a Fashion Plate

Rossellini is still every bit the fashion plate she was in the '70s and '80s. Last summer, she was spotted outside the Dolce & Gabbana Alta Moda fashion show at Foro Romano in Rome wearing a flowing black tunic adorned with beautiful gemstones. Her trademark dark hair featured platinum streaks, a strong departure for the legendary brunette. As always, she paired it with a deep red lip.

Vittorio Zunino Celotto / Getty Images

Isabella Rossellini Isn't Just a Beautiful Face

While the iconic stunner is known for her work in front of the camera, many people are unfamiliar with her work as an animal behaviorist. For over 20 years, Rossellini has raised future service dogs that will go on to assist people ranging from veterans with PTSD to those living with a visual impairment, PEOPLE previously reported.

"I always had dogs, cats, hamsters, and little birds," Rossellini told PEOPLE. "As I grew older, and I was working less as a model and actress, I went back to university and finished my master's in Ethology, which is the science of animal behavior."

You'll find a variety of pups at Rossellini's house, in addition to the dog Rossellini looks after for the Guide Dog Foundation. There's Darcy, who is trained as an acrobat, and Pinocchio, a rescue dog Rossellini found on the street, the piece continued.
