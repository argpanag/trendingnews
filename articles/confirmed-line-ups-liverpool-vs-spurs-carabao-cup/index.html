Confirmed line-ups | Liverpool vs Spurs | Carabao Cup

TheTools · 15.09.2026 18:51 · source · permalink
  • Spurs
  • Tottenham Hotspur Stadium

We can confirm the team line-ups for this evening's Carabao Cup third round tie with Liverpool (8pm UK).

Roberto De Zerbi makes eight changes to the team that started Saturday evening's 0-0 draw with Everton.

Martin Dubravka comes into the side to make his competitive debut for the Club while Tosin Adarabioyo comes in for his first start after making his debut in that stalemate with the Toffees. The duo replace Antonin Kinsky and Jan Paul van Hecke.

Marcos Senesi also comes into the side for Micky van de Ven while Destiny Udogie comes in for Andy Robertson with Archie Gray keeping his place and taking the captain's armband. In midfield, Rodrigo Bentancur also keeps his place with Conor Gallagher and Lucas Bergvall coming in for Sandro Tonali and Mateus Fernandes.

In our forward line, Omar Marmoush also keeps his place in the side with Mathys Tel and Mohammed Kudus coming in for Dominic Solanke and Savio. Mo makes his first start for the Club since 4 January.

Here’s the team in full…

Starting XI: Dubravka, Tosin, Senesi, Gallagher, Tel, Udogie, Gray (c), Bergvall, Kudus, Marmoush, Bentancur.

Substitutes: Kinsky, Robertson, van Hecke, Maddison, Savio, Fernandes, Solanke, Davies, van de Ven.

Our hosts meanwhile have made 10 changes to their side which started their 0-0 Premier League draw with Fulham on Saturday.

Here's how they line-up...

Starting XI: Mamardashvili, Gomez (c), Kerkez, Mac Allister, Gakpo, Frimpong, Araujo, Nyoni, McConnell, Koumas, Ngumoha.

Substitutes: Allisson, Endo, Jacquet, Szoboszlai, Isak, Munoz, Barcola, Gravenberch, Chambers.
