Woman, 19, in critical condition after drive-by shooting in Queensland

TheTools · 24.10.2026 13:38 · source · permalink
  • Australia
  • QLD
  • Shootings

Updated October 24, 2026 - 8.30pm. First published at 1.38pm

Four people have been shot in a drive-by shooting in Queensland’s south-east, with a 19-year-old woman in a critical condition.

Police were called to Bryants Road in Loganholme about 8.15pm on Saturday, to reports of shots fired from a vehicle outside Fitzy’s Loganholme, which was holding a boxing event.

The venue was hosting a boxing event at the time of the shooting. Today

Authorities confirmed four people were injured and taken to hospital, with police telling nine.com.au one of the victims, a 19-year-old woman, was in a critical condition after being shot in the head.

Police said the other victims were a 27-year-old man, a 37-year-old man and a 38-year-old man, who are all in a serious but stable condition.

Witness recalled hearing loud and consecutive noises, followed by a quick response from emergency services.

“We heard a pretty consistent bang, [it went] bang bang bang,” Tyler Florance told 9News.

A car matching the description of one fleeing the scene was later found burnt out. Today

“There were ambos everywhere, cops everywhere, ambulance flew off as soon as we got here.”

The gunman is still at large, with the car seen fleeing the scene.

A car matching the description was later found burnt out on a street in Waterford West.

However, police don’t believe there’s an ongoing threat to the public.
