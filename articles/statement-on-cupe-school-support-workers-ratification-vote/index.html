Statement on CUPE School Support Workers’ Ratification Vote

TheTools · 27.11.2026 17:57 · source · permalink

We’re happy to inform parents, students and all Nova Scotians that CUPE school support workers have ratified the tentative agreement reached on November 20.

Our goal has always been to make sure students get the education they deserve, employees are treated fairly and to avoid a potential disruption in learning. We appreciate all the work both sides have done to reach this agreement.
