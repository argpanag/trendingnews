WEATHER: Pretoria to hit 33°C on Tuesday as thunderstorms threaten Gauteng, Cape Town stays cool

TheTools · 27.10.2026 07:31 · source · permalink

Kabous Le Roux

Pretoria could reach 33°C on Tuesday as morning thunderstorms threaten parts of Gauteng. Johannesburg will be warm, while Cape Town stays cool at 23°C.

  • Weather forecast
  • Cape Town weather

Weather forecast for Tuesday, 27 October: Pretoria could reach 33°C as morning thunderstorms threaten parts of Gauteng, while Johannesburg braces for extreme UV levels and Cape Town enjoys cooler conditions with a high of 23°C. (123rf.com)

Pretoria is set for a very warm Tuesday (27 October), with temperatures expected to climb to 33°C as the capital faces the prospect of isolated morning thunderstorms, according to AccuWeather.

There is a 55% probability of precipitation and a 21% chance of thunderstorms, with 1.8mm of rain forecast. The expected rainfall could last for approximately 1.5 hours.

Winds are forecast to blow from the north-west at 13km/h, with gusts reaching 48km/h.

The UV index is expected to reach 5, classified as moderate.

Tuesday night should bring a significant change, with clear skies and temperatures dropping to 15°C. No rain or thunderstorms are expected.

Johannesburg weather: Very warm conditions and extreme UV levels

Johannesburg is also in for a warm Tuesday, with temperatures forecast to reach 31°C as cloud cover decreases throughout the day.

No rainfall is forecast.

The UV index is expected to reach 11, classified as extreme.

Winds are forecast to blow from the west-south-west at 17km/h, with gusts reaching 44km/h.

Johannesburg residents can expect a cooler evening, with clear skies and temperatures dropping to 13°C.

Cape Town weather: Cooler conditions as skies brighten

Cape Town is expected to experience considerably cooler conditions than Gauteng on Tuesday, with a forecast high of 23°C.

The Mother City is set for a day of clouds followed by a brightening sky.

No rainfall is expected.

Winds are forecast to blow from the west-south-west at 15km/h, with gusts reaching 32km/h.

The UV index is expected to reach 5, classified as moderate.

Tuesday night will be partly cloudy, with temperatures dropping to 14°C and southerly winds blowing at 17km/h, with gusts reaching 32km/h.

No rain or thunderstorms are expected overnight.
