Rocky River football ranked No. 1 in Division IV state poll; Avon 11th in D-II

TheTools · 15.09.2026 20:54 · source · permalink

In a world without Glenville, Rocky River is considered Ohio’s best high school football team in Division IV.

On Sept. 15, the Ohio Prep Sports Media Association released its first high school football state poll of the 2026 season.

Although defending state champion Glenville was not spotted anywhere in the rankings, the undefeated Pirates at Rocky River were awarded the top spot by voters.

At 4-0 (1-0 Cleveland West Conference), Rocky River collected wins against Akron Manchester (28-7), John Marshall (63-0), Uniontown Lake (20-16) and Lakewood (49-7), defeating two undefeated teams in the Blue Streaks and Rangers in Weeks 3 and 4.

The Pirates are led by a dominant offense that averages 40 points per game, while its defense is allowing less than eight per game.

Quarterback Carter Atwater has thrown for 887 yards, completed 64.2% of his passes for 13 touchdowns to three interceptions.

The offense is spearheaded by Kentucky commit Tristin Hughes, who has 862 all-purpose yards and scored touchdowns on the ground (three touchdowns), through the air (two touchdown receptions), on defense (one) and punt return (one).

While Hughes is Rocky River’s leading tackler with 39 stops, the Pirates' defense also has linemen Jackson Grear and All-Ohio lineman Steven McHenry.

Rocky River received 52 points with two first-place votes, as Cincinnati Wyoming (50 points) slightly trails the Pirates in D-IV.

Glenville, the winner of three of the last four state titles, was not ranked.

The Tarblooders, which are in D-IV Region 13 with Rocky River, are currently being evaluated by the OHSAA.

With the consolidation with Collinwood, Glenville could potentially change divisions.

In D-II, Avon — as back-to-back state champs — was ranked No. 11th with nine points.

The 2-2 Eagles defeated St. Ignatius (15th in D-I) and North Ridgeville (0-4).

Their losses were to Pine-Richland (2-1) from Pennsylvania and Upper Arlington, ranked 13th in D-I this week.

At the top of Division II sits Columbus Watterson (70 points) and is followed by Walsh Jesuit (60), Massillon (60), Big Walnut (51) Wadsworth (33) and Cincinnati Anderson (32).

When it comes to D-II Region 6, Toledo Central Catholic (30) is the highest-rated team in Avon’s region.

Columbia (4-0, 1-0 Lorain County League) was mentioned with two points for 21st place in D-VI.
