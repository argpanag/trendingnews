Brunello Cucinelli hosted a special dinner to celebrate the grand opening of the Brunello Cucinelli Paris boutique on Rue Saint-Honoré, followed by a screening of the documentary film Brunello, The Gracious Visionary. The Paris celebration followed similar screenings held in Rome and New York, with guests naturally dressed in the Italian house for the occasion.

Courtesy of Brunello Cucinelli

Black-Tie Contrast

It has been a quiet summer for Colman Domingo, but he continues to be one of the men I look forward to seeing most, and this Brunello Cucinelli look shows why. As you know, ivory and white isn't always my favourite combination, but with the black trousers providing a sleek contrast, what's not to love?

Courtesy of Brunello Cucinelli

Leather After Dark

There is an ease to Juliette Binoche in Brunello Cucinelli that I really like here. Some might find that the leather blazer relaxes the formality, but I feel that it gives the all-black look its focal point and prevents it from feeling too basic.

Courtesy of Brunello Cucinelli

Borrowed From The Boys

Charlotte Le Bon often embraces a menswear mood, and in Brunello Cucinelli, this might be her best interpretation yet. The undone bow tie is the detail that gives the look its character. Add the tinted glasses and this feels smart without becoming predictable, and so natural to her.

Courtesy of Brunello Cucinelli

Sequins Meet Shirting

I love the contrast Cindy Bruna created with her Brunello Cucinelli look. A sheer white shirt and sequin-drenched oversized trousers shouldn't necessarily feel like natural partners, yet that is exactly what makes the combination interesting. The relaxed shape of the shirt offsets the glamour of the trousers, making sequins feel entirely appropriate for an intimate dinner rather than a red carpet.

Courtesy of Brunello Cucinelli

Back To Black

Bianca Balti kept things sleek in all-black Brunello Cucinelli. The plunging black blouse, choker and smoky eye add to the after-dark mood. It is simple and sexy.

Courtesy of Brunello Cucinelli

A Tale Of Two Tuxedos

Vincent Cassel joined Brunello Cucinelli himself, giving us two different approaches to evening tailoring. Vincent's blue double-breasted suit and open-neck white shirt feel more relaxed, while Brunello's grey double-breasted jacket, bow tie and white trousers lean into traditional black-tie codes with a lighter touch. Side by side, they make a compelling case for not defaulting to an all-black tuxedo.
