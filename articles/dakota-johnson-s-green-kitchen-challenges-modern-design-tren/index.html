Dakota Johnson's green kitchen challenges modern design trends

TheTools · 19.10.2026 12:40 · source · permalink

Dakota Johnson's green kitchen continues to inspire interior design, demonstrating the enduring appeal of vintage details, rich colours and natural materials. First attracting attention six years ago, the space offers an alternative to minimalist kitchens dominated by handleless cabinetry and white surfaces.

© Fred Duval | Dreamstime

Designed by Pierce & Ward, the kitchen combines painted green cabinets with warm timber, Persian rugs and ceramic accessories. The contrasting textures create a layered, personal interior that balances colour with warmth. The design reflects growing appreciation for heritage-inspired interiors that prioritise character over short-lived trends. Green cabinetry, vintage pottery and brass fittings offer ways to recreate the aesthetic without a complete renovation.

Six years later, Johnson's kitchen remains an example of how thoughtful material choices and individuality can create a timeless interior.
