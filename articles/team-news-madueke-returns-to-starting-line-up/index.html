Team news: Madueke returns to starting line up

TheTools · 22.10.2026 04:01 · source · permalink

Noni Madueke is back in the starting XI after his match-winning goal against Nottingham Forest at the weekend.

The winger played the second-half on Sunday, grabbing the winner late on, and he’s one of two changes to the side that started that match as we take on Bayern Munich in the Allianz Arena.

The other change sees Jurrien Timber start at right back, so Ben White is on the bench. If he comes on he will be making his 200th Gunners appearance.

The rest of the team for this Champions League matchday three fixture remains unchanged, so Ezri Konsa and Gabriel continue their defensive partnership with Riccardo Calafiori at left back. The midfield consists of Declan Rice, Bruno Guimaraes and captain Martin Odegaard. Kai Havertz is up front with Bukayo Saka on the wing.

The hosts include Harry Kane up front. He’s scored 15 times against us in his career, and has netted nine times in all competitions so far this season. Michael Olise is suspended while Joshua Kimmich is also unavailable.

Former Gunner Serge Gnabry, who played alongside Mikel Arteta during his Arsenal days, is also in the starting line up for Bayern. 

LINE-UPS

Arsenal: Raya, Timber, Konsa, Gabriel, Calafiori, Rice, Bruno, Odegaard, Saka, Madueke, Havertz.

Subs: Meslier, Arrizabalaga, White, Mosquera, Lewis-Skelly, Zubimendi, Merino, Eze, Dowman, Gyokeres.

Bayern Munich: Neuer, Upamecano, Minjae Kim, Davies, Laimer, Gnabry, Bischof, Saibari, Stanisic, Pavlovic, Kane.

Subs: Ulreich, Urbig, Tah, Brown, Luis Diaz, Ito, Boey, Ndiaye, Karl, Binder.
