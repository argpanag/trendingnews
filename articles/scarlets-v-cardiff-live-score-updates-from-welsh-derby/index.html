Scarlets v Cardiff LIVE: Score updates from Welsh derby

TheTools · 26.09.2026 19:32 · source · permalink

The Scarlets take on Cardiff in Llanelli in the first Welsh derby of the season.

Nigel Davies' side are missing some key players, with the likes of Fletcher Anderson, Taine Plumtree, Archer Holz and Sam Costelow sidelined.

That means there is a competitive debut for former Wales playmaker Gareth Anscombe, while Wales U20s backrower Osian Williams makes his first United Rugby Championship appearance at No 8.

Cardiff have named a strong side for their trip to Llanelli and have handed a first URC start to former Wallabies prop Scott Sio.

You can follow live updates below.

Scarlets: Murray; Mee, J Roberts, E James, Page; Anscombe, Blacker; S O'Connor, Elias, Henry Thomas, W Evans, Cuckson, Lousi, Macleod (capt), O Williams.

Assistant referees: Carwyn Sion (WRU), Lloyd Edwards (WRU)

TMO: Keith David (WRU).

Debuts

There are plenty of players making their competitive debuts tonight.

From the Scarlets there is former Wales playmaker Gareth Anscombe, No 8 Osian Williams, second-row Will Evans and hooker John McKee.

For Cardiff there is former Wallabies prop Scott Sio and Namibian centre Le Roux Malan.

View from the Cardiff camp

Cardiff head coach Corniel van Zyl:

“We are really looking forward to kicking-off the season and the fact that it’s a Welsh derby first up makes it even more exciting.

“With that excitement also comes a massive challenge and we know we are going up against a formidable Scarlets side at home, and they will also be determined to start well.

“We had two really close and competitive games against the Scarlets last season. They were two crazy games where I believe both home side’s should have won but on both occasions the sway team took the win.

“So we are expecting another full-blooded derby but we have prepared well and are looking forward to it. It’s about getting as much of our game on the pitch, applying pressure on them and fighting for every moment.”

Scarlets coach Nigel Davies: We need these rivalries

Here's some pre-match thoughts from Nigel Davies...

The rivalry is the best bit, isn't it? There's a need for that. People want to come and see those local derbies we still have. When you play against the Ospreys, Cardiff and Dragons, that's fantastic. Long may that continue because that means so much to people.We don't get great crowds in the URC because of the league's logistics, but in those events we do."

Gwyn Jones' verdict

S4C pundit Gwyn Jones is one man really looking forward to tonight's match, describing it as a chance for fans to forget about the off-filed strife in Welsh rugby for 80 minutes.
