Newcastle vs Hull: Newcastle (8/15)

Some Hull City fans may be starting to get a nosebleed, stunned by how high they are riding in the Premier League table, but the Tigers fun may stop at St James' Park on Saturday.

There is surely not enough depth in the Hull squad to sustain this league position and Matthias Jaissle appears to have ridden the early storm at Newcastle, going unbeaten in all three home games and winning at Tottenham.

Newcastle have scored in every game this term - 13 consecutive fixtures if including pre-season friendlies - and they can shrug off Monday's defeat to a buzzing Leeds side by claiming all three points at home to Hull.

Brighton vs Arsenal: Arsenal (4/6)

Arsenal are bursting with confidence, having finally become champions again, and the Gunners have started the season in imperious fashion. It is difficult to see the London giants coming unstuck at the AmEx Stadium on Saturday.

Brighton got lots of headlines for beating Manchester United at Old Trafford on Wednesday night, but their opponents are enduring a crisis of confidence, so that formline may not be as strong as it first appears.

Brighton went through a ding-dong EFL Cup encounter in Manchester, while Arsenal cruised past Ipswich 24 hours earlier. Mikel Arteta's side are fresher and superior and have to go in the weekend acca.

Nottingham Forest vs Coventry City: Forest (3/5)

Coventry City are on zero goals and zero points, having conceded ten goals in their first four games of the league campaign, so Nottingham Forest's players will probably be licking their lips at the prospect of meeting the Sky Blues.

Coventry have had some tough fixtures to start life back in the top-flight, but their squad is full of new faces who do not appear to have settled under their young manager Frank Lampard.

Forest have gone three games unbeaten in the league, winning at Aston Villa last Saturday, and the Tricky Trees will expect to claim three points against Coventry.

Man City vs Sunderland: City (1/4)

Enzo Maresca will be thrilled with how quickly he has got the Manchester City players to start performing at their best - and a smooth transition from the Pep Guardiola era seems likely.

City's squad is strong enough to handle a Thursday-night EFL fixture and Sunderland should be rapidly dismissed at the Etihad Stadium on Sunday. 

Bournemouth vs Liverpool: Liverpool (11/10)

Liverpool can bring home the acca by beating Bournemouth, who had a Thursday-night Europa League fixture in Spain.

Alexander Isak is threatening to justify his huge price tag and the Reds can be expected to be such a threat on the counter against a limited home side who have made a habit of throwing leads away of late.
