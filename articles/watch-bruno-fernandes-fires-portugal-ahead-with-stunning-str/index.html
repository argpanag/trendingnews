WATCH: Bruno Fernandes fires Portugal ahead with stunning strike before David Brooks hits back for Wales

TheTools · 17.11.2026 20:22 · source · permalink

Watch Bruno Fernandes score for Portugal.

Last Update:

Bruno Fernandes scored a stunning opening goal for Portugal against Wales on Tuesday night, only for David Brooks to hit back just two minutes later to bring the hosts level at 1-1.

The Manchester United midfielder produced a brilliant finish to hand Roberto Martinez’s side an early advantage, capitalising on a mistake from Wales midfielder Josh Sheehan.

Sheehan was surrounded by Portugal players on the edge of his own penalty area and lost possession to Ruben Neves, who quickly shifted the ball to Fernandes on his left.

The Portugal captain wasted no time in taking aim, unleashing a powerful right-footed strike that flew into the top corner and left Wales goalkeeper Karl Darlow with little chance.

However, Wales responded almost immediately, producing an excellent attacking move of their own to restore parity just two minutes later.

Rubin Colwill played a clever pass through the Portugal defence to release Harry Wilson, who found himself one-on-one with goalkeeper Diogo Costa.

Wilson attempted to beat the Portugal goalkeeper with a left-footed effort towards the near post, but Costa managed to parry his shot away.

The rebound fell back to Colwill, who showed excellent awareness to square the ball across the penalty area towards Brooks.

The Bournemouth winger made no mistake, meeting the pass with a first-time finish that flew across Costa and into the far corner to make it 1-1.
