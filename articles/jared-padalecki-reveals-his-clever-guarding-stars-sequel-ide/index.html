Jared Padalecki Reveals His Clever 'Guarding Stars' Sequel Idea

TheTools · 19.11.2026 20:13 · source · permalink

Exclusive Interview

We never thought we’d see the day when Sam Winchester needed a bodyguard.

On November 18, Supernatural alum Jared Padalecki swapped his signature jacket for scrubs and a Christmas hat, bringing action star Jack Stapleton from Katherine Center’s The Bodyguard to life in Netflix’s adaptation, Guarding Stars. While it may be hard to believe, this was his first rom-com leading role.

Padalecki told Swooon that it was his wife and co-producer, Genevieve Padalecki, who pitched both the movie and him for the role to the author herself. While he was thrilled to bring another iconic character to life, Padalecki admitted, “I get really nervous trying to play a comedic character.”

He added, “I’m not lying. I’ve been doing this for 26 years. It’s kind of nerve-wracking when you’re going to go into a scene, and as a producer, you put yourself in the eyes of a future viewer like, this needs to be kind of cute, or kind of funny, or kind of silly, [or] kind of goofy. But I try to just find my foundation [and] make it true to Jack. The words alone are funny enough.”

Netflix

While Padalecki was part of the project from the get-go, Padalecki explained that Gossip Girl star Leighton Meester, the Hannah Brooks to his Jack Stapleton, joined at the last minute. “She was in the process of filming like her third movie in a row, and we wanted her badly,” Padalecki revealed. However, they knew that production had to take place at a specific time so there would be real snow in Calgary while they filmed.

Fortunately, Padalecki explained that “three weeks, maybe not even” before they were set to film, her team confirmed that she was “legally available.” He added, “When we got the phone call that she said, ‘Yes,’ we all had a Zoom party.”

Just a few weeks later, Padalecki and Meester made it to Calgary to film one of the book’s most iconic scenes: Hannah flipping Jack flat on his back moments after they meet. As viewers know, Jack Stapleton doesn’t do his own stunts. Similarly, Padalecki admitted, “I’m older than I used to be during Supernatural, when [I was] jumping through windows and stuff like that.”

So, no, that isn’t actually Blair Waldorf flipping Sam Winchester on a tarmac. However, they did film the final bit of him sitting up and her slamming him onto the ground multiple times. “That was awesome,” he told Swooon. “It was fun to shoot. It was super cold, but it kind of kept us awake. [It was] long days, cold days, dry air, and we were out in the middle of nowhere, but we were having fun doing it.”

In fact, Padalecki “loved playing the character” so much, he would “totally be open to a sequel.” Of course, when asked what the sequel could be about, he explained, “I’d leave it to Katherine Center, who’s the genius behind the book, The Bodyguard.”

That said, he teased, “I think it’d be funny to role-reverse the role reversal. Like [Hannah] has to do something that’s well out of her comfort zone that she doesn’t want to do, and [Jack has] to serve some purpose for her, and I probably mess most of it up, and she’d have to clean up the mess.”
