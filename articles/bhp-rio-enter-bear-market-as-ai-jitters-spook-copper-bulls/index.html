BHP, Rio enter bear market as AI jitters spook copper bulls

TheTools · 19.11.2026 05:00 · source · permalink

The rapid unravelling of the global artificial intelligence trade has unleashed carnage across metals markets, prompting even the most bullish investors to rush for the exits and intensifying a sell-off in the world’s largest mining companies.

Prices of copper – a major beneficiary of the AI build-out because it carries the electricity powering data centres – had gradually fallen about 10 per cent from their September peak as traders scaled back their positions spooked by the wild price swings in the US technology sector.
