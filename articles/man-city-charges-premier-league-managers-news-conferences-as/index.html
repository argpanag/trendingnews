  4. What the managers are saying about the Man City verdictpublished at 15:47 BST
    15:47 BST

    It comes as no surprise that most of the Premier League managers speaking today were asked about Man City being found guilty. They've all kept very tight-lipped about their personal opinions but weren't shy talking about it.

    • Coventry manager Frank Lampard: "What can you say when an appeal is going on? I'm not inside the story. They are a huge football club now, a huge squad of quality players, so we will see. I'm outside the loop. It's not my role to talk about a punishment or how much."
    • Leeds boss Daniel Farke: "It makes no sense if right now everyone wants to have an opinion about this topic. There are more qualified people to talk about this. It's nothing to do with me, I was not involved in the title race during those years. This is not my topic. Football is my topic."
    • Bournemouth manager Marco Rose: "I trust in the guys who are involved in these kinds of things and situations and then we will see what happens. I'm a football coach not a politician and I love to be a football coach and it should stay like this."
    • Newcastle manager Matthias Jaissle: "It is not in my job description to judge on that. The authorities are taking care of it and there is a clear process now which will be followed and speaking on behalf of the whole club, it is the right approach to focus on what we we can do better and what we can influence on the pitch."
    • Sunderland boss Regis Le Bris was not asked about Man City.
