See the Lingerie-Inspired Looks Megan Thee Stallion, Tate McRae, Katseye, and Megan Moroney Wore on the Victoria’s Secret Runway

TheTools · 19.10.2026 00:23 · source · permalink

SCREAM!

Megan Thee Stallion, Tate McRae, Katseye, and Megan Moroney performed during the 2026 Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show, and all of them wore killer lingerie-inspired looks down the runway.

Megan Moroney kicked off the show wearing a pair of fluffy pink wings that she paired with a glittering white corset and miniskirt, not to mention a garter for good measure.

Tate was up next and wore a pair of hot red underwear paired with a corset, matching garters, and red heels. Even her mic was red! The commitment.

Meanwhile, Katseye showed up for the PINK section of the runway wearing matching mini skirts and corsets and I cannot emphasize how much I need them.

And finally, Megan Thee Stallion closed out the show wearing one of the most fun looks of the night: silver wings, a lacy bra top, a black thong, and sparkly stockings. Like, she looks amazing; there are no words.

All in all an iconic night!
