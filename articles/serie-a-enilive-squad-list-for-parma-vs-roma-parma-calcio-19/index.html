SERIE A ENILIVE | SQUAD LIST FOR PARMA VS. ROMA

Collecchio, 20 November 2026 - Alberto Gilardino has named a 25-man squad for Parma vs. Roma on Serie A Enilive Matchday 12, kicking off tomorrow at 18:00 CET at the Stadio Ennio Tardini.

Defenders
