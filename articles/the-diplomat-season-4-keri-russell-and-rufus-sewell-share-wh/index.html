Keri Russell doesn’t always watch her own projects, but The Diplomat is one she definitely makes an exception for.

“This I actually do watch, I love it so much,” Russell, 50, told Today.

Keri Russell and Rufus Sewell play husband and wife Kate and Hal Wyler in The Diplomat. Netflix

She’s not the only one.

Ahead of the political thriller’s highly anticipated fourth season, Russell and co-star Rufus Sewell sat down with superfan Richard Wilkins, who said it was his favourite show on television.

The leads said that just like viewers, the show’s dramatic turns are revealed to them little by little as their scripts “trickled out” slowly – and they promised an impressive season ahead.

Find out what to expect, and their true feelings about their character’s very messy marriage, by watching the full interview above.
