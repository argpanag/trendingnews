Thousands eligible for little-known social welfare payment with no set rate

TheTools · 19.09.2026 04:00 · source · permalink

Need to know

The Additional Needs Payment from the Department of Social Protection has no maximum limit, but eligibility criteria apply

There's a little-known one-off payment that households could be entitled to claim.

The Department of Social Protection provides this payment, which has no upper limit, and there are two requirements you must satisfy to receive it.

The Additional Needs Payment is intended to help cover an expense that cannot be met from your weekly income or other sources, and it has no maximum ceiling.

The payment was introduced to supplement the income of individuals struggling to make ends meet when their weekly income does not cover the spiralling expenses of late.

Here's what you need to know about the Additional needs payment and how to qualify.

  • With September bringing back-to-school costs and increasing energy bills, many Irish households are experiencing financial pressure.
  • A lesser-known Additional Needs Payment could offer vital assistance for families receiving social welfare or those on modest incomes. This Supplementary Welfare Allowance helps meet essential expenditure that cannot be covered through weekly earnings or savings.
  • The payment is accessible to anyone in receipt of social welfare or on low incomes, though it covers only essential costs. There is no fixed rate, with sums determined by household income and the nature of support needed.
  • You might qualify for an Additional Needs Payment if you are:Getting a social welfare payment, or Working and on a low income, regardless of the number of hours that you work
  • Applicants must supply thorough financial information regarding their household. They need to submit particulars of income, outgoings, savings and investments for all residents, reports Galway Beo.
  • Documentation requirements include payslips, bank statements, utility bills and repair quotations. Personal particulars, evidence of requirements, and payment information must also be supplied.
  • Once you apply for an Additional Needs Payment, a Community Welfare Officer (CWO) will assess your case. You'll be required to supply information about your weekly household income, expenses, savings and investments, along with those of other household members. You'll also need to demonstrate evidence of the support you're seeking.
  • Complete application details are available at mywelfare.ie.
