    [SINGAPORE] The following companies saw new developments that may affect trading of their securities on Thursday (Nov 19):

    Frencken : The technology manufacturer announced on Wednesday (Nov 18) a 31.3 per cent year-on-year increase in earnings to S$13 million for its third quarter ended Sep 30. Revenue grew 12.8 per cent to S$238.5 million, from S$211.5 million a year earlier. The increase was mainly driven by Frencken’s mechatronics division’s revenue, which rose 13.4 per cent to S$214.3 million. The counter ended S$0.01, or 0.6 per cent, up at S$1.71 before the news.
