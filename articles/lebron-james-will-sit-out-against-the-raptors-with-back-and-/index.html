LeBron James will sit out against the Raptors with back and sciatica management

TheTools · 12.11.2026 23:31 · source · permalink

James will miss his third game of the season but it’ll be the first that isn’t part of a back-to-back set.

    LeBron James will be out for the 76ers’ matchup against the Raptors on Thursday, the team said.

    James will miss his third game of the season because of low back and sciatica management, which also caused him to miss two previous games. This is the first game James will miss that wasn’t part of a back-to-back set of games, and will give him four days of rest before a 1 p.m. start Sunday against Indiana, should he return for that game.

    In 10 games this season, James is averaging 15.1 points, 7.2 rebounds and 6.5 assists per game.

    Joel Embiid was also listed on the team’s injury report as questionable because of right foot plantar fascia management, and said at shootaround “we’re going to see” when asked if he would play Thursday. Embiid has also missed two games this season and finished Tuesday’s loss to Boston despite limping off the floor in the first half because of what he said was an “ankle turn.”

    The Sixers also provided updates on a pair of injured frontcourt players, Adem Bona and Dominick Barlow. Bona hasn’t played since Nov. 3 after suffering a cervical spine sprain late in a win over the Pistons. He will be re-evaluated in one week. Barlow participated in shootaround as he recovers from a low back stress reaction that has kept him out since training camp, but the Sixers say there is no timeline for Barlow’s return.
