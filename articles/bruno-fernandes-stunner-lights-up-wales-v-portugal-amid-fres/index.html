Bruno Fernandes scored a stunning goal for 10-man Portugal in their 3-2 win against Wales in the UEFA Nations League as speculation continues to surround the Manchester United captain’s future amid reported interest from Serie A.

Fernandes – wearing the captain’s armband – opened the scoring in Cardiff after just 11 minutes on Wednesday (AEDT), curling a superb effort past goalkeeper Danny Ward after Joao Neves dispossessed Josh Sheehan.

But Wales responded almost immediately through David Brooks before Sorba Thomas completed the turnaround with a brilliant goal in the 40th minute.

Wales’ lead was short-lived, however, as Portugal equalised within three minutes via Pedro Neto to make it 2-2 at half-time before substitute Carlos Forbs scored the winner 20 minutes from the end.

Portugal played the remaining 16 minutes of regulation, plus stoppage time, with 10 men following Neto’s red card.

Fernandes’ moment of brilliance, though, came at an intriguing time for the United star, whose long-term future at Old Trafford remains uncertain despite his decision to reject lucrative opportunities elsewhere.

Bruno Fernandes transfer latest: AC Milan and Juventus interest

Fernandes’ future is firmly in the spotlight after reportedly rejecting a second contract offer from Manchester United.

According to Fabrizio Romano and Italian transfer journalist Matteo Moretto, Fernandes attracted serious interest from Serie A giants AC Milan and Juventus the last summer transfer window, but United blocked any potential departure.

The Portugal international also turned down more lucrative offers from Turkish giants Galatasaray and Saudi Pro League powerhouse Al-Hilal.

However, his decision to remain at Old Trafford has not ended speculation surrounding his future.

Fernandes is contracted to United until June 2027, with the club holding an option to extend his agreement by a further year.

His existing deal reportedly includes a €65million ($106million AUD) release clause, while negotiations over a new contract have been ongoing for months without an agreement.

The midfielder is understood to be weighing up his options following United’s disappointing start to the Premier League season, with the club’s sporting direction expected to influence his decision.

The arrival of Mauricio Pochettino as head coach could prove significant, particularly with further changes potentially on the horizon at Old Trafford.

Meanwhile, Italian interest remains, with Milan and Juventus continuing to be linked with the midfielder.

And the idea of returning to Italy, where he played for Udinese previously, reportedly “appeals” to Fernandes.
