1. FollowersPanda

FollowersPanda takes the top spot in this guide because it brings several useful stream controls together. You can manage how viewers join, pause delivery when needed, and adjust raid and poll settings from its panel.

Its key features include:

  • Instant Viewers: Delivery can begin after you place an order for your live stream.
  • Pause & Resume: Pause or restart viewers and chatters from the panel.
  • Interval Control: Set how quickly viewers or chatters appear. The feature panel shows a suggested range of 60–300 seconds.
  • Raid Followup: Supports follow-on raids when the channel you raid goes on to raid another streamer.
  • Poll Participation: Enable or disable viewer participation in stream polls.
  • 24/7 Premium Support: Get help through live chat and email.

Raid Followup deserves special attention. It is designed for the next step in a raid chain, such as when you raid a friend and that friend later raids another channel. Check the available setting and package limits before planning the full chain.

2. BoostHill

BoostHill lets you choose a viewer count and stream duration. Its product page lists automatic connection when you go live, plus options to adjust joining speed, pause or resume delivery, and switch poll participation on or off.

For raids, BoostHill lists a specific add-on that supports taking viewers through 1–3 raids. Make sure that add-on is part of your order if raids are the main reason you are buying.

The timed package format makes it useful to compare costs for a set broadcast window, such as a guest session or gaming event.

Best fit: Streamers who want a set delivery period and a clearly listed raid add-on.

What to Check Before Choosing a Raid Package

Compare these details before placing your order:

  • Raid coverage: Does it support the first raid, follow-on raids, or both?
  • Duration: How long does delivery continue after a raid?
  • Viewer controls: Can you pause delivery and change joining speed?
  • Chat options: Are chatters included, optional, or a separate order?
  • Auto connect: Will delivery start when your channel goes live?
  • Total cost: Compare the same viewer count, duration, and raid extras.
