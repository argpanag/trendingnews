Office Decorating Competition Returns for Homecoming Season | UToledo News

TheTools · 21.09.2026 07:45 · source · permalink

The Campus Activities and Programming Board will again host the Office Decorating Competition for UToledo faculty, staff and administration to celebrate our Homecoming spirit with students.

The deadline to sign up for participation in this year’s Homecoming Office Decorating Competition is Sept. 28. Offices must be decorated by Oct. 2.

This year’s theme is Olympus Unleashed: Rockets Reign.

The deadline to sign up for participation is Monday, Sept. 28. Offices must be decorated by Friday, Oct. 2. Judging will take place on Monday, Oct. 5, and Tuesday, Oct. 6.  The Campus Activities and Programming Board will provide additional information to participating offices on the judging criteria and when judging for their area will occur.

Winners will be announced during the Homecoming game on Saturday, Oct. 10, with a follow-up UToledo News story the week after Homecoming. The winning office will be awarded a free lunch for their entire team and second place will receive a plaque.

To sign up for the competition, complete the participation form via InvoNet.
