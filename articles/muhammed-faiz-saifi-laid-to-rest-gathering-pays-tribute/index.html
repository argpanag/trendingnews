Muhammed Faiz Saifi Laid to Rest, Gathering Pays Tribute

TheTools · 03.09.2026 17:42 · source · permalink

09-03-2026     3 رجب 1440

New Delhi, Sep 3: Muhammed Faiz Saifi, Director of Saifi Hospitals and son of Mohammad Shakeel Saifi, Chairman of World Peace Harmony, was laid to rest on Thursday, with family members, friends, colleagues and associates gathering to pay their final respects.

The last rites of Faiz Saifi were performed at 5:00 PM at RZT 100, Saifi Mahal, where a gathering of people assembled to bid farewell to him.

Those present expressed grief over his untimely demise and conveyed condolences to his bereaved family. Several people remembered Faiz Saifi for his professional association with Saifi Hospitals and his social engagements.

The gathering offered prayers for the departed soul and expressed solidarity with Mohammad Shakeel Saifi and other members of the family during this difficult time.

The news of Faiz Saifi’s passing earlier in the day had left his family, friends, colleagues and associates deeply saddened.

May the departed soul rest in peace, and may the bereaved family be granted strength and solace to bear the loss.
