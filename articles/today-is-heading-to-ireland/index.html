TODAY Is Heading to Ireland!

TheTools · 10.09.2026 11:16 · source · permalink

TODAY is packing its bags and taking the show across the pond! In a special TODAY extravaganza, NBC is taking the entire show on the road to broadcast LIVE from Ireland on a one-of-a-kind adventure.

On Friday, Oct. 16, all the TODAY hosts, alongside a few special guests and friends of the show, will broadcast live from the beautiful Sheen Falls Lodge in County Kerry, Ireland. You’ll see Savannah Guthrie, Craig Melvin, Al Roker, Carson Daly, Dylan Dreyer, Jenna Bush Hager and Sheinelle Jones as they travel across the island to take in the incredible views, sample local food and drink, and meet the amazing people of Ireland. The show is sponsored by Tourism Ireland, and the venue is sponsored by Sheen Falls Lodge.

Whether you are an Irish local or a fan here in America, we want to see you there! Join the whole crew in experiencing all that Ireland has to offer. Space is limited, so secure your place to attend this free live broadcast by registering now.
