Nicholas Herring wins the $100K Giller Prize for novel Your Breath in Charcoal | CBC

TheTools · 17.11.2026 03:31 · source · permalink

The Latest

  • P.E.I. author Nicholas Herring is the winner of the 2026 Giller Prize.
  • Five Canadian books were up for the $100,000 Giller Prize, the biggest in Canadian fiction.
  • The shortlisted authors were Tara Gereaux, Michelle Good, Nicholas Herring, Nazanine Hozar and Patmeena Sabit.
