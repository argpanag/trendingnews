Man, 80, and boy, 16, killed in plane crash

TheTools · 17.10.2026 08:22 · source · permalink

An 80-year-old man and a 16-year-old boy have died in a plane crash in Scotland.

Police Scotland said officers were deployed at around 4.45pm on Friday following reports of a light aircraft crash north east of Drymen.

A 16-year-old boy and an 80-year-old man were pronounced dead by emergency services at the scene.

A spokesman for Police Scotland said the Air Accidents Investigation Branch had been informed, and inquiries were ongoing to establish the full circumstances of the incident.

The flight is understood to have taken off from Glasgow airport on Friday.

Temporary airspace restrictions are now in place over the Drymen area.
