Punjab CM Mann launches Smart Ration Card scheme

TheTools · 26.11.2026 19:20 · source · permalink

Chandigarh: Punjab Chief Minister Bhagwant Singh Mann on Thursday launched the Smart Ration Card Scheme to provide food security benefits to 10 lakh deserving families, asserting that the selection process was transparent and free from political interference.

Along with AAP national convenor Arvind Kejriwal, Mann distributed ‘Meri Rasoi’ ration kits containing pulses, sugar, salt, turmeric and mustard oil to beneficiaries.

The Chief Minister said 19.25 lakh online applications were received during the special campaign launched in July. Applications were submitted through Sewa Kendras, Common Service Centres and online platforms, followed by door-to-door verification by district administrations.

He said the selection was conducted digitally under the principle of ‘No Cash, No Farmaish’, ensuring that only genuinely needy families received benefits.

Under the scheme, beneficiaries will receive 2 kg pulses, 2 kg sugar, 1 kg salt, 200 grams turmeric and one litre mustard oil. They will also get 5 kg wheat per member under the National Food Security Act.
