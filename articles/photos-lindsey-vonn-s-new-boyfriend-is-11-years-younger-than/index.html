Lindsey Vonn hard-launched her new boyfriend on social media on Wednesday afternoon, September 23.

The U.S. Winter Olympics legend hard-launched her relationship with French skier Matthieu Bailet. Vonn, 41, is officially dating Bailet, 30. Bailet, a downhill skier, made his World Cup debut in March 2016 at St. Moritz, Switzerland. He competed at the Winter Olympics in 2022.

"About that time for a summer recap… pt 1❤️," Vonn wrote on Instagram on Wednesday, hard-launching the relationship.

Bailet confirmed: "Can't wait to see the summer recap part 2 😉😘."

Top photos of Lindsey Vonn's new boyfriend

Bailet has more than 17,000 followers on Instagram – and he's likely to see his profile rise in the days to come, following the official relationship launch.

"Putting the pieces together. 🧩 Ski after ski. Run after run. Testing, adjusting, learning. A lot of work goes into finding those little details that make the difference. Chile camp 🇨🇱," he shared on Instagram earlier this week.

"Some really good days down here. 🇨🇱 Good training, good work and a lot of fun along the way. It just feels so good to be back in this routine, doing what I love and getting ready for the season ahead. ❄️," he added last week.

The French skier spends most of his time on the mountain, though he's an avid cyclist, as well.

The couple has been together for several months, at least.

"In May, an exclusive source told PEOPLE that the pair were 'getting to know each other' after they were spotted together in New York City," PEOPLE reported this week.

Vonn, who suffered a catastrophic injury at the 2026 Winter Olympics, has been making a strong recovery in recent months, and she's been enjoying her new boyfriend over the summer.
