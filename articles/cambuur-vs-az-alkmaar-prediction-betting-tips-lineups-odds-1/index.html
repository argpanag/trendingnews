Cambuur vs AZ Alkmaar Preview

Cambuur welcome AZ Alkmaar to Kooi Stadium, with kick-off set for 07:30 on Sunday 18 October.

Cambuur triumphed in their last game which was away from home. PEC Zwolle were beaten 1-0 at Mac3Park Stadion. They had four shots on target and boasted 54% possession, with a late strike from Nicolas Binder proving the difference.

AZ Alkmaar were beaten in their previous Eredivisie game and this was away from home at De Kuip. Feyenoord claimed a 2-0 win. The Cheeseheads enjoyed 57% possession and had one shot on goal.

Three days ago, AZ won 2-1 at home in the Europa League against Hapoel Be'er-Sheva.

H2H: AZ 2-1 Win in Previous Head-to-Head Encounter

AZ have claimed back-to-back victories over Cambuur. A 2-1 home success at AFAS Stadion was preceded by a 1-0 away win at Cambuur Stadion. Aside from one draw, AZ have dominated the previous 4 H2H meetings with three wins.

Cambuur have 2 wins, 4 losses and 2 draws, averaging 1.4 goals from 4.1 shots on goal and 11.6 attempts. Johan Plat's side have averaged 47.3% possession, 380.1 passes and 3.9 corners per match, with their opponents getting 2.4 goals from 6.0 shots on goal, 15.5 attempts and 5.8 corners.

Rafik El Arguioui is the top scorer with 4 goals, while Ilyes Hamache has 3 and Nicolas Binder 2. Nicolas Binder has the most assists with 2, while goalie Thijs Jansen has recorded 2 clean sheets.

AZ have 6 victories, 1 defeat and 3 draws, with an average of 2.2 goals from 6.7 shots on goal and 16.8 attempts. The Alkmaar outfit have averaged 58.9% possession, 528.2 passes, 7.6 corners awarded and 4.3 corners against, while they’ve conceded 1.2 goals from 3.9 shots on goal and 9.6 attempts.

Mexx Meerdink and Ro-Zangelo Daal have 4 and they’re top of the team’s scoring charts, with Ayoub Oufkir on 2. Mateo Chavez has clocked up 3 assists, with Jari De Busser keeping 3 clean sheets in the last 10 games.
