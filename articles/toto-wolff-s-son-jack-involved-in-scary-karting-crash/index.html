Jack Wolff, son of Mercedes team boss Toto Wolff, is just starting to make a name for himself in karting. However, things went wrong during a race at the Leopard Circuit Viterbo in Italy when he was involved in a crash, and another kart flew over him. Fortunately, the nine-year-old driver walked away unharmed. He was able to get out of the kart on his own and, after some medical checks, he even said he wants to race again on Saturday.
