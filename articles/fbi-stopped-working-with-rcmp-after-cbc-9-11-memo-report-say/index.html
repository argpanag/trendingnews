Scott Stinson: Doug Ford says he’s not endorsing Olivia Chow for mayor, and if it sounds like he did, he’s taking it back

On Wednesday, when asked for his thoughts on the Toronto mayoral race, the Ontario premier responded, “We’re going to continue supporting the mayor. She’s doing an incredible job. So, read between the lines.” Stinson writes that “by Friday morning, Ford had found a predictable source for the confusion: the media, which had incorrectly interpreted his words as meaning what they are generally accepted to mean.”

John Ivison: Repairing Trudeau’s salmon-industry sabotage is next on Carney’s agenda

The subject of farmed salmon will be high on the agenda when Norway’s prime minister meets with Mark Carney in Ottawa next week, Ivison says. The problem is an “extremely dubious” ban on open net salmon farms enacted by former PM Justin Trudeau, which “was a death sentence for an industry that employs thousands of people” and that Norwegian companies have made significant investments in. “The existing plan is a staggering level of economic self-sabotage, and it should be reversed,” Ivison states.

Ontario trucker sent to prison in U.S. for hauling cocaine allegedly for Ryan Wedding’s cartel

The transport truck owner-operator based in Brampton, Ont., was handed a nine-year sentence after admitting to a Los Angeles judge that he helped knit a drug transportation network in Canada to an immense cocaine cartel in Mexico and the U.S. that was allegedly run by former Canadian Olympian Ryan Wedding.
