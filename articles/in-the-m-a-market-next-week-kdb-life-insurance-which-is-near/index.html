In the M&A market next week, KDB Life Insurance, which is nearing the end of the preferred bidder (friendship) period, and JTBC, which is about to announce the sale, are expected to attract attention.

The sale of KDB Life Insurance Co., Ltd. is set to end its friendship period with Korea Investment Finance Holdings Co., Ltd. It is known that the once-extended cooperative period will end again next week.

The two sides are engaged in a war of nerves over the scale of capital expansion. It is explained that negotiations are struggling as differences are not narrowed even during the extended period. As the positions of the two sides are parallel, some in the investment banking (IB) industry are cautiously predicting that the KDB life deal is likely to fail.

In August, the Korea Development Bank selected Korea Investment Corporation as the KDB Life Friendship Association. It is the first negotiation table made after the seventh attempt to sell it since 2014. All six previous sales were canceled. KDB Life Insurance needs to strengthen additional capital as its K-ICS ratio before applying transitional measures at the end of the first quarter of this year was 75.5%, far below the authorities' recommendation of 130%.

JTBC will announce the sale on the 9th and begin the process of selling the open competitive bidding method. The announcement is the first step toward the sale of JTBC.

According to the IB industry, about 10 companies, including the original buyers of the JoongAng Ilbo, are reportedly interested in acquiring JTBC. In addition, Taekwang Group and Hoban Group are also likely to seek participation. As the number of original buyers continues even before the announcement, expectations for the success of the bid are growing.

JTBC has been pushing for M&A before approval since the Seoul Rehabilitation Court decided to start the rehabilitation process in August. The court allowed EY Hanyoung to be selected as the sales manager. JTBC's corporate value, which is discussed in the market, is estimated to be around 400 billion won.

The deadline for submitting the rehabilitation plan is Jan. 29. JTBC plans to conduct the main bidding in mid-January after a preliminary audit for about a month from December 7th.
