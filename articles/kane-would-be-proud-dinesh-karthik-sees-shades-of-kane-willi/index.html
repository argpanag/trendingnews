"Kane Would Be Proud": Dinesh Karthik Sees Shades of Kane Williamson in Henry Nicholls' Masterclass

TheTools · 19.11.2026 09:06 · source · permalink

Dinesh Karthik stated that Kane Williamson would be proud of Henry Nicholls after watching his game against India as he saw shades of Kane in Henry during his masterclass on Day 1 in Wellington.

Former Indian wicketkeeper batter Dinesh Karthik praised Kiwi batter Henry Nicholls for his masterclass against India on Day 1 of 1st Test and also saw shades of former Kiwi skipper Kane Williamson in him. He stated that Kane would be proud of Nicholls. Karthik also added that Nicholls’ played in a very Kane Williamson by leaving deliveries, playing it late and some backfoot punches.

Speaking on Cricbuzz, Karthik elaborated on the innings of Nicholls. “But I think Kane would be proud looking at how well Henry Nicholls played today. Although he averages very poor against India up to today, he was able to make merry in a ground where he is comfortable playing. Scored 100 in Wellington,” he said.

“I remember him saying those are two bigger shoes to fill when he replaced Kane Williamson. But he has filled those shoes very nicely so far. It was a very Kane Williamson kind of knock if you say. In the first session, he survived and left a lot of balls, went close to the ball, played it late, the backfoot punches, the guide through the third man. Very Kane Williamson. Only thing is it is inverted with a left-handed batter doing it,” Karthik added.

“Exactly the template they followed”: Karthik

Dinesh Karthik highlighted the template Nicholls and Phillips followed while batting. “In the middle phase, they always say try and survive the first session, slightly more confidence in the second and make merry in the third. That's exactly the template they followed. They tried to survive but they lost wickets through the middle. We are talking about Wellington. It was quite warm today by their standards. The pitch did heat out and got much better to bat on. But for Henry Nicholls and Glenn Phillips to build their partnership was important. Henry Nicholls is a great story,” he said.

“He is someone who was dropped from the team, went back to domestic cricket and has piled on the runs. And they had to pick him because the amount of runs he got and he came to England, he did well there batting at number 3 in those conditions is never easy. The fact that he survived and then he eventually got the fruit of what he did in the first session in the backend of the session where he was able to get runs and get past his hundred. He is in a very comfortable place right now. Glenn Phillips on the other hand is extremely talented and what a knock he has played again while getting them out of trouble and the confidence and stroke making, the counter attack he showed is very impressive.,” Karthik added.
