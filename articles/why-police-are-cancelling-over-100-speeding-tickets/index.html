Why police are cancelling over 100 speeding tickets

TheTools · 17.11.2026 03:34 · source · permalink

More than 100 speeding tickets issued on a Queenstown road over two-and-a-half years are being cancelled and refunded after it emerged the 60km/h limit drivers were caught breaching had never been legally approved.

The infringements were issued between May 14, 2024 and November 11, 2026 to motorists caught exceeding the posted 60km/h limit on Gorge Road, between Industrial Place and the north end of Matakauri Park.

Director of road policing Inspector Peter McKennie said the error happened because NZ Transport Agency Waka Kotahi had not yet officially approved the 60km/h signs.

"The current legal speed limit for this section of road remains at 80km/h," he said.

Police said cancellation letters were expected to go out to affected drivers this week. Any of the infringements that had been referred to the Ministry of Justice will also be cancelled.

McKennie said officers had enforced the posted limit in good faith.

"We apologise to all those affected, for any inconvenience this error has caused."
