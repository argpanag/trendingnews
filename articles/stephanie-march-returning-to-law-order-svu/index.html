Stephanie March Returning To 'Law & Order: SVU'

TheTools · 23.10.2026 20:30 · source · permalink

Breaking News

Law & Order: SVU fans will reconnect with a familiar face from the past in Season 28.

Stephanie March will reprise the role of Alexandra Cabot in the 9th episode of the season. The last time March appeared on the Law & Order spinoff series was in Season 19’s 19th episode titled “Sunk Cost Fallacy.” Upon her return, it will have been 8 years since March appeared on the series.

Starring Mariska Hargitay as Olivia Benson and her second-in-command, Ice-T as Sgt. Odafin “Fin” Tutuola, the procedural chronicles the lives of the Special Victims Unit of the New York City Police Department, an elite squad of detectives who investigate crimes of sexual assault, child abuse, and domestic violence. Kelli Giddish, Peter Scanavino, Kevin Kane, Aimé Donna Kelly and Corey Cott also star.

Law & Order: SVU airs Thursdays at 9 p.m. ET/PT on NBC and the next day on Peacock.

Watch on Deadline

Dick Wolf, Michele Fazekas, Mariska Hargitay, Kenneth Brown, Rebecca McGill, Anastasia Puglisi and Peter Jankowski executive produce. Law & Order: Special Victims Unit is produced by Universal Television, a division of Universal Studio Group, in association with Wolf Entertainment.

In addition to her work on SVU, March’s credits include the Ricky Gervais comedy The Invention of Lying, Matthew Newton’s indie drama Who We Are Now and the mockumentary The Social Ones, which she also produced. For the small screen, March most recently recurred on Ava DuVernay’s series Naomi and collaborated again with DuVernay in her feature film, Origin. She also appeared in Harlan Coben’s Shelter for Amazon. March is repped by Paradigm and Navigation Media Group.
