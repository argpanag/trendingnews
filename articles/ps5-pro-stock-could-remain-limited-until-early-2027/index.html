PS5 Pro Stock Could Remain Limited Until Early 2027

TheTools · 18.09.2026 18:40 · source · permalink

According to German publication Playfront.de, which cites an alleged message sent by Sony to distributors, there seems to be a possibility that PS5 Pro will continue to have limited availability for part of 2027.

The console already has lower stock levels compared with other models, to the point that it is often sold out at retailers and also on Sony’s official store, PlayStation Direct, and the situation does not seem likely to improve in the near future.

PS5 Pro Availability Could Continue to Be Limited Even in 2027

According to the Playfront.de (thanks PlayStation Lifestyle), the company appears to have warned that the limited availability is expected to continue “through early 2027 at the earliest.”

The situation will become more difficult especially in the coming months: autumn is a particularly busy period for purchases, and above all, GTA 6 is coming in November, which will probably also push console sales.

For some time, retailers have warned about the possibility that both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S could be difficult to find right when GTA 6 is released, and this issue also seems to be supported by this rumor.

Considering that PS5 Pro could also be the platform where the Rockstar Games title should look its best, given the greater power of the hardware, it is easy to expect that it could be especially popular this autumn.

However, with the memory and general hardware crisis, it is reasonable to expect some difficulty in getting enough stock, despite Sony saying that materials had already been reserved in large quantities.
