Will he play? Ascacíbar trains alone on eve of Copa Sudamericana final

Santiago Ascacibar set alarm bells ringing at Boca after training separately during the final session before the continental final. However, the club has moved to reassure fans, insisting that the midfielder will start the decisive Copa Sudamericana clash against Atlético Mineiro.

In the final session before the most important match of the second half of the year, Ruso did light fitness work in the gym under the supervision of the physiotherapists. The coaching staff’s decision was purely a precaution to prevent muscle overload ahead of an exceptionally demanding match. The blue-and-gold club’s medical team therefore prioritized protecting the player’s fitness on the eve of the clash with the Brazilian side.

The physical issue prompting this special care is nothing new, as Ascacíbar has been nursing discomfort in one knee since the match against Defensa y Justicia. Despite that joint discomfort, the former Estudiantes de La Plata player has shown remarkable resilience to remain available for the final stretch of the season.

Santiago Ascacíbar training in Barranquilla. Photo: Javier Martino/CABJ Press Office.

In fact, that same physical issue did not stop him from playing a key role in the recent Copa Argentina semifinal against Banfield. In that match, the holding midfielder played the full ninety minutes, delivering an outstanding performance in the middle of the park. He also opened the scoring in what ended as an emphatic 3–0 victory to secure a place in the final.

With that recent performance to draw on, all signs suggest the coaching staff will be able to count on his usual balance and energy in central midfield. The squad is already focused on the crucial continental clash, where every physical and tactical detail matters in the pursuit of glory. The midfielder’s presence is vital to the tactical setup the coaching staff is planning to disrupt the opposition’s passing game, alongside captain Leandro Paredes and the tireless Milton Delgado.
