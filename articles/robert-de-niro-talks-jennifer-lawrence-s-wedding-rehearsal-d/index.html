Robert De Niro talks Jennifer Lawrence's wedding rehearsal dinner

TheTools · 20.11.2026 19:48 · source · permalink

Robert De Niro has confirmed Jennifer Lawrence told him to leave her 2019 wedding rehearsal dinner during a playful lie detector test with his Focker-in-Law co-stars Ben Stiller and Ariana Grande.

In 2024, Lawrence revealed that she had whispered 'go home' to De Niro at the rehearsal dinner because she saw him wandering around a room full of total strangers and worried he didn't want to be there.

On Thursday, the 83-year-old acting legend denied having a 'resting b***h face,' as she had described it, or being unhappy at Lawrence's elegant affair.

Vanity Fair features celebrity polygraph-style tests regularly as a recurring video series, and all three stars willingly took part in the shenanigans to promote the new film.

De Niro, who held hands with his girlfriend Tiffany Chen at the Focker-in-Law UK premiere, had his turn on the hot seat and seemed to take it in stride as he was hooked up to the sensors.

The moment of truth came when Grande asked him the question: 'This famous actress said she asked you to leave her wedding [rehearsal dinner] after feeling worried you weren't having a good time.'

Was Jennifer Lawrence being a considerate host by encouraging Robert De Niro to leave?

'Yes,' De Niro said after a moment's thought.

Through giggles, Grande went on: 'Would you say you had an RBF - a "resting b***h face?"'

'No, I was actually having an okay time,' De Niro said. 'It was an interesting experience but there was nobody I knew there. I didn't know anybody.

'It was their party. It was a great place to be. It was one of these Upper East Side mansions turned into a museum,' he said about the venue.

De Niro shared that Lawrence had merely misread his mood and he appreciated her concern.

'It was nice that Jennifer was being very sensitive to my situation that I was not having a good time. But I was okay,' he said.

The lie detector operator nodded as indication that the Raging Bull star was telling the truth.

'Yeah, that's very honest,' Grande responded, while Stiller chimed in: 'I believe that.'

Lawrence, 36, married Cooke Maroney in October 2019 in a star-studded ceremony at the historic Belcourt of Newport Mansion in Rhode Island.

Four years later, during an interview with E! News, the actress recalled that being a bride was 'stressful' because she worried so much about hosting and making her guests feel comfortable.

She remembered telling De Niro he could head home if he felt out of place.

'I looked over and I saw Bob, who doesn't know anybody and he's kind of wandering around, and I immediately was like, "No, this isn't what he wants to be doing. I don't want him here,"' she told the outlet.

'So I went over and whispered, I was like, "Go home," and he was nice — he, like, talked to my parents and was polite — but I was like, "Go."'

De Niro did leave, and 'that just genuinely made me feel better,' Lawrence added.

In a conversation with People in 2024, De Niro said he was only at the rehearsal dinner for an hour or so. 'It wasn’t that long.'

De Niro and Lawrence have appeared in three films together, Silver Linings Playbook (2012), American Hustle (2013) and Joy (2015), all directed by David O Russell, and maintain a close and easygoing bond.

The Godfather II star, who has seven children across four relationships including three-year-old daughter Gia with current squeeze Chen, has described the Kentucky-born actress as a close friend who feels 'like a daughter' to him, per People.
