Updated DraftKings $200 Promo Code ($200 in Bonuses) for the 2026 NFL Season

Last Sunday, September 13, NFL Sundays officially returned to the calendar for the 2026 NFL regular season. To welcome back the football action, DraftKings currently offers a sign-up bonus for new users, which doesn’t require a DraftKings $200 promo code.

Before claiming this offer, though, there are a few important details to keep in mind, and this article will focus on them. Here, we’ll discuss what you get in free bets after meeting the eligibility requirements. We’ll also look at how these conditions apply to your location. At the end, you can follow our guide to grab your own bonus.

What does DraftKings currently have on offer?

With the 2026 NFL season already underway, the current DraftKings new-user bonus gives eligible customers $200 in free bets. To activate this promo, you have to make a qualifying $5 first wager. For NFL fans, that means you can use a Week 2 or upcoming NFL market as the qualifying bet. Based on our findings, you also don’t need to enter a promo code to be eligible.

However, we should point out that the $200 DraftKings sign-up bonus doesn’t come as a single amount added to your balance. Instead, the operator issues the reward as four $50 bonus bets spread out over 21 days. On the first day after creating an account, you’ll receive a $50 bonus credit. Then on Days 7, 14, and 21, you’ll receive $50 via click-to-claim. Keep in mind also that each free bet comes with a seven-day expiration period once claimed.

More on the $5 wager that triggers the $200 bonus bets

We liked that while there’s a qualifying wager, it’s a reasonable amount. DraftKings requires a minimum $5 bet, unlike some other US betting sites. So if you’re targeting an NFL game, you can choose from spreads, moneylines, totals, and player props. The result of this first wager is also irrelevant to receiving the reward. In other words, the qualifying bet can win or lose as long as it meets the promotion’s terms and settles. Once it settles, DraftKings begins the free bet distribution, which of course can’t be withdrawn as cash.

Before you sign up, make sure you meet these requirements

Although the DraftKings bet-$5-get-$200 promo is easy to claim, there are some conditions you must fulfill. Being a new customer is only the starting point, as you also need to be in an eligible location. Speaking of that, the bonus bets are only available to users in the following US regions: Arkansas, Arizona, Colorado, Connecticut, District of Columbia, Iowa, Illinois, Indiana, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana (only some parishes), Massachusetts, Maine, Michigan, Missouri, North Carolina, New Jersey, New Hampshire, New York, Ohio, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Puerto Rico (residents only), Tennessee, Virginia, Vermont, Wyoming, and West Virginia.

In addition to the above-mentioned locations, make sure you tick all of the following boxes, as discovered in our DraftKings review:

  • You’re a new customer who hasn’t previously used the platform
  • Meet the minimum betting age of 21 years old or the age specified in your location
  • Be physically located in a state or jurisdiction where DraftKings is available
  • Have a registered and verified account
  • Must have deposited and bet the qualifying $5 on an eligible market (could be NFL)
  • There’s only one qualifying wager per customer
  • Claim the offer within the promotional window. The offer runs from August 24th at 12 AM (ET) to September 20, 2026 at 11:59 AM (ET).
  • You must use the bonus credits within 7 days after claiming them, or else they’ll expire.

Step-by-step guide to claim and use the offer on DraftKings

Once you’ve confirmed that you reside in a supported region and you’re of the minimum age, the process is fairly straightforward. That said, below is a guide on grabbing the $200 bonus credits and how to use them:

  1. Visit the official DraftKings website and sign up within the promotional window
  2. At the top of the homepage, you’ll see the “Bet $5, Get $200” banner. Tap on the “Sign Up” button
  3. Fill in your email, username, password, mobile phone number, promo code, and click “Agree and Create account”
  4. Next, enter your full legal name, date of birth, SSN and all other requested information
  5. Once you’ve finalized the signup process, visit the “Rewards” tab if you’re not immediately redirected there
  6. Look for the offer and opt in
  7. Deposit and place at least $5 on an NFL market or any other sports event that satisfies the promo rules
  8. Allow the qualifying bet to settle before checking your available rewards
  9. You’ll receive the $200 as eight $25 credits over 21 days

How to use the promo to place bets

After you’ve received the first round of free credits, you can follow these steps to use them on any sports event. They come in handy if you’re new to US betting promos:

  1. Select an upcoming NFL matchup and add your preferred market to the bet slip
  2. Choose the bonus bet you want to use for this wager and enter the full amount
  3. Place the wager after confirming that the bonus bet has applied
  4. Track the result under “My Bets”
  5. If the wager wins, keep in mind that only the resulting winnings are credited to your cash balance. That is, the Bonus Bet stake is not part of the payout.
