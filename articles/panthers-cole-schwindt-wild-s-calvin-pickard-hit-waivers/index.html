Panthers' Cole Schwindt, Wild's Calvin Pickard hit waivers

TheTools · 24.11.2026 14:26 · source · permalink

Two notable players are up for grabs.

Florida Panthers forward Cole Schwindt and Minnesota Wild goalie Calvin Pickard were both placed on waivers on Tuesday, Sportsnet's Elliotte Friedman reported.

Meanwhile, goalie Remi Poirier was re-claimed by the Washington Capitals while Dallas Stars forward Sheldon Dries cleared.

Schwindt, 25, has been held without a point in 15 games for the Panthers this season after registering five goals and two assists in 29 contests for the club last season.

The Kitchener, Ont., native is a victim of the Panthers' roster crunch — which also saw fellow forward Cole Reinhardt waived and claimed by the Vancouver Canucks — as Aleksander Barkov and Brad Marchand get set to return from injuries.

Pickard, the veteran goalie, appeared in three games for the Wild, recording an .855 save percentage and 3.60 goals-against average. Over 194 career NHL games, he has marks of .900 and 3.60.

Poirier continues his tour around the NHL, which has also had stops with the New Jersey Devils and Dallas Stars this season. He has managed three AHL games with the Stars affiliate along the way, recording a .938 save percentage and 2.02 GAA.
