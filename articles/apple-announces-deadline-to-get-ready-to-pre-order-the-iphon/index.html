Apple Announces Deadline to 'Get Ready' to Pre-Order the iPhone Duo

TheTools · 15.10.2026 13:10 · source · permalink

Apple today announced that customers have until 9 p.m. Pacific Time today to "get ready" for their iPhone Duo pre-order.

By completing this process via Apple's online store or the Apple Store app, Apple said customers can "check out with a single click" when iPhone Duo pre-orders go live on Friday, October 16 at 5 a.m. Pacific Time. The device launches on Friday, October 23.
