Applause for Orangemen?

TheTools · 01.10.2026 21:44 · source · permalink

Sir, – Surely the smart response of the residents of Garvaghy Road to the renewal of the Orange Parade down their street would have been to turn out in numbers to applaud and cheer the marchers on.

This would have so baffled and depressed the Orangemen that they would have packed it in there and then. – Yours, etc,

JOHN DILLON,

Regius professor of Greek (Emeritus),

Trinity College Dublin.
