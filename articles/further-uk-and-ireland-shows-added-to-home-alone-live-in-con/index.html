Further UK And Ireland Shows Added To Home Alone Live In Concert Tour In December - Stereoboard

TheTools · 08.09.2026 10:58 · source · permalink

More UK and Ireland shows have been added to the Home Alone Live In Concert tour this winter.

The 1990 Christmas classic will be played on a huge screen, accompanied by an orchestra performing composer John Williams' iconic musical score at each date.

New shows include two opening nights at Manchester's Bridgewater Hall on December 2 and 3, as well as stops at Sheffield City Hall, Bristol's Beacon, Bradford Live, Dublin's 3Arena, Nottingham's Royal Concert Hall and Birmingham's Symphony Hall between December 9 and 23.

Notably, the Dublin show on December 20 will feature the RTÉ Concert Orchestra and Dublin Gospel Choir, followed by a conversation with Home Alone star Macaulay Culkin, who will join live from Los Angeles to share behind-the-scenes stories.

It was previously announced that the show will also visit Gateshead's Glasshouse and London's Royal Albert Hall on December 5 and 6, Hull's Connexin Live on December 8, Derby's Valliant Live on December 10, Leeds' Grand Theatre on December 13 and Plymouth Arena on December 14.

Tickets for all dates are on sale now, except Dublin, which goes on sale at 10am on September 10.
