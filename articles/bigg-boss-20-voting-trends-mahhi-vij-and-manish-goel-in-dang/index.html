Bigg Boss 20 Voting Trends: Mahhi Vij and Manish Goel in Danger Zone

TheTools · 19.11.2026 09:21 · source · permalink

The drama inside the Bigg Boss 20 house is getting increasingly interesting for viewers, thanks to the contestants, especially Love Gill and Scout Op.

Rumors are doing the rounds on social media that Bigg Boss 20 is likely to get extended for two more weeks. Before jumping to any conclusions, let’s wait for an official confirmation from the makers.

Talking about the show, Qazi Touqeer, Scout Op, Yung DSA, Gullu, Arishfa Khan, Amrapali Dubey, Kanika Mann, Love Gill, Mahhi Vij, and Manish Goel have been nominated for the 11th-week elimination.

While all the other contestants are in the safe zone, Mahhi Vij and Manish Goel are in the bottom positions with the fewest votes.

It remains to be seen who will get evicted from the house.

Bigg Boss 20 airs on Colors TV and streams on JioHotstar.
