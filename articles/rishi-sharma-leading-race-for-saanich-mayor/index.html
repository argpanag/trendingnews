Rishi Sharma leading race for Saanich mayor

TheTools · 18.10.2026 05:22 · source · permalink

Results are still rolling in for the Saanich municipal election, but Rishi Sharma is in the lead for the mayor’s seat.

Four people are running for mayor and Sharma has 10,816 votes as of 10:28 p.m., or 45.89 per cent. The next frontrunner, Zac De Vries, has 9,064 votes, or 38.46 per cent.

Karen Harper trails with 3,319 votes, or 14.08 per cent, while Robin Morton Stanbridge has 371 votes, or 1.57 per cent.

As of 10:28 p.m., 17 of 29 tabulators have been counted in Saanich.
