American F-16 fighter jet crashes near US air base in Germany, military says

TheTools · 22.09.2026 18:40 · source · permalink

The pilot "ejected safely" and is receiving medical care, the air base said.

An American F-16 fighter jet crashed near a U.S. air base in western Germany on Tuesday, according to military officials.

The pilot "ejected safely" and is receiving medical care, U.S. Air Base Spangdahlem said in a statement.

"Emergency response personnel are on scene," the statement said. "The cause of the incident is under investigation."
