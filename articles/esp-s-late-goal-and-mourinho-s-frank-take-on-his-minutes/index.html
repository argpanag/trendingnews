Espí's late goal and Mourinho's frank take on his minutes

TheTools · 22.11.2026 00:10 · source · permalink

Mourinho explains why Espí plays so little, praises his profile and confirms Vinicius starts on Tuesday after the comeback against Celta.

By TuFutbolero Newsroom·November 21, 2026 at 07:10 PM

Carlos Espí struck again late on, scoring in the 85th minute to complete Real Madrid's comeback against Celta Vigo. It is his fourth goal of the season, yet he has played only 62 minutes so far: 43 across eight LaLiga matches and 19 in four Champions League games.

Mourinho explained why he gets limited minutes: starting Mbappé and Espí together would leave the team exposed in transition, forcing him to go and tackle an opponent himself. Still, he praised the striker's qualities in the box and said history is full of decisive players who get few minutes.

According to El Mundo, the coach also confirmed that Vinicius will start on Tuesday. Madrid are hoping Barcelona slip against Villarreal: both sit on 33 points, although Madrid have a game in hand.
