How Rafa Jódar can qualify for the ATP Finals semifinals: scenarios and final day schedule

TheTools · 18.11.2026 10:10 · source · permalink

TENNIS

Rafa Jódar will have a chance to reach the ATP Finals semifinals on Thursday, but his fate will depend on more than just his result against Ben Shelton

  • Ángel Rigueira

    Journalist

Journalist

Actualizado el 18/11/2026 05:10 EST

It is one of the fascinating aspects of the ATP Finals. Rafa Jódar has lost both of his matches so far, falling to Germany’s Alexander Zverev (6-4, 6-4) and France’s Arthur Fils (4-6, 6-2, 6-2), yet he heads into the final round of matches in the Ivan Lendl Group with a chance to reach the semifinals. However, his fate is not entirely in his own hands.

The ATP, which organizes the tournament at the Inalpi Arena in Turin, has outlined ten possible scenarios based on the outcomes of Thursday’s two matches, both of which now have confirmed start times.

At 2 p.m., Rafa Jódar will face American left-hander Ben Shelton for the first time on the professional tour. Germany’s Alexander Zverev and France’s Arthur Fils will then close out the day at 8:30 p.m., with the Hamburg native holding a 5-2 advantage in their head-to-head record.

Of those ten scenarios, only one guarantees Jódar a place in the semifinals. It is a difficult task, but not an impossible one: the Spaniard must defeat Shelton in straight sets, while Zverev must also beat Fils in straight sets.

The world No. 1 has already secured his place in the semifinals but will still be competing to finish atop the group. There is also plenty at stake financially and in the rankings, with each round-robin victory worth 200 ranking points and $405,000.

There is one other possibility, although it is considerably less favorable. If Jódar defeats Shelton in three sets and Zverev beats Fils in straight sets, the Spaniard would enter a three-way battle with Shelton and Fils for second place, with the final standings determined by the percentage of games won. Jódar currently trails both rivals by a significant margin in that category.

Here are the ten official scenarios outlined by the ATP:

1. Zverev defeats Fils and Shelton beats Jódar: Zverev finishes first and Shelton second.

2. Fils defeats Zverev and Jódar beats Shelton: Fils finishes first and Zverev second.

3. Zverev defeats Fils in straight sets and Jódar beats Shelton in straight sets: Zverev finishes first and Jódar second.

4. Zverev defeats Fils in straight sets and Jódar beats Shelton in three sets: Zverev finishes first, while second place is determined by the percentage of games won.

5. Zverev defeats Fils in three sets and Jódar beats Shelton in straight sets: Zverev finishes first and Fils second.

6. Zverev defeats Fils in three sets and Jódar beats Shelton in three sets: Zverev finishes first and Fils second.

7. Fils defeats Zverev in straight sets and Shelton beats Jódar in straight sets: Zverev finishes first and Fils second.

8. Fils defeats Zverev in straight sets and Shelton beats Jódar in three sets: Zverev finishes first and Fils second.

9. Fils defeats Zverev in three sets and Shelton beats Jódar in straight sets: Zverev finishes first and Shelton second.

10. Fils defeats Zverev in three sets and Shelton beats Jódar in three sets: Zverev finishes first and Fils second.
