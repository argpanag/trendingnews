WARRIORS BATTLE SPURS IN FRIDAY'S NBA CUP MATCHUP
The Warriors will face the San Antonio Spurs for their second NBA Cup matchup at Frost Bank Center on Friday. The Dubs are 1-0 in group play, beating the Los Angeles Lakers by 19 points on Oct. 30. The Spurs enter Friday on a 10-game winning streak, while the Warriors had their eight-game winning streak snapped by the Houston Rockets on Wednesday.

LAST TIME OUT
The Warriors' eight-game winning streak was snapped in a 115-108 loss to the Houston Rockets at Toyota Center on Wednesday. The Warriors capped a streak of at least eight wins for the first time since their 2022 championship season. Yaxel Lendeborg led all scorers with 25 points and added 10 rebounds for his third career double-double. Brandin Podziemski tallied 22 points, six rebounds, four assists, and four 3-pointers. COMPELLING NOTE:
Warriors: Stephen Curry will be re-evaluated in two weeks after suffering an oblique abdominal tear. So far this season, the Warriors have fared better in the non-Curry minutes than in years past, outscoring their opponents by 2.3 points per 100 possessions when he’s off the court in 318 minutes. Draymond Green is confident that this year’s group is better equipped to withstand Curry’s absence. 

"We're more certain of who we are, what makes us a good team,” Green said. “I think that puts us in a better position to be successful without him as opposed to where we were last year.” 

Spurs: Victor Wembanyama has been dominant during the Spurs’ 10-game winning streak, averaging 32.6 points, 11.4 rebounds, and 4.5 blocks. Among players who have played at least half their team’s games, Wembanyama leads the league in blocks per game (4.4 BPG), is second in scoring average (30.3 PPG), and is fifth in rebounds per game (11.7 RPG).
