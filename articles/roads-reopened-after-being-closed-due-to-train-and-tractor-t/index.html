Roads reopened after being closed due to train and tractor-trailer crash

TheTools · 26.09.2026 14:44 · source · permalink

ROANOKE COUNTY, Va. (WDBJ) - All roads have been reopened, according to Roanoke County Police.

Officers were at the scene of a crash involving a tractor-trailer and a train Saturday morning, according to the Roanoke County Police Department.

On Sept. 26, officers reported that the crash happened near the intersection of Garman Road and Garman Circle.

No injuries were reported, and mechanical issues caused the crash, according to police.
