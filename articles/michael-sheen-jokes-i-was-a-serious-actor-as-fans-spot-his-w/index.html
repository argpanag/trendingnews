Michael Sheen jokes ‘I was a serious actor’ as fans spot his wild transformation

TheTools · 30.10.2026 20:36 · source · permalink

Michael Sheen has swapped Shakespeare for something a little more sinister—Claudia Winkleman.

The Welsh actor shared a Halloween transformation on Instagram ahead of tonight’s Celebrity Traitors final, dressing up as the show’s iconic host.

Mr Sheen wore a dark outfit and a long black wig with Ms Winkleman’s signature full fringe.

He captioned the post: "I was a serious actor," alongside the hashtags #Traitors, #Halloween, and #DignityIsOverrated.

Fans were quick to praise the look, including Claudia herself, who dubbed the outfit "majestic" while another fan wrote: "This is the best thing I’ve seen all day". 

Other comments described the costume as "perfection," "iconic," and simply "brilliant."

The timing of the costume was fitting, as Mr Sheen has become one of the major players in this year’s Celebrity Traitors.

Ms Winkleman’s fashion choices on the show have also drawn attention, with viewers noting her dramatic coats, chunky boots, and trademark fingerless gloves.

Mr Sheen’s Halloween post followed a tense penultimate episode in which he played a key role in turning suspicion toward comedian James Acaster.

He pointed out that Mr Acaster was the only remaining player who did not leave the library during the murder of Ross Kemp.

During the Round Table, Mr Acaster admitted to being a Traitor before being formally banished from the game.

Mr Sheen now heads into the final having successfully helped expose one of the Traitors—a turn of events that carries some irony.

He previously said he believed being an actor could put him at a "disadvantage", as other contestants might immediately think someone who "pretends for a living" would be capable of deceiving them.

Now, hours before the final, he has taken on a lighter role by impersonating the woman at the centre of the drama.

For fans, it seems Mr Sheen’s Halloween humour was just as sharp as his gameplay.
