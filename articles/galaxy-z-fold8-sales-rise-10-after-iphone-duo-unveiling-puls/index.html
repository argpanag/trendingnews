Galaxy Z Fold8 sales rise 10% after iPhone Duo unveiling - 매일경제 영문뉴스 펄스(Pulse)

TheTools · 17.09.2026 18:05 · source · permalink

Sales of Samsung Electronics Co.’s Galaxy Z Fold8 in South Korea rose about 10 percent in the week after Apple Inc. unveiled its first foldable iPhone, suggesting some consumers who had been waiting for Apple’s new device opted for Samsung’s foldable instead.

According to industry sources on Thursday, domestic sales of the Galaxy Z Fold8 increased about 10 percent from the previous week following the iPhone Duo unveiling.

Some consumers who had delayed purchases while waiting for Apple’s new product appear to have shifted to the Galaxy Z Fold8. The expected late October to early November launch of the iPhone Duo in Korea may also have prompted consumers looking to buy a foldable immediately to choose Samsung.

Price and hardware specifications are also likely to have influenced buying decisions.

The iPhone Duo starts at 3.29 million won ($2,385) for the 256GB model in Korea, more than 1 million won above the Galaxy Z Fold8’s 2.28 million won.

The 2TB iPhone Duo is priced at 5.09 million won. The price gap in Korea is significantly wider than in the United States, where the 256GB models differ by about $100.

The iPhone Duo weighs 254 grams and is 11.3 millimeters thick when folded, making it 53 grams heavier and 0.7 millimeters thicker than the Galaxy Z Fold8.

For large-screen use, the iPhone Duo supports multitasking primarily with a two-way split screen, while the Galaxy Z Fold8 allows users to run up to three apps simultaneously in split-screen mode.

With Apple entering the foldable smartphone market for the first time, its competition with Samsung Electronics is expected to intensify.

Once the iPhone Duo is released in Korea, consumers are expected to continue comparing the two devices in terms of price, portability and large-screen usability.
