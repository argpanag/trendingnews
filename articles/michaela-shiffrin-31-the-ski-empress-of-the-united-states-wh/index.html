Michaela Shiffrin (31), the "Ski Empress" of the United States, who won 110 Alpine Ski World Cup titles, drew attention by sharing her unexpected concerns.

German media "Sport Bild" reported on the 14th (local time) that "There is a problem that Shiffrin, who won 110 World Cups, has not solved in years," adding, "The ski superstar openly asked for help."

Shiffrin recently confessed to Kylie Kelsey, the wife of former American professional football player Jason Kelsey, who appeared on her podcast What the Point, that "I love my grandmother's panties." Shiffrin said, "I'm looking for underwear that fits well and is comfortable," adding, "It's been a process that's been going on for six years." "I really need help, so I have to ask for advice," he said.

In the West, women's panties that cover their hips widely are often referred to as "Granny Panties." After hearing Shiffrin's concerns, Kylie recommended the brand she likes to wear. In response, Shiffrin replied that she would look for the recommended product.

Shiffrin is a living legend of women's alpine skiing with three Olympic gold medals and nine world championship gold medals. At the 2018 PyeongChang Winter Olympics, he also won the gold medal in the women's event.
