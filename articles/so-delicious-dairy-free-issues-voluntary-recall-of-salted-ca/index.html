So Delicious Dairy Free® Issues Voluntary Recall of Salted Caramel Cluster Non-Dairy Frozen Dessert Pints Due to Potential Presence of Foreign Material

TheTools · 17.09.2026 07:26 · source · permalink

News provided by

Sep 15, 2026, 12:30 ET

WHITE PLAINS, N.Y. and LOUISVILLE, Colo., Sept. 15, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Danone USA is issuing a voluntary recall of So Delicious Dairy Free® Salted Caramel Cluster Non-Dairy Frozen Dessert pints with best-by dates on and before April 3, 2028 due to the potential presence of foreign materials, such as small stones and other hard objects, within the cashew inclusions.

The recalled product is packaged in pint containers and includes the indicated best-by dates and codes below. No other So Delicious Dairy Free® codes, flavors, or products are affected.
