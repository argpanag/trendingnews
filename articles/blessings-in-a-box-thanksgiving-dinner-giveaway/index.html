GRAND RAPIDS, Mich. (WOOD) - Thanksgiving is a time for family, gratitude, and togetherness, but for many households, preparing a holiday meal can be a financial challenge. Through Blessings in a Box, families can be provided with the means to prepare a Thanksgiving dinner at home. Royal Priesthood Ministries Full Life Center is preparing for its 3rd Annual Blessings in a Box Thanksgiving Outreach, with a goal of serving 500 families throughout West Michigan. This program provides Thanksgiving food boxes to households of two or more people, with a limit of one box per household and no income verification required.
