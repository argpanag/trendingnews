Al Ittihad vs Neom SC prediction

2024-25 Saudi Pro League winners Al Ittihad endured a tough defence of their title last year. They have been improved this time around, albeit unconvincing. They eased to a 2-0 defeat of Pakhtakor in the AFC Champions League Elite the other day.

In their second season in the top-flight, big spending and ambitious Neom are building on a top half finish in their debut campaign. They head into this one on the back of back-to-back victories in late-October.

Neom will therefore fancy themselves to bloody the nose of their opposition and so both teams scoring seems a decent bet for this one at the King Abdullah Sports City Stadium.
