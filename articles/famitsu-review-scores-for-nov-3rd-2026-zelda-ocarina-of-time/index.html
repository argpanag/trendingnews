Famitsu review scores for Nov. 3rd, 2026 (Zelda: Ocarina of Time score revealed)

TheTools · 04.11.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

And the big winner of the week is...

The latest issue of Famitsu has made its way to subscribers in Japan, and that means yet another round of review scores. Want to see how this week’s games fared? Check out the score round-up below!

Remember, each Famitsu review is handled by 4 critics. They each give a game a score of 1 to 10, and then the scores are added together. In other words, a perfect game would get a 40 out of 40.

  • Zelda: Ocarina of Time - 9/9/9/10
  • Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 - 9/9/9/9
  • Prinny Party: Going Overboard! - 7/7/8/7
  • The Fallen Kingdom - 7/7/7/8
  • Moomin Midsummer Madness - 7/7/8/7
  • Japanese Rural Life Adventure - 8/7/7/7

For Zelda: Ocarina of Time, the reviewers were impressed by the camera control, improved button layout and inputs, and graphics. That said, the game was marked down a bit for being a simpler title compared to modern-day games, and there were also some moments that might leave players confused as to what to do.
