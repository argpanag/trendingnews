Nicola funeral revenge and Sadie twist as Emmerdale spoilers confirm what's next

TheTools · 08.10.2026 19:45 · source · permalink

Sadie King (Patsy Kensit) was reeling in Emmerdale after being double-crossed by Robert Sugden (Ryan Hawley) and she wasted little time plotting revenge ahead of Jimmy King’s (Nick Miles) funeral.

Robert, however, may soon be the least of her worries.

Sadie, as viewers know, returned to the Dales in the wake of Jimmy’s demise, claiming that she’d been in a secret relationship with the haulage owner for three years.

The news came as a shock to grieving wife Nicola (Nicola Wheeler), who long thought she was Jimmy’s ‘one and only’. Sadie’s claims proved too hard to dispute and thus Nicola’s life has been turned upside down as she attempts to come to terms with the fact that her beloved beau was allegedly cheating on her.

Sadie’s claims about Jimmy, however, are all lies. She’s simply out to get her hands on his money and is using Nicola in order to do so. Robert, meanwhile, is in cahoots with Sadie to track to down the cash – and he double crossed her this evening after he managed to locate the funds all on his own.

Sadie was livid when she discovered his betrayal and will make it her mission to get revenge.
