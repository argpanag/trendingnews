Blade Runner 2099 Boss Reveals if Harrison Ford’s Deckard Appears in New TV Show - AOL

TheTools · 11.09.2026 21:39 · source · permalink

Blade Runner 2099 showrunner has addressed Harrison Ford’s Deckard appearance in the new TV show. Her comments clarify what fans can expect regarding the character’s place in the series.

Showrunner Silka Luisa addressed the question while discussing the Prime Video series in her interview with Polygon. She said, “Harrison Ford is not in the show. I feel like as a Blade Runner fan, I would want to know that, so I’m just gonna be a full disclosure: I wish, but no.”

She explained that Alcon Entertainment wanted the series to separate itself from the two films through its characters. However, the story will still connect with the franchise’s mythology and canon. Luisa also credited Ridley Scott’s involvement as key to maintaining those links without bringing Ford’s character directly into the story.

What is Blade Runner 2099 about?

Blade Runner 2099 is set 50 years after the events of Blade Runner 2049. The limited series follows Cora, a fugitive who takes on one final identity as a Blade Runner. She then teams with Olwen, a Replicant nearing the end of her life, to track down someone hiding a dangerous secret.

Michelle Yeoh plays Olwen, while Hunter Schafer portrays Cora. The cast also includes Dimitri Abold, Lewis Gribben, Matthew Needham and Johnny Harris. The recurring cast includes Katelyn Rose Downey, Daniel Rigby, Amy Lennox, Sheila Atim, Tom Burke and Maurizio Lombardi. Silka Luisa serves as creator and showrunner, while Jonathan van Tulleken directs the first two episodes and also executive produces the series.

Ridley Scott serves as an executive producer on the series. Alcon Entertainment also produces the show. All eight episodes will stream on Prime Video from November 25, 2026. It will premiere exclusively in more than 240 countries and territories worldwide.
