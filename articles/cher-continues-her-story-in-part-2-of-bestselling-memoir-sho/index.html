The Goddess of Pop is not done telling her story. Cher: Part Two: The Memoir continues the account from Cher: Part One: The Memoir, which arrived on Nov. 19, 2024, and topped the charts before it even hit bookshelves. Born Cheryl Sarkisian in El Centro, California, the first volume covers the singer's childhood, rise to fame and complicated marriage to Sonny Bono, with revelations about Bono's financial control, infidelity and an admission that he had considered killing her.

The cover has not yet been revealed, but it features the same color scheme as Volume One, making it a fitting companion piece for the collection. The book is listed at 480 pages, adding to the already considerable 432 pages delivered in the first tome. Clearly, the "Believe" singer has a lot more to say.
