Bake Off fans "so confused" as finalists' letters are shared two days before finale

TheTools · 24.11.2026 17:13 · source · permalink

Fans of The Great British Bake Off were left scratching their heads when the show's official Instagram posted letters from all three of the show's finalists.

It's the norm for Bake Off's social media page to feature farewell letters from recently eliminated contestants, most recently Connie and Gabe.

So, some viewers were left a little bewildered when three new letters - from Mo, Tom and Moyin - were shared ahead of Tuesday's finale episode (24 November). Had they missed something? Had someone already been eliminated?

Channel 4

"Legit thought I missed an episode. I'M SO CONFUSED," wrote one fan, with another asking: "What is happening right now?!" On the post featuring Mo's letter, a third person said: "Had a heart attack, thought you left!"

As it turns out, it was nothing so dramatic - just a chance for the three bakers to express their gratitude before their final turn in the tent.

Mo's letter read: "I honestly don't know where to begin! I never thought I'd actually get selected for Bake Off, and applied as a joke during my uni lunch break.

"Coming into the tent, I didn't realise my potential or worth. I lacked confidence and thought I didn't belong here. Bake Off gave me so much more than I expected. It challenged, pushed, scared and forced me to believe in myself, while balancing a law degree.

"I've grown so much, not just as a baker, but as a person. I've learnt that I'm capable of more than I give myself credit for and that I deserve to be proud of myself and my achievements."

Tom's read: "What an experience! I can't quite believe we all actually did all that. It was one of the most insane and amazing things I've ever done.

"Coming into the competition my main goal was just to get to episode 3 so that I could share the experience with my partner Alex. The fact I made it to the final was absolute craziness to me.

"I ticked every box that I wanted to and I couldn't have asked for a better time. I'm so grateful I got to share this first-time experience with not only the 11 other bakers, but also NIGELLA. She's dreamy! It was a fabulous ride and I'd do it all again if I could!"

And, last but not least, Moyin wrote: "WHAT A MADNESS!!! THANK YOU SO MUCH!!! Bake Off has been an unforgettable experience that cannot simply be described by words.

"From Danni's sticky willy in her cake (luckily it's just a plant) to the addition of at least two mothers to my family, every single second of this experience has been nothing less than amazing. It has been a huge privilege to bake alongside these 11 incredible bakers, they have all individually touched my heart in a unique way! Through this I have been blessed to learn, grow, laugh and cry alongside them (and I am not a massive crier!!!). Who knew I'd change!"

"It is hard to put anything into one letter so what I will say is a resounding thank you to each and every single one of you. All of us appreciate your love & kindness more than you could imagine."

He went on: "To sum it all, please remember that you are AMAZING! You have the ability to be a light! Anything you put your mind to, you can achieve & conquer! Above all, these amazing bakers have shown that you should stand in the unique essence of yourself, you won't regret it!!! AND EAT CAKE."

Earlier this month, viewers reacted to one particularly "unfair" exit from Bake Off. Fans have since been sharing their thoughts on the judges' selection of the three finalists for this season.

The Great British Bake Off's finale will air on Tuesday 24 November at 8pm on Channel 4.
