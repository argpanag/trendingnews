Beverley Callard thought she was 'losing' cancer battle as she offers update

TheTools · 12.11.2026 09:50 · source · permalink

Beverley Callard has said she thought she was 'losing' her cancer battle as she shared a more positive update after recently getting the results of some tests she had been waiting weeks for.

Fans may recall that the Coronation Street legend announced that she had been diagnosed with breast cancer in February, which came ahead of her first appearance in the Irish soap, Fair City, and she underwent radiotherapy treatment over the summer.

Beverley, who is famed for having played Liz McDonald in the ITV soap, which is filmed in Salford, initially had surgery before completing a course of radiotherapy and has been seen documenting her journey with the disease on social media.

And fans were keen to rally for the actress as she was left in limbo for weeks. Having been struggling with a number of symptoms, including nausea, doctors subsequently advised her to have further scans of her stomach, and at the end of last month, Beverley shared that she was still waiting for the results and had been so unwell that she had been forced to miss work for the first time.

In an update in early November, Beverley was seen fighting back tears as she told fans she was 'sick of having cancer' before being able to deliver some more hopeful news just last week. After a five-week wait for stomach biopsy results, she explained that the tests had found "chronic inflammation and H. pylori bacteria, but no stomach cancer whatsoever" and declared to her loyal followers that "the fight is back on."

Now, in a new video shared on Wednesday (November 11), Beverley opened up about her results, admitting it needed time to sink in. "As you know, I got the results of the biopsies on my stomach last week, and they said it's definitely not stomach cancer, which I was over the moon with," the soap star shared. "But I think it took a couple of days to sink in because we've been waiting all those weeks for the results, and you suddenly realise then that maybe you've been thinking the worst, even though you pretend that you're optimistic and everything.

"And I know everybody who's going through this feels the same way. And I did have a few days before the results where I honestly thought, because I felt so ill with this stomach thing, I honestly thought, 'I'm losing the battle here.'

"And one day I was at work, and the people have been amazing, and I was working with an actress, Una, we've worked together a lot. She's fantastic. And she said, 'How are you doing today?' And you know when somebody's nice to you you just burst into tears? And I just said, 'I'm losing the battle. I know I'm losing the battle,' because I just felt so poorly."

Sharing how she's feeling now, Beverley continued: "I'm beginning to feel quite a lot better now, and I'm sorry if I moaned at everybody as well, because I felt as though I was really self-indulgent, but we all get like that. And I've been speaking to other people who are in exactly the same boat and at the same going through the same process.

"Some are ahead of me, some are a bit behind me, and so it's good to have everyone to chat to and listen to what you have all got to say. But today has been a good day. I should have gone out this evening to something, but I'm still a bit too nauseous to go.

"And you know, it's weird, because my 94-year-old mum-in-law was here, and she's drinking Jägerbombs and Black Russians, and I was on like water and tea! And you think, 'What is going on with my life?' But there we go, I will party again at some point. But I just am sending lots of love to everybody."
