September 2's New Marvel Comics: The Full List

All hell breaks loose in this week's Marvel Comics! 

In BLACK PANTHER/NAMOR: DOOMED (2026) #1 by Ethan S. Parker, Griffin Sheridan, and Stefano Nesi, Black Panther and Namor get trapped in hell... with Doom! Spinning out of the events of Captain America (2025) and Avengers: Armageddon (2026). Black Panther and Namor must investigate Hell when Wakanda and Atlantis are attacked by demons! But once there, these rival kings discover they aren't alone, as they cross paths with the wandering soul of Doctor Doom!

Go to hell with Black Panther and Namor in BLACK PANTHER/NAMOR: DOOMED (2026) #1, on sale this Wednesday, September 2!

Celebrate 50 years of Marvel's Dark Angel with SPIDER-WOMAN 50TH ANNIVERSARY SPECIAL (2026) #1! Ann Nocenti returns Spider-Woman to Los Angeles for a detective story for the ages! Dan Watters gives us a spine-tingling look into Jessica Drew's future! Day 1 and Day 18262 Spider-Woman fans alike won't want to miss this jam-packed issue!

Meanwhile, Armageddon rages on in Chip Zdarsky and Valerio Schiti's CAPTAIN AMERICA (2025) #15! A giant battle breaks out as Mephisto's volatile ally, Red Skull, and his army of vengeful spirits fight the free souls of the underworld. Can Captain America rally the dead and form his own army in time to topple Mephisto's restless hordes?

Elsewhere, Queen in Black continues in VENOM (2025) #261 by Al Ewing and Carlos Gomez, where Venom's oldest enemy returns! Long ago, a friend of Reed Richards had a problem: an alien costume that didn't want to come off. Reed Richards blasted it off with a sonic weapon and imprisoned it for study. It seemed like a good idea at the time... but now he's got to work with that alien to save the world—and Venom holds one heck of a grudge!

Then, a zombie outbreak hits Manhattan and the whole city is rapidly overrun in Tom Waltz and Tony Parker's MARVEL ZOMBIES: WAR ZONE (2026) #1! As Earth's Mightiest succumb to the virus, a barrier is hastily erected to protect the world—but inside the barrier, a war zone has broken out as survivors fight for their lives against the ever-growing horde. However, one man isn't looking for survival; he's after answers as he carves a bloody swathe of his own through the ravaged city. And if any zombies get in his way? Well, the Punisher has plenty of lead that they can eat!

Additionally, return to the Mangaverse in MARVEL MANGAVERSE: WEB OF BLOOD (2026) #1—just in time to see it end?! Celebrating the 25th Anniversary of Marvel Mangaverse, father-son duo Joe and Jack Kelly are teaming up with acclaimed artist Kenny Ruiz to revisit the Mangaverse as readers have never seen it before! In a world where technology no longer works and magic reigns supreme, a looming apocalypse threatens to end the world in a sea of flames—that is, unless Spinja, A.K.A. Miles Morales, Weapon X-Tremis, A.K.A. Laura Kinney, and Legion, A.K.A. Illyana Rasputin, have something to say about it! But as each attempt to stave off Armageddon leads each further down the road of forbidden magic, these friends turned enemies must confront the question of if their choices really will save the world—or doom it entirely!

Go to hell with Black Panther and Namor, celebrate 50 years of Spider-Woman, catch Venom's contentious reunion with Mr. Fantastic, and more by picking up the latest titles this week! From fresh comics to collections and even Marvel Unlimited, find the full list of new releases here!
