Victoria's Secret Angel Adriana Lima's Gorgeous Evolution, In Photos - Glam

TheTools · 18.10.2026 00:00 · source · permalink

She might be one of the most iconic Victoria's Secret models to ever walk the runway, and Adriana Lima has only gotten more mesmerizing with time. It was back in 1999 that she first made her debut at the legendary Victoria's Secret Fashion Show at just 18 years old, before being signed as an official Victoria's Secret Angel a year later in 2000. Throughout her illustrious career, Lima has strutted down the famed Victoria's Secret catwalk a total of 20 times—and she's still counting. She has opened the show five different times, including once in 2012, just two months after giving birth to her second child. At the peak of her modeling career, Lima became the second-highest paid supermodel in the world, second only to fellow Brazilian native, Gisele Bündchen. At the time, Lima's net worth reportedly reached $9 million.

As one of the longest-running Victoria's Secret models ever, Lima eventually decided to hang up her Angel wings in 2018—but she wasn't gone for long. She later made a triumphant return to the catwalk in 2024, and she has continued to hypnotize fans with her age-defying beauty ever since. In the same way that side-by-side pics of Heidi Klum's face transformation prove she's always been stunning, her fellow model Lima has had a gorgeous evolution over the years, and there are photos to prove it. Keep scrolling to relive Lima's graceful transformation through photos from the '90s and beyond.

Adriana Lima rocked sleek hair and a dramatic side part in her early days as a Victoria's Secret Angel

In the first few years of her career as a Victoria's Secret Angel, Adriana Lima brought a youthful and spunky energy to the runway, and her look was very 2000s-coded at the time. This photo of Lima was taken at her third VS Fashion Show in 2001, where she demanded attention in a baby pink silk corset with ruched edging and matching panties. Her look was highly representative of the early aughts, as she rocked sleek, straightened hair with an exaggerated side part to achieve a particularly edgy look.

Adriana Lima brightened things up with caramel highlights in 2004

Adriana Lima has tried out a number of different hair colors throughout her modeling career, including this warm shade of honey brown that she sported in 2004. In stark contrast to the rich, dark brown locks she was previously known for, the caramel-colored highlights brightened up her entire appearance and made her light blue eyes pop more than ever. This strikingly stunning pic of Lima was snapped backstage at the "Angels Across America" tour in 2004, which replaced Victoria's Secret's annual televised fashion show that year.

Adriana Lima tried out old Hollywood glamour in 2007

By 2007, Adriana Lima was a seasoned veteran in the modeling world, and her top-tier sense of style only seemed to get better. She channeled old Hollywood glamour while attending GQ Magazine's 50th Year Celebration in September 2007, walking the event's red carpet in a plunging black gown and a fur-trim sweater. Her makeup for the occasion was elegant and refined, complementing her defined eyes and porcelain skin with a classic bold red lip. To top off the look, Lima's hair was styled into glossy finger waves around her face, along with large, voluminous curls at the ends.

Adriana Lima made her baby bump a fashion statement in 2012

Adriana Lima was pregnant with her second child in 2012, but she didn't let her baby bump get in the way of a good fashion moment. Like the numerous other times pregnant ladies of Hollywood weren't afraid to show off their bumps, Lima hit the red carpet with her growing belly on full display in a body-hugging LBD at a New York City event in April 2012. Lima gave birth to her daughter Sienna in September of the same year. The model wowed everyone two months later, returning to the Victoria's Secret runway for the annual fashion show in November.

Adriana Lima chopped her hair into a fierce shoulder-length cut in 2018

By 2018, Adriana Lima had significantly changed up her look as she chopped her hair into a breathtaking shoulder-length cut. Seen striking a fierce pose in the photo above, taken at a September 2018 event for Harper's Bazaar, Lima proved that she can rock hair at any length. Like other celebs who complemented their face shape with shorter hair, Lima's features really popped as they shone through her dark brunette middle-parted locks. Just a couple of months after that photo was taken, Lima said farewell to the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show in what was supposed to be her final walk.

Adriana Lima looked more gorgeous than ever in 2020

Even years after hanging up her Angel wings, Adriana Lima didn't stop making jaws drop — and her appearance at the 2020 Vanity Fair Oscars Party is proof enough. While posing for the cameras at the star-studded event in February 2020, Lima looked more stunning than ever, gracing the carpet in a white ballgown, embellished with sparkly blue star-shaped rhinestones and white pearls. The dazzling look was finished off with a pair of statement drop earrings and killer hair and makeup featuring simple yet sharp details like defined brows, a neutral lip, and slicked-back hair complete with a kiss curl.

Adriana Lima shocked fans with her unrecognizable appearance in 2023

In 2023, Adriana Lima was the talk of the internet after red carpet photos showed her looking drastically different from how fans remembered. With a noticeably fuller face at the time, plastic surgery rumors instantly started circulating online, prompting the model to post a makeup-free selfie to her Instagram Story to shut down the speculation (per Standard). She wrote at the time, "The face of a tired mom of one teenage girl, two pre-teens, one active boy, a 1-year-old learning to walk, and three dogs ... thanks for your concern," per ABC News.

Adriana Lima triumphantly returned to the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show runway in 2024

One year after Adriana Lima's appearance had the rumor mill running rampant, the beloved supermodel put an end to the internet chatter in the best way possible. She made a triumphant return to the Victoria's Secret Fashion Show in October 2024, putting her wings back on for the first time in six years. She stepped out in a sparkly black bra and underwear with plaid sleeves and a slicked-back high pony. After the show, she took to Instagram to celebrate the momentous occasion, writing in her caption at the time, "Grateful for every step that led me back to this stage."

Adriana Lima stepped out with a trendy slicked-back look in 2026

More than 25 years after making her Victoria's Secret Fashion Show debut, Adriana Lima showed off just how gorgeous she still is while attending Paris Fashion Week in September 2026. In the photo seen above — taken just a few months after she celebrated her 45th birthday — Lima went back to basics with a sleek side part, minimal makeup, and a fierce gaze toward the camera. Wearing nothing but a trendy, oversized leather jacket dress and pointed black heels, the legendary supermodel reminded us exactly why she's always been that girl.
