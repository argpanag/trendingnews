Wild's Nick Foligno: Could return Saturday

TheTools · 06.11.2026 22:53 · source · permalink

Foligno (upper body) might be available to face the Lightning on Saturday, Michael Russo of The Athletic reports.

Foligno has been sidelined for the Wild's last 14 games and will be out for at least one more when the team squares off with San Jose on Friday. In the 39-year-old winger's three games, he generated one goal, three shots and a plus-two rating while averaging just 9:57 of ice time.
