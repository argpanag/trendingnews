Your kid could be brushing their teeth with one of millions of tubes of recalled toothpaste

Some of the recalled tubes feature popular kids characters and toys like Barbie, Hot Wheels, My Little Pony, and Crayola.

Parents should check their bathroom cabinets after millions of tubes of toothpaste were recalled this week, including several brands marketed toward kids.

More than half of the 4 million tubes subject to the recall are flavored pastes from the brand Brush Buddies that feature popular kids characters and toys like Barbie, Hot Wheels, My Little Pony, and Crayola, according to a recall notice shared by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration.

The recall --- that was issued because the products deviate from the Current Good Manufacturing Practice regulations in either the way they were manufactured or tested --- applies to 17 different toothpaste products sold nationwide from the brands Brush Buddies, EVO, and ARU, according to the FDA.

The FDA categorized it as a Class II recall, which is defined as "a situation in which use of or exposure to a violative product may cause temporary or medically reversible adverse health consequences or where the probability of serious adverse health consequences is remote."

Pritam International Private Limited initiated the recall, though the products were distributed by Ashtel Studios Inc., based out of Ontario, California.
