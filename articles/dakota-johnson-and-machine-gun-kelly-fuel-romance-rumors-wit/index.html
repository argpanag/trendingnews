Dakota Johnson and Machine Gun Kelly fuel romance rumors with late-night studio session (with video)

TheTools · 19.10.2026 10:51 · source · permalink

The duo’s latest joint appearance captures the performer opening the passenger door of his sports car for the Fifty Shades of Grey star following a late-night studio session, before the two drove off together. The casual outing adds to a growing series of high-profile public appearances by the pair across major metropolitan cities in recent weeks, according to TMZ.

Rumors surrounding the relationship first surfaced late last month when the two were spotted arriving together in New York City. Industry sources indicate that the actress and the music artist originally crossed paths earlier in the summer while attending a star-studded celebrity wedding in Manhattan. Since then, the couple has been seen together on multiple occasions, including an appearance at a Milan Fashion Week event where Machine Gun Kelly walked the runway.

Both individuals have recently emerged from long-term, high-profile relationships within the entertainment industry. Johnson previously concluded a multi-year relationship with Coldplay frontman Chris Martin, while Machine Gun Kelly shares a child with actress Megan Fox following his own previous long-term partnership. Neither party’s representatives have officially commented on the nature of their relationship.

The persistent public outings highlight an ongoing crossover between Hollywood and the music industry. As Johnson prepares for her upcoming cinematic projects and Machine Gun Kelly continues writing and recording new material, observers continue to follow the frequent joint outings between the two stars in Los Angeles and New York.
