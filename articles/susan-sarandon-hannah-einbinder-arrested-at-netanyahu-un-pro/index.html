Susan Sarandon, Hannah Einbinder arrested at Netanyahu UN protest

NewsFeed

Actors Susan Sarandon and Hannah Einbinder were among roughly 100 protesters arrested by New York City police outside the UN ahead of Israeli PM Benjamin Netanyahu’s speech. Protest organiser Jewish Voice for Peace had accused the UN of “playing host to a war criminal”.
