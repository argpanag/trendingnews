The Kyiv Independent online newspaper was only a few months old when Russia launched its full-scale war on Ukraine. Starting life as a newsletter with a team of 12, the English-language edition now employs around 100 people making news and documentaries about life in Ukraine. At the heart of the operation are two women, editor-in-chief Olga Rudenko and CEO Daryna Shevchenko join Anita Rani to reflect on 5 years of creating content, editorial independence and how the conflict has impacted their lives.

Whether or not to have children can be one of the biggest, most sensitive decisions we make in our lives and one for which women often still feel judged, whatever we decide. Some of course feel that choice is taken away from them by infertility or by circumstance. In the 80th year of Woman’s Hour, as we focus on using our voices to have agency over our lives, we discuss the big picture issue of falling birth rates and ask what it means for women in particular. Nuala McGovern hears from Baroness Camilla Cavendish and Anna Louie Sussman.

Zoe Smith was 27 when she lost her leg below the knee in a car accident. A few years later she decided to try surfing and loved it. She's now Britain's number one para surfer. Nuala spoke to her before she headed off to the International Surfing Association World Para Surfing Championships which are held in Palm Desert, California.

Laura Linney has built an incredibly extensive body of work across theatre, television and film, and is well known to British audiences for her starring roles in The Truman Show, Ozark, Frasier and Love Actually. Alongside Rhys Ifans, Linney stars in a new film about two people brought together by receiving diagnoses of Parkinson's on the same day. 'Onwards and Sideways' explores their blossoming friendship and how they each navigate a life re-shaped by Parkinson's.

As part of our series meeting women behind some of the UK's fastest-growing businesses, we speak to Hellen Bowey, who gave up a six-figure salary to establish care technology company Alcove, inspired by her experience growing up with a sister with a learning disability. The company helps older and vulnerable people live independently and has grown from seven employees before the pandemic to more than 150 today.

The average Women's Super League attendance surged by 192% in the season after the Lionesses' Euro 2022 triumph, but the speed of the boom has stalled. A report from Populous, based on original research undertaken with YouGov Sport gives new insights into what women’s football fans value from the matchday experience and how casual supporters interest in the game could be turned into regular club attendance. Maggie Murphy, Managing Director of Aston Villa Women's team and Ruth Hooper, Chief Marketing Officer at Women’s Super League discuss why some of the biggest fixtures are now attracting significantly smaller attendances and what can be done to get more fans through the turnstiles.

Presenter: Anita Rani

Producer: Dianne McGregor
