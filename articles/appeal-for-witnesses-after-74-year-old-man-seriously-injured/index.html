Appeal for witnesses after 74-year-old man seriously injured in Scarborough crash

TheTools · 14.11.2026 10:01 · source · permalink

Collision involving a pedestrian and a white Suzuki prompts police appeal

North Yorkshire Police are appealing for witnesses after a 74-year-old man sustained serious injuries in a traffic collision in Scarborough.

The incident occurred around 4.30pm on Wednesday, 11th November, near Scalby and Newby library on Scalby Road.

The pedestrian was struck by a white Suzuki car and subsequently taken to hospital, where he remains with serious injuries.

Authorities are seeking information from anyone who was in the area at the time, witnessed the collision, or has dashcam footage of the event.

Individuals who can assist with the investigation are urged to contact PC Nicholas Simpson at the Roads Policing Unit.

They can email [email protected] or call 101, quoting reference number 12260222146.

The driver of the Suzuki, a man in his 70s, has been arrested in connection with the collision and released while under investigation.

Police encourage anyone with relevant information to come forward.
