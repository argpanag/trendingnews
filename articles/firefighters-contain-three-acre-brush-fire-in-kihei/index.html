KIHEI, Maui (KHON2) -- A brush fire that burned an estimated three to four acres near Piʻilani Highway was brought under control.

Firefighters were dispatched at about 3:52 p.m. to the mauka side of Piʻilani Highway near Kaonoulu Street.

Crews arrived to find brush burning along the highway and spreading into an adjacent pasture.

Firefighters attacked the blaze from the roadway while wildland units accessed the fire through the pasture. Air 1 also conducted water drops to support crews on the ground.

The fire was declared under control at 5:05 p.m., though extinguishment efforts remained ongoing as firefighters continued mopping up hot spots.

Officials said full extinguishment was anticipated later in the evening.

No evacuations were ordered, no one was displaced and no injuries were reported.

The cause of the fire remains under investigation. No damage has been reported.

Maui County said the southbound lanes of Piʻilani Highway have fully reopened.

One northbound lane remains blocked while firefighters continue working along the highway.
