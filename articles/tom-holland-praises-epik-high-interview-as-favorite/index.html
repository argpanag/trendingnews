Tom Holland praises Epik High interview as favorite

TheTools · 09.10.2026 23:10 · source · permalink

"Spider-Man" Tom Holland surprised the group Epik High with a sudden "shoutout," drawing attention.

On the 9th, Tom Holland posted on his social media, "my favourite interview ever," along with part of an interview video he did with Epik High.

The interview was conducted in July to promote the film "Spider-Man: Brand New Day," and Tom Holland, Zendaya and Jacob Batalon met with Epik High. Epik High traveled to the UK and conducted the interview in a cheerful atmosphere.

In particular, during the interview Epik High amused viewers with playful moments such as giving Tom Holland, who plays "Spider-Man," a CD by Korean singer Gummy. The interview has recorded more than 3.6 million views on Epik High's official YouTube channel.

In the interview with Epik High, Tom Holland recommended restaurants in the U.K. and also mentioned and gifted the nonalcoholic beer he represents. Posting that part on social media, Tom Holland was "hard at work" as a CEO as well.
