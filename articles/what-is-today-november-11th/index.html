What is today, November 11th?

TheTools · 18.11.2026 21:15 · source · permalink

What is the date today, November 11th, according to the lunar and solar calendars? Are you looking forward to the moment at 11:11:11 on November 11th?

Today, Wednesday, marks the 315th day of the year 2026. According to the lunar calendar, today, November 11th, corresponds to the 3rd day of the 10th month, specifically the day of the Earth Ox, the month of the Earth Pig, and the year of the Fire Horse. What is the significance of November 11th that so many people are interested in today?

What is special about November 11th?

According to the lunar calendar, this month of Kỷ Hợi has 30 days, starting from November 9th to December 8th in the Gregorian calendar. In the lunar calendar, this is the 30th day of the 10th month, also known as Bính Thìn, Kỷ Hợi month, Bính Ngọ year.

Today, November 11th, is also the 5th day that Vietnamese people celebrate the beginning of winter (Lập đông), the 19th of the 24 solar terms of the year. This term means "beginning of winter," marking the transition between autumn and winter in a year.

What is special about November 11th?

Social media users are excitedly sharing about today, November 11th, when both the day and the month contain four identical number 1s. This day recalls a moment from 15 years ago: 11:11:11 AM on November 11, 2011.

Fifteen years ago, on November 11th, 2011, was considered a "once-in-a-century" moment, when at 11:11:11 AM on November 11th, 2011, the day and hour formed a perfect palindrome (read the same forwards and backwards), an event that can only happen once every 100 years.

The reason this day is special is because 11.11.11 is a unique two-digit date sequence, as there is no month (22). For the whole world , November 11th is Armistice Day, commemorating the end of World War I in 1918.

November 11th is also known as Singles' Day. It's an occasion for single young people to gather, have fun, and overcome the sadness of not having found their "other half." This day is believed to have originated in China , initially simply as a day for single students to celebrate their single status.

November 11th each year is also known as Origami Day. According to Timeanddate.com , origami, derived from the Japanese words "ori" meaning to fold and "kami" meaning paper, originated in Japan in the 6th century, although for a long time, this art form was reserved for religious ceremonies.

What day is it in Vietnam today? According to information from the "People - Events" section (nvsk.vnanet.vn), the official news website of the Vietnam News Agency (VNA), November 11, 1940, is the day the poet Han Mac Tu passed away.

Hàn Mạc Tử, whose real name was Nguyễn Trọng Trí, was born on September 22, 1912. He was a poet whose poetic style was incredibly rich, creative, and mysterious. Alongside his frenzied, intoxicated, and surrealist poems, his work possessed a lyrical, tender voice, expressing a passionate love for life and a burning desire for human connection. Dying at the young age of 28, Hàn Mạc Tử left behind an invaluable poetic legacy that can be considered among the finest in Vietnamese poetry.
