Burundi vs Zambia: How and Where to Watch LIVE

Matchday 4 of the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations Qualifiers brings an important encounter to Intwari Stadium in Bujumbura, where Burundi welcomes Zambia on Tuesday, November 17.

The Chipolopolo enter the match in second place in Group I with four points, while Burundi occupies fourth place with two points, just one behind third-placed Togo.

Kickoff is scheduled for 8:00 AM ET / 5:00 AM PT, with English-language coverage beginning at 7:50 AM ET / 4:50 AM PT on beIN SPORTS CONNECT in the United States.



Burundi Looks for Its First Victory After Dramatic Equalizer in Zambia

Burundi, managed by Patrick Sangwa, remains without a victory in the qualifying campaign but has shown encouraging signs in its recent performances.

The Intamba mu Rugamba began with a 1-0 defeat against Togo on September 25 before earning an impressive 2-2 draw against Algeria four days later.

Burundi established a two-goal advantage against the Desert Foxes but was unable to hold on for the victory.

Their latest encounter ended in another 1-1 draw against Zambia on November 13 at Levy Mwanawasa Stadium.

After Edward Chilufya gave Zambia the lead in the 65th minute, Jean Claude Girumugisha equalized in the 82nd following an assist from Gaël Bigirimana.

Sangwa's squad includes experienced players such as Youssouf Ndayishimiye, Frédéric Nsabiyumva, Bigirimana and goalkeeper Jonathan Nahimana.



Zambia Aims to Return to Winning Ways With Klings Kangwa Available

Zambia, coached by interim manager George Lwandamina, has collected four points from its opening three matches.

The Chipolopolo began with a 3-1 defeat against Algeria, before recovering with a 2-0 home victory over Togo thanks to goals from Albert Kangwanda and Eliya Mandanji.

However, Zambia was unable to secure another victory against Burundi, settling for a 1-1 draw despite taking the lead through Edward Chilufya.

Lwandamina continues to deal with several important absences, including Patson Daka, Lameck Banda and Gamphani Lungu, who were ruled out through injury.

Nevertheless, midfielder Klings Kangwa is eligible to return after serving his suspension, providing another option alongside captain Fashion Sakala.



A Crucial Battle for Second Place in Group I

Algeria leads Group I with seven points after defeating Togo 1-0 on November 14, thanks to a second-half goal from Ramy Bensebaïni.

Zambia follows with four points, while Togo has three and Burundi remains fourth with two.

The top two teams will qualify for the 2027 Africa Cup of Nations, hosted by Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda.

A victory would allow Zambia to strengthen its position in second place, while Burundi could move ahead of the Chipolopolo with a win, keeping the qualification race wide open ahead of the final two rounds in March 2027.
