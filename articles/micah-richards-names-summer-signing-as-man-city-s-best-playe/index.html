Micah Richards Names Summer Signing As "Man City's Best Player This Season"

TheTools · 07.11.2026 03:00 · source · permalink

Ex-Man City defender Micah Richards has named Elliot Anderson as the club’s “best player” so far this season.

Speaking to Goal, Richards said that after a shaky debut in the Community Shield, Anderson has managed to establish himself as a key figure in City’s engine room:

“I think he’s actually been Man City’s best player this season. Obviously, Haaland scores a lot of the goals, and Haaland does what Haaland does, essentially. I think [Iliman] Ndiaye has done all right coming in. [Jeremy] Doku has played really well in spells. [Rayan] Cherki has been amazing in spells.”

“If you’re looking for consistency, whether that be defensive work or going forward, his [Anderson’s] pass the other night for Haaland’s goal against Leipzig, it was just unbelievable. I think he’s been amazing.”

Anderson has had big shoes to fill

The £116m summer recruit from Nottingham Forest has effectively replaced Rodri in the middle of the park.

Richards correctly points out that Anderson is a “different player” to the Ballon d’Or winner, but he has already shown that he is able to make a positive impact on the team.

Man City are still a work in progress

The dynamics between Anderson and the likes of Enzo Fernandez, Rayan Cherki and Ayyoub Bouaddi mean that he is having to adapt his game to accommodate each of them.

Anderson himself has spoken about how his midfield role has shifted since his switch to the Etihad. At Forest, he was given more license to do things off the cuff, but he is playing in a more structured system now.

When playing alongside someone like Fernandez, who tends to make late runs into the box, Anderson has to show more discipline so the midfield isn’t completely vacated.

Alongside Bouaddi, on the other hand, the England international can afford to be a little more expansive with his game.

These players are still working on their relationships, and it will take a bit of time for things to settle down, but Anderson is already shining, which is a very good sign.
