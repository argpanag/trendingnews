Florence Pugh Fires Back After Recent 'Surgery' Speculation: 'Guys. I Turned 30...'

TheTools · 18.11.2026 08:16 · source · permalink

Florence Pugh is shutting down speculation about her supposed “changing” face.

Over the weekend, the British star made an appearance at the Governors Awards in Hollywood, where some online commentators suggested that her face was looking more angular than usual.

A post from Evie magazine then speculated as to whether Florence had “fallen victim to Hollywood’s war on round faces”.

“Don’t get me wrong, Florence Pugh still looks beautiful,” the outlet’s reporter continued. “But I think we can all collectively agree that Hollywood seems to have this thing against softer features.”

Florence herself then popped up in the comments to deny having had facial surgery.

“Guys. I turned 30,” she wrote. “I’m a woman. [Bodies] and faces change.

“It’s not that deep, the fact that people can’t even tell between what’s aging and what’s surgery is disturbing.”

Florence added: “Sadly puppy fluff doesn’t last forever.”

View this post on Instagram

So far, 2026 has already been another amazing year for Florence.

Not only has she appeared in one of the biggest films of the last 12 months, making a cameo as her Black Widow character in Marvel’s Spider-Man: Brand New Day, she also won a wave of praise for her performance in Netflix’s adaptation of East Of Eden.

Florence also has two major movies still to come, appearing in both Dune: Part Three and Avengers: Doomsday, which famously share the same release date in December.

Speaking to Extra, Florence admitted: “I’ve got, like, two of the most bonkers press tours I’m about to start. I’ve got Marvel and Dune at the same time, so a lot of this is really getting dressed up and talking and celebrating and hopefully seeing the people like the films.”

Her next project will be Michael Schwartz’s sci-fi rom-com 42.6 Years, in which she’s set to portray a younger version of Annette Bening’s character.
