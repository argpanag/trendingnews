Andy Cohen says two Real Housewives cities hold a place in his heart: New York and Atlanta. However, he stops short of naming one cast member as his favorite.

"I will say New York and Atlanta have always been kind of close to my heart," he told Page Six. In addition, the Bravo producer made clear that his affection reaches beyond those two shows.

"I have many favorites," Cohen said while discussing his book. Rather than choosing one woman, he pointed to the value of the whole cast.

After all, a quiet season does not mean someone has nothing left to offer. According to Cohen, a cast member can return and take the spotlight because real life keeps changing.

Beyond those picks, the Watch What Happens Live host is looking back on two decades of Bravo. His book, Andy's Bravo Scrapbook: Twenty Years Behind the Scenes of the Real Housewives and the Bravoverse, arrives October 20.

Inside, readers will find more than 600 photos from Cohen's archive. Alongside them, he shares memories of reunions and moments viewers have followed over the years.

However, his look back also explores how those tense sit-downs can change a cast member's future. Cohen recalled expecting some women to leave, only for their reunion appearances to change that thinking.

Conversely, he said others damaged their standing with the group or the show during those gatherings. Those examples explain why he avoids treating any season's outcome as certain.

Elsewhere, the book stretches beyond Housewives to include Southern Charm, Married to Medicine, and Vanderpump Rules. It also covers Summer House and Shahs of Sunset, reflecting the TV world Cohen helped build.

Despite the arguments, Cohen pushed back against calling Housewives a show only about conflict. Instead, he said the series also explores sisterhood, describing his connection to the franchise as "a gift" that has brought viewers joy.
