JAKARTA - The Meteorology, Climatology and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) predicts that some areas of Jakarta on Saturday will experience light rain in the afternoon.

BMKG through the Instagram account @infobmkg predicts that on Saturday morning at 07.00 WIB, most of the Jakarta area is expected to be thick clouds, except for Central Jakarta and East Jakarta which are predicted to be cloudy. Then, towards noon or around 10.00 WIB, almost all of Jakarta is predicted to be thick clouds.
