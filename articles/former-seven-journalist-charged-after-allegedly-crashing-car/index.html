Taylor Auerbach, a former Seven Network journalist and “fixer” for Bruce Lehrmann, has been charged by police after allegedly crashing a car into a garbage truck and jumping into the front of a second truck.

Auerbach, 35, was filmed leaping into the windshield of a small, stationary truck on Ward Avenue in Potts Point at 10.20am on Monday.

The footage shows a white hatchback rental car, allegedly driven by Auerbach, smashed into the back of a garbage truck.

NSW Police say they were called to the scene following reports of a crash.

Officers attached to Kings Cross Police Area Command attended, and found a car had collided with the rear of a garbage truck, the police statement reads.

“Police were told the driver – a 35-year-old man – had allegedly also run into the front of a truck, damaging the front of the vehicle and cracking its windscreen.”

Auerbach was breath-tested but returned a negative result for alcohol.

A roadside drug test allegedly tested positive for cannabis.

He was arrested and the car searched. Officers allegedly found a small quantity of drugs, which were seized for forensic testing.

Auerbach was taken to Kings Cross police station for a second drug test and then to hospital for further treatment.

Police charged him with driving recklessly, destroying property less than $2000 in value, refusing to give particulars to another driver, and driving under the influence of drugs.

He will face court on January 12.

Auerbach worked as a producer for the Seven Network when it was courting Lehrmann, a Liberal staffer accused of rape in federal parliament.

Auerbach claimed he and Lehrmann had dined lavishly and procured drugs and sex workers on Seven’s accounts, which the network denied.
