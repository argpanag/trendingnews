Sofia Vergara Nearly Spills Out of Plunging Martha Medeiros Bustier Top - AOL

TheTools · 05.09.2026 16:41 · source · permalink

Sofia Vergara was back at the judges’ table for another round of America’s Got Talent auditions, and she made sure to bring plenty of glamour with her. The actress shared a series of behind-the-scenes photos from week two of the auditions, posing alongside fellow judges and giving fans another look at her latest AGT style.

Sofia Vergara almost pops out of plunging Martha Medeiros bustier top at ‘AGT’

For the occasion, Vergara wore a white strapless bustier top by Brazilian designer Martha Medeiros. The fitted piece featured a dramatically plunging sweetheart neckline and intricate lace detailing, creating a daring silhouette as it hugged her curves.

Medeiros celebrated the look on Instagram, sharing footage of Vergara at the judges’ table and writing, “Start the day with Sofia Vergara,” while praising the craftsmanship behind the brand’s designs.

Vergara paired the plunging top with high-waisted white trousers for a head-to-toe monochromatic ensemble. The wide-leg pants featured a relaxed fit and pockets, balancing out the structured, form-fitting design of the bustier.

The “Modern Family” star also offered a better view of the outfit in a mirror selfie posted to her Instagram Stories. Standing in what appeared to be her dressing room, Vergara posed with one hand tucked into her trouser pocket while showing off the top’s fitted lace construction.

She wore her long brunette hair loose in soft waves and accessorized with sparkling bracelets and rings, keeping the focus on the all-white ensemble. Vergara later shared photos from the AGT set alongside Howie Mandel and Mel B. In one snap, she leaned toward the camera with her hand on her hip as the audience filled the seats behind the judges.

“Week 2 of @agt auditions,” she wrote, adding that fans would soon find out who makes it to the finals. The star’s latest look is another example of the glamorous wardrobe she has been bringing to the show.
