Theo Wahlgren in raptures – Bianca's luxurious PSG surprise in Paris

TheTools · 12.11.2026 09:32 · source · permalink

Bianca Ingrosso pushes the boat out for little brother Theo Wahlgren! In Wahlgrens värld, she and mum Pernilla surprise him with a real dream experience in Paris – a Champions League match with his favourite team, Paris Saint-Germain.

Theo Wahlgren, 19, has long harboured a big dream – to see a Champions League match in person. In the latest episode of Wahlgrens värld, the dream comes true, and it's big sister Bianca Ingrosso who is behind the surprise.

As early as this spring, Bianca and mum Pernilla Wahlgren took Theo on a trip to Paris. It was a lovely present in itself, but after a couple of days in the French capital, it turns out that the trip holds something more – something Theo could never have suspected.

"You're kidding, right?!"

When Theo realises what awaits him, he is completely shocked. He is going to see his favourite team Paris Saint-Germain take on Liverpool in the Champions League.

– You must be joking? You must be joking?! he says on the programme.

Then he starts laughing and hugs both Bianca and Pernilla. For his big sister, he has only warm words.

– I love you all so much … I'm in ecstasy right now, he says.

In an interview on the programme, Theo says it has been his dream ever since he was little to see a Champions League match. The joy is so great that he even promises to put old conflicts behind him.

"Everything is forgiven, all quarrels are forgotten," he says.

Driver, VIP lounge and champagne

And the experience is luxurious, to say the least. The family is picked up by one of PSG's chauffeurs and driven straight into the stadium. Once there, they get to hang out in the VIP lounge, where a buffet is served including champagne and canapés.

– Do you understand how crazy this is, says Theo.

After the match, another surprise awaits. The family receives goodie bags that include, among other things, match shirts with their own names on them – a memory for life for a football-loving little brother.

The match also ends exactly as Theo had hoped. PSG win 2–0, and he is pleased with both the result and the whole experience.

– I just have to say: Thank you … for this trip. I'm bloody chuffed, he says.

Bianca's contacts solved it

The fact that it was possible to arrange such an exclusive experience is down to Bianca's network of contacts. She has known PSG's chairman Nasser Al-Khelaïfi for some time, and it was through this that she managed to arrange the gift for her little brother.

Instagram

The moment means a lot to Bianca.

– Finally I could do something good as a sister, she says in the programme.

And she is more than satisfied with the result.

– Have I succeeded? Yes, you can safely say so. If I'm not the world's best big sister right now, then I don't know what I am, she says.

A long-awaited moment of joy

The scene becomes extra special against the backdrop of this autumn's season, which has offered several emotional moments within the family. Viewers have, among other things, followed the friction between Bianca and Pernilla – but in Paris, it's the laughter, the hugs and the joy that take centre stage.

For Theo it has also been an eventful year. This summer he graduated from upper secondary school and was celebrated in style by his family, and together with Felicia Wahlgren he is through to Nordic Star Cup 2026. Getting to end a trip to Paris in the stands of his favourite team hardly makes the year any worse.

Ten years in front of the camera

Wahlgrens värld celebrates ten years this autumn. The anniversary season, which is the twentieth in the series, premiered on 17 September and became the series' strongest premiere ever on HBO Max. Theo was just a child when the series started in 2016, and viewers have since watched him grow up in front of the camera – from little brother in the background to a young man with dreams of his own.

Seeing your favourite team live in the Champions League, with VIP treatment and your own name on the match shirt, is something most football fans can only dream of. For Theo, the dream became reality – thanks to a big sister who really wanted to show how much she cares about him!

Wahlgrens värld is shown on Kanal 5 and HBO Max.
