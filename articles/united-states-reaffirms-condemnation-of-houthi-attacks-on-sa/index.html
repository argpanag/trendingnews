United States Reaffirms Condemnation of Houthi Attacks on Saudi Arabia | وكالة خبر للأنباء

TheTools · 25.11.2026 10:43 · source · permalink
  • منذ ساعة

The United States has formally reiterated its condemnation of the ongoing attacks launched by Iranian-backed Houthi militias against civilian infrastructure and energy facilities within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, while emphasizing the critical importance of maintaining maritime security in the Straits of Hormuz and Bab el-Mandeb.

The stance was conveyed during a telephone conversation between U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Saudi Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan. According to a statement issued by the U.S. Department of State, the two officials addressed the regional security implications of the Houthi assaults, the stability of international shipping lanes, and various matters of mutual concern.

The diplomatic engagement follows a recent Houthi strike targeting a tower at the King Abdullah Financial District in Riyadh, which resulted in 11 injuries, as reported by Saudi civil defense authorities. This incident underscores the persistent threat posed by the militia to urban centers and economic infrastructure within the Kingdom.

Providing further context on the escalation, the Saudi Embassy in Washington announced that the Kingdom's air defense systems successfully intercepted 209 aerial threats launched by the Houthi militia between September 24 and November 24, 2026. These hostilities have targeted vital infrastructure and energy installations across diverse regions of the country.

The embassy highlighted that the scope of these attacks has extended to areas in proximity to the holy cities of Mecca and Medina. Officials confirmed that the sustained campaign of violence has resulted in multiple casualties, prompting renewed international calls for the protection of civilian populations and the safeguarding of global energy supplies.
