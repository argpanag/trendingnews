F1 LIVE: Spanish GP decisions made amid investigation as Hamilton makes Ferrari demand - AOL

TheTools · 08.09.2026 11:01 · source · permalink

Hello and welcome to Express Sports' live Formula 1 blog for Tuesday, September 8.

Liam Lawson will replace Isack Hadjar for the Spanish Grand Prix this weekend, having represented Red Bull in Monza at the weekend. It comes as Hadjar has been out of action due to a wrist injury, with Yuki Tsunoda stepping up to fill the vacancy within the Racing Bulls set-up.

Lewis Hamilton, meanwhile, is vying to reach an agreement with Ferrari with regards to rules of engagement between drivers, following a row with Charles Leclerc at the weekend. The Englishman went wheel-to-wheel with Leclerc from the outset at the Italian GP, kicking up gravel as he veered slightly off-track.

And now, the seven-time F1 champion is hopeful of the Prancing Horse allowing competitiveness between himself and team-mate, Leclerc. Elsewhere, Franco Colapinto (Alpine) and Sergio Perez (Cadillac) each received reprimands in Monza for breaches ahead of the opening of the Italian GP.

Both men are deemed to have disregarded instructions from the race director at the pit exit, with the FIA investigating matters before making their decision.
