Heat's Andrew Wiggins: Slotted to bench

TheTools · 09.11.2026 05:30 · source · permalink

Wiggins will come off the bench during Sunday's game against the Kings, Zachary Weinberger of ClutchPoints.com reports.

Wiggins will shift to a reserve role for just the 13th time in his regular-season career and the first time as a member of the Heat. He has averaged just 4.0 points, 3.3 rebounds and 2.3 assists in 22.7 minutes per game over his last three appearances.
