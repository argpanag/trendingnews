TheTools · 01.11.2026 05:16 · source · permalink

Here are all 33 new Fortnite Sprites in the Treasure Hunters Mini Season, including their powers, Fortune and Cursed variants.

Epic Games has added 33 new Fortnite Sprites in the Treasure Hunters update, including Fortune and Cursed variants. Here are all the Fortnite Treasure Hunters Sprites added in the November 1 patch, their powers, and how to find them.

All Fortnite Treasure Hunters Sprites

Fortnite Treasure Hunters has added 33 new Sprites to the battle royale in the November 1 update. The collection includes 11 base Sprites, each with its own Fortune and Cursed variants.

How to Find Sprites in Fortnite Treasure Hunters

Fortnite Treasure Hunters has also introduced a new way to find Sprites. Instead of cheat codes, players will now need to track down Wanted Posters around the map.

However, finding a poster is only the first step. You will then have to follow a treasure map and dig up your reward. Here is how to find Fortnite Sprites in the Treasure Hunters mini season:

  1. Find a Wanted Poster: Locate a Wanted Poster on a wall and tear it down.
  2. Follow the Sprite Treasure Map: The poster turns into a Sprite Treasure Map, which leads you to a buried Sprite Chest.
  3. Dig up the Sprite Chest: Head to the marked location and hit the buried chest with your pickaxe to uncover it.
  4. Collect your Sprite: Open the chest to obtain the Sprite inside.

Fortnite Sprite Mastery Rewards Return

Even though Fortnite Treasure Hunters only lasts 35 days, Epic Games has brought back Sprite Mastery rewards. Players can master Sprites to earn Lucky Locators, Golden Fireworks, and other Gizmos, with a new page unlocking every week.

You can find the Sprite Mastery rewards under the Treasure tab in the Lobby. The Sprite Mastery Strongbox Back Bling also upgrades as you progress, giving you another reason to keep tracking down the collectibles.

Finally, mastering 40 Sprites unlocks the Navigator’s Nosedive Umbrella. If you want to collect all the rewards during the mini season, you will want to keep an eye out for Wanted Posters as you explore the island.
