Fund managers bid big for Anthropic stake, but temper expectations

TheTools · 18.11.2026 01:00 · source · permalink

Australian fund managers expect their allocation in artificial intelligence provider Anthropic’s highly anticipated float to be scaled back considerably amid fierce competition for shares.

The Claude chatbot maker is expected to raise $US90.4 billion ($134.5 billion) when it lists on the Nasdaq this month, valuing the business at $US1.4 trillion and surpassing SpaceX as the biggest initial public offering ever.
