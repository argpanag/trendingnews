M18 crash latest as traffic queues form with delays - Updates

TheTools · 22.10.2026 08:41 · source · permalink

There are long delays following a crash on the M18.

Traffic sensors show traffic was held from Junction 1 to Junction 2, also for the A1M Junction 35. There are now residual delays.
