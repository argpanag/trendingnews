Camarda to Start in Salzburg After Just Eight Minutes This Season as Amorim Unleashes Another 18-Year-Old

TheTools · 15.10.2026 03:52 · source · permalink

Milan

In the Europa League, the Milan coach has decided to give the young talent a chance. The other 2008-born player, Comotto, could also get an opportunity in Austria.

Mozart was already performing in Salzburg at six years old, so what's a Europa League start at 18? Ruben Amorim has decided to play Francesco Camarda, and to play him properly: he will start for a much-changed Milan side tomorrow evening in Salzburg in the Europa League. Gonçalo Ramos will be on the bench for the first time this season, and only he knows how much he might need it, after an international break with three starts out of four for Portugal. 

the eight minutes

Camarda has only played 8 minutes this season, which, while not Rivera's 6 in Mexico, is certainly very little spread across two months: he only came on late against Lazio and Lecce. Could he have been used more? There were certainly opportunities, and 8 minutes in seven matches is undoubtedly very few for a reserve striker, a role that almost always finds space in modern football with five substitutions.

the disallowed goal against Bruges

Francesco is no longer a boy; he has turned 18 and can play freely, just like two years ago against Bruges at San Siro in the Champions League. He came on eager to take on the world, scored, and celebrated standing in front of the curva in the biggest adrenaline rush of his life. However, that goal was disallowed, so while the emotions remain, his name did not make it onto the scoresheet. In Salzburg, Camarda will be looking for his first European goal after his summer brace in a friendly against Celtic, the match that sealed his stay at Milan for these six months. 

Cardinale and Amorim

«Camarda will stay with us. We believe in him a lot,» Ruben Amorim said after that match. The decision to consider him as Ramos's deputy and not to sign another player in that role had just been made. Shortly after, the coach explained: «It helps me not to have other options, so if Gonçalo Ramos isn't there, I'll put him on. He needs to feel he's a deputy to Ramos, not a third choice. That way, he'll grow faster.» This is that moment. Gerry Cardinale, when renewing the contracts (for significant figures) of both Camarda and Comotto, added: «Our commitment to them is fully consistent with the vision I have outlined for the club. We will prioritise players developed in our youth sector, support them in their growth, and maximise their potential so they contribute to the team's success on the pitch. Both represent the best of Milan.» 

Comotto

Then there's Comotto. Christian has gone under the radar in recent months because two champions, Modric and Rabiot, play in his position. Furthermore, there's Ardon Jashari, a €35 million player, and Yunus Musah, who has become indispensable for Amorim. From August to October, Comotto has trained with his idol Modric and become familiar with the first team. His day will come, perhaps as early as tomorrow evening, even if not as a starter. CC will soon get his promised opportunity. The advantage for both him and Camarda is their birth year: 2008. They have time to make mistakes. The important thing is that they don't waste that time.
