Riders Republic Coming to Nintendo Switch 2, Hosts Free Weekend, Premieres Henri PFR In-Game Concert, and More to Celebrate 5th Anniversary

It's been five years since Riders Republic launched, welcoming over 17 million players to date to ride, board, fly, ski, and more in a massive outdoor playground designed for extreme sports. To commemorate this milestone, Riders Republic hosted a livestream showing off all the in-game celebrations, including special events, new and returning in-game content, an in-game Henri PFR concert, in-game rewards, and more. In case you missed it, here's everything you need to know about Riders Republic's fifth anniversary celebrations:

Riders Republic is hosting a Free Weekend from October 22-26, giving you a chance to race your way through the wildlands of US National Parks for free. Henri PFR is a Belgian DJ, musician, producer, and composer. After releasing his first tracks at the age of 14, the Brussels artist quickly attracted the attention of Armin Van Buuren's label Armada, on which he released his first track Tarida. A fan of melody, always on the lookout for sunny sounds, he made his way in the EDM world. In 2020, he entered the Top 100 of the best DJs in the world. After many remixes and gold and platinum records, he releases he music on renowned labels like Warner, Spinnin, Sony, and more.

Riders Republic is coming to Switch 2 on December 9, letting you compete in extreme sports on the go.

With the Nintendo Switch 2 version's upcoming release and the game being certified on Steam Deck, the team worked on empowering players to continue their progression even without an internet connection. This Nomad mode allows players to enjoy the majority of Riders Republic's content in single-player. Explore the open world freely and progress through your careers at your own pace. Unlock landmarks, uncover beauty spots and find all the balloons scattered across the world. Continue to customize your character as you wish.

Once reconnected, the game seamlessly reconciles all actions performed offline with your online save. Every bit of progress made while offline is fully reintegrated into your overall Riders Republic progression. Nothing is lost.

This feature is especially valuable for portable platforms like Steam Deck and Switch 2, and also provides a smooth experience during network disruptions or server maintenance for all platforms.

Riders Republic remains a live game, some content is not available online: the shop, the season progression, sponsors, multiplayer modes and user-created events require an internet connection to access.

Offline Play will launch December 9 on all current platforms, including Nintendo Switch 2.

Play Riders Republic today on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC via the Ubisoft Store, Steam, and the Epic Games Store. You can also play with a Ubisoft+ subscription.
