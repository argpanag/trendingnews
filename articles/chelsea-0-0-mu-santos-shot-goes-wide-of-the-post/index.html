Chelsea 0-0 MU: Santos' shot goes wide of the post.

TheTools · 31.10.2026 13:34 · source · permalink

After reconciling with manager Michael Carrick, the Portuguese midfielder was given the responsibility of leading Manchester United's play in the Premier League matchday 9 game on the evening of October 31st.

Bruno Fernandes has been confirmed to return to playing.

7:40 PM: MU had a chance to open the scoring in the 8th minute, but Andrey Santos' left-footed shot from outside the penalty area went wide of the Chelsea goal.

7:30 PM: The match begins. MU faces a tough challenge, but has the opportunity to win two consecutive Premier League matches at Chelsea's home ground, something that hasn't happened in over two decades.

Tactical diagrams of the two clubs.

The clash between Chelsea and Manchester United at Stamford Bridge has become a particularly significant match for both teams, given the current slump in form which is putting immense pressure on managers Xabi Alonso and Michael Carrick.

Chelsea missed the chance to return to the top four of the Premier League after a 0-2 defeat to Tottenham at home. This result extends a disappointing run of form for "The Blues," as they have won only two of their last six matches, alongside one draw and three defeats. With 13 points currently, Chelsea are experiencing one of their worst starts since the 2015/16 season.

More worryingly, the London team's defense has consistently shown weaknesses, especially in set pieces and after halftime. Chelsea have conceded eight goals from dead-ball situations, the most in the league, and have also let in 10 goals in the second half. These are weaknesses that MU could exploit if they can rediscover their attacking organization.

However, the visitors also have little reason to be confident. The 0-3 defeat to Bournemouth last week has left Michael Carrick's future uncertain, although the club's management ultimately decided to keep him on at least until this away game. Manchester United have gone eight consecutive matches without a win in all competitions, including five defeats.

In the Premier League, the Red Devils' last victory came in August, leaving them just one point above the relegation zone. Their away record is also a major concern, as they haven't won a single away game this season, with three draws and three defeats.

However, history offers a glimmer of hope for Carrick. Manchester United have lost only one of their last eight away games in London, and have beaten Chelsea in their last two encounters. The 1-0 victory at Stamford Bridge in April further reinforces the belief that the Red Devils can continue to trouble their city rivals.

MU has gone eight matches without a win.

One of the notable points of interest is Chelsea's defensive ability against players with speed and the potential to create breakthroughs. Matheus Cunha has repeatedly caused "The Blues" problems, scoring five goals against them, including the decisive goal in the same match last season.

On Chelsea's side, Morgan Rogers is also a notable name, having scored a brace in their last home match against Manchester United. However, both teams are facing personnel losses, with Chelsea without Joao Pedro and Maxence Lacroix doubtful. Manchester United also need to assess the fitness of Kobbie Mainoo, Luke Shaw, and Marcus Rashford.
