Son of Norway’s queen reportedly attacked by intruder at home - VnExpress International

TheTools · 20.11.2026 19:54 · source · permalink

The unidentified man arrived shortly after midnight on Nov. 6 and began banging and kicking at the door, Norwegian outlet Se og Hør reported.

Norway's Marius Borg Hoiby and his mother, Queen Mette-Marit, in Oslo on Jun.16, 2022. Photo by AP

When Høiby, 29, opened the door, the man allegedly pushed him and entered the property. Høiby managed to force him outside before calling police, who reportedly arrived about 90 seconds later.

The suspect was arrested and subsequently released, according to People. The Royal House of Norway and the Royal Court did not immediately respond to the magazine’s requests for comment.

Høiby is Mette-Marit’s son from a relationship before her marriage to King Haakon and holds no royal title. He remains under house arrest at Skaugum Palace following his rape conviction in June, according to Hello!

The Borgarting Court of Appeal is scheduled to begin hearing his appeal in February 2027, NRK reported.
