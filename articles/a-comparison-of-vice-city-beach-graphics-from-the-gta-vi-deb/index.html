A comparison of Vice City beach graphics from the GTA VI debut trailer and a South Park episode.

TheTools · 02.11.2026 07:28 · source · permalink

A dedicated community conducted a detailed visual comparison of the Vice City beach location from the 2023 Grand Theft Auto VI debut trailer and current gameplay footage from 2026, showcased in a new episode of South Park. Players analyzed changes to the global illumination system, surface detail, and NPC behavior in anticipation of the Rockstar Games project's release, scheduled for November 19, 2026.

The initial debate about the location's population density stemmed from differences in virtual camera settings and lens focal lengths. The 2023 production trailer used a narrow field of view with the effect of a long lens, visually compressing perspective and bringing background objects closer. In the actual 2026 gameplay, a standard wide-angle third-person camera is used, positioned behind Jason, making the space around the character appear more open, while a massive crowd of beachgoers is clearly rendered in the distance along the coastline. Additionally, beach population in the game is dynamically adjusted by simulation algorithms depending on the specific coastal area, time of day, and day of week.

Most participants agreed that the current 2026 build demonstrates a significant technological advancement compared to earlier footage. The new footage highlights deeper shadow contrast, benchmark-quality surf physics, and detailed sand textures with footprints and seaweed. Users also noted the subtle vignetting effect and increased color saturation, which is activated when the protagonist wears sunglasses.
