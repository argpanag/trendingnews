Giant cranberry baseball glove cheering on Brewers

TheTools · 21.10.2026 22:32 · source · permalink

WISCONSIN RAPIDS, Wis. (WSAW) - Wisconsin cranberry growers took a break from harvesting to show support for the Milwaukee Brewers with a giant cranberry baseball glove. They’re wishing the team berry good luck at the World Series.

Fawn Gottschalk, president of Gottschalk Cranberry, said the idea came together recently.

“This was sort of a figment of an imagination late last week after the Brewers clinched the National League title,” Gottschalk said.

Gottschalk is a third-generation farmer and lifelong Brewers fan. She’ll be at American Family Field on Friday.

“I was four the last time the Brewers went to the World Series. And this might not happen again in my lifetime. So, I am very excited,” Gottschalk said.

Sharing October

Wisconsin produces about one-third of the world’s supply of cranberries and has been the number one producer for many years.

“We are already world champions in Wisconsin because Wisconsin produces about one-third of the world’s supply of cranberries and has been the number one producer for many years. So, we are super happy to share October with the Brewers,” Gottschalk said.

Building glove

Kris Parker, a GPS layout designer and a board member with the Wisconsin State Cranberry Growers Association, built the design on his computer.

“I think it turned out better than I imagined,” Parker said.

He also helped for hours in the bog to fill in the glove shape and remove berries around it.

The glove measures 100 feet by 115 feet and contains millions of cranberries. It was a collaboration between many different cranberry farmers and the Wisconsin State Cranberry Growers Association.

“They have to win. It’s been so long since they’ve been there, they’re going to go all the way this year. If I put all this work into this, they better win it,” Parker said.
