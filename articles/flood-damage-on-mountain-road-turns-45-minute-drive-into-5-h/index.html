Flood damage on mountain road turns 45-minute drive into 5-hour detour - Denver Gazette

TheTools · 23.10.2026 18:58 · source · permalink

Amid extreme flooding in southwest Colorado, multiple heavily trafficked mountain passes were closed on Thursday, with closures stretching into Friday morning.

Per a Friday morning update from the Colorado Department of Transportation, these closures included Red Mountain Pass (Million Dollar Highway), Wolf Creek Pass, Pagosa Springs to Bayfield, Chimney Rock to Arboles, and the New Mexico state line to Antonito. All closures are subject to change, with the most up-to-date information published to the CoTrip travel map.

Two mountain pass closures that are particularly notable include the Million Dollar Highway closure and that of Wolf Creek Pass, both resulting in significant detours.

The closure between Silverton and Ouray was related to flooding concerns, with images shared by CDOT showing the side of the route being washed out down the roadside cliff, depicting significant damage. This closure is resulting in a detour that turns a drive that typically takes about 45 minutes between Ouray to Silverton into one that’s close to five hours long, traveling through the Telluride area and Durango.

Meanwhile, the Wolf Creek Pass closure is related to flooding that brought heavy debris onto the road. Per CDOT, this is expected to be an extended closure, turning what’s typically a 50-minute drive between South Fork and Pagosa Springs into a drive that’s about four hours, dipping down into New Mexico.

As noted, both of these closures are subject to change, but for now, at time of reporting, expect some travel headaches in southwest Colorado. It’s one of those days where it’s probably best to stay put.
