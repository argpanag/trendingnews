Candice Swanepoel Models Her Latest Tropic of C Bikini

TheTools · 18.10.2026 23:28 · source · permalink

Supermodel and swimwear entrepreneur Candice Swanepoel showcased her latest Tropic of C designs in a poolside modeling shoot shared on Instagram, highlighting the beige bikini collection and the sustainable fashion business she founded in 2018. The 37-year-old South African model continues to pursue modeling while prioritizing her two children.

Swanepoel posed on her stomach beside a swimming pool, wearing a beige bikini with glass bead detailing on the straps. Her latest images featured the brand's newest release and highlighted the sun-kissed look associated with the collection.

Fans responded enthusiastically to the photographs, with one reaction describing her as a goddess. The shoot also offered a showcase for Tropic of C, the swimwear label Swanepoel developed with an emphasis on sustainable fabrics and female empowerment.

The model established the business while pregnant with her second child. Since its launch in 2018, the brand has formed a central part of her work beyond the runway.

Swanepoel's career also remains connected to Victoria's Secret, where she became one of the brand's Angels in 2010. Although the company ended its Angels contracts in 2021 amid declining television ratings and criticism over its lack of inclusivity, she later returned to its runway shows in 2024 and 2025.

The company replaced its traditional Angels model with the VS Collective as part of a broader brand overhaul. Swanepoel has credited her former role with helping establish her international modeling career.

Reflecting on the impact of the lingerie brand, Swanepoel told News24, "I wouldn't be anywhere without Victoria's Secret,"

She also described the professional opportunities that came with being one of the company's Angels.

"Being one of the Angels is one of the few jobs that can totally change someone's life. For example, clients and designers who would have never given you a second look now sit up and take notice."

Alongside her business and modeling commitments, Swanepoel has reduced the number of jobs she accepts to spend more time raising her children, Anacan and Ariel. She and her husband, Hermann Nicoli, welcomed their first son in October 2016 and their second in June 2018.

In a previous Mother's Day Instagram caption, Swanepoel reflected on the responsibilities and emotional changes associated with becoming a parent.

"What a gift and precious responsibility it is to bring a new soul into the world. To love and teach to love just the same. My life definitely started over on the day I met my baby."

Her family life has also included opportunities for her eldest son to appear alongside her professionally. Anacan participated in a 2017 Gap campaign with Swanepoel in support of Every Mother Counts.

In an interview with People, Swanepoel discussed the relationship between her two children and the demands of raising them. She said their bond had been rewarding to watch while acknowledging that parenting two children presents additional challenges.

"To see the love between them is amazing. It's a lot, I'm not gonna lie. The second one is a lot easier, but the situation of having two makes it harder," Swanepoel told People.

She also described her younger son as particularly easygoing in the same interview.

"My second one is an angel. He's like, 'Mommy, I know you don't need more stress.'"
