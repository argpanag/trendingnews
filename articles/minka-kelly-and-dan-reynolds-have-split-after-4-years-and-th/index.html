Minka Kelly and Dan Reynolds Have Split After 4 Years and the Timing Is Unexpected

TheTools · 08.10.2026 13:52 · source · permalink

They were just being SO cute on Instagram.

Ransom Canyon star Minka Kelly and Imagine Dragons singer Dan Reynolds have reportedly broken up, and apparently their split is very new (and pretty unexpected if you’ve been following them for a minute!). People was the first to report news of the breakup, with a source confirming it “happened recently.” No further details, but the former couple were last spotted together on Minka’s Instagram in August:

A month before that in July, Minka celebrated Dan’s birthday with a sweet and heartfelt post, writing “Thank you for making ordinary days feel extraordinary, for making me laugh until I can’t breathe, and for loving so deeply and so freely. Celebrating you is one of my favorite things to do. I love you, Dan. Happy birthday. ♥️”

Dan and Minka started their relationship with long phone talks, which eventually led to Dan reading Minka’s memoir, and her listening to his music.

“She’s like, ‘Well, if you’re going to share your art, I can share something with you, but it’s very vulnerable for me. Do you want to do this or not?’” Dan explained to People. “She was very scared for me to read the book because we hadn’t met, just as much as I was scared for her to hear a song that I’d written. But we shared it and then it ended up being a really beautiful thing and brought us a lot closer. I think it helped us both kind of understand each other on a deeper level quite quickly.”

He added, “It was a really strange and awesome way to meet someone because I was hearing all her life stories from youth to now, and she had a really hard, colorful, beautiful life. Getting to read it all before you even meet somebody… I’ve never had that kind of introduction to someone.”

Dan and Minka still follow each other on Instagram, and he last liked one of her posts in early September.
