The Legend Of Zelda: Ocarina Of Time Switch 2 Updated (Version 1.1.2), Here Are The Patch Notes

TheTools · 26.11.2026 01:15 · source · permalink

It resolves multiple bugs

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the Switch 2 has today received its second game update, bumping the title up to Version 1.1.2.

Here are the full patch notes, according to Nintendo's official support page. As you can see, it comes loaded with some bug fixes, including one that prevented progress of a certain event. Nintendo has also resolved "several other issues" to improve the overall experience.

Here's the full rundown from its support page:

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina Of Time - Version 1.1.2 (25th November 2026)

Bug Fixes

Subscribe to Nintendo Life on YouTube855k

  • Fixed an issue where the event "Malon's Friend" could not be progressed.
    • Even if you have already encountered this issue, downloading the update data will allow you to proceed with the event.
  • Fixed several issues to ensure a smoother gameplay experience.
  • Even if you have already encountered this issue, downloading the update data will allow you to proceed with the event.

You can find out more about the Version 1.1.1 update in our previous post. It added support for the mobile app Zelda Notes.

If we hear anything else about this latest game update for the Ocarina remake, we'll let you know. You can find out more about this title in our review here on Nintendo Life.
