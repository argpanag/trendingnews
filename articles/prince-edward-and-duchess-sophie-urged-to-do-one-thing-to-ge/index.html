Prince Edward and Duchess Sophie urged to do one thing to get more attention - AOL

TheTools · 23.11.2026 08:47 · source · permalink

The Duke and Duchess of Edinburgh have been urged to do one thing to get more attention for their royal work. As working members of the Royal Family, Edward - the King's brother - and his wife Sophie carry out various engagements every year.

This includes engagements in the UK and abroad, where they take trips both solo and together. While the couple themselves are popular and tend to score well in popularity polls, they don't seem to generate much public interest when it comes to their royal work.

This was shown on their recent tour to Finland, which received very little publicity. While this is not necessarily a problem, one royal expert thinks there is one thing the couple should do to get more attention for their royal work.

That is to bring their daughter, Lady Louise Windsor, who recently graduated with a degree in English and International Relations from the University of St Andrews, with them on royal tours. This is despite the fact that Louise is not a working royal and does not currently use her HRH titles.

Royal expert Richard Fitzwilliams told the Daily Mail: "As the (late) monarch's grandchild, Lady Louise technically has the option of becoming a Princess, so she's quite a senior royal really."

He continued: "Edward and Sophie are also popular and do well on YouGov ratings, but much of their hard work goes unnoticed. If they were to involve their children, then they'd be introducing younger royals into the world, which they've really got to do, because there are now very few senior royals under the age of 70.

"It's going to be years before Kate and Williams's children, George, Charlotte and Louis, take on royal duties if indeed they choose to. One of the great social cachets of the British Royal Family is all the institutional links they have to charities and military organisations.

"But in order to do the job properly, you need working royals. The Prince and Princess Michael of Kent are not young, Princess Anne is in her 70s and obviously so are King Charles and Camilla, so it's a good idea to introduce some younger members."

Lady Louise has never expressed an interest in a life as a working royal. Instead, she has always lived a very ordinary life away from the royal spotlight, only stepping out at big events.

Over the last few years, Louise's ordinary life has been shown in the jobs she has taken on. One of these saw her work in a garden centre in Surrey while she completed her A-levels.

Another job she had involved working in the kitchens at Balmoral Castle. She also took on a paid role as the principal organiser, administrator, and concierge at the Royal Windsor Horse Show.
