IND vs NZ ODIs: New Zealand's pacer Kyle Jamieson speaks about teammates' injuries

TheTools · 13.11.2026 05:47 · source · permalink

Updated On: 10 November, 2026 01:22 PM IST | Mumbai | mid-day online correspondent

The third ODI match has been abandoned due to persistent rain at the Seddon Park in Hamilton. The fourth ODI match will be played on November 13 at the Bay Oval in Mount Maunganui, followed by the fifth game on November 15 at the same venue

Kyle Jamieson (Pic: X/@BLACKCAPS)

New Zealand's pacer Kyle Jamieson said that it is frustrating that his team has been facing injury issues in the ongoing five-match ODI series against Team India.

"It's a wee bit of frustration, right? You're trying to always put a similar attack out and you sort of can build that camaraderie and build that cohesion. We haven't had the reps (repetitions) that we'd like," he told the broadcasters.
