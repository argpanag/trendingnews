Lindsey Vonn 'Hard Launches' New Boyfriend Months After Winter Olympics Injury - AOL

TheTools · 24.09.2026 09:12 · source · permalink

Lindsey Vonn is in a new relationship.

The 41-year-old Winter Olympics legend hard-launched her new relationship with a PDA-filled Instagram post on Wednesday, September 23.

Vonn, who suffered a catastrophic leg injury at the Winter Olympics earlier this year, is dating a fellow athlete. She’s officially dating French ski racer Matthieu Bailet. The happy couple appears to have enjoyed some summer fun.

“About that time for a summer recap… pt 1❤️,” she wrote.

The Instagram post included photos of the couple kissing in a pool and lying together in a hammock in the ocean.

Vonn’s fans are happy to see it.

“😍😍😍😍,” one fan wrote.

“Okay hard launch! 👏👏 But in all seriousness, happiness looks great on you LV!!” one fan added.

“YES!!!!! 🔥 …and he’s a skier!!! Sooo good!! ☺️💃🏻💥” one fan added.

“I’m so happy for you Lindsey 😃 ❤️ Wishing you both all the best!” one fan added.

Who is Lindsey Vonn’s new boyfriend?

Bailet, 30, is a French downhill skier. He competed at the Winter Olympics in 2022 and the World Championships in 2021 and 2025.

The boyfriend of the Winter Olympics star is known for specializing in the events of downhill and super-G.

Bailet was excited to see that his new girlfriend hard-launched their relationship on social media, posting in her comment section on Instagram.

“Can’t wait to see the summer recap part 2 😉😘,” he wrote on Instagram.

The French athlete has a little more than 10,000 followers on Instagram, but that number is likely to go up in a big way, especially following his relationship hard-launch on social media today.

It’s good to see that Vonn is doing well, both physically and emotionally, just months after her disastrous injury exit at the 2026 Winter Olympics in Italy.
