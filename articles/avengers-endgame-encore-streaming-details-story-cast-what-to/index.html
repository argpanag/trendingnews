Avengers Endgame: Encore: streaming details, story, cast & what to watch next | Explore Disney+

TheTools · 13.11.2026 14:19 · source · permalink

Movies & Shows

by Explore Disney+ Contributor

12 November 2026

Revisit the Avengers’ showdown with Thanos in an updated movie cut featuring new scenes and colour grading

Thanos may have snapped his fingers, but the Avengers aren’t giving up. In Marvel Studios’ Avengers: Endgame, the surviving heroes reunite to fight back after their devastating defeat. Now, an updated cut of the 2019 movie, Avengers Endgame: Encore, arrives on Disney+ on 13 November with new scenes and colour grading. Here’s your guide to this edition, the familiar faces joining the fight and what to watch next.

What is Marvel Studios’ Avengers Endgame: Encore?

The new Encore version is an updated cut of the same 2019 movie, the 22nd film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. It features new colour grading and additional scenes linking to Marvel Studios’ Avengers: Doomsday, giving you something extra to discover. It’s the movie you already love with a few extra surprises. The September 2026 cinematic re-release opened at number one globally, earning an estimated $86 million worldwide in its opening weekend. And when you combine the box office results of the original release with Encore, Avengers Endgame captured the slot as the highest-grossing movie of all time with nearly $3 billion in total box office revenue so far. 

When did Avengers Endgame: Encore arrive in cinemas?

The new cut of the movie arrived in cinemas on 25 September 2026 for a limited run.

How much longer is Avengers Endgame: Encore than the original?

This version runs about four minutes longer than the original release.

Is Avengers Endgame: Encore available to stream on Disney+?

It will be! Avengers Endgame: Encore arrives exclusively on Disney+ on 13 November 2026.

What is Marvel Studios’ Avengers: Endgame about?

The fourth instalment in the Avengers saga is the culmination of 22 interconnected Marvel films and the climax of an epic journey. The world’s greatest heroes will finally understand just how fragile our reality is and the sacrifices that must be made to uphold it. It’s a story of friendship, teamwork and setting aside differences to overcome an impossible obstacle.

Who are the cast and characters of Marvel Studios’ Avengers: Endgame?

Tony Stark brings the tech, Thor brings the thunder, and Hawkeye has the perfect aim. Get a quick refresher on the Super Heroes taking on Thanos and what makes each one stand out.

Robert Downey Jr. as Tony Stark / Iron Man

Genius. Billionaire. Philanthropist. Tony Stark's confidence is only matched by his high-flying abilities as the hero called Iron Man.

Chris Evans as Steve Rogers / Captain America

Recipient of the Super Soldier serum, World War II hero Steve Rogers fights for American ideals as one of the world’s mightiest heroes and the leader of the Avengers.

Mark Ruffalo as Bruce Banner / The Hulk

Dr. Bruce Banner lives a life caught between the soft-spoken scientist he’s always been and the uncontrollable green monster powered by his rage.

Chris Hemsworth as Thor

The son of Odin uses his mighty abilities as the God of Thunder to protect his home Asgard and planet Earth alike.

Scarlett Johansson as Natasha Romanoff / Black Widow

Natasha Romanoff, separated from the now-fractured Avengers, confronts the dark path she took to becoming a spy and assassin.

Jeremy Renner as Clint Barton / Hawkeye

A master marksman and longtime friend of Black Widow, Clint Barton serves as the Avengers’ amazing archer.

Paul Rudd as Scott Lang / Ant-Man

Ex-con Scott Lang finds a new lease on life, and a chance to redeem himself in the eyes of his daughter, after taking over the mantle of Ant-Man.

Josh Brolin as Thanos

The Mad Titan Thanos quests across the universe in search of the Infinity Stones, intending to use their limitless power for shocking purposes.

Who else is in the Avengers: Endgame cast?

Don Cheadle as James Rhodes / War Machine, Benedict Cumberbatch as Doctor Strange, Chadwick Boseman as T’Challa / Black Panther, Brie Larson as Carol Danvers / Captain Marvel, Tom Holland as Peter Parker / Spider-Man, Karen Gillan as Nebula, Zoë Saldana as Gamora, Evangeline Lilly as Hope Van Dyne / Wasp, Tessa Thompson as Valkyrie, Elizabeth Olsen as Wanda Maximoff / Scarlet Witch, Anthony Mackie as Sam Wilson / Falcon, Sebastian Stan as Bucky Barnes / The Winter Soldier, Tom Hiddleston as Loki, Danai Gurira as Okoye, Benedict Wong as Wong, Pom Klementieff as Mantis, Dave Bautista as Drax, Letitia Wright as Shuri, and more.

What are some other Marvel Studios movies and shows to watch on Disney+?

Check out the stories that came after Marvel Studios' Avengers: Endgame's historic release, from Loki’s encounter with the TVA to Sam Wilson’s next chapter and a new kind of team-up. 

Marvel Studios’ Loki

In Marvel Studios’ Loki, the mercurial villain Loki (Tom Hiddleston) resumes his role as the God of Mischief in a new series that takes place after the events of Avengers: Endgame.

Add Marvel Studios’ Loki to your Watchlist

Marvel Studios’ The Falcon and The Winter Soldier

Marvel Studios’ The Falcon and The Winter Soldier stars Anthony Mackie as Sam Wilson aka The Falcon, and Sebastian Stan as Bucky Barnes, aka The Winter Soldier. The pair, who came together in the final moments of Avengers: Endgame, team up on a global adventure that tests their abilities and their patience. Directed by Kari Skogland with Malcolm Spellman serving as head writer, the six-episode series also stars Daniel Brühl as Zemo, Emily VanCamp as Sharon Carter, and Wyatt Russell as John Walker. 

Add Marvel Studios’ The Falcon and The Winter Soldier to your Watchlist

Marvel Studios’ Captain America: Brave New World

After meeting with newly elected U.S. President Thaddeus Ross, Sam Wilson finds himself in the middle of an international incident. He must discover the reason behind a nefarious global plot before the true mastermind has the entire world seeing red.

Add Marvel Studios’ Captain America: Brave New World to your Watchlist

Marvel Studios’ Thunderbolts*

Marvel Studios assembles an unconventional team of antiheroes with Yelena Belova, Bucky Barnes, Red Guardian, Ghost, Taskmaster, and John Walker. After finding themselves ensnared in a death trap set by Valentina Allegra de Fontaine, these disillusioned castoffs must embark on a dangerous mission. Forced to confront the darkest corners of their pasts, will this dysfunctional group tear themselves apart, or find redemption and unite before it’s too late?

Add Marvel Studios’ Thunderbolts* to your Watchlist
