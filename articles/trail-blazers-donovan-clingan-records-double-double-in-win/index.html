Trail Blazers' Donovan Clingan: Records double-double in win

TheTools · 22.11.2026 02:54 · source · permalink

Clingan contributed 13 points (6-13 FG, 1-4 3Pt), 11 rebounds, two assists, two blocks and two steals across 27 minutes during Friday's 120-107 win over the Kings.

Clingan posted a 13/11 double-double while adding two blocks. The young center wasn't as efficient as usual, but his size made a difference in the paint, especially against Domantas Sabonis. He'll have a tough matchup in Minnesota early next week.
