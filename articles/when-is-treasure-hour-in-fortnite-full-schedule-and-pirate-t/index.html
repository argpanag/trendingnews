When Is Treasure Hour in Fortnite? Full Schedule and Pirate Token Odds

TheTools · 11.11.2026 12:54 · source · permalink

When is Treasure Hour in Fortnite? See the Saturday schedule, local start times, and how much Pirate Token odds are boosted.

If you’re hunting for the Pirate Token in Fortnite, Treasure Hours give you two chances every Saturday to find it with boosted drop rates. The event has taken over the Saturday Power Hour slot in Treasure Hunters. Here’s when the next Fortnite Treasure Hour is expected to start, along with the full schedule and the odds from the last event.

When Is the Next Treasure Hour in Fortnite?

The next Fortnite Treasure Hour is Saturday, November 14, at 11 AM PT / 2 PM ET. For players outside of North America, a second session will go live at 6 PM PT / 9 PM ET that same day. Both rounds last two hours.

Fortnite Pirate Token Boosted Drop Rates During Treasure Hours

The Fortnite Pirate Token drop rate normally has a 1 in 137,500 chance per Treasure Chest. However, Epic Games has confirmed that Pirate Token odds are boosted by 1.5× during Treasure Hours.

If those boosts return, the second session is the better time to hunt for the Pirate Token. Epic has yet to confirm whether the November 14 evening session will bring back double odds or not.
