Teoscar Hernandez's lone blast helps Dodgers blank Giants

TheTools · 26.09.2026 13:24 · source · permalink

by Chase Davis | Dodgers Correspondent | Sat, Sep 26th 2026, 9:24am EDT

Teoscar Hernandez went 1-for-4 in a 2-0 win over the Giants with a home run, a run scored and one RBI.

Fantasy Impact:

Through 123 games, Hernandez is batting .268 with 16 home runs, 64 RBI, 66 runs scored and five stolen bases across 436 at-bats. It's been slow-going for him for most of the season, but he's picked it up in September. Over his last 22 games, he's batting .354 with eight XBH, 13 runs, 18 RBI and three steals.
