Jon Pardi and Summer Pardi are facing new claims about what was happening behind the scenes during the final months of their marriage.

The country singer, 41, and Summer, 37, met in 2017 and married in November 2020. They share daughters Presley, 3, and Sienna, 2. However, as In Touch previously reported, they confirmed their breakup in a joint Instagram statement on July 3.

"After much thought, we have made the difficult decision to end our marriage," they said on social media. "Our daughters will always remain our highest priority and we are committed to moving forward with love and respect as coparents."

Now, sources suggest infidelity played a role in the breakup.

Curtis Hilbun / AFF-USA.COM / ME / MEGA

According to a Daily Mail report published Tuesday, September 8, Summer discovered evidence that Jon had been unfaithful. One source said the singer was caught on camera with other women on multiple occasions.

"There were security cameras on his tour bus that she had access to," the source said, per the report. The source also claimed "he cheated on her two different times, at least, and she saw it all on camera. Pretty stupid of him."

A second Nashville source backed up the account and claimed one of the women was a "groupie."

"I feel for her," the second source said of Summer, according to the outlet. "She's so nice, and it's just embarrassing and humiliating. They have two young girls, and they're going to find out about it and see all this one day."

Summer filed for divorce in Tennessee on May 12, citing irreconcilable differences. Court documents previously obtained by Us Weekly showed that she asked to be named the "primary residential parent" of their daughters if she and Jon could not agree on a parenting plan. She also requested an equitable division of their assets and spousal support.

JE1 / WENN.com / MEGA

According to the Daily Mail, Summer also requested that Jon cover her attorney and court fees. "He's gonna be lucky if he has any pennies left. He's in deep [expletive]," the source said, per the report.

Another source claimed Summer remained in the family's home outside Nashville while Jon had moved out.

"She definitely doesn't live with that man," the additional source said, according to the outlet.

In the months since the split, Summer has focused on moving forward and adjusting to life outside the marriage, according to the new report.

"She had her phase of being angry, but now she's come to terms with it," the source said, per the report.
