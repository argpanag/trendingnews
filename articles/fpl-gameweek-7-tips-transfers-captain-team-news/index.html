FPL Gameweek 7 Tips: Transfers, Captain & Team News

TheTools · 17.10.2026 16:18 · source · permalink

Man City host an Ipswich side still searching for their first clean sheet, making a double or triple-up tempting for Gameweek 7. But who should join Haaland — and how much rotation risk are you willing to take?

Here are the key pros and cons of five City options:

Antoine Semenyo – £8.3m

✅ Reliable minutes: Played all 540 minutes so far.
✅ Five attacking returns: Two goals and three assists.
❌ Limited goal threat: Just eight shots and 0.36 xG.
❌ Highest price: Costs £0.4m more than Cherki.

Rayan Cherki – £7.9m

✅ Delivering points: Four goals, two assists and 44 points — just one behind Haaland.
✅ Strong involvement: 14 shots and 14 chances created, both ahead of Semenyo.
❌ Minutes risk: Played 395 of a possible 540 minutes.
❌ Finishing above expectation: Four goals from 1.84 xG.

Jérémy Doku – £7.4m

✅ Instant impact: One goal and one assist in just 49 minutes.
✅ Cheapest attacking option here: £0.5m less than Cherki.
❌ Big minutes gamble: Only two appearances so far.
❌ Little evidence to judge: His impressive output comes from a tiny sample.

Joško Gvardiol – £5.8m

✅ Attacking upside: Five attacking returns, including four assists.
✅ Multiple routes to points: Attacking returns alongside clean-sheet potential.
❌ Rotation concern: Benched in Gameweek 6.
❌ Uses a City slot: Limits you to one other City attacker alongside Haaland.

Gianluigi Donnarumma – £5.5m

✅ Save-point potential: Six save points — joint-most among Premier League goalkeepers.
✅ Cheaper defensive route: £0.3m less than Gvardiol.
❌ Premium goalkeeper price: Takes budget away from outfield upgrades.
❌ No attacking upside: You sacrifice the goal and assist potential of a defender.
