Jeff Bezos has some advice for Nvidia’s board on its CEO search

TheTools · 18.11.2026 01:05 · source · permalink

Jeff Bezos says Nvidia should look inside the company for its next CEO, and he doesn’t give an outsider much chance of success. The Amazon founder, now co-CEO of AI engineering startup Prometheus, made the comments Tuesday at Semafor’s Silicon Valley & The World event.

“If you don’t bring Jensen back, I would not hire an outsider. Nvidia is too complicated,” Bezos said. Outside CEOs at complex companies, he added, “have a vanishingly small chance of succeeding,” and he would consider hiring one “a last resort.”

Nvidia’s board says it has no plans to bring Huang back. Harvey Jones, the longtime director named the company’s first board chair last week, opened Nvidia’s earnings call Tuesday afternoon by saying the board “does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO.”

Jones said the search will include internal and external candidates, and that the board wants a permanent CEO in place quickly but won’t rush the pick.

Huang, the Nvidia co-founder and CEO for 33 years, had posted a statement on X that morning offering to return. He said he had “made serious mistakes” in his personal life and would cooperate with the board’s independent investigation.

“If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO,” he wrote.

Nvidia announced Huang’s resignation last Thursday, a day after the New York Times reported that he’d had an affair with an Nvidia executive who reported to him. Days earlier, he admitted to an affair with a woman who U.S. officials believe was recruited by Chinese intelligence, according to the Wall Street Journal.

CFO Colette Kress, who spent 13 years at Microsoft earlier in her career, is interim CEO.

Two major Nvidia investors, Altimeter Capital’s Brad Gerstner and Atreides Management’s Gavin Baker, asked the board to leave open “the potential return of Jensen Huang.” Analyst Dan Ives of Yorkville Ives wrote in a note Sunday that talk of a Huang comeback is “ridiculous.”

The Amazon precedent (not really): Bezos has been through a founder handoff of his own. He turned over the Amazon CEO job in 2021 to Andy Jassy, a longtime insider who built AWS. When Semafor raised the comparison, Bezos called it a poor one, saying there has never been a more carefully planned transition.

He said he didn’t know enough about what happened in Nvidia’s board meetings to say whether Huang should return. But he called Huang a remarkable founder who deserves “admiration and respect no matter what.”

Huang himself said in a Bloomberg interview recorded Oct. 14 that he hoped his successor was already inside the company.

The Nvidia comments came in a wide-ranging interview. Bezos also said Blue Origin is using Prometheus tools on two projects, called President Trump by far the most communicative of the presidents he has worked with, and said Robert Pattinson was the clear choice to play the next James Bond after screen tests of five finalists.

Business as usual, officially: Nvidia reported third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year earlier, including $107.6 billion from its data center business. On the call, it raised its forecast for next fiscal year’s revenue growth to about 75%, from 70%.

Kress didn’t mention Huang by name on the call. Asked about the leadership transition, she said customers, suppliers and employees remain behind the company, calling it “business as usual.” She ended the call with a short pep talk that finished, “We’ve got this.”

In case the board was wondering about one outsider in particular, Bezos is spoken for. During the Semafor interview, he described running Prometheus as “Nirvana.”
