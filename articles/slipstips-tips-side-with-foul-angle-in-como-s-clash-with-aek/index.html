Como vs AEK Athens (Tuesday, 20:00)

Como will be looking to continue their impressive Champions League campaign by beating AEK Athens on Tuesday night.

Cesc Fabregas’s side recorded 4-1 victories over both RB Leipzig and Manchester United before running out resounding 9-1 winners away at Lens on matchday four.

They are also ticking along nicely in Serie A, winning five of their last six matches and most recently beating Cagliari 2-1 on Saturday.

As for AEK Athens, they have three points on the board after opening with a 1-0 win over LASK before losing to Shakhtar Donetsk, Manchester City and Real Madrid.

Given those contrasting starts, it is no surprise to see Como priced as heavy 2/7 favourites with bet365.

Those odds hold little appeal, so I’m turning to the foul markets, where I’ve found a selection that offers value.

Nicolas Paz is 5/6 with bet365 to be fouled at least twice, and that looks well worthy of support.

The Argentine has lit up the Champions League this season, which has seen him come in for plenty of close attention from Como's opponents.

Indeed, Paz has been fouled 2+ times in each of the hosts’ last three league-phase matches, including three times against Manchester United.

That knack for winning free kicks has also been evident in Serie A, most recently when he was brought down four times in the 2-0 defeat away at Inter Milan earlier this month.

AEK’s Kervin Arriaga could be given the thankless task of trying to keep Paz in check.

The tough-tackling Honduran committed three fouls and was booked against Real Madrid on matchday four, adding weight to the case for the Como ace to be fouled at least twice here.
