Xiangshan Forum: Tsinghua senior fellow Zhou Bo on US-China ties

TheTools · 18.09.2026 18:14 · source · permalink

Delegates at the Xiangshan Forum in China have voiced concerns over unchecked AI. They say governments are being forced to act faster amid diminished public trust. Mr Zhou Bo, a senior fellow at the Center for International Security and Strategy at Tsinghua University discussed more about the "fundamental" US-China relationship and the rise of China.
