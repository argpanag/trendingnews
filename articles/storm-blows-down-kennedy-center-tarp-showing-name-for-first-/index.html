Storm blows down Kennedy Center tarp, showing name for first time in months

TheTools · 28.11.2026 10:01 · source · permalink

Most of the tarp remains attached to the scaffolding after the Thanksgiving storm. But an upper corner of the tarp detached itself and now shows the empty wall where Trump's name used to be.

Heavy rain and powerful wind gusts swept through the D.C. area on Thanksgiving Day, damaging homes, roads and infrastructure.

Those same gusts also loosened the tarp that has covered signage on the Kennedy Center for months, revealing that President Donald Trump’s name appeared to have been removed from above John F. Kennedy’s name.

News4 video shows most of the tarp remained attached to the scaffolding after the storm. An upper corner detached and fell outward, and the gap revealed blank space where Trump’s name had been.

In June, a day after a federal judge’s deadline to remove Trump’s name from the iconic performing arts center, crews put up scaffolding and a tarp over the lettering.

As the legal process continues, that tarp has remained. A Kennedy Center official assured a judge in June that all references to Trump inside, outside and online were removed from the Kennedy Center, in compliance with the court’s order.

But with the tarp covering the area, it was difficult to confirm.

Mallory Miller, a former Kennedy Center employee and a co-founder of the activist group Hands Off the Arts, said in June she thinks the tarp’s continued presence is an intentional act to spare Trump’s ego.

“What is clear to me is the Trump administration does not want to see that building without Trump’s name on the facade before they could go through all their appeals,” Miller said at the time.
