US Strictly viewers left 'so mad' as they point out judges' 'bias' reactions

TheTools · 21.10.2026 08:49 · source · permalink

Popular series Dancing with the Stars, the US version of Strictly Come Dancing, made its return as the remaining 10 couples stepped onto the dance floor for one of the programme's most eagerly awaited weeks - Dedication Night. The celebrities were visibly moved as they delivered routines specially dedicated to important people in their lives.

Emotions ran high as the couples gave their all to honour their loved ones, with judges Carrie Ann Inaba, Derek Hough, and Bruno Tonioli, alongside guest Kym Johnson, present to offer their assessments.

Viewers have expressed worries about potential 'bias' from the judging panel following a performance by TV personality Maura Higgins and her professional partner, Mark Ballas. The duo performed a Paso Doble celebrating her native Ireland, but numerous viewers believed she had been awarded excessive marks.

Many turned to Instagram to voice their opinion that actress Tatyana Ali warranted equivalent, if not superior, scores, as Maura received 35 points while Tatyana secured 32. Judge Carrie remarked she "loved the attack", while Kym observed: "You made me want to go to an Irish pub with you or something. It was so good."

Bruno and Carrie both advised her to focus on her shoulders, with Carrie additionally deducting a point for an unauthorised lift. Carrie awarded a score of eight while the remaining three judges gave nines, reports the Express US. One viewer wrote on Instagram: "Maybe it's just me, but she seemed a beat behind almost the whole dance. Idk.. she seemed uncertain. This should have been all 8's."

Another remarked: "Love Maura, but think Tatyana's scores should have been the same if not higher." A further viewer fumed: "All talked abt her frame but then gave her 9's? ? ? Already starting w the voting bias." [Sic] A fourth commented: "Maura gets multiple critiques about her shoulders and gets 9s but Tatyana gets no criticism and gets all 8s? I'm so mad rn."

Many viewers felt Tatyana was deserving of higher marks, with one posting: "How did this get better scores than Tatyana." Another concurred: "They had way more critiques for this vs Tatyana...idk." While a final viewer added: "I'm sorry but how did this get better scores than Tatyana's with all those mistakes bffr." [Sic].

Despite the controversy, numerous fans rallied behind Maura, praising what they considered to be a stunning performance. One enthused: "This was more than just a dance, this was the Irish spirit coming to life." Another declared: "Favorite dance of the night!". A third fan noted: "That was different for dedication and really refreshing."

While a fourth proclaimed: "She doesn't need the luck of the Irish, she was FABULOUS!" Prior to her performance, Maura turned to Instagram to share a touching tribute to her homeland, writing: "Tonight, I'm dancing for home. I've been lucky enough to chase my dreams across the world, but no matter how far I go, I'll never forget where I came from.

"I'm so proud to come from a little country with such a big heart. The kindness, the humour, the strength of our people and the way we always show up for one another is something I'll never take for granted. To be able to celebrate my roots on a stage like @dancingwiththestars is something my younger self could only have dreamed of!"

"And to every little country girl out there with big dreams, never let where you come from make you feel like there's a limit to where you can go. If anything, let it be the reason you dream even bigger. This one's for every single person back home who has cheered me on, believed in me and supported me along the way! I'll always be your Irish girl, and I really hope I make you proud."

Dancing with the Stars airs live on ABC and can be streamed on Disney+ and Hulu.
