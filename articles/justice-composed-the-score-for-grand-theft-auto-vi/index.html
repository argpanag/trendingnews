Justice Composed the Score for Grand Theft Auto VI

TheTools · 15.10.2026 16:52 · source · permalink

Rockstar Games has revealed that Justice composed the original score for Grand Theft Auto VI. Today the French electronic duo have also shared their contribution to the video game’s expansive soundtrack. “Flower in a Hurricane” features a bonkers trifecta of Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard, and you can hear snippets of it in the new GTA VI launch trailer below.

The last Grand Theft Auto score, way back in 2013, was a collaborative effort between the Alchemist, Tangerine Dream, Woody Jackson, and Oh No, mixed and synced by DJ Shadow. Justice take up the mantle following the release of their last LP, 2024’s Hyperdrama. That album’s “Neverender,” another Tame Impala team-up, nabbed them a Grammy for Best Dance/Electronic Recording in 2025.

The 34-track GTA VI soundtrack is being slow-rolled ahead of its official release alongside the game on November 19. So far there have been nine songs released, including contributions from Yung Lean, Travis Scott, and Cardi B. Last week, Rockstar also unveiled the in-game radio DJs for GTA VI. Soon enough, you’ll be able to tune in to Bad Bunny’s Cocoteo FM, Robyn’s Flash FM, Lana Del Rey’s Back Country Radio, and more.
