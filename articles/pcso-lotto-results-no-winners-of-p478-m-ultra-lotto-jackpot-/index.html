PCSO Lotto Results: No winners of P478-M Ultra Lotto jackpot on Tuesday, Nov. 3

TheTools · 04.11.2026 23:31 · source · permalink

There were no winners of the P478 million Ultra Lotto 6/58 jackpot drawn by the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO) on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

The Ultra Lotto 6/58 prize was P478,298,684.14. The winning numbers are 16-55-01-06-27-14.

It was better luck next time for Superlotto 6/49 bettors after no one chose the combination of 22-08-19-36-20-44 that would have yielded the P82,569,686.42 jackpot.

No one also guessed the correct combination for Lotto 6/42 which was 35-41-08-28-19-27. A lucky bettor would have won the jackpot of P10,000,000.
