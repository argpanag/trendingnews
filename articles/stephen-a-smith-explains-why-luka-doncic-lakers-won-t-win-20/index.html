Stephen A. Smith believes that the Lakers will not win the 2027 NBA title because of having three top players being white in Luka Doncic, Austin Reaves, and Walker Kessler.

Stephen A. Smith boldly believes that while the Los Angeles Lakers are an exceptional contending team, he doesn't believe they'll win it all this NBA season because their top-three players are white.

"I think the Lakers are going to be a good team. I think they have an exceptional young coach in JJ Redick and his coaching staff. The new ownership, led by [Josh] Kushner and Bob Iger. Bob Iger happens to be, in my opinion, the Michael Jordan of sports. He's the greatest executive I've ever known. He's a mentor and a friend. The bottom line is that, I think the Lakers are in good hands, and the future is going to be incredibly bright," the sports personality said on his Stephen A. Smith Show.

"But with that being said, I do not think they're getting past the second round. I'd be shocked if they did. I'm on a world record being controversial because I've never seen an NBA team with its top three players being white deliver a championship.

"It's never happened. And I'm here to tell you I don't think it's going to happen this year."

The Los Angeles Lakers are currently being led by Luka Doncic, Austin Reaves, and Walker Kessler.

So far, the Lakers are enjoying the best start amongst all NBA teams in the league. They are holding a league-leading 10-2 record entering Wednesday's game against the Milwaukee Bucks.

Doncic is delivering a historic run and a strong early MVP campaign for LA, averaging a mind-blowing 39.3 points with 7.8 rebounds and 7.9 assists. Reaves has been huge as the co-star for the Slovenian guard with 25.3 points and 4.6 assists per game, while Kessler is simply impressive thus far as the Lakers' interior anchor with 14.9 points, 10.8 rebounds, and 2.3 blocks.

Stephen A. Smith acknowledged the hardships that several other Western Conference powerhouses are dealing with to start the NBA season. Nevertheless, he still remains unconvinced of the Lakers' chances to clinch the 2027 championship. 

"The injuries to the Oklahoma City Thunder have not gone unnoticed. Denver looks a bit worn down, even though [Nikola] Jokic and [Jamal] Murray still lead them," Smith said, "I mean, it's possible. I think Minnesota is a team to be reckoned with with LaMelo Ball and [Anthony Edwads]. You can't ignore them, but Luka has given it to Minnesota in the postseason before, so who knows what the hell’s going to happen with that?

"The Lakers have a chance. I just don't think it's going to materialize into a championship."
