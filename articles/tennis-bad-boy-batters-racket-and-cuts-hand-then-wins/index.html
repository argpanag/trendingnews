Tennis bad boy batters racket and cuts hand… then wins

TheTools · 15.10.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

World No 22 Alexander Bublik destroys his racket before smashing a drinks container in latest on-court outburst

Alexander Bublik “pulverised” his tennis racket and cut his hand open after losing a close first set to Brandon Nakashima at the Shanghai Masters.

After carving out two set points that he was unable to take advantage of, Bublik lost the first-set tie-break 9-7 and immediately smashed his racket on the ground five times, crushing it.

The Kazakh then walked over to his chair for the changeover, kicking his destroyed racket en route before hitting his hand through the lid of a drinks cooler – sustaining an injury.

He had already directed angry comments at his team after missing the set points in third-round match, but his anger bubbled over when Nakashima fired a backhand winner down the line.

John Blom, the chair umpire, announced a code violation warning for unsportsmanlike conduct as one of the Sky Sports commentators declared that the racket has been “pulverised”.

To make matters worse for Bublik, the self-inflicted wound on his hand had started bleeding and he covered it with a towel before informing the umpire that he would need a physio, and a subsequent three-minute medical time-out.

After that, Bublik regained enough composure to hold serve at the start of the second set and went on to win the match 6-7, 6-3, 7-6.

However, the incident in Shanghai was far from a one-off for a player known for his outbursts and controversial opinions.

Bublik controversies

‘I hate tennis’

Bublik said in 2020: “I hate tennis with all my heart” and that he only played the sport because of the financial benefits, and repeated similar sentiments throughout his 20s.

It was epitomised by his first appearance at Wimbledon against defending champion Andy Murray when he reached the main draw as a lucky loser and only returned to London the day before his first-round match. He then lost in straight sets.

Thiem outburst

At the 2023 US Open, Bublik received a code violation for his conduct during his match against former champion Dominic Thiem.

On that occasion he smashed his racket after losing the second set, and then reportedly threatened not to return to the tournament any more in comments aimed at his team in Russian, and also saying: “I am f------ sick of giving careers back to invalid people,” in reference to Thiem and other losses against players beset by injuries.

‘Bald and old’: Nadal-Murray rant

In 2025, Bublik went on a bizarre rant against what he called the “circus” retirements of Rafael Nadal and Murray, before adding that the manner in which they bowed out of the game was a “disgrace”.

He said: “These two people achieved everything there is to achieve. Even we as fellow tennis players used to look at them with our mouths open in the locker room – and then you see them bald and old.”

Murray ended his 20-year career in August 2024 at the Paris Olympics and Nadal followed a year later at the Davis Cup Finals in Malaga.

‘Little girl’

Earlier this year, Bublik could not resist aiming an insult at Arthur Rinderknech’s appearance and nationality after beating him in three sets. During their match, he said: “He’s French. And if he’s French, then he’s basically a little girl who could fold at any moment. There’s nothing manly about him – just look at him.”
