      Key Points from the Article

      • Rewards are obtained simply by logging into Ultimate Team on five different days with no requirement for consecutive days or completing challenges
      • Day 1-2 rewards include player packs and consumables, Day 3 gives 1,000 Season Points, Day 4 offers a 78+ Player Pick, and Day 5 provides a 500 Coins Boost for 5 matches
      • Missing a day does not disqualify you from receiving remaining rewards as long as you complete five total logins

      Find out every EA FC 27 Ultimate Team Daily Login Reward, including packs, Player Picks, SP and Coins Boosts, plus how to claim them.

      With the release of EA FC 27 there are many ways for you to improve your Club. The Daily Login Rewards give you a few free rewards to help you get started. These include multiple packs, a Player Pick and Season Points and Coins Boost.

      As mentioned before you can receive five Daily Login Rewards. These will not make your new Ultimate Team but it's nice to have some extra players and items when starting out.

      You don't have to complete any crazy SBCs or goals to get these rewards. Just login to Ultimate Team on five different days.

      All EA FC 27 Daily Login Rewards 

      Below are all the Daily Login Rewards you can get and how many logins you'll need to receive them.

      • 1 Day - Untradeable 75+ x2 Pack & Tradeable Small Silver Players Pack
      • 2 Days - Consumables Pack & 5 Match Loan Item
      • 3 Days - 1,000 SP 
      • 4 Days - Untradeable 78+ Player Pick (Choose 1 of 2)
      • 5 Days - 500 Coins Boost for 5 Matches

      1,000 SP is a great reward to get early on. Season points help you work your way through the Season Pass which includes various rewards.

      The Packs allow you to gain more players that could be beneficial to your squad and with the Player Pick you're able to pick one of two players who are rated 78+.

      How To Get EA FC 27 Daily Login Rewards

      To get these Daily Login rewards all you need to do is load up into EA FC 27 Ultimate Team.

      There are no SBC's or tasks that you need to complete to gain these rewards. Every time you load into Ultimate Team will count towards your daily login rewards.

      You do not have to play EA FC 27 for five days in a row. As long as you load up into Ultimate Team five times you'll receive all the Daily Login rewards. So if you miss a day you wont be unable to receive the rest of the rewards just make sure you play the game five times.
