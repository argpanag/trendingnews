Robert De Niro's $1 Billion Franchise Officially Plots a Sequel With a Major Ariana Grande Twist

TheTools · 26.11.2026 18:31 · source · permalink

After becoming world-famous for movies like The Godfather Part II, Raging Bull, and Goodfellas, Robert De Niro is still one of the biggest names in the movie industry in the 21st century. He continues to diversify his resume every year, adding massive hits like Joker and The Irishman to his long list of appearances over the last decade. On top of that, he has more than a handful of comedy credits to his name, including his most recent release this year.

On Nov. 25, De Niro reunited with longtime collaborator Ben Stiller for Focker-In-Law, the fourth movie in their long-running Meet the Parents saga. After the first three movies combined to gross over $1.1 billion between 2000 and 2010, the two reunited for the fourth chapter in this story, which brings in Superman star Skyler Gisondo and Wicked's Ariana Grande as the new young lovers. Now that it has hit theaters, conversations are already teasing another potential movie.

Speaking with Entertainment Weekly, Focker-In-Law director John Hamburg discussed the chances of a fifth movie being developed after his film came to theaters. Notably, this movie ended with Stiller's Greg welcoming Grande's Olivia into the family before Olivia leaves the movie on a cliffhanger by saying it was time to meet her family. "Even if [they] never get to do another one," Hamburg explained, he enjoys the idea that "audiences might end the movie just imagining in their minds who it could be."

While Hamburg explained that ideas for another Meet the Parents sequel "did come up" during production on this movie, he is waiting to see "if there is an appetite" for a new sequel after this one hit theaters. While he has not "developed a script" for a fourth movie, he is more open to making a sequel happen than he was after the third movie, noting that "everybody needed a break" after 2010's Little Fockers:

"I haven't developed a script. I'm not sitting on a script for 'Meet the Parents 5.' But I'd say, as opposed to when we finished the third one, and everybody needed a break, this one was so much fun that who knows?"

Hamburg recalls chatting with Stiller and Grande on set during the fourth movie and discussing ideas about who could play Olivia's family; for now, he is not inclined to share any details about what came up between the trio:

"I remember sitting on the set one day with Ben and Ariana talking, talking, just having fun thinking, 'Oh, could it be this person or that person? But there's no way I can share."

Focker-In-Law is less than 48 hours into its theatrical run, although the reviews are not the most favorable (29% Tomatometer rating on Rotten Tomatoes as of Nov. 26), it still ranks higher than Little Fockers, the last movie in the franchise released before this one. MovieWeb's own review spoke positively about the new entry to the franchise, even if it did feel like a retread on past plot ideas from some angles.

Should a sequel be greenlit, Gisondo and Grande are sure to be pivotal pieces of the plot again, as Hamburg teased the idea of meeting Olivia's family after Greg gave his seal of approval for Olivia's engagement to his son, Henry. While the two both have jam-packed schedules (including multiple DCU appearances for Gisondo and a new behind-the-scenes feature on Grande's last concert tour), Universal Pictures could surely look into reuniting them for another comedic romp soon.
