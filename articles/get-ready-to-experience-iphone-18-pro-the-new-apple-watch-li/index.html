    September 11, 2026

    UPDATE

    Get ready to experience iPhone 18 Pro, the next generation of Apple Watch, and AirPods 5

    Pre-orders for iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max begin Saturday, September 12

    It’s almost iPhone Pro time. Starting Saturday, September 12, at 5 a.m. PT, customers can pre-order iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max on apple.com and in the Apple Store app.

    iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max deliver massive advancements across camera, performance, battery, and intelligence. The new 48MP Fusion Main camera with variable aperture unlocks creative control that wasn’t possible before, and new Pro controls let users customize their experience in the Camera app for even easier access to their most used settings. Both models are powered by A20 Pro and feature a next-generation vapor chamber for the highest sustained performance yet, while updated battery designs leverage powerful silicon efficiencies to deliver incredible battery life. Featuring iOS 27 with the next generation of Apple Intelligence and Siri AI, an entirely new version of Siri, iPhone 18 Pro models bring powerful AI capabilities together with personal context to become an intelligent personal hub with privacy and security at its core.1

    Whether shopping in stores or online, customers can connect with Apple Specialists for personalized support to help choose the right device for them and learn how to protect their products with AppleCare+ or AppleCare One, as well as for assistance with ways to save and pay, including leasing, financing options, and carrier deals. Customers can then take advantage of convenient home delivery or arrange for pickup at their local Apple Store at a time most suitable for them.

    Apple Watch Series 12, Apple Watch Ultra 4, AirPods 5, Mac mini, and Mac Studio are all available to pre-order now on apple.com and in the Apple Store app. Along with iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max, Apple Watch and AirPods 5 will be available in stores and online worldwide beginning Friday, September 18. Mac mini and Mac Studio will be available in stores on Tuesday, September 22.

    Save Directly with Apple

    With more ways to pay, upgrading Apple devices has never been easier. Customers can take advantage of leasing, financing, and exceptional carrier deals — and because iPhone holds its value longer than other smartphones, they get even more value back with Apple Trade In.2

    Apple Trade In
    Apple Trade In is one of the smartest ways for customers to save on the latest iPhone models. When trading in an iPhone 13 or higher, customers can get $175 to $885 in credit instantly. Even better, they can apply this credit to lower their monthly payments when choosing Apple Upgrade, Apple Card Monthly Installments,3 or one of the great carrier deals.

    Apple Upgrade
    Apple Upgrade, provided by Klarna, makes it even easier for customers to get the Apple products they love with a lease term that is right for them.‡ Apple Upgrade offers 12- and 24-month leasing options for iPhone and Apple Watch, with prices starting as low as $34.99 (excluding taxes and any trade-in credit) per month for a 24-month lease for iPhone and $11.99 (excluding taxes and any trade-in credit) per month for a 24-month lease for Apple Watch.^ Apple Upgrade also offers 24- and 36-month leasing options for Mac Studio, with lease prices starting as low as $48.99 (excluding taxes and any trade-in credit) per month for a 36-month lease.^ At the end of their lease term, eligible customers can upgrade their device to the latest generation, purchase it with a one-time payment, or simply return it and exit the program.

    Carrier Deals Directly with Apple
    Apple partners with the major wireless carriers to offer incredible deals. This fall, customers can get up to $1,200 in credit with select carriers when they trade in iPhone 14 or newer in any condition to put toward iPhone 18 Pro or iPhone 18 Pro Max.4 Customers can explore available carrier offers and activate their new phone with AT&T, T-Mobile, and Verizon — all directly at Apple.

    Apple Card Monthly Installments
    Customers in the U.S. can choose Apple Card Monthly Installments (ACMI) to pay for their new devices over time at 0 percent APR when they choose to check out with ACMI at Apple, earning 3 percent Daily Cash back all up front. Customers can apply in as little as a minute and start using it instantly.3 Managing payments is just as effortless, as installments are automatically combined into one easy-to-track balance directly in the Wallet app.

    Explore the Lineup

    iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max feature the most advanced camera Apple has ever made, with huge leaps in battery life and performance. The new 48MP Fusion Main camera with variable aperture automatically adjusts for depth of field and lighting, while upgraded Photographic Styles introduce new texture and grain controls, giving users more creative freedom over their photos.5 Pro controls let users customize their Camera app experience, and Cinematic effects can be applied after capture to videos shot at up to 60 fps. Updated battery designs leverage powerful silicon efficiencies to deliver incredible battery life, with iPhone 18 Pro Max offering the largest increase in battery life ever on iPhone, and a next-generation vapor chamber unlocks maximum performance from A20 Pro — a powerhouse for on-device AI workloads. iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max feature the next generation of Apple Intelligence and Siri AI, a profoundly more personal, capable, and conversational assistant rolling out in beta with iOS 27, with personal context understanding, broad world knowledge, and onscreen awareness to help users find what they need in the moment and get things done throughout the day.6 iOS 27 also brings an expansive set of improvements that make iPhone more responsive, reliable, and easy to use. Both models will be available in black, silver, glacier, and an all-new burgundy.

    Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4 are engineered with the new Health Sensing System and the all-new S11 chip to deliver the most accurate heart rate sensing in a wearable,7 higher-frequency heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV) measurements, and advanced health and fitness features, including a new readiness score offering users insight into their capacity to take on the day ahead. Apple Watch Series 12 offers up to 24 hours of everyday battery life, and with faster charging, just 15 minutes now gets up to 12 additional hours. Apple Watch Ultra 4 is the ultimate sports and adventure watch, and now offers up to 45 hours of outdoor workout tracking — and over two days (up to 50 hours) of battery life for everyday use. Siri AI comes to Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4 with watchOS 27, and new Audio Intelligence features arrive later this year to help users remember key moments from their conversations and stay alert to important sounds, built with privacy, security, and accessibility innovations at their core.1 Apple Watch Series 12 is available in dark bronze, black, light gold, and space gray aluminum options, in addition to radiant gold titanium and natural titanium, and a stunning new ceramic version in pearl white and night blue.

    AirPods 5 deliver the industry’s best Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) in an open-ear design and even better sound quality, at an outstanding value.8 AirPods 5 feature an all-new multiport acoustic architecture and next-generation Adaptive EQ for even more immersive sound. Their breakthrough open-ear ANC removes up to 50 percent more external noise compared to AirPods 4 with Active Noise Cancellation, alongside a more natural Transparency mode. AirPods 5 with Wireless Charging Case feature longer battery life9 and a force sensor with volume swipe — a first for the open-ear form factor — to quickly adjust volume by swiping up or down on the stem. Combined with Siri AI and iPhone, AirPods enable users to draw on their personal context and get answers with broad world knowledge, entirely hands-free. Users can also respond to Siri using head gestures or use Live Translation for help connecting across languages.

    The new Mac mini with the all-new M6 and powerful M5 Pro delivers a dramatic boost in performance and even more versatility in its supersmall desktop design. With M6, Mac mini now delivers up to 4x faster AI performance,10 2x faster storage11 and graphics,11 and 40 percent faster CPU performance10 than the previous generation. Mac mini with M5 Pro brings even more pro-level performance to breeze through demanding projects, from video production to game development. And this new level of performance takes on business workflows with ease whether Mac mini is being used as a primary desktop or for always-on, deskside agentic computing.

    The new Mac Studio featuring M5 Max and the all-new M5 Ultra delivers a monumental leap in AI performance and even faster graphics for the most demanding pro workflows, all in its signature compact design that lives right on a user’s desk. Now featuring up to 4.3x faster AI performance,12 up to 2x faster storage,13 up to 1.8x faster graphics,12 and up to 1.3x faster CPU speed,12 along with higher memory bandwidth, Mac Studio empowers creatives, developers, AI researchers, data scientists, and more to push the boundaries of what they can do. It also features up to 512GB of unified memory,14 up to 1.2TB/s of unified memory bandwidth, and extensive connectivity, including Thunderbolt 5. Mac mini and Mac Studio also feature Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6. Combined with the power of macOS 27 and the next generation of Apple Intelligence, including Siri AI, both desktops are tremendous upgrades.

    Get Ready to Upgrade with Apple

    The quickest way to secure iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max is to prepare today. Customers have until 9 a.m. PT on Friday, September 11, to bypass the morning rush by selecting their model, pairing accessories, securing leasing or financing pre-approval, and setting up their trade-in. By saving their shopping bag, they’ll be one click away when pre-orders begin on Saturday, September 12, at 5 a.m. PT.

    Customers get the very best of Apple when shopping directly online or in-store. Knowledgeable Specialists can provide personalized guidance from start to finish on everything from discovering the latest trade-in offers and ways to save, to safely and securely transferring data with Personal Setup. Customers can also discover how to go further with their new devices through free Today at Apple sessions. With updated offerings like “Say Hello to iPhone 18 Pro” and “Tips: What’s New on iOS 27,” these hands-on classes are the perfect way to explore the capabilities of the latest products.

    Add a Personal Touch

    iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max introduce a range of new Silicone Cases, TechWoven Cases, Crossbody Straps, and FineWoven Wallets — all designed to complement the four beautiful finishes. The Clear Case features an updated design and can now attach to the Crossbody Strap or the new Wrist Strap, which offers a convenient, hands-free way to carry iPhone, AirPods Pro 2, or AirPods Pro 3. Woven with 100 percent recycled polyester yarns and embedded with flexible magnets that keep it neatly fitted together, the Wrist Strap will be available in six colors: black, taupe, olive, crisp blue, magenta, and burgundy.

    Protect Every Product

    The AppleCare lineup is expanding in the U.S. with the introduction of AppleCare One Family — one simple plan to cover every eligible Apple product across a user’s Family Sharing group. Beginning September 14, new purchases, upgrades, and devices a family already owns are covered automatically15 at no additional cost — with theft and loss protection for iPhone, Apple Watch, and iPad; unlimited repairs for accidents; and 24/7 priority support. At launch, customers can choose between AppleCare One Individual or Family to cover multiple products, or AppleCare+ to cover a single product. Learn more at apple.com/applecare, or in Settings on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

    Try Apple One

    New and qualified returning subscribers who purchase iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max can unlock three months of Apple One for free. Apple One bundles up to six Apple services — including Apple TV, Apple Music, up to 2TB of iCloud+ storage, and more — into one convenient plan across multiple Apple devices.16
