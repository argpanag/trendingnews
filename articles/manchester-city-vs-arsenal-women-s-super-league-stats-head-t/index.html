  5. 'More than one way to win'published at 17:33 BST
    17:33 BST

    FT: Everton 2-1 Birmingham City

    Everton

    Everton manager Scott Phelan: "It is a big win. There is more than one way to win a match and that was evident in the second half. I thought we were good in the first period - "Ruby managed to get a really good goal and then a quick counter-attack transition pegged us back but I thought we were the better team.

    "Second half they pushed hard, we knew their threat - there's a lot of speed and running power in their front line, there's some talent in their team and that proved the case in certain moments.

    "We're happy to get the win, Ruby managed to get another goal, which she more than deserved. Three points was the aim and we've managed to do that."

    On Ruby Mace's goals: "Her development in terms of goalscoring maybe suffered last season from playing a lot at centre-back. She's worked really hard on her final actions, including shooting at goal from distance.

    "She deserves that. We know the talent that she is, the ability that she's got and she just needs to keep performing at the level and help us win games of football."

    On Zara Kramzar's injury: "She felt some discomfort in her knee. We don't know the severity of that but it was obviously too much for her to continue, which isn't a great sign."

    On three wins in a row at Goodison Park: "It feels like home anyway. It's just nice to add the goals to the nice play we've had this season. Hopefully that can continue."

  6. 'We didn't compete to the levels we're capable of'published at 17:32 BST
    17:32 BST

    FT: Everton 2-1 Birmingham City

    Birmingham City

    Birmingham manager Amy Merricks: "We had positive moments but not enough. I actually thought we didn't compete too well today. I didn't think we looked like we have been looking.

    "That's probably just such a busy period taking it our of us. I know the characteristics of this group and unfortunately we were a little loose in moments which gave them some momentum.

    "We didn't compete to the levels I know we're capable of today.

    "We weren't getting out enough, we weren't aggressive enough. We had a couple of good moments where we caught them and turned over the ball. Normally we'd capitalise on that quite well but we didn't.

    "We had some really good opportunities that we didn't take and allowed them to grow into the game. I thought we responded to the first goal, it was the second we didn't respond to.

    "For us, it's about how do we compete consistently, today we didn't quite do that. We have to review that.

    "This is a great group, we've done really well over the first few weeks, it's been a tough block of games and we just need to recuperate.

    "I know having this time to refresh and recover will really help us."

    On another goal for Oceane Hurtre: "Another goal, which is brilliant. She's very clinical and I actually thought she should have got a penalty as well. She's done really well and we've got to credit the others that have found her successfully.

    "She is o the top line and it's important we highlight some of those players."

  9. 'I am happy with the strong character we showed'published at 17:28 BST
    17:28 BST

    FT: West Ham 0-3 Chelsea

    Chelsea

    Chelsea boss Sonia Bompastor: "I'm really pleased with the result. We said before the game that we wanted to finish the block strong. It was a really condensed block and the best teams just find a way to win. We took a bit of time to break the game and score the second goal but overall I am happy with the performance and the strong character we showed today.

    "Melvine Malard scored a great goal, she was clinical with the finishing. We probably did not create as much as we wanted in the first half but never easy to play a back five and a low block so we needed to be patient for them to open more spaces on the pitch and get more fatigued in the legs. In the second half we were in control most of the time. We didn't concede much which is good and we kept a clean sheet again so I'm really happy with that."

    On bringing Lexi Potter on at half-time: "I think as you could see when Lexi is playing right now for our team she is really good. I think she brings calmness, she brings quality on the ball and the level that she is performing right now she is a starter for us, there is no doubt on that. I think our second half we controlled the ball a little bit more and the quality on the ball was better. With Lexi being on the pitch it helps a lot."

    On Keira Walsh: "I think Keira is playing a little bit higher than last season. It helps us a lot in terms of creating opportunities. We know how good she is when she is at the edge of the box. Her shot is so great so she is able to score. Right now it's good to have a player with confidence who brings us the solution to score goals, that's really important."

    On the international break: "For them it is always a good time to go with the national team. I think it will be a good break coming at a good time for everyone. For them just to be in a different environment, be with different team-mates but also for us to rest and recharge. This block has been intense with many big games and a lot of travel so it is good to recharge and be ready for the next one."
