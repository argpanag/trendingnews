Malaysia Aviation Group acquires 10 Boeing 787 Dreamliner

TheTools · 25.11.2026 03:19 · source · permalink

KUALA LUMPUR (Nov 25): Malaysia Aviation Group, the holding company of Malaysia Airlines, on Wednesday said it is buying 10 787 Dreamliner from Boeing to strengthen its long-haul fleet.

The aircraft are scheduled for delivery in 2032-2035, and also comes with options for an additional six aircraft, Malaysia Aviation Group said in a statement. Malaysia Airlines will become the first local airline to operate the type of aircraft.

The acquisition represents an investment in the future of Malaysia Aviation Group, said its president Captain Nasaruddin A Bakar.

“As we look ahead to the next phase of growth, these aircraft will strengthen our ability to serve key international markets with greater capacity, efficiency and flexibility,” he said.

Malaysia Airlines currently operates more than three dozen Boeing 737 aircraft and 19 Boeing 737-8s. The national flag carrier has a total order book of 55 Boeing 737 aircraft, comprising 43 737-8s and 12 737-10s, through a combination of direct Boeing orders and leasing arrangements.

The remaining aircraft are expected to be delivered by 2030.

The latest acquisition will also support the gradual replacement of existing seven Airbus 350s, which are expected to be phased out between 2032 and 2035 as part of the group’s broader fleet modernisation programme, the company said.

The new planes are expected to serve key international markets across Europe and the Asia-Pacific, including London, Paris, Sydney, Melbourne, Tokyo, Osaka and Seoul.

“Beyond fleet renewal, this investment reinforces our commitment to maintaining a modern, competitive airline that supports Malaysia's connectivity and global aspirations,” Nasaruddin added.

Apart from Malaysia Airlines, the group also operates regional airline Firefly and Amal that serves Muslim pilgrims travelling for umrah and haj, mainly between Kuala Lumpur and Jeddah and Madinah. The group also runs logistics firm MASkargo.
