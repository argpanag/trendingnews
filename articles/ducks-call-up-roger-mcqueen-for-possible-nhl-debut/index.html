Ducks call up Roger McQueen for possible NHL debut

TheTools · 13.10.2026 10:56 · source · permalink

Another top Anaheim Ducks prospect is about to play his first game at the top level.

The Ducks are calling up six-foot-six forward Roger McQueen from AHL San Diego, Sportsnet's Elliotte Friedman reported Tuesday.

McQueen could make his debut Tuesday against the Calgary Flames (Sportsnet West, Sportsnet+, 9:45 p.m. ET / 7:45 p.m. MT).

The Ducks picked McQueen 10th overall in the 2025 NHL Draft.

After three seasons with the WHL's Brandon Wheat Kings, the Saskatoon native played NCAA hockey at Providence College last season, notching 11 goals and 16 assists in 37 games.

McQueen has three goals in four games with San Diego this season.

Forward Nikita Klepov, picked 15th overall by the Ducks this season, made his NHL debut with Anaheim last week.
