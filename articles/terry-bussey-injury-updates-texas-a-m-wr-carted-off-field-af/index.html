Terry Bussey injury updates: Texas A&M WR carted off field after opening kickoff

TheTools · 19.09.2026 20:16 · source · permalink

Texas A&M football suffered a blow to its receivers' room on Saturday, Sept. 19, when third-year Terry Bussey was carted off the field against Kentucky.

Bussey suffered the injury to his right leg, according to the ESPN broadcast. The injury occurred on the opening play of the game when he received the kickoff and landed awkwardly before fumbling, although he was later ruled to be down.

Bussey, a former five-star recruit, was set for a larger role in the Aggies' offense this season next to Mario Craver, Isaiah Horton and Ashton Bethel-Roman, who is out for Week 3 after suffering an injury against Arizona State last week. Bussey is also Texas A&M's primary kick and punt returner.

The 5-foot-10 receiver has 42 receptions for 503 yards with four total touchdowns for his career.

Here's what to know of Bussey's injury:

Terry Bussey injury update

Bussey was carted off the field against Kentucky on Sept. 19 after suffering an apparent injury to his right leg on the opening play of the game. The Aggies kick returner awkwardly cut as he was attempting to avoid a Wildcats defender.

Bussey was down on the field for multiple moments before the cart came out. It's unclear the severity of his injury.
