The Lakers are "optimistic" that Austin Reaves (heel) will rejoin them for their NBA Cup showdown against the Spurs.

The Los Angeles Lakers are now looking forward to the potential return of Austin Reaves ahead of their biggest test yet this 2026-27 NBA season.

During his pregame speech ahead of Monday's home game against the Utah Jazz, head coach JJ Redick announced that Reaves' pregame workout was a "big step" toward his return.

As such, the entire team is "optimistic" about Reaves rejoining them for Friday's NBA Cup showdown in San Antonio against the Spurs.

Still dealing with a left heel bruise, the Los Angeles Lakers' Monday game against the Utah Jazz will be the fifth consecutive absence of Austin Reaves.

He sustained the brutal injury in Milwaukee during the Lakers' previous five-game road trip.

With Reaves possibly set to rejoin them on Friday, the Lakers can't be more energized to perform as a team again. His return could not come at a better time, as they are set to face the San Antonio Spurs, their current top opponent in the Western Conference standings.

Entering their respective games on Monday, the Lakers and Spurs are tied for the best record in the conference (14-3).

Reaves is averaging 24.7 points, 3.3 rebounds, and 4.7 assists across 12 games this season before going down with a heel injury for the Lakers.
