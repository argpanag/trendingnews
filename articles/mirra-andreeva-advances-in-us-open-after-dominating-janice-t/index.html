Mirra Andreeva advances in US Open after dominating Janice Tjen

TheTools · 03.09.2026 02:34 · source · permalink

Mirra Andreeva, of Russia, celebrates winning a match against Janice Tjen, of Indonesia, during the first round of the U.S. Open tennis championships, Tuesday, Sept. 1, 2026, in New York. (AP Photo/Yuki Iwamura)

MANILA, Philippines–French Open champion Mirra Andreeva advanced easily in the US Open, cruising past Indonesia’s 36th-ranked Janice Tjen 6-2, 6-2 in the first round.

Andreeva, seeded fifth, has had a lackluster run on North American hard courts since her scintillating run to a first Grand Slam title in Paris.

But she was dialed in as she produced her second win over Tien in less than two weeks.

After a tough win against the Indonesian in Cincinnati, Andreeva said, “I was just trying to be very aggressive.”

Andreeva faces Eva Lys of Germany in the second round.
