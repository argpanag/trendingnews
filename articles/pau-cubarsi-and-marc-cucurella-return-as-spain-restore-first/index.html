Pau Cubarsi and Marc Cucurella return as Spain restore first-choice XI in Croatia

Pau Cubarsi and Marc Cucurella return to Spain’s starting XI in Croatia on Tuesday at 20:45 in the Nations League group stage.

According to L'Équipe, head coach Luis de la Fuente has restored his core players after heavy rotation in Saturday’s 2-1 win over Czechia on matchday three.

OneFootball Videos

Rested against the Czechs, Cubarsi and Cucurella come back in. Cubarsi partners Aymeric Laporte in central defence, the latter having been spared in the reverse meeting with Croatia, a 4-1 game.

In midfield, Rodri and Pedri continue. Pedri keeps his place after impressing on Saturday, while Fabian Ruiz is absent, having returned to Paris at the weekend for the birth of his first child.

Up front, Mikel Oyarzabal is also restored. De la Fuente handed Roberto Fernandez his first cap in Oviedo at the weekend. Ferran Torres, a starter in England on matchday one, misses out with an ankle issue.

Withdrawn at half-time against Czechia, Lamine Yamal now starts for the fourth time in this international window, the only Spaniard to do so. Even goalkeeper Unai Simon was on the bench on Saturday.

Spain’s XI: Unai Simon, Pubill, Pau Cubarsi, Aymeric Laporte, Marc Cucurella, Rodri, Pedri, Lamine Yamal, Dani Olmo, Alex Baena, Mikel Oyarzabal.

Source: L'Équipe
