The beginning of a brand new season

TheTools · 22.09.2026 22:37 · source · permalink

TONIGHT’S OUTLOOK: Scattered showers and thunderstorms will be possible during the evening, mainly across the southeastern portion of the viewing area. Rain activity will gradually clear out of the area overnight, leaving behind mostly clear skies, with temperatures cooling into the upper 60s.

FALL: Today marks the official start of fall. Even though it signals cooler weather, the temperatures didn’t get the memo. We will stay relatively warm this week, with afternoon highs reaching the mid-to-upper 80s.

However, after experiencing the seemingly endless wave of summer heat these past few months, this is still a change. Temperatures will be in the 80s rather than the 90s, which means better opportunities to enjoy outdoor activities without having to worry as much about overheating.

RAIN CHANCES: In front of the cold front, scattered showers and thunderstorms swept through the viewing area. Behind the cold front, cooler, drier air is now pushing into the region, allowing temperatures to take a step back and making it feel a bit more fall-like, despite it still being very warm. Essentially, we will be in a dry spell through the rest of the week, with mostly sunny skies. However, a stray shower or thunderstorm can’t be ruled out for some parts of the week.
