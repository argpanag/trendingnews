Carys Douglas links arms with famous dad Michael on his special night

TheTools · 05.11.2026 23:36 · source · permalink

The Wall Street star shares his two youngest children with Catherine Zeta-Jones

Michael Douglas had two very special supporters by his side as he celebrated his latest honour in New York.

The Hollywood star, 82, was joined by his children Dylan, 26, and Carys, 23, for the Tibet House US Art of Freedom Gala on Wednesday, November 4, where he received a Lifetime Achievement Award recognising his humanitarian work.

While Michael has spent decades commanding attention on screen, it was a sweet family moment that stood out in photographs from the evening, with Carys linking arms with her father as the trio posed together.

Standing between his youngest children, the actor smiled broadly for the cameras, with Dylan leaning in beside him against the charity's burgundy-and-white backdrop.

Carys and Dylan Douglas' striking gala outfits

Carys looked effortlessly elegant in a fitted black roll-neck top, which she paired with a pale gold midi skirt embellished with rows of light-catching sequins.

The understated silhouette allowed the shimmering details to take centre stage, while black strappy heels and a small textured handbag completed her evening look.

She wore her brunette hair loose around her shoulders, tucking it behind one ear to reveal an ornate gold drop earring. Soft pink lipstick and a delicate flick of eyeliner added the finishing touches.

Dylan, meanwhile, embraced autumnal tailoring in a brown checked suit, worn over an open-neck patterned shirt in shades of green, rust and gold.

With the shirt's cuffs extending beyond his jacket sleeves, the outfit had a relaxed, retro feel, complemented by brown suede shoes.

Their father looked equally smart in a navy blazer and coordinating open-neck shirt, teamed with dark trousers, a black belt and polished shoes.

The family opted for individual looks, but their warm tones and relaxed tailoring made for a particularly stylish appearance together.

Michael Douglas receives a Lifetime Achievement Award

The evening recognised a side of Michael's life that extends far beyond his celebrated film career. Tibet House US honoured the actor for his longstanding service as a United Nations Messenger of Peace and his advocacy for peace and nuclear disarmament. He was appointed to the UN role in 1998, making the award a recognition of almost three decades of commitment.

Photographs captured the delighted star holding his glass award above his head, smiling as guests applauded around him.

In another image, Michael stood at a lectern beneath a large screen bearing his name and the words "The Art of Freedom", addressing guests seated at candlelit dinner tables.

The gala also honoured Birgitte Nena Thurman and, posthumously, Robert A.F. Thurman, recognising their contributions to preserving Tibetan culture and their foundational roles in Tibet House US.

Catherine Zeta-Jones on supporting Carys and Dylan's ambitions

Despite having two Oscar-winning parents, Carys and Dylan grew up with a deliberate distance from the Hollywood spotlight.

Catherine previously explained to HELLO! that she and Michael arranged their schedules so that one of them could be with the children while the other worked. "We never took them on location. If I was working, Michael wasn't and vice versa. 

That upbringing did little to diminish the siblings' enthusiasm for performing. Catherine described their passion for the craft, explaining that both remained determined to pursue acting despite their parents' frank conversations about the challenges of finding work.

The actress also acknowledged that their famous surname brought its own expectations, with the children potentially having to work harder to establish themselves beyond their family's achievements.

Having experienced how much her own career had enriched her life, however, Catherine was determined to support their ambitions. "But I would never quash anyone's dreams, especially my children."
