Pinheiro Braathen leads ahead of Meillard, with Odermatt slightly behind

TheTools · 25.10.2026 13:06 · source · permalink

Alpine Skiing

Loïc Meillard is the top Swiss skier after the first run in Sölden

Keystone

Following the women's giant slalom, the men's World Cup season also kicked off with the first run on Sunday. The top Swiss finisher is Loïc Meillard in second place. Lucas Pinheiro Braathen is in the lead.

The Brazilian, who secured the small crystal globe ahead of Marco Odermatt last season, put on a bold and daring performance. Pinheiro Braathen was rewarded for this with the fastest time. Only Meillard, who started immediately after him, came within 0.24 seconds of his time.

The first run—which began from the reserve start due to weather conditions—did not go as well as hoped for co-favorite Marco Odermatt. The three-time winner of the season opener in Sölden is currently in 4th place after 30 racers, about half a second behind the fastest time. Nevertheless, Odermatt is still in contention for a podium finish.

The other Swiss skiers in the top 30 were less impressive. Thomas Tumler is currently in 21st place in the provisional standings, while Luca Aerni (28th) and Gino Caviezel (29th) trail a bit further behind. It will likely be difficult for both of them to advance to the second run.

For Caviezel, it was the “right” comeback, since he had competed only once—in Schladming this past January—since his fall in Bromio in December 2024.
