Rothy's is celebrating its 10th anniversary with its largest brand campaign to date. Dubbed "Point of View," the campaign stars 16 women across fashion, culture, journalism, literature and film, including Meryl Streep's daughter and actress Mamie Gummer, Emmy Award-winning sports journalist and ESPN host Malika Andrews and New York Times bestselling author Coco Mellors. Along with these ladies, the campaign also features celebrity stylists Anda Gentile and Masha Mandzuka; social entrepreneur, founder and author Elisa Sednaoui; actress and creative producer Nathalie Love; model Laura Love; fine art photographer Tierney Gearon; artist and model Kesewa Aboah; artist, writer and model Jane Moseley; ceramic artist Teya Kelipa; writer, photographer and model Grace Burns; model Rai Leme; men's celebrity groomer Mira Chai Hyde; and music producer Julianne Jordan. As for how the company decided on these new campaign stars, Anna Doré, vice president of brand and communications at Rothy's, told FN in an interview that it all started with who were "organic fans" of the brand. Rather than using influencers or trend-driven personalities, Doré noted that the company deliberately chose women for the strength of their work and whose careers have been defined by strong points of view, reinforcing the campaign's broader message around individuality over trends. "We
