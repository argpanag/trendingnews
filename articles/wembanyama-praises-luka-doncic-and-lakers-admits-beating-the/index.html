Victor Wembanyama and the Spurs staged a 20-point comeback to beat Luka Doncic and the Lakers in the NBA Cup.

After how things have unfolded in their intense Friday NBA Cup matchup, Victor Wembanyama is now convinced that the Los Angeles Lakers are a dangerous and legit contending team.

Speaking in postgame after the San Antonio Spurs' 99-96 comeback victory, the French superstar praised Luka Doncic and the Lakers, admitting that he did not expect them to be this competitive at the start of the season.

"[Luka Doncic] is the same that I've seen all season and I was surprised of the quality of the game that they offered and that he offered collectively," Wembanyama told reporters. "I didn't know what to expect with Luka, but as a team, I wasn't sure what they were built off just from the start of a season, but it's real.

"It's for real. They're good."

The San Antonio Spurs and the Los Angeles Lakers have entered their Friday night showdown as two of the best teams in the Western Conference to start the 2026-27 season.

As such, the Spurs found themselves facing a huge deficit against the Lakers to start the game, trailing by as many as 20 points before launching a strong second-half comeback led by Victor Wembanyama (25 points, 16 rebounds, 4 steals, and 2 blocks) and Stephon Castle (26 points and 9 assists).

With the dramatic win, the Spurs clinched a spot in the 2026 NBA Cup Knockout Rounds while eliminating the Lakers.

"Yeah, it is," Wembanyama said when asked if beating the Lakers has been the most satisfying win of their season so far after their comeback. "It's a different scenario that we faced before, but I like how my guys reacted. It just showed character and it's getting us ready for the next step of the season.

"Even if we had lost this game somehow, we would have learned a lot from it. So we learn and those games are going to be more and more common and we're getting ready.
