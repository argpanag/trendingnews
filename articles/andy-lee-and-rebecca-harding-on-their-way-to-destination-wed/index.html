Andy Lee and Rebecca Harding on their way to destination wedding after months of secrecy

TheTools · 10.10.2026 00:42 · source · permalink
  • Entertainment
  • Celebrity
  • Aussie stars

October 10, 2026 - 12.42am

Aussie comedian Andy Lee and his fiancée Rebecca Harding are heading to their destination wedding!

Lee, 45, obliquely confirmed that the couple were on their way to their wedding in an Instagram story this morning, posting a video of his friend running towards him in the airport with the caption “almost had to replace a groomsman.”

Andy lee drops massive hints about upcoming wedding Instagram

His friend was lugging a suit bag while a woman can be seen speed walking behind him with their carry on luggage, heading towards Lee.

Lee then pans the camera to a Qantas gate attendant, asking her to “page him” while he sprinted towards them.

“Can you page him? Michael Horgan,” Lee can be heard asking.

“Hello everyone Qantas 7 to Dallas-Fort Worth is urgently paging Michael Horgan, place make your way to the gate for boarding,” she says over the intercom.

The short video revealed the most details we’ve heard about the wedding yet, as the event has been kept top secret by Lee and his finance, Harding.

Harding has also been posting snaps to her Instagram, with one showing a fruit platter with a card reading “congratulations.” She also posted a photo of her and lee, captioning the post “husband-to-be.”

Videos and photos of her “pre-wedding” facial followed, as well as a picture of her wearing Qantas’s signature business class pyjamas.

Rebecca harding and andy lee on the way to their wedding Instagram/@Rebeccaharding

Rebecca Harding on Qantas flight headed to her wedding Instagram/@Rebeccaharding

While we still don’t know the exact date, we now know that Lee and his bridal party are jetting across the world for the event.

They might be getting married in Dallas, or they could be getting onto a connecting flight to their final destination somewhere near the Americas - perhaps the Caribbean?

A second post from Lee showed his groomsmen sitting in the lounge section on board the plane with beers in front of them.

He captioned the post “He made it! Sky Beers #itmustbenice.”

Lee and Harding both celebrated their Hens and Buck’s nights in August.

Harding’s hens included a lavish start at the Crown Melbourne before embarking on a period themed garden party. Meanwhile, Lee was ambushed by his friends in the basement of his new home, who shoved him into a bus and took him on a day of drinking and footy.

Andy lee drops massive hints about upcoming wedding Instagram

Speaking to nine.com.au in June about their upcoming nuptials, the star revealed the pair are going for a “fairly traditional” vibe, but also want it to feel like a “big, fun party”.

She also gave a hint about her bridal look, teasing it will be traditional.

“My personal style is much the same, so I imagine my bridal look will follow suit,” she revealed.

The soon-to-be newlyweds, who are building their “dream home”, announced their engagement in 2024, getting engaged after a milestone ten years together.

“People keep saying we’ve rushed into this but we don’t care what people say! 💍,” Lee wrote in a humorous joint Instagram post at the time.
