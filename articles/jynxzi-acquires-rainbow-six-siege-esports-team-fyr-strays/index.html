Jynxzi Acquires Rainbow Six Siege Esports Team FYR Strays

TheTools · 11.11.2026 02:40 · source · permalink

November 6, 2026

2 Min Read

Noted Rainbow Six Siege player and Twitch streamer, Jynxzi, has acquired Rainbow Six Siege esports team the FYR Strays from CAMPFYR. The team will now be called Jynxzi Esports. Founded in 2026, the team has been undefeated in its inaugural season, taking first prize in the NA Open Cup Palmetto Bay 2026 and the NA Open Cup Finals 2026. Currently, the FYR Strays have qualified directly into the Six Invitational (SI) Last Chance Qualifier (LCQ) playoff phase. During that tournament, eight teams will battle it out in best-of-three double-elimination matches to earn a coveted spot to the next SI. The FYR Strays also qualified directly into the playoff phase for the Challenger Finals tournament. If they win, they will earn a spot in the Blast R6 Tier 1 North America League in 2027.

Jynxzi is one of the most popular Rainbow Six Siege creators in the world, and has collaborated with Ubisoft on several Siege events. Earlier this year, he hosted Jynxzi's Tournament, where 16 creators faced off in 2v2 matches for six hours.
