CAPE ELIZABETH, Maine (WGME) -- Two animals have tested positive for West Nile virus in Cape Elizabeth.

The town announced Thursday that a crow and wild turkey tested positive for the disease, two months after another positive case was recorded there.

Falmouth also reported an infected crow earlier this month.

  • Also read: Maine CDC finds case of West Nile virus in crow in Falmouth

While humans can become infected with West Nile virus, there have been no human cases in Maine this year, and the risk of contracting a mosquito-borne illness is still low.

As a precaution, the CDC encourages people to use bug spray or wear clothes that cover their limbs.

If you find three or more dead animals in one place, contact the Maine Department of Inland Fisheries and Wildlife.
