How They Met: Gráinne Seoige fell for Leon Jordaan's non-game playing ways

TheTools · 25.09.2026 13:52 · source · permalink

The old saying that love hits when you least expect it might just ring true for Gráinne Seoige when a normal day of the office turned into so much more.

These days the TV host is happily married to Leonard (Leon) Jordaan and the past few years have been a whirlwind of major life changes from launching new businesses to moving halfway around the world and back.

For Gráinne, who was previously married to Stephen Cullinane until 2010, meeting Leon was definitely a lucky second chance at love as she told VIP magazine: 'I'm really lucky that I met the love of my life a little bit later.'

'We were both 38 when we met, and at that age, you're not game-playing. And if you don't have love in your life, how successful are you really?'. So, just who is the businessman who captured her heart and how did their relationship blossom?

South African-born Leon hails from the Cape Town area.

Sadly he has lost both of his parents. His father passed away shortly before their wedding in December 2019, something he said prompted the couple to return to Ireland (they were living in South Africa at the time) to be closer to Gráinne's parents.

Leon began his career as a rugby player with spells at the Cape Town Harlequins, Hamilton, and with teams based in Pretoria and Northern Transvaal U-20s.

He studied personal training and sports psychology, achieving a diploma in both before turning his hand to coaching with a stint training at the high-performance coaching academy in New Zealand.

It was his coaching that brought him to Ireland, and in 2010 he took up a position at Navan RFC, which he held for three years.

He is listed as a co-founder of Gráinne's Grace Diamonds jewellery business and in 2021 set about bringing the South African tradition of Braai (hosting BBQ's for friends and family) to Ireland. Leon teamed up with CJ Stander and Darrin Claasin to launch Fireboks, which sells high-end BBQ equipment.

Leon met Gráinne in 2011 while they were both working on Reality Bites and, by all accounts, have been inseparable ever since, even with an ocean between them.

They went public with their relationship at trainer Paul Byrne's birthday bash in 2012, where she apparently introduced him to her celeb pals. A month later they were the main attraction at an Irish Cancer Society fundraiser at Leon's rugby club where Gráinne was the guest of honour.

When his contract at Navan wound up in 2013, Leon returned to South Africa while Gráinne remained in Ireland with work commitments, but even the long distance between them didn't get in the way of their relationship.

Amid mounting speculation in 2014 that Gráinne was about to up sticks to be with Leon, her spokesperson told The Mirror: 'There's no joint living between here and South Africa; she is based here because she has her Crimecall going on, and all her other RTÉ stuff. She does everything from here; she and Leon are definitely still together. Absolutely. '

In November 2013, Leon got down on one knee and proposed with a ring that he had specially made for Gráinne in South Africa.

The super-private couple managed to keep their good news under wraps, but the secret was out in 2014 when they hosted an engagement party at Lillie's Bordello nightclub.

Speaking to The Independent at the time, she gushed: 'He understands what makes me tick. He gets me. What I love about him is his sense of kindness and fun. He also has a strong sense of what's fair and what's right. And we're obviously incredibly happy together.'

Not only did Gráinne gain a hubby-to-be with the proposal, but she was also inspired to launch Grace Diamonds. 'He had the ring made for me over there and designed it. I thought you walked into a shop and somebody showed you a tray and you pick one.

'He showed me it was a different way, that you could have a ring designed and made especially for you... Then when we had our engagement party, and we showed it to people, and they were so touched by it, we said "I think this is a business",' she explained to The Sunday World.

Gráinne ultimately decided that the relationship was worth leaving showbiz for, and in 2016 she moved to Pretoria to be with Leon.

Her home away from home proved to be a great fit, and she soon adapted to the outdoorsy lifestyle, telling RTÉ Today: 'It's quite a different lifestyle in that you spend a lot of time outdoors because the weather is so beautiful.

'You can hop into your van and drive for an hour and be in the bush and be watching a giraffe stroll by or you see a rhino. Those kinds of things help you focus on your space in the world, how important you are, which is not very, which I found really wonderful.

Another selling point was anonymity. She told EVOKE: 'In South Africa, there’s a completely different world that I get to experience. I have a lot of private time as well and that’s wonderful. I really enjoy that decompression from the spotlight.'

After a three-year engagement, which Gráinne explained was down to the pressures of launching the business and the arrival of her sister Sile's baby, they finally tied the knot in Ireland in 2019.

The couple said their 'I dos' in front of family and friends at the luxurious Cliff at Lyons Hotel in Kildare, which she described as 'such a beautiful venue, it’s like a chocolate box, and then at Christmas time when it’s all decorated, it’s at another level altogether.'

Her sweeping beaded dress was made by New York-based designer and pal Don O'Neill.

As she told EVOKE. 'This is somebody who knows me really well, knows my body shape, knows my style, and would have a really good feeling for what I would like. And then he took it to the next level.'

Gráinne and Leon stunned fans when they traded in their life in the sun to return to Galway in 2020, but they had good reason.

'Leon explained to the Irish Independent: 'I love Ireland, I’ve got my citizenship already. Plus Grainne’s parents are both still alive and in very good shape and they’re young at heart and very active.

'I lost my dad last December, just before the wedding actually, so both my parents have passed away and we felt her parents were getting old without us being there.'

Gráinne dropped another bombshell when she announced that she was putting down her presenting mic to step into the world of politics, running for Fianna Fáil in Galway West in 2024.

She believes an AI-generated pornographic image of her, which made the rounds during the elections, played a huge part in her failure to secure a seat.

On RTÉ's Prime Time, Gráinne said this 'was the most shocking thing that’s ever happened to me in my life', and that it took her a long time to process it.

Gráinne has since made a documentary about her experience.
