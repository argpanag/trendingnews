The Gala Where Magic Begins: Once Upon a Match: The Magic of Mentorship - Signals AZ

TheTools · 11.09.2026 22:23 · source · permalink

Yavapai Big Brothers Big Sisters is inviting the community to step into a fairytale evening at its annual gala, “The Gala Where Magic Begins: Once Upon a Match: The Magic of Mentorship,” on Saturday, October 24, 2026, at the Prescott Resort Ballroom.

This year’s gala celebrates the life-changing power of mentoring relationships and the magic that happens when a young person is matched with a caring adult who believes in their potential.

Guests will enjoy an enchanted evening featuring dinner, silent and live auctions, dancing, a cash bar, magical stories, a Share-Your-Mentor Wall, and a special raffle prize from Raskin’s Jewelers, along with other fairytale surprises.

The evening will feature Patrick Schweiss, Executive Director of the Sedona International Film Festival, as special emcee.

An Enchanted Evening of Dining

Guests will be treated to a selection of entrées from the Enchanted Kitchen, including:

  • Grilled Flat Iron Steak
  • Wild Mushroom Sauce Breast of Chicken
  • Grilled Vegetable Tofu Tower
  • Pan-Roasted Atlantic Salmon

Entrées will be served with garlic whipped potatoes and honey-roasted baby carrots, followed by a flourless chocolate cake for dessert.

The gala is a formal-attire event, with guests also encouraged to bring some storybook magic to the evening by dressing as their favorite fairytale character.

Sponsors include APS, Findlay Toyota, Dignity Health, Pro Sound, Summit Administrative Services, Triple E Construction, Tlaquepaque Arts & Shopping Village, Davinci Realty, and Mark Carlile, Edward Jones.

Doors open at 5 p.m., with dinner served at 6 p.m.

Tickets are $150 per ticket or $285 per couple and are available at azbigs.org. Guests may also call 928-778-5135 for tickets, table pricing, or sponsorship opportunities.

Creating More Moments That Matter

Yavapai Big Brothers Big Sisters creates and professionally supports one-to-one mentoring relationships that help ignite the power and promise in young people. Through the generosity of community members, businesses, sponsors, and donors, the organization is able to connect youth with caring adult mentors and provide the professional support needed to help those relationships thrive.

The 2026 gala offers an opportunity not only to enjoy a memorable evening, but also to invest in the magic of mentorship and the possibilities it creates for local youth.

About Yavapai Big Brothers Big Sisters

Yavapai Big Brothers Big Sisters connects young people with caring adult mentors and provides the professional support necessary to build strong, meaningful mentoring relationships. Through mentoring, the organization works to help young people discover their potential and create their biggest possible futures.
