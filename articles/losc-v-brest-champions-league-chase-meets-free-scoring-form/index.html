LOSC v Brest: Champions League chase meets free-scoring form

LOSC host Brest in Ligue 1 on Saturday, 17 October 2026 at Stade Pierre-Mauroy in Villeneuve d'Ascq, with the hosts second on 13 points targeting a firmer grip on the Champions League places and the visitors in seventh looking to climb.

Recent meetings have been tight: the last two league games between LOSC and Brest ended level, 1-1 in February 2026 and 3-3 in August 2025. That balance frames a contest with points and early season positioning at stake.

Form points to contrasts. LOSC have won four of six league games with four clean sheets, edging Le Havre last time out. Brest arrive on a high after hitting five against Angers and have two wins and two draws from six, suggesting growing momentum.

Selection could matter. LOSC list Ayase Ueda, Hamza Igamane and Tiago Santos as injured, while Brest have Brendan Chardonnet, Giovani Versini and Raphaël Le Guen sidelined. In the hosts' attack, Olivier Giroud and Maurits Kjaergaard have supplied two league assists each, while Dilane Bakwa leads their scoring with two. For the visitors, Kamory Doumbia sits on four league goals and Ludovic Ajorque offers a focal point.

The match-up may hinge on LOSC's control and defence against Brest's volume and delivery. The hosts have conceded only four league goals with 85.84% passing accuracy, while Brest have scored 12 from 94 shots, slung in 86 open play crosses and won 110 aerial duels. Crosses towards Ajorque could test the LOSC back line, but the home defence has been efficient.

A home win would keep LOSC firmly in the Champions League spots and could apply pressure to the leaders. Victory for Brest would push them up from seventh and closer to the European places in a congested early table.
