Callum Osmand Injury Scare Eased as Celtic Confirm Cramp Against Celta Vigo

TheTools · 23.10.2026 13:20 · source · permalink

Prefer dave.sport on Google and see our stories first in Top Stories.

Martin O’Neill says Callum Osmand was suffering from cramp when he came off against Celta Vigo, easing concerns over another injury setback for the Celtic forward.

James Forrest replaced Osmand after 58 minutes of Thursday’s Europa League defeat, according to Celtic’s official match centre.

The withdrawal raised questions after an injury-disrupted start to his season, but O’Neill attributed the problem to cramp rather than announcing a fresh injury.

What did O’Neill say about Osmand?

“Yeah, he was cramping. That’s totally understandable,” O’Neill said in his post-match assessment, reported by The Scotsman.

The Celtic manager explained that Osmand had played little football because of his injury absence and had been struggling with cramp whenever he moved during the final few minutes before his substitution.

O’Neill also praised the forward’s contribution as he works his way back into regular action.

Osmand was still involved in Celtic’s attack shortly before coming off. The club’s match commentary records him setting up Benjamin Nygren for a saved effort in the 56th minute, two minutes before Forrest replaced him.

Can Osmand play against Hibernian?

O’Neill’s explanation is encouraging ahead of Sunday’s trip to Hibernian, but it does not amount to confirmation that Osmand will start or be included in the squad.

Celtic’s fixture list has the Premiership match at Easter Road scheduled for noon GMT on Sunday 25 October.

The immediate question is therefore how Osmand recovers from Thursday’s exertions. No new lay-off or return date was announced in O’Neill’s post-match comments.

Celtic return to league action after European setback

Osmand left the field with Celtic leading through Mika Baur’s goal, before Javi Rodríguez struck twice to give Celta a 2-1 victory.

As our Celtic–Celta Vigo match report explains, the defeat leaves O’Neill’s side without a point from their opening three Europa League matches.

Sunday brings a return to domestic competition and a decision over Osmand’s involvement, with the manager’s initial assessment offering reassurance after his early exit.
