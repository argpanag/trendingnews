Spice Girls news live as announcement set and reunion rumours swirl

TheTools · 10.09.2026 13:24 · source · permalink

The Spice Girls sent fans into a frenzy after sharing a cryptic post on Instagram. The post, which was posted on Wednesday, September 9 to their official Instagram page, suggests that there will be an announcement at 2pm on Thursday, September 10.

The post is of an Instagram reel, with the instrumental to the group's first single "Wannabe" as the music. The Spice Girls released their iconic debut single Wannabe 30 years ago on July 8, 1996. The video appears to show the Grand Staircase at St. Pancras Renaissance Hotel which was used for the Wannabe music video.

It has now been revealed that the girl group will release 14-disc colour vinyl + CD box sets, Original artwork + B-sides, and previously unreleased demos. There has been no confirmation of a reunion or tour.

The ECHO will continue to share live updates after the Spice Girls revealed their 2pm announcement.

It reads: "For the first time, all 14 Spice Girls singles come together in one collection available as two limited-edition 14-disc box sets on 7” colour vinyl and CD.

"From Wannabe to Holler, Spice Up Your Life to Goodbye, The Singles Collection brings together the singles, with original artwork, B-sides, rare remixes, and previously unreleased recordings from the archive."

Victoria Beckham has not performed with the group since the closing ceremony of the London 2012 Olympic Games. Although Posh hasn't toured with the group since 2007 and said she "didn't belong on the stage", she admitted she would be open to a residency.

She said: "It would be tempting. But could I take on a world tour? No I can't. I have a job. How good would the Spice Girls be at the Sphere! I love the idea of it. I mean I don't know if I could even still sing, I mean I was never that great."

The post has caused many fans to speculate that a long-awaited reunion could be on the cards. It may come as a surprise, but the Spice Girls have only had four concert tours. They are listed below:

  • Spiceworld Tour - 1998
  • Christmas in Spiceworld Tour - 1999
  • The Return of the Spice Girls Tour - 2007-2008
  • Spice World - 2019 (as a four piece after Victoria Beckham chose not to take part)
