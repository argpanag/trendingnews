Ireland's Fittest Family: Meet tonight's competitors

TheTools · 08.11.2026 06:02 · source · permalink

Ireland's Fittest Family is back with more thrills, more fierce competition and an exciting brand new location thrown into the mix.

Now in its 14th season, the beloved show is back with coaches Anna Geary, Donncha O'Callaghan, Davy Fitzgerald, Ellen Keane, Michael Darragh MacAuley and reigning champion Andrew Trimble.

Kicking off in the historic Haulbowline Navy Base, the Heats will unfold over three weeks. Each week, two coaches will go head-to-head, each with two families.

For week two, it's the turn of Michael Darragh MacAuley to take on Davy Fitzgerald.

Michael Darragh's teams

The Gibsons from Donegal

Mam Kathy-Ann, 49, is a PE Teacher and TY Coordinator. She played badminton and hockey but gave it up two years ago and now does her own fitness routine and swims once a week. The staff in her school convinced her to sign up this year - her children have been wanting to do it for the last five years!

Daughter Jessica, 14, is a new addition to the team as her older sister Hannah was the family's first pick but due to scheduling conflicts Hannah is unable to compete. Jessica is very excited to step up! She is a 2nd Year student at the school that Kathy-Ann teaches at.

Son Joel, 19, is a 1st year student in Magee University of Ulster studying Sport and Exercise Science. He plays football for Dergview United, Castlederg.

Son Matthew, 21, is in his 3rd year at University of Ulster studying Engineering and is currently on placement in Manchester. He comes home every second weekend. He plays rugby for his University and Coleraine.

The Muwaniris from Laois

Mum, Rudo, is a 65-year-old nurse living in Portarlington who loves to run - something she feels is a great stress reliever. So far she has run several half-marathons and two full marathons. She is very excited to be taking part with her two sons and grandson. She has already been showing them up and beat all three of them in the plank at the Fitness Test Day in Athlone!

Jaden is Oz’s son and is in 5th year in secondary school. He used to play a lot of football but in the last year has been focusing on school. He wants to get back into football and has continued to keep fit.

Son Solly is the one who signed up the family. He currently lives in Amsterdam but loves the show and convinced them all that they should take part. Solly is a personal trainer and runs his own fitness company, Famba Fitness.

Ozmond (or Oz), 42, is a Pastor at L15 church in Galway. He lives in Clarecastle and can’t wait to compete with his son Jaden, his Mam and his brother. Oz is also a runner and used to play to rugby. He is very passionate about fitness and keeping fit.

Jaden is Oz’s son and is in 5th year in secondary school. He used to play a lot of football but in the last year has been focusing on school. He wants to get back into football and has continued to keep fit.

Davy's teams

The Treacys from Tipperary

Dad Stephen, 55, is a quality engineer in Limerick. He has played hurling for Portumna for the past 20 years and was on the 2006 team that won the Club All-Ireland. Despite playing for Portumna, they live in Tipperary and Stephen trains the local senior camogie team. He ran five marathons in his 40s and continues to run regularly.

Daughter Ailbhe is 17 and in 5th year. She also works part-time in a local nursing home. She plays camogie and soccer and is on the Minor Tipperary Camogie panel. She recently won a Schools All-Ireland Soccer title and is a Munster Schools Camogie Championship winner.

Son Darragh is 19 and studies General Engineering in UL. He plays hurling for his club Silvermines and has won U19 and U21 titles. He recently won a Freshers All-Ireland Hurling title with UL. Outside of sport, he milks cows "for a lad he knows" and goes to the gym as much as possible.

Cathal is 20 and studies civil engineering at UL. He plays hurling, soccer, rugby and GAA and has won many titles at club and college level.

The Healys from Cork

Dad Noel, 59, is a fitter/welder who loves to keep active, particularly with his family. They run, hike and walk together whenever possible and Noel’s favourite tradition is their annual Christmas Day swim, which they do "every year no matter the weather".

Daughter Melua, 19, has just completed her first year at UCC, studying Biological and Chemical Sciences, and, like the rest of her family, loves GAA, running, hiking and swimming. She was also on the Cork Minor Camogie panel.

Daughter Shanna, 27, is a primary school teacher who also plays GAA. She "loves just being outdoors" and loves running. She is a member of her local athletics club and has completed several half-marathons.

Son Kelvin,31, is an electrical and instrumentation technician for a pharmaceutical company. He plays GAA for his local club, as well as being a marathon runner, going to the gym, surfing and sea swimming. He has a black belt in taekwondo and recently won a county final title with his GAA club.

Ireland's Fittest Family returns on RTÉ One and RTÉ Player on Sunday 1 November at 6.30pm.
