Rahman Dashdamirov: "Barcelona's strength lies in their team play, not individual stars"

TheTools · 24.11.2026 12:12 · source · permalink

Sabah defender Rahman Dashdamirov has shared his thoughts ahead of the UEFA Champions League clash against Barcelona.

"We analyse Barcelona as a team rather than focusing on individual players. That's what our preparation is based on. Barcelona's strength lies in their collective play. Great teams don't depend on individual players, and Barcelona have an entire team of stars," the 27-year-old defender told EFE.

Dashdamirov described facing the Catalan giants as a source of pride for Sabah and a dream for every footballer.

"Playing against a great team in front of a packed stadium brings added responsibility. But once the referee blows the whistle, all those emotions disappear. From that moment on, you're focused solely on the game."

The defender expressed confidence in head coach Valdas Dambrauskas's ability to devise the right tactical approach.

"It's difficult to maintain your own playing style against such strong teams. However, I believe our head coach will devise a tactical system that allows the players to fulfil their potential."

The Azerbaijan international also reflected on Sabah's remarkable rise to the Champions League just nine years after the club was founded.

"Hosting such great European teams in Baku already means we've achieved what we set out to do. But on Wednesday, we'll go out there looking to get the best possible result. If our fans leave the stadium happy, it will mean we've achieved our objective."

Dashdamirov is expected to start in place of suspended South African defender Aiden McCarthy, who was sent off against Norwegian side Viking.

After four rounds of the Champions League league phase, Barcelona sit fifth in the standings with nine points, while Sabah occupy 31st place with three points.

The match will take place at Baku Olympic Stadium on November 25, with kick-off scheduled for 21:45 local time.
