Oppo A7 Pro Launched: 8,000mAh Battery, IP69K Rating, and Price Revealed

TheTools · 08.09.2026 18:11 · source · permalink

After the August debut of the monstrous 10,000mAh A7 Pro Max, Oppo has quietly expanded its battery-centric lineup with the official listing of the Oppo A7 Pro on its Chinese website. Ahead of its scheduled September 11 market release, the listing reveals a smartphone that strikes a rare balance between ultra-high battery capacity, a remarkably slim profile, and rugged-tier durability.

Here is a comprehensive breakdown of Oppo’s latest mid-range endurance champion, including its pricing, specifications, and potential global future.

Oppo A7 Pro : Unprecedented Battery Life in a Slim Profile

The undeniable centerpiece of the Oppo A7 Pro is its massive 8,000mAh battery. Historically, power cells of this magnitude have been relegated to bulky, ruggedized niche devices. However, Oppo’s engineering has managed to squeeze this colossal battery into a chassis measuring just 8.8mm thick and weighing a manageable 205 grams. When the device does eventually need a top-up, it supports 45W fast wired charging.

Beneath the surface, the A7 Pro is powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 6360 Max processor, capable of reaching peak clock speeds of 2.4GHz. To ensure sustained performance during demanding tasks or gaming sessions, Oppo has equipped the phone with a substantial 4,300mm² vapor chamber (VC) cooling system. The device utilizes LPDDR4X RAM and UFS 2.2 storage, with the added flexibility of microSD card expansion.

Premium Display and Rugged Build

Oppo has not compromised on the visual experience or build quality. The A7 Pro sports a 6.57-inch flexible OLED display offering a crisp 2,372 x 1,080 resolution. With a 120Hz refresh rate, a 240Hz touch sampling rate, 10-bit color depth, and a peak brightness of 1,400 nits, the screen is built to handle both fluid media consumption and bright outdoor conditions.

What truly sets the A7 Pro apart in its price bracket is its durability. The handset boasts an impressive trifecta of ingress protection ratings—IP69K, IP68, and IP66—making it highly resistant to dust, continuous submersion, and even high-pressure, high-temperature water jets.

AI-Enhanced Software and Camera Hardware

Out of the box, the A7 Pro runs on ColorOS 16, based on the latest Android 16. This new iteration of ColorOS places a heavy emphasis on user security, introducing a suite of AI-powered fraud protection tools. These include real-time scam call warnings, advanced AI face-swap detection, and robust cross-app data protection.

For optics, the device features a streamlined setup housed within a horizontal, pill-shaped rear module. The primary shooter is a 50-megapixel lens with an f/1.9 aperture and autofocus, paired with an 8-megapixel front-facing camera for selfies and video calls. Interestingly, the rear camera module also seamlessly integrates the LED flash and an infrared (IR) blaster, allowing the phone to double as a universal remote for household appliances.

Other notable features include a 300% “super-volume” audio mode designed for noisy environments, an in-display optical fingerprint sensor, and comprehensive connectivity options including 5G, Wi-Fi 5, Bluetooth 5.4, and multifunctional NFC.

Pricing and Global Availability

The Oppo A7 Pro will officially go on sale in China on September 11 in three evocative colorways: Riding the Waves, Every Step Blossoms, and Desert Brown.

Pricing is aggressively set for the mid-range market:

  • 6GB RAM + 256GB Storage: 2,199 Yuan (approx. $330)

  • 8GB RAM + 256GB Storage: 2,499 Yuan (approx. $370)

6GB RAM + 256GB Storage: 2,199 Yuan (approx. $330)

8GB RAM + 256GB Storage: 2,499 Yuan (approx. $370)

Will it launch globally? Evidence strongly suggests an international rollout is imminent. A global variant carrying the model number CPH2931 has recently cleared regulatory certifications in several regions, including Thailand (NBTC), Malaysia (SIRIM), and the UAE (TDRA). Furthermore, the device has appeared in India’s BIS database, fueling speculation that the A7 Pro will be rebranded as the Oppo F35 for the Indian market.
