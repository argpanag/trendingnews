Reliance Jio has launched a new 3-Year Unlimited Connectivity Plan in India, priced at Rs 10,000. The plan offers unlimited 5G data, voice calling, daily high-speed data benefits and access to digital services, including JioHotstar, Canva Pro Lite and JioAICloud storage.

It comes with a validity of three years, allowing subscribers to access these benefits through a single recharge without annual renewals.

The new Jio plan will be available starting November 7, 2026. Customers can purchase it through the MyJio app, Jio.com, Jio stores, retail outlets and other Jio recharge channels across the country.

The Jio 3-Year Unlimited Connectivity Plan is priced at Rs 10,000 and comes with a validity of 36 months. It offers unlimited 5G data, unlimited voice calling, 2.5GB of high-speed data per day and 100 SMS per day.

Apart from connectivity benefits, the plan bundles subscriptions and cloud storage services. These include JioHotstar Mobile + Hollywood access, which provides access to live sports, premium entertainment, Hotstar Originals and Hollywood content.

Subscribers will also receive a 12-month Canva Pro Lite subscription, offering access to premium design templates and tools. Additionally, the plan includes 50GB of complimentary storage on JioAICloud, allowing users to store and access their files, photos and documents.
