Writer Banned in Belarus Dies – REFORM.news

TheTools · 23.10.2026 14:47 · source · permalink

Reform.news Editorial Team · 23 October 2026, 11:59

Japanese writer Ryu Murakami has died. According to a statement by Japanese publisher Gentosha, he died on October 17 at the age of 74. A farewell ceremony was held in a small family gathering.

Ryu Murakami’s debut novel, “Almost Transparent Blue“, was published in 1976. It received the Akutagawa Prize and became a bestseller. Murakami wrote more than 40 novels in total.

Last year, novels by the Japanese writer were added to the “list of printed publications containing information and/or materials whose distribution may harm the national interests of the Republic of Belarus.” The banned titles included “Topaz“, “Ecstasy“, “All Shades of Blue“ and “Karaoke“.
