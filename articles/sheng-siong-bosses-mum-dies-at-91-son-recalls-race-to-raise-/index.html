Sheng Siong bosses' mum dies at 91; son recalls race to raise $2m ransom after bank released only $800k

TheTools · 07.09.2026 03:43 · source · permalink

Ng Lye Poh, mother to the founders of supermarket chain Sheng Siong, has died at the age of 91.

She died at home on Saturday (Sept 5) surrounded by family. 

Thrust into the public eye after being abducted in 2014, Ng was fondly nicknamed "Sheng Siong ah ma", and remembered as someone approachable and kind, reported Lianhe Zaobao.

Speaking to Zaobao at Woodlands Memorial, CEO Lim Hock Chee, the 5th of nine children, said his mother liked to patronise markets and had a "voice which arrived before her".

Recalling the abduction case in January 2014, Lim said he had experienced a moment of disbelief when kidnapper Lee Sze Yong contacted him and demanded $20 million in ransom for Ng's release.

Lee had tricked the elderly woman by telling her that Lim had suffered a fall, and that he was tasked to drive her to the Sheng Siong office. Ng was later blindfolded by Lee and held in the car.

Lim told Zaobao that he was unable to contact his mother. The man and his two brothers took the threat seriously and lodged a police report.

"I foolishly called the bank to tell them to prepare $20 million in cash," he said. "How could the bank have that much cash? I could only get $800,000."

Lim said he then scrambled to withdraw all the $1,000 banknotes he could, and ultimately amassed $2 million in ransom money.

He dropped the cash off at a tree in Sembawang Park that night and left without searching for Lee's presence. Ng was subsequently let free at a bus stop along Jalan Kayu.

Lim added: "When she was rescued, I said, 'You have nine children. If I really fell and was bleeding badly...you still have eight others who will fetch you.'"

Ng continued her outings as usual after the incident, with family reminding her to bring their helper along, reported Zaobao.

According to Lim, Ng had suffered from diabetes for over a decade and had heart surgery about three years ago. The senior was admitted to hospital in 2025 for asthma, with her heath deteriorating since then.

Lim told Zaobao that the $2 million ransom recovered by the police was donated to the National Crime Prevention Council.

The family will donate condolence money given by wake attendees to charity under Ng's name.

Lee was sentenced to life imprisonment in 2016.
