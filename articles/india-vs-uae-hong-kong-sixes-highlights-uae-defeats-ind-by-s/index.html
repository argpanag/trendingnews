India and UAE met in a Group C encounter at the Hong Kong Sixes 2026.

UAE finishes it off in style! Khalid Shah deposits Krunal Pandya for a six and a four to complete the chase! UAE wins by six wickets! What a comprehensive performance!

The sixes and fours continue to flow and UAE are now just 11 runs away from the target with 14 balls to go! 

UAE continues to pile on the runs! Nitish Rana becomes the latest to feel the wrath of the UAE openers! 

Four, four six! Whatever Khalid can do, so can Ansh Tandon, he’s smashing Shashank Singh to all parts of the park. UAE 42/0 in two overs.

Best possible start for UAE! Khalid Shah smacks Natarajan for two fours and as many sixes! UAE collect 25 runs off the first over!

Natarajan showing the batters how its done, he is on a roll at the moment! Sixes and fours flying off his bat as he takes India close to the 100-run mark. Two balls to go, can he finish on a high? Not quite, he falls on the last ball of the first innings. India finish on 94/6, over to UAE 

Krunal gets in on the act, strikes a four and a six! And just as I type that, he becames the latest to walk back to the dugout.

Krunal Pandya c Ansh Tandon b Mohammed Faraazuddin 15(8) [4s-2 6s-1]

Kulwant Khejroliya is the last batter for India.

India can’t afford to lose any more wickets and need some boundaries. Zahid Ali to bowl the third over. Tripathi smacks him for a four and a six! That’s more like it from India! Make that a hattrick of sixes from Rahul Tripathi!

Oh no! Not again! India loses another and its the dangerous looking Rahul Tripathi! 

Rahul Tripathi c Mohammed Faraazuddin b Zahid Ali 24(7) [4s-1 6s-3]

T Natarajan is the new batter at the crease. India has run out of batting options, things not looking good with three more overs to go.

Muhammad Farooq will bowl the second over for UAE. Gets off to the worst possible start, starts with five wides! Another one bites the dust! Shashank Singh is the latest to fall! India in all sorts of trouble.

Shashank Singh c Mohammed Faraazuddin b Muhammad Farooq 1(3)

A couple of singles to finish the second. UAE all over India at the moment.

What a start for India! Nitish Rana gets going with a six off the very first ball! Oh no! I spoke too soon! Rana falls in the next delivery, to a caught and bowled to Zahid Ali. The UAE pacer gets the last laugh! 

Nitish Rana c and b Zahid Ali 6(2) [6s-1]

Interesting, change of tactics from India as its Rahul Tewatia, not Shashank Singh who is the new batter at the crease.

Oh no! India loses two in the first over! After exchanging a couple of singles, Tewatia is trapped LBW! Dream first over for Zahid! Tewatia departs, Shashank takes his place at the crease.

We are just a few moments away from the start of the match. India will bat first, with openers Rahul Tripathi and Nitish Rana leading the way. 

India: Rahul Tripathi(w), Nitish Rana, Shashank Singh, Rahul Tewatia, Krunal Pandya(c), T Natarajan, Kulwant Khejroliya
