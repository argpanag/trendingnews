Marco Silva could turn to Anísio Cabral for Benfica v Gil Vicente after Jhon Durán red

According to Glorioso 1904, Jhon Durán’s suspension has opened the door for Marco Silva to consider Anísio Cabral for Benfica’s Taça da Liga quarter-final against Gil Vicente.

Durán was sent off against Santa Clara and had been in line to start, but the straight red currently rules the number 22 out.

OneFootball Videos

Cabral has only 12 first-team minutes in 2026/27 and could make a maiden senior start, as fellow youngsters Daniel Banjaqui and José Neto have already managed. He could be chosen ahead of Vangelis Pavlidis.

Pavlidis remains an option. The number 14 is the fourth most used player this season and, with a league game against Alverca on Monday, he might be managed carefully.

Anísio, an under-17 European and world champion, recorded those 12 minutes in the 4-1 win at Omonia in Cyprus, and has been on the bench 16 times.

The tie against Luís Pinto’s side is scheduled for 29 October at 20:45 at Estádio da Luz.
