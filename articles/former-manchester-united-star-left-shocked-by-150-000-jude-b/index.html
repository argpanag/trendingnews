Former Manchester United star left shocked by £150,000 Jude Bellingham offer

TheTools · 15.11.2026 16:58 · source · permalink
  • Anton Stanley, Deputy Editor of talkSPORT.com
  • Published: 16:58, 15 Nov 2026
  • Updated: 17:14, 15 Nov 2026

Former Manchester United star Ben Foster has entered the realm of trading card collecting and one moment in the hobby left him in awe.

The ex-England goalkeeper has built a successful media empire, starting just before his retirement and continuing it as he, supposedly, sticks his feet up.

What is Ben Foster doing now?

His YouTube channel and his podcast have got him hitting new heights, along with the odd appearance on talkSPORT too.

And now he's also entered the booming hobby of football trading card collection, already snagging a Rio Ngumoha one of one.

Panini and Topps have completely changed the landscape of the industry with the latter now having flagship stores in London and Paris.

How valuable are sports trading cards?

Meanwhile, the amounts paid for cards is going through the roof with one Lewis Hamilton card around the £1million barrier.

As you might expect, Lionel Messi and Cristiano Ronaldo have seen their cards hit the seven-figure mark.

With their era ending, the next generation of superstars could also see their cards reach extraordinary sums.

Real Madrid star Jude Bellingham is one of those and Foster couldn't believe a story he heard about one of his cards.

What happened at the London Card Show?

"The London Card Show was incredible. Honestly," Foster said on the Fozcast.

"The amount of cards that people have in these collections and they have just got them behind a Perspex screen. You're talking collections worth £250,000.

"I watched a guy do a deal for a Rio Ngumoha one of one booklet for £14,000.

"Just watching them negotiating the deal. Just these two guys...

"One guy hit it on a Whatnot break, the other guy went 'I love that card, it's a one of one. It's beautiful. It's got an unbelievable Liverpool patch - £14,000'.

"I'm like 'wow'."

Co-host Tubes added: "Listen to this, Liam. He was there the other week. He's got that Jude Bellingham card.

"A guy walks up, he's like - '£150,000 for that card'. Liam went no."

"Wow," said a shocked Foster.

"That was his opening offer to buy that one card," Tubes continued.

"[Liam] went no because he's so passionate about Jude Bellingham and Birmingham, and his cards."

Why is the Jude Bellingham card worth more than £150,000?

The card in question is a 2023/24 Topps Stadium Club Chrome UCL, Marks of Excellence black foil one of one.

It's completely unique thanks to the inscription above the autograph 'Brum to the Bernabeu'.

It's now owned by a collector who also owns a number of other Bellingham cards as well as being a Birmingham City fan.

The card was even pictured at St Andrew's where they will no doubt hope Bellingham returns to when he's finished conquering in Madrid.
