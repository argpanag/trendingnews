Actor Patrick Bergin selling his Irish castle after 35 years

TheTools · 03.09.2026 10:33 · source · permalink

Sleeping with the Enemy star Patrick Bergin has put his Irish castle home on the market, with an asking price of nearly €1m.

The Dublin actor starred alongside the enigmatic Julia Roberts in the 1991 movie, where he played the coercive Martin Burney.

From there, Patrick has played a host of memorable roles including the titular role of Robin Hood. More recently, he appeared on EastEnders and Virgin Media's Red Rock.

Many of these roles were played when Patrick called the beautiful Emmel Castle, in Cloughjordan, Co Offaly, his home.

Now, 35 years after purchasing the four-bedroom castle, Patrick and his wife Helen Goldin are putting it on the market.

Speaking to the Irish Times, the 75-year-old confirmed the couple had been 'very happy' at the castle, but it 'needed young people.'

The castle is set on 20 acres of private woodlands and gardens and has four bedrooms and three bathrooms.

Located in County Offaly, the castle boasts a 15th-century Tower House as well as a 17th-century country house.

The home has three reception rooms, as well as plenty of woodland and gardens outside and a courtyard and outbuildings.

Access to the courtyard is by wrought iron gates, with the stone and bricks of the outbuilding dating back to 1883

Despite being etched in history, the castle is complete with electricity, broadband, oil-fired central heating and drainage via a septic tank.

Water comes from a private bore well.

Speaking to The Irish Times, Bergin admitted the castle was a 'big place for two' and conceded that 'everything is a drive here.'

He explained that the closest restaurants were in Nenagh, which is a 20-minute drive away.

Those happy to make use of their car will be greatly rewarded, with the castle only an hour and a half from Dublin Airport, and only an hour from both Galway and Shannon Airports.

Sherry Fitzgerald Talbot, who are looking after the sale, say potential buyers will be granted an 'exceptional rural lifestyle.'

The location is perfect for those particularly interested in country pursuits such as equestrian, fishing, shooting, water sports and hiking.
