Is the stock market open today? See Labor Day closures

TheTools · 07.09.2026 12:13 · source · permalink

Labor Day marks the unofficial end of summer, and it also means on Monday, most federal and state buildings will be closed.

Here's a guide to plan for the holiday.

When is Labor Day 2026?

Monday, Sept. 7, is Labor Day.

Are government offices closed on Labor Day?

Labor Day is a federal holiday, meaning Indiana's state, local and federal offices will be closed.

Schools, courthouses, and banks will be closed as well.

Is the stock market closed on Labor Day?

The US Stock Market is closed Monday, Sept. 7.

Is the bank open on Labor Day?

Banks following the Federal Reserve holiday schedule will be closed on Monday. Upcoming bank holidays are Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day.

Will I get mail on Labor Day?

There will be no mail delivered Monday, Sept. 7. The United States Postal Service will be closed.

Will UPS deliver on Labor Day?

UPS will not deliver packages on Labor Day.

Will FedEx deliver on Labor Day?

No, FedEx will not deliver on Labor Day.

Will trash be picked up on Labor Day in Indianapolis?

No trash or recycling will be picked up Monday, Sept 7, in Indianapolis because of the Labor Day holiday. All Indy residents who normally have it picked up on Monday will have their trash collected on Tuesday instead.

Are Indianapolis Public Libraries closed on Labor Day?

All locations will be closed Monday, Sept. 7.

Is the Indiana Bureau of Motor Vehicles closed on Labor Day?

All Indiana Bureau of Motor Vehicles (BMV) branches will be closed Monday, Sept. 7, for Labor Day.

For a complete list of branch locations and hours, to complete an online transaction, or to find a 24-hour BMV Connect kiosk near you visit IN.gov/BMV.
