📋 Santos line-up to host Bahia in fierce battle for a top-six spot

Santos host Bahia at Vila Belmiro this Saturday (24) at 9 p.m. (Brasília time), in Matchday 32 of the 2026 Brasileirão.

Peixe sit eighth with 45 points.

OneFootball Videos

Tricolor de Aço are one place above them in the table with 49 points.

Following their last match (a 3–0 defeat to Flamengo), Bahia coach Rogério Ceni brought in Kanu and Erick for Moreno and Jean Lucas, respectively.

Meanwhile, Santos, coming off a 2–1 win over Fluminense, have made just one change, with coach Cuca bringing in João Alencar for Lucas Veríssimo.
