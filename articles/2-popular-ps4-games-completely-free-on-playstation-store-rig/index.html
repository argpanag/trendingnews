2 Popular PS4 Games Completely Free on PlayStation Store Right Now - AOL

TheTools · 07.10.2026 07:02 · source · permalink

A pair of popular PS4 games are currently completely free to claim and download via the PS Store right now. For some reason, Call of Duty: WWII and Mafia III can simply be added to players’ libraries. It’s unclear if this is a glitch or if Sony planned to offer these games for free, possibly via PS Plus, at a later date.

PS4 games Call of Duty: WWII and Mafia III free to claim via PS Store in several regions

I can confirm that both Call of Duty: WWII and Mafia III are free in UK/European regions’ PS Stores. I’ve seen similar reports coming from other regions, like India and some South American countries.

If you have a secondary account in any of the aforementioned regions, you can claim the games, download them, then switch to your main account to play.

The caveat is that Sony will probably revoke your license and remove the games from your library once the glitch is discovered. For now, enjoy the freebies!
