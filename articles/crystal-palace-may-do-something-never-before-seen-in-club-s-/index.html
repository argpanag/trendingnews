Crystal Palace may do something never before seen in club's 165-year history against Fulham

TheTools · 04.09.2026 13:42 · source · permalink

Pierre Sage takes charge of his first London derby as Crystal Palace boss on Saturday as the Eagles take on Fulham at Craven Cottage.

The Frenchman is still searching for his first point in the Premier League after a 2-0 defeat against Everton and a 4-1 loss to Manchester City in the opening two games.

In fairness to Sage, the fact 13 players went to the World Cup, and also the fact there was widespread uncertainty in the final days of the transfer window, made it tough to prepare for those two games.

Thoughts on the deadline day business, Palace fans? 🦅

With the transfer window now closed, many fans expect the season to truly get started against Fulham.

However, Craven Cottage could be the stage for a piece of unwanted Crystal Palace history – should the Eagles concede two goals.

Crystal Palace could ship two goals in nine consecutive league games for the first time ever

Having been founded in 1861, Crystal Palace are 165 years old and the oldest football club in London. In all that time, however, the Eagles have never conceded at least two goals in nine consecutive league games – according to BBC Sport.

That is now potentially on the verge of happening should Fulham – whose own fans are not that optimistic about a result – score twice.

The final seven Premier League fixtures saw Crystal Palace ship 15 goals in total, as Oliver Glasner put all of his eggs into the UEFA Conference League basket.

2025/26 season

  • Liverpool 3-1 Crystal Palace
  • Bournemouth 3-0 Crystal Palace
  • Crystal Palace 2-2 Everton
  • Manchester City 3-0 Crystal Palace
  • Brentford 2-2 Crystal Palace
  • Crystal Palace 1-2 Arsenal

2026/27 season

  • Everton 2-0 Crystal Palace
  • Crystal Palace 1-4 Manchester City

Should Crystal Palace concede another two goals against Fulham, a bit of history would be made.

The Cottagers did score twice in their opening game of the season at Craven Cottage as Alvaro Arbeloa watched his side suffer a 3-2 defeat against Chelsea.

In that respect, it could well happen, although Sage will not care one bit so long as his side manage to outscore the west Londoners and take all three points back to south London.
