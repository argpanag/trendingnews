F1 Mexico GP: Antonelli becomes youngest world champion as Verstappen holds off Hamilton for victory

Kimi Antonelli becomes Formula 1's youngest-ever world champion at the Mexico City Grand Prix, as Max Verstappen defeats Lewis Hamilton

Lewis Hamilton: second in the Mexico City GP: "Firstly, look at this crowd. We have to acknowledge them, and a huge congratulations to Kimi and the Mercedes team, they're all here celebrating his success. I'm so grateful to be in the race to witness it.

"I think we had the pace to win it today, but it wasn't meant to be. We'll take it offline and figure out what we can do in the background. 

"In drivers' briefing, we decided if you went off at Turn 1 you have to give the place back. Last year I got 10 seconds for the same thing he did, but ultimately which lost us the win today. I think we have to have some serious talks behind the scenes. But the team is working behind the scenes, we were on the old engine today, but good result and good points. Some you win, some you lose.:"

Max Verstappen, Mexico City GP winner: It was very hectic out there today, the first start made the race for me. I had a very good first lap, let's say it like that. After that we had a bit of a different strategy. I do think we lacked a bit of pace, but we just hung in there, made the right calls at the right time, the penalty of Charles put us in the lead. And I was sweating at the end to keep Lewis behind.

"It's such a big run into Turn 1, and you have to pick your braking point especially with a big bump there.

"It's incredible, what a season [Kimi] had. To do that at such an age is really impressive. I'm sure this is a very emotional day with him."

Kimi Antonelli, 2026 F1 champion: "We did it. We did it, and we wouldn't have done it without Mercedes, without my family. I'm very grateful for this moment, and one I will live forever. I'm speechless, and trying to process what happened!

"It's been an incredible season, and to share this podium with Max and Lewis is truly incredible. 

"This weekend, I did feel a bit more tense, but I'm just so happy we got it done. Now we can enjoy the rest of the season."
