Barcelona share Jesse Bisiwu update after summer signing stretchered off

TheTools · 03.09.2026 10:40 · source · permalink
  • Barcelona Team News

Some good news on the injury front.

Barcelona have confirmed that Jesse Bisiwu has avoided serious injury after being stretchered off playing for Barca Atletic on Wednesday night.

Bisiwu was injured in a heavy tackle early on in the match and had to be replaced. He was initially helped off the pitch and was then taken down the tunnel on a stretcher.

The summer signing has now undergone further tests and Barcelona have confirmed there is no serious injury.

“Player Jesse Bisiwu received a blow to his left thigh during yesterday’s match. The medical tests carried out this morning have confirmed that it is solely a contusion. His return to training is expected in a short time frame,” the club said in a statement.

The news will be a big relief to Barcelona and Bisiwu. The 18-year-old has only just arrived at Camp Nou from Club Brugge but there are high hopes he can earn first-team opportunities this season.
