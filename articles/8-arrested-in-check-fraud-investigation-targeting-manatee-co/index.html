8 arrested in check fraud investigation targeting Manatee County churches

TheTools · 06.11.2026 20:46 · source · permalink

MANATEE COUNTY, Fla. (WWSB) - Eight people have been arrested in a check fraud investigation that targeted churches across Manatee County, according to the Manatee County Sheriff’s Office. Detectives said donation checks meant for local congregations were stolen from mailboxes and cashed by a group of suspects. Seven more suspects are still wanted.

Detectives said the checks were stolen from mailboxes and deposited into the suspects’ own accounts, often using mobile banking. Investigators said money people intended as church donations ended up in other people’s bank accounts.

Suspects had no connection to churches

Detective Rachael Fossaceca said the suspects had no ties to the churches.

“The suspects were not connected to the churches. They were not parishioners. They did not work for the church. They targeted the churches,” Fossaceca said.

Investigation began in December 2025

The investigation started in December 2025, when Harvest United Methodist Church in Lakewood Ranch noticed a drop in the mail it was receiving.

“It was basically a giant jigsaw puzzle that was scattered everywhere. As we were finding out things, more churches were being victimized.The number of victims grew,” Fossaceca said.

Scheme extends beyond Manatee County

Investigators said they uncovered a scheme involving at least 17 churches in Manatee County and more than 20 suspects overall.

Detectives said the case extends beyond Manatee County. They said hundreds of stolen checks have been identified across multiple counties, with nearly $194,000 in additional losses outside the county.

“A lot of these accounts had numerous checks from all different types of churches from Manatee County, Tampa, Michigan, everywhere,” Fossaceca said.

Detectives said this case is separate from other check washing investigations.

The investigation is active.
