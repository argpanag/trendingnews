Mourinho: "We are happy, united, and working well"

TheTools · 11.09.2026 11:30 · source · permalink

"We respect our opponent, who is coming off a victory and has shown quality; we need to stay focused," the coach stated.

José Mourinho addressed the media in the press room at the Bernabéu the day before the match against Rayo for the fifth matchday of La Liga, which will be played at our stadium (Saturday, 9:00 pm CEST; Orange and Dazn). The coach explained: “We are happy, united, and working well. Working well also means recovering well. Today we conducted tactical training without much intensity, for a short time, but with great respect for our opponent. They are coming off a victory, and though they didn’t win other matches, they showed their quality and why we must be very focused for tomorrow's match. I’d like to take this opportunity to thank Beñat San José because he has said some overly kind words about me. I didn’t deserve so much, and I want to thank him publicly. Tomorrow, I will have the chance to give him a hug.”

“In Portugal, there is a proverb that I don’t know if it exists in Spain: you only throw stones at trees that bear fruit. What has Vini Jr. done here in recent years? How many Champions Leagues has he won, and how many goals has he scored? What has he done here in the last five or six years? He is a tree that bears a lot of fruit, and people throw stones because they want the fruit. They want the best of Vinicius, and the best will come. Against Benfica, he single-handedly decided the tie. That Vinicius will come, but at this moment, he hasn’t been at that level yet. This is a Vini who is working harder than ever. In this era of data, he is very close to his top speed. He broke his record this season in terms of balls recovered and also in the defensive third... He will arrive soon.”

Where should the World Cup final be held?
“One thing is where it should be held, and another is where I would like it to be. Forgive me, but I would like it to be in Lisbon. I'm already happy that a country like ours has any World Cup matches. Then, my answer is not because I am the coach of Real Madrid, I think you know me a little. If you can play in a mythical, historical stadium where some of the best players in history have played, and it is the stadium of the most important club in football history, I think it should be here. Regardless of whether I am the coach of Real Madrid. If I were Benfica's coach, I would say exactly the same thing.”
