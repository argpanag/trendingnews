Michelle Keegan at centre of awkward situation after Olly Murs naked prank

TheTools · 20.09.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

Michelle Keegan is in ITV's new drama The Blame (Image: BBC)

Michelle Keegan was once left having to deal with a rather awkward situation following a hilarious prank mix-up. The actress, who returns to screens on Sunday (September 20) for ITV's The Blame, found herself having to drive her car through London with a naked picture of her husband Mark Wright's friend, Olly Murs, plastered across it.

This came after Olly put a printed cut-out of himself on what he believed was Mark Wright's car outside the Heart Radio studios.

Explaining all during their Saturday show, Olly said to Mark: "Do you remember that time I put my face on your car?"

"Face?," Mark replied. "Are you forgetting the rest? Your naked body and I had to drive through London with it because couldn't get it off!".

Olly went on to point out that the funniest part of the story was that, unbeknownst to him at the time, it was actually Michelle's Range Rover.

Olly Murs pranked Mark Wright in 2021

Mark explained: "I drove Michelle's car to work one night when I used to do the evening show here at Heart and Olly decided, when I was in the studio, to do a prank on me, he got this massive print out, like bigger than himself, bigger than life size of himself laying down butt naked and he put it on the side of my car, like printed it on, there was no way of getting it off.

"And when Michelle woke up to drive to work the next morning she walked out and saw a massive Olly Murs on the car and she drove it all the way through London."

Laughing as he recalled the moment, Mark told listeners that he would reshare footage of the prank.

At the time, Olly had sneakily disguised himself as a cleaner to give Mark's car the very cheeky makeover in 2021.

Speaking in a video shared on Heart's social media, Olly said: "Hi, I'm Olly Murs and I'm here at Heart to prank Mr Mark Wright, one of my closest friends.

Michelle Keegan and Mark Wright have been married since 2015 (Image: Getty Images for Atlantis The Royal)

"He has no idea that I'm here tonight. He is on show right now and I've been told he's super happy because today I've been told he's allowed to drive his car and park it in the basement at Heart."

He added: "What he doesn't realise is that I'm the person allowing him access to park his car downstairs."

He also claimed that his co-star "loves talking about his body", joking: "He's always got his top off when you see him."

Olly went on to say he himself had been getting "ripped" over the last few months in the build-up to the prank and was eager to show off his new and improved body.

After putting naked snaps of himself over Mark's car, he hid away in the corner of the car park to film his co-star's reaction.

The Blame airs tonight at 9pm on ITV and ITVX
