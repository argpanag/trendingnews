How to Watch Philadelphia Phillies vs. Atlanta Braves Wild Card Game 3 (Start Time, Network)

TheTools · 01.10.2026 14:55 · source · permalink

The National League showdown is heading to a win-or-go-home Game 3 — here's how to catch every inning!

Key Takeaways

  • Phillies and Braves are tied 1-1 in their best-of-three MLB Wild Card series, making Game 3 on October 1 a decisive “win or go home” matchup.
  • Game 3 begins at 8:15 p.m. ET at Truist Park in Atlanta, with coverage starting at 8 p.m. ET on NBC and Peacock.
  • The winner advances to face the Los Angeles Dodgers in the National League Division Series, while the broadcast team includes Matt Vasgersian, John Kruk, C.J. Nitkowski, Joey Votto, and Ashley ShahAhmadi.

After the first two games of their Major League Baseball Wild Card matchup, the Philadelphia Phillies and Atlanta Braves find themselves knotted at one game apiece in their best-of-three series, setting up an epic Game 3 tonight (Thursday, Oct. 1).

How to Watch

Watch MLB on NBC and streaming on Peacock.

Tonight's game is the literal definition of "win or go home" — the Braves took Game 1 on September 29, winning 5-3 thanks to a clutch 3-run, 8th-inning home run to right field by third baseman Austin Riley. One day later, the Phils tied the series with a wild 4-3 come-from-behind, extra-inning victory of their own — courtesy of a homer by infielder Alec Bohn — to set up a pivotal Game 3 that has all the makings of an instant classic.

How to watch Phillies-Braves Wild Card Game 3 ?

Baseball fans have two options to catch tonight's Game 3 between the Phillies and Braves: Coverage will begin tonight, October 1, at 8 p.m. ET/5 p.m. PT — live on NBC and Peacock.

The game is set for a 8:15 p.m. ET first pitch.

Whoever wins Game 3 tonight advances to Los Angeles to take on the Dodgers in the National League Division Series. Game 1 of the NLDS is scheduled for Saturday, October 3.

Where is Game 3 being played?

Game 3 of the MLB Wild Card series between the Phillies and Braves will be played on Atlanta's home turf: Truist Park.

Atlanta secured home-field advantage through the Wild Card round by clinching the #3 postseason seed with a 94–68 regular season record. Philadelphia's 88–74 record earned the team the #6 seed.
