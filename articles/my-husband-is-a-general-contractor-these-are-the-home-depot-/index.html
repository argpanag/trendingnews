My Husband Is a General Contractor & These Are the Home Depot Black Friday Deals He Highly Recommends

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, SheKnows may receive an affiliate commission.

The time has come! Black Friday deals have landed at The Home Depot, and I’ve got your guide on the best deals and gift-worthy items you won’t want to miss.

Shop The Home Depot Black Friday Sale

But first, trying to find the right gift for someone who works with tools, either professionally or as a hobby, is overwhelming. There are so many different types of tools out there, and while many may seem interchangeable, you’d be surprised by how different they actually are. Space for tool storage is limited, so even though it’s useful to have multiples of certain tools, you want to be intentional about what makes it into your collection.

While there are many advancements in tools, sometimes a tool-guy/gal loves using their tried and true tool. Even if it looks like it’s about to burst into dust. Oh, and don’t even get me started on the brand debate. You do have to know if you’re a Dewalt, Milwaukee, Ryobi, Makita, or Bosch household and buy accordingly. But there’s only so many years in a row you can get someone a gift card. Wouldn’t it be nice for them to have something to “play with” on Christmas day?

My husband, Will Sherer, is a contractor who remodels and repairs homes, with previous experience installing industrial renewable energy systems, building Broadway sets, experiential events, and quirky pop-ups, and designing and building furniture. Safe to say, a lot of tools have been in his hands and oh boy does he have strong opinions on what tools are worth the space in your tool closet.

So below is a detailed list of some of his favorite tools at Home Depot that are either on sale or being featured in their Black Friday Sale. While some might be a little tricky to put under the tree, I know the tool-lover on your list is going to love them. And maybe finish that pesky project before the new year.

How We Chose the Tools on This List

My husband sat down next to me and looked at the best deals and gave his opinions on each one I have listed below. He was looking for ones that make the most sense for beginners and for those who have been hammering away for a long time. It was important that each tool wasn’t a one trick pony, and could be used on multiple types of projects but still did everything it needed to do really well.

He was also looking to see if this was the right time to get these tools. The Christmas price bloat is real, which means sometimes companies inflate prices knowing people will be buying. My husband shops for tools year round (insert a sarcastic “I love it” here) so he’s been paying attention to any price fluctuations. According to my husband, there’s genuinely a great amount of items that would make great gifts available this season.

Why Brands Matter for Tool Lovers

Some tool collectors will be brand-fluid and have a little bit of everything but there might be one or two that make the most sense to make sure you stick with a color. Mostly because of batteries and storage. My husband is a Milwaukee and Dewalt guy so our house has several battery chargers around our house with charged, ready to go batteries. If I were to give him a tool that was not in either of those lines, he would probably still use and appreciate it but it wouldn’t get used as much since the batteries he has ready to go aren’t compatible.

If you don’t know if your giftee is brand-agnostic, or a die-hard “we bleed yellow/red/green/turquoise in this house” type, either look at what color all their tools are as each brand usually has a typical color or, if you have 20-40 minutes to spare, ask them to rank them.

Home Depot Black Friday 2026: Full Price But Worth it

Husky 12000 Lumen LED Portable Corded Work Light

It seems like projects always seem to happen in dark corners. Or after work hours when the sun has already set. Having an extra light handy is vital for getting projects done in a safe way. As my husband wisely says, “In a world of batteries and cordless lights, it’s sometimes still best to own a plug-in that is reliable.” This Husky light is a great choice for that. It’s extremely bright and durable. Not to mention, it also comes with a very versatile built-in stand, so you don’t have to recruit a helper to hold it. Though not on sale for Black Friday, it’s one of those purchases worth adding to cart if you’re getting some holiday shopping done.

Top Deals From Home Depot’s Black Friday Sale

DeWalt 12-Inch Double-Bevel Sliding Compound Miter Saw

This DeWalt saw literally showed up on my kitchen table last week (insert another sarcastic “I love it”) so yes, my husband is only recommending what he is truly using and loves. I remember hearing about this saw when it was first on sale because he was so excited about it finally being in a price range he was willing to spend. He says, “It is the gold standard for Miter saws. Makita and Bosch both have competitive saws in this category but the Makita is over twice the price. And while it has a more compact footprint and a couple features that might be important for some, it’s hard to justify the price tag.”

He loves it so much, I’m honestly surprised I haven’t come home to everything in our house sawed in two. It does everything very well, and “has amazing capacity, vertically and horizontally.” It feels like the design team at DeWalt “really knocked it out of the park” with this one. Everything feels intentional and the precision on it can’t be beat.

Note: DeWalt offers two nearly identical saws, the DWS779 and DWS780, you want the 780. With the current sale, the DWS779 has a similar price. While it’s not a bad saw either, you might as well get the 780 with all its bells and whistles.

27 Gal. Storage Bin Tote Taxi

Probably not the sexiest of the tools listed, but probably one of the most helpful. The Home Depot HDX 27 Gal. Tough Totes is a pretty popular system for a lot of hobbies. It makes moving, stacking, and rolling so much easier. We use these bins in our storage units and they hold everything, from his tools to Christmas decorations to collectibles. Having stacks of like-items on wheels has proved much more handy and efficient than having shelving dedicated to each and every bin. It’s allowed us to be more modular with how things are stored together.

DeWalt 20V MAX XR 18 Gauge Cordless Brad Nailer

Cordless nail guns have been around for a while, and have just gotten better year over year. So if you’re ready to take the plunge on some big projects or are switching over from pneumatic nailers, both DeWalt and Milwaukee have great deals on their flagship nailers. If you’re looking to do some trim work inside this year, see if crown molding is as tricky as people say, or looking to build that dream built-in you saw on TikTok, we love DeWalt’s new 18-gauge Brad Nailer, and if you’re already in the Milwaukee battery ecosystem their 18-gauge Brad is a very close second place.

Milwaukee M18 FUEL 30-Degree Cordless Framing Nailer

If you’re really looking to tackle that big DIY project or build your perfect she-shed both brands have their flagship framing nailers on sale. While he usually says it’s fine to stick with whatever battery platform you already have, my husband says the Milwaukee 30º and 27º Framing nailers are worth jumping ship. “The Milwaukee uses a different technology for the action, the bit that actually drives the nails, and it’s extremely fast, and hits like an air tool. The only drawback is it’s heavy, 2-3 pounds more than a pneumatic and probably a pound or two more than the DeWalt.”

<strong>Milwaukee Packout Kit</strong>

The Home Depot‘s Black Friday event has a great price for this packout kit, which is well below what the individual pieces usually run. My husband recommends it over the Ryobi and DeWalt kits. In fact this packout kit is what got him more into Milwaukee tools years ago. He has almost fully retired any of his past DeWalt Tough System boxes.

<strong>Werner Ladder 6 ft. Fiberglass Step Ladder</strong>

Stepping on a ladder is already risky, so you have to trust the brand you’re using. My husband’s favorite for any job site is Werner and two of their most popular heights, 8 and 6 foot fiberglass step ladders are going for well below their usual price. Great for hanging up holiday decor inside or out or any other project you need to get high for.

<strong>Werner Adjustable Height Work Platform</strong>

This is one of the tools my husband almost left to go get while we were coming up with this list. The platform is great for so many house projects that might require you to have multiple tools and items with you (plenty of room for snacks too). The adjustable height and wide platform means you don’t have to keep moving your ladder over a few inches, thus making it safer. You can tackle larger areas at once. He says it’s particularly great for painting and any “wide tasks” like crown molding, or those not so glamorous pre-holiday tasks like cleaning the tops of your kitchen cabinets.

<strong>DeWalt Atomic Brushless 2-Tool Kit</strong>

This is my husband’s go-to recommendation any time someone asks what kind of drill/driver they should buy first. “It’s a really solid option in the middle of DeWalt’s lineup — not beginner, but not top-tier-pro —you sacrifice a couple features that really only matter if you’re using it all day every day. Plus, you get back some weight savings that really makes a difference between finishing something today versus dragging into tomorrow”. At this price he says it’s a no-brainer.
