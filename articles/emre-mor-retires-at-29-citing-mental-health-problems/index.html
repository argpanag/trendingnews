Emre Mor retires at 29 citing mental health problems

TheTools · 23.11.2026 17:26 · source · permalink

The Turkish winger, Celta's third most expensive signing ever, steps away from football after a groin injury and seven games for NEC Nijmegen.

By TuFutbolero Newsroom·November 23, 2026 at 09:26 AM

Emre Mor has announced his retirement from professional football at 29, temporarily and, according to MARCA, citing «mental health problems». The Turkish winger, whom NEC Nijmegen signed on a free transfer last summer, has not played since mid-September because of a groin injury.

His career began with a costly move: in 2017 Celta paid 13 million euros to Borussia Dortmund, the third most expensive signing in the club's history, but he never settled in Vigo. He then played for Galatasaray, Olympiacos, Karagümrük and Fenerbahçe before arriving in Nijmegen, where he made seven appearances.

NEC sporting director Carlos Aalbers said the player told them he could not perform at the level he wanted, and that both sides agreed the decision was best for everyone, with health first. Mor, for his part, spoke of listening to his body and keeping hope of returning one day.
