Great British Bake Off spin-off revamp as Extra Slice is axed - with Jo Brand and Tom Allen exiting

TheTools · 04.09.2026 15:51 · source · permalink

Extra Slice is no more.

It seems the arrival of Nigella Lawson isn't the only change being made to The Great British Bake Off ahead of the new series, as Channel 4 have confirmed that companion show Extra Slice has been replaced by a new programme with new hosts.

The long-running competition returns later this month, but this time around, there will no longer be Friday night check-ins with Jo Brand and Tom Allen.

Instead, a new show called Seconds Helpings will air on the same night as the main show, immediately after the episode airs.

Hosting it will be comedians Judi Love and Jon Richardson, who have both taken part in The Great Celebrity Bake Off For Stand Up To Cancer. In a statement, the latter paid tribute to Brand and Allen.

"Bake Off is an institution," he said. "Jo Brand is a comedy God. Together with Tom Allen, they have made a spin off show that is warm, witty and unique. I pity the poor sap they lure into stepping into those shoes."

Love said: "I’ve been lucky enough to be in the tent a few times and work alongside Jon, both were pure joy! Joining the show to talk all things baking and celebrate showstopping bakes, including some unique spins sounds like absolute blast. I can’t wait to get started and of course eat a few cakes or whatever treats they may have."

Explaining the changes, Channel 4's Senior Commissioning Editor, Vivienne Molokwu, said: "We have revamped Extra Slice by giving it a brand-new shape and a brand-new home.

"Second Helpings will be available on the same night as Bake Off meaning viewers don’t have to wait long to chew over what’s gone down in the tent. With the deliciously funny Jon and Judi at the helm we’ll all have a hilarious time talking all things sweet treats."

As anticipation builds for Bake Off's return, Channel 4’s Director of Streaming and Content Strategy, Kiran Nataraja, has shut down tabloid speculation that judges Nigella Lawson and Paul Hollywood have not been getting along.

Making light of the reports, she said: "We’re really excited to have her on the show. Contrary to ‘the Sidebar of Shame,’ her and Paul are not arguing about helicopters the whole time."

The Great British Bake Off will return to Channel 4 next month.
