Australian consumer confidence falls faster than almost anywhere

TheTools · 18.10.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

Australians have suffered one of the steepest falls in consumer confidence in the developed world over the past year as stubborn inflation and rising interest rates batter household budgets, highlighting the Albanese government’s struggle to convince voters it is doing enough to tackle cost-of-living pressures.

Figures from the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) show Australian consumer confidence fell 1.1 index points over the 12 months to September. Only households in Chile and Belgium recorded sharper declines in sentiment.
