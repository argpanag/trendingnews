ACTION Civil War Results (9/25) - Fightful | WWE News, AEW News, Pro Wrestling Backstage News

TheTools · 30.09.2026 18:45 · source · permalink

ACTION Wrestling held its ACTION Civil War event on September 25 from Line Creek Brewing Bus Barn in Fayetteville, Georgia. The event aired on IWTV.

Full results are below.

ACTION Civil War Results (9/25)

  • Christian Taylor def. Dante Darko
  • Tyler Stevens def. Prince Moulay
  • Ravenna Vein def. Corinne Joy
  • Sal Rinauro def. Tyson Malrick
  • No DQ Match: Jamesen Shook def. Mr. Danger
  • Grayson Pierce def. KJ Valentine
  • ACTION Championship: Darian Bengston (c) def. Kelsey Raegan to retain the title.
