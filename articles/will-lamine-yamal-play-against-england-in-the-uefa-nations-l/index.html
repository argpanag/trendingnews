Luis de la Fuente Confirms Lamine Yamal Is Ready to Face England

The Spain national team received encouraging news ahead of its clash with England on the final matchday of the 2026 UEFA Nations League group stage: Lamine Yamal has fully recovered and will be available for selection.


The FC Barcelona winger had raised concerns after experiencing discomfort in his right knee, casting doubt on his participation in Sunday's November 15 showdown at the Metropolitano Stadium in Madrid.

However, Spain head coach Luis de la Fuente confirmed that the 19-year-old is in excellent physical condition after taking advantage of recent days to rest and recover.

The Spanish manager explained that avoiding several demanding trips helped Yamal return to international duty in better shape. Although he did not confirm whether the youngster would start, he made it clear that the winger is available to play without restrictions.

Lamine Yamal Will Wear Spain's No. 10 Jersey Against England

Beyond his recovery, Lamine Yamal will have another reason to make Sunday's match special.

The young forward will wear Spain's No. 10 jersey, taking advantage of the absence of Dani Olmo, who normally wears the number but is currently sidelined through injury.



It will be the second time Yamal has worn the iconic number for Spain's senior national team, having previously done so against Denmark in October 2024.

The Spanish Football Federation also confirmed that Jacobo Ramón, Jorge de Frutos, Roberto Fernández, and Álvaro Valles were left out of the final matchday squad.

Spain and England Battle for the Top Spot in the Nations League

The highly anticipated encounter represents a major test for both national teams, with first place in the group and a favorable position heading into the quarterfinals at stake.

Spain enters the match riding an impressive 43-game unbeaten streak, including 12 consecutive victories.

Luis de la Fuente's side needs only a draw to secure the top spot, although the Spanish coach has made it clear that his team will be playing for all three points.

Meanwhile, England must win to overtake Spain in the standings.

One of the biggest attractions will be the showdown between Lamine Yamal and Harry Kane, the Ballon d'Or winner, in a match featuring some of European soccer's brightest stars.
