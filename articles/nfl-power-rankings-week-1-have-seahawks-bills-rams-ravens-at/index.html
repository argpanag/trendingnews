NFL power rankings Week 1 have Seahawks, Bills, Rams, Ravens at top

TheTools · 05.09.2026 16:38 · source · permalink

The 2026 NFL season will soon kick off with the NFL Week 1 schedule and provide a clean slate for all 32 teams in the league.

Every team has the same record. Every team has a shot at the NFL playoffs.

Every team has reasons for optimism as it gets a new start.

For our first NFL power rankings of the season, we're listing every team according to its BetMGM Sportsbook odds to win the Super Bowl, giving us an idea of how one sportsbook perceives how each team will fare in the 2026 NFL regular season.

After NFL Week 1, our NFL Week 2 power rankings will rank teams based on our first impressions from the season-opening games, so there could be a big shakeup before the NFL Week 2 schedule.
