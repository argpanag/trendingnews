SYRACUSE, N.Y. — Toys R Us is making a return to Syracuse inside Destiny USA Mall later this year.

The mall posted to its social media accounts on Tuesday that the store will be located on the second level of the mall in the IKEA wing.

The standalone store is part of a 120 store rollout according to a recent company announcement.

It will be operating before the holiday shopping season according to the mall.

This is part of the brand's effort to rebuild its physical retail presence after it filed for bankruptcy in 2017 and completed a liquidation of its U.S. stores.

The brand was most recently seen inside of select Macy's across the country, including here in Syracuse.
