Why Did Micron Technology Stock Fall?

TheTools · 23.11.2026 07:39 · source · permalink

Today’s big story: Reported SEC Probe: Could Nvidia Stock Fall To $92?

Update, Nov 23: Nvidia’s board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO on Nov 22. In Monday morning trading, Nvidia rose more than 30%, and Micron rose about 19%, to about $705. Micron moved with its chip group again, and the figures below are at Friday’s close.

Micron Technology (MU) stock fell 13.9% on Friday, November 20, 2026, while the S&P 500 lost 3.7%. There was no announcement, earnings report or filing from the company around the move. All 32 other semiconductor companies worth $2 billion or more fell the same day, in a week when AI stocks lost heavily. So how closely is Micron’s business tied to AI?

Micron’s 13.9% drop came in a single session, on a day with no word from the company. If much of your savings sits in this one chip stock, a day like that can cost you real money and sleep. Micron Makes Memory Chips That AI Runs On

Micron’s business is closely tied to AI. Micron supplies high-bandwidth memory, DRAM and storage used to run AI workloads. That demand has reshaped its results. Revenue in fiscal Q4 2026, the quarter ended September 3, was $54.2 billion, up 379.3% from a year earlier. On its September 30 call, management said it expects market conditions to remain tight through 2028.

That ties Micron’s fortunes to AI spending, and AI stocks lost heavily that week. The selling got worse as the week went on. On November 20, shares of AI company Anthropic dropped a day after their market debut, as the sell-off in technology stocks deepened. The S&P 500 was on track for its longest losing streak since 2020.

Every Other Large Chip Stock Fell Too

Micron moved with its chip group, and it fell further than most of it. In its sub-industry, semiconductors, all 32 other companies worth $2 billion or more fell, by a median of 10.0%. Of those, 28 dropped 5% or more. The technology sector fund XLK lost 7.3%.

Micron’s Trefis peers fell too. Nvidia dropped 14.0%, a little more than Micron. Western Digital fell 12.0% and Advanced Micro Devices 13.2%. Intel fell 8.0% and Qualcomm 7.5%.

How Unusual Was Micron’s Drop?

A fall this large is rare for Micron, but it is the second one in November 2026. In the prior 365 days, the stock had fallen this much in a single session only once, a 17.5% drop on November 5.

Those drops came after a big run. Micron stock is still up 163.4% over the past 12 months, against 5.3% for the S&P 500. Over those 12 months, its highest close was $1,213.56, on June 25, 2026. Its lowest was $201.37, on November 20, 2025, a day after the 12-month count begins. The slide has sped up lately. At its November 20 close of $594.51, Micron sat about 51% below that June high. Over the past month the stock lost 30.6%, against 9.7% for the S&P 500, which also fell.

After the fall, Micron trades at 5.0 times its sales over the past 12 months, against 3.0 for the S&P 500. Micron is expected to report its fiscal first quarter of 2027 in December 2026. That report will show how its gross margin compares with the 86.8% it reported for fiscal Q4 2026.

Days like November 20 show how fast one chip stock can drop when its whole group sells off.
