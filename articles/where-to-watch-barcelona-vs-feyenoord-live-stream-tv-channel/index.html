Barcelona kick off their 2026/27 UEFA Champions League campaign at home to Feyenoord on Wednesday.

So far, Hansi Flick's side won all four La Liga games this season and scored 17 goals, last lifting the Champions League trophy in 2015 as compared to Feyenoord's 1970 triumph.

Barcelona vs. Feyenoord live stream, TV channel for UEFA Champions League match

Here's how to watch the Barcelona vs. Feyenoord UEFA Champions League match in India:

  • TV channel: Sony Sports Network
  • Live stream: Sony LIV

In India, Sony Sports Network (Sony Sports Ten 1, 2, 3 (Hindi) and 5, along with their respective HD channels; besides Sony Sports Ten 4 with commentary in Tamil, Telegu and Kannada) will provide live broadcast of UEFA Champions League 2026/27 matches.

Live streaming of all the matches will be available through Sony LIV app and website.

What time does Barcelona vs. Feyenoord UEFA Champions League match start?

The UEFA Champions League match between Barcelona and Feyenoord will off at 6:45 p.m. local time (CEST) on Wednesday, September 9, at Camp Nou in Barcelona, Spain.

That translates to 10:15 p.m. IST on Wednesday, September 9, for viewers in India.

Barcelona vs. Feyenoord lineups, team news

Barcelona will be without Frenkie de Jong (knee), Roony Bardghji (knee) and suspended defender Eric Garcia. Jesse Bisiwu is also a doubt despite returning to training.

Flick faces selection decisions, with Marc Bernal, Dani Olmo and Karim Adeyemi pushing for starts. Jules Kounde is expected to replace Garcia at right-back, while Lamine Yamal, Anthony Gordon and Fermin Lopez could support Raphinha in attack.

Barcelona predicted XI (4-2-3-1, right to left): J Garcia — Kounde, Cubarsi, Christensen, Espart — Rodri, Pedri — Yamal, Fermin, Gordon — Raphinha.

Feyenoord will be without Thomas Beelen, Bart Nieuwkoop, Gijs Smal, Jakub Moder and Jordan Bos due to injuries.

Gjivai Zechiel has impressed with four goals and three assists in five appearances, while Luciano Valente has scored three times. Nacho Ferri is also expected to start, with Shaqueel van Persie likely to be among the substitutes.

Feyenoord predicted XI (4-3-3 right to left): Ernst — Read, Juste, Watanabe, Marmol — Vanhoutte, Zechiel, Targhalline — Valente, Ferri, Diarra.
