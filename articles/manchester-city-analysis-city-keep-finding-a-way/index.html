Manchester City analysis: City keep finding a way

TheTools · 22.09.2026 18:41 · source · permalink
  • Published
    20 September 2026

Five wins from five in the league, 13 goals scored and victories in Europe and the Carabao Cup too. On the face of it, things could hardly be going better for Manchester City early on in this post-Pep Guardiola era.

When a legendary figure leaves the club after a prolonged period of success, there is always uncertainty as to what comes next – and despite a string of such positive results, that remains the case.

Against Sunderland, Enzo Maresca's side were far from convincing for long spells with a defence that had conceded just two league goals coming into the game looking remarkably suspect - the Black Cats registered a whopping 4.25 expected goals.

And yet they come away having scored another five goals with Enzo Fernandez's cheeky free-kick a prime example of their attacking quality.

While there is clearly so much that can be improved upon, such brilliance at the top end of the pitch can get you out of many a hole. That, and the ability to find different ways to win, is a pretty good place from which to build.
