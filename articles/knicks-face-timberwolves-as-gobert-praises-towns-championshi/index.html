Knicks Face Timberwolves as Gobert Praises Towns Championship

TheTools · 19.11.2026 23:50 · source · permalink

New York's championship success has given Karl-Anthony Towns a special place in the Knicks' recent history, while his former Minnesota Timberwolves teammate Rudy Gobert has publicly celebrated the achievement ahead of their teams' NBA matchup in Minneapolis on Thursday, November 19, 2026.

Gobert expressed happiness for Towns, describing him as a close friend who had endured criticism before finally winning an NBA title. The Knicks acquired Towns from Minnesota before the 2024-25 season, and he became a significant contributor to their championship campaign.

The meeting at Target Center also brings together two teams enjoying strong stretches of form. New York entered the contest with an 8-6 record after winning consecutive games against Toronto, while Minnesota held an 11-4 record following back-to-back victories over Portland on Monday and Tuesday, according to CBS Sports.

Gobert reflected on Towns' personal achievement while acknowledging the complicated emotions surrounding the NBA Finals, particularly because Victor Wembanyama played for the opposing team.

"KAT deserves to win. He's been through a lot … there's always been some criticism. He's obviously someone that's a very unique player that wants to win. He's one of my best friends, one of my favorite guys to be around, so I was really happy," Gobert said, as reported by Zone Coverage MN through Andrew Dukowitz.

The Minnesota center also discussed his feelings about Wembanyama's involvement in the championship series. His comments reflected his support for Towns alongside his connection to his fellow Frenchman.

"It was hard obviously with Wemby was on the other team too. So my philosophy was, 'may the best win,' but I was really happy for him," Gobert said.

Gobert and Towns shared Minnesota's frontcourt for two seasons, from 2022 to 2024. During that partnership, the Timberwolves reached the playoffs twice and advanced to the 2024 Western Conference Finals, their first appearance at that stage since 2004.

Towns subsequently moved to New York in a trade shortly before the 2024-25 season. His arrival helped complete the Knicks' championship push, ending the franchise's more than 50-year wait for another NBA title, according to Yahoo Sports.

For the upcoming matchup, Minnesota faced an additional challenge with Anthony Edwards sidelined by a knee injury. Jonathan Kuminga was listed as questionable because of a shoulder problem, according to CBS Sports.

The previous season's meetings between the franchises ended with one victory apiece, with the home team winning each game. Those two encounters averaged 233 combined points.

New York's offense ranked among the NBA's top 10 in three-pointers made per game and free-throw percentage. The Knicks also ranked third in offensive rebound rate, while Minnesota placed among the top 10 in field-goal and three-point percentage and ranked fourth in pace, according to CBS Sports.

The matchup was scheduled for an 8 p.m. ET tipoff at Target Center in Minneapolis, with both teams seeking their third consecutive victory.
