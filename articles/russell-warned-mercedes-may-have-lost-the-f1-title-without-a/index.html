Russell warned Mercedes may have lost the F1 title without Antonelli

TheTools · 25.10.2026 22:30 · source · permalink

Kimi Antonelli and Mercedes have a chance to clinch both the Drivers’ and Constructors’ Championship at the upcoming Mexican Grand Prix.

There was nothing more that Kimi Antonelli could have done other than to win the United States Grand Prix to inch closer to becoming Formula 1’s youngest-ever champion.

George Russell and Lewis Hamilton’s finishing positions in Austin mean that the 20-year-old Italian must wait to clinch the title, but his dominant drive around the Circuit of the Americas suggests it is only a matter of time until Antonelli lifts the trophy.

Mercedes told they may not have won the championship without Kimi Antonelli

Mercedes staff can now put a big red circle around the Mexico City Grand Prix next weekend as the event where they are likely to scoop both the Drivers’ and Constructors’ Championship.

Antonelli didn’t just win in Austin, he dominated, including finishing 50 seconds ahead of teammate George Russell, who was tipped as the championship favourite at the start of the season.

Speaking on F1 TV, Jolyon Palmer warned Mercedes that, with Russell’s current form, they may not have strolled to the championship without Antonelli’s dominance.

“Without Antonelli doing the business week in, week out, Mercedes may not be winning the Championship this year.” – Joylon Palmer

“Without Antonelli doing the business week in, week out, Mercedes may not be winning the Championship this year.” – Joylon Palmer

To reinforce his point, Antonelli has scored 365 of Mercedes’ 607 points this season. Russell is now just one point clear of Lewis Hamilton in the Drivers’ standings and could lose his P2 position.

Anthony Davidson stunned by Kimi Antonelli’s dominance

Antonelli’s performance in Austin was a statement of intent, and a drive of a champion.

Not only did he open up a clear gap to the rest of the pack, but he did it with the championship battle in the back of his mind.

Speaking on Sky Sports, Anthony Davidson piled praise on Antonelli but also pointed out the staggering gap to his teammate.

“It’s crazy to think that we’re talking about a learning curve for such a young driver when he’s basically won it, hasn’t he? He’s just got to win it at a canter next time out. To beat George Russell by over 50 seconds… wow.”
