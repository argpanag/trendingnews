Cross-country skiing: American Kevin Bolger and Swedish skier Maja Dahlqvist are now engaged | Nordic Mag | N°1 Biathlon | Nordic skiing

TheTools · 28.09.2026 09:01 · source · permalink

11:01. On Sunday evening, Swedish cross-country skier Maja Dahlqvist announced on her Instagram account that she was engaged to the American Kevin Bolger, who is also a cross-country skier on the World Cup circuit.

View this post on Instagram

“It was about time,” she wrote in the caption of a photo showing the couple – who made their relationship official in February 2021 – looking more in love than ever.

  • Kevin Bolger, Maja Dahlqvist’s boyfriend, is not sure whether he will take part in the World Championships in Trondheim
  • Maja Dahlqvist and Kevin Bolger are set to move to Trondheim to prepare for the 2025 World Championships
