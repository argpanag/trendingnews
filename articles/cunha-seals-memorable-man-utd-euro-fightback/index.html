  6. 'This place does magical things'published at 23:00 GMT 3 November
    23:00 GMT 3 November

    FT: Man Utd 3-2 Roma

    Manchester United

    Manchester United manager Michael Carrick, speaking to Amazon Prime: "It was a good night. I have to give a lot of credit to the players for starters, showing that character and quality when we needed it.

    "This place does incredible things, magical things and we spoke about it at half-time. I thought we grew into it a little bit first half after giving away the goals too easily.

    "We spoke at half-time about how this place can change, it only takes one moment. The boys made every little bit of every little opportunity really and were fantastic in the end."

    On what was said at half-time: "Just not to get too disappointed about things, I felty we were coming into the game and getting around their box.

    "It didn't feel that way to the players because they were carrying too much frustration and disappointment. I knew that we were doing some good things in the game against a really good team and playing man-for-man as much as they are, you probably get as much failure as success.

    "It's still being positive and the boys responded so well second half."

    On if this is a turning point: "It's one game so it depends on what happens next. We've got to capitalise on that but tonight the boys showed an awful lot second half.

    "Unfortunately with Carlos [Baleba] having to go off and Youri [Tielemens] coming on, I thought he was fantastic and showed everything he is as a personality, a character, his experience to step into a game like that.

    "The boys just went with the flow and you could feel how positive [it was], it was nice to see this place like that again."

    On Shea Lacey's assist for the winner: "Delighted for Shea. Love him to bits, he's so talented, he's desperate to do well. It's such a good pick out, Matheus [Cunha] takes it on his back foot.

    "We moved Matheus inside second half and put Bryan [Mbeumo] wide left to give us better balance. Shea is certainly well liked, the boys respect him a lot. He's desperate to do well and he was even defending at right-back at the end! It shows what he's prepared to do."

    On what tonight represents: "It's a win for us as a football club. To see the supporters go home happy is what we live for, to give them performances and nights like this so they can get back again energised.

    "As a team, we've shown a lot of character to weather what we've had to. You find out a lot about each other. The players will find out things about me, I certainly do about them and their character, what it means to them and what they're prepared to put in.

    "Tonight was right at that moment. It depends what happens next, we've got to make the most of it, we've got to move forward but I'm feeling a lot better about things tonight."

  10. 'Boys got what they deserved in the end'published at 22:52 GMT 3 November
    22:52 GMT 3 November

    FT: Man Utd 3-2 Roma

    Manchester United

    Manchester United manager Michael Carrick, speaking to Uefa: "It was a challenging game. I think we started OK in the game, we knew it was going to be tough - they've only had one defeat all season. They're a very, very good team.

    "So we talked about what we wanted to do in the game, we started OK and then the goal came from nowhere. We caused ourselves a few issues. In some ways understandable with the run we've been on, a little bit anxious and a few nerves at times.

    "Then I thought we grew into the game a bit at the end of the first half and I spoke at half-time about being positive. Anything can happen here, I've seen it many times over the years. You just need something to go our way and the boys were fantastic second half.

    "They kept going, didn't get disappointed and got exactly what they deserved in the end."

    On if he sensed comeback after first goal: "This place does it. No matter what situation you're in, how tough it can seem, the supporters are desperate to get behind you and we gave them something to go on.

    "We gave them other opportunities to score and it was close, and you're thinking 'that's another chance gone by' but we kept looking, kept pushing for answers and we found them in the end."

    On chaotic few weeks and his position: "I don't have a crystal ball to know what happens next. We move on to the weekend, we've got a really important game here against Aston Villa, which we need to prepare for right."

Man Utd stage epic second-half comeback to beat Roma

Manchester United dragged themselves up off the canvas to come from two goals down to record a sensational Champions League victory against Roma at Old Trafford.

United seemed finished and head coach Michael Carrick destined for the sack as Roma scored twice in six first-half minutes, through Matias Soule and a Donyell Malen penalty.

The goals turned the atmosphere inside Old Trafford venomous. Green tennis balls were thrown onto the pitch as a further sign of protest against the club's ownership. Green and gold balloons were released as the teams came out and songs were chanted against the Glazer family, minority owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe and director of football Jason Wilcox.

United's evening got even worse when £70m midfielder Carlos Baleba suffered a hamstring injury that looks set to keep him out for a period of time.

But from the depths of despair, United rallied.

Skipper Bruno Fernandes kept his nerve to drive home a twice-taken penalty after a Roma player entered the area.

Then, after a Bryan Mbeumo shot had hit both posts, Matheus Cunha capitalised on Leonardo Balerdi's error to equalise, before the Brazilian drilled home a shot from an acute angle four minutes from time.

Cunha was sent off for a second booking as the game entered stoppage time but in front of a frenzied crowd, United survived to end their nine-game wait for a victory.
