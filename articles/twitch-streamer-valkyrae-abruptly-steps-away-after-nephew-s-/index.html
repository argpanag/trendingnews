Popular Twitch streamer Valkyrae posted a message on X on Tuesday, Oct. 13, saying she will be stepping back from streaming and social media after the death of her nephew.

On Oct. 11, the streamer was playing an online game with some fellow creators when she received a message in the middle of it: "I think I have to end stream. I'm sorry, chat. I need to mute," she can be heard saying. "Okay, my nephew passed away. And I can't… I can't, like, do anything because… I'm far. It's my half-brother's son. He was so young. He was only two years younger than me."

The creator, who has over four million YouTube subscribers and over one million on Twitch, said in her Tuesday statement that she will focus on spending time with her family. Her post reads: "Grief is complex, whether inevitable loss is slow or sudden. It's something we all will experience so sharing my own story and hearing others is comforting, knowing that you're not alone, knowing I'm not alone."

This news comes after she shared in February that her brother died. USA TODAY has reached out to Valkyrae to ask if she has any further comment.

Valkyrae got her start over a decade ago streaming on Twitch, where she'd play games like "Fortnite." In 2018, she gained a significant following, and in 2020 her star continued to rise as she livestreamed herself playing the viral game "Among Us" and signed an exclusive streaming contract with YouTube. That ended in 2025, and she now streams on both YouTube and Twitch.
