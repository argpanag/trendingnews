Knicks Championship Gear Is Dropping Left and Right Ahead of Banner Raising at Madison Square Garden

TheTools · 20.10.2026 23:07 · source · permalink

Tuesday is a momentous day for fans of the New York Knicks, as the 2025-26 NBA championship banner will finally be raised at Madison Square Garden — a moment longtime fans have been awaiting for for 53 years. With the celebratory night and new season ahead, several brands are celebrating New York's champions with new collections spanning headwear, outerwear and apparel. Find a roundup of new Knicks collections below.

 New York or Nowhere x Knicks 

New York or Nowhere x Knicks capsule collection.

New York or Nowhere is back with another Knicks collection which launched Tuesday, this one debuting a tonal black colorway of the brand's popular "Always" motif across T-shirts, crewnecks and hoodies. There's also a commemorative championship T honoring the Knicks' title run and a lineup of headwear in collaboration with New Era, the official headwear partner of the NBA.

"Last season was a special one for New York, and being part of it through our partnership with the Knicks made it even more meaningful," said New York or Nowhere founder and chief executive officer Quincy Moore in a statement. "We're proud of what we've built with the Knicks over the past eight years, and this assortment is just a taste of what's in store."

New Era x Spike Lee 

New Era x Spike Lee.

In commemoration of Tuesday's banner raising, Knicks superfan and film director Spike Lee has teamed up with New Era on a limited-edition collection of caps representing his love and dedication to the New York squad.

The New Era x Spike Lee x New York Knicks caps are made with the 59Fifty silhouette awash in the signature Knicks blue and emblazoned with a classic Knicks logo on the front of the crown. Lee's "Orange and Blue Skies" signature branding shows up on both caps, and each design tributes the team's three championship seasons in unique ways.

The "Banner Cap" features three woven banner appliqués to reflect the look and feel of the championship banners which will soon all be hanging in Madison Square Garden. The "Rings Cap," meanwhile, features detailed printed appliqués with conceptual artwork of all three Knicks championship rings.

 New Era x 2026 NBA Ring Ceremony Collection 

Karl-Anthony Towns modeled the New Era x 2026 NBA Ring Ceremony Collection.

The Knicks are set to wear New Era's 9Fifty Locker Room Cap on Tuesday night, and fans can twin with them thanks to the 2026 NBA Ring Ceremony Collection. The 9Fifty snapback and 9Twenty adjustable silhouettes are made with a beige crown and Knicks-blue visor. Every cap showcases white and metallic gold patches of the Knicks logo alongside the NBA logo and Larry O'Brien Trophy on the front of the crown. "NBA 2025-26 Champions" is embroidered on the side in blue and metallic gold thread, with a blue New Era flag on the other side.

Additional styles are offered in the Knicks team colorways, too, with the logo, trophy, "National Basketball 2025-26 Champions" patch, New Era flag and NBA logo. Knicks star and New Era ambassador Karl-Anthony Towns models the collection in a campaign video in which he plays New Era's newest store employee.

 Fanatics x Angelo Baque New York Knicks 

Fanatics x Angelo Baque New York Knicks.

Fanatics teamed up with Awake NY founder Angelo Baque on a collector's item limited-edition jacket. The Fanatics x Angelo Baque New York Knicks Championship Varsity Jacket is handcrafted in the USA by heritage maker Settlemiers; the suede garment suede showcases premium leather, intricate embroidery and commemorative patches tributing the Knicks' championship season and the new banner going up at MSG. In a limited drop of just 100 jackets, each piece is individually hand-numbered. The jacket retails for $1,500.

 Terez x Knicks Championship Collection 

Ali Brunson for Terez x Knicks Championship collection.

New York-based brand Terez tapped physical therapist Ali Brunson, the wife of Knicks captain Jalen Brunson, to model the Terez x Knicks Champions collection, which launched in September. Pricing starts at $75 and pieces range from a T-shirt to a bomber jacket to sweaters awash in the orange and blue. Designs range from a classic Knicks logo to the Larry O'Brien trophy.
