19 students sent to hospital after school bus crash in Grant Parish

TheTools · 11.09.2026 22:21 · source · permalink

GRANT PARISH, La. (KALB) - A school bus crashed in Grant Parish on Friday evening.

The crash happened north of Montgomery on Highway 71 at Campground Cutoff.

According to Sheriff Steven McCain, 21 students were on board and 19 were sent to a hospital, with most going to Rapides Regional Medical Center. Two students had to be airlifted to a Shreveport-area trauma center.

There are no confirmed fatalities.

Louisiana State Police will be leading the investigation into the crash.
