‘It’s about time’: Push to change NY rape law after disturbing Cornell allegations | CNN

TheTools · 30.09.2026 04:28 · source · permalink

New York state officials are pushing for changes to the state's rape law after disturbing gang rape allegations at Cornell University. Lawmakers want to close what they call a loophole, which currently makes it harder to prosecute cases where a victim becomes incapacitated through voluntary alcohol or drug use.
