  • Blade Runner 2099 will not feature Harrison Ford's character Rick Deckard, as the show aims to separate itself from the previous Blade Runner films.
  • The creator of Blade Runner 2099, Silka Luisa, is focused on moving the franchise forward by introducing fresh characters and avoiding heavy reliance on nostalgia, unlike Indiana Jones 5.
  • While Blade Runner 2099 will reference the older Blade Runner films, it is set to embrace the future and tell a unique story without depending on Harrison Ford's involvement to attract audiences.

Blade Runner 2099 is poised to avoid Indiana Jones 5's biggest mistake when handling the past versus the future.

The one name synonymous with Blade Runner is Rick Deckard, played by Harrison Ford. Deckard has appeared in Blade Runner and Blade Runner 2049, raising the question: will he show up in the Prime Video series Blade Runner 2099?

"Harrison Ford is not in the show," Blade Runner 2099's creator Silka Luisa told Polygon. "I feel like as a Blade Runner fan, I would want to know that, so I'm just gonna be a full disclosure: I wish, but no. Like I was saying before, it was important to Alcon that we separated ourselves from both films in terms of characters."

Hollywood has an obsession with nostalgia. It certainly has its place in movies and shows. Luisa mentioned that Blade Runner 2099 will reference the older Blade Runner films. That is the right move. However, Blade Runner 2099 will avoid the Indiana Jones 5 trap by showing how it can move on from the past and into the future.

Blade Runner 2099 can show how to move a franchise forward without relying on the past

Indiana Jones 5—officially titled Indiana Jones and the Dial of Destiny—relied too heavily on nostalgia. For starters, Ford was 80 when the movie premiered in 2023. I'm fine with Ford appearing in Indiana Jones 5. However, Ford should've been paired with a stronger sidekick or put Phoebe Waller-Bridge's Helena Shaw on equal footing with Indy.

The real issue is that the Indiana Jones franchise has not told stories without Ford. They don't have to recast Ford just yet. However, they cannot rely on Ford to entice audiences to theaters when he's 80 years old. The Indiana Jones franchise is at a standstill because it did not set up Ford's successor, and they refused to craft a new story without Indy's involvement.

It's an encouraging sign to see Blade Runner 2099 embracing the future. Blade Runner 2099 is not relying on Ford's involvement to sell the show. Instead, Luisa is telling a unique story with fresh characters that should advance the universe into the future.

If you're worried that Luisa doesn't understand the mythology or ethos of the text, Blade Runner director Ridley Scott is attached as an executive producer, so he can help honor the show and carry on the franchise's legacy.

Nostalgia does not have to be destroyed. It simply must be altered. Blade Runner 2099 understands that concept.
