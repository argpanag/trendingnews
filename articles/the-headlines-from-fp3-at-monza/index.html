The Headlines from FP3 at Monza

TheTools · 08.11.2026 14:18 · source · permalink

Nick Cassidy (Hypercar) and Alessio Rovera (LMGT3) set the fastest laps in FP3 at a wet Monza this morning…

Hypercar

  • The final free practice session took place this morning ahead of qualifying for tomorrow’s 6 Hours of Monza, as competitors again took to a wet track in cool conditions at the ‘Temple of Speed’ – Italy’s legendary Autodromo Nazionale Monza

  • Several cars took turns at the head of the times during the first half of the session, with Malthe Jakobsen initially setting the pace in the #94 Peugeot 9X8

  • But it was the sister #93 Peugeot piloted by Nick Cassidy that ultimately claimed the best lap courtesy of a last-ditch time of 1m42.706s, as the New Zealander topped a Hypercar session for the first time

  • There was, however, an investigation into the #93 car’s tyre pressures immediately after the session ended

  • Cassidy’s lap was a mere 0.040s quicker than the Victor Martins-driven Alpine A424, which will contest its final FIA WEC race tomorrow; the young Frenchman punched in a 1m42.746s to continue his excellent rookie form

  • The #83 AF Corse Ferrari ensured the tifosi was warmed up sufficiently for the qualifying action, as Robert Kubica set the third-fastest time in the distinctive yellow 499P

  • BMW took fourth position, with the #20 M Hybrid V8 driven by Sheldon van der Linde registering another late lap as the track dried slightly; that car had earlier survived a trip down the escape road, when Robin Frijns struggled under braking

  • The #50 Ferrari of Antonio Fuoco, Miguel Molina and Nicklas Nielsen ended up fifth, just ahead of the #19 Genesis Magma Racing GMR-001, which rounded out the top six

  • There was drama at the Parabolica after 15 minutes of the session, when the Kubica-driven #83 AF Corse Ferrari and #007 Aston Martin Valkyrie of Tom Gamble collided, with both spinning but able to continue unscathed; the Brit was later given a 10-second stop/go penalty for triggering the altercation

  • There were several other off-track excursions during the session, with the championship points-leading #8 Toyota TR010 Hybrid skating across the gravel at the Ascari Chicane and the #36 Alpine going off at Parabolica after Martins almost tripped over the #33 TF Sport Corvette

  • Elsewhere, the #17 Genesis GMR-001 took to the escape road at the Rettifilio Chicane, with ‘Pipo’ Derani managing to rejoin without significant issue

  • That corner was similarly the site of a trip across the gravel for Sébastien Bourdais in the #38 Cadillac Hertz Team JOTA V-Series.R the Frenchman shares with Earl Bamber and Jack Aitken; the sister #12 car also had a couple of incidents, including a spin at Parabolica, but continued largely unaffected

  • There were issues for last year’s title-winning #51 Ferrari AF Corse 499P, which completed only seven laps after a brake-by-wire issue confined it to its pit garage for the majority of the session

LMGT3

  • Alessio Rovera claimed bragging rights in the production-based LMGT3 category, as Ferrari gave the large number of home fans a boost in FP3

  • The Italian peaked on a 1m56.069s lap in the #21 VISTA AF Corse 296 Evo he shares with François Heriau and Simon Mann

  • Rovera’s time was threatened in the final minutes of the drying session, with Maxime Martin coming closest in the #61 Iron Lynx Mercedes-AMG; the Belgian stopped the clocks with a time of 1m56.351s

  • That Mercedes had survived a wild ride across the gravel trap earlier on, when Martin Berry overcooked it on the exit of the Ascari Chicane

  • The sister #79 Mercedes similarly showed strong pace, as Matteo Cressoni posted a 1m56.442s lap to put both Iron Lynx cars inside the top three positions

  • Title-chasing TF Sport took fourth position, as Jonny Edgar, Ben Keating and Nicky Catsburg continue to prepare for what could be the team’s and Edgar’s crowning ceremony tomorrow afternoon

  • Team WRT claimed fifth place with the #32 BMW M4 Evo, while the #87 Akkodis ASP Team Lexus RC F completed the top six spots

  • There was trouble for the #34 Racing Team Turkey by TF Corvette when Peter Dempsey spun at the Ascari Chicane and was temporarily stranded on-track; the Irish ace was able to re-start and return to the pits
