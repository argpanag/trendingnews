Charles De Ketelaere Hails Manchester City’s Jeremy Doku After Starring Belgium Display

Jeremy Doku has endured a topsy-turvy start to the season at Manchester City.

It has been a frustrating start to the season for Jeremy Doku. A calf injury sustained in the FA Community Shield kept the Manchester City winger out of the opening weeks, and he has struggled for rhythm and end product - barring a stunning cameo at Anfield - since returning.

OneFootball Videos

Since he arrived from Rennes in 2023, the Belgian has been regarded as perhaps the most electric dribbler in the Premier League, albeit one who often struggles to turn that into consistent goals and assists. Whilst on November international duty, though, he offered a timely reminder of exactly what he can do.

Doku was back to his destructive best for Belgium this week, prompting praise from a teammate who knows his game well - and who had no hesitation in placing the 24-year-old among the very best on the planet in one particular area.

De Ketelaere’s verdict after the Turkey win

Doku was the standout figure in Belgium’s 2-1 win over Turkey, a game that marked his first international appearance since the World Cup.

His comeback was a welcome sight for new Belgium head coach Mark van Bommel, and the winger repaid the faith with a decisive display.

He provided the assist for his former City teammate Kevin De Bruyne to equalise, then sparked the winner with a moment of individual quality that led to Charles De Ketelaere’s finish. Afterwards, De Ketelaere was happy to complement his international colleague.

“We know how good he is,” he said in an interview with Sporza, as relayed by Sport Witness. "And we know how important it is for a team to have players who can take on and beat opponents. It creates space for the others. In one-on-one situations, he is one of the strongest footballers in the world.”

A timely lift for a stuttering season

If this is to spark a return to Doku’s best form, it could hardly come at a better time for Manchester City. Doku has managed just one goal and one assist in 10 appearances in all competitions this term, numbers well below his best, and he has failed to contribute in any of City’s last three league games - a chastening run of defeats to Aston Villa, Brighton and Hove Albion and Nottingham Forest.

Arguably still working his way back to full sharpness after that calf problem, the Belgian will hope this is the sort of display that kick-starts his campaign.
