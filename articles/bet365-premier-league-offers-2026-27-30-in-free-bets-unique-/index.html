The Premier League season brings a fresh wave of fixtures, and bet365 is marking the occasion with a new customer offer designed to get bettors started with some Free Bets in hand. Sign up using bonus code HR365, place a £10 bet, and you'll receive £30 in Free Bets once that bet settles.

Below, we break down exactly how the offer works, what to watch for in the terms, and what else bet365 has on offer for both new and existing customers this season.

What Is the bet365 Sign Up Offer?

bet365 is offering new customers the chance to bet £10 and get £30 in Free Bets ahead of the new Premier League season. The offer runs using bonus code HR365, which new customers enter during registration.

It's worth understanding how the reward is paid before signing up. The £30 isn't handed over as cash. It's paid as Bet Credits, which become available once your qualifying bet has settled, up to the value of your qualifying deposit. This distinction matters because Bet Credits work differently to a straightforward cash Free Bet: the Bet Credits stake itself is never returned, even on a winning bet, so a £10 Bet Credit at 2/1 pays out £20 in winnings rather than £30. They must be used within the stated time limit or they expire.

How to Claim the bet365 Bet £10 Get £30 Offer

Claiming the offer follows a straightforward sequence:

  1. Register a new account using bonus code HR365.
  2. Deposit the minimum required amount into your account.
  3. Place a qualifying bet of £10 at the minimum qualifying odds.

For Premier League bettors, timing is part of the appeal. Signing up ahead of the opening weekend, or before a heavy fixture list, means Bet Credits are ready to use across the markets that matter most during the early rounds of the season.

Bet Credits from this offer aren't restricted to single bets either. They can be used across match markets, Bet Builder selections, and outright markets, giving new customers reasonable flexibility in how they put the £30 to use once it's credited.

bet365's Edit Bet Feature: An Underused Tool for Premier League Bettors

One feature worth knowing about alongside Cash Out is Edit Bet. It lets customers add, swap or remove selections from a bet that's already been placed, without manually cashing out and placing a fresh bet themselves. Behind the scenes, bet365 does exactly that automatically: the existing bet is cashed out at its current value, and a new bet is placed using that amount as the stake, at the odds available at the time of the edit. It's only available where Cash Out is possible on every unsettled selection in the bet.
