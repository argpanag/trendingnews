After widespread rain, a passing Monday morning shower is possible before clearing - The Boston Globe

TheTools · 14.09.2026 00:02 · source · permalink

Much-needed rainfall was unleashed across the Greater Boston area and the southern portions of Massachusetts, Rhode Island, and Connecticut, which saw the brunt of the precipitation — up to 4 to 6 inches’ worth in some locations.

For the second time this year, Geoffrey Saunders, of Fairfield, on Sunday saw his 1984 Pontiac Fiero get flooded from all the rain. “Remember me? With the flooded Fiero. Well it happened again,” Saunders posted on Facebook under three frames of his car showing it slowly being submerged by the floodwaters. “Going, Going, Gone!”

Monday: Sun finally returns

As we start the new work week, the first thing that you’re going to notice is clearing skies taking place during the Monday morning commute. A frontal system will be passing through the area and there may be a quick shower, even a thunderstorm, up until about 8 or 9 a.m.

Dew points, which are in the 60s by sunrise Monday, will fall back into the 50s and even some 40s across Northern New England. This means much more comfortable air once again. It’s worth noting that during this time of the year, any noticeable humidity tends not to stick around very long. Rather, we get muggy for a day or two, followed by the drier air from Canada, and eventually it becomes even more rare to have the high humidity.

Temperatures Monday afternoon will reach near 70 degrees, and it should be pretty bright for much of that time with a deeper blue to the sky. You’ll also notice a bit of a northwest wind at times that’s rather gusty. As the sun sets in the 6 p.m. hour, Monday night temperatures will quickly fall, and many areas will be back down in the 40s when you get up Tuesday morning.

Tuesday: Abundant sunshine, a little cooler

With abundant September sunshine, it’s a little cooler and back into the lower 70s to upper 60s in some spots Tuesday afternoon, and near 50 Tuesday night. The dry weather should continue into Thursday as well.

With the cooler air in place and the ground still warm, this is a good time to overseed your lawn and thicken it up, if it has had summer damage. Even dew in the mornings this time of year can be enough to help the seeds germinate.

Monday-Tuesday breakdown

Greater Boston: Look for an early shower, otherwise clearing skies and temperatures near 70 this afternoon with the sun. It’s in the upper 60s to low 70s on Tuesday with a cool start in the 40s.

Central and Western Mass.: A possible quick pre-sunrise shower or even a thunderstorm followed by drier air with temperatures approaching 70 in the afternoon. Readings will start in the 40s on Tuesday with highs 68 to 71.

Southeastern Mass.: Look for an early shower or thunderstorm Monday morning followed by clearing skies. Temperatures will be near 70 with lowering humidity. Look for sunshine on Tuesday with the highs near 70.

Cape Cod and the Islands: Look for an early shower or thunderstorm on Monday with highs in the lower 70s there will be afternoon sunshine. Temperatures will start near 50 Tuesday morning with highs in the upper 60s with brilliant sunshine.

Rhode Island: Look for plenty of clouds in a quick shower or a thunderstorm Monday morning followed by sunshine in the afternoon, highs 70 to 75. More sunshine on Tuesday with highs in the upper 60s to near 70.

New Hampshire: Look for skies to become mostly sunny Monday with highs near 70. Temperatures will be in the 40s Monday night. Highs near 70 again on Tuesday along with sunshine.
