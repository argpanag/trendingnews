Chiefs-Cardinals Inactives: DT Peter Woods to miss third straight game

TheTools · 22.11.2026 16:45 · source · permalink
  • Kansas City Chiefs Injuries

Kansas City and Arizona have released their inactive player lists for Sunday’s game.

The Kansas City Chiefs have released their list of inactive players for Sunday’s game against the Arizona Cardinals.

Running back Kenneth Walker, tackle Jaylon Moore, guard Trey Smith defensive tackles Peter Woods and Omarr Norman-Lott and cornerback Melvin Smith will not dress. Rookie Garrett Nussmeir has been designated as the third quarterback and is eligible to enter the game should starter Patrick Mahomes and backup Justin Fields become unavailable.

Walker (groin), Smith (concussion) and Moore (ankle) had already been ruled out on Friday’s final injury report. Woods (neck) and Norman-Lott (knee) had been deemed questionable, but the team will hold them out at least one more week. Norman-Lott is a listed inactive for the first time this season as the Chiefs added him to the active roster on Wednesday with his practice window to return from the Reserve/Physically-Unable-to-Perform list expiring. Norman-Lott has spent the first half of the season continuing to recover from an ACL tear suffered in 2025.

Woods left Week 9’s contest against the Denver Broncos with a neck injury. The first round rookie will now miss his third straight game.

With a short turnaround before Thursday’s road game against the Buffalo Bills, the Chiefs were expected to be conservative with injuries against the Cardinals.

On Saturday, Kansas City elevated running back Nate Carter and center Pete Nygra from the practice squad. As elevated players, they will automatically be returned to the team’s practice squad after the game. Carter and Nygra are both active for the contest.

The Cardinals have also released their list of inactives.

Quarterback Carson Beck, cornerback Starling Thomas V, offensive linemen Kingsley Eguakun and John Gaines II, and former Chiefs practice squad defensive tackle Andrew Billings will not go for the Cardinals.

Obviously, the biggest name on Arizona’s list is Beck. The team turned to the rookie quarterback after former Chiefs backup Gardner Minshew suffered a season ending elbow injury in Week 9. However, Beck was a midweek downgrade in practice with back spasms. Despite being ruled questionable to close the week, Arizona downgraded Beck to out on Saturday before leaving for Kansas City.

40-year-old journeyman backup Josh Johnson — who has been with the team less than two weeks — will start for the Cardinals. Johnson has not appeared in game in 2026.

Gaines had already been ruled out with an ankle injury.

The Cardinals elevated practice squad wide receiver Zay Jones for the matchup. Jones is active for the game.
