Osimhen's brace fires Galatasaray to 2-1 derby victory over Fenerbahce

TheTools · 26.10.2026 23:58 · source · permalink

Reporting the Changing World

Anadolu Ajansı © 2026

Victor Osimhen scored twice in the first half on Monday as Galatasaray defeated cross-town archrivals Fenerbahce 2-1 in round nine of the Turkish Super Lig.

The Nigerian striker struck in the 29th and 44th minutes at RAMS Park in Istanbul, while Irfan Can Kahveci pulled one back for the visitors with an acrobatic effort in the second half.

Galatasaray made a promising start, with Baris Alper Yilmaz forcing Fenerbahce goalkeeper Ederson into a save from a tight angle in the 15th minute.

Fenerbahce came close to taking the lead in the 26th minute when Davinson Sanchez's attempted clearance from N'Golo Kante's cross struck the post.

Three minutes later, Osimhen broke the deadlock after exchanging passes with Baris Alper Yilmaz before driving into the penalty area and firing a powerful shot into the top right corner.

Ederson prevented the Nigerian from doubling his tally in the 38th minute, diving to push his low effort from a tight angle out for a corner.

However, Osimhen struck again in the 44th minute, heading home after redirecting Sanchez's powerful first-time effort following a long throw-in by Ismail Jakobs.

Galatasaray dominated the opening half, registering 11 shots, including six on target, while Fenerbahce failed to produce a single attempt before the break.

The hosts continued to threaten after halftime, with Jakobs hitting the crossbar with a header in the 55th minute following Roland Sallai's cross.

Fenerbahce, however, pulled one back in the 62nd minute when Irfan Can Kahveci controlled Oguz Aydin's pass and scored with an acrobatic bicycle kick despite goalkeeper Ugurcan Cakir getting a hand to the ball.

The visitors pushed for an equalizer in the closing stages, but Milan Skriniar's header was cleared near the goal line in the 90th minute.

Galatasaray substitute Leroy Sane then forced Ederson into another save in stoppage time as the hosts held on to secure all three points.

The victory lifted Galatasaray back to the top of the Turkish Super Lig standings with 22 points after nine matches, while Fenerbahce dropped to fifth place with 16 points.
