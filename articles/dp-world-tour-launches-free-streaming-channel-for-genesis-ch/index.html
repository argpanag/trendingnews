DP World Tour launches free streaming channel for Genesis Championship

TheTools · 21.10.2026 23:06 · source · permalink

A free channel for this week's event in Korea

The DP World Tour has launched a free, ad-supported streaming channel. It will show this week's Genesis Championship in South Korea. The service is called the "DP World Tour channel" and is run in partnership with C15 Studio. It is a so-called FAST channel, short for Free Ad-Supported Streaming Television. Viewers do not need a subscription. Coverage is funded by advertising.

The Genesis Championship is co-sanctioned by the DP World Tour and the KPGA. It runs from Thursday, October 22, to Sunday, October 25, at Woojeong Hills Country Club in Cheonan, South Chungcheong Province. The event carries a $4 million purse, the largest prize fund on the KPGA Tour. This is the ninth edition of the tournament, and it is the DP World Tour's only stop in Korea this season.

Where the channel is available

How you can watch depends on where you live. In the UK and Ireland, the DP World Tour channel is available on Samsung TV, Amazon Prime, LG, Rakuten, Xiaomi, Vidaa and Roku.

In the US and Canada, the channel is listed on Samsung TV, FuboTV, LG, Amazon Fire TV, Vidaa, Google TV and Sony. Plex, Roku and DirecTV are also listed for the US. Outside these markets, the Tour also offers the channel through its YouTube channel.

Geo-blocking in markets with rights holders

The channel is not free everywhere. Where another broadcaster already holds DP World Tour rights, the free stream may be geo-blocked. Reported exclusions include large parts of Latin America, among them Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Mexico and Peru. Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland and Japan are also on the list. In the Nordic countries, for example, Viaplay holds the rights.

Viewers in the United States should check carefully what they can watch. The US also appears on the reported exclusion list for the free coverage. Golf Channel and the NBC Sports app have shown this event in previous years, but the 2026 arrangements have not been confirmed. Wherever a local rights holder exists, the safest option is to use that broadcaster's coverage. Fans should not expect free access in every country.

How to watch the Genesis Championship this week

The Tour has published a full viewing guide with broadcast windows for each round. Because the event is played in Korea, live coverage will air during the early hours of the morning in Europe. Fans in the UK and Ireland can look for the DP World Tour channel on the platforms listed above. Viewers in other regions should first check whether a local broadcaster holds the rights.

Lee chases a historic repeat

The tournament has a strong storyline. Jeong-hwan Lee returns as defending champion. Last year he won the Genesis Championship by three strokes over Nacho Elvira and Laurie Canter. It was his maiden DP World Tour title. No player has ever won this event twice in a row, so Lee can make history this week.

Two of Korea's biggest names are also in the field. Bookmakers have Si Woo Kim as the pre-tournament favourite, with Sungjae Im just behind him. Both will have strong home support at Woojeong Hills.
