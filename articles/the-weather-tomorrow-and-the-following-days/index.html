The weather tomorrow and the following days

TheTools · 19.10.2026 21:45 · source · permalink

Tomorrow in Kosovo, spring weather is expected to continue, with further increases in daily temperatures. The morning will be quite cool with minimum temperatures expected to drop to 5 degrees Celsius, while stable, sunny and warm with temperatures reaching up to 22 degrees Celsius.

In Albania, North Macedonia, the Presevo Valley and Montenegro, sunny and slightly warm weather is expected tomorrow, considering the time period we are in. Cloudiness will be few and only in a few regions. The sun will prevail in all areas with almost spring-like temperatures.

On Wednesday, first in Albania, clouds will begin to appear with local rainfall and torrential rain, then during the day, clouds will also include the territory of the Republic of Kosovo, also with local and temporary rainfall. In parts of Albania, more intense rainfall with lightning and thunder is sometimes expected. Cloudiness and torrential rain on Wednesday will include Montenegro, as well as North Macedonia, but with less and more local rainfall.

Thursday will be sunny and cloudy with isolated showers, and during the night and Friday morning there will be more intense rainfall in many areas, with lightning and thunder in places, and in the highest mountains there will be snowfall.

On Friday, slightly cooler during the day with gradual stabilization of the weather./MeteoBalkan
