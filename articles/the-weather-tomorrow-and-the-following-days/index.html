The weather tomorrow and the following days

TheTools · 23.11.2026 21:53 · source · permalink

After the strong cold front, temperatures have experienced an intense drop, and in addition to rain, there have also been mainly short-term snowfalls in some places, and snow layers have formed in some mountainous areas. Now the weather will clear up.

Tomorrow in Kosovo the morning will be quite cold, with frost in places, while the day will be mostly sunny, but cold compared to the days we left behind.

Although it will be cold, the atmosphere will be mostly stable.

Temperatures are expected to drop to -4 degrees Celsius in some regions, while the maximum during the day is expected to be around 6 degrees Celsius.

In Albania and our entire region tomorrow, mainly sunny but cold weather is expected. The morning will be frosty, especially in the mountainous areas of the continental regions. Frost will also be present in the lowlands of the continental regions.

The day will continue to be mostly sunny and relatively cold. Normally a little warmer in the Mediterranean areas of Albania.

On Wednesday, after a frosty morning, there will be increasing cloudiness during the day and precipitation will begin in the late afternoon or evening. In some regions where humidity meets the remnants of cold air, snowfall is expected, but very soon with the increase in temperatures all precipitation will turn into rain, with the exception of the highest mountain areas. Warmer Mediterranean air will penetrate along with another powerful cyclonic system from the Mediterranean centered in northern Italy. Heavy rainfall will begin in northern Albania and Montenegro, including parts of Dukagjini in Kosovo.

On Thursday, cloudy weather with rain awaits us./MeteoBalkan
