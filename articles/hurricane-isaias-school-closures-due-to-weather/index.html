HURRICANE ISAIAS: School closures due to weather

TheTools · 07.10.2026 18:39 · source · permalink

PANAMA CITY, Fla. (WJHG/WECP) - Here is a list of schools closing due to the potential impacts of Hurricane Isaias.

  • Bay District Schools - CLOSED 10/8-10/9 - Per BDS: “In the likely event that Deane Bozeman School will open as a shelter for the approaching storm, all Bay District Schools campuses, district offices and Bay Base will also be closed tomorrow, Thursday, October 8.”
  • Liberty County Schools- CLOSED 10/9
  • Jackson County Schools- CLOSED 10/9
  • Walton County Schools- CLOSED 10/8-10/9
  • Okaloosa County Schools- CLOSED 10/8-10/9
  • Washington County Schools- Early dismissal on Thursday 10/8 at 12:00 p.m., CLOSED on 10/9
  • Gulf County Schools- Early dismissal on Thursday 10/8, CLOSED on 10/9
  • Holmes County Schools- CLOSED on 10/9, All extracurricular activities scheduled for Friday are cancelled as well. After-school activities on Thursday, October 8 may continue as planned.
  • Franklin County Schools- CLOSED on 10/9
  • Calhoun County Schools- CLOSED on 10/9
