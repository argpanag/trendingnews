How to Claim Kerala Lottery Prize Money

Winners must follow the Kerala State Lotteries Department's prescribed process to claim their prize money.

According to the claim information supplied, prizes of up to ₹5,000 can be claimed through authorised lottery agents or retailers in Kerala. For prizes above ₹5,000, winners are required to submit their original winning ticket and valid identity proof at an authorised bank or government lottery office.

Winners should ensure that the ticket is genuine and follow the official instructions for submitting a claim.
