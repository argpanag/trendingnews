Plejd just reported Q3 2026 numbers that kept the growth story intact, yet the stock came into the release looking stretched after a weak run, with the share price down over the past week and month and trading on a rich 43.9x P/E. The headline is simple: revenue landed at 315.014 million SEK and net income reached 56.385 million SEK, which fed into trailing 12 month earnings of 251.215 million SEK. The real tension now is whether that earnings strength is enough to justify a valuation that already prices in a lot of good news.

Plejd’s earnings power backs a positive tilt

Plejd gives bulls some real numbers to work with. Revenue of 315.014 million SEK and net income of 56.385 million SEK both track above last year’s Q3, while trailing 12 month profit of 251.215 million SEK is well ahead of the prior TTM figure. That pattern points to a business in smart building automation that is scaling its product ecosystem, not just holding ground. For anyone attracted to the structural theme in connected lighting and controls, the latest income statement broadly supports a constructive view on the underlying franchise.

Short term share weakness keeps risk questions alive

The recent share performance tells a more cautious story. The stock declined 2.9% over 7 days, 7.6% over 30 days and 1.2% over 90 days as of 16 October 2026. That slide, despite rising quarterly and trailing 12 month earnings, suggests some investors still worry about execution, competition or cyclicality in hardware centric smart home exposure. Plejd’s results reduce concerns about immediate earnings pressure, yet the market’s cooler tone implies the debate on durability and capital intensity of this business model remains open.
