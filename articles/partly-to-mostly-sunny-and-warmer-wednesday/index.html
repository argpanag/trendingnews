Good Morning! Today is Wednesday, October 14, 2026. Here is today’s forecast:

It will be partly to mostly sunny and dry today. Patchy fog is possible this morning, and some of the fog may be locally dense, especially in the valleys of southwestern Montana. A DENSE FOG ADVISORY is in effect for the Canyon Ferry area, the Missouri Headwaters, and the Gallatin Valley until 12pm today.

Temperatures will be warmer today than they have been over the past few days, with highs climbing into the 50s and low to mid 60s, warmest along the Hi-Line. Along the Rocky Mountain Front, it will be breezy today, with sustained winds of 10 to 25 mph. Elsewhere, there will be a little breeze around today, with sustained winds of 5 to 20 mph.

Tomorrow looks beautiful, with abundant sunshine, dry conditions, and cool temperatures. Highs will generally be in the upper 40s and low to mid 50s. Around Helena and across western portions of north-central Montana, there will be little to no wind tomorrow. Across eastern portions of north-central Montana, gusty winds are expected tomorrow, with sustained winds of 10 to 25 mph, and gusts over 40 mph are possible.

A significant warm-up is expected Friday and Saturday, with highs climbing into the 60s and low to mid 70s. Saturday is expected to be the warmest day. Sunshine will be abundant on Friday, while Saturday will feature a mix of sun and clouds. Dry conditions are expected both days.

It will be breezy in some areas on Friday, with sustained winds of 10 to 25 mph. On Saturday, widespread gusty to strong winds are expected across central and western portions of north-central Montana. Along the Rocky Mountain Front, sustained winds of 25 to 45 mph are expected, with gusts up to 70 mph possible. Across the plains, sustained winds of 10 to 30 mph are forecast, with gusts exceeding 40 mph possible.

It will be breezy in some areas on Friday, with sustained winds of 10 to 25 mph. On Saturday, widespread gusty to strong winds are expected across north-central Montana, with wind speeds gradually decreasing farther east. Along the Rocky Mountain Front, sustained winds of 20 to 45 mph are expected, with gusts up to 70 mph possible. Across the plains, sustained winds of 10 to 30 mph are forecast, with gusts exceeding 40 mph possible.

Pleasant and cooler weather is expected Sunday and Monday, with highs in the mid to upper 50s and low to mid 60s. Sunday will be mostly sunny and dry, while Monday will be partly cloudy and mostly dry. Light winds are expected both days.

There are then going to be some scattered rain and mountain snow showers around on Tuesday as a disturbance begins to move into the region. Skies will be mostly cloudy, and temperatures will be cooler, with highs mainly in the upper 40s and 50s.

Have a wonderful Wednesday!
Chief Meteorologist Ryan Dennis
