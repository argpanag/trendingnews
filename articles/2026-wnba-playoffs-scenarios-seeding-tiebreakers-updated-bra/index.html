TheTools · 21.09.2026 20:56 · source · permalink

With four days left to play in the WNBA 2026 regular season, the eight playoff teams are still looking to lock in their bracket spots.

The regular season ends on Thursday, Sept. 24, with the first round of playoffs starting on Sunday Sept. 27.

The eight teams listed above are locked in for the playoffs, but still looking to secure their seeding. The Minnesota Lynx lead the charge for the top spot, needing just one win or one Valkyries loss. The Lynx own the tiebreaker over Golden State having won the season series 3-1.

The top four teams in the playoffs receive home court advantage in first round. The Lynx and Valkyries are the only teams that have clinched home court advantage in the first round so far.

The Aces can clinch home court advantage as soon as Monday night if the Dream lose. The Dream can can clinch by winning out or with one Fever loss. The Dream cannot clinch home court advantage tonight vs the Liberty.
