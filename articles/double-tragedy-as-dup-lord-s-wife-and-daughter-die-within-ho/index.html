Double tragedy as DUP Lord's wife and daughter die within hours of each other

TheTools · 16.11.2026 10:36 · source · permalink

DUP chairman Lord Morrow has been struck by a double tragedy as his wife, Lady Jennifer, and daughter, Rachel, died within hours of each other on Sunday.

The pair's daughter, Rachel Burrows, passed peacefully, surrounded by her family. A few hours later Lady Jennifer Morrow also passed away.

Rachel is survived by her road racing husband, John, and her two children, Jack and Isla.

Paying tribute, DUP leader Gavin Robinson said: “Words cannot adequately express the heartbreak and sorrow felt across our Party at the devastating news that our Party Chairman, Lord Morrow, has lost both his beloved daughter Rachel and his dear wife Jennifer within hours of each other yesterday.

"On behalf of the Democratic Unionist Party, I extend our deepest sympathy to Maurice, to Rachel’s husband John, and to the entire Morrow and Burrows families at this most difficult of times.

"Maurice Morrow has been a rock of strength and support to so many of us through difficult moments in our lives. Now, in his darkest hour, it is our turn to be a rock for him."

Gavin Robinson said that throughout Jennifer and Rachel’s long battles, Lord Morrow "demonstrated extraordinary love and strength."

"I know that his deep Christian faith has sustained him through those difficult days, but I also know that today his heart is breaking as a husband, a father and a grandfather," he continued.

"There are no words that can ease the pain of such an unimaginable loss. But Maurice and his family should know that they will be supported by the prayers of their many friends and colleagues across Northern Ireland.

"We pray that the Lord will draw especially near to Maurice, John and the entire family, and grant them the strength and peace that only He can give in the days ahead.”

Writing on X, Deputy First Minister Emma Little-Pengelly said: "Absolutely tragic for the whole family circle. Lady Morrow, Jennifer was a much-loved and respected woman who gave many years of loyal and dedicated service to her role and to politics.

"She will be deeply missed. Our hearts are absolutely breaking for Maurice, her surviving daughter Victoria and the whole family circle. It is hard to fathom the depth of grief of the family and friends of this double tragedy and loss."
