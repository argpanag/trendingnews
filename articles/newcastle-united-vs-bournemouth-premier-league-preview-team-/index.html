Match Facts

  • Newcastle United are winless in their last eight Premier League meetings with Bournemouth (D5 L3) since a 4-1 win in July 2020 against a Cherries side managed by Eddie Howe.

  • Bournemouth have won their last two away Premier League matches against Newcastle and have a 1.75 points-per-game at St James’ Park (14 points in 8 games). Of stadiums they’ve played at five or times, only at Molineux (2 points per game) do they enjoy a higher ratio.

  • Newcastle have won three of their last six Premier League games (D2 L1), as many as they had in their previous 13 (D1 L9).

  • Bournemouth have opened the scoring in both Premier League games this season, losing 2-1 to Man City and drawing 1-1 with Everton. No side has ever scored first but failed to win any of their first three games in a campaign in the competition.

  • Bournemouth have drawn 19 Premier League games since the start of last season, four more than any other side, while only opponents Newcastle (29) have dropped more points from leading positions than the Cherries (27) in that time.

  • No side has faced more shots in the Premier League this season than Newcastle United (44), who only faced eight shots in their opening two games in 2025-26. It’s the most they’ve faced in their first two Premier League games of a season on record since 2003-04.

  • In the last two seasons, Bournemouth have conceded the most set piece goals in the Premier League (28), with the Cherries conceding from a corner in both games so far this season.

  • Anthony Elanga has scored in both of Newcastle’s two Premier League games this season. The only player to score in each of the Magpies’ first three in a Premier League campaign is Andrew Cole in 1994-95.

  • Anthony Elanga has put Newcastle 1-0 up in both of their Premier League games this season. The last player to score the opening goal in each of the first three matchdays of a campaign was Alexandre Lacazette for Arsenal in 2020-21.

  • Bournemouth defender James Hill ranks first for aerial duels won (107) and clearances (158) in the Premier League in 2026, while he’s joint-top for headed clearances (91) and second for duels won (152), behind Elliot Anderson (163).
