The Pittsburgh Steelers cap off Sunday’s slate of games with a highly anticipated matchup against the Cincinnati Bengals.

With the Pittsburgh Steelers possessing the tiebreaker over the Cincinnati Bengals, the stakes are high heading into Sunday night with the two currently sitting at 6-2 in the AFC North division standings. Unfortunately for the Bengals, negative regression is looming large in their rematch while Joe Burrow tries to throw over the top of a defense that excels in coverage.

Even though there is only one game taking the field on Sunday night, there are still plenty of prop bets and markets to wager on which you can read a detailed breakdown of them below.

Best betting promos and sportsbooks for NFL prop bets

Find the best promos and sportsbooks to use for today’s Sunday Night Football prop bets.

Pittsburgh Steelers vs Cincinnati Bengals (8:20pm, EST)

Joe Burrow Under 26.5 Pass Completions (-121) at Caesars Sportsbook

At the halfway point of the regular season, Joe Burrow has built a strong case for winning the MVP award, ranking in the top-3 in QB EPA per play, Completion Percentage Over Expected, and in Success Rate. With Burrow playing at a high level, the Cincinnati Bengals are able to set a scoring pace too fast for their opponents to keep up with, improving their probability of winning on a weekly basis.

In a daunting matchup against the Pittsburgh Steelers, Burrow will struggle to connect with his pass catchers while battling it out against a secondary that ranks seventh in Dropback EPA Allowed per play. Especially with Chase Brown underwhelming on the ground, allowing the Steelers to stretch out in coverage, smothering Burrow’s passing lanes at multiple levels of the field.

Chase Brown Under 43.5 Rushing Yards (-115) at BetMGM Sportsbook

Speaking of Brown, the Bengals running back has been unable to get into a groove this season, ranking below league average in Percentage of Carries Over Expected while averaging less than four Yards per Carry. Even with Ja’Marr Chase and Tee Higgins on the outside, Brown has been unable to take advantage of the extra space in the middle, going down as soon as a defender makes contact.

Heading into Sunday night’s contest against the Steelers, Brown will continue to struggle to burst through the trenches while combatting a front seven that currently leads the league in Def Rush Success Rate. For some more exposure, escalator bet Brown’s Rushing Yardage prop by placing smaller wagers on his alternate unders at the best available odds across multiple sportsbooks.

Chase Brown Longest Rush Under 12.5 Yards (-125) at BetMGM Sportsbook

With Pittsburgh’s secondary capable of holding their own in coverage, the Steelers will be able to stack the box at a higher rate, giving them extra bodies to slow Brown down before the Bengals running back is able to reach the second level. To help diversify your risk, place a separate wager on Brown to be held under the total of 11.5 on his Rush Attempts prop as well.

Jaylen Warren Over 61.5 Rushing Yards (-114) at FanDuel Sportsbook

While Brown has struggled, Jaylen Warren has thrived, leading the position in Yards Over Expected per Attempt while averaging 5.9 Yards per Carry. The protection Warren has received from his offensive line has played a major role in his success, running behind a group that ranks in the top-5 in Run Block Grade per PFF.

Luckily for Warren, the Steelers running back will be able to consistently gash the Bengals defense by taking advantage of a front seven that resides in the bottom half of the board in Def Rush EPA Allowed per play. Even with Dexter Lawrence II anchoring the middle of the defensive line, the Steelers' ability to open up running lanes on the outside will help counter the Bengals interior pressure, leading to an uptick in Warren’s total number of explosive runs to help clear the over on his Rushing Yardage prop.

Pat Freiermuth Anytime Touchdown Scorer (+275) at FanDuel Sportsbook

When in scoring position, Pat Freiermuth has been as efficient as it gets, hauling in all six of his Targets while converting four of his Receptions into touchdowns. With the Steelers expected to get inside the 20 at a higher rate by keeping the ball on the ground, Freiermuth will be able to generate a higher volume of Targets near the goal line, increasing his chances of scoring.
