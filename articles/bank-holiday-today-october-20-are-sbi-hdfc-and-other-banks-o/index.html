Bank Holiday Today, October 20: Are SBI, HDFC and other banks open or closed on Dussehra?

TheTools · 20.10.2026 04:06 · source · permalink

Dussehra Bank Holiday Today: Banks in India will remain closed in several cities on Tuesday, October 20, 2026, on account of Dussehra (Vijaya Dashami), Maha Navami, Ayudhapooja and Durga Puja, depending on regional observances. However, customers can continue to access digital banking services, including internet banking, mobile banking, UPI and ATMs.

Customers of State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Punjab National Bank and other financial institutions should note that bank branches will remain shut in select cities on October 20.

As per the Reserve Bank of India’s (RBI) bank holiday schedule, holidays vary across states and cities depending on regional festivals and occasions. Therefore, branches in several cities will not operate on Tuesday.

On Tuesday, October 20, banks will remain closed in the following cities for Dussehra (Vijaya Dashami), Maha Navami, Ayudhapooja and Durga Puja, as applicable, according to the RBI holiday calendar:

Customers in these cities are advised to plan branch visits accordingly and complete important banking work before the holiday. Customers should check the applicable city-wise holiday schedule, as closures may differ across regions.

Banks will observe additional holidays in different regions over the coming days of the week.

October 21 (Wednesday): Banks in Agartala, Bengaluru, Gangtok, Guwahati, Kolkata, Kochi, Shillong and Thiruvananthapuram will remain closed for Vijaya Dashami, Durga Puja (Dasain) and Maha Dashami, as applicable.

October 22 (Thursday): Bank branches in Gangtok will remain closed on account of Durga Puja (Dasain).

October 23 (Friday): Banks in Gangtok will remain closed for Durga Puja (Dasain).

October 24 (Saturday): Banks across India will remain closed as it is the fourth Saturday of the month, under the RBI’s regular second- and fourth-Saturday holiday rule.

October 25 (Sunday): Bank branches across India will remain closed for the regular weekly holiday.

Customers should note that bank branches may operate normally in cities where no local holiday has been declared. Checking the RBI’s city-wise holiday calendar before visiting a branch can help avoid inconvenience.

Types of bank holidays as per the RBI are divided into three categories:

1) Bank Holidays as per Negotiable Instruments Act–This is related to cheque clearance and branch banking services.

2) RTGS Holidays–Days on which no transactions are made by way of Real Time Gross Settlement system through branches of the banks.

3) Bank Holiday of Account Closing–Generally falls on April 1 each year.

Yes, despite physical branches being closed, digital and online banking services remain fully operational.

24/7 Availability: RTGS, NEFT, UPI and internet banking services operate continuously throughout the holidays. Customers can use the digital banking services to transfer funds, pay bills and shop online at any hour of the day, including Sundays, second and fourth Saturdays and on official bank holidays.

Transaction Processing: Digital transactions reach completion in less than one minute or within thirty minutes in most situations. When a user performs a payment through mobile banking or internet banking, the transaction will process without any delays during holidays.

Branch Limitations: Physical bank branches have restrictions, while digital channels enable users to access all services without interruptions. Bank branches will not process RTGS transactions that customers initiate at the branch during holidays or during the second and fourth Saturdays when the branch remains closed. The bank only processes transactions that customers submit through its online digital platforms during these specific times.
