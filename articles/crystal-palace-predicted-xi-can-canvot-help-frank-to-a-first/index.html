Crystal Palace predicted XI: can Canvot help Frank to a first win on matchday 5?

Crystal Palace predicted XI for Real Sociedad, with a decision alongside Chris Richards in defence. See the projected teams for Thomas Frank’s home debut.

Jaydee Canvot partners Chris Richards in our Crystal Palace predicted XI for the Europa League game against Real Sociedad. Frank used a back four at Coventry and must decide who partners Chris Richards against Real Sociedad after conceding three on his debut.

Canvot, Axel Disasi and Honest Ahanor offer different options alongside Richards. Ben Chilwell could replace Tyrick Mitchell on the left if Frank wants a full-back who will get forward outside his winger.

Who partners Chris Richards?

With Ismaïla Sarr unavailable, Yeremy Pino and Dwight McNeil are candidates to supply Jørgen Strand Larsen. Against a side with Takefusa Kubo available on its right flank, Palace’s left-sided pairing will also have defensive work to do.

Real Sociedad could use Mikel Oyarzabal through the middle with Takefusa Kubo, Luka Sučić and Gonçalo Guedes behind him. Frank’s full-backs would then have to judge when to go forward and when to stay close to the centre-backs.

Crystal Palace predicted XI for Thursday

Henderson. Khalaili, Canvot, Richards, Chilwell. Timber, Doucouré, Hughes. Pino, McNeil. Strand Larsen.

Real Sociedad projected starting line-up

Remiro. Aramburu, Martín, Zubeldia, Gómez. Gorrotxategi, Soler. Kubo, Sučić, Guedes. Oyarzabal.

Both elevens are predictions. The selections take account of Sports Mole’s match preview and Crystal Palace’s update from Thomas Frank. The official team sheets are due on Thursday.

Palace’s midfield selection matters as much as their defensive pairing. A three of Timber, Doucouré and Hughes would give Frank an extra player to cover either full-back, at the cost of one fewer runner supporting Strand Larsen.

What could change on the team sheet?

The visitors have already drawn 1-1 with both Union Saint-Gilloise and Lyon. When opponents have let them into the game, the damage has been greater: Lillestrøm conceded six. A second passing option for Palace’s full-backs would help them avoid turning clearances straight back over.
