Bayern Munich Aim to Extend Their Unbeaten Bundesliga Run

Vincent Kompany's side have made an impressive start to the season. After nine matchdays, the Bavarians have collected 21 points from six wins and three draws, scoring 29 goals while conceding just nine.

Bayern Munich are also unbeaten in their last 24 Bundesliga matches, a remarkable run highlighting their consistency and ability to deliver results against demanding opponents.

The biggest news for Bayern supporters is the return of Vincent Kompany to the touchline after missing five competitive matches for health reasons.

The Belgian coach returns to a team that recently defeated Mainz 2-1 and secured an important victory against Atlético de Madrid in the Champions League.

In attack, Harry Kane will be one of the players to watch. Although the English striker has recently struggled to find the net for his club, he has demonstrated another dimension to his game through his ability to create chances for his teammates.

Köln Look to End Their Difficult Run Against Bayern

Köln travel to Munich with nine points, sitting 15th in the standings.

René Wagner's team have failed to win their last four Bundesliga matches, increasing the pressure ahead of a visit to one of Germany's most intimidating stadiums.

History also favors the hosts. Bayern Munich are unbeaten in their last 22 league meetings with Köln, recording 20 victories and two draws.

One player capable of making a difference for the visitors is Saïd El Mala, the young forward who has emerged as one of Köln's main attacking threats.

However, the challenge will be enormous. Bayern Munich have already scored 20 goals in their opening four home matches of the Bundesliga season.
