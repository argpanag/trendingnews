Johnny McEvoy’s Funeral Cortège To Pass Through Greystones This Saturday

TheTools · 10.11.2026 16:54 · source · permalink
  • News Home
  • More from Entertainment & Lifestyle

Tuesday, 10 November 2026 at 15:09

The Offaly musician died on Sunday.

Fans of Offaly singer-songwriter Johnny McEvoy will have a chance to pay their respects this Saturday.

The Banagher musician who lived in Greystones in Wicklow, is to have a private funeral for family and close friends at his own request.

His funeral cortège is set to leave the Greystones Harbour Marina at 2:20pm this Saturday.

It will then drive along Cliff Road and Marine Road , which was a route he enjoyed walking daily.  

Donations in memory of Johnny are also asked to go torwards the Dublin Simon Community in lieu of flowers.
