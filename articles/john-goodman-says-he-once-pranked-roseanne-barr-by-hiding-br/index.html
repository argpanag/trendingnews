John Goodman is sharing a prank he played on Roseanne co-star Roseanne Barr, in which he hid Bruce Willis on set during a scene.

In an interview on the Happy Sad Confused podcast with Josh Horowitz, Goodman, 74, said that when he was working as a young actor in New York, he met Willis, who was at the time making a name for himself as a bartender at Cafe Central in the West Village.

"The success came a little later, but I met all these great actors that hung out there and we would just talk about acting. That's how I got to know Bruce," he said, adding, "He was in love with acting and so was I. And we just hit it off."

Goodman continued: "And when he made it, anytime I needed to be in Los Angeles, he'd put me up."

Shortly after Willis shot to stardom in Moonlighting, Goodman made his own claim to fame in Roseanne — and then the two decided to collaborate on a prank on Goodman's co-star.

"Bruce came over to hang out in my dressing room one day and I surprised Roseanne," Goodman recounted. "There was a reveal. It was supposed to be me in bed and she comes up and yanks the covers off. So I put Bruce in there and I think they have it on film. It's pretty cool."

Roseanne originally aired for nine seasons on ABC from 1988 to 1997 and returned for a 10th season in 2018.

The revival's planned 11th season was canceled after Barr, who played the titular matriarch of the working-class Conner family, sent a tweet likening Valerie Jarrett, a former advisor to President Barack Obama, to an ape.

The show went on as The Conners, with Goodman leading the cast.

Barr, now 73, recently shared where her relationship with Goodman and fellow Roseanne co-star Laurie Metcalf stands today, saying she's not in touch with either.

In an interview with The Hollywood Reporter, Barr was asked if she was in contact with the two "at all," to which she replied, "Of course not," adding, "I did too," when the interviewer said, "That's kind of sad. I assumed you guys were like a family."
