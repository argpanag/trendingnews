Aman Gandhi supports Uditi Singh as she wins captaincy task in Bigg Boss 20

TheTools · 03.10.2026 07:00 · source · permalink

Aman Gandhi has once again caught attention in Bigg Boss 20 with his sporting spirit and supportive approach during the latest captaincy task. With Uditi Singh, Mahhi Vij and Yung DSA competing for captaincy, the contestants were asked to choose their partners. Uditi chose Aman Gandhi, Yung DSA picked Scout, while Mahhi selected Qazi Tauqeer.

As the task unfolded, Aman remained committed to supporting Uditi. He backed her throughout the challenge and played his role as her partner with consistency. Uditi eventually emerged victorious and became the new captain of the Bigg Boss 20 house.

Aman’s approach during the task stood out as he focused on supporting Uditi rather than overshadowing her moment. His actions reflected a balance between competition and camaraderie, adding another side to his personality inside the house.

His journey in Bigg Boss 20 has given viewers an opportunity to see different facets of his personality beyond his work on television. From participating in tasks to sharing lighter moments with housemates and taking a stand when he feels it is necessary, Aman has continued to make his presence felt in the house.

The actor has been part of several television projects, including Naagin 3, where he played Daksh Munshi, Bhagya Lakshmi as Ayushmaan “Ayush” Chopra, Kyunki Saas Bhi Kabhi Bahu Thi 2 as Hrithik Virani, and Kyunki Rishton Ke Bhi Roop Badalte Hain.

His support for Uditi during the captaincy task further highlighted his dependable and easy-going side. With Uditi taking charge as the new captain, Aman’s contribution to her victory adds another memorable moment to his Bigg Boss 20 journey.
