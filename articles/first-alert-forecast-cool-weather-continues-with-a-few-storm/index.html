FIRST ALERT FORECAST: Cool weather continues with a few storms today

TheTools · 23.09.2026 09:37 · source · permalink

LEXINGTON, Ky. (WKYT) - The cloudy and cool weather continues for today. Some of our region will get in on a few storms that could lead to locally heavy rainfall. A storm or two could be on the strong to severe side as well.

A Few Storms Possible Today

Cloudy skies will be with us to start the day with the chance of a storm or two during the morning. As we head into the afternoon, there may be some peeks of sunshine as there will continue to be a few showers and storms possible. Storms may contain locally heavy rainfall that could lead to flash flooding issues in eastern Kentucky. We may even see a strong to severe storm with damaging winds and large hail in southeastern Kentucky. Highs will run into the upper 60s to low 70s for highs.

Staying Cloudy Tonight

It will continue to stay cloudy as we head into this evening and this will not allow temperatures to drop that much once again. Overnight lows will be in the upper 50s to low 60s.

Sunshine Eventually Shows Up

Cloudy skies will continue throughout the day Thursday. It looks to stay dry and cool with high temperatures in the low to mid 70s. More sunshine arrives as we head into Friday as highs climb into the mid 70s. Clearer skies Friday night will add some chill to the air as we see lows in the mid to upper 40s!

Beautiful Fall Weekend Ahead

It stays very nice for this weekend as we see mostly sunny to partly cloudy skies. Highs will continue to be in the mid 70s with lows in the mid to upper 40s.

Turning Warmer to Start Next Week

Monday of next week will feature more sunshine around with highs more into the mid to upper 70s. We look to sneak back into the low to mid 80s next Tuesday.
