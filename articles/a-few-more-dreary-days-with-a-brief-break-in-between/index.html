A few more dreary days with a brief break in between

TheTools · 28.11.2026 01:23 · source · permalink

OHIO — If you're reading this with a cup of something hot in your hands, you're already off to a great start, because it's a day to take it easy.

We've got one more round of the wet stuff before we get a break. Morning rain, snow and mixed precipitation will hang around through midday Friday, and chances are widespread early on, from the lakeshore all the way down to the Ohio River.

By the afternoon and early tonight, things turn hit-or-miss, with mostly rain showers and wet roads. It's still a sloppy day for shopping, and temperatures are very chilly, so this is the day for your coziest coat, a few warm layers and an umbrella you can toss in the back seat. Give yourself some extra time getting to the stores, take it slow in the parking lots, and remember that the deals will still be there when you arrive. A hot drink between stops never hurt anyone.

Now for the good news. Saturday is the day you've been waiting for. It starts off cloudy, but a little afternoon sunshine breaks through, and for the first time this weekend we get a dry day. Temperatures are seasonable, in the 40s to lower 50s, which is just right for a walk around the neighborhood, getting the holiday lights up, or catching up with family over leftovers. If you've got outdoor plans, this is your window, so soak up every minute of it.

Sunday starts out calm, with a mainly dry morning, so there's time for a slow breakfast, one last cup of coffee with the family, and a relaxed pack-up. But more showers are set to move through during the afternoon and evening, and heavy rain is possible late Sunday into Sunday night, right when many of us are heading home from a long holiday weekend. If you're traveling, our best advice is to get on the road earlier rather than later and try to beat the worst of it.

Expect slowdowns and slick roads, whether you're on I-71, I-75, I-70 or I-90. Keep your headlights on, leave plenty of room between you and the car ahead, ease off the brakes, and never drive through standing water. Before you head out, check road conditions and your flight status, charge your phone, and fill up the tank so you're ready for the road.

Whatever you've got planned this weekend, we hope it's full of good food, good company and a little bit of sunshine. Stay warm, stay safe, and we'll be here with you every step of the way.
