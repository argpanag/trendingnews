CENTER TOWNSIP, Ind. (WSBT) — Center Township Fire Rescue is preparing to add a new tanker truck to its fleet after members of the department’s equipment committee visited Spencer Manufacturing, Inc. on Friday to check on its progress.

The new tanker will hold 2,000 gallons of water and be built on an International chassis.

It will replace the department’s current 2,000-gallon tanker, a 1995 model built on a Ford chassis.

Fire officials say the new truck will be a valuable addition to the department’s equipment as it prepares to retire the older vehicle.
