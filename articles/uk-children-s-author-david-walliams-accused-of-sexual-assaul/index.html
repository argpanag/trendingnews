UK children’s author David Walliams accused of sexual assault – denies claims

TheTools · 10.11.2026 15:12 · source · permalink

Two media outlets said they’d talked to multiple women who accused Walliams of preying on them. One said she was assaulted when she was 15

British children’s author and comedian David Walliams used his fame to groom girls and young women for sex, two British news outlets reported on Tuesday.

ITV News and the Guardian newspaper said they spoke with multiple women who accused Walliams of preying on them.

One woman said he sexually assaulted her when she was 15.
