Green Flexibility starts first of six batteries in Lower Saxony

TheTools · 24.11.2026 17:15 · source · permalink

A 10 MW/25 MWh battery at Bad Bederkesa is the first of six that Green Flexibility plans in Lower Saxony under one connection agreement with the grid operator EWE NETZ.

Green Flexibility, a Germany-based developer and operator of large battery storage systems, has begun building a 10 MW/25 MWh battery at Bad Bederkesa in Lower Saxony. The site is in Geestland, in the Cuxhaven district, and operation is scheduled to begin in the second quarter of 2027. The Bad Bederkesa battery is the first of six projects that Green Flexibility is planning in the state. The six projects are expected to add about 120 MW of power and 480 MWh of storage, for an investment of around €150 million (~ $165.45 million). Each of them is to be covered by a single flexible connection agreement, which the company has concluded with the grid operator EWE NETZ. That agreement has been made for REGIOlink, a concept that Green Flexibility has developed. Under this concept, the batteries are to be run according to the needs of the regional grid, and not only according to power market prices.
