vivo V80 Review: ZEISS Portrait Photography Takes Centre Stage With a 7,200mAh Battery | TechNave

TheTools · 19.11.2026 09:34 · source · permalink

Smartphone cameras have become increasingly capable, but taking a portrait that genuinely stands out still requires more than just a high megapixel count. The right focal length, background separation and colour reproduction can make a significant difference, especially when capturing people in busy environments or challenging lighting conditions.

This is where the vivo V80 puts much of its focus. Developed around its ZEISS portrait photography capabilities, the smartphone features dedicated 85mm and 100mm Centre-Stage Portrait modes, a 50MP ZEISS Super Telephoto Camera and several AI-powered imaging tools designed to give users more creative freedom. Beyond photography, the vivo V80 also brings a distinctive Starwish Purple design, a 1.5K 144Hz display, a Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 chipset and a sizeable 7,200mAh battery.

So, what makes the vivo V80 worth considering? Here's a closer look at its main features.

 

One of the first things that stands out about the vivo V80 is its Starwish Purple colour option. Rather than relying on a plain finish, the rear panel features a 3D Stellar Design created using Laser Holographic Lithography. This gives the back cover a galaxy-inspired appearance, with its visual effect changing depending on how light hits the surface. It is a distinctive design choice for users who prefer their smartphones to have a little more personality. The vivo V80 also comes in Wander Green and Vast Gray for those who prefer different colour options.

Beyond the appearance, the phone features a Flagship One-piece Cold-Carved Glass back, a floating camera module and an aerospace-grade metal frame. The upgraded glass finish is designed to feel smooth in the hand while resisting dust buildup. Overall, the V80 combines a more expressive rear design with a premium-looking construction.

 

The biggest highlight of the vivo V80 is undoubtedly its camera system, particularly its ZEISS portrait capabilities. The vivo V80 features a 50MP ZEISS Super Telephoto Camera with a Sony IMX882 sensor, a 1/1.95-inch sensor size, OIS and 3x optical zoom. It also supports up to 100x zoom, giving users more flexibility when photographing subjects from a distance. Alongside this, the rear camera setup includes a 50MP ZEISS OIS Main Camera with a Sony LYTIA 700V sensor and an 8MP ZEISS Ultra Wide-Angle Camera.

For selfies and group photos, the front-facing camera is a 50MP ZEISS Group Selfie Camera with autofocus and a 92-degree wide-angle field of view. It also supports 0.8x–2x free zoom, allowing users to adjust their framing depending on whether they're taking a close-up selfie or fitting more people into the shot. Importantly, all cameras on the vivo V80 are designed to meet ZEISS optical standards.

 

What makes the camera experience more interesting is the dedicated ZEISS 85mm and 100mm Centre-Stage Portrait modes. The 85mm Portrait mode uses a ZEISS Sonnar-inspired look, with creamy background bokeh and a blue-and-yellow cinematic film style. It is recommended for three-quarter-length portraits, particularly when there are point light sources in the background.

Meanwhile, the 100mm Centre-Stage Portrait mode uses a ZEISS Planar-inspired look with dreamy bokeh and a softer, low-saturation platinum tone. It is designed for closer half-length portraits, allowing users to bring their subjects into focus while reducing distractions in the background. The longer focal length also makes it useful when photographing people in crowded environments. Instead of having to move close to the subject, users can shoot from further away and make the person stand out more prominently.

Both modes are accessible through the Camera app's Portrait mode, where users can select the desired focal length. These options give portrait enthusiasts more ways to experiment with framing and background effects, whether they're taking casual photos with friends, capturing travel moments or photographing performances and events.

 

The vivo V80 also supports 4K video recording at 60fps on both its front and rear cameras. This is useful for users who regularly record videos for social media, especially when capturing events, performances, gatherings or everyday moments with friends.

The higher frame rate allows videos to capture movement more smoothly, while the option to record using the front camera means users can also record themselves without switching to the rear camera. For the best results, vivo recommends using the 1x or 3x focal lengths when recording performances. It also advises avoiding direct stage lighting shining into the camera lens, as this may affect the footage.

 

Another interesting addition is CCD Flash Portrait, which aims to recreate the nostalgic appearance of photos taken with older digital cameras. This feature is particularly suited to night portraits, where poor lighting can sometimes result in darker faces, visible noise and less accurate skin tones.

According to vivo, the flash brightness has been increased by 150% to help produce clearer night portraits and more faithful colours. The CCD-style effect also brightens the subject while darkening the background, creating a retro-inspired appearance. Users can access it through Camera, Portrait Mode and Style, then select CCD Flash Portrait. The flash will turn on automatically. It is a fun alternative for those who enjoy experimenting with different photography styles, particularly when taking pictures at night-time gatherings, restaurants or parties.

 

Photography does not always end when the shutter button is pressed. Sometimes, an unwanted passerby appears in the background, the framing is not quite right or the lighting could use some improvement. The vivo V80 includes several AI-powered tools to help users refine their pictures directly on the phone.

Its AI Creative Camera can introduce artificial lighting effects into photographs, including Glow, Sunlight and Top Lighting. It also offers more than 30 styles across different creative directions, including travel portraits, stage photography, anime and scenery. For existing photos, the AI Retouch suite includes several useful editing options:

AI Erase: Removes unwanted objects, passersby and reflections from photos.

AI Image Expander: Extends the proportions of a photo by filling in additional space around the image.

AI Magic Move: Allows users to reposition, resize and adjust the angle of subjects.

AI UHD: Enhances image clarity and can improve blurry photographs.

There is also Color Palette, which allows users to adjust the colour temperature and tint of their photographs. Whether they prefer cooler blue-green tones, warmer colours or a nostalgic film-inspired appearance, they can experiment with different looks. These features provide more flexibility for users who enjoy editing and sharing photographs, without necessarily needing to open a separate editing application.

 

Beyond its cameras, the vivo V80 features a 6.59-inch 1.5K Eye Protection Display with a refresh rate of up to 144Hz. The higher refresh rate is designed to deliver smoother scrolling and a more fluid viewing experience, although the maximum refresh rate is supported only in selected scenarios. The display also supports up to 5,000 nits local peak brightness, helping improve visibility in bright environments. For nighttime use, it can go down to just 1 nit, providing a softer screen glow when using the phone in darker surroundings.

vivo also highlights its paper-like display experience and SGS Low Blue Light Certification as part of its eye protection features. Overall, the display is designed to balance sharp visuals, smooth interactions and more comfortable viewing across different lighting conditions.

 

Powering the vivo V80 is the Qualcomm Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 Mobile Platform, which is paired with features intended to support everyday multitasking, entertainment and gaming. In addition, one of its biggest hardware highlights is the 7,200mAh BlueVolt Battery, which uses fourth-generation silicon-anode technology to increase energy density without requiring a larger battery size.

According to vivo, the battery can support up to 39.5 hours of local video playback or 14 hours of navigation under its stated testing conditions. When the battery runs low, the phone supports 90W FlashCharge. vivo claims that it can reach 50% charge in approximately 30 minutes.

The V80 also supports bypass charging, which routes power directly to the phone's components instead of charging the battery when the device is being used while plugged in. This is particularly useful for gaming while charging, as it is designed to reduce battery heat and help maintain battery durability. For users who spend long hours watching videos, navigating or playing games, having a 7,200mAh battery should provide more flexibility between charging sessions.

 

The vivo V80 also features 3D Ultrasonic Fingerprint Scanning 2.0. Unlike conventional optical fingerprint sensors that rely on light, the ultrasonic system uses sound waves to map fingerprints in three dimensions. This allows it to recognise fingerprints even when users have wet, oily or smudged fingers.

vivo claims that screen-off fingerprint unlocking can take as little as 0.23 seconds. However, the company recommends using the original screen protector, as certain third-party tempered glass protectors may affect fingerprint recognition. The phone also comes with IP68 and IP69 dust and water resistance, as well as an SGS Overall Unit Drop Resistance Certification.

 

To recap, the vivo V80 places portrait photography at the heart of its smartphone experience, with its ZEISS 85mm and 100mm Centre-Stage Portrait modes giving users more creative options when capturing people. Its 50MP ZEISS Super Telephoto Camera, front and rear 4K 60fps video recording, CCD Flash Portrait and AI editing tools further expand its imaging capabilities.

But it is not just about photography. The 1.5K 144Hz display, Snapdragon 7 Gen 4 platform, 7,200mAh battery and 90W FlashCharge also make up an important part of the overall package. For users who enjoy taking portraits, experimenting with different photography styles and recording everyday moments, the vivo V80 offers a feature set worth exploring. And with its Starwish Purple finish, it certainly has a design that is hard to overlook.
