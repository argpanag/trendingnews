MPL: Aurora Gaming denies Smart Omega in marathon play-in series

TheTools · 22.10.2026 09:23 · source · permalink

Defending world champion Aurora Gaming avoided a major blunder after surviving the gritty Smart Omega, 3-2, in the MPL Philippines Season 18 play-ins on late Wednesday at the PhilSports Arena.

Aurora Gaming stared down an early exit that would have derailed its world title defense, but rallied to win the last two games of the best-of-five series and book a ticket to the next round.

The comeback also ended Smart Omega's world championship run, setting up Aurora for an upper bracket showdown against three-time reigning MPL champion Team Liquid.

Rookie gold laner Wilburt "Cull" Salinda made an impressive postseason debut for Smart Omega, shining on Obsidia and Bruno in Games 2 and 3 with back-to-back spotless KDAs of 7/0/4 and 8/0/3, respectively, to put the squad on the brink of an upset victory at 2-1.

But Aurora Gaming's own gold laner, Jan "Domeng" Delmundo, made sure he had an answer of his own.

Domeng answered Cull's breakout showing with an Obsidia masterclass of his own in Game 4, tallying three kills and five assists without a single death to keep Aurora Gaming's title defense afloat and force a winner-take-all Game 5.

Domeng put the finishing touches on Aurora's comeback, unleashing Claude's Battle Mirror Image to score a double kill late as Aurora committed to a tower lock despite Smart Omega's Lord marching down the top lane.

Up next for Aurora is a more formidable foe in Team Liquid, which wrapped up the regular season with a 12-2 record and is chasing an unprecedented four-peat in MPL Philippines history.

—JKC, GMA News
