Hayden Panettiere case closed by Greenville police | Here's what was determined

TheTools · 28.10.2026 17:56 · source · permalink

The Greenville Police Department's investigation into the death of Hayden Panettiere has been closed.

Panettiere found dead in Greenville

Panettiere, 36, died Aug. 16, 2026, in an apartment at Judson Mill Lofts on Easley Bridge Road in Greenville, South Carolina, according to the coroner's office and police.

The Greenville County Coroner's Office released the final report on her death last month.

It said, "The cause of death has been ruled as toxic effects of fentanyl, 4-ANPP, Alprazolam, Methocarbamol, and Quetiapine. The manner of death has been ruled an accident."

It also said, "No signs of trauma were discovered that would have contributed to the death."

Police close case

Greenville police said Wednesday that based on the coroner's determination and the investigation, Panettiere's death has been ruled accidental.

The department said it has concluded its investigation with no charges filed.

What's next?

As previously reported by WYFF News 4, the Drug Enforcement Administration is also assisting in the investigation.

In our previous reporting from Sept. 22, the DEA provided the following statement:

"When requested by state or local authorities, and when appropriate based on the circumstances, DEA may provide investigative or technical assistance in drug-related death investigations. These cases are complex and take time to develop. To protect the integrity of ongoing investigative work, DEA does not comment on specific investigations."

WYFF News 4 reached out to the DEA on Wednesday after Greenville Police Department announced its case was closed. We will update this story when they come back.
