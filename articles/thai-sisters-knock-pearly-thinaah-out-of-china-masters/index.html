Thai sisters knock Pearly-Thinaah out of China Masters

TheTools · 03.09.2026 07:18 · source · permalink

The world No 5 pair, who received a first-round bye, lost to the world No 43 Thai duo in three games in their second-round match.

The world No 5 Malaysians, who received a first-round bye, lost 16-21, 21-17, 19-21 in 67 minutes to the world No 43 pair in their second-round match at the Sports Centre Gymnasium in Shenzhen, China.

Pearly-Thinaah have struggled to regain their form since Pearly took a month-long break in June to recover from an injury. Their only title this year came at the Indonesian Masters in January.

A strong showing at the World Tour Super 750 event would have boosted their preparations for the lucrative World Tour Finals in December.

Meanwhile, Malaysia’s challenge in the women’s doubles ended when Noraqilah Maisarah Ramdan-Low Zi Yu also lost in the second round.

The world No 52 Malaysians were beaten by China’s 4th-ranked Jia Yi Fan-Zhang Shu Xian 21-13, 21-16.
