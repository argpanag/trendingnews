ZIPAIR Opens Fall 2027 Tokyo Bookings From LAX and SFO

TheTools · 24.11.2026 04:07 · source · permalink

ZIPAIR’s newly released LAX dates start September 1. October departures from Los Angeles move 35 minutes earlier and reach Narita at 14:10 the next day.

ZIPAIR has opened fall 2027 bookings between Tokyo Narita and Los Angeles for September 1 through October 30, and between Narita and San Francisco for October 1 through October 30. The airline’s November 19 timetable and ticket release (PDF) extends sales for both California routes. ZIPAIR plans one daily round trip on each, subject to approval by the relevant authorities.

Los Angeles travelers get a second change on October 1. ZIPAIR moves the departure from LAX 35 minutes earlier, from 11:00 to 10:25, while the Narita arrival remains 14:10 the next day. Book your room for the arrival date on the ticket. Our Tokyo neighborhood guide shows which areas have direct rail connections from Narita.

  • New LAX dates: September 1 through October 30, 2027.
  • New SFO dates: October 1 through October 30, 2027.
  • Los Angeles clock change: The local departure and arrival each move 35 minutes earlier October 1.
  • Japan arrival: The California departures reach Narita the next calendar day.
  • Schedule: Tickets on sale. Flights subject to relevant authority approval.

Los Angeles gets September dates and an earlier October departure

ZIPAIR lists ZG23 from Los Angeles at 11:00 in September, reaching Narita at 14:10 the following day. From October 1 through 30, the Los Angeles departure becomes 10:25. If you are driving to LAX or connecting on a separate booking, check the airport arrival plan against the earlier flight time before settling on a date.

In the opposite direction, ZG24 leaves Narita at 14:35 in both months. It reaches Los Angeles the same calendar day at 09:00 in September or 08:25 in October. Aviation Wire’s November 19 coverage reports the same two changes. Use the local arrival time when picking a ride or a separate onward flight.

San Francisco travelers land at Narita in the evening

The new San Francisco sale starts with October 1 departures. ZG25 leaves SFO at 16:50 and reaches Narita at 20:00 the next day. ZG26 leaves Narita at 21:30 and reaches San Francisco at 14:50 the same day. Those times apply to the newly released October dates in the airline timetable.

If you land at 20:00, leave a realistic margin for entry formalities and luggage before committing to your train or hotel check-in time. Compare the last workable connection on your travel date with ground transport to your exact address. Our four-day Tokyo itinerary can help travelers decide how much sightseeing to save for the next morning.

The same sale extends Bangkok and Seoul bookings

ZIPAIR added tickets on two Asian routes in the same published schedule. The daily Bangkok pair is newly on sale September 1 through October 30. ZG51 leaves Narita at 17:00 and lands at Suvarnabhumi at 21:40. ZG52 leaves Bangkok at 23:10 and reaches Narita the next morning at 07:30.

Seoul Incheon’s new booking dates run October 1 through 30. ZG45 leaves Narita at 08:55 and reaches Incheon at 11:25. ZG46 leaves Incheon at 12:55 and reaches Narita at 15:30. Both are same-day flights. If you buy an onward leg separately, check the first airline’s actual airport and the second airline’s check-in cutoff before treating the two flights as a connection.

Compare prices from the airport you will use

ZIPAIR’s release lists one-way starting fares from Narita in yen. A California departure needs a price for that origin and date. Checked luggage, additional carry-on weight above the published 7 kg allowance and meals can add to the fare. Use the new sale dates to compare the two California departures on the same travel day. Then check the actual Narita arrival date and the cost of getting to your hotel before you commit to prepaid arrangements.

Which fall 2027 dates just went on sale from Los Angeles?

ZIPAIR released September 1 through October 30, 2027 for the Los Angeles to Narita route.

When does the newly released San Francisco schedule begin?

The dates in this sale run October 1 through 30, 2027. Flights from San Francisco are scheduled to land at Narita at 20:00 the next day.

What time does the Los Angeles flight leave in October?

Beginning October 1, ZG23 is scheduled to leave LAX at 10:25 local time, 35 minutes earlier than in September.

Are these new ZIPAIR routes?

These are added ticket-sale dates on existing routes. ZIPAIR says the scheduled flights are subject to relevant authority approval.
