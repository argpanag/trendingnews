Police at North Island waterfall tourist spot after incident

TheTools · 16.11.2026 09:16 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

Police are at the scene of a water-related incident at a popular Waikato swimming spot.

A spokesperson said the incident happened at Owharoa Falls Scenic Reserve near Waikino.

First reports of the incident came in at 1.25pm.

Police were unable to give more details at this time.

Hato Hone St John sent an operations manager, ambulance and rapid response unit. A spokesperson directed further inquiries to police.

The picturesque waterfall is 200m from SH2 along the Karangahake Gorge.
