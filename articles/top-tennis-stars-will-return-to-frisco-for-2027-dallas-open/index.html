Top Tennis Stars Will Return To Frisco For 2027 Dallas Open

TheTools · 29.10.2026 21:42 · source · permalink

Frisco is gearing up for another week of big serves, fast rallies and some of the biggest names in American tennis. The Nexo Dallas Open announced that Ben Shelton, Taylor Fritz and Frances Tiafoe will compete in the 2027 tournament, bringing three of the sport’s most recognizable American players back to North Texas.

The ATP 500 event returns to Ford Center at The Star from Feb. 6-14, 2027, following a record-setting tournament earlier this year. Single-session tickets are now on sale, giving fans the option to catch a few matches or settle in for a full week of tennis.

Shelton has some unfinished business to attend to in Frisco, even if he already has a trophy waiting for him. The world No. 4 won the 2026 Dallas Open after defeating top-seeded Fritz in the championship match, which drew more than 6,000 fans to a sold-out Ford Center.

The recent US Open finalist will return in February with a title to defend and plenty of familiar faces in the stands.

“Dallas has become a really special stop for me,” said Shelton. “I’m excited to come back, see the fans and get another opportunity to compete at the Nexo Dallas Open and hopefully put on another great week of tennis.”

Shelton’s return also sets up the possibility of another showdown with Fritz, who will be looking to finish one spot higher this time around.

Fritz, currently ranked No. 13 in the world, is coming back after reaching the 2026 final. The American has become a regular contender on the ATP Tour, and his return gives fans another chance to see whether he can get past Shelton and claim the Dallas title.

Tiafoe, ranked No. 9, rounds out the trio of announced headliners. Known for his energetic playing style and big personality, he also reached the semifinals of the 2026 US Open, adding another strong Grand Slam showing to his résumé.

With all three players expected in Frisco, the tournament is bringing together a particularly notable group of American talent. Additional players for the 2027 field are expected to be announced later.

“After the success we experienced in 2026, bringing Ben, Taylor and Frances back to Dallas is a tremendous way to begin building the 2027 field,” said Peter Lebedevs, tournament director of the Nexo Dallas Open. “Ben and Taylor gave our fans an incredible championship match last year, and having both return, along with Frances, gives us another opportunity to showcase some of the best tennis in the world. We saw tremendous growth across the tournament in 2026, and we’re excited to continue that momentum.”

The tournament has plenty of reasons to build on its success. Attendance climbed 10% in 2026 compared with the previous year, while ticket sales rose 18%. The championship crowd was a sellout, and Kids Weekend brought in more young fans than any previous edition of the event.

The tournament also featured 12 of the top American players and competitors from 11 countries, with broadcasts reaching audiences in more than 180 countries.

Off the court, organizers introduced Nexo as the event’s first title partner, expanded premium hospitality options and upgraded the Fan Zone with daily live music and additional fan activities. Those additions helped make the tournament a destination for more than just the tennis itself.

Before the main tournament gets into full swing, fans can catch the All-American Classic presented by Baker Tilly on Feb. 6, at 8 p.m. The exhibition will feature Shelton and Alex Michelsen, with additional American players still to be announced. The event offers an early chance to see some of the country’s top talent share the court before the tournament continues through championship weekend.

Single-session tickets are available for the All-American Classic, early-round matches and the final days of competition.

The 2027 Nexo Dallas Open runs Feb. 6-14 at Ford Center at The Star in Frisco. For the full schedule and ticket information, visit dallasopen.com.
