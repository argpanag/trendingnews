Cardi B is the latest superstar to land on Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album. The Grammy-winning rapper surprised the Bardi Gang on Thursday night (Oct. 8) when she revealed that her brash new song, "Don't Chart," was a part of the highly anticipated video game's soundtrack.

"Run it up," Cardi B instructed fans on Instagram when announcing the track. "Don't Chart" is a playful cut and feels like the rapper was having fun in the studio, but still oozes her signature bold confidence that never wavers.

"Look at me now, Cardi a whole star/ I can name this song 'Don't Chart,' it's gon' chart," she brags about her Billboard chart success, which includes 66 Billboard Hot 100 entries — with five of them being No. 1s — to date.

The Bardi Gang got a kick out of Cardi subliminally dissing an opp with a lacerating bar. "You love my style, you love my taste/ I got a different Birkin bag for every bump in your face, b—h," she raps.

"Don't Chart" marks Cardi B's second single of 2026, which follows the arrival of "AH HA" in July. "AH HA" reached No. 25 on the Hot 100 and topped the Rhythmic Airplay chart.

The 33-year-old strongly hinted on her IG Story that she would be involved with Grand Theft Auto earlier when she posted the same cropped "Baddie" hoodie that the game's female protagonist, Lucia, wears in the game's trailer.

Cardi joins a star-studded cast on GTA: VI The Album, which includes Travis Scott, Morgan Wallen, Metro Boomin, Future, Yung Lean, Keith Richards, Rauw Alejandro, PinkPantheress and Fuerza Regida. The rest of the 34-track album arrives alongside GTA VI on Nov. 19.
