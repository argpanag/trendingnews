Welcome to the final match of the inaugural 2026 College Swim League season!!

The last two months have seen some exciting and close meets and some fast swimming, and tonight it all comes to a close as an overall champion will be crowned.

All four teams competing tonight have earned $25,000 for their school, and will be looking to also take home the overall title. Virginia is the favorite as the only undefeated team in the League, but NC State and Tennessee are not going down easy.
