Spertsyan Plays Full Match but Cannot Save Al-Ahli from Defeat

TheTools · 11.09.2026 18:19 · source · permalink
  • Sept. 9, 2026, 2:28 p.m.
  • Author: Vahe Hakobyan
  • Sportaran

Armenia national team captain Eduard Spertsyan featured in the sixth round of the Saudi Arabian championship as his Al-Ahli side faced Al-Qadsiah away from home.

The match produced five goals and ended in a 3-2 defeat for the Armenian midfielder's team.

Spertsyan was named in Al-Ahli's starting lineup and played the entire 90 minutes. Following the match, statistical platform SofaScore gave the 26-year-old a rating of 6.1.

During the game, Spertsyan registered two shots and demonstrated impressive passing accuracy. The Armenian midfielder completed 51 passes with a 95% success rate. He also completed two of his three dribbling attempts.

Defensively, Spertsyan recorded one tackle and two ball recoveries. He won five of his ten duels during the match.

Despite the Armenia captain's efforts, Al-Ahli were unable to secure a positive result. After six rounds, the team sits seventh in the Saudi Arabian league table.
