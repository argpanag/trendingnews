Toronto man scores second big lottery win | Toronto

TheTools · 20.10.2026 17:01 · source · permalink

By Ryan Rocca

Published October 20, 2026 at 1:01 pm

A Toronto man has scored his second big lottery win.

The Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation said Scarborough resident Junior H. took home a major $250,000 prize in the Sept. 5 Lottario draw.

Junior, who is 68-years-old, said he has been playing the lottery with the OLG each week for over 20 years.

“I was at the store when I checked my ticket and I was absolutely stunned,” Junior said.

“I couldn’t believe I actually won. I was overwhelmed with excitement – my whole world felt like it was spinning.”

Since discovering the win, Junior said he feels “like a kid in a candy store.”

“I can’t even begin to describe the amount of joy this win has brought me. It’s an out of body experience,” he said.

As mentioned, it’s not Junior’s first big win, either.

The OLG said he was previously part of a lottery group that won $100,000, with his share being $20,000.

This time, however, the money is all his.

He said he plans to put his win towards a down payment on a house.

Junior H. won $250,000 in the Sept. 5 Lottario draw. (Handout / OLG)

The winning ticket was purchased in Scarborough at Becker’s Convenience Store on Finch Avenue.

Lottario draws happen every Saturday and the game costs $1 per play.

News of Junior’s big win comes as Canadians prepare for a historic Lotto Max draw Tuesday night.

A massive $90 million jackpot is up for grabs and the OLG said there will also be an estimated 20 $1 million maxmillion prizes that could be won, along with 90 $100,000 maxplus prizes.
