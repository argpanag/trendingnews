Sofia Vergara Flaunts Her Curves in Plunging Nadine Merabi Corset Top

TheTools · 02.09.2026 09:28 · source · permalink

Sofia Vergara brought her signature glamour to the America’s Got Talent set, stepping out in a romantic floral look that perfectly highlighted her famous curves. The actress and longtime judge shared several glimpses of the outfit on Instagram, including backstage mirror selfies and close-up shots from the judges’ table.

Sofia Vergara shows off her famous curves in plunging Nadine Merabi corset top for ‘AGT’

View this post on Instagram

Vergara wore what appeared to be a Nadine Merabi ensemble, with further tailoring by Laura Basci. The standout piece was a structured corset top covered in red and pink roses and green leaves against a cream backdrop.

Its strapless construction featured a plunging sweetheart neckline that framed her bust, while the sculpted cups and fitted bodice cinched her waist. The cropped hem also left a subtle strip of her midriff exposed.

She coordinated the floral plunging corset top with matching floral trousers. The pants continued the same statement rose print and sat high on her hips before falling into a long, slightly flared silhouette. The head-to-toe print gave the two-piece set a polished finish while keeping the focus firmly on its curve-hugging shape.

Vergara also piled on the jewelry for her latest AGT appearance. Her Instagram post tagged Dena Kemp, Anabela Chan, and Anayah Jewelry. She accessorized with sparkling drop earrings, an eye-catching diamond-style bangle, an evil eye bracelet, and multiple statement rings. Red gemstones on her fingers perfectly picked up the crimson roses scattered across her outfit.

The actress kept her beauty look classic, wearing her long brunette hair sleek, straight, and center-parted. Defined lashes, rosy cheeks, bronzed skin, and a nude-pink lip completed the glam.

Vergara later showed off the full ensemble backstage with both arms raised before heading to the judges’ table. She also reminded fans to tune in, writing, “See you mañana!! America dont forget to vote!” alongside an AGT tag.
