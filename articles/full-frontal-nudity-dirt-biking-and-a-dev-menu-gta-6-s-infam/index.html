Full-frontal nudity, dirt-biking, and a dev menu: GTA 6's infamous leaker is back with the most X-rated look at the game yet

TheTools · 08.10.2026 12:09 · source · permalink

Naked ambition.

It looks like the GTA 6 leaker, 'CyberLeek' is back - and this time the infamous leaker has posted a 25-minute video of footage from Rockstar's upcoming game that even features nude scenes, including a fully naked Jason, and a topless Lucia. Eurogamer has seen the video, and it appears to be legitimate - and, as is now traditional, has 'CyberLeek's' emblem overlaid on top of the footage.

Note: obviously, there are some mild spoilers pertaining to what you might do in GTA 6 below.

Most of the new video takes place around the Thrillbilly Mud Club, and follows the protagonist duo as they prepare for a dirt-biking event - buying new clothes and participating in the off-road driving tournament. Jason wanders around a garage and chooses which bike he'd like to rent to take part in the festivities.

Then, for over 10 minutes of the footage, Jason is completely naked. 'CyberLeek' shares footage of the character riding around on a dirtbike fully nude, with his penis out and seemingly reacting realistically to the world with its own dedicated physics. It's worth noting at this point that whoever is playing the game seems to have access to a dev menu - they spawn in a tank out of thin air - so it's possible that nudity in this way is not available in-game, and has rather been allowed in this dev version of the game. My instinct is that you will be able to be nude in-game, though; it's rated 18+ and it's a Grand Theft Auto game, the series has previous, and a ratings board has confirmed there'll be 'full on sex scenes'.

Towards the end of the footage, we see Lucia in a thong and topless, preparing for a base dive (it looks like the same one that was initially shown in the Netflix reveal) - albeit this time it's night time, and she is mostly naked.

The video ends with the apparent leaker trying to petition viewers for money and teasing 'an expensive project' that 'needs a lot of hardware'. It reiterates that the leaker is not affiliated with Rockstar. There is no crypto begging in this video, though, unlike in other videos. Rockstar has previously issued statements regarding the leaker, noting that the leaks are "heartbreaking" and that "this is obviously not how we intended for you to see the game after all this time." Take-Two, Rockstar's parent company, has been hunting for the person behind the leaks, and has tried to subpoena Discord for information pertaining to the leaker's identity.

'CyberLeek' went to ground for a while following the Netflix reveal, with today's post taking many by surprise. Will we see more from the leaker in the coming days? It's anybody's guess at this point.
