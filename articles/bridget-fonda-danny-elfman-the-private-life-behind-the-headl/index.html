Bridget was born into an acting dynasty that includes her grandfather Henry Fonda and her aunt Jane Fonda, yet she opted not to pursue a long-term career in front of the camera.

Bridget Fonda Danny Elfman: Meeting, marriage and family

The couple met in 1998 on the set of A Simple Plan and later exchanged vows in 2003 at an intimate candlelit ceremony in Los Angeles, where Bridget was walked down the aisle by her father, Peter. Two years after the wedding they welcomed a son, Oliver, and Bridget has not returned to acting since his birth. Bridget Fonda Danny Elfman’s household also includes Danny’s two older children from a previous marriage, Mali and Lola, making for a blended family centered on privacy and home life.

Bridget Fonda Danny Elfman have rarely appeared together in public; in May 2025 they made news when photographers captured them returning to their California home, the first images of the pair together in 16 years. When asked whether she would like to resume her acting career, she told a photographer, “I don’t think so, it’s too nice being a civilian.”

Career highlights: from acting pedigree to film scoring

Before her retreat from Hollywood, Bridget built a respected résumé that included stage and screen roles across the 1980s and 1990s. Notable credits in her filmography include Scandal (1989), in which she portrayed the real-life model and showgirl Mandy Rice-Davies; The Godfather III (1990), as journalist Grace Hamilton opposite Andy García and Al Pacino; and Single White Female (1992), playing Allison opposite a menacing roommate. Bridget also earned critical attention for the television film In the Gloaming (1997), which dealt with a family confronting AIDS and featured an ensemble cast including Whoopi Goldberg, Glenn Close and David Strathairn. In A Simple Plan (1998) she played Sarah, a pregnant wife in a thriller centered on a crashed plane that contained $4.4 million in cash.

Danny Elfman, in contrast, has remained a prominent figure in the entertainment world as a composer and performer. Born and raised in Los Angeles, he first came to notice as the singer of the band Oingo Boingo before leaving to concentrate on film scoring in 1995. His long-running creative relationship with director Tim Burton produced signature scores for Edward Scissorhands, Batman Returns and The Nightmare Before Christmas, and he later provided music for the Netflix series Wednesday. Danny is also credited with creating The Simpsons theme and music for Desperate Housewives, and his work has garnered nominations across Grammys, Oscars and Emmys.

Danny continues to perform publicly at times: he took the stage at Coachella in 2022 and in interviews has reflected on the risk and exhilaration of live performance. He said, “You know, my wife once said to me, and she really summed it up properly, she said, ‘You’re on a high wire without a safety net every time you go out there. I think that’s really true. Part of me must love the fact that there’s no net. If I [expletive] up, it’s really gonna be a nasty [expletive]. When I’m on stage for whatever kind of show, there’s a million reasons why things could [expletive] up; I make my shows, it seems, as difficult as possible. And I think that must be something I’m hooked on.'”

Despite their different professional paths—Bridget leaving acting to live a quieter life and Danny maintaining a visible creative career—the couple have built a low-key family existence in Los Angeles focused on their son, Oliver, and on staying shielded from relentless celebrity scrutiny. Bridget Fonda Danny Elfman remain a rare example of two high-profile talents choosing privacy and domestic stability over ongoing public exposure.
