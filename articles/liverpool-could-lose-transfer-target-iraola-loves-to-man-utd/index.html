Liverpool could lose transfer 'target' Iraola loves to Man Utd after admission

TheTools · 15.11.2026 19:13 · source · permalink

Andoni Iraola is a big fan of his former Bournemouth winger Rayan(Image: Getty Images)

Liverpool may have been handed a transfer blow after one potential target revealed himself to be a Manchester United fan.

Attention will be turning to the January transfer window soon, with just a month-and-a-half to go until teams get the chance to make more signings. Liverpool’s returning sporting director Julian Ward could be among those who are kept busy in the New Year.

Reds head coach Andoni Iraola will be relatively pleased with the start made by his side this season, with Liverpool sitting fourth after 10 games while also being in the quarter-finals of the Carabao Cup and well-placed in the Champions League. He will know there are some areas that need improving as well though.

A lot has been made of the balance of the Reds’ squad this season. Iraola has a surplus of wingers naturally suited to the left-hand side, but no clear options on the right, meaning the likes of Bradley Barcola and Rio Ngumoha have had to double up on their positions.

It’s no surprise then that Liverpool continues to be linked with right-wingers, and one of his former Bournemouth stars is among the most prominent names being touted.

Rayan name cropped up a lot during the summer, and speculation continues over his future, having impressed since making the move to the Premier League in January. The 20-year-old though might have his eye trained elsewhere though.

Rayan has admitted to being a Manchester United fan despite being linked with Liverpool

In an interview with ESPN Brasil alongside Bournemouth teammate Evanilson, Rayan explained that he’s actually a fan of Liverpool’s biggest rival.

“I was even talking to the people at home, my wife and the people who are with me, and I told them I’ve always supported Manchester United here in England,” Rayan said when discussing Bournemouth’s 3-0 win at Old Trafford in October.

“Then I said, ‘Wow, we’ve beaten Manchester United, the team I’ve always supported.'”

When asked why he supported United, he responded: “Because of Cristiano Ronaldo.”

That admission may come as a blow to Liverpool if there is any truth to the speculation surrounding a move to Anfield for Rayan. Iraola is of course a big fan of the player, having managed him for the second half of last season and developed him to a point where he made his international debut this year and earned a spot in Brazil’s squad at the World Cup.

“I think he comes, I said it before, from a big club in Brazil,” Iraola said of Rayan in March. "He's used to pressure, he's used to be very analyzed. And I think his integration has been really good.

“Obviously, it's going to be better when he understands the language, when he knows better the teammates, when we also know him better so we can put him in the best positions.

“But I think from, let's say, the first two months he has been here have been really good.”
