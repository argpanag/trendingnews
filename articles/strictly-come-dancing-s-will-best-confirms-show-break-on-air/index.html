Strictly Come Dancing's Will Best confirms show break on air 'we'll miss you'

TheTools · 30.09.2026 15:18 · source · permalink

Strictly Come Dancing star Will Best has made a significant career announcement. The 41-year-old presenter is currently strutting his stuff in the Strictly ballroom with professional partner Dianne Buswell.

He also juggles hosting weekly evictions from the Big Brother house alongside co-star AJ Odudu, and presents the Hits Radio Breakfast Show with Fleur East and James Barr. However, due to his packed schedule, it has now been announced that Will will be taking a hiatus from radio duties while Strictly and Big Brother are being broadcast.

The news was shared by former Strictly: It Takes Two host Fleur during Wednesday's (September 30) live Breakfast Show, which marked Will's last day for now. "It's a sad day for us in the studio this morning," she began, reports Wales Online.

Will is currently competing in Strictly Come Dancing(Image: BBC)

"Now, we all know right now our very own Will Best is the busiest man in show business. Hits Radio Breakfast, Big Brother, Strictly Come Dancing." Will then noted: "I'm doing it all!"

Fleur added: "Will, we're going to give you a little break... A gift from us to you." Will responded: "Oh, that's really sweet, thanks guys! [I'm going to] polish up the dancing shoes, get the twinkly toes ready to go. And that means that I can represent you guys to the best of my ability."

Fleur then disclosed that her co-host is expected to make his return to the morning radio show in early November, so fans won't have to wait too long. "Can we speak on the phone every Monday?" Will then enquired.

"I can try and speak to you on a Friday to tell you how I think it's going to go... And then on a Monday to tell you what went wrong!" Fleur admitted that it was an "emotional time", before encouraging viewers to cast their votes for Will during his stint in the Strictly ballroom.

"We'll keep the campaign going on your behalf," James assured, with Fleur wrapping up: "We're all behind you." A snippet of the chat was swiftly uploaded to Instagram, where fans flooded the comments section with messages of support.

"You shall be missed!!!" one person penned. Another chimed in: "Vote Will!!! Enjoy a well deserved break mate."

Will and his partner Dianne Buswell performed a Samba in week one(Image: BBC/Guy Levy)

A third commented: "I'm so glad he's not gonna be getting up at 5am. I was on the verge of a second hand nervous breakdown on his behalf. The busiest man in showbiz needs some sleep. VOTE WILL."

A fourth fan echoed these sentiments, saying: "We'll miss you Will." Another follower remarked: "Oh man! ! Good luck on @bbcstrictly but hurry back @iamwillbest you will be missed first thing in the morning!!"

Will was tasked with opening the first week of Strictly 2026, as all 15 new celebrities made their dance floor debut. Will and Dianne performed a Samba to Rock DJ by Robbie Williams, earning a respectable 18 points from the judges.
