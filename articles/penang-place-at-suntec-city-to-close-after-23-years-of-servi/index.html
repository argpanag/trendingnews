Penang Place at Suntec City to close after 23 years of serving buffets under $50

TheTools · 20.11.2026 04:59 · source · permalink

If Penang Place is one of your go-to spots for Malaysian fare, you might want to drop by soon.

After 23 years in business, the buffet restaurant is set to close as its lease at Suntec City ends on March 31, 2027, the owners announced in a Facebook post on Thursday (Nov 19). 

"We are deeply grateful for your love and support throughout the years. It has been our joy and privilege to serve you and your families," they wrote. 

However, it appears it may not necessarily be the end of the road for the family-run restaurant. 

"We remain open to opportunities that will allow the Penang Place legacy to continue," the owners said, while welcoming those keen on taking over the business to reach out. 

In an update in the comments section, the owners shared that they are currently in discussion with interested parties. 

The announcement quickly drew the attention of netizens, with several taking to the comments section to show their support. 

"Please update us on where you are going. Really enjoy your food, especially the buffet!" one wrote, while another said: "Thank you too! I enjoy the food and atmosphere in Penang Place. I had lunch there just a few weeks ago. Shall visit again before you guys move on." 

First opened in 2003 at International Business Park in Jurong East, Penang Place is a buffet restaurant serving Penang fare such as Assam Laksa, Penang Char Kway Teow, Hokkien Mee Noodle Soup and more.

[embed]https://www.instagram.com/p/C2PhQiOyFka/[/embed]

It was founded by Malaysia-born Paul Ooi and his wife Hannah after the former was retrenched from his job at a Fortune 500 company in 2002. He had worked there for 21 years, he shared in a 2019 interview with Salt&Light.

In 2011, the restaurant moved to Fusionopolis, before relocating to its current location at Suntec City in 2017.

Buffet prices at Penang Place start from $42.89 for adults, $21.50 for children aged five to 10 and $34.31 for senior citizens aged 60 and above.

An ala carte menu is also available, with items such as Ayam Percik with Butterfly Pea Rice ($18.50), Penang Fruits Rojak ($10.90) and Fried Rice with Salted Egg Fried Chicken ($14.90).

AsiaOne has reached out to Penang Place for more information. 

Address: 3 Temasek Boulevard, Suntec City, #02-314/315/316, 038983
Opening hours: 11.30am to 3pm, 6pm to 10pm (Monday to Saturday), 11.30am to 3pm, 5.30pm to 10pm (Sunday)
