Taylor Swift announces she's releasing a new song

TheTools · 22.09.2026 18:30 · source · permalink

Taylor Swift has a new single coming out — and fans won't have to wait long for it.

The singer posted on Instagram that the single, called "Patient Zero," will be released Sept. 25.

It's her first new song since the lighthearted, nostalgic "I Knew It, I Knew You," a country track written for "Toy Story 5" and released in June. Before that, her most recent releases had been remixes from her 2025 album "The Tortured Poets Department" and double album addition "The Anthology."

Fans had been speculating that new music might be coming after Swift appeared to leave her hallmark cryptic clues in a "Law and Order" sketch with Mariska Hargitay taped for the Emmys.
