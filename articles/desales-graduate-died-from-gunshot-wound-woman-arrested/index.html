DeSales graduate died from gunshot wound; woman arrested

TheTools · 15.09.2026 16:24 · source · permalink

New details have emerged in the death of a recent DeSales High School graduate.

The Fayette County coroner says Brandon Williams died on last Tuesday, Sept. 8, after being shot in the head.

A press release from the coroner indicated the shooting took place at a home in Lexington two days before Williams died at UK Hospital.

Trinity Buckner, 18, was arrested on assault and wanton endangerment charges the night of the shooting. Those charges could be upgraded.

According to her arrest citation, she told police the shooting was accidental, but a witness told police the shooting was intentional.

The case remains under investigation.

Williams graduated from DeSales in 2025, where he was a standout athlete in football, basketball and track.

WLKY spoke to Williams' former football coach, Mike Jackson, on Monday.

He described Williams as "one of a kind."

"Some kids are just a little, little special and the kind of personality they have and the kind of demeanor and the way they draw other kids to them and they can inspire other people. And Brandon had that," Jackson said.
