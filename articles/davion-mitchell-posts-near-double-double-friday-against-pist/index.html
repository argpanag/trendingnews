Davion Mitchell posts near double-double Friday against Pistons

TheTools · 24.10.2026 14:23 · source · permalink

by Chris Schommer | Heat Correspondent | Sat, Oct 24th 2026, 10:23am EDT

Davion Mitchell posted a near double-double for the Heat Friday night, dishing out five assists and scoring 18 points (6-14 FG, 3-9 3PT, 3-5 FT) while also grabbing two rebounds and blocking two shots in a 128-111 loss to the Pistons.

Fantasy Impact:

Mitchell has been steady on the offensive end of the floor to begin the season, finishing with five or more assists and 11 or more points in each of the first two contests. The guard will look to take advantage of an increased role in the starting backcourt for head coach Erik Spoelstra with the departure of Tyler Herro, averaging 14.5 points and 8.0 assists per game while posting a usage rate of 18.3% so far this season.
