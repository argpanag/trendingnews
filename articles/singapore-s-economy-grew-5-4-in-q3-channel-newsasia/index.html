Singapore’s economy grew 5.4% in Q3 — Channel NewsAsia

TheTools · 14.10.2026 03:05 · source · permalink

Singapore’s economy grew by 5.4% in the third quarter of 2026, down from 6.2% in the previous quarter. The Ministry of Energy, Trade and Industry (METI) released these preliminary estimates on October 14, Channel NewsAsia reports.

GDP dynamics

On a seasonally adjusted quarter-on-quarter basis, the economy grew by 1.1%. In the second quarter, this figure stood at 1.5%.

The preliminary estimates are largely based on statistics for the first two months of the quarter — July and August. The ministry noted that these data are an early indicator and may be revised after additional information becomes available.

Manufacturing and construction

The manufacturing sector grew by 7.5% in the third quarter, while its growth reached 12.9% in the second quarter. Expanded output in electronics, precision engineering, transport engineering and general manufacturing outweighed declines in the chemicals and biomedical industries.

At the same time, on a seasonally adjusted quarter-on-quarter basis, the manufacturing sector contracted by 0.9% after growing by 6.3% in the second quarter. The construction sector grew by 8.8% after 6.1% a quarter earlier, as work volumes increased in both the public and private sectors. On a quarter-on-quarter basis, construction grew by 3.4% after contracting by 2.7% in the previous quarter.
