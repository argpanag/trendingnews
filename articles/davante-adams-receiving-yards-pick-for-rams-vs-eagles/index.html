Davante Adams Receiving Yards Pick for Rams vs. Eagles

TheTools · 04.10.2026 16:00 · source · permalink

Published: October 4, 2026

By: Alex Hunter

The Rams are visiting the Eagles for a Week 4 matchup Sunday. On DraftKings Sportsbook, Los Angeles is favored by 3.5 points and this game carries a total of 42.5 points.

Let’s dive into the top Davante Adams prop bet for Sunday’s matchup.

Davante Adams Best Receiving Yards Prop Bet vs. Eagles

Davante Adams U 67.5 Receiving Yards (-112)

Adams has been on a tear, but he should come back down to earth Sunday with Puka Nacua (hip) returning. Adams has exploded for 195 and 137 yards the last two weeks with Nauca sidelined, but with the receiver active in Week 1, Adams only caught 3-of-6 targets for 26 yards vs. the 49ers, despite Matthew Stafford throwing the ball 55 times.

Nacua is one of the most dominant target earners in the NFL and he should resume his role as Stafford’s main pass catcher. Nacua and Adams have played 17 games together over the last two seasons and in these games, Adams is averaging 56.8 yards per game, compared to 122.3 yards per game in the three Adams has played without Nacua.

Adams’ target share drops from 26.1% without Nacua to 22.7% with Nacua, and Adams has fallen short of 67.5 yards in 11 of the 17 games he has played alongside Nacua. This matchup vs. the Eagles isn’t appealing, either.

This season, 80.2% of Adams’ routes have come out wide and Philadelphia is giving up the seventh-fewest yards to players who line up outside (240). Surpassing 67.5 yards should be a challenge for Adams in this spot and he is a smart bet for this under.
