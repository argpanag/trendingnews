Zack Gelof steals base in win over Mariners

TheTools · 06.09.2026 14:44 · source · permalink

by Chris Schommer | Athletics Correspondent | Sun, Sep 6th 2026, 10:44am EDT

Zack Gelof went 1-for-4 in a win over the Mariners with two runs scored and one stolen base.

Fantasy Impact:

Gelof has heated back up of late, reaching base in eight of his last nine contests. The 26-year-old remains one of the more stable options in an injury-ravaged Athletics lineup for manager Mark Kotsay, slashing .260 with 17 home runs, 42 RBI, 63 runs scored, and 12 stolen bases through 92 games.
