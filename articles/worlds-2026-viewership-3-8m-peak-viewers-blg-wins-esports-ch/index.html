Worlds 2026 Viewership: 3.8M Peak Viewers, BLG Wins | Esports Charts

TheTools · 15.11.2026 01:40 · source · permalink

Table of content:

    The 2026 League of Legends World Championship has concluded in Brooklyn, New York, with Bilibili Gaming defeating Gen.G 3:1 in the grand final at Barclays Center. The deciding series attracted 3.8 million Peak Viewers (excluding platforms in mainland China), bringing another edition of one of esports' biggest annual tournaments to a close.

    How Worlds 2026 compares to previous championships

    According to preliminary Esports Charts data, Worlds 2026 generated over 129 million Hours Watched, representing about a 3% decrease compared with the 2025 edition. Average Viewers reached 1.2 million, down 19% year-over-year. Notably, the 2025 tournament featured just under 89 hours of airtime, while this year's edition ran for well over 100 hours.

    In terms of peak viewership, Worlds 2026 fell 44% short of its predecessor. Last year's grand final between T1 and KT Rolster reached 6.75 million concurrent viewers, the second-highest figure ever recorded in esports outside mainland China.

    Several factors have influenced viewership, including the expanded Play-In stage and the tournament's return to North America, where match times were less convenient for much of the Asian audience. Another factor was the absence of T1, one of esports' biggest audience draws, from the grand final after winning three consecutive world titles.

    For the first time since 2021, Worlds will not be the year's most popular esports tournament by Peak Viewers. The tournament had topped the annual ranking for four consecutive years, but Worlds 2026 fell short of the 5.68 million viewers recorded by the M7 World Championship, the world championship for mobile MOBA Mobile Legends: Bang Bang.

    The last time another tournament outperformed Worlds was in 2021, when Free Fire World Series Singapore attracted 5.42 million Peak Viewers, approximately 1.4 million more than Worlds that year.
