AFC Fylde sign goalkeeper George Pickford on loan from Everton

TheTools · 25.10.2026 13:08 · source · permalink
  • Published
    24 October 2026

AFC Fylde have signed goalkeeper George Pickford on a short-term loan deal from Everton.

The 19-year-old has featured for Everton's U-18 and U-21 sides and appeared for Southport on loan during the second half of last season.
