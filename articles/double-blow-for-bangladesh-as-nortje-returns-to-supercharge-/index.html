Express fast bowler Anrich Nortje injects raw pace into what stand-in captain Aiden Markram describes as a “fired up” Proteas bowling attack for the second Test against Bangladesh, which gets underway at SuperSport Park on Monday.

ALSO READ: Bitter sweet triumph as Bavuma ruled out for second test

Addressing the media on Sunday, Markram confirmed two changes to the South African XI that cruised to a nine-wicket victory in the Johannesburg series opener. Nortje replaces medium-pacer Dane Paterson, while top-order batter Tony de Zorzi steps in for injured skipper Temba Bavuma.

While Bangladesh posted an impressive 400 in their first innings at the Wanderers, Markram notes that more than half of those runs came via a record 206, sixth-wicket stand between centurions Towhid Hridoy and Litton Das.

“Those are two big wickets for us and we didn’t get them (cheaply) in the first game. The bowlers are quite fired up to get one back in the second game,” Markram declareD.

Double injury blow rocks Bangladesh

While the hosts welcome back firepower, Bangladesh faces a major selection headache after losing two vital pillars of its squad to injury.

Veteran batter Mushfiqur Rahim, who at 39 boasts 108 Test caps, sits out after scans revealed inflammation following left groin pain sustained during the first Test.

Meanwhile, right-arm seamer Hasan Mahmud, Bangladesh’s standout bowler in Johannesburg with four wickets, misses not only the Centurion clash but the remainder of the white-ball tour.
