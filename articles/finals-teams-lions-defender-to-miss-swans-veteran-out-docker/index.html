FINALS TEAMS: Lions defender to miss, Swans veteran out, Dockers play it safe

TheTools · 18.09.2026 10:53 · source · permalink

The teams are in for the preliminary finals on Friday and Saturday

Brought to you by

BRISBANE will be without important defender Noah Answerth in its preliminary final clash with Hawthorn, while Sydney has lost veteran Harry Cunningham to a calf injury for its prelim against Fremantle. 

The Lions, Hawks and Dockers will all take unchanged line-ups into their preliminary finals but Brisbane won't have Answerth (ankle) to help combat superstar Hawthorn goalsneak Nick Watson in Saturday's game at the MCG.

Sydney has made two forced changes to the team that thrashed the Lions by 57 points at the SCG a fortnight ago, both through injury. Cunningham suffered a calf strain at training this week, while captain Callum Mills will miss most of the next 12 months after rupturing his ACL against Brisbane. 

Joel Peterson, Nathan Schmook and Emily Patterson bring you the latest news in Footy Feed

Key defender Lewis Melican replaces Mills in the backline, while winger Justin McInerney will play his first game since round 13 after recovering from a significant hamstring injury. 

Swans coach Dean Cox has opted not to bring in key forward Joel Amartey, who is building fitness after an Achilles injury sustained in round 16. 

The only selection query at Fremantle surrounded key defender Brennan Cox, who is on the road back from a calf injury, but he will play in the WAFL Grand Final for Peel Thunder instead. 

Three-time premiership captain Michael Voss joins Matthew Lloyd preview a blockbuster weekend of Preliminary Finals from all angles.
