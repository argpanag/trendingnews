Orlando weather: Cool air moves in, sunshine takes over through the weekend

TheTools · 26.11.2026 23:40 · source · permalink

Orlando AM Weather Forecast: Cool, breezy Thanksgiving

Expect a cooler and windy Thanksgiving for Central Florida. Highs will reach the mid-60s to lower 70s. FOX 35 Storm Team meteorologist Brooks Garner takes a look at the forecast. 

ORLANDO, Fla. - After a very pleasant day, winds will still be breezy throughout the rest of your Thanksgiving evening and overnight. Winds will come in from the west at 15 to 20 mph and gusts around 30 mph. 

With the cooler air moving in behind the front and clear skies on Thursday night, temperatures will drop quickly Thursday evening, making for a chilly start to Black Friday with temps outside Northwestern locales dropping into the low 40s.

Black Friday will be cool all day long with clear and breezy conditions. Friday morning, temperatures will range from the upper 40s to the mid 50s and top off in the low 60s.

River & coastal flooding

What to expect:

All five monitored river forecast points along the St. Johns River in east Central Florida remained in Major Flood Stage, including Cocoa, Geneva, Sanford, DeLand and Astor.

All five are forecast to remain in Major Flood Stage for at least another week. Today’s rainfall could contribute to additional rises across the river basin, prolonging flooding impacts for communities already dealing with high water.

Rivers have reached a new record high and will continue to stay high until further notice. That is why flood warnings are in effect along St. johns until further notice. We will start to see a slow retreat from the river heights, but it is certainly going to take a while for this water to go down.

On top of all the flooding we got from intense rain, the King Tides from the Supermoon on Monday will also pose the threat of flooding throughout the rest of the holiday week.

The combination of increasing winds, building seas and elevated astronomical tides will create hazardous conditions along the Atlantic coast for Thanksgiving.
A Coastal Flood Advisory is in effect for Flagler County with minor coastal flooding possible around high tide through tomorrow afternoon. 

Rough surf and a high risk of rip currents will continue through at least Thursday. Offshore seas will build to 6 to 10 feet today, with hazardous boating conditions continuing through Thanksgiving.

STAYING DRY THIS WEEKEND
High pressure builds over Florida Friday and Saturday, providing a welcome stretch of dry weather with plenty of sunshine and significantly lower humidity. Afternoon highs this weekend will generally be in the mid to upper 60s. 

Winds will gradually diminish, with light winds expected Saturday as high pressure settles overhead. Sunday clouds will work their way back across Florida.

NEXT RAIN CHANCE
Our dry stretch will be relatively short-lived. Our next chance of rain works its way in late Sunday night through Tuesday. Confidence remains low regarding thunderstorm intensity and potential rainfall totals. 

Given the saturated ground and ongoing major river flooding, we’ll be paying particularly close attention to how this system develops over the holiday weekend.
Drier conditions are expected to return Wednesday as the front moves south of Central Florida.
