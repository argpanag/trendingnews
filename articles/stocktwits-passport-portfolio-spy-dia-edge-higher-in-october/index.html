US Markets Vs Asia Markets

The SPDR S&P 500 ETF Trust (SPY) and the SPDR Dow Jones Industrial Average ETF (DIA) are headed higher this month, holding strong amid a massive U.S. tech selloff.

DIA leads, up nearly 3% so far, while SPY is set to close about 1% higher.

Meanwhile, the Invesco QQQ Trust (QQQ), which tracks the Nasdaq-100, is poised to end the month lower as investors grow wary of the sustainability of returns on massive AI infrastructure investments.

Among Asian markets, the iShares MSCI South Korea ETF (EWY) is down the most in October, declining more than 11% as of Oct. 29 as a selloff in chip stocks weighed heavily on Korean stocks.

Meanwhile, the iShares MSCI Taiwan ETF (EWT), the iShares MSCI China ETF (MCHI), and the iShares MSCI Japan ETF (EWJ) are also headed lower in October amid tech weakness and rising global inflationary pressures.

Tech Pressures, Central Bank Policies Weigh On US And Asian Stocks

Amazon.com (AMZN) reported capital expenditure of $77.3 billion in its latest quarterly results, soaring past Wall Street expectations, and leading to a $7.6 billion cash burn.

Google parent Alphabet Inc. (GOOG, GOOGL) also maintained its full-year spending expectations, while Microsoft lowered it to $170 billion from $175 billion. Tesla pulled back its annual capex forecast to about $20 billion, even though quarterly capex increased 88% to $4.7 billion.

Major chip stocks, including Nvidia Corp. (NVDA), Micron Technology (MU), and Intel Corp. (INTC), are also set to end the month lower.

The Federal Reserve held interest rates steady at the 3.75%-4.00% target range in its October meeting, saying it would wait for more economic cues before another change.
