Exeter City v Hashtag United to be televised

TheTools · 17.10.2026 12:47 · source · permalink

Exeter City's Women's FA Cup tie against Hashtag United has been selected as the broadcast match for the first round proper.

The match will be broadcast live on TNT Sports 1 on Sunday 25 October at 12pm, in what is the first televised Women's FA Cup tie of 2026-27.

Now in its second year, the historic Women's FA Cup broadcast deal will see TNT Sports broadcast 19 matches in the competition, commencing with Hashtag United's trip to Exeter.

In addition, from the third round proper, Channel 4 will televise one match per-round.
