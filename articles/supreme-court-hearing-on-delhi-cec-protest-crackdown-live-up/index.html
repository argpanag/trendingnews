Supreme Court Hearing On Delhi CEC Protest Crackdown [Live Updates]

TheTools · 15.10.2026 02:25 · source · permalink

15 Oct 2026 7:55 AM IST

The Supreme Court will hear petitions today related to the crackdown on the Delhi protests against the Chief Election Commissioner.

A bench of CJI Surya Kant and Justice Joymalya Bagchi will hear the matters.

The petitions raise issues of mass detentions, alleged police brutalities, arbitrary closure of metro stations, internet shutdown etc.

Follow the page for live-updates.
