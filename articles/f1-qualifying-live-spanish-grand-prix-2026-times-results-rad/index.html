  1. Report: Norris takes Madrid pole with 'gorgeous' lappublished at 16:55 BST
    16:55 BST

    Andrew Benson
    BBC F1 correspondent in Madrid

    Lando Norris took a superb pole position for McLaren at the Spanish Grand Prix, beating Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli and Red Bull's Max Verstappen in a breathless final few seconds of qualifying.

    "Oh, what a lap, gorgeous," said an ecstatic Norris after pipping world championship leader Antonelli by just 0.011 seconds. "Oh, that might be my best one."

    Verstappen was 0.140secs off the pace, as all three moved ahead of Lewis Hamilton's Ferrari in Madrid.

    The seven-time champion had held the top spot after the first runs in the final session.

    Hamilton's team-mate Charles Leclerc was fifth, ahead of Mercedes' George Russell and the second McLaren of Oscar Piastri.

    Norris' pole came from nowhere. He had been fifth in the first session of qualifying, seventh in the second, and 0.449secs slower than Hamilton's pace-setting lap after the first runs in the final session.

    Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton who will start the Spanish Grand Prix in fourth: "Second lap was just good as the other one. It was the same time pretty much.   5. Madring 'really tough circuit' - Russellpublished at 16:50 BST
    16:50 BST

    Mercedes' George Russell, speaking to Sky Sports, after qualifying sixth: "I was feeling good at the start of the session. Just come Q3, when I tried to push a bit more the tyres just couldn't take it and that cost me a good couple of tenths so that was a little bit frustrating.     16:45 BST

    McLaren's Oscar Piastri speaking to Sky Sports after qualifying P7: "I think the first lap wasn't too bad but the second lap was pretty shocking. Disappointing.   7. Norris' lap 'a masterpiece' - Stellapublished at 16:42 BST
    16:42 BST

    McLaren boss Andrea Stella on Lando Norris' pole position: "I think it was a piece of masterpiece. A masterpiece. We had seen across the whole weekend, that if we put it all together, we were not far from the best cars. So we worked our way, step by step, to try and put all of the performance together.

    "I think Lando did it only in the final run of Q3 and if you want the news, is that in the final corner he made a mistake of three tenths. So it was just an incredible lap. I think definitely one of the best of his life until the last corner.

    "He just realised what is available in the car. We are happy with this pole position. At the same time the group keeps being so tight that if you are a few tenths behind like Oscar, you are P7. There is a lot of balance in Formula 1, it is exciting and it is exciting going into tomorrow's race."

  11. 'One of the best laps in my career' - Norrispublished at 16:26 BST
    16:26 BST

    McLaren's Lando Norris who will be on pole for the Spanish Grand Prix: "Thank you. I am shocked and a little bit surprised to be here now but my God, it was probably one of the best laps I have done, ever in my career so very, very happy, very proud, very happy for the team.

    "It was just one of those laps where everything came together. I had a good Idea of what I wanted to do but it was whether my brain would let me do it on the last lap and everything worked perfectly. I'm just very happy. It was one of those laps where you look at the lap time at the end and you're like oh, that was pretty good.

    "The adrenaline was high on the last lap. It is just one where you have got to relax, just let it all flow. It is easy to get tense around here, there is a lot of walls that come out at you pretty quickly.

    "Honestly, I am feeling pretty confident and the car felt good, so I felt good out there. I just wasn't doing what I wanted. I wasn't relaxed and on the last lap I took a breath and everything came together. It was pretty much perfect. Bar the last corner, I thought I was in the wall and I thought the boys were going to repair the car tonight. Everything I wanted to achieve, I achieved. It put a big smile on my face so I am happy."
