The probable starting XIs for the Gençlerbirliği-Galatasaray match have been announced.

TheTools · 17.10.2026 12:48 · source · permalink

17.10.2026 14:00

In the 8th week of the Trendyol Süper Lig, Gençlerbirliği and Galatasaray will face each other. The match, which will start at 16:00 at Ankara Eryaman Stadium, will be refereed by Reşat Onur Coşkunses. Erkan Engin will serve as VAR. The match will be broadcast live on beIN SPORTS 1.

Gençlerbirliği: İrfan Can, Metehan, Goutas, Thalisson, Fıratcan, Ousmane, Rafael, Abdürrahim, Tiago, Franco, Mendes.

Galatasaray: Uğurcan, Sallai, Davinson, Abdülkerim, Eren, Nhaga, Lemina, Sane, Sara, Leao, Osimhen.

At Galatasaray, Lucas Torreira and Lesley Ugochukwu will not be able to play in the match due to red card suspensions. Wilfried Singo, who is injured, is also not expected to take part in the match.

It is expected that head coach Okan Buruk will assign Renato Nhaga and Mario Lemina in midfield, and prefer Leroy Sane, Gabriel Sara, Rafael Leao, and Victor Osimhen in the attacking line.

Galatasaray, which has 5 wins, 1 draw, and 1 loss in 7 matches played in the Süper Lig, is in the lead with 16 points. The yellow-reds, who lost 4-1 to Barcelona in the UEFA Champions League during the week, aim to gain morale by leaving the Gençlerbirliği away match with a win.

Gençlerbirliği, which is in 13th place with 8 points in the league, has not won in its last 5 matches. Bernd Storck, who was appointed as head coach after Metin Diyadin, drew 2-2 with Amed Sportif Faaliyetler in his first match in charge of the capital team. The red-blacks aim to end their longing for a win against Galatasaray.
