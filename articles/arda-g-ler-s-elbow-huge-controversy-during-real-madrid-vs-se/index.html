📸 Arda Güler's elbow: Huge controversy during Real Madrid vs Sevilla

The controversy of the night.

Real Madrid host Sevilla this Sunday evening, a week before El Clasico against Barcelona.

OneFootball Videos

A week after Vinicius Jr was sent off for pulling a Villarreal player's hair during play, Real almost suffered the same fate this Sunday evening!

As Los Blancos launched a counterattack, Arda Güler, who was off the ball, was held back by Peque Fernández, Sevilla's number-10.

The Real Madrid playmaker then struck his opponent with an elbow, sending him to the ground.

A lucky escape for Arda Guler? Sevilla want a card given against the Real Madrid midfielder for this action off the ball on Peque Fernandez.

After a three-minute stoppage for a VAR review, the match resumed without even a booking for Güler.

The Turkish attacker went on to score Real Madrid's opener not long after.
