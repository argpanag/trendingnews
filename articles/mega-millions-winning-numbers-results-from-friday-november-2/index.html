Thanksgiving weekend could deliver a life-changing surprise for a lottery player in the United States after the Mega Millions jackpot climbed to an estimated $673 million following another drawing without a grand prize winner earlier this week.

Friday's drawing on November 27 represents another opportunity for someone to claim what would be the second-largest Mega Millions jackpot of 2026, with the prize continuing to grow after Tuesday's winning combination failed to produce a jackpot winner.

The latest rollover has increased the estimated lump sum cash option to $282.6 million, although the amount an eventual winner receives would depend on their preferred payment method and applicable taxes.

The jackpot is also closing in on the $800 million Mega Millions prize claimed by a Florida ticket holder in July, which remains the lottery's biggest payout of the year.

Mega Millions winning numbers for Friday, November 27, 2026

The latest Mega Millions drawing is scheduled for 11 p.m. ET on Friday, November 27, with the official winning numbers expected to be released shortly afterward.

Friday's winning numbers have still to be drawn.

The winning numbers from the previous Mega Millions drawing on Tuesday, November 24, were:

White balls: 38, 42, 44, 45, 62.

Mega Ball: 16.

Players must match all five white balls and the gold Mega Ball to claim the jackpot, although several additional prize categories are available to ticket holders who match fewer numbers.

The odds of securing the Mega Millions grand prize stand at approximately one in 290.4 million, compared with Powerball's jackpot odds of approximately one in 292.2 million.

How much will Mega Millions jackpot winner receive after taxes?

The advertised $673 million jackpot represents the total value of the annuity option, which consists of 30 payments that increase by 5% annually. Winners can alternatively choose to receive a single cash payment of $282.6 million before taxes.

Under the cash option, mandatory federal withholding of 24% would initially reduce the payout to approximately $214.8 million. Applying the highest federal marginal income tax rate of 37% would leave an estimated $178 million before additional state or local taxes.

Someone selecting the annuity could receive approximately $424 million over the payment period, assuming a federal tax rate of 37%, although future tax rates could affect the final amount.

Friday's prize ranks behind three other major lottery jackpots awarded in 2026, including the $1.04 billion Powerball jackpot won in Illinois in August and an $847 million Powerball prize claimed in New Jersey earlier this month.

If nobody matches all six winning numbers on Friday, the Mega Millions jackpot will roll over again ahead of the next scheduled drawing on Tuesday, December 1.
