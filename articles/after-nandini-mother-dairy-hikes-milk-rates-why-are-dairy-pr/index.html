After Nandini, Mother Dairy hikes milk rates: Why are dairy prices rising?

TheTools · 11.11.2026 10:31 · source · permalink

Mother Dairy's ₹2-4 per litre increase follows price hikes by Nandini and Amul. Higher milk procurement, feed, fodder, transport and packaging costs are driving the rise

Dairy companies have cited higher input costs, including rising milk procurement prices, diesel expenses and packaging costs, as reasons for the price increases.
