Cold Snap Delivers a Big Powder Top-Up, with Sunny Days Ahead

Summary

Sunday’s icy blast delivered a big late-season powder top-up, with 15-25+cm across many South Island ski fields and fresh snow also falling on Mt Ruapehu. After another windy Monday, conditions improve from Tuesday, with mint spring weather midweek before another front arrives Friday.

Short Term Forecast

Forecast for Monday, 28th September

Fresh snow will be underfoot nationwide, but strong, cold winds could still cause lift issues. Mt Ruapehu may add another 5-10cm, while Canterbury clears into a cracking sunny afternoon.

Forecast for Tuesday 29th and Wednesday 30th September

Conditions settle down Tuesday as cold southwesterlies ease, bringing plenty of sunshine by the afternoon. Wednesday stays mint for the South Island despite strengthening northwesterlies, while Mt Ruapehu sees a mix of sun and cloud.

Forecast for Thursday 1st and Friday 2nd October

Northwesterlies strengthen through Thursday and Friday, bringing increasing cloud and showers to the Southern Lakes before more widespread rain arrives Friday afternoon. Canterbury stays mostly dry, while Mt Ruapehu enjoys a sunny Thursday before cloud and a few showers return Friday.

Extended Forecast

A little South Island snow is possible next weekend (3rd & 4th of October) after a spell of rain, although confidence remains low. Fine weather should then dominate the South Island next week, while Mt Ruapehu stays more unsettled and showery.

Thanks for reading. Wednesday will be the season's final forecast. It's been an absolute blast!

Mike
