By Joe Lombardi,

A reality TV star has died at 35, prompting a heartbreaking tribute from the man who shared her televised love story.

Sheila Mangubat, whose relationship with David Dangerfield appeared on TLC’s “90 Day Fiancé: Before the 90 Days,” died in the Philippines on Saturday, Nov. 21. Dangerfield announced her death in an Instagram post, saying he learned the news from her family.

“I am devastated and still trying to process this loss,” Dangerfield wrote in the post. “Please keep her son, her family, and me in your prayers. Thank you for all your love and support.”

A separate @90dayfianceupdate post on Instagram alleged that Mangubat had uterine cancer and was hospitalized days before her death. Dangerfield’s announcement did not identify an illness or cause.

Mangubat was days away from her 36th birthday. She leaves behind her son, Jhonreil.

Viewers met Mangubat and Dangerfield during season six of “Before the 90 Days.” Their story followed a relationship that began online and faced the challenges of distance, communication, and plans for a shared future.

The pair connected in 2021 through a Facebook group for deaf singles. Dangerfield, who is deaf, traveled from Nebraska to Cebu, Philippines, to meet Mangubat in person, according to Entertainment Weekly.

Mangubat had partial hearing loss and used a hearing aid. Her limited knowledge of American Sign Language presented a communication challenge as the couple began spending time together.

Dangerfield proposed before returning to the US. Financial pressures and K-1 visa setbacks later strained their relationship, and they called off their engagement in October 2025, Entertainment Weekly reported.

Tributes followed the announcement of her death. Her sister, Grace, remembered her on Facebook, while her brother, Jonathan, shared footage from the family’s farewell.

Fellow franchise cast members, including James and Meitalia of “90 Day Fiancé: The Other Way,” also offered condolences.
