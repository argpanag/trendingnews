Five Lotto players share prize as Powerball rolls over to $22m

TheTools · 25.11.2026 12:24 · source · permalink

Lotto's huge $20 million Powerball prize was not struck in last night's draw, meaning the jackpot will roll over to an even bigger $22 million for Wednesday's draw.

Five lucky players won Lotto First Division, each taking home $200,000. The winning tickets were purchased in Kaitaia, Bay of Plenty, Wellington, Christchurch and Oamaru.

One Northland MyLotto player won Strike Four, claiming a whopping $400,000.

Lotto Second Division saw 22 winners, each receiving $14,500. Two of the players also won Powerball Second Division, making their total winnings $26,186 each.

Lotto urged anyone who bought a ticket to check it as soon as possible in-store, on MyLotto, or through the MyLotto App.

Wednesday's draw will be the biggest jackpot on offer since Lotto's September changes, which increased top jackpots but made odds of winning Powerball Division 1 harder, rising from one in 38.4 million to one in 53.7 million.
