At least three Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) personnel were injured after militants opened fire in Jammu and Kashmir’s Kulgam district on Wednesday (November 11, 2026), officials said.

#WATCH | J&K | Two CRPF personnel injured after unidentified motorcycle-borne assailants opened fire on security personnel deployed at a naka in Kulgam district this afternoon. The injured personnel were shifted to a hospital in Anantnag district for treatment. (Visuals from the area, deferred by unspecified time; no live operational details disclosed)

Confirming the number of injured personnel to The Hindu, CRPF DG G.P Singh said, “Operations [are] going on to nab the perpetrators,” adding that best possible treatment will be given to the injured.

In a separate statement, the CRPF said that the personnel, “were injured after unidentified motorcycle-borne assailants opened fire on security personnel deployed at a naka in Kulgam district of Jammu and Kashmir this afternoon. The incident took place at around 2.15 p.m. when the assailants fired at the CRPF personnel before fleeing the spot.”

“The injured personnel were shifted to a hospital in Anantnag district for treatment,” the statement added.
