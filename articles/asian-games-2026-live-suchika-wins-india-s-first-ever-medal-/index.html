Suchika Tariyal's bronze is India’s first medal in mixed martial arts which made its debut at the ongoing Asian Games. (Photo: X/@ani_digital)

Suchika Tariyal signed off with a bronze medal in the women’s traditional 60kg mixed martial arts event after losing 0-2 to Singapore’s Hui Hoon Teo in the extra round of their semifinal at the Asian Games in Nagoya, Japan on Monday (September 21, 2026). Her bronze is India’s first medal in mixed martial arts that made its debut at the ongoing Asian Games.

India also clinched a silver medal in the men’s 10m air rifle team event at the Asian Games, with shooters Rudrankksh Patil and Himanshu Dhillon also qualifying for the individual final.

The feat comes a day after the women’s 10m air rifle shooters also clinched two silver medals.

India’s Himanshu Dhillon and Rudrankksh Patil won individual silver and bronze, respectively, at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games. The duo had earlier helped India secure silver in the men’s 10m air rifle team event.

Himanshu finished second with 252.4 points, while Rudrankksh claimed bronze with 230.9. China’s reigning Olympic champion Sheng Lihao won gold with 254.2. Both Indians had qualified for the final after strong performances in the morning’s qualification round.

Indian MMA fighter Suchika Tariyal made history by winning India’s first-ever Asian Games medal in Mixed Martial Arts, securing bronze in the women’s Traditional MMA 60kg category in Japan.

After defeating Mongolia’s Altanchimeg Baigalmaa 2-0 in the quarterfinals, Tariyal lost 0-2 to Singapore’s Teo Hui Hoon in the semifinals. However, both losing semifinalists are automatically awarded medals in Traditional MMA, earning Tariyal a historic bronze and adding to India’s growing medal tally.

Star Indian swimmer Srihari Nataraj qualified for the 50m backstroke final at the Asian Games here on Monday.

The two-time Olympian clocked 25.37 to finish third in his heat and joint ninth overall to book his place in the final.

Rishabh Das, who had finish sixth in the men’s 100m backstroke on Sunday, clocked 25.41s to miss out the final by a whisker. He finished 11th overall, ending as the reserve.

-PTI

India clinched a silver medal in the men’s 10m air rifle team event at the Asian Games here on Monday, with shooters Rudrankksh Patil and Himanshu Dhillon also qualifying for the individual final.

Rudrankksh, Himanshu and Parth Mane finished with a team total of 1890.1.

China claimed the gold with a world record team total of 1899.0 points, bettering their previous mark by 0.6 points.

South Korea produced a strong fightback but had settle for bronze with a total of 1884.2 points.

-PTI

In sepak takraw, India started off their three regu-match series against Japan in Group B with a 2-0 win. 

Team India started their regu Group B match with a point and eventually went down 3-6. The visitors tried clawing their way back, reducing the deficit to 5-6. Japan managed to take the lead again at the time of timeout, leading by 10-5. After the time out, India managed five quick points, making it 10-10. India managed to secure the first set 15-13, making a fine comeback from being down 5-10 to winning 15-13. 

India started the second set on a tight note with a 2-2 scoreline, but eventually rattled Japan team with a stunning lead of 9-3. The Indian team continued to demoralise the local favourites with their game, taking a 14-5 lead. 

India eventually prevailed, beating Japan 2-0 (15-13, 15-9) for a positive start in their three-match regu series.
