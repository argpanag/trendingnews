Weather stays mostly steady for several more days

TheTools · 21.10.2026 22:58 · source · permalink

Weather Headlines

  • NEXT 9 HOURS: Cool and quiet
  • SHORT TERM: Lots of nice weather
  • LONG TERM: Shifting next week

Latest Forecast

CEDAR RAPIDS, Iowa (KCRG) - Little change in our forecast means more nice conditions to come.

Expect another evening and night of really decent weather. Skies remain mostly clear throughout, with temperatures falling steadily as the sun sets. Overnight lows dip to around 50 degrees, give or take a bit depending on where you live. Overall, this is a touch warmer than Wednesday morning’s temperatures.

This steady, but small, climb in temperatures from day to day continues on Thursday. Highs push toward the mid 70s, though don’t expect a lot of difference otherwise. A patch or two of clouds will be present at times, but sunshine will be the thing you see most of the time.

The weather remains on repeat for Friday, though we’ll see a bit more cloud cover at times compared to most of this week. Don’t expect a gloomy, overcast sky, but sunshine could be blocked or filtered by clouds a bit more. Still, it’ll be a warm one with lows around 50 and highs in the mid 70s. This will be a big change for high school football games compared to the windier, colder weather of just a week ago.

This type of weather carries throughout the weekend, too. Dry weather will be there, but it could be a little bit windier on Saturday. The bottom line is that both days look excellent to get out and enjoy, especially for this time of year. Another weekend in the 70s gets less and less likely each week that passes, so don’t miss this opportunity if you prefer warmer weather.

That’s especially important because changes happen as we kick off the following work and school week. Temperatures get limited by more cloud cover and, eventually, the chance of showers. These should generally hold off until later in the day, perhaps as late as evening for much of eastern Iowa. The chance runs through Monday night into portions of Tuesday, too, as a storm system slowly moves through the region.

It doesn’t look like there will be a ton of moisture available for this system, meaning that rainfall totals will be on the light end here in eastern Iowa. Perhaps a tenth or two of an inch will fall if you’re lucky, though most areas stay on the low end of that range at most.

The bigger impact from this system is the shift toward cooler conditions next week. For Tuesday through next Friday, lows will be in the 40s and highs hanging around 60 degrees. While this is a pretty big shift from where we’re at right now, it’s still at least a handful of degrees above normal for the end of October.

There’s also some indication that next week will be a bit more active compared to this week. Another opportunity for some rain returns on Thursday, with dry conditions for Friday.
