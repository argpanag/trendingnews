Gilmer’s Neighbors scores 4 TDs, Buckeyes snap Carthage’s 38-game win streak

TheTools · 31.10.2026 00:18 · source · permalink

GILMER, Texas (KLTV) - The Gilmer Buckeyes snapped the Carthage Bulldogs’ 38-game winning streak Friday night, securing a 49-42 victory at Jeff Traylor Stadium.

Aiden Thomas scored the game-winning touchdown on a 40-yard run with 48 seconds remaining in the fourth quarter, capping a back-and-forth contest between the two East Texas rivals. Running back Kipton Neighbors rushed for 247 yards and scored 4 times on the ground.

First-half shootout

Carthage opened the game with a 10-play, 70-yard drive capped by a 2-yard touchdown run from quarterback Jacob Dixon to give the Bulldogs a 7-0 lead with 7:41 left in the first quarter.

Gilmer responded three plays later when quarterback Zade Taylor connected with Kaden Canady on a 36-yard touchdown pass to tie the game at 7 with 6:39 remaining in the period.

Carthage regained the lead on its following possession when Dixon threw a 6-yard touchdown pass to JD Edwards, putting the Bulldogs up 14-7 with 5:18 left in the quarter. Gilmer answered with an eight-play, 55-yard drive ending in a 2-yard touchdown run by Kipton Neighbors to tie the score at 14.

In the second quarter, the Gilmer defense sacked Dixon, forcing a fumble that the Buckeyes recovered at their own 23-yard line. Neighbors capped the ensuing nine-play, 77-yard possession with a 27-yard rushing touchdown to give Gilmer a 21-14 lead with 4:18 left before halftime.

Carthage countered as Dixon connected with Edwards on a deep pass, setting up a 1-yard rushing touchdown by Shawn Bundy. The Bulldogs converted a two-point attempt to take a 22-21 lead with 3:31 remaining in the half. Gilmer closed the second quarter with a 30-yard field goal to carry a 24-22 advantage into the break.

Second-half momentum swings

Gilmer extended its lead to 27-22 in the third quarter with another field goal. Carthage responded with a touchdown drive to take a 28-27 lead after a failed two-point conversion attempt.

Neighbors put the Buckeyes back in front on the next possession with a 71-yard rushing touchdown. Gilmer added a two-point conversion to push its lead to 35-28. Carthage answered on its next drive to tie the game at 35.

Neighbors then scored his fourth rushing touchdown of the night to put Gilmer ahead 42-35, but the Bulldogs countered again to level the score at 42.

Late-game finish

With 48 seconds remaining in regulation, Thomas broke free for a 40-yard rushing touchdown to give Gilmer a 49-42 lead.

Edwards returned the ensuing kickoff to set Carthage up in Gilmer territory at the 25-yard line. The Buckeyes’ defense sealed the win with four consecutive incomplete passes, ending the defending state champions’ 38-game streak before Gilmer ran out the final seconds.

Red Zone Overtime recap

Michael Coleman and Kyle Owens broke down the game on Red Zone Overtime. Owens said the game resembled a boxing match.

“It was the resiliency of Gilmer to you know, there was a lot of times in that game where big bad Carthage comes down and answers. And a lot of teams aren’t able to respond and stay toe to toe. It was almost like two heavyweight boxers just slugging it out tonight in the middle of the ring. They weren’t trying to duck and dodge each other. They were going right at each other,” Owens said. “Gilmer delivered that final blow. And they got the stop when they needed to. That fumble by Jacob Dixon in the second quarter. And that fourth down stop there at the end of the game Gilmer got the two stops they needed. Coach Metzel also talked about this earlier in the week. He said, when we don’t turn the football over, we’re a really good football team. Zero turnovers for the Gilmer Buckeyes tonight. They played a perfect game when they needed to play a perfect game.”

Carthage (7-1, 3-1) wraps up the regular season Friday night at home against Center, while Gilmer (7-2, 3-1) ends the regular season on the road against Spring Hill.
