Hollywood A-Listers Send A Message To Voters On Social Media

TheTools · 04.11.2026 00:56 · source · permalink

As the first polls close on the 2026 Midterm elections, celebrities took to social media to share their “I voted” photos — and some even shared who they voted for.

While messages varied, all urged Americans to vote, while some went even further to express anger at politicians and the ruling elite.

Scroll through the list below to see a selection of images and comments from the likes of Pedro Pascal, Jennifer Lawrence, Chris Pratt, Olivia Rodrigo, Jennifer Aniston, Samuel L. Jackson, Alec Baldwin, Jessica Alba and others.

  • Pedro Pascal: “Just Vote”

    Pedro Pascal has made no secret of his political leanings, but in a video posted to his Instagram Story on Monday (and later reposted by fans), the Avengers: Doomsday actor ditched the partisan messaging, saying with a wink, “Just vote.”

  • Jennifer Lawrence: “If We Burn, You Burn With Us!”

    Jennifer Lawrence urged Americans to “Go vote” on her Instagram story.

    She also posted a photo of then added an image of herself making the District 12 salute from The Hunger Games, and added for fans, “Don’t forget who burns with us.” (For the uninitiated, in The Hunger Games: Mockingjay Lawrence’s character Katniss Everdeen issues the following threat to President Snow and the ruling elite: “Fire is catching! And if we burn, you burn with us!”)

  • Chris Pratt’s “Patriotic” Election Post

    The Guardians of the Galaxy actor, who stayed neutral in the last presidential election, posted an image of himself and his “I voted” sticker with the caption, “It’s like a gold star, but patriotic.”

    That’s likely an unintentionally poor choice of words, given that the term “gold star,” for many, conjures the idea of Gold Star military families.

  • Jennifer Aniston Is Feeling ‘Naughty’

    On election day, Jennifer Aniston may be looking forward to a happier season: The holidays.

    The Emmy winner posted a video to her Instagram Stories that showed an inflatable Santa Claus being lowered down onto the ground with the word “mood” across the image.

    The video is likely from the set of Olivia Wilde’s upcoming Naughty, about which Deadline broke the news. In it, Aniston stars as Mallory, a woman whose only hope of securing custody of her son from her gaslighting ex is to find Santa Claus (Peter Dinklage) and convince him to testify at her divorce hearing.

    Lest fans not see the election-day connection, the actress put a link to voting centers at the bottom of the image with the words “go vote!”

  • Samuel L. Jackson Says It With A T-Shirt

    Samuel L. Jackson had a familiar message for those who might be apathetic or seek to deny him his right to the ballot.

    The Academy Award honoree posted an “I voted” photo with the caption, “What the shirt says!!”

    So what did the shirt say? “fcuk you, i’m voting!

  • Carrie Coon & Tracey Letts Endorse A Democrat

    Gilded Age star Carrie Coon posted a photo of herself and husband Tracey Letts (currently in Netflix’s East of Eden) with their “I Voted” stickers. She added an endorsement for Democrat Cait Conley, who’s running in the U.S. House’s 17th District, which covers southern New York.

  • Dave Bautista Urges, “Vote Them Out”

    Kratos himself, Dave Bautista, urged his followers to vote Tuesday on Threads. The God of War actor posted an on-brand shirtless photo of himself holding up his Florida vote-by-mail ballot. He added the hashtag, “#votethemout.”

  • Julia Roberts: “Power To The People”

    Oscar-winner Julia Roberts shared her “I voted’ sticker and offered a simple, party-agnostic slogan: “Power to the People.”

    She also paid a more partisan tribute to husband Danny Moder with her jewelry selection.

  • Bruce Springsteen: “You Have A Solemn Duty”

    Bruce Springsteen recently endorsed Abdul Al-Sayed and today, he urged people to get out and vote.

    In a video posted to social media, The Boss thanked election workers and government officials for their service at the polls and reminded them of their sworn oath to the constitution.

  • Jessica Alba Has A Message For Latino Voters

    Jessica Alba rattled off a series of election-day messages on her Instagram Stories, including a screenshot of a post from America Ferrara addressing Latino voters.

    “Make sure they know what’s important to you,” the message read. “When Latino voters show up, we shape the future.”

    It included a text number for voting information.

  • Tom Hanks Makes His Point

    Tom Hanks had a simple message to voters: vote.

    The Oscar-winner posted a photo of himself pointing to his “I voted” sticker. He included a link for fans to find their polling place.

  • Alec Baldwin: “For Me, It’s Been A Nightmare”

    A haggard-looking Alec Baldwin got up Monday morning and took to Instagram to admit that the past few years have been, for him, “a nightmare,” politically.

    “It’s been a nightmare,” he said. “I can’t believe we’ve arrived where we’ve arrived. The beginning of the end of that nightmare is tomorrow.”

    He added that he is looking forward to a “more sane” world.

    “The world we are living in now is insane.”

  • Olivia Rodrigo’s “I Voted” Selfie

    Olivia Rodrigo shared a selfie showing off her “I voted” sticker and urging fans to “get out there and vote!”

    The superstar has not shied away from politics, recently calling out an ICE ad that made use of lyrics from her song “All-American Bitch.”

    And then there was her Daisy Chain Fields Festival this summer. It raised $20 million, for charities that benefit women.

  • Rosie O’Donnell: “Never…Again”

    Rosie O’Donnell has published a steady stream of election-day walkup posts on Instagram. Today, she asked the question, “Is it me or does not feel like our entire nation is waiting for the results of a biopsy?”

    That followed a simpler repost: A photo of red hat emblazoned with text paraphrasing a post-holocaust mantra of determination and opposition. Given O’Donnell’s politics and the color of the hat, it’s probably safe to say the message was decidedly un-MAGA.

  • Kerry Washington Does The Jon Ossoff Dance

    Longtime Democrat Kerry Washington didn’t try to hide her enthusiasm or her loyalty, posting last week an Instrgram video of herself wearing a Jon Ossoff sweatshirt and dancing in front of a Jon Ossoff step and repeat.

    The Emmy-winner wrote, “The message is clear: VOTE EARLY GEORGIA! Polls are open on or before 10/30 or on 11/3.”
