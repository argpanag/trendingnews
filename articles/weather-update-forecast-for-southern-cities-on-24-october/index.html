Weather update: Forecast for southern cities on 24 October

TheTools · 24.10.2026 01:30 · source · permalink

Published Oct 24, 2026 | 7:00 AM ⚊ Updated Oct 24, 2026 | 7:00 AM

Weather forecast. (iStock)

Planning your day in the South Indian cities of Amaravati, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, or Thiruvananthapuram? Here’s what the weather has in store.

Cloudy skies and rain are expected across several southern cities on Friday, according to the India Meteorological Department’s forecast.

Amaravati is expected to experience generally cloudy skies, with one or two spells of rain or thundershowers.

The temperature is expected to range between a low of 27°C and a high of 34°C.

A warning has been issued for thunderstorm accompanied with lightning and strong surface winds.

Bengaluru is expected to experience generally cloudy sky with one or two spells of rain or thundershowers.

The temperature is expected to range between a low of 21°C and a high of 28°C.

No warning has been issued.

Chennai is expected to experience generally cloudy sky with heavy rain.

The temperature is expected to range between a low of 25°C and a high of 32°C.

A warning has been issued for heavy rainfall, thunderstorm accompanied with lightning.

Hyderabad is expected to experience partly cloudy sky with haze.

The temperature is expected to range between a low of 23°C and a high of 34°C.

No warning has been issued.

Thiruvananthapuram is expected to experience generally cloudy skies with few spells of rain or Thundershowers.

The temperature is expected to range between a low of 24°C and a high of 32°C.

A warning has been issued for heavy rainfall, thunderstorm accompanied with lightning & gusty winds(40-50kmph)
