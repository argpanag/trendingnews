Roads blocked in Roanoke County after vehicle, train collision

TheTools · 26.09.2026 14:57 · source · permalink

Local News

ROANOKE COUNTY, Va. – 5:10 p.m. UPDATE

Diuguids Lane and Barley Drive have been reopened following the collision.

RCSO said that traffic control for Garman Road was turned over to Norfolk Southern as repairs continue.

ORIGINAL STORY

A collision between a train and a vehicle in Roanoke County has resulted in multiple temporary road closures, Roanoke County Police Department said.

RCPD said as of 10:27 a.m. on Saturday, they are on scene of a collision between a train and a vehicle at the intersection of Garman Road and Garman Circle.

The following roads are blocked as a result:

  • Garman Road
  • Diuguids Lane
  • Barley Drive

We will updte you as more information becomes available and when the roads reopen.
