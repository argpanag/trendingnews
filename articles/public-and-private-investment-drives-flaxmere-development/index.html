Public and private investment drives Flaxmere development

TheTools · 09.09.2026 00:00 · source · permalink

Current filter:

The building of two new roads through the Flaxmere town centre is scheduled for the second half of 2027, subject to funding confirmation.

The roads are the next stage of Hastings District Council infrastructure work in the area, following the three waters upgrades and surrounding new roads already completed to support housing.

Council’s urban development director Raoul Oosterkamp said the new roads would be the next visible part of the infrastructure programme underway in Flaxmere.

“A lot of the work completed so far has been underground, providing the water, wastewater and stormwater infrastructure needed for housing growth happening around the town centre.

“The new roads are the next major piece of Council work and will create better access between the shopping and residential area.”

Before the road works begin, changes will start to be seen within the privately owned shopping centre. The centre is owned by TW Property, which is carrying out its redevelopment separately from, but aligned with, Council’s infrastructure programme.

TW Property commercial director Terry May said the company planned to demolish the block containing the former supermarket and pharmacy towards the end of this year. The work would prepare the site for the new roads and the first stage of the centre’s redevelopment.

Mr May said the company planned to begin construction during 2027 on the first of the new retail blocks on the site, designed to accommodate eateries.

The redevelopment would be staged to allow TW Property to work closely with existing tenants as the project progressed.

“People will start to see some significant changes in the area later this year with the demolition work,” he said.

“We are also working closely with Council to make sure our development and the new public infrastructure are coordinated, while allowing the existing business to operate during construction.”

TW Property was taking a long-term view of the redevelopment and was looking forward to seeing the next stage of the centre take shape, he said.

“There has been a lot of work happening around the town centre, and it’s exciting to see that coming together. There is still a lot to do, but we are looking forward to getting underway and working with the existing tenants as the project progresses.”

Flaxmere ward councillor Henare O’Keefe said seeing the next stages of the town centre taking shape would mean a great deal to the community.

“Our people have wanted this for a long, long time. We have talked about the future of our town centre for years, and I’ve been privileged to be around the Council table for many of those conversations.

“I’ve always said Flaxmere is an opportunity. We know what we have here, we know the pride our people have in this place, and now others are seeing that too.

“There has been a huge amount happening – new infrastructure, new homes, the new supermarket and pharmacy – and the redevelopment of the town centre is another part of that journey.

“For years we have said it’s Flaxmere’s time. Well, here we are. There is still work to do and it won’t all happen overnight, but we are seeing real change and that is something our community can feel very positive about.”

Mr Oosterkamp said Council and TW had different but closely connected roles in the work underway. “The rebuilding of the shopping centre is a TW Property project. Council’s role is to provide the two roads running through the new town centre.

“It is important that the two programmes line up, and we have a good working relationship with TW as both sets of work progress.”

More detail on the road works will be provided ahead of construction.
