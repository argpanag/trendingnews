Ashton Jeanty Delivers 142 Yards Against Broncos After Benching

TheTools · 22.11.2026 23:17 · source · permalink

Las Vegas Raiders running back Ashton Jeanty delivered a major offensive performance against the Denver Broncos on November 22, 2026, at Empower Field, recording 142 yards before halftime. His contribution included an explosive rushing play and a 68-yard touchdown reception from quarterback Fernando Mendoza, marking a significant response after he was benched against the New York Jets.

Jeanty's performance featured 64 rushing yards on seven carries and 78 receiving yards on three catches during the first half. The combination demonstrated his ability to contribute through multiple offensive roles as Las Vegas attempted to close the gap against its AFC West opponent.

One of the standout moments came when Jeanty broke through an opening on the left side of the Raiders' offensive line. The resulting 46-yard run brought Las Vegas into scoring position and helped produce the team's first points through a field goal.

According to statistics attributed to Next Gen Stats in the source report, Jeanty's maximum speed on the run reached 21.38 mph. The article's accompanying narrative separately listed the figure as 21.88 mph, creating a discrepancy in the reported measurement. The 21.38 mph figure was described as the fastest maximum speed of Jeanty's professional career.

The running back also made an impact on the following offensive possession, when Mendoza connected with him on a wheel route. Jeanty found himself open downfield and completed the play with a 68-yard touchdown reception, giving the Raiders another significant offensive contribution.

The performance also highlighted how head coach Klint Kubiak has expanded Jeanty's responsibilities within the offense. Rather than limiting his involvement to conventional rushing opportunities, Kubiak has increasingly incorporated the second-year player into the passing game.

Jeanty entered the NFL after establishing himself as a standout running back at Boise State. During his college career, he became a 2,000-yard rusher and a Heisman Trophy finalist, achievements that helped establish expectations surrounding his professional prospects.

Former Raiders head coach Pete Carroll selected Jeanty in the first round of the 2025 NFL Draft. Carroll subsequently departed following a season in which Las Vegas finished with a 3-14 record, leaving the franchise under a different coaching direction.

Under Kubiak, Jeanty's increased involvement in receiving opportunities has provided another way to utilize his offensive abilities. His 68-yard touchdown against Denver illustrated that expanded role, while his 46-yard rushing play demonstrated his continued involvement in the ground game.

Despite Jeanty's production, the Raiders still faced a 17-10 deficit against their division rivals in the first-half update provided by the source report.
