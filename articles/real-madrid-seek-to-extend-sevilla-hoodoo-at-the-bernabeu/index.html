Real Madrid seek to extend Sevilla hoodoo at the Bernabeu

Real Madrid host Sevilla in La Liga’s ninth round at the Santiago Bernabeu, armed with imposing home form and a dominant recent record. The visitors arrive intent on challenging a hoodoo that stretches back years.

According to Goal.com, Sevilla have lost 16 of their last 17 league trips to Madrid, drawing the other, and have not won in the capital since a 4-3 victory in December 2008.

OneFootball Videos

Madrid have strung together seven straight home league wins. They have bettered that only once since 2020-2021, a run of 10 victories between April and November 2025.

Expect intensity without the ball. Madrid lead La Liga for shots after high regains with 17, while Sevilla top the division for high recoveries with 87.

Kylian Mbappe has four goal involvements in four league games against Sevilla, three goals and one assist, scoring in three different matches. Only against Real Sociedad has he scored in more different games, four.

There are signs of resilience from the Andalusians. They have taken points in four of their last six away league games against Madrid-region teams, two wins and two draws.

This season they are unbeaten across four away league matches, two wins and two draws, their best start since 2011-2012 when they avoided defeat in their first six.

On the touchline, Jose Mourinho has won five of six La Liga meetings with Sevilla, losing one, and his Madrid scored six or more in two separate wins, 6-2 in May and December 2011, a feat otherwise only achieved against Malaga. Luis Garcia has lost his last six against Real Madrid, though he earned a win and a draw in six clashes with Mourinho’s Madrid.

Source: Goal.com
