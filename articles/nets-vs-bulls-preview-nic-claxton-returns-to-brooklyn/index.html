Nets vs. Bulls Preview: Nic Claxton Returns to Brooklyn

TheTools · 18.11.2026 16:17 · source · permalink
  • Nets Game Previews

Claxton won’t be the only familiar face at Barclays on Wednesday night.

Well, Nets fans, Wednesday night at Barclays Center might feel a little strange.

Nic Claxton is coming back to Brooklyn. Only this time, he’ll be wearing a Chicago Bulls uniform.

After spending seven seasons with the Nets, Claxton returns to the building he once called home. While the reunion provides a little extra intrigue, both teams have bigger problems to worry about. Brooklyn and Chicago enter Wednesday with identical 3-10 records, each searching for some much-needed momentum.

Something’s gotta give, right? Perhaps the Nets will channel some old fortune from the ABA days on ABA Night. It’s the first of six nights dedicated to different eras in Nets history.

Game Info

  • Who: Chicago Bulls (3-10) at Brooklyn Nets (3-10)
  • When: Wednesday, November 18, 2026, 7:30 p.m. ET
  • Where: Barclays Center, Brooklyn, NY
  • TV: YES Network, DAZN

The Game

Neither team is exactly setting the Eastern Conference on fire.

The Nets are coming off Monday’s 135-123 loss to Philadelphia, their fourth defeat in five games. Julius Randle delivered 33 points, while rookie Nolan Traore chipped in 23.

Unfortunately, another productive offensive night wasn’t enough.

Brooklyn found itself trailing by as many as 24 points before showing some life in the second half. And that’s becoming a familiar storyline for Jordi Fernandez’s group. The Nets have shown flashes of competitive basketball. The problem? Putting together 48 minutes of it.

There are bigger problems of course. Rotations aren’t consistent and the team isn’t playing hard. Scouts believe the roster wasn’t constructed properly. Whatever it is, it ain’t working.

Fortunately, Chicago isn’t arriving in much better shape.

The Bulls recently snapped an eight-game losing streak with consecutive victories over Atlanta and Minnesota before falling 126-115 to Cleveland on Monday.

Meanwhile, Norman Powell scored 27 points against the Cavaliers, continuing an impressive offensive stretch. He’s averaging 28 points over his last three games while shooting 57.1% from the field.

Defensively, though, Chicago has been vulnerable. The Bulls allowed Cleveland to shoot 52.6% from the field Monday, including 69 points in the second half, which presents an opportunity for the Nets.

Brooklyn needs to establish an offensive rhythm early, limit the defensive breakdowns that have plagued its recent outings, and avoid spending another night digging out of a massive hole.

And with both teams sitting at 3-10, Wednesday represents a legitimate chance to get back in the win column.

Player to Watch: Nic Claxton

Come on. Who else were we going to pick?

For seven seasons, Claxton was a familiar presence in Brooklyn’s frontcourt, developing from a second-round pick into a defensive anchor and one of the franchise’s most recognizable faces.

Now he’s on the other side.

Traded to Chicago during the offseason as part of the deal that brought Julius Randle to Brooklyn, Claxton missed the Bulls’ first eight games with a hamstring injury before making his season debut on November 8. He’s played four games since, averaging 7.8 points, 4.5 rebounds and 2.0 assists in 17.9 minutes per night. He’s been efficient from the field, converting 61.1% of his attempts — nothing new for Nets fans to see.

Claxton gave Chicago a glimpse of what he can provide offensively with 17 points against San Antonio last week. We’ll see if he has the same chip on his shoulder against the team who drafted him.

From The Vault

It’s ABA Night at Barclays Center! The Nets will bring all living players and coaches from the franchise’s only two title teams (1974, 1976). It aligns with the old jerseys they’ve been repping along with an alternative court which has the names of every player to ever suit up in a Nets uniform.
