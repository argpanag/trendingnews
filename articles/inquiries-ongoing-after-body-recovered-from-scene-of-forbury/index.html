Inquiries ongoing after body recovered from scene of Forbury fire

TheTools · 19.10.2026 06:01 · source · permalink
PHOTO

Police inquiries are ongoing after a person was found dead at the scene of a Dunedin house fire last week.

A body was discovered inside a Loyalty St house, in the southern Dunedin suburb of Forbury, on Friday after a small fire was extinguished.

A large police presence was visible alongside firefighters.

On Sunday, a police spokeswoman said there was no “substantial update” she could provide, though inquiries were ongoing.

The scene remained under guard through Monday. 

— Allied Media
