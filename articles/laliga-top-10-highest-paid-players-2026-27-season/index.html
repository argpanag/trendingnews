Real Madrid completely dominates the podium of the highest paid players in Spanish football.

Kylian Mbappe leads the overall list with a massive gross salary exceeding 71 million euros per year.

Stars from FC Barcelona, Atletico de Madrid and Athletic Club complete the top ten salary rankings.

The start of the 2026/27 LaLiga season highlights the immense financial power required by top Spanish clubs to attract and retain the world’s greatest football talent. According to recent salary data compiled by Capology, the list of the top ten highest earning players in Spanish football is heavily dominated by Real Madrid and FC Barcelona, with key figures from Atletico de Madrid and Athletic Club also securing spot in the upper financial tier of the domestic league.

10. Rodrygo (Real Madrid)

The Brazilian winger opens the top ten list after serving as a pivotal figure for Real Madrid since his arrival in 2019, despite suffering a severe knee injury that sidelined him for most of last season and forced him to miss the 2026 World Cup. Real Madrid maintains full confidence in his long term recovery, backing the attacker with a gross annual salary of €20,840,000 which guarantees him a net income of €10,000,000.

9. Nico Williams (Athletic Club)

The talented Spanish winger earned his spot among the league’s top earners after Athletic Club offered him a lucrative contract extension to stave off intense transfer interest from FC Barcelona and other European giants. In order to keep their prized asset in Bilbao, the club agreed to a gross annual salary of €31,360,000 that earns the dynamic attacker a net figure of €10,250,000 per season.

8. Bernardo Silva (Real Madrid)

After establishing himself as a club legend at Manchester City, the Portuguese playmaker opted for a new professional challenge by joining Real Madrid during a transitional period for the Spanish powerhouse. Embracing the pressure of driving the team forward, the experienced midfielder commands a gross annual salary of €24,170,000 which translates to a net income of €11,600,000.

7. Jude Bellingham (Real Madrid)

The English midfielder transitioned from an elite prospect into a world class star for both Real Madrid and the national team following his high profile arrival from Borussia Dortmund. His seamless adaptation to the high expectations at the Santiago Bernabeu justifies his place on the list, earning a gross annual package of €25,000,000 that delivers €12,000,000 net per year.

6. Jan Oblak (Atletico de Madrid)

As one of the long standing leaders at Atletico de Madrid alongside Koke and Josema Gimenez, the Slovenian goalkeeper remains a vital pillar in Diego Simeone’s defensive system. Backed by a historic track record that includes multiple Zamora Trophies, the veteran goalkeeper receives a gross annual compensation of €26,040,000 that amounts to €12,500,000 net.

5. Lamine Yamal (FC Barcelona)

Barcelona’s wonderkid enters another crucial season looking to solidify his status among the world’s elite while managing the heavy physical demands of a crowded match calendar. Recognizing his immense potential and central role in the club’s sporting future, Barcelona secured his long term commitment with a contract worth €31,250,000 gross per year, equivalent to €15,000,000 net.

4. Rodri (FC Barcelona)

The Spanish midfielder became the marquee signing of the transfer window after FC Barcelona successfully won his signature following a fierce bidding war with Real Madrid. His arrival provides massive tactical quality to Barca’s midfield, earning him a financial package matching Yamal’s at €31,250,000 gross per season for a net total of €15,000,000.

3. Trent Alexander-Arnold (Real Madrid)

The English full back joined Real Madrid as the premier summer signing but is still working to show his full potential after dealing with recurring physical setbacks during his initial months in Spain. Real Madrid’s financial commitment to secure his signature places him on the podium with a gross salary of €31,670,000 per year, yielding €15,200,000 net.

2. Vinicius Jr. (Real Madrid)

Following detailed contract negotiations, the Brazilian winger agreed to financial terms that establish him as one of the highest paid players in European football. Positioned just below the top spot, the dynamic forward earns a gross annual package of €50,000,000 that provides him with an impressive net salary of €24,000,000 per season.

1. Kylian Mbappe (Real Madrid)

The French superstar reigns supreme at the very top of the LaLiga salary rankings following a sensational 2025/26 campaign where he won both the LaLiga Pichichi trophy and the UEFA Champions League top scorer award. Fresh off winning the Golden Boot at the 2026 World Cup, the forward leads all players in Spain with a staggering gross annual salary of €71,670,000 that nets him €34,400,000 per year.

Financial hierarchy shaping the competitive landscape of Spanish football

The heavy dominance of Real Madrid players at the top of the salary rankings illustrates the club’s commitment to assembling an all star squad capable of contending for major titles across all competitions. Meanwhile, FC Barcelona’s significant financial outlay to sign Rodri and secure Lamine Yamal reflects their determination to challenge for domestic supremacy, setting the stage for an intense sporting and financial battle throughout the 2026/27 campaign.
