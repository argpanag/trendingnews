Byron Fire crews urge boaters to use caution on Rock River during Labor Day weekend

TheTools · 05.09.2026 05:34 · source · permalink

(Rockford News First) - Byron Fire crews want boaters to keep safety in mind as people head to the Rock River for Labor Day weekend.

Byron Fire Protection District Battalion Chief Tim Free said changing water levels can create hazards that boaters may not always see.

“The Rock River, it’s very unpredictable. So, we have a lot of sandbars. We get a lot of debris from flooding. It’s very easy to be traveling full speed down the river and hit debris causing accidents, causing damage to your boat,” explained Free.

Free said boaters should slow down, understand the waterway and carry all safety equipment required by the Department of Natural Resources.

He also stressed the importance of life jackets, especially for children.

If a boat capsizes, Free recommends keeping a life jacket on, holding onto the boat when possible and trying to move toward shore or signal for help.

“The moment it happens. You can be swept under very quickly, carried downstream very quickly,” he said.

Longtime Rock River boater Robert Misitano spent years taking his children onto the river and plans to return to the water during the holiday weekend.

Misitano said holiday weekends can bring more activity to the river, which means boaters need to pay close attention to people around them.

“The kids and the people in the water that are off the tubes and off the skis. That’s the biggest thing to watch for and each other,” said Misitano.

Misitano said he keeps life jackets and a first-aid kit on his boat and makes sure everyone knows what to expect before heading onto the water.

He also has one rule he wants other boaters to remember this weekend.

“I would just say keep the drinking out of the water and on the shore. That’s where it belongs.”

Free also encouraged boaters to stay hydrated, take their time and avoid alcohol while operating a boat.
