Over $500 million in expansion fees for owners?

TheTools · 16.09.2026 05:50 · source · permalink

ESPN insider Brian Windhorst has shared new financial details surrounding potential NBA expansion. "I talked to an owner recently who told me that they are budgeting getting between $500 and $600 million in expansion fees," Windhorst said on The Hoop Collective podcast. "Which means they believe that the two teams will generate between $15 and $18 billion in sale." "He was talking about their team spending and their cap position, and he's like, 'Well, we've got this $500 or $600 million windfall coming on the expansion,'" Windhorst added. "Obviously, they don't have the money yet. It sure sounds like he's expecting to get it."
