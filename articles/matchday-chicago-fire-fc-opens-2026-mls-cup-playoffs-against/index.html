Match Preview

Chicago Fire FC returns to the postseason for a second consecutive year, facing Charlotte FC in the opening match of the Round One Best-of-3 Series on Sunday, Nov. 22 at SeatGeek Stadium.

Every match of the Audi 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs will be available on Apple TV, with Sunday’s Game 1 and select matches on FS1 and FOX Deportes in the U.S. All Fire postseason matches will also be transmitted locally on WLS 890 AM in English and TUDN Chicago 106.7-HD2 in Spanish. Audi MLS Cup Playoffs matches will also be available nationwide on Sirius XM FC Ch. 188.

Chicago is back in the playoffs after finishing fourth in the Eastern Conference and posting consecutive seasons with more than 50 points and 60 goals. The 2026 regular season was the third-highest scoring campaign in Club history with 64 goals, behind last year's record of 68 and the 67 scored in 2000. Leading the attack are winger Maren Haile-Selassie – who clinched a second team Golden Boot with 13 goals this season and career-high 10 assists – and newcomer Robert Lewandowski, who finished his first MLS season with nine league goals, including three in the last two matches. Backing them in defense are South African World Cup sensation Mbekezeli Mbokazi and USMNT call-up Chris Brady in goal.

Charlotte is very familiar with the postseason, having qualified for the fourth year in a row. At the core is an attack that finished fourth in the league with 66 goals in 2026, with a variety of offensive weapons at their disposal. The attack is headlined by midfielder Pep Biel, who finished with a league-leading 18 assists – one more than perennial MVP candidates Leo Messi and Evander. The Israeli duo of Idan Toklomati and Liel Abada both scored in the double-digits with Biel pulling the strings and now boast the addition of dangerous winger Allan Saint-Maximin. But the defense does not fall far behind, led by USMNT captain Tim Ream and backstopped by Kristijan Kahlina’s MLS-leading 158 saves.

Sunday will mark the first meeting of the two Eastern Conference rivals in postseason play. After dropping their inaugural matchup in 2022, Charlotte rattled off five consecutive victories against Chicago in the regular season series. But the Fire came back with four wins in a row since 2025, including the two meetings this season.

Match Notes

  • Sunday will mark Chicago’s 15th appearance in the MLS Cup Playoffs. Chicago has a record of 22-18-7 in 47 postseason matches, including a record of 5-7-4 at SeatGeek Stadium. Chicago has enjoyed recent success in Bridgeview, opening the 2025 postseason with a 3-1 win over Orlando City SC in the Eastern Conference Wild Card match on Oct. 22.
  • After starring in all but one postseason since 2019, including every playoff match in Minnesota United FC history, Robin Lod will look for his first with Chicago on Sunday. Lod has produced under pressure, scoring twice and adding three assists in 13 such games with Minnesota. Defender Noah Allen is not far behind, starting all six postseason contests for Inter Miami CF last season, including the biggest one of all – a victorious 90-minute shift in the 2025 MLS Cup final.
  • Philip Zinckernagel has enjoyed much success against Charlotte, recording two goals and two assists in his first three matches against the Crown. The Danish winger scored in each of his first two appearances at Bank of America Stadium – a 4-1 regular season victory on May 17, 2025, and a 4-3 win over the Liga MX All-Stars in the 2026 MLS All-Star Game on July 30.
  • Forward Robert Lewandowski’s two goals in his only match against Charlotte – his first two in MLS – are tied with Zinckernagel for most scored against Charlotte among the current squad. Johan Gómez scored also scored his first MLS goal against Charlotte, tied with Robin Lod, Jonathan Bamba and Mauricio Pineda. Lod leads the current team with three assists, including the game-winning assist against Charlotte on August 1.
  • Chicago Fire FC has beaten Charlotte FC in all head-to-head matches since the appointment of Director of Football & Head Coach Gregg Berhalter. After two wins by a combined score of 7-3 in 2025, the Fire defeated the Crown in both meetings this season.

Starting XI

Chicago Fire FC Director of Football and Head Coach Gregg Berhalter has named the first Starting XI of the 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs, with a goal of taking a 1-0 lead in the Round One Best-of-3 Series:

  • The first starting lineup of the 2026 postseason will see two changes from the Fire's last lineup of the regular season.
  • Jack Elliott will don the captain's armband at center back for Chicago. He'll kick off his second straight postseason with Chicago alongside Andrew Gutman in his fifth playoff start in his familiar left back spot. To the right of Gutman is Mbekezeli Mbokazi in his postseason debut, while Jonathan Dean starts at right back.
  • Robert Lewandowski will make his MLS Cup Playoffs debut at the head of the Fire attack. The winner of multiple league titles in Europe will line up alongside 2026 Fire Golden Boot holder Maren Haile-Selassie.
  • Complementing the attack on the left wing is Philip Zinckernagel, who finished with a team-leading 13 assists in the regular season. The Danish winger is joined on the right by Jonathan Bamba, who finished the regular season with four goal contributions in his last three matches.
  • Anton Salétros joins the midfield in his first postseason match, paired with Dje D'Avilla in his fourth playoff start in as many matches.
  • Chris Brady will be in goal for the third time in his postseason career after finishing the season with a career-high 14 wins.
  • Available off the bench are goalkeeper Josh Cohen; defenders Leonardo Barroso and Andreas Hanche-Olsen; midfielders André Franco, Robin Lod, Mauricio Pineda and Anzor Mekvabishvili; and forwards Puso Dithejane and Johan Gómez.
  • Defenders Sam Rogers (Lower Body) and Noah Allen (Lower Body) are not available for selection in Game 1 against Charlotte.
