Ayra Starr Set to Headline Halftime at NFL London Game - Music Industry Weekly

TheTools · 11.10.2026 11:20 · source · permalink
  • News

Afrobeats star Ayra Starr will perform a halftime show when the Philadelphia Eagles face the Jacksonville Jaguars in London on Oct. 11. The game is part of the NFL’s international series, which has increasingly paired matches with musical performances to boost crossover appeal.

The appearance adds to Ayra Starr’s growing international profile as she continues to expand beyond Afrobeats audiences into mainstream sports and entertainment programming.
