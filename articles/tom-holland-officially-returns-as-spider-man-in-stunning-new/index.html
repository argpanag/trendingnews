Tom Holland Officially Returns as Spider-Man in Stunning New Look

TheTools · 23.11.2026 15:19 · source · permalink

Tom Holland has been playing Spider-Man for a decade now, and while Peter Parker has already found himself caught up in some of the Marvel Cinematic Universe's biggest adventures, his latest solo movie has given him another chance to take center stage. Spider-Man: Brand New Day brought the character back to theaters earlier this year, with Destin Daniel Cretton taking over directing duties and a cast that includes several familiar Marvel faces. Now, with the movie preparing to return to the big screen for the holidays, fans are getting another opportunity to see Spider-Man in a format that offers a rather different perspective on his adventures.

Collider can exclusively reveal four new variant posters for Spider-Man: Brand New Day, celebrating the movie's December 4 re-release in ScreenX theaters. The film made history as the first movie shot for ScreenX, the panoramic theatrical format that extends selected scenes beyond the main screen onto the theater's side walls, and the new artwork celebrates that achievement with four very different designs.

The posters, titled "Seasons Greetings", "Small Potato," "Phoenix Fire," and "Gold Prism," all feature Spider-Man swinging through the city, although each has its own color scheme and references to other parts of the Marvel universe. The holiday design features snow and festive colors, while "Small Potato" pays tribute to Yelena Belova (Florence Pugh) and her heavily memed line from the Russian baths, “This is small baby potato stuff, not big potato brain stuff.”

'Spider-Man: Brand New Day' Posters Are Packed With Marvel Easter Eggs

The "Phoenix Fire" poster takes inspiration from Jean Grey's Phoenix form, using the familiar colors associated with her costume, while "Gold Prism" draws on the look of the popular Marvel Snap card game. Together, the four designs give fans some very different options, particularly those hoping to add another Spider-Man poster to their collections. There may also be an opportunity to own one physically, with plans being discussed for a limited print run of the "Phoenix Fire" design, although further details about availability have yet to be announced.

The movie stars Holland as Peter Parker/Spider-Man, Zendaya (Dune) as MJ, Jacob Batalon (Spider-Man: No Way Home) as Ned Leeds, Jon Bernthal (The Punisher) as Frank Castle, Mark Ruffalo (The Avengers) as Bruce Banner, and Florence Pugh (Thunderbolts) as Yelena Belova. Cretton, who previously directed Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings, took over from Jon Watts, who directed Holland's first three solo Spider-Man movies.
