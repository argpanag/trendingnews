Who Is Sitting in Elena Rybakina's Box for the US Open Women's Final vs. Aryna Sabalenka?

TheTools · 12.09.2026 20:56 · source · permalink

The 2026 US Open women’s singles final on Saturday, Sept. 12, features a massive matchup between world No. 1 seed Aryna Sabalenka and No. 2 seed Elena Rybakina set to take place at Arthur Ashe Stadium in New York.

While Sabalenka is aiming for a rare US Open three-peat and her fifth major title overall, Rybakina, who has already guaranteed she will dethrone Sabalenka for the World No. 1 ranking following the tournament, is looking to lift her second Grand Slam title of 2026.

Elena Rybakina’s player’s box at the 2026 US Open Women’s Final features her longtime head coach Stefano Vukov.

Following a brief split and a subsequently overturned WTA suspension, Vukov successfully appealed to an independent tribunal in August 2025 and rejoined her team. Alongside him is co-coach Stanislav Khmarskyi who officially integrated into her major tournament setup during the 2025 season.

Another core member of her team is her Italian strength and conditioning coach Aldo Chiari, who has been handling her fitness and physical preparation since 2025.

Sometimes her sister Anna Rybakina is also present to support her during her tournament runs.

Three years older than Elena, Anna frequently travels with her to WTA tournaments to provide a source of support. Her parents, Andrey and Ekaterina Rybakina, are typically not present in the stadium box.
