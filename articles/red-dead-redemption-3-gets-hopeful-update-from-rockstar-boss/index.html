Red Dead Redemption 3 Gets Hopeful Update From Rockstar Boss

TheTools · 18.11.2026 21:15 · source · permalink

From Screen Rant (Written by James Lynch) on November 15, 2026 | OpenCritic

Red Dead Redemption 2 is one of those games that only comes along once in a generation, striking the perfect balance and creating an experience that players are never likely to forget once it has run its course. It was also, unsurprisingly, an enormous commercial success and one of the best-selling games of all time, turning over a tidy profit for Rockstar Games and parent company Take-Two Interactive in the process.

You might therefore expect it to be a foregone conclusion that we would be getting a third game in the series at some point, but that's a little more up in the air than its success might suggest. Dan Houser and the original creative team behind the game only ever intended for it to be played out in two parts, both of which we have now had.

Though Houser is no longer involved in the company, we still haven't heard anything from official sources about what they're planning to do with the franchise next. Now the man in charge of Take-Two Interactive has weighed in on several issues and possibly given us a hint that we could get a game sooner than we were expecting.
