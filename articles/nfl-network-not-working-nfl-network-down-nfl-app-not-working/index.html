NFL Network not working. NFL Network down. NFL app not working

TheTools · 13.09.2026 18:38 · source · permalink

This might be the worst day of the year for the NFL Network to have issues.

But on Sunday about an hour before kickoff, the NFL Network had issues that continue to mount.

The NFL Network has more than 6000 reports of issues Sunday according to Downdetector. The NFL kicked off its opening Sunday at 1 p.m. The outage started around 12:30 p.m.

The outage was also trending on Google.

According to Downdetector, the biggest problem is people being able to get on the app and stream video.

There is no timetable for when the issues will be resolved.
