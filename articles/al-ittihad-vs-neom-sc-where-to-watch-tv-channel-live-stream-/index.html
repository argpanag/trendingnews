Match Preview

Al Ittihad host Neom SC in the Saudi Pro League at King Abdullah Sports City in Jeddah, with the league back after the international break.

The home side arrive in decent shape. Jens Wissing's team beat Pakhtakor Tashkent 2-0 in the AFC Champions League Elite on Monday, a result that ended a miserable run of European-style travel and fatigue.

That win followed a heavy 4-1 home defeat to Al-Ain in the same competition, so Wissing will want consistency rather than another swing in results. Al Ittihad sit fifth in the league table.

Neom SC come to Jeddah in sixth place under Christophe Galtier. They have won two of their last five league games, including a 2-1 victory at Al Diriyah, but they also lost back-to-back matches to Al Nassr and Al-Ettifaq earlier in October.

The two sides met twice last season in matches that produced seven goals apiece in total, so another open game would not be a surprise.
