1 dead, 1 critically injured after train strikes pickup truck in Maple Ridge: RCMP | CBC News

TheTools · 01.10.2026 21:41 · source · permalink

Police are asking anyone with information to come forward

Maple Ridge RCMP say a woman is dead and a man was critically injured after a pickup truck was struck by a train on Wednesday.

In a news release, RCMP said police responded to a report at about 2 p.m. PT of a motor vehicle incident involving a train in the 25700 block of Lougheed Highway in Maple Ridge.

Two people were found in the vehicle, which sustained "significant damage," according to police.

A woman was pronounced dead and a man was transported to hospital in critical condition.

Canadian Pacific Kansas City (CPKC) acknowledged the collision in an emailed statement and said the incident is under investigation.

"A freight train made contact with a passenger vehicle at a private crossing off Lougheed Highway (Hwy 7) in Maple Ridge early Wednesday afternoon," said CPKC.

The train crew was not injured and the train was moved from the area on Wednesday evening, according to the CPKC statement.

Police are asking anyone with information to contact Maple Ridge RCMP.
