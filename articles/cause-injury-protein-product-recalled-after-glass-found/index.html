‘CAUSE INJURY’: Protein product recalled after glass found

TheTools · 25.10.2026 22:01 · source · permalink

The food safety watchdog has warned consumers to not eat the products or risk serious harm.

A range of protein puddings have been pulled from supermarket shelves after glass was found in the products.

Food Standards Australia and New Zealand has issued a recall notice for batches of Arla Protein Pudding 200g in the chocolate and salted caramel flavours after the foreign matter was discovered.

Tubs of the chocolate puddings marked with the best before date 09/05/2027 and packages of the caramel flavour labelled with the best before date 28/04/2027 have been recalled.

The chocolate variety has been available for purchase at Coles in NSW and QLD, and independent food retailers including IGA and Supabarn in the NSW and ACT.

Costco in New South Wales has been selling the salted caramel puddings.

The food safety watchdog warned consumers to not eat the puddings from the recalled batches, saying glass could cause serious harm if consumed.

“Consumers should not eat this product,” the alert read.

“Food products containing glass fragments may cause illness/injury if consumed.”

Customers who purchased an affected batch are urged to return the product to the place of purchase for a refund.

“Consumers should return the products to the place of purchase for a full refund,” the statement continued.

“Any consumers concerned about their health should seek medical advice.”
