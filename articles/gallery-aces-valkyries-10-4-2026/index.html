GALLERY: Aces @ Valkyries (10/4/2026)

The Las Vegas Aces (0-1) lost the battle on the boards, and dropped Game 1 of the WNBA Semifinals 71-60 to the Golden State Valkyries (1-0) on Sunday afternoon in the Bay Area. A'ja Wilson led the Aces with 24 points, Chelsea Gray scored 16 and finished the game as the league's all-time postseason assists leader after dishing out 3 and Jackie Young chipped in 11 points to go with 4 boards, 5 assists and 3 steals.
