AHSAA News | The 2026 AHSAA Playoff Brackets are Set

TheTools · 07.11.2026 02:37 · source · permalink

Quick Links 

 

    

By: Scott Griffin

 

 

 

MONTGOMERY – The dreams of some are fulfilled, and others must wait another year. For those coaches and players that get to prepare for next week’s first round of the 61st AHSAA State Football Playoffs, it is a second season and a chance to chase the coveted blue map. This season, another champion is added as titles will be rewarded in 1A-6A and in the private classifications of Single-A and Double-AA. The games in the first round were announced Saturday at the AHSAA playoff coaches’ meetings on the campus of Alabama State. AHSAA Executive Director Heath Harmon announced the official matchups during the meeting.

Another change from last year is the new 24-team field in 6A (up from 16). That allows for first round byes in that classification with the lower total team’s numbers. Those with a break in the first week of the playoffs will be James Clemons, Auburn, Prattville, Daphne, Central-Phenix City, Sparkman, Fairhope and Thompson.

The entire 6A bracket can be viewed here: https://www.si.com/high-school/stats/alabama/football/brackets/19337

The teams in 5A and beyond have their traditional 16-team field in the North and South. Some of the exciting opening round games include Pelham (8-2) tying for their best regular season mark ever under second-year coach Ross Newton, on the road at mighty Saraland (9-1). Only two teams in the South bracket failed to win at least seven games.

In the North, as expected, more scintillating games to see, including the AHSAA Game of the Week with Muscle Shoals (6-3) living out of the suitcase against Oxford (7-2). Arab (8-2) and Minor (7-3), under first-year head coach Rico Jackson, is another to keep an eye on. Jackson coached Anniston to the 4A runner-up finish last year at 14-1.

The 5A brackets are: https://www.si.com/highschool/stats/alabama/football/brackets/19335

The same sentiment holds true in the chase for the 4A state championship. When you see first-round games like Citronelle (8-2) at Marbury (9-1), Sipsey Valley (6-3) at Andalusia (7-3) and Hamilton (8-1) at Alexandria (9-1), they have a far deeper playoff feel than opening round. The complete 4A brackets can be viewed here: https://www.si.com/high-school/stats/alabama/football/brackets/19334

In 3A, there are four unbeaten teams (Cleburne County, Bullock County, Plainview, and Hatton), plus only three teams at .500 and none with losing records. It promises to be a spirited chase for the state title in Mobile. The complete 3A brackets are here: https://www.si.com/high-school/stats/alabama/football/brackets/19333

As expected, 2A brings a load of teams capable of winning it all as well. Eleven of the 32 teams have nine wins or more and include strong openers such as J.U. Blacksher (7-3) at Reeltown (10-0).  Others to notice include Clarke County (7-3) at Lanett (7-3), Cleveland (7-3) at Fyffe (9-1), Colbert County (7-3) at Walter Wellborn (8-2) and Pleasant Valley (8-2) at Phil Campbell (7-3).  The complete bracket for 2A is here: https://www.si.com/high-school/stats/alabama/football/brackets/19332

There are only two unbeaten teams in 1A with Addison (10-0) and Winterboro (10-0), and they reside in the same North part of the bracket. That gives you an idea of the competition available in the title chase in this classification. Addison’s defense will be something to see as the Bulldogs have only given up 26 points (2.6 per game) on the year so far. That leads the AHSAA, regardless of classification. The 1A schedule is: https://www.si.com/high-school/stats/alabama/football/brackets/19331

In the inaugural season for the private school classifications, there is still drama to unfold. When you have seasoned AHSAA veterans like Briarwood (10-0), McGill-Toolen (8-2), Westbrook Christian (10-0) and Tuscaloosa Academy (8-1), then you have what looks like it will be a sensational first playoff season. The Single-A schedule is: https://www.si.com/high-school/stats/alabama/football/brackets/19338

Double-AA is as follows: https://www.si.com/high-school/stats/alabama/football/brackets/19339

A vast majority of these games will be livestreamed on the NFHS Network (www.nfhsnetwork.com) on subscription-based fees. Of course, there will be a weekly AHSAA Playoff Game of the Week each week by Alabama Public Television and WOTM-TV accessible on-line, on the APT app and statewide where WOTM-TV is shown. Check out www.wotm.tv to access those channels.
