Keri Russell Recalls the Steamy Early Days of Her Romance With Matthew Rhys on “The Americans” Set

TheTools · 14.10.2026 19:55 · source · permalink

The couple fell in love on the set of The Americans

Some on-screen romances are simply too good to leave on set. For Keri Russell and Matthew Rhys, the chemistry they shared on The Americans eventually spilled over into real life—and more than a decade later, they’re still together.

In 2012, the actors were cast as undercover spies Elizabeth and Philip Jennings in the FX political drama, and they eventually developed a romance of their own. Fast-forward many years (and a series finale) later, and the real-life couple is still going strong. Since then, Russell and Rhys have welcomed a child together and kept much of their relationship out of the spotlight.

But The Diplomat actress recently offered a rare glimpse into the early days of her romance with Rhys during an appearance on Chelsea Handler’s Dear Chelsea podcast, recalling, “We were falling in love and staying up all night and having sex all the time.” She added, “It was so fun and sneaky, and sexy, and so good.”

Ahead, a look back at Keri Russell and Matthew Rhys’s relationship timeline.

They first meet at a kickball game party

2002

During a 2017 appearance on Watch What Happens Live with Andy Cohen, Rhys shares the story of the first time he met Russell—and failed to land a date with her. The actor says he and Russell first crossed paths at a kickball game in 2002: “I very drunkenly asked for her number,” he recalls.

According to Rhys, he left her a message, but she never returned his call.

They’re both cast on The Americans

2012

A decade after their first chance encounter, Russell and Rhys reunite when they’re cast as Elizabeth and Philip Jennings in The Americans. Filming on the pilot begins in late 2012. At the time, Russell is still married to her first husband, Shane Deary, with whom she shares two children: a son, River, and a daughter, Willa.

Keri and Matthew spark romance rumors

2013

By early 2013, reports circulate that Russell has separated from her first husband, and their divorce is finalized a year later. Then, Russell and Rhys spark romance rumors when they’re spotted out together around New York City on multiple occasions.

They do a steamy photoshoot together

January 2014

Those romance rumors escalate when Russell and Rhys appear together in a very sexy photoshoot for GQ. The Americans costars channel major Mr. & Mrs. Smith energy for the photos, which feature Russell straddling Rhys in black lingerie. Their real-life romance is still not confirmed (or denied) at the time.

They’re confirmed to be dating

April 2014

People confirms that Russell and Rhys are a real-life couple after months of speculation. The news comes as the two are spotted together at the Public Theater in New York City, where they see Steven Soderbergh’s play The Library. Bystanders see Russell stroking Rhys’s hair, and the two whispering closely during the date night.

They’re expecting a baby together!

January 2016

At the start of 2016, People reports that Russell is pregnant with her and Rhys’s first child together. A source says the couple is “thrilled.” Russell also has two children with her first husband.

Keri reveals how she hid her pregnancy on The Americans

March 2016

In an interview with The New York Times, Russell discusses being pregnant at 40 while filming The Americans. The actress jokes about how the show had to get creative to hide her pregnancy, “There was a lot of winter-coat wearing,” she reveals. “We justified it, like, ‘My mind is full of so many things, and I’m so worried that I just keep my coat on, even in the bedroom.’ I held a lot of giant salad bowls.”

The same month, she’s photographed biking in New York with Rhys, with her baby bump covered by layers of cozy basics.

They welcome their first child together

May 2016

Russell and Rhys welcome a son named Sam in May 2016.

They make their official Emmys debut as a couple

September 18, 2016

Russell and Rhys make their first major red-carpet debut as a couple at the 2016 Emmy Awards. For the occasion, Russell stuns in a sweeping white Stéphane Rolland gown, while Rhys keeps things classic in a black tuxedo.

Keri reflects on how The Americans changed her life

July 2017

In an interview with People, Russell reflects on the upcoming final season of The Americans and how the series has changed her life in “many many ways” from her and Rhys’s relationship to the birth of their son, Sam. “It’s all been good change, and I’m very, very grateful for it,” she says.

While Russell admits she’s upset to see the series come to an end, she feels the timing is right. “It will be sad, but it feels kind of right too,” she says. “The hardest thing would be watching something that is so creative and well thought out to become played out. It feels like the right time to get out while it’s good, and I feel like they have a real solid last season of storylines. I feel like it’s the right time.”

Russell also opens up about what it’s like working alongside her real-life partner, acknowledging that sharing a set comes with its own challenges. “That brings its own complications—as anyone who works with a partner experiences—but I think I’ll miss it, too,” she says.

Matthew thanks Keri in his Emmys speech

September 2018

Rhys wins the Emmy Award for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama Series. During his acceptance speech, the actor shouts out Russell and jokes about proposing to her on stage. “And finally to the woman who truly got me this award who just stands in front of me every day and puts up with me,” Rhys says, “She said, ‘If you propose to me, I will punch you clean in the mouth.’”

They’re both nominated for Golden Globes for The Americans

January 6, 2019

Rhys and Russell, who both receive Golden Globe nominations for The Americans, joke with E! on the red carpet about what they’ll do if they both don’t win.

“We’ve discussed that, whether, in fact, one of us wins,” Rhys says. Russell jokes, “We break up.” Rhys agrees, “Yeah, if you win, I’m gone. If I win, you’re gone.”

They reunite on screen in Cocaine Bear.

March 2023

For the first time since The Americans, Russell and Rhys reunite on screen in the 2023 movie Cocaine Bear. During an interview with Conan O’Brien, Rhys shares that he asked Russell (who was already starring in the movie) to text director Elizabeth Banks to see if he could play a role in the film after reading the script.

He jokes to the A.V. Club that he used Russell to land his cameo: “I unashamedly jumped on my partner’s coattails.”

They attend the 2024 Golden Globes together

January 7, 2024

Kicking off 2024, Russell and Rhys attend the Golden Globe Awards together. The Diplomat actress looks ethereal in a white fringe Jil Sander gown, while the actor keeps it classic in a black tux and bowtie.

Keri discusses asking more of Matthew at home

October 2024

In an interview with Variety, Russell reveals she asked Rhys to take on more responsibility at home when she accepted her role on The Diplomat.

“I have done the birthday cupcakes,” she recalls telling Rhys. “I have done the doctor’s appointments and the scary nights and the washing laundry. And I have been there. I’ve done the back rubs, but I want three months—or a fucking year—where I don’t. It’s someone else’s turn—pick up the slack.’”

Rhys agreed. “And he did. And it was hard,” she said. “You know, Matthew was shooting at the time, too, and I’m sure that was hard, because he was trying to fly back and forth. And I was like, ‘Yeah, it’s hard—it’s called being a mom.’”

They walk the Emmys red carpet hand in hand

September 14, 2025

While attending the 2025 Emmys together, Russell and Rhys hold hands on the red carpet. As they pose for photos, they share plenty of sweet PDA moments in their coordinating all-black ensembles.

Matthew supports Keri at a screening of The Diplomat

October 14, 2025

Rhys joins Russell at a special screening of her Netflix show, The Diplomat, in New York City.

Keri supports Matthew at his Widow’s Bay premiere

April 22, 2026

Russell supports Rhys at the New York premiere of his Apple TV series, Widow’s Bay. The couple poses together on the red carpet, with Russell wearing an oversized blazer with a matching cropped top and tailored Bermuda shorts and Rhys wearing a brown jacket and trousers tucked into rubber boots.

The couple gives a rare joint interview

August 12, 2026

Russell and Rhys join forces for an interview with Variety ahead of the 2026 Emmys, where they’re both nominated for separate projects. In addition to discussing their respective TV shoes (The Diplomat and Widow’s Bay), they discuss the possibility of someday co-starring together again after meeting on The Americans. “It would have to be the right thing for many reasons,” Russell says. “There’s a real electricity that happens between people when it goes well—and that was such a part of our formative experience together.”

They also reflect on the 2018 Emmys, when Rhys joked about proposing to Russell on stage following Glenn Weiss proposing to his girlfriend on the show’s stage that night.

Russell confirms she would have hated it if Rhys proposed to her on stage. “For me to have to do anything public is horrifying,” Russell says. Had Rhys proposed, “I would die. I would say no. Good night!”

They attend the 2026 Emmys together

September 14, 2026

The couple goes for coordinating all-black looks at the 2026 Emmys. Russell opts for an edgy black leather Khaite gown, while Rhys wears a classic three-piece suit. Both actors are nominated—Rhys for Widow’s Bay and The Beast in Me and Russell for The Diplomat.

Keri recalls the steamy start of her romance with Matthew

October 13, 2026

During an episode of Chelsea Handler’s Dear Chelsea podcast, Russell shares more about the early days of her romance with Rhys on the set of The Americans.

The actress confesses that she and Rhys, who met when the show began in 2013, were “having sex all the time,” on set in their characters’ costumes.

“We were falling in love and staying up all night and having sex all the time and just wearing disguises, like, I’m fucking [in] crazy wigs and wearing pearls,” Russell reveals. “When have you ever worn pearls and [been] fucking in trailers and fucking everywhere? I mean, it was insanity. It was so fun and sneaky, and sexy, and so good.”

In the same interview, Russell also shares more about why she and Rhys haven’t tied the knot yet. “I’ve had a big party,” she explains. “I get to dress up all the time, and I know for a lot of people, marriage is something different.”

“People used to be married to have a credit card,” she adds. “We can have credit cards now. I don’t need my last name to go away. Like, I’m cool. If you want to be here, be here. If you don’t, that’s cool too.”
