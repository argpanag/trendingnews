Man City 2-2 Napoli: Ayyoub Bouaddi and Rayan Cherki score for hosts

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:05 · source · permalink
  • Report (active)
  • Scores

  • Live Text

Ayyoub Bouaddi scored his first goal for Manchester City since joining from Lille in the summer

Rayan Cherki rescued a late point but Manchester City's poor run of form continued as they were held to a draw at home in the Champions League by Napoli.

Enzo Maresca's side started the season in perfect fashion but have dropped off dramatically since, now having won just one of their past seven games in all competitions.

City had chances in the early stages. Elliot Anderson marauded forward and saw a low strike in the opening minute batted away by Vanja Milinkovic-Savic, before Nico O'Reilly headed over from close range.

But the opportunities dried up and it was Napoli who almost broke the deadlock on the stroke of half-time when Sam Beukema rose highest to meet Kevin de Bruyne's corner, but the header was kept out by Gianluigi Donnarumma's sharp save.

Against the run of play, the visitors opened the scoring in the second half as former Manchester United striker Rasmus Hojlund turned Marc Guehi inside out before drilling home an unerring finish.

City boss Maresca sent on Jeremy Doku and the Belgium international was the catalyst for the equaliser, picking out Ayyoub Bouaddi in the box for the Moroccan teenager to stroke home his first goal for the club.

But the Serie A side restored their lead on 76 minutes courtesy of Noa Lang smacking home a sensational volley from Leonardo Spinazzola's cross towards the far post.

The home supporters were becoming frustrated and Josko Gvardiol rattled the post as they searched for an equaliser, but Cherki showed quick footwork and drilled low into the bottom corner to salvage a draw.

Man City ratings

Gianluigi Donnarumma: Made an important save at the end of the first half to deny Sam Beukema but could do little about either goal. 6

Matheus Nunes: Returned to the side after injury and provides City with more width. 6

Marc Guehi: Poor defending for the first goal, Didn't know which way to turn and was punished by Hojlund. 5

Josko Gvardiol: Played in his preferred centre-back role, the Croatian looked more comfortable in that position, contributing two tackles and two interceptions. 6

Nico O'Reilly: Left a gaping hole for Napoli's second goal after trying to nick the ball up the pitch. 5

Ayyoub Bouaddi: Starting to gain confidence. Steady in the middle of the park and always unruffled, before scoring the equaliser. 8

Elliot Anderson: The England international played further forward and proved to be a goal threat, forcing Milinkovic-Savic into a couple of decent saves. 7

Enzo Fernandez: Disappointing. Restored to the starting line-up after being dropped at the weekend but the Argentine needs to start performing and living up to his hefty price tag. 5

Allan: Quiet. Given a rare start but saw little of the ball and was substituted early in the second half. 5

Rayan Cherki: Attempted to pull of his box of tricks and was rewarded for his perseverance with the equaliser. 7

Erling Haaland: Captain for the night, he was visibly frustrated by the lack of chances early on and it didn't get any better as the match wore on. 6

Subs:

Jeremy Doku (on 54, Allan) Lifted the place after coming on and was a constant threat. Provided the assist for Bouaddi's goal and one of his better outings. 7

Iliman Ndiaye (on 63, Fernandez) Lovely ball slipped through for Cherki's late leveller. 7

Subs not used: Rulli, Dias, Kovacic, Ait-Nouri, Reis, Khusanov, Foden, McAidoo, Samba, Lewis.
