Wrexham pair Broadhead and Sheaf available after injury

TheTools · 08.09.2026 18:35 · source · permalink

NATHAN BROADHEAD and Ben Sheaf have returned to training and could be included in the squad for tonight's Championship clash against Burnley at The Racecourse.

Wales international Broadhead has missed the last two games while midfielder Sheaf hasn't featured yet this season but the duo are close to returning.

"Sheafy and Broady were back in training, so really pleased with that and we want as many options as we can going into this next run of games," said Reds' boss Phil Parkinson, who is still without Libby Cacace and George Thomason.

"We'll decide today with them, but they both got through okay.

"Broady's only been out a couple of weeks. I thought he had a really good pre-season broadly, to be fair.

"He showed some real quality. The moment of a goal didn't quite come for him in pre-season, but he was unlucky.

"The keepers made some great saves over in America and he's hit the bar, but some of his play and his build-up play was really good.

"He's had seven-ten days out, but he's done some work over the weekend and he's joined in with the lads."

Recent signing Callum O'Hare and wing-back Ryan Longman picked up knocks in Saturday's goalless draw at Swansea City but neither are serious.

"No fresh injuries," added Parkinson. "Callum got a nasty whack in that challenge which we've got to look at and Longy's got a bit of a dead leg.

"There's a couple of things which you're always going to get after a game, especially the competitive nature of the match."
