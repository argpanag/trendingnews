Chicago Dealer Selling Brand-New Dodge Challenger SRT Demon 170

TheTools · 08.09.2026 00:06 · source · permalink

And it is usually a Hellcat that captures our attention, regardless of the configuration, because, in case you forgot, even the most basic version came with over 700 brake horsepower straight from the factory. Superior versions had more than 800 horsepower to play with, and among the series, one version stood out in terms of output and performance. We are talking about the one and only Dodge Challenger SRT Demon 170, a devilish muscle car born for drag racing.

The model opened the gates of hell in the first half of 2023, limited to 3,300 units in total, including 3,000 for our market and 300 destined for Canada. Production ended in late 2023, though this model remains alive and well in today’s exciting car world, where it is not uncommon at all to see various examples doing what they were born to do, namely put their money where their mouth is in a straight-line acceleration at drag strips all over the world.

For a rare product, there are quite a few units of the Dodge Challenger SRT Demon 170 that are searching for new homes on the used car market at any given time. Usually, this model starts at north of the $125,000 mark, with the lightly driven examples fetching up to $140,000. Those in collector grade condition with only the delivery miles under its belt are known to fetch between $145,000 and $185,000 in today’s market.

This further proves that we are dealing with a brand-new example, a muscle car that deserves to be pushed to its limits at the drag strip rather than kept in a climate-controlled garage, because as cool as it may be, it was born for daily drives, and to teach a lesson about straight-line acceleration sprints to full-blown exotics, proving that you can own a car that’s faster in a straight line and costs a fraction of the price compared to real supercars. Let’s not forget that this model started at $96,666 when it was still available at Dodge dealers.

The selling dealer behind this ad did not reveal the magic numbers, but as everyone and their pet can tell you, the Dodge Challenger SRT Demon 170 featured a heavily upgraded version of the Hellcat lump. The 6.2-liter supercharged V8 pumped out a whopping 1,,025 bhp (765 kW/1,040 ps) and 945 lb-ft (1,280 Nm) of torque on E85. On E10, the mill was rated at 900 bhp (671 kW/913 ps) and 810 lb-ft (1,097 Nm) of torque.

We’ve told you what we know about the pictured Dodge Challenger SRT Demon 170, and we saved one of the most important aspects for the end of the story. You do know what that one is, don’t you? Yep, the asking price. Keep in mind that a still-new example fetches between $145K and $185K in today’s used car market, so how much do you think this one costs? Surprisingly, a bit less than that, because the asking price at the time of writing was $139,995.

That does not make it a bargain, obviously, because that kind of money opens up a whole new world of exciting machines on the used car market, but then again, the Dodge Challenger SRT Demon 170 is definitely a lot of car for the money, and this one likely retains that new-car smell, if you are into that. So, if you were shopping for a very cool muscle car and had a budget of almost $150K, would you blow it on this Demon 170?
