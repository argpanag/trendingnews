Final Four race goes down to the wire as UAAP men's basketball elims conclude

TheTools · 27.11.2026 10:21 · source · permalink

The race for the remaining Final Four spots and the coveted twice-to-beat incentives takes center stage as the UAAP Season 89 collegiate men’s basketball eliminations reach their climax Saturday at the Smart Araneta Coliseum.

Far Eastern University and De La Salle University battle for one of the two remaining Final Four berths at 2:30 p.m., while National University and the University of the Philippines collide at 5 p.m. with the top seed and the first twice-to-beat advantage on the line.

With all four teams still having something to play for, the results of Saturday’s doubleheader will determine the final playoff picture heading into Wednesday’s games at the Philsports Arena.

The Tamaraws and the Green Archers, both carrying identical 8-5 records, face off in a virtual knockout game for the third semifinal berth and a chance to remain in contention for the other twice-to-beat incentive.

The winner will advance directly to the Final Four, while the loser will have to take on University of Santo Tomas.

UST forced that do-or-die scenario after defeating Adamson on Wednesday to finish the eliminations with an 8-6 record. The Tigers will await the outcome of the FEU-La Salle showdown before knowing their next opponent.

A victory would also mark FEU’s first Final Four appearance since the 2022 bubble tournament, giving the Tamaraws another opportunity to reestablish themselves among the league’s contenders.

“Now, we’ve put ourselves in a good situation of controlling our own destiny. That game on Saturday is still a must-win. At the end of the day, in order for us to belong, we have to beat NU, La Salle, UP, to show that we’re actually getting to the level of respect that we’re trying to get at FEU and try to bring back the glory days,” said third-year coach Sean Chambers.

FEU already has the upper hand in its head-to-head matchup, having defeated La Salle, 90-78, during its strong 4-1 start to the first round.

The Tamaraws will once again lean on their depth, particularly their backcourt tandem of reigning MVP Janrey Pasaol and Jorick Bautista, against a Green Archers squad hoping to get a healthy Jacob Cortez back to full strength.

Cortez, who has formed a promising partnership with rookie Joaquin Tovera, believes he is hitting his stride at the right time as La Salle attempts to keep its back-to-back championship bid alive and secure its fourth consecutive Final Four appearance.

“I think I’m peaking at the right time, and it’s because of our strength and conditioning staff. Hopefully, I can take this momentum coming into the next game, and hopefully, into the Final Four,” Cortez said.

Meanwhile, the winner of the NU-UP showdown will claim the top seed and the first twice-to-beat incentive, avoiding a potentially difficult route to the championship.

Both the Bulldogs and the Fighting Maroons enter the contest with identical 9-4 records after missing their first opportunity to secure the coveted advantage with respective losses to FEU and La Salle on Sunday.

For NU, finishing atop the standings would provide an opportunity to make amends for last season’s disappointing Final Four campaign, when the Bulldogs squandered their twice-to-beat advantage and lost twice to La Salle.

UP, on the other hand, is looking to preserve its remarkable consistency by extending its streak of Top 2 finishes in the eliminations to seven consecutive seasons.

The Fighting Maroons are making their third attempt to secure the twice-to-beat incentive after also falling to FEU in their first bid.

Standing in their way is an NU squad determined to complete a season sweep after claiming a 72-64 victory in their first-round meeting.

The loser of the NU-UP clash will still have another opportunity to secure the other twice-to-beat incentive, facing the winner of the FEU-La Salle showdown in a playoff on Wednesday.

With a Final Four berth, the top seed, and both twice-to-beat advantages still up for grabs, Saturday’s doubleheader promises to shape the entire postseason picture.

Luis Licas is a sportswriter from the Philippines. He is currently taking up AB Communication at the Ateneo de Manila University. He writes for Ateneo’s official school publication The GUIDON, where he covers basketball, football, baseball, volleyball, and other collegiate sports.
