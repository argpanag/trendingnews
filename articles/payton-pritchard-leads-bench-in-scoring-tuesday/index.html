Payton Pritchard leads bench in scoring Tuesday

TheTools · 11.11.2026 04:20 · source · permalink

by Leo Sells | Celtics Correspondent | Tue, Nov 10th 2026, 11:20pm EST

Payton Pritchard scored 18 points (7-15 FG, 4-8 3PT) to go with six rebounds and five assists in Tuesday's 123-101 win over the Philadelphia 76ers.

Fantasy Impact:

Pritchard was effective on Tuesday, despite coming off of the bench. He was able to lead the second unit with 18 points and six rebounds over the course of 20 minutes, which also left him with a very respectable fantasy total in this big win over the 76ers.
