United vs. 36ers Predictions - NBL Round 9 2026-27

TheTools · 10.11.2026 07:18 · source · permalink

Match Details

Date: Thursday, 12 November 2026
Start Time: 7:30 PM (AEST)
Stadium: Adelaide Entertainment Centre, Adelaide

Melbourne United are the $1.51 favourites to keep their winning streak alive against the Adelaide 36ers on Thursday night. A good game is expected in Adelaide, where superstars Bryce Cotton and Cole Anthony are expected to battle all night.

Best Bet

United -4.5 @ $1.90

We expected United to go on a winning run, and after posting four consecutive wins over the Bullets, Breakers, Taipans, and Kings, they’re now eyeing the 36ers.

Beating the 36ers in Adelaide isn’t an easy task, but it’s mostly a case of slowing down Cotton. If they can limit Cotton to under 30 points, they should win, and Cotton comes off a 22-point performance against the Hawks.

He’s a superstar, but so is Cole Anthony, who dropped 32 points against the Bullets last week. United scored 120 points in that win, and if their offence is anywhere near that level, we can’t see Adelaide doing enough to keep up.

United’s defence can lock down teams, and that was the case when they met last month. United held Adelaide to 91 points, and if that happens again, they should win and cover the -4.5 spread, so take the line before it moves.

Same Game Multi

United -4.5, Josh Oduro 20+ Points, Chris Goulding 2+ Assists, Total Points Under 187.5 @ $12.50

Most of the attention goes to Anthony and Cotton, but don’t overlook Josh Oduro for United. He is playing at an elite level, having scored 28 points in his last game, and $1.98 for 20+ points this week is a good price.

Chris Goulding isn’t a huge assist player, but his role has changed for United. He’s not a primary, or even secondary scorer, anymore. Therefore, he could pass up some shots to find open players, so we’re backing him to record 2+ assists.

We expect United’s defence to stand up, so we’re taking under 187.5 points to boost payouts.

Best Value Bet

United 11+ @ $2.95

Beating a confident United team isn’t easy, and although Adelaide has the advantage of playing at home, they could struggle to produce the goods.

We see United getting out to a fast start and putting the 36ers under pressure. That pressure could see them fold in the second half, and there is enough value in the $2.95 odds to include United 11+ in NBL picks this week.
