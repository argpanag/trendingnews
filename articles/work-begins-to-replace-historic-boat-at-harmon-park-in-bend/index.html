Work begins to replace historic boat at Harmon Park in Bend

TheTools · 16.11.2026 19:52 · source · permalink

BEND, Ore. (KTVZ) -- The Bend Park and Recreation District is taking steps to install a new boat-themed piece of playground equipment at Harmon Park in Bend, Oregon. The initiative aims to replace a historic play structure that was removed earlier this year due to age and safety concerns.

For more than 50 years, a previous metal boat at Harmon Park provided a unique opportunity for imaginative play and became a landmark for generations of local families. Known as the P.S. Happy Harmon, the structure began as a lifeboat that park employee Ken Lehto converted into a tugboat in 1974. Its presence in the community earned Harmon Park the nickname “the boat park” before district officials removed it in summer 2026.

To replace the landmark, the park district is working with three playground representatives and their respective design teams. Each team will develop one boat-themed concept using the same project parameters and design requirements. BPRD will review the submitted concept renderings for consistency before posting them on the project webpage.

Ian Isaacson, project manager and landscape architect for the park district, emphasized the importance of maintaining the site's legacy.

“BPRD recognizes the special meaning and cherished memories the boat held for so many members of the community and understands the importance in carrying forward Harmon Park's tradition of boat-themed play by installing a new boat-themed play structure in its place,” Isaacson said.

Through the online project page, community members will be able to review the designs, complete a preference survey, sign up for email updates and submit feedback. Public input will help inform the district's selection and identify potential refinements to the chosen concept. Isaacson noted that any modifications would remain within the project budget, available space and design requirements.

Design concepts are expected to be available for public review and feedback by the end of 2026. BPRD tentatively plans to install the new boat play feature in summer 2027.
