National Assembly discusses draft law amending 3 tax laws | HTV News, November 6, 2026

TheTools · 06.11.2026 17:58 · source · permalink

(HTV) - The 8 PM news bulletin - November 6, 2026 brings you the following noteworthy news information.

The National Assembly discusses the draft law amending three tax laws.

On the morning of November 6th, the National Assembly held a plenary session to discuss the draft Law amending and supplementing a number of articles of the Law on Corporate Income Tax, the Law on Non- Agricultural Land Use Tax, and the Law on Personal Income Tax.

The National Assembly discussed amendments to tax laws on the morning of November 6th.

The 10th National Congress of Buddhist Delegates: Election of leadership personnel for the new term of the Buddhist Church.

The 10th National Congress of Buddhist Delegates, for the term 2026-2031, held its first working session with many important contents, focusing on the personnel work for the leadership of the Church in the new term.

The Ho Chi Minh City Party Committee presented Party badges in Phuoc Thanh commune.

The Party Committee of Phuoc Thanh commune also organized a ceremony to present Party badges on November 7th. Attending the ceremony was Comrade Duong Anh Duc - Member of the Standing Committee of the City Party Committee, Secretary of the Party Committee of Universities and Colleges in Ho Chi Minh City.
