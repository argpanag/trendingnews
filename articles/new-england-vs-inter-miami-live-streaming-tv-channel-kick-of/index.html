New England vs Inter Miami LIVE streaming, TV channel, kick-off time & where to watch MLS 2026

MLS- Major League Soccer

Both sides will fight for second spot in the table tonight.

When the New England Revolution visit Inter Miami in Major League Soccer (MLS) tonight at Gillette Stadium in Foxborough, they will be hoping to secure second place in the Eastern Conference.

After defeating the New York Red Bulls 3-2 in the middle of the week, the Revs are now in second place in the East, two points ahead of Miami, who drew 2-2 with FC Cincinnati at home.

As they approach the MLS Cup playoffs, the Revolution are gaining some good momentum after making significant progress in the past year.

With a victory on Sunday, Marko Mitrovic’s team will advance to the postseason as the second seed in the East and make their first postseason appearance since 2023.

Miami may lose second position in the East as a result of a slight setback they experienced in the latter part of October.

The only way they can do that is if the current MLS Cup winners finish the regular season with more points than the Revs, who have three more victories and may use that as the first tiebreaker if necessary.

On Sunday, Kily Gonzalez’s team will attempt to end a two-match losing streak in Major League Soccer and avoid losing back-to-back domestic road games for the first time this season.
