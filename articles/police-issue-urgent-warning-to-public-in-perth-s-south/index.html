Police issue urgent warning to public in Perth’s south

TheTools · 14.11.2026 01:31 · source · permalink

A major emergency response is unfolding in Perth’s south due to a “serious incident”.

Significant police resources were sent to Keysbrook, about 73km south of Perth, on Saturday morning

One person has been arrested at the scene and is “assisting with inquiries”, a police spokesman said.

“There is no ongoing threat to public safety,” he said.

“The investigation remains ongoing.”
