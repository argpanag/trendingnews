Simon Cowell's fiancée 'ashamed and embarrassed' over affair

TheTools · 08.10.2026 21:15 · source · permalink

Simon Cowell's fiancée says she felt "ashamed and embarrassed" by the affair that sparked their relationship.

Lauren Silverman has spoken candidly about the affair that led to the couple's relationship, admitting she felt "ashamed and embarrassed" by the backlash that followed.

The couple, who own a home in West Oxfordshire, have been together for more than a decade and share a son, Eric, who was born in 2014.

According to Female First, Ms Silverman reflected on the controversy surrounding the start of their relationship in an interview with The Sun's Fabulous magazine.

She became pregnant with Mr Cowell's son while still married to businessman Andrew Silverman, with whom she also shares an older son.

The 48-year-old said:"I felt ashamed and embarrassed, like I had a scarlet letter on my chest."

She added: "For a long time I struggled a lot with it. I get why people had that opinion of me. I would feel exactly the same way if I was looking in as an outsider."

Ms Silverman said she does not seek to avoid responsibility for what happened but feels she has spent enough time dwelling on the past.

She said: "I'm not trying to evade accountability or say that what I did was correct, but I'm human, and it happened. What am I supposed to do for the rest of my life? F*ing beat myself up over it? I don't think that's fair."

She continued:"I feel a lot of compassion for that girl. It makes me want to hug my former self. I beat myself up so much and allowed what other people were saying about me to define me."

Ms Silverman had been married to Andrew Silverman for 10 years when their marriage broke down. In July 2013, he filed for divorce, citing adultery, according to the report.

The article also referenced previous comments from Simon Cowell, who admitted he had felt guilty about the circumstances surrounding the affair.

Speaking previously to the Daily Mail's weekend magazine, the music mogul said: "I let down a friend and I feel bad about that, but the good thing it is that I'm having a baby and I never thought I would."

He added: "And it totally changed everything. It was like coming out of a fog; that's the only way I can describe it."

Mr Cowell and Ms Silverman became engaged in December 2021, having remained together since their relationship first made headlines more than a decade ago.

The couple share son Eric, who was born in 2014, and have frequently spoken about how parenthood changed their lives.

They own a home in West Oxfordshire and have been together since their relationship became public in 2013.

They have since divided their time between London, the United States and their home in West Oxfordshire.
