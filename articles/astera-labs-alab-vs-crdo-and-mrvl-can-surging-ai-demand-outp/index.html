Before getting into our analysis, let's take a look at the business and why some investors like the stock so much.

Astera Labs, Inc. (NASDAQ:ALAB) provides semiconductor-based connectivity solutions for cloud and artificial intelligence infrastructure. As AI data centers pack in more GPUs, processors, and memory, moving data between them quickly and without errors becomes harder to manage. Astera Labs, Inc.'s chip lineup — Aries, Taurus, Leo, and Scorpio — is built to solve exactly that problem, which is why AI infrastructure is the company's biggest growth driver.

In the twelve months through the second quarter of fiscal 2026, revenue jumped 98.5% year over year to $1.2 billion, far ahead of the peer median growth rate of 28.7%. The company is also highly profitable, with a 75.1% gross margin, 22.8% operating margin, and 30.7% net margin. Even so, the stock trades at a steep 142.9 times trailing earnings and 72.3 times forward earnings, meaning investors are pricing in several more years of strong execution.

The bull case rests on the idea that as AI systems scale, connectivity problems get harder to solve, and that's where Astera Labs, Inc. fits in. Once a hyperscaler like Amazon.com, Inc. (NASDAQ:AMZN) builds Astera's chips into a server design, such as its Trainium AI chips, switching suppliers becomes costly and slow, giving Astera some staying power even as Credo Technology Group (NASDAQ:CRDO) and Marvell Technology (NASDAQ:MRVL) pour resources into competing.
