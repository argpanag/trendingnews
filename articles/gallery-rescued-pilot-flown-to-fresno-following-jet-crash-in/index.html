FRESNO, Calif. (FOX26) — A military training fighter jet crashed into the waters off Morro Bay on Tuesday night, prompting a rescue that brought the pilot to a Fresno hospital.

The aircraft, identified as a Hawker Hunter MK-58 with the pilot call sign Snake4, took off from Point Mugu Naval Air Station and went down at 6:49 p.m.

The U.S. Coast Guard said the crash occurred roughly six miles northwest of Morro Bay.

The Coast Guard said the pilot was rescued by a Naval Air Station Lemoore UH-60 search-and-rescue helicopter with a rescue swimmer on board.

The pilot was then flown to Fresno’s Community Hospital in downtown Fresno, with the helicopter touching down about 8:20 p.m.

Before the jet hit the water, the pilot radioed a Mayday reporting a complete loss of engine thrust and ejected, according to the information provided.

The aircraft is owned by Airborne Tactical Advantage Company (ATAC), which plays an aggressor role at a variety of locations, including Point Mugu.

John Rupp, Director of Global Military Sales at ATAC, called FOX26 with an update on its pilot.

The pilot is doing well. He is still at Community Hospital receiving tests.

All of ATAC's pilots are former military jet pilots with many flying hours. He could not go into what exactly the mission was, but it did involve both the Navy and Marine Corps.

It's too early in the investigation to know if the aircraft will be recovered, how deep it is, or the cost to retrieve it.

Rupp wanted people to know that he and the company are very grateful for the exemplary care its pilot is receiving at CRMC.
