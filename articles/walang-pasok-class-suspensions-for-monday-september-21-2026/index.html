WALANG PASOK: Class suspensions for Monday, September 21, 2026

TheTools · 22.09.2026 21:58 · source · permalink

Classes for Monday, September 21, 2026, have been suspended in some areas in observance of the 54th anniversary of the declaration of Martial Law or for other reasons including school safety and air quality.

Below are the class suspensions for Monday, September 21, 2026:

Metro Manila

  • Manila: face-to-face classes suspended, all levels, public and private. Shift to Alternative Delivery Mode

Cebu

  • Catmon: no classes, all levels, public and private
  • Carmen: no face-to-face classes, all levels, public and private
  • Cebu Province: No face-to-face classes
  • Cebu City: face-to-face classes suspended, all levels, public and private due to an "acutely unhealthy" Air Quality Index (AQI) at 4 p.m. on Sunday; atmospheric conditions expected to remain at unhealthy levels on Monday
  • Talisay City: face-to-face classes suspended, all levels, public, due to "acutely unhealthy" air quality; for private schools and Talisay City College, decision to suspend will be up to the school administrations
  • Mandaue City: No face-to-face classes, all levels, public and private
  • Minglanilla: No face-to-face classes
  • Consolacion: No face-to-face classes, all levels, public and private
  • Lapu-Lapu: no face-to-face classes, day care-high school, public and private
  • Liloan: No face-to-face classes, all levels, public and private
  • Toledo: no face-to-face classes, all levels, public and private

South Cotabato

  • Banga: all levels, public and private in the aftermath of the shooting at Banga National High School
  • Koronadal: no face-to-face classes, all levels, public and private
  • Lake Sebu: face-to-face classes, all levels, public and private
  • Sto. Niño: face-to-face classes, Elementary to Senior High School, public and private
  • Surallah: no face-to-face classes, elementary to senior high school, public and private
  • Tantangan: face-to-face classes, all levels, public and private (September 21 and 22, 2026)

Sultan Kudarat

  • Lebak: face-to-face classes suspended and all activities including practices that require in-person attendance in school premises are suspended, at all levels from pre-school to tertiary, including post-graduate studies and vocational courses, public and private, due to imminent threat to school safety and public security. Shift to Alternative Delivery Mode.
  • Sen. Ninoy Aquino: face-to-face classes suspended, all levels, public and private. Shift to Alternative Delivery Mode (Modular)
  • Palimbang: face-to-face classes suspended, all levels, public and private. Shift to Alternative Delivery Mode (Modular)

Refresh this page for updates. —KG/RF/BM/BAP, GMA News
