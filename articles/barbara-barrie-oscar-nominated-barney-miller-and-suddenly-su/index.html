Barbara Barrie, Oscar-nominated 'Barney Miller' and 'Suddenly Susan' actress, dead at 95

TheTools · 29.10.2026 09:46 · source · permalink

The Broadway and screen veteran died with medical assistance after a terminal cancer diagnosis, according to a report

Barbara Barrie played Hal Linden's wife on "Barney Miller." (Disney General Entertainment Content via Getty Images)

Barbara Barrie has died at age 95 after a terminal cancer diagnosis, according to a report.

The "Barney Miller" actress’s death at her home in New York City was medically assisted, her daughter told The New York Times.

The Oscar-nominated actress’s TV and film career spanned multiple decades from the 1950s until last year.

Barbara Barrie, 95, died with medical assistance this week after a terminal cancer diagnosis. (Disney General Entertainment Content via Getty Images)

Barrie was known for her small screen roles as Barney Miller’s wife on "Barney Miller," a police series that ran from 1975 to 1982, and as Brooke Shields’ grandmother on "Suddenly Susan."

She was nominated for an Academy Award for 1979’s "Breaking Away," and she won best actress at the Cannes Film Festival for 1964’s "One Potato, Two Potato."

She lost to Meryl Streep for "Kramer vs. Kramer" at the Oscars.

She also played Goldie Hawn’s mother in 1980’s "Private Benjamin."

Barrie was also a prolific Broadway actress who was nominated for a Tony for her performance in the original 1970 cast of Stephen Sondheim’s "Company."

Barrie told the Westside Rag in 2024 she has been acting for 55 years, and "I think it took 45 years to become a good actor. I think it takes that long. You strip away everything. You strip away all the junk. You strip away all the egos. You strip away all the temperament, and you just deal with the person you’re playing. That’s all you have to do, but it takes years to learn that."
