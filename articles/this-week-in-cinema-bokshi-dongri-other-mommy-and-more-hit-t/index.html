From the misty forests of Sikkim to Mumbai’s underworld, this week’s theatre releases bring horror, crime, supernatural suspense and comedy to the big screen. Bokshi follows a troubled teenager confronting ancient witchcraft and a cursed lineage. Dongri: Gangster’s Paradise takes audiences into Mumbai’s dangerous gangster world. Jessica Chastain starrer Other Mommy delivers supernatural horror, while Tamil sci-fi thriller Nova – Phase 1: The Arrival explores a mysterious mountain mansion and a dark 1969 connection. Marathi comedy Afra Tafri adds family chaos, three brothers and a one-crore-rupee insurance policy to the mix. Watch Business Today TV’s This Week In Cinema for the latest theatrical releases.
