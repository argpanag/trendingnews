Where is GTA 6 Online Multiplayer? GTA Online 2 Release Window

TheTools · 19.11.2026 15:33 · source · permalink

Rockstar’s multiplayer plans in recent GTA games have shifted quite dramatically from the fourth main game to GTA 6. The single-player for GTA 6 is weeks and months of gameplay in itself, but the community will eventually want online multiplayer.

GTA Online was the perfect way to extend GTA 5’s life for years. Now, Grand Theft Auto 6 has enough content to provide diverse and divergent gameplay that you can easily get hundreds, if not thousands, of hours out of it. On the other hand, people like multiplayer.

Does GTA 6 Have Online Multiplayer?

No, GTA 6 did not launch with any sort of online multiplayer component, and it’s strictly a single-player experience.

For context, GTA 4 DID launch with online multiplayer. Whereas GTA 5 didn’t, and it was two weeks after launch until GTA Online went live.

From what we understand, Grand Theft Auto 6 is taking a slightly different direction.

Why Doesn’t GTA 6 Have Online Multiplayer?

While not explicitly stated by Rockstar Games or Take-Two, the most logical reason GTA 6 hasn’t launched with multiplayer is so players can enjoy the rich world of GTA 6 first.

It’s a common trend you might have seen with Call of Duty games. Once developers realised players were skipping the newest CoD campaign to jump into multiplayer instead, leaving that hard work untouched, Activision started making the campaign available earlier, usually through pre-order incentives and special editions to give CoD fans a little shove in the direction of the campaign first.

It feels like the same thing has happened with GTA 6. Encourage players to spend many hours in the campaign, and release multiplayer much later on. Speaking of which…

When is Online Multiplayer Coming to GTA 6? Expected Release Window

Again, there’s no concrete date, but GTA Online 2 is expected to launch in 2027 at some point.

You know Rockstar; it likes keeping things tight to its chest. The developer hasn’t publicly revealed a release date, but we know it’s coming, and even Twitch’s CEO expects GTA 6 Online to launch next year in 2027.
