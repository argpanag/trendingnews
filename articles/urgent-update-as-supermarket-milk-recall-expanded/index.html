Urgent update as supermarket milk recall expanded

TheTools · 26.10.2026 21:30 · source · permalink

Consumers have been warned not to consume the product and return it for a full refund.

Aldi has updated its recall on one of its milk products that was pulled from shelves last week.

The supermarket issued an alert on Friday afternoon that its Farmdale Full Cream Long Life Milk 1L was contaminated with microbes and, as a result, was spoiled.

The original recall indicated that the affected batches were located in Victoria and South Australia. However, Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ) advised that another state was impacted on Monday.

Aldi has recalled milk available in Victoria and South Australia.

The milk was also sold in two stores in New South Wales: Albury and Lavington.

The affected milk had a Best Before date of 5 June 2027.

Yahoo Lifestyle has a wrap-up of all the items across Australia that had recently been recalled, which you can find here.

Spoilage caused by microbes can mean that organisms have grown inside the milk when it should have been sterile.
