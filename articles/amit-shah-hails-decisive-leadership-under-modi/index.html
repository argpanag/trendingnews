Amit Shah Hails ‘Decisive Leadership’ Under Modi

TheTools · 16.11.2026 17:53 · source · permalink

Union home minister Amit Shah has said the nation witnessed a decisive leadership under the leadership of Narendra Modi. “A long era of policy paralysis has given way to the politics of performance,” he claimed.

Shah was here on Monday to attend a private programme, where, addressing the gathering, he said, “Under Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s leadership, 98 per cent of gram panchayats have been connected through 4G connectivity. In the past 12 years, 50 crore Indians have moved out of poverty, a development which is also acknowledged by the World Bank.”

He said, “Andhra Pradesh is poised to become the most developed state. Google’s $15 billion AI hub being established in Visakhapatnam will be its largest hub outside the US, making this a matter of pride for the entire nation.”

Shah went on to say, “It is a matter of pride that Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu has brought in investments worth `30 lakh crore on the ground in just two-and-a-half-years.”

He said that the world’s longest and most powerful hydrogen-powered train was built in India using entirely indigenous technology and began running on the tracks.

Referring to the Polavaram project, he said Naidu was making determined efforts to revive the project that had been stalled due to mistakes committed by the previous government.
