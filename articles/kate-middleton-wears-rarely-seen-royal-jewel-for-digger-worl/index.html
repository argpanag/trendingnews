Kate Middleton Wears Rarely Seen Royal Jewel for ‘Digger’ World Premiere

TheTools · 22.09.2026 18:42 · source · permalink

The Princess of Wales looked regal in a flowing purple gown and the Queen Mother’s amethyst sautoir necklace.

Kate Middleton and Prince William made a rare red carpet appearance tonight at the world premiere of Digger in London, joining Tom Cruise, director Alejandro G. Iñárritu, and other cast members at the ODEON Luxe Leicester Square.

For the occasion, the Princess of Wales chose a flowing purple gown, notably accessorizing with the Queen Mother’s Amethyst Sautoir Necklace. The pearl necklace—featuring diamonds and amethysts—was a gift from Queen Alexandra to Elizabeth Bowes-Lyon (the future Queen Mother) upon her wedding day in 1923. It has rarely left the royal vaults; in fact, the necklace was last worn over a decade ago, by Queen Camilla (then the Duchess of Cornwall) for the premiere of Skyfall in 2012.

In recent years, Kate has infrequently attended red carpet events—going only to the BAFTAs twice, the Royal Variety Performance twice, the premiere of Top Gun: Maverick in summer 2022, and the Earthshot Awards just once.

Her appearances at movie premieres are even less frequent; during her time in the British royal family, Kate has been to the premieres of just eight total films. In addition to today’s Digger and 2022’s Top Gun: Maverick, that includes two James Bond films, No Time to Die (2021) and Spectre (2015). Her premiere style has largely stuck to neutrals, with the exception of the sparkling gold gown she wore at No Time to Die.

The Prince and Princess of Wales’s appearance this evening comes on the heels of the release of Princess Diana’s brother Charles Spencer’s memoir Swan Song. Earlier today, reporters asked the Prince of Wales, “Sir how do you feel about your uncle’s book?” and “Have you read it?” Prince William ignored their questions. Prince Harry, too, was asked about the book last week and declined to comment.
