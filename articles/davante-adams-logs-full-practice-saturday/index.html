Davante Adams logs full practice Saturday

TheTools · 21.09.2026 23:55 · source · permalink

Los Angeles Rams WR Davante Adams (rest) was a full participant in practice Saturday, Sept. 19, and is not listed on the Week 2 injury report.

SOURCE: TheRams.com

Why it Matters: Adams was quiet in the season opener, but to be fair, the entire passing attack seemed out of sync. Adams could be in for a rebound performance against the Giants, and with his red zone prowess, he will carry WR2 potential into Week 2.
