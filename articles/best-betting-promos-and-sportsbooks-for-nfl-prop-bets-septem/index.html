The Los Angeles Rams will have the opportunity to close the gap in the NFC West with a favorable matchup against the Denver Broncos.

After getting back on track with a win over the New York Giants, the Los Angeles Rams have been able to nip at the Seattle Seahawks and San Francisco 49ers lead in the NFC West.

Heading into Sunday night’s contest against the Denver Broncos, expect the Rams to build on their momentum by gashing a front seven who struggles to stop the run.

Even though there is only one game taking place on Sunday night, there are still a wide range of prop bets and markets to groom through which you can read a detailed breakdown of them below.

Best betting promos and sportsbooks for NFL prop bets

Find the best promos and sportsbooks to use for today’s Football prop bets.

Los Angeles Rams vs Denver Broncos (8:20pm, EST)

Kyren Williams Over 52.5 Rushing Yards (-110) at BetMGM Sportsbook

Even though Blake Corum has continued to chip away at Kyren Williams workload, the Los Angeles Rams still have him listed as the RB1 on their depth chart heading into Week 3.

Williams is an efficient runner when given the nod, ranking in the top half of the board in Yards Over Expected per Attempt while averaging 5.5 Yards per Carry.

Fortunately for Williams, he will be able to hit the ground running against the Denver Broncos by relentlessly attacking a front seven who ranks 24th in Def Rush Success Rate and 32nd in Def Rush EPA.

By struggling to generate pressure at the line of scrimmage, the width of Williams running lanes in the trenches will widen, making it easier for the Rams running back to generate Yards After Contact.

Kyren Williams Longest Rush Over 12.5 Yards (-120) at BetMGM Sportsbook

Correlating this prop with the previously mentioned one, the Broncos inability to plug the gaps will allow Williams to reach the second level of the defense, leading to an explosive play while taking advantage of the extra space on the outside.

With each successful carry, the Rams will lean heavier on him as the game progresses, leading to an uptick in his total number of rush attempts, improving the probability he clears the over on his Longest Rush prop.

Matthew Stafford Under 243.5 Passing Yards (-114) at FanDuel Sportsbook

After winning the MVP award last year, Matthew Stafford has been unable to carry over his success into this season, falling to the bottom half of the board in EPA per play, CPOE, and in Success Rate.

The injury to Puka Nacua has made it difficult for the Rams pass catchers to create space with opposing secondaries getting to stretch out instead of having to shade their coverage towards the star wide receiver.

Against the Broncos, expect Stafford to continue to struggle to connect with his pass catchers while battling it out against a Broncos secondary who thrives in coverage per their top-10 marks in Def Dropback EPA and in Dropback Success Rate.

For some more exposure, escalator bet Stafford’s Passing Yardage prop by placing smaller wagers on his alternate unders at the best available odds across multiple sportsbooks.

Kyren Williams Anytime Touchdown Scorer (-130) at FanDuel Sportsbook

When near the goal line, the Rams have called Williams number at a high rate, comfortably leading Corum in Red Zone Carries with a 75% Red Zone Usage Rate.

With the Broncos failing to clog the middle while pulling a linebacker to Davante Adams, Williams will face minimal resistance in his efforts of rushing up the gut and punch one in for six.

Bo Nix Longest Completion Under 33.5 Yards (-110) at BetMGM Sportsbook

Like Stafford, Bo Nix has also underwhelmed this season, ranking below league average in EPA per play while averaging just 6.7 Air Yards.

His lack of production under center brings in a high amount of variance into the contest, lowering the Broncos chances of winning on a weekly basis.

With Aaron Donald back in the fold, Nix will continue to struggle to air out the ball while having to throw under pressure, leading to a decline in his total number of completions.

The Rams ability to generate pressure with just their defensive line also allows them to drop their linebackers in coverage to help keep a body on the Broncos pass catchers and bring them down at the point of contact.
