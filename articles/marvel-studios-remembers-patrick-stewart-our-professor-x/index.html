Marvel Studios Remembers Patrick Stewart, Our Professor X

Marvel Studios is heartbroken to learn of the passing of Sir Patrick Stewart, the legendary English actor who brought Professor Charles Xavier to life. He was 86. 

Stewart made his debut as Professor X in 2000’s X-Men, where he embodied the composure, class, and wisdom of the beloved telepathic mutant and mentor of the X-Men. For more than 25 years, he brought a gravitas and profundity to the role, earning fans across the globe and helping to transform Super Hero storytelling forever.  

He reprised the role in multiple X-Men films, culminating with an unforgettable performance in 2017’s Logan. In 2022, Stewart joined the Marvel Cinematic Universe as an alternate-universe Professor X in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness. He returned to the role once more with 2026’s Avengers: Doomsday, his final on-screen appearance.

In addition to his work as Professor X, Stewart was also known for his celebrated performance as Captain Jean-Luc Picard in Star Trek: The Next Generation. His range knew no bounds, stretching from a comedic role in Robin Hood: Men in Tights to a chilling performance in Green Room. 

Stewart also devoted much of his career to championing the works of William Shakespeare, which he performed on stage and on screen, and he spent many years as a lauded member of the Royal Shakespeare Company. His unparalleled career spanned more than 60 years and earned him many accolades, including two Olivier Awards and a Grammy Award. In 2010, Stewart was knighted by Queen Elizabeth II in recognition of his many contributions to drama and the arts.

Sir Patrick Stewart leaves behind an indelible cultural footprint and legacy. Marvel Studios extends its deepest condolences to his family, friends, and fans around the world.
