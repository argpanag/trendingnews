Jennifer Lawrence Shares First Instagram Reel, Jokes She'll 'Quit Immediately' If Anyone Says Anything Negative

TheTools · 10.09.2026 14:20 · source · permalink

Jennifer Lawrence is posting her first Instagram Reel after launching the account earlier this week…and she’s already threatening to quit the app if anyone says anything negative about her!

“So yeah, I’m giving it a shot,” she began in a Reel posted on Thursday (September 10).

“And if anyone says anything negative, I will quit immediately,” she warned. “No, no questions asked.”

“If anyone thinks something mean, um…I’ll know. I’ll know. And I’ll quit,” she amusingly added.

Jennifer is now on the app as @jlaw and she revealed in her Instagram bio that she had to use the name as “@jenniferlawrence” was already taken!

Why Did Jennifer Lawrence Join Instagram?

It’s unclear why Jennifer has finally decided to join the app, but she’ll likely use the page to promote her professional projects. While she’s rumored to have a small cameo appearance in the upcoming Hunger Games prequel movie, due in theaters this November, she won’t be seen in a new movie until next year’s What Happens at Night.

Who Is Jennifer Lawrence Following on Instagram?

Jennifer is currently following a few dozen accounts.

The accounts include her What Happens at Night collaborators Leonardo DiCaprio and Martin Scorsese, The Hunger Games‘ Josh Hutcherson, friends like Dakota Johnson and Suki Waterhouse, and Kardashian-Jenner family members Kim, Khloe, Kourtney, Kendall, Kylie, and Kris.
