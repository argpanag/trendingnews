Walmart Holiday Gift Guide 2026: AirPods, Switch 2, Instax, and more top picks

TheTools · 21.10.2026 09:32 · source · permalink

TL;DR: Walmart’s official 2026 Holiday Gift Guide is live. Shop top picks from the catalog — ranging from $50 off Apple AirPods Pro 3 and the $499 Nintendo Switch 2.

Walmart’s 2026 Holiday Gift Guide is officially live, giving shoppers an early look at what the retail giant expects to be the season’s biggest hits. Rather than a fast-moving doorbuster event, Walmart’s Gift Guide functions as a complete holiday roadmap — curating top picks across every age group and budget tier.

Whether you want to score major savings on flagship Apple gear, hunt down the new Nintendo Switch 2, or grab budget-friendly blind boxes under $15, the blog maps out the best places to spend your money wisely. You can check out the full ad scan directly over on BlackFriday.com to browse every page.

Below, we’ve rounded up 10 of the best picks from Walmart's lineup, ranging from budget stocking stuffers to premium, splurge-worthy tech.

The internet’s favorite stocking stuffer hits Walmart

Blind boxes are taking over TikTok for a reason, and the Sad Baby Animals figures are almost illegally cute. Each box holds a surprise palm-sized vinyl creature — featuring capybaras, highland cows, and axolotls — with big, dramatic eyes that beg you to adopt them all. They’re an instant serotonin hit, a perfect stocking stuffer, and a dangerously fun addiction if you end up hunting for that ultra-rare 1-in-128 secret figure.

Ditch the Keurig: Fresh coffee for under $25

You don’t need to drop triple digits on a massive, space-hogging countertop appliance just to grab a quick cup of coffee before rushing out the door. The Mainstays Single-Serve Coffee Maker handles both standard K-Cups and loose grounds with its included reusable filter. It brews up to 14 ounces straight into a travel mug, automatically shuts off after every cycle, and takes up minimal counter space — making it an absolute no-brainer gift for college dorms, small apartment kitchens, or home office setups.

Stop 'NeeDoh hunting': The viral fidget cube is right here

If you tried tracking down this squishy cube earlier this year, you know it was practically impossible to find. As we reported during the height of the craze, demand was so intense that Schylling sold through a year's supply in nine weeks — triggering long lines, sold-out shelves, and wild reseller prices. What makes the NeeDoh Squish Toy worth the hype is its unique texture: soft when squeezed slowly, firm when compressed quickly, and always snapping back to its original square shape. Snagging one at regular retail is an instant holiday win for kids and desk-bound adults alike.

Self-care season made easy (and deeply discounted)

Gifting self-care shouldn't require a loan. For $45.99, this 24-day advent calendar gives you 24 individual daily treats packed with natural bath and body essentials that leave your skin feeling soft through the cold winter months. It takes all the guesswork out of holiday gifting for moms, friends, or co-workers — or gives you a perfect excuse for a little daily self-care as Christmas gets closer.

Physical photos are back (and this kit includes the film)

Instant cameras are a holiday staple for almost anyone, but buying the camera and film separately is annoying. This Walmart-exclusive bundle packages the sleek, pastel-hued Instax Mini SE together with a starter 10-pack of Instax Mini film. It features manual exposure control dials so you can tweak brightness for indoor lighting versus sunny outdoors, making it an easy, ready-to-shoot gift that lets anyone start printing physical memories the second they tear open the box.

The sleekest co-op upgrade for your PS5 setup

Upgrading your console setup doesn't have to mean sticking to basic white. The Sony DualSense Cosmic Red Wireless Controller brings a sleek, vibrant look to your PlayStation 5 or PC setup while delivering the top-tier features that make the DualSense iconic. With precise haptic feedback, dynamic adaptive triggers, built-in motion sensors, and an integrated microphone, it offers incredible immersion whether you're picking up a secondary pad for local co-op or replacing a worn-out main controller.

Apple’s best earbuds are officially $50 off

Earning a 4.75-star Mashable Choice Award, the Apple AirPods Pro 3 are our top earbuds of 2025. In his review, Adam Doud found that the upgraded Active Noise Cancellation creates an unmatched "cone of silence" that shuts out background noise. Add in workout heart rate tracking, Live Translation via Apple Intelligence, and 8 hours of battery life, and catching them for $199 makes them an absolute slam-dunk holiday deal.

Salon-worthy blowouts without the Dyson price tag

Winning a Mashable Choice Award with a stellar 4.5-star rating, the Shark FlexStyle doesn't just copy the Dyson Airwrap — it actively outperforms it for a fraction of the cost. In our hands-on testing, we loved how the base physically twists to transform from a powerful multi-styler into an ultra-compact hair dryer. Packed with Coanda auto-wrap curlers, smoothing brushes, and heat monitoring that checks temperature 1,000 times per second, it delivers salon-worthy, heat-safe blowouts at home without requiring a huge investment.

The ultimate $99 AI assistant for your living room

Moving past rigid, robotic commands, the Google Home Speaker brings conversational Gemini AI right onto your nightstand or kitchen counter. You can chat naturally with Gemini Live, brainstorm ideas, and control Matter-compatible smart devices in a single breath. Wrapped in room-filling 360-degree sound, a physical privacy mute switch, and a sleek underglow light, it delivers next-gen smart home help for under $100.

Say hello to 4K Zelda and magnetic Joy-Cons for $499

The Nintendo Switch 2 takes everything we loved about the original hybrid console and gives it a massive hardware upgrade. You get a larger, stunning 7.9-inch 1080p display with HDR support up to 120Hz in handheld mode, crisp 4K TV output through the upgraded dock, and magnetic Joy-Con 2 controllers with new mouse-like precision controls. With 256GB of internal storage and full backward compatibility for your existing Switch library, it’s the ultimate crown jewel gift for any gamer this holiday season.
