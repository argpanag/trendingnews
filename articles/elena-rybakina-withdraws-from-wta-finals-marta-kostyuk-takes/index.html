Elena Rybakina withdraws from WTA Finals, Marta Kostyuk takes her place

TheTools · 07.11.2026 20:46 · source · permalink

The WTA Finals will now be without its No. 1 seed.

The defending champion and World No. 1 player Elena Rybakina has withdrawn from the event at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden on the eve of her first match.

"I am really sad to inform all my fans and supporters that I unfortunately have to withdraw from the WTA Finals this year,” Rybakina said. "I’ve had an incredible season but my body is just not physically ready to compete. I’m going to spend some time resting and recovering at home and getting myself ready for the 2027 season. The WTA Finals is a very special tournament and I wish everyone the best of luck this week. Can’t wait to be back in Indian Wells in March!”

Rybakina had missed her scheduled media day appearance on Friday as the tournament said she was "not feeling well." Her interview time was rescheduled for 11 a.m. Saturday morning, but at 11:14 a.m. the tournament released a statement that she would be a medical withdrawal from the event.

Marta Kostyuk of the Ukraine moves into the draw as the first alternate, and will open play Sunday against Linda Noskova.

It's a tough way to end the season for Rybakina who won the Australian Open and the U.S. Open and ascended to the world's No. 1 post for the first time in her career.

Kostyuk, who had been on site training the past couple of days just in case, now goes into a pool with Noskova, Coco Gauff and Jessica Pegula. Kostyuk will play Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday in the round-robin portion of the event.
