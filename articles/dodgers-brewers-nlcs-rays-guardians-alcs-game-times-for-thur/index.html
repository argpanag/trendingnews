Dodgers-Brewers NLCS & Rays-Guardians ALCS Game Times For Thursday Changed

TheTools · 14.10.2026 22:46 · source · permalink

Want to watch the back-to-back World Series Champion Dodgers fight for their lives tomorrow? You’ll have to tune in a little bit earlier than expected.

The threat of rain in Cleveland has led Major League Baseball to move Game 3 of the American League Championship Series between the Rays and Guardians on Thursday ahead to 1 p.m. ET from the previously scheduled 6 p.m. ET start time. The ALCS is tied 1-1.

That change has allowed the league to shift Thursday’s Game 4 of the National League Championship Series between the Brewers and Dodgers further into primetime for East Coasters. It has been moved from 9 p.m. ET to 8 p.m. ET. (For Angelenos not good with time zone calculations, this means Game 4 of the Dodgers-Braves in Los Angeles will now air Thursday at 5 p.m. PT.)

That Game 4 is a potential do-or-die for L.A.’s season. With the Brewers up 2-0, if Milwaukee wins tonight, the Dodgers will face elimination tomorrow. On the positive side for L.A., Dodgers manager Dave Roberts said slumping superstar Shohei Ohtani, who will be batting sixth Wednesday, has indicated he is “close to finding something” in batting practice that will help him end his postseason struggles.

Watch on Deadline

All ALCS games are telecast on TNT Sports platforms, while and Fox/FS1/Fox One carries the NLCS. They are also available to MLB.TV subscribers. ESPN Radio provides live national coverage of all 2026 MLB postseason games.
