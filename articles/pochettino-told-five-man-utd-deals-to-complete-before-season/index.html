Gary Neville has called on Mauricio Pochettino to complete an overhaul at Mancheter United, naming five players to be moved on by the end of the season.

The former Man United defender has also suggested another four or five players for the chopping block in a ‘second wave’ of exits.

Having previously commented that the Red Devils’ current defence would lead to relegation, it is no surprise that Neville focused on shipping out defenders.

But Neville fears that a complete reset of the Man Utd squad could take three seasons for Pochettino to complete.

Analysing the squad, Neville said on The Overlap’s Stick to United podcast: “Out of the defenders he’ll keep [Luke] Shaw because he knows him and he’ll probably get the best out of him. The problem with Luke is injuries and you’ll have to manage him.

“He’ll keep [Harry] Maguire because he’ll trust him and think he’s basically a soldier, someone who can do a job for him.

“He’ll keep [Leny] Yoro because he’ll think there’s something to work with. [Ayden] Heaven, I’d loan him out, get some experience in the Championship. He’s nowhere near it, it’s unfair to put him in the situation he’s in.

“I’d keep Bruno [Fernandes], I’d keep Kobbie [Mainoo], I’d keep [Youri] Tielemans, [Mason] Mount would have to go.

“[Andrey] Santos - Mauricio may like him because he’s young, quite mobile, he’s aggressive, he might get something out of him. Him and [Carlos] Baleba will be in that middle bit. Mount’s the one that goes definitely, in that midfield section.”

Neville then went on to explain that Marcus Rashford’s wages meant he needed to be moved on at the ‘earliest opportunity’.

“[Joshua] Zirkzee definitely to go, I would think,” he restarted. “I think Marcus 100 per cent to go because of the fact he’s on massive money and that story has been well told.

“They can’t afford to pay him that money, he’s in that section where you have to get rid of that wage at the earliest opportunity.

“He’ll work with Amad [Diallo], he’ll work with [Bryan] Mbeumo, he’ll work with [Matheus] Cunha and he’ll work with [Benjamin] Sesko and think, ‘okay, I’ll make you into a centre-forward’.

“He’ll use [Patrick] Dorgu in the short term but he’ll be one who will go in the next couple of years. [Manuel] Ugarte will go, 100 per cent.”

Neville then highlighted the players he would prioritise finding an exit for before the start of next season.

He continued: “If you look through it now I’d say [Lisandro] Martinez would go, [Matthijs] De Ligt would go, [Noussair] Mazraoui would go, Mount would go, first wave next summer. Rashford if you can, and Zirkzee.

“And then the year after you’d be looking at Shaw, Maguire, [Diogo] Dalot, Bruno, that’s your second wave and maybe then you’ve made your mind up on Baleba and Santos.

“But the earliest Mauricio Pochettino can unravel this squad is two seasons if he does well. It could take three to get this squad unravelled.”
