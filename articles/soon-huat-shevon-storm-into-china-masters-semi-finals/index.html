Soon Huat-Shevon storm into China Masters semi-finals

TheTools · 04.09.2026 10:16 · source · permalink

The husband-and-wife duo beat home pair Gao Jia Xuan-Wu Meng Ying in straight games in the quarter-finals.

The world No 11 pair silenced the home crowd by defeating world No 29 duo Gao Jia Xuan and Wu Meng Ying 21-15, 21-11 in 35 minutes at the Shenzhen Sports Centre Gymnasium in Shenzhen today.

Soon Huat-Shevon, who had not progressed beyond the quarter-final stage in any World Tour event this year, were in control throughout the match.

They will face China’s World No 1 pair Feng Yanzhe-Huang Dongping in the semi-finals.

Malaysia’s Hoo Pang Ron and Lai Pei Jing also booked a semi-final spot in the same category this morning after defeating Taiwan’s Liu Kuang Heng-Hsu Yin Hui 24-22, 21-5.

Pang Ron-Pei Jing, who only teamed up in March, endured six early exits before reaching the semi-finals of the Macau Open in June.
