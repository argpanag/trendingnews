Where to celebrate Mid-Autumn Festival 2026 this weekend

TheTools · 23.09.2026 10:41 · source · permalink

It’s time for lantern walks and mooncake appreciation again. This year, the Mid-Autumn Festival falls on Friday, 25 September 2026.

By now, you should have shopped for all your mooncakes, be it for your own enjoyment or as a gift for loved ones. But if you haven’t, and need some last-minute recommendations, check out this curated list.

If what you need are suggestions for places you can celebrate the occasion with family and friends, don’t worry. Keep reading for the most vibey spots and activities this weekend.

Table of Contents

Pixel Moon at Chinatown Point

For a fun Mid-Autumn Festival for the whole family, consider heading to Chinatown Point for their Pixel Moon event. From now till 11 October 2026, the mall will become a playful zone filled with games and prizes.

The Pixel Moon Arcade, located on the Level 1 Indoor Atrium, invites you and your family to enjoy a series of arcade games and carnival games. Spending a minimum of S$88 in the same period anywhere in the mall will also get you a Lantern Scratch Card.

Simply accumulate your spending in up to 3 same-day receipts, then Scratch & Win Rewards! Receipts produced after 6pm will earn you a bonus Lantern Scratch Card. Sure-win prizes include S$10 NTUC FairPrice vouchers and S$10 Timezone game credits.

📍 133 New Bridge Road, Chinatown Point, Singapore 059413
🕚 10am–10pm (Daily)

Celestial Nights: Mid-Autumn Edition 2026

For a different take on the Mid-Autumn festivities, head to Science Centre Singapore, where Celestial Nights: Mid-Autumn Edition 2026 will take place. This reimagined celebration of the Mid-Autumn Festival will have a few activities you and your family can look forward to.

If you’d like a good view of the moon, there’s arguably no better place than Science Centre Singapore. Those who bought tickets previously will be able to view the moon through a telescope at The Observatory.

Didn’t manage to secure your spot? Fret not. Head to the Water Lily Pavilion for a clear view or join the Celestial Trail for a free, illuminated night walk between Science Centre Singapore and Japanese Garden. 

Along the way, you will find mini-exhibitions, cosmic displays and light installations. On the actual day of the Mid-Autumn Festival on 25 September, there will also be live acoustic performances happening from 6.30pm to 9.30pm. Talk about pure atmosphere. The Celestial Trail is open from 19 to 27 September 2026. Register for free here.

While you’re outdoors, look out for F&B pop-ups where you can recharge with some food and drinks. Free White Rabbit ice cream is also up for grabs!

For something even more immersive, you might be interested in checking out the Digital Planetarium Show at the Omni-Theatre. Titled Moon Legends: From Myth to Mission, the show will take you on a journey through lunar exploration, ancient folklore, and modern space missions.

Tickets are priced S$4, with the show available at select times on 25 & 26 September 2026. Book yours here.

Celestial Nights: Mid-Autumn Edition 2026
Facebook | Instagram | TikTok | Website
📍 15 Science Centre Road, Science Centre Singapore, Singapore 609081
🕚 6pm–10pm
🗓️ 19 to 27 September 2026

Lights by the Lake 2026

When it comes to yearly Mid-Autumn festivities, we have to talk about Jurong Lake Gardens’ Lights by the Lake. The annual light-up returns this year with a theme centred around the Legend of the White Snake. 

As usual, you can expect to walk among Lantern Displays and glowing scenes that will transport you into a classic Chinese tale. From the Lakeside Field to the Cloud Pagoda to the Chinese Garden and more, witness larger-than-life installations you will want photos with.

The Cloud Pagoda Light Show is also making a return and will play every night until 27 September 2026, from 7.30pm to 10pm with 30-minute intervals.

While you’re exploring Jurong Lake Gardens, you might find yourself hungry at some point. Head over to the festive food market at Lakeside Garden, which will be packed with bites featuring local and international flavours.

There are also carnival games to enjoy and lanterns for you to take on your stroll! On the weekend of 26 & 27 September 2026, the fun expands to bouncy castles, where kids can unleash all their energy. Definitely drop by this food market if you’re planning a visit!

Lights by the Lake 2026

📍 Yuan Ching Road, Jurong Lake Gardens
🕚 6.30pm–10.30pm
🗓️ 19 September 2026 to 4 October 2026

Jurong Point Food Feastival: Momiji Matsuri Edition

Westies are truly blessed this Mid-Autumn Festival season. It can’t be helped when there are so many themed events to look forward to in the area! But we promise there is more for all of you who don’t stay in the west.

Back in the west, Jurong Point is hosting Food Feastival, a collection of Mid-Autumn fairs and themed markets. Running from 7 August 2026 to 1 November 2026, the event sees a rotation of fairs every few weeks.

Currently, you will find a Mid-Autumn Fair at the JP1 Centre Stage, running till 25 September 2026. Here, you can shop for mooncakes from hotels and speciality bakeries. Those who spend at least S$228 in a single receipt or across 3 combined receipts will receive a complimentary 2-piece bowl and chopsticks set worth S$29.90.

The festivities don’t stop once the Mid-Autumn Festival is over, though. Shoppers can also look forward to the Isetan Momiji Matsuri Fair from 6 to 18 October 2026. This Japan-themed food fair will feature more than 20 vendors offering Japanese street food, seafood, seasonal specialities, and more.

That’s more than enough reason to visit Jurong Point this week and beyond! For more information, including details on how to earn and redeem vouchers during this period, visit their website.

Jurong Point Food Feastival: Momiji Matsuri Edition
Facebook | Instagram | TikTok | Website
📍 1 Jurong West Central 2, Jurong Point, Singapore 648886
🕚 10.30am–9pm (Sun to Thu), 10.30am–9.30pm (Fri & Sun)
🗓️ Now till 18 October 2026

Mid-Autumn Festival 2026 at Gardens by the Bay

If there is any other place known for its festival-themed light-ups and events in Singapore, it would have to be Gardens by the Bay. From now till 27 September 2026, the gardens have been transformed into a land of glow, magic, and wonder. 

For those who want a stroll through nature with family and friends, look no further. Lantern Displays will be up every night from 6pm to 10pm, at spots like Dragonfly Lake and Supertree Grove.

Beyond the light installations, Supertree Grove will also be home to a bustling marketplace. Carnival games, family-friendly rides, and a delicious range of food and drinks will be available for visitors to explore. 

There is also a Craft Corner located at Colonnade for all you artsy people. Other than DIY Lantern Painting, all of these activities require paid tickets. Find out more here.

Sanrio characters Moonlit Garden at VivoCity

At another corner of Singapore, VivoCity is also buzzing with Mid-Autumn festivities. If you’re a fan of Sanrio characters like Hello Kitty, Kuromi, and Cinnamoroll, you will definitely have lots of fun here. 

That is because the shopping destination has transformed into a Sanrio-themed land, with a Moonlit Garden and Kuromi Carnival Kingdom. Located at the Sky Park on Level 3, you will find towering character displays and light installations that are perfect for photos.

There are also carnival games (S$10 each) you can play for a chance to win cute prizes, and a Mini Express Train ride (S$10) you can take to get an exclusive Sanrio Sticker Set.

For a list of all the events and rewards happening at VivoCity this Mid-Autumn Festival season, click here.

Moonlit Wonder at Pasir Ris Mall

Easties, don’t think we left you out! Pasir Ris Mall is also in the midst of its Mid-Autumn Festival celebrations. Moonlit Wonder introduces a series of themed promotions, activities, and activations to the east-side mall until 27 September 2026.

Whether you live in the area or don’t mind visiting the mall to check it out, there’s plenty to see and do. The Moonlit Bunny Adventure Stamp Collection invites shoppers to redeem a stamp card with a minimum spend of S$30 in a single same-day receipt.

With the card, solve riddles at 9 different stations to collect exclusive Moonlit Bunny stickers. Once your stamp card is complete, you will also receive a special set of Jade Bunny hand towels (2pcs), while stocks last.

At the Level 2 Central Greenway, you will also find Chang’e’s Moonlight Spectacular, filled with photoworthy spots. Want something more hands-on? Head to the Event Space next to Decathlon on the same floor and join Lantern-Making Workshops and more.

For more information on how to participate in these activities, visit Pasir Ris Mall’s website.

📍 7 Pasir Ris Central, Pasir Ris Mall, Singapore 519612
🕚 10am–10pm (Daily)
🗓️ Now till 27 September 2026
