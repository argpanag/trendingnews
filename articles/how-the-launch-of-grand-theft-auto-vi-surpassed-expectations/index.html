How the launch of ‘Grand Theft Auto VI’ surpassed expectations

TheTools · 23.11.2026 01:29 · source · permalink

NEW YORK – You can paint your bedroom walls. You can scuba dive and explore shipwrecks. You can go birdwatching and catalogue up to 62 different species. You can even rob banks and steal cars, just like in the old days — but you’ll need to be careful, because the police are smarter now and if you run out of gas, well, you’ll have to fill up the tank.

The level of realistic detail in Rockstar Games’ video game “Grand Theft Auto VI,” which was released last week, has astonished gamers and helped the release roar beyond the already sky-high expectations that had built up after a 13-year wait and an estimated $1 billion production budget. It has a score of 99 out of 100 on Metacritic, the joint highest score of any game ever, and high praise for it has been almost universal.

“I feel like I truly have waited all this time and it’s come, and for me, it’s the ideal version of what they could have done,” said Brandon Nightingale, a 34-year-old music production lecturer from Manchester in Britain and longtime fan of Rockstar Games.
