Storm Team4: More storms move through DC area damaged by last night's severe weather

TheTools · 05.09.2026 05:24 · source · permalink

Storm Team4 has the weather forecast for D.C., Maryland and Northern Virginia.

Storm Team4’s Doug Kammerer is tracking storms tonight.

Another round of storms moved through the D.C. area Friday evening, but they didn't leave the damage that Thursday night's severe weather inflicted on the region.

Stream NBC4 newscasts for free right here, right now.

A severe thunderstorm watch for the D.C. area was canceled after the storms moved out Friday evening.

Prince George's County Public Schools closed for students Friday because of the overnight storms that caused damage throughout the region and left homes without power.

Temperatures reached 90° Friday afternoon, but felt much hotter with the humidity.

Download the NBC Washington app on iOS and Android to check the weather radar on the go.

We will keep a chance for some showers Saturday. Temperatures on Saturday will top out mid-to-upper 80s once again with muggy conditions.

A front will eventually drop through the region by Saturday afternoon. Humidity levels will fall by Sunday morning. Expect sunshine to return Sunday and Monday as temperatures fall back into the lower 80s.
