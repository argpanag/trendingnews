Top picks to stream tonight

‘Animals’ (2026) (Netflix)

Genre: Crime thriller movie

What it's about: When the son of Los Angeles mayoral candidate Mark Kimball (Ben Affleck) is suddenly kidnapped, he and his wife, Abigail (Kerry Washington), have to find a way to pay the ransom. With time running out, the couple are forced to make difficult decisions as the situation spirals out of control and secrets threaten to come to light.

Why you should watch it: Ben Affleck is back behind the camera for this crime thriller, which keeps things tense with a kidnapping, a desperate family and plenty of uncertainty. Kerry Washington and Gillian Anderson are also worth watching, bringing some star power to the cast. It’s a solid pick for a suspenseful movie night, especially if you enjoy stories full of twists and difficult choices.

Watch "Animals" on Netflix now

‘Matchbox the Movie’ (2026) (Apple TV)

Genre: Action-comedy movie

What it's about: "Matchbox The Movie" follows Sean (John Cena), an undercover CIA agent who returns to his hometown after years away and reunites with his childhood friends. Their reunion takes a dangerous turn when they become caught up in an international conspiracy involving a nuclear weapon. With enemies closing in, the group must work together, using their skills, fast cars and quick thinking to save the world.

Why you should watch it: .John Cena leads this action comedy, bringing plenty of energy to a globe-trotting adventure packed with car chases, stunts and shootouts. Director Sam Hargrave, known for the "Extraction" movies, knows how to stage a thrilling action sequence, while Jessica Biel and Sam Richardson help round out the cast. It’s an easy pick for anyone after a fun, fast-paced movie night.

Watch "Matchbox the Movie" on Apple TV now

‘Disclosure Day’ (2026) (Peacock)

Genre: Sci-fi movie

What it's about: In "Disclosure Day," a Kansas City weather presenter, Margaret Fairchild (Emily Blunt), experiences a strange incident during a live broadcast that raises questions about whether humanity is alone. Meanwhile, government whistleblower Daniel Kellner (Josh O'Connor) investigates evidence of extraterrestrial life and a long-running cover-up. As their paths converge, the truth threatens to change everything people believe about the world and our place in the universe.

Why you should watch it: Steven Spielberg returns to sci-fi with "Disclosure Day," a thriller starring Emily Blunt and Josh O’Connor. The film delivers plenty of suspense, spectacular set pieces and the kind of big-screen wonder Spielberg does so well. Its mix of alien mysteries, government conspiracies and questions about humanity makes it an appealing pick for anyone who enjoys thought-provoking sci-fi.

Watch "Disclosure Day" on Peacock now
