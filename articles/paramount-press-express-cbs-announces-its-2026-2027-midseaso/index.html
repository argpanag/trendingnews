CBS ANNOUNCES ITS 2026-2027 MIDSEASON PRIMETIME SCHEDULE

 

 

CBS today announced its 2026-2027 midseason primetime schedule, featuring EINSTEIN, a new drama starring Matthew Gray Gubler and Melissa Fumero; a reimagined, Emmy Award-winning UNDERCOVER BOSS, hosted by Drew Carey; and STACKS OF CASH, a new game show hosted by Mike O’Malley. Returning hits include the season premiere of MATLOCK, starring Kathy Bates; the sixth season of the comedy GHOSTS; and new extended two-hour SURVIVOR episodes. The schedule also features special events, including the 84TH ANNUAL GOLDEN GLOBES and a special episode of #1 new series NCIS: NEW YORK airing after the NFL ON CBS: AFC Championship game. This fall season to date, CBS has averaged 8.1 million viewers with seven of the top 10 series, including three of the top four new series in Nielsen live plus 7-day multiplatform viewership.

 

Below are highlights and the schedule for the 2026-2027 midseason lineup. All times are Eastern/Pacific unless noted; programs also stream live and on demand on Paramount+*.

 

NEW SERIES PREMIERES

 

  • STACKS OF CASH, hosted by Mike O’Malley, premieres Wednesday, Jan. 6 (9:00-10:00 PM).
  • EINSTEIN, starring Matthew Gray Gubler and Melissa Fumero, premieres Friday, Feb. 12 (8:00-9:00 PM).
  • UNDERCOVER BOSS, hosted by Drew Carey, premieres Wednesday, Feb. 24 (10:00-11:00 PM) following the SURVIVOR season 52 premiere.

 

RETURNING SERIES PREMIERES AND NEW TIME PERIODS

 

  • CUPERTINO, new drama rated 100% on Rotten Tomatoes, starring Mike Colter, moves to a new time period, Sundays (10:00-11:00 PM), beginning Sunday, Jan. 17.
  • MATLOCK, starring Kathy Bates, returns for its third season Thursday, Jan. 21 (10:00-11:00 PM).
  • GHOSTS, season 6, airs twice a week on Thursdays (8:30-9:00 PM) starting Thursday, Jan. 21 and Mondays (8:00-8:30 PM) starting Monday, Jan. 25.
  • ETERNALLY YOURS moves to a new time period Mondays (8:30-9:00 PM) following GHOSTS starting Monday, Jan. 25.
  • FBI and CIA move to new time periods on Mondays, (9:00-10:00 PM) and (10:00-11:00 PM), respectively, starting Monday, Jan. 25.
  • NCIS: SYDNEY season four premieres Tuesday, Jan. 26 (10:00-11:00 PM).
  • NCIS: NEW YORK to air a special original episode following the AFC Championship game on Sunday, Jan. 31.
  • SHERIFF COUNTRY premieres Friday, Jan. 8 (8:00-9:00 PM) and then moves to a new time period Fridays (9:00-10:00 PM), starting Friday, Feb. 12.
  • SURVIVOR season 52 premieres Wednesday, Feb. 24 with new extended two-hour episodes (8:00-10:00 PM).

 

CBS SPECIALS EVENT PROGRAMMING

  • The 84TH ANNUAL GOLDEN GLOBES hosted by Nikki Glaser airs Sunday, Jan. 10 (Live) (7:30-10:30 PM, ET/4:30-7:30 PM, PT)
  • 26TH ANNUAL SUPER BOWL GREATEST COMMERCIALS: HALL OF FAME COUNTDOWN airs Wednesday, Feb. 10 (8:00-9:00 PM)
