Xbox Insiders Can Cloud Stream Games Without Game Pass (But There's a Catch) - IGN Daily Fix - IGN

TheTools · 22.10.2026 23:18 · source · permalink

In today's Daily Fix:

Xbox is testing some of its Game Pass features under new plans via Xbox Insider. Members can now pay to use the cloud streaming service separately from a Game Pass plan by buying blocks of time. Previously, this feature was locked behind the Game Pass Ultimate tier, but now you can pay as little as $3.99 for 10 hours of streaming over the cloud, all the way to $11.99 for 50 hours. Your hours don't expire so they can roll over day-to-day. In other news, Dispatch is celebrating its 1st anniversary with new like a commentary track and storyboard mode coming to the deluxe edition. There is also a live music concert planned in Hollywood, and more merch such as a three-record vinyl set, shirts, hoodies, and more. And we’re heading back to How to Train Your Dragon’s Isle of Berk with the Ride Guys to soar through Hiccup’s Wing Gliders – all at Universal Orlando Resort.

Presented by Universal Orlando Resort, and its newest theme park,  Universal Epic Universe.
