TUNE IN | FC Cincinnati vs New England Revolution | FC Cincinnati

TheTools · 14.10.2026 23:48 · source · permalink

FC Cincinnati return home Wednesday to host the New England Revolution. Kickoff from TQL Stadium is set for 7:30 p.m. ET. Here is how to tune in and follow along with the match:

HOW TO WATCH AND FOLLOW #CINvNE on WEDNESDAY, OCTOBER 14, 2026 (7:30 p.m. ET)

Watch: Apple TV (English and Spanish)

English Local Radio: iHeart Media ESPN 1530

Spanish Local Radio: La Mega 101.5 FM

FC Cincinnati Social Media: X/Twitter | FC Cincinnati Communications X/Twitter Account | Instagram | Facebook | TikTok

FC Cincinnati Official Mobile App: iOS | Android

HOW TO WATCH
Wednesday's match airs globally on Apple TV.  Starting this season, Apple TV subscribers in more than 100 countries and regions can enjoy access to every MLS match with no blackouts – the standalone MLS Season Pass subscription on the Apple TV app has concluded.

Nate Bukaty and Devon Kerr have the call on Apple TV in English and Carlos Ramirez is on the call in Spanish on Apple TV.

HOW TO LISTEN
The Official English Radio Broadcast Home of FC Cincinnati, iHeart Media will feature Tom Gelehrter and Kevin McCloskey on the call on ESPN 1530 in Cincinnati and iHeart. 

The Official Spanish Radio Broadcast Home of FC Cincinnati – La Mega Cincinnati 101.5 FM – will carry the match with Gustavo Luques and José Romero on the call on La Mega 101.5 FM.

FOLLOW ON FC CINCINNATI SOCIAL MEDIA
Be sure to follow @FCCincinnati on X/Twitter, Instagram and Facebook for live updates from the game.

DOWNLOAD THE OFFICIAL MOBILE APP
Cincinnati’s official mobile app, available now for download on iOS and Android devices. The FC Cincinnati app keeps fans closer than ever to the action and include real time push notifications, news, ticket information and more.

HEAD TO A PUB PARTNER
Fans who cannot make it to TQL Stadium are encouraged to head to one of the club’s official Pub Partners, presented by Coors Light. The Pub Partner program includes bars and restaurants around Greater Cincinnati, Northern Kentucky, Middletown and Louisville. Pub Partners are the spot to go to watch the Orange and Blue in 2026 and feature watch parties, drink specials and convenient locations for fans to gather to watch FC Cincinnati.

For a full list of Pub Partners and for more information on the program, visit FCCincinnati.com/Matchday/Pub-Partners.
