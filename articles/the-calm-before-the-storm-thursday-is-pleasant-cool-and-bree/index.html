The calm before the storm: Thursday is pleasant, cool, and breezy - The Boston Globe

TheTools · 23.09.2026 23:34 · source · permalink

Wednesday saw partly to mostly sunny skies across New England, with afternoon temperatures in the low 60s, about 5 degrees cooler than average for this time of year.

High pressure will remain in control just north of New England, and it’s quite strong, meaning most of us will feel breezy conditions through Thursday.

Quick look Thursday

This high pressure will keep clouds limited across most of the region. A few scattered clouds may stick around Boston and areas to the south, but the sun should come out in extended stretches throughout the day.

Coastal storm develops

Expanding outside of New England, it’s worth noting that the storm we’ve been monitoring this week will solidify off the coast of North Carolina and Virginia on Thursday and begin to slowly move north.

Chilly Thursday morning, highs in low 60s

This high pressure will continue to sling a northerly flow through New England, setting up chilly temperatures to start the day. Folks outside of Boston may head out the door with temperatures in the 40s. Boston won’t be much warmer, with commuting temperatures in the low 50s. Despite the bright skies, especially north of the Mass Pike, the high temperatures will struggle to reach the mid-60s. Boston and the immediate coast should barely crack the 60s for the afternoon.

Greater Boston and most of Southern New England will continue to feel a breeze throughout the day, with wind gusts reaching 20 to 25 mph at times, especially during the afternoon.

Thursday breakdown

Greater Boston: Partly to mostly cloudy, with some sun at times. Breezy with highs in the low 60s.

Central/Western Mass.: Partly to mostly sunny as highs reach the upper 50s in the Berkshires, low to mid-60s east. Slight breeze.

Southeastern Mass.: Mostly cloudy with some sun at times. Highs in the low and mid-60s, breezy.

Cape Cod and the Islands: Mostly cloudy with some sun at times. Highs in the low to mid-60s, turning breezy.

Rhode Island: Partly to mostly cloudy, highs in the low to mid-60s. Slight breeze.

New Hampshire: Mostly sunny with highs in the low to mid-60s.

Vermont/Maine: Mostly sunny with highs in the low to mid-60s.

Connecticut: Some clouds, but some sun, too. Highs in the mid-60s.
