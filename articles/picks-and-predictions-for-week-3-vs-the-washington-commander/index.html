Picks And Predictions For Week 3 vs. the Washington Commanders

TheTools · 27.09.2026 18:13 · source · permalink

The Seattle Seahawks take on the Washington Commanders in Week 3 at Northwest Stadium.

The Seattle Seahawks face the Commanders on September 27 at Northwest Stadium as part of Week 3 of the 2026 NFL season. Kickoff is set for 10:00 a.m. PT. Here's what local and national media are predicting:

Mike Florio, NBC Sports: Seahawks 24, Commanders 7

"Once upon a time, Dan Quinn was the Seahawks' defensive coordinator. Those days are gone forever. (Although he could be a defensive coordinator again, as soon as next year.)"

Vic Tafur, The Athletic: Seahawks

"There is a quarterback shortage in this country, best shown by Mariota popping up as a starter somewhere seemingly every season. Against bad teams, he can sometimes overcome his inaccuracy with his mobility, but here, he gets the Seahawks' Cover 6-heavy defense — complete with two defensive tackles who don't budge from run blocks — which is allowing only 125 passing yards a game. The Commanders have not thrown a TD against a Cover 6 defense since Week 13 of 2024. The Commanders fall to 0-3, but Daniels' injury might save Dan Quinn's job because no one expects him to win with Mariota."

Pete Prisco, CBS Sports: Seahawks 24, Commanders 13

"The Seahawks are out on the road for the second straight week, but they get a break playing Commanders backup quarterback Marcus Mariota. With Jayden Daniels out, the Seattle defense will have a much easier day. Drew Lock played well last week, and that will continue here. Seattle takes it."

Pamela Maldonado, EPSN: Seahawks 27, Commanders 20

Eric Moody, EPSN: Seahawks 27, Commanders 14

Seth Walder, EPSN: Seahawks 23, Commanders 9

Jeremy Bergman, NFL.com: Seahawks 27, Commanders 17

Brooke Cersosimo, NFL.com: Seahawks 24, Commanders 18

Gennaro Filice, NFL.com: Seahawks 30, Commanders 13

Bobby Kownack, NFL.com: Seahawks 21, Commanders 13

Dan Parr, NFL.com: Seahawks 27, Commanders 15
