Dozens of beachgoers witness swimmer killed by shark at WA beach

TheTools · 18.09.2026 03:12 · source · permalink

Fatal great white shark attack at Sorrento beach

Fatal great white shark attack at Sorrento beach

Beachgoers have recounted the moment a sunny spring day turned to horror in an instant when a swimmer was taken by a shark at a popular beach on Friday.

Witnesses at Sorrento Beach, in Perth’s north, told 7NEWS the man was in the water with a friend when he was taken by a monster predator about 15m from shore.

Police, including Water Police and the Police Air Wing, and paramedics raced to the scene after reports of a shark attack about 10.30am.

Just before 3pm, police confirmed the swimmer had died.

Dozens of people saw the attack, which happened where school students had been swimming earlier in the morning.

The incident has been described as like a scene out of Jaws, with witnesses watching helplessly as the man’s body was tossed into the air.

Ingrid Michell said the man was dragged out into deeper water “at a rate of knots”.

“It obviously was a big shark,” she said.

The nurse said her first instinct was to see if she could help, but there was nothing that could be done.

“To see somebody being mauled to death in the water in front of you is hard,” she said.

She added: “There’s no way I’m going to be getting in that water again.”

Another witness said the attack “happened so fast” and “was like a scene off Jaws”.

“I just can’t believe it,” Darrel Haythornthwaite said, “especially in a place like this”.

The missing man’s friend made it back to shore but was inconsolable.

“He felt bad because he didn’t go back to save his mate and I said, ‘Nah you couldn’t have done that’,” Haythornthwaite said.

“There’s nothing that anyone could have done.”

WA Police were patrolling the beach, and a chopper was seen hovering just off the shoreline.

Locals say sharks have been spotted circling local waters in recent weeks, and there are questions about where enough was done to stop a tragedy like this.

A troubled shark barrier was removed in 2020.

The search will resume at first light on Saturday.

The death comes just days after 56-year-old surfer Mel Ismail was mauled by a shark at Glenfield Beach in Geraldton, 420km north of Perth, on Monday.

He was dragged to shore by a stranger who had just arrived at the beach and provided first aid before being rushed to hospital.

He suffered extensive injuries to his right leg, which had to be amputated below the shin.
