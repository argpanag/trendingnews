Report: Buccaneers, QB Baker Mayfield agree to three-year extension

TheTools · 08.09.2026 17:20 · source · permalink

The Tampa Bay Buccaneers have locked up their quarterback for the rest of the decade.

Baker Mayfield and the Buccaneers have agreed to a three-year contract extension on the eve of the regular season, according to ESPN's Adam Schefter.

The extension is worth $165 million, according to Schefter.

The new deal keeps Mayfield under centre in Tampa Bay through the 2029 season, and takes him off the free-agent market after this one.

Mayfield, 31, is entering his fourth season in Tampa and has enjoyed his best seasons with the Buccaneers. He has eclipsed 4,000 yards twice in a Buccaneers uniform and last year passed for 3,693 yards and 26 touchdowns while starting all 17 games.

With Mayfield leading the way, the Buccaneers made the playoffs in 2023 and 2024, and just missed last season with an 8-9 record.
