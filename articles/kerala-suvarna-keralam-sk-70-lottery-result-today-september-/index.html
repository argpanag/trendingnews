The Kerala Lottery Suvarna Keralam SK-70 draw is scheduled for today, Friday, September 18, 2026, with a first prize of ₹1 crore. The weekly lottery is being conducted by the Kerala State Lotteries Department, with the winning numbers expected to be announced after the afternoon draw. The ticket price for the Suvarna Keralam lottery is ₹50.

The SK-70 draw is the latest edition of the Suvarna Keralam weekly lottery, which is conducted every Friday. The draw is scheduled to take place at Gorky Bhavan near Bakery Junction in Thiruvananthapuram. The first prize is ₹1 crore, while the second prize is ₹30 lakh and the third prize is ₹5 lakh. A consolation prize of ₹5,000 is also available for eligible tickets in other series.

The SK-70 draw has multiple prize categories, giving ticket holders several opportunities to win. The top three prizes are common across all series, while several lower-tier prizes are awarded based on the last four digits of the ticket number. The published prize structure is as follows:

The published details for SK-70 list the draw number as SK-70 and the draw date as September 18, 2026. The previous Suvarna Keralam draw, SK-69, was held on September 11, with ticket number RA 686822 winning the ₹1 crore first prize.

The SK-70 draw is scheduled for the afternoon at Gorky Bhavan in Thiruvananthapuram. Current result listings place the draw at around 3 p.m. IST, with the official winning numbers expected to be published after the draw. As the result is not yet available at the time of writing, candidates should wait for the official result before treating any number circulating online as confirmed.

Once the result is announced, ticket holders should compare their complete ticket number and series with the published winning numbers. Results for lower prize categories are determined by the specified last four digits, while the major prizes are awarded to the full winning ticket numbers.

Ticket holders can check the Suvarna Keralam SK-70 result after the draw through the Kerala State Lotteries Department's official result publication. The winning numbers are also reproduced by several result platforms, but the official lottery publication should be treated as the final source for verification.

Prize winners should verify their tickets against the official result before making a claim. The ticket and required identity documents may need to be submitted to the appropriate lottery authority depending on the prize amount. Winners should also check the latest instructions issued by the Kerala State Lotteries Department regarding claim deadlines and documentation.

The Suvarna Keralam lottery is part of Kerala's weekly state lottery programme. With the SK-70 draw offering a ₹1 crore first prize, attention is focused on the winning numbers that will be released following today's draw.
