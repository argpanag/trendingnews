Celtics will be without 3 key players against Kings

TheTools · 21.11.2026 23:34 · source · permalink
  • Celtics News

Jayson Tatum, Derrick White, and Mitchell Robinson are all out as the Celtics face the Kings on the first night of a back-to-back.

SACRAMENTO — The Celtics will be without Jayson Tatum, Derrick White, and Mitchell Robinson when they face the Sacramento Kings on Saturday night.

For the first time this season, Tatum is resting on the first night of a back-to-back; the Celtics play the Warriors on Sunday at 8:30pm ET. White is out with a left AC joint contusion (the acromioclavicular joint connects the collarbone to the top of the shoulder blade), while Robinson is out with illness.

Tatum played in both nights of the Celtics’ first three back-to-backs of the season, and said in Charlotte that it would be something the time would play by ear.

“It’s my 10th year, so I understand the balance of wanting to play every game and rest and back-to-backs, three in four nights, five in seven,” Tatum said. “It’s always a conversation. We talk about it. We have a plan. I don’t really like sitting out on away games, but we just kind of figure it out as we go.”

Tatum said he did feel good in the back-to-backs that he played in.

“I used to always play back-to-backs, and obviously health is most important, and how I’m feeling physically,” Tatum said. “But it was an opportunity for me to test myself out to see how I felt playing the back-to-backs, traveling, getting in at a certain time. See how I felt after that, and I kind of just really wanted to go through that for myself, and I felt really good.”

White will miss his first game of the season, while Robinson will miss his second (he missed one earlier this season due to rest).

With three rotation players out, it’s likely that backups like Sam Hauser, Baylor Scheierman, Hugo Gonzalez, Luka Garza, and Chris Cenac Jr. will alll see increased minutes.

The Kings, who are 1-14 on the season, will be without Ben Simmons, who is out for the season with a right Achilles tendon rupture. Maxime Raynaud is out with a right patella subluxation, and Precious Achiuwa is questionable with a left low leg contusion.

Celtics-Kings will tip off at 10pm ET.
