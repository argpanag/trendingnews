ST. CLAIRSVILLE, Ohio — A familiar toy brand is returning to the Ohio Valley Mall this fall, with Toys“R”Us set to open a new shop in October.

The store will be operated by Go Retail Group and will take up about 2,000 square feet at the mall’s center court. Mall officials say the opening is expected to bring more than just toys, but also a sense of sentimentality for shoppers.

“Nostalgia for so many of our shoppers where a kid can be a kid and the mall definitely will benefit and the shoppers from having toys inside the mall,” said Candi Noble-Greathouse, marketing director for the mall. “Many of our stores do offer toys but this is a toy store.”

The store will offer an assortment of popular toys and brands. An official opening date has not been announced.
