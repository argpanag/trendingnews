Status report

Brady Tkachuk will play his first game in Ottawa since the Panthers acquired him in a trade with the Senators on June 21. ... Markstrom is out 1-2 weeks; the goalie was injured during practice Monday; Schmid will start and Black, recalled from Charlotte of the American Hockey League along with Schwindt on Tuesday, will back up. ... Gudas will return after missing three games with a lower-body injury. ... Sanderson will return after missing eight games; the defenseman sustained a lower-body injury in a season-opening 3-2 win at the Toronto Maple Leafs on Oct. 3. ... Hodgson will replace Burakovsky, a forward.
