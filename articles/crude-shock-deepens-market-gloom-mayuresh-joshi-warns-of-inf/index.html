Crude oil prices, rising bond yields and currency pressure are weighing on global equity markets, with Indian stocks opening sharply lower. Prolonged oil price volatility could intensify inflationary risks and put pressure on corporate earnings across sectors. Mayuresh Joshi, Head Equity Research, William O Neil India, explains how global markets are responding to these concerns and what investors should watch in the coming weeks. He also highlights the possibility of a quick recovery if geopolitical tensions ease and oil prices stabilise, potentially supporting Q3 earnings. The market outlook remains cautious amid heightened uncertainty and persistent inflation concerns.
