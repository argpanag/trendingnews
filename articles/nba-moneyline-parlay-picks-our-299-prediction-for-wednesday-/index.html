NBA Moneyline Parlay Picks for Wednesday, November 25

  • Sacramento Kings Moneyline (+220)
  • Houston Rockets Moneyline (-400)

Parlay Odds: +299

Kings vs Warriors Moneyline Pick

The Kings square off against Golden State on Wednesday night at Chase Center, coming in at +220 to pull off the outright upset.

Despite how poorly Sacramento has played all year — winning just two of its first 17 games — this is a prime opportunity to back the road underdog against a short-handed Warriors squad missing Steph Curry.

Warriors forward Yaxel Lendeborg has been phenomenal this year, racking up nearly 20 PPG and over seven rebounds per contest. Lendeborg boasts impressive versatility at both ends of the floor, but he headlines a relatively undersized frontcourt lacking a true center.

That could prove problematic against a Sacramento frontcourt anchored by center Domantas Sabonis, who ranks second in the NBA at 13.5 rebounds per game.

On top of the visitors' size advantage, Lendeborg is questionable for Wednesday night with an illness, which would make this an even better spot to target the upset. Without Lendeborg, Golden State lacks an elite scoring threat — Steve Kerr’s bunch will also be missing Jimmy Butler, leaving guard Brandin Podziemski as the next leading scorer.

On the other side, guard Darius Acuff Jr. is putting together a remarkable campaign, averaging nearly 25 PPG to rank inside the top 15 in NBA scoring. The rookie also serves as the primary facilitator for the offense, dishing out 7.2 assists per game (9th in the NBA).

With a potential edge in both the backcourt and frontcourt, Sacramento’s +220 moneyline offers significant value against a host that could be rolling out an extremely underwhelming lineup the night before Thanksgiving.

Pick: Kings Moneyline (+220)

Suns vs Rockets Moneyline Pick

For the second leg of my Wednesday night NBA parlay, I feel confident backing the Rockets at -400 to beat the Suns outright at Toyota Center. Houston has been on an absolute tear at home this season, winning eight of its first nine games in front of the home crowd.

The loaded lineup features center Alperen Sengun, forward Kevin Durant, and forward Amen Thompson — all of whom are dominating statistically. Sengun has been a monster at both ends of the floor, leading the team in points, rebounds, and assists; the frontcourt boasts massive interior size, with Durant and Jabari Smith Jr. also making it tough on opponents to find open looks near the basket.

While Houston possesses one of the strongest interiors in the league, the same can't be said for Phoenix, which struggles to score inside the paint.

Even with Miles Bridges and Devin Booker thriving statistically, the visitors' frontcourt has been terrible offensively while starting center Mark Williams remains sidelined with an injury — however, Khaman Maluach and Oso Ighodaro have been solid in Williams's absence.

Booker should still produce as usual, though he draws a brutal defensive matchup against Thompson, currently one of the premier on-ball defenders in the NBA.

Outside of Booker, Bridges, and guard Jalen Green — who just returned to the lineup — the Suns may struggle to execute offensive sets in the halfcourt. Houston enters as a massive favorite on Wednesday night and should round out our +300 moneyline parlay quite nicely.

Pick: Rockets Moneyline (-400)
