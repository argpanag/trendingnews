'Avengers: Doomsday' Runtime Officially Confirmed, And It Breaks a Major MCU Precedent

TheTools · 22.11.2026 20:25 · source · permalink

In less than four weeks, Marvel Studios will return to theaters with its biggest movie ever, Avengers: Doomsday, on Dec. 18. Directors Joe and Anthony Russo return for their first MCU movie in seven years alongside former Iron Man star Robert Downey Jr., who takes on a new role as Doctor Doom. Behind them, the biggest cast of stars of any comic book movie in history will assemble in an attempt to stop the destruction of the multiverse.

One of the biggest discussions surrounding this movie has centered on its runtime, given that Avengers movies are typically long outings in theaters. This is especially the case now with ticket presales breaking financial records, as Doomsday is expected to at least challenge for the biggest opening weekend at the box office in cinema history. As the sequel inches closer to its debut, one of its directors has also teased what to expect from that runtime, marking a change of pace for the MCU.

Avengers: Doomsday co-director Joe Russo indicated that the movie will have a 2-hour-30-minute runtime. Speaking at the Qatar Film Committee's second Doha Industry Days (via Deadline), he discussed the marketing for the new sequel and explained that these pieces of footage will all "equal about two and a half hours of your time on December 18:"

"We've done a series of tags around these characters because the story actually started in January of last year, and you've been watching it slowly unfold and these are all pieces of something that are going to equal two and a half hours of your time on December 18."

"We've done a series of tags around these characters because the story actually started in January of last year, and you've been watching it slowly unfold and these are all pieces of something that are going to equal two and a half hours of your time on December 18."

While this would make Doomsday the second-longest movie in the Avengers franchise, it would still be 31 minutes shorter than 2019's Avengers: Endgame. That film came to theaters with a 3-hour-1-minute runtime, making it the longest movie in MCU history by a wide margin (which still stands to this day. Doomsday would also be the fourth-longest movie in MCU history behind Endgame, Black Panther: Wakanda Forever (2 hours, 41 minutes), and Eternals (2 hours, 37 minutes).

Looking back at MCU history, every Avengers movie released thus far has been at least 2 hours and 21 minutes long. While Doomsday does not exceed that length by much, it breaks a long trend seen from movies in the Multiverse Saga. Specifically, only two movies (Eternals and Black Panther 2) have run longer than two and a half hours, and nearly a dozen movies have hovered around two hours (including The Marvels ranking as the shortest movie in MCU history).

Reports have been consistent that Doomsday is about two and a half hours long, while other rumors point to 2027's Avengers: Secret Wars coming closer to Endgame's three-hour length. With close to three dozen characters confirmed for this year's Avengers sequel (and more expected to come later), the question now is how much screen time the individual heroes will get as Downey embraces the leading role through his new villain.
