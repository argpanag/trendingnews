Miley Cyrus is married: What to know about her husband, wedding

TheTools · 24.11.2026 17:18 · source · permalink
  • Culture

Miley Cyrus is a married woman!

The "Let's Get Married" singer tied the knot with musician, songwriter, and producer Maxx Morando, 28, on Nov. 21, two days before her 34th birthday.

Cyrus shared photos from their nuptials on Instagram.

"M + M 🤍 11.21.26," Cyrus captioned the carousel of black-and-white photos, which feature the couple in front of a white backdrop and sharing their first kiss after their wedding ceremony.

Below, take a look back at Cyrus and Morando's love story as they embark on a new chapter together.

What to know about Maxx Morando

Like Cyrus, Morando is also a musician and is a drummer for the band Liily.

Morando is a Los Angeles native and formed Liily in 2015 with frontman and vocalist Dylan Nash, guitarist Sam De La Torre, and bassist Charlie Anastasis.

Liily performed their song "Applause" on "Miley's New Year's Eve Party" in 2022.

Morando and Cyrus were first linked romantically in 2021, according to People.

Cyrus opened up about the pair's relationship to British Vogue in 2023 and revealed that they first met on a blind date.

"We got put on a blind date," she said at the time. "Well, it was blind for me and not really for him. I thought, 'The worst that can happen is I leave.'"

Engagement

The three-time Grammy winner confirmed to "Good Morning America" in 2025 that she and Morando were engaged after four years of dating.

Cyrus had sparked talk of an engagement after she was photographed that December wearing what appeared to be an engagement ring at the "Avatar: Fire and Ash" premiere, which she attended with Morando.

Cyrus opened up about how much Morando meant to her in an interview published in The Cut in September 2025.

"I ended up with a person who means a lot to me and treats me really well and respects me," she said at the time. "I had to learn that the hard way because my mommy taught me the wrong way and then I had to learn the right way by myself."

Proposal

Morando asked Cyrus to marry him while they were on vacation in Kyoto, Japan.

The musician opened up to Wonderland magazine this past July about keeping his plans under wraps before he asked for Cyrus' hand in marriage.

"There were talks before we went about me popping the question but I was trying to keep it secret," Morando said, adding that he "had to be really sneaky about it."

He continued, "We went to Tokyo first for a couple days and then on to Kyoto. I think she had a sense that it was coming, so I was waiting until the very last moment of the very last day to throw her off the scent as much as I could."

Cyrus opened up to "GMA" in December 2025, calling Morando a "great partner," and saying he "did good" when he proposed.

"Me and Max have been together for four years, and it's very obvious the drastic amount of growth that I've had in those four years ... and that really, a lot of that is because I have a great partner," she said at the time. "You just want to dream the biggest you can, and you want to have someone by your side that always tells you you can do it."

Morando supported Cyrus in May when she received a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame, posing for photographs with her at the event.

Cyrus was previously married to actor Liam Hemsworth. The former couple divorced in 2020.
