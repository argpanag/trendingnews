Does Sirius XM Holdings Look Undervalued on Cash Flow?

The Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) model here focuses on the cash that Sirius XM Holdings can send to shareholders over time. Latest twelve month free cash flow sits at about $1.47b, and the projections used in the DCF assume that this stream broadly grows from that base rather than shrinking away.

What really matters is how that cash picture compares with the trading level. On these assumptions, the DCF projections put Sirius XM Holdings' estimated intrinsic value substantially above the current share price of $27.25. That suggests the market is pricing the business on more cautious cash flow expectations than the model.
