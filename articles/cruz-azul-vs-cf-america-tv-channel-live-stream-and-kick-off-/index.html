Cruz Azul vs CF America: TV channel, live stream and kick-off time

Cruz Azul host CF America at Estadio Banorte in a Liga MX Apertura clash with real stakes for both clubs. The league leaders face a stern examination of where La Máquina stand this season.

According to Goal.com, Cruz Azul have two wins from their last five, most recently a 1-0 success over Santos Laguna. That steadied them after a difficult spell, although inconsistency still defines their Apertura.

America arrive as table toppers, with three victories in their last five across all competitions. Under Guillermo Almada they score freely and defend with discipline, and they will look to widen their lead with a result on the road.

Recent outcomes include a 2-0 Liga MX win against Puebla and a 2-0 Leagues Cup victory over Columbus Crew. Back-to-back Leagues Cup defeats to Monterrey and Leon add a note of caution.

The head-to-head is tight, with their most recent meeting ending 1-1 in April 2026. A home win would do more than bank three points, it would signal genuine Apertura intent from Cruz Azul.
