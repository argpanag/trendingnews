Chilly mornings and mild afternoons will stick around for some time thanks to a parade of cold fronts on display

TheTools · 04.11.2026 22:43 · source · permalink

EAST TEXAS (KTRE) - The clear skies and dry air will lead to a chilly night with overnight lows in the middle 40’s. There could be some low clouds and ground fog in the morning, but that will lift, giving way to a mostly sunny sky on Thursday with highs climbing into the upper 70’s.

Another trough of low pressure will drag in another fast-moving cold front on Thursday night into Friday morning. Other than some clouds moving in, this front should come through on the dry side given the lack of moisture we have to work with.

The cool, refreshing air will be here all weekend and going into next week, which will give our air conditioners a much-needed break.

Morning lows will generally be in the 40’s and 50’s with highs in the 60’s and 70’s from now through next week. It will remain festively chilly as sweater weather will be here to stay.

A cutoff area of low pressure over the desert southwest will throw some clouds in our direction for the weekend. If this system edges closer to Texas, we could see a few sprinkles come our way late Saturday night and during the day on Sunday. This thick cloud cover will also play a role in keeping our highs in the 60’s.

We will then go back to mostly sunny skies by next week as chilly mornings continue to give way to mild, but comfortable afternoons.
