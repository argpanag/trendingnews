Three Russian helicopter crews reportedly killed during drone strikes near St. Petersburg

TheTools · 13.11.2026 14:21 · source · permalink

A pro-Russian Z-channel published a black-and-white photo of Mi-8 and Ka-52 helicopters. (Photo: Ya i drug moy vertolyot/Telegram/Screenshot)

Russian pro-war Telegram channels reported that three military helicopter crews were killed during a drone attack on St. Petersburg and Russia’s Leningrad Oblast, Mediazona and The Insider reported on Nov. 11.

The outlets examined posts by pro-Russian Z-channels reporting the deaths of pilots and helicopter crashes. In particular, the propaganda channel Voevoda Veshchayet reported that three crews had been killed, without specifying what type of aircraft they operated.

The Voyenny Osvedomitel channel reported that two helicopters had crashed, possibly a Mi-8 and a Ka-52 Alligator. Another channel, Ya i Drug Moy Vertolyot, published poems about the pilots’ deaths alongside a black-and-white image of helicopters of those models.

Ukrainian monitoring channel Exilenova+ had earlier reported that a Ka-52 helicopter had crashed.

The pro-Russian Fighterbomber channel also published a post reporting the deaths of three crews on Nov. 11.

Overnight on Nov. 11, drones attacked the port of Ust-Luga in Russia’s Leningrad Oblast, the country’s largest port on the Baltic Sea. A fire broke out at the facility following the strikes, and a glow was visible in the sky above the port.

The OSINT channel Astra later reported that a fire was burning at the northern production and technological cluster of the Novatek-Ust-Luga stable gas condensate fractionation and transshipment complex at the port.
