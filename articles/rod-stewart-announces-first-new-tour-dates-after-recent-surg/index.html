Rod Stewart announces first new tour dates after recent surgery

TheTools · 24.09.2026 09:09 · source · permalink

23 September 2026, 10:58 | Updated: 23 September 2026, 11:07

By Mayer Nissim

Sir Rod is coming back on the road!

Listen to this article

Rod Stewart was forced to cancel a number of live shows earlier this year after undergoing a heart procedure, sparking fears that he might have to retire from touring.

But worry not, as Sir Rod has now announced a series of live shows for 2027.

While he has not directly replaced the four weeks of cancelled concerts, the legendary singer has confirmed a trio of US shows for March 2027.

He will also play two back-to-back dates in September.

Tickets for the shows go on sale this Friday, September 25.

View this post on Instagram

Sir Rod's upcoming tour dates are as follows:

  • March 8 - Enmarket Arena, Savannah, GA
  • March 12 - Hard Rock Live, Hollywood, FL
  • March 16 - St. Augustine Amphitheatre, St. Augustine, FL
  • September 2 - Red Rocks Ampitheatre, Morrison, CO
  • September 3 - Red Rocks Ampitheatre, Morrison, CO

While he's been an ever-present on the road for decades Sir Rod has had to pull out of or postpone a number of concerts in recent years for health reasons.

There were shows missed in Vegas in 2025 and in Boston and California earlier this year.

He was also forced to pause during a show in Utah to take a puff from an oxygen tank.
