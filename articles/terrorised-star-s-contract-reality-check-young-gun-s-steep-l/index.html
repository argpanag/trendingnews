Terrorised star’s contract reality check; young gun’s steep learning curve — Dolphins Player Ratings

TheTools · 25.09.2026 13:30 · source · permalink

The Dolphins’ terrific season ended with a whimper inside Suncorp Stadium on Friday night.

It was a disappointing way to finish, with the Dolphins thoroughly outplayed by a classier Roosters outfit.

Read on for the Dolphins’ player ratings!

1 Hamiso Tabuai-Fidow 5

The Hammer didn’t get the open pastures the Warriors afforded him late in the Dolphins’ week one finals win, but still showed his class with a rifled pass to set up Jamayne Isaako’s opener. But there just wasn’t the usual magic the Dolphins have become so accustomed to.

2 Jamayne Isaako 5.5

Did he really get that ball down inside the chalk for the try? Surely not? Anyway, superb effort through the air, only matched by a sideline conversion from 30(!) metres out to beat 300 points for the season. Got a second try, too. But there was some bad too. Great servant for the Dolphins but Melbourne beckons.

3 Jack Bostock 5

He couldn’t believe he was sent for a concussion test on the direction of the independent doctor and couldn’t quite work his magic in the air in the first stanza. Had joy minutes into the second half when he did enough to be around the ball for James Tedesco’s fumble. Remarkably, first defeat of the season.

4 Herbie Farnworth 5.5

His two biggest contributions early were important tackles on Mark Nawaqanitawase and Siua Wong. That’s great, but the Dolphins needed his best to be in attack and only got a try late. But it will most be remembered for the pass which landed in Nawaqanitawase’s lap and led to a 90-metre intercept try. Couldn’t even mug Siua Wong for his customary one-on-one strip. Robert Toia had his measure.

5 Selwyn Cobbo 5

With the ink barely dry on his new mega deal, was kept relatively quiet in attack by a canny Roosters who preferred to kick to the Isaako-Tupou side. And then had Nawaqanitawase tormenting him in the second half. Couple of nice attacking touches late when the game was gone.

6 Kodi Nikorima 5

Last game as a Dolphin and will be having nightmares at Siua Wong and Nawaqanitawase barrelling at him. Couldn’t find the same magic with Isaiya Katoa in the halves. Showed a couple of small glimpses late on. Tough night.

7 Isaiya Katoa 4

Battled injury from opening exchanges after falling awkwardly under a Lindsay Collins tackle. Just never looked completely comfortable after that. On report for hip drop on Tupou and finished a forgettable first half with a bizarre kick straight into James Tedesco’s lap. Best play was in the dying minutes to help set up Herbie Farnworth’s effort. Will get more chances at this stage before his career is done. Huge learning curve.

8 Tom Flegler 5

The banana farmer from Tully probably never thought he’d be 80 minutes from a grand final again after his career-threatening injury. Forced to do a lot of tackling in a lung-bursting first sting and finished with just 34 run metres.

9 Jeremy Marshall-King 6

What would the Bulldogs do to have him back at No. 9? Only had one run in the first half and almost split them. Got through a team-high 44 tackles, but found it hard going against a physical Roosters rearguard and easily outpointed by Reece Robson. Not his side’s worst though.

10 Tom Gilbert 6.5

Best ground exponent since Hoover. Seemingly spent his first stint either army crawling on his back for extra inches or diving on loose balls. Tried his absolute best. As always, all heart.

11 Max Plath 5.5

Everywhere man who started on an edge and then made his customary shift into the middle. Maybe a little at fault for the crucial Victor Radley try in the first half. Had to truck through 37 tackles in 57 minutes which meant little energy in attack.

12 Kulikefu Finefeuiaki 6.5

Only Dolphins forward to crack 100 running metres and got a late consolation try, which came with his shorts accidentally being ripped down near his knees. Not the best night for his footing in defence but at least carried a small threat with ball in hand.

13 Morgan Knowles 5.5

Tried to set the tone with a great kick pressure on Sam Walker early, but made a silly high tackle on Mark Nawaqanitawase which invited the Roosters downfield before Daniel Tupou’s try. No missed tackles in defence, which was better than most of his mates.

14 Kurt Donoghoe 4

Guilty, I’m the No. 1 ticket holder in Donga’s fan club. But this was not a great one. A mistake with his first carry which led to a Victor Radley try, in which he couldn’t close a gap fast enough. Spent some time in the centres in lieu of Bostock too. Couldn’t create as much as usual in the middle as the Roosters middle men dominated. Finished with five missed tackles.

15 Ray Stone 5

One of the most fascinating NRL players who is known for barely saying a word. Should suit his new mixed martial arts career as the curtain came down on his rugby league days. Tried to add physicality, but powerless to stop the Roosters’ punch in the middle.

16 Francis Molo 5.5

One of the more effective Dolphins forwards in terms of run metres (89), but like most of his teammates, couldn’t make any significant impact.

17 Connelly Lemuelu 5

Couldn’t have his customary impact on the edge after coming off the bench.

18 Felise Kaufusi NA

Unused interchange.

19 Brad Schneider NA

Unused interchange.
