Klay Thompson sinks seven three-pointers vs. SAC

TheTools · 09.11.2026 05:24 · source · permalink

    Klay Thompson sinks seven three-pointers vs. SAC

    Klay Thompson

    MIA Guard-Forward #11
    Player Stats

    Klay Thompson amassed 23 points (8-of-15 FGs), two rebounds, four assists, two blocks and seven three-pointers in Sunday’s win over the Kings.

    Apparently, all Thompson needed to gain some semblance of consistency was to hit the road. He’s scored at least 19 points in three of the first four games of Miami’s five-game road trip, which concludes on Tuesday against the Jazz. On Sunday, Thompson shot 7-of-13 from beyond the arc, the third time this season he’s made at least that many triples in a game. Klay remains mainly a points and three-pointers option in fantasy leagues, but his assists and blocks were also solid against the Kings.
    - Raphielle Johnson
