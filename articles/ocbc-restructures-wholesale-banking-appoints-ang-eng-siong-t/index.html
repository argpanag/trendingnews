OCBC restructures wholesale banking, appoints Ang Eng Siong to head Global Corporate Banking

TheTools · 24.11.2026 05:19 · source · permalink

AI-translated article with transliterated names. Read original Chinese article here.

OCBC Bank has announced a restructuring of its wholesale banking business and appointed its current China chief executive, Mr Ang Eng Siong, 46, as head of Global Corporate Banking, effective 1 January 2027.

It is organising its wholesale banking business into three divisions to strengthen its ability to drive business growth under its Next Frontier corporate strategy.

Ms Elaine Lam, 55, the current head of Global Corporate Banking (GWB 1), will head the new Global Institutional Banking, Investment Banking and Transaction Banking division (GWB 3).

Announcing the changes after the market closed on Monday (23 November), OCBC said the transformational reorganisation aims to sharpen its focus on serving large corporates, multinational companies, financial institutions, the public sector and small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).

The restructuring will take effect on 1 January 2027, alongside changes in senior leadership roles.

OCBC group chief executive Mr Tan Teck Long said: “All companies, including banks, are navigating a volatile and fast-evolving environment. A transformational reorganisation of our wholesale banking business will position us even better to elevate how we serve customers, and meet both the challenges and opportunities facing us.

“There will now be a sharper, stronger focus under the new division, GWB 3, on products and structured solutions to support large corporates and SMEs – not just locally but globally, to accelerate business growth and keep us comprehensively executing under our Next Frontier strategy.

“Ang Eng Siong and Elaine Lam have spent many years at OCBC, gaining extensive experience across various areas of wholesale banking. I am confident that their experience and deep expertise will provide a strong foundation for them to succeed in their new roles.”

Ang will remain chief executive of OCBC China until a successor is appointed. The appointment will be announced in due course. Global Corporate Banking will further expand OCBC’s corporate banking business by deepening its penetration of the large corporate segment in core markets including Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia and Greater China, as well as across its overseas branches.

OCBC said in its statement that the new Global Institutional Banking, Investment Banking and Transaction Banking division will have a broader mandate to strengthen product and solution capabilities in transaction banking, investment banking, structured finance and financial institutions, while developing new growth platforms. By broadening its global institutional and sector coverage, it will drive business development and the formulation of strategic solutions to deepen cross-selling.

Global Commercial Banking (GWB 2) will continue to be led by Ms Elaine Heng, who will focus on gaining a larger share of the small and medium-sized enterprise market.

Ang joined OCBC as a management trainee in July 2009. He moved to China in 2015 to join the corporate banking team and became chief executive of OCBC China in 2023.

Lam joined OCBC in 2001 as a relationship manager. She has headed Global Corporate Banking since 2016.
