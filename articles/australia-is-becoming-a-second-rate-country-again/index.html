Australia is becoming a second-rate country again

TheTools · 25.09.2026 01:39 · source · permalink

Opinion | Michael Stutchbury

Bigger government, higher taxes, more expensive energy, more regulated workplaces and closing off natural growth options will make Australia second-rate again.

Critics of this week’s Intergenerational Report queried its assumption that artificial intelligence would rescue Australia from its decade-long productivity drought.

But even this hopeful assumption would leave a sub-par future that Treasury and the government of the day seem satisfied to bequeath to the next generation.
