Scores, live updates from Week 1 of Bayside Conference high school football

TheTools · 07.09.2026 11:44 · source · permalink

The High School Football season kicks off tonight across Maryland's Eastern Shore, as Bayside Conference teams begin what they hope will be successful 2026 seasons. And Week 1 holds a strong slate of interesting games across the Bayside.

Kicking things off on Friday night will be five games involving Bayside South teams. Wi-Hi will be on the road to face Snow Hill, while Parkside hosts Delmar and Stephen Decatur hosts Laurel. Washington and James M. Bennett will also be on the road Friday night, as Washington makes the trip to Virginia to face Northampton, and the Bennett Clippers face Colonel Richardson.

Other big games taking place across the Bayside Conference includes Cambridge-South Dorchester at Kent Island, and Easton vs. Caesar Rodney.
