PlayStation issues GTA 6 software warning before launch

TheTools · 18.11.2026 21:18 · source · permalink

Owners of PlayStation 5 consoles have been told they must update their software before they can play Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6) when it launches on Thursday.

“Sony Interactive has issued a PS5 system software update that is required before playing Grand Theft Auto VI,” PlayStation Support US wrote on X. “Players should download this update prior to midnight on November 19 so that they’re ready to play on launch day, November 19.”

GTA 6 is arriving more than a decade after the release of Grand Theft Auto V, making Thursday’s launch a major event for players who have waited years for Rockstar Games' next installment.

Rockstar has advised players to prepare their consoles ahead of the release, given it requires up to 212 GB of space.

The game becomes playable at midnight local time on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S consoles.

Newsweek has contacted Rockstar Games for comment via email. Newsweek also reached out to Sony Interactive Entertainment via online contact form.

More Than 15 Million Pre-Orders—Estimate

Even before its launch, the game has made more than $1.5 billion in sales, according to an unofficial estimate.

A tracker from Sensor Tower, a market research firm, says the game had an estimated 15.88 million digital pre-orders across PS5 and Xbox Series X|S as of Tuesday. Those sales were worth approximately $1.54 billion, according to the tracker.

Rockstar Games has not confirmed how many pre-orders have been placed.

Grand Theft Auto V is one of the bestselling video games ever made and reached $1 billion in retail sales in just three days.

Altogether, the GTA franchise has sold more than 470 million units worldwide, with Grand Theft Auto V accounting for more than 230 million of those sales.

Players Told to Check Their Consoles

Rockstar Games says the GTA 6 pre-load requires up to 212GB of available space, depending on the device.

Players have been advised they may need to free storage on their consoles, configure automatic downloads and allow enough time for the installation to finish.

Players short on internal space can also expand their console storage; PS5 supports compatible M.2 NVMe SSDs, while Xbox Series X|S supports dedicated Storage Expansion Cards capable of running current-generation games directly.

Automatic downloading also depends on the console's settings. PS5 owners need auto-downloads enabled and the machine either switched on or in Rest Mode. Xbox players need automatic updates enabled and their console switched on or in Sleep mode.

What Players Should Do Before Launch

PlayStation recommends checking that a pre-order was successful, enabling auto-download, keeping the PS5 connected to the internet in Rest Mode and making sure its system software is up to date. Players can check remaining capacity under Settings > Storage and confirm whether the pre-load has started through their Game Library.

Xbox Series X|S players should also make sure they have enough free storage and that automatic updates are enabled. Rockstar says an Xbox must be switched on or placed in Sleep mode for automatic pre-loading to work.

If a pre-load stalls or returns an error, players should first check their console’s internet connection and available storage, then restart the download if necessary. Rockstar also directs PS5 and Xbox Series X|S users experiencing pre-load problems to the relevant PlayStation or Xbox troubleshooting guidance.
