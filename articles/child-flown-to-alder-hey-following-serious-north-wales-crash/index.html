Child flown to Alder Hey following 'serious' North Wales crash

TheTools · 05.09.2026 11:58 · source · permalink

A child was flown to hospital last night following a "serious" collision. The accident took place on Dean Road, Wrexham at around tea-time and forced the road to close for several hours as emergency teams worked at the scene.

North Wales Police and paramedics attended and an air ambulance was deployed.

The child was flown to Alder Hey Hospital for treatment.

North Wales Police closed Dean Road last night, from the junction with Borras Road to the roundabout, as a result of the accident.

Motorists were advised to avoid the area as officers worked to have the road cleared. The road has since reopened.

Police, the Welsh Ambulance Service and the Wales Air Ambulance service have been approached for comment.

Unconfirmed reports suggest the collision may have involved an e-scooter and car.
