Cowboys vs Packers Picks & Predictions | NFL Week 6 2026 | Betway Insider

TheTools · 18.10.2026 01:10 · source · permalink

Our Cowboys vs Packers betting picks for NFL Week 6 2026 include odds analysis, best bets, key matchups and picks for your same-game parlay

The Dallas Cowboys head to Lambeau Field for tomorrow night’s NFL Week 6 showdown against the Green Bay Packers, looking to rebound from an ugly home loss to the Tampa Bay Buccaneers, who didn’t have Baker Mayfield.

That was one of the worst performances by the Cowboys in recent memory.

Game time tomorrow is 8:20 p.m. EST.

Bettors face an intriguing matchup between teams with contrasting strengths and weaknesses.

Dallas has an elite offence led by Dak Prescott and CeeDee Lamb, but its defence ranks second-to-last in the NFL, allowing 395.4 yards per game.

The Packers, meanwhile, under QB Jordan Love, have surged lately, 3-2, coming off an impressive 31-7 win over the Chicago Bears.

Love, third in the NFL with 1,408 passing yards, for a 7.6-yard average and 10 touchdowns, versus Dallas’ secondary is a key matchup. If Green Bay can establish the run to create play-action opportunities, that could tilt the game. Love’s passing yards and touchdown props are worth watching.

Lamb versus the Packers’ secondary is another one. Dallas needs Prescott to sustain drives and convert third downs.

Watch for Lamb’s receptions and receiving yards, especially if Dallas plays from behind.

Here are my Cowboys vs Packers picks.

Cowboys vs Packers Odds

In NFL betting at Betway, the game is essentially a toss-up, with both teams listed at -110 on the moneyline. Green Bay is a 1.5-point spread favourite at +100, while Dallas is getting 1.5 points at -120. The over/under is set at a lofty 53.5 points, with both sides priced at -110.

With both teams at -110, bettors backing Dallas might prefer the moneyline rather than paying -120 for just 1.5 points of protection. That 53.5-point total is significant, though; oddsmakers expect a high-scoring game. That means a focus on quarterback passing yards, receiving props and anytime touchdown markets.

Cowboys vs Packers Betting Trends

With my NFL picks, I said I wouldn’t do this again, after the Tampa debacle, but I am going to take Cowboys +1.5. The betting line has been moving toward Dallas.

Dallas has a strong offensive profile, with Prescott, Lamb, George Pickens, and Javonte Williams, while Green Bay is dealing with significant attrition up front.

DT Javon Hargrave and DT Jonathan Ford are out, while DT Anthony Campbell is questionable. CB Benjamin St-Juste is out, and CB Brandon Cisse is questionable. And remember – no Micah Parsons.

Dallas is 2-0 against the spread as an underdog this season. Plus, they’ve had the extra rest.

I also like the over 53.5, at -110. Both teams tied 40-40 last season. The Cowboys defence ranks among the worst in the NFL, while Green Bay just put up a big passing performance; Love threw for 367 yards and two touchdowns in a big win over Chicago.

Two QBs who can really air it out.

I’ll also go the player-prop route in my NFL picks: Prescott Over 275 passing yards at -115. The Dallas QB is averaging 276 yards per game, including 335 yards against Houston and 316 yards against Tampa.

With the Cowboys coming in as underdogs, that creates a more pass-happy game. Plus – the Packers haven’t faced a lot of elite quarterbacks, which Prescott is. And I keep going back to those Packers injuries.

Cowboys vs Packers Same Game Parlay Picks

I’m banking on a high-scoring game and a Prescott bounce-back performance. So, I’m backing Prescott 275+ passing yards, Lamb 70+ receiving yards, and Love 250+ passing yards for a combined +240 at Betway. If you’re leaning Green Bay, Love 250+ passing yards and Christian Watson anytime touchdown at +210.
