All you need to know about Richard Hammond's production company closing down

TheTools · 20.10.2026 09:56 · source · permalink

Richard Hammond and his ex-wife, Mindy, are set to receive a pay-out of around £4 million after their production company shut down.

Here is all you need to know about Richard Hammond's Hamster Wheel Productions.

Hamster's Wheel Productions

Richard Hammond established Hamster's Wheel Productions Limited in January 2008 as his own television production business.

The company's registered office is in London, while it has strong links to Herefordshire through Hammond, who lives in the county.

Hammond also operates The Smallest Cog, his classic vehicle restoration business near Hereford. The production company has supported projects associated with his television and media ventures.

What the company does

Companies House lists the business under SIC code 59113, covering television programme production activities.

It has been involved in producing and developing factual entertainment and motoring-related programming.

Richard Hammond's role

Richard Mark Hammond, known for presenting Top Gear and The Grand Tour, has served as a director since the company was incorporated.

His wife, Amanda Hammond, has also held director and company secretary roles within the business.

The company name

The name refers to Hammond's long-standing nickname, "The Hamster", which he acquired during his years on Top Gear.

"Hamster's Wheel Productions" combines that nickname with his television career.

Company status and pay-out

Companies House records show the company is in liquidation proceedings as of 2026.

It was incorporated on January 16, 2008, making it one of Hammond's longest-running business ventures.

Richard Hammond and his ex-wife, Mindy, are set to receive a pay-out of around £4 million after the production company shut down.

The company formed part of Hammond's media portfolio for nearly two decades, alongside ventures including The Smallest Cog.

The liquidation brings an end to one of the businesses connected to Hammond's television and production career.
