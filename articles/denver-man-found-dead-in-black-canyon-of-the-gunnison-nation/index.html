Denver man found dead in Black Canyon of the Gunnison National Park

TheTools · 05.11.2026 15:38 · source · permalink

John Countryman, 57, was found dead in Black Canyon on Oct. 28

An abandoned car in Black Canyon of the Gunnison National Park launched an investigation that led deputies in western Colorado to the body of a missing Denver man, found on the canyon floor, sheriff’s officials said.

Montrose County deputies responded to reports of an abandoned car along Black Canyon’s south rim shortly after 5:30 p.m. on Oct. 27, according to a news release from the sheriff’s office. Investigators believed the vehicle was related to a missing person.

“Personal belongings” were also found near Devil’s Lookout, an overlook reached by a 0.34-mile dirt trail, sheriff’s officials said. The sheriff’s office did not specify what was found by the overlook, but said it was collected as evidence.

A drone search of Black Canyon at about 6:30 a.m. the next day discovered human remains on the canyon floor, according to the sheriff’s office. Law enforcement recovered the body on Friday.

The Montrose County Coroner’s Office identified the remains as 57-year-old John Countryman of Denver. His cause and manner of death remained under investigation Thursday.
