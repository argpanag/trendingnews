Ben Verlander shares heartfelt tribute to Justin Verlander before brother’s final start

TheTools · 26.09.2026 16:16 · source · permalink

Detroit Tigers pitcher Justin Verlander makes the final start of his storied career on Saturday, Sept. 26 against the Pittsburgh Pirates.

The occasion means a little more to one observer than most: Verlander’s brother, Ben.

“Today my brother makes the final start of his career,” the “Flippin’ Bats” host and former Tigers minor leaguer wrote on Twitter/X. “It will be his first start in Detroit for the Tigers in 9 years and 37 days (August 2017). So many memories in Detroit. Baseball is my life. A huge reason for that is my brother. I fell in love with baseball watching him play.

“Today I get to watch him play one more time. Let’s do this. Go Tigers.”

Verlander announced in July that this season would be his last. Prior to Saturday’s game, he was activated from the injured list, where he had resided since April because of hip inflammation.

Verlander ranks eighth in MLB history in career strikeouts (3,554) and 29th in games started (556). That’s the second-most appearances in MLB history by a pitcher to exclusively pitch as a starter; only Tom Glavine (682) had more.

In his first 11 seasons in the league, all with Detroit, Verlander won a Rookie of the Year, Cy Young, and MVP Award, twice leading the American League in wins and four times in strikeouts.

Verlander probably won’t throw his fourth career no-hitter, or catch Don Sutton for seventh place on baseball’s all-time strikeout list (he needs 21).

For Ben Verlander — and most fans — that’s OK. Verlander’s legacy is secure no matter how his 557th career start goes.
