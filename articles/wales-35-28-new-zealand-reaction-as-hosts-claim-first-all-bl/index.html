Wales 35-28 New Zealand: Reaction as hosts claim first All Blacks win in 73 years

TheTools · 14.11.2026 16:32 · source · permalink
  • Live Reporting (active)
  • Line-ups
  • Watch & listen2

Summary

  • Wales beat New Zealand for the first time since 1953

  • Tomos Williams, Dewi Lake and Archie Griffin cross for Wales, with New Zealand levelling at the break through Cortez Ratima, Wallace Sititi and Fehi Fineanganofo

  • Eddie James and Louis Rees-Zammit put Wales back in front before Peter Lakai pulls one back, but hosts hold on for historic win

Wales beat New Zealand for the first time since 1953

Tomos Williams, Dewi Lake and Archie Griffin cross for Wales, with New Zealand levelling at the break through Cortez Ratima, Wallace Sititi and Fehi Fineanganofo
