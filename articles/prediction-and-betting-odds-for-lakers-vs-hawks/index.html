Prediction and betting odds for Lakers vs. Hawks

TheTools · 10.11.2026 00:02 · source · permalink

The Los Angeles Lakers visit the Atlanta Hawks on Monday, and if you’re looking to do some last-minute betting research, you’re in luck! We’ll give you the latest point spread, money line odds and over/under number, as well as the information you’ll need to make the smartest bet.

Without Luka Doncic, who sat out due to low back tightness, the Lakers surprisingly defeated the Detroit Pistons 121-116 on Sunday, as Austin Reaves exploded for 43 efficient points and their bench produced 45 points. That victory gave them a five-game winning streak, as well as the best record in the Western Conference at 9-2.

Now they will face a Hawks team that is 7-4 and may not have Trae Young anymore but is still dangerous. Atlanta has won four contests in a row, and even though most of its wins have come against bad or mediocre teams, it can score plenty of points and force turnovers on the other end of the court.

The lines, courtesy of BetMGM

  • Point spread: Lakers -1.5
  • Money line: Lakers -120/ Hawks +100
  • Over-under: 236.5

Lakers vs Hawks injury reports

Lakers: No injury report has been submitted yet, but Luka Doncic (low back tightness) missed the team's last game.

Hawks: Jock Landale (left hip strain) is questionable. Keshon Gilbert (left knee inflammation/non-surgical procedure), Mouhamed Gueye (left foot fracture), Asa Newell (left great toe hyperextension) and Henri Veesaar (right ACL tear) are out.

Advice and Prediction:

Both the Lakers and the Hawks can score points in bunches -- L.A. leads the NBA with 121.9 points a game, and it is also first in field-goal percentage and offensive rating, while the Hawks are fourth in scoring at 118.6 points a game. The Hawks rank first in made 3-pointers, while L.A. is tied for third in 3-point accuracy at 38%.

Both teams are also adept at forcing mistakes. The Lakers are second in opponents' turnover percentage and the Hawks are fourth, while the Lakers are first in points off turnovers a game and Atlanta is third in that department.

On the other hand, L.A. is last in opponents' second-chance points per game, and the Hawks are third in second-chance points per game. The Lakers will have to keep forward Jalen Johnson and big man Onyeka Okongwu off the boards, and that responsibility won't just fall on Walker Kessler. They will also need to, needless to say, limit their turnovers, something they have been getting better at during their last couple of games but is still a concern.

The Hawks had Sunday off, so they will be more rested than the Lakers. That could be the thing that tips this game just enough in Atlanta's favor, especially since this is still almost a brand-new Lakers squad. Therefore, we're going against the grain with our pick to win this matchup.

Prediction: Hawks 119, Lakers 113
