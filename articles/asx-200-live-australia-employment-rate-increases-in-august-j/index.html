ASX slides on higher oil price, unemployment data

The Australian sharemarket is lower after unemployment rose, in line with losses in New York as oil surged back above $US100 a barrel and bond yields spiked higher.

The S&P/ASX 200 Index fell 76 points, or 0.9 per cent, to 8689.10 after midday on Thursday. Three of the 11 sectors were in the green, led by energy.

New data published on Thursday showed that the unemployment rate in Australia rose to 4.6 per cent in August despite the economy adding 39,500 jobs.

The S&P 500 slipped 0.8 per cent, and the Nasdaq dropped 1.1 per cent overnight, retreating from recent highs as the yield on US 10-year bonds leapt to 5.11 per cent.

Oil prices jumped back above $US100 a barrel again after trading lower on Wednesday, fuelled by anxieties around supply as Iran and the US hit another stalemate in re-opening of the Strait of Hormuz. There was also speculation that the White House is considering a ban on the export of diesel for 90 days, according to Politico. US diesel futures tumbled on the news.
