October Hunter's Moon 2026: What it means for your zodiac sign

TheTools · 27.10.2026 23:38 · source · permalink
  • Living

October's full moon, known as the Hunter's Moon, is here, bringing powerful energy that could spark changes in relationships, finances and personal growth, according to celebrity astrologer Kyle Thomas.

Thomas, who is known for his cosmic guidance for celebrities, businesses and online influencers, spoke to "Good Morning America" about what the October full moon could mean for each zodiac sign.

But first, here's what to know about this month's Hunter's Moon.

When can you see the full Hunter's Moon?

The Hunter's Moon reached peak illumination on Monday, Oct. 26, at 12:12 a.m. ET, according to the U.S. Naval Observatory.

The moon will appear full for several nights around its peak, giving skywatchers a chance to see it illuminate the night sky.

Why is it called the Hunter's Moon?

The Old Farmer's Almanac, which began publishing full moon names in the 1930s, explains that the October full moon became known as the "Hunter's Moon" because it signaled the start of hunting season and served as a reminder for hunters to prepare for the colder months ahead.

According to the Almanac, after fields were cleared during the harvest season, animals would venture out to scavenge remaining crops, making it easier for hunters to spot game such as deer and foxes.

The October full moon has also been called the sanguine moon or blood moon, a nod to the hunting season or the deep red hues of autumn leaves, the publication noted.

What zodiac sign is the Hunter's Moon in October?

The zodiac sign of each full moon is determined by its position in the night sky relative to the astrological signs. Each year, the moon shifts, bringing different characteristics to each full moon, according to Thomas.

This year's Hunter's Moon will occur in Taurus, an earth sign associated with stability, finances, relationships and personal values.

Thomas said Taurus is ruled by Venus, the planet associated with love and beauty, which could encourage people to focus on what brings them happiness, comfort and security.

"Taurus also inherently brings attention to what we value, as well as our inherent values, and the worth we place on everything in which we have or desire," Thomas said. "During these times, we can feel more sensual, crave beauty and luxury, and may be a bit more indulgent."

Rituals and manifestations to try during the Hunter's Moon

Because Taurus is an earth sign, Thomas recommends incorporating natural elements such as plants, gemstones and soil into full moon rituals.

For those looking to attract financial abundance, he suggests creating a space dedicated to financial goals or using green candles while focusing on intentions.

"Taurus is ruled by Venus, so if you’d like to attract romance or increase love, use a pink candle," Thomas suggested. "Write your desires either on the candle or on a piece of paper and then burn the candle atop it -- but make sure that you never leave a candle burning by itself unattended."

Potential meditations, mantras or journal prompts:

  • I attract abundance in all forms.
  • Wealth is mine and I open myself to it.
  • Bring me stability and security.
  • I enjoy the pleasures of life.
  • I align with the endless flow of resources.
  • To receive, I give.
