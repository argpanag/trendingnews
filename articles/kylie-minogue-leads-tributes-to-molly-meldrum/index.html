Kylie Minogue leads tributes to Molly Meldrum

TheTools · 23.11.2026 08:47 · source · permalink

Kylie Minogue has led tributes to Ian 'Molly' Meldrum following his death

The Australian TV star and music journalist - who is credited with shaping the country's music scene during his career on Countdown between 1974 and 1987 - passed away at a hospital in Melbourne on Monday (23.11.26) at the age of 83 and the news has prompted a flood of messages from artists across the industry remembered Molly for his friendship and support over the years.

His death was confirmed in a statement from his longtime friend and manager, Mark Klemens, who told the Guardian: "We have lost a true Australian icon, a man who opened doors for so many and entertained millions of people here and around the world.

"He was unconventional and wonderfully unpredictable, but always caring, always funny and always down to earth, with a passion the rest of us could only envy. He made the most of his life, and boy, was it a full one. Rest in peace, Molly."

A statement from Molly's family and partner Yan Wongngam, added: "Affectionately dubbed 'the Guru', Molly was the heartbeat, the champion and the spirit of Australian music.

"He shaped not only the sound of a nation but also the dreams of generations who grew up with his voice guiding them.

"Yan cared for Molly with great love and devotion, especially through his final years. The family is deeply grateful to him, to Molly's many loyal friends, and to the staff at Epworth Richmond [hospital] for their wonderful care."

Former Neighbours star Kylie revealed she used to watch his TV show Countdown and was thrilled when she finally got a chance to be a guest. In a post on Instagram, she wrote: "As a kid, I watched Molly Meldrum on Countdown every single weekend, in awe of the show and the magic it brought to our little TV in the lounge room and dreaming of how music might become part of my life.

"I was over the moon to be in the Countdown audience one time and by my late teens I was on the show! (and Molly visited us in Neighbours!)

"The stories are many and there was never a dull moment! He championed so many Aussie artists and welcomed international artists to Australia in his own wonderful and inimitable way.

"I'm so grateful for his unwavering love and support. Vale Molly. I'm going to turn the music up in your honour."

Kylie's former Neighbours co-star Jason Donovan also paid tribute to Molly in a post on Facebook, writing: "Growing up in Australia, Countdown was such a big part of my childhood, and Molly Meldrum was at the very heart of it.

"A truly unique character, a great Australian and one of this country's greatest pioneers and ambassadors for music and entertainment.

"Wherever you travelled in the entertainment world, whether in Australia or overseas, there was always a Molly story.

"He was loved, recognised and respected around the world for the extraordinary contribution he made to Australian music and the artists he championed.

"There will never be another quite like him. I have some wonderful memories of Molly over the years. I particularly remember him appearing on Neighbours alongside Kylie, Guy [Pearce] and myself. What a day that was!

"Always spirited, full of fun, with a wonderful sense of humour and an infectious enthusiasm for life. He was such great company."

He concluded by adding: "Thank you, Molly, for the music, the memories, the laughs and for helping put Australian artists on the world stage.

"A true original. An Australian legend. 'Do yourself a favour' (his signature line … ) mate!! Rest easy!"

US band Blondie also acknowledged Molly's influence and credited him with opening "doors" for them Down Under. In a post on Instagram, they wrote: "Molly was instrumental in Blondie's success in Australia, championing our music and introducing us to a whole new audience.

"We will always be grateful for his belief in us and the doors he helped open. He had a true passion for music, a wonderful sense of humor, and was truly one of a kind. He will be greatly missed."

In a post on Facebook, Leo Sayer called Molly a "wonderful, fun, crazy, knowledgeable, influential and irrepressible soul" adding he will "will live on in all our memories as long as rock'n'roll will … "

Sayer concluded: "Ian introduced me to the Australian scene back in 1974 when I first appeared on ABC's Countdown TV and was a huge part of my success down under."

Pop star Peter Andre also credited Molly with boosting his music career. In a post on Instagram, he wrote: "This really hurts.

"If it wasn't for this man , I would not of had any of the chances I had to make it in any way in the entertainment industry back when it was near impossible to get noticed.

"He believed in me from day one and I am forever grateful to Ian 'Molly' Meldrum. I've had the chance over the last few years to call him many times and thank him for all he's done.

"I was very happy I got to visit him recently at his home and reminisce about 'the good ole days'. Molly did a lot for many people. A true icon, a friend. Someone I will miss dearly. Rest easy Molly."
