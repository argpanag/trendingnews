Dianne Buswell shares moving moment with son as she returns to Strictly

TheTools · 19.09.2026 16:14 · source · permalink

Dianne Buswell has been seen sharing a moving moment with her baby son as she prepares to return to Strictly Come Dancing, a year after she announced she was pregnant.

It was ahead of the 2025 series in September last year that the professional dancer announced she and her partner, YouTube star Joe Sugg, were expecting their first child, several years after the couple met on Strictly when they paired together for the 2018 series.

Dianne went on to compete in the BBC One dance contest with Neighbours star Stefan Dennis, but it sadly ended in an early exit when the actor had to withdraw due to injury.

It was then in March that she and Joe welcomed their son into the world. They announced the special news on Instagram with the first pictures of their baby boy and said: "Never felt a love like it. Baby Bowden Mark Richard Sugg. 16/03/26."

Since then, both Dianne and Joe have been seen sharing updates on their new life as a family of three, including sharing regular vlogs on their respective YouTube channels, and in recent weeks, Dianne has been sharing how she's been balancing her return to work on Strictly and being a new mum.

It'll be during Saturday night's (September 19) launch show that Dianne will be seen back in the famous ballroom but ahead of the pre-recorded episode airing, she took to Instagram with a video of herself and her most important dance partner.

She posted a black-and-white video of herself with Bowden, who is nicknamed 'Bowie', in her arms as she danced him around their home. "This time last year, you were dancing in my tummy. Now you're my fav little partner," a message across the clip read. She then added in the caption around blue heart emojis: "My tiny dancer," after using the Sir Elton John track of the same name in her pregnancy announcement.

And it didn't take long for the comments to come flooding in. @iamsophietay said: "He really is Strictly's youngest dancer!!!" @eddo_official replied: "So very special Di." @challierey gushed: "Seeing you become a momma has been the sweetest." @alisonbirc9 wrote: "Oh this is so beautiful." @norfolk_girl_home added: "He is sooooo gorgeous!!!! Looking forward to Saturday!!! Good luck Dianne."

Dianne will be returning to Strictly for her 10th year alongside her fellow dancers Aljaz Skorjanec, Amy Dowden, Alexis Warr, Carlos Gu, Jowita Przystal, Julian Caillon, Kai Widdrington, Katya Jones, Lauren Oakley, Nancy Xu, Neil Jones, Nikita Kuzmin and Vito Coppola.

The group will also be joined by five new professional dancers - Aleksandra Isaeva, Cristian Priori, Lethabo Monametsi, Maddie Ingoldsby and Mark Karmalita -after previous pros Karen Hauer, Nadiya Bychkova, Luba Mushtuk, Michelle Tsiakkas and Gorka Marquez all left the show ahead of the 2026 series.
