Apple Introduces iPhone Handoff Feature in iOS 27 During WWDC 2026 - The CSR Journal

TheTools · 03.09.2026 13:03 · source · permalink

The new iPhone Handoff feature in iOS 27 allows users to utilise a single phone number across two different iPhones seamlessly. This innovation addresses a common inconvenience experienced by smartphone users, eliminating the need for manual SIM card swaps, call forwarding, or acquiring a second cellular line.This feature was previewed during the WWDC 2026 keynote in June, although detailed information was not immediately provided. Recent updates from MacRumors have clarified how this feature will actually function, providing users with a clearer understanding of its implementation.Users can conveniently configure the iPhone Handoff feature via the Settings app, specifically within the Cellular menu. During the initial setup process, individuals will select one iPhone to serve as the primary device, while designating the other as a companion device. This setup is crucial for allowing users to switch between devices effortlessly while maintaining the same phone number.According to Apple, the primary eSIM profile remains linked to the main iPhone, whereas a companion eSIM will be created for the secondary device. This arrangement ensures that users can alternate between their two iPhones without any disruption in service or connectivity.Account management is centralised, meaning all cellular plans and settings are controlled through the main iPhone. Additionally, the location sharing feature automatically updates based on the device that is currently connected to the service, allowing contacts to accurately view the location of the active phone in the Find My application.For the iPhone Handoff feature to operate effectively, having iOS 27 installed on both devices will not be the sole requirement. The functionality appears to be significantly dependent on telecom carrier support, similar to how cellular provisioning functions with the Apple Watch. Early indications suggest that major carriers like T-Mobile in the United States and Deutsche Telekom in Germany may be among the first to endorse this feature.This advancement could benefit users who rely on secondary devices for activities such as exercise, travel, or outdoor tasks, as it offers a safer alternative to using high-end flagship phones in potentially hazardous environments. The feature is positioned as a major improvement for those who balance multiple devices in their daily lives.iOS 27 is anticipated to be released to the public later this month following a rigorous three-month beta testing phase. Users can expect an official announcement from Apple concerning carrier compatibility and specific markets eligible to access this new feature shortly.Long or Short, get news the way you like.
