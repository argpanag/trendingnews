OTS on heightened alert for Undas travel surge

TheTools · 29.10.2026 05:39 · source · permalink

METRO

Oct 29, 2026

1:39 PM GMT+8

Office for Transportation Security Administrator Gilberto Cruz (Screenshot from PTV)

MANILA – The Office for Transportation Security (OTS) is on heightened alert to ensure passenger safety amid the expected surge in travelers during the observance of Undas, Administrator Gilberto Cruz said Wednesday.

 

“The OTS is on heightened alert. We don't want anyone to take advantage of the crowded areas due to the high volume of passengers,” Cruz said in a Bagong Pilipinas Ngayon interview.

 

He said security auditors have been deployed to ensure airports are implementing the necessary security measures, while a 24/7 information center has been activated.

 

The OTS is also coordinating closely with the Philippine National Police to strengthen airport security, he added.

 

Cruz said the agency has launched an information campaign to remind passengers of airport safety rules, including prohibited items and the requirements for transporting firearms.

 

He noted that sharp objects are prohibited, while firearms may be transported subject to the presentation of the required documents.

 

Cruz also advised travelers to limit their luggage to make it easier to monitor their belongings.

 

He reminded passengers not to touch suspicious objects and to immediately report them to police or security personnel. (Cristina Arayata/PNA)
