Fi, a New York-based pet supplement brand operated by Vet Plus, Inc., is recalling two dog supplement products because they may be contaminated with Salmonella, the FDA said. The products were sold directly to consumers and through online retailers Amazon and Chewy.

The recall covers two specific lots: Fi Calming Supplement for Dogs, 180 g, lot number 26118, and Fi 8-in-1 Formula Supplement for Dogs, 180 g, lot number 26159, according to the FDA. The contamination risk stems from an ingredient supplied to Fi's manufacturer, which has suspended production while it investigates.

Salmonella is a health hazard for dogs as well as for the humans who interact with the products or care for sick pets afterward. According to the FDA, infected dogs may show signs including lethargy, gastrointestinal distress such as diarrhea or bloody diarrhea, fever, and vomiting. In less severe cases, an animal's symptoms may be limited to reduced appetite, fever, and stomach discomfort. The FDA notes that dogs showing no outward signs of illness can nonetheless harbor and transmit the bacteria to other pets or humans.

People who handle contaminated products or come into contact with animal waste after a pet has eaten the supplements face an elevated risk, particularly if they do not wash their hands thoroughly, the FDA said. Human Salmonella infection may produce symptoms such as nausea, vomiting, abdominal cramping, fever, and diarrhea, which can sometimes be bloody. Though uncommon, Salmonella can also lead to more serious complications such as infections of the arteries, inflammation of the heart lining, joint problems, muscular pain, ocular irritation, and urinary issues. Anyone experiencing these symptoms after contact with the recalled products should consult a healthcare provider.

Anyone who bought one of the recalled lots is advised by the FDA to immediately discontinue use, place the product back in its sealed container, and dispose of it in household trash. Fi said it is notifying affected customers directly. Customers with questions may reach Fi by phone at 1-888-434-3647, which is staffed daily from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. Eastern time, or by email at support@tryfi.com.

Adverse events or product quality issues can be reported to the FDA's Center for Veterinary Medicine Adverse Event Reporting program at www.fda.gov/reportanimalae.
