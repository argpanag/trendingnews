Massive 192 GB Rockstar Leak Surfaces, Including Cut Content For GTA 5 and Early GTA 6 Files

TheTools · 19.09.2026 09:18 · source · permalink

Friday, September 18th 2026 Massive 192 GB Rockstar Leak Surfaces, Including Cut Content For GTA 5 and Early GTA 6 Files A roughly 192 GB archive of Rockstar Games data has surfaced online, as reported by Insider Gaming. Contrary to how it may appear at first, this doesn't seem to be a new breach. Insider Gaming says the files come from the 2022 hack that put around 90 unreleased GTA 6 clips online, and that the data apparently stayed with a small group of people until recently, when it started spreading more widely. Due to the sheer size of the archive, the community is still digging through the files. However, clips and screenshots are already starting to spread on social media. Most of what has been found so far is cut GTA 5 content. On X, GTA modder TJGM shared a video of a file that appears to hold zone data for a canceled Liberty City expansion. Loaded into the CodeWalker map editor, it shows an outline that looks larger than Liberty City from GTA 4. Separately, user @affmalyt found assets from the canceled Agent Trevor single-player DLC, including a spaceship location and some mission names. There is also cut dialogue, such as an alternate Trevor ending and lines between Brucie and Franklin. There are GTA 6 files in there too, but they seem to be very low-quality map renders. As mentioned previously, people are still digging through the archive, so we may see more in the coming days. We haven't seen a statement from Rockstar about this "new" leak yet, and I wouldn't expect them to acknowledge it since it is mostly old files resurfacing. This leak certainly doesn't tell us much about what GTA 6 looks like in its finished state, but it is interesting to see what Rockstar planned for GTA 5 before eventually pulling the plug. Sources: Insider Gaming, TJGM on X, affmal on X
