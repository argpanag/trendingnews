Monkey steals couple’s anniversary cake during hotel room raid in Bali - Discover SWNS

TheTools · 13.11.2026 07:00 · source · permalink

A cheeky monkey rampaged through a hotel room and helped itself to a couple’s anniversary cake.

Chinese holidaymakers were staying at a hotel in Bali, Indonesia, when the mischievous long-tailed macaque entered their room on October 4.

Footage shows the wild primate sitting on a glass coffee table beside a welcome tray containing fruit and drinks, with a plate marked ‘Happy Anniversary’ placed in front of it.

The creature can be seen picking up a piece of cake from the plate and eating it while sitting just metres from the shocked woman filming.

The tourist can be heard calling out to the monkey as she watches it tuck into the anniversary treat.

The holidaymaker said that the monkey later walked out of the room.

She added that they were unharmed and kept a safe distance from the animal to avoid threatening it or provoking it into attacking.

Long-tailed macaques are widespread in Bali and are commonly encountered around tourist attractions, including the Sacred Monkey Forest in Ubud, Sangeh Monkey Forest and Uluwatu Temple.

Authorities advise visitors not to feed, tease, chase or directly approach the monkeys, and to keep food, drinks and small belongings securely stored.

The animals can become accustomed to people and may approach visitors when they associate humans with food.
