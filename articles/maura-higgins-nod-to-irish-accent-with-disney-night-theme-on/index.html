Maura Higgins' nod to Irish accent with Disney Night theme on DWTS

TheTools · 03.11.2026 01:35 · source · permalink

Maura Higgins paid homage to her distinct Irish accent during Disney Night on Dancing With the Stars.

The 35-year-old reality television star who hails from County Longford has been competing alongside pro Mark Ballas on Season 35 of the dance competition series. The duo has dubbed themselves 'TEAM TINK' in a nod to Higgins' Irish accent, which endeared her to millions of viewers during Season 4 of NBC's The Traitors.

The pair performed a jive to a song from Peter Pan, with the Irish beauty channeling Tinkerbell on the dance floor. This comes after Higgins made Ireland proud with a 'brilliant' tribute to her home country earlier in the season.

On Monday, November 2, Higgins and Ballas took to the dance floor for the competition show's Disney Night episode, performing a jive to 'You Can Fly! You Can Fly! You Can Fly!' from Peter Pan.

After becoming a breakout star on Season 5 of Love Island UK, Higgins rose to fame in the States competing on the fourth season of NBC's The Traitors, where she made it all the way to the end alongside fellow Love Island alum Rob Rausch, who revealed he was a traitor during the jaw-dropping final moments of the show.

Traitors viewers rallied behind Higgins throughout the season, praising the reality star's fashion, bubbly personality, and thick Irish accent, which became a major talking point during her run on the show.

Higgins quickly became a fan favorite on the popular show hosted by Alan Cumming, with fans going wild over the way she said 't' words like 'traitor,' 'three,' and 'think.'

She and Ballas explained their 'TEAM TINK' moniker in September during an interview with Extra, revealing that the name is a nod to her distinct Irish accent and her self-proclaimed habit of 'over-tinking' her choreography while learning the grueling ballroom routines week after week.

Higgins previously paid homage to her Irish heritage during the show's Dedication Night, when she and her partner earned their highest score of the season, a 35 out of 40, for their Irish Paso Doble.

Decked out in emerald green, the duo danced to the song 'I’m Shipping Up to Boston' by Dropkick Murphys and were joined by Irish dance champion Lochlan 'Lucky' Howard.

During the show, she said, "I’m so proud to come from a little country with such a big heart. The kindness, the humor, the strength of our people and the way we always show up for one another is something I’ll never take for granted."

Higgins added, "This one’s for every single person back home who has cheered me on, believed in me, and supported me along the way! I’ll always be your Irish girl, and I really hope I make you proud."

Dancing With the Stars airs Tuesdays on ABC and Disney+ at 8 PM EST.
