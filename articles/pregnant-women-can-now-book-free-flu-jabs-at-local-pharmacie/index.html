Pregnant women can now book free flu jabs at local pharmacies

TheTools · 27.11.2026 07:28 · source · permalink

Pregnant women can now book free flu jabs at their local pharmacy.

If you're pregnant, you can now book your free flu vaccination and new for this year, you will also be able to use the national booking system to book your jab locally.

Flu can be more serious during pregnancy, but the vaccine helps protect both mother and baby, giving them some protection during their first few months of life too.

The NHS said: "Taking a few minutes to get vaccinated can help you stay well and enjoy the months ahead with greater peace of mind."

People can book their jab via the NHS website.
