Celebrate the art of gifting with L'Opéra’s Diwali 2026 Collection

TheTools · 27.10.2026 17:25 · source · permalink

This festive season, ‘L’Opéra’ presents its ‘Diwali 2026 Collection’, bringing together thoughtfully designed gift hampers and handcrafted gourmet creations that celebrate the joy of giving. Rooted in French craftsmanship and refined aesthetics, the collection reflects the brand’s continued commitment to creating meaningful festive experiences.

At the heart of this year’s launch is an all-new hamper collection. Designed as a cohesive collection rather than a series of individual boxes, the hampers explore distinct visual narratives. Inspired by serene botanicals, ornamental florals, celestial motifs, lush landscapes and the spirit of travel, each design is a keepsake, making the packaging as memorable as the gift it holds.

Laurent Samandari, CEO & Co-Founder of ‘L’Opéra’, said, “A festive gift should be remembered long after it has been opened. This collection reflects that philosophy, where thoughtful design and exceptional craftsmanship come together to create hampers that are as meaningful as the delicacies within.”

Complementing the new hamper collection is a range of seasonal gourmet additions, including five new artisanal biscuit flavours: Rose & Pistachio cookies, Coconut Lime cookies, Blueberry Lemon Butter cookies, Fig & Walnut Sablé and Gianduja Swirl cookies, created exclusively for the festive season. These join L'Opéra’s signature pâtisserie, chocolates, macarons, teas, preserves and handcrafted delicacies to complete this year’s festive offering.

Kevin Duthel, Executive Chef at ‘L’Opéra’, added, “This year, we've explored a wider flavour palette for our biscuits, from delicate floral notes and bright citrus to buttery fruit-induced combinations and timeless chocolate indulgence. Each recipe has been developed to complement the festive season while offering our guests something new to discover.”

Guided by its philosophy of ‘Bringing Joy Every Day’, ‘L'Opéra’s Diwali 2026 Collection’ offers an elegant way to celebrate the season with gifts that are crafted to be shared, savoured and remembered. For customisations or pre-orders, please contact +91 88000 97255.
