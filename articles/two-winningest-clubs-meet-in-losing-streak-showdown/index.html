Two Winningest Clubs Meet in Losing-Streak Showdown

TheTools · 29.10.2026 08:37 · source · permalink

Hyundai Mobis on Two-Game Skid, KCC Mired in Six Straight Losses KCC Won the First Meeting of the Season

Ulsan Hyundai Mobis and Busan KCC, the two most decorated clubs in South Korean professional basketball with seven championships each, meet in a must-win game as both sides look to snap losing streaks.

Hyundai Mobis hosts KCC at Ulsan Dongchun Gymnasium at 7 p.m. on the 29th in a 2026-2027 KBL regular-season game.

Both teams have struggled early in the season. Hyundai Mobis sits ninth at 2-6, while KCC, which won last season's championship series, has slid to last place at 2-7. Hyundai Mobis has lost two in a row and KCC six straight, making a win urgent for both. In their first meeting this season, KCC won 97-87.

Host Hyundai Mobis has been dealt a blow, with key foreign player Gage Prim out for about six weeks with a knee injury. The team has yet to secure a temporary replacement, and without Prim it lost 71-91 to Seoul Samsung on the 24th, its lowest point total and largest margin of defeat this season. One encouraging sign was Darius Beasley, who posted a double-double with 33 points and 11 rebounds as he tried to fill Prim's absence.

Among the domestic players, a rebound from Lee Seung-hyun is overdue. He has averaged 3.6 points and 2.1 rebounds in 20 minutes and 10 seconds per game across eight games this season, but he was especially effective against KCC last season. In six games against KCC, he averaged 12.7 points on 50% shooting from the field, his best offensive output against any of the league's nine other clubs. He also averaged 7.2 rebounds, showing strength under the basket.

KCC, meanwhile, will likely see the game decided in the second and third quarters, when two foreign players are on the floor at the same time and Shawn Long and Lewis King take center stage. Among the domestic players, Heo Hoon has stood out for his consistency. Apart from the season opener against Changwon LG, when he scored four points, he has reached double figures in each of his last eight games. In the most recent game against Seoul SK on the 27th, he recorded a double-double with 11 points and 12 assists, playing his part as the team's floor general. Attention is also on the shooting touch of Jang Jae-suk, who erupted for a team-high 22 points on 91.7% shooting from the field in that game.
