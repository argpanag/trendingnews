There's ANOTHER "twisty" Harlan Coben thriller confirmed for Netflix, set to 'get viewers hooked'

TheTools · 18.11.2026 12:38 · source · permalink

"Nobody builds a can't-look-away thriller like Harlan..."

Harlan Coben's relationship with Netflix is still going strong, and we've just had official word that there's another adaptation for fans to get excited about.

This time, it's his best-selling novel The Innocent that's getting the TV treatment, following a previous Spanish-language limited series based on the book (that is actually streaming right now, if you didn't want to wait).

The story centres on Matt Hunter, a man whose outwardly-tranquil life hides a dark past. According to Netflix's description, when he and his wife Olivia try to buy a house in Matt's old hometown, things begin to unravel — and, in a truly Coben-style twist, people start to turn up dead. Matt then finds himself the prime suspect, with the couple forced to fight to clear his name and save their future.

This new version of The Innocent will bring back together the same creative team who were behind I Will Find You, which premiered on Netflix in June of this year.

Starring Sam Worthington and Britt Lower, the hit series marked the first US production for the popular author on Netflix and, unlike his other hit thrillers, was also written with a television show in mind.

What to Read Next

"We're thrilled to call Netflix our home and couldn't be more grateful to our friends Jinny Howe, Cole Galvin, James Flannery, Mike Azzolino, and of course, the incredible Harlan Coben, for their continued partnership," the showrunners said in a joint statement (via Tudum).

"Bringing I Will Find You to life and seeing it connect with audiences around the world was an amazing experience. We're excited to bring our signature brand of twisty and propulsive storytelling to The Innocent and the many new stories we look forward to telling at Netflix."

Meanwhile Netflix's Vice President of Drama, Cole Galvin, has said that "nobody builds a can't-look-away thriller like Harlan", praising the team behind I Will Find You and later also adding that the streamer "can't wait for the world to get hooked" on The Innocent.

The next Harlan Coben thriller to arrive to Netflix is likely to be The Woods, with filming already having wrapped.

The exciting cast includes Michelle Keegan, marking a Harlan Coben reunion following the success of Fool Me Once, and long-time collaborator Danny Brocklehurst is also back as lead writer.

The Woods will stream on Netflix, but a release date has not been confirmed.
