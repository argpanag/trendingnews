Lufthansa resumes Frankfurt to Kuala Lumpur flights

TheTools · 10.09.2026 11:00 · source · permalink

Lufthansa will restart nonstop service from Frankfurt to Kuala Lumpur on October 25, 2026, after a decade. The 13-hour route runs five times weekly using Boeing 787-9 aircraft with Allegris cabins. It’s the airline’s longest 787-9 route by great circle distance.

By InsideFlyer

Sep 10 · 1:00 PM · 1 min

The Frankfurt-Kuala Lumpur flight (LH704/LH705) departs evenings and arrives next-day mornings, timed for European connections. It uses a 287-seat 787-9 configured with 28 business, 28 premium economy, and 231 economy seats. The route spans 5,400 nautical miles but may extend due to airspace restrictions or routing factors.

Lufthansa targets transfer passengers from cities like Zurich, Milan, and Madrid, where no direct flights exist. Though local demand is modest-54,000 round-trip passengers annually-the airline expects growth. Most current travelers connect via Gulf or Istanbul hubs; Lufthansa aims to capture that traffic through its hub network.

Travelers gain a direct link between Germany and Malaysia, cutting connection time and complexity. For Lufthansa, it unlocks higher-yield transfer revenue from underserved European markets, despite lower local demand and fare pressure.
