Luis Enrique names 23 for PSG vs Roma; Lucas Digne included

TheTools · 25.11.2026 11:10 · source · permalink

PSG host AS Roma tonight at 21:00 in the fifth league-phase matchday, and Digne is back in the squad after missing the trip to Nice.

By TuFutbolero Newsroom·November 25, 2026 at 06:10 AM

PSG have finalised their squad to host AS Roma tonight at 21:00 in the fifth matchday of the Champions League league phase. Luis Enrique has called up 23 players, and Lucas Digne is among them, according to L'Équipe.

Digne was left out of the trip to Nice on Saturday, but he is now back in the group for a European night. It is a chance for the full-back to show his form in a demanding match against an Italian side.
