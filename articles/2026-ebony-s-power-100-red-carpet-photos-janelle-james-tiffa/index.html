2026 Ebony’s Power 100 Red Carpet Photos: Janelle James, Tiffany Haddish, Rachel Lindsay, ‘Summer House’s Ciara Miller & More

TheTools · 10.11.2026 18:27 · source · permalink

Ebony’s 2026 Power 100 event celebrates influential leaders, visionaries, and changemakers shaping Black culture and driving impact across industries.

The event took place at the Hollywood Palladium in Los Angeles, with Tisha Campbell hosting the award ceremony and Kirk Franklin offering a live performance of “Revolution.”

This year’s ceremony commemorated the theme “The Stories We Shape,” centered on narrative ownership and the mission to keep Black people at the center of storytelling and define our collective future.

RELATED: Soul Train Awards 2026 Red Carpet Photos: Chaka Khan, Common, LisaRaye McCoy & More

Stars at the gala included Abbott Elementary star Janelle James, comedian Tiffany Haddish, Higher Learning and Morrally Corrupt podcast host Rachel Lindsay, among many others.

Reality stars also attended, including Ciara Miller of Summer House and Love Island USA stars like Serena Page, Tierra Davis, Trinity Tatum, Aniya Harvey, Chelley Bissainthe, Ja’na Craig, and Kayda Bosse.

Other guests included cast members from A Different World (Cornell Young, Felicia Pride, Kennedi Reece, Maleah Joi Moon and Renee Harrison), Jackie Aina, Jacquees, Kandi Buruss, Karen Pittman, Kendra G, Kyla Pratt, Lauren Speed, Marsai Martin, Matt Barnes, Miguel, Muni Long, Natalia Bryant, Rick Ross, Scott Evans, Taylor Rooks, Wale and more.

RELATED: ‘Ebenezer’ Red Carpet London Premiere Photos: Johnny Depp, Rupert Grint, Tramell Tillman, Jedward &amp; More

Ebony’s Power 100 Court included Brandy (Grammy-winning singer and actress), Byron Allen (Allen Media Group founder), Flau’jae Johnson (Seattle Storm WNBA player), Keith Lee (Digital influencer and culinary tastemaker), Lena Waithe (Emmy Award-winning writer, producer and actor) and Victor Glover (NASA Astronaut Emeritus), who took to the stage to receive their award during the evening’s program.
