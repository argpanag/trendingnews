The Princess of Wales just wore a 103-year-old heart necklace with the most incredible history

TheTools · 23.09.2026 09:26 · source · permalink

She wore the historic piece to the world premiere of Digger in London

The Princess of Wales looked immaculate on Tuesday night, when she graced the red carpet in London for the premiere of Digger, starring Tom Cruise.

Kate arrived at the global premiere alongside her husband, Prince William, with the royals attending the starry event in support of The Film and TV Charity. Kate was a vision in a cascading purple Jenny Packham gown, but it was the royal's beautiful, heart-shaped amethyst necklace that really stole the show.

Although it looked totally contemporary, Kate's necklace actually has a rich history. It is the Queen Mother’s amethyst sautoir necklace, gifted by Queen Alexandra to Lady Elizabeth Bowes-Lyon (the future Queen Mother) to mark her wedding to the Duke of York (the future King George VI) in 1923. The 103-year-old British royal jewellery piece was designed in the sautoir style of the 1920s.

And the magnificent necklace wasn't the only history-rich item of jewellery Kate brought out for her rare red-carpet moment last night. The royal also wore Princess Diana’s Collingwood pearl drop earrings adorned with white diamonds. These were a 1981 wedding gift from Collingwood Jewelers—a longtime favorite of Diana’s and the entire Spencer family.

Meanwhile, a wavy half-up, half-down hairdo, a dark smoky eye, and a glossy pink lip completed Kate's elegant look. Next to her, William was dapper in a smart tuxedo that included a velvet double-breasted suit jacket and bowtie. The two were joined by several movie industry greats at the premiere, including longtime friend Cruise.
