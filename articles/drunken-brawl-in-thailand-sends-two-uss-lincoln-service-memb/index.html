Drunken brawl in Thailand sends two USS Lincoln service members back to ship

TheTools · 03.09.2026 13:22 · source · permalink

Thai police responded to an emergency call from a Thai citizen involving two American service members assigned to the USS Abraham Lincoln who were involved in a physical fight outside of a bar in Pattaya at 1:30 a.m. on Thursday morning near the city’s famous Walking Street, a hub for nightlife, the Pattaya police superintendent told MS NOW.

In an interview, Col. Anek Srathongyoo called the incident “minor” but said the sailors were drunk and the Thai citizen who called the police was concerned that the brawl would escalate without intervention.

Thai police and members of the ship’s shore liaison group were dispatched by the Thai emergency call center to the incident. Two Americans were taken to the joint operations center run by the U.S. Navy and Thai local authorities at the Hard Rock Cafe in Pattaya. 

Srathongyoo said the Americans were then taken back to the aircraft carrier, and in the daily meeting between U.S. Navy officials and Thai authorities to discuss security incidents and response, Navy officials told him those two service members have now been restricted to the ship for the rest of the port call.

Navy officials in Washington, Japan and Thailand have not responded to requests for comment about the incident from MS NOW.

Srathongyoo said that Thai police will only arrest an American service member if there is an incident involving a Thai citizen or if they break Thai law. He said he emphasized that to Navy officials and feels satisfied that the crew of the Lincoln was properly briefed on the consequences of their actions. 

This is the only security incident Thai police are aware of relating to American service members during this port call so far, Srathongyoo said.

The alcohol-related incident comes as the 5,000 sailors and Marines aboard the beleaguered Lincoln are getting the first liberty port call of their more than nine-month deployment.
