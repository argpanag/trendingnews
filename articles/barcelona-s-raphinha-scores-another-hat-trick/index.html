Barcelona's Raphinha Scores Another Hat Trick

TheTools · 19.09.2026 22:48 · source · permalink

Spain

Raphinha has gotten off to a stellar start to the new season

Keystone

Seven games, seven wins, and 31 goals: FC Barcelona is dominating the competition in the Spanish league at the start of the season. Raphinha is the man of the moment.

On Saturday, the Catalans came from behind to defeat Sevilla FC 3-1. Raphinha scored all of Barcelona’s goals, marking his second hat trick in three days. The Brazilian has already scored twelve goals this season in the league, having netted three each in the 7-2 win over Santander and against Sevilla FC. He has also added two more goals in the Champions League.

FC Sevilla, which took the lead in the 19th minute on a goal by Youssouf Fofana, was still without Ruben Vargas, who is out with a knee injury. He is expected to return to the team after the international break.
