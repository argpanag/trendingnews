Aleksander Barkov Jr. (knee) back in lineup Wednesday

TheTools · 26.11.2026 01:04 · source · permalink

Aleksander Barkov Jr. (knee) is back in the lineup for the Florida Panthers against Ottawa Senators Wednesday according to Elliotte Friedman. (Elliotte Friedman on Twitter)

Fantasy Impact:

Barkov Jr. returns to the ice for the first time since October 1 for head coach Paul Maurice, reclaiming his top-line center role against the Senators Wednesday night.
