The season-ending ATP Finals continue with a crucial Ivan Lendl Group matchup between World No. 5 Daniil Medvedev and No. 6 Flavio Cobolli. Scheduled for November 16, 2026, at 2:30 PM ET at the Inalpi Arena, the match carries significant implications for the round-robin standings and semifinal race.

Medvedev enters as the betting favorite, and while Cobolli has shown he can trouble the Russian, the hard-court matchup and season-long performance metrics suggest Medvedev has the upper hand.

Medvedev vs Cobolli Pick: Medvedev -3.5 Games (-117, Novig)

The best bet is Medvedev to cover the -3.5 game spread. His significant hard-court Elo advantage and stronger serve-and-return numbers support the pick, while his previous hard-court meetings with Cobolli produced winning margins of four and six games. Medvedev also generates more break-point opportunities per game (0.67 to 0.53) and converts a higher percentage of them (41.5% to 37.7%). He holds an advantage in return points as well, winning 33.4% of first-serve return points and 54.0% of second-serve return points, compared with Cobolli’s 28.4% and 48.7%, respectively. These numbers suggest Medvedev has the tools to establish a multi-game advantage without relying exclusively on tiebreaks.

Cobolli’s Madrid victory shows he can challenge Medvedev, and his 61.8% tiebreak win rate is considerably higher than the Russian’s 35.7%. However, Medvedev’s superior return statistics and substantial hard-court Elo advantage make him the stronger pick to cover the -3.5 game spread.

Medvedev vs Cobolli H2H History

The players have met three times, with Medvedev winning both hard-court encounters in 2024 before Cobolli claimed a three-set victory on clay in Madrid in 2026.

Those previous hard-court meetings are particularly relevant to this matchup. Medvedev won in straight sets in Beijing and needed three sets to defeat Cobolli at the US Open, while the Italian’s only victory came on clay.

Recent indoor results provide a more balanced picture. Medvedev has recorded wins over Cameron Norrie and Adrian Mannarino but lost his most recent indoor match to Ignacio Buse. Cobolli defeated Grigor Dimitrov indoors before retiring against Matteo Berrettini.

The underlying statistics also favor Medvedev. Tennis Abstract’s Nov. 9 Elo ratings give the Russian a 1,960.6 hard-court rating, well above Cobolli’s 1,797.2. Tennis Ratio’s 52-week comparison further highlights Medvedev’s advantage, with a higher dominance ratio (1.532 to 0.977), higher service-games-won rate (84.2% to 80.0%) and return-games-won rate (27.9% to 20.0%).
