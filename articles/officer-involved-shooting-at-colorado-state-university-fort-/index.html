Officer-involved shooting at Colorado State University Fort Collins campus Monday evening

FORT COLLINS, Colo. (KMGH) — The public is being asked to avoid the area around the Chemistry and Microbiology buildings on the Colorado State University Fort Collins Campus Monday night.

According to Colorado State University officials, a man was shot after he was behaving erratically and approached a university officer with a handgun near Newton Plaza.

Gunfire occurred, and the suspect was shot, according to a campus official. At the time of this article's publication, it is unclear if the man shot himself or if he was struck by the officer's shot.

Aid was rendered, and the man was taken to a local hospital.

The identity of that person has not been released at the time of this update; their current condition is unknown.

The Eighth Judicial District Critical Incident Response Team has been activated for an officer-involved shooting.

On October 12, 2026 around 5:35 p.m., Colorado State University Police received a report of a person acting erratically near Newton Plaza on Main Campus. An officer responded and located a man, who was armed with a handgun and advanced toward the officer. Gunfire occurred and the suspect was shot. The officer immediately rendered first aid, and the suspect was transported to an area hospital for care. No other injuries have been reported. This was not an active shooter incident.

CIRT was activated and responded to take over the officer-involved shooting investigation. Fort Collins Police Services is the lead investigating agency and any further information regarding this incident will be released by that agency.
