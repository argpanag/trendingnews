Sparta Praha 2-4 AZ Alkmaar: Oufkir's two goals (min. 73 and 84) settled the match after the visitors' comeback.

By TuFutbolero Newsroom·November 26, 2026 at 05:24 PM

On Thursday 26 November, Sparta Praha hosted AZ Alkmaar at Stadion Letná in matchday 5 of the Europa League. Antonio Nobre refereed, and the match ended 2-4 in favour of the visitors. Sparta led 2-0 before half-time, but AZ turned the score around in the second half.

Jonatan Braut Brunes opened the scoring in min. 4, assisted by John Mercado. Patrik Vydra doubled the lead in min. 27, set up by Braut Brunes. Peer Koopmeiners pulled one back in min. 52, assisted by Wassim bouziane, and Ro Zangelo Daal equalised in min. 68, with a pass from Dave Kwakman. Ayoub Oufkir made it 2-3 in min. 73, assisted by Daal, and sealed the result in min. 84 with an assist from Calvin Stengs.
