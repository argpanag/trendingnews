OAKLAND PARK, Fla. (Gambling911.com) - BetOnline on Monday released a series of prop bets tied to the highly anticipated release of Grand Theft Auto 6.  Among those, will there be an outage on Nov. 19, the day we get to finally check out GTA VI. 

What to expect from GTA VI

The game centers on two protagonists, Jason and Lucia, whose criminal partnership takes them into the underworld of Leonida, Rockstar's fictional Florida-inspired state.

Vice City, inspired by Miami, returns as a major setting. The game promises an expansive open world featuring coastal cities, nightlife, highways and the distinctive culture of South Florida.

The story follows the pair after a robbery goes wrong, forcing them into a dangerous criminal conspiracy.
