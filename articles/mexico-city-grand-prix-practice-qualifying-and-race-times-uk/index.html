Mexico City Grand Prix practice, qualifying and race times, UK times, plus weather forecast

TheTools · 30.10.2026 01:45 · source · permalink

The Mexico City Grand Prix signed a contract extension last year to stay on the F1 calendar until 2028

  • Published
    26 October 2026

Round 19 of the 2026 Formula 1 season heads to the Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez for the Mexico City Grand Prix, from 30 October to 1 November.

Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli sealed victory from pole position last time out in the United States, and was joined on the podium by Ferrari's Charles Leclerc and Red Bull's Max Verstappen.

The 20-year-old Italian is now 123 points clear of team-mate George Russell in the drivers' standings, and will make history in Mexico as the youngest F1 world champion if he finishes ninth or better.

If Russell or Lewis Hamilton do not win the race, Antonelli will lift the title wherever he finishes.

Lights out for Sunday's 71-lap race is at 20:00 GMT.

What is the Mexico City weather forecast?

The forecast suggests it will be a warm weekend in the Mexican capital, but Friday's practice sessions could see some light rain.

For Sunday's race, which starts at 2pm local time, conditions should be dry with a high temperatures of 24C.
