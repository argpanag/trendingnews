How to Complete the Emperor Maze Step by Step

1. Enter the Facility and Destroy the Canisters

After entering the Emperor facility, avoid the electrical boxes scattered around the opening area. Touching them causes damage and wastes valuable time.

A Hydra usually guards the initial route. Players can use smoke to obscure its vision, cross with a Snap Hook, and deploy a Barricade Kit to reduce the risk of incoming fire. Once past this section, focus on destroying the yellow canisters.

The Bettina requires approximately seven shots per canister, making it an effective choice for ammunition management. After the initial targets are destroyed, additional wall-mounted canisters will appear and must also be cleared.

2. Follow the White-Lighted Doorways

The central challenge involves navigating moving platforms, vertical passages, and multiple maze layouts. The most important rule is to look for doorways illuminated by white lights. These indicate the routes players should take to progress through the puzzle.

Yellow-lit doorways generally indicate an exit or a route back toward the starting area, so avoid confusing them with the correct progression path.

Use the moving circular platforms to climb between levels, waiting for them to reach a safe position before jumping. The exact puzzle sequence can vary between runs, but the available layouts ultimately lead to the same destination.

3. Use Mobility Tools to Save Time

Several sections contain lasers, moving obstacles, elevated platforms, and breachable containers. Players aiming for a fast completion should prioritize movement over optional loot.

The Zip Line is especially useful in sections where two possible doorways sit at different elevations. Setting up a line can bypass substantial portions of the surrounding platforming.

4. Reach the Final Room

The later maze section requires players to find the correct doorway and climb through several elevated platforms. Pay close attention to the lighting, particularly when a yellow-lit exit appears near a white-lit progression route.

Once the correct path is identified, continue upward and move toward the final room. With a suitable loadout and minimal detours, the entire puzzle can be completed in around 10 minutes.

How to Loot the Final Room Safely

Reaching the final room is only part of the objective. Once the red lights activate, a Vaporizer and smaller ARC enemies may appear outside. Do not engage them immediately. Instead, remain inside and allow them time to lose interest and leave.

Shoot the remaining canisters to unlock the available loot. Additional ARC enemies may spawn as more canisters are destroyed, so wait for them to disengage before continuing whenever possible.

Prioritize the following items:

· Purple amplification components: Important for weapon amplification upgrades.

· Amplification modules: Higher-level modules are particularly valuable for improving weapons.

· ARC Data Modules: Can be broken down for research points.

· Weapon components: Useful for future crafting, upgrades, and repairs.

Level 5 amplification modules are particularly desirable for legendary weapons, while Level 4 modules support epic weapons. Lower-level modules can still provide useful upgrade materials or repair resources.

Inventory management also matters. Skip low-value items when space is limited, and consider leaving behind equipment that can be crafted easily.

Final Thoughts

The Emperor maze is no longer something players can bypass using the previously available glitch. However, the intended route remains achievable in approximately 10 minutes with efficient movement, suitable weapons, and a clear understanding of the white-light doorway system.
