After Celebrity Traitors will Maya Jama take on Hollywood?

TheTools · 30.10.2026 20:37 · source · permalink

She is carefully considering her next steps, but is facing a dilemma

Fans of Celebrity Traitors have been praising Maya Jama’s stint in the ominous cloak – with one tweeting, ‘I hope your back is OK after single-handedly carrying the Traitors since Richard E Grant left.’ And while the presenter continues to add to her diverse CV – including a role in Guy Ritchie’s Netflix hit The Gentlemen and her ongoing gig hosting Love Island – we’re told the positive reception she’s received from Celebrity Traitors has fuelled her desire to crack America.

‘Maya is determined to take her career to the next level now, and Hollywood is very much on her mind,’ says an insider. ‘She’s achieved so much in the UK, but she’s incredibly ambitious. The US is a difficult market to break into, but Maya has the confidence, personality and determination to give it a go. She knows she would have to prove herself, but she’s so prepared to put in the work. She would hate to look back and regret not giving America a go.’

Maya, 32, was 16 when she moved from Bristol to London to pursue a career in broadcasting. She presented shows on MTV, Rinse FM and Radio 1 before taking the helm of Love Island in 2023. Her love life has also remained a fixture in the headlines. She dated rapper Stormzy for four years until 2019, before rekindling their romance in 2023, then splitting the following year. She was also engaged to NBA basketball player Ben Simmons in 2021, and her last relationship, with Manchester City footballer Rúben Dias, ended in May.

‘Maya’s a hopeless romantic and, while she’s hugely ambitious, she also wants to settle down and find love,’ says our insider. ‘She knows that pursuing Hollywood could mean spending long periods away from home, putting her personal life on the back burner, and potentially making it harder to meet someone and build a relationship. She’s not prepared to give up on her ambitions, but equally, she doesn’t want any regrets or to miss out on finding someone special.’

The source adds, ‘The dream would be to have both a successful career and someone special at home.’
