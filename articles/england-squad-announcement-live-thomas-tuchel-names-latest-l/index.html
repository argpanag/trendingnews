  10. A must pick or unreliable? Tuchel's Palmer dilemmapublished at 10:00 GMT
    10:00 GMT

    Has Cole Palmer done enough to earn an England call-up for their November Nations League games against Croatia and Spain?

    The 24-year-old Chelsea forward is regarded as one of the stars at Stamford Bridge, but his status with the Three Lions has been the subject of major debate, particularly since Thomas Tuchel's appointment as manager at the beginning of 2025.

    Palmer's withdrawal from the most recent extended three-week camp came after his omission from their World Cup squad, prompting Tuchel to say he "misses too many opportunities to play for England".

    Yet there is an argument Palmer has done enough to earn an England recall with his start to this campaign - despite Tuchel's obvious reservations.

    Having scored five goals and provided three assists in all competitions, only his Chelsea team-mate Morgan Rogers has had more goal involvements during the opening months of the campaign - when looking at players he is in competition with for England.

    It must be noted, though, that penalties do inflate Palmer's total more than that of some of those rivals for a Three Lions spot.

  15. A rare Championship call-up on the cards?published at 09:51 GMT
    09:51 GMT

    There is a lack of number nines in the English game and the job of being Harry Kane's understudy is harder than it sounds.

    The England captain has just won the Ballon d'Or, likes to play almost every minute for his country and is the captain, record goalscorer and arguably the best player in Tuchel's side.

    Dominic Calvert-Lewin has been selected in the squads before and since the World Cup but, despite doing well for Leeds, it has not quite clicked for him under Tuchel and he was given just 15 minutes in two substitute appearances in the last window.

    Tuchel has started to use Bellingham as the back-up to Kane, but he could opt for more of a prospect in his squad in this shorter window and turn to Will Lankshear.

    The 21-year-old striker, who like Kane started at Tottenham, was sold to Middlesbrough this summer for £20m and has scored 15 goals in all competitions so far this campaign.

    He has scored 13 goals in as many games in the Championship, helping Middlesbrough to the top of the division.

    He only made his debut for the England Under-21s during the last international window and scored in that game so it would be quite a rise for a player who is not in the Premier League.

    Tuchel is aware of Lankshear and with a lack of number nines across the age groups it could be an interesting option for a manager who is not afraid of making big calls.

  17. The Henderson debate - should the Chelsea man be included?published at 09:45 GMT
    09:45 GMT

    Chelsea midfielder Jordan Henderson has not been in the England set-up since the World Cup, where he fell over an advertising hoarding following the win over Mexico in the Azteca Stadium and broke his arm.

    Many would have thought the 36-year-old's international career was over but Henderson, then at Brentford, made a move to Chelsea as they looked to add experience to their young squad.

    After recovering from the arm injury, Henderson has surprised some to become an important part of the Chelsea midfield, starting five games in a row and scoring twice in a win over Bournemouth, before he missed the Blues' win over Manchester United with injury.

    Henderson is a popular figure, respected by staff and players and is again performing at the top level.

    His inclusion is often one of the biggest reasons for debate but now he's involved so regularly in west London, would it cause less of a stir?

  20. Who could receive their first call-up?published at 09:35 GMT

    Some of the more exciting selection decisions we could see revolve around potential debuts for the upcoming England camp.
