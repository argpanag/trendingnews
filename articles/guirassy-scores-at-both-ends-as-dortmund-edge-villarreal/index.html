  6. 'Bouaddi a gem'published at 22:31 BST 8 September
    22:31 BST 8 September

    FT: Porto 0-2 Man City

    Manchester City

    Erling Haaland, speaking to Amazon Prime: “It was a difficult fight, man against man. They are a top team, honestly they are impressive.

    "They play amazing football so well done to them, but with all the pressure [on us] we had to deliver on the biggest stage. That’s what we did today.

    “I think honestly we controlled the first half. A bit uncomfortable when they came a bit high, but we created a lot of chances. I could of easily of had a lot more goals, but second half we scored and they pushed us for a good 25 minutes, but again we managed to hold through and then finish the game.

    “I think these are the games that we have to win, and we have had a great start to the season. In the Champions League you have to win games because if not you suddenly get all the pressure and then you never know what’s going to happen."

    On 18-year-old Bouaddi making his debut: “I think he played a very good game. Last couple of weeks in training, I told the chairman that he has been amazing, so I think we have a gem here. But to be honest, I think he should of played me through in the second half when I was all alone. I was a bit angry, so I will tell him right now, he played a good game, except that pass."

    On the pressure playing for Man City: “We have to demand a lot from each other, and we have to win games. At Man City you have to win games and you have to win trophies."

    On if Meresca will be pleased: “I hope so. We win away with a difficult game. Look at this atmosphere it was unreal at times. I hope he’s happy.”

FULL-TIMEpublished at 21:56 BST 8 September
21:56 BST 8 September

Porto 0-2 Man City

A first Champions League win for Enzo Maresca as Manchester City boss as Erling Haaland's double gets the job done.

Guirassy has 26 goal contributions in his past 25 Champions League games

Greece midfielder Konstantinos Karetsas had to leave the pitch with "circulatory problems" during Dortmund's Champions League win against Villarreal.

The 18-year-old, capped 10 times by his country, was substituted in the 27th minute after receiving treatment having slumped to the ground after playing a pass.

"Konstantinos Karetsas had to leave the field due to circulatory problems," said Dortmund in a statement. "Kosta is doing well under the circumstances and is on his way to the hospital for further tests."

Serhou Guirassy scored twice and netted an own goal as Borussia Dortmund opened their European campaign with a victory.

A Renato Veiga own goal gave hosts Dortmund the lead early in the second half, but Santiago Mourino drew Villarreal level after his side missed several opportunities.

Dortmund improved and Guirassy restored their lead in the 80th minute, before the Guinean striker converted a late penalty and then scored an own goal.

Villarreal failed to score in any of their four away matches in the Champions League last season. That curse looked set to continue as Georges Mikautadze scuffed a glorious chance from close range and Tajon Buchanan forced Gregor Kobel into a smart save.

Dortmund were fortunate to go into the break level, and in the 53rd minute were gifted the lead when Veiga inadvertently deflected a Maximilian Beier cross past his own goalkeeper.

But 14 minutes later Mourino caught out Kobel with a flicked near-post header, seconds after the Dortmund goalkeeper had received treatment.

Kobel made amends a minute later by tipping a fierce Buchanan strike over the crossbar, before Guirassy and Beier got in each other's way attempting to convert from a Marcel Sabitzer cross.

Guirassy's 100th Dortmund appearance had been a frustrating one, but he finally got his goal in the 80th minute when he tapped in from a Fabio Silva ball across the face of goal.

Since the start of 2024-25, Guirassy has been directly involved in 26 goals in 25 Champions League appearances, a record bettered only by Erling Haaland.

Guirassy scored his 64th Dortmund goal three minutes later from the penalty spot after he was fouled at a corner by Mourino.

Villarreal were given late hope when a Kobel save bounced back into the goal off Guirassy's face, and almost equalised in the fifth minute of injury-time when Veiga's close range effort was deflected wide.

Match Facts

  • The only previous meeting between Borussia Dortmund and Villarreal came in the UEFA Champions League last season, with the German side running out 4-0 winners at SIGNAL IDUNA PARK (November 2025).

  • In their 4-0 win against Villarreal last season, Borussia Dortmund recorded 4.03 xG from just 13 shots in the match. This was their second highest xG total in any game they played in 2025-26 (all competitions), only behind their 5.99 against Hamburg in March.

  • Borussia Dortmund have lost just two of their last 23 home games in the UEFA Champions League (W14 D7), with those defeats coming against Barcelona (2-3 in December 2024) and Internazionale (0-2 in January this year).

  • Villarreal lost each of their final six UEFA Champions League league phase matches last season; they’ve never endured a longer losing run in major European competition.

  • Borussia Dortmund conceded 21 goals in 10 games in the UEFA Champions League last season (2.1 per game), their second-highest per game average in a single edition, behind only 2017-18 (2.17).

  • Last season in the UEFA Champions League, Villarreal netted just five goals across their eight matches – the joint-fewest of any side, alongside Slavia Prague. They also had the lowest shot conversion rate (4.6%) of all 36 teams involved in the previous edition.

  • Villarreal have lost each of their last five away UEFA Champions League games without scoring in the process. The last side to lose six in succession on the road without scoring were Malmö FF between September 2015 and December 2021 (six games).

  • This will be Borussia Dortmund manager Niko Kovac’s fifth UEFA Champions League campaign (P27 W14 D7 L6); he’s lost as many games in his previous four (W1 L3) as in his first 23 games in the competition combined (3).

  • Across the last two editions (2024-25 and 2025-26), Borussia Dortmund striker Serhou Guirassy has been directly involved in 24 goals in 24 UEFA Champions League appearances (17 goals, 7 assists). Only Harry Kane (29) and Raphinha (26) have more combined goals and assists in the same period.

  • Villarreal winger Nicolas Pepe had 19 shots without scoring in the UEFA Champions League last season, while his xG (1.57) was the highest of any player who failed to find the net in the 2025-26 edition.
