Zidane Gets Off to a Winning Start; Italy Loses to Belgium in Rome

TheTools · 25.09.2026 22:08 · source · permalink

Nations League

Zinédine Zidane was looking to secure a victory to kick off his tenure as coach of the French national team

Keystone

France won its first match under national team coach Zinédine Zidane in Turkey. In Friday's other top Nations League match, Italy lost 0-2 at home to Belgium.

Zinédine Zidane didn't see a stellar performance from his new team in the 1-0 win in Izmit. A goal by Kylian Mbappé in the 54th minute was enough to secure the victory for the World Cup fourth-place finisher. For France’s all-time leading scorer—who was substituted shortly afterward due to injury—it was his 67th goal in his 107th international match. Goalkeeper Mike Maignan played just as big a role in the narrow away victory as Mbappé did. The AC Milan goalkeeper prevented the score from becoming 0-1 twice with strong saves. Meanwhile, Turkish goalkeeper Ugurcan Cakir was shown a red card in the 87th minute for handling the ball outside the penalty area.

Zidane’s next matches are on Monday in Belgium and next Friday against Italy. These two teams also faced off in Rome with new national team coaches on the sidelines. Dutchman Mark van Bommel is Belgium’s new hope, while Roberto Mancini has returned to the role of “Commissario Tecnico” for Italy.

Van Bommel had more reason to be satisfied. Mika Godts, the Paris Saint-Germain winger, and Benfica Lisbon’s Dodi Lukebakio scored the winning goals following strong individual plays (20th and 69th minutes). The Belgians could have scored more goals, especially in the first half. However, Italy’s goalkeeper Gianluigi Donnarumma, like his Belgian counterpart Senne Lammens, had a strong game. After the break, Lammens made several saves to prevent a potential 1–1 tie against the Italians, who had been playing more strongly since the start of the second half.
