First look at the Grand Theft Auto VI Limited-Edition DualSense Wireless Controllers

TheTools · 03.09.2026 15:46 · source · permalink

Vice City’s unmistakable style comes to life in these two new limited-edition DualSense wireless controller designs.

Vice City has a style all its own. From its sun-soaked streets to its bright neon lights, the city will be Leonida’s ultimate destination when players jump into Grand Theft Auto VI on November 19. 

At today’s State of Play, we shared a first look at two new limited edition DualSense wireless controllers inspired by Grand Theft Auto VI which capture that unmistakable Vice City energy: DualSense Wireless Controller – Grand Theft Auto VI Black Limited Edition and DualSense Wireless Controller – Grand Theft Auto VI White Limited Edition.

The black and white limited-edition DualSense wireless controllers each capture the essence of Vice City, from bright days along the coast to vibrant city nights. Both editions feature a shimmering, color-shifting finish that brings together the hues of a Vice City sunset and the glow of neon lights. They both also feature custom palm tree details molded directly into the controller, adding a unique tactile element alongside official Grand Theft Auto VI branding.  

The DualSense Wireless Controller – Grand Theft Auto VI White Limited Edition embodies Vice City at daybreak, from its white sands and pastel skies to all its laid-back glamour.  The DualSense Wireless Controller – Grand Theft Auto VI White Limited Edition will be available in limited quantities globally at direct.playstation.com and at participating retailers.

The DualSense Wireless Controller – Grand Theft Auto VI Black Limited Edition captures the energy of Vice City’s iconic nightlife. The DualSense Wireless Controller – Grand Theft Auto VI Black Limited Edition will be available in limited quantities exclusively through direct.playstation.com where available* and in other regions through select retailers.

In Grand Theft Auto VI, the DualSense wireless controller’s haptic feedback and adaptive triggers instantly respond to the action and provide dynamic resistance, bringing the sights, sounds and sensations of Jason and Lucia’s story to life in your hands. Find out more on how Grand Theft Auto VI plays best on PS5 when it launches on November 19 and check out Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look here.

Both limited-edition DualSense wireless controllers will launch starting on November 19 for a recommended retail price of $84.99 USD/ ¥12,480 (including tax) / €84.99/ £74.99.** Pre-orders start September 10 at 10:00am Local time.

direct.playstation.com Purchase Information

Starting on September 10 at 7:00am PT/10:00am ET in the U.S., and 10:00am local time in the U.K., France, Germany, Austria, Spain, Italy, Netherlands, Belgium, and Luxembourg, players will be able to pre-order DualSense Wireless Controller – Grand Theft Auto VI Black Limited Edition and White Limited Edition through direct.playstation.com in limited quantities. In Portugal, players can pre-order through playstation.com/store/hardware.

When ordering directly from PlayStation, enjoy free launch day delivery for eligible pre-orders.

*Regions where direct.playstation.com is available: U.S., U.K., France, Germany, Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg, Spain, Italy and Austria. Visit direct.playstation.com for more details. 

**The exact launch date and availability for controllers may vary by country/region
