Deals: iPhone Duo pre-orders begin as Galaxy Z Fold8 gets a price cut

TheTools · 17.10.2026 16:18 · source · permalink

Login

Siddharth 17 October 2026

Deals Apple Samsung Google

Apple’s first foldable iPhone is now available for pre-order, but the iPhone Duo sold out quickly in most regions. Delivery estimates for the foldable have already slipped to late January.

Instead, you could get yourself the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8, which also has the wide-screen aspect ratio as the iPhone Duo. Meanwhile, the Galaxy S26 series and the Pixel 11 lineup are also on sale.

The Apple iPhone Duo is available for pre-order from the Apple UK website and starts at £1,999 for the 256GB model.

It gets a 7.6-inch main folding display and a 5.4-inch cover screen. The foldable is powered by the A20 Pro chipset and has a dual-rear camera setup.

Samsung’s latest Galaxy Z foldables, the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra and the Galaxy Z Fold8, have both received sweet discounts. The 512GB Ultra is £560 off its original price, whereas the wide-screen 512GB trim of the Z Fold8 gets a £370 discount.

Both foldables are powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset. The Ultra has a triple-rear camera setup, whereas the Galaxy Z Fold8 gets a dual camera setup.

Next, Samsung’s Galaxy S26 series is also on sale alongside the foldables. The Galaxy S26 Ultra with 512GB storage gets a £490 price cut.

It’s powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset, offers a quad-rear camera setup, and comes with the Privacy Display feature.

The base Galaxy S26 is also available with a steep discount of £370, for the 512GB storage option. Meanwhile, the Galaxy S26+ gets a modest £80 price cut across its 512GB and 1TB options.

Both the Galaxy S26+ and the base model come with the Exynos 2600 SoC and offer a triple-rear camera setup.

We’ve also got Google’s Pixel 11 lineup on sale. The base Pixel 11 is up to £130 off based on the color and storage configuration you choose.

The Pixel 11 Pro and 11 Pro XL with 256GB storage are £80 and £180 off their launch prices.

All three phones are powered by the Tensor G6 chipset and run Android 17. The phones also come with a triple-rear camera setup, however, the Pro models get the better hardware.
