Pelicans open preseason schedule Tuesday against the Thunder on GCSEN

TheTools · 05.10.2026 16:09 · source · permalink

NEW ORLEANS (WVUE) - The NBA preseason slate of games for the New Orleans Pelicans is almost here.

Head coach Jamahl Mosley and his squad open at the Oklahoma City Thunder on Tuesday at 7 p.m. The game can be watched on the Gulf Coast Sports & Entertainment Network.

The Thunder lost to the Spurs in the Western Conference Finals last season. New Orleans finished with a 26-56 record.

The Pelicans play a total of four preseason games. The Pels also visit the Heat and Mavericks, and finish with a home game on Friday, Oct. 16, against the Wizards. All four games will be broadcast on GCSEN.
