Bruce Willis’s daughter Tallulah apologizes for sharing ‘intimate’ photo of actor

Tallulah Willis has apologized after sharing a rare picture of dad Bruce amid the star’s dementia diagnosis.

The 71-year-old retired Die Hard actor is living with frontotemporal dementia. His family announced the diagnosis in February 2023, shortly after Bruce had retired from acting due to a brain disorder known as aphasia — a condition that affects speech and language abilities.

Bruce has largely kept a low profile since then, with the much-loved Hollywood star rarely seen in public. On Saturday (November 21), daughter Tallulah shared a new photo of herself and her dad holding hands on an outdoor porch swing on a sunny day, surrounded by lush greenery.

In it, 32-year-old Tallulah had her arms wrapped around her father, who was wearing a grey cardigan over a salmon-colored shirt. She captioned the photo with two emojis: a smiley face wearing sunglasses, and the “COOL” image.

The next day, Tallulah deleted the picture from Instagram and shared her regret in a new post, writing: “Hi, I posted a photo of my dad yesterday [because] we were having a BEAUTIFUL visit. I had someone take a super sweet photo of a shared moment between us watching a video of Frankie Valli singing.”

She continued: “I was very excited by the sweetness of the moment that I didn’t think farther ahead that this was possibly too intimate a photo to show.

“I can acknowledge that and vow to do better,” she wrote. “I know you all love him. I love him. My family loves him. And he’s still always the absolute hero we know him to be.”

Tallulah is the youngest of the star’s three daughters with his former wife Demi Moore. Her elder sisters are Rumer, 37, and Scout, 35.

The post comes months after Willis made a rare public appearance at Tallulah’s wedding to musician Justin Acee.

The actor was captured in a Vogue photo series, embracing his daughter while clasping hands with his new son-in-law. Tallulah wrote in a caption: “A tender moment with my dad and Justin at the start of wedding weekend.”

Willis moved out of their family home as his condition became progressively worse, his wife Emma Heming Willis revealed last year. “Bruce would want that for our daughters. He would want them to be in a home that was more tailored to their needs, not his needs,” she said. He currently lives with a full-time care team in a one-story home.

In January, Heming Willis – who he married in 2009 and with whom he shares two daughters, Mabel, 14, and Evelyn, 12 – said that the actor “doesn’t know” that he has the degenerative disease because one of the neurological symptoms includes anosognosia, which prevents a person from accurately recognizing their own illness.

Earlier this year, Heming Willis said that she was torn over whether to mark her 50th birthday in June, saying that she has “not really been in the celebratory feelings of late” because of her husband’s condition.

“I was unsure if I really wanted to do anything, but I had a friend of mine who kept sort of pushing me and saying, ‘You know what, you don't want to miss out on your 50. You have to celebrate in some form or fashion.’ And I really sat with that and I thought about it,” she told Hoda Kotb on her Making Space podcast.

She added that she “always wrestles” with guilt, stating: “I think guilt is something that I am always carrying, but I've learnt that it is really not helpful.”

“I always go back to, what would my husband want for me?” she continued. “And he would want me to have a big, fun bash. He would want me to celebrate my life with my friends and family. And that is sort of what I always think about when guilt of any sort comes in front of me.”

FTD is a rare type of dementia that causes problems with behaviour and language, according to the NHS. It affects the front and sides of the brain (the frontal and temporal lobes) and tends to develop slowly and gradually worsen over several years.
