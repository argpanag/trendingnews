Mission: Impossible? Traitors creator wants Tom Cruise on show

TheTools · 13.10.2026 07:29 · source · permalink

Stephen Lambert also wants a big sports star to go with Hollywood actor for dream third series

Tom Cruise may soon be trading his daring movie stunts for a more cerebral challenge, if the creator of Celebrity Traitors has anything to do with it.

Stephen Lambert said the 64-year-old Top Gun star would be his dream guest for the BBC reality show.

Speaking to Radio Times about his ideal cast for the award-winning show’s third series, the producer said: “Tom Cruise, maybe. And we would like to have a big sports star.”

However, the head of Studio Lambert, the production company in charge of The Traitors for the UK and US versions, added that athletes were unlikely given their busy schedules. “If they are an active one, they have commitments,” he conceded.

Cruise, who most recently starred in the box-office flop Digger alongside Riz Ahmed and Sandra Hüller, has dedicated his career to feature films and has never appeared in a TV show.

The franchise has already recruited acclaimed actors for its Celebrity spin-off series, including Oscar-nominated Richard E Grant, thespian Sir Stephen Fry and veteran actress Celia Imrie.

However, securing Cruise would mark a Hollywood coup for the star-studded show.

The show’s celebrity contestants have also included decorated names from the sporting world such as Olympian Tom Daley and former England rugby international Joe Marler, who reached the final of the first Celebrity Traitors.

The US version has included Olympic champions Ryan Lochte and Tara Lipinski, WWE star Nikki Garcia and former heavyweight world champion Deontay Wilder.

Discussing why the show’s format has proven so popular with audiences across the country – with nine million people tuning in for the first episode of the second Celebrity Traitors – Lambert said it was enjoyable because of the “dramatic irony”.

He said: “It’s the engine of The Traitors. It’s about the pleasure that we know more than the protagonists – we know who is lying. And it’s fun to try and watch people work out who the liars are.”

Lambert added that for the star-studded version, it was fun to watch familiar faces squirm, saying: “Celebrities find it uncomfortable because they have so little control. It’s such an immersive game that people get caught up in it, and show how they really are.

“It’s very revealing of character, and celebrities care, a lot, how they come across.”

Addressing how the Studio Lambert team goes about casting the stars, he said: “Casting was simple. We didn’t need to tempt people on to the show with the promise of being alongside a huge star.”

He added: “The fact that so many famous people did it last year meant they all wanted to do it this year.”

This year’s series, filmed in the Scottish Highlands, also stars former model Jerry Hall and Welsh actor Michael Sheen.

“The show has universal appeal,” Lambert said. “It has established itself as ‘Appointment to View TV’.

“It’s very difficult to get people to watch programmes, and there is a huge joy coming up with something that a very large amount of people watch.”

The hit game show set in Ardross Castle depicts a group of Faithful contestants attempting to seek out the Traitors among them.

Studio Lambert is also behind the successful reality shows Gogglebox, Race Across the World and Squid Game: The Challenge.
