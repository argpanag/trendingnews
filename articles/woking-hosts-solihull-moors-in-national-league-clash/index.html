Woking Hosts Solihull Moors in National League Clash

TheTools · 29.09.2026 20:17 · source · permalink

Woking will face Solihull Moors at Laithwaite Community Stadium on Tuesday night in a National League encounter. The match kicks off at 19:45 UK time, with both teams seeking crucial points as the season progresses.

Woking currently sits 17th in the National League with 12 points and is on a four-match unbeaten streak. Their recent 5-2 away victory at Carlisle featured a hat-trick from John Marquis, lifting the team’s confidence under interim management of Jake Hyde and Craig Ross, according to wokingfc.co.uk and solihullmoorsfc.co.uk.

Solihull Moors, positioned 23rd with only 7 points, have struggled this season, suffering four defeats in their last six matches and conceding seven goals in the last two games. They finished 14th last season but have only recorded one win so far in the current campaign, as reported by bbc.co.uk and solihullmoorsfc.co.uk.

Woking manager Craig Ross has set a 4-2-3-1 formation for the home side, including players such as goalkeeper Jääskeläinen and striker Marquis. The team's recent form contrasts with Solihull’s difficulties in creating chances, notably their 0-3 home loss to league leaders Boreham Wood where their expected goals were low, according to solihullmoorsfc.co.uk.

Solihull Moors manager Chris Millington highlighted the arrival of new signings to strengthen the squad, including experienced defender Chey Dunkley and young center-back Carter Pinnington on loan. Millington said, "Chey brings a huge amount of experience and leadership to the group. He’s a commanding defender who will give us real strength at the back, and we’re delighted to have him with us."

He also added about Pinnington, "Carter is a talented young defender who has developed within an excellent academy environment. He gives us another strong option at the back, and we’re looking forward to working with him during his time at the Club." These additions aim to improve the team's defensive resilience.

Millington's squad will be looking to gain momentum from this match to climb the league table, while Woking is focused on continuing their unbeaten run and distancing themselves from the relegation zone. The match promises a competitive encounter between a team in form and one seeking to recover from a difficult start to the season.
