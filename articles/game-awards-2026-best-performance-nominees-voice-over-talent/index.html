Game Awards 2026 Best Performance nominees - Voice Over Talent Industry News

TheTools · 19.11.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

Five actors are up for Best Performance at The Game Awards 2026: Angela Sant’Albano for Resident Evil Requiem, Liam McIntyre for Marvel’s Wolverine, Manni L. Perez for Grand Theft Auto VI, Patrick Gibson for 007 First Light and Rahul Kohli for Saros. The show lists each performer beside a game and never names a character, which is how the actor long tipped to play GTA VI’s Lucia got her first official credit two days before the game came out.

Geoff Keighley read out the nominees in a livestream on YouTube and Twitch on November 17. The winners are named on Thursday, December 10, at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles. Grand Theft Auto VI reaches players today, November 19, with eight nominations already to its name.

For anyone who works behind a microphone, Best Performance is the category to watch. These are performance capture roles, where the voice is recorded together with the face and often the body, then rebuilt as a character on screen. Three of the five games are also up for Game of the Year. Saros and Marvel’s Wolverine aren’t, so Kohli and McIntyre are on the list for the acting alone.

Kohli’s story about finding his character’s voice is one every working voice actor will recognize. He was late, stuck in Los Angeles traffic and fuming, on his way to record Saros’s first trailer before a six-month gap in his schedule. “And that voice that came out was angrier, more intense than anything we’d done or explored previously,” he said. He and the Housemarque team knew on the spot that they’d found their man.

Hear the voice: Rahul Kohli as Arjun Devraj in the Saros announce trailer. Video: PlayStation, on YouTube

A nomination that says more than Rockstar has

Rockstar has kept quiet about who plays Lucia Caminos, one of GTA VI’s two leads. Fans have pointed to Manni L. Perez for years. The nomination put her name beside the game in public for the first time, and The Game Awards’ account on X congratulated her directly: “Congratulations on your nomination for Best Performance!”

It still isn’t a cast announcement. VICE’s Brent Koepp was careful to say that “Manni L. Perez has not technically been confirmed to be playing Lucia,” since the show left character names off every nominee in the category. Polygon called it a nomination “for Manni L. Perez in an undisclosed role.” Perez is also the only GTA VI performer the jury picked. The actor who plays Jason, Lucia’s partner in crime, wasn’t nominated.

The timing raised eyebrows too. A game released on November 19 can compete because the eligibility window closes the next day, at 9 p.m. Pacific on November 20. Keighley told Variety the calendar was never moved for Rockstar: “everyone thought that we had changed our dates to accommodate ‘GTA,’ and it’s like no, actually our dates have been very set every year.”

Grace, who didn’t know she’d last the whole game

Angela Sant’Albano plays Grace Ashcroft, an FBI analyst sent to the Wrenwood Hotel in Resident Evil Requiem. She told VICE she had no idea how big the part was when she signed on. At the first table read, Capcom walked the cast through the game chapter by chapter, and she had a moment of “Oh my God. I think I go all the way through this game.”

She rang home straight after: “I think it might be a big part, Mom.” The ending wasn’t settled either. The final chapter, she said, “wasn’t officially locked until about halfway through shooting,” so she played Grace without knowing where Grace would end up, which is exactly how Grace lives it. Capcom’s note to the cast was simple: “We want these performances to be as cinematic or like film as possible.”

Grace Ashcroft in the Resident Evil Requiem launch trailer. Image: Capcom

“I hated it”: Saros and the lycra suit

Kohli is candid about his first day on the capture stage. “I hated it,” he told the PlayStation Blog. “You spend most of your time worrying how you look in this lycra wet suit, wishing you’d gone to the gym.” After an hour or two, he said, the technology falls away and it feels like an empty stage with actors, a director and a script, which is where most actors are at home.

He also passed on some advice he got from Troy Baker, one of the best-known names in game performance: “He said it was the purest form of acting you’ll ever do. And he was right.” Housemarque’s senior narrative designer Khalil Osaimi remembers Kohli arriving with notes on everything, even the script, and describes him as “a gamer as well, so he knows what he is looking for a videogame character.”

Bond, Wolverine and the weight of a famous name

Patrick Gibson and Liam McIntyre had a different problem: everybody already has a picture of their characters in their head. Gibson plays a young James Bond in IO Interactive’s 007 First Light. He told Creative Bloq it’s “bizarre to have players play me,” before he landed on the point of the job: “They’re doing it as Bond.” His co-star Priyanga Burford, who plays M, summed up the stage itself: “The motion capture suits are humbling.”

Patrick Gibson's James Bond in the 007 First Light story trailer. Image: IO Interactive

McIntyre, who led the TV series Spartacus, plays Logan in Insomniac’s Marvel’s Wolverine. In a Deadline interview that was later taken down, and quoted by Beebom, he said of Logan: “He doesn’t know why he feels the way he feels or where this rage comes from a lot of the time.” He has also read plenty of strong opinions about his casting. His reply on social media stayed cheerful: “I’m proud as hell of this game. It’s a blast.”

That’s the job in a sentence. A performer gives everything on a bare stage, in a suit with dots on it, and then hands the character to millions of players who will decide for themselves who Logan, Bond or Grace really is. The Best Performance category is the one night a year the industry turns round and looks at the person underneath.

How the winner gets picked

A jury of more than 100 media and influencer outlets from around the world chose the nominees, according to the show’s official FAQ as reported by Make Tech Easier. That jury’s vote carries most of the weight when the winners are decided, and the public gets a smaller share. Fan voting is open at TheGameAwards.com until 6 p.m. Pacific on Wednesday, December 9.

The ceremony streams free on the major platforms, with the opening act at 7:30 p.m. Eastern. By then GTA VI will have been out for three weeks, and players will know for themselves whether Lucia’s voice belongs on that stage.
