Penny Lancaster bans Rod Stewart from meeting Trump after 'appalling' comment

TheTools · 19.11.2026 10:06 · source · permalink

Sir Rod Stewart used to call Donald Trump "a dear friend", but his wife Penny Lancaster has banned him from meeting the president after an "appalling" comment

Former model Penny Lancaster boasted that she has banned husband Sir Rod Stewart from meeting Donald Trump after the president's "appalling" comment. The singer and politician had known each other for some time as neighbours in Palm Beach, Florida.

Rod, 81, would regularly attend Trump's parties and even performed at his casinos on occasion. The star even called Trump a "dear friend" during a 2018 interview, despite admitting Trump's "moral compass is way off".

That close bond seems a thing of the past, however, as Penny revealed she has banned her spouse from meeting the leader of the free world. She revealed the president's "rhetoric" about women "appalled" her so much that she told Rod he can't attend events where Trump will be present.

The 55-year-old told the Times: "It was (Trump's) rhetoric - basically, you can treat women how you want if you're in a powerful position. It's sickening, appalling. So I was like, 'I can't go anywhere near him.'"

Rod then appealed about a charity event Trump was putting on, but that too fell on deaf ears. Penny continued: "Rod was like, ‘But what about the charity event he does for the local police?’ I said, ‘We can send a cheque.’ ”

The Maggie May hitmaker has since revised his opinion on his "dear friend" Trump. It's not just the president's views on women which prompted the U-turn.

Rod criticised Trump for commenting on the bravery of NATO troops during the Afghanistan conflict earlier this year. He signed off his message by calling Trump a "draft dodger", a reference to the draft deferments he received during the Vietnam War.

The singer had previously commented on the state of his friendship with Trump in 2025: "I'm not a great fan of Trump. I knew him very, very well. I used to go to his house. I live literally half a mile away (in Florida).

"We're both on the beach. I used to go to his Christmas parties. He's always been a bit of a man's man. I liked him for that.

"But he didn't, as far as I'm concerned, treat women very well. But since he's become president, he became another guy. Somebody I didn't know."

Rod might be a superstar musician who has sold more than 120 million records worldwide, but it seems clear who rules the roost in the family's Essex mansion. He tied the knot with Penny in 2007 and have sons Alastair, 21, and Aiden, 15.

Penny said that Rod sometimes comes home from touring to realise that "he's a nobody sometimes". She added: "It's back to reality and it's quite amusing. People like Rod are used to having 'yes' people around them."
