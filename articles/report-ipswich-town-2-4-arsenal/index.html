Report: Ipswich Town 2-4 Arsenal

TheTools · 16.09.2026 00:15 · source · permalink

A Max Dowman double helped us cruise into the fourth round of the Carabao Cup as we beat Ipswich Town 4-2 at Portman Road.

The 16-year-old opened the scoring before Noni Madueke fired home to double our lead. Dowman slotted home a third before Mikel Merino netted his first goal of the season to make it four.

Anis Mehmeti and former Gunner Chuba Akpom then scored for the Tractor Boys but it didn't stop us from progressing to the round of 16.

DOWMAN LEADS EARLY DOUBLE

Mikel Arteta handed Max Dowman his first competitive start of the campaign and he took little over six minutes to show what he's all about.

Winning the ball back high up the pitch, his interception found Ebere Eze, who flicked it back to the 16-year-old to glide into the box. Our academy starlet then wrong-footed Cedric Kipre to create space before slamming the ball into the bottom corner beyond Christian Walton.

Max, at 16 years and 258 days, is now our second-youngest scorer in the competition, behind Cesc Fabregas, who scored against Wolves at 16 years and 212 days old.

The youngster was almost in again after Mikel Merino closed down Kipre's clearance and squared to Dowman, who couldn't quite get a shot off just inside the box.

But we didn't have to wait for long for a second. Good pressing saw Bruno Guimaraes get first to Walton's pass out from the back. The ball fell to Merino who produced a delicious chip to Madueke - the England international had time and space to smash his effort into the bottom corner.

SETTLING IN

It took until the 19th minute for Ipswich to register a threat. Good play down the left saw Abdoul Ouattara's cross palmed into the air by Kepa Arrizabalaga, only for Zian Flemming's volley to be cleared well by Marli Salmon.

A ranging ball from Ouattara then found Flemming at the back-post, where the Dutchman popped it back to Mehmetti, who couldn't keep his effort under the crossbar.

Having turned 17 a little over two weeks ago, Salmon showed plenty of composure at the back alongside Gabriel. A burst into midfield showcased his ball-carrying as we worked the ball wide to Eze, whose shot was blocked by Ipswich captain Darnell Furlong.

Down the other end, Jaden Philogene did well to get to the byline inside the box, but Kepa snuffed out his low cross. 

MIGHTY MAX

Within 90 seconds of the second half, we had our third of the night, and it was Max once more.

Madueke, who tormented Ouattara all night, found Martin Zubimendi in the middle. The Spaniard, who was playing at full-back, flicked the ball through to Dowman via a deflection off of Jacob Greaves, and the teenager did the rest, slotting into the bottom corner.

In netting his second, Max became the second 16-year-old to score two goals in a game for a Premier League side in all competitions, behind Wayne Rooney, who netted twice as a 16-year-old in October 2002.

Having seen Max get his double, Noni was on the hunt for his. Good work down the right saw him break clear and fire his effort across the goal, which narrowly missed Viktor Gyokeres.

But a fourth goal emerged minutes later as Bruno Guimaraes pinged the ball into Merino, who took a touch to steady himself and fire into the net off the post.

LATE STRIKES

Ipswich then made changes, which saw former academy graduate Chuba Akpom introduced. Another substitute, Jack Clarke, helped make Ipswich's goal in the 62nd minute. The attacker was able to race clear down the left and find Anis Mehmeti, who was unmarked and fired into the bottom corner.

Following the goal, Mikel Arteta introduced Theo Julienne for his debut as he came on for Bruno, with Zubimendi slotting into midfield.

Julienne, who started at right-back in pre-season against Como, had to be alert on numerous occasions, blocking crosses as the Tractor Boys went in search of another.

Former Norwich player Christos Tzolis was then thrown on by Mikel Arteta, and the winger nearly had a goal against his former rival. Bursting into the box his first effort was well blocked before his second was smothered by the diving Walton.

There was enough time for former Gunner Akpom to net against us in stoppage time. The striker was able to find space after tussling with Myles Lewis-Skelly in the build-up and rifle home to score his second of the campaign.

WHAT'S NEXT

Following our success at Portman Road, we'll be on the road for one last match before the international break as we take on Brighton & Hove Albion in the Premier League.

The match will take place on Saturday, September 19, kicking off at 3pm (UK time).
