Vivica A. Fox, 62, Set To Star In Michelle Obama’s Netflix Dating Series 'The Later Daters' — And We’re Obsessed!

TheTools · 13.11.2026 12:00 · source · permalink

Vivica A. Fox is ready to give love another shot, and this time, she’s letting the cameras come along for the ride. The 62-year-old actress is joining a star-studded lineup for Season 2 of Netflix’s The Later Daters, premiering Dec. 30. Also joining the cast are Christie Brinkley, Margaret Cho, Caroline Rhea and musician Pebe Sebert, mother of pop star Kesha.

What is The Later Daters about?

Executive-produced by Michelle Obama, the reality dating series follows the women as they navigate the highs, lows and unexpected surprises of dating later in life,“as they try for their second (or third, or fourth) chance at love,” according to the show’s synopsis.

Leading the romantic adventures is Harvard-trained behavioral scientist and relationship coach Logan Ury, who returns after helping singles navigate the dating scene during the show’s first season in 2024.

“Whether navigating the highs and heartbreaks of modern romance for the first time in years or getting a second chance at love, these women prove that finding a real connection has no expiration date,” the synopsis from the streamer reads, according to People.

Fox is ready to find love.

And if anyone is wondering whether Fox is serious about finding Mr. Right, she’s already made it clear that she’s open to possibilities. During a 2024 appearance on Today with Hoda & Jenna, the actress got candid about her dating life and what she’s hoping to find in a future partner.

“I’m wanting to date, it’s just limited resources out there, right? Because I’m not getting on an app. I’m old-fashioned,” Fox said. “I need to meet you and sparks happen because I just believe people make up stories, saying who and what they are.”

The Not Another Church Movie star explained that she’s not necessarily looking for a fairytale romance but rather someone who can complement her lifestyle and keep things interesting.

“When I say a partner, I want someone who’s going to be fun, who’s not intimidated by who and what I am, that likes to travel, and is gonna let me go shopping.”

And she’s not exactly keeping the applications closed!

“I’m taking applications, so if you got a friend, I’m good. And I don’t discriminate. I’m not against different nationalities, so you can know that too,” she added.

Vivica A. Fox’s love life.

Fox has had a few high-profile romances over the years. She married singer Christopher “Sixx-Nine” Harvest in 1998, but the marriage ended in divorce in 2002.

In 2011, she accepted a marriage proposal from Atlanta club promoter Omar “Slimm” White, who was 19 years her junior. The two dated for approximately two years before ultimately going their separate ways.

And who could forget her much-talked-about romance with rapper 50 Cent? The two dated briefly in 2003, with their relationship lasting roughly two to three months. The exes had a few rocky encounters afterwards.

Now, with The Later Daters, Fox is stepping back into the dating scene with a fresh perspective, a little experience under her belt and, hopefully, a few sparks waiting to happen. After all, as the show promises, love doesn’t come with an expiration date. 

Will you be watching when the show premieres on Dec. 30?
