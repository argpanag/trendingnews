Python on the loose in UK city as 'gridlock' reported after 999 crews block road

TheTools · 23.09.2026 18:16 · source · permalink

Firefighters are trying to catch the python(Image: Submitted)

A python is currently on the loose in a UK city after it wrapped itself around a tree branch, with "gridlock" reported in the area as result of the emergency response.

"Gridlock" was reported on Plymouth Hoe this evening after firefighters confirmed they are trying to catch the snake. Cars are reportedly turning around on Hoe Road with fire engines blocking the road, reports Plymouth Live.

It's not clear who the snake belongs to or how it got there. A spokesperson for Devon and Somerset Fire and Rescue Service said: "We got a call at 5.10pm, to the public toilets at West Hoe Park on Hoe Road. We have a fire engine and an aerial ladder platform at the scene.

The snake pictured in the tree

"It's believed a carpet Python is coiled around a branch up high. A vet is unable to retrieve it and the police requested the help of the fire service with a ladder to get the Python down.

"Fire crews are working with the police to retrieve the snake from a tree approximately 3m high. The road was closed while crews dealt with the incident.

"The animal is still loose."

Emergency services block Hoe Road as a carpet python is reported on the loose near the toilets at West Hoe Park(Image: Submitted)

Firefighters were pictured near the toilets at West Hoe park on Hoe Road, rummaging through the bushes in an attempt to locate the snake.

The fire service confirmed that the snake is yet to be found in an update just before 9pm. A team will be sent out again tomorrow (Thursday, September 24).

A fire engine was blocking the road for a time while crews used an aerial ladder to reach the spot the snake was seen. The road is now clear.
