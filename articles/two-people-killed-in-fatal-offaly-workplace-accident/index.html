Two People Killed In Fatal Offaly Workplace Accident

TheTools · 28.09.2026 15:12 · source · permalink
  • News Home
  • More from Midlands News

Monday, 28 September 2026 14:45

A man aged in his 50s and a woman in her 20s were pronounced dead at the scene.

A father and daughter have been killed in a workplace accident in Offaly

Gardaí and emergency services responded to the incident at an industrial premises at around 11.40am.

The man who was in his 50s, and the woman, who was in her 20s, were pronounced dead at the scene where they remain until moved to the Midlands Regional Hospital in Tullamore for a post mortem examination.

The Health and Safety Authority says it's aware of the fatal incident and is launching an investigation.
