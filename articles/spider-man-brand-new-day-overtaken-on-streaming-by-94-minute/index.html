Spider-Man: Brand New Day Overtaken on Streaming by 94-Minute Horror Reboot

TheTools · 05.11.2026 04:35 · source · permalink

Resident Evil (2026) is now available to buy or rent on streaming. Just one day after its release on Nov. 3, the horror adaptation has shot up to the top of the streaming charts in the United States, dethroning Spider-Man: Brand New Day.

Currently, Resident Evil is the No. 1 most-watched movie in the Apple TV Store in the United States, with Spider-Man: Brand New Day at No. 2, per FlixPatrol. However, globally, Spider-Man: Brand New Day is still No. 1, while Obsession is No. 2, and Resident Evil is No. 3. Despite being No. 3 in the world, Resident Evil is still No. 1 in 26 countries.

The 2026 Resident Evil Reboot Is Now on Streaming

Zach Cregger's 2026 reboot of Resident Evil is a fresh take on the franchise, different from the films that came before it. Cregger is known for horror, with Barbarian and Weapons under his belt. The new movie, rather than being a straight adaptation of any of the previous films, takes its inspiration from the video game franchise itself, which Cregger said he spent "thousands of hours" of his life playing. The movie is filled with game references and visuals, while also creating a unique story.

On Nov. 3, 2026's Resident Evil officially came out on streaming. According to Fandango's press release, "Your next nightmare is just a click away! Resident Evil is now available on Fandango. The terrifying new chapter in the iconic horror franchise, which is Certified Fresh and Verified Hot on Rotten Tomatoes, is available to purchase for $24.99 or rent for $19.99. Dare to take a closer look? Fandango just debuted the first 10 minutes of the film, giving fans a chance to get in on the action early. Watch HERE!"

The movie is available to rent or buy on streaming services like Fandango, Apple TV, and Prime Video. And for fans of Cregger's work, Fandango is offering a three-movie bundle: "Plus, for any horror enthusiasts looking for their next scare, Fandango is offering an exclusive Zach Cregger 3-Film Collection (Bundle), including Resident Evil (2026), Weapons (2024), and Barbarian (2022), all available for $34.99!"

Spider-Man: Brand New Day Has Been Topping the Streaming Charts

Spider-Man: Brand New Day came out on digital on Oct. 6, and since then, it has maintained the No. 1 spot in the Apple TV Store. While it's still No. 1 on the global charts, the movie is finally losing a tiny bit of steam in the U.S., as it drops down to No. 2. Spider-Man: Brand New Day also broke records at the box office. The movie grossed $2.5 billion against a $225 million budget, making it the third-highest grossing film of all time.

In terms of Spider-Man's future, a fifth movie has not yet been announced, but it will almost certainly be in development. In July 2026, Tom Holland said of another Spider-Man movie, "I will do it for as long as they’ll have me. So if this movie does good, I guess we’ll see!"

Producer Kevin Feige also stated that he would let Holland and Zendaya "take a good and restful victory lap, which they very much deserve," before starting on a fifth movie.

Resident Evil and Spider-Man: Brand New Day are available to rent or buy on digital.
