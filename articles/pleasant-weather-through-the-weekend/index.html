Pleasant weather through the weekend

TheTools · 24.09.2026 08:47 · source · permalink

HUNTSVILLE, Ala. (WAFF) - Pleasant early fall conditions are ahead. We’ll enjoy comfortably warm afternoons followed by seasonably cool nights. The next change in the weather isn’t showing up until the latter half of next week.

For today look for morning cloudiness with some patchy fog, mist and drizzle. We’ll see partial sunshine by afternoon. Temperatures today will top out in the upper 70s to near 80 for highs.

We’ll be partly cloudy tonight with lows in the low 60s. Friday looks pleasant again with a mix of clouds and sunshine and highs in the lower 80s.

The weekend will bring quiet and mild conditions. Look for partly cloudy skies with warm afternoons in the low 80s. Mornings will be cool with temperatures starting off in the mid to upper 50s.

Early next week will see temperatures edge up slightly into the mid 80s but we’ll keep the cool mornings in the 50s.

Details regarding our next weather maker aren’t entirely clear yet, but we’re likely to see rain chances pick back up again during the second half of the week.
