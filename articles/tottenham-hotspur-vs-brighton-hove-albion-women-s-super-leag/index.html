  11. Key statspublished at 13:59 GMT
    13:59 GMT

    Crystal Palace v Charlton (14:00 GMT)

    • Crystal Palace have only won one of their past six league meetings with Charlton (W1 D1 L4), but did win 3-2 at Selhurst Park last time out.
    • Charlton are the fifth team to lose each of the opening eight games in a WSL season. Of the previous four, three finished in the bottom two.
    • Charlton are searching for their first top-flight win since 2006-07.
    • Crystal Palace are yet to win a London derby in the WSL (D1 L11), conceding 50 goals and scoring just six in the process.
    • Charlton have not won away in the league since February (WSL2), when they beat Nottingham Forest.

  12. Spurs performances all or nothing in 'more competitive' WSLpublished at 13:58 GMT
    13:58 GMT

    Tottenham v Brighton (14:00 GMT)

    Tottenham have been all or nothing this season, winning three league games by 3+ goals and losing two by 3+ goals.

    But after a three-match losing streak threatened to derail their bright start, Martin Ho’s side returned to winning ways last Sunday with a 4-1 victory over Birmingham.

    Led by six goals and three assists from summer signing Victoria Pelova, Tottenham head into their meeting with Brighton just three points off the Champions League places.

    “What you've definitely seen now is a more competitive league. What’s very clear to see now I think, more than any other season, is the points are being shared a lot more,” said Ho.

    “I think that’s probably the beauty of this league. I think this league is the best in the world because those games when you go up against anyone you know you’re in one.”

  13. Bottom two meet in south London derbypublished at 13:56 GMT
    13:56 GMT

    Crystal Palace v Charlton (14:00 GMT)

    Two sides desperately searching for a win meet in Sutton for the first south London derby of the season.

    Crystal Palace, in 13th, are without a win in seven WSL matches, registering three draws and four defeats since beating Everton in their season opener.

    Charlton have endured a challenging start to life in the WSL, losing all eight matches to find themselves rooted to the foot of the table. Karen Hills’ side have conceded 25 goals – and scored just three – including leaking at least four goals in half of their matches.

    Last season in WSL2, the two sides picked up one win apiece with Crystal Palace winning 3-2 at home and Charlton taking a 1-0 victory in the reverse fixture.

    Share page
    About sharing

  14. 'Big opportunity' for Brighton to bag elusive away winpublished at 13:54 GMT
    13:54 GMT

    Tottenham v Brighton (14:00 GMT)

    After picking up just two points from their opening four matches, Brighton’s season has sparked into life.

    Arturo Ruiz’s side have won three of their past four matches – scoring 12 and conceding just five – and could win three consecutive WSL games for the first time since November 2021.

    However, they have only picked up one point from three away outings this season and haven’t won on the road since March 2026.

    “Sunday is a big game, a very big opportunity for us. We are in a very good moment; we are working at a very good rhythm, a very good pace, so I’m very happy,” said Ruiz.

    “We feel super strong at home now but we need to finish building our ideas, our base, and our principles, and that will help us in winning points and games away from home.”

    Brighton will be hoping for another lively showing from Manuela Vanegas and Olivia Garcia, who spearheaded the Seagulls’ comeback win over Crystal Palace last Sunday, but are once again without top-scorer Kiko Seike.
