Former Prince Andrew and his ex-wife Sarah Ferguson‘s controversy has been one of the biggest royal scandals in recent memory. Sure, the royals have had to deal with marriages imploding, cheating, and all sorts of dalliances, but Andrew has been accused of sexual assault and misconduct in public office.

Amid all of this, there have been a lot of eyes on Andrew and Fergie’s two daughters, Princess Beatrice and Princess Eugenie. There has especially been speculation about how the situation has been affecting their respectives marriages and family lives.

Beatrice’s husband, Edoardo Mapelli Mozzi, recently did a quick-fire Q&A on the Instagram of his property development company, Banda. Although he didn’t share anything too personal, he did discuss some of the things he enjoys. When asked about his “Instagram crush,” Edoardo replied, “Augustin Deleuze and Sophie Pinet, two entirely distinctive visual worlds that inspire us.”

Deleuze is the co-founder of Parisian furniture house Pierre Augustin Rose. Pinet, meanwhile, is the co-founder of hardware label Inventaire Mobilier. Pretty on-brand for a property development company’s page.

This, of course, says nothing about his marriage or his family life. Edoardo and Beatrice share two daughters, Sienna and Athena, and Edoardo has a son from a previous relationship, Wolfie. But it does make it seem like things are just fine, despite the fact that right after Andrew was arrested, there were rumors about both Beatrice and Eugenie’s marriages.

Earlier this year ,RadarOnline reported that Beatrice’s 2026 birthday plans were a lot more low-key than previous years due to the drama surrounding her family. “This birthday will be a world away from the extravagant celebrations Beatrice has enjoyed in the past, and far more gloomy. The atmosphere around the York family has changed enormously over the last year, and she has no desire for a high-profile party or anything that could become a media spectacle,” a source said at the time.

“Her focus is on marking the occasion quietly with her husband, immediate family and a small circle of trusted friends. She recognizes the family remains under intense scrutiny, and she feels this is the right time to keep the emphasis on private moments rather than public celebrations,” the source added.

Closer later reported that their marriage was going through some bumps in the road. “Edo wants Beatrice to draw a line in the sand with her parents. From his perspective, creating some distance is the only thing to do right now if they want to save their own skins, but Beatrice isn’t willing to do it,” the report claimed.

Reportedly, he didn’t take it well. “Beatrice is panicking and has told friends she’s worried that he’s pulling away. She’s saying this is all her parents’ fault, that things were perfect between her and Edo before they turned the family name radioactive. She says they’ve ruined nearly every aspect of life and now it’s threatening her marriage,” they added, explaining that “things between her and Edo are hanging by a thread.”
