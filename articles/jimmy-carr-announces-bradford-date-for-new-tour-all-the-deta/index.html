Jimmy Carr announces Bradford date for new tour - all the details

TheTools · 21.10.2026 09:13 · source · permalink

Award-winning comedian Jimmy Carr is set to bring his new stand-up tour, Killing Jokes, to Bradford.

The show is scheduled for February 4, 2028, at Bradford Live.

Known for his fast-paced, dark humour, Carr’s brand of comedy is not for the faint-hearted, as the show promises a night filled with controversial jokes.

A spokesperson said: "If you're attracted to Jimmy's brand of comedy and think you can take the jokes, then by all means come out and play.

"If you think you'd be offended, then stay away."

Carr has performed stand-up to sell-out crowds globally, with his previous tour, Terribly Funny, selling over 1.2 million tickets worldwide.

His latest tour, Jimmy Carr: Laughs Funny, surpassed this total and marked the first time the comedian toured arenas in the UK and Ireland.

Carr, a household name in UK television, is known for hosting Channel 4’s 8 Out Of 10 Cats, 8 Out Of 10 Cats Does Countdown, and Big Fat Quiz.

He also has a massive following online, making him the most-followed UK stand-up comedian on YouTube.

Tickets for the Bradford show go on general sale at 10am on Friday, October 23, at bradfordlive.co.uk.

Priority Live members can access an exclusive pre-sale from 10am on Thursday, October 22.
