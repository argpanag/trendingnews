Hong Kong wakes up to coolest morning so far this autumn at 17.5 degrees | South China Morning Post

TheTools · 12.11.2026 01:28 · source · permalink

The Strong Monsoon Signal remains in effect, as the mercury in parts of the New Territories dropped as low as 15.1 degrees.

Hong Kong recorded its coolest morning so far this autumn on Tuesday, with the city’s weather forecaster posting a minimum temperature of 17.5 degrees Celsius (63.5 Fahrenheit). A strong northeast monsoon has brought cool and windy weather for the day, but temperatures are expected to rise again later in the week as the monsoon eases.
