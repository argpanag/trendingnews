Former Plymouth Airport site set to be taken over in £4.4m deal

TheTools · 28.11.2026 01:21 · source · permalink

Council bosses are set to take back control of a former airport site, 15 years after it shut.

Plymouth City Airport closed in December 2011 after operators Sutton Harbour Group (SHG) said it was no longer viable.

Plymouth City Council confirmed it is acquiring the long leasehold interest of the 95-acre site from Plymouth City Airport Ltd, a subsidiary of SHG, for just under £4.4m.

The authority said it would also acquire a 15-acre adjacent parcel of freehold-owned land in the north of the city from the same company for about £2.6m.

Council leader Tudor Evans said Plymouth had been in a "holding pattern" over the site for the last 15 years.

He added by owning the land outright, the authority would be "finally in control of decision making" for the grounds, which are "of huge strategic importance to Plymouth".

The council already owns the freehold interest in the site, but Plymouth Airport Ltd has 127 years left on the lease which gives it control of the site.

Holding both the freehold and the long leasehold interests would give the council complete control of future decisions relating to the area, the local authority said.

Evans added: "The council has been working hard to acquire this land for a long time and I'm pleased we are now in a position to move forward.

"It has been a complex undertaking, but this is a significant moment in the history of the site."
