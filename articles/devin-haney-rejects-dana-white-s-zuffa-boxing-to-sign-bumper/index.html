Devin Haney rejects Dana White's Zuffa Boxing to sign bumper deal with DAZN

TheTools · 23.10.2026 19:02 · source · permalink
  • George Glinski
  • Lewis Thompson
  • Published: 20:02, 23 Oct 2026
  • Updated: 17:16, 24 Oct 2026

Devin Haney has inked a multi-fight deal with DAZN.

He becomes the latest high-profile boxer to exclusively sign with the streaming network alongside Anthony Joshua and Teofimo Lopez.

Haney had an offer on the table to join the Dana White-fronted Zuffa Boxing earlier this year.

The offer came with the stipulation that he would fight Shakur Stevenson next.

However, the WBO welterweight champion chose instead to join DAZN in a deal that will allow him to remain a promotional free agent.

Haney ready to make boxing's biggest fights

“I’m at a point in my career where I want the biggest challenges and the fights the fans want to see,” Haney said.

"I’m excited to partner with DAZN. This agreement removes any doubt or uncertainty about my ability to make the major fights in boxing.

"I’m in position to fight anyone, and I’m focused, motivated and ready for whoever is next.”

Head of Boxing, North America at DAZN Britton Hardin added: “Devin has proven that he belongs on boxing’s biggest stages.

“He is an elite champion, a proven headliner and one of the most influential figures shaping the future of the sport.

“This partnership creates the opportunity to deliver the marquee matchups fans want while bringing the next defining phase of Devin’s career to DAZN’s global audience.”

What's next for Haney?

Haney is expected to have a unification clash against IBF king Liam Paro announced imminently.

Paro snatched the belt from Lewis Crocker by unanimous decision after 12 epic rounds in his native Australia in June.

It was reported this week that Haney had agreed to the fight, which is expected to take place by the end of the year in the US.

Haney was originally ordered to defend his belt against fellow undefeated American Keyshawn Davis in July.

However, the bout collapsed due to a disagreement over the purse split.

According to the WBO, Davis pulled out of the ordered title bout after rejecting a 25 per cent purse split.

Haney hasn’t fought since outpointing Brian Norman Jr in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, in November 2025 to extend his professional record to 33-0.

The 27-year-old previously defeated Jose Ramirez in New York after a majority decision loss to current WBC titleholder Ryan Garcia.

However, Garcia's win over Haney was later overturned to a no-contest when the former was found to have failed two drug tests taken around the fight.

Lopez, another DAZN fighter, holds the final WBA belt at 147lbs, having outclassed Rolly Romero over 12 rounds in Las Vegas in August to become a three-weight world champion.
