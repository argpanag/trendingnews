A high building far north of the state has strengthened the trade winds today. Gusts have not been overly impressive nor persistent, but peak gusts of 45 to 50 mph have been measured in the typical windy areas around Maui County and the Big Island. Have left the Wind Advisory in place until 6 AM Thursday with the potential for a further uptick this evening. Winds will be strongest through gaps and over and downslope of ridges, where gusts of 50 to 55 mph are possible. The combination of strong trade winds and dry conditions has elevated fire weather concerns today.
A weakening frontal boundary will bring somewhat deeper moisture south through the islands tonight into Thursday. Increased shower coverage is expected over mostly windward and mauka areas, but a few may make it into leeward areas too.
The strong trade winds should begin to ease some Friday down to breezy to locally strong speeds. Another high pressure ridge will slide east well to the north of the state this weekend. Some increase in the trades is possible again Saturday night and Sunday before the high stalls to our northeast early next week. A reduced pressure gradient should allow trades to relax some by that time. Brief pulses of better moisture in the trades will keep typical trade wind showers over windward and mauka areas mostly during the overnight and early morning hours through the period.

Locally strong trade winds will continue tonight before gradually weakening through the second half of the week. A dying frontal boundary will push through the island chain tonight into Thursday, providing an increase in shower activity. Windward and mountain areas will see most of the showers, but leeward areas could see some spillover showers as well. MVFR conditions are expected within showers while VFR prevails elsewhere. Showers will quickly decrease during the afternoon across Kauai and Oahu, and linger around Maui County and the Big Island a bit longer on Thursday.

Expect the strong surface winds to weaken by tomorrow morning, and the upper level moderate turbulence to diminish by early tonight. AIRMET Tango will likely remain in effect for low level moderate turbulence through the next several days.
Mountain obscurations are likely across windward sections of the islands as the frontal boundary moves down the island chain later tonight.
