Sometimes the best betting angles are the ones that make you feel uncomfortable. Backing a goalless first half when Crystal Palace's defence has been leaking goals like a sieve certainly falls into that category, but there is a compelling reason to think the market might be looking in the wrong direction.

A no-risk, no-thrills first half might be exactly what the doctor ordered.

When a team plays with that level of directness, there are plenty of opportunities for centre-backs to be fouled.
