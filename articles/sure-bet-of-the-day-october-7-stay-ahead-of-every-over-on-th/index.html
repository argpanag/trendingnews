Why are the United States of America Still Well Backed?

Their recent run has been mixed, with 2 wins, 2 losses and 1 abandoned game in their last 5 matches. Namibia are 5th in the League 2 standings, with 29 points from 33 matches. They have managed 13 wins, suffered 17 defeats and had 3 no-results. Their recent form has been less convincing, with just 1 win from their last 5 matches, along with 3 defeats and 1 no-result. Jan Frylinck has been one of their better performers, making 363 runs in his last 10 matches at an average of 45.38. Malan Kruger has scored 170 runs in his last 7 games. Pitch Report and Final Prediction

As the Grand Prairie Stadium in Dallas has a firm playing surface, batters can expect decent pace and bounce throughout the innings. The ball generally comes nicely onto the bat, giving stroke-makers a good chance to score quickly once they settle in. An average 1st-innings T20I score of around 165 suggests that the venue can produce competitive totals. Seamers could get a little help with the new ball, especially in the early stages, while spinners may become more useful as the pitch wears. Chasing has often been the preferred option after winning the toss, but a team posting a big score can still put pressure on the opposition. Overall, the conditions should suit the USA, giving them the edge in this match.

For the 3rd T20I, Takashinga Sports Club in Harare should provide a decent surface for both batters and bowlers. Batters may find it easier to score once they get their eye in, with the average total sitting at around 156. The new ball could keep the seamers interested, especially during the early overs, while the pitch has generally offered more to pace bowlers than spinners. A team winning the toss could look to bat first and make use of the better batting conditions before the surface slows down. Given the way the series has gone so far, West Indies Women will head into the match with the advantage.
