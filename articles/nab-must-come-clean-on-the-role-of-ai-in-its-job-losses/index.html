NAB must come clean on the role of AI in its job losses

TheTools · 25.10.2026 04:04 · source · permalink

The AFR View

The bank says there is no link between its redundancies and artificial intelligence. But we need more transparency about how roles are changing to know this for sure.

The major banks are facing into myriad headwinds: the economy is softening thanks to high inflation, rising interest rates, sagging confidence, low productivity and falling house prices.

Higher rates are typically positive for banks’ earnings, but the benefits from four increases in the official cash rate this year are being diluted by fierce competition for deposits and mortgages, with brokers arranging $8 in every $10 of home lending. Changes to property taxes in the May budget have hit mortgage applications; retail credit growth is sluggish.
