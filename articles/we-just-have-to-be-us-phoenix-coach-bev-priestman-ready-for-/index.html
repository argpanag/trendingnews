'We just have to be us' — Phoenix coach Bev Priestman ready for first game - Friends of Football

TheTools · 16.10.2026 15:00 · source · permalink

Wellington Phoenix head coach Bev Priestman wants her side to start their new women’s A-League season strongly, regardless of who pulls on the shirt for the opening game against champions Melbourne City.

“We’ve got a list of injuries unfortunately going into this game, but what I’ve seen today on the training pitch and this week is that it’s next player up,” she said ahead of Sunday’s match at Hnry Stadium.

“It’s just a game,” she told media. “Put any team in front of us, this game has to be about us attacking the season with a fresh mindset.

“It is a rematch from the final and all of these things, but at the end of the day we know the A-League week one looks nothing like the end of the season.

“That’s the nature of the league, the travel, the injuries. I’m not going to get carried away.

“We want to start stronger than we did last year, it took us until that Sydney game [a 7-0 win], to really go for it.”

Priestman believes the team is ready to make another step up from last season’s record-breaking performance.

“My expectations are that anyone putting on that shirt, we’re the most together team.

“We’ve got talent in the team that can make things happen, and I think that is the bit I’ll be measuring the most in this first game.”

“It’s new for us, but I think we’re excited to take the field as Wellington Phoenix, where the opposition know they have a difficult game on their hands.

“We just have to be us.”

A short-handed squad is still waiting for CJ Bott to return after having her first baby during the off-season, while Sabitra “Samba” Bhandari is another long-term absentee.

New signings Kam Pickett and Mikaela Bangalan will also miss this game, while the four Football Ferns who travelled to Panama may be short of match sharpness after only recently returning to the capital.

“We’ve been in these circumstances before, we’ve rose to some great challenges. There’s a huge opportunity available to us.

“This is the first step; we’re excited and ready, and it’s a really good opportunity for us to put our best foot forwards on our turf.”
