Aries

It is a great time for you and your team to branch out and try something totally new. It could be procedural or a whole new approach to your work. Whatever the case may be, make sure your main priority is change, change, and more change.

Taurus

Make sure you step up and take responsibility if things go south—it will look much better than your slinking away, leaving a big mess for others to deal with. Of course, things might turn out golden or far better than anticipated.

Gemini

You are at the mercy of your moods for the time being, but you might be able to work that to your advantage. Be as open and honest as can be if you want to pull it off. Someone who has a keen sense of BS is watching you closely. Keep it on the level.

Cancer

Nobody, at least nobody you know, likes change, but you are in a position that practically demands it. Be as gentle as you can with your coworkers, but do the right thing now that you can see what is needed. Slow changes will yield the best reactions.

Pisces
