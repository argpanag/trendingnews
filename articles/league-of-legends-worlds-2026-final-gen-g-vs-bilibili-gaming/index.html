Gen.G and Bilibili Gaming (BLG) meet tomorrow, Saturday, November 14, in the League of Legends World Championship final at Barclays Center in Brooklyn. The best-of-five is scheduled for 2 p.m. ET (11 a.m. PT). Gen.G swept both knockout opponents, while BLG beat three-time defending champion T1 to reach their second Worlds final.

The Gen.G vs BLG prediction favours Gen.G to win the series. The LCK champions have dropped just one game at Worlds, but their semifinal sweep included a comeback from more than 4,000 gold down. BLG, meanwhile, won back-to-back games after T1 levelled their semifinal. Both teams have already had to adjust when a series stopped going their way.

Gen.G arrive in the final unbeaten in knockout play, with a 3-0 sweep of LYON in the quarterfinals and another 3-0 sweep of Invictus Gaming in the semifinals. BLG, meanwhile, swept Dplus KIA 3-0 and then beat T1 3-1, ending T1’s run of four straight Worlds finals.

The stakes are significant for both sides. It is Gen.G’s first final under the Gen.G name and their first since Samsung Galaxy won in 2017, while BLG are back in a second final after losing to T1 in 2024. Anyone weighing the esports odds and betting markets for this series should note that it is also the first Worlds final without T1 since 2021.

The two finalists took different routes through the Swiss Stage:

Gen.G dropped their only game at Worlds in a 2–1 Swiss Stage win over LYON, then swept them in the quarterfinals. In the semifinal, Gen.G came back from 6–0 down in kills in Game 3 to complete a 3–0 sweep of Invictus Gaming.

BLG lost twice in Swiss and qualified with a 2–0 win over Hanwha Life Esports. They’ve since won six of seven knockout games, including a 3–1 semifinal win over T1 after the defending champions tied the series in Game 2.

Treat that record as historical context: the bot lane was different at the time, with Peyz for Gen.G and Elk for BLG, and the two current starting fives have never met in a best-of-five.

In his first Worlds final, Chovy faces Knight in a mid-lane matchup that could shape the early dragon fights. Whoever clears the wave first can help Canyon or Xun secure vision and set up in the river before the other team arrives.

Ruler vs Viper could decide the tighter teamfights. Viper went 7/1/8 on Ezreal in Game 3 against T1, putting BLG ahead for good. Against Gen.G, ON and Bin can give him room to deal damage by starting fights before Ruler is in position.

Under Fearless Draft, champions picked in one game are unavailable to both teams for the rest of the series. If the final reaches Game 4 or 5, both teams will have fewer options for starting fights and protecting their bot carries.
