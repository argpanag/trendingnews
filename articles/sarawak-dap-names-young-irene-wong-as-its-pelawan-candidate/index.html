Sarawak DAP names young Irene Wong as its Pelawan candidate

TheTools · 20.11.2026 08:56 · source · permalink

Sarawak DAP chairman Chong Chieng Jen says this is part of efforts by the party to bring younger voices into the state legislative assembly.

Sarawak DAP chairman Chong Chieng Jen said this was part of efforts by the party to bring younger voices into the state legislative assembly, Bernama reported.

He said Wong, 24, had spent two years serving the constituency as an assistant to Sibu MP Oscar Ling.

“Who better to represent the voices of young people than a young person herself?” he told a press conference in Sibu today.

Chong also announced Stanley Chiew, an aide to Lanang MP Alice Lau, as DAP’s candidate for Bukit Assek.

He said both candidates were familiar with local issues and hoped voters would allow them to bring alternative views from Sibu to the assembly.

DAP will announce its candidates for the southern region in Kuching on Nov 22.

The Election Commission has set Dec 12 for polling, with nominations on Nov 28.

In the 12th Sarawak state election in 2021, DAP contested 26 of the 82 seats but won only two – Padungan and Pending.
