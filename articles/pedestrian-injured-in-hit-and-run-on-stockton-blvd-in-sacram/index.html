Pedestrian Injured in Hit-and-Run on Stockton Blvd in Sacramento

A pedestrian injured in hit-and-run on Stockton Blvd in Sacramento was struck by a vehicle on Monday morning, August 24, 2026.

Pedestrian Struck in Stockton Boulevard

The collision was reported at approximately 7:22 a.m. in Stockton Boulevard, reportedly in front of a library along the busy commercial corridor.

According to initial reports, the driver involved left the area after striking the pedestrian.

Fire personnel responded to the scene to provide medical assistance.

Pedestrian Suffers Foot Injury

The pedestrian reportedly suffered an injury to the foot in the collision. Additional information regarding the severity of the injury or whether the pedestrian was transported to a hospital was not immediately available.

Authorities have not released the identity of the injured person.

Search for Hit-and-Run Driver Continues

The available information did not include a description of the vehicle or driver involved. It was also not immediately clear which library the collision occurred in front of.

Authorities were continuing efforts to identify and locate the driver as the circumstances surrounding the collision remained under investigation.

Hit-and-Run Collisions Put Pedestrians at Risk

Pedestrians have little protection when struck by a motor vehicle and may suffer significant injuries even in lower-speed collisions. Drivers involved in an injury crash are required to stop and provide reasonable assistance rather than leaving the scene.

Anyone who witnesses a hit-and-run collision should safely document any identifying details about the vehicle and provide that information to law enforcement.

Why Hiring a Personal Injury Attorney Can Help

A pedestrian injured in a hit-and-run may still have options for recovering compensation even if the responsible driver has not yet been identified.
