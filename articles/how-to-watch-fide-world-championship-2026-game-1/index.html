How To Watch FIDE World Championship 2026 Game 1

TheTools · 23.11.2026 21:01 · source · permalink

The 2026 Salesforce FIDE World Championship between defending World Champion Gukesh Dommaraju and Challenger Javokhir Sindarov begins on Tuesday, November 24, at 9 a.m. ET / 15:00 CET (local time in Geneva) /  7:30 p.m. IST.

Sindarov will have the white pieces in game one and therefore make the first move of the match. Be sure to tune in to check out the action!

Match score

The 2026 Salesforce FIDE World Championship in Geneva, Switzerland decides the next world champion. Uzbek Challenger Javokhir Sindarov Indian Defending Champion plays Gukesh Dommaraju in a 14-game match, with the winner the first to 7.5 points. The players have two hours for 40 moves, then 30 minutes to the end of the game, with 30 seconds added each move from move 41 onward. The prize fund is $2,500,000, with $200,000 for a win and the remaining money split equally. If tied 7-7, a playoff will take place, starting with four games of 15+10 rapid chess.
