Let’s get back on track, boys. Please.

It’s time to get right. Our Seattle Seahawks face-off today against a San— umm excuse me, Los Angeles Chargers team that is struggling mightily, in much different ways than the Commanders. The ‘Hawks certainly have it in them to smash the Bolts. But, we shall see. Where will our squad show improvement? What will the running game look like? Will Sam Darnold be throwing the ball all over the gridiron? Or, will Coach Fleury try to find more of a balance with the play-calling? Will the DLine be more of a factor than last week’s game? Tune in to find out! Happy Sunday, and such. Thanks for being here. Go, ‘Hawks!
