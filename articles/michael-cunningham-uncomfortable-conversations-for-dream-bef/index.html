Michael Cunningham: ‘Uncomfortable conversations’ for Dream before WNBA Finals elimination game

TheTools · 29.10.2026 16:44 · source · permalink

The Dream were feeling good Wednesday after a video review of Game 5 that forward Angel Reese said included “some uncomfortable conversations” about what individual players must do to improve the team in the WNBA Finals.

They need to make more open shots. They need to play at the faster pace that they prefer. They need to cut down the unforced turnovers.

Checking those boxes will give the Dream a good chance of avoiding elimination in Game 6 against Golden State on Thursday at State Farm Arena. But the Dream also can’t falter when things get physical, and the referees don’t call it, as they did in Game 5.
