Mbappé's Absence Raises Questions

The latest news from the France national team has given Real Madrid reason to pay close attention. Kylian Mbappé missed Wednesday's team training session on November 11, instead working with France's medical staff and physiotherapists just one day before their UEFA Nations League clash against Italy.


The French Football Federation initially sought to ease concerns, insisting that the forward was not injured. However, officials offered few details about his absence and did not confirm whether he would be available for the match.

Later, French newspaper L'Équipe reported that Mbappé was experiencing hamstring discomfort, raising doubts about his participation against Italy.

What's Happening With Mbappé?

Although the reported discomfort is not believed to be serious, Zinedine Zidane and his coaching staff are reportedly unwilling to take unnecessary risks with their captain.

According to L'Équipe, the striker could miss Thursday's match in Milan and focus on recovering in time to face Turkey on Sunday. For now, the French federation has not officially ruled him out.

There is also a recent reason for caution. In September, Mbappé suffered a left knee problem during a match against Turkey, having scored France's winning goal.

That setback forced him to miss subsequent international fixtures, making his latest return to the national team particularly significant.


Real Madrid Monitor His Condition

Mbappé's absence comes at an inconvenient time for Real Madrid, who have also received concerning news about Aurélien Tchouaméni.

The midfielder was forced to leave the France camp early because of physical problems and return to Madrid, where he will continue his recovery.

In Mbappé's case, initial reports suggested that Real Madrid remained calm. According to Spanish media, the club trusts the forward's ability to manage his workload and avoid unnecessary physical risks.

However, reports of muscle discomfort have made his fitness worth monitoring. José Mourinho's side will want their leading attacking figure back in the best possible condition when domestic competition resumes.

Zidane Faces a Selection Dilemma

Mbappé's situation also complicates France's preparations for two important matches to close out the UEFA Nations League group stage.

Zidane's team will visit Italy on Thursday before hosting Turkey on Sunday, with qualification for the quarterfinals at stake.

Physical problems have already ruled out Tchouaméni, Marcus Thuram, and Pierre Kalulu, meaning a potential absence for Mbappé would create another headache for the French coach.

The main question is whether France's captain will recover in time for the second international fixture. Until then, both France and Real Madrid will be closely following his condition and awaiting further medical updates.

  • Confirmed absence: Mbappé missed France's November 11 training session and worked with the national team's medical staff.
  • Reported muscle discomfort: According to L'Équipe, hamstring problems could keep him out of the match against Italy.
  • Real Madrid on standby: The Spanish club is also monitoring Tchouaméni, who left the French national team camp early.
