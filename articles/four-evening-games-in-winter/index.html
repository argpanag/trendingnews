Four evening games in winter

The next seven matchdays in the Bundesliga have been scheduled. Eintracht play four times at 18:30, while the second half of the season begins on a Sunday.

The Bundesliga has confirmed the schedule for Matchdays 12 to 18. Four of Eintracht Frankfurt’s seven games in December and January kick off at 18:30, including the final match of the first half of the season against FC Bayern Munich at Deutsche Bank Park on Saturday 16 January. The second half of the campaign begins for the Hessians eight days later, also at home, against 1. FC Union Berlin on Sunday 24 January (15:30).

OneFootball Videos

Before that, there’s a pair of fixtures in the second week of the new year: the Eagles host Schalke 04 on Saturday 9 January and travel to SV Elversberg on Wednesday 13 January before the clash with the double winners from Munich.
