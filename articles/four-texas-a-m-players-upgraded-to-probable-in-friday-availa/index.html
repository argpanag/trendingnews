Four Texas A&M players upgraded to probable in Friday availability report

TheTools · 24.10.2026 00:50 · source · permalink

TUSCALOOSA, Ala. (KBTX) - After spending the week listed as questionable, four Texas A&M football players were upgraded to probable in Friday’s Southeastern Conference availability report before Saturday’s game against Alabama.

Wide receiver Mario Craver, defensive tackle DJ Hicks, linebacker Daymion Sanford and offensive lineman Trovon Baugh all have a 75% chance to play Saturday, according to the SEC’s availability policy.

Defensive end Anto Saka remains questionable for the 2:30 p.m. kickoff in Tuscaloosa.

Wide receivers Terry Bussy and Kelshaun Johnson, as well as offensive lineman Blake Ivy, are out for Saturday’s game.

On the other sideline, three Alabama players were downgraded to doubtful. Defensive back Zabien Brown, running back EJ Crowell and offensive lineman Racin Delgatty all have a 25% chance to play Saturday, according to SEC policy.

Crowell has rushed for 216 yards and four touchdowns in 59 total snaps this season. Delgatty has played the fourth-most snaps on the Alabama offense with 389. Brown has participated in the fourth-most snaps on defense with 385. He has 19 tackles and four passes defended this season.
