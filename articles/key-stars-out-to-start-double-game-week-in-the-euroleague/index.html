EuroLeague clubs adjust their rosters for Round 2 of the 2026–27 Regular Season

By Johnny Askounis/ info@eurohoops.net

Euroleague Basketball has released the injury report ahead of Round 2 in the 2026–27 EuroLeague Regular Season, revealing critical roster changes across multiple teams.

Review the official updates on EuroleagueBasketball.net.

Tuesday, September 29

Dubai Basketball vs. FC Barcelona (18:00 CEST)

Dubai has not yet sent an injury report for its game against Barcelona on Tuesday.

  • Dzanan Musa did not play during the Euroleague Basketball SuperCup, nor during Round 1.

Barcelona

  • Josh Nebo, Tosan Evbuomwan and Yoan Makoundou are not available for Tuesday’s game.
  • Evbuomwan is dealing with a shoulder injury, it was announced on September 4.
  • Makoundou is recovering from a knee injury, it was announced on September 11.

Zalgiris Kaunas vs. Olympiacos Piraeus (19:00 CEST)

Zalgiris has not yet sent an injury report for its game against Olympiacos on Tuesday.

  • Saben Lee was a game-time decision for Round 1 but ultimately did not play.
  • Nigel Williams-Goss is out for two weeks with a hamstring injury suffered in Round 1.
  • Deividas Sirvydis and Kaodirichi Akobundu-Ehiogu did not make the roster that made the trip for Round 1.

Olympiacos

  • Nikola Milutinov did not travel with the club to Kaunas.
  • Bryant Dunston and George Papas both signed with the club on Monday.

Anadolu Efes Istanbul vs. Real Madrid (19:00 CEST)

Efes

  • Isaia Cordinier remains sidelined after undergoing surgery in May, as was announced on May 18.
  • Georgios Papagiannis injured his ACL in October 2025 and has been recovering ever since.

Real Madrid has not yet sent an injury report for its game at Efes on Tuesday.

  • New signing Nick Smith has not yet been registered.
  • Usman Garuba suffered an Achilles injury at last May’s Final Four.

Fenerbahce Tarfin Istanbul vs. FC Bayern Munich (19:45 CEST)

Fenerbahce

  • Shane Larkin and Marcus Bingham are game-time decisions for Tuesday’s game.

Bayern has not yet sent an injury report for its game at Fenerbahce on Tuesday.

  • Niels Giffey, Kamar Baldwin and Duane Washington were not on the game-day roster in Round 1.

Crvena Zvezda Meridianbet Belgrade vs. Hapoel IBI Tel Aviv (20:00 CEST)

Crvena Zvezda has not yet sent an injury report for its game against Hapoel on Tuesday.

  • Nikola Djurisic was ruled out for Round 1 due to a thigh injury.

Hapoel

  • Tomer Ginat remains sidelined with a knee injury

Armani Olimpia Milan vs. Virtus Bologna (20:30 CEST)

Milan has not yet sent an injury report for its game against Virtus on Tuesday.

  • Garrison Mathews was sidelined for Round 1 with a minor muscle strain.

Virtus

  • Kevin Kokila is not available for Round 2.

Valencia Basket vs. Kosner Baskonia Vitoria-Gasteiz (20:30 CEST)

Valencia has not yet sent an injury report for its game against Baskonia on Tuesday.

  • Yankuba Sima, Gonzalo Corbalan and Lucas Mari did not travel to Istanbul for Round 1.

Baskonia has not yet sent an injury report for its game at Valencia on Tuesday.

  • Alex Len has left the club.

Paris Basketball vs. Partizan Mozzart Bet Belgrade (20:45 CEST)

Paris has not yet sent an injury report for its game against Partizan on Tuesday.

  • Frank Ntilikina and Maozinha Pereira were not on the roster in Round 1.

Partizan

  • Vanja Marinkovic (achilles) and Joffrey Lauvergne (calf) remain out for Round 2.
  • Derek Willis is expected to be sidelined until late October with an ankle injury.

Wednesday, September 30

Maccabi Rapyd Tel Aviv vs. Besiktas Istanbul (20:05 CEST)

Maccabi has not yet sent an injury report for its game against Besiktas on Wednesday.

  • Jimmy Clark returned to action in Round 1, having missed the Israeli Super Cup.

Besiktas has not yet sent an injury report for its game at Maccabi on Wednesday.

  • DaQuan Jeffries suffered a wrist injury in Round 1 and had to undergo surgery.

Panathinaikos AKTOR Athens vs. LDLC ASVEL Villeurbanne (20:15 CEST)

Panathinaikos has not yet sent an injury report for its game against ASVEL on Wednesday.

  • Kendrick Nunn, Kostas Sloukas, Nigel Hayes-Davis and Moustapha Fall are missed Round 1.

ASVEL has not yet sent an injury report for its game at Panathinaikos on Wednesday.

  • TyTy Washington, Nate Sestina, Edwin Jackson and Mathis Dossou-Yovo were not on the roster in Round 1.
