Transfer: Bayer Leverkusen open January exit for Victor Boniface as two clubs ask

TheTools · 15.11.2026 05:19 · source · permalink

Bayer Leverkusen are ready to sanction Victor Boniface’s departure in January, either permanently or on loan, after two clubs asked about the Super Eagles striker’s availability.

According to Sky Sport's Florian Plettenberg, Boniface wants to leave Bayer Leverkusen, and the Bundesliga club are prepared to let him.

However, neither of the two interested clubs seeking to sign Boniface has been named.

Boniface's current contract with Leverkusen will expire in 2028, and the Nigerian currently ranks as the club's third-choice striker.

He spent the 2025-26 season on loan at Bundesliga side Werder Bremen and has not found top form since returning from a knee injury.
