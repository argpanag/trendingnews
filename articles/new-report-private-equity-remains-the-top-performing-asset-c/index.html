NEW REPORT: Private Equity Remains the Top-Performing Asset Class for America’s Public Pension Funds - American Investment Council

TheTools · 19.10.2026 17:11 · source · permalink

Farouki Majeed, CIO of Ohio SERS: “Private equity continues to be the highest returning asset class in our portfolio over 10 years and longer periods and thus contributes positively to the long-term sustainability of the pension fund.”

WASHINGTON, D.C. – Today, the American Investment Council released its 2026 Pension Trends Report, showing that private equity remains the best-performing asset class for America’s public pension funds. Private equity investments delivered a median annualized return of 13.2% over a 10-year period across the 200 pension funds studied, representing nearly 34 million public-sector workers and retirees. Private equity outperformed every other asset class over the long term, including public equity, real estate, and fixed income.
 
“America’s firefighters, police officers, and teachers spend their entire careers serving the public. They deserve a secure retirement, and private equity helps provide it,” said AIC President and CEO Will Dunham. “Year after year, private equity has consistently been the highest-performing asset class for public pension funds across the country, helping more than 34 million public servants benefit from strong returns and diversified portfolios built for the long term.”

AIC’s annual study has found that private equity has been the highest-returning asset class in public pension portfolios every year since 2012. Nearly nine in ten public pensions studied invest in private equity, underscoring its longstanding role in supporting retirement security for public servants across the country.

By The Numbers:

  • 13.2% – The private equity median annualized return over a 10-year period, higher than every other asset class, including public equity, real estate, and fixed income.
  • 34 Million – The number of U.S. public sector workers and retirees served by the pension funds in the study.
  • 86% – The share of public pension funds nationwide that invest a portion of their portfolio in private equity.
  • 88% – The share of institutional investors surveyed by Preqin who plan to increase or maintain their private market investments over the next year.

Top Ten Public Pension Funds by Private Equity Returns:

  1. Vermont Pension Investment Commission – 16.61%
  2. Ohio School Employees Retirement System – 16.27%
  3. Massachusetts Pension Reserves Investment Trust – 16.18%
  4. West Virginia Investment Management Board – 16.12%
  5. Tennessee Consolidated Retirement System – 16.04%
  6. City of Baltimore Employees’ Retirement System – 15.80%
  7. Nebraska Investment Council – 15.40%
  8. The Florida Retirement System – 15.33%
  9. Public School and Education Employee Retirement Systems of Missouri – 15.20% (tie)
  10. Illinois State Board of Investment – 15.20% (tie)
