O'Callaghan departs as Shamrock Rovers boss

TheTools · 29.10.2026 12:51 · source · permalink

Shamrock Rovers have confirmed the departure of boss James O'Callaghan after less than a year in charge.

O’Callaghan, who enjoyed success during his eight-year stint at Peamount United prior to joining the Hoops, had just completed his first Women’s Premier Division season.

They finished fourth, 15 points behind champions Athlone Town.

It was a similar campaign to which Shamrock Rovers had in 2025 in which they ended fifth, 18 points behind top side Athlone.

In a statement, the club said: "Following the conclusion of the 2026 season, we can confirm that James O'Callaghan will be departing his role as First Team Head Coach.

"We would like to thank James for his commitment and professionalism during his time at the Club and wish him all the very best for the future."

Meanwhile the nominees for SSE Airtricity Manager of the Year award have been confirmed.

League-winning boss Lily Agg of Athlone Town, FAI Cup winner Sean Russell of Shelbourne and Gavin Hughes of Sligo Rovers are in with a chance of winning the award.
