Turkey vs Belgium - Prediction, where to watch, kick-off time and line-ups 12-11-2026

TheTools · 12.11.2026 12:00 · source · permalink

Belgium and Turkey meet on Thursday, 12 November, in an international fixture that brings together two of Europe’s most talented national teams. Both sides possess attacking quality, making this an intriguing contest for football fans and bettors alike.

Belgium will look to use their experience and attacking options to gain the upper hand, while Turkey’s energetic midfield and technically gifted forwards could make life difficult for their opponents.

Find out where to watch Belgium vs. Turkey, what our experts are predicting, and the probable line-ups for this international encounter.

Belgium vs. Turkey - Head-to-head record

Belgium and Turkey have a history of competitive meetings, including encounters in European Championship qualifying. Unlike two unfamiliar opponents, these nations have previous results to consider when preparing for their latest meeting.

Belgium enjoyed success in their two Euro 2012 qualifying encounters, winning 3-2 away in September 2010 before drawing 1-1 at home in June 2011. Those matches demonstrated how closely contested this fixture can become, even when one side enters as the favourite.

However, these results are now more than 15 years old. Both national teams have undergone substantial changes, so their historical record offers limited insight into how this particular Belgium vs. Turkey match might unfold.

Belgium - Looking to establish control

Belgium enter this encounter with considerable attacking talent and the expectation of competing against Europe’s strongest international teams.

Recent form

Belgium’s biggest advantage is their ability to create chances through several different attacking combinations. Romelu Lukaku provides a physical presence in the penalty area, while players such as Leandro Trossard and Jérémy Doku offer different ways to break down organised defences.

The midfield is equally important. Youri Tielemans can dictate possession, while Kevin De Bruyne, when available, provides the creativity needed to unlock compact defensive lines.

Defensive organisation remains a key consideration against Turkey. Belgium cannot afford to leave excessive space between their midfield and back line, particularly against opponents capable of moving the ball quickly into attacking positions.

urkey - Searching for their first Nations League points

Turkey face a difficult challenge against a Belgium side that comfortably defeated them in October. Their home advantage could help, but defensive improvements are essential.

Recent form

Turkey have endured a disappointing start to their UEFA Nations League campaign, losing all four of their opening matches. They began with a 1-0 defeat against France before suffering a heavy 4-1 home loss to Italy.

Their struggles continued in October. Belgium secured a convincing 3-0 victory in the reverse fixture, while Italy defeated Turkey 3-1 just three days later. That leaves the Turkish side with only two goals scored and 11 conceded in four matches.

Kenan Yıldız’s absence is another setback, removing an important attacking option. Kerem Aktürkoğlu and İrfan Kahveci have also been omitted from the squad, while Abdülkerim Bardakcı is suspended.

Turkey will need more from Arda Güler and Hakan Çalhanoğlu if they hope to trouble Belgium. Their attacking quality is undeniable, but defensive organisation has been their biggest weakness throughout the competition.

Belgium enter this fixture as favourites, having already beaten Turkey 3-0 in October. Their attacking options give them a clear advantage, although playing away from home makes a convincing victory less certain.

Turkey have conceded 11 goals in four Nations League matches, suggesting Belgium could create enough opportunities to win by more than one goal. The Belgium -1.25 Asian Handicap is an aggressive option for those expecting another comfortable result.
