Al Roker Inducts Scarlett Johansson into Very Special ‘5-Timer’s Club’

TheTools · 05.11.2026 17:30 · source · permalink

Chrissy Callahan

If the whole acting thing doesn’t work out for Scarlett Johansson, she can always fall back on another one of her skills: forecasting the weather.

While stopping by Studio 1A to discuss her new film “Paper Tiger,” the star marked a special milestone with TODAY’s resident weatherman, Al Roker, and helped him share the forecast for the fifth time live on TODAY.

“This is your fifth time. So much like they do on ‘SNL,’ we got you your fifth weather robe,” Al said as he helped the star put the robe on.

“Five-timers club!” Savannah said.

On “Saturday Night Live,” celebrities who host the show five times are typically presented with a special velvet jacket with the number five on it. Johansson is one of many stars who have received the honor.

The actor was touched by the gesture and hugged Al, saying, “This is a dream.”

“And we even have a little thunderbolt on the back for you,” Al pointed out.

“How chic,” the star said. “Oh my God, can I wear this out? It’s like a little dinner robe.”

As she approached the weather map, Johansson was impressed by how much activity was going on across the country.

“My gosh, you guys, look at all this. It looks like there’s a bunch of rain happening over here here along the coast,” she said.

As she examined the map, the actor pointed out one state that’s currently having gorgeous weather.

“Florida is where you want to be right now. That looks nice and roasty toasty,” she said.

As she made her way to the west coast, Johansson was surprised to see weather in the 30s in the Western Plains.

“What’s that all about?” she asked Al.

“It’s about fall,” he quipped.

Al and Johansson then highlighted some more rain on the east coast and the weatherman acknowledged that it’s been pretty dry lately.

“We’re so happy that there’s rain out there,” the actor said.

And just like that, the segment was over, and Al took a moment to congratulate the star.

“This was the best fifth weather ever!” he said.

“Yay!” Johansson exclaimed as she raised her arms in the air.
