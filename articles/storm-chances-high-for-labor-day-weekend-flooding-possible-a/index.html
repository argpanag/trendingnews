LAFAYETTE, La. (KLFY) - Showers and thunderstorms will be likely throughout the day Saturday. At times, storms will produce heavy rain and could lead to flooding in parts of the area especially in the afternoon hours. Highs today will reach near 90 degrees ahead of the rain. Rain chances remain high through Labor Day.

The tropics are quiet and look to remain that way over the next week.

After Labor Day, we will see a return to typical early September weather with daily rain chances and highs in the lower 90s. Each day, storms will develop after the lunch hour before raining themselves out prior to sunset.
