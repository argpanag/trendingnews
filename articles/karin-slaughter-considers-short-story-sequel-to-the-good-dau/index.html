Karin Slaughter Considers Short Story Sequel to 'The Good Daughter' - The Chenab Times

TheTools · 15.11.2026 20:38 · source · permalink

Author Karin Slaughter is contemplating a return to the characters of her 2017 novel, ‘The Good Daughter,’ potentially through a follow-up short story focusing on sisters Charlie and Sam.

The Chenab Times has learned that Slaughter, who also penned and showran the recent Peacock series adaptation of the novel, revealed her inclination to revisit the compelling sibling dynamic.

Slaughter expressed that she has “actually been thinking about” creating a new narrative that bridges the gap from her original bestselling novel.

While details remain scarce, the prospect of a new literary installment offers fans an opportunity to further explore the complex relationship between Charlie and Sam, central figures in ‘The Good Daughter,’ which delved into themes of family trauma, secrets, and the enduring impact of past events.

The novel, a staple in the crime fiction genre, established Slaughter’s reputation for crafting intricate plots and psychologically rich characters. Its adaptation into a streaming series brought these elements to a wider audience, further solidifying the enduring appeal of her work.

The announcement, if materialized into a published short story, would mark a significant development for readers eager for more content from the author and the universe she has meticulously built.
