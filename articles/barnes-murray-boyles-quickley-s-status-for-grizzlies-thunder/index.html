Barnes, Murray-Boyles & Quickley's status for Grizzlies-Thunder: Injuries & Preview

TheTools · 09.11.2026 23:01 · source · permalink

In this story:

Game date, time and location: Monday, Nov. 9, 6:30 p.m. CST, Scotiabank Arena, Toronto, Ontario, Canada

TV: DAZN (Memphis), Sportsnet, SN+ (Toronto)

Radio: 92.9 FM, 680 AM (Memphis), 590 AM The Fan (Toronto)

VITALS: The Memphis Grizzlies (6-3) and Toronto Raptors (5-4) meet for the first of two matchups in 2026-2027. Last season, the Raptors won the season series, 2-0.

The Grizzlies are 29-31 all-time in the regular season against the Raptors, including 22-34 at home and 13-43 on the road.

The Grizzlies head into this regular season with somewhat of a blank slate. With Ja Morant, Jaren Jackson Jr. and Desmond Bane all playing for different teams, the Grizzlies have moved on to a young core surrounding No. 3 pick Cameron Boozer, 2024 No. 9 pick Zach Edey, 2025 No. 11 pick Cedric Coward and 2024 No. 39 pick Jaylen Wells, with veteran guard Ty Jerome running the show.

The Raptors are coming off a season where they finished with a 46-36 record, good for the fifth-best record in the Eastern Conference. They were eventually eliminated by the Cleveland Cavaliers in seven games in the first round of the NBA Playoffs.

This offseason, the Raptors pulled off a blockbuster trade, bringing back their 2019 Finals MVP, Kawhi Leonard, by trading away Brandon Ingram, Gradey Dick, two first round picks, a first round pick swap and a second round pick.

In the 2026 NBA Draft, the Raptors drafted Allen Graves with the 19th pick and Jaden Bradley with the 50th pick. Additionally, they signed Kyle Anderson, re-signed Alijah Martin and extended Jamal Shead.

Most recently, the Grizzlies are coming off a, 128-97, loss to the Oklahoma City Thunder.
