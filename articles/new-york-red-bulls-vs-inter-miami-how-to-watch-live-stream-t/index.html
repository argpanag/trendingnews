Match Preview: What’s at stake?

Inter Miami arrive in New Jersey in second place in the Eastern Conference, with 55 points from 30 games, while New York Red Bulls sit eighth with 36 points. The difference highlights the contrasting campaigns: Miami are chasing a strong finish to the regular season, while New York need points to protect their position in the playoff race.

Lionel Messi #10 of Inter Miami during a training session (Source: Leonardo Fernandez/Getty Images)

Lionel Messi remains the central figure for Inter Miami. The Argentine has scored 23 goals and provided 13 assists in the league, according to MLS‘s official player statistics, making him one of the most influential attacking players in the competition. Luis Suarez has also contributed 15 goals, giving Miami a formidable partnership as they look to finish the regular season strongly.

New York, meanwhile, will look to Julian Hall and Jorge Ruvalcaba to provide the attacking spark. Hall has scored 11 goals this season, while Ruvalcaba has added eight. The Red Bulls will need their attacking players to deliver against a Miami team that has scored 73 goals in the league, compared with New York’s 39.

The teams drew 2-2 in their previous meeting on April 11, 2026, so New York have already shown they can trouble Miami. However, the Red Bulls will need to improve defensively after recent defeats to D.C. United and Toronto FC. Miami, meanwhile, come into the matchup with Messi in productive form after he scored twice against Atlanta United.
