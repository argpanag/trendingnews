DIMAPUR — The Nagaland State Lottery results for the Dear Victory Friday draw, held on Friday, at 1 pm, have been declared.

According to the official information provided by the Nagaland State Lotteries, the winning number of Dear Victory Friday, the 47th draw held at 1 pm Friday, is 84L 10051 the winner of the 1st prize will receive INR 1 crore for a ticket priced at INR 7.

Where to claim prizes

The winners of Nagaland State Lottery draws must present their winning tickets, along with a completed claim form available online, a government-recognized photo ID, and passport-size photographs in order to claim their prizes.

Nagaland State Lottery results are also accessible on the official Nagaland Lotteries website, Lottery Sambad, and Eastern Mirror. Director Zhothisa Dawhuo stated that the lottery results can also be viewed on YouTube chaunnels including Nagaland Lotteries, Nagaland Dear Lottery, Nagaland Lottery, Nagaland State Lottery Live, and Nagaland Dear Laxmi.
