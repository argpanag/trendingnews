Taylor Swift Is Making a Big CMAs Return

TheTools · 10.09.2026 22:00 · source · permalink

On Thursday, Taylor Swift announced on Instagram that she will be returning to the Country Music Awards in November for the first time in ten years.

Swift last attended the Country Music Awards in 2016, when she presented Garth Brooks with the Entertainer of the Year award, her squad was still going strong, and she had not yet released Reputation. A lot has changed since then, but it seems Swift hasn’t left her country roots entirely behind. “Looks like I haven’t yee’d my last haw,” she wrote in her Instagram Story. “Thank you CMA!!!! See you there Nov 18.”

Swift has gotten a handful of CMA nominations since 2016 — she won Song of the Year in 2017 for “Better Man” and was nominated for Video of the Year in 2018 and 2022 for “Babe” and “I Bet You Think About Me,” respectively. This year, she’s nominated for Single of the Year for her (very country sounding) “I Knew It, I Knew You,” which she wrote for Toy Story 5. Add this to the rapidly growing list of evidence that her next album will have more than a little twang.
