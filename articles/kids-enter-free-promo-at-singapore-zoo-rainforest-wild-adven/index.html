Kids Enter Free Promo At Singapore Zoo & Rainforest Wild Adventure For Children's Day 2026

TheTools · 29.09.2026 07:18 · source · permalink

Looking for a wild way to celebrate Children’s Day this year? How about taking the kids to meet the animals at Singapore Zoo and Rainforest Wild Adventure? For Children’s Day 2026, there is a Kids Enter Free promotional bundle for Singapore Zoo and Rainforest Wild Adventure available from now till 4 October, for visits from 2 to 18 October 2026 only. 

Kids Enter Free Promotion at Singapore Zoo and Rainforest Wild Adventure

There is currently a bundle where one child can enter into Singapore Zoo or Rainforest Wild Adventure for free with every paying adult. 

This promotion is currently available for purchase and valid for visits between Friday, 2 October 2026 and Sunday, 18 October 2026.

GREAT Bundle Of Joy Promotion

This Kids Enter Free promotion at Singapore Zoo and Rainforest Wild Adventure is for local residents only. 

The admission ticket price for Singapore Residents to Singapore Zoo is $44 under the promotion. This allows for admission for one adult and one child. The usual price for one adult and one child’s admission to Singapore Zoo is $74. 

The admission ticket price for Singapore Residents to Rainforest Wild Adventure is $50 under the promotion. This allows for admission for one adult and one child. The usual price for one adult and one child’s admission to Rainforest Wild Adventure is $85.

 

What to Do at Singapore Zoo

Singapore Zoo is a perennial favourite with families and for good reason. Not only do kids get to meet the animals at the zoo but they can also enjoy play time at KidzWorld. 

 

What to Expect at Rainforest Wild Adventure

Rainforest Wild Adventure is divided into two thematic zones – East and West and there are incredible animals found there. You may even be able to spot the newly born Okapi calf at Rainforest Wild Adventure EAST. 

There are also exciting Adventure+ activities to enjoy inside the wildlife park.

 

Singapore Zoo and Rainforest Wild Adventure Children’s Day Promotion

Get your Kids Enter Free promotion tickets from now till 4 October 2026 for visits from 2 to 18 October 2026. Terms and conditions apply.
