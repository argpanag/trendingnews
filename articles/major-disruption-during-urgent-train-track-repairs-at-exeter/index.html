Major disruption during urgent train track repairs at Exeter

TheTools · 13.11.2026 14:13 · source · permalink

Train services passing through Exeter St Davids have been disrupted

  • Published
    13 November 2026, 08:30 GMT

Train services have restarted after a track defect on a main rail line brought delays and cancellations.

National Rail said urgent repairs were carried out on Friday morning to the track at Exeter St Davids, with disruption continuing until 12:00 GMT and delays of up to 60 minutes.

Train services between Exeter St Davids and Plymouth via Newton Abbot may be cancelled, delayed or revised, as well as services into Cornwall, according to Great Western Railway (GWR).

The train operator said it was working with Network Rail to return to running its normal timetable as soon as possible.

GWR added: "We have had reports of a track defect in the Exeter St Davids area.

"If a small defect in the rail gets bigger from repeated bending and pressure as trains pass over it, it can break."

Services between Exeter St Davids and Paignton via Newton Abbot may also be cancelled, delayed or revised.
