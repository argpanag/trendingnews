  • Stacey Solomon has spoken out against media intrusion, stating that press photographers sitting outside her home make her feel harassed and stalked.
  • The television presenter described the behaviour as outrageous and argued that following children around at family events should be illegal.
  • Solomon explained that while public appearances are fair game, the heightened press intrusion has forced her to reconsider what she shares online.
  • She emphasised that family safety comes before everything and indicated she will take action if necessary to protect her privacy.
  • Her comments come ahead of the return of her reality series alongside husband Joe Swash, which airs on BBC One on 10 November.
