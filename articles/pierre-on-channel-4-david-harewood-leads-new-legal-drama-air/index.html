Pierre on Channel 4: David Harewood Leads New Legal Drama Airing Tonight - wheretowatch.tv

TheTools · 18.10.2026 23:01 · source · permalink

David Harewood leads Channel 4’s new legal drama Pierre, airing tonight, Monday 19 October, at 9pm. The six-part series follows a determined West London duty solicitor whose investigation into the suspicious death of a young client exposes a disturbing world of institutional corruption, prejudice and abuse of power.

Starring the award-winning actor known for Sherwood and Homeland, the drama explores what happens when a lawyer refuses to accept the official version of events. As Pierre challenges the police’s portrayal of his client as a criminal, his search for the truth puts his career, friendships and personal life under increasing pressure.

Produced by The Lighthouse, the company behind Scoop, Pierre combines a tense legal investigation with a character-driven story about justice and the risks of challenging powerful institutions.

What Is Pierre About?

Pierre, played by David Harewood OBE, is a West London duty solicitor who is as charming and funny as he is determined. A force of nature in the courtroom, he spends his days juggling the demands of his job, a fragile personal life and the constant struggle to keep his finances under control.

His resilience is tested when Michael, a young Black client, dies in suspicious circumstances. Unconvinced by the police’s account of the young man’s life and death, Pierre begins investigating the case himself.

As he questions the authorities’ portrayal of Michael as a criminal, Pierre uncovers a troubling web of institutional corruption and prejudice. His determination to expose the truth brings him into conflict with a system that appears determined to protect itself.

The series poses a difficult question: how far can one person go in pursuit of justice before the consequences become too great?

With the investigation threatening to consume him, Pierre must decide whether uncovering the truth is worth the personal cost.

What Happens in Pierre Tonight?

Tonight’s episode finds Pierre unable to stop thinking about video footage connected to Michael. The material raises questions that he cannot easily put aside, leaving him increasingly troubled by what it might reveal about the circumstances surrounding the young man’s death.

His best friend, DS Ronnie Miller, advises him to hand the footage over to the police. But Ronnie’s reaction also raises questions of its own. Why does he seem so uneasy, and is there something about the case that he is reluctant to confront?

As Pierre weighs his next move, the friendship between the solicitor and the detective becomes an important part of the unfolding mystery. Their different positions within the justice system may complicate matters as Pierre continues to pursue answers.

Meanwhile, Pierre’s colleague Danny Headman takes on a drugs case that threatens to derail a young woman’s future. The case introduces another perspective on the consequences of criminal accusations, as the legal team confronts the potentially life-changing impact of the decisions made within the justice system.

Together, the two storylines establish the central concerns of Pierre: the gap between justice and procedure, the power of the authorities and the human consequences when the system gets things wrong.

David Harewood Leads the Pierre Cast

Harewood takes centre stage as Pierre, bringing his experience in acclaimed dramas including Sherwood and Homeland to the role of a solicitor determined to challenge the official narrative surrounding his client’s death.

He is joined by Michele Austin, who plays Charlotte Manners, Pierre’s joint managing partner at the solicitor’s firm. Charlotte works alongside Pierre while navigating the pressures of running a legal practice.

Jason Flemyng stars as DS Ronnie Miller, Pierre’s best friend. His position as a police detective creates an intriguing tension as Pierre begins to question the handling of Michael’s case.

The wider ensemble includes:

  • David Harewood OBE as Pierre, the West London duty solicitor at the centre of the drama.
  • Michele Austin as Charlotte Manners, Pierre’s joint managing partner.
  • Jason Flemyng as DS Ronnie Miller, Pierre’s best friend.
  • Dean-Charles Chapman as Danny Headman, a member of the legal team handling a drugs case.
  • Nikkita Chadha as Alecia Khan.
  • Christopher Fairbank as Sean Lambert.
  • Sara Powell as Celeste Jeffreys, Pierre’s wife.
  • Cherrelle Skeete as Brenda Dean.
  • Dylan Ennis as Eli Jeffreys.
  • Ezrae Maye as Michael, the young client whose death triggers Pierre’s investigation.

Ennis and Maye join the series as newcomers, while the established cast brings experience from productions including 1917, Andor, Pennyworth, Boiling Point and This Is Going to Hurt.

A Legal Drama About Corruption and Prejudice

At the heart of Pierre is the tension between an individual’s determination to do the right thing and an institution’s ability to resist scrutiny.

Pierre is not presented as an untouchable hero. He is under financial pressure, his personal life is fragile, and his commitment to his work threatens to draw him into increasingly dangerous territory. His determination to challenge the police’s account of Michael’s death could expose wrongdoing, but it may also put the people closest to him at risk.

The series explores how assumptions about race and criminality can shape the way a case is investigated, and what happens when a solicitor refuses to accept those assumptions without question.

As Pierre digs deeper, the consequences extend beyond the courtroom. His relationship with Ronnie, his responsibilities at work and his family life all face pressure as he pursues a truth that others may prefer to remain hidden.

The drama’s central warning is stark: challenging a powerful system can carry a heavy price.

Who Made Pierre for Channel 4?

Pierre is a six-part original drama produced by The Lighthouse, whose previous work includes Scoop.

Sarmad Masud, known for You Don’t Know Me and Boarders, serves as lead director, while Jo Johnson, whose credits include Adolescence and 1000 Blows, is the producer.

The series is co-written by BAFTA-winning screenwriter and playwright Roy Williams OBE, known for Soon Gone: The Windrush Chronicles and Death of England: Face to Face, and BAFTA-nominated writer John Donnelly, whose credits include Summerwater and Utopia.

The creative team brings together experience in contemporary drama and socially engaged storytelling, placing questions of institutional power and personal responsibility at the centre of the series.

Pierre Release Time: When Is It on Channel 4?

Pierre airs tonight, Monday 19 October, at 9pm on Channel 4.

The six-part legal drama begins with Pierre’s investigation into Michael’s suspicious death, setting him on a collision course with the authorities as he attempts to uncover what really happened.

With David Harewood leading a strong ensemble cast, the series promises a tense examination of justice, corruption and the personal consequences of refusing to look the other way.

As Pierre becomes increasingly consumed by the case, the question is whether he can expose the truth without losing everything in the process.
