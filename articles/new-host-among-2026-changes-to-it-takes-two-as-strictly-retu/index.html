New host among 2026 changes to It Takes Two as Strictly returns

TheTools · 19.09.2026 18:25 · source · permalink

Strictly Come Dancing’s behind-the-scenes spin-off show It Takes Two is set to return to the screen with some major updates, as well as a new host. Strictly Come Dancing: It Takes Two returns for its 2026 series on Monday, September 28, 2026. The long-running spin-off will air on weeknights on BBC Two and BBC iPlayer.

The latest series will bring in comedian, presenter, and former contestant Ellie Taylor as its host, replacing former frontwoman Emma Willis. Ellie will also replace Emma on her regular BBC Radio 2 show while she presents Strictly alongside Josh Widdecombe and Johannes Radebe.

Ellie said: “What a total joy to return to Strictly in this new role. I feel incredibly honoured to follow in the footsteps of the phenomenal line-up of hosts who've made Strictly - It Takes Two so loved over the years.

“I can’t wait to immerse myself in all the gossip and sequins flying out of the ballroom in order to bring my fellow Strictly fans everything they need to know.

"I’m so excited, I could cha cha! (But I won’t - don’t worry… no one needs to see that again.)”

The 42-year-old comedian, was a contestant on the main show in 2022.

New features of It Takes Two will also include in-depth discussions among groups of celebs and groups of Pros - often without their partners.

A new Strictly alumni panel will feature on screens to revisit the Strictly ballroom for an insight into each weekend's show.

Costume Design Queen Vicky Gill will still be on hand to keep fans in the know about who's wearing what, and key members of the creative team will give viewers a deep dive into every aspect of what it takes to create a spectacular Strictly performance.

Helen Munson, BBC Commissioning Editor, said: "We'd like to say a huge thank you to the brilliant Janette Manrara and Fleur East, who've been a massive part of It Takes Two's continued success over the years.

"It Takes Two is a joy to make and we're so proud to have it on the BBC every autumn. As we embark on what will be an incredibly exciting new series of Strictly, we can't wait to be there every step of the way."

Rhe-an Archibald, Strictly - It Takes Two Executive Producer, BBC Studios, said: “This is Strictly – It Takes Two, reinvented for the 2026 season.

"With a wealth of new features and deep dives, we want to reflect the conversations happening in living rooms up and down the country – full of opinions, laughs, surprises and those moments everyone wants to talk about.

"With the fun and funny Ellie at the helm, it will be an utterly unmissable companion to Strictly Come Dancing.”
