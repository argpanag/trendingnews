WNBA Playoffs 2026 Results: Liberty Stun Lynx, Aces Beat Fever, Valkyries Defeat Wings & Dream Down Mystics | Game 1 Scores & Highlights

TheTools · 28.09.2026 05:01 · source · permalink

The 2026 WNBA playoffs got off to a fast-paced start Sunday, with several first-round series producing decisive Game 1 results. The Golden State Valkyries, powered by Cecilia Zandalasini’s 22 points and Gabby Williams’ 20, pulled away from the Dallas Wings for a 104-80 victory. The Atlanta Dream also took control against the Washington Mystics with a 92-77 win, while the Las Vegas Aces defeated the Indiana Fever in their series opener.

The New York Liberty, meanwhile, delivered one of the night’s biggest surprises by beating the top-seeded Minnesota Lynx 91-75. The result leaves New York one victory away from advancing and potentially ending the Lynx’s run as the league’s top seed. With Game 2s now approaching, the Fever return home to face Las Vegas on Tuesday, while the other first-round matchups continue to take shape.

Golden State Valkyries Dominate Dallas Wings in Game 1

The Golden State Valkyries opened their 2026 WNBA playoff campaign in emphatic fashion, beating the Dallas Wings 104-80 on Sunday. Cecilia Zandalasini led the way with 22 points, including four three-pointers, while Gabby Williams added 20 and Tiffany Hayes contributed 21 off the bench. Golden State broke the game open in the second quarter, outscoring Dallas 37-11 to take a 58-31 lead into halftime. The Valkyries also shot an impressive 18-for-36 from beyond the arc.

Paige Bueckers was a bright spot for Dallas, finishing with 21 points on 9-for-10 shooting and six rebounds, but the Wings struggled to get enough production around her. Dallas went just 6-for-20 from three-point range and now face elimination in Game 2 at home on Wednesday. If the series goes to a deciding third game, it will return to Chase Center on Friday. The result also extended Golden State’s perfect 4-0 record against Dallas in their 2026 meetings.

Atlanta Dream Rally Past Washington Mystics in Game 1

The Atlanta Dream overcame a slow start to defeat the Washington Mystics 92-77 in Game 1 of their 2026 WNBA playoff series on Sunday. Washington led 41-36 at halftime after limiting Atlanta to below 29% shooting, but the game changed dramatically after the break. The Mystics committed seven third-quarter turnovers that turned into 14 Dream points, while Washington managed just 20% shooting in the period compared with Atlanta’s 62.5%.

Rhyne Howard sparked the turnaround with 12 of her 16 points in the third quarter, while Allisha Gray led all scorers with 17 points and Dewanna Bonner added 11, all during the decisive third period. Sonia Citron paced Washington with 15 points, while Shakira Austin recorded 10 points and 13 rebounds. The Dream’s victory puts the Mystics on the brink of elimination, with Game 2 set for Wednesday at CareFirst Arena.

Las Vegas Aces Take Control Against Indiana Fever in Game 1

The Las Vegas Aces opened their 2026 WNBA playoff series against the Indiana Fever with a 102-84 victory on Sunday. The teams stayed close through the opening quarter, but Las Vegas pulled away in the second to take a 62-47 halftime lead and maintained the advantage through the third. Indiana had a stronger fourth quarter but could not erase the deficit, leaving the Fever facing a must-win Game 2 at Gainbridge Fieldhouse on Tuesday, September 29, at 6:30 p.m. ET.

Caitlin Clark recorded her first career postseason double-double with 15 points and 10 assists, with her assist total also setting a new playoff career high. Kelsey Mitchell added 12 points, while Aliyah Boston grabbed four offensive rebounds. Indiana rookies Raven Johnson and Grace VanSlooten also scored their first career playoff points, finishing with 14 and two points, respectively.

Breanna Stewart Leads New York Liberty Past Top-Seeded Lynx in Game 1

Breanna Stewart delivered another standout postseason performance as the New York Liberty stunned the top-seeded Minnesota Lynx 91-75 in Game 1 of their best-of-three first-round series Sunday. The two-time WNBA MVP finished with 34 points and 12 rebounds, while also recording six assists, two steals and two blocks on 11-of-18 shooting. Stewart had most of her points against former college teammate Napheesa Collier, who fouled out alongside Minnesota center Natasha Howard. Nia Brodie led the Lynx with 16 points off the bench, while Kayla McBride added 14 despite a slow start from three-point range.

The result puts New York one win away from eliminating the team that finished the regular season with the league’s best record. The Liberty, who won their first WNBA championship in 2024 after beating Minnesota in a five-game Finals thriller, will host Game 2 on Tuesday with a chance to close out the series. Minnesota will need to respond at Barclays Center to force a decisive Game 3 back at Target Center on Thursday, adding another chapter to the growing playoff rivalry between the two teams.
