Sydney Thunder vs Sydney Sixers prediction

Heading into the final round of league stage fixtures, Sydney Thunder still have a chance of a playoff place despite sitting outside the top four. But with a negative net run rate, the Thunder not only need to win this game, they likely need to win it by a significant margin, although qualification will also depend on results around them, including the Melbourne derby earlier in the day.

Unfortunately for the Thunder, they're up against the standout side of this season's WBBL in crosstown rivals Sydney Sixers. Ashleigh Gardner's side have won eight of their nine matches this campaign and have been utterly relentless. The Sixers will need to be on guard against the likes of Phoebe Litchfield, who starred against Adelaide Strikers last time out with an incredible 121 not out, but you'd expect a team with their depth to handle whatever the opposition throws their way.

All bases are covered from the Sixers' point of view, while the Thunder are more reliant on the bigger names in their line-up, such as Litchfield. The sensible move is to back the league stage leaders to secure a double over the Thunder this season.
