Terrance McKinney apologizes after failing to make weight for UFC 334

TheTools · 13.11.2026 11:23 · source · permalink

All of the main-card fights for UFC 334 will proceed as planned. But there are a couple of hiccups with the preliminary bouts.

Terrance McKinney weighed in at 160.2 pounds, 4.2 pounds over the lightweight limit. His fight with Jim Miller will proceed, but McKinney will forfeit 30 per cent of his purse to his opponent.

McKinney, 32, issued an apology on social media for failing to make weight:

"I want to apologize to everyone and the UFC for missing weight. I’ve never missed weight in my life and I’m sorry but ship has sailed for 155. It’s official my last bout at lightweight. I’m trusting in God. Will make sure I’m ready for whatever comes at welterweight," McKinney's statement read.

Meanwhile, the fight between Dante Johnson and Cesar Almeida, will be moved from middleweight to catchweight. Both camps agreed on this Friday, with Almeida forfeiting 30 per cent of his purse to his opponent.

And Juan Diaz weighed in at 136.4 pounds. 0.4 pounds over the bantamweight limit. He is expected to forfeit a percentage of his purse to his opponent. No official update yet.

All of the fighters competing on the main card weighed in successfully.
