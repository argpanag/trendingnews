Disney and Formula 1 Reveal NEW Merch, Hot Wheels & More

TheTools · 20.11.2026 16:48 · source · permalink

Disney and Formula 1 are teaming up again for the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix, and this time there’s a whole lot more than Mickey Mouse making an appearance at the Bellagio fountains! Disney has revealed new merchandise, a collectible Hot Wheels car, a special experience at Disney Springs, and even a Marvel takeover in Las Vegas.

The latest announcements are part of Disney and Formula 1’s ongoing “Fueling the Magic” partnership, which brings Disney characters and racing together through merchandise, entertainment, and special events.

New Disney x Formula 1 Merchandise

Let’s start with the merchandise, because there’s quite a bit of it!

Disney and Formula 1 have teamed up with Culture Kings on a new collection of racing-inspired clothing featuring Mickey Mouse. The designs include a No. 28 racing jersey, oversized sweatshirts, and other streetwear pieces that combine Formula 1 graphics with familiar Mickey details.

There’s also a new Disney x Formula 1 Hot Wheels collector car from Mattel Creations. The miniature race car features a black, red, and yellow design inspired by Mickey Mouse, complete with racing graphics and Mickey details.

Some of the designs are pretty clever, too. One features tire tracks arranged to resemble Mickey Mouse ears, while others use racing flags, bold lettering, and Mickey’s signature colors.

Fans attending the Las Vegas Grand Prix can find the new Culture Kings collection at the retailer’s U.S. flagship store inside The Forum Shops at Caesars Palace.

Disney is also returning to the F1 Las Vegas Hub at The Venetian Resort Las Vegas with additional merchandise. Shoppers can browse Mickey & Friends apparel inspired by the 2026 racing season, along with F1 ACADEMY merchandise, Disney and Pixar’s Cars products, and Mattel Creations collectibles.

Disney Springs Guests Can Join the Fun Through November 22

Here’s something for Disney World visitors!

Through November 22, 2026, the UNIQLO store at Disney Springs is offering a special Disney x Formula 1 experience where guests can pose for a photo and create their own racing-inspired driver’s license.

The activity celebrates the recently launched Disney x Formula 1 UNIQLO UT collection, which includes graphic T-shirts featuring Mickey Mouse and Formula 1 designs.

The driver’s license experience is also available at UNIQLO’s Fifth Avenue flagship store in New York City.

If you’re heading to Disney Springs this weekend, it might be worth stopping by UNIQLO to check it out before the promotion ends.

Marvel’s Avengers: Doomsday Takes Over Las Vegas

Disney isn’t limiting the celebration to Mickey Mouse merchandise.

Marvel Studios is bringing Avengers: Doomsday to Las Vegas ahead of the film’s December 18 theatrical release. The movie will be featured on the Las Vegas Sphere as the first stop on a global promotional tour.

Fans attending race weekend can also look for a specially branded Archer Aviation air taxi and a Formula 1 show car displayed in the trackside Fan Zone.

And speaking of cars, Disney says a full-size Hot Wheels Formula 1 car featuring a special Disney design will be displayed in the East Harmon Fan Zone.

New F1 ACADEMY Documentary Streaming on Disney+

For anyone who would rather watch the racing action from home, The Pursuit: F1 ACADEMY is now streaming globally on Disney+.

The documentary follows the 2025 F1 ACADEMY season, taking viewers behind the scenes with drivers Doriane Pin, Maya Weug, Chloe Chambers, and Alisha Palmowski.

Filmed at race locations around the world, the series also features the people working behind the scenes, including F1 ACADEMY Managing Director Susie Wolff.

Between the new merchandise, special experiences, and entertainment announcements, Disney’s Formula 1 partnership is continuing to grow. And even if you aren’t headed to Las Vegas for the Grand Prix, there’s still a little racing fun to be found at Walt Disney World this weekend!
