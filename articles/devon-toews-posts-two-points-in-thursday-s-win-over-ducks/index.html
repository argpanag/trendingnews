Devon Toews posts two points in Thursday's win over Ducks

TheTools · 26.11.2026 03:46 · source · permalink

by Chris Schommer | Avalanche Correspondent | Wed, Nov 25th 2026, 10:46pm EST

Devon Toews posted two points for the Avalanche Thursday night, scoring one goal and picking up one assist in a 4-0 win over the Ducks.

Fantasy Impact:

Toews continues a solid stretch in the offensive zone, picking up points in four of his last six contests. The 32-year-old has offered head coach Jared Bednar another impactful offensive option from the Colorado blueline, notching 21 points (seven goals, 14 assists) through 23 games so far this season.
