Jonah Heim

Jonah Heim and his Athletics will square off against the Cleveland Guardians at Progressive Field, on Friday, Sept. 18 at 7:10 p.m. ET. Heim has +760 odds to hit a home run as of Friday afternoon.

What It Means

Heim is hitting for a .208 BA, .264 OBP and .374 SLG with an 18.5% strikeout rate and a 6.8% walk rate. His OPS is .638 and he has scored 32 runs. In 367 plate appearances, he has hit 13 home runs and driven in 42 runs. In his most recent game he had a hitless performance (0 for 4) against the Rays.
