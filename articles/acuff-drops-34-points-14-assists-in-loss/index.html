Acuff drops 34 points, 14 assists in loss

TheTools · 01.11.2026 02:05 · source · permalink

    Acuff drops 34 points, 14 assists in loss

    Darius Acuff

    SAC Guard #5
    Player Stats

    Darius Acuff Jr. led the Kings with 34 points (13-of-26 FGs), six rebounds, 14 assists, one steal, three three-pointers and six turnovers in a 132-120 loss to the Pelicans on Thursday.

    The trend of Acuff’s field goal percentage improving every game continued in this one, which resulted in the best game of his young career. Acuff became just the fourth teenager in NBA history to have at least 30 points and 10 assists in a game, and while it didn’t lead to a win, it was an impressive performance. He cooled off in the second half after entering the break with 21 points, but it still led to a fantastic outing for the rookie in just his fifth game. It may not be this gaudy or efficient every night, but Acuff should continue to have plenty of usage all season.
    - Noah Rubin
