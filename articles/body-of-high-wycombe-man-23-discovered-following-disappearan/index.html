Body of High Wycombe man, 23, discovered following disappearance in Portugal

TheTools · 10.09.2026 08:47 · source · permalink

The body of a 23-year-old man from High Wycombe has been found following a major search operation in Lisbon, Portugal.

Julian Amaral disappeared while on holiday in the Portuguese capital after attending a party boat event on the River Tagus.

He was last seen shortly before midnight on Wednesday, September 2, when the vessel returned to dock in the Alcântara area of the city.

Reports from Portuguese media said Mr Amaral had been attending the event with a cousin and told relatives he was heading to the toilet before the boat docked.

When he failed to return, concerns were raised and a search operation involving police, firefighters and maritime authorities was launched.

Portuguese Maritime Police confirmed on Sunday, September 6, that the body of a 23-year-old man who had been missing since earlier in the week was recovered from the Tagus River near Alcântara.

Authorities said the discovery was made following an alert at around 7.20pm local time.

The body was recovered by firefighters before being transferred to Lisbon's National Institute of Legal Medicine and Forensic Sciences.

Support is also being provided to Julian's relatives.

Following the discovery, Mr Amaral's sister, Jennifer, confirmed the tragic news to The Sun, saying: "They have found Julian. He is no longer with us."

During the search, Jennifer had spoken movingly about her brother in a social media appeal shared widely online.

She wrote: "If you know me, you know Julian has always been my baby."

Jennifer described him as "the brother I laugh with" and said he would make a two-hour journey to help care for his niece when she was unwell.

She added: "He's the brother that calls me for advice or to tell his secrets too."

In the emotional post, Jennifer said Julian was "so important to so many but especially to me" and thanked the public for their support during the search.

"The love and support have been overwhelming, and I will forever be grateful to every single one of you," she wrote.

The post concluded with a heartfelt message to her brother, saying: "I love you so much bro. I miss you so much."

The post remains on Jennifer's Instagram account, where her profile bio now carries the tribute #RIPJULIAN.

Portuguese officials have indicated that their strongest line of inquiry is that Mr Amaral may have fallen into the water, although investigations into the circumstances surrounding his death are ongoing.
