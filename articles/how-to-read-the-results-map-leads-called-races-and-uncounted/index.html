How to read the results map: Leads, called races and uncounted ballots

TheTools · 06.11.2026 22:56 · source · permalink

    During election nights, maps can distort perceptions of party success across states. Candidates might perform well in various smaller areas, but larger counties typically hold more electoral weight. Reputable news sources like AP and DDHQ will relay these results. Innovative representations, such as bubble maps, can provide clearer views of voting behaviors. As we approach the pivotal 2026 midterms, understanding these details is essential for voters.
