World's 2nd best airport maintains top service rating for five years - VnExpress International

TheTools · 12.09.2026 09:12 · source · permalink

The program evaluates airports on a five-level scale based on their customer management and service delivery.

According to ACI, Incheon Airport scored highly for its use of big data, efforts to address passenger inconveniences and adoption of smart technologies, including artificial intelligence, The Chosun Daily reported.

Among its initiatives is a passenger forecast system, which uses flight reservation data to predict congestion at different times and alert passengers in advance. The system has an error rate of about 1%.

The airport also operates a real-time arrival baggage information inquiry service, allowing passengers to check the location of their checked luggage using their mobile phones.

Passengers at Incheon International Airport in South Korea. Photo by Reuters

ACI, the world's largest association of airports, introduced its customer experience accreditation program in 2019. Unlike the Airport Service Quality (ASQ) program, which measures passenger satisfaction through surveys, the accreditation program assesses how airports manage and improve the customer experience, according to Seoul Economic Daily newspaper.

It evaluates eight areas, including customer understanding, customer experience strategy, operational improvements, service innovation and collaboration with other organizations.

The accolades come as passenger traffic at Incheon Airport continues to rise.

Ranked the world's second best airport after Singapore Changi in the 2026 Skytrax World Airport Awards, Incheon handled 38.39 million international passengers in the first half of this year, a 6.3% increase from the same period last year.

The airport will undergo a complete renovation of its aging Terminal 1 by 2035, with the airport operator planning to invest more than 1.77 trillion won (US$129 billion) in the project.
