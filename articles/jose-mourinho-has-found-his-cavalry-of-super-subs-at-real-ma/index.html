Jose Mourinho has found his cavalry of super subs at Real Madrid

TheTools · 21.11.2026 22:46 · source · permalink

Real Madrid beat Celta Vigo on Saturday night after goals from Jude Bellingham and Carlos Espi made sure that the team went back home with three points.


Overnight, it was a very tough game to watch as a Real Madrid fan because the team did not play all that well. They looked tired and physically compromised because, obviously, they were returning from the international break. However, they dug deep and got things done despite that, and a lot of it was down to the substitutions that Jose Mourinho made and the way those substitutions actually played on the pitch.

Two of those substitutions were players who were usually on the bench either way. Endrick and Carlos Espi came on off the bench and looked very good. Carlos Espi provided what he was there to provide the team with and was able to score the winner against Celta on the night.

Real Madrid have the perfect substitute for every situation


It wasn't just that Espi and Endrick are the only two substitutions that would make a difference off the bench either. It seems like, slowly but surely, Mourinho is building his cavalry for substitutions. You can look at players like Brahim Diaz along with Endrick and Espi in that conversation.

There's also the case of Eduardo Camavinga whenever he is on the bench, because he is usually considered a supersub. Trent Alexander-Arnold, as long as Fede Valverde is starting, is also going to be a substitute, even though he was not against Celta on the night.

These sorts of players on the bench will dramatically increase the quality of the team because, in the last few seasons, they have not had most of these profiles. Brahim was the only player of these four who was consistently reliable off the bench, and the other three were either starting or not at the club.

Even when you need some defensive solidity, Real Madrid have a couple of really good centre-backs in Eder Militao and Antonio Rudiger. Overall, it is a very good situation for the club to be in because they have ticked every box when it comes to profiles, apart from similar to Toni Kroos.

They have a great player off the bench in Espi who can come on and score goals, meet crosses and set pieces and win aerial battles. They also have, in Endrick, a energetic, powerful, and pacey Brazilian who can play as a striker as well as a winger.

Rodrygo coming back to this team, someone who is very technically gifted and can easily help Real Madrid when there is little to no space and they need a moment of magic.

Brahim is more of the same, but also one of the hardest-working players on the entire team. Camavinga provides that defensive solidity, and there are other defenders who will be there for the team whenever they are needed.

This is a very good situation for all of these players, and hopefully they are able to accept their roles and provide the maximum amount of output they possibly can whenever they are relied upon, which these players are absolutely doing. Mourinho has found his brilliant soldiers off the bench, and that is not something every Real Madrid manager can say.
