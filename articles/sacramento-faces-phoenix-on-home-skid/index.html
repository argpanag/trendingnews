Sacramento faces Phoenix on home skid

TheTools · 19.11.2026 03:06 · source · permalink

Sacramento will attempt to end its eight-game home losing streak when the Kings face Phoenix

Phoenix Suns (6-7, 11th in the Western Conference) vs. Sacramento Kings (1-12, 14th in the Western Conference)

Sacramento, California; Wednesday, 10 p.m. EST

BOTTOM LINE: Sacramento is looking to end its eight-game home losing streak with a win against Phoenix.

The Kings are 0-2 against the rest of their division. Sacramento has a 1-8 record against teams over .500.

The Suns have gone 4-7 against Western Conference opponents. Phoenix ranks sixth in the Western Conference shooting 36.6% from 3-point range.

The Kings average 12.4 made 3-pointers per game this season, 1.7 fewer makes per game than the Suns allow (14.1). The Kings average 113.0 points per game, 8.2 fewer points than the 121.2 the Kings allow.

TOP PERFORMERS: Domantas Sabonis is averaging 14.2 points, 13.2 rebounds and 5.6 assists for the Kings. Darius Acuff Jr. is averaging 26.4 points over the last 10 games.

Devin Booker is averaging 24.5 points and 5.4 assists for the Suns. Miles Bridges is averaging 15.0 points over the last 10 games.
