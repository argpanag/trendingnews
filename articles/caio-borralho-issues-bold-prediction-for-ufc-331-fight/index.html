Caio Borralho Issues Bold Prediction for UFC 331 Fight

TheTools · 20.09.2026 05:54 · source · permalink

Caio Borralho is backing his fellow Brazilian Mauricio Ruffy as he gears up to take on Arman Tsarukyan in what is possibly a No.1 contender matchup for the lightweight division.

UFC middleweight contender Caio Borralho is not mincing words ahead of the lightweight co-main event at UFC 331. As top-ranked contender Arman Tsarukyan prepares to lock horns with Brazilian striker Mauricio Ruffy, Borralho believes Tsarukyan is playing with fire if he decides to test his stand-up game. In fact, the Brazilian standout thinks even a brief exchange on the feet could end in a lights-out night for the Armenian star.

Speaking out on the upcoming clash involving his former training partner, Borralho warned that Ruffy possesses game-changing power capable of altering the division in a single second.

"I think Ruffy has the one power, one shot knockout power that he can turn off anyone in the division," Borralho said. "And I think if Arman wants to strike him, even if it's a little bit, he's going to get knocked out."

Playing with Fire on the Feet

It is a stern warning for Tsarukyan, who currently sits near the top of the 155-pound rankings. While Tsarukyan’s world-class wrestling and relentless top control remain his clearest path to victory, averaging 3.26 takedowns per fight, his confidence in his own striking has occasionally led him to engage on the feet against dangerous opposition.

Against Ruffy, however, those striking error margins shrink to zero. The Fighting Nerds representative holds a 14-2 professional record, with 13 of those victories coming by knockout. Known for his surgical timing, spinning kicks, and explosive hand speed, Ruffy has quickly established a reputation as one of the most lethal finishers in the weight class.

Whether Tsarukyan leans on his elite wrestling pedigree or gets baited into a firefight, Borralho expects fireworks when the Octagon door closes. But if Tsarukyan decides to test his hands, Borralho is convinced the title hopeful will be leaving the canvas on the receiving end of a viral highlight.

Allan Binoy is a combat sports journalist and Staff Writer at MMA Sucka.
