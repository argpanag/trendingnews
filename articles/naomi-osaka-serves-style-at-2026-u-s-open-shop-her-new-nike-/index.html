Naomi Osaka serves style at 2026 U.S. Open, shop her new Nike looks

TheTools · 03.09.2026 17:41 · source · permalink
  • Naomi Osaka paid tribute to NBA legend Allen Iverson with her outfit at the U.S. Open.
  • Fans can now shop pieces from Osaka's signature Nike collection.
  • Osaka is scheduled to play her second-round match on Thursday, Sept. 3.

Naomi Osaka is once again turning heads at the U.S. Open, this time with a basketball-inspired Nike look paying tribute to NBA legend Allen Iverson.

Osaka made a statement at her opening match Monday, stepping onto the court in an asymmetrical gray Who Decides War hoodie and white tulle skirt, a look inspired by Iverson's signature gray sweats.

She paired the look with braids, an athletic headband and mismatched black and red arm sleeves, before revealing an all-black Nike crop top and mesh tennis skirt. Close-up photos of the look revealed newspaper clippings woven throughout the hoodie.
