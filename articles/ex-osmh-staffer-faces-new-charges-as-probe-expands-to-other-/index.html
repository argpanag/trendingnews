Ex-OSMH staffer faces new charges as probe expands to other hospitals

TheTools · 26.09.2026 22:32 · source · permalink

A former Orillia Soldiers' Memorial Hospital (OSMH) staffer, charged following an investigation at the hospital earlier this year, is now facing new charges related to similar incidents at several other Ontario hospitals.

Nassim Eronn, a 34-year-old Burlington woman, was charged in May of 2026 following an investigation into alleged theft and fraud at the Orillia hospital.

Today, the Orillia OPP announced they have identified additional victims and laid further charges in connection with the ongoing fraud investigation.

The investigation involves incidents that are alleged to have occurred at the following healthcare facilities during the dates indicated:

"This investigation was prompted by several complaints of theft and fraud from patients and staff within the hospital," noted a media release from the Orillia OPP issued in May. 

Multiple search warrants have since been executed, resulting in significant amounts of property being seized, today's release noted.

As a result of the investigation, Eronn now faces a total of 65 Criminal Code offences:

The investigation remains ongoing and is being conducted by the Orillia OPP's major crime unit.

Police believe there may be additional victims who were patients, visitors and/or staff members in various units of these hospitals.

"Investigators are asking any individuals who attended these medical facilities during the above timeframes to check their financial records for any unauthorized transactions," notes the media release. 

Anyone wishing to report a fraudulent transaction or theft of cash is asked to contact the Orillia OPP at 1-888-310-1122 and reference incident number E250834926.

Anyone with information related to this investigation or other suspected unlawful activity can also contact Crime Stoppers anonymously at 1-800-222-TIPS (8477) or submit a tip online through the Crime Stoppers website.
