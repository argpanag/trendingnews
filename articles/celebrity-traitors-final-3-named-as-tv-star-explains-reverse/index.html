Celebrity Traitors 'final 3' named as TV star explains 'reverse editing' theory

TheTools · 16.10.2026 14:58 · source · permalink

Dan Walker has predicted who he thinks will be in the final three of The Celebrity Traitors using the theory that the show is edited in reverse.

The newsreader, 49,is very invested in Claudia Winkleman's BBC hit game show in which a star-studded line up of celebrities are currently trying to make it to the end of the ultimate game of deception, and Dan thinks that the final three will be made up of Faithfuls Michael Sheen, Romesh Ranganathan, and Joanne McNally, even though fans recently predicted she will be out next.

Claiming that his theory stems from the way bosses edit the programme, the former Strictly Come Dancing star said: "Someone told me last year that they edit Traitors in reverse order… so, they start with the final episode and then work back to the beginning.

"I don't know if that is true but, with that in mind - based on airtime, narrative so far and the fact that it's good to keep someone who is so sure but wide of the mark - my stab in the dark is that Michael, Romesh and Joanne are there at the end . I will delete this if I'm wrong."

The show is all pre-filmed before broadcast and only a small handful of people know who went on to be crowned the winner(s) of the current series. On screen, we've seen the Traitors - Maya, James A and Hannah - faced with a big decision, as they have to murder one of four stars - Leigh Anne, Romesh, Rob and Ross.

This year, it has been reported that BBC bosses are panicking after talk of the finalists has been making the rounds at high-profile parties.

"Because of the leak, who makes the final is now a hot topic at showbiz parties. It's also spread how certain players get on, not helped by the fact that another source has been suggesting who's been placed for interviews around the final," a source said.

"It's vital the leaks don't make it to gossip forums online and on social media, where fans can find the information. The BBC is hoping that anyone happening upon the info or hearing it from friends will help keep the secrets of the castle and stay quiet.

"The problem is a small group will be aware of The Celebrity Traitors' secrets, including those who work on the show, the celebrities who ran the course and possibly their close family and friends."

As speculation continues to grow about who the finalists are, fans have been talking about the series so far and think they've found a clue left by BBC bosses about who might make it all the way.

Last year, many fans had theories that the two 'Big Dogs ' - Jonathan Ross and Stephen Fry - would spearhead either side of the castle. Jonathan was a Traitor, until he was voted out, and Stephen was a Faithful.

Following that, the theory has continued to plague this year's celebrities too, with fans suggesting a similar thing has been done for Michael and his fellow Hollywood star Richard E Grant, who were named a Traitor and a Faithful respectively
