Lee Kang-in (25, Atletico Madrid) will start the league's leading FC Barcelona match.

Atletico will face Barcelona in the 12th round of the Spanish Primera Liga in 2026-27 at Riyadh Air Metropolitano in Madrid, Spain, at 5 a.m. on November 9 (Korean time).

Atletico is a 4-4-2 formation. Lee Kang-in will work with Julián Álvarez on the front line. Koke and Pablo Barrios will form the midfield, while Alex Baena and Giuliano Simeone will play as left and right side midfielders. Marc Puville and David Hantzko will work together as central defenders, while Alejandro Grimaldo and Marcos Llorente will start as left and right fullbacks. The goal is guarded by Jan Oblak.

Barcelona is a 4-3-3 formation. Hafinya will be at the forefront, while Anthony Gordon and Ramin Yamal will attack left and right sides. Marc Bernal, Fermin López and Pedri make up the midfield. Herard Martin and Pau Kubarsi will work together as central defenders, while João Cancelo and Brian Parignas will start as left and right fullbacks. The goal is defended by Joan Garcia.

Atletico has nine wins, one draw and one loss (28 points) in 11 league games this season. It ranks second among the 20 La Liga clubs.

Barcelona is in the lead with 10 wins and 1 loss (30 points) in 11 games. It is only two points behind Atletico.

[Lee Geunseung, MK Sports Reporter]
