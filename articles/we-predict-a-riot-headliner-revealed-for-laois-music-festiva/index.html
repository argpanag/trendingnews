'We predict a riot': Headliner revealed for Laois music festival - News - Laois Nationalist

TheTools · 25.11.2026 15:10 · source · permalink

The Greenfields Festival in 2026. Photo: Alf Harvey

A HUGE headliner has ben announced for a Laois music festival next year.

The legendary Kaiser Chiefs will be among the main acts at The Greenfields Festival in May 2027.

Best known for such hits as ‘Ruby’ and ‘I Predict a Riot’, the long-running Leeds band has released eight studio albums and won three Brit Awards.

The Greenfields Festival will return to the Ballykilcavan Farm and Brewery estate near Stradbally for the second year, over the May Bank Holiday weekend.

First established near Athy as a two-day camping music festival in 2022, the growing festival moved to the larger Ballykilcavan site last year.

Making the big announcement today, breathless organisers said on social media:

‘We predict a riot at The Greenfields Festival 2027!

‘We are absolutely buzzing to announce that the legendary Kaiser Chiefs will headline The Greenfields Festival.

‘Get ready for an unforgettable night of iconic indie anthems, massive singalongs and incredible festival energy!’ The 2026 festival featured 90 acts over five stages, with a line-up that ranged from the Vengaboys to Cry Before Dawn, The Riptide Movement, The Fureys, Aslan and David Geraghty of Bell X1.

Early Bird tickets for 2027 are on sale now at https://thegreenfieldsfestival.ie/
