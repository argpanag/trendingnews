Apple confirms iCloud outage as users report issues with Apple Pay, App Store and other services

TheTools · 17.11.2026 21:40 · source · permalink

Following a string of high-profile online service outages this past week, several Apple services appear to be experiencing issues today.

Apple experienced issues with its online services today, preventing some users from signing into iCloud and other Apple services. According to Downdetector, a site that tracks reports of online service outages, iCloud, Apple Support, Apple Pay, and the App Store all saw spikes in reports.

Apple's support page initially indicated issues with iCloud accounts and sign-ins, as well as other services. As of 3:52 p.m. ET, services appeared to be recovering, though the support page still showed some small issues.

According to Downdetector reports, these are the services that saw spikes in outage reports:

  • Apple Support

  • iCloud,

  • Apple Pay

  • App Store

  • Apple Business Manager

  • Apple Music

  • Apple Store

  • Sign In With Apple

  • Apple Card

"Many of our systems are back up and running after an internal update caused some outages this morning," an Apple spokesperson told us in a statement. "We're working to get the remaining systems online as soon as possible."

In addition to iCloud, Apple's support page also flags that AppleCare on Device, which lets you purchase AppleCare on your phone or tablet, is experiencing an outage. That's alongside Apple Business, Apple School Manager, iCloud Mail, iCloud Web Apps and Schoolwork

As of 1:10 p.m. ET, the page also listed the App Store and Apple Music as experiencing an outage. Around 1:41 p.m. ET, the affected services on Downdetector started showing a downward trend in reports, indicating they may be recovering.

Users online reported being signed out of services and unable to sign back in. Others reported being unable to activate new iPhones.

The issues follow a string of outages that have plagued online services over the past week. On Nov. 12, Microsoft Outlook and Teams were knocked out for more than 30 hours due to what Microsoft described as a technical issue. On Nov. 13, a technical issue caused all air traffic across the U.S. to be grounded, leading to thousands of canceled and delayed flights.

On Monday, online services at several major U.S. banks experienced problems for several hours after an issue with JPMorgan Chase's systems. Today, CNN reported that a highly classified communications network used by the National Security Agency (NSA) went down.
