Kenny Gainwell surpasses 100-yard mark in Week 8 - AOL

TheTools · 02.11.2026 00:38 · source · permalink

Tampa Bay Buccaneers RB Kenny Gainwell rushed for 140 yards on 25 carries against the Atlanta Falcons in Week 8.   

Advertisement

SOURCE: USA Today

Why It Matters: Gainwell saw a bigger role in the running game for the second straight week and was able to post his first 100-yaard game of the season. It is unlikely he will see that many carries in Week 9, but he has earned himself a solid role in the offense. He can be considered a No. 3 running back or flex option in leagues.
