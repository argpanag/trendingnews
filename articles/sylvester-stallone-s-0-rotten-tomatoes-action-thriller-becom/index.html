Sylvester Stallone's 0% Rotten Tomatoes Action Thriller Becomes a Surprise Streaming Hit

TheTools · 21.11.2026 15:00 · source · permalink

One of Sylvester Stallone’s latest movies may have a 0% Rotten Tomatoes score, but that isn’t stopping fans from watching it. 2025’s Alarum is a surprise streaming hit.

According to FlixPatrol, the action crime thriller is No. 9 on Starz in the U.S. as of Nov. 20. On Paramount+ in Austria, Germany, and Switzerland, Alarum shot up from the No. 10 spot to No. 1 within a day. The sudden success is unexpected, given that the movie did not do that well when it was released, but that’s the power of streaming and Stallone.

What is Alarum About?

Directed by Michael Polish and written by Alexander Vesha, Alarum was released in select theaters and video on demand on Jan. 17, 2025. The film, which comes from Lionsgate, follows two married spies who are caught in the crosshairs of an international intelligence network and will stop at nothing to obtain a critical asset.

Agents Joe Travers/Archibald (Scott Eastwood) and Lara Travers (Willa Fitzgerald) live off the grid. Their quiet retreat at a winter resort is blown to shreds when members of the old guard suspect the two may have joined an elite team of rogue spies, known as Alarum.

Alarum also stars Mike Colter, Ísis Valverde, Joel Cohen, D.W. Moffett, Mark Polish, Abigail Spear, Anton Narinsky, and La Monde Byrd. Despite some A-list stars and an intriguing plotline, the film has received negative reviews. It has a rare 0% score on Rotten Tomatoes, with just a 14% audience score.

For Radio Times, David Parkinson wrote that the movie is “not unwatchable, but all rather dispiriting.” Leslie Felperin of The Guardian, meanwhile, said, “A film so bad it shames American cinema itself. And it wasn’t even made in Hollywood!”

CBR’s own Brittany Frederick gave Alarum a 3/10. “There’s a good action movie in this, but it never emerges because everything is so convoluted,” Frederick wrote.

What is Sylvester Stallone Doing Next?

Meanwhile, Stallone is staying plenty busy. The actor can be seen in the fourth season of the Taylor Sheridan crime dramedy Tulsa King. Season 4 premiered on Oct. 16 and is set to run through Dec. 18. While Season 4 has only been airing for a month, Paramount+ has already renewed Tulsa King for Season 5, with filming kicking off in October.

On the day the Season 4 finale premieres, Tulsa King’s first spinoff, Frisco King, will have its debut. Starring Samuel L. Jackson, Frisco King was ordered to series in December 2025. Stallone will be reprising his role as Dwight Manfredi in the new series, which will consist of eight episodes and run through Jan. 29.

Aside from Tulsa King and Frisco King, Stallone has a handful of movies coming up. According to IMDb, he has seven films in the works, including the sequel to 2022’s Samaritan. Additional projects include Though as They Come, The Epiphany, Scavenger Hunt, and Never Too Old to Die. More details on the films should be announced in the coming months.

Alarum can be streamed on Starz, as well as Hulu, YouTube, and Prime Video with a Starz subscription.
