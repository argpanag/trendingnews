Dense fog tonight; 70s return for the end of the week

IMPACT TONIGHT FOR FOG:

Tonight, areas of dense fog will develop across the Susquehanna Valley a few hours before sunrise, as the sky clears. Watch for visibility dropping to 1/4 mile or less. Lows will fall to 46.

Allow extra time for the Thursday morning commute due to low visibility. By mid-morning, low clouds and fog will decrease. Thursday afternoon will feature sunny skies, and highs will bounce back to 70°.

After a dry cold front passes Friday morning, look for sunny and pleasant conditions in the afternoon. Highs will reach 70 degrees, which is almost 10 degrees above average for the end of October.

High pressure will build overhead Saturday. We'll see partly sunny skies and highs in the mid-60s. Trick-or-treaters are in for a treat, as temperatures hover in the mid-50s between 6 to 8 p.m.

We'll see a mix of clouds and sunshine Sunday, and highs will remain above average, near 68 degrees.

Early next week now looks dry. Look for partly sunny skies and highs in the upper 60s.

Clouds will gradually increase by the end of the week, and a cold front will bring a chance of showers Thursday into Friday.
