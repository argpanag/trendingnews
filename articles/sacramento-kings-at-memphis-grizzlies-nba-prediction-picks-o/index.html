Sacramento Kings at Memphis Grizzlies NBA Prediction, Picks & Odds for Oct. 31, 2026 - AOL

TheTools · 01.11.2026 02:04 · source · permalink

The Memphis Grizzlies will be on the court for the full four quarters on Saturday, but the oddsmakers think they’ll have things wrapped up well before then. They will square off against the Sacramento Kings at 8 p.m. ET at FedExForum. Sacramento is crawling into this matchup hobbled by six consecutive losses dating back to last season, while Memphis will bounce in with five straight wins.

Memphis is headed in fresh off its highest-scoring game of the season. Everything went the Grizzlies’ way against the Portland Trail Blazers on Thursday as they made off with a 124-103 victory. The offensive explosion also rewarded Memphis with its most commanding win of the season.

Cameron Boozer had a dynamite game, shooting 4-for-5 from long range and posting a triple-double with 18 points, 12 assists, and 12 rebounds. Jerami Grant didn’t exactly have the same impact after going 1-for-8 from the field.

Meanwhile, Sacramento came up short against the New Orleans Pelicans on Thursday and fell 132-120.

The loss doesn’t tell the whole story, though, as several players had good games. One of the most active was Darius Acuff Jr., who dropped a double-double with 34 points and 14 assists. Precious Achiuwa also nearly posted a double-double with 24 points and nine rebounds.

Even though they lost, Sacramento was picking pockets and finished the game with 11 steals. The Kings hadn’t recorded that many since March 3, when they also snagged 11 vs. Phoenix.

Both teams have notable injury concerns. Memphis’ leading scorer, Ty Jerome (back), is questionable, while Sacramento will be without Keegan Murray (ankle).

The Kings have now lost nine of their last 10 games on the road. That’s part of the reason they have an 0-5 record this season. As for the Grizzlies, the win improved their record to 5-0.

The 3-point arc could be a decisive factor in this one. Memphis has been dynamite from deep, shooting 39.9 percent from 3-point range. Meanwhile, Sacramento has struggled from long range, shooting just 30.5 percent.

Memphis beat Sacramento 123-112 in their most recent meeting on Sunday. One of the biggest obstacles the Grizzlies faced in that game was the Kings’ Zach LaVine, who scored 37 points and added five assists. Can Memphis overcome another big performance from LaVine in the rematch? Check Athlon Sports after the game to find out.
