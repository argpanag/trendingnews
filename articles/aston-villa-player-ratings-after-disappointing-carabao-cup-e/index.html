Aston Villa player ratings after disappointing Carabao Cup exit at Bournemouth

TheTools · 28.10.2026 23:09 · source · permalink

Villa were comfortably beaten in the fourth round of the League Cup

One of the potential routes back into European competition is now closed off thanks to an infuriatingly anaemic League Cup performance away at AFC Bournemouth.

Aston Villa manager Unai Emery made nine changes for this fourth round tie on the south coast but it was the heavily altered Bournemouth team who had the desire to go out and win the match.

Villa started both halves badly and were punished both times, creating next to nothing in response. They exit the League Cup having won their first game and deservedly lost their second 3-0.

None of the players who got their chance from the start at the Vitality Stadium made a case for themselves, which presumably would have been Emery’s demand of them.

Not a good night.

Here are our ratings:

Marco Bizot - 4

Dealt relatively well with the ball at his feet but might have come off his line to grab the cross from which Bournemouth scored in the seventh minute. Had to stay tall to tip over from Ben Gannon-Doak, as he did again 30 seconds into the second half.

Made some good stops having been given little protection at times but killed the game with a piss-poor mistake on 50 minutes.

Lamare Bogarde - 3

Opening goal originated on Villa’s right and Bogarde looked lost. Did much better on the cover in the middle of the first half to track the run of Julio Soler. The better Villa have got over the years, the more Bogarde’s failings with the ball stand out.

Taylor Harwood-Bellis - 4

Showed a good nose for danger but ultimately unable to impose himself defensively in the key moments. Calm and unhurried in possession, and increasingly keen to step forward with the ball. Didn’t connect properly with a header right in front of the Bournemouth goal in the middle of the second half.

Tyrone Mings - 4

Confident when passing through the lines – much more so than playing long – and solid in the air. Made an important block to deny Justin Kluivert, but looked vulnerable in one-on-one situations.

Ian Maatsen - 5

Generally positive in the first half and showed his pace both defensively and in attack. Played himself out of trouble a couple of times inside the first few minutes and into it after quarter of an hour.

João Gomes - 5

Limited but competent involvement in possession early on. Villa looked slightly improved when they were able to get Gomes on the ball, which they did from around the 25-minute mark, but it didn’t last.

Leon Goretzka - 5

Protected the ball nicely and got stuck in out of possession. Good contributions in the Bournemouth half at the base of Villa’s play. Headed over from a corner with the last touch of the first period. Substituted at 3-0.

Ibrahim Mbaye - 5

Never shy of putting in a shift. Willing to get back to support Bogarde on the right. Embarrassed Soler in the middle of the first half and the Bournemouth winger should have been booked for waving an imaginary yellow card. Ran 50 yards with a touch of good fortune to get a deflected shot away after Villa cleared a corner.

Substituted in the 58th minute.

Emi Buendía - 3

Got himself a needless yellow card within 12 minutes and was already playing on the edge. Misplaced a simple pass to spike a rare early attack, the first example of bad decisions in those areas but not the last. Dragged a shot wide just before half time. Substituted at 3-0.

George Hemmings - 4

Fired wide with Villa’s first shot of the match and, many minutes later, the second. Both poor finishes, out of keeping with an otherwise passable first half. Looked at his best with space to power into. Substituted 13 minutes into the second half.

Brian Madjo - 3

Villa’s performance did him no favours but some clever movement still got him a sniff of a chance in the first half. Never really had anything to go at.

Booked for diving by a referee who bothered himself more with being busy than getting anything right. Substituted with quarter of an hour to go.
