Minnesota (12/23/25) – The Minnesota Timberwolves defeated the New York Knicks, 115-104. Karl-Anthony Towns led New York with a season-high 40 points on 58.3-percent shooting, 13 rebounds, one assist and one steal. Tyler Kolek recorded a career-high 20 points, a career-high 11 rebounds, eight assists and three steals. Josh Hart finished with 12 points, 15 rebounds, eight assists and two steals. Anthony Edwards led Minnesota with 38 points on 55.6-percent shooting, four steals, two assists, one rebound and one block. Rudy Gobert collected 11 points on 66.7-percent shooting, 16 rebounds, three blocks, two assists and two steals. Julius Randle scored 25 points on 50.0-percent shooting, six rebounds, three assists and one steal.

Knicks center Karl-Anthony Towns was traded from Minnesota to New York in exchange for Julius Randle and Donte DiVincenzo… Knicks assistant coach Rick Brunson coached in Minnesota (2016-18)…Timberwolves assistant coach Pablo Prigioni played for the Knicks for three seasons (2012-15)…Timberwolves player development assistant James White played for the Knicks in 2012-13 … Knicks guard Jordan Clarkson was teammates with Timberwolves center Rudy Gobert (2019-22) on the Utah Jazz…Knicks head coach Mike Brown coached Trey Lyles on the Sacramento Kings (2022-24)…Knicks head coach Mike Brown was the lead assistant coach for the Golden State Warriors and coached Timberwolves forward Jonathan Kuminga and Knicks center James Wiseman during their 2022 NBA championship run.

The New York Knicks defeated the Toronto Raptors, 111-102. Jalen Brunson led 31 points, five assists, five rebounds and one block. Karl-Anthony Towns registered 16 points on 77.8-percent shooting, 16 rebounds, eight assists and one steal. Mikal Bridges added 17 points on 63.6-percent shooting, five rebounds and one assist. Ja’Kobe Walter led Toronto with 25 points on 60.0-percent shooting, two assists, one rebound and two steals. RJ Barrett contributed 22 points on 52.9-percent shooting, five rebounds, five assists and one block. Scottie Barnes produced 20 points on 50.0-percent shooting, four rebounds, two assists and two blocks.

KNICKS SPOIL OVO NIGHT; WIN 15TH STRAIGHT GAME AGAINST RAPTORS:

Karl-Anthony Towns recorded 16 points on 77.8-percent shooting, 16 rebounds, eight assists and one steal against Toronto (Nov. 17). This marks his 14th consecutive double-double to start the season, extending his own record for first in franchise history. Towns’ 40th game as a Knick with 15+ points and 15+ rebounds ties David Lee for ninth in franchise history. His 127th career 15+ point, 15+ rebound performance also ranks seventh amongst active players. Towns’ 14th straight double-double is the longest such streak to start a season in the NBA since 2020-21 (Jokic- 20 games, Sabonis- 16 games).

Jalen Brunson recorded 31 points, five rebounds, five assists and one block against Toronto (Nov. 17). He becomes the fifth fastest Knick to 2,000 assists in franchise history. Brunson’s 32nd game recording 30+ points, five or more assists and five or more rebounds ties Walt Frazier for the third-most such games in franchise history (T1- Guerin & Randle: 33).

Mikal Bridges recorded 17 points on 63.6-percent shooting, five rebounds and one assist against Toronto (Nov. 17). This marks his 11th game as a Knick scoring 15+ points on 60.0-percent shooting or better with five or more rebounds.

Andre Drummond recorded his 4,000th career offensive rebound against Toronto (Nov. 17). He becomes just the 10th player in NBA history with 4,000+ offensive rebounds.

The Knicks have beaten Toronto 15 consecutive times, tying the Knicks current longest win streak against a single opponent (Brooklyn Nets). The Knicks pulled down 40+ rebounds as a team for the 13th time this season, tying for second in the NBA. The Knicks dished out 25+ assists as a team for the 11th time this season, tying for second in the NBA.
