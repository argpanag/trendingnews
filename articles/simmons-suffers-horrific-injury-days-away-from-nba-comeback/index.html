Simmons suffers horrific injury days away from NBA comeback

  • Basketball

October 18, 2026 - 10.44pm

Australian basketball star Ben Simmons is feared to have suffered a torn Achilles, days away from his NBA comeback season with the Sacramento Kings.

ESPN’s Shams Charania is reporting the injury occurred at a team practice on Monday morning (AEDT).

Ben Simmons. AP Photo/Eric Gay

Simmons had starred through the pre-season and was set to play a big role for the Kings, after spending 12 months out of the sport.

ESPN is set to air a documentary about his comeback story on Monday evening.
