Jeremy Clarkson’s favorite Lamborghini is now a tractor, so perhaps it shouldn’t come as a surprise that he’s selling off a trio of cars that starred with him on The Grand Tour.

The TV presenter’s love of Italian cars is legendary, and the sale includes both an Alfa Romeo and a Lancia, with a Lincoln also in the mix.

The Alfa is a 1983 GTV6, which appeared in the 2019 episode “Well Aged Scotch,” in which he joined James May in a Lancia Gamma Coupe and Richard Hammond in a Fiat X1/9 on a road trip around the North Coast 500 route in Scotland. The GTV6 is a car that Clarkson had owned before and always regretted selling. He bought this GT for £10,000 (roughly $27,750 today) with just 20,000 miles on the clock from its 83-year-old original owner. While it didn’t escape its Scottish excursion completely unscathed, the GTV has been maintained since and is offered with 28,315 miles and a valid U.K. MOT certificate.

In 2021’s “Lochdown,” Clarkson bought a 1978 Lincoln Continental Mark V, and headed once again to Scotland due to Covid travel restrictions. Beside him was Hammond in a boattail Buick Riviera and May in a Cadillac Coupe de Ville. Despite sustaining a customary amount of abuse, and barely fitting on the Scottish streets, the Lincoln survived, and Clarkson kept it.

Clarkson’s 1982 Lancia Montecarlo Spider is even more special. It was the car he drove on the very last episode of The Grand Tour, “One For The Road,” in which the trio returned to Zimbabwe and Botswana for an emotional send-off. May drove a Triumph Stag and Hammond a Ford Capri, and they all completed their journey across the Makgadikgadi salt pans with the doors of their cars removed. They’ve since been put back on, and Clarkson’s car has been fettled by Surrey’s Max500. The Lancia “runs and drives well,” apparently.

As to why the cars are for sale, Clarkson confesses that he “rarely [gets] a chance these days to drive or enjoy these very special and quite famous cars, so it’s probably for the best if they end up with someone who can.”

All three of Clarkson’s cars are for sale without reserve at Iconic Auctioneers‘ event at the NEC Classic Motor Show on November 14. Bid for your chance to get hold of one of the best cars … in the world.
