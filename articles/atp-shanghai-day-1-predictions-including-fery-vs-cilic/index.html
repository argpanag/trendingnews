ATP Shanghai Day 1 Predictions Including Fery vs Cilic

TheTools · 06.10.2026 17:01 · source · permalink

Several of the younger names are stepping onto a major Masters 1000 stage to kick off the action at the Rolex ATP Shanghai Masters against opponents who have already experienced the pressure of big tournaments.

Arthur Fery gets perhaps the most eye-catching assignment against former Grand Slam champion Marin Cilic, while China’s Zhou Yi will try to make the most of another wild card opportunity. As always, we here at LWOT will be offering our predictions for every match on the schedule. But who will advance?

ATP Shanghai Day 1 Predictions

Arthur Gea vs Jaime Faria

Head-to-Head: First meeting

Two young players with plenty of upside meet in a match where neither has a previous encounter to fall back on. Arthur Gea has the explosive athleticism to make points uncomfortable, while Jaime Faria is capable of taking control with his serve and aggressive baseline play.

Faria’s first-strike ability should be particularly useful on a hard court, but Gea can make him work if he gets enough returns back into play. This could come down to which player handles the big moments better, and Faria’s ability to earn cheap points should help him stay ahead on the scoreboard. Gea should have his chances, but Faria gets the nod.

Prediction: Faria in 3

Mattia Bellucci vs Yi Zhou

Head-to-Head: First meeting

Yi Zhou gets another opportunity to make an impression in front of the ATP Shanghai crowd, but Mattia Bellucci represents a significant challenge. The Italian is a naturally aggressive left-hander who can create awkward angles and disrupt opponents with his combination of speed and shot-making.

Zhou will need to embrace the occasion rather than allowing Bellucci to dictate from the outset. His familiarity with the conditions and home support could help keep him competitive, but Bellucci’s ability to change direction and attack from both wings should eventually create enough openings. If the Italian keeps his unforced errors under control, he has the greater weapons.

Prediction: Bellucci in 2

Alex Molcan vs Federico Cina

Head-to-Head: First meeting

This is an appealing clash between experience and a player who is still discovering just how high his ceiling might be. Alex Molcan has spent years competing at tour level and knows how to construct points patiently, while Federico Cina represents the younger generation eager to establish itself.

Cina’s ball-striking can trouble Molcan if he finds his range early, particularly if he is able to take the ball on the rise and prevent the Slovak from settling into long patterns. Molcan, though, has the experience to make Cina earn every point and should be comfortable dragging the match into a physical battle. I expect the younger Italian to have his moments, but Molcan’s experience gives him the narrow edge at ATP Shanghai.

Prediction: Molcan in 3

Arthur Fery vs Marin Cilic

Head-to-Head: First meeting

Arthur Fery could hardly ask for a more interesting Masters 1000 opportunity at ATP Shanghai. He faces Marin Cilic, a former US Open champion and one of the most experienced players in the draw. This will also be their first meeting, adding another layer of uncertainty to a matchup that pits Fery’s rising game against Cilic’s proven big-match experience.

Cilic’s serve and flat ball-striking remain dangerous on hard courts, but Fery has already shown this season that he can compete at Masters 1000 level, earning his first main-draw win in Cincinnati. His ability to redirect pace and stay aggressive could make this closer than the experience gap suggests. Still, Cilic’s serve should give him enough free points to control the key moments.

Prediction: Cilic in 3
