WTA Guadalajara Predictions Including Bucsa vs Jovic

TheTools · 18.09.2026 05:39 · source · permalink

This Friday at WTA Guadalajara will feature the two semifinals, with a very exciting lineup led by the fast rising Iva Jovic. Both semifinals should be very high quality and entertaining; even if Jovic is the title favorite, the other three players definitely have enough ability to aim for the title as well and it should be a very intriguing end to this tournament. But who will advane to the WTA Guadalajara final?

WTA Guadalajara Semifinal Predictions

Liudmila Samsonova vs Peyton Stearns

Head-to-head: first meeting

There is very little to separate the two players in terms of ranking, general level of play and even when it comes to their level so far this week. Stearns hasn’t lost a set all week, but Samsonova managed to topple the #1 seed Marta Kostyuk in a very long three set battle. It remains to be seen whether that victory will spur her on further or whether she will be feeling the effects of it physically and mentally.

In any case, this should be a very close match, which could very well go the distance and is likely to be decided by small details, namely by who manages to hold their nerve and execute better on the big points.

Prediction: Stearns in 3

Cristina Bucsa vs Iva Jovic

Head-to-head: first meeting

On paper, these are the two strongest players left at WTA Guadalajara and both have made easy work of their draw so far, not dropping a single set combined. That said, while Bucsa certainly has a chance if she can play her best tennis, Jovic is one of the fastest rising young players on the WTA Tour, looks headed for the top 10 and Slam contention soon and should have the edge here all things considered. It could be a close match, but Jovic should be able to prevail in the end.

Prediction: Jovic in 3
