WWE's Charlotte Flair shares her reaction to 'via cash-in' meme going viral after Tiffany Stratton's title win

TheTools · 19.11.2026 11:28 · source · permalink

Flair's 2025 needling of Stratton, sparking hundreds of posts on X after the latter won the title at Crown Jewel

WWE's Charlotte Flair on 'via cash-in' meme going viral

WWE star Charlotte Flair talks to OutKick about her response to Tiffany Stratton's Crown Jewel win going viral.

Charlotte Flair decided to have some fun after Tiffany Stratton cashed in her Money in the Bank contract at Crown Jewel and defeated Chelsea Green to win the WWE Women’s Championship.

Flair, who was not at the Saudi Arabia event, saw that Stratton had won the title for the second time and the second time using the Money in the Bank opportunity to her advantage. She decided to go to social media and call back to her feud with Stratton before WrestleMania 41 when she needled the champ for winning the title "via cash-in."

Hilarious results ensued and an internet meme was born.

"You mean, expecting her to cash in or the picture of my face? No," she told OutKick Dropkick when asked whether she expected the immense internet reaction. "I wanted to see what was happening at Crown Jewel online because I wasn’t on the PLE. But I was like, ‘Oh man, she cashed in. You know what would be kinda funny? I don’t know if people would get it, but when I told her you won via cash-in.’ And I guess people thought it was funny."

Hundreds of posts on X came through with Flair reposting some of them to her own timeline.

She said she wasn’t exactly prepared for how her post was received on social media.

"I was not prepared … I’m like, some people might get it, some people might not. ‘Why is she posting a Throwback Thursday photo when it’s Saturday?’"

Flair won the 2025 Royal Rumble, becoming the first woman to win the match twice. She challenged Stratton for the title, culminating in a bitter feud and a match at WrestleMania 41. Flair failed to win the championship in Las Vegas that year.

However, she has regrouped and put herself in a great position to potentially win it for a third time in 2027 when the Royal Rumble takes place at State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Arizona, in February.

She talked to OutKick Dropkick about the "pressure" she felt to win for a third time.
