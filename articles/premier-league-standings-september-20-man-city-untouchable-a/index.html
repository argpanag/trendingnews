Premier League Standings: Premier League Matchday 5 delivered late drama and statement wins across England, with Manchester City’s ruthless efficiency setting the standard at the summit while Manchester United continue to stutter. Leeds United provided the shock of the weekend with a rampant home win, and Liverpool kept their unbeaten start alive on the road.

By full time, the EPL table had reshuffled meaningfully. City sit imperious at the summit of the Premier League standings, Arsenal and Brighton chase hard, and the Old Trafford outfit remain stuck in mid-table limbo as September draws to a close.

Manchester United Held To A Draw At Craven Cottage

Manchester United were made to settle for a share of the spoils at Fulham, with Rodrigo Muniz’s 58th-minute own goal handing the visitors an early lead before Emile Smith Rowe restored parity for the Cottagers in the 73rd minute.

The 1-1 stalemate leaves United winless in three of their last four league outings, with Ruben Amorim’s side now sitting mid-table on 5 points from 5 games.

Manchester City Continue Perfect Start Against Sunderland

Manchester City extended their flawless run to five wins from five, brushing aside Sunderland 3-0 at the Etihad. Ruben Dias got the breakthrough, while Rayan Cherki was the standout performer, contributing two assists and a match-high rating in a dominant display that keeps Pep Guardiola’s side firmly on top of the pile with 15 points.

Liverpool Stay Unbeaten With Win At Bournemouth

Liverpool FC made it four league games without defeat, edging out Bournemouth at the Vitality Stadium to move up to sixth. The result stretched their record to two wins and three draws from five outings, with seven goals scored and four conceded so far, enough to keep Arne Slot’s men firmly inside the European qualification picture heading into the international break.
