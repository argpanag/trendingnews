Budget 2027: Why does Simon Harris have no college degree?

TheTools · 30.09.2026 13:35 · source · permalink

Simon Harris is preparing to deliver his first Budget as Minister for Finance, but the man now in charge of the State’s purse strings never completed his college degree.

The Tanaiste, 39, studied journalism and French at the Dublin Institute of Technology, now TU Dublin, but left before graduating to pursue a career in politics.

He has never hidden it. Speaking to Hot Press while he was Minister for Further and Higher Education, he explained that the degree was a four-year course, and that roughly three years in, a chance came up to get more involved in politics, so he deferred.

‘Life came at me a lot faster than I expected it to,’ he said.

Harris was involved in politics from a very young age, joining Young Fine Gael at 16. His first job was as a parliamentary assistant to Frances Fitzgerald – then opposition leader in the Seanad, who is regarded as an early mentor.

The rise that followed was rapid. He was elected to the Dail in 2011, became a junior minister in 2014 and was made Minister for Health two years later.

The lack of a degree drew most attention when he became Ireland’s first ever Minister for Further and Higher Education, Research, Innovation and Science in 2020.

At the time, critics were quick to point out that the Minister for Higher Education had left college before finishing his own degree.

Harris has since described his career path, tongue in cheek, as an apprenticeship in politics. In the same period, he argued that Ireland obsesses too much over CAO points for college entry.

Obviously, none of this has slowed him down. He served as Taoiseach from April 2024 to January 2025, and has been Tanaiste since then and Minister for Finance since November 18, 2025.

The Irish Independent has reported that Ireland is now the only country in Europe whose finance minister has no third-level qualification.

He is the first Tanaiste to also hold the Finance portfolio since Brian Cowen in 2008, and his first Budget in the role is due next week.
