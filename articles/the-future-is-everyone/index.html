The Future Is Everyone

TheTools · 18.11.2026 05:00 · source · permalink

When more women advocate for the challenges facing boys and men, real change becomes possible. Let’s start with International Men’s Day.

Last year, Evie became the first major women’s magazine to mark International Men’s Day. We are doing it again this year for a simple reason: the problems facing the boys and men that we love have not gone away, and neither has the need for women to say so.
