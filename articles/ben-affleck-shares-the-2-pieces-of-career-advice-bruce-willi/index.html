Not all advice from a friend is good advice.

Ben Affleck recently opened up to Josh Horowitz on the Happy Sad Confused podcast about the less-than-helpful advice his Armageddon costar Bruce Willis gave him while working on the sci-fi film before he began Shakespeare in Love.

"He was like, 'Man, don't go do that Shakespeare movie, man. You don't wanna do that movie,'" Affleck, 54, remembered.

Despite Willis being one of the biggest movie stars at the time, the Good Will Hunting actor noted that he "was wrong about that one."

Bruce Willis and Ben Affleck in 2000
Credit: Sonia Moskowitz/Getty

"But he hadn't read the script, in all fairness," Affleck added.

The 1998 romantic comedy, which also starred Joseph Fiennes, Gwyneth Paltrow, Judi Dench, Geoffrey Rush and Colin Firth, went on to win seven Academy Awards in 1999, including for Best Picture. Affleck played Ned Alleyn, an actor who frequently starred in Shakespeare's plays at the Globe Theatre.

Affleck noted that Willis' career advice wasn't limited to whether or not he should take certain projects.

"Bruce Willis once turned to me and was like, 'Your voice is too high,'" Affleck shared on the podcast. "I was like, 'What?' He was like, 'Don't have that high voice.' I was like, 'Bruce, that's just my voice. Can I change that?' "

Ben Affleck in 2026
Credit: Stefanie Keenan/Getty

According to Affleck, the Sixth Sense actor just reiterated, "You don't wanna sound like that, man! I'm helping you out!"

Still, Affleck affirmed that Willis is "the sweetest guy in the world," and that his advice was coming from a good place.

"I love Bruce, and he really was giving me a tip," Affleck shared.
