Stoke City team news vs Portsmouth as key man returns

TheTools · 05.11.2026 10:01 · source · permalink

Stoke City welcome back key man Ethan Galbraith from suspension as they make the long trip to Portsmouth for a lunchtime kick-off.

Summer signing Galbraith had not missed a minute for Stoke in the Championship before he served a one-match suspension in mid-week for picking up five bookings.

Mark Robins is hoping to welcome back players from injury soon but warns it isn't always straightforward to manage the returns of players who have been out for a while.

Sam Gallagher and Aaron Cresswell are nearing the end of the rehabilitation programmes while Eric Bocat is hoping to be available soon after the international break which follows this match. The prognosis on Lamine Cisse is a little longer and Ato Ampah is out for the foreseeable.

Robins said in his pre-match press conference: "It's the same as previously. We've got players who are starting to emerge from the treatment room but it takes time.

"Those players that have picked up nasty injuries are recovering, they're in the midst of their recoveries, and hopefully won't be too long after the international break has ended and the season has resumed."

Stoke won 2-1 at Blackburn Rovers without Galbraith. Million Manhoef came into a central role in his first start since early September and Robins will have to weigh up how best to line up at Fratton Park - aiming to equal a club record fifth away win in a row.

The manager said: "Million came in and we all know Million's qualities. Like I said at the time, he was really good. He had really good spells and he made the last goal after receiving the pass from Justin Devenny, whose performances are going under the radar a little bit. Million showed really good energy and the quality he's got.

"It was a strong performance without being a scintillating performance but you've got to be happy because they're trying to stop you. To overcome that game and take a two-goal lead - the first goal was absolutely fantastic in terms of vision and execution but to get the second at that moment was really important.

"Obviously on the back of that we made some changes and conceded two goals, one fortunately was disallowed, was a disappointment.

"We've got some really good players and they are prepared to fight and scrap and a little bit more quality at the other end where we look a threat."
