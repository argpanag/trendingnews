Nintendo is touring a Zelda 40th Anniversary Concert: First dates and venues confirmed | VGC

TheTools · 08.09.2026 16:21 · source · permalink

The tour will take in Japan, North America and Australia, with four UK dates so far

Nintendo has announced that it will be touring an orchestral concert celebrating the music of The Legend of Zelda series.

The Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary Concert will “include a variety of nostalgic tunes and modern melodies from The Legend of Zelda series”, according to Nintendo.

The tour will start in Tokyo on January 23, before splitting into three separate continental tours.

This includes a North American tour which starts in Chicago on February 22, a European tour starting on April 3 in London, and an Australian tour starting on May 13 in Melbourne.

Ticket sales will start this month, with exact sale dates depending on the venue. Nintendo says more cities and countries will be announced in the future.

So far, four UK dates have been confirmed – two in London, one in Glasgow and one in Edinburgh.
