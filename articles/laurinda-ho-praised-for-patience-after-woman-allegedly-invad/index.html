Laurinda Ho praised for patience after woman allegedly invades space

TheTools · 20.11.2026 09:00 · source · permalink
    bookmark

Casino heiress Laurinda Ho, daughter of late Macau gambling tycoon Stanley Ho, has won praise from netizens for keeping her cool after a woman allegedly invaded her personal space.

The 35-year-old was attending a fashion show in Shanghai on Nov 16 when a woman seated behind her approached and asked to take a photo with her.

However, the woman did not appear to have her phone ready, and with the show about to begin, Laurinda Ho initially did not respond to the request.

According to HK01, the woman then proceeded to film Ho from extremely close range.

Despite the awkward encounter, Ho eventually obliged and posed for a photograph with her.

Footage of the incident has since circulated on social media.

Many netizens criticised the woman for failing to respect Ho’s personal space, while others praised the heiress for remaining composed throughout the encounter.

“It’s so disrespectful and unpleasant to shove your phone so close to someone’s face,” one user commented.

Another wrote: “Laurinda has such a good temper. Her upbringing deserves more praise.”

This is not the first time a member of the Ho family has attracted attention for their down-to-earth behaviour.

Earlier this month, Ho’s younger brother Mario, 31, won praise for his humility after he was spotted handling his own luggage while travelling alone on a high-speed train.

Many netizens commended him for travelling without bodyguards and avoiding unnecessary attention despite his family’s prominence.

Others also expressed surprise at him opting for public transport instead of private transportation.
