Coronation Street's Kit Green faces a major shock next week when he runs into Bethany Platt as she's on her way out of an ultrasound scan.

Bethany does her best to evade Kit and her mum Sarah, but she gets caught and Kit learns that Bethany's pregnant.

The scene airs in Wednesday's episode (11 November).

"Is everything alright?" Kit asks Bethany. She tries her best to hide the real reason for her hospital visit, only for Sarah to press her with questions about the scan.

"Scan? Are you ill?" Kit asks.

"Oh no, it's not that kind of scan," Sarah replies. "Bethany didn't want to say anything until she knew everything was alright. But now that everything is alright, I hope you're going to be fine with the fact you're going to be dating a grandma."

Sarah and Bethany share a hug, while Bethany looks up to Kit with a look of shame and fear in her eyes.
