Aoibhlinn, beloved wife of Fionnuala and devoted mother of Naoise and Fionn, passed away peacefully in Blackrock Hospice, surrounded by her loving family.

 

Predeceased by her mother Avril, deeply missed by her wife, sons, father Aidan, brother Kevin, sister Sinéad, sister-in-law Sinéad, brother-in-law Chris, father-in-law John, mother-in-law Anne, brothers-in-law John-Hugh and Fergus, sister-in-law Róisín, nieces and nephews, colleagues, neighbours and friends.

 

 

“Ní bheidh a leithéid arís ann.”

 

 

Evie lived life to the full and requested that those attending her funeral wear a splash of colour in her memory.

 

 

Family flowers only, please. Donations, if desired, to Blackrock Hospice.

 

 

 

Reposing in Fanagans Funeral Home, Dundrum on Friday afternoon (18th September) from 6pm to 8pm.

 

 

Complimentary car parking is available at the old Dundrum Shopping Centre (Lidl) car park - entrance on Dundrum Village Main Street (not Dundrum Town Centre Car Park). Tickets to exit car park, free of charge, available on request at Fanagans Funeral Home, Dundrum.

 

 

Funeral Mass on Saturday morning (19th September) at 10am in The Church of St. John The Evangelist, Ballinteer, Dublin 16, followed by cremation in Mount Jerome.
