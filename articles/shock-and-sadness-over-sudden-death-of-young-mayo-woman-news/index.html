Shock and sadness over sudden death of young Mayo woman - news - Western People

TheTools · 14.10.2026 09:04 · source · permalink

There has been immense sadness in the community following the death of Danielle Mulcahy, Rathbawn Grove, Castlebar.

She died suddenly and unexpectedly at Mayo University Hospital, Castlebar on Sunday, October 11.

Danielle, aged 27, worked at Café Rua in Castlebar and was a very popular and much-loved member of the team. Condolences online praised her warmth and kindness and described her as ‘the brightest soul in the room’.

She was the dearly loved daughter of Kathleen and Daniel and much loved sister of Nadian, Emma and J.P.

She will be sadly missed by her heartbroken parents, sisters, brother, grandparents Ethna and P.J. O’Gara (Ballindine), aunts, Anne and Joanne and uncles Darren, Carlos, Barry and Patrick, her niece Maleeah and nephews A.J., Leo, Jayden and Mylo and all her family, friends and her boyfriend Wello and her work colleagues in Café Rua.

Danielle will repose at Kilcoyne’s Funeral Home, Thomas Street, Castlebar on Wednesday 14th October from 6.30pm to 8.30pm. The Funeral will arrive at the Church of the Holy Rosary, Castlebar on Thursday 15th for Funeral Mass at 2.30pm.

Afterwards Danielle will be laid to rest in Castlebar New Cemetery.

May she rest in peace.
