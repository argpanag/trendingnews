'All clear' issued after active threat reported at Miami University in Ohio

TheTools · 15.10.2026 19:03 · source · permalink

An initial alert from Miami University in Oxford, Ohio said an active threat was reported near McGuffey Hall.

School officials at Miami University in Oxford, Ohio announced an "all-clear" Thursday after a series of emergency alerts sent out reporting an active threat on campus.

The alerts unfolded just after 1 p.m. CT, with school officials saying an "active threat" had been reported on campus, warning people to "be prepared to face a life-threatening situation."
