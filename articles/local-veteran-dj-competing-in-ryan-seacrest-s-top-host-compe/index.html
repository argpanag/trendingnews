Local veteran & DJ competing in Ryan Seacrest’s ‘Top Host’ competition

TheTools · 07.10.2026 19:46 · source · permalink

MERCER COUNTY, W.Va. (WVVA) - Local veteran and broadcasting voice in the Two Virginias, Mike ‘Doc’ Atwell is competition for the title of ‘Top Host.’

This contest in partnership with iHeartRadio, RODE & On Air with Ryan Searest.

The winner receives a podcast produced by iHeartRadio, $25K and the opportunity to be a featured guest on On Air with Ryan Seacrest.

“As a Navy Corpsman Combat Veteran, I want to use this platform to connect veterans nationwide. To get Veterans to talk, not commit suicide,” said Atwell on TopHost.org.

As of this writing, Atwell is 2nd in his group.

Click here to cast your one free daily vote.

Atwell is a Concord University graduate, frequent emcee of the annual Veterans Ball, PA Announcer for the Blue Ridge Bobcats and is the owner and operator of NFA Entertainment Production alongside his wife, Erica.

Programming note: Tune into WVVA @ Noon with Joshua Bolden on October 9, 2026 where Atwell discusses more about his career and the competition.
