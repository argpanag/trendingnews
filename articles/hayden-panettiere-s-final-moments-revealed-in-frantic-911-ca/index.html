Hayden Panettiere’s final moments revealed in frantic 911 call from boyfriend’s brother

TheTools · 16.10.2026 20:42 · source · permalink

The 'Heroes' and 'Nashville' actress was found unresponsive at a Greenville, South Carolina, residence on Aug 16

Hayden Panettiere's cause of death released by coroner

The Greenville County Coroner released the cause of death for 36-year-old actress Hayden Panettiere, ruling it a tragic accident from the toxic effects of fentanyl and other substances. Fox News chief correspondent Jonathan Hunt reports from Los Angeles on her sudden death in South Carolina.

Hayden Panettiere’s final moments were captured in a frantic 911 call as the brother of her on-and-off boyfriend desperately tried to revive the actress after finding her unconscious at a South Carolina apartment Aug. 16.

"​​Oh, God, oh, my God — you do like CPR or something?" Zach Hickerson asked the dispatcher in the newly released audio obtained by ABC News and CBS News. 

The seven-minute call reveals Hickerson performing CPR on Panettiere while Brian Hickerson administered Narcan.

Panettiere, 36, was found unresponsive at the Greenville residence    Aug. 16. Zach told the 911 operator that he had arrived at the apartment after returning from church and discovered his brother and Panettiere inside.

Panettiere died from the toxic effects of fentanyl, 4-ANPP, alprazolam, methocarbamol and quetiapine, according to the Greenville County Coroner's Office. The manner of death was ruled an accident.

"On August 16, 2026, at approximately 1:51 PM, a 911 call was received regarding a reported cardiac arrest at 701 Easley Bridge Road in Greenville," the Greenville County Coroner's Office said in a release obtained by Fox News Digital. 

"First responders and Emergency Medical Services arrived on scene and located a female in cardiac arrest. EMS personnel initiated advanced cardiac life support measures; however, resuscitation efforts were unsuccessful, and the individual was pronounced deceased at 2:32 PM.

"The Greenville County Coroner’s Office responded to the scene and initiated a parallel investigation with the Greenville City Police Department. The decedent has been identified as Hayden Leslie Panettiere, 36, of California. Panettiere was found unresponsive inside the residence, where she had been temporarily staying.
