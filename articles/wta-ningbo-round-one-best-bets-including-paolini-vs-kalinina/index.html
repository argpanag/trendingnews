WTA Ningbo Round One Best Bets Including Paolini vs Kalinina

TheTools · 19.10.2026 22:12 · source · permalink

The WTA Ningbo Open continues the women’s tennis tour’s fall run in China. The WTA Tour went to Beijing at the start of October. It then moved to Wuhan in the middle of October. Ningbo carries into late October, giving the WTA a tournament in China for a fourth straight week. This is a 500-point tournament following the 1,000-point events in Beijing and Wuhan. Players who did really well in Beijing and Wuhan are unlikely to stay in China for a smaller-point tournament. This event is tailor-made for the players who lost early in Beijing and Wuhan and are still in China. Those who stay around for Ningbo want to say their October journey in China was worth something. They get a third chance to post a good result in China with the tennis season winding toward its conclusion. Losing early in a tournament is not what any player wants, but the benefit of doing so is that an athlete will be rested before the next tournament. You will want to pay attention to the players who lost early in Wuhan over the past 10 days. Those players will be hungry for success in this WTA Ningbo tournament. LWOS has our best bets for three matches. Post your best bets and thoughts in the comments.

WTA Ningbo

Putintseva – Shnaider: 1:00 EST

H2H: 2-1

This WTA Ningbo match features two players who met in the previous tournament. Diana Shnaider beat Yulia Putintseva in the WTA Wuhan tournament, which immediately preceded Ningbo. Shnaider and Putintseva decided to stay in China, and now they have been slated to meet early in yet another WTA tournament. Shnaider’s win was 6-2, 6-2, so she covered a 7.5-game spread. Even at 5.5 games, Shnaider covering would have brought in a good price. Let’s see what happens in this rematch.

Best Bet to Make

The WTA Ningbo betting angle here is to stick with Shnaider. She won by eight games in Wuhan. She can win by only four games in Ningbo and still cover. That seems like a decent bet to make. Tapping the sign here and highlighting the fact that Shnaider literally just obliterated this same opponent in her last tournament. 

That being said, betting on Shnaider -3.5 games is a value bet for sure.

Value bet/ the best odds: Shnaider -3.5 games @1.61 @Betfair

Paolini – Kalinina: 2:30 EST

H2H: 1-0

This WTA Ningbo match features two struggling players. Jasmine Paolini is 2-3 in her last five matches. Anhelina Kalinina is 1-4 in her last five matches. Those backing Kalinina will point to Paolini getting crushed by Sonay Kartal, 6-2, 6-0, in her most recent match in Wuhan. Those backing Paolini will point out that Kalinina is 5-8 on hardcourt this year and is not a good player on the surface.

Best Bet to Make

This WTA Ningbo match should lean to Paolini. She ran into a hot player, Kartal, in Wuhan. Kalinina is the opposite of hot, given her current form and her 2026 hardcourt record. Paolini against the spread is the right standalone wager to make here. 

That being said, betting on Paolini -2.5 games is a value bet for sure.

Value bet/ the best odds: Paolini -2.5 games @1.66 @Betfair

Bucsa – Kudermetova: 2:30 EST

H2H: 0-1

This WTA Ningbo match figures to be close. Polina Kudermetova beat Cristina Bucsa in three sets in Washington this past summer. Now the two meet again in China. Bucsa has lost five matches in a row. Kudermetova is 3-2 in her last five, including two wins in the Ningbo qualifying rounds to get into the main draw.

Best Bet to Make

This WTA Ningbo match is a close one. The fact that the previous meeting in Washington a few months ago went three sets is a decent reason to pick over 2.5 sets here. You wouldn’t trust Bucsa to win since she has lost five in a row, but Kudermetova is not an elite player and shouldn’t be trusted either. Over 2.5 sets is the compromise.

That being said, betting on over 2.5 sets is a value bet for sure.

Value bet/ the best odds: over 2.5 sets @2.41 @betsson
