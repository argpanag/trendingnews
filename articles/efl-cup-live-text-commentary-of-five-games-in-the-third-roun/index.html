    19:38 BST

    West Ham v Fulham (19:45 BST)

    Fulham

    Alvaro Arbeloa is wary of the threat posed by Fulham’s Carabao Cup opponents West Ham after they hammered Wrexham 6-0 to move top of the Championship.

    The Hammers have picked themselves up quickly from relegation in May to make a commanding start to life in the second tier as they seek an immediate return.

    “An away game in another difficult stadium, against a team that are performing so well,” he said. “They won their last game 6-0, they beat Southampton in the Carabao Cup also, so we know them."

    Liverpool v Tottenham (20:00 BST)

    Richarlison was not included in the Tottenham squad for tonight's game and earlier he publicly expressed his displeasure at Roberto de Zerbi's decision to leave him out.

  6. 'He needs to feel full confidence'published at 19:32 BST
    19:32 BST

    Liverpool v Tottenham (20:00 BST)

    Tottenham boss Roberto de Zerbi believes "for sure" that Dominic Solanke will rediscover his form and score 10 to 15 goals this season.

    The 29-year-old striker endured an injury-hit campaign last season, scoring three goals in 15 Premier League games.

    Solanke, who joined the club in 2024 from Bournemouth for a deal worth up to £65m, is yet to score in the league this season.

    "I can't understand why I have to think negatively. OK, because last season he was injured many times, yes," De Zerbi explained.

    "It's not the same last season [compared to] this season. This season, we have top physical and medical staff. We don't have three games per week.

    "I spoke with him and I would like to manage him in the best way I can, in the physical aspect and in the mental aspect, because he's a sensitive guy. He needs to feel full confidence, and from myself he has big, big confidence.

    "I believe he can score 10-15 goals for sure. He's already scored at Bournemouth, so why can't he score at Tottenham? It's the same sport, different stadium, no pressure, because in our stadium, if we push, if we play well, the fans are with us."

  8. Dowman must 'earn' Arsenal game timepublished at 19:31 BST
    19:31 BST

    Ipswich Town v Arsenal (20:00 BST)

    Arsenal

    Boss Mikel Arteta has insisted Arsenal's Max Dowman will get minutes this season but warned they must be earned.

    Arsenal were Carabao Cup runners-up last season after they lost at Wembley to Manchester City and head to Ipswich Town for a tricky all-Premier League tie eager to make it five victories from five for the new campaign.

    Dowman, 16, has been an unused substitute on four occasions for Arsenal this season, most recently at Napoli last Wednesday, but has topped up his game time with a handful of runouts for the under-21s and featured in a 4-1 loss to Chelsea at the weekend.

    Last season Dowman became the youngest player to start a Premier League fixture when he featured at Crystal Palace and scored his first professional goal against Everton.

    Dowman did not travel to Sunderland, but is in the starting 11 tonight at Portman Road.

    “We have a limitation with the numbers we have,” Arteta said in his latest remark about the Premier League rule of 20 players in a matchday squad.

    “Max will have his chance. He will have his minutes and he will have to earn them like anybody else. You know the talent that we have, the players we have there, we need to help him and find a way to make sure he continues to develop.”

  9. West Ham to pay tribute to goalkeeper Mikloskopublished at 19:28 BST
    19:28 BST

    West Ham v Fulham (19:45 BST)

    It promises to be an emotional evening at London Stadium as West Ham will hold a period of appreciation to former goalkeeper Ludek Miklosko at the Carabao Cup third-round tie against Fulham.

    Miklosko died earlier this week, aged 64, following a battle with cancer.

    He made almost 400 appearances for the Hammers between 1990 and 1998 after joining from Banik Ostrava. He announced his decision to withdraw from further cancer treatment in December 2024, three years after his diagnosis.

    The Czech helped West Ham win promotion to the top flight in 1991 and 1993 and was much loved by supporters, who regularly sang his name long after he left the club. He later returned as a goalkeeping coach.

    Miklosko, who represented Czechoslovakia at the 1990 World Cup, ended his playing career with QPR.

    West Ham's Czech midfielder Tomas Soucek will place a shirt with Miklosko's name on the back on the pitch before the game as a tribute.

    Ipswich Town v Arsenal (20:00 BST)

    Ipswich Town

    Ipswich Town boss Gary O’Neil wants his side to harness the energy of the Portman Road support under the lights as they look to topple Arsenal in the Carabao Cup third round.

    “So we can correct that. We can start the game well. We can take the fans with us on an attempt to beat one of the best sides around.”
