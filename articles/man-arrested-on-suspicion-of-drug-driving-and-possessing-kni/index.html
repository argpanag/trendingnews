Man arrested on suspicion of drug-driving and possessing knives and knuckle dusters

TheTools · 15.11.2026 01:35 · source · permalink

A man has been arrested on suspicion of drug-driving while possessing knives and knuckle dusters.

In the early hours of Friday morning, police officers stopped a driver after noticing "suspicious circumstances" in Lowestoft.

Officers then arrested the man on suspicion of possession with intent to supply Class B drugs, possession of offensive weapons and driving whilst unfit through drugs.

They recovered a quantity of suspected cannabis, drug-related paraphernalia and a number of offensive weapons, including knives and knuckle dusters.

The man has been conditionally bailed pending the results of blood tests relating to the drug-driving offence, and the vehicle has been seized.

A spokesman for Suffolk Police said: "This was an excellent example of officers identifying suspicious circumstances, taking positive action and achieving a significant result that has undoubtedly prevented further offending and reduced the risk to the public."
