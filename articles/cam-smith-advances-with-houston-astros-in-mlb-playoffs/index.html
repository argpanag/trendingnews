Cam Smith Advances with Houston Astros in MLB Playoffs

TheTools · 30.09.2026 22:28 · source · permalink

Cam Smith, former Florida State Seminoles standout, is making his mark with the Houston Astros in the MLB Playoffs as they face the Chicago White Sox. The postseason matchup began on Tuesday with Houston falling 6-3 in the series opener.

Smith impressed as an outfielder in the regular season, leading in putouts and ranking high in triples and outfield assists. Offensively, he recorded 21 home runs, 56 RBIs, and 12 stolen bases, though his batting average was .210.

Astros manager Joe Espada praised Smith's consistency and offensive performance earlier in the year.

"He's been very consistent, putting some good swings on pitches that we know he can handle," Espada said. "He looks really good offensively; I really like the at-bats."

Smith, originally a shortstop converted to third base at Florida State, excelled in college with a .387 average, 16 home runs, and 57 RBIs in his final season. He was drafted 14th overall by the Chicago Cubs in 2024 before being traded to Houston.

During the first playoff game, Smith went 1-for-4 and made a key defensive play. The Astros, AL West Champions with an 81-81 record, will face Chicago pitcher Sean Burke next, against whom Smith has one hit in five career at-bats.
