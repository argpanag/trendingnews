Taylor Swift’s “Pink Clouding” Lyrics Are About an Ex She Met at a “Cursed Time” Who She Regrets Letting Down - AOL

TheTools · 25.09.2026 07:21 · source · permalink

Attention everyone who thought “Pink Clouding” was going to be about reality crashing in after thinking you’ve found love. We were...correct! Ish. Taylor Swift’s new single is about thinking you’re in love after a bad breakup and making promises to someone but actually being on a pink cloud. As she puts it: “Years later, I still wonder how to tell you sorry / I didn't wanna let you down.”

Tay also laments meeting this person after having her heart broken, singing, “Hе crossed my heart, I hoped to diе (hoped to die) / Ripped all my orchids from the vine / You met me at a cursed time / I told you I was fine.”

As for the name, according to Healthline, Pink Clouding “describes a stage of early addiction recovery that involves feelings of euphoria and elation.” Just saying!
