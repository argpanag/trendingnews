Police called to Chargers QB Justin Herbert's fiancée Madison Beer's home for possible burglary

Police were called to the Hollywood Hills home of singer Madison Beer — fiancée of Los Angeles Chargers quarterback Justin Herbert — on Wednesday evening after reports of a possible burglary.

According to The New York Post, police received a call at 7:56 p.m. local time about a possible burglary and two men wearing dark clothing and facemasks in the neighborhood outside of Beer's home.

The reports also claim that the two suspects carried large backpacks and were driving a white SUV. However, when police arrived on the scene, they did not find any signs that a burglary had occurred.

It remains unclear whether Beer or Herbert was in the home at the time the call came in, though Los Angeles TV station ABC7 shared video that appears to show the singer and the Chargers quarterback speaking to police outside the home.

TMZ reports that the initial call came after someone spotted a prowler on their security camera.

Purchased in 2022, Beer’s three-bedroom, four-bath home is worth an estimated $5.5 million.

Herbert is coming off his and the Chargers' first win of the season, which happened on Monday night against the Baltimore Ravens. The victory snapped an 11-game losing streak for the team.

This incident comes as there has been a rash of break-ins and burglaries targeting homes tied to professional athletes.
