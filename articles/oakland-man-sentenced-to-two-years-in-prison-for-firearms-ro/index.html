Oakland man sentenced to two years in prison for firearms robbery

TheTools · 07.10.2026 20:13 · source · permalink

BANGOR, Maine (WABI) - An Oakland man was sentenced in federal court in Bangor for possessing stolen firearms.

A judge sentenced Cody Smith, 25, to two years in prison.

He was also ordered to pay nearly $7,000 in restitution.

According to court records, in August 2025, Smith broke into a pawn shop in Waterville and stole 20 handguns which was captured on video surveillance.

They say Smith was posting videos of himself on social media displaying some of the stolen firearms and admitting to the break-in.

Smith pleaded guilty in May 2026.
