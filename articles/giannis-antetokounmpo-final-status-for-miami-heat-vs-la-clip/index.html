Giannis Antetokounmpo (wrist) will suit up for the Miami Heat against the LA Clippers.

Giannis Antetokounmpo is all set to go for the Miami Heat this Wednesday evening against the LA Clippers.

After being initially listed as probable to play with a right wrist sprain, the Greek superstar has been made available by the Heat for the road matchup.

Giannis Antetokounmpo will shrug off his wrist injury issues as he aims to lead the Miami Heat once again on their lengthy West Coast trip.

The Heat will face the LA Clippers for the first night of their back-to-back games. As such, the status of the Greek big man on Thursday against the Golden State Warriors will be monitored.

Antetokounmpo is averaging 28.4 points, 12.3 rebounds, and 5.7 assists so far while logging 31.9 minutes per contest.

He and the Heat are off to a slow 2-5 season start heading into Wednesday's clash. They will look to bounce back after a brutal 138-117 Monday loss against the Los Angeles Lakers.
