North Charleston hospital hosts expo for stroke survivors and families

TheTools · 28.10.2026 09:15 · source · permalink

NORTH CHARLESTON, S.C. (WCSC) — HCA Healthcare Trident is hosting the Strike Out Stroke Expo on Wednesday to connect stroke survivors, their caregivers and their families with groups that can help with recovery and independence.

South Carolina ranked in the top 10 for stroke deaths in 2024, according to the state’s Department of Public Health.

The expo aims to help survivors return to daily life in their community. Strokes can happen at any age and can affect skills such as speaking, moving and working.

Attendees can meet with a variety of groups to make sure they get the help they need.

Briana Templeton, a speech-language pathologist with HCA Healthcare, said groups from the Charleston community are attending.

“We have all sorts of people coming from the Charleston community that are here to help you,” Templeton said.

She said the resources include meals, home modifications, research programs and vocational rehabilitation.

The expo runs from 2 to 4 p.m. at HCA Healthcare Trident Hospital in North Charleston.
