Hero MotoCorp strengthens women's cricket association with Jemimah Rodrigues signing

TheTools · 17.11.2026 05:30 · source · permalink

The partnership strengthens the manufacturer’s efforts to connect with younger consumers while building on its growing engagement with women’s sport.

Hero MotoCorp has expanded its association with Indian women’s cricket by appointing Indian international Jemimah Rodrigues as brand ambassador for its motorcycle business and electric mobility brand, VIDA.

The partnership strengthens the manufacturer’s efforts to connect with younger consumers while building on its growing engagement with women’s sport.

Rodrigues will feature in Hero MotoCorp and VIDA’s upcoming multimedia campaigns, product launches, and grassroots youth engagement initiatives. The partnership is expected to leverage the cricketer’s growing popularity and influence to strengthen the brands’ engagement with younger audiences.

Speaking on the association, Sanjay Bhan, Executive Vice President, Hero MotoCorp, said, “At Hero MotoCorp, our brand philosophy is centered on empowering young talent, embracing dynamic performance, and connecting with the aspirations of young India. Jemimah Rodrigues embodies these attributes effortlessly. Having recently welcomed Indian captain Smriti Mandhana to the Hero fold, bringing Jemimah on board further cements our commitment to championing excellence and leadership in women’s sports. On the field, she is a fearless competitor with nerves of steel—evident in her historic, match-winning performances on the world stage. Off the field, her vibrant energy, genuine passion, and positive outlook connect seamlessly with the youth. Jemimah represents a modern, unstoppable generation of achievers, and we are thrilled to welcome her to the Hero family as we continue to inspire millions to chase their dreams.”

Hero MotoCorp Brand Ambassador, Jemimah Rodrigues, added, “I am incredibly proud and honored to partner with Hero MotoCorp, a brand that has championed Indian sports and empowered Indian athletes across multiple disciplines. Cricket has taught me to play with grit, joy, and conviction, and I see those exact values reflected in Hero MotoCorp’s journey. I look forward to this exciting partnership as we encourage young Indians to break boundaries, trust their journey, and ride forward with confidence.”

Himanshu Sharma, Head of Sales, JSW Sports, continued, “We are happy to welcome a legacy brand like Hero MotoCorp to Jemimah Rodrigues’ growing brand portfolio as she continues to inspire a new generation of young athletes through her performances on and off the field. Partnerships like these reflect the growing opportunities for women in sport to build strong connections with leading brands and extend their influence beyond sport. It is encouraging to see Jemimah’s journey resonate with brands that share her values, and we look forward to seeing this association grow.”

The appointment also comes at a significant stage for VIDA as Hero MotoCorp accelerates its electric mobility ambitions. The brand recorded 30,143 dispatches in October 2026, while retail volumes grew 80% year-on-year, according to the company’s latest business update.

By bringing Rodrigues into its ambassador portfolio, Hero MotoCorp is looking to strengthen its connection with women’s cricket while aligning its established motorcycle business and emerging electric mobility portfolio with the aspirations of a younger generation of consumers.
