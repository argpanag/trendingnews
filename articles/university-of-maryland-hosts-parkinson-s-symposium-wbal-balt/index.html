University of Maryland hosts Parkinson's symposium | WBAL Baltimore News

TheTools · 24.09.2026 21:06 · source · permalink

Thousands of Maryland residents live with Parkinson’s disease, a chronic condition for which there is no cure.

The University of Maryland will host their annual Parkinson’s Disease Symposium on Friday, which will focus on “finding meaning in daily life with chronic conditions.”

Dr. Lisa Shulman, a professor of neurology at University of Maryland School of Medicine, said it is important for patients to regain a sense of control by managing office visits and discussing their disease.

“We need to talk to our patients about the issues involved with talking to others and their family and their friends, other people they meet out socially about what’s going on in their life. Not to go overboard, but so people can understand it and have comfort,” Shulman said.

For more information on the symposium, visit the university’s website.
