Ather Energy - Annual Capacity enhancement by 2.25 lakh units

To meet rising demand, Ather will increase annual production capacity at Hosur by 75,000 units, from 4.2lakh to 4.95 lakh units by December 2026. Ather’s new facility in AURIC, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, ison track to begin commercial production in phases from December 2026, with Phase 1 having an annualproduction capacity of 5 lakh units. Ather will further expand the plant’s annual capacity by 1.5 lakh unitsto 6.5 lakh units, with the additional capacity expected to commence production by Q2 FY28.With these expansions, Ather’s combined installed annual capacity across Hosur and AURIC will reachapproximately 11.4 lakh units by Q2 FY28. The company will invest approximately ₹122 crore across thetwo facilities to support these capacity enhancements.

Ather saw a sharp rise in customer interest, with enquiries up 109% YoY to about 10 lakh, while pre-orders more than doubled to 1.80 lakh in Q2 FY27. Demand outpaced available production capacity, with the Hosur facility operating at 98% capacity utilisation during peak production in Q2 FY27
