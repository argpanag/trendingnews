URC: Leinster host rivals Munster at Aviva Stadium

  • Line-ups

Summary

  • United Rugby Championship - Leinster 24-18 Munster (Result)

  • Munster ahead 13-0 through Daly try and O'Shea's eight points off tee, before Tommy O'Brien try cuts lead.

  • Ryan Baird's converted score brings Leinster within one point before Charlie Tector completes comeback.

  • Leinster's second and third tries come with Munster's Nankivell in sin bin.

  • Jamie Osborne makes game safe for Leinster and secures try bonus; Munster earn losing bonus point in final play.

  • Last game for Irish internationals before Nations Championship fixtures.

United Rugby Championship - Leinster 24-18 Munster (Result)

Munster ahead 13-0 through Daly try and O'Shea's eight points off tee, before Tommy O'Brien try cuts lead.

Ryan Baird's converted score brings Leinster within one point before Charlie Tector completes comeback.

Leinster's second and third tries come with Munster's Nankivell in sin bin.

Jamie Osborne makes game safe for Leinster and secures try bonus; Munster earn losing bonus point in final play.

Last game for Irish internationals before Nations Championship fixtures.
