Marius Borg Høiby’s House Arrest Extended Again

TheTools · 05.10.2026 13:49 · source · permalink

The defense attorney for Queen Mette-Marit’s son says he plans to use this period to find a job.

Marius Borg Høiby will remain in custody for four more weeks, the Oslo District Court ruled today. The prosecution is still concerned about a risk of recidivism, and a restraining order remains in place to protect one of his ex-girlfriends.

“Høiby agrees to continued detention for four weeks. He is motivated to start working and will use this period to secure a concrete job offer,” his defense attorney, Alexander Greaker, said in a press release (translated from Norwegian). His lawyers added, “There has been a dialogue with the police about the conditions for a possible release of Marius Borg Høiby. Høiby believes the conditions the police have set are unreasonable and therefore wishes to continue the current arrangement.”

The current arrangement is that Høiby, Queen Mette-Marit’s son, is serving his house arrest at royal residence Skaugum. In June, Oslo District Court sentenced Høiby to four years in prison, finding him guilty of two counts of rape, assaulting a former girlfriend, and a number of other offenses. Høiby denied all four alleged rapes, but pleaded guilty on some lesser charges, including bodily harm, damage, and threats. His team later filed for appeal on the rape and assault charges; the appeal trial is set to begin on February 9, 2027.

His sentence is not legally binding while he appeals, though the house arrest relates to his pre-trial detention. Høiby was arrested in February 2026, on the eve of his trial, for charges alleging assault, threats with a knife, and violating a restraining order.

Recently, Høiby made a rare public appearance for King Harald’s funeral, and he was was a pallbearer as the Norwegian royal family carried Harald’s coffin through the Palace.
