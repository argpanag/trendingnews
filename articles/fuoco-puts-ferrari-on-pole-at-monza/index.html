Fuoco puts Ferrari on pole at Monza

TheTools · 08.11.2026 14:18 · source · permalink

Antonio Fuoco thrilled the ever-enthusiastic tifosi that made the trip to the Autodromo Nazionale Monza today (7 November), by clinching pole position for Ferrari ahead of tomorrow’s final round of the 2026 FIA World Endurance Championship.

As had been the case back at the beginning of the campaign at Imola, the Ferrari 499P was the car to beat on home soil at Monza, and on this occasion, it was Fuoco who made best use of it. The Italian comfortably topped the timesheets in the opening part of the two-stage qualifying session, before seeing off his rivals once again in the 10-minute Hyperpole shootout en route to a fifth outright pole in FIA WEC and first in 18 months.

The result represented the 499P’s 11th pole in the series – and fourth in a row in Italy, having similarly snared the prime starting spot at Imola for three seasons in succession. On Sunday, Fuoco together with #50 team-mates Nicklas Nielsen and Miguel Molina will be aiming to convert that advantage into a second victory of what has predominantly been a challenging title defence for the Prancing Horse.

“Coming to Italy is always special for us and to be on pole here at Monza, as an Italian driver with Ferrari, is really emotional,” the 30-year-old reflected. “It was quite tricky because with practice having all been wet, we didn’t have chance to do any prior dry-weather running, but I really like these conditions when you just have to go and send it and try to put everything together. I think today, we did that really well. 

“I want to thank the team because in situations like this, we need to trust them on the set-up and what they give us. I’m pretty sure tomorrow in the grandstands, we will have a lot of support, and the Italian fans bring so much passion. We know it’s going to be a long race, so we need to keep cool, keep calm and continue to do our best.” 

If Fuoco was the indisputable class of the field in the first part of the session, he had it far from his own way in Hyperpole, when the former 24 Hours of Le Mans winner faced a number of threats to his supremacy and had to dig deep to come out on top. 

Continuing Genesis’ stellar Spanish form from three weeks ago, Mathieu Jaminet grabbed a second consecutive front row berth for the Korean motorsport newcomer behind the wheel of the #19 entry, just 0.096s adrift of the pace-setting Ferrari, although Barcelona hero Mathys Jaubert failed to make the top ten in the sister GMR-001.

Row two will be shared by the #38 Cadillac V-Series.R and #51 Ferrari courtesy of strong laps from Earl Bamber and Antonio Giovinazzi, with the top five rounded out by Théo Pourchaire, who produced a superb effort in the #94 Peugeot 9X8 in its final event in its current iteration, backed up by #93 stablemate Nick Cassidy in ninth.

Further down the order, neither of the title-chasing Toyota TR010 Hybrids advanced beyond the initial phase of the session, with Ryō Hirakawa and countryman Kamui Kobayashi languishing down in 15th and 16th positions respectively, ahead of only the #15 BMW M Hybrid V8, currently best-of-the-rest in the championship standings.

The 6 Hours of Monza will begin at 11:00 local time (CET) tomorrow (Sunday, 8 November).
