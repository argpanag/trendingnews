ALW PREVIEW: Brisbane v City

TheTools · 20.11.2026 03:10 · source · permalink

Home News

Staff Writers

20 November, 2026

After a little over a week on home soil, our City Women are back on the road this weekend, heading north to face Brisbane Roar on Saturday evening.

Michael Matricciani’s side will be looking to maintain our unbeaten start to the Ninja A-League season, with just one more fixture to come before the first international break of the campaign.

There’s plenty to look forward to, with another three points on the line in Brisbane.

Here are a few of the major talking points ahead of Saturday’s clash!

Going into the break on a high

With the first international break of the season fast approaching, Matricciani and his coaching staff can be pleased with how things have started.

City has played six games across all competitions so far this season, tasting defeat just once – and responding brilliantly to that setback to secure a third consecutive AFC Women’s Champions League home Quarter Final.

Our most recent league fixture ended in a share of the points against Newcastle Jets, although there were still plenty of positives to take from the performance.

Having only returned from Uzbekistan a few days earlier, City controlled large periods of the contest and created plenty of opportunities, with Cortnee Vine putting us ahead just before half-time after a brilliant through ball from Melina Ayres.

The Jets ultimately found an equaliser in the second half, leaving City to settle for a point.

Now, with one final fixture before a well-earned break, we’ll be looking to finish this opening block of the season with another big three points and head into the international window on a high.

A reshuffle in attack

The biggest selection question heading into Saturday will be how Matricciani and his staff replace Vine.

Our newest Matildas star will unfortunately miss the remainder of 2026 after sustaining a high-grade hip flexor injury in the latter stages of last weekend’s draw, meaning at least one change is guaranteed in Brisbane.

There are certainly no shortage of options.

Kelli McGroarty has already shown she can step up when called upon, starting during our AFC Women’s Champions League campaign while Vine was unavailable from the outset and producing a standout performance that included a brace against Kaya FC-Iloilo.

Avaani Prakash is another option, having already started three games across the opening weeks of the campaign, while Tully Heatley could also provide a more like-for-like replacement after featuring as a substitute on several occasions this season.

Whoever gets the nod, there’s an opportunity for someone else to step up and make their mark as City looks to keep our unbeaten league record intact.

Familiar surroundings, new colours

Saturday will be a little different for two members of our squad.

Aimee Medwin and Momo Hayashi will both return to Brisbane for the first time since making the move south to City over the off-season, with the pair set to face their former side in City colours.

Both have made an immediate impact since arriving in Melbourne.

Hayashi has already made her mark on the scoresheet, finding the back of the net three times across her opening games – including a couple of spectacular strikes.
