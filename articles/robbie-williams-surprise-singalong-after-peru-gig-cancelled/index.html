Robbie Williams' surprise singalong after Peru gig cancelled

TheTools · 25.09.2026 18:20 · source · permalink

Robbie Williams gave an impromptu performance outside a hotel in Peru's capital Lima, after his concert at a stadium in the city was cancelled.

Authorities announced that the Arena 1 Park gig could not go ahead, citing failures by the venue to comply with safety regulations.

Robbie told fans outside his hotel he was "broken-hearted" and held a brief singalong with the cheering crowds.
