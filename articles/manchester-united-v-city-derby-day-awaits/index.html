Manchester United v City: Derby day awaits...

TheTools · 25.10.2026 17:30 · source · permalink

City travel to tackle Manchester United at the Progress With Unity Stadium on Sunday 25 October in a 18:00 (UK) kick-off.

It represents the first bout between the two sides in 2026/27 with Andrée Jeglertz’s reigning Barclays Women’s Super League champions seeking to further extend our perfect start to the league season.

The Blues have won all six of our WSL games to date and will go into the contest full of confidence.

However, United, now under the stewardship of Eva Olid, go into the game on a back of fine run of form themselves having recovered from a tough start to the season to lie fifth.

So, there will be everything to play for in the latest instalment of the battle for Manchester supremacy

Can City take the bragging rights and make Manchester blue once more?
