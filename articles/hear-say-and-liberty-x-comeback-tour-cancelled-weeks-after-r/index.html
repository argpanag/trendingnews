Hear'Say and Liberty X comeback tour cancelled weeks after reunion announcement

TheTools · 24.11.2026 16:25 · source · permalink

The Hear'Say and Liberty X reunion tour tickets will be refunded

The Hear'Say and Liberty X comeback tour will not be going ahead. The legendary 2000s pop groups were teaming up for a nation wide tour in 2027 and announced the plans mere weeks before the cancellation news.

They were meant to be performing 13 dates in arenas up and down the UK and Ireland throughout April and May 2027, but they have now revealed that this will no longer be happening.

Hear'Say took to Instagram to write: "This is not the news we wanted to share, but we wanted you to hear it from us first. We are incredibly disappointed to let you know that the upcoming UK & Ireland arena tour with Liberty X will no longer be going ahead as planned."

The group assured fans that "all tickets will be fully refunded" as they continued: "We know how much excitement there has been around us getting back together and you have shown us an overwhelming amount of love and support."

Hear'Say were gearing up for a reunion in April 2027(Image: Getty Images)

"This reunion was never just a tour. It was about reconnecting with each other and reconnecting with you. We want to thank Liberty X and everyone involved for their collective hard work and commitment."

The band explained: "While this particular run of shows won't be happening, it has reminded us just how much this means to us and, judging by the response, how much it means to you too. We're genuinely sorry for the disappointment this news will bring, particularly to those of you who were looking forward to seeing us live."

Both Hear'Say and Liberty X rose to fame on the TV talent show Popstars(Image: Getty Images)

They close out the message by writing: "If you would like to stay connected, please sign up to our website www.hearsayofficial.com (link in bio). Thank you for standing by us. We love you [red heart emoji] Hear'Say."

Devastated fans gathered down in the comment section to share their suppot to the group and their disappointment at the sudden news. One person said: "Gutted for you all."

Another wrote: "Such a shame, especially having added extra dates too for Sheffield and Aberdeen! [crying emoji]."

Someone else added: "Omg what?! [crying emoijs] April Fools is too early."
