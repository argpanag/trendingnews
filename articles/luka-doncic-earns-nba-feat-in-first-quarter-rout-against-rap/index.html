Luka Doncic Earns NBA Feat in First-Quarter Rout Against Raptors

TheTools · 02.11.2026 05:38 · source · permalink

Luka Doncic records an untouched milestone in a stellar first half against the Toronto Raptors

Luka Doncic hasn’t slowed down ever since his 52-point game against the Utah Jazz. The Lakers guard has recorded two back-to-back 35+ point games and has now added another to the tally against the Toronto Raptors on Monday. Coming after a tough defeat to the Golden State Warriors, this game at the Crypto.com Arena was a night for vengeance, and Doncic made sure that it stayed that way.

By the time the first frame of play ended, Doncic had already recorded 16 points, 4 rebounds, and steals. The Lakers guard went 5 of 7 from the field during this quarter and 4 of 5 from downtown. Doncic’s rout early in Q1 gave the Lakers an early 13-point lead in the game.

Moreover, Doncic’s box score in Q1 also made him the first NBA player ever to record 4 three-pointers and steals in a single quarter. In the Lakers’ last game against the Warriors, Doncic had already achieved the record of having the most number of quarters with 20+ points, 5+ assists, and 5+ three-pointers.

Now, this night against the Raptors adds another silver lining to Doncic’s stellar arsenal. Notably, Luka Doncic didn’t just stop at his first-quarter dominance against the Toronto Raptors on Sunday. By the concluding minutes of the fourth quarter, Doncic’s stat line showed 41 points, 14 rebounds, and 5 assists with a plus-minus of +22.

However, the Lakers guard has struggled massively with turnovers in the game and has turned the ball over 7 times already. This has been a major issue for him this season, as the 27-year-old is currently averaging 5.2 turnovers per game, which often gives opponents openings in transition.

Even in the game against the Raptors, the Lakers have given up 19 turnovers, giving away 25 points in the process. But for now, the Lakers’ hot offense led by Doncic has compensated quite well, giving the hosts a 15-point advantage with just over 4 minutes left in Q4.

Luka Doncic Adds Another Silver Lining To Lakers Career

In his performance against the Raptors, Doncic recorded a stellar 8 three-pointers in the game. This has now made the former Dallas Mavericks star the player with the most games with more than 7 three-pointers as a Laker. Notably, Doncic has achieved this feat six times in the game, surpassing Kobe Bryant and LeBron James, who achieved this feat five and four times, respectively.

However, it needs to be understood that the Lakers have been in the franchise for just around two years now. Even in this small time frame, Doncic has surpassed franchise records like no other. This gives the basketball circuit even more reasons to believe that Doncic is the franchise’s face for years to come.
