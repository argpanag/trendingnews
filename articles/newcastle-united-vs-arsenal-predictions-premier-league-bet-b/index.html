My betting recommendations also include this to be a low-scoring game and a bet builder that will pay out at 3.64/1 if it’s a winner. Take a look at my tips below and read on for more analysis.

Arsenal’s defence can keep it clean

Arsenal come into this match off the back of five straight wins in the Premier League and everything points to them claiming a sixth victory tonight. 

The Gunners have been beaten only twice in 17 games in all competitions this season. Just one of the defeats has been in the Premier League – the 3-0 loss at Brighton – with the other being the shock EFL Cup upset at League Two Fleetwood Town, where Mikel Arteta’s team lost a penalty shootout. 

Aside from the Brighton defeat, the visitors have won four of their five away games in the Premier League and kept a clean sheet in all of those victories, including at Aston Villa and Liverpool. 

There’s every reason to believe Arsenal can contain the Magpies, who have failed to score in two of their last four league games – both ending in home losses – and are on a run of one win in five in the top flight.

With Arsenal priced at 3/5 to win this one, the best way to back an away victory is to add the caveat of the Gunners keeping a clean sheet, which boosts the price at 13/8.

Selection: Arsenal to win to nil @ 13/8

 

Don’t expect goals from the leaders

With Arsenal regularly keeping clean sheets, they don’t need to score many goals to win games. 

The Londoners have not conceded in any of their last four Premier League victories and in all of these games a bet on under 2.5 goals would have landed. 

Under 2.5 goals has also been a successful bet in each of Newcastle’s last four league games, at a time when the Magpies are struggling to score. 

So I’ll be on that bet at 11/10. For those of you exploring an alternative to my first two selections, you might consider combining them, with an Arsenal win and under 2.5 goals priced at 3/1.

Selection: Under 2.5 goals @ 11/10

 

Havertz habit part of 3.64/1 bet builder

My third recommendation is a four-fold bet builder that will pay out at odds of 3.64/1 if successful. 

I’m starting with an Arsenal win. Odds of 3/5 are too short to back that bet as a single, but a perfectly acceptable choice in a multiple. 

Having pointed out the likelihood of there being under 2.5 goals scored, the addition of under 3.5 goals in this bet builder looks like something of a banker. It has been a winner in all 10 of Arsenal’s Premier League games this season. 

I’m also adding under 11.5 corners. This has been a successful bet in nine of Newcastle’s 10 top-flight fixtures and in eight of Arsenal’s. 

Finally, I’m going for Kai Havertz to be caught offside on at least one occasion. No player has been flagged more often this season than the German, who has strayed offside 15 times. For context, the next-highest total from a Premier League player is Dominic Calvert-Lewin of Leeds United with eight.

Selection: Bet builder - Arsenal to win, under 3.5 goals, under 11.5 corners, Havertz 1+ offsides @ 3.64/1
