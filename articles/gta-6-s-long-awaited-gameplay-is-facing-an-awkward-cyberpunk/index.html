GTA 6's Long-Awaited Gameplay Is Facing an Awkward Cyberpunk 2077 Reality Check - PlayStation LifeStyle

TheTools · 18.11.2026 18:07 · source · permalink

Home »

Grand Theft Auto VI is facing criticism after a gameplay clip surfaced online showing what appears to be an awkward glitch. The footage has drawn comparisons to Cyberpunk 2077 and has raised questions about the long-awaited game’s technical polish.

In a GTA 6 gameplay clip Michael Does Life shared on X, the player character grapples a police officer, and their bodies awkwardly overlap. Moreover, they pass through their surrounding furniture, resulting in unnatural movements that seem out of place.

Expressing their frustration in the caption, the streamer wrote, “It’s Cyberpunk 2077 all over again. What is this glitchy, buggy mess? 13 Years for this. Yikes!”

The Cyberpunk 2077 comparison raises questions about GTA 6’s polish

The latest GTA installment’s comparison to Cyberpunk 2077 largely stems from the latter’s poor reception at launch in December 2020. Upon its launch, players encountered numerous technical problems across platforms. The game drew criticism for bugs and performance issues, leading to refunds and the temporary suspension of digital sales on the PlayStation Store. Eventually, CD Projekt Red improved the game through updates.

Beyond the awkward glitches, gamers have also raised concerns about FPS drops, with some claiming frame rates fall below 20 FPS during combat. Because the game took longer than expected to release, with multiple postponed launch dates, comparisons to Cyberpunk 2077 have raised questions about its polish. However, since the game recently launched, gamers can expect updates soon. As of now, Metacritic shows the most-awaited game with a 99 score from 153 critic reviews on PlayStation.
