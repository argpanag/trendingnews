Your Complete Guide To Securing Kings Of Leon Australia Tour Presale Tickets Today

TheTools · 22.10.2026 00:10 · source · permalink

THE RUNDOWN

  • Kings of Leon are touring Australia from February 28, 2027 to March 5, 2027.
  • Presale tickets from most vendors go on sale today at 12:00 pm AEDT.
  • Kings of Leon will be touring their new 13-track album, O My Beloved, which debuts November 6, 2027.

If your concert calendar is currently missing a heavy dose of stadium-sized 2010s indie rock, consider it sorted. Grammy-winning American rock royalty Kings of Leon have officially announced their massive return to Australia and New Zealand for a headline tour in March February and March 2027. Urgently need tickets? Kings of Leon pre-sale starts Thursday, October 22, 2026, with general sale commencing Tuesday, October 27. 

The tour arrives hot on the heels of their tenth studio album, O My Beloved, which will be released worldwide on November 6, 2026, and will see the family band Caleb, Nathan, Jared and Matthew Followill (three brothers and a cousin) taking over massive outdoor stages and racecourses across the country. Even better? They’ll be bringing much-loved Australian psychedelic rock troupe Ocean Alley along as special guests. 

Whether you’re planning a nostalgia-fuelled trip back to 2008 or a recently recruited fan ready to scream their latest anthems, here is everything you need to know about securing pre-sale tickets today. 

Kings Of Leon Australian Tour Dates and Venues

  • Sunday, February 28, 2027: Sydney Summertime, Centennial Park, Sydney, NSW
  • Wednesday, March 3, 2027: Brisbane Showgrounds, Brisbane, QLD
  • Friday, March 5: Flemington Racecourse, Melbourne, Victoria

Presale and On-Sale Schedule:

Not all presales are created equal. To ensure you don’t hit a brick wall at checkout, we’ve broken down the specific T&Cs, access criteria and fine print for the Kings of Leon presale window.

Album Order Presale: Thursday, October 22 at 12:00pm

Fans must have pre-ordered Kings of Leon’s tenth studio album, O My Beloved, from the official artist or designated music store (such as Universal Music Australia) prior to the cut-off window. An exclusive access link and unique code will be dispatched via email to the exact address during the album transaction.

Vodafone Presale: Thursday, October 22 at 12:00pm

Restricted to active Vodafone customers in Australia, the Vodafone presale requires verification via the dedicated portal where customers can authenticate their mobile account details and retrieve a unique presale code. Allocation is limited, and access does not guaruntee tickets: standard ticket limits per customer apply. Visit the dedicated portal.

Mastercard Presale: Thursday, October 22, 2026 at 12:00pm

Transactions must be completed using a valid Mastercard (credit or debit). Register on the website.

Live Nation Presale: Friday, October 23, 2026 at 12: 00 pm

Open to all registered Live Nation members. Create your free account on the Live Nation homepage.

General Sale: General sale will go live Tuesday, October 27 at 1:00pm local time via Ticketmaster and Moshtix.
