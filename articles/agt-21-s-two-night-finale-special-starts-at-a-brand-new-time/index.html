AGT 21's Two-Night Finale Special Starts at a Brand New Time! What to Know

TheTools · 21.09.2026 19:31 · source · permalink

The Live Finals fun starts a little later than usual!

Key Takeaways

  • America’s Got Talent's Season 21 Finale airs live on NBC over two nights, Tuesday, September 22 and Wednesday, September 23, at the new time of 9/8c instead of 8/7c.
  • The Tuesday Finale episode features performances from the Top 10 Acts, while Wednesday reveals the winner of America’s Vote and includes surprise guests and special performances.
  • Fans can vote through the NBC website, the AGT mobile app, or the official AGT TikTok page, with voting open from the start of Tuesday’s episode until 7 a.m. ET on Wednesday.

America's Got Talent Season 21 is going out with a bang! As always, summer's hottest show comes to a conclusion with an epic party, bringing together talents from years past as well as famous stars for a talent extravaganza that takes two nights and culminates with the crowning of a new AGT champion.

How to Watch

Watch America's Got Talent Tuesdays at 8 p.m. ET on NBC and next day on Peacock.

Will it be one of the animal Acts, either trainer Olivia Befus or the dancers of Acro Canine Crew? Perhaps a magic Act, like magician Geno Ploeger or the many hands of Hundred Fingers? Come Here and Royal Lasers represent the dance Acts, while Lai Noelle and Nene Royal are both teenage musicians. Rounding out the Top 10 are robotics team Unitree and aerial pole artist Veronika Goroshkova.

This year, AGT is pushing the start time for late-night fun, and we know Judges Howie Mandel, Mel B, and Sofía Vergara will make the most of it. But don't worry, they're not getting too crazy: It's only premiering one hour later, and the show is still live on NBC. Here's exactly when to tune in:

This is what time AGT 21 airs the two-part Live Finale

AGT will air live at 9/8c (instead of its usual time slot of 8/7c) on both Tuesday, September 22 and Wednesday, September 23. The Tuesday episode will consist of performances from all 10 Final Acts, and the Wednesday episode will reveal the results of America's Vote. There will also be surprise guests and special performances on Wednesday, so be sure to tune in for fun and excitement!

Having trouble finding it? You can log into the NBC website or NBC app on your Smart TV using your cable provider's credentials. Shortly after they air, clips from Lives are often uploaded on the show's official YouTube account and social handles. So if you're on the go during the Finale, sneak a peek at your phone and you may catch a spoiler!

As always, new episodes of America's Got Talent are available to stream on Peacock the day after they air.

How to vote for the winner of AGT

This season, there are three methods available for AGT voters. The first is by logging in via email on the AGT section of the NBC website where you can cast your vote up to 10 times, distributing your votes however you like. The second is by using the AGT App on your smart phone or other compatible mobile device, where the same rules apply. The third is the newest method: commenting on the official AGT TikTok page when the "vote here" post goes up. You can comment up to 10 times, and you have to have a public TikTok account to do so.

Voting opens at the start of Tuesdays episode and ends at 7 a.m. ET on Wednesday, so be sure to hope onto your voting platform of choice once you know where you'd like to send your support.
