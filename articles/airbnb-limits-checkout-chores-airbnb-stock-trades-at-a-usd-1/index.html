Airbnb Inc. (ISIN US0090661010) announced on October 21, 2026, that new host rules will limit checkout chores, while the company had a USD 104.0 billion market capitalization on October 21, 2026. According to ABC News, the policy will generally prevent hosts from requiring guests to strip beds, wash dishes or take trash offsite for reservations booked from November 20, 2026.

Rules arrive before the results

The change gives Airbnb a fresh consumer-facing policy point before its next quarterly report. Hosts must also disclose fees and house rules before guests book, according to FOX 29 Philadelphia.

Three dates define the story

On August 6, 2026, Airbnb reported second-quarter revenue of USD 3.6 billion, up 17.00 percent year over year, according to Benzinga. On October 1, 2026, the company announced that third-quarter results would be released on November 5, 2026, after the market close, according to Morningstar.
