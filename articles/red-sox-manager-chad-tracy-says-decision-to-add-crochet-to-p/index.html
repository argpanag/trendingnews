Red Sox manager Chad Tracy says decision to add Crochet to playoff roster 'not overly difficult'

TheTools · 29.09.2026 15:58 · source · permalink

Red Sox, Yankees announce rosters for Wild Card series; Crochet in, Aaron Judge out

Red Sox, Yankees announce rosters for Wild Card series; Crochet in, Aaron Judge out

The Red Sox and Yankees are gearing up for another postseason meeting as the league's most intense rivalry continues.

Boston and New York each released their full rosters for the best-of-three series, and there are some notable additions and omissions.

For the Red Sox, Garrett Crochet is on the roster as a reliever after missing most of the season with left shoulder inflammation and a lat strain. He has not pitched since April 25, going 3-3 with a 6.30 ERA in six starts.

In a press conference ahead of Game 1, Red Sox manager Chad Tracy said putting Crochet on the roster was not a hard choice.

“I don’t think it was an overly difficult decision. It was just about making the right decision, having all of the information, outside of just Garrett saying he felt really good, which is a huge part of it, but also doing our due diligence medically,” Tracy said.

New Hampshire native Mickey Gasper, a key piece for the team in the second half of the season, will not play in this series. He's on the injured list with a biceps injury suffered last week.

First baseman Willson Contreras, meanwhile, will play in the playoffs for Boston. He had been battling a hand injury for most of September.

For the Yankees, much of the chatter has been about whether superstar Aaron Judge would be able to return from his injury. In the end, Judge was left off the roster, but slugger Giancarlo Stanton, who has missed most of the season, will be active as a pinch-hitting option for New York.

In Game 1, rookie Payton Tolle will take the hill for Boston. He'll be opposed by Massachusetts native Cam Schlittler, the likely Cy Young Award winner in the American League.

Sonny Gray will start for Boston in Wednesday's Game 2, while Ranger Suarez would start Thursday's Game 3, if it's necessary.
