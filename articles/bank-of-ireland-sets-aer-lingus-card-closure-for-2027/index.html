Bank of Ireland sets Aer Lingus card closure for 2027

TheTools · 22.11.2026 04:11 · source · permalink

Bank of Ireland will close the Aer Lingus Aer Credit Card in 2027, with benefits continuing until the shutdown. Lounge and priority passes must be used for travel by that date; insurance ends under return-trip rules. The bank says transfer details will follow in early 2027.

Originally reported by headforpoints.com · The Aer Lingus credit card is being closed down by Bank of Ireland

Edited by Randy Petersen

Nov 22 · 5:11 AM · 1 min

The card earns 1 Avios per €4 spent and carries a €108 annual fee including Government Stamp Duty. New applications remain open, but applicants will receive benefits for only about a year before closure. Bank of Ireland says the card and its benefits operate normally through Nov. 30, 2027; that includes existing cardholders until the withdrawal date.

The bank's notice says travel insurance remains in force through Nov. 30, 2027, subject to policy terms, then ends at midnight. For a trip underway on that date, cover ends when the cardholder returns home to Ireland or 45 days after the card is withdrawn, whichever comes first. Lounge and Fast Track or priority boarding passes cannot be used for travel after the cutoff.

Cardholders must weigh switching to another Bank of Ireland card against waiting for transfer details, which are not yet specified. Waiting could complicate plans for travelers who rely on the card's insurance or airport benefits.
