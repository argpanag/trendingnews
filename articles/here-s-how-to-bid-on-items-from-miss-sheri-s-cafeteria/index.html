Here's how to bid on items from Miss Sheri's Cafeteria

TheTools · 21.09.2026 20:53 · source · permalink

St. Louis County, Mo. — Tuesday is the last day to bid on hundreds of items from Miss Sheri’s Cafeteria in south St. Louis County.

The restaurant closed earlier this month after serving comfort-style food for 54 years. 

More than 400 items from the restaurant will find new homes thanks to the online auction.

The auction features restaurant equipment, furnishings, appliances, decor and more. 

Bidders also can go to the former restaurant to see items in person. Miss Sheri’s Cafeteria is located at 5406 Southfield Center.

The auction closes Tuesday, Sept. 22, at 8 p.m. For full details and to place a bid, click here.
