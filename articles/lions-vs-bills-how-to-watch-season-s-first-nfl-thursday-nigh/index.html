Lions Vs. Bills: How To Watch Season's First NFL 'Thursday Night Football' Livestream Online & On TV For Free

TheTools · 17.09.2026 22:21 · source · permalink

Breaking News

Week 2 of the 2026 NFL season opens with the first Thursday Night Football game, a matchup between the Detroit Lions and the Buffalo Bills.

The game is set to take place at the new Highmark Stadium in Orchard Park, New York, where the Bills QB Josh Allen will face off against Lions QB Jared Goff.

Both teams won their Week 1 games as they continue their road to the 2027 playoffs and Super Bowl LXI.

What time is the game between the Detroit Lions and the Buffalo Bills?

The NFL game between the Detroit Lions and the Buffalo Bills is scheduled to start at 8:15 p.m. ET and 5:15 p.m. PT.

Watch on Deadline

Where to watch and stream the Detroit Lions and the Buffalo Bills game live?

Thursday Night Football is a night owned by Amazon’s Prime Video, where subscribers can livestream the game.

Fans in the teams’ local markets can catch the action for free on local over-the-air TV.

Another free option is Amazon’s Twitch, where the game will stream on Prime Video’s channel.

NFL+ is another option for streaming the game on mobile devices. The streaming service has access to your local games and primetime games.
