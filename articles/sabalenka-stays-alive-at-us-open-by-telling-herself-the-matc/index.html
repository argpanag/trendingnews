Sabalenka stays alive at US Open by telling herself 'the match is over'

Aryna Sabalenka was down a break before coming back to win the deciding set. (Getty Images: Al Bello)

In short:

Aryna Sabalenka has come back from a break in the third set to beat Linda Noskova in the US Open quarterfinals.

Sabalenka said she thought "the match is over" while trailing 2-4 in the decider, before coming back to win it in a tiebreak.

What's next?

She will face the winner of an all-American clash between third seed Jessica Pegula and 26th seed Emma Navarro.

World number one Aryna Sabalenka has survived her toughest test of the tournament to beat Wimbledon champion Linda ​Noskova 7-6(7/1), 3-6, 7-6(10/7) and reach the US Open semifinals for a sixth consecutive year.

Two-time defending champion Sabalenka had not dropped a set before facing the Czech sixth seed, who fired ​18 aces and saved ‌seven of nine break points but faltered when leading by a break at 2-4 in the final set.

Sabalenka, chasing a third straight title at Flushing Meadows, next plays the winner of ‌an all-American clash between Jessica Pegula and Emma Navarro.

By reaching the final four for a sixth straight time, Sabalenka matched the 2011-2016 run of Serena Williams, during which she won three of her six US Open crowns.
