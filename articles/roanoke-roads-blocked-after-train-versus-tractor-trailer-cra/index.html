ROANOKE, Va. (WSET) — Police in Roanoke County are currently on the scene of a crash involving a tractor-trailer and a train.

The crash took place near the intersection of Garman Road and Garman Circle.

At this time, Garman Road, Diuguids Lane, and Barley Drive are blocked at railroad crossings.

Drivers are asked to seek alternate routes in these areas, which will remain closed until the big rig can be removed from the tracks.

Police did not provide any information on possible injuries.
