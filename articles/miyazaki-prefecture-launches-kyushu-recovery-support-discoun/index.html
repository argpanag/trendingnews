Miyazaki Prefecture will offer the “Kyushu Recovery Support Discount – Miyazaki Discount Campaign” under the government’s Kyushu Recovery Support Discount travel assistance program, for stays from October 1 through December 25.

The Kyushu Recovery Support Discount applies to travel and accommodation products involving stays of one night or more in the Kyushu region. It provides a 50% discount on travel and accommodation costs, while the discount is 60% in Kumamoto and Kagoshima prefectures, where cancellations have had a particularly significant impact on tourism. The maximum discount is 20,000 yen per night for accommodation-only products and accommodation travel products including transportation; 30,000 yen for accommodation travel products including transportation for stays of two nights or more; and 35,000 yen for multi-destination travel products covering accommodations in two or more prefectures. Travel for business purposes, publicly funded business trips, and reservations made before sales begin are not eligible for the discount.

Sales will begin at 10 a.m. on September 25 through online booking sites (Jalan and Rakuten Travel), and sequentially from September 28 through accommodation facilities and travel agencies.

The official website is scheduled to be launched at around 3 p.m. on September 16.
