Spalletti and Neto speak ahead of Juventus vs Omonia in the UEL - Juventus

TheTools · 26.11.2026 23:40 · source · permalink

25 November 2026

The Men’s First Team take on Cypriot side Omonia in the UEFA Europa League on Thursday night.

Luciano Spalletti and Norberto Neto spoke to the press ahead of the Matchday 5 clash, which kicks off at 21:00 CET.

LUCIANO SPALLETTI

“Omonia have six points in the table and are third in their domestic league. They’re a side with players who are willing to run, they’re strong on the counter-attack. They’ve had a couple of difficult matches, but they’ve still played well in every game, causing problems for their opponents. We’ll need to be focused to get a result. We can’t afford to take even a single training session lightly, let alone a Europa League match. We must pick up points in every match we play. We need to be even more relaxed when we play. On some occasions, I still see them looking a bit tense. I can see things coming together, the training sessions themselves are going better. We’re not quite there yet in terms of consistently bringing our best to every match, but we’re on the right track. We have a squad with plenty of top-class players; our players are all good. 

“[Jeff] Ekhator will play tomorrow. We want to have an extra option up front. He’s a penalty-area striker, a physical centre-forward who gets into space well and is capable of making a loose ball playable. He’s a promising player. I expect him to have the right composure to play. People always talk about the striker. I mentioned a physical centre-forward to highlight a particular characteristic. Physical presence isn’t just about scoring goals but also about providing an option when the game gets scrappy or tight. He can be an extra option, a centre-forward allows you to develop other options alongside your Plan A. [Andrea] Cambiaso will play tomorrow. He’s a serious professional. I’m always watching him and judge him based on what he gives me. He knows that, I’ve told him, and I have complete faith in him. [Kenan] Yildiz’s recovery? It’s going very well. We need to give him space, wait for the right moment without taking any risks. Not having a player of his calibre leaves a gap that we’re forced to fill somehow.”

NORBERTO NETO

“Every competition is tough. We’re at Juventus and we know that wherever we take to the pitch, we have to do so to win. We have to fight to achieve that, that’s my aim, and everyone else’s too. Tomorrow’s match will be a very tough one. Omonia are a strong side. We’re talking about European competitions here, every match is a tough one. [Guglielmo] Vicario is a top-class goalkeeper, otherwise he wouldn’t be here. It’s a pleasure to work with him, with [Carlo] Pinsoglio and with the whole staff. 

“Seeing the determination of these lads is infectious, it’s very positive. We’re talking about Juventus – there are key players with great potential. We’re a team on the up, the league season is so long that there are bound to be different phases. We have to fight to find balance and consistency. What matters is commitment, and that’s certainly there in this environment. We know where we stand and what we represent. I’m here trying to achieve great results, and I sense the same drive all around me. When Juventus called me, I was at a point where I was waiting for something that would make sense for my career. Life has offered me this opportunity and I’m very pleased. There’s enthusiasm and pride in being part of this team and in being able to fight for the goals we’ve set ourselves.”
