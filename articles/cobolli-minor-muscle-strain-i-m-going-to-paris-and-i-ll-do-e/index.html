Cobolli, minor muscle strain: “I’m going to Paris and I’ll do everything I can to play”

TheTools · 30.10.2026 09:03 · source · permalink

The Roman tennis player updated fans via social media following the injury he sustained in Vienna during the match against Berrettini

“Tomorrow I’ll be heading to Paris and will do everything I can to play in the tournament.” An update on Flavio Cobolli’s physical condition has been posted on social media. The Roman tennis player himself has provided an update following the muscle injury he sustained during his match against Berrettini, which forced him to withdraw from the Vienna tournament: “I’ve had some medical scans, which revealed a minor muscle injury. Tomorrow I’ll be heading to Paris and will do everything I can to play in the tournament. I’m working with my team to get back to my best as soon as possible and tackle the challenges of the coming weeks.”

The race to the Finals

Cobolli is well on course for the ATP Finals in Turin (15–22 November) and has been selected by Volandri for the Davis Cup Finals, scheduled to take place in Bologna from 24 November.

Cobolli’s tears

The Roman, ranked number 6 in the world, had burst into tears after suffering a muscle injury during his match against Berrettini. “Those tears,” he wrote in an Instagram story, “weren’t from pain. They were from emotion, perhaps a bit of stress and the disappointment of having to leave the court when I just wanted to stay there. Don’t worry, I plan to be there in Paris. Thank you for all your support.”

Berrettini himself had tried to console his friend and colleague: “I’m really, really sorry for him. I’ve been through it so many times, perhaps even too many. That’s why I got emotional. I know how difficult it can be. I know how much you want to be in Turin and I know that the last thing you’d want is to get injured just a few weeks before such an important event. But let’s hope it’s nothing too serious.”
