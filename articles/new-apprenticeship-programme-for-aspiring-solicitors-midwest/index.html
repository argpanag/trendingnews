A new apprenticeship programme will allow aspiring solicitors to qualify without a third-level degree.

The Law Society of Ireland is launching a new route to for school leavers to pursue the career.

The Irish Examiner reports that trainees on the professional solicitor apprenticeship will earn while they learn, as they are paid for their work as part of the seven-year programme.

The new route has been designed to broaden access to the profession, and will be of equal standing to existing qualification pathways.
