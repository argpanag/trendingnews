Ex Emmerdale star investigated by ITV after police called

TheTools · 22.11.2026 13:45 · source · permalink

Actor at centre of probe as police confirm no arrests have been made

ITV has confirmed it is looking into allegations involving Coronation Street and former Emmerdale actor Jacob Roberts following a reported incident outside a Manchester city centre bar.

The 36 year old English actor has portrayed Detective Constable Kit Green on the ITV soap since 2024. According to the Sun newspaper, video footage purportedly shows Roberts shouting outside Kiely's Irish Bar in Manchester.

Roberts was reportedly attempting to enter the bar on 8 November, with staff alleging he behaved aggressively and used foul language when informed he could not enter as the establishment was closing.

Greater Manchester Police confirmed officers were called to a bar in Watson Street at approximately 2.30am following reports of a disturbance. No arrests were made, reports the Manchester Evening News.

An ITV spokesperson said: "We are investigating this matter and will be speaking to Jacob Roberts."

Roberts previously portrayed Damon Kinsella on the Channel 4 soap Hollyoaks from 2017 until 2023 before joining the cobbles in 2024. He has also featured in Emmerdale and recently appeared in the crossover episode Corriedale.

His storylines on Coronation Street have included receiving a diagnosis of male breast cancer as well as fathering a child following an illicit affair with his girlfriend's daughter.

Roberts' representatives have been contacted by the Press Association for comment.
