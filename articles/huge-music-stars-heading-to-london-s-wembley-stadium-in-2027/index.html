Huge music stars heading to London's Wembley Stadium in 2027

TheTools · 25.11.2026 12:19 · source · permalink

Some of the world's biggest music stars are heading to London's Wembley Stadium in 2027, with Harry Styles, Foo Fighters, Sam Fender, and more confirmed for 12 huge concerts.

The line-up also includes Noah Kahan and Slipknot, with several artists scheduled for multiple nights beneath the famous Wembley arch.

Harry Styles is set to return to the stadium after his record-breaking run of 12 concerts in 2026, while Foo Fighters will be bringing their Take Cover Tour to the capital in September.

Meanwhile, Sam Fender has announced three Wembley dates as part of his Proper Massive Stadium Tour.

With tickets already on sale for several performances, music fans are being encouraged to plan ahead for what promises to be another busy year at the national stadium.

Sam Fender

Sam Fender is preparing for three concerts at Wembley Stadium in 2027.

The North Shields musician, famous for hits like Seventeen Going Under, Hypersonic Missiles, and People Watching, will take to the stage on Friday, June 18, and Sunday, June 20.

He will then return for a third performance on Thursday, September 2.

The shows mark another major milestone for the singer, who has become one of Britain's most successful contemporary rock artists.

Fans can expect a blend of his biggest songs and more recent material.

Slipknot

American heavy metal band Slipknot will bring their (sic)est Stadium Tour to Wembley Stadium on Saturday, July 3.

The concert is anticipated to be one of the summer's biggest metal events, with the band known for their theatrical performances and high-energy live shows.

Slipknot rose to prominence in the late 1990s and has since built a worldwide following with songs including Duality, Psychosocial, and Before I Forget.

The Wembley performance will be part of the band's international stadium tour.

Noah Kahan

American singer-songwriter Noah Kahan has confirmed three consecutive nights at Wembley Stadium.

The Stick Season singer will perform on Friday, July 23, Saturday, July 24, and Sunday, July 25, as part of his The Last of the Bugs World Tour.

Kahan has enjoyed international success with his blend of folk and pop music, attracting millions of fans through songs such as Stick Season, Dial Drunk, and Northern Attitude.

The three Wembley dates will be among his biggest UK headline performances.

Fans are advised to check official ticketing websites for the latest availability, as demand for the shows is expected to be high.

Harry Styles

Harry Styles will return to Wembley Stadium for three nights in August 2027.

The former One Direction star will perform on Wednesday, August 25, Friday, August 27, and Saturday, August 28, as part of his Together, Together tour.

The concerts follow his record-breaking 12-night run at Wembley in summer 2026.

Styles is expected to perform songs from his fourth studio album, Kiss All the Time. Disco, Occasionally., alongside hits from his earlier solo career.

Fans could hear favourites including As It Was, Watermelon Sugar, Adore You, and Sign of the Times.

British pop duo Tears for Fears have been announced as special guests for all three Wembley performances.

The final concert on August 28 is currently listed as sold out on Wembley Stadium's official website.

Foo Fighters

American rock band Foo Fighters will bring their Take Cover Tour to Wembley Stadium for two nights in September.

The band, fronted by Dave Grohl, will perform on Thursday, September 9, and Saturday, September 11.

Known for songs including Everlong, The Pretender, Best of You, and Learn to Fly, Foo Fighters have built a reputation for their major stadium performances.

The Wembley concerts will form part of the band's 2027 tour, bringing their music back to one of London's most famous live music venues.

Concert list

The confirmed schedule currently includes 12 concerts across five headline acts.

Further performances could be announced as the year approaches.

How to get to Wembley Stadium for concerts

Wembley Stadium is in Wembley Park, north-west London, and is accessible by public transport.

The nearest Underground station is Wembley Park, served by the Jubilee and Metropolitan lines.

Wembley Central is also within walking distance and is served by the Bakerloo line, London Overground, and National Rail services.

Wembley Stadium railway station provides Chiltern Railways connections to London Marylebone.

Concertgoers are advised to use public transport where possible, as roads around the stadium can become particularly busy during major events.

Passengers should also check for planned engineering works or travel disruption before setting off.

Where can fans buy Wembley Stadium concert tickets?

Tickets for the confirmed 2027 concerts are available through official ticketing partners, subject to availability.

Some shows have already sold out or have limited ticket options remaining.

Wembley Stadium also offers hospitality packages for selected events.

Fans are advised to purchase tickets through official channels and check the terms of any resale tickets before buying.

Further information, including ticket availability and event details, can be found on Wembley Stadium's official website.
