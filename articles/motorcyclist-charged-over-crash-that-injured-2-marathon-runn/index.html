Motorcyclist charged over crash that injured 2 marathon runners

TheTools · 05.10.2026 08:05 · source · permalink

The 21-year-old faces two charges for driving under the influence.

Kuala Lumpur traffic police chief Zamzuri Isa said the accused faced two charges under Section 44(1A)(b) of the Road Transport Act 1987 for driving under the influence.

If found guilty, he can be jailed for between seven and 10 years, and fined between RM30,000 and RM50,000.

The court allowed bail of RM5,000 for each charge. However, the accused was believed to have failed to raise bail and was ordered to be sent to the satellite prison in Kuala Kubu Bharu.

The court fixed Jan 7 for mention of both charges.

Yesterday, it was reported that a motorcyclist and his 19-year-old pillion rider had allegedly crossed the marathon route on Jalan Munshi Abdullah, which had been marked by traffic cones.

A 50-year-old female participant sustained a neck injury and was taken to a hospital, while a 41-year-old male participant suffered injuries to his right knee and hand.
