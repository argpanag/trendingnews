Casualties continue to rise after Brooklyn building collapses

TheTools · 13.11.2026 05:53 · source · permalink

The Picture Show

The Picture Show

A view of the site where a building collapsed in Brooklyn, New York on Wednesday. Yuki Iwamura/AP hide caption

The New York City Fire Department reported Thursday that more victims had been found after a three-story residential building in the Flatbush neighborhood of Brooklyn collapsed a day earlier.

Mayor Zohran Mamdani said six people had been declared dead at the scene. Two others died in a hospital. 18 other people were injured in the building collapse.

Search efforts are continuing with more than 250 firefighters and emergency workers deployed to the site, according to city officials.
