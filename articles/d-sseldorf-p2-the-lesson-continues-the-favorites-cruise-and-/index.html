Düsseldorf P2 – The lesson continues: the favorites cruise and the semi-finals promise a spectacle | Padel Magazine

TheTools · 09.10.2026 18:03 · source · permalink

Le  The festival of favorites continues at Düsseldorf's P2. Before Friday's final quarter-final match, the top seeds not only lived up to expectations, but above all...  unfolded on the central German runway .  Alejandro Galán and Federico Chingotto  have returned to their best level, while in the women's category,  Triay/Brea and Sánchez/Ustero  They left no chance for their opponents. In the slow conditions of Düsseldorf, the favorites ruled the roost.

Galán / Chingotto are back to their best

After a particularly difficult round of 16 match against  Juani de Pascual and Javi Garrido ,  Alejandro Galán and Federico Chingotto (TS2)  were expected around the corner.

The day before, the Spanish-Argentine pair had to battle for three sets, conceding the first before finally pulling through.  7/5 in the last round A match during which Galán appeared to be struggling. The Spaniard even admitted after his quarter-final that he was  a little sick .

But this Friday, we found  Galán on his best days .

Facing the Argentinians  Maxi Sánchez Blasco and Sanyo Gutiérrez The number 2 players were practically never troubled. After a controlled first set, they completely dominated their opponents to win.  6/4 6/1 in just 1 hour 03 minutes A true demonstration that allows  Galán / Chingotto to reach the final four , after their premature elimination in Rotterdam.

They are now waiting to find out who their opponents will be, who will come from the last quarter-final of the day between  Franco Stupaczuk / Jon Sanz (TS4)  et  Martín Di Nenno / Paquito Navarro   (TS5).  

In the other part of the table,  Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia  also responded by dominating  Gonzalo Alfonso / Tino Libaak The world number ones will meet again in the semi-finals  Juan Lebrón and Leo Augsburger , fallers of  Maxi Arce / Juan Tello .

A poster that already promises a lot.

Sánchez / Ustero inflict a beating on Salazar / Osoro

In the ladies,  Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero (TS3)  have sent a very clear message to the competition.

Facing  Alejandra Salazar and Aranzazu Osoro (TS7) , the recent champions of  P2 Rotterdam  left no room for suspense. It must be said that Salazar and Osoro arrived particularly exhausted after their  marathon of over 3 hours 30 minutes in the round of 16 It's difficult, under these conditions, to compete with one of the most in-form pairs on the circuit.

Sánchez and Ustero had also had to go to a third set the day before, but their match had been significantly shorter. Result:  6/1 6/1 in just 50 minutes A real thrashing by the Spanish pair, who confirm their excellent momentum after their title in the Netherlands.

 Sánchez / Ustero have therefore reached another semi-final. , with the firm intention of continuing their series.

Triay / Brea do not give Jensen / Cánovas any chance

The  world number 1, Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea They too delivered a convincing performance.

Opposed to  Claudia Jensen and Noa Cánovas (TS8) The top players on the circuit quickly imposed their pace. Jensen, who continues to fill in for the absence of  Tamara Icardo Injured, she had managed to reach the quarter-finals with her new partner. But this time, the challenge was too great.

 Triay / Brea win 6/3 6/1 in 1h20 , without really trembling. A clear victory that allows them to once again  Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero , in what already looks like a final before the final.

Triay / Brea against Sánchez / Ustero: a revenge from Rotterdam

This is the poster that many were waiting for in the women's draw.

On the one hand,  Gemma Triay and Delfi Brea , the world's number 1 players. On the other hand,  Ari Sánchez and Andrea Ustero , probably the most in-form pair at the moment. In their last three encounters,  Triay/Brea lead two wins to one , including two semi-final victories at  London and Madrid .

But the last confrontation went in favor of Sánchez and Ustero. Just a few days ago, in the semi-final of the  P2 Rotterdam The Spanish women had delivered a remarkable performance to secure the victory.  6 / 2 6 / 3 , before going on to claim the title the next day.

This time, Triay and Brea will have the opportunity to take their revenge in Düsseldorf.  A semi-final that feels like a final , between the leaders of the world rankings and a pair that continues to gain momentum.

In the other women's semi-final,  Paula Josemaría / Bea González  will face  Martina Calvo / Claudia Fernández .

Rotterdam P2: Sánchez and Ustero outclass Triay / Brea and reach the final

In Düsseldorf, the favorites impose their will

Since the beginning of the week, the  particularly slow conditions at Düsseldorf P2  seem to be smiling on the top seeds.

The best pairs in the world manage to impose their rhythm, build their points and let their consistency speak for itself.

 Galán / Chingotto have rediscovered their form, Tapia / Coello are on track, Sánchez / Ustero continue to perform well and Triay / Brea confirm their status as world number 1. 

As we approach the final four,  The hierarchy is largely respected. And with the matchups taking shape, the semi-finals promise a spectacle of a whole new level.
