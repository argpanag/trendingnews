Senate inquiry over USD 6.3 million deal: What it means for the Tether USDt price

TheTools · 27.10.2026 07:33 · source · permalink

Senate inquiry and stablecoin supply

  • On October 22, 2026, lawmakers requested records concerning a USD 6.3 million arrangement involving Tether.
  • USDT balances across 19 platforms declined by USD 4.6 billion between early July and October 1, 2026.
  • The Tether USDt price stands at 0.9994 USD as of October 25, 2026, against the UTC daily close of October 24, 2026.
  • EU-licensed crypto firms face an October 8, 2027 deadline concerning unauthorized stablecoins.

Tether USDt (USDT) faces a Senate records request tied to a USD 6.3 million arrangement dated October 22, 2026. OneSafe reported that Senator Richard Blumenthal requested records by November 3, 2026, while the Tether USDt price stands at 0.9994 USD as of October 25, 2026.

USD 6.3 million arrangement draws Senate request

The October 22, 2026, letters sought contracts, payment records and communications related to the arrangement between Tether and Nxum Group, according to OneSafe. The request concerns whether the arrangement created a conflict or improper influence over stablecoin legislation, giving the inquiry a direct connection to the USD 6.3 million payment figure.

The same report said the request also covers Tether's policy engagement and the role of USDT in transactions linked to Iran sanctions. The date named for the requested response is November 3, 2026, while the inquiry remains a regulatory and political matter rather than a change to the token's dollar target.

USDT balances fell USD 4.6 billion by October 1

Balances of USDT across 19 major exchanges and lending platforms fell by USD 4.6 billion between early July and October 1, 2026, according to Crypto Briefing. The same report said total USDT supply declined 0.4 percent through September 2026 and stood near USD 183 billion on October 20, 2026.

A separate regulatory date concerns European Union trading access. Yahoo Finance reported that EU-licensed crypto firms have until January 8, 2027, to stop offering unauthorized stablecoins, and identified USDT as lacking approval under the Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation.

USDT trades at 0.9994 USD on October 25

According to Investing.com, Tether USDt trades at 0.9994 USD as of October 25, 2026. The daily high is 0.9995 USD and the daily low for the UTC day is 0.9989 USD. Market capitalization is 182.41 billion USD, ranking USDT third, while 24-hour volume is 51.59 billion USD and circulating supply is 182.64 billion USDT.
