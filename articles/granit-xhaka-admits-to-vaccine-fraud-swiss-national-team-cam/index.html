Granit Xhaka Admits to Vaccine Fraud – Swiss National Team Camp Without Captain

TheTools · 21.09.2026 10:32 · source · permalink

"It was a mistake"

Granit Xhaka has released a statement.

blue News

Swiss national team captain Granit Xhaka has admitted to obtaining a COVID-19 vaccination certificate in 2022. He will miss Switzerland’s upcoming Nations League matches. “It was a mistake,” says Xhaka.

Here's what it's all about

  • Granit Xhaka admitted in a statement on Monday that he purchased fake COVID-19 certificates in 2022.
  • “At the time, I was personally very uncertain and had serious reservations. I felt a deep mistrust toward the vaccine,” explains the national team captain.
  • The SFV wants to “calm the situation down.” Xhaka will miss the upcoming Nations League matches.

Swiss national team captain Granit Xhaka has admitted to obtaining a vaccination certificate during the COVID-19 pandemic. “At the time, I was personally very uncertain and had serious reservations. I felt a deep distrust of the vaccine and was plagued by fears and uncertainty,” Xhaka wrote on Instagram.

He continued: “In that state of emotional turmoil, I obtained a vaccination certificate instead of clarifying my unanswered questions through the proper channels. Today, I clearly see that this was not the right course of action. It was a mistake. I’m sorry.”

In a press release, the SFV issued the following statement regarding the matter: “Following his personal statement regarding his vaccination status, Granit Xhaka will not participate in the upcoming men’s national team training camp. This decision was made jointly during a discussion between Xhaka and the SFV leadership.”

Granit Xhaka will not be participating in the upcoming Swiss national team training camp.

KEYSTONE

Nations League Matches Without Xhaka

Xhaka informed the association of the situation on Sunday evening. “We welcome the fact that Granit is taking this step and facing up to the issue. At the same time, we have jointly concluded that it is in the team’s best interest to play this match without him. “For us, the priority now is to calm the situation and focus entirely on the team and the upcoming matches in the UEFA Nations League,” explains SFV President Peter Knäbel.

The national team is gathering today in Belek, Turkey, where it will prepare through Friday for its two away matches against North Macedonia and Scotland. On October 3 and 6, the national team will face Slovenia and North Macedonia in Lucerne. “Once this international break is over, we’ll reassess the situation together with Granit and discuss how to proceed,” said Knäbel.

In his statement, Xhaka also writes: “I take full responsibility for this decision, am prepared to address the facts of the matter, and will cooperate fully with the relevant authorities.”
