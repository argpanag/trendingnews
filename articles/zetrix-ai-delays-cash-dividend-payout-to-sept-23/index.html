Zetrix AI delays cash dividend payout to Sept 23

TheTools · 18.09.2026 10:55 · source · permalink

Have a question, feedback, or need assistance with your account?

KUALA LUMPUR (Sept 18): Main Market-listed digital services group Zetrix AI Bhd (KL:ZETRIX), formerly known as My E.G. Services Bhd, is postponing its cash dividend payout to shareholders.

This comes after the company’s share registrar received a sealed court injunction dated Sept 14, initiated by a substantial shareholder, Zetrix said in a bourse filing.

The legal action seeks to halt the disbursement of cash dividends to specific Central Depository System (CDS) accounts claimed by the substantial shareholder as beneficial owner. The shareholder, which Zetrix did not name, alleged that nominees had failed to execute instructions to elect to receive shares under the company’s Dividend Reinvestment Plan (DRP) in lieu of cash.

The company originally scheduled the payment of its final dividend of 2.89 sen per share for the financial year ended Dec 31, 2025 on Friday (Sept 18). The disbursement is now expected to be completed on or before Sept 23.
