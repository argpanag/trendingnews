PlayStation Plus Game Catalog Lineup for September 2026 Revealed

TheTools · 09.09.2026 15:48 · source · permalink

A new announcement lists the games coming to the Game Catalog for PlayStation Plus Extra and Premium members in September, with WWE 2K26 as the highlight.

PlayStation Plus Game Catalog is a library of PS4 and PS5 games that is available for PS Plus Extra and Premium subscribers, with new titles being added each month. In September, players will see 8 games added to the Game Catalog along with two Classics Catalog additions this month. Check out the full list below:

PlayStation Plus Game Catalog for September Features WWE 2K26

As revealed earlier, the PlayStation Plus Game Catalog gets 8 new games in September, but the biggest one on the list is WWE 2K26. It is the latest instalment to the series, bring the most expansive gameplay with Legends of the Attitude Era along with the Superstars of today. It has over 400 playable stars with iconic wrestlers like The Rock, Triple H, John Cena, Rhea Ripley and so many more.

Other highlights for the month of September are RuneScape: Dragonwilds, Ball x Pit, Ninja Gaiden: Ragebound and Metro Redux for the PS Plus Premium players. All the new additions will be available to play from September 15, check out the full list of games below:

September’s Game Catalog Titles

  • WWE 2K26 – PS5
  • RuneScape: Dragonwilds – PS5
  • Ball x Pit – PS5
  • Date Everything! – PS5
  • Slitterhead – PS5, PS4
  • Ninja Gaiden: Ragebound – PS5, PS4
  • Dungeons of Hinterberg – PS5, PS4
  • Sniper Elite: Resistance – PS5, PS4

September’s Classics Catalog Titles

  • Mega Man X Command Mission – PS5, PS4
  • Metro Redux – PS4
