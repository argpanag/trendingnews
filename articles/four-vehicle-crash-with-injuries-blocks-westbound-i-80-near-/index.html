Four-Vehicle Crash With Injuries Blocks Westbound I-80 Near Greenback Lane

A four-vehicle crash with injuries blocks westbound I-80 near Greenback Lane, causing significant traffic disruptions on westbound Interstate 80 near the Antelope truck scales in the Citrus Heights area on Wednesday morning, August 19, 2026, according to California Highway Patrol dispatch records.

Four Vehicles Involved in Morning I-80 Crash

The collision was reported at approximately 8:56 a.m.

Initial CHP reports indicated that the crash blocked the No. 2 and No. 3 lanes of westbound I-80. As emergency personnel responded, the incident expanded to affect additional lanes.

By approximately 9:06 a.m., all westbound lanes were reported closed while crews worked at the scene.

At Least One Person Extricated From Vehicle

Personnel from the Sacramento Metropolitan Fire District responded to assist those involved in the collision.

At approximately 9:15 a.m., firefighters reportedly extricated at least one person from a vehicle.

Authorities have not released information regarding how many people were injured or the nature and severity of their injuries.

Crash Causes Heavy Westbound Congestion

The lane closures resulted in significant traffic backups along westbound I-80. A Caltrans traffic camera showed heavy congestion shortly before 9:30 a.m.

Information regarding when all westbound lanes reopened was not immediately available.

Investigation Ongoing

The initial incident information did not establish what caused the four vehicles to collide.

Authorities continue to investigate the circumstances surrounding the crash, and additional details may become available as the investigation progresses.
