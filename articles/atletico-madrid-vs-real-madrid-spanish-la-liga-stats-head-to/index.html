  15. 'Every derby is special in every city'published at 15:14 BST
    15:14 BST

    Atletico Madrid v Real Madrid (15:15 BST)

    Real Madrid boss Jose Mourinho was asked yesterday how the Madrid derby compares to others he has been involved with.

    "When you wear different club shirts, it straight away changes your mindset," he said.

    "You either isolate yourself from the outside world or you stay there, get the message and realise every derby is special in every city. They have a special moment.

    "For example, Inter and Milan, you can feel a certain hate maybe from the outside but Milan and Inter have a great relationship. You could see Inter and Milan fans shake each others hands, in Portugal it’s absolutely impossible. In Turkey, even less so."
