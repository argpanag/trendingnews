Pedestrian dies after being struck by a car in Limerick

TheTools · 08.11.2026 22:41 · source · permalink

A pedestrian has died after being struck by a car in Co Limerick.

It happened on the M20 motorway northbound between Junction 3 (Raheen) and Junction 2 (Dooradoyle) at around 5.20pm.

The pedestrian was pronounced dead at the scene and no other injuries were reported.

The road remains closed for a technical examination, and local diversions are in place.

Gardaí are appealing for any witnesses to come forward.

They want to hear from any road users who have camera footage from the M20 between Junctions 3 and 2 from 5pm to 5.30pm.

Anyone with any information is asked to contact Henry Street Garda Station on 061 212400, the Garda Confidential Line on 1800 666 111, or any Garda Station.
