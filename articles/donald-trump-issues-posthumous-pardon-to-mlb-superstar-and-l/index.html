Donald Trump issues posthumous pardon to MLB ‘superstar and legend’ Pete Rose

TheTools · 19.11.2026 22:09 · source · permalink

  • Rose pleaded guilty to tax evasion in 1990

  • Player also banned from baseball over bets

Rose pleaded guilty to tax evasion in 1990

Player also banned from baseball over bets

Donald Trump says he has posthumously pardoned Pete Rose more than two years after the baseball star’s death and a year after he was reinstated by Major League Baseball Commissioner Rob Manfred.

“I have just given a Complete and Total Pardon to the Late, Great Pete Rose, a true Baseball SUPERSTAR + LEGEND,” Trump posted on social media.

Rose, MLB’s career hits leader, entered guilty pleas in April 1990 to two counts of filing false tax returns, admitting he failed to report $354,968 during a four-year period. Facing a sentence of up to six years in prison and $500,000 in fines, Rose was sentenced to five months in prison. Rose also repaid the Internal Revenue Service $366,042.

The pleas came eight months after Rose agreed to a permanent ban from baseball. He agreed to the ban after an MLB investigation concluded he bet numerous times on the Cincinnati Reds while playing on and managing his hometown team from 1985-87. ″I know I’m not a bad person, but I did make some mistakes and it’s in the eyes of the judge,″ Rose said on the day he was banned from baseball. ″All you can say is it’s up to the judge.″

The pardon comes after Manfred issued a sweeping decision last May that opened a pathway for Rose to be considered for baseball’s Hall of Fame. Manfred declared that individuals on MLB’s permanently ineligible list would have their statuses restored at death, a ruling that affected 17 people, most notably Rose and Shoeless Joe Jackson. The earliest Rose or Jackson could now be inducted into the Hall of Fame would be in 2028.

A 17-time All-Star during a playing career from 1963-86, Rose was the 1963 National League Rookie of the Year, 1973 MVP and 1975 World Series MVP. A three-time NL batting champion, he broke the prior hits record of 4,191 set by Ty Cobb from 1905-28.
