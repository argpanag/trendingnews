Shanghai Masters Day Six Features Three Key Matchups

TheTools · 12.10.2026 04:31 · source · permalink

The remaining Round of 32 matches at the 2026 Shanghai Masters feature several notable contests, including Rafael Jodar against Jiri Lehecka, Casper Ruud against Learner Tien, and Taylor Fritz against Stefanos Tsitsipas. According to Last Word on Sports, Lehecka, Ruud, and Tsitsipas are favored to advance in three sets based on their recent form and the playing conditions.

Lehecka faces Jodar in a matchup expected to be competitive, while Ruud takes on Tien in a contest between two players who have yet to establish convincing form. Fritz and Tsitsipas headline the day's schedule in what the publication identifies as a potential standout match.

Jiri Lehecka faces Rafael Jodar

Lehecka enters his match against Jodar with stronger recent form, according to Last Word on Sports. Both players are considered suited to the conditions in Shanghai, but Lehecka's greater consistency gives him the edge in a contest expected to remain close.

The preview predicts that Lehecka will win in three sets, with his steadier performance expected to help him navigate a difficult encounter.

Casper Ruud meets Learner Tien

Ruud and Tien face each other in a matchup that could hinge on attacking execution and consistency. Last Word on Sports notes that the faster playing conditions are not ideal for either player's game, making the Norwegian's ability to generate effective forehand attacks particularly important.

Ruud has the offensive tools to dictate rallies if his forehand is working effectively. Tien, meanwhile, is expected to rely on consistency to remain competitive and challenge Ruud throughout the match.

The publication favors Ruud to prevail in three sets, although it anticipates a closely contested match. His ability to take the initiative could prove decisive against Tien's more consistency-focused approach.

Taylor Fritz takes on Stefanos Tsitsipas

Fritz and Tsitsipas meet in one of the day's most anticipated matches, with their head-to-head record and recent performances shaping the preview's assessment. Fritz trails in their head-to-head series, while Tsitsipas showed encouraging signs of improvement at the US Open.

Both players can compete effectively from the baseline, but Last Word on Sports considers Tsitsipas the more reliable option based on his recent performances. The Greek player is projected to have a slight advantage in what could be a tightly contested encounter.

The prediction favors Tsitsipas to win in three sets, giving him the edge over Fritz in one of the Shanghai Masters' featured Round of 32 matches.

These three contests form part of the remaining Round of 32 schedule on Day 6 of the Shanghai Masters, with the tournament continuing its progression through the men's singles draw.
