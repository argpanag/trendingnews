Goalkeeper who caused the death of an opponent in a "shocking kick" appears at the deceased player's memorial service: "I am suffering beyond words..." First public appearance since the incident | STARNEWS

FC Andorra goalkeeper Jesús Owono (25), who caused the death of an opposing attacker in a reckless collision during a match, made his first public appearance since the tragic accident. He attended the late player's memorial service.

The British media outlet "The Sun" reported on the 21st (Korean time) that goalkeeper Owono was captured attending a memorial event honoring the deceased for the first time following the horrific collision that led to the death of Tenerife attacker Jesús de Miguel.

According to the report, Owono appeared at the de Miguel memorial service held recently at Encamp Stadium, Andorra's home ground. This is the first time Owono has stood before the public since the accident, during which he was in a state of extreme shock and even considered retiring from professional football.

According to the Spanish media outlet "Marca," Owono arrived at the stadium wearing dark clothing and a black beanie. As the Andorra squad lined up on the pitch holding jerseys bearing de Miguel's name and white floral tributes, Owono was seen bowing his head and praying earnestly for the deceased even after the one-minute moment of silence concluded.

It was a tragedy. In the second-half stoppage time of the Spanish second-division match between Tenerife and Andorra on the 2nd, which was tied at 2-2, Owono, who had ventured deep into midfield to deal with an aerial ball, jumped with his knees and legs raised high, striking de Miguel squarely in the chest as he charged toward the ball.

De Miguel received immediate first aid at the scene but suffered cardiac arrest inside the ambulance before being urgently transferred to an intensive care unit at a nearby hospital. However, his condition deteriorated rapidly, and he ultimately passed away at the young age of 30 less than 24 hours after the incident. It is also known that the deceased's wife and their 16-month-old daughter were present in the stands at the time of the accident.

Subsequently, Owono received a 12-match suspension, the maximum penalty under regulations, from the disciplinary committee of the Royal Spanish Football Federation (RFEF). He is also undergoing police investigation for negligent homicide under the supervision of judicial authorities, as required by mandatory procedures under Spanish criminal law.

Owono, who had closed his personal social media accounts and abstained from team training to seclude himself immediately after the incident, confessed his devastated state of mind in an official statement released through his club last week. At the time, Owono stated, "I am going through the hardest days of my life. I am at a loss for words and it is incredibly difficult to accept everything that has happened," adding, "I feel an indescribable, immense pain over losing de Miguel. I am thinking only of him, his family, and everyone who loved him."

He continued, "There will be many issues to deal with going forward. Whatever happens, I will face it with the utmost respect," while also stating, "Right now, I cannot think of anything other than de Miguel and the pain we are all experiencing. May he rest in peace," expressing his condolences for the deceased.

For now, Carles Manso, manager of Owono's club FC Andorra, supported the player by stating, "The timing of his return will be decided entirely by Owono himself."
