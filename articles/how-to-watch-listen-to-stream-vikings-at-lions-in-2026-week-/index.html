How to Watch, Listen to & Stream Vikings at Lions in 2026 Week 8 Game

TheTools · 01.11.2026 09:12 · source · permalink

EAGAN, Minn. — The Vikings (6-0) are visiting the Detroit Lions (4-2) at noon (CT) Sunday, Nov. 1.

This will be the 130th official meeting between the franchises.

The contest will be broadcast to a northern swath of the country by FOX.

BROADCAST TV

MAP KEY: Vikings at Lions (red areas); Cardinals at Cowboys (blue areas); Raiders at Jets (yellow areas); Falcons at Buccaneers (orange areas); Chargers at Rams (green areas)

FOX (KMSP in the Twin Cities market) will televise the game.

Kickoff: Noon (CT)

Play-by-Play: Kevin Burkhardt

Analyst: Tom Brady

Sideline reporters: Erin Andrews & Tom Rinaldi

PREGAME

Vikings GameDay Live will air on FOX 9 in the Twin Cities market from 9:30 a.m. to 11 a.m. (CT).
