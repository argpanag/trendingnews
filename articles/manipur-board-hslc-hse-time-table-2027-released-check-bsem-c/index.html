Manipur Board HSLC, HSE Time Table 2027 Released! Check BSEM class 10 & COHSEM class 12 date sheet here - The Times of India

TheTools · 11.11.2026 10:34 · source · permalink

Manipur Board HSLC & HSE Time Table 2027: The Council of Higher Secondary Education Manipur (COHSEM) and the Board of Secondary Education Manipur (BSEM) have released the Manipur Board Time Table 2027 for the upcoming Class 10 (HSLC) and Class 12 (HSE) final board exams.

Students who are preparing to appear for the Manipur Board exam 2027 can now check the subject-wise exam schedule on the official websites. According to the official date sheets, the Manipur Board exams 2027 HSLC & HSE are scheduled to commence in February 2027. The Class 10 HSLC exams Manipur Board will be conducted first, followed by the Manipur Board HSE exam 2027 class 12.

As per the official timetable, the Manipur Board HSLC 10th Exams 2027 will be held from February 10 to February 25, 2027.

On the other hand, the Manipur HSE 12th Exam Dates 2027 will begin on February 11 and conclude on March 19, 2027.

In addition to the theory exam dates, the board has also released the HSE Class 12 practical assessments date. The COHSEM intermediate practical exams will be conducted between January 2 and 30, 2027. Candidates can access and download the Manipur Board date sheet 2027 PDF files for both classes by visiting cohsem.nic.in and bsem.nic.in.
