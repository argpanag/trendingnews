Wolvaardt, McGrath power Adelaide Strikers to five-wicket win

TheTools · 21.11.2026 01:13 · source · permalink

It took 11 fours and a six from Laura Wolvaardt in her 58-ball 91 and a partnership between her and Tahlia McGrath (46 off 35) to overcome the first-innings efforts of Sophie Molineux (63 off 38). The Wolvaardt-McGrath duo added 100 runs off 66 balls during the Adelaide Strikers' successful chase after the Melbourne Renegades posted 165/4.

After losing a couple of early wickets, the Renegades' innings was built brick by brick by their skipper. Molineux put together two vital partnerships - 57 off 41 with Courtney Webb and 54 off 28 with Georgia Wareham. She tonked eight fours and two sixes and was instrumental in her side adding 65 runs off the last 30 balls. Renegades were also pushed ahead by a blitzy cameo from Georgia Wareham (27 off 16), which included hitting four fours off Anesu Mushangwe in the 16th over.

However, all of that was rendered moot by Wolvaardt and McGrath. Strikers too had a wobbly start, losing openers Emma de Broughe and Maia Bouchier in the PowerPlay. But those early wickets brought Wolvaardt and McGrath together for a game-turning alliance. When Molineux finally broke through by cleaning up McGrath in the 16th over, the Strikers needed 38 off 4.1 overs.

Renegades made a late push for a heist as Madeline Penna was dismissed in the 17th over by Wareham, which yielded only three runs. With 30 runs needed from 18 balls, Wolvaardt took charge, attacking Tayla Vlaeminick for a 16-run over. Molineux conceded 13 in the 19th over, which tipped the scales in favour of the chasing side. The equation went from 7 runs off 6 balls to 2 runs off 3 balls when Deandra Dottin dismissed Wolvaardt but it was too little too late as Eleanor Larosa took her team over the line.

Brief Scores: Melbourne Renegades Women 165/4 in 20 overs (Sophie Molineux 63*) lost to Adelaide Strikers 168/5 in 19.5 overs (Laura Wolvaardt 91, Tahlia McGrath 46; Georgia Wareham 2-17) by 5 wickets
