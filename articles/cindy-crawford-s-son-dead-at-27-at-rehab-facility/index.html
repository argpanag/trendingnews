Cindy Crawford's son dead at 27 at rehab facility

TheTools · 21.09.2026 12:14 · source · permalink

Cindy Crawford’s son, Presley Gerber, died Sunday at the age of 27.

TMZ reports, citing the Los Angeles County Medical Examiner, Gerber died at a rehab facility.

“The family is asking for privacy during this very difficult and painful time,” a family representative told TMZ.

An autopsy has yet to be completed.

Crawford’s husband Rande Gerber, and their daughter Kaia Gerber both confirmed to TMZ that Presley Gerber died.

According to the report, Presley was arrested for a DUI in 2019, then was sentenced to three years of probation and ordered to complete a DUI programme, as well as two days of community service, later that year. – Advance Local Media LLC/TNS
