KALISPELL, Mont. — As open burning is set to resume Oct. 1, 2026, weather permitting, officials are reminding residents that anyone planning to burn must first obtain a permit.

Burn permits expire each calendar year on Dec. 31. Officials say the permit system helps them monitor how much open burning is happening and where it is taking place.

By tracking burning activity through the permit system, officials say they will be able to allow open burning into the winter months of December, January and February when opportunities arise, depending on weather conditions.
