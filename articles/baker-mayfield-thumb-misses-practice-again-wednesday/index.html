Baker Mayfield (thumb) misses practice again Wednesday

TheTools · 07.10.2026 14:50 · source · permalink

by Nate Miller | Buccaneers Correspondent | Wed, Oct 7th 2026, 10:50am EDT

Baker Mayfield (thumb) sat out Wednesday's practice session. (Greg Auman on Twitter )

Fantasy Impact:

With the Buccaneers playing Thursday night, Mayfield is on track to miss a second straight game due to a thumb injury, as he has not practiced this week. Jalon Daniels is in line to start at QB once again.

Rain has Bucs indoors again this morning before their flight to Dallas.
