BBC Young Musician 2026 finalists announced

TheTools · 02.11.2026 00:36 · source · permalink

Recorder player Alma Nunez Debretzeni, saxophonist Kezia Colton and violinist Sofia Demetriades will appear in the final

From hundreds of entries, just three talented young classical musicians remain as BBC Young Musician 2026 reaches its conclusion.

The three finalists set to compete for the title of BBC Young Musician 2026 are:

  • Recorder player Alma Nunez Debretzeni (17) from St Albans, who studies at Parmiter’s School in Garston, Hertfordshire, and at the Royal Academy of Music Junior Department in London.
  • Saxophonist Kezia Colton (18) from London, who studied at the Royal College of Music Junior Department in London before recently taking up a course at CRR Conservatoire à rayonnement régional de Paris - Ida Rubinstein.
  • Violinist Sofia Demetriades (18) from London, who studies at the Royal Academy of Music in London and Beare’s Chamber Music.

Alma, Kezia and Sofia were announced as finalists on Sunday 25 October during the Young Musician semi-final which aired on BBC Four and BBC iPlayer, having been recorded in July at Swansea University's Bay Campus. They progressed from a group of five semi-finalists to secure their places in the Final.

The Final was recorded at Bristol Beacon on Thursday 15 October and will be broadcast on BBC Two and BBC iPlayer, BBC Radio 3 and BBC Sounds on Sunday 1 November. Presented by broadcaster and writer Petroc Trelawny, who fronts all rounds of BBC Young Musician 2026, the three finalists will compete for the title by performing a concerto accompanied by the BBC National Orchestra of Wales, conducted by Alpesh Chauhan.

Judging every televised round of this year's competition are pianist and composer Joanna MacGregor and conductor Ben Gernon, and for the final they are joined by celebrated composer and current Master of the King’s Music, Errollyn Wallen. Together, the trio of judges will crown the winner of BBC Young Musician 2026.

BBC Young Musician 2026: The Final airs on BBC Two and BBC iPlayer, and BBC Radio 3 and BBC Sounds, on Sunday 1 November from 7pm until 9pm.

BBC Young Musician 2026 is produced by BBC Studios Music and Entertainment Wales. The Commissioning Editor is Stephen James-Yeoman.
