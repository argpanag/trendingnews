The medical board will publish the NEET PG 2026 result on September 30 and to qualify the post MBBS examination, general and Economically Weaker Section (EWS) category candidates will have to score 50th percentile, while general person with disability (PwD) will have to secure 45th percentile.

Those in the Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), and Other Backward Classes (OBC) category, must obtain 40th percentile to clear the NEET PG 2026 exam.

As per the marking scheme, candidates will be given 4 marks for each correct answer key, and one mark will be deducted for each wrong response. No marks will be awarded for unanswered questions.
