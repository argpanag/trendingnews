Kuwait faces Iraq to keep Gulf 27 semifinal hopes alive - kuwaitTimes

TheTools · 26.09.2026 09:58 · source · permalink

JEDDAH: Kuwait's national football team faces Iraq on Saturday in the second round of Group A at the Arabian Gulf Cup (Gulf 27) in Jeddah. The team is looking to recover from its opening defeat and stay in contention for a semifinal place.

The Blues, as the Kuwaiti squad is known, lost 1-0 to Saudi Arabia in their first match, while Iraq holds a single point after a 1-1 draw with Oman. Kick-off at Prince Abdullah Al-Faisal Stadium is at 6:55 pm.

The group standings make the match important for both sides. Kuwait is seeking a positive result to improve its position before its final group match against Oman on Tuesday.

In the group's other match, Saudi Arabia plays Oman at 9 pm at King Abdullah Sports City Stadium. The Saudi side, known as the Greens, leads the group with three points from its win over Kuwait, while Oman has one point from its draw with Iraq. — KUNA
