Afghanistan vs India 2026: Match preview, head-to-head and streaming details

TheTools · 13.09.2026 06:30 · source · permalink

India have dominated the T20I head-to-head record against Afghanistan, going unbeaten in nine games.

The first T20I of the Afghanistan vs India 2026 is all set to be held today, September 13, at 7 PM, at the Arun Jaitley Stadium in Delhi.

Afghanistan, who have been designated as the official host for this three-match T20I series at the Arun Jaitley Stadium in Delhi, will be striving for their first victory against India in men’s T20Is. The Afghan side is led by Ibrahim Zadran and features a world-class bowling assault that includes Rashid Khan, Mujeeb Ur Rahman, Noor Ahmad, and veteran Mohammad Nabi. Afghanistan boasts the tactical firepower to exploit spin-friendly conditions and disturb any top-order lineup, led by high-octane explosive openers Rahmanullah Gurbaz and dynamic pace alternatives Fazalhaq Farooqi and Naveen-ul-Haq.

India start the series with a significantly overhauled lineup led by Shreyas Iyer, with Tilak Varma acting as vice-captain. The team mixes raw aggressive purpose with vast batting depth, boasting a dynamic combination of explosive top-order hitters like Abhishek Sharma and Sanju Samson as well as exceptional all-rounders like Axar Patel and Shivam Dube. India, led by Jasprit Bumrah and Arshdeep Singh, as well as a potent spin combination of Varun Chakaravarthy, Ravi Bishnoi, and Washington Sundar, hopes to extend its overwhelming bilateral home run and preserve a perfect T20I head-to-head record against Afghanistan.

Head-to-Head:

India have dominated the T20I head-to-head record against Afghanistan, going unbeaten in nine games. India have won eight of these nine matches, while one has resulted in a draw. Afghanistan is still looking for their first T20I victory over the Men in Blue, while India has an 89% win rate in this series.

Where to watch:

TV: Sony Sports Network

OTT: FanCode
