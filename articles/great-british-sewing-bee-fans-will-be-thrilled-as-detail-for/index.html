Great British Sewing Bee fans will be thrilled as detail for next series dropped

TheTools · 09.09.2026 11:49 · source · permalink

There's good news for fans of Sophie Willan as it looks as though the star is set to return for another series of The Great British Sewing Bee. It comes after BBC viewers saw her make her debut on the beloved programme earlier this year, as she replaced Sara Pascoe.

Ahead of the upcoming final of the show, which will see either Anna, Emma, or Beth emerge victorious in this year's Great British Sewing Bee, judge Patrick Grant spoke to Morning Live about what viewers can expect to see.

However, while speaking about the anticipated final, the discussion quickly turned to Sophie, who joined the show as the latest presenter. Over the years, the programme has seen a plethora of faces front the series, including the likes of Claudia Winkleman, Joe Lycett and Kiell Smith-Bynoe, reports the Mirror.

As the Morning Live star noted that the series remains 'fresh' throughout with the introduction of new hosts, Patrick agreed, as he praised Sophie, calling her 'amazing'.

Admitting he was a 'massive fan' of her programme Alma's Not Normal, Patrick shared that he had to 'pinch' himself when learning she was joining the show as the new host.

Sharing that his fellow judge Esme also 'loves' Sophie, Patrick let slip they are currently filming the next series of the show already, hinting Sophie will be back as he said he 'loves going to work with her every day'.

During her debut, Sophie was seen preparing to make her introduction at the start of the episode in a hilarious sketch. When a producer arrived to tell the star, "Got the most amazing idea for the pre-title sketches. We're gonna dress you as a giant bobbin and roll you in."

Another member of the crew could then be heard saying: "Ok, Sophie, standby! Don't forget, once you've introduced the challenge, head straight to the haberdashery to talk applique."

Although Sophie looked confused, Esme jokingly cut in, telling: "Wing it. I've been winging it for years."

It wasn't long before people commented on Sophie fronting the show, as many are thrilled with the latest addition. One person on X said: "Sophie's a great new presenter on #SewingBee." Another added: "I already prefer Sophie Willan to all the other hosts," while a third said: "Great presenter," alongside a smiley face emoji.

A fourth fan echoed the sentiment, saying: "Loving the new presenter and sewers this year."

Morning Live is available to watch weekdays on BBC One from 9:30am. The Great British Sewing Bee final airs Tuesday, September 15, on BBC One at 8pm .
