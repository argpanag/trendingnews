Police seek information on missing 13-year-old girl last seen near Geylang apartments

TheTools · 23.10.2026 01:00 · source · permalink

The police are appealing for information on the whereabouts of a 13-year-old girl, Rachel Pun Ziqi, who was last seen in Geylang.

Police said in a news release on Friday (Oct 23) that Rachel was last seen at about 11.35pm on Thursday in the vicinity of Block 8 Lorong 39 Geylang, wearing a light-coloured t-shirt and dark-coloured pants.

The address is the location for the 112-unit residential development Sims Residences.

Anyone with information is urged to contact the police hotline at 1800-255-0000 or submit information online at www.police.gov.sg/i-witness.

All information will be kept strictly confidential.
