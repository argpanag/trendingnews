PL confirm wrongful Everton penalty call

TheTools · 18.11.2026 21:00 · source · permalink

 

The Premier League has ruled Everton should have conceded a penalty in their win over Coventry City.

David Moyes’ side headed into the international break on a high after their 3-1 win over the top flight newcomers at Hill Dickinson Stadium on November 6.

But the Blues were afforded a reprieve late in the first half as the visitors appealed for a penalty after James Tarkowski pulled Caleb Yirenkyi’s hair.

Referee Stuart Attwell did not flag the incident while it was also not picked up by VAR, much to the frustration of Sky Blues’ manager Frank Lampard.

The Premier League’s latest Key Match Incident Panel has now concluded that the visitors were wrongfully denied a spot kick during the incident.

A unanimous ruling deemed that Tarkowski should have conceded a penalty and received a booking for the pull on Yirenkyi in line with league guidelines.

“Tarkowski had hold of Yirenkyi’s hair as the Coventry attacker attempted to challenge for the ball,” read the panel’s verdict.

“The panel unanimously felt that this was a punishable offence and that a penalty and a yellow card should have been awarded under the new guidelines for hair pulling actions this season.

“They also felt that VAR should have intervened, 0:5, as the non-award of a penalty kick was a clear and obvious error.”

Everton’s second goal of the game from Thierno Barry was also scrutinised amid claims of handball as the forward met Tyrique George’s far-post cross.

VAR checked the incident for potential infringement which the panel voted 2-3 as an incorrect on-field call and unanimously agreed VAR was not required.

They stated: “On the balance of probability the ball did accidentally strike Barry’s hand.

“However, the readily available footage did not provide conclusive evidence.”
