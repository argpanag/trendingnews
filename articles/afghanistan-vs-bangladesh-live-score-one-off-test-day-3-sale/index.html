Afghanistan Vs Bangladesh Live Score, One-Off Test, Day 3: BAN 346 & 44/2 (10.4) At Lunch

Bangladesh remain in control at lunch on Day 3, with a 44/2 score in their second innings after bowling Afghanistan out for 240.

Tanzid Hasan Tamim made an aggressive start, smashing Yamin Ahmadzai for four boundaries in an over, but Afghanistan fought back with Saleem Safi dismissing Shadman Islam before Mominul Haque fell just before the break. With Bangladesh leading by 150 runs overall, Afghanistan need more wickets after lunch to prevent the visitors from building a commanding advantage.

Afghanistan Vs Bangladesh Live Score, One-Off Test, Day 3: BAN 346 & 31/1 (7.4)

Saleem Safi gets the breakthrough for Afghanistan as Shadman Islam falls for 13. Attempting to pull a short delivery, the Bangladesh opener mistimes his shot off the toe-end, sending it straight to Rahmat Shah at deep square-leg.

Afghanistan Vs Bangladesh Live Score, One-Off Test, Day 3: BAN Innings

Bangladesh are out to bat, with Shadman Islam and Tanzid Hasan Tamim opening the innings. Shadman will face the first ball, with Yamin Ahmadzai handed the new ball as Afghanistan look to make early inroads.

Afghanistan Vs Bangladesh Live Score, One-Off Test, Day 3: AFG 223/9 (86.5)

Hasan Mahmud completes his five-wicket haul after Yamin Ahmadzai is caught behind by Litton Das following a successful review. UltraEdge confirms the outside edge, overturning the on-field decision. Mahmud also becomes the fastest Bangladesh pace bowler to reach 50 Test wickets.

Afghanistan Vs Bangladesh Live Score, One-Off Test, Day 3: AFG 220/7 (82.4)

Hasan Mahmud strikes again! Qais Ahmad departs for a golden duck after edging a back-of-a-length delivery to Litton Das, who pulls off a stunning one-handed diving catch to his right. Afghanistan lose another wicket as Mahmud picks up two in two balls.

Afghanistan Vs Bangladesh Live Score, One-Off Test, Day 3: WICKET

Hasan Mahmud strikes! Ismat Alam is caught off guard by a delivery that keeps unusually low, sneaking under his bat to crash into the lower part of the off-stump. The Afghan batter can only look on in disbelief as Bangladesh pick up a crucial wicket.

Ismat departs for 1 off four balls, leaving Afghanistan five down as their pursuit of Bangladesh's first-innings total takes another hit.

Afghanistan Vs Bangladesh Live Score, One-Off Test, Day 3 - Underway

Day 3 is underway, and Ikram Alikhil wastes no time in getting going! Mehidy Hasan Miraz returns to the attack, but Alikhil welcomes him with a cracking four through cover-point.

A short delivery outside off is cut crisply by the Afghan batter, who rocks back and sends the ball racing to the fence. A positive start for Afghanistan as they look to reduce Bangladesh's lead.

Afghanistan Vs Bangladesh Live Score, One-Off Test, Day 3: Day 2 Highlights

Day 2 of the one-off Test between Afghanistan and Bangladesh saw the visitors bowled out for 346 after their tailenders added crucial runs in the morning. Afghanistan's top order struggled against Bangladesh's disciplined bowling, slipping to 139/4 after Sediqullah Atal, Kamal, Abdul Malik and Hashmatullah Shahidi fell.

Rahmat Shah then steadied the innings with an unbeaten 84 off 186 balls, while Ikram Alikhil remained not out on 26. Their unbroken 72-run partnership helped Afghanistan reach 211/4 at stumps, leaving them 135 runs behind Bangladesh, with Rahmat closing in on a century.
