Irdai Forms Expert Panel to Examine 2.18 L Responses

Mumbai: The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Irdai) has set up an expert committee to examine the 2.18 lakh responses received to its consultation paper on insurance distribution reforms. The panel will review the proposals, including their potential impact on agents' incomes and employment, commission limits on smaller-premium policies, transition measures for smaller insurers and distributors, and ways to expand insurance coverage in rural areas and smaller towns.

"The Committee's role is to help make the framework as robust, clear and effective as possible. Its work will complement the ongoing engagement Irdai maintains with all segments of the industry," said Ajay Seth chairman Irdai.
