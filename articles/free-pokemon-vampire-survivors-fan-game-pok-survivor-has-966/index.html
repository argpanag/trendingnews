Free Pokemon Vampire Survivors Fan Game PokéSurvivor Has 966 Pokemon - Shane the Gamer

TheTools · 13.10.2026 05:45 · source · permalink

PokéSurvivor, a free Pokemon fan game built on the Vampire Survivors formula, went live on 11 October 2026 with 966 playable Pokemon from all nine regions, Kanto through Paldea. Developer JPTER78 made it to run straight in a web browser at pokesurvivor.com, with nothing to download, no ads, and no purchases.

It plays on PC and on phones and tablets held in landscape, and can be added to a home screen like an app. You get online co-op for up to four friends, shinies, legendary fights, and a gacha, in Spanish or English depending on your browser. After JPTER78’s trailer spread on X.

It is also a different game from Pokémon Survivor Burst, a separate Vampire Survivors-style fan project from developer ClessioTV that released on PC, Mac, and mobile in August.

How PokéSurvivor Plays

A run starts with a personality test that assigns your partner Pokemon. The idea comes from the Mystery Dungeon series, whose sprite style the game uses and whose Spanish name, Mundo Misterioso, appears in its subtitle. If you don’t like the test’s pick, you can swap to any region’s starter or two extra options. From there you roam an endless map while your Pokemon fires its active move automatically, so your job is movement and dodging. On PC that means WASD or the arrow keys, with 1 to 4 or Q and E switching moves. On mobile, a joystick appears wherever you put your finger.

Collecting experience levels you up, and each level offers three cards: a new move, an upgrade to a move you have, or a boost to your Pokemon. Moves evolve at max level with the right upgrade, so Ember becomes Fire Blast and Bubble becomes Surf. The type chart applies, weather changes the matchups (rain strengthens Water moves and weakens Fire), and held items such as Leftovers, Choice Band, and Life Orb turn up during runs. Enemies have roles too: some heal, shield, or summon help, and Meowth sometimes run off carrying coins.

Legendaries live behind rifts, portals that open occasionally and pull you into one of 18 arenas, one per type. Win and you leave with rewards; lose and you return to the main map. Since the 11 October update, each legendary you beat also grants a Bond, which fuses two moves so they fire together from one slot. You can have up to four Bonds at once, which leaves room to carry more moves than the four slots would normally hold.

Gacha Tickets, Shiny Odds, and What It Costs

Gacha usually means spending real money, but PokéSurvivor’s pixel-art slot machine runs only on tickets earned in play. Wild Pokemon occasionally drop single tickets, and bosses and legendaries sometimes drop 10-pull tickets. The tickets cannot be bought, and the machine is split by region. Coins, earned the same way, buy permanent upgrades that apply only to the Pokemon you spent them on.

Shinies come from two places, at very different rates.

A single gacha pull is about 41 times as likely to be shiny as a single wild spawn (4,096 divided by 100), so the slot machine is the practical route to a shiny collection. Wild shinies still have their moment: they glow, an arrow at the edge of the screen points to them, and defeating one adds it to your collection. The only money involved is voluntary donations, which JPTER78 says pay for servers and the domain and give no in-game advantage.

Co-op Connects You Directly to Other Players

Co-op needs an account, made with a username and password or through Google. You add friends by nickname, invite them to a room, and share experience during the run. You revive a downed teammate by standing next to them, and if the host switches browser tabs, the game pauses for everyone. Leaderboards rank survival time, solo or in groups, from daily to all-time, and got anti-cheat checks on 10 October.

Group games link players’ browsers directly, much like a video call, so each player may be able to see the others’ IP addresses. The game’s privacy notice recommends playing co-op only with people you know, and says matches fall back to Firebase servers when a direct link fails. Guest play saves nothing outside your browser. Account saves sync through Google Firebase, and you can delete the account along with your nickname, friend list, and leaderboard times.

Seven Updates in Five Days, Built Without an Engine

The game’s changelog lists seven entries between 8 and 12 October. The three dated 8 October added co-op and leaderboards, legendary rifts and weather, and missions with 127 achievements. Mobile support and English arrived on 9 October, and bosses with four telegraphed attacks plus 12 new moves on 10 October. Bonds followed on 11 October. The 12 October patch fixed a bug that turned the screen black or triggered endless level-ups after 84 minutes, and made rifts appear no more than 2.5 minutes apart, with legendaries you haven’t caught showing up more often.

The public GitHub repository shows a game written in plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript on a 2D canvas, with no engine and no external dependencies. JPTER78’s notes say a full frame with 420 enemies on screen runs at about 3 milliseconds in Chrome. The sprites come from PMD SpriteCollab, a community library of Mystery Dungeon-style Pokemon sprites licensed for non-commercial use only, and Pokedex data comes from PokeAPI. The music and sound effects are original, generated by a synthesiser built into the game.

Can PokéSurvivor Avoid a Nintendo Takedown?

JPTER78 describes PokéSurvivor as a non-profit fan project with no connection to Nintendo, Creatures, Game Freak, or The Pokémon Company. The game’s legal page invites rights holders to request removals. The same page warns that PokéSurvivor has no official cryptocurrency, token, or NFT, and that anything trading under its name was made by third parties.

Being free has rarely protected Pokemon fan games. In 2016, Pokémon Uranium’s developers pulled their official download links after more than 1.5 million downloads, once lawyers for Nintendo of America began sending takedown notices to sites hosting the game. Later that year, Pokémon Prism was shut down at Nintendo’s request before it could release. Uranium’s notices came right after its biggest release drew wide attention, which makes a viral week like this one the riskiest stretch for any Pokemon fan game, regardless of how carefully it handles money.

For now PokéSurvivor is live and has been patched every day since launch. JPTER78’s legal page already states what happens if a rights holder objects: the content comes down as quickly as possible.
