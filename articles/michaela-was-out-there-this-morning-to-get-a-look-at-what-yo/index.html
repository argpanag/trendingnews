The 10-day salute to sausage is back, of course we're talking about Wurstfest, where food, live music, and Bavarian traditions all come together for one of the best celebrations in the state. Michaela was live there this morning to bring us all the fun.

Wurstfest has a lot of delicious food from sausage on a stick, bratwurst, and so much more! Wurstfest has been going for 65 years, and they pride themselves on incredible entertainment, food, drink, and celebration! There are over 13 acres to explore, and you'll want to explore every single inch.
