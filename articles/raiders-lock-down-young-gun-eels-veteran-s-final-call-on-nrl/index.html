Raiders lock down young gun; Eels veteran’s final call on NRL future revealed — Daily

TheTools · 15.10.2026 03:51 · source · permalink

Promising halfback Ethan Sanders is ready to fight for the No. 7 jersey at the Raiders after the club announced he would be staying until the end of 2028.

The 22-year-old arrived at the Raiders in 2025 after just two games for the Eels and he was given the task of replacing the departing Jamal Fogarty in the halves.

He’s gone on to play 25 games for the Green Machine and was named Dally M Rookie of the Year for 2026.

He formed a good partnership with Ethan Strange in the halves and Ricky Stuart and recruitment manager Chris Hutchison were keen to tie him down for longer.

“Ethan had a great 2026 season, and we were impressed with the way he was able to lead the team to some memorable wins,” Hutchison said.

“Ethan has shown strong maturity since arriving in 2025 and brought a level of calmness and professionalism to the Raiders.

“Ethan’s long-range kicking ability is a real highlight of his game, and he was able to put us in some great positions on the field.”

While Sanders has played well for Canberra, he will face stiff competition for the halfback spot from Coby Black.

The ex-Bronco was given his first NRL game by Stuart in Round 27 this year and set a new record for the most points scored on debut in a 50-30 win over the Cowboys in Townsville.

Black scored two tries and kicked nine goals to finish the match with a 26-point individual haul.

Despite Black’s impressive debut, Sanders said it was an easy decision to stay in the nation’s capital.

“Two years ago, the club put a lot of faith in me to bring me down to Canberra. I can’t thank Ricky enough for all the belief he’s given me, and I just want to repay that now,” Sanders said.

“To win the Rookie of the Year was a huge surprise but it’s a testament to the players I have around me.”

BIG MAN STAYS ON AT EELS

The Eels have announced Junior Paulo has signed a contract extension with the club for a further season, ending speculation surrounding the veteran’s future.

Paulo was widely linked with a move to the Bears, which reportedly fell through due to a disagreement on the terms of the expansion club’s contract offer.

Now the 32-year-old will now remain at Parramatta for the 2027 season in a huge boost for Jason Ryles.

“What Junior brings to our team goes far beyond his performances on the field,” Eels general manager Mark O’Neill said in a statement.

“It is not only his leadership and experience, but he is also an outstanding role model, a family man, mentor to our younger players and someone who helps drive the standards and culture of our club every day.

“He’s incredibly well respected throughout the game and, more importantly, he’s well-loved within our organisation.

“Keeping someone of Junior’s character and quality at the Eels is extremely important for our future and we’re delighted he’ll continue his journey with us.”

Paulo made his NRL debut with the Eels in 2013, enjoying a three-year stint with the Raiders between 2016 to 2018 before returning to Parramatta.

The gun prop is set to welcome both Spencer Leniu and Jaydn Su’A into his forward pack in 2027 in a recruitment push from head coach Ryles.

BENJI’S SHOCK ASSISTANT CANDIDATE REVEALED

Former Castleford Tigers head coach Ryan Carr is reportedly set to join Benji Marshall’s Tigers coaching staff after the departure of John Morris.

According to Code Sports, Carr is the leading candidate to fill the club’s vacant assistant coaching role after Morris, who focused on the Tigers’ defence, was sacked.

Carr endured a turbulent spell in the Super League, being suspended by Castleford bosses before he “left the club due to personal reasons” earlier this year.

The 38-year-old previously spent time under Shane Flanagan at the Dragons and was the club’s interim head coach after Anthony Griffin was sacked in 2023.

He left for the Super League two years later and was due to welcome a host of recruits into his squad for 2027, including Damien Cook, Tyrell Sloan and Matt Feagai.

Carr is now expected to join Marshall and Chris Heighington as the Tigers’ coaches for 2027, with the merger club spending another season outside of finals contention in 2026.

Off-field issues cruelled their campaign after a strong start, with Jarome Luai’s ugly exit and Adam Doueihi standing himself down proving to have a major impact on their performances.

The Tigers finished their 2026 campaign with the second-worst defensive record in the NRL.

Carr’s arrival is set to be finalised in the coming weeks.

STARLING’S RAIDERS DEPARTURE CONFIRMED

Tom Starling has confirmed he will be departing the Raiders. revealing his exit in a classy Instagram post on Wednesday.

The gun hooker had 12 months remaining on his contract with the Canberra club, but was given to green light to negotiate with Super League club St Helens by Ricky Stuart.

Multiple reports emerged that Starling had been offered a three-year deal to make the move to England, having slipped behind rising star Owen Pattie in the Raiders’ hooker pecking order.

Now Starling has all-but confirmed his move abroad, taking to social media to pay tribute to the Raiders.

“Thank you @RaidersCanberra for giving me the opportunity to live out my childhood dream for the past 8 years,” he wrote.

“It’s been an honour to represent this great club. I never once took for granted the privilege of pulling on that jersey and wearing the badge.

“The memories and friendships will stay with me for a lifetime. #362 over and out.”

A wave of current Canberra stars and club legends left messages of support for the gritty rake, including Jack Wighton, Jordan Rapana and Jarrod Croker.

Wighton commented “Love ya tbomb” while Croker added “Yea Jimbo” and Rapana wrote: “Chur bolll one of the best, love ya Tman”.

Stuart previously confirmed to the Canberra Times that Starling had been given permission to negotiate with St Helens.

“Tommy and I had a really mature conversation about exactly where he stood and I totally understand his position, Tommy has a career to run as well and he had an opportunity with St Helens and he totally understands where Owen Pattie is at,” Stuart explained.

“He’s still young enough to be playing for a number of years still, and he’s got a good opportunity with the Super League instead of sitting back here behind Owen. I can keep him for depth, but it’s the wrong thing to do by the individual.

“He’s done a lot for the club, and it’s our time now to give Tommy that opportunity to keep his career alive and healthy.”

Starling ends his time in the nation’s capital with 156 appearances, having made his NRL debut at the Knights before making the switch.

MANLY CULT HERO’S NEW CLUB REVEALED

Nathan Brown’s next club has been revealed, with the Catalans Dragons announcing the former Sea Eagles cult hero’s signing.

The 33-year-old middle forward has been handed a one-year deal with an option for a further season after his time in Manly came to an end at the conclusion of the 2026 season.

Brown emerged as a fan favourite at Brookvale Oval due to his rampaging runs and willingness to put his body on the line.

The veteran played 201 NRL games across stints at the Tigers, Rabbitohs, Eels, Roosters and eventually the Sea Eagles.

Brown also made two appearances for NSW during the 2020 Origin series, as well as six Test caps for Italy.

PHINS’ SIGNING CONFIRMED

George Williams’ return to the NRL has been confirmed, with the Dolphins announcing they’ve signed the crafty halfback to a two-year deal.

The England skipper will leave Super League club Warrington to form a talented halves combo with Isaiya Katoa.

The two will go head-to-head on Saturday night with Williams’ England taking on Tonga in Perth.

The veteran half will replace Kodi Nikorima in the Dolphins’ halves with Nikorima linking up with the Dragons from 2027.

The move to replace Nikorima with Williams has copped some criticism given the partnership Nikorima had forged with Katoa.

Moreover, Williams and Katoa have similar styles as they are both dominant, on-the-ball halfbacks.

Still, adding a player with the ability and experience of Williams should only help a Dolphins side who were one win from a grand final this year.

“George is one of the most accomplished halves in the game and to have someone of his experience and leadership joining the Dolphins is a huge boost for our squad,” Dolphins CEO Terry Reader said via a club-issued statement.

“He has performed at the highest level in both the NRL and Super League and has proven himself as a leader on the international stage with England.

“George brings a strong kicking game, competitiveness and a wealth of experience that will complement the players we already have within our spine.

“We’re really excited about the opportunity to bring George to the Dolphins and to see what he can add alongside Isaiya, Brad and the rest of our squad from 2027.”

Williams returns to the NRL after playing 32 games for Canberra in 2020 and 2021.

DOGS LAND RISING HOOKER

The Bulldogs have confirmed the signing of rising hooker Jaxson Allen, who has put pen to paper on a fresh two-year deal to join Cameron Ciraldo’s squad.

The 19-year-old will make a cross-town switch from the Roosters, having played 13 NSW Cup games this season — scoring five tries from behind the ruck.

In 2025, Allen captained a premiership-winning Tricolours SG Ball team and also featured for the Blues’ under 19s outfit the same year.

“Jaxson is a crafty hooker with a great running game and high footy IQ,” Bulldogs supremo Phil Gould said in a statement.

“He has enormous potential and we’re excited to watch him continue to develop in the Bulldogs system.”

The Bulldogs’ hooker position has been under the microscope since the club made the decision to allow Reed Mahoney to depart at the end of the 2025 season.

Allen is now set to contend with Bailey Hayward, who took over the No.9 jersey after Mahoney’s exit.

MANLY LEGEND’S RETURN CONFIRMED

The Sea Eagles have welcomed Brent Kite back, confirming the club legend has signed on to join Kieran Foran’s coaching staff.

Kite has been unveiled as Manly’s newest assistant coach, taking up a “key role working primarily with the Sea Eagles’ forward pack”.

He will also have a “particular focus on the club’s middle forwards”, joining after the club began a football department clear-out after they faded out of finals contention.

Three key figures left the club, with leadership manager Aaron Greaves, head of football Peter Gentle and head of performance Jon Clarke all departing.

Kite has previously enjoyed a stint as a coach with the Tweed Coast Raiders, leading them to a Northern Rivers Regional grand final and Clayton Cup double in 2018.

“As a player Brent was extremely tough, brave, smart and his leadership skills were a pivotal part of our success,” Foran said in a statement.

“I think he’ll be really good for our players, especially our forwards. His experience and resilience will bring that extra hard edge to our pack.

“He has Manly blood running through his veins and is hugely respected by the Manly community. It’s great to welcome Brent back to the club.”

Foran and Kite played together for five seasons at the Sea Eagles, winning the Clive Churchill Medal in Manly’s 2008 premiership victory.
