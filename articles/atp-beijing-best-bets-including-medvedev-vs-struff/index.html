ATP Beijing Best Bets Including Medvedev vs Struff

TheTools · 02.10.2026 23:00 · source · permalink

The 2026 ATP Beijing tournament continues in China. This is an ATP 500 event which follows the 250-point tournaments played this past week. It is more of a draw and therefore more of a prize for ATP players trying to stack points and qualify for the ATP Finals in November. The other reason a lot of top players are playing in the ATP Beijing event is that it immediately flows into the ATP Shanghai 1,000-point tournament next week. Players will spend two and a half to three weeks in China so that they can play this 500-point event and then the 1,000-point event back-to-back. Players took the opportunity to rest before this tournament, and then when Shanghai is over, they will be able to get some more rest on the back end and then regroup for a final push in Europe in late October. The Beijing-Shanghai combination of tournaments will have a lot to say about the ATP rankings and the 2026 points race, so this is an event worth keeping an eye on. LWOS has our best bets for three matches. Post your best bets and thoughts in the comments.

ATP Beijing

Daniil Medvedev – Jan-Lennard Struff: 5:00 EST

H2H: 10-3

This ATP Beijing match discussion begins with the fact that Daniil Medvedev has a 10-3 head-to-head lead over Jan-Lennard Struff. Medvedev winning this match seems like an obvious outcome, but what is the betting play to make here? Medvedev beat Pablo Carreno-Busta by six games in the first round of Beijing, barely beating the 5.5-game spread. Struff looked good in the first round of Beijing, beating Thiago Agustin Tirante.

Best Bet to Make

Welcome to our best bet section. You’re going to get a collection of bets to consider, also a confidence level which should guide how much you should invest in this match.

Parlay/hedge options: Medvedev -1.5 sets at 1.55 at @1xbet

Confidence level on a scale of 1-10: 1 – You should not bet on this match. 

This ATP Beijing match is not a good one to bet on. Medvedev should win, but will he win big or small? He covered the spread by half a game against Carreno-Busta. It was basically a match which was right on the spread. This match could be the same. We don’t think you should bet on this match, but if you absolutely insist, we’re going to lean to Struff just because Medvedev might be a little fatigued after winning Hangzhou and then coming straight to Beijing.

That being said, betting on Struff +5 games is the best bet you can make.    

Value bet/ the best odds: Struff +5 games at 1.62 at @1xbet

Alexander Zverev – Juncheng Shang: 7:00 EST

H2H: 0-0

This ATP Beijing match features US Open champion Alexander Zverev, who win his first-round Beijing match by a total of three games (7-6 6-4) over Cameron Norrie. We correctly predicted that Norrie would keep the match close if only because Zverev doesn’t have a ton to prove here in China and might not be supremely hungry. Now let’s see if Zverev plays better in his second-round match.

Best Bet to Make

Parlay/hedge options: Zverev -4.5 games at 1.75 at @unibet

Confidence level on a scale of 1-10: 1 – you should not bet on this match.

This ATP Beijing match is a terrible match to bet on because the winner is obvious, but the margin of victory is not. We guessed right on Zverev and the spread in his previous match, but he might be better here. It feels like a match which might be half a game off the spread either way. That’s not a good match to bet. We are going to lean slightly to Shang, if you insist on a bet.

That being said, betting on Shang +5.5 games is the best bet you can make

Value bet/ the best odds: Shang +5.5 games at 1.61 @betfair

Francisco Cerundolo – Jakub Mensik: 5:00 EST

H2H: 2-0

This ATP Beijing match is intriguing because Francisco Cerundolo is 2-0 against Jakub Mensik, but Mensik is 24-11 on hardcourts this year while Cerundolo is only 13-9. Both players posted good wins in the first round in China. Mensik beat Alexander Bublik while Cerundolo defeated Botic Van de Zandschulp.

Best Bet to Make

Parlay/hedge options: Cerundolo +1.5 sets at 1.65 at @betway

Confidence level on a scale of 1-10: 4 – you should not bet more than half a unit on this match.

This ATP Beijing match could go either way. Note that Cerundolo +1.5 sets and Mensik -1.5 games are priced exactly the same. One could be your main bet and the other could be a parlay piece. We’re going to choose Mensik -1.5 games as the main bet because his hardcourt form is very strong right now.

That being said, betting on Mensik -1.5 games is the best bet you can make

Value bet/ the best odds: Mensik -1.5 games at 1.65 @betfair
