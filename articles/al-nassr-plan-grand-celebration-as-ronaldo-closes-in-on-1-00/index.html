Al Nassr 'plan grand celebration' as Ronaldo closes in on 1,000 career goals - Blueprint Newspapers Limited

TheTools · 09.11.2026 04:45 · source · permalink

November 9, 2026 Austine Agbo Emmanuel Sports

Al Nassr are reportedly considering an elaborate celebration for Cristiano Ronaldo when the Portuguese forward reaches the remarkable milestone of 1,000 career goals.

The reported plans, which have generated excitement among football fans, are said to include a series of special tributes to the five-time Ballon d’Or winner, whose pursuit of the landmark continues to attract global attention.

Among the claims circulating are proposals for a national holiday in Saudi Arabia, a gold statue of Ronaldo outside Al Awwal Park and a personal congratulatory call from Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, Blueprint reports.

The reports also suggest that Al Nassr could rename their home ground the Cristiano Ronaldo 1,000 Goals Stadium as part of the celebrations.

Al Nassr reportedly consider grand celebration as Ronaldo approaches 1,000 career goals

Al Nassr are reportedly considering an elaborate celebration for Cristiano Ronaldo when the Portuguese forward reaches the landmark of 1,000 official career goals, although several details circulating about the proposed plans remain unverified.

The five-time Ballon d’Or winner is closing in on the extraordinary milestone after taking his career tally to 985 goals with a hat-trick in Al Nassr’s 5-3 victory over Al Kholood on October 29.

The achievement would make Ronaldo the first player to reach 1,000 goals in officially recognised senior competitive matches, further cementing his place in football history.

Social media reports have suggested that Saudi Arabia could organise an unprecedented celebration to mark the occasion, with claims of a national holiday, a gold statue outside Al Awwal Park and a personal congratulatory call from Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman.

Other claims suggest that Al Nassr are considering renaming their stadium the Cristiano Ronaldo 1,000 Goals Stadium, while the Saudi Pro League could offer the forward a lifetime contract that would allow him to continue playing until the age of 60.

However, these specific proposals have not been confirmed by Al Nassr, the Saudi Pro League or Saudi authorities.

Seven-day celebration reportedly among proposals

The viral reports have also suggested that Ronaldo’s 1,000th goal could trigger seven consecutive days of celebrations, featuring fireworks, a special documentary and commemorative activities honouring his career.

Another extraordinary claim is that every Al Nassr player would be required to wear Ronaldo’s iconic number seven as part of the tribute.

The reports further mention the possibility of a special edition of the Ballon d’Or featuring the Portuguese superstar’s image.

While the suggestions have generated excitement among his supporters, there is no verified announcement establishing that these measures have been approved or are being formally planned.

Ronaldo’s remarkable journey towards 1,000 goals

Ronaldo’s pursuit of 1,000 career goals has become one of the most closely followed individual achievements in world football.

The 41-year-old has built his scoring record across Sporting CP, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus, Al Nassr and the Portugal national team.

His 985-goal tally, recorded after the October 29 hat-trick, left him 15 goals short of the historic mark.

The forward has remained prolific despite his age, continuing to score regularly for Al Nassr in the Saudi Pro League and continental competitions.

His career has also been defined by numerous individual and collective honours, including five Ballon d’Or awards, five UEFA Champions League titles and the European Championship with Portugal in 2016.

A milestone beyond ordinary football records

Reaching 1,000 official career goals would represent an extraordinary achievement in the history of the sport, particularly given the longevity required to maintain a high scoring rate across different leagues and competitions.

Ronaldo has repeatedly demonstrated his determination to continue playing and scoring, with the 1,000-goal target remaining a major personal ambition.

For Al Nassr and Saudi football, the milestone would also provide an opportunity to celebrate one of the most recognisable players in the global game and the impact of his move to the Saudi Pro League.

Whether the elaborate celebrations described in the viral reports eventually materialise remains to be seen. What is certain is that Ronaldo’s pursuit of 1,000 goals has placed the football world on alert.
