Jason Statham's New Action Thriller Is Officially One of the Most-Watched Movies on Streaming

TheTools · 11.10.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

Jason Statham has never let small problems like insignificant box office returns or a slightly middling critical response ruin his career or slow him down. Yes, his latest action movie didn't make the noise everyone hoped for in theaters, but anyone who skipped it the first time around won't have to wait long for another chance.

Mutiny is heading to digital platforms, giving Statham's latest action thriller a second shot at finding an audience at home. The film may not have made waves (get it?) at the box office, so that means the movie's quick shift to VOD is an important next step for a picture built around one of the most reliable action stars working today.

Cole Reed (Statham), a former police officer and Special Forces op, has left the UK for Singapore to live a simple life as a bodyguard and chauffeur for Tibu Campallo, the billionaire owner of a freight business. However, tragedy strikes on the day Tibu announces his retirement; their car is ambushed, and he’s shot and killed by mercenaries. Believing the attack must have been an inside job, Cole goes on the run as the murder is pinned on him, stowing away on board one of Tibu’s freight ships to buy time and find answers.

However, once setting sail, a shocking discovery makes him realize the imminent danger he and others are in, and he needs to step in before it’s too late. It's pretty comfortable territory for Statham. There are conspiracies, professional killers and a hero with exactly the sort of résumé you don't want to discover after you've decided to frame him for murder.

Mutiny stars Annabelle Wallis (Malignant); Jason Wong (The Covenant) as Taran; Roland Møller (Land of Mine) as Marko Madsen; Arnas Fedaravicius (The Last Kingdom); and Adrian Lester (Primary Colors). The movie is directed by Jean-François Richet and written by J.P. Davis and Lindsay Michel. Statham produces alongside Marc Butan.

How Good Is 'Mutiny'?

The theatrical run didn't turn Mutiny into Statham's next The Beekeeper. The movie struggled to make much of an impression at the box office, which makes its arrival at home particularly useful. Statham's movies tend to have long lives after leaving theaters anyway. Action thrillers are easy streaming viewing, and his back catalog regularly finds its way onto platform charts years after release.

Mutiny is officially getting its second chance at home on digital platforms.
