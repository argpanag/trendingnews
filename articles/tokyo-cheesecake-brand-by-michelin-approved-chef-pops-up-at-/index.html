Tokyo Cheesecake Brand By Michelin-Approved Chef Pops Up At ION Orchard

TheTools · 02.11.2026 07:59 · source · permalink

Mr CHEESECAKE has a limited-time pop-up in Orchard

Image credit: Inside Retail Asia

ION Orchard has recently been a hotspot for Japanese brand debuts, such as I’m Donut and fried sando shop Age.3. Now, Mr CHEESECAKE is joining the mix, making its Southeast Asian debut from 11 November 2026 to 30 December 2026.

For the uninitiated, Mr CHEESECAKE is a Tokyo-born cheesecake brand founded by Chef Koji Tamura, who has worked in Michelin-starred restaurants, including three-Michelin-starred Mirazur in France and one-Michelin-starred Restaurant ES in Paris. Since its launch in 2018, Mr CHEESECAKE has expanded to five stores in Japan and one in Hong Kong, selling over 1 million cakes to date.

Their cheesecakes are meant to be savoured at three different temperatures: frozen for a mouthfeel that’s similar to ice cream cake, fully thawed for a smoothness similar to creme brulee, and half-thawed for a mix of both textures.

Image credit: Mr CHEESECAKE

The BASQUE CHEESECAKE Original ($62) boasts a toasted, caramelly exterior, a result of baking the batter at a high temperature, and a smooth, creamy interior with real vanilla specks. The combination of browned butter, vanilla, and brown sugar is said to give the cake a butterscotch aroma that’s best enjoyed with a cup of hot coffee.

Image credit: Mr CHEESECAKE

Those looking to amp up the coffee flavour should order the Mr CHEESECAKE Cream Brew Coffee ($68), comprising coffee beans steeped in the same cheesecake mixture, which lends the cheesecake a rich, earthy, and slightly fruity flavour.

Image credit: Mr CHEESECAKE

Of course, matcha lovers can also get their cheesecake fix with the Mr CHEESECAKE Matcha ($69). It features stone-ground Yame matcha from Fukuoka, prized for its mellow sweetness and rich umami flavour, complemented with creamy mascarpone and vanilla for a decadent treat.

Image credit: Mr CHEESECAKE

For a more tropical variant, the Mr CHEESECAKE Sakekasu Coconut ($69) combines rich coconut, refreshing lychee puree, and umami sakekasu, AKA sake lees, for a cake that’s not too sweet.

Image credit: Mr CHEESECAKE

Those who aren’t too keen on cheesecake can opt for their RICH TIRAMISU ($69) instead—their interpretation of the Italian classic. Expect a delicate, crisp almond dacquoise layered with a light, mousse-like cream flavoured with coffee, cacao, and hojicha, further enhanced by the nutty aroma of browned butter.

Do take note that the cakes are available while stocks last, so it’s worth getting there early.

If you’re feeling peckish after your cheesecake haul, our guide to the best ION Orchard food has you covered. Then, wash it all down with a drink from Chinese chain Yee3, known for their unique coconut drinks.

Address: 2 Orchard Turn, #03-32/33, ION Orchard, Singapore 238801
Opening hours: Daily 10am to 10pm
