MM 9.11: Malik Washington named to Jason Witten Collegiate Man of the Year watch list

TheTools · 11.09.2026 10:30 · source · permalink
  • The Maryland Minute

This is the Maryland Minute, a short story followed by a roundup of Terps-related news.

Maryland football quarterback Malik Washington has received seemingly endless acclaim for his progression and achievements on the football field. However, being named to the Jason Witten Collegiate Man of the Year watch list recognizes the sophomore phenom for more than just his excellence on the gridiron.

The award was created to honor an FBS college football player for their off-the-field character and commitment to improving their community. The namesake of the award is the current tight ends coach at Oklahoma, after an illustrious 17-year career in the NFL.

As for Washington’s charitability, the quarterback hosted the Malik Washington Free Youth Football Camp at Under Armour Stadium in July and continues to participate in other local community service projects. The list will narrow down to 20 semifinalists on Oct. 13.

In other news

Maryland men’s basketball sits in the final four schools for four-star class of 2027 combo guard Payton Jones. He announced his decision on Friday at 2 p.m.

The Terps also announced their co-MVPs from their fifth boot camp session.

Maryland baseball earned the commitment of catcher Angelo Ciminiello.

Anthony Cowan Jr. has been balling out in Slovenia.
