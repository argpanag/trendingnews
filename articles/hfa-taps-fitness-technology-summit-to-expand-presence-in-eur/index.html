HFA Taps Fitness Technology Summit to Expand Presence in Europe

TheTools · 21.10.2026 17:50 · source · permalink

Fitness•Industry News

The fitness industry trade association is joining forces with the executive-focused technology conference for a major London event in 2027, with the partnership also extending to the upcoming HFA Show in Las Vegas

The Health & Fitness Association (HFA) is joining forces with the Fitness Technology Summit (FTS) to expand its presence in Europe, combining the organizations’ respective European conferences into a single event in London next year.

The newly announced partnership will bring together HFA’s European Congress and FTS’s European event for the Fitness Technology Summit: European Congress in Partnership with HFA, scheduled for May 25-26, 2027.

The event will combine HFA’s focus on industry advocacy, research and business growth with FTS’s expertise in technology, artificial intelligence and data-driven innovation.

“Bringing our European Congress together with the Fitness Technology Summit creates an exciting opportunity to build on what both organizations have established and deliver even greater value to our industry,” said HFA interim president and CEO Greta Wagner.

The partnership builds on HFA’s existing efforts to strengthen relationships with European fitness operators and industry associations. Its 2026 European Congress was held in partnership with U.K.-based fitness trade organization ukactive.

HFA and FTS say the combined 2027 event will bring together fitness operators, technology providers, investors and industry associations to explore topics including AI, member engagement, retention and the evolving role of fitness in health and wellness.

The partnership will also extend to The HFA Show, scheduled for March 10-12, 2027, at Mandalay Bay in Las Vegas. FTS will curate three education sessions focused on how technology is reshaping fitness business operations and the member experience.

Inside the Fitness Technology Summit

Now in its 14th year, FTS is an invitation-only executive conference that brings together fitness and wellness operators, technology leaders, founders and investors to discuss the industry’s digital transformation.

The summit is presented by AltaDX, an AI and technology company serving the fitness, beauty and wellness industries. Its European edition is jointly operated by AltaDX and LeisureLabs, a U.K.-based digital transformation agency whose clients include David Lloyd Clubs, The Gym Group and Fitness First.

FTS’s most recent U.S. event, held Oct. 12-14 in National Harbor, Maryland, attracted 300 executives and industry leaders for discussions on AI adoption, technology integration, member retention and the relationship between physical fitness facilities and digital platforms.

Among the findings presented at the event, a technology benchmarking survey of 68 fitness operators found that 78% were testing or deploying AI, although just 18% had implemented the technology at scale. Meanwhile, 70% identified systems integration as a major challenge.

The event also featured HFA’s Global Retention Study, which analyzed more than 12 million membership accounts across 38 countries and found that the median fitness club member stays for 13 months.

The organizations said additional details about the London event and the FTS-curated sessions at The HFA Show will be announced in the coming months.

“Supporting the Health & Fitness Association matters to us because HFA plays a vital role in uniting our industry’s voice around the world. … This partnership is about building something bigger than any single event or organization,” said Al Noshirvani, co-founder and CEO of AltaDX.
