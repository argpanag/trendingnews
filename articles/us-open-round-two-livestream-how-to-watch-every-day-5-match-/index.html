How Long Is the 2026 US Open?

The US Open is a three-week event in August and September. This year, it runs from Sunday, August 23, to Sunday, September 13 at the USTA Billie Jean King National Tennis Center in Queens, New York. The first week of the event will feature qualifying events for the men's and women's singles competition, as well as mixed doubles. You can check here for a full schedule.

  • Qualifying events - August 24 - August 27

  • Men's and women's singles: August 30 - September 13

  • Mixed doubles: August 24-26

  • Men's and women's doubles: September 3 - September 12

  • Junior events: September 3 - September 12

Who Is Playing in the 2026 US Open?

The field for the 2026 US Open will have 17 former Grand Slam singles champions, 10 of whom are former US Open winners. For the men's competition, Jannik Sinner has been ruled out due to injury. That means Alexander Zverev (1) and Carlos Alcaraz (2), and Felix Auger-Aliassime (3) rank among the top three. Aryna Sabalenka (1), Elena Rybakina (2), and American Jessica Pegula (3) are among the top three women in the tournament.

The United States will have 28 players in the main draw, leading all nations in the competition. In addition to Pegula, standouts include Coco Gauff, Amanda Anisimova, Ben Shelton, Taylor Fritz, and Venus Williams.
