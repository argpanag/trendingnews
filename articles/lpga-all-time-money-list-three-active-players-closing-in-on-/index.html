LPGA all-time money list: Three active players closing in on Annika Sorenstam's record

TheTools · 08.10.2026 14:31 · source · permalink

Annika Sorenstam’s grip on the LPGA Tour’s all-time money list is coming to an end.

When Sorenstam’s record of $22,583,693 goes down is an unknown, but 2026 is as good a time as any seeing as the PGA Tour saw Scottie Scheffler pass Tiger Woods on its all-time career earnings list this summer.

Lydia Ko is primed to be the first player to take away Sorenstam’s No. 1 spot, but she might not hold the title for long.

Nelly Korda is third on the list, just $30,000 behind Ko. And after Ko and Korda is Jeeno Thitikul, an 11-time LPGA winner at the age of 23. She sits about $83,000 behind Karrie Webb for the fourth-highest earner in LPGA history.

Thitikul’s most recent win in Hawaii moved her over Cristie Kerr for fifth place. Let’s take a look at the current top 20 all-time (as of Oct. 8, 2026):

1. Annika Sorenstam, $22,583,693
2. Lydia Ko, $22,360,465
3. Nelly Korda, $22,329,772
4. Karrie Webb, $20,293,617
5. Jeeno Thitikul, $20,210,011
6. Cristie Kerr, $20,179,848
7. Minjee Lee, $19,048,450
8. Inbee Park, $18,262,344
9. Sei Young Kim, $17,504,015
10. Amy Yang, $16,851,701
11. Brooke M. Henderson, $16,765,683
12. Lexi Thompson, $15,622,907
13. Jin Young Ko, $15,020,143
14. Lorena Ochoa, $14,863,331
15. Suzann Pettersen, $14,837,578
16. Stacy Lewis, $14,676,866
17. Ariya Jutanugarn, $14,534,787
18. Anna Nordqvist, $14,075,602
19. Juli Inkster, $14,047,830
20. Hyo Joo Kim, $13,551,780

It was less than a year ago that Thitikul moved to seventh on this list when she won the CME Group Tour Championship.

One name that’s not on the above list but could be in the months to come is Charley Hull.

The 30-year-old Englishwoman has earned $12,763,868 and is No. 21 all-time in LPGA career earnings. Hyo Joo Kim is a contemporary for Hull but several other names above have locked in their career monies.
