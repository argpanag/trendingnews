Ex-Glasgow Warriors player set to make international debut after switching allegiance

TheTools · 20.11.2026 12:04 · source · permalink

Sports writer

Montpellier fly-half to earn first Italy cap against Fiji in Nations Championship

Former Glasgow Warriors fly-half Domingo Miotti has been named in the starting XV for Italy’s Nations Championship match with Fiji this weekend.

The 30-year-old will earn his first cap for the Azzurri having previously represented Argentina, the country of his birth, between 2020 and 2022. Now playing for Montpellier in the Top 14, having left Scotstoun in 2023 to join Oyonnax, the stand-off earned 10 caps for Los Pumas but is now eligible to switch international allegiance after four years without playing in a Test.

Gonzalo Quesada’s side are searching for their first win of the inaugural tournament when the Flying Fijians visit the Stadio Olimpico. They have so far lost all five of their matches and are guaranteed to finish bottom of the Northern Hemisphere table.

On Miotti’s first cap, the head coach said: “Domingo will get his chance, as I explained from the start, we planned to play him in the match against Fiji and then we would evaluate whether to start him or come off the bench. Tommy Allan and Paolo Garbisi also knew that; we've always been transparent. Everyone knew he'd be in the squad on Saturday.

“ We even considered starting with Garbisi or Allan at number 10, Miotti on the bench, and Capuozzo at fullback, but with so many injuries to the wingers—like Lynagh, Faissal, Pani, and Trulla—the best lineup for us is to move Ange to the wing, with Tommy Allan at fullback, and Domingo starting. And then, when you give a player his debut in these key positions, paradoxically, it's easier to start him, allowing him to work as a potential starter all week.”

If the Italians win Saturday’s match then they will face Fiji once again as part of Finals Weekend at Twickenham. A defeat with the visitors picking up the bonus point could set up a meeting against Japan depending on the result of the Brave Blossoms’ fixture against Scotland at Murrayfield.

Miotti moved to Scotstoun in 2021 from Australian side Western Force having previously played for Jaguares in Argentina. He joined current side Montpellier, last season’s EPCR Challenge Cup winners, in 2024 and currently plays alongside a host of fellow former Warriors.

Ex Scotland captain Stuart Hogg and scrum-half Ali Price ply their trades at the Septeo Stadium and were joined by fly-half Adam Hastings in the summer who has been competing with Miotti for the 10 jersey. Montpellier currently sit third in the Top 14 table having won six and lost three of their opening nine matches.

If Miotti does play for his new country this weekend then he will become only the second number ten in the history of international rugby after Diego Dominguez to wear both the Pumas and Italy shirts. Changing international allegiances after a stand down period has become a fairly common sight in international rugby in recent years.

Scotland themselves have benefited from the rule with Australian born number 8 Jack Dempsey, another former Glasgow Warriors star, having won 14 caps for the Wallabies between 2017 and 2019. He made his Scotland debut in 2022 and has since won 37 caps for Gregor Townsend’s side.
