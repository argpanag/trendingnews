FIRST ALERT FORECAST: Temps increase throughout the week ahead

TheTools · 15.11.2026 21:27 · source · permalink

HAZARD, Ky. (WYMT) - It has been a chilly weekend but temperatures will be on the increase for the week ahead as mild air returns to the region.

Skies Finally Clear Tonight

Partly cloudy skies are expected for this evening with clearing skies heading into the overnight. It will be another cold night as we see lows in the upper 20s to low 30s.

Sunny and Seasonal Monday

Plenty of sunshine will be with us throughout the day Monday as temperatures will start getting closer to average for this time of year. Highs Monday will climb into the mid 50s. Clear skies will be with us again for Monday night as we see overnight lows of 25-30 degrees.

More Sunshine for Tuesday

The sunshine continues for Tuesday as temperatures continue to improve. Highs by Tuesday reach the upper 50s to low 60s. We will see clear skies again Tuesday night as we fall into the upper 20s to low 30s again.

Mild Mid to Late Week

Wednesday will be another perfectly sunny day as temperatures continue to climb. Highs will be into the mid 60s Wednesday. Some clouds will start to show up later in the week as it turns really mild. Highs of 65-70 degrees are expected Thursday, Friday, and next Saturday.

Still Dry Even Into Next Weekend

It does start to cool down a little bit by the second half of next weekend but it will continue to remain dry. Highs will look to reach the low to mid 60s next Sunday.
