Why this was Rennie's hardest All Blacks team to pick yet | All Blacks News

TheTools · 08.10.2026 22:00 · source · permalink

At the All Blacks team press conference yesterday head coach Dave Rennie confirmed why the team to face the Wallabies this Saturday at Eden Park was so tough to nail.

Firstly, a fresh injury blow for Richie Mo'unga who had been pencilied in to start on Monday threw a spanner in the works, with the first-five suffering a calf strain. The pivot is also likely to miss the return test in Sydney next week.

That plus the continued absense of Ardie Savea and the need to manage the return to Test-level footy for Scott Barrett and Shannon Frizell further complicated things.

The coach confirmed it required three selection meetings for the Three Wise Men 'Rens', 'Barnsy' and 'Ted' to come to a consensus, unprecedented thus far under this regime.

Rennie mentioned that their final decision to start Tupou Vaa'i at blindside also had a flow-on effect elasewhere, with Wallace Sititi and Peter Lakai confrimed to start at Number 8 and openside respectively, with the returning Shannon Frizell to come off the pine as he continues to build fitness for the challenges of top flight footy.

Anton Segner will provide additional legs in the loose, also named on the bench.

With Mo'unga down, the choice was made to name Damian McKenzie in the 10 jersey, while Test centurian Beauden Barrett earns another start in the 15 jersey, his 146th Test cap.

Rennie confirmed this was largely due to McKenzie having trained at lot at first-five, while Beauden Barret's most recent games have been at fullback. Both will play a role in the backfield and at first receiver.

Elsewhere upfront, hooker Codie Taylor has been named captain and will start alongside Ethan De Groot and Fletcher Newell in the front row.

Scott Barrett makes his long awaited return and will also start and has been paired with Josh Lord in the second row.

On the substitues bench, the wrecking ball Asafo Aumua, George Bower and Pasilio Tosi will look to add impact early in the second half, as the All Blacks look to make improvements at setpiece.

Out wide few will be surprised to see record All Blacks try scorer Will Jordan named in the 14 jumper, with the illusive and effervescent Leroy Carter set to start on the left wing. Fehi Fineanganofo was unavailable after suffering a head knock in Bay of Plenty's final round NPC match last Sunday where he was also unlucky to have picked up a hamstring niggle.

Likely some of the first names locked in at the first of those three selection meetings, Cam Roigard will start at halfback, with Jordie Barrett and Quinn Tupaea providing consistency in the midfield.

Kyle Preston and Rieko Ioane along with standout newcomer Josh Moorby will provide backs cover from the bench.
