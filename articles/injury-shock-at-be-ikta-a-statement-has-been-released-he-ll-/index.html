Injury shock at Beşiktaş! A statement has been released—he’ll be out for 4–6 weeks...

TheTools · 24.10.2026 17:59 · source · permalink

Beşiktaş issued a statement regarding the condition of Fabio Miretti, who was forced to leave the game after sustaining an injury during last night’s UEFA Europa League match against Crystal Palace. The Italian player is expected to be sidelined for 4–6 weeks. Here are the details...

Beşiktaş drew 2-2 at home against Crystal Palace in the UEFA Europa League last night. Fabio Miretti, who suffered an injury in the first half of the match, was unable to continue playing. A statement released by the club provided an update on the star player’s condition.

'Injury and joint dislocation'

Beşiktaş’s statement read as follows: “Our player Fabio Miretti, who was injured after falling onto his shoulder during the first half of last night’s match against Crystal Palace and was unable to continue playing, underwent an evaluation and radiological imaging at Acıbadem Altunizade Hospital, an injury (acromioclavicular joint separation) was detected in the right shoulder-clavicle joint area, and our medical team has begun treating the player."

He will be sidelined for 4–6 weeks

Meanwhile, the length of time Fabio Miretti—who was unable to finish last night’s match—will be sidelined has also been determined. According to a report on TRT Spor, the 23-year-old midfielder will be unable to play for 4–6 weeks. Miretti has appeared in seven matches across all competitions for Beşiktaş this season, logging 317 minutes on the field.
