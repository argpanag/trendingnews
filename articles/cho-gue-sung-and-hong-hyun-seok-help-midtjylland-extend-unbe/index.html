Cho Gue-sung and Hong Hyun-seok help Midtjylland extend unbeaten run to 15 games

TheTools · 24.11.2026 02:40 · source · permalink

Cho Gue-sung, 28, and Hong Hyun-seok, 27, both played a part in the scoring as they led Midtjylland's unbeaten run. Cho scored his sixth goal of the season, while Hong forced an own goal.

Midtjylland beat Brondby 2-0 in the 15th round of the 2026-27 Danish Superliga at Brondby Stadium in Brondby, Denmark, at 3 a.m. on the 24th Korea time.

Midtjylland extended its unbeaten start to the season to 15 games (10 wins, five draws) and remained atop the standings with 35 points. Second-place FC Copenhagen suffered a 5-0 defeat to Nordsjaelland, widening the gap between the teams to three points.

Two South Korean players were at the heart of the win. Cho scored the opening goal, while Hong's shot led to an own goal by an opposing defender and Midtjylland's second.

Midtjylland got off to a difficult start, struggling under Brondby's pressure in the first half. In the 22nd minute, the team escaped a goal when Christopher Olivier's volley hit the post.

Midtjylland brought on Cho late in the first half to change its attack. Cho made an immediate impact after halftime.

In the fourth minute of the second half, goalkeeper Patrick Pentz saved a shot, but Cho pounced on the rebound and drove it into the net with his left foot. The goal gave Midtjylland the lead.

It was Cho's sixth goal in official competition this season.

Cho, who returned from injury, struggled to score early in the season but began to regain his scoring touch with a goal in the Union of European Football Associations (UEFA) Europa Conference League in October. He later scored in the league against Lyngby and found the net again against Brondby.

Cho played 53 minutes and attempted three shots, two of which were on target. He also created one scoring chance. He touched the ball three times in the opponent's penalty area and completed 75% of his passes (9 of 12).

He also had some shortcomings, winning just two of 10 aerial duels, but he delivered at a crucial moment and did his job as a striker.

Hong also contributed to the win. Introduced in the 19th minute of the second half, he attempted a shot from inside the opponent's penalty area in the 31st minute. The ball deflected off Brondby defender Frederik Alves and went into the net.

The goal was officially credited as an own goal by Alves, not as a goal for Hong. Still, Hong's aggressive shot was significant because it led to Midtjylland's second goal.

Hong touched the ball 15 times in 26 minutes and completed eight of his 10 passes. He had three touches inside the opponent's penalty area and recorded two ball recoveries.

It was also a welcome moment for Hong. After failing to get enough opportunities at Mainz and spending time with Nantes and Gent, he joined Midtjylland ahead of this season. He has since gradually increased his playing time as he adapts to his new team.

Midtjylland also took note of the South Korean players' contributions. After the game, the club said, "The South Korean players' goals decided the match," adding, "Substitutes Cho Gue-sung and Hong Hyun-seok led the team to victory."

The club added, "South Korean national team striker Cho Gue-sung responded after coming on as a substitute with the opening goal. Hong Hyun-seok found space among the opposing players and shot, sending the ball past the goalkeeper and into the net."

With contributions from Cho and Hong, Midtjylland earned three points on the road. Copenhagen, which had been chasing the leaders, suffered a heavy defeat, allowing Midtjylland to extend its advantage and strengthen its position in the title race.

South Korean players are helping Midtjylland pursue its first league title in three years, since the 2023-24 season.
