Goa’s batting fireworks overshadowed by Khare’s century

TheTools · 24.11.2026 18:58 · source · permalink

PANAJI

Goa suffered a four-wicket defeat against Chhattisgarh in an Elite Group D match of the Syed Mushtaq Ali T20 Trophy. Chasing a target of 197, Chhattisgarh reached 197 for six in 19.5 overs, thanks to a brilliant unbeaten century by Amandeep Khare.

Batting first, Goa posted a competitive 196 for six in 20 overs. Captain Abhinav Tejerana led from the front with an aggressive 62 off just 33 balls, striking five boundaries and four sixes. Lalit Yadav provided valuable support with 57 off 36 deliveries, including six fours and two sixes.

Deepraj Gaonkar remained unbeaten on 23, while Vikash Singh contributed 14 runs. Vijay Yadav was the pick of the Chhattisgarh bowlers with two wickets.

In reply, Amandeep Khare produced a match-winning knock, smashing an unbeaten 116 off 62 balls, studded with nine fours and seven sixes. Dev Aditya Singh provided a late flourish with an unbeaten 24 off just seven deliveries to seal the victory.

For Goa, Shubham Tari claimed three wickets, while Vasuki Koushik picked up two. However, their efforts were not enough to defend the total.
