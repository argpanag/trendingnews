Blue Jays closer Louis Varland named AL Reliever of the Year

TheTools · 11.11.2026 19:27 · source · permalink

Toronto Blue Jays closer Louis Varland is taking home some hardware for his exceptional 2026 season.

Varland was named the American League Reliever of the Year on Wednesday, topping Canadian Cade Smith and Tampa Bay Rays closer Bryan Baker in voting conducted by the Baseball Writers' Association of America (BBWAA) before the post-season began.

This is the first year the BBWAA has handed out the award.

Varland earned 16 of 30 first-place votes, checking in second on four ballots, third on five, fourth on one and fifth on another.

“We couldn’t be happier for Louis," Blue Jays general manager Ross Atkins told Sportsnet's Shi Davidi. "The growth we’ve seen since we acquired him has been so impressive. He continues to take on more responsibility and thrive in any situation, including stepping up in a big way as our closer this year.

"The impact he had on our bullpen was significant, but just as important was the teammate, competitiveness, preparation and selflessness he brought to our team every day. He earned the trust of everyone around him, and this is incredibly well-deserved recognition.”

Fellow Blue Jays Spencer Miles and Tyler Rogers received down-ballot votes. Miles got fifth-place votes on two ballots, and Rogers got a fourth-place vote on one.

Smith, Baker and Josh Hader collectively received the other 14 first-place votes.

The 28-year-old Varland was lights out for the Blue Jays in 2026, converting 35 of 37 save opportunities after taking over the closer job in late April.

Varland finished the season with a 1.27 ERA and 97 strikeouts in 78 innings. He topped all qualified AL relievers in fWAR and ERA and finished in the top five in strikeouts, saves, win probability added and home runs allowed per nine innings.

He was also named to the AL All-Star Team in July, where he threw a scoreless eighth inning with one strikeout in the junior circuit's 4-0 victory.

Toronto acquired the six-foot-one right-hander from the Minnesota Twins at the 2025 trade deadline for outfielder Alan Roden and pitching prospect Kendry Rojas.

San Diego Padres closer Mason Miller took the Reliever of the Year honour in the National League, topping Philadelphia Phillies righty Jhoan Duran and Milwaukee Brewers closer Tevor Megill.

Miller was 5-2 with a 1.67 ERA, 38 saves and 129 strikeouts in 70 innings for San Diego.
