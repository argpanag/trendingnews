Passport Seva Locator: Find the Right PSK Before You Visit

TheTools · 08.09.2026 10:55 · source · permalink

Applicants seeking new passports, renewals, or related services can use the Passport Seva Locator to find suitable centers. The official tool helps applicants across India identify nearby Passport Seva Kendras (PSK) and avoid confusion over appointment locations. 

Passport Seva provides a locator through its official portal, helping applicants check centers before planning their visit. The tool lets applicants search using location details and identify relevant PSK or POPSK options. 

The hack becomes useful when applicants face limited appointment slots or unfamiliar service locations. Checking the center beforehand can reduce unnecessary travel and prevent visits to unsuitable offices. 

Applicants can open the official Passport Seva website and select the locator feature. The search process allows location details to identify available Passport Seva centers nearby. The portal can display relevant center information for easier planning before an appointment. 

Passport Seva Kendra centers handle passport-related services under the Regional Passport Office network. Post Office Passport Seva Kendras also provide passport services from selected India Post locations. Applicants should distinguish between finding a nearby center and confirming the assigned appointment center. 

An appointment remains important for most passport services before visiting a PSK. Official guidance asks applicants to complete registration, submit the application, and schedule an appointment online. 

The appointment confirmation specifies the Passport Seva Kendra selected for the visit. Applicants should carry required documents and the application reference details during their visit.

Using the official portal also helps applicants avoid unreliable agents and incorrect location information. The Passport Seva locator therefore offers a simple way to reduce travel mistakes and appointment confusion.
