LaMelo Ball to miss Friday's Emirates NBA Cup game with ankle sprain | NBA.com

TheTools · 28.11.2026 01:18 · source · permalink

Top Stories

The Wolves' star guard will miss Friday's game vs. Memphis after suffering a left ankle injury Wednesday against OKC.

Tim Willert | The Associated Press

LaMelo Ball exited early from Wednesday’s win against the Thunder.

OKLAHOMA CITY — Minnesota Timberwolves guard LaMelo Ball will miss Friday’s Emirates NBA Cup game against the Memphis Grizzlies (8:30 p.m. ET, League Pass) with an ankle sprain he suffered on Wednesday.

The Timberwolves have a chance to win West Group B and earn home court advantage in the Quarterfinals of the Knockout Rounds of the NBA Cup with a win over the Grizzlies after going 3-0 so far in the group. Memphis sits just behind Minnesota at 2-1 and will clinch the group with a win, but is eliminated with a loss. The Timberwolves can still qualify for the Knockout Rounds in a loss based on different scenarios of how Friday night’s Group Play finale unfolds.

Ball was helped off the court after landing awkwardly in the closing minutes of Wednesday night’s 113-106 win over the Oklahoma City Thunder.

Ball was battling Isaiah Hartenstein for a rebound with 2:29 remaining when he landed on Hartenstein’s foot and twisted his left ankle.

Coach Chris Finch said Ball’s injury “didn’t look good.”

“We’re going to have to figure out who we put in the starting lineup, activate our two-ways at this point and try to soldier on, figure out how to tweak things,” he said.

Ball, a 2022 NBA All-Star, was acquired in a trade with Charlotte in July. He scored 19 points on 7-of-18 shooting but made five of nine 3-pointers.

Teammate Jaden McDaniels, who led the Timberwolves with 32 points and also connected on five 3s, said he didn’t Ball get hurt.

“You don’t ever want to see your teammate get hurt,” he said. “I tried not to dwell on it … we still had a game to win.”

Ball was not made available for comment in Minnesota’s locker room after the game, and no additional information was provided by the Timberwolves at the time before he was officially ruled out on Minnesota’s injury report on Friday.
