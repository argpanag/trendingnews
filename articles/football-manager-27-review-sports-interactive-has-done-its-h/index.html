Football Manager 27 review: Sports Interactive has done its homework.

TheTools · 09.11.2026 16:00 · source · permalink

Our test conclusion

Football Manager 27

Football Manager 27 is a comeback after its buggy predecessor: more stable, more accessible, and with a tactics system that once again forces players to think carefully about their own team. Anyone disappointed by Football Manager 26, or who skipped it altogether, should definitely pick this up, especially with Game Pass. However, those who love Football Manager 2024 should prepare for a different experience.

  • Tactics & Training 9
  • Service 7
  • Match Engine 7
  • Presentation 8
  • Stability 8

Pro

  • Significantly more stable than Football Manager 26
  • A tactical system with effectiveness and complexity ensures greater realism.
  • More accessible interface with navigation bar
  • Tactical adjustments directly in the game
  • Cup draws and Deadline Day are back

Contrary to

  • The surface still needs to be adjusted to.
  • Defenders often react too late
  • VAR reviews almost every goal
  • Questions in press conferences tend to be repeated quickly.
  • Minor bugs at release
