Gypsy Rose working on new songs - Sleaze Roxx

TheTools · 12.11.2026 23:29 · source · permalink

Posted on November 12, 2026 by Olivier in News // 0 Comments

Gypsy Rose working on new songs

Photo courtesy of Michael Ross and Mikael Svensson

Canadian melodic rockers, Gypsy Rose, featuring frontman Michael Ross are working on new songs and one titled “I’m Sorry” is sounding like it’s in the final stages. Back in 1990, Gypsy Rose released their debut album, Prey, which was produced by KISS‘ Gene Simmons. The group has since released some compilation type albums with some unreleased songs consisting of Poisoned By Love (2012) via FnA Records, and Shattered Affair-1986-1989 Roots & Early Days (2018) and Rosary Of Tears-1988-1991 Outakes & Unreleased (2018) via Progaor.

The following message was posted on GypsyRose‘s Facebook page on November 6, 2026:

“The new track, “I’m Sorry” is freaking killer!!! Can’t wait for everybody to hear it. It’s not quite done yet. Still needs mixed, put in the harmonies and a few other things. Give it just a little more time.

Also working on a another track called, “Drive Me Like You Stole It”. Heavy and in your face, you guys will LOVE it!!!

Working on these new tracks is none other than:

Brian Joyce on guitar (Yep he’s back)
Rob Laidlaw on bass
Scott Charlton drums (Who was also in Shattered Heart)
Michael Ross on lead vocals and acoustic guitar

So hoping everybody is pumped and ready for some new Gypsy Rose!!! The band sure is! “
