The players are out on the pitch, in a fug of firework smoke after some very rapid-fire pyro greeted their arrival. Peter Drury, never knowingly understated, is the Sky commentator and is already talking nights with the stars, marble halls and “two teams galaxies apart”. Let’s go!

“I like to root for the underdog, so fingers crossed the Fishermen of the Cod Army will catch a really big one today!” cheers Peter Oh. It may interest you, Peter, that Fleetwood have a sub called Andy Cannon – surely destined to come on and sink the Gunners.

“‘Arsenal have paid us a lot of respect by naming such a strong team’?Not sure why Matt Lawlor’s being so polite, Arteta’s taking the proverbial Michael with that side. Three kids and a goalie on debut?” writes Dave Estherby. To be fair, one of those kids is Max Dowman, and there are a few first-team regulars in there.

Here’s the Fleetwood head coach, Matt Lawlor: “We’ve had to make changes, with the champions in town. But we’ve got a plan with substitutions to manage the game. Arsenal have paid us a lot of respect by naming such a strong team … it’s a very difficult challenge, but one we should enjoy, and not be overawed.”

“I was just thinking, with the two Highburys – I can only think of the St James(’) Parks (Exeter and Newcastle), but are there any other grounds with very similar names?” asks Shaun T.

“It’s very special, coming to these kinds of grounds – it’s proper English football,” says Mikel Arteta. “In these kinds of conditions, you’re in or you’re out. It’s great to have Marli, Max and Ceadach with us, Ilhan Meslier as well who is making his first appearance for us.” His advice to those young Gunners? “Give me a headache, give me a problem – give me a reason to play you.”

There is one other fourth-round Carabao Cup tie tonight, and it’s an all-League One affair. Bradford City, finalists in 2013, host Robbie Savage’s Peterborough – guaranteeing that at least one third-tier side will make the quarter-finals. Like the match at Highbury, it will go to penalties if still level after 90 minutes – and there’s no VAR.

So, Ilhan Meslier makes his debut in goal for Arsenal, with Christian Mosquera and Piero Hincapie starting as they return from injury; Martin Zubimendi also gets a run-out, while youngsters Max Dowman, Marli Salmon and Ceadach O’Neill all included in Mikel Arteta’s starting XI.

To give everyone a flavour of conditions, it is blowing a gale around the rather quaint Highbury Stadium, almost blowing my laptop onto the pitch. Mikel Arteta will not be too worried about his players adapting to the conditions and will see a front pairing of Kai Havertz and Viktor Gyökeres as integral to killing this game off as soon as possible.

Fleetwood, however, are here to give the Gunners a bloody nose at the very least. They know how to utilise the conditions to their favour and will be straight into the faces of the Premier League champions. They may sit 18th in League Two, but they saw off Sheffield United in the last round and will fancy doing likewise in front of a packed ground.

Some sad news to bring you: Peter Dobing, the captain of Stoke’s 1972 League Cup winning side, has died at the age of 87.

Dobing made 377 appearances over a decade with the Potters and also played for Blackburn and Manchester City. He captained Stoke to glory as they beat Chelsea 2-1 to clinch their first (and only) major trophy, before his playing career was ended by injury just one year later.

Stoke said in a statement: “All at Stoke are deeply saddened to learn of the passing of our ’72 League Cup-winning captain Peter Dobing at the age of 87. The thoughts and sincere condolences of all are with Peter’s friends and family at this immensely difficult time.”

Preamble

Some Arsenal fans spent years pining for one more game at Highbury Stadium; tonight, they finally get their wish. This particular Highbury is 250 miles northwest of Islington – the home of Fleetwood Town, a team the Gunners have never played before.

While their League Two form could hardly be less remarkable (18th place, with 14 points from 13 games), Fleetwood have picked off Chesterfield, Shrewsbury and second-tier Sheffield United in the Carabao Cup without conceding a goal, earning a last-16 glamour tie against the English champions.

With a trip to Anfield looming on Sunday, Mikel Arteta is expected to give his weariest stars a rest. Young talents like Marli Salmon, Ceadach O’Neill and, yes, Max Dowman are expected to start. They can expect testing conditions at a seaside ground where “the wind goes in all directions”.

Of course, even a depleted and under-experienced Arsenal side will be hot favourites to advance, but this has all the makings of a classic midweek Cup test. Kick-off: 8pm GMT.
