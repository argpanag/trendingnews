Sony will release approximately six internal exclusives for the PlayStation 5 console.

TheTools · 02.11.2026 07:29 · source · permalink

Sony Interactive Entertainment is building a large slate of releases from its internal PlayStation Studios division, slated for release exclusively on the PlayStation 5 home console next year. According to industry reports, the platform holder intends to release approximately six full-length exclusive titles created by its internal development teams. This strategy is aimed at significantly strengthening the market position of the desktop gaming device during the final stages of the current console cycle, eliminating the simultaneous release of these titles on personal computers or competing devices.

The list of upcoming exclusive premieres includes major, high-budget blockbusters across various genres from leading divisions of the Japanese publishing house. Among the most anticipated new releases is a cinematic interactive horror film. Until Dawn 2 from Firesprite, the superhero action film Marvel's Venom from Insomniac Games, and a new chapter or reimagining of the iconic God of War franchise. Fans of story-driven adventures can also look forward to the ambitious sci-fi project Intergalactic from Naughty Dog, which offers a deep story set in a completely new sci-fi universe.

The remaining announced list of exclusive releases includes projects from studios with original game concepts. Media Molecule is preparing to release its next unannounced game based on a new intellectual property, focused on unconventional creative interaction between users. Meanwhile, Bend Studio will present a full-fledged co-op action adventure, finally replacing its previously closed service offerings. This tight schedule of high-budget exclusives will provide PlayStation 5 owners with a continuous stream of high-quality single-player and co-op games throughout the coming year.
