ANOTHER large cash prize is on its way to County Cavan as some lucky winner has just scooped €100,000 in the Prize Bond Draw!

The €100k was won on Prize Bond number ABL243499 which was bought in 2015 and is held in Cavan.

Ireland State Savings made the announcement earlier today.

Prize Bonds are an Ireland State Savings product which, instead of paying interest, offers bond holders the chance to win cash prizes every week.

All winnings are tax-free, and Prize Bonds can be cashed in at any time after the minimum holding period of three months.

In addition to a €500,000 prize awarded on the last weekly draw of every calendar month, there are weekly draws where the top prize is €100,000.

Other cash prizes awarded each week include 50 of €1,000 and the remainder of €100.

Prize Bond holders can check draw results online at StateSavings.ie. The main winning numbers are also available in most Post Offices.
