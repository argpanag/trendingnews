Roman Reigns vs. LA Knight (Men's World Heavyweight Championship) Part 1

We're kicking things off with the men's world title bout between Roman Reigns and LA Knight. The Megastar was out first, and he got a nice reaction from the NOLA crowd, but the reaction for The OTC was louder.

As usual, Roman took his sweet time getting to the ring. Byron Saxton was doing the ring announcing for the introduction.

When they finally locked up, Reigns put Knight in a headlock, and then he scored the first takedown with a shoulder tackle. Knight popped up and caught him off guard before taking him out, which sent Roman out of the ring to regroup.

He took most of the 10-count to get back in. Knight put him in a headlock, but Reigns avoided the shoulder tackle and jumped over him to he could hit a hip toss.

Reigns stayed aggressive and started to build some momentum. He lined up in the corner for the Superman Punch, but Knight ducked and hit a series of jabs.

LA telegraphed a back body drop and took a kick to the face, but then he sent Reigns out of the ring so they could continue the fight at ringside.
