Andoni Iraola says he told Liverpool to keep Lewis Koumas amid transfer interest

TheTools · 15.09.2026 18:46 · source · permalink

Premier

League Action

"I see him as ready to help us," the Liverpool head coach said of Koumas. Robbie Jay Barratt - AMA/Getty Images

Andoni Iraola says he urged Liverpool not to loan out Lewis Koumas this season because he was convinced that the young forward was ready to step up and deliver at the highest level.

The 20-year-old Wales international has enjoyed a whirlwind few weeks with a Premier League debut off the bench against Newcastle United followed by Wednesday’s Champions League bow against Atletico Madrid late on.

Koumas, who helped Hull City win promotion from the Championship while on loan last season, attracted a lot of interest this summer, but Iraola was not prepared to sanction another temporary move.

“For me, Koumas, the first week I was here I told the club that he was staying for all the season,” he told reporters at a press conference to preview Saturday’s Premier League clash with Fulham at Anfield.

“I had very few doubts because he’s someone who covers us on the No 7, No 9, No 10 and No 11. He trains very well, good attitude, good player, scores goals.

“He needs to add more things like every young player but he already has a good amount of experience playing in the Championship. I see him as ready to help us. I like what he gives us. Whether it’s Newcastle away when we were losing or Atletico the other night when we were winning, he came on and helped us and I’m very happy to have him.”

Koumas’ only senior appearance for Liverpool before this season was against Southampton in the FA Cup under Jurgen Klopp in February 2024, when he scored on his debut. Since then,  had loan spells at Stoke City, Birmingham City and Hull.

“He’s very clear about what he needs to do to help the team,” Iraola added. “Sometimes that’s going to be on the ball, sometimes it’s going to be without the ball, sometimes it’s about attacking the spaces for someone else to receive the ball. He understands that and when you have someone with this mentality, it’s good for the team.”

Team news ahead of Fulham visit

Iraola expects Bradley Barcola to be fit to face Fulham after the £123million ($166m) summer signing was forced off during the 2-1 win over Atletico with cramp. However, Cody Gakpo, who missed midweek due to a groin issue, remains a doubt.

“We still need to train later but Barcola was just cramp,” he said.

“We have to evaluate and see how he feels today but there is no injury. He should be available if everything goes normal. With Cody, we will see. He didn’t train yesterday, today we will try with him. Let’s see if he’s ready for tomorrow or we have to wait for the next one.”

Liverpool named Hugo Ekitike in their Champions League squad last week as he continues his recovery from a ruptured Achilles. Iraola confirmed that the hope is that the French striker will be able to feature in the latter stages of the league phase after Christmas.

“It’s difficult with long-term injuries. He’s still far (away),” he said. “We had a meeting with the medical department before we completed the list for the European games. A big part of the rehab is still to come. But we decided to put him in the (Champions League) squad because there’s a hope and a realistic chance that he could help us in January.”
