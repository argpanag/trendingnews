Fire crews battle huge blaze at former primary school in Coatbridge

TheTools · 22.09.2026 17:59 · source · permalink

Fire crews are battling a huge blaze at a former primary school in Coatbridge.

Emergency services were called to Greenhill Primary School on Coltswood Road at around 5.20pm following reports of the blaze.

It is understood that seven appliances are at the scene and two high-reach appliances.

Footage and images shared with Glasgow Live show the building well ablaze with fire crews at the scene.

The historic 1902 building on Coltswood Road officially closed as an active school in August 2012 when the school co-located with Drumpark Primary School into a modernized campus on Albert Street.

A spokesperson for Police Scotland said: "Emergency services are responding to a fire at a derelict building on West George Street , Coatbridge. Please avoid the area. Those nearby should close all windows and doors. West George Street is closed at Stewart Street and Coltswood Road is closed at Inveresk Place."
