Tragedy as two North East men die during Great North Run after collapsing

TheTools · 14.09.2026 13:17 · source · permalink

The start line of the Great North Run 2026.(Image: Newcastle Chronicle)

Two North East men tragically died during this weekend's Great North Run. Both participants were 25-years-old and collapsed during the 13.1 mile route, it has been confirmed.

The hugely popular run returned on Sunday, with 63,000 runners taking part. The sad news of the deaths was confirmed in a statement on Monday by The Great Run Company.

It said: "It is with deep sadness that The Great Run Company can confirm the death of two participants at yesterday’s Great North Run. The two runners, both 25 year old males from the North East, collapsed at the event, and despite the very best efforts of our medical teams later passed away. In each case the cause of death will be determined in due course.

"Everyone involved in organising the Great North Run extends their heartfelt condolences to the families and friends of the two participants. We are continuing to support their families during this incredibly difficult time, and our thoughts and sympathies are with all those who loved and knew them. To respect the privacy of the families, no further details will be released."

In 2024, Sam Wealleans sadly passed away after falling ill during the Great North Run. The 29-year-old, from Newbiggin-by-the-Sea, was raising money on behalf of mental health charity, Mind in memory of his sister, Carly, and a close family friend.
