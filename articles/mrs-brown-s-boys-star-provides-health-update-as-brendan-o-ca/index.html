Mrs Brown's Boys star provides health update as Brendan O'Carroll misses NTAs

TheTools · 08.09.2026 21:23 · source · permalink

A Mrs Brown’s Boys star provided a health update after its star Brendan O'Carroll missed the BBC comedy’s National Television Awards win.

It was recently confirmed that Brendan cancelled recordings of the 2026 Mrs Brown’s Boys Christmas and New Year’s Specials as he needed immediate surgery. The actor has been working on the BBC series since 2011.

The series sees Brendan playing grandmother Agnes Brown, who likes interfering in the lives of her friends and family members. She goes to any extent to find out what secrets her family is keeping from her.

Brendan said in a statement last month that he needs the surgery to fix fractures in his jaw, meaning he won't be able to go ahead with filming the BBC show. He also announced that he would have to postpone his Mrs Brown’s Boys D’Panto? shows in Dublin and Killarney, which were scheduled for later this year.

“It is with sincere regret I must announce that due to an unexpected medical issue I will have to cancel the recordings of the 2026 Mrs. Brown’s Boys Christmas and New Year’s Specials.

“Following a CT Scan, it was found that I had two fractures in my mandible (jaw) which requires immediate surgery and a few months recovery. I am talking with the BBC about getting Mrs. Brown’s Boys back on the screen in 2027.

“I will sadly have to postpone both the 3 Arena Dublin and Gleneagle Arena Shows “Mrs. Brown’s Boys D’Panto?” which were scheduled for later this year. We are currently working with MCD to try to reschedule these shows for 2027. I would like to thank MCD and the BBC for their support and understanding at this difficult time,” he said at the time.

Mrs Brown’s Boys was up for Best Comedy at the 2026 National Television Awards. The series had not won the accolade since 2014, which was the last of its three consecutive victories.

It faced stiff competition in the form of Amandaland, Changing Ends, Last One Laughing and Not Going Out. However, members of the public crowned it the winner in a shock result.

Instead, a member of the Mrs Brown’s Boys team came to the stage as he stated: “Apologies I’m not Brendan, Brendan is in Florida recovering from an operation. All love to you Brendan and get well soon. Of course, behind every man is a great woman, love to Jenny [Brendan’s wife] as well.”

From here a speech from Paddy Houlihan, who plays Brendan’s son on the series, was read. It stated: “Brendan O’Carroll wasn’t expecting to win…

“We are truly humbled that after 16 years of making this show with this great family we have won this… Thank you so much for voting for us.

“Normally if we won an award Brendan would be up here thanking every member of the team, it means that much to him and we are that much a family.”

Paddy added: “I want to break with tradition, and thank the one person who without none of us would be here. I know he’s in your hearts tonight, a huge thank you and get well soon to Brendan O’Carroll.”
