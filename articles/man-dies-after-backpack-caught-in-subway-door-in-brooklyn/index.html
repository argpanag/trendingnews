Man dies after backpack caught in subway door in Brooklyn

TheTools · 30.09.2026 22:56 · source · permalink

The man, in his 50s, fell under the train before it was left the Utica Avenue station.

A man died in Brooklyn after falling between subway cars when his backpack got caught in a closing door, police sources said.

The horrific death unfolded aboard an A train during the Monday evening commute, shortly before 6 p.m., in Bedford-Stuyvesant.

The man, in his 50s, fell under the train before it was left the Utica Avenue station.
