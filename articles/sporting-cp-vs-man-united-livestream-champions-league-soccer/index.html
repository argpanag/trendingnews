Pochettino’s first game in charge as United boss couldn’t have gone much worse. Defender Leny Yoro was sent off within 10 minutes, and the Red Devils subsequently crashed to a nightmare 4-0 defeat to bitter rival Liverpool at Anfield in the English Premier League on Sunday. While United now appear to be in a domestic relegation battle, a win here could see it move to within a point of the automatic qualification spots. 

Sporting, meanwhile, comes into this game in an encouraging 10th position in the UCL standings with nine points from a possible 12 available, having claimed a narrow 1-0 win over Shakhtar Donetsk at Stamford Bridge last time out. 

More from CNET

Sporting CP takes on Manchester United on Wednesday, Nov. 25, at the Estádio José Alvalade in Lisbon, with kickoff set for 8 p.m. WET in Portugal. That makes it a 3 p.m. ET or 12 p.m. PT start in the US and Canada, and an 8 p.m. GMT kickoff in the UK. For soccer fans Down Under, it’s a 7 a.m. AEDT start in Australia on Thursday.
