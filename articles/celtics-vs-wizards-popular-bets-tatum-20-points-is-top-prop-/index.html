The Wizards host the Celtics in the nations capital tonight, with Boston looking to avoid a fifth straight loss.

The Boston Celtics got off to a hot start this season, winning 11 of their first 13 games, but have since dropped four straight games in agonizing fashion. In the last three games, Boston has lost by a combined total of just six points.

The main issue for the Celtics is that their offense has severely fallen off. The team has averaged just 97.3 points per game during their four-game losing streak—a scoring rate that would rank last in the league. Fortunately, Boston has leaned heavily on its defense, currently holding opposing teams to a league-low 102.1 points per game.

Jayson Tatum did not play against the Sacramento Kings last week, but has scored 24+ points in each game during the losing streak.

The Washington Wizards have endured nearly the opposite fate. After managing just one win in their first 14 games, Washington has bounced back to win three straight.

Anthony Davis has looked particularly impressive in the last three games, averaging 27.7 points and 10.7 rebounds over that stretch. Davis has been a quality veteran addition to the Wizards, helping to guide youngsters AJ Dybantsa, Alex Sarr, and Kyshawn George as they develop into stars in their own right.
