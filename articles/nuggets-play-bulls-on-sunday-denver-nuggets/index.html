The Nuggets head into Chicago, playing their best basketball of the season. They're currently riding the high of two huge home wins.

First, Denver pulled off an impressive 17-point comeback against the Boston Celtics on Wednesday. The Celtics are currently the second-best team in the Eastern Conference at 11-5, and the Nuggets earned a big win behind 18 points from reserve guard DeMar DeRozan, who got to the free-throw line four separate times in the fourth quarter.

The Nuggets were even better on Friday when they defeated the Houston Rockets, 133-124. Pristine execution played a big role in the result, as the Nuggets shot 54.7 percent from the field, 43.3 percent from three-point range, and 92.9 percent from the free-throw line. They also had seven different players record 10 or more points on the same night.

It was a true team effort.

Those wins brought Denver's overall record to 9-5. Chicago, meanwhile, is just 4-11 this season.
