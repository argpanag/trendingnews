Aha, Miles Starforth’s match report is with us.

That being the case, we’re finished here. Thanks a lot for your company and comments – peace out.

“Top to bottom, the PL is the best football league in the world,” returns Mary Waltz. “This match would convince no one of that argument. My boys won so I am fine. I wouldn’t want to be on the losing side of this stinker.”

I thought the first half of Liverpool v Arsenal wasn’t bad, but I’ve seen very few Premier League matches of requisite standard so far this season, and the excitement-level hasn’t been great either. I hope things improve, but I’m not certain they will.

The Premier League table

That’s one win in five for Newcastle, and the fans register disapproval at the whistle. Most of the clubs near the bottom I can see improving, but Newcastle, in 13th, I can see staying roughly the same as they’re passed.

FULL TIME: Newcastle United 0-1 Everton

A decent enough performance from Everton, who move seventh; Newcastle are in trouble.

90+8 min Hall tries to lift Armstrong off the ground, but it was a nasty challenge and he’s hurt. Burn is booked for some kind of shoving/dissent situation, so too Johnson.

RED CARD! Newcastle's Jacob Ramsey is sent off for a second bookable offence

Ramsey’s studs perhaps tickle the top of the ball, but he careers into Armstrong, leaving studs on foot. We’ve seen those given as straight reds but instead, it’s two yellows in a minute – have that, Lisandro Martinez.

90+8 min There are lots of empty seats in the ground now; I don’t think anyone expects Newcastle to score.

90+6 min A clever pass from Hackney finds Johnson, but the ref opts not to play advantage when Ramsey clatters the former; he’s booked.

90+5 min Dewsbury-Hall drives Everton forward, then Hackney and Garner combine to win a free-kick. I wonder, will Moyes ever pluck up the courage to go with those as a midfield trio?

90+3 min Newcastle are a really poor side, and might just find themselves in a relegation ruckus. Who is going to score their goals, and how will they be created?

90+2 Everton send on Hackney for George.

90+1 min We’ll have eight additional minutes; Everton clear the corner.

90 min Here come Newcastle though, Toure nashing down the left and taking Steur’s pass away from O’Brien before hitting the line and cutting back for Wissa! This is a chance! He spins, shoots low, and Pickford dives right, away from it … only to save with his feet! That’s excellent work, but really should’ve been the equaliser.

89 min In comes the corner and O’Brien leaves it for Tarkowski, who can’t control. Still, Everton are nearly there, while the home fans move mooths into circles, warming up for the inevitable full-time booing.

88 min George finds Dewsbury-Hall, whose cross is blocked behind for a corner – exactly what Everton want.

87 min Gwan Bazoumansa lad, it’s your big chance. He replaces Dedic and immediately, skates down the left, his cross not bad, but one Pickford is able to collect.

85 min Murphy sends over an outswinging cross, Osula pulling off Tarkowski. But, 15 yards out, he’s got to turn body and neck in different directions to flicks the ball towards the far post, the effort landing wide. Then, seconds later, Steur, who’s made a difference, finds Wissa, who shoots straight at Pickford from the edge.

84 min Newcastle are still to manage a shot on target. I don’t think the fans will tolerate much more of this.

82 min Change for Everton, Keane on for Branthwaite – who’s played fairly well and might just have the attributes to mature into a top player. But can he stay fit? Oh, and a couple of minutes ago, Johnson replaced Barry.

79 min Murphy crosses and Pickford punches, but Newcastle recycle and Dedic sidefoots into Ramsey’s path, begging to him to unleash a volley from the edge, which he does … and the contact is beautiful … but the effort skids just wide of the near post.

77 min Why did Newcastle sign Toure if they’re not going to use him when a goal down, in the closing stages, at home? How much worse can he be?

75 min Changes for Newcastle: Murphy and Ramsey for Elanga and Joelinton. Their team have a free-kick, following an unnecessary foul by Barry, but Barnes’ delivery is poor and O’Brien heads clear.

73 min Barry’s up and looks like he’ll be OK; Moyes is booked for coming on to the field to protest the decision. I’d be keen to hear VAR’s working because I think it’s clear Schar took man before ball.

72 min Hmmm, I’m not sure. I think the foot with which Schar plays the ball comes through Barry’s legs and catches him first, but VAR says not. Barry, meantime is down and in pain.

70 min And here comes one, Dewsbury-Hall finding Rohl down the right, and he crosses low, seeking Barry, but Schar slides in to get there first, just, before ploughing through the striker. Great tackle, provided he got there first; if not, VAR will have some thoughts.

70 min Newcastle are dominating now, but Everton might easily nobble them on the counter.

68 min “This is why I despise xG,” returns Maryb Waltz. “Does it indicate superior play? Or does it show an inability to finish? I lean towards the last question.”

I find it largely useless for individual games – we can watch those. It’s fairly useful as a predictor of the direction in which a team or player might go, looking at a raft of games.

66 min And again Barnes crosses to, the ball flicking off Dewsbury-Hall; Elanga sneaks around the back and looks certain to score, but his poke hits Mykolenko, the post and Pickford, rolling across the line, and Everton clear.

65 min A Barnes cross causes minor panic but Everton clear, though Newcastle maintain pressure.

64 min Changes for Newcastle, Steur and Wissa on for Gonzalez and Willock.

63 min Garner’s corner picks out Branthwaite at the far post and he heads down and for the near post, Hornicek doing well to tip behind; the following corner is punched clear weakly, but Garner’s ensuing shot foes out for a throw. It’s not entirely dissimilar to this:

62 min Everton counter, Dewsbury Hall collecting possession 25 yards out. He’s got Geroge to his left, but instead tries a curler and it’s going over, but Hornicek helps it on its way, conceding a corner in the process.

61 min Newcastle have improved a little, Elanga looking to get around the side of the Everton defence and crossing, the ball bouncing about before Willock heads for goal, Pickford fielding easily enough.

58 min Elanga shoots from distance and Garner wears it in the phizog; that should remind him he’s alive.

56 min The corner comes to nowt but Everton keep on Newcastle, who have been every bit as tediously tepid tonight as they were at Leeds and Coventry. The game being the game, if they don’t start playing soon, Matthias Jaissle will himself getting played.

55 min Newcastle were, I think booed off at half-time, and they’ve not played any better since then. Everton win a corner and though it’s cleared, they have Newcastle boxed, George crossing to the back pot where Branthwaite is up way above Hall, his header tipped behind by Hornicek.
