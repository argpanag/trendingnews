Halftime Observations

  • Germany is playing much better in this game than the one in Augsburg. The team is actually getting chances in good areas and even half chances, some of which nearly resulted in a goal.
  • Derry Scherhant is causing headaches for the Greek defence, using his pace and creativity to try and make something work. He would’ve gotten an assist if Jonathan Burkardt’s goal stood.
  • The Greek players like to swarm the player with the ball, body them off it, and then start a counter attack. Germany seems to have figured out that smart passes, timing, and patience is the key to break them down.

Germany finally won a football match under the tutelage of ex-Liverpool FC boss Jürgen Klopp courtesy of a 2-0 win over Serbia in Munich. Bayern Munich star Tom Bischof and Liverpool attacker Florian Wirtz did the damage, scoring a goal each to secure the three-points for Die Mannschaft.

The team plays in Thessaloniki today, where they will be hosted by Greece who sit top of the Nations League group.

Germany wins 2-0. The young Germans will have a spring in their step from the win against Serbia.
