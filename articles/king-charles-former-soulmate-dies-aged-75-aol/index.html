King Charles' former 'soulmate' dies aged 75 - AOL

TheTools · 12.09.2026 16:50 · source · permalink

King Charles' former girlfriend, who was once considered his "soulmate" and a possible future Queen, died at the age of 75 of September 2, it has been announced. Davina Mary Morley, the granddaughter of ICI tycoon Lord McGowan, died with her three sons, Tom, Billy and Henry, at her side.

Her family revealed that a celebration of her life will take place in November at her local church near her beloved home in the Cotswolds. Her relationship with the future King began in 1976 and she was considered one of his "English Roses," as well as the most beautiful of "Charlie's Angels" - the press' nickname for his girlfriends, which also included Lady Jane Wellesley, Georgiana Russell and Lady Sarah Spencer. After they were seen attending a wedding together, the pair were tipped for marriage. She had also been pictured with Queen Elizabeth II and Prince Philip at an equestrian event.

Born Davina Sheffield, she was the granddaughter of Lord McGowan, the Scottish industrialist who led Imperial Chemical Industries for two decades. She was also a cousin of Samantha Cameron, the wife of the former Tory Prime Minister, Lord Cameron, The Sun reported.

The Sheffield family lineage traces back to King Charles II.

Her husband, Jake, died in April 2022 at the age of 80. They married in 1981, the same year Charles wed Princess Diana. The couple went on to have three sons - Thomas, born in 1983, William, born in 1984 and Henry, born in 1987. She is also survived by two stepsons, Dominic and Sebastian, from Mr Morley's first marriage.

Ms Morley and the then Prince of Wales' relationship fell apart after it emerged she had lived with her previous boyfriend, James Beard, in a "love nest". It was also rumoured that she had initially turned down Charles because of her relationship with Mr Beard.

However, despite winning the Royal Family's approval, the pair split, as it was tradition that the heir to the throne find an "unblemished bride", which reportedly left Charles devastated.

Ms Morley was known for her charity work, having travelled to Vietnam to volunteer at an orphanage as the war concluded.
