Gordon stars in comfortable Barca win over Sabah

Anthony Gordon scored twice as Barcelona cruised to a Champions League victory over Sabah in Azerbaijan.

The England winger opened the scoring just past the half hour with a tap-in after Karim Adeyemi's cross was pushed into his path by Sabah goalkeeper Stas Pokatilov.

Gordon doubled the visitors' lead early in the second half, again finding himself in the right place to pounce after Gabriel Jesus' shot was saved.

Barcelona kicked on and put the game to bed as Adeyemi slid in to convert Alejandro Balde's teasing low cross before Jesus bundled the ball in from a corner.

Having conceded three goals in nine minutes, Sabah responded well and pulled one back through Joy-Lance Mickels' cool finish following good work from Christian Nwachukwu.

It was no more than the hosts deserved having caused Barcelona problems prior to Gordon's opener, with Ivan Lepinjica's sliced effort looping up and over visiting keeper Joan Garcia, only to bounce back off the bar.

But, even without the injured Rodri and Lamine Yamal, and with top-scorer Raphinha among those rested from the start, Barcelona's superior quality told and the three points were never really in doubt.

A fourth victory in five European games keeps Barca firmly in the top eight and on course for automatic qualification for the last 16, while Sabah's fourth loss in the competition leaves them 32nd of the 36 teams.
