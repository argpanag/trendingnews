Avengers: Doctor Doom to Appear at Baseball - Marvel Prepares a Surprise for the Start of the World Series

TheTools · 22.10.2026 20:07 · source · permalink

Translated with AI from the Russian original.

Fans hope to see a new trailer for the upcoming blockbuster.

The official Major League Baseball (MLB) account announced the showing of new footage from the film "Avengers: Doctor Doom" during the first game of the World Series. The organizers published a short teaser, advising not to miss the "exclusive preview" of the upcoming blockbuster.

It is currently unknown what exactly will be shown during the broadcast, but fans are already hoping to see a new trailer, as there are less than two months left until the release.

The first game of the World Series will take place on the evening of October 23 in the USA, and according to Moscow time, the broadcast will begin on October 24 at 03:00. The exact time for showing the materials has not yet been announced.
