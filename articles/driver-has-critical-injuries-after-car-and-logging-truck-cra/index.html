Driver has critical injuries after car and logging truck crash

TheTools · 26.11.2026 23:38 · source · permalink

RNZ / News / Emergencies / Local

Link: https://www.rnz.co.nz/news/eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiJ9._v4.laWzzQVil69j0DGqQk4Ywh3JMAvc5jXoA1Fn8ldP8DY/emergencies_local/2760691%2Fdriver-has-critical-injuries-after-car-and-logging-truck-crash

A person is in critical condition after a crash involving a car and a logging truck in Tasman District.

Emergency services were called to the crash on Kotere Tophouse Road in Atapo at about 8:15am on Tuesday.

Police said the driver of the car was reported to have critical injuries.

St John confirmed it took one person in a critical condition to Nelson Hospital.

It sent a helicopter, an ambulance, and one rapid response vehicle to the scene.

Police said Kotere Tophouse Rd remained closed while they worked at the scene.
