Why Nick Saban is so impressed with Tennessee coach Josh Heupel

TheTools · 21.11.2026 16:49 · source · permalink

Volquest Football

Much of the attention surrounding Tennessee’s matchup with LSU has centered on Lane Kiffin’s team’s struggles leading into this all-important matchup for Tennessee, but former Alabama head coach Nick Saban believes more attention should be on Josh Heupel and the job he has done with the Vols this season.

During Saturday morning’s edition of ESPN’s College GameDay, Saban praised Heupel for his ability to adapt offensively and find different ways to win.

“We talk about Lane, but I think Josh Heupel is who we should be talking about,”Saban said. “Talking about the job that he’s done, the changes he’s made philosophically from fastball to beating them with speed to run the ball, play good defense. I mean, Josh Heupel has done a phenomenal job of adapting to the team that he has.

Saban is certainly familiar with Heupel’s fast-paced offense, having experienced it firsthand during Tennessee’s 52-49 win over Alabama in 2022. The Vols totaled 567 yards of offense that afternoon, including 385 through the air, as Hendon Hooker threw five touchdown passes to Jalin Hyatt.

Heupel has found success with a much different offensive approach in 2026, drastically in comparison to just a season ago.

In 2025, Tennessee averaged 466.3 yards of total offense per game, including 292.8 passing yards and 173.5 rushing yards. Through ten games this season, the Vols have leaned heavily on the ground game, averaging 214 rushing yards per contest compared to just 171.5 yards through the air.

Tennessee has scored 24 rushing touchdowns compared to 11 passing touchdowns this season, including just four touchdown passes during SEC play.

The change in offensive philosophy, along with Tennessee’s ability to navigate significant injuries on both sides of the football, has caught the attention of ESPN’s Rece Davis as well.

“He should be coach of the year regardless of what happens the rest of the way. And all the noise around LSU, Tennessee has just been quiet and they keep winning. They lost their best pass rusher, they lost their best wide receiver, you mentioned the quarterbacks, they just keep running. They’ve thrown four touchdown passes in conference play,” Davis said.

The Vols have lost leading receiver Mike Matthews and top pass rusher Tyree Weathersby to season-ending injuries in recent weeks, but enter Saturday’s matchup with LSU at 8-2 overall and 5-2 in SEC play.
