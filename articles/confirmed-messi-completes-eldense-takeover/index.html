Confirmed: Messi completes Eldense takeover

TheTools · 01.10.2026 21:44 · source · permalink

It is now completely official: Lionel Messi is the new owner of Eldense. The Alicante-based outfit confirmed the news, having initially reported the Argentine forward's takeover almost three weeks ago.

"Club Deportivo Eldense officially announce the acquisition of the club by Leo Messi, ushering in an exciting new era in the institution's history of over 100 years," read the club's official statement.

The club embark on this fresh chapter with "the ambition to consolidate and grow within professional football". However, Spain's National Sports Council (CSD) will have the final say, as the governing body must ratify the transaction "under the terms established in the Sports Law".

On the pitch, the 'Azulgrana' outfit currently sit 19th and in the relegation zone to Primera Federacion, having mustered just five points from a possible 21 (one win and two draws).
