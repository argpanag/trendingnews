  8. Hang on, what exactly is an AI data centre?published at 09:15 GMT
    09:15 GMT

    Data centres are warehouses full of powerful computer servers, relied on by the digital world.

    They are used to run a range of digital services - including banking, public services and video streaming - and are crucial for cloud computing and artificial intelligence (AI). Many of us interact with a data centre hundreds of times of day.

    And they are at the heart of the AI boom, housing the advanced computer chips and servers needed to run services like ChatGPT and Claude.

    But the speed at which they're growing, the enormous amount of electricity they consume and concerns about their environmental impact are fuelling a public backlash around the world.

    Data centres have typically been located near to cities where it's easier to access high voltage power lines or water resources for cooling. But as they get bigger, more are being built in rural areas.

    When it comes to actual capacity, the US is ahead by a much larger margin, with China in second place and Europe lagging far behind.
