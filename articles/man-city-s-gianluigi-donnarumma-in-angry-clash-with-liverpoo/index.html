Man City’s Gianluigi Donnarumma in angry clash with Liverpool fans

TheTools · 14.10.2026 20:31 · source · permalink

Italy international escorted away by staff after confronting rival supporters over ‘cheat’ chants

Gianluigi Donnarumma was involved in an angry tunnel flashpoint as tempers boiled over following Manchester City’s 3-0 win at Liverpool.

Footage posted on social media showed City’s players being berated as they left the pitch and made their way to the dressing room, having celebrated in front of their own supporters for five minutes after the game. They were repeatedly told they were “cheats” and “a disgrace” in the wake of being found guilty of breaching Premier League financial regulations. 

Many of Enzo Maresca’s squad ignored the abuse and Iliman Ndiaye – their £65m signing from Everton – even made light of the situation, giving as good as he got by gesturing to those taunting him that they should keep on crying.

The situation, however, threatened to spill over as City goalkeeper Donnarumma came back down the tunnel to confront someone who had shouted at him. He was ushered away by club staff before anything could escalate.

It was a game that had high potential for difficulties off the pitch and Merseyside Police said on Monday that they made 10 arrests and one of their officers was left with a serious facial injury and needed hospital treatment, following an incident as fans left the stadium.

In total, one man was arrested on suspicion of two offences of assaulting a police officer, five were arrested on suspicion of tragedy chanting, one arrest was made on suspicion of being drunk and disorderly; two were for affray and one was on suspicion of throwing items on the pitch.

Chief Inspector Leanne Hobin said: “Officers were on duty outside the stadium to smoothly facilitate the movements of those travelling to and from the game, as well as minimise any disruption on the roads outside. The officers and stewards are there to protect the public and keep them safe.

“It is extremely disappointing that an officer has sustained a serious injury while carrying out that duty. Enquiries into the full circumstances are ongoing so that anyone responsible for the criminality can be brought to justice.”
