Jackson Bowling looking to keep the ball rolling in 10th season - The Telegram News

TheTools · 18.11.2026 22:00 · source · permalink

After a pair of third-place finishes in the Frontier Athletic Conference (FAC) last season, Jackson bowling is looking to continue that success in the program’s 10th season.

Last year, the boys’ team had a 13-9 record, but returned all but one varsity player, including former State Qualifier Blake Woodyard. Woodyard qualified for State as a freshman, and now entering his senior year is expected to be among the best bowlers in the FAC this season.

With fellow senior Deegan Evans also returning, Head Coach Scot Kilgour has high expectations for his team this season. “I could easily see an individual making it to State,” Kilgour said. “If the boys do what they are capable of, we could end up going to State as a team.” 

Both the Ironmen and Ironladies will be tested in conference. “The FAC is by far one of the toughest bowling leagues in the southern part of the state,” Kilgour said. Hillsboro and Miami Trace are perinatally tough and will be challenging to take down for Jackson. 

Kilgour graduated two starters from his girls’ team that had a 16-6 record last season. With seniors Emily Miller and McKenna Davis, along with junior Navaeh Heitmann returning, Kilgour believes the Ironladies will have a strong core to start the season. “I lost a couple really good starters, but I’ve got some good returners that should do pretty good,” Kilgour said. 

Ohio is one of the top states for high school bowling in the country, and Kilgour hopes that people will get hooked by the atmosphere.

“If you’ve never been to a high school bowling match, I think you’d be shocked at how lively it is,” Kilgour said. Jackson opened its season on Wednesday, Nov. 18 against Vinton County at LaElla Lanes in Wellston.
