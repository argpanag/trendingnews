Doctor X Episode 9: Jang Hui-suk's Secret

TheTools · 06.11.2026 16:29 · source · permalink

Jang Hui-suk surprises the operating room in Episode 9, revealing a connection to Su-jeong that goes far beyond her agency.

Jang Hui-suk is more than the agent managing Gye Su-jeong’s career in Doctor X: Mafia in White. At the end of Episode 9, she enters the operating room and reveals that she is a general surgeon. The surprise grows when Su-jeong tells the medical team that Jang is also her teacher.

  • Episode 9’s Cuba flashback shows Su-jeong missing her graduation ceremony to observe surgery, then joining the procedure.
  • A medical strike leaves the hospital short-staffed; during eight-year-old Hyeon-a’s operation, falling blood pressure prompts a call for help.
  • Episode 9 ends without revealing the outcome of Hyeon-a’s operation.

The discovery changes what we know about their relationship. Episode 4 established that Jang raised Su-jeong. Now, the drama reveals that their connection also involves the surgeon’s medical training.

Is Jang Hui-suk a doctor in Doctor X?

Yes. The revelation comes around 1:02:49, during the operation on Lee Hyeon-a, an eight-year-old girl with a complex malignant tumor.

Su-jeong had insisted on performing a procedure that other surgeons considered too dangerous. With the medical strike leaving the hospital short-staffed, the operation reaches a critical point: Hyeon-a’s blood pressure drops, and the team calls for additional help.

That is when Jang enters and introduces herself as general surgeon Jang Hui-suk. She then asks the team to prepare a harmonic scalpel. The doctors are stunned, until Su-jeong explains: “She is my teacher.”

Su-jeong’s graduation in Cuba foreshadowed the revelation

Episode 9 opens with a memory from Su-jeong’s graduation in Cuba. She misses the ceremony because she chooses to observe a surgery and ends up taking part in the procedure. Jang is frustrated, while people close to Su-jeong try to organize a celebration.

After repeating the medical oath, Su-jeong thanks Jang and says she became a doctor because of her. The words already established Jang’s importance in her life. The operating-room revelation now shows that her influence extended beyond personal support.

What changes between Jang Hui-suk and Su-jeong?

Until now, Jang was primarily presented as the businesswoman who negotiates Su-jeong’s contracts and manages her finances. Episode 4 revealed that Jang Hui-suk raised Su-jeong, giving new meaning to the doctor’s 100,000-won monthly allowance and unexplained debts.

Episode 9 adds another role that the hospital staff apparently knew nothing about. Jang does not simply manage Su-jeong’s career: she has surgical training and is recognized by Su-jeong herself as her teacher.

However, the drama has not explained where Jang practiced medicine, when she stopped operating or how she came to run an agency for temporary doctors.

Does Jang’s secret reveal who Doctor X really is?

Not entirely. In the same episode, the director questions Jang about Doctor X’s identity. She argues that Su-jeong deserves that name because she puts patients first and takes on operations other doctors refuse.

That answer does not definitively establish who performed an earlier surgery in Cuba under the name Dr. X. The connection was already introduced in our review of the first two episodes of Doctor X.

Episode 8 brought that mystery directly into the hospital, as explored in our article asking whether Gye Su-jeong is the real Doctor X. Episode 9 adds Jang’s medical background without conclusively resolving the older case.

The chapter ends without revealing the outcome of Hyeon-a’s operation. For now, one fact is confirmed: Jang Hui-suk is a surgeon and Su-jeong’s teacher. What remains unanswered is why she kept that part of her life hidden from Guseo Hospital.
