  8. Nyoni, Koumas and Ngumoha are all 'first team players'published at 17:45 BST
    17:45 BST

    LASK v Liverpool (17:45 BST)

    Liverpool

    Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola speaking to TNT Sport before kick-off on trusting young players: "For me Trey [Nyoni], [Lewis] Koumas and Rio [Ngumoha] are young players coming in from the academy but they are first team players.

    "They are first team players because we trust them, and in our situation, where we have some injuries, and we come from playing three days ago. I think they can play well, and I hope they do well."

    On what a convincing win would mean: "Confidence, and especially the points, because this is the Champions League and in the Champions League it's very costly, especially away from home, where we have to be ready to play very well. We won the first game, let's win today and put ourselves in a better position."

    LASK v Liverpool (17:45 BST)

    Andoni Iraola said that he wasn't too bothered about the transfer fees of Florian Wirtz and Bradley Barcola, despite Wirtz only managing one assist so far this season and Barcola failing to get a goal involvement yet.

    "The possibilities are the same. The other day, if (Lewis) Koumas scores the goal that he connects with really well, then it is one assist for Bradley Barcola. His performance is exactly the same but we will all say, 'he played really well with an assist'.

  19. 'City deserved to win on the pitch'published at 17:16 BST
    17:16 BST

    Man City v PSG (20:00 BST)

    Paris St-Germain boss Luis Enrique says he was surprised to learn Manchester City had been found guilty of breaching Premier League financial rules and hopes the club can avoid sanctions.

    "There is a lot of noise around Manchester City and there is often noise around big clubs," said Enrique when asked about City's guilty verdict at a news conference at Etihad Stadium on Tuesday.

    "We just have to wait and see what the final decision will be. I hope everything works out well for Manchester City because I think what they have done on the pitch has been incredible over the last few years and they certainly deserved to win on the pitch all the trophies they have won.

    "Now we have to wait to see what the authorities decide or the judge is going to say."
