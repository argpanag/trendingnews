Tests carried out on the Nerazzurri midfielder

Curtis Jones underwent clinical and instrumental tests this morning at the Humanitas Research Hospital in Rozzano.

The tests revealed a myofascial strain in the adductor muscles of his right thigh.

His condition will be reassessed over the coming days.
