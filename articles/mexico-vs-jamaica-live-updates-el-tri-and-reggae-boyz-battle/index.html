Mexico vs Jamaica LIVE updates: El Tri and Reggae Boyz battle in Concacaf clash

TheTools · 14.11.2026 21:10 · source · permalink

Mexico men's national team coach Rafa Marquez wasn't able to get a win in his first four matches in charge, but there is little margin for error in his first official contest, the first leg of a Concacaf Nations League quarterfinal with Jamaica on Saturday, Nov. 14 in Kingston.

The second leg is scheduled for Tuesday, Nov. 17 in Monterrey, Mexico. The aggregate winner advances to the semifinals of the tournament, which will played in March at SoFi Stadium in Southern California.

Mexico won the 2025 Nations League, its first title after the United States took home the hardware in the first three editions. The Reggae Boyz reached this round by finishing second in Group B in qualifying with seven points, clinching their spot with a 1-1 draw at Honduras in October.

20': Jahmali Waite keeps Jamaica-Mexico scoreless

Twenty minutes into the match and neither team has been able to find a way through, with Mexico dominating possession. Jahmali Waite has had to make two saves for Jamaica already and it could be a long night.

10': Mexico dominating possession

El Tri has enjoyed about 70% possession in the first 10 minutes of the match but have been unable to capitalize, managing just one shot – a speculative go from Gilberto Mora.
