Google is a technology partner for the launch of America.gov.

TheTools · 29.09.2026 00:00 · source · permalink

Sep 29, 2026

Today, the White House announced America.gov, a streamlined "front door" designed to simplify how citizens access federal services online. Google is proud to be named a technology partner in this vital initiative, leveraging Gemini to help more than 100 million people access critical public resources with greater speed and ease.

By supporting the administration’s vision for digital modernization, we are committed to making these everyday public services seamless, accessible, and responsive for all Americans.
