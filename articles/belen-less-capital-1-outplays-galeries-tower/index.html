Belen-less Capital 1 outplays Galeries Tower

TheTools · 19.11.2026 09:03 · source · permalink

MANILA, Philippines — Despite the absence of its top gunner Bella Belen, Capital 1 closed its campaign in the 2026-27 Premier Volleyball League (PVL) All-Filipino Conference Siklab Series with a bang, taking down Galeries Tower, 26-28, 25-19, 21-25, 25-19, 15-10, on Thursday afternoon at the Mall of Asia Arena in Pasay City.

Belen, the tournament’s leading scorer, was sidelined by the flu but the rest of the Solar Spikers stepped up their game to secure the team’s third victory in eight games.

 The Highrisers, on the other hand, dropped to 1-6.

Both teams will not advance to the next round.

In the absence of Belen, Erika Santos and Vanie Gandler paced Capital 1 with 26 and 20 points, respectively.

“Vanie and I share the same mindset heading into the next game, and we know our responsibility, so we did what we could and did our work,” Gandler said.

Galeries Tower was led by Jean Asis who netted 14 points.

In the 10th anniversary of the PVL All-Filipino Conference, the tournament features a three-phase series wherein each phase will crown a champion and the overall winner at the end of the season will be named the grand champion.

Win-loss records will be carried over into the next phase.
