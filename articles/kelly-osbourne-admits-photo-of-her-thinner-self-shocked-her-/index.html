Kelly Osbourne opened up about her recent weight gain after a photo of herself left her stunned.

The Osbournes alum, 41, admitted that she was alarmed when she saw photos of herself at the 2026 BRIT Awards on February 28, following her drastic weight loss.

At the event, Kelly wore a long black dress paired with a fur shawl. She completed the look with a blonde bob wig featuring full bangs.

"Do you know that I've gained 2st since the BRITs? I didn't know that I looked like that until I saw the pictures," Kelly admitted in an October 10 interview with The Sun. "When I saw them, I couldn't [expletive] believe it. I was as shocked as everyone else."

Kelly, who underwent gastric sleeve surgery in 2018, has also denied having plastic surgery, admitting that it's difficult to have her body constantly scrutinized and discussed by others.

"People are always saying I'm too fat, I'm too thin, I'm too this and too that," she continued. "People still think I've had all this plastic surgery. I've not [expletive] had plastic surgery! The only thing I've had is Botox between my eyebrows so that I don't have 11 lines, and in my jaw because I grind my teeth and it gives me crazy migraines."

Although Kelly acknowledged that she had become too thin, she hopes people will show her more compassion when discussing her appearance.

"I'm not asking for kindness, because most people aren't kind," she added. "I was just asking for a little bit of understanding, because there's a lot more to the story than meets the eye."

Xavier Collin/Image Press Agency / MEGA

Kelly also opened up about mourning the death of her father, Ozzy Osbourne, who passed away in July 2025 at the age of 76.

"My grief is my biggest badge of honor. I'll have that pain for the rest of my life," Kelly told the outlet. "I don't have to look after anyone any more except my son. That makes me free to do what I want to do."

"I'm going to write my own narrative now. I get to start over," she added. When you've been left with nothing, there's nowhere to go but up."
