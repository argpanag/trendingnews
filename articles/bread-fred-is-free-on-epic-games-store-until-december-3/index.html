Bread & Fred is free on Epic Games Store until December 3

TheTools · 26.11.2026 19:39 · source · permalink

Contents

Epic Games Store is giving away the penguin co-op climber Bread & Fred this week, while Firestone players can claim a free in-game bundle worth $100.

Epic Games Store has refreshed its weekly giveaway, and this time PC players can add Bread & Fred to their libraries at no cost. Alongside it, Firestone – Idle Clicker Online RPG is offering a $100 in-game bundle to anyone who logs in through the store.

According to information Oyun Günlüğü obtained from GameGrin, the offer went live on November 26, 2026 at 4pm GMT (11am ET) and runs until December 3, 2026 at the same time. Once claimed, Bread & Fred stays in your library for good.

Two penguins, one mountain, plenty of falls

Published by Atari and developed by Sand Castles Studio, Bread & Fred asks two players to guide a pair of penguins, Bread and Fred, to the top of a snowy summit. Success comes down to timing jumps, clinging to walls and swinging across gaps. A jump that is easy for one penguin can send both tumbling all the way back down if the partner isn't ready, which makes communication as important as reflexes.

What's inside the Firestone bundle

Holyday Studios' Firestone is a fantasy idle RPG built around collecting heroes and gear, managing formations and taking down bosses, with progress and rewards that keep coming while you're offline. The Epic bundle includes The Gladiator skin for the hero Talia, two profile avatars, 30 gear and jewel chests across four tiers, 360 Strange Dust, 50 scrolls of each type, 35 Tavern game tokens, 5 Temple tokens and 200 gems. Both new and returning players are eligible, but the game must be installed and launched through Epic Games Store, and newcomers need to reach stage 15 first.

Next up: Strange Antiquities

Epic has already confirmed the following week's freebie. From December 3 to 10, Bad Viking's Strange Antiquities will be free to claim. The game follows Strange Horticulture, which Epic gave away in August last year, and swaps the plant shop for a store full of occult relics. Players identify each item before handing it to a customer, and the mystery and ending shift depending on which villager they choose to help.
