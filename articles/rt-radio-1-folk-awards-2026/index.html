RTÉ Radio 1 Folk Awards 2026

TheTools · 11.11.2026 10:34 · source · permalink
  • Vicar St, Dublin

The RTÉ Radio 1 Folk Awards return to Vicar Street for a special night celebrating the very best of Ireland’s thriving folk music scene.

John Creedon will host the awards ceremony, with live performances from Beoga, The Unthanks, Declan O’Rourke, Jillian O’Malley, Joshua Burnside, Hammy Hamilton and the Ora Quartet, Kris Drever with Noriana Kennedy, Nicola Joyce and Pauline Scanlon, and Edwina Guckian.

The awards will be broadcast live on RTÉ Radio 1 on 10 November at 8pm, with television highlights on RTÉ One on Saturday 14 November.

For full details on the nominees, see here: https://journalofmusic.com/news/nominees-announced-rte-radio-1-folk-awards-2026

The event is sold out.
