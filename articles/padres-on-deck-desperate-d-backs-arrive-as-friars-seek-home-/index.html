Padres on deck: Desperate D-backs arrive as Friars seek home-field advantage

TheTools · 25.09.2026 19:02 · source · permalink

Arizona chasing Phillies for final NL wild-card spot; Padres looking to hold off Cubs to get NL Wild Card Series to Petco Park

Arizona Diamondbacks vs. San Diego Padres

at Petco Park

  • Game 1: 6:40 p.m. Friday (Padres.TV)
  • Game 2: 5:40 p.m. Saturday (Padres.TV)
  • Game 3: 12:10 p.m. Sunday (Padres.TV)

 

In the standings

Arizona Diamondbacks (85-74, 2nd in NL West)

The Diamondbacks swept the Rockies in Denver to start the week to keep their playoff hopes alive, but they are chasing the Phillies for the No. 6 seed after the Padres and Cubs clinched postseason spots on Thursday. The Diamondbacks are two games behind the Phillies with three games left. Arizona and San Diego have split the first two series this season.

San Diego Padres (89-70, 2nd in NL West)

The Padres took two of three at Dodger Stadium to clinch a third straight postseason berth. They’ve also moved a game ahead of the Cubs in the chase for the NL’s top wild-card spot and home-field advantage in the opening round of the postseason.

 

Trending

Arizona: The Diamondbacks have a .756 OPS in September (T-6th) and a 3.67 ERA (6th). 3B Nolan Arenado has eight of his 29 homers in September (1.131 OPS) and C Gabriel Moreno (.975 OPS has five homers this month. 2B Ketel Marte (.699) and OF Corbin Carroll (.689 OPS) are the regulars under .700 this month.

San Diego: The Padres lead the majors with an .806 OPS in September and rank 16th with a 4.04 ERA this month, helping them lead the majors with a 16-5 record. OF Fernando Tatis Jr. leads the team with a 1.088 OPS and eight homers this month. 3B Manny Machado has five homers this month despite a .689 OPS, while OF Jackson Merrill has four homers and a .912 OPS).

 

Pitching matchups

Friday

D-backs RHP Brandon Pfaadt (7-3, 3.72 ERA)   |   The Diamondbacks are 12-3 in his first starts (2.79 ERA) since returning from Triple-A in late June. Pfaadt has a 56-to-13 strikeout-to-walk ratio over those 90⅓ innings. That includes limiting the Padres to one unearned run in 11⅔ innings in a pair of Arizona wins.

Padres RHP Casey Mize (6-9, 3.96 ERA)   |   He’s got a 6.64 ERA in nine starts since the trade deadline, striking out 24 against 17 walks over 40⅔ innings. That includes a 7.27 ERA in four starts at Petco Park. Mize allowed a career-high eight runs in 3⅓ innings in his first start as a Padres against the Diamondbacks in Arizona.

Saturday

D-backs RHP Corbin Burnes (0-0, 7.84 ERA)   |   The former NL Cy Young winner is making his fourth start since returning from Tommy John surgery. He threw three shutout innings in his first start back and has allowed nine earned runs over his last two starts (7⅓). The Diamondbacks have won all three starts. Burnes last faced the Padres in 2022, allowing six runs in 9 ⅔ innings across two starts.

Padres RHP Walker Buehler (9-6, 4.56 ERA)   |   Buehler has allowed three earned runs or less in 12 straight starts (3.39 ERA), helping the team go 9-3 in that stretch. The former Dodger is 7-1 with a 2.57 ERA in 91 career innings against Arizona in the regular season, but he has allowed eight runs in nine innings in two starts this year.

Sunday

D-backs RHP Michael Soroka (9-5, 3.31 ERA)   |   Arizona’s week began with Soroka throwing seven innings of two-run ball in a win at Coors Field. The seven innings tied a season high. Soroka has an 0.90 ERA in 20 career innings against the Padres. He last faced them in a no-decision in 2025 (5 IP, 1 ER).

Padres TBA   |   RHP Michael King (12-10, 3.21 ERA) was only going to start in the event of a win-or-go-home game, so the regular-season finale is likely a bullpen tuneup before the NL Wild Card Series starts Tuesday.

Training room

Arizona: The Diamondbacks are without four arms heading into the final weekend—RHP Ryan Thompson (back), LHP A.J. Puk (shoulder), RHP Cristian Mena (teres major) and LHP Andrew Saalfrank (shoulder).

San Diego: DH Miguel Andujar (wrist) and DH Gavin Sheets (plantar fasciitis) had both been in Arizona for regular at-bats, but they joined the team in time for Thursday’s celebration. RHP Joe Musgrove (elbow) threw a bullpen on Sunday. RHP Jeremiah Estrada (shoulder) is rehabbing in Arizona, while OF Ramón Laureano (hip surgery) has begun baseball activity but is not close to game action. INF Luis Rengifo (knee) is done for the year.
