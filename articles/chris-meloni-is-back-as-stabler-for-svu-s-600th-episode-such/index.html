Chris Meloni Is Back as Stabler for SVU's 600th Episode: "Such a Reward for the Fans"

TheTools · 09.10.2026 17:19 · source · permalink

Let the next Bensler chapter of SVU begin!

Key Takeaways

  • Chris Meloni will return as Elliot Stabler for Law & Order: SVU’s 600th episode in Season 28, reuniting with Mariska Hargitay’s Olivia Benson in what cast members describe as a major moment for longtime fans.
  • The milestone episode celebrates the series’ unprecedented longevity, with Kelli Giddish and creator Dick Wolf emphasizing the rarity and significance of reaching 600 episodes after 28 seasons.
  • Meloni’s last SVU appearance was in the Season 27 premiere, which featured an emotional reunion between Stabler and Benson and hinted that their relationship story is still evolving.

It's official: Chris Meloni is returning as Elliot Stabler on Law & Order: Special Victims Unit for a very special milestone. 

How to Watch

Watch Law & Order: SVU Thursdays at 9 p.m. ET on NBC and next day on Peacock.

Kelli Giddish confirmed the exciting news during an interview with NBC Insider, revealing that Meloni will be back for Law & Order: SVU's 600th episode, which is set to air in Season 28.

Chris Meloni will star in SVU's milestone 600th episode in Season 28

Det. Olivia Benson (Mariska Hargitay) and Det. Elliot Stabler (Christopher Meloni) appear on Law & Order: Special Victims Unit Season 8 Episode 20.

Giddish, who plays Detective Amanda Rollins, revealed that Meloni will star in SVU's upcoming 600th episode, calling the news a "reward for the fans." 

“Of course the fans are going to be excited to know that Chris Meloni is coming back and starring [in the 600th episode]," she told NBC Insider. "So you get to have Mariska and Chris together again, which I think is such a reward for the fans. They started the show and here they are doing the 600th."

SVU’s 600th episode will be more than just an opportunity for a beloved character to return and reunite with Olivia Benson (Mariska Hargitay), it's also a stunning milestone for a TV series that has stood the test of time for 28 seasons and counting. 

"It's crazy. It's such a milestone to celebrate," Giddish said. "The day we were filming it, Dick Wolf got to come to the studio and celebrate with us, and he was like, 'This has never been done before. Probably not going to happen again.' And it just sunk into all of us like, 'Oh my God, what an accomplishment.'" 

So, what should fans expect from the 600th episode? Giddish is keeping her cards close to her chest, but she did tease that it's going to be one to remember.  

"I flew through the script. I loved it," she said. "It's not an easy one."

When did Chris Meloni last appear on Law & Order: SVU?

Meloni last appeared on SVU in the Season 27 premiere, "In the Wind," famously reuniting Stabler with Benson after the two attended Donald Cragen's wake. The episode is also notable for Stabler throwing a casual "love you" Benson's way as they said goodbye after the wake.  

Capt. Olivia Benson (Mariska Hargitay) and Det. Elliot Stabler (Christopher Meloni) appear on Law & Order: Special Victims Unit Season 27 Episode 1 "In The Wind". 

"He just threw that in," Hargitay revealed during a 2025 visit to TODAY. "Listen, we lost our captain, who was our north star, that we both came up under. It's a deeply emotional scene and it's also the beginning of a new era and the end of an old one. It was a very emotional scene, and as we know, those things happen when we lose somebody we love — we renew our connection, right? We plug in that way emotionally. I felt like he just needed to say it."
