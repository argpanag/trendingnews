Spalletti: "Only Kalulu and Nico out. Conte's words? I find them normal. But I..."

The manager in his press conference on the eve of Juve v Venezia: "There have been some important recoveries, over the last two days we've had good training sessions and we've regained the team core"

  • Italiano
  • Deutsch

The day of his 600th Serie A match in the dugout arrives (“I'm preparing for this one against Venezia with the same emotion as the first...”) and the moment comes to get going again without falling into the trap of a restart merely after a break without any real intensity. Luciano Spalletti returns to the pitch and brushes aside the noise from those speculating about the bianconero world. “Conte's words? I find it normal that an unattached manager hopes to sit on an important bench like that of the national team, Milan, Manchester United, Juventus... I don't see what the fuss is about. Whether I would have made the same statements? When I wasn't coaching – said Spalletti – I went to watch other colleagues to learn new things, and I went to study and look for solutions for my next team. I did it with total respect for my present and the future, waiting for the right moment to return". New paragraph: without going in with a tackle, the response to the Conte topic goes straight to the heart of the matter.

next stop

And Venezia? The former Italy coach takes stock of the players who have recovered and those who remain sidelined. “Kalulu and Nico Gonzalez won't be there, just to stick with the short-term injuries... Conceiçao – he says – is available, we just need to figure out whether to play him from the start or bring him on later, because when you get hurt where he got hurt (the shoulder, ed.) you need those first two shoulder barges to feel fine. Gatti? He is trying to interpret the role in a different way, he has all the attributes to do it”. In other words: it is likely, very likely, that the attacking line will be set up with Woltemade as the focal point up front and Kolo Muani plus Aajbegovic behind him and wider. “Over the last two days we've had good training sessions and we've regained the core of the team. Whether this is the moment to step it up? In my opinion there is still time to get to the top. And, in any case, our objective is to become something and we are working towards that. We want to build habits of constant striving...", underlined the bianconero manager. The final point concerns McKennie and his departure from the USA national team camp: a misunderstanding over his future that had set off alarm bells. “As soon as he got back he came looking for me; we have an open dialogue, a great relationship just like with the rest of the dressing room: what he said – said Spalletti – was a misunderstanding, he was speaking in general, not about himself. Nothing happened, it's not even worth sitting here talking about it...”. The moment of 600 matches in the Serie A dugout arrives, the moment comes to see off Venezia so as not to fall into the trap: the Tuscan coach knows it very well.
