Utah Starts Back-to-Back in Nashville | Utah Mammoth

TheTools · 27.11.2026 17:56 · source · permalink

The Utah Mammoth will play a matinee game in Nashville to start a holiday weekend back-to-back

THE DETAILS

WHO: Utah (13-10-3) vs Nashville (13-6-5)

WHEN: Nov. 27, 2026 – 12:00 p.m. MT

WHERE: Bridgestone Arena

TV: Utah16, Mammoth+

RADIO: Mammoth App, NHL App, KSL Sports Zone (1280AM), Sirius XM (Online), NHL.com

MORNING SKATE NOTES

  • Head coach André Tourigny shared that defenseman Gregor Biber will make his NHL debut. 
  • Utah is starting a back-to-back with today’s game in Nashville.

Debut in Nashville

It’s time for a rookie lap. Defenseman Gregor Biber will become the first player drafted by Utah to play in an NHL game. Utah recalled Biber on Nov. 18.

“He played really well in Tucson and (Steve Potvin and John Slaney) said a lot of good things about him, so we’re excited. We liked him during training camp. He closes quickly, strong in his battles, so it’ll be a good test.”

His experience playing professional hockey in Sweden will also help Biber today.

“(Biber) is a guy who had a really good season last year overseas, played pro before, so had a good start this year. We’re excited about that.”

Facing the Predators

Nashville enters today’s game with eight wins in their last 10 games, and is playing with confidence.

“On (this) trip, if you look at our four opponents, there are a lot of similarities,” Tourigny said. “They’re rolling, they’re playing well defensively. They’re heavy on (the) puck. They like possession, so that will be a good challenge for us, to sustain what we did in (Minnesota). I think there’s similarities like I said, but at the same time, it’s a new challenge, a new day, you have to do it all over again.”

Utah held a practice on Thursday in St. Paul before traveling to Nashville. That practice was an opportunity to reset and get ready for today.

“I think it was good to get over that game,” Tourigny explained. “As much as you don’t want to carry over your bad performances, same thing for your good performances. It’s important to manage our success properly, so we keep having success, and same thing when you have a failure. If you carry over your failure, now everything snowballs. So, it’s important to have a good reset, to be humble in (this) league. As much as we like our game against (Minnesota), it’s over now. Now, we need to start from scratch and make sure we have the right mindset, and the right pace, the right physicality, the right urgency in our game against Nashville.”

GAME PREVIEW

Tonight's Matchup

Utah

  • Utah is 13-10-3 this season and 3-4-3 over the last 10 games. However, the team has won three of their last four games. Utah is seventh in the Central Division with 29 points.
  • In their most recent game, the Utah Mammoth beat the Minnesota Wild, 4-3. Lawson Crouse, Logan Cooley, Dmitri Simashev, and Dylan Guenther scored for Utah, while goaltender Karel Vejmelka stopped 22 of the 25 shots he faced.
  • Cooley’s 13 goals lead the team while Captain Clayton Keller’s 24 assists and 29 points are the most on Utah’s roster.
  • Vejmelka and Sebastian Cossa make up the team’s goaltending tandem. Vejmelka has a 7-7-2 record with a 3.14 goals-against average, a .873 save percentage, and two shutouts. Cossa has a 6-3-1 record with a 3.09 goals-against average and a .880 save percentage.
  • Utah will wrap the second half of a back-to-back in Dallas on Saturday night. It will also wrap up an all Central Division road trip.

Nashville

  • Nashville is 13-6-5 this season and is 8-2-0 over the last 10 games. The Predators have won their last two-straight and are in the first wild card spot with 31 points.
  • In their most recent game, the Predators beat the Dallas Stars, 3-1. Mavrik Bourque, Ross Colten (EN), and Jack Drury (EN) scored for Nashville in the win. Juuse Saros stopped 20 of the 21 shots he faced.
  • Steven Stamkos’ 12 goals, Romas Josi’s 22 assists, and Filip Forsberg’s 26 points are all team-highs.
  • Saros and Justus Annunen make up Nashville’s goaltending tandem. Saros has a 9-4-4 record with a 2.41 goals-against average, a .918 save percentage, and one shutout. Annunen has a 4-2-0 record with a 3.98 goals-against average, and a .864 save percentage.
  • Following Friday’s game, the Predators travel to Detroit to face the Red Wings on Saturday night.

By the Numbers

  • Vincent Trocheck’s 60.5% faceoff win percentage is fourth in the NHL. Ryan O’Reilly is ninth with a 58.9% win rate. Utah has the third-highest faceoff percentage (55.2%) and Nashville is 11th (51.3%). 
  • Friday is a battle between top special teams units. Utah has the third-best power play in the NHL (32.3%) while Nashville has the second best penalty kill (89.2%).
