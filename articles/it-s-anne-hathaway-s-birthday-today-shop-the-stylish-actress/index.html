It's Anne Hathaway's birthday today — shop the stylish actress's best looks

TheTools · 12.11.2026 23:36 · source · permalink

We're loving the star's wardrobe IRL, so we tracked down her jeans, shoes, coat and bag — plus a few inexpensive swaps.

Our experts independently review everything we recommend. If you buy through our links, we may earn commission.

We're sending a big birthday shoutout to Anne Hathaway, who turns 44 today! After a supremely productive year in which she delivered five movies and a third child, the actress is taking a well-deserved break from the public eye. (Last month, Hathaway dramatically disappeared on Instagram.) Now, as she celebrates by (hopefully) relaxing with those near and dear, we're celebrating the stylish celeb with a mini shopping trip of her best real-life looks.

Below we name the actual brands Hathaway has been rocking (we heart you, Adidas Tokyo), plus some affordable alternatives that are good enough for us, thank you very much.

In fact, after all the transformation montages we've watched her characters go through — from Mia Thermopolis's Princess Diaries makeover to Andy's Devil Wears Prada pivotal Runway closet sequence — I'm declaring Anne Hathaway's birthday a national shopping holiday. Enjoy!

Hathaway mixed silk glam with denim comfort at the "The End of Oak Street" premiere in August.

Levi's Women's 94 Baggy Wide Leg Jeans

At theEnd of Oak Street premiere a few months ago, Hathaway dramatized her designer La Ligne Jenny drawstring jeans ($225) with a cool architectural Prabal Garung top that highlighted her beautiful baby bump.

The comfy-looking jeans really grounded the look, and left us wanting a pair of our own.

In case the La Ligne price tag is too steep, we’re highlighting a pair of Levi’s with a very similar cut and color! The bagginess is just right. (Prefer a drawstring? Try these!)

The Devil Wears Prada star knows a classic double-breasted camel coat adds instant polish to any outfit. Still, we went looking for something a little easier on the wallet and found this fetching lookalike, which has earned over 1,100 five-star ratings. "Obsessed," wrote a happy wearer.
