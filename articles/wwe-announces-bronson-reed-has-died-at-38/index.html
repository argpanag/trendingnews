WWE Announces Bronson Reed Has Died at 38

TheTools · 28.11.2026 00:36 · source · permalink

WWE announced on November 28, 2026, that professional wrestler Bronson Reed, whose real name was Jermaine Haley, had died at the age of 38. According to the organization, Reed passed away earlier that day due to medical complications.

The announcement identified Reed as a prominent figure within WWE and highlighted his personal character, family life, and relationships with colleagues. His death was also confirmed through a statement published on WWE's official social media account.

In its official announcement, WWE expressed its grief over the loss and shared details about Reed's family.

"WWE is devastated to announce that Jermaine Haley, also known as Bronson Reed, passed away earlier today at the age of 38 due to medical complications. Jermaine was a larger-than-life figure in WWE, but more importantly, he was a phenomenal person, a devoted family man, and a beloved colleague. Our thoughts and deepest condolences are with Jermaine’s entire family, including his pregnant wife, Paige, their daughter, as well as all his friends, co-workers and fans." WWE said in its statement.

The organization specifically acknowledged Reed's pregnant wife, Paige, and their daughter while extending condolences to his relatives, friends, colleagues, and supporters.

WWE published its announcement on November 28, 2026, through its official X account. The statement confirmed Reed's death and the reported cause of death but did not provide further details about the medical complications.
