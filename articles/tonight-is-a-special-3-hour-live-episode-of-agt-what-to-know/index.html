Tonight Is a Special 3-Hour Live Episode of AGT: What to Know

TheTools · 08.09.2026 18:16 · source · permalink

Here's when to tune in.

Key Takeaways

  • America’s Got Talent airs a special 3-hour episode tonight at 8 p.m. ET on NBC, featuring both the fourth round of Quarterfinal performances and live results, with Heidi Klum filling in for Simon Cowell.
  • Voting opens at the start of the episode and closes at 10:05pm ET, with fans able to vote up to 10 times per platform through the NBC website, AGT App, and AGT TikTok comments.

America's Got Talent fans are in for a treat tonight! 

How to Watch

Watch America's Got Talent Tuesdays at 8 p.m. ET on NBC and next day on Peacock.

Not only is super-Judge Heidi Klum returning to the Judges' Table (alongside Mel B, Sofía Vergara and Howie Mandel) to fill in for Simon Cowell, but the September 8 episode extends to encompass both the fourth round of Quarterfinals performances and the Live Results. 

Here's everything you need to know.

When is the 3-hour AGT episode?

America's Got Talent comes on at its usual time Tuesday, September 8 at 8 p.m. ET on NBC. But instead of ending at 10 p.m. ET, it'll end at 11 p.m. ET. 

This means there will be no new episode this Wednesday, September 9. As always, the episode will be available to stream the day after it airs on Peacock.

How does voting work?

Voting will open at the start of the episode and stay open for two hours, closing at 10:05 p.m. ET. The final hour of the episode will be devoted to the Live Results Show. That means if you want your vote to count, you have to watch live and you have to cast it right away. No waiting until the episode is over or waking up early like you might be used to, because the results will be calculated on the spot and announced immediately.

The voting methods are the same as they have been all season. You can vote up to 10 times on each of the three voting platforms: via the NBC website, the AGT App, and by commenting on the AGT TikTok account.

What happens next on AGT?

The next new episode of America's Got Talent Season 21 will air on September 15, when the Top 16 Acts compete in the Live Semifinals. This will be a regular 2-hour episode, with the results revealed the following evening on September 16.
