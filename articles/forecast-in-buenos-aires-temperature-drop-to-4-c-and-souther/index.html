Forecast in Buenos Aires: temperature drop to 4°C and southern winds of 27 km/h this September 12 - Noticias Ambientales

TheTools · 12.09.2026 11:15 · source · permalink

This Saturday, September 12, the City of Buenos Aires and the Metropolitan Area (AMBA) will experience a significant drop in temperatures due to an influx of cold air.

Throughout the day, cloudiness will be constant, accompanied by southerly winds that will intensify the low thermal sensation throughout the weekend.

Forecast of temperature and winds for CABA and AMBA

In the early hours of the day, temperatures will range between 10°C and 12°C, with a maximum barely reaching 10°C. As the day progresses, these figures will decrease.

The expected minimum will reach 4°C towards the night. Although the probability of rain is low, between 10% and 20%, morning drizzles are not ruled out.

The wind, coming from the south, will blow at an average speed of 27 km/h, increasing the feeling of cold in the region.

The relative humidity will remain around 78%, creating a humid and unstable environment.

In the Federal Capital and Greater Buenos Aires, conditions will be similar: cloudy skies and notably cool weather.

Weather outlook and alerts for the coming days in Buenos Aires

On Sunday, September 13, there will be slight improvements in the weather, with partly cloudy skies. However, the cold air will persist, with minimum temperatures of 4°C and maximums of 12°C, accompanied by moderate southeasterly winds.

At the start of the workweek, low temperatures will continue, with a minimum of 6°C on Monday and a gradual rise to 15°C.

By Thursday and Friday, a slight thermal increase is expected, with maximums potentially reaching 21°C under clear skies.

In other regions of the country, the movement of cold fronts towards the north will continue to be monitored.
