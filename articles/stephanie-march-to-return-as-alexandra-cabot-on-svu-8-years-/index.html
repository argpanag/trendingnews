An iconic Law & Order: Special Victims Unit character is making her return to the squad room.

Stephanie March will be stepping back into Alexandra Cabot's shoes for the upcoming ninth episode of the long-running NBC show's 28th season, which is currently airing. An exact release date for the episode has yet to be announced.

Season 28 is proving to be a red letter season for SVU. March's return announcement comes ahead of SVU's milestone 600th episode, which is slated to air on Nov. 12. The landmark installment will act as the sixth episode of season 28, meaning March's return as Cabot will come a few weeks later.

Stephanie March as ADA Alexandra Cabot
Credit: Virginia Sherwood/NBC/Getty

March appeared on 97 episodes of SVU, making her the third-longest-running ADA on the show. The actress made her SVU debut during season 2 in 2000, teaming up with then-Detective Olivia Benson (Mariska Hargitay) to get justice. The two women made an iconic TV duo for three seasons before March's initial departure from the show early on in season 5.

During what was one of the most surprising episodes of SVU at the time (the show has come a long way), Cabot was forced to fake her death and go into Witness Protection after attempting to convict a ruthless gang member for rape. When her life was threatened, the SVU team stopped their investigation, and Cabot stepped away from her job.

March called the SVU experience "fantastic" in a 2004 interview with TV Guide, explaining that she felt like she "said everything you can say" with Cabot. "I got really lucky and I thought, I just have to do a couple of more things before I get too comfortable. Perhaps all I need is a long break."

Well, a long break it was because March returned as Cabot during season 10. After a two-season stint, Cabot moved on to work for the International Criminal Court. However, SVU fans hadn't seen the last of her. March has returned as Cabot several times over the years, making guest appearances in seasons 13 and 19. The latter was a one-episode return, which aired in 2018, and revealed that Cabot has retired from her job as an attorney.

During a 2024 interview with Entertainment Weekly sister site PEOPLE, March confirmed that she'd be happy to return as Cabot if the opportunity presented itself. She quipped, "I mean, I wouldn't say no to the opportunity and cash prizes!"

What could bring Cabot back to the SVU squad room now? Only time will tell.

New episodes of Law & Order: SVU premiere Thursdays on NBC at 9 p.m. ET.
