Does GTA 6 have New Game Plus?

No, GTA 6 does not have New Game Plus.

It's not much of a surprise, especially considering how easy it seems to spend hundreds of hours as Lucia and Jason in the game's story mode.

With so many areas to explore, missions to complete and side quests to unravel, there is a mammoth of content inside this game already.

However, Rockstar's Scott Mitchell explained that there is something alternate to New Game Plus: "So, we don’t have a new game plus, we have a 'continue' option after the endings that put you back into the world prior meeting Abe, and it unlocks cheats (which are in turn unlocked by certain challenges being completed) and changes the percentage of the save to represent the 100% achievement, rather than the story progression.

"This will allow players to clear remaining content and go for a more completionist play if they want without losing their Jason and Lucia.

"We have to make certain decisions in development based on our available time and resources, so we prioritised systems and gameplay that supported the narrative of the game."

It's clear that the game is so huge with players' decisions impacting the story and the world around them, that a New Game Plus would've felt a bit redundant.

Regardless, completionists will rejoice about this continue option, allowing them to fully explore everything the game has to offer consequence free.
