NFL Week 1 live updates, highlights: Chargers-Cardinals, Dolphins-Raiders and Commanders-Eagles

TheTools · 13.09.2026 21:19 · source · permalink

Get real-time scores, injuries, big plays and key updates from every game as Sunday's action unfolds

The first NFL Sunday of the year is here, and there are plenty of storylines to zero in on. The new-look Bengals defense will face off against Baker Mayfield and the Bucs, Josh Allen will try to register his first career win in Houston against one of the best defenses in the league, Robert Saleh makes his Titans debut against his former team in the Jets, and two former No. 1 overall picks face off in Charlotte with Caleb Williams vs. Bryce Young.

Later in the day, we'll watch an NFC North grudge match in the form of Packers vs. Vikings, and an NFC East rivalry in Commanders vs. Eagles. Plus, Malik Willis makes his Dolphins debut in Sin City, while Jeremiyah Love is expected to make his NFL debut against Jim Harbaugh's Chargers.

It's sure to be an action-packed day, and you've come to the perfect spot to digest it all. Below, we will break down all the notable highlights from around the league.

Kyler Murray out after brutal hit

The Vikings' quarterback depth is already being put to the test. Kyler Murray left the field and entered the locker room for evaluation after taking a vicious hit from a pair of Packers defenders. The team promptly ruled him out for the game. The collision drew a penalty flag for unnecessary roughness.

Ravens WRs banged up

With 31 points on the scoreboard early in the third quarter, the Ravens' offense is having itself a monster day. But two of the wide receivers responsible for a lot of that production are on the sideline.

Rookie Ja'Kobi Lane is out with a wrist injury and star Zay Flowers is out due to a hamstring problem.

Lane made one catch for 11 yards in his regular-season debut and had a touchdown called back for holding. Flowers was up to five grabs for 150 yards and a score and on pace for a truly monster performance, but after they initially deemed him questionable to return, the Ravens played it safe with their highest-paid receiver and held him out of a game that got out of hand.
