Joshua Van has confidently declared he is “going to finish” Alexandre Pantoja within three rounds as the pair enter the octagon for their highly-anticipated title rematch at UFC 331.

UFC 331: Van v Pantoja 2 | SUN 20 SEPTEMBER 11AM AEST | The UFC returns to Los Angeles for a blockbuster title showdown, as Joshua Van defends his crown against former champion Alexandre Pantoja in a rematch for the ages. | Order Now with Main Event on Kayo Sports.

The two last fought in December 2025, with Van winning the flyweight championship in dramatic circumstances after Pantoja dislocated his elbow just 26 seconds into the fight.

Van came under fire for his overzealous celebration in the wake of the injury and given the circumstances, his critics questioned whether the 24-year-old was a legitimate champion.

Well, Van more than answered his sceptics with a dominant fifth-round knockout victory over Tatsuro Taira at UFC 328 earlier this year and now he is back in the octagon looking to prove he can match it with Pantoja.

Pantoja was on an eight-fight win streak when he ran into the rapidly-rising Van, who became the second-youngest champ in UFC history with his contentious victory.

Van carries a seven-fight win streak into Sunday’s rematch against Pantoja, who will be making his first appearance in the octagon since that fateful first title fight.

The champ is the betting favourite despite entering the UFC 323 bout as the clear underdog, with most experts favouring Pantoja’s consistency and familiarity in the big title fights.

Former UFC middleweight champion Rob Whittaker said on Main Event’s ‘Fight Week’ that “it boggles the mind” that Pantoja is the betting outsider, although he added that he saw plenty from Van in his last fight to suggest Sunday’s bout will be a very even affair.

“That is crazy,” Whittaker said of Van being the favourite.

“But I feel like it’s no surprise to Pantoja because everyone in MMA bet against him, especially in those first three or four fights. He kept walking down people and steamrolling them and everyone, I feel, jumped on the wagon and were like Pantoja is my GOAT now.”

Now, Pantoja is back to prove that is still the case and that the injury setback has not taken his experience and well-rounded skillset away.

“Pantoja is going to have to fight for this one but Pantoja was the reigning, dominant champion for a long time for a reason,” Whittaker said.

The Australian said that he saw enough in Van’s last fight against Taira to suggest he can be a “real problem” for Pantoja.

“Whilst I think Van took a lot of the fans back and obviously cemented why he is the champion in his last fight, the way Pantoja lost, it always leaves that lingering doubt in Van’s mind and in the fans’ mind,” Whittaker added.

“This fight needs to happen... before Van’s last fight I would have had a very different opinion. But seeing that fight, seeing that skillset (and) the complete workaround, the way he was able to defend takedowns, fight from the ground, get back up and utilise his striking to protect the fight — it just showed to me he can pose some real problems to Pantoja.

“The fact that he’s (Pantoja) been in the game relatively short might play to his strengths because he hasn’t those wars and years and a decade of firefights. He’s coming in hungry. He’s got talent for it obviously and he’s got a skillset that is exciting to watch.”
