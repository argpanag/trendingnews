LIVE UPDATES: Proteas quick hurt, Aussie top-order decimated

TheTools · 21.10.2026 07:30 · source · permalink

Australia start day four in trouble after Temba Bavuma put them to the sword but the pitch is flat and if they can survive the new ball, there are runs to be had.

Kagiso Rabada picked up Nic Maddinson for a duck on Tuesday night and will be firing in with the Proteas needing to win this Test in Gqeberha to keep the series alive.

And don’t forget Anrich Nortje and Marco Jansen, who will be eager to make up for defeat in the first Test.
