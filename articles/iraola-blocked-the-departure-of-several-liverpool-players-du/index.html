Iraola 'blocked' the departure of several Liverpool players during the summer

TheTools · 11.09.2026 18:40 · source · permalink

Andoni Iraola has admitted that he blocked another player's departure from Liverpool this summer, after what he saw during the opening weeks of the season convinced him of this decision.

The Reds were criticised for their lack of signings during the summer transfer window, spending £123m on winger Bradley Barcola from PSG, and after the final day of the transfer window they were left dangerously close to having a very tight squad.

Although the first team is still short of players, the worst-case scenario has been averted after the club did not give the green light to several departures – and Iraola has now revealed that another first-team player could be added to that list.

Speaking ahead of Liverpool's Premier League clash with Fulham on Saturday, Iraola explained how he prevented young striker Lewis Koumas from leaving Anfield in the summer.

The 20-year-old, who is the son of former Cardiff, Tranmere and West Brom midfielder Jason Koumas, was expected to leave the club after previous manager Arne Slot loaned him to Hull City for the second half of last season.

But Iraola decided that, under his guidance, Koumas would not be going anywhere – stating that it took him less than a week of watching him in training to make the decision.

"I believe that from the first week I was here, I told the club that he would stay for the whole season," he said. "I had very few doubts because he is someone who covers us at seven, nine, 10 and 11. He trains very well, has a good attitude, is a good player, scores goals," he added.

"I like what you give us," Iraola continued.

"Even in situations when we were losing, like in the transfer to Newcastle, and the other day when we were winning, I think he helps us in both situations and I'm very happy to have him, yes. I think he's very clear about what he has to do, and what he has to do is help the team. Sometimes he'll be with the ball, sometimes he'll be without the ball, sometimes he'll attack the spaces for someone else to receive the ball. And he understands that. When you have that mentality, normally it's good for the team," he said.

Koumas' case is not the first time Iraola has acted to keep a player at Anfield.

Midfielder James McConnell was close to joining Championship side Preston North End on loan this summer but the deal fell through at the last minute. The 21-year-old has yet to feature under Iraola, but has been on the bench in three of Liverpool's four games this season.

According to the Daily Mail, Iraola urged the club to keep McConnell and he could play an important role as the season progresses and the match calendar becomes busier.

Meanwhile, young striker Jayden Danns attracted interest on loan from Championship side West Bromwich Albion during the summer, but the Reds rejected it.

Danns did not play for Liverpool's first team last season but, having recently returned from a thigh injury, Iraola was keen to integrate him into the first team and he was on the bench for the 2-1 win over Atletico Madrid in the Champions League on Wednesday.

Luke Chambers, 22, was also a candidate for loan, with several Championship clubs interested, but Iraola decided to keep him after making a good impression during pre-season preparations in the centre-back role.

He was also on the bench on Wednesday and, although he was not activated, Chambers can play at centre-back and left-back and could have more opportunities to play as the season continues. /GazetaExpress/
