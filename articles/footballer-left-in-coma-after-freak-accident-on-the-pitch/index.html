Footballer left in coma after freak accident on the pitch

TheTools · 09.09.2026 05:00 · source · permalink

A footballer was left with a broken skull and bleeding on the brain after a freak accident during a football match.

George Strange was playing 5-a-side football with some friends when a collision during play led to him losing his balance and fall backwards, hitting his head on a brick wall surrounding the pitch.

After being taken to hospital, scans found that the 26-year-old, of Portishead, had broken his skull in two places and had three spots of bleeding on the brain - a further bleed was discovered later. He was taken straight into surgery and has since been in an induced coma.

George's close friend Corey Baker, and Corey's sister Leah, set up a GoFundMe page with the aim of raising £10,000 to help George 'get back on his feet when he is finally woken up'. At time of writing, almost £9,800 has been raised.

Corey said: "George is closer to a brother to me. I've only known him for just under four years, but for the last two he's stayed at my house in Australia while out on a working visa. I spent almost everyday with him, him living with me, and out at social events, and he even worked with me for a while.

'One of the most giving people I've ever met'

Corey says that George was in a "very bad way and waiting for an ambulance for just over an hour" after the incident, which Corey said stemmed from a 'collision between George and one of the other boys, just a standard 50/50 that you'll see in football all the time'.

He added: "Needless to say it's not a common type of injury you would see, but when the wall is only just under chest height I feel like you're asking for problems. We have been told that there has potentially been other accidents involved there in the past."

Corey and George's loved ones say they are not naming the venue publicly at this time for legal reasons.

George's family and friends say that the group he was playing football with on that day 'genuinely saved his life'.

"They did everything right, including pulling out his tongue, which he was swallowing," Corey says.

'Everyone's been so generous'

"The doctors are confident the surgery went well and are hopeful over time he will make a full recovery. They just can't wake him up until his temperature and brain pressure stops spiking and stays steady.

"Obviously with an injury like this it's the uncertainty of it all that has been hard. We won't know the severity until he wakes up. When he'll be able to work or drive again is unsure, so I thought perhaps if I set up this GoFundMe, when he wakes up he won't have to worry about his finances for at least a little while and can focus on getting himself better.

"I knew with how popular and loved he is both here and in Australia that he'd get some donations. But never expected him to receive as many as he has, everyone's been so generous.

"We are obviously hoping he makes a full recovery and he can go about life like he was before. Although it's likely it will be very different for at least a while."

Giving person

"We kind of became a duo and whatever I was invited to, people knew that and were happy that George would be coming along as well. He only just returned to England in May this year."

"He's one of the most giving people I've ever met. He'd always be the first to offer someone a lift home or sacrifice his enjoyment to make sure everyone else could have a good time.

"He'd never let anyone be out of pocket for him. He wouldn't take a hand out and no matter what you bought for him he'd always pay back every penny. He'd also always be the first to order a round for everyone. Even since he's been back here he's always checked in on me, asked how I'm doing, how's work, and things like that.

"And to know what an impact on people he's made, all you have to do is look at the donors on the GoFundMe. There's that many people from Australia that he's only known for a short time. Even some he's only met once or twice, so he clearly is appreciated by so many people. However, he would deny it - he's very humble and doesn't realise how much he's done for so many."
