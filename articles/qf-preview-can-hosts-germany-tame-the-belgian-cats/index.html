QF Preview: Can hosts Germany tame the Belgian Cats?

TheTools · 10.09.2026 17:40 · source · permalink

BERLIN (Germany) - The atmosphere inside Berlin Arena promises to be seriously special when host nation Germany face Belgium in Thursday's FIBA Women's Basketball World Cup 2026 Quarter-Finals.

Key Matchup

Unstoppable in so many of the biggest games down the years, few people would be surprised if Emma Meesseman comes up with a game-winning show.

With legendary status already assured, she signed off in the last game with the joint-best performance efficiency valuation so far in the competition, so is in good shape ahead of this one.

That could give Nyara Sabally the most important task of the night. The forward is likely to have to not only slow down Belgium's marquee baller, but also do some damage down the other end of the floor. She's capable of producing her best display for when it matters most. Especially after impressing last time out against Korea.

Stats Don't Lie

Germany have a phenomenal bench scoring average of 42 points per game compared to Belgium who rely a lot more on their starters and get 20.7 points per outing from that source.

The Fiebich effect: Dominance without the scoring punch

The other major gap statistically is in the favour of Belgium who have shot the ball nicely from behind the three-point line, averaging 38.1 percent. That's far better than Germany who have sunk only 25.2 percent from downtown.

The Killer Questions

Germany coach Olaf Lange has already spoken about whether his team can focus on taking care of all elements of Belgium's impressive game? Not just taking care of a few primary elements like coping with Korea's ball movement in the last game for example. Germany may also need to finally wake up and make some perimeter shots.

Can Belgium get the defensive rebounds they need to push the ball down the floor and keep the tempo high when they need, using their superior ball movement to leave Germany chasing some shadows?

Recent Game History

The last meeting between the teams came at the same Quarter-Finals stage when they clashed at FIBA Women's EuroBasket 2025. Belgium completed a comfortable 83-59 victory en-route to defending their continental title as Emma Meeseman put on a 30-point master class.

On the global level, Germany can proudly reflect on their famous debut win at Paris 2024 when they powered their way past the Cats 83-69, led by 15 points and 8 assists from Alexis Peterson.
