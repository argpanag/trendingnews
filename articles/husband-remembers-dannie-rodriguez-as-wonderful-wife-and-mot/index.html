Husband remembers Dannie Rodriguez as ‘wonderful wife and mother’ after deadly Old Town shooting

TheTools · 16.11.2026 18:20 · source · permalink

KISSIMMEE, Fla. — The husband of Dannie Darlene Rodriguez is remembering her as a loving wife and mother after an Osceola County deputy shot and killed the 35-year-old outside a bar in Old Town on Saturday night.

In a statement shared with Channel 9, Anthony Rodriguez described his family’s grief and said their priority is caring for their children.

“Dannie will be dearly missed by so many. She was a wonderful wife and mother, and her warmth and compassion brought joy to many others,” he wrote. “It is impossible to describe the grief that we and our children will experience.”

The statement comes after Channel 9 obtained witness video showing the confrontation before the shooting. The Florida Department of Law Enforcement is investigating.

Family focuses on children and her memory

Anthony Rodriguez and Dannie Darlene Rodriguez Anthony Rodriguez said the loss had brought “immeasurable grief and sorrow” to the family. “Our priority at this time is to care for our children and to keep Dannie’s memory alive as we continue to live our lives in the days to come,” he wrote.

He thanked those who had offered prayers and condolences.

“We wish for Dannie to be remembered for the joy and happiness she brought to those around her and the impact that she left on their lives.”

What deputies say happened

The shooting happened shortly after 10:30 p.m. Saturday, Nov. 14, outside Sun on the Beach restaurant and bar.

According to the Osceola County Sheriff’s Office, deputies saw Rodriguez following a man with a handgun and repeatedly ordered her to drop it. Investigators said she pointed the weapon at the man before a deputy fired once.

Rodriguez was taken to a hospital, where she was pronounced dead.

Sheriff Christopher Blackmon has said he believes the shooting was justified. The deputy, a seven-year employee of the agency, was placed on paid administrative leave.

Witness video captured the confrontation

Video recorded by witness Erika Lugo and her boyfriend shows an argument inside the business before the confrontation moves outside.

Channel 9’s previous reporting described Rodriguez holding a firearm as a deputy approached. The sheriff’s office said the deputy initially drew a Taser before switching to his gun after realizing she was armed.

Channel 9 stopped the video before the deputy fired.

Investigation continues

As Channel 9 previously reported, Anthony Rodriguez was detained after deputies accused him of battering an officer during the aftermath.

The sheriff’s office said it did not immediately arrest him because of the circumstances surrounding his wife’s death and would refer the allegation to the State Attorney’s Office for review.

Detectives were also seeking the man involved in the confrontation, saying he left after the shooting.

Anthony Rodriguez’s new statement focuses on his wife and their family and does not address the sheriff’s account of the shooting.
