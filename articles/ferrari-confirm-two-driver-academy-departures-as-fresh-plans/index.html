Ferrari confirm two driver academy departures as fresh plans revealed

TheTools · 16.10.2026 09:08 · source · permalink

Ferrari has confirmed who will be part of its driver academy for 2027.

Ferrari has confirmed that Dino Beganovic and Rafael Camara have graduated out of the Scuderia's driver academy, as the Maranello outfit unveiled its 2027 junior line-up featuring three new recruits.

Camara will step up to Formula 1 with Haas next year, while Beganovic stays on at Ferrari as a development and reserve driver, sharing those duties with Antonio Giovinazzi.

The Scuderia Ferrari Driver Academy will field eight drivers across karting, F4, Formula Regional, F3, F2 and F1 Academy in 2027.

Three newcomers join the roster: Turkish driver Alp Aksoy, Colombian Salim Hanna and Dutchman Daniel Miron.

Miron, the youngest of the new arrivals at just 13, moves up to the OK karting class after finishing runner-up in the European FIA Championship's OK-Junior category in 2026.

Noah Baglin and Filippo Sala make the leap from karting to single-seaters, both racing in Spanish F4 next year. Baglin arrives as reigning European OK champion and Junior World champion, while Sala impressed across the European Championship and Champions of the Future series.

Aksoy steps up to the European Formula Regional Championship after a strong rookie single-seater campaign that yielded three wins in Italian F4 and the Euro4 series.

Alba Larsen continues in F1 Academy for a second year, having claimed back-to-back podiums at Zandvoort in her debut campaign.

Hanna moves into a full Formula 3 season after winning at Imola in Formula Regional this year, joined by Niccolò Maccagnani, who steps up from F4 after victories at Misano and Mugello.

Tuukka Taponen completes the roster with a move to Formula 2 with ART Grand Prix, the team he raced with in his first F3 season in 2025. The Finn finished third in this year's F3 standings with a win and two further podiums.
