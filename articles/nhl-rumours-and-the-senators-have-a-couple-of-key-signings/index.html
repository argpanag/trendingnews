NHL Rumours and The Senators Have a Couple of Key Signings

TheTools · 11.11.2026 02:10 · source · permalink

Welcome to an evening of NHL Rumours as Bruce Garrioch had his segment on the TSN5 broadcast with a lot to say. The point is, as you shall see, that Garrioch does have rumours, which isn’t always the case in the usually quiet and calm Ottawa organizational camaraderie of the roster. So sit back, we are about to get sensational.

Rumour: The rumour is one you kind of know already. As a general manager Steve Staios trade partner search has been ongoing from the GM meetings, so just what is it? A top-six forward has been leading the talks for months in Ottawa.

Analysis: Between Nick Cousins on your top line where you need improvement, and Michael Amadio changed his agent and is still a pending free agent, something has to give. Right? Well it is possible.

We do believe that the 30-year-old Amadio will seek a deal north of $5 million per season. That will give him the leverage to look for a five-year deal, until he’s 35 or so. Amadio changed agents, and is now represented by Andy Scott, and even though there hasn’t been talks with Sens management to this point, Garrioch went on to indicate that both sides are eager to get a deal locked in. Amadio is a very effective analytic player and puck thief, especially so being on the third line. He has a home for years to come.

The Senators Bigger Situation at Forward

One item that Garrioch didn’t really speculate on was who the Sens could find to replace some offensive production. Of course, the William Eklund return is going to be a boost to the lineup. The Warren Foegele injury was cause for even further strain on the depth chart. So it made sense that there could be some rumours develop, as a result.

We have touched on it before in fact, some potential names include not Teuvo Teravainen, but possibly Andrew Copp, Vladimir Tarasenko, or Michael Rasmussen. There are other names, too, but the problem is it is such a hot commodity (a top-six forward), and teams in a rebuild would expect big future assets in return. So there is plenty of competition, and we know the Sens believe in their group. It is interesting to speculate on what will happen with Andre Burakovsky, who makes $5.5 million on an expiring contract, and has zero points in eight games through an injury riddled start to 2026-27.

Another Free Agent to be, on the Blueline

Another steady blueliner who is due for a new contract is Artem Zub. Garrioch gave the impression that he thought they would’ve been in discussions already with his agent Dan Milstein. Zub works wonders within the Sens top four, and even more relevant that he has chemistry with Jake Sanderson. One of the Sens best players, let alone defencemen.

At this point that hasn’t been talks. So the question becomes why?

The Last Word

With youngster Carter Yakemchuk watching from the press box five of the last six nights, it says that the Sens don’t have any injuries on the blueline. That is a good thing, but it begs the question is Zub on his last legs with the Sens, as Yakemchuk eventually replaces him. The obvious choice would be Nikolas Matinpalo, but he is a very effective third-pair guy and cost efficient while doing it well.
