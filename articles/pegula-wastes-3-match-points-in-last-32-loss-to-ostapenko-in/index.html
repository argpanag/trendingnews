Pegula wastes 3 match points in last-32 loss to Ostapenko in Wuhan after month off

TheTools · 15.10.2026 09:07 · source · permalink

Jessica Pegula has squandered three match points and lost to Jelena Ostapenko in three sets at the Wuhan Open in the American’s first match in more than a month

WUHAN, China -- Jessica Pegula squandered three match points and lost to Jelena Ostapenko in three sets at the Wuhan Open on Wednesday in the American's first match in more than a month.

Ostapenko won 2-6, 7-6 (7), 6-3 in an unlikely last-32 comeback that included saving match points on three occasions in the second set against the second-seeded Pegula, who hadn't played since losing in the U.S. Open semifinals.

Pegula, a finalist in Wuhan last year, took an extended break — missing events in Singapore and Beijing — because of the physical toll of her busy schedule over the summer.

Iga Swiatek had a much easier time of it, advancing to the round of 16 with a 6-2, 6-1 win over Magdalena Frech.

The six-time major winner started slowly against her Polish compatriot, dropping an early service game and falling behind 2-0 before winning six straight games to take the first set. She continued momentum by winning four straight games to start the second set.

Swiatek is seeded No. 7 in the WTA's 1,000-level event. She reached the quarterfinals of the recent China Open in Beijing.
