Jeremy Clarkson's farm shop announces major change after seven years

TheTools · 21.10.2026 09:19 · source · permalink

Jeremy Clarkson's Diddly Squat Farm Shop has announced a major change after seven years.

The Oxfordshire farm shop has launched a new loyalty programme called Heart & Soil, which will allow customers to collect points when buying products.

Lisa Hogan, Mr Clarkson's partner, announced the scheme in a video shared on the farm shop's Instagram account.

She said: "It’s been quite the ride over the last… since 2019, almost eight years, at Diddly Squat Farm Shop, and we’ve done it with you guys. Our customers, and everyone that’s been to the shop.

"So we’ve been putting our heads together to think [about] how we can thank you, and we’ve come up with a loyalty program. We have called it Heart and Soil.

"So when you purchase anything in the shop or online, you get an Acre. And then you get to harvest it with a gift from us. I hope you enjoy it!"

The scheme gives shoppers one “Acre” for every £1 spent, with points also available for activities including subscribing and referring friends.

The farm shop said: "A few years ago Diddly Squat was a shed with some potatoes. A lot has changed, and that’s entirely down to you.

"So we wanted to say thank you properly. We’ve launched Heart & Soil, our way of giving something back to the people who got us here.

"Every time you shop, you earn Acres. You build those up, and you harvest your rewards. Sign up via the link in our bio."

There are four ranks in the scheme, with benefits increasing as customers accumulate Acres.

After spending £100, customers can receive a surprise reward, early access to new farm produce and exclusive Heart & Soil merchandise.

Those spending £500 can receive another reward, access to limited-edition products and invitations to events.

The top £1,000 level includes free delivery on produce, a "surprise bumper bundle" and exclusive experiences described as something "money can’t buy".
