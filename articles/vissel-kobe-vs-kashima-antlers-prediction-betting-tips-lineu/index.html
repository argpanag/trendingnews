Vissel Kobe vs Kashima Antlers Preview

Vissel Kobe clash with Kashima Antlers at Noevir Stadium Kobe, with kick-off set for 06:00 on Friday 11 September.

Vissel Kobe clinched their third consecutive league victory in their latest J1 League match, defeating V-Varen Nagasaki 3-1 at home at Noevir Stadium Kobe. The Cows had most of the ball boasting 55% possession and seven shots on target, while Yuta Goke bagged two goals and Katsuya Nagato scored the other.

Kashima Antlers have suffered back-to-back J1 League defeats, recently being beaten at home by Urawa Red Diamonds 1-0 at Kashima Soccer Stadium. Toru Oniki's team boasted 58% possession and had three shots on goal.
