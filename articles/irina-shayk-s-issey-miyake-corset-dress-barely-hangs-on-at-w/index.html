TheTools · 01.11.2026 09:05 · source · permalink

Irina Shayk brings high-fashion drama and sculpted elegance to the 2026 WSJ. Magazine Innovator Awards. The supermodel steals the show in a futuristic white gown direct from the runway. She wears an architectural strapless Issey Miyake creation that redefines red-carpet style. Irina effortlessly proves that avant-garde corsetry, backless cuts, and clean minimalist lines create an unforgettable evening statement.

Irina Shayk attends WSJ Magazine Innovator Awards in strapless Issey Miyake corset dress

Irina models an all-white runway gown from Issey Miyake’s Fall/Winter 2026 Ready-To-Wear collection. The strapless dress features a structured, glossy patent-like corset bodice with a distinct rounded cup silhouette. Sculpted fabric panels flare out gently at the sides, while the completely backless cut plunges low to highlight her toned spine. The sleek column skirt flows gracefully to the floor with a subtle back slit, giving the futuristic silhouette an effortless sense of fluidity.

Posing on the blue carpet, Irina turns her back to showcase the dramatic rear view of her strapless dress. She stands tall with her hands resting naturally at her sides, projecting pure high-fashion poise and total confidence. She skips all heavy jewelry, omitting necklaces and earrings to keep total focus on the architectural design of the dress.
