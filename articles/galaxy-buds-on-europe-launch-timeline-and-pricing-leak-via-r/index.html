Samsung unveiled its first clip-on wireless earbuds, the Galaxy Buds On, earlier this month. However, the company has only confirmed that the earbuds will go on sale in its home market of Korea starting October 27, leaving global users waiting for availability details.

Now, we’ve spotted some European retail listings that offer a better idea of when the Galaxy Buds On could arrive in other markets, along with their possible pricing in Switzerland.

Galaxy Buds On Switzerland launch timeline and pricing leaked via retailers

Several European retailers have listed the Galaxy Buds On on their websites, complete with official product images. While Samsung has already revealed the renders, the more interesting details are the release dates mentioned in these listings.

The listings mention different dates, but together, they point to a possible European release in the first half of December 2026. India and the US could also get the earbuds around the same time, although regional availability remains unconfirmed.

What about pricing?

Swiss stores have listed the Galaxy Buds On for around CHF 179 and CHF 199, including VAT. The lower price could reflect a discount, while CHF 199 might be the recommended retail price (RRP).

These details come from third-party retailers, so Samsung’s official pricing and release plans could differ.

For those living under a rock, the Galaxy Buds On are Samsung’s first open-ear wireless earbuds. Unlike traditional earbuds, they sit around the outside of your ears without blocking the ear canal, letting you stay aware of your surroundings.

They also offer automatic volume adjustment, AI-powered calling features, and up to 9.5 hours of playback on a single charge. With retail listings and support pages already appearing, Samsung may not keep global users waiting much longer.
