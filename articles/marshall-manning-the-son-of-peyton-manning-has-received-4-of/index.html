Marshall Manning, the son of Peyton Manning, has received 4 offers so far, including offers from two of the biggest programs in the nation

TheTools · 06.10.2026 14:39 · source · permalink

Marshall Manning, the son of Tennessee Vols legend Peyton Manning, is a quarterback in the 2030 recruiting class.

2030 quarterback Marshall Manning, the son of Tennessee Vols legend Peyton Manning, is a high school freshman at Baylor School in Chattanooga who is currently serving as the backup to 2027 Ole Miss commit Keegan Croucher.

Marshall has played sparingly as a freshman, but he’s expected to take over as Baylor’s starting quarterback in 2027.

For now, Marshall’s recruitment is fairly quiet, but things should kick up a notch next year.

Marshall Manning has four scholarship offers so far

Marshall’s recruitment is still in the very early stages, but he has already picked up a few offers.

And two of those offers are from a couple of the biggest college football programs in the nation — Miami and Ohio State.

According to 247Sports, Miami, Ohio State, Purdue, and Missouri State are the four schools that have offered Marshall so far.

Marshall told 247Sports that he doesn’t have a favorite school at this time — he’s just looking to “build relationships” at this point in the process.

I’m sure Tennessee will eventually get involved in Marshall’s recruitment, but I think they’re waiting until he’s the starter at Baylor so it doesn’t seem like they’re offering him just because he’s Peyton’s son.
