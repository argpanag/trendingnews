I went on a Western Sydney Airport test run. Here’s the lowdown

TheTools · 23.10.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

Can you get a coffee? And how long will you have to bring a packed lunch? Here’s everything we know and don’t know about the megaproject.

On a dry run to Western Sydney International Airport, two days before opening. Louise Kennerley

I’m looking for signs of a Maccas. Surely, every self-respecting airport has one.

But not Western Sydney International (Nancy-Bird Walton) Airport, which officially opens to passengers on Sunday without a Golden Arches – and little else in the way of proper dining. Not yet, anyway.
