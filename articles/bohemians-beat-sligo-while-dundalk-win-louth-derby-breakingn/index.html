Bohemians beat Sligo while Dundalk win Louth derby | BreakingNews

TheTools · 05.09.2026 16:46 · source · permalink

Bohemians lead Sligo Rovers 3-0 thanks to goals from Douglas James Taylor, Dawson Devoy and Jordan Flores, in the League of Ireland Premier Division.

Elsewhere in the Premier Division, Dundalk secured victory with an 89th-minute goal from Daryl Horgan against Louth rivals Drogheda United.

It had looked for a while that the home side was heading for victory with a 2-1 lead over Dundalk in tonight’s derby, before Ronan Teahan got it level in the 67th minute.

It ended in a deadlock between Galway United and Shelbourne, despite an 80th-minute lead taken by the visitors, thanks to Daniel Kelly. Galway’s Jimmy Keohane fired home an injury-time shot to rescue a point for the home side.

Meanwhile, goals by Norwegian Jorgen Voilas and John Mahon gave Waterford FC a win away at St Pats.
