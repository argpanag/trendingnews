Creditor alleges "unusual arrangement" in Paddy McKillen Jr insolvency application - The Currency

TheTools · 19.10.2026 15:16 · source · permalink

Paddy McKillen Jr

Two creditors, owed €11m, may seek to set aside the High Court protective cert shielding the former Press Up boss from his creditors.

With personal debts of over €27 million, former Press Up boss Paddy McKillen Jr currently has a High Court protection certificate shielding him from his creditors as part of a personal insolvency application filed last month. It lasts for 70 days. But two of those creditors Herbert Street Property Finance (HSPF) and alternative lender Cabriz Finance are threatening to push for the removal of that safeguard over claims that the former hospitality mogul has not been sufficiently candid about his financial situation.
