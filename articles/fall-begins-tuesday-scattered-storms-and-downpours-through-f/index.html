Fall begins Tuesday; Scattered storms and downpours through Friday

Fall commences at 8:04 p.m. today, but don't break out the sweaters and fall boots just yet! The Gulf Coast will stay warm and muggy with highs in the low 90s.

Your Gulf Coast Weather Most Accurate Forecast calls for coastal showers before lunch. Storms will push inland and become more widespread. Watch out for heavy rain, gusty winds, and lightning.

We could see a few straggling showers after sunset. Temperatures will drop overnight. We will wake up to the 70s early Wednesday morning.

We have another opportunity for morning showers along the coast Wednesday. Rain coverage will expand through the afternoon. Temperatures will top out in the upper 80s and low 90s.

While storm chances stay high over the next few afternoons, drier air works into the region toward the end of the week. Make outdoor plans this weekend as rain chances significantly decrease with slightly lower humidity in place. Highs will only reach the upper 80s with morning lows in the low 70s.
