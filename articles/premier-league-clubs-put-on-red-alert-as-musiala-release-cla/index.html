Premier League clubs put on red alert as Musiala release clause revealed

TheTools · 21.11.2026 10:46 · source · permalink
  • Josh Fordham, News Editor
  • Published: 10:46, 21 Nov 2026
  • Updated: 10:47, 21 Nov 2026

Premier League clubs are expected to be on red alert amid fears at Bayern Munich that Jamal Musiala’s wages are no longer feasible.

The 23-year-old was diagnosed with a fainting condition in the summer after collapsing on the pitch twice in just a few days.

Musiala has appeared in eight matches this season, but has currently been out for the last two months with a muscle injury.

Speaking on Friday evening, Former Bayern chief Karl-Heinz Rummenigge told Sky Germany: “(Musiala) has a health-related issue. It's also well known that he has a contract involving very substantial sums of money. As a club, we have to consider how to deal with that situation. I think that's responsible and perfectly normal. 

“What we know unfortunately suggests it will be difficult for him to get back to the level he has often shown here in this stadium.” 

Rummenigge continued: “[We need to] talk to the player, to the family and, above all, to the agency, because, with all due respect, those sums are then no longer feasible.”

“I think we’ve handled the player’s situation very carefully, and we all want him to return, ideally in the best possible shape.

“But what happens if that doesn’t work out? Then, of course, we’ll also have to continue taking the financial implications into account.” 

Musiala contract

The Germany international is currently under contract at Bayern until 2030 and earns a reported £20.9m-a-year.

Reports in Germany claim that this contract includes a release clause that will become active in 2027.

Musiala has long been linked to a move to the Premier League, with Man City often touted as a destination. He was previously seen as a successor to Kevin De Bruyne.

Arsenal and Liverpool have long-term interest, meanwhile Chelsea, his former youth club, have also been linked.

talkSPORT verdict

European football expert Kevin Hatchard joined talkSPORT on Friday evening to talk about Musiala - and said the decision makers still back the player.

He said: “Jamal Musiala has had a horrible time in the last year and a half. A couple of summers ago, he had that clash with Donnarumma.

“Donnarumma dived for a ball that was on the floor and he kind of came through Musiala's leg to get there. That's not me blaming Donnarumma. That's just what happens sometimes.

“It's just really unfortunate. That was a break of the leg and there was some ankle damage there as well. So that was a really complicated injury.

“He's been out for the last eight weeks with a muscle tear. But in the middle of all that, it's emerged that he's been dealing with this neurological condition, which has caused him to have a couple of fainting fits. That's just what we know about. So he's on medication for that.

“Uli Hoeneß is the honorary president and Karl-Heinz Rummenigge, who's a Bayern legend as well, and was the CEO for a long time. What they say still carries weight, even though they haven't got official admin positions per se. 

“They've both said that they're concerned that Musiala won't be able to regain his best form and that if that's the case, maybe he shouldn't be getting 25 million euros a year. 

“That's a contract they've committed to and Max Eberl, thankfully, who is the senior sporting director there, he's come out and said, look, this is a human, first of all, that we like very much and that we respect very much. And he's still a very talented player.

“The people that matter and have the most important voices at Bayern are still in his camp and are still very locked into getting him back to where he was.

“There's no doubt that since the injury, he's not been the player that we know. But the kid's 23. So I think we can cut him some slack. He's shown already what he's capable of.

I still believe he can get back to where he was.”
