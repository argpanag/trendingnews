Take a sneak peek inside Empire's Avengers: Doomsday issue

TheTools · 19.11.2026 11:30 · source · permalink

Buckle up: Avengers: Doomsday is only a month away. And it’s going to be gigantic, a Marvel mega-event with several universes worth of heroes – and one big big big bad – colliding on the screen. As the Doomsday clock ticks down, the new issue of Empire is here to give you the best look yet at Marvel’s most massive movie, on newsstands from Thursday November 19.

Before it hits shelves, here’s a sneak peek inside.

Avengers: Doomsday

The return of the Avengers is no small thing. So neither is our Doomsday coverage – with 22 pages spanning 20 brand new interviews and never-before-seen images.

Inside, we speak to: Kevin Feige, Joe and Anthony Russo, Robert Downey Jr., Chris Evans, Chris Hemsworth, Pedro Pascal, Ian McKellen, Florence Pugh, Anthony Mackie, Simu Liu, David Harbour, Vanessa Kirby, Sebastian Stan, Letitia Wright, Tom Hiddleston, Hannah John-Kamen, James Marsden, Rebecca Romijn, and Winston Duke. Don’t miss it.

Werwulf

Robert Eggers never holds back. But even by his standards, Werwulf sounds like a wild feat. He and star Aaron Taylor-Johnson discuss the lengths they want to in order to make the scariest Eggers film yet.

Children Of Blood And Bone

There’s never been an Afrofantasy world on screen like that of Children Of Blood And Bone. Director Gina Prince-Bythewood – and stars Thuso Mbedu, Damson Idris, Tosin Cole, and Chiwetel Ejiofor – talk Empire through a YA adventure with a very specific point of view.

The Debut

Already, the directorial return of Jesse Eisenberg is getting major awards buzz. He and star Julianne Moore talk to Empire about the weird and wonderful world of his offbeat am-dram musical drama.

Little Shop Of Horrors At 40

It’s been four decades since cult horror-comedy musical Little Shop Of Horrors was willed into existence by a handful of creative geniuses. Rick Moranis, Frank Oz, Alan Menken, and Ellen Greene look back on the legacy and making of a true one-off.

First Word

In this month’s news section, we pay tribute to the late, great Sir Patrick Stewart, explore the fallout of the Paramount/Warner Bros merger, dive deep into the romantasy trend that’s spicing up Hollywood, talk Shakespeare and tragedy with Elsinore’s Simon Stone, speak to Rian Johnson about his viral photography app, and much more.

Final Cut

In the home entertainment section, we break down the colossal success of Spider-Man: Brand New Day with director Destin Daniel Cretton, rank the legendary movies of 1987, give Broadcast News the Masterpiece treatment, revisit the funniest moments of Gail Daughtry And The Celebrity Sex Pass, go movie-by-movie with filmmaker Peter Berg, and more.

Reviews

In this issue, you’ll find reviews of The Lord Of The Rings: The Rings Of Power Season 3, smash documentary You Can See Everything, Tony Gilroy’s stirring classical drama Behemoth!, Mahershala Ali’s vivid action drama Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother, the return of The Hunger Games in Sunrise On The Reaping, and plenty more.
