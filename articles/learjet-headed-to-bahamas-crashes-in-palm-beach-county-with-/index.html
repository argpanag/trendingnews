Learjet headed to Bahamas crashes in Palm Beach County with 9 people on board

TheTools · 01.11.2026 18:46 · source · permalink

GREENACRES, Fla. — A Learjet carrying nine people crashed in a residential area near Cresthaven on Sunday afternoon, according to the Federal Aviation Administration.

The FAA said the Learjet 60 crashed around 12:50 p.m. The aircraft had departed Austin-Bergstrom International Airport in Texas and was headed to North Eleuthera Airport in the Bahamas.

Nine people were on board, according to the FAA. Information about their conditions has not yet been released.

Video from the scene shows flames, thick black smoke and debris scattered across the roadway.

Palm Beach County Fire Rescue said Military Trail is closed to all traffic between Cresthaven Boulevard and 10th Avenue North. The agency described the response as an "extended incident" and urged people to avoid the area.

The FAA said the information released so far is preliminary and subject to change.

The FAA and National Transportation Safety Board will investigate the crash. The NTSB will lead the investigation and provide further updates, according to the FAA.

WPTV has crews at the scene and will bring you a live update on the investigation during the 6 p.m. newscast.

Drivers should continue to avoid the area and seek an alternate route.
