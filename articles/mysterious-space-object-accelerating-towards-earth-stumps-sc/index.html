Mysterious space object accelerating towards Earth stumps scientists | Fox Business Video

Scientists at the Georgia Institute of Technology are stumped by a mysterious space object accelerating towards Earth. Lauren Simonetti reports on speculation that the massive object could be a 1988 Soviet spacecraft.

A mysterious space object the size of a school bus is accelerating towards Earth, leaving scientists stumped. Lauren Simonetti explains that researchers at the Georgia Institute of Technology are unsure why it is gaining speed. One Harvard professor suggests the object, currently 189 million miles away, could be a Soviet spacecraft launched in 1988 that disappeared on its way to Mars.
