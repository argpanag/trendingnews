MANILA – Senate President Sherwin Gatchalian said Thursday he expects President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. to sign the measure moving the Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan Elections (BSKE) to 2028 before the start of the filing of certificates of candidacy (COCs) on Sept. 28.

The enrolled measure has already reached Malacañang, leaving presidential action as the final step before the new election schedule and five-year terms for barangay and SK officials can take effect.

“Wala naman akong nababalitaan na ive-veto nila ng Malacañang. So, masa-sign 'yun anytime, maybe this week (I haven't heard anything that Malacañang will veto it. So, it could be signed anytime, maybe this week),” Gatchalian said in a chance interview.

The House of Representatives adopted Senate Bill 2387 on Sept. 16 instead of sending the measure to a bicameral conference committee, allowing Congress to transmit it directly to the President on Sept. 22.

The measure fixes the term of barangay and SK officials at five years and moves the next BSKE from Nov. 2, 2026 to the second Monday of November 2028.

Barangay officials would be limited to two consecutive terms, while SK officials would remain limited to one term.

Gatchalian said differences in the House version were incorporated into the Senate measure before its adoption, avoiding another round of congressional action.

“Lahat ng mga nakita namin iba sa House, pinasok na lang sa Senate para hindi naman pabalik, simple bill lang (All the differences we saw in the House version were incorporated into the Senate version so it need not be returned, as it is a simple bill),” he said.

The timing is crucial for the Commission on Elections (Comelec), which is still preparing for the Nov. 2 polls while waiting for a law formally changing the schedule.

Comelec Chairperson George Garcia earlier said the filing of COCs from Sept. 28 to Oct. 5 would proceed unless the postponement becomes law before then.

He said the poll body cannot halt election preparations based merely on the expectation that the measure will be signed.

With the filing period nearing, Gatchalian said, action should be taken within the week. (PNA)
