Tonight’s starting XI, drawn by | Como 1907 Official Site

TheTools · 25.11.2026 02:09 · source · permalink

Article Content

Childhood Leukemia

Ahead of tonight’s UEFA Champions League match against AEK Athens, supporters at the Sinigaglia will see a different kind of starting XI on the big screen. The players’ portraits have been drawn by children receiving treatment at Monza Hospital.

Made possible with the support of UEFA, the initiative brings together Como 1907, Quelli che con Luca ODV and Fondazione Maria Letizia Verga ETS. Following the match, the drawings will be auctioned to raise funds for research into childhood leukaemia.

Dr Marco Spinelli explained: "Taking an active part in such an important sporting event means giving them excitement, enthusiasm and an opportunity to experience moments of normality and togetherness, even while undergoing treatment. Seeing their drawings accompany the team onto the pitch will be a tremendous source of pride and a beautiful message: children’s imagination and eagerness to participate can take centre stage well beyond the hospital walls."

For more than 15 years, Quelli che con Luca ODV has supported research into childhood leukaemia, working alongside doctors and researchers to improve treatment for children.

The initiative is part of a wider series of activities designed to strengthen the bond between the club and its supporters during Como 1907's historic first Champions League campaign. From the president personally inviting long-standing fans to attend matches, to involving loyal supporters in carrying the giant centre-circle banner onto the pitch, the club has consistently sought to make its fans an integral part of this extraordinary journey.

As part of Como 1907’s ongoing support for the association, its logo features on the back of the Club’s Champions League shirts for five of the eight league phase matches. Supporters can also purchase the dedicated charity patch for their own shirts, with all proceeds going directly to the association.

Thank you to all the children who have helped bring tonight’s starting XI to life.
