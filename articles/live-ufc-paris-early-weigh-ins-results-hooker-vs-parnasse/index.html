Live: UFC Paris early weigh ins results | Hooker vs. Parnasse

TheTools · 04.09.2026 03:45 · source · permalink
  • UFC Weigh-Ins

Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) will hold its early (and official) weigh ins for the upcoming UFC Paris mixed martial arts (MMA) event, LIVE at 3 a.m. ET from inside the promotion’s host hotel in Paris, France. We’ll have real-time results and updates below, even if you are too busy sleeping to read them, but at least you have something to look forward to when you wake up! The good news for all the East Coast sleepyheads is that Dan Hooker, Salahdine Parnasse, Michael Page, Fares Ziam, and the rest of the UFC Paris cast and crew will head to the nearby Accor Arena for the ceremonial weigh ins festivities, streaming LIVE at 12 p.m. ET.
