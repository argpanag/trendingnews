Film crews and TV star spotted in Liverpool for new BBC drama series

TheTools · 20.11.2026 19:49 · source · permalink

Film crew have been spotted in Liverpool for upcoming BBC series, Hercule. Northern Irish actor James Nesbitt was spotted among the ensemble cast on Ormond Street yesterday, Friday November 20. The city centre street looked unrecognisable while filming for the show, which follows Agatha Christie's famous detective during early cases in his career.

James, 61, has appeared in several popular programmes over the years including Cold Feet, and Netflix thriller Run Away. He is part of the cast of Hercule alongside Edward Bluemel, who is taking on the leading role of Hercule Poirot. Although James' exact role is yet to be announced.

Work continued on the six-part series, which is set for a 2027 release. Crews have been spotted in various locations across Liverpool this year, including Canning Street and Back Sandon Street in October.

Agatha Christie's Poirot, widely regarded as one of fiction's greatest detectives, first appeared in her 1916 debut novel The Mysterious Affair at Styles, which was published in the UK in October 1920.

The new series will shine a light on some of Christie's most celebrated stories while charting Hercule's burgeoning friendship with Captain Arthur Hastings, his early encounters with Scotland Yard's James Japp, and introducing him to a particular nemesis.

According to the Liverpool Film Office, the series serves as an "intimate study of Hercule the man and an epic portrait of Britain between the wars."
