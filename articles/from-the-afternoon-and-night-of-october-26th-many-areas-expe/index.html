From the afternoon and night of October 26th, many areas experienced rain due to a cold front.

TheTools · 25.10.2026 01:03 · source · permalink

According to the National Center for Meteorological and Hydrological Forecasting, from October 25th to 26th, most areas across the country will experience sunshine, with increased rainfall in the North and Central regions from the afternoon of October 26th. In the South, there may be localized showers and thunderstorms, mainly in the late afternoon or at night.

According to the meteorological agency, from October 25th to 26th, the northern region and the area from Thanh Hoa to Ha Tinh will mainly experience sunshine. The southern region is forecast to have localized showers and thunderstorms, mainly concentrated in the late afternoon or at night.

On October 25th, the highest temperatures in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City were generally 32-34 degrees Celsius. In the North, the area from Thanh Hoa to Hue, and the South, temperatures were generally 31-34 degrees Celsius, with some places exceeding 34 degrees Celsius. In the South Central coastal region, temperatures were generally 30-33 degrees Celsius, with some places exceeding 33 degrees Celsius.

From tomorrow afternoon, October 26th, due to the influence of a cold front, Hanoi and the North are forecast to be cloudy; scattered showers and thunderstorms will begin to appear in the area from Thanh Hoa to Ha Tinh. From the night of October 26th to October 29th, rain will increase in the Central region.

Specifically, the area from Thanh Hoa to Hue City will experience scattered showers and thunderstorms, with some areas experiencing heavy rain. Afterwards, the rain will spread to the area from Da Nang City to Quang Ngai. In the northern part of the South Central coastal region, there will be showers and thunderstorms from October 27th to 29th, with some areas experiencing heavy rain. The southern part of the South Central coastal region will have less rain, with mostly sunny days.

On the night of October 26th and throughout October 29th, the northern region is likely to experience showers. The southern region will also see increased rainfall around October 28th and 29th.

From October 30th to November 3rd, rainfall in Central Vietnam is forecast to decrease but not stop completely. Scattered rain is expected in the area from Thanh Hoa to Hue City. In the South Central coastal region, scattered showers and thunderstorms are expected in the northern part until around October 31st. The southern part of this region will experience less rain and sunny days.

At this time, the North will mainly experience sunshine, with fog in the early morning. The South and other areas will mostly have sunshine, with showers and thunderstorms in the afternoon and evening.
