Adéla Channels Her Inner Movie Star in 'Nicole Kidman' Music Video

TheTools · 04.09.2026 06:50 · source · permalink

Adéla brought Nicole Kidman‘s most famous roles to life in a new music video for her single “Nicole Kidman.”

The video, directed by Hannah Lux Davis, is filled with references to some of the Oscar-winning actress’s most beloved films, including Eyes Wide Shut, Moulin Rouge, Birth, and Babygirl, as well as her iconic AMC commercial.

The singer recently explained of the song’s inspiration, “Somehow, I got invited to a Golden Globes event. I’ve never been in a movie, but I got all glammed up, put into a dress, had my hair done, and I looked really good. My hair was super bouncy, and my corset was so tight that I thought, ‘I feel like Nicole Kidman right now’. I actually watched Eyes Wide Shut, and became obsessed with her. To me, she is the movie star.”

“Nicole Kidman” appears on Adéla’s debut album, Prima, out today. It follows her debut EP, The Provocateur. She told Rolling Stone last year, “My EP was people realizing that Adéla is a good performer and artist, but I don’t think I’ve had the chance to build a world yet. I really want to do that with the album.”

She added, “At the end of the day, I’m just a pop girl. I’m young, I’m going to be singing and I want to be dancing. And I want to be really honest.”

Adéla will kick off her first-ever headlining tour next week. The trek will bring her across North America for shows in cities like Detroit, Toronto, New York, Nashville, Atlanta, Seattle, Los Angeles, and Mexico. She’ll then head to Europe and the U.K. next year for shows in Dublin, London, Berlin, Prague, Oslo, Warsaw, and more.
