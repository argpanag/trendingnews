Nvidia's (NVDA) stock rocketed more than 30% Monday after the company's board announced it reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO and president of the chip giant.

The chip giant added nearly $1 trillion to its market cap, posting its best day since May 2023. Shares of neoclouds CoreWeave (CRWV) and Nebius (NBIS) also climbed more than 30%.

In a statement Monday, Nvidia's board said it had a series of discussions with Huang and was satisfied with his candor and ability to lead the company.

"The board believes Jensen is the right person to lead Nvidia, and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners and customers," Nvidia chair Harvey Jones said.

"We have every confidence in Jensen and look forward to working with him as he resumes his leadership of the Company. We are grateful to Colette Kress for her outstanding service as interim CEO, in addition to her continuing responsibilities as Nvidia's CFO," he added.

Jones will remain the chair of Nvidia's board.

Just last week, Jones told investors during the company's Q3 earnings call that Nvidia had no plans to bring back Huang.

Huang resigned from Nvidia on Nov. 12 after an alleged affair with Alice Huang, suspected of being a Chinese spy. Huang admitted to having an affair at the time but didn't name the woman.

He also allegedly had affairs with two Nvidia employees, including executive Kimberly Powell.

Alice Huang was the executive of Singapore-based Megaspeed, which is suspected of smuggling high-powered Nvidia chips to China.

The company said the board received input from senior levels of the US government and, based on that input, doesn't believe Huang's reinstatement poses a national security risk.

"I am honored to return to Nvidia and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution," Huang said in a statement.
