Lana Del Rey, Dua Lipa, Zara Larsson Contribute New Music to 'GTA VI: The Album'

TheTools · 14.11.2026 20:37 · source · permalink

Ahead of the much-anticipated arrival of Grand Theft Auto VI, the track list for the game’s soundtrack has (accidentally) been revealed, with Lana Del Rey, Dua Lipa, Zara Larsson, and more contributing new music.

In addition to the previously released soundtrack cuts by Travis Scott, Morgan Wallen, Keith Richards (“Bright Lights, Big City“), and Justice featuring Tame Impala and SZA, the 34-song GTA VI: The Album also features tracks by Rauw Alejandro, Jennie, Rina Sawayama, Young Thug, Arca, Tove Lo, Playboi Carti, a collaboration between Justice (who also scored the game) and Michael McDonald, and more.

The GTA VI: The Album track list leaked this weekend when images of the (prematurely sold) vinyl release appeared online; sources confirmed the track list to Rolling Stone. GTA VI: The Album will arrive on Friday, November 20, a day after GTA VI itself drops digitally.

On November 21, Cardi B, Kodak Black, and Don Toliver — all three of whom also appear on GTA VI: The Album — will join Travis Scott and Justice at a one-off Grand Theft Auto 6 music festival in Miami. 

It was previously revealed that Lana Del Rey, Morgan Wallen, Bad Bunny, members of Slayer, Burna Boy, and Robyn would all DJ their own stations within the world of GTA VI.

“One of the coolest things about this is the fact that the first time some of our family got to LA they actually knew the roads because of playing the game,” “Video Games” singer Del Rey said of contributing her voice to the game. “On so many different levels this is super special and a privilege. Can’t wait to get into the game and start playing.”

Del Rey’s new song is titled “Crazy for You,” which shares its name with a track that’s been in the singer’s unreleased arsenal for over a decade.
