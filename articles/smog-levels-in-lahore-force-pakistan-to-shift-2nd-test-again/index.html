Smog levels in Lahore force Pakistan to shift 2nd test against Sri Lanka to Rawalpindi

TheTools · 11.11.2026 10:31 · source · permalink

RAWALPINDI, Pakistan (AP) — Rawalpindi will host back-to-back test matches this month due to an increase in smog levels in Lahore, the Pakistan Cricket Board said on Tuesday.
