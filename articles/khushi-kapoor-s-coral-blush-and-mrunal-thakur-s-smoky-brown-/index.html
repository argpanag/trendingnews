Khushi Kapoor's coral blush and Mrunal Thakur's smoky brown eyes were the highlights of this week's best beauty looks

TheTools · 21.11.2026 19:51 · source · permalink

This week's best beauty looks made a case for soft glam, with warm blush à la Khushi Kapoor, Ananya Panday's sculpted cheeks and multiple takes on nude lips emerging as recurring favourites. While Sobhita Dhulipala and Mrunal Thakur opted for defined eyes with smoky brown eyeshadow and dark eyeliner, Sharvari and Diana Penty kept their makeup understated, allowing luminous skin and naturally flushed cheeks to take centre stage. Hairstyles ranged from voluminous blowouts and flowing waves to elegant updos, offering plenty of inspiration for both everyday makeup and occasion dressing.

For her beauty look, Ananya Panday opted for sculpted makeup that complemented her voluminous, side-parted hairstyle. Her base had a smooth, satin finish, with warm peach blush blended across the cheeks to add colour and definition. The eyes were lined with black eyeliner, while soft brown eyeshadow added depth to the lids. Long, fluttery lashes and neatly defined brows framed her eyes, while a brown lip liner paired with a muted rosy nude lipstick in a satin finish kept the overall look balanced. Her hair was styled in loose, outward-facing waves, with plenty of volume at the crown.

Sharvari's look had a romantic, traditional feel. Her hair was pulled into a soft, low updo adorned with red roses, with loose, face-framing tendrils adding movement around the face. Her complexion had a luminous finish, with peachy pink blush diffused across the cheeks and a subtle sheen highlighting the high points of her face. Soft brown eyeshadow and defined lashes drew attention to her eyes, while brushed-up brows kept the look natural. A muted pink nude lipstick and a small red bindi completed the look.

Warm-toned makeup took centre stage in Khushi Kapoor's look. Her base was a velvetty matte, with a generous sweep of coral blush adding warmth across the cheeks. Brown eyeshadow was blended across the lids and paired with a darker shade along the outer corners for added definition. Her lips were outlined in a deeper brown shade and filled with a muted nude lipstick, creating a softly contoured effect. The hair was styled in a sleek, voluminous half-up look, with straight lengths falling past her shoulders.

Mrunal Thakur's look leaned towards understated glamour, with subtly smoked eyes and a luminous complexion. Her base had a satin finish, with warm rose-brown blush adding definition to the cheeks. Brown eyeshadow was diffused across the eyelids, while dark eyeliner along the upper and lower lash lines created a subtle smoky effect. Her brows were kept full and defined, framing the eyes without overpowering the look. A muted rose-nude lipstick complemented the neutral makeup, while her long, straight hair was parted down the middle and left flowing around her shoulders.

Diana Penty favoured a fresh, luminous finish. Her skin had a dewy appearance, with peach blush adding warmth to the cheeks and highlighter catching the light along the cheekbones. The eyes featured softly blended brown eyeshadow and defined lashes, while neatly shaped brows framed the face. A rosy pink lip colour brought a subtle contrast to the warm-toned makeup. Her hair was swept back into an updo, keeping the focus on her face and statement jewellery.

A more defined approach to beauty emerged in Sobhita Dhulipala's look, paired with a rust-coloured outfit. Her complexion was softly sculpted, with warm terracotta blush complementing the earthy tones of her ensemble. The eyes featured smoky brown eyeshadow, dark eyeliner and defined lashes, creating depth without relying on heavy shimmer. A brown-toned nude lipstick completed the monochromatic makeup. Her short, naturally textured curls were styled with volume and movement, lending the look a slightly undone finish.
