RTÉ staff evacuated after bomb threat- live updates

TheTools · 06.11.2026 10:18 · source · permalink

An incident is under way at the RTÉ studios in Donnybrook, Dublin 4.

Staff have been evacuated from the building and emergency services are at the scene following a bomb threat.

RTÉ said the alarm was raised after it received an email on Friday morning. The broadcaster says scores of other media outlets across Ireland and Britain received the threat.

RTÉ has moved to pre-recorded programmes as a precautionary measure while gardai investigate.

Newstalk and Mediahuis staff have also been evacuated

Their offices at Talbot Street and Marconi House in Dublin have been evacuated

The alarm was raised after an email was sent to the broadcaster this morning

Gardai remain on the scene

An Garda Siochana at the scene

An Garda Siochana surround RTE Studios in Dublin after receiving a bomb threat

Gardai confirm bomb threats to Irish media

"An Garda Síochána is aware of a threat made against media organisations here and in Europe" a Garda spokesperson told Dublin Live.

"An Garda Síochána has been liaising with the Irish media organisations and the threats will be investigated."

RTE Radio One update

RTÉ Radio One is off air and it has replaced its programme Today With David McCullagh with a pre-recorded programme of music.

Irish Independent also targeted

Media offices across Dublin have been evacuated, including the Irish Independent, as a number of publications have been targeted

RTE confirm bomb threat

RTE have cinfirmed that the bomb threat was sent via email to RTE and a number of media outlets across Ireland.

"Bomb threat"

Journalist and broadcaster Sarah McInerney has shared details from the scene via social media.

"I am standing outside RTE, we have just come off air on morning Ireland and the whole building has been evaculated because there has been a bomb threat" she said.

She said that there is a general sense that the bomb threat is a hoax, but "information is sketchy" at this time.

RTE staff evacuated

Staff at RTÉ have been evacuated from the building after an alleged security alert.

There is a major garda presence at the HQ of the national broadcaster
