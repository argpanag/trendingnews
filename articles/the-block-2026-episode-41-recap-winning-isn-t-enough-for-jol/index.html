The Block 2026 Episode 41 recap: Winning isn’t enough for Jolene, she also wants her rivals crushed - realestate.com.au

TheTools · 11.10.2026 15:27 · source · permalink

It’s a mad scramble to the finish line for pool and outdoor terrace week with even Block super-landscaper Troy Lovett shaking his head at the amount of work to be done in one week.

“Let me tell you, I’m good at what I do, and this is f**king out of control,” he says.

Sev, meanwhile, is complaining at how unfair it is that she and Courtney have to present double the amount of decking of other teams – in the house that they chose for its expansive yard.

“I think it’s unfair that our deck is twice the size and we had the same amount of time as across the road. Unless someone is going to give us another day it’s looking like we’ll be at the bottom of the leaderboard,” she says.

The judges found Adam and Jolene’s outdoor terrace cluttered and didn’t like the high table and chairs blocking the view of the pool from the rumpus room.

So it’s not the best timing when the teams are dragged off site for another befuddling challenge, this time involving archery. The prize is a safe containing a pricey watch, and a revolving watch display case with automatic watch winder, a gadget that could only appeal to the kind of people who post photos from business class.

Then it’s on to judging.

Adam and Jolene have spent $39,079 on a space that includes the $60k poolside outdoor kitchen they won in an earlier week as well as a mini-sauna on their outdoor terrace, along with a high table and seating overlooking the pool.

Adam and Jolene’s pool area with outdoor kitchen.

Shaynna Blaze’s complaint is the high table and chairs blocks the view of the pool, and with the sauna in a corner the outdoor terrace is “cluttered with furniture and accessories”.

But all the judges love the outdoor kitchen with its built in barbecue, pizza oven, sink and wok burner.

“This exceeds the market,” is Marty Fox’s assessment. “You’d only see it in a $10 million plus house.”

Chantel and Wyatt’s pool area.

Chantel and Wyatt have dropped $35,314 for their outdoor terrace with kitchenette and heater and pool area with built-in long bench.

The judges are incredulous that they’ve neglected to have a barbecue.

“We as Australians entertain around a barbecue,” Marty notes.

Wyatt’s explanation? The barbecue from their upstairs balcony is on wheels, so just needs to be rolled into the lift and brought down to the pool.

“Sounds like a pain in the arse to me,” Scott Cam says, for the nation.

Shaynna is unimpressed by the level of seating around the pool, singling out the long padded bench for particular scorn.

“That is a mean, stingy bench seat that feels like you’ve gone to a public pool,” she says.

She’s also not a fan of the rolled up towels or blow-up flamingo used for styling.

Shaynna objected to the towels on Chantel and Wyatt’s day beds. Your guess is as good as ours.

Chantel as usual shrugs off any and all criticism, saying she’d be more worried if she gained their approval given her style is the polar opposite of the judges’.

“Them saying they like anything of mine will just be offensive,” she says.

Lisa and Rosco have spent $21,369.

The judges love their choice of a dark pool, the only one on The Block, and they get away with having a high table on their terrace because the minimalist stools they’ve chosen mean the view of the pool isn’t blocked.

Lisa and Rosco’s outdoor terrace with smoker in the corner.

But their big miss is not having the glass balustrade to the outdoor terrace high enough to act as a pool fence, meaning they’ve had to add one below, creating a weird and space-sucking walkway between the house and the pool.

“This is a huge mistake that is non-negotiable to fix,” Shaynna says.

The judges are also unimpressed with a handwritten sign explaining that concrete steps will be installed later when the concrete path is poured.

The judges hate Lisa and Rosco’s unnecessary walkway.

“That is a bulls**t excuse,” Darren Palmer says.

On top of that, there’s no lighting around the pool and not enough seating.

“This is the worst one today,” Marty says, lamenting the fact they haven’t delivered something that capitalises on having the best views of the ocean and surrounding mature trees. “I’m annoyed.”

Lisa and Rosco’s pool area os deemed the worst of the day.

“They have been given one of the best backyards on The Block and they haven’t made the most of what they got,” Darren agrees.

Courtney and Sev have shelled out $36,806 for a curved glass pool fence and an absolutely enormous deck, which is all but finished despite Sev’s worries.

They have a huge barbecue and outdoor kitchen won in week two, plus a terrace with fan, heater, speakers and lighting.

Courtney and Sev’s winning pool area.

The judges aren’t sure of the cow-grate built into the terrace area but acknowledge it will come into its own as the ground cover planting below it fills out and are otherwise rapturous.

“They have absolutely knocked this out of the ballpark,” Darren says. “They have done as much decking as any of the houses down there and then some. It’s little wonder they didn’t finish, the square metreage is double, triple the other houses.”

Courtney and Sev’s outdoor terrace.

“This would have been a 10 if the decking was screwed down,” Shaynna says, dispensing with the judges’ usual policy of handing out 10s to Courtney and Sev for unfinished spaces.

“It is an architect’s dream,” Marty agrees.

Rick and Tayla’s outdoor terrace.

Rick and Tayla have spent $30,472 on an outdoor kitchen with stone bench top, enormous TV that can be watched from the pool and an olive tree planted through the deck, all of which the judges like but it’s not even close to being finished thanks to Tayla, as usual, spending her time mugging for the camera rather than helping her dad.

Rick and Tayla shoved all their outdoor furniture into one corner of their pool deck and stuck with their tradition of not finishing rooms.

The stars are aligned for another victory for Courtney and Sev, but Adam and Jolene play a secret bonus point they won last week and pip them by three quarters of a point (thanks to a 9 ¾ from Marty Fox).

The episode ends with a characteristically gracious and classy Jolene saying “I think the girls were salty. F**ken good.”
