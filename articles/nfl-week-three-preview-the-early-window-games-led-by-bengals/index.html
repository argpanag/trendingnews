NFL Week Three Preview: The Early Window Games, Led by Bengals at Steelers

TheTools · 25.09.2026 19:25 · source · permalink

Nine games in the early window this Sunday, with the pick of the slate being the all-AFC North clash between the Cincinnati Bengals and the Pittsburgh Steelers.

There are nine games in the early window on Sunday evening, with a few cracking matchups, none more so than the all-AFC North clash between the Pittsburgh Steelers and the Cincinnati Bengals.

Cincinnati Bengals (2-0) @ Pittsburgh Steelers (1-1)

What a game this promises to be! Last season's meeting saw the Steelers destroy the Bengals 34-12. The Bengals had backup Joe Flacco under center, and the Steelers ended with their backup Mason Rudolph in for Aaron Rodgers. This season will see Rodgers go up against Joe Burrow.

The Bengals have beaten the Buccaneers and the Texans so far this season, and QB Joe Burrow has been almost back to his best this season after his turf toe injury. He has 45 of 66 for 461 yards, three touchdowns and an interception.

RB Chase Brown has rushed for 136 yards on 36 carries and a touchdown. WRs Tee Higgins and Ja'Marr Chase have had good seasons as well, Higgins with eight receptions for 154 yards, and Chase 87 yards on nine receptions and two touchdowns.

The Steelers beat the Falcons in week one, but lost heavily to the Patriots 20-3 last weekend. Veteran QB Aaron Rodgers has been good so far this season, with 47 of 79 for 408 yards, one touchdown and an interception.

Jaylen Warren has been the top RB for the Steelers, with 81 yards on 21 carries, and seven receptions for 61 yards. WR D.K. Metcalf only has eight receptions for 67 yards so far this season, so he will want to start getting into the game more.

Prediction - The Bengals to beat the Steelers by five points.

Carolina Panthers (1-1) @ Cleveland Browns (1-1)

A bit of a surprise game now, as both sides are perhaps better than everyone expected. Their last meeting was back in week one of the 2022 season, with the Browns coming out on top 26-24.

The Panthers lost in a shootout with the Bears 59-37, but beat the Falcons 34-3 last week. QB Bryce Young has 46 of 73 for 648 yards, six touchdowns and only one interception so far.

WRs Jalen Coker and Tetairoa McMillan have had the lion's share of receptions, with Coker having 16 receptions for 204 yards and two touchdowns, and McMillan 10 receptions for 176 yards. RB Chuba Hubbard has 102 yards on 22 carries and a touchdown.

The Browns were soundly beaten in week one by the Jaguars 34-10, but beat the Buccaneers in a tight game, 23-19. QB Deshaun Watson has 40 of 52 for 443 yards, three touchdowns and one interception, so he will start to win the crowd over if he keeps up with those numbers.

He has also rushed for 60 yards on 13 carries. WR Denzel Boston has seven receptions for 154 yards and two touchdowns.

Prediction - Another tight game, but I have the Panthers beating the Browns by five points.

New England Patriots (1-1) @ Jacksonville Jaguars (1-1)

The last time these two sides met was at Wembley Stadium in week seven of the 2024 season, with the Jaguars winning 32-16.

The Patriots lost to the Seahawks in the Super Bowl rematch of week one 13-10, but beat the Steelers 20-3. QB Drake Maye has 37 of 55 for 386 yards, one touchdown and four interceptions. RB Rhamondre Stevenson has 94 yards on 24 carries, as well as six receptions for 47 yards. WR Mack Hollins has six receptions for 69 yards.

The Jaguars beat the Browns 34-10 in week one, but lost to the Broncos 20-13 last week. QB Trevor Lawrence has 35 of 52 for 434 yards, four touchdowns and an interception. RB Bhayshul Tuten has 131 yards on 28 carries and a touchdown. WR Brian Thomas has six receptions for 80 yards, and Jakobi Meyers has three receptions for 68 yards and a touchdown.

Prediction - The Patriots beat the Jaguars by three points.Embed from Getty Images

Best of the Rest

The New York Jets (1-1) are another surprise package, and they take on the Detroit Lions (1-1) at Ford Field. The Jets have Geno Smith at QB, and he has 46 of 65 for 462 yards and a touchdown so far this season. Lions QB Jared Goff has 52 of 77 for 533 yards and six touchdowns.

Prediction - The Lions to beat the Jets by seven points.

This all AFC South clash has two sides who are both searching for that first win, as the Houston Texans travel to Lucas Oil Stadium to take on the Indianapolis Colts. Texans QB C.J. Stroud has 56 of 93 for 627 yards and two touchdowns. Colts QB Daniel Jones has 41 of 62 for 376 yards, two touchdowns and two interceptions.

Prediction - The Texans to beat the Colts by five points.

Next up for the winless Los Angeles Chargers (0-2) is a trip to Highmark Stadium to take on the Buffalo Bills (2-0). Chargers QB Justin Herbert has 32 of 54 for 401 yards, two touchdowns and three interceptions. Bills QB Josh Allen has 40 of 60 for 582 yards and five touchdowns. He has also rushed for 92 yards on 20 carries and four touchdowns.

Prediction - The Bills to beat the Chargers by 10 points.

The Tennessee Titans (0-2) go to MetLife Stadium to take on the New York Giants (1-1). Titans QB Cam Ward has 32 of 52 for 323 yards and a touchdown, whilst also scoring two touchdowns on eight carries for 28 yards. Giants QB Jaxson Dart is out with a knee injury, so Jameis Winston takes over as starter.

Prediction - The Titans to beat the Giants by a point.

Reigning Super Bowl champs, the Seattle Seahawks (2-0) travel to the capital to take on the Washington Commanders (0-2) at Northwest Stadium. The Seahawks have had Drew Lock under center after Sam Darnold suffered a glute injury just five plays into the season. Lock has 35 of 48 for 422 and four touchdowns, but Darnold has been back on the practice field and may start this weekend.

The Commanders also have their injury issues at the QB position. Starter Jayden Daniels dislocated his elbow last week and is out for a period of time, so backup Marcus Mariota starts this week.

Prediction - The Seahawks to beat the Commanders by 10 points.

And finally.... The Kansas City Chiefs (2-0) travel down to South Florida to take on the winless Miami Dolphins (0-2). Chiefs QB Patrick Mahomes has 47 of 74 for 566 yards and five touchdowns, and I would not be surprised if he doubled his tally of both against the Dolphins.

Prediction - The Chiefs to thrash the Dolphins by 20 points (at least)
