MLBB M8 World Championship Format and Tickets Revealed for Istanbul 2027

TheTools · 22.10.2026 12:26 · source · permalink

The voyage is officially charted. MOONTON Games has today revealed the full tournament format, confirmed venues, and opened ticket sales for the M8 World Championship — Mobile Legends: Bang Bang’s flagship world title event — which will sail across two continents in January 2027, from Bangkok, Thailand, to Istanbul, Türkiye.

One Last Stand in Bangkok: The M8 Wild Card

Before any team can compete in Istanbul, a record 10 Wild Card teams must first survive Bangkok. This is the largest Wild Card stage in M Series history — up from eight teams at M7 — with three coveted spots in the 16-team Main Stage on the line.

The Wild Card unfolds in two phases. In Phase 1, the 10 teams are split into two groups of five for a single round-robin in Best-of-One (BO1) format; the top team from each group advances directly to Istanbul. The four remaining second- and third-place finishers then meet in Phase 2 — a cross-group, BO5 single-elimination bracket — where just one more Golden Ticket to the Main Stage is up for grabs.

Each team that earns a Main Stage berth receives an M8 Golden Ticket, a boarding-pass-inspired keepsake symbolising their passage to Istanbul. For Singapore and the broader SEA region, the pathway runs through the MLBB Championship Tour (MCT) SEA: MCT SEA’s top two finishers book direct Main Stage flights to Istanbul, while the third- and fourth-place sides must fight it out at the Bangkok Wild Card for a shot at one of the remaining places.

Stand Above the World: The Istanbul Main Stage

The 16 qualified teams arrive in Istanbul for a gruelling journey across two stages. First up is the Swiss Stage: teams play five rounds and need three wins to advance, while three losses end their M8. Eight sides emerge to contest the double-elimination Knockout Stage, hosted at ESA Espor Arena from 14 to 19 January 2027. Matches start at BO3 and escalate, with the Grand Finals at Ora Arena played as a BO7 showdown — the biggest match format in M Series history.

The winner claims the US$1 million prize pool and the title of MLBB World Champion on European soil for the first time in the franchise’s history.

Tickets and the Gen-M Grand Finals Experience

Fans can now plan their trip to Istanbul. Knockout Stage tickets at ESA Espor Arena start from just ₺150 (approximately US$3), with early-bird pricing available 24 October to 21 November. Tickets are sold through the official partner Biletix. Visa cardholders receive an exclusive 20% discount on early-bird purchases as part of the payments brand’s extended three-year partnership with MOONTON for the M Series.

Grand Finals tickets at Ora Arena come in four tiers — Epic Match Pass, Legend Match Pass, Mythic Match Pass, and the premium M-Circle Pass — with more details dropping on 1 November. New for M8 is Gen-M, an exclusive programme recognising devoted MLBB fans: register for verification from 7 to 12 November to unlock priority access to early-bird Grand Finals ticket sales running 18 to 21 November.

MLBB at 10: A Global Game in Full Sail

The M8 lands as Mobile Legends: Bang Bang celebrates its 10th anniversary. Since launching in 2016 the title has clocked over 1.5 billion installations and 110 million monthly active users, and the M7 World Championship set a record 5.68 million Peak Concurrent Viewers to become the most-watched mobile esports event in history, according to Esports Charts.

The European pivot has been building all year: the MLBB Mid Season Cup and the MLBB Women’s International both debuted in Paris in 2026, and MLBB competed as an official medal event at the Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, where Myanmar took gold. The M8 in Istanbul is MOONTON’s most ambitious statement yet about where mobile esports is headed.

Tiger Xu, Global Head of MLBB Esports at MOONTON Games, said in the announcement: “As we celebrate MLBB’s tenth anniversary, we are setting our sights on an even bigger next decade. Bringing the M Series to Europe is an important step in building on our strength in Asia and expanding MOONTON Games’ global presence. We see an opportunity for new markets to become lasting centres of MLBB Esports, with their own communities, competitive ambitions, and investment.”
