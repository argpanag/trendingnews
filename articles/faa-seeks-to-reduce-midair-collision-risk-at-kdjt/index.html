FAA Seeks to Reduce Midair Collision Risk at KDJT

TheTools · 13.11.2026 20:00 · source · permalink

Agency proposes amendments to airport’s Class C airspace to lower potential for conflicts within primary arrival and departure corridors.

Key Takeaways:

  • The FAA proposes expanding Class C airspace around Donald J. Trump International Airport (KDJT) to reduce the potential for midair collisions and enhance safety in its "complex and crowded" terminal area.
  • The expansion includes increasing the vertical and lateral limits of existing Class C areas (A, B, C) and establishing two new areas (D, E) to cover primary arrival and departure corridors, extending up to 22 nm from the airport.
  • This proposal stems from a staff study prompted by record-high traffic at KDJT and surrounding general aviation airports, which identified numerous instances where air traffic control had to intervene to avoid conflicts with nonparticipating VFR aircraft.

The FAA is proposing to expand the boundaries of Class C airspace surrounding Donald J. Trump International Airport (KDJT) in Florida to reduce the potential for midair collisions in the terminal area.

The agency in a notice of proposed rulemaking (NPRM) recommended expanding the vertical limits and lateral boundaries of the airport’s existing Class C airspace and creating two new areas.
