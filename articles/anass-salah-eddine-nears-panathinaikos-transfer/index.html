Anass Salah-Eddine Nears Panathinaikos Transfer

TheTools · 12.09.2026 16:16 · source · permalink

Rabat – Moroccan international Anass Salah-Eddine is close to leaving AS Roma for Panathinaikos, according to French outlet Foot Mercato.

The two sides have reportedly reached a verbal agreement over the transfer. Salah-Eddine is now expected to undergo a medical before completing the move.

The deal is expected to include a €7 million purchase option, according to Foot Mercato.

The Moroccan left-back has struggled for regular playing time at Roma. He was reportedly not part of the Italian club’s plans and wanted to find a new destination to relaunch his career.

Salah-Eddine had previously turned down a move to Olympiacos earlier in the transfer window. He could now remain in Greece but join their city rivals, Panathinaikos.

The Greek club has continued to strengthen its squad with Moroccan players. Panathinaikos already has Moroccan internationals Azzedine Ounahi and Imran Louza.

Salah-Eddine’s arrival would therefore make him the third Moroccan international to join the club.

The transfer would give the 24-year-old defender an opportunity to revive his career and secure more regular playing time in Greece.
