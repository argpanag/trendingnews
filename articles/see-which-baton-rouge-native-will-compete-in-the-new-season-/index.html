See which Baton Rouge native will compete in the new season of 'Dancing with the Stars'

TheTools · 17.09.2026 01:10 · source · permalink

BATON ROUGE — Baton Rouge native Sarah Jane Nader will be featured in season 35 of "Dancing with the Stars." 

Born and raised in Baton Rouge, Nader currently works as an actress and a model, starring in the Hulu/Disney+ series "Love Thy Nader," according to ABC. The show follows Nader and her family as they move from their Louisiana roots to their current life in New York. 

Her sister Brooks Nader also appeared in season 33 of the show, finishing in ninth place. 

Outside of television, Nader serves as the head of Non-Profit and Talent Partnerships at Influencer Social Responsibility, according to ABC.
