Lech Poznan vs. Sunderland - Head-to-head record

This fixture marks the first competitive meeting between Lech Poznan and Sunderland in European competition.

The Railwaymen have crossed paths with English clubs on five previous occasions in major UEFA tournaments, recording one victory and four defeats.

Their solitary success came against Manchester City in November 2010, when a memorable 3-1 Europa League victory sent the Poznan crowd into raptures.

While Sunderland are stepping onto Polish soil for the first time, Le Bris’ men can lean on their perfect European start to navigate a hostile atmosphere.

With both teams needing a statement performance to steady their campaigns, Thursday’s clash promises to set an intriguing benchmark in this new continental fixture.

Lech Poznan - Looking for European form

Niels Frederiksen’s men return home aiming to halt a nightmare run in Europe after sweeping Korona Kielce aside 6-1 to stay in the Polish title race on Sunday. Below is an overview of the hosts’ current form, squad news, and projected starting XI:

Recent form

Domestically, the Railwaymen have been ruthless, winning seven of their last 10 matches at the Stadion Poznan and scoring 26 goals in that run.

However, bridging the gap to elite European opposition has been a painful exercise: Lech have lost both of their league-phase fixtures 4-0 against Crystal Palace and Bayer Leverkusen, trailing by three goals at the interval in each match.

Pablo Rodríguez will run the show as the central playmaker after producing two goals and three assists in Sunday’s domestic rout. Flanking the Spaniard will be in-form Swedish winger Patrik Wålemark, who leads the club with 11 goals this season, alongside Luis Palma and veteran captain Mikael Ishak up front.

Frederiksen welcomes back holding midfielder Radosław Murawski from suspension to partner Antoni Kozubal, but must navigate a lengthy injury list.

Defender Wojciech Mońka (muscle) is sidelined alongside Alex Douglas (rib), Ali Gholizadeh (ACL), and Allahyar Sayyadmanesh (groin). Mateusz Lis starts in goal behind Joel Pereira, Mateusz Skrzypczak, Terry Yegbe, and Michał Gurgul.

Sunderland - Hoping to build on European success

Régis Le Bris takes his Sunderland squad to Poland aiming to protect their unblemished Europa League record after falling 2-0 at Bournemouth in the Premier League on Sunday. Here is an examination of the visitors’ tactical setup, injury updates, and expected starting lineup ahead of kickoff:

Recent form

The Black Cats have lived two completely different realities this autumn.

While four consecutive Premier League defeats have left them languishing in the bottom three, Sunderland have looked composed in Europe, grinding out a 1-0 home win over AZ Alkmaar before Noah Sadiki’s 93rd-minute strike sealed a 2-1 comeback win at Torreense.

However, defensive concentration away from the Stadium of Light remains a major problem: Sunderland have conceded in 10 straight away matches across all competitions, shipping 19 goals.

Le Bris is expected to stick with 21-year-old Ukrainian striker Timur Tutierov in attack, with Brian Brobbey (hamstring) and Wilson Isidor (knock) unlikely to start. Talented teenager Chris Rigg will start in the number 10 role, flanked by Belgian winger Malick Fofana and Nilson Angulo.

In midfield, Swiss international Granit Xhaka partners European hero Noah Sadiki, providing experience and grit in central areas.

Le Bris must cope without defenders Thomas Meunier and Daniel Ballard (both hamstring), while Kevin Danso is eligible to start alongside Luke O’Nien, Trai Hume, and Dayann Méthalie in front of goalkeeper Robin Roefs.

Predictions for Lech Poznan vs. Sunderland - Black Cats to Continue European Run

Lech have had a torrid time before the break in Europe, trailing 3-0 at half-time against both Crystal Palace and Bayer Leverkusen. With Sunderland looking to shake off their domestic rust quickly and assert control through Xhaka and Rigg, backing Sunderland to be winning at half-time looks like a sharp angle.

While Lech have failed to register a goal in the Europa League so far, they just hit six past Korona Kielce at the weekend and possess genuine firepower through Wålemark and Ishak. Coupled with a Sunderland defence that has conceded in 10 straight away trips, backing Over 2.5 goals in total carries plenty of weight.

Sunderland have looked confident on the continent, winning both matches with a level of tactical maturity that has deserted them in the Premier League. Lech have lost five straight Europa League games by an aggregate score of 14-0, and that gulf in technical quality should tell over 90 minutes. Back Sunderland on the Moneyline to take all three points.
