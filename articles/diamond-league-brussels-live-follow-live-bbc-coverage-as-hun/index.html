    Brussels marks the end of the Diamond League series. It is the 15th meeting across four different continents, spanning across the Friday and Saturday.

    GB's Amy Hunt is looking to end the diamond league season on a high when she competes in the sprint finals after her success in the European Championships last month.

    St. Lucian sprinter and Olympic gold medallist in Paris Julien Alfred will be looking to continue her domination to add to her back-to-back Diamond League titles in 2024 and 2025.

    Mondo Duplantis is also competing in the pole vault, but the world record holder faces a serious challenge from Manolo Karalis. The world number two has pushed Duplantis to the limit in recent weeks, and is eyeing to end the season on a high.
