Pubill is back! Atleti's squad for a do-or-die night against Viking 📋

Atlético de Madrid have named their squad for tonight’s must-win showdown (21:00) at the Riyadh Air Metropolitano against Viking FK.

With European qualification hanging in the balance, Diego Pablo Simeone welcomes back Marc Pubill, but will have to cope with the significant absences of Lookman and Cuti Romero to keep their continental dream alive.

OneFootball Videos

Mixed injury news for Atlético

Pubill’s return provides a crucial boost to Cholo’s defensive options after overcoming his fitness issues.

However, Cuti Romero’s absence forces a defensive reshuffle in a match with no margin for error: both teams are level on four points, teetering on the edge of the Champions League play-off places.

Up front, with Lookman out, the burden of providing the firepower falls on big names such as Julián Álvarez, Sørloth, Kang-in Lee and Álex Baena.
