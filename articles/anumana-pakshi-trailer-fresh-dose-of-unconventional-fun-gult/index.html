Anumana Pakshi Trailer: Fresh Dose Of Unconventional Fun - Gulte

TheTools · 14.09.2026 11:19 · source · permalink

Article by Kumar Published on: 4:49 pm, 14 September 2026

Rag Mayur’s wholesome entertainer Anumana Pakshi, directed by Vimal Krishna of DJ Tillu fame, is presented by Chilaka Productions and produced by Rajiv Chilaka, Rajesh Jagtiani and Hirachand Dand. The film will arrive in theatres on September 18th. Siddhu Jonnalagadda has launched the theatrical trailer.
 
What happens when a person finds suspicion in literally everything? Anumana Pakshi takes that amusing idea and turns it into the foundation for a quirky comedy. Sunny is a man who cannot stop doubting the situations around him. His journey takes an interesting turn when he travels to Kashmir, with a police officer starts chasing him.
 
Rag Mayur gets a role that suits his comic strengths perfectly. His expressions, reactions and timing bring out the humour in Sunny’s suspicious behaviour. Merin Philip brings a fresh presence to the film and her pairing with Rag Mayur works well. Ajay adds another layer to the proceedings by playing a character of a police officer.
 
Rather than depending on conventional comedy, Vimal Krishna designed the humour around the protagonist’s peculiar personality. Sunil Kumar Nama captures the picturesque Kashmir locations effectively. Sricharan Pakala’s music complements the humorous tone. Chilaka Productions has maintained good production standards.
 
Anumana Pakshi stands out with a fresh dose of unconventional fun, bringing together humour, romance and mystery. The lively characters, amusing situations and intriguing setup have sparked considerable curiosity.
