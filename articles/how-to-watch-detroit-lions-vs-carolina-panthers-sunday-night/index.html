Lions vs. Panthers preview

The Lions are on a quest for redemption. Two years after being a playoff favorite, they missed the postseason last year, and Coach Dan Campbell's squad has put a lot of work into making sure that doesn't happen again. So far they've stumbled a little, dropping their first loss to the Bills and barely surviving their season-opener against New Orleans, but signs point to more consistency in their offense, and their pash rush has been among the most effective in the league so far. If their defense can further tighten up, they've got a very good shot at beating the Panthers on the road.

It'll be a battle, though, because despite their 1-2 record, including last week's loss to the Browns, the Panthers have a formidable offensive attack, particularly when it comes to passing. They've led the league in passing yards so far this season, and quarterback Bryce Young seems more and more comfortable as a pocket passer. If they can put together an air attack, and keep Young protected, they'll be hard to stop, and we could have a shootout on our hands.

Lions vs. Panthers players to watch

Jahmyr Gibbs, #0 of the Detroit Lions, during a game against the Chicago Bears at Soldier Field on January 4, 2026 in Chicago, Illinois.

Carolina's offense has been the Bryce Young show so far this season. He's already thrown for more than 900 yards and seven touchdowns, with two interceptions across three games. As the Panthers try to build on last season's NFC South title, all eyes will be on him to emerge as the leader the Panthers hoped for when they drafted him in 2023. 

Quarterback Jared Goff has put together a consistent passing game for the Lions so far, but when we think of Detroit, we think more about major playmakers who can blow a game wide open with a moment of athletic brilliance. The Lions have those all over the field, but because the Panthers rush defense has so far been the worst in the league, Jahmyr Gibbs is the one to watch this week. He's already got more than 300 yards and four touchdowns, and he could have a huge day in Charlotte on Sunday.
