Stafford goes off even without Puka in MNF blowout

TheTools · 22.09.2026 04:02 · source · permalink

    Stafford goes off even without Puka in MNF blowout

    Matthew Stafford

    LA Quarterback #9
    Player Stats

    Matthew Stafford completed 22-of-31 passes for 327 yards, four touchdowns, and one interception in the Rams’ 28-6, Week 2 win over the Giants.

    It was an inspiring effort from the 38-year-old quarterback while playing without his top option through the air in Puka Nacua. Stafford locked onto Davante Adams, the latter of whom turned 10 targets into eight receptions for 195 yards and two touchdowns, on his way to a 22nd career game with four touchdown passes. He also moved into sixth on the all-time passing touchdown leaderboard, overtaking Philip Rivers with the four-touchdown performance. The Rams shook off their travel woes in convincing fashion to move to 1-1 ahead of road meeting with the Broncos in Week 3, where Stafford will remain a locked-in QB1 in fantasy circles.
    - Mark Garcia
