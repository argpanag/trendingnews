James Gunn just cleared up one of the DCU's biggest lingering mysteries about one of its most iconic villains. The DC Studios head addressed whether the Joker exists in the new DC Universe during The Suicide Squad: Is It Canon? podcast on HBO Max.

We don't know if Joker exists in the DCU till now

James Gunn confirmed that the DC Universe has not yet established the Joker's existence. During the official Is It Canon? podcast for The Suicide Squad on HBO Max, Gunn addressed Harley Quinn's backstory and its ties to the Joker directly.

"We definitely haven't established the Joker yet in the DCU at all," Gunn said. He noted that Batman has appeared briefly in Creature Commandos, but the Joker remains absent. "We've established Batman, we've already seen that he exists, we've seen him in Creature Commandos for a glimpse, but we don't know if Joker exists yet," he added.

Gunn acknowledged that the Joker is central to Harley Quinn's origin. "Obviously, Joker is a part of Harley's past," he said. He emphasized that Harley's abusive relationship with the Joker defines her character. "She was born of this sort of abusive relationship where she was taken advantage of. And by a crazy guy who was a lot more evil than she was," Gunn explained. He called removing that backstory "difficult" for the character's identity.

The Suicide Squad film references Harley's "ex-boyfriend" without naming the Joker specifically. Gunn pointed this out during the podcast. "He's not mentioned by name," Gunn confirmed, adding that the relationship status remains undecided. "So, I would say TBD," he stated.

Gunn also tied Harley's future to actress Margot Robbie's decision. He expressed interest in using Harley Quinn in the DCU going forward. "Harley is a character I'm almost certainly going to use in the DCU," he said. However, he noted uncertainty about Robbie's interest in returning. "If Margot wants to play her, then I'll bend over backwards to explain that in the world," Gunn said.

He added that if Robbie declines, he may cast someone else without inheriting this film's continuity for Harley.
