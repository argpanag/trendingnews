City centre street closed after three men stabbed - live updates

TheTools · 20.09.2026 10:14 · source · permalink

Police have launched an investigation after three men were stabbed in Liverpool city centre. A huge cordon remains in police in parts of the city centre following the incident.

Police were called to Tithebarn Street in the city centre after three men were found with injuries and were taken to hospital.

Two of the men are in a stable condition and the third suffered minor injuries.

It is believed that the incident took place outside G-Bar on Eberle Street.

Detective Chief Inspector Lisa McNaughton said: “We understand the concern that such a serious incident will cause in the community. This is why it is so vital that anyone with information comes forward as a matter of urgency. It appears that some form of altercation broke out near to G-Bar, resulting in the three victims sustaining injuries.

“The suspected weapon has been recovered and will be forensically examined. We will do all we can to identify the person responsible and support the victims as this investigation progresses. If you saw or captured any part of this incident, let us know and we will take action."

We have a reporter at the scene who will be providing live updates.

'I feel like knife crime is getting worse'

Rob Swain, 28, who is from Essex and is up in Liverpool for a stag do, said: “I didn’t know there had been a stabbing. I’m staying nearby and have had a great time here. I feel like knife crime is getting worse. You have young people listening to music which glorifies it. You get people riding around with balaclavas on.”

'It makes you worried about going out in town'

A woman from the Wirral said: “It seems to be getting worse and worse. It makes you worried about going out in town. My daughter lives in Cheapside in the city centre. She’s been there for seven years and she says crime in town has got worse.”

'More education is needed'

A man who had travelled to Liverpool from Widnes today said: “It is a shock but it’s also a sign of the times. You can do stop and search but that stagnates people. More education is needed.”

Evidence at the bus stop being bagged up

Forensic officers are placing items from the bus stop into bags and removing them.

'It's a shock'

Another man said: “It is a shock. I live in town and feel quite safe - I go to the gym at 3am in the morning. It’s surprising to hear about this.”

People in the city centre reacting to stabbing news

Two men who live in a building around the corner also expressed their shock. One said: “What the hell. That’s really scary to hear. I’m just glad no-one’s dead.”

One woman said: “That’s mad. I live around the corner and didn’t hear a thing.”
