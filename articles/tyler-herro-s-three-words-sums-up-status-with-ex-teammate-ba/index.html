The Miami Heat welcomed the Milwaukee Bucks on Wednesday night in a matchup that might otherwise blend into the early-season NBA schedule.

But a summer feud involving a former Heat player and one of Miami’s current stars has added an unexpected layer of intrigue to the matchup. In July, tensions between Bam Adebayo and Tyler Herro reportedly boiled over in Las Vegas.

Adebayo and Herro were involved in a physical altercation at an NBA Summer League practice facility, during which the Heat center allegedly struck the Milwaukee Bucks guard in the head area.
