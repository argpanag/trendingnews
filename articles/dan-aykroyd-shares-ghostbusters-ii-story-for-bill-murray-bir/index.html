Dan Aykroyd Shares Ghostbusters II Story For Bill Murray Birthday

TheTools · 25.09.2026 01:56 · source · permalink

Actor and filmmaker Dan Aykroyd shared a story about taking an unauthorized late night police motorcycle ride through Central Park with co-star Bill Murray during the production of Ghostbusters II, while wishing Murray a happy belated birthday on social media on September 25, 2026.

Aykroyd sent the birthday message to his long-time collaborator on social media, referring to Murray as the Murricane and praising his contributions to comedy film history.

"The greatest comedy leading man of our time. So many adventures over the years," said Dan Aykroyd, co-creator of Ghostbusters.

Detailing one specific outing, Aykroyd recalled borrowing two equipped police vehicles from the movie set's motor pool during late-night filming in New York City.

"One night shooting GB2, we appropriated two fully equipped police motorcycles from the movie's motor pool and spent an hour chasing each other around a closed Central Park with full lights and sirens. No consequences. After all, we were Ghostbusters," said Aykroyd.

The lighthearted post follows a recent health update from Aykroyd regarding an emergency foot surgery for an infection. The recovery forced the creator to postpone his fall promotional tour for his upcoming book, Soulhider, pushing its official release to May 18, 2027.
