Hilary Duff shone on the stage in a custom Paul Aaron micro minidress featuring cutouts and fringe. The singer wore the showstopping ensemble for her Lucky Me tour concert in Sydney, Australia, on October 24. The lavender dress had a playful, retro-futuristic flair to it that absolutely echoed her popstar persona.

Hilary Duff's Paul Aaron micro minidress has cutouts and fringe throughout for Australia show

Hilary Duff wore a custom dazzling, celestial-inspired micro minidress by Paul Aaron. The shimmering dress featured crochet stars in dark metallic thread and 20,000 hand-woven lavender glass beads.

The asymmetrical halter bodice featured geometric cutouts across the chest and waist, blending edgy structure with a glittering, galaxy-themed aesthetic.

Extending from the dress was a floor-sweeping veil of metallic fringe that flowed downward in fluid, shimmering strands. This long fringe veil created captivating movement and drama as she performed under the bright stage lights.

Meanwhile, the back of the dress featured a big cutout that revealed her upper back, breaking up the visual line.

Duff's silver Gianvito Rossi boots peeked through the floor-length fringe of her outfit. The reflective surface of the shoes perfectly mirrored the silver hues in the beaded dress, tying the whole high-energy stage look together.

They also elongated her frame while she performed on stage. Styled by Neelo Boory, she wore a dainty sparkling bracelet on one wrist, with a few rings adorning her hands.

The "We Don't Talk" singer's beauty styling also complemented the Y2K-infused glamour. She wore her voluminous honey-blonde hair in loose waves that fell over her shoulders. Meanwhile, her makeup was minimal, featuring a glossy lip, black eyeliner, and fluttery lashes.

Previously, Duff ended the USA leg of her Lucky Me tour in a custom Paul Aaron shooting star beaded gown. It also featured thousands of hand-woven glass beads, cutouts in the torso, and a fringe veil that added dynamic movement.
