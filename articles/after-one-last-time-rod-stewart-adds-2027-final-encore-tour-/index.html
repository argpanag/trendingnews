After ‘One Last Time,’ Rod Stewart adds 2027 ‘Final Encore’ tour: How to secure tickets

TheTools · 29.09.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

If you purchase a product through a link on our site, we may receive compensation.

Rod Stewart has said goodbye to touring before. This time, he says he means it.

The 81-year-old singer announced a 21-date U.K. and European tour called “The Final Encore,” which he says will close out a touring career stretching back more than six decades.

“The time has come to say farewell to life on the road. After more than six decades, The Final Encore will most definitely be my last,” Stewart said in a statement. “I’ve been singing the music I love since I was 19 years old, and I’ve had the privilege of taking it around the world to all of you. It has given me a life beyond anything I could ever have imagined, and I hope I’ve given you some fond memories in return.”

The run begins April 23 at 3Arena in Dublin, moves through arenas in the U.K. and Europe, including The O2 in London, Ziggo Dome in Amsterdam and Uber Arena in Berlin and wraps July 24 at ZAG Arena in Hanover, Germany.

U.K. dates include stops in Belfast, Glasgow, Birmingham, Manchester and London before the extensive European leg. Howard Jones will support Stewart on all six U.K. and Ireland dates.

Didn’t he already do a farewell tour?

He did, and it’s still technically running. Stewart’s ”One Last Time" tour has carried him through North America and other markets over the past several years, and in 2026 he added 15 more U.S. dates to it.

That tour hit serious turbulence. Two June 2026 shows at Red Rocks Amphitheatre were postponed to September 2027 after doctors found significant inflammation and strain in Stewart’s vocal folds and ordered strict rest. During the tour, he also paused a Utah concert to use an oxygen mask after nearly fainting onstage.

In August, he canceled the remaining U.S. and Mexico dates after undergoing a coronary stent procedure, saying he was recovering well but had been told to take four weeks off from performing. His Las Vegas residency was another casualty of the same coronary stent procedure. Earlier in 2025, flu, COVID, strep throat and vocal rest had already forced cancellations on the same tour.

Stewart has promised ”one hell of a show" for “The Final Encore,” closing out a career that began in the early 1960s.

Planning a trip around Rod Stewart’s ‘The Final Encore’ tour?

With stops spanning Dublin to Kraków, many fans are turning this into a full trip.

If you need a place to stay, Hotels.com and Expedia are worth checking first. Both let you compare rates near each venue and often bundle in flexible cancellation options, which is handy if travel plans shift. If you’re traveling with a group, Airbnb is a solid alternative, especially in cities like Amsterdam, Prague or Budapest, where apartment-style stays can be cheaper and roomier than a hotel room.
