FairPrice's S$5 Return Voucher Promotion Starts Today for Every S$50 Spent

TheTools · 29.10.2026 02:45 · source · permalink
  • News

FairPrice’s return voucher promotion starts today (29 October), giving shoppers a S$5 voucher for every S$50 in qualifying spending on a single receipt.

The promotion runs until 9 November 2026, with each transaction capped at four vouchers worth S$20 altogether. Vouchers are available while stocks last.

Where You Can Earn and Use the Vouchers

The offer covers FairPrice, FairPrice Finest, FairPrice Xtra and Unity stores. Any form of payment qualifies, subject to the spending exclusions.

For example, S$100 in qualifying nett spending earns two vouchers, while S$200 earns the maximum four.

There is no minimum spend when redeeming the vouchers, and multiple physical vouchers can be used in one transaction.

However, vouchers only become valid the day after issuance. A paper voucher issued today can therefore be used from 30 October. All vouchers expire on 30 November 2026, with no extensions allowed.

They cannot be used for FairPrice online purchases or combined with another discount voucher.

Digital Vouchers Have Different Checkout Rules

Eligible Scan & Go and Smart Cart transactions, plus digital pre-orders collected in-store by 9 November, qualify for digital vouchers. These will reach the app’s Voucher Wallet within four working days after the transaction.

Scan & Go vouchers can only be redeemed through Scan & Go, with a limit of seven per transaction.

Smart Cart vouchers can be used through Smart Cart, self-checkout or staffed counters. Link Membership must be added to the transaction, and up to seven vouchers will be automatically applied, though shoppers can remove those they do not wish to use.

Paper vouchers can only be redeemed at self-checkout or staffed counters.

Some Purchases Do Not Qualify

Spending exclusions include beverage container deposits, cigarettes, lottery products, FairPrice Group gift vouchers and gift cards, carrier bags, membership and consultation fees, infant milk powder for babies aged up to 12 months, medicines, and donation products or bundles.

Redemption also carries product exclusions, including deposits, cigarettes, lottery products, gift cards, infant milk powder and medicines. Additional exclusions may apply.

FairPrice Group said the promotion aims to help households manage everyday expenses and save on preparations for Deepavali and Christmas, 8world reported.
