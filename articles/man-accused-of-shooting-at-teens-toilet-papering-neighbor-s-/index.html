Man accused of shooting at teens toilet papering neighbor’s house in prank gone wrong

TheTools · 15.10.2026 04:24 · source · permalink

ARNAUDVILLE, La. (KATC/Gray News) - A Louisiana man is facing multiple charges after authorities say he shot at a group of high school students during a toilet-papering prank gone wrong.

Travis Frederick, 71, was arrested and booked on charges of attempted manslaughter, aggravated assault with a firearm and aggravated criminal damage to property. His bond has not yet been set.

St. Martin Parish Sheriff Becket Breaux said deputies responded to a shooting on a street right outside Arnaudville just before midnight Monday. Several high school students had been in the area throwing toilet paper on neighborhood homes as part of a homecoming prank, according to deputies.

Frederick, who lives next door to one of the targeted homes, went outside with a gun and fired a shot toward at least five of the students, Breaux said.

Breaux added that as the students tried to leave in a vehicle, Frederick fired a second shot, striking the car.

No injuries were reported, but Breaux said the shooting was an overreaction that could have turned deadly.

“You use deadly force on someone toilet papering someone’s house. That’s a little bit overboard, and basically, you’re sending someone to a death sentence for toilet papering,” Breaux said.

Breaux noted the case highlights how quickly a neighborhood prank escalated into a criminal investigation. He urges the public not to take the law into their own hands.

”You elected me to be your sheriff to enforce the law, enforce public safety, and we have 240 deputies here who do the same thing. Let them earn their money, call them and let us go do our job."
