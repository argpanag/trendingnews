Cucurella: “Güler flies under the radar but we’re a different team when he plays”

TheTools · 10.11.2026 11:06 · source · permalink
  • Real Madrid CF: News

Real Madrid defender Marc Cucurella talked to Cadena COPE and shared some thoughts about the team’s current form all while praising teammate Arda Güler.

When asked about which one of his teammates has impressed him the most, the defender praised the Turkish playmaker.

“I’m going to tell you about one that goes unnoticed, which is Arda Güler. When he plays, we are a different team. He has that baby face, but he is an impressive player and has a lot of quality,” he said.

Cucurella has a point, as Arda Güler has arguably been the team’s best player so far this season and one of the main reasons why Real Madrid put together an eight-game winning streak this past month. Arda has established himself as a crucial player for Mourinho and it seems clear that he will be an undisputed starter.
