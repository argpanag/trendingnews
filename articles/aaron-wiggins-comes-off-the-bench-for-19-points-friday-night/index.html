Aaron Wiggins comes off the bench for 19 points Friday night

TheTools · 24.10.2026 14:16 · source · permalink

by Nate Miller | Hawks Correspondent | Sat, Oct 24th 2026, 10:16am EDT

Aaron Wiggins scored 19 points (7-9 FG, 3-5 3PT, 2-4 FT) and came up with two steals in Friday's 128-81 win over the Hornets.

Fantasy Impact:

Wiggins has gotten off to a great start this season, and he certainly looks like the Hawks' top man off the bench. Wiggins has scored 16 and 19 points in the first two games of the campaign, shooting 66.7% from the field and 6-of-10 from long range.
