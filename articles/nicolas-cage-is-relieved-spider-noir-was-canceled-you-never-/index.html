Nicolas Cage is relieved ‘Spider-Noir’ was canceled: “You never wanna get stuck repeating a character”

TheTools · 06.11.2026 23:05 · source · permalink

The actor believes the Ben Reilly story works best as a single season.

During a recent red carpet appearance for his upcoming sports comedy ‘Madden,’ Nicolas Cage discussed the cancellation of ‘Spider-Noir’, the live-action superhero series in which he starred as Ben Reilly.

Speaking with IGN, the actor explained that he prefers projects with a defined beginning and ending over long-running commitments that can lead to repetitive character dynamics. “I meant it when I said what I said. It’s a perfect season,” said Cage. “You never wanna get stuck repeating a character and wear out your welcome. Ben Riley is so idiosyncratic. I was doing something that was pretty high-risk, tightrope stuff — if you do that for season 2, season 3, you could really wear out your welcome.

“So when they didn’t pick it up, I was like, ‘Whew, thank you!’ Because now I’m doing ‘True Detective’, and that’s only one season. It’s better that way, you know? I liked it when you would see Rock Hudson and Macmillan and it’s one season. I didn’t wanna keep repeating it. I don’t like repeating myself. It’s perfect, and I love that character. I love that season, and it will always be there and always be fresh.”

The obstacle in the Cage - Amazon relationship

His relationship with Amazon also faced another obstacle involving the use of generative AI. Cage reportedly refused to sign a waiver that would have allowed the production to use AI to replicate his performance, a decision that put him at odds with the studio. The actor has been vocal about protecting traditional performance and the work that goes into creating a character.
