Xabi Alonso defends Wesley Fofana after jeering during Chelsea’s loss to Forest

TheTools · 28.11.2026 01:23 · source · permalink

Chelsea boss Xabi Alonso has vowed to support Wesley Fofana after he was abused by supporters during the 3-2 defeat to Nottingham Forest.

The defender, who has been singled out for poor performances as the Blues slumped to a third successive loss, has angered fans after an unauthorised interview French YouTuber Zack Nani, where he appeared to joke about the club’s Carabao Cup exit to Liverpool.

He apologised for the interview and Alonso said the matter was closed, but visiting supporters at the City Ground were less forgiving on a night which saw him substituted after he gave away a penalty.

Morgan Gibbs-White scored from the spot to make it 2-0, after Igor Jesus’ brilliant opener, and the England international ensured Forest’s three points with a second later on.

Emmanuel Emegha scored twice for Chelsea to give the scoreline a different complexion, but they did not deserve anything out of the game.

“Well, we have to stick together, and everyone that is in the changing room, everyone in the team is needed,” he said.

“We need to help, and we need to go through these tough moments all together, and we don’t leave anyone apart.

“We need to support everyone. That is because we need everyone, and we need the fans.

“We need all the players. We need to stick together because if not, things will get more complicated, and that’s what we want to do.”

Asked whether the Chelsea fans’ reaction, where Fofana was booed throughout and subjected to derogatory chants, made it more difficult to pick him going forward, Alonso added: “Well, I’m thinking right after the game and for the next games, we will see.”

Fofana’s situation is just one of a growing list of issues for Alonso to deal with as they slide down the table.

Jesus’ brilliant opening goal, an acrobatic volley into the top corner, put Forest ahead and they doubled the lead when Gibbs-White struck from the spot.

Emegha got Chelsea back into it, with their first shot on target, but the hosts restored their two-goal lead when Gibbs-White latched on to Xaver Schlager’s fine pass.

Emegha’s late second set up a grandstand finish but Forest held on for a deserved three points.

Forest claimed back-to-back home wins after beating Manchester City last time out at the City Ground and this performance was an indication that they are moving in the right direction under Oliver Glasner.

But the Austrian is not getting carried away.

“It looks like we are progressing, and this is what we all want to see,” boss Glasner said.

“But also, I said last time it was the first swallow, and now it’s the second swallow, and I think everybody could feel it tonight, we are far away from summer!

“So staying humble, take this feeling of the win and the confidence and the belief, but staying humble and prepare well for Hull because we have three-game week before we face Brighton.

“After the three-game week we can summarise.”
