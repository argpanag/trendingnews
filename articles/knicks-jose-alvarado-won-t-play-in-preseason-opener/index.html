Knicks' Jose Alvarado: Won't play in preseason opener

TheTools · 06.10.2026 00:41 · source · permalink

Alvarado (knee) won't play in Monday's preseason game against the 76ers, Steve Popper of Newsday reports.

Alvarado is dealing with a left knee contusion and will be held out of the Knicks' preseason opener. His next chance to play during the preseason will come Thursday against the Wizards.
