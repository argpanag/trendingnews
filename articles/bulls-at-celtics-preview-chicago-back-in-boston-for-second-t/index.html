Bulls at Celtics preview: Chicago back in Boston for second time, this time in NBA Cup play - The Boston Globe

TheTools · 31.10.2026 00:37 · source · permalink

The Bulls are back in town.

The Celtics seek their third win at home in five days when Chicago returns to TD Garden, which will have a different look for games that are part of the NBA Cup. Friday’s game is the in-season tournament opener for both teams.

Boston blew out the Bulls, 134-99, on Monday behind a memorable shooting performance from Payton Pritchard.

Jayson Tatum added a signature highlight, beating the buzzer in overtime to clip the Nets, 125-124, on Tuesday. Tatum finished with 40 points, eight assists, and five rebounds.

“That was a hell of a feeling, just kind of getting back to doing things that I used to do before I was injured,” Tatum said. “And sharing that moment with my teammates on the court, seeing them rally and be excited for me.”

Derrick White elicited MVP chants from the Garden faithful in the win against Brooklyn. He finished with 16 points, seven assists, and five rebounds.

“D-White is my favorite teammate, one of my favorite people,” Tatum said. “That’s what I love about this city and the fans here. They know what winning basketball is and they know when guys are contributing and doing spectacular things or maybe all the little things ... I think it’s really cool and much deserved.”

Caleb Wilson, the fourth pick of the 2026 NBA Draft, is one of three rookies this season averaging at least 20 points. ... “Obviously, I have a lot of expectations put on [me] from other people, and I’m just trying to manage that and play to the best of my ability every game,” Wilson said. “I feel every game I’ve done well in some aspect.
