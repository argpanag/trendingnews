Zhipu rolls out GLM-5.4 with vision and million-token context · TechNode

TheTools · 23.11.2026 06:51 · source · permalink

Zhipu has made GLM-5.4 available through its Coding Plan subscription service, adding visual understanding and stronger capabilities across coding-related tasks. The model appeared in the service’s selection menu on Monday, where it is listed with vision support and a one-million-token context window.

A member of Zhipu’s operations team said the company had been scaling both its model size and its Loop Transformer approach over the past three months. Subscribers on V2, V3 and team plans have received a 50% increase in their GLM-5.4 usage allowance and can access it by setting the model name to “glm-5.4.”

The rollout puts the new model directly into Zhipu’s developer subscription product. The company had not yet published a full technical introduction detailing its architecture, parameter count or benchmark results at the time of the report. [IT Home, in Chinese]
