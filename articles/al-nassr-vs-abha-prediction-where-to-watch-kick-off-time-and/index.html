Al-Nassr vs Abha - Prediction, where to watch, kick-off time and line-ups 09-09-2026

TheTools · 09.09.2026 20:03 · source · permalink

Al-Nassr vs. Abha meet in round 6 of the Saudi Arabia Pro League. The match takes place this Wednesday, 09 September 2026, with kickoff scheduled for 23:00. Al-Nassr will be looking to capitalise on their home advantage and continue their strong start to the season.

The hosts are clear favourites, having won seven of the last ten encounters between the two sides, including an 8-0 thrashing in their most recent meeting. Abha, on the other hand, have struggled to find their form and face a difficult challenge on the road.

Keep reading to discover our betting tips and analysis for this exciting encounter.

Al-Nassr vs. Abha - Head-to-head record

The historical record between Al-Nassr and Abha reveals a clear pattern of dominance for the home side. In their last ten meetings, Al-Nassr secured seven victories, while Abha managed only one win, with two matches concluding in a draw. This head-to-head analysis highlights Al-Nassr’s consistent superiority over Abha.

Recent history further emphasizes Al-Nassr’s overwhelming strength. The most recent Al-Nassr vs. Abha fixture saw Al-Nassr achieve a stunning 8-0 away victory, demonstrating their potent attacking capability against this opponent.

However, Abha did manage to secure a 2-2 draw in their last away game against Al-Nassr. This result might offer the visitors a glimmer of hope, proving they can occasionally trouble Al-Nassr on the road. Nevertheless, the overall head-to-head record strongly suggests an Al-Nassr win in this upcoming encounter.

Al-Nassr - Looking to bounce back

Let’s take a closer look at Al-Nassr’s current situation. After a strong winning streak was surprisingly ended, we will analyse the team’s momentum and recent performances. We will also look at how they might approach this upcoming test and the possible Al-Nassr vs. Abha line-ups for this important fixture.

Recent form

Al-Nassr sits second in the Saudi Arabia Pro League table, having collected an impressive 12 points from five matches. Their strong start, featuring four consecutive league victories, was recently halted by a narrow 2-1 defeat against Al-Ittihad FC. Returning home, they will be determined to bounce back immediately.

The team’s form in front of their own fans has been perfect in the league, with two wins from two games. They have been particularly effective in attack, with stars like Cristiano Ronaldo and Sadio Mané leading the line. Midfielder Ângelo is also in fine form, having already netted three goals this season.

This attacking depth makes them a formidable opponent, especially at home where they’ve scored five and conceded just once in the league. With no significant injuries reported, the manager has a full squad available. Their strong home record and star-studded lineup make them clear favourites in the Al-Nassr vs. Abha fixture.

Abha - Struggling for form

Let’s review Abha’s current situation. We will analyse the team’s momentum, their expectations and standout players for the trip to face Al-Nassr.

Recent form

Abha are enduring a difficult start to the Saudi Arabia Pro League campaign, sitting bottom of the table in 18th position. The team is still searching for its first win after five rounds, having managed just a single point from one draw and four defeats. Their struggles are clear, with only four goals scored and a worrying eleven conceded so far.

The team’s form on the road is particularly alarming. Abha have lost both away league fixtures this season, conceding seven goals in the process. Recent defeats against Al-Ettifaq and Al-Fayha have extended their winless streak, highlighting a lack of confidence when playing away from their home ground. This poor run puts them under immense pressure.

With no injury data available, Abha faces an uphill battle in this fixture. The attack has lacked a cutting edge, as top scorers M. Batshuayi-Atunga and A. Gory each have only one goal. Given their defensive frailties and limited firepower, it is difficult to see them causing an upset against a stronger opponent.
