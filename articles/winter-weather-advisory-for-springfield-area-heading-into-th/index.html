Winter Weather Advisory for Springfield area heading into Thanksgiving

TheTools · 24.11.2026 22:51 · source · permalink
  • Print

UPDATED 4 a.m. Wednesday

Thanksgiving travelers across central Illinois are facing a messy holiday commute as a winter storm moves into the region starting Wednesday afternoon.

The National Weather Service has placed portions of central Illinois under a Winter Weather Advisory, effective from noon Wednesday through 6 p.m. on Thanksgiving Day.

Forecasters say a quick-hitting system will arrive. Forecast models as of Wednesday morning have downgraded the expected snowfall totals in the Springfield area.

Total snow accumulations of 1 to 3 inches from Canton to Petersburg to Springfield.

Plan on slippery road conditions to develop during the afternoon and continue tonight into Thursday morning. The hazardous conditions may impact the Wednesday evening and Thursday morning commutes.

Higher snowfall amounts are possible in some locations, such as west-central Illinois. The Jacksonville area and counties to the west and south are under a Winter Storm Warning and could see 3 to 6 inches or more.

The National Weather Service said most accumulations will be on grassy and elevated surfaces. Heavier bands of snow could overcome warmer pavement and cause more travel difficulties. A potential for rain/snow mixture brings uncertainties in snowfall amounts.

If driving, you are advised to slow down and leave plenty of room between your vehicle and others.

The Springfield area forecast:

Wednesday
A chance of rain and snow between noon and 3pm, then snow. High near 39. Northeast wind 7 to 11 mph, with gusts as high as 20 mph. Chance of precipitation is 90%. Little or no snow accumulation expected.

Wednesday Night
Snow before 7pm, then rain and snow. Low around 32. North wind 10 to 13 mph, with gusts as high as 20 mph. Chance of precipitation is 100%. New snow accumulation of less than a half inch possible.

Thanksgiving Day
Rain and snow, becoming all rain after 8am. High near 41. North northeast wind 13 to 15 mph, with gusts as high as 24 mph. Chance of precipitation is 100%. New snow accumulation of less than a half inch possible.

Thursday Night
Rain, mainly before midnight. Low around 32. Northwest wind 14 to 16 mph, with gusts as high as 25 mph. Chance of precipitation is 80%. New precipitation amounts between a tenth and quarter of an inch possible.

Friday
Partly sunny, with a high near 43. West northwest wind 7 to 14 mph, with gusts as high as 21 mph.
