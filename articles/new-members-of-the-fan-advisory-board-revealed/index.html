New members of the Fan Advisory Board revealed

TheTools · 23.09.2026 16:29 · source · permalink

Chelsea Football Club is pleased to announce the newest members of the Fan Advisory Board (FAB). Following a formal selection process, three new members of the FAB have been selected.

We are pleased to welcome Darren Williams, Peter Trenter and Jon Kelly to the FAB for the first time. We also welcome back Arjun Pillai who was successfully reselected to his existing position. All four have been appointed for two seasons.

We received a high volume of applications, and each candidate was interviewed by a panel formed of independent representatives from the supporter base, the club board and the Football Supporters’ Association.

Our newest members will be joining our elected representatives, Neil Beard, Tracey Chapman and Anna Phillips, who all hold the position of supporter advisors to the board.

For the 2026/27 season, the FAB will continue to meet with the club on a regular basis, providing advice and guidance on strategic initiatives and the impact on fan sentiment.

Michael Cornall, head of supporter relations, said: ‘We look forward to welcoming Jon, Darren and Peter to the Fan Advisory Board.

‘It is vital that the FAB is representative of our supporter base. We are delighted to welcome our newest members and look forward to their range of perspectives contributing to and enriching the discussions we have as a group.’

Short biographies of our newest members can be read below:

Peter Trenter

Peter’s first game at Chelsea was in 1968. He is a senior concession home and away season ticket holder as well as chair of the Chelsea Supporters Group. He is looking forward to joining the FAB and representing Chelsea supporters when important decisions are proposed that will affect our passionate fan base.

Darren Williams

Darren is a military veteran and lifelong Chelsea fan. After many years of being a Chelsea member, Darren finally achieved his goal and is an ambulant season ticket holder for the 2026/27 season. He proudly follows Chelsea regularly and is excited by his role on the FAB while continuing to support the club home and away.

Jon Kelly

Jon is an avid Chelsea supporter and a Club Chelsea season ticket holder who regularly attends home and away matches with his three sons. A retired finance professional with over 20 years' industry experience, Jon is committed to ensuring fans' voices genuinely provide input on decisions across the club.

Arjun Pillai

Arjun has been reappointed for a further two years on the FAB. He attended his first match at the age of six and has been a season ticket holder for nearly 20 years as well as a Chelsea Pitch Owners shareholder. Arjun has worked in investment banking in both New York and London. He is also chair of the Growth Board at Bloomsbury Football Foundation and is a graduate from the University of Oxford.

The newest members of the FAB are in place of Zane Elmasri, Emily Kraftman and Mark Meehan. We thank all three for their dedication and commitment to the club.
