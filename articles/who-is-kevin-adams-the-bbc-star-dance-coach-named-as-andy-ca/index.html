Who is Kevin Adams? The BBC star dance coach named as Andy Carroll attacker

TheTools · 04.10.2026 10:44 · source · permalink

The man who sexually attacker footballer Andy Carroll has been named. But what do we know about his attacker, Kevin Adams?

Carroll disclosed that BBC personality Kevin Adams was the man who sexually assaulted him in 2021 - an incident he opened up about in his new book.

Until now, the man convicted of the assault had remained unnamed but Carroll made the confirmation following Adams' identification in publicly available court documents.

Prior to the book's publication, Carroll had stated that only a handful of close family and friends were aware of the attack, and he had deliberately withheld the perpetrator's name from the memoir. However, he has now confirmed to The Sun that his attacker was Kevin Adams, who served as a dance coach on the BBC's Fame Academy in 2002.

Who is Kevin Adams?

Adams, now 59, rose to fame in the noughties when he was the star coach on BBC's popular new reality show Fame Academy.

Throughout his career, Adams reportedly choreographed for some of the biggest names in music including Mariah Carey, The Spice Girls, Victoria Beckham, Cher, Boyzone, Louise Redknapp and Dannii Minogue.

Adams also worked on GMTV as a fitness instructor in Inch Loss Island and Inch Loss Beach and went on to present The Mint on ITV1, ITV2 and ITV Play.

What was Adams convicted of?

Adams assaulted Carroll as he slept naked. As the assault unfolded, Carroll woke up and threw Adams out, knocking him unconscious when he tried to return. Adams then told the police that he had been hit for no reason.

Adams was convicted at Chelmsford Crown Court in 2023, where he was jailed for 20 months and placed on the sex offenders register. He was also banned from visiting the village in which Carroll lives.

Upon conviction, the judge told him: "In your narrative, you were the put-upon reluctant friend doing no more than helping out. The end of your unbelievable tale was that you were the victim of the most serious sexual assaults yourself.

"However incredible your account, it seems to me that you’ve actually started to believe your own lies."

What has Andy Carroll said about the assault?

In his book, Carroll revealed: "Something happened to me a few years ago that was so traumatic I only decided to include it in this book at the very last minute. I was sexually assaulted; there I said it. Scary to even think about, and only a few people knew about it before now."
