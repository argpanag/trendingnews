Ajay Devgn returns as the iconic Vijay Salgaonkar in Drishyam: The Conclusion. The makers have created a buzz among the fans by finally unveiling the trailer for Drishyam 3 on Wednesday, September 9.

Jaideep Ahlawat as SP Crime Branch Rajveer Singh Tomar, a formidable new force determined to expose the truth once and for all, stands squarely in the way of Ajay Devgn's return as the legendary Vijay Salgaonkar.

Drishyam: The Conclusion continues where Drishyam 2 left off, taking the franchise into exciting new territory with a whole new plot and storyline.

Also Read: Drishyam The Conclusion first look: Ajay Devgn returns as Vijay Salgaonkar, Jaideep Ahlawat's first look as SP Rajvir Singh Tomar out

The trailer starts with Tabu asking of Vijay writes a sequel, what it would be. It then shows Shriya Saran and Mrunal Jadhav throwing ornaments into the fire, with Ajay Devgn’s worried voice in the background asking someone what evidence they have.

Vijay encounters one of his most persevering obstacles to date as Rajveer Singh Tomar assumes control of the seemingly insurmountable case. While Tabu, Shriya Saran, and Rajat Kapoor return to a world where the past won't go away, Prakash Raj pushes the stakes even higher.

Tabu then vows to take revenge rather than justice. The trailer then shows Prakash Raj with the Drishyam CD in his hands and says, “Anpadh hai, bewakuf nahi. Ise halke me lene ki galti main nahi karunga”

The trailer promises an intense struggle of cunning in which every move might alter everything, with long-forgotten secrets coming to light, unsolved mysteries drawing near, and new forces joining the game.

The makers returned Drishyam The Conclusion to Goa, the region known for Vijay Salgaonkar and his legendary tale, to celebrate the franchise's scope, fandom, and history.

Before culminating in a spectacular trailer unveiling in Panaji with the actors and producers, the trailer premiere turned into an immersive Drishyam experience that took the media and influencers through iconic locales from the films, reliving historic events and entering Vijay Salgaonkar's universe.

Also Read: Ajay Devgn's latest installment in Drishyam franchise titled Drishyam: The Conclusion, set to release on October 2

Drishyam: The Conclusion, directed by Abhishek Pathak, features a formidable cast that includes Ajay Devgn, Tabu, Shriya Saran, Rajat Kapoor, Jaideep Ahlawat & Prakash Raj, Kamlesh Sawant, Ishita Dutta, and Mrunal Jadhav.

Aamil Keeyan Khan, Parveez Shaikh, and Abhishek Pathak are the writers and directors of the Panorama Studios production, which is presented by Star Studio18. The latest instalment is produced by Alok Jain, Ajit Andhare, Kumar Mangat Pathak, and Abhishek Pathak.

Drishyam: The Conclusion is scheduled to open in theatres on October 2, 2026.
