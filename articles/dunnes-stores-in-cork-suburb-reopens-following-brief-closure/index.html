Dunnes Stores in Cork suburb reopens following brief closure this morning

TheTools · 05.10.2026 14:31 · source · permalink

A Dunnes Stores representative said the Bishopstown store has reopened its doors and will remain open until 10pm as normal. Picture: Dan Linehan

The Dunnes Stores outlet in Bishopstown has reopened to customers following a brief closure this morning.

It is understood the shop was closed for a number of hours on Monday due to an internal system error.

However, despite efforts to contact Dunnes Stores HQ in Dublin, a cause for the closure has not been confirmed.

It has been reported that the store experienced complications that impacted the operation of tills within the shop.

Speaking to The Echo, a representative at the Bishopstown store confirmed that the closure had occurred.

The representative said the shop has since reopened and will remain open until 10pm as normal.

They could not provide further details on the closure.
