Manchester United news: Contract situation 'not important now - it's irrelevant' says Bruno Fernandes

TheTools · 25.11.2026 22:15 · source · permalink
  • Published
    25 November 2026, 10:30 GMT

Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes says he is "more worried about getting results" than his contract situation.

The midfielder is out of contract at the end of the season, though the club have the option to trigger a 12-month extension until 2028.

Before United's Champions League game at Sporting on Wednesday night (20:00 GMT), he was asked what conditions have to be in place for him to sign "that long-term deal" at Old Trafford.

He responded: "For us to start winning games, and then we can start speaking about contract. That's not important now, it's irrelevant.

"Obviously I know it's my future and I shouldn't probably look into that way because I haven't got much [time] left in my contract, but I'm not really worried about that.

"I'm more worried about getting results, trying to bring the club back in a position that we can be proud of, and we're not there at the moment. We're going to have time to speak about that.

"As I said, we had conversations. The conversations stopped in the moment that we thought that was not as important at the moment because results and performances have not been at the level we should be. So we're going to have time to think about that."
