Zoe Saldana Pushes Taylor Sheridan to Change Lioness Finale

TheTools · 12.10.2026 22:51 · source · permalink

Zoe Saldana persuaded Lioness creator Taylor Sheridan to make a major change to the Season 3 finale, allowing Neal McNamara to leave his wife, Joe, after escalating threats put their family at risk. In the episode The Unraveling, Joe returns home following a kidnapping and rescue, only to discover that Neal and their children have moved out.

The episode ends with a For Sale sign in the family's front yard, marking a significant rupture in Joe's marriage. Neal, played by Dave Annable, leaves with their children, Kate, played by Hannah Love Lanier, and Charlie, played by Celestina Harris, after Joe's abduction and her refusal to accept a desk job deepen existing tensions in their relationship.

In an interview with Entertainment Weekly, Saldana explained why she believed Neal needed to prioritize the children's safety over his marriage. She said she repeatedly contacted Sheridan while he was writing the season to argue that the character had a compelling reason to leave.

"I just kept calling Taylor as he was writing this season and sharing with him, 'I don't know why he would stay ... If violence and threats approach the door of our home because of what my partner does, I would have to put my children first and leave,'" Saldana said. "Taylor and I would have these little micro-disagreements about it.

He believes in their love and that their love can withhold and survive everything. And that may very well be the case. I am grateful to Taylor that he gave Neal the f***ing strength to leave her in order for him to make a point."

Saldana's comments highlight a creative disagreement over how far the series should test Joe and Neal's relationship. While Sheridan believed their love could withstand extreme circumstances, Saldana argued that the danger created by Joe's work made Neal's departure a credible response.

The future of the couple's relationship remains uncertain, as Paramount+ has not announced a fourth season of Lioness. Saldana has nevertheless expressed interest in continuing their story if the series returns.

In the same Entertainment Weekly interview, Saldana described Joe and Neal as partners whose connection should not necessarily end with their separation. She said she hoped a potential fourth season would explore what happens to their relationship after the finale.

"I'm selfish, and if we are able to come back and do a Season 4, I wouldn't want their story to just have ended like that. Because these are two people who really found each other. I feel that Joe and Neal are like doves, they mate for life," Saldana said.

Paramount+ has yet to confirm Season 4, leaving the roles of Neal and the children in any future episodes unresolved.
