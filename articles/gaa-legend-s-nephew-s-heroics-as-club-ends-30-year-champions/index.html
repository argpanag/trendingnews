GAA legend's nephew's heroics as club ends 30-year Championship wait

TheTools · 07.10.2026 10:45 · source · permalink

Rian Stafford, the nephew of the legendary Brian Stafford, proved that talent runs in the family as he kicked a remarkable 1-16 to help his club win their first adult championship title for 30 years.

The nephew of the 1987 footballer of the year was in magnificent form over the weekend, playing the key role as his Kilmainhamwood bested Kilmainham on a score of 1-26 to 1-11 as they secured promotion from Junior B to Junior A.

That triumph marked Kilmainhamwood's first adult men's title for 30 years, breaking a duck that stretches back to 1996, when they won the senior championship title.

Since then, Kilmainhamwood lost their senior status and fell through the ranks to the Junior grade, with last weekend's triumph a welcome boost to the rural club.

Former U20 county star Rian was the star last weekend, much like how his uncle was the star at club and county level for many years in the 1980's and 1990's, which saw Brian win a glut of All-Stars and two All-Ireland winning medals.
