“People are struggling to buy gas”: Heidi Klum slammed online over surprising son with Ferrari F430 for his 21st birthday in viral video - AOL

TheTools · 13.09.2026 11:24 · source · permalink

Heidi Klum has made headlines recently, after going all out for her son Henry's twenty-first birthday. The supermodel, fifty-three years old, surprised the birthday boy with a red Ferrari F430 on Saturday, Sept. 12, a vehicle that retails for well over six figures. Klum shared a video of the moment on Instagram, capturing Henry walking outside alongside his stepfather, Tom Kaulitz, completely unaware of what was waiting for him.

"I can't believe it is that time. You've been waiting for so many years,"
