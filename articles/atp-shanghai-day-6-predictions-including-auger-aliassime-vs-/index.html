ATP Shanghai Day 6 Predictions Including Auger-Aliassime vs Carreno Busta

TheTools · 11.10.2026 18:47 · source · permalink

The ATP Rolex Shanghai Masters is into Day 6 and Round of 32 is set to complete on what promises to be an enthralling day of tennis. The draw is whittling down nicely and as ever, we preview and predict the matches scheduled for you.

ATP Shanghai Day 6 Predictions

Arthur Fils vs Botic van de Zandschulp

Head-to-head – First Meeting

Arthur Fils comes into this matchup in good form and should have plenty of confidence in his game. Botic van de Zandschulp is capable of troubling top players when he finds his rhythm, but his level has been inconsistent recently. Fils has the greater firepower and should look to dictate the rallies from the baseline. If he maintains his current level, the Frenchman should have enough to advance.
Prediction: Fils in 2

Daniil Medvedev vs Dalibor Svrcina

Head-to-head – Medvedev 2-0 Svrcina

Daniil Medvedev will be pleased to have come through his match against Jan-Lennard Struff, whose aggressive game can be a difficult matchup for the Russian. Dalibor Svrcina is a capable player, but the consistency and depth Medvedev brings from the baseline represent a significant step up. Medvedev should be able to absorb Svrcina’s pace and force him into playing extra shots. Expect the Russian to control the exchanges and progress comfortably.
Prediction: Medvedev in 2

Tommy Paul vs Valentin Vacherot

Head-to-head – First Meeting

Tommy Paul has been playing some solid tennis and will be a difficult opponent to deal with on these courts. However, Valentin Vacherot has already shown that his breakthrough run last year was no fluke, and his confidence on the Shanghai courts should not be underestimated. This could develop into a fascinating baseline battle, with Vacherot’s bigger serve giving him an advantage in the shorter exchanges. Expect a tight three-setter, with the defending champion doing just enough to get through.
Prediction: Vacherot in 3

Felix Auger-Aliassime vs Pablo Carreno Busta

Head-to-head – Auger-Aliassime 1-0 Carreno Busta

Felix Auger-Aliassime will be looking to put together a strong week in Shanghai, and his comeback win over Camilo Ugo Carabelli should give him a timely confidence boost. Pablo Carreno Busta has also battled through two three-set matches to reach this stage, showing that he remains a tough opponent to put away. However, the Spaniard has not maintained the consistency he showed during his peak years, while Auger-Aliassime has the bigger serve and more attacking weapons. If the Canadian can stay patient in the longer rallies, he should have enough to advance.
Prediction: Auger-Aliassime in 2
