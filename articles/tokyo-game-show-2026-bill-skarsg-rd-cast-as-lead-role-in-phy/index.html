Tokyo Game Show 2026: Bill Skarsgård Cast as Lead Role in PHYSINT

TheTools · 17.09.2026 10:37 · source · permalink

In partnership with XBOX, KOJIMA PRODUCTIONS announced the casting of the protagonist of the upcoming action-espionage game alongside a new poster.

Just when it appeared that the XBOX Tokyo Game Show 2026 Broadcast had come to a close, legendary game creator Hideo Kojima arrived with one more surprise: fresh updates on OD – KNOCK and PHYSINT, including the news that Swedish actor and producer Bill Skarsgård will portray the lead role in PHYSINT, the upcoming action-espionage game from KOJIMA PRODUCTIONS. 

Kojima-san also shared a new poster for the title featuring a photo he personally shot of Skarsgård alongside Charlee Fraser, offering a fresh glimpse at the talent helping bring this ambitious project to life. 

“I’m delighted to confirm the lead role for PHYSINT will be Bill Skarsgård,” said KOJIMA PRODUCTIONS founder Hideo Kojima in a press release. “We are both long-time fans of each other’s work, and we’ve decided to embark on this new adventure together. The costume fittings, scans, and camera tests we conducted with Bill and Charlee earlier this year gave me tremendous confidence in the direction of the project.”

Most recently, Skarsgård received rave reviews for his performance in Gus Van Sant’s Dead Man’s Wire (2026). He next stars in Peter Bergs’s The Mosquito Bowl and Andrew Niccol’s Lords of War opposite Nicolas Cage. Earlier this year he co-starred in The Death of Robin Hood (Michael Sarnoski, 2026) with Hugh Jackman and Jodie Comer.

Skarsgård gained international recognition for his portrayal of the iconic clown Pennywise in It (Andy Muschietti, 2017), a role he reprised in It: Chapter Two (Andy Muschietti, 2019).

His film career spans titles such as Nosferatu (Robert Eggers, 2024), Barbarian (Zach Cregger, 2022), The Crow (Rupert Sanders, 2024), Atomic Blonde (David Leitch, 2017), John Wick: Chapter 4 (Chad Stahelski, 2023), The Devil All the Time(Antonio Campos, 2020) and Boy Kills World (Moritz Mohr, 2023).

On television, he recently starred in It: Welcome to Derry (2025), the It prequel series for HBO Max, in which he reprised the role of Pennywise and also served as executive producer. His television credits also include Clark (Jonas Åkerlund, 2022), Castle Rock (2018–2019), and Hemlock Grove (2013–2015).

He joins an ensemble cast that includes Fraser, Don Lee, and Minami Hamabe with Kojima-san promising further casting updates in the future.

For OD – KNOCK, Kojima-san shared an update on development progress, revealing behind the scenes footage of stars Sophia Lillis and Hunter Schafer in a performance capture session.

Last week, XBOX and KOJIMA PRODUCTIONS announced that XBOX will publish PHYSINT, expanding a relationship that already includes OD. The collaboration also extends beyond games, with the companies working together across film and television to bring new stories to broader audiences.
