Photo: Brad Pitt, wearing camouflage, kicked off the promotional tour for his new movie

TheTools · 17.09.2026 13:27 · source · permalink

Hollywood actor Brad Pitt has kicked off the promotional tour for his new film, *Heart of the Beast*. At the film’s premiere in Franklin, Tennessee, the 62-year-old actor was accompanied by his girlfriend, 33-year-old jewelry designer Ines de Ramon.

On the red carpet, Brad Pitt appeared in an unusual camouflage-print suit, pairing it with a khaki T-shirt.

The actor’s outfit is symbolic and echoes his role in the film, in which Pitt plays a former member of an elite special forces unit.

Meanwhile, Ines de Ramon posed for the paparazzi in a peach-colored, multi-layered maxi dress with a plunging neckline and ruffles.

The couple looked relaxed and sweetly held hands during their joint public appearance.

Brad Pitt’s look was intentionally given a slightly rugged, worn-in feel, in keeping with his character’s style in the new film.

The actor and Ines de Ramon also posed together for photographers.

The movie star was joined on the red carpet by dogs, as the main character’s four-legged friend plays an important role in the film *Heart of a Beast*.

The dogs’ appearance was another notable detail of Brad Pitt’s premiere look and caught the attention of event guests and photographers alike.

The day before, the streaming service Netflix unveiled the first teaser for the sequel to Quentin Tarantino’s black comedy *Once Upon a Time in Hollywood*, which was released in 2019. At the same time, it was revealed that the sequel is titled *The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth*.

Earlier, Lucasfilm unveiled the first trailer for the second season of the series *Star Wars: Ahsoka* at the D23 event. The new season is set to premiere on January 20, 2027. Also at D23, footage was shown from the upcoming film *Star Wars: Starfighter*, starring Ryan Gosling.
