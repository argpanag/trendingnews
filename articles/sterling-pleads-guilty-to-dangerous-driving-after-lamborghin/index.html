Sterling pleads guilty to dangerous driving after Lamborghini crash

TheTools · 30.09.2026 19:03 · source · permalink
  • Raheem Sterling pleaded guilty Tuesday to dangerous driving after crashing his Lamborghini on a motorway in southern England in May, adding two further admissions during his appearance at Basingstoke Magistrates’ Court.
  • The 31-year-old footballer also admitted possessing six nitrous oxide canisters for wrongful inhalation and failing to provide a specimen for analysis.
  • Sterling appeared at the court, located about 50 miles southwest of London, dressed in a black suit jacket, black trousers and black shoes with a dark top.
  • No other vehicles involved The incident occurred on the M3 motorway on the morning of May 28, when Sterling’s Lamborghini crashed.
  • Police said the collision did not involve any other vehicles and that no injuries were reported.
