From extra storage to charging docks and gaming headsets, these picks can help get your setup ready for GTA 6.

  • Grand Theft Auto VI will be available only as a digital download, with no physical disc version.
  • Preloading for the game on PlayStation and Xbox consoles begins on Tuesday, November 10.
  • Players will need to have up to 212GB of free storage space to download the game.
  • The article suggests must-have gaming gear like storage expansion and headsets ahead of the release.

Anticipating the release of Grand Theft Auto VI has felt like an absolute eternity, but the wait is almost over. In a first for the series, GTA VI will not be sold on a physical disc and is only available as a digital download code. Following preorders opening in June, developer Rockstar Games confirmed that the game will begin downloading on PlayStation and Xbox consoles on Tuesday, Nov. 10. Because of its massive footprint, Rockstar is opening preloads more than a week before official access begins.

If you’re ready to dive into this highly anticipated adventure, you’ll need to make sure your setup can handle a launch this big—starting with securing up to 212GB of free storage space.
