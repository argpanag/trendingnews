Jessica Tarlov says she learned her father was dying trying on her wedding dress

TheTools · 19.09.2026 02:44 · source · permalink

Jessica Tarlov says she learned her father was dying while trying on her wedding dress, a story she reveals in an exclusive book excerpt published ahead of her memoir’s release.

Tarlov, the Fox News co‑host and political commentator, recounts the moment in an excerpt published by People that describes standing in her wedding dress when she was told her father was gravely ill and would soon die.

The anecdote appears as part of a longer personal memoir excerpt; People published the excerpt on Sept. 10 and presented it as an exclusive account of that wedding‑day moment.

Tarlov has also promoted the new book on her public social accounts, announcing a September release date. Her social post and publisher materials state the memoir is due on Sept. 15.

The story is one of several personal essays Tarlov has discussed in publicity for the book; she has been doing interviews and excerpts in the run-up to publication.

For readers wanting more, the site’s earlier coverage includes an article on Tarlov learning the news while trying on her wedding dress and a separate item about the book announcement and release details.
