Lithuania beat Serbia, breathe new life into World Cup hopes

TheTools · 26.11.2026 23:41 · source · permalink

KLAIPEDA (Lithuania) - With their backs against the wall, Lithuania turned up the heat against Serbia and claimed a much-needed 97-78 win, but their FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027 hopes remain far from assured.

Margiris Normantas paced Lithuania with 23 points on a deadly 7-for-9 shooting, along with 6 assists, helped by 17 from Gytis Radzevicius.

"I would say, [it was] not an easy game, but an emotional game. For us, it was very important, but we did what we're supposed to do," Normantis said post-game.

Lithuania started strongly, racing to an early lead and shooting 7-for-14 from three-point range in the first 18 minutes, while shooting a solid 34.8 per cent, so the threat was evident.

They went on to shoot 10-for-20 from beyond the arc to secure the win, improving their record to 5-4 in Group J ahead of their next game against Bosnia & Herzegovina on Sunday.

Serbia were led by 24 points from Alen Smailagic, as they prepare to face Italy next.

Before tonight’s clash, the two combatants shared a 3-3 head-to-head record, with another fascinating chapter added to the story of these two basketball powerhouses.

One of their most memorable encounters came in the Semi-Finals of FIBA EuroBasket 2015, when Lithuania edged Serbia 67-64 to book their place in the Final, where they eventually lost to Spain.

Their last matchup was in the Quarter-Finals of the FIBA Basketball World Cup 2023, which Serbia won 87-68, en route to their silver medal.

And now, three years later, the rivalry remains as compelling as ever.

With qualification hopes and a long-standing competitive history on the line, Lithuania and Serbia once again demonstrated why this is one of international basketball's most captivating matchups.

Their next meeting will be in March on Gameday 12 in what could be a potentially decisive matchup for World Cup qualification.
