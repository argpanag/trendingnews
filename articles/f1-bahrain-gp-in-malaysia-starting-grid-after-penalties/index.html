F1 Bahrain GP in Malaysia starting grid after penalties

TheTools · 03.10.2026 09:35 · source · permalink

The Race Team

Max Verstappen and Lewis Hamilton will share the front row for the first Formula 1 race in Malaysia in nine years, just as they did the last time F1 raced there in 2017.

Three drivers have grid penalties for the Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia.

Isack Hadjar has a five-place grid penalty for taking his seventh internal combustion engine of the season.

Franco Colapinto's Alpine has 15 places of penalties, five from causing a pile-up in Baku and 10 from taking engine components.

Arvid Lindblad has a back-of-the-grid penalty from multiple fresh engine components.
