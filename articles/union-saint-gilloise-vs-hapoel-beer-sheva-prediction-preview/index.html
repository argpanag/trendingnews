Union Saint-Gilloise vs Hapoel Beer Sheva — Tale of the Tape

Union Saint-Gilloise head into this fixture in a stronger position after collecting four points from their opening two Europa League matches. 

Their 3-0 away victory over Viktoria Plzen provided an encouraging start, while the subsequent 1-1 draw with Real Sociedad helped build on it.

Union have lost just one of their last 11 group/league phase fixtures as designated hosts in the Europa.

The Belgian side have also remained competitive domestically, unbeaten across all domestic competitions this season, beating Nijlen 3-0 in the Belgian Cup last time out.

Union will need to manage their available squad carefully, with Ross Sykes listed with a shoulder injury, while Adem Zorgane and Ilan Hurtevent have also been reported as injury concerns in the build-up to the match. 

Hapoel Be'er Sheva, meanwhile, arrive after a difficult start to their European campaign, as they began with a goalless draw against Dinamo Zagreb but were beaten 2-1 away to AZ Alkmaar in their latest fixture. 

The result left them with one point from two matches and increased the importance of Thursday's encounter if they are to improve their position in the league phase.

Their domestic performances have offered more encouragement, with a 2-1 victory over Hapoel Jerusalem on Sunday extending their winning streak in domestic competition to four matches.

However, their Europa League away form remains a concern, having failed to win any of the last 10 such games and have lost the last five. 

Hapoel have failed to win either of their two encounters against clubs from Belgium.

Hapoel also have injury concerns to manage, with Lucas Ventura nursing a hamstring problem, while Dan Biton has been dealing with a heart problem.

Idan Nachmias is recovering from a hamstring injury, while Joao Victor has been listed with an illness.
