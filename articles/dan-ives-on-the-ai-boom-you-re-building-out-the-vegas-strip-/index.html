Dan Ives on the AI boom: 'You're building out the Vegas Strip in 1955'

TheTools · 30.09.2026 06:31 · source · permalink
  • ANTH.PVT
  • OPAI.PVT

Anthropic's (ANTH.PVT) huge losses and obligations aren't a warning sign; they are the cost of building the ground floor of a new AI economy, according to Dan Ives, partner and senior managing director at Yorkville Ives.

"You're building out the Vegas Strip in 1955," Ives told Yahoo Finance's Brian Sozzi (video above). "I'm sure there were people then being like, 'Oh, this thing's never going to work.' The reality is on the Strip. If you don't get on the Strip, it's not like they're going to create another one."

Anthropic's leaked IPO prospectus outlines about $518 billion in cloud, compute, and infrastructure obligations and a GAAP net loss of $41.97 billion last year, up from $8.31 billion in 2024. Sales surged 1,088% to $4.59 billion in 2025, and the company is aiming for a roughly $2 trillion valuation.

Ives, a longtime AI bull, described Anthropic (ANTH.PVT) as "the heart and lungs" of the AI revolution trade, adding, "That goes for OpenAI as well."

Read more: How Anthropic overtook OpenAI to become the AI lab to beat

Where critics see the industry's circular financing as "some sort of negative doomsday," Ives sees something else: "You are essentially building out a new economy over the next 10 years … from data centers to chips to GPUs. And for the first time in 30 years, [the] US is ahead of China when it comes to tech."

'A 1997 moment, not a 2000 bubble moment'

Ives reiterated that the AI boom is in its early stages.

"We're only 15% through the spending, the $4 [trillion] to $5 trillion that's going to happen," Ives said. "I still believe this is a 1997 moment, not a 2000 bubble moment."

He expects some "choppiness" in the IPO market but sees Anthropic's eventual listing as "bullish for the broader tech trade," specifically because the AI space will receive "more transparency, and I think that's something investors will crave."
