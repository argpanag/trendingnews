Liverpool News Today: Szoboszlai Doubt, Isak Penalty and Koumas (13 November)

TheTools · 13.11.2026 05:24 · source · permalink

Dominik Szoboszlai's fitness for Hungary is in doubt, the Premier League's Key Match Incidents panel says Isak should have had a penalty, and Craig Bellamy has praised Liverpool's handling of Lewis Koumas.

Liverpool news round-up for Friday 13 November, updated 05:24 UK.

The latest liverpool news from the international break is mostly about fitness. Dominik Szoboszlai is the big doubt for Hungary, and the Premier League’s own panel has ruled that Liverpool should have had a penalty at Anfield. Manchester United come to Anfield on 22 November.

Szoboszlai fitness call will come at the last minute

Hungary say they will do “everything possible” to have Dominik Szoboszlai ready for their Nations League games against Georgia and Northern Ireland. The Hungarian federation said in a statement on X that any decision will “almost certainly only be made at the last minute”, as BBC Sport reports.

Szoboszlai was replaced after 69 minutes of Sunday’s 3-1 win over Crystal Palace. Andoni Iraola said he was “not 100%” because of a knee problem. “I’m sure that Dom, if he is fine he will want to go, but I’m not sure if he’s going to be fine,” the head coach said. Hungary play Georgia on Saturday. Northern Ireland lead the group by one point, so their game in Budapest on 17 November is the decider.

Isak should have had a penalty, panel rules

The Premier League’s Key Match Incidents panel says Alexander Isak should have been awarded a penalty in the 1-0 defeat by Arsenal at Anfield on 1 November. Jurrien Timber grabbed his shirt in the 21st minute, and the panel voted 4-1 that the spot-kick should have been given on the field. The majority called the VAR decision a “clear and obvious error”, according to BBC Sport.

Iraola was blunt after the game. “There is a clear grab of the shirt,” he told Match of the Day. “The answer is you have to dive. If you don’t dive they won’t call it.” Isak has seven Premier League goals in nine games this season.

Bellamy on Koumas

Wales boss Craig Bellamy has praised Liverpool’s handling of Lewis Koumas ahead of Saturday’s Nations League game in Norway. “I like what Liverpool have done with him,” Bellamy said. “They’ve exposed him but also known when to take him out as well.”

Koumas is 21 and has nine Liverpool appearances this season. He scored his first Champions League goal in a 6-3 win at LASK. Wales play Norway at Ullevaal Stadion in Oslo at 17:00 on Saturday 14 November, BBC Sport says.

Trey Nyoni set for a new contract

Liverpool are preparing a new contract for Trey Nyoni, the 19-year-old England Under-20 midfielder, as a reward for how he has played this season. A report from GiveMeSport, picked up in the BBC Sport gossip column on 11 November, puts the deal in those terms. Our earlier piece covered Ben Jacobs’ comments on the deal.
