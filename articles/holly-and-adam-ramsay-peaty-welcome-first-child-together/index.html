Holly and Adam Ramsay-Peaty welcome first child together

TheTools · 26.11.2026 14:27 · source · permalink

Holly and Adam Ramsay-Peaty have welcomed their first child together, a baby girl.

The 26-year-old digital creator - the daughter of celebrity chef Gordon Ramsay - gave birth to "beautiful baby girl" Daphne June Ramsay-Peaty on Wednesday.

The couple announced they were expecting their first child together in June.

Olympic champion swimmer Adam Ramsay-Peaty, 31, is already father to six-year-old George from his previous relationship with the artist Eirianedd Munro.

The couple shared photographs in a joint Instagram post that read: "Daphne June Ramsay-Peaty, Welcome to the World.

"You'll forever be our beautiful baby girl and George's baby sister.

"Thank you to all the Doctors, Nurses and Midwives who made sure we were safe, healthy and happy – we will forever be grateful."

The post included photographs of the couple in hospital holding their daughter.

Famous friends offered their congratulations, including model Romeo Beckham and singer Pixie Lott.

The baby's birth comes nearly a year after the couple married in a lavish wedding at Bath Abbey in Somerset.

Gordon Ramsay became a grandfather for the first time on the same day it was announced he will host the BBC's Top Gear.

The 60-year-old, known for shows including Hell's Kitchen and Ramsay's Kitchen Nightmares, is stepping into the driver's seat in 2028.

Ramsay will also front an ITV Hell's Kitchen reboot with Claudia Winkleman in the new year.

Source: Press Association
