(WJAR) — The Massachusetts State Lottery announced that a Lincoln, Rhode Island, man won a $1 million prize in its "100X" scratch ticket game.

According to lottery officials, Phillip Perrault chose the cash option on his prize and received a one-time payment of $650,000 before taxes.

He told the Massachusetts State Lottery that he plans to use his winnings to retire.

The winning ticket was purchased at Border Bets & Butts in Attleboro, lottery officials said.

The store will receive a $10,000 bonus for selling the winning ticket.
