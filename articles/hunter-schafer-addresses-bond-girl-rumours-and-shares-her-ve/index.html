Hunter Schafer addresses Bond Girl Rumours and shares her verdict on Robert Pattinson as 007

TheTools · 18.11.2026 11:44 · source · permalink

At the premiere of Blade Runner 2099, Hunter Schafer spoke to HELLO! about the new 007 casting and whether she would take on the role as Bond Girl

The new Amazon series Blade Runner 2099 premiered in Leicester Square on Tuesday night, and we spoke to rumoured Bond Girl Hunter Schafer about her thoughts on the latest casting announcement, Robert Pattinson.

When HELLO! asked about her initial thoughts on the Twilight actor playing the secret service agent, the 27-year-old replied: "Very cool, I love it." Another outlet asked whether she would be a Bond Girl, following the various rumours. Hunter coyly said: "Yeah, I mean, I don't know who wouldn't."

The actress, who appeared on Variety's recent list of 19 actresses tipped to play the role, seemed a little caught off guard by the Bond questions and kept her answers vague, adding to suspicion that she may be in the running. Other huge names on Variety's list include stars Emily Blunt, Sydney Sweeney and Margaret Qualley, but they could also cast a complete unknown star too.

The news has been full of Bond buzz because earlier this week, Denis Villeneuve (who directed the 2017 sequel Blade Runner 2049) unveiled Robert Pattinson as the new James Bond for Amazon MGM Studios' upcoming reboot. Hunter would be a standout choice because she would become the first transgender actress to play a leading Bond Girl, making franchise history and propelling her career even further.

Aside from the Bond rumours, her new role in the series Blade Runner 2099 sits amongst a stacked line-up who attended the premiere last night, including former Bond Girl Michelle Yeoh (who starred as Wai Lin opposite Pierce Brosnan in 1997's Tomorrow Never Dies), Tom Burke (The Musketeers) and Lewis Gribben (Somewhere Boy).

Opting for a futuristic fashion look for the event, The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes star wore a monochrome Celine ensemble featuring an asymmetrical bustier dress with a flowing train over tailored tuxedo trousers. The satin outfit was paired with pointed-toe pumps and subtle makeup, adding the finishing touches and oozing the elegance of a potential Bond Girl.

What is Blade Runner 2099 about?

Created by Silka Luisa for Amazon Prime, the sci-fi series acts as a sequel to the films Blade Runner (1982) and Blade Runner 2049 (2017). Hunter plays Cora, who is a human pretending to be a replicant, and Michelle plays Olwen, who is her partner and a replicant Blade Runner in her final days of life.

The show is set in 2099 Los Angeles, following an uprising where replicants are the power brokers in the city, whereas humans are second-class citizens. Olwen is nearing the end of her life and unites with her partner Cora, who is a human pretending to be a replicant, to hunt down a gang of rogue replicants.

Where can I watch it?

The ten-part series will be available to watch on Amazon Prime Video on November 25 2026.
