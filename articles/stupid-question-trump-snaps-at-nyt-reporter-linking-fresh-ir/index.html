“Modi hatao, democracy bachao” — Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge’s rallying cry at the INDIA bloc’s mega protest in Delhi. Kharge accused the BJP-led governments at the Centre and in Maharashtra, Bihar and Uttar Pradesh of being “governments of vote chori”. He alleged that the votes of youth, women and Dalits were being deleted and targeted Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Home Minister Amit Shah over their support for Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. Kharge said the Opposition would continue its fight until Gyanesh Kumar was removed, and declared: “We will fight together, remove Modi, protect the Constitution and strengthen democracy.”

Congress president Mallikarjun Kharge has accused Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar of “treason against the nation and the Constitution of India”. Addressing an INDIA bloc mock parliament in Delhi, Kharge levelled six allegations against the CEC, including claims of large-scale voter deletions during the Special Intensive Revision (SIR), changes to voter registration rules and alleged interference in the Election Commission's functioning. He also accused Kumar of favouring the ruling BJP and compromising the poll body's independence. BJP spokesperson Sambit Patra hit back, accusing the opposition of trying to vitiate the atmosphere through its mock parliament and protests over alleged vote theft.
