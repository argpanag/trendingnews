Scottie Scheffler's caddie details conversation about potential $1 billion LIV Golf offer, why he'd decline

TheTools · 03.09.2026 17:39 · source · permalink

The world No. 1's caddie shared how Scheffler dismissed a potential $250M LIV offer to stay on the PGA Tour

Scottie Scheffler recently took over the top spot on the PGA Tour's career money list with more than $130 million earned. It's fair to say that the 30-year-old who has already taken home $10 million more than Tiger Woods did in his career is doing just fine financially, but as his caddie, Ted Scott, recently explained, it's not all about the money for Scheffler.

Scheffler became the No. 1 player in the world in March 2022, amid LIV Golf's efforts to lure players away from the PGA Tour, and just three months before the then Saudi-backed circuit's inaugural event.

Scott began caddying for Scheffler in November 2021, and having been in the partnership for less than six months at the time, he was still very much in the feeling-things-out stage with his new boss, but one conversation very much set the tone.

Scottie Scheffler of the United States and caddie Ted Scott look on from the ninth hole during the first round of the 126th U.S. OPEN at Shinnecock Hills Golf Club on June 18, 2026 in Southampton, New York. (Photo by Christian Petersen/Getty Images) (Christian Petersen/Getty Images)

"When I first started working for him, he was 19th in the world, and he had a white golf bag," Scott said, referring to Scheffler, on a recent appearance on PGA Tour Radio. "I didn't know him that well, and I asked him, I was like: 'Why don't you have sponsors?'

"He's like, 'Well, because I just want to play with the equipment that I want to play.' And I was like, 'Okay.' And then I remember whenever all the LIV stuff was going on, you know, he was number one in the world at the time, I didn't know, but I said, 'They're probably going to offer you like $250m to go over there.'

Scottie Scheffler of the United States walks with his caddie Ted Scott on the first hole during Day Four of the The 154th Open Championship at Royal Birkdale on July 19, 2026 in Southport, England. (Photo by Stuart Kerr/R&A/R&A via Getty Images) (Stuart Kerr/R&A/R&A via Getty Images)

"And he says, 'I don't care what they offer me. They can offer me a billion dollars. I'm going to play where the best players are because I just want to beat people.'"

Scott went on to admit that it was at that moment he realized just how differently Scheffler is wired.

Scottie Scheffler of the United States fist-bumps his caddie Ted Scott on the 18th hole green during the third round of the FedEx St. Jude Championship at TPC Southwind on August 15, 2026 in Memphis, Tennessee. (Photo by Tracy Wilcox/PGA TOUR via Getty Images) (Tracy Wilcox/PGA TOUR via Getty Images)

While Scott already had a stellar resume as a caddie before joining forces with Scheffler, having been on the bag for both of Bubba Watson's Masters victories, he and Scheffler have turned into a two-headed monster over the last half-decade.

Scott has been on the bag for each of Scheffler's 22 PGA Tour wins, dating back to his first in February 2022, plus the four major championships Scheffler has won at this point in his young career.
