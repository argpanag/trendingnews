United return home: Save 20% on final tickets for Saturday night | Melbourne United

TheTools · 03.10.2026 12:21 · source · permalink

2 Oct

1

min read

Melbourne United are back at John Cain Arena tomorrow night, Saturday 3 October, taking on the Cairns Taipans from 7:30pm — and you can save 20% on the final remaining General Reserve tickets.

After a hard-fought battle in Tasmania saw United fall just two points short, the team is eager to return home, feed off the energy of the United faithful and put on another huge show.

There’s nothing quite like a Saturday night inside John Cain Arena. From the opening tip to the final buzzer, the noise, passion and support from the stands help make our home arena a special place to play. Tomorrow night, we want you there to help lift the team as we take on the Taipans.

From now until tip off, fans can secure their place for 20% off. It’s the perfect opportunity to get your family or friends together, bring the noise and make United part of your weekend.

The team is ready to respond.

Secure your discounted General Reserve tickets now via AXS and join us at John Cain Arena tomorrow night. Doors open at 6pm.
