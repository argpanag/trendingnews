Lionel Messi & Inter Miami enter MLS Cup Playoffs with historic ambitions | MLSSoccer.com

TheTools · 21.11.2026 22:56 · source · permalink

It's already been another record-breaking year for Lionel Messi.

Whether it's a memorable FIFA World Cup journey with Argentina or chasing a third straight Landon Donovan MLS MVP award with Inter Miami CF, the legendary No. 10 has continued to put up jaw-dropping numbers and outstanding performances throughout 2026.

Now, Messi and the Herons shift attention to their ultimate goal: winning MLS Cup presented by Audi and becoming the first back-to-back champions since the 2011-12 LA Galaxy.

Their Audi 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs journey begins Saturday in their Round One Best-of-3 Series with a home clash against the Columbus Crew, who enter the postseason as the league's most in-form team (6 pm ET | Apple TV).

World Cup journey

Messi started the year with a massive motivator: preparing for his sixth FIFA World Cup and arriving at the 2026 tournament in peak form, with Argentina looking to defend their 2022 title.

By full-time of Argentina's opening match, Messi had tallied a hat trick in a 3-0 win vs. Algeria and looked more than ready for the moment. 

An epic tournament ensued for the GOAT, who contributed 8g/4a to finish second in the Golden Boot race (behind France's Kylian Mbappé) and drag his side to the final, where they lost 1-0 to Spain in extra time.

Although the tournament ended in heartbreak, Messi again proved that age is just a number, setting numerous World Cup records before falling just short vs. Lamine Yamal, Rodri & Co.

Just over a month later, Messi announced his retirement from the international game, passing the torch to the next generation with one last memorable performance in his Argentina farewell. He closed his La Albiceleste  career with an unparalleled 126 goals in 208 caps.

Back-to-back Golden Boots

After the World Cup pause, Messi's sensational form carried over to the MLS stage.

The legendary No. 10 led Inter Miami to the Eastern Conference's No. 2 seed and finished the regular season with a league-best 43 goal contributions (26g/17a).

His 26 goals guaranteed a second straight Golden Boot presented by Audi and were four more than FC Dallas striker Petar Musa, the next closest challenger. Meanwhile, Messi's 17 assists were tied for second-most in MLS alongside FC Cincinnati star Evander.

MVP loading?

Not only did Messi lock up back-to-back Golden Boot honors, but he also looks set to become the first player in league history to win three straight Landon Donovan MLS MVPs.

Already named a finalist, Inter Miami's Argentine superstar previously took home the year-end award in 2024 and 2025.

Safe to say the GOAT has been nothing short of spectacular since joining the Herons midway through the 2023 campaign.

Ready to repeat?

In a league defined by parity, repeating as MLS Cup champions is increasingly becoming a rarity.

Case in point: It's been nearly 15 years since the 2011 and 2012 LA Galaxy, led by now-Miami owner David Beckham alongside Landon Donovan and Robbie Keane, won back-to-back titles.

But if there's any team that can accomplish that, it might be a Miami side featuring Messi, Rodrigo De Paul, Casemiro and Luis Suárez. They've already won silverware this year, claiming Campeones Cup over LIGA MX side Cruz Azul, and are just five playoff wins away from rewriting the record books.

If last year's postseason run is any indication, the Herons shouldn't be counted out. In 2025, en route to their first-ever MLS Cup title, Miami set a record for playoff goals scored (20) and Messi made history with a playoff record for goal contributions (15; 9g/6a).

The soccer world is well aware of what Messi is capable of, especially in must-win moments. Can he lead the Herons to more glory and cap what's already been a magical 2026 season?
