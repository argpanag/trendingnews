NPL: Biratnagar presents 187 run target for Karnali to win

TheTools · 29.10.2026 16:46 · source · permalink

Kathmandu, Oct 29: In today's match of the Nepal Premier League (NPL) 2026, Biratnagar Kings set a target of 187 runs for Karnali Yaks to win. 

In the match held at the Tribhuvan International Cricket Ground in Kirtipur, Biratnagar scored 186 runs for the loss of 7 wickets in their allotted 20 overs against Karnali, who chose to bowl first after winning the toss. 

Lokesh Bam was the top scorer for Biratnagar with 87 runs. He played 52 balls and hit 3 fours and 9 sixes. His superb batting helped Biratnagar post a strong total. 

Similarly, Andre Russell added 27 runs off 15 balls, hitting 3 fours and 2 sixes, while Angelo Mathews scored 31 runs off 23 balls with the help of four 4s. 

Basir Ahmed scored 13 runs off 7 balls, and Shehan Jayasuriya contributed 11 runs. 

On the bowling side, Karnali Yaks captain Sompal Kami took 4 wickets for 33 runs in his 4 overs. 

Mark Watt and Nandan Yadav took one wicket each. 

Currently, Karnali Yaks are batting in response, scoring 33 runs for the loss of 1 wicket in 3 overs. (RSS)
