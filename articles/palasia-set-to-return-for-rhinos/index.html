Palasia set to return for Rhinos

TheTools · 11.09.2026 18:16 · source · permalink

9 Sep 2026

Leeds Rhinos Head Coach Brad Arthur has named his 21 man squad to face Hull FC this Friday at AMT Headingley, kick off 8pm.

The Rhinos welcome back forward Keenan Palasia after has recovered from a foot injury whilst Maika Sivo is also set to return on the wing following his broken arm. 

James McDonnell will not feature this week to allow extra time for his pectoral injury to heel. 

Brodie Croft will make his 200th career appearance on Friday night. The stand off has been in sensational try scoring form this season. 

Lachie Miller has been ruled out for the season following the torn hamstring he suffererd last week against Wigan. The Rhinos are still without Danny Levi, Max Simpson (knee), Ben Littlewood (hamstring) and George Brown (knee). 

Tickets for the game are available now at tickets.therhinos.co.uk
