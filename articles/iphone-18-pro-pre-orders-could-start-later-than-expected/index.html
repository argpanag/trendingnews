iPhone 18 Pro pre-orders could start later than expected

TheTools · 02.09.2026 03:49 · source · permalink

Was this useful?

Why did you choose this rating?

Apple is set to unveil the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max on 10 September 2026 for those of us in Australia.

Based on past timelines, pre-orders usually open the first Friday afterwards.

That Friday falls on 11 September, the 25th anniversary of the 9/11 attacks, so Apple is expected to push pre-orders to Saturday 12 September instead.

Reports suggest pre-sales could open at midnight Pacific Time, which is around 5pm AEST.

The pre-order date has changed before, too.

Back in 2015, when the iPhone 6s event also fell on 9 September, the company held pre-orders on Saturday 12 September rather than the usual Friday.

The expectation is that Apple will follow the same approach again.

The highly-anticipated Apple Event will kick off at 3am AEST.

You can stream it live on Apple's website, on YouTube and through the Apple TV app.

But if 3am is far too early for you, you can catch the keynote later in the morning with a cup of coffee, as it will be available to watch on demand.
