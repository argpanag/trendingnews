Winning EuroMillions numbers on Tuesday, September 1

TheTools · 01.09.2026 18:36 · source · permalink

Tonight's National Lottery EuroMillions draw offers players the chance to bag a stunning £67million.

To win, ticket holders need all five main numbers and the two Lucky Stars to stake their claim.

We bring you the crucial winning Lotto numbers below as they drop, as well as the results for the Thunderball.

The winning EuroMillions numbers:

2, 10, 23, 37, 47

The lucky stars are: 3 and 5

Winning Thunderball numbers:

3, 21, 23, 37, 39

The Thunderball is: 7

There are chances to win EuroMillions every Tuesday and Friday. You can buy a ticket for £2.50 (on draw days up until 7.30pm).

If you want more games to play, there is also Lotto every Saturday and Wednesday and Set For Life every Monday and Thursday.

The Thunderball draw (which also takes place tonight, as well as every Wednesday, Friday and Saturday) has a £500,000 top prize.
