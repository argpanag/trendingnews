Match preview

Ipswich Town

For a newly promoted team, Ipswich have given supporters plenty of reasons for optimism. Gary O’Neil’s side has won three of its first seven league matches and scored 11 goals, showing a willingness to attack even against the Premier League’s stronger clubs.

That was evident in last weekend’s 3-2 loss to Manchester City. Ipswich became only the second team this league season to score multiple goals against City, and the Tractor Boys stayed within striking distance until the final whistle. Before that, they picked up a 2-1 win over Fulham.

With only one point separating Ipswich from sixth place, another victory could send them temporarily into the European places and further reinforce the idea that they belong at this level.

Nottingham Forest

Forest, meanwhile, desperately need to reverse their momentum.

Oliver Glasner’s team has one win, two draws and four losses through seven matches and enters Friday on a three-game losing streak. A 1-0 defeat to Arsenal last time out highlighted both sides of Forest’s early-season story. They held the defending champions to only one shot on target but once again failed to generate enough offense.

Forest have scored a league-low five goals this season. Their defense has been considerably stronger, conceding nine times compared with Ipswich’s 15, but the lack of finishing is leaving very little room for error.

What to expect Friday

There is some reason for Forest to feel confident. Their best performances have come away from home, including their lone victory at Aston Villa in September. Recent history against Ipswich is also firmly on their side. Forest are unbeaten in the last six meetings between the clubs, winning five of them, including a 4-2 victory in March.

Team selection could also play a major part Friday. Ipswich may stick largely with the lineup that pushed Manchester City, though Zian Flemming provides another attacking option after scoring off the bench in that defeat. Sasa Lukic could also return from a groin issue.

Forest will hope Morgan Gibbs-White recovers from a muscular problem, while Igor Jesus and Nicolo Savona have been ruled out. Former Ipswich striker Liam Delap is expected to lead the attack as he searches for just his second league goal of the season.

This matchup ultimately pits Ipswich’s early momentum against Forest’s strong recent record in the series. The Tractor Boys have looked capable of troubling anyone, while Forest’s defensive resilience keeps them competitive.
