ogwizard climbs twelve spots in HLTV September Prospects ranking

TheTools · 05.09.2026 16:47 · source · permalink

HLTV has released its prospect list for the month of September, with two Americans and one NA-adjacent player making the list. The two American players in question are Marsborne players Bogdan "ogwizard" Savula (#33) and Chance "WUMBO" Boyer (#48).

ogwizard gained a big piece of ground on the list, as he improved twelve places from his 45th-place ranking in August. WUMBO fell a single place, but was singled out as an opener to look out for.

ogwizard is the highest of the two Americans on the list, coming in at 33rd in his third appearance in the HLTV Prospects ranking. The Marsborne AWPer is joined by his teammate, entry player WUMBO. This is WUMBO's second month in a row in the top-50, after he briefly fell out in the month of July.

WUMBO was called out as one to look out for in the opener position, receiving the earlybird accolade for his role in the article. The American rifler isn't quite ready to make the jump to Big Events yet, but he'll be a player to keep an eye on in the future.

Liquid's sniper Georgi "⁠Jorko⁠" Mitev advanced five spots, improving from twelfth to seventh in the September rankings. Jorko impressed in the Esports World Cup Open Qualifier, posting a 1.11 rating, the second-best on the team.
