Scotland team v Wallabies: Lions duo bolster backline for Gregor Townsend as debutant on bench

TheTools · 13.11.2026 14:20 · source · permalink

Darcy Graham and Blair Kinghorn start for Scotland against Australia.

Scotland have made two changes to their XV for the Nations Championship fixture with Australia as Blair Kinghorn and Darcy Graham both start.

There is also a potential first outing for Newcastle Red Bulls back-row Ollie Leatherbarrow, who has been included amongst the replacements.

Kinghorn is promoted to full-back with Scotland‘s all-time leading try scorer Graham named on the left wing and Kyle Steyn on the right wing.

Same midfield and half-backs

It’s as you were in the rest of the back division, with captain Sione Tuipulotu lining up at inside centre and Huw Jones in the number 13 jersey.

Scrum-half Ben White starts alongside fly-half Finn Russell for the 28th time in a Test match, the same number as current head coach Gregor Townsend and former half-back partner Bryan Redpath.

Townsend has selected the same starting pack for this Australia clash, with Ewan Ashman, fresh from his historic Nations Championship hat-trick against New Zealand last weekend, named at hooker, either side of loosehead prop Rory Sutherland and tighthead, Zander Fagerson.

Gregor Brown and Scott Cummings continue their partnership in the second-row, and Scotland are similarly unchanged in the back-row, with Matt Fagerson, Rory Darge and Jack Dempsey reprising their roles in the loose.

Leatherbarrow, who qualifies for Scotland due to his grandmother being from Perth, has represented Scotland at U16s and 20s levels, and takes his place as one of six forward replacements. He is joined by fellow back-row Josh Bayliss, with Max Williamson covering the second-row.

Gregor Hiddleston, Pierre Schoeman and Will Hurd are the three front-row replacements and Scotland’s matchday 23 is completed by versatile backs Jamie Dobie and Tom Jordan.
