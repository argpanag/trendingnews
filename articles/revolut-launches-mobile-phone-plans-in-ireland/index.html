Revolut launches mobile phone plans in Ireland

TheTools · 09.11.2026 00:00 · source · permalink

Getty

Revolut has become the latest company to enter the Irish mobile-phone network market, with the fintech bank launching monthly plans for customers.

The non-contract 5G offering will cost €9.99 per month for the first 25,000 customers who sign up (rising then to €12.99) and will run on the Vodafone network.

The service will be offered via eSIM through the Revolut app, and will include unlimited data in Ireland, as well as 20GB of roaming data in the UK and EEA.

The company said Revolut Mobile will also include a 'Messaging Pass', giving users 1GB of additional low-speed data in over 120 non-EU countries.

According to Revolut, customers will be able to use RevPoints - which are given based on the level of card spending as part of the bank’s loyalty programme - to offset some or all of the cost of their monthly mobile services.

However, Revolut customers on the bank’s Ultra plan (which costs up to €60 per month) would need to spend around €1,800 each month to accumulate enough RevPoints in order to cover their entire bill based on the €12.99 price.

The launch follows a similar move from the German supermarket chain Lidl last month.
