Tanzid Tamim blames mindset, not skill, for Bangladesh’s recent struggles

The star opener also said that Bangladesh need to perform consistently, whether they play at home or away.

Bangladesh opener Tanzid Hasan Tamim believes the team’s recent struggles have more to do with their mindset than a lack of skill. He feels that if the players had a problem with their skills, they would have struggled in home conditions as well.

“If there was a problem with our skills, we would struggle at home too. If we can play with the same mindset we have at home and stay fully focused, I think we can do well. We had a very good discussion in the team meeting. I believe positive things will come from here,” Tamim said.

The star opener also said that Bangladesh need to perform consistently, whether they play at home or away. “We always try to do something good for the team. Sometimes we succeed, sometimes we don't. The more consistently we play, the further we will move forward,” he added.

Bangladesh suffered a 99-run defeat to Zimbabwe in the first match in Abu Dhabi. Chasing a target of 272, they were bowled out for 172 despite playing on a pitch that offered good conditions for batting.

Tamim felt the pitch was good enough to chase the target, but Bangladesh failed to build partnerships. The loss of wickets in quick succession made the chase much harder. “The pitch was good for chasing. We couldn't build partnerships, and that was the main reason. Wickets fell one after another, which put us under pressure. We need to work on how to handle that pressure, stay calm in difficult moments, and spend more time at the crease. We have to learn how to bat for more overs in those situations,” he said.
