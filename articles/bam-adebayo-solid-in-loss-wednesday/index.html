Bam Adebayo solid in loss Wednesday

TheTools · 22.10.2026 12:02 · source · permalink

by Leo Sells | Heat Correspondent | Thu, Oct 22nd 2026, 8:02am EDT

Bam Adebayo scored 15 points (6-12 FG, 1-5 3PT, 2-3 FT) to go with seven rebounds and three steals in Wednesday's 131-106 loss to the Minnesota Timberwolves.

Fantasy Impact:

The big man had an okay season debut on Wednesday. He scored 15 points on okay shooting splits, but he left a bit to be desired in other areas of the game. He should continue to be an above-average fantasy asset at the center position moving forward, although he doesn't project to be elite at any point.
