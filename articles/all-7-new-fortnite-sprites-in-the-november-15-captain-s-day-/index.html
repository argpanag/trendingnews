All 7 New Fortnite Sprites in the November 15 Captain’s Day Update

TheTools · 15.11.2026 13:15 · source · permalink

Here are all 7 new Fortnite Sprites in the November 15 Captain’s Day update, including Mini Miku, Squiplash, and Zee, plus their powers.

Epic Games has added seven new Fortnite Sprites in the November 15 Captain’s Day update. Players can now find the Squiplash and Mini Miku Sprites in Battle Royale. The update also unlocked Zee, this season’s rare Zero Point Sprite, which now has a chance of appearing on the map.

All 7 New Fortnite Sprites You Can Find Starting November 15

A total of seven new Fortnite Sprites are now available to find following the November 15 Captain’s Day update. While most of these Sprites were added to the game’s files in the November 12 patch, they only became available to collect today.

Videos by VICE

The new Fortnite Sprites include the Hatsune Miku-inspired Mini Miku Sprite and the Squiplash Sprite. The Captain’s Day update also added a secret rare Sprite called Zee, a new version of the incredibly rare Zero Point Sprite from Chapter 7 Season 3.

To make them easier to track, here’s the full list of new Sprites you can find starting November 15:

New Fortnite Sprites – Complete List

  • Mini Miku Sprite
  • Fortune Mini Miku Sprite
  • Cursed Mini Miku Sprite
  • Squiplash Sprite
  • Fortune Squiplash Sprite
  • Cursed Squiplash Sprite
  • Zee Sprite

Note: Zee is a secret rare Sprite, just like the Sweet Dream Sprite. You’ll likely need to use a Lucky Locator to find it or trade for it. Zee only appears in the menu after you obtain it.

New Fortnite Sprite Powers Explained

Three of the new Fortnite Sprites added in the November 15 Captain’s Day update bring new powers to the battle royale. For example, the Mini Miku Sprite grants a Friendship Bubble when you emote with another player.

What Time Do the New Fortnite Sprites Go Live?

The new Fortnite Sprites go live at 6 AM PT / 9 AM ET on November 15 with the Captain’s Day update. The countdown in Fortnite’s in-game Treasure menu shows when Hatsune Miku arrives as the new Captain.

Gold Mini Miku and Squiplash Sprites Arrive November 22

Finally, the Mini Miku and Squiplash Sprites also have Gold variants that will be released on November 22, 2026. According to Epic Games, the Gold versions of these collectibles will unlock once Oscar joins Battle Royale in the next Captain’s Day update.
