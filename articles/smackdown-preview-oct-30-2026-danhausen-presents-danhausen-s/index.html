Danhausen presents "Danhausen's Very Nice, Very Evil SmackDown"

Halloween is in the air, and the best show on Friday night is embracing the scary. 

Danhausen presents "Danhausen's Very Nice, Very Evil SmackDown."

Expect the unexpected with the unsettling special edition of SmackDown, Friday at 8 ET/7 CT on USA.


 


 

Trick Williams challenges Sami Zayn in an Undisputed WWE Championship Match

After barely escaping with the Undisputed WWE Championship by defeating Je'Von Evans in an impromptu title match on Raw this past Monday, Sami Zayn will now defend the title against Trick Williams.

After World Heavyweight Champion Roman Reigns suggested that Zayn might not make it to WWE Crown Jewel as Undisputed WWE Champion, Zayn has been doing everything in his power to prove The OTC wrong. 

On Raw, Zayn took the mic to demand that he finally be "acknowledged" by the WWE Universe and the WWE locker room. 

With everybody gunning for him, can Zayn survive a challenge from the dynamic Williams?

Don't miss all the action as part of "Danhausen's Very Nice, Very Evil SmackDown," Friday at 8 ET/7 CT on USA.
 

Chelsea Green defends WWE Women's Title against Nikki Bella 

With successful title defenses against Nia Jax and Roxanne Perez, WWE Women's Champion Chelsea Green will now put her title on the line against WWE Hall of Famer Nikki Bella.

Green is quickly proving herself as a fighting champion, but her decorated challenger has made every attempt to reduce her to just a "bootleg Nikki Bella."

Who will emerge with the title to battle Women's World Champion Liv Morgan at WWE Crown Jewel?

Find out as part of "Danhausen's Very Nice, Very Evil SmackDown," Friday at 8 ET/7 CT on USA.
 

Ricky Saints looks to take AAA World Cruiserweight Title from Rey Fenix

  • Two weeks ago, Ricky Saints shoved the referee during his AAA Cruiserweight Championship Match against Rey Fenix and lost by disqualification. 

    As the rivalry intensifies, Fenix will once again defend the title against Ricky Saints as part of "Danhausen's Very Nice, Very Evil SmackDown," tonight at 8 ET/7 CT on USA.
