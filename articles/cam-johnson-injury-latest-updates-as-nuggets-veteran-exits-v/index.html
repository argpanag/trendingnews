Cam Johnson (ankle) is questionable to return for the Nuggets' NBA season opener vs. OKC Thunder.

Cam Johnson is questionable to return with a right ankle sprain, the Denver Nuggets announced.

Alpha Diallo starts the second half for the injured Johnson in the Nuggets' Thursday season opener against the Oklahoma City Thunder.

Cam Johnson appeared to tweak his right ankle as he hit the deck hard with 3:30 left in the second quarter upon contesting Ajay Mitchell.

Johnson was limping before he was taken out of the game by Nuggets head coach David Adelman. He eventually entered the locker room.

Johnson dropped five points on 2-of-3 shooting in 11 minutes, and his status will continue to be monitored.
