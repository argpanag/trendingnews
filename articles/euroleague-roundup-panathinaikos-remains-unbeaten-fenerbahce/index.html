EuroLeague roundup: Panathinaikos remains unbeaten, Fenerbahce beats Zalgiris, Jones hits game winner for Zvezda

TheTools · 13.10.2026 21:40 · source · permalink

Stefan Acevski

Make Your Preferred Basketball Source.

The fifth round of the EuroLeague starts on Tuesday, with seven games on the schedule

By Eurohoops team/ info@eurohoops.net

Fenerbahce secured a victory in the first game of the fifth round of the EuroLeague, as they easily defeated Zalgiris at home with a score of 99-80.

In a match where Sarunas Jasikevicius played against his Zalgiris, the hosts were in the lead throughout, with the first and third quarters being crucial, as Fener won them 27-14 and 28-13 respectively.

Shavon Shields scored 17 points, Braxton Key added 15, and Will Clyburn 14. On the other side, Carson Edwards scored 20 points but was the only double-digit scorer. Jonas Valanciunas finished the game with eight points and ten rebounds.

Jones hits a buzzer-beater for Zvezda’s victory

Crvena Zvezda also secured a victory, defeating ASVEL on the road with a score of 84-82, thanks to Chris Jones’ buzzer-beater.

We witnessed a great drama in Villeurbanne, resolved in the last two minutes. Tremont Waters missed, and the visitors had nine seconds for an attack, with Jones shocking the home team at the buzzer.

Chima Moneke scored 18 points, Jordan Nwora added 17, Semi Ojeleye 15, while the hero Jones had eight points and nine assists. On the other side, Both Gach scored 19 points, and Tremont Waters had 15.

Panathinaikos’ victory on Zeljko Obradovic’s return to Belgrade

Panathinaikos registered their fifth victory in the EuroLeague, remaining perfect after five rounds. Zeljko Obradovic‘s team easily defeated Partizan in Belgrade with a score of 72-55.

On Obradovic‘s return to the Arena, there was a great reception and atmosphere, even though Obradovic declined a gift from his former club.

In the end, PAO showed their strength and why they are one of the top favorites for the title.

Nigel Hayes-Davis scored 14 points, Juancho Hernangomez added 10, and they were the only double-digit scorers for the visitors. For Partizan, Kevarrius Hayes scored 10 points, while Carlik Jones underperformed with eight points.

Real Madrid celebrated in Milan

Real Madrid also secured a victory, defeating Olimpia Milano in northern Italy with a score of 98-89.

After three initial losses, Pedro Martinez’s team found their rhythm and easily defeated Milano, pushing them further to the bottom of the EuroLeague standings.

Real controlled the game, at one point leading by more than 20 points, while the hosts eventually reduced the deficit to soften the loss.

Theo Maledon scored 16 points, Andres Feliz had 15, Facu Campazzo recorded 13 points and six assists, and Damian Jones added 10. For Milano, Leandro Bolmaro was the top scorer with 21 points, while Devon Hall had 17.
