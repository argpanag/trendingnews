WTA Guadalajara Day 1 Predictions Including Bianca Andreescu vs Cristina Bucsa - Last Word On Tennis

TheTools · 13.09.2026 01:55 · source · permalink

Tennis truly never stops, as just after the conclusion of the US Open, we have WTA Guadalajara all set to go, with some exciting encounters on the slate for Day 1. As always, we here at LWOT will be giving you our predictions for all the matches taking place, but who do you think will advance to the next round?

WTA Guadalajara Day 1 Predictions

Peyton Stearns vs Emiliana Arango

Head-to-head: Stearns 0-1 Arango

Emiliana Arango is a player more suited to the slower surfaces, where her superior defensive skills and court coverage can end up deciding points. On a hard court, Peyton Stearns, with her more aggressive brand of tennis, should be favored. I still expect this to go the distance, with the American winning on the back of being more comfortable on this surface.

Prediction: Stearns in 3

Darja Vidmanova vs Janice Tjen

Head-to-head: First meeting

Darja Vidmanova’s aggressive all-court game can prove to be tricky on hard courts, as shown by her run in Monterrey earlier this summer. Janice Tjen, on the other hand, is the more crafty player, using her variety to construct points and beat players tactically. If Tjen can disrupt enough of Vidmanova’s contact points from the back of the court and return well, she should win this match.

Prediction: Tjen in 3

Panna Udvardy vs Lois Boisson

Head-to-head: First meeting

Lois Boisson will always be remembered for her run at the French Open in 2025, as since then, injuries have been a major part of her struggles. This match against Panna Udvardy pits two players who like to get into points and dictate from the back of the court. If Boisson does not struggle physically, I give her heavier topspin game the edge in this matchup.

Prediction: Boisson in 3

Alycia Parks vs Varvara Lepchenko

Head-to-head: First meeting

Alycia Parks’ high-variance game always means that her opponents are in the match. In this encounter, she takes on Varvara Lepchenko, a player who is willing to stay at the baseline and grind out points with her lefty angles. While that tactic can work against Parks, she is still the overall more suited player to this surface and the one with the higher ceiling.

Prediction: Parks in 2

Bianca Andreescu vs Cristina Bucsa

Head-to-head: Andreescu 0-1 Bucsa

Currently ranked near the Top 200, Bianca Andreescu is far removed from the level that once saw her win the US Open. While her counterpunching style used to be tough to navigate on a hard court, in this matchup at WTA Guadalajara, it is more than likely that Cristina Bucsa, on the back of her better movement and court coverage, makes it physical and grinds out a win.

Prediction: Bucsa in 2
