Weather Planner: Thursday, October 1, 2026

Thursday will be another mild day with southerly winds blowing steadily throughout the day. Sunshine will be limited and mainly in the afternoon, while scattered rain showers will be most likely during the morning. Highs will be in the low and mid 70s, with overnight temperatures well into the 60s. Additional rain ahead of a cold front may being to work into the area from the northwest well after midnight and especially into Friday morning.
