French cycling prodigy Seixas claims first Monument victory at Giro di Lombardia

TheTools · 10.10.2026 15:58 · source · permalink

French prodigy Paul Seixas claimed his first Monument win at the Giro di Lombardia on Saturday. The victory makes the 20-year-old the fourth-youngest winner of a Monument race.
