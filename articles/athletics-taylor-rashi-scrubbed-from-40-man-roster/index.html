Athletics' Taylor Rashi: Scrubbed from 40-man roster

TheTools · 11.09.2026 23:59 · source · permalink

The Athletics designated Rashi for assignment Friday.

The transaction frees a spot on the 40-man roster for the addition of waiver claim Hunter Bigge. Rashi was a waiver claim himself in August and has allowed five earned runs with a 5:3 K:BB over 5.2 frames between the Diamondbacks and Athletics this season.
