LIVE! UFC 331 Results: Van vs. Pantoja 2

TheTools · 19.09.2026 04:00 · source · permalink
  • UFC

Joshua Van, Alexandre Pantoja clash in the UFC 331 main event

MMA Fighting has UFC 331 results for the Van vs. Pantoja 2 fight card, live blogs of the entire main card, and more from the Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, Calif., on Saturday night.

In the main event, UFC flyweight champion Joshua Van defends his title against former UFC champion Alexandre Pantoja in a rematch. Pantoja lost the title against Van via injury at UFC 323 this past December.

Arman Tsarukyan makes his long-awaited return to the UFC Octagon when he squares off against Mauricio Ruffy in a pivotal lightweight contest. Tsarukyan has won five straight fights, while Ruffy has won four of his past five fights.
