Halloween, Nevada Day fall on Saturday. That won't happen again until 2037

TheTools · 31.10.2026 13:27 · source · permalink

Halloween and Nevada Day both fall on Saturday this year, a concurrence that won't happen again until 2037.

This means the state parade and trick-or-treating are on the same day for most Northern Nevadans communities.

One exception is Carson City, where trick-or-treating will officially be observed on Friday, Oct. 30 so as to not collide with the Nevada Day parade held in the state's capital.

Reno plans to observe trick-or-treating on Saturday, Oct. 31.

Why will Halloween not fall on a Saturday for 11 years?

The last time Halloween fell on a Saturday was in 2020, in the midst of the COVID-19 pandemic when Washoe County Health District discouraged trick-or-treating.

Now, Halloween won't be on a Saturday until 2037 because three leap years -- in 2028, 2032 and 2036 -- will add an extra day to the calendar, Feb. 29, and disrupt the cycle.

Here are the days Halloween will fall on through 2037:

  • 2027: Sunday
  • 2028: Tuesday
  • 2029: Wednesday
  • 2030: Thursday
  • 2031: Friday
  • 2032: Sunday
  • 2033: Monday
  • 2034: Tuesday
  • 2035: Wednesday
  • 2036: Friday
  • 2037: Saturday

When is trick-or-treating in Northern Nevada this year?

Northern Nevadans may think that's an odd question — doesn't trick-or-treating always happen on Halloween night?

But actually, there was a trend across the country in 2019 to officially move Halloween to the last Saturday of October and label it National Trick or Treat Day. A petition to move the date got over 150,000 signatures. The day has also made it on the National Day Calendar.

The organizations pushing the change said the goal was to increase safety for children and help parents navigate a holiday that often lands on a school night.

The discussion of moving trick-or-treating has been floating around Northern Nevada since at least the 2010s, due to the overlap with the annual Nevada Day parade held on Oct. 31. The idea was to move trick-or-treating to the last Friday of October.

What else is special about Halloween 2026?

This year, Halloween night will actually get an extension, since daylight saving time ends Sunday, Nov. 1, 2026, the day after Halloween and Nevada Day.

Northern Nevadans can plan to turn their clocks back one hour, from 2 a.m. to 1 a.m., giving everyone an extra hour of sleep or an extra hour of festivities.

Daylight saving time began on the second Sunday in March and ends the first Sunday in November at 2 a.m. local time. Next year, it will begin on Sunday, March 14, 2027 and end on Sunday, Nov. 7, 2027.
