Simeon Brown responds to concerns over health care system

TheTools · 15.09.2026 19:33 · source · permalink

The health system has been under significant pressure this winter, with Auckland City Hospital saying it's been worse than expected. Just yesterday, Tauranga Hospital ED staff declared a "state of emergency", saying the ED was running up to 200% capacity, without the staffing needed. Health Minister Simeon Brown spoke to Ingrid Hipkiss.
