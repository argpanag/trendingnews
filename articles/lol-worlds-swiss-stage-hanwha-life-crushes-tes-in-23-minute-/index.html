LoL Worlds Swiss Stage — Hanwha Life Crushes TES in 23-Minute Rout

TheTools · 23.10.2026 19:09 · source · permalink

Hanwha Life Esports made a statement at the LoL Worlds Swiss Stage by completely dismantling Top Esports (TES) in a blistering 23-minute match.

Zeka’s 17-Kill Masterclass Destroys TES

Displaying flawless synergy and overwhelming firepower, Hanwha Life bounced back from early skirmishes to decisively secure their opening match in the Swiss stage. The team triumphed over TES with a staggering 24-7 kill score in just 23 minutes and 51 seconds at the Credit Union of Texas Event Center.

Kim ‘Zeka’ Geon-woo claimed the Player of the Series (POS) honors after delivering an incredible performance, racking up 17 kills, 1 death, and 5 assists on his champion.

Dominant Progression in the Swiss Stage

Opting for a dive-heavy team composition featuring Nocturne, Ambessa, Jhin, Shen, and Zeka’s pick, Hanwha Life maintained relentless pressure throughout the mid-game. After securing vital objectives including Rift Herald and three successive dragons, the team widened the gold gap past 10,000.

With Baron secured to seal the deal, Hanwha Life closed out the series with a 15,000 global gold advantage, advancing smoothly to the 1-0 bracket of the Swiss stage.
