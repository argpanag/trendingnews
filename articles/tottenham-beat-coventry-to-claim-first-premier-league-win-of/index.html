Yup, and Spurs have some winnable games coming up too. Chelsea away this weekend will be tricky but then comes Palace home, Leeds away, Ipswich home, Sunderland away, Fulham home. We’ll have a decent idea of the kind of season they’ll have at the end of that run.

The league table

FULL TIME: Tottenham Hotspur 3-1 Coventry City

A terrific night for Spurs, Maddison making a scoring return, Kulusevski also coming back, and a first league win of the season. They didn’t play especially well but they’ll be better for this, while Coventry again showed enough to suggest they’ve a chance of staying up.

90+7 min The third goal is, in a way, just reward for trying to score it – last week, Spurs benefited from a side too scared to pursue a clincher, a mistake they’ve not made themselves.

GOAL! Tottenham Hotspur 3-1 Coventry City (Davies 90+6)

After 148 days, Tottenham are going to win a league game! Tel does well to take time out of the game and, when his cross is cleared to the edge, an important touch from Solanke allows Davis to swipe a low trivela that arcs just inside the near post. Relief inside the ground!

90+5 min Tel keeps the ball in the corner, then his cross is cleared to the edge of the box…

90+4 min The delivery isn’t bad but Van de Ven heads clear at the front post and Spurs are halfway there.

90+3 min Coventry opt not to launch a long throw, instead putting a pass into the box and keeping the attack alive well before Mason-Clark wins a corner.

90+2 min Now Spurs attack, Tel looking to draw a curler into the far top corner and making out like he was close, but he wasn’t.

90+1 min From a seemingly impossible position close to the by-line and Van Hecke in front of him, Mason-Clark drives over a cross that Awoniyi attacks … but can’t quite reach.

90 min We’ll have eight additional minutes – just as there were when Spurs were trailing last weekend.

89 min Davies leaves one on Kesler-Hatden and Hamer sends the free-kick into the box, Thomas up, but Spurs clear and Kulusevski leads the counter alone, crossing way beyond the far post to no one.

88 min Kulusevski was given a proper welcome and his first touches are cheered around the ground; his heart will be doing happy hardcore beats.

87 min Coventry, meanwhile, send on Kesler-Hayden for Rudoni.

86 min After Maddison’s goal another affirming moment for Spurs, Marmoush going down with what might be cramp, Kulusevski replacing him. He gets the big welcome he deserves; let’s hope he can stay fit now, because if he can, he’s a proper player. Oh and Tel also comes on, with Kudus going off.

84 min Caoventry are now building the play with four men on the last line and Mason-Clark lays back for Dasilva whose cross isn’t bad, but Rudoni can’t quite crane his neck-muscles enough to head at goal.

83 min Spurs keep it well, burning time, but I bet there are some fans who half want them to concede now, so they’ve time to seek a winner. Such is the torment of a low-scoring sport.

81 min Davies nashes down the left and Spurs again move the ball right to Kudus. Again, he comes infield but this time he crosses and, when the clearance reaches Fernandes, he curls a sidefooter into Rushworth’s arms.

80 min Good work from Tonali, who weaves by a couple of challenges in midfield before the ball makes its way out to Kudus, who dips infield to open the angle for a curler, before sending his effort over the bar.

79 min Tonali humps out of play, yet more evidence Spurs are feeling this, and if they can’t rely on him to keep the head, who can they rely on? But they do get the long throw away and set off on the attack.

78 min Changes for Coventry, Mason-Clark and Hamer replacing Sakamoto and Thomas-Asante, who’s probably been their best player.

77 min Thomas-Asante intercepts a poor pass from Gallagher and seeks Rudoni, who can’t quite take the ball away from Van Hecke. Still, though, Spurs look nervous already, a feeling that’ll only intensify as we get nearer full time.

74 min I guess whatever colour Coventry’s kit is, it looks like what it’s meant to be. The first time I saw Sunderland’s pink Elvis top, not one part of me believed it not to be leisurewear.

73 min Van Ewijk robs Kudus deep inside the Spurs half and flights a cross to the back post … just over Rudoni. Coventry are playing with the belief that they can equalise.

72 min Play resumes with a Coventry throw, Van Eweijk hurling into the box and Spurs heading clear.

70 min Changes for Spurs, Davies for Robertson, Gallagher for Maddison – who enjoys the acclaim of the crowd as he departs – and Fernandes for the cramping Tosin. I guess they’ll go to 4-2-3-1.

68 min Coventry are building a bit of pressure, a cross-kick into the box allowing Thomas to leap all over Gray, heading back across and forcing Van de Ven to diving head away. Already, it’s a bit panicked and, with so long left, that is not the way spurs want to be defending.

67 min “Completely agree about how horrible Parma Violets taset,” says David Wall. “However they’re very useful when ground up if you want to colour the purple layer of a rainbow cake and haven’t got the right food dye. Fortunately the violet flavour is subtle enough that you can’t actually taste it in the cake.”

That is good information, thanks – though would one not use Fizzers to get the same effect and a bit more action?

65 min Coventry are indeed getting Rudoni into the game, as we said they’d have to; he’s drifting about unchecked and lasers a pass into the box that Awoniyi can’t quite get on the end of. As long as it stays 2-1, Coventry have a decent chance of sneaking a point.

64 min A fine long pass from Van Hecke sets Solanke at Thomas, right side of the box, and he chops inside well only to see the shooting lanes closed down, thwacking an effort that’s blocked.

64 min Tonali runs away from Rudoni, who slides in for the hell of it and snags an ankle; he’s booked, as he knew he would be.

62 min Credit to Spurs, by the way, who didn’t feel sorry for themselves after conceding but went after a second goal – which they ought really to have done straight after their first. They’ve not made that mistake this time, though, pushing hard for a clinching third.

61 min A double change for Coventry, Onyeka and Awoniyi for Yirenkyi and Cherif.

60 min There was proper contact but VAR deems it accidental, which I think is a bit of a touch for Spurs. I guess it’s one of those you’re vexed if you score it and it’s chalked off, but you’re also vexed if it’s scored against you and it isn’t, so it makes some sort of sense to go with the original call.

ANOTHER LOVELY GOAL! Tottenham Hotspur 2-1 Coventry City (Marmoush 59)

Marmoush spins Van Ewijk, introducing elbow to nose in the process, feints the shot then, stepping inside, punches a curler from 20 yards that Rushworth gets hands to but can’t keep out. That’s his first goal for Spurs, but will VAR disallow it?

56 min “Coventry’s is kit the same colour as Farage’s blazer on big paper’s lead story,” notes James Chambers.

What are we calling this? Innocent guv lilac?

55 min Again, Tonali seizes on a loose ball, swiping studs across it to evade a challenge before welting another shot over the top.

52 min We said Spurs would need a second goal and they’re seeking one, Kudus hitting the line and cutting back a decent cross, half-cleared to Tonali, charging in … and he hammers an effort that flies over the top.
