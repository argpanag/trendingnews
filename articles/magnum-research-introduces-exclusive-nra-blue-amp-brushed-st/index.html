Magnum Research Introduces Exclusive NRA Blue & Brushed Stainless Desert Eagle .50 AE

Magnum Research, Inc., maker of the world-renowned Desert Eagle pistol, is proud to announce a striking new custom edition produced exclusively for Chattanooga Shooting Supplies. Chambered in the legendary .50 Action Express cartridge, this powerhouse handgun pairs iconic big-bore performance with an eye-catching, multi-textured custom finish designed to stand out in any collection.

The exclusive edition features a vibrant Cerakote NRA Blue frame and slide, accented with contrasting matte black controls. Complementing the bold blue finish is a brushed stainless steel barrel equipped with an integral muzzle brake to significantly reduce felt recoil and muzzle flip. Built upon a precision-engineered steel frame, the pistol features an integral top Picatinny-style accessory rail for straightforward mounting of optics, fixed combat sights, and textured wrap-around rubber grips with finger grooves for optimal control during rapid fire.

Manufactured with pride in the United States, each pistol is built to the rigorous quality standards that have defined the Desert Eagle platform for decades. The custom NRA Blue .50 AE Desert Eagle ships complete with one 7-round magazine and is distributed exclusively through Chattanooga Shooting Supplies dealer network.

The manufacturer's suggested retail price is $2,186. For purchasing information, contact your local Chattanooga Shooting Supplies dealer.
