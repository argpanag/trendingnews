HALF-TIMEpublished at 20:53 BST
20:53 BST

Real Madrid 3-0 Rayo Vallecano

Real Madrid in total control after some sublime football with goals from Kylian Mbappe, Alvaro Carreras and Jude Bellingham.

Real Madrid's Kylian Mbappe has scored 34 goals in 36 home games in La Liga

Real Madrid moved level on points with Barcelona at the top of La Liga after thrashing city rivals Rayo Vallecano at the Santiago Bernabeu.

The home side bounced back from their shock defeat by Real Betis eight days earlier by racing into a three-goal lead inside the opening 35 minutes, making it four wins from their opening five matches of the new campaign.

Kylian Mbappe opened the scoring in the 14th minute when he sent Rayo goalkeeper Emil Audero the wrong way from the penalty spot after Alvaro Carreras was fouled inside the area.

The Frenchman, who earlier in the week said this could be the year he finally wins the Ballon d'Or,, external returned the favour to Carreras three minutes later by setting up the left-back to slam home and double his side's advantage.

England international Jude Bellingham added Real's third 10 minutes before half-time, side-footing Vinicius Jr's cut-back into the net at the end of a sweeping move.

Mbappe was denied a second goal of the night, and a sixth of the La Liga campaign, in first-half stoppage time when he rattled the woodwork with a fierce effort.

Although the visitors pulled one back early in the second half through Sergio Camello, it was Real who had the final say in stoppage when Mbappe fired low and hard into the far corner to ensure Real made it 14 wins from their last 15 home meetings with Vallecano.

Barcelona can re-establish a three-point lead over Real if they beat Levante on Sunday (15:15 BST).
