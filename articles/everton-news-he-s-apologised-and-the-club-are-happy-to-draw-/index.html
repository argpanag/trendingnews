Everton news: 'He's apologised and the club are happy to draw a line' - fan view on Grealish

TheTools · 23.11.2026 21:03 · source · permalink
  • Published
    16 November 2026

Everton are "happy to draw a line" under Jack Grealish's recent disciplinary issues, believes The Unholy Trinity podcast's Mike Richards.

The on-loan Manchester City playmaker missed the Toffees' two games against Newcastle United in the Premier League and Carabao Cup, as well as the win over Coventry City prior to the international break, following a "breach of club discipline".

The 31-year-old posted an apology to the club and fans on social media, saying he "can't wait to be back playing in front of you", and Richards is happy to move on.

On the apology, he told BBC Radio Merseyside: "Clever wording. It doesn't give us a great deal and he hasn't gone into any detail, and neither would he.

"The club have been very quiet about things - they did change their stance from 'unwell' against Arsenal to then he has breached club disciplines.

"With any player at the football club there are standards you have to be held by. It doesn't matter who you are and the football club have held him to the high standards. The manager has.

"He's apologised and the club are happy to draw a line it seems.

"You get on with it - it's as simple as that. Everton can't afford to have one of their 18 outfield players unavailable."
