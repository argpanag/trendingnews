Mike McCarthy's 'Ice Cream Sandwich' Assistant Detailed in NFL Rumors After Aaron Rodgers' Comments

TheTools · 25.11.2026 22:13 · source · permalink

It has been quite the Wednesday for Pittsburgh Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy, but he's at least reportedly got someone who can get him an ice cream sandwich if needed.

Following Aaron Rodgers' explosive interview on The Pat McAfee Show in which he heavily criticized McCarthy, former NFL player Breiden Fehoko took to social media to reveal one Steelers player said the coach "has a personal assistant who brings him ice cream sandwiches on demand."

It is yet another bizarre headline for McCarthy after Rodgers told McAfee he was done with the Steelers while also criticizing the coach for falling asleep in meetings, struggling to learn the game plan and failing to watch film, among other things (h/t ESPN).

Rodgers also said he learned from teammate Pat Freiermuth he was being benched and detailed a moment during Pittsburgh's loss to the Philadelphia Eagles that McCarthy said, "one, two, three Packers" in the locker room.

McCarthy used to coach the Green Bay Packers when Rodgers was also the team's quarterback.

As for Rodgers, NFL Network's Ian Rapoport reported the Steelers decided to waive him following the interview.
