US Open semi-finals 2026 recap: Alexander Zverev beats Karen Khachanov to reach final

TheTools · 12.09.2026 00:05 · source · permalink
  • Live Reporting (active)
  • Scores

Summary

  • French Open champion Alexander Zverev beats Karen Khachanov 6-3 7-6 (9-7) 7-6 (8-6) to reach second US Open final

  • Top seed Zverev is into a third Grand Slam final in a row, having lost Wimbledon final to Jannik Sinner

  • Germany's Zverev won maiden major in Paris earlier this year

  • Zverev will face either Frances Tiafoe or Ben Shelton in Sunday's showpiece

  • Tiafoe and Shelton bidding to become first American man in 23 years to win a Grand Slam singles title

  6. 'We were laughing at how bad I was playing'published at 23:28 BST 11 September
    23:28 BST 11 September

    Zverev 6-3 7-6 (9-7) 7-6 (8-6) Khachanov

    Alexander Zverev speaking on court after his win: "After the second round, at about 3am I was sitting with my team in the locker room and we were laughing at how bad I was playing, and that sense of humour helped me.

    "In practice I started feeling the ball better, and then moving forward I was playing much better tennis. I obviously have improved a bit otherwise I would not be in the US Open final.

    "Sometimes things just go your way, I have had a lot of struggles at the major level and I was so close so many times - and maybe this year God has said this is going to be my year and to enjoy my time on the court. I am enjoying it, I love playing these big finals and I can't wait for Sunday."

    23:25 BST 11 September

    Hewett/Reid 2-6 2-2 De la Puente/Spaargaren

    British duo Alfie Hewett and Gordon Reid are going to have to do it the hard way if they're to claim a sixth US Open title in the men's wheelchair doubles.

    The top seeds have just lost the first set 6-2 to Spain's Martin de la Puente and Ruben Spaargaren of the Netherlands on Court 11.

    De la Puente and Spaargaren won six games on the trot to take the opening set.

    But Hewett and Reid did come from behind to win yesterday's huge semi-final against defending champions Argentina's Gustavo Fernandez and Tokito Oda and they're going to need to do it again.
