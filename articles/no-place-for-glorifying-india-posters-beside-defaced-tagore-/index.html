No place for glorifying India: Posters beside defaced Tagore mural in Bangladesh

TheTools · 07.11.2026 08:44 · source · permalink

Rabindranath Tagore's mural at Khulna Railway Station was defaced with black ink, while posters declaring there was "no place for glorifying India" appeared beside it. Police have launched an investigation.
