Match Preview

Austin FC host Portland Timbers at Q2 Stadium in Austin in a Western Conference MLS fixture that matters considerably for both clubs as the regular season enters its final stages.

Davy Arnaud's side arrive into this one in difficult form. Austin have lost four of their last five MLS matches, including a 4-1 defeat to LA Galaxy in their most recent outing, and their playoff hopes are growing thinner with each passing week. Sitting 14th in the Western Conference, the pressure on the home side is acute.

Portland arrive with momentum behind them. Marti Cifuentes's side have won three of their last five matches, including back-to-back victories over Charlotte FC and St. Louis City, and they beat Houston Dynamo FC 3-1 in their most recent outing. The Timbers are eighth in the West and firmly in the playoff conversation.

For Austin, this is a game they cannot afford to lose. A home defeat would all but extinguish their postseason ambitions and heap further pressure on Arnaud's squad with little time left to recover.

Portland, by contrast, will travel with confidence. Their recent run of results has turned what looked like a difficult season into a genuine push for the postseason, and a win in Austin would strengthen their position further.
