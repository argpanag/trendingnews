SK Hynix Record Profit Lifts Micron, Nvidia and AI Chip Stocks

TheTools · 28.10.2026 23:07 · source · permalink

Micron Technology Inc. (MU, Financials), the memory-chip maker, rose alongside other semiconductor stocks after SK Hynix reported record quarterly profit and announced a major shareholder-return plan.

Following significantly better-than-expected results, SK Hynix stock in South Korea jumped 10.8 percent.

Increased investor optimism in the memory cycle was accompanied by the announcement of over $25 billion in dividends and share repurchases by the corporation.

Names associated with AI, memory, equipment, and data center infrastructure were among those lifted as the response rippled across the semiconductor sector.

Since SK Hynix is a formidable rival in the high-bandwidth memory and advanced DRAM markets, the read-through holds great significance for Micron.

As the need for high-performance memory grows in AI systems, the company's strong profitability indicates that market circumstances are favorable in terms of both price and demand.

Spending on AI infrastructure is expected to continue supporting chipmakers and suppliers, which is reflected in the larger move.

The next factor to consider is if Micron's own financials and predictions continue to indicate that robust demand for artificial intelligence and memory is continuing into the following quarter.
