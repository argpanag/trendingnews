Status report

The Canucks held an optional morning skate. ... Bains is expected to enter the lineup for Quillan, a forward ... After missing two games with a lower-body injury, Willander is expected to replace Brisebois, a defenseman. ... Schenn is expected to come in for Mancini, a defenseman. ... Hyman returns after missing five games with a neck injury, replacing Dach, a forward, who wasn't on the ice for the morning skate. ... Frederic comes in for Hallander, a forward. ... Emberson replaces Walman. ... Levi will play after missing the past seven games with an undisclosed injury, while Jarry will dress after sitting out Edmonton's previous game due to illness. ... The Oilers assigned goalie Matt Tomkins to Bakersfield of the American Hockey League, while emergency backup goaltender Brett Epp was inactivated after dressing for one game. ... Defenseman Atro Leppanen was placed waivers for the purpose of being assigned to the AHL.
