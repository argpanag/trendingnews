‘America’s Got Talent’ Finals recap: 10 acts perform for the $1 million prize

For the America's Got Talent Season 21 finale, the top 10 acts take the stage for their last chance to win the $1 million prize and be named "AGT Champion." While judges Howie Mandel, Mel B, Sofia Vergara, and Simon Cowell had a hand in selecting a few of them as Golden Buzzer acts along the way, the American audience will have final say in deciding who is the most talented act in the country.

During the quarterfinals, four of the 10 acts were chosen by the judges to advance straight to the finale by receiving Live Golden Buzzers. They are: Korean magician group Hundred Fingers, teenage singer Lai Noelle, Thai guitarist Nene Royal, and Chinese robot dance group Unitree.

The other six acts earned their spot by placing in the top three of America's vote during the quarterfinals and in the top six in the semifinals. They are: Canadian dancing dog group Acro Canine Crew, Korean dance crew Come Here, magician Geno Ploeger from Arizona, dog trainer Olivia Befus from Michigan, light-up dancers Royal Lasers from Las Vegas, and aerialist Veronika Goroshkova also from Las Vegas.
