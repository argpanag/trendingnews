  14. Leeds response will tell us a lotpublished at 15:12 BST 17 September
    15:12 BST 17 September

    Newcastle v Hull City (Sat, 15:00 BST)

    Ciaran Kelly
    BBC Sport reporter

    It was one of Newcastle United's compartively shorter bus journeys home, but you suspect it did not feel that way on Monday night.

    Not after a bruising 4-1 defeat against Leeds United at Elland Road.

    No wonder head coach Matthias Jaissle swore in his post-match news conference to illustrate how low he felt after his first defeat.

    This was a night where Newcastle's tendancy to give up chances and their failure to create enough opportunities caught up with them following a five-game unbeaten run in all competitions.

    But what was most startling was how the visitors were bullied by rampant Leeds.

    There will be plenty of ups and downs for this young side this season, but a response is sorely needed for the visit of in-form Hull City at the weekend.

    Given the number of injuries Newcastle have at the moment - defender Dan Burn, midfielder Joelinton and forwards William Osula and Anthony Elanga are among those sidelined - it will fall to the majority of the players who floundered at Elland Road to bounce back.

    We will learn a lot more about them and, indeed, Jaissle by how they react.
