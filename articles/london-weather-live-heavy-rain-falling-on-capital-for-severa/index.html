London weather LIVE: Heavy rain falling on capital 'for several hours' as commuters share reactions - AOL

TheTools · 09.09.2026 09:04 · source · permalink

London is facing its most rain in months as millions of commuters battled wet weather over morning rush-hour.

A band of rain pushed across the capital overnight into Tuesday, drenching the capital’s streets and parks.

According to forecasts, the rain is expected to continue for much of the day, although BBC Weather’s Simon King suggested it would not be enough to alleviate the record drought, which has sparked hosepipe bands across the country including in London.

Londoners are taking to social media to share video and pictures of the rare heavy rain.

What is the forecast for the rest of the week?

10:45 , Tom Place

After today’s washout, more settled weather is expected in London with largely dry days through to the weekend and temperature peaks of between 20C and 23C.

The outlook after that looks reasonably good for London, with the Met Office's long-range forecast predicting "a trend toward some longer dry interludes between systems as we head toward mid-September", although a return of the dry, settled weather of the summer "looks unlikely".

Looking towards the end of September and into October, the Met Office predicts "a continuation of more settled conditions", before "more changeable" weather develops.

"Temperatures will likely be a little above average overall, with relatively cool nights possible at first tending to reduce in frequency later in the period," it says.

'Really quite soggy'

10:34 , Tom Place

Speaking on Radio 4’s Today earlier this morning, BBC Weather Centre forecaster Simon King described today’s conditions as “really quite soggy”.

“We’ve had some rain overnight across England and Wales. It’s useful rain but it’s not significant rain to alleviate the drought situation for most of us,” he added.
