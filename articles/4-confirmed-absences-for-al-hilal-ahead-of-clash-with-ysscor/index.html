4 confirmed absences for Al-Hilal ahead of clash with… | YSscores

TheTools · 04.09.2026 14:13 · source · permalink

Al-Hilal face a significant blow ahead of their clash with Al-Shabab, with four players confirmed to miss the fifth-round derby in the Saudi Pro League, scheduled for today, Friday.

Al-Hilal will be without Frenchman Théo Hernandez due to a muscle injury, ruling him out of consideration for the coaching staff during the upcoming match against Al-Shabab.

Yusuf Akçiçek will also miss the match due to knee pain, while Ali Lajami remains sidelined with a hamstring injury.

The team will also be without their captain, Salem Al-Dosari, who will miss the derby due to injury, bringing the confirmed absences to four players.

Al-Hilal face a tough test due to consecutive absences, especially as the match holds significant importance for both teams in the race of the fifth round of the Saudi Pro League.

The coaching staff needs to prepare suitable alternatives to compensate for the absent players while maintaining the team's balance and ability to cope with the strength of Al-Shabab during the match.

In contrast, Al-Shabab enter the match knowing the extent of the absences in their opponent's ranks, which may give them the opportunity to enhance their prospects and achieve a positive result in the derby.

Al-Hilal face Al-Shabab today, Friday, in the 5th round of the Saudi Pro League, in a match that has garnered significant public interest due to the rivalry between the two teams.
