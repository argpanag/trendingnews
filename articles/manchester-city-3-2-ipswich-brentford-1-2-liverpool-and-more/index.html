Final score: Brentford 1-2 Liverpool

And it’s all over at Brentford, who have lost a game for the first time this season. Two red cards for the home side, two goals for the visitors, three points for Liverpool.

Final score: Manchester City 3-2 Ipswich

The final whistle blows in Manchester, where City bank three more points.

But Liverpool should have ended it! Barcola is played into space on the left, but having fluffed a one-on-one in the first half it seems he just doesn’t believe in himself enough to go for goal. Instead he delays, and passes inside to Koumas, who is crowded out before he can shoot.

Still Keith Andrews wants his team to attack! Koumas ends the latest Liverpool counter with a hard, low, but straight-at-the-keeper 20-yarder, and Brentford will keep attacking!

Another update from Simon McMahon: “Dundee United get a point at Perth, but will be disappointed not to take all three, after the Tangerines sweet midfield duo of (Dylan) Tait and Lyall (Cameron) dominated proceedings for much of the 90 minutes. Still, a point’s a point, and United remain in the top six.”

Red card! Brentford go down to nine men!

Ngumoha leads another Liverpool break, but he’s eventually brought down on the edge of the Brentford penalty area by Vitaly Janelt. Janelt has already been booked once for dissent, and that’s the end of his game!

Joe Gomez very slowly takes the ball down the right as the clock ticks to 90 minutes at Brentford. Six minutes’ stoppage time to play there.

Liverpool are no longer in control at Brentford, with the home side committing numbers forward in search of an equaliser. Then they get a chance to break, four on two, only for Barcola to linger on the ball and get robbed of it!

Final score: Fulham 1-0 Hull City

A first win of the season for Fulham! Three precious points lift the Cottagers, temporarily at least, out of the bottom three. Zero shots on target from Hull today.

They’re in the sixth of three minutes’ stoppage time at Craven Cottage, with Vaz now limping off. At Manchester City 12 – twelve! – minutes of stoppage time have been indicated. And at Brentford they’re only in the 86th minute, given all the first-half stoppage time played.

Red card! Fulham go down to 10 men!

Sessegnon has been shown a straight red card for a foul on Robinio Vaz, after catching the Hull player on the heel with a pretty feeble challenge that was made with no hope of or attempt at winning the ball.

GOAL! Manchester City 3-2 Ipswich (Flemming, 90 mins)

Ipswich are back in the game! Donnarumma dives to his right as Zian Flemming’s shot flies down the middle of his goal, and there’s just one in it as the game goes into stoppage time!

Penalty to Ipswich!

The VAR has got involved again, this time to tell the referee to look at Guehi’s challenge on Abdul Fatawu, and the penalty is given!

And while that was happening Barcola hit the bar for Liverpool, with a nice (but not nice enough) floating curler.

Disallowed! Still Manchester City 3-1 Ipswich!

Turns out Fernandez was offside, so VAR intervenes to chalk the goal off and 3-1 it remains.

GOAL! Manchester City 4-1 Ipswich (Haaland, 85 mins)

Great save! And a wonderful goalline clearance! And a hat-trick for Haaland! Enzo Fernandez has the first chance, Ndiaye has the second, but Haaland isn’t going to miss his!

Queen of the South’s comeback has stalled somewhat. Having fought back from 5-0 down to make it, um, 5-1, Brian Graham has scored twice since then, the latest from the spot, to make it 7-1.

From nowhere, a chance for Brentford! A low cross from the left to Igor Thiago, inside the six-yard box, but though he gets in front of Van Dijk he doesn’t get anything on it at all, and the ball is cleared!

GOAL! Brentford 1-2 Liverpool (Isak, 70 mins)

And now Liverpool lead! A cross from the right beyond the pack post, a lovely header down from Barcola to Isak – where most players would have gone for goal, from an unpromising angle – and the striker, in no space at all, manages to take a touch before shooting low across goal and in off a defender!

For the first 15 minutes of the second half, despite being a man up, Liverpool did little to suggest they were capable of penning Brentford back. But the goal has changed all that, and the pressure is now asphyxiating. But Liverpool aren’t actually creating much with it. 20 minutes plus stoppages to play there.

Thriller of the day: Kilmarnock were 1-0 up against Motherwell in the second minute, 2-1 down in the 56th, and they’ve just gone 3-2 up! Also in Scotland, Queen of the South have got on the scoresheet and now trail by just the four goals, 1-5 down at home to goal-crazy Ross County.

GOAL! Manchester City 3-1 Ipswich (Haaland, 69 mins)

An impeccable finish, inevitably, from Haaland surely makes the points safe for City!

GOAL! Brentford 1-1 Liverpool (Munoz, 62 mins)

Liverpool have their equaliser! Victor Munoz is played down the right, chops inside Lewis-Potter and strokes a left-footed shot inside the far post!

Team sheets have landed for the evening Premier League game, and here are the big names:

Newcastle: Hornicek, Miley, Thiaw, Botman, Hall, Gonzalez, Joelinton, Willock, Wissa, Barnes, Fernandez-Pardo. Subs: Pope, Schar, Bamba, Osula, Steur, Toure, Murphy, Burn, Dedic.
Aston Villa: Suzuki, Cash, Lindelof, Mings, Wan-Bissaka, Kamara, Joao Gomes, Alysson, Buendia, Manzambi, Jackson. Subs: Bizot, Harwood-Bellis, Barkley, McGinn, Torres, Mbaye, Bogarde, Goretzka, Madjo.
Referee: Jarred Gillett.

“In some regards, Frimpong reminds me of Reece James, and not in a good way,” writes Joe Pearson. “Seems like every time he gets on the pitch, the next shot we see of him is sitting down getting ready to be subbed off for injury. He appears to have a lot of potential, but we never get to see it. Sigh.” Today has been pretty ugly for him, very much putting the pong into Frimpong. Another layoff beckons.

Liverpool are being forced into another substitution – and the half-time substitute Frimpong is going off again! His six-minute cameo is over, and Rio Ngumoha is on!

Frimpong, with one of his first touches, pushes the ball down the right flank and takes on Sepp van den Berg for pace. It should be easy, against a lumbering 6ft 4in centre-half, but Van den Berg wins the race.

Manchester City have also been forced into a change because of a midfield injury: Kovacic has gone off, and Enzo Fernandez is on.

Liverpool are making a halftimely change, with Frimpong coming on. Brentford are going to make two. Thanks to all that first-half stoppage time they’re yet to get back going there (while Fulham, who only had a single added minute at the end of the first half, are already 10 minutes into their second half).

Second halves are ongoing and goals already flying in: the league-doubling department has seen Birmingham go 2-0 up at home to QPR (Liam Millar with the goal) and Salford 2-0 up at home to Crawley (Macaulay Langstaff).

A brief half-time report from voluntary Scotland correspondent Simon McMahon: “Goals for every team in the Scottish Premier at half time, Simon, apart of course from Dundee United who, despite enjoying the better of the first 45, trail 1-0 at St. Johnstone. Elsewhere it’s Aberdeen 1-1 St. Mirren, Hibs 1-2 Falkirk and Motherwell 1-1kilmarnock. And Ross County have five at the break.”

“Hello Simon,” writes Kári Tulinius. Hello to you!. “To my admittedly amateur eyes, Manchester City haven’t been playing very well, with Ipswich clearly the better team. However, City have plenty of players who can create chances out of nothing, and forwards who can score from very little. That’s what shopping with with a bottomless purse buys you.” Not playing very well, perhaps, but certainly doing enough to be deservedly ahead. But as to how they’re doing it, yes that helps.
