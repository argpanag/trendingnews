Music composer Anirudh Ravichander and Sunrisers Hyderabad CEO Kavya Maran are once again making headlines over their rumoured wedding. According to the latest social media reports, the two are expected to tie the knot in Greece on November 11, 2026, followed by a grand reception in Chennai on November 14. Reports also claim that Kavya's father, Sun Pictures chairman Kalanithi Maran, has begun inviting prominent personalities to the celebrations. However, the wedding dates and arrangements have not been officially confirmed.

Popular music composer Anirudh Ravichander and businesswoman Kavya Maran have been at the centre of wedding speculation for several months. Now, fresh reports suggest that the rumoured couple could be preparing for a destination wedding in Greece next month.

According to an Instagram post shared by Telugu360, Anirudh and Kavya are reportedly planning to marry on November 11, 2026, with a reception scheduled for November 14 at a five-star hotel in Chennai.

The post further claims that Kavya's father, media entrepreneur Kalanithi Maran, has personally invited actor Vijay and former Tamil Nadu Chief Minister MK Stalin to the celebrations.

Neither Anirudh nor Kavya has publicly confirmed these details.

The latest reports suggest that the wedding ceremony could take place in Greece on November 11, while the couple may host a reception for guests in Chennai three days later.

The reported arrangements have attracted attention because both Anirudh and Kavya belong to prominent families associated with the entertainment and business industries.

Earlier reports in August had also suggested that the two were planning to marry in Greece after Diwali, although November 10 was initially mentioned as a possible date.

The exact wedding date, venue and guest list remain unverified.

According to Telugu360, Kalanithi Maran has reportedly started extending invitations for the celebrations.

The social media post claims that Vijay and MK Stalin are among the prominent personalities who have been personally invited.

Kalanithi Maran is the chairman of Sun Group, which operates Sun Pictures and owns the IPL franchise Sunrisers Hyderabad.

His daughter Kavya Maran is closely associated with the franchise and regularly attracts attention during IPL matches and player auctions.

However, no official wedding invitation or confirmation from either family has been made public.

The speculation surrounding Anirudh and Kavya gained momentum in June 2026 after Anirudh's uncle, actor and theatre personality Y Gee Mahendra, spoke about the composer's marriage in an interview.

Mahendra suggested that a wedding was on the cards and praised Kavya for her ability to manage a major cricket franchise.

"From what I've heard, they are going to get married," he said.

His remarks fuelled speculation about the relationship, although neither Anirudh nor Kavya subsequently announced an engagement or wedding.

Interestingly, Anirudh had previously addressed speculation surrounding his personal life.

In 2025, when reports about his alleged relationship with Kavya began circulating online, the composer responded on X.

"Marriage ah? lol .. Chill out guys pls stop spreading rumours," he wrote.

Despite that response, rumours about the two continued, particularly after his uncle's remarks and subsequent reports of a possible destination wedding.

Anirudh, who has composed music for films including Jawan, Jailer and Jailer 2, remains one of the most prominent music directors in Indian cinema.

For now, the reported November 11 wedding in Greece and November 14 reception in Chennai remain unconfirmed, with fans awaiting an official announcement from Anirudh Ravichander, Kavya Maran or their families.
