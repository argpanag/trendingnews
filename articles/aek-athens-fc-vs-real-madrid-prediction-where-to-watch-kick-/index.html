AEK Athens FC - Searching for consistency

Here’s a closer look at AEK Athens FC as they prepare for a huge European night. We’ll delve into their recent, somewhat inconsistent, form and analyse how they might set up for this challenge against Real Madrid. We will also consider the probable AEK Athens FC vs. Real Madrid line-ups for this crucial encounter.

Recent form

AEK Athens FC has shown mixed form recently, displaying both attacking strength and defensive vulnerabilities. They enter this match after consecutive domestic losses to Panathinaikos in the Super League and PAOK in the Greek Cup. This contrasts sharply with their commanding 5-0 league victory over Panetolikos, indicating a struggle for consistent performances.

Their UEFA Champions League campaign has been inconsistent. The Greek side has been formidable at the OPAP Arena, winning both home fixtures without conceding a goal. However, their away performances have been disappointing, with heavy defeats against Manchester City and Shakhtar Donetsk, raising concerns about their road competitiveness.

Key attackers Barnabás Varga and R. Marin, who have each scored twice in the competition, will be crucial for AEK. A significant boost for the hosts is having a full squad with no reported injuries, which could be vital for this challenging European encounter.

Real Madrid - On a winning streak

Let’s review Real Madrid’s current situation. We will analyse the team’s momentum, their expectations and standout players for the trip to Athens.

Recent form

Real Madrid arrive in Athens in formidable form, boasting a six-match winning streak across all competitions. This impressive run includes significant victories in Spain’s Primera Division, such as a 3-0 triumph over Racing Santander and a win against rivals Barcelona. Overall, they have secured 18 victories in their last 21 games.

Their UEFA Champions League campaign has been flawless, with the Spanish giants winning all three group stage matches to earn nine points. They have showcased potent attacking power, scoring ten goals while conceding five in victories over RB Leipzig, AS Roma, and Internazionale.

A key factor in their success is the stellar form of attacker Kylian Mbappé, who has netted three goals in the competition. Real Madrid also travel with a full squad, as no injuries are reported, providing them with a significant advantage for this upcoming game.
