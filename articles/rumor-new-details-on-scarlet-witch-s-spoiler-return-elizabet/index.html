Rumor: New Details On Scarlet Witch's [Spoiler] Return & Elizabeth Olsen's Avengers: Doomsday Status

TheTools · 17.11.2026 18:00 · source · permalink

We have some new details on Elizabeth Olsen's recent return as the Scarlet Witch, and the character's status heading into Avengers: Doomsday and Secret Wars...

The season finale of VisionQuest is now streaming, and "Forever" left viewers with plenty to discuss - especially after the inclusion of a surprise post-credits scene.

We're sure most of you have seen the episode by now, but just in case, here's your spoiler warning.

Following a brief stinger teasing Jocasta's return, we cut to the events of Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, as we saw Wanda Maximoff (Elizabeth Olsen) burying herself under the rubble of Mt. Wundagore.

A shadowy Doctor Doom (who was indeed voiced by Robert Downey Jr.) then "resurrects" the former Avenger, while making it very clear that he intends to claim the Scarlet Witch's vast powers for his own.

This scene did not play after the screener episode critics were sent, and according to Daniel Rictman, it was included at the last minute because it was only filmed fairly recently.

Not too surprisingly, it also sounds like Wanda will have a minor role in Doomsday, but a much more significant part to play in Secret Wars.

"The Wanda post-credits scene was shot only recently, after they decided to add Olsen to Avengers: Doomsday. Olsen's stunt double shot some other stuff lately, and then Olsen herself. Some of it will be used for Secret Wars."

Vision and Wanda are both expected to appear in Avengers: Doomsday, but we probably won't see the full family reunion we're all waiting for until Avengers: Secret Wars - and even then, the Scarlet Witch may not be in control of her actions.

In a recent interview with Collider, showrunner Terry Matalas debunked the rumor that "Ghost" Agatha Harkness (Kathryn Hahn) was originally going to appear.

"I don't know that we could have afforded that. [Laughs] So not really, no. We always knew that reunion was going to be, and how much of it, and how early — what would be right for Tommy, what would be right for Billy, what would be right for the family story. But I feel like we got the proper emotional reunion for them. And I think the way that his return impacts Vision — without being too spoilerific — is important."

Marvel has also shared a new (unofficial) VisionQuest poster featuring Wanda.
