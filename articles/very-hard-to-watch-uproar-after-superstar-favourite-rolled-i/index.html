‘Very hard to watch’: Uproar after superstar favourite rolled in $10m showcase in dramatic fashion

TheTools · 31.10.2026 06:48 · source · permalink

Superstar mare Sheza Alibi has been rolled in sensational fashion in the $10 million Golden Eagle, which was taken out by Skyhook.

Sheza Alibi went around a $1.50 favourite but was cluttered up between runners back in the field and jockey Luke Nolen couldn’t find clear air at all and she finished towards the rear.

“That was very, very hard to watch if you were on Sheza Alibi,” Bruce McAvaney said on Channel 7.

Sheza Alibi’s trainer Peter Moody couldn’t hide his frustration when interviewed post race.

“We had a barrier trial didn’t we? Long way to come,” Moody said.

“You can’t jump over them. Every other race it’s opened up but it didn’t in this race.”

Punters were happy to voice their displeasure with Nolen’s ride on social media following the race.

Chad Schofield piloted Skyhook to victory, replacing the suspended Kerrin McEvoy.

It’s been a fruitful spring for the jockey after collecting the Everest-Golden Eagle double, the two richest races in Australia.

VICTORA DERBY

Chris Waller claimed a fourth Victoria Derby win when Stormy Marco proved much too strong for his opposition at Flemington on Saturday.

Stormy Marco joined Waller’s previous winners – Preferment (2014), Manzoice (2022) and Riff Rocket (2023).

Stormy Marco came from well back, coming widest on the turn and he surged to the front to give his jockey James McDonald his third win in the 2500 metre blue riband.

Stormy Marco defeated the Gai Waterhouse and Adrian Bott-trained Cold Shower with Quick Finish from Tony and Calvin McEvoy’s yard in third.

The win was Waller’s first Group 1 success of the season with powerhouse team of three-year-old colts.

Stormy Marco’s win took his earnings to more than $2.1 million with two wins from eight starts.

Chris Waller Racing, Mulcaster Bloodstock and Hermitage Thoroughbreds paid $300,000 for Stormy Marco at the 2025 Inglis Australian Easter Yearling Sale.

COOLMORE STUD STAKES

The Next Episode produced a withering turn of foot to win the Group 1 Coolmore Stud Stakes at Flemington on Saturday.

The Ciaron Maher-trained colt made it three straight wins and five from seven starts when exploding to the front and holding off the late challengers.

The Next Episode defeated Chris Waller’s St Gotthard with Mbube in the third.

A son of Snitzel, The Next Episode was purchased for $2.8 million at the 2025 Magic Millions Gold Coast Yearling Sale while St Gotthard cost his connections $2.7 million at the Inglis Australian Easter Yearling Sale.

The Next Episode was ridden to victory my Mark Zahra and was Maher’s fifth Group 1 success of the season.

The $1.2 million of prizemoney took The Next Episode’s earnings to more than $2.1 million.
