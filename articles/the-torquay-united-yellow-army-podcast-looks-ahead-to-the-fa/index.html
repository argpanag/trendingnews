The Torquay United Yellow Army podcast looks ahead to the FA Cup

TheTools · 17.09.2026 15:39 · source · permalink

It's an FA Cup weekend and we all love one of those - but this week's Yellow Army Podcast is not a full one with all of your favourites away. So it is left to 'The Short One' Richard Hughes to ask the question at Jimmy Ball's weekly press conference in a much meaner episode today.

After the cracking game that was Torquay 2 Dorking 2 last weekend, Torquay welcome AFC Stoneham to Plainmoor in the second qualifying round of the nation's favourite cup competition - and Jimmy tells us how the squad is, and what to expect from the visitors from Hampshire.

Next week, when everyone is back together again, we shall see if the game was a cake walk, or a banana skin.... Come on you Yellows!
