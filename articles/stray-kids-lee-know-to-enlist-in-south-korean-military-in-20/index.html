Stray Kids' Lee Know to enlist in South Korean military in 2027

TheTools · 05.11.2026 14:21 · source · permalink

Lee Know of Stray Kids is set to enlist in the South Korean military soon, his agency JYP Entertainment announced.

According to the agency’s statement as published by Soompi, the idol is set to fulfill his military service in the first quarter of 2027.

“As a result, he may be unable to participate in some of the activities scheduled for the first quarter of 2027. However, prior to his enlistment, he will continue to connect with STAY through various content and individual activities,” JYP said.

Lee Know is the first member of Stray Kids to enlist in the military.

“We understand that this news may come as a surprise to many STAY, and we appreciate your kind understanding,” the agency said.

More details about his enlistment have yet to be announced.

JYP expressed their gratitude to STAY for their love and support for Stray Kids’ journey since their debut, and hopes this will continue.

Lee Know debuted with Stray Kids in 2017. Their songs include “God’s Menu,” “Maniac,” and “Chk Chk Boom.”

—Nika Roque/CDC, GMA News
