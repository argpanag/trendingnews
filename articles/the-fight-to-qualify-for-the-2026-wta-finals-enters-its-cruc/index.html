The fight to qualify for the 2026 WTA Finals enters its crucial phase

TheTools · 13.10.2026 21:11 · source · permalink

There are two tickets left to obtain for the Masters tournament, and there are four strong candidates. 

Ending a season among the eight best tennis players in the world is too enticing a prize to not give it your all in pursuit. This is what several players will do in the coming weeks, aware of how important it would be for them to be at the WTA Finals 2026. How will the battle for the last two spots unfold?

There are just three weeks left of competition on the women's circuit, and the WTA 1000 Wuhan 2026, taking place starting this Monday, the 12th, is set to be crucial in the fight for one of the two tickets that are still up for grabs for Indian Wells. It will be there that the Masters tournament is held, where six players have already secured their spots.

Here's how the race for the WTA Finals 2026 stands

Elena Rybakina, Aryna Sabalenka, Mirra Andreeva, Jessica Pegula, Cori Gauff, and Linda Noskova can now breathe easy knowing they are qualified. The case of the Czech player is particular as, although she is ranked eighth, her presence is mathematically secured as a Grand Slam champion, being enough to finish among the top 20 if she has won a title of that magnitude this season.

Thus, the two remaining spots have several candidates. Elina Svitolina and Karolina Muchova are the best positioned, but a misstep in Wuhan would greatly increase the chances for Iga Swiatek, who is under pressure, and Marta Kostyuk. Both are aware that they must maximize their opportunities, which is why they are also entered in the tournaments in Ningbo and Tokyo, both WTA 500 events.

There will be special media interest to see if Iga Swiatek can regain her best level, as it would have seemed almost impossible at the start of the year for the Polish player not to finish among the best. Karolina Muchova seems the least worried about the matter, given that her schedule only includes the Wuhan tournament, although it wouldn’t be surprising if she requested an invitation to another tournament in case of early elimination.
