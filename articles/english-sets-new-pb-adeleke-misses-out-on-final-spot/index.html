English sets new PB, Adeleke misses out on final spot

TheTools · 13.09.2026 16:58 · source · permalink

Getty

Mark English set a new personal best at the World Athletics Ultimate Championship in Budapest as he finished sixth in the men's 800m final.

The Donegal man posted a time of 1:42.78 in a rapid race having broken 1.43 for the first time (1:42.97) at a Diamond League meeting in London in July.

The time also secures him the qualification standard time required for the 2027 World Championships in Beijing, something already achieved by fellow Irish athletes Sarah Healy, Kate O'Connor and the women's 4x400m relay team.

English, who claimed European gold in Birmingham in August, won his semi-final on Saturday but was always facing a real ask to finish among the top three in a packed field; understandable given the inaugural biennial invitational had a prize pot of $10m (€8.67m).

It was Algeria's Djamel Sedjati who took the $150,000 on offer for the winner with a lightning pace of 1:41.91 while the race itself saw two PBs (English and Yanis Meziane), a seasonal best (Eliott Crestan) and a new national record for Italian Francesco Pernici.

English was towards the back of the field for the majority of the race, but his strong kick saw him finish strongly to just edge France's Meziane on the line.

He goes away with $14,000 for his efforts.

Rhasidat Adeleke missed out on a spot in the final of the women's 200m after a fifth-place semi-final finish on Sunday.

The Tallaght athlete was running in the first semi-final, a quality field featuring the likes of Paris Olympic champion Gabby Thomas, who won in a time of 21.88, and five-time world champion Melissa Jefferson-Wooden with the top four progressing.

Adeleke was always a step back from the leaders throughout, but with 50 metres remaining looked like she could sneak into fourth. However, her time of 22.81 was behind American trio Thomas, Jefferson-Wooden (22.15) and Kayla White (22.28), as well as Switzerland's Fabienne Hoenke (22.72).

Sharlene Mawdsley was not involved in the women's 400m final on Saturday. She failed to qualify from the semi-finals on Friday.
