Rubio says Kim Jong Un wants nukes for regime survival, not to support invasions | NK News

TheTools · 16.11.2026 06:53 · source · permalink

Get behind the headlines

North Korea’s nuclear ambitions should be viewed differently from Iran because leader Kim Jong Un seeks regime survival rather than territorial expansion, Secretary of State Marco Rubio said.

Speaking at a convention in Washington on Thursday, Rubio emphasized the need to prevent Iran from acquiring nuclear weapons, contrasting Tehran’s ambitions with those of Pyongyang.

“He just wants to survive in his regime, and I’m not excusing the terrible things that have happened there,” Rubio said, referring to Kim. “I’m just saying. But he’s not expansionist. He’s not looking to, like, invade Japan and invade China, whatever.”
