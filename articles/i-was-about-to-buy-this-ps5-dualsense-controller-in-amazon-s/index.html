I Was About To Buy This PS5 DualSense Controller In Amazon’s Black Friday Sale, But Spotted Something Even Better

TheTools · 20.11.2026 10:45 · source · permalink

Perfect if you're aiming to start fresh with GTA 6.

GTA 6 is finally here, and what’s a fantastic bit of timing for a sale: both Amazon and PlayStation have dropped their controller prices amongst their early Black Friday offerings.

With Grand Theft Auto VI out on PS5, those looking to pick up a second controller or replace a worn-out DualSense have landed on some of the best prices we've seen in 2026. Black Friday is still over a week away, but both Amazon UK and PlayStation have already gone below £45 on Sony's gamepad — and one doesn't require a paid membership.

Save On This PS5 Controller, Perfect For GTA 6

PS5 DualSense Controller (various colours)

Right now, you can get a brand-new PlayStation DualSense Wireless Controller for just £42.99 at Amazon UK, rather than the usual £64.99 RRP. That's £22 off, or approximately 34%, and the lowest price we've tracked for the controller so far. Better yet, it's sold and dispatched by Amazon itself, not one of the site's third-party marketplace sellers.

Along with the default white model, the £42.99 Black Friday price extends to Midnight Black, Cosmic Red, Nova Pink, and Starlight Blue.

Several other finishes are discounted too, though some are a tad more expensive; the Rhythm Blue version, for example, is now £47.23 (from £74.99) — sporting a striking black and light blue colour combination.

PlayStation Plus members can save even more

PS5 DualSense Controller (various colours)

If you’re either not an Amazon Prime member who wants to save on delivery, or who simply wants to save as much as possible, there's a slightly cheaper route for a PS5 controller if you already subscribe to PS Plus.

At PlayStation directly, the standard DualSense is £44.99 instead of £64.99, but eligible members receive a further 5% off accessories when signed into their account — bringing the total for a new PS5 controller to £42.74, undercutting Amazon by 25p.

That lower checkout cost applies to the White, Midnight Black, Galactic Purple, Grey Camouflage, Cosmic Red, Nova Pink and Starlight Blue colour models, so you've actually got more colour choices than Amazon's £42.99 selection. Non-members, however, will pay £44.99.

Still, if you were on the fence about buying PlayStation Plus anyway, though, subscriptions are on sale as part of some early Black Friday deals, too. You can of course buy just a month subscription for £7.99, or you can grab its 12-month memberships on sale up to 33% off — the cheapest, though, being a year of the PS+ Essential at £47.99 (£12 off the base £59.99 price.
