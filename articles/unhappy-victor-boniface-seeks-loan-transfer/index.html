Unhappy Victor Boniface seeks loan transfer

TheTools · 24.10.2026 05:57 · source · permalink

Sports

October 24, 2026 by Our Reporter

Victor Boniface is unhappy at Bayer Leverkusen and has told his handlers he wishes to move on loan in January.

Sky Sports reported that Victor Boniface has informed his advisors that he wants to move on loan again in the winter.

The 25-year-old Super Eagles striker has made only a five-minute appearance for Bundesliga club Bayer Leverkusen this season.

He is hugely disappointed with his relegated role at the club since returning from a loan spell at Werder Bremen and recovering from injury.

He has a contract at Leverkusen until June 2029 and the German club are open to a loan or permanent transfer for the player.
