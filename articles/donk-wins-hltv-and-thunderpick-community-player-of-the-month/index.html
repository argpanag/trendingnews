Donk wins HLTV and Thunderpick Community Player of the Month for August 2026

TheTools · 04.09.2026 12:58 · source · permalink

The Team Spirit rifler earned nearly half of all community votes after a dominant performance at the Esports World Cup 2026

Share

Via skin.land

Danil “donk” Kryshkovets picked up the HLTV and Thunderpick Community Player of the Month award for August 2026, pulling in 47.8% of the community vote.

The award aggregates votes across HLTV’s social media platforms in partnership with official social media partner Thunderpick.

What happened in August

Donk earned MVP honors at the Esports World Cup 2026, leading Team Spirit to a 3-1 grand final victory over FUT. Across the tournament, he posted multiple maps with ratings above 1.50.

Previous winners of the monthly award include HUASOPEEK in July 2026 and NiKo in June 2026.

From Tomsk to the top of Counter-Strike

Donk was born on January 25, 2007, in Tomsk, Russia. He reportedly started playing Counter-Strike at the age of four.

His path to stardom accelerated in mid-2023 when he was promoted from Team Spirit’s academy roster.

By the time IEM Katowice 2024 rolled around, he posted a historic 1.70 rating at the event. That year, at just 17 years old, he was named HLTV Player of the Year.

His career prize money has since surpassed $1 million, and he holds multiple Major MVP awards and top-2 HLTV year-end rankings.
