Tata Curvv Accomplished X Dark latest updates

Tata Curvv Accomplished X Dark Prices: The price of the Tata Curvv Accomplished X Dark in New Delhi is ₹14.25 Lakh (Ex-showroom).

Tata Curvv Accomplished X Dark Colours: This variant is available in 5 colours: Glossy Carbon Black, Opera Blue With Dual Tone, Pristine White Dual Tone, Pure Grey-Dual Tone and Nitro Crimson With Dual Tone.

Tata Curvv Accomplished X Dark Engine and Transmission: It is powered by a 1199 cc engine which is available with a Manual transmission. The 1199 cc engine puts out 118bhp@5500rpm of power and 170Nm@1750-4000rpm of torque.

Tata Curvv Accomplished X Dark vs similarly priced variants of competitors: In this price range, you may also consider Tata Nexon Fearless Plus PS Red Dark, which is priced at Rs.12.72 Lakh. Tata Sierra Pure Plus, which is priced at Rs.14.64 Lakh and Hyundai Creta S (O), which is priced at Rs.14.21 Lakh.

Curvv Accomplished X Dark Specs & Features:Tata Curvv Accomplished X Dark is a 5 seater Petrol car.Curvv Accomplished X Dark has Multi-function Steering Wheel, Touchscreen, Automatic Climate Control, Engine Start Stop Button, Anti-lock Braking System (ABS), Alloy Wheels, Passenger Airbag, Driver Airbag, Power Steering.
