  • Queen Camilla and Zara Tindall, Princess Anne’s daughter, attended British Champions Day at Ascot racecourse on Saturday.
  • The Queen wore a bright blue coat-dress and a navy hat, while Zara wore a grey and brown ensemble featuring a leather skirt and slouchy boots.
  • Billed as the country's richest race day, the event featured £4.35 million in prize money.
  • The Queen named Talk Of New York as the best turned out horse before he won the Queen Elizabeth II Stakes.
  • Camilla met key figures during the event, including jockey Saffie Osborne and pony race winner Camilla Froggatt.
