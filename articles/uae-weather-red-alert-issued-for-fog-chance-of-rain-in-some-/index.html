UAE weather: Red alert issued for fog; chance of rain in some areas

TheTools · 26.11.2026 14:51 · source · permalink

The weather on Thursday, November 26, is expected to be fair to partly cloudy with chance of rains in some areas, as per a forecast by the National Centre of Meteorology (NCM).

The met department issued red and yellow alerts for fog for a large part of the country on Thursday morning, warning of reduction in horizontal visibility till 9am.

Low clouds are set to appear over islands and some western coastal areas with a probability of light rainfall, the NCM said. Conditions are expected to get humid by night and Friday morning over some coastal and internal areas with a probability of fog or mist formation.

Light to moderate southeasterly becoming northwesterly winds are set to blow, freshening at times, with a speed of 10-20 km/hr, reaching 35 km/hr.

The sea will be slight to moderate at times in the Arabian Gulf and slight in Oman Sea.

Overall temperatures across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah are expected to rise to highs of 30ºC, 30ºC and 31ºC, and dip to a low of 21ºC in all three emirates.
