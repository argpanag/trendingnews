‘Practical Magic 2’ Stars Cast a Style Spell at LA Premiere

TheTools · 01.09.2026 18:14 · source · permalink

Kidman debuted Pieter Mulier's first Atelier Versace look, Bullock went custom in Louis Vuitton and more.

The cast of “Practical Magic 2” continued their witchy style streak at the film’s Los Angeles premiere held at the Directors Village on Monday night. Nicole Kidman debuted couturier Pieter Mulier’s inaugural design for Atelier Versace, Sandra Bullock opted for Louis Vuitton and more designer pieces hit the event.

Mulier, who left Alaïa for Versace in January 2026, created a dress for Kidman that merged the method dressing witchy style cues of her previous looks for the film, and also teased his vision for the Italian luxury fashion house. Though “The Hours” Oscar-winner certainly led the high-fashion brigade alongside Bullock, young stars Maisie Williams and Joey King conjured their own respective style statements. WWD unpacks those looks ahead.

Nicole Kidman

Nicole Kidman teased creative director Pieter Mulier‘s vision for Atelier Versace in a witchy black hand-intarsia lace dress. The sleeveless look was designed with an all-over ornate lace overlay, with intricate elements and slight botanical references. A neutral bodice and miniskirt added dimension to the overall look, while the asymmetric skirt added variation.

The dress was coordinated with a set of floral lace heels by Manolo Blahnik and jewelry pieces, including rings and earrings, courtesy of Parure Atelier. Kidman’s look was curated by stylist Jason Bolden.

Sandra Bullock

Sandra Bullock embraced a black and white color scheme for the “Practical Magic 2” press tour in a custom look by Louis Vuitton. The black velvety dress was designed with long sleeves and bold white cuffs with a coordinated statement collar.

A plunging V-shaped neckline gave the dress added dimension, while jewelry pieces by Anita Ko, including earrings and ear cuffs, added a touch of sparkle. Bullock’s Los Angeles premiere look was curated by stylist Leslie Fremar.

Maisie Williams

Maisie Williams turned to Maison Margiela’s fall 2026 ready-to-wear collection. Designed by Glenn Martens, the midi dress toyed with structured fabric, buttons and a print of bird bones and wispy blue ribbon for a dark glamour-centric style statement.

The long-sleeve dress was paired with heel-less ankle boots by Maison Margiela, along with cluster show sphere earrings from the Belgian legacy house. The “Game of Thrones” alum’s look was curated by stylist Dino Bonacic.

Joey King

Joey King favored a design by Ilya Migmoon. The actress’ dress evoked a similar witchy-ness to Kidman’s, featuring a black lace overlay with floral design cues along with a contrasting black and green base fabric.

The strapless dress included a bustier bodice with exaggerated hips that included feather elements. King accessorized her look with a gold Bulgari necklace. The actress’ look was curated by stylist Jared Eng.

A sequel to the 1998 film based on the book by Alice Hoffman, “Practical Magic 2” follows sisters and witches Sally and Gillian Owens, played by Bullock and Kidman, as they set out to break a curse that’s been cast on the women in their family for generations. The film hits theaters Sept. 11.
