Pedro Neto tells Xabi Alonso where he wants to play after Chelsea position debate

TheTools · 17.11.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

Pedro Neto has made his preferred position clear to Xabi Alonso after explaining which position he feels most comfortable in.

The Chelsea forward has featured on both sides of the Blues' attack since arriving at Stamford Bridge from Wolves.

However, Neto believes he has more options when operating on his natural side, with the Portugal international explaining why he prefers a slightly more traditional approach to playing out wide.

Pedro Neto explains why he prefers playing on the right wing

Speaking about his preferred position, Neto admitted that the right side of the attack is where he feels he can do his best work.

"I feel more comfortable on the right. I have more options on that side. It's rare to see a winger playing on the same side as their dominant foot. People say it's a bit like an 'old-fashioned winger'. Although I like playing on both sides," he said recently.

Modern wingers are often expected to play on the opposite flank to their stronger foot, allowing them to cut inside and get shots away.

However, the Chelsea forward enjoys having the option to stay wide and use his stronger foot to deliver crosses into the penalty area.

That traditional approach is becoming less common in modern football, but Neto clearly believes it still has a place in today's game.

What Neto's preferred position could mean for Chelsea

Alonso will have to consider Neto's admission when deciding how best to use him in Chelsea's attack.

The 26-year-old has the ability to operate on either flank, which is an important quality in a squad with plenty of competition for attacking places. He has also been in great form, and, as a result, has kept Estevao out of the starting line up.

Of course, there is a difference between being capable of playing in multiple positions and being most effective in one particular role.

Neto's preference of playing on the right gives Alonso something to think about, especially as one of his biggest challenges as been balancing the Blues' attacking options.

Neto's comments don't mean the the winger will always be selected on the right side, nor does it suggest he is unhappy when playing elsewhere.

Instead, it gives Chelsea's manager a clearer idea of where one of his attacking options believes he can offer the most.
