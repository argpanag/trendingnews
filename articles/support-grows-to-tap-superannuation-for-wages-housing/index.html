Support grows to tap superannuation for wages, housing

TheTools · 22.09.2026 02:29 · source · permalink

The Coalition is wrestling with whether to allow people to use superannuation to pay off a mortgage or for immediate take-home pay, as One Nation claims new polling shows the idea is gaining popularity with voters.

About 62 per cent of voters support using superannuation to boost take-home pay, compared with 28 per cent against, according to a Compass poll commissioned by One Nation.
