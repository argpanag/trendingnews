Friday international football tips: Best bets for Nations League matches | Betway Insider

TheTools · 25.09.2026 08:54 · source · permalink

Check out our Friday football tips featuring the best football betting tips, value selections and free football predictions from today's matches.

Nations League action continues on Friday evening, with several big-hitters in action.

Italy take on Belgium, the hosts under new management of Roberto Mancini, while Zinedine Zidane takes charge of France away at Türkiye.

We pick out four selections in our football betting acca, including a selection from both of those games.

Here are our football betting Tips, making a 17/1 total bet.

Italy v Belgium tips

Italy are under the management of Mancini for the second time and it does feel likely the Euro 2020-winning boss will get them back to a decent level.

Gli Azzurri have had a terrible time of things in recent years, failing to qualify for three World Cups around the Euros triumph, but now have a firmer hand on the wheel.

Belgium were very nearly knocked out of the World Cup by Senegal only to launch a remarkable comeback, before a strange victory over USA was followed by defeat to Spain.

They are not a great side and Italy are likely to improve, so the hosts are worth backing at an odds-against price of 23/20 .

Montenegro v Cyprus tips

Montenegro have won two and drawn one of their four matches in 2026 and look likely to be much the better team against Cyprus.

The hosts beat Faroe Islands and Gibraltar at home in World Cup qualifying and are ranked 80th in the world, compared to Cyprus down in 125.

Cyprus only beat San Marino in qualifying and have only played very low-profile opposition in more recent times.

Backing Montenegro to get the job done at 8/13 makes sense.

Sweden v Romania tips

Sweden had a pretty positive World Cup, hammering Tunisia 5-1 and securing a creditable 1-1 draw with Japan before being knocked out by France.

Graham Potter seems very comfortable in the national team hotseat, and has an in-form Alexander Isak even fitter than a few months ago and ready to lead the line.

Romania have lost by a couple of goals away to Bosnia and Slovakia in the last 12 months.

The Swedes should cruise to a home win here and backing them to do so by more than one goal at 5/4 looks good.

Turkey v France tips

France were sensational at the World Cup before being stopped by Spain, and they can continue that form here.

With Zidane in charge, Les Bleus are likely to be even more of a frightening attacking outfit, Michael Olise, Kylian Mbappe and Ousmane Dembele forming a remarkable frontline.

Türkiye were a disappointment in the US, losing to Australia and Paraguay before a consolation win over the hosts.

France can do some damage to their patchy defence and win by at least two goals at 19/20 .

Italy to beat Belgium 23/20

Montenegro to beat Cyprus 8/13

Sweden to beat Romania by 2+ goals 5/4

France to beat Türkiye by 2+ goals 19/20
