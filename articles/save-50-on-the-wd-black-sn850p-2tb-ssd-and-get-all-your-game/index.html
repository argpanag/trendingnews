Save 50% on the WD_BLACK SN850P 2TB SSD and get all your games on one drive

TheTools · 14.11.2026 01:32 · source · permalink

A bigger drive is one of the easiest ways to stop juggling which games and projects stay installed on your PC. The WD_BLACK SN850P 2TB Internal SSD is 50% off in a doorbuster deal at Best Buy, and it can give you the extra storage you need (whether on PS5 or PC) with a heatsink to spare.

The WD_BLACK SN850P 2TB is an officially licensed M.2 NVMe SSD for the PlayStation 5 that ships with a heatsink designed for the console's M.2 slot. It uses PCIe Gen 4 x4 and is rated for up to 7,300MB/s read and 6,600MB/s write speeds.
 

Save $300 on the WD_BLACK SN850P 2TB Internal SSD at Best Buy

Fast storage for your next build

The SN850P uses a PCIe Gen 4x4 connection and comes with an attached heatsink. If you're building or upgrading a PC, 2TB gives you room for a large game library and your everyday files on one drive, so you don't need to keep uninstalling games.

A second drive like this is also an easy way to keep a build organized. You can leave your operating system and everyday apps on your main drive and use this one for games and big projects, so a growing library never crowds the drive your PC runs on.

But the above specs are relatively standard for higher-end SSD drives—It's the built-in heatsink that sets this model apart. Fast drives get warm when they work hard, and a heatsink helps draw that heat away from the drive. You also have one less part to shop for, since the cooling is already included, which keeps installation simple. A heatsink is especially important if you plan to install this in your PS5, which needs extra cooling due to the tight space.

There is one thing to check first. Best Buy lists this drive for the PS5, and the listing doesn't spell out PC compatibility, so confirm that your motherboard has a free M.2 slot with room for a heatsink before you order.

At $299.99, the WD_BLACK SN850P 2TB Internal SSD is still a serious spend, but it's half of the full retail price, and you're getting 2TB of fast storage with cooling included. That makes it easier to justify if you've been putting off a storage upgrade. So if you're looking for an SSD with a heatsink, now's the time to get to Best Buy and pick this deal up.
