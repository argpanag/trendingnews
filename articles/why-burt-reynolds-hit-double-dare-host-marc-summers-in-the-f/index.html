  • Marc Summers, host of Double Dare, recalls the time Burt Reynolds hit him in the face during an appearance on The Tonight Show.
  • Summers had always dreamed of being on The Tonight Show with Johnny Carson, but ended up appearing with Jay Leno alongside Reynolds during a messy divorce.
  • A planned Double Dare-style challenge with pies turned into a real pie fight on the show, leading to a memorable and chaotic moment for Summers.

Double Dare host Marc Summers is reflecting on the time Burt Reynolds hit him in the face on The Tonight Show.

Summers, 74, rose to fame as the host of the beloved kids' competition show Double Dare on Nickelodeon. The series sent kids through a wild obstacle course that often covered them in everything from slime to fake boogers. But in a very adult moment, Summers appeared on The Tonight Show opposite legendary actor Burt Reynolds. However, the moment didn't go over well.

NBC / Getty Images

NBC / Getty Images

"My entire life, I wanted to be on The Tonight Show with Johnny Carson. I was obsessed with Johnny, and I got to interview him. I was working at a radio station in Indianapolis when I was 15, WBMP FM, which I did on weekends, and by the way, when I left BMP, a guy by the name of Dave Letterman took my place," Summers told PEOPLE. While Carson was gone, Jay Leno took over.

"So I didn't get on with Johnny, but I got on with Jay Leno. At the time, Burt Reynolds was going through a nasty divorce [with Loni Anderson], so he was supposed to do two segments. I was going to be segment three and Carrot Top was going to be segment four. Well, Burt was so peculiar that night, but entertaining, they kept him for one extra segment," Summers added.

What Happened Between Summers and Reynolds

Leno brought out pies that were meant for a potential Double Dare-style challenge. But with constant scheduling delays, Summers kept getting bumped.

"All hell broke loose after that. Jay brought out some pies, and we got into a massive pie fight," he told the publication. However, the situation was not fake as many people assumed.

"It was not fixed. Trust me. I was fighting for my life on that show, and, yeah, this is what happened. We got into a pie fight," he said. "Dave Letterman was beating Jay every night; that's the first night that Jay tied Letterman, and, yeah, it was a night I'll never forget."
