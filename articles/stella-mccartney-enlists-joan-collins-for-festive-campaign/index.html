GIVE A DAME: Stella McCartney has enlisted Joan Collins, who received her damehood from King Charles back in 2015, to star in a festive campaign that turns friendship, British humor and gift-giving into a playful showcase for the designer’s spring 2027 collection.

Dubbed “Stellabration,” the campaign stars McCartney and the famed British actress. The two traded playful exchanges in an intentionally unpolished, intimate series of scenes, including Collins unwrapping the brand’s signature bags and accessories like the Falabella styles finished with lead-free crystals, metallic details, and chains.

The campaign also marks the 25th anniversary of the Stella McCartney brand for the spring 2027 season. Presented in the garden of the British Ambassador’s residence in Paris, the collection put environmental concerns at the center of a wardrobe built for warm-weather ease.

It drew on McCartney’s childhood fascination with underwater ecosystems: wave motifs traced lapels and poplin shirts, while dolphin prints and oyster-like knitwear brought a touch of whimsy. She also offered tailored jackets cinched with drawstrings, sheer-leg trousers, draped evening gowns, pastel windbreakers, bralettes and utility trousers to balance her environmental message.

The campaign also builds on the brand’s high-profile interactions with legendary actors. Last November, McCartney hosted a lights countdown at its Old Bond Street flagship with Robert De Niro, ahead of its planned closure at the end of 2025. The new location of the Stella McCartney London store has not yet been revealed.
