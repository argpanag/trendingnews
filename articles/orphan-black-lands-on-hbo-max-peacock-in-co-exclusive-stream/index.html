'Orphan Black' Lands On HBO Max & Peacock In Co-Exclusive Streaming Deal

TheTools · 19.10.2026 22:49 · source · permalink

Boat Rocker Studios and BBC Studios have secured new U.S. deals for the critically acclaimed sci-fi drama series Orphan Black. Under the co-exclusive pacts, all five seasons of Orphan Black are now available to stream on HBO Max and Peacock.

Emmy-winning Tatiana Maslany stars as Sarah Manning, an outsider and street-smart chameleon who witnesses the suicide of a woman who looks just like her. After assuming the identity of the dead stranger, Sarah learns that she and the dead woman are clones – and that there are many other clones out there. Over the course of five seasons, Sarah and her clone sisters uncover the truth of their identities, why they were created, and fight to survive together.

Since its debut in 2013, Orphan Black has maintained a dedicated fanbase and is frequently ranked as one of the best sci-fi shows of the 21st century. It is the winner of numerous awards, including the 2014 Peabody Award for Drama.

Watch on Deadline

Orphan Black was produced by Boat Rocker Studios, in association with BBC America and CTV Sci-Fi Channel. Boat Rocker Studios’ co-founders Ivan Schneeberg and David Fortier (Mix Tape) executive produced alongside series co-creators Graeme Manson (Snowpiercer) and John Fawcett (Memory of a Killer).

Boat Rocker Studios and BBC Studios jointly distribute the series in the U.S., with BBC Studios distributing internationally. Boat Rocker Studios manages the Orphan Black global franchise.
