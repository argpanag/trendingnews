Resident Evil First Reviews: Not Just the Best Resident Evil Movie, but One of the Best Video Game Movies Ever

TheTools · 17.09.2026 01:14 · source · permalink

News

Critics say Zach Cregger's take on the iconic franchise is pure escapist fun that's briskly paced, surprisingly funny, and true to the spirit of the games.

by Christopher Campbell | September 16, 2026

TAGGED AS: Horror, movies

The latest adaptation of the Resident Evil video games arrives in theaters this weekend, and the first reviews are now online. The horror reboot is co-written and directed by Zach Cregger, the filmmaker behind last year’s award-winning hit Weapons, and apparently his caliber of talent is what this franchise has needed. Not only are some calling this easily the best Resident Evil movie, but also one of the best films based on a video game ever.

Starring: Austin Abrams, Zach Cherry, Kali Reis, Johnno Wilson

Directed By: Zach Cregger
