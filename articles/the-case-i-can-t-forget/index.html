Season: 5 | Episode: 1 | Length: 60m

First-hand accounts of detectives who worked on difficult cases. This edition takes a look at the murder of 36-year-old childcare worker Elaine O'Hara, who disappeared in 2012. Her remains were discovered by a dog walker on Killakee Mountain in September 2013. With almost no forensic evidence and an undetermined cause of death, the investigation pivoted entirely to Elaine's digital footprint.
