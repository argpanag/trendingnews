“Has to go” – Neville names three Man Utd defenders INEOS should get rid of before Dalot

TheTools · 17.11.2026 11:13 · source · permalink

Gary Neville agrees that Manchester United are in dire need of a defensive exodus, but he wouldn’t start with Diogo Dalot.

The Red Devils have been missing a genuine striker up front, but the team’s biggest crux has been their leaky backline.

Therefore, INEOS are expected to launch a defensive rebuild in 2027, so several defenders will be making way for new arrivals.

Gary Neville would keep Diogo Dalot and get rid of Man Utd trio

Michael Carrick’s last game in charge ended on a relatively positive note, with Man Utd mounting a late comeback to rescue a point against Aston Villa. That dramatic draw wasn’t enough to lift the club from the relegation zone, or rescue the manager, who has since been replaced by Mauricio Pochettino.

But while Carrick was at least applauded by the Old Trafford supporters, Dalot had a miserable experience, as he was cheered off the pitch after being substituted.

The 27-year-old was identified as the main culprit on two of Aston Villa’s goals, but Neville rose the Portuguese’s defence, arguing he deserves credit for volunteering to play at left-back in Luke Shaw’s absence.

“I know this is going to be so unpopular here, right?  Do I think Dalot is Dennis Irwin? No. Let’s be really clear, but I would get rid of a few players before Dalot, and Dalot got booed last week at Old Trafford,” said the former Man Utd captain during his appearance on the Stick to United podcast.

“I was really surprised at that. Dalot’s been shoved onto that left-hand side constantly. If you put me at left wing-back or left-back, and I’m a right-back, by the way, and he’s a right-back, I’d be like, ‘Not a chance. What are you putting me there for?’

“So he’s literally sticking his hand up and saying, ‘Go on, I’ll play there. I’ll do anything for the team.’ I never see him shirk anything, right? He might not be a brilliant player. He’s not an amazing player. Let’s be clear. He never shirks anything. I never look at him and think he could work harder. Ever. No.”

“I actually think, to be fair, if he was in a decent back four, he’d be all right. I actually do. But he’s got a shocking back four alongside him.”

Neville would instead start the revolution at the back by selling Noussair Mazraoui, Matthijs de Ligt and Lisandro Martinez.

“I’d be getting rid of Mazraoui well before him. I’d be getting rid of Martinez well before him. I’d be getting rid of De Ligt well before him.”

The pundit justified his stance on De Ligt by pointing out his terrible injury record, insisting that the club needs reliable players at the back.

“I think De Ligt has got one big problem: he doesn’t play football. If a player is injury-prone and doesn’t play football, it doesn’t mean he should go above a player who’s played every single week and is throwing his hand up.

“You want someone who’s actually reliable and available to play. So De Ligt has to go. I mean, seriously, well, get rid of him.”

Neville also expects Mauricio Pochettino to confirm Luke Shaw and Harry Maguire, at least for the time being.

Should Man Utd get rid of Mazraoui, De Ligt & Martinez?

At this stage, Martinez’s departure is considered a matter of time after publicly berating Director of Football Jason Wilcox.

The Argentine has also been poor in his most recent outings, so a divorce would be beneficial for all parties.

On the other hand, De Ligt represents a headache for the club, as he has the ability to be a top defender when fit, but he’s being gradually devalued by his injury concerns. Unless the Dutchman bounces back during the remaining part of the season, he should be placed on the transfer list.

As for Mazraoui, many would argue he hasn’t been any worse than Dalot, but Man Utd desperately need an upgrade at right-back, so one or the other must make way. The club might be inclined to keep the Portuguese due to his superiority and growing importance in the dressing room.
