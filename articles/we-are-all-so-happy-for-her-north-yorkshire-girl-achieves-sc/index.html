'We are all so happy for her' - North Yorkshire girl achieves scouting feat

TheTools · 09.11.2026 15:39 · source · permalink

A NORTH Yorkshire girl is among the first in the UK to accomplish every achievement in the world of Scouting.

Sienna Chilcott - who is 13 from Ripon, North Yorkshire - has earned all 95 badges available to her in the Scout section, making her one of the first Scouts in the UK to do so.

She completed the full set in just two years as a member of 4th Ripon Scout Troop.

Sienna said: "I enjoyed doing my badges because of the new experiences and opportunities I have gained. I also liked meeting new people within Scouting."

Her collection includes 63 activity badges, 15 staged badges, all nine challenge awards, four YouShape awards, one Earth Tribe award, an Assistant Patrol Leader badge, her two-year joining-in award, and the Chief Scout Gold Award.

This is the highest achievement a Scout can earn in the UK, with Sienna completed the award with a full year remaining in Scouts before moving up to Explorers.

She said: "My favourite badge to complete was my parascending badge because I loved the experience that I gained from completing it and if I wasn't a Scout I wouldn't have had the opportunity."

Sienna in front of her badges (Image: Provided)

Sienna also became a young navigator leader as part of her hill walking badge, helping lead Year 7 students from her school on day-long hikes in the Yorkshire Dales.

Dave Baker, 4th Ripon Scout leader, said: "It goes without saying that Sienna is an incredible Scout showing amazing passion, resilience, and a will to learn and make use of many new skills.

"Through completing all her Scout badges Sienna has generated for herself new opportunities and experiences as well as complete something that I have never personally seen before in my near 20 years as a volunteer.

"Sienna knew from the start what she wanted to achieve and I can only applaud her for this incredible achievement of 95 badges earned. Sienna is a superb asset to 4th Ripon Scout Group and we are all so happy for her."

Sienna with the scout's flag (Image: Provided)

Sienna's mother, Clare, has supported her throughout the journey, helping her reach various places to accomplish certain badges such as Parascending, Air Activities and Activity Centre Service.

Clare said: "We are unbelievably proud of Sienna and her achievements. She has shown dedication, hard work, and sheer determination to achieve this goal.

"She has worked hard for each and every badge she has earned. What makes us happiest is watching the growth and confidence being a member of Scouting has offered her."

Sienna follows in the footsteps of her brother Jakoby, who previously earned every badge during his time with 4th Ripon Cubs.
