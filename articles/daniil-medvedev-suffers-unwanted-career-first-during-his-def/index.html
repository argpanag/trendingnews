Daniil Medvedev suffers unwanted career first during his defeat to Alexander Zverev at the ATP Finals

TheTools · 22.11.2026 18:23 · source · permalink

Daniil Medvedev was unable to return to the final of the ATP Finals for the first time since 2021.

Medvedev has beaten Alexander Zverev more than any other player on the ATP Tour, but he was unable to add another victory in their head-to-head after facing the world number one in Turin.

In a match of small margins, Zverev was able to beat Medvedev 7-6(0), 6-4 to set up a blockbuster final against Carlos Alcaraz at the ATP Finals.

While Medvedev was competitive in his defeat to Zverev, he still achieved an unwanted career first in the final tournament of his 2026 season.

Is Daniil Medvedev the best player in the world other than Carlos Alcaraz and Jannik Sinner now?

Daniil Medvedev loses a 7-0 tie-break for the first time in his career

Zverev is yet to be broken at the ATP Finals this year, with Medvedev failing to create one break point opportunity against the German in Turin. Despite this, Medvedev also did not face a break point in the first set against Zverev.

This enabled Medvedev to reach a tie-break against Zverev, but the former world number one failed to win a single point in this tie-break, which he lost 7-0. This is the first time that this happened to Medvedev in his main tour career.

Medvedev was asked about the first set tie-break in an interview with Russian outlet Bolshe after the match, as he admitted some mistakes he made as he explained how the first set got away from him.

“Well, the first one doesn’t matter, he served, but maybe I chose the wrong placement for serving, a little too close to the line,” said Medvedev. “There were two mistakes. One easy one, one slightly unlucky on the passing shot. And the score is already 5-0 and it’s already difficult.”

Daniil Medvedev’s 2026 season has ended after ATP Finals exit

While Medvedev’s 2026 season did not end in perhaps the way that he would have hoped it to, it has still been a year of mostly positives for the Grand Slam champion.

Last year was the worst season of Medvedev’s career this decade, having finished the year outside the top 10 for the first time since 2019 after winning just one Grand Slam match all year and winning one title.

Medvedev appears to have rediscovered his form this year, having returned to the top five in the rankings after winning four titles and reaching the final of Indian Wells, where he beat Alcaraz. Medvedev did not reach the quarter-finals of any Grand Slam tournament this year, but he was still able to win seven more matches at majors than he did in 2025.

With Medvedev’s 2026 season now over, he can be much more satisfied with how his year has panned out, with opportunities to continue gaining points at the Grand Slams next year.

Medvedev has already confirmed his first tournament of the 2027 season, as he will be defending his title at the Brisbane International.
