Charlotte Casiraghi and Alexandra of Hanover attend Chanel show at Paris Fashion Week | Riviera Radio

TheTools · 06.10.2026 14:54 · source · permalink

Charlotte Casiraghi and Alexandra of Hanover stepped out together for Chanel’s Spring/Summer 2027 show during Paris Fashion Week on Monday, 5 October.

Share

By Camille Bidard

The half-sisters, both daughters of Princess Caroline of Monaco, were among the guests at the Grand Palais for Matthieu Blazy’s latest collection for the French fashion house.

Listen to this article

Charlotte, a Chanel ambassador, opted for a dark tailored look with a midnight-blue jacket, dark trousers and a black top.

She completed the outfit with a burgundy Chanel bag and red lipstick.

Alexandra chose a similarly understated style, wearing a black knitted top with charcoal trousers, gold bracelets and a small shoulder bag.

The appearance came just weeks after her engagement to Ben-Sylvester Strautmann was announced on 11 September.

A star-studded front row

The sisters joined a number of well-known guests at the Grand Palais, including Nicole Kidman, Dua Lipa, Marion Cotillard, Adèle Exarchopoulos, Carole Bouquet and Angèle.

The Spring/Summer 2027 collection saw Matthieu Blazy revisit several of Chanel’s signature elements, including black, tweed, tailoring, jewellery and the house’s iconic bags.

For Charlotte and Alexandra, the occasion offered two different interpretations of the Chanel aesthetic, with both opting for understated dark looks for their latest appearance together in Paris.
