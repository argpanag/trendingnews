Ratings: ‘Uncharacteristic errors’ sum up Bunnies demise

TheTools · 11.09.2026 13:56 · source · permalink
  • Sport
  • NRL
  • The Mole

Updated September 11, 2026 - 1.56pm. First published at 1.56pm

The elimination final between the Knights and Rabbitohs had a simple conclusion and now the latter is off to Mad Monday and Newcastle live another day.

In the early exchanges, it was clear how much the Knights were up for it and that intent continued until the full-time whistle.

Francis Manuleleua. Getty Images

Nine.com.au’s The Mole has rated every player from the final.

1. Kalyn Ponga 

Not at his brilliant attacking best but solid at the back under pressure and gained more yardage than anyone on the field, producing some neat plays with the ball.

Rating: 8

2. Dominic Young

Probably his best performance in a pressure game - a wonderful solo try, two try assists and got the better of his battle with Johnson. 

Rating: 8.5

3. Dane Gagai 

Rattled Latrell with a bit of niggle early on to put him off his game and also grabbed a fine try from a grubber. 

Rating: 7.5

4. Bradman Best 

Always looked dangerous without quite being able to split open the Souths defence. 

Rating: 6

Bradman Best. Getty Images

5. Greg Marzhew 

The bullocking winger was a danger throughout and could have finished with a couple of tries but for last-ditch defence.

Rating: 6.5

6. Fletcher Sharpe 

One of the heroes of the Knights’ charge to the big games, had a quiet night in the pressure cooker of finals footy. 

Rating: 5

7. Sandon Smith 

Sandon Smith.  Getty Images

Superb kicking game, setting up two tries and turning Souths around with his long rakes downfield. 

Rating: 8

8. Tyson Frizell 

The veteran forward pushed his case for one more season with a strong display up front against the fancied Souths pack. 

Rating: 7

9. Phoenix Crossland 

One of the big improvers of 2026, was busy on and off the ball again, topping the tackle count. 

Rating: 6.5

10. Cody Hopwood 

Inexperienced young prop handled the biggest game of his life well, holding up his spot in the middle. 

Rating: 6

11. Jermaine McEwen 

Probably the best player on the field, on the spot to score two fine tries to lead the way for a memorable Knights upset. 

Rating: 9

12. Francis Manuleleua 

Came up with the biggest hit of the night and inspired his team-mates with his work rate and enthusiasm. 

Rating: 6.5

13. Mathew Croker 

Very under-rated forward again did his job, running the ball well and working hard in defence.

Rating: 7

14. Harrison Graham 

Played 20 minutes off the bench and struggled to get involved, making just one run. 

Rating: 4

15. Lachlan Crouch 

Busy game when he got his chance, running for nearly 100 metres. 

Rating: 5.5

16. Pasami Saulo 

Only managed two runs in his 15 minutes late in the game. 

Rating: 4

17. Thomas Cant 

Also got very little game time but did his best to get involved. 

Rating: 5

South Sydney Rabbitohs

1. Matthew Dufty 

Solid in the last line of defence but failed to produce the attacking spark he showed during the regular season. 

Rating: 5.5

2. Alex Johnston 

Had virtually no chances in open play as Souths’ backline mis-fired and struggled to contain Dom Young before injury forced him off the field.

Rating: 5

3. Latrell Mitchell 

Latrell Mitchell and Dane Gagai come to blows. Getty Images

Lost his cool early on when Gagai got under his skin but calmed down and produced some moments of decent footy - yet not enough to lift his team to victory.

Rating: 7.5

4. Jack Wighton 

A polished performance, laying on a try and making over 140 run metres. 

Rating: 7

5. Campbell Graham 

Scored the Rabbitohs’ first try and kept out the dangerous Marzhew with some last-ditch defence. 

Rating: 6.5

6. Cody Walker 

Left the field in tears after 30 minutes following a calf tear in what may well be his farewell appearance for Souths, which would be a sad way to go out. 

Rating: 2

7. Jayden Sullivan 

Good in the lead-up games but struggled in his first final tonight - poor kicking game and did little to organise the attack. 

Rating: 4

8. Tevita Tatola 

Not involved enough - just five runs for 29 metres - well down on what you expect from a starting prop. 

Rating: 4

9. Brandon Smith 

Played more minutes than usual due to injuries to team-mates and struggled to keep up to the pace. 

Rating: 5

Brandon Smith.  NRL Imagery

10. Keaon Koloamatangi 

The best big man on the paddock, doing the hard yards and playing a key role in Souths’ only try. 

Rating: 8

11. David Fifita 

Danger man was kept well in check by the well disciplined Knights defence for most of the night. Missed six tackles as well. 

Rating: 5.5

12. Tallis Duncan 

Had a superb season with his running game out wide but like Fifita, struggled to make an impact through the strong Newcastle defensive line. 

Rating: 6

13. Cameron Murray 

Cameron Murray. NRL Imagery

The never-say-die Rabbitohs skipper kept going from start to finish but marred his game with a couple of uncharacteristic errors. 

Rating: 6.5 

14. Jye Gray 

Thrown into the fray at hooker late in the game with the result already determined by that stage, but did grab late try. 

Rating: 4.5

15. Lachlan Hubner 

Hard worker but like many of his team-mates, was slightly off his game in the pressure off sudden death footy.

Rating: 5

16. Sean Keppie 

Just one run in his 15 minutes… too little, too late. 

Rating: 3

17. Jamie Humphreys 

Replaced the injured Walker and tried hard to lift an out-of-sorts team without much success. 

Rating: 4.5
