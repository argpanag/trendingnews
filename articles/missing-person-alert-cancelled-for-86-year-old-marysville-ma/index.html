MARYSVILLE, Ohio (WSYX) — This alert has been cancelled.

Marysville police issued a statewide missing adult alert for an 86-year-old man who drove away from his home and needs medication.

Police said Billy Jones left his residence on Claudia Ln. in Marysville at 5:30 p.m. and was declared missing.

Law enforcement said they were concerned for his safety. The alert was later cancelled.

Jones is described as a White male who is 5 feet, 10 inches tall, weighs 204 pounds and has gray hair and brown eyes.

Marysville police can be reached at (937) 645-7300.
