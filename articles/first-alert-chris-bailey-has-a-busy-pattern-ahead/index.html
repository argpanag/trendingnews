FIRST ALERT | Chris Bailey has a busy pattern ahead

TheTools · 18.11.2026 19:35 · source · permalink

LEXINGTON, Ky. (WKYT)- We are one week away from Planes, Trains and Automobiles Day taking center stage and it looks like our pattern is about to change for the long Thanksgiving travel period. This setup looks active and much colder.

Let’s begin with the mild of this week before looking ahead.

Highs are in the 60s today through Friday with dry skies. Enjoy.

The weekend finds those changes taking shape as low pressure rolls up the east coast while a cold front sweeps in from the west. This front won’t arrive until late Sunday or Sunday night, keeping those mild temps into the weekend.

The front will bring some showers from west to east through Monday as cold air plunges in behind the boundary.

Much colder air crashes in on Monday with even colder air settling in for all of next week as temps go below normal.

I’ve mentioned this is a busy pattern for Thanksgiving week and there’s so much energy crossing the country that the forecast models can’t keep up. Not only do they disagree on how to handle all this energy, but they totally change from one run to the next.

Some winter weather can’t be ruled out.
