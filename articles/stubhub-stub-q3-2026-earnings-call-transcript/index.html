RISKS

  • Baker noted that the Major League Baseball playoffs involved teams from small markets and lasted only five games compared to a seven-game series in the prior year, impacting fourth-quarter comparisons.
  • Baker stated, "we do not control when they go on sale, and Live Nation and other promoters may put them on sale before the beginning of the year, or they may not," referring to the uncertainty regarding the timing of major concert tour on-sales.

SUMMARY

StubHub Holdings, Inc.(NYSE:STUB) reported third quarter financial results including 7% growth in gross merchandise sales and significant expansion in adjusted EBITDA margins. Management stated that the performance was driven by consumer demand for live experiences, market leadership in resale, and improved operating efficiency at scale. The company reported the repayment of $100 million in debt and an extension of term loan maturities to 2033, which reduced net leverage to 2.5x. Management increased its full-year top-line guidance while maintaining its adjusted EBITDA outlook, citing uncertainty regarding the specific timing of major concert tour on-sales scheduled for the end of the year.

  • Management announced a transition plan for the Chief Financial Officer role, with Constance James scheduled to step down in early 2027 and Mike Noonan, currently the Chief Financial Officer of TripAdvisor, slated to join the company in January.
  • Baker reported that the regulatory environment remains constructive, noting that price cap legislation failed to advance in California and New York as lawmakers recognize that caps can reduce consumer access.
  • The company is developing AI-augmented experiences to help fans discover tickets, with Baker stating, "We are not seeing material traffic from AI based referrals to date," but expecting usage of Agentic technologies to grow.
  • The National Football League embraced open distribution by allowing season ticket holders to sell across more marketplaces, a move Baker described as a proof point for the company's nonexclusive distribution strategy.
  • Management identified women's sports as a driver of new ticket sales, noting that rapidly growing fan bases are increasing the number of events and first-time buyers on the platform.
  • The company is expanding its advertising platform through sponsored listings, testing the offering with individual fan sellers in addition to professional point-of-sale users.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

  • Adjusted EBITDA: A non-GAAP financial measure that excludes interest, taxes, depreciation, and other non-operating or non-cash items to evaluate core performance.
  • Agentic Technology: Artificial intelligence software designed to act as an agent to fulfill complex tasks or transactions.
  • GMS: Gross Merchandise Sales, representing the total dollar value paid by buyers for ticket transactions and fulfillment.
  • Net Leverage: A ratio calculated as net debt divided by trailing 12-month adjusted EBITDA.
  • NOLs: Net operating loss carryforwards used to offset future taxable income.
  • Open Distribution: A model where content owners can sell tickets directly across multiple nonexclusive marketplaces.
