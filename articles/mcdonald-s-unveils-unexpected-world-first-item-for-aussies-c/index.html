McDonald's unveils ‘unexpected’ world-first item for Aussies coming in days

TheTools · 08.10.2026 07:08 · source · permalink

The product is being released to promote its new McCafe range, featuring a tiramisu-inspired latte.

McDonald's has released a "world first" fashion accessory that is certain to turn heads — if you're lucky enough to snag one.

The fast food chain has created the McCafe Tray Bag, and it's coming to Australia in a few days.

For those wondering what that might be, think of the cardboard tray that you're used to when carrying a set of drinks, only make it high fashion.

McDonald's has released a handbag modelled after its drink tray.

The dark red bag features a chunky shoulder strap and gold accents with McCafe written on the side.

However, when it comes to storage space, the bag doesn't seem very practical, as it can fit, at most, some sunglasses, a lip gloss, and maybe a mobile phone.

The items could also fly out as you strut down the street, as there's no zip or lock to cover your belongings.

But it could be perfect for office workers who care about the environment or busy parents who are sick of being at the behest of a cardboard tray.

Riley Hemson, who owns Australian label Remmie by Riley, gave the bizarre bag her stamp of approval.

"I love anything that's unexpected, and this is probably the most unexpected accessory I've ever owned,"  said Riley. 

"It's instantly recognisable, a little bit playful but still looks chic."

It comes just two days after Woolworths announced it had turned its iconic hot chicken bag into a real-life handbag that customers could snag for $10.

The initial release will only have 100 bags available, but McDonald's has teased a second release very soon.

The Macca's bags will be available from October 9, with only 100 available in the first drop.

However, the Golden Arches has promised a second release will be announced very soon.

Macca's rarely releases merchandise without a reason, and the McCafe Tray Bag is being released to promote the fact that the restaurant chain is adding three new items to its menu.

This includes the Ice Tiramisu Latte with cold foam, which is McCafe espresso paired with tiramisu-flavoured syrup and milk, topped with cold foam and cocoa dusting.

There is also the Red Bull Strawberries and Cream Energiser and an Affogato, which is vanilla soft serve topped with espresso.

The new items will hit stores on October 14.
