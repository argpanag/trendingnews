    15:25 BST

    Everton v Chelsea (Sat, 17:30 BST)

    Chelsea

    Some more team news from the Chelsea news conference as Xabi Alonso confirms both Emmanuel Emegha and Jamie Gittens are unavailable.

    "When he is fit, he is very special. He is 1.95 metres, is quick, attacks space, and is a threat. Unfortunately, he’s had little things and he hasn’t been available."

  16. 'Tough' to play against 'world-class' Arsenalpublished at 14:57 BST
    14:57 BST

    Nottm Forest v Arsenal (Sun, 16:30 BST)

    Nottingham Forest

    Oliver Glasner on whether his Nottingham Forest side can play to their strengths against defending champions Arsenal:

    "It's huge credit to Mikel Arteta and Arsenal. It's now a team that has been built over years and always at the beginning finding a core and then always adding the right pieces.

    "It's not just the quality, it's also the mentality in how they play. I think in every game this season they were outrunning the opposition. It's incredible. The effort, the mindset Mikel created there is incredible.

    "This makes it so tough to play against them and you see it from every single player. They have so many world-class players they can react from the bench.

    "It was now in the second consecutive game where the players who came on had a huge impact and this shows the quality they have.

    "This is what we are facing and I think the structure how they are playing, everybody knows and analyses but the way they execute it makes it so difficult."

  17. Glasner asks Wood to help encourage side's struggling strikerspublished at 14:54 BST
    14:54 BST

    Nottm Forest v Arsenal (Sun, 16:30 BST)

    Nottingham Forest

    Nottingham Forest boss Oliver Glasner on how his side can hurt Arsenal:

    "No team has seven, eight big chances like we had against [Crystal] Palace [against Arsenal] but it's possible to have two or three and then it's on us to be clinical, taking the chances and then you have the chance to win the game."

    On how he encourages his strikers when they'e struggling: "Today the main topic was finishing in training and before the training I just told the players what we're going to do and then I said the one who can explain it best how to deal with this situation is Chris Wood because I don't know how many years [he has been] in the Premier League, and he's scored almost 100 goals in the Premier League.

    "Overall [he's scored] around 200 goals in English football and he had to deal with these situations.

    "You are in a flow and then it seems every chance is a goal and then [you have a run] where you miss some chances.

    "I like what he said to the group. I said he could explain it better than me because I didn't score many goals. I didn't miss many chances because I didn't get many chances.

    "The key message, and I loved it, was 'always be confident, never lose trust in yourself and there's always another one around the corner'.

    "That's exactly how it is and I could see players start to smile and that's how you best to deal with it."

  20. James could return vs Man Utd, Rodon also progressingpublished at 14:45 BST
    14:45 BST

    Leeds Utd v Man Utd (Sun, 14:00 BST)

    Leeds United

    In terms of team news, Leeds United head coach Daniel Farke said winger Daniel James has a chance of returning from a back injury, while centre-back Joe Rodon is closing in on a return to full training.

    "Progression, yes. Joe will still miss this game," Farke said on Rodon.

    "We spoke at the beginning of this injury and it would last eight to 10 weeks. I think we’re at week seven, so he’s edging closer. He’s doing good progress, it won’t last too long until he’s back in team training but not yet and also not yet in contention for this upcoming game."

    On James, Farke added: "Daniel James with his back problems also missed games with the national team. Since yesterday he’s back on the pitch with us.

    "Right now, we have to wait to see how his body reacts but at least good news with him that he’s back in training and he seems to be improving.

    "The intensity in the last days was not too high, we have to wait and see how his body reacts in the last two sessions before the game but there’s a chance he’s involved, yes."

Leeds Utd v Man Utd (Sun, 14:00 BST)

Leeds United

In terms of team news, Leeds United head coach Daniel Farke said winger Daniel James has a chance of returning from a back injury, while centre-back Joe Rodon is closing in on a return to full training.

"Progression, yes. Joe will still miss this game," Farke said on Rodon.

"We spoke at the beginning of this injury and it would last eight to 10 weeks. I think we’re at week seven, so he’s edging closer. He’s doing good progress, it won’t last too long until he’s back in team training but not yet and also not yet in contention for this upcoming game."

On James, Farke added: "Daniel James with his back problems also missed games with the national team. Since yesterday he’s back on the pitch with us.

"Right now, we have to wait to see how his body reacts but at least good news with him that he’s back in training and he seems to be improving.

"The intensity in the last days was not too high, we have to wait and see how his body reacts in the last two sessions before the game but there’s a chance he’s involved, yes."
