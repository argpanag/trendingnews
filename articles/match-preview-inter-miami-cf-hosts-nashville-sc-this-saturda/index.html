Clear Bag Policy

To provide a safer environment for the public and to expedite guest entry into the stadium, Nu Stadium policy limits and restricts the size and type of bags that are permitted into the stadium during any events taking place. We encourage guests NOT to bring any bags, however, the following will be permitted after proper screening:

  • One bag that is clear plastic, vinyl or PVC and do not exceed 12” x 6” 12” or a one-gallon clear plastic freezer bag (Ziplock bag or similar).
  • Small clutch bags, approximately the size of a hand (no larger than 4.5” x 6.5”), with or without a handle or strap, may be carried into the stadium along with one of the clear bag options.
  • Any medically necessary items must be pre-approved by Stadium management. Please contact guestservices@intermiamicf.com to be considered for approval. All items will be subject to proper inspection at entry gate.
  • Diapers and wipes can be carried in a clear bag. Each member of a family including children will be able to carry an approved clear bag, providing ample storage capacity.

Consult Nu Stadium's complete Clear Bag Policy HERE.

Inter Miami CF This Regular Season

Overall, Inter Miami has recorded 12 wins, four losses, and eight draws so far this MLS regular season for a total 44 points, and sits second in the Eastern Conference standings.

Messi, the reigning MLS Landon Donovan MVP Award and Golden Boot presented by Audi winner, leads the team in goals with 18 and is tied for the lead amongst the league's top scorers. Messi also leads Inter Miami in assists this regular season with 13, followed by midfielders Rodrigo De Paul and Telasco Segovia with 10 each.

Previously Against Nashville SC

Saturday's meeting will be the 23rd between the sides in Club history. Inter Miami leads the series to date with 10 wins to six for Nashville, while six other matches resulted in draws.

Last time the sides met was in Nashville in August, with Nashville securing a 4-1 win.

Scouting Nashville SC

Nashville visits Nu Stadium after a 2-1 midweek loss on the road against Toronto FC. The Tennessee side has recorded 16 wins, three losses, and five draws so far for 53 points this regular season, and is first in the Eastern Conference.

Forward Sam Surridge has been the team's top scorer so far with 13 goals. Captain Hany Mukhtar has also been a key player for Nashville so far, registering 10 goals and seven assists.
