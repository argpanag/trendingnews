PCSO Lotto Results: 6/55 prize now P248M as no winners of major jackpot draws on Oct.5

TheTools · 06.10.2026 15:00 · source · permalink

The Grand Lotto 6/55 prize is now over P248 million as there were no winners of the major lotto jackpot draws on October 5, 2026, the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO) announced on Monday.

No bettor selected the winning combination of 28-34-18-32-53-20 for the Grand Lotto 6/55 jackpot prize that now stands at P248,358,380.63.

According to the PCSO, the odds of winning the Grand Lotto 6/55 jackpot are exactly 1 in 28,989,675. This means that for every single ticket combination a bettor buy, their chance of hitting all six numbers correctly is approximately 0.00000345%.

No one also chose the winning numbers of 13-25-03-02-12-21 for the Megalotto 6/45 jackpot prize of P92,169,920.96.
