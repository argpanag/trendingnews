Lehecka vs Tsitsipas Head-to-Head History

Lehecka and Tsitsipas have met four times on the ATP Tour, with the head-to-head series tied 2-2. Lehecka won their most recent two meetings, including straight-sets victories at the 2024 Antwerp and Indian Wells tournaments, while Tsitsipas prevailed in the 2023 Australian Open quarterfinals and 2022 Rotterdam semifinals.

The recent meetings favor Lehecka, who has won four consecutive sets against Tsitsipas. However, the broader 2-2 record and Tsitsipas’s earlier three-set victory in Rotterdam show that this matchup has produced competitive tennis across multiple hard-court events.
