The Azzurri will be in Doha for the World Shooting Championships - Caccia Passione

TheTools · 30.10.2026 15:27 · source · permalink

The world championship

The highlight of the 2026 international competitive season has finally arrived. In Doha, Qatar, everything is ready for the start of the Gran Mondiale, a world championship for all shooting sports specialties that will offer the first Olympic Charters for the 2028 Los Angeles Olympic Games up for grabs —three for each individual specialty. The Lusaill Shooting Complex will host the event, which will feature 1.055 athletes representing 90 countries competing in the Clay Pigeon and Target Shooting competitions.

Who will go on stage?

As for Clay Pigeon Shooting, the first to depart for Doha will be the Skeet specialists led by National Technical Director Luigi Agostino Lodde, who are traveling to Qatar tomorrow. Representing Italy on the platform will be Tammaro Cassandro (Carabinieri) from Capua (CE), Erik Pittini (Fiamme Oro) from Sutrio (UD), and Gabriele Rossetti (Fiamme Oro) from Ponte Buggianese (PT) for the men, and Diana Bacosi (Army) from Cetona (SI), Martina Bartolomei (Air Force) from Laterina (AR), and Sara Bongini (Fiamme Oro) from Impruneta (FI) for the women.

The summons

Their first official match is scheduled for Tuesday, November 3rd, with pre-competition training. The Italian Olympic Trap Team, led by National Technical Director Marco Conti, will also depart for Qatar on November 6th. To defend the Italian title, the Umbrian coach has called up Mauro De Filippis (Fiamme Oro) from Taranto, Massimo Fabbrizi (Carabinieri) from Monteprandone (AP), and Giovanni Pellielo (Fiamme Azzurre) from Vercelli (VC) for the men, and Jessica Rossi (Fiamme Oro) from Crevalcore (BO), Erica Sessa (Fiamme Oro) from Cava de' Tirreni (SA), and Silvana Maria Stanco (Fiamme Gialle) from Sturno (AV) for the women. Their official training is scheduled for Monday, November 9th (source: FITAV).
