Who was Ted Bundy? The backstory: Ted Bundy 1946-1989 was one of the most notorious American serial killers, kidnappers, and rapists. Before his execution in Florida's electric chair in January 1989, he confessed to 30 homicides committed across seven states between 1974 and 1978, though investigators suspect his true victim count was significantly higher. FILE - Accused murderer Ted Bundy attends the second day of jury selection in his murder trial, June 27, 1979, in Miami, Fla. AP Photo,File Bundy was born Theodore Robert Cowell in Vermont, but his formative years and early adulthood were centered entirely in the Pacific Northwest. What made Bundy uniquely terrifying to the public and elusive to law enforcement was his outward presentation: The Chameleon Persona: Unlike the stereotypical image of a violent criminal at the time, Bundy was conventionally handsome, articulate, charismatic, and politically connected. Modus Operandi: He frequently feigned physical disability wearing a fake plaster cast or walking with crutches or posed as an authority figure like a police officer to ask young women for assistance loading items into his bronze Volkswagen Beetle. Once his victims were close, he would overpower, abduct, and assault them. Significance to Washington state Washington state was the crucible of Bundy's life, his education, his public integration, and the launchpad of his killing spree.
