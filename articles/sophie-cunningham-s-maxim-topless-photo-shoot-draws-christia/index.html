Sophie Cunningham’s Maxim Topless Photo Shoot Draws Christian Backlash After Theo Von Gospel Interview

TheTools · 27.11.2026 07:29 · source · permalink

News

Indiana Fever guard Sophie Cunningham is drawing criticism from some Christian commentators over a Maxim photo shoot, days after a Texas pastor praised her for sharing the Gospel on Theo Von's podcast. The two appearances have prompted a debate over how churches and ministry leaders respond when celebrities speak publicly about their faith.

Cunningham, 30, appears on the magazine's latest cover. One photo inside the issue shows the WNBA guard posing topless with her hair covering her chest. Cunningham, who was baptized again earlier this year, discussed the shoot on No Hesitation, the podcast she co-hosts with basketball trainer Drew Hanlen.

She said her agents were initially hesitant about the collaboration because they associated the magazine with its earlier image. Cunningham said she believed Maxim had changed since then and that she enjoyed the shoot. She also recalled that it took four people to help her get into a pair of leather pants she said cost $27,000.

The spread drew criticism from Catholic legal scholar Robert P. George, who said it changed how he viewed Cunningham as a role model. George said he had admired some of her public positions, including her advocacy for women's sports, but expressed disappointment over her decision to pose for the magazine.

The strongest criticism of the earlier praise came from evangelist Justin Peters. Texas pastor Josh Howerton had celebrated Cunningham's conversation with Theo Von as an "evangelism MASTERCLASS," highlighting how she brought Jesus into the discussion. Peters argued that evangelical leaders are often too quick to elevate celebrities who profess faith. After the Maxim photos appeared, he asked Howerton whether he would retract his endorsement.

Howerton's initial praise focused on how Cunningham spoke about Jesus in a setting where many listeners might rarely hear someone explain the Gospel. Peters raised a broader question about how quickly praise for a public statement can turn into an endorsement of the person who made it.

Cunningham, meanwhile, used her Maxim interview to talk about her competitive side on the court and the values she says matter to her off it. She said people regularly approach her to thank her for speaking up for women and girls in sports. On her own podcast, her comments about Maxim focused on why she agreed to the shoot and what happened on set.
