President Lee: "Government Will Fully Support Treatment and Recovery of DMZ Landmine Explosion Victims" - The Asia Business Daily

TheTools · 21.09.2026 08:42 · source · permalink

by Song Seungseop

Published 21 Sep.2026 17:42(KST)

President Lee Jae-myung stated on the 21st that "the government will provide all necessary support for the treatment and recovery of our troops" after three soldiers were injured in an explosion accident during the development of a search route in the Demilitarized Zone (DMZ).

On this day, President Lee wrote on his X account (formerly Twitter), "I sincerely wish for the swift recovery of the injured soldiers." He continued, "My heart feels heavy thinking about how shocked and worried the families must be by this sudden news," and extended his deepest condolences to the families.

President Lee Jae-myung is speaking at the Senior Presidential Secretaries meeting held at the Blue House on September 21, 2026. Photo by Yonhap News Agency

On the morning of the 21st, an incident believed to be a landmine explosion occurred at a frontline unit of the Army’s 25th Division, which is responsible for the western front. As a result of this accident, two soldiers participating in the DMZ search operation were seriously injured, and three soldiers were injured in total.

According to the Joint Chiefs of Staff, all injured soldiers were transported to the Armed Forces Capital Hospital via helicopter and ground routes and are currently receiving treatment.
