NFLX stock dips after mixed analyst notes, per CNBC and Yahoo Finance

TheTools · 18.09.2026 13:24 · source · permalink

nflx stock dipped on Sept. 17, closing at $75.31 after CNBC and Yahoo Finance posted coverage saying analyst notes were mixed and that some price targets had been reduced.

CNBC’s quote page lists Netflix’s close at $75.31 and shows a 52-week range of 65.08 to 124.86, noting the share price moved lower that day; the page also displays a volume figure for the session.

Yahoo Finance’s Netflix quote and analysis pages similarly record the stock’s recent slide and track analyst price targets and ratings, framing the coverage as a mix of upgrades and cuts across the street.

Market commentary on CNBC has highlighted that some analysts trimmed targets after softer growth signals, while others flagged potential upside on valuation, producing the mixed notes that traders cited, according to CNBC’s reporting.

Investors following nflx stock are also watching broader market drivers this week, including the upcoming Fed decision and overall market tone that Yahoo and CNBC say are shaping short-term flows.

Sources

  • CNBC — closing price $75.31, session data and reporting that analysts issued mixed notes and price-target changes.
  • Yahoo Finance — Netflix quote page and analyst pages that track price targets, ratings and the stock’s recent slide.
