Certain salmon products have been recalled over the possible presence of Listeria.
Four packets from SuperValu and one from Centra with expiry dates later this month and early next month are affected.

Listeria can cause mild flu-like symptoms or potentially nausea, vomiting or diarrhoea in healthy people.
However the Food Safety Authority says people with underlying conditions or small children can suffer serious complications.
They are from the Centra Inspired By and SuperValu Smoked Salmon ranges and the following batches are affected:

SuperValu Signature Taste Dingle Gin Infused Smoked Salmon 100g 31/10/2026
SuperValu Signature Taste Handcut Smoked Salmon Ribbons 80g 04/11/2026
SuperValu Signature Taste Applewood Smoked Salmon 100g 31/10/2026
SuperValu Signature Taste Dill and Black Pepper Smoked Salmon 100g 04/11/2026
Inspired By Centra Applewood Smoked Salmon 80g 31/10/2026
