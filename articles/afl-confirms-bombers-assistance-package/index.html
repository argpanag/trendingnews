AFL confirms Bombers' assistance package

TheTools · 22.09.2026 09:17 · source · permalink

Club to receive extra list spots in 2027.

Essendon will receive two additional Category A Rookie List spots for the 2027 season as its assistance package from the AFL.

The league confirmed the final outcome this week following the Club’s request, with the list positions to be reviewed at the conclusion of next season.

This package provides support for 2027 only, however the AFL reserves the right to recommend additional list spots for the Club in 2028 and 2029.
