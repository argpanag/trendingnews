Jazz coach Will Hardy simply reminded his team that "Hope is not a strategy" in containing the brilliance of Luka Doncic ahead of their Wednesday game.

Utah Jazz head coach Will Hardy can only admit that stopping Luka Doncic is nearly impossible to accomplish.

Facing Doncic and the Los Angeles Lakers on Wednesday, Hardy gave a humorous answer when asked how he planned to deal with the Slovenian superstar.

"I have written on my [white] board, ‘Hope is not a strategy.’ So that’s out," Hardy told reporters ahead of the game, via ESPN's Dave McMenamin.

Will Hardy and the Utah Jazz find themselves facing Luka Doncic again who has been enjoying a historically dominant tear to lead the Los Angeles Lakers' start to the 2026-27 NBA season.

Entering Wednesday's game, Doncic is leading the league in scoring with 38.8 points along with 7.2 rebounds and 7.7 assists across 13 games. Behind the staggering production of the NBA superstar, the Lakers are holding an NBA-best 13-2 record.

Doncic delivered his best performance of the 2026-27 season so far on October 27 in Salt Lake City, overwhelming the Jazz with a 52-point explosion to lead the Lakers to a 132-123 victory.

Stopping Doncic will be a major challenge for the Jazz, especially with the team significantly shorthanded. Jaren Jackson Jr. (knee) and Lauri Markkanen (hand) are expected to remain sidelined until December, while Utah has struggled to a disappointing 2-13 start to the season.
