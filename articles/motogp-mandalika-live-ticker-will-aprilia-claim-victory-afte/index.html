In the Sprint, almost everything went against Aprilia. With Marc Marquez at the top, the lead in both the riders’ and manufacturers’ championships has switched back to red. A chance for revenge awaits on Aprilia’s favourite circuit.

By

11.10.2026 - 08:29

This article is an automatically generated English version. The Original Article was published in German.

Of all places, it was at the Mandalika International Circuit – a track on the MotoGP calendar that seemed tailor-made for the characteristics of the Aprilia RS-GP – that Bezzecchi and co. suffered a bitter disappointment. Following the double retirement of ‘Bez’ and pole-sitter Fernández, Jorge Martín took the lead. But the championship leader also ran into trouble – and Marc Márquez claimed the winner’s medal and the top spot in the standings. If Aprilia are to keep the world championship title race alive, they’ll need to score plenty of points on Grand Prix Sunday. SPEEDWEEK.com will be reporting live from Mandalika to see if they can get their revenge.
