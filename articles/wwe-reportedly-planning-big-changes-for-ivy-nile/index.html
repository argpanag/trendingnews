WWE reportedly planning big changes for Ivy Nile

TheTools · 03.09.2026 04:30 · πηγή · μόνιμος σύνδεσμος
  • By Shounak Chakrabarti
  • Published: 2 Sep 2026, 19:30

According to recent reports, WWE’s creative reportedly has big plans for Ivy Nile.

The Creed Brothers of The American Made recently made their WWE NXT return. Shortly after, speculation also emerged about Nile’s potential return to WWE television.

As per a new report from Fightful Select, WWE’s creative could be working toward her return. “Creative sources within WWE claim that there have been plans for Ivy Nile being developed for quite a while that would see a big change for her,” Fightful Select reported.

It was also noted that sources close to them indicated that Nile’s WWE contract is coming close to its expiry date this fall. Although no details about a new deal have been heard, there were “creative plans being discussed in recent months,” which suggested WWE’s interest in retaining her.

WWE signed Nile to a developmental deal after offering her a tryout in 2020. She spent three years in NXT before moving to Monday Night Raw alongside The Creed Brothers. Later, the trio aligned themselves with Chad Gable to form the heel stable of American Made. However, despite their regular presence on RAW, they slowly started fading away after Gable’s injury last year.

Nile’s last WWE match was a win against PJ Vasa on the August 3rd episode of Main Event, while her last match on RAW was in March, 2026.
