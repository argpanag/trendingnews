BUNCOMBE COUNTY, N.C. (WLOS) — Buncombe County saw record turnout during early voting this year, with 96,838 voters casting ballots before Election Day.

WMYA

In 2022, about 70,000 people voted early in the county. The previous record was 78,000 in 2018, according to Buncombe County Elections Director Corrine Duncan.

EARLY VOTING ENDS IN NORTH CAROLINA; WHAT VOTERS NEED TO KNOW FOR ELECTION DAY

The busiest early-voting sites included South Buncombe Library and the Weaverville Community Center.

Voters planning to head to the polls Tuesday, Nov. 3, should check their assigned polling place, election officials say. Voters must go to their designated location to receive the correct ballot for their precinct. North Carolina has an online search tool to help voters find their polling place.

Voters should also bring an acceptable form of photo ID. A driver’s license is one option. For a full list of acceptable IDs, visit BuncombeNC.gov/vote or call the Buncombe County elections office.

Sample ballots are available on the North Carolina State Board of Elections website. Polling places will be open from 6:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. Tuesday. Same-day registration will not be available on Election Day.
