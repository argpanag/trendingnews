David Beckham, 51, surprises football clinic participants at Bedok South Secondary School

TheTools · 25.11.2026 12:24 · source · permalink

Legend.

November 25, 2026, 07:10 PM

Photos from David Beckham Instagram & Marina Bay Sands

Former England star David Beckham is in Singapore.

He was at Bedok South Secondary School on the morning of Nov. 25.

The 51-year-old's appearance at the football clinic came as a surprise for 39 participants from schools and the network of non-profit Tasek Academy and Social Services (Tasek).

Beckham played in an eight-a-side game with the participants, including Singapore goalkeepers, Hassan Sunny and Izairida Shakira.

The clinic marked the launch of Sports for Good: Football Edition, a new initiative by Community Chest.

Marina Bay Sands (MBS), via its community engagement programme, Sands Cares, supported Beckham's appearance.
