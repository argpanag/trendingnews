Best NFL Player Prop Bets Tonight for Bengals vs Steelers

Mike Gesicki Over 2.5 Receptions (+116)

FTN projects Gesicki for 3.02 receptions against a line of 2.5, an 11.07% edge. The +116 price leads the card because the model gets above the number without asking bettors to lay juice.

Gesicki caught three passes for 37 yards and a touchdown in the first meeting with Pittsburgh. He has reached three receptions in four of eight games, including four catches for 76 yards against Tennessee two weeks ago.

His weekly volume can disappear when Ja’Marr Chase and Tee Higgins dominate the targets. The plus price offsets that risk, and FTN still projects him above the line.

Germie Bernard Under 52.5 Receiving Yards (-115)

FTN projects Bernard for 38.69 receiving yards, 13.81 below the market, with a 10.7% edge on the under. Bernard has 284 yards in eight games, an average of 35.5.

The rookie caught five passes in Pittsburgh’s Week 8 win over Cleveland but finished with 30 yards. His 87-yard game at Tampa Bay accounts for nearly one-third of his season total. Pittsburgh’s passing game has leaned short, with 26.2% of Rodgers’ attempts going at or behind the line of scrimmage.

Michael Pittman Jr. and Roman Wilson remain out, so Bernard should stay involved. The under is based on yardage efficiency rather than a reduced snap count. DK Metcalf, Jaylen Warren and Pittsburgh’s tight ends still spread the targets across the formation.

Aaron Rodgers Under 36.5 Pass Attempts (-130)

FTN projects Rodgers for 33.27 attempts, giving the under a 10.57% edge against 36.5. Rodgers attempted 34 passes in the first meeting with Cincinnati and has stayed below this line in four of eight starts.

Pittsburgh has reduced his workload in its past two games. Rodgers threw 30 passes in a 16-10 win over New Orleans and 25 in a 27-6 win over Cleveland. Jaylen Warren enters Week 10 averaging 5.9 yards per carry, and Rico Dowdle had 15 carries in the Steelers’ last game.

A Cincinnati lead is the main threat because Pittsburgh could abandon the run. The -130 price remains within FTN’s playable range, and the model leaves more than three attempts of room below the line.
