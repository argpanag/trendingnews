Lili Reinhart's Plunging Lace Micro Minidress Gets a Jimmy Choo Upgrade

TheTools · 18.09.2026 08:38 · source · permalink

Lili Reinhart embraced effortless Y2K-inspired glamour while promoting “The Love Hypothesis” in New York City. The actress stepped out in a plunging lace micro minidress layered beneath a vintage-inspired coat, proving that slip dresses remain a timeless fashion staple. She completed the sophisticated look with a pair of elegant Jimmy Choo heels, giving the romantic outfit a polished finish.

Lili Reinhart elevates plunging lace micro minidress with chic Jimmy Choo heels for ‘The Love Hypothesis’ press

View this post on Instagram

The star wore Fleur du Mal’s Juliet Lace Mini Slip in the rich Cocoa Plum shade. Crafted from luxurious silk charmeuse, the dress featured a plunging neckline and adjustable spaghetti straps.

Additionally, delicate black floral lace appliqués gracefully traced both the neckline and asymmetrical hem. The lingerie-inspired silhouette hugged Lili Reinhart’s figure while the intricate lace trim added a feminine, vintage touch.

Reinhart paired the outfit with Jimmy Choo Purple Watersnake Bow Slides. The shaded purple leather sandals featured a subtle watersnake finish, delicate bow details across the vamp, and leather-lined insoles. The designer heels, valued at $224, perfectly complemented the plum tones of her dress while offering an understated pop of color.

Layered over the slip dress was a textured cream Nanette Lepore coat featuring dark contrast piping along the notched lapels. The vintage outerwear, reminiscent of Jennifer Garner’s iconic wardrobe in “13 Going on 30,” added structure and warmth while allowing glimpses of the cocoa-plum dress underneath. The nostalgic styling gave the entire ensemble a chic early-2000s feel.

The actress accessorized minimally, opting for slim, retro-inspired black oval sunglasses that enhanced the vintage aesthetic. Her blonde hair was styled in loose, soft waves with a center part, while her beauty look remained fresh and natural. Rosy blush, glossy pink lips, softly defined eyes, and a luminous complexion completed the effortless daytime glam.
