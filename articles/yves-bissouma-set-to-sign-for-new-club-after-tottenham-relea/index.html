Yves Bissouma set to sign for new club after Tottenham release

TheTools · 15.09.2026 15:32 · source · permalink

Former Tottenham Hotspur star Yves Bissouma is reportedly closing in on completing a move to a new side, just a few months after leaving the club. Bissouma left Spurs in the summer following the expiration of his contract at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

The Mali international joined Tottenham from Brighton in the summer of 2022 for £30million, and he went on to make 124 appearances for the club across his four years in north London.

Bissouma only made 11 appearances during his final season with Tottenham, with his last outing coming on May 3 with a cameo from the bench in a 2-1 win over Aston Villa.

The midfielder suffered from a combination of knee and ankle injuries during his final season with Tottenham, which restricted the minutes that he was able to get.

Roberto De Zerbi named Bissouma on the bench for the final three Premier League matches of the season, where the 30-year-old remained an unused substitute.

Now, it seems that Bissouma could be about to join a new club after almost three months as a free agent.

De Telegraaf have reported that Bissouma is expected to undergo a medical ahead of a proposed move to Ajax on a free transfer.

The report claims that Ajax and Bissouma have reached an agreement on a one-year contract that will run until the summer of 2027, which will also include the option to extend by a further 12 months.

It remains to be seen if and when a deal could be announced, with the report suggesting that there is only the medical to be completed before Bissouma officially becomes an Ajax player.

Tottenham moved to strengthen their midfield options following Bissouma’s departure, with Sandro Tonali and Mateus Fernandes both signing for the club on permanent deals.

Spurs also brought in new additions to their forward line with Omar Marmoush, Savio and Mykhailo Mudryk, while the defence also saw the arrivals of Andy Robertson, Marcos Senesi, Tosin Adarabioyo and Jan Paul van Hecke, as well as goalkeeper Martin Dubravka.
