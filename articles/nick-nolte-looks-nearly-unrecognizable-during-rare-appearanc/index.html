Nick Nolte Looks Nearly Unrecognizable During Rare Appearance at Venice Film Festival

TheTools · 12.09.2026 22:03 · source · permalink

Celebrity News

Nick Nolte looked nearly unrecognizable as he made a rare public appearance at the Venice Film Festival.

The veteran actor, 85, stepped out for a photocall for his new film Company at the 83rd Venice Film Festival on Tuesday, September 8. Nolte joined costars Casey Affleck, Scoot McNairy and Caylee Cowan at the event ahead of the film’s festival premiere.

With his full white beard and thinning hair swept back from his face, Nolte looked worlds away from the rugged blond heartthrob who shot to fame in the 1970s. After becoming a sex symbol with the 1976 miniseries Rich Man, Poor Man, Nolte cemented his leading-man status with films including The Deep, North Dallas Forty and 48 Hrs. He remained a major Hollywood star well into the 1990s, starring in The Prince of Tides and Cape Fear.

The Venice outing comes more than a year after Closer reported that Nolte had settled into a quieter life in Malibu with his wife, Clytie Lane. “Nick and Clytie have this quiet, old-school kind of love that you rarely see in Hollywood anymore,” an insider exclusively told Closer. “They’ve been through so much together. Clytie has been Nick’s rock, she keeps him grounded, and healthy. He says all the time that he’d be lost without her and it’s true, she’s been such a stabilizing force in his life.”

Nolte and Lane tied the knot in 2016 and share a daughter Sophia, who was born in 2007, per Today. The father-daughter duo even appeared together in the 2018 film Head Full of Honey, with Sophia, 18, playing his granddaughter onscreen.

Despite embracing a slower lifestyle away from the Hollywood scene, Nolte has made it clear that he has no plans to walk away from acting. “I don’t understand retirement. I just don’t know what I’d do with myself,” he said in 2018, per the New York Post. “I’ll just keep at it until it doesn’t make any sense anymore.”
