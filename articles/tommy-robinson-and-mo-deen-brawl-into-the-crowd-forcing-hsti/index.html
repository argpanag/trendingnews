Tommy Robinson and Mo Deen brawl into the crowd forcing HSTikkyTokky Unhinged Promotions event cancellation

TheTools · 08.11.2026 07:05 · source · permalink

Tommy Robinson and Mo Deen’s bitter brawl forced an early end to an influencer fight night in Ireland tonight.

Controversial political activist Robinson – who was once the leader of the EDL – was ringside to watch Unhinged Promotions 3. The event was hosted by influencer HSTikkyTokky, and featured unlicensed Celebrity Boxing matches.

It was supposed to be headlined by a battle between rappers Russ Millions and Kirky, with Mo Deen fighting on the undercard. However, when the promoters attempted to start the build for a future fight between him and Robinson, chaos ensued.

Despite as recently as last month indicating some of the worst boxing skills and most limited heart in history, Mo Deen managed to record a win this evening. He faced fellow influencer MrTikTokUK, and managed to drop him three times before securing a stoppage.

After the fight, he lunged out of the ring over the announcers’ desk, where HSTikkyTokky and co-owner Ed Matthews were speaking about the event. He was attempting to get to Tommy Robinson, who had been watching the show from ringside.

Even after getting their clip and causing a reasonable amount of property damage, the promoters decided that they needed an in-ring face-off between Mo Deen – a Muslim – and former EDL leader Tommy Robinson.

The Luton native was wildly unpopular with the diverse Irish crowd and they made their voices heard loudly. However, HSTikkyTokky persisted with forcing a face-off, which meant bringing Mo Deen back to the ring.

Robinson taunted his rival with a book, and the pair immediately got physical once within touching distance. This led to an arena-wide brawl at The Hub in Kilkenny, where the event had attracted hundreds of young fans.

Chairs were thrown amid the melee, and eventually the host – whose real name is Harrison Sullivan – was forced by officials to call a stop to the show.
