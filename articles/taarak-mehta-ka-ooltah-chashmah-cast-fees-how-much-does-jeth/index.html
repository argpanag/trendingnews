Taarak Mehta Ka Ooltah Chashmah Cast Fees: How Much Does Jethalal, Bhide, Babita Ji And Other Cast Members Earn Per Episode?

TheTools · 30.10.2026 15:44 · source · permalink

For viewers, an episode of Taarak Mehta Ka Ooltah Chashmah means another round of Jethalal's troubles, Bhide's complaints and the residents of Gokuldham Society getting caught up in a new situation. From Daya's garba to Tapu Sena's adventures, the sitcom has built its humour around characters who have remained familiar to audiences for years.

The show premiered in July 2008 and has continued to feature a large ensemble of actors. Naturally, this raises another question: how much do these actors reportedly earn for each episode?

These figures are media-reported estimates, not salaries officially confirmed by the actors or the production team.

Jethalal's Paycheque: How Much Does Dilip Joshi Earn?

Dilip Joshi, who plays Jethalal Champaklal Gada, reportedly earns between Rs. 1.5 lakh and Rs. 2 lakh per episode, according to the August 2026 report.

Jethalal's business troubles, his exchanges with Bhide and his interactions with Bapuji have made him central to the sitcom's comedy. His on-screen relationship with Babita Ji is another recurring source of humour.

Joshi has also appeared in films such as Maine Pyar Kiya and Hum Aapke Hain Koun..!. His reported per-episode fee places him at the top of the salary estimates covered in the report.

Babita Ji And Iyer: What Do Munmun Dutta And Tanuj Mahashabde Earn?

Munmun Dutta, who plays Babita Ji, reportedly earns Rs. 75,000 to Rs. 80,000 per episode.

Her character's friendly interactions with Jethalal often lead to comic situations, while her on-screen relationship with her husband, Krishnan Iyer, adds another dimension to the show's neighbourhood dynamics.

Tanuj Mahashabde, who plays Iyer, reportedly earns Rs. 40,000 to Rs. 45,000 per episode. His frequent arguments with Jethalal, particularly over Babita Ji, have become a familiar part of the sitcom.

Bapuji And Bhide: The Other Big Earners In Gokuldham

Amit Bhatt, who plays Champaklal Gada, or Bapuji, reportedly earns Rs. 70,000 to Rs. 75,000 per episode.

Bapuji regularly scolds his son, Jethalal, but shares a softer relationship with his grandson, Tapu. His presence often brings a family angle to the show's comedy.

Mandar Chandwadkar, who plays Gokuldham Society's secretary, Atmaram Bhide, reportedly earns around Rs. 80,000 per episode. His insistence on discipline and frequent disagreements with Jethalal have made him another central character.

Sonalika Joshi, who plays Madhavi Bhide, reportedly earns Rs. 40,000 to Rs. 50,000 per episode. Madhavi runs a pickle and papad business in the show's fictional world and often balances her work with her family responsibilities.

Daya And Tapu: What Were Their Reported Fees?

Daya, played by Disha Vakani, was one of the show's defining characters. Her garba performances, distinctive mannerisms and interactions with Jethalal became an integral part of the Gada family's story.

Vakani has been away from the main television series for years. A 2024 Radio City report estimated that she earned Rs. 1 lakh to Rs. 1.5 lakh per episode during her stint. This is a historical estimate, not a current salary.

Tapu, Jethalal and Daya's son, has also been played by different actors. Bhavya Gandhi originally portrayed the character, followed by Raj Anadkat. Nitish Bhaluni later took over the role.

A report estimated Bhaluni's fee at around Rs. 20,000 per episode. 

Popatlal: How Much Does Shyam Pathak Earn?

Shyam Pathak, who plays journalist Popatlal, reportedly earns Rs. 60,000 to Rs. 65,000 per episode.

Popatlal's persistent search for a life partner has been a recurring storyline. His anxious reactions and repeated hopes of getting married often bring him into comic situations with other Gokuldham residents.

His reported fee places him below the show's highest-paid stars but above several supporting cast members in the published estimates.

Taarak And Anjali Mehta: The Couple Behind Jethalal's Stories

Taarak Mehta is Jethalal's closest friend and confidant in Gokuldham Society, often helping him navigate everyday troubles. Shailesh Lodha originally played the character before Sachin Shroff took over the role. According to a 2021 India Today report, Lodha earned around Rs. 1 lakh per episode during his stint on the show.

Taarak's home life is another recurring part of the sitcom, particularly his interactions with his wife, Anjali Mehta. Neha Mehta played Anjali from the show's launch in 2008 until she left in 2020, when Sunayana Fozdar took over the role.

According to Koimoi, Neha Mehta reportedly earned around Rs. 35,000 per episode, while Fozdar's reported fee is approximately 14% less than Neha's. Anjali's focus on healthy eating often leads to comic exchanges with Taarak, giving the couple their own distinct place in the show's everyday stories.

Tapu Sena: What About Sonu, Goli, Gogi And Pinku?

Tapu Sena brings a different kind of humour to Taarak Mehta Ka Ooltah Chashmah, with school friendships, pranks and adventures forming a key part of the show's storylines. Alongside Tapu, the group includes Sonu, Goli, Gogi and Pinku, with several actors taking on these roles over the years.

A 2025 ABP report put Tapu Sena members' fees at around Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 15,000 per episode, with individual figures varying by actor.

Bagha, Nattu Kaka And Abdul: The Faces From Gada Electronics

Gada Electronics has several familiar faces, including Bagha, played by Tanmay Vekaria, and Nattu Kaka, played by Ghanashyam Nayak. Bagha works at Jethalal's shop, while Nattu Kaka was known for his role as the shop's older employee. According to Koimoi, Vekaria reportedly earns around Rs. 22,000 to Rs. 25,000 per episode.

Nayak died in October 2021 after battling cancer. A 2021 Jansatta report put his fee at around Rs. 30,000 per episode. After Nayak's death, Kiran Bhatt took over the role of Nattu Kaka, with Koimoi reporting a fee of around Rs. 10,000 per episode in August 2024.

Sharad Sankla plays Abdul, the shopkeeper who is also a familiar presence in Gokuldham Society. According to Filmibeat, Sankla reportedly earned around Rs. 35,000 to Rs. 40,000 per episode.

Dr. Hathi, Komal, Sodhi And Sundarlal: The Wider Cast

The sitcom's supporting characters bring their own family dynamics and comic moments to Gokuldham Society. Dr. Hansraj Hathi, played by Nirmal Soni, and his wife Komal, played by Ambika Ranjankar, are regular members of the community. Their storylines often revolve around family life, food and health.

According to Koimoi, Ranjankar reportedly earns around Rs. 30,000 per episode for playing Komal. Nirmal Soni's reported fee is around Rs. 20,000 to Rs. 25,000 per episode.

Roshan Singh Sodhi is another familiar face in the society. Gurucharan Singh, who previously played the character, was reportedly paid around Rs. 65,000 per episode during his stint on the show.

Mayur Vakani plays Sundarlal, Daya's brother, whose visits often leave Jethalal dealing with unexpected complications. Vakani reportedly e0arns around Rs. 20,000 per episode.

The Bottom Line: Who Tops The Reported Salary List?

Dilip Joshi, who plays Jethalal, tops the latest published salary estimates for Taarak Mehta Ka Ooltah Chashmah. Mandar Chandwadkar, Munmun Dutta and Amit Bhatt also rank among the show's highest-paid cast members, reflecting their long-running roles as Bhide, Babita Ji and Champaklal.

The cast's earnings also reflect the different stages of the show's history. While established actors continue to lead reported fee lists, performers such as Disha Vakani and the actors who have played Tapu and Nattu Kaka have each contributed to the sitcom's legacy through memorable characters that audiences continue to associate with the show.
