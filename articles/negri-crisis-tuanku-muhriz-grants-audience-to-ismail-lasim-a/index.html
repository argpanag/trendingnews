Negri crisis: Tuanku Muhriz grants audience to Ismail Lasim ahead of meet with senior Umno leaders

TheTools · 26.11.2026 11:18 · source · permalink
    bookmark

SEREMBAN: Tuanku Muhriz Tuanku Munawir has granted Datuk Ismail Lasim an audience on Thursday (Nov 26) afternoon, a meeting expected to break the months-long political impasse in the state.

The Yang di-Pertuan Besar is scheduled to meet the Mentri Besar at Istana Hinggap here, with Ismail's position topping the agenda.

It is learnt that Ismail will offer to resign as he has been unable to come up with a list of candidates to fill the vacant exco positions to ensure the smooth running of his administration.

A source said Ismail was also considering stepping down as he was running out of time to table the state Budget for 2027.

After meeting Ismail, Tuanku Muhriz is scheduled to meet senior Umno leaders including its president Datuk Seri Dr Ahmad Zahid Hamidi and his deputy Datuk Seri Mohamad Hasan.

The Umno leaders are expected to brief the Ruler on the state's political situation after 24 of the 25 Barisan Nasional and Perikatan Nasional assemblymen in the state gave the mandate to the party leadership to decide who should be the Mentri Besar.

A source said a decision if Ismail remains as MB or if he will be replaced will be known by Friday (Nov 27) at the latest.

On Nov 16, Barisan secretary-general Datuk Seri Zambry Abd Kadir had, in a statement, announced Ismail's resignation.

He said Ahmad Zahid had also received a copy of the resignation letter, addressed to Tuanku Muhriz.

The following day, the Negri Sembilan palace clarified that Ismail remained Mentri Besar pending royal assent, stressing that any resignation only took effect after it was accepted by the Ruler in accordance with the state constitution.

Ahmad Zahid then said Barisan had also submitted one name for royal consideration as the next mentri besar.

He added that any political resolution in Negri Sembilan must respect the state constitution and the authority of the Ruler.

On Nov 20, Ismail sought an audience with Tuanku Muhriz and later told reporters that he had neither submitted nor consented to the submission of the resignation letter, claiming it had been held by Umno's leadership.

He then said Tuanku Muhriz had also decreed that he continue to serve as mentri besar.

The palace later clarified that Ismail must, however, prove he commands majority support in the 36-seat state assembly.

On the same night, 22 of the 25 Barisan and Perikatan assemblymen met and reached a consensus to abide by any directive from their top leadership to resolve the political turmoil in the state.

Two others who were abroad also gave their full support to any decision made at the meeting, which Ismail did not attend.

On Sunday (Nov 22), Ismail's aide said that he had been admitted to a private hospital here and that his condition was improving. He did not give details.

Attempts to get comments on the latest developments from Barisan assemblymen including state Umno chief Datuk Seri Jalaluddin Alias since then have been unsuccessful.
