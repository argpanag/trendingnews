Still Figuring Out Diwali Gifting? Here Are 20 Ideas To Get You Started - MissMalini

TheTools · 05.11.2026 07:24 · source · permalink

Diwali gifting is one of those things that sounds exciting until you actually have to make a list. You want something thoughtful, a little different and, most importantly, something the person receiving it will genuinely enjoy. After all, another box of assorted sweets is lovely, but sometimes it’s nice to surprise your friends and family with something they wouldn’t necessarily buy for themselves.

Whether you’re shopping for someone who loves beauty, has a thing for fashion, enjoys experimenting in the kitchen or simply loves making their home look pretty, there’s plenty to explore this festive season. From fragrances and skincare treats to statement accessories, gourmet hampers and even a quirky pair of clogs, here are 20 Diwali gifting ideas worth adding to your list.

20 Diwali gifting Ideas for the beauty and fashion lover

1) LIBRE Eau de Parfum And MYSLF Eau de Parfum By YSL Beauty

Some gifts make an impression even before they’re unwrapped, and fragrances are definitely one of them. YSL Beauty’s LIBRE Eau de Parfum blends lavender with orange blossom, musk and vanilla for a bold floral scent. Meanwhile, MYSLF Eau de Parfum brings together bergamot, orange blossom and woody notes for a fresher, contemporary fragrance. Whether you’re gifting someone who loves a statement scent or someone who prefers something more understated, this pairing has both moods covered.

2) Lancôme Sonam’s Festive Faves

A beauty hamper is always a welcome surprise, especially when it comes with a little bit of everything. Curated with Sonam Kapoor’s favourites, this box includes Idôle Eau de Parfum, the new Curl Goddess Mascara in Black and Génifique Ultimate Recovery Serum. The serum features Beta-Glucan-CM and is designed to help repair and strengthen the skin barrier. It’s a lovely combination of fragrance, makeup and skincare in convenient sizes.

3) Old School Rituals Arrack Of Rice & Bananas Cleansing Nectar

Skincare gifts don’t always have to involve elaborate routines or complicated ingredients. This cleansing nectar takes inspiration from traditional ingredients, bringing together black rice, Navara rice, bamboo rice and banana extract. It gently cleanses the skin while helping retain moisture, leaving it feeling soft and refreshed. Add it to a self-care hamper for someone who enjoys discovering interesting skincare finds.

4) Casa Di Naso Gift Box By Naso

Why stop at gifting a personal fragrance when you can give someone an entire sensory experience? Casa Di Naso brings together two 50 ml Eau de Parfums, a 100 ml Surround Scent, incense sticks and a 310 g soy wax candle. It’s a thoughtful collection for someone who enjoys filling their home with beautiful scents, whether they’re hosting friends or simply winding down after a long day.

5) Pink Blend Printed Straight Suit Set By Biba

A pretty ethnic outfit is always a good idea during Diwali. Biba’s pink printed straight suit set comes with a kurta, matching solid pants and a coordinating dupatta. The embroidered round neckline, delicate motifs and three-quarter sleeves give it a classic festive appeal. Comfortable enough for daytime celebrations and pretty enough for family get-togethers, it’s an outfit that won’t need much styling to look complete.

6) Texas Maison Silver Clutch By Lino Perros

Sometimes, all an outfit needs is the right bag. The Texas Maison Silver Clutch by Lino Perros features pearl and crystal embellishments that give it a dressy, occasion-ready finish. Its crystal-studded top handle makes it a lovely accessory to carry by hand, while the detachable shoulder strap offers another way to style it. Think festive dinners, wedding functions and all those occasions when a regular handbag simply won’t do.

7) Red Full Rim Cat Eye By Masaba x John Jacobs

Eyewear can completely change the way an outfit comes together, and this collaboration proves it. The Masaba x John Jacobs collection combines John Jacobs’ premium eyewear design with Masaba Gupta’s bold aesthetic. This red full-rim cat-eye pair features striking red and gold detailing and a sleek, narrow fit. It’s a fun gift for someone who enjoys accessories that stand out rather than blend in.

8) Honey Hobo Bag By Miraggio

From a morning coffee date to an evening reservation, this bag is designed to keep up with a busy schedule. Miraggio’s Honey Hobo Bag has a spacious compartment for everyday essentials, along with a detachable zipper compartment and a slip pocket to keep smaller belongings organised. The asymmetric silhouette and abstract gold accent add a distinctive touch without making the design difficult to style.

9) Odyssey Laptop Backpack By Harissons

Not every Diwali gift needs to be decorative. Harissons’ Odyssey backpack is a practical option for someone who is constantly travelling between meetings, workspaces and destinations. With a 40-litre capacity, it accommodates a laptop of up to 15.6 inches, along with gadgets, work essentials and even a separate clothing compartment. The interiors also include a tablet sleeve, gadget organiser, sunglass holder and dedicated laptop cradle. Available in Midnight Black and Titanium Grey, it makes a useful gift that can travel well beyond the festive season.

10) Birkenstock Foot Care Essential – Softening Ritual

We spend plenty of time looking after our face and hair, but our feet could use some attention too. Birkenstock’s Softening Ritual set makes foot care a little easier with an Exfoliating Foot Scrub and Nourishing Foot Balm, both in 30 ml sizes. It’s a compact duo for someone who enjoys a little at-home pampering after long days of festive shopping and celebrations.

11) The Anaajkaar Hamper By Two Brothers

This hamper is practically an invitation to gather everyone around the table. Two Brothers’ Anaajkaar brings together traditional snacks, festive sweets and a Diwali-themed deck of playing cards. There’s Khapli Mathri, Shankarpali, banana chips, spiced jaggery bites, kaju katli made with jaggery and misri, and date-based laddoos. The mix of sweet and savoury treats makes it perfect for those long festive evenings when everyone keeps reaching for one more bite. And the playing cards? That’s an added reason to keep the family together a little longer.

12) Bertolli Dress & Drizz Extra Virgin Olive Oil

For someone who enjoys spending time in the kitchen, this is a gift that is both thoughtful and practical. Bertolli Dress & Drizz Extra Virgin Olive Oil is cold-extracted from carefully selected olives to retain its natural antioxidants, rich flavour and Mediterranean character. What makes it particularly handy is its specially designed bottle, which allows you to dress salads or drizzle olive oil over finished dishes with better control and less mess. From roasted vegetables and dips to everyday meals that need a finishing touch, it’s an easy way to add a little something extra to the table. The bottle is also made from 100% recycled plastic, making it a useful addition to a modern kitchen.

13) Lasyi Purple Fitted Mini Dress By Virgio

If your friend is more likely to celebrate Diwali with a night out than a traditional family gathering, this one might be right up her street. Virgio’s Lasyi mini dress comes in purple, with a boat neckline, cap sleeves and ethnic-inspired motifs. Its fitted silhouette and concealed side zip give it a neat finish, making it a fun alternative to the usual festive outfit.

14) Crocs Women’s Classic Saturday Sandal

If your Diwali gifting list includes someone who likes their everyday style with a bit of sparkle, these sandals are worth a look. Inspired by Crocs’ iconic Classic Clog, the Women’s Classic Saturday Sandal comes with a tinted metallic upper and shiny metallic buckles that give the simple two-strap design a fun update. The adjustable straps make it easy to find a comfortable fit, while the lightweight Croslite™ foam footbeds keep things comfy. They’re also easy to clean, quick to dry and come with holes for Jibbitz™ charms, so the wearer can add a little personality to every step.

15) Classic Filter Coffee Yeast Protein By Happy Cultures

If your gym buddy is always talking about their protein intake, here’s a gift they might actually get excited about. Happy Cultures’ Classic Filter Coffee Yeast Protein delivers 24 grams of protein per scoop at 125 calories. Made with 83% fermented yeast protein isolate, it also contains BCAAs and 18 amino acids, with no added sugar or lactose. The filter coffee and cocoa flavour gives it a familiar Indian coffee-inspired twist, making it an interesting addition to a fitness enthusiast’s routine.

16) Pure Brass Ganesh And Lakshmi Idol Set By Nestasia

A gift with a traditional touch can feel especially meaningful during Diwali. Nestasia’s miniature Ganesh and Lakshmi idol set features two intricately detailed brass figures seated on lotus-inspired bases. The antique-inspired finish gives the pair a timeless appearance, while their compact size makes them suitable for a mandir, prayer corner or display shelf. The gift box also makes it ready to present during the festive season.

17) Pineapple Club Game Night Essentials Set By FRAGNOTE

Forget the usual gifting hamper; this one is made for a little friendly competition. FRAGONOTE’s Pineapple Club set includes a 50 ml Pineapple Club Eau de Parfum, playing cards, poker chips and a card wallet. It’s an unusual combination that brings together fragrance and entertainment, making it a fun pick for someone who loves hosting friends and keeping the evening lively.

18) Mom’s Therapy – Abhyanga Ritual Box

The festive season can be exciting, but between social commitments, celebrations and endless to-do lists, taking a moment for yourself often takes a backseat. If you want to slow down and prioritise mindful self-care, Mom’s Therapy’s Abhyanga Ritual Box is a thoughtful addition to your wellness routine. Rooted in the ancient Ayurvedic practice of Abhyanga, which focuses on mindful oil massage and body care, the ritual is designed to help you release, restore and reconnect with yourself.

This thoughtfully curated box brings together a nourishing Massage Oil, a lightweight pre and post shower oil formulated for dry skin, and a traditional Kansa Wand. Each product encourages a more intentional approach to everyday body care, turning an otherwise ordinary routine into an opportunity to pause and unwind.

19) Aqua Eclipse By Cida

Not everyone gravitates towards heavy, warm perfumes. Cida’s Aqua Eclipse takes a fresher approach, opening with citrus notes before moving into a softer, warmer fragrance profile. Inspired by the meeting of the shore and the sea, it has a calm, airy character that feels elegant without being overpowering. It’s a lovely option for someone who enjoys fragrances that are subtle yet memorable.

20) Chocolate Obsession By Nisara

If there’s one fragrance that sounds like a dessert menu, it’s Nisara’s Chocolate Obsession. Inspired by the richness of fine chocolate, the fragrance combines bergamot, orange and plum with a heart of chocolate, raspberry, florals and Tolu balsam. Cedarwood, vanilla and praline round out the base, giving it a warm, indulgent finish. It’s particularly suited to date nights and parties, making it an interesting pick for someone who likes their fragrances on the sweeter side.

The best gifts are often the ones that make someone feel seen. And with so many options across beauty, fashion, food, wellness and home, there’s plenty of room to find something that feels just right this festive season. Happy gifting and Happy Diwali!
