Has Xavi Espart become an undisputed starter for Barcelona?

TheTools · 23.10.2026 15:00 · source · permalink
  • Cant del Blog: Barcelona Opinion

Time to talk the teenage full-back

The best chapter yet of Xavi Espart’s incredible rise this season took place on Tuesday in Paris, when the 19-year-old full-back had a fantastic performance against reigning back-to-back Champions League winner PSG at the Parc des Princes.

Espart started at right-back and dominated his matchup against one of the best wingers in the world in Kvicha Kvaratskhelia, and scored a great goal to cap off a brilliant individual display despite Barça’s defeat at the French capital.

The midfielder turned full-back is a key part of the lineup especially with all the injuries in defense, but Espart continues to make a strong case to be a key part of the lineup even once the squad is fully healthy once again.

So today’s Barca Blaugranes Question of the Day is:

Has Xavi Espart become an undisputed starter for Barcelona?
