Chelsea fans’ preferred lineup against Leeds United: Back four and Mike Penders

TheTools · 21.11.2026 09:15 · source · permalink
  • Chelsea FC: Premier League

Chelsea vs. Leeds United, Premier League: Preferred lineup

Prior to the international break, Chelsea had begun to use a back-four setup instead of the back-three that we had seen for much of the early season. Whether Xabi Alonso sticks to the new shape or the old after the break, for the WAGNH Community, it’s the former that’s preferred, by about a 65-35 margin combined.

That’s a landslide compared to the battle for goalkeeper, where young Mike Penders narrowly edges out the veteran Emiliano Martínez, 50-48, with Gaga Slonina and Teddy Sharman-Lowe sharing the remaining tiny slice of that pie (chart).

The rest of the lineup is fairly familiar. Up front, neither Emmanuel Emegha (39%) nor Danny Welbeck (28%) are able to get a spot so we’re once again without a striker, while at the back, Wesley Fofana (50%) just misses out. In midfield, Jordan Henderson (26%) watches on as a distant third.

4-2-3-1 (52%)
Penders (50%) | Chavarría (83%), Colwill (94%), Acheampong (94%), Palestra (53%) | Lavia (86%), Caicedo (94%) | Rogers (96%), Palmer (98%), Estêvão (71%) | Neto (54%)

Malo Gusto (11%) and Aarón Anselmino (10%) were the only others to get in the double-digits.
