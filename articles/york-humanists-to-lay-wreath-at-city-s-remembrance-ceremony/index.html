York Humanists to lay wreath at city's Remembrance ceremony

TheTools · 08.11.2026 07:01 · source · permalink

York Humanists will lay a wreath for the first time at the York Memorial Gardens.

The group will take part in the ceremony at York Memorial Gardens on Sunday, November 8, joining civic dignitaries, armed forces personnel, and members of the public in paying tribute to those who lost their lives in service.

Rachel Austin, joint co-ordinator of York Humanists, said: "We represent the many thousands of people of York who want to demonstrate their sharing in the heartfelt trauma of those who fought for Great Britain, but who have no religious affiliation.

"It is about the ordinary people of York who gave their lives, mostly young men who had no choice but to fight and to put themselves at risk."

The wreath will be laid by Geoff Houlton, fellow joint co-ordinator of York Humanists.

Andrew Copson, chief executive of Humanists UK, said: "Remembrance is a time for unity as we honour the courage and humanity of all who have served.

"Humanist participation makes sure that remembrance speaks to the whole of our society – a shared commitment to peace, compassion and understanding."

Humanists UK has been involved in the national Remembrance ceremony at the Cenotaph since 2018.
