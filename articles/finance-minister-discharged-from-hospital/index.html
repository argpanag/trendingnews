Finance minister discharged from hospital

TheTools · 03.11.2026 21:49 · source · permalink

However, he will not return to work immediately and will remain on sick leave until Friday, Deputy Finance Minister Jolanta Butkevičienė confirmed to ELTA on Wednesday.

"Yes, the minister has been discharged from hospital. He is now on sick leave," Butkevičienė told ELTA.

According to her, the minister has been granted sick leave until 30 October, with Transport and Communications Minister Juras Taminskas standing in for him during his absence.

As previously reported by ELTA, Valys felt unwell at work on Monday morning and consulted his family doctor, who referred him to a cardiologist.

However, his condition later worsened, prompting him to seek treatment at the Emergency Department of Vilnius University Hospital Santaros Clinics.

On Tuesday evening, it was reported that Valys would remain in hospital for treatment.
