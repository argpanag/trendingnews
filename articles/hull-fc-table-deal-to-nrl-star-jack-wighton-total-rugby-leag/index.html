Hull FC ‘table deal’ to NRL star Jack Wighton – Total Rugby League

TheTools · 19.11.2026 11:28 · source · permalink

HULL FC have reportedly tabled a three-year deal for South Sydney Rabbitohs star Jack Wighton.

The Rabbitohs are weighing up a move to sign Sydney Roosters’ Australia international halfback Sam Walker as early as next season.

That means South Sydney will attempt to move on big earners – and Wighton’s name is one that popped up late last week, with Super League and NRL clubs interested.

No Super League clubs were mentioned at the time, but that has now changed, with veteran journalist Buzz Rothfield stating that Hull FC are interested in the 33-year-old – and that a three-year deal has been tabled.

If Hull were able to pull off the signing, Wighton – who played seven times for the Kangaroos, most recently in their World Cup success of 2022 in England – would offer utility value at fullback, centre or in the halves.

Wighton has scored 16 tries in 55 appearances for Souths since joining three years ago from Canberra Raiders, where he registered 75 tries in 242 appearances.

Speaking on The Bye Round podcast, Rothfield said of the Walker situation: “We all know Souths don’t have the cap space.

“They’re going to have to move Jack Wighton across to Hull in the UK. There’s talk that Hull have offered him three years on quite attractive money which makes this a realistic chance of happening.”
