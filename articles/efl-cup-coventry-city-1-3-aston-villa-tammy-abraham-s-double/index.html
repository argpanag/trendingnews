  7. 'We have to find the answers for Sunday'published at 22:42 BST 16 September
    22:42 BST 16 September

    FT: Man Utd 2-3 Brighton

    Manchester United

    Manchester United boss Michael Carrick, speaking to BBC Radio 5 Live: "It seems a long time ago now but the start of the game was fantastic. It was everything we hoped for, even more really, in some ways in Shea [Lacey] getting his goal. The night's gone in a different direction but I think for Shea for the moment in the academy and everyone who's been connected with his journey, to have a moment like which he should be celebrating and enjoying a little bit more really to have that start of the game. Then we let the game get away from us with the longer the game went.

    "We ended up with a pretty disappointing night. It's obvious for me to say that. We accept that. We can't be in a position like that and lose the game in that manner. I'll understand the disappointments and frustrations. I accept that. That's on me. It'll need to be better than that."

    On whether he's addressed that: "We can't change that. It's done. We've got to keep evolving as a group. We have to keep learning and dealing with situations. Sometimes they come a bit easier than others. Tonight was a tough one. We didn't find a way out of it. That's up to me and the staff to help the players and guide them through it. I think we all know that in the end tonight feels like a big disappointment.

    "We've had small, little things that have gone against us in the last week or two and then it accumulates to feel that it's a lot bigger. I think we have to be honest and say that. The late goal against Everton from outside the box. The [Manchester] City goal the other day. It can all of a sudden roll into something a bit greater but I understand the disappointment because we shouldn't finish the game in that manner with the position we were in and we have to be humble and take that.

    "We have to find the answers for Sunday."

22:29 BST 16 September

Here we go, the fourth-round draw is about to get under way...

Tammy Abraham had scored three goals in 21 appearances for Aston Villa since joining in January

Tammy Abraham's double helped Aston Villa overcome Coventry City to reach the Carabao Cup fourth round.

The striker made his point to Unai Emery after the Villa boss was willing to let him leave in the summer.

Abraham rejected loan moves to Ipswich and Everton in the transfer window to fight for his place, with Emery then calling for him to produce more.

Two calm finishes, either side of Ross Barkley's header, underlined the striker's desire to prove his worth following the £65m arrival of Nicolas Jackson from Chelsea.

Abraham's crucial contribution helped ease Villa through as they look to find some momentum after a difficult start to the season, with Emery's men losing three of their opening four Premier League matches.

They were briefly rattled when the Sky Blues levelled through Victor Torp's fine free-kick and the hosts pressed but it was ultimately a comfortable win with Coventry's season already becoming a nightmare.

Struggling Coventry scored their first home goal of the campaign through Torp, but suffered a fifth defeat in their opening six games.

Abraham, one of seven Villa changes to the side that lost to Nottingham Forest on Saturday, had scored just once in his previous 16 appearances - back in April - but got the opener after seven minutes

Torp levelled when he found the top corner from 25 yards and Gustavo Hamer went close with a long-range strike.

But the Sky Blues' soft centre gifted Villa two more goals.

Coventry have now conceded 15 goals in all competitions this season and followed up Sunday's 5-0 rout at home to Brighton by allowing an unmarked Barkley to glance in a corner before the break.

John McGinn had earlier gone close, but Abraham sealed victory just before the hour mark when he shrugged off debutant Stephen Mfuni to tap in McGinn's corner.

Villa face a trip to Bournemouth in round four, with ties taking place during the week commencing 26 October.
