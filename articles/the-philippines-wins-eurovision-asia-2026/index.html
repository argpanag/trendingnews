The Philippines wins Eurovision Asia 2026

TheTools · 14.11.2026 16:00 · source · permalink

Carmelle from the Philippines wins the very first Eurovision Song Contest Asia with the song "Sobra-Sobra"

One Night in Bangkok: The first Eurovision Asia took place today live from One Bangkok Forum in Thailand.

11 countries competed at this year's edition. And once all was said and done, Carmelle from the Philippines was declared the winner of the Eurovision Song Contest Asia 2026 with the song "Sobra-Sobra".

"Sobra-Sobra" was written by Camille herself along with Mart Sam Emmanuel Olavides.

Eurovision Asia 2026

Eurovision Asia 2026

Broadcast live from One Bangkok Forum in Thailand, this very first Eurovision Asia was hosted by Woody Milintachinda and Cindy Bishop.

Carmelle from Philippines was crowned the winner based on voting from national juries and the public in all 11 competing countries. Viewers from around the world could also vote for their favorite songs.

Carmelle

Carmelle Collado is a Filipino singer from Camarines Sur. She first reached a national audience in 2019, finishing second in the fourth season of The Voice Kids as a member of coach Bamboo's team.

In January 2025, she won the School Showdown edition of Tawag ng Tanghalan, performing a medley of Aretha Franklin songs in the final. Her prize included recording and management contracts with Star Music and Star Magic. She has also appeared on reality show Pinoy Big Brother.

Eurovision Asia

Eurovision Song Contest Asia is the Asian counterpart to the Eurovision Song Contest, bringing together artists from across Asia to compete with original songs representing their countries. Each participating broadcaster selected its entry through a national selection, with the artists competing live.

The idea of an Asian Eurovision dates back to 2017, with several attempts to launch the competition over the years. After numerous delays and abandoned plans, the European Broadcasting Union (EBU) partnered with Voxovation to bring the contest to life in 2026.
