UEFA Nations League group stage: Matchday 3 fixtures played - RESULTS

The latest matches of Matchday 3 in the UEFA Nations League group stage have been played, APA reports.

A total of 10 matches took place in Leagues A, B and C on October 2.

One of the day's notable fixtures was held in Group 1 of League A, where France hosted Italy. The tightly contested match ended in a 1:1 draw.

In League B, Hungary defeated Georgia 1:0, while Northern Ireland beat Ukraine 3:0. The Bosnia and Herzegovina-Sweden match ended in a 1:1 draw, while Poland thrashed Romania 6:0.

In League C, Kazakhstan lost 1:2 to Moldova, Cyprus defeated Armenia 2:0, and Latvia suffered a 1:2 defeat against Montenegro. The Faroe Islands-Slovakia match ended in a 1:1 draw.

In another League A match, Belgium defeated Türkiye 3:0.
