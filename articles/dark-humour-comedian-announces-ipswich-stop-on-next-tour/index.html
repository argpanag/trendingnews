Dark humour comedian announces Ipswich stop on next tour

TheTools · 21.10.2026 09:13 · source · permalink

An award-winning comedian is set to return to Ipswich with a new tour.

Jimmy Carr will perform at Ipswich Regent Theatre on June 1, with his show "Killing Jokes."

The show promises Carr's signature style of rapid-fire, dark humour, laden with one-liners on a variety of controversial topics.

A description of the tour reads: "This brand-new show is packed with fast, dark one-liners about all kinds of terrible things. But they are just jokes. They’re not the terrible things."

Jimmy Carr, who will perform at the Ipswich Regent next year (Image: Matt Crossick/PA Media Assignments)

Carr, 54, has carved out a successful career in both television and live comedy, appearing on well-known programmes like "8 Out of 10 Cats," "The Big Fat Quiz of the Year," and "8 Out of 10 Cats Does Countdown."

Tickets for the Ipswich performance will be available from 10am on Friday, October 23.

The show is expected to attract a large audience, as Carr's previous performances at the Regent have done.
