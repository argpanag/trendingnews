The following is a press release from the Professional Regulation Commission.

The Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) announces that 1,343 out of 2,632 passed the Midwives Licensure Examination given by the Board of Midwifery in 18 testing centers all over the Philippines this November 2026.  

The Board of Midwifery is composed of Hon. Melchor C. Dela Cruz, Jr., Chairman; Hon. Lerma M. Valenzuela, Hon. Corazon F. Landicho, Hon. Lourdes S. Mangahas and Hon. Maria Teresa C. Padilla, Members.

Starting December 18, 2026, registration for the issuance of Professional Identification Card (ID) and Certificate of Registration will be done on-line. Please visit the official PRC website and follow the instructions for registration. Those who will register are required to bring the following: 1) Screenshot of system-generated Registration Form; 2) valid government-issued ID. Successful examinees should personally register and sign in the Roster of Registered Professionals. 

The date and venue for the oathtaking ceremony of the new successful examinees in the said examination WILL BE ANNOUNCED LATER.

The top performing schools in the November 2026 Midwives Licensure Examination as per Commission Resolution No. 1814 series of 2024:

  1. WITH 50 OR MORE EXAMINEES AND WITH AT LEAST 80% PASSING PERCENTAGE
  1. WITH 20 TO 49 EXAMINEES AND WITH AT LEAST 80% PASSING PERCENTAGE
