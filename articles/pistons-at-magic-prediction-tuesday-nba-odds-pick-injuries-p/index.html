Pistons at Magic Prediction: Tuesday NBA Odds, Pick & Injuries | Predictem

TheTools · 24.11.2026 09:56 · source · permalink

by Joe Jensen | Nov 24, 2026 | NBA Picks

The Pistons are 2.5-point favorites at most books with a total of 213.5, and my play is Detroit -2.5.

The last time these two shared a floor that mattered, Detroit won the final three meetings of last spring’s playoff series, including a 93-79 grinder at Kia Center and a 116-94 runaway in Detroit. Seven months later the Magic are 7-9, they still can’t win at home, and their best player is in a walking boot again. The market is asking Detroit to win by a field goal. I’ll take that deal.

Orlando without Paolo is a different team. Banchero is out with a right high ankle sprain and has played only two games all season. Franz Wagner has carried the load, averaging 24.5 points and 8.2 assists, and Desmond Bane adds 18.5 a night. That’s a real one-two punch, but the supporting cast has been shaky. Sunday night in Washington the Magic fell behind 11-0, never led, and lost 116-96 to a Wizards team that had just snapped an eight-game skid. Jalen Suggs went 2-for-11 and missed all six of his threes.

The bigger problem is the building. Orlando is 1-5 at Kia Center this season and 6-4 everywhere else. Whatever home-court edge the line is pricing in hasn’t shown up yet.

Detroit’s defense travels. The Pistons are 10-6 and a tidy 7-3 on the road, and they get it done on the defensive end. They hold opponents to 108.6 points per game, best of these two by more than four points, and they lead this matchup in steals (9.3 per game) and blocks (5.8). Ausar Thompson is the engine of that chaos, pulling down 8.6 rebounds a night from the wing. Against a Magic offense that turns it over 15.8 times a game, that’s a fight Detroit should win.

Cade Cunningham is listed as probable with right knee soreness, and that’s the whole risk in this bet. He sat out the win in New Orleans last Wednesday, scored 23 against Toronto on Friday, and then had his first single-digit night of the season Sunday in Atlanta: five points on 2-of-9 shooting, foul trouble, and a season-high 15 assists. Coach J.B. Bickerstaff said before that one that protecting him was “the most important thing.” If Detroit holds him out at the last minute, this line moves and so would my confidence.

The Pistons are also thin up front with Jalen Duren (hamstring) and Duncan Robinson (quadriceps) out. Robinson was scoring 17.4 a night, so somebody else has to make shots. And Detroit blew an 11-point fourth-quarter lead in Atlanta by giving up an 18-0 run, so this team isn’t immune to a bad stretch. Still, Detroit won by 13 in New Orleans without its star last week, and Daniss Jenkins (14.1 points) plus Paul Reed and John Collins in the frontcourt give them enough to survive a quiet Cade night against this opponent.

About 80% of moneyline bets are on Detroit, so I’m not exactly hiding in a corner here. Sometimes the public is right because the price is wrong. A Magic team missing its best player, losing at home, and leaking threes shouldn’t be priced this close to a pick’em.

Projected score: Pistons 112, Magic 105.

The Pick: Pistons -2.5
