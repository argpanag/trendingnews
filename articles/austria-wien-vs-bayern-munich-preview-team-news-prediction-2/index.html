Austria Wien vs Bayern Munich prediction

Austria Wien sit rock-bottom of the league phase table having collected just one point from their four fixtures to date, with their latest outing seeing them lose 3-1 to Benfica in Lisbon.

Stefan Kenesei's side have found it tough going in Europe and it would take a huge turnaround for them to qualify for the knockout phase play-offs.

Bayern Munich are, unsurprisingly, faring much better and are unbeaten in their four matches so far, while they are also flying high in the Frauen-Bundesliga with a maximum 30 points from their ten fixtures to date.

There is a sizeable gulf in class between these two sides, and given that there have been at least three goals scored in all of Bayern's league phase outings so far, and four in two of them, so we think there is decent value in backing over 3.5 goals here.

Austria Wien vs Bayern Munich tip: Over 3.5 goals
