  15. 'Challenging period' ahead for depleted Liverpoolpublished at 18:51 BST
    18:51 BST

    Man City v Liverpool (19:00 BST)

    Liverpool are still without a few key players, but there are some hopeful signs that things will improve in the coming weeks.

    Marie Hobinger made her return from an anterior cruciate ligament injury at the weekend, coming off the bench in their 1-1 draw with Tottenham.

    Speaking in his pre-match news conference, Gareth Taylor said: "We're getting closer to seeing Lucy Parry joining back with the team, and Mari Ward. I think it's going to be a little bit too soon for them.

    "Mao Itamura is back on-pitch but, again, [it is] too soon for her.

    "A couple of little niggles from last weekend's game because it was quite a highly physical game.

    "I think it's going to be a challenging period for us once this game is done – we play again Wednesday, we play again Sunday and then repeat that the following week.

    "I think with the additional issues of player availability, the likes of Anna Josendal, that will be a challenging period for us.

    "At this moment in time, playing each weekend has been OK and we're able to play the game that we want to play."
