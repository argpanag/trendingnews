Cherries cruise into cup quarter-finals with big win over Villa

TheTools · 28.10.2026 23:09 · source · permalink

THE Cherries eased into the quarter-finals of the Carabao Cup with a comfortable 3-0 win over Aston Villa at Dean Court.

Bournemouth led 1-0 at the break after an early Amine Adli goal following a fast start from the hosts.

They made another quick start to the second half and Lewis Cook extended the Cherries' lead when his cross from the left went all the way in six minutes after the resumption.

Álvaro Rodríguez added the third 21 minutes from time when he finished low after a driving run into the box to complete the victory as Bournemouth moved into the final eight of the competition for just the fourth time in their history.

Marco Rose made eight changes from his side that beat Manchester United at the weekend.

Three of those were in the back four, as Julio Soler, Bafodé Diakité and Juanlu Sánchez all started, the latter making his first start in a Cherries shirt.

Ben Gannon-Doak was lively on the wing. (Image: Richard Crease)

Ryan Christie returned in midfield, while Adli, Ben Gannon-Doak and Álvaro Rodríguez started in attack.

Michele Di Gregorio took his place between the sticks.

Bournemouth made a bright start and deservedly hit the front in the seventh minute.

After threatening a couple of times down the left channel, Marcus Tavernier picked out Soler with a brilliant ball over the top.

The left-back brought it down superbly before clipping a cross to the edge of the six-yard box, where Adli applied the finishing touch.

Amine Adli gave the Cherries the lead. (Image: Richard Crease)

A first chance for the visitors came shortly after the goal, but George Hemmings fired wide from range.

Bournemouth, though, were relentless and went close when Gannon-Doak burst into space in the box and powered towards the top corner, but Marco Bizot made an important save to tip it over the bar.

Tavernier saw a shot blocked from the top of the box before he shot over the bar, and Adli shot over the bar when stretching for the ball and off balance.

Hemmings had a good opportunity when slid through on the left, but he curled wide with only Di Gregorio to beat.

The Italian then saved from Brian Madjo when a deflected Emi Buendia effort came to him on the edge of the six-yard box.

Marcus Tavernier (Image: Richard Crease)

The Cherries again went close when Soler played the ball across the top of the box to Tavernier, who took a touch and curled the ball just the wrong side of the post.

Adli shot over the bar when found by a Juanlu cross, before Buendia shot wide with a low effort from range after a fast Villa counterattack.

Leon Goretzka headed over the bar when he got free in the box from a corner on the stroke of half-time, as Bournemouth held their lead at the break.

Rose made a double change for the second half, as Justin Kluivert and Rayan replaced Tavernier and Adli.

The Cherries made another fast start to the half as Gannon-Doak drew a good save out of Bizot with a powerful effort that was again aimed at the top corner.

Ibrahim Mbaye shot wide via a big deflection off a Villa teammate following a quick counterattack, but it was Bournemouth who extended their lead six minutes into the second 45.

Lewis Cook scored the second. (Image: Richard Crease)

Cook beat Bizot at his near post and found the net when he crossed from the left after a short Cherries corner and the ball went all the way in for the midfielder's first goal since the 3-2 comeback victory over Everton in August 2024.

Villa substitute Alysson fired towards goal not long after coming on, but Diakité blocked well, before Buendia volleyed wide from range.

Kluivert also volleyed off target at the other end when Gannon-Doak headed down to him from a Christie cross, before the Dutchman drew a block out of Tyrone Mings with a shot from the right of the box.

Antonio Silva (Image: Richard Crease)

Rodríguez headed over the bar when Diakité clipped the ball to the back post after a Bournemouth corner.

Tyler Adams and David Brooks replaced Christie and Gannon-Doak for the final 27 minutes, and the Welshman immediately saw an effort blocked when he was slid in by Kluivert.

Di Gregorio saved comfortably from a Taylor Harwood-Bellis header and Rodríguez got his goal moments later to make it 3-0 to the Cherries.

Adams won the ball high and played it to the striker in space on the edge of the box.

He turned and drove towards goal before applying a low finish and wheeling away in celebration.

Alvaro Rodriguez scored the Cherries' third. (Image: Richard Crease)

The Bournemouth keeper kept the clean sheet intact with an excellent save to deny Madjo when he made space for a shot away from Diakité.

Kluivert fired wide from range, before Junior Kroupi replaced Rodríguez for the final ten minutes plus stoppage time.

Alysson headed wide from an Alejandro Garnacho cross when well placed.

Brooks shot over the bar after Kroupi slid him in behind on the right, as the Cherries saw out a big win to progress comfortably into the Carabao Cup quarter-finals.
