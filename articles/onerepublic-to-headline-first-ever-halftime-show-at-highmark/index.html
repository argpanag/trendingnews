OneRepublic to headline first-ever halftime show at Highmark Stadium for Bills vs. Lions

TheTools · 05.09.2026 10:19 · source · permalink

The Bills are bringing live music and an immersive fan experience to the first game at Highmark Stadium in a big way.

OneRepublic will headline the first-ever halftime show at the Bills' new home when Buffalo hosts the Detroit Lions for the 2026 home opener on Thursday, Sept. 17. Thursday Night Football on Prime Video will carry the game and a portion of OneRepublic's halftime performance live on broadcast.

The internationally renowned band will take the stage on the Bills Legends Deck, giving fans throughout the stadium a great viewing experience for a historic night. Led by frontman Ryan Tedder, OneRepublic has produced some of the biggest hits of the past two decades, including "Counting Stars," "Apologize," "I Lived", "I Ain't Worried" and "Secrets."

The performance will add another layer to an already memorable night as the Bills officially open their new home in front of a national audience.

From the first kickoff to the first halftime show, Buffalo's new home is set for an unforgettable opening night.
