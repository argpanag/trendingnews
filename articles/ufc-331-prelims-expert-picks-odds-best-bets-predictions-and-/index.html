UFC 331 prelims predictions

The following odds are all courtesy of the DraftKings Sportsbook.

Marlon Vera vs. Charles Jourdain odds (Bantamweight)

Vera vs. Jourdain prediction and best bet

  • Marlon Vera to win via decision (+500 at DraftKings)

A former title contender, Vera aims for his first win since 2023. "Chito" lands 4.18 significant strikes per minute. He also averages 0.47 takedowns landed per 15 minutes. Jourdain has won three straight. He lands 5.36 significant strikes and averages 0.38 takedowns landed.

Vera is a tough fighter but is susceptible to getting hit, absorbing 5.34 significant strikes. His last six fights have ended via decision. Jourdain can either knock an opponent out or submit them. He looks much more comfortable at bantamweight.

Vera has not yet been taken out in his career. That streak may continue. It’s also entirely possible he guts out a win if he can tire out and expose Jourdain early. An upset before the main card could make this a fun fight.

Tai Tuivasa vs. Robelis Despaigne odds (Heavyweight)

Tuivasa vs. Despaigne prediction and best bet

  • Robelis Despaigne to win via KO/TKO/DQ (-450 at DraftKings)
  • Total rounds: Under 1.5 (-280 at DraftKings)

It wasn’t long ago that Tuivasa was on top of the world. Now, he's on a seven-fight losing streak and is looking for his first win since 2022. "Bam Bam" lands 3.37 significant strikes per minute. He also absorbs 4.61 strikes.

Despaigne signed with the UFC in 2023. After losing two fights, he was cut, went to Karate Combat, and then went to MVP MMA, where he knocked out Junior dos Santos. The UFC brought him back following the dos Santos win. Six of his seven wins in Karate Combat ended via knockout.

If you’re hoping for the massive upset, look away. Despaigne is out to prove he belongs in the UFC. Unfortunately, it will come at the Aussie’s expense.

Ryan Gandra vs. Ozzy Diaz odds (Middleweight)

Gandra vs. Diaz prediction and best bet

  • Ryan Gandra to win via KO/TKO/DQ (-340 at DraftKings)
  • Total rounds: Under 1.5 (-260 at DraftKings)

Gandra has knocked out three straight opponents inside the octagon. He lands 11.71 significant strikes per minute. Diaz has only won one fight in four contests in the octagon. He lands 5.33 significant strikes. It may be an ugly fight, especially with Diaz absorbing 6.52 significant strikes.

Diaz is not afraid of anyone, which sometimes doesn’t bode well for fighters. Gandra could make a mistake, but his sharp striking should break past Diaz’s defense.

Casey O'Neill vs. Eduarda Moura (Flyweight

O'Neill vs. Moura prediction and best bet

  • Casey O'Neill to win via decision (+100 at DraftKings)

O'Neill lands 7.89 significant strikes per minute. She also averages 1.40 takedowns landed per 15 minutes. "King" Casey absorbs 5.53 strikes, but she normally packs the harder punch. Moura lands 2.57 strikes and averages 3.58 takedowns landed, with 13 in her last four fights.

Despite being tough on the floor, Moura's last two losses have come against strikers. O’Neill is tough as nails and should be able to avoid significant damage to earn the win.
