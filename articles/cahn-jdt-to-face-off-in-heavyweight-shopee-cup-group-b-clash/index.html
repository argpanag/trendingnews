CAHN, JDT TO FACE OFF IN HEAVYWEIGHT SHOPEE CUP™ GROUP B CLASH

TheTools · 18.11.2026 06:14 · source · permalink

Nov 18 2026

Inaugural ASEAN Club Championship Shopee Cup™ runners-up Công An Hà Nội FC host Malaysia Super League champions Johor Darul Ta’zim FC on Thursday in a blockbuster clash between two of Group B’s unbeaten sides in an early battle for supremacy.

The V.League 1 champions will entertain Xisco Muñoz’s side with both clubs on three points after kicking off their 2026/27 campaigns with wins over Lion City Sailors FC and PKR Svay Rieng FC respectively in last month’s opening round of group fixtures.

JDT top the early standings on goal difference after a complete performance against the Cambodian champions that saw the 12-times Malaysia Super League winners record a 6-0 win at Sultan Ibrahim Stadium.

Công An Hà Nội, who missed out on the knock-out rounds of last season’s ASEAN Shopee Cup™ after reaching the final in 2024/25, defeated Lion City Sailors through a Léo Arthur strike as both clubs sit on three points alongside Persib Bandung.

Mano Pölking’s side are unbeaten in their last five matches in all competitions, a run that includes picking up their first-ever victory in the AFC Champions League Elite when they handed Chinese Super League side Shanghai Port FC a 3-2 loss. 

Persib, making their first appearance in the Shopee Cup™, rallied in their Shopee Cup™ opener to pick up a victory, scoring three times in nine minutes late in the game to hand Thailand’s Port FC a 3-1 defeat.

Mariano Peralta, Berguinho and Luka Menalo scored in the dying moments at Thammasat Stadium to seal that win and set up Igor Tolić’s side for their meeting on Thursday with Shan United FC, who will be making their first appearance in this season’s Shopee Cup™.

The side from Myanmar booked their place in the group phase of the Shopee Cup™ for the third season in a row with a penalty shoot-out win in the qualifying play-off over Ezra FC from Laos in early September.

Svay Rieng will be hoping to put their heavy loss to JDT behind them when they host Port in Phnom Penh with Matt McConkey’s side unbeaten in all competitions since that defeat.

The Cambodian champions have recorded back-to-back wins over fellow Shopee Cup™ participants Tampines Rovers FC from Singapore in the AFC Champions League Two and sit two points off the summit in their domestic league.

Svay Rieng will meet a Port side that has endured a mixed run of form, which includes a win over Beijing Guoan FC in the AFC Champions League Elite and a draw in their most recent match against Chonburi in Thai League 1.
