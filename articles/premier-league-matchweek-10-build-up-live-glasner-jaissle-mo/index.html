  4. Encouraging signs?published at 14:14 GMT
    14:14 GMT

    Nottingham Forest v Man City (Sat, 17:30 GMT)

    According to Opta, Forest's Expected Points (xPTS) is 15.6 which would have them currently sat third in the Premier League behind the current top two, Arsenal and Manchester City.

    This stat is formed by looking at the expected goals for and against in games involving the Reds so far this season and had they registered as expected, then this would have been the outcome.

    The most telling one of course is the Expected Goals (xG) with Oliver Glasner's side racking up an xG of 14.3 across nine Premier League games so far.

    As previously mentioned, Forest have only found the net six times in 2026-27 but with the chances they have created, they should actually be one of the top scorers in the division.

    So, whether you buy into the stat or not, it proves that Glasner's side are creating plenty - they just need to start hitting the net.

  7. Delap? Jesus? Wood? Forest forwards falling flatpublished at 14:08 GMT
    14:08 GMT

    Nottingham Forest v Man City (Sat, 17:30 GMT)

    One of the biggest reasons for Nottingham Forest struggling in the early days of 2026-27 is their ineffectiveness in front of goal.

  8. Forest need some quick resultspublished at 14:05 GMT
    14:05 GMT

    Nottingham Forest v Man City (Sat, 17:30 GMT)

    Nick Mashiter
    BBC Sport football news reporter at the City Ground

    Oliver Glasner and Nottingham Forest are still waiting for their first win and goal at the City Ground this season.

    One-goal defeats to Leeds, Coventry and Arsenal and a stalemate with Tottenham in the Premier League - along with the 2-0 Carabao Cup defeat by Leeds - has not got the Glasner era off to the brightest of starts.

    The Austrian is not under immediate pressure but with Forest in the relegation zone ahead of Saturday's visit of Manchester City the hosts need some quick results.

    There has been evidence of what Glasner is trying to do - with draws at Liverpool and Ipswich and victory at Aston Villa - but more will have been expected after his summer arrival.

    Four defeats in their past five leaves Forest in danger of another season of struggle.
