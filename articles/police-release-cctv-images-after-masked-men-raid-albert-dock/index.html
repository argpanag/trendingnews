Police release CCTV images after masked men raid Albert Dock restaurant

TheTools · 30.10.2026 15:42 · source · permalink

POLICE have released CCTV images after a burglary at a restaurant at Albert Dock.

The break-in happened at What's Cooking on the Albert Dock, Liverpool at about 7.50am on Monday, August 17, when two masked men entered the office area and stole a quantity of cash before fleeing toward The Strand.

They were confronted by a member of staff during the incident, which took place at Britannia Pavilion.

Police have released CCTV images following a burglary at a restaurant on Albert Dock (Image: Merseyside Police)

Detective Inspector John Bolton said: "We are appealing for anyone who recognises either male or has any information which could assist with our enquiries to contact us.

"Incidents such as this can have a huge impact on businesses and we are committed to finding the persons responsible and bringing them to justice."

Merseyside Police have released CCTV images following the theft from What's Cooking on Albert Dock, Liverpool. (Image: Merseyside Police)

Extensive CCTV and witness enquiries have been carried out, and images of two men believed to have information that could help the investigation have been released.

Anyone with information is asked to contact Merseyside Police via their social media desk on X or Facebook @MerPolCC, through their website at www.merseyside.police.uk/ro/report/ocr/af/how-to-report-a-crime/, or by calling 101 and quoting incident reference 26000672133.

Alternatively, information can be given anonymously to Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111 or through their website at www.crimestoppers-uk.org/give-information/forms/give-information-anonymously.
