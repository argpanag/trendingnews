2026.10.23［STADIUM INFO.］

stadium gourmet Food Halloween Event! Announcement of eligible products for the MOFLEM Harchimo mini can badge giveaway.

On Saturday, October 24th, at the MEIJI YASUDA J1 LEAGUE 12th Sec. against Kyoto Sanga FC, we will be holding the "MOFLEM and Harchimo's 'Spooky' Halloween" event!
A "stadium gourmet Halloween Event" will be held at food and beverage stalls inside and outside the stadium on the main side, where you can receive a mini can badge!

The mini can badges come in four designs and are limited to 3,000 pieces.
This is an original design featuring MOFLEM and Harchimo enjoying Halloween in costumes!
Enjoy stadium food and collect mini badges!

■ Distribution time
- Food and beverage outlets outside the stadium (main side shops/Gamba Well-being Park only): 12:00 onwards *Ends when mini badges run out
- Food and beverage stalls inside the venue: 13:00 onwards *Ends when the mini badges run out

■Distribution method
Receive one mini can badge with each eligible product purchased.

*The mini pin badge has a diameter of 44mm.
*The four designs will be given out randomly. We cannot accept exchanges at the store.
*Distribution is on a first-come, first-served basis at each store, and may end while the target product is still on sale.
Please note that there may be differences in the time it takes for distribution to end depending on the store.
