F1 United States GP: Antonelli dominates from pole - but forced to wait for title

Kimi Antonelli has moved to the brink of a maiden world title after taking his ninth win of the 2026 Formula 1 season at the United States Grand Prix

Kimi Antonelli, winner of the US GP: "Thank you, it was the result we needed. The whole team deserved it so much, putting in so much work on bringing this new package and finally seeing the result is very pleasant. It was also a perfect execution from strategy to call the one-stop, and pleased to see it stick."

"We're very close, but we try to finish on a high and hopefully we can clinch [the title] very soon."

Max Verstappen, third place in Austin: "I think our overall pace was not good enough, and the tyres we had available were not the right ones. We have to understand because the last few races, it was working well for us. We still managed to get on the podium, maybe a little bit lucky with the VSC."

On the Norris overtake: "I had to! It was either get overtaken, or go for the overtake -that's what I did."

Charles Leclerc, second place in Austin: "It was quite a good race, unfortunately came short of the victory which was our target. But it was a positive, we had good pace. The first stint was a bit more difficult, Kimi went for the one-stop which was not really on our cards, but we were in the race with Max. It was quite a fun race, with the start and then our battle with Lando."

So, Antonelli just needs to finish ninth in Mexico and the title is his. 

Leclerc sounded a bit muted there, I think he knew a win was potentially on today. 

"Good job everyone, that's the result we needed. Let's keep pushing," Antonelli says. "All the way," comes the response from Pete Bonnington.
