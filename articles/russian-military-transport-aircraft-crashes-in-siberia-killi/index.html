Russian military transport aircraft crashes in Siberia, killing 19, defense ministry says | CNN

TheTools · 17.11.2026 21:28 · source · permalink

A military An-12 transport aircraft crashed in Russia’s Kemerovo region in southwestern Siberia, killing all 19 people on board, Russia’s defense ministry was quoted as saying on Tuesday.

“The preliminary cause of the disaster is a technical malfunction,” state news agency RIA quoted the Defence Ministry as saying.

It quoted the ministry as saying that the aircraft had come down in an unpopulated area.

Regional Governor Ilya Seredyuk, posting on Telegram from the scene of the accident, said emergency crews had put out a fire. He said the main task of crews on site was to help the investigation into the accident.

Seredyuk, in a video posted online and showing the site of the accident near the village of Yagunovo, said the plane had been flying through the region when the accident occurred.
