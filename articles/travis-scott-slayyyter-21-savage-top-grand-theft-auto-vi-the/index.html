Travis Scott, Slayyyter, 21 Savage Top 'Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album (Deluxe Edition)'

TheTools · 23.11.2026 14:00 · source · permalink

A deluxe edition of Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, featuring 23 original songs from artists like Travis Scott, Offset, 21 Savage, Slayyyter, and Latto, arrived on Monday. The tracks expand on the already-released Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, a 34-song soundtrack for the highly anticipated new game.

The initial release included new music from Lana Del Rey, Dua Lipa, Zara Larsson, Morgan Wallen, Keith Richards, Rauw Alejandro, Jennie, Rina Sawayama, Young Thug, Arca, Tove Lo, Playboi Carti, and more.

The news of the deluxe edition, from Atlantic Records and Rockstars Games, comes after Cardi B, Kodak Black, and Don Toliver — all three of whom also appear on GTA VI: The Album — joined Scott and Justice at a one-off Grand Theft Auto 6 music festival in Miami on Saturday. The festival also featured performances from Metro Boomin, Rauw Alejandro, Justice, Kodak Black, Fcukers, and the Dare.

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album (Deluxe Edition) is available on all streaming platforms now. Fans can purchase the original edition of soundtrack on vinyl and CD in multiple versions.

Grand Theft Auto VI, released last week after a 13-year wait, broke records by earning $3.79 billion in the first 24 hours of its launch. GTA VI was already competing against itself for the highest release-day sales. Its predecessor, GTA V, earned $815.7 million within 24 hours of its September 2013 release.
