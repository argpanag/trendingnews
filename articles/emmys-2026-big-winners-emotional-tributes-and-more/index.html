Emmys 2026: Big winners, emotional tributes and more

TheTools · 15.09.2026 04:09 · source · permalink
  • Culture

Television's biggest stars gathered at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles.

Television's biggest night of the year is in the books!

The 78th Emmy Awards brought together television's biggest stars, shows and creators to celebrate the best of the small screen.

Mariska Hargitay hosted the event for the first time. "Widow's Bay" dominated a crowded field, ending the night with six wins on top of the eight wins the show took home previously at the Creative Arts Emmys, held earlier this month.

On Monday, "Widow's Bay" took home the awards for outstanding comedy series, as well as outstanding supporting actress in a comedy series, outstanding supporting actor, lead actor, writing and directing.

Continue below to see who won, red carpet arrivals, memorable speeches, reactions and more must-see moments.

'Widows Bay' wins Emmy for outstanding comedy series

"Widows Bay" won the Emmy for outstanding comedy series, beating out “Abbott Elementary,” “The Bear,” “Hacks,” “Margo’s Got Money Troubles,” “Nobody Wants This,” “Only Murders In The Building” and “Shrinking.”

“Widows Bay” was a huge winner at its Emmys debut. The show received 19 nominations, and on Monday night, it took home a total of six awards, including supporting actress, lead actor, writing and directing.

At the Creative Arts Emmys, held Sept. 5-6, the show took home eight additional awards, including outstanding guest actress in a comedy series for Betty Gilpin, outstanding casting and more.

'The Pitt' wins Emmy for outstanding drama series

"The Pitt" won the Emmy for outstanding drama series.

R. Scott Gemmill, the series' creator, showrunner, and executive producer, accepted the award and spoke about the need to continue telling "honest stories about real human struggles."

"When I look around this room, I see something more important than the awards and the ratings," Gemmill said. "I see a whole community that is still trying to do the right thing."

He continued, "It is so hard to tell what is real and what we can believe anymore, which makes telling honest stories about real human struggles so much more important than ever. What you do, it changes people's lives."

Other nominees in the category included "The Diplomat," "The Gilded Age," "A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms," "Paradise," "Pluribus," "Slow Horses" and "Your Friends & Neighbors."

Rob Reiner, Michele Reiner honored in moving tribute

The late Rob Reiner and Michele Reiner were honored at the Emmys at the end of this year's 'In Memorium' segment.

Jamie Lee Curtis took the stage to share words on Rob Reiner's legacy.

"He was generous and kind and funny as hell and a friend to all. And we all miss Rob and Michele, and all those we lost this year," Curtis said.

She also shared that with his posthumous Emmy win for his performance in "The Bear," Rob Reiner had broken the record for longest gap between acting Emmys, a title previously held by his dad, Carl Reiner.

The legendary filmmaker and his wife, a photographer and movie producer, were found stabbed to death in their family home in December 2025. The couple's son, Nick Reiner, was charged with the murders of his parents.

Nick Reiner pleaded not guilty to murder charges and remains held on no bond. He is next due in court on Sept. 15 for a pretrial hearing. No trial date has been set.

Noah Kahan delivers powerful performance during 'In Memoriam' segment

Noah Kahan performed "Bridge Over Troubled Water" during the "In Memoriam" segment.

The segment honored stars and others in the TV industry who have died over the past year, including Eric Dane, James Van Der Beek, Hayden Panettiere, Dolly Parton, Tim Curry, James Burrows and Bob Mackie.
