How GTA 6 is taking over sport - from the Premier League to rugby and F1

TheTools · 19.11.2026 17:10 · source · permalink

Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA VI) has taken the world by storm on Thursday, invading the lives of just about everyone - and sport is no exception.

Even people with no interest in gaming have likely heard about the release of GTA 6, with Rockstar finally letting fans play their heavily anticipated game.

The 13-year wait has helped ramp up the hype to ludicrous levels, with fans booking days off work to get stuck into their new virtual world. There will be millions of people around the world with square eyes right now, even professional sports stars.

From top-level Premier League stars to F1 drivers, here Mirror Sport takes a look at those who are spending their spare time in a fictionalised version of Miami.

Football

Liverpool striker Alexander Isak put his cards on the table when asked about GTA 6 while away on international duty with Sweden this week. “Yeah, so I think it’ll be difficult to reach me from the 19th, when it comes out. I’ll be locked in. That’s the plan,” he said.

Isak is far from the only footballer who is locked in. Manchester United forward Bryan Mbeumo gave his followers a glimpse into his life with an Instagram story showing the loading screen of GTA 6 with the caption "historic".

In a previous clip, Mbeumo was being interviewed while on his console and showed he'd pre-ordered the game. He added: "I'm just waiting now, the game will make me stay at home every single day... after training."

Arsenal and England star Declan Rice is also a gamer. He feels it helps him switch off after playing high-pressure matches. “I can never sleep after night games," he told GQ recently. "It’s impossible. I’ll be that wired.

"I'll play Call of Duty, Grand Theft Auto, FIFA. Anything. Just play with a load of my friends. Adrenaline, everything. You don’t really settle down. I feel like when you’re playing at this level, your mind’s just constantly – it’s just going."

Rugby

Steve Tandy expects his Wales squad to get ready for Australia by playing GTA 6 ahead of their Nations Championship match in Cardiff on Saturday.

Wales head coach Tandy said: “It would definitely be the players’. I’m more of an Atari man myself. Or a Spectrum. That’s more my thing.

“It has been put on my radar actually this morning because one of the staff’s family members is part of the design team on it. I think I may have played the first one a little bit, but it’s definitely going to be massive. That’s why we (staff) stay out of the team room after 6pm, because we don’t know what’s going on down there.”

F1

Formula One world champion Kimi Antonelli may only be 20 years old, but the release of the game has made him feel like “being a little kid again”. Speaking before the Las Vegas Grand Prix at the weekend, the Mercedes driver said: “I’ve already downloaded it. I pre-ordered it. I will ask my dad to bring over the PlayStation.

“In my free time I will play. Many of us have been waiting for this game and we are all super excited. There is a friend of mine in Italy who is already playing it. I have been video calling him and I’ve been asking him how is the game and he has been showing me. It feels like going back 10 years and being a little kid again.”

Ferrari’s Lewis Hamilton is putting GTA VI on the backburner as he focuses on the Grand Prix, but Red Bull great Max Verstappen could well indulge in some game time to “clear his mind” over the weekend.

Hamilton said: “I have got my console with me but I have the F1 game because that is the one I use on a race weekend because that is the thing that will help me get the result I need but I can’t wait (to play GTA). I think everyone’s been looking forward to it.”

Verstappen said: “It’s already downloaded. I use to play the previous one. Of course my life has changed a little bit since then! It clears up your mind sometimes because it is nice and exciting to do these kind of things.”
