Nets at Pacers betting preview: Prediction, point spread, odds

TheTools · 02.11.2026 00:40 · source · permalink

The Brooklyn Nets (0-5) appear to be frustrated with how their season has began, especially considering how excited everyone was to prove the doubters wrong about how good they would be. Brooklyn thankfully has another chance to win its first game against the Indiana Pacers (2-4) and if you're wondering where you can watch all the action live, you've come to the right place!

The Nets come into this game following a 110-94 loss to the Detroit Pistons in the NBA Cup on Friday in which Brooklyn put up a good fight in the first quarter, but their performance went downhill after that. Backup center Mo Wagner had 16 points and eight rebounds while forward Julius Randle had 15 points and eight rebounds in one of his rougher performances of the season.

The Pacers enter this matchup after a 145-125 loss to the Miami Heat on Friday in the NBA Cup in which Indiana was unable to keep up with Miami with all of the injuries they're dealing with at the moment. Guard Quenton Jackson came off the bench to drop 24 points and four rebounds while guard Andrew Nembhard had 22 points and nine assists as the starting point guard.

The Lines, Courtesy Of BetMGM

  • Point-spread: Nets (+3.5)
  • Money-line: Nets (+135)
  • Over/Under: 230.5

Notable Injuries

Nets: OUT: Tyler Bilodeau (G League), Mikel Brown Jr. (back injury management), Egor Demin (leg), Joshua Jefferson (G League), Grant Nelson (G League), and Ben Saraf (G League).

Pacers: OUT: Johnny Furphy (right knee surgery), Tyrese Haliburton (left hamstring), TJ McConnell (right hamstring), Ben Sheppard (left meniscus surgery), and Ivica Zubac (left ankle sprain). QUESTIONABLE: Obi Toppin (back). PROBABLE: Aaron Nesmith (left hamstring).

Advice and Prediction

The Nets have not played the way they were hoping for when they began this season, but it seems like they're ready to turn the corner sooner rather than later. Head coach Jordi Fernandez and the rest of the players have spoken about the team needing to deal with adversity better and now, they get a rematch against the Pacers team that is missing some of its best players.

The Pacers unfortunately are once again dealing with injuries as they are dealing with Tyrese Haliburton and Ivica Zubac being out of the lineup for the time being. In the meantime, Indiana will have to trust that Pascal Siakam and Andrew Nembhard can keep the team afloat until Haliburton and Zubac are able to return and hopefully stay healthy.

The Nets rank towards the bottom of the league in offensive rating while the Pacers rank more of the middle of the pack in that same category. Defensively, both Brooklyn and Indiana rank towards the bottom of the Association in defensive rating. Both the Nets and Pacers rank top-8 in pace so this matchup should include plenty of scoring and fast-paced action.

Prediction: Nets 115, Pacers 110
