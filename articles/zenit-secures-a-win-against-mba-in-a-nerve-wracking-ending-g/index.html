Zenit secures a win against MBA in a nerve-wracking ending. Game Recap and Highlights | VTB United League - Official Website

TheTools · 24.11.2026 17:18 · source · permalink

St. Petersburg’s Zenit defeated Moscow’s MBA on the road, 79–75 (23–20, 20–12, 23–26, 13–17).

Main Story
The visitors seized the initiative right from the start of the game. They controlled the pace, prevented their opponents from getting into a rhythm, and shut down MBA’s leading scorers, Pavel Savkov and Danil Pevnev (6 points between them). In set plays, Zenit’s players’ skill shone through. Nate Mason and Markus Howard combined for 22 points, giving the St. Petersburg team a comfortable lead at halftime—43–32.

The Clutch Moment
After halftime, the home team managed to even out the game and then gradually began to close the gap. Ultimately, they managed to drag the match into a nail-biting finish. In the final minute, the Muscovites cut the deficit to 2 points—75–77. With 12 seconds left on the clock, Pavel Savkov took a three-pointer to take the lead, but his shot missed. The Zenit players grabbed the rebound, and Vladislav Emchenko sealed the final score from the free-throw line—79–75.

X-Factor
Nate Mason had his best game yet for Zenit. The American guard made 9 of 15 field goal attempts and led the winning team in points (23), assists (6), and efficiency (23).

Interesting Facts
Zenit secured its third consecutive victory.
MBA center Maxim Ogarkov scored 22 points in this game, setting a new career-high in scoring.

Press Conference
MBA Head Coach Sergey Voznyuk:
“Before the game, I said we needed to redeem ourselves in front of our fans after that disappointing loss to Samara. Based on our performance, I think we managed to do that. We had a chance to force overtime; we even took a shot at the win. You could call it bad luck, which wasn’t on our side. In the second half, we adjusted our strategy and did a better job of containing Howard. I think we handled him well today, even though he scored 15 points.

On the other hand, on offense we could have launched more fast breaks. The guys showed a lot of desire and grabbed 16 offensive rebounds. We went into the closing minutes, where Zenit had a bit more luck. We have a break coming up, and we hope that some of our injured players will be able to return, but it’s a long process. Timelines sometimes change, and right now the break is especially important because it will give us the opportunity to work harder and practice specific plays on both defense and offense.”

Zenit Head Coach Duško Ivanović:
“We looked good in the first half and had a double-digit lead, but because of Chris Ledlum’s injury, we had to adjust our lineup a bit; we tried different players at the point guard position, and that’s not exactly a typical situation for them. But credit must also go to MBA—they managed to get back into the game, effectively drove the ball into the paint, and had a chance to win. Today we won thanks to our defense—we defended very well during the final possessions, which is why we were able to secure the victory. That’s what we want—to win through defense.”

Top Performers
MBA: Maxim Ogarkov (22)
Zenit: Nate Mason (23 + 6 assists), Markus Howard (15)
