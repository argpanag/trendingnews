4 pedestrians struck at Tsawwassen Mills, people told to avoid the area - BC | Globalnews.ca

TheTools · 30.09.2026 18:56 · source · permalink

If you get Global News from Instagram or Facebook - that will be changing. Find out how you can still connect with us.

Delta police confirm they are dealing with a collision on First Nations land at Tsawwassen Mills Mall.

In an post on X, they said at this time, it appears to be a medical incident.

Four pedestrians have been struck and Emergency Health Services are on the scene, along with police and other first responders.

Police did not confirm if there are any fatalities.

In an alert, the First Nation said that the incident has ended its Truth and Reconciliation event. Participants are asked to walk back the way they came and stay out of the area.

Local restaurants answering their phones told Global News that emergency services are on the scene.
