The countries with the highest arms trade in the world have been announced

TheTools · 28.09.2026 03:04 · source · permalink

The Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (SIPRI), which reveals the balance in international defense markets, has released its global military export report based on projections for the end of 2025. The report shows that the US has maintained its top position with a massive volume of $44.6 billion.

According to Milli.Az, Turkey is projected to reach an export figure of $4.23 billion by the end of 2025, rising to 8th place globally. The analyses in the report particularly highlight the steady growth in export trends of Germany, South Korea, and Turkey. It was stated that Turkey is increasing its market share day by day especially in the fields of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV/UCAV), ground equipment, ammunition technologies, and naval platforms.

While the top three positions in the global military export list are held by the US, Russia, and Germany, Turkey, which has continued its rise in recent years with the local technologies it developed, has solidified its place in the top 10 and once again demonstrated its weight in the global market.

The top 10 countries with the highest defense industry exports globally:
USA - $44.603 billion

Russia - $15 billion

Germany - $8.903 billion

South Korea - $8.779 billion

France - $8.630 billion

Israel - $5.995 billion

Italy - $5.225 billion

Turkey - $4.230 billion

Spain - $3.476 billion

China - $2.970 billion
