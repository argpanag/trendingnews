Kentucky Forecast Brings Snow While UAE Faces Dense Fog

TheTools · 26.11.2026 21:10 · source · permalink

Weather conditions across Kentucky and the United Arab Emirates are bringing different travel hazards, with cold temperatures, strong winds and possible snow affecting parts of the United States while dense fog threatens visibility across the Gulf region. Separate forecasts highlight the need for caution as unsettled conditions continue in both locations. According to FOX 56 meteorologist Justin Esterly, rain and possible snow showers are expected in Lexington on Friday, accompanied by strong winds and temperatures struggling to reach 40 degrees.

Wind chill will make outdoor conditions feel even colder. The weather system is expected to move northeast Friday night, allowing drier conditions to return. Sunshine is forecast for Saturday, when temperatures could climb into the 50s.

However, another stronger weather system is expected to reach the region Sunday afternoon. According to FOX 56, the system will bring additional rainfall and gusty winds before drier conditions return Monday.

Temperatures are forecast to increase gradually through the following week, with highs approaching 60 degrees late in the week. Meteorologists are also monitoring another potential system that could bring rain and isolated thunderstorms by next Friday. Meanwhile, the United Arab Emirates is facing a separate weather concern involving dense fog across extensive areas. The National Centre of Meteorology has advised motorists traveling between Abu Dhabi and Al Ain to exercise extreme caution because of potentially dangerous visibility conditions.

The National Centre of Meteorology described the expected conditions as an event of "exceptional severity," with the warning extending until approximately 10am. Western parts of the UAE are particularly likely to experience renewed fog and mist during Monday morning. Although clearer conditions are expected later on Sunday, light rain could affect some eastern and northern areas.

According to forecasts reported by The National, patches of fog could persist along coastal areas through Tuesday and Wednesday. Daytime temperatures are expected to reach approximately 31°C. Khalifa University estimates that the UAE experiences approximately 50 foggy nights annually.

The phenomenon, known as radiation fog, develops when the desert surface cools rapidly overnight during winter. As temperatures fall, moisture in the lower atmosphere condenses into tiny water droplets. Sea breezes can subsequently carry moisture over land, contributing to fog formation and reduced visibility.

Authorities have also highlighted several precautions for motorists. Drivers should use low-beam headlights and front and rear fog lights, reduce speed gradually and maintain a distance of at least four to six seconds between vehicles.

Motorists are also advised to switch off cruise control to maintain better control of their vehicles during unexpected braking. According to UAE traffic regulations cited by The National, driving with hazard lights activated while moving in fog carries a Dh500 fine and four black points.
