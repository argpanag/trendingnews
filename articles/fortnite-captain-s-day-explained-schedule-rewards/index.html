Fortnite Captain’s Day Explained: Schedule & Rewards

Learn what Captain’s Day changes in Fortnite, when weekly rewards rotate, and which bosses, weapons, and Sprites arrive.

Captain’s Day is a recurring content update in Fortnite Treasure Hunters that adds weapons, Sprites, bosses, and Captain-themed island changes.

If you want a signature weapon, follow the featured Captain. If you want a Solid Gold outfit, watch the Treasure Chest rotation instead. The name can make the event sound like a single day of boosted rewards, but its main purpose is to introduce the season’s changing content. Your priority depends on whether you want combat loot, seasonal collectibles, or cosmetics before their reward window closes.

What changes on Captain’s Day?

Captain’s Day introduces additions to the Battle Royale island, with the main weekly updates built around a featured character. These Captains take over locations on Crossbones Isle and bring themed equipment and collectibles.

Location changes do not necessarily mean an entirely new point of interest. A Captain can alter an existing area, so a familiar part of the island may become the focus of the next update.

Map Fragments and the Adventure page

The weekly Captain updates also add Map Fragments. These belong to an overarching seasonal collection goal, so collecting them is a separate task from defeating bosses or hunting cosmetic rewards.

The Adventure page displays the season’s Captain lineup. Check it to see how the featured characters fit into the weekly progression.

Jonesy and Captain X appear in the later-week artwork, but their portraits do not establish additional confirmed Captain’s Day bosses or dates.

Captain’s Day schedule and the next refresh

The next weekly Captain rotation is scheduled for November 15, 2026. The season’s main Captain-led rotations fall on Sundays, although Captain’s Day is not exclusively a Sunday event. An additional update was scheduled for November 12 alongside update 43.10.

Weekly Captain lineup

Expected weapons for Cap’n Hatsune Miku and Kasane Teto’s week include the Leekcapper and Flapjack Rifle. Oscar’s expected additions include Oscar’s Frenzy Auto Shotgun and the Sir Oscar the Second Sprite. Treat those upcoming lineups as expected content, not as items already available from those updates.

When do Treasure Chest rewards change?

Treasure Chest rewards follow their own weekly windows. A new Captain’s Day date and a cosmetic refresh should not automatically be treated as the same deadline, particularly when an extra midweek update appears on the schedule.

Which weapons and rewards have arrived?

The first two weekly Captain updates show how the event divides its rewards. Boss encounters provide a route to signature combat equipment, while Treasure Chests contain a separate rotating cosmetic lineup.

A$AP Rocky’s update

A$AP Rocky arrived aboard the $.$. Rocky battleship. His signature weapon is A$AP Rocky’s Shock Rocket, making the battleship encounter the relevant destination if that weapon is your goal.

His week introduced the Lil’ Flacko Sprite and Fortune Sprite variants. The first Map Fragment also became available during this opening weekly update.

A$AP Rocky on Crossbones Isle

Lil Uzi Vert’s update

Lil Uzi Vert’s boss encounter is at the Stronghold. His signature weapon, Lil Uzi Vert’s Lil’ SMG, fires faster the longer you hold the trigger and provides a movement speed boost. Its firing behavior rewards sustained fire, so account for the increasing fire rate when deciding whether it belongs in your loadout.

This update introduced the Lil’ Uzi Vert Jr. Sprite, brought Cursed Sprite variants into the wild, and added the second Map Fragment.

Lil Uzi Vert at the Stronghold

Treasure Chest cosmetic comparison

The first two chest rotations contain different outfits, accessories, music, and vehicles. If you are looking for a specific item, match its name to the correct weekly pool instead of assuming every seasonal reward remains available together.

These are Treasure Chest rewards, not the signature weapons associated with the Captain encounters. Choose your activity around the reward you actually want.

Does Captain’s Day increase drop rates?

Captain’s Day should not be treated as a guaranteed rare-drop boost. New weapons and Sprites change what you can find, but a larger or different reward pool does not by itself mean that a particular rare item has better odds.

Captain’s Day versus Treasure Hour

Treasure Hour is the separate event to prioritize when your goal is improved chances of finding rare items. Captain’s Day is the date to watch for new content and changing weekly lineups.

Pirate Shop Tokens can also drop outside Captain’s Day. You do not need to wait for a Captain update to pursue one, and the event’s arrival does not guarantee a token. Plan your next session around the relevant boss, collectible, or chest deadline instead of the event name alone.
