GTA 6 credits confirm Dylan Rourke as Jason Duval as both leads go public on launch day

TheTools · 19.11.2026 20:16 · source · permalink

Rockstar Games has confirmed that Dylan Rourke plays Jason Duval in Grand Theft Auto VI. The game's full credits, published on Rockstar's website as GTA VI launched on November 19, 2026, list Rourke as Jason in the main cast alongside Manni L. Perez as Lucia Caminos. The confirmation settles a casting question fans have debated since at least May 2024, when YouTuber LegacyKillaHD claimed a source had identified Rourke as Jason.

Jason and Lucia's actors break their silence

Rourke and Perez went public on the same day. In a joint Instagram post, their first public comment on the roles, the pair praised what they called "the hardest working and most skilled artisans game making has to offer" and said they were honored to have collaborated on the project.

The timing fits a September report that Rockstar had reminded talent working on the game that their non-disclosure agreements barred them from announcing their involvement before November 19. That reminder followed Stephen Root's early confirmation that he voices Brian Heder.

The pair also appeared together in a launch-day photo, standing with their arms around a third person beneath a GTA VI billboard in New York. The image reportedly first appeared on the Instagram account of stunt performer Victoria Lee, who is pictured with the pair, before film news outlet DiscussingFilm shared it on X. Its caption described "Jason & Lucia's actors Dylan Rourke & Manni L Perez" posing in front of a GTA 6 poster for the first time.

How the two leads were identified before launch

Perez was named first: The Game Awards nominations, announced on November 17, list her among five Best Performance nominees for GTA VI ahead of the December 10 ceremony. The listing names the game rather than the character, but it was widely taken as confirmation that she plays Lucia. Jason's actor was not nominated.

Rourke's name surfaced a day later, when IGN's Dutch edition identified him as Jason in its GTA VI review on November 18. The claim spread quickly on social media, but without a word from Rockstar or Rourke himself, it remained unconfirmed until the credits went live.

From Diamond Casino dealer and TV guest star to GTA leads

Perez already had a history with Rockstar; She is credited with voicing two unnamed blackjack dealers in GTA Online's 2019 Diamond Casino & Resort update, and fans had long pointed to her resemblance to Lucia in the game's trailers.

Rourke, by contrast, has no previous video game credits. His screen work includes guest roles in Grey's Anatomy, Station 19, and Modern Family, as well as the films Bury the Bride and Send It!.

The published credits also confirm the rest of the main cast, including Saturday Night Live alum Bobby Moynihan as Jason's friend Cal and Stephen Root as Brian Heder.
