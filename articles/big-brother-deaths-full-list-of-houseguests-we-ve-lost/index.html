‘Big Brother’ deaths: Full list of houseguests we’ve lost

TheTools · 12.10.2026 20:40 · source · permalink

Amber Siyavus

Season 8

Amber died on Oct. 9, 2026, in Nevada, according to the Clark County Office of the Coroner/Medical Examiner. While the cause of death was not made public, Season 8 winner “Evel” Dick Donato suggested on social media that it was drug-related and wrote, “RIP Amber your battle is over.” Amber made it to seventh place in the competition and was in the Late Night Crew alliance with Dick and his daughter, Daniele Briones.
