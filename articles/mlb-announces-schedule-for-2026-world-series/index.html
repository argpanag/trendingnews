MLB announces schedule for 2026 World Series

TheTools · 16.10.2026 19:30 · source · permalink

The stage is nearly set for MLB's biggest event, with the 2026 World Series schedule officially announced.

The Milwaukee Brewers will host the opening two games of the best-of-seven series after sweeping the Los Angeles Dodgers in the National League Championship Series to advance to their first World Series since 1982.

Milwaukee will face either the Cleveland Guardians or Tampa Bay Rays, who are currently battling for the American League pennant.

All seven games are scheduled to begin at 8 p.m. ET, with the Brewers holding home-field advantage while awaiting the American League winner.

All games can be seen on Sportsnet and Sportsnet+.

Here is the full World Series schedule (all times Eastern).
