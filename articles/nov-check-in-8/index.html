NOV check in #8

NVIDIA is bouncing about 4.3% in pre-market to around 189.5, after closing the last session near 181.6. On the weekly chart, the stock fell sharply from its peak near 235, and that last leg down was steep, dropping roughly 20% in just a few weeks. The long-term trend is still up, with a series of higher lows since 2024, but this is the biggest pullback in a while, so I'm treating it carefully.
A pre-market pop after a heavy drop is not a reversal on its own, so I'm not rushing in. My plan is to hold my core position and add only if price stays above the 180 area, which is the level that held at the close. If that fails, the next area I'm watching is 160, where buyers stepped in earlier this year, and I'd keep my risk line below it. On the upside, I want to see a recovery back above 200 before I trust the bounce, and a move toward 220 would confirm the uptrend is back. Volume has been fading for most of this run, so I want to see real buying come in on the way up.
This month's catalysts are what make NVDA so sensitive right now. The Fed's November FOMC meeting affects high-growth valuations, the midterm elections could bring headlines on chip exports and trade policy, and the late Q3 earnings season will show whether AI spending from big tech keeps growing. Any surprise on those fronts could move NVDA more than the broader market.
Sharp drops and sharp bounces both test patience, so I'm keeping my size steady and letting the chart show me where demand really is. Are you buying this bounce in NVDA or waiting for a retest of the lows?
