Pedro Neto opens up to Portuguese media about where he likes to play most

TheTools · 17.11.2026 12:15 · source · permalink

Pedro Neto is doing really well under Xabi Alonso, and he’s been speaking in the Portuguese media about how it’s going.

Pedro Neto is on international duty right now, and he’s spoken a little about his current position at Chelsea, where he’s made a bright start to life under Xabi Alonso.

“To be honest, I feel more comfortable on the right. I think I have more options on that side,” Neto said in quotes picked up by SportWitness.

“It’s rare to see a winger playing on the same side as their dominant foot. People even say it’s a bit like an ‘old-fashioned winger’. I feel more comfortable playing on the right, although I like playing on both sides.”

As well as making sure he’s on the right, an important change under Alonso has been Neto’s starting position. While he’s no longer a proper wing back as he was earlier in the season, he’s still picking up the ball deeper and driving forward at defenders rather than receiving it to feet while already squared up against a settled defence.

He looks so much more effectively when he’s driving forward rather than stuck stationary.

We still want to seem more from Neto – but he’s gone up a level this year.
