The death has occurred of

Seamus Tuohy, 22nd September 2026, Doogary, Breaffy, Castlebar, unexpectedly.

 

Predeceased by his parents Tom and Claire Tuohy, sister Christina, aunts and uncles.

 

Deeply missed by his heartbroken wife Marie, daughter Shona and son-in-law Peter, son Evan and daughter-in-law Amy, his adored grandchildren Ave, Aedamar and Isla, brother Tommie, sisters Mary, Claire, Helen and Brid, brothers-in-law, uncle Jimmy, aunt-in-law Nell, nieces, nephews, cousins and a wide circle of family and friends.

 

Seamus will also be deeply missed within the G.A.A community where he had a lifelong involvement.

 

Rest in peace dear Seamus you are deeply missed and forever in our hearts.

 

Seamus will repose in the Dr. Michael Loftus Room, McHale Park, F23 AC90 on Friday (25th September) from 4pm with removal at 7.30pm to his residence.

 

House private please.

 

Removal from his residence on Saturday at 1.30pm for Requiem Mass at 3pm in St. Aloysius Church, Breaffy, followed by burial afterwards in Breaffy Cemetery.

 

Mass can be viewed live on; https://www.churchtv.ie/breaffy/

 

Family flowers only please, donations if desired to:

https://sunflowerchildrenshospice.ie/

 

Anyone wishing to offer their sympathies to the family can leave a message by clicking on the “Condolences” link below.
