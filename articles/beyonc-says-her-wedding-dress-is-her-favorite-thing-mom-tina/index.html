Beyoncé is reflecting on the most special piece her mom, Tina Knowles, has designed for her.

In an interview with i-D published on Wednesday, Nov. 11, the superstar, 45, said her "favorite dress" Knowles made for her was the white strapless gown she wore for her 2008 wedding to Jay-Z.

"My favorite dress my mother designed was my wedding dress—it was gorgeously simple and made with her love," she told the outlet.

The "Single Ladies" singer continued, "We had a very small wedding in our home. We told the guests we were having a party, and no one knew they were coming to our wedding but our parents and a few friends. Wearing a dress she designed was very important to me, and more important than any designer dress."

Tina Knowles and Beyoncé.
Credit: Julian Dakdouk/Parkwood Media/WireImage

Knowles, 72, has famously designed clothes for Beyoncé throughout her career, from stage costumes to red carpet looks. The mother-daughter duo launched the clothing line House of Deréon in 2004 and work together on Beyoncé's haircare line Cécred, which debuted in 2024.

Beyoncé and Jay-Z, 56, married in an intimate wedding on April 4, 2008. The pair tied the knot in their own home, transforming the rapper's 13,500-square-foot penthouse in New York City's TriBeCa neighborhood into a space fit for the special occasion.

Beyoncé and Jay-Z on their wedding day.
Credit: Tina Knowles/ Instagram

The "Crazy in Love" duo — who now share daughter Blue Ivy and fraternal twins Rumi and Sir — took measures to keep their nuptials private, including having guests arrive discreetly and not record.

Afterward, nearly all of the special event's details stayed under wraps until the couple began sharing snippets of the day through different projects.

In 2011, Beyoncé revealed the first glimpse of her wedding dress in a video for Live at Roseland. Three years later, Jay-Z joined in and shared clips of their ceremony during the pair's joint On The Run tour.
