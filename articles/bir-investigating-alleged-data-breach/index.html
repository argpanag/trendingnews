BIR investigating alleged data breach

TheTools · 09.11.2026 07:38 · source · permalink

BUSINESS

Nov 9, 2026

3:38 PM GMT+8

BIR logo

MANILA – The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) is coordinating with the Cybercrime Investigation and Coordinating Center (CICC) and the Department of Information and Communications Technology-National Computer Emergency Response Team (DICT-NCERT) to address claims of cybersecurity breaches.

 

“Immediate response measures have been implemented to assess the situation and protect the security and integrity of its systems and taxpayer data,” the BIR said in a statement on Monday, following reports of an alleged data leak circulating online.

 

“The BIR remains committed to strengthen its cybersecurity posture,” it added.

 

The BIR advised the public to remain vigilant and conduct all transactions exclusively through its official channels and platforms.

 

It assured that further updates will be issued once available. (Anna Leah Gonzales/PNA)
