Aizen Is Back in BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War Episode 48 Preview - Anime Corner

TheTools · 11.09.2026 11:03 · source · permalink

BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War (TYBW) Part 4 has revealed the preview for Episode 8 (Episode 48 overall). The anime returned with the fourth and final cour, subtitled The Calamity, on July 25. It is set to run for 10 episodes.

The fifth episode is titled “THE END TWO WORLD,” and it will premiere on Saturday, September 12. You can see the synopsis and preview images below.

Uryu slammed the central island of Wahrwelt, which possessed an enormous amount of energy, into the “Distortion,” successfully causing their mutual annihilation. However, for Yhwach, who had reclaimed all of the Schrifts and elevated the power of “The Almighty,” the Distortion was no longer necessary.

Declaring that he would destroy the Seireitei as one final amusement, Yhwach vanished. With his Bankai shattered and his Zanpakuto broken, Ichigo had sunk into the depths of despair, unable even to stand. The flame of his courage was on the verge of fading. Then, before him appeared men with whom he had once crossed swords…

  • Script: Masaki Hiramatsu

  • Animation Directors: Manamu Amasaki (爆画大阪スタジオ), Natsuko Suzuki, Izumi Kondo, Kazuo Takigawa, Mika Okubo

Created by Tite Kubo, BLEACH was serialized in Weekly Shonen Jump from 2001 to 2016 and concluded with 74 volumes. The manga has exceeded 130 million copies in circulation worldwide, making it one of the best-selling manga series of all time.

The original anime adaptation aired from 2004 to 2012 and received four theatrical films. The franchise returned in 2022 with BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War, adapting the manga’s final arc. Its final part (Part 4) is set to release in July. Tite Kubo has confirmed it will include even more original material than the previous parts, expanding the manga’s final battles.
