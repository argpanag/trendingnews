Brush fire erupts near Getty Center, prompting evacuation warnings

Firefighters appeared to make quick progress against a brush fire the broke out Wednesday near the Getty Center.

As of 5 p.m., evacuation warnings had been issued for zones LFD-0895 and LF-0967, although the LAFD said no structures were immediately threatened as the water-dropping helicopters attacked the fire from above. Forward progress of the fire was reported stopped shortly after 6 p.m. at a little over 20 acres.

The fire broke out as Southern California endures high heat and record-breaking humidity.

The basics

  • Acreage: 20+ acres as of Wednesday evening
  • Containment: Forward progress stopped
  • Structures destroyed: None reported
  • Deaths: None
  • Injuries: Unknown
  • Personnel working on fire: Unknown
