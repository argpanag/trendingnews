Littler sends message to Hearn family over schedule that will be treat for fans

TheTools · 24.09.2026 20:49 · source · permalink
  • Tom Hancock, Sports Writer
  • Published: 21:49, 24 Sep 2026
  • Updated: 21:49, 24 Sep 2026

Luke Littler has questioned the scheduling of Anthony Joshua and Tyson Fury's blockbuster fight.

The long-awaited, all-British heavyweight bout between Joshua and Fury is slated to take place at Cardiff's Principality Stadium on Friday, December 11.

That's the same day on which the 2027 PDC World Darts Championship begins, with two-time reigning world champion Littler in action on the opening night.

And Littler appears unimpressed by the scheduling conflict.

The fight is being promoted by the Hearn family's Matchroom Sport, which also runs darts' showpiece event at the Alexandra Palace.

Matchroom Sport founder Barry Hearn's son Eddie Hearn is now chairman of the company and the PDC.

Littler upset at Hearn family over 'clash'

Posting to his Instagram Story, Littler wrote: "A message to the Hearns: what is this?

"Opening night of the World Championship (world champion plays on opening night) and you put one of the biggest fights in Britain on the same night. You had one job."

Littler concluded his post with two emojis: a monkey covering its eyes and a despairing face.

However, the two events are unlikely to clash directly, with Joshua vs Fury expected to get underway in the early hours of Saturday morning UK time in order to maximise interest from the USA.

But it'll be a Friday night for sports fans to remember who will have arguably the biggest boxing event in a generation to feast on after the opening night of the PDC World Championship.

Littler looking to emulate Taylor

Littler heads to the Alexandra Palace this winter aiming to become just the second player to win three consecutive World Championships.

The legendary Phil Taylor achieved that feat in two separate streaks, triumphing a staggering eight years running from 1995 to 2002.

But the 19-year-old will back himself to emulate 'The Power', who, at the age of 66, has not ruled out a sensational return to the oche.

Littler has enjoyed a phenomenal 2026 so far and only recently saw his hopes of an unprecedented clean sweep of PDC majors ended.

He suffered a surprise defeat to Jonny Clayton in the last eight of this month's World Series of Darts Finals in Amsterdam.

That came after a hostile reception from Dutch fans, which led Littler to threaten a boycott of future tournaments in the Netherlands.

Littler to defend crown at biggest World Championship yet

Littler retained his World Championship Crown by thrashing Gian van Veen 7-1 in the 2026 final.

This year's World Darts Championship will be the biggest edition of the event yet.

For the first time, play will take place in the Alexandra Palace's Great Hall, substantially boosting crowd capacity.
