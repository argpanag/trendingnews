Ex-Arsenal star at centre of mass brawl in Champions League and receives flying head kick

TheTools · 27.08.2026 15:16 · source · permalink

Ex-Arsenal man Matteo Guendouzi was at the centre of a brawl after Fenerbahce knocked Lyon out of the Champions League.

The French international and former Marseille player started and played the full 90 minutes as the Turkish side booked their place in the Champions League group stage with a 2-1 win over Lyon, winning 3-2 on aggregate.

However, the unsavoury scenes afterwards marred the huge occasion for Fenerbahce, who are in the Champions League proper for the first time since 2008.

Guendouzi, who was with Arsenal from 2018 to 2022, is known for his fiery character, and following the full-time whistle, he took to celebrating right in front of the Lyon fans, who are of course rivals to his beloved Marseille, whom he played for and supported as a child.

Other Fenerbahce players joined in, but as they walked down towards the tunnel, both sets of players clashed over Guendouzi’s antagonising celebration.

After that boiled over after several minutes, Matteo Guendouzi and a teammate were walking back down the tunnel when a Lyon member of staff came in with a high kick to Guendouzi before a brawl broke out again.

The scenes were quite surreal, and speaking to the media afterwards in the mixed zone, Guendouzi claimed he was berated with abuse from the Lyon fans all evening.

‘You know, when you have 60,000 people insulting your family, your mother, your children, even before the match, and no one in the refereeing camp tells the stadium to be quiet…

‘Because I know that in France, when this kind of thing happens, there comes a point where they talk to the fans and the match can be stopped. For 90 minutes, even longer with the warm-up, they insulted my family. It’s something that affects you.

‘If you want to tease, then it goes both ways. They insulted my mother and my family, which I found disrespectful. But if we can’t even celebrate the way we want anymore, I think that’s a shame.’

Fenerbahce join AEK Athens, Bodo/Glimt and Sabah of Azerbaijan in the Champions League. Celtic looked all set to make it through when they went 4-0 up on aggregate against LASK, before they somehow conspired to lose 5-4 after extra time on aggregate.
