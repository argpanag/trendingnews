CAN bids farewell to Nepali National Men's Cricket team

TheTools · 11.10.2026 15:29 · source · permalink

Kathmandu, Oct 4: The Nepali National Men's Cricket team playing in the ICC World Cup Cricket League-2 one-day series was given a farewell. 

At a programme organised here on Sunday, President of Cricket Association of Nepal (CAN), Chatur Bahadur Chand, Secretary Paras Khadka, and other office-bearers bade farewell to the team. 

On the occasion, CAN President Chand expressed the belief that Nepal would perform best in the series and extended best wishes to the Nepali team. 

In the series, Nepal will face Oman and Canada under ICC World Cup Cricket League-2. Nepal will play against Canada on October 11 and 17 and against Oman on October 13 and October 19.

This series is important for Nepal to maintain its One-Day status. (RSS)
