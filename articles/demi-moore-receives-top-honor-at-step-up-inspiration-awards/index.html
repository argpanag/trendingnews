Demi Moore Receives Top Honor at Step Up Inspiration Awards

TheTools · 02.10.2026 22:21 · source · permalink

Actress and producer Demi Moore was honored for her career and mentorship efforts at the 2026 Step Up Inspiration Awards on September 25 at the Skirball Cultural Center in Los Angeles. The sold-out luncheon raised a record $770,000 for the nonprofit organization.

More than 560 attendees joined Step Up Chief Executive Officer Delores Druilhet Morton to recognize Moore alongside fellow honorees Ali Larter, Becky G, Priah Ferguson, Leila Milano, and youth recipient Natalia Benitez. The national mentorship group focuses on guiding young women toward their goals.

During the event, Moore and Larter, who co-star on the Paramount drama series Landman, discussed their careers in entertainment, the mentors who guided them, and the importance of female support systems.

"I’ve always believed in supporting other women," said Ali Larter, actress and author. "I’m so grateful for the incredible women I have in my life. The women in my world, we don’t compete with each other. That mindset is essential."

Addressing the audience alongside Larter, Moore emphasized unity over competition among women in the industry.

"It’s not a competition; it’s about coming together and lifting each other up," said Demi Moore, actress, producer, and New York Times bestselling author. "For me, there is no better feeling than being able to show up for someone else."

Moore arrived on the red carpet wearing an illustrated landscape-print Thom Browne skirt suit paired with a blue shirt, tie, tube socks, and T-bar Mary Jane pumps. She attended with her youngest daughter, Tallulah Willis, who wore a coordinating minimalist gray version of the Thom Browne design.

The recognition caps off a prominent period for Moore, who recently earned an Oscar nomination and wins at the SAG, Golden Globe, and Critics' Choice Awards for her role in The Substance. The organization's fundraising tour will next move to Chicago in November.
