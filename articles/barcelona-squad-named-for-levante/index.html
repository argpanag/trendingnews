Barcelona squad named for Levante

TheTools · 13.09.2026 03:00 · source · permalink
  • Barcelona: La Liga

﻿Hansi Flick has called up 24 players for Sunday’s clash

The next Barcelona match is upon us! The Blaugrana travel to Valencia for a tough La Liga away test against Levante, and Hansi Flick has called up the following 24 players for Sunday’s clash:

Goalkeepers: 1. Joan Garcia, 13. Wojciech Szczesny, 25. Dominik Livakovic

Defenders: 2. João Cancelo, 3. Alejandro Balde, 5. Pau Cubarsí, 12. Xavi Espart, 15. Andreas Christensen, 18. Gerard Martín, 23. Jules Kounde, 24. Eric Garcia

Midfielders: 4. Brian Fariñas, 6. Gavi, 7. Fermín López, 8. Pedri, 16. Rodri, 20. Dani Olmo, 22. Marc Bernal

Forwards: 9. Gabriel Jesus, 10. Lamine Yamal, 11. Raphinha, 14. Karim Adeyemi, 17. Anthony Gordon, 29. Hamza Abdelkarim

There are good news for this one as Eric Garcia returns to the squad after missing the Champions League win over Feyenoord through suspension, and the full-back will play with a mask following his broken nose last week. Only two players don’t make the trip for this one as Frenkie de Jong (knee) and Roony Bardghji (knee) miss out through injury, but Flick will have to leave one player out of the final 23-man squad once the lineups are announced.

Predicted starting XI (4-3-3):

Joan; Eric, Cubarsí, Martín, Espart; Fermín, Rodri, Pedri; Yamal, Raphinha, Gordon

The match kicks off at 4.15pm CET (Barcelona), 3.15pm BST/WAT (UK & Nigeria), 10.15am ET, 7.15am PT (USA), 7.45pm IST (India), and you can join us to follow and comment all the action.
