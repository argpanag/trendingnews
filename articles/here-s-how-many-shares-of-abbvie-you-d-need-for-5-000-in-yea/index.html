How many shares of AbbVie should you own for $5,000 in annual income?

To earn $5,000 in annual dividends from AbbVie, you would need to own 723 shares for about $206,358 as of this writing. That might not seem like a great deal, especially when the same investment in Pfizer (NYSE: PFE) would generate nearly $12,000 in annual dividends.

But over the past three years, AbbVie's stock rallied 93% as it overcame its loss of U.S. exclusivity for Humira (its blockbuster autoimmune drug) and expanded its new drugs to offset that pressure. Pfizer's stock sank 8% as its sales of COVID-19 vaccines evaporated and its other major drugs faced patent cliffs. Therefore, AbbVie could still be a solid investment for long-term income investors, even though its yield seems unimpressive.
