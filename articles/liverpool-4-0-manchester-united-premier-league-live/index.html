A memorable victory for Liverpool, who played some scintillating football to thrash a hapless Man Utd. The match was over after 14 minutes: Leny Yoro was sent off before Dominik Szoboszlai and the marvellous Florian Wirtz scored excellent goals.

All four goals were beauties, in fact. Rio Ngumoha, a joy to watch, and Victor Munoz benefitted from great work by Bradley Barcola and Wirtz respectively.

A beaming Andoni Iraola salutes the Kop, mock-sheepishly punches his first Jurgen Klopp-style. “We need this,” he said before the game. He meant three points, but Liverpool gave him so much more.

Full time: Liverpool 4-0 Manchester United

90+2 min Van Dijk’s header across the face of goal is kneed to safety by Maguire. There was a player waiting behind him to make it 5-0.

84 min Man Utd officially have the worst defence in Europe’s top five leagues: they’ve conceded 34 goals in all competitions. Bring back Daisy and Dolly.

83 min In the last five years (and a month), Liverpool have beaten Man Utd 7-0, 5-0, 4-0 (twice, including this game) and 3-0.

United lost the ball in their own third and were punished ruthlessly. Florian Wirtz, who may have had his best game for Liverpool, played a gorgeous disguised pass into Munoz, who rapped a first-time shot past Lammens on the run. Super goal.

78 min: Liverpool substitution Jeremie Frimpong replaces Rio Ngumoha, whose goal capped a joyful performance – and made him the youngest score in this fixture at 18 years 85 days. Watching Ngumoha could lift anyone’s spirits.

For Liverpool, Jurgen Klopp is the gift that keeps on giving. Wirtz looks a different player since working with him at international level.

75 min Isak dummies a couple of defenders and screws a shot is pushed away well by Lammens. Within ten seconds he has to leap to his left to paw a long-range shot from Szoboszlai round the post.

74 min “Hi Rob,” writes Paul Howarth. “Is that James Nesbitt sat next to Sir Alex?”

It is indeed. Can he play in defence?

73 min: Double substitution for Liverpool Trey Nyoni and Victor Munoz come on for Alexis Mac Allister and Bradley Barcola.

71 min Man Utd have let in 33 goals in all competitions this season. No club in Europe’s big five leagues have conceded more.

69 min I thought De Ligt had gone off but it was actually Youri Tielemans, my apologies.

68 min Almost a fourth goal for Liverpool. Wirtz plays another terrific angled pass to release Barcola, whose shot from 12 yards hits the stretching Martinez and flies wide. Vital defending.

Liverpool’s front four are not the most consistent, not yet, but my god their ceiling is high.

So much for Bradley Barcola having no confidence: he made that goal with a sensational piece of play. Barcola slipped Martinez on the halfway line, roared into the space and kept going all the way into the area. Then he got his head up to find the onrushing Ngumoha with a perfectly weighted square pass. Ngumoha – the best player on the pitch – swept a first-time shot past Lammens.

GOAL! Liverpool 3-0 Man Utd (Ngumoha 66)

Brilliant goal!

65 min: Man Utd substitutions Mason Mount and Noussair Mazraoui replace Patrick Dorgu and Matthijs De Ligt Youri Tielemans.

64 min Ngumoha’s curler from the left edge of the area is blocked. Moments later, Barcola turns beautifully away from De Ligt on the edge of the area, only to stab over the bar with his left foot. Decent chance.

62 min: Great chance for Isak! The beleaguered De Ligt misjudges a routine ball forward, allowing Isak to surge through on goal. He’s usually so composed in front of goal but this time he splatters a lamentable half-volley well wide.

61 min Szoboszlai, who has been majestic, tees up Mac Allister for a shot that is deflected behind. Szoboszlai clips the corner into Barcola on the edge of the area; he takes a heavy touch, then fouls Tielemans and is booked.

I’m sure Barcola will be fine in the medium-term, but right now his confidence looks subterranean.

58 min Jacquet shows great pace to stop Fernandes reaching Mainoo’s through ball. At the other end, Barcola is put through on goal by a superb pass from Wirtz. He tries to go round Lammens but his touch is far too heavy and the ball flies behind for a goalkick. That was a really poor attempt for a player of his ability/fee.
