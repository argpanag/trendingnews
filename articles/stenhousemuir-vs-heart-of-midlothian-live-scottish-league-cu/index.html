    19:14 BST

    Stenhousemuir v Heart of Midlothian (19:45 BST)

    Wouter Vrancken has been insisting he is not treating Stenny lightly, but Hearts' Belgian head coach nevertheless makes six changes, with much-praised forwards Claudio Braga and James Wilson, as well as lauded wingers Josh McPake and Calvin Miller, dropping to the bench along with defender Jordi Altena.

    Into defence come Christian Borchgrevink and Harry Milne, Sabri Geundouz is in midfield, while Sabah Kerjota, Rogers Mato and JJ Williams are up front, the latter making his first start since his arrival from Rhode Island.

    Utility man Oisin McEntee misses out after picking up an injury in the dramatic comeback win at home to Dundee.

    19:07 BST

    Stenhousemuir v Heart of Midlothian (19:45 BST)

    In years gone by, Stenhousemuir wouldn't be given much of a hope against Hearts.

    Oh how times have changed.

    The part-timers surprised many by running Inverness Caledonian Thistle close for the League 1 title last season before winning promotion to the Championship for the first time ever via the play-offs.

    Losing manager Gary Naysmith to Ayr United this summer has not halted their momentum and they sit top of the second tier under new boss Marvin Bartley - and are the only team in Scotland not to have lost a game this season, albeit they did lose a bonus-point penalty-shoot in the group stage.

    Ten games unbeaten is a club record.
