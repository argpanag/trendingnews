After a slow start to the campaign, things finally look to be clicking for the home team under new boss Iraola. On Wednesday, Liverpool notched a UEFA Champions League win over Atletico Madrid, building on its first Premier League victory of the season last weekend against newly promoted Ipswich.

The home side will come into this game confident of making it three wins on the bounce, with Fulham all at sea under its new head coach, Álvaro Arbeloa. The former Real Madrid boss saw his side crash to a third straight defeat in its 3-2 loss to London rival Crystal Palace last weekend. That performance did little to dispel speculation that it’s a strong relegation candidate this season.

Liverpool takes on Fulham on Saturday, Sept. 12, at Anfield, with kickoff set for 3 p.m. BST. That makes it a 10 a.m. ET or 7 a.m. PT start in the US and Canada, and a 12 a.m. AEST kickoff in Australia.

Can I livestream Liverpool vs. Fulham in the UK?

No broadcaster in the UK has the rights to show this game live because of the traditional Saturday 3 p.m. kickoff blackout. This prohibits matches from being shown in the region at that time to protect attendances throughout the English football pyramid.   

That also means that if you’re traveling in the UK, you’re unlikely to be able to watch the game as you normally would at home due to geoblocking.
