Sunny skies and windy weekend ahead

TheTools · 24.10.2026 00:14 · source · permalink

SARASOTA, Fla. (WWSB) - Sunshine and dry conditions will continue across the Suncoast this weekend, with comfortable mornings and warm afternoons. However, breezy conditions will stick around, especially near the coast. Small craft advisories are now in effect through Wednesday evening ahead of the windy conditions.

Friday afternoon temperatures will remain in the lower 80s before dropping into the lower to mid-60s overnight. Northeast winds will gust over 20 mph at times, with nearly a 0% chance of rain.

Saturday will bring another beautiful day, with sunny skies and afternoon highs in the lower 80s. By Sunday, temperatures will warm into the mid-80s, but northeast winds will strengthen, with gusts approaching 30 mph. Boaters should be prepared for choppy to rough conditions on the water.

Looking ahead to the workweek, sunshine and dry conditions will continue, with afternoon highs in the lower to mid-80s. Winds will remain breezy, with gusts potentially exceeding 30 mph on Monday.

One noticeable change will be the gradual return of humidity. Dew points will climb from the lower 60s this weekend into the upper 60s and lower 70s by midweek, bringing back a slightly muggy feel.

Rain chances will remain near 0% through much of next week, with only a slight chance of isolated showers returning Thursday and Friday.

For the tropics, no activity is expected over the next 7 days.

Overall, expect plenty of sunshine, warm afternoons, and breezy conditions across the Suncoast as we head into the final week of October.
