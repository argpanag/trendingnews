'The X-Files' Officially Returns After 8-Year Hiatus

TheTools · 13.10.2026 16:33 · source · permalink

Sinners director Ryan Coogler has been attempting to re-open The X-Files for some time. Three years ago, Coogler began drawing up a revival of the classic 1990s science fiction procedural at Hulu as part of his overall deal with Disney, and he wrote and directed a pilot for a potential series earlier this year. The series had yet to get a pickup, but fans, as always, wanted to believe.

Now, the truth is out there. Deadline reports that Coogler's reboot of The X-Files has now been picked up to series by Hulu. The series will star Himesh Patel (The Odyssey) and Danielle Deadwyler (The Piano Lesson) as two mismatched federal agents who are assigned to re-open a long-shuttered department of the FBI dedicated to investigating unexplained and paranormal phenomena. The pilot is also set to star Amy Madigan, who won Best Supporting Actress last year for her performance as the macabre Aunt Gladys in Weapons, as well as Steve Buscemi (Fargo), Ben Foster (Hell or High Water), Devery Jacobs (Reservation Dogs), Lochlyn Munro (Freddy vs. Jason), Tantoo Cardinal (KIllers of the Flower Moon), Joel D. Montgrand (True Detective), and Sofia Grace Clifton (The Muppets Mayhem).

Collider's Perri Nemiroff recently spoke to Deadwyler regarding her role in the series. She was tight-lipped about spoiling the series, and would only reveal that "it is cooking slowly and with lots of flavor. That's the most I've given away today. I’m sorry! That’s the most I’ve given away today!" The series will be produced by Sev Ohanian and Zinzi Coogler; original X-Files creator Chris Carter is also on board as a producer. No release date has yet been announced for the series.

Will 'The X-Files' Original Stars Return for the New Series?

Gillian Anderson, who starred in the original series as skeptical doctor Dana Scully, has also been reticent about a possible return to the FBI. However, she has read Coogler's script for the pilot, and she was effusive in her praise for it at a MegaCon Orlando panel moderated by Collider's Maggie Lovitt:

It's very different and very similar all at the same time, and it's very special. I think he's incredibly talented, and I think he's one of the only people who can do something unique and do it justice, and he's completely obsessed with the series. He's one of the most talented filmmakers who's out there. The fact that Chris has him as the person who's going to be involved in the franchise is awesome, and a huge gift. You guys will see what he has in mind, and I don't think you'll be disappointed.
