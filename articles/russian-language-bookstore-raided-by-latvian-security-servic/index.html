Russian-language bookstore raided by Latvian security service

TheTools · 04.09.2026 17:41 · source · permalink

The operations were carried out as part of a criminal investigation launched by the VDD on 28 July into suspected breaches of sanctions imposed by the European Union.

During the criminal proceedings, one person was detained and remanded in custody as a precautionary measure.

In the interests of the investigation, the State Security Service has not provided any further comments at this stage.

The VDD pointed out that the criminal proceedings carried out are not related to the amendments to legislation concerning industrial goods prohibited from being imported into Latvia, which came into force on 1 September and prohibit the import into Latvia from Russia and Belarus of books, newspapers and other printed matter into Latvia.

The service also pointed out that the control of goods imports falls within the remit of the Customs Administration of the State Revenue Service and the Tax and Customs Police.

On Friday, the LETA news agency observed that the “Mnogoknig” shop on Augusta Deglava Street in Rīga was open to the public and that people were shopping there without hindrance. However, “Mnogoknig” staff encountered at the shop declined to answer questions about whether and why representatives of law enforcement agencies had visited the shop.

Information from “Firmas lv” shows that the owner of SIA “Mnogoknig” is Aleksandrs Karabeļņikovs. In 2025, the company had a turnover of 613,683 euros and made a profit of 9,696 euros.
