FC Cincinnati to honor Fiona the hippo at Saturday’s MLS Cup Playoffs Game 2 | FC Cincinnati

TheTools · 24.11.2026 17:12 · source · permalink

FC Cincinnati will honor Fiona, the Cincinnati Zoo’s beloved hippo who became a symbol of Cincinnati and captured hearts around the world, during the club’s Audi 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs match against Nashville SC on Saturday, Nov. 28 at TQL Stadium following her passing last Thursday.

As part of the tribute, FC Cincinnati supporters will unveil a special tifo honoring Fiona prior to the match. The club will also make a donation for every fan in attendance at TQL Stadium to the Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden in Fiona’s memory.

FC Cincinnati and the Cincinnati Zoo have a longstanding relationship rooted in serving and celebrating the Cincinnati community. The Zoo has partnered with the club through Learning Is Cool, FC Cincinnati’s educational program that celebrates and rewards students for strong academic performance.

In addition, the groups teamed up to launch the club’s 2023 primary River Kit, which paid tribute to the Ohio River and its importance to Cincinnati. As part of the River Kit initiative, FC Cincinnati partnered with the Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden and Living Lands & Waters for an Ohio River cleanup, bringing the organizations together to support the river that inspired the kit and has played an important role in the history and identity of the city.

Fans who would like to honor Fiona can support the Cincinnati Zoo & Botanical Garden and its work caring for animals and supporting wildlife conservation. More information about Fiona and ways to support the Zoo are available at cincinnatizoo.org.

FC Cincinnati hosts Nashville SC in a critical Round One, Game 2 in the Audi 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs on Saturday, Nov. 28 at TQL Stadium. The Orange and Blue won Game 1 in Nashville Sunday night, 1-0, and can advance to the Eastern Conference Semifinal with a win. Kickoff is scheduled for 6:30 p.m. ET. Tickets are available through SeatGeek.
