League of Legends Patch 26.21 Notes

TheTools · 23.10.2026 18:57 · source · permalink

Worlds has begun, but time still passes for the rest of us non-professional mortals, so here we’ve got 26.21. This is a pretty chunky patch, with changes to some chunks like Rammus, Sion, Nunu (& Willump), as well as to a bunch of other champions across roles who’ve been on top of the game, either in presence in their role or in power. Others can have a bit more breathing room with the Worlds patch locked in, like Azir, Orianna, and Ryze. Finally, we’ve been seeing Gluttonous Greaves & Hubris overperform on ranged characters in particular, so those are getting nerfed a bit, and maybe Dawncore can work its way into some more builds with buffs.

This patch we did work to adjust Varus' top and bottom power skew, which involved shifting the balance between his Q and W for laning. Replay Download Limits

Starting during Patch 26.21, replay downloads will be limited to 10 per hour. You will still be able to download your own replays, as well as replays from your friends and other players.

This change is necessary to prevent large-scale abuse of downloaded replays by bad actors using vibecoded apps to scrape thousands upon thousands of replays. We opted for a time-based limit to still allow creators to make content, while preventing mass exploitative downloads. We’ll continue to monitor how many replays are downloaded to ensure players have access to this important feature while preventing clear misuse.

Arena

Welcome back to another Arena patch! This time around we're making a larger set of changes with a couple of goals.

We're looking to lower the power level of some of the strongest augments and increase the power of some of the weakest, as well as reduce the sharpness of some of the "one augment combos" so that the champions who're able to high roll with them require a little bit of extra juice to reach their potential.

We're also looking to bring some of the champion outliers in power (both high and low) closer to the center. One of the ways we're doing that is through more non-standard changes that allow players the opportunity to use their champions in different ways, and to take advantage of different augments and items.

Overall, we've shifted power in ways that we hope will change up the meta a bit and provide some fresh new build options and experiences.
