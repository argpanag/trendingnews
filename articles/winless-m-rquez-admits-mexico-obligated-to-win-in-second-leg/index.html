Winless Márquez admits Mexico 'obligated to win' in second leg vs. Jamaica

TheTools · 18.11.2026 03:30 · source · permalink

Mexico coach Rafael Márquez said his team must beat Jamaica in the second leg of their Concacaf Nations League quarterfinal after falling 3-2 in the opener.

Winless in five matches since taking over El Tri from Javier Aguirre, the former assistant faces mounting pressure to get his first victory and avoid an early tournament exit.

"Tomorrow, we're obligated to win, and the pressure is the same as always. Nothing has changed. We need this victory to qualify, we need this win to build confidence, and we need it to keep moving forward," Márquez said Monday.

Marquez saw his side stumble to the recent narrow defeat that initially started with a 3-0 setback by the 63rd minute. Regarding whether he believes that a firing could soon follow if his form continues, the former Barcelona star brought up the need for patience in his role.

"I've only been a coach for a short time, I still have a great deal of enthusiasm and motivation to keep contributing," said Marquez ahead of Tuesday's second leg in Monterrey, Mexico.

"I see myself continuing here with the national team. Perhaps things aren't at their best right now, but I believe better things are yet to come."

Sitting alongside him, defender César Montes admitted to the mistakes that led to last Saturday's defeat.

"The responsibility ultimately lies with us, it's a collective responsibility. We know when we're doing well and when we're doing poorly...we cannot allow that to happen [again]. We are self-critical, we are fully aware of what we did," said Montes.

Were Mexico to lose the series to Jamaica, it would mark the first time that the current Concacaf Nations League title-holders would fail to make the semifinal round. El Tri's current worst performance was a third-place finish in the 2022-23 edition of the competition.

Jamaica's best finish was in the 2023-24 tournament, in which they earned third place.
