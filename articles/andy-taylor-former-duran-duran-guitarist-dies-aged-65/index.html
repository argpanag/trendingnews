Andy Taylor, former Duran Duran guitarist, dies aged 65

TheTools · 25.11.2026 21:31 · source · permalink

Getty

Former Duran Duran guitarist Andy Taylor has died aged 65 after "a long and courageous battle with prostate cancer", the band has said.

Taylor rose to fame as a member of the award-winning rock band in the 1980s, famous for tracks such as Hungry Like the Wolf and Rio.

A joint statement from Taylor's family and Duran Duran described him as a "beloved husband, father and grandfather".

"Andy passed away peacefully at home, surrounded by his family, on Monday 23rd November," the statement continued.

Andy Taylor's wife of 44 years, Tracey Wilson, said: "To the world, Andy was a brilliant guitarist, but to us, he was the most wonderful husband, a loving dad and the proudest grandfather. He was the rock of our family. For all the incredible experiences music brought him, family always came first. Nothing made him happier than having us all together, and no matter how far music took him, his heart was always at home.

"Andy faced the last few years with so much courage and determination. He never gave up, fighting until the very end, never losing his love of life. The kindness and support shown to him by fans from all over the world throughout his illness brought him immense comfort and strength, and for that we will always be grateful.

"Andy knew just how deeply he was loved, and we take great comfort in that. We will miss him every single day."

Duran Duran added: "Andy was there at the very beginning of our story. From the moment he arrived in Birmingham from the North East with a guitar and an unshakeable belief in what we could become, he brought an indelible fire and swagger to our music.

"His playing drove the songs that took us around the world, and with a dry wit that was matched only by his wonderfully unsentimental, no-nonsense outlook on life he kept us grounded and gave the band its edge through the extraordinary years that followed.

"It was great to make music with him again when the original five reunited in the early 2000s, and that time together, both on stage and in the studio, meant a tremendous amount to all of us.

"When we were inducted into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame in 2022, Andy was too unwell to join us, but his words that night reminded everyone of his strength and the bond we shared.

"Andy faced his illness with remarkable courage, honesty and grace.

"Our thoughts are with his wife Tracey, his children Georgina, Isabella, Bethany and Andrew, and grandson Albie. They all loved him deeply and we ask that their privacy be respected at this very difficult time.

"In lieu of flowers, the family has asked that donations be made in Andy's name to Prostate Cancer UK: prostatecanceruk.org.

"With love from Simon, John, Nick and Roger."

While a member of Duran Duran in the 1980s, Taylor also enjoyed success with bandmate John Taylor in the supergroup The Power Station.

After leaving Duran Duran for the first time in 1986, Taylor embarked on a solo career and became an in-demand producer.

He rejoined the band in 2001 but left again in 2006. In 2023, he contributed to Duran Duran's album Danse Macabre.

Additional Reporting: Press Association
