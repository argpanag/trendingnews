Disappearance of 13-year-old in Saskatoon under investigation

TheTools · 22.11.2026 10:55 · source · permalink

SASKATOON, Sask. — The Saskatoon Police Service (SPS) is asking for the public's assistance in locating 13-year-old Gage Whitehawk.

According to the SPS, he was last seen on the morning of Nov. 20 near 23rd Street and Avenue U.

He is described as standing 6'1" and weighing 150 pounds with a fair complexion, a slim build and long black hair typically in a ponytail. He was wearing a black Columbia jacket with a fur-trimmed hood, grey jeans, a white T-shirt with a rose on the front and a black bunny hug.

Anyone with information regarding his whereabouts is asked to call the SPS at 306-975-8300.
