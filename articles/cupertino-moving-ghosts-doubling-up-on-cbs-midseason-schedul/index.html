‘Cupertino’ Moving, ‘Ghosts’ Doubling Up on CBS’ Midseason Schedule

TheTools · 16.11.2026 15:02 · source · permalink

'Matlock,' 'NCIS: Sydney' and new series 'Einstein' are also on tap for early 2027.

CBS is making a couple of notable moves with its midseason lineup.

The network will move first-year drama Cupertino to Sunday nights starting in mid-January, after a solid tryout there earlier in the month. Veteran comedy Ghosts is also getting two spots on the schedule — on Monday and Thursday — in order to fit its full season in by spring.

Returning dramas Matlock and NCIS: Sydney and new series Einstein are also set for midseason premieres, along with a pair of new unscripted shows.

Cupertino had a Sunday trial run on Nov. 8, following 60 Minutes, and scored its largest audience of the season so far. Starting Jan. 17, it will air at 10 p.m. Sundays, following the two most watched scripted shows on broadcast TV in Marshals and Tracker. Season three of Matlock will move into Cupertino’s former Thursday spot beginning Jan. 21.

Ghosts is also set to premiere on Jan. 21, and then again on Jan. 25. CBS is making the unusual move of airing episodes on two nights of the week, partly to fit in a full, 20-plus episode season in the four months between January and May and also to anchor a relaunched Monday comedy block. The show will follow Georgie & Mandy’s First Marriage on Thursdays and pair with Eternally Yours — which comes from the same showrunners, Joe Port and Joe Wiseman — on Mondays. FBI and CIA will each move back an hour when the Monday block debuts.

Einstein, starring Matthew Gray Gubler as the renowned physicist’s great-grandson, will join CBS’ Friday lineup starting Feb. 12. It will air at 8 p.m., while Sheriff Country moves back an hour to 9. Fire Country is running a shorter season and will wrap in January. NCIS: Sydney will premiere Jan. 26, a week after the season finale of NCIS: Origins.

A game show called Stacks of Cash will premiere Jan. 6, pairing with The Price Is Right at Night to fill the gap between seasons of Survivor on Wednesday nights. Survivor begins its 52nd edition on Feb. 24 — with episodes expanded to two hours — and will be paired with a new take on Undercover Boss, hosted by Drew Carey.
