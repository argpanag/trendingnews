Nxled caps perfect elims run, sets sights on Siklab crown

TheTools · 28.11.2026 10:03 · source · permalink

Nxled put a fitting finishing touch to its remarkable elimination-round campaign, turning back ZUS Coffee, 25-21, 25-20, 20-25, 25-19, to complete an unbeaten run and seal the No. 1 seed heading into the semifinals of the Premier Volleyball League All-Filipino Conference at the Philsports Arena in Pasig City Saturday.

Already assured of the top ranking and the coveted twice-to-beat advantage in the Final Four, the Chameleons showed no signs of easing up, imposing their authority from the opening set and maintaining their grip on the match to complete their eighth consecutive victory in as many games.

Though they stumbled in the third set and failed to complete the sweep, the Chameleons quickly reasserted their dominance in the fourth, racing to a 6-1 start before cruising to a one-hour, 49-minute romp capped by MJ Phillips’ back-to-back quick attacks.

The emphatic finish left playmaker Gel Cayuna beaming with pride, knowing the team’s hard work had paid off.

“Super proud ako sa team kasi alam ko kung gaano namin pinaghirapan at pinagtrabahuhan ito,” said Cayuna, who orchestrated the Chameleons’ unbeaten elimination-round campaign with 24 excellent sets, while chipping in five points, including on her trademark 1-2 play and a rare attack.

Cayuna also pointed to the team’s veteran core as a major factor behind its remarkable run, with their experience and leadership helping the newcomers find their footing and raise their level of play.

“Malaking bagay na may mga kasama kaming may experience na. Nadadala nila kaming mga bago. Kaya patuloy lang kami sa pagtatrabaho para mas lalo pang umangat ang laro namin,” she said.

With an experienced core setting the tone and the newcomers embracing the challenge, Nxled is now firmly focused on turning its unbeaten run into a breakthrough championship after falling short in the recent PVL Invitationals.

It was another emphatic statement from a team that has steadily emerged as the tournament's most formidable contender, combining firepower, discipline and consistency in a conference where parity has made every match a potential test of survival.

The four-set triumph was Nxled's fourth in the eliminations, complementing a string of three straight-set victories that underscored its ability to find ways to win even when opponents managed to extend the contest.

Only the Farm Fresh Foxies managed to push the Chameleons to the limit, taking them to a five-set marathon after Nxled squandered a 2-0 set advantage. But the league leaders recovered their composure and prevailed in the deciding frame, displaying the resilience that has become another defining feature of their campaign.

ZUS Coffee missed out on a three-win run to close the series, settling for a 2-6 slate.

Phillips finished with 16 points, Brooke Van Sickle and EJ Cariño came through with 14 and 12 points, respectively, and Myla Pablo and Jackie Acuña combined for 14 points as Nxled dominated in attacks, 60-49.

The Chameleons also edged the Thunderbelles in blocks, 10-9.

Zam Nolasco paced ZUS Coffee with 16 points, battling Phillips in a fierce quick attack duel, while Kate Santiago scored nine markers and Jolina dela Cruz and AC Miner matched eight-point outputs.

Nxled coach Ettore Guidetti made sure his main rotation remained on the floor in the opening set against ZUS Coffee despite the team's already-secured top seeding, underscoring his desire to keep the Chameleons sharp and build further confidence ahead of the more demanding semifinal assignment.

Van Sickle, Pablo and Phillips imposed their dominance early, while Guidetti’s decision to hand Chiara Permentilla a starting spot paid off as the former Adamson standout responded with three points. Nxled outhit ZUS Coffee, 17-11, in attacks and asserted its dominance at the net with six blocks against its opponent’s two.

The Chameleons’ offensive and defensive superiority more than compensated for their seven errors, especially as the Thunderbelles managed to commit just two.

Still, Nxled’s vaunted firepower proved too much for ZUS Coffee to handle, with the Chameleons also overcoming the absence of top hitter Jonah Escamillan, who was sidelined for the semifinals due to an knee injury.

Nxled’s second six proved a tougher challenge for the Thunderbelles, as the Chameleons threatened to pull away with a 20-11 lead behind Cariño, Acuña, Leyann de Guzman and Jovelyn Fernandez.

But ZUS Coffee refused to go quietly, fighting back to close the gap to 20-23, with Laure’s wide attack adding to Nxled’s woes.

Guidetti brought Van Sickle back into the game, and the Fil-American delivered a clutch backrow attack after a brief exchange to put the Chameleons at set point. Jules Tolentino then capitalized on ZUS Coffee’s shaky reception, firing an ace to cap the set and give Nxled a 2-0 lead.

Nxled held ZUS Coffee to an equal number of attacks but made the most of the Thunderbelles’ miscues, scoring nine points off errors against just four to cruise through the second set.

ZUS Coffee, however, refused to fold. The Thunderbelles battled Nxled point for point early in the third set, taking their first lead of the match at 8-7. They stretched the advantage to 10-7 after the resumption on Zam Nolasco’s running attack, before another Nxled error pushed the margin to four.

Anchored by solid blocking, ZUS Coffee remained in control and raced to set point at 24-14. Nxled threatened a late comeback, saving six set points, the last on a Chai Troncoso attack error, as Nolasco delivered the finishing blow, unleashing a power hit to the open spot in Zone 1 to seal the hard-earned set win for the Thunderbelles.

ZUS Coffee’s seven blocks, compared to Nxled’s none, proved decisive in the frame. The Chameleons still held the edge in attacks, 13-10, but the Thunderbelles made them pay for their eight errors while committing just six of their own.
