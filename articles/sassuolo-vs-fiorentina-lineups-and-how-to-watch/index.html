Sassuolo vs Fiorentina: Lineups and how to watch

TheTools · 02.11.2026 16:48 · source · permalink
  • Match Coverage

The Viola head north in search of their first win since September.

Late news

Alberto Aquilani hasn’t sprung any major surprises with his Sassuolo. Former Viola target Kristian Thorstvedt is suspended and Ismaël Koné is suspended but the Neroverdi machine will grind on regardless. Paolo Vanoli has an injury crisis of his own with Luca Ranieri and Marin Pongračić both out, alongside the perhaps mythical Arthur Atta. He’s deployed the same XI that outplayed Atalanta on Thursday.

Lineups

Sassuolo (4-3-3): Turati; Doig, Candé, Idzes, Walukiewicz; Adzić, Matić, Sulemana; Laurienté, Esposito, Berardi

Fiorentina (4-3-3): de Gea; João Mário, Viery, Drǎgușin, Jimenez; Brescianini, Fagioli, Ndour; Njie, Pellegrino, Mastantuono
