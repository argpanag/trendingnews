Hilary Duff‘s see-through micro minidress delivered a dazzling fashion moment as the singer showed off the statement look in a new Instagram video. The colourful, star-covered design featured a barely-there silhouette, delicate straps and cascading embellishments that shimmered with every movement.

Duff posed and danced in the playful outfit, which featured multicoloured stars in pink, green, blue, and purple. However, the dress truly stole the spotlight when the lighting changed, making its star details glow under vivid blue and purple lights. The singer shared the clip with the caption, “the star dress is exactly who she thinks she is.”

Fans quickly joined the celebration in the comments, praising both the outfit and Duff’s confident energy. “This is so iconic,” one fan wrote, while another admitted, “the way I need this outfit.” A third commenter declared, “Best outfits EVER!” Meanwhile, another admirer summed up the moment perfectly: “The dress is glowing, you’re glowing, we Duffenders are glowing.”

With its playful stars, sheer finish and dramatic glow-in-the-dark effect, Duff’s look proved that a bold outfit can be both fun and unforgettable.
