ATP Hangzhou Day 4 Predictions Including Medvedev vs Royer

TheTools · 25.09.2026 18:07 · source · permalink

The Asian swing has begun this week with a couple of 250 events in China, and so far Hangzhou has lived up to expectations and produced some very nice matches. Day 4 should be no exception, with the #1 seed Daniil Medvedev making his tournament debut and looking to shake off the disappointment of his US Open loss and poor recent results in general.

ATP Hangzhou Day 4 Predictions

Quentin Halys vs Roman Safiullin

Head-to-head: Halys 1-1 Safiullin

Halys might be the much higher ranked player of the two, but the gap in level is nowhere near as large as the rankings would suggest. Despite being highly inconsistent in general, Safiullin has the potential to play very high level tennis at times and could definitely get the win here, even if it should be a very close match either way.
Prediction: Safiullin in 3

Yunchaokete Bu vs Kamil Majchrzak

Head-to-head: first meeting

Majchrzak is having the best season of his career so far, but he really cooled off after his fantastic run to a first career title in Den Bosch back in June. Meanwhile Bu recently got the best win of his career when he shockingly routined Rafael Jodar in the first round of the US Open. Playing at home, he might well continue that positive momentum with a good run here.
Prediction: Bu in 3

Chak Lam Coleman Wong vs Adolfo Daniel Vallejo

Head-to-head: first meeting

While Vallejo is much higher ranked at the moment, he has been struggling lately while Wong has been in very good form. In addition, Wong is also far more comfortable on fast hard court surfaces so all things considered he should have the edge in this match, even if it could well be a very close one decided by small details.
Prediction: Wong in 2

Daniil Medvedev vs Valentin Royer

Head-to-head: first meeting

Despite being ranked #6 (and #8 in the race), Medvedev is certainly having a disappointing season for his expectations, especially at Slam level where he couldn’t make a single deep run and getting in strong scoreboard positions in matches he lost. To make things worse, his generational rival Alexander Zverev has won two Slams; it remains to be seen whether that will light a fire under Medvedev, but last year it was in Asia that he started building momentum for a Top 10 return. He is comfortable in these conditions and has an excellent opportunity to get some strong results these coming weeks.
Prediction: Medvedev in 2
