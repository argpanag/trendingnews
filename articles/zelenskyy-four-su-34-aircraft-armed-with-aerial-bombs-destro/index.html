Zelenskyy: Four Su-34 aircraft armed with aerial bombs destroyed in Russia's Voronezh Oblast

TheTools · 15.10.2026 22:08 · source · permalink

Ukraine's defence forces have destroyed four Su-34 aircraft at a Russian airfield in Voronezh Oblast, which Russian forces had been using to carry out attacks on Ukraine.

Source: President Volodymyr Zelenskyy on X (Twitter)

Quote: "Last night, Ukraine's defence forces struck an airfield in Voronezh Oblast. Four Su-34 aircraft were destroyed and two more Su-34s were hit. The Russians used them to terrorise our people. These very warplanes were ready to launch new attacks on Ukraine and were loaded with aerial bombs.

Our long-range Neptune missile was used to strike the assets. I thank the warriors from the Ukrainian Navy for their precision!"

Details: Zelenskyy also said that strikes had been carried out on a site used to store and launch attack drones in Russia's Oryol Oblast, as well as on targets in the Black Sea.

Background:

  • Earlier, the Ukrainian Navy confirmed that six Russian Su-34M bombers had been hit by a Neptune coastal missile system on the night of 14-15 October.
  • A satellite image has shown the results of the strike on Russia's Borisoglebsk airfield.
