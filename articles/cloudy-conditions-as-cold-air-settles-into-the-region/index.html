Cloudy conditions as cold air settles into the region

TheTools · 12.11.2026 13:12 · source · permalink

BOWLING GREEN, Ky. (WBKO) -A few isolated showers are likely headed into the evening and overnight.

Temperatures are staying cool for the rest of the week. Forecast highs will stay in the 40s-50s, but could feel even colder than that. Strong winds result in temperatures feeling cooler than expected, especially with no sunlight to warm us up.

However, morning temperatures will be the real shock of the week. Overnight lows drop into the 20s for this weekend, and they will stick around for next week as well. The gusty winds will also lead to wind chills near 20 degrees, so make sure to wear multiple layers when outdoors in the early mornings or after dark.

Towards next week, high pressure returns to the region, so we’ll see sunny, cool afternoons with temperatures in the 50s.
