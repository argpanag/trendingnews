Getting to Old Trafford is the reward, but once you're there nobody remembers the runner-up. There's no nice way of losing a Grand Final.

Who will win the Rob Burrow award?

The obvious names for the Rob Burrow award are George Williams and Max Jowitt. Ben Currie will be right in the mix and don't rule out one of Wakefield's big boppers Caius Faatili or Caleb Hamlin-Uele causing some damage.

Josh Thewlis is another young man made for the big stage but I keep coming back to Jack Sinfield. Sometimes the biggest stage finds the youngest star. If I was putting a pound on anyone, my pound would be on Jack.

Playing at Old Trafford on the day his dad delivers the match ball as well... you couldn't write it. Although knowing the Sinfields, they probably could.

Who will win the Super League Grand Final 2026?

I know this will be a very closely-matched final, but I have a feeling Wakefield finish off their fairytale and become the sixth-ever Super League winner. Just two seasons ago they were in the Championship, and now they are in the biggest match of them all.

Simply put, we have never quite had a final like this before. For the first time, there is no Wigan, Leeds or St Helens.

It is a seismic moment for the sport and the latest signal that clubs, with the right backing and motivation, can get to the top.

Warrington have been waiting 71 years for a trophy. Wakefield were relegated less than five years ago. These are two sides who know that history is on the line - one of them now needs to grasp it.

Who will win the Rob Burrow award?

Sport has a funny way of doing things that are too beautiful to be believable.
