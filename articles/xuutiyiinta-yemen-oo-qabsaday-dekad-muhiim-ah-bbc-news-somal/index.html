  1. 2 Saacadood ka horXuutiyiinta oo qabsaday dekad muhiim ah

    Wakaaladda wararka ee Reuters ayaa sheegtay, iyadoo soo xiganaysa saddex sarkaal oo ka tirsan dowladda Yemen, in Xuutiyiintu ay qabsadeen dekedda Makkah.

    Xuutiyiintu waxay isku dayayaan inay la wareegaan dekedaha istiraatiijiga ah ee ku yaalla Badda Cas, tallaabadaas oo kordhin karta gacan-ku-hayntooda marin-biyoodka Bab al-Mandab, oo ah marin muhiim u ah isu-socodka maraakiibta ee Badda Cas.

    Horey, dagaallo culus oo u dhexeeyay ciidamada ay Sacuudigu taageeraan iyo Xuutiyiinta Yemen ayaa walaac ka dhaliyay in dagaalku sii ballaarto.

    Diyaaradaha Sacuudiga ayaa sidoo kale duqeeyay dhowr goobood oo ku yaalla Yemen, iyagoo ka jawaabaya weerarro lagu qaaday kaabeyaasha iyo xarumaha muhiimka ah ee dalka Sacuudiga.

    Kororka xiisadda ayaa horseeday in xaalad degdeg ah loogu dhawaaqo magaalada Khamis Mushait, oo ku taalla koonfur-galbeed Sacuudiga.

    Warar dheeraad ah:

  2. 5 Saacadood ka horSomaliland oo ka hadashay xariga wariye Xassan Gallaydh

    Booliska jamhuuriyadda gooni isu taageeda ku dhawaaqday ee Somaliland ayaa ka hadlay xarigga wariye Xasan Galleydh oo shalay la xiray.

    Waxay sheegeen in Galeydh loo haysto eedo ku saabsan faa-faainta warar been abuur ah.

    War kooban oo ka soo baxay booliska ayaa sidoo kale lagu sheegay in wariyuhu uu caddeyn u waayay wararka uu baahiyay oo boolisku been abuur ku tilmaameen.

    Intaa waxey ku dareen laamaha ammaanku in ay maxkamad hor-geyn doonaan isla markaana arrimahaas wax laga waydiin doono.

    Somaliland bilihii la soo dhaafay waxaa lagu xiray tiro wariyaal ah, iyadoo xukuumaddu ku eedeysay wariyaasha gudbinta warar aan sax aheyn.

  3. 6 Saacadood ka horWeerarkii Iiraan ee Urdun oo waxyeelleeyay 20 diyaaradood oo dagaal oo Mareykanku leeyahay

    Iiraan ayaa subaxnimadii Arbacadii weerar gantaallo ah ku qaaday saldhigga diyaaradaha ee Muwaffaq Salti ee ku yaala dalka Urdun.

    CBS News, oo ah warbaahinta Mareykanka ee ay bahwadaaga yihiin BBC-da, ayaa sheegtay in dhowr diyaaradood oo milatari oo Mareykan ah ay waxyeello ka soo gaartay weerarka.

    Wararka ayaa sheegaya in ku dhowaad siddeed diyaaradood oo dagaal oo nooca F-15 ah ay waxyeello fudud soo gaartay. Ilo wareedyo ayaa sheegay in diyaaradahaas markii la dayactiray dib loogu celiyay adeegga Ciidanka Cirka Mareykanka.

    Sidoo kale, ilo wareedyo ayaa sheegay in diyaarad nooca A-10 Thunderbolt ah uu gantaal ku dhacay, taas oo gebi ahaanba burburisay mid ka mid ah baalashii diyaaradda. A-10-ka waxaa sidoo kale si weyn loogu yaqaan magaca “Warthog.”

    Ilo sirdoon oo la hadlay CBS News ayaa sheegay in uusan jirin askari Mareykan ah oo lagu dilay weerarka. Hase yeeshee, maadaama xaaladdu xasaasi tahay, ilo-wareedyadu waxay codsadeen inaan magacyadooda la shaacin.

    Sida lagu sheegay warbixinta, ciidamada Mareykanka ee ku sugan Urdun ayaa in ka badan 30 gantaal oo Patriot ah ku riday si ay uga jawaabaan weerarka Iiraan.

    Waxaa xusid mudan in ka hor weerarkan Iiraan, Mareykanku uu sheegay inuu bartilmaameedsaday shan markab oo shidaal qaada oo laga leeyahay Iiraan.

  4. 7 Saacadood ka horDonald Trump oo shaaciyay goorta uu dagaalka Iran dhammaan karo

    Madaxweynaha Mareykanka, Donald Trump, ayaa sheegay in dagaalka socda ee Iran uu dhammaan karo marka durbaba la qabto doorashada xiliga dhexe ee Mareykanka ee bisha Nofeembar.

    Trump ayaa Khamiistii u sheegay warbaahinta in Iran aysan hadda awood u lahayn inay sii waddo dagaalka. Waxa uu sidoo kale sheegay in qiimaha shidaalku ay u badan tahay inuusan hoos ugu dhicin illaa waqtigaas.

    Trump ayaa yiri: “Waxaan u maleynayaa in dagaalku dhammaan doono wax yar kaddib doorashada, sababtoo ah iyagu oo uu ula jeedo Iiraan, muddo dheer ma sii adkeysan karaan.”

    Hadalkan Donald Trump ayaa yimid xilli uu isku diyaarinayay shirkii ugu horreeyay ee xisbiga Jamhuuriga ee doorashada dhexe, kaas oo ka dhacay Texas. Trump ayaa doonaya in shirkan uu ku mideeyo hoggaamiyeyaasha xisbiga, isla markaana uu dhiirrigeliyo taageerayaasha xisbiga ka hor doorashada dhexe ee bisha Nofeembar.

    Waxaa xusid mudan in cadaadiska Trump kaga imaanaya gudaha xisbigiisa Jamhuuriga uu sii kordhayo, gaar ahaan arrinta dagaalka Iran.

    Sidoo kale, hadal heyntiisa ayaa hoos ugu dhacaya, sida ay muujinayaan sahanno kala duwan, iyadoo dagaalka iyo saameyntiisa dhaqaale ay sii socdaan.

  5. 8 Saacadood ka horWaxaan ku dhow nahay inaanmeesha ka saarno maamulka Iran — Netanyahu

    Raysal Wasaaraha Israa’iil, Benjamin Netanyahu, ayaa sheegay in “ay ku dhow yihiin inay ridaan maamulka Iran”, xilli uu booqanayay gobolka Buurta Jabal al-Sheikh ee Suuriya oo ay Israa’iil gacanta ku hayso.

    Kadib markii ay dhacday xukuumaddii Bashar al-Assad, Israa’iil waxay la wareegtay silsiladda buuraha Jabal al-Sheikh ee Suuriya.

    “Weli waxaa jira shaqo la qabanayo; ujeeddada ugu dambaysa waa in meesha laga saaro maamulka Iran ee taageera argagixisada. Waxaan ku dhow nahay inaan gaarno hadafkaas. Waxaan ognahay in ugu dambayn isbahaysigan oo dhan uu burburi doono,” ayuu yiri Netanyahu, isagoo sidaas ku sheegay muuqaal uu kasoo duubay gobolka.

  6. 9 Saacadood ka horTrump oo ballanqaaday $5,000 haddii Jamhuurigu ku guuleysto labada aqal ee Congress-ka

    Madaxweyne Donald Trump ayaa sheegay inuu $5,000 siin doono qof kasta oo qaangaar ah oo muwaadin Maraykan ah, haddii xisbiga Jamhuuriga uu ku guuleysto labada aqal ee Congress-ka doorashooyinka dhexe ee bisha November.

    Lama oga halka ay lacagtaasi ka iman doonto iyo in bixinta lacagta noocaas ah ay sharci ahaan suurtagal tahay.

    Trump ayaa hadalkan ka sheegay shirkii ugu horreeyay ee xisbiga Jamhuurigu qabto oo loogu diyaar-garoobayo doorashooyinka dhexe, isaga oo taageerayaashiisa ku boorrinayay inay si xooggan uga qayb qaataan doorashooyinka muhiimka ah ee soo socda.

    Inkasta oo heerka taageerada shakhsiyeed ee Trump uu hoos u dhacayo, madaxweynaha ayaa dadka shirka ka qaybgalay ugu baaqay inay doorashooyinka dhexe u codeeyaan “sida isaga laftiisa uu codbixinta ugu tartamayo.”

  7. 9 Saacadood ka horKhubaro: Qabsashada gujis Maraykan ah waxay Iran siin kartaa fursad ay ku barato farsamadiisa

    Reuters ayaa Arbacadii, 9-kii Sebtembar, soo xigatay khubaro milatari oo sheegay in haddii Iran ay dhab ahaan gacanta ku dhigto gujis militari oo Maraykan ah oo iskiis u shaqeeya, ay fursad u heli karto inay si faahfaahsan u baarto qalabkaas, xitaa ay isku daydo inay dib u farsamayso si loogu isticmaalo barnaamijyadeeda milatari.

    Khubaradu waxay arrintan la barbar dhigeen wixii dhacay 2011, markaas oo Iran ay gacanta ku dhigtay diyaaraddii sirdoonka ee Maraykanka ee RQ-170, kaddibna ay sheegtay inay dib u farsamaysay oo ay samaysay noocyo gudaha ah oo ku salaysan naqshaddeeda.

    Khubaradu waxay sidoo kale aaminsan yihiin in dhacdadani ay tahay dib-u-dhac ku yimid Pentagon-ka iyo shirkadda Endurance ee samaysay qalabkan.

    Gujiska loo yaqaan Dive-LD waa gaari biyaha hoostooda ku shaqeeya oo iskiis u socda, waxaana loogu talagalay hawlo ay ka mid yihiin sahan masaafo dheer, khariidaynta gunta badda, la dagaallanka miinooyinka badda iyo kormeerka kaabayaasha biyaha hoostooda.

    Waa jiil cusub oo ka mid ah nidaamyada badda ee aan naaquuda lahayn, kuwaas oo shaqayn kara dhowr maalmood iyaga oo aan faragelin bini’aadan u baahnayn.

    Reuters waxay sheegtay in Maraykanka iyo shirkadda samaysay gujiskan ay isku dayeen inay yareeyaan muhiimadda ay leedahay sheegashada ah in Iran ay gacanta ku dhigtay.

    Hase yeeshee, Maraykanku hore ayuu si dhab ah uga walaacay markii qalabkiisa milatari ee casriga ah uu ku dhacay gacanta Iran.

    Sannadkii 2011, kaddib markii Iran ay qabatay diyaaradda sirdoonka qarsoon ee RQ-170 Sentinel, madaxweynihii xilligaas ee Maraykanka, Barack Obama, ayaa Tehran ka dalbaday inay soo celiso diyaaradda.

    Iran ayaa markii dambe sheegtay inay dib u farsamaysay diyaaradda, isla markaana ay samaysay noocyo gudaha ah oo ku salaysan naqshadeeda.

    CBS News ayaa sheegtay, iyadoo soo xiganaysa ilo si toos ah xog uga haya dhacdada, in dhowr diyaaradood oo milatari oo Maraykan ah ay waxyeello ka soo gaartay gantaallo ay Iran ku weerartay saldhigga cirka ee Mowaffaq Salti ee Jordan, aroornimadii hore ee Arbacada, waqtiga maxalliga ah.

    Ilo-wareedyadan oo aan magacyadooda la shaacin sababo la xiriira xasaasiyadda arrintan ayaa sheegay in ku dhowaad siddeed diyaaradood oo dagaal oo F-15 ah ay soo gaartay waxyeello fudud, balse ay dib ugu soo laabteen hawlgallada kaddib markii la dayactiray.

    Ilo-wareedyadu waxay sidoo kale sheegeen in diyaarad weerar oo nooca A-10 Thunderbolt, oo loo yaqaan “Warthog”, ay mid ka mid ah baalasheeda wayday kaddib markii uu gantaal ku dhacay.

    Sida ay CBS u sheegeen ilo sirdoon oo arrintan xog u leh, wax askari Maraykan ah laguma dilin weerarrada.

    Weerarradan ayaa dhacay saacado kaddib markii Maraykanku sheegay inuu bartilmaameedsaday shan booyadood oo shidaal oo Iran leedahay, taas oo uu ku sheegay inay jawaab u ahayd weerar Iran ay ku qaaday markab dagaal oo ay leedahay ciidamada badda Maraykanka.

    Ilaalada Kacaanka Iran ayaa iyaguna sheegay inay bartilmaameedsadeen siddeed booyadood oo shidaal ah iyo laba markab dagaal oo Maraykan ah, iyo sidoo kale saldhigga cirka ee Mowaffaq Salti ee Jordan.

    Warbixintan ayaa soo baxday kaddib markii CBS ay horay u sheegtay, iyadoo soo xiganaysa saraakiil Maraykan ah, in kaydka Maraykanka ee gantaallada Patriot iyo hubka kale ee ku habsada bartilmaameedyada la hago uu si degdeg ah u sii yaraanayo, kaddib bilo dagaal ah.

Khubaradu waxay arrintan la barbar dhigeen wixii dhacay 2011, markaas oo Iran ay gacanta ku dhigtay diyaaraddii sirdoonka ee Maraykanka ee RQ-170, kaddibna ay sheegtay inay dib u farsamaysay oo ay samaysay noocyo gudaha ah oo ku salaysan naqshaddeeda.

Khubaradu waxay sidoo kale aaminsan yihiin in dhacdadani ay tahay dib-u-dhac ku yimid Pentagon-ka iyo shirkadda Endurance ee samaysay qalabkan.

Gujiska loo yaqaan Dive-LD waa gaari biyaha hoostooda ku shaqeeya oo iskiis u socda, waxaana loogu talagalay hawlo ay ka mid yihiin sahan masaafo dheer, khariidaynta gunta badda, la dagaallanka miinooyinka badda iyo kormeerka kaabayaasha biyaha hoostooda.

Waa jiil cusub oo ka mid ah nidaamyada badda ee aan naaquuda lahayn, kuwaas oo shaqayn kara dhowr maalmood iyaga oo aan faragelin bini’aadan u baahnayn.

Reuters waxay sheegtay in Maraykanka iyo shirkadda samaysay gujiskan ay isku dayeen inay yareeyaan muhiimadda ay leedahay sheegashada ah in Iran ay gacanta ku dhigtay.

Hase yeeshee, Maraykanku hore ayuu si dhab ah uga walaacay markii qalabkiisa milatari ee casriga ah uu ku dhacay gacanta Iran.

Sannadkii 2011, kaddib markii Iran ay qabatay diyaaradda sirdoonka qarsoon ee RQ-170 Sentinel, madaxweynihii xilligaas ee Maraykanka, Barack Obama, ayaa Tehran ka dalbaday inay soo celiso diyaaradda.

Iran ayaa markii dambe sheegtay inay dib u farsamaysay diyaaradda, isla markaana ay samaysay noocyo gudaha ah oo ku salaysan naqshadeeda.

10 Sebtembar 2026CBS: Weerarradii gantaallada Iran ee saldhigga ciidamada cirka Mareykanka ee Urdun ayaa burburiyay dhowr diyaaradood oo milatari

Xigashada Sawirka, AFP

CBS News ayaa sheegtay, iyadoo soo xiganaysa ilo si toos ah xog uga haya dhacdada, in dhowr diyaaradood oo milatari oo Maraykan ah ay waxyeello ka soo gaartay gantaallo ay Iran ku weerartay saldhigga cirka ee Mowaffaq Salti ee Jordan, aroornimadii hore ee Arbacada, waqtiga maxalliga ah.

Ilo-wareedyadan oo aan magacyadooda la shaacin sababo la xiriira xasaasiyadda arrintan ayaa sheegay in ku dhowaad siddeed diyaaradood oo dagaal oo F-15 ah ay soo gaartay waxyeello fudud, balse ay dib ugu soo laabteen hawlgallada kaddib markii la dayactiray.

Ilo-wareedyadu waxay sidoo kale sheegeen in diyaarad weerar oo nooca A-10 Thunderbolt, oo loo yaqaan “Warthog”, ay mid ka mid ah baalasheeda wayday kaddib markii uu gantaal ku dhacay.

Sida ay CBS u sheegeen ilo sirdoon oo arrintan xog u leh, wax askari Maraykan ah laguma dilin weerarrada.

Weerarradan ayaa dhacay saacado kaddib markii Maraykanku sheegay inuu bartilmaameedsaday shan booyadood oo shidaal oo Iran leedahay, taas oo uu ku sheegay inay jawaab u ahayd weerar Iran ay ku qaaday markab dagaal oo ay leedahay ciidamada badda Maraykanka.

Ilaalada Kacaanka Iran ayaa iyaguna sheegay inay bartilmaameedsadeen siddeed booyadood oo shidaal ah iyo laba markab dagaal oo Maraykan ah, iyo sidoo kale saldhigga cirka ee Mowaffaq Salti ee Jordan.

CBS waxay sidoo kale sheegtay in ciidamada Maraykanka ee Jordan ku sugan ay rideen in ka badan 30 gantaal oo Patriot ah si ay uga hortagaan weerarrada.

Warbixintan ayaa soo baxday kaddib markii CBS ay horay u sheegtay, iyadoo soo xiganaysa saraakiil Maraykan ah, in kaydka Maraykanka ee gantaallada Patriot iyo hubka kale ee ku habsada bartilmaameedyada la hago uu si degdeg ah u sii yaraanayo, kaddib bilo dagaal ah.

Saraakiisha Wasaaradda Difaaca Maraykanka (Pentagon) ayaan weli si rasmi ah u xaqiijin ama u beenin xogta ku saabsan waxyeellada soo gaartay diyaaradaha ku sugnaa saldhigga Mowaffaq Salti.
