James Bond Frontrunner Takes Himself Out of the Running, and for Good Reason

TheTools · 04.09.2026 16:19 · source · permalink

As Amazon MGM Studios narrows in on who will take over as the face of the James Bond franchise, one fan-favorite pick has taken himself out of the running. The Gentlemen star Theo James recently explained why he will not be the next actor to ask for his martini "shaken, not stirred."

James, who has been a strong candidate in the betting odds for the next 007 casting, shared last month that he wouldn't be the next 007 due to being "definitely too old" for the role. Now, while speaking with Extra TV to promote the second season of The Gentlemen, the actor revealed the real reason why he took himself out of consideration.

The Gentlemen's Theo James Won't Be the Next James Bond

"I think every British actor or non-British actor seems to be linked at the moment. It's great rumor circling. I think everyone, you know, it's a big part of kind of cinematic culture," James began.

He continued, "I’ve always said with a character like that, I think what you sacrifice in terms of privacy and reality of life are too large a thing to take, you know? When you have children and you have a vaguely normal life, I think it would be too complicated a world to step into."

At 41 years old, James is correct in that he is probably older than Amazon would like the next Bond actor to be. Insiders previously noted that the studio was looking for someone in their thirties to take on the role.

It has also been reported that Amazon and new Bond producers David Heyman and Amy Pascal are looking to cast someone who closely matches the description of Bond from author Ian Fleming's books. In the novels, 007 is described as a blunt instrument; lethal but extremely dull and uninteresting; and over six feet tall.

While playing Bond would be huge for any actor's career, James isn't exactly hurting for work. The actor is currently the lead star of Netflix's The Gentlemen, based on the 2019 Guy Ritchie film of the same name. A third season was ordered by the streamer last month ahead of its Season 2 premiere.

The Next James Bond Has Almost Been Found

Development on Bond 26, as it is colloquially known, began in 2025. It will be the first Bond movie to be released by Amazon MGM Studios and the first not produced by Eon Productions.

Plot details remain under wraps for Bond 26, although Villeneuve intends for the spy reboot "to honor the tradition and open the path for many new missions to come." This extends to the casting of the titular 007, who is expected to remain male, British, and, based on the current Bond frontrunners, white.

Jack O'Connell, Callum Turner, Jacob Elordi, Harris Dickinson, Paul Mescal, Jack Lowden, and Jack Barton are all reportedly on the shortlist for the role. It is unclear which of the seven actors will be part of the screen tests Amazon is planning to do early this month.

While it's still anybody's game, at least for those seven, the latest betting odds for the 007 casting do have a favorite. Turner remains the frontrunner for the role.

The One Night Only actor addressed the James Bond casting rumors back in June, remarking to The Hollywood Reporter, "I know as much as you do — really, I know as much as you do."

When pressed whether he even wanted the role, Turner reportedly said, "I’m not going to comment on that."

However, George Clooney, who directed Turner in The Boys in the Boat, believes that he is the perfect actor for the role. "I think he would be a great Bond. He’s tall and handsome and charming and British, so he’s the perfect guy to do it," Clooney remarked.

With production on Bond 26 expected to begin by 2027, an announcement as to who will be the next on-screen iteration of 007 will likely be shared before the end of the year.
