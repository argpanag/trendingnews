Gusty winds and low rain chances this weekend

TheTools · 24.10.2026 10:21 · source · permalink

Get ready for a windy weekend here on the Gulf Coast.

Your Gulf Coast Weather Most Accurate Forecast calls for wind gusts near 25 mph on Saturday and 30 mph on Sunday. These gusty winds will lead to hazardous conditions on the water. A small craft advisory is in effect through Wednesday evening for seas ranging from 6-10 ft. It would be a good idea to not take your boat out this weekend.

Temperatures should peak in the low-to-mid 80s this weekend. Morning lows will climb higher into the 60s and even low 70s, too.

Rain chances are minimal, with the best chance for rain falling on Sunday with 30% coverage.

In the tropics, no tropical cyclone activity is expected over the next seven days.
