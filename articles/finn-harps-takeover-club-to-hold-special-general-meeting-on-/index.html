Finn Harps Takeover: Club to hold Special General Meeting on October 19th - Highland Radio - Latest Donegal News and Sport

TheTools · 10.10.2026 13:32 · source · permalink
  • Sport
  • October 10, 2026

Finn Harps will hold the first Special General Meeting with regards to the proposed takeover of the club by Filipino businessman and former professional footballer Freddy Gonzalez on Monday, October 19th at Jackson’s Hotel in Ballybofey.

This is a Special General Meeting where members will be asked to consider and vote on the Special Resolution concerning the proposed acquisition of the business and assets of Finn Harps Co-operative Society Limited.

No other business will be considered.

The Special Resolution must be approved by at least 75% of the eligible members voting at the meeting.

In other news, it has emerged Freddy Gonzalez would not acquire ownership of the club’s new stadium or the proposed Centre of Excellence which the club received provisional funding for this week.

In a message to its members, the club say:

“The club and Freddy aimed to agree a transaction that included the New Stadium and The Centre of Excellence however the legal complications involved did not leave this as an option at this time and the proposed transaction now does not involve the Grant Funding and the related matched funding.

The Committee are unanimous that this proposed transaction provides a fantastic opportunity for the club to thrive without any further delay and the opportunity thereafter to work with Freddy in relation to our ambitions of New Stadium and Centre of Excellence for Finn Harps.

In this regard the Committee is strongly recommending the proposed transaction with Freddy.

The proposed acquisition does not include Freddy taking any interest in the New Stadium or the Centre of Excellence at this time but does include a commitment by Freddy and the Club to work together in respect of grants and a possible structure of how these facilities will be owned and run.

Alternatively, Freddy is committed to using his own funds towards delivering the New Stadium and Centre of Excellence”.
