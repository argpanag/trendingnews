UFC Releases Countdown To UFC 334 Featuring Harrison-Nunes

TheTools · 09.11.2026 17:00 · source · permalink

This author receives a commission through recommended links in this content. See our affiliate disclosure.

UFC released its video preview package for the Nov. 14 card at Madison Square Garden, spotlighting the two title fights headlining UFC 334.

The Countdown special covers heavyweight champion Ciryl Gane's clash with unbeaten Josh Hokit and Kayla Harrison's bantamweight title defense against former champion Amanda Nunes, MMA Mania reports. The Paramount Plus event marks the promotion's latest visit to New York City.

Gane Understands Top-Five Frustration Over Hokit Shot

Gane said ranked contenders have good reason to question why Hokit earned a championship opportunity. "If I put myself in the shoes of the guys in the top five, I wouldn't be happy," he told RMC Sport Combat, adding that he'd wonder how an unranked fighter jumped the line.

The Frenchman said the social media complaints make sense given that fighters who climbed the ladder feel they deserve their chance at gold. Still, Gane noted, understanding how the promotional business works matters as much as rankings do.
