PORTLAND, Ore. (KOIN) -- The Pacific Ocean's hurricane season has been active compared to the quiet Atlantic summer season.

Three named hurricanes are spinning out over the open waters of the Pacific Northwest. A devastating Category 1 storm impacted Hawaii earlier this summer, but now another, stronger hurricane nears the islands.

Three hurricanes spin over the Pacific Ocean on Thursday, September 3, 2026 (NOAA GOES-18 Satellite)

Major Hurricane Lowell is expected to turn sharply north Saturday, just missing the Hawaiian Islands. Heavy rain and wind are still expected as the Aloha State prepares to miss the brunt of this latest storm. Some of the northernmost islands could feel the worst of the wind and rain by late Sunday.

One driving factor in this active Pacific Hurricane season is the 'super' El Niño that's taking shape over the equatorial Pacific Ocean. This could also be influencing the relatively calm Atlantic Hurricane Season.
