Deep Purple postpone remainder of their 2026 tour until further notice

Deep Purple have postponed the rest of their 2026 European and UK shows following vocalist Ian Gillan's medical emergency this weekend.

The singer was hospitalised before the band's show at Leipzig's QI Arena on October 31, and their subsequent date at Hamburg's Sporthalle the following evening was also shelved.

While the details surrounding his emergency medical treatment have not been disclosed, Gillan has written to fans outlining his current health status and news of the postponed dates.

He writes: "Dear friends, Thank you for all the good wishes and support over the last few days... it means the world.

"I had a medical problem whilst in Leipzig which required urgent attention at the hospital.

"I was hoping that I would get the all-clear to hang on until the end of the tour, but there are very serious risks involved in me doing so.

"As a result, and on the advice of my medical team and management, we have made the decision to postpone the rest of the European/UK 2026 tour until further notice.

"I'm very sorry about any inconvenience this may cause, but it's in the best interest all round.

"Thank you for your understanding.

Cheers, IG"
