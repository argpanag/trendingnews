Two people found dead at property in Tauranga

TheTools · 11.11.2026 10:34 · source · permalink

Police have launched an investigation after two people were found dead at a property in Kairua, Tauranga this morning.

Detective Senior Sergeant Natalie Flowerdew-Brown said police were called to an address on Mangatawa Lane at around 8.30am following reports of a deceased person.

"During inquiries at the address, the body of a second person was located on the property," she said.

Police were working to establish the circumstances surrounding the deaths, but said there was not believed to be any threat to the public.

An increased police presence could be expected on Mangatawa Lane while officers continued their inquiries at the scene.
