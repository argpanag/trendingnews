Idumbo redeems, Hradecky decisive as Monaco edge PSG: player ratings

Stanis Idumbo went from struggler to match-winner as Monaco beat PSG 2-1 at the Parc des Princes on Friday. Goalkeeper Hradecky was outstanding in a backs-to-the-wall display.

According to Nice-Matin, Hradecky earned 8 after reflex stops from Doué on 12 and Kvaratskhelia on 36. He was finally beaten by Marquinhos as PSG went ahead.

OneFootball Videos

Monaco levelled through Nazinho’s header. Dier had earlier been unable to cut out Kvaratskhelia’s cross, while Sané’s pace repeatedly bailed his side out. A sliding challenge halted Hakimi.

Vanderson struggled with Kvaratskhelia and made way for TEZE on 52, who defended better and registered an assist. Idumbo had wasted plenty but finished with his left foot for 1-2, his second Monaco goal, before making way for MAWISSA on 87. Hradecky then kept out Akliouche and Dembélé late on, the latter at 90+5.

Golovin tracked Vitinha and, after passing up a simple square to Brunner on 16, fed Zakaria for the leveller and found Idumbo smartly in the build-up to the winner. Camara, three days after a collapsed Chelsea move, mixed errors with industry but never hid. Coulibaly was loose in possession yet worked for the team and was withdrawn for SOUBEIR on 73, while Brunner laboured against Pacho, his only shot wide on 45, before ABLINE replaced him on 73.
