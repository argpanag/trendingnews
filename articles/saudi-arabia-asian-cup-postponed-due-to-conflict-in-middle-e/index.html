Saudi Arabia Asian Cup postponed due to conflict in Middle East

TheTools · 03.11.2026 06:24 · source · permalink

  • Topic:Socceroos

Topic:Socceroos

The Socceroos will have to wait until December 2027 to have a crack at the Asian Cup. (Getty Images: Albert Perez)

The Socceroos will have to wait another year for the chance to win a second Asian Cup, with the 2027 tournament postponed by nearly a year.

Scheduled to be played in Saudi Arabia in January and February of 2027, the tournament will now be played from December 23, 2027 to January 21, 2028.

The new dates are pending approval from FIFA.

The decision has been forced by conflict in the Middle East, with Yemen's Iran-allied Houthis claiming responsibility for a series of attacks on Saudi airports and oil facilities.

A strike on Riyadh's international airport last month killed at least 12 people and wounded more than 300.

"The Organising Committee for AFC Asian Cup approved to reschedule the flagship national team tournament in light of the current exceptional circumstances arising from developments in the region, including substantial logistical complexities and travel disruptions," an AFC statement read.

The Saudi government has invested heavily in sport through its Public Investment Fund in recent years and the Asian Cup was pitched as an early rehearsal for hosting the World Cup in 2034.

Disruptions in travel and security concerns have affected other sporting events in Saudi Arabia, with golf's Asian Tour postponing the Saudi International indefinitely, while cycling's Urban World Championships were also cancelled.

The Six Kings Slam exhibition tennis tournament in Riyadh has been moved from October to December and FIFA postponed the FIFAe Finals 2026 video game competition in Jeddah until an unspecified future date.
