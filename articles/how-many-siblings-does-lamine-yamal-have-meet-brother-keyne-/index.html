Lamine Yamal is one of the favorites for the 2026 Ballon d'Or after helping Spain win the World Cup, and the Barcelona star will be hoping to add another major honor to his growing list of achievements. At just 19, he has already made his mark on the biggest stages in football.

But during Spain's World Cup celebrations, it was not just Yamal who captured fans' attention. His younger brother, Keyne, became a familiar face throughout the tournament, with his joyful reactions and close bond with the Barcelona star stealing hearts around the world.

Here's what to know about Keyne and the family life behind one of football's brightest young talents.

Meet Lamine Yamal's Brother, Keyne

INGLEWOOD, CALIFORNIA – JULY 10: Keyne, the little brother of Lamine Yamal #19 of Spain, is seen on the screen during the FIFA World Cup 2026 Quarter Final match between Spain and Belgium at Los Angeles Stadium on July 10, 2026 in Inglewood, California. (Photo by David Ramos/Getty Images)

Keyne is Yamal's younger half-brother through his mother, Sheila Ebana, and her current partner. Born in September 2022, the three-year-old has become an unexpected star of Spain's World Cup campaign.

Every time Spain wins, cameras seem to find Keyne. Whether he's clapping from the stands, smiling in the crowd, or racing across the pitch to throw his arms around his big brother, he has delivered some of the tournament's most heartwarming moments.

The bond between the two is impossible to miss. Despite the 16-year age difference, Yamal has said Keyne feels "like a son" to him. He has often spoken about how protective he is of his little brother and how much he enjoys every moment they spend together away from football.

Their celebrations have quickly become a fan favorite.

Meet Lamine Yamal's Sister, Baraa

Yamal also has a younger half-sister, Baraa, from his father Mounir Nasraoui's relationship with his current partner.

Unlike Keyne, Baraa has grown up away from the cameras. Her family has chosen to keep her life private, and she rarely appears in public or on social media. That decision has allowed her to enjoy a childhood far removed from the attention that now follows her famous brother around the world.

Although little is known publicly about Baraa, Yamal has always made it clear how much family means to him.
