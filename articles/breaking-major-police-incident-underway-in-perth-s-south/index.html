Breaking: Major police incident underway in Perth's south

TheTools · 14.11.2026 01:41 · source · permalink

  • Topic:Police

Topic:Police

Several police vehicles have been sent to the rural suburb of Keysbrook, south of Perth. (ABC News: Keane Bourke)

Police are responding to reports of a serious incident south of Perth. 

One person has been arrested following a major police operation in Keysbrook, a rural outer suburb about 35 kilometres north-east of Mandurah and 70km south-east of Perth. 

Members of the public had initially been advised to stay away from the area but police now say there is no ongoing threat to public safety. 

"The investigation remains ongoing. More information will be provided when available," police said in a statement. 

"Significant resources" have been deployed to the area, they said.
