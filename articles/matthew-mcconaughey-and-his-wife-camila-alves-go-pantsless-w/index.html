Matthew McConaughey and Camila Alves are promoting their tequila brand just in time for football season. The couple went half-naked in a hilarious new campaign for their liquor company, Pantalones, which was shared on the actor's Instagram feed.

McConaughey and Alves posed pantsless for the advertisement, tailgating in Western garb. Both husband and wife opted for cowboy boots and hats, while Alves layered a fringe jacket over a coral top. McConaughey, meanwhile, wore a tan jacket with a white collared shirt.

Matthew McConaughey and Camila Alves in 2025.
Credit: Getty

"Tailgates and tanlines," the Oscar winner wrote.

Last month, McConaughey and Alves enjoyed a rare date night in New York City. The couple stepped out for dinner at Balthazar, a French restaurant in SoHo.

In pictures obtained by Backgrid, McConaughey, 56, tucked a khaki button-down into a pair of black trousers, adding on a leather belt, aviator sunglasses, and heeled boots. As for Alves, she dressed down her silver pinstriped suit by wearing a black bandeau bra beneath her boxy blazer. The 43-year-old model accessorized with some colorful sneakers.

Camila Alves and Matthew McConaughey in 2025.
Credit: Arnold Jerocki/Getty Images for Jacquemus

Alves and McConaughey tied the knot in 2012 and share three children: Levi, Vida, and Livingston.

Back in January, the Oscar winner discussed why his relationship with Alves takes "maintenance."

Speaking with host Jay Shetty on his podcast "On Purpose," McConaughey explained why it's "humanly impractical" for a couple to be in the honeymoon phase forever.

Matthew McConaughey and Camila Alves at the 2024 Oscars.
Credit: Getty Images

"Honeymoon's only in the perfection stage. It's only up in the air," he said.

McConaughey also said that constantly "seeking perfection" in a relationship is "unfair."

"That's a tough nut to handle when you project that on someone," the actor shared. "Neither one of you can live up to it."
