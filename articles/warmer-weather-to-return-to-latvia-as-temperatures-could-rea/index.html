Warmer weather to return to Latvia as temperatures could reach +15°C

TheTools · 19.10.2026 05:25 · source · permalink

Summary: Latvia is set for a warmer week, with sunny spells and temperatures of up to +12°C initially, followed by more clouds, occasional rain and daytime highs reaching +15°C later in the week.

Warmer weather is expected to return to Latvia next week, with temperatures potentially reaching +15°C in some areas, according to the Latvian Environment, Geology and Meteorology Centre.

At the beginning of the new week, sunny spells will become more frequent, and no significant precipitation is expected. However, fog may develop in some areas during the night and early morning hours, reducing visibility.

On Monday, mainly light northerly winds are expected, shifting to northeasterly and easterly directions during the morning. Winds will strengthen slightly on Tuesday.

Overnight temperatures will drop to between -1°C and +4°C across most of Latvia, while coastal areas of Kurzeme will remain warmer, with temperatures ranging from +5°C to +9°C.

During the day, temperatures will rise to between +8°C and +12°C.

In the second half of the week, cloud cover will increase again, bringing more frequent precipitation.

At the same time, temperatures will rise, generally remaining above +5°C at night and reaching as high as +15°C in some areas during the day.
