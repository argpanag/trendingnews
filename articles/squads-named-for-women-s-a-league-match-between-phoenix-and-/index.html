Squads named for women's A-League match between Phoenix and Victory - Friends of Football

TheTools · 23.10.2026 11:20 · source · permalink

Squads have been named for Sunday’s women’s A-League match between the Wellington Phoenix and Melbourne Victory.

The Phoenix will be without Sabitra Bhandari, Mikaela Bangalan, Zoe Benson and Kam Pickett, who are injured, and CJ Bott, who is on maternity leave.

Striker ‘Samba’ Bhandari is reportedly making good progress in her recovery from her second Anterior Cruciate Ligament (ACL) injury.
