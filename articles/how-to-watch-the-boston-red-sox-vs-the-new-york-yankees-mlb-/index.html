How to Watch the Boston Red Sox vs. the New York Yankees MLB Wild Card Game (Even for Free)

TheTools · 29.09.2026 23:34 · source · permalink

As summer comes to a close and the air turns crisp, you know what's coming: postseason baseball. The NFL season is already underway, and if you're also a baseball lover, this next month is essential viewing. The MLB has been through a number of changes in recent years. From the universal DH and the pitch clock to video replay and an automated strike zone, baseball may look very different if you haven't been paying attention.

Regardless of rule changes, the playoffs are here, and today's Wild Card Series kicked off with the Braves beating the Phillies 5-3, and the Chicago White Sox are still facing off against the Houston Astros. Next up, we have the Boston Red Sox vs. the New York Yankees. Don't want to miss a minute of the action? Here's how you can catch the action, live.

What Time Is the Boston Red Sox vs. New York Yankees?

The Boston Red Sox vs. the New York Yankees Wild Card Series Game 1 will take place today, Tuesday, September 29 at 5 p.m. ET at Yankee Stadium in the Bronx.

How to Watch the Boston Red Sox vs. New York Yankees

Tonight's Red Sox vs. Yankees Wild Card Series game will air on NBC and Peacock. You can also watch it with a live TV or sports streaming subscription from DirecTV, Fox One, Fubo, HBO Max, Hulu, Sling, Sportsnet+, or YouTube TV.

Can You Watch the Red Sox vs. Yankees Game For Free?

If you don't want to subscribe to any of the above platforms, you may be able to sign up for a few free trials to gain access to today's game. Fox One and Hulu offer three-day trials, DirecTV and Fubo offer five days, and YouTube TV gives you 10 days for free.

2026 MLB Playoffs Game Schedule

MLB's expanded postseason means more teams on the playoff schedule. The Wild Card Series, the first round of MLB's playoffs, is best-of-three games. This round begins on September 29 and includes the three Wild Card winners for each league, plus the lowest-ranked division winner. Upon advancing to the Division Series, which starts October 3, teams will need to win three out of five games against the top two division winners.

The Championship Series extends to seven games to determine the best team in each league, then advances to a best-of-seven World Series to crown the champion. Who will make it all the way?

How to Stream the 2026 MLB Playoffs

This year's MLB postseason is stretched across multiple networks and streaming services. All Wild Card Series games for the American League and National League will be broadcast on NBC, NBCSN, and Peacock. Additional rounds will be on other networks. ALDS and ALCS games are on TBS/TruTV and HBO Max, while NLDS and NLCS games are on Fox/FS1. For the World Series, all games will be broadcast on Fox and the Fox One streaming service.

  • DirecTV: DirecTV's MySports base pack costs $45 per month for the first six months to see every game on Fox, FS1, NBC, and TBS/TruTV.
  • Fox One: Fox's own streaming app lets you watch the NLDS, NLCS, and World Series on Fox and FS1 for $20 per month.
  • Fubo: Fubo's Sports plan costs $45.99 for the first month to watch the Wild Card Series, NLDS, NLCS, and World Series on Fox, FS1, and NBC.
  • HBO Max: If you pay for the $18.49-per-month HBO Max Standard, you'll be able to watch the ALDS and ALCS games on TBS/TruTV.
  • Hulu: For $90 per month, you can watch every game on Fox, FS1, NBC, TBS, and TruTV.
  • Peacock: Instead of a live TV service, you'd need to pay $10.99 per month for Peacock Premium to see the Wild Card Series games.
  • Sling: The $60.99-per-month Sling Orange & Blue bundle will let you watch the ALDS and ALCS on TBS/TruTV and the NLDS and NLCS on FS1. Day Passes are also available for Sling Orange, allowing you to watch games on TBS for up to seven days.
  • Sportsnet+ (Canada only): The $30-per-month Standard tier lets you watch every postseason game.
  • YouTube TV: Though the standard plan for YouTube TV costs $83 per month, you can actually now pay for the cheaper $65 Sports package to see all games on Fox, FS1, NBC/NBCSN, and TBS/TruTV.

Can I Watch Postseason Games on MLB.TV?

Are you already an MLB.TV subscriber? You can watch the Division Series, Championship Series, and World Series games through MLB's own streaming service—but there's a catch. To watch on MLB.TV, you'll first need to authenticate through a participating pay TV provider. It's such a sticking point that it makes the entire question moot. If you already have a TV provider, then you wouldn't need to use a streaming service. If you don't already have an MLB.TV subscription, don't pay for one now.

How can I listen to the 2026 postseason?

You can also listen to the games via ESPN Radio.

Which Teams Are In the Playoffs?

Since MLB expanded its postseason picture in 2022, a total of 12 teams make the playoffs each year. That includes six teams per league, including the three division winners and three Wild Card teams.

  • American League Division Winners: Tampa Bay Rays (1), Cleveland Guardians (2), Houston Astros (3)
  • American League Wild Card: New York Yankees (4), Boston Red Sox (5), Chicago White Sox (6)
  • National League Division Winners: Milwaukee Brewers (1), Los Angeles Dodgers (2), Atlanta Braves (3)
  • National League Wild Card: Chicago Cubs (4), Philadelphia Phillies (5), San Diego Padres (6)
