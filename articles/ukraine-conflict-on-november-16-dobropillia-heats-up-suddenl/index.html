Ukraine conflict on November 16: Dobropillia heats up suddenly, Liman situation reverses.

TheTools · 16.11.2026 09:17 · source · permalink

(Dan Tri Newspaper) - The Russian army has regained the initiative on the Liman front and is recovering positions previously lost. Fighting in Dobropillia is intensifying as Ukrainian forces launch a fierce counterattack.

Ukrainian soldiers patrol past a ruined building after a Russian attack (Illustrative image: Skynews/Reuters).

Russia attacks UAVs, Ukraine retaliates.

According to Rybar news, while traffic restrictions remain in effect in Kyiv and attacks on supply depots continue, the Russian Armed Forces (RFAF) are targeting enemy river crossings in other areas.

Russian Geran drones attacked the Preobrazhensky Bridge in Zaporizhia (an area controlled by Kyiv), resulting in power outages and heating system failures in the city. In Dnipropetrovsk, Russian drones struck a locomotive near the station, while an oil refinery in Kremenchuk was also targeted. A series of other attacks targeted logistics facilities in Odessa.

Conversely, throughout November 15, the Ukrainian Armed Forces (AFU) launched a large-scale offensive against several areas of Russia. In Taganrog, a substation and a bridge over the Mius River – located near the city – were attacked. In Lugansk and Donetsk, UAVs targeted infrastructure, disrupting electricity and water supplies in both regions. Ukrainian attacks were also recorded targeting an oil refinery in Neftekamsk and facilities in Akhtubinsk.

A Russian oil refinery in Neftekamsk is believed to have caught fire after a Ukrainian drone strike ( Video : Exilenova+).

According to Military Summary, throughout the night of November 14-15, Russia continuously dropped glide bombs on the city of Zaporizhia – including an attack reportedly using a Tornado-S system at 3 a.m. – focusing on the Vantovyi and Preobrazhensky bridges, aiming to cut off Ukraine's supply lines across the Dnieper River. The Kremenchuk oil refinery caught fire again, while a key railway logistics facility in Zolochiv was completely destroyed.

Ukraine retaliated with 533 UAVs – attacking the Nizhnekamsk oil refinery for the second time, blowing up a missile depot believed to be for the Russian strategic air force at the Akhtubinsk air base, and completely destroying a crucial logistics bridge in the south.

The situation in Dobropillia suddenly became complicated.

The fighting in Dobropillia is intensifying, both inside the city and in the village of the same name to the south. Due to the sparse Russian defenses, Kyiv's forces are launching counterattacks to penetrate RFAF formations.

The presence of Ukrainian soldiers has been confirmed in the eastern part of the settlement, where they have come under fire from Russia's 82nd Unmanned Systems Regiment. Fierce fighting continues in the area.

It is highly likely that the AFU will not only counterattack at Dobropillia, but may soon attempt to advance towards Matiashevo, along the Vodianyi Gorge. This risks threatening Russian lines of communication in the Shevchenko area.

As the RFAF Western Task Force advances deeper into Dobropillia, the AFU will likely intensify attacks on the flanks of the Russian formation. The arrival of winter will complicate such attacks – as the foliage that provided natural cover will have shed – however, to completely neutralize this threat, Moscow's forces must first firmly consolidate control of the positions they have captured.

Rybar reports that Ukrainian soldiers have advanced into the eastern outskirts of the village of Dobropillia, although the center of the settlement remains under the control of Moscow's units. Given the current situation on the front, it can be predicted that Kyiv's forces will continue their efforts to advance towards Matiashevo in the near future.

The situation in Dobropillia is becoming complicated for the Russian army due to fierce counterattacks by Ukrainian forces (Photo: Rybar).

Russia regains the initiative in Liman.

According to the Readovka news site, the RFAF's 25th Army Group has regained the initiative on the Liman front and is recovering previously lost positions. The offensive against the city has also been restarted.

Moscow's vanguard units are now present in the southeastern suburbs of the city. According to some sources, Russian troops have appeared in the northern area. Forces that held their ground in the southeast during Ukraine's Operation Vivaldi have now rejoined friendly units. The next objective of the 25th RFAF Army Group is to link up with their comrades fighting north of the city.

Russia's success was largely due to the fact that its units – despite being completely surrounded – maintained a presence as a "thorn in the side" of the Ukrainian rear, preventing Kyiv's forces from gaining full control of the city.

Russian troops are gradually regaining the initiative in Liman, continuing their offensive on the city (Photo: Readovka).

However, the RFAF has not yet been able to accelerate the pace of the offensive. Kyiv's units, fearing the loss of positions they had worked hard to retake in early autumn, are actively putting pressure on Yampol, preventing the 25th Army from concentrating its full strength on the Liman front.

But most importantly, the situation on the front lines is far from stable in the long term. Moscow's forces have not yet shifted to a deep defensive posture. The 25th RFAF Army Group holds the initiative, while the 20th Army Group continuously applies pressure, giving the enemy no time to rest.

The Ukrainian army is forced to maintain a significant force here to avoid rapidly losing the positions it has captured. Furthermore, thanks to the operations of the 25th Army Group, the northern flank of the RFAF's 3rd Army Group near Nikolaevka is now relatively secure. The AFU is no longer able to redeploy its entire force across the Seversky Donets River to establish a bridgehead at Krivaya Luka.

AMK Mapping provides a comprehensive picture of the situation in Liman, stating that over the past few weeks, Kyiv forces have continued their counterattacks west and east of the Zherebets River, regaining 1.56 square kilometers, while Russia has carried out a series of deep penetration operations.

In the north, Ukrainian forces continue their gradual advance at Zarichne, progressively gaining control of the village despite fierce resistance from the Russian side. Leading AFU assault groups have now penetrated the center and are attempting to clear remaining settlements from multiple directions.

Additionally, several Ukrainian assault groups crossed the Zherebets River and advanced into the center of Torske, while smaller groups penetrated further east of the village. The Ukrainian infiltration groups are moving east towards the border with Lugansk province, along the eastern bank of the Zherebets River south toward Yampil, and north toward the northern Torske region.

To the south, Russia launched a series of deep penetration operations, taking advantage of the AFU's lack of defensive positions in the vast forests southeast of the city. Several small assault groups reached the northern suburbs of Dibrova and nearby summer camps, while others advanced to the eastern suburbs of Liman, near the railway line.

Russian reconnaissance and special forces units are also operating near the southern and eastern streets of Liman. Meanwhile, Kyiv forces continue their offensive in Yampil and the forests to the north, attempting to eliminate the last remaining Russian positions west of the Zherebets River in the area.

Despite fierce counterattacks by Ukraine in Liman, Russian troops continue to penetrate deep into the southern part of the city (Photo: AMK Mapping).

Russia continues its advance in the Zaporizhia direction.

At the beginning of the week, the 36th RFAF Army Group continued its breakthrough into enemy defenses along the Verkhnyaya Tersa River. After losing Mirovka and Obshchy, the Ukrainian side was unable to stabilize the situation and suffered another defeat at the hands of Vasylkovo, another settlement.

Based on the progress of the offensive, the RFAF Eastern Task Force intends to advance further into the rear of remaining Ukrainian defensive positions upstream. Meanwhile, the Kyiv command cannot redeploy units from the Tersyanka - Zalivnoye - Voskresenka - Barvinovka line to halt the Russian breakthrough, as doing so would create a vulnerability for a new attack across the river.

Ukraine's defensive line along the Verkhnyaya Tersa River is lacking essential support, partly because the 42nd Guards Motorized Infantry Division (RFAF) has drawn enemy attention to Orekhov – where the Kyiv command has even had to deploy its scarce elite units.

Russian troops expanded their control in the eastern Zaporizhia region, penetrating Ukraine's central defensive line along the Verkhnya Tersa River like a "fan" (Photo: Readovka).

Over the weekend, the 36th Guards Army Group (RFAF) continued its winning streak and launched another attack on Ukraine's defenses. The Eastern Task Force's breach of the defensive line on the western bank of the Verkhnyaya Tersa River, in the Lyubitske-Novosoloshino area, forced the AFU to react.

The 260th and 122nd AFU Territorial Defense Brigades deployed a portion of their forces to prevent Russian assault units from approaching Barvinovka. This move put Kyiv's forces at a disadvantage: defensive lines along the river were weakened, allowing the 36th RFAF Army Group to attack and capture the village of Voskresenka. Ukrainian troops are now facing the risk of being encircled in the Barvinovka and Zarnitsa areas.

Thus, the RFAF Eastern Combat Group executed a "classic" breakthrough into the enemy's defenses in a narrow area, followed by flanking attacks on the northern and southern wings.

Ukraine's defensive line along the Verkhnyaya Tersa River has essentially collapsed, although it will still take time for the RFAF to completely eliminate the remaining "debris". Further back, Kyiv's forces still have two heavily fortified areas: Volnyansky and Kamyshevakha.

The first area is more than 10km from the Verkhnyaya Tersa line and lacks a network of fortified positions, while the second area serves as cover for AFU's retreat routes from Kamyshevakha and Orekhov – which are facing a clear risk of being encircled.

According to AMK Mapping, over the past few weeks, Moscow's forces have continued to intensify offensive operations northeast of Omelnyk, aiming to consolidate a firm right flank toward Orekhov. A total of 16.82 square kilometers have come under Russian control.

After seizing the railway line running along the strategic high points west and northwest of Huliaipole, the Moscow command launched a series of large-scale attacks on Vozdvyzhivka from multiple directions.

The fighting mainly took place on the flanks, where Russian soldiers controlled a series of tree-lined areas north and south of the settlement. From there, they began their advance into the southern, eastern, and then central Vozdvyzhivka areas, gradually pushing Kyiv forces out of the residential areas.

Some Russian assault groups and reconnaissance/special forces teams even penetrated deep west of the town, reaching as far as Lyubytske and Novosoloshyne.

The Russian army capitalized on its victories on the eastern Zaporizhia front, continuously advancing along the red arrows and expanding its control (Photo: AMK Mapping).

To the south, following previous incursions, the RFAF had secured the wooded areas northeast of Verkhnya Tersa and began advancing into the village from the north and east. Other assault groups were continuing their advance westward, toward the eastern outskirts of Dolynka.

Further south, Moscow forces continued clearing the fields northwest of Zaliznychne and consolidated their positions in the eastern suburbs of Hirke, where control of the area was nearing completion. Several other assault groups were continuing to penetrate deeper westward through forested ravines, toward Babashi and Kopani.

Ukraine launches localized counterattack in Sumy.

The RFAF Northern Task Force, after achieving a significant breakthrough by crossing the Psel River near Barilovka, did not stop there. They opened a new line of attack to the west, advancing into the vast jungle surrounding the capital city of Sumy and capturing the village of Nikolskoye. This became a springboard for the Russian army to continue its advance deeper into the jungle.

Meanwhile, Ukraine had assembled a significant force for a localized counteroffensive, mobilizing all resources from the Northern Command. The objective of this operation was to regain control of the Oleshnya River line – the main defensive line protecting Sumy.

Here, the AFU had managed to build a network of field fortifications, while the dense jungle areas behind them lacked a complete defense system. Furthermore, this jungle terrain was crisscrossed with rivers, lakes, and swamps, along with a high groundwater level, making it difficult for Ukrainian soldiers to dig trenches. Mobile operations under such conditions required significantly larger troop numbers than a semi-active defense along pre-prepared lines.

Ukrainian troops are launching a localized counterattack to relieve pressure around the capital city of Sumy (Photo: Readovka).

In this situation, the Kyiv command could not allow the fighting to spill into the jungle area. However, the Russian breakthrough towards Nikolskoye disrupted the communications of the AFU units concentrated in the forests near the defensive line along the Oleshnya River.

The appearance of the RFAF Northern Task Force right behind the attacking formation forced the Kyiv command to make a choice: either immediately halt the counterattack to "extinguish the fire," or accept the risk of having their retreat route cut off by attacks from both the front and the rear.

Thus, the Russian army not only advanced into a large forested area but also launched a flanking attack on the enemy forces, who were already bogged down in the fighting. The situation for the Ukrainian units is now extremely critical.

Silence in Kharkiv

On the Kharkiv front, a lull in operations has occurred. The RFAF Northern Combat Group is concentrating troops while simultaneously using offensive fire against the enemy's front lines and tactical rear areas.

Preparations are underway for decisive actions targeting the defensive line protecting the vast rural fortified area at Velykyi Burluk.

The "Vovchansk cauldron" has been decisively dealt with, freeing up Russian forces to conduct other proactive combat operations. The situation worsened for Ukraine as the RFAF began crossing the Polnaya River at several points, attempting to quickly gain control of villages on the southern bank.

In this context, Kyiv decided not to wait for the "fire" to spread but to proactively impose its intentions on Russia. The 225th and 475th Assault Regiments of the AFU, with support from other units, attempted to penetrate the Russian defensive lines east and west of Prykolotne.

While Russia was consolidating and redeploying its forces in northeastern Kharkiv, the Ukrainian army took advantage of the fog to counterattack, causing many difficulties for the enemy (Photo: Readovka).

In the west, taking advantage of the fog, Ukrainian troops are attempting to advance into Bely Kolodez from the Pogorelye and Garbuzovka areas. In the east, they are also trying to penetrate and consolidate their positions at Seleznevo and Dolzhanka. Despite the constant depletion of manpower, this has not deterred the determination of the Kyiv command.

Clearly, Ukraine is trying to create hotspots right behind the Russian front lines, while simultaneously pushing the front line away from Prykolotne – a key link in the northern defensive perimeter of the fortified Velykyi Burluk region.

The scale of the countermeasures deployed suggests that this defense area has received reinforcements from the Kupyansk direction – where Russian troops have recently made significant advances.

However, despite the active efforts of Ukrainian infantry, the Russian side maintained the ability to penetrate the outer defensive perimeter protecting Velykyi Burluk at other locations. Specifically, over the weekend, the RFAF captured the village of Kotovka located on the southern bank of the Polnaya River.

The Rybar news site provides further details, stating that, in the direction of Velykyi Burluk, the RFAF Northern Task Force has expanded its control in the area approaching Mala Vovcha, with forward units now engaging in combat near the Khrypunove settlement.

Ukrainian troops are attempting to break the siege in northeastern Kharkiv but have not yet succeeded (Photo: Rybar).

Kyiv's forces continue their efforts to break the siege, regularly resupplying their units in the area with UAVs.
