What Benfica can reveal before Torreense v Celtic

TheTools · 22.11.2026 08:31 · source · permalink

Torreense face Benfica tonight and Celtic on Thursday. That gives Martin O’Neill a chance to watch his next European opponents against the side that beat Celtic 4–1 last month. I’d be watching the final half-hour especially closely. The last team to visit Torreense in the Europa League was still ahead when stoppage time began. It left Leiria with nothing.

Benfica offer Celtic a fresh look at Torreense

A Bola confirmed that tonight’s Portuguese Cup tie kicks off at 8.45pm at the Estádio Nacional in Oeiras. Torreense know that ground well enough. They beat Sporting there in last season’s final. On Thursday, they host Celtic in Leiria, where kick-off is at 8pm.

There’s an obvious temptation to look at tonight’s result and decide what it means for Celtic. I’d pay more attention to how Torreense play. Where do they go when Benfica press them? Do their wide players stay high enough to give them an outlet? And how quickly do they get bodies forward when they win the ball back? Those are the details that can help O’Neill prepare for Thursday.

Torreense’s late comeback should get Celtic’s attention

Região de Leiria’s report on the Ararat-Armenia game tells you why the closing stages matter. Torreense trailed 2–1 late on. Both teams had lost a player to a red card by then. In the third minute of stoppage time, left-back Javi Vázquez headed in David Bruno’s cross. Torreense kept pushing and Musa Drammeh grabbed the winner.

The detail I’d take from that is who was still getting into the box. A defender arrived to score the equaliser and Torreense still went looking for another. If Celtic are protecting a lead late on Thursday, the players tracking those runs will matter as much as whoever makes the first challenge.

What’s at stake in Torreense v Celtic?

UEFA’s Europa League results show Torreense have six points from four matches. They beat Lillestrøm and Ararat-Armenia. Celtic have three points, all from the 3–2 win over Beşiktaş. A win in Leiria would draw Celtic level with Thursday’s hosts on points.

Celtic have already given themselves a foothold in this competition. The next step is to back up the Beşiktaş result away from home. Tonight might show O’Neill where Torreense can be hurt and how they change when the game gets stretched. On Thursday, I want to see Celtic use that information early enough to take charge of the game. If they get ahead, keep making Torreense defend. We already know what can happen when you give them one last chance to come forward.

Key takeaways

  • Torreense face Benfica in Oeiras on Sunday, then host Celtic in Leiria on Thursday.
  • Torreense scored twice in stoppage time to beat Ararat-Armenia 3–2.
  • A Celtic win would put both sides on six Europa League points.
