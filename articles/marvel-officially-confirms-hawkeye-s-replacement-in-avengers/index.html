Marvel Officially Confirms Hawkeye’s Replacement in ‘Avengers: Doomsday’

TheTools · 23.11.2026 01:20 · source · permalink

Jeremy Renner is turning into the hokey-cokey man of the Marvel Cinematic Universe. It seems like one movie in, one movie out, in, out, shake it all about, and shoot an arrow at someone too. As for Avengers: Doomsday, it seems like this might be an out. After the heart-wrenching chaos he went through over the course of Avengers: Endgame, it seemed almost certain that one of the original Avengers would play a key part in the war to come with Doctor Doom. But no, apparently now. However, there's still the small matter of who's going to replace Clint Barton, with Renner having previously suggested he won't be appearing in the movie. Fortunately, one of Marvel's younger heroes might have dropped a big hint.

Robert Downey Jr. is returning as the villain, the Fantastic Four are joining the fight, and several familiar X-Men are getting another chance to save the world. In fact, the cast is so big that you and I are in the movie too, dear reader. But we aren't the only ones.

Hailee Steinfeld has seemingly teased her return as Kate Bishop in Avengers: Doomsday by recreating Marvel's famous cast announcement in a new Instagram video. Shame she didn't do it on the Jeremy Renner app. While promoting her upcoming animated movie Hexed, Steinfeld pans the camera toward a director's chair bearing her name, in a moment that looks remarkably similar to the chair reveal Marvel used to announce the cast of Doomsday. Remember that? When we sat for hours looking at chairs?

Steinfeld made her MCU debut in the Disney+ series Hawkeye, where Kate Bishop trained alongside Clint Barton before taking on the Hawkeye mantle herself. She later appeared in the post-credits scene of The Marvels, when Iman Vellani's Kamala Khan approached her about forming a new team of young superheroes.

Who Else Stars in 'Avengers: Doomsday'?

The confirmed Doomsday cast includes Downey as Victor von Doom, Chris Hemsworth (Thor) as Thor, Anthony Mackie (The Falcon and the Winter Soldier) as Sam Wilson, Pedro Pascal (The Last of Us) as Reed Richards, Vanessa Kirby (The Crown) as Sue Storm, Florence Pugh (Midsommar) as Yelena Belova, Sebastian Stan (The Winter Soldier) as Bucky Barnes, and Kathryn Newton (Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania) as Cassie Lang. This was just a random selection of names. We could have said anyone and you'd believe it.
