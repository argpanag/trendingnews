Where a $380,000 Balance Sits Among Couples Near Retirement

The Federal Reserve's 2022 Survey of Consumer Finances sets the average retirement account balance for households aged 65 to 74 at $609,230. The median is $200,000, so a $380,000 balance sits well above the typical household in this age group but below the average.

How $47,500 Can Be Converted With No Federal Tax in 2026

A Roth conversion counts as ordinary income. For tax year 2026, the standard deduction for married couples filing jointly is $32,200, and each spouse aged 65 or older adds $1,650.The One Big Beautiful Bill Act was signed in July 2025, and it created a senior deduction of $6,000 per person aged 65 or older for tax years 2025 to 2028. It phases out for joint filers with modified adjusted gross income above $150,000. This couple stays well below that threshold.
