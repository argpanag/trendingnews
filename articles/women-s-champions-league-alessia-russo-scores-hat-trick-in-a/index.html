  3. Postpublished at 22:34 GMT
    22:34 GMT

    FT: Arsenal 3-0 Benfica

    Arsenal

    Arsenal boss Renee Slegers speaking to Disney+: "It's been tough for us, haven't got the results and the performances until now. The north London derby was really good for us, and we said we wanted to back it up. Really proud of the players because they did that today. We created a lot of chances, a lot of shots, and Alessia's hat-trick.

    On how important Alessia Russo's first goal was: "Very important. The one chance they had was in the first half. We survived that moment, then Less scores a really good goal to go into half-time with a 1-0 lead."

    More on Russo: "She is everything. It's hard to know where to start. She is such a humble, nice human being, really good, cares for everything and everyone, does everything to the fullest. She trains, does meetings, prepares, recovers. Everything is 100% and she brings so much quality. There is so much to say about Alessia and it's a privilege for me to work with her. To have a player that's world-class in the way she plays, the way she attacks, the way she defends does everything for the team and being this person, it's amazing. The whole team is amazing, we have so many good personalities in the group. we care for each other, and Alessia leads the way and she was captaining the team today."

    On tonight's debutants: "Good, pleased with their performances."

    On what was different tonight: "There was another way of creating today, our discipline in the press and getting the opportunities going forward. That was a big one for us."

  5. 'To score a hat-trick is rally special'published at 22:24 GMT
    22:24 GMT

    FT: Arsenal 3-0 Benfica

    Arsenal

    Arsenal's hat-trick hero Alessia Russo, speaking to Uefa: "It feels really nice. I love playing in the Champions League and I love scoring goals. it was really good to get the win. To score a hat-trick is rally special.

    "We knew they were going to be tough. We were patient. It was a good night, lots of attacking football and really nice to get the win.

    We've had a tough time but on Saturday and then tonight it was about carrying on that form, building mor momentum. It's a long season, we've started not as we wanted to but it can be really exciting what this team has still got to offer.

    We've got such a good squad. Anyone can step up and perform when they're needed. The squad is massive, we're in a good place and we are enjoying our football."

    On how it felt to wear the captain's armband: "It was really nice, an honour to captain this club."

  6. 'It's incredible for Russo'published at 22:19 GMT
    22:19 GMT

    FT: Arsenal 3-0 Benfica

    Arsenal

    Arsenal's Caitlin Foord, speaking to Uefa: "We know every game how important it is especially in this comptition. We are happy to be back here. We wanted to back up our win in the league and we are happy to get the win."

    On Alessia Russo's hat-trick: "It's incredible for Less, so happy for her. she's been pushing and pushing to get goals and to get three tonight I'm not surprised. I see her do it every day in training. We've got a nice connection and I love plying with her and feeding her the ball. I'm really happy for her."

    On Benfica: "The keeper made some great saves. We knew they were going to be a tough side. We knew we had to stick into it and win every battle. We hoped that we could grind them down and get some goals and we did that in the end, so job done."
