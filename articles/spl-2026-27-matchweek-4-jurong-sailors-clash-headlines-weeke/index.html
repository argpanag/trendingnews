SPL 2026/27 Matchweek 4: Jurong-Sailors clash headlines weekend action – Singapore Premier League

TheTools · 16.10.2026 12:15 · source · permalink

Matchweek 4 of the 2026/27 Singapore Premier League (SPL) season kicks off on Friday, with teams looking to build momentum early in the season.  Tanjong Pagar United and Hougang United look to turn their form around, while league leaders Geylang International aim to extend their perfect start when they face Balestier Khalsa. Sunday’s action sees Tampines Rovers host Young Lions, while FC Jurong take on the Lion City Sailors in a clash between two title-chasing sides.

Jaguars and Cheetahs look to bounce back from defeats

Tanjong Pagar and Hougang will be looking for a response when they meet at Jurong East Stadium on Friday night. The Jaguars suffered a 4-0 home defeat to Tampines in their last outing after conceding four second-half goals, while the Cheetahs fell to a 2-1 loss against Balestier, with Huzaifah Aziz and Khairin Nadim sent off.

The Jaguars will be looking to put their heavy defeat behind them and add to the three points they have picked up so far this season. Hougang, meanwhile, are still searching for their first win of the campaign, with Akbar Nawas’ side aiming to turn their early-season fortunes around.

Hougang have won three of the last five competitive meetings between the sides, but Tanjong Pagar claimed a 2-1 home victory in their most recent encounter on the final matchweek of the 2025/26 season.

Tigers and Eagles set for another high-scoring clash

Balestier and Geylang meet at Bishan Stadium on Saturday night in a fixture that has historically produced plenty of goals. The Tigers secured their first win of the season last time out with a 2-1 victory over Hougang United, scoring twice in the opening 11 minutes before holding on against the nine-man Cheetahs. Geylang, meanwhile, came from behind to beat Jurong 3-1, with second-half goals from Sahil Suhaimi and Dejan Račić sealing their third consecutive win.

The Eagles sit top of the table with a perfect record after three matches and will be looking to make it four wins from four. Račić has been instrumental in their attack, leading the SPL scoring charts with three goals in as many games. For Balestier, Marin Kuzminski will be one to watch after scoring in consecutive matches, and the Croatian forward also assisted Jakov Katusa’s goal in their 2-1 win over Hougang.

Goals have rarely been in short supply when these sides meet, with their last five competitive encounters producing 32 goals, an average of 6.4 per game. Balestier have won four of those five meetings, including a 4-3 victory in their most recent league encounter in May.

Stags look to extend dominant run against Young Lions


The Stags will look to make it three consecutive league wins when they host Young Lions at Our Tampines Hub on Sunday night. The visitors, meanwhile, are seeking to end a three-match losing run.

The hosts have made an impressive start to their league campaign, scoring six goals without conceding in their opening two matches. By contrast, Young Lions have conceded 15 goals across their three defeats. Stags forward Hide Higashikawa will look to trouble the Young Lions defence again, having scored six times against them last season, including four goals in Tampines’ 7-1 victory in January.

The Stags have won their last five competitive meetings with Young Lions, and scored 20 goals across their three league encounters last season. They will be confident of continuing that dominance on Sunday, while Young Lions face an uphill battle as they search for their first points in this campaign.


Tampines Rovers vs Young Lions | Sunday, 18 October | 7:30pm | Our Tampines Hub | Watch on YouTube or meWATCH

Jurong and Sailors set for high-stakes clash


Jurong will look to bounce back from their first league defeat of the season as they host the Sailors at Jurong East Stadium on Sunday night in a clash between two title-chasing sides. The third-placed hosts saw their eight-match SPL winning streak come to an end at the hands of league leaders Geylang last time out, while the Sailors head into the encounter on the back of a morale-boosting 1-0 away win over Tai Po in the AFC Champions League Two.

Toa Suenga has contributed two goals and two assists so far this season for the White Swans and will be looking to add to his tally against the Sailors. Former Japan international Keisuke Honda, meanwhile, will be eager to make a bigger impact for Jurong after coming off the bench for a combined 17 minutes across the last two fixtures. For the defending champions, Matthias Phaëton will be looking to build on his stellar two-goal, two-assist performance against Young Lions in their last league outing. The Sailors will however be without marauding left-back Nur Adam Abdullah for the foreseeable future after he suffered an anterior cruciate ligament injury.

Last season’s three meetings produced a win apiece and a 3-3 draw, in which Sailors goalkeeper Ivan Sušak scored a dramatic equaliser in the eighth minute of second-half stoppage time to rescue a point. Sunday’s encounter promises to be another fiercely contested battle between two sides eager to strengthen their title credentials.
