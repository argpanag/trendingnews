Wales vs Denmark betting tips

  • Wales to win & BTTS - 6/1 Bet365
  • Koumas to score - 7/2 Bet365

Wales are back in action on Sunday when they face Denmark for the second time in less than a week, but this time in Cardiff (7:45pm, BBC Two).

Craig Bellamy’s side were hugely disappointing in the 2-0 defeat in Copenhagen, when an own goal from Ben Davies and a deflected strike from Gustav Iskasen secured the win.

But they bounced back in style on Thursday, defying the odds from betting sites as they produced an excellent performance against Norway at home to secure their first-ever Group A win in nine attempts.

The visitors, who reached the quarter-finals of the World Cup in the summer, opened the scoring through Fulham striker Oscar Bobb, but Daniel James equalised just before the break.

Things got so much better for Wales just seconds into the second half when Torgjorn Heggem was sent off for bringing down the goalscorer just outside the box.

From the resulting free kick, Neco Williams gave Wales the lead when he fired across the goal and into the bottom corner.

While Wales were picking up their first points of this Nations League campaign, Denmark were beaten 4-2 by Portugal, who made it three wins from three after opening the campaign with a 1-0 win over Wales.

They didn’t seem to miss Cristiano Ronaldo, who controversially left the squad earlier in the week and took just 13 minutes to open the scoring through Joao Cancelo in Copenhagen.

Mikkel Damsgaard levelled things up 10 minutes later, but they were level for just two minutes before Goncalo Ramos struck.

Former Manchester United striker Rasmus Hojlund equalised again in the 53rd minute, but Portugal’s quality eventually shone through, and they secured the win thanks to goals from Vitinha and Joao Felix.

Wales vs Denmark betting preview: Wales to make it two from two

The crowd in Cardiff were almost as impressive as the players, and if both can turn up again on Sunday, then this will be a very different and difficult match for Denmark.

Football betting sites have the visitors as the favourites at odds of 10/11, while you can get 3/1 on a Wales win, which seems generous after their performance against Norway.

Just 17 places separate the two sides in the world rankings, with Bellamy’s side currently 38th while Denmark are 21st, just behind Uruguay and Scandinavian neighbours Norway.

There isn’t too much history between them with just seven meetings, but Denmark lead the head-to-head by five wins to two, while there has never been a draw.

This is the second time the two sides have been drawn together in the Nations League, and back in 2018, it was Brian Riemer’s team who came out on top twice, while the Danes also eliminated Wales from Euro 2020.

However, buoyed by their performance against Norway on Thursday, Wales look a tough proposition at home and could be celebrating their second win of the campaign if all goes right.

Koumas to be rewarded with a goal

Lewis Koumas was excellent on Thursday and offered more for his side than a certain Erling Haaland, who was largely ineffective for Norway.

He also offered more than his teammate Kieffer Moore, who started in the 2-0 defeat in Copenhagen. He was unlucky not to add to his international goal tally when only a fingertip save from Orjan Nyland prevented him from netting a third goal.

He’s 7/2 on betting apps to score anytime on Sunday, while you can also get 17/4 on him having over 1/5 shots on target.

Despite being just 21 and still waiting for his first Premier League start for Liverpool, the youngster has already scored for his country, netting in added time to earn the Dragons a 1-1 draw with Ghana back in June.
