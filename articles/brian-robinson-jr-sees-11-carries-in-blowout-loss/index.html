Brian Robinson Jr. sees 11 carries in blowout loss

TheTools · 21.09.2026 03:39 · source · permalink

by Garrett Ball | Falcons Correspondent | Sun, Sep 20th 2026, 11:39pm EDT

Brian Robinson Jr. ran 11 times for 51 yards and caught both of his targets for 16 yards in the Falcons' 34-3 loss to the Panthers on Sunday.

Fantasy Impact:

Robinson likely saw an uptick in work in this one because of the blowout, otherwise Bijan would have seen more work. Brian will be a good handcuff with potential flex upside if the game script is right, but for now should mostly stay out of lineups.
