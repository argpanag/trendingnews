Alison Hammond 'getting heart checked' after worrying This Morning moment

Alison Hammond has revealed she’s in hospital having her heart checked after worrying This Morning viewers when she was rushed off air today.

The beloved presenter, 51, was hosting alongside Dermot O’Leary, 53, when the incident occurred, with audiences at home saying it was ‘really worrying to watch’.

At the time, Dr Scott Miller was leading a phone-in, offering advice to a dog owner.

While the camera was focused on the TV vet, sitting on the sofa, Dermot came into frame midway through as he escorted Alison off the set.

The Great British Bake Off host could be heard panting and breathing heavily as she clutched her chest.

Dr Scott continued his segment but was visibly concerned, with a loud retch heard off-camera.

Taking to her Instagram Story later this afternoon, she filmed herself wearing a hospital gown – but still with flawless makeup! – to reassure her followers.

‘Hello, my lovelies,’ she began in her famous cheerful tone. ‘I know you’re all worried about me – honestly, I am fine, first of all.

‘I’m getting my old ticker checked out! I had a little bit of a palpitation, and now I’m getting it all checked out.’

‘But I think I’m OK. I’m hoping it was just a little panic attack or something like that,’ she added, crossing her fingers.

‘I love you all,’ she said sweetly. ‘Thank you for all your lovely messages. I’m so sorry I left you! I would never normally do that. I would never leave a TV show, so you know I wasn’t very well.

‘But hopefully I’m gonna be alright. The doctors are so lovely, and they’re checking me out.’

She signed off with a sassy quip, stating ‘everything is fine; I’m rocking it!’

‘Look how good I look! I’ve still got my makeup on from this morning! Lush!’

She also wrote jokingly on the screen: ‘Anything to have a day off’.

After Alison became unwell, Dermot returned to the sofa and provided an update and confirmed she was ‘unwell’.

Following an ad break, the former X Factor host said: ‘Welcome back to This Morning.

‘Alison is feeling a little unwell, so she’s just had to duck out. Hopefully she’ll be back in a sec.’

When Alison didn’t return, he told viewers: ‘Ali’s feeling a little unwell, so I’ll be here for the rest of the show.’

Alison had been on-screen for just over an hour when she fell ill, with Dermot steering the ship for the remainder of the show, helming the cooking segment and interviewing N-Dubz.

A source then told the Daily Mail: ‘She’s gone to hospital, but we think she’s OK. She walked into the ambulance after leaving the studio.’

Taking to X, fans sent Alison well-wishes, with @Lisalisey87 writing: ‘I hope we get an update regarding Alison soon as that was really worrying to watch. Hopefully she’s okay’.

@Momrocks50 added: ‘Hope @AlisonHammond Is ok clutching chest and gasping for breath. 🥹 Thinking of you Alison sending 🙏’

‘I hope Alison is okay. It sounded like she was either throwing up or having a panic attack. Hope she gets well soon 💗’, tweeted @_xpatss.

@MrGerryMcDowell echoed: ‘I am very worried about Alison, i hope she is ok, she’s a great presenter, i love her’.

Since rising to fame on Big Brother in 2002, Alison has become one of the nation’s most popular television stars.

She joined the This Morning team shortly after her reality debut, appearing as a roving entertainment reporter.

Then, in 2021, she became a main co-presenter on Fridays, during school holidays and bank holidays, alongside Dermot.

In recent years, her career has soared to new heights, as she and Noel Fielding became the new GBBO hosts on Channel 4 in 2023.

The following year, she succeeded the late Paul O’Grady, taking over ITV’s For the Love of Dogs and melting hearts at Battersea Dogs & Cats Home.

She’s also made cameos on the likes of Ant and Dec’s Saturday Night Takeaway, RuPaul’s Drag Race UK, and The Masked Singer: I’m A Celebrity Special, and hosted the backstage interviews at the 2023 film Baftas.

The meme queen has also been taken to the nation’s hearts thanks to her openness regarding her personal life.

For the past three years, she’s been dating Russian model and massage therapist David Putman, with a 22-year age gap between them.

They live together just outside of Birmingham, and while Alison initially only gave brief comments on how the relationship was going ‘really, really well’, she recently lifted the lid further.

Speaking to the Daily Mail, she said: ‘I’ve always had issues with my boyfriends; this one I don’t. We get on really well. We love each other.

‘I never have to worry and second-guess how he feels about me. He’s wonderful.’

She added that they’ve had ‘lovely holidays’ involving ‘a lot’ of kissing, quipping that his ‘healing hands’ make for great massages.

Admitting that marriage was ‘archaic’ to her, as she doesn’t want to ‘belong to anyone’, she said she ‘might get engaged’ in the future.

Alison previously defended their age difference, with sparks flying immediately when they met after Alison booked a massage with him.
