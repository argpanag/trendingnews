Field of 11 set for Doncaster St Leger

TheTools · 10.09.2026 15:55 · source · permalink

Getty

All major players stood their ground for the final British Classic of the season, with Joseph O'Brien's Enceladus and Billy Loughnane looking set to head the market for the Betfred St Leger at Doncaster on Saturday.

O'Brien’s impressive Gordon Stakes winner has been well supported in the build up and looks likely to go off favourite ahead of Epsom Derby winner Christmas Day.

Loughnane rides the colt in the absence of regular rider Dylan Browne McMonagle after the latter’s fall at Bellewstown ruled him out, with trainer O’Brien reaching for cheekpieces for the colt for the first time.

Four of the 11-strong field are trained at Ballydoyle by Aidan O’Brien with Christmas Day, Pierre Bonnard, Action and Cheshire Oaks winner Amelia Earhart completing the quartet.

As well as Enceladus the younger O’Brien saddles lively outsider Highwayman, set to be ridden by Saffie Osborne.

O’Brien, who won the Leger with Galileo Chrome in 2020, said: "Enceladus has been progressing well and, while this represents another step up in class, we’re hopeful he can run a good race.

"Highwayman has improved plenty over his last few starts. This is a big step up for him, but hopefully he can continue that progression and be competitive at this level."

A home team hoping to keep the prize in Britain is led by Andrew Balding who runs two in Galiyan, fifth in the Great Voltigeur last time and another in first-time cheekpieces, and Hatteen, who steps up from a Goodwood handicap to the top level.

Faleh Bugenaim has picked up the ride on the Al Shaqab Racing runner Hatteen for what will be his first ride in a Classic.

Richard Hannon is represented by Derby fifth Alderman and Faye Bramley will pitch Rebel Rocker, sixth at Epsom, at a Group One again, while Karl Burke’s Golden Story completes the line up.
