Iowa City School board expected to deny $14 million Coralville pool deal | Cedar News

TheTools · 22.10.2026 02:13 · source · permalink

ICCSD would have had to pay the City of Coralville $1.1 million per year for 19 years for the pool lease agreement. They’ll soon face the possibility of school closures and layoffs as they attempt to make nearly $14 million in cuts for fiscal year 2028.

The move comes as the district grapples with a looming financial crisis.
