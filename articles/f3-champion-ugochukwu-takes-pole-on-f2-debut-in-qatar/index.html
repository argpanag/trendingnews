F3 champion Ugochukwu takes pole on F2 debut in Qatar

TheTools · 27.11.2026 18:03 · source · permalink

Formula 3 champion Ugo Ugochukwu impressed on his Formula 2 debut in Qatar, taking pole position at the first time of asking.

Ugo Ugochukwu put in a stunning performance to take pole position on his Formula 2 debut in Qatar.

The Van Amersfoort Racing driver, who won the F3 title earlier this season, made an early appearance in F2 ahead of a full-time drive in the feeder series next year.

The American made the perfect start after posting a 1m 35.818s in the final minutes during Friday’s session, which was enough to beat MP Motorsport’s Gabriele Mini by 0.044s to top spot.

Mini’s title rival Alexander Dunne was third for Rodin Motorsport, with the second MP driver Oliver Goethe in fourth.

Noel Leon finished fifth ahead of TRIDENT’s John Bennett and his Campos Racing team mate Nikola Tsolov, who sits in prime position for the title with Rafael Camara’s promotion to F1.

Invicta Racing duo Joshua Duerksen and Freddie Slater – who is replacing Camara for the final two rounds – followed in P8 and P9.

Audi-backed Slater, who finished runner-up in the F3 standings, had been on provisional pole ahead of the final laps in Qualifying and is set to start on the front row in Saturday’s Sprint Race.

Rodin’s Martinius Stenshorne, who qualified in P10, will start from pole position.

Lights out for Saturday’s Sprint Race – which is set to be the 250th Formula 2 race – is scheduled for 1915 local time.
