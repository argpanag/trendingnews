Suwon is very hot this season.

Suwon KT won 88-79 in the first round of the 2026-27 KCC professional basketball home game against Goyang Sono at KT Sonicboom Arena in Suwon on the 9th, winning two consecutive games.

After losing the first game against Samsung, KT definitely rebounded by catching strong teams such as LG and Sono in a row. In particular, he boasted a solid balance of offense and defense, which made him look forward to this season.

KT led ace Paris Bath to victory with 30 points, 9 rebounds, and 4 assists.

Miguel Okzon (21 points, 3 rebounds, 5 assists) and Moon Jung-hyun (12 points, 5 rebounds, 2 steals) also had great presence. The two players played their part in offense and defense in the fourth quarter, when Sono's chase was hot, leading KT to its second consecutive victory.

Trey Porter (9 points, 8 rebounds, 2 assists) was also powerful on the day. And Han Hee-won (6 points and 2 steals) also did his part following the game against LG.

What made the victory even more meaningful was that KT won despite Bath's lack of performance in the fourth quarter. Okzon hit a three-point goal at an important moment, breaking Sono's willingness to chase, and Moon Jung-hyun brought victory with a solid defense, rebound, and decisive 3-point shooting.

Sono struggled hard with Janie O'Brient scoring 26 points, 15 rebounds and four steals, but the presence of domestic players was disappointing. Lee Jung-hyun seemed to fire in the fourth quarter, but he was sluggish with 12 points and two assists.

Scottie James didn't have a presence on this day either. 11 points, 4 rebounds, 3 assists, and poor performance.

Kevin Kembao is in a bad situation with 11 points and 4 assists, also sluggish and injured.

Sono lost to KT following DB and fell into a swamp for two consecutive losses after winning the opening game.

[MIN JUN KU, MK Sports Reporter]
