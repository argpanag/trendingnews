SINGAPORE: Hazy conditions continued across Singapore on Friday (Oct 9), with air quality in the unhealthy range across the island.

The 24-hour Pollutant Standards Index (PSI) rose steadily through the morning, with the central region recording the highest reading of this year's haze season so far.

Hourly PM2.5 levels also remained elevated, with some regions moving in and out of the high band after conditions worsened overnight.

The haze is caused by fires in Indonesia’s forests and peatlands, many of which are deliberately set to clear vegetation for farming and plantations.

Malaysia has also been affected, with schools in some areas closed on Friday.
