Kentucky vs. Northern Arizona game discussion and pregame reading

TheTools · 13.11.2026 22:17 · source · permalink
  • Kentucky Wildcats discussion

Time to get back in the win column.

The Kentucky Wildcats are set to take on Northern Arizona tonight at Rupp Arena. Game time is set for 7:00 PM ET on SEC Network+, so this will be another stream-only game.

There’s no sugarcoating how bad Tuesday’s performance was vs. Kansas in the Champions Classic. That was a painful beatdown to take, but now it’s time for Mark Pope and his Cats to get up off the mat and start a winning streak.

That begins tonight with the 0-3 Lumberjacks out of the Big Sky Conference. Northern Arizona enters this game ranked 299th in KenPom, with their three defeats coming by an average of 24 points to the likes of UNLV, New Mexico, and Arizona.

Time for a get-right game.
