Massachusetts Lottery Reports October 17 Powerball Results

TheTools · 18.10.2026 14:42 · source · permalink

The Massachusetts Lottery released the winning numbers for its October 17, 2026, Powerball drawing and The Numbers Game midday and evening draws. The published results provide players in Massachusetts with the numbers drawn for each listed game, while the lottery's regular schedule outlines when its daily and weekly drawings take place.

According to the results published by Yahoo, the October 17 Powerball numbers were 12, 13, 24, 32 and 35. The Powerball number was 11, and the Power Play multiplier was 3.

The Numbers Game also had two draws on October 17, with separate midday and evening results. The published midday numbers were 6, 8, 3 and 8, while the evening numbers were 1, 0, 5 and 3.

The source listed MassCash Evening, Megabucks and Millionaire for Life among the October 17 results categories but did not provide winning numbers for those games in the published text.

According to the Massachusetts Lottery's published drawing schedule, Powerball drawings take place at 10:59 p.m. Eastern Time on Mondays, Wednesdays and Saturdays. Mega Millions drawings are scheduled for 11 p.m. on Tuesdays and Fridays.

Lucky For Life drawings take place daily at 10:38 p.m. Eastern Time, while The Numbers Game has two daily drawings at 2 p.m. and 9 p.m. MassCash drawings are held daily at 9 p.m.

Megabucks drawings are scheduled for 9 p.m. Eastern Time on Mondays, Wednesdays and Saturdays. Millionaire for Life drawings take place daily at 11:15 p.m., according to the schedule included in the report.

Yahoo identified the results page as automatically generated using information from TinBu and a template written and reviewed by a Massachusetts editor.
