I'm a Celeb bosses 'left scrambling' as first trial ends in chaos & cast pulled

TheTools · 13.11.2026 15:22 · source · permalink

IT IS ITV’s biggest show of the year, but I’m A Celebrity… Get Me Out Of Here! bosses were left scrambling just hours into filming.

My jungle mole Down Under tells me bosses of their blockbuster first trial were forced to halt filming for the day after tech issues brought their elaborate plans to a standstill.

Production had devised an elaborate trial which saw celebs atop a speeding lorry in a daredevil mission to win stars and make it to camp.

However, while plans initially went off without a hitch, things suddenly went south after the beefed-up cargo truck broke down.

My insider says producers initially hoped the issue was a simple engine failure, but it quickly became clear it was more complex.

Celebs and staff were left twiddling their thumbs before being dramatically sent back to their plush accommodation for the night.

Filming then picked up the following morning.

A source said: “Production had been working on the first trial for months.

“After axing their iconic Walk the Plank task last year, bosses have constantly been thinking up amazing trials.

“As it’s the first task, they went all out for this new truck challenge, so it’s pretty awkward they had to stop filming.

“Also, it can’t be helped, but the fact celebs got to go back to their plush beds and take on the task in the morning kind of ruins the point of the show.”

Meanwhile, a second source added: “There was a technical issue on the shoot, so production decided to wrap early and resume filming the following day.

“This was to avoid having any celebs or crew waiting around in the hot sunshine.”

It’s not clear exactly which celebs were in line to do the challenge, but this year’s line-up of campmates is the best in years.

Wayne Rooney, AJ Odudu, Claire Sweeney, Chloe Burrows and Ricky Wilson are just some of the famous faces taking part in the show.

Back in August, I broke the news that ITV was forced to scrap its usual 9pm Sunday night launch show after a nightmare schedule clash.

They had hoped to kick off on Sunday, November 15, but England are set to play Spain in the UEFA Nations League.

It will now kick off on Monday, November 16, at 9pm.
