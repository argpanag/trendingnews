Anthony Gordon enjoys 'very special' Baku again to lead Barcelona rout

TheTools · 26.11.2026 04:52 · source · permalink

Baku brought the best out of Anthony Gordon once again as the England forward netted twice in Barcelona's 4-1 Champions League victory over Sabah FK.

Gordon, 25, scored four times for Newcastle United in the Azerbaijani capital in last season's victory over Qarabag in the same competition.

Returning to the city on Wednesday, he opened the scoring in the first half and added a second after the break as Barça made it four wins from five in this season's Champions League.

"It's a very special place for me," Gordon told Movistar after the game following another goal-filled night in Baku.

"I should have scored a hat trick again as well, but I missed a good chance to add another. I'm really happy and obviously enjoying my time in Baku so much.

"Of course I would have to say yes [I want to come back]. Every time I come, I [score]. I hope it's soon!"

With Lamine Yamal missing the game with an ankle problem and Raphinha left on the bench, Gordon led an attack which also featured Karim Adeyemi and Gabriel Jesus.

He responded by scoring his first brace for the club since joining in a €70 million deal in the summer, taking his goal tally for the campaign to five in addition to five assists.

Adeyemi was also on target, scoring Barça's third, with Jesus adding the fourth before Joy-Lance Mickels scored a consolation for Sabah.

Like Gordon, former Arsenal striker Jesus now has five goals for the season despite playing just 376 minutes, but he was not happy with his display in Baku.

"I hadn't played in the last few games and it's not easy [coming into the side], although the team was good," Jesus said.

"I am happy with my goal, it's always good for a striker to score, but I am not happy with my personal performance.

"I understand my situation. Raphinha is doing really well, scoring every game and is so important. I can't complain about not playing. It was good for me to score today and I want to keep getting more minutes and chances."

Barça coach Hansi Flick was also pleased to get the job done in Azerbaijan, even if he was disappointed about the manner of the goal his team conceded.

"I am happy with [the win], it's normally not easy here" he said. "In the first half, I think we had 70% or more ball possession; the only thing I am not happy with is the mistakes we make, this is not how we want to play.

"I am not happy with the goal we conceded. You can't do that at this level. We have to make these things better, but three points is very important. In the second half, three goals, it's good."
