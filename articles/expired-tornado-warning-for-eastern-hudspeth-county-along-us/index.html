(EXPIRED) Tornado Warning for Eastern Hudspeth County along US 62/180; now a Severe Thunderstorm Warning for Cornudas until midnight for 60 mph winds!

TheTools · 26.10.2026 02:49 · source · permalink

The National Weather Service has expired the Tornado Warning for Eastern Hudspeth County along US 62/180.

The warning is now replaced by a Severe Thunderstorm Warning for Cornudas until midnight for 60 mile per hour winds.
