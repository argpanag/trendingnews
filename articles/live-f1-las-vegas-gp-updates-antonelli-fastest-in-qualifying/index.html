F1 Las Vegas GP: Antonelli tops qualifying but Verstappen gets pole

Kimi Antonelli was fastest in qualifying at the Las Vegas GP but pole goes to Max Verstappen due to the Italian's grid penalty

George Russell, third fastest: "I think, overall it's been a solid session and a solid day. I wanted to be pole, but I'm probably more pleased with the performance today than Mexico a couple of weeks ago. Timing the outlap and getting the slipstream was huge, pretty crazy, pretty similar to what we saw in Monza.

"Kimi did a great job, he probably it wasn't going to work out! Starting P2 tomorrow with his penalty, it's everything to play for."

Max Verstappen, second fastest: "Struggling with some things that were in the car in Brazil and I was trying to get around it to make the best of it. We executed well, my first lap in Q3 was pretty much as good as it can be. I think to be on the front row is very good for us.

"Track temp, being in the right position...it's incredibly hard when you have so many straights around here. But we executed well, and we can be happy with that."

Kimi Antonelli, fastest in qualifying: "It was a very weird qualifying, I couldn't get the tyres working, zero feeling with the car and the brake pedal was very long. But all-in-all, it was so unexpected. 

"I thought I was screwed, but the tow is so powerful here I was able to gain from it and put it on pole."

Good lap from Lindblad, who will start fifth tomorrow with Antonelli starting behind him. Lawson, Gasly, and Colapinto will all move up a place as Piastri takes a 10-place drop.
