Power outage hits Luas Green line services

TheTools · 24.09.2026 07:45 · source · permalink

There are no Luas services running through Dublin city centre due to a power fault on the line.

Services are suspended between the St Stephen's Green and Dominick Street stops.

Luas tickets are being accepted on Dublin Bus.
