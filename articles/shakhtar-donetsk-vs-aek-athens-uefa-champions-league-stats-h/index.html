    Paris St-Germain boss Luis Enrique says he was surprised to learn Manchester City had been found guilty of breaching Premier League financial rules and hopes the club can avoid sanctions.

  17. LASK Lowdownpublished at 17:08 BST
    17:08 BST

    LASK v Liverpool (17:45 BST)

    Matt Jones
    BBC Sport journalist

    LASK's first home match in the Champions League is a high-profile one, as they welcome Liverpool to Raiffeisen Arena.

    The Austrian side won a domestic double last season for only the second time in their history. They then qualified for the Champions League in dramatic fashion too, overcoming a 3-0 first-leg loss to Celtic in the play-offs to win 5-1 at home after extra time and 5-4 on aggregate.

    Key player Christoph Lang has had a red-hot start to the season, scoring 11 goals in 14 appearances.

    The forward was called up to the Austria senior squad for the first time during the most recent international break and scored on his second appearance, in a 1-1 draw against Kosovo.

    He once joked he was "unbeatable" at backgammon and has a tattoo that reads "speech is silver, silence is golden".

  18. 'No excuses' - but is Villa's stadium rebuild affecting team?published at 17:05 BST
    17:05 BST

    Aston Villa v Fenerbahce (20:00 BST)

    Nick Mashiter
    BBC Sport football news reporter

    "No excuses," said Unai Emery.

    The Aston Villa head coach was talking about his team's form amid Villa Park's rebuild.

    He had already turned the air blue when describing their defeat to Nottingham Forest last month.

    It was a rare moment of candour from the Spaniard with Villa yet to win at home this season after Saturday's 2-2 draw with Brentford.

    The North Stand is a shell and currently closed for building work as the club increases the stadium's capacity to over 50,000.

    It is part of the club's plan to help Villa bridge the financial gap in the Premier League, but they may have to endure some short-term pain for long-term gain.

    "Our handicap is less supporters for the situation, but the club decided it. It is something to improve us for the future," said the Spaniard.

    "We must try to be together and without 7,000 supporters we must push everyone. We must play better football, compete better, be more focused. Those are more important than the stand.

  20. Can Villa grab first home win?published at 16:58 BST
    16:58 BST

    Aston Villa v Fenerbahce (20:00 BST)

    Matt Jones
    BBC Sport journalist

    Aston Villa will be on the hunt for their first home win of the season when they welcome Fenerbahce to Villa Park. After a 2-2 draw with Brentford on Saturday, Villa have now only won four of their past 12 home league matches.

    Head coach Unai Emery will hope a change of competition will spark a change in fortunes for his team, although a Fenerbahce team packed with Premier League experience will be a threat.

    The Turkish giants are back in the Champions League for the first time since 2008-09 and come into the game after a 3-0 win at Rizespor in the Turkish Super Lig. After a tricky start to the season, they are now up to fourth in the Turkish top flight.

    Villa have won all three of their meetings with Fenerbahce, the most recent being a 1-0 away victory in the Europa League in January.
