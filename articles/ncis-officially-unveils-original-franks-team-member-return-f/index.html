Another member of Mike Franks' original team is coming back in the present-time NCIS.

CBS's fall 2026 lineup has no shortage of hits, but there's something very special about NCIS this year that sets it apart from its fellow crime shows. While the Donald Bellisario and Don McGill-created procedural franchise has always been a staple of the Eye Network's annual offering, the last few years haven't been as strong as its predecessors. After taking a while to figure out a new rebrand, the universe has been delivering quality storytelling every week.

NCIS: Origins season 3 arguably leads the way in this regard. Showrunner David J. North has done a great job crafting a two-timeline narrative — one set in the early '90s with Austin Stowell's Young Gibbs and the other set in the present timeline, spearheaded by Mark Harmon's Old Gibbs. However, they are not the only characters who exist in both eras, as NCIS: Origins season 3 has brought in older versions of its main cast, and they're adding a new one when it returns for its latest episode.

CBS shares the new trailer for NCIS: Origins season 3, episode 7 (via TVPromos), “Don’t Let the Days Go By,” which includes the first-look at Mary-Jo in the present timeline. The preview also reveals the reunion between Old Gibbs and Old Vera, played by Roma Maffia. Her return was previously announced, however, as part of NCIS: Origins' three-character comeback announcement, with Muse Watson and Joe Spano. Watch the video below:

Based on the trailer, it does seem like Tyla Abercrumbie is playing the older version of Mary-Jo, as well. Since the character is original to NCIS: Origins, it hadn't been played before the actor debuted in the prequel, hence why CBS can simply bring her back for her present-day appearance. For what it's worth, NCIS: Origins has already done the same with the introduction of Old Woody and Phil, which were also both played by Bobby Moynihan and Ely Henry, respectively.

While there was an official announcement of Old Mary-Jo's introduction in NCIS: Origins, "Shot Through The Heart" heavily implied that she was coming. During the final moments of Old Gibbs' conversation with Old Mike, the latter guided his probie towards reaching out to her. Described as the woman that knew him the most, Watson's line was accompanied by a close-up shot of Mary-Jo's table name-plate. Beyond that, however, NCIS: Origins hasn't shared any update about her situation in 2026.

At this point, it's safe to say that NCIS: Origins season 3 is planning to reveal what its main characters are doing in the present time. All of it is, of course, leading towards the final payoff with regard to the mystery surrounding Lala Dominguez's true fate. The genius of the NCIS prequel, however, is that it also devotes time to developing other characters in the show outside its main arc.
