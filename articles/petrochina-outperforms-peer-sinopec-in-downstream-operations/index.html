PetroChina Outperforms Peer Sinopec in Downstream Operations

TheTools · 11.09.2026 01:02 · source · permalink

PetroChina Co Ltd Class H

Business Strategy and Outlook

PetroChina is the listed arm of one of China's two integrated oil majors and the largest oil producer. With revenue and assets more heavily weighted toward upstream activities, PetroChina is more sensitive to swings in the oil price than its peer, Sinopec. In addition, the firm’s downstream operations lag Sinopec, which has better scale and efficiency in the sector. However, PetroChina will benefit more in a rising oil price environment.
