Singapore Airlines takes world’s best carrier crown from Qatar Airways

TheTools · 18.09.2026 13:26 · source · permalink

Asian carriers dominated the Skytrax rankings and only one European airline – Air France-KLM – made the top 10

The award, which is based on a survey of almost 25 million travellers, was announced in London on Friday, with a list dominated by Asian carriers. Only one European airline – Air France-KLM – made the top 10, and not a single US company, despite their efforts to upgrade their passenger experience and premium cabins.

The Skytrax recognition gives airlines bragging rights with the flying public at a time when premium travel is rising in popularity around the world.

Besides the overall ranking, Air France was also awarded the top prize for its La Premiere first class, which the French airline is expanding amid booming demand.
