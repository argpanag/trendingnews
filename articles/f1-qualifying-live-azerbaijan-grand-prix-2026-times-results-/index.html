Just wanted ti give back a little bit to the factory , all the efforts that we've put into this chassis, the small floor upgrade that we've brought.   4. 'We just couldn't compete with Mercedes' - Hamiltonpublished at 15:05 BST
    15:05 BST

    Ferrari's Lewis Hamilton to Sky Sports after qualifying sixth: "It was pretty bad. Qualifying once again. It's not for the lack of trying.

    "We just couldn't compete with the Mercedes. They're just outright so much quicker on the straights. They did a great job."

  5. 'A disaster of qualifying'published at 14:59 BST
    14:59 BST

    Max Verstappen, who qualified eighth quickest: "The whole qualifying I was lacking top speed. The engine was just not working. That was one problem and then in Q3 I was about to start my middle lap but the battery didn't charge, so you start a lap with half a battery so you have to abort and then you lose temperature because you don't have enough power basically. It then snowballs into the final lap. Again no top speed and a yellow flag in my lap. That's where you are.

    "I have no idea for tomorrow. We were in a really good place all weekend but then had a disaster of a qualifying because of a lot of issues. Let's see what we can do."

  6. 'I'd rather die trying to get a pole than settle' - Norrispublished at 14:51 BST
    14:51 BST

    McLaren's Lando Norris speaking to Sky Sprts after qualifying P5 for the Azerbaijan GP: "Qualifying was working well to be honest. Oscar is P3, I think P2 was possible. The car's not bad, we're just a long way off. Especially my car is a long way off in the straights. It's limiting because there's lots of straights here.

    "Of course I hope for a podium but with my straight line speed it will be impossible."
