 When Is Walmart Black Friday 2026?  

 This year, Walmart is going BIG for Black Friday by dropping three major sale events. The first phase of their Black Friday sale starts on Thursday, November 12th at 12:01 a.m. ET online (in stores at 6 a.m. local time) and runs through Sunday, November 15. This is considered to be their Early Black Friday event, which basically acts as a preview of what's to come. At this time, Walmart's Hot Deal Drops will begin for Walmart+ members to take advantage of before everyone else. These deals will open up to all shoppers on November 13th.

 Walmart's official Black Friday Sale starts on Sunday, November 23rd and runs through Sunday, November 29th. Like the above, Walmart+ members will have access to Hot Deal items on the first day of the sale, while everyone else can shop the remaining Hot Deals on the 24th.


 Is Walmart Having a Cyber Monday Sale in 2026?  

 Absolutely! As mentioned before, Walmart's Black Friday event is basically broken up into three parts, with the last being Cyber Monday. This year, Walmart's Cyber Monday event begins at 12:01 a.m. ET on November 30th and runs through December 2nd.
