Cher's son Elijah Allman indicted after alleged New Hampshire home break-in

(TNND) — Elijah Blue Allman, Cher’s son, has been indicted on a felony criminal mischief charge in connection with an alleged break-in at a New Hampshire home.

The indictment stems from Allman’s March 1 arrest, when police said he broke into a Windham home by smashing a glass panel in a back door, WMUR reported.

A woman inside the home called 911 while hiding in a closet, according to an affidavit obtained by the station. Officers said they found Allman sitting on a couch inside the home, smoking a cigarette.

Court records allege Allman used loppers to break the window and enter the home. Police said the damage included the broken door and a cigarette burn on a rug, though the homeowners reported that nothing appeared to be missing.

Allman has pleaded not guilty to charges including criminal mischief, burglary and breach of bail.

The grand jury indictment alleges Allman “purposely damaged the property of (the alleged victim) by breaking a glass window.” His arraignment is scheduled for October.

Before the alleged break-in, Allman was accused of posing as the parent of a prospective student at a Concord school, acting “belligerently” and allegedly poking a student with his cane.

He faces charges of simple assault, criminal trespass, criminal threatening and disorderly conduct, according to WMUR. A competency hearing is scheduled for the fall.

Cher previously sought conservatorship over Allman in 2023, later withdrew the petition, and filed again this spring after the New Hampshire arrests. A judge denied the temporary request in April.

In the filing, Cher said Allman had struggled with substance abuse and mental health issues and was receiving treatment at a locked psychiatric facility in New Hampshire while officials worked to restore his competency to stand trial. Allman had also been hospitalized in California in 2025 after authorities said he was found acting erratically.
