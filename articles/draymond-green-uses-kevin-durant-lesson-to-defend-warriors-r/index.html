Draymond Green fiercely defended Yaxel Lendeborg for going at Luka Doncic, saying the rookie should keep talking until someone shuts him up and recalling that even after Kevin Durant once dropped 54 points on his team, he never stopped talking.

Draymond Green strongly backed Yaxel Lendeborg after the Golden State Warriors rookie exchanged trash talk with Luka Doncic during a 127-105 season-opening loss to the Los Angeles Lakers on Wednesday.

Lendeborg spent much of the game challenging Doncic verbally and later criticized the Lakers star publicly, saying he had lost respect for him after their first NBA matchup.

"I think everybody is going to be like, 'Man, he shouldn't talk to Luka. You should not talk to Luka.' And I disagree with that 1,000%," Green stated post-game.

"If you're f**king going to talk to somebody, you talk until you can't f**king talk anymore. And if he busts your ass, guess what? You play him again three more times, and you go back at his ass again. Then you see him the third time, and you go back at his ass again."

Green said a player's reputation should not automatically command respect from an opponent who has never faced him.

"In this league, everybody always says, 'Certain guys you've got to respect.' I didn't come into this league like that. You earn respect."

"Everybody is like, 'The rookie learned his lesson.' What did he learn? Next time you see him, go at his ass again."

Asked whether he liked everything about Lendeborg's confrontation with Doncic – including the rookie continuing to criticize him afterward – Green doubled down.

"Yeah. If you start talking to someone, you got yourself into a battle," Green said. "I lose respect for guys when you start talking to someone, realize, 'Oh, I'm in a battle,' and then stop. You keep going. You get all my respect."

"There's not a single basketball player in this league who's done anything to earn his respect if he hasn't played against them. Where do you earn respect? Because I watched you on TV do something to someone else? No."

Green compared Lendeborg's mentality to the attitude he carried when he entered the NBA.

"As someone who walked in here with the same mindset, and 15 years later I still sit here, walk in with that same mindset. Shut me up. And if you ain't going to, guess what? I ain't shutting up. Do it again and again and again, because I ain't going to shut up."

"So, nice. I like it, and I hope he keeps talking."

Green was then asked whether he wanted Lendeborg to carry the same attitude into every matchup.

"Absolutely. You approach every game trying to be great, right? So if I'm going to approach the game and I'm going to be talking, then yeah, that's how you approach every game."

"And guess what? It's the NBA. You're going to get your ass sometimes."

He also pushed back on the idea that Doncic had completely overwhelmed the rookie.

"By the way, it wasn't the craziest Luka game of all time. We've all seen crazier Luka games. So yeah, approach it the same way, and whatever happens, happens."

Doncic finished with 28 points, eight rebounds, and seven assists in 30 minutes, while Lendeborg recorded 12 points, three rebounds, and two assists in his NBA debut.

Green has also tested Lendeborg himself during training camp and said the rookie has consistently responded when challenged.

"He responds. You go at him, he responds. You yell at him to get him to do something, he responds."

"I think he played a better second half than he did the first half, so he responded."

"Now, that's a nine-year superstar versus a rookie. You should have a better game. But talk that shit like you did. I love it."

Green then recalled an early-career experience that helped shape that mindset.

"Yeah. KD gave us 54. One thousand percent. Guess what? I kept talking, and I can guard him now."

Doncic noted that Lendeborg's trash talk brought out a different version of him and suggested the rookie's words helped fuel his performance.

"Everybody knows when somebody starts talking, you got a different me," Luka said after the game. "So that was it."

"He was talking crazy," Doncic added in his postgame press conference. "But, you know, I wasn't trying to talk back this season. I'm not trying to get techs."

"This is the first time that was not a double tech because I didn't say nothing. So I'm working on it. But as you see, I think it worked great."

Lendeborg was far more critical afterward, saying he had lost a significant amount of respect for Doncic because of what he viewed as constant complaining, foul-baiting, and conversations with officials.

The rookie also revealed that he repeatedly told Doncic he could not score on him and even told the Lakers star that he "sucks."

"I was telling him he sucks," Lendeborg said. "'You can't score on me.' Talking, 'You can't score on me.' I said it the whole game."

"I said a couple cuss words, obviously. That’s why I got the technical and all that."
