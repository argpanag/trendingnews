Owain Wyn Evans left 'shaken' after incident during Children in Need challenge

TheTools · 11.11.2026 09:57 · source · permalink

Owain Wyn Evans has opened up about his difficult Children In Need challenge during a BBC Breakfast appearance.

The morning programme resumed on Wednesday (November 11) with hosts Jon Kay and Emma Vardy once again presenting. During the long-established show, the duo were scheduled to speak with Owain Wyn Evans.

The Welsh presenter is undertaking the longest distance ever attempted in a Radio 2 fundraising endeavour for Children in Need. Commencing in Glasgow, Owain's five-day cycling challenge began on Monday (November 9).

It will involve him covering nearly 400 miles across Scotland, Northern Ireland, England and his native Wales. The expedition will culminate in a spectacular finish in central Cardiff on Friday (November 13), before Children in Need's live appeal programme.

During Wednesday's (November 11) broadcast, BBC Breakfast caught up with Owain, who was cycling through Merseyside. The situation became uncomfortable, however, when technical problems brought the BBC Breakfast conversation to an abrupt end, reports the Mirror.

"How are you feeling?" Jon enquired of Owain, who appeared live on screen from Merseyside while riding his bicycle. Owain responded: "Aw, Jon, morning both. Today is harder." Nevertheless, the audio was subsequently lost.

Emma remarked: "Oh oh," before adding: "I tell you what, the reception in Northern Ireland was better than it is today isn't it! Let's see if we can get him back."

Jon said: "We can hear you, Owain; let's try and see what he says. You say you came off the bike yesterday?" However, Owain could not be heard replying.

The programme subsequently ended the interview and returned to Jon and Emma in the studio, where Emma said: "Ah poor Owain. I hope he's alright because he said his knee has been playing up."

Jon added: "Fair play to get back on the bike and carry on. He's so focused isn't he? He and the whole team around him are doing a remarkable job!".

Later in the programme, BBC Breakfast eventually managed to speak with Owain. Appearing on screen while cycling, Owain said: "Sorry, signal is a bit ropey round here guys! Yesterday was a tough old day but we had an amazing welcome from Northern Ireland."

He went on to say: "I've got a bad knee, I fell off my bike yesterday which wasn't ideal and it just shakes you a bit. As a non-cyclist, that is a thing I was quite nervous about. But yeah I am feeling it today. Yesterday was a long day." He also shared: "I got a bit of a croak in the throat, I got a bad knee and when I fell off, I landed on my shoulder on a curb."

BBC Breakfast airs Monday to Sunday from 6am on BBC One.
