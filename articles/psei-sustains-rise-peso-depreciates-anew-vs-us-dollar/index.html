PSEi sustains rise, peso depreciates anew vs US dollar

TheTools · 22.10.2026 05:34 · source · permalink

METRO

Oct 22, 2026

1:34 PM GMT+8

RISKS. The Philippine Stock Exchange index rises anew Wednesday (Oct. 21, 2026) due to bargain-hunting and investors’ decision to keep a  wait-and-see stance ahead of Thursday’s (Oct. 22) rate setting meet of the Bangko Sentral ng Piliipinas’ Monetary Board. On the other hand, the Philippine peso weakened against the greenback. (PNA file)

MANILA – Bargain hunting sustained the Philippine Stock Exchange index’s (PSEi) rise on Wednesday, but the Philippine peso finished weaker against the U.S. dollar. 


The local bourse’s main index rose 0.26 percent to 5,821.51 points, and the broader All Shares by 0.14 percent to 3,238.61 points. 


Half of the sectoral gauges trailed the main index, namely Services, 1.53 percent; Property, 0.93 percent; and Mining and Oil, 0.43 percent.


On the other hand, Financials dipped by 0.61 percent, Industrial by 0.57 percent and Holding Firms by 0.39 percent.


Volume reached 986.59 million shares amounting to PHP7.05 billion. 


Advancers led decliners at 92 to 83, while 73 shares were unchanged. 


“The local index ended higher as investors continued bargain hunting. However, optimism remained capped amid cautious sentiment,” Luis Limlingan, Regina Capital Development Corporation head of sales, said. 


He said investors were on a wait-and-see stance a day ahead of the rate setting meet of the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas’ (BSP) policy-making Monetary Board (MB) on Thursday.


The MB is forecast to hike the central bank’s key rates, given the acceleration of domestic interest rate. 


Meanwhile, the local currency ended the day at 63.36  to a greenback, a depreciation from its 63.09 close a day ago.


It opened the trade at 63.01, and ranged between 63.37 and 63.00. Average level stood at 63.22. 


Volume amounted to USD1.34 billion, a tad lower than the USD1.36 billion in the previous session. (PNA Joann Villanueva)
