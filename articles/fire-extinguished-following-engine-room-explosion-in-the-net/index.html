Fire extinguished following engine-room explosion in the Netherlands

TheTools · 07.10.2026 09:12 · source · permalink

An explosion and fire in the engine room of the vessel Condor prompted a response from the Dutch Coast Guard and the Royal Netherlands Sea Rescue Institution (KNRM) off the Westland coast on Tuesday evening, October 6.

The incident was reported at around 17:58 local time while the vessel was offshore from Ter Heijde, with nine people on board. KNRM stations in Scheveningen and Ter Heijde were alerted, along with the Coast Guard Coordination Centre in Den Helder and the Haaglanden emergency control room.

Two fire extinguishers were used to tackle the fire, after which the Condor was able to continue operating on one engine.

Seven people transferred to the vessel George Dijkstra and were taken ashore at Scheveningen, while KNRM personnel boarded the Condor to assist.

No injuries were reported. The Condor was subsequently escorted to Scheveningen harbor by the Kitty Rosmale.
