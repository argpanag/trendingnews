Barça: João Cancelo's missile under the bar against Santander

TheTools · 16.09.2026 20:16 · source · permalink

Barça currently leads 1-0 against Racing Santander on the 6th day of La Liga. The Catalans quickly took control of the match thanks to João Cancelo, who opened the scoring in the 7th minute of play.

And the Portuguese did not do it by half measures! Cancelo sent a veritable missile to clear Santander’s top corner and give Barça an early advantage. A golazo that perfectly kicks off the Catalan evening!
