Katy Perry is bringing back one of her most recognizable hairstyles, but not everyone is loving the singer's latest beauty look.

The "Teenage Dream" singer stepped out in New York City with her signature micro bangs on full display, prompting some fans to share their unfiltered opinions about the dramatic fringe. Though this isn't the first time she's sported bangs, especially short ones, this time around, her haircut did not receive the same positive feedback and even sparked some online backlash. Read more about it below.

Katy Perry Debuts New Hairstyle While Promoting Her Concert Film In NYC

Perry turned heads in New York City while promoting her concert film, Katy Perry: The Lifetimes Tour — Live from Paris. The 41-year-old singer stepped out in a coral Khaite micro minidress featuring a draped cowl neckline, which she paired with neutral heels, sunglasses, and a white clutch. She completed the look with a sleek updo that put her hairstyle front and center.

While Perry has experimented with different hairstyles throughout her career, her latest look brought back a controversial style that fans have debated before. The singer's dark hair was swept up into a polished bun, leaving her short, blunt micro bangs to frame her face.

The fringe sat high above her eyebrows, revealing most of her forehead and giving her otherwise sleek hairstyle a bold, retro-inspired finish.

Fans Slam Katy Perry's Controversial Micro Bangs On Social Media

Although the vocalist's outfit made a statement during her NYC appearance, her bangs quickly became a major talking point online.

Katy has worn the ultra-short fringe before, but its return left some social media users less than impressed, with fans questioning the dramatic style and sharing their brutally honest reactions to the transformation.

"Here we are… KCs vs the MICROBANGS!" commented an Instagram user. "I thought we'd watched THAT burn!"

"The microbangs are back," responded another. "Oh lord, help."

"These bangs," began a fan. "Mom really loves them."

Referring to her microbangs during her 143 album era in 2024, one fan commented, "The bangs can stay in that era for a lifetime."
