Donald Trump threatens to ban popular hobby with his 'crazy power'

TheTools · 19.09.2026 14:50 · source · permalink

Trump said he has 'crazy power'(Image: Samuel Corum - Pool via CNP/Shutterstock)

President Donald Trump has threatened to ban a popular pastime - just because he can. The US leader bragged he is "so powerful" that he can do what he wants, saying he can ban fishing if it takes his fancy. He joked that the "power" of his office is "crazy" - boasting to pals inside the White House that he can do whatever he wants.

While celebrating the signing of executive orders expanding hunting and fishing access on public lands, he began his bizarre remarks by directing them to Johnny Morris, a billionaire who founded Bass Pro Shops. He laughed as he bragged to an audience, telling the onlookers he could ban the favourite pastime, and may still do so just to put his friend out of business.

“I could've banned fishing. The power of this office is crazy. I could've banned fishing, and driven Johnny right the hell out of business…” Trump said threateningly, before he began to laugh.

US President Donald Trump said he could ban fishing if he wanted

“I think I’m gonna do that, until he calls me and says, ‘Please, president, please, you’re putting me out of business, sir’… anyway,” he trailed off. The clip was shared to X by journalist Aaron Rupar, and most people did not share the president’s sense of humour.

One wrote: "A man with this much power having such small man syndrome is a problem, I don't care what anyone says", while another added: "Ego much?"

Another person added: "Literally everyone in that room thinking, what a huge t***"

Others took the threat seriously. “I prefer presidents who don’t get excited over having crazy powers,” replied one person, while another reminded people, “He could end the world with the push of a button. Sleep tight.”

One user also wrote, “Man, if only we had any sort of indication the King was mad with power.”

And one American added: "“With the price of groceries, you don't need to ban fishing; you already banned eating."

But Trump was not done there with his boasts, telling his captive audience about "all the work" he was having done in the White House, so it is all to his tastes.

Trump boasted of his 'crazy power' in The White House

“We've done a lot of work on the White House. The exterior was in bad shape, very, very bad shape,” he began. In August, Trump put statues of some of America’s founders in the Rose Garden, while replacing the lawn with a white stone patio, turning the historic outdoor space into a look-alike of the one at his Mar-a-Lago home and private club in Palm Beach, Florida.

He continued: "What they've been able to do with stone, with granite, six inches thick, carved with a beautiful eagle. It's a helipad where the helicopters can land. Never had that before.”

“Frankly, the new helicopters that were purchased are so powerful that they ripped the grass out. I said, ‘What about trying to build a helipad,’ and it really came out good, very good.”
