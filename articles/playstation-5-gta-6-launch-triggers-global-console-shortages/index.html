PlayStation 5 — GTA 6 Launch Triggers Global Console Shortages

TheTools · 16.11.2026 20:09 · source · permalink

Anticipation for PlayStation 5 consoles has reached a fever pitch as gamers scramble to secure hardware ahead of the highly awaited global launch of Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6). Retail inventories have rapidly depleted, leaving many prospective buyers relying on online lottery systems or secondary markets to find available systems.

Global Frenzy and Record-Breaking Demand

Developed by Rockstar Games, GTA 6 is scheduled for a worldwide release, continuing the legacy of the open-world franchise. Known for its high degree of freedom and immersive gameplay, the series has accumulated massive global popularity over decades. Following the opening of pre-orders, the title shattered expectations with millions of reservations and staggering revenue figures within just days, intensifying the race for compatible hardware.

Hardware Shortages and Surging Resale Prices

Because the game is optimized exclusively for current-generation hardware and does not run on older systems like the PlayStation 4, demand for the high-end PlayStation 5 Pro has spiked dramatically. Standard retail supplies have vanished, pushing secondary market prices significantly higher as supply chain bottlenecks involving memory chips and graphics processors further restrict manufacturing volume.
