Güler escapes red card after off-the-ball elbow on Peque goes unpunished

LALIGA

The Turkish midfielder shook off his marker by striking him in the face as they ran

Actualizado el 18/10/2026 16:24 EDT

In a match that had offered little excitement up to that point, the first major controversy of the night erupted in the 22nd minute. Arda Güler was at the center of the incident after appearing to elbow Peque while the two were running toward goal, with neither player in possession of the ball.

The Sevilla player was left on the ground before Huijsen aggressively pulled him back to his feet, sparking a heated confrontation between players from both teams. Tensions eventually subsided, with the referee showing yellow cards to the Real Madrid defender and Félix, who had confronted him.

The original incident was reviewed by VAR, which remained in constant communication with referee Busquets Ferrer. However, the officials decided that Güler's actions did not constitute violent conduct, allowing the Turkish midfielder to escape without punishment.
