New campground coming to Rosemont Reservoir

TheTools · 24.10.2026 19:05 · source · permalink

COLORADO SPRINGS, Colo. (KKTV) -Big changes are coming to Rosemont Reservoir on the south side of Pikes Peak, where visitors will soon have more places to camp and new ways to get out on the water.

Gov. Jared Polis joined state and local leaders Friday to break ground on a new campground at the reservoir.

The project will bring about 40 campsites, picnic areas, restrooms and a boat ramp for kayaks, canoes and paddleboards.

Colorado Parks and Wildlife hopes to open the campground in summer 2027, possibly earlier in the season. That will depend on the weather and how construction goes.

For years, the reservoir has been a place for fishing. The new boat ramp will allow visitors to use kayaks, canoes and paddleboards, which weren’t previously allowed.

But there are limits to what visitors can do.

Rosemont Reservoir is part of Colorado Springs’ water supply. That means swimming and motorized boats will not be allowed.

Colorado Springs Utilities CEO Travas Deal said the expansion could actually make the area safer.

Right now, Utilities doesn’t have staff dedicated to patrolling the reservoir. Colorado Parks and Wildlife will take over recreation management and provide staff and patrols.

Deal said having more officers in the area could help address illegal shooting and camping along Gold Camp Road and lower the risk of wildfires.

Utilities will still manage the dam and water operations.

Colorado Parks and Wildlife Southeast Region Manager Frank McGee said Colorado Springs Utilities customers won’t be paying for the expansion through their utility bills.

Instead, the project will be funded mainly through camping fees, park passes and some Colorado Lottery money.

The final cost of the project has not been announced.

Fishing will remain open during construction, and CPW plans to offer online reservations once the campground opens.

An exact opening date has not been set.
