No winners of major lotto games on Sunday; Ultra Lotto jackpot at P315 million

TheTools · 21.09.2026 16:57 · source · permalink

There were no winners of either of the two major lotto games played on Sunday, September 20, 2026, with the Ultra Lotto jackpot now at over P315 million.

According to the results from the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes' live draw, there were no winning bettors in the Ultra Lotto 6/58 game, whose jackpot is now at P315,343,028.12. The winning combination was 28-33-20-02-16-23.

There was also no lucky bettor in the Superlotto 6/49 game, with the winning numbers 05-33-29-22-12-44 for the prize of P25,292,028.28.
