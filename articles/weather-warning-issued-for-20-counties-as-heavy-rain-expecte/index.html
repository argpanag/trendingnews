Weather warning issued for 20 counties as heavy rain expected - Donegal Daily

TheTools · 26.09.2026 14:39 · source · permalink

Pic: Donegal Daily.

Met Éireann has issued a weather warning for 20 counties in the south and east of the country.

The national forecaster has applied the status yellow heavy rain warning for the entirety of Leinster and Munster, as well as County Cavan and County Monaghan.

Along with heavy, thundery rain, Met Éireann say that there is a risk of localised flooding in areas, and that motorists face poor visibility and difficult travelling conditions on roads.

The warning comes into effect from 4am on Sunday morning, and will stay in place until 11am on the same day.

Even though the warning has not been extended into Donegal, the county still faces a wet and windy start to Sunday morning.

Outbreaks of rain are expected, with them to be heavy or thundery in places.

However conditions will clear as the day goes on, with sunny spells and isolated showers expected from the mid-afternoon tomorrow.

Highest temperatures are expected to be between 14 and 16 degrees celsius.
