'Slow Horses' Seasons 9 & 10 Coming To Apple TV

TheTools · 16.11.2026 12:50 · source · permalink

Breaking News

Slow Horses has been greenlighted by Apple TV for unprecedented ninth and 10th seasons.

The news comes ahead of Season 7 airing and just a couple of days after star Olivia Cooke said it is unlikely the show will go beyond Season 8.

Based on the Mick Herron novels, the hit spy drama stars Gary Oldman, Jack Lowden, Cooke, Kristin Scott Thomas and Jonathan Pryce. It is Apple’s longest-running show and one of its biggest.

The Season 9 and 10 renewal opens up the possibility that the Slow Horses creative team could go beyond Herron’s books.

Watch on Deadline

Herron’s latest book in the Slough House series is Clown Town, which came out last year and is being adapted for Seasons 7 and 8 of Slow Horses along with his previous novel, Bad Actors.

An Apple spokeswoman said more is to come on synopses for Seasons 9 and 10.

In Slow Horses, Oscar winner and three-time Lead Actor Emmy nominee Oldman stars as Jackson Lamb, the brilliant but cantankerous leader of the spies who end up in Slough House due to their career-ending mistakes and who frequently find themselves blundering around the smoke and mirrors of the espionage world.

Slow Horses is produced for Apple TV by See-Saw Films, with Jamie Laurenson, Hakan Kousetta, Douglas Urbanski, Iain Canning, Emile Sherman, Gail Mutrux, Billy Bowring, Mick Herron and Oldman serving as executive producers. See-Saw Films is part of the Mediawan group.
