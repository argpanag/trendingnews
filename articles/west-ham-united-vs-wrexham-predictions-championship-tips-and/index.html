Hammers to continue winning streak 

It looks as though the penny has dropped for West Ham in the Championship. They have won three games in a row, scoring 10 goals in the process, and moved top of the table on Tuesday thanks to their 3-2 victory at Bolton Wanderers.

Pressure had been building on head coach Nuno Espirito Santo after a shaky start in which his team failed to win any of their first three games following their relegation from the Premier League.

They are understandably odds-on favourites at 3/5 to record a fourth straight win when they take on Wrexham tonight. The Red Dragons have lost only once in the Championship, but they also have just one win to their name.

Clean sheets have been hard to come by for both sides – Wrexham have had two, West Ham one – but the Hammers have scored in every league game and Wrexham in all but one, so I am expecting goals in this fixture.

The best bet of the day looks to be West Ham to win and both teams to score, which can be backed at odds of 11/5.

This wager has paid out in two of West Ham’s three victories this season and I think it is good value to do so again, given the infrequency with which the sides keep clean sheets.

The Hammers should have too much firepower for Wrexham going forward, so they are a confident selection.

Tip: West Ham to win and both teams to score @ 11/5

 

Bowen starting to find his range

West Ham’s hero at Bolton on Tuesday was, unsurprisingly, Jarrod Bowen. The club captain looks a cut above the Championship, as many expected, and he netted the opener and the winner.

Bowen now has three goals and an assist in six matches, also scoring twice and setting up another in the Hammers’ two EFL Cup wins against Portsmouth and Southampton.

I tipped Bowen to have two shots on target against the Trotters and I am sticking with that bet for tonight’s game.

The 29-year-old’s two goals were his only efforts on target. He has now had two shots hit the target in three consecutive games, which is not a shock given the volume of attempts he takes.

Bowen has had 25 shots in six matches, at least 14 more than any of his team-mates. Only two other players have reached double figures in that department.

It appears that Bowen’s accuracy is improving, so he looks a nice price to once again have two shots on target at 11/8.

Tip: Jarrod Bowen 2+ shots on target @ 11/8

 

Expect cards at the London Stadium

It should be another busy night for referee Matt Donohue, who has brandished 16 yellow cards in three Championship appointments this season.

Donohue is the man with the whistle for tonight’s game and I think there is a good chance his card-happy streak will continue.

West Ham have collected 12 bookings so far this season, with only five teams earning more cards than the Hammers.

Although Wrexham have received just eight cautions, the numbers suggest they have been fortunate not to pick up more.

The Red Dragons are conceding 12.2 fouls per match on average, which is the seventh-highest tally in the division. For context, that is just 0.3 behind Preston North End, who have had 16 yellow cards.

It is unlikely that Wrexham will get away with persistent fouling with Donohue on the scene, so I am backing both teams to receive two or more cards at 6/4 with bet365

Tip: Both teams to receive 2+ cards @ 6/4
