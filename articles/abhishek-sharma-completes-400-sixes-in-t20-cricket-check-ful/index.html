Abhishek Sharma completed 400 sixes in T20 cricket during his half-century against Pakistan in the Asia Cup 2026 Final. Check the full list of players to reach the milestone and the records achieved by Abhishek.

Abhishek Sharma scored a brilliant half-century against Pakistan in the Asia Cup 2026 Men’s Cricket Final on Saturday, October 3. 

With this knock, Abhishek has now added another significant milestone by completing 400 sixes in T20 cricket. The left-handed batter has already had a successful T20 career and is regarded as one of the finest batsmen in the format.

In this article, we will go through Abhishek Sharma’s innings, the milestone he achieved and the other milestones he achieved during this knock.

Abhishek Sharma’s Innings Overview 

India opted to bat first in this match, and Abhishek Sharma made full use of it. The opening batsman came out to bat and started aggressively, hitting sixes for fun. He went on to score 61 runs in just 28 deliveries, including 3 fours and 7 sixes, with a strike rate of 217.86.

Abhishek Sharma has now taken the second-fewest balls to complete 400 sixes in T20 cricket. West Indies' Andre Russell leads the list by a significant margin.

India Batters With Most Sixes in a Calendar Year in T20s

For the second consecutive year, Abhishek Sharma has smashed 100-plus sixes in a T20 calendar year. He is the only Indian batter to achieve the feat.

  • Abhishek Sharma – 108 sixes in 2025 (7.3519 balls/six)

  • Abhishek Sharma – 104 sixes in 2026 (6.4327 balls/six)

  • Abhishek Sharma – 87 sixes in 2024 (6.5402 balls/six)

  • Suryakumar Yadav – 85 sixes in 2022 (10.0471 balls/six)
