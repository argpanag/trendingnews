Search continues for missing man at lake in Co Westmeath

TheTools · 22.09.2026 12:43 · source · permalink

A search for a missing man in his 20s is continuing at a lake in Co Westmeath.

A multi-agency search operation has been ongoing since Sunday at Lough Ennell near Mullingar.

The Garda Water Unit, assisted by local sub aqua units, Civil Defence and the Garda Dog Unit are taking part in the search.

A 17-foot fishing boat belonging to the man has been found in the lake.

Local volunteers are taking part in the effort and have arrived with boats and kayaks to assist in the search.

One of the search co-ordinators Frankie Wright has issued an appeal for specialist devices, including sonar equipment and also underwater drones.

The visibility in the water is very poor which is hampering the search effort he said.

He said this equipment, as well as aerial drones, would be very helpful, in the search operation.
