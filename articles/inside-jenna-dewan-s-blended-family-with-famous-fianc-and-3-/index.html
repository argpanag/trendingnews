Jenna Dewan has been engaged to her fiancé Steve Kazee since February 2020.

The upcoming Dancing with the Stars contestant welcomed Callum Kazee, six, and Rhiannon Kazee, two, into the world with her partner.

She was previously married toChanning Tatum from 2009 to 2019, and the former duo welcomed Everly Tatum, 13 together.

Learn all about their relationship ties and their blended family below.

Who is Steve Kazee?

Steve is originally from Kentucky, however, he moved to New York City, where he got his graduate degree from New York University's Tisch School of the Arts' acting program.

Post-graduation, he appeared in As You Like It in 2005 during the New York Shakespeare Festival. 

The following year, he portrayed Sir Lancelot in Broadway's Spamalot and in 2007, he also appeared in 110 in the Shade on Broadway as well.

As he continued his Broadway run, in 2012, he won the Tony Award for Best Performance by a Leading Actor in a Musical for the Broadway show Once.

His cast was also honored with the Grammy for Best Musical Theater Album for the project during the 55th Annual Grammy Awards.

Apart from the stage, Steve also appeared in the TV shows Conviction, Medium, Numb3rs, CSI: Crime Scene Investigation, Working Class, Shameless and more.

What is Jenna Dewan and Steve Kazee's relationship timeline?

The duo first met in 2012 when Jenna and her mother saw Once together and met him after the show. Steve ended up messaging Jenna years later when they were both single and they started talking.

In October 2018, the duo confirmed their relationship. The following year, the duo made their red carpet premiere with Jenna showing off her pregnant belly in the public eye for the first time.

In February 2020, the couple got engaged. The following month, they welcomed Callum, and in June 2024, they welcomed Rhiannon into the world.

Meet Jenna Dewan's three children

The actress has a blended family and is co-parenting with Channing. She revealed to Harper's Bazaar: "We're just getting used to it. We're in a very positive energy together, trying to be the best parents to Everly. We support each other."

Everly is very passionate about dancing, especially Irish dancing, which was ignited after Jenna showed her Riverdance. She also enjoys anything makeup and fashion related, and got her sense of style from her mom. 

Callum is musically inclined like his parents and loves playing around with his baby guitar.

As for Rhiannon, when the baby arrived two years ago, Jenna shared on social media: "From the moment you arrived, you have brought immense joy and love into our lives…your gentle grace, sweetness, and beauty have captivated our entire family. Our hearts are overflowing with love, and we are truly blessed by your presence. Welcome to our world baby girl."
