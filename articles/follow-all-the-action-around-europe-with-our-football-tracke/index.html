After another midweek of European football, clubs are returning to domestic action, attempting to juggle a busy period as we near the start of November. Our Football Tracker is the place to be for all the key coverage.

Sunday's highlights

• The headline game of the weekend sees Barcelona host Real Madrid in the first Clasico of the season, as Hansi Flick's side look to increase their lead at the top of LaLiga

• Liverpool face a tricky test against third-placed Brighton in the Premier League, while Michael Carrick's struggling Manchester United host Bournemouth, and Newcastle come up against Crystal Palace

• Bayern Munich take on Freiburg in a crucial clash at the top of the Bundesliga, after Bayer Leverkusen travel to Sinsheim to face Hoffenheim

• Inter will look to move joint-top of Serie A with a win at home to Fiorentina, before Juventus and AC Milan take on Lecce and Udinese, respectively, while Monaco host Lille in a high-stakes match in Ligue 1

Key updates to keep you informed

Sunday, October 25th

13:52 CET - We've got a jam-packed afternoon around Europe, with four Premier League matches kicking off at 15:00 CET, including Liverpool's home clash with high-flying Brighton, and a must-win game for Manchester United against Bournemouth.

Cody Gakpo, Bradley Barcola and Alexander Isak all start for Liverpool, while Bruno Fernandes is surprisingly dropped to the bench by under-fire United boss Michael Carrick.

Saturday, October 24th

23:20 CET - That's all for today on the Football Tracker, but be sure to tune in again tomorrow morning as we gear up towards the big match of the weekend: the season's first Clasico between Barcelona and Real Madrid. Plus, we have five Premier League games to enjoy!

Lens have moved into the top six thanks to the victory, with the top five all still level on points.

22:42 CET - Lazio have edged Parma 2-1 in Rome to go top of the Serie A standings, at least for the time being, with Inter to play on Sunday.

Gennaro Gattuso’s side have been in sublime form this season, and haven’t lost yet in Serie A. What’s more, they’ve now won four on the spin, and look to be mounting a real title push in Italy.

20:27 CET - Tottenham claim a huge win at Stamford Bridge!

They so rarely beat Chelsea away from home, and so they've so rarely won anywhere over the last year or so, but you wouldn't have guessed from how they played tonight. Two goals in the final 15 minutes, an OG from Chelsea goalie Mike Penders and a stunner from Mathys Tel, gave Spurs a win that will mean a huge amount and that they fully deserved.

Their life was made easier by a sending-off for Cole Palmer, but they were dominating and leading even before that.

Thanks to back-to-back wins, they now sit 12th in the table.

19:58 CET - Napoli and Roma have shared the spoils in an absolute thriller of a 3-3 draw in Naples.

Roma staged a comeback to make it 2-2 with 25 minutes to go, but McTominay then scored his second of the game to seemingly give the hosts all three points. However, super-sub Leonardo Balerdi then completed his own brace in the penultimate minute of the 90 to maintain his side's unbeaten start to the season.

19:23 CET - There's nothing to separate Chelsea and Tottenham at half-time, with both sides lacking a cutting edge in the final third in a closely fought opening 45 minutes at Stamford Bridge.

The visitors have acquitted themselves well, going close through Mohammed Kudus and Omar Marmoush, while Cole Palmer has seen Chelsea's best opportunity expertly saved by Antonin Kinsky.

Elsewhere, goals from Frank Anguissa and Scott McTominay have given Napoli a 2-0 lead over Roma heading into the final half-hour, while Borussia Dortmund's clash with Eintracht Frankfurt is goalless at the break.

18:13 CET - Our attention now shifts to the Premier League's late kick-off, as Xabi Alonso's Chelsea take on Tottenham in a fascinating London derby at Stamford Bridge.

Mike Penders starts in goal for the Blues, while Geovany Quenda comes into the attack in the absence of Joao Pedro. As for Spurs, Mateus Fernandes and Mathys Tel replace Tosin Adarabioyo and Dominic Solanke.

Elsewhere this evening, Napoli host high-flying Roma in Serie A, while Borussia Dortmund will look to go top of the Bundesliga when they take on Eintracht Frankfurt.

17:58 CET - Arsenal have moved level on points with Manchester City at the Premier League summit after goals from Ezri Konsa and Gabriel secured a fairly comfortable 2-0 win over Everton.

It's also been a productive afternoon for Fulham, with Alvaro Arbeloa's men claiming a second straight league victory after Oscar Bobb's late strike clinched a hard-fought 1-0 success at Coventry City.

15:19 CET - Aston Villa have handed Man City their first league defeat of the season!

 A brace from Nicolas Jackson has given Unai Emery's side all three points at Villa Park, and you can't say they didn't deserve it. They may not have had much possession, but they took their chances and defended their own box excellently.

Other than Jackson, their key man was goalkeeper Zion Suzuki, who saved six of the seven shots that he faced.

City's first Premier League loss under Enzo Maresca gives Arsenal the chance to go level on points with them this afternoon - the reigning champions kick off at home to Everton in just under an hour.

15:53 CET - Nicolas Jackson is unstoppable!

Man City drew level through a header from Erling Haaland, only for the Aston Villa striker to restore their lead with a great strike from the edge of the box, one that finished off a move he started himself.

He now has seven goals in his last eight starts for Villa.
