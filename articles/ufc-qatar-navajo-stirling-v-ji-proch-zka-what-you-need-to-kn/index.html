UFC Qatar: Navajo Stirling v Jiří Procházka

7am Sunday, 22 November

ABHA Arena, Doha, Qatar

Live blog updates on RNZ

The UFC returns to Qatar for the second time with a New Zealand fighter headlining the event again.

Navajo Stirling will fight former light heavyweight champion Jiří Procházka with potential title implications on the line.

A win over Procházka would make it hard for the UFC to deny him a shot at the belt, currently held by City Kickboxing teammate, Carlos Ulberg.

Meanwhile, Dan Hooker will be hoping to have this hand raised for the first time in over two years and halt a three-fight losing streak when he takes on American Brian Ortega.

The co-main event is a featherweight match-up between former bantamweight champion Aljamain Sterling and rising star Kevin Vallejos.

Flyweights Kyoji Horiguchi and Asu Almabayev, and middleweights Jared Cannonier and Ikram Aliskerov round out the main card.

Stirling's golden opportunity

The event's feature fight will be Stirling's first time headlining a UFC card in a 25-minute showdown with fan favourite Procházka.

Stirling is coming off a career-best performance against another former light heavyweight champion in Jan Blachowicz.

The 29-year-old earned performance of the night honours with a first-round TKO finish, his third in his last three fights, making him the bookies favourite on Sunday.

A win over Procházka could see Stirling handed a title shot in a division desperate for new talent.

After winning and vacating the belt in 2023 due to injury, Procházka has failed in his last three attempts as the challenger.

The last being against current champion and Kiwi Ulberg, who overcame a leg injury to knock out the unorthodox Czech fighter in the first round at UFC 327 in April.

Stirling's age and novelty as a fresh face puts him in the driver's seat for a title shot with a win over Procházka.

However, it also could present another challenge entirely, a potential clash with teammate Ulberg for the light heavyweight crown.

Stirling has previously said he didn't want to fight Ulberg, and believed he could bide his time with youth on his side.

What's Stirling saying?

Stirling - "Prior to UFC I was definitely a fan of Jiri, I knew who he was before the UFC did and here I am fighting him. But at the end of the day, I'm just going to go and smash his face in.

"I love violence, I just enjoy smashing peoples faces in, just teeing off and watching them squirm on the ground a little bit is always fun. I think that's why I was born to do this, I'm a weapon. I'm carrying that energy into the fight, no mercy.

"I can impose my game on anyone, I have so many tools, I can fight forward, I can go back, I can scrap if I want. In this sport, to be a name you have to beat a name."

Hooker's bid to end losing skid

Having headlined Qatar's UFC debut last year, Hooker returns in hopes of ending his losing streak against former two-time featherweight challenger Ortega.

That main event defeat to Aman Tsarukyan was the start of a three-fight skid - all finishes -, with his last loss against UFC newcomer Salahdine Parnasse in September.

His opponent on Sunday hasn't fared much better in recent times, with his last win also coming in 2024.

Ortega last fought for the featherweight title in 2021, losing by decision to UFC great Alexander Volkanovski.

Sunday's fight presents the opportunity for Hooker or Ortega to find a new wind in the organisation, or risk flirting with it being the end of their UFC career.

The other Sterling

Much like the headline billing, the co-main event is another classic rising star against a former champion.

Vallejos is coming off back-to-back performance of the night honours, and another against Sterling could propel him up the featherweight rankings.

Sterling was the previous bantamweight champion, and defended the belt three times before losing his title in 2023.

Since then, he's won his last three of four fights, including a victory over Ortega.

New Zealand women's bantamweight Michelle Montague was originally slated to fight Jacqueline Cavalcanti this Sunday, but the bout has been pushed back to December.

Where and what time?

UFC 321 returns to Doha in Qatar a year on from its debut.

The 7700-capacity Ali Bin Hamad al-Attiyah Arena will host the event.

The main card gets underway at 7am Sunday (NZT), with preliminary fights starting at 4am.

Who else is on the card?

Main card:

  • Light heavyweight: Navajo Stirling v Jiří Procházka
  • Featherweight: Aljamain Sterling v Kevin Vallejos
  • Lightweight: Dan Hooker v Brian Ortega
  • Flyweight: Kyoji Horiguchi v Asu Almabayev
  • Middleweight: Jared Cannonier v Ikram Aliskerov

Preliminary card:

  • Bantamweight: Aleksandre Topuria v Santiago Luna
  • Heavyweight: Shamil Gaziev v Tallison Teixeira
  • Flyweight: Amir Albazi v Alessandro Costa
  • Welterweight: Jake Matthews v Tahir Abdullayev
  • Lightweight: Nikolas Motta v Dom Mar Fan
  • Light heavyweight: Diyar Nurgozhay v Alik Lorenz
