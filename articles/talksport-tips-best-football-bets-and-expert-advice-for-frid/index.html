Bet of the Day 

Seville to beat Valencia @ 11/10

Valencia failed to add much quality to their squad in the summer, with Harvey Elliott's loan move from Liverpool the highest-profile move.

Los Ches have started the La Liga campaign by losing three of their four matches and scoring only one goal.

A 0-0 draw with Celta Vigo on Matchday 1 is all Carlos Corberan's men have to show for their efforts and Valencia were hammered 5-0 by Barcelona last weekend.

Of course, Seville are not in the class of Barca, but a trip to the Ramon Sanchez Pizjuan is still likely to be beyond Valencia.

Seville have picked up good wins over Rayo Vallecano and Athletic Club and Los Nervionenses won the shot count 20-12 and conceded an expected goals total of only 0.93 when losing 3-1 to Atletico Madrid.

Luis Garcia Plaza's men were solid when Arouna Sangante was shown a red card at Espanyol last time out and that didn’t stop Seville leaving Catalonia with a point following a 1-1 draw.

Seville are 11/10 for victory at the Ramon Sanchez Pizjuan and that rates as a nice price.

Hearts to beat Stenhousemuir and Over 3.5 goals @ 7/4

Hearts will be eying silverware this season and the Jambos should progress past Scottish League Cup opponents Stenhousemuir and book a spot in the semi-finals.

The capital club have scored 17 goals in their last six matches and six of their last nine games have gone over 3.5 goals.

Wouter Vrancken's forward-thinking side have won four Scottish Premiership matches on the bounce and they hammered Inverness 6-2 in the previous round of the Scottish League Cup.

Stenny have enjoyed a good start to the campaign and downed Motherwell 1-0 at Ochilview in the previous round of this competition.

However, Marvin Bartley's men have enjoyed a fair amount of good fortune and they are running into a good Hearts side on Friday night.

West Ham to win, Livingston to win & Rennes to win @ 15/4

West Ham are back in action just a few days after Tuesday's 3-2 win at Bolton and the Hammers are fancied to beat Wrexham on home soil.

The Dragons have only one victory across their opening six Championship matches and the prospect of a tie against the Irons is unlikely to offer any respite.

Morton have financial problems off the field and the Ton have lost eight of their nine matches this season. Livingston can bounce back from Saturday's loss against Stenhousemuir to win at Cappielow Park.

Marseille conceded 18 shots and an expected goals (xG) figure of 3.29 in Sunday's 3-2 home loss to Paris FC and that defeat followed a 2-0 reversal at Monaco. Rennes can record a third straight Ligue 1 win on Friday night.
