Bet of the Day

Monza to beat Torino @ 21/10

Monza have taken seven points from their opening seven Serie A matches, but the White and Reds have often been good value for more than that tally. 

A 3-2 loss at Lecce and 1-0 defeat to Parma should have yielded points, but Ivan Juric's side have lost only two of their last six games. 

Monza crushed Cagliari 4-0 at Stadio Brianteo on Monday and they look ready to race to success at Torino.  

The White and Reds meet Torino for a second time this season, following last month's 1-0 Coppa Italia win at Olimpico Grande Torino and Monza are a decent bet for a repeat success in Serie A. 

Monza had 19 efforts on the Torino goal and recorded expected goal numbers of 1.99 in the Coppa Italia tie and, with the Bulls winning only two of five home games this term, Ignazio Abate's men may struggle to turn the tide. 

Torino conceded 25 efforts on goal and six corners when beating Parma 3-0 on Monday night and, while that was an impressive result, there was a large chunk of good fortune mixed into that success. 

At 21/10, Monza look an excellent bet. 

Bet Builder

Ipswich v Nottingham Forest draw, under 2.5 goals, under 4.5 Ipswich corners bet builder @ 29/4 

It is three straight defeats for Nottingham Forest, who attempted to stifle Arsenal before the Gunners found a late breakthrough to win 1-0 at the City Ground last weekend. 

Oliver Glasner's men have scored only five goals in their eight matches this season and they have failed to score in five of those games.

Clearly, the Tricky Trees are tough to break down, but Liam Delap and Chris Wood are poor forward options and Forest are struggling to put the ball in the opposition net. 

Ipswich have won three of their seven top-flight matches and the Suffolk side ran Manchester City close in a 3-2 loss last weekend.

However, Town failed to score in games against Everton and Liverpool and they may find the pragmatic approach of Forest difficult to breach. 

Gary O'Neil's men have hit only 23 corners in seven top-flight matches, at an average of 3.28 per game and that number may not increase when Forest come to Portman Road. 

Playing the draw, under 2.5 goals and under 4.5 Ipswich corners pays 29/4 in a bet builder.

Middlesbrough to win, Alaves to win, Brest to win @ 23/5 

Middlesbrough have lost only one of their last 11 matches, a run which includes wins over Norwich, QPR, Stoke and Wolves and they should be too strong for a Bolton side who have lost three of their last four at home. 

Malaga are still searching for their first win after nine matches of the La Liga season and the maiden success is unlikely to come at Alaves. The home side have beaten Getafe, Villarreal and Osasuna on their own patch already this term. 

Brest have impressed in Ligue 1 this term, with the Pirates in the top three of the expected goals numbers this season. Nice have lost all of their three away games and another loss awaits at Stade Francis-Le Ble.
