ATP Shanghai Day 4 Predictions Including Auger-Aliassime vs Ugo Carabelli

TheTools · 09.10.2026 17:40 · source · permalink

Day 4 at at the ATP Shanghai Masters will the round of 64 completed on what promises to be an action-packed day in southern China. As ever, we here at LWOT are offering our previews and predictions for every match on the slate, including Daniil Medvedev vs Jan-Lennard Struff and Carlos Alcaraz vs Juan Manuel Cerundolo. But who will reach the third round?

ATP Shanghai Day 4 Predictions

Nuno Borges vs Jiri Lehecka

Head-to-head: Borges 0-1 Lehecka

Nuno Borges got his ATP Shanghai campaign off to a good start, but Jiri Lehecka presents a different challenge altogether. The Czech has been striking the ball cleanly on hard courts and comes off a strong run reaching the final in Tokyo. Borges can be competitive from the baseline, but Lehecka has the heavier groundstrokes and should be able to dictate the exchanges. Expect the Czech to make his superior firepower count against a defensive Borges.

Prediction: Lehecka in 3

Arthur Fils vs Pavel Kotov

Head-to-head: Fils 1-0 Kotov

Arthur Fils will be keen to solidify his chances of qualifying for the year-end ATP Finals. The Frenchman has not had a perfect 2026 by any stretch, but his main obstacle has been injury rather than a lack of form. Pavel Kotov has found some rhythm through qualifying and his opening-round victory, but the step up in opposition will be significant. Fils has the more explosive game and should be able to put Kotov under pressure on return. If he keeps the unforced errors in check, the Frenchman should have enough to progress.

Prediction: Fils in 2

Valentin Vacherot vs Sebastian Baez

Head-to-head: first meeting

Valentin Vacherot arrives in Shanghai as the defending champion and he is starting to show decent form again. Sebastian Baez had to dig deep to get past Marcos Giron in the opening round, but his best comes on clay which could become a factor against a more aggressive opponent. Vacherot has the ability to dictate with his forehand and should look to keep the rallies on his terms. Given his recent form and his better serve, the Monegasque looks well placed to advance.

Prediction: Vacherot in 2

Felix Auger-Aliassime vs Camilo Ugo Carabelli

Head-to-head: Auger-Aliassime 1-0 Ugo Carabelli

Felix Auger-Aliassime has been searching for consistency lately, but the fast conditions at the ATP Shanghai Masters should suit his aggressive game. Camilo Ugo Carabelli showed plenty of fight in his opening match, recovering from a set down to advance at the expense of Russia’s Ilia Simakin, although hard courts remain a less natural setting for the Argentine. Auger-Aliassime’s first serve and forehand give him a clear advantage, particularly if he can take control of points early. The Canadian should be able to keep Ugo Carabelli on the defensive and move through.

Prediction: Auger-Aliassime in 2
