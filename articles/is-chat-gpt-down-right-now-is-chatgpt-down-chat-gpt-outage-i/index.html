Don't bother asking ChatGPT if it's down. It won't answer.

"Is Chat GPT down?" is trending on Google and according to Downdetector, OpenAI is dealing with outages on Thursday morning.

According to Downdetector users are having problems using Open AI on Thursday with more than 12,000 reports shortly before 11 a.m.

Most of the Open AI complaints had to deal with ChatGPT, where about 85 percent of the issues were reported.

There is no timetable for when the issues will be resolved. The issues just popped up around 10:30 a.m., so it could be a quick fix.
