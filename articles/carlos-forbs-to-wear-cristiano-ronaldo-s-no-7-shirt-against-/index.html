Carlos Forbs to Wear Cristiano Ronaldo’s No. 7 Shirt Against Denmark

TheTools · 14.11.2026 17:18 · source · permalink

Rabat – Portugal have selected Carlos Forbs to wear the iconic No. 7 shirt in their upcoming UEFA Nations League clash against Denmark on Saturday, following Cristiano Ronaldo’s retirement from international football earlier this month.

According to Portuguese outlet A Bola, the Club Brugge winger will take over the number for the match, as Rafael Leao, who is considered the leading candidate to become its permanent successor, remains unavailable due to injury.

Ronaldo brought his 23-year international career to an end in early November, leaving behind a legacy closely associated with Portugal’s No. 7 shirt.

The former Portugal captain made 234 appearances for his national team, making him the most-capped player in international football history. He also finished his international career as the world’s leading men’s international goalscorer, with 146 goals.

Forbs to wear Portugal’s No. 7 temporarily

Forbs will wear the famous number against Denmark, although his selection does not necessarily mean he will inherit it permanently.

The 22-year-old has worn the No. 7 shirt on previous occasions, while several other players have also taken the number in Ronaldo’s absence over the years.

Ronaldo, who previously played for Real Madrid and Manchester United, established himself as Portugal’s regular No. 7 during Euro 2008, turning the number into one of the most recognizable symbols of his international career.

Portugal’s squad regulations for the competition require players to wear numbers between 1 and 23, making it necessary for another player to take the vacant number despite Leao’s absence.

The AC Milan forward is expected to become the long-term successor, according to A Bola, but his current injury has opened the door for Forbs to wear it against Denmark.

Portugal drop three players gainst Denmark

Meanwhile, Portugal coach Jorge Jesus has finalized his squad for Saturday’s encounter after naming 27 players for the current international camp.

Joao Cancelo was automatically ruled out through suspension, while three other players were left out of the matchday squad.

Sporting CP defender Goncalo Inacio was among those omitted after joining the national team camp late as a replacement for the injured Tomas Araujo.

Tottenham midfielder Mateus Fernandes also missed out, despite being among the new faces called up for the current international break.

Monza forward Gustavo Varela completed the list of omissions. The striker had been hoping to make his Portugal debut but remained behind Goncalo Ramos and Fabio Silva in the pecking order.

Portugal will face Denmark in Braga on Saturday, with attention also turning to Forbs as he prepares to wear Ronaldo’s famous No. 7 shirt for the first time since the former captain’s international retirement.
