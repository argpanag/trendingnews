Fenerbahce vs. Roma Champions League prediction, start time, how to watch

TheTools · 10.09.2026 17:41 · source · permalink

Fenerbahçe and Roma open their Champions League league phase on Thursday in Istanbul.

The Turkish side is back in the main competition for the first time since 2008 after three qualifying rounds.

Ismail Kartal's side lost 2-1 at home to Beşiktaş last time out and has won just two of its first four league matches to start the season.

Roma has won all three Serie A matches, scoring 10 goals and conceding one.

Gian Piero Gasperini's side came from behind to beat Atalanta 2-1 on Saturday. Donyell Malen already has five goals and will be heavily leaned on to provide the scoring spark in Europe.
