Air Force One refueling stop diverted because of weather

President Donald Trump landed at Alaska’s Fairbanks International Airport after a planned refueling stop at Elmendorf Air Force in Anchorage was diverted because of weather, the White House told travel pool reporters on the trip.

Air Force One landed around 4:30 p.m. ET for the refueling stop on its way to the Philippines.

After taking off from Alaska Monday evening, Air Force One is expected to arrive at Ninoy Aquino International Airport in Manila early Tuesday morning ET for the ASEAN Summit.

-ABC News Isabella Murray

Logistical hurdles with Trump-Kim Jong Un meeting

President Trump has repeatedly expressed interest in reviving diplomacy with North Korea, but beyond the apparent lack of developed plans, there are logistical barriers that could make an impromptu meeting between the leaders on the sidelines of APEC unlikely.

Kim Jong Un has traveled internationally by plane before, but the last publicly documented time he did so was all the way back in 2018 -- when he borrowed a Boeing 747-400 from China to meet President Trump in Singapore.

Since then, he has traveled either by car for a couple short, cross-border trips or via notoriously slow armored trains. North Korea’s rail fleet travels at an average speed of around 35 mph -- topping out at 50 mph on China’s more advanced tracks.

While it is technically possible to travel by armored train from Pyongyang to Shenzhen, a one-way journey is projected to take as long as 60 hours.

Kim made a quick and quiet visit to China last month, taking a car over a newly opened bridge connecting the countries, according to Chinese state media. He did not meet with Chinese President Xi Jinping during the short trip.

South Korea's top diplomat previously said he believed there was little chance of Kim attending the APEC summit. North Korea is not a member of APEC, and Kim has never attended an APEC meeting.

-ABC News' Shannon Kingston

Trump says he may meet with North Korea's Kim Jong Un

President Donald Trump is traveling to Asia for meetings with global leaders and for his second visit to China for a meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping.

As he departed the White House Monday morning, Trump said he "may" meet with North Korea's Kim Jong Un in China as well, though he said it would ultimately be Xi's decision.

"He may come. I don't know. That's up to President Xi," Trump said of Kim Jong Un.

Trump will participate in the first-ever trilateral meeting between the leaders of the U.S., China and Russia on the sidelines of the APEC Summit in China, senior U.S. officials said. The president is expected to discuss the war in Iran with Xi and Russian President Vladimir Putin. However, he does not intend to discuss Ukraine during the trilateral meeting, officials say.
