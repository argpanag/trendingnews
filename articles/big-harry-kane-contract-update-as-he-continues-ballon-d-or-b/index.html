Big Harry Kane contract update as he continues Ballon d'Or bid

TheTools · 05.10.2026 17:43 · source · permalink
  • Jack Johnson, Sports Writer
  • Published: 18:43, 5 Oct 2026
  • Updated: 18:44, 5 Oct 2026

Harry Kane is in the final stages of agreeing a new contract with Bayern Munich, talkSPORT understands.

The England captain, who joined Bayern from Tottenham in 2023, is expected to sign a fresh three-year deal.

Kane has entered the last 12 months of his current contract in Germany, which is set to expire at the end of this season.

But he is now going to commit his future to the club until the summer of 2029, having scored a staggering 152 goals in 154 appearances for them across all competitions.

The two-time Bundesliga champion will be one month shy of his 36th birthday when his new contract runs out.

What has Kane said about a new Bayern deal?

Kane confirmed that talks were underway with Bayern over a new deal in August, admitting it may be the final one of his career.

He said on August 29: "The first meeting started last week. I'm 33. It's probably going to be, not my last contract.

"But it's a big contract for me to sign. They need to make sure I'm happy. They obviously need to make sure they're happy… there's no panic from either side.

"I'm sure we'll get to a point where both sides are happy."

And it now seems like that point has been reached, with Kane set to stay at Bayern to potentially see out his playing days.

Is there any chance Kane returns to Spurs?

Many Tottenham fans will have been dreaming of seeing Kane back at his beloved club that he played almost 450 games for, to have a go at breaking Alan Shearer's Premier League goal record.

And the striker does have a clause in his current Bayern contract that allows Spurs to match any transfer offers made.

That has reportedly led to a few issues in sorting out his new deal, and it is unclear whether that clause will remain.

If not, the dream of Kane returning to north London may be over, as well as his hopes of a brand new top-flight record.

Kane's bid for the Ballon d'Or

Kane's incredible form for Bayern Munich and England has him as one of the frontrunners to win the Ballon d'Or.

The 33-year-old scored 73 goals for club and country in the 2025/26 season and has maintained that form since.

He also led the Three Lions to the World Cup semi-finals after winning his third straight Bundesliga Golden Boot.

If he is to win the award and become the first Englishman to do so since Michael Owen in 2001, then he is sure to send some praise Bayern's way after committing his future to the club.

Lamine Yamal is expected to be his biggest rival after winning the World Cup, with Kylian Mbappe also a contender.

Kane's England teammate Anthony Gordon has been speaking about his skipper's chances of winning the prestigious title.

And he thinks that it is between Kane and Barcelona teammate Yamal for the crown.

Gordon said: “For me, it has to be one of the two in terms of what they've achieved. What Lamine's achieved at such a young age.

"The kind of player he is, is ridiculous. He's on a whole other level right now. For a 19-year-old to be doing what he's doing is crazy.”

He added: “But if we were talking off last season, H [Kane] has got as big of an argument as anybody because his season was historic. 

“The amount of goals he scored, the things he's done for us within the World Cup, they both have large arguments. So I wish them both the best of luck."
