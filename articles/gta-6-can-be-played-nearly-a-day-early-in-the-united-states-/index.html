GTA 6 Can Be Played Nearly a Day Early in the United States via What's Called the New Zealand Trick — Here's How It Works

TheTools · 17.11.2026 09:47 · source · permalink

It's about time.

GTA 6 will have a regional release, which means certain people can actually play it early thanks to what's called the New Zealand trick.

Rockstar Games has confirmed that GTA 6 will become available at midnight local time, as the clock strikes November 19, meaning some parts of the world will get the game earlier than others. Xbox has a pretty well-known loophole known as the New Zealand trick, which allows you to play games a full day early with ease.

How to Do the New Zealand Trick for GTA 6

All you have to do is open your Xbox's settings menu, scroll down to System, and click on Language and location. Change the location tab to New Zealand and hit the "Restart Now" button on the right-hand side of this menu to reset your console. When you come back online, your console will be "in" New Zealand. As a result of this, make sure to buy GTA 6 before you change your console's location; otherwise, you will have to pay in New Zealand currency.

As for PlayStation, there is also a New Zealand trick, but it's not nearly as easy or convenient. You have to create a new account based in New Zealand and buy a copy for that account. You can then enable game sharing to give the game back to your main account, but you will likely need to play the game on the New Zealand profile until it unlocks for your regular account. So, it may be too much of a headache to even bother with this on PS5.

For those in the United States, using the New Zealand trick lets you play GTA 6 almost a full day early, at around 7am ET on November 18. This trick applies to those outside of the United States as well, but folks in the U.S. will arguably get the most mileage out of this, and playing early will help with the inevitable spoiler problem as GTA 6 goes live around the world ahead of you.

If you're avoiding the New Zealand trick, you'll need to wait until midnight local time.

GTA 6 global release times on PS5 and Xbox Series X and S:

  • Los Angeles: 9pm PST (November 18)
  • Denver (Mountain): 10pm MST (November 18)
  • Chicago (Central): 11pm CST (November 18)
  • New York: 12am EST (November 19)
  • Mexico City: 12am CST (November 19)
  • Brazil: 12am BRT (November 19)
  • London: 12am GMT (November 19)
  • Beijing: 12am CST (November 19)
  • Seoul: 12am KST (November 19)
  • Tokyo: 12am JST (November 19)
  • Melbourne: 12am AEST (November 19)

As is well documented, GTA 6 is not being released on-disc. Rockstar Games has released a 'code-in-box' version that is available now. So, all will have to download GTA 6, which weighs in at 212GB. Rockstar will sell a double-sided map/poster for 99 cents on its online store. It will also be included for free with purchases from the company's store, but fans can also download and print out a free one as well if they want.
