More sunshine today, but time is running out on this stretch of fine fall weather

TheTools · 12.11.2026 12:45 · source · permalink

Pattern about to get active again with potentially several rounds of rain and mountain snow

Pattern about to get active again with potentially several rounds of rain and mountain snow

We are riding out a cool and crisp fall morning with clear skies overhead, it's the first morning this week that we don't have foggy conditions across Southeast New Mexico and West Texas! Once we ride out the daybreak hour and get the sun up, temperatures should climb pretty quickly and we will have another super-nice fall afternoon to enjoy with mild temperatures and bright skies. Winds will stay light again today too so it will be an absolutely beautiful afternoon to spend some time outdoors.

Changes start to move in tomorrow as clouds increase - especially during the afternoon - and rain showers start to move in to Southwest New Mexico during the afternoon and then spread across the rest of Southern New Mexico Friday night and into Saturday morning. There is some uncertainty as to whether or not we will see some scattered showers push into Albuquerque late in the day tomorrow, right now it looks like there will only be a slight chance of rain later in the day. Now on Saturday we will see a better chance of rain and mountain snow around Albuquerque and through Central and Northern New Mexico, this storm will be gone by Sunday though - however temperatures will be running cooler on Sunday in the wake of this storm.

Next week looks active and continued cool as well. Monday still looks dry, then we have another storm system coming for Tuesday and Wednesday. That Tuesday/Wednesday storm could be a real fast mover and timing looks tricky right now, but Tuesday brings rain through probably later in the day and at night, and we'll have to see if there are still some showers around on Wednesday or not. The areas most-favored for snow with this system will be the mountains of Western and Northern New Mexico and also Southern Colorado. Then we'll look towards next weekend for the possibility of another storm system.
