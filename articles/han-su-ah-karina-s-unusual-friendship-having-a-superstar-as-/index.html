Han Su-ah, Karina's Unusual Friendship: "Having a Superstar as a Friend Is Tiring… We Call Each Other 'Honey'" [Usa Pyeon] | STARNEWS

Actress Han Su-ah boasted of her special closeness with aespa's Karina.

On the 10th, a video titled "Did anyone know that Han Su-ah and Karina love each other this much?" was released on the YouTube channel "There Is a Letter Between Us."

On that day, Han Su-ah revealed her special bond with Karina, saying, "Today, my best friend Jimin is going to write a letter to Karina."

She joked, "I'm tired. Having a superstar as a friend is no small feat," and added, "She was already a star when we first met."

Regarding how she became close with Karina, Han Su-ah said, "When people mention the name Karina, they often mention ITZY's Ryujin together." She explained, "After filming the movie 'Noise,' Ryujin came to the premiere as an acquaintance of my senior Lee Sun-bin."

She continued, "My senior Lee Sun-bin told me, 'You're the same age, so talk to her.' At first, I forced small talk, but at some point, our conversation flowed naturally." She explained that while she was getting closer to Ryujin, Ryujin said she wanted to hang out with Karina.

Han Su-ah later revealed that she first met Karina at Ryujin's home. She recalled, "Karina asked me for my phone number. That night felt like a magic day."

In particular, Han Su-ah cited their similar personalities as the reason they became close so quickly. She said, "The deeper our conversations went, the more I realized this friend and I are alike. From our values in life to our anger codes, humor codes, and tear codes, everything was the same." She added, "That's why we became much closer in a short amount of time."

She continued, "We saw each other almost every day. It felt natural for Karina to finish her schedule and come to our house to eat dinner." She revealed their special friendship by saying, "I thought an adult friend is someone who maintains a distance that is neither too close nor too far from others, but I think we broke that rule."

Han Su-ah also confessed that there were moments when she was consciously careful because Karina is a famous star. She said, "Because she is a star, there were things I consciously avoided, but when I called her, she was really happy about it." She added, "But for this content release, we did almost all the interviews together, and she even gathered up all the photos taken with me."

The three also revealed special nicknames they use for each other. Han Su-ah said, "Jimin's nickname for me is 'Toa.'" She explained, "When I was meeting Karina every day, that friend was working, and I stayed home for about two days. When Karina came home, I ordered food and said, 'Honey, why are you only now coming?' and she loved it a lot."

She then boasted of their unusual closeness by saying, "Ryujin, Jimin, and I all call each other 'honey.'"
