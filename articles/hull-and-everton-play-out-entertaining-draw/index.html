  5. Should second Palace goal have stood?published at 16:37 BST 11 October
    16:37 BST 11 October

    FT: Crystal Palace 3-1 Nottingham Forest

    Dale Johnson
    Football issues correspondent

    Nottingham Forest boss Oliver Glasner might have something to say about Crystal Palace's crucial second goal.

    While it has been credited to Jefferson Lerma, replays seem to suggest that he may not have been the goal scorer.

    There appears to be evidence that Lerma headed the ball onto the elbow of teammate Anan Khalaili, and that is what took the ball towards goal before it found a way past keeper Matz Sels.

    A goal cannot be scored if the ball touches the arm, so Forest may believe the goal should have been ruled out. The VAR determined that there was no conclusive evidence to get involved.

    This will sting for Forest after they had a Neco Williams goal ruled out for handball against Spurs, after he bundled the ball into the net on the goal-line.

    There is a case that the evidence was clearer for handball by Khalaili than for Williams, and Forest have been on the wrong end of both.

  7. 'It was like I didn't deserve to win here'published at 16:34 BST 11 October
    16:34 BST 11 October

    FT: Crystal Palace 3-1 Nottingham Forest

    Nottingham Forest

    Nottingham Forest boss Oliver Glasner, speaking to Sky Sports: "We didn't kill the game off and we should have done it, creating so many chances. I played many games here and I haven't seen a team create so many chances. At the end we did not convert them.

    "We conceded the two goals before half time then we had two big chances but then we conceded the slapstick goal and we are 3-1 down.

    "Today it looks like it is just me who is responsible for this, call it karma or whatever but it was like I didn't deserve to win at Selhurst Park because the players did everything. Nobody to blame for anything, the effort was there and the quality was there.

    "This performance will give us confidence even if it feels strange with the result.

    "Crazy game but for us it is important not just to look at the result.

    "A huge thank you to the Palace supporters and the Forest supporters. It is not often you are supported from both sides, so thank you very much."

    On the Liam Delap chance: "He has scored 90% of his goals with power, that is his strength and I don't blame him for this. He knows he could have done it differently but I think 10 times with the same situation he would have scored that."

    On the second Palace goal: "I haven't watched it back. Maybe today it was not clear and obvious."

Hull continue good start with entertaining Everton draw

Mohamed Belloumi scored a second-half leveller as Hull City and Everton continued their good starts to the season with an entertaining draw at MKM Stadium.

Hull took the lead in the 43rd minute as Joe Gelhardt slipped the ball past onrushing Everton keeper Jordan Pickford after Sorba Thomas had intercepted Ainsley Maitland-Niles' poor pass.

But less than five minutes later, Everton levelled when Maitland-Niles' cross found Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall who nodded in at the back post.

Everton turned things around in the 68th minute when Thomas gave the ball away, allowing Dewsbury-Hall to play in Thierno Barry who finished well.

But Belloumi equalised with 16 minutes to go, firing a shot past Pickford after a cut-back from Elliot Stroud.

Hull's fine start continues

Jakirovic post-match interview - Hull City v Everton

After scoring for Algeria on Tuesday, Belloumi was only fit enough for the bench but his late goal was enough to save his side's unbeaten home start to the season.

The midfielder looked set to shoot first time but smartly tapped the ball past Dewsbury-Hall and beat Pickford for pace with his driven shot.

A win would have taken Hull into the top four. Their strong start has been built on a solid defence and Everton's double were the first home goals they have conceded this season.

Still, City fans will be delighted with their side's start to the season. Last season's Championship play-off winners are now up to nine points from six Premier League games thanks to two wins and three draws.

Barry misses and offside goal frustrate Everton

Moyes post-match interview - Hull City v Everton

Similarly, Everton are in good form and have now avoided defeat in their opening six league matches for the first time since 2013-14. In that season, they finished fifth under Roberto Martinez.

A point at Hull puts The Toffees in fifth and only outside of a Champions League spot on goal difference.

Everton were narrowly the better side and created several good opportunities. They thought they had scored in the first half only for Brennan Johnson's effort to be ruled out, with the Wales forward offside when steering the ball in after Harrison Armstrong's shot had been cleared off the line.

Barry also had several chances before his eventual goal, having a close-range header saved and failing to connect to a pass across goal when three yards out and the scores level at 1-1.

Everton were playing their first match since it was revealed their owners The Friedkin Group (TFG) are exploring the sale of the club.
