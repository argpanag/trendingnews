Nations League: When is the quarter-final draw?

TheTools · 20.11.2026 11:04 · source · permalink

England are a seeded side for the Nations League quarter-finals after topping their group

  • Published
    17 November 2026

The draw for the final eight teams in the 2026-27 Nations League will be on Friday, 20 November at 11:00 GMT.

It will take place at Uefa's headquarters in Nyon, Switzerland and be broadcast on Uefa TV.

England are the only home nation to have made it to the quarter-final stage, however Northern Ireland's draw against Hungary saw them top Group B2 and secure promotion to League A in the 2028-29 tournament.

Scotland will remain in League B following their third-place finish in Group B1, and Wales face a League A/B relegation play-off in March after being consigned to last place in Group A4.

The Republic of Ireland finished second in Group B3 and so will take part in a League A/B promotion play-off in March.

How does the draw work?

The winners of groups A1-A4 are seeded for the draw, playing the runners-up of other groups in League A.

England have sealed their place in the last eight as Group A3 winners and could face:

  • Belgium

  • The Netherlands

  • Norway

The Netherlands

Norway

They cannot face A3 runners-up Spain as teams must be drawn against sides from other groups.

The quarter-finals will be played over two legs during the international break between 25-30 March.

Seeded teams get to play the second leg of their quarter-final at home.

Nations that reach the semi-finals can bid those penultimate matches as well as the final.

The remaining dates for the Nations League are:

  • Quarter-finals first leg: 25-27 March

  • Quarter-finals second leg: 28-30 March

  • Semi-finals, third-place play-off and final: 9-13 June

Quarter-finals first leg: 25-27 March

Quarter-finals second leg: 28-30 March

Semi-finals, third-place play-off and final: 9-13 June
