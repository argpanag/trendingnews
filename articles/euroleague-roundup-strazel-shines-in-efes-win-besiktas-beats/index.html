EuroLeague roundup: Strazel shines in Efes’ win, Besiktas beats Bayern

On Friday, the sixth round of the EuroLeague concludes, and the first victories have been achieved by Besiktas and Anadolu Efes

By Eurohoops team / info@eurohoops.net

Besiktas achieved their third victory in the competition after defeating Bayern on their home court with a score of 95-87.

Besiktas had a +28 lead (67-39), but Bayern erased that advantage, and for the second time in three days, we could witness history. However, Bayern did not complete the comeback, and in a dramatic finish, the home team secured the win.

Jaylen Nowell scored 21 points, Scottie Wilbekin had 20, while Ante Zizic finished the game with 13. On the other side, Vladimir Lucic scored 17 points, Kamar Baldwin 14, and Oscar da Silva also had 14.

Efes gets the win on the road

In the other match at the same time, Anadolu Efes easily defeated Zalgiris in Kaunas with a score of 85-70.

After a series of losses, the Turkish team bounced back with a dominant performance to defeat the Lithuanians in their arena.

Mathew Strazel scored 19 points, Cameron Payne 16, Collin Malcolm 15, while Dario Saric achieved a double-double with 14 points and ten rebounds. For Zalgiris, the only players in double digits were Carsen Edwards with 16 and Sterling Brown with 13 points.

Crvena Zvezda secures the victory at home

Crvena Zvezda struggled in the final minutes to secure the win in Belgrade (90-81). With a 46-36 score at the end of the first half, Ibon Navarro’s team secured its fourth win on the row in this EuroLeague, bringing its record to 4-2. Paris pushed harder in the final quarter, but it wasn’t enough (1-5).

Chima Moneke led the scoring with 23 points, accompanied by an omnipresent Jordan Nwora (12 points, 6 rebounds). Nadir Hifi did not play for Paris, and Tyson Etienne led the offense with 24 points in the loss.

Hapoel Tel Aviv ends Panathinaikos’s unbeaten run

In Round 6, Panathinaikos suffered its first defeat of this EuroLeague season. Hapoel Tel Aviv won on the road against Obradovic’s team in a thrilling finish (81-91). Itoudis’s team fought until the end and now stands at 4–2 in the standings.

Zach LeDay (17 points) and Elijah Bryant (14 p.) led the hard-fought victory in Athens. For Panathinaikos, Sylvain Francisco’s 22 points were not enough to prevent the defeat. Kendrick Nunn and Isaac Bonga did not play for Obradovic’s team.
