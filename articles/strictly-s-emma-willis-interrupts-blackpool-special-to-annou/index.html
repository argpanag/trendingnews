Strictly's Emma Willis interrupts Blackpool special to announce major show first

TheTools · 21.11.2026 19:12 · source · permalink

Viewers were left up in arms over the explosive record-breaking announcement.

Strictly: Emma Willis, Josh Widdicombe and Johannes Radebe introduce Movie Week

Strictly Come Dancing presenter Emma Willis halted the beloved Blackpool special to announce a major show first. On Saturday (November 21), the star-studded cast returned to the iconic Tower Ballroom for week nine of the competition. This is a milestone moment in each and every series, but this wasn't the only reason the BBC show made the history books. After opening the show with her co-hosts Josh Widdicombe and Johannes Radebe, the 50-year-old broadcaster revealed that the cast have broken BBC records. The mum-of-three began: "Listen up, all fact fans, because I've got a huge one for you.

"We are nine weeks into the competition, and for the first time ever at this point, every single one of our remaining couples has topped the leaderboard. Now that has never happened before in 22 years of Strictly."

The audience soon burst into applause as the contestants gawped at one another before Johannes interjected with some much-needed advice before the show kicked off.

He added: "Now, you are all amazing, and I know exactly how much hard work it took to get here, so I just want to say to you: take it all in and have an amazing night."

It didn't take long for viewers to comment on the revelation online, with many slamming the judges for their scoring throughout the competition.

One user fumed: "All contestants have been top of the leaderboard at some point is a reflection on the ridiculous quality of judging." Another fumed: "How many have deserved to top the leaderboard though..."

Meanwhile, a third pointed out: "This fact is unofficial because week 2 was a combined leaderboard, and Graeme Hall and Lauren Oakley therefore didn't top it officially."

Throughout the show, viewers were quick to call out the judges' scores following sky-high scores for the stars despite obvious errors made on the stage. One example of this was Dani Dyer and Nikita Kuzmin's Couple's Choice routine, Word Up.

The judges awarded her a staggering 35 out of a possible 40 points. One user fumed: "I love Dani, but a higher score than Jamie Winstone and Graeme Hall when she made several mistakes? Just makes me confused."

Another agreed: "Those scores were nuts; it was not good", as a third chimed in with: "I love Dani, but that's a gross overmark." A fourth chimed in with: "It's disgraceful. The judges shouldn't be able to manipulate the voting. They can continue to critique, but no scoring."
