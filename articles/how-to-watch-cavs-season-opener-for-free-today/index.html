Here are five storylines to watch for today’s game:

1. LeBron James hosts Cavs after another wild summer, unexpected landing

James, who won a historic NBA championship with the Cavs in 2016, will debut in a Philadelphia uniform at home for the first time since deciding to sign with the Sixers over the summer. Cleveland was one of the top teams in the mix for the talents of the NBA’s all-time leading scorer.

2. Cleveland enters season with high expectations behind familiar core

Following an Eastern Conference Finals sweep courtesy of the eventual NBA champion New York Knicks, head coach Kenny Atkinson returns a formidable core anchored by Donovan Mitchell, Evan Mobley, and James Harden.

Acquired from the Denver Nuggets in a multi-team trade after averaging 14.6 points and shooting 49.1% last season, Watson is expected to immediately raise Cleveland’s defensive ceiling, while also providing a potent punch on the offensive end.

With center Jarrett Allen questionable and both Nae’Qwan Tomlin and Khalifa Diop sidelined, Cleveland could rely on Thomas Bryant against Joel Embiid, while Mario Hezonja prepares for his first NBA action since August 2020.

The 76ers enter at 1-0 after defeating the defending champion Knicks 106-100, led by Tyrese Maxey’s 28 points alongside strong contributions from Jaylen Brown, James, and Embiid.
