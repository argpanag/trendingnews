ALBUQUERQUE, N.M. (KRQE) – A new health destination is bringing fitness and business to Albuquerque's North Valley. ES Gym and Luxiv Wellness celebrated their grand opening over the weekend.

The 16,000-square-foot facility combines fitness, recovery and wellness, all in one building. The owners said the goal goes beyond just a gym.

"The thing that really sets us apart is the vibes," Dexter Henderson, COO of ES Gym and Luxiv Wellness, said. "We're all about welcoming everyone and anyone and making sure we get every single person on the right track."

Saturday's grand opening featured more than 60 local vendors.
