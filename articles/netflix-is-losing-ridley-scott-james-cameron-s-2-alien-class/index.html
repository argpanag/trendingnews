Netflix Is Losing Ridley Scott & James Cameron's 2 Alien Classics on the Same Day

TheTools · 22.11.2026 02:00 · source · permalink

An expert on obscure productions from the ’80s and ’90s, John is known for his encyclopedic recall of cult classics like Beetleborgs.

Two of the best sci-fi horror features of all time are about to leave Netflix. Thankfully, fans still have plenty of time to revisit the Alien franchise's terrifying beginnings before they're gone.

Subscribers officially have less than two weeks left to revisit 1979's Alien before the sci-fi horror classic makes its exit from the Netflix streaming library. Not only is Alien slated to leave the platform on Dec. 1, but James Cameron's 1986 sequel Aliens will also be leaving Netflix on the very same day, and all in the midst of fans waiting for original director Ridley Scott's return to the franchise.

Alien and Aliens Set a New Bar for Sci-Fi Horror in Film

1979's Alien was directed by Ridley Scott from a screenplay by Dan O'Bannon, who co-created the franchise alongside Ronald Shusett. Alien introduced audiences around the globe to Sigourney Weaver's instantly iconic Ellen Ripley, not to mention the monstrous Xenomorphs who have come to define the wider Alien franchise.

Over the course of its box office run, Alien grossed well over $180 million, all against a budget of barely $14 million. Unfortunately, the mixed critical reception that Alien received at the time didn't do it any favors, although it didn't keep a sequel (or countless cinematic imitations) from coming to fruition nearly a decade later, either.

In 1986, Aliens brought Sigourney Weaver back for another trek through one of the most dangerous franchise's in history, as written and directed by James Cameron. Like its predecessor, Aliens was a massive commercial success, grossing more than $180 million against an $18.5 million production budget. Better yet, Aliens garnered near-unanimously positive critical reviews at the time of its release, cementing the franchise's place in the annals of pop culture for decades to come.

Ridley Scott Is Finally Finishing His Latest Alien Epic

It was reported in October that a new Alien film is in the works, this time with Ridley Scott attached as a producer on the project. Although details remain tightly under wraps, it is expected that the next Alien movie will close out the mind-bending story that began in 2012's Prometheus and continues in 2017's Alien: Covenant, both of which Scott returned to direct.

Back in 2024, Scott discussed his latest entries in the series as well as the overall trajectory of the Alien franchise, which suffered for years due to critical misfires such as Alien 3 and Alien: Resurrection. "I should have locked them up, as [Steven] Spielberg did with Jurassic, and everything he does, and James Cameron has done with what he has,” Scott said at the time.

"I resurrected a dead Alien [franchise] with Prometheus and Alien: Covenant, and we should have rejoined the ownership right then, and we didn't, because someone was careless."

Before the next Alien film arrives in theaters, fans of the franchise can look forward to seeing Season 2 of Alien: Earth make its way to the small screen. Incoming Alien: Earth star Sam Spruell gave fans an idea of what they could expect from his character in Season 2 during a recent interview, stating, "So audiences know partly from the first season that the world is divided into quadrants, and they're all run by not really elected officials, but people who have trillions of dollars at their disposal. I play a character who runs a quadrant of the Earth, who has trillions of dollars at his disposal, and he's great."

Alien and Aliens are available for streaming on Netflix until Dec. 1.
