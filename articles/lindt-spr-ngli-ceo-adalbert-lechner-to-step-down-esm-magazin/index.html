Lindt & Sprüngli CEO Adalbert Lechner To Step Down | ESM Magazine

TheTools · 19.10.2026 12:38 · source · permalink

The Lindt & Sprüngli Group has announced the departure of Adalbert Lechner, chief executive officer of the group, as well as additional leadership changes in Germany and Austria.

Vice-chairman of the board of directors and former CEO Dieter Weisskopf will take over as interim CEO with immediate effect, while the search for a successor is under way.

Weisskopf commented, “My focus in the coming months will be to return our European business to growth and to build on the strong performance in North America, as well as in Asia-Pacific markets such as Australia, China and Japan, where we continue to gain market share.

“We will invest significantly in our brands while improving our profitability. My task is to hand over to the next CEO a company with renewed momentum.”

Leadership Changes

Lindt & Sprüngli has also announced the departure of Michal Spiller, CEO of its German operations, following 23 years with the company.

Ali Kulein, who currently serves as CEO for Lindt & Sprüngli’s Austrian operations, will succeed Spiller, subject to approval by the German supervisory board.

Fabian Reinhardt will succeed Kulein as CEO for Austrian operations.

The leadership changes will take effect on 26 October.

Ernst Tanner, executive chairman of the board of directors of Lindt & Sprüngli, said, “The board is convinced that this leadership change will drive future growth.

“Our brands and market positions are strong, and our focus continues to be on delivering exceptional products and experiences for our consumers, and on profitable growth for our shareholders.”

Tanner added, “The current search process will assess the best-qualified people to identify the CEO for the next growth phase, and we expect to appoint a new CEO in the second quarter of 2027.”
