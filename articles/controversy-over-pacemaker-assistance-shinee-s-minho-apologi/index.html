Controversy over pacemaker assistance: SHINee's Minho apologizes, saying, “I won't accept the marathon result”

SHINee member Minho apologized over the controversy surrounding claims that he received physical assistance from a pacemaker during a full marathon.

Minho issued a statement on the 3rd, saying, “I'm sorry for disappointing so many people at the marathon I took part in on November 1.”

He continued, “Everything happened because of my shortcomings, and as someone who cares about and loves sports, I feel deeply ashamed.” He added, “Once again, I apologize for causing concern.”

He also said, “I'll take greater care to make sure this never happens again and will work to always show you my best. I, too, will not accept the result of this marathon, and I won't forget the lessons I learned through this process.”

He also expressed his respect for runners who work hard, shedding countless drops of sweat.

Earlier, Minho took part in the 2026 JTBC Seoul Marathon, held on the 1st, and completed the 42.195-kilometer full course in 3 hours and 18 seconds. His time in his first-ever attempt at a full marathon was just 18 seconds short of a “sub-3”—finishing in under three hours—and drew attention.

However, controversy erupted after a video spread online showing what appeared to be a pacemaker running alongside Minho and pushing him in the back during the race.

Some netizens raised suspicions of a rules violation, saying that competition rules set by the Korea Association of Athletics Federations (KAAF) and World Athletics (WA) prohibit receiving physical assistance or propulsion from another person during a race.

[Ji Seunghun, Star Today reporter]
