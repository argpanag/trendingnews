Scottsdale neighbors say short-term rental party ended in gunfire

TheTools · 13.10.2026 02:08 · source · permalink

SCOTTSDALE, AZ (AZFamily) — A burst of gunfire early Sunday rattled a south Scottsdale neighborhood, where residents said they heard more than 40 shots during a party at a short-term rental.

No one was injured, police said, but several nearby homes were struck by bullets. Neighbors said the shooting left them shaken and concerned about recurring problems with parties at short-term rentals.

Papago Road has been home for Loree Heffron and her husband, Mike, for nearly 25 years. The couple rode bikes, walked their dogs and built close friendships with neighbors, Heffron said.

“We’re like family. All of us,” she said.

Mike died Jan. 20, and Heffron now lives in their home alone. She said she wishes he had been there to see their growing family and the neighborhood’s latest ordeal.

Just after 3:30 a.m. Sunday, gunfire woke Heffron. She stepped outside but ran back into her house after neighbors warned her that people had guns.

A neighbor’s audio recording captured more than 40 shots over about 10 seconds.

Neighbors said about 25 people ran out of the rental home as police arrived. They said detectives told them the gathering was at a short-term rental property and that people ages 16 to 22 were inside. Neighbors also said police found drugs, alcohol and firearms.

Police remained at the scene for hours collecting evidence, residents said. No arrests have yet been made.

Several homes were hit by gunfire, including one where a bullet struck inches above a window, neighbors said. A 5-month-old baby was sleeping inside, Heffron said.

“That’s unacceptable,” she said.

Heffron said the home has become an “oasis” for parties and said residents have dealt with ongoing noise and fights. She said the shooting was not the kind of change she and her husband imagined for the neighborhood.

The short-term rental listing appeared to have been taken down from Airbnb after the shooting. It was not immediately clear whether the listing had been removed by the host or the platform.
