    Preamble

Linda Dahlmann: “I think, especially in the first half, was a very good game from us but the performance dropped in the second.

“We had the chances, also at the end we had two big chances. At this level you have to score.”

Full time: Bayern Munich 2-2 Manchester City

A strange old game which both sides will be justifiably upset they did not win. Bayern should have been out of sight by the break but fluffed their chances. City then took control of the game in the second half but missed a penalty before eventually getting the equaliser.

90 mins: Bayern break on City and Harder plays in Edna but she cannot turn home when stretching.

Two minutes added on.

88 mins: Very much a game of two halves. City would deserve a point if they can hold on here.

GOAL! Bayern Munich 2-2 Manchester City (Wamser, 86)

City break impressively and reach the edge of the Bayern box, concluding with Blindkilde Brown playing in Wamser who pummels the equaliser into the bottom corner.

84 mins: City are on top at the moment. Can they make it count?

Bayern are looking scruffy and disjointed, something City can take credit for.

82 mins: City take their time over a corner and in the end it is appallingly executed. They get lucky and the ball reaches Hemp on the left, who sends in a dangerous cross but no one in black reads it.

80 mins: Bayern send on Edna for Amani. The theory seems to be that Edna will play as a No 9 and give Bayern an outlet.

Fujino comes to life on the edge of the box and takes aim but her effort is deflected wide. City are in with a chance of an equaliser here.

78 mins: Fowler tries to attack the box but is crowded out, so panics and plays a through ball to no one.

76 mins: Clinton is finally on, replacing Mead.

74 mins: Fujino wins a corner on the right. Mead plays it short to Hasegawa, who wins another corner. This one comes to nothing as the goalkeeper claims it.

72 mins: City have plenty of injuries so are short of options on the bench. Only Clinton can class herself as a senior pro of those who could come on.

70 mins: Amani is booked for taking down Hemp, who hits the ground hard and grabs her shoulder. She gets up in the end.

Bayern send on Zadrazil for Damnjanovic.

68 mins: City have a corner on the right. Mead lifts it into the box and it is easily cleared.

City come back at them through Hemp but without the height of Shaw, no one is capable of attacking the cross.

66 mins: Harder barges her way to the edge of the box but her shot cannot make it through all the bodies in black.

63 mins: Shaw is then immediately replaced as is Coffeey. Fowler and Blindkilde Brown on.

PENALTY SAVED!

It is a poor penalty from Shaw and Mahmutovic is able to palm away.

61 mins: PENALTY TO CITY! Fejino is taken down by the goalkeeper in the box.

59 mins: Mead is caught in possession inside her own half and Bayern have a chance to attack but it breaks down on the edge of the pitch box.

57 mins: City are trying to play out from the back and it feels like they are playing with fire against the Bayern press. They make it up the pitch on this occasion but still have to turn back eventually.

55 mins: City have failed to make it out of their half thus far.

53 mins: Casparij goes into the book for taking down Kett. The City right-back getting a red would make it a truly dreadful night for her after the earlier own goal. The set-piece is flicked wide.

50 mins: Buhl tries to whip a free-kick into the box but the delivery is too flat and easily cleared.

The late goal in the first half has changed very little, with Bayern completely dominant still.

48 mins: Harder is on the deck and Bayern force City to kick the ball out. The forward then immediately gets up. What a waste of time.

47 mins: Damnjanovic is cleaned out by Wamser, earning Bayern a free-kick. It is pumped into the box and flicked wide by Viggosdottir.

Second half

Here we go again!

Half-time reading.

Half time: Bayern Munich 2-1 Manchester City

Mead has given City hope after spending the previous 45 minutes being completely dominated. It will be an intriguing second half after all.

GOAL! Bayern Munich 2-1 Manchester City (Mead, 45)

It is all happening! There is a flare on the pitch just where Casparij is flying down the right. She puts in a dangerous cross which reaches Shaw, only to see her shot blocked. Mead, however, is there to poke home. Mahmutovic moans about the flare but it has nothing to do with the goal.

VAR is having a look at something. I am just not sure what. Goal stands.

43 mins: Another failure to clear their lines gives Tanikawa a chance from the edge of the box but she gets under her shot and it goes well over.

41 mins: City need to be quicker in possession because they are repeatedly being caught out by Bayern’s intensity. The amount of times City have been forced back is getting a touch silly.

39 mins: Fujino cannot cope with the Bayern press, forcing her to face her own goal a lot and inevitably give possession away. I would be surprised if she comes out for the second half.
