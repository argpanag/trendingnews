Haas’ return to Brisbane awaits as fresh blockbusters confirmed

  • Breaking
  • Sport
  • NRL
  • NRL

November 19, 2026 - 7.04pm

Payne Haas′ return to Brisbane has been confirmed, with the superstar Rabbitohs recruit set to face his former club in round 10.

The champion front-rower shocked the rugby league world when he announced his decision to quit the Broncos and head to South Sydney, linking up with the same coach who handed him an NRL debut as a teenager, Wayne Bennett.

Payne Haas at Rabbitohs training. Instagram/@ssfcrabbitohs

Haas will pull on the cardinal and myrtle in 2027 and will first run out to face the Broncos at a packed Suncorp Stadium on Thursday, May 6.

It will no doubt be a strange feeling for Haas, who spent his entire career at Red Hill before making the surprise move to Sydney.

Speaking of the gun prop’s new team, the latest instalment of the “Book Of Feuds” has also been confirmed for early in the season, with Souths to meet the premiership-winning Sydney Roosters in round two.

The traditional rivals will collide on Friday, March 12 at Allianz Stadium.

The NRL have also announced some other key fixtures ahead of the full draw being released on Friday morning.

  • Monday, April 26: Bulldogs vs Bears, Accor Stadium (Round 8)

  • Saturday, May 8: Knights vs Roosters, McDonald Jones Stadium (Round 10)
