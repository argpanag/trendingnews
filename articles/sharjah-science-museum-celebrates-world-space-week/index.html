Diverse activities explore space and astronomy

Speaking exclusively to "Sharjah 24," Al Mulla highlighted that the programme includes diverse activities such as astrophotography and astronomical observation, alongside scientific lectures, workshops, and interactive programs designed to give visitors a broader understanding of space and astronomy.

Specialised entities and institutions join the programme

The Assistant Curator added that events on Wednesday and Thursday will take place during the morning session, from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM, while on Friday and Saturday, the museum will welcome visitors from 4:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Al Mulla noted the participation of several specialized entities and institutions—including the Emirates Astronomy Society, the International Astronomical Centre, the American University of Sharjah, and the Sharjah Academy for Astronomy, Space Sciences and Technology—with the UAE Space Agency also taking part in the events.

Scientific experiments and activities engage visitors

The programme features a range of scientific experiments and activities, such as rocket-launching workshops, planetarium shows, and solar observation, alongside interactive activities for children and the "Space Through an Emirati Lens" exhibition, all aimed at fostering a passion for space and astronomy sciences among children, youth, and families.
