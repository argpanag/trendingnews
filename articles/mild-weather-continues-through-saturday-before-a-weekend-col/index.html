Mild Weather Continues Through Saturday Before a Weekend Cold Front Brings a Cooldown

TheTools · 19.11.2026 11:14 · source · permalink

Weather

Rain chances increase for the latter part of the weekend.

Metro Detroit is enjoying an extended stretch of dry, above-average temperatures, and that trend continues through Saturday. High pressure is gradually shifting east, allowing southeasterly winds to pick up and bring even milder air into Southeast Michigan.

Breezy Today With Highs Near 60

Expect a bit of a breeze this afternoon, with gusts around 20 mph. Despite the wind, temperatures will climb back to around 60 degrees, well above normal for this time of November.

Perfect Weather for Downtown Detroit’s Tree Lighting

The weather looks fantastic for Friday night’s tree lighting at Campus Martius Park. Temperatures will be in the 50s for much of the festivities, with dry conditions expected. It should be a comfortable evening to enjoy the lights and holiday activities downtown.

Rain Arrives Sunday, Followed by a Cooldown

Our next chance for rain arrives Sunday as a cold front approaches. Temperatures will make another run toward 60 degrees to close out the weekend, with showers expected to move in later in the day. Behind the front, a noticeable cooldown settles in for next week. Highs drop into the mid-40s Monday, then struggle to reach 40 degrees Tuesday and Wednesday.

Thanksgiving Forecast Still Trending Dry

Temperatures will likely remain in the 40s on Thanksgiving Day. The encouraging news is that early forecast guidance continues to favor dry conditions across Metro Detroit. There is still time for the forecast to evolve, and we’ll keep monitoring the trends as we get closer to Thanksgiving morning and the downtown parade.
