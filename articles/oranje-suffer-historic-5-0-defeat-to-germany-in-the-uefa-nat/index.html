Oranje suffer historic 5-0 defeat to Germany in the UEFA Nations League

TheTools · 16.11.2026 21:53 · source · permalink

Oranje suffered a 5-0 defeat to Germany in Berlin on Monday, bringing their UEFA Nations League group campaign to a painful end. The defeat meant the Netherlands finished second in Group A2, behind Germany, despite both teams collecting 11 points. The 5-0 loss marks the Netherlands' heaviest-ever defeat in an official competitive match. It is also their biggest loss against Germany since a 7-0 friendly defeat in 1957.

Germany scored once before half-time and added four more goals after the break. Derry Scherhant opened the scoring, before Florian Wirtz scored twice. Nico Schlotterbeck and Brajan Gruda completed the rout.

Oranje made one change from the side that beat Greece 7-0. Quinten Timber came in to midfield to replace Frenkie de Jong.

The opening stages were relatively cautious, with Germany enjoying more possession. Oranje had an excellent opportunity around the 11th minute. Cody Gakpo broke down the left and sent a powerful cross into the penalty area. Donyell Malen could not get his header under control despite having a good opportunity.

Germany responded with a quick attack that ended with Scherhant hitting the outside of the post. Scherhant also had another opportunity after breaking through the Dutch defence, but shot over.

Jurgen Klopp’s side got their first goal in the 41st minute. Scherhant received the ball on the left, took on the Dutch defence and cut inside before firing into the top corner. It was his first international goal in his fifth appearance for Germany.

Scherhant threatened again shortly after the restart, but shot over, before Gruda subsequently had a long-range attempt that went wide.

Germany got their second goal after an hour played. Wirtz punished Denzel Dumfries for losing possession near the Dutch penalty area. He took the ball, created space for himself and curled a superb left-footed shot into the corner from around 20 yards.

This was followed by another goal five minutes later. Germany scored from a free kick, with Brajan Gruda delivering the ball from the left and Nico Schlotterbeck getting to it first, flicking a header into the corner.

The fourth goal came in the 71st minute. Gruda and Wirtz combined again. Gruda worked his way into the penalty area and set up Wirtz, who finished from close range.

Gruda followed the assist up with a goal of his own in the 77th minute. Scherhant struck the post, but Gruda was in the right position to collect the rebound and smash it into the net.

Following the goal, Oranje goalkeeper Bart Verbruggen was visibly furious. He called his teammates together and appeared to demand an explanation for their defensive performance.

Germany finished first in the UEFA Nations League group A2, with the Netherlands second. Greece missed out despite collecting the same number of points, while Serbia finished bottom.

The draw to find out who Oranje will face in the quarter-finals is on Thursday. They will be paired against one of the other League A group winners, meaning Oranje will face either France (Group A1 winners), England (Group A3 winners), or the winners of Group A4 (currently led by Portugal, ahead of Denmark).

The quarter-finals will be played over two legs in March 2027, with Oranje hosting the first leg at home before traveling away for the decisive second leg.
