Fair City on Sunday: 'Did you send someone to Babs?'

TheTools · 07.11.2026 07:00 · source · permalink

Carol demands an answer from JJ in Sunday's Fair City on RTÉ One and the RTÉ Player.

The showdown comes after Babs was terrorised in her home by a masked intruder - sent by JJ in an attempt to discover the whereabouts of Babs' husband, Victor.

In Sunday's episode, Carol looks JJ in the eye and says: "Did you send someone to terrorise Babs?"

Fans can watch what happens next on Sunday on RTÉ One and the RTÉ Player at 8:30pm.

Fair City airs on RTÉ One and the RTÉ Player on Sundays, Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Fridays.
