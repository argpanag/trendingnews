Another Hodge joins the Hawks!

Hawthorn has welcomed Cooper Hodge to the club as a father-son selection with Pick 58 in the 2026 Telstra AFL Draft.

18-year-old Cooper will follow in the footsteps of his father Luke, who played 305 games in the brown and gold, which included four premierships.

Standing at 181cm, Hodge is a versatile prospect with strong competitiveness, spending several seasons developing as part of the Brisbane Lions Academy, where he played eight VFL games as an underage player.

Hodge also represented the Allies during the National Championships this year, where he played an important role in his side’s win over Western Australia, logging 20 disposals (nine contested) and three tackles.
