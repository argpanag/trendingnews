Week 3 inactives for Eagles

TheTools · 29.09.2026 01:38 · source · permalink

Philadelphia Eagles wide receiver Hollywood Brown (ankle), defensive end A.J. Epenesa, tight end Dallas Goedert (knee), offensive lineman Fred Johnson (knee), quarterback Tanner McKee (emergency - third quarterback) and quarterback Cole Payton are inactive for the Week 3 game against the Chicago Bears on Monday, Sept. 28.

Meanwhile, TE Zach Ertz will make his return to the team, and LB Jonathan Greenard (pectoral) will make his team debut.
