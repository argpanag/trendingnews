Como extend forward Roberta Picchi's contract after cancer diagnosis

TheTools · 08.09.2026 10:58 · source · permalink

Roberta Picchi was part of the Serie B-winning Como women's squad last season

  • Published
    4 hours ago

Como have extended the contract of their forward Roberta Picchi after she was diagnosed with breast cancer.

The Italian club, who will make their women's Serie A debut this season, said they extended Picchi's contract until the end of the 2027-28 season as part of their "continued commitment" to the player.

The 34-year-old Italian is set to begin cancer treatment and will be supported by Como's medical team, who will work with the ​specialists overseeing her care.

"Everyone at Como ​1907 is alongside Roberta and will give her and her family all the ​support they need during the ​months ahead," the club said in a statement.

"At this time, we ask that Roberta's privacy is respected as she focuses on her treatment ​and recovery."

Picchi was part of the Como side that finished 11 points clear of Lumezzane as they claimed the Serie B title last season to earn their first promotion to the top division of Italian women's football.

"Nothing ​should ever be taken for ​granted, ⁠and I feel fortunate," Picchi wrote on social media.

"It's time to play my match, but ⁠I'll ​be back even ​more determined."

Como, managed by Selena Mazzantini, begin their season against AC Milan on 26 September.
