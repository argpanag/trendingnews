Panathinaikos vs CSKA Sofia: Conference League leaders host opponents seeking their first victory

Panathinaikos and CSKA Sofia meet on November 5, 2026, at 22:00 local time at the OAKA "Spyros Louis" Stadium in Athens, in the third round of the 2026/2027 Conference League league phase. Head-to-head record: CSKA Sofia have four victories, but the most recent came in 1988

The history of these encounters provides an interesting background to the match. Panathinaikos and CSKA Sofia met in European competitions during the 1970s and 1980s. According to the record of officially recognized matches, the Bulgarian club won all four previous encounters, with an aggregate goal difference of 7:1.
Results of previous officially recognized encounters:

OAKA "Spyros Louis": Olympic Stadium with a capacity of 69,618 spectators

The match will be played at OAKA "Spyros Louis", located in the municipality of Marousi in the northern part of the Athens metropolitan area. The stadium is part of a large Olympic sports complex and was built in 1982.
Its most recognizable feature is the roof structure designed by architect Santiago Calatrava for the 2004 Athens Olympic Games. The large white arches above the stands are clearly visible from the surrounding area, making the stadium one of the architectural symbols of modern sporting Athens.
According to the complex's management, the stadium has a capacity of 69,618 spectators. This is the total infrastructure capacity, not confirmation that every seat will be available for this match. The actual configuration of the stands and open sections depends on the organization of the specific event.
OAKA differs from compact football stadiums because an athletics track separates the pitch from the stands. The matchday experience is therefore different: spectators in the lower stands are not immediately next to the touchline, while elevated sections provide an overview of player positioning and the movement of entire teams.
