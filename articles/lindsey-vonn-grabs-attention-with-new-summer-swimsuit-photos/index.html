United States Olympic star skier Lindsey Vonn continues to recover from a complex tibia fracture she suffered just 13 seconds into her run in the women's downhill final during the 2026 Olympics in Cortina d'Ampezzo, Italy.

Despite her injury, the United States Ski and Snowboard team officially announced the 49 athletes named to the Stifel U.S. Alpine Ski Team for the 2026-27 season. Vonn made the team thanks to existing alpine criteria – she's currently ranked in the top 25 of the FIS World Cup Start List.

As she recovers, Vonn has been busy with her summer travel plans and suggested summer was officially over after a recent trip to Paris. "I guess this means summer is over….but it also means my birthday is coming up soon," she said in the caption a post on Instagram last week.

However, she appears to have found a bit more summer.

The new post from Vonn shows the skier enjoying another recent trip to France with her dog, Chance, and new boyfriend, French alpine skier Matthieu Bailet, who is tagged in one of the photos. Another set of photos shows the new couple hike around the cliffs near the water – along with a close-up of Chance getting a ride on a motorized scooter.

"Ok I lied… Chance and I managed to catch a few more days of summer….maybe I've just been in the wrong place all these years. And now I'm tired and happy," she said in the caption of the post.

Vonn later posted on her Instagram story showing she's back home, hitting a workout to get over the jet lag.
