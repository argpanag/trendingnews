The Kerala State Lotteries Department is set to conduct the Dhanalekshmi DL-70 lottery draw today, September 23, with participants awaiting the results of a draw that carries a first prize of ₹1 crore. The weekly lottery draw is scheduled to take place at 3 p.m. at Gorky Bhavan near Bakery Junction in Thiruvananthapuram.

The Dhanalekshmi lottery ticket is priced at ₹50. Along with the top prize of ₹1 crore, DL-70 offers a second prize of ₹30 lakh and a third prize of ₹5 lakh. The official Kerala lottery prize structure also includes several lower-tier prizes ranging from ₹5,000 to ₹100, as well as a consolation prize of ₹5,000 for 11 tickets linked to the first-prize number across other series.

The winning numbers are expected to be published after the draw through the Kerala State Lotteries Department's official results system. The official results page currently shows Dhanalekshmi DL-69, drawn on September 16, 2026, as the latest published Dhanalekshmi result.

The Dhanalekshmi weekly lottery is issued in 12 series. The first three prize categories are common to all series, while several lower prizes are decided based on the last four digits of the ticket number. The prize structure notified by the Government of Kerala provides for a total prize amount of ₹24,35,95,000 across the draw.

Under the notified structure, up to 21,600 tickets can receive the fourth prize of ₹5,000, while up to 6,480 tickets can receive ₹2,000. The sixth prize of ₹1,000 can have up to 32,400 winners, followed by up to 82,080 winners for ₹500, 1,03,680 winners for ₹200 and 1,49,040 winners for ₹100.

Once the draw is completed, participants should compare their ticket numbers with the official results published by the Kerala State Lotteries Department. The department's previous Dhanalekshmi results show that winning numbers are published along with the draw date, venue, prize categories and numbers corresponding to the various last-four-digit prizes.

The Government of Kerala's notification states that lottery draws are conducted using a mechanical device based on random technology and supervised by a panel of at least three judges, including a chairman. The winning numbers are recorded in an official prize proforma and verified by the judges.

Lottery winners should preserve their original winning ticket and complete the claim process within the period prescribed by the Kerala State Lotteries Department. The ticket and required identification and claim documents may be needed when submitting a prize claim.

For prize claims, winners should verify the latest instructions issued by the Kerala State Lotteries Department before submitting their documents, particularly for higher-value prizes. Participants should also rely on the official lottery results and department notifications rather than unofficial social media posts or unverified result lists.

The Dhanalekshmi lottery is one of the weekly lotteries operated by the Kerala State Lotteries Department. The department conducts several weekly draws during the year, with Dhanalekshmi scheduled as a Wednesday lottery. The official notification identifies Dhanalekshmi as a weekly lottery and sets its ticket price at ₹50.
