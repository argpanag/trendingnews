Teacher loses Sh1.7m car over Myanmar human trafficking link

TheTools · 09.10.2026 20:47 · source · permalink

The High Court ordered a primary school teacher to forfeit his Sh1.7 million Toyota Axio after the Assets Recovery Agency proved it was bought using money extorted from human trafficking victims forced into cybercrime hubs in Myanmar.

By  Benson Matheka

Correspondent

  • Article
  • Related

A teacher has lost his Sh1.7 million car after the High Court found that the vehicle was acquired using proceeds linked to an alleged human trafficking operation.

Justice BM Musyoki ordered the forfeiture of the vehicle to the government after finding that Evans Kimtai Sichei had failed to explain how he raised the money used to buy it.
