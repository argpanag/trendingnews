Russell Brand sentenced to 10-and-a-half years in prison for rape and sexual assault

TheTools · 09.11.2026 15:42 · source · permalink

Summary

  • Warning: This page contains distressing details

  • Russell Brand has been sentenced to 10-and-a-half years for rape and three counts of sexual assault - he is likely to serve two-thirds of the sentence in prison

  • The judge tells Brand he is a "sexual predator", who told a "series of lies" and displayed "staggering arrogance"

  • Earlier, one victim told the court she was "in shock and turmoil... I didn't have any respect for myself"

  • Another victim said "Russell Brand is a sexual predator who has no remorse", while another said she "felt powerless in that moment"

  • The woman who Brand raped in 2009 told the court she was "living with trauma", and feared she had contracted HIV

  • Brand faced seven charges: three of rape, three of sexual assault and one of attempted indecent assault. He was found not guilty on three counts

  • His crimes took place between 2004 and 2009, and related to four women. They are all entitled to lifelong anonymity

  1. Sentencing demonstrates 'why investigative journalism matters' - Channel 4 bosspublished at 15:41 GMT
    15:41 GMT

    Channel 4's Dispatches programme says the sentencing of Russell Brand is a "powerful demonstration of why investigative journalism matters".

    The programme – alongside journalists from the Sunday Times and the Times – in 2023 first exposed a litany of sexual misconduct by Brand, leading to women making complaints to police.

    In a statement, director of news Louisa Compton pays tribute to the "courage of these women who gave evidence through the criminal justice system".

    "Today, and throughout the trial, we have heard of the trauma suffered by the women in this case," she says.

    "Above all else, we commend their bravery."

  2. Judge calls Brand a 'sexual predator' as he's sentenced to 10-and-a-half years - a recappublished at 15:23 GMT
    15:23 GMT

    Judge Mr Justice Bennathan opened his sentencing remarks by describing Russell Brand as a "sexual predator".

    First, the judge talked about a sexual assault that took place in a bar in Soho in 2004 - he said that Brand's claim during the trial that he had "never touched a woman without her consent" was one of a "series of lies".

    Moving on to a sexual assault against a woman at the Choice FM studios in 2004 or 2005, where Brand grabbed the victim's face and kissed her before touching her breasts and bottom, the judge said this was sustained and there was added humiliation in front of others.

    Addressing the rape in 2009, the judge said when Brand ordered the victim to clean his sheets, it was a "humiliation".

    Justice Bennathan said the sexual assault by Brand at Channel 4 in 2009 yet again showed his "staggering arrogance", adding: "Your entourage indulged your dreadful behaviour."

    The judge then moved to sentence Brand to 10 years and six months in prison for rape and sexual assault.

    Brand did not react to the sentence, our reporter in court described, but he mouthed to his wife "I love you".

  5. Sentence 'cannot undo the harm the victims have lived with', CPS prosecutor sayspublished at 15:05 GMT
    15:05 GMT

    In a written statement, Crown Prosecution Service (CPS) senior crown prosecutor David Roberts thanks the women who were "at the heart of this case":

    "They did not know each other, and each has carried what happened to them for almost two decades," he says.

    Giving evidence in the trial and being questioned about these "deeply traumatising, personal events" took extraordinary courage, he says.

    Roberts adds that, while today’s sentence "cannot undo the harm the victims have lived with", he hopes it brings them some measure of justice.

    The trial showed Brand to be a "manipulative sexual predator, using his growing fame and power to create situations in which he could pursue women sexually, regardless of what they wanted and with a complete disregard for the impact of his actions", Roberts says.

    He adds: "Today’s result shows that fame, status and the passage of time do not change the fundamental issue of consent.

    "No matter who the abuser is, or what the situation or circumstances are, we will always take allegations of rape and sexual assault seriously."

  7. Met Police detective thanks women for 'bravery, dignity and resilience'published at 14:55 GMT
    14:55 GMT

    We can now bring you a statement from Det Supt Andy Furphy, of the Metropolitan Police.

    He says the sentence is a "significant milestone", and means Brand has been held accountable for his crimes.

    Furphy says his thoughts are with the women who came forward and made the investigation possible, adding that they displayed "bravery, dignity and resilience" throughout the process.

    "The victims have had to revisit deeply traumatic experiences and endure significant public and media attention. I hope today’s outcome provides some measure of closure and allows them to continue rebuilding their lives," he says.

    Furphy adds: "From the very beginning, our investigation was never about Russell Brand’s profile, status or celebrity.

    "Our focus has always been on the women who reported these offences and on conducting a thorough and impartial investigation into the allegations they made."

    He ends with an appeal to anyone who has been a victim of rape or any other sexual offence. "If you come forward, you will be listened to, supported and treated with care and compassion," he says.

  11. A breakdown of Russell Brand's sentencepublished at 14:40 GMT
    14:40 GMT

    In total, Russell Brand has been sentenced to 10 years and six months.

    He has been sentenced to nine years' imprisonment for the one count of rape, which he will serve consecutively with the following three sentences:

    • Nine months for count three - sexually assaulting a woman whose breasts he grabbed at a bar in London in 2004
    • Eighteen months for count five - sexually assaulting a woman at the Choice FM studios in London in either 2004 or 2005, when he grabbed her face and kissed her before touching her breasts and bottom
    • Eight months for count seven - sexual assault while working on a Channel 4 project in 2009, when he laid his head into the groin area of a woman working with him without her consent

    These three sentences will all be served concurrently to one another - but consecutively to the rape sentence.

    This means the total sentence is one of 10 years and six months.

  12. Likely Brand will serve two-thirds of sentence in custodypublished at 14:36 GMT
    14:36 GMT

    Sima Kotecha
    Reporting from Southwark Crown Court

    Brand is likely to serve two-thirds of his sentence in custody according to the Sentencing Council.

    The council states offenders jailed for four years or more for some serious violent or sexual offences will be released at the two-thirds point.

    Once released, he is likely to spend the rest of his sentence on licence and will have to abide by specific rules - such as wearing a tag.

    His sentence will not be included in the new early release scheme. Sex offenders - along with murderers - are exempt from the scheme.
