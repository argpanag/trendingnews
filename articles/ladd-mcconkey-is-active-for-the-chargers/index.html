Ladd McConkey is active for the Chargers

TheTools · 11.10.2026 18:49 · source · permalink

Wide receiver Ladd McConkey will be available for the Chargers as they try to avoid an 0-5 start to the season on Sunday.

McConkey was listed as questionable to play with a foot injury on Friday’s injury report, but he avoided the inactive list for the home game against the Broncos. McConkey has 12 catches for 183 yards and a touchdown so far this season,

The Chargers ruled wide receiver Quentin Johnston, right tackle Joe Alt, cornerback Donte Jackson, guard Kayode Awosika, wide receiver Brenen Thompson, and safety Derwin James out on Friday. Cornerback Rodney Shelley is the only other inactive player.

Cornerbacks Pat Surtain II and Riley Moss were ruled out after being injured in Week 4. Quarterback Sam Ehlinger, defensive lineman Jordan Jackson, defensive lineman Tyler Onyedim, offensive lineman Kage Casey, and tight end Dallen Bentley are also out.
