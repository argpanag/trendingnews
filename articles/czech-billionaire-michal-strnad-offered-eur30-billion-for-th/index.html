Czech billionaire Michal Strnad offered EUR30 billion for the company owned by the Del Vecchio family | Межа

TheTools · 30.10.2026 19:21 · source · permalink

Delfin Sarl’s assets are valued at EUR40 billion, but Strnad is willing to acquire a controlling stake rather than the entire company.

Czech entrepreneur Michal Strnad offered EUR30 billion for the Italian holding company Delfin Sarl, which manages the Del Vecchio family’s assets. Bloomberg reported this, citing a letter dated October 20 that came into the agency’s possession.

This is reported by Інтерфакс-Україна.

Delfin Sarl’s assets are valued at EUR40 billion. Strnad said he would be willing to acquire a controlling stake of more than 62.5% in the company, rather than the entire company.
