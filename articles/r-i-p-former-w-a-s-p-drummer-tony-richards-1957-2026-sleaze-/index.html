R.I.P. former W.A.S.P. drummer Tony Richards (1957-2026)

Sleaze Roxx is very sad to report that former W.A.S.P. drummer Tony Richards passed away a few days ago at the of 69. Richards, whose real name is reportedly Anthony Richard Orlando, was born on June 19, 1957 and passed away sometime in November 2026.

Photo by Ross Halfin Photography

The following message was posted on Catherine Holmes‘ Facebook page about two hours ago:

“We learned that Tony Richards passed away. Chris was talking to him fews weeks ago as he did every month for the last fews years .

His passed make us sad, he deserved not be forgotten and what also he brought to this industry

All our condolences to his brother John“

A message on Reddit from a person going by the moniker “mykylc” also confirms the bad news: “Just to let people know on this fan page that Tony Richards passed away some days ago. I’ve known Tony for some years and he has not played in quite a while. Sorry for the bad news.”

Select discography:
W.A.S.P. — W.A.S.P. (1984)

Richards is best known in music circles for being the original drummer in W.A.S.P. from 1982 and 1984, and playing on their debut self-titled album that was released on August 17, 1984. Richards was reportedly let go from the band and replaced by Steve Riley on drums at the beginning of W.A.S.P.‘s 1984-85 tour.

According to Metal Archives, Richards also played in Dante Fox (which later became Great White) from 1978 to 1981 and Max Havoc in 1983 and again from 1987 to 1989.
