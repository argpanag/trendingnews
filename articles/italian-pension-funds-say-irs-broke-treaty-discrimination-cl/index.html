Italian Pension Funds Say IRS Broke Treaty Discrimination Clause

TheTools · 02.10.2026 19:31 · source · permalink

Two Italian pension funds are contesting the IRS’ attempts to reclaim nearly $1.8 million in previously paid refunds of US dividend withholding taxes in US Tax Court.

Fondo Pensione Alifond, an occupational pension fund serving Italy’s food industry workers, and Arca Previdenza Fondo Pensione Aperto Comparto Altacrescita, an open pension fund managed by Arca Fondi SGR S.p.A., each received IRS Notices of Deficiency on May 5, 2026, seeking to recover refunds the agency paid in 2023 for their 2019 tax years.
