Team news: Havertz, Saka and Odegaard return v Forest

TheTools · 18.10.2026 17:54 · source · permalink

Bukayo Saka, Kai Havertz and skipper Martin Odegaard are among five changes the boss has made to the team to face Nottingham Forest on Sunday.

Ben White and Riccardo Calafiori are the other two players to be recalled for the Premier League match at the City Ground, after neither started our midweek home win over Lille.

It means Eberechi Eze retains his place to make his first Premier League start of the season.

Martin Zubimendi has recovered from injury to take his place on the bench, and Jurrien Timber is also among the subs. If the Dutchman comes on in will be his 100th Gunners appearance.

Substitute goalkeeper Illan Meslier is also named in the squad as Kepa Arrizabalaga is ruled out with injury.

The hosts lost 3-1 away to Crystal Palace last weekend, and make two changes from that side - Morgan Gibbs-White misses out with injury while Ola Aina drops to the bench. In come Nicolas Dominguez and Jair Cunha. 

 

LINE-UPS

Arsenal: Raya, White, Gabriel, Konsa, Calafiori, Rice, Guimaraes, Odegaard, Eze, Saka, Havertz.

Subs: Meslier, Timber, Lewis-Skelly, Mosquera, Zubimendi, Merino, Madueke, Dowman, Gyokeres.

Nottingham Forest: Sels, Murillo, Diomande, Dominguez, Williams, Munoz, Schlager, McAtee, Cunha, Ndoye, Delap.

Subs: Victor, Sangare, Hudson-Odoi, Kalimuendo, Yates, Netz, Milenkovic, Aina, Wood.
