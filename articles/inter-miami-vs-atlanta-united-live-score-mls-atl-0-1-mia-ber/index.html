Welcome to Sportstar’s LIVE coverage of the MLS match between Atlanta United and Inter Miami on Saturday.

The Atlanta players were out on the field for some time, while the Miami players took a bit more time. Atlanta gets the ball rolling as soon as all of the Miami players were on. Suarez protest for a second, but the game goes on.

That goal was the last bit of play from the half. Despite not dominating the ball in the first period, it is Miami has its nose just ahead. See you on the other side.

And it’s Miami which takes the lead! Berterame heads home! Messi flicks it towards Suarez with a header. The forward then shows his strength to stave off his marker, turn, and lift the ball towards Berterame, who rushes onto the ball and heads it into the back of the net.

Messi goes for goal, and sends the ball to the top-right corner. But the host custodian sees it all the way and punches it away to safety.

It’s Messi who is fouled by Baez, looks like, at the edge of the box. It’s peak Messi freekick distance. Can he do it for the nth time?

Miami still very nervy, giving the ball away in dangerous areas. As I type this, Atlanta launches another attack, most of them coming through the right. A cutback comes and an Atlanta player gets to it, but the touch is too heavy.

Too much on that pass from Almiron as the ball is collected by Miami ‘keeper St. Clair.

Embolo brought down by Reguilon on the right flank. A freekick for the host. Almiron stands over the dead ball.

A cross comes in from the right. Straight to Muyumba. And the midfielder goes for an overhead kick. Decent contact but lukewarm effort and wide of the target.

Miami had a good minute or so keeping the ball. A stat for you. Almiron has had more touches, 16, than Messi, 12. Well, that was expected. Remember how Messi was pulling the strings at the World Cup despite walking most of the time? Still early days to rule out the famed No. 10 from doing something special.

The host might be having lesser of the possession pie, 40 per cent, but the passing has been smooth. Miami still hasn’t looked its best, choppy in transition and seemed to stutter in the final third. The host, meanwhile, has looked confident in and around the Miami box, pinging in crosses inside and asking questions of the visiting backline.

Messi fouls Almiron in the middle of the park as Atlanta builds from the back once more. That is what the host has been doing for much of the opening 20 minutes or so. Miami, meanwhile, is waiting to hit on the counter.

Aleksey Miranchuk steals the ball right at the edge of the Miami box and drops his shoulders. But Ayala, looks like, swoops in and steals the ball back to recover well for the Herons.

And that was almost a counter! It just took one splitting pass to release a Miami player on the right. But Atlanta defence does well to cut that move at the bud.

The host is employing a press. Only two of its players, its centre-backs, are in its own half. Rest of the players are inside Miami’s half. But will this exposed the host to counter-attacks?

Not sure whether Miguel Almiron was onside, but he ran behind the defence to get at the end of a lofted pass. Then, he cut it back for Breel Embolo, whose first shot wasn’t connected clean. He gets a second bite at it, but then too, the Miami ‘keeper isn’t troubled.

The Herons, clad in black, comfortable on the ball. Sergio Reguilon tries to whip a cross in from the left flank. The former Premier League player asks for a corner but the referee says its a goal kick for the host.

First time Messi in the final third. He looks to play a one-two with Suarez. How many times have we heard that, huh? But the Uruguayan is marked well and Atlanta retrieves the ball.

The ball gets rolling and its the visitor, Inter Miami’s, Luis Suarez who gets us going. The Herons, led by talisman Lionel Messi, attacking from right to left.
