AMD Must Deliver a Powerful Earnings Beat to Keep Its AI Rally Alive

TheTools · 03.11.2026 21:46 · source · permalink
  • AMD

This article first appeared on GuruFocus.

Advanced Micro Devices (NASDAQ:AMD) heads into its third-quarter earnings report Tuesday with Wall Street looking for another quarter of rapid sales growth, driven by demand for artificial intelligence chips and server processors.

Analysts expect revenue of approximately $13.08 billion to $13.09 billion, up about 41% from $9.25 billion a year earlier. Adjusted earnings per share are forecast at $1.88 to $1.95, compared with roughly $0.97 to $1.20 in the year-ago period. AMD is scheduled to release results after the market closes on Nov. 3.

Data center operations are likely to remain a major focus. The segment generated $6.7 billion in second-quarter revenue, nearly double its level a year earlier, supported by EPYC server processors and Instinct AI accelerators. Management previously projected a non-GAAP gross margin of about 56%.

However, the report may matter as much for what comes next as for the latest quarter. Investors will examine fourth-quarter revenue guidance and margin expectations for clues about AI infrastructure spending and demand. AMD's shares have also experienced volatility ahead of the results amid weakness across technology stocks.
