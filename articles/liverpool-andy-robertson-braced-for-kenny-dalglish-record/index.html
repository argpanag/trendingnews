Liverpool: Andy Robertson braced for Kenny Dalglish record

TheTools · 12.11.2026 09:29 · source · permalink
  • Published
    1 hour ago

Andy Robertson says it gives him "goosebumps" any time he thinks about it.

Friday at Hampden, North Macedonia the visitors, a 102nd cap to be won and, when it is, he'll be level in the pantheon with Kenny Dalglish, the football man he loves more than any other.

On Monday in St Gallen, Switzerland - all being well - Robertson will break the great man's record, a mark that has stood for 40 years.

The depth of feeling comes tumbling out of him when he starts telling stories about what King Kenny means to his family and how he seemingly created a miracle by momentarily curing Robertson's beloved late grandfather's hearing.

"I always remember my agent talking to me about the first time he met Liverpool," Robertson says of a time when he was still with Hull but about to move to Anfield.

"He met the chief scout and sporting director and all of a sudden Kenny 'accidentally' walked into the room. It was 100% planned, I know it was planned.

"One of the things right at the top of the list for me was the fact [by joining Liverpool] I got to meet Kenny in person and I've actually got to have a relationship with him and his family, because his family are special.

"My dad, in particular, has met people in football through me, but I've never seen him being starstruck by anyone apart from Kenny.

"I always remember, we'd just beaten Barcelona 4-0 [in May, 2019] to qualify for my second Champions League final. My grandad was at the game and I managed to grab Kenny afterwards. I says, 'Kenny, would you mind coming in to see my grandad and my uncle? They would love to see you'.

"Bear in mind his grandson's just qualified for another Champions League final and he basically barged me out of the way and shook Kenny's hand.

"My grandad had hearing aids. He couldn't really hear much of us but he heard everything Kenny said, so it did open a lot of questions in the family as to how bad the ear problem was with my grandad. He's no longer with us but to see how happy he was… Kenny was his hero."
