Tom Jones confirms 2027 tour after overcoming health scare

TheTools · 16.11.2026 17:58 · source · permalink

Tom Jones has confirmed his first live shows for 2027 after overcoming a recent health scare while on tour in North America.

Just weeks ago, Jones was forced to reschedule two concerts in California, which later took place earlier this month, due to medical advice.

Upon returning to the stage at the Greek Theatre in Los Angeles on November 4th, Jones told fans that he spent a week at the Ronald Regan UCLA Medical Center due to a severe bout of flu.

“[I thought] Oh well, this is it, you’ve had a wonderful life, Tommy, but it’s over right now,” he admitted to thinking in the hospital while on stage.

After praising the “wonderful” staff at the hospital, he added, “[The medical staff asked] What do you want to do now? I was in there for a week, and I said, ‘I’ve got to get back on the stage’. This is where I belong. I can’t live without this, believe me.”

Now that he’s back doing what he does best, Jones, aged 86, is looking forward to touring once again, writing alongside the announcement of his first 2027 shows on X, “Thrilled to say I will be touring in 2027, the first shows have been announced, head to my website for all the details.”

The Welsh icon also promised more shows would be revealed in due course, adding, “Keep your eyes peeled for more show announcements soon. See you along the road!”

His summer UK tour is set to kick off on May 28th at the LNER Community Stadium in York, which he will follow up with a return to home soil on May 30th at Cardiff Castle.

In June, Jones is set to perform at the Hampton Court Palace Festival in London, Pier Head in Liverpool, Lincoln Castle, Llangollen Pavilion, and at Blenheim Palace.

The following month will see Jones perform at the Electric Bay in Torquay on July 8th, before next taking to the stage on July 11th at the complete other side of the country at Exhibition Park in Newcastle. The final tour date announced as part of the first batch will see Jones headline On The Beach in Brighton on July 18th.

Tickets for the new shows are set to go on general sale at 10am on November 20th following a series of pre-sales.
