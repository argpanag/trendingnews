Tip of the Day: Goals as Bees get physical to stop beanpole Barry

TheTools · 23.11.2026 11:37 · source · permalink

The MNF clash between Brentford and Everton is the focus of Monday's Tip of the Day

Last Update:

Monday’s Tip of the Day focuses on the final game in the 11th round of the Premier League.

Everton head to the Gtech Community Stadium tonight to face Brentford with both teams knowing a victory would propel them to the fringes of the top four.

The Toffees have the better form with three successive wins in all competitions, although two were against a poor Newcastle side. They also have one of the top-flight’s in-form marksmen in Thierno Barry. The lanky striker has scored six goals in 10 games this season.

Brentford will be without their main attacking threat with Igor Thiago suspended following his red card at Brighton last timeout.

The Bees have won just one of their last six in league and cup and will be eager to kick-start their season.

We fancy both teams to score and for the Brentford defence to get physical in a bid to stop Barry, who has been fouled 10 times in his last five games.
