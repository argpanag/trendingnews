Adeyemi, Schade and Frankurt duo destined for Germany call-ups

TheTools · 16.09.2026 22:48 · source · permalink

After it was announced that Said El Mala will receive a German national team call up, four other attackers are destined for nomination.

According to Sky Germany, the Eintracht Frankfurt duo Jonathan Burkardt and Younes Ebnoutalib are among those to be included.

New national team head coach Jürgen Klopp recently visited Frankfurt's training grounds to take a look at both strikers up close.

Ebnoutalib and Burkardt have started the Bundesliga season with 3 goals and 2 goals scored in their opening 3 games respectively.

According to the pay-TV channel, a strong start to the season for Karim Adeyemi at FC Barcelona will also be rewarded with a call-up.

Finally, Kevin Schade of Brentford, who has scored three goals in the Premier League, is set to be part of Klopp's Nations League plans.
