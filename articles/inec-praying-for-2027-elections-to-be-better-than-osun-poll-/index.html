INEC praying for 2027 elections to be better than Osun poll: Amupitan

TheTools · 28.11.2026 06:19 · source · permalink

INEC says it is determined to surpass the successes recorded in recent off-cycle elections in conducting the 2027 elections.

The Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) says it is making efforts to surpass the successes recorded in recent off-cycle elections in conducting the 2027 elections.

INEC chairman, Joash Amupitan, gave the assurance on Friday during a visit to the Gbong Gwom Jos, Da Jacob Buba, at his palace in Jos.

Mr Amupitan expressed the commission’s commitment to conducting credible elections that would strengthen national unity and deepen public confidence in Nigeria’s electoral process.

He said all elections conducted since he assumed office in October 2025 had been conclusive and successful, without post-election violence.

Mr Amupitan identified the Anambra, Ekiti and Osun governorship elections, alongside the Federal Capital Territory area council elections, as evidence of progressive improvements.

He described the Osun governorship election as particularly successful, citing its impressive voter turnout.

“So our prayer, our plan, our determination and desire is that the general elections will be more successful than the Osun governorship election. It will be an election that will further unite Nigeria and all of us,” he said.

Mr Amupitan also announced that INEC would conduct nationwide mock accreditation on Saturday across 17,656 polling units, comprising two polling units per ward.

He said the exercise would test the efficiency of the Bimodal Voter Accreditation System (BVAS) and the simultaneous transmission of results to the INEC Result Viewing portal. According to him, the exercise is aimed at preventing a recurrence of the technical glitches experienced during the 2023 presidential election.

Mr Amupitan appealed to traditional rulers to mobilise eligible voters to participate actively in the mock accreditation and the general elections. He also sought their support in combating misinformation and disinformation ahead of the elections.

The chairman warned that malicious actors could deploy AI and deepfake videos to mislead citizens and suppress voter turnout. He urged Nigerians to verify information circulating on social media before acting on it.

“We want our people to come out and vote in this election. Your voice is very weighty, not just in Jos, not just in Plateau, but all over the country,” he said.

Mr Amupitan described his visit as a homecoming, saying it was also to formally notify the traditional ruler of the election dates and seek his support.

Mr Buba, chairman of the Plateau State Council of Chiefs and Emirs, expressed confidence in Amupitan’s integrity and capacity to deliver credible elections.

The traditional ruler urged the INEC chairman to remain steadfast in discharging his responsibilities, irrespective of political pressure, criticism and threats.

“Even with the noise, the attacks, as long as you convince yourself you’re doing the right thing, you will always have the strength to move ahead,” he said.

Mr Buba reaffirmed traditional rulers’ commitment to mobilising eligible citizens to register and participate in elections. He assured INEC of the council’s continued support and prayed for divine wisdom and strength for Amupitan to discharge his responsibilities effectively.

(NAN)
