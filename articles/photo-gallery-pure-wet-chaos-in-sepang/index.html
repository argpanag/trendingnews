Photo Gallery: Pure wet chaos in Sepang

TheTools · 04.10.2026 16:28 · source · permalink

Heavy rain, a delayed race and a "significant amount of standing water" on the track.

It's pure wet chaos at the 2026 Formula One Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia at the Sepang International Circuit (SIC) -- but the ecstatic fans are enjoying every bit of the excitement.

Over 100,000 fans turned up to catch the final day of action. The race started at 4.35pm.
