Rebecca Ferguson’s Sci-Fi Series Officially Returns to Apple TV’s Global Top 10

TheTools · 19.10.2026 00:15 · source · permalink

If you’re going to spend years trapped underground with 10,000 other people, it helps to have a decent mystery to keep everyone occupied. Murder, conspiracies, forbidden questions about the outside world, and a government that really doesn’t want you looking out the window have certainly done the trick for one of Apple TV’s biggest sci-fi shows, which has just wrapped another season without losing its grip on streaming audiences.

Silo is officially back among streaming’s biggest shows, with Rebecca Ferguson’s (Dune) dystopian sci-fi series continuing to land in the top 10 on Apple TV following the release of its Season 3 finale last month. Based on Hugh Howey’s bestselling trilogy, Silo follows Juliette Nichols, an engineer living among the 10,000 inhabitants of a gigantic underground structure where humanity has supposedly taken refuge from a deadly outside world. The first two seasons gradually exposed the secrets surrounding the silos and their creation, while Season 3 expanded the story considerably by exploring both Juliette’s continuing struggle and events hundreds of years earlier that helped create the world she now inhabits.

The cast is led by Ferguson as Juliette alongside Common (John Wick: Chapter 2) as Robert Sims, Chinaza Uche (Dickinson) as Paul Billings, Tim Robbins (The Shawshank Redemption) as Bernard Holland, Harriet Walter (Succession) as Martha Walker, Avi Nash (The Walking Dead) as Lukas Kyle, Jessica Henwick (The Matrix Resurrections) as Helen Drew, and Ashley Zukerman (Succession) as Daniel Keene.

What Can We Expect From 'Silo' Season 4?

When the two timelines come spiraling together in the most stunning of Season 3 climaxes, the final season will have the unenviable but incredible task of bringing the mystery to a close in a satisfying ending. We've been building for years towards answers about why humanity was forced underground, who created the silos, and what really happened to the world outside, and with the explosive final two episodes bringing it all to a head, we can't wait to see what's going to happen in the last ten episodes. A sneak peek at Season 4, which premieres next July, has given us a look at what's to come.

Silo creator Graham Yost spoke to Collider in the wake of the shocking finale. He was asked what questions we should be asking heading into Season 4:

"Why. I think that's the big question. Why? And Bernard had an answer earlier on, I think in Episode 8, where Juliette says, 'We're going to risk everyone's lives to do this?' And he says, 'Well, if we cap the Safeguard, then we can have a conversation. If they convince us of everything they're doing,' he says, 'We'll uncap the pipe, and we'll drink the waters of oblivion if their reasons are good. But we have to be part of the conversation. We have to be told. We can't just be ignorant and say, ‘Okay, kill us if you want.’' So, what's the why? That's the big question of Season 4, and a big part of the end of Season 3 is to set that up, set up that here comes war."
