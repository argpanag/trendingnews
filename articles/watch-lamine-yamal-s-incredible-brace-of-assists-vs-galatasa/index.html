Watch: Lamine Yamal's incredible brace of assists vs Galatasaray - Get Spanish Football News

TheTools · 13.10.2026 20:36 · source · permalink

Barcelona star Lamine Yamal conjured up a pair of nothing short of wonderful assists in the space of mere minutes on Tuesday evening. This came as Hansi Flick’s troops drove home their advantage against Galatasaray.

Barcelona are of course currently locked in action on the continent, having made the trip to Istanbul for a League Phase clash in the Champions League.

After falling into an early deficit, the Blaugrana have since turned the fixture on its head, courtesy of goals on the part of all of João Cancelo, Fermín López, Anthony Gordon and Karim Adeyemi.

As alluded to above, though, it is another member of Flick’s attacking ranks who has truly stolen the show at RAMS Park…

After enduring an uncharacteristically quiet first-half, Lamine Yamal exploded into life on the other side of the interval. The 19-year-old first laid on Fermín López’s go-ahead goal, courtesy of a sumptuous left-footed delivery:
