Cooks runs over KC with 32/117/1 line in Week 12

    Cooks runs over KC with 32/117/1 line in Week 12

    James Cook

    BUF Running Back #4
    Player Stats

    James Cook rushed 32 times for 117 yards and a touchdown in the Bills’ Week 12 win over the Chiefs.

    Sportsbooks correctly cratered the total of this game throughout the week to account for gusting winds, pouring rain, and a chilly temperature. Joe Brady’s response to these miserable conditions was to feed his top back for all four quarters. Cook set a season-high in carries and scored his 10th straight rushing touchdown. Buffalo’s lead back didn’t score in Week 1 and has found the end zone in every subsequent game. Cook will be a good bet to find the end zone at least once when he takes on the downtrodden Patriots next week. Per usual, Cook will be a top-five fantasy option.
    - Kyle Dvorchak
