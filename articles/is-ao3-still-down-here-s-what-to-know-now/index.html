AO3, which stands for Archive of Our Own, is a nonprofit, open-source vault for over 18 million pieces of fanfiction and other types of fanworks. And sadly, it's been down for weeks.

The Organization for Transformative Works, the nonprofit behind AO3, announced on Oct. 29 that the site would be down for 15 hours for maintenance. However, in their announcement, they later increased it to two to four weeks and identified evidence of a security incident on the website, but confirmed none of users' data had been deleted.

As users hit two weeks without their favorite fanfiction, they are taking to social media to air out their frustration and longing for the website.

"I used to open AO3 like it was the morning paper," one X user joked.

"happy 2 weeks of ao3 being down surely it will be back any day now ... right?" Another chimed in recently.

"guys how much longer is ao3 gonna be down? my life feels so empty without ao3," one posted.

What is the current status of AO3? See latest update

As of Nov. 12, AO3 is still down. The last update from the Organization for Transformative Works was on Nov. 5, when they said the maintenance was going well and would not take longer than estimated.

Why is AO3 down in the first place?

Initially, AO3 was down for service restoration, saying they were "hopeful that we will be able to bring the site back up in a degraded state sooner."

Now, according to CNN, the archive has also gone offline due to a site security breach that is still under investigation. The outlet noted AO3 doesn't collect users' personal information, so it seemed less likely to be targeted for a break-in.
