English soccer club sorry for launching ‘United 93’ clothing collection around 9/11 anniversary | CNN

TheTools · 18.09.2026 15:19 · source · permalink

English soccer club Oxford United has apologized for releasing US-style apparel with “United 93” on the back around the 25th anniversary of the 9/11 attacks, saying the clothing range was launched “without understanding of the historical context and associations.”

The team, which plays in England’s third tier, said Friday it has removed the collection with immediate effect and was reviewing its approval processes.

Oxford United was founded in 1893, initially as Headington FC before a name change in 1960.

United Airlines Flight 93 was one of the four planes hijacked on September 11, 2001, during the attacks that brought down the World Trade Center’s twin towers and blew a hole in the Pentagon. The plane was headed for Washington, officials have concluded, but the aircraft went down in Pennsylvania after passengers and crew members stormed the cockpit and fought the hijackers.

In a club statement, Oxford United said it wanted to “sincerely apologize” for the launch of its Campus Collection, which, according to local media, featured an American football jersey and a baseball jersey.

“The range was released without understanding of the historical context and associations,” the statement read. “It should not have reached the point of sale and we take full responsibility for not undertaking proper due diligence.

“We have removed the collection with immediate effect and are reviewing our approval processes to ensure nothing like this happens again.”

The page announcing the launch of the Campus Collection has been deleted.
