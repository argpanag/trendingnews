Usman Nurmagomedov slammed by fellow PFL star Dakota Ditcheva after alleged attack on Merab Dvalishvili

TheTools · 11.11.2026 18:33 · source · permalink

Dakota Ditcheva is not happy with one of her promotion’s champions.

Usman Nurmagomedov recently entered free agency, but he is still listed as the current PFL lightweight champion.

While his future and a potential UFC move have been the main talking points ever since he knocked out Archie Colgan to complete his contract, Nurmagomedov is making headlines for a different reason this week.

On Wednesday, Merab Dvalishvili accused a group of Dagestanis of attacking him and a teammate in Abu Dhabi, and Ditcheva is among those strongly condemning the alleged assault.

Before Nurmagomedov fought out his deal in August, he and Ditcheva were widely reported to be the two highest-paid athletes on the PFL roster.

The Russian has been expected to cross over to the UFC, but the leading MMA promotion may pause their plan to sign the cousin of Khabib Nurmagomedov if recent allegations are accurate.

Dvalishvili claims Usman attacked him alongside bantamweight star Umar Nurmagomedov and multiple others in the Dagestani team.

Ditcheva, Mike Perry, and a number of others reacted in the comments section.

“And we call ourselves ‘professional athletes’,” Ditcheva wrote, alongside a facepalm emoji.

The former UFC champion showed the injuries he and a 21-year-old wrestler sustained in the alleged assault, which happened in Abu Dhabi ahead of this weekend’s PFL and Real American Freestyle events.

Usman and Khabib Nurmagomedov are in the Middle East to support their teammates, as well as cousin Umar, who will be facing Urijah Faber at RAF Abu Dhabi.
