Sreenidi Deccan FC vs Chanmari FC  Preview: Predicted Lineups, Team News | IFL 2026-27

TheTools · 24.11.2026 08:56 · source · permalink

Indian Football League

Deccan Arena is set to welcome the Aizawl-based club

Sreenidi Deccan FC, the Indian Football League (IFL) side that has gathered its team in a great manner are set to play all out in their next tie when their fortress, Deccan Arena, will host Chanmari FC on Tuesday, 24th November, 2026, from 18:30 IST.

Along with Gokulam Kerala FC, the table toppers from the IFL 2026-27 season, the Aizawl-based club Chanmari FC are the one that remains unbeaten in the league, with two wins and one draw in their three league phase fixtures till now Chanmari side are now set to play their next game against Sreenidi Deccan FC in an away clash.

Talking about Sreenidi Deccan FC, they are one of the most expensive sides on the table with Gokulam Kerala FC, as both of the sides have spent a good amount of money this season, and results have paid off for only Gokulam side, while Sreenidi trail behind sitting seventh on the points table as the upcoming game at home over Chanmari FC will be much needed for the southern side to pull off a win to secure all three points.

The Stakes

Sreenidi Deccan FC

A win is what Sreenidi Deccan FC needed right now after three back-to-back games where they didn’t secure the victorious three points from the tie, as their previous game against Bengaluru United FC ended in a 2-2 draw, and before that they played a howler over Shillong Lajong, where the mountain side punished them 2-1.

Chanmari FC, being the opponent of the Deccan side, turned on their ultimate potential with some great players, as they have spent a lot of money to build the side. Having strengthened their squad with experienced additions such as Pritam Kotal, Pronay Halder and Nishu Kumar, Sreenidi entered the campaign with clear promotion ambitions, but the results are still not here, so a proper commanding win over the visitors will surely boost their team morale.

Chanmari FC

The Mizoram outfit were at the summit of the IFL table last season for the first few match weeks before their winning momentum got derailed.

This time around, they kicked off their season with a 1-1 draw against reigning runners-up Shillong Lajong, then got the thrashing win against Dempo SC, and with the win against the Golden Eagles, they might be buzzing with confidence when they face the hosts, Sreenidi Deccan, at their helm after they punished Rajasthan United 4-1 in the previous game.

Against Sreenidi Deccan, Chanmari FC need to engage their attacking transitions into the play to score as many goals as they can to outclass the hosts by numbers, as a win in the upcoming game will accelerate and extend the unbeaten run of the Aizawl-based side.

There is no news of any injury concerns from either side, and both coaches will have a fully fit and available side to pick their best eleven.
