Tributes are pouring in for William Jervis after the Grimsby sports student earned dual titles in Scotland

  • By NOTICEBOARD
  • •11 November 2026

A talented sports student is celebrating an extraordinary international victory after claiming two gold medals while representing England in Scotland. William Jervis dominated the tatami at the 12th Commonwealth Karate Championships in Glasgow, securing a remarkable double triumph on the international stage.

The Level 1 Sport student at Grimsby Institute took top honours in the men's Para K22 category before going on to claim a second gold at the Club Championships. The tournament was staged at Glasgow International Arena from Thursday, November 5 to Sunday, November 8, bringing together top martial artists from across the Commonwealth.

The victory follows an impressive competitive rise, coming just over a year after William represented England at the 2025 Senior European Karate Championships in Armenia. His latest achievement reflects countless hours of rigorous preparation balanced alongside his coursework at the college campus.

His mum, Clare Jarvis, said: "We couldn't be prouder of William. Watching him step onto the tatami at the Commonwealth Karate Championships and come away with two gold medals across both the main event and the club championships is a moment we will treasure forever.

"He has worked so hard for this, showing incredible dedication, resilience, and independence throughout the entire competition. Seeing him thrive alongside his teammates and represent his country and club with such maturity is everything a parent could wish for."

College leaders and classmates have united in praising the young athlete for showcasing regional talent on a major stage. His achievements serve as a powerful inspiration for aspiring sports students throughout the borough and neighbouring districts.

Jonathan Davy, sport programme leader at Grimsby Institute, said: "Everyone at Grimsby Institute is incredibly proud of William and what he has achieved in Glasgow. To represent your country at this level is an incredible achievement in itself, but to come home with two gold medals is something really special.

"William's success is a fantastic example of what young people from Grimsby and the surrounding area can achieve when they are given the opportunity to pursue their ambitions. We're delighted to be able to celebrate this achievement with him and his family."

The martial arts standout now looks forward to building on his international momentum as he continues his academic studies. His latest double gold haul has firmly put his hometown on the global karate map.
