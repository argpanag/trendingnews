Barcelona vs Real Madrid: Preview

The first El Clásico of the 2026/27 La Liga season can't come quickly enough for Barcelona, who will be relishing the chance to strengthen their grip on the title race at the expense of Real Madrid, led by their arch-nemesis Jose Mourinho.

Hansi Flick's side have been in unstoppable form in the league this term, winning nine games in a row and scoring 39 goals. If they beat Madrid, they will break their rivals' record for the most victories at the start of a La Liga season, set way back in 1968/69.

It would also put Barca in pole position for a third successive domestic title, having sealed the 2025/26 crown with a 2-0 win over Los Blancos at the Nou Camp in May, but they will need to bounce back from a defeat for the first time this term after losing 4-2 at Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League on Tuesday.

There was no shame in their result against the back-to-back European champions, but another defeat would be hugely disappointing, especially considering the opposition. They will be without the injured Dani Olmo, Eric Garcia and Andreas Christensen, while Jules Kounde is doubtful.

Jose Mourinho returns to the Nou Camp as Real Madrid manager for the first time since 2013, when he ended a fiery three-year spell in the Spanish capital that featured several blockbuster battles with Pep Guardiola's Barca.

The Portuguese has always loved beating the club who he served as an assistant coach in the late 1990s and started the mind games earlier this week, complaining that his side had 24 hours less than their rivals to prepare following their 4-1 win over RB Leipzig in the Champions League on Wednesday.

That was Madrid's fourth successive win and the third game in a row in which they have scored four goals, but they will be without suspended Brazilian superstar Vinicius Junior at the Nou Camp. Rodrygo, Raul Asencio and Ferland Mendy are also absent through injury.

The all-time head-to-head record between these two sides is remarkably close, with Madrid winning 107 games to Barca's 106 and 51 matches ending level.

Barcelona vs Real Madrid: Need to Know

What time is kick-off in El Clásico?

Kick-off arrives at 8:00pm GMT in the UK. That is 4:00pm ET and 1:00pm PT in America and 7:00am AEDT in Australia.

Who is the referee for El Clásico?

Juan Martínez Munuera will take charge of tonight’s El Clásico, having issued 15 yellow cards and awarded three penalties across five matches this season.

This will be his fifth Clásico, with Real Madrid winning three of the previous four he has officiated. Barcelona have also previously lodged a complaint with the RFEF over his decisions in their Copa del Rey clash against Atlético Madrid in February.
