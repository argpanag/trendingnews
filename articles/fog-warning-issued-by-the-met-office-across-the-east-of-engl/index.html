Fog warning issued by the Met Office across the East of England

TheTools · 02.11.2026 07:38 · source · permalink

People have been warned travel is likely to be disrupted due to fog across parts of the East of England.

A yellow weather warning has been put in place until 10:00 GMT, the Met Office said.

Fog might be dense in some areas and is expected to cause slower journey times with delays to bus and train services and flight issues, it added.

The Met Office urged drivers to check road conditions and to allow extra time for journeys.

It is the second warning in two days.

The warning covers parts of Bedfordshire, Buckinghamshire, Cambridgeshire, Peterborough, Hertfordshire, Norfolk and Northamptonshire.
