Limited playing time opens the door for Brahim Díaz's… | YSscores

TheTools · 18.10.2026 12:54 · source · permalink

Brahim Díaz's future with Real Madrid is in question due to his limited appearances with the royal team since the start of the current season. This situation may lead him to consider a new experience during the upcoming winter transfer window.

Spanish newspaper 'Marca' has revealed that Díaz's situation at the Madrid club could make him open to exploring departure options in January, in search of a greater opportunity for participation and getting playing minutes that would help him maintain his fitness and develop his level.

According to the same source, the Moroccan player has only participated for 178 minutes since the start of the season, indicating a decline in his presence in the coach's plans, especially with his absence from Real Madrid matches since September 15.

Brahim Díaz's absence from participation raises questions about his status within the team, especially given the strong competition for attacking positions, which makes securing a starting spot a difficult task.

As the winter transfer window approaches, the player may consider leaving Real Madrid if the situation remains unchanged, in an attempt to join a team that offers him regular playing opportunities and a chance to regain match rhythm.

However, the departure of the Moroccan international remains a possibility in January, with no confirmation regarding a final decision in this direction, as the next phase of developments regarding his role within Real Madrid is awaited.
