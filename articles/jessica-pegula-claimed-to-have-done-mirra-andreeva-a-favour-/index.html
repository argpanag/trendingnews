Jessica Pegula claimed to have done Mirra Andreeva a favour as she storms through at the WTA Finals

TheTools · 15.11.2026 21:33 · source · permalink

Mirra Andreeva has managed to have the last laugh in her intriguing drama with Jessica Pegula at the WTA Finals.

Andreeva battled past Pegula in the semifinals of the year-end tournament in Indian Wells, where the American had been caught badmouthing her Russian counterpart. Pegula called Andreeva a “diva” and said she needed to be “put in her place” during a practice session at the WTA Finals, having been unaware that her audio was being recorded. The footage has since been deleted.

Andreeva and Pegula poked fun at the exchange shortly afterwards, before going head-to-head on court for the first time in their WTA careers. Former player Rennae Stubbs has now shared her thoughts on the situation, suggesting that Pegula has done Andreeva a favour with her controversial comments.

Was Jessica Pegula out of line with what she said about Mirra Andreeva? 😳

Jessica Pegula claimed to have done Mirra Andreeva a favour at the WTA Finals

Taking to social media, former doubles star Stubbs posted on X: “I think Jessica Pegula did Mirra a favour by making those comments last week. It was a wake-up call by a peer and it may have just been the catalyst to make her the best player in the world! Because she has been very un-diva-like this week! Head down, great attitude & crushing!”

It’s an interesting take from Stubbs, who like many others has been left really impressed by Andreeva at Indian Wells. The 19-year-old beat Elina Svitolina, Aryna Sabalenka and Diana Shnaider to top her WTA Finals group before overcoming Pegula to reach the final.

And while Pegula’s comments may have motivated her somewhat, the Russian deserves all the credit for her stunning run so far. She now faces a reunion with Svitolina, who is into the final after finishing runner-up in their group before a semifinal win over Linda Noskova.

Despite winning their recent tie in Indian Wells, Andreeva will be taking nothing for granted against Svitolina, who won the WTA Finals in 2018. Moreover, the Ukrainian has just claimed her 11th top 10 win of the 2026 season, more than any other player and equaling her career-high. She has just surpassed world number one Elena Rybakina in that regard for 2026, which should give her a huge confidence boost for her upcoming meeting with Andreeva.
