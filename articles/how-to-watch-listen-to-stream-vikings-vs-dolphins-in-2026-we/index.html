How to Watch, Listen to & Stream Vikings vs. Dolphins in 2026 Week 4 Game

TheTools · 04.10.2026 22:57 · source · permalink

EAGAN, Minn. — The Vikings (3-0) are hosting the Miami Dolphins (0-3) at 3:05 p.m. (CT) Sunday, Oct. 4.

This will be the 15th official meeting between the franchises and Minnesota's annual Crucial Catch home game in support of all who have been affected by cancer.

The contest will be broadcast to pockets of the country by FOX.

BROADCAST TV

MAP KEY: Dolphins at Vikings (orange areas); Rams at Eagles (red areas); Cowboys at Texans (blue areas); Jets at Bears (yellow areas); Packers at Buccaneers (green areas)

FOX (KMSP in the Twin Cities market) will televise the game.

Kickoff: 3:05 p.m. (CT)

Play-by-Play: Kevin Kugler

Analyst: Daryl Johnston

Sideline reporter: Allison Williams

PREGAME

Vikings GameDay Live will air on FOX 9 in the Twin Cities market from 9:30 a.m. to 11 a.m. (CT).

VIKINGS AUDIO NETWORK

KFAN (100.3-FM), KTLK 1130-AM and the five-state Vikings Audio Network with 70 affiliates across the Upper Midwest will provide audio coverage.

SPANISH AUDIO
