US government restricts activities in Nogales due to threat

TheTools · 07.09.2026 00:30 · source · permalink

NOGALES, MEXICO (AZFamily) — United States government activities in Nogales have been restricted “due to threat information,” according to the U.S. Consulate in Nogales.

Government officials will only travel to Nogales for “essential movement” during daylight hours, officials said.

Information about the specific threat was not given, but officials reminded citizens that Sonora is currently a Level 3 “Reconsider Travel” area.

Officials said if you are traveling to Nogales, use increased caution and be aware of your surroundings. Seek shelter if there is any security incident and notify friends and family of your safety.

U.S. citizens can receive alerts from the Embassy via WhatsApp and can sign up here.
