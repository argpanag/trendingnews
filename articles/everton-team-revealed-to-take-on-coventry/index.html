Everton Team Revealed To Take On Coventry

TheTools · 07.11.2026 02:40 · source · permalink

Men

7 hours ago

By Everton

Share

Tyler Dibling comes into the starting XI as the only change for Everton's clash against Coventry at Hill Dickinson Stadium (8pm GMT).

Manager David Moyes – taking charge of the Blues for a record-equalling 592nd time to go level with Harry Catterick – gives the nod to Dibling on the right wing, with Merlin Röhl dropping to right-back in place of Jake O'Brien, who is named among the substitutes.

The rest of the Toffees' team remains the same from the 1-0 win at Newcastle United on Monday night, as Moyes' men bid to record a third victory on the trot ahead of the international break.

Jarrad Branthwaite – named in the England squad alongside Jordan Pickford and James Garner for upcoming matches against Croatia – once again partners captain James Tarkowski in central defence, alongside left-back Vitalii Mykolenko and keeper Pickford.

Garner joins Harrison Armstrong and Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall in central midfield, flanked by wingers Tyrique George and Dibling, with striker Thierno Barry looking to add to his tally of 10 goal involvements for the season so far.

Everton XI: Pickford, Röhl, Tarkowski (c), Branthwaite, Mykolenko, Garner, Armstrong, Dewsbury-Hall, Dibling, George, Barry.

Subs: Travers, Maitland-Niles, Keane, O'Brien, Johnson, Nørgaard, Alcaraz, Hackney, Graham.
