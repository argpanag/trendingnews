When is Costco’s Black Friday sale?

Costco’s Black Friday ad has already been released, giving shoppers a look at the retailer’s current and upcoming discounts. Some promotions are already available, and some have already expired. If you see something you’ve been wanting at a price you’re happy with, it’s worth buying it now before the deal expires or the product sells out.

Are Costco deals available online or only in-store?

Many Costco Black Friday deals are available both online and in stores, though some promotions are exclusive to one channel. The ad scan clearly notes whether an item is online-only, warehouse-only or available both ways, so it’s worth checking before making the unnecessary trip.

Do I need to be a Costco member to shop its Black Friday sale?

Yes. A Costco membership is required to shop in warehouses and to receive member pricing online. Some products may be available to nonmembers with an additional surcharge, but the best savings are typically reserved for members.

Should I shop Black Friday deals at Costco or other retailers?

Costco is one of the best places to shop if you’re looking for premium TVs, appliances and household essentials, often with extended warranties or bonus savings. However, retailers like Amazon, Best Buy and others usually price-match Costco on items like electronics. Be sure to compare prices before you buy, especially on big-ticket purchases.

Will my local Costco be open on Thanksgiving?

No, Costco traditionally closes all of its US warehouses on Thanksgiving Day. If you’re planning to shop in person, you’ll need to visit before Thanksgiving or wait until the store reopens on Black Friday, Nov. 27.
