Where to Watch the Miss Universe 2026 Live Online for Free

For cord-cutters, there are a few ways to watch the Miss Universe if you don’t have cable, especially if you want to watch for free. DirecTV has a five-day free trial, while other streaming services offer free trials, so you can watch for free.

Comes with a five-day free trial.

A subscription to DirecTV gets you access to live TV, local and cable channels, including REELZ and Telemundo, at $49.99 per month for the first two months of service ($94.99 per month afterward). The service even offers a five-day trial to watch for free, if you sign up now.

FuboTV

Fubo is an affordable option offering a five-day free trial for new users who sign up.

Fubo starts at $63.99 for the first month ($88.99 per month afterward) with more than 220 channels, including local and cable networks, like Telemundo, that are streamable on smart TVs, smartphones, tablets and web browsers. And with a five-day free trial, you can watch for free if you act fast and sign up now.

For Miss Universe, the cable network Telemundo is available to watch with Hulu + Live TV. Pricing for the cable alternative starts at $99.99 per month for more than 100 live channels, while each plan comes with Hulu, Disney+ and ESPN Select at no additional cost. Starts at $12.99 per month.
