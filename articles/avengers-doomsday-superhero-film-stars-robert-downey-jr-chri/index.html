‘Avengers: Doomsday’ Superhero Film Stars Robert Downey Jr., Chris Evans, Chris Hemsworth, Pedro Pascal – Trailer and Release Date - Movie Vine

TheTools · 16.09.2026 05:46 · source · permalink

Directed by Anthony Russo and Joe Russo, the film ‘Avengers: Doomsday’ follows beloved heroes from three distinct universes as they’re set on a deadly collision course and face an existential threat unlike anything they’ve ever encountered.

‘Avengers: Doomsday’ stars Robert Downey Jr. (as Victor von Doom / Doctor Doom), Chris Evans (as Steve Rogers), Chris Hemsworth (as Thor), Pedro Pascal (as Reed Richards / Mister Fantastic), Paul Rudd (as Scott Lang / Ant-Man), Anthony Mackie (as Sam Wilson / Captain America), Florence Pugh (as Yelena Belova / Black Widow), Vanessa Kirby (as Sue Storm / Invisible Woman), Ebon Moss-Bachrach (as Ben Grimm / The Thing), Wyatt Russell (as John Walker / U.S. Agent), Channing Tatum (as Remy LeBeau / Gambit), Simu Liu (as Shang-Chi), Ian McKellen (as Erik Lehnsherr / Magneto),  Tom Hiddleston (as Loki), James Marsden (as Scott Summers / Cyclops), Patrick Stewart (as Charles Xavier / Professor X), Joseph Quinn (as Johnny Storm / Human Torch), Sebastian Stan (as Bucky Barnes / Winter Soldier), David Harbour (as Alexei Shostakov / Red Guardian), Letitia Wright (as Shuri / Black Panther), Lewis Pullman (as Bob Reynolds / Sentry), Kelsey Grammer (as Hank McCoy / Beast), Kathryn Newton (as Cassie Lang), Danny Ramirez (as Joaquin Torres / Falcon), Winston Duke (as M’Baku), Alan Cumming (as Kurt Wagner / Nightcrawler), Hannah John-Kamen (as Ava Starr / Ghost), Rebecca Romijn (as Raven Darkhölme / Mystique), Mabel Cadena (as Namora), and Tenoch Huerta Mejía (as Namor).

Written by Michael Waldron, Stephen McFeely, Chris McKenna, and Erik Sommers, based on the characters by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby, ‘Avengers: Doomsday’ is produced by Kevin Feige, Louis D’Esposito, Jonathan Schwartz, Anthony Russo, and Joe Russo, with executive producers Callum McDougall. Production companies are Marvel Studios, AGBO, and The Walt Disney Company.

The film’s cinematography is by Newton Thomas Sigel, the music is by Alan Silvestri, with editing by Jeffrey Ford.

Robert Downey Jr. is most known for Iron Man, Sherlock Holmes, and Tropic Thunder. Chris Evans is most known for Captain America: The First Avenger, Knives Out, and Snowpiercer. Chris Hemsworth is most known for Thor, Extraction, and The Avengers. Pedro Pascal is most known for The Last of Us, The Mandalorian, and The Fantastic Four: First Steps. Vanessa Kirby is most known for The Crown, Mission: Impossible – Fallout, and Pieces of a Woman.
