Mikel Arteta has reverted to the side that started our last Premier League game away to Liverpool, as we welcome Hull City to Emirates Stadium.

The boss has made six changes from the XI who played in the Champions League in midweek, recalling Jurrien Timber and Riccardo Calafiori in defence, and Bruno Guimaraes and Martin Odegaard in midfield. Kai Havertz returns up front, with Christos Tzolis and Noni Madueke on the wings. So it's the same team that beat Liverpool 1-0 at Anfield last weekend.

William Saliba, who was on the bench in midweek in the Champions League, is not involved at all. Neither is Max Dowman, who is serving the second of his three-game suspension.

The visitors drew 2-2 at home to Ipswich Town last time out, and make two changes to that side. In come Lucas Gourna-Douath and Mohamed-Ali Cho, with Ryan Giles dropping to the bench while top scorer Mohamed Belloumi misses out entirely.

LINE-UPS

Arsenal: Raya, Timber, Konsa, Gabriel, Calafiori, Rice, Guimaraes, Odegaard, Madueke, Tzolis, Havertz.

Subs: Arrizabalaga, White, Mosquera, Hincapie, Lewis-Skelly, Zubimendi, Merino, Eze, Gyokeres.

Hull City: Tzolakis, Egan, Ajayi, Coyle, Gourna-Douath, Mendy, Stroud, Slater, Iroegbunam, Cho, McBurnie.

Subs: Butland, Giles, Thomas, Gelhardt, Hjerto-Dahl, Norton-Cuffy, Herrington, Mouzakitis, Vaz.
