Watch: It's raining in Fujairah, Ras Al Khaimah

TheTools · 18.11.2026 21:17 · source · permalink

Convective clouds bring rain and strong winds to UAE's eastern and northern areas

The rains have begun in earnest across some of the emirates and they are expected to continue sporadically until Friday.

UAE’s National Center of Meteorology said earlier today that there was a chance of convective cloud formation associated with rainfall and fresh to strong winds at times causing blowing dust and sand, with a speed of 50 km/hr over some Eastern, Northern and coastal areas from 1pm onwards. It added that the conditions would last till Friday.

Stormcentre.com has started to report the rain tapping down Al Tawyeen Road, north of Fujairah; over Jabal Mubrah in Ras Al Khaimah; and across Dibba Al-Fujairah.

Forecasts had also indicated that there was a chance of clouds forming over some (especially mountainous) areas this week.

Sharjah has also begun to enjoy a drizzle.

Earlier in the day Abu Dhabi Police also urged motorists to drive carefully amid rainy weather, calling on road users to follow the dynamic speed limits as displayed on electronic information boards.

Think you can get away with just moisturizer on a cloudy day? You couldn't be more wrong, explains NCM. In an X post, it explained: "Temperature alone doesn’t indicate UV intensity. Check the UV Index before heading outdoors to understand exposure levels, plan your activities, and take the right precautions to reduce UV risks."
