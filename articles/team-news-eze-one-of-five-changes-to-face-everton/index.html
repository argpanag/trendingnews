Team news: Eze one of five changes to face Everton

TheTools · 24.10.2026 20:40 · source · permalink

Mikel Arteta has made five changes for this afternoon’s Premier League clash against Everton, including Ebere Eze coming in for the injured Bukayo Saka.

Bukayo was forced off with a muscle injury just 10 minutes into our Champions League draw at Bayern Munich on Wednesday and is set for a spell on the sidelines, and Arteta has once again turned to Eze to fill his boots, having done so in style in Bavaria by getting on the scoresheet after replacing our No. 7.

Ben White has been given the nod to start at right-back in what will be his 200th Arsenal appearance, lining up alongside Gabriel in the backline, with the Brazilian playing his 200th Premier League game.

Myles Lewis-Skelly comes in at left-back in place of Riccardo Calafiori, while Mikel Merino is preferred to Bruno Guimaraes in midfield. Up top, Viktor Gyokeres will hope to emulate his goalscoring performances in both games against the Toffees last term, with Kai Havertz dropping to the bench which also includes Piero Hincapie again after he recovered from his ankle injury.

David Moyes meanwhile has made three switches to his team, which fell to their first defeat of the campaign at home to Chelsea last weekend.

Having indicated there may be some injury issues ahead of their trip to N5, he is without Jarad Branthwaite after this heavy collision with Emiliano Martinez, with Jake O’Brien partnering James Tarkowski in defence.

Merlin Rohl has moved to right-back, with Brennan Johnson coming in at right wing, and former Gunner Ainsley Maitland-Niles benched. Jack Grealish also misses out on the other flank, with Tyrique George coming in.

LINE-UP

Arsenal: Raya, White, Gabriel, Konsa, Lewis-Skelly, Rice, Merino, Odegaard, Madueke, Eze, Gyokeres.

Subs: Arrizabalaga, Timber, Hincapie, Calafiori, Mosquera, Zubimendi, Guimaraes, Dowman, Havertz.

Everton: Pickford, Rohl, Tarkowski, O’Brien, Mykolenko, Garner, Armstrong, Dewsbury-Hall, Johnson, George, Barry.

Subs: Travers, King, Maitand-Niles, Keane, Norgaard, Hackney, Alcaraz, Dibling, Graham.
