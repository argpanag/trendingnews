Mourinho: "The team is happy to play again at the Bernabéu"

TheTools · 20.11.2026 09:06 · source · permalink

"Celta is much better than the points they have, and we are going to try to start this week well with three matches and get the points," added our coach.

José Mourinho appeared before the media in the press room of Ciudad Real Madrid and analyzed the match for Matchday 13 against Celta, which will be played at the Bernabéu (Saturday, 9:00 pm CEST; Orange TV and Movistar Plus+): “Thank you for the only question about the match. Celta is much better than the points they have. They are a team that last season beat us here, at the Bernabéu. They are playing in Europe, and perhaps playing matches on Thursdays and then in the league is also costing them some points, but tomorrow this factor does not exist. They have had practically their entire team available to work during these two weeks. They will surely present themselves as always: organized and ambitious. They concede few goals because defensively they are very organized, and they know how to do it and how to press."

"They are a very good team, and it is a very important match for us. We have been away from the Bernabéu for a long time, it's true, but we have done well, we have taken all the points we needed to take. Today is the first day we have all the players together to train. We are talking about 30 hours before the match, but we are fine. People are happy to be back together, to play again at the Bernabéu, and we are going to try to start this week well with three matches and get the points."

The Return of Vini Jr.
“The first of ten questions about Vinícius. The start of the season was summer; now it's autumn-winter, and this is the period that interests us. The summer period has passed, so I have little to say about Vini's statements. What matters is how he prepares for this period, which is critical and very challenging; also, the Copa del Rey is approaching. There are three competitions to play. Then comes the Super Cup, hopefully with two matches. This accumulation is happening because we are bringing forward the match we were supposed to play in February. Obviously, we want a good Vinícius, and that is why I insist that this is what we expect.”

The Few Injuries
“Players are getting injured less so far. We cannot predict the future, and injuries have many factors. The main thing with injuries is how players think about and feel their profession, how they work, how they recover, and how they use all the tools we have that can help them. The total responsibility, the credit, and the merit go to the players. This issue of being physically well is not really my way of thinking. When things are going well, we are talking about a multifactorial situation, just as when things are not going well, I also don't like to say it is because of physical, emotional, or tactical reasons.”

 

“For me, both good and bad are multifactorial, and at this moment, the good results we have had in the past month are also multifactorial. The players are doing well together, they have empathy, there is a good group we can work with—not much, but when we can, we work well. We have good communication, we understand each other well, and we have our ambitions and challenges. We are facing everything as a group. I remember the two defeats we have had and the atmosphere we experienced together in those difficult moments, which was not very different from what we are experiencing with the good results. We are doing well globally, and now we have to get back on the field and get results.”

 

Vini Jr.: Starter or Off the Bench?
“Starter, off the bench, or in the stands, it doesn’t matter. It's a strange thing because a couple of weeks ago people were talking about Vinícius as if he were the huge problem Real Madrid had, and after scoring goals against Singapore and Japan, we can't stop talking about Vinícius: if he plays, if he doesn't, why he has to play. I think it’s too much. What I say about Vinícius is what I’ve said one, two, three, four, and five weeks ago: he is an incredible player, we love him, we need him, we have always tried to help him, and that’s what we will continue to do. Here, we don’t have players with privileges. We don’t have players who have to play by decree. We have a group of people working for the team, for Real Madrid.”

 

“Then, a question that also extends to other players you like to talk about is this one has to leave because he plays little. Real Madrid must have a strong squad with solutions, where the coach is not worried if a player is injured or if one has gone with his national team and comes back tired. Real Madrid must have a squad with many good players. This story about being a starter, a substitute, playing or not playing… He’s a substitute today, a starter tomorrow, and then a substitute again and then not even a substitute because other players are arriving, which forces me—though not yet, but it’s close—to leave players in the stands. This is the life I want for Real Madrid, which is different from your work. It’s different from the emotions of the fans. I want players who give me options to have a great season. I don’t want eleven good players; I want many.”

 

JJ Gabriel
“I have nothing to say. I have no feeling. I have feeling with my players, with those I work with every day, with those who are at my disposal. I have no feeling with players who don’t work with me.”

 

Tchouameni, Bernardo, and Valverde
“They are all available to play. I’m not saying they will play, but they are all available. Valverde arrived yesterday, but his first training session is today.”

 

Fatigue After the International Break
“We have three matches in eight days. Then we have others next week. We end a cycle playing against Arsenal, and the Arsenal match will be two days after playing in Bilbao, and the Bilbao match will be three days after playing in San Sebastián. It is, as you say, a heavy schedule, and that’s why I talk about the squad and not this story about the starting eleven. I haven’t seen any of my players arrive and, to the most basic question, ‘How are you?’ None have answered, ‘I’m tired.’ This is a good sign—that maybe, even if they’re tired, they want to play, to be available, and to stay motivated. Fede’s case was very well managed in Uruguay. In the last match, he didn’t even play 45 minutes. Obviously, we’re talking about intercontinental travel, nights on the plane, jet lag, but people want to play. Nobody has been left out; we’re all here.”

 

Does He Close the Door on Endrick and Other Players?
“He’s doing very well here. For me, the situation is very clear: Real Madrid is more important than Endrick. I care a lot about Endrick; he’s a fantastic kid, and I have great affection for him, but for me, Real Madrid is more important than Endrick. That means Real Madrid needs him. And the Real Madrid coach needs him. You already have your answer—you, the people who write, his agents, and all the clubs that send news to the newspapers. After that, Real Madrid, as a superior structure, if my superiors decide something different, in today’s football, it must be respected. But I, as the coach of Real Madrid, the superior structure knows that for me, Endrick is important.”

 

Does He Now Consider Arda Güler’s Role More Important?
“They are all important. He’s playing very well. There is an evolution in his game, and I don’t want to say that evolution is because of me. I don’t want to say it; I think it’s a natural evolution of a kid who is less and less a kid and more and more a man. A natural evolution of a super talent who keeps doing the things he has always done and is doing things he has never done before. That’s why he is becoming a more complete and super important player for Real Madrid, which wants to have the best players in the world. I see his renewal with complete naturalness, also because Arda thinks he wants to stay at Real Madrid and wants to stay for a long time. That’s where the world’s greatest club and one of the best players come together.”

The two groups I have coached
“Don’t ask me to compare. It’s a group that was very eager to win, willing to sacrifice to achieve victory, that gave everything and, due to factual circumstances, timing, profiles, and even my own profile at that moment, the relationship during the last season started to change. More than the relationship, my feeling evolved, realizing that the profile I had brought as a coach and as a leader had run its course, and it was better for the club to start a new phase with a coach of a different profile than mine. The club couldn’t have chosen a better coach to succeed me. I returned to England, to a club I already knew, won the Premier League again, and it worked out well for everyone. But comparing groups, I don’t want to compare them. Both groups were incredibly talented, with players of the highest level, but this is Real Madrid, season after season and generation after generation.”

 

Self-criticism of Vini Jr.
“That’s in the past now. As I said: summer and winter, heat and cold. Summer has passed, the impact of the World Cup has passed, a period where a young man—because at the end of the day, someone who is 24-25 years old is still a young man—has made his evaluations and self-analysis. Maybe he shouldn’t have said certain things, but I never like to criticize honesty and openness. Now winter begins, the real grind. We are going to reach, hopefully, a situation in the Champions League with knockout matches. We’ll reach a point in the league where every point lost or gained will be decisive as the season comes to an end. That’s where Vinícius can make a huge difference for us as the great player he is, with the extensive experience he already has in reaching these decisive moments of the season and being decisive. He’s a player everyone wants in the locker room. Sometimes there are players who the locker room isn’t in love with and doesn’t see as one of them, but Vinícius is the exact opposite. That’s where we stand. I think he is very committed to being decisive for Real Madrid again, as he has been for many years.”

 

Portugal playing this international break without Cristiano Ronaldo
“As a Portuguese, the only thing I will say is that I always want Portugal to win. After that, I wash my hands of it and won’t comment further.”
