Jose Mourinho has spent this autumn revisiting his old addresses.

First came Inter Milan, beaten 2-1 at the Santiago Bernabeu through Kylian Mbappe and Federico Valverde; now, on Wednesday, the Real Madrid manager returns to Rome, where he spent three years from 2021, winning the Conference League and reaching a Europa League final.

Waiting for him at the Stadio Olimpico is a Roma side back in the Champions League after seven seasons away, still hunting a first win after a gritty 1-1 draw at Fenerbahce. Gian Piero Gasperini must also cope without goalkeeper Mile Svilar, who has fractured a toe.

Roma vs. Real Madrid prediction: Who will win?

Gasperini's side are unbeaten this season, they came away from Fenerbahce with a 1-1 draw on their return to this competition, and in Paulo Dybala they have a playmaker who has been through plenty of nights like this. But with Svilar out injured, Pierluigi Gollini in goal is a small crack in the wall.

Madrid haven't been pretty lately. They edged Inter 2-1 and scraped past Villarreal 1-0, but winning ugly has never bothered them in Europe. Mbappe is set for his100th Champions League appearance, and one moment from him or from a rival in white can undo a plan that has held for 80 minutes.

Roma vs. Real Madrid best bets

We've picked three betting angles for Roma vs. Real Madrid. Check them out below before placing your bets on the match.

Both teams to score: Yes

Real Madrid have found goals easy this season, but keeping them out has been harder. They have conceded in seven of their nine games, with clean sheets only against Malaga and Villarreal, and they have let in a goal in all four of their away matches.

Roma, for their part, have scored in six of their seven games, and the only blank was the 0-0 at Como. The caveat is that Roma's defence has been tight, with four clean sheets in seven. Still, a leaky Madrid backline and a Roma side that rarely goes dry make it the most natural pick on the board.

Kylian Mbappe to score anytime

Mbappe has eight goals in nine games, and the shot numbers back that up. He averages 5.34 shots and 3.11 on target per 90, and 28 of his 48 attempts have hit the target. Only one of his eight goals came from the penalty spot, so the record is not padded by spot-kicks. He also scored in the opening win over Inter.

Real Madrid to win 

Madrid have won seven of their past nine games, with defeats only at Real Betis and Atletico Madrid. Roma have drawn three of their last four, so they are tough to beat but short on wins. Madrid's quality makes them favourites for this win.

Roma vs. Real Madrid lineups: Predicted starting XIs

Here's how we think the two teams will line up at the Stadio Olimpico.

Roma predicted lineup to play Real Madrid

Gollini (GK) — Mancini, Ndicka, Hermoso — Molina, Kone, De Roon, Wesley — Dybala, Soule — Malen

  • Svilar is out with a fractured toe, so Gollini deputises in goal again.
  • Donyell Malen is facing a domestic suspension after his red card at Como, but he is eligible for the Champions League and is expected to lead the line.
  • Bryan Cristante is a doubt with a knee problem. If he misses out, Marten de Roon will keep his place in midfield.
  • Paulo Dybala is fit and expected to start behind Malen, alongside Matias Soule.

Real Madrid predicted lineup to play Roma

Courtois (GK) — Dumfries, Konate, Rudiger, Carreras — Valverde, Bernardo Silva — Guler, Bellingham, Vinicius — Mbappe

  • Dean Huijsen is available again after serving a domestic suspension and could come into the defence.
  • Eduardo Camavinga, Arda Guler and Bernardo Silva are back from UEFA bans.
  • Eder Militao, Ferland Mendy, Rodrygo and Raul Asencio are unavailable.
  • Vinicius Junior was sent off against Villarreal but that doesn't have any bearing on this match.
  • Mbappe is set to lead the attack in his 100th Champions League appearance.
