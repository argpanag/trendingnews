Sampras, Lynch latest sports stars to appear in ads for Kalshi

TheTools · 11.09.2026 21:35 · source · permalink

Kalshi is enlisting former tennis player Pete Sampras to “make a rare return to commercial advertising” with a new 30-second spot for the U.S. Open that “shows vintage footage of Sampras from the tournament” in 1990, according to Tim Nudd of AD AGE. Sampras reflects on that year’s tournament -- which he won at age 19 for his first major championship -- in the spot’s voiceover, saying that “after you win one, the expectations rise -- and there are ‘more odds to overcome.’” The Sampras partnership builds on Kalshi’s multiyear deal with the USTA to be the exclusive prediction market partner of the U.S. Open beginning with this year’s tournament. Sampras has “largely withdrawn from ads in recent years, making the Kalshi spot particularly notable” (AD AGE, 9/10).

UNLEASH THE BEAST: ADWEEK’s Alixandra Rutnik reported former NFLer Marshawn Lynch features in a Kalshi ad with him “checking out the Kalshi 2027 football champion report while checking out at a convenience store -- buying some Skittles of course.” A girl “recognizes him, activating Beast Mode as Lynch transforms into a superhero-type version of himself.” In the spot, the convenience store transformation “quickly turns very inconvenient when he can’t pick up his phone, smashes some glass, and his car turns into a monster truck.” The “key message: Kalshi unlocks your inner Beast Mode” (ADWEEK, 9/10).
