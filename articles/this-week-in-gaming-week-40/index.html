This Week in Gaming (Week 40)

TheTools · 29.09.2026 15:30 · source · permalink

Sunday, September 27th 2026 This Week in Gaming (Week 40) Welcome to week 40 where we move from September to October and this week's major release is a life sim set in a cyberpunk city. All the other bigger releases this week are kind of clumped together, with the week kicking off with transporting goods, followed by a blocky spin-off sequel. Next up we have another life sim, but this time set in a cozy fantasy world. We end the week with some action, first up we have a disco-punk, space horror action game, followed by the 8th installment in a well known air combat franchise. We also have a couple of remasters this week, so make sure you check out those as well. Nivalis Nights / This week's major release / Tuesday 29 September Grow your business, manage restaurants and nightclubs, make friends and enemies, buy and decorate apartments, go fishing and maybe even find love in a cyberpunk city that stretches from the ocean to the clouds. Steam link Transport Fever 3 / Tuesday 29 September Transport Fever 3, the ultimate transport tycoon returns, bigger, deeper, and more dynamic than ever. Build the routes that transform the world across land, sea, and sky using over a century of evolving transport options. Design and build your ideal transport network. Steam link Minecraft Dungeons II / Tuesday 29 September Drop the pickaxe, grab a sword, and charge into a thrilling new adventure in Minecraft Dungeons II. Journey through unexplored lands and mysterious locations, in a quest to stop a new evil from causing chaos in not one, but two worlds! Clash with menacing foes in explosive encounters and fight your way through hordes of mobs that will do everything in their power to stop you. Steam link Songs of Glimmerwick / Demo available / Wednesday 30 September Become immersed in a musical woodland fantasy world where casting a spell is as easy as playing a song. Attend classes, grow the university garden, make friends with classmates and townsfolk, and explore the island's many oddities and mysteries in this story-driven witch academy RPG. Steam link RetroSpace / Demo available / Thursday 1 October Immersive, disco-punk, space horror where you can be a sneaky chap or a mutant action hero as you try to survive on a space station swallowed by a black hole. A mind-bending ride with weird creatures, immersive features, time jumps, and '70s mustaches. Steam link Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve / Friday 2 October Soar through the skies and rise above every challenge to earn your place among the elite. Engage in intense aerial combat to dominate the battlefield with powerful weapons. Experience a breathtaking campaign filled with high-stakes missions, hard choices, and deep squad connections. Steam link Also launching this week: The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt—Remastered, Dynasty Warriors 3: Complete Edition Remastered, TOEM 2 and Sengoku Rance.
