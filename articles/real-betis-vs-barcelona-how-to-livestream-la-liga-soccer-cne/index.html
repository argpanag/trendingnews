Barça’s perfect start to the campaign continued last weekend with a comfortable 3-0 win over midtable Getafe. Hansi Flick’s team followed up that win with an equally comfortable 4-1 triumph over Galatasaray in the UEFA Champions League in midweek to make it 10 straight wins across all competitions for the Catalan giants.

Third-placed Betis has also enjoyed a productive week. Manuel Pellegrini’s Verdiblancos claimed a 5-2 win over Osasuna last time out in Spain’s top flight, followed by an impressive 3-2 win over Porto in the UCL in midweek.

Real Betis takes on Barcelona on Saturday, Oct. 17, at El Estadio La Cartuja in Seville, with kickoff set for 6:30 p.m. CEST local time.
