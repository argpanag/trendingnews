Gowna v Crossmaglen: Time, TV and betting on today’s Ulster Club quarter-final

TheTools · 07.11.2026 13:29 · source · permalink

Cavan champions Gowna entertain Armagh kingpins Crossmaglen in the quarter-final of the Ulster Club Championship this evening.

No team from Cavan has ever won the Ulster Club SFC.

Crossmaglen alone have won this competition 11 times.

Yet, Vinny Corey’s men won’t be in awe of their opponents tonight after blitzing their opponents in Cavan this season.

They also defeated holders Kingscourt 4-17 to 0-14 in the county final while Crossmaglen battled to a 1-19 to 0-17 win over Killeavy in the Armagh decider.

Here’s all the information you need to know ahead of today’s game. . .

When and where is the game?

Gowna v Crossmaglen is on Saturday, November 7 at Kingspan Breffni, Cavan.

What time is throw-in?

Tonight’s game throws-in at 7.15pm.

Where can I watch it?

Today’s game is live on RTÉ2.

Betting odds

  • Gowna 7/4
  • Draw 8/1
  • Crossmaglen 4/7
