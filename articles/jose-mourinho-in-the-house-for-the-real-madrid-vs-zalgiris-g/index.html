The celebrated coach took advantage of the break to come and watch the basketball section’s match

By Stefan Acevski / info@eurohoops.net

Real Madrid and Zalgiris are currently playing their EuroLeague Round 14 match in the Spanish capital, and there is a very familiar face in the audience.

It is Jose Mourinho, the coach of Real Madrid‘s football section, who is sitting courtside watching the match between Real and the Lithuanian giant.

The Portuguese, one of the best and most successful coaches in football history, led Real Madrid to another victory in the Champions League last night, where the Spanish club defeated PSV 5-1 at home.

It’s not unusual to see Real Madrid footballers in the audience at basketball section matches; they’ve always attended in previous years, and now coach José Mourinho has done the same.
