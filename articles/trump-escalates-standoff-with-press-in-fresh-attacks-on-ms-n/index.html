Trump escalates standoff with press in fresh attacks on MS NOW, CNN

TheTools · 26.09.2026 19:38 · source · permalink

President Donald Trump launched a fresh attack on the press on Saturday morning, a day after escalating his standoff with news outlets by blocking CNN from traveling with him on Air Force One as he travels to Tennessee this weekend.

“Why should perpetrators of FAKE NEWS, like CNN and MSDNC, be allowed access to a very sacred place, the White House?” he wrote on Truth Social. “Despite my big Election Win, almost 100% of ‘TRUMP’ coverage is negative, and has been for years!”

In brief remarks to reporters later that morning, the president denied that he only wanted news outlets that support him in the press pool, telling MS NOW’s Akayla Gardner, “I want real news, not fake news — like you.” He added, “I love an open, free press. I don’t like the fake press.”

Trump also railed against the media for reporting that Rep. Byron Donalds, who is running for Florida governor with Trump’s endorsement, removed references to the president from his campaign website. (Donalds has said that the website changes had been in the works for about two months and that they were meant to emphasize his campaign’s platform.) Trump wrote on Truth Social it should be up to the courts to “stop this totally dishonest form of Politics” and called the media “corrupt and dishonest.”

By mid-afternoon on Saturday, Trump had lashed out against the press four times on social media, including writing in one post to vote for “Republican and ‘Trump’ candidates” in the midterms.

Trump’s latest affront to legitimate news outlets follows a week of attempts to bend the media to his will. Unhappy about straightforward coverage of his administration, his sagging approval rating, and the impact of his policies, he has tried to freeze out news outlets that don’t deliver the coverage he wants, upending the traditional role of the White House press corps at a pivotal moment, with just over five weeks to go before the midterm elections.

Republicans have said privately they are bracing for defeat on Election Day.

The president first announced that he would ban MS NOW, CNN and Politico last week, and the outlets’ journalists were denied access to White House grounds Saturday. In the days that followed, the three outlets sued Trump and several officials in his administration and the TV network pool, which includes Fox News, suspended its coverage of Trump. The White House countered by creating “Trump TV,” an online stream of the administration’s so-called “greatest hits.”
