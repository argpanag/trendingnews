NICKELBACK ANNOUNCES THE EVERYTHING UNDER THE SUN TOUR! | Ticketmaster AU

Canadian and RIAA Diamond-certified, and GRAMMY® Award-nominated rock legends Nickelback announce the 2027 Everything Under The Sun Tour, a massive global arena run celebrating the band’s highly anticipated eleventh studio album, Everything Under The Sun, out October 30 via Virgin Music Group. Following the momentum of 2022’s Get Rollin’ and its record-setting world tour, Nickelback will bring their signature arena-sized live show to fans across North America, the UK, Europe, and Australia, with Don Broco joining as direct support and special guests

The announcement arrives as Nickelback continue to unveil the emotional and sonic breadth of Everything Under The Sun. The band’s explosive single “Rattle The Cage” (feat. John 5) is currently No. 1 at Active Rock radio in the U.S., marking another chart-topping moment for a band whose catalog has defined rock radio for more than two decades. The track introduced Everything Under The Sun with blistering guitars, pounding riffs and arena-sized energy, while subsequent releases “Leave Me Behind” and “Make Me Love You” have revealed a more vulnerable and emotionally expansive side of the record.

Nickelback 2027 Australia Tour Ticket Tips

What is the ticket limit for Nickelback?

You may purchase a maximum of 8 tickets per show during the presales and 10 tickets during the General Sale. Companion tickets are included within the total ticket limit.

Please adhere to the published ticket limit. Persons who exceed the ticket limit may have any or all of their orders and tickets cancelled without notice by Ticketmaster at its discretion. This includes orders associated with the same name, e-mail address, billing address, credit card number or any other information.

How can I purchase accessible tickets for Nickelback?

Accessible tickets (including Companion Cards) can be purchased by calling our Customer Service team on the Accessible Bookings line Monday – Sunday 9am – 5pm AEDT: 1300 446 925. This phone number is reserved for patrons with accessibility requirements and companion cardholders and cannot process any other ticketing requests.

Accessible tickets, both during the Presales and the General Public sale period, can also be requested via our Submit a request – Ticketmaster Help. Please be sure to include your relevant presale code or direct URL if wanting to make a booking during the presale period. You DO NOT need to call us once this form has been submitted, we will contact you to confirm your booking once the sales start.

Venues are solely responsible for establishing their own accessible ticket policies. Availability and type of accessible seating tickets will vary by venue.

Get to know Ticket Transfer so everyone has their own ticket

1. Open the Ticketmaster App

2. Tap on my events and select the event you want to Ticket Transfer

3. Tap ‘Transfer’ below the ticket you want to share (please note: you may have to wait a moment for the ‘Transfer’ button to load)

4. On the ‘Select Tickets To Transfer’ page, confirm the ticket you want to share (please note – you must Transfer each ticket individually)

5. Tap the ‘Transfer To’ button. Tap ‘Manually Enter A Recipient’ & enter your friend’s ‘First Name’, ‘Last Name’, and ‘Email Address or Mobile Number’

6. Tap ‘Transfer Ticket’. The ticket in your account will now say “Sent”. You can cancel or recall the Ticket Transfer at any time.

7. Your friend receives an email, asking them to accept the transferred tickets, and they can either login or create a new Ticketmaster account to accept.

8. The tickets are now ready to go in your friend’s Ticketmaster account and marked as ‘Transferred’ in your account
