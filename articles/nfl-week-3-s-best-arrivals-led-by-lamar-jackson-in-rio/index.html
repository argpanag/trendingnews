NFL Week 3's best arrivals, led by Lamar Jackson in Rio

TheTools · 27.09.2026 16:54 · source · permalink


The official start of fall means it's fit season, when players start dominating the gameday tunnels with eye-popping jackets, sweaters and pants.

Last week, the Philadelphia Eagles played a blazing hot game in Nashville that had players' cleats melting with with temperatures of up to 157 degrees Fahrenheit on the turf.

Hopefully this week is the start of a heat decline with more cloudier 50-to-75 degree days ahead to allow players to comfortably show off their fits.

To kick off Week 3, Green Bay Packers quarterbacks Jordan Love and Tyrod Taylor rocked my two favorite outfits of the Thursday primetime slate.

Love came in shining -- literally -- with a Public School New York x KAY jewelry collaboration jacket: the definition of a statement piece.

Meanwhile, Taylor's Dries Van Noten sweater is the perfect kickoff to fall -- warm but fashion-forward. There are so many fabric design details within this one piece, showing the value of layers.

As always, Sunday is where the big show begins. Here are Week 3 fits that caught our eye:

The Baltimore Ravens and Dallas Cowboys brought their contrasting styles to Maracanã Stadium for the NFL's first game in Rio de Janeiro. Lamar Jackson donned a graphic tee paying homage to the late rapper DMX, while George Pickens' fit featured Brazil's national colors in a nod to the host country.

From Stefon Diggs' black jacket covered in NBA throwback logo patches, to Malik Nabers' custom "H1M" pendant, players brought plenty of style and personality ahead of the early slate.

The Green Bay Packers host the Atlanta Falcons to kick off Week 3's slate, and quarterback Jordan Love entered the home opener in style, pairing an oversize studded leather pullover with black trousers, a crisp white shirt and black tie. He completed the look with dark sunglasses and a checkered duffel bag.
