76ers Face Pacers in NBA Cup Group Play Showdown

TheTools · 27.11.2026 23:45 · source · permalink

Philadelphia and Indiana meet in an Eastern Conference NBA Cup group-stage matchup on Friday, November 27, 2026, at Gainbridge Fieldhouse in Indianapolis. The 7:30 p.m. ET contest gives the 76ers an opportunity to strengthen their position in the competition, while the Pacers seek another important home victory.

Philadelphia enters the matchup with a 15-4 record, leading the Eastern Conference, according to Sports Illustrated. The team recently recovered from a 128-111 defeat against Portland by beating the Miami Heat 111-104 on Wednesday.

Indiana holds a 6-12 record, although its recent results include a 125-120 victory over the Oklahoma City Thunder on Monday. The Pacers also ended a four-game losing streak with that win, according to the coverage published by The Big Lead.

The availability of several key players remains an important factor. Philadelphia listed Jaylen Brown as out, Joel Embiid as questionable, and Ariel Hukporti as unavailable. Indiana reported that Tyrese Haliburton, who is dealing with a hamstring injury, would miss the game.

The Pacers also listed Johnny Furphy and Ben Sheppard as unavailable. T.J. McConnell, Kelly Oubre Jr., and Larry Nance Jr. were questionable ahead of the contest, according to Sports Illustrated's injury report.

Philadelphia's recent performance has included strong results in close contests. Sports Illustrated reported that the 76ers had won all 12 of their clutch games and 11 of 12 matchups against teams with records below .500.

Indiana, meanwhile, has struggled to maintain consistency without Haliburton. Sports Illustrated placed the Pacers 20th in the NBA in net rating, highlighting the difficulties facing the team during his absence.

Tyrese Maxey remains an important offensive contributor for Philadelphia. According to The Big Lead, his scoring ability in the backcourt is expected to play a significant role as the 76ers attempt to control the pace against Indiana.

The matchup will be available on NBC Sports Philadelphia, with streaming coverage through Fubo in the Philadelphia market. Viewers in Indianapolis can watch through DAZN, according to the broadcast information published by Yahoo Sports.

Friday's game is part of the NBA Cup group stage. Philadelphia is scheduled to travel to Houston on Saturday, November 28, while Indiana is set to visit the Chicago Bulls the same evening.
