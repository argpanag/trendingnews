Star Wars Starfighter Sets a New Speed Record for the Franchise

TheTools · 16.10.2026 11:34 · source · permalink

Ryan Gosling's upcoming film Star Wars: Starfighter is introducing a spacecraft that surpasses every previously established starfighter speed benchmark while taking the franchise into a new narrative era. The film, directed by Shawn Levy and scheduled to reach theaters on May 28, 2027, follows pilot Kade Auberon and a young boy played by Flynn Gray in a story set approximately five years after Star Wars: The Rise of Skywalker.

According to The Direct's October 16 report, footage presented privately at Disney's D23 Expo in August revealed that Auberon pilots a decommissioned G17 Imperial Reaper. The character describes the vessel as the fastest starfighter ever built, establishing a new speed record within the franchise's established spacecraft hierarchy.

The trailer has not been officially released online. Fan-recorded versions circulated briefly before Lucasfilm removed them, leaving the D23 presentation as the primary source of the reported footage details.

The sequence reportedly shows Gray's character discovering the ship in a dusty workshop before Auberon warns him against touching anything. After the boy asks about the unfamiliar spacecraft, the pilot explains its significance, followed by scenes of the vessel evading blaster fire and maneuvering between enormous pillars of water.

The G17's reported performance places it ahead of previously documented spacecraft in the franchise. The TIE/vn space superiority fighter, commonly known as Kylo Ren's TIE silencer, previously held the speed benchmark at 155 megalights per hour, or MGLT, according to The Direct.

Other fast vessels include the TIE dagger at 150 MGLT, Major Vonreg's TIE Baron at 145 MGLT, and both Kylo Ren's TIE Whisper and the TIE Avenger prototype at 140 MGLT. Din Djarin's modified N-1 starfighter reaches 130 MGLT, while the Resistance's RZ-2 A-wing registers 125 MGLT and the Rebel Alliance's RZ-1 A-wing reaches 120 MGLT.

Luke Skywalker's T-65 X-wing has a recorded maximum of 100 MGLT, while the Millennium Falcon reaches 75 MGLT in realspace. The G17's designation as the fastest spacecraft therefore represents a major departure from the franchise's established comparisons, although its precise speed has not been disclosed in the reported footage.

The vessel's Imperial origins also raise questions about its history. The Galactic Empire fell at Endor in 4 ABY and formally surrendered after the Battle of Jakku in 5 ABY. Because Starfighter takes place around 40 ABY, the spacecraft would predate the film's events by at least 35 years if it was constructed during the Imperial era.

The Direct reported that the ship's decommissioned status could prove relevant to its unusual capabilities, although the film's explanation has not been revealed. The vessel's history may be connected to Auberon's mission and the larger story.

Beyond its spacecraft reveal, Starfighter will mark a significant narrative shift for the franchise. CBR reported October 13 that the film will tell a standalone story separated from the Skywalker Saga, whose central characters and conflicts have shaped the franchise since the original 1977 release.

Gosling plays Kade Auberon, a pilot responsible for protecting a mysterious young boy and escorting him across a fractured galaxy. The cast also includes Matt Smith, Mia Goth, Aaron Pierre, and Amy Adams. Shawn Levy directs from a screenplay by Jonathan Tropper.

The film follows The Mandalorian and Grogu, which reached theaters in May 2026, as Lucasfilm expands its theatrical storytelling. Unlike previous productions connected to the Skywalker family, Starfighter is designed to stand independently within the same fictional universe, without making the established family saga its central narrative.

The franchise's creative direction remains a subject of debate among fans. In an October 12 opinion article, The Observer reported contrasting views among Notre Dame students about Dave Filoni, who succeeded Kathleen Kennedy in January 2026. Freshman William Wetzel expressed confidence in Filoni's work, while freshman Liam Skillings argued that the franchise needed to respond more carefully to audience expectations.

Those differing perspectives reflect broader discussion about the balance between established characters and original storytelling. Starfighter's release on May 28, 2027, will bring that debate into focus as the franchise marks the 50th anniversary of its theatrical beginnings.
