Industry decarbonisation: Singapore to invest S$143m to develop low-carbon technologies

TheTools · 24.10.2026 00:29 · source · permalink

Singapore is putting S$143 million towards developing and commercialising cleaner technologies for industrial activities. These include carbon capture, low-carbon hydrogen and other emerging tech. Of this, S$100 million will help companies develop and test their own ideas through R&D projects. The move comes as Singapore works towards its climate goals, with industries that are difficult to cut emissions in posing a key challenge. Claudia Lim with more.
