Kenneth Walker goes nuclear in first game in KC

TheTools · 15.09.2026 04:03 · source · permalink

    Kenneth Walker goes nuclear in first game in KC

    Kenneth Walker

    KC Running Back #9
    Player Stats

    Kenneth Walker rushed 23 times for 173 yards and a touchdown in the Chiefs’ Week 1 win over the Broncos, adding 18 yards and a touchdowns on three receptions.

    Walker single-handedly out-gained the entire Denver offense on the evening as he was the clear point of emphasis in Patrick Mahomes’ first game back after tearing his ACL in 2025. He was fed seven carries on the team’s first nine offensive plays run from scrimmage, a drive that would result in a 15-yard Mahomes rushing touchdown on the team’s first possession. The highlight of the game for the explosive back was a 60-yard touchdown run on fourth-and-one, in which he bounced the football outside and dusted the secondary on his way to the end zone. He would add a receiving score on a “Kansas City Special” later in the game, taking a Mahomes shuttle pass to the end zone from two yards out to further bolster his counting stats. What’s more, walker played all but five offensive snaps in the first half with the game still within reason, highlighting a massive ceiling for the remainder of the season. Walker is a locked-in RB1 ahead of a Week 2 matchup against the Colts.
    - Mark Garcia
