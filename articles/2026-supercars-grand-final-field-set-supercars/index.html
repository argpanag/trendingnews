2026 Supercars Grand Final field set | Supercars

TheTools · 08.11.2026 07:01 · source · permalink

The four 2026 Repco Supercars Championship combatants have booked their tickets to the bp Adelaide Grand Final, following an enthralling Penrite Oil Sandown 500.

Matt Payne, Brodie Kostecki, Broc Feeney and Chaz Mostert will fight for the championship on November 26-29.

In a Supercars first, in just the second season under the Finals ruleset, four different drivers from four different teams will vie for the championship at the Grand Final.

Payne will start Adelaide with a 30-point lead over Kostecki, with Feeney and Mostert 54 and 72 back respectively.

Tickets are on sale for the bp Adelaide Grand Final.
