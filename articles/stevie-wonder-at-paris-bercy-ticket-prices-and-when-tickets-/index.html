Stevie Wonder at Paris Bercy: ticket prices and when tickets go on sale

TheTools · 10.09.2026 08:48 · source · permalink

Stevie Wonder in Paris: a concert that should draw a big crowd of the American star's fans in the eastern part of the city. And Stevie Wonder's admirers won't have to wait long to see the musician back on stage, as this new tour lands in Europe this fall! In the capital, Stevie Wonder will set up shop at Paris Bercy on Thursday, October 15, 2026.

This particular tour, titled "Songs In The Key Of Life Performances", will celebrate the 50th anniversary of the iconic album "Songs in the Key of Life". Released in 1976, the opus is widely regarded as one of Stevie Wonder's two masterpieces. "Songs in the Key of Life" notably features the singles "I Wish", "Sir Duke", "Another Star", and "As".

This concert, Stevie Wonder's first at Paris Bercy since 2008, is expected to draw a huge crowd of fans of the American musician and singer, eager to see their idol live in the capital. Tickets are likely to sell out fast, so you'll need to act quickly to land the prized pass. So, how much will it cost and when does the on-sale start? Here’s everything you need to know.

Ticket prices range from €78.50 to €276.50. The lowest tier, Category 4, is listed at €78.50. Next comes €98.30 in Category 3, €166.50 in Category 2, €199.50 in Category 1, €249 in Gold category, and finally €276.50 for the Diamond category.

Please note that all seats are in a seated configuration. In addition, if you choose seats from the plan, be aware that the numbering of the bleachers is reversed due to the venue’s modular layout. Thus, row 01 is the farthest from the stage. By contrast, the orchestra and the balconies use standard numbering.

On the ticketing front, the presale for Mastercard holders is open this Wednesday, September 9, at 10 a.m. Next up are the presales for Paris Bercy and Live Nation France, available from Thursday, September 10 at 10 a.m. The general sale goes live on Friday, September 11 at 10 a.m.
