How to watch 2026 World Aquatics Swimming World Cup

TheTools · 28.09.2026 15:49 · source · permalink

All three stops of the 2026 World Aquatics Swimming World Cup stream live on Peacock.

The World Cup stops in Baku, Azerbaijan (Oct. 1-3); Tashkent, Uzbekistan (Oct. 8-10) and Astana, Kazakhstan (Oct. 15-17).

Top swimmers entered include Olympic gold medalist Cameron McEvoy of Australia, world champions Marrit Steenbergen of the Netherlands and Qin Haiyang of China and world record holder Evgeniia Chikunova of Russia, plus Americans Carson Foster and Shaine Casas.

Full entries are here.

Competition will be in short-course meters pools — which are 25 meters contrasted to 50-meter pools used at the Olympics.

Swimmers are preparing for the World Short Course Championships in Beijing from Dec. 1-6.
