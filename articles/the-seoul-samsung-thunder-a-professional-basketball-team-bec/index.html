The Seoul Samsung Thunder, a professional basketball team, became the first Korean professional basketball team to achieve a record of nine consecutive wins.

Samsung defeated Goyang Sono 95-87 in an away game of the 2026-2027 KCC professional basketball regular league at Sono Arena in Goyang, Gyeonggi Province on the 30th. Samsung, which had already won eight consecutive games in the opening season and tied the league's largest opening win streak with Dongbu (current DB) in Wonju in the 2011-2012 season and Goyang Orion in the 2014-2015 season, set the first record of winning nine consecutive games and winning the first round.

Samsung has never reached the semi-finals since the 2016-2017 season, and has been at the bottom of the last three consecutive seasons. However, under Kim Sang-sik, who has experienced both national teams and KBL coaches, the team has been transformed into a team that does not lose with balanced capabilities, including No. 1 team field goal success rate (50.9%) and No. 2 team rebound rate (36.9).

In the last game of the first round, Samsung played a close game with Sono, but succeeded in winning the first round by showing its last-minute concentration. Foreign center Kerem Kanter made a double-double with 32 points and 12 rebounds, and Lee Kwan-hee scored 18 points, including three 3-pointers.
