'F--k That,' Todd Monken Blasts 'Browns Are Gonna Browns' Phrase on Video After Mike Tomlin Comments

TheTools · 21.09.2026 04:48 · source · permalink

The Cleveland Browns are notorious for squandering games that should be winnable. New head coach Todd Monken is hoping to snap the cycle.

Monken got his first win as a head coach on Sunday after beating the Tampa Bay Buccaneers 23-19 in a game that came down to the wire. It was the kind of game that Cleveland is known to lose.

"Browns are gonna Browns," Monken said after the win. "F--k that. The guys took that to heart."
