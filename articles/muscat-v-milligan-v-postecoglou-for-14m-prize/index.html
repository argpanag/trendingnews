The AFC Champions League is back for another season as Asia’s best compete for top honours across the ACL Elite and ACL Two, and there are a host of Australian clubs, players and coaches involved in 2026-27.

Newcastle Jets will fly the flag in the AFC Champions League Elite after winning the A-League Men Premiership last season and there is $14million AUD cheque up for grabs in Asia’s premier club competition also featuring Aussie trailblazers Ange Postecoglou and Kevin Muscat.

A-League pair Melbourne Victory and Adelaide United are in the AFC Champions League Two this term.

Here is everything you need to know.

How To Watch

The 2026-27 ACL Elite and ACL Two are both available to stream via Paramount+.

ACL Elite

Newcastle Jets are set to go head-to-head with some of Asia’s biggest clubs in the ACL Elite.

The Jets’ maiden Premiership after 21 years in the A-League secured their passage to the continent’s premier competition.

Newcastle have only featured in the Champions League group/league phase once before in their history back in 2008-09 after winning the A-League Championship. That season, the Jets reached the Round of 16.

In 2026-27, Mark Milligan’s Jets will face J1 League champions Kashima Antlers, Japan’s Kyoto Sanga, Kashiwa Reysol and Vissel Kobe, Malaysian powerhouse Johor Darul Ta’zim – boasting ex-Central Coast Mariners player Brad Tapp.

Kevin Muscat’s Chinese Super League champions Shanghai Port await, as well as Nick Montgomery’s Beijing Guoan.

There will also be a reunion with former star Eli Adams, who left the Jets for Gamba Osaka at the end of last season.

ACL Elite Format

The AFC Champions League Elite begins with 32 teams split into East and West leagues. Each club play eight league-stage matches – four at home and four away – against different opponents from their region.

The top eight teams from each league advance to the Round of 16, played as two-legged regional ties. The eight winners then progress to a centralised Finals tournament, where the quarter-finals, semi-finals and final are all single-leg matches and feature cross-regional match-ups.

Saudi Arabia’s Al-Ahli are the reigning champions.

Final (champions): $10million ($14million AUD)
Final (runners-up): $4million ($5.6million AUD)
Semi-finals: $600,000 per team ($841,155 AUD) / $2.4million total ($3.36million AUD)
Quarter-finals: $400,000 per team ($560,770 AUD) / $3.2million total ($4.45million AUD)
Round of 16: $200,000 per team ($280,383 AUD) / $3.2million total ($4.45million AUD)
League stage: $800,000 per team ($1.12million AUD) / $19.2million total ($26.9million AUD)

Other Aussies involved

Ange Postecoglou is head coach of Cristiano Ronaldo’s Al-Nassr, who are in the West Region.

In the East, Kenny Dougall is captain of Thai champions Buriram United, who also boast Curtis Good.

Stefan Mauk plays for Vietnamese champions CAHN, while Bradley Tapp and Shane Lowry are on the books of Malaysian juggernaut Johor.

Socceroos great Kevin Muscat is head coach of Shanghai Port, where Vincenzo Ierardo, Tony Vidmar and Greg King are part of the coaching staff while former Central Coast Mariners boss Nick Montgomery is in charge of Beijing Guoan.

Off the pitch, Australian Chris Pehlivanis is chief operating officer at Al Ain.

ACL Two

Australia has two representatives in the AFC Champions League Two: Melbourne Victory and Adelaide United.

With the Jets the 2025 Australian Cup champions, therefore, their position went to another club – Melbourne Victory.

Auckland FC finished third behind Adelaide but as they are from the OFC, the Black Knights are ineligible. So, after 2025 Australia Cup runners-up Heidelberg United did not meet the AFC’s requirements, fourth-placed Victory were best placed in the A-League Men standings.

Central Coast Mariners won the competition, then known as the AFC Cup, back in 2023-24.

Melbourne Victory

Victory are back in the Champions League proper for the first time since 2020, when they reached the ACL Elite Round of 16. They featured in the ACL Elite play-off round in 2022 but lost to Vissel Kobe.

Newly-appointed Venezuelan head coach Giovanni Savarese is ushering in a new era for Victory.

Adelaide United

Adelaide will feature in the ACL Two after losing their ACL Elite play-off to Stefan Mauk’s CAHN.

After the AFC’s decision to increase Australia’s spots in continental competition from 2026-27, Craig Goodwin and the Reds are back in Asia for the first time since 2016-17 when they competed in the ACL Elite group stage.

ACL Two Format

The AFC Champions League Two starts with 32 teams split into eight groups of four across the East and West regions. Each club play six group-stage matches, home and away against the other three teams in their group.

The top two from each group advance to the Round of 16. The Round of 16, quarter-finals and semi-finals are all played over two legs, before the tournament concludes with a single-leg final.

Japan’s Gamba Osaka are the reigning champions.

Prize Money

Final (runners-up): $1million ($1.4million AUD)
Semi-finals: $240,000 per team ($336,480 AUD) / $960,000 total ($1.34million)
Quarter-finals: $160,000 per team ($224,304 AUD) / $1.28million total ($1.8million AUD)
