First Alert Weather: Cloudy, cool Thanksgiving with COLD mornings in view

TheTools · 26.11.2026 11:18 · source · permalink

JACKSONVILLE, Fla. — Bands of showers are leaving the NE FL/SE GA area early this morning

  • There may still be a lingering shower or some drizzle after sunrise
  • Besides that, it’s a dry & cloudy day for the holiday
  • Temperatures struggle in the 50s and low 60s with a stiff breeze all day, grab the jackets
  • We may have a peek or of sun before sunset this afternoon south of I-10
  • Then the mornings get COLD!
  • Temperatures fall all the way down into the 30s inland and the 40s all the way to the beaches
  • We’ll see more sunshine Black Friday and even into Saturday
  • Daytime temps stay below average in the 60s through the weekend
  • The Jags game on Sunday looks cloudy & a touch cool, but not bad
  • A fast-moving and weakening storm system brings us some rain late Sunday & early Monday
  • Then we’re back to clouds & onshore winds through at least mid-week next week

TROPICS

  • Nothing
  • Hurricane season ends next Monday, November 30
