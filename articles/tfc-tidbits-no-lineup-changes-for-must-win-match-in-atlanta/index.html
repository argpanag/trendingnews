Same starting 11 for 2nd game in a row from Fraser

There are no changes to Toronto FC's starting lineup for its must-win match vs. Atlanta United FC at Mercedes-Benz Stadium on Sunday (4:30 p.m. ET/Apple TV). 

Coach Robin Fraser is using the exact same starting 11 from Wednesday's 2-1 home win over D.C. United, putting his trust in those players for a second game in a row after they helped Toronto earn a crucial result in its final home match of the regular season.
