Gold Rate Today: 24K, 22K Gold And Silver Prices Fall

TheTools · 25.11.2026 05:31 · source · permalink

Gold and silver prices declined across the Indian retail market on Wednesday, with gold falling by ₹290 to ₹1,22,570 per 10 grams and silver dropping by ₹340 to ₹1,45,530 per kg.

In the futures market, gold prices extended their decline amid weak spot demand. On the Multi Commodity Exchange (MCX), gold contracts for December delivery fell by ₹627, or 0.51 per cent, to ₹1,21,702 per 10 grams, with a business turnover of 1,039 lots.

Analysts attributed the decline in gold prices to weak spot demand. Globally, gold futures declined by 0.31 per cent to USD 3,545.92 per ounce in New York. Meanwhile, MCX silver futures for the December contract were trading 0.44 per cent lower at ₹1,44,795 per kg at 9.10 am.

Among major cities, 24-karat gold was priced at ₹1,22,060 per 10 grams in New Delhi, ₹1,22,270 in Mumbai, ₹1,22,360 in Bengaluru, ₹1,22,100 in Kolkata and ₹1,22,460 in Hyderabad.

The 22-karat gold rate stood at ₹1,11,888 per 10 grams in New Delhi, ₹1,12,081 in Mumbai, ₹1,12,163 in Bengaluru, ₹1,11,925 in Kolkata and ₹1,12,255 in Hyderabad.

Silver (999 fine) was quoted at ₹1,44,810 per kg in New Delhi, ₹1,45,060 in Mumbai, ₹1,45,180 in Bengaluru, ₹1,44,870 in Kolkata and ₹1,45,290 in Hyderabad.

In Hyderabad, the 24-karat gold rate stood at ₹1,22,460 per 10 grams, while 22-karat gold was priced at ₹1,12,255 per 10 grams. Silver was quoted at ₹1,45,290 per kg.
