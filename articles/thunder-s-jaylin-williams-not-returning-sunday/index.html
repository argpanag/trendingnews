Thunder's Jaylin Williams: Not returning Sunday

TheTools · 26.10.2026 04:01 · source · permalink

Williams won't return to Sunday's game against the Clippers due to right wrist soreness, Nick Crain of SLAM reports.

The Thunder will be shorthanded in the frontcourt for the rest of the game since Williams sees plenty of minutes as Isaiah Hartenstein's backup at center. He went scoreless (0-1 FG, 0-1 3Pt) and didn't record a single stat in five minutes. His status should be considered questionable for the second half of the back-to-back set Monday against the Suns.
