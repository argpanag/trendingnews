Arsenal handed Jobe Bellingham warning as he emerges from brother Jude's shadow

TheTools · 24.11.2026 13:14 · source · permalink
  • Alex Conrad, Sports Writer
  • Published: 13:14, 24 Nov 2026
  • Updated: 13:14, 24 Nov 2026

It's taken some time, but Jobe Bellingham appears to have found his feet at Borussia Dortmund.

And, perhaps most crucially, he's shaken off the tag of simply being Jude Bellingham's brother.

Jobe, who is two years younger than Jude, joined Dortmund from Sunderland in June 2025.

The move sparked instant comparisons to the elder Bellingham given he made a similar switch to the German giants in July 2020, albeit at the ripe age of 17 and from Birmingham City.

Jude spent three years at Dortmund before he earned a big money move to Real Madrid, where he continues to shine.

Given the trajectory of Jude's career, it placed an almighty amount of pressure on Jobe's shoulders to live up to his brother's feats.

But after a mixed debut campaign in Germany in which he started 29 of his 45 appearances, the weight of the Bellingham surname no longer appears to be weighing as heavily on Jobe.

Raph Honigstein's review of Jobe Bellingham

"He's taken a bit of time to emerge from the shadows of his brother, who was such a huge success at Dortmund," German football reporter Raph Honigstein said on talkSPORT Breakfast.

"The last six weeks or so, he's really come into his own. He's started to establish himself, started to become a starter. He was noticed by [Thomas] Tuchel and picked for England accordingly.

"All of a sudden, he's no longer a young player developing, but a real impact and a player Dortmund can count on."

Jobe is now set to face one of his biggest midfield tests this season as Dortmund travel to Arsenal for a bumper Champions League clash.

Honigstein believes 'all eyes' will be on the 21-year-old as he looks to nullify whoever lines up in Arsenal's midfield.

But the Gunners have their own issues to worry about as star goalkeeper David Raya will miss the contest due to an arm injury sustained in the 2-2 draw with Newcastle on Saturday.

Regardless of who Mikel Arteta names in his Arsenal line-up on Tuesday, Dortmund will go into the game brimming with confidence.

Why Borussia Dortmund could cause Arsenal serious headaches

Niko Kovac's side sit top in the Bundesliga with 24 points after ten games and boast the league's stingiest defence, having conceded just seven goals all season.

However, Dortmund have scored only 17 goals in Bundesliga action, a figure bettered by seven of their domestic rivals.

"They grind out results," Honigstein said.

"Niko Kovac is known for his defensive stability. His teams like to play a little on the counter-attack.

"They don't set the world alight in terms of entertainment, but he has brought a consistency to the team that has eluded them ever since [Jurgen] Klopp left them. Thomas Tuchel had a really decent go at it, played some brilliant football, but no-one's been able to win the league since Klopp left.

"Now, they have a decent shot."

Dortmund's Champions League showdown with Arsenal gets underway from the Emirates Stadium at 8pm.
