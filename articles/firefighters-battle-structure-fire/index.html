Firefighters battle structure fire

TheTools · 28.09.2026 05:30 · source · permalink

CHARLESTON, W.Va. (WSAZ) - Kanawha County 911 says that firefighters were battling a structure fire over on Maryland Avenue just after 11 p.m.

911 says that the structure was abandoned, and nobody was inside when it started.

Charleston Fire responded to the scene alongside Charleston Police.
