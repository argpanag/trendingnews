Bruno Fernandes: Man Utd captain confirms contract offer but says negotiations are on hold

TheTools · 22.11.2026 18:24 · source · permalink

Bruno Fernandes exclusively told Sky Sports that Manchester United have offered him a new contract but he has put discussions on hold as he insists the club's priority must be returning to winning ways.

Fernandes has rejected two contract offers from United, Sky Sports News understands, with his current deal due to expire at the end of the season. The club's initial proposal was made in the summer when discussions first began over a new deal, but was turned down by the Portuguese, who signed his last contract in August 2024.

United hold an option to extend the 32-year-old's stay at Old Trafford by a further year, which would also prevent him from speaking to foreign clubs in January over the possibility of a free transfer.

The United captain, who has repeatedly been linked with a move away from Old Trafford, confirmed an offer has been made but dismissed suggestions that negotiations are ongoing or that he has demanded more money or an extended deal.

Fernandes also made clear he would not stand in the club's way if they decided his future lay elsewhere, insisting he has always prioritised United and would not simply stay to run down his contract.

Asked for an update on his contract situation ahead of Manchester United's clash with Liverpool, live on Sky Sports on Super Sunday from 4pm (kick-off at 4.30pm), Fernandes said: "No, there's no negotiations ongoing, but I've seen people saying that he's asked for more money, he's asked for more years. If I wanted more money, I wouldn't be here. I had somewhere to go to get much more money than being at Manchester United.

"I could have been in a place that is much warmer than Manchester.

"Two summers ago, three summers ago, last summer, I always had the chance to leave and I always chose to be here. The thing is, I've always showed how much I care about the club and how much I want to win at this club. And I always said, in the moment the club thinks it's time for me to move on, I will move on.

"I'm not going to be the player that I've seen many in this time at Manchester United. They're going to stay here just by staying, just finish my contract and then we will see. In the moment the club says, 'Bruno, it's time to part ways', I'm going to be the first one walking out the door.

"Yeah, the club offered me a new contract, so I think that, from their side, shows that they want me to stay. And as you said, there are no negotiations going on.

"We spoke about the contract at the end of last season, we came in the beginning of this season speaking about the contract, but about the context of the team and the club, I just put that to a side because I don't think it's the right moment, neither for me, nor for the club, to be talking about negotiations because the most important thing now is that the club gets back to win games."

'I still want to win Premier League and Champions League with Man Utd'

Fernandes insists his ambitions at United have not changed, despite the club's struggles, and remains determined to bring major honours back to Old Trafford.

"My aim will always be to win the Premier League and the Champions League within the club I am," he said.

The midfielder refused to accept that simply qualifying for the Champions League should become United's primary target. "I will never hide from that, I will never say, no, our position at the moment needs to be to get top four."

Fernandes acknowledged winning the Premier League this season was likely to be beyond United but insisted that was not his expectation when the campaign began.

He also believes supporters have been too patient with the club, recognising their frustration after years without a league title.

"They've been far too patient with us," he said.

'There was never a problem with Carrick'

Fernandes dismissed suggestions of a breakdown in his relationship with former United boss, Michael Carrick, following his comments during an interview after a 4-1 defeat to Como last month and his subsequent spell on the bench.

The United captain revealed Carrick had personally explained his decision to leave him out against Bournemouth, insisting it had nothing to do with his interview.

"The manager came to speak to me after that game, or before the Bournemouth game, to say, 'look, I'm not going to start you tomorrow' and everything.

"There's nothing to do with the words of Como, I know people will make it around that. I just want to tell you that we need to change something, I need to make a change, performances have not been great, so I just want you to know that there's nothing to do with that," Fernandes said.

He added that he subsequently approached Carrick to clear up any potential misunderstanding, with the manager reassuring him there was no personal issue.

"I told him, 'I think that you probably got our words in the wrong way, and you might be upset with me, and rightly so, if you got it in the way everyone is speaking about it'. And he said, 'Bruno, I'm not; I just put you on the bench because I thought it was the best for the team'."

Fernandes said his return to the starting XI the following game was further evidence that there had been no fallout between the pair.

'Pochettino brings good, positive energy'

Fernandes offered a positive early assessment of new United manager Mauricio Pochettino after beginning work with him.

The captain described the new boss as energetic and demanding, revealing Pochettino had already made clear the standards he expects from his players.

"He's someone that likes to bring a very good energy to the team, someone very positive, very strong, strict, said it straight away that he won't allow us to not give him what he demands from the team, from each player," Fernandes said.

"He wants the club to succeed so much, he wants to win games, and that's pretty clear and obvious by the way he and his staff speak and want us to improve."

Fernandes revealed he came close to joining Tottenham in 2019 when Pochettino was in charge at the north London club.

The midfielder confirmed he met with Tottenham's board, who outlined how he could fit into the manager's system, with the prospect of playing alongside Harry Kane, Dele Alli and Heung-Min Son proving attractive.

"Obviously I was a very young player that was eager to come to the Premier League. They had Kane there, they had Dele Alli, Son, the chance of playing with those players in front was very attractive," he said.
