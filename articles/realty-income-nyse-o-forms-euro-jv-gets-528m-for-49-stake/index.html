Realty Income (NYSE:O) Forms Euro JV; Gets €528M for 49% Stake

TheTools · 15.09.2026 04:09 · source · permalink

Realty Income will receive approximately €528M as KKR buys a 49% equity interest in a euro-denominated JV holding stabilized European net lease assets at a 5.9% initial cap rate post-fees; closing expected Sept. 30, 2026.

This extends Realty Income’s private capital platform into Europe, continuing a trend of using JV capital to fund growth and diversify funding. A larger catalyst would be closing on time and scaling the JV or repeating similar deals that materially lift FFO or reduce leverage.
