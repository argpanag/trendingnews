Formula 1 fiesta at the Madring: The latest episode of the ServusTV programme ‘Sport & Talk’ from ‘Hangar-7’ looks back at the Grand Prix debut at the IFEMA exhibition centre on the outskirts of the Spanish capital, Madrid. The stars of the sport put their foot down for the first time on the Spanish street circuit, and once again the beaming winner was Kimi Antonelli.

The championship leader was also in luck on his way to the eighth Grand Prix victory of his career, as pole-sitter Lando Norris was hampered by the timing of the virtual safety car and ultimately had to settle for third place. As a result, the Mercedes hopeful was able to celebrate his second consecutive Grand Prix victory following the race at Monza.

Memories of a horrific fire accident

Speaking of Monza – these days mark the 50th anniversary of Niki Lauda’s comeback following his fire accident. Eyewitness Hans-Joachim Stuck recalls the events. Meanwhile, a modern-day hero is taking on a whole new challenge. Four-time Formula 1 world champion Max Verstappen is set to compete against 100 rivals in an epic kart race. And he’ll be starting from the very back of the grid.

Will the Dutchman manage to overtake all 100 opponents? Stuck and former Grand Prix driver and current ServusTV F1 expert Patrick Friesacher explore this and other questions. Anyone who doesn’t want to miss this expert discussion can tune in today, Monday, from 9.15 pm on ServusTV and ServusTV On.

MotoGP: Spectacle at Spielberg

The two-wheeled heroes aren’t being left out either. The fastest racing series on two wheels is entering the final quarter of the season, and high tension is guaranteed as the World Championship is fiercely contested. The leader, record-holding world champion Marc Márquez, has the same number of points on his World Championship tally as his closest rival, Jorge Martín. Marc’s brother Alex has already secured one win this season and will be joining the Red Bull KTM Factory Racing Team next year. In Hangar-7, he and KTM’s Head of Motorsport, Pit Beirer, will discuss the present and the future, joined by the 2021 Spielberg winner, Brad Binder.
