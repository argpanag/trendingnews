New England Revolution host Inter Miami in heavyweight clash | MLSSoccer.com

TheTools · 02.11.2026 00:37 · source · permalink

The Eastern Conference's No. 2 seed is up for grabs when the New England Revolution host Inter Miami CF in Matchday 34's Sunday Night Soccer presented by Continental Tire matchup.

How to watch & stream

  • Apple TV

When

  • Sunday, Nov. 1 | 7 pm ET/4 pm PT

Where

  • Gillette Stadium | Foxborough, Massachusetts

Standings

  • 2nd in Eastern Conference
  • 58 points (18W-10L-4D)

Last game

New England topped Red Bull New York, 3-2, for their fourth straight win. 

Club legend Carles Gil became the eighth player in MLS history with 100 regular-season assists, setting up Dor Turgeman's first of two goals. Dániel Gazdag netted the late game-winner at Gillette Stadium.

Players to watch

  • Carles Gil: The 2021 Landon Donovan MLS MVP is enjoying a stellar season, producing a career-best 13 goals and a team-high 26 goal contributions.
  • Peyton Miller: The 18-year-old homegrown attacker has jumped into the US men's national team picture amid tallying a career-best 7g/8a.
  • Matt Turner: Alongside representing the USMNT at his second FIFA World Cup this summer, the former Goalkeeper of the Year's 134 saves are the second-most in MLS.
  • Brooklyn Raines: Like Miller, the box-to-box midfielder featured in this year's 22 Under 22 Rankings and made his USMNT debut last month.

Need to know

With a win on Sunday, New England can lock up the Eastern Conference's No. 2 seed in the Audi 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs.

To do so in their regular-season home finale, against the reigning MLS Cup presented by Audi champions, would add further proof of concept for the club's project under first-year head coach Marko Mitrović.

Standings

  • 3rd in Eastern Conference
  • 56 points (15W-6L-11D)

Last game

Miami needed a 90th-minute equalizer from summer signing Riquelme Fillipi to salvage a 2-2 home draw with FC Cincinnati. 

Legendary Uruguayan striker Luis Suárez opened the scoring at Nu Stadium with a trademark golazo, before Fillipi cancelled out strikes from Kenji Mboma Dem and Samuel Gidi.

Players to watch

  • Lionel Messi: The legendary Argentine No. 10 is approaching a third straight Landon Donovan MLS MVP award behind a league-best 40 goal contributions (25g/15a).
  • Luis Suárez: At age 39, the iconic striker is still producing at an elite level and is second on the Herons with 28 goal contributions (16g/12a).
  • Rodrigo De Paul: A fellow 2022 World Cup champion with Messi, De Paul is a midfield enforcer who also leads Miami with 16 assists.
  • Casemiro: The former Real Madrid and Manchester United star joined over the summer, giving even more A-List talent to Miami's high-profile squad.

Need to know

Winless in their last two games, Miami need a road victory to keep the East's No. 2 seed within reach and stay on track for a 2027 Concacaf Champions Cup berth.

Playoff momentum will be key as the Herons look to become the first back-to-back MLS Cup champions since the LA Galaxy in 2011-12.
