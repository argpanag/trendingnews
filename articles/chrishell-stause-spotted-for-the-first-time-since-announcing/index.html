Chrishell Stause is stepping out for the first time since announcing her split from partner G Flip.

The Selling Sunset alum, 45, was spotted out in Los Angeles walking her dog Peace on Wednesday, Nov. 4. The star was seen with a serious expression on her face as she wore a white striped top, black sweatpants, a long tan jacket and pink Givenchy slides.

Stause's first public spotting comes over a week after she revealed she and the Australian musician, 33, were no longer together in a teary Instagram video posted on Oct. 26. That same day, G Flip later shared their own announcement, noting they were "feeling very heavy" about the split and they weren't "able to talk."

Chrishell Stause in L.A. on Nov. 4
Credit: Clint Brewer Photography / BACKGRID

Shortly after their breakup, a source close to Stause shed light on the reasons why the couple ended their relationship after more than four years together, telling PEOPLE that being an ocean apart played a big role in their split.

"The distance is what really drove the two of them apart," the insider said. The two were splitting their time between California and Australia, and the extensive travel proved to be too difficult in the end. "The long flights, going weeks without seeing each other, and time difference took its toll."

"They were trying to make the 'marriage,' that was never legal, work," the source added. "They both wanted to have kids, but they also both didn't want to uproot their lives for the other." Despite celebrating multiple weddings together over the course of their relationship, Stause and G Flip never legally wed, PEOPLE previously confirmed.

While announcing their split last week, both partners said they will "always" love the other, and the source suggested the future isn't completely clear for the pair.

Chrishell Stause in L.A. on Nov. 4
Credit: Clint Brewer Photography / BACKGRID

"Chrishell and G Flip still very much love each other and haven't ruled anything out, as far as reuniting romantically, in the future. It was hard to dream about a life together, when they both had that dream on different continents," the insider said.

The now-exes first met on Halloween in 2021 when Stause was still dating her former Selling Sunset costar Jason Oppenheim, and G Flip was also in a relationship.

Five months after revealing her split from Oppenheim, Stause went public with her relationship with G Flip during a Selling Sunset reunion episode in May 2022.

"I recently have been spending a lot of time with someone that's very important to me. Their name is G Flip. They're non-binary, so they go by they/them," she said during the episode. "And they are an extremely talented musician."

Stause has since stepped away from the real estate docusoap, which is currently filming its 10th season, after revealing it was negatively impacting her mental health.
