The MCU's Final Project Before 'Avengers: Doomsday' Hailed as "One of Marvel's Best" in First Reactions

TheTools · 09.10.2026 10:35 · source · permalink

As the MCU marches toward the chaotic crescendo of Phase Six, with Avengers: Doomsday promising a multiversal reckoning unlike anything Earth's Mightiest Heroes have faced before, Marvel is releasing one final project before the universe ushers in its massive crossover event. Following a surprise screening at New York Comic Con 2026 (NYCC), the first reactions out of the convention have officially hit the internet, with the early wave of buzz suggesting that Marvel may have saved its most ambitious, masterfully crafted story for last, as viewers are already hailing it as one of the absolute best entries in the entire superhero franchise.

Serving as the highly anticipated conclusion to the visionary "Westview trilogy" that began with WandaVision and continued in Agatha All Along, VisionQuest is spearheaded by showrunner Terry Matalas. The series stars Paul Bettany reprising his role as the rebooted, soul-searching synthezoid on a deeply internal mission to reclaim his lost memories and humanity. The ambitious premise has certainly caught the attention of viewers, as the early consensus regarding the eight-episode series is overwhelmingly positive, with many people branding it as a definitive must-watch that completely revitalizes Marvel Television's storytelling.

Industry insiders are celebrating its unique format as an anthology, noting how its distinct film style and tone breathes fresh life into the franchise. @Mar_Tesseract on X raved about VisionQuest after watching all eight episodes, saying, "This is easily one of Marvel's best shows – up there with Wonder Man and Loki. A strange, beautiful, visually striking, and emotionally powerful conclusion to the trilogy that began with WandaVision and continued with Agatha All Along. James Spader's inclusion as a different version of Ultron is a stroke of genius and Paul Bettany has never been better (or wittier) as Vision. This is the kind of series that proves, without question, comic book shows can have the emotion to match, and even surpass, their spectacle."

MovieWeb's own @mollyrockit wrote, “I’ve seen the first few episodes of VisionQuest and it's great. The writing is on par with WandaVision, and it has a very grounded, human aspect at its heart. Feels very entrenched in the MCU (like Brand New Day) and delivers some fun twists." Podcast host and journalist @BrandonDavisBD concurred, asserting that, "VisionQuest is a fantastic sequel to WandaVision. It's smart, weird, ambitious, and creative with a beating father/son heart," further noting that, "It seems to love being a part of the MCU and embracing as many corners as it can. As a longtime fan, it's very rewarding. Loved it."

Another glowing reaction arrived courtesy of @aeronchion, who made a bold claim, writing on X, "VisionQuest is Marvel Studios' best TV show of 2026. Insightful, emotionally precise, and mind-bending, it’s a reminder of why the MCU still owns the small screen when it trusts the story. The series delivers a genuine love letter to WandaVision fans, moving the story into something deeper and more mature that serves as a perfect closer to the trilogy. Paul Bettany shines in every episode, while James Spader's Ultron is exactly what the show needs." @DeeGlobalNerdz echoed these comments, saying, "VisionQuest is MARVEL running on top tier gas. It's raw and emotional. All the best parts of the MCU. Everyone involved understood the assignment."

Audiences will not have to wait long to see if VisionQuest lives up to its monumental hype, as the series is officially scheduled to premiere on Disney+ on Oct. 14, 2026. Rather than opting for a standard weekly release or a full binge-drop, the eight-episode limited series is following a unique mixed format: it kicks off with a two-episode premiere, followed by two-episode weekly batches, before shifting to single weekly installments for the final two chapters. This calculated rollout ensures that viewers have plenty of time to dissect every twist and turn before the finale streams on Nov. 11, 2026, ahead of Avengers: Doomsday on Dec. 18, 2026.
