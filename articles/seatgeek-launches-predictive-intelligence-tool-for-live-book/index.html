SeatGeek launches predictive-intelligence tool for live bookers

TheTools · 05.10.2026 12:30 · source · permalink

Ticketing-tech firm SeatGeek has launched a new product that’s for promoters and venues rather than music fans. TourIQ is a predictive-intelligence tool.

According to the company, it “tells a promoter or venue what a concert or third party event is worth at any building, for any artist, on any date, before a single ticket is sold, then generates a price for every seat in the house”.

The pitch is that it “replaces the guesswork with a number, before anything goes on sale”. The tool will sit within SeatGeekIQ, the company’s suite of tools for live-show bookers.

According to SeatGeek, TourIQ is already being used “on stadium tours from three of the largest promoters in the country”.

The company also revealed results from its use at a college-football match at Nissan Stadium in Nashville, claiming that TourIQ’s predictive scaling “sold 29% more tickets and generated 13% higher gross revenue than projected, with an average ticket price 13% lower”.
