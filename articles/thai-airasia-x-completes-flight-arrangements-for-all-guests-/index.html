Thai AirAsia X Completes Flight Arrangements for All Guests Affected by Delay of Flight XJ607 (Tokyo NRT – Don Mueang DMK) on 20–21 November 2026 — AirAsia Newsroom

TheTools · 24.11.2026 05:20 · source · permalink

Bangkok, 22 November 2026 – Thai AirAsia X sincerely apologizes to its guests for the prolonged delay of flight XJ607 from Tokyo (Narita) to Don Mueang on 20–21 November 2026, caused by an aircraft technical issue. The airline confirms that no stranded guests remain at Narita International Airport in Tokyo, Japan, following expedited arrangements to fly all affected guests back to Thailand via Thai AirAsia X flights over the past two days, as well as a special Thai AirAsia flight (FD9611) today.

Ms. Pattra Boosarawongse, Chief Executive Officer of Thai AirAsia X, stated, "Thai AirAsia X deeply apologizes for the flight delay that disrupted our guests' weekend travel plans. Following the detection of an aircraft technical issue on flight XJ607 prior to its scheduled return from Narita International Airport on the night of 20 November 2026, we were required—in strict adherence to our highest safety standards—to conduct an aircraft engineering inspection and await replacement parts from Thailand. However, the maintenance did not proceed according to the initial timeline, resulting in knock-on impacts for our guests the following day."

"Our ground staff at Narita and our support teams in Bangkok made every effort to secure overnight hotel accommodation for our guests. However, as the weekend coincided with a Japanese national holiday, hotels near the airport were fully booked, leaving us unable to provide alternative rooms. We also humbly accept the feedback regarding delayed on-ground communications, which we are taking urgent action to address and improve," Ms. Pattra added.

At present, the first group of guests affected by the night of 20 November flight has arrived in Thailand, and the second group has departed from Tokyo. The airline confirms that no further guests remain stranded.

To compensate for the delay and hotel accommodation, Thai AirAsia X is providing a total of THB 5,000 in Credit Account compensation to all affected guests, with no additional documentation required. This amount will be automatically issued to guests' registered AirAsia  Credit Accounts. Guests who do not have an AirAsia Credit Account may contact the dedicated Thai AirAsia X special assistance hotline at +66 2-078-1094 (available daily during operating hours), where our staff are ready to provide full assistance.
