Celtic Euro opponents make 'earthquake' vow ahead of Hoops showdown

TheTools · 23.11.2026 00:06 · source · permalink

Celtic's next Euro opponents Torreense's sports director has revealed he's proud of their performances after being tipped to struggle to win a single point in the Europa League.

The Hoops travel to Portugal next week for their latest game in the league phases, boosted by their first win in the section when they defeated Besiktas.

They play the tiny club of Torreense next in Leiria after the second-tier outfit won the Portuguese Cup last season with a shock win against Sporting Lisbon.

They return to the scene of their and Celtic's greatest-ever triumph at the weekend after they switched their home cup tie against Benfica to the Estadio Nacional do Jamor to accommodate a bumper crowd - at the same national stadium where Celtic famously became the first British side to win the European Cup in 1967.

And, ahead of two huge games for them, Torreense sports director Andre Sabino praised the players for collecting six points in the Europa League so far.

He said: "Never in my wildest dreams could I have imagined achieving so much in so little time.

"We have kept alive the honour of Portuguese football this season in the Europa League.

"Everyone expected that Torreense would only manage a maximum of one point in this Europa League.

"Everyone anticipated difficulties, but we didn't want to give up on being audacious.

"We've already achieved something historic and we want to continue on this path. We have confidence at home.

"We never considered not playing in Europe. We know that our qualification meant a small earthquake in national football and for us it was a source of pride.

"But we are worthy representatives of Portugal.

"It's not normal for a second division team to experience these demands. We don't have the conditions, the physical and human resources of the best clubs to recover the athletes, handle the travel and withstand all the mental and physical demands of European and league competitions.

"But what we're doing is exceeding expectations. We're in the Europa League to increase the value of the club and the players, so they become valuable assets in the market.

"We'll do our best and compete."
