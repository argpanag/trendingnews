Gallery: ‘Ramos plus Cisse’, ‘The derby on their mind’ – Today’s front pages in Italy

TheTools · 28.10.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

AC Milan are back in front of their own fans this evening as they welcome Bologna to San Siro, hoping to make it three league wins on the spin.

Milan bounced back from their defeat against Bournemouth in the Europa League with a 2-0 win away at Udinese on Sunday. Now, the task is to continue to decent start in Serie A with a victory against the Rossoblu, with an eye on Saturday’s derby against Inter.
