Pinnacles earthquake downgraded to magnitude 5.0; 2 foreshocks and 6 aftershocks recorded

TheTools · 20.11.2026 06:57 · source · permalink

The U.S. Geological Survey now lists the earthquake that struck near Pinnacles at 10:49 p.m. Thursday as magnitude 5.0, down from its earlier preliminary estimate of 5.1. Shaking was felt in Marina.

The quake was centered about 10 kilometers northwest of Pinnacles at a depth of 6.7 kilometers, according to the USGS.

Two smaller quakes were recorded before it: a magnitude 3.1 at 9:53 p.m. and a magnitude 3.3 at 10:12 p.m.

The USGS recorded several aftershocks, including a magnitude 3.0 at 10:52 p.m.; two magnitude 2.5 quakes at 10:57 and 10:58 p.m.; a magnitude 3.4 at 11:14 p.m.; a magnitude 2.6 at 11:19 p.m.; and a magnitude 2.7 at 11:21 p.m.

A Marina resident said they received a USGS ShakeAlert and felt shaking. The alert advised people to “drop, cover, hold on” if shaking intensified.
