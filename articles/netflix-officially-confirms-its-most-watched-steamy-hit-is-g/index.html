Netflix Officially Confirms Its Most-Watched Steamy Hit Is Getting a New Spin-Off Series

TheTools · 08.10.2026 15:15 · source · permalink

When you think of the most popular Netflix shows, a few names come to mind, including Stranger Things, Wednesday, and Squid Game. One of the streaming service's most enduring hits has been Bridgerton. Based on the books by Julia Quinn, each season explores the romantic life of a different member of the aristocratic Bridgerton family. The first and third seasons are among the top 10 most watched English-language seasons in Netflix history, and the fourth spent 10 weeks in Nielsen's top 10 rankings of streaming original series for the United States.

Netflix has capitalized on the success. It's already produced one spin-off series: Queen Charlotte: A Bridgerton Story. On the original show, Queen Charlotte is played by Golda Rosheuvel, but her spin-off turns back the clock and explores her life as a younger woman, where she's played by India Amarteifio.

Now, Netflix wants to give the same treatment to Violet Bridgerton (Ruth Gemmell), the mother of the eight Bridgerton siblings. According to The Hollywood Reporter, Netflix has picked up a limited series all about her romance with Edmund Bridgerton, the father of the Bridgerton children. Edmund dies some 10 years before the events of the original series.

At this point, we don't have a title for the new prequel series, nor do we know anything about casting. That said, Edmund Bridgerton is played by Rupert Evans in flashbacks on Bridgerton. As for Violet, she's played by Connie Jenkins-Greig on Queen Charlotte.

Nicholas Nardini (Queen Charlotte, Inventing Anna) will serve as a co-showrunner on the prequel series alongside Shonda Rhimes, who's an executive producer on the original show. Rhimes has talked about potentially making a show about Violet for years. "I think Violet is fascinating," she said back in 2023. "And there’s a story to be told there." She returned to that thought on an episode of Craig Melvin’s Glass Half Full podcast this past March, saying that there will "definitely" be more Bridgerton spin-offs in the future:

That’s an area that we’re excited about and know that that could be really rich and really mined well. I’ve always said that I thought that if we were gonna do something like that, Violet would be a great person to tell the story about, the Bridgerton mom.

Julia Quinn has written a novella about Violet's younger years, "Violet in Bloom," that could give the producers of the new show a place to start. While we wait for the new spin-off to materialize, the fifth season of Bridgerton is in the works. It will revolve around Francesca Bridgerton (Hannah Dodd) and Michaela Stirling (Masali Baduza).
