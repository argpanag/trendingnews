Telangana rains to ease as depression weakens

TheTools · 25.09.2026 02:49 · source · permalink

HYDERABAD: Rain activity is expected to gradually ease across Telangana on September 25 as the impact of a depression weakens.

Light to moderate rain is likely in several northern and northeastern districts, including Adilabad, Kumaram Bheem Asifabad, Nirmal, Mancherial, Jagtial, Peddapalli, Jayashankar Bhupalpally, Mulugu, Bhadradri Kothagudem, Karimnagar and Hanamkonda, till the evening.

Isolated thunderstorms likely in southern districts

Isolated thunderstorms are expected over Vikarabad, Sangareddy, Narayanpet, Mahbubnagar, Nagarkurnool, Wanaparthy and Jogulamba Gadwal during the afternoon and night.

Hyderabad may see isolated rain

Other districts are likely to experience isolated rain or remain dry. Hyderabad is likely to receive isolated rain during the day.
