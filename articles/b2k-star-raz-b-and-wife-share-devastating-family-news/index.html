B2K singer Raz B and his wife, Mona Li, are mourning the loss of their baby son, Astro Monte Thornton. The couple welcomed Astro just three months ago. His death came unexpectedly and suddenly earlier this week. The family has now asked for privacy during this painful time.

Raz B and Mona Li announce the sudden death of their 3-month-old son Astro

According to TMZ, sources close to the family said Astro died earlier this week. The cause of the death is still not disclosed. A family spokesperson confirmed the heartbreaking news to the outlet. "It is with heavy hearts that we announce the unexpected passing of Astro Monte Thornton, beloved son of Mona and De'Mario 'Raz B' Thornton of the R&B supergroup B2K," the spokesperson said.

The family spokesperson also asked people to give the family privacy as they cope with their loss. The statement said the family wants space as they work through this painful time. The spokesperson also expressed gratitude to everyone who has offered their support. The family said they are deeply thankful for all the love and support they have received.

Raz B announced Astro's arrival on Instagram on May 18. "Right now we're at the hospital getting ready to welcome our newest addition, Astro … another baby boy to the Thornton family," he wrote. Raz also recently celebrated Father's Day with his family. He shared a message on their Instagram account that said, "Thank you for your love, commitment and generosity for us and those you care for."

The sudden loss has left Raz B and Mona Li facing an unimaginable family tragedy. Astro's brief time with his family came during a joyful period for the Thornton family. Raz had recently shared a glimpse of that happiness with fans. Now, the family is asking for privacy as they grieve their son.
