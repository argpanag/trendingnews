Introducing “Delta Disruptive” Class For Flights Within Florida (Hilarious SNL Skit) - Live and Let's Fly

TheTools · 02.11.2026 13:50 · source · permalink

Forget Delta One. Saturday Night Live has imagined another cabin for Delta Air Lines it calls “Delta Disruptive Class,” and this one addresses a problem even a lie-flat seat cannot solve: the passenger who makes everyone else’s flight miserable.

In a spoof commercial from its October 31 episode, Saturday Night Live introduces “Delta Disruptive,” a separate section for passengers whose behavior makes them particularly unwelcome elsewhere on the aircraft.

The premise is simple: instead of letting these travelers ruin the flight for everyone, put them together behind a thick plexiglass partition.

The fictional cabin accommodates everything from obnoxious personal habits to full-blown onboard meltdowns. There are passengers clipping toenails, fighting over seats, and shouting conspiracy theories. One customer offers a testimonial while duct-taped to her seat. A Tiffany Gomas character also makes an appearance..

Naturally, the airline presents all of this with the polished enthusiasm of a commercial announcing an exciting improvement to your travel experience.

“All Delta Disruptive flight attendants are the toughest, gayest, ex-Spirit Airlines employees who have seen much worse.”

I love how Kenan Thompson, who plays flight attendant “Kevin,” grabs an emotional support iguana and throws it off the plane. He also has a ring on every finger, lol.

The new cabin class is rolling out soon…on flights within Florida. Book now…

CONCLUSION

“Delta Disruptive” is a joke, but the desire to enjoy a flight without becoming an unwilling participant in someone else’s viral video is quite real. Part of me wishes that we could throw unruly passengers into padded part of the plane instead of diverting and costing everyone time and money.

As usual with SNL skits, the joke captures a frustrating truth about flying: as carefully as you may choose your airline seat, you cannot choose the person sitting nearby. One passenger can make an otherwise pleasant flight unbearable.
