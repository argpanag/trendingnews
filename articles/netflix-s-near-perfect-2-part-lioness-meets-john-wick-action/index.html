Netflix's Near-Perfect 2-Part "Lioness Meets John Wick" Action Thriller Is a Global Streaming Sensation

TheTools · 07.11.2026 22:00 · source · permalink

Netflix has a new global streaming hit on its hands in the form of a two-season action thriller that blends the spy aspects of Taylor Sheridan's Lioness with the action of John Wick. Dark thrillers are currently taking over Netflix. Collision Course is the #1 movie on the platform right now, along with the dark crime thriller series Lupin, the fantasy thriller Dead-End Job, and the true crime thriller Taken: Natalee Holloway (that's a lot of thrillers). But with CBS delaying the new episodes of Tracker and Marshals, now is the perfect time to binge-watch Netflix's near-perfect action hit.

Season 2 of Black Doves is sneaking its way up the global streaming charts. Season 1, which dropped in 2024, was a major action hit and was quickly greenlit for a second season. Season 2 debuted on Nov. 5, and it wasted no time in becoming one of Netflix's biggest shows. At the time of writing, Black Doves is the fifth most popular series on Netflix globally. The aforementioned Taken: Natalee Holloway holds the top spot.

Things are slightly less impressive on the US charts, where Black Doves is the seventh most popular series right now. But it has only been two days, and the series is expected to quickly rise up the charts in the coming days.

'Black Doves' Blends 'Lioness' and 'John Wick' Into One Hit Series

Keira Knightley leads Black Doves as Helen, the wife of the British Defense Secretary who is secretly an undercover spy is forced to come out of hiding when the man she actually loves is murdered. With the help of her lifelong friend, a fellow brutal killer, Helen must get to the bottom of the murder while fighting for her life against the agency she used to work for.

The action is ripped straight from the world of Keanu Reeves' John Wick, while the worldbuilding feels reminiscent of Taylor Sheridan's Lioness, which also follows a female-led intelligence agency. Joe Barton created Black Doves. The series also stars Ben Whishaw, Sarah Lancashire, Andrew Buchan, Ella Lily Hyland, Andrew Koji, and Paapa Essiedu.

'Black Doves' Is a Near-Perfect Action Thriller

Season 1 of Black Doves was one of Netflix's highest-rated action series, and Season 2 is continuing that trend. Season 1 holds a near-perfect 92% Rotten Tomatoes rating. But Season 2 has managed to surpass that. At the time of writing, Season 2 has a 94% Rotten Tomatoes rating. Although, that could change in the coming days as more reviews are released.

As for the Popcornmeter, that remains to be seen. Season 1 held a modest 64% RT user rating. Season 2's audience score hasn't been generated yet, as not enough RT users have submitted reviews. But the audience ratings that have been submitted are overwhelmingly positive.
