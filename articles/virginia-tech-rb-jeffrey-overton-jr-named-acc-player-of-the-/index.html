Virginia Tech RB Jeffrey Overton Jr. Named ACC Player Of The Week

TheTools · 28.09.2026 18:29 · source · permalink

On Monday, Virginia Tech running back Jeffrey Overton Jr. was named ACC Running Back of the Week after his career day at Boston College.

In the Hokies' 21-14 victory in Chestnut Hill, Overton had 209 rushing yards on 25 carries for an 8.4 average, including the game-winning 76-yard touchdown run.

View tweet

"I know when Jeffrey Overton gets the ball, man, he's one play away from making a play," Tech defensive tackle Kemari Copeland said. "When he had that long touchdown, that was a sigh of relief. I was like, 'My boy Jeffrey Overton, he's going to get it done, man,' and I'm excited. He's young and he has a lot more time to play. He's going to be a dawg. He's going to be in the NFL one day, so I'm really excited we've got him here."

Overton was the first Tech player to run for 200-plus yards since Bhayshul Tuten posted a school-record 266 yards vs. Boston College in 2024 (the last time a Hokies running back was named ACC Player of the Week).

Here's the full list of 200-yard rushers, dating back to 1987:

• 2026: Jeffrey Overton Jr.'s 209 yards at Boston College
• 2024: Bhayshul Tuten's 266 yards vs. Boston College
• 2020: Khalil Herbert's 207 yards at Duke
• 2008: Darren Evans' 253 yards vs. Maryland
• 2006: Branden Ore's 203 yards vs. Clemson
• 2006: Branden Ore's 207 yards vs. Southern Miss
• 2004: Mike Imoh's 243 yards at North Carolina
• 2003: Kevin Jones' 241 yards at Pitt
• 2000: Michael Vick's 210 yards at Boston College

Overton also caught three passes for 19 yards, with a long of 12, and returned one kickoff for 19 yards, finishing with 247 all-purpose yards at BC.

Since 1987, here's a list of Tech players with 247-plus all-purpose yards in a single game:

• 2024: Bhayshul Tuten's 286 yards vs. Boston College
• 2020: Khalil Herbert's 357 yards at Duke
• 2015: Isaiah Ford's 250 yards vs. Tulsa
• 2012: J.C. Coleman's 253 yards vs. Duke
• 2008: Darren Evans' 273 yards vs. Maryland
• 2004: Mike Imoh's 294 yards at North Carolina
• 2002: Ernest Wilford's 279 yards at Syracuse
• 2000: Andre Davis' 273 yards vs. West Virginia
• 1986: Eddie Hunter's 258 yards vs. South Carolina

For the season, Overton leads the Hokies in rushing, with 409 yards and four touchdowns on 60 carries (6.8 average). He's also hauled in 10 passes for 114 yards and a score.
