Electro Optic Systems has appointed Kaïs Mnif as MARSS Group president and chairman.

Electro Optic Systems has appointed Kaïs Mnif as MARSS Group president and chairman.

MARSS, the counter-drone specialist, was acquired by EOS in May 2026.

Mnif reports to EOS chief executive and managing director Dr Andreas Schwer and will be based at the MARSS office in Nice.

“MARSS has built technology and a team that customers trust to protect what matters most,” Mnif said.

“I am delighted to join and look forward to working with Johannes, Andreas and everyone at MARSS and EOS to grow the business.”

Mnif will help MARSS meet urgent customer needs and continue its high growth as part of EOS.

Johannes Pinl remains chief executive officer of MARSS and will continue to lead sales and growth in new markets across Europe, the Middle East and Africa.

Mnif has held senior general management, commercial, business development and transformation roles in technology, defence, security, transport and medical imaging.

He spent the past 12 years at Thales Group, most recently as CEO of a subsidiary, and served on the boards of several affiliated companies.

Before Thales, he held leadership roles at Motorola and Alstom in semiconductors, urban transport and air navigation systems. Mnif holds an engineering degree from INSA and an executive MBA from INSEAD.

“MARSS continues to win new contracts and customers, and Kaïs brings the experience we need for its next phase,” Schwer said.

“His record in integrating acquisitions and growing international businesses will help MARSS and EOS operate as one, while Johannes drives growth in new markets.”
