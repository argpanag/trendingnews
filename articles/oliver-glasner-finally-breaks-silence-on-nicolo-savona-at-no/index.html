Oliver Glasner finally breaks silence on Nicolo Savona at Nottingham Forest

TheTools · 10.09.2026 16:00 · source · permalink

Oliver Glasner has now issued an update on the fitness of Nicolo Savona at Nottingham Forest.

The Italian has not featured for the Reds since the 1-0 defeat against Braga in the UEFA Europa League last season, which fell on 22nd January, due to a serious knee injury.

There hasn’t been much in terms of an update on Savona in recent weeks, but Glasner has now provided one for Nottingham Forest fans.

Oliver Glasner admits Nicolo Savona “had a few setbacks”

Speaking in his press conference on Thursday afternoon ahead of the trip to Villa Park for Saturday’s Premier League clash, the Austrian said: “It’s just Nicolo Savona that is still out unfortunately. He’s had a few setbacks, so we’re progressing slowly, but he’s on track, and there is no pressure on him. So far he has reacted fine.”

It will be a special day when the 23-year-old finally makes it back out onto the pitch.

Savona – who Roma admire – was making excellent progress after arriving from Juventus in a £13.4m move back in August 2025, breaking into the side and also having an impact at the other end of the pitch with two goals and two assists in 20 appearances last term.

The Nottingham Forest No.37 has since seen the competition for starts increase following the arrival of Daniel Munoz from Crystal Palace – but we hope that will only motivate the Italy U21 international to return as quickly as possible, rather than discourage him.

Fortunately for Glasner, with Ola Aina and Munoz also available in the right-back department, there is no need for Nottingham Forest to rush Savona back.

The man born in Aosta can afford to take his time and ensure his knee has made a full recovery before returning to action.

Furthermore, Glasner is not the type to rush anyone back, having given Cheick Doucoure and Chadi Riad considerable time to focus on recovering from serious injuries at Crystal Palace.
