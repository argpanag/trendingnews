It's back-to-school time in New Jersey, and with that comes the start of the college journey for some high schoolers.

The first stop? Taking the SAT.

Statewide, the average SAT score was 1,052 out of a possible 1,600, according to the latest state data from the 2024-25 school year. Students averaged 519 on math and 533 on reading and writing. (Look up your high school's average scores in the chart below.)

New Jersey was above the national average. Nationally, the average SAT score was 521 on reading and writing and 508 on math. That's a combined average score of 1,029.

But some of New Jersey's most selective public high schools have average SAT scores that blow the state and national averages out of the water.

Edison Academy Magnet School in Middlesex County had an average SAT score of 1,539, just 61 points off of a perfect score of 1,600.

Students at the admissions-only high school scored an average of 778 points on the math portion and 761 on the reading and writing portion.

Less than 180 students were enrolled in the Edison Academy Magnet School last school year. The school offers students concentrations in several STEM fields, including mechanical and civil engineering, and electronics engineering and computer science.

Magnet schools and specialized public high schools — which require students to apply and qualify for admission — dominate the list of schools with the highest SAT scores in New Jersey.

The highest scoring traditional public school is Millburn High School in Essex County, with an average score of 1,391.
