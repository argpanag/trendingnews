Freo roars back to end Swans fairytale

The Swans’ finals fairytale is over after a stunning, 21-point final-quarter comeback by Fremantle - the Dockers reaching just their second grand final in club history.

For three quarters at the SCG in front of more than 45,000 fans, Fremantle had been second best, hustled and harried by Sydney’s physicality.

But in the fourth quarter, Sydney finally succumbed to pressure and goals from Patrick Voss, Mason Cox, Shai Bolton and Isaiah Dudley to win the game by 12 points.

Fremantle’s Shai Bolton spoke to Kayo Sports post game.

Special. The whole game we were down. The fourth quarter, we just wanted to bring the way we play and I felt we did that. Now we are into a grand final and I can’t wait.

On the last term:
All these boys were just playing their role. JL always talks about it. If we play our way, I think we will go far.

Elation for those in purple, heartbreak for the Swans

The Swans players are gutted. Dane Rampe was in tears and consoled by Tom McCartin while those on the team bench and the coaches box were similarly distraught.

The Swans have given everything they had but couldn’t find the spark they needed in the last term.

Caleb Serong looked to be giving some caring words to Errol Gulden.

The Dockers guys are elated, just overjoyed. They looked gone when they fell four goals behind but they have run over the top of the Swans.

Embattled coach Justin Longmuir had a lovely smile on his face after looking anything but happy for much of this match.

Full-time: Fremantle are into the grand final

The Fremantle Dockers are into the 2026 AFL grand final after winning tonight’s preliminary final at the SCG in front of 45,129 fans.

Isaiah Dudley kicked the game-clinching goal after a sharp handball from Murphy Reid deep inside their forward 50 with about 90 seconds to go.

Tom Papley looked to have given the Swans a late chance to force extra time, but his shot bounced into the post.

The Dockers lost their qualifying final but have recovered to make the decider for just the second time in their club’s history.

Last time? 2013, when they lost to… Hawthorn.

The Hawks face the Brisbane Lions at the MCG tomorrow.
