Spring is making itself felt across South Africa today, with showers and thundershowers spreading over much of the interior, from Gauteng and the Free State to North West, Northern Cape and Mpumalanga. Limpopo sits under cloud, while the Eastern Cape wakes to mist in Gqeberha. The Western Cape is the exception, with clear skies and plenty of sunshine in Cape Town. Winds stay mostly light and gentle throughout.

Cape Town is the warmest at a lovely 31°C, while Limpopo's Polokwane is coolest overnight at 11°C. Durban and the storm belt see the wettest weather. Please note severe thunderstorm warnings for Escourt, Ekuvukeni and Ladysmith in KwaZulu-Natal, plus veld fire conditions around Springbok.
