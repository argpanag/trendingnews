Ukraine strikes major Russian petrochemical and oil refining plants, helicopter manufacturer, military confirms

TheTools · 15.11.2026 03:23 · source · permalink

Ukrainian forces struck three Russian military-industrial facilities overnight on Nov. 15, including two major petrochemical and oil refining plants in Russia's Republic of Tatarstan and a helicopter manufacturer in Bryansk Oblast, Ukraine's General Staff said.

The strikes are part of Ukraine's broader campaign targeting Russian military-industrial facilities and infrastructure supporting Moscow's war effort.

"Every day these plants are idle means fewer tires for the vehicles transporting ammunition to the front, fewer rubber parts for armored vehicles and aircraft, fewer raw materials for the missile industry, and less fuel for the Russian military," the General Staff said.

Explosions and a fire were reported at Nizhnekamskneftekhim, which is part of the Russian petrochemical holding Sibur, the General Staff said. It is located approximately 1,200 kilometers (745 miles) from Ukraine.

The complex produces synthetic rubber, plastics, and other petrochemical products used in various industries, including the production of military vehicle tires, aircraft components, and other military equipment.

Ukrainian monitoring Telegram channel Exilenova+ previously on Nov. 15 reported that residents had witnessed an attack on one of the city's industrial facilities, likely the Nizhnekamskneftekhim petrochemical complex.

Ukrainian forces also struck the TAIF-NK refinery in Nizhnekamsk, hitting its sole primary oil-processing unit, known as ELOU-AVT-7. A fire was reported at the site.

The facility supplies petroleum products and raw materials that can support Russia's fuel and military-industrial needs. It is located more than 1,000 kilometers (620 miles) from Ukraine's border.

The extent of the damage at both facilities is being assessed, according to the General Staff.

Nizhnekamsk Mayor Radmir Belyaev said the city came under a prolonged drone attack on Nov. 15, damaging industrial facilities and an apartment building. Residents were evacuated, and several people were injured, he said.

The third target was the Monolit company in Trubchevsk, in Russia's Bryansk Oblast. The enterprise manufactures aircraft, including helicopters, as well as spare parts and components, and carries out metalworking.

The industrial facilities in Nizhnekamsk have been targeted in previous Ukrainian long-range attacks.

On Sept. 13, Ukraine's General Staff confirmed strikes against the TANECO refinery in Nizhnekamsk and the Slavyansky refinery in Krasnodar Krai.

Ukraine also struck the TANECO refinery on Aug. 20, according to the General Staff.
