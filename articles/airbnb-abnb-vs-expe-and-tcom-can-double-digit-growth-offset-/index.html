Before getting into our analysis, let's take a look at the business and why some investors like the stock so much.

Airbnb, Inc. (NASDAQ:ABNB) operates a global online marketplace connecting hosts and guests for stays, experiences, and services. The big question for investors is whether Airbnb can keep growing beyond home rentals into hotels, Experiences, Services, advertising, and AI-powered travel tools, even as regulators tighten the rules around short-term rentals. Airbnb's business model requires little capital, runs on healthy margins, and its bookings are growing faster than major rivals in online travel.

In the second quarter of fiscal 2026, revenue rose 17% year over year to about $3.6 billion, and the number of nights and experiences booked grew 10%, outpacing 6% growth at Expedia Group (NASDAQ:EXPE) and 5% at Booking Holdings (NASDAQ:BKNG). Over the past twelve months, revenue grew 13.6% to roughly $13.2 billion. Even so, the stock trades at about 39.8 times trailing earnings, well above the peer median of roughly 26.7 times, meaning investors are already paying up for several more years of strong growth to materialize. The bullish case is built on Airbnb continuing to outgrow the broader travel market by using its large base of travelers and hosts to expand into new categories. The company is adding boutique and independent hotels, Experiences, Services, sponsored advertising, smarter fees, and AI-powered search, and it says artificial intelligence has already cut the time it takes to build and launch new features by as much as 60%. Airbnb is also on solid financial footing, holding about $9.6 billion in net cash after accounting for its debt, and it generated $4.6 billion in operating cash flow in fiscal 2025.
