Dogecoin News: DogeOS Tests DeFi as Remittix Gives 1,000 Holders PayFi App Access

TheTools · 02.10.2026 22:35 · source · permalink

Dogecoin’s latest development story is no longer just about a meme. DogeOS has opened a public testnet intended to bring DeFi applications to the DOGE community, giving holders a possible reason to do more than wait for the next price surge. A separate payments project is also putting a new product into users’ hands, with a more direct route toward everyday money movement.

The first 1,000 existing holders invited by Remittix can access PayFi testing with EUR and USD options. Its wallet and Markets product already give users other ways to interact with the app. The two tests raise different questions: can DOGE grow an application economy, and can RTX become the token behind a service people return to whenever they need money in a bank account?

Dogecoin News: What Is DogeOS Testing?

DogeOS announced a public testnet on September 30 for applications including DeFi, gaming and AI experiences. The team behind the MyDoge wallet wants to give builders new ways to use DOGE without changing Dogecoin’s base layer.

DOGE traded around $0.096 in early October. A successful application layer could broaden the asset’s narrative if developers build tools that people actually use. The public testnet is an early proving ground, so traders will watch what applications attract users as the project progresses.

Dogecoin’s community gives developers access to a large audience. The key price question is whether new apps make that audience more active in ways that sustain demand for DOGE beyond a short-lived announcement.

Why Remittix’s PayFi Access Matters for RTX

Remittix PayFi aims to take supported crypto and arrange fiat delivery through compatible bank accounts. Initial access has been offered to 1,000 existing RTX holders. A contractor paid in digital assets could use the service to receive local currency for ordinary expenses, then return whenever another payment arrives.

That repeated use is the commercial heart of the idea. Global remittances reach hundreds of billions of dollars annually; a sliver of that market could mean close to a billion dollars in yearly transfers through an app. Fees on such volume could become meaningful revenue, with RTX intended for settlement and staking as the ecosystem grows.

Remittix reports more than 10,000 wallet downloads and over $50 million in cumulative Markets volume. A user who starts with the wallet could find trading and PayFi within one service, while an existing trader may discover the bank payout when they need to use proceeds. Remittix is testing whether those actions can become an easy, repeatable journey.

The Next Milestones for Both Communities

The DogeOS testnet gives DOGE holders a development story to follow. Remittix’s PayFi invitations come ahead of an announced $0.46 final presale stage and planned November 24 RTX debut. More than 40,000 people have joined the RTX presale, according to the project.

Both groups will learn more as people actually use the products. For RTX buyers, successful transfers could be the first visible step toward a recurring payments business in an enormous market. If that use grows before exchange trading starts, the opportunity to examine RTX at this stage could pass quickly. Explore the live Remittix allocation while the first holders are testing PayFi.
