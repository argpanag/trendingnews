Tesla v taxi: Hollywood star Cliff Curtis injured in waterfront crash

TheTools · 01.10.2026 08:38 · source · permalink

Subscribe to listen

Kiwi Hollywood star Cliff Curtis was injured in a crash on Auckland’s waterfront that also left a taxi driver seriously injured.

The 58-year-old actor has said he is “deeply saddened by this incident” and is “collaborating” with authorities.

Curtis was one of two people in a Tesla when it struck a parked Prius taxi about 10.30pm on September 23 at the intersection of Jellicoe and Halsey Sts, next to Karanga Plaza.

Firefighters arrived at the crash to find a man trapped inside a Prius, Fire and Emergency Auckland city senior station officer Michael Manning told the Herald at the scene.

Crews cut him out of the car and provided life support until St John arrived.

“Given this area of town, it was a very high-speed impact,” Manning said.

Hato Hone St John said two people – one in a serious condition and one moderate – were taken to Auckland City Hospital.

A third person also suffered minor injuries.

Earlier this week police said no charges had been laid and the investigation into the cause of the crash was ongoing.

A witness said of the crash, “It was just a big, big impact.

“The crash made the taxi move maybe three to four metres.”

The owner of Wynyard Mart, which is about 20m away from where the crash happened, described the crash as a loud “boom”.

He was told by a witness the two men in the Tesla were middle-aged between 45 and 50 years old. The crash precinct was particularly busy that night because of a show at the nearby ASB Waterfront Theatre.

He said they were thankful no one else was hurt, as the Karanga Plaza is popular with children and tourists.

No one was home when NZME visited Curtis’ Rotorua home after the crash and again this week.

Curtis’ New Zealand agent Gabriella Larkin-Bruce did not respond to multiple approaches by the Herald.

Curtis issued a statement this afternoon confirming he was hospitalised with internal injuries after the crash.

“I am deeply saddened by this incident. My thoughts are with those who were hurt, and their families at this time,” he said.

Curtis said that he assisted the other injured parties with first aid until emergency services arrived.

The statement said he was “collaborating fully with relevant authorities”. No further comment would be made “out of respect for the process”.

Curtis is one of New Zealand’s most successful movie stars, having appeared in blockbuster films like the Avatar and Fast & Furious franchises, Once Were Warriors, Whale Rider and Live Free or Die Hard.

Curtis was photographed alongside UFC star Israel Adesanya at the Breakers’ season-opening win over Illawara at Spark Arena three days before the crash.
