WEST PALM BEACH, Fla. (CBS12) — More than 4.3 million tubes of toothpaste are included in a nationwide recall involving children’s and adult products, according to an FDA Enforcement Report.

The recall covers products sold under the Brush Buddies, evo and ARU brands. Pritam International Private Limited initiated the voluntary recall on Sept. 10. The FDA classified it as Class II on Oct. 19, citing deviations from current good manufacturing practices.

The recalled products include:

  • Brush Buddies Barbie anticavity toothpaste, bubble gum flavor
  • Brush Buddies Hot Wheels anticavity toothpaste, strawberry flavor
  • Brush Buddies Crayola Silly Scents anticavity toothpaste in bubble gum, watermelon and strawberry flavors
  • Brush Buddies My Little Pony Scents anticavity toothpaste, bubble gum flavor
  • Brush Buddies vanilla ice cream, mint chocolate chip ice cream, cookies ’n cream and chocolate ice cream toothpastes
  • Brush Buddies whitening anticavity toothpaste with baking soda, cool mint flavor
  • BB (Brush Buddies) cavity protection toothpaste, cool mint, two-pack
  • BB (Brush Buddies) advanced whitening toothpaste, peppermint flavor
  • evo advanced whitening toothpaste, peppermint flavor
  • evo sensitive toothpaste, sweet mint flavor
  • ARU gum health toothpaste, soothing mint flavor
  • ARU cavity protection toothpaste, cool mint flavor

The FDA’s Class II designation means use of or exposure to a recalled product may cause temporary or medically reversible health effects; the likelihood of serious adverse health consequences is considered remote. The FDA report lists identifying information for the affected products.
