Cherries relentless in cup win over Villa as rotated side impresses - Player ratings

TheTools · 29.10.2026 02:27 · source · permalink

THE Cherries were relentless as they cruised into the quarter-finals of the Carabao Cup with a 3-0 win over Aston Villa on Wednesday night.

Goals from Amine Adli, Lewis Cook and Álvaro Rodríguez saw them safely progress with a comfortable win over their visitors, booking a place in the last eight where they will meet Andoni Iraola’s Liverpool.

The Daily Echo rates the Cherries players in victory.

Michele Di Gregorio: 8

The Italian made a few top saves to preserve the Cherries’ clean sheet. The pick for me was a big palm to deny Brian Madjo in the second half.

Michele Di Gregorio (Image: Richard Crease)

Julio Soler: 8

I thought the left-back was excellent and got plenty of joy down the Bournemouth flank. He picked up an assist for the first Cherries goal with a good run, top touch and clipped cross for Adli to score.

António Silva: 8

Mr Consistent at the moment, the Portuguese was imperious again at the back as the Cherries centre-back pair dealt well with Brian Madjo, who looks like he will develop into a handful as a centre-forward.

Bafodé Diakité: 8

Diakité looked to be the centre-back taking most of the work against Madjo and dealt well with his physicality.

Julio Soler (Image: Richard Crease)

Juanlu: 8

A top first start for the team. He had a slightly shaky start, where he wasn’t perhaps as precise as he needed to be and had a couple of moments defensively, but he really grew into the game and looked confident by the end.

Lewis Cook: 8

A great, if a little fortunate, goal, but Cook continues to impress in midfield.

Ryan Christie: 7

All about the tempo for Christie, as Rose said they tried to control the ball with a bit more possession against a side who like to build with the ball. The Scot helped Bournemouth to do just that.

Lewis Cook (Image: Richard Crease)

Amine Adli: 8

The Moroccan showed excellent tenacity to get on the end of Soler’s cross and finish for a goal on his first start of the season. Otherwise, the winger looked bright and was replaced at the break, presumably to manage his minutes as he returns from injury.

Marcus Tavernier: 7

The Englishman was pulling the strings in the first half and wasn’t far away from getting on the scoresheet again before he too was replaced at the break to give him a well-deserved rest.

Ben Gannon-Doak: 9

The Scot looks to be building into the season and he was impressive yet again. He continued to look like he could beat his man at will and looks to be confident.

Álvaro Rodríguez: 9

The striker got his first open-play Bournemouth goal with a good finish, but it was his all-round play that impressed me most. He led the press excellently and Rose was keen to praise him after the game.
