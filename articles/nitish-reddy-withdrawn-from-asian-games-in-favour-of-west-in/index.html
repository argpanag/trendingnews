Nitish Reddy withdrawn from Asian Games in favour of West Indies ODIs

TheTools · 16.09.2026 22:47 · source · permalink

India have announced their squad for the home ODI & T20I series against the West Indies. Shubman Gill leads the side which includes the experienced duo of Rohit Sharma and Virat Kohli. KL Rahul has been named vice-captain for the series, replacing Shreyas Iyer, who deputised for Gill in the previous ODI assignment, against England. Iyer will lead the Indian team in the one-off T20I against Japan as well as the Asian Games that overlap with the one-dayers.

Nitish Kumar Reddy has been withdrawn from the Asian Games in favour of the ODIs. Naman Dhir, the Punjab batter, has earned his maiden call-up along with Auqib Nabi. Yashasvi Jaiswal, who played in the Afghanistan ODIs but did not feature in the England one-dayers, has been included as the backup opener.

Alongside Iyer, the other players who toured England but are not in the squad are Ishan Kishan, Washington Sundar, Axar Patel, Jasprit Bumrah, Harshit Rana, Arshdeep Singh and Shivam Dube. All of them are part of the Asian Games, barring Rana, who was replaced by Yash Thakur.

Rishabh Pant, however, does not find a place in the ODI squad, with Dhruv Jurel preferred as the second wicketkeeper. Pant, meanwhile, will lead Rest of India in the Irani Cup.

India's ODI squad vs West Indies: Shubman Gill [C], Rohit Sharma, Virat Kohli, Ruturaj Gaikwad, KL Rahul [VC], Dhruv Jurel, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Ravindra Jadeja, Kuldeep Yadav, Prasidh Krishna, Gurnoor Brar, Mohammed Siraj, Auqib Nabi, Yashasvi Jaiswal, Naman Dhir.

The ODIs will be followed by the T20Is, for which Iyer returns as captain along with the rest of the T20I regulars. A full squad will feature in the T20Is, making their way back from the Asian Games.

India’s T20I team vs West Indies: Shreyas Iyer [C], Abhishek Sharma, Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, Ishan Kishan [WK], Sanju Samson [WK], Tilak Varma [VC], Shivam Dube, Kuldeep Yadav, Axar Patel, Washington Sundar, Ravi Bishnoi, Nitish Kumar Reddy, Arshdeep Singh, Prince Yadav, Mayank Yadav.

BCCI confirmed that Varun Chakaravarthy has been ruled out of the upcoming Japan T20I as well as the Asian Games due to a side strain, with Vipraj Nigam as his replacement.

Updated India squad for Japan T20I & Asian Games: Shreyas Iyer (C), Abhishek Sharma, Sanju Samson (WK), Ishan Kishan (WK), Shivam Dube, Tilak Varma (VC), Axar Patel, Washington Sundar, Ravi Bishnoi, Jasprit Bumrah, Arshdeep Singh, Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, Yash Thakur, Vipraj Nigam
