Pedro Pascal welcomes you to yet another Big Coat Season

TheTools · 06.10.2026 09:31 · source · permalink

Every single year, there comes a point when your little jacket stops being much help. You're cold. Your hands are cold. You're telling whoever you're with that you're actually fine, thanks, when you're obviously not. Maybe that time hasn't even come yet for you – but it will. Thankfully, Pedro Pascal has arrived with a much better solution: get a massive coat and wear the hell out of it.

At Chanel's spring/summer 2027 show in Paris last night, the actor pulled up in exactly the sorta outerwear that makes October feel like a great idea. While creative director Matthieu Blazy was showing what the ladies will be wearing when it gets warm again, Pascal was getting very comfortably dressed for right now. Next summer looks lovely, but some of us have only just worked out where we put our jumpers.

His big Chanel overcoat was decked out in checked tweed, with giant lapels and cute belt. But just when things were starting to look a bit too sensible, it was fired up with five orange buttons up front. You might miss them from a distance, but up close, they're a nice reminder that even a very grown-up coat can have some fun, too.

Underneath, the Materialists star wore a cosy black rollneck, black trousers, and some chunky heeled boots, finishing things off with his signature black-framed glasses. It's a straightforward combo, and you probably have some version of it at home.

Chanel has always been a womenswear brand, but guys have been helping themselves for years. Jacob Elordi, Kendrick Lamar, and A$AP Rocky have all worn pieces from the house, and just last month, Central Cee swapped his usual Swoosh sneakers for the Chanel Trainer. Pascal has been a friend of the brand for a while. Surely he can put in a good word for a menswear line?

And that's the nice thing about Big Coat Season. You can spend ages working out what to wear underneath, or you can throw on a black jumper and some trousers and let your outerwear do most of the work. Pedro Pascal went with the second option, and he was sitting front row at Chanel. Should be more than enough for your dinner reservation, then.
