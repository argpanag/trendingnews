'I love this place' James Martin on why Bolton Food Festival remains in his diary

TheTools · 05.09.2026 10:13 · source · permalink

James Martin has revealed why he continues to return year after year, as Bolton Food and Drink Festival.

The celebrity chef has now appeared at the festival 18 times, making it one of the longest-running dates in his calendar.

James said: "What a difference 18 years has made, and what a difference 21 years has made as well.

(Image: Dominic Holden)

"This is the one that's in the diary every single year, I love this place."

The chef headlined on the Saturday, taking part in three live cookery demonstrations, autograph signings and appearances around the festival site.

He also spent time meeting traders and visitors during the event, which attracted people from across the region and beyond.

More than 200 traders took part in the festival, alongside live music, cookery demonstrations and free family activities.

(Image: Paul Heyes)

James said: "What makes this festival special is not only the fact that you've got amazing traders and free activities out there, it's the people; that's what's special about Bolton."

The festival took place over four days during the August Bank Holiday weekend, with visitors also seeing appearances from Michelin-starred chef Marco Pierre White and Mowgli Street Food founder Nisha Katona.

Comedy from Justin Moorhouse and the Fawlty Towers Dining Experience also formed part of the programme.

(Image: Paul Heyes)

Bolton Council's Executive Cabinet Member for Culture, Cllr Debbie Newall, said: "Major events like this are an essential part of the council's Visitor Economy Strategy which is boosting Bolton businesses and delivering real benefits for our residents.

"Every August Bank Holiday Weekend the festival brings hundreds of thousands of people to Bolton and contributes millions to the local economy. 

(Image: Paul Heyes)

"But it also serves as an opportunity to show Bolton off to a major audience, and thanks to Visit Bolton's Insider's Handbook everyone can easily see everything our borough has to offer visitors all year round."

Live music was staged across two stages, with Liberty X and The Real Thing joined by local performers.

Despite changeable weather during the four days, the festival continued to welcome visitors throughout the event.

(Image: Paul Heyes)

Cllr Akhtar Zaman, Leader of Bolton Council, said: "For more than 20 years, this festival has been one of Bolton's greatest success stories.

"What began with a small number of gazebos in Victoria Square has evolved into one of the largest and most highly regarded food festivals in the UK.

"From renowned chef demonstrations and artisan markets to live music, family entertainment and street performances, the event continues to grow in both scale and reputation year after year."
