Salvation Army of Lorain County kicks off Red Kettle Campaign with luncheon

TheTools · 05.11.2026 00:00 · source · permalink

Members of the Salvation Army of Lorain County recognized local volunteers and community workers on Nov. 4 as they celebrated their annual Christmas Luncheon at Lorain County Community College in Elyria.

The Salvation Army kicked off its Red Kettle Campaign through the luncheon, highlighting its past work in assisting families in need.

The luncheon brought together community members, business representatives and local leaders to celebrate the organization’s work in the county during the holiday season. During the event, representatives shared the impact of the organization over the past year.

From July 1, 2025, through June 30, 2026, the Salvation Army distributed $218,387 in rental assistance. During the same period, 585 households received a combined $302,643 in utility assistance.

This assistance was provided through the United Way’s UCAN program, which combines service from United Way, the Salvation Army and seven other social service providers to help low-income families.

Jody Keller, the Salvation Army’s service extension director, said the organization’s food assistance and Christmas programs distributed 26,733 food boxes and gave toys to 2,308 children for Christmas last year.

“These numbers represent so much more than services provided,” Keller said. “They represent hope restored, families supported, hunger relieved and smiles brought to children’s faces.”

Speakers at the Nov. 4 luncheon encouraged attendees, both individuals and organizations, to support the Red Kettle Campaign through financial contributions and volunteer opportunities.

Volunteer activities include ringing bells at red kettle locations, distributing food boxes and toys, assisting with the food pantry and preparing and serving hot meals.

“Behind every meal served, every food box distributed and every Christmas gift given out is a person, a family or a child whose life has been touched by someone’s generosity,” Keller said.

Elyria Municipal Court Judge Gary Bennett served as the luncheon’s keynote speaker. Bennett described the established relationship between the court and the Salvation Army.

An Elyria Municipal Court community service program allows convicted individuals to fulfill community service requirements by purchasing and donating needed supplies to the Salvation Army, rather than traditional physical labor service.

Donatable items include diapers, food and personal hygiene products that would benefit families throughout the community.

At the end of the luncheon, several individuals and organizations were recognized for their contributions to the Salvation Army.

Darlene “Sue” Wells and Diane Yuhasz were honored with Volunteer Champion Awards for their volunteer services. Wells has been volunteering with the food pantry program since 2011 and Yuhasz has spent the past five years helping prepare and serve hot meals.

Kaplan’s Furniture and Mattress and M.J. Griffith Paving both received Community Champion Awards for their support of the Salvation Army as well.

Red Kettle sponsors were also recognized, including businesses, organizations and individuals who have supported this past year’s campaign.

Nancy Scharfeld, Lorain County Advisory Board member, wrapped up the luncheon with a speech to attendees.

“Your dedication, generosity and commitment to our community reflect the very spirit of the service the Salvation Army strives to demonstrate every day,” Scharfeld said.

People interested in the organization were encouraged to consider joining. The Salvation Army currently operates two community centers in Lorain and Elyria, as well as five service units across the county.

For more information about volunteer opportunities, people can visit salvationarmyvolunteer.org.
