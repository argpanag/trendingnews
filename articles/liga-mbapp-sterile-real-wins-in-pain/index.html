Liga: Mbappé sterile, Real wins in pain

TheTools · 10.10.2026 21:10 · source · permalink

FC Barcelona having easily won against Getafe earlier in the evening, Real Madrid had to win otherwise they would already be well behind in the standings. In the seventh minute, Kylian Mbappé saw his left-footed shot hit the post.

A few moments later, the captain of the France team this time came up against Péter Gulacsi (21st). The Madrid center forward and his teammates returned to the locker room under the Bernabéu bronca.

In the second half, the whistles continued to rain due to the lack of intensity provided by the Merengue club. Shortly after the hour mark, Yan Diomandé opened the scoring with a cross shot from the right. Although the flag was raised, the Ivorian international’s first goal since his big transfer was finally validated by the VAR (1-0, 63rd).

Vinicius, the backcombing
