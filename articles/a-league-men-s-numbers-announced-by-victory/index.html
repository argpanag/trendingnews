Melbourne Victory can confirm player squad numbers for the 2026/27 A-League Men’s season. 

Giovanni Savarese’s team are primed for the new campaign after a pre-season that has seen them bank plenty of game time courtesy of their opening AFC Champions League Two fixtures and a run to the 2026 Hahn Australia Cup Final.

Their A-League effort starts away to Wellington Phoenix on Sunday, October 18, kick-off 1:00pm AEDT, before a Melbourne Derby at AAMI Park on Saturday, October 24, kick-off 7:40pm AEDT, with tickets available to purchase by clicking here.
