Alex Michelsen's love for tennis stems from his parents.

The tennis player, who is competing in the 2026 US Open, was born to Erik and Sondra on Aug. 25, 2004, according to his US Open profile. The couple also share their older son, Jake, whom they welcomed three years earlier, per Jake's former swimming and diving profile for Biola University.

Athleticism runs in the family, particularly in tennis. Sondra played college tennis at San Diego State, while Erik was a three-time All-American player at California's University of Redlands, according to The New York Times.

Growing up in California, Alex started playing tennis by the age of 4, per the United States Tennis Association (USTA) of Southern California. While his parents dreamed of him earning a tennis scholarship to college, Alex knew turning pro as a teenager was his destiny.

"That was always the goal, just get a scholarship to a Division I school," Sondra told the organization in May 2026. "But he was doing so well and finally just said, 'OK, I want to go pro.' So we said, 'Let's do it.' "

"He's believed this was all gonna happen the way it's happened," Erik previously told the Tennis Channel. "We, as parents, didn't quite see that, but I think Alex did."

Here's everything to know about Alex Michelsen's parents, Erik and Sondra Michelsen.

Erik and Sondra share two children

Alex Michelsen and his parents Erik and Sondra.
Credit: USTA Southern California/Instagram

Erik and Sondra welcomed their older son, Jake, on July 31, 2001. Three years later, they welcomed their younger son, Alex, on Aug. 25, 2004.

Their kids both pursued sports. Alex built a career in tennis, while Jake pursued swimming and diving at Biola University in La Mirada, Calif. According to the USTA of Southern California, the pair were also involved in other hobbies growing up, including soccer, baseball and piano lessons.

After graduating from college, Jake began working in business as an assistant project manager for Guthrie Development Co. in Irvine, Calif., according to his LinkedIn profile.

Erik and Sondra have backgrounds in tennis

Alex comes from a family of tennis players, as both of his parents played the sport in college. Growing up, Alex benefited from both of his parents' expertise in tennis.

"My parents had contrasting game styles," Alex told The New York Times in August 2023. "My mom would stay at the baseline and make every ball. I took that from her. My dad likes to serve and volley, come to the net and be more creative, so I took that from him. I feel like I've combined those two very well."

Sondra was once an educator, which is why she hoped Alex would pursue tennis in college at the University of Georgia, where he was enrolled before deciding to pursue the Association of Tennis Professionals (ATP) Tour.

"I wanted him to go to college because it was so much fun," Sondra told the USTA. "Traveling and being with your teammates and the football games and parties. It's a good way to grow up a little, too."

However, going pro early just made sense. "He was already No. 140 in the world," she added. "And there wasn't one college player at that time even close to that." 

Alex told The New York Times that he was "really happy with my timeline."

"I never thought this would happen this quickly," he added. "I thought I would go to college and then try to figure out the tour after college."

Sondra was Alex's first tennis coach

Alex Michelsen of the United States during day seven of the 2025 Australian Open.
Credit: Hannah Peters/Getty

Sondra started playing tennis with Alex when he was just 4 years old, she told the USTA of Southern California.

She also said Alex's consistent wins continually delight her. "He always surprises me. I'm like, maybe just win one round and then he wins a tournament," she told the association. "He still surprises me now with what he's doing on the tour."

Sondra and Erik often support Alex from afar

Sondra and Erik often support Alex's tennis career without stepping into stadiums. During the 2023 US Open, they remained home to work and care for Holly, the family's King Charles cavalier spaniel.

"He doesn't really like us going too much because he says he worries about us too much," Sondra told the USTA of Alex's view on his parents attending his matches.

At the 2025 Australian Open, Alex spoke about his mom keeping up with his tennis journey in a post-match interview. "I'm sure she's watching … no chance I'd be here without her. Thanks, Mom. Love you."
