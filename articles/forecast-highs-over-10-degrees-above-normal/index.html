FORECAST: Highs over 10 degrees above normal

TheTools · 16.09.2026 22:23 · source · permalink

BOWLING GREEN, Ky. (WBKO) - Strong high pressure will keep hot temperatures locked in place through the weekend. While most will stay dry, a few spotty afternoon showers or storms cannot be ruled out.

Persistent Heat: Highs will reach the middle 90s each afternoon, with heat index values climbing into the triple digits (100°–104°). Overnight lows will stay in the low to middle 70s, offering little relief from the heat.

Autumn Pattern Shift: Relief arrives just in time for the official start of fall on Tuesday as the heat dome weakens and shifts east. This shift opens the door for rain chances Monday into Tuesday, and cooler highs by midweek. Middle 80s are still not quite sweater weather, but definitely more seasonable.
