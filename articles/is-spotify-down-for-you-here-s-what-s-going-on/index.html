Is Spotify down for you? Here's what's going on

TheTools · 29.09.2026 14:16 · source · permalink

Affiliate links on Android Authority may earn us a commission. Learn more.

Sep 29, 2026 — 10:16 AM ET

  • Spotify appears to be down at the moment.
  • Thousands of reports are coming in that the service is experiencing issues.

If you were trying to listen to some music or your morning podcast on your drive to work today, you may have noticed that there’s something off with Spotify. Don’t worry, the problem isn’t on your end. The digital audio streaming service is currently down.

A wave of reports are coming in on Downdetector, claiming that Spotify is offline. At the time of writing, there are over 3,500 reports, and the numbers are still rapidly climbing. Based on when the reports started coming in, it appears that the outage started at around 9:30 AM ET.

The complaints are far and wide. There are reports that the service is slow, while others are unable to view their profiles or listen to music. Some are even saying that they got logged out on both web and mobile and can’t log back in.

Users are also flocking to Twitter to report the outage. They claim that they are unable to search for songs. Additionally, these users are running into error messages, like the one above, which reads, “Something went wrong. Have another go?”

We will keep an eye on the situation. When it looks like the service is back online, we will update this article. Stay tuned…
