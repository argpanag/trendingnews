The Game Awards 2026 Nominees – Play Gears of War: E-Day, Grand Theft Auto VI, Resident Evil Requiem, and More on XBOX

TheTools · 17.11.2026 19:30 · source · permalink

Jump in and start playing 50 nominated games today across XBOX Series X|S and XBOX on PC.

For 25 years, XBOX has remained focused on building games players love, and our anniversary year has been a particularly strong example. From Gears of War: E-Day, Forza Horizon 6, and Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4, to hugely anticipated games like Fable, players have more great games to play on XBOX than ever.

Today, The Game Awards has announced this year’s list of nominees and titles across XBOX’s creative teams have earned eight nominations across multiple categories. Outside of gaming, Prime Video’s ‘Fallout’ has been nominated for Best Adaptation. Thank you to all of the teams that brought these great games to players.

Voting is now open on The Game Awards’ official site – tune into The Game Awards on December 10 to see if your favorite games from 2026 take home an award.

Our publishing partners also received nominations across a range of categories, with many games available to play today with Game Pass. 007 First Light and CONTROL Resonant are both nominated for Game of the Year and playable across devices with XBOX Play Anywhere. Game Pass subscribers can play nominees like Gears of War: E-Day, Forza Horizon 6, No Man’s Sky, and more. See below for the full list of nominees playable on XBOX, including Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4, and remember you can vote for your favorites right now over at TheGameAwards.com.

In the meantime, many of these titles will be available at their lowest prices yet with XBOX’s Black Friday sale, starting November 19 – find out more here.
