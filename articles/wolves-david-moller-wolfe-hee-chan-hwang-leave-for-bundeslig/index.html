Wolves: David Moller Wolfe & Hee Chan Hwang leave for Bundesliga

TheTools · 04.09.2026 13:22 · source · permalink

David Moller Wolfe (left) and Hee Chan Hwang have both left Molineux on season-long loans

  • Published
    1 September 2026

Wolves duo David Moller Wolfe and Hee Chan Hwang have made deadline day departures to move to the German Bundesliga in season-long loans.

Norwegian left-back Moller Wolfe, 24, who signed for £10m from AZ Alkmaar last summer, has moved to former European champions SV Hamburg.

Moller Wolfe had started the season still in a Wolves shirt following last season's relegation from the Premier League, but has headed to Northern Germany with an option to make the move more permanent next summer.

Hee Chan Hwang, who first arrived at Molineux in 2021, also has an option to turn his move to Gelsenkirchen-based Schalke permanent next summer.

The South Korean scored 22 goals in his 119 games in a Wolves shirt.
