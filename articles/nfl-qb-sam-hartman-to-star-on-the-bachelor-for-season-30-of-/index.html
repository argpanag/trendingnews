NFL QB Sam Hartman to Star on 'The Bachelor' for Season 30 of Hit ABC Show

TheTools · 13.11.2026 14:14 · source · permalink

Sam Hartman is keeping busy between his job as a quarterback on the Washington Commanders' practice squad and now searching for love on national television.

ABC announced on Friday that Hartman will be the star of the new season of The Bachelor premiering on March 21.

Hartman spent six seasons playing college football from 2018 to 2023. He won the starting job at Wake Forest as a true freshman and went on to appear in 48 games over five years.

After the 2022 season, Hartman transferred to Notre Dame for his final year of college eligibility. He was named the starter and led the Irish to a 10-2 record in 12 starts.

Hartman went undrafted in the 2024 NFL draft before signing with the Commanders. His primary role has been on their practice squad, though he has been elevated to the active roster twice. He has yet to appear in a regular season game.

The 27-year-old Hartman will join a long list of athletes to appear on the reality dating show. Jesse Palmer, Arie Luyendyk Jr. and Colton Underwood were all leads on The Bachelor.

Jordan Rodgers and Clay Harbor were both contestants on The Bachelorette. Rodgers won his season and went on to marry Joelle Fletcher in 2022.
