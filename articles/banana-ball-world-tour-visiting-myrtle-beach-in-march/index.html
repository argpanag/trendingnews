Banana Ball World Tour visiting Myrtle Beach in March

TheTools · 16.10.2026 02:01 · source · permalink

MYRTLE BEACH, SC (WMBF) - Banana Ball World Tour 2027 will be making its way to Myrtle Beach in March.

The Myrtle Beach Pelicans announced on Thursday that the Banana Ball will play at the Pelicans Ballpark on March 18-20.

The series will feature the Loco Beach Coconuts and the Indianapolis Clowns.

Tickets will be available by entering the ticket lottery list here.

Lottery entries close on Oct. 31, 2026.
