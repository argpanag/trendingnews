Air quality is considered unhealthy when the 24-hour PSI is in the range of 101 to 200, while an hourly PM2.5 reading between 56 and 150 indicates an elevated concentration of fine particles.

The PSI – a 24-hour rolling average of six key air pollutants that is used in planning for outdoor activities the next day – had begun an upward trajectory at midnight on Oct 5.

PSI readings remained in the unhealthy range in central and western Singapore for most of Oct 5. Air quality in eastern Singapore also reached unhealthy levels at 6pm.

At 9pm, the PSI readings for the north and south of Singapore climbed to the unhealthy band, joining the other three regions in that zone.

Dry conditions are expected to continue over Singapore and the surrounding region on Oct 5. The risk of haze affecting the Republic remains, as the prevailing winds are expected to blow from the south-east or south-west.
