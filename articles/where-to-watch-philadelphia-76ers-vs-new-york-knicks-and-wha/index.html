Aclassic Eastern Conference night at the Wells Fargo Center. The Philadelphia 76ers host the New York Knicks in a divisional matchup that comes with the two franchises on opposite trajectories at the start of the season: Philadelphia, sitting atop the standings; New York, stuck below .500 and desperate to turn things around.

When and where to watch the Sixers vs Knicks game?

The game will be played at the Wells Fargo Center in Philadelphia, a venue with a capacity of 20,478 fans, on Friday, October 30, 2026 at 7:30 pm Eastern Time (6:30 pm Central Time and 4:30 pm Pacific Time).

In the United States, the clash between New York and Philadelphia is broadcast nationally via Prime Video streaming.

Philadelphia 76ers vs New York Knicks: Two trains going in different directions

The picture of the standings says it all. The Philadelphia 76ers come in with a 4-1 record, leading the Atlantic Division and in seventh place overall in the league, tied in winning percentage (.800) with several of the current top teams.

They have scored 554 points and allowed 537 in their five games: they are not a defensive juggernaut, but they win close games, which is what defines solid teams in October.

Facing them, the New York Knicks have a 2-3 record, which leaves them fourth in the Atlantic and 19th in the league ranking, two games away from the divisional top.

Their point differential is almost neutral (+1: 549 points scored, 548 points allowed), a sign of a competitive team that, nevertheless, can't seem to turn the tide of the game. For a team with their aspirations, a start like this raises early red flags.

Star clash: the names that lead the Knicks and Sixers rosters

In Philadelphia, the team's core revolves around Joel Embiid in the paint and Tyrese Maxey running the perimeter, a one-two inside-outside combination that forces any opponent to choose their weaknesses. Around them, the franchise has assembled a veteran and formidable roster.

LeBron James, Jaylen Brown, Kentavious Caldwell-Pope, and Anfernee Simons form a rotation with plenty of experience and scoring ability, while the young point guard VJ Edgecombe, just 21 years old, brings energy and potential from the second unit.

The Knicks respond with their own offensive engine. Jalen Brunson is the brains and the executioner in clutch moments, supported by Karl-Anthony Towns, a power forward capable of punishing opponents from the post and from three-point range.

On the wings, the defensive tandem of Mikal Bridges and OG Anunoby gives New York the tools to disrupt Philadelphia's playmakers, with Josh Hart providing a wild card of energy, rebounding, and competitiveness. The bench adds firepower with Jordan Clarkson and interior experience with Andre Drummond.

The key tactical adjustment will be how New York contains Embiid's presence in the paint. If Towns and Drummond can keep him in check without getting into foul trouble, the game opens up. If Embiid imposes his physicality, Philadelphia dictates the pace from the free-throw line and inside.

What's at stake in the Eastern Conference standings

This is, above all, a battle for the Atlantic Division. The 76ers lead at 4-1, closely followed by the Toronto Raptors (3-1) and Boston Celtics (3-1), both half a game back . A win would keep Philadelphia firmly in first place and strengthen their bid for the top seed in the East, where the Detroit Pistons (4-1) currently share the top spot.

For the Knicks, the calculation is one of short-term survival: at 2-3, they're two games out of the division lead and, above all, they need to start picking up points against direct rivals before the gap becomes inconvenient. A win in Philadelphia would be the kind of result that changes the narrative of a lackluster start.

What's the prediction for the Knicks vs 76ers game?

Home-court advantage, a better record, and current momentum all point to Philadelphia. The 76ers have more depth in their rotation and a dominant inside presence who often decides close games at home.

The Knicks have the talent to cause problems—and with Brunson on fire, nothing is certain—but their inconsistency down the stretch is costly. A close victory for the Philadelphia 76ers, with Embiid and Maxey leading the charge at the Wells Fargo Center.
