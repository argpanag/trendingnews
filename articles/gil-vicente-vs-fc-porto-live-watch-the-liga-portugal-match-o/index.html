Gil Vicente vs. FC Porto: How and Where to Watch LIVE

Matchday 9 of the 2026-27 Liga Portugal season brings an intriguing showdown on Saturday, October 24, as Gil Vicente welcomes league leader FC Porto to Estádio Cidade de Barcelos.



The Dragons have made a flawless start to their title defense, collecting 24 points from eight victories, while Gil Vicente sits sixth with 14 points.

Kickoff is scheduled for 3:30 PM ET / 12:30 PM PT, with coverage beginning at 3:25 PM ET / 12:25 PM PT on beIN SPORTS.


Gil Vicente Looks to Build on Its Impressive Recent Form

Gil Vicente, managed by Luís Pinto, has rediscovered its confidence after an inconsistent stretch, winning its last two Liga Portugal matches.

A 1-0 victory over Moreirense was followed by a dramatic 2-1 comeback win away to SC Braga on October 19.

In that match, Gil Vicente fell behind to an early goal from Pau Víctor, but Walace França equalized in the 49th minute before an own goal from Diogo Travassos completed the turnaround.

Defensive organization has been another important strength. The team has conceded just six goals in eight league matches, placing it among the competition's strongest defensive sides.

In attack, Héctor Hernández, the team's leading league scorer with three goals, and Murilo offer the creativity and finishing ability needed to challenge the league leaders.



FC Porto Chases a Ninth Straight League Win After Another European Setback

FC Porto has experienced two very different realities this season.

Domestically, Francesco Farioli's team has been unstoppable, winning all eight of its Liga Portugal matches and establishing a five-point lead over second-place Benfica.

Its latest league victory came against Marítimo, a 2-1 away win secured through goals from Victor Froholdt and Oskar Pietuszewski.

However, the team's UEFA Champions League campaign has been far less successful.

On Tuesday, October 20, the Dragons suffered a 1-0 home defeat against PSV Eindhoven, leaving them with three losses from their opening three European matches.

Despite those setbacks, FC Porto remains a formidable attacking side. Gabri Veiga, who leads the team's league scoring chart with four goals, joins Borja Sainz and André Silva among its key offensive threats.

The question is whether the Dragons can maintain their perfect domestic record while dealing with the frustration of another European defeat.



Can Gil Vicente End Porto's Perfect Start?

Recent meetings favor FC Porto, which won the most recent encounter 3-0 in January 2026 and also recorded a 2-0 victory on its previous competitive visit to Barcelos.

However, Gil Vicente enters Saturday's game with momentum after its impressive comeback against Braga, while the visitors must quickly recover from another disappointing night in Europe.

The hosts will be chasing a third consecutive league victory, while FC Porto has the opportunity to make it nine wins from nine and strengthen its position at the top of the table.

With contrasting European and domestic fortunes, strong defensive records, and plenty at stake, this matchup promises to be one of the most intriguing fixtures of the Liga Portugal weekend.
