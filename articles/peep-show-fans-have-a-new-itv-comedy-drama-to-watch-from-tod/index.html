Peep Show fans have a new ITV comedy drama to watch from today for free

TheTools · 13.09.2026 09:52 · source · permalink

Fans of Peep Show have a new comedy to add to their watchlist.

Kevin Can F**k Himself is arriving on ITVX for free from Sunday, September 13. Starring Emmy Award-winning actress Annie Murphy, best known for playing Alexis Rose in Schitt’s Creek, the series takes the familiar sitcom formula and turns it completely on its head.

Much like Peep Show, the comedy plays with the traditional rules of television and gives viewers a very different perspective on its characters. While Peep Show became famous for its awkward humour, internal monologues and unusual point-of-view storytelling, Kevin Can F**k Himself takes a similarly unconventional approach by switching between classic sitcom-style scenes and much darker, realistic sequences.

The series centres on Allison McRoberts (Murphy), who appears to have the life of a typical sitcom wife. She is attractive, good-natured and seemingly content to laugh along with her husband Kevin's jokes, despite often being the target of them.

But behind the bright lights and familiar sitcom set-up, Allison's life looks very different. When the story moves away from Kevin and into Allison's world, viewers get to see what is really happening beneath the surface. Increasingly unhappy with her marriage and the way she has been treated, Allison begins to question the role she has been playing and eventually decides she has had enough.

The show deliberately switches between a traditional multi-camera sitcom style and a single-camera drama format, creating two very different versions of Allison's world.

The result is a darkly comic look at the conventions of sitcoms and the way audiences have been encouraged to laugh at certain characters and situations over the years.

Murphy stars alongside Mary Hollis Inboden as Allison's neighbour Patty O'Connor, a tough and cynical woman who becomes increasingly close to Allison. Eric Petersen plays Kevin, Allison's immature but supposedly lovable husband, while Alex Bonifer portrays Kevin's beer-loving best friend Neil.

The cast also includes Brian Howe as Kevin's father Pete and Raymond Lee as Sam Park, a former Worcester resident who has a history with Allison.

Created by Valerie Armstrong, whose credits include Lodge 49 and SEAL Team, Kevin Can F**k Himself is executive produced by Rashida Jones and Will McCormack through their production company Le Train Train.

Craig DiGregorio, known for his work on Shrill, serves as showrunner and executive producer.

With its mix of uncomfortable comedy, darker storytelling and a clever take on the traditional sitcom format, Kevin Can F**k Himself could be right up the street for viewers who enjoyed the unconventional humour of Peep Show.

Kevin Can F**k Himself is available to stream for free on ITVX from Sunday, September 13.
