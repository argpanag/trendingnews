Jennifer Lawrence and Josh Hutcherson attended the event alongside actors from every era of the dystopian franchise.

Fans of The Hunger Games movies were treated to a very nostalgic night on Tuesday, as past, present and future stars of the franchise came together at the New York premiere of Sunrise on the Reaping, ahead of the movie's theatrical release on Nov. 20.  

Many of the original cast members, including Jennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson, Woody Harrelson and Elizabeth Banks, attended alongside the stars of the two prequels, bringing together actors from across all six movies for the first time. 

Lawrence, who finally confirmed she and Hutcherson would be reprising their roles for Sunrise last month, hasn't been photographed on the red carpet with her original costars since 2015, when The Hunger Games: Mockingjay — Part 2 was released. Liam Hemsworth and Sam Claflin were among some of the other original lead cast members who weren't at the event.

The cast of the original Hunger Games movies and the first prequel, Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes, at the Sunrise on the Reaping New York City premiere.

Willow Shields, Jena Malone and Amandla Stenberg, who each appeared in one or more of the original collection, were there alongside Rachel Zegler, Tom Blyth and Josh Andrés Rivera, the leads of the first prequel, Ballads of Songbirds and Snakes.

Shields, who played Jennifer Lawrence's character's younger sister in the original movies, was just 10 years old when she was cast as Prim in 2011. Stenberg, who played Rue, was 12. 

The cast of Sunrise on the Reaping at the New York City premiere.

Of course, the cast of the latest movie was there. Joseph Zada, Mckenna Grace, Whitney Peak, Glenn Close, Elle Fanning, Maya Hayke, Ralph Fiennes, Kieran Culkin, Billy Porter, Jesse Plemons and more walked the red carpet. 

Celebrating such a visually spectacular franchise, many members of the cast used the event to showcase their sartorial prowess and pay homage to their characters. Elle Fanning, who takes on the role of stylist Effie, the role that Elizabeth Banks originated, wore a Givenchy dress covered in vintage makeup compacts, lipsticks and blushes.

Mckenna Grace continued her fashion tributes by going full Mockingjay with her winged dress, and Whitney Peak turned heads with her midnight blue Robert Wun gown, complete with a bird taking flight on her shoulder.
