Domantas Sabonis (knee) questionable Tuesday

TheTools · 10.11.2026 08:56 · source · permalink

by Nicholas Rodriguez | Kings Correspondent | Tue, Nov 10th 2026, 3:56am EST

Domantas Sabonis is listed as questionable for Tuesday's game against the Sacramento Kings. (James Ham on X)

Fantasy Impact:

Sabonis missed Sunday's game against the Miami Heat due to left knee soreness and is in danger of missing his second straight contest. Fantasy managers will need to wait until morning shootaround for an update on his status. If he's unable to play, Dylan Cardwell could slide into the starting lineup again, while Precious Achiuwa should see a bump in minutes.

Keegan Murray (right wrist soreness) and Domantas Sabonis (left knee soreness) are both listed as questionable against the Magic on Tuesday.
