Ticketmaster app gets Dice-style update with more personalisation

TheTools · 03.09.2026 12:30 · source · permalink

Ticketmaster is giving its mobile app a refresh in 21 countries, with a bigger focus on recommendations and personalisation, including a ‘watchlist’ for keeping tabs on upcoming concerts and events.

The app’s home page has also been given a rejiggle, with ‘For You’, ‘Trending’ and ‘Last Minute’ sections offering recommendations based on fans’ previous activity.

“It’s the beginning of a broader effort to build more fan-inspired experiences that make discovering, following and enjoying live events feel more personal every step of the way,” said global president Saumil Mehta.

It also feels quite a lot like Dice, the ticketing app that made discovery and personalisation a key plank in its efforts to disrupt the biggest ticketing firms from its launch more than a decade ago.

Dice was acquired by live-events firm Fever last year, and has recently been adding more deals with venues and promoters in the US – Ticketmaster’s heartland.

The latter’s app revamp isn’t just a defensive measure, though. The company cited research showing that “around 40% of fans say they didn’t attend an event because they didn’t know it was happening”. Doing more work to plug that gap makes sense, even if Ticketmaster isn’t the first to get its teeth into the challenge.
