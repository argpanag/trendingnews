Moroccan Rapper French Montana to Perform at Ballon d’Or 2026 Ceremony

TheTools · 24.10.2026 15:55 · source · permalink

Rabat – Moroccan-American rapper French Montana will perform at the 2026 Ballon d’Or ceremony, scheduled for Monday, October 26, in London, according to an announcement by France Football.

The Casablanca-born artist will deliver a special performance during the prestigious event, which will bring together some of the biggest names in international football to celebrate the year’s outstanding players and achievements.

French Montana will not be the only musical star entertaining the audience. Organizers have also confirmed that British singer Jorja Smith will perform during the ceremony, adding a musical touch to the highly anticipated evening.

French Montana’s growing presence at major sporting events

French Montana has become a familiar face at major international sporting events, combining his musical career with appearances at global football celebrations.

The Moroccan-American rapper previously performed at the opening ceremony of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations (AFCON) in Morocco, sharing the stage with Moroccan producer RedOne and Nigerian singer Davido.

He also contributed to the official music album for the 2026 FIFA World Cup, further strengthening his connection with international football.

Born in Casablanca, French Montana moved to the United States during his childhood and later established himself as an internationally recognized rapper.

His upcoming performance at the Ballon d’Or ceremony marks another major appearance for the artist, who continues to represent his Moroccan roots on the international stage.

Thierry Henry and Kate Scott to host the ceremony

The 2026 Ballon d’Or ceremony will mark the 70th edition of the prestigious football awards.

Former French international Thierry Henry and British sports journalist Kate Scott will host the event, according to an earlier announcement by France Football.

Henry, who received nine Ballon d’Or nominations during his playing career, finished second in the 2003 edition of the award.

Football fans worldwide will also have the opportunity to follow the ceremony live through DAZN, which will broadcast the event free of charge, with viewers only required to register or sign in.

Moroccan football stars among Ballon d’Or nominees

Beyond French Montana’s musical performance, Morocco will also have a strong presence among the football personalities competing for individual honors.

Moroccan international Achraf Hakimi, who plays for Paris Saint-Germain (PSG), is among the 30 nominees for the 2026 Men’s Ballon d’Or.

The Atlas Lions captain will compete against some of the world’s leading footballers for the prestigious individual award.

Meanwhile, Moroccan goalkeeper Yassine Bounou is among the contenders for the Yashin Trophy, which recognizes the world’s best goalkeeper.

Young Moroccan midfielder Ayyoub Bouaddi has also earned a nomination for the Kopa Trophy, awarded to the best footballer under the age of 21.

The nominations highlight the growing presence of Moroccan footballers at the highest level of international football.

With French Montana preparing to entertain the audience and three Moroccan players competing for major individual honors, the 2026 Ballon d’Or ceremony promises to be a significant evening for Moroccan football and music.
