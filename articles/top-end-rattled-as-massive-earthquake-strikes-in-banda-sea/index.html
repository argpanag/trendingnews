Top end rattled as massive earthquake strikes in Banda Sea

TheTools · 16.11.2026 22:54 · source · permalink

An earthquake has hit Indonesia with enough strength that Australians have now reported feeling its tremors.

The Magnitude 6.6 quake struck in the Banda Sea in the Indonesian Maluku province, near West Papua, about 8am AEDT on Tuesday.

Indonesian authorities said current models indicate earthquake as “no tsunami potential”.

Geoscience Australia reported 400 instances of Australians feeling tremors from the earthquake in the hours following.

According to the organisation’s Felt Report, people in Darwin reported feeling “light” shakes while others as south as Kununurra felt “weak” shakes.

Indonesian reports were much stronger, with people in numerous cities reporting vibrations as strong as “if a truck was passing” outside their home while others said hanging objects “swayed”.

While the threat of a tsunami may not be present, officials have urged locals to remain cautious.

“Remain calm and not be influenced by issues whose truth cannot be justified,” Indonesia’s meteorology agency, BMKG, said.

“Avoid buildings that are cracked or damaged due to earthquakes.”
