Two further arrests after death of Siminic Rostas (24) in Leopardstown

TheTools · 09.09.2026 16:54 · source · permalink

Two more men have been arrested as gardai continue to investigate the violent disorder incident in Leopardstown, Dublin 18, which led to the death of 24-year-old Siminic Rostas.

Gardai made the arrests on Tuesday, September 8 as part of their investigation into the incident at the South County Business Park on Saturday, 29 August.

The two men, aged in their 20s and 40s, were arrested in the Dublin area.

Both have since been released without charge.

Gardai have confirmed that files will be prepared for the Office of the Director of Public Prosecutions (DPP).

The latest arrests bring to six the total number of people arrested to date in connection with the investigation.

Siminic Rostas was seriously injured during the confrontation and was taken to hospital. He was pronounced deceased on Tuesday, 1 September 2026.

The circumstances surrounding Rostas's death emerged during court proceedings involving two men arrested in connection with the incident.

Dun Laoghaire District Court on Tuesday, September 1 heard that gardai were called to an abandoned premises at the South County Business Park at approximately 8.25pm on Saturday, August 29, following reports of a large-scale disturbance involving several males.

Three men, including Siminic Rostas, were found with significant injuries and required hospital treatment.

Gardai told the court that their initial inquiries indicated that around 15 people had been living at the abandoned premises for several months.

It was alleged that a group arrived at the premises after stating they were there to collect personal property.

Gardai said an initial verbal dispute then escalated suddenly into a physical confrontation.

According to evidence presented to the court, the confrontation involved several men on both sides, with CCTV capturing significant portions of the incident.

The court heard that poles, sticks and shovels were among the implements used during the violence.

Kevin Rostas, 21, of Harrington Street, Dublin 8, and Cristian Demian, 23, of Howth Road, Raheny, appeared before the court charged with violent disorder.

Demian also faced a charge of assault causing harm.

Gardai alleged that both men could be seen participating in the confrontation on CCTV.

It was further alleged in court that Demian struck Siminic Rostas with a shovel, causing catastrophic injuries, and that Rostas was struck again after he had become unconscious.

Kevin Rostas maintained that he had acted in self-defence. Demian also raised self-defence and told gardai, according to evidence given in court, that he had not gone to the premises to fight.

During the proceedings at that court hearing on Tuesday, September 1 the court was informed that Siminic Rostas had died in Beaumont Hospital after his life support was withdrawn.

The development was communicated to gardai as Demian's bail hearing was beginning.

Another man, 22-year-old Augustin Rostas, had previously appeared before the court charged with violent disorder in connection with the incident.

He was granted conditional bail, subject to conditions including having no contact with witnesses.

The court proceedings also heard that one of the injured men had allegedly received threatening messages following the confrontation, including references to possible "retribution".

Gardai raised concerns during the earlier hearings about the seriousness of the violence and the possibility of further intimidation.

The investigation remains ongoing, gardai confirmed.

Gardai have appealed to anyone with information that may assist the investigation to come forward.
