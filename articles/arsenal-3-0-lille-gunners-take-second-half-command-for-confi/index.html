  1. 'We were always in control'published at 22:09 BST
    22:09 BST

    FT: Arsenal 3-0 Lille

    Arsenal

    Arsenal full-back Jurrien Timber speaking to TNT Sports about his goal:

    "I had some moments in the first half but second half I had more. First touch was good, which was half the job, and then Noni [Madueke] was screaming "shoot" so I thought OK I have to shoot. And then yeah it went in nice.

    "I wasn't thinking too much. I saw the goal and thought I need to shoot. We needed that first goal, we were playing well just needed the goal. Happy to get that for the team."

    On team performance: "We have confidence in ourselves, we know how good we are. First half we had some moments where we were sloppy. But we were always in control and we needed that first goal but the way we came out the second half showed character and the guys that came in did an amazing job. We need to keep going.

    "These are the demands. We played three days ago, today again. We need everyone, all the players. Injuries are part of the season so we need other players to step up. We need to keep going, keep improving and another tough match on Sunday."

21:41 BST

Arsenal 3-0 Lille

Steven Gerrard
Former England and Liverpool captain on TNT Sports

I think it's a nice touch - Olivier Giroud definitely deserves an ovation. Arsenal fans showing the respect to the service and the quality that he brought to them.

But for me, the Lille manager waited too long to make these substitutions. I think they could have done with Giroud a lot earlier because Lille have struggled to keep the ball this whole second half and they have missed that focal point that can hold the ball up.

Bukayo Saka scored and assisted after coming off of the bench.

Arsenal scored three goals in seven second-half minutes to maintain their perfect start to the league phase of the Champions League by beating Lille at Emirates Stadium.

Defender Jurrien Timber sent them on their way in the 64th minute after he collected a pass from Noni Madueke and lifted a clever finish over Berke Ozer.

England forward Eberechi Eze added the second three minutes later when he finished first time from substitute Bukayo Saka's cross.

Having won at Napoli, Arsenal sealed a second victory in the competition when Saka himself rolled in the third in the 71st minute.

Boss Mikel Arteta had made five changes to the side that beat Leeds 2-1 in the Premier League at the weekend, but the frontline of Eze, Madueke and Viktor Gyokeres did not click in a laboured first half from the Gunners.

He resisted the temptation to make wholesale changes at the break and was rewarded when Dutch defender Timber found the breakthrough with a neat finish.

Saka, Kai Havertz and captain Martin Odegaard were then introduced and all three substitutes played a major role in the next two goals as last season's beaten finalists wrapped up a convincing success.

Arsenal won all eight of their league phase games in the previous campaign before they lost the final to Paris St-Germain - and look like they will be hard to beat at this stage of the competition again.
