The fans at Mullanpur were up for a major shock as in-form Shubman Gill and Virat Kohli were dismissed cheaply in the third and final ODI between the India and West Indies on Saturday. Gill was caught-behind for 1(6) by Jayden Seales, who then Kohli for a golden duck in the next legal delivery.

Kohli was rattled by a delivery pitched slightly up in the middle and leg-stump line. The ball then passed between his bat and leg and knocked the middle stump as he tried to play it across the line. The dismissal reduced India to 4/2 in 2.4 overs after the hosts won the toss and opted to bat.

Meanwhile, Seales became only the fourth bowler to dismiss Kohli off the first legitimate ball he faced in an ODI. Current West Indies coach Darren Sammy, Tim Bresnan, and Kieron Pollard are the other three to have done this in the past.

Rohit Sharma, who completed 10,000 runs as opener in ODIs, is batting in the middle alongside Ruturaj Gaikwad.

Meanwhile, India have handed an ODI debut to bowling all-rounder Anshul Kamboj. Mohammed Siraj and Nitish Kumar Reddy also were included in the playing XI.
