Maresca: Everyone who plays against Norwich will be vital for us this season

TheTools · 17.09.2026 18:04 · source · permalink

The Canaries clash comes towards the end of an intensive opening period of fixtures for City – with a key Premier League game against Sunderland following on Sunday ahead of the September international break.

Maresca’s men have made a flying start to the season, winning all four Premier League games as well as triumphing in our UEFA Champions League opener at FC Porto.

Tomorrow’s clash also comes on the back of last Sunday’s physically and emotionally draining 1-0 derby win at Manchester United where the Blues were down to 10 players for 70 minutes.

Addressing the media at Wednesday’s pre-match press conference as City set about on the defence of the League Cup, the boss confirmed he would look to afford opportunities for a number of players who haven’t had too many minutes for far this term.

And he insisted that those who are involved will have a key role to play looking both to tomorrow and the rest of the season.

“For sure, we are going make some changes,” Maresca revealed.

The Canaries have made a mixed start to the Championship season and currently sit in 14th place in the table with nine points from their seven games to date.

However, for the City boss, the one-off nature of the tie brings with it a sense of jeopardy and danger with Maresca insisting City would not take our opponents lightly.

“Absolutely they are always tricky games,” the boss added.

“The one game they are difficult games.

“We have to do our job in the right way, like we are trying to do and no doubt it will be a tricky game for many reasons.

“It is one more competition. We need to compete in all the competitions.
