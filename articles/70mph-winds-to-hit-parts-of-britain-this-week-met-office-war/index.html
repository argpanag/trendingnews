70mph winds to hit parts of Britain this week, Met Office warns

TheTools · 20.10.2026 04:04 · source · permalink

Weather warning issued for parts of the South East, South West and Wales on Friday

A yellow severe weather warning for wind has been issued for parts of the UK this week, with the potential for gusts of 70mph to disrupt travel and cause damage.

The Met Office has warned that strong winds could affect England and Wales, with heavy rain also expected to make journeys more difficult.

The warning is in place from midnight on Thursday until midday on Friday and covers large parts of the South West, areas of Wales and a stretch of the South East coast extending to Brighton and East Sussex.

Gusts of 50-60mph are possible, while speeds could exceed 70mph in some coastal and exposed areas.

People are being advised to secure loose items outside their homes, check travel conditions before setting out, prepare for the possibility of power cuts and stay safe from large waves and cliffs on the coast, and keep dogs on leads.

The Met Office has forecast an increasingly autumnal weather for this week generally, with low pressure bringing spells of wind and rain.

Rain is set to move northwards across a large part of the UK overnight and into Tuesday, with another front moving in from the west by the evening, bringing further rain which will be heavy at times.

By Thursday, a developing area of low pressure brings the potential for some powerful winds and heavy rain. This has prompted the yellow warning.

Tom Crabtree, the Met Office’s deputy chief forecaster, said: “An area of the forecast we’re particularly focusing on is the development of a potentially deep area of low pressure that could cross the UK overnight on Thursday and through Friday.

“It has the potential to bring some strong winds, though the location of the strongest winds will be determined by the low position.

“At this stage, people should keep an eye on the forecast and be prepared for some unsettled weather at the end of the week.”
