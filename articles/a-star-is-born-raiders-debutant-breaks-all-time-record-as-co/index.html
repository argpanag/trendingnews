‘A star is born’: Raiders debutant breaks all-time record as Cowboys face finals blow

TheTools · 05.09.2026 10:21 · source · permalink

Canberra’s rookie halfback Coby Black enjoyed a dream debut as the Raiders stunned the finals-bound Cowboys with a 50-30 ambush in Townsville.

Black set a new NRL record for the most points scored on debut with a try in each half, kicking all nine of his attempts at goal to finish with a 26-point individual haul.

The 20-year-old playmaker became Raider No.413 on the same night Raider No.300 Josh Papali’i bowed out as a winner.

It was a chaotic kick-off as Reuben Cotter was sin-binned on the first play of the game for a reckless high tackle on the retiring legend, forcing both off the field for a brief period.

The Cowboys struggled to contain the explosive speed of Kaeo Weekes and Cairns junior Xavier Savage, with the pair playing a major part in Canberra’s red-hot start as the Raiders scored five first-half tries.

North Queensland’s hopes of a second-half comeback were dashed when Savage completed his hat-trick with a runaway try against the run of play, sending the Cowboys into finals with a loss.

BIG HITS

BIG PAPA BOWS OUT

One of the NRL’s most beloved forwards played his 342nd and final game for the Raiders, retiring as one of rugby league’s last remaining one-club champions.

Papali’i enjoyed 16 stellar seasons in Canberra, with the 2020 Dally M Prop of the Year also boasting representative games for Australia, Samoa, Queensland and the Prime Minister’s XIII.

The 34-year-old prop was hit with a high tackle on the first run of the game and had to come off the field for an HIA, which he thankfully passed before returning to score a try.

NERVOUS WAIT FOR COTTER

Cotter could be in hot water with the NRL judiciary after his reckless high tackle on Papali’i.

The Cowboys co-captain has already attracted two grade 1 judiciary charges this year in round 8, however he successfully fought his grade 1 careless high tackle on Jayden Campbell in round 23.

North Queensland will likely take on the Sharks next week and Cotter’s tireless work in the middle of the park will be vital to stopping Cronulla’s powerful pack.

DEBUT DELIGHT

Spare a thought for Raiders halfback Ethan Sanders, who just watched Black lay claim to his No.7 jersey with a record-breaking debut.

Two tries, two try assists, nine goals and 26 points only tells half the story; Black’s full arsenal of talents were on display during an unforgettable first outing in the NRL.

Black forced a Scott Drinkwater error with a towering spiral bomb in the first half, broke the line with his strength and footwork, and set up Ethan Strange with a perfect grubber.

The Broncos junior’s pass selection was excellent, creating space on the right edge for Savage to tear the Cowboys apart with his speed.

Canberra utility Malachi Smith also made his debut with a solid outing, however his special occasion was overshadowed by Black’s brilliance.
