Eagles continue off-season changes with three more delisted

TheTools · 16.10.2026 20:43 · source · permalink

Clay Hall, Harry Schoenberg and Jack Williams have not been offered contracts for 2027

WEST Coast has made three more list changes, with Clay Hall, Harry Schoenberg and Jack Williams not offered contracts for 2027.

Williams departs after 54 games and 46 goals at the Eagles, having arrived from East Fremantle with pick No.57 in the 2021 national draft. The 198cm forward-ruck made his debut in 2022 and was a regular presence during West Coast's rebuild, producing his best return in 2025 when he kicked 16 goals from 13 appearances.

Hall, the son of former Eagle Derek Hall, was selected from Peel Thunder with pick No.38 in the 2023 draft and played 16 AFL games, but none this year. The big-bodied midfielder broke through for 13 matches in 2025 and earned a Telstra AFL Rising Star nomination after a 22-disposal performance against Collingwood in round 16.

Schoenberg joined West Coast via the Supplemental Selection Period ahead of the 2026 season after six years and 62 games at Adelaide. The former pick No.24 played five matches for the Eagles.

List and recruiting boss Matt Clarke thanked the trio for their contributions during their time at the club.

"It's always a challenging time when list decisions are made and today we have informed Clay, Harry and Jack they will not be offered contracts for next season," Clarke said.

"We would like to thank each of them for their commitment to West Coast and for the contributions they have made on and off the field. We wish them every success in their future endeavours."

Their delistings follow that of Harry Barnett, Tyler Brockman and Harvey Johnston, while Jamie Cripps has retired.

The Eagles landed Trent Rivers and Jack Viney from Melbourne during this year's Continental Tyres AFL Trade Period.
