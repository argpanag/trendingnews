What to Expect From the Miss Universe 2026 Final
A total of 116 contestants are competing in the 2026 edition of Miss Universe. The first major announcement of the evening will reveal the 30 semifinalists, who will move on to the next rounds and face a series of challenges as they compete for the crown.
