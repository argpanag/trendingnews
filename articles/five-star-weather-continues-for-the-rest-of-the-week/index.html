Five star weather continues for the rest of the week

TheTools · 21.10.2026 08:52 · source · permalink

We must have hit the weather lottery because the stretch of weather that we’ve seen over the past couple of days has been nothing short of both remarkable and award winning! We will see that great stretch of weather continue throughout the day on Wednesday as we start off on the crisp side and watch temperatures rebound heading into the afternoon. We will see plenty of sunshine with those afternoon high temperatures topping off in the middle 60’s. Into the overnight hours, we cool down very efficiently as lows bottom out in the upper 30’s.

Dry weather sticks with us heading into the day on Thursday, although we are poised to be a touch cooler with temperatures only bouncing back into the low 60’s. Skies will remain sunny and we will still have a stagnant airmass overhead, keeping things calm across the region.

If you’re behind on any yard work or maybe the car needs to be washed, now is certainly a good time to get that done due to how low rain chances look. Heading into this upcoming weekend, rain chances are virtually non-existent across not only our region, but much of the southeaster United States as a whole.
