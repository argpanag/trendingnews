Al-Dawsari shock compounded as Milinkovic-Savic ‘to miss’ Al-Hilal’s Clasico with Al-Ittihad

According to Al-Riyadiyah, Sergej Milinkovic-Savic will miss Al-Hilal’s Clasico with Al-Ittihad, adding to the absence of captain Salem Al-Dawsari.

The Riyadh side host their rivals today, Saturday, at Kingdom Arena in the Saudi capital in the eighth round of the Roshn League.

OneFootball Videos

Milinkovic-Savic failed fitness tests during Friday’s final training session after sustaining a calf bruise on international duty with Serbia.

Al-Hilal’s medical staff chose to rest the Serbian rather than risk aggravating the problem by playing him.

Al-Dawsari also skipped Friday’s session due to pain in his ankle joint.

Head coach Simone Inzaghi must now find alternatives, with the matchday squad to be finalised immediately before kick-off.

Victory would send Al-Hilal back to the top of the Saudi League. They are third on 18 points, one behind Al-Qadsiah and Al-Nassr, who have both played a game more.
