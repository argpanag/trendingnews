ITV investigating Coronation Street actor Jacob Roberts after bar incident

TheTools · 22.11.2026 18:25 · source · permalink

ITV is investigating a Coronation Street actor following an alleged bar altercation.

Jacob Roberts is known for playing Det Con Kit Green on the ITV soap since 2024. According to reports by the Sun newspaper, video footage appears to show him shouting outside Kiely's Irish Bar in Manchester on 8 November.

Greater Manchester Police said officers had attended a bar in Watson Street at about 02:30 GMT following reports of a disturbance. No arrests have been made.

An ITV spokesperson said they were investigating and would be speaking with Roberts.

Roberts was allegedly trying to get into the bar, with staff claiming he was acting aggressively and swearing when he was told he could not come in as the venue was closing.

Roberts previously played Damon Kinsella on the Channel 4 soap Hollyoaks from 2017 until 2023, before joining the cobbles in 2024.

He has also appeared in Emmerdale and recently starred in the crossover episode Corridale.

His storylines on Coronation Street include being diagnosed with male breast cancer as well as fathering a child after an illicit affair with his girlfriend's daughter.

Roberts' representatives have been contacted for comment.
