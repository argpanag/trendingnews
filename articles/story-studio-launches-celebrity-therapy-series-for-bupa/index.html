Story Studio launches celebrity therapy series for Bupa

TheTools · 19.11.2026 10:00 · source · permalink

Mindplace Sessions

NEWS BRIEF: Independent creative agency Story Studio has partnered with international healthcare company Bupa to launch a branded content series exploring the power of talking and challenging perceptions of therapy.

Mindplace Sessions features Dame Mary Berry, Freddie Flintoff, Miquita Oliver and Olly Bowman speaking with psychotherapist Owen O’Kane. Captured in a single take, each session will appear in shortform social video across Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn and YouTube Shorts, with full episodes available to watch on YouTube from today.
