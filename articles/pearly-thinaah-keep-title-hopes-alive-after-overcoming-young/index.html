Pearly-Thinaah keep title hopes alive after overcoming youngsters Zi Yu-Noraqilah in Hong Kong

TheTools · 21.11.2026 20:33 · source · permalink
    bookmark

PETALING JAYA: Pearly Tan-M. Thinaah held off a spirited challenge from youngsters Low Zi Yu-Noraqilah Maisarah Ramdan to reach the Hong Kong Open women’s doubles final on Saturday (Nov 21).

The second seeds went comfortably ahead 21-13 in the first game of the all-Malaysian semi-final match at the Kai Tak Arena.

Zi-Yu-Noraqilah showed more bite in the second by pulling ahead 13-7 but experience proved too strong in the end as Pearly-Thinaah won the match 21-13, 21-19 in 35 minutes.

The 2024 champions will next face Japan’s Sumire Nakade-Miyu Takahashi in Sunday’s final.
