BMKG Forecasts Heavy Rain Throughout Jakarta Today, Beware of Lightning

TheTools · 16.11.2026 20:09 · source · permalink

Share:

JAKARTA - The Meteorology, Climatology and Geophysics Agency (BMKG) predicts that all areas of DKI Jakarta will be hit by rain on Saturday. A number of areas even have the potential to experience rain accompanied by lightning.

Based on information conveyed through the BMKG official website, quoted by Antara, Saturday, November 14, rain is predicted to occur in the Thousand Islands, Central Jakarta, North Jakarta, West Jakarta, South Jakarta, and East Jakarta.

In the Thousand Islands Islands Administration area, BMKG predicts light rain with air temperatures ranging from 28-29 degrees Celsius. The air humidity level is estimated to be in the range of 74-81 percent.

Meanwhile, Central Jakarta is predicted to experience light rain with an air temperature of 25-31 degrees Celsius and a humidity of 66-91 percent.

Similar conditions are expected in North Jakarta. The area has the potential to experience light rain with a temperature of 26-30 degrees Celsius and air humidity of 67-88 percent.

Unlike other areas, West Jakarta is predicted to experience rain accompanied by lightning. The air temperature is estimated to range from 25-32 degrees Celsius with a humidity of 63-93 percent.

The potential for lightning rain is also predicted to occur in South Jakarta, with an air temperature of 25-33 degrees Celsius and humidity of 59-94 percent.

Meanwhile, East Jakarta is predicted to experience thunderstorms with an air temperature ranging from 25-31 degrees Celsius. The air humidity level is estimated to reach 63-95 percent.

BMKG urges the public to remain vigilant against the potential for extreme weather, especially rain accompanied by lightning. The public is also asked to update weather forecast information through the Info BMKG application, the official website www.bmkg.go.id, and the @info.bmkg social media account.
