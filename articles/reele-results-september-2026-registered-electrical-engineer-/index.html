REELE RESULTS September 2026: Registered Electrical Engineer Licensure Exam Passers

TheTools · 09.09.2026 22:51 · source · permalink

REELE RESULTS 2026 – The September 2026 Registered Electrical Engineer Licensure Exam results, including the complete list of passers, top 10 examinees, and performance of schools, are expected to be released by the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) Board of Electrical Engineering a few working days after the last day of exams.

The Registered Electrical Engineering board exams were conducted on September 5-6, 2026, at PRC testing centers in NCR, Baguio, Butuan, Cagayan de Oro, Calapan, Cebu, Davao, Iloilo, Legazpi, Lucena, Pagadian, Palawan, Pampanga, Rosales, Tacloban, Tuguegarao, and Zamboanga. The Board of Electrical Engineering is headed by its Chairman, Engr. Adelino V. Garcia Jr., and its members, Engr. Jaime V. Mendoza and Engr. Alex C. Cabugao.

Release of Results

According to the Professional Regulation Commission’s official schedule, the September 2026 Registered Electrical Engineer Licensure Exam (REELE) results are targeted for release on September 10, 2026, four (4) calendar days after the last day of examinations on September 6, 2026.

However, the release of the Registered Electrical Engineer Board Exam results may occur earlier or later than the target date without prior notice. In the previous REELE held on April 21-22, 2026, PRC released the results on April 28, 2026, after three (3) working days.

We advise examinees to bookmark this page and stay connected with us on our social media channels for the latest and most accurate result announcements.

Schedule of Registration

The schedule for online appointments to obtain the new Registered Electrical Engineers’ Professional ID Cards and Certificates of Registration will be announced after the release of the September 2026 REELE results.

PRC advises online registration through the official website at https://online.prc.gov.ph/. Follow these steps for online registration and ensure you have the following requirements ready:

  1. Notice of Admission (NOA) – for identification purposes only
  2. Completed Oath Form (Panunumpa ng Propesyonal)
  3. Two (2) passport-sized ID photos on a white background, each with a complete name tag
  4. Two (2) sets of documentary stamps
  5. One (1) short brown envelope

PRC emphasizes that successful examinees must complete their registration in person and sign the Roster of Registered Professionals.

Verification of Rating

Online access to verify Electrical Engineering licensure exam ratings and test results will be available shortly after the results are posted.

All exam takers can check their passing rate on the official PRC website using the Verification Page.

To initiate the verification process, candidates must provide the exam name, examination date, application number, first name, last name, and date of birth.

Oath-Taking Ceremonies

All passers interested in attending the face-to-face mass oath-taking shall register no later than 12:00 PM on the day before the date of oath-taking. Register at http://online.prc.gov.ph to confirm attendance.

Those who cannot attend the mass oath-taking may opt to participate in the virtual oath-taking or request a special oath-taking ceremony. Once confirmed, the schedule will be posted on the official website.

Passers may schedule an online appointment at http://online.prc.gov.ph and select “e-OATH” as the transaction.

The online registration will close five (5) calendar days before the online oath-taking day. Inductees must print the “OATH OF PROFESSIONAL” form, downloadable from the PRC official website under PRC Online Services.

After the oath-taking, the inductees shall proceed with the Initial Registration by securing an online appointment at http://online.prc.gov.ph.

Coverage of Exams

The two-day REELE covers the following topics:

  • Mathematics
  • Engineering Sciences and Allied Subjects
  • Electrical Engineering Professional Subjects

Previous Exams

The REE board exam is among the programs with the average number of examinees conducted by the PRC.

In the most recent REELE held in April 2026, 3,769 out of 6,556 (57.49%) examinees successfully passed the examination.

In the August 2025 REELE, 1,240 out of 3,186 (38.92%) examinees passed. Jolly Babe Mallari Siago from Cebu Institute of Technology – University ranked first in the exams, garnering a 93.45% rating, followed by Joshua Enrick Bravo Salvador from Mapua University, having 92.60%. The University of the Philippines – Los Baños was the top-performing school, with a 100.00% overall passing rate.

In the April 2025 exams, 4,137 out of 6,741 (61.37%) examinees passed. Kimberly Rose Dalay Capiral from Polytechnic University of the Philippines – Sto. Tomas ranked first in the exams with a 93.00% rating, followed by Mustafa Ali from Rizal Technological University with 92.85%.
