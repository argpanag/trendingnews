“Cyberpunk 2 is Canceled” Jokes CDPR Director After Playing GTA 6

TheTools · 23.11.2026 19:48 · source · permalink

Cyberpunk 2 Creative Director jokingly declared the sequel canceled after playing GTA 6, praising Rockstar’s scale, density, coherence, and technology.

Cyberpunk 2 Creative Director Igor Sarzyński has jokingly declared the upcoming RPG “canceled” after experiencing the extraordinary scale and technical detail of Grand Theft Auto VI.

According to Sarzynski, GTA 6 could very well be the “single greatest artistic achievement in history,” highlighting the scale, density, coherence, and thoughtfulness of its world. This has led to a not-so-serious (or at least just minorly subconsciously serious) bout of "depression" as an open-world game designer.

Sarzyński described the finished game as a “technological and creative miracle” before joking that Cyberpunk 2 had been canceled and that he would instead spend his time sweeping leaves from his backyard.

Rockstar Games

"GTAVI-induced-open-world-game-dev-depression is real. probably the single greatest artistic achievement in history, i mean it. scale, density, coherence, thoughtfulness of it all, just a technological and creative miracle. cyberpunk 2 is cancelled, imma go sweep autumn leaves in my backyard now.

seriously though i think we're doing something of our own, with a different unique flavor. GTA is impressive in a way all great art is, it expands what is possible, a captivating vision flawlessly executed."

— Igor Sarzynski, Creative Director on Cyberpunk 2 at CDPR

To be completely clear, Cyberpunk 2 has not actually been canceled. Sarzyński followed the joke with a more serious reflection on what Rockstar has achieved and how CD Projekt Red intends to differentiate its own open-world RPG.

He said Cyberpunk 2 is becoming something with a distinct identity and “different unique flavor.” Rather than treating GTA 6’s achievements solely as intimidating competition, Sarzyński argued that great art expands the boundaries of what creators believe is possible.

The response reflects the unusually enthusiastic reception GTA 6 has received from developers across CD Projekt Red. Joint CEO Michał Nowakowski called it a “masterpiece” and congratulated Rockstar on accomplishing something exceptionally difficult, while Cyberpunk 2 Associate Game Director Paweł Sasko described it as a “generational banger.”

It is not difficult to understand why the game might feel daunting to another open-world development team. GTA 6 incorporates an enormous collection of interconnected animation, weather, audio, NPC, police, physics, and character simulation technologies. We examined many of those systems in our breakdown of the incredible new technology Rockstar built to make Leonida feel alive.

The game has also become the biggest entertainment launch in history, generating an estimated $3.79 billion in retail sales during its first 24 hours. That followed a 99 Metascore and some of the strongest critical reception any game has received.

Cyberpunk 2 nevertheless remains in active development. The project entered pre-production in 2025 and is being created with Unreal Engine 5 by CD Projekt Red’s North American operation, which includes teams in Boston and Vancouver. CDPR reported earlier this year that 163 developers were assigned to the project, up from 149 several months earlier.

Rockstar Games

The sequel therefore appears to be growing, not disappearing. Sarzyński’s reaction is better understood as unusually enthusiastic praise from one open-world developer confronting the work of another—and perhaps a preview of the new expectations every large-scale open-world game will face after GTA 6.
