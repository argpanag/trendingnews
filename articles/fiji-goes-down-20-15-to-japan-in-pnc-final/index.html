Fiji goes down 20-15 to Japan in PNC final

TheTools · 20.09.2026 05:55 · source · permalink

Navitalai Naivalurua · 20/09/2026

Conceding too many penalties proved too costly for the FIJI Water Flying Fijians as they went down 20-15 to Japan in the 2026 Asahi Super Dry Pacific Nations Cup final at Prince Chichibu Memorial Stadium in Tokyo.

This was the third consecutive time the two teams had met in the final.

Fiji conceded 14 penalties, where Japan had only 5.

The final did not disappoint, with the match going down to the wire.

The game was filled with intensity as both teams hunted for the win.

Virimi Vakatawa scored the first try of the match, putting Fiji 7-3 ahead, but Japan responded with a try from Mamoru Harada to take a 10-7 lead.

Captain Tevita Ikanivere then crossed the try line in the 26th minute to put the Fijians back in front, 12-10.

Fiji continued to commit mistakes, giving Japan plenty of possession, but they held their line to take a 12-10 lead into halftime.

The second half was played with the same intensity as the Japanese side brought their A-game, putting pressure on the Fijians and disrupting their game plan.

Japan managed to score two more tries in the second half through Ben Gunter and Samisoni Tua to put the Fijians on the back foot.

The final five minutes saw both teams produce some spectacular rugby, but in the end, Japan’s defence stood tall to secure the victory.
