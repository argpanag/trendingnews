Millie Bobby Brown's summer is all about family: sweet moments with her daughter and Jake Bongiovi

TheTools · 02.09.2026 16:28 · source · permalink

Family summer

Brown is sharing an intimate look at her summer

Millie Bobby Brown is closing out the summer with a stunning recap. In her most recent Instagram post, Brown shared a look at what she and her family have been up to over the summer, showing a look at her adventures with her husband, Jake Bongiovi, and their daughter. 

The post was shared earlier today, showing numerous photos of her gorgeous looks, including a video of her enjoying the European coast atop a boat. She also shared photos of her husband driving it, and another image of him pushing their daughter in her stroller.

On that same vacation, she also shared a look at herself and her daughter wearing matching bucket hats. She also shared a photo of herself and her baby lying on a paddleboard, with their feet making for a sweet image. All of the photos keep her daughter's face shielded from the public, keeping it in line with the family's desire to raise her as privately as possible.

"fresh out of august," Brown captioned the post.

Millie Bobby Brown and Jake Bongiovi's journey as parents

Millie Bobby Brown and Jake Bongiovi got married in 2024, in ceremonies hosted in Europe and the US. Since then, they have been enjoying their relationship, adopting a daughter in the summer of 2025.

“It’s been a beautiful, amazing journey—she’s taught us so much already,” said Brown in an interview with British Vogue. She also opened up about her dynamic with Bongiovi and how the two carry out their duties as parents. 

“We are 50-50 on everything. That’s why I’m so grateful to have partnered with him in this life—he is just the most amazing dad," he said. 

When it comes to her daughter's privacy, Brown has a particular insight about growing up in the spotlight as a child actor herself. She wants her daughter to grow up as normally as possible. “For me, it’s really important to protect her and her story until she’s old enough to potentially one day share it herself,” she said.
