Reform UK clears itself of wrongdoing after undercover investigation

TheTools · 07.10.2026 19:09 · source · permalink

Catch up on TV bulletins

Reform UK has today published its internal report into allegations that senior figures in the party breached the law on party donations.

It relates to an undercover investigation broadcast last month by this programme and Verbatim, a group of journalists from the Centre for Climate reporting. Their footage and findings were verified by us.

Today’s Reform UK report says there was no law breaking. Labour said “you can find more whitewash in this report than at your local B&Q.”

The Metropolitan Police is carrying out its own criminal inquiry into the findings of the undercover investigation.
