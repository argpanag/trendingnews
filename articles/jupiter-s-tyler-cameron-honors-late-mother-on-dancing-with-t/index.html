JUPITER, Fla. (CBS12) — Jupiter native and former contestant on The Bachelorette, Tyler Cameron dedicated his “Dancing with the Stars” performance to mothers, including his late mother, Andrea Cameron, whose legacy continues through a foundation supporting students in Palm Beach County.

Cameron and professional partner Sharna Burgess performed a contemporary routine to Ed Sheeran’s “Supermarket Flowers” during Tuesday’s Dedication Night episode.

Andrea Cameron died in 2020 from a brain aneurysm. Her family created the Andrea C. Cameron Foundation in her honor. The foundation supports education programs and scholarships for Palm Beach County students.

Reports from ABC News, say the performance also became a family announcement. Cameron’s fiancée, Tate Madden, joined them at the end of the routine to reveal that they are expecting their first child.

Host Alfonso Ribeiro then announced a gender reveal, with pink confetti revealing they are expecting a girl.
