Disneyland offers nearly 50% more low-priced $104 dates in 2026

TheTools · 02.09.2026 15:06 · source · permalink

The Disneyland calendar has 18 cheap dates left this year — 10 in September and 8 in November.

Disneyland is making a big push to attract families to the Anaheim theme park with rock bottom-priced kids tickets this summer and nearly 50% more low-priced tickets this year for their parents.

Disneyland increased the number of the lowest-priced $104 dates from 38 in 2025 to 56 in 2026 — a 47% increase, according to MickeyVisit.

Disneyland made a similar move in 2023 when the park boosted the number of low-priced days by approximately 50% throughout the year by offering nearly two months of dates with daily tickets at $104.

Disneyland hasn’t raised the price of the cheapest $104 one-day single park admission since 2019 — and is unlikely to change the low price anytime soon.

There are still 18 dates left on the Disneyland calendar when $104 tickets will be available — 10 in September and eight in November.

Disneyland and Disney California Adventure are offering kids parkhopper tickets for as little as $50 through Sept. 7.

Disneyland has paired the cheap kids tickets with the family-friendly “Bluey’s Best Day Ever” show that has been drawing big and enthusiastic crowds to the Fantasyland Theatre.

Disneyland introduced tiered pricing in 2016 with lower prices during slower periods of the year and higher prices during the busiest seasons. Since then, the park has expanded the number of ticket tiers and steadily raised prices on the top end while holding the lowest price steady.

Top tier one-day tickets to Disneyland and DCA currently cost $224 for a single park admission and $314 for a parkhopper ticket.

The remaining $104 tickets in 2026 will be available on Sept. 8-9, Sept. 15-17, Sept. 21-24 and Sept. 29 and Nov. 2-5 and Nov. 9-12.
