Astera Labs Shares Fall 5.3% in Monday Trading | AskTraders.com

TheTools · 21.10.2026 14:59 · source · permalink

Astera Labs shares were down 5.3% in Monday trade, sitting at the bottom of the session's range and 32.9% below their 52-week high, though still up strongly for the year.

Astera Labs (NASDAQ: ALAB), the semiconductor company, was down 5.3% at $323.98 by 18:30 London time on Monday. That quote is the low of the session so far, with the shares having traded between $323.98 and $335.97 by that time.

The shares are 32.9% below their 52-week high. They are 223.1% above their 52-week low and up 94.7% so far this year.

Second large move since Thursday

Astera Labs also made a large move on Thursday, with only Friday’s session in between.
