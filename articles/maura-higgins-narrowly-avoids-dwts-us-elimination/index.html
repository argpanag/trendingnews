Maura Higgins narrowly avoids DWTS US elimination

TheTools · 03.11.2026 12:06 · source · permalink

Maura Higgins has secured another week on Dancing with the Stars in the US after a challenging night saw her finish at the bottom of the judges' leaderboard.

The Longford native was among the contestants fighting to keep their place in the competition during Monday night's Disney on Broadway-themed night.

Dressed as Tinkerbell, Higgins joined professional partner Mark Ballas on the dancefloor for a jive to You Can Fly! You Can Fly! You Can Fly! from Disney's Peter Pan.

The performance earned the pair 32 points out of 40, with all four judges awarding them eight points each.

They later returned to the ballroom for a group routine with Team Slaytopia, picking up another 36 points.

But their overall tally of 68 points left them behind the other remaining couples, putting their future in the competition in doubt.

As the results were revealed, Higgins and Ballas were left awaiting the final announcement alongside actress Julia Stiles and her professional partner Ezra Sosa. Stiles and Sosa were ultimately sent home.

The result comes just a week after the former Love Island star impressed the judging panel with a contemporary routine inspired by The Lost Boys during Horror Movie Night.

Her performance prompted high praise from judge Derek Hough, who described her as a "phenomenal performer".
