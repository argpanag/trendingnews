Nov. 8 (UPI) -- Godzilla Minus Zero is the No. 1 movie in North America, earning $31.6 million in receipts this weekend, BoxOfficeMojo.com announced Sunday.

Coming in at No. 2 is The Cat in the Hat with $20.5 million, followed by Drummer Boy at No. 3 with $9.3 million, You Can See Everything at No. 4 with $8.9 million and Ramayana at No. 5 with $8.7 million.

Rounding out the top tier are Clayface at No. 6 with $6.7 million, Wildwood at No. 7 with $6.6 million, Wild Horse Nine at No. 8 with $6 million, Wicker at No. 9 with $3.4 million and Jimmy at No. 10 with $2.2 million.
