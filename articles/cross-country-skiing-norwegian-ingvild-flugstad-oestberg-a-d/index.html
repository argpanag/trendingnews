Cross-country skiing: Ingvild Flugstad Oestberg calls time on her career

Having last been seen at the Cross-Country Skiing World Championships at the end of the 2022/2023 season, during which she became world relay champion, the Norwegian Ingvild Flugstad Oestberg had not been heard from for many months.

On Wednesday evening, she spoke out in a post on Instagram, announcing the end of her sporting career. “I am both proud and immensely lucky to have enjoyed such a long and wonderful career at the very highest level – one I didn’t even know I’d dreamt of! Above all, I am incredibly grateful for all the friendships I have made, ” she began.

View this post on Instagram

A double Olympic champion in the relay and team sprint, Ingvild Flugstad Oestberg, aged 36, has an incredible track record. Also a winner of the Crystal Globe and the Tour de Ski, she has racked up 88 World Cup podium finishes and has quite simply left her mark on her generation.

“I also think back on all those training sessions, training camps, trips and crazy moments, all those competitions, moments of nervousness, challenges, victories and medals,” she says. “I fully appreciate everything I’ve learnt and all the happy moments I’ve experienced! A lot of hard, dedicated work has gone into this, and the keys to success were as simple as consistency, determination and enjoyment.”
