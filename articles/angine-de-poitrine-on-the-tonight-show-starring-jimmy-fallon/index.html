Angine de Poitrine

Musician

Angine de Poitrine is a Canadian music duo comprised of two band members who perform under the pseudonyms Khn de Poitrine and Klek de Poitrine. They are best known for their rock musical style and absurdist costumes. Angine de Poitrine can currently be heard on their new album Vol.II, which released in April 2026.
