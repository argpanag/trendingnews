WALANG PASOK: Mga suspendidong klase sa Biyernes, September 4, 2026

TheTools · 03.09.2026 23:12 · source · permalink

Suspendido ang klase sa mga paaralan sa ilang bahagi ng bansa sa Biyernes, September 4, 2026, dahil sa baha at masamang lagay ng panahon na dulot ng Habagat.

Metro Manila

  • Manila City: all levels, public and private, shift to Alternative Learning Modalities (ALMs).

Cebu

  • Cebu City - face-to-face classes in all levels both public and private

Batangas

  • Taal - face-to-face classes all levels public and private

Pangasinan

  • Basista: all levels, public and private
  • Binmaley: all levels, public and private
  • Mangatarem: all levels, public and private

Ilocos Sur

  • Magsingal: all levels, public and private, shift to Alternative Learning Modalities (ALMs).

La Union

  • Tubao: all levels, public and private, shift to modular distance learning or other alternative learning modalities

Benguet

Bokod: Pre-School to Senior High School, public and private

Tarlac

  • Tarlac City: all levels, public and private

Pampanga

  • Apalit: all levels, public and private,
  • San Fernando: All levels, public and private
  • Guagua: All levels, public and private
  • Masantol: All levels, public and private
  • Minalin: no face-to-face classes, all levels, public and private, shift to Alternative Delivery Modes
  • San Simon: All levels, public and private, maliban sa Concepcion Integrated School
  • Sta. Ana: no face-to-face classes, all levels, public and private, shift to Alternative Delivery Modes
  • Sta. Rita: no face-to-face classes, all levels, public and private, shift to Alternative Delivery Modes
