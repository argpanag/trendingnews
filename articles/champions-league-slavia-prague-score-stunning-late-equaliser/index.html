Champions League: Slavia Prague score stunning late equaliser to deny Fenerbahce

Kacor scored his first senior goal for Slavia Prague

Pavel Kacor scored a stunning late equaliser for Slavia Prague to deny Fenerbahce a much-needed Champions League win.

The substitute scored his first senior goal for the club with a brilliant volley when he met Emmanuel Ayaosi's cross in the 90th minute.

Kacor, 20, managed to get his boot high enough to meet the ball in and beat former Manchester City goalkeeper Ederson as the Czech side came from two goals down to earn their first point in the competition this season.

Fenerbahce, who now have two points from three games, had raced to a two-goal lead after just 11 minutes and looked set to bounce back from last week's 6-0 thrashing by Aston Villa with ease.

Oguz Aydin scored a sublime goal from 25 yards in the fourth minute when he cut in from the right and curled home left-footed into the top left corner.

Mason Greenwood got his seventh of the season with a fine finish just seven minutes later when he controlled Nelson Semedo's pass with his left foot before firing home with his right.

Big-spending Fenerbahce were looking for a first win in the Champions League proper since beating Chelsea in the first leg of a 2008 quarter-final, but they gifted the visitors a way back into the game in the 15th minute.

Matteo Guendouzi misplaced a pass when trying to play out from the back and to Lukas Provod found the net with a first-time finish for the away side.

Fenerbahce squandered good chances to restore their two-goal lead, but Slavia Prague pushed on in the second half for an equaliser and Kacor found it with an acrobatic finish.
