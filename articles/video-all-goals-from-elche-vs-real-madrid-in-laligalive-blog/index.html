Elche Look to Stop a Red-Hot Real Madrid

Real Madrid visit Elche on Tuesday at the Martínez Valero Stadium for Matchday 6 of LaLiga, in a game that pits two teams in very different situations against each other. While the Spanish giants arrive full of confidence after a convincing win over Rayo Vallecano, the hosts are still searching for their first league victory and urgently need a response.

The pressure is firmly on the home side. Elche have just two points from a possible 15 and currently sit near the bottom of the table, just behind Valencia, while Real Madrid are second and looking to remain firmly in the title race.



Madrid's Attacking Power Is the Main Threat

The biggest challenge for Elche will be containing an attack that is enjoying a particularly productive spell. Real Madrid have scored 12 goals in their last five matches across all competitions, a return that helps explain their position near the top of the table and leaves the home side with virtually no room for defensive mistakes.

The 4-1 win over Rayo Vallecano was especially impressive because of how quickly Madrid took control of the match.

Mbappé remains the most important name in that attacking formula. The French forward is not only finding the net consistently, but has also publicly highlighted the influence of José Mourinho on his development within the team.



Elche Need to Turn Resistance Into Points

After a start featuring zero wins, two draws and three defeats, every point is becoming increasingly valuable for Elche. The 1-1 draw against Athletic Club at San Mamés ended a run of three straight defeats, but it has not solved the team's larger problems.

Elche have conceded 13 goals and scored only five in their last five matches, a record that highlights their struggles to compete consistently. The 0-5 defeat to Barcelona was one of the lowest points of their campaign so far, and now another of the title favorites stands in their way.



Recent History Favors Real Madrid

Recent history also makes Real Madrid the clear favorite. In the last five meetings between the two teams, Madrid have recorded three wins, while two matches have ended in draws. Elche have yet to beat the Spanish giants during that stretch.

The latest meeting took place on March 14, 2026, when Real Madrid earned a 4-1 victory at the Santiago Bernabéu. Across those last five encounters, Madrid have scored 14 goals and conceded five, another sign of the attacking dominance they have established against Elche.
