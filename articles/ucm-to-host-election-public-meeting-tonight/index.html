UCM to host election public meeting tonight

TheTools · 19.09.2026 09:16 · source · permalink

Friday, 18 September 2026 14:14

A constituency meeting for voters in Douglas North will take place this evening.

It gives the chance for people to hear from and question candidates standing in their area. 

The meeting is taking place at University College Isle of Man, from 7pm. 

All seven candidates in the constituency have been invited to attend.
