NSW reporting rise in hospital visits over unapproved peptide use

TheTools · 20.10.2026 04:09 · source · permalink
  • On Air
  • Heather du Plessis-Allan Drive

New data from NSW Health shows there's an increase in hospitalisations because of unregulated peptides, and it's prompted concerns among experts.

Peptides have seen a boost in popularity thanks to social media, with many people turning to them to boost muscle mass and reduce the impact of aging.

Australian correspondent Oliver Peterson says people are reporting liver and kidney damage, as well as allergic reactions.
