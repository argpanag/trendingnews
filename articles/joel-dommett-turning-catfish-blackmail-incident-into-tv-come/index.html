Joel Dommett turning catfish blackmail incident into TV comedy drama - British Comedy Guide

TheTools · 17.09.2026 15:26 · source · permalink

Thursday 17th September 2026, 4:26pm

Joel Dommett is writing a TV script based on the real-life story of how he was blackmailed after filming an intimate video.

The Mirror reports the comic is working up a comedy drama based on his 2016 experience.

The stand-up star was tricked, by a woman he believed was called Staci Taylor, into filming a video of himself masturbating. Several years later, as he was about to host I'm A Celebrity... Get Me Out Of Here!, the people behind 'Staci Taylor' then got in touch and attempted to blackmail him. When they failed to extort money, they posted the footage they had recorded on the internet.

The comic owned the situation, and went on to base his next Edinburgh show on the ordeal.

In a twist to the story, it later emerged that the comic later met Natalia, the woman whose face had been used by the extortioners in their blackmail trap, and they ended up dating for six months.

Talking about his TV plans, Dommett said: "They say you should write about what you know so basically I am focusing on my own story at various points in my life and going into detail about the catfish scenario, the attempt to extort money from me and what happened there.

"I think it's a really interesting story to tell and I'm actually finding it a cathartic, therapeutic experience to write about a decade on from when it happened even though, at the time, it could have finished my career.

"I'm part way through writing the scripts for the show and won't be rushing to submit them to a TV company - I would prefer to have a really good product before I give it to people. But I love the idea of doing a comedy drama about my own life with myself in it."

The comic said he was still considering a title for the show, joking: "Who knows what it will be called? I Got Caught With My Dick Out, probably that!"
