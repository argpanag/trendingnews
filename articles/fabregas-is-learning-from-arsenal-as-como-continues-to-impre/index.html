Fabregas is learning from Arsenal as Como continues to impress - Just Arsenal News

TheTools · 15.09.2026 20:15 · source · permalink

Cesc Fabregas is doing an amazing job at Como as they cope well with the fixture congestion of playing in the Champions League and Serie A this season.

Many people expected Como to struggle after they overachieved in the previous campaign, but they have so far looked comfortable. They have won three and lost none of their opening four league matches, while also beating their first Champions League opponent.

This is shaping up to be another surprisingly comfortable season for Como, and Fabregas’ reputation as one of the best managers in the game continues to grow. His work has already attracted attention as he looks to build on the progress made last season.

He is one of the former Arsenal players who followed in the footsteps of Arsene Wenger by becoming a manager. Fabregas learnt a great deal from the Frenchman during his playing career, and that experience is proving useful as he develops his own approach from the touchline.

Arsenal provide inspiration

However, it is not only Wenger’s influence that has helped Fabregas. The current Arsenal team has also served as an inspiration to him as he follows the exploits of Mikel Arteta’s side from Italy.

Fabregas has admitted that Como are learning from the Gunners, particularly when it comes to the contribution defenders can make in attack. Arsenal have developed a reputation for getting important goals from their defenders and from set plays.

That approach has clearly caught Fabregas’ attention, and he believes Como could benefit by adding similar qualities to their own capabilities. His comments also show the influence Arteta’s team can have beyond the Premier League.

Fabregas wants defensive goals

After Como’s 2-1 win against Parma, Fabregas explained how Arsenal have influenced his thinking. He said via Football Italia:

“In modern football, it is very important to have defenders who score goals. Arsenal won the Premier League largely with goals from defenders and on set plays. If we add this to our capabilities, then it’s an advantage.”

Fabregas’ comments underline his admiration for Arsenal’s approach and his willingness to learn from other successful teams. With Como balancing domestic and European football, incorporating ideas that can improve their attacking threat could prove valuable as the season progresses.
