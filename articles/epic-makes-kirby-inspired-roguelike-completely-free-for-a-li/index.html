Epic Makes Kirby-inspired Roguelike Completely Free for a Limited Time

TheTools · 12.11.2026 15:59 · source · permalink

Enjoy a 'mostly positive'-rated roguelike autobattler for 100% off

The Epic Games Store is offering a great free PC game this week for users. For a limited time, you can grab a roguelike tower defense game at no charge.

Knightica

Knightica is a roguelike auto-battler with a bright aesthetic reminiscent of Kirby; its knight mascot even looks a little bit like Meta Knight. You'll lead an army in turn-based auto-battles as you attempt to free the Kingdom. Like many roguelikes, you'll have freedom in your approach to battle and the paths you take, like tackling a powerful enemy or dipping into a strange shop.

Knightica came out last summer and has been a hit with players. It has over 1,100 "Mostly Positive" player reviews on Steam. "Knightica scratches my ever-present roguelike autobattler itch," one player said. "Ludicrously addicting," another added.

If Knightica catches your eye, all you need to do is log in to the Epic Games Store and claim it. The game will be free until 11 a.m. EST on Thursday, Nov. 19.
