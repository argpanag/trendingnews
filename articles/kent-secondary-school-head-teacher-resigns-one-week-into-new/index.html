Kent secondary school head teacher resigns one week into new term

The head teacher of a secondary school has resigned just one week into the new term for “personal reasons”.

Alex Holmes had led the Whitstable School since 2024, but in a letter sent to parents last week, the boss of Swale Academies Trust said the head had chosen to step down.

“I am writing to inform you that Mr Holmes has decided to leave his position of Headteacher at The Whitstable School due to personal reasons,” wrote Owen McColgan.

"We wish him the best for the future and thank him for his commitment and service to the Whitstable School.”

“As Chief Executive of Swale Academies Trust, I wanted to write to you personally to reassure you about the leadership arrangements we have put in place.”

He added that deputy head teacher Stephen Scales would serve as acting head teacher and Swale Academies Trust Kent leader of education Simon Cox would be brought in to support him as executive head teacher.

“I know that changes in leadership can naturally raise questions for families,” continued Mr McColgan.

“I want to reassure you that the Trust will work closely with Mr Scales, Mr Cox and the wider leadership team to support the school through this transition.

“Our focus remains firmly on the pupils: ensuring that they continue to receive a high-quality education and that the school continues to move forward positively.”

The Whitstable School – previously known as Community College Whitstable – converted to an academy in 2018.

When last visited by Ofsted in March 2024, the Bellevue Road site was rated “Good” in every inspection area.

At the time of his appointment, Mr Holmes wrote in a KentOnline sponsored editorial: “It has been a real pleasure to become the new Headteacher of The Whitstable School and to be welcomed so warmly by the pupils, parents and local community.

“I am very much looking forward to welcoming the community into the school.”

No other details have been released about the nature of the reasons for his departure.

The Whitstable School and Swale Academies Trust have been contacted for comment.
