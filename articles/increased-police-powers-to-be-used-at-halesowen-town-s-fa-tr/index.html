Increased police powers to be used at Halesowen Town's FA Trophy tie

TheTools · 14.11.2026 16:27 · source · permalink

West Midlands Police says it has had information that groups of youths may want to use the game in Halesowen "to engage in violence and disorder"

  • Published
    14 November 2026, 12:24 GMT

Extra patrols and increased police powers will be in force as Halesowen Town and Redditch United clash in the FA Trophy on Saturday.

West Midlands Police said it had received information that groups of youths may want to use the fixture as a "pretext to engage in violence and disorder".

In response, the force will use Section 60 powers from 10:00 to 22:00 GMT around Halesowen, which gives officers the ability to stop and search people without the need to have reasonable grounds.

Ch Insp Adam Henderson, of Dudley Police, said: "We won't tolerate anyone who is seen to be committing crime, taking part in disorder or causing a nuisance in the area."

He added: "You may see more police around the town centre, but this is purely for reassurance purposes, and we are working closely and in contact with both football clubs ahead of the game.

"Whatever the result, we want to encourage everyone to enjoy the day and play their part in making it a positive experience for everyone."

The FA Trophy second round match kicks off at 15:00 GMT at Halesowen Town's Grove Recreation Ground.

Halesowen Town told the BBC the safety of everyone attending match was the club's priority.

A spokesperson said: "We're confident that the arrangements in place will allow supporters from both clubs to enjoy what should be a fantastic occasion.

"We would encourage everyone attending to follow the guidance of stewards and police officers, be respectful of others and enjoy the day."

Redditch United's chairman David Faulkner admitted disorder at non-league games was a "massive issue".

He said he expected about 200 of the club's supporters to attend, but added he believed most trouble would be caused by "massive chunks" of fans from other clubs in the area.
