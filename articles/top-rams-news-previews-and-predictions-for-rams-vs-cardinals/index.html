Top Rams News: Previews and predictions for Rams vs. Cardinals on Sunday at SoFi Stadium for Week 6

Experts make their predictions for Week 6

Here's a collection of analysts' predictions and previews for Rams-Cardinals:

  • All five NFL.com analysts picked the Rams to beat the Cardinals. Read why Brooke Cersosimo picked the Rams to win here.
