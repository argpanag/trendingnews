Future PNG Chiefs in blue for now, as Toa Samoa seek to topple the Kumuls - ABC Pacific

TheTools · 22.10.2026 20:00 · source · permalink

The second round of pool matches in the Rugby League World Cup kicks off in Sydney when Tonga play Lebanon in the men's competition, and Fetu Samoa take on Wales in the women's tournament.

Meanwhile the excitement levels will be rising in Port Moresby ahead of the blockbuster that pits Papua New Guinea against Toa Samoa.

The Kumuls will be out to make a statement against a team who many pundits think can win the whole tournament, while the Samoans will have two big names in their ranks who are set to star for the PNG Chiefs in 2028.
