Liam Neeson's 114-Minute Action Thriller Sequel Flop Becomes One of the Biggest Movies in the World

TheTools · 03.10.2026 18:00 · source · permalink

One of the most underrated action thrillers in Liam Neeson's massive catalog has finally found success on streaming. The Academy Award-nominated star, best known for franchises like Taken and movies like Unknown and The Commuter, has become a reliable action draw. But curiously, not all his films have received instant acclaim, with some of them falling under the umbrella of commercial flops. Among these, we will find a sequel that flew under the radar last year while Neeson's involvement in The Naked Gun was taking up all the headlines. Fortunately, sometimes it's just about letting time pass, finding the right audience, and making a comeback on streaming.

Neeson Once Again Shines in 'Ice Road: Vengeance'

Ice Road: Vengeance is the sequel to Neeson's 2021 underrated action thriller The Ice Road. Neeson reprises his role as truck driver Mike McCann, who travels to Nepal to scatter the ashes of Gurty, his brother who passed away in the previous film. The problem is that McCann finds himself in trouble again when he joins a tour group that takes him across a dangerous mountain pass. On the bus, there's a man being targeted because of his opposition to local corruption, and those who are looking for him have also joined the tour. McCann decides to stand up to the mercenaries who have hijacked the bus, and must fight to keep everyone alive.

Though the original saw Neeson star beside familiar faces like Laurence Fishburne, Amber Midthunder, and Holt McCallany, the sequel doesn't feature a well-known supporting cast. Only Marcus Thomas returns, appearing in flashbacks as his deceased character. This leaves the movie in Neeson's hands, who does what he can despite the thin script. Luckily, his on-screen presence is appealing enough to carry the movie to the very end.

Ice Road: Vengeance is far from being a perfect thriller. It has its faults, but the action sequences are good enough to keep viewers on the edge of their seats, and Neeson delivers his usual tough-guy performance. While it doesn't need anything else to provide a quick, mindless watch, the film failed to capture the attention of critics when it was released last year.

'Ice Road: Vengeance' Becomes a Hit and Defies Critics

Ice Road: Vengeance had a short theatrical run before going straight to digital streaming in July 2025. The reaction wasn't positive at first, but when it went to subscription-based platforms, it became one of the biggest titles in streaming. The critical reception remains poor, and today, it sits at 16% on the critics' score and 24% on the audience score on Rotten Tomatoes. It is one of Neeson's lowest-rated projects of his entire career.

However, according to the latest update to the charts, Ice Road: Vengeance has now become one of the biggest movies in the world. At the time of writing, the 114-minute sequel sits at No. 6 on HBO Max's global most-watched movies list. While titles like Supergirl and Pearl currently rank higher, Neeson's film has topped other popular titles like Interview with the Vampire, Puss in Boots: The Last Wish, and Scream 7.
