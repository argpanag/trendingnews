Mathernová describes the work of the EU Delegation as attacks on Kyiv intensify | Межа

TheTools · 15.11.2026 17:45 · source · permalink

At the EU Delegation in Kyiv, the exterior walls are being reinforced with sandbags, and soft seating has been placed in the corridors for air raid alerts.

The EU ambassador to Ukraine, Katarina Mathernová, described the delegation’s working conditions amid intensifying Russian attacks on the capital.

This is reported by The Guardian.

She described this in an interview with The Guardian, the European Pravda reports.

Areas where staff can stay during air raid alerts have been set up in the corridors. The building has folding beds and additional shower stalls.

“I believe these are warning shots. The Russians are becoming ever more brazen and ruthless. The attacks are much more intense than last year, and power cuts are happening earlier. There was no water in our building. A modern city’s weak point is its sewage system”

- Katarina Mathernová

During Russian strikes, Mathernová sleeps on a mattress in the bathroom of her hotel room.

According to the ambassador, Vladimir Putin’s strategic goal is the destruction of the EU. She believes the recent threats against embassies in Kyiv were made in the hope that some diplomats from countries of the “Global South” might leave Ukraine.

At the same time, most ambassadors say that their continued presence is a powerful signal of solidarity with Ukraine’s leadership, which is in a difficult position. Earlier, all embassies of EU countries announced their decision to remain in Kyiv.
