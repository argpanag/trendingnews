Worlds 2027 heads to Japan and Korea, MSI goes to Spain and First Stand moves to China

TheTools · 14.11.2026 06:13 · source · permalink

Riot Games has set new hosts for all three League of Legends international events in 2027, with Worlds starting in Japan for the first time and MSI going to Spain. The plan also moves an expanded First Stand to China, reshaping travel plans for fans across Asia and Europe.

Riot Games will split League of Legends’ 2027 international calendar between East Asia and Europe. Worlds 2027 goes to Japan for the first time. Its Swiss Stage will be held in Nagoya. The quarterfinals and semifinals move to the Suwon Convention Center in South Korea. The final follows at the new Seoul Arena.

MSI 2027 heads to the Feria de València, the first time the event has gone to Spain. First Stand also changes shape and location. The 2027 edition becomes First Stand: All In, a one-off expanded version with more teams and players. It moves from its earlier Southeast Asia slot to China. First Stand will return to Southeast Asia in 2028.

Riot announced the plan on November 13, the day before the Gen.G vs BLG final in Brooklyn. It had first given an early look at the roadmap and host cities during its League of Legends Showcase. The changes touch all three international events next year.

The Grand Final venue is not finished yet. Seoul Arena was 81% complete, with completion due in May 2027 and an opening in June 2027, according to Financial News. The main hall is designed for up to 28,000 people. A second hall will hold 7,000.

Exact dates for Worlds 2027 have not been announced. Neither has the Nagoya venue. The split route means fans following the event from start to finish will have to travel between two countries. Japanese fans get a Worlds stage at home, while South Korea hosts the knockout rounds and the final.

MSI 2027 will be the first international League of Legends tournament in Europe since the Worlds 2024 final in London. It is listed with two teams from each major region. The tournament is expected to run from June to July 2027, with exact days still to be set. Dates and ticket details have not been released.

That window matches this year’s event. MSI 2026 was held in Daejeon, South Korea, from June 28 to July 12, 2026. Hanwha Life Esports beat Bilibili Gaming in that final.

The First Stand move shifts an event away from Southeast Asian fans for a year. Riot is billing First Stand: All In as the largest tournament it has ever produced. Its structure is still open. Riot will return in January with a detailed look at First Stand: All In, including its format and how teams qualify.
