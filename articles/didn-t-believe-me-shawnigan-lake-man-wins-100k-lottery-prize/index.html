'Didn’t believe me': Shawnigan Lake man wins $100K lottery prize

TheTools · 22.10.2026 17:35 · source · permalink

A Shawnigan Lake man is six figures richer after winning a $100,000 lottery prize last week.

Dylan S. won a $100,000 MAXPLUS prize from the Lotto Max draw on Oct. 1.

He says he was “freaked out” when he learned of his win after checking the ticket on a whim outside a local gas station.

“I scanned it a few times and then went inside the gas station to double-check,” he said in a release Thursday.

Dylan says he shared the good news with friends and close family members, who were a little skeptical at first.

“They are happy for me and half of them didn’t believe me,” he said. “My dad said I should make sure it’s not $100.”

Dylan says he plans to use the winnings on helping with a down payment for a home. His winning ticket was purchased at Mason’s Store & Patio on Renfrew Road in Shawnigan Lake.

“I’m super excited. Before I was happy to just win a free play!” he said.

The odds of winning a $100,000 MAXPLUS prize from a Lotto Max draw are 1 in 33,446,140.
