British soldier killed in Ukraine crash named

TheTools · 17.09.2026 01:10 · source · permalink

‘Exceptionally bright and professional’ Major Paul Wilks died in road traffic accident

A British Army intelligence officer who died in a crash in Ukraine has been hailed as “an exceptional leader” with a “razor-sharp intellect”.

The Ministry of Defence (MoD) identified him as Major Paul Wilks, 40, in a statement on Wednesday evening.

The father of two died in a “road traffic incident” which is understood not to have resulted from hostile action.

Major Wilks, who served in Afghanistan, was awarded two operational service medals during his tours.

Lt Gen Matthew Jones, chief of defence intelligence in the UK, said Major Wilks was one of the “most talented” intelligence officers in a generation.

He said: “On behalf of the whole Intelligence Corps, we are heartbroken to learn of Major Paul Wilks’s death.

“Paul was an exceptional leader who was undoubtedly one of the most talented intelligence officers of his generation.

“With a selfless and compassionate approach, his primary concern was always his team. Paul possessed a razor-sharp intellect and a steely determination to succeed.

“He leaves behind a legacy that will endure within the Intelligence Corps and beyond.”

‘He embodied the best of the UK military’

The MoD said: “Major Wilks was an exceptionally bright and professional officer who embodied the best of the UK military.

“He was a gifted leader who earned the admiration and respect of all those fortunate enough to have served alongside him. He was a formidable officer, colleague and friend to all those who knew him.

“He was a man of great intellect, yet he remained a mate to those he served with.”

His commanding officer said: “It is with profound sadness that we honour the memory of Major Paul Wilks.

“Even in high-pressure environments, Paul maintained a sharp sense of humour and a genuine warmth that connected him to everyone in this unit.

“He spoke often of his love for his wife and two sons, expressing his deep gratitude for the support they provided, which allowed him to serve with such distinction.”

Wes Streeting, the Defence Secretary, said: “The tributes paid by those who worked with Paul illustrate his exceptional talent and ability, underscored by genuine warmth, compassion and a desire to support others.

“His loss is a poignant reminder of the enormous sacrifice that our Armed Forces and their families make in the service of our country. My thoughts remain with his family, loved ones and colleagues.”

Second fatality of Armed Forces member in Ukraine

The Government previously acknowledged the presence of a small number of British troops operating in Ukraine to provide security for UK diplomats and to support Kyiv’s military.

The incident is the second confirmed death of a member of the Armed Forces in Ukraine.

L/Cpl George Hooley, a paratrooper, was killed in December while watching a test of a new Ukrainian drone.

The MoD said the 28-year-old died in an accident away from the front line. He was not involved in any combat operations in Ukraine.

Vladimir Putin has continued with fresh strikes across Ukraine, which included a drone strike on a railway station on Sunday.
