Long-Awaited PS5 Exclusive RPG Out on PS Store, Players Love It - PlayStation LifeStyle

TheTools · 29.10.2026 12:37 · source · permalink

Home »

S-Game’s highly anticipated PS5 console exclusive action RPG is finally out on the PS Store, and players are already in love with it. Semi-open-world wuxia RPG Phantom Blade Zero has already wooed critics, and now players have the chance to check it out for themselves.

PS5 console exclusive RPG Phantom Blade Zero launched to a stellar reception

Phantom Blade Zero has already earned 4.82/5 Stars on the PS Store based on nearly 4,000 verified votes and counting. The game’s standard version costs $59.99, and its Digital Deluxe Edition costs $69.99. Here’s what the latter includes:

  • Deluxe Edition Exclusive Outfit: Wulin Legend
  • Deluxe Edition Exclusive Outfit: Blood-Steel Form
  • Digital Artbook
  • Digital Soundtrack (OST)
  • Phantom Blade Zero full game

Players step into the shoes of “Soul,” who is framed for a murder and only has 66 days to live.

“Alone and hunted by his former comrades, he moves through a world of shifting loyalties and silent blades — determined to unravel the conspiracy that has condemned him,” the plot reads. “Rain mixes with blood, mercy entangles with vengeance. Soul’s journey is one of love, hate, and the fragile search for what it means to have a heart.”

Phantom Blade Zero is PS5 Pro enhanced. PS Plus Premium members can cloud stream the title.
