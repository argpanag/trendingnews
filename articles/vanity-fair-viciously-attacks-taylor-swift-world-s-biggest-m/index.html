Vanity Fair really hates Taylor Swift.

They’re not holding back, either.

A new article has appeared online by May Jeong. It’s called “The Mastermind,” and it accuses Taylor of everything except the Lincoln assassination. Or having satanic rituals in her bedroom.

Jeong’s piece seethes with something personal. It’s like it was written on a high school girls’ room wall.

She compares Swift to the villain in “Mean Girls.”

Jeong’s article pretty much guarantees Swift will never do an interview with them. She certainly won’t come to their Oscar party. Really, what was the point of this vivisection?

The new Vanity Fair seems to have Asperger’s. They can’t take social cues. They don’t know how to behave in a crowd they want to impress except to spit into drinks. It’s bewildering. Vanity Fair needs Taylor Swift, not the other way around. She can live without them quite nicely.

Taylor Swift is not an easy profile. She’s very complex. She’s annoying, but most geniuses are. What she’s accomplished is stunning, and cannpt be explained with a slag off.

What’s going to happen? The Swifties are going to boycott Conde Nast magazines. They should start with Vogue, where it will really hurt.
