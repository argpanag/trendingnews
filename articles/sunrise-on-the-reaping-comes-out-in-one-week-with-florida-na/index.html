'Sunrise on the Reaping' comes out in one week with Florida native

TheTools · 14.11.2026 02:03 · source · permalink
  • A Jacksonville, Florida resident, Devon Singletary, was cast in the new "Hunger Games" prequel after an open casting call.
  • Singletary will portray Blair, the best friend of a young Haymitch Abernathy, in "Sunrise on the Reaping."
  • The new film follows Haymitch Abernathy's story during the 50th Hunger Games, 24 years before the original series.

One Florida resident is entering the world of Panem next week.

After broadcasting an open casting call, Lionsgate announced last year that Devon Singletary of Jacksonville, Florida, will appear in the latest Hunger Games prequel, "Sunrise on the Reaping."

“I love the ‘Sunrise on the Reaping’ book,” he said in the announcement video posted on his Instagram. “Being selected into the ‘Hunger Games’ is a great honor.”

Now over a year later, "Hunger Games" fans and Floridians alike will get to see the actor as "Blair" in the new movie. The movie follows the story of fan-favorite character Haymitch Abernathy as he wins the Hunger Games.

Who is Devon Singletary? Which Hunger Games role did he get?

Devon Singletary is a 27-year-old actor and filmmaker based in Jacksonville, Florida. According to his online portfolio, he has appeared in commercials and done some personal projects. He describes himself online as an actor, "but most importantly, I'm a ball of energy."

"I'm super passionate about what I do. I have been doing this for nearly a decade, and I have a desire to do more in this career field," he writes.

He will portray Haymitch's best friend, Blair. A video posted to the Hunger Games social media accounts shows the moment Singletary learned he had been cast and met the other stars of the film.

“I’m blown away,” he said in the video. “I’m really just speechless. I’m super excited.”

What is 'Sunrise on the Reaping' about?

Beware! Slight spoilers below.

The fifth book in the series is set during the 50th Hunger Games — the second Quarter Quell — through the eyes of a victor and Katniss Everdeen's mentor, Haymitch Abernathy. During these special games, twice as many children are reaped.

Readers meet Haymitch as a lovestruck, sarcastic teenager trying to make the most of his 16th birthday (which always falls on Reaping Day).

He spends most of the morning trying to hang out with his girlfriend, a Covey girl named Lenore Dove, before heading to the ceremony. However, after an incident draws unwanted attention to him, Haymitch is unfairly reaped for the Games.

The story shows how Haymitch won his games and how he tried to end them forever, with unfortunate consequences.

The book also features several characters from the original trilogy and gives several Easter eggs for eagle-eyed fans. For example, readers learn the origin story of Katniss's iconic Mockingjay pin, which she is gifted in the first novel.

Where does 'Sunrise on the Reaping' take place in Hunger Games universe?

The latest "Hunger Games" novel takes place 24 years before Katniss and Peeta Mellark stepped into the arena. The original novel and film depicted the 74th Hunger Games.

It also takes place 40 years after the first prequel story, "The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes." This novel focused on the future president of Panem, Coriolanus Snow, before his rise to power and his relationship with a District 11 tribute during the 10th annual Hunger Games.

Where can I stream 'The Hunger Games' movies?

All five Hunger Games movies are currently available on Hulu and Disney+ — "The Hunger Games (2012)," "The Hunger Games: Catching Fire "(2013), "The Hunger Games: Mockingjay, Part 1" (2014), "The Hunger Games: Mockingjay, Part 2 (2015) and "The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes" (2023).
