    Parker Washington tallies elite first half in win

    Parker Washington

    JAC Wide Receiver #11
    Player Stats

    Parker Washington caught 5-of-6 targets for 83 yards and a touchdown in the Jaguars’ Week 1 win over the Browns.

    Washington saw elite usage in the first half, making the first Jacksonville catch of the game and ending the Jaguars’ first drive with a diving 30-yard touchdown reception. He followed it up with a one-handed sideline catch on the second drive and tallied five receptions in the first half. His usage was that of a legitimate No. 1 wide receiver for the Jaguars’ fantasy-friendly offense. The game was out of hand in the second half, leading to no more receptions the rest of the day, but fantasy managers will be happy with Washington’s utilization and production as he adds to his elite box scores from the end of last season. Washington is a fantasy WR2 heading into Week 2 against the Broncos.
    - Aditya Fuldeore
