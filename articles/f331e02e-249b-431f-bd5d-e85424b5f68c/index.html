Who will win the AJ Auxerre vs Olympique de Marseille pro soccer game originally scheduled for Nov 29, 2026?

This market will resolve based on the result at the end of 90 minutes of regulation play (including any stoppage time added by the referee). Extra time and penalty shootouts are excluded.

This market will resolve to “Yes” for the outcome that matches the official result:

• Away Team: Resolves to “Yes” if the Away team is leading at the end of 90 minutes (including stoppage time). • Home Team: Resolves to “Yes” if Home team is leading at the end of 90 minutes (including stoppage time). • Draw: Resolves to “Yes” if the score is level at the end of 90 minutes (including stoppage time).

All other outcomes will resolve to “No”.

If a game is paused after it has already started and play resumes within 48 hours of the original start time, all contracts will proceed with normal settlement.
