No. 1 Nebraska volleyball team stays unbeaten with four-set win over No. 13 Purdue

TheTools · 17.10.2026 23:16 · source · permalink

LINCOLN, Neb. (KOLN) - Nebraska volleyball improves to 19-0 with a four-set win over No. 13 Purdue, 25-13, 20-25, 25-19, 25-14.

The No. 1 Huskers dropped a set for the first time in a month, but used a career-high 15-kill performance from Teraya Sigler to lead the winning charge. Harper Murray chipped in with 13 kills, while Virginia Adriano had 11.

Nebraska had a .303 hitting percentage, compared to .133 for Purdue. The Huskers return to action at home on Thursday against UCLA.
