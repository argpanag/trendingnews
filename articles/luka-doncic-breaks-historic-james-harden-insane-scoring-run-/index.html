After a 43-point masterpiece against the Suns, Lakers superstar Luka Doncic now holds the most points (468 PTS) through the first 12 games of the season in the last 50 years, surpassing James Harden.

Luka Doncic went ballistic once again for the Los Angeles Lakers.

The all-time offensive run of the Slovenian superstar to start this 2026-27 NBA season continues, as Doncic dropped another 40-point bomb to lead the Lakers' 131-123 Sunday victory on the road against the Phoenix Suns.

For 36 minutes, Doncic erupted yet again with 43 points, going 13-of-28 from the field while splashing eight triples to overwhelm Phoenix.

Combined with nine key assists, Doncic completely paced the Lakers' red-hot season start as they are now holding an eight-game win streak to further improve their NBA-best 12-2 record.

After delivering a 43-point explosion in Phoenix, Luka Doncic earned himself another compelling NBA feat.

The Slovenian superstar now holds the most points (468 PTS) through the first 12 games of an NBA regular season in the last 50 years, surpassing James Harden.

Harden went off with a staggering 464 total points through his first 12 games for the Houston Rockets back in the 2019-20 NBA season.

Doncic's scoring spree across a 12-game run is now the most by any player since the great Wilt Chamberlain (1962-63).

Across his 12 appearances so far for the Lakers, Doncic is averaging a mind-blowing 39.0 points with 7.8 assists and 7.2 rebounds. Right now, he continues to build momentum for an early MVP campaign in this new NBA season.

Doncic and the Lakers will now head back to LA as they finish their four-game road trip with a perfect record. They will host the Charlotte Hornets for the first night of their back-to-back games on Tuesday.
