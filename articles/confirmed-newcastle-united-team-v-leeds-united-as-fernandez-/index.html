The confirmed Newcastle United team v Leeds United has now been announced.

Tuesday seeing the sixth match of the season for NUFC.

A third competitive fixture for Matthias Jaissle away from St James’ Park.

The new manager having seen his team win at both Millwall and Spurs on the road.

At SJP a 3-2 win in the Carabao Cup over West Brom, plus 2-2 draws against both Liverpool and Bournemouth in the Premier League.

Now tonight at Elland Road for an 8pm kick-off.

Newcastle United team v Leeds United:

Hornicek; Dedic, Thiaw (c), Botman, Hall; Miley, Gonzalez; Murphy, Wissa, Barnes; Fernandez-Pardo

So it is three changes from the team that started against Millwall on Tuesday.

IN:

Thiaw, Fernandez-Pardo, Lewis Miley

OUT:

Schar, Ramsey, Willock

Subs:

Pope, Livramento, Schar, Bamba, Steur, Toure, Willock, Ramsey, Mason Miley

No Anthony Elanga in the squad and Matthias Jaissle has confirmed he misses due to an (unspecified) injury.
