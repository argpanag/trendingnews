Amouta considers starting Soufiane Benjdida against S… | YSscores

TheTools · 02.09.2026 14:32 · source · permalink

Al-Hussein's coach, Amouta, is considering starting Soufiane Benjdida in the match against Smouha tomorrow in the Egyptian League, according to the newspaper "Youm7".

The newspaper reported that the coaching staff of Al Ahly FC is exploring the possibility of giving Soufiane Benjdida a chance to appear in the starting lineup against Smouha SC, after his participation in recent matches and a positive assessment of his performance by the coaching staff.

Al Ahly FC will face Smouha SC tomorrow, Thursday, at Cairo International Stadium in the third round of the Egyptian League.

In contrast, the coaching staff is moving towards putting Algerian Mounsef Bakrar on the bench against Smouha SC, after he participated in the starting lineup during the last match against ZED.

The newspaper added that Amouta's decision is due to his desire to implement rotation and involve the most prepared players, ensuring strong competition among Al Ahly FC's attackers, given the multiple attacking options available in the team's lineup.
