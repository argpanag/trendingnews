Snowball draw tonight: Estimated HK$73 mn jackpot for single winner, check out hottest numbers

TheTools · 24.11.2026 17:17 · source · permalink

The Mark Six "Lucky Two" Snowball will be drawn tonight (Nov. 24). This time, the Jockey Club is trialling a "boosted snowball" mechanism for the first time, meaning the snowball amount changes from a fixed sum to a floating one — the more bets placed, the higher the prize. The snowball amount will be determined by the accumulated betting turnover recorded at 2 pm the day before the draw. As of 2 pm yesterday, over HK$65 million had been recorded, with the estimated first prize for a single winning ticket reaching HK$73 million.

Looking back at 50 years of Mark Six draws, the highest first prize in history was set at last year's Chinese New Year Snowball on the fifth day of the Lunar New Year (Feb. 2), when total betting turnover reached HK$380 million and a single winning ticket took home HK$193 million. On Dec. 6 of the same year, there was also a half-unit first prize win, with each unit paying out over HK$120 million.

Feb. 2, 2025 draw results: 14, 18, 30, 36, 40, 42; Extra number: 1 (one first prize winner, payout HK$193 million)

Dec. 6, 2025 draw results: 4, 6, 26, 28, 34, 40; Extra number: 25 (half-unit first prize, each unit paid over HK$120 million)

Six hottest numbers: 30, 49, 24, 7, 22, 13

According to draw statistics from 2002 to Nov. 19 this year, 9 of the 49 numbers have been drawn more than 500 times: No. 30 tops the list with 527 appearances; No. 49 ranks second with 520; No. 24 is third with 519; No. 7 is fourth with 511. In addition, Nos. 22 and 13 are tied for fifth with 509 appearances each.
