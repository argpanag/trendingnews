I Shopped Hundreds of Black Friday Clothing Deals—15 Best Sales From Aritzia, Lululemon, Quince That Are Live Now

TheTools · 20.11.2026 20:48 · source · permalink

Time to update the wardrobe and stock up on some gifts!

We've been independently researching and testing products for over 120 years. If you buy through our links, we may earn a commission. Learn more about our review process.

I’ve been trying to curb my clothing shopping lately, but these Black Friday deals are testing my willpower. There are some pretty good markdowns this year, and even the early sales are worth checking out now.

As a shopping editor who has covered Prime Day since 2019, I firmly believe that some of these discounts—like those from Madewell and Lululemon—are at the best prices they will be all season. Below, I rounded up pieces and brands that our staffers love, including top-tested names that our pros at the Good Housekeeping Institute Textiles Lab recommend again and again.

Quince​

I got my mom a Quince sweater last Christmas, and she’s worn the heck out of it to parties. I was even more proud that I got it for under $100. Our Textiles Lab pros praise the brand’s quality, especially at the price. Cashmere, on sale? Count me in.

Levi’s​

I’ve only recently discovered Levi’s tops and dresses—and I fear I’ve missed out for so long! Luckily, a lot of its clothing is on sale now, including festive sweaters for the holidays. To get the discount, just make sure you sign up for its free Red Tab rewards program. And of course, tons of denim styles are on sale too, including the classic 501s that our pros love.

Amazon​

Hey, don’t skip Amazon when it comes to fashion finds. Our editors have successfully discovered affordable, trendy pieces. “I love the gold-colored buttons that help gold jewelry pop,” says Senior Editor Amanda Fama about this polished buttoned cardigan. We also love this faux leather moto jacket that acts as the perfect layer for casual and dressier occasions.

Aritzia​

Aritzia can be pricey, but the brand’s designs never miss. The shorter version of our experts’ favorite winter coat offers the same warmth, just up top. And beyond being stylish, the cropped cut makes it easier to move. Also, this merino wool cable knit sweater feels classic yet fresh every season. It also comes in taupe and black, which are also on sale.

Aritzia The SuperPuff XtraShorty

Aritzia Heritage Knit Merino Wool Crew Sweater

Ugg

Love ‘em or not, Uggs are here to stay. They’re in demand this time every year, and the brand comes out with new styles that tweens and adults love. Grab select styles on sale, like the Classic Micro and Tasman II. I’m actually shocked about the Tasman markdown because this is rare (I was watching them like a hawk last holiday, waiting for a discount).

Ugg Classic Micro

Ugg Tasman II

J. Crew​

Preppy style may be having a moment, but J.Crew has been doing it for years. I’m a little obsessed with this scarf sweater and plaid pajamas with bows. Also, if you have the J.Crew app, you can get 20% off full-price styles for a limited time.

J.Crew Blouson-Sleeve Scarf Sweater

J.Crew Flannel Pajama Set with Bows

Madewell​

The deals at Madewell are too good, we couldn’t gatekeep. Sweaters, tops, jeans, and trousers are all on sale at up to 60% off, along with some fun accessories. And on top of these markdowns, Madewell is offering an extra 40% off—just use code “LETSGO” at checkout.

Madewell The Perfect Vintage Wide-Leg Jean

Carhartt​

I love Carhartt outerwear, and Amazon has a bunch of the brand’s popular pieces on sale. This vest is something I’d wear even if it’s from the men’s section, and I love that this women’s stretchy fleece will keep you warm and move with you.

Tons of cute and cozy pajama sets are on sale at Abercrombie right now, and they would undoubtedly make great gifts (or maybe I’m projecting because I want some for myself). Lots of denim are also seeing discounts, like these linen-blend high-rise jeans. Bonus: Some items get an extra markdown at checkout.
