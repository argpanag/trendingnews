Video: UFC 331 full card final faceoffs with Tsarukyan vs. Ruffy, Steveson, more

TheTools · 19.09.2026 04:29 · source · permalink

LOS ANGELES – UFC 331 ceremonial weigh-ins are in the books, and the fighters came face-to-face one final time before the organization's eighth numbered event of the year.

The weigh-ins took place at Crypto.com Arena, which hosts the promotion's first visit to the venue in "The City of Angeles" since August 2018 on Saturday (Paramount+).

UFC 331 is built around a flyweight title rematch between Joshua Van (17-2 MMA, 10-1 UFC) and Alexandre Pantoja (30-6 MMA, 14-4 UFC). Additionally, lightweight standout Arman Tsarukyan (23-3 MMA, 10-2 UFC) clashes with Mauricio Ruffy (14-2 MMA, 5-1 UFC) in a five-round matchup that could decide the next title challenger.

Outside of the that, Olympic gold medalist Gable Steveson (4-0 MMA, 1-0 UFC) looks to continue to make waves at heavyweight, plus veterans such as Patricio Freire (37-9 MMA, 1-2 UFC), Marlon Vera (21-12-1 MMA, 15-11 UFC) and Tai Tuivasa (14-10 MMA, 8-10 UFC) will be in action.

All 12 scheduled matchups for UFC 331 had staredowns on the eve of the event Friday, and you can see the complete video highlight above.
