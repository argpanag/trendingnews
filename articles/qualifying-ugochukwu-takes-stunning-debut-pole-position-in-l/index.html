QUALIFYING: Ugochukwu takes stunning debut pole position in Lusail

TheTools · 27.11.2026 18:03 · source · permalink

Ugo Ugochukwu takes pole position on debut ahead of Gabriele Minì and Alexander Dunne in Lusail

Ugo Ugochukwu put in a sublime performance on his debut weekend to take the Aramco Pole Position Award in an entertaining Lusail Qualifying session.

The American’s 1:35.818 gave him and Van Amersfoort Racing their first Formula 2 pole, with Gabriele Minì in second ahead of Alexander Dunne.

After the first set of laps it was Invicta Racing’s debutant Freddie Slater who set the pace with a 1:37.162, leading Noel León by 0.231s.

On the next set of laps, Minì topped the timesheets, with Oliver Goethe making it an MP 1-2.

But the teammates were beaten by León and then Slater, who went nearly half a second clear of the field on a 1:36.446.

Slater’s Formula 3 title rival Ugochukwu reduced his lead to 0.167s on his next attempt, moving up to second for Van Amersfoort Racing.

The drivers returned to the pitlane with just less than half of the session to go, fitting on a new set of the Soft tyres.

With just over eight minutes left in the session, the drivers returned to the track. But the Red Flags were out as Sebastián Montoya stopped on track with his right rear tyre having come off the car.

Qualifying resumed with five minutes and 37 seconds to go, and Minì went to provisional pole on a 1:36.862, just 0.157s ahead of his teammate Goethe.

Slater, having gone fastest in the middle sector spun at Turn 13, while Ugochukwu crossed the line to go to P1 by 0.044s, with Dunne moving up to P3.

Rafael Villagómez then came to a stop on track, and the Red Flags were waving, which meant there was no time for further improvements and the session came to an end.

This meant Villagómez’s VAR teammate Ugochukwu takes a superb debut pole, with Minì second ahead of Dunne, Goethe, and León.

John Bennett was sixth ahead of Tsolov, Joshua Duerksen and Slater, as Martinius Stenshorne rounded out the top 10.

That sets up the Lusail Sprint Race, which is set to be the 250th Formula 2 race. Lights out is scheduled for 19:15 local time.
