Haaland's scoring streak against Napoli ends at three games

TheTools · 25.11.2026 01:46 · source · permalink

autty

Published 2026-11-25 09:46:15

In November 24, Manchester City hosted Napoli at the Etihad Stadium in their UEFA Champions League Matchday 5 fixture. Haaland started and wore the captain's armband, but he managed only one shot and did not score. His run of scoring in three consecutive games against Napoli came to an end.

Both teams created chances in the first half but could not break the deadlock. After the break, Højlund scored with a low shot from the left side of the box in the 56th minute. Ayyoub Bouaddi equalized with a tap-in in the 67th minute, and Lang put Napoli ahead again with a volley in the 76th minute. Cherki rescued a point for City in the 86th minute with a powerful shot from a tight angle, and the match ended 2-2.

Haaland was not involved in either of City's goals. The Norwegian striker touched the ball only 14 times, and several of his key statistics were zero. In his previous three matches against Napoli, he had scored every time, netting four goals in total.
