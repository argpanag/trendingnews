Harrowing new details about Hayden Panettiere’s final moments revealed by boyfriend’s brother

TheTools · 25.11.2026 21:37 · source · permalink
  • Entertainment
  • Celebrity
  • Showbiz

November 25, 2026 - 9.37pm

Zach Hickerson has opened up about Hayden Panettiere’s final moments before her tragic death at age 36. 

The 39-year-old, who is the brother of Panettiere’s on-and-off-again boyfriend Brian Hickerson, was with the Heroes star when she died and shared his side of what happened. 

New details about Hayden Panettiere’s final moments have been revealed by her boyfriend’s brother, Zach Hickerson.  AP Photo/Matt Sayles

“I was not even supposed to be in Greenville that Sunday. I was supposed to go down to Atlanta to play golf,” Zach said in a new interview with TMZ. 

“Upon learning that Hayden was like, ‘Absolutely not.’”

Zach arrived at the Airbnb in South Carolina where Panettiere was with his brother Brian, and at first didn’t notice anything unusual. 

He noted that she was sitting cross-legged, but didn’t think anything of it.

“Her hair was longer, so it was covering. I just assumed that she would pop up and be like, ‘Zachary, what are we doing today?’”

But he soon realised that something was wrong when the Nashville star didn’t wake up. 

Brian tried to wake her up but according to Zach, she didn’t move or make any noise at all in response. 

“Brian … pulled her onto his chest, and when he did, I had a clear view of her face, [which] was pale, lips were purple. I immediately knew something was incredibly wrong,” he recalled. 

Panettiere was with her on-and-off-again boyfriend Brian Hickerson (pictured) and his brother Zach when she died. Getty

Zach was reportedly the one who called 911 for help, and he told the outlet they did CPR on the actress until the emergency responders got there, although he’s got no idea about how long that went on for.

He said he prayed and cried while emergency services worked on Panettiere, however their attempts to revive her were unsuccessful and she died at the scene on August 16. 

“I remember him [Brian] being in more of a distraught, disbelief, kind of, shock the whole time,” Zach claimed. 

“I don’t know what was going through his mind or what his reaction is, but to me he looked just in disbelief.” 

The brothers called 911 and started CPR on the actress until emergency services got there.  Getty

Brian and Panettiere were first linked in 2018 and had a tumultuous, on-and-off relationship that included multiple alleged incidents of domestic violence.

The pair had reconciled again just months before her death, and were spotted together at the Los Angeles International Airport in March.

Panettiere’s official cause of death was ruled as “toxic effects of fentanyl, 4-ANPP, Alprazolam, Methocarbamol, and Quetiapine” and an accident.

The actress had spent time in rehab in the weeks before her death, according to her dad.

Skip Panettiere confirmed his daughter “had recently been released [from] rehab” in “Malibu”. He said she spent “two or three weeks” at the treatment facility, according to a report from the Greenville County Coroner’s Office released in September.

Panettiere, pictured with her daughter Kaya, had been in rehab in the weeks before her tragic death.  Twitter

Skip said his daughter had “struggled with addiction since her early 20s and [had] been in multiple rehab facilities since that time”.

He also noted that Panettiere had “suffered from postpartum depression after the birth of her daughter,” Kaya, 11, and said her “addiction issues … escalated since then”.

The actress was farewelled at an emotional celebration of life service on September 19, during an intimate service in Malibu. 

The ceremony was attended by several high-profile guests including Panettiere’s former Nashville costar Connie Britton, Evan Ross and Ruby Rose.
