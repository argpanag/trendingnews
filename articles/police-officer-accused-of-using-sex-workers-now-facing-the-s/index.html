Police officer accused of using sex workers now facing the sack

TheTools · 07.09.2026 14:36 · source · permalink

A police officer is accused of making a string of approaches to sex workers before paying them for sex over a nine-day period.

PC Oliver Hawley is set to go before a misconduct hearing for South Yorkshire Police on September 10. The police officer is alleged to have contacted a sex worker in order to make arrangements to meet on October 1 and then used the services of a sex worker on the same day.

He is again accused of contacting a sex worker on on October 9, although he did not use the services on this day. He is then accused of contacting a sex worker twice more on October 10 and then used the services on the same day.

The police said this constitutes a break of the force's standards, and would be so serious as to justify dismissal.

The full list of accusations are as follows:

  • On 1st October 2025 the officer contacted a sex worker in order to make arrangements to meet and have sexual relations in exchange for payment.
  • On 1st October 2025 the officer used the services of a sex worker in exchange for payment.
  • On 9th October 2025 the officer contacted a sex worker in order to make arrangements to meet and have sexual relations in exchange for payment.
  • On 10th October 2025 the officer contacted a sex worker in order to make arrangements to meet and have sexual relations in exchange for payment.
  • On 10th October 2025 the officer used the services of a sex worker in exchange for payment.

South Yorkshire Police said: "This is a breach of the Standards of Professional Behaviour in respect of Discreditable Conduct and Authority, Respect and Courtesy.

"The matters set out above are breaches of the Standards which are so serious as to justify dismissal and therefore constitute gross misconduct."
