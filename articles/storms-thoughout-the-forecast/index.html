Storms thoughout the forecast

TheTools · 10.09.2026 11:53 · source · permalink

BOWLING GREEN, Ky. (WBKO) - Heading throughout the morning, we having lingering showers east of I-65. We’ll clear up briefly towards the middle of the day, and we’ll start to see a bit of sunshine to heat us up into the mid-to-upper 80s. We’ll see another round of showers and storms engage for tonight as a cold front moves through.

Overnight, we could see stronger storms with risks of gusty winds and excessive rainfall. We have a Marginal Risk for severe storms from the Storm Prediction Center for our entire viewing area, lasting until 7am Friday.

The setup for these storms is resulting in many rounds of heavy rain, also known as ‘training thunderstorms’. This can lead to ponding on the roads and localized areas of flooding, so be cautious when driving, especially at night.

We’ll see lingering moisture in the region throughout Friday and Saturday with isolated showers and storms popping up throughout the days. Next week will dry up in terms of rain but certainly not in humidity, and hot conditions will return with temperatures back into the 90s.
