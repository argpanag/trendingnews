James Bond front-runner quits film company in major hint he's set for 007 role

TheTools · 31.10.2026 21:42 · source · permalink

JAMES Bond front-runner Callum Turner has stepped down from his film company as he remains in the race to become 007.

The actor, 36, has resigned as a director of Twentytwo Films Ltd that he founded with his pop star wife Dua Lipa last August.

It comes as screen testing for Bond is understood to have taken place in Berkshire last week.

A source said: “Callum is making all the right moves to become the next James Bond.

“The role is all-consuming and commitment to it is a top priority of the producers.”

Daniel Craig, who left after his fifth Bond film No Time To Die in 2021, said he did not have the “headspace” to take on other roles while playing the secret agent.

Bond bosses are also famously protective of the brand.

It is rumoured that 007 actors are contractually restricted from wearing a tuxedo in non-Bond films.

Callum is a Louis Vuitton ambassador and wore its white Bond-style tux to a Frankenstein premiere last year, though he was not cast in that movie.

Dua, tipped to record the next 007 theme song, has now appointed lawyer David Farquharson as a director of Twentytwo Films Ltd.

The company’s nature is listed as “motion picture and television programme production activities.”

Slow Horses star Jack Lowden is the bookies’ favourite to take over as Bond, followed by Callum, and The Crown’s Josh O’Connor.
