Sarah Ferguson's next move exposed as former duchess set for return

TheTools · 30.10.2026 10:12 · source · permalink

Sarah Ferguson will "certainly have a try" at making a comeback.

Sarah Ferguson will attempt to make a comeback despite denials the disgraced former duchess is writing a book, a royal author says. Phil Dampier said Fergie will "no doubt" attempt "some sort of comeback".

He told Hello! magazine only Ms Ferguson knows exactly what that will look like, adding: "But she is the comeback queen. Who knows whether, at 67, she's capable of doing it or if it's too late. But she will certainly have a try."

Mr Dampier's comments came as Fergie made a shock return by appearing at a service celebrating the life of her former lover and confidant, Paddy McNally, on Thursday (October 29).

Ms Ferguson was joined by her daughters, Princess Beatrice and Princess Eugenie, along with their husbands, at the event at St Paul's Church in Knightsbridge.

Pictures showed Fergie smiling slightly as she arrived for the service, flanked by her immediate family.

The former duchess did not attend Mr McNally's funeral and before the service in central London she had only been spotted in public once in 15 months when she was found at a spa in the Austrian Alps.

Prior to that she appeared at the Duchess of Kent's funeral at Westminster Cathedral in September 2025, appearing alongside Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor.

Weeks later Andrew's former wife was embroiled in scandal when emails emerged showing she maintained contact with Jeffrey Epstein even after he was convicted of procuring a minor for prostitution in 2008.

In one email from 2011, Ms Ferguson described Epstein as a "supreme friend". She also called him the brother she had always wished for in another piece of correspondence.

Files released by the US Department of Justice suggested Ms Ferguson visited Epstein with Beatrice and Eugenie days after he was released from prison.

Both Ms Ferguson and Mr Mountbatten-Windsor lost their titles and honours when revelations about their links to Epstein emerged. They were also evicted from their Royal Lodge home. A number of charities also dropped them as patrons.

Reports emerged in September that Ms Ferguson was days away from signing a book deal. But a spokesperson for the former duchess later told The Independent she has "no plans to write any tell all memoir".

Mr Mountbatten-Windsor now lives at Marsh Farm on King Charles's private Sandringham Estate. Ms Ferguson is believed to be living somewhere in the Home Counties after reportedly returning to the UK in August.

She lives in a "gated" property with "six bedrooms" and a "granny annexe", according to The Sun newspaper.

The former Duke of York is currently under police investigation over alleged misconduct in public office. He denies any wrongdoing in relation to that allegation and in relation to Epstein.
