Neville wants four Man United transfers cancelled and says players 'have to go'

TheTools · 18.11.2026 13:27 · source · permalink

Gary Neville has delivered a blunt assessment(Image: United Stand / YouTube)

Gary Neville does not want to see Weston McKennie, Calvin Bassey, Antonee Robinson or Folarin Balogun arriving at Manchester United during the January transfer window, insisting "they’re nowhere near" the level the club should be targeting. The Old Trafford legend has also urged Mauricio Pochettino to get rid of the likes of Matthijs de Ligt, Lisandro Martinez and Noussair Mazraoui this winter.

It's a dire state of affairs at Old Trafford at present, with United languishing in 18th in the Premier League standings after 10 games played. Michael Carrick's ability to win just one of those matches ultimately cost him his job, with Pochettino quickly named as his successor.

The Argentine arrived in the UK on Wednesday morning and is now at Carrington, as he prepares for a busy first few days before his first match in charge. Pochettino has just three days to prepare his inherited first-team before United travel to Anfield to play fierce rivals in Liverpool.

That match will give the 54-year-old a perfect insight into the work that needs to be done when the transfer market re-opens at the turn of the year, with a number of players linked with a move to Old Trafford. Speaking on the most recent instalment of the United Stand, however, Neville was unimpressed by some of the transfer rumours presented to him by Mark Goldbridge and urged United to cancel any potential transfer plans now.

When Goldbridge told him that a US-heavy trio of McKennie, Robinson and Balogun, as well as Fulham's Bassey had been brought up in conversation, Neville replied: "Is that serious? Not a chance. They’re nowhere near. No chance. I’m not saying all of them but no."

Elsewhere, Neville also claimed that the former United States national team manager needs to be fierce in moving on a number of his current crop of players, citing how he has not seen enough from them since their respective arrivals. Speaking about De Ligt, who has only just returned as a result of a back injury from this time last year, Neville said: "He’s got one big problem, he doesn’t play football.

"If a player is injury prone and doesn’t play football...Just because he hasn’t played doesn’t mean to say that he should go above a player who has played every single week and has thrown his hand up. You want someone who’s actually going to be reliable and play.

"De Ligt has to go. They’ve got lots of problems, but De Light, Martinez, Mazraoui."

Elsewhere, Neville believes that Pochettino will look to bring in a new goalkeeper to challenge Senne Lammens for his place in the team, given how threadbare the Reds look behind the Belgian shot-stopper.

He added: "I think he’ll want to bring a new goalkeeper in. He’ll want someone to compete with Lammens.

"I’m not saying he changes Lammens, I think it’s too early. I think we saw enough last season to carry on with him, hopefully build his confidence back up. I like his composure.

"He did a great interview a couple of weeks ago in the midst of this Michael situation. I thought, ‘Do you know something? I could listen to you.

"At least you stand up a little bit, at least you say something, at least you’ve got something about you. You speak like somebody who should wear that badge.'

“So, I think, Lammens is not someone I would change, but I definitely would have to bring someone in to make sure that there is a contingency. So, he needs a goalkeeper.”
