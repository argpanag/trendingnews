Miami Heat (3-6, ninth in the Eastern Conference) vs. Sacramento Kings (1-7, 14th in the Western Conference)

Sacramento, California; Sunday, 9 p.m. EST

BOTTOM LINE: Sacramento plays Miami looking to stop its three-game home skid.

Sacramento went 22-60 overall a season ago while going 15-26 at home. The Kings averaged 16.4 points off of turnovers, 14.8 second-chance points and 37.9 bench points last season.

Miami finished 43-39 overall, 27-25 in Eastern Conference play and 17-24 on the road last season. The Heat averaged 120.9 points per game last season, 54.7 in the paint, 19.6 off of turnovers and 18.4 on fast breaks.

INJURIES: Kings: Ben Simmons: out for season (achilles), Keegan Murray: out (ankle), De'Andre Hunter: out (illness), Maxime Raynaud: out (knee).

Heat: None listed.
