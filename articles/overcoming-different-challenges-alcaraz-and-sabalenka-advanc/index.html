Overcoming Different Challenges, Alcaraz and Sabalenka Advance to the Third Round

TheTools · 03.09.2026 05:31 · source · permalink

Through matches in different situations, Carlos Alcaraz and Aryna Sabalenka won their second-round matches. They are on a mission to defend their US Open titles.

03 Sep 2026 12:31 WIB · Olahraga

By Yulia Sapthiani

NEW YORK, WEDNESDAY — Carlos Alcaraz and Aryna Sabalenka are still on track to defend their titles at the US Open Grand Slam. With circumstances presenting challenges for each of them, they advanced to the third round.

At the Arthur Ashe Stadium, the largest stadium at the Billie Jean King National Tennis Center, Flushing Meadows, New York, USA, on Wednesday (2/9/2026) during the evening session, Alcaraz lost the first set against Jaime Faria but responded with a solid performance in the next three sets. Alcaraz won with a score of 4-6, 6-0, 6-3, 6-2 after competing for two hours and 40 minutes to face Wu Yi Bing in the third round.

Alcaraz is on a mission to become the men's singles champion of the US Open for the third time after 2022 and 2025. He is still on track for that despite the tournament having only completed two rounds.

The same mission, in fact, has also been in the hands of Sabalenka for three consecutive years in the women's singles competition. The world number one player advanced to the third round through an easier match, defeating the Russian qualifier, Polina Latcenko, 6-1, 6-1 in just 56 minutes.

Winning 16 consecutive matches at Flushing Meadows, Sabalenka stated that she feels stronger, both physically and mentally, compared to the middle of the season. "My level of play is good. I know we cannot be perfect, but I strive to give my all," commented Sabalenka, who will face Kamilla Rakhimova in the third round.

Sabalenka stated that her mental state has improved after experiencing a series of poor results in her performance standards. After winning the WTA 1000 Indian Wells and Miami consecutively in March-April, her best result was reaching the quarter-finals of the French Open in June.

Sabalenka lost without winning a game in the third set when she was eliminated by Diana Shnaider at the French Open with a score of 6-3, 5-7, 0-6. She expressed frustration and stated that she would take a break from tennis for a while.

However, the desire to take a break from competition cannot be realized due to a packed schedule. A month after the French Open, the next Grand Slam awaits, which is Wimbledon. Instead of achieving better results, he was eliminated in the fourth round.

In the US Open warm-up tournament, the results were not any better. At the WTA 1000 events in Toronto and Cincinnati, she was eliminated in the fourth round, and not by top-ranked players. In Toronto, Sabalenka lost to Ekaterina Alexandrova, while Sara Bejlek defeated her in Cincinnati. The player, who often displays emotional outbursts that disrupt her game, is trying to control her emotions in her appearance at Flushing Meadows.

Alcaraz faces another challenge as he has just returned to the tournament after a four-month absence due to a right wrist injury. This injury caused him to miss the French Open and Wimbledon.

After going through two sets, the Spanish tennis player, who has seven Grand Slam titles, stated that he is in good condition and does not feel any pain in his arm. Regarding the loss of the first set against Faria, he mentioned that he was too hasty in making decisions at several moments.

"I actually played well, but made a mistake on one forehand that should have been easy to execute. I was impatient, and that became a disadvantage as my serve was broken by my opponent. It is very difficult to break back his serve because Jaime has a serve that reaches speeds of 225 km/h," said Alcaraz.

Alcaraz then tried to remain calm and focus more on the next set. The second set was won with a "bagel," a term used to describe when a tennis player wins 6-0.

The sole women's finalist of the 2024 US Open, Jessica Pegula, advanced to the third round after defeating Sofia Kenin 6-3, 6-1 and will face Leylah Fernandez in the next match.

Wimbledon champions and finalists, Linda Noskova and Karolina Muchova, also won in two sets in the second round. The same goes for Marta Kostyuk.

In the men's singles category, those advancing to the third round include Daniil Medvedev as well as the home favorites, Ben Shelton and Frances Tiafoe.

The women's doubles competition is colored by the return of the legendary duo, Serena and Venus Williams. In the draw, the sister duo will face Hao Ching Chan/Maya Joint, who are seeded 14th, in the first round. This match will mark the first appearance of Serena/Venus in a Grand Slam arena since the 2022 US Open.

The duo with 14 Grand Slam titles was initially set to play at Wimbledon after Serena announced her return to professional competition. Serena, who stepped away from competition in 2024, had been preparing to appear alongside Venus by playing women's doubles in the Wimbledon warm-up tournaments, namely at WTA London (partnering with Victoria Mboko) and WTA Berlin (Muchova).

At Wimbledon, Serena began her appearance in the singles event but lost in the first round to Joint in a three-set match. However, she subsequently withdrew from the women's doubles due to a right ankle injury sustained after twisting it in the match against Joint.

From her experience at Wimbledon, Serena decided not to compete in the singles event at the US Open even though she could receive a wildcard from the tournament organizers. "I do not want to take the opportunity away from other players who could receive a wildcard when I am not ready to compete," she told ESPN.

Also competing in the women's doubles event, which starts on Thursday, are Indonesian athletes Aldila Sutjiadi and Janice Tjen. Aldila is paired with Erin Routliffe (New Zealand) and is seeded ninth, while Janice is paired with Fernandez. In the singles event, Janice lost to the fifth seed, Mirra Andreeva, in the first round.
