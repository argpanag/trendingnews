NC internal differences an exercise of democracy, no rebellion in party: Showkat Mir

TheTools · 31.10.2026 06:45 · source · permalink

 Srinagar, Oct 31 (KNS): National Conference (NC) Provisional President Showkat Mir on Saturday dismissed reports of rebellion and factionalism within the party, saying a meeting of party MLAs held recently was an internal exercise in democracy and there was no confrontation with the leadership.
