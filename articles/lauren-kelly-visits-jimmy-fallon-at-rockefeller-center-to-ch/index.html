Fall season brings more sketches, celebs and surprises to The Tonight Show with Jimmy Fallon

HOUSTON – The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon is in full swing for a brand new season -- with more sketches, more celebs, and more surprises planned for the fall.

Jimmy invited Houston Life’s Lauren Kelly to his studio in Rockefeller Center to sit in the same seat that has been occupied by some of the biggest stars in the world.

Watch the full interview to see Lauren and Jimmy chat about what he has in store for upcoming episodes, his experience years ago at Rodeo Houston, and why Lauren’s high school yearbook with Beyoncé’s picture in it might have inspired a new segment for his show.
