Tom shares his memories of cinema legends

TheTools · 19.10.2026 23:28 · source · permalink

Tuesday, October 20 2026, 04:58 AM

Oct 19, 2026 05:00 PM | UPDATED: Oct 18, 2026 11:42 PM | 6 min read

As the 1986 American sports drama The Color of Money completed 4 decades since its release on Saturday, Tom Cruise, who plays Vincent Lauria, a young, highly talented and cocky pool player, in the drama, penned a heartfelt note, sharing his experience of his first meeting with director Martin Scorsese and lead Paul Newman. 

He revealed that as part of his preparation, he watched both of their films in sequence.

Cruise wrote on his official handle, “40 years since making The Color of Money. I had the privilege as a young artist, to have a general meeting with Paul Newman and separately with Martin Scorsese years before making this film, where they shared some of their wisdom on cinema. Of course, I’d seen their films, but prior to meeting them for the first time, I set up a personal study course where I would watch their films in sequence from the beginning of their careers, up until that point in time. (sic).”

Cruise added that once he was cast in The Color of Money, he once again studied all of their films in sequence and later got a first hand experience of seeing them in action during the shoot.

Expressing his gratitude for getting a chance to work with Newman and Scorsese, Cruise penned, “I will always be grateful for this opportunity and for the generosity that Newman and Scorsese showed me with their knowledge of cinema and insight throughout the entire process.”
