GOAL! Manchester United 3-2 Roma (Cunha, 86)

RUDDY HELL!

85 mins: Castro and Maguire are down inside the Roma half after a coming together.

84 mins: I think it is far to say this has been a game of two halves. Roma were dominant in the first, United in the second. Will we get a dramatic end?

GOAL! Manchester United 2-2 Roma (Cunha, 83)

Cunha profits from a dreadful defensive mistake from Balerdi to score from close range.

82 mins: Lacey is looking to take Wesley on whenever he gets the ball.

81 mins: United offer some promise going forward but it all falls down on the edge of the box.

79 mins: Castro battles with Maguire and catches an arm in the face, which the referee deems is worthy of a foul and booking for the United defender.

78 mins: Malen off, Castro on for Roma.

77 mins: United look a threat from set-pieces, so maybe should try and work a way of winning more corners.

In the meantime, Cunha forces the goalkeeper into a decent save with a shot from distance. Lacey picks up the loose ball and defeats Wesley, getting a cross in, only to be taken down by the wing-back. United want a penalty but the officials are having none of it.

75 mins: Martinez and Lacey are coming on. The Argentinian gets a mixed reception as he replaces Heaven, while the academy graduate is cheered as Diallo goes off.

Roma take off Mancini and Hermoso, Ndicka and Ghilardi on.

74 mins: The game has become a little bit scrappy in the past two minutes. Carrick has readied Martinez …

72 mins: Everything was going so well, and then Dalot falls over to give away a throw.

71 mins: Cunha wins the ball on the right and drives at Balerdi who takes it off his toes.

70 mins: There’s something akin to a zip about United at the moment.

That is briefly halted by Heaven being penalised for a foul and then booked.

68 mins: Another corner! Mbeumo lifts it to the back post where Maguire is battling to get on the end of it, having got the better of Balerdi, but he can’t get his effort on target.

67 mins: Mbeumo swings in a second corner from the right, which is headed back across goal for Dalot to nod towards the net, only to it cleared off the line.

66 mins: United have a corner on the left for Fernandes to whip in. It reaches Maguire at the back post but his header is blocked by Wesley.

One key issue for United is a lack of options on the bench. Carrick could bring on Lacey, Mount or Zirkzee if he wants an attacking option but it’s not the most exciting thought.

65 mins: United are moving at greater speed, going from one end of the pitch to the other. It catches Roma out and gives Dalot a chance but he hits the side netting.

United go back at Roma and Fernandes finds Diallo in the box, he cuts in but his shot is blocked. United are on top here.

63 mins: Obviously, we should also remember the wider Champions League context because a loss here would mean it is three defeats in four for United in the competition.

61 mins: United need to build on this because they have half an hour to save the manager’s job, I would argue. The fans are doing their bit.

59 mins: Cunha sends the ball through for Mbeumo who reaches it first and takes aim. His shot defeats the goalkeeper but comes back after hitting both posts. Old Trafford is rocking now.

GOAL! Manchester United 1-2 Roma (Fernandes, pen, 58)

Like a bit of drama, do you? Fernandes scores at the second time of asking.

PENALTY RETAKE!

The Roma players are adjudged to have encroached!

Penalty saved!

Fernandes sends the penalty to the goalkeeper’s right and Vasquez gets down to save. The rebound is then cleared.

55 mins: PENALTY TO UNITED! Cunha is bundled over in the box by Mancini. The Brazilian then demands some passion from the crowd.

53 mins: Cunha receives a pass over the top from Maguire and thinks he’s won a corner but it is a goal-kick. Nothing is going for United but you earn your own luck in this game.

52 mins: James writes: Loving the minute by minute. United fans can blame Jason Wilcox, Omar Berrada and the Jim Reaper all they like, but ALL of this mess can be traced back to Alex Ferguson and that horse—Rock of Gibraltar. The man simultaneously built and then broke the club with that disagreement over money. It let the Glazers in, sent some good businessmen packing and now look where they are. For all the clowns that are now running the circus, it was the original ringmaster who this can all be traced back to.

“Ego is the enemy folks.”

51 mins: Baleba removes his shirt and gives it to a young fan before walking down the tunnel. That’s the only decent moment for the supporters thus far.

Tielemans finally gets on after a long wait.

Burt Bosma writes: “Every United player seems to have forgotten how to play. No one moves until the pass is made, no one is anticipating, no one is running into spaces or just trying to create space by just moving. A bunch of rabbits caught in the headlights waiting to be hit by the truck.”

49 mins: Old Trafford is silent except for the Roma fans.

47 mins: Baleba produces a stunning moment for United and gets through the Roma defence. He looks set to cross for Mbeumo to knock into the net, only to pull up with a hamstring injury. Tielemans is prepared. Baleba looks upset.

In even worse news for United, Dalot is still on the pitch.

Pisilli replaces Mora for Roma but United have stuck with the same XI.

Second half

Can it get any worse for United?

David Johnson quips: “I’m a City fan, and I should be rejoicing, but for the first time in my life I feel sorry for fans of the Forces of Darkness. Keep this up and we’ll be having a Manchester derby in the Northern Premier.”

Surely, Jason Wilcox cannot survive this utter incompetence.

I am not sure why anyone would want to replace Carrick. Not much going on here.

Jason emails: “The biggest problem is not the ownership. It doesn’t help, but it’s not the main issue. It’s the team building strategy. They spend too much money on too many replacement-level players (offered to them by agents happy to get a payday), and have forgotten that the passion for playing for United comes from a blend of established players and the youth program.

“Look at the lineup today. Put it in Coventry’s shirts. You wouldn’t be surprised at the results.

“How many players on this team would have gotten games for Man U during the 1998-99 season? Bruno, the “player of the year,” would have been the 6th best midfielder, at best. No forward would have played in front of those four great strikers. And the D? No chance.

“There is Man United’s problem.”

Half-time reading.

BOOOOOOOOOO! Comes the sound from the stands. Wilcox and Berrada seek some warmth.

Half time: Manchester United 0-2 Roma

Another lamentable performance from United who have been outclassed by Roma, aiding the Italians with some utterly pathetic defending. Will Carrick be allowed to limp on until Sunday at this rate?

45 mins: Malen latches onto a flick on and then thwacks a shot across Lammens who gets down well.

Two minutes added on.

44 mins: Fernandes swipes over from 18 yards with his left foot and then complains about the pass not being good enough. He seems frustrated by how bad things are.

43 mins: Eddie Munro writes: “There seem to be many reasons for why United are doing so badly but some I don’t see mentioned enough are that compared to last season, there’s a big Casemiro shaped hole in the midfield (both physically and metaphysically) which means poorer build up play and no protection for the defence and no Sesko to come on as a super-sub to turn draws into wins and defeats into draws (and hide bad performances).

“Also members of this squad have shown repeatedly in the past that they cannot handle two games a week, either mentally of physically (which is funny considering they were complaining in the 2nd half of last season that they didn’t have enough games to stay fit).

“Obviously Carrick is trying to change the style of play as well (from mostly counter-attacks to mostly possession) which is what did for Ole. Sack and sell everyone except Carrick and any player under 24 (but sell Zirkzee cos it’s not working) any other solution is just more of the same cycle.”

42 mins: Malen is booked for pulling back Baleba around 28 yards out. Maybe United should just shoot. Fernandes lines it up … and hits the wall. Everyone go for a half-time pint.

41 mins: United have enjoyed slightly more possession, so there is that.

Rob Hopkins emails “Surely Amass isn’t as bad as this, the reluctance to trust a youth player who’s trained and played that position his whole life is worth a shot. Sure, he might make a mistake, but that would be 4 less than Dalot has made in 30 mins.”

You’d hope not. They were open to selling him in the summer despite the fact they had no other options.

40 mins: Almost a third for Roma who easily play through United and the ball is set up for Mora to finish but Yoro saves the day.

Krishnamoorthy writes: “Now I realise that Jose has gone to Real and am kicking myself for my earlier mail of Jose driving the nail

“Whoever it is , the problem at United today is not going to be fixed by resting their captain, dropping their goal keeper , or removing their manager - the united problem needs a complete overhaul - and you need a man of SAF’s calibre to build something new from scratch.”

38 mins: United are working away around the edge of the Roma box but do not look like crating anything. They have yet to test Roma’s third-choice ‘keeper.

“I really thought with City’s existential crisis that United would revel in former glory but also lay low in terms of drama, instead it’s as if they’ve gone - hold my beer …” says Nicola Schofield. “I can see if City get a points deduction which team they’ll potentially save from relegation!! (May 2027 final plot twist).”

36 mins: Lulli is booked for a very blatant handball. He seemed genuinely worried if Dalot got beyond him in possession of the ball, he could do some damage. How naive.

“I hope we’ll get Conte soon. If not, I doubt we’ll win anything in the Championship next year as well.”

34 mins: Mbeumo gets the wrong side of the Roma defence but is naturally caught and tackled before any danger comes of it. United are just going through the motions now.

32 mins: To be fair, Cunha is putting in the hard yards here but the chances of it making a difference are slim.

“I’d be less inclined to blame the 20 year old Ayden Heaven,” writes Andy Donald. “I’d be looking squarely at Dalot for that - he’s been exposing his fellow defenders for many years now. 30 mins: If Malen can show United something, it is that underperforming Dutch strikers can come good. Surely Zirkzee can’t be any worse than this anonymous effort in the final third.

28 mins: Diallo drives into the box and goes down but it is fine tackle from Wesley, even if the United fans think it merits a penalty.

Jason Wilcox, why didn’t you sign a backup left-back?
