Pokémon Night Out Twitch Drop: Free Timed Research and Avatar Jersey | Pokémon GO Hub

TheTools · 02.10.2026 14:03 · source · permalink

Watch 30 minutes of the Pokémon Night Out livestream to receive exclusive Pokémon GO Timed Research and a special avatar item.

Pokémon GO Trainers around the world can earn an exclusive Pokémon Night Out Twitch Drop during the upcoming Pokémon Night Out livestream on Saturday, October 24, 2026.

Watch at least 30 minutes of the official Pokémon Twitch livestream to receive a code for exclusive Timed Research in Pokémon GO. Completing the research will reward an exclusive Pokémon Night Out baseball jersey for your avatar.

Pokémon Night Out Twitch Drop

The Pokémon Night Out livestream begins on Saturday, October 24, 2026, at 7:30 p.m. PDT.

To qualify for the Pokémon GO Twitch Drop, Trainers must watch the livestream on the official Pokémon Twitch channel for at least 30 minutes.

The Los Angeles Pokémon Night Out show will also be streamed on YouTube, but Trainers looking to obtain the Pokémon GO reward will need to participate through Twitch and have their accounts properly connected.

Timed Research Reward

The Twitch Drop provides a code for exclusive Pokémon GO Timed Research.

Completing the research will reward:

  • Icon not found Exclusive Pokémon Night Out baseball jersey avatar item

The full Timed Research tasks and any additional rewards have not yet been announced.

How to Get the Pokémon Night Out Twitch Drop

  1. Follow the official Pokémon Twitch channel.
  2. Connect your Pokémon Trainer Central account to Twitch.
  3. Watch at least 30 minutes of the Pokémon Night Out livestream on October 24.
  4. Claim the Twitch Drop. A prompt should appear in Twitch chat once you have watched for long enough. You can also claim it through your Twitch Drops Inventory.
  5. Visit Pokémon Trainer Central, open your inventory, and select Show Code for the reward.
  6. Redeem the code through Pokémon GO’s official Offer Redemption page.

Important: Minimizing the Twitch stream will pause your Twitch Drop progress timer, so make sure the stream remains active while completing the required 30 minutes.

Each Trainer can claim the Timed Research code only once.

What Is Pokémon Night Out?

Pokémon Night Out is part of Pokémon’s 30th anniversary celebrations, combining Pokémon with an electronic dance music event.

The first Pokémon Night Out takes place at the Intuit Dome in Los Angeles on October 24, 2026, featuring Marshmello as the headliner alongside Alison Wonderland.

A second Pokémon Night Out is scheduled for the O2 Arena in London on November 10, 2026.

For Pokémon GO players who cannot attend in person, the Twitch Drop gives Trainers around the world a small piece of the celebration, along with an exclusive avatar item for tuning in.
