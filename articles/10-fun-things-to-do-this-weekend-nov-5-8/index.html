10 Fun Things to Do This Weekend: Nov. 5-8

Veterans Day Parade, Wurstfest, Doja Cat, Diwali and more

By San Antonio Magazine Staff

1. Veterans Day Parade

The United States Military Parade Association’s annual procession will feature marching bands, military units and floats parading through downtown to honor veterans and active-duty military members and their families. Noon, Nov. 7, Starts at Milam Park and travels down historic Houston Street

 

2. Wurstfest

New Braunfels’ annual celebration of all things German features music by polka hotshot Alex Meixner, the Bavarian Oktoberfest band Die Bayrischen Hiatamadln and the polka fusion band Bavarski. The food menu goes deep on wurst and schnitzel, often served on a stick. Nov. 6-15, Landa Park, New Braunfels

 

3. Bands of America

High School marching bands from around the country are at the Alamodome this weekend to compete in a super-regional championship. Competition takes place throughout the day on Friday and Saturday with the top-ranked bands performing in a finals competition on Saturday night. Various times. Nov. 6-7, 100 Montana St.

 

4. First Friday Art Walk

Explore the latest exhibitions at Blue Star Contemporary as well as at surrounding galleries in the Blue Star Arts Complex and Southtown neighborhood at this monthly community art event. 6-11 p.m. Nov. 6, 1414 S. Alamo St. 

 

5. Doja Cat

Rapper and singer Doja Cat is touring with a new album, the ’80s pop-funk inspired “Vie.” Doja Cat won a Grammy Award in 2022 for her song “Kiss Me More.” 7:30 p.m. Nov. 6, Frost Bank Center, 1 Frost Bank Center Drive

 

6. Nikki Glaser

Comedian Glaser, the once and future host of the Golden Globe Awards, is returning to town on her Stunning Tour. Glaser’s latest special, “Good Girl,” is streaming on Hulu. If you’d rather see her in Vegas, she’s doing a residency with David Spade in January at Caesars Palace. 7 p.m. Nov. 7-8, Majestic Theatre, 224 E. Houston St.

 

7. Diwali SA

This popular traditional Indian Festival of Lights will feature food, art, folk dances, floating candles, fireworks, a river parade and more. Admission is free. 4:30 p.m.-midnight, Nov. 7, Arneson River Theatre, 418 Villita St., and Hemisfair, 210 S. Alamo St.

 

8. Ryan Bingham and The Texas Gentlemen

From his Academy Award-winning song “The Weary Kind” to fan favorites like “Southside of Heaven” and “Hallelujah,” Bingham’s music captures the spirit of the open road and the heart of the American West. Fans may also recognize him from his acclaimed role on the hit television series “Yellowstone.” 8 p.m. Nov. 7, Tobin Center for the Performing Arts, 100 Auditorium Circle

 

9. David Sedaris

Humorist and New Yorker contributor Sedaris will tell stories and take questions during this “Evening With” appearance. His latest book, “The Land and Its People,” collects personal essays and reports on travels to Vatican City, Guatemala and Kenya. 8 p.m. Nov. 7, Charline McCombs Empire Theatre, 226 N. St. Mary’s St.

 

10. Kathy Sosa Exhibit

“Revolutionary Women of Texas and Mexico” is the first exhibit in the Briscoe Western Art Museum recently reopened Women of the West Gallery. It features more than 30 portraits by San Antonio artist Sosa of subjects such as author Sandra Cisneros and activist Emma Tenayuca. Ongoing, 210 W. Market St.
