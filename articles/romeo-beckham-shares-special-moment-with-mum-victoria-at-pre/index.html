Romeo Beckham Shares Special Moment With Mum Victoria At Premiere Of His New Film

TheTools · 18.11.2026 12:34 · source · permalink

Romeo Beckham was joined by some very special guests at the launch of his new film.

The Beckhams’ second eldest son is set to make his acting debut in Pierre-Ange Carlotti’s queer romance movie Forty Love, about two rival tennis pros at the top of their game who become embroiled in a secret affair (no, we’re sure Heated Rivalry had nothing to do with it, we don’t know what you mean).

On Tuesday night, Forty Love had its world premiere in Paris, and as well as his girlfriend, Kim Turnbull, Romeo was pictured on the red carpet with his famous parents, Sir David and Victoria Beckham.

Romeo was pictured throwing his arms around his mum on the red carpet while his dad looked on beaming.

The family also posed for pictures with Romeo’s co-star, Paul Kircher, director Pierre-Ange and producer Hugo Selignac.

After the event, Sir David posted a picture of himself and Victoria in the audience at the screening on Instagram.

“So proud of you mate,” he told Romeo in the caption. “You see the challenge and you take it… we love you.”

View this post on Instagram

Of course, it’s well-documented that 2026 has been something of a tumultuous year for the Beckham family.

Back in January, Sir David and Victoria’s eldest son Brooklyn Peltz Beckham made international headlines when he shared a series of candid Instagram posts, in which he accused his parents of “trying to endlessly ruin” his relationship with his wife, Nicola Peltz Beckham.

He went on to take aim at his parents for what he referred to as “performative” and “controlling” behaviour on their part over the course of his “entire life”, as well as alleging his mum “hijacked” his first dance with Nicola during their 2022 wedding reception.

This came after months of speculation about a supposed fall-out in the family, with Sir David and Victoria have done their best to remain tight-lipped about ever since.

“We’ve always tried to be the best parents that we can be,” Victoria said in April. “And you know, we’ve been in the public eye for more than 30 years right now, and all we’ve ever tried to do is protect our children and love our children.”

She added: “That’s all I really want to say about it.”
