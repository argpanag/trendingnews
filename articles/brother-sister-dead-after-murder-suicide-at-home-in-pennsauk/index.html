Brother, sister dead after murder-suicide at home in Pennsauken, New Jersey

TheTools · 16.09.2026 13:53 · source · permalink

PENNSAUKEN, N.J. (WPVI) -- Authorities say a man fatally shot his sister outside a Pennsauken, New Jersey, home Wednesday morning before taking his own life, prompting an approximately 90-minute barricade situation as law enforcement secured the scene.

Police responded around 9 a.m. to the 4400 block of Bethel Avenue for a report of a shooting and a man armed with a handgun at the residence. When officers arrived, they found a victim on the front steps of the home.

The victim was identified as 59-year-old Janet Camacho of Pennsauken. She was taken to Cooper University Hospital, where she was pronounced dead.

After the shooting, authorities established a perimeter around the residence. The Camden County Sheriff's Emergency Response Team responded to the scene, and officers were seen escorting residents from a neighboring home.

A robotic dog was later deployed and entered the residence through the garage. Inside, authorities found Camacho's brother, 60-year-old Daniel Lopez, suffering from a self-inflicted gunshot wound. He was pronounced dead at the scene.

Authorities said the investigation determined the incident was a murder-suicide.

Action News obtained surveillance video of the shooting, though the footage was too graphic to air. According to the station, the video captured gunshots and showed a man pacing outside the home after the killing.

The incident shocked neighbors.

"This is so rare. This doesn't happen here," said Ozzie Marrero of Pennsauken.

"It's unfortunate, and I'm sorry that the families have to deal with this," said Jabril Smitherman of The Barbers Lounge.

Neighbor Michelle Jimenez said Lopez had been close to her family.

"We used to invite him a lot for family parties. He would always be our guest. He was really close to our family. He was always nice. He would bake stuff for me and my siblings," Jimenez said.

She said she was stunned when she learned what had happened.

"It was just shocking to me. I didn't believe it," Jimenez said.

Authorities have not released information about a possible motive.

The investigation remains ongoing. Anyone with information is asked to contact Pennsauken police or the Camden County Prosecutor's Office.
