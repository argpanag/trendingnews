Jimmy Kimmel's Guillermo joins Joelle Garguilo for a salsa surprise at ABC News HQ

TheTools · 15.09.2026 23:48 · source · permalink

NEW YORK CITY -- Though most recognizable for his part in "Jimmy Kimmel Live!," Guillermo Rodriguez is booked and busy.

Not only is he part of the newest season of "Dancing with the Stars," but he also has a new line of salsas available for purchase in stores now.

Entertainment reporter Joelle Garguilo brought him to ABC News Headquarters in New York City, and you can see what they got up to in the report above.

Tune into "Dancing with the Stars" season 35 on Tuesday, Sept. 15, at 8 p.m. ET on ABC and Disney+, followed by a special second episode Wednesday, Sept. 16, at 8 p.m. ET. Episodes stream the next day on Hulu.
