Disneyland Just Emailed Discount Tickets Code That May Be Hidden in Your Spam Folder

TheTools · 10.09.2026 16:55 · source · permalink

If you’ve been thinking about taking a Disneyland trip sometime through the end of 2026 but haven’t purchased tickets yet, you might want to check your email. Disney sent a targeted offer with a specially priced park hopper ticket, granting access to both Disneyland Park and Disney California Adventure on the same day. This is an easy email to miss, as it looks a lot like their other marketing materials.

This new Disneyland ticket discount was only sent to certain guests. We have details on what’s being offered and how to book low ticket prices even if you didn’t receive the email. Disneyland Special Email Pin Ticket Offer

Disneyland Resort is distributing a targeted email offer to select guests for discounted tickets that run through the remainder of Halloween Time and most of the upcoming holiday season festivities. We don’t know if all emails have been sent out or if more are coming.

Through the offer, guests can purchase a 1-day Disneyland Resort park hopper ticket for $149 for guests ages 3+. Tickets can be used any day from September 10, 2026, through December 18, 2026.

This is a huge savings, as Disneyland park hopper ticket prices for those same dates fluctuate from $174 to as high as $314 for guests ages 10+ and $168 to $304 for guests ages 3-9, depending on the day. Guests ages 2 and under do not need a ticket.

Guests who did not receive the PIN offer in their email can still access special pricing on Disneyland tickets through our travel partner, Get Away Today. They give Mickey Visit readers additional savings on Disneyland tickets. The email provides guests with a unique, non-transferable PIN code to access the offer. Guests may purchase up to 5 tickets per transaction, with no more than a total of 3 transactions per PIN.

The two people in our circles who received the discount do not have Magic Key passes. One of them had not purchased tickets on their Disney account for quite a few years.

Here are the exact details on the deal email so that you can search for it in your junk mail:

Subject Line: You’ve been chosen for a special ticket offer!

Email Body: Hey NAME, you’ve been selected for this special ticket offer. Unlock this special rate by copying your unique PIN*:

Here’s the full fine print from the email for your reference:

“Offer valid only for recipients of an email with a personalized, nontransferable PIN, and valid PIN is required to purchase the tickets. Tickets valid for use between September 10, 2026 and December 18, 2026. May purchase up to 5 tickets per transaction, with no more than a total of 3 transactions per PIN. Tickets are nonrefundable, may not be sold or transferred for commercial use and excludes activities/events separately priced. Offer may not be combined with other ticket discounts or promotions. Subject to restrictions and change or cancellation without notice. Sales may be paused from time to time or terminated at any time.”

Disneyland officials shared with Mickey Visit that the eligibility for which guests received this offer was based on multiple criteria, and that the resort will continue rolling out discounts available to all guests, like last summer’s kids ticket offer, in addition to more targeted campaigns like this one and the Anaheim resident offer happening through October 8.
