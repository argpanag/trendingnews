This is not just another match in Portugal. This is O Clássico.

Don't miss the match between Benfica and Porto on beIN SPORTS.

Porto and Benfica meet this Sunday at the Estádio do Dragão in a clash between the top two teams in Liga Portugal, with first place potentially changing hands just before the international break.

The Dragons enter as defending champions and league leaders with 18 points after winning their first six matches. The Eagles sit right behind them with 16 points and remain unbeaten.

Only two points separate them.

That means a Benfica victory on enemy territory would be enough to take first place away from Porto, while the home side has the opportunity to open up a five-point advantage over its biggest rival.

Everything is ready for another chapter of O Clássico.


Porto Enters O Clássico With a Perfect Record

Porto’s start to the season could hardly have been better.

The team coached by Francesco Farioli has won all six of its league matches against Alverca, Rio Ave, Arouca, Académico de Viseu, Moreirense and Casa Pia, while also beginning the campaign by lifting the Supertaça Cândido de Oliveira.

The Dragons also enter the match after an emphatic 4-1 victory over Casa Pia, confirming the strong form of a team determined to defend the crown it won last season.

Gabri Veiga, Borja Sainz and André Silva are Porto’s leading scorers in the league with three goals each, while Gabri Veiga and Pepê have each provided three assists.

In addition, Farioli has recovered some important players ahead of O Clássico. Victor Froholdt, Oskar Pietuszewski and Samu Aghehowa are once again available to face Benfica.

Porto Lineup

Benfica Can Leave the Dragão as League Leader

Benfica is also enjoying an excellent run of form.

After drawing its opening match, the team coached by Marco Silva has recorded five consecutive victories in Liga Portugal and enters O Clássico with the opportunity to finish the matchweek at the top of the standings.

The Eagles have built much of their attack around Vangelis Pavlidis, who enters the match as the league's leading scorer with eight goals.

Gianluca Prestianni has also emerged as one of the team's biggest attacking threats, while João Palhinha provides experience and balance in midfield.

Marco Silva made it clear ahead of the match that his team has no intention of changing its identity simply because it is visiting the Dragão.

The coach acknowledged Porto’s ability to press high and the difficulty of playing O Clássico away from home, but urged his players to maintain their personality.

“We have to be brave,” the coach said ahead of the match.

O Clássico Once Again Brings Portugal to a Standstill

The standings add even more tension to a rivalry that hardly needs any additional ingredients.

Porto and Benfica are two of the biggest institutions in Portuguese football, and their meetings have shaped battles for championships, cups and domestic supremacy for decades.

This time, O Clássico arrives very early in the calendar, but with important consequences already at stake.

The defending champions can confirm their perfect start and pull away at the top. Benfica, meanwhile, has the opportunity to end its rival's winning streak and return from Porto as the new league leader.

Two points separate them. Two unbeaten teams. And the Estádio do Dragão is ready to host the first O Clássico of the season.
