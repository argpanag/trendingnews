  8. Who are the Champions League newcomers?published at 14:47 BST
    14:47 BST

    Talking about debuts, for the 2026-27 Champions League season there are four clubs making their first appearance in the competition:

    Viking

    After winning their first Norweigan title in 34 years, Viking beat Dinamo Zagreb in the qualifying round to reach the league phase.

    Their previous participation in a European group stage came in the 2005/06 Uefa Cup.

    Como

    Led by former Arsenal and Barcelona midefielder Cesc Febregas, Como qualified automatically after finishing fourth in Serie A last season.

    Como, who are the 12th Italian side to play in the Champions League, is their first appearance in a European club competition of any kind.

    LASK

    Austrian champions LASK will make their first appearance in the Champions League this season and beat Celtic 5-4 on aggregate in the qualifying round.

    LASK, who won the domestic double by winning the Austrian Cup, did reach the last 16 of the 2019/20 Europa League.

    Sabah

    Sabah qualified through the play-offs after overturning a 2-1 first-leg deficit against Hapoel Beer-Sheva with a 5-2 extra-time victory in the return leg.

    Formed as recent as 2017, Sabah won its first Azerbaijani league title in 2025/26 and also retained the Azerbaijani Cup.

  9. Champions League debut sleeve patch introducedpublished at 14:45 BST
    14:45 BST

    Similar to what we saw in the World Cup, players making their first-ever appearance in the Champions League will from this season have the occasion marked with a new "debut patch".

    The blue patch will only be valid when players make their on-pitch debut, which includes entering a match as a substitute.

    It will be incorporated into the Champions League Starball badge worn on a player's jersey sleeve.

    Once a player has made their Champions League debut, they will not wear the patch again.

    Uefa said the patch will give clubs, broadcasters and partners a "visible way to recognise a significant career milestone in real time".

    Patches will also be turned into a one-of-one trading card that will be signed by the player and sold as a piece of memorabilia.

  17. Mac Allister had a lot to get off his chestpublished at 14:21 BST
    14:21 BST

    Liverpool v Atletico Madrid (Wed, 20:00 BST)

    Aadam Patel
    Football Reporter

    It's fair to say that Alexis Mac Allister had quite a lot to get off his chest and he has done that and more here.

    We walked into the press conference room at Anfield thinking the focus would be on Andoni Iraola's first game as a manager in the Champions League. Instead, the talk is all about Mac Allister.

    Quite clearly, there is a sadness on his part that he hasn't had the same treatment as Dominik Szoboszlai and Ryan Gravenberch when it comes to a new deal and it is interesting that Mac Allister has chosen to air his thoughts in such a public setting.

    Now it remains to be seen how this saga will play out over the course of the season.

    At 27, and with his proven quality, Mac Allister would command a hefty fee in a market where the value of midfielders is only rising.

    But Mac Allister is clear that he loves Liverpool and Andoni Iraola's suggestions that the Argentine can develop as a player suggests that there could be another chapter to Liverpool and Mac Allister's relationship.

Mac Allister had a lot to get off his chestpublished at 14:21 BST

Liverpool v Atletico Madrid (Wed, 20:00 BST)

Aadam Patel
Football Reporter

It's fair to say that Alexis Mac Allister had quite a lot to get off his chest and he has done that and more here.

We walked into the press conference room at Anfield thinking the focus would be on Andoni Iraola's first game as a manager in the Champions League. Instead, the talk is all about Mac Allister.

Quite clearly, there is a sadness on his part that he hasn't had the same treatment as Dominik Szoboszlai and Ryan Gravenberch when it comes to a new deal and it is interesting that Mac Allister has chosen to air his thoughts in such a public setting.

Now it remains to be seen how this saga will play out over the course of the season.

At 27, and with his proven quality, Mac Allister would command a hefty fee in a market where the value of midfielders is only rising.

But Mac Allister is clear that he loves Liverpool and Andoni Iraola's suggestions that the Argentine can develop as a player suggests that there could be another chapter to Liverpool and Mac Allister's relationship.
