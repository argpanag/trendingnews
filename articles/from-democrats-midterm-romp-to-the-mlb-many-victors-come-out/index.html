From Democrats’ midterm romp to the MLB, many victors come out of the blue

TheTools · 14.11.2026 12:15 · source · permalink

MILWAUKEE — The 2026 World Series went through Wisconsin for the first time in 44 years and provided the most surprising champion Major League Baseball produced in more than a decade. The Milwaukee Brewers, the sport’s smallest market with a middling payroll, forged one of the hottest runs in postseason history — an 11-2 march from divisional winner to last team standing after the Fall Classic.

Playoff games, watch parties, and obsessed fans dominated the Badger State’s headlines, pushing the gubernatorial race timed with the 2026 midterms down the front page. However, when the confetti cleared from the avenues of downtown Milwaukee, Wisconsin had a governor-elect even more unlikely than the Hometown Nine hefting the Commissioner’s Trophy in this dawning age of the accidental candidate.
