Yahoo Finance's Josh Lipton takes a closer look at the top stories for investors to watch on Thursday, Sept. 3, including weekly initial jobless claims data and quarterly earnings results from Lululemon (LULU) and DocuSign (DOCU).

The time now for what to watch Thursday, September 3rd. Going to start off here on earnings. Lulu Lemon announcing second quarter results after the market's closed. Wall Street looking for signs that fresher product and tighter inventory are starting to stabilize the business in North America.
