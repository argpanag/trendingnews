Champions Galway take home seven All-Stars

TheTools · 15.11.2026 08:51 · source · permalink

TG4 All-Ireland senior champions Galway led the way with seven representatives on the 2026 TG4 Ladies Football All-Star team, as Kerry's Síofra O'Shea won Player's Player of the Year.

Galway's seven winners were defenders Brónagh Quinn, Kate Geraghty (TG4 All-Ireland winning captain), Hannah Noone and Nicola Ward, midfielder Niamh Divilly and attackers Olivia Divilly and Leanne Coen.

Runners-up Kerry were rewarded with five All-Stars. Goalkeeper Mary Ellen Bolger, defenders Róisín Rahilly and Emma Costello, midfielder Mary O’Connell, and team captain Síofra O’Shea, the leading scorer in this year’s All-Ireland senior Championship, were all listed.

There was one award each for All-Ireland semi-finalists Armagh and Dublin, with Aimee Mackin and Orlagh Nolan honoured in attack.

Waterford’s Kellyann Hogan was also included in the half-forward line, which means the Déise have a TG4 All-Star for the second year in succession, following Emma Murray’s award in 2025.

There were nine first-time award winners in the 15 selected by the Ladies Gaelic Football Association’s All-Star committee, as Bolger, Rahilly, Quinn, Noone, Costello, Niamh Divilly, O’Connell, Hogan and Coen picked up maiden All-Star gongs.

O'Shea's individual award caps off a remarkable season for her and the Kingdom.

She led them to TG4 Munster Senior Championship glory and a fourth TG4 All-Ireland senior final appearance in five seasons. She also finished as top scorer with a tally of 3-30.

Her highlights included a brilliant 1-8 in the All-Ireland semi-final win over reigning champions Dublin, which also earned her The Croke Park Hotel/LGFA Player of the Month award for July.

O’Shea, who scored 0-5 in the showpiece Brendan Martin Cup decider against Galway at Croke Park, held off stiff competition from Galway’s Olivia Divilly and Armagh’s Mackin to win the award.

The 2026 TG4 Intermediate Players’ Player of the Year award went to Roscommon star Saoirse Wynne, ahead of Fermanagh’s Eimear Smyth and Westmeath’s Anna Jones.

Carlow’s Sara Doyle was honoured as the 2026 TG4 Junior Players’ Player of the Year.

Elsewhere, Mayo legend Cora Staunton was officially inducted into the LGFA Hall of Fame.

2026 TG4 All-Star team:

Mary Ellen Bolger (Kerry) – 1st award

Róisín Rahilly (Kerry) – 1st award

Brónagh Quinn (Galway) – 1st award

Kate Geraghty (Galway) – 2nd award (previous in 2024)

Hannah Noone (Galway) – 1st award

Nicola Ward (Galway) – 4th award (previous in 2019, 2024 and 2025)

Emma Costello (Kerry) – 1st award

Niamh Divilly (Galway) – 1st award

Mary O’Connell (Kerry) – 1st award

Olivia Divilly (Galway) – 3rd award (previous in 2024 and 2025)

Orlagh Nolan (Dublin) – 3rd award (previous in 2021 and 2023)

Kellyann Hogan (Waterford) – 1st award

Aimee Mackin (Armagh) – 4th award (previous in 2015, 2017 and 2022)

Leanne Coen (Galway) – 1st award

Síofra O’Shea (Kerry) – 2nd award (previous in 2025)

County by county breakdown:

Galway 7, Kerry 5, Armagh 1, Dublin 1, Waterford 1.
