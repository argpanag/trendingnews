Your Guide To Securing Pre-Sale Tickets To Calvin Harris' Australian Tour Today

TheTools · 24.09.2026 03:21 · source · permalink

Australia has been treated to a slew of major tour announcements in recent weeks, and adding another name to an increasingly enticing tour calendar is global DJ and record producer Calvin Harris.

The Scottish superstar has announced his first-ever Australian headline tour, bringing his hypnotic brand of electronic pop to Perth, Melbourne, Brisbane and Sydney next February.

Harris last graced our shores in 2022, when he headlined Festival X, having previously played Stereosonic and Big Day Out. In a statement released on Wednesday, Live Nation said: “The Calvin Harris – Australia Tour 2027 will celebrate his expansive repertoire, seamlessly blending his seminal, record-breaking studio albums – such as 18 Months, Motion, and the critically acclaimed Funk Wav Bounces Vol. 1 and Vol. 2 – with his latest club bangers, massive chart-topping collaborations, and his recent compilation album 96 Months.”

We’re excited, to say the least. With his formidable catalogue of pop anthems including “One Kiss” with Dua Lipa, “We Found Love” featuring Rihanna, “Summer” and “Feel So Close”, Harris has no shortage of hits to draw from, and fans are likely to be clamouring for the chance to hear them live.

Ahead, everything you need to know about the tour, including when and where to secure one of its coveted tickets.

Calvin Harris Australia Tour Dates 

Harris will be heading Down Under in February 2027, with a headline show in Brisbane, Sydney, Melbourne and Perth.

When Do Calvin Harris Tickets Go On Sale? 

The general on-sale tickets will be released at 1pm (local time) on Thursday, September 24 via Ticketmaster. A number of pre-sales are taking place ahead of this general release, so availability may be limited.

Is There A Pre-Sale For Calvin Harris’ Australian Shows?

Two pre-sales are happening before the general ticket on-sale release. Telstra Plus members can get in first with an exclusive pre-sale, which will run for 48 hours from 11am (local time) on Monday, September 21 until 11am on Wednesday, September 23. If you missed out on those, then today’s artist pre-sale could be your chance.

The artist pre-sale will then commence from 12pm on Wednesday, September 23 and will close on Thursday, September 24 ahead of the general on-sale which will begin at 1pm the same day. For further details, head here.
