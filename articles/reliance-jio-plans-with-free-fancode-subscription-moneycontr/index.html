Reliance Jio has several prepaid plans that bundle entertainment services with regular telecom benefits. For subscribers looking for access to FanCode along with mobile data and calling, there are four plans priced between Rs 399 and Rs 500 that include the sports streaming platform.

The Rs 399, Rs 459, Rs 495 and Rs 500 Jio prepaid plans come with 28 days of validity. However, the data allowance and additional subscriptions vary across these recharges.

Jio Rs 399 plan

The Rs 399 prepaid recharge offers 2GB daily data for 28 days, along with unlimited voice calls and 100 SMS per day. Eligible users also get unlimited True 5G data.

FanCode is included with the plan along with JioHotstar Mobile, JioGames Pass, JioTV and JioAICloud. The recharge also comes with an 18-month Google Gemini Pro subscription, which includes 5TB cloud storage and access to additional AI features.

Jio Rs 459 plan

The Rs 459 recharge also has a 28-day validity and provides 2GB daily data, unlimited calls and 100 SMS per day. Eligible subscribers get unlimited 5G data as well.

The plan bundles FanCode, JioGames Mobile and Snapchat+. It also includes an 18-month Google Gemini Pro subscription with 5TB cloud storage and a 12-month Canva Pro Lite subscription. JioHotstar Mobile, JioTV and JioAICloud are included as well.

Jio Rs 495 plan

The Rs 495 plan comes with 1.5GB daily data and an additional 5GB data benefit. Users get unlimited voice calls and 100 SMS per day for 28 days.

FanCode and JioGames Cloud are included for the validity period. The recharge also comes with an 18-month Google Gemini Pro subscription, including 5TB cloud storage and AI features. JioTV and JioAICloud are bundled with the plan.

Jio Rs 500 plan

The Rs 500 recharge offers 2GB daily data, unlimited 5G data for eligible users, unlimited calls and 100 SMS per day for 28 days.

Its main attraction is the entertainment bundle, which includes 15 premium OTT apps and services, along with FanCode and JioGames Mobile. Subscribers also get JioTV, an 18-month Google Gemini Pro subscription and a 12-month Canva Pro Lite subscription.

For users specifically looking for FanCode, these four Jio plans provide multiple options depending on their data and entertainment requirements.
