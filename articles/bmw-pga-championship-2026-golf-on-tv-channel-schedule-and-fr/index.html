BMW PGA Championship 2026 golf on TV: Channel, schedule and free stream

TheTools · 17.09.2026 18:05 · source · permalink

Your complete guide to how to watch the BMW PGA Championship 2026 on TV and live stream, including full broadcast details.

  • Ned Holmes

Some of golf's best and brightest make the annual pilgrimage to Wentworth this week for the BMW PGA Championship – and fans can tune into free coverage across all four days.

One of the flagship events of the DP World Tour season, the tournament always draws a star-studded field, and this year is no different.

World No.2 Rory McIlroy is set to be joined by Irish Open champion Shane Lowry, Justin Rose, Tommy Fleetwood, Patrick Reed, Tyrell Hatton, and John Rahm, among others, in the hunt for the title.

  • Watch the BMW PGA Championship 2026 on Sky Sports

Reigning champion Alex Norén is certainly set to face plenty of competition in his title defence, having beaten Adrien Saddier in a dramatic play-off last year.

It's set to be another thrilling week of DP World Tour golf and UK fans can tune into extensive coverage, including free streams on all four days.

Radio Times brings you all the details about how to watch the BMW PGA Championship 2026.

How to watch the BMW PGA Championship 2026 on TV and live stream

You can watch the BMW PGA Championship 2026 live on Sky Sports from Thursday 17 September 2026 until the final round on Sunday 20 September 2026.

Coverage of play begins at 7:30am UK time on the first two days, and from 9am on Saturday and Sunday. The full schedule can be found below.

Sky Sports can be added to any Sky TV package for just £20 per month for all nine sports channels, or you can pick up the complete sports package plus Netflix for £35 per month.

  • Find out more about the best Sky packages

Sky Sports customers can live stream the tournament via the Sky Go app on a variety of devices including most smartphones and tablets as part of their subscription.

You can also watch the action via NOW with a day membership (£14.99) or month membership (£34.99).

NOW can be streamed through a computer or apps found on most smart TVs, phones and consoles. NOW is also available via TNT Sports.

BMW PGA Championship 2026 free stream

Fans can also tune in to watch Featured Group coverage of the BMW PGA Championship 2026 for free via the DP World Tour live stream.

The live stream can be accessed on the DP World Tour YouTube channel and on the DP World Tour free streaming channel, which is available on Samsung TV Plus, Amazon Prime, LG, Rakuten, Roku, Xiaomi and Vidaa platforms across the UK and Ireland.

The Featured Group coverage will run between 6:30am and 11:45am on Thursday and Friday, and between 8am and 1:15pm on Saturday and Sunday.
