Spalletti refuses to speak ahead of Lecce clash, Juve… | YSscores

TheTools · 24.10.2026 16:31 · source · permalink

Luciano Spalletti, Juventus' head coach, has decided not to hold the usual press conference ahead of the match against US Lecce, scheduled for Sunday, October 25, as part of Serie A, in coordination with the club's management, according to what was reported by the Italian website Tuttosport.

The decision came amid the pressure of the fixture schedule, after Juventus played a match against Rennes in the Europa League, which reduced the time available for the players to recover and prepare for the upcoming domestic match.

Juventus are looking to overcome their recent results after losing to SS Lazio and drawing with Rennes. The match against US Lecce represents an opportunity for the team to regain their balance and return to winning ways in Serie A.

Spalletti is expected to rely on the readiness of his players and the available options to determine the suitable lineup, given the pressure of matches and the significant absences within the team. Meanwhile, Juventus fans are anticipating a strong reaction from the players during the upcoming match.
