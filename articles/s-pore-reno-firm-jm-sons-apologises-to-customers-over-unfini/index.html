S'pore reno firm JM & Sons apologises to customers over unfinished projects, says it's insolvent & can't continue operating

TheTools · 26.11.2026 04:49 · source · permalink

Customers previously said they were unable to contact the firm, which left their renovation projects unfinished despite having paid substantial sums.

November 25, 2026, 03:28 PM

Photo by Mothership & Google

Local renovation contractor JM & Sons, which many customers said had become uncontactable, has announced that it cannot continue operations.

Insolvent and winding up

In a statement to Mothership on Nov. 25, the management team said that the firm has become insolvent and is unable to continue operations "due to severe financial pressures and challenging market conditions".

This "was never an outcome we wanted," they said, adding that the company has been operating in Singapore since 1990.

According to the statement, JM & Sons has initiated the winding-up process and will engage Clearview Associates, a liquidation and restructuring firm, to administer the process.

JM & Sons is currently preparing its accounts and aims to issue the notice of creditors' meeting and proof of debt forms to all affected customers and suppliers in early December 2026, the statement said.

"We sincerely apologise to all affected homeowners, suppliers and our long-serving staff for the distress caused.

Customers and suppliers with queries regarding the liquidation process or submitting a claim may contact the nominated liquidator’s office directly."

Several projects unfinished

It was previously reported that several customers of JM & Sons were unable to contact the firm, which left their renovation projects unfinished despite having paid substantial sums.

One client told Mothership that his kitchen was left unusable despite paying more than S$20,000.

He has filed a complaint with the Consumers Association of Singapore (CASE) and the police.

The police confirmed with Mothership that a report was lodged and that investigations are ongoing.

Another customer told Mothership that she paid JM & Sons more than S$30,000 for a full renovation of her home.

However, she was informed by their sales consultant that by Nov. 20, the company's own workers were unable to contact their management.

When Mothership visited the company's premises on Nov. 23 evening, the office was closed despite it being a weekday.

On Nov. 24, messages were sent in chat groups with affected customers where JM & Sons' staff announced that they had had their employment terminated.

As of Nov. 25, the Google Maps profile of the firm is now marked as "permanently closed".

Mothership has reached out to CASE for comment.
