Four Seasons invites runners, walkers, riders, and spectators to join Founder and Chairman Emeritus Isadore Sharp, along with hundreds of Four Seasons corporate and hotel employees, for the annual Terry Fox Run at Wilket Creek Park in North York on Sunday, September 20, 2026.

Realizing Terry’s Dream

Four Seasons Hotel Toronto and Four Seasons corporate home office organize Canada’s largest Terry Fox Run site at Wilket Creek Park, located at the corner of Leslie Street and Eglinton Avenue. Four Seasons employees and their families volunteer every year to register runners, serve food and refreshments, and manage the children’s play area, with hundreds also joining in the run itself.

Last year, Wilket Creek achieved record funds raised of over CAD 1 million. Close to CAD 16 million has been raised at the Wilket Creek Terry Fox Run site since 1981, with every dollar going directly to the Terry Fox Foundation. This event supports critical projects, including precision oncology for young people, genetic research into hereditary cancers, and advanced studies in leukemia, brain cancers, and more.

Terry Fox Run at Wilket Creek Park Event Details

Award-winning Canadian media personality Gurdeep Ahluwalia will serve as emcee for the event on Sunday, September 20, 2026, beginning with the Opening Ceremonies at 8:20 am.

Joining us for the National Anthem, followed by a performance, is Josiah Hibbs, a 12-year-old musical prodigy whose passion and talent have already earned him widespread recognition. Entirely self-taught on the guitar, Josiah is also an accomplished vocalist and enjoys playing the keyboard and drums. His dedication led to him being named Grand Champion of the 2025 Rising Stars Competition at the CNE, where he showcased his exceptional musical abilities on a major stage.

Fitness expert Sandra Parise will lead participants through a pre-run warm-up ahead of the official start at 9:05 am.

Four Seasons is also proud to welcome Domenico Meleca, a Terry Team Member and cancer survivor who was diagnosed at the age of 17 and continues to inspire others through his journey of resilience and determination.

The event will feature a complimentary BBQ, family-friendly activities for children, and convenient parking shuttle service. Returning by popular demand are not one, but two King Kone ice cream trucks, ensuring plenty of sweet treats for participants and spectators alike.

New this year, we are excited to welcome InsideOut Health + Fitness Studio, an organization dedicated to helping individuals achieve their health and wellness goals through personalized fitness programming and a diverse range of services. Recognized as one of Toronto's top fitness studios, InsideOut is committed to excellence and innovation, continually redefining the fitness experience for its clients.

A special thank you to our AV partner, Encore, for their ongoing support, expertise, and exceptional service in helping make this event a success year after year.

Registration is free and can be completed online or at Wilket Creek Park starting at 8:00 am.

Follow the event on Instagram @terryfoxwilketcreek and Facebook at TerryFoxWilketCreek, and look out for the Terry Fox Banner on Leslie Street, north of Eglinton.

The Marathon of Hope Continues

Inspired by his 1980 Marathon of Hope, Terry Fox’s legacy continues through more than 9,000 annual Terry Fox Runs worldwide, all led by volunteers. To date, more than CAD 1 billion has been raised globally, funding more than 1,300 innovative research projects for cancer research in Terry’s name.  Through the Terry Fox Foundation, in Canada more than 20,000 passionate volunteers and 3.5 million students host and participate in nearly 10,000 annual runs and other fundraising events.

To learn more about Terry Fox, the Terry Fox Foundation, and how to support cancer research, visit the Terry Fox Foundation website and follow the fight against cancer on Facebook and @terryfoxfoundation on Instagram.

Commitment to Fighting Cancer

Four Seasons is dedicated to fighting cancer through annual charity runs and other initiatives globally. This commitment has been steadfast since Terry Fox’s historic Marathon of Hope 46 years ago, when Isadore Sharp pledged support for Terry and encouraged others to support the cause. The first Terry Fox Run organized by Four Seasons followed in 1981. This commitment has expanded globally, with Four Seasons hosting, sponsoring, and participating in numerous local events to benefit the Terry Fox Foundation and local cancer research and care facilities. In 2025, Four Seasons properties and offices around the world raised nearly CAD 6 million for this cause. 

Learn more about Four Seasons charitable initiatives and achievements in communities around the world on our Four Seasons for Good website.
