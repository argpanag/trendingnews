Ireland v Australia: Jacob Stockdale returns as Irish make two changes for Nations Championship fifth-place play-off

TheTools · 27.11.2026 07:32 · source · permalink

Stockdale was not involved in Ireland's wins over Argentina, Fiji and South Africa

  • Published
    25 November 2026

Ulster wing Jacob Stockdale has been recalled to the Ireland line-up for Friday's Nations Championship fifth-place play-off match against Australia.

Stockdale, who was not involved in Ireland's home wins over Argentina, Fiji and South Africa, replaces Mack Hansen on the left wing.

Nick Timoney comes in for Dan Sheehan at number eight in the only other change to the line-up that started Saturday's 20-17 victory over world champions South Africa at Aviva Stadium.

Sheehan is ruled out of Friday's match at Twickenham's Allianz Stadium (20:10 GMT) after being banned for two games for a croc-roll tackle during the victory over the Springboks.

Tadhg Beirne will captain Ireland in Sheehan's absence for the second time this autumn, having also stepped up against Fiji when Sheehan was ruled out through concussion.

Stockdale returns to the line-up for his 44th cap after a strong start to the season with Ulster, with three tries in as many games.

The 30-year-old started Ireland's Six Nations defeat by France in Paris in February, and while he was not picked against Italy or England, he made a try-scoring return against Wales.

Friday will be Stockdale's first appearance in green since July's Nations Championship victory over Japan.

He is one of eight Ulster players in head coach Andy Farrell's matchday squad. Robert Baloucoune, who starred with two tries against the Springboks, is retained at right wing with Stuart McCloskey kept at inside centre.

With Sheehan out, Timoney returns to number eight. Farrell is dealing with an injury crisis in the back-row position with Caelan Doris, Jack Conan and Sean Jansen ruled out.

Timoney started at the base of the scrum against Fiji having been preferred to Josh van der Flier at open-side flanker for the Argentina game.

Hooker Tom Stewart and prop Tom O'Toole are retained on the bench alongside Leinster's Thomas Clarkson, who have been dubbed the 'Tom Squad'.

James McNabney is back among the replacements after his eye-catching debut against Fiji was cut short by a concussion.

Nathan Doak, who like Stockdale has not been involved so far this month, is named as the back-up scrum-half.

Despite a taxing win over South Africa, Farrell has opted against making wholesale changes with Andrew Porter, Ronan Kelleher and Tadhg Furlong retained in the front row.

Joe McCarthy and Beirne are kept in the second row with Ryan Baird and Van der Flier alongside Timoney in the back row.

Despite a morale-boosting win over South Africa to round out the Nations Championship campaign with five wins from six games, Ireland finished third in the northern hemisphere standings behind France and England.

Beale returns for Wallabies

Beale is set to win his 96th cap for the Wallabies after a five-year Test exile

Australia finished third in the southern hemisphere table behind South Africa and New Zealand despite losing four of their six games, including a 33-31 defeat by Ireland in the opening round in Sydney.

Since that game, Joe Schmidt has been replaced as Wallabies boss by former Ireland defence coach and Ulster director of rugby Les Kiss.

Kiss has named two uncapped players in his matchday squad with James Ramm selected on the left wing and Teddy Wilson - son of former World Cup winner David - named as the replacement scrum-half.

The Wallabies bench also features 37-year-old utility back Kurtley Beale, who is set to earn his first cap in five years.

Allan Alaalatoa will co-captain the Wallabies on his 100th cap alongside Harry Wilson, who is named on the bench, while scrum-half Kalani Thomas earns his first Test start after coming off the bench in last weekend's win over Wales in Cardiff.

Line-ups

Ireland: Keenan; Baloucoune, Henshaw, McCloskey, Stockdale; Prendergast, Gibson-Park; Porter, Kelleher, Furlong, McCarthy, Beirne (capt), Baird, Van der Flier, Timoney.

Replacements: Stewart, O'Toole, Clarkson, Ryan, McNabney, Doak, Frawley, Aki.

Australia: Wright; Jorgensen, Suaalii, Ikitau, Ramm; Gordon, Thomas; Kailea, Paenga-Amosa, Alaalatoa (co-capt), Canham, Williams, Hooper, Tizzano, Valetini.

Replacements: Nasser, Bell, Tupou, Shaw, Wilson (co-capt), McReight, Wilson, Beale.
