The Final Four - PTSB Dublin Senior 1 Football Championship semi-finals weekend

TheTools · 04.10.2026 16:43 · source · permalink

Saturday, 3 October 2026 06:00

By Michael Carolan @carolanmj

The line-up for the 2026 PTSB Dublin Senior 1 FC Final will be decided this weekend

Parnell Park plays host to the first of the PTSB Dublin Senior 1 Football Championship semi-finals this evening.

Thomas Davis take on Castleknock with throw-in at 5:30pm. Tomorrow afternoon at 4pm, it's the meeting of Ballyboden St. Endas and Cuala.

Castleknock raised a few eyebrows when overcoming Kilmacud Crokes last Saturday week but many observers were right to fancy them given the absence of Dara Mullin, Shane Walsh and Joe Quigley from the Crokes attack.

Wing-back Rob Shaw's four points against Crokes reflects his scoring threat and they’ll also look to the likes of Dylan Clarke and Shairoze Akram to chip in with scores in what is expected to be another tight encounter.

Thomas Davis were equally impressive when getting the better of Ballymun Kickhams at the last-eight stages, winning far more comfortably than the 1-16 to 1-13 scoreline would suggest.

The Tallaght outfit controlled large portions of that match with Fionn Murray’s development this year a crucial aspect of that supremacy.

His brace of two-pointers in the second-half highlighted his ability to keep the team’s scoreboard ticking over with full-forward Mark Nolan scoring their crucial goal.

This evening's semi-final at Parnell Park throws-in at 5:30pm.

Tomorrow, the clash between Cuala and Ballyboden St. Endas looks equally difficult to call and many will be hoping the teams produce something on a par to their thrilling clash at the corresponding stage last year.

Defending their county, provincial and national titles, Cuala looked on course for victory as they led by seven points by the 55th minute but two goals from the excellent Daire Sweeney, the second in added time, saw Boden time their comeback to perfection, winning by 3-21 to 2-22.

Tomorrow's game throws-in at 4pm.
