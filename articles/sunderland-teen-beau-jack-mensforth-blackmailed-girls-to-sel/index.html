Sunderland teen Beau-Jack Mensforth blackmailed girls to self-harm

TheTools · 28.11.2026 09:58 · source · permalink

This aticle contains distressing details

A "cowardly monster" who blackmailed girls across the world into self-harming for the entertainment of others has been detained for 14 and a half years.

Beau-Jack Mensforth was 17 when he began forcing vulnerable girls to send sexual images, hurt animals and carve his assumed name into their bodies, Newcastle Crown Court heard.

One of the girls' mothers said he used the internet to breach the safety of his victims' homes and minds, causing lifelong devastation.

Mensforth, 20 and from Sunderland, admitted 17 offences. They included crimes against nine victims, the youngest of whom was aged 12, and having indecent images of children as young as five.

Mensforth was in online groups who made content for their "com", short for community, by victimising "vulnerable young girls", prosecutor Charlotte Hole said.

They often targeted girls in self-harm support groups and used apps including Telegram and Discord to befriend them.

Abusers convinced the girls they were in romantic relationships and coaxed them into sending indecent images of themselves, then threatened to publish the pictures unless they committed more "extreme" acts, the court heard.

The girls would be forced to carve the names of their abusers into their flesh and write messages and logos in blood promoting the "com" groups.

They were also ordered to "sacrifice" animals under the threat their personal information would be released online or false reports would be made to trigger armed police raids of their homes.

Under the username Yuri, Mensforth targeted girls in the UK, USA and Australia, making them write "I worship Yuri" in blood among a litany of other abuses.

When one of his victims asked him why he was doing it, Mensforth replied "for content", the court heard, with the abuser seeking to rise to the top of the group's hierarchy.

Even after being arrested in October 2023, Mensforth used other hidden phones to message girls and post in his group.

He boasted and shared bloody images of his victims, and complained when other users were ranked higher than him.

Mensforth met one victim in real life, later sharing pictures he took without her knowledge and bragging to his friends about raping the 16-year-old.

He later told police his role was to "recruit leaders who would be taught how to extort girls".

'Hiding behind a screen'

A victim from the USA said she was 16 when she was "lured" into Mensforth's "online child predator group".

Having been forced to harm herself, the pictures of her "mutilated" body then got thousands of views online, she said.

She said she begged her abusers to take the images down but they refused, adding: "The more I cried about it the more amusement it seemed to bring them."

Her mother said she had no idea there were "cowardly monsters" like Mensforth "hiding behind a screen tormenting our baby girl".

A UK-based victim, who was 13 when Mensforth began abusing her, reported him to police.

"Instead of looking back at my early teen years and being filled with joy, I'm reminded of the fear and violation I felt," she said.

Her body was "covered" with scars inflicted at Mensforth's behest, she said, describing him as a "psychopath" who "feeds off the pain" of others.

Other victims said they had ongoing mental health issues and their trauma was worse because the images were still online.

Mensforth admitted blackmail, sexual communications with children, making and distributing indecent images of children and participating in an organised crime group between 2023 and 2024.

Judge Edward Bindloss said Mensforth was a "calculating predator" who groomed, manipulated and blackmailed girls for those who shared his "taste for sexual violence and enjoyed watching females self-harm".

Mensforth "rose to play a leading role" in the harm group and "thrived" on his status, the judge said.

Bindloss said Mensforth, who told probation workers he hoped to push a girl into killing herself, would have to serve six years on extended licence.
