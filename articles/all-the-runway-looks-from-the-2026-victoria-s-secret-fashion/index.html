All the Runway Looks From the 2026 Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show

TheTools · 18.10.2026 23:44 · source · permalink

It’s Supermodel Sunday!

The last time that the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show was held in Los Angeles, the year was 2007. Heidi Klum served as the host of the sparkling evening, supermodel Adriana Lima opened the runway, Selita Ebanks wore the “Very Sexy Holiday” million-dollar bra, and the one-and-only Candice Swanepoel made her angel debut, all to a soundtrack that included live performances from Will.I.Am, Seal, and the Spice Girls.

Fast forward nearly two decades, and the annual spectacle has returned to the West Coast for its 2026 edition—with Lima and Swanepoel both back on the catwalk alongside fellow VS veterans Alex Consani, Anok Yai, Ashley Graham, Devyn Garcia, Gigi Hadid, Imaan Hammam, Joan Smalls, Liu Wen, and Paloma Elsesser. Barbara Palvin, who welcomed her first child with Dylan Sprouse just last month, has also returned for her fifth season; after making their debuts last season, Amelia Grey Hamlin, Abby Champion, and Precious Lee have all committed to their sophomore Victoria’s Secret appearances; and Elsa Hosk has signed on for a grand return after an eight-year hiatus.

The event, which you can stream on the official Victoria’s Secret Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube channels, will feature performances from Megan Thee Stallion, Tate McRae, Katseye, and Megan Moroney. And the pink carpet has been just as star-studded as the runway, with Nicole Kidman and her daughter Sunday Rose Kidman Urban, Jordyn Woods, and Tinashe all making an entrance to sit front row.
