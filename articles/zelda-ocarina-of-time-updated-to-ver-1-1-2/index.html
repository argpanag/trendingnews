Zelda: Ocarina of Time updated to Ver. 1.1.2

TheTools · 26.11.2026 04:45 · source · permalink

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time has just been updated to Ver. 1.1.2. You can see the patch notes for this update below.

  • Fixed an issue where the event “Malon’s Friend” could not be progressed. Even if you have already encountered this issue, downloading the update data will allow you to proceed with the event.
  • Fixed several issues to ensure a smoother gameplay experience.
