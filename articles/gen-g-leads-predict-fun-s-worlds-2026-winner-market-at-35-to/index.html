Gen.G Leads Predict.fun’s Worlds 2026 Winner Market at 35% | TokenPost

TheTools · 15.10.2026 05:50 · source · permalink

Hanwha Life Esports was next at 19%. The tournament was scheduled to begin at 2 p.m. ET (18:00 UTC) Oct. 15.

Gen.G led Predict.fun’s market for the 2026 League of Legends World Championship with a 35% implied chance of winning, 16 percentage points higher than the next-listed team.

Hanwha Life Esports stood at 19%. Bilibili Gaming and T1 were tied at 18%, while Dplus KIA had a 5% implied chance of taking the title.

The figures are market probabilities and can shift as users trade. Worlds 2026 was scheduled to begin at 2 p.m. ET (18:00 UTC) Thursday, Oct. 15.
