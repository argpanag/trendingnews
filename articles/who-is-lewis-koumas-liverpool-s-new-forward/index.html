Who is Lewis Koumas, Liverpool’s ‘new’ forward?

TheTools · 11.10.2026 15:00 · source · permalink

The 21-year-old has been handed his first Premier League start as Liverpool face Manchester City with two forwards sidelined by injury.

With Alexander Isak and Cody Gakpo unavailable due to injuries, Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola has handed young forward Lewis Koumas his first Premier League start in Sunday’s eagerly anticipated game against Manchester City.

Koumas gets his chance against Man City

The 21-year-old has already played in three Premier League games so far this season, playing a total of 22 minutes as a substitute. He made his first appearance in the draw at Newcastle United on the opening weekend and then came off the bench in away victories against Ipswich Town and Bournemouth.

Having been an unused substitute in the home draws against Nottingham Forest and Fulham, Koumas is now set to play his first Premier League game at Anfield.

The attacker’s only previous appearance for the first team came in the FA Cup in February 2024, when he scored in a 3-0 fifth-round win over Southampton.

What position does Lewis Koumas play?

Koumas, who has been with the Liverpool academy since November 2016, joining when he was 11, generally plays as a center forward but is also capable of occupying the wide attacking positions on either flank.

In recent seasons, he has spent time on loan in the English Championship, first with Stoke City, for whom he scored six goals in 49 games in 2024-25. Last term, he spent half a season each with Birmingham City, where he scored one goal in 25 appearances, and Hull City, where he scored three goals in 19 matches.

Despite those modest numbers, he has clearly done enough to convince Iraola he deserves to be part of the first-team squad.

International experience

Born in England, Koumas has earned 16 caps for Wales since June 2024, scoring once.

The Liverpool youngster is the son of former Welsh international Jason Koumas, who played in the Premier League for West Bromwich Albion and Wigan Athletic in the 2000s.
