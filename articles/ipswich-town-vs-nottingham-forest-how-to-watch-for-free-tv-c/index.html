Ipswich Town vs Nottingham Forest: Preview

Gary O'Neil's side have begun life back in England's top flight with some respectable displays, no more so than a 3-2 defeat at league leaders Manchester City last weekend.

Before that came a 2-1 win over strugglers Fulham, with two further victories arriving over Crystal Palace and Sunderland.

O'Neil has a virtually full-strength squad available ahead of Friday's clash, with previously sidelined players like Azor Matusiwa, Jaden Philogene, Florentino Luis, and Jack Taylor having all worked their way back to fitness.

The good news for Forest is that star man Morgan Gibbs-White looks set to return against the Tractor Boys. The captain and England international is arguably Glasner’s biggest asset, and another strong season at the City Ground could see him attract interest from further afield.

Despite his absence, there were encouraging signs from the rest of the team against Arsenal. Chris Wood came off the bench and could provide some much-needed competition for Liam Delap, who has converted just one of his 18 shots so far this season.

The underlying metrics are positive for Forest, too. They have had little trouble creating chances, ranking seventh for expected goals (xG) this season. The problem, however, is their lack of clinical finishing: despite an xG of 11.5, they have scored just five goals — the biggest underperformance in the league.
