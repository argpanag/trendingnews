Egypt, Germany, EU hold 2nd Labor Mobility Forum

TheTools · 31.10.2026 18:28 · source · permalink

Egypt’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs, International Cooperation and Egyptian Expatriates, in cooperation with the German Agency for International Cooperation (GIZ) and the European Union (EU), held the second edition of the Labor Mobility Forum 2026 under the theme “Terminal Europe.”

The three-day forum was inaugurated by Deputy Foreign Minister for Migration and Egyptian Expatriates Nabil Habashi, German Ambassador to Egypt Jürgen Schulz, and EU Ambassador to Egypt Angelina Eichhorst. It brought together representatives of 26 German and European institutions and entities for the first time.

Habashi stressed the need to expand safe, regular skilled labor migration and match Egyptian talent with European labor market needs through vocational, language, and cultural training. He highlighted opportunities under the THAMM Plus program and a September 2026 letter of intent with the German state of Saxony to facilitate regular migration.

The “Terminal Europe” activities enabled young Egyptians to engage directly with European institutions and companies to explore employment and training opportunities, qualification recognition procedures, visa processes, and fair recruitment requirements.

The forum also saw the signing of a memorandum of understanding between the Ministry and the German Federal Employment Agency to strengthen skilled labor mobility and align Egyptian talent with Germany’s labor market needs. A letter of intent was also signed with Siemens Healthineers to train and qualify Egyptian personnel in radiology and medical imaging.

Habashi attended the graduation ceremony for the first cohort of a specialized radiology technician training program, comprising 47 technicians.

The ceremony was attended by Mohamed Fawzy, Adviser to the Health Minister for Radiology Affairs, and Amr Kandil, Chairman of Siemens Healthineers in Egypt.

The forum reflects Egypt’s efforts to deepen its partnership with Germany and the EU, develop sustainable pathways for regular migration and skilled labor mobility, and expand decent employment opportunities for young Egyptians. It also aims to strengthen vocational training and cooperation with relevant institutions and the private sector, support shared interests, combat irregular migration, and promote sustainable development.
