Clear Bag Policy

To provide a safer environment for the public and to expedite guest entry into the stadium, Nu Stadium policy limits and restricts the size and type of bags that are permitted into the stadium during any events taking place. We encourage guests NOT to bring any bags, however, the following will be permitted after proper screening:

  • One bag that is clear plastic, vinyl or PVC and do not exceed 12” x 6” 12” or a one-gallon clear plastic freezer bag (Ziplock bag or similar).
  • Small clutch bags, approximately the size of a hand (no larger than 4.5” x 6.5”), with or without a handle or strap, may be carried into the stadium along with one of the clear bag options.
  • Any medically necessary items must be pre-approved by Stadium management. Please contact guestservices@intermiamicf.com to be considered for approval. All items will be subject to proper inspection at entry gate.
  • Diapers and wipes can be carried in a clear bag. Each member of a family including children will be able to carry an approved clear bag, providing ample storage capacity.

Consult Nu Stadium's complete Clear Bag Policy HERE.

Inter Miami CF This Regular Season

Overall, Inter Miami has recorded 15 wins, six losses, and 10 draws so far this MLS regular season for a total 55 points and sits third in the Eastern Conference standings. Notably, Inter Miami has been the top goal-scoring team in MLS this season with 73 total goals in 31 regular season games, seven more than the next highest-scoring side.

Messi, the reigning MLS Landon Donovan MVP Award and Golden Boot presented by Audi winner, leads both the team and the league in goals with 25. Additionally, our captain is tied for the lead for most assists this regular season with 15.

Previously Against FC Cincinnati

Wednesday's match presents the 16th meeting between the sides in Club history. Inter Miami has recorded eight wins, five losses, and two draws to date against FC Cincinnati. 

Inter Miami will look to extend its unbeaten run against Cincinnati to four after posting a draw and two wins in the most recent matchups. Last time out, Inter Miami secured a 3-5 victory on the road in the first encounter between the clubs this regular season in May.

Scouting FC Cincinnati 

FC Cincinnati visits Nu Stadium for the first time afer winning their past two matches. So far this regular season, the Ohio-based side has recorded 11 wins, 11 losses and nine draws for a total 42 points and is eighth in the Eastern Conference table.

Midfielder Evander has been the team's top contributor in attack with 14 goals and 15 assists this regular season, while forward Kevin Denkey is the top scorer with  with 17 goals.
