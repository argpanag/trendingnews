October's Best: Vote for the PNJ's Team of the Month

TheTools · 11.11.2026 18:34 · source · permalink

Decide which of the six nominees was the Pensacola area's best high school sports team in October.

  • Voting for the PNJ's October Team of the Month is now open.
  • Readers can cast their vote from now until 12 p.m. CT on Nov. 17.
  • Nominees include six district champions, one of whom advanced to the Final Four.

October brought plenty of memories for Pensacola-area sports teams.

From district champions to deep postseason runs, teams across Escambia and Santa Rosa County brought home the hardware as fall sports postseason began.

Now it's time to choose the best team from the Pensacola area in October's "Team of the Month" poll. Readers can vote for their top choice for the Pensacola area's best team in October until 12 p.m. CT on Nov. 17. Jay football won the September Team of the Month with 57.7% of the vote, while Tate boys golf took second with 33.9% of the vote.

To nominate a team for future editions email sports reporter Justin Fitzgerald at JFitzgerald@gannett.com.

Navarre volleyball

The Raiders won their first district title in school history with a four-set win over Pace in the District 1-6A final on Oct. 15.

Pensacola Catholic football

The Crusaders played just two games in October, but they were the final two needed to wrap up a third straight District 1-2A title. They beat Freeport 49-7 two days before Hurricane Isaias rolled through the area, then thrashed Walton 38-8 on Oct. 23 to clinch the District 1-2A title, Catholic won its three district games by a combined score of 132-23.

Pensacola Catholic girls golf

The Crusaders won their 15th district title in the 18 years, beating Freeport by six strokes to win the District 1-1A title on Oct. 27. Senior Amelia McIntosh shot 8-over par 80 to finish as the low individual medalist.

Pensacola Catholic volleyball

The Crusaders rolled to a 9-0 October that included a District 1-3A title and back-to-back Final Four appearances. Catholic dropped just one set across the district and regional tournament.

Tate boys golf

The Aggies repeated as District 1-3A champions on Oct. 26, beating Tallahassee Leon by two strokes. Sophomores Jaxson Thomason (4th, 74) and Brycen Massey (6th, 76) both finished in the top 10.

West Florida girls swimming and diving

The Jaguars made it five straight District 1-2A titles on Oct. 30, totaling 399 points to beat Fort Walton Beach by 22.5 points. Freshmen Marcela Dos Santos (200-yard individual medley, 100 butterfly) and Isla Fox (100 breaststroke) and sophomore Sophia Bridges (50 freestyle) won individual district titles and West Florida also won the 200 and 400 freestyle relay.
