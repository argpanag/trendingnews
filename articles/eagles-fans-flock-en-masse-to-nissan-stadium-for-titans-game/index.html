Eagles fans flock en masse to Nissan Stadium for Titans game

TheTools · 20.09.2026 18:08 · source · permalink

In its final season, Nissan Stadium has not seen a visiting fan takeover quite like the one on Sept. 20.

Philadelphia Eagles fans have a huge presence in and around the Titans' home stadium in Nashville ahead of the noon kickoff between the two teams.

Social media posts as early as Sept. 17 this week showed Eagles fans on Nashville-bound flights.

That continued into the weekend, and Eagles fans have flocked here in numbers.

When the Titans entered the field for pre-game introductions, the reaction was mixed with cheers and boos.

Nashville has unsurprisingly been a visiting fan destination before, and notable Titans home games with visiting fans making an impact include 2019 games against the Bills and Chiefs, and the 2012 game against the Bears. The 2024 Vikings game also saw a lot of purple in the Nissan Stadium stands.

We'll see about how the game shakes out in terms of fan presence and noise, but the Eagles will have plenty of support.

It's important to note that the Eagles are 0-3 at Nissan Stadium all-time, including the Titans overtime win in 2018.

And no matter how much green is in the stands, it'll still feel like Nashville in summertime. The National Weather Service forecast calls for temperatures in the mid-to-upper 90s and a heat index past 100.
