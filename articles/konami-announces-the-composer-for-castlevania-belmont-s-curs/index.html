Konami Announces The Composer For Castlevania: Belmont's Curse

TheTools · 07.10.2026 02:15 · source · permalink

From the talent behind the Rogue Prince of Persia soundtrack

As we get closer to the launch of Castlevania: Belmont's Curse later this month, Konami has announced the composer of the new game.

If you didn't already know, this new Castlevania soundtrack was created by composer and musician ASADI (aka Danny Asadi).

Co-developer Evil Empire added to this, noting how Asadi was the only "sound crusader" it could trust. The same team previously called on him to compose the score for Ubisoft's Rogue Prince of Persia soundtrack.

Konami: "As the night falls, a new composition of Bloody Tears fills the night air—a fitting soundtrack for the hunt. We're thrilled to announce the composer for Castlevania: Belmont's Curse, ASADI (@dannyasadi). ASADI's formidable skills as a composer and musician have found a perfect match in Castlevania's musical heritage—new blood revitalises a storied bloodline. We're looking forward to you all hearing the rest of what the game has to offer."
