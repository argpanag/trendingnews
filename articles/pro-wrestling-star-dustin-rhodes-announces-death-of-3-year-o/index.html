Pro wrestling star Dustin Rhodes announces death of 3-year-old grandson

Pro wrestling star Dustin Rhodes suffered an immeasurable loss in his family on Wednesday.

The All Elite Wrestling (AEW) competitor announced on social media his 3-year-old grandson, Rowan, had died. No cause of death was announced.

"Guys my grandson Rowan has passed. Please keep us all in your prayers during this time," he wrote on X, adding that he was with his daughter Dakota.

Terri Runnels, a former WWE star, also shared the news on her Facebook page. Rhodes and Runnels were previously married and had a daughter together.

"On behalf of Dakota, Bleau, and their families, we share the tragic news of the untimely passing of their sweet beautiful boy, Rowan Dane Oney," she wrote on her social media page. "We ask that you respect their privacy at this time. Please no calls or visits. We will post more details soon. Please be in prayer for our family."

Rhodes, the son of Dusty Rhodes and half-brother of Cody Rhodes, had a legendary career in the WWE as Goldust. He was an intercontinental champion three times and a hardcore champion seven times. He’s appeared in several promotions after his run in WWE was finished, including in AEW – where he is now.
