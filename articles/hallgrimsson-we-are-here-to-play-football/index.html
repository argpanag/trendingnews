Hallgrimsson: 'We are here to play football'

TheTools · 27.09.2026 17:39 · source · permalink

Republic of Ireland manager Heimir Hallgrimsson has said "we are here to play football" after the squad arrived for their UEFA Nations League clash with Israel in Hungary tonight.

The Irish team were at the Nagyerdei Stadion in Debrecen at 6pm after a chaotic 48 hours which had seen the fixture put in doubt.

The game will begin at 7.45pm after debate raged for months over whether it should be played.

Ireland will play Israel again next week, as well as Austria in Dublin on Thursday.

Hallgrimsson has chosen his team for tonight's fixture with Gavin Bazunu not in the squad after yesterday’s withdrawal.

Speaking to RTÉ Sport, the Irish boss said: "It has been one of the most challenging weeks for me as a coach. And I guess it’s the same for the players. It’s a situation we haven’t been in before.

"We are here to play football."

"It has been tough. I feel for the players mostly. There are a lot of young players in this squad that we need to take good care of. A lot of players have mixed emotions for this game, it is our job to try to protect all of them.

"The game is going to be played. It is my job to think about the football. That is tough. As you can expect, the preparation has been as bad as it can be. I just hope the players come in here and do a good performance. We are here to play football."

Asked if he hopes the group can be united again in the future after the game, after such a contentious build-up, Hallgrimsson said: "I hope so truly. If we get through this, we will be stronger after. I hope some good will come out of all of this but I know it’s a tough and tricky situation."

Around 225 supporters will be present in the stadium, including 150 Israeli fans.

FAI chief executive David Courrell has said the Irish team will wear black armbands in recognition "of all lives lost in the conflict" and that elements of the pre-match ceremony will not take place.
