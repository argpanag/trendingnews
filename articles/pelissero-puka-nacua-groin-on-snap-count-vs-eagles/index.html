Pelissero: Puka Nacua (groin) on snap count vs. Eagles

    Pelissero: Puka Nacua (groin) on snap count vs. Eagles

    Puka Nacua

    LA Wide Receiver #12
    Player Stats

    Rams WR Puka Nacua (groin) may be on “stricter snap count than usual” in Week 4 against the Eagles, reports the Ringer’s Tom Pelissero.

    Rams HC Sean McVay said Nacua, who has been dealing with a groin injury, is good to go after getting a full practice in on Friday. The coach also noted that they’re still working through Nacua’s snap count and now Pelissero reports that it could be stricter than usual. Fantasy managers should still treat Nacua as a high-end WR1 even in a difficult matchup against the Eagles in Week 4.
    - Nick Shlain
