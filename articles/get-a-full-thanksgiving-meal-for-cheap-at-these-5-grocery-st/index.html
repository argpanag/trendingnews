Get a full thanksgiving meal for cheap at these 5 grocery stores

TheTools · 16.11.2026 20:11 · source · permalink

With Thanksgiving next week, people are starting to stock up on ingredients to make their holiday dinners.

Planning and cooking a Thanksgiving meal can be quite stressful, especially if you find yourself missing an item that's sold out.

For those looking to relieve such stress, a number of grocery stores chains offer Thanksgiving meal bundles that include everything you'll need to make dinner, and at a great price.

Target Thanksgiving meal deal

At Target, you can get a complete Thanksgiving meal for six people for $30, which equates to $5 per person. The meal includes:

  • Butterball Premium Whole Young Turkey
  • Good & Gather Russet Potatoes
  • Ocean Spray Jellied Cranberry Sauce
  • Del Monte French Style Green Beans
  • Campbell's Condensed Cream of Mushroom Soup
  • Good & Gather French Fried Onions
  • Stove Top Stuffing Mix for Turkey
  • Heinz Homestyle Turkey Gravy
  • Favorite Day Take & Bake Baguette

Walmart Thanksgiving meal deal

  • Butterball Frozen Whole Turkey
  • Great Value Cut Green Beans
  • Campbell's Condensed Cream of Mushroom Soup
  • Great Value French Fried Onions Salad Topping
  • Great Value Golden Sweet Whole Kernel Corn
  • Jiffy Corn Muffin Mix
  • Fresh Whole Sweet Potato
  • Jet-Puffed Mini Marshmellows
  • Stove Top Stuffing Mix
  • Fresh Whole Russet Potatoes
  • Great Value Brown Gravy Mix
  • Great Value Brown and Serve White Dinner Rolls
  • Fresh Cranberries
  • Great Value 100% Pure Pumpkin
  • Great Value Evaporated Milk
  • Great Value Frozen Traditional 9-Inch Pie Crusts and Pans
  • Great Value Whipped Topping

Sam's Club Thanksgiving meal deal

Sam's Club is offering its largest Thanksgiving meal yet, with the Maker's Mark meal feeding 16 people for around $160. Here's what comes in the meal:

  • Boneless carver ham
  • Hickory smoked turkey
  • Yukon Gold mashed potatoes
  • Turkey gravy
  • Green beans with dried cranberries and almonds (2)
  • Macaroni and cheese (2)
  • Sweet corn with garlic butter
  • Cranberry crunch salad
  • Yeast dinner rolls
  • Charcuterie entertainment tray
  • 12” pumpkin pies (2)

Aldi offers a Thanksgiving meal that feeds 10 people for under $39. The deal is also customizable, as you can choose what to purchase from Aldi's suggest shopping list. All the items on the list come to a total of $36.11. Here are the items featured in the deal:
