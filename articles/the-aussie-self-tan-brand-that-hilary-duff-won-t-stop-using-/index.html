Full of so many fans radiating love and joy — all while looking the perfect shade of sun kissed.

The secret? Australian self tanning brand, Luna Bronze.

The game-changing instant tan product combined with important skincare is a product that even Hilary can’t go without on tour.

Tanning has become incredibly rooted in Australian culture, but more often than not many formulas leave the skin looking and feeling patchy.

With that overwhelming tan smell that doesn’t come out of your clothes for days.

However, thanks to Australian founded self tan brand, Luna Bronze, Hilary’s concert level glow can be found in our very own backyard.

Make up artist Taylor Healey shared the details behind the signatures iconic ‘Lucky Me’ glow.

“To create the perfect natural-looking tan, I added Luna Bronze bronzing drops into her face moisturiser and Instant Bronzing Cream over the rest of her décolletage and arms,” Healey shared.

And for the founders at Luna Bronze, the support has been nothing short of life changing for the last six years.

Co-founder Rhiannon Hall said that the relationship between the brand and Hilary happened in a completely natural way.

“Hilary has been a organic fan of our products since 2020 which is such an incredible feat for our small Australia business,” Healey said.

“To have someone we grew up idolising become such a genuine, organic supporter of Luna Bronze is incredibly special, and to now be her exclusive tanning partner for the Australian leg of her tour feels like a full-circle moment.”

The products that Hilary can’t go without can be added into your everyday routine or can simply level up your weekly self care ritual.

The Luna Bronze Instant Bronzing Cream is the perfect way for shoppers to get a sun kissed glow in just seconds — without stepping beneath the harsh sun rays.

And the Cosmic Bronzing Drops provide that same instant glow to your face but in a completely customisable way.

Shoppers can add these bronzing drops to their daily moisturiser or beneath their makeup to enhance an already radiant look.

“As a small Australian business, having the opportunity to shine alongside such an icon is something we could have only dreamed of,” Hall added.

“It’s a testament to what we’ve always believed in: that you can achieve a beautiful, confidence-boosting glow without compromising on sun safety. To see that message embraced on a global stage is something we’re incredibly proud of.”

And for Hilary it’s her favourite way to step out onto the stage.
