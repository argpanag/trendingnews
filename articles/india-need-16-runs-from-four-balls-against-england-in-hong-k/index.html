India Need 16 Runs from Four Balls Against England in Hong Kong Sixes

TheTools · 31.10.2026 05:41 · source · permalink

Hyderabad: India need 16 runs from the final four balls against England in their Hong Kong Sixes 2026 match on Saturday.

Chasing England’s target of 125, India reached 109/4 in 5.2 overs at Mission Road Ground, Mong Kok.

Rahul Tripathi led India’s chase with an unbeaten 55 off just 13 balls. His innings included two fours and seven sixes.

Krunal Pandya was batting on 17 off four deliveries. Nitish Rana remained at the crease on four off four balls.

Shashank Singh contributed 29 off nine balls before Nathan Gilchrist dismissed him. Rahul Tewatia also fell for a duck.

England Post 124 Runs in Six Overs

England scored 124/2 in their allotted six overs after India elected to field.

Martin Andersson top-scored with 51 off 13 balls before retiring hurt. His innings featured three fours and six sixes.
