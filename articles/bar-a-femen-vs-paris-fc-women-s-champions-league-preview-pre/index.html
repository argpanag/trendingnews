Barça Femení vs Paris FC: Women's Champions League preview, predictions, team news and TV info

TheTools · 22.09.2026 17:33 · source · permalink

Barcelona Femení have won their only previous encounter with Paris FC; Vicky López and Caroline Graham Hansen on target in a 2-0 victory at Stade Jean-Bouin last December.

Overall, Barça have won all 15 Champions League group stage or league phase home matches, scoring a whopping 77 goals, while Paris FC have lost their last three games against Spanish clubs.

So, ahead of this season's league phase opener at Estadi Johan Cruyff, we've put together a preview as well as predictions, team news and TV listings.

Barça Femení vs Paris FC match preview

Barcelona are set to commence their Champions League title defence, and are overwhelming favourites to retain their title. Back in May, Pere Romeu's team demolished Lyon 4-0 in Oslo, Ewa Pajor and Salma Paralluelo both bagging second half braces, thereby crowned European champions for the fourth time.

Amazingly, the Blaugrana have featured in each of the last six Champions League Finals, winning four of them, hence why most expect they will be at the Stadion Narodowy in Warsaw at the end of this season too. Well, Barça Femení have begun the new Liga F season on fire, winning four matches out of four, scoring 19 goals. Given their domestic dominance, European success is always the priority.

In the league phase, Barcelona will also host Arsenal and Servette Chênois Féminin, as well as taking on Roma, HB Køge and Bayern Munich on the road. Thus, as was the case last season, most expect the defending champions to finish top of the table, starting with a win here.

As for Paris FC, they finished tenth in last season's league phase, before being ousted by Real Madrid in the first knockout round. Thus, last year's Première Ligue runners-up are hoping to better that performance this time round.

Earlier this month, Sandrine Soubeyrand's side were ignominiously smashed 7-1 by serial French champions OL Lyonnes, underlining the gap that exists between Paris FC and Europe's very best. Well, a similarly emphatic scoreline could be on the cards at Estadi Johan Cruyff on Wednesday.

Barça Femení team news

Pere Romeu rotated his team for Friday's 6-0 demolition of Alavés, but the stars will return. These include goalkeeper Cata Coll, Kika Nazareth and Esmee Brugts. Polish striker Ewa Pajor was the top scorer in last season's Champions League with 11.

Assuming Marta Torrejón features, it would be her 90th appearance in the Champions League, equalling the record for a Spanish women, currently held by the recently departed Alexia Putellas.

Paris FC team news

Sandrine Soubeyrand's side were also last in action on Thursday, beating Strasbourg 2-0, reacting to that aforementioned 7-1 drubbing in Lyon. Maeline Mendy bagged a brace in the latter, while Théa Greboval could keep her place at centre-back, chosen over Hillary Diaz.

Predicted Line-ups

  • Barça Femení predicted lineup: Cata Coll; Schertenleib, Fernández, Guijarro, Brugts; Pina, Kika, López; Graham Hansen, Kerolin, Pajor.
  • Paris FC predicted lineup: Chavas; N'Dongala, Ould Hocine, Greboval, Baquerizo; Le Moguédec, Korošec, Mateo, Toloba; Viens, Mendy.

Score prediction: Barça Femení 5-0 Paris FC.
