The day has arrived!

It’s finally here – the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show is back for 2026!

Who’s returning? Barbara Palvin, Rosie Huntington-Whiteley and the $32 million fantasy bra. (We know - HUGE!)

Who’s out? Miranda Kerr said no, and Romee Strijd was rejected. (Don’t remind us.)

And who’s making their debut? The daughter of late NBA legend Kobe Bryant, Natalia.

Stay with us for live updates as they happen.

Angel incoming: Rosie’s comeback after 16 years

Rosie-Huntington Whiteley, who walked in five shows between 2006 and 2010, is dusting off her wings in an “incredibly special” comeback.

Rosie Huntington-Whiteley is making her return to the VS Fashion Show 20 years after her debut. Getty

“I return a different woman from the girl who first stepped onto it. Older, hopefully a little wiser, with a family of my own and a lifetime of experiences I could never have imagined at 19. But with every bit of that same excitement, gratitude and spirit,” she wrote on Instagram.

“I couldn’t be more excited to walk alongside so many beautiful, bold and inspiring women this Sunday and experience all the creativity, fantasy and magic once again.”

The English model said Victoria’s Secret had been “the first major brand to welcome me with open arms and celebrate me just as I was”.

“I return a different woman from the girl who first stepped onto it. Older, hopefully a little wiser,” the British model said on Instagram. Getty Images

“A little wild, full of life and endlessly excited about what the future might hold,” she added.

“I remember being completely starstruck backstage, taking pictures with my favourite Angels, Gisele, Alessandra and Adriana, who so warmly took me under their wings. It was everything I had ever dreamed of and more.”

One iconic Angel is out

Being cast in the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show is a hard-won milestone – but it seems even Angel status won’t guarantee you a spot every time.

Romee Strijd, who walked in five Victoria’s Secret Fashion Shows from 2014-2018 and was named an Angel in 2015, surprised fans last week by revealing she didn’t make the cut this year.

Romee Strijd is a five-time Victoria’s Secret Angel, but she’s been rejected from this year’s show - much to the shock of fans. Getty

The Dutch model detailed her rejection from the event after five weeks of uncertainty and being “kept in the dark”. She had travelled to New York to take part in the casting, feeling this year was the right time to return.

In a TikTok video, a tearful Strijd said she’d finally received a verdict via her agency, and was told “[the team] thought you wouldn’t fit in the show this year”.

“I don’t feel supported by my agency. I don’t feel like I have the straight story. Just be honest, just tell me, and I can go on with my life and let it go ... if it’s a closed chapter, I just would love to know,” she said.

“I don’t think I’m gonna cast any more in the future, because I don’t like the way it went. I don’t like the way I was treated, and I would love to keep the positive memories.”

While Strijd dealt with rejection, one of her fellow former Angels is celebrating her return to the show 20 years after debut on the VS runway.

Recognise her?

Charli XCX? No, it’s Amelia Gray Hamlin. Getty Images

You’re not fooling us with those sunnies, Amelia Gray Hamlin!

The daughter of Real Housewife of Beverly Hills’ Lisa Rinna and actor Harry Hamlin is returning to the VS runway for a second time.

Selfie time!

‘I’m back, bitches’: Angel’s return six weeks after giving birth to first child

Barbara Palvin has walked the Victoria’s Secret runway six times, but this year’s show will be unlike any other – it’s her first since becoming a mum.

The Hungarian model welcomed her first child with husband Dylan Sprouse, a little girl named Sylvie, six weeks ago.

She’s back! Barbara Palvin is getting back into her Angel wings six weeks after welcoming her first child. Getty

Over the weekend, her return to the VS Fashion Show was confirmed via Instagram.

“Motherhood looks good on you. So does the runway – see you in LA,” the official Victoria’s Secret account captioned a video of Palvin with a pram.

“I’m back, bitches,” the model says in the video, with a cheeky wink to the camera.

The announcement has sparked plenty of discussion, with the idea of a new mum returning to work just six weeks postpartum proving divisive.

This will mark the Hungarian model’s seventh time walking the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show runway. Variety via Getty Images

Speaking to Page Six behind the scenes before the show, Palvin defended the decision.

“I tell everyone, it’s normal to be back, maternity leave is six to eight weeks,” she said.

“I’m six weeks tomorrow, so I’m just doing what everyone’s doing ... and I only have to walk down a runway.”

The added that she’s feeling healthy and has let her body “heal on its own”, doing “nothing crazy” in preparation for the show.

Surprise! The Fantasy Bra is back

After weeks of speculation, the cat’s out of the bag: Yes, the Fantasy Bra is making a return to the VS runway after eight years!

The designer at Jacob & Co. said they wanted to “make this the most expensive bra ever made”. While we don’t have an official price tag, we know the centre diamond will set you back a cheeky $32 million.

The Fantasy Bra, seen here on Karolina Kurkova, was an iconic part of the VS Fashion Show until its hiatus began in 2018. Getty

The glittering showpiece, worth millions of dollars and worn by some of the most iconic Angels through the years, has been missing in action since the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show returned from its five-year hiatus in 2024.

The Fantasy Bra dates back to 1996, when Victoria’s Secret created a tradition of having one lucky Angel model a bespoke luxury bra encrusted with millions of dollars worth of gems and jewels during each show.

2018 was the last time we saw one grace the runway, with Elsa Hosk given the honour of wearing the Dream Angels Fantasy Bra.

The lingerie brand announced the return of the Fantasy Bra in an Instagram post over the weekend.

View post on Instagram
 

“Designed in partnership with Victoria’s Secret, and created by @jacobandco, this one-of-a-kind masterpiece is set to make fashion history all over again,” they said.

Now, a mystery remains: Who will have the honour of wearing it this year?

Natalia Bryant is joining the crew! Getty Images

Miranda Kerr turns down historic VS return

Australia’s golden girl Miranda Kerr has turned down the opportunity to walk in the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show 20 years after she first hit that famous runway.

Kerr was just 23 when she made her VS debut and was honoured to be invited back for the 2026 show, but chose not to return.

Miranda Kerr turned down the chance to return to the VS runway 20 years after her debut. Instagram/@mirandakerr

“Looking back with so much gratitude for the friendships, the magical memories and for everyone who made that chapter of my life so special,” she wrote in a statement on Instagram.

“It meant so much to be invited to return this year, especially as it marks 20 years since my first show…. but I ultimately felt in my heart it was right to cherish this chapter as it was.

“Sending so much love and light to everyone in this year’s show!”

Kerr, 43, shared the statement alongside a series of snaps from her VS runway appearances through the years.
