Every word from Arteta's post-Brighton presser

TheTools · 19.09.2026 22:51 · source · permalink

Mikel Arteta could not hide his disappointment after we fell to our first defeat of the season on Saturday afternoon.

Brighton ran out 3-0 winners at the Amex Stadium, and afterwards the manager met the media, to discuss the performance, the start to the season, changes and more. 

Here’s every word from his post-match press conference.  

On the immediate feeling after the game…

Extremely disappointed. But first I think we have to congratulate Brighton for the victory. I think they deserve it, I think they were the better team. From our side, there were certain basics you need to accomplish in football, especially in the Premier League that we were very far from. Winning the first duel, the second ball, that put to the game, especially how direct they were in a really difficult place for us. On top of that, especially in our own half, we didn’t respect any of the principles of play to get out of that man-to-man situation. That puts us on the back foot, so it was very difficult to control the game. When we had moments of dominance and control, we did open them up, but we didn’t finish those actions with the quality that is required to compensate for the things we did wrong. So overall, they deserved to win the game, and it’s a big lesson for us.

On how tough every Premier League game is…

I think we knew how difficult it was going to be today. The first thing we discussed was the first part - if you don’t get out of that first pass as winners, it becomes very difficult to dominate the game and to win the game. That’s the game - credit to them for overcoming us and having another edge to what we did. At this level it’s extremely important to understand that before anything else.

On why the performance was uncharacteristic of previous matches…

Firstly, because Brighton were really good, they had another bite and their timing was better. There are a lot of moments when the ball is 50/50 and it changes hands, and most of the time it was for them. They opened us up in a really difficult way, that’s something to learn. That’s not going to take away what this team has done, and what the boys have done in the last six or seven weeks since coming back from the World Cup, because with 18 players coming back, the way we’ve competed and the number of games we have won has been remarkable. But this is a big lesson on what it takes to win at this level

On whether he felt something was off early in the game…

You could see from the first throw-in, it was so clear. We put ourselves in trouble one, two, three times. We need to get out much quicker than that, and that’s one of the biggest lessons we have to take from the game today.

On if he was tempted to make changes at half-time…

I was, but I believed it wasn’t the right moment to do that with the things we wanted to do in the second half, and that decision was made.

On if he sees Declan Rice and Bruno Guimaraes as a long-term midfield duo…

It’s one of them, and it worked extremely well when we played Sunderland away and managed to win the game, and today it didn’t work so well for a lot of things that happened behind them as well to make sure they could play a completely different game. It’s something that will for sure evolve, they’ve only played together a few minutes in these early stages, and that relationship will for sure improve and give us a lot of joy.

On how this league cannot be easily won…

That for us crystal clear. We knew when we talked for two days what we had to do here. We’ve played our fourth away game in a very short space of time, and we know what it takes to win every single match. We know what it has to take to beat Leeds when we come back. That’s for sure not a surprise, but doesn’t take away from the disappointment and emotional state we have right now.

On what was going through his mind when each goal went in…

Ways to be able to change it. Okay, we started to get more control and dominance for another action, but that gave them more belief and more momentum again. When you get into those moments, it’s extremely difficult to be able to change it. We tried to change it at half-time, we started the second half and then we made two or three really difficult decisions that put us under more pressure. By the time we got some momentum, it was probably too late.
