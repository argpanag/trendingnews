Barcelona Face Athletic Club in Liga F Leaders Clash

TheTools · 22.11.2026 17:44 · source · permalink

Barcelona Femení face a demanding Liga F test against Athletic Club on Sunday, November 22, 2026, as Pere Romeu's side welcome the fourth-placed visitors to Estadi Johan Cruyff at 19:00. The Catalan leaders aim to strengthen their position before the international break, while Athletic seek another significant result against the competition's strongest side.

According to the match preview published by Yahoo Noticias, Barcelona enter the 12th round with 28 points from 11 matches. Their record stands at nine victories, one draw and one defeat, placing them at the top of the standings.

Athletic Club occupy fourth place with 19 points. The Bilbao side have demonstrated their competitiveness against leading opponents, most recently defeating Real Sociedad 4-1 away from home after previously drawing 1-1 against Real Madrid, according to Mundo Deportivo.

Barcelona, meanwhile, dropped points in their previous league fixture after drawing away against Deportivo de A Coruña. Their only other league setback came against Real Sociedad, although the Catalan team also secured a convincing Champions League victory during the following week.

Romeu will have to manage several absences for the encounter. Kika Nazareth, Vicky López, Laia Aleixandri and Kerolin Nicoli remain unavailable, while Laia Cabetas has also been left out of the squad compared with Barcelona's previous league selection.

Athletic head coach Víctor Martín has acknowledged the difficulty of facing Barcelona but believes his players are prepared for the challenge. Speaking to Mundo Deportivo, he highlighted the Catalan team's quality and warned against judging their strength solely through recent domestic results.

"They are a tremendous team. We cannot base our assessment solely on results. This week they competed in the Champions League and won comfortably.

I did not like them as much as in previous matches. They had come from two draws, and there is too much result-oriented thinking; that is what we live by. If we evaluate performances based on a draw or a victory...

We know they are the best team in the world and have the best coach in the world. It will be difficult, but we are prepared," said Víctor Martín, Athletic Club's head coach.

Martín also cautioned against allowing his team's recent positive results to create unrealistic expectations. Athletic have shown improvement, but he believes their performances still require a balanced assessment.

"We must not get carried away at all. We have had matches in which we created chances but failed to score. We need to maintain a balanced assessment, especially from my position," Martín said.

The Athletic manager expects concentration and discipline to be essential throughout the contest. He believes Barcelona's individual quality could punish even small defensive mistakes.

"We have to do many things well, make sure they are not at their best, and avoid being carried away by emotions. We must remain attentive. They have players who have won Ballon d'Or awards and can make the difference.

We need to maintain an extremely high level of concentration. It is a very interesting challenge to expose ourselves to that level of competition," Martín said.

The match represents Barcelona's final club competition commitment before the international break. The encounter will take place at Estadi Johan Cruyff, with coverage scheduled on TV3, Teledeporte, DAZN, RTVE Play and 3Cat, according to Yahoo Noticias.
