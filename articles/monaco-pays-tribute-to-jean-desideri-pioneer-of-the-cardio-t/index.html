Monaco Pays Tribute to Jean Desideri, Pioneer of the Cardio-Thoracic Center

TheTools · 19.09.2026 16:37 · source · permalink

Jean Desideri played a key role in the creation and development of the Monaco Cardio-Thoracic center (Centre Cardio-Thoracique de Monaco), working alongside renowned cardiac surgeon Professor Vincent Dor. A religious ceremony in his memory will be held in Nice on September 22.

In 2012, when the Centre Cardio-Thoracique de Monaco published a magazine to mark its 25th anniversary, Jean Desideri recalled the phone call that first drew him into the project conceived by renowned cardiac surgeon Vincent Dor.

"While you’re comfortably settled in your office and the minutes tick by, filled with the usual day-to-day concerns, the phone rings, jolts you out of your routine, and suddenly plunges you into a new world."

It was 1980. "That’s how I received a call from Professor Dor, inviting me to help create a center in Europe specializing in the treatment of thoracic and cardiovascular diseases."

A Pioneer of the Project

Professor Dor found the support he needed to bring his vision to life in the Principality, with the backing of Prince Rainier III and Princess Grace.

Inspired by his experience in the United States, the project introduced an innovative model of specialized care and brought Jean Desideri from his role as director of Hôpital Lenval in Nice to the helm of the CCM.

He would go on to help strengthen Monaco’s medical reputation and international profile.

Professor Dor himself described Desideri as "one of the project’s pioneers" and a "hospital administrator."

Related stories

Desideri later served as Executive Chairman of the investment company behind the Cardio-Thoracic center, overseeing, among other responsibilities, the expansion of the CCM in the early 2000s.

In recognition of his service to Monaco and to the Sovereign, he was appointed an Officer of the Order of Saint-Charles in 2009.

A religious ceremony in his memory will be held on Tuesday, September 22, at the Cimiez Monastery in Nice.

The editorial team at Monaco-Matin extends its condolences to his family and loved ones.
