Orlando Magic at Washington Wizards: Game Preview | Orlando Magic

TheTools · 23.11.2026 00:11 · source · permalink

Over the course of their last six games, the Orlando Magic are one of the hottest teams in the NBA.

During that span, Orlando is tied for the second-best record in the league, winning five of those outings.

The Magic (7-8) look to keep that momentum rolling when they visit the Washington Wizards (6-11) on Sunday at 7 p.m. ET.

“Us playing on a string, communicating, and also listening,” said Magic center Wendell Carter Jr. “That’s going to be a big thing for us tonight.”

The vibes are high for Orlando after it opened its two-game road trip with a thrilling victory in Brooklyn. Franz Wagner capped off a sensational showing by knocking down a game-winning jumper with 2.4 seconds remaining to give the Magic a 113-111 victory.

“The biggest thing for us is to make sure we keep doing a great job in some of these areas where we’ve been improving upon,” said Magic head coach Sean Sweeney. “Understanding that it’s hard to win on the road, it’s hard to win period in this league. So, when you get a win like that, where you have to gut it out, you’ve got to take that and understand that it can give you some confidence in yourselves. But now, it’s back to building those fundamentals, building those habits.”

In order to record a triumph in the nation’s capital, Orlando will look to put forth another balanced effort. Over the last six games, the Magic have the association’s sixth-best net rating (7.6), ranking both top 10 in offense and defense.

But still, Orlando feels it has room to improve.

“It starts with our communication and our energy,” said Magic guard Desmond Bane. “Sustaining our energy throughout the game. Offensive rebounding (is) an area where we’ve been slipping in as of late. And then, just being disciplined offensively with our spacing and our running habits. All the things that we’ve done, just do them better and do them at a high level.”

Washington, meanwhile, has won back-to-back games by holding its opponents under 100 points. The Wizards are one of the best units in the NBA at guarding the 3-point line, holding opposing teams to 33.2 percent shooting from beyond the arc.

Even though they surrender the second-most points in the paint (52.8) per game in the league, they are a threat to reject shots at the rim. Washington blocks 5.6 shots per outing, tied for the sixth most in the association.

Per Sportradar, among qualifying squads, Washington is the only team with four players averaging at least one swat per contest: Alex Sarr (1.3), Kyshawn George (1.2), Anthony Davis (1.1) and Bilal Coulibaly (1.1).

“We talked about just doing what we do,” said Bane. “I think we’ve done a good job this season of being aggressive. Touch the paint, and if it’s not there, then we have opportunities to kick the ball out to our shooters. So, just continuing to lean into that.”

IN AND OUT: Paolo Banchero (right high ankle sprain), Jonathan Isaac (left knee injury management), Izaiyah Nelson (left ankle injury recovery) and Noah Penda (right partially torn hamstring) are out for Orlando.

For Washington, Reece Beekman (right AC joint sprain / separation), Justin Champagnie (right foot surgery), Tristan Vukcevic (left hip impingement) and Jamir Watkins (left ACL repair / surgery) are out.

KEY STATS: Wagner now has 16 career games with 30-plus points, five-plus rebounds and five-plus assists (four times this season). After his latest performance, he moved past Penny Hardaway for the third-most 30-five-and-five games in Magic history.
