Adam Wolke, the German livestreamer who spent over ten years building the Skyline TV brand, has died at 36.

In an Instagram post from Tuesday (Oct. 20), Wolke's partner, Verena Ullrich, and his close friend Jens "Knossi" Knossalla, announced the untimely news. "It is with deep sadness that we must inform you that our Adam has sadly passed away," Ullrich wrote, according to TMZ.

Ullrich said the late internet personality died from a hemorrhage caused by a ruptured cerebral aneurysm.

"We were by his side until the very end and accompanied him on his final journey," she wrote. "We are deeply shaken and still can hardly put into words what has happened."
