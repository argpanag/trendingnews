Ben Shelton vs. Rafael Jodar Predictions & Best Bets

TheTools · 19.11.2026 20:15 · source · permalink

Having won one of his first two matches in the John McEnroe Group at the ATP Finals, American favourite Ben Shelton will be targeting the victory that would see him book his place in the semi-finals when he takes on Rafael Jodar on Thursday.

Shelton battled past Arthur Fils in his opening assignment in Turin, winning in three sets, but was no match for world number one Alexander Zverev on Tuesday as he limped to a 6-2 7-6 defeat.

But the American knows a win over Jodar would see him reach the semi-finals and his Spanish opponent has already lost to both Zverev and Fils on his ATP Finals debut.

Shelton would have learned plenty from the experience of his own debut in last season’s ATP Finals when he suffered a group-stage exit, and it has certainly been a year to remember for the Atlanta-born star.

The 24-year-old made his first Grand Slam final on home soil at the US Open in September and, although he was beaten in four sets by Zverev, it suggested that a high-profile success may not be too far away.

Jodar has made huge strides in a short period of time but his level has dipped on the back of a long season and defeats to Zverev and Fils suggest he may be ready for a freshen up.

Despite being two top-10 performers, Shelton and Jodar get set for their first meeting and everything points to a comfortable success for the American.

Shelton thrives under quick conditions and rediscovered some winning form in the build-up to the ATP Finals, finishing runner-up to Carlos Alcaraz in Paris.

A US Open runner-up to Zverev in September, Shelton is capable of beating anyone on his day and this is a match he must win in order to make the knockout stages.

That should ensure motivation is high and, having been beaten by Zverev and Fils in Turin, Jodar has lost four of his last five matches.

The Spaniard comes with a lofty reputation but his ranking may be slightly flattering at this early stage of his career and this was his first season on the ATP Tour.

Jodar made only one quarter-final from the four Grand Slam events and Shelton should have too much tactical nous.

Although versatile regarding surface, clay would see Jodar at his strongest currently while Shelton is a hard-court specialist blessed with a potent serve.

Shelton has had a habit of going to a deciding set recently but he knows a commanding performance is required and he can stamp his authority in a 2-0 win.

Big serving and utilising his powerful forehand are what Shelton is all about and he will need those attributes to come to the fore if he is to succeed.

Shelton doesn’t rely solely on his serve but it is a dangerous tool in his armoury and that was clear to see in his opening two assignments in Turin.

The American had 12 aces in his three-set win over Fils in his opening clash and then he had 15 against world number one Zverev, which was despite losing in straight sets and winning only eight service games.

Zverev fired in 10 aces in his 6-4 6-4 win over Jodar and there is every possibility that Shelton can surpass the 13 required to land this wager.

Getting off to a quick start is imperative for Shelton and he should be confident with Jodar feeling the effects of a long season, losing four of his last five matches.

Shelton is an extremely difficult man to break whereas Jodar looks to be operating way below the level he was earlier in the campaign.

With that in mind, it may be worth backing Shelton to win the first set 6-3 or 6-4.

Jodar serves big, too, so won’t be completely overawed but none of his last four matches have featured a tiebreak.

Zverev won the first set 6-4 when beating Jodar and Shelton can take the opener in this match in similar style.
