A Grand Traverse County man is now a millionaire after he won the $6.97 million Lotto 47 jackpot from the Michigan Lottery.

The 48-year-old man, who chose to remain anonymous, matched the six Lotto 47 numbers drawn—04-16-28-30-36-37—in the Aug. 15 drawing to win his jackpot.

He bought the winning ticket at Northland Food Center, located at 2377 East M 113 in Kingsley.

"I play Lotto 47 and some of the other draw games regularly because my theory is that you can't win if you don't play," said the player. "I got on the Michigan Lottery app to check the winning numbers the day after the drawing. When I saw I matched all six and won the $6.79 million jackpot, I wanted to scream! I kept blinking and thinking to myself: 'This can't be right.' It was unreal."

He chose to receive his winnings as a one-time lump sum of about $4.7 million.

With his winnings, the man said he plans to buy a house, invest and take a few trips.

The $6.79 million jackpot was the largest Lotto 47 jackpot won this year.
