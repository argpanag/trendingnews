  1. Report: Verstappen dominates for first grand prix win in Singaporepublished at 16:10 BST
    16:10 BST

    Andrew Benson
    BBC F1 correspondent in Singapore

    Red Bull's Max Verstappen took his second dominant victory within a week as he secured his first win in the Singapore Grand Prix.

    Verstappen fought off an attack from Ferrari's Charles Leclerc off the start line to control the race from start to finish.

    The win followed his and Red Bull's maiden victory of the year in Malaysia last weekend and sprint race success in Singapore on Saturday, confirming the team's re-emergence as a major force.

    Leclerc took second as team-mate Lewis Hamilton finished sixth after a baffling strategy that was a consequence of the Briton choosing not to pit when asked.

    Hamilton, trying to last out on a set of soft tyres for a mammoth 44 laps, lost out in the closing laps to Mercedes' Kimi Antonelli and the McLarens of Lando Norris and Oscar Piastri.

  8. Vasseur - 'We called them both to pit'published at 15:55 BST
    15:55 BST

    Ferrari team principal Fred Vasseur speaking to Sky Sports after the race: "Overall it's a good weekend, we did P2 and P3 yesterday in the sprint with wet, P2, P3 in quali and P2 and we are not far away from P3 also today.
