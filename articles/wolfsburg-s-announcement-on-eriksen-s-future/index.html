Wolfsburg’s announcement on Eriksen’s future

The Danish midfielder, who collapsed again last June and has not played since, has terminated his contract with the German club

Christian Eriksen reached an agreement with Wolfsburg on Wednesday to terminate his contract, nearly four months after the Danish midfielder collapsed on the field for the second time in his career, the German club announced.

Eriksen, 34, has not played since collapsing after clutching his chest during Denmark’s friendly against Ukraine in Odense, Denmark, on June 7. His contract had been due to expire in June 2027.

Dieter Hecking, Wolfsburg’s sporting director, explained that the club wanted to give Eriksen the freedom to decide his own future.

Eriksen said in a statement released by Wolfsburg: “At this moment, it is important for me to be close to my family in Denmark. I am glad that we were able to find a solution together. It was a great pleasure for me to play for you, and I will look back fondly on this period.”

Eriksen’s Second Collapse Revived Memories of Euro 2020

The incident in June brought back memories of the cardiac arrest Eriksen suffered during Euro 2020, which was held in 2021 because of the pandemic.

On June 12, 2021, his teammates formed a protective circle around him as he received medical attention during Denmark’s match against Finland. Eriksen subsequently had an implantable cardioverter defibrillator fitted, allowing him to resume his playing career.

Following his collapse against Ukraine on June 7, Eriksen said later that month that he was doing well and that the defibrillator implanted in 2021 had worked as intended.

The midfielder subsequently began his rehabilitation in Denmark.

The former Tottenham, Inter Milan, Brentford and Manchester United player joined Wolfsburg as a free agent ahead of the 2025-26 season, which ended with the German club’s relegation to the second division.
