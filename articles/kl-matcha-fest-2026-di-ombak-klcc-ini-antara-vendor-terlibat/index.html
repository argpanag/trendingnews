KL Matcha Fest 2026 Di Ombak KLCC: Ini Antara Vendor Terlibat & Menu Menarik Ditawarkan | TRP

TheTools · 16.09.2026 05:51 · source · permalink

Matcha Walk KL Matcha Festival 2026 akan berlangsung dari 16 hingga 20 September di Concourse, Ombak KLCC, Kuala Lumpur.

  • KL Matcha Fest 2026 will be held from 16 to 20 September at Concourse, Ombak KLCC, featuring over 25 matcha vendors.
  • Vendors include popular names like Matcha Eight, Keyes Bakehouse, and Warabimochi Kamakura, offering exclusive matcha menus and products.
