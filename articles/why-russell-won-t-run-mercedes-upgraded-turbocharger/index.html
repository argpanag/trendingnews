Why Russell won't run Mercedes' upgraded turbocharger

TheTools · 19.11.2026 03:03 · source · permalink

World Champion Kimi Antonelli is set to run with Mercedes' upgraded turbocharger at this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix, but team mate George Russell will not.

Mercedes are set to launch their upgraded turbocharger at this weekend's Las Vegas Grand Prix, but World Champion Kimi Antonelli is the only factory team driver set to run it.

The newly-crowned World Champions were permitted one upgrade on the power unit as part of the Additional Development and Upgrade Opportunities (ADUO) mechanism.

They focused that work on their turbocharger and ahead of the Las Vegas Grand Prix, round 21 of the World Championship, the new part is ready for use by their factory team and customers, with rivals McLaren set to run it on both their cars.

Antonelli can take the new turbocharger without getting hit with a penalty, however his team mate George Russell has already used five of that part and thus would get a five-place penalty for the use of a sixth.

Following discussions internally, Mercedes do not feel it's worth taking the hit on Russell's starting position as they battle to help him secure second in the Drivers' Championship where he trails Max Verstappen by 17 points.

"This is primarily about proving out the development in race-weekend conditions, with the expected performance benefit relatively small," said Mercedes boss Toto Wolff.

"We anticipate running it with Kimi, whilst with George our priority is protecting his grid position with the performance benefit likely too limited to justify taking a grid penalty."

He added: "Our targets are clear: helping George finish as high as possible in the Drivers’ Championship, ending the year with momentum, and continuing to unlock more performance from the car.

"There is more work to do on that front, and the final three races give us three more opportunities to learn and improve."

Mercedes have won 12 times this season in the Grand Prix format, but their advantage over the field has reduced as the season has gone on - with just one of those wins coming in the last five rounds. This latest upgrade is just one of many steps to help them refine their 2027 project.

With both championships now sorted, after Antonelli became F1's youngest champion in Mexico with the team wrapping up their first Teams' Championship since 2021 last time out in Brazil, Mercedes' focus is on helping Russell take P2 and making sure the team are on a good foot for their title defences next year.

With three rounds still to go, Russell is very much in the fight for P2 as 33 points separate Verstappen, Russell, Lewis Hamilton, Charles Leclerc and Lando Norris with a maximum of 75 on the table.

Since the Las Vegas Grand Prix returned to the calendar in 2023, Mercedes have won once with Russell coming out on top in 2024.
