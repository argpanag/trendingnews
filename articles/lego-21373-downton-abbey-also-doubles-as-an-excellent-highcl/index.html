LEGO 21373 Downton Abbey also doubles as an excellent Highclere Castle model - Jay's Brick Blog

TheTools · 10.09.2026 13:00 · source · permalink

LEGO has officially unveiled the new LEGO 21373 Downton Abbey set, the 12th LEGO Ideas set of 2026.

Celebrating the hit ITV historical drama, this massive 4,711-piece model lets you build a minifigure-scale version of the historical Highclere Castle, the main location where Downton Abbey is set.

Like the series, the LEGO Downton Abbey model also explores the “upstairs, downstairs” dynamics of the aristocratic Crawley family, and their servants in the sprawling Yorkshire country estate.

The set also comes with a whopping 13 minifigures, including the Crawley family, Tom Branson, Mr. Carson, Mrs. Hughes, Mrs. Patmore, Daisy Mason and more!

21373 Downton Abbey will be released on 1 October 2026 (via LEGO Insiders Early Access) ahead of a 4 October general relase, and will be available from LEGO.com or your local LEGO Store.

The LEGO Downton Abbey model has quite a large facade, measuring 44cm (17.5 in.) wide and 30cm (12 in.) tall. Even if you don’t watch or know much about Downton Abbey, the set easily doubles as a historical model celebrating grand English country houses and mansions, complete with the bath stone facade, and period-accurate architecture.

The LEGO Downton Abbey minifigure lineup is superb, and I’m really glad that LEGO threw in 13 minifigures in total, which will go a long way to filling up the interiors. The dresses, and handsome suits and jackets are also fantastic, and will be incredibly useful for LEGO minifigure customisers.

You get: The Dowager Countess, Lord Grantham, Lady Grantham, Lady Mary, Lady Edith, Tom Branson, Mr Carson, Mrs Hughes, Mr Bates, Thomas Barrow, Mrs Patmore, Anna Bates and Daisy Parker

The set is a doll-house type model, with an open back for easier access to the detailed interiors which include the dining room, main hall and library on the upper level, and the kitchen, servants’ hall and Mr Carson’s office.
