Will Ashley, Mika Salamanca, Vince Maristela remind new housemates to show true selves inside Bahay ni Kuya

TheTools · 24.10.2026 10:40 · source · permalink

With a new batch of housemates ready to enter Bahay ni Kuya for Pinoy Big Brother Collab 3.0, three former boarders shared some helpful reminders on how they will immerse inside the PBB house.

In an interview with Will Ashley, Mika Salamanca, and Vince Maristela with GMA Interviews, the trio was in unison in voicing out that showing their authentic self is important in this journey.

Will emphasized to the new housemates that they should focus on being natural the moment they stepped inside the PBB house.

“Don't plan kasi the more you plan, hindi lalabas 'yung totoong personality mo. Enjoyin mo lang talaga kung ano 'yung meron sa loob ng bahay lalo na't bagong bahay 'yung papasukan nila ngayon, panibagong experience.”

Mika added that the new housemates should focus on appreciating the experience of being an official housemate.

The Sparkle actress explained to 24 Oras, “And I-enjoy mo muna 'yung nasa loob kasi pagkalabas mo, wala kang magagawa kung hindi harapin 'yung labas. Kaya hanggang meron kang 'shield na Bahay ni Kuya, live there [and] enjoy it. Mahalin mo 'yun, kasi wala kang choice 'pag labas mo, hindi 'yun 'yung makikita mo.”

For his part, Vince Maristela credited the big change in his life when he joined PBB. “Iniba niya talaga 'yung buhay ko.”

He continued that they should not worry too much about what the people outside the house would think of their actions. “Lahat sinasabi nila na ang tunay na laban nasa labas ng Bahay ni Kuya. Totoong totoo 'yun! And pagdating mo sa loob ng Bahay ni Kuya, para sa akin, kailangan mo talaga mag-immerse.”

The 26-year-old hunk reiterated that showing their true colors inside Bahay ni Kuya is essential.

“Kailangan mo ipakita 'yung totoong ikaw, 'yung sarili mo, 'yung personality mo. Huwag mo isipin na baka hindi ako magustuhan ng mga tao kapag ganito ako, ganyan,” Vincent stressed.

Will, Mika, and Vince are all part of the new afternoon series Delivery Boi airing at 3:20 p.m. on GMA Afternoon Prime.
