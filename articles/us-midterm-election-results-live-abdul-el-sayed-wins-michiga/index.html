What are midterms and what do they decide?

The midterm elections are held in the middle of each four-year presidential term. However, by definition, they do not involve the president. They can be an opportunity for voters to grade the president’s policies and performance by his term’s first two years.

The midterm results will decide which political party – Republicans or Democrats – would control the Congress and how easy it would be for the President to carry out his policies and plans.

Key issues in midterms

While the ongoing war between Iran and the United States is a major focus, the American economy remains a significant issue for voters in the midterm elections.

Immigration is another major issue as Trump’s Republican administration runs a tight ship. Trump’s trade policies, healthcare, and his leadership would also play a key role in the midterms.
