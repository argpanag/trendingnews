Hurricanes determined to reward home faithful | Hobart Hurricanes

TheTools · 21.11.2026 01:13 · source · permalink

The Hobart Hurricanes will be desperate to give their loyal home supporters something to cheer about when they take to Ninja Stadium for the final time in WBBL|12 tomorrow.

After a frustrating campaign which has failed to deliver the results the side had hoped for, the Hurricanes are determined to finish their home schedule on a positive note and reward the fans who have stuck by them throughout the season.

The Hurricanes have named an unchanged 13-player squad for the clash, with captain Nicola Carey again leading the side as it searches for a breakthrough victory.

Despite the disappointment of a challenging season, the Hurricanes will be eager to put on a strong performance in front of their home crowd before turning their attention to the remaining fixtures of the tournament.

Fans are also welcome to join the Happi on the Hill special Pilates session which take place at 10:40am. Registrations are essential, and it includes your entry ticket. Find out more about Happi on the Hill here.
