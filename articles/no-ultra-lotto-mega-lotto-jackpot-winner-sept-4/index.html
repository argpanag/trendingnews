No Ultra Lotto, Mega Lotto jackpot winner Sept. 4

TheTools · 05.09.2026 05:56 · source · permalink

MANILA, Philippines—No bettor won the jackpots in the Ultra Lotto 6/58 and Mega Lotto 6/45 draws Friday, Sept. 4, according to the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office.

The Ultra Lotto 6/58 winning combination was 53-34-12-09-05-47 for a jackpot of ₱265.47 million. No ticket matched all six numbers.

The Mega Lotto 6/45 winning combination was 26-38-17-35-11-16, with a jackpot of ₱41.6 million. There was also no jackpot winner.

Minor games

PCSO also released the results of the day’s minor lotto draws:

  • 4D Lotto: 3-7-9-6 — ₱112,081; 8 winners
  • 3D Lotto 2 p.m.: 0-8-2 — ₱4,500; 85 winners
  • 3D Lotto 5 p.m.: 4-9-6 — ₱4,500; 97 winners
  • 3D Lotto 9 p.m.: 4-2-7 — ₱4,500; 406 winners
  • 2D Lotto 2 p.m.: 14-17 — ₱4,000; 206 winners
  • 2D Lotto 5 p.m.: 21-23 — ₱4,000; 118 winners
  • 2D Lotto 9 p.m.: 24-08 — ₱4,000; 767 winners

Draw schedule

Ultra Lotto 6/58 draws are held every Tuesday, Friday and Sunday, while Mega Lotto 6/45 draws take place every Monday, Wednesday and Friday.
