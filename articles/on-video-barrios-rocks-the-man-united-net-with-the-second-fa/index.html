On video: Barrios rocks the Man United net with the second-fastest goal in Atletico's history | Goal.com

TheTools · 13.10.2026 21:08 · source · permalink

A historic shock goal

Pablo Barrios wrote his name into Atletico Madrid's Champions League history on Tuesday night, beating the Manchester United goalkeeper inside the opening minute of their second league phase fixture.

The midfielder struck after just 49 seconds. That makes it the second fastest goal in the Spanish club's Champions League history, and the third fastest United have ever shipped in the competition.

Mister Chip's numbers put Conor Gallagher top of Atletico's list in the current Champions League era, his effort against Real Madrid in 2025 arriving after 28 seconds. Barrios slots in second, with Koke's one minute and 29 seconds strike against Monaco in 2018 third.

United's own unwanted list reads differently. Alessandro Del Piero needed only 20 seconds for Juventus in 1997, and Christian Gimenez 31 seconds for Basel in 2002. Barrios is third.

Sonny Anderson comes next, the Brazilian scoring for Barcelona against United after 51 seconds in 1998.
