ERDA, Utah (KUTV) — State Route 179 in Tooele County has been closed in both directions after an officer-involved shooting.

Officials with the Utah Department of Transportation said the road was closed from Interstate 80 to the north and Sheeps Lane in Erda.

Details about the nature of the incident were not available.

Officers from the Utah Highway Patrol responded to the scene. A UTA truck was also seen in the area.

UHP spokesperson Lt. Zach Randall said Exit 94 from I-80 will be closed "through the evening commute."

Drivers have been advised to use State Route 36 to State Route 112 as an alternative way to get to and from Grantsville.
