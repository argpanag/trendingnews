Charlize Theron Stuns in Daring Fringe Dress at L.A. Gala

TheTools · 30.10.2026 08:25 · source · permalink

Charlize Theron was a vision in Givenchy at the Airbnb.org Gala in L.A. on Wednesday.

The actress turned heads in a daring green fringe dress from Givenchy’s Ready to Wear Spring 2027 collection.

The gown featured a halter neckline and cinched waist, and the dramatic skirt was all fringe!

Theron took advantage of the design to show off her legs as she posed for photos.

Her black heels, adorned with puffy black circles, were just as eye-catching as her dress.
