Get exclusive access to Powderfinger Live 2027


After years of speculation, beloved Australian band Powderfinger have confirmed that next year, 17 years after they last exited the live arena, the iconic act will reform for a major national run of tour dates, landing in Brisbane, Cairns, Perth, Sydney, Adelaide and Melbourne, across September and October 2027.

Bernard Fanning, Darren Middleton, Ian Haug, Jon Coghill and John ‘JC’ Collins - together as Powderfinger - created seven studio albums and a catalogue of music that has become ingrained in Australian cultural lore. Anthems such as “My Happiness”, “These Days”, “Sunsets”, “On My Mind”, “Burn Your Name” and “Pick You Up” have soundtracked our core memories for decades and transcend generations.

FAQs

Purchase of Telstra tickets is strictly subject to availability during sale window. Ticket prices and the ticket purchase terms, including ticket limits, are available on the ticket partners' websites. Offer only available for Telstra Plus members.

Check the Telstra Plus Tickets pre-sale Terms and Conditions for full details.

Tickets can be purchased through the Ticketmaster Website, or by submitting a request through the Ticketmaster Help Centre following these steps:

  1. From the dropdown menu on Ticketmaster, select Accessible Bookings as the topic.
  2. Then select Powderfinger - Live 2027 as the event name.

Alternatively, you can call Ticketmaster Accessible Booking Line on 1300 446 925, available Monday to Sunday, 9:00 am – 5:00 pm AEDT. Submitting a request doesn’t guarantee a ticket, availability and type of accessible tickets will vary by venue. A Ticketmaster representative will be in touch if further information is required.

For more information, visit Ticketmaster help page Accessible Tickets: Everything you need to know.
