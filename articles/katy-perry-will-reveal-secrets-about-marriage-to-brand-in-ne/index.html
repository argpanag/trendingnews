Katy Perry will 'reveal secrets about marriage to Brand in new series'

TheTools · 08.11.2026 00:32 · source · permalink

Their sudden split sent shockwaves through the world of showbusiness.

And in the 15 years since her divorce from Russell Brand, Katy Perry has largely maintained what fans have called a 'dignified silence'.

She has yet to comment on his conviction for rape and sexual assault, but The Mail on Sunday has learned that the pop singer is planning to tell all about her 'dark marriage' in a book and docuseries.

Sources close to the star claim she was waiting for the outcome of Brand's trial before finalising a deal but has been in 'active discussions' to tell her side of their 14-month marriage, which ended in 2011.

A well-placed Hollywood source said: 'Katy has received multiple offers over the years to tell her story but would not consider anything until after the trial. She has a lot to say.

'At the moment she's considering a Netflix-type series and an autobiography. She and her team have been in active discussions. 

'There is a lot about that marriage which people don't know. It was a very dark period in her life.' 

The source claimed her team have been in touch with streaming giants including Netflix and Hulu. 

Katy Perry with then-husband Russell Brand at the 2011 MTV Video Music Awards in Los Angeles

Ms Perry, 42, was not asked to testify during the trial but it has emerged she may have been in the room during one of Brand's sexual assaults, which took place in 2009.

Brand, 51, was convicted of sexually assaulting a female Channel 4 employee after asking her for a massage and then 'thrust and pressed his head into her lap around her groin area' leaving the woman feeling 'repulsed'.

In legal submissions before Brand's rape trial – made public after his conviction – Ian Winter KC, defending, said of the 2009 incident: 'The victim says that other people were in the room and witnessed it, possibly including the defendant's future wife, Ms Katy Perry.'

Brand's legal team successfully argued that his marriage to the singer was irrelevant to the allegations and prosecutors agreed not to mention her.

The Crown Prosecution Service confirmed that Ms Perry – who is now dating former Canadian prime minister Justin Trudeau – was never approached or asked to provide a witness statement to police.

The couple met while Brand was filming Get Him To The Greek in 2009, marrying the following year in a Hindu ceremony in India.

However, by the end of 2011 the marriage was over, with Brand dumping Ms Perry by text moments before she was due on stage.

The I Kissed A Girl and Firework singer has hinted she knows more about Brand's dark side, telling fans this summer that he should be 'in jail'. She has not spoken about his conviction.
