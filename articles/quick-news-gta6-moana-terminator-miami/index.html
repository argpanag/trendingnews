Quick News: GTA6, Moana, Terminator, Miami

TheTools · 13.10.2026 17:08 · source · permalink
  • Game News

Grand Theft Auto VI
A new report indicates Rockstar Games plans to release the PC version of “Grand Theft Auto VI” within the first three months of 2027 – less than half a year after the game’s console release on November 19th.

GamesBeat broke the news, citing four industry sources familiar with the studio’s plans. A Rockstar Games rep did not respond to a request for comment on the article. By comparison, GTAV got its PC port 19 months after its console release.

Moana
Disney+ has set an October 21st premiere date for the live-action “Moana” remake on its service. The title arrives on SVOD just three months after its theatrical premiere in early July, where it landed mixed-to-negative reviews and a gross of $323.6 million worldwide. It’s estimated the film could lose the studio between $100-125 million. [Source: Variety]

Terminator: Dark Fate
“Below” actress Mackenzie Davis has reflected on her starring role in 2019’s “Terminator: Dark Fate,” and says she thinks the film deserved a better fate:

“I feel like that movie has such a bad rap, and it just came out at the wrong time. I don’t think that’s a mark on its quality because I think it’s really good. I loved [making] it, to be honest. It was so out of my comfort zone. We all felt this strange ownership and sort of indie filmmaking [spirit], like, ‘This is our little movie. I’m so happy I did it.”

Miami Vice ’85
“The Holdovers” and “Tony” star Dominic Sessa and “The White Lotus” actress Haley Lu Richardson have joined the cast of Joseph Kosinski’s “Miami Vice ’85,” which is currently in production. Photos have already been spotted of Sessa on-set via Just Jared and show Austin Butler decked out in 1980s appropriate gear for the film which also stars Michael B. Jordan, John Leguizamo, Alden Ehrenreich, Walton Goggins, André Holland, Andrew Schulz, Danny Ramirez, Whitney Peak, Camila Morrone and Edi Gathegi.
