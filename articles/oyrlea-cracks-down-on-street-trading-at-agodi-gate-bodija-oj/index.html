OYRLEA cracks down on street trading at Agodi Gate, Bodija, Ojoo markets

TheTools · 04.09.2026 03:24 · source · permalink

City Beats

September 4, 2026 by Yinka Adeniran, Ibadan and Segun Showunmi, Ibadan

The Oyo State Rule of Law Enforcement Authority (OYRLEA), in collaboration with security operatives, has intensified enforcement against street trading and the illegal occupation of public spaces at Gate, Bodija, and Ojoo markets in Ibadan.

The operation, which was carried out during the closing hours of Wednesday, ensured compliance with the laws on trading, use of roads, walkways and other public spaces.

The enforcement followed observations that many traders still display and sell goods on roads, walkways, drainage channels, and road setbacks, despite the provision of adequate market facilities by the state government.

The Authority impounded substantial quantities of goods, perishable and non-perishable, from locations where trading is illegal.

OYRLEA stressed that the exercise was not to frustrate legitimate business owners, but ensuring that traders operate legally.

The Authority called on market men and women to desist from trading on roads, walkways, drainage channels, and setbacks, and to utilise the designated spaces provided for commercial activities.

OYRLEA warned that enforcement would be sustained across the affected markets and other parts of the state until there is significant compliance with the state laws.

The Authority urged traders and the public to cooperate with enforcement personnel, adding that maintaining orderly markets and accessible public spaces remains a collective responsibility.
