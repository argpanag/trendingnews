Kat Edmonson Announces Holiday Album And "Have Your Merry Christmas With Me Tour" - That Eric Alper

TheTools · 16.09.2026 22:34 · source · permalink
  • Music

Kat Edmonson is getting an early start on the holidays with a new album, ‘Have Your Merry Christmas With Me,’ arriving November 6, followed by a U.S. tour beginning later that month.

The singer-songwriter’s new holiday collection brings her jazz and traditional pop sensibilities to seasonal standards alongside newly written material. The accompanying “Have Your Merry Christmas With Me Tour” begins November 27 at Blue Strawberry in St. Louis and runs through December 30 at the Triple Door in Seattle.

Edmonson describes the album as a reflection of what the holidays mean to her, including “love, hope, joy in the face of loss” and nostalgia.

The first taste is “It’s Snowing,” a reimagining of “It’s Raining,” the Allen Toussaint-penned song recorded by Irma Thomas. Edmonson transforms the original into a winter song, introducing the album through one of its more unexpected reinterpretations.

She has also written two originals for the project: the title track, “Have Your Merry Christmas With Me,” and “Hush! There’s a Falling Snow.”

Elsewhere, Edmonson reaches into several generations of holiday music.

The album includes her versions of familiar standards such as “Jingle Bells,” “Hark! The Herald Angels Swing” and “Santa Baby,” alongside Nancy Wilson’s “That’s What I Want For Christmas” and The Andrews Sisters’ “I’d Like To Hitch a Ride with Santa Claus.”

She also takes on Wham!’s “Last Christmas,” giving the pop perennial a moodier treatment that fits alongside the record’s blend of nostalgia and classic pop.

The live celebration begins the day after Thanksgiving.

After opening in St. Louis, Edmonson travels through Cincinnati, Ann Arbor, Evanston, Minneapolis, Boise and Denver before reaching California. A December 13 appearance at Joe’s Pub brings the tour to New York City, followed two nights later by a Los Angeles show at Vibrato.

Austin gets the longest stay on the itinerary, with three consecutive nights at The State Theatre from December 17 through 19.

Shows in Dallas and Houston follow before Edmonson heads to the Pacific Northwest for the final two dates. She performs at Alberta Rose Theater in Portland on December 29 and closes the tour at Seattle’s Triple Door on December 30.

Tickets for the tour are available now.
