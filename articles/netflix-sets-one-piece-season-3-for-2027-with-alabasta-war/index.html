Netflix Sets One Piece Season 3 for 2027 With Alabasta War

TheTools · 09.10.2026 18:47 · source · permalink

Netflix will bring its live-action adaptation of One Piece back for a third season in 2027, with Monkey D. Luffy and the Straw Hat Pirates entering the conflict surrounding the desert kingdom of Alabasta. The upcoming season will focus on Princess Vivi's efforts to protect her homeland from a rebellion secretly fueled by Sir Crocodile and his criminal organization, Baroque Works.

Netflix has confirmed the 2027 release window but has not announced an exact premiere date. As with the first two seasons, the third installment will stream exclusively on the platform, according to Digit.in's October 6 report.

The new season will adapt the Alabasta storyline, bringing the crew into a struggle that threatens to tear the kingdom apart. The conflict places Vivi's future and the stability of her country at the center of the adventure.

Netflix's official season description states, "In a season defined by unbreakable bonds and impossible choices, the Straw Hats must face a brewing civil war and a powerful warlord to save Vivi’s kingdom before it crumbles into the sand."

The series' returning cast includes Iñaki Godoy as Monkey D. Luffy, Mackenyu as Zoro, Emily Rudd as Nami, Jacob Romero as Usopp, Taz Skylar as Sanji, and Charithra Chandran as Princess Vivi. Mikaela Hoover will also appear as Tony Tony Chopper.

Additional cast members include Joe Manganiello as Mr. 0, Lera Abova as Miss All Sunday, Sendhil Ramamurthy as King Nefertari Cobra, Xolo Maridueña as Portgas D. Ace, Cole Escola as Bon Clay, Daisy Head as Miss Doublefinger, and Awdo Awdo as Mr. 1.

The announced lineup also includes Emilio Sakraya as Koza, Omid Abtahi as Toto, Jamie Ward as Pell, and Nabeel Khan as Chaka. Their characters expand the ensemble involved in the Alabasta storyline.

According to Screen Rant's October 9 report, production on the third season has wrapped. The installment is subtitled "The Battle of Alabasta," signaling that the kingdom's central conflict will take precedence over the introductory adventures that shaped earlier episodes.

The first two seasons established the Straw Hats' journey from the East Blue into the broader world of the series. Season 2 introduced Princess Vivi and featured locations including Reverse Mountain and Whiskey Peak, laying the groundwork for the Alabasta confrontation.

The live-action adaptation draws from Eiichiro Oda's long-running manga, which has expanded into an extensive franchise encompassing an animated series, films, video games, specials, and other productions. The scale of the source material has made the adaptation's long-term direction a central consideration as the television story progresses.

Screen Rant reported that Netflix has not officially renewed the series for a fourth season. However, the show's creators have discussed a six-season plan, with another six seasons outlined as a possible longer-term scenario if the production continues adapting the original manga.

If the series advances beyond Alabasta, the next major storyline in the manga is the Sky Island Saga. That arc begins with the Straw Hats reaching Mock Town and leads into a conflict involving the Shandia warriors and the Skypieans. Netflix has not confirmed whether this storyline will be adapted in a fourth season.
