Celebrity Traitors star's 'terrifying' health issue had them indoors for months

TheTools · 30.09.2026 13:33 · source · permalink

The Celebrity Traitors will return to screens tomorrow night (October 1) for its highly anticipated second series. Claudia Winkleman will welcome 21 new famous faces to the Scottish Highlands castle to play the ultimate game of deception against one another.

This year's line up includes a whole range of stars including Scots actress Sharon Rooney, comedian Miranda Hart, Little Mix star Leigh Anne Pinnock and Love Island host Maya Jama. The celebs recently revealed whether they would they would prefer to be a Faithful or a Traitor in the castle.

One of the contestants who is hoping to be selected as a Traitor is Last of Us star Bella Ramsey. The 23-year-old, who is non-binary and uses them/they pronouns, will make their debut on the BBC show tomorrow evening along with the rest of the cast.

According to ActionNetwork.com, Ramsey is already one of the favourites to win the series this year with the bookies placing them with the third highest odds to take home the jackpot charity prize.

Ramsey's major breakthrough role came when they starred in Game of Thrones as young noblewoman Lyanna Mormont in 2016. However, many viewers will recognise them as Ellie Williams in the post-apocalyptic HBO show Last Of Us with Pedro Pascal.

What fans may not know is that Ramsay suffers from a 'terrifiying' health disorder which left them unable to leave the house "for months". Bella opened up about their emetophobia disorder on the The Louis Theroux Podcast, which is the fear of vomiting or seeing others vomit.

According to the NHS, the phobia affects around eight per cent of people in the UK and can result in anxiety and obsessive behaviour.

Ramsey revealed that their condition previously became so bad that they were left housebound when they were around 13-years-old.

The actress added: "Everything was a threat. You go out and it's like you see germs, you see sickness everywhere. Terrifying.

"Every memory that I have of vomit, since nursery, is scary. I remember every single time anyone in my life has ever felt sick or been sick near me."

The Last of Us star admitted that their fear is "irrational" as they explained that it started at a very young age and there was a point where they'd "rather die than throw up."

Bella's condition began to make more sense to them when they were diagnosed with autism while on set of The Last of Us.

During an interview with British Vogue, Ramsey recalled how a crew member on the series who has an autistic daughter identified similar traits in their behaviour.

Then 18-years-old, this led to Bella being given an official psychiatric diagnosis which was something they had "always wondered about".

Ramsay said: "It enables me to walk through the world with more grace towards myself about not being able to do the easy everyday tasks that everyone else seems to be able to do."

The neurodivergent star previously revealed that they experience sensory issues and hyperawareness of others' micro-expressions.

They said: "I've always been watching and learning from people. Having to learn more manually how to socialise and interact with the people around me has helped me with acting."

Ramsey will make her debut on Celebrity Traitors tomorrow evening (October 1) where fans will discover whether or not she has been chosen as a Traitor.

The Celebrity Traitors returns to the BBC on Thursday October 1 at 8pm
