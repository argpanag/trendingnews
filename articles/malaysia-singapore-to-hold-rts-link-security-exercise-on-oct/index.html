Malaysia, Singapore to hold RTS Link security exercise on Oct 27

TheTools · 24.10.2026 18:50 · source · permalink

PUTRAJAYA: The RTS Link Malaysia-Singapore Cross-Border Joint Exercise Field Training Exercise (FTX) 2026 will be held at the RTS Link Bukit Chagar Station and Wadi Hana Depot in Johor Baru on Tuesday (Oct 27).

The National Security Council said the exercise would run from 9am to 5pm and be conducted in collaboration with Malaysia Rapid Transit System Sdn Bhd (MRTS) and RTS Operations Pte Ltd (RTSO).

In a statement on Saturday (Oct 24), the NSC said the exercise aimed to test and assess preparedness, response capabilities and coordination among Malaysian and Singaporean security agencies and emergency services.

"It is being held ahead of the full operation of the RTS Link and will also test the standard operating procedures for cross-border incident management, jointly developed by Malaysia and Singapore," it said.

The public is advised to remain calm if they see security personnel, operational assets or exercise activities during the simulation.

The NSC said there would be no actual threat and all activities were simulations conducted solely for the exercise. – Bernama
