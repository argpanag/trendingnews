Two people arrested after hours long stand-off with Nelson police

TheTools · 08.09.2026 18:38 · source · permalink

Two people have been arrested after an hours long stand-off with police in Nelson.

Senior Sergeant Mark Surman said police were at an address in Toi Toi regarding an unrelated incident when a man became enraged, threatened police, then locked himself inside a residence and refused to leave.

Armed police were at the scene as a precaution and cordons were put in place along Emano Street from Murphy Street to Toi Toi Street.

A neighbour who entered the property during the stand-off was also arrested.

Both were taken into custody about 6.25pm and cordons were lifted at 7pm.

Police want to reassure the public that there was no threat to public safety.
