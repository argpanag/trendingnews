UBS cuts Kering target to EUR 224.00; stock pre-market at EUR 210.85, minus 0.35 percent

TheTools · 08.10.2026 15:25 · source · permalink

Analyst target revisions put Kering in focus before the Xetra open. The stock was at EUR 210.85, down 0.35 percent at Lang & Schwarz versus the October 7 close.

On October 7, 2026, Ideal Investor reported that UBS had lowered its Kering price target to EUR 224.00 from EUR 285.00, while RBC Capital had reduced its target to EUR 260.00 from EUR 280.00. Kering stock was at EUR 210.85 in pre-market trading on October 8, 2026, down 0.35 percent versus the prior close at Lang & Schwarz of EUR 211.58 on October 7, 2026.

Kering pre-market quote

Lang & Schwarz quoted Kering at EUR 210.85 at 7:50 a.m. CEST on October 8, 2026. The bid was EUR 210.55 and the ask was EUR 211.15. The further course from the Xetra open at 9:00 a.m. CEST is shown by the continuously updated real-time quote of Kering stock.

Analyst target revisions

The October 7 report attributed the UBS revision to a “hold” rating. RBC Capital maintained a sector-performance opinion while lowering its target. The report also said the revised targets remained above the stock’s cited market level at the time, but those analyst views are separate from the October 8 Lang & Schwarz quote.

Kering’s financial calendar

Kering’s finance page lists the company’s First-Half 2026 Results as published on July 28, 2026, after market close. It also lists the 2026 Capital Markets Day held on April 16, 2026, in Florence.
