Excitement surrounds Miss Universe 2026 as its coronation night unfolds on November 24, 2026 (November 25, starting at 8 AM Philippine time) at the José Miguel Agrelot Coliseum in San Juan, Puerto Rico.

The 75th edition of the prestigious pageant features delegates from 116 countries and territories competing for the title and crown. From this impressive lineup, 30 candidates advance as semifinalists.

As for the crown, Miss Universe 2025 Fátima Bosch of Mexico crowns her successor with “The Radiance of Eternal Legacy,” featuring 3,150 diamonds and masterfully designed by renowned Venezuelan jewelry designer and crown maker George Wittels.

The candidates' official activities for Miss Universe begin on November 11 with the Welcome Parade. The National Costume pre-show follows on November 21, while the Preliminary Competition takes place on November 22.

Miss Universe 2026 coronation night is hosted by Miss Universe 2022 R'Bonney Gabriel and Mario Lopez, with actress Zuri Hall and Miss Universe 1993 Dayanara Torres serving as commentators.

The official hosts of this year's Miss Universe coronation night, Miss Universe 2022 R’Bonney Gabriel and American actor and television personality Mario Lopez, take the stage, officially kicking off the highly anticipated beauty pageant.
