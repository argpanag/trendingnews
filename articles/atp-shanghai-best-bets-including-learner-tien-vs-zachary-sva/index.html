ATP Shanghai Best Bets Including Learner Tien vs Zachary Svajda

TheTools · 09.10.2026 22:26 · source · permalink

The 2026 ATP Shanghai tournament is underway in China. This is a 1,000-point tournament and the heart of the fall Asian swing. It’s the last big tournament of the year in Asia before the ATP Tour heads back to Europe for the home stretch of indoor tournaments which precedes the season-ending ATP Finals in Turin. We have seen Carlos Alcaraz and Novak Djokovic win the 500-point tournaments in Tokyo and Beijing, immediately before Shanghai. Will either of them be fresh enough to make a run at a 1,000-point title? This is yet another 2026 ATP event in which the wider field has a tremendous opportunity make a big points gain. We have seen so many wide-open tournaments on the ATP Tour this year, due to Alcaraz being out for months with an injury. Now we have the news that Jannik Sinner is out for the remainder of the year with a bothersome knee injury he can’t seem to shake. Djokovic has already lost in Shanghai, falling to Hubert Hurkacz. Which players will take advantage of a fluid situation in men’s tennis? ATP Shanghai is a fascinating tournament to watch for that reason. LWOS has our best bets for three matches. Post your best bets and thoughts in the comments.

ATP Shanghai

Learner Tien – Zachary Svajda: 3:00 EST

H2H: 0-0

This ATP Shanghai match is a mysterious one, because Learner Tien has played four non-tour matches in recent weeks at the Davis Cup and Laver Cup. Will that sufficiently prepare him for this match in Shanghai? Tien and his opponent, Zachary Svajda, both have 19-11 hardcourt records this year, and are both 2-3 in their last five matches. Tien, though, is ranked in the top 20 while Svajda is outside the top 80. The gap in ranking is real, but form and hardcourt quality aren’t as clear-cut here.

Best Bet to Make

Welcome to our best bet section. You’re going to get a collection of bets to consider, also a confidence level which should guide how much you should invest in this match.

Parlay/hedge options: Tien -1.5 sets at 1.86 at @1xbet

Confidence level on a scale of 1-10: 1 – You should not bet on this match. 

This ATP Shanghai match is not a good one to bet on because Tien’s lack of good form and tour-match play in recent weeks makes him hard to fully evaluate. The slight lean is to Svajda, but Tien is the generally better player. There’s no really attractive play to make here.

That being said, betting on Svajda +4.5 games is the best bet you can make.    

Value bet/ the best odds: Svajda +4.5 games at 1.60 at @betfair

Arthur Fils – Pavel Kotov: 3:00 EST

H2H: 1-0

This ATP Shanghai match puts World No. 10 Arthur Fils against World No. 187 Pavel Kotov. As you might expect, Fils is a massive favorite. Fils winning in straight sets is priced at 1.40. So, there’s not a lot of value there. Fils is 24-7 on hardcourts this year, while Kotov is 17-5. Kotov’s matches have come against lower-level competition in smaller tournaments.

Best Bet to Make

Parlay/hedge options: Fils -4.5 games at 1.70 at @betway

Confidence level on a scale of 1-10: 1 – you should not bet on this match.

This ATP Shanghai match is one of those matches where one player has to win decisively to cover a spread. Fils has to win by five games to cover a spread and bring home a decent return on investment. He has to win by six games to offer especially good value to anyone backing him. He could win 6-4 6-4 and lose the spread bet. It’s just not a situation which is conducive to making a confident bet. We will slightly lean to Kotov +5.5 games but think you shouldn’t touch this one at all. You could play Fils -4.5 against Kotov +5.5 and hope the match is decided by precisely five games, but that’s a narrow window.

That being said, betting on Kotov +5.5 games is the best bet you can make

Value bet/ the best odds: Kotov +5.5 games at 1.71 @1xbet

Tommy Paul – Adolfo Daniel Vallejo: 4:30 EST

H2H: 2-0

This ATP Shanghai match is a rematch of a battle from Cincinnati two months ago, in which Tommy Paul beat Adolfo Daniel Vallejo in three sets. Paul is 3-2 in his last five matches, Vallejo 4-1. Paul is 20-11 on hardcourts this year, Vallejo 11-7.

Best Bet to Make

Parlay/hedge options: Vallejo +1.5 sets at 1.68 at @unibet

Confidence level on a scale of 1-10: 4 – you could bet half a unit on this match.

This ATP Shanghai match isn’t a stone-cold lock, but Tommy Paul is generally a better tennis player than Adolfo Daniel Vallejo. Though Vallejo is in good form, Paul is not in bad form. He should have enough quality to cover a small game spread.

That being said, betting on Paul -2.5 games is the best bet you can make

Value bet/ the best odds: Paul -2.5 games at 1.82 @betfair
