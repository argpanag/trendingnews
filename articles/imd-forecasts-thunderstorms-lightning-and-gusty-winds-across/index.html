IMD Forecasts Thunderstorms, Lightning and Gusty Winds Across Several States Tomorrow

The India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast thunderstorms accompanied by lightning and gusty winds over  Haryana, Chandigarh, Delhi, Punjab, Chhattisgarh, Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Karaikal for tomorrow. Similar weather conditions are expected over Andhra Pradesh and West Rajasthan.  IMD said that conditions are likely to become favourable for further withdrawal of southwest monsoon from some more parts of eastern India and many parts of northeast India during the next two days. Himachal Pradesh, Jammu and Kashmir, Ladakh and Kerala can also experience thunderstorms accompanied with Lightning.
