Live: All Blacks v South Africa Springboks fourth test in Baltimore, USA - Rugby's Greatest Rivalry tour

TheTools · 12.09.2026 22:05 · source · permalink

The Springboks lead the All Blacks 17-14 at halftime in a nail-biting fourth test in Baltimore.

The Springboks dominated for much of the half, but the All Blacks struck back with four minutes left when Will Jordan surged over for a try, to go with one from Codie Taylor early in the game.

The Springboks have scored two tries as well.

There was a lengthy delay midway through the half, when referee Karl Dickson lost communication with the Television Match Official. Play resumed after the two captains agreed that there would not be TMO involvement unless there was foul play, in which case a phone call would be made.

British Open golf winner Ryan Fox carred the New Zealand flag, standing next to the All Blacks at the pre-match anthem.
