Denver weather: More clear skies as heat rises slightly

TheTools · 13.09.2026 00:45 · source · permalink

It is another typical and predictable day of weather in Denver on Friday.

The National Weather Service said to expect a high of 92 degrees with calm winds and clear skies.

The weather in Denver continues to remain monotonous through the month of September. Expect no precipitation over the weekend and for temperatures to stay in the 90s.

A cold front should roll in next Monday or Tuesday, bringing with it some relief from the heat and much-needed precipitation.

Tonight, expect a low of 60 degrees with more calm wind.
