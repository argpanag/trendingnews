And now Hamilton. “Wow. That feels great … So happy. Oh man, I’m lost for words. It’s been a really, really tough, challenging weekend … The engine issue we had yesterday … But it’s so close between us all … Wow. To have a pole here.” He is told he is the oldest driver to have a pole since Mario Andretti in 1982. “Where there’s a will, there’s a way. It’s all in the mind, honestly, for all of us … I love what I do … I dream about it. I have this amazing support around the world, these guys keeps me going, I hope they are happy today, and I hope I can deliver for them tomorrow.”

Now Russell, who is asked if he is happy. “I am, I am. It’s been a tricky few races for me and still struggling to get on top of this car, to be honest because it’s been a very difficult weekend until one lap in Q3. To almost get pole … it was very close in the end. I always know it’s in there.” His self-belief remains, despite all the frustrations.

“I was fighting with the car particularly in Q3 … There is such low downforce here.”

Leclerc speaks. “I just didn’t do a good job. The outlaps were difficult to put the tyres in the right window … A bit disappointed but to start third here is not too bad.”

So there was no false Ferrari qualifying dawn. And Russell took second – David Croft points out that the two drivers who need to win to keep the title race alive are one and two.

Lewis Hamilton takes pole!

Hamilton does it. After Verstappen locks up on 13th then backs off, the Briton gets his first Ferrari pole, his 105th overall.

Final seconds. Here comes Antonelli. Only fifth fastest.

George Russell +0.009 in second. Leclerc third +0.119.

Hamilton goes fastest! 1:16.514.

All going wrong for Hadjar. He misjudged the line coming into the pits. Bernie Collins says the Frenchman is all over the place mentally, complaining more than Gordon Ramsay in a Wimpy.

Bernie Collins eavesdropping on the drivers, saying their messages are full of uncertainty as to the best strategy.

Though Verstappen has that one-place penalty.

Turn 13 unlucky again for Hadjar but Verstappen gets second, +0.045.

Russell P3, Antonelli P4, then Norris beat Hamilton at +0.177.

Leclerc puts in a 1:16.647 for provisional pole. Hamilon +0.422, a middle sector mistake.

Q3 is go.

Karun Chandhok analyses Piastri’s lap. Kerb after kerb after kerb.

Yet Norris went fastest. Top 10: Nor, Ver, Lec, Ham, Rus, Had, Ant, Lin, Law and Gas.

Q2 eliminations – with Piastri out

Blimey. Out go Hulkenberg, Colapinto, Piastri, Bortoleto, Bearman and Sainz. McLaren saved a set of soft tyres but an aborted fast lap doomed Piastri.

Piastri out! He is 13th fastest!

Relief for Hamilton. Up in third, at a minuscule +0.077.

Norris shot up to third. Piastri is 10th. Hulkenberg at seventh is the big disruptor, a threat to Lawson, Hadjar and Piastri. And HAMILTON.

Drop zone is looking like Gasly, Bortoleto, Bearman, Colapinto, Sainz and HAMILTON.

Verstappen goes fastest, 1:16.876. Hulkenberg is sixth at present.

Hamilton into the pits. Down in the drop zone at present, as is Norris, who was on used tyres initially.

Lindblad is second at +0.191, ahead of Antonelli at +0.259 and Russell at +0.307.

Charles Leclerc gets a 1:16.944, while Hamilton goes off the track at turn 13 when on a flyer, which is causing a lot of problems.

Q2 is go.

Hadjar used another set of soft tyres, which explains his margin over Verstappen.

Something weird happened to Leclerc, a power surge. The spirits of the night?

Top 10: Ham, Had, Lec, Pia, Rus, Bor, Nor, Hul, Ant, Ver.

Q1 eliminations

Out go Albon, Alonso, Hirakawa, Stroll, Perez and Bottas. Hirakawa was about a second slower than Bearman but got a lot of sympathy on the team radio.

Hadjar has split the Ferraris! +0.044.

Ollie Bearman 10th fastest for Haas, his new teammate 19th.

Yellow cleared. Phew.

Yellow flags … Could settle things.
