Former pupils at one of Ireland’s best-known private boarding schools bid to prevent its closure | BreakingNews

TheTools · 27.10.2026 14:15 · source · permalink

From The Irish Times

Former pupils and parents of students at Glenstal Abbey, one of Ireland’s best-known private boarding schools, have written to the school in a bid to explore “alternative options” to its closure, announced on Friday.

The Co Limerick Benedictine school, in operation for almost 100 years, is set to close its doors by August 2028 following declining demand for boarding school education and rising costs.
