Nintendo Switch 2 System Update 23.5.0 Is Now Live, Here Are The Full Patch Notes

TheTools · 10.11.2026 01:36 · source · permalink

Adds eShop gifting feature support and more

Nintendo has released a new firmware update for the Switch 2 this week, bumping it up to Version 23.5.0.

Most notably, it's added support for "gifting" eShop products to a Nintendo Account family group member. This is an "upcoming feature" and is scheduled to go live on 17th November 2026.

Apart from this, there's now also the ability to configure accessibility settings during the first-time setup, and there are some language updates. A Switch 1 update is out today as well, adding gifting feature support, and some general adjustments & fixes.

Here are the full patch notes for Version 23.5.0, courtesy of Nintendo's official support page:

Nintendo goes into some more detail about the gifting feature on its website, explaining how it can be used to give a child or family member a game for a special occasion.

You can send a gift by selecting the "Give as a Gift" option on an eShop product page, and even choose your gift wrap colour. From there, all the recipient has to do is accept the gift, and they can then play it.

The Version 23.5.0 update has also supposedly patched the recently discovered jailbreak exploit. You can find out more about this in our previous story. This latest update also follows a major Switch 2 system update (Version 23.0.0) in September, which added VRR for TV mode and much more.
