The Voice UK coach 'dropped' by ITV as show needs 'major refresh'

TheTools · 09.09.2026 08:59 · source · permalink

The Voice UK might soon be losing one of his long-term judges as singer Tom Jones is said to be sidelined ahead of the new season.

Jones, who joined the series in 2012, is reported to not be returning to the singing competition in a full-time capacity as the ITV series undergoes a 'major refresh' with a number of new coaches, as reported by The Sun.

If confirmed, this means that season 15 will be the first The Voice UK season since 2012 to not feature the Welsh crooner on the coaching panel.

Digital Spy understands that the show is looking to work with Jones in the future as a show source confirmed: "ITV absolutely loves working with Sir Tom. They are in talks with him and are hopeful he will return to the series."

Digital Spy has also reached out to representatives for Jones for comment.

Meanwhile, singer Kelly Rowland is expected to return for her second season on The Voice UK after debuting in the role this year alongside Jones, will.i.am, and McFly members Danny Jones and Tom Fletcher.

With Aitch reportedly set to replace Jones and Fletcher next year amid their apparent upcoming departures, all clues are now pointing to Girls Aloud singer Cheryl taking Tom Jones's red chair as he reportedly steps back.

Rachel Joseph - ITV

Last month, it was also reported that The Voice UK had signed up two new hosts replacing Emma Willis, who will shortly commence her Strictly Come Dancing stint alongside Johannes Radebe and Josh Widdicombe.

ITV has apparently hired Stacey Solomon and Alesha Dixon as the new presenters, with the former focusing on the contestants behind the scenes and the latter taking on the traditional stage presenter role.

The Voice UK airs on ITV1.
