Princess Beatrice's husband Edoardo Mapelli Mozzi sparks royal excitement after unexpected public gesture towards Brunei's Prince Abdul Mateen - Istituto Impariamo

Princess Beatrice’s husband Edoardo Mapelli Mozzi has publicly celebrated a heartfelt royal update from Brunei’s Prince Abdul Mateen, offering an affectionate sign of support to his overseas counterpart. The 42-year-old British property developer quickly reacted to a charming photograph shared by the Bruneian royal, underscoring an enduring connection between the two high-profile households.

Prince Mateen presented his three million Instagram followers with an intimate glimpse into domestic life alongside his infant daughter, Princess Zahra. The photograph showed the royal father cradling the young girl, who clutched a pink star-shaped toy whilst seated in his lap in front of a cooling fan, displaying a remarkably full head of dark hair.

The candid snapshot immediately drew a public “like” from Edoardo’s verified personal account, marking yet another cross-continental show of camaraderie between two high-profile girl dads.

The shared world of royal fatherhood

This subtle social media gesture is far from an isolated interaction. Edoardo previously left a red heart emoji beneath an image of young Zahra shortly after her arrival, having also sent warm felicitations to Prince Mateen and his wife, Princess Anisha, writing: “Congratulations Mateen and Anisha. Zahra ❤️.”

Both men belong to an exclusive, globally connected circle of modern royals navigating family life in the public eye. Their personal acquaintance extends beyond digital screens, as Beatrice and Edoardo were fellow guests alongside Prince Mateen at the lavish Jordanian royal wedding in 2023.


Princess Beatrice and husband Edoardo Mapelli Mozzi attending a high-profile public event.

Edoardo and Princess Beatrice, the daughter of Prince Andrew and Sarah Ferguson, Duchess of York, tied the knot in Windsor in 2020. Since then, the couple have expanded their own household, bringing plenty of direct parenting experience to their conversations with other young royals.

Inside the growing York family nursery

The British couple are parents to two young daughters: Sienna, born in 2021, and Athena, born in 2025. Edoardo also co-parents his elder son, Christopher Woolf—affectionately nicknamed Wolfie—from his prior relationship with architectural designer Dara Huang.

  • Christopher Woolf (Wolfie): Edoardo’s eldest son, born to Dara Huang.
  • Sienna Mapelli Mozzi: Beatrice and Edoardo’s first daughter together, born in 2021.
  • Athena Mapelli Mozzi: The couple’s second daughter, born in 2025.
  • Adelaide Brooksbank: Princess Eugenie and Jack Brooksbank’s third child, born earlier this year.


Princess Beatrice with husband Edoardo Mapelli Mozzi and children at a parade in London.

The York household has experienced a true baby boom in recent seasons. Beatrice’s sister, Princess Eugenie, welcomed baby daughter Adelaide with her husband Jack Brooksbank, providing a sprawling cohort of young royal cousins growing up together across the UK.

Inside Brunei’s joyous milestone

Prince Abdul Mateen, the son of Sultan Hassanal Bolkiah and Puan Hajah Mariam binti Abdul Aziz, announced Princess Zahra’s arrival on 8 February. Official confirmation from state channel RTB News noted that 17 ceremonial cannon shots were fired within the grounds of the Sultan’s residence, Istana Nurul Iman, to herald the birth.

The birth arrived on the heels of the couple’s magnificent ten-day wedding extravaganza in 2024, hosted at the same royal palace in Bandar Seri Begawan. Since welcoming their first child, the couple have carefully documented her early development for royal watchers.


Princess Anisha Rosnah and Prince Abdul Mateen holding their infant daughter, Princess Zahra.

Public fascination has centred especially on Zahra’s signature thick brunette hair, alongside curated matching style moments with her mother. At four months old, Zahra appeared in a miniature ruffled floral ensemble matching Princess Anisha’s ‘Ellie Nap’ design from Hill House, sparking admiration across royal style channels.
