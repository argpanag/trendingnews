GAME DAY I Bulls, Stormers to win at home, Sharks to upset Munster in Limerick

TheTools · 31.10.2026 18:30 · source · permalink

UNITED RUGBY CHAMPIONSHIP

WILLIE LE ROUX The Bulls are favourites to beat the Scarlets this afternoon.

Image: Backpagepix

This weekend is one final push for South Africa’s URC teams ahead of the November break for the Nations Championship. It is difficult to grasp that a week after the Bulls, Stormers and Sharks play URC matches, the Springboks kick off against Italy in Turin.

The rugby calendar is so congested these days that the Springboks in the Sharks team playing in Limerick on Saturday night will travel straight to Italy on Sunday to await their Springboks teammates’ arrival from South Africa.

Ditto for Lions scrumhalf Morne van den Berg, who featured in the Lions' gritty 29-20 defeat to the Warriors in Glasgow on Friday night. The Lions were unlucky not to get a log point from that match after a last-minute penalty stretched Glasgow’s lead beyond seven points.

The Lions return home in 11th on the points table, but the other three SA teams could well end the weekend in the top four.

The Bulls will surely dispatch the Scarlets with relative ease at Loftus Versfeld, and the same goes for the Stormers, who host Ulster in Cape Town. The tricky South African fixture on Saturday is the Sharks’ visit to Munster in Limerick, but I’m backing the Durbanites to win.

The bookies have Munster as slight favourites, which is fair enough, but do not be surprised if the Sharks make it three from three on their tour. They are unquestionably a different team this season thanks to a proper pre-season and a culture reset. The Sharks are playing with tenacity, confidence and belief. I predict they will get past 9th-placed Munster.

Stormers coach John Dobson is resting Sacha Feinberg-Mngomezulu, but that shouldn’t be an issue because his replacement, Yaqeen Ahmed, is similarly talented and will want to justify his Springbok selection with a strong performance.

All eyes will be on Bongi Mbonambi in his first game back at the Stormers following his sudden departure from the Sharks. I have the Stormers to win by at least 12 points.

In Pretoria, Johan Ackermann made a whopping 13 changes to his team that struggled past Ulster, but the Bulls’ excellent depth means the team doesn’t look weaker. Curwin Bosch is taking over at flyhalf from injured Handre Pollard, and it is a big game for him. Has his game improved after two years in France? He has had some reasonable cameos for the Bulls this season, but this match, where he will probably play the full 80 minutes, will tell us more.

The Scarlets are missing a raft of players called up to the Wales squad, and I’m tipping the Bulls to win by 20 or more.
