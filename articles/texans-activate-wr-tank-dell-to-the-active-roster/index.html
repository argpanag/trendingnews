Texans activate WR Tank Dell to the active roster

TheTools · 24.10.2026 21:14 · source · permalink

Tank Dell will play his first game in nearly two years on Sunday when the Texans play the Giants.

The team officially activated the wide receiver back to the active roster from reserve/PUP.

Dell dislocated his kneecap and tore multiple knee ligaments in a Dec. 21, 2024, game against the Chiefs. He missed the rest of that season, all of last season and the first six games of this season with the gruesome knee injury.

Dell’s rookie season of 2023 ended on a broken leg in Week 13.

The Texans also announced they elevated cornerback Bryce Hall and linebacker Kenneth Murray from the practice squad.
