‘Greatest sporting theatre ever’: Samoa stars face off with Kangaroos in spine-tingling scenes

TheTools · 08.11.2026 14:21 · source · permalink

Andrew Voss declared it may be “the greatest sporting theatre we have ever witnessed”.

In the highlight of the men’s Rugby League World Cup so far, Samoa’s stars advanced right up towards Australia’s players until they were nose to nose in a stirring Siva Tau.

Brian To’o and Tino Fa’asuamaleaui even touched foreheads at one point while Jazz Tevaga took it to Jack Bostock and stared him down despite giving up 16 centimetres to the Kangaroos winger.

“These are footballers about to play rugby league. They’re not actors. They’re not in a movie,” Voss said in commentary on Channel 7.

“That’s the passion and emotion of representing your nation.”

“They’re (Samoa) up for it,” Kiwis legend Shaun Johnson added.

“It’s out of respect. You know exactly what this is all about. The Aussies acknowledged it, stood tough, stood physical, and Samoa did not back down one bit.”

It was the perfect tone-setter for Sunday’s semi-final, which will see the Kangaroos and Kiwis face off after Australia outclassed Samoa.
