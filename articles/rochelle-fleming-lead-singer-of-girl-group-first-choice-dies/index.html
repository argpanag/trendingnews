Rochelle Fleming, lead singer of girl group First Choice, dies

TheTools · 10.11.2026 00:05 · source · permalink

Rochelle Fleming performs onstage on May 25, 2019 in New York City. (Credit: Santiago Felipe/Getty Images)

PHILADELPHIA - Rochelle Fleming, the lead singer and founder of the 1970s girl group First Choice, has died. She was 72 years old. 

Fleming’s passing was confirmed by a post on her official Instagram page.

Rochelle Fleming dies at 72

What they're saying:

"It is with a heavy heart to share the passing of Rochelle Fleming-Finney," her manager wrote on behalf of Fleming’s family. 

Her manager said she passed away on Nov. 5 in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

"She brought light, laughter and love to the world," the post continued. 

She said services to celebrate her life would be announced at a later date.

No cause of death or other information was provided. 

Rochelle Fleming’s legacy

The backstory:

According to Celebrity Talent Agency, Fleming was part of the girl group First Choice – a three-piece disco girl group from Philadelphia in the 1970s. 

Led by vocalist Fleming, they gained fame with hits like "Armed and Extremely Dangerous," "The Player," and "Love Thang."

Initially known as the Debronettes, they collaborated with producer Norman Harris and found success both in the U.S. and the UK. 

Their track "Let No Man Put Asunder" became a classic in the Chicago house music scene after remixes by Shep Pettibone and Frankie Knuckles, according to the agency.

The group disbanded in 1984 but re-formed in 1987 and continued to see success through remixes and reissues of their disco hits. 

The Source: The information for this story was provided by Rochelle Fleming's Instagram account, Celebrity Talent Agency. This story was reported from Los Angeles.
