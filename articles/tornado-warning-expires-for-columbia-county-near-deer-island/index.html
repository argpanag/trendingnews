Tornado warning expires for Columbia County near Deer Island

TheTools · 03.09.2026 19:37 · source · permalink

COLUMBIA COUNTY, Ore. (KPTV) - A tornado warning was issued by the National Weather Service for Columbia County near Deer Island just after noon on Thursday.

The warning expired at 12:45 p.m. and the storm moved out of the area. NWS Portland issued the warning after radar indicated rotation.

There have been no reports of any damage at this time.
