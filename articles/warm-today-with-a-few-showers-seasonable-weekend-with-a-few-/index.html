Warm today with a few showers; seasonable weekend with a few shower chances

TheTools · 02.10.2026 16:23 · source · permalink

High temperatures will reach the low/mid-80s across the Susquehanna Valley on Friday before a cold front moves through during the evening.

High temperatures will reach the low/mid-80s across the Susquehanna Valley on Friday before a cold front moves through during the evening.

Good afternoon!

Today will be the warmest day of the week, with highs in the low 80s. A few showers are possible Friday afternoon and early evening after 2 p.m., including during Friday night high school football games. The showers will be very isolated and will not impact everyone in the Susquehanna Valley.

The first weekend of October looks dry to start for most of the Susquehanna Valley, and much cooler and more seasonable. Showers will pass by to the south in Maryland on Saturday, but likely miss the Susquehanna Valley. This will result in a dry day with highs around 70 degrees. Clouds will gradually decrease throughout the day.

There is a better chance for showers on Sunday; however, it’s not a washout. A low-pressure system will pass by to our south, providing the moisture and clouds needed for rain. At this time, the best chance for showers will be east of I-81 in the afternoon. Temperatures will stay cooler, with highs in the lower to mid-60s under a partly to mostly cloudy sky.

The following week looks dry and pleasant, with cool highs in the low to mid-60s, and chillier overnight lows in the 40s and 50s. Some locations in the high elevations may get patchy frost Monday night and Tuesday night. Tuesday night will be the coldest, with overnight lows in the upper 30s for the higher elevations.

Heading into late next week, temperatures are back up to the upper 60s to near 70 degrees under partly to mostly sunny skies.

Have a great day!
