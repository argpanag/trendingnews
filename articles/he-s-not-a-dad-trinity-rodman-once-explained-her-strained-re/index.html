‘He’s not a dad’ – Trinity Rodman once explained her strained relationship with NBA legend Dennis Rodman

TheTools · 10.09.2026 20:37 · source · permalink

Trinity Rodman once opened up about the complicated relationship she shared with her father, NBA legend Dennis Rodman, revealing just how distant they had become.

The US soccer star discussed their strained bond in December 2024, looking back at moments from her childhood and an emotional encounter involving her father during a Washington Spirit playoff game.

Rodman made it clear that, despite their biological connection, she did not view Dennis as a present father in her life.

Speaking on the Call Her Daddy podcast, Rodman was direct when asked about her relationship with Dennis.

“He’s not a dad. Maybe by blood, but nothing else,” she said.

Rodman had previously discussed the relationship in 2022, explaining that her mother, Michelle Moyer, had tried to protect her and her brother from Dennis’ chaotic lifestyle while they were growing up.

Their relationship became particularly difficult after an unexpected appearance by Dennis at one of Rodman’s games in 2021.

Rodman recalled that she had not spoken to her father for months when he arrived unannounced at a Spirit playoff game.

The situation became emotional, with Rodman later being photographed crying in her father’s arms. However, she said the experience left her feeling angry rather than bringing them closer together.

“The whistle blew and I was so mad, like, ‘You took this happy moment from me. You f—– with my head again,'” Rodman recalled.

For Rodman, the incident became more than an emotional reunion.

She felt that Dennis’ unexpected appearance had disrupted what should have been a positive moment for her, leaving her questioning his intentions.

She also questioned whether her father’s decision to attend the game was really about supporting her or just for a news flash about him appearing at his daughter’s game.

“Even at that game, I don’t think it was for me, I think he wanted to have a good conscience and then be like, ‘Headline, Dennis Rodman showed up to his daughter’s game.'”

Rodman said she did not speak to her father again until after the 2023 Women’s World Cup.
