Alexa Minton’s Forecast | Steamy and Scattered storm chances continue today

TheTools · 17.09.2026 10:04 · source · permalink

LEXINGTON, Ky. (WKYT) - Another hot and humid day is on tap across Kentucky, with most areas warming into the low to mid 90s this afternoon.

Toasty with Isolated chances again for our daytime

There will be plenty of dry time for our daytime hours, but the heat and humidity may spark a few scattered showers or thunderstorms during the afternoon and evening. Any storm that forms could bring brief heavy rain, lightning, and gusty winds. The best chance of rain comes farther east in the state. Grab your umbrella as you head out the door - if it’s not stormy in your area, it is still steamy!

Front Slides In Tonight

A backdoor cold front will begin working from northeast to southwest across the state late tonight and Friday. The front will not make it all the way through Kentucky, but it should advance far enough to bring a more comfortable temperature trend to central and eastern Kentucky as the weekend begins.

Showers and thunderstorms will be possible in some areas along and ahead of the front Friday. Not everyone will see rain, but locations closer to the boundary will have a better opportunity for a passing storm.

More scattered chances into the weekend

Central and eastern Kentucky should see lower temperatures compared with the recent heat, though scattered showers and thunderstorms may still develop through parts of the afternoon. Keep the umbrella handy this weekend - there will still be a lot of dry moments in the forecast.

Have a great Thursday!
