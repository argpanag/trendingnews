The Remarried Empress Episodes 7 & 8: Release Date, Time, Where to Watch & Episode Guide | Dojeon Media

TheTools · 17.11.2026 12:00 · source · permalink

The latest episodes of The Remarried Empress have brought more trouble to the Eastern Empire, with Navier, Sovieshu and Rashta caught in an increasingly complicated palace situation. After releasing its first four episodes together, the Disney+ fantasy romance has continued with two new episodes each Wednesday.

Episodes 5 and 6 arrived on November 11, and viewers will not have to wait long for the next chapter. Episodes 7 and 8 are scheduled to premiere on November 18, bringing the series closer to its two-part finale later this month.

If you are wondering when the next episodes will arrive and where to watch them, here is everything you need to know about the release schedule.

When Are The Remarried Empress Episodes 7 and 8 Releasing?

Episodes 7 and 8 of The Remarried Empress will be released on Wednesday, November 18, 2026.

The series follows a two-episode weekly release pattern after its special four-episode premiere. The first four episodes arrived on November 3, one day earlier than originally announced. Episodes 5 and 6 followed on November 11, while the next two episodes are scheduled for November 18.

The final two episodes, Episodes 9 and 10, will arrive on November 25. This means the complete 10-episode season will be available before the end of the month.

Disney has not announced an exact release time for the upcoming episodes. Viewers should check their regional streaming service on November 18 for availability.

What Happened in Episodes 5 and 6?

The latest episodes continued to explore the growing distance between Navier and Sovieshu, while Heinrey's interest in the empress became increasingly noticeable.

Rashta's actions also created fresh tension inside the palace. Her position as Sovieshu's favourite continues to affect the imperial household, but her growing confidence does not mean everyone is willing to accept her without question.

Meanwhile, Sovieshu's attempts to repair his relationship with Navier raise another question: does he genuinely want to make things right, or is he only beginning to understand what he might lose?

The episodes also bring more attention to Rashta's past, adding another layer to the problems surrounding her new life in the palace. With several relationships becoming more complicated, the next two episodes could be important for the direction of the story.

Where to Watch The Remarried Empress Episodes 7 and 8

The Remarried Empress is available to stream on Disney+ internationally, with Hulu carrying the series in the United States.

The platform's official announcement confirms that two new episodes will arrive every Wednesday until the November 25 finale. Viewers in India should check the JioHotstar catalogue for regional availability rather than assume that the international Disney+ listing applies to every market.

The series stars Shin Min-a as Empress Navier, Ju Ji-hoon as Emperor Sovieshu, Lee Jong-suk as Crown Prince Heinrey, and Lee Se-young as Rashta. It is a live-action adaptation of the popular NAVER WEBTOON of the same name.
