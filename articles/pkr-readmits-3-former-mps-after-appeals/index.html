PKR readmits 3 former MPs after appeals

TheTools · 03.10.2026 13:49 · source · permalink

The party's central leadership council has reportedly approved appeals from Edmund Santhara Kumar, Kamarudin Jaffar and Steven Choong.

The central leadership council approved the appeals from Santhara, Kamarudin and Choong at its Sept 27 meeting, according to Malaysiakini.

Santhara and Kamarudin were among 11 MPs who left PKR after the Sheraton Move in 2020 which caused the collapse of the Pakatan Harapan federal government and was replaced by a coalition headed by Bersatu. The pair later joined Bersatu.

Choong left PKR in February 2021 alongside Julau MP Larry Sng after they expressed support for the Perikatan Nasional government.

FMT is awaiting comment from Choong and PKR secretary-general Fuziah Salleh.

The letter approving their readmittance to PKR, however, stated that the decision did not alter the party’s position on a bond signed before the 2018 general election. “Any matters arising from this are subject to discussions between you, the treasurer-general and the president,” it read.

PKR had sued Santhara, Kamarudin and four other former MPs in February over alleged breaches of the bond, which required them to pay RM10 million if they won their seats on a PKR ticket but later resigned from the party, joined another political party or became independent representatives.

The return of the three MPs comes after former PKR vice-president Dr Xavier Jayakumar confirmed last month that he had returned to the party. PKR news portal Suara Keadilan also reported that former vice-president Tian Chua had been accepted back into the party.

The party also recently saw the exits of former deputy president Rafizi Ramli and former vice-president Nik Nazmi Nik Ahmad, who left in May to lead Bersama, as well as Wong Chen, who left in August.

Yesterday, Lee Chean Chung announced his resignation as Petaling Jaya MP and said he would quit the party on Monday.
