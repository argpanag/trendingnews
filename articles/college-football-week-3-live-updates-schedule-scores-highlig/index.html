Duke recovers own onside kick on opening kickoff!

As NC State-Vandy finished in unbelievable fashion and weather disrupted three other games, Duke decided to start with a jolt against Stanford.

In case you missed it, the Blue Devils surprised the Cardinals with an onside kick to start the game — and it worked. Stanford was set to receive the opening kickoff, but Duke had other plans. Sophomore Cosme Salas kicked the ball 12 yards before jumping on it himself to recover the kick, giving Duke the ball at their own 47.

NC State chokes game to Vandy in absurd fashion

Finally! We have some Week 3 drama in the FBS, and this ending just might dethrone the controversial Michigan-Western Michigan game. We'll let you decide. 

Picture this: with six seconds left on the clock, on fourth down, and NC State leading, 31-28, backed up on its own five-yard line, the Wolfpack opted to run backwards and concede the safety to run out the clock. Easy right? 

Well... things didn't go quite to plan, to say the least. Quarterback CJ Bailey scrambled in the end zone trying to run out the entire clock, but the Vanderbilt defense swarmed him, punching the ball out and recovering it for a touchdown and the win.

Iowa State RB Aiden Flora totals three 42+ TD runs

We didn't get much drama in the early games of Week 3, but little drama often leads to lopsided performances which was certainly the case in Ames, Iowa, today. 

Iowa State running back Aiden Flora racked up three touchdowns in a standout performance during his team's dominant 55-7 win over Bowling Green. The third-year back rushed for 194 yards and three touchdowns, including the 61- and 42-yard touchdown runs in the first half. 

His third touchdown came on his longest scramble of the day — a 65-yard punt return, dodging several Falcon defenders on his way to the end zone in the third quarter.

No. 7 Lehigh escapes Dartmouth in the FCS

The FBS hasn't provided much close-game action outside of Clemson-North Carolina, which has just entered a lightning delay, so let's turn our attention to some FCS action. 

The Ivy League was a little late to the college football party, not kicking off until Week 3, and Dartmouth was on track to pick up a huge upset win in its season opener over No. 7 Lehigh after carrying a 20-17 lead into the fourth quarter. 

Lehigh responded in the fourth, piecing together a 75-yard touchdown drive to retake a 24-20 lead, but Dartmouth was threatening just outside the red zone with 30 seconds to play.

Lehigh safety DJ Brown, however, spoiled the Big Green's opener with a one-handed interception headed toward the end zone to seal the game. 

The Mountain Hawks secured their 21st consecutive regular season win with the interception.

Last season, Dabo Swinney's Tigers led Bill Belichick's Tar Heels 28-3.... after the first quarter. But new year, new Tar Heels apparently, as North Carolina carries a 17-6 lead into the locker room in South Carolina.

FBS college football has made it to London!

The inaugural Union Jack Classic kicks off today at Wembley Stadium in London, featuring a Big 12 showdown between Arizona State and Kansas.

It marks the second international college football game of the season, following North Carolina's Week 0 victory over TCU in Dublin. College football has been played internationally since the 1880s with Harvard in Canada, however.

The NFL has established a strong presence over in London since 2007, so it was only a matter of time before college football made its way over. Expect a loud stadium as both universities brought their marching bands, cheerleaders and mascots, while about 60,000 fans are expected to pack Wembley.

FOX's Big Noon Kickoff made the trip over to London today, featuring an appearance from U.S. Men's National Team member, Chris Richards. The center back for Premier League club Crystal Palace and a native of Alabama, was a natural fit for college football's debut in London.

Ranked matchup thriller opens Week 3 of college football

What a start to Week 3 of college football! Four ranked teams were in action last night, with Miami QB Darian Mensah shining in a record performance.

No. 13 Texas Tech survives No. 22 Houston

A top-25 matchup came down to the last minute. The Cougars jumped out to an early 10-point lead in the first quarter, before the Red Raiders answered with two touchdowns in the second. 

Texas Tech regained the lead in the third quarter on a 29-yard touchdown run from J’Koby Williams. 

With 2:35 remaining and Houston trailing by eight, the Cougars marched 88 yards down the field for a touchdown. Attempting to tie the game, Houston QB Conner Weigman rushed for the two-point conversion but came up short of the goal line. The Raiders escaped with a 28-26 victory.

Weigman led all passers with 282 yards, completing 27 of 38 passes and throwing three touchdowns. Texas Tech QB Will Hammond threw for 257 yards, one touchdown and one interception.

No. 5 Miami outlasts Wake Forest

Both teams threw for touchdowns on their opening possessions, before Miami built a comfortable 24-7 lead at the half. 

Wake Forest opened the fourth quarter with a touchdown, followed by a safety, to cut the deficit to a two-score game. The Demon Deacons could not find their footing, however, turning the ball over on downs twice and throwing an interception as the Hurricanes sealed the win, 33-20.

Miami QB Darian Mensah delivered a standout performance, completing 30 of 34 passes for 220 yards and three touchdowns. He was perfect in the first half, completing his first 24 pass attempts and setting ACC records for consecutive completions marks.

The biggest storyline is far and away Lane Kiffin's return to Oxford as No. 7 LSU takes on No. 8 Ole Miss tonight.

No. 10 Alabama hosts Florida State after last season's stunner

Now, I am not claiming the Seminoles are going to win this game. I want to make that clear. But, no one thought FSU would win last year either... just saying. 

Kentucky held the lead over the Tide after 30 minutes last week after some sloppy first half ball from Kalen DeBoer's squad. If the Noles can force some similar chaos, things could get interesting in Tuscaloosa. 

One stat to mention though—Mike Norvell's squad has not won a road game in 1,029 days. So there's that. 

No. 16 SMU at No. 23 Louisville lines up as pivotal ACC showdown

Yes, this appears to be Miami's conference to lose. But, if another ACC team wants to find itself playing postseason football in the CFP, SMU and Louisville are the early favorites.

They are the two highest-ranked ACC teams outside of the Canes, and the Cardinals already proved they can compete with top-10 talent after taking Ole Miss to the wire in a 41-38 loss in Week 1.

Both quarterbacks have been on fire through two weeks, with Louisville's Lincoln Kienholz already eclipsing 600 yards and SMU's Kevin Jennings sitting at a modest 767 yards. Expect to see some points this afternoon. 

Whoever comes out on top will be in the driver's seat to make the conference championship game, as neither team faces Miami in the regular season.
