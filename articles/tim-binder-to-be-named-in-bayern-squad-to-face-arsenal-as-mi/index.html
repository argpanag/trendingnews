Tim Binder ‘to be named’ in Bayern squad to face Arsenal as Michael Olise is replaced

According to Sky, Tim Binder will be in Bayern Munich’s senior squad for the first time on Wednesday when they face Arsenal in the Champions League.

The 19-year-old takes Michael Olise’s place. Last week against Viking Stavanger, Olise received a second yellow card and, despite being a man down, the Norwegian underdogs equalised late to make it 3-3.

OneFootball Videos

Binder was a derby hero at the weekend. For Bayern’s second team in the Regionalliga against 1860 Munich, he assisted Anton Heinz’s opener then scored himself for 2-0.

He then sparked tempers by celebrating in front of the home end at the Grünwalder Stadion, cupping his left ear towards 1860 supporters before sliding on his knees to the corner flag.

Highly regarded in Bayern’s academy, Binder joined from FC Augsburg’s youth set-up in 2019. Comfortable on either wing, he is a Germany under-19 international.

A few weeks ago he signed a professional contract running until 2030. Sporting director Christoph Freund has praised his potential, speed, dribbling strength and character, noting his pre-season work with the first team and steady development on the club’s campus pathway.
