Fulham vs Crystal Palace: Team News and Confirmed Lineups for Oct. 28

TheTools · 28.10.2026 18:00 · source · permalink

Here’s the latest team news and the confirmed lineups ahead of the Fulham vs Crystal Palace clash this evening at Craven Cottage.

Palace suffered yet another disappointing defeat on the weekend to Newcastle, while Fulham won their second game in a row with a hard-fought win at Coventry.

Palace did win at Craven Cottage just last month and hold a superb record in West London, with Fulham last beating Palace at home in January 2005.

Fulham vs Crystal Palace: Team News and Confirmed Lineups

Fulham Team News

Fulham boss Alvaro Arbeloa has enjoyed his last couple of weeks as Fulham gaffer after a difficult start and plans to go strong for this Carabao Cup tie.

“I expect to put the strongest team possible out tomorrow; obviously we have another match in three days, but I am only thinking about winning this game,” stated Arbeloa in his pre-match press conference.

Ryan Sessegnon will miss out as he serves a three-match suspension, and Emile Smith Rowe may also miss out after being left out of the squad for the win over Coventry.

Fulham will fancy their chances against an unsettled Palace defence, which has conceded twelve in their last four competitive games.

Crystal Palace Team News

Palace manager Pierre Sage has endured a difficult couple of weeks, with disappointing defeats to Lyon, Brighton and most recently Newcastle.

There is some positive news for Sage, however, with the return of captain Dean Henderson after more than a month on the sidelines.

“I think he will play on Wednesday. For Ismaila Sarr, it’s difficult because his groin is really making him struggle; we will see on Wednesday about Jorgen Strand Larsen,” outlined Sage.

Palace have won in their last four trips to Craven Cottage, which included a 3-0 win over Fulham in their 2025 FA Cup winning run.

Fulham Confirmed Lineup and Substitutes

Starting XI: Lecomte, Tete, Andersen, Bassey (C), Robinson, Berge, Larsson, Iwobi, Bobb, Muniz, Palacios.

Subs: Leno, Castagne, Cuenca, Reed, Cairney, Manuel Ángel, King, Kevin, Gonzalo.

Crystal Palace Confirmed Lineup and Substitutes

Starting XI: Henderson (C), Mingueza, Lerma, Richards, Ahanor, Chilwell, Hughes, Doucouré, Osorio, Guessand, Nketiah.

Subs: Benítez, Disasi, Khalaili, Mitchell, Kamada, Wharton, McNeil, Pino, Gozo.

Last Word: Match Prediction

Both sides will back themselves to progress in this London derby, but Fulham seem to be in a better place than Palace at this moment.

Palace seemingly can’t solve their defensive problems, and if Fulham put out a strong side, then they will fancy themselves to turn over the Eagles.

With Palace also competing in the Europa League, a rotated side could be played, and an unsettled defence could help Fulham progress to the Carabao Cup quarter-final.

Match Prediction: Fulham 3-1 Crystal Palace
