PSG: the group summoned for the trip to Brest

TheTools · 13.09.2026 09:41 · source · permalink

At the end of the 4th day of Ligue 1, Paris Saint-Germain travels to Brest this Sunday evening. While the reigning French champion has still not won a single match in the league (two draws and a defeat), this meeting already appears to be crucial for Luis Enrique’s men.

This Sunday morning, PSG also communicated its group of 21 players which includes new recruits Godts, Akliouche and Torres. Also note the presence of Dembélé and Kvaratskhelia. Young 17-year-old goalkeeper, Adam Mouak is also called by the Spanish technician.
