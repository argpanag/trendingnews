Michael Owen warns Man Utd has 'free hit' at Liverpool after Pochettino change

TheTools · 21.11.2026 07:00 · source · permalink

Michael Owen has had his say on the game(Image: Getty Images)

Former Liverpool forward Michael Owen believes this weekend's clash between the Reds and Manchester United at Anfield has added spice to it following the appointment of Mauricio Pochettino.

On Sunday, the Reds welcome Man United to Anfield in what will be Pochettino's first game in charge since opting to leave his post with the USMNT to replace the outgoing Michael Carrick. While Liverpool sits fourth in the Premier League, United's campaign has been calamitous this season.

Through 10 games of the 2026/27 Premier League season, United sit inside the relegation zone, with them having won just once in the league so far. The bad start to the season cost Carrick his job, with the club replacing him with Pochettino, who previously coached Tottenham Hotspur and Chelsea.

In Owen's eyes, Sunday's game at Anfield is a free hit for United, with him of the belief that while Liverpool have picked up results of late to climb up to fourth, the arrival of Pochettino could provide the former with an added boost.

Speaking exclusively to Liverpool.com, via Casino.org, a platform designed to help British players make sense of the different online casinos operating in the UK, Owen said, "[There is] added spice now, of course, with the managerial situation [Pochettino's appointment].

"Liverpool have sort of perked up a bit in recent weeks. Obviously, they’re back up into an area of the table you would expect them to be in.

Mauricio Pochettino will take charge of Manchester United for the first time against Liverpool(Image: Getty Images)

"And with Man City dropping a few points, all of a sudden, it just looks like they’re in a competitive position again."

Owen, who won the Premier League in 2008/09 with United, continued, "And then at the other end, Manchester United are in a place they can’t believe they’re there.

"I think the new manager is going to be the headline act; it’s almost like a free hit. No one expects them to get anything from a trip to Anfield, but, new manager, that bounce factor you can sometimes have."

Manchester United beat Liverpool at Anfield last season

The former Liverpool forward also believes that Anfield is not the fortress it once was, which he thinks could be a factor should United manage to come away with a result against the Reds.

In each of the last three games between the two sides at Anfield, Liverpool has failed to beat United. Last season, United came away with their first win at Anfield in almost a decade after beating the Reds 2-1.

"Liverpool’s not the most intimidating place as things stand," added Owen.

"Obviously Arsenal and Man City beating them in recent times, it’s not like the Anfield of three or four years ago when it was virtually impossible to get anything. So you never know."
