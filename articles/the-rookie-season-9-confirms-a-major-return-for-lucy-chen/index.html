‘The Rookie’ Season 9 Confirms a Major Return for Lucy Chen

TheTools · 04.11.2026 19:44 · source · permalink

Not many stars have seen as strong an uptick in their popularity in the last few years as Nathan Fillion, who is a frequent collaborator with James Gunn, the new co-CEO of DC Studios. Fillion and Gunn have been working together since long before Gunn took the role at DC, though, as their collaborations date back to the first Guardians of the Galaxy movie, which was released in theaters in 2014. In Gunn’s new DCU, Fillion has settled into the role of Guy Gardner, one of the Green Lanterns who is tasked with protecting Earth. After debuting in the role in Superman (2025), he reprised the role in Peacemaker and Lanterns, and he’s even set to return next year in the first DCU crossover event film, Man of Tomorrow.

However, one character who Fillion has played far more than any under James Gunn is John Nolan, who he first played all the way back in 2018 in the first season of The Rookie. ABC has released new seasons of The Rookie regularly, with Season 9 set to be released on ABC on January 6, 2027, exactly one year after the premiere of Season 8. ABC is keeping things tight-lipped about The Rookie Season 9 for now, but news broke not long ago that Dylan Conrique will reprise her role as Tamara Collins in the next season. She has not appeared in the show since Season 7. She was first introduced all the way back in Season 3, and has since become a critical ally for Lucy Chen, who is played by Melissa O'Neil. Details about what role Tamara will serve in Season 9 are being kept under wraps for now.

Has ‘The Rookie’ Been Renewed for Season 10?

ABC has not officially picked up The Rookie for Season 10, and this decision will likely come down to how the show performs when it returns in two months. It took ABC longer than usual to renew The Rookie for Season 9, but the network ultimately decided it was worth it to move forward with another season. Fillion has expressed interest in playing the character of John Nolan for at least another 10 years, so if the show goes off the air before then, it likely won’t be because he was pushing for it to happen.
