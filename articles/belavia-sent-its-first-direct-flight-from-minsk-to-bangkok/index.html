"Belavia" sent its first direct flight from Minsk to Bangkok

TheTools · 25.10.2026 23:05 · source · permalink

The first plane to the capital of Thailand departed from Minsk on the night of October 25. Flights will operate once a week, on Sundays, reports Onlíner.

Departure from Minsk — at 03:10, arrival in Bangkok — at 17:05. The flight will take 9 hours and 55 minutes.

The return flight departs at 19:05 and arrives in Minsk at 01:50 the next day. The return journey will take about an hour longer. All times are local.

"Belavia" already flies to Thailand: in January 2026, the company started operating charter flights to Phuket and Pattaya. Bangkok has become a new destination.
