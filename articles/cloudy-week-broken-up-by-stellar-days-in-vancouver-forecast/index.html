Cloudy week broken up by 'stellar' days in Vancouver forecast

TheTools · 14.10.2026 06:01 · source · permalink

While clouds are certainly in Vancouver's forecast this week, there are bright spots as well.

And while the region is often hit by an autumnal storm by this point, Environment Canada meteorologist Morgen Shull says it'll be another week without a heavy-duty weather system.

Metro Vancouver will be mainly cloudy on Tuesday (Oct 13) but remain dry through the day, with highs around 14 C, Shull says. But overnight, a system is expected to slide down the coast and over the region.

That'll bring showers with five to 10 millimitres of precipitation, lasting into Wednesday morning. The majority of that will fall before sunrise, though.

"I don't think we're looking at anything substantial as far as rainfall amounts, but we'll definitely see some moisture," Shull says.

By Wednesday afternoon, the city should begin to dry out with sun peeking through the clouds, foreshadowing the next two days. A high of 15 C is forecast.

The sunny skies will remain for a couple of days.

"Thursday and Friday look absolutely stellar," Shull says.

Highs of 17 C are expected in Vancouver on Thursday, she says, with highs peaking even higher inland, up to 22 C. Friday will be more of the same, if not a degree warmer. For context, average highs this time of year are 14 C.

A bit of high pressure and a southerly flow are the reason for the burst of warm weather, she explains. The heat may cause some fog Thursday morning.

That said, overnight it will still cool off, especially on Thursday, when temperatures are expected to hit 8 C.

Friday likely won't cool off as much, due to clouds moving in for the evening along with a new system.

"It doesn't look like anything substantial, but it does look like we'll be getting wet again Friday night and into Saturday morning," Shull says.

While the system is expected to move quickly, it still might drop 10 mm by Saturday morning. While the sunshine is expected to return midday on Saturday, it won't get as warm as it was the prior two days, with a peak of 15 C predicted.

Sunday is forecast to start off clear, but cloud cover will likely move in later in the day.

"There is a system that is approaching Sunday night," Shull says. "That one looks like it is a little more intense."

Higher-speed winds and some higher amounts of precipitation are very likely, she adds.
