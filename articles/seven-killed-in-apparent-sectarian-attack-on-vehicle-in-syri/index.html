At least seven people were killed and four others injured when an armed attack targeted a vehicle carrying employees of the al-Mateen company in western Homs province on Wednesday, according to the Syrian Observatory for Human Rights.

The vehicle was attacked while crossing a bridge close to the al-Houla area, the observatory said, adding that most of those killed and injured were from the Alawite village of Fahel in Homs.

The observatory said the attack directly struck the vehicle carrying engineers and workers. The injured were taken to medical centres and hospitals, while security forces were deployed to the area.

The death toll could rise, the observatory said, as some of the injured were reported to be in critical condition.

There was no immediate information on who carried out the attack or what type of weapon was used.

The Syrian Observatory for Human Rights described the incident as a sectarian attack and called for an immediate and independent investigation to establish the circumstances of the incident and identify those responsible.

The attack comes amid continued security tensions and sporadic violence in parts of Syria, where sectarian tensions have remained a sensitive issue.
