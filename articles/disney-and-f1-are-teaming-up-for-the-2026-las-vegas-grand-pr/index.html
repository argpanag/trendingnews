Disney and F1 are Teaming Up for the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix

TheTools · 23.11.2026 00:05 · source · permalink

Quick Facts

  • Disney and F1 have a slew of collab merch releasing for the Vegas Grand Prix
  • In addition to the apparel and collectibles, the two will host activations in Las Vegas
  • Check below for details on all the Disney x F1 collab

This content may contain affiliate links. If you make a purchase through these links, we may earn a commission.

This weekend, Formula 1 and its massive fanbase will descend upon Nevada for the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix. And with hundreds of thousands entering the Vegas Valley, brands across the globe also join in on the action. For Disney, which last year took over the Fountains of Bellagio to celebrate F1 in Vegas, 2026 is a chance to go even harder on its F1 tie-ins.

Marvel Studios Avengers: Doomsday Takeover

While fans will have to wait for Avengers: Doomsday to hit theaters December 18, those in Vegas can count down the days to Doomsday with multiple activations across Vegas. The Sphere will be taken over by the franchise, while a custom-branded Archer Aviation air taxi and trackside Formula 1 show car help celebrate the latest Avengers film.

New Disney x Formula 1 Product

Hot Wheels x Disney x F1

Of course, Disney knows how to celebrate events with product launches, and they didn’t skimp on the 2026 Vegas Grand Prix. Disney, F1, and Hot Wheels join forces for a collectible vehicle in a black, red, and yellow livery that’s inspired by Mickey Mouse’s signature color palette. Included are three sets of interchangeable Pirelli tires. The car is available to purchase now online, but if you’re in Vegas, you can see a full-size Hot Wheels F1 car at the East Harmon Fan Zone.

Culture Kings x Disney x F1

Streetwear brand Culture Kings crafted an apparel capsule with Disney and F1, which is perfect, given the brand’s motorsport-inspired aesthetic. Included are hoodies, knitwear, jackets, and headwear, with some highlights including a jersey-inspired design featuring a classic Mickey Mouse caricature, a leather jacket, and graphic T-shirts. The drop is available on Culture Kings’ website.

Uniqlo UT x Disney x F1

Finally, Disney completes its 2026 F1 collab collection with a three-way link-up with Uniqlo. Coming under the Uniqlo UT label, 14 different apparel items ranging from men’s, women’s, and kids’ styles merge Mickey Mouse and F1 graphics. A co-branded Mickey Mouse and F1 logo anchors most of the designs. The collection is available now through Uniqlo online and in-store.
