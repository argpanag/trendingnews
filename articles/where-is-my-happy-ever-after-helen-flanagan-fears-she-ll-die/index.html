'Where is my happy ever after?' Helen Flanagan fears she'll die alone

TheTools · 10.11.2026 16:46 · source · permalink

Helen Flanagan has revealed her fears she’ll die alone as an 'old hag’. 

The actress, 36, asked fans on TikTok 'Where is my happy ever after?',  just hours after admitting she felt 'sad and lonely' as she took a swipe at her ex Scott Sinclair. 

Uploading a video of herself with a face mask, Helen wrote: 'Being brought up on Disney films f***s you up and thinking you’re a Disney princess.

'Where is my happy ever after? Where’s my prince? Will I ever get married? I’m 36. Or am I going to die an old HAG on my own? Just sharing thoughts.'

The actress, who is mother to Matilda, 11, Delilah, eight, and Charlie, five, with Scott, 37, was in an emotional mood as she shared a post hours earlier with the caption: 'Being a single mum is really hard but not as hard as being in a hard relationship with kids x...

'Still hard x You pick your hard x When I’m older, I’ll look back and know that I did it x I did it when I was stressed, sad and lonely x.'
