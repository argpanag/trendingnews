Kick Launches Events Hub for Livestream Discovery and Multiview

TheTools · 16.10.2026 22:39 · source · permalink

Livestreaming platform Kick announced a dedicated events hub on October 16, 2026, giving viewers a central place to discover live and upcoming broadcasts, including esports competitions, creator collaborations, and real-life streams. The feature also supports watching up to four broadcasts simultaneously on desktop, while creators can apply to have their events featured.

According to win.gg, Kick Events organizes broadcasts in one location rather than requiring viewers to search individual channels. Creators continue streaming through their own channels, while the hub provides a dedicated directory for ongoing and scheduled events.

The multiview feature allows desktop users to arrange up to four streams in a shared layout or emphasize one broadcast while displaying the others in smaller windows. Only the active stream's audio plays, and viewers can switch between the participating streams' chats.

Users can add or remove broadcasts, but multiview requires at least two streams. Kick previously announced the functionality in September 2026 through a partnership with Prodigy, according to win.gg.

Kick has also introduced other platform features, including unban requests and daily rewards. The company released a mobile application earlier in the year as part of its efforts to support creators producing real-life content.

Creators must apply through Kick's dedicated portal to have an event included in the hub. The platform reviews submissions and decides which applications to approve, according to win.gg.

After approval, Kick contacts organizers with instructions for providing event images and other details. Hosts can then invite participating creators, meaning streamers cannot independently join an event simply by adding a keyword to their broadcast titles.

The platform has not published specific criteria governing which events receive placement in the hub. Its current listings include Brand Risk 16, Safe Zone, COB Halloween Bash, and Valorant Champions 2026, alongside previously held events such as MrBeast Team Water and GTA VI Launch.

The listings include events that are underway or scheduled for the future, as well as records of past broadcasts. According to win.gg, major creators and events attracting substantial audiences are likely candidates for prominent placement, although Kick has not confirmed formal selection criteria.

The hub could also provide viewers with a way to discover creators they do not already follow. Event organizers retain control over invitations, allowing them to determine which streamers participate in their broadcasts.
