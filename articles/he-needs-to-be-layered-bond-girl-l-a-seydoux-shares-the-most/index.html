'He Needs To Be Layered': Bond Girl Léa Seydoux Shares The Most Important Traits For The Next 007

TheTools · 06.10.2026 09:22 · source · permalink

What it takes to play James Bond from the actor who’s played his most influential love interest.

If there’s one Bond girl who has influenced the course of 007 history more than anyone, it’s Dr Madeleine Swann. Played by French actor Léa Seydoux, who is this month’s ELLE cover star, psychotherapist Dr Swann disrupts the James Bond narrative in a way that no previous lover has done: she challenges his misogyny.

Appearing in the last two films in the series – Spectre (2015) and No Time To Die (2021) - Dr Swann has a long-term relationship with secret agent 007, eventually telling him that he’s the father to her daughter, making Seydoux’s part in the legendary franchise unique.

Asked whether she would consider a return to the franchise, now under the creative direction of Amazon MGM Studios and without Daniel Craig as 007, the actor said: ‘I really loved working with Barbara Broccoli and her brother Michael [G Wilson]. It was one of the best experiences, a pure delight. And I don’t know, it will be different.’

She has already worked with Denis Villeneuve, the director attached to the next Bond film, in Dune: Part Two. ‘He’s a great choice, let’s see. I’m curious how they will reinvent it. But for me, it will never be the same because it was Barbara’s father who initiated the Bond movies. Now, I don’t know,’ she told ELLE. ‘It became American… I hope they will keep the British spirit.’

As for casting Seydoux in the role, former Bond producer Barbara Broccoli said that her scenes in No Time To Die still move her to tears. ‘When we were filming the goodbye scene at the Italian train station, Léa approached it with such subtlety – she grasped her stomach to express her pain, but also the secret heartbreak that she was carrying James’ child. It moved me to tears then and continues to move me each time I watch it.’

As for the question of who will play Bond, she won’t be drawn into the speculation, although she has heard Jack Lowden as the latest name to be touted. Seydoux admits that finding the right person to replace Daniel Craig is difficult. ‘He needs to be layered, you know? And that is something that’s hard to find in young actors.’
