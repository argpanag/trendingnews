Honda’s Boss Says Australia Doesn’t Need Acura, So the Avancier Stays a Honda | CarSauce

TheTools · 20.11.2026 03:45 · source · permalink

Honda Australia CEO Jay Joseph tells CarSauce Australia does not need another brand, with the 2027 Avancier to arrive wearing a Honda badge.

Honda Australia CEO Jay Joseph tells CarSauce Australia does not need another brand, with the 2027 Avancier to arrive wearing a Honda badge.

Jacob Brooke

November 20, 2026 at 3:45 AM

“If there's one thing that Australia does not need more of, it's another brand,” Honda Australia President and CEO Jay Joseph told CarSauce, ruling out a local launch for Acura.

Speaking at the Australian reveal of the 2027 Honda Avancier on Friday 20 November 2026, Joseph was asked whether the upmarket SUV should have arrived under Honda's luxury badge.

Joseph added that the “Honda brand has plenty of cachet”.

The three-row hybrid Avancier will therefore wear a Honda badge when it reaches Australian Honda Centres in April 2027, rather than introduce Acura to the local market.

Acura is sold mainly in North America and has never been sold in Australia. Honda considered launching the luxury brand here in the mid-2000s before ruling it out in 2008.

At the time, Honda's regional boss for Asia and Oceania said the Honda brand was strong enough in the region that a separate luxury badge was unnecessary. Joseph's comments echo that position nearly 20 years later, with Australia's new-car market now crowded by brands including numerous recent Chinese arrivals.

Joseph became President and CEO of Honda Australia in April 2025 after decades at American Honda in the United States, where Acura is sold.

As detailed in CarSauce's coverage of the Avancier's Australian reveal, the SUV will sit above the CR-V as Honda Australia's flagship and mark its first large SUV here in almost 20 years.

It will also be the first Australian Honda with the company's next-generation Honda Hybrid technology, featuring an eAWD system with rear-wheel electric drive.

The Avancier introduces a new global Honda design direction and an updated Honda logo. Australian pricing and full specifications remain unconfirmed, with more details due in early 2027.
