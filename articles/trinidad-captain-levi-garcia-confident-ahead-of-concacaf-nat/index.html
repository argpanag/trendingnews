Trinidad Captain Levi Garcia Confident Ahead of CONCACAF Nations League Clash

TheTools · 24.09.2026 23:58 · source · permalink

Trinidad and Tobago faces Haiti in a CONCACAF Nations League League A Group A fixture on Thursday, September 24, 2026, at Sabina Park in Kingston, Jamaica, with kickoff set for 8:20 PM EDT.

Haiti enters the match following a 4-2 defeat to Morocco on June 24 at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta, where Yassine Bounou scored an own goal and Wilson Isidor scored for Haiti. Meanwhile, Trinidad and Tobago lost 3-0 to Russia in a friendly match on June 9.

Haiti earned promotion to League A after going 6-0 in League B during the previous cycle with 29 goals scored, while Trinidad and Tobago finished fourth in their League A group last season with five points. Following the departure of manager Dwight Yorke in February, Derek King took charge of Trinidad and Tobago, while Spanish coach David Badia leads Haiti in his managerial debut.

Addressing off-field issues surrounding unpaid player salaries that previously threatened a potential squad boycott, 28-year-old Panathinaikos forward Levi Garcia expressed full confidence in his national team's readiness despite recent administrative challenges.

"Firstly, it's always a pleasure to be here, and it's always good to join the boys," said Levi Garcia, captain of Trinidad and Tobago. "We know the importance of the camp at the moment. We have tough opponents ahead, but I have full confidence in what we are going to try to accomplish."

The squad has resolved major off-field disputes to concentrate fully on upcoming international competition.

"Of course, you know, there are things that always need to be dealt with, and this is in the past," said Levi Garcia, captain of Trinidad and Tobago. "We are still finishing up on a couple of things, but so far things have been going well, and this is something that needs to be done in the professional world."

Garcia highlighted the team's balance of experienced players alongside newly integrated teammates.

"So these are things we have to be able to balance both on and off the field," said Levi Garcia, captain of Trinidad and Tobago. "So we came here to get the job done, and we're going to try our best."

The captain emphasized squad readiness and the positive mindset present in training camp.

"I think the boys are really confident," said Levi Garcia, captain of Trinidad and Tobago. "Now, I've seen our confident boys and a lot of new faces, but not that many at the same time. I believe that they are ready for the job ahead, and we're going to go give our all."

Ahead of fixtures against recent World Cup participants, Garcia urged his teammates to execute opportunities decisively.

"We know they are confident in," said Levi Garcia, captain of Trinidad and Tobago. "We just have to show what we have been showing, and whenever we get our opportunities, try our best to take them. We respect all our opponents, but we have to remind them that we are Trinidad and Tobago and we are also giants in the Caribbean. So, I believe with confidence, we're going to get the job done."

New call-ups Luke Phillip, Andrew De Gannes, Jahiem Joseph, and goalkeeper Jahiem Wickham joined the squad for this window.

"I think the importance of that is key because I'm no longer at a young age anymore, and what I've seen all these years is something that I've thought about a lot, and new guys come in and try to meet the environment as comfortably as possible, knowing the importance of representing the Red, White, and Black," said Levi Garcia, captain of Trinidad and Tobago. "So once these guys settle in, you'll like for them to have the best international career possible."

Managing dense fixture schedules across domestic clubs and national duty presents physical recovery challenges.

"Yeah, it is a bit tricky because knowing the amount of games you have at club level coming into the international team would be difficult, but you know we do whatever it takes to get our bodies recovered, and yeah, we have to do what we have to do," said Levi Garcia, captain of Trinidad and Tobago.

Garcia highlighted the importance of collective effort against competitive opposition.

"Tonight will be a collective one because we know, as I said before, Haiti is coming off a World Cup campaign, and they are confident, but I have full confidence in my team," said Levi Garcia, captain of Trinidad and Tobago. "Of course we need to do our individual stuff to go and get a victory or get whatever results need to be done. I would like to have a victory, and I have full confidence in the boys, and we're going to go for it."

Trinidad and Tobago hosts the Dominican Republic on Sunday at Hasely Crawford Stadium in Mucurapo at 9 PM.

"It will be my first time playing at home since the World Cup qualifiers, and of course I know that there was something a few months back or a few weeks back that, of course, caused a little bit of havoc, but hopefully we could get a proper result and be able to perform for our fans," said Levi Garcia, captain of Trinidad and Tobago.

The match airs on FOX Soccer Plus and stream on Fubo, while both teams compete in Group A alongside Costa Rica, Curaçao, Dominican Republic, and Nicaragua.
