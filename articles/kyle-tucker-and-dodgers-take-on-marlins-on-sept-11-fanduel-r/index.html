Kyle Tucker

Los Angeles Dodgers • #23 • RF

Kyle Tucker and his Los Angeles Dodgers will square off against the Miami Marlins at loanDepot park, on Friday, Sept. 11 at 7:10 p.m. ET. Tucker has +680 odds to hit a home run as of Friday afternoon.

What It Means

Tucker is hitting for a .225 BA, .323 OBP and .361 SLG with a 17.6% strikeout rate and a 12.4% walk rate. His OPS is .684 and he has scored 76 runs. In 563 plate appearances, he has hit 13 home runs and driven in 60 runs. Tucker has recorded 10 steals on 10 attempts. In his previous appearance, he collected two extra-base hits (3 for 5 with a double, a home run and an RBI) against the Reds.

Ryan Gusto (1-4 with a 4.23 ERA and 62 strikeouts in 72 1/3 innings pitched) makes the start for the Marlins, his 16th of the season.
