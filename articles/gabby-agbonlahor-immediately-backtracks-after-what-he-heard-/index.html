Gabby Agbonlahor was forced into an immediate backtrack when speaking about James Tarkowski's hairpull controversy in Everton's win versus Coventry City.

Before the international break, David Moyes' side earned a 3-1 win over Coventry City, with Thierno Barry scoring twice.

However, it was a that was high on incident, and Toffees skipper James Tarkowski was at the heart of it.

The experienced centre-back avoided punishment for a hairpull on Coventry's Caleb Yirenkyi.

It was a flashpoint in the game that sparked plenty of debate about what course of action referee Stuart Attwell should have taken.

Two weeks on, it reared its head again, with former Aston Villa striker Agbonlahor having to change his mind.

The controversy has reared its head again after the Premier League's KMI Panel made its final ruling on the controversy.

At the time of the incident, a VAR check occurred to see if there was a handball from Tarkowski.

However, there was not a review of the hairpull from Tarkowski, which was deemed an error.

Indeed, the panel voted unanimously that a penalty should have been given.

Agbonlahor, though, at first stated it should have been a red card, clearly not aware that the rules were changed in the summer to state that the severity of the force is what determines if it should be a sending off.

He said live on talkSPORT: "Everyone knows now you pull the hair a little bit, Tarkowski's the sort of player you suspect would do it on purpose as well, I don't know if he's got previous has he of doing it? But it's a blatant red card and that can change the game can't it for Coventry?

"Coventry end up losing the game, did they against Everton? If you're Frank Lampard, these fine margins, these little decisions, they mean a lot to a club like Coventry if they want to stay in the Premier League."

However, Matterface quickly pointed out it was not an automatic red card.

He said: "I don't think it needs to be a red card for that and I don't think they would have given a red card but they would have given a penalty which would have changed the nature of the game."

Agbonlahor replied: "Isn't it a red card for pulling hair, though?"

Matterface merely said: "It depends on how violently you do it."

That led to a comical "ohhhhhhh" reaction from Agbonlahor, who realised immediately he had to backtrack.

It's pretty clear that Everton got away with one against Coventry.

It would not have been a red card against Tarkowski, but clearly a penalty should have been given.

Sometimes you just kind of have to take the good fortune when it comes don't you?

Let's face it Everton have had plenty of shoddy decisions go against them in the past, so the Toffees were due a bit of luck.
