Angelina Jolie Reveals What Her 6 Children Want for Her Now—and What They’re Like as Adults

TheTools · 10.09.2026 15:16 · source · permalink

She gave a rare interview where she discussed Maddox, Pax, Zahara, Shiloh, Vivienne, and Knox.

THE RUNDOWN

  • Angelina Jolie discussed her children in a rare interview with L’Officiel.
  • Now that her kids are all grown up, Jolie is planning to spend more time abroad—and shared what Maddox, Pax, Zahara, Shiloh, Vivienne, and Knox want for her.
  • She also touched on Maddox and Pax’s careers, along with Zahara’s HBCU experience at Spelman College and how honored she was to be part of it.

Now that her youngest children, twins Vivienne and Knox, are officially 18 years old, Angelina Jolie is charting the next chapter of her life. The actress gave a rare interview to L’Officiel in which she candidly discussed her six kids and shed some light on their interests. Jolie’s older children are 25-year-old Maddox, 22-year-old Pax, 21-year-old Zahara, and 20-year-old Shiloh.

“They’re definitely individuals,” she said of the group. “That makes me very proud, because one of the most important things, I think, is to basically not get in their way. As a parent, you try to do many things, but you also try to not get in the way of this human being who has a life separate from yours and so much potential in their own mind. You want to make sure that you support them.”

She mentioned Maddox and Pax’s work as assistant directors, sharing why they gravitated toward the job and what else they’ve been pursuing, “I think if you have any interest in working on film, you need to be a part of the crew and do the hard work and know all the different departments,” she said. “They’ve worked on a few films that way, and they’re both doing other things, too: Maddox is focusing on being a pilot, and Pax has his photography, but I think he does have an interest in film. Obviously, it’s nice for me to be around them, but I am just Mom, wanting to be around her family.”

Jolie also discussed Zahara’s experience at Spelman College and how touched she was to watch her daughter’s journey at the HBCU. (Zahara graduated this May.) “I just felt so deeply honored to be a part of it,” she said. “I came in knowing that she belonged and it was a special place for her. I didn’t assume I would be as welcomed and educated and supported as I was. There’s no place that would’ve been better for her—it really transformed her and it’s really a part of our family now. They actually pulled me aside when she became an AKA [member of Alpha Kappa Alpha, the historically Black sorority]. I didn’t know what that really meant, but they talked to me about how it was for life. It’s an extraordinary place. Our family felt so proud of her, so honored to be a part of it and to feel like we were welcomed into something that is very special, unique, and important.”

The actress spoke more broadly about her children’s relationship with her, as well. “They know me pretty well,” she said. “For better or worse, they know all sides of me.”

As she prepares to spend more time outside the U.S., her children have their own hopes for her next chapter, too.

When asked what she wants now, Jolie said, “I really love and am looking forward to more connection and community. I’m not somebody who vacations or shops or things like that. I envision more field work, more travel, and just being out in the world. Get my motorcycle back, get back in a plane. I don’t know. That’s definitely what my kids want! They had Mom when Mom needed to be Mom. Now, they’d like me to call them from Tunisia having just landed a plane to meet with local artists. I agree that would be very nice.”
