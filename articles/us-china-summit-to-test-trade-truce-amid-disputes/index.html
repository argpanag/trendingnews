US-China summit to test trade truce amid disputes

TheTools · 19.09.2026 00:04 · source · permalink

Have a question, feedback, or need assistance with your account?

BEIJING: Trade frictions between the world’s top two economies are set to take centre stage next week when US President Donald Trump hosts Chinese counterpart Xi Jinping at the White House.

Although a shaky truce was reached last October that halted the frenzied escalating tariff battle launched by Trump, a comprehensive and permanent deal has remained elusive.

Here is where things stand ahead of the summit, which is expected to take place on Sept 24. Tariffs have represented a major hurdle in the China-United States trade relationship since 2018, during Trump’s first term.

In April 2025, the base US tariff rate on Chinese goods reached an eye-watering 145% – matched by China’s reciprocal levies of 125% – although both sides have since significantly reduced rates.
