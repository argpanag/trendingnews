Taylor Swift has fans guessing after a mysterious countdown appeared on her website Tuesday. The two-hour timer was set to end at 2 p.m. ET/11 a.m. PT, sparking speculation about a possible announcement. Fans wondered whether the pop star could finally reveal her wedding photos or tease new music.

Taylor Swift's website launched a two-hour countdown as fans speculated about what she may announce

Swift's countdown immediately caught fans' attention online. Speculation ranged from new music to the long-awaited release of photos from her July wedding to Travis Kelce.

The excitement was especially visible under an X post from @PopBase. The account shared the news with the caption, "? Taylor Swift launches website countdown, ending today at 2pm ET." Fans flooded the post with reactions. One user wrote, "She doesn't do a countdown for nothing,, whatever is behind those curtains, she's going to deliver."

Another joked, "Taylor really knows how to turn a countdown into a worldwide emergency ?." The excitement continued with comments such as, "IM SO FAWKING EXCITED OH MY GOD" and "SHE COMING EVERYBODY MOVE."

Swift, 36, has used countdowns and cryptic messages before. They have previously teased new albums, photoshoots and limited-edition merchandise. This time, she also changed her Twitter and Instagram bios. Both now read, "And, baby, that's sh0w business f0r y0u" alongside a fire heart, with zeros replacing the letter O.

Meanwhile, Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce's wedding photos remain one of the biggest theories among fans. Swift and Kelce married at Madison Square Garden on July 4. However, no photos from inside the ceremony have been released.

Swift's publicist Tree Paine previously confirmed that the couple wore custom Christian Dior designs. Swift also wore Cartier jewelry and custom Christian Louboutin shoes.

As per Daily Mail, the ceremony included both families. Austin Swift served as Taylor's Man of Honor, while Jason Kelce was Travis' Best Man. Their friend Adam Sandler officiated the ceremony, according to Paine's statement. Swift reportedly performed for the guests after the ceremony. Stevie Nicks and Paul McCartney were also reportedly involved in the celebration.

Still, strict non-disclosure agreements kept details and images from the event private. Now, the mysterious countdown has given fans another reason to wonder whether that could finally change.
