ATP Basel Best Bets Including Fritz vs Fils

TheTools · 30.10.2026 02:00 · source · permalink

The ATP Basel tournament, also known as the Swiss Indoors, continues into the quarterfinals in Switzerland. This is a 500-point tournament which runs concurrently with the 500-point event in Vienna. It’s a chance for players in the ATP race to Turin to bank a significant amount of points and change the math in their favor before next week’s defining Paris Masters event, the 1,000-point bonanza which will finally and fully decide which eight players get to Italy for the year-end championship and which ones are either staying home or get to go to Italy as first alternate just in case a retirement or injury occurs. It’s crunch time for the ATP players on the margins of the race, those above and just below the cut line. A key point to remember is that with Jannik Sinner out, No. 9 in the race gets into the ATP Finals as the eighth and final player. Sinner is No. 2 in the race but won’t play in Turin due to his season-ending injury. The goal is to be first in this 2026 ATP Basel event, but ninth or better in the race in two weeks. The chase is on. LWOS has our best bets for four matches. Post your best bets and thoughts in the comments.

ATP Basel

Jerome Kym – Jiri Lehecka: 13:30 EST

H2H: 0-0

This ATP Basel match is not what anyone expected. Jerome Kym sent shockwaves through the tournament but delighted the Basel crowd when the Swiss knocked out 2026 US Open runner-up Ben Shelton in the Round of 16. Kym notched one of the best wins of his career and will take the court with a lot of confidence against Jiri Lehecka. However, Kym played the late match on Thursday night – it ended after midnight local time in Switzerland – and will not have a lot of time to turn around for this quarterfinal.

Best Bet to Make

Welcome to our best bet section. You’re going to get a collection of bets to consider, also a confidence level which should guide how much you should invest in this match.

Parlay/hedge options: Kym +3.5 games at 1.73 at @bet365

Confidence level on a scale of 1-10: 6 – You could bet two thirds of a unit on this match. 

This ATP Basel match should go to Lehecka, who is in good form and will be playing a very tired opponent, Jerome Kym, who did not get a lot of rest after his Round of 16 win over the top seed in the tournament (Ben Shelton). Take Lehecka in straights, and the price is surprisingly good.

That being said, betting on Lehecka -1.5 sets is the best bet you can make

Value bet/ the best odds: Lehecka -1.5 sets at 2.04 at @1xbet

Taylor Fritz – Arthur Fils: 9:30 EST

H2H: 0-1

This ATP Basel match is the best of the four quarterfinals. Taylor Fritz is pushing for a spot in the ATP Finals. Arthur Fils will be in Turin. Fritz therefore has a lot more incentive to win here. He is roughly 400 points behind Rafael Jodar, who currently occupies the last position but has not clinched a berth in Turin.

Best Bet to Make

Parlay/hedge options: Fils +1.5 games at 1.61 at @unibet, over 2.5 sets at 2.12 at @unibet

Confidence level on a scale of 1-10: 1 – you should not bet on this match.

This ATP Basel match figures to be extremely close, so it’s not a good match to bet on. If you insist on a bet, though, Fritz needs this win more than Fils does, so Fritz moneyline makes plenty of sense.

That being said, betting on Fritz moneyline is the best bet you can make

Value bet/ the best odds: Fritz moneyline at 2.00 at @bet365

Holger Rune – Valentin Vacherot: 8:00 EST

H2H: 0-1

This ATP Basel match should be fun. Holger Rune, after a very long injury layoff, is finally winning tour matches again. He has won twice this week in Basel, his most recent win being over Hubert Hurkacz. His opponent, Valentin Vacherot, just defeated Rafael Jodar. Both players should be confident going into this competition.

Best Bet to Make

Parlay/hedge options: Rune -0.5 games at 1.85 at @1xbet

Confidence level on a scale of 1-10: 1 – you should not bet on this match.

This ATP Basel match feels like a coin flip. Rune was away from tour for a long time. Vacherot has had an injury-interrupted season. Can you fully trust either man with your money? If you absolutely insist on a bet, though, we are leaning to Vacherot moneyline because he just beat Jodar, a better win than Rune over Hurkacz.

That being said, betting on Vacherot moneyline is the best bet you can make

Value bet/ the best odds: Vacherot moneyline at 2.22 @betfair

Jan-Lennard Struff – Nuno Borges: 12:00 EST

H2H: 0-2

This ATP Basel match offers a basic question: After beating Novak Djokovic in a very close and tough three-set match which went to a final-set tiebreaker, can Jan-Lennard Struff physically and mentally bounce back the next day and beat Nuno Borges, who beat Casper Ruud in Basel?

Best Bet to Make

Parlay/hedge options: Struff -0.5 games at 1.75 at @betsson

Confidence level on a scale of 1-10: 4 – you could bet half a unit on this match.

This ATP Basel match isn’t an easy one to predict, but we think Borges – who leads Struff in the head-to-head matchup – will benefit from Struff being mentally tired following his win over Djokovic.

That being said, betting on Borges moneyline is the best bet you can make

Value bet/ the best odds: Borges moneyline at 2.23 @unibet
