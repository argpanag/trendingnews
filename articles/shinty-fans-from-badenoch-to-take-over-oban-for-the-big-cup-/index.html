Shinty fans from Badenoch to take over Oban for the big cup final

TheTools · 18.09.2026 06:00 · source · permalink

Supporters from the Badenoch giants will set off west tomorrow morning for a near 200-mile round trip to attend a match that means absolutely everything to two shinty communities.

Kingussie and Newtonmore - just three miles apart - will be battling it out for the biggest trophy in the sport at Mossfield in Oban.

‘More are the holders having sprung a big surprise to defeat Kingussie 3-2 in last year’s final and they have already beaten the Kings 1-0 in the Mowi Premiership this season.

They lead the way in the title race with the Red and Blues hot on their heels and both sides still to meet at The Dell for the title decider.

Iain Robinson captains Newtonmore and despite missing four games this year, he remains shinty’s top scorer with 25 goals to his name.

He is only one goal adrift of last year’s count at the same stage of the season.

Robinson said ahead of tomorrow’s massive game: “I’m fairly happy with my total so far this year but there’s always room for improvement as our manager Peter Ross is always telling us.

“You always think of the ones that got away, including a penalty against Fort William, so I maybe should be on the 30-goal mark right now.

“The lead up to the final can be nervy and I was lying in bed trying to get to sleep the other night, wondering if this is what it’s going be like all week.”

Kingussie can point to two more recent wins over their fierce rivals, 2-0 in the MacTavish Cup Final and 4-1 in the MKM Oban Macaulay Cup quarter-finals.

Captain Ruaridh Anderson is their top scorer with 20 goals in all competitions, and the club have scored 26 more goals than at the same time last season albeit that figure is skewed by a whopping 17-0 league victory over Kyles Athletic in May.

Anderson said: “We’ve had some big wins this year but we have also had some really close games too.

“(Manager) Ronald (Ross) does some really good drills with the forwards at training and has us working on patterns of play, getting us to play the way he wants us to.

“That has been more difficult given the injuries we’ve had this year meaning we haven’t had a set forward line week-in, week-out.

“Given all Ronald has achieved in the game, especially as a forward himself, when he speaks to you, you listen.

“Ally MacLeod works with the defence and then we all come together at the end of the session. Hopefully it all comes together for us on Saturday.”

Previous form goes out the window and this is a mouthwatering clash that’s too close to call.

Anyone unable to attend the game can follow the action live on BBA Alba from 2pm.
