Critics Brand Prince Harry and Meghan Markle 'Pathetic' as Former Protection Chief Slams Media Strategy

TheTools · 02.11.2026 18:05 · source · permalink

Prince Harry and Meghan Markle are facing fresh criticism after reportedly stepping back from public engagements for a two-week family break while King Charles undertakes a high-profile tour of the Caribbean.

The pause, which coincides with the school half-term holiday for Prince Archie, seven, and Princess Lilibet, five, has drawn accusations of attention-seeking from online detractors and renewed questions about the couple's media strategy from a former royal protection chief.
