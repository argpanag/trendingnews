City crashed to a 4-2 defeat at home to Paris Saint-Germain last time out in the UCL, and could be without star man Erling Haaland for today’s game. The Norwegian striker picked up a knock late on in their 3-2 win in the Premier League over Ipswich at the weekend. With Antoine Semenyo also sidelined with a knee injury, Maresca may be forced to play either Phil Foden or Rayan Cherki in an unconventional false nine role.

Greek Super League champs AEK Athens meanwhile got off to a winning start in this season’s Champions League with a narrow 1-0 win at home to Austria’s LASK, but followed that result with an abject 3-1 defeat against Shakhtar Donetsk last week. The omens aren’t great for Marko Nikolić’s team, who are bouncing back from that result today, with AEK having lost seven of their last eight games against English opposition.
