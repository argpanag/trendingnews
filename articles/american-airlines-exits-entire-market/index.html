American Airlines exits entire market

TheTools · 15.10.2026 22:11 · source · permalink

Due to its proximity and popularity as a warm-weather beach destination, Mexico is by far the foreign country Americans visit most. In an average year, approximately 40 million tourists come to the country for vacations, family visits, or business.

Both American and Mexican airlines have long seized upon this demand by offering a range of flights to major hubs such as Mexico City and Guadalajara, as well as smaller destinations that see spikes in popularity at different points of the year.

American Airlines is the U.S.-based carrier that, with 30 destinations as of August 2026, has long positioned itself as flying to more cities in Mexico than any other.

But flights to smaller cities can either help fill an underserved niche or become a money drain if traffic numbers do not offset the cost of running the route.

American Airlines has not run its flight between Phoenix and Tijuana since August

In early August 2026, American Airlines cut its flight to Tijuana International Airport (TIJ) in the northwestern Baja California state, according to Simple Flying. American Airlines launched the route to the city from Phoenix Sky Harbor International (PHX) at the beginning of 2024 but ultimately did not see the expected passenger uptake.

With just 290 miles between the two cities and the flight running an hour gate to gate, it was the carrier's shortest flight to Mexico from the U.S. Tijuana is also located just 20 miles south of the border from San Diego, while Los Cabos International (SJD) is the main airport serving Baja California.

In 2024, the route between Phoenix and Tijuana was marketed as a game-changer for linking two cities with strong family and business connections, as well as reaching those looking to explore less touristy parts of Mexico. The route ran for the final time on Aug. 2.

Its cancellation came at a time when American Airlines was generally streamlining its network out of Phoenix.

Last August, the airline also confirmed that it will phase out its route from the city to Monterrey's General Mariano Escobedo International Airport (MTY) by December 2026 due to insufficient passenger demand.

Why American Airlines significantly reworked its flight schedule out of Phoenix

In past statements, American Airlines has said it regularly evaluates its network and trims flights that do not bring in the necessary traffic.

American Airlines continues to fly to nine Mexican cities from Phoenix — including Cancun, Los Cabos, and Mexico City, as well as a seasonal route to Zihuatanejo on the country's Pacific coast.
