Lappi told Hyundai Paddon should do Chile – DirtFish

TheTools · 16.09.2026 08:00 · source · permalink

Hayden Paddon contested Rally del Paraguay for Hyundai, but Esapekka Lappi came in two weeks later for Chile

Esapekka Lappi told Hyundai it should’ve taken Hayden Paddon to last week’s Rally Chile instead of him.

Paddon drove the third i20 N Rally1 at Rally del Paraguay two weeks prior, and starred by taking second place on his first gravel event in the car.

Entries for Rally Chile closed before Paraguay so Paddon’s result can only be factored in with hindsight, but Lappi felt the continuity of Paddon in the car for both South American events made sense.

The Finn struggled for pace and finished sixth in Chile.

He told DirtFish: “For this rally, I know it was not the right choice to have me in the car, but this I already said in Finland that they should take Hayden.

Lappi felt Paddon should've driven in Chile instead of him

“Just also because he has, you know, the rally under his belt from Paraguay and that would be more natural. But OK they made a different decision.”

Lappi revealed his main motivation to accept his Hyundai role in 2026 was “because I just wanted to do Finland”, but he still believes it was the right decision to accept it.

“Well, I think, personally, it was the right [decision],” he said. “I have enjoyed many days, let’s say like this.”

While Hyundai’s third driver for the final event is not yet confirmed (with the final event itself not even confirmed), Lappi is not expected to return.

That brings his three-season association with Hyundai to a close, starting with a full season in 2023 and then two part-time campaigns in 2024 and ’26.

“It’s been a lot of ups and downs,” Lappi reflected. “The first full season, the middle part of it was actually fairly good. We had many podiums in a row.

“Then we had a bit of a downhill and then to come back to win in Sweden was for sure the highlight. And yeah, this year has been a bit of a downhill.

“I think in Sweden we were good, Kenya was just a good strategy and then the last three events hasn’t been so successful. But yeah, the guys have tried to support me all the time, so I need to give thanks to them.”

Lappi voluntarily gave up a full-time WRC seat for 2024 as he wanted to spend more time with his family, and seemed at peace with his WRC career ending before he received the call to return with Hyundai this season.

Asked if he’s finding the right work / life balance, the two-time WRC winner concluded: “Most of the time, yes. But, if I’m honest, the idea of having a free calendar without anybody saying where I need to be except my wife, it feels good at the moment.”
