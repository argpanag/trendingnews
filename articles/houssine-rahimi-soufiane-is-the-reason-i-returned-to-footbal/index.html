Houssine Rahimi: ‘Soufiane Is the Reason I Returned to Football’

TheTools · 16.09.2026 17:08 · source · permalink

The Al Ain striker made the revelation following his outstanding performance against Saudi Arabia’s Al Nassr on Tuesday, when he scored twice to help the Emirati club secure a 4-0 win in their opening AFC Champions League Elite fixture.

Speaking to Abu Dhabi Sports after the match, Houssine expressed his gratitude to his older brother for supporting him during a difficult period in his career.

“Soufiane was the reason I returned to playing football because, some time ago, I was going to retire. I thank him very much for what he did for me,” he said.

The Moroccan forward did not specify when he had considered retirement or elaborate on the circumstances behind that decision.

Rahimi brothers lead Al Ain to emphatic victory

Houssine opened the scoring in the 25th minute at Hazza bin Zayed Stadium before doubling Al Ain’s advantage in the 63rd.

Soufiane then added the third goal in the 72nd minute, while Greek striker Giorgos Giakoumakis completed the victory five minutes before full time.

The brothers accounted for three of Al Ain’s four goals against a Saudi side featuring Cristiano Ronaldo, giving the Emirati club a winning start to their continental campaign.

Houssine’s two-goal performance earned him the Man of the Match award, while his brother’s contribution helped secure a memorable result for the UAE club.

The victory gave Al Ain their first three points of the AFC Champions League Elite league phase, with the Rahimi brothers marking their return to action with three goals and a tribute to their late father.

 

The two brothers have been enduring an emotional period following the recent death of their father.

“I pray for my father, who passed away. Soufiane and I are still deeply affected by his death, but that does not prevent us from fulfilling our duties to the team. We dedicate this victory to him,” Houssine said.
