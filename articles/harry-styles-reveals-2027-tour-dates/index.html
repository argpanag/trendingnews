Harry Styles Reveals 2027 Tour Dates

TheTools · 10.09.2026 12:19 · source · permalink

Anyone missing out on Harry Styles’ ongoing 30-date residency at Madison Square Garden will have a chance to catch him when he crosses North America and Europe next spring. The pop star just announced a handful of 2027 dates for his Together, Together tour, kicking off in April in Phoenix and hitting a slew of additional Stateside dates before heading back overseas in July. A full list of stops can be found below. Tickets go on sale later this month.

“I love playing music, I love being with all of you and being a part of the love and joy you all create together every night,” Styles wrote in an email to fans teasing the announcement, per Rolling Stone.

Each stop on the next round of Styles’ trek comes with its own unique opener, including Tinashe in Phoenix, Kylie Minogue in Los Angeles, Caroline Polachek in Paris, Feist in Madrid, LCD Soundsystem in Rome, and Tears for Fears in London.
