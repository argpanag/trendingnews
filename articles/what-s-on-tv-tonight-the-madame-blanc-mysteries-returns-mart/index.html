What’s on TV tonight: The Madame Blanc Mysteries returns, Martin Lewis on the Budget, and more

TheTools · 29.10.2026 21:39 · source · permalink

Your complete TV guide to the week’s best shows, across terrestrial and digital platforms

What’s on TV tonight?

The Madame Blanc Mysteries
Channel 5, 9.05pm
We’re back in the sunny south of France for a new run of Sally Lindsay’s cosy mystery drama about a bereaved Cheshire widow, Jean White (Lindsay), embarking on a new life as an antiques dealer in the fictional Provençal town of Saint Victoire. Though she usually spends rather more time helping investigate the multitude of bizarre murders that occur there. Six series in and she’s engaged to the likeably dim Dom (Steve Edge), and there’s an established cast of fellow expats, including local mechanic Gloria (Sue Vincent) and wealthy eccentrics Judith (Sue Holderness) and Jeremy (Robin Askwith). Plus, of course, the resolutely French police chief André Caron (Alex Gaumond).

The recently elected mayor of Saint Victoire proves to be the victim this time round, when Jean and Dom discover her lifeless body dumped in the town-square fountain. It’s not long before Jean stumbles across a clue that points towards a case of bribery and corruption. Followed by A Night in with Sally Lindsay (Channel 5, 10.05pm), a TV airing of the actress’s podcast (which launched in April) in which she invites showbiz pals round for a cosy chat, beginning with Suranne Jones.

Breast Cancer Scandal: Exposing the Harm
BBC Two, 7pm; already available on iPlayer
After a report last year found widespread failings in breast cancer services – including missed diagnoses and at least 20 unnecessary mastectomies – at County Durham and Darlington NHS Foundation Trust, more than 4,500 cases are now under review. BBC Health correspondent Sharon Barbour reports on the deepening scandal as hundreds more cases emerge.

The Martin Lewis Money Show Live: Budget Special
ITV1, 7.15pm
Away from all the politics, what will be the day-to-day impact on the pound in your pocket of the measures announced in Chancellor John Healey’s autumn budget yesterday? Martin Lewis outlines the key takeaways regarding taxation, household bills and personal finances.

The Celebrity Traitors
BBC One, 8pm
It’s the semi-final and, for now, nine players remain. The Traitors are continuing to fly well under the radar, and last week’s episode left us on a cliffhanger of which Faithful was about to be banished from the castle: Joanne or Sharon. 

The Yorkshire Vet
Channel 5, 8pm
Returning for another series, tensions run high at Kirkbymoorside when Lola the much-loved dachshund has surgery to remove a bladder stone. Julian, meanwhile, must contend with two not-so-friendly pet rabbits and Matt tackles a peacock with a sinus infection.

Not Going Out
BBC One, 9.30pm
When Lee (Lee Mack) finds an undiscovered Banksy artwork in a derelict warehouse, his first instinct is to try to steal it for himself. His second instinct is what causes all the trouble, though, and it’s not long before he finds himself at the centre of a blackmail plot and an escalating media storm.

It Gets Worse
Channel 4, 10.05pm & 10.35pm
The three-way friendship comedy continues with another double-episode. Sam’s (Olive Gray) moral fibre is tested at a warehouse sex party, Abi (Emily Fairn) tries to make her boyfriend jealous, and Ethan (Leo Reich) spends the night trying to figure out whether Aaron is gay. GO

What’s on TV this week?

Friday 30 October

The Celebrity Traitors: Finale
BBC One, 8pm
There is a parallel universe out there in which Richard E Grant did not inexplicably forget what game he was playing and survived beyond the first week as a Traitor. With the series ending tonight, you cannot help but wonder what could have been. Have we been denied another magical Alan Carr-esque arc? Or was Grant simply too eccentric to play the game? Some critics have lamented that this series of The Celebrity Traitors has missed the spark of last year’s lightning-in-a-bottle debut. Yet even a middling series of The Traitors is better than most shows on TV. Plus, the stupendously paranoid Ross Kemp has more than made up for it.

As you would expect, the events of tonight’s finale are top secret. What is known is that at 80 minutes, it is the longest episode of The Traitors yet. Could we be in for a blockbuster showdown? Ed Gamble’s Uncloaked follows at 9.20pm to digest all the drama. And if you’re hungry for more treachery, the second series of The Traitors Ireland begins immediately afterwards at 10pm on BBC Three. Presented by Derry Girls’ Siobhán McSweeney, the opening two episodes introduce four Traitors and a shock banishment.

Nocturne
Apple TV
Stephen Graham stars in this pulpy 10-part thriller as an imprisoned serial killer who is somehow terrorising a small American town. It is formulaic, but today’s opening two episodes – in which retired cop Jonah (Liev Schreiber) investigates the reappearance of a victim long thought dead – are twisty and moreish enough to keep you guessing.

Beat Luke Littler
Netflix
You have to respect the delusion required to think that you could beat Luke Littler, the best darts player in the world. Nonetheless, Gary Lineker and Richard Osman host this lavish live contest in which 20 of the UK’s best amateurs will step up to the oche in the hopes of winning a fitting £501,000.

Dexter: Resurrection
Paramount+
This playful Dexter revival is devilish fun. Today’s return finds Michael C Hall’s principled serial killer at a low ebb as a 50-something Uber driver. Fortunately, his second job of hunting down less principled colleagues is picking up – putting him on a collision course with the deranged Five Borough Killer, a new Zodiac-style nemesis played by Dan Stevens.

Have I Got News For You
BBC One, 7.30pm
The dependable Victoria Coren Mitchell returns to hosting duties this week, with comedian Julian Clary and political correspondent Ava Santina Evans joining regulars Ian Hislop and Paul Merton across the panels.

Gardeners’ World
BBC Two, 8pm
The growing season is drawing to a close, but the work never stops on Gardeners’ World. Tonight, a busy Adam Frost is laying turf in his new garden, while in the Peak District, Rekha Mistry offers timely advice on growing veg in pots. SK
