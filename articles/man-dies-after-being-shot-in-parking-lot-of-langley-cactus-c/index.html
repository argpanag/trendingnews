Man dies after being shot in parking lot of Langley Cactus Club

TheTools · 07.10.2026 07:01 · source · permalink

A victim died after being found in medical distress in a car outside the restaurant in the 6000-block of 200th Street on Monday night

A 23-year-old Langley man is dead after a Monday night shooting outside the Cactus Club restaurant in Langley.

The Langley RCMP said they received reports of shot being fired in the 6000-block of 200th Street at about 9:45 p.m.

The victim died a short time later at the scene, said Sgt. Zynal Sharoom.

A bunch of people came running into the kitchen as word spread Monday night that someone had been shot outside, said Terrilyn Bell, whose son works as a line cook at the Cactus Club. Bell, who drove over to check on her son’s car and see what was happening, said it was a shock: “It’s scary as a parent. It’s happening way too close to home.”

Bell said she heard it was gang-related and that she believed it wasn’t the first shooting at that location.

Less than 20 minutes after the shooting, police received a report of a vehicle on fire near 232nd Street and 75th Avenue in Langley, and investigators are working to determine if the two incidents are linked.

In an update Tuesday, Integrated Homicide Investigation Team Sgt. Freda Fong said the shooting was targeted.

“This investigation is in its early stages, and it is essential for investigators to establish a timeline of events leading up to the shooting,” said Fong in a statement. “A lot of work is underway as we continue an extended canvass for witnesses and video at both the shooting and arson scenes.”

On Tuesday morning, investigators remained in the area gathering video security footage and talking to witnesses.

A black Lower Mainland evidence unit tent was set up, with another tent beside it covering a grey car. Officers were combing the bushes for evidence while others conferred near the entrance to the restaurant.

A large section of the parking lot on the south end of the Cactus Club was behind police tape Tuesday morning, with more than half-a-dozen RCMP vehicles around the perimeter.

At about 10 a.m., a few people believed to be staff met outside the restaurant on the north side and entered the Cactus Club, though the restaurant was expected to remain closed into the afternoon. It usually opens for lunch at 11 a.m.

Dozens of vehicles also remained parked in the lot behind police tape with a few topped by numbered evidence markers.

A white Range Rover with a black roof, its driver’s-side window shattered, was removed from the plaza on a flatbed tow truck, its licence plate concealed under a piece of cardboard.

Outside the police cordon — which also blocked access to a Dollarama outlet — it was largely business as usual in the large shopping plaza known as The Corner, at the southeast corner of 200th Street and the Langley Bypass.

Anyone with information who hasn’t already spoken to police is asked to call IHIT at 1-877-551-4448 or email ihitinfo@rcmp-grc.gc.ca.
