Inter 2-0 Stuttgart: Sučić and Lautaro, Champions League

Inter beat VfB Stuttgart 2-0 on Wednesday 25 November at San Siro in Milan on matchday 5 of the Champions League league phase. The two sides were level at 0-0 at half-time.

All the goals came after the break. In the 55th minute, VAR confirmed a penalty for Inter. Petar Sučić converted it in the 57th. Lautaro Martínez doubled the lead in the 74th, from a Federico Dimarco pass. Both men came on during the game: Dimarco in the 41st minute, replacing Alessandro Bastoni, Martínez in the 69th, replacing Marcus Thuram. No red cards were shown.

On the stats front, Inter had 20 shots, 5 on target, against 13 shots, 2 on target, for Stuttgart. Expected goals, which measure the quality of chances created, stand at 2.77 for the Milan side and 1.08 for the Germans. Possession was evenly balanced: 51% for Inter, 49% for Stuttgart.

Cristian Chivu set Inter up in a 3-5-2 against Hoeneb's 3-4-3 VfB. Sučić played the full 90 minutes: one goal, three shots with one on target, one key pass and three duels won out of six. SNA rating: 6.5/10.
