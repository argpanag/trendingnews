FINALS TEAMS: Cats' selection shock, blow for injured Blues, Swans' big call

TheTools · 03.09.2026 23:05 · source · permalink

The teams are in for the two elimination finals and second qualifying final

Brought to you by

GEELONG has dropped Sam De Koning for its elimination final against Carlton on Friday night.

De Koning is one of three omissions, including Jack Martin, to make way for returning midfield ace Max Holmes and trusted veteran Mark Blicavs.

>> KEEP SCROLLING TO SEE THE FULL TEAMS OR CLICK HERE

The Blues will be without Nick Haynes, who has not recovered from an ankle injury, while Will Hayward (ankle) has also failed to get up.

Sydney has left Joel Amartey out of its 23 for Saturday's qualifying final against Brisbane, despite the key forward overcoming an Achilles injury.

Hayden McLean has been recalled to play in the forward line alongside Charlie Curnow and Logan McDonald and offer ruck support to Brodie Grundy.

Three-time premiership captain Michael Voss joins Matthew Lloyd to preview a blockbuster weekend of elimination and qualifying finals

Youngster Ty Gallop is the unlucky Lion to make way for returning co-captain Hugh McCluggage.

In Saturday night's second elimination final at Adelaide Oval, the Crows have named Callum Ah Chee, who was rested for the final home and away match, to replace Zac Taylor (hamstring).

Will Lewis comes in for the Western Bulldogs in place of Matt Kennedy, who is in the concussion protocols.

Chad Wingard, Josh Gabelich and Nat Edwards preview each of this weekend’s blockbuster Elimination and Qualifying Finals
