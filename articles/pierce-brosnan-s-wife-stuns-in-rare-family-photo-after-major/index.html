Pierce Brosnan's wife stuns in rare family photo after major transformation

TheTools · 26.10.2026 16:00 · source · permalink

Pierce Brosnan's wife, Keely Shaye Smith, flaunted her slender figure in a satin slip dress at a recent family gathering. The actress and journalist, 63, looked like a vision in a purple dress as she celebrated her mother Sharon's 82nd birthday.

The James Bond star, 73, was also spotted with his wife, and their sons, Dylan, 29, and Paris, 25. The family smiled ear to ear along with Sharon and her birthday cake. Keely captioned her Instagram post, "Please join me in wishing a very Happy 82nd Birthday to my beloved mother Sharon Rose!"

Keely has reportedly lost around 100lbs, and her slender frame has become the talk of the town since 2024. The actress told Vogue in a 2006 interview, "I never shy away from... my curves. I never hide in baggy clothing." This comes after the unexpected reason Pierce Brosnan and Keely Shaye Smith are one of Hollywood's most cherished couples was revealed.

Meanwhile, Pierce has also often expressed his admiration for his wife. "I strongly love every curve of her body. She is the most beautiful woman in my eyes. And also because she had our children," he said in a previous interview.

"In the past, I truly loved her for her person, not only for her beauty, and now I'm loving her even more that she is my children's mother. And I am very proud of her, and I always seek to be worthy of her love."

Additionally, an insider previously shared some insight into Keely's weight-loss journey. “Keely’s not made changes to keep her critics off her back, nor has she done it for Pierce. She’s done this for herself. During COVID, she became very curious about nutrition and natural remedies for her own health and wellbeing," the insider told New Idea magazine.

She has, according to sources, been consistently following a gym routine and going on daily walks with her husband—measures that, along with other lifestyle changes, have led to her dropping around 100lbs.

The source added, “Keely wanted a stronger body, so she started light workouts, swimming, and plenty of nature walks with Pierce.

"She’s even harvesting her own coconut water! Pierce’s not getting any younger either, so there’s been a concerted effort on both their parts to simply take better care of themselves.”

Various media sources track her weight gain back to when she had her two sons, and potential problems related to the thyroid gland and a hormonal imbalance; however, nothing has been publicly confirmed by the family.

Pierce has previously said, "We like each other a lot. We love each other a lot. And we've been down the road, and we've seen many, many hardships as families do, as we all do."

"But watching someone grow up with you and grow old with you is a very spiritual journey. To watch each other change …whatever it may be, the hair, the waist. But it's the love in the heart."
