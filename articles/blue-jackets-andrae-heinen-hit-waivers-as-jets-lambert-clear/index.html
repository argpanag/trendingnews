Blue Jackets' Andrae, Heinen hit waivers as Jets' Lambert clears

TheTools · 04.11.2026 14:16 · source · permalink

A pair of Columbus Blue Jackets are hitting the waiver wire.

Defenceman Emil Andrae and forward Danton Heinen were both waived by the Metropolitan Division club on Wednesday, Sportsnet's Elliotte Friedman reported.

Elsewhere, Winnipeg Jets forward Brad Lambert cleared waivers.

Andrae, 24, joined the Blue Jackets on the eve of the season as part of the return from the Toronto Maple Leafs for forward Kirill Marchenko.

The Finnish blue liner has played five games for Columbus with two assists.

Heinen, a veteran winger, has yet to hit the scoresheet in 10 games this season.

Lambert featured in 11 games for the Jets this season, notching one assist.

He was selected 30th overall by the Jets in the 2022 Draft and has tallied three goals and six assists in 42 games across four seasons.
