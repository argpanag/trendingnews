PlayStation reveals limited-edition GTA VI controllers.

TheTools · 03.09.2026 13:57 · source · permalink

GTA VI: all the news on Rockstar’s next entry in the Grand Theft Auto series

See all Stories

The new DualSense controllers will launch alongside GTA VI on November 19th. They come in black and white with a sparkly blue-purple sheen surrounding the thumbsticks and extending into the grips, as well as a “VI” logo stamped on the touchpad.

You can preorder the new controllers for $84.99 starting September 10th at 10AM ET.
