Rains and thunderstorms expected in the days ahead with a drop in temperature

TheTools · 30.09.2026 06:30 · source · permalink

Local rains, isolated thunderstorms, and a slight drop in temperature are expected in the days ahead, according to the forecast from the Department of Meteorology, due to low pressure that is anticipated to affect the area.

On Sunday afternoon, locally increased cloudiness may bring isolated rains in the mountainous areas or even inland. Winds will blow from the southwest to the west, moderate to strong, 4 to 5 Beaufort, and the sea will be slightly disturbed.

In the evening, local rains and isolated thunderstorms are expected, initially in the west and north, while gradually other areas will also be affected. Hail is possible during thunderstorms. Winds will mainly blow from the southwest to the northwest, weak to moderate, 3 to 4 Beaufort, and temporarily locally, moderate to strong, 4 to 5 Beaufort. The sea will be slightly disturbed. The temperature will drop to around 18 degrees inland, around 20 along the coasts, and around 11 degrees in the higher mountainous areas.

According to the forecast, on Monday, local rains and isolated thunderstorms are expected. Hail is possible during thunderstorms. Winds will blow from the southwest to the northwest, moderate to strong, 4 to 5 Beaufort, and the sea will be slightly disturbed to disturbed. The temperature will rise to around 28 degrees inland, in the southeastern and eastern coasts, 26 in the other coasts, and around 17 degrees in the higher mountainous areas.

On Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday, local rains and isolated thunderstorms are also expected. The temperature will gradually decrease slightly to fluctuate just below the average climatic values.
