Predict.fun Market Gives Warriors 30% Chance to Beat Nuggets | TokenPost

TheTools · 27.10.2026 01:27 · source · permalink

Golden State is 0-3 to open the 2026-27 NBA season. Denver entered at 1-1 after an overtime comeback win over New Orleans.

A predict.fun market assigned the Golden State Warriors a 30% chance of beating Denver ahead of Monday night's game, as Golden State seeks its first win of the 2026-27 NBA regular season.

The Warriors have lost their first three games. Denver entered at 1-1 after overcoming a deficit of as many as 23 points to beat New Orleans in overtime.

Golden State is scheduled to visit the Nuggets at 10 p.m. ET Monday (02:00 UTC Tuesday).
