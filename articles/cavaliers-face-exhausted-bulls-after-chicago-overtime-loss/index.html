Cavaliers Face Exhausted Bulls After Chicago Overtime Loss

TheTools · 24.11.2026 00:23 · source · permalink

The Cleveland Cavaliers entered their road matchup against the Chicago Bulls with a significant rest advantage ahead of Monday, Nov. 23, 2026. The teams were scheduled to meet at 8 p.m. EST at United Center in Chicago, where Cleveland faced a struggling opponent coming off an exhausting overtime defeat.

According to Sports Yahoo, Chicago had a 4-12 record entering the matchup and played the Denver Nuggets less than 24 hours before hosting Cleveland. The Bulls lost that contest 151-145 in overtime, leaving their players with limited recovery time.

Chicago's starters each logged more than 40 minutes against Denver. Meanwhile, Cleveland benefited from an entire weekend off before traveling to Chicago, giving the Cavaliers a considerable advantage in preparation and recovery.

Defensive performance remained another major concern for Chicago. The Bulls allowed an average of 124.6 points per game, ranking 30th in the league according to the team statistics cited by Sports Yahoo.

Despite averaging 116.6 points offensively, Chicago struggled to contain opposing offenses. Cleveland entered the matchup averaging 118.8 points per game, placing fourth in the league. The Cavaliers also ranked third in three-pointers made, averaging 15.3 per contest.

Chicago averaged 13.7 three-pointers per game, ranking 16th in that category. However, its three-point shooting percentage stood at just under 33%, placing the Bulls 28th in the league, according to Sports Yahoo.

That weakness could provide Cleveland with an opportunity, particularly because the Cavaliers had experienced difficulties defending perimeter shooting throughout the season. However, Chicago's shooting struggles did not guarantee an easy defensive assignment.

The Cavaliers had previously allowed the Charlotte Hornets to record their highest three-point total in a game, resulting in a heavy defeat. That performance highlighted the importance of maintaining defensive discipline rather than relying on an opponent's poor shooting percentage.

James Harden also entered the matchup following an outstanding performance against the Indiana Pacers, scoring 51 points. His offensive contribution provided Cleveland with a significant scoring boost, while Donovan Mitchell had a comparatively difficult outing.

However, Sports Yahoo emphasized that Cleveland could not depend on Harden to produce another 51-point performance. Mitchell remained an important part of the offense, particularly when opposing defenses focused their attention on Harden and created opportunities for other players.

The Cavaliers' previous game also exposed a potential weakness. Despite building a lead of more than 20 points against Indiana, Cleveland allowed the Pacers to reduce the deficit because the Cavaliers struggled to maintain their scoring output.

Chicago's leading individual statistical contributors included Norman Powell with 20.6 points per game, Jalen Smith with 7.6 rebounds and 1.1 blocks, Josh Giddey with 9.2 assists, and Caleb Wilson with 1.8 steals, according to the figures published by Sports Yahoo.

Cleveland entered the contest averaging 118.8 points scored and 116.5 points allowed per game. Chicago averaged 116.6 points scored and 124.6 points allowed, illustrating the contrasting defensive records of the two teams ahead of their meeting at United Center.
