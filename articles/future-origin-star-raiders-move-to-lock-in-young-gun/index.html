Future Origin star: Raiders move to lock in young gun

TheTools · 26.11.2026 04:45 · source · permalink

The Canberra Raiders see Noah Martin as a future Origin player.

It's why they're looking to lock the 21-year-old in long term.

He's already contracted to the Green Machine until the end of 2028, but the club is looking to open talks to extend him well beyond that.

Martin's looked right at home in the NRL since making his debut last year.

If it wasn't for injuries he would've played almost double his 17 NRL games, in which he's scored six tries.

He suffered syndesmosis injuries to both ankles in round eight, which kept him out for eight games, and then he had a quad injury that ruled him out for another five.

With Hudson Young signing with the PNG Chiefs in 2028, the Raiders want to lock in their young second-rowers.

It's a position they've got plenty of depth in, with Martin, Matty Nicholson, Zac Hosking and Simi Sasagi all options to start in the back row.

Martin's enthusiasm and trademark mullet made him an instant hit with the Raiders fans - and the club's keen to keep him in lime green for years to come.

They see him having the potential to follow Young onto the State of Origin stage during his career.

As well as looking to lock in their young guns, the Raiders are also looking at who's available on the market.

Penrith premiership star Liam Martin's keen to re-sign with the Panthers, but he's also open to a move to the capital to replace Young.

His manager Allan Gainey said Martin was set to meet Canberra coach Ricky Stuart and tour Raiders HQ next week, once the 29-year-old has returned from a post-World Cup holiday, but nothing's been locked in as yet.

There's been no movement regarding England international Ethan Havard since the Raiders met him when he was in Australia for the World Cup.

Havard's contracted to Wigan for next season and while the Raiders were open to bringing him Down Under earlier, the Warriors weren't willing to let him go early.

Canberra's interested in signing him for 2028 and beyond.

He's one of a number of middle forwards the Green Machine was interested in to bolster its prop depth, after the retirement of Josh Papali'i and departures of Morgan Smithies and Corey Horsburgh to the Perth Bears.

Moses Leota was another option, but he's reportedly re-signed with Penrith for another three years.

The Raiders also looked at Melbourne second-rower Ativalu Lisati, but he's re-signed with the Storm.

Canberra will continue to target both positions.
