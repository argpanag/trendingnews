O'Sullivan's amateur replacement draws flying world number one at British Open

TheTools · 03.09.2026 16:59 · source · permalink

Mark Joyce got a late call-up to replace Ronnie O’Sullivan at the British Open and has won his way into the last 16 where he faces world number one Zhao Xintong.

The 43-year-old was only confirmed as an entrant in Cheltenham on Friday after the Rocket withdrew from the event.

Joyce has taken advantage of the opportunity, winning three matches and only conceding one frame so far.

The Walsall cueist beat Mateusz Baranowski 4-1, Jordan Brown 4-0 and scored a big upset by downing Pang Junxu 4-0 late on Wednesday night.

Things will get a lot tougher for him on Thursday night when he takes on Zhao, though, who was in wild form as he demolished Michael Holt 4-0 on Thursday afternoon.

The former world champion made breaks of 100, 63, 139 and 112 in a ruthless dismissal of the Hitman which lasted just 40 minutes and 29 seconds of playing time.

The cue looked absolutely fine on Thursday as he hammered Holt and Joyce will be facing a flying world number one on Thursday night for a place in the quarter-finals.

Zhao is the bookmakers’ favourite to win the British Open ahead of the last 16, followed by the likes of Judd Trump, Mark Selby and John Higgins.

Trump takes on Liam Davies or Dave Gilbert, Selby meets Ukraine’s Iulian Boiko and Higgins plays English teenager Stan Moody.
