Wild Place Calvin Pickard On Waivers

TheTools · 25.11.2026 12:19 · source · permalink

Following Filip Gustavsson’s season debut on Monday, the Minnesota Wild have placed third-string goaltender Calvin Pickard on waivers. This will solidify Minnesota’s goaltending duo after Gustavsson recovered from his offseason hip surgery.

Pickard appeared in three games while serving as backup to Jesper Wallstedt through the start of the season. He set a 1-1-1 record with a .855 save percentage and 3.60 goals-against-average. Minnesota signed Pickard to a one-year, $1MM contract over the summer. That deal will maintain his $1MM salary through an assignment to the minor-leagues – a small perk for the veteran of 15 pro seasons.

Across his career, Pickard has recorded a 161-117-40 record and .913 save percentage in 329 AHL games. He has also set a 75-78-15 record and .900 save percentage in 194 NHL games. Now 34 years old, he brings the leadership of a seasoned pro to Minnesota’s depth chart. A seven-figure salary will likely prevent any teams from claiming Pickard, setting him up for a second-straight season of AHL minutes.

Pickard could take over an Iowa Wild crease that has been split between Riley Mercer and William Rousseau. The two have nearly identical stat lines – with Mercer setting a 3-5-2 record and .900 save percentage in 10 games while Rousseau has a 3-5-1 record and .899 save percentage in nine games.

Notably, Rousseau is on an AHL contract, which could make him the most expendable option in a room with two other goalies on NHL deals. Rousseau could even be a candidate for a move to the ECHL, though it’ll be to a new team after the Iowa Heartlanders folded over the summer. Minnesota is now affiliated with the Jacksonville Icemen.
