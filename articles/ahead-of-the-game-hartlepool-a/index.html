AHEAD OF THE GAME: HARTLEPOOL (A)

Hartlepool vs Woking

Enterprise National League

Saturday 5th September 2026

Following a stalemate against Southend United, Woking travel to Hartlepool, looking to build on recent form, having obtained four points from three matches.

Our hosts had a disappointing bank holiday weekend. They lost 1-0 in two consecutive matches against Halifax and Eastleigh, with their misery being compounded by a sending off against Eastleigh. The fixtures against Hartlepool last season saw a multitude of goals for both sides, with each team securing a big victory at home. Woking won 4-0 against Hartlepool in the home fixture, with Harry Beautyman among the scorers.

Your support for the trip would be massively appreciated!

Opposition Watch

Hartlepool United have performed with consistency in the National League since their relegation from League Two, without challenging for promotion. The club have been in the division for four years, with 9th place being their highest finish under Nicky Featherstone.

Former Newcastle, Sunderland and Fulham player Lee Clark is now the manager at Hartlepool, having been appointed in the summer. Clark’s side have won two and lost three of their fixtures so far, sitting 14th.

Clark’s side opted for predominantly youthful additions in the summer. However, Hartlepool have acquired the signature of striker Jordan Hugill, who has featured throughout the EFL and in the Premier League for clubs including West Ham and Rotherham.

Around the Ground

Please note that this fixture is a 15:00 kick off.

Carers and U5’s can attend the fixture for free, with other ticket prices ranging from £6 for U14’s to £22 for adults. Tickets purchased on the day will be subject to a £2 price increase.

Food and drink will be available around the ground.

Player to Watch

Jordan Hugill has been unable to find the net so far for Hartlepool. However, the 34-year-old adds a wealth of experience to the Hartlepool side and should help to elevate the performance levels of those around him with his previous experience.
