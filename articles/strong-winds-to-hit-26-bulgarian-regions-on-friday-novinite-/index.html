Strong Winds to Hit 26 Bulgarian Regions on Friday - Novinite.com - Sofia News Agency

TheTools · 14.11.2026 02:08 · source · permalink

Strong winds are expected in 26 Bulgarian regions on Friday, November 13, with orange alerts issued for nine areas and yellow warnings for another 17. Temperatures will remain unusually high for the season, reaching 27°C to 28°C in some locations.

The sky will be partly cloudy, while moderate southerly to southwesterly winds are forecast to strengthen to gale force in eastern Bulgaria and along the northern mountain slopes. Cloud cover will spread from the northwest, with rain expected to begin by the evening.

The orange alert applies to Montana, Vratsa, Stara Zagora, Yambol, Haskovo, Burgas, Varna, Dobrich and Shumen. Wind speeds in these regions are expected to reach 20-29 meters per second, or 70-100 km/h, with gusts of up to 32 m/s, or 115 km/h. Local wind phenomena, including the föhn and bora, are also possible.

A yellow warning for strong winds has been issued for 17 other regions.

Overnight lows will range from 11°C to 16°C, while daytime temperatures are expected to reach 20°C-25°C across the country. In areas most exposed to the southerly winds, readings could climb to 27°C-28°C.

Sofia will also have partly cloudy skies and strong winds, with sustained speeds of 14-19 m/s, or 50-69 km/h, and gusts reaching 24 m/s, or 90 km/h. The temperature in the capital will fall to around 12°C overnight before rising to 21°C during the day.

Mountain areas will see considerable cloud cover, with rain in some locations and strong winds from the west-southwest. Temperatures are forecast to reach approximately 14°C at an altitude of 1,200 meters and 9°C at 2,000 meters.

Along the Black Sea coast, cloud cover will vary. Morning fog is expected along the southern coastline, potentially reducing visibility, while southwesterly winds will be strong to gale force. Maximum temperatures will range from 21°C to 23°C. Seawater temperatures will be between 16°C and 18°C, and sea conditions are expected to reach force 3-4.

Across the Balkans, windy conditions and extensive cloud cover are forecast, with morning fog possible in low-lying areas. Rain will spread from the northwest and become heavy in some locations, particularly along the northern Adriatic coast, where thunderstorms are also expected.

Central parts of the peninsula will receive light rain in the afternoon. By evening, precipitation in the northwestern regions will turn to snow, creating icy conditions. More sunshine is expected in eastern Bulgaria and northeastern Greece.

Daytime temperatures across the region are forecast to reach 21°C in Sofia, Istanbul and Athens, 23°C in Thessaloniki and Belgrade, 25°C in Bucharest and 26°C in Skopje.
