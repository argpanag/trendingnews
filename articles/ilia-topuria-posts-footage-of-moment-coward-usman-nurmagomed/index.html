Merab Dvalishvili’s claim that he was jumped by Usman Nurmagomedov has been quickly substantiated.

Merab Dvalishvili thanks friend for helping him

Merab Dvalishvili was covered in blood when he took to social media to react to the brawl.

The 35-year-old laughed as he recalled his friend fighting Umar and Usman Nurmagomedov.

“It’s so funny. My young friend, he’s only a 21-year-old wrestler from Georgia, and he was with me. And he fought okay. He fought for both brothers. Usman and Umar, and he only had this face [hurt]. Nothing else, bro,” Dvalishvili said in an Instagram video.
