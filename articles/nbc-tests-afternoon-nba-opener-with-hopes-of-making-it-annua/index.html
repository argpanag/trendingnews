NBC tests afternoon NBA opener with hopes of making it annual tradition

TheTools · 20.10.2026 19:08 · source · permalink

The first game of the 2026-27 NBA regular season will officially tip off in the middle of the afternoon, and NBC “hopes it can become an annual addition to the league’s traditional opening night schedule,” according to Mark Giannotto of USA TODAY. The Pistons host the Celtics at 3pm ET on Tuesday, “with ‘College Gameday-style activations,’ a ‘Blue Carpet’ experience, a marching band rendition of ‘Roundball Rock’ and other special festivities planned around Little Caesars Arena and downtown Detroit throughout the day,” per league officials. The afternoon tip is an experiment conceived by the NBA and NBC entering Year 2 of their media rights partnership, with the “goal of turning opening night into an all-day event.” The network saw an “opportunity to generate excitement before the two primetime windows that have typically defined the NBA’s regular season debut.” NBC “pointed to strong viewership” for its 2026 MLB Opening Day doubleheader, which also began with an afternoon game. NBC “is confident putting Celtics vs. Pistons on a national broadcast network in the afternoon can add to the buzz leading into the Knicks’ pregame title coronation.” The NBA, through NBC’s wide reach as a national broadcast network, “sees a chance to create excitement throughout the day on platforms more likely to attract the casual fans that can grow the product moving forward.” For NBC, this is “another way to potentially enhance the value of its NBA package and create the tentpole events that attract more viewers” (USA TODAY, 10/19).

BOLD MOVES: FORBES’ Mark Medina writes NBC Sports “will be even more ambitious later in the season,” broadcasting a quadruple header on Martin Luther King Jr. Day on its linear and streaming platforms. NBC Sports “hardly worries that such an extensive lineup will yield diminishing returns.” This approach “actually mirrors the NBA’s recent tradition in featuring five games on Christmas Day.” Medina: “So why shouldn’t NBC also showcase marquee games on two other national holidays? It also helps that President’s Day takes place a day after Super Bowl LXI. Most fans will have the day off from work and school and become more inclined to watch games throughout both days” (FORBES, 10/20).

BEYOND THE BROADCAST: THE ATHLETIC’s Jerry Brewer notes Basketball HOFer Tracy McGrady begins his second season analyzing the NBA on NBC’s “NBA Showtime” studio show with Vince Carter, Carmelo Anthony and Maria Taylor. Now, he has a “new deal that ties together most of his business interests.” McGrady “discussed a novel two-year strategic partnership with NBC Sports.” Peacock will stream select games from the Ones Basketball League that McGrady founded in 2022. The network will “collaborate on live tapings of ‘Cousins,’” the podcast featuring McGrady and Carter, which debuts its second season Wednesday. In addition, NBC is “exploring content related to the ABCD Basketball Camp,” the storied high school showcase in which McGrady acquired a controlling stake, reviving it in July after 20 dormant years (THE ATHLETIC, 10/20).
