Tip of the Day: Ferran Torres to star for PSG

TheTools · 26.10.2026 09:00 · source · permalink

Ferran Torres will lead the line for Paris Saint-Germain tonight, and he is the focus of Monday's Tip of the Day

Last Update:

Monday’s Tip of the Day sees us focus on the rearranged Ligue 1 clash between Paris Saint-Germain and Lyon.

The game was put back 24 hours due to large-scale demonstrations in the French capital over the weekend and now clashes with the Ballon d’Or ceremony.

And while 2025 winner Ousmane Dembele, who is injured, will be in London, his team-mates will look to claim the win that will move them back to the top of the table.

Dembele’s injury has coincided with team-mate Khvicha Kvaratskhelia sustaining a ruptured ACL, depriving PSG of two of their regular attacking starters.

That, however, provides an opportunity for Ferran Torres to shine. The World Cup winner has been productive despite limited appearances, scoring four times in seven Ligue 1 appearances since moving from Barcelona.

Lyon can also go top with a victory but we think the European champions will show their class tonight.
