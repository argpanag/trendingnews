Cristiano Ronaldo Misses Penalty and Other Chances Against Neom SC, Falling Short of 1,000-Goal Milestone


Cristiano Ronaldo endured a frustrating night on Tuesday with Al-Nassr. The Portuguese striker missed the chance to score his 982nd career goal after failing to convert a penalty against Neom SC, in a match that ended in a 1-0 victory for his team in the Saudi Pro League.

The mistake came just days after the Portuguese Football Federation announced a disciplinary sanction against the forward, who once again found himself in the spotlight for reasons unrelated to his usual on-field performances.


CRISTIANO RONALDO MISSES PENALTY AND SHOWS HIS FRUSTRATION

In the closing moments of the first half, Cristiano Ronaldo stepped up to take a penalty with the opportunity to open the scoring and reach 982 career goals. However, his shot went wide of the right post of the goal defended by Marcin Bułka.

The Portuguese star's reaction reflected his frustration. After wasting what appeared to be an ideal opportunity to break the deadlock, the Al-Nassr captain showed his disappointment as fans expected a different outcome from their leading figure.


TWO MORE MISSED CHANCES AS AL-NASSR SECURE VICTORY

The penalty was not Ronaldo's only costly miss during the match. In the 48th minute, following a cross from Sadio Mané, the forward had a golden opportunity in front of an unguarded net but sent the ball over the crossbar.

Later, he squandered another chance with a wayward effort inside the penalty area. Despite their star player's lack of finishing precision, Al-Nassr found the breakthrough through João Félix, who scored the winning goal with a powerful strike after the hour mark.

The result lifted the Saudi side to 22 points from nine matches, keeping them in contention for the Saudi Pro League title.


HOW MANY PENALTIES HAS CRISTIANO RONALDO MISSED?

The missed penalty against Neom represented Cristiano Ronaldo's fourth failed spot kick in the Saudi Pro League. The Portuguese forward had taken 31 penalties in the competition for Al-Nassr.

Across his entire professional career, Transfermarkt's statistics listed 36 missed penalties before being updated to include this match. The total therefore needs to be revised to account for his latest miss, and a definitive figure should not be given without confirmation.

The frustrating performance contrasts with the finishing efficiency that has defined the striker's career. Ronaldo will now look to rediscover his scoring touch and move closer to the 1,000-goal milestone as Al-Nassr continues its pursuit of the league title.
