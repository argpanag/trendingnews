"I've never seen an NBA champion whose three stars were white, and it's not going to happen this year"

TheTools · 11.11.2026 12:22 · source · permalink

Controversial analyst Stephen A. Smith makes a prediction about the Lakers based on the race of their three main stars

  • Los Angeles Lakers.  Stephen A. Smith stands by “too white” Lakers championship take despite Luka Doncic’s start
  • Los Angeles Lakers.  What's going on with Luka? Doncic's inner circle reveals the shocking off-field reason behind his incredible form

Like in the old days, the NBA slate has left the Celtics atop the Eastern Conference and the Lakers in charge of the West. The Los Angeles team, with a 10-2 record, are the leaders of the competition. Good vibes with a Luka Doncic from another planet (averaging 39.3 points, 7.8 rebounds, 7.9 assists and 2.0 steals), but perhaps too early to know what the team’s true aspirations are.

However, there are those who are already certain they will not be contenders for the title. And in reaching that conclusion, he does not point to their play or talent... but to race. The controversial analyst Stephen A. Smith claims: "I have never seen an NBA team with three white players as its main stars win a championship. It has never happened, and I assure you I do not believe it is going to happen this year."

The aforementioned Doncic, Austin Reaves, and newly signed Walker Kessler are the Lakers' stars, and being Caucasian seems to be a drawback in Smith's opinion. "I know it's controversial, but I don't think they'll get past the second round. I would be very surprised if they did", says the analyst, who has the Thunder, the Spurs, and even the Nuggets ahead of them, and believes they would be evenly matched with the Rockets and the Timberwolves once Anthony Edwards recovers.

Eliminated in the second round despite having Doncic

Smith's speech does not deny the obvious, the great moment of Doncic: "He is unstoppable. He is leaving everyone open-mouthed. He is going to be an MVP candidate. There is no doubt that he is special”. He believes that "the Lakers are a playoff team," but once in the title series he does not give them many chances: "They will reach the second round, suffer a humiliating defeat and go home".

The analyst does believe that in the future the Lakers can aspire to very great heights. "The new front office is led by Kushner and Bob Iger. Bob Iger is, in my opinion, the Michael Jordan of sports. He is the best executive I have ever known, a mentor and a friend. I believe that the Lakers are in good hands and the future looks incredibly promising", he concluded.
