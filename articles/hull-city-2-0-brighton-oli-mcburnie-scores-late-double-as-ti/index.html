Oli McBurnie doubled his tally of Premier League goals this season from two to four against Brighton

Oli McBurnie's stoppage-time double earned victory for Hull City against second-placed Brighton in the Premier League.

The Scotland striker twice finished from close range, converting passes from Lucas Gourna-Douath and Mo Belloumi, after Brighton full-back Costinha had been sent off for a second bookable offence in the closing stages.

However, the Tigers were indebted to goalkeeper Konstantinos Tzolakis as they preserved their unbeaten home record in the league.

Tzolakis, whose previous appearance had been for Greece in a 7-0 Nations League loss to the Netherlands nine days earlier, was in inspired form to keep out the free-scoring Seagulls.

Diego Gomez, Yasin Ayari and Georginio Rutter were all superbly denied by Tzolakis during the first half, with Sweden midfielder Ayari also hitting the crossbar with a shot from just inside the penalty area.

Brighton continued to threaten after the interval as Lewis Dunk, Pascal Gross and Charalampos Kostoulas also went close to an opener.

Hull's best opportunities were created in the second half, with winger Belloumi sliding a low effort just wide and a looping cross by substitute Sorba Thomas coming back off the bar, before the late drama.

Brighton remain six points behind leaders Arsenal, while the win takes Hull 11th and five points above the relegation zone.

Tzolakis stars to repel Brighton

Sergej Jakirovic post-match interview - Hull v Brighton

McBurnie's two goals turned one point into three for Hull - but only after Tzolakis had kept them in the contest.

The 24-year-old has started every league game since signing for a reported £20m fee, then a club record, from Greek side Olympiacos in August.

He has made more saves than any other goalkeeper in the top flight this season and produced a series of fine stops to repel the highest scorers in the division.

Among his best was a block to deny a sliding Gomez from close range early on, while Rutter's powerful strike was somehow clawed away with an outstretched hand.

Kostoulas, on as a second-half substitute for the visitors, pounced on a poor Hull backpass late on to go one-on-one with his compatriot, who again proved equal to the task - albeit with some help from a covering defender on the goalline.

Those interventions proved crucial as Hull kept their first Premier League clean sheet since a run of three in succession at the start of the campaign, and registered their first league win since 29 August.

Brighton miss chance to close gap to Arsenal

Fabian Hurzeler post-match interview - Hull v Brighton

Brighton began the game knowing a win at Hull and another in their next Premier league match against Newcastle would have taken them top of the table before Arsenal and Manchester City meet on 29 November.

However, a seventh win in eight league games eluded the Seagulls on Sunday.

They dominated possession for long spells at MKM Stadium and put eight of their 15 attempts on target, but came up against into a goalkeeper in top form and were twice punished by swift Hull breaks late on.

With Brighton chasing an equaliser after McBurnie's 92nd-minute opener, the home side capitalised on a two-on-one situation at the other end as Belloumi squared the ball to the unmarked Scot, who finished into an unguarded net.

Fabian Hurzeler's side have a chance to bounce back from their third league defeat of the season when they host Romania side Craiova on Thursday in the Conference League.
