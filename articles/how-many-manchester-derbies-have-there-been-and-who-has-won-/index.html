How many Manchester derbies have there been and who has won the most?

TheTools · 13.09.2026 12:32 · source · permalink

On Sunday, Manchester United host Manchester City in the latest instalment of a more than century-long local rivalry.

On Sunday, Old Trafford is the venue for the 199th edition of the Manchester derby - and for the first time in a decade, it’s a crosstown Premier League showdown that won’t involve Pep Guardiola.

The Catalan departed as Manchester City head coach in the summer, after an illustrious, 10-season spell that yielded 15 major trophies. That haul included six Premier Leagues and the team’s first ever Champions League crown, won against Inter Milan in 2023.

Guardiola was in June succeeded by Enzo Maresca, who heads into his first derby seeking to extend City’s 100% start to the league season.

Charged with reclaiming the English title from Arsenal, the Italian has so far led the team to three wins from three top-flight games, beating Bournemouth, Crystal Palace and Coventry City.

Pep’s City not so dominant against United

During Guardiola’s time in charge at the Etihad Stadium, City were the clear preeminent force in English soccer. Indeed, a tally of 14 major domestic trophies puts them nine ahead of Liverpool, the next-most successful club in the English game between 2016/17 and 2025/26.

United, moreover, were limited to an FA Cup and two EFL Cups on the home front during that period. Their last Premier League title came back in 2012/13.

Yet despite outstripping their neighbors’ silverware count over the past 10 years, City’s head-to-head record against United was not so overwhelmingly in their favor. In 27 meetings in all competitions, Guardiola’s success rate against United only just cleared 50%: his City team won 14 and lost 10, with three draws.

Nevertheless, it was a positive overall record that ate some way into City’s deficit in the clubs’ all-time series, which stretches back over 130 years.

What is United and City’s all-time H2H record?

When Guardiola took charge of City in 2016, United had a 71-49 lead in competitive encounters since 1891, with 51 draws; now, ahead of the clubs’ first meeting of the 2026/27 campaign, the Red Devils’ advantage stands at 81-63, with 54 draws.

What happened in the last Manchester derby?

In Guardiola’s final Manchester derby, in January this year, Old Trafford witnessed a 2-0 Premier League win for United. Goals by Bryan Mbeumo and Patrick Dorgu earned the hosts victory, in what was Michael Carrick’s first game after replacing Rúben Amorim at the helm of the 20-time English champions.

Carrick, who has since become United’s full-time boss, had taken interim charge only four days earlier, after Amorim had been sacked just 14 months into his tenure.

“Most important thing is to be ourselves”

January’s derby saw City draw a second straight goalscoring blank at the home of their arch rivals, following a 0-0 draw at Old Trafford in April 2025. It’s a trend that Maresca is now tasked with reversing, as he attempts to match Arsenal’s perfect points tally of 12 from 12 so far this season.

“We know how important a derby is, especially here,” Maresca told a news conference on Friday. “For the club, for the fans, for everyone. So it’s a special game and the most important thing that we can try to do is be ourselves in the way we have been until today.”

Carrick, who signed a two-year deal as United’s permanent boss in May, vowed that his side will draw on growing self-belief to “make the most” of this weekend’s matchup with City.

“I think the confidence that we’ve built over a period of time, feeling better all the time, I think that helps us,” the 45-year-old told reporters. “It’s a big game, it’s an important game. There’s a lot riding on it - that’s pretty obvious to say! We’re looking forward to it.”

Carrick’s United go into matchday four with a total of four points from three Premier League games, having followed up an opening-day defeat to Hull City with victory over Ipswich Town and a draw with Everton.

On the European stage, meanwhile, United and City both geared up for the derby with victory in the Champions League in midweek. The Red Devils routed Sabah FK 4-0 on matchday one of the league phase, while City were 2-0 winners at FC Porto.
