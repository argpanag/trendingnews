AMBER Alert issued for missing 17 year-old last seen on West Side

SAN ANTONIO – An AMBER Alert has been issued for a missing 17-year-old last seen Monday night, according to the Texas Department of Public Safety.

Darian Rangel was last seen around 8:30 p.m. Sept. 7 in the 7200 block of Marbach Road.

Recommended Videos

DPS said Rangel was last seen wearing a red Bass Pro Shops hat, a black shirt, blue jeans and black shoes. He was also carrying a crossover Coach bag.

He is 5 feet, 7 inches tall and has brown hair and brown eyes, according to DPS.

Anyone with information on his whereabouts is asked to call the San Antonio Police Department at 210-207-7660.
