Afghanistan Pacers Fight Back After Tanzid's Blitz Puts Bangladesh on Top

TheTools · 10.10.2026 06:50 · source · permalink

Afghanistan's fast bowlers turned the opening day of the one-off Test against Bangladesh into an absorbing contest, striking repeatedly after Tanzid Hasan's attacking 83 had threatened to put the visitors firmly in control in Abu Dhabi on Friday.

Qais Ahmad picks up his first wicket in the game as he gets Najmul Hossain Shanto edging one behind to the slip cordon for fall for 29 and gives Afghanistan the 5th wicket in the process.

Bangladesh, cruising at 221 for 2 in the second session, lost three wickets for just four runs before another late collapse left them at 317 for 8 at stumps.

Mohammad Saleem led Afghanistan's fightback with three wickets for 70 runs, while Yamin Ahmadzai and Ismat Alam shared four wickets. Bangladesh had three half-centurions in Tanzid, Shadman Islam and Litton Das but failed to capitalise on their promising starts.

Tanzid Takes Attack to Afghanistan

Tanzid dominated the morning session, smashing 83 off just 71 balls with eight fours and four sixes.

The left-hander repeatedly used his feet against the fast bowlers, clearing the off-side field with ease. Ahmadzai came in for particular punishment as Tanzid struck a spectacular six over extra cover and sliced another delivery over point.

The spinners were not spared either, with Zia-ur-Rehman and Hashmatullah Shahidi disappearing for three sixes down the ground.

Tanzid and Shadman added 124 for the opening wicket, giving Bangladesh the perfect platform.

A century before lunch appeared within reach, but Tanzid's aggression eventually cost him. Attempting another attacking stroke, he edged Ismat behind, falling three runs short of Tamim Iqbal's Bangladesh record of 86 runs in the opening session of a Test.

Three Wickets for Four Runs

Shadman continued to build the innings alongside Mominul Haque, the pair adding 60 for the second wicket.

The opener reached his ninth Test half-century before falling for 67 off 111 balls, pulling Saleem's short delivery to square leg.

Ahmadzai then removed Mominul for 39 before producing a sharp yorker to dismiss Mushfiqur Rahim for a duck.

Captain Najmul Hossain Shanto briefly counterattacked, striking the ball cleanly during his 29, but edged leg-spinner Qais Ahmed to leave Bangladesh reeling at 225 for 5.

From a position of strength, the visitors suddenly found themselves needing a rescue act.

Litton Leads Recovery

Litton Das, coming off three successive ducks in Australia, provided some much-needed stability alongside Mehidy Hasan Miraz.

The pair added 62 for the sixth wicket, with Litton looking increasingly assured during his 53 off 89 balls, which included four boundaries.

Mehidy adopted a more cautious approach, scoring 24 off 67 deliveries before Ismat ended his resistance.

Litton's innings also ended before the second new ball, with Ahmadzai taking a fine diving catch near the fine-leg boundary.

Saleem then cleaned up Hasan Mahmud with a yorker, ensuring Afghanistan finished the day strongly.

Afghanistan Keep Contest Alive

Afghanistan's seamers deserve credit for refusing to let Bangladesh run away with the game.

Saleem's short-ball tactics, Ahmadzai's fuller lengths and Ismat's disciplined approach repeatedly unsettled the batters on a surface that had initially looked favourable for scoring.

Bangladesh will rue failing to convert three half-centuries into a substantial individual score, particularly after their commanding start.

Afghanistan, meanwhile, will look to wrap up the remaining two wickets quickly on the second morning and give their batters an opportunity to take control.

Brief Scores: Bangladesh 317/8 (Tanzid Hasan 83, Shadman Islam 67, Litton Das 53; Mohammad Saleem 3/70) vs Afghanistan. Stumps, Day 1.
