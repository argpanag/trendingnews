Cloudy skies and cool temperatures

TheTools · 23.09.2026 11:16 · source · permalink

BOWLING GREEN, Ky. (WBKO) - Today, we’ll once again see overcast skies and muted temperatures with highs around 70 degrees.

Residual low-level moisture from the cold front will lead to sporadic drizzle or mist throughout the day.

Winds from the north continue to push cool air into our region, and we’ll see another overcast day for Thursday, before the atmosphere dries up slightly to allow for more sunshine for Friday and into the weekend.

This dry airmass moving into our region will also push dewpoints into the low 50s and potentally upper 40s. This will lead to crisp morning low temperatures, and you may need a jacket or sweater for a morning walk.
