Korea Open: Quiet Step Forward

TheTools · 08.11.2026 07:06 · source · permalink

Thursday, November 5, 2026
TEXT BY DIANNE PIERRE | JNANESH SALIAN/BADMINTONPHOTO

Ten tournaments into their partnership, Chia Weijie/Teo Ee Yi finally have a breakthrough to show for their efforts.

The Malaysian pair booked their maiden Super 500 quarterfinal at the YEOSU VICTOR Korea Open 2026 with a 21-9 21-19 win over He Zhi Wei/Huang Jui-Hsuan, building on their earlier upset of fourth seeds Chen Bo Yang/Liu Yi in the opening round.

“It means a lot. It boosts my confidence,” Chia said. “It’s just a starting point for us. We want to go further.”

For Teo, who reached a career-high world No.6 alongside Ong Yew Sin, it marked an encouraging milestone in a partnership that has taken time to find its footing. Their win over Chen/Liu came as a surprise even to themselves. Rather than focusing on the opposition, the world No.39 duo concentrated on trusting the work they had put in on the training court.

“We didn’t expect that. During that match, we just played our best as we have in training.”

At 33, Teo remains one of the most experienced players on tour and spoke candidly about the challenge of staying competitive. For him, managing the mental side of the game is every bit as important as maintaining physical condition.

“If you control the mental side, in a ‘you can do it’ way, that’s good,” he said, adding that then “the physical side is no longer a problem”.

Chia highlighted Teo’s resilience as one of the qualities he admires most, describing his partner’s attitude as “never say lose, never say die”. The pair also credited their coaches in Yeosu, fellow players Hoo Pang Ron and Lai Pei Jing, for reinforcing a simple message throughout the week: maintain shot quality, regardless of the scoreline.

The Malaysians will look to go a step further when they take on fifth seeds Takuro Hoki/Yugo Kobayashi in the quarterfinals on Friday.
