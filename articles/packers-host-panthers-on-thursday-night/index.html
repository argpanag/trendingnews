Packers host Panthers on Thursday night

TheTools · 30.10.2026 01:47 · source · permalink

Green Bay has won three of the last four games against Carolina

The Green Bay Packers return home to take on the Carolina Panthers in primetime.

  • It is the first-ever Thursday meeting between Green Bay and Carolina.
  • It is the fifth meeting with the Panthers in the last eight years, including the second year in a row.
  • Green Bay has won three of the last four games against Carolina.
  • Including the postseason, the Packers are 12-7 (.632) against the Panthers.
  • The Packers have scored 24-plus points in 10 of the last 11 matchups.
  • Eight of the last nine contests, including each of the last six, have been decided by single digits.
  • This is the last of three home games for Green Bay over a 19-day span this month (Oct. 11-29). Next week, the Packers head east to take on the New England Patriots.

WITH THE CALL

The game will be broadcast across the country on Prime Video. If you live in the Green Bay market, it will also air on free, over-the-air television on NBC26 (WGBA), and if you live in the Milwaukee market, it will be shown on FOX6 (WITI). It will be available on mobile devices with NFL+ as well.

  • Play-by-play man Al Michaels joins analyst Kirk Herbstreit and sideline reporter Kaylee Hartung.
  • All Packers games can be heard on the Packers Radio Network, which is made up of 54 stations in Wisconsin, Michigan, Minnesota, Illinois, North Dakota and South Dakota. The flagship station of the Packers Radio Network is 95.7 BIG FM - WRIT. For a map and full listing of radio affiliates, go to packers.com/video/radio-network. Wayne Larrivee (play-by-play), two-time Packers Pro Bowler Larry McCarren (analyst), and three-time Packers Pro Bowler John Kuhn (sideline reporter) call the action. McCarren first joined the team's broadcasts in 1995 and enters his 32nd season calling Packers games. After originally being paired together in 1999, McCarren and Larrivee enter their 28th season of broadcasts together. They surpassed Jim Irwin and Max McGee for the most regular-season and postseason games broadcast for the Packers with 313 at the 2018 season opener against Chicago.
  • The radio broadcast can also be heard on Sirius XM NFL Radio on Channels 81 or 192 or in the app (Packers Radio Network feed).
  • Westwood One will be broadcasting on radio to a national audience with Ryan Radtke (play-by-play) and Marcel Reece (analyst) on the call and Scott Graham hosting from the studio.

MATT LaFLEUR…In his eighth season as the Packers' 15th head coach.

  • Is in his 18th season in the NFL in 2026, having served as an offensive coordinator for two seasons (Los Angeles Rams, 2017; Tennessee Titans, 2018), a QBs coach for six seasons (Washington Redskins, 2010-13; Atlanta Falcons, 2015-16) and an offensive assistant for two seasons (Houston Texans, 2008-09).
  • In his first seven seasons leading the Packers, has authored one of the finest starts to a head-coaching career in NFL history. Per the Elias Sports Bureau, his 76 regular-season wins are tied with Paul Brown for the second most in league history by a head coach in his first seven seasons in the NFL, trailing only George Seifert (86 in 1989-95). Green Bay's 76-40-1 (.654) regular-season record since 2019 ranks No. 1 in the NFC and No. 3 in the NFL over that span.
  • In his first three seasons in Green Bay (2019-21), became the first head coach in NFL history to post 13-plus wins in three straight years. His 39 victories over that span passed Seifert (38 in 1989-91) for the most wins by an NFL head coach in his first three seasons in the league and are also tied for the fourth most by a coach over any three-season span in league history (Mike Ditka, 40 from 1985-87; Andy Reid, 40 from 2020-22 and 2022-24).
  • Led the Packers to six playoff berths in seven seasons (tied for No. 2 in the NFL over that span), tied with Vince Lombardi and Mike Holmgren for the most playoff appearances by a Green Bay head coach in his first seven seasons.
  • In his first season leading the Packers in 2019, became the first coach in club history to guide the team to the playoffs in his debut season. Also led the largest one-year win improvement in team history (plus-seven wins).

DAVE CANALES…In his third season as the Panthers' seventh head coach.

  • Has 16 years of NFL experience, having previously spent 13 seasons with the Seattle Seahawks in various offensive roles (2010-22) and a season as the Tampa Bay Buccaneers' offensive coordinator (2023).
  • In 2025, helped lead the Panthers to an NFC South title after a 1-3 start, becoming just the 10th team to win its division after a 1-3 start over the past 20 seasons.
  • Joined Dom Capers (1996) and John Fox (2003) as the only Carolina head coaches to appear in the postseason in one of their first two seasons, with 2025 marking the 12th time Canales has coached in the playoffs.
  • In his lone season as offensive coordinator in Tampa Bay, helped QB Baker Mayfield throw for 4,044 yards, and 28 TDs, completing 364 passes with a 64.3 percent completion rate.

THE PACKERS-PANTHERS SERIES

  • The first meeting between these franchises came in the 1996 NFC title game, when the Packers defeated the Panthers, a team that was coached by former Green Bay defensive coordinator Dom Capers, 30-13, at Lambeau Field.
  • Green Bay holds the longest win streak in the series, winning three consecutive games from 2001-04, and leads the all-time series at 11-7.
  • Green Bay has won three of the last five regular-season matchups at Lambeau Field, scoring 24-plus points in all three victories.
