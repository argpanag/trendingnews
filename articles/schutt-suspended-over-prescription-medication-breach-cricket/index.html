Schutt suspended over prescription medication breach | cricket.com.au

TheTools · 22.10.2026 09:17 · source · permalink

Australia fast bowler accepts penalty and apologises for 'failing to meet the standards' expected of her

Megan Schutt has been handed a two-match suspension for failing to adhere to requirements around the use of prescription medication.

The veteran Australia pace bowler will miss Thursday's third and final T20I against New Zealand at North Sydney Oval, as well as the Adelaide Strikers' opening game of Weber WBBL|12 against Melbourne Stars on October 31.

A statement from Cricket Australia said: "The breach was a failure to adhere to requirements under the Sports Science and Sports Medicine guidelines with regard to prescription medication.

"This relates to a single incidence which occurred while out of competition and was not for performance enhancing purposes.

"We will continue to provide appropriate support to Megan and reinforce the standards and responsibilities expected of everyone representing our game."

In a statement, Schutt, 33, said she had accepted the penalty.

"I fully accept the penalty handed down and apologise for failing to meet the standards expected of me," Schutt's statement read.

"I'm sorry for the attention this has caused and appreciate the support I've received.

"While this occurred out of competition and was not performance related, I understand that I am responsible for following the Sports Science and Sports Medicine Guidelines, which players receive regular education on, and I should have adhered to these guidelines."
