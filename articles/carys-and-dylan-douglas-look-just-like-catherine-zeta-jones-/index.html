Carys Douglas and Dylan Douglas, the lookalike children of Michael Douglas and Catherine Zeta-Jones, just hit the red carpet with their dad.

Michael, 81, has a prolific film background with iconic movies ranging from Wall Street and Basic Instinct to Traffic and Wonder Boys. He's been married to Zeta-Jones, 56, since 2000, and one look at Dylan and Carys makes it clear who their parents are. The sweet kids hit the red carpet to attend the Tibet House US Art of Freedom Gala 2026 in New York City with their dad, and with the dark coloring of their mother, it's proof the apple didn't fall far from the tree.

Douglas Also Has a Son, Cameron

Douglas has spoken openly about his struggles with son Cameron, who has battled drug and alcohol addiction. A lot of his inner battle has been due to not being a presence in Cameron's life as he grew up.

"I wasn't around a lot, so I have my own guilt with Cameron," Douglas candidly shared in an interview with The New York Times.

Douglas Opened Up About His Challenging Childhood

Douglas may be a proud dad these days, but his childhood wasn't exactly the easiest. In a recent interview with Diane Sawyer, Douglas shares his triumphs and challenges growing up in Hollywood as the son of legendary star Kirk Douglas.

In the book, Douglas writes, "I spent some portion of my career playing some version of the modern, flawed American male, and it was not a stretch."

When Sawyer asked about the statement, Douglas said he "rarely smiled or laughed" as a child and was often in tears.

"He was larger than life," Douglas said of his father, a strict disciplinarian. "He meant well, but he was out of that generation where you're smacked around a fair amount."
