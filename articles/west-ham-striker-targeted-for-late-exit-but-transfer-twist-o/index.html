West Ham Striker Targeted for Late Exit — But Transfer Twist Offers Hope

TheTools · 03.09.2026 07:37 · source · permalink

By Kris Gonzo | Senior West Ham Columnist (Follow on X)

Whilst the transfer window might have slammed shut for West Ham in terms of buying players, there still lies the opportunity for some more of Nuno Espírito Santo’s squad to be picked off by leagues where Deadline Day hasn’t happened yet.

One of those is Turkey’s Süper Lig, where the transfer window remains open until 4 September.

And now The Times are reporting interest from Trabzonspor in Hammers striker Pablo Felipe – fresh off his best game in a West Ham shirt.

There were reports earlier this summer that Pablo’s agent, a certain Jorge Mendes, was seeking to find a move for his client.

No surprise there, I guess. That’s precisely what Mendes does.

And there were certainly more than a few West Ham fans, myself included, who wouldn’t have been sorry to see him go.

West Ham striker Pablo Felipe is reportedly attracting interest from Turkish Süper Lig club Trabzonspor.

Selling Pablo Now Would Be Madness

However, summer dealings have seen two strikers leave in the form of Callum Wilson and Niclas Füllkrug, with Joel Piroe arriving.

Factor in Pablo’s improved performance against Wolverhampton Wanderers, and the fact that West Ham do seem to perform better with him in the side, and it would be absolute madness to sell him.

I’ve been critical of Pablo plenty of times, but against Wolves he was integral to West Ham’s attacking performance.

He may not have registered a shot, but his work rate, link-up play and involvement in the build-up to Divine Mukasa’s opener helped make it his best performance since joining West Ham.

With the squad already short of attacking options, allowing another striker to disappear after the English window has closed would leave West Ham unable to replace him.

Trabzonspor Have Already Signed Another Striker

Not that I completely believe Pablo will end up at Trabzonspor.

The Turkish club have just completed the signing of Franculino Djú from FC Midtjylland for €17million.

That’s particularly interesting because West Ham themselves were offered the opportunity to sign Djú earlier this summer, only to look elsewhere.

Trabzonspor have now committed significant money to the 22-year-old, so it could simply be a case of Pablo having been on their list rather than still being somebody they need.

Let’s hope so.

Because whatever I might have thought about Pablo previously, West Ham’s circumstances have changed.

Selling him now would be absolute madness.
