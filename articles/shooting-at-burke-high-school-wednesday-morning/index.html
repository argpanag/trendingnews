Shooting at Burke High School Wednesday morning

TheTools · 30.09.2026 12:40 · source · permalink

Officials have confirmed to KETV that there has been a shooting at Burke High School.

We know that the call went out around 7:30 a.m. Wednesday, at least one person has been shot and has critical injuries.

Officials confirmed to KETV that one person is in custody.

The Omaha Police Department is investigating a shooting incident at Burke High School.

We believe this was an isolated incident between two people. The victim suffered a non-life-threatening injury, and we believe the other involved party is detained at this time.

— Omaha Police Dept (@OmahaPolice) September 30, 2026

The Omaha Police Department is investigating a shooting incident at Burke High School.

We believe this was an isolated incident between two people. The victim suffered a non-life-threatening injury, and we believe the other involved party is detained at this time.

Burke High is on "secure" right now and police are on scene.

Omaha Public Schools tells KETV the shooting happened on school grounds, but not inside the school.

Around 8:15 a.m., the district said a firearm has been recovered.

Omaha police said secure status was lifted at the school shortly after the firearm was recovered.

This is a developing situation. OPS says they will update their alert page every 30 minutes, see that here.
