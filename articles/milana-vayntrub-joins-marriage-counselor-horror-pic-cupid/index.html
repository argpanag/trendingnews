Milana Vayntrub Joins Marriage Counselor Horror Pic 'Cupid'

TheTools · 20.11.2026 17:36 · source · permalink

Cupid has hit Milana Vayntrub (pictured above in Werewolves Within), reports Deadline.

She joins Catherine Zeta-Jones (The Haunting), Daisy Ridley (Ebenezer), Darren Criss (Glee), Travis Fimmel (Dune: Prophecy), and Judy Greer (Halloween Kills) in the horror pic.

Cupid follows a couple (Ridley and Criss) whose relationship is on the rocks as they try, over one weekend, to repair their marriage at the home of a seemingly helpful but unorthodox marriage counselor (Zeta-Jones). What starts as a hopeful endeavor quickly turns dark as her methods become crueler and more dangerous.

Tate Taylor (Blumhouse’s Ma, The Girl on the Train, The Help) directed from a screenplay by the Van Dyke brothers (Don’t Worry Darling).

No word yet on who Vayntrub will play, but I love this casting choice. She’s a highly engaging actor who displayed a knack for horror comedy in Werewolves Within. Haven’t seen it yet? You really, really need to. And I’m not just saying that because I’m obsessed with werewolves. It’s legitimately great.

John Norris and Taylor will be producing alongside Ryan Donnell Smith. Executive producers include Brian Edwards, Zack Sherman, Jon Hamm, and Jaan Bains for Time Zone Capital.

Cupid is now filming in Natchez, Mississippi.
