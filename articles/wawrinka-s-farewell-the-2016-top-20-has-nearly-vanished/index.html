Wawrinka's Farewell: The 2016 Top 20 Has Nearly Vanished

TheTools · 28.10.2026 16:49 · source · permalink

Tennis changes generations: with the farewells of Wawrinka, Nishikori, Raonic, Monfils, Goffin and Bautista-Agut, the world elite of October 2016 has almost completely disappeared.

Stan Wawrinka said farewell to professional tennis this Tuesday against Jiri Lehecka in Basel. In the rankings of October 31, 2016, ten years ago, the Swiss player was world No. 3.

Six players retiring in 2026, nine already retired before

As noted by the X account @PhilBlack09, six players from that era's Top 20 have officially announced their retirement this year.

Wawrinka, Nishikori, Raonic, Monfils, Goffin and Bautista-Agut have just said their goodbyes.

Nine players had already retired: Murray, Nadal, Thiem, Federer, Berdych, Tsonga, Ferrer, Gasquet and Karlovic.

Only five players from that era are still active: Djokovic (still in the Top 20), Marin Cilic (still in the Top 100), as well as Kyrgios, Pouille and Dimitrov (outside the Top 100).
