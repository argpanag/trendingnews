9.06pm: Broncos @ Chargers – Denver Broncos -3.5

There are so many injuries hampering teams this week across the league that we’re focusing on the three late games on Sunday evening, starting with the game at SoFi Stadium in LA.

We’ve liked the Broncos all season – I went for them as a Super Bowl pick in the season preview – and they were unlucky to lose to the 49ers in last week’s big game.

The Chargers haven’t won this season and were flattered by some garbage time scores that made their loss to the Seahawks look closer on the scoreboard than it was on the field.

LA are one of the teams that have been battered by injuries and along the offensive line especially, which looks a complete mess ahead of this game and should help Denver bounce back from a tough loss last week.

9.26pm: 49ers @ Seahawks – Seattle Seahawks -3

Super Bowl champions Seattle haven’t been all that impressive this season so far, but they have had the 49ers’ number, beating them twice in their last two meetings.

We discussed the 49ers injuries last week and they picked up another few notable knocks on Sunday.

It means the Seahawks at home to cover a field goal looks tempting.

9:26pm: Lions @ Cardinals – Trey McBride anytime touchdown

After Brock Bowers delivered an anytime score for us last week, we’re going for another tight-end to do the business in Try McBride of the Arizona Cardinals.

He’s been peppered with targets this season by quarterback Jacoby Brissett. He hasn’t had fewer than 10 targets in any game this season, he’s scored a touchdown in half the games so far and we think he’ll do it again in what should be a shootout in Glendale.
