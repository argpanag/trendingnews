Opta predicts where Leeds United will finish in the Premier League, and their chances of Europe

TheTools · 03.09.2026 10:13 · source · permalink

So far, so good for Leeds United. A transfer window fans could only dream about, and unbeaten in their three games this season. But where will Leeds finish come the end of the season?

It is incredibly early to predict, but some believe Europe is not out of the question. The signings of James Trafford, Tarik Muharemovic, Harry Wilson and Jean-Matteo Bahoya are causes for optimism, however.

BARD AND BEHOYA: How would you rank Leeds United’s business on deadline day? 🏆

Leeds have signed both on loan, but they could cost a combined £31m.

As things stand, Leeds sit in eighth position after four points from their opening two games against Nottingham Forest and Brentford. The latter are also poised to have a good season under Keith Andrews.

On Saturday, Leeds take on a fixture that has proven to be difficult for them in the past. Last season, Brighton away resulted in a 3-0 loss as Georginio Rutter ran the show and even apologised for an assist.

Opta predict Leeds to improve on last season’s 14th place

While it would be incredibly underwhelming, success is merely avoiding relegation. Consolidation as a Premier League side is crucial; removing any of the financial drawbacks of being in the Championship.

Given the summer Leeds have had and the start they have made, there is optimism to finish above 14th in the Premier League. But realistically, just staying in the top tier is still a step forward for the Yorkshire club.

However, Opta are slightly more optimistic than a mere 17th-placed finish. Indeed, they believe Leeds will improve upon last season’s 14th place and finish in a very respectable 11th, with a total of 49.99 points.

They think Leeds will finish above Tottenham, who are projected to finish 14th, and even Bournemouth who are predicted to finish 12th. Ipswich, Fulham and Coventry are the sides expected to be relegated.

What are Leeds’ chances of European qualification this season?

Leeds have played 22 Premier League matches in 2026, earning a total of 31 points. That’s an average of 1.41 points a game, and if they managed that across the whole of last season, they’d have 53.58 points.

LEEDS V BRIGHTON: What are your predictions for Saturday’s clash?

Daniel Farke can make history with a win.

Rounding up to 54, Leeds would finish a point above Brighton and Brentford, level with Sunderland. The Black Cats and The Seagulls earned European qualification last season, with a 50+ total possibly enough.

Opta give Leeds a surprising 10.5% chance of qualifying for the Champions League, and a 4.6% chance of a Europa League spot. The latter is smaller due to fewer qualifying places for the Europa League.

But as aforementioned, safety is paramount. Achieving 40 points is the first aim, and whatever comes after that is a bonus. Leeds’ sole aim is to remain a Premier League club come the summer of 2027.
