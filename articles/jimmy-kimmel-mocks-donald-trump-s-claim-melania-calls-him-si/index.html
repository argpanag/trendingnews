The punchline drew laughter from the audience as Kimmel challenged Trump’s account of the conversation. His comment was a joke about the couple’s relationship, rather than evidence about their private communications.

The exchange began as Kimmel discussed Trump’s attacks on Democrat James Talarico. According to the host, Trump had repeatedly mispronounced “vegan” while describing the politician.

Kimmel said he had been wondering whether anyone close to the president would correct him. "And finally someone did and she happens to be from Slovenia," he said, introducing footage in which Trump credited his wife with the correction.

In the clip, Trump began describing his wife’s intervention, saying, "You know, my wife told me, ‘Sir, do me a favor.’" Kimmel immediately stopped the footage to focus on the formal address. He then expanded his skepticism, telling viewers that the detail stood out even among other presidential statements he doubted.

"Of all the things of the thousands of things he has said that I do not believe Melania call him sir is the thing I believe less than all the other things I don't believe at all but I digress please go on with the vegan thing," Kimmel said.

When the footage resumed in front of viewers, Trump explained that Melania had told him to pronounce the word properly. He defended his unfamiliarity with the term by pointing to his food preferences.

“How the hell would I know? I like steak,” Trump said. Kimmel seized on that explanation for another personal jab, mocking the contrast between Trump’s emphasis on steak and his appearance.

"That's right. He likes steak. He's a man's man with a hair weave and a half a tub of bronzer on his face," Kimmel said. The segment formed part of a broader monologue targeting Trump’s political rhetoric and public statements.

Kimmel also mocked the president’s repeated calls for a Nobel Peace Prize and challenged his claims about keeping campaign promises.

But the Melania exchange supplied one of the night’s most direct personal punchlines, with Kimmel moving from a dispute over a single word to a joke about whether the first lady phones her husband.
