'Perfect' Strictly Come Dancing couple 'will give fans exactly what they want'

TheTools · 22.09.2026 15:40 · source · permalink

A body language expert has revealed the Strictly Come Dancing pairing that is "sure to give the fans exactly what they want".

Strictly Come Dancing has finally returned to BBC One with a brand new season, with the fresh celebrity faces waiting to take to the dance floor each week to showcase their skills.

Amongst the line up include Lacey Turner, Delta Goodrem, Chris Appleton, Cach Mercer, Dani Dyer and Will Best, with the full line-up available here.

In the hopes of being crowned this year's winners, the 15 celebrity contestants will battle it out each week on the dance floor with their celebrity partners as one of the UK's favourite programmes has finally returned.

But with the new show dominating weekend television, one body language expert has already had their say on what the Strictly paired couples really think about each other, based on their chemistry.

Speaking on behalf of Paddy Power, expert Judi James revealed what she really thought about the show's opening, where the pairings are officially announced and confirmed.

She said: "Fans of the show should always ignore the reactions when the pairings are first announced as they are always fake and over-performed.

"Where some hints of genuine reactions did begin to leak out though was on the balcony during the interviews, especially during those telling moments after each interview when the camera ‘performances’ stopped and some real reactions started to appear as pro dancers in particular thought they were off-screen."

However, it's good news for Dany Dyer fans as she has again been paired with Nikita. Dani made her iconic return over the weekend, after a horror injury forced her to quit last season.

Judi confirmed: "This is a meeting of reality icons which means a perfect body language acting pairing that is sure to give the fans exactly what they want.

"Comfortable tactile and visually compatible they had no signs of any negative leakage as they never came out of role here. We had eye contact; heads pressed together and even a totes-emotive hand on the heart from Dani. Prescriptive body language displays that will probably not falter through the series."

Dani has since taken to social media following her announced pairing with Nikita as the support came pouring in. In a video, captioned: "GUYS! We need your help. What should our team name be?", the stars were seen asking for viewer help with their chemistry undeniable on screen.

Replying to the sweet clip, one person wrote: "Twice as nice you guys have the best energy." Another said: "Team Nani! so happy you two are together again! Looking forward to watching you two every week."

Strictly Come Dancing returns Saturday, September 26 at 6.15pm on BBC One
