Coleman to decide future 'very soon'

TheTools · 08.09.2026 12:22 · source · permalink

Republic of Ireland defender Séamus Coleman has revealed he turned down the chance to join Championship side Sheffield United and expects to make a decision on his future "very, very soon".

After amassing 433 appearances for Everton after signing from Sligo Rovers in 2009, the Donegal man brought an end to a 17-year stay with the Toffees during the summer.

The 37-year-old struggled with injuries for the past couple of seasons and slipped down the pecking order, making just three appearances last season, the last of which occurred in the 1-0 win away to Manchester United in November, when he went off injured after just 10 minutes.

His international career however was rejuvenated under Heimir Hallgrimsson, having been preciously excluded by the Icelander, with Coleman starring in victories over Portugal and Hungary that secured a World Cup play-off against Czechia, a match that ended in penalty heartache.

Back in May he hinted that the prospect of playing at Euro 2028 has given him food for thought, and speaking on Stick to Football on Disney+ this week, the full-back admitted that he would have hung up the boots if it weren't for the Republic of Ireland manager.

"I probably was done 100% at the end of the season, until I spoke with the Ireland manager," he said.

"I'd done quite well in the Ireland games over the last year, we got close to the World Cup...he mentioned about me staying on, to be part of it for the Euros in a couple of years. That's a long way away.

"That kind of planted a seed in my mind - maybe I could carry on and see what it's like."

Coleman confirmed that he is keeping fit as he assesses his next move. He was back in Donegal for six weeks and trained with both his local Gaelic football and soccer teams, and is calm about what the future holds.

"I'm keeping fit and training and not stressing too much about what's next…But, yeah, now the window closes, things might open up a little bit," he said. "I'll be making a decision very, very soon. Again, if it wasn't for the Ireland stuff, my decision would have been made."

The 81-cap international insisted that he isn’t going to rush any decision, while also revealing that he turned down the opportunity to drop down to the Championship with Sheffield United after discussions with Blades manager Chris Wilder.

"I met the manager, I went down there...it just felt a bit soon after Everton," he said. "It all happened too soon, I didn't want to keep them hanging on, because I didn't want to mess them around trying to get players in...I decided it just wasn't right."

Joining Everton as a 20-year old, Coleman went onto become a club legend, winning the club's player of the season award in 2013-14 and earning inclusion on the PFA Team of the Year the same season.

In January 2025, he briefly served as interim player-manager, alongside Leighton Baines in the dugout, in the wake of Sean Dyche's sacking, with Everton beating Peterborough United 2-0 in the FA Cup in his sole game in the role.
