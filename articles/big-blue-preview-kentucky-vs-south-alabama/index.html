Info to Know

Preview

A week after beating a Top-10 team on the road, Kentucky returns to Kroger Field to face South Alabama on Saturday (12:45 p.m. ET, SEC Network).

The Cats (2-1) beat No. 9 Texas A&M 31-21 in College Station last Saturday. Kenny Minchey passed for 252 yards and two touchdowns, while C.J. Baxter rushed for 87 yards and a pair of scores.

South Alabama (2-1) beat Ohio University 41-36 on Saturday in Mobile. The Jaguars’ loss came at Tulane on Sept. 12.

This will be the first football meeting between  Kentucky and South Alabama.

Cat Scratches: Stein, Cats Moving Forward After Big Win at Texas A&M

Kentucky earned a huge road win on Saturday, beating then-ninth-ranked Texas A&M 31-21 at Kyle Field in College Station. On Tuesday, UK head coach Will Stein met the media and he discussed just how important getting that road win was to his program.

“It’s really hard to get road wins, no matter what league you’re in,” Stein said. “So that was really big to get (the win) on the road.”

Stein knows that his team’s preparation for Saturday’s game is what directly led to the performance on the field.

“Direct correlation with how we practice,” Stein said. “There’s no fluke about it. We practice hard, we play hard. Practice physical, play physical. Make plays in practice, make plays in the game.”
