Xbox Disc-To-Digital Officially Exits Testing

TheTools · 02.10.2026 15:32 · source · permalink

Original XBOX and Xbox 360 game discs are not supported. Disc to digital licensing works with supported Xbox One and XBOX Series X game discs only. However, not all Xbox One or Xbox Series X game discs are eligible for this licensing. Eligibility may vary due to technical, licensing, publishing, manufacturing, or other factors. Microsoft may also change eligibility over time.
