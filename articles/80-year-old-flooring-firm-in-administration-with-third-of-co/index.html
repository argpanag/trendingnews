80-year-old flooring firm in administration with third of counters to close

TheTools · 09.09.2026 11:14 · source · permalink

UK flooring distributor Headlam has entered administration with 154 job losses and the closure of a third of its UK trade counters. The Birmingham-based firm, founded in 1948. is closing 28 of its 76 counters, which are stores set up within factories to serve tradespeople directly.

Headlam's remaining 17 distribution centers and 48 trade counters will stay open. Operating under a Company Voluntary Arrangement restructuring, the company will continue trading while administrators implement cost reductions and refinance its debt facilities to build long-term financial sustainability.

The floor-covering specialist currently employs around 1,300 people. Trading in its shares was suspended in early September after the company warned of liquidity shortages driven by wider economic pressures, such as cost inflation and weak consumer confidence. Advisors from Interpath were appointed as administrators on Tuesday to manage the restructuring.

"Our immediate priority is to those employees whose roles have been lost as a result of today’s site closures," said Will Wright, UK chief executive at Interpath and joint administrator to Headlam Group. "We understand this is devastating news and we are committed to ensuring that everyone impacted receives the support and information they need as quickly as possible.

"For the rest of the business, our focus is on implementing a restructuring that will give Headlam the best chance possible of recovery. Subject to the ongoing support of the group’s key stakeholders, including customers and suppliers, we believe the proposed plan provides a credible path to stabilising the business."
