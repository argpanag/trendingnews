Motul-Powered Vance & Hines Suzuki Teammates Will Battle for NHRA Championship at Season Finale

TheTools · 12.11.2026 14:10 · source · permalink

POMONA, CA – November 12, 2026 – (Motor Sports NewsWire) – The 2026 NHRA Mission Foods Drag Racing Series Pro Stock Motorcycle championship comes down to the wire this weekend at the In-N-Out Burger Pomona Dragstrip.

Richard Gadson and Gaige Herrera of the Motul-backed RevZilla/Vance & Hines Suzuki team enter the final race weekend positioned 1-2 in the world championship standings, setting up a championship battle between the Vance & Hines teammates.

Defending 2025 champion Richard Gadson leads the points chase (2,579 pts) following victories in Gainesville, Norwalk, and Rockingham. Chasing him is 2024 champion Gaige Herrera in second (2,524 pts), who stays within striking distance (55 points back) after claiming wins in Charlotte, Bristol, and St. Louis. Both riders rely on Vance & Hines-tuned Suzuki Hayabusas protected by high-performance Motul lubricants, operating under severe thermal strain at over 10,000 RPM and 200+ MPH.

In an additional twist, the final round of the recent Las Vegas event, a matchup between Herrera and Gadson, was postponed due to high winds and will be run this weekend in Pomona, making this a doubleheader for the Vance & Hines team. This matchup, scheduled to run before the third round of qualifying this weekend, will impact point standings going into the final race on Sunday.

WHAT:
2026 NHRA Drag Racing Series Season Finale — Pro Stock Motorcycle Championship Fight

WHO:

    • Richard Gadson (Points Leader, RevZilla/Vance & Hines Suzuki)
    • Gaige Herrera (2nd in Points, RevZilla/Vance & Hines Suzuki)
  • Richard Gadson (Points Leader, RevZilla/Vance & Hines Suzuki)
  • Gaige Herrera (2nd in Points, RevZilla/Vance & Hines Suzuki)

WHEN:
This Weekend (November 12-15)

WHERE:
In-N-Out Burger Pomona Dragstrip, Pomona, CA

About Motul

Motul specializes in the formulation, production, and distribution of high-quality lubricants that elevate performance in multiple usages from powersport to passenger cars, heavy duty, and industrial applications. Motul has pioneered synthetic oils with its flagship 300V, the first 100% synthetic multigrade lubricant. Building on this long-term know-how, Motul is now extending its expertise to care products for cars, motorbikes and bicycles, as well as advanced fluids for the thermal management of electric vehicle batteries and digital infrastructures such as data centers, addressing the performance and sustainability challenges of tomorrow. Founded in the United States in 1853, Motul is a global company present in 160 countries. Over the years, the brand has built a solid reputation as a trusted partner to support the performance of leading manufacturers, top motorsport teams and major championships, such as the 24 Hours of Le Mans, IMSA, the WEC, MotoGP, MotoAmerica, Dakar, Goodwood, or the UCI Mountain Bike World Series.
