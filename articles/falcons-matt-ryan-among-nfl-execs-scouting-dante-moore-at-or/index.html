Falcons' Matt Ryan Among NFL Execs Scouting Dante Moore at Oregon vs. Oklahoma State

TheTools · 12.09.2026 19:03 · source · permalink

Several NFL talent evaluators will reportedly be in attendance when the Oregon Ducks and Oklahoma State Cowboys clash Saturday in Stillwater, Oklahoma.

According to ESPN's Max Olson, Atlanta Falcons president of football Matt Ryan and general manager Ian Cunningham, Arizona Cardinals general manager Monti Ossenfort and 20 NFL scouts will be on hand.

Their primary focus will likely be on the quarterbacks, as Oregon's Dante Moore and Oklahoma State's Drew Mestemaker are both in the mix as potential first-round picks in the 2027 NFL draft.
