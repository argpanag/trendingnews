NBA games on today, full TV schedule for Monday, November 2

TheTools · 02.11.2026 21:27 · source · permalink

The 2026-27 NBA season continues Monday with 15 games on the schedule. The Oklahoma City Thunder visit the Cleveland Cavaliers in the first matchup of the day, with tipoff set for 7 p.m. ET.

There are several other games to watch, including the Chicago Bulls vs. Philadelphia 76ers, Detroit Pistons vs. Charlotte Hornets and Brooklyn Nets vs. New York Knicks. The Boston Celtics also visit the Houston Rockets, while the Miami Heat take on the Los Angeles Lakers in one of three games scheduled for 10 p.m. ET.

Thunder vs. Cavaliers is the only nationally televised game on Monday’s slate. The matchup will air on NBCSN and stream on Peacock, while NBA League Pass will carry the other games alongside their respective local networks. Here’s the full NBA TV schedule, including tipoff times, TV channels and streaming options.
