Four Italian soldiers injured at Ādaži military base

TheTools · 27.09.2026 22:03 · source · permalink

All four soldiers were taken to a medical facility following the incident, where they are receiving the necessary medical attention.

According to the NBS, none of the injured soldiers’ lives are in danger.

The Italian Ministry of Defence has stated that the incident occurred during live-fire training involving a non-automatic firearm mounted on an Italian military vehicle. The rounds struck another Italian military vehicle, in which four soldiers were travelling.

The injured have been taken to a medical facility in Rīga, where they are currently receiving the necessary medical care and undergoing examinations. Although none of the soldiers’ lives are in danger, one of them is in a more serious condition, according to the Italian Ministry of Defence.

The soldiers’ families have been informed.

An investigation has been launched to establish the circumstances of the incident.
