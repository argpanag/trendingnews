Life under new boss Jürgen Klopp hasn’t gone quite as smoothly for Germany as many would have expected, with Die Mannschaft currently in third place with just one win from its four games so far. Three points behind the Netherlands and Greece, another poor result here in Belgrade in its penultimate match could see the team miss out on the quarterfinals.

Germany faces a Serbian team very much playing for pride, with Veljko Paunović’s squad having failed to pick up a single point so far and looking likely to be relegated to the tournament’s second tier.
