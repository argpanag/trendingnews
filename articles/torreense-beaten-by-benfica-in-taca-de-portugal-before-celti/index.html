Torreense beaten by Benfica in Taca de Portugal before Celtic Europa League visit

TheTools · 23.11.2026 00:06 · source · permalink

Musa Drammeh scored for Torreense but their night ended in disappointment

  • Published
    1 hour ago

Celtic's next Europa League opponents Torreense lost 3-1 to Benfica, ending the second-tier side's defence of the Taca de Portugal at the fourth-round stage.

Torreense qualified for the Europa League by winning the domestic cup tournament for the first time last season but could not overcome the team from Lisbon at Estadio Nacional.

Jhon Duran gave Benfica a first-half lead only for former Hearts forward Musa Drammeh to level after the break. Gianluca Prestianni put Benfica back in front and his replacement Jakub Kaminski got their third late on.

Celtic travel to Portugal for Thursday's fifth Europa League fixture, having lost to Benfica in Lisbon earlier in the competition.

Martin O'Neill's Scottish champions have three points from their opening four games and sit 32nd out of the 36 sides. Torreense have six points in 19th place, with Benfica leading the standings as one of three teams with a 100% record.

The top eight progress to the round of 16, with the sides placed ninth through 24th competing with each other to join the top eight sides later in the tournament.
