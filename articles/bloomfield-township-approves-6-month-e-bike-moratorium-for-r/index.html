Bloomfield Township approves 6-month e-bike moratorium for riders under 16

TheTools · 15.09.2026 21:43 · source · permalink

Local News

The moratorium applies to Class 1, 2, and 3 electric bicycles and electric scooters, commonly referred to as e-bikes

BLOOMFIELD TOWNSHIP, Mich. – The Bloomfield Township Board of Trustees has approved a six-month moratorium on certain e-bikes and electric scooters for people under 16 on township-owned property and rights-of-way.

The board approved the moratorium on Monday (Sept. 14) after township officials described numerous accidents involving electric bikes and scooters over the summer, including incidents involving minors.

The moratorium applies to Class 1, 2, and 3 electric bicycles and electric scooters commonly referred to as e-bikes.

Under the resolution, people younger than 16 are prohibited from using the vehicles on township-owned property and rights-of-way under township jurisdiction.

Riders 16 and older with a valid driver’s license may use e-bikes under the township’s rules.

The moratorium does not apply to electric bicycles that are explicitly permitted under Michigan law.

Residents who believe they qualify for an exemption can submit a written request to the township through its online Contact Us form.

Township officials said the six-month moratorium is intended to give the community time to conduct additional research and consider potential legislation.

Officials also warned residents that not every electric-powered, two-wheeled vehicle legally qualifies as an e-bike under Michigan law.

Some vehicles may meet the state’s definition of a motorcycle and therefore fall under different laws and requirements.

Gallagher also said identifying electric vehicles can challenge public safety officials.

The board-approved draft resolution will be signed when the meeting minutes are approved.

Township officials said they will post any changes to the moratorium on the township’s website.
