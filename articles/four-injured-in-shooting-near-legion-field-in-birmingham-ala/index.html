Four Injured in Shooting Near Legion Field in Birmingham, Alabama | Cedar News

TheTools · 01.11.2026 01:29 · source · permalink

Four people were injured in a shooting near Legion Field in Birmingham, Alabama, Saturday evening, according to Birmingham Fire and Rescue.

Gunfire erupted shortly before 8 p.m. on Graymont Avenue West. Emergency crews were treating victims near Third Street West.

Further details were not immediately available.
