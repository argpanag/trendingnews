Prince William's brutal one word verdict on Prince Harry sums up true feelings

TheTools · 24.09.2026 06:00 · source · permalink

Prince William is still believed to be estranged from his brother(Image: Getty Images)

Prince Harry seems to have had a busy time since his return to the UK several weeks ago.

The Duke of Sussex, along with his wife, Meghan Markle, and their two children, Archie and Lilibet, made the decision to return to the UK six years after their bombshell decision to quit both Britain and their roles as senior working royals.

The family set up their new home in Montecito, California, with the aim of becoming financially independent. They carried out a series of high profile interviews, including with Oprah Winfrey, featured in their own Netflix series, Finding Freedom, and Harry published his memoir, Spare.

In these, the couple made a number of allegations about their time within the royal family, which led to already stretched tensions with Harry's relatives becoming even more strained.

Since the Sussex's return to the UK, their children have attended two schools following concerns raised by their security team about the journey to the classroom at the first chosen.

Prince Harry has been busy since his return

Meghan has also given fans of the couple an insight into their lives back in the UK with an adorable social media video showing walks in the woods, Harry fishing with his children, Meghan jumping in puddles and Lilibet riding her bike while Archie runs along beside her.

The duke also made a public appearance at the WellChild Awards on Monday, a cause close to his heart and an event he has attended every year even after his relocation to the US.

During his time away from the UK, especially in recent months, Harry has had several meetings with his father, King Charles, but he is not believed to have reunited with his estranged brother, Prince William, or spoken to him since his return.

Meghan and Harry are back in the UK(Image: Getty Images)

The Prince and Princess of Wales are said to be keen to avoid any "drama" surrounding the return of Harry and Meghan. Tensions between the two couples, who were once known as the Fab Four, are thought to have started some time before the Duke of Duchess of Sussex made the decision to quit the UK in 2020.

In his book William and Catherine: The Intimate Inside Story, Mirror royal editor Russell Myers explains they were visible as early as 2019 when Meghan and Harry went on a royal tour of South Africa.

While they were there the couple filmed a documentary with ITV's Tom Bradby about the visit, during which Meghan told the interviewer she had been finding things difficult, while Harry revealed that he and his brother were on "different paths".

The Prince and Princess of Wales have visited the Isle of Bute (Image: Ben Birchall/PA Wire)

According to Myers, the programme confirmed something in William's mind, demonstrating to the future king and queen just how significantly matters had changed between themselves and Harry. William also felt Harry had become "paranoid" - despite his own attempts to support him.

"He [William] felt as though somewhere down the line, perhaps it is impossible to even say when, that he lost his brother," a source close to William told to Myers.

"He became paranoid, angry, obsessive and firmly rooted in the past. There's no doubt, at that time at least, there was a lot of love and support available for Harry."

It has been extensively documented — and acknowledged by Harry himself — that William cautioned his younger brother to proceed carefully with Meghan.
