Bench Cam: The best of Arteta at Ipswich

TheTools · 16.09.2026 22:48 · source · permalink

We extended our winning start to the season with a 4-2 win over Ipswich Town in the Carabao Cup, and you can see how Mikel Arteta and his coaching staff reacted to the best moments of the game with Bench Cam.

Goals from Max Dowman and Noni Madueke put us 2-0 up within 16 minutes, before another strike from Dowman and a brilliant finish from Mikel Merino made it four after half time. 

While the Tractor Boys became the first side to put two past us this season with a couple of late goals, we comfortably saw out the tie to book our spot in Round Four.
