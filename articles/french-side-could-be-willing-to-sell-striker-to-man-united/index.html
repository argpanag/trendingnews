French side could be willing to sell striker to Man United

TheTools · 16.11.2026 21:50 · source · permalink

Mauricio Pochettino will take charge of his first match in charge of Manchester United when they visit Liverpool on Sunday.

The Argentine was named as the Red Devils’ new head coach last week, after Michael Carrick was sacked by those in charge of the club.

Carrick had overseen an impressive second half of last term after being appointed as caretaker manager midway through the campaign, but this season had not gone to plan so far.

After ten Premier League games, United sit in the Premier League relegation zone and the club’s bosses have acted to appoint former Tottenham Hotspur and Chelsea boss Pochettino.

Ahead of their visit to Anfield, it has emerged that Pochettino could be reunited with one of his former players in Manchester.

As per Football Insider, French side Monaco could be willing to sell striker Folarin Balogun in January, with Pochettino said to be a ‘big fan’ of the player.

Pochettino worked with Balogun during his time as manager of the United States mens national team, which ended when he agreed to take charge of United last week.

Balogun, 25, joined Monaco from Premier League side Arsenal in 2023 and has since made a total of 98 appearances for the club in all competitions, scoring 32 goals and registering 13 assists.

The England-born striker has also been a regular for the USMNT over the past couple of years, and scored three times for Pochettino’s side at the World Cup in North America earlier this year.

The report suggests that the forward would be open to the idea of a move to Old Trafford, while the Ligue 1 outfit are also open to the prospect of a sale as they need to raise funds.

Balogun’s current contract with Monaco is set to run until the end of the 2027/28 season.
