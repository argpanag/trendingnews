Tottenham Hotspur remain interested in move for £100m-rated forward

Tottenham Explore Eli Junior Kroupi Deal as Bournemouth Face January Transfer Test

Tottenham Hotspur are exploring what would be one of the more ambitious moves of the winter window, with AFC Bournemouth forward Eli Junior Kroupi emerging as a serious target amid growing concern over Spurs’ attacking output.

The north London side have underperformed in front of goal this season and that has sharpened focus on the centre-forward department. Roberto De Zerbi has limited options available. Dominic Solanke has yet to properly establish himself since arriving in 2024, Omar Marmoush has not found rhythm since joining on loan from Manchester City, and Richarlison remains out of the picture after his breakdown in relations with the head coach.

OneFootball Videos

Those issues help explain why Tottenham are looking closely at Kroupi, a player understood to be highly regarded internally and viewed as a strong fit for the profile De Zerbi wants. Credit to TeamTalk, who report that Spurs are even considering the structure of a swap arrangement to improve their chances of landing the Bournemouth attacker.

Spurs striker search gathers pace

Tottenham’s summer outlay exceeded £300 million, yet the results have not followed with any consistency. Three wins from the opening stretch, alongside four defeats and three draws, underline why further recruitment is already being discussed. A recent defeat to Leeds United, after a short run of encouragement, reinforced the sense that more is needed at the top end of the pitch.

Kroupi has been on Tottenham’s radar for some time. Interest dates back to the last window, although injury complicated the situation then. Having returned from a foot problem and made three league appearances, he is back in focus as Spurs assess whether a fresh approach can be made.

Photo: IMAGO

Wilson Odobert has been mentioned as a potential makeweight, although his recovery from an ACL injury may weaken that angle from Bournemouth’s side. Mikey Moore and Luca Williams-Barnett are also among the names said to have entered the conversation, which suggests Tottenham are examining a number of creative structures rather than a straightforward cash deal.

Bournemouth stance on Eli Junior Kroupi

Any negotiation would be difficult. Bournemouth are in a strong position competitively and contractually. Kroupi is tied down until 2030, the club are pushing for an impressive league season, and they remain hopeful of making progress in the Europa League. There is little incentive for them to sell in January unless terms become exceptional.

That is reflected in the valuation previously attached to the player, with £100 million cited as the level required. For Tottenham, after such substantial summer spending, that would represent another enormous commitment. It is also possible Bournemouth would prefer to shut the door entirely this winter and revisit the situation later, particularly if the player is open to waiting until the summer.

The wider market may also become a factor. Kroupi is admired beyond Spurs and Bournemouth know they are dealing with an asset whose value could yet rise again.

Kroupi profile suits De Zerbi plan

Although he has not yet hit top scoring form this season, Kroupi’s body of work remains compelling. He scored 13 Premier League goals last term and built a reputation as one of the division’s most gifted young attackers. Internal and external praise has followed. Danny Murphy said, “He’s an accomplished finisher already. He has just come in and it seems like he already knows what his role is in the team.”

There is also previous testimony from Bournemouth over the qualities he brings, from movement around the box to work-rate and technical ability. Tottenham’s interest therefore makes sense at a football level. The bigger question is whether it makes sense financially and politically in the middle of a season when Bournemouth have every reason to resist.

For now, this looks like a situation worth monitoring rather than one close to resolution. Tottenham clearly admire Eli Junior Kroupi, but admiration and deal-making are very different things, especially in January.

Our View

This is exactly the sort of move that would show real ambition. Eli Junior Kroupi feels like a player with genuine upside, someone who could give this attack freshness, energy and a cutting edge that has been missing too often. There is enough evidence already to suggest he could thrive in a side that creates chances regularly, and if De Zerbi truly sees him as a major fit, that matters.

The concern is obvious, Bournemouth hold the cards. Mid-season deals between Premier League clubs are hard enough before a £100 million valuation enters the picture. Spurs would need to be clever, probably brave as well, if they are going to make any progress. Including a player or two in discussions might help, but only if Bournemouth see immediate value in what is being offered.

Even so, this feels like the right kind of target. Young, high ceiling, already proven in the league, and capable of improving further. Tottenham cannot keep drifting through windows hoping the current options suddenly click into gear. More competition is needed, more goals are needed, and more threat is needed.

If a January move proves impossible, laying the groundwork now would still be worthwhile. Get ahead of the competition, build the relationship, and be ready. If Spurs are serious about climbing the table and backing this manager properly, Kroupi looks like one worth pushing for.
