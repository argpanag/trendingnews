Ridley Scott & Leonardo DiCaprio's R-Rated Spy Thriller Is Coming to Netflix in 1 Week

TheTools · 28.11.2026 01:00 · source · permalink

Ridley Scott and Leonardo DiCaprio's 2008 spy thriller Body of Lies will soon be making its way to a new streaming home. The film lands on Netflix at the start of December.

The full list of movies and television coming to Netflix in December reveals that Body of Lies will be added to the streaming giant on Dec. 1, 2026 along with several other blockbuster films like Edward Scissorhands, Now You See Me and Shrek. Scott directed and produced the thriller film, which saw DiCaprio starring alongside Russell Crowe and Golshifteh Farahani in leading roles. It tells the daring story of a CIA agent who launches a plot to capture an elusive terrorist leader, Al-Saleem.

Leonardo DiCaprio's Spy Thriller Body of Lies Heads to Netflix Dec. 1

DiCaprio stars as the bold CIA agent Roger Ferris who plots to finally capture the leader of the terrorist group. Along the way, he gets the help of his boss and the CIA's Chief of Near East Division (Crowe), and they create a fake terrorist organization to prompt Al-Saleem out of hiding. They must also keep everything a secret from another intelligence officer, Hani (Mark Strong), with lives hanging in the balance.

Upon its release in 2008, Body of Lies was a surprisingly mixed bag - which was surprising given both Scott and DiCaprio's status in Hollywood. The film received mixed reviews from critics and stumbled to find its footing at the box office, grossing only $119 million worldwide against an estimated $70 million budget.

Despite the mixed reviews, critics still praised Scott's direction and his typically strong visual aesthetics. DiCaprio and Crowe's performances were also deemed a standout, but the film's story and reliance on overused spy tropes were what held it back, often deemed as formulaic or derivative.

Nearly twenty years later, Body of Lies is still labeled 'Rotten' on Rotten Tomatoes with a 54% approval rating from 205 reviews. The site's consensus echoes how the film relies on Crowe and DiCaprio to "elevate it beyond the conventional espionage thriller."

While it may not have been a huge hit in 2008, the film's arrival on Netflix does give it a further shot at redemption. Audience reviews have been much more forgiving of the film, and it has a 62% Popcornmeter from over 100,000 user reviews. A recent audience review from October 2026 gave it 5 stars and called it "an underrated movie with great performances," adding that its "gripping and compelling."

Body of Lies Shares Similarities With Another Mixed-Bag Ridley Scott Drama

Body of Lies isn't the first time Ridley Scott ventured out of his typical wheelhouse to tell a story showing conflict between Western and Arab civilizations. In 2005, he released Kingdom of Heaven with Edward Norton, Orlando Bloom and Liam Neeson — telling the story of the Third Crusade and focusing on the fight to defend the Kingdom of Jerusalem.

Body of Lies examines similar themes, albeit in the context of more modern operations and terrorism. Still, both films examined the conflict and power struggles along with intense moral choices across their respective eras, and they also have another common denominator: Scott's pairing with screenwriter William Monahan. Similar to Body of Lies, Kingdom of Heaven also received generally mixed reviews when it was released — though a director's cut that followed was released to much more positive feedback.

Ridley Scott's Body of Lies will begin streaming on Netflix on Dec. 1, 2026.
