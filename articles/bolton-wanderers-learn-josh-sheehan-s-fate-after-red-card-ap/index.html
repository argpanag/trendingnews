Bolton Wanderers learn Josh Sheehan's fate after red card appeals to FA

TheTools · 08.09.2026 18:35 · source · permalink

JOSH Sheehan will be available for Wanderers’ home game against West Ham United tonight.

The Football Association upheld Bolton’s appeal against the Welsh midfielder’s red card at Millwall, which would have carried a three-game suspension for violent conduct.

Millwall’s Josh Metcalfe, who was also sent off by referee Will Finnie, has also been cleared of any wrongdoing by a Football Association disciplinary panel.

Wanderers will be without defenders Luca Stephenson, Akin Famewo and Lewis Brunt at the Toughsheet Stadium, with Schumacher also considering other changes to the team after Saturday’s 4-0 defeat at The Den.

The Wanderers boss believes his side have shown in their opening five games that they can compete in the Championship but admits a run of three successive defeats have shown how readily mistakes are punished at this level.

“It is more ruthless, less forgiving,” he said.

“The opponents that you come up against, they are more likely to punish any mistake that you make and then Richie (Kyle) tells me, when you step into the Premier League it's even worse.

“That is what it's all about, you can feel as though you're doing really well in a game and all of a sudden you come off with a 4-0 scoreline and you think, how did that happen?

“But we've got to look internally at that as well, what can we do about it, what can we fix, what can we try and improve on and for me it's being more ruthless in the moments that we have.”

“When we have big spells of pressure like the ones against Lincoln where we battered the goal and the first goal changes the game.

“In the first half, if you remember, we were really strong, Sam Dalby hit the post, then against Sheffield United in the second half at 2-2, we should have scored, Michael Cooper makes three or four really good saves, and then at 1-0 down in the second half against Millwall we have three or four really big moments that if we score and we get the equaliser, again the game changes.

“They are small margins and as I've just said, it's unforgiving, this division.”
