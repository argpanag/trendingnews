The Victoria's Secret Fantasy Bra is set to return to the brand's 2026 fashion show on Oct. 18

This year's Fantasy Bra is created in partnership with JACOB & CO. jewelers and promises to "make fashion history"

The extravagant item, known for its staggering price tag, debuted in 1996 and last appeared on the runway in 2018

The 2026 Victoria's Secret Fashion Show is bringing back its most iconic item.

On Oct. 15, Victoria's Secret confirmed that the iconic Fantasy Bra would return for this year's show, taking place on Oct. 18. Created in partnership with JACOB & CO. jewelers, the sparkling bra promises to "make fashion history," per the brand's post on Instagram.

In another video shared to Instagram, Jeweler Jacob Arabo says that he created a rectangular angel-cut stone to be featured on the 2026 Fantasy Bra, as well as a variety of other gemstone shapes.

"When the model's actually walking, the movement and everything that's moving on her, this is gonna create a lot of attention and a lot of beauty and a lot of sparkle," Arabo teases.

He also adds, "I wanna make this [the] most expensive bra ever made."

The Fantasy Bra, known for its dazzling gemstones and staggering monetary value, has a long history of wowing fans. Dubbed the Million-Dollar Miracle bra, it debuted in 1996 and was only featured on Claudia Schiffer in the catalogue and on billboards, not at the show.

Claudia Schiffer in 1996
Credit: Evan Agostini/Liaison

The bra, which came with a $1 million price tag, was decked out in more than 100 carats of diamonds as well as a variety of other semi-precious gemstones. It was designed by four New York City-based private jewelers.

Over the years, the Fantasy Bra has only gotten more extravagant. In 1997, Tyra Banks walked the runway in the $3 million Diamond Dream Bra, which was designed by Harry Winston and featured 100 diamonds on the sweetheart neckline of a Miracle Bra and a 42-carat pear-shaped diamond focal point.

Tyra Banks in 1997
Credit: Ron Galella/Ron Galella Collection via Getty

In 2003, Heidi Klum wore the Fantasy Bra for the third time, this one the whopping $11 million Very Sexy Fantasy Bra. The piece took 370 hours to create and featured a 70-carat diamond in the center, the second largest in the world.

Heidi Klum in 2003
Credit: Frank Micelotta/Getty

The most expensive Fantasy Bra ever made debuted in 2000. The "Red Hot Fantasy Bra," modeled by Gisele Bündchen, came with a price tag of $15 million thanks to 1,300 gemstones — including 300 carats of Thai rubies.

The 2000 bra was not featured in the show but included in the catalogue and could only be purchased via cashier's check. The sparkling lingerie held the Guinness World Record as the most expensive piece of lingerie ever created until 2006.

Gisele Bündchen in 2000
Credit: DMIPhoto/FilmMagic

In 2014, Victoria's Secret introduced two "Dream Angels" Fantasy Bras. Designed by Mouawad in 2014, they included two $2 million sapphire, ruby and diamond sets, worn by Alessandra Ambrosio and Adriana Lima. Each lingerie set contained 16,000 perfect gems, accompanied by 18-karat-gold-strung jeweled body pieces, which took more than 1,380 hours to create.

Adriana Lima and Alessandra Ambrosio in 2014
Credit: Michael Stewart/FilmMagic

"Words can't describe how excited I was when I received a call from Victoria's Secret to let me know that I would be wearing it," Ambrosio told PEOPLE.

Lima — who had worn the Fantasy Bra twice previously — told PEOPLE: "This year was even more special because for the first time two of us would be wearing it and the other Angel was my close friend Alessandra. I was super excited. It's so great to be able to share the honor with such a good friend!"

The most recent Fantasy Bra went down the runway on Elsa Hosk in 2018. The sparkling bralette and matching body chain were valued at $1 million and adorned with more than 2,100 Swarovski Created Diamonds and responsibly sourced topaz set in sterling silver. The set took upwards of 930 hours to create and contained more than 71 carats within the center piece.

Elsa Hosk in 2018
Credit: J. Lee/FilmMagic

Speaking to PEOPLE that year, Elsa Hosk said the honor of wearing the 2018 Dream Angels Fantasy Bra was "beyond exciting."

"It was just a sensation that I've never felt before," Hosk told PEOPLE of the first time she tried on the bra, after being surprised during her VS fashion show fitting. "My body was violently shaking. And it wasn't because I was nervous, I was just excited. It was a cool feeling that I've never felt before. And it was definitely a moment that I'll never forget."
