Half Yours escapes serious injury, but Cup now unlikely

TheTools · 18.10.2026 22:54 · source · permalink

Andrew Eddy

Last year’s Cups champion Half Yours has emerged from his heavy Caulfield Cup fall without any serious concerns, but the Melbourne Cup has been all but ruled out.

Half Yours came down in the home straight at Caulfield on Saturday but emerged from his box on Sunday morning a bit sore and sorry but seemingly without any lingering physical worries, according to co-trainer Tony McEvoy.

Half Yours' rider Mark Zahra and Ohope Wins' jockey Jye McNeil both escaped serious injury with the Victorian Jockeys Association confirming on Sunday morning that Zahra was discharged from hospital on Saturday night, while McNeil is to be discharged on Sunday morning. 

Half Yours has had to be treated for his injuries and therefore would be unable to properly prepare for the Melbourne Cup.

“No decision at the moment but he won’t be able to work for 10 days so I think our campaign is over,” McEvoy said on Sunday morning.

“So, as disappointing as that is, we are still overjoyed that he seems OK.

“We are very lucky. He had four staples in a near foreleg and four staples put in an off-hind leg and he had a laceration under his chest, but all pretty superficial stuff in the scheme of things.

“He had a dipper of feed overnight and he’s out now having a pick now and he seems pretty good, so we’re lucky, very lucky.

“We are just going to get him well. I’d say his preparation is over but that is not a decision, that’s just a feeling. How can you bounce back after that and run in a Melbourne Cup?”

McEvoy said he hoped the horror fall, that claimed the life of Yulong mare Ohope Wins, would not worry his horse in future racing campaigns.

“Over the years I have been involved in horses that have fallen and it effects some and not others, but he’s a pretty tough horse, so I think he’ll be fine.”
