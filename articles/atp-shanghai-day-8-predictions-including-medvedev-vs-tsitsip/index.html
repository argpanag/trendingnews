ATP Shanghai Day 8 Predictions Including Medvedev vs Tsitsipas

TheTools · 13.10.2026 17:17 · source · permalink

Day 8 at the Rolex Shanghai Masters 2026 edition sees the remaining Round of 16 matches on schedule. It promises to be a fantastic day of tennis as we have a highly talented mix of players fighting for the quarterfinals. As ever, we preview and predict all the scheduled matches for you.

ATP Shanghai Day 8 Predictions

Rafael Jodar vs Botic van de Zandschulp

Head-to-head – First Meeting

Rafael Jodar produced an impressive comeback win over Jiri Lehecka, showing plenty of confidence and composure under pressure. Botic van de Zandschulp also arrives in good form after upsetting Arthur Fils, but his level can fluctuate from match to match. Jodar is more dynamic from the baseline and should be able to use his movement and aggressive shot-making to dictate the exchanges. Expect a tight contest, but the Spaniard’s energy and attacking game could see him through.
Prediction: Jodar in 3

Zizou Bergs vs Tommy Paul

Head-to-head – Bergs 0-1 Paul

Tommy Paul looked particularly sharp in his straight-sets win over defending champion Valentin Vacherot, controlling the match with his consistency from the baseline. Zizou Bergs arrives with plenty of confidence after pulling off a major upset against Carlos Alcaraz, but repeating that level will be a challenge. Paul is the more established player at this stage and has the movement and shot tolerance to neutralise Bergs’ aggression. Expect the Belgian to put up a fight, but Paul should have the edge in the longer exchanges.
Prediction: Paul in 2

Felix Auger-Aliassime vs Casper Ruud

Head-to-head – Auger-Aliassime 5-3 Ruud

Casper Ruud has looked solid in Shanghai, but Felix Auger-Aliassime is a dangerous opponent on these quicker hard courts. The Canadian had to battle through three sets against Pablo Carreno Busta, while Ruud secured a straight-sets win over Learner Tien. The head-to-head favors Auger-Aliassime, whose serve and forehand should allow him to dictate the shorter rallies. Ruud’s baseline consistency will make this competitive, but the Canadian looks better equipped to take control.
Prediction: Auger-Aliassime in 3

Daniil Medvedev vs Stefanos Tsitsipas

Head-to-head – Medvedev 10-5 Tsitsipas

Daniil Medvedev and Stefanos Tsitsipas renew their familiar rivalry after both produced impressive wins to reach this stage. Medvedev comfortably handled Dalibor Svrcina, while Tsitsipas came from a set down to eliminate Taylor Fritz, which should give the Greek plenty of confidence. Medvedev’s deep court positioning has been less effective at times, and Tsitsipas could take advantage if he maintains an aggressive approach. With both players striking the ball well, this should be a tight contest, but Tsitsipas has the momentum to edge it.
Prediction: Tsitsipas in 3
