Nartatez lauds PNP personnel for PH’s successful 2026 ASEAN hosting

TheTools · 18.11.2026 11:58 · source · permalink

NATIONAL

by

Priam Nepomuceno

Nov 18, 2026

7:58 PM GMT+8

PNP chief Gen. Jose Melencio Nartatez Jr. (PNA file photo by Lloyd Caliwan) 

MANILA – Philippine National Police (PNP) chief Gen. Jose Melencio Nartatez Jr. on Wednesday commended all police commanders and personnel who helped ensure the security and peace and order during the 49th ASEAN Summit and Related Summits.


“Despite some challenges, we were able to deliver all the commitments that we made to ensure the success of this important international event. I commend all our personnel for their sacrifices, dedication, and hard work to provide the peace of mind that all ASEAN Summit delegates fully deserve while in the country,” he said in a statement.


The PNP said over 23,000 police officers were tapped when red alert was raised over Metro Manila and two nearby regions during the country’s hosting of the annual regional bloc event. 


More than 630 meetings were held during the Philippines’ hosting of the ASEAN Summit since last year, and Nartatez said all of them were completed successfully and without disruption.


He said the PNP would not have successfully done their job without the help of all government agencies and law enforcement agencies.


He said the experience should serve as a reminder for police personnel to apply the same level of dedication in carrying out their daily responsibilities in communities across the country.


“To every police officer who stood on the frontlines and sacrificed precious time with your families, you have my deepest gratitude and respect. Your sacrifices may not always be seen, but they are felt in every Filipino's sense of safety and in the confidence of our international guests,” Nartatez said. (Priam Nepomuceno with a report from Lloyd Caliwan/PNA)
