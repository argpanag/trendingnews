Nino Schurter suffers a serious crash on a scooter and is taken to the hospital by helicopter

TheTools · 08.09.2026 10:53 · source · permalink

"Retirement Is Dangerous"

Nino Schurter broke his arm while riding a mountain scooter.

instagram/nschurter

A nasty fall instead of a leisurely ride: Mountain bike legend Nino Schurter crashes while riding a mountain scooter downhill and injures his arm. The 40-year-old checks in from the hospital.

Nino Schurter had actually planned to spend a relaxing day in the mountains. But a hike—which included picking blueberries—ended with the former mountain bike star in the hospital.

As the Graubünden native reports on Instagram, he took a serious fall on a mountain scooter during the subsequent descent. “I experienced a level of pain I didn’t even know existed,” Schurter writes.

The reason for the severe pain: His arm is not only dislocated but also broken. After the accident, Schurter had to be flown to the hospital by helicopter.

"I'll be out of commission for a few weeks"

Despite his serious injury, Schurter is relieved that the accident did not turn out even worse. He thanks his loved ones, as well as the emergency responders and doctors, for their care.

The 40-year-old is allowed to leave the hospital late that evening. Now, however, patience is called for. “I’ll be out of commission for a few weeks while everything heals,” Schurter writes.

Despite the pain, the ten-time cross-country world champion hasn't lost his sense of humor. At the end of his post, he jokes: "Retirement is proving to be dangerous."
