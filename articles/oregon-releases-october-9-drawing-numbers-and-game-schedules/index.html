Oregon Releases October 9 Drawing Numbers and Game Schedules

TheTools · 11.10.2026 06:21 · source · permalink

Oregon's published drawing results for October 9 include five numbers and a Mega Ball result, while available reporting leaves the corresponding Pick 4 numbers unresolved. Suara Garut published the update on October 11, 2026, citing results data attributed to Yahoo and outlining schedules for several Oregon drawing games.

According to the report, the October 9 results were 02-07-16-63-70, with Mega Ball 08. The figures appeared in a results report dated October 10, providing the latest drawing information included in the article.

The report did not provide corresponding Oregon Pick 4 results for October 9. It also noted that the available earlier coverage had not established the Pick 4 results for October 8, leaving both dates without confirmed numbers in the cited material.

Suara Garut's schedule information lists four daily Pick 4 drawing times in Oregon: 1 p.m., 4 p.m., 7 p.m., and 10 p.m. These times establish the published schedule but do not supply the missing results for either date.

The report also identifies schedules for other drawing games in Oregon. According to the published information, Powerball drawings take place at 7:59 p.m. on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Saturdays.

Mega Millions drawings are scheduled for 7:59 p.m. on Tuesdays and Fridays, according to the same report. Win for Life drawings take place at 7:30 p.m. on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Saturdays, while Megabucks drawings are scheduled for 7:29 p.m. on those same days.

The article also references separate North Carolina results published by Star News Online on October 5, covering drawings held October 4. Those results provide additional regional drawing information but do not resolve the missing Oregon Pick 4 figures.

According to Star News Online, North Carolina's October 4 Pick 3 daytime result was 7-5-9 with Fireball 0, while the evening result was 0-4-1 with Fireball 2. The report also listed the daytime Pick 4 result as 9-1-9-2 with Fireball 7 and the evening result as 6-3-4-6 with Fireball 2.

Star News Online listed North Carolina Pick 3 and Pick 4 drawings at 3 p.m. and 11:22 p.m. daily. Its schedule also placed Cash 5 drawings at 11:22 p.m. daily and Millionaire for Life drawings at 11:15 p.m. daily.

Suara Garut stated that its Oregon results pages were automatically generated using TinBu information and an editorial template reviewed by an Oregon editor. The report attributed its October 9 results information to data published by Yahoo.
