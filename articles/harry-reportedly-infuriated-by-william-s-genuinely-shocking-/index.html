Harry Reportedly 'Infuriated' by William's 'Genuinely Shocking' Behavior as He Feels His Brother Wants Him 'Banished'

Whatever Prince Harry expected when he moved back to the UK, it probably wasn’t that there would be no change to his relationship with his brother, even now that they are much closer. Harry has, after all, managed to somewhat repair his relationship with his father, King Charles, and even Queen Camilla, and has expressed publicly his desire to reconcile with Prince William.

However, William isn’t budging. “William’s acting like Harry and Meghan don’t exist,” a source told RadarOnline. “Ignoring Harry is his way of handling what he considers to be a wretchedly unpleasant situation.”

And Harry is not happy. “It’s infuriating to him,” insiders said, adding that Harry has reached out to his brother to try to set up a meeting, without getting an answer. And that, according to sources, might end up escalating things.

“Harry’s known to be impulsive, and the more wound up he gets, the more likely he is to follow through on threats to make William regret this.”

William is reportedly unwilling to reconcile with his brother, despite the fact that Harry has repeatedly apologized for making their problems public. “He insists he’s tried to find every possible way to say that he’s sorry,” the source said. “He’s genuinely shocked that there’s not even been a shred of forgiveness on William’s part.”

And the position from William is even more surprising considering everyone seems to be pressuring him to let bygones be bygones. Including Charles. “The king has given the orders for this feud to be squashed, but as everyone knows William is refusing to come to the table,” the source explained.

What’s the use of being the king if your sons still won’t listen to you?

“William is openly going against the king’s wishes by acting as though Harry and Meghan are banished,” the insider added, “when they’re clearly right at his doorstep.”

And Kate Middleton has also been urging her husband to reconcile with his brother. “Kate is desperate to find a compromise that will end this ugliness,” the source explained. “She wants peace and hates the fact that the monarchy is suffering. She’s also saddened to see her husband at war with his own flesh and blood. After all, they used to be so close.”

Basically, at this point, everyone is pushing for reconciliation, including courtiers. “They fear the Sussexes could make things seriously ugly if tensions are allowed to continue,” the source added.

Will it happen? No one seems to be sure, but the source did make one thing clear: no one would be more upset about this rift than Princess Diana. “She would be devastated,” the source said. “There’s no question.”
