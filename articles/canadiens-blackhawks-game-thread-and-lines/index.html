Canadiens @ Blackhawks: Game thread and lines

TheTools · 23.10.2026 23:00 · source · permalink

A four-game road trip begins in Chicago with a major promotion for Oliver Kapanen.

How to watch

Game 10: Montreal Canadiens @ Chicago Blackhawks

Start time: **8:00 PM EDT / 5:00 PM PDT**
In the Canadiens region: TSN2 (English), RDS (French)
In the Blackhawks region: CHSN
In the United States: NHL Network
Streaming: TSN+, RDS

With Nick Suzuki out, Martin St-Louis has tried to make as few alterations to the lineup as possible to maintain the chemistry that has been building up for nine games. Oliver Kapanen rises up from the fourth line to centre Cole Caufield and Juraj Slafkovský, and Florian Xhekaj will slide into the vacated spot on the fourth-line wing in his return from injury.

The Canadiens now have extra motivation as they seek a rebound from their latest blown two-goal lead and loss on Saturday. The players have grown tired of spoiling so many of their own results, letting their frustration show after the game, so we will find out how that manifests in the tonight’s game.

Chicago is looking for a bounceback of its own after getting shut out in its previous game to end a sequence of consecutive victories. The Blackhawks may not be at the same level as the Habs, but they will have the most skilled player in the game in Connor Bedard. We saw what one high-level player can achieve on Saturday when the Habs handed Macklin Celebrini too many opportunities to burn them. Things need to be a lot cleaner for the Canadiens during the next few weeks to allow their solid possession game to make up for the loss of their top point-producer.
