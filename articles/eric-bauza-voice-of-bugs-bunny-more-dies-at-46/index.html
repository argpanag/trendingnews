Eric Bauza, Voice of Bugs Bunny & More, Dies at 46

TheTools · 26.10.2026 03:23 · source · permalink

Eric Bauza's family shared on Instagram that Bauza has tragically passed away at the age of 46. While no specific details about his passing were offered, including a cause of death, his account honored his Emmy-winning legacy after voicing some of the most iconic cartoon characters in history over the last 25 years:

"From the Family of Eric Bauza: Eric Bauza, Emmy Award-winning voice actor and the modern voice of iconic Looney Tunes characters Bugs Bunny and Daffy Duck, along with dozens of other beloved cartoon characters, has passed away suddenly. More details to come."

"From the Family of Eric Bauza: Eric Bauza, Emmy Award-winning voice actor and the modern voice of iconic Looney Tunes characters Bugs Bunny and Daffy Duck, along with dozens of other beloved cartoon characters, has passed away suddenly. More details to come."

Bauza first came onto the scene in 2001 and amassed an astonishing 421 acting credits over the next 25 years, establishing himself as one of Hollywood's biggest voice-over stars. Most notably, he became the eighth actor to voice Bugs Bunny of Looney Tunes, one of the most popular animated characters in history, in 2018 and continued playing the role until 2026. His biggest credits in this role include 2021's Space Jam: A New Legacy and the 2024 video game Multiversus.

Bugs Bunny is far from the only Looney Tunes character for which Bauza provided his voice, as he was credited for playing over a dozen roles from this iconic franchise. That long list includes Daffy Duck, Porky Pig, Tweety, Sylvester, Marvin the Marian, Yosemite Sam, Elmer Fudd, Foghorn Leghorn, Pepé Le Pew, Speedy Gonzales, the Tasmanian Devil, and other supporting roles. The final time he played these roles was in 2026's Coyote vs. Acme, which became one of the year's biggest surprise hits (grossing over $126 million worldwide).
