Mads Pedersen out of Vuelta - Canadian Cycling Magazine

TheTools · 02.09.2026 12:21 · source · permalink

Lidl-Trek announced that the Dane will not start Stage 11 of La Vuelta a España

Green jersey from the Tour de France, Mads Pedersen, hasn’t had the same run at La Vuelta a España, and now it makes sense.

It seems like the former world champion has been battling sickness.

On Wednesday, Lidl-Trek posted the news.

“Unfortunately, Mads Pedersen will not take the start of today’s Stage 11 of La Vuelta.
After battling illness over the past few days, Mads will now focus on making a full recovery before turning his attention to his remaining targets this season,” the post read.

Pedersen is part of a strong Danish squad for the world championships–although one that didn’t include the national champion, Magnus Cort.

On Tuesday, he admitted he hadn’t been having the best Vuelta, but he thought the form could come around–especially with the Montreal worlds looming.

“I want to continue in the race. But at some point it has to make sense. As far as I’m concerned, I’m not heading home. I think things will turn around, and there’s still a long way to Granada. I still think it’s good preparation for the world championships,” he said, as reported by Feltet.dk.

As far as his form, he wasn’t quite sure what was up–especially given a strong season, including a stage win, multiple long breaks and the green jersey at the Tour.

But after Stage 10, when he was dropped, he said he too was confused.

“I don’t really know what to say. The level is not there now. It is just not good enough. Something is clearly not right,” he said to Cyclingnews. “Now is the time to really put our finger on the sore spot, instead of in a few days. We are now going back to the table to see what is best.”

Stage 11 is probably for the sprinters–although there is a category-3 climb towards the end. But as long as the fastmen can catch back up, and there’s no break, expect a big ole bunch gallop.
