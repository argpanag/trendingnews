Is Instagram down right now? Why is instagram not working - AOL

TheTools · 21.09.2026 10:35 · source · permalink

If you're trying to use social media Sunday night, you could be having issues. Both Facebook and Instagram are down Sunday night.

Downdetector shows Instagram, the photo sharing social media site has more than 5000 reports of issues, and it's also trending on Google.

The site shows that shortly before 9:30 P.m. EST, there were more than 5,600 reports of issues with Instagram. The majority of the problems, nearly 60 percent, are with the website.

Facebook also has a lot of issues, also mostly with the website.
