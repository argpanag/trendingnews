The Opener: King The Closer, Playoff Kiké, Acuna's Knee Injury

TheTools · 07.10.2026 20:35 · source · permalink

The Brewers can’t seem to win a playoff game away from American Family Field. Milwaukee has dropped 12 straight postseason games on the road, matching the longest streak in playoff history (h/t Adam McCalvy of MLB.com).

1. King keeps Padres alive

With Adrian Morejon ailing and Mason Miller on paternity leave, the Padres turned to Michael King to close out Game 3. The Game 2 starter navigated the final six outs to keep San Diego alive in the NLDS. It was the first time a pitcher secured a six-out save in the postseason two days after making a start, relays Jayson Stark of The Athletic. King ran into some trouble in the ninth inning, walking two to put the tying run in scoring position. Christian Yelich then scorched a grounder right to Xander Bogaerts for a game-ending double play. Walker Buehler will get the ball for the Padres tonight. The Brewers’ starter is TBD.

2. Playoff Kiké does it again

Chris Sale and Yoshinobu Yamamoto cruised through three innings on Tuesday. The Dodgers finally broke the seal in the fourth inning thanks to their secret weapon. Kiké Hernandez launched a Sale changeup into the left field stands to get the Dodgers on the board. Freddie Freeman would soon homer as well. The lead would be more than enough for Yamamoto and company. Hernandez now has 17 home runs in 332 plate appearances in the postseason. “We needed a little bit of that Kiké dust,” manager Dave Roberts said (h/t Fabian Ardaya of The Athletic).

3. Acuna dealing with knee injury

Braves outfielder Ronald Acuna Jr. was limited to DH yesterday because of knee soreness. Manager Walt Weiss told reporters that the injury could keep him from playing right field in Game 4 tonight (h/t Mark Bowman of MLB.com). Acuna was initially in the lineup in right field for Game 3, but was moved to DH before game time. Lane Thomas started in right field, with Mauricio Dubon covering left field and Ha-Seong Kim at shortstop.
