France 2-1 Turkey: Eli Junior Kroupi scores first international goal as hosts qualify top

TheTools · 16.11.2026 01:46 · source · permalink
  • Report (active)
  • Scores
  • Tables
  • Line-ups
  • Match Stats
  • Head-to-head
  • Live Text

Eli Junior Kroupi was also eligible for Portugal but turned down the chance to play for them before the World Cup

Bournemouth forward Eli Junior Kroupi scored his first goal for France as they beat Turkey to seal a place in the Nations League quarter-finals.

The 20-year-old came off the bench for his second cap and slotted in from Rayan Cherki's through ball to put France 2-0 up in Bordeaux.

Zinedine Zidane's side had led through Emirhan Topcu's own goal from a French corner.

Abdulkerim Bardakci headed in Arda Guler's corner in injury time but Turkey could not mount a comeback.

They finish Group A1 bottom with six defeats from six.

France, on the other hand, topped the group - ahead of Belgium, who beat Italy 4-1 to beat the Azzurri to a last-eight spot.
