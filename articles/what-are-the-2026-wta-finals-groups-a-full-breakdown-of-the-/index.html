What are the 2026 WTA Finals groups? A full breakdown of the draw

TheTools · 02.11.2026 15:41 · source · permalink

The draw for the 2026 WTA Finals took shape Tuesday, dividing the eight singles players and eight doubles teams into their round-robin groups in Indian Wells.

With World No. 1 Elena Rybakina's withdrawal, World No. 4 Coco Gauff and No. 2 Aryna Sabalenka lead their respective singles groups at the 2026 WTA Finals, while top-seeded Gabriela Dabrowski and Luisa Stefani, and second-seeded Taylor Townsend and Katerina Siniakova head the doubles groups.

Secure your seats for the WTA Finals

After a season-long race, the world's top players converge in the California desert with the WTA's biggest title still to be won.

Tuesday’s draw set the round-robin groups for the season-ending tournament, which runs Nov. 8-15 at the Indian Wells Tennis Garden. Each player and team will face the other three entrants in their group, with the top two advancing to the semifinals.
