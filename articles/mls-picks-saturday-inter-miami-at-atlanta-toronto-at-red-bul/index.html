MLS Picks Saturday: Inter Miami At Atlanta, Toronto At Red Bulls

TheTools · 15.10.2026 13:00 · source · permalink

In the final stretch of the MLS regular season, Saturday brings Inter Miami to Atlanta, Toronto FC to the Red Bulls, Western leaders Vancouver to Austin and Charlotte to Philadelphia. The best US odds on each game and four picks.

The best bet on Saturday's MLS card comes from the league's best road team. Inter Miami have clinched, Vancouver have clinched, and both are priced as clear favorites, but only one of those prices is generous. Elsewhere, the Red Bulls vs Toronto FC total and a Philadelphia side in form round out the card.

Times are Eastern; odds are the latest available as of October 14. For the latest sportsbook promos, see RG.org.

Atlanta United Vs Inter Miami: Back The Road Team

Saturday, October 17, 7:30 p.m. ET. Inter Miami clinched a playoff spot with a 2-0 win over NYCFC, sit second in the East and lead MLS in away wins (8) and set-piece goals (16). Messi has been involved in a goal in every match he has played since the Leagues Cup. Atlanta are on the verge of elimination and have not beaten Miami at home in the regular season since 2023.

Inter Miami at -118 at LetsBetMD is the pick of the day. A three-way price that short of even money undersells the gap between these teams. The risk is a clinched team easing off.

New York Red Bulls Vs Toronto FC: Goals Expected

Saturday, October 17, 7:30 p.m. ET. The Red Bulls lost 2-1 at D.C. United on Wednesday. Toronto drew 3-3 with Orlando after leading twice.

The Red Bulls at +120 at BetRivers are not a favorite to trust off a loss, and Toronto at +210 at Fanatics is a fair underdog. The play is over 3.5 at -110 at BetRivers, with both teams coming off games that featured three or more goals.

Austin FC Vs Vancouver Whitecaps: Lay The Price

Saturday, October 17, 8:30 p.m. ET. Vancouver lead the West and have clinched, but drew their last two (1-1 at LAFC, 0-0 at St. Louis) and are dealing with a hamstring injury to Thomas Muller. Austin lost 3-1 at LAFC on Wednesday.

Vancouver at -154 at LetsBetMD is the better team at a fair number. Muller's injury is the reason this is a standard-stake play rather than a strong one.

Philadelphia Union Vs Charlotte FC: Ride The Form

Saturday, October 17, 2:30 p.m. ET. Philadelphia won 3-1 at Chicago in midweek; Charlotte lost 3-1 at Columbus and have not yet clinched.

Philadelphia at -175 at BetRivers is fine but not exciting. Charlotte's need for points is the risk.

Seattle Sounders Vs CF Montreal: Skip It

Saturday, October 17, 10:30 p.m. ET. Seattle lost 1-0 at Dallas on Wednesday, and Montreal are close to elimination.

Seattle at -200 at Fanatics and LetsBetMD is too expensive after a loss, and there is no case for Montreal at +485 at LetsBetMD. Pass.
