OneCourt, NHL activating through Ticketmaster sponsorship

TheTools · 06.11.2026 10:38 · source · permalink

Accessibility tech startup OneCourt and the NHL reached a deal to bring the company’s haptic devices for blind and low-vision fans to the league’s tentpole events. The activation, which comes in collaboration with sponsor Ticketmaster, kicked off at last week’s Tim Hortons NHL Heritage Classic and will also bring OneCourt to the Discover NHL Winter Classic, Honda NHL All-Star, and Navy Federal Credit Union NHL Stadium Series.

OneCourt -- the Sports Technology of the Year winner at SBA: Tech in May -- has an in-venue product featuring haptics along with a surface that outlines the field of competition. By combining that device with game tracking data, vibrations turn into low-latency play-by-play for users. For NHL games, OneCourt is ingesting the league’s NHL Edge puck- and player-tracking data.

On top of the league connection, the Avalanche, Blackhawks, Capitals and Red Wings will have OneCourt devices at all of their home games this season. Ticketmaster’s sponsorship has helped OneCourt spread throughout professional sports: nine NBA teams deployed OneCourt with the help of the ticketing giant, along with the previously mentioned NHL clubs.

Recently, OneCourt supported games at the FIFA Men’s World Cup and is activating around select Seattle Seahawks games. The 2026 Super Bowl included a OneCourt deployment as well. OneCourt is also producing an at-home device, with an official launch sometime in December.
