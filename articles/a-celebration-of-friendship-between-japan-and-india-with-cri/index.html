A celebration of friendship between Japan and India with cricket as the backdrop

TheTools · 22.09.2026 14:33 · source · permalink

Japan and India have celebrated 75 years of friendship with an international T20 cricket match as the backdrop, marking a new chapter in sports and diplomacy between the two

SANO, Japan -- Japan and India celebrated 75 years of friendship with an international T20 cricket match as the backdrop on Tuesday, marking a new chapter in sports and diplomacy between the two.

The match was hosted as part of an agreement reached during a summit between Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi and Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi to mark the 75th anniversary of their diplomatic relationship.

It was delayed nearly three hours, first by the late arrival of match officials and the Indian team due to traffic and then by a wet outfield. The match was then reduced to five overs per side as Japan gave the three-time world champions a royal scare before falling two runs short.

India opted to bat and finished 32-4 in its five overs, with skipper Shreyas Iyer scoring 16 off 12 balls. Japan’s Charlie Hara-Hinze, a 17-year-old left-arm spinner, etched his name in the history books by getting Abhishek Sharma out for a duck and also dismissing Iyer.

Axar Patel and Washington Sundar then held their own to defend India’s low score. Skipper Kendel Kadowaki-Fleming scored 12 runs, but was unable to take his side past the finish line as Japan finished 30-3.
