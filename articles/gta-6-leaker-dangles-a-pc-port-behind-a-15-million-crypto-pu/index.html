GTA 6 Leaker Dangles a PC Port Behind a $15 Million Crypto Pump - Decrypt

TheTools · 21.11.2026 18:01 · source · permalink

The anonymous leaker, Cyberleek, released a 209 GB Xbox build but is withholding the executable that could enable a PC port until its CYBERLEEK token hits $15 million.

In brief

  • GTA 6 leaker Cyberleek released what it claims is a near-complete 209 GB Xbox build via torrent but is holding back the main executable until its meme coin reaches a $15 million market cap.
  • The token, CYBERLEEK, trades near a $1.3 million market cap, far short of the goal; a Nov. 23 release is promised regardless.
  • Observers warn of a possible rug pull; none of the files are confirmed genuine, and there’s still no official PC version.

A notorious Grand Theft Auto 6 leaker is holding a potential PC version of the game hostage to a cryptocurrency payday, promising to release the final file needed to make a leaked build playable once its meme coin hits a $15 million market cap.

The leaker, who goes by Cyberleek, has released what it claims is a near-complete 209 GB Xbox build of GTA 6 from November 2026 via torrent, according to a report from PC Gamer.

The one piece withheld is the main executable, the file that would let modders begin emulating the game on PC, potentially bringing it to the platform long before Rockstar Games intends.

Here’s the crypto catch. Cyberleek says it will release the executable on Nov. 23 regardless, but will drop it early if its token, called CYBERLEEK, reaches a $15 million market cap. That’s half the $30 million target the leaker floated in October.

The pressure tactic doesn’t appear to be working: the token is trading around $0.002 as of the weekend, with a market cap of roughly $1.3 million, more than $13 million short of the goal. The coin is down some 20% on the week, but the numbers will fluctuate wildly given the very thin liquidity on a small cap coin.

BTC price on November 22, 4:59PM UTC

The setup has drawn open skepticism that it’s a cash grab, or worse. Observers have warned it could be an attempt to pump the coin before a rug pull, the crypto term for abandoning a token after luring buyers in. Cyberleek has also claimed to hold a PS5 build as a fallback if the Xbox files can’t be made to work.

The episode sits at the increasingly busy intersection of crypto, piracy, and AI. Modern Xbox games are architecturally closer to PC software than PlayStation titles, and AI coding tools have accelerated the kind of decompilation work that makes fast ports plausible, the same dynamic behind a wave of AI-assisted game ports this year.

It also caps a chaotic launch window for GTA that crypto has repeatedly crashed. One trader banked around $66,000 on a GTA 6 meme coin amid ticker confusion, and the game’s disc-free, digital-only release has fueled broader debate, with GameStop’s CEO arguing that digital-only games have hurt consumers.

None of the leaked files have been independently confirmed as genuine, and there is still no official GTA 6 PC version; Rockstar, famously litigious, could respond with legal action rather than a port.
