Pablo Durán puts Celta ahead at the Bernabéu after six minutes

TheTools · 21.11.2026 20:39 · source · permalink

Durán finished into an empty net after a Swedberg run down the left; Real Madrid now have to come back to take the points.

By TuFutbolero Newsroom·November 21, 2026 at 02:39 PM
