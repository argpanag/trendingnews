This combined game thread is for all the marbles — at least for the Atlanta Dream.

The Atlanta Dream (3-3) and the Golden State Valkyries are knotted in the first ever WNBA finals to go seven games. A champion will be crowned tonight.

Just before that, however, the Hawks (3-3) travel to New Orleans to take on the Pelicans (1-4).
