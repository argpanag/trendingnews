The Clippers announced that Max Strus, Yanic Konan Niederhauser, and Brandon Ingram are making progress but will remain in LA to rehab and miss the upcoming road games against Minnesota and Golden State. They are out for at least a week.

The Los Angeles Clippers will remain without Brandon Ingram and several other key players due to injuries, although they are making progress toward potential returns.

The Clippers announced Wednesday that Max Strus, Yanic Konan Niederhauser, and Ingram are progressing in their recoveries but will not join the team for its upcoming road games against Minnesota and Golden State after their home matchup against the Los Angeles Lakers.

All three will remain in Los Angeles to continue their rehabilitation and are ruled out for at least a week.

Brandon Ingram is currently sidelined as he continues to recover from offseason right heel surgery that was performed in May and also repaired his partially torn Achilles tendon. He has engaged in light on-court activities, but is not yet ready to return to action for the LA Clippers.

Max Strus is out with a partial tear of the plantar fascia in his right foot. He sustained this non-contact injury just five minutes into the first quarter of the Clippers' preseason opening game against the Golden State Warriors.

And lastly, Yanic Konan Niederhauser remains out as he rehabilitates from a right Lisfranc surgery he underwent back in March.

Holding a 1-2 record to start their season, the Clippers will host the Los Angeles Lakers this Wednesday.
