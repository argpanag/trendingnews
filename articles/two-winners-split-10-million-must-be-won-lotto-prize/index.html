Two winners split $10 million must-be-won Lotto prize

TheTools · 13.09.2026 00:45 · source · permalink

Two people have taken home $5.03 million each after Saturday's must-be-won Powerball draw.

Powerball First Division was not won, with the winners coming from the Second Division draw.

They won $5.01 million from Powerball and another $13,353 from Lotto Second Division, bringing their total winnings to $5,027,676 each.

Both winning tickets were sold via MyLotto to players from Wellington and Tasman

Two players also won Lotto's First Division, receiving $500,000 from the draw.

One ticket from the First Division was sold at Sweet Lotto Centre in Auckland, while the other was sold online to a customer in Dunedin.

One person from Christchurch won $200,000 from Strike Four, and 28 players collected $13,353 each with Lotto Second Division.

The winning Second Division tickets were sold at the following stores:

- West City Lotto in Auckland

- Woolworths Manukau City Mall in Auckland

- Pak N Save Papakura in Auckland

- Woolworths Pukekohe in Pukekohe

- Two from My Pharmacy Papamoa Plaza in Papamoa

- Dennis Bookshop in Westport

- Four Square West Melton in Christchurch

- New World Halswell in Christchurch

- Fresh Choice Leeston in Leeston

- Nineteen on MyLotto
