Israel says Hezbollah tunnels destroyed in south Lebanon

TheTools · 11.09.2026 09:57 · source · permalink

Getty

Israel has said it demolished key tunnels built by the Iran-backed Hezbollah group beneath the strategic Ali al-Taher ridge in southern Lebanon, with a 4.1 magnitude tremor recorded in the area.

"This is strategic terrorist infrastructure that was built over two decades, with Iranian funding and planning," a joint statement by Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and Defence Minister Israel Katz said.

The destruction "completes the achievement of operational control over the Ali al-Taher ridge, above and below ground", the statement added.

The United States Geological Survey meanwhile reported a 4.1 magnitude tremor in the area, after Lebanon's state-run National News Agency (NNA) reported that Israel carried out a "massive explosion" on the Ali al-Taher ridge in the Nabatieh region, "causing tremors and a powerful rumble".

Journalists in south Lebanon also said they heard "huge explosions" yesterday.

"We were sitting there and everything shook," Joelle Fares, who recently moved to Majayoun, said.

The Israeli army said the Hezbollah infrastructure extended for more than two kilometres and contained "dozens of Iranian-made rockets, missiles, and unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), as well as machine guns, dozens of anti-tank missiles, and hundreds of mines and explosive devices".

The underground compound also included a command centre of Hezbollah's "Badr" unit, the army added.

A military official said some of the militants that had operated in the area of the ridge had abandoned it, while others were "eliminated by aerial strikes and artillery fire".

Israel announced last week that it had captured the Ali al-Taher ridge.

In March, Hezbollah dragged Lebanon into the Middle East war in support of its backer Iran.

There had been a lull in violence after the understanding reached between Iran and the United States in June, followed by an agreement between Lebanon and Israel.

But the intensity of strikes have increased in recent days.

During negotiations, the US, which sponsored the talks, proposed that the Lebanese army take control of the Ali al-Taher ridge, but Hezbollah refused, a source close to the parties said.

Beirut authorities say Israeli strikes have killed more than 4,300 people in total since March, and Israeli forces continue to occupy a so-called "security zone" in the south.
