  10. 'I don't get impression they are going to rip up template'published at 23:09 BST 21 October
    23:09 BST 21 October

    FT: Como 4-1 Manchester United

    Simon Stone
    BBC Sport Manchester United reporter in Como

    It's not easy to see someone like Antonio Conte going into Manchester United and living at peace with the ownership. I can't see that because he has never done it anywhere else, and that's not really worked at Man Utd when you look at people like Jose Mourinho, Louis van Gaal to an extent, but I'm not sure that Manchester United are set up in that way and I don't get the sense that they are going to rip up the template of what they had.

    I think the problem for Manchester United is that again, in the summer of 2024 they had a good think about Erik ten Hag to be their manager, they gave him some money to spend in the transfer window and sacked him in October.

    The following year last summer they did something similar with Ruben Amorim and then sacked him in January, but many people thought he could have gone a long time before that. And now, they are in a similar position again. I don't get the impression that they are going to rip up the template. But you have to look back over three years and think are the decisions being made the right decisions, and it doesn't look like it.

  15. 'Man Utd need to decide who will be in charge against Bournemouth'published at 23:01 BST 21 October
    23:01 BST 21 October

    FT: Como 4-1 Manchester United

    Simon Stone
    BBC Sport Manchester United reporter in Como

    I think Michael Carrick is almost struggling for answers at the moment.

    I don't know if it's the angriest he's been, I think he's been quite forceful in his pre-match press conferences with the media lately, I have been on the wrong end of that. He does it in quite a calm way, not like Sir Alex Ferguson but I think he's trying to be on the front foot and today is just a really bad day.

    I immediately thought when he went to the fans, acknowledging them like he did, hands in the air, four or five times, was a bit like Ole Gunnar Solskjær who did something very similar after a 4-1 defeat to Watford. He admitted that he knew at half-time in that game that his time at Manchester United would come to an end.

    Now, United have flown straight back after the game so presumably there will be a lot of thought going on on that plane as it heads back to Manchester. But, first of all, Manchester United need to decide who will be in charge of that game against Bournemouth on Sunday, and Bournemouth aren't in brilliant form, they won the last game, but they have only won one game this season, and they are playing tomorrow night so Manchester United should be really looking to win that game even though Bournemouth have a fantastic record at Old Trafford.

    Do Manchester United give Michael Carrick that game and see from there, or do they think we need to make a change?
