Sauce Gardner (calf) will not play in Week 7

TheTools · 24.10.2026 16:02 · source · permalink

by Nate Miller | Colts Correspondent | Sat, Oct 24th 2026, 12:02pm EDT

Sauce Gardner (calf) will miss Sunday's game against the Vikings. (Adam Schefter on Twitter)

Fantasy Impact:

Gardner sustained a calf injury in Week 6 and has been missing at practice all week. Justin Walley and Cam Taylor-Britt will likely see increased snaps in Gardner's absence.

Colts HC Shane Steichen ruled out CB Sauce Gardner (calf) and TE Tyler Warren (groin) for Sunday’s game vs. the Vikings.
