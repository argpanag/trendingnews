UNC OL Braces for ‘Big Challenge’ Against Elite Notre Dame DL

TheTools · 29.09.2026 08:10 · source · permalink

Aidan Banfield says UNC’s offensive line faces a major test against Notre Dame on Saturday.

By any metric, one of the nation's best defenses comes to Chapel Hill on Saturday when North Carolina hosts No. 3 Notre Dame. The Fighting Irish rank No. 2 nationally in scoring defense, allowing just 8.2 points per game through a 4-0 start.

Notre Dame's veteran defensive line, under first-year coach Charlie Partridge, has become one of the country's top units. The Irish rank third nationally against the run (53.8 yards per game) and are coming off a six-sack night in a 49-10 rout of Purdue last Saturday.

UNC's offensive line fell to No. 87 in Pro Football Focus' pass-blocking rankings after allowing a season-high 17 pressures at Clemson in Week 3. The Tigers had three pressures against Georgia Southern the week before the Tar Heels arrived and totaled just five at California on Friday.

Tar Heels right guard Aidan Banfield acknowledged the challenge he and his teammates face at home Saturday during his Monday media availability.
