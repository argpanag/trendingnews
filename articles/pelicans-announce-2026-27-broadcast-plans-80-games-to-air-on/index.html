Pelicans announce 2026-27 broadcast plans; 80 games to air on GCSEN

TheTools · 30.09.2026 22:52 · source · permalink

NEW ORLEANS, La. (Acadiana News First) - The New Orleans Pelicans announced their broadcast schedule for the 2026-27 season Wednesday, highlighted by a franchise-record 20 regular-season games airing free over the air locally on WVUE FOX 8.

Through a continuing partnership with Gray Media, 80 regular-season games and all four preseason contests will air across the region on the Gulf Coast Sports & Entertainment Network (GCSEN), with local games simulcast on the team’s direct-to-consumer streaming service, Pelicans+. Amazon Prime will exclusively televise the team’s two European matchups against the San Antonio Spurs in January, while WWL Radio (870 AM / 105.3 FM) will carry all 82 regular-season games.

Coverage tips off Oct. 21 with the regular-season opener against the Indiana Pacers on both FOX 8 and GCSEN, followed by a broadcast lineup featuring returning voices Joel Meyers, Antonio Daniels, and Andrew Lopez, along with newcomers Kelly Crull and Kalia Butler.
