NHL Hometown Remix jersey tier list: Ranking all 32 teams from best to worst

TheTools · 03.11.2026 00:03 · source · permalink

The NHL gave us a gift on Monday — 32 to be exact. In conjunction with Fanatics, the league unveiled its new Hometown Remix jersey line where they created a special-edition jersey for each and every team inspired by the culture, history, landmarks, and fans of its respective city.

Naturally, with nearly three dozen different designs, some team’s sweaters absolutely cooked, while others seemed to get “lost in the sauce,” as Maryland-born WWE wrestler LA Knight would say.

To rank our favorites, we gave the keys of the blog to RMNB jersey designer KP, who lives near Calgary, Alberta, Canada, and has a refined taste and style above our own. KP tells me he considered how evident the ties to the city were, the color schemes, logos, originality, nostalgia, and perhaps most importantly, whether he’d be embarrassed to wear one of the jerseys to his local Sobeys. I also added some important context and links to each team’s merch if you want to explore further.

Please note: KP’s rankings are entirely subjective, completely arbitrary, and, of course, 100 percent correct. So, no arguin’ in the comments.

Okay, class is now in session.

KP’s tier list for the Hometown Remix jerseys

S tier

Vegas Golden Knights

The Vegas Golden Knights’ new sweater draws inspiration from the city’s casino culture with a predominantly black design accented by jade green, metallic gold, and red.

The familiar Golden Knights crest appears in gold embroidery within an ornate green frame, while the shoulder panels feature casino-felt textures decorated with playing-card suits. Red-and-black sleeve patterns resemble sections of a roulette wheel, and customized player numbers take inspiration from playing cards. A scripted Vegas inscription appears inside the hem.

KP’s take: Perfection. Nailed the nod to the city by going with a roulette themed jersey. It’s wacky and fun but still looks great. The sleeve details are outstanding as well. Perfect.

KP’s take: Perfection. Nailed the nod to the city by going with a roulette themed jersey. It’s wacky and fun but still looks great. The sleeve details are outstanding as well. Perfect.

Nashville Predators

The Nashville Predators’ design pays tribute to Music City’s country music heritage with a deep navy base and intricate gold detailing inspired by the elaborate stagewear worn by country music stars.

The sweater features subtle pinstripes, a gold Predators crest filled with decorative filigree, and matching ornamental patterns across the shoulders, sleeves, and numbers. Additional details include Tennessee’s state flower, the iris, incorporated into the sleeve design, and a hidden “NASHVILLE” wordmark inside the hem.

KP’s take: Also labelling this one as perfect. It looks sick, is something totally new that we’ve never seen before, and ties into Nashville evidently and does it well. Looks like a classic western button up shirt immediately.

KP’s take: Also labelling this one as perfect. It looks sick, is something totally new that we’ve never seen before, and ties into Nashville evidently and does it well. Looks like a classic western button up shirt immediately.

Montreal Canadiens

The Montreal Canadiens’ Hometown Remix jersey reimagines the franchise’s iconic CH logo on a white sweater featuring red architectural detailing.

The crest incorporates patterns inspired by Montreal’s historic stone buildings, while red gradient panels along the sides reference Mount Royal. Five symbols taken from the city’s flag appear along each sleeve, representing Montreal’s cultural heritage. The customized player numbers feature stonework-inspired textures and fleur-de-lis details, with additional fleurs-de-lis surrounding a Montreal inscription inside the hem.

KP’s take: Always worry the Habs will play it safe. They didn’t and it looks slick as hell. The arms and logo treatment both are super clean but still new and fun.

KP’s take: Always worry the Habs will play it safe. They didn’t and it looks slick as hell. The arms and logo treatment both are super clean but still new and fun.

A tier

Washington Capitals

Ah, our hometown team! The Washington Capitals’ Hometown Remix jersey combines the franchise’s late-1990s and early-2000s identity with architectural details inspired by Washington, DC.

The steel-blue sweater brings back the vintage Capitol Dome logo on the chest and Screaming Eagle patches on the shoulders. White sections of the jersey feature marble-like textures inspired by the city’s monuments, complemented by bronze-gold trim and sculpted lettering. Cherry blossom imagery and a tribute to “The DMV” appear inside the hem. See additional photos and learn more.

KP’s take: Nailed a look using the teal from the 90’s here. The marble textured white is such a cool nod to the city that still looks clean. I think a pink instead of copper, or more cherry blossom elements would’ve had it in the S tier but no complaints.

KP’s take: Nailed a look using the teal from the 90’s here. The marble textured white is such a cool nod to the city that still looks clean. I think a pink instead of copper, or more cherry blossom elements would’ve had it in the S tier but no complaints.

Anaheim Ducks

Flying off the shelves online, the Anaheim Ducks’ Hometown Remix jersey plays off the famous Mighty Ducks movies and brings back the franchise’s classic green, purple, and gold color scheme with a Kelly green base and purple sleeves.

The team’s current duck-mask logo appears on the chest in matching colors, while purple and gold stripes wrap around the sleeves and waist. The sleeve design incorporates Anaheim’s familiar flying-V pattern, while an “Orange Country” inscription inside the hem references the team’s Southern California roots.

KP’s take: Fantastic and unique, nostalgic nod to a classic movie. My wanting to limit the S tier to my personal 3 favs for the purpose of exclusivity to the top tier is the only reason it’s not up there as well. Top 5 in this rollout. Love that they finally rolled an iteration of these out.

KP’s take: Fantastic and unique, nostalgic nod to a classic movie. My wanting to limit the S tier to my personal 3 favs for the purpose of exclusivity to the top tier is the only reason it’s not up there as well. Top 5 in this rollout. Love that they finally rolled an iteration of these out.

The Carolina Hurricanes’ Hometown Remix jersey features a storm-grey base and an unexpected centerpiece: Stormy, the team’s “beloved” pig mascot. A full-body illustration of Stormy wearing a white bucket hat, T-shirt, red shorts, and sneakers replaces the Hurricanes’ traditional crest.

Red storm-inspired bands wrap around the sleeves, incorporating embossed oak leaves, while a Raleigh skyline design references the city’s nickname, the City of Oaks. The shoulders feature custom patches inspired by North Carolina landmarks and the state flag.

KP’s take: I hate gray jerseys. It’s dark enough where I don’t hate it at all. The logo is awesome, it’s new and Carolina, who can’t get uniforms right to save their lives, got it right.

Navy sleeves feature gold striping and throwback panther-head shoulder patches. The tropical color scheme continues around the waist, with a hidden “SOUTH FLORIDA” inscription inside the hem.

KP’s take: Insane and fun. It’s crazy but that’s the point of these. It feels very Miami/South Beach even though they didn’t go traditional al Miami vice pink blue and black. It rocks.

The same vein patterns appear in the navy sleeve stripes and player numbers. Inside the hem, a “TORONTO FOREVER” inscription is accompanied by silhouettes of historic Maple Leafs logos.

KP’s take: Sharp classic look. Really like the leaf veins in the logo and numbers. Removal of the white accents would’ve probably had it in the S tier.

A black shoulder yoke contrasts with black-and-white sleeve and waist stripes, arranged to resemble a pair of rising mammoth tusks when the player’s arms are lowered. Subtle snow-inspired textures decorate the white portions of the jersey, reflecting Utah’s mountainous landscape. The Wasatch Mountains appear alongside a Utah wordmark inside the hem.

B tier

Boston Bruins

The Boston Bruins’ design embraces the city’s college hockey culture with a vintage brown-and-gold design inspired by varsity jackets and collegiate sweaters.

Gold block lettering spells out “BRUINS” above the player’s number on the chest, replacing the traditional Spoked-B logo. The jersey features a lace-up collar, gold and cream striping, and letterman-style shoulder patches. A brick pattern inside the hem pays tribute to Boston’s historic Freedom Trail.

The years 1983 and 2027 appear inside the hem, commemorating the building’s opening and planned closure.

KP’s take: While I’m not crazy about how the red and gold looks overall, I love all the Saddledome nods. Between the main waist stripe detail, the stitching bevel detail in the C alluding to the Dome’s ceiling, and the arms, they nailed that aspect.
