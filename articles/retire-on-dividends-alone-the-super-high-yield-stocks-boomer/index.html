Retirees who want their portfolio to pay the bills face a tough benchmark: the 10-year Treasury yield is 5.28%. Mortgage real estate investment trusts (REITs) and business development companies (BDCs) are built to clear that bar by a wide margin, because they pass almost all of their earnings through to shareholders. Yield alone does not make a dividend safe, though. The five names below are ranked by dividend safety, from the safest payout to the most extended, so you can see what backs each check.

Main Street Capital: Monthly Checks Plus a Quarterly Bonus

Main Street Capital (NYSE:MAIN) pays a regular monthly dividend of $0.265 per share. On the regular payout alone, the forward yield is 5.9% at a share price of $53.89. That makes it a high-yield stock, not an ultra-high-yield one. Supplemental dividends lift the trailing 12-month total to $4.33, for a trailing yield of 8.0%.
