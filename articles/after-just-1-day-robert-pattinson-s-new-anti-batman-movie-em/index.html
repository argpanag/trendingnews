After Just 1 Day, Robert Pattinson's New Anti-Batman Movie Emerges as a Streaming Smash

TheTools · 06.11.2026 01:00 · source · permalink

Robert Pattinson has had an absolutely huge year, starring in multiple high-profile movies. One, in which he portrays a legendary journalist and personality, has arrived on streaming and is already a huge hit.

Primetime, which casts Pattinson as To Catch a Predator host Chris Hansen, is available to rent now on services like Apple TV, Prime Video, and Fandango at Home. After one day, Primetime has risen into the top five of Apple TV's digital store.

Primetime Has Been a Controversial Hit for A24

Currently, Primetime is the fourth most-watched movie in the U.S. according to FlixPatrol data, behind Verity, Spider-Man: Brand New Day, and Resident Evil. Primetime doesn't have a subscription streaming release date yet, but A24 has a deal with HBO Max, sending their movies to the streaming service a few months after their theatrical runs end.

Directed by Lance Oppenheim and written by Ajon Singh, Primetime is based on a 2007 Esquire article titled "Tonight on Dateline This Man Will Die" written by Luke Dittrich. The movie largely follows Hansen, a Dateline journalist who creates a television show, To Catch a Predator, that aims to catch child predators in sting operations. The thriller then chronicles the show's rise to fame and Hansen's own life changes with the newfound superstardom, as well as the lengths to which Hansen and NBC go to keep the show a hit. Some might consider it the antithesis of Pattinson's biggest role, Bruce Wayne in The Batman​​​​​​, depicting a real-life crime fighter battling real-life demons instead of a costumed vigilante in a gothic battle between good and evil.

Alongside Pattinson, Primetime also stars Merritt Wever, Skyler Gisondo, Matthew Maher, Phoebe Bridgers, Bokeem Woodbine, Alex Winter, Tony Revolori, Jonathan Lipnicki, and Anna Faris. While reactions to the movie have largely been very positive, not everyone is a fan. Specifically, the movie's real-life subject was not too pleased with it during its entire development.

Hansen has been outspoken in his displeasure with the movie. Ahead of Primetime​​​​​'s theatrical run, Hansen purchased pre-show advertising for every screening promoting his streaming service TruBlu. In an interview with The Hollywood Reporter earlier this year, Hansen also called Primetime "an insult to the thousands of men and women who work hard every day to protect children from predators. They turn something that is very positive, very challenging, and not without risk into what is essentially a horror film."

Despite Hansen disliking the movie, Primetime was a moderate hit for A24. The thriller grossed over $57 million against a $14 million budget. It also earned high critical acclaim. Primetime holds an 86% rating on Rotten Tomatoes, with Pattinson and Gisondo's performances especially being praised. Both actors have been discussed as potential contenders for this year's awards season.

Robert Pattinson Has Another Movie Coming in 2026

It's an understatement to say that 2026 has been a huge year for Pattinson. The actor has been in several of the year's biggest movies, and still has another big one coming. Pattinson started the year starring in April's The Drama, Kristoffer Borgli's dark romantic comedy that also starred Zendaya. The Drama was a smash hit for Pattinson, earning over $134 million, but Pattinson's next movie would be even more successful.

In July, Pattinson then starred in Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey, as Antinous, a suitor of Anne Hathaway's Penelope and foil to Tom Holland's Telemachus. The Odyssey is the second-biggest movie of 2026 so far, grossing over $1.8 billion, with Pattinson once again being praised for his performance. After The Odyssey came Primetime, and in December, Pattinson is set to close out the year with yet another huge role.

Pattinson is set to star in the upcoming Dune: Part Three, which releases on Dec. 18. The final installment of Denis Villeneuve's Dune trilogy will see Pattinson star as Scytale, and once again feature stars like Timothée Chalamet, Zendaya, Jason Momoa, Florence Pugh, Rebecca Ferguson, Isaach de Bankole, Anya Taylor-Joy, Florence Pugh, Charlotte Rampling, Javier Bardem, and more. Pattinson will also star in the upcoming The Batman Part II, set for release on Feb. 18, 2028.
