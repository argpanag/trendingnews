Adam Sykora out 4-5 months, Vincent Iorio day to day

TheTools · 23.09.2026 15:10 · source · permalink

Some rough injury news, as the Adam Sykora injury was about the worst case scenario. The young winger will be out 4-5 months after sustaining a lower body injury in the Rangers preseason opener on Monday night. Sykora took a hard hit form Devils defenseman Mikael Diotte. The hit was clean, but Sykora’s leg got caught underneath him and he wound up going awkwardly into the boards. The injury leaves Sykora, who was in a tough battle for a roster spot to start the season, sitting until the calendar turns to 2027, impacting the Rangers forward depth.

Vincent Iorio also left Monday’s preseason game early, but his prognosis is far less severe. Iorio is day to day and will likely be sent to Hartford (waived) when he is ready. Iorio is likely the 8/9D on this club right now.
