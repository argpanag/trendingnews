Kaley Cuoco and her fiancé, actor Tom Pelphrey, are now parents to two daughters. Their first daughter, Matilda Carmine Richie Pelphrey, was born on March 30, 2023.

While Cuoco and Pelphrey were congratulated by Hollywood stars Josh Brolin, Olivia Munn, Sharon Stone, and Big Bang Theory co-star Kunal Nayyar, the NFL world also sent her a lot of love.

Cuoco is a big-time football fan. She has revealed that she got into the sport because of Pelphrey, who is “obsessed with football,” she tolf the Los Angeles Times in 2024. She also added how the season usually looks for them.

“Every hour of it is taken very seriously.” The rest of the year, Sundays are reserved for decompressing with reality TV and true crime. “Instead of calling it trash [TV], I call it my ‘girl TV,’” she says. “I love ‘Dancing With the Stars.’ Andy Cohen, I watch all his [series]. I’m watching every season of ‘Love Is Blind.’”

During an appearance on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon, in January 2025, Cuoco revealed that Pelphrey manages 10 different fantasy football teams. She struggled to understand how he could keep up with so many teams.

But Kaley Cuoco approach is a lot different. She is not someone who likes to support losing teams. The actress described herself as a “fair-weather fan” on the Jimmy Fallon show.

But she loves supporting her fiancé’s interest in the game, and both of them do fun stuff to show it off.

Kaley Cuoco and her fiancé, Tom Pelphrey, celebrated Halloween in 2024 by dressing up as each other. Cuoco copied Pelphrey’s style by wearing a New York Giants No. 30 jersey, sweatpants, and sneakers. She also wore a black baseball cap and a fake black beard and carried his personalized thermos.

“She knows what’s important in this family,” the actress wrote.
