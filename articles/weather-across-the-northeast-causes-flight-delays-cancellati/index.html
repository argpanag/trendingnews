Weather across the northeast causes flight delays, cancellations at Logan, other airports

TheTools · 13.11.2026 03:23 · source · permalink

Severe weather across the Northeast has led to flight delays, cancellations, and flooding, leaving travelers and residents dealing with disrupted plans.

Lyric Hadley traveled from Connecticut to Logan Airport, catching a flight to Chicago to celebrate his cousin's 21st birthday.

"We're just going to stay over the night, figure out what we got to do," Hadley said.

Like thousands of passengers across the Northeast, Hadley is facing flight delays and cancellations due to bad weather.

"It was just bummer after bummer, I would say," he said.

Rebecca Koblish, who is hoping to make it to Nashville, shared her concerns about the worsening conditions.

"My flight was pushed back by an hour," Koblish said. "I'm worried because it's going to start raining. I'm worried that it's going to get delayed further."

Heavy rains and strong winds have wreaked havoc on air travel.

In New York, drivers faced flooded streets this afternoon. The National Weather Service issued a flash flood warning in Queens County, not far from JFK Airport, shortly after a tornado warning expired near the busy airport.

In Delaware County, Pennsylvania, hail blanketed the area, making it look more like a winter wonderland. Crews worked to clear the slippery mess using shovels and backhoes.

For travelers at Logan Airport, disrupted plans are sometimes unavoidable.

"You got to just live with it. Sometimes things happen to you, so you can't really control that," one traveler said.
