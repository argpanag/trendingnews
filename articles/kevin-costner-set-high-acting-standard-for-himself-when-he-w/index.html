Kevin Costner Set High Acting Standard for Himself When He Was on 'Yellowstone'

TheTools · 26.11.2026 18:06 · source · permalink

Kevin Costner holds a special place in Hollywood and in audiences' esteem. There have been two outstanding recurring motifs in his work - the American West and baseball. Who can forget Costner's roles in films like Field of Dreams, Dances with Wolves, Bull Durham, Wyatt Earp, and many more movies? Perhaps his adherence to a very high standard of acting is at least partially responsible for his success. That seems to be true for his performance in the Taylor Sheridan-helmed TV series Yellowstone as patriarch John Dutton. According to one of his former co-stars, Costner, 71, was fierce when it came to delivering the goods every time he stepped in front of the camera. He never allowed himself to slack off one bit. And he knew when he fell short of the mark.

Videos by Wide Open Country

Actor Josh Holloway Recalls a Scene He Did With Costner

On Yellowstone, Holloway played the character Roarke Morris. He spoke about it when he was a guest being interviewed on the podcast Inside of You hosted by Michael Rosenbaum. He recalled doing a scene with Kevin Costner when the Academy Award-winner seemed to be going through the motions. It was just emotionally and dramatically unimpressive and flat. However, Costner could do better. He didn't wait for the director to upbraid him for the flub. The iconc actor chastised himself.

Said Holloway, "He did a close-up with me where he's telling me about the rules, and he did it, and he just went inside and he was like, 'Ah, that [expletive] was lazy.' I was like, 'he cares, he's still working.'And then he brought it. I was impressed that's he's still keeping his standard high...."

There Were Also Alleged Eruptions on the Set

Although Kevin Costner was often a consummate pro when he was in character for Yellowstone, there were also some alleged tiffs on the set as well. One of them was allegedly with fellow actor Wes Bentley. He and Costner reportedly got into a heated argument over how a scene should be played. According to The Hollywood Reporter, the two men were supposedly on the brink of a scuffle.

One unnamed source who claimed to have been there said, "No fists were thrown, but they were in each other's faces, pushing and shoving and just getting hot until they had to be separated."

Was Kevin Costner being a volatile prima donna or a relentless perfectionist? Who knows?
