Inter travel to Bergamo for a heavyweight evening showdown against Atalanta, while AC Milan welcome surprise package Frosinone to San Siro. Earlier in the day, Juventus welcome relegation-threatened Venezia. 

Don’t miss any Serie A action with our expert tips and predictions for all matches. 

Today’s Serie A betting tips & predictions

Juventus vs. Venezia (11:30am)

Juventus benter the weekend unbeaten in their last three matches across all competitions, following a 2-2 draw at Fiorentina prior to the international break. The Bianconeri have been particularly reliable in Turin, losing just one of five home fixtures this season. 

Venezia occupy the relegation zone on six points, though they snapped a three-match losing streak with a 2-1 home victory over Udinese last time out. Away from home, however, they have lost all five of their Serie A road trips whilst conceding 10 goals.

Juventus have won seven of the last 10 H2Hs with Venezia, and currently boast a clear edge in defensive stability. I’ll be backing them to win to nil. 

Juventus vs. Venezia top betting tip: Juventus to win to nil

Bologna vs. Udinese (2pm)

Bologna enter the round in blistering domestic form, having won five of their last six Serie A outings. The number of goals they have scored is even more impressive – a minimum of two in each win across this stretch. 

Rock-bottom Udinese languish in 20th place on five points, suffering from a nine-match winless league run that includes a 2-1 defeat at Venezia.

Surprisingly, the odds remain fairly good for a home win, and we have to take advantage. 

Bologna vs. Udinese top betting tip: Bologna to Win

Sassuolo vs. Genoa (2pm)

Following a disastrous stretch of six defeats in seven games, Sassuolo are now a point above the relegation zone. The Neroverdi boast the league’s worst defence, having conceded 29 goals across 11 fixtures. 

Genoa are a few steps ahead following their recent three-match winning streak, but the fact remains that their attack has not been very effective overall. However, they are significantly more organised in the back. 

Despite mostly losing and conceding wild numbers, Sassuolo have also been scoring plenty of goals. Every match is a must-win now, so I’m tempted to back the goal markets here. 

Sassuolo vs. Genoa top betting tip: BTTS, Over 2.5 goals

AC Milan vs. Frosinone (5pm)

AC Milan enter the weekend riding back-to-back Serie A wins, as one of the most in-form teams. The Rossoneri have been very competitive on the domestic front, although their defence can benefit from some improvement. 

One of the standout surprises of the campaign, Frosinone sit just four points behind AC Milan, and boast an even better attack. Their matches have been extremely entertaining thanks to their attacking style, which, however, often leads to them conceding a lot too. 

Both teams' attacking profiles point towards an open contest at San Siro, which has generally been the theme in most of their previous H2Hs. 

AC Milan vs. Frosinone top betting tip: Over 2.5 goals

Atalanta vs. Inter Milan (7:45pm)

Atalanta gained welcome relief before the international break by edging Monza 1-0, but this rare win doesn’t change the fact that their season so far has been a disaster, highlighted by the fact that they concede nearly two goals on average per game.  

Inter Milan sit just a point behind leaders Lazio ahead of this round, which means that they cannot afford more mistakes, after recently losing the derby with AC Milan. 

Although the last Serie A clash between these sides ended in a 1-1 stalemate in March, Inter have won each of their last four trips to Bergamo. Atalanta remain a tough team to beat at home, but their defence has been abysmal this season. 

Atalanta vs. Inter Milan top betting tip: Inter to win
