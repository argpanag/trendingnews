India will take on New Zealand in a five-match T20I series that begins on October 22. History is on India’s side as they have won six out of nine bilaterals. It just goes to show how dominant the Men in Blue are.

Table of Contents

Team India’s Strong Batting Line-Up

Beating New Zealand in their own den will not be easy but the three-time T20 World Cup winners do have a batting line-up to boast of. These men with the willow can come down hard on the Kiwi bowlers. The Men in Blue take on the Black Caps in what promises to be a thrilling battle between bat and ball.

The Men Who Can Wreak Havoc with the Willow

So, who are the Indian batters that the Kiwis need to be worried about. It’s not just one player and that is why it gives India the advantage. There are as many as five players who could prove to be a living nightmare for the hosts.

How are these five players? Let’s take a look and find out:

1. Abhishek Sharma

Abhishek is never shy to give the ball a good bash and when on song can prove to be a thorn in the flesh of any bowling unit. He is particularly lethal in the powerplay and a brisk start in the first six overs will boost India's total or run chase early on. The price of his wicket is high, and the bowlers must pay it in order to see him off early.

2. Tilak Varma

The ultimate finisher and dangerous in the death overs. Tilak's explosion with the bat can often be the difference between a decent or great total or defeat and victory. He's done it before and he can do it again. The middle and end overs are where he thrives the most and can make the bowlers pay for any lapses in line and length.

3. Shreyas Iyer

The skipper and stabiliser, Shreyas is one who can change gears as and when the situation demands it. Not only can he anchor in the middle overs, he can also go for the kill at the death. The man can build an innings quickly and before you know it, has laid the bowling attack to waste. A smooth operator who knows how to manipulate the bowler and the field.

4. Sanju Samson

Be it opening or coming in at the fall of a wicket, Samson can attack early on and also be there at the end. In run chases, this is a huge asset for the Men in Blue. Sometimes he may not get a huge score, but his brisk start takes the pressure of someone like Abhishek. Swift cameos have often given India the much-needed momentum and that is why Samson is favoured more by Gautam Gambhir over Vaibhav Sooryavanshi.

5. Ishan Kishan

Ishan is known to be an attacking player and going after the bowling at every stage of the innings is what sets him apart. New Zealand have reason to be wary as he can obliterate the field set and find the gaps with precision or clear the ropes. Not to mention running well between wickets.
