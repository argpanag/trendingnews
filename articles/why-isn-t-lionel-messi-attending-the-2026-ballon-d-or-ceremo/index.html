Why isn’t Lionel Messi attending the 2026 Ballon d’Or ceremony today?

TheTools · 26.10.2026 11:47 · source · permalink
  • /Soccer

A new edition of the Ballon d'Or ceremony is set to take place, and Lionel Messi is once again one of the nominees for the prestigious award.

By Matías Persuh

Updated on October 26, 2026 04:25PM EDT

One of the most significant events in the world of awards is undoubtedly the Ballon d’Or ceremony. Lionel Messi, the most decorated winner in the award’s history, has once again been nominated. However, his recent commitments prevented him from attending the ceremony.

Last week, Messi traveled to Spain to receive the 2026 Princess of Asturias Award for Sports on Thursday. Then he want back to the US to play for Inter Miami in the second half of their 2-0 loss against the New York Red Bulls on Saturday. And the Herons return to action on Wednesday against Cincinnati.

As if that weren’t enough, the No. 10 is currently hosting the Messi Cup, where he has been actively involved and present at some of the matches. This packed schedule ultimately prevented the Argentine superstar from joining the other nominees at the ceremony.

Messi is the most decorated player in Ballon d’Or history, with eight awards to his name, and he is looking to further cement his legendary career. Will he be able to do so in the twilight of his career?

Messi is aiming to win his ninth Ballon d’Or

Lionel Messi’s Ballon d’Or journey is nothing short of legendary, kicking off back in 2009 when he picked up his very first golden trophy as a unstoppable youngster at Barcelona. Over the next decade and a half, he turned winning soccer’s most prestigious individual award into a regular habit, taking home the top spot in 2010, 2011, 2012, 2015, 2019, and 2021.

The ultimate crowning moment came in 2023, when he claimed his eighth and final Ballon d’Or—fresh off leading Argentina to World Cup glory in Qatar—solidifying a record that will likely stand untouched for generations.
