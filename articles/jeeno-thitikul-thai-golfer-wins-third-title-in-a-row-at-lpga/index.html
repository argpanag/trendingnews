Jeeno Thitikul: Thai golfer wins third title in a row at LPGA season-ending event

TheTools · 23.11.2026 08:48 · source · permalink

Thitikul's victory gave her a 12th career LPGA title

World number two Jeeno Thitikul produced a seven-under final round of 65 to claim a third successive CME Group Tour Championship title.

The 23-year-old from Thailand started the fourth round of the season-ending event with an eagle and went on to card five birdies as she finished on 24 under at the Tiburon Golf Club in Florida.

"The only words I have is not to change the course until I'm retired," said Thitikul.

"It's just me and the course, we just are best friends. I think she is my best friend on the tour."

Her victory earned the $4m (£3.1m) top prize and also broke Annika Sorenstam's career earnings record of $22,583,693 to become the tour's all-time prize money leader on $24,292,858.

Japan's Akie Iwai also carded a 65 in the fourth round to claim second place on 22 under, while American Nelly Korda's 68 put her third on 20 under.

World number one Korda also overtook Sorenstam to move to second on the LPGA Tour career prize money list.

South Korea's A-Lim Kim had an overnight lead of one shot but her challenge faltered after a double bogey and bogey on the first three holes as she finished with a 72 and tied seventh.

England's Lottie Woad carded a 67 to end on 14 under, with compatriot Charley Hull one shot further back after a 73 in the final round.
