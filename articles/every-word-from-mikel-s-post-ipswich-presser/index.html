Every word from Mikel's post-Ipswich presser

TheTools · 17.09.2026 01:13 · source · permalink

We progressed into the fourth round of the Carabao Cup on Tuesday night, courtesy of an enthralling 4-2 away victory at Ipswich Town.

Max Dowman shone on his first start of the season with a goal in each half, the first getting the ball rolling before Noni Madueke lashed in a quickfire second.

After the teenager made it three, Mikel Merino swept in a delightful fourth before Anis Mehmeti and Hale End graduate Chuba Akpom replied for the hosts.

Mikel Arteta addressed the media after the tie, and you can read every word from the boss below.

On his reaction to tonight…

I'm really pleased with a lot of things that we've done tonight – the way we attacked the game, how dominant, fluent and aggressive we were right from the beginning to try to influence the results. We scored two very early goals that gave us a great platform for the game, and individually some big performances as well. Some debuts tonight, which is great to see as well. So, we take a lot of positives from the night. 

On Max Dowman’s impact…

A joy to have him, Marli as well, 16-years-old, to play at the level with the physicality that they have to be able to handle that level, it's remarkable. And Max, yeah, it's inevitable to talk about him when he scores and he creates the moments that he has created tonight - it’s normal.

On how impressive he was both attacking and defending…

That's something very natural for him. And he can deliver those actions effortlessly, I would say, that's his biggest quality. And defensively, his application, his capacity as well to sustain the actions, which is not easy, he's 16-years-old. And he can create moments, but consistently to go through the game, every phase with the demands that we put in them, to be able to do that is extremely difficult. And he's coping better and better and better [with that]. He did the action where he regained the ball and wins it. In the first goal, he loses his man, and that's the thing that we need to continue to reiterate with him, because he's an unbelievable player because of the character that he has and how much he loves to play at the biggest stage and still handle the situation with such naturality. 

On keeping Max grounded…

Well, dependent if he what he does on the pitch, it's not easy, I understand. But what we have to do is look after him - he's 16 years old, you don't see that in this league. In other leagues in Europe, that's a totally different context. But in this league, with the demands that the league, the players and everything puts on you, we really need to make sure every time we throw him in, he's in the right context with the right preparation, and that's what we are trying to do. 

On if we’ll use him sparingly…

We're going to make sure that when we just throw him there, he's ready to handle that at every level, and it's not just that he can run past somebody. There's a lot more things that we need to look after him, but he will dictate that in terms of how he behaves every single day and how many reasons he gives to play because he's part of our squad, like anybody else, no different. The only thing that we have to manage is that he needs to constantly be aware that he needs to be prepared for what he needs to do. 

On how much excitement Max and Marli give him…

I live in the moment and just having two 16-year-olds playing for Arsenal at this level against a Premier League team is so fulfilling, because I've been there and just to be able to contribute on somebody's career and dream, to be able to experience something like tonight is just phenomenal. Medium and long term, we know that if they continue to develop in the way that they are, we're going to have two tremendous footballers for the future.

On Mikel Merino growing into his leadership role…

He’s unbelievable. First of all, he's one of the most intelligent players that I've seen and I've worked with. Then the personality is just full of... I don't know, he just wants to give for everybody. He thinks about everything, he thinks about everybody. And on top of that, he has qualities that are unique in football and in our team, his capacity to arrive in the box, he's capable of occupying different places, to play in different positions, to link play with people. And as a midfielder, the ability that he has to score goals like tonight is a different one that we've seen lately, but another incredible one.

On if Max is developing his physicality…

I'm glad you're talking about that, because that's a big part of the game as well, he really wants to do it. The more he plays, the more he trains with us, and the more ready he is physically to sustain that level, he will do it. And that will help him in his attacking phase for sure, because he will force errors and make mistakes on the opponent, and he will then be in a really open situation to exploit his qualities, he knows that. Today is a big example for him as well, and I'm sure that's going to trigger even more will for him to do it. 

On what else he needs to develop…

You need to be at his level the best in every phase, and we know what he can do when he's in and around the ball, especially the way he's got a bit of space around him. Defensively, on set pieces, it's not only that. His leadership has to increase, his voice has to increase, his role has to increase within the team, in the dressing room. There are a lot of things that you need to grow, but at 16, to already do what he does, it's just incredible.

On if a 16-year-old can play regularly in the Premier League…

Everything is possible. The probability probably is extremely low, because you don't see the players with the capacity to handle just the physicality of the game. Not only that, but the demands that you have to put your bodies through in the week to be able to be in a good condition to compete, the amount of information, the challenges that you have to face. But he's doing it, and he's doing it really well every time he's got a chance.
