Stansted care home residents welcome visitors for vintage games night

TheTools · 31.10.2026 06:47 · source · permalink

CARE home residents in Stansted welcomed visitors for a vintage games night.

The group enjoyed traditional board games in a 1950s-style diner setting at Care UK's Mountfitchet House.

They also ate diner-style food and sipped milkshakes.

Jacqui Jones, a 103-year-old resident, said: "I had such a lovely evening, especially playing games with Willow – despite our 100-year age difference.

"Dominoes is my favourite game, and I'm often searching for someone willing to challenge me to a match."

The event was part of Care UK’s Bored Games initiative, a nationwide campaign encouraging communities to reconnect through traditional board games.

Susan Whitaker, customer relations manager at Mountfitchet House, said: "We had such a wonderful time opening our doors to the community to take part in our vintage games night.

"It was fantastic to see everyone getting involved and enjoying some friendly competition.

"For many residents, traditional games nights represent a lifetime of treasured memories, so this was a great opportunity to revisit those moments while creating new memories with the local community.
