NFL Week 3 bold predictions: Raiders upset Saints to improve to 3-0! Baker Mayfield bounces back

TheTools · 27.09.2026 13:40 · source · permalink

NFL.com analysts provide bold predictions -- spanning everything from team-based takes to fantasy forecasts -- for Week 3 of the 2026 season.

NOTE: All statistics come courtesy of Next Gen Stats, unless otherwise noted.

Dan Parr

Senior Editor, Draft Strategy

Upstart Raiders upset Saints in New Orleans to hit 3-0 for first time since 2021.

Aside from the Raiders' most optimistic fans, I don’t think anyone had Las Vegas matching its 2025 win total by Week 3 in 2026, but I’m already on record picking the franchise to win its third consecutive game to open the season. The difference against the Saints -- playing without injured LT Kelvin Banks Jr. -- will be Maxx Crosby and the Raiders’ pass rush, which leads the league in QB pressure rate (50%).

Grant Gordon

Digital Content Editor

Baker Mayfield bounces back, wins No. 1 battle.

Quite quickly, the Buccaneers could be panicking, off to a 0-2 start and facing an undefeated Vikings squad. Have no fear, Baker is here and will bounce back against Brian Flores' maniacal Minnesota defense. Mayfield, opposed by another former No. 1 overall pick out of Oklahoma, will surpass 300 yards and throw three touchdown passes against the Vikings. Kyler Murray’s return to the fold for Minnesota will see him match Mayfield’s 300-yard game. However, Murray’s first time starting and finishing a game for the Vikings will end in defeat, as Mayfield and the Bucs pull off the upset.

Ben McWilliams

Manager, NFL Research

Record-setting kick for futebol fans in Brazil.

Before he was one of the NFL’s best kickers, Brandon Aubrey was an All-American soccer player at Notre Dame and first-round draft pick by Toronto FC in the 2017 MLS Draft. Given Aubrey's futebol accomplishments prior to his football stardom, I predict he will put on a show for the Brazilian fans in Rio de Janeiro and set the NFL record with a 70-yard field goal.

Gennaro Filice

Editor at Large

Jeffery Simmons wrecks Giants with sack hat trick, carrying Titans to first win.

In the wake of Jaxson Dart's crushing knee injury, the Giants are down bad. And here comes King Kong to take New York. After signing a nine-figure extension in June, Simmons has started this season as a man possessed, racking up 13 QB pressures in two games. That total leads all interior defensive linemen, but it has only resulted in one sack. That changes on Sunday, when the 6-foot-4, 305-pound monster gets home not once, not twice, but THRICE. The Giants have yielded the league's highest pressure rate through two weeks (44.5%, per NFL Pro), and now they could be without their best offensive lineman in Andrew Thomas (groin). The left tackle's absence would change New York's entire protection plan, and given how lost this offense already looked with Jameis Winston under center this past Monday, that's a recipe for disaster. Simmons completely wrecks the game to give Tennessee its first victory under Robert Saleh.

EDITOR'S UPDATE: Giants LT Andrew Thomas returned to practice in a limited capacity on Friday, clearing the way for him to start on Sunday.

Raza Umerani

Seasonal Fantasy Football Researcher

Jaxon Smith-Njigba sets early-season record.

No player in NFL history has caught seven touchdown passes in the first three weeks of a season. In an encore of last week, Smith-Njigba will snag three more scores against the Commanders to become the first one to do so. Washington has allowed the most touchdown passes (seven) in the NFL through two weeks, and it's the only team to have allowed three different players to catch two touchdowns in a game against it. JSN is going to have a historic field day just east of the nation's capital.

Lance Zierlein

Draft Analyst

Bengals spank Steelers, logging second straight double-digit road win.

Cincinnati made noise in Week 2 with a 20-6 victory at Houston, thanks in part to a dominant performance from first-year Bengal Dexter Lawrence. This week, the Joe Burrow-led offense takes it to Pittsburgh's proud defense, spearheading a two-touchdown win at Acrisure Stadium.

Chad Reuter

Draft Analyst

Cowboys stars put on show in Rio.

Baltimore is the favorite over Dallas in the NFL's first-ever game in Rio de Janeiro and third in Brazil. But Dak Prescott gets his star receivers involved, with CeeDee Lamb and George Pickens each catching a touchdown pass. Plus, rookie safety Caleb Downs announces himself as an emerging NFL playmaker with a late interception to seal the victory.

Brooke Cersosimo

Senior Staff Editor

Dolphins RB gets off the schneid.

Through two weeks, De'Von Achane is averaging fewer than 80 scrimmage yards per game and has yet to score a touchdown. Achane notched at least 11 TDs in each of his first three NFL campaigns. The Pro Bowl back gets the ball rolling in Week 3 with 150 scrimmage yards and a touchdown against a Chiefs defense that ranks third in yards allowed and 10th against the run.
