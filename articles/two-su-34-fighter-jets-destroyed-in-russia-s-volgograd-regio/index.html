Two Su-34 fighter jets destroyed in Russia’s Volgograd region — Ukrainian Navy | (shelter)

TheTools · 30.10.2026 01:51 · source · permalink

news feed editor

Two Su-34 fighter-bombers, which the Russian military uses to launch guided airstrikes against Ukraine, were destroyed at the "Marinovka" airfield in Russia's Volgograd Oblast. The two fighter-bombers are worth approximately $100 million.

This was reported by the Ukrainian Navy.

Update, 8:00 p.m.:

President Volodymyr Zelenskyy commented on the successful operation by the Ukrainian Navy.

We carried out two missile strikes, destroying two Russian Su-34s, and also hit an Iskander launcher. Thank you, guys, for the result!

We carried out two missile strikes, destroying two Russian Su-34s, and also hit an Iskander launcher. Thank you, guys, for the result!

The General Staff of the Armed Forces of Ukraine also confirmed the downing of the aircraft.

The Su-34 is a front-line multi-role fighter-bomber. The aircraft is capable of carrying up to 8 metric tons of payload.

The estimated market value of a single Su-34 is about $50 million, so the destruction of two such aircraft could represent a loss of approximately $100 million in aviation assets.

The General Staff notes that the destruction of aircraft of this type weakens the Russian military’s ability to strike frontline territories, civilian infrastructure, and Ukrainian military positions with guided bombs.

As previously reported by the Navy, the destroyed aircraft were located at the 11th Mixed Aviation Regiment, situated near the town of Oktyabrsky in the Volgograd region.

The strike on the Russian aircraft’s base at the “Marinovka” airfield was carried out by the “Neptune” coastal missile system.

Background

The Su-34 is one of the main aircraft of the Russian tactical air force. The Russian Armed Forces use these fighter-bombers to drop guided bombs on Ukrainian cities, Defense Forces positions, and frontline territories.

The Ukrainian Navy emphasized that it continues to strike Russian military targets to reduce Russia’s ability to conduct combat operations against Ukraine.

As a reminder, Russia confirmed the deaths of two pilots following the crash of a Su-30 on October 21. At that time, the Ministry of Defense reported that a Su-30 fighter jet had crashed during a routine training flight in the Kaliningrad region.
