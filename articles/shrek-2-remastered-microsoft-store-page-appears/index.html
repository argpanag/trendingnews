Shrek 2 Remastered Microsoft Store page appears

TheTools · 16.10.2026 16:32 · source · permalink
  • Xbox Series

A Microsoft Store page for the unannounced Shrek 2 Remastered developed by Aspyr has gone live.

The game is likely meant to be officially announced during the ID@Xbox Showcase on October 20, as Shrek briefly appears in the teaser trailer.

News of a Shrek 2 remaster first surfaced during a large-scale Steam Achievements leak in September.

Here is an overview of the game, via Microsoft Store:

About

Journey through the fairytale kingdom of Far Far Away from Shrek’s swamp to Fiona’s castle and everywhere in between, in a classic action adventure experience inspired by the DreamWorks Animation movie. Play solo or with up to three friends in cooperative play, take control of your favorite Shrek heroes as you smash, throw and kick your way against iconic characters from the franchise.

Key Features

  • Improved Graphics – Everything has been rebuilt with better textures, enhanced UI, 4K resolution and cutscenes.
  • New Features – Create timeless snapshots using the new Photo Mode and unlock cheats in Cheat Mode to make your playthrough a seamless experience.
  • Up to Four-Player Couch Cooperative Play, Online or Mixed – A cozy, team-based cooperative action adventure game, playable solo or up to Four-Player Couch Cooperative Play, Online Multiplayer, and Mixed Online Multiplayer and Couch Cooperative Play.
  • Immersive Levels Straight Out of a Fairytale – From Shrek’s swamp to the castle of Far Far Away, relive iconic moments from the movie featuring beloved characters and hidden Easter Eggs.
  • Swap Heroes at Any Time – Swap your hero to battle enemies, conquer bosses, and solve environmental puzzles using their unique abilities to advance in the story.

Travel back to a nostalgic era of gaming for some pick-up-and-play casual fun.
