Incoming Minister of State Foo Cexiang seeks to connect people, make good outcomes for Singapore

TheTools · 04.09.2026 03:46 · source · permalink

Incoming Minister of State Foo Cexiang says he wants to connect businesses, workers and government to deliver tangible outcomes for Singaporeans. Ahead of taking office at the Manpower and Trade and Industry ministries tomorrow, the Tanjong Pagar GRC MP says his priorities include how firms can adopt AI without compromising workers' development and how Singapore can tap new growth markets overseas. Sherlyn Seah reports.
