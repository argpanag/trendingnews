Your AI 80s Photo Looks Cool — But What Does It Cost The Environment? | TRP

TheTools · 10.09.2026 17:40 · source · permalink

That nostalgic 1980s makeover might only take a few seconds to create, but the technology behind it still uses electricity, water and plenty of computing power.

  • Generating one AI image uses roughly the electricity needed to power a 10-watt LED bulb for 17 minutes, but viral trends multiply this enormously.
  • AI image generation requires around 1,450 times more energy than a basic text-classification task, making it one of AI's most resource-intensive activities.
  • The real responsibility lies with companies, not individual users, to build more efficient models and adopt renewable energy and better cooling systems.
