NBA basketball: Portland loses at home to San Antonio, 15 points for Avdija

TheTools · 08.11.2026 05:38 · source · permalink

For the first time since the start of the NBA season, Portland suffers two consecutive losses, after the Blazers were defeated 125-103 at home by the San Antonio Spurs, in a game in which they trailed from start to finish. Deni Avdija scored 15 points for the losing team, whose record now stands at six wins and three losses. Victor Wembanyama led the winners' scorers with 29 points.
