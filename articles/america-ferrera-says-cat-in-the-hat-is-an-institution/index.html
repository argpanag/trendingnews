America Ferrera says Cat In The Hat is 'an institution'

TheTools · 22.11.2026 16:14 · source · permalink

Barbie and Ugly Betty star America Ferrera said she had a lot of fun bringing the Cat in the Hat to life after a big part of her own childhood: "It is such an institution."

The 42-year-old actress lent her voice for the upcoming animated adaptation of the beloved Dr Seuss book which follows a mischievous cat who is sent to cheer up siblings Gabby and Sebastian after they move to a new town.

The Superstore actress, who is also known for voicing Astrid Hofferson in the How To Train Your Dragon franchise, plays Carmen, a hospital nurse and mother of the two children, in the feature-length film.

Speaking at the London premiere, Ferrera told the Press Association: "I think what's different about this project than like How To Train Your Dragon, which I did forever, was this is, like, such an institution.

"Cat in the Hat is a classic. It’s a part of so many people’s childhoods, including my own.

"So, you’re sort of helping to bring something to life that already has, like, an amazing legacy and that was a ton of fun."

Directed by Alessandro Carloni and Erica Rivinoja, the film also stars comedian Bill Hader, who voices the mayhem-causing magical cat known for wearing a tall stripey white and red hat.

She added: "I loved playing the mom, playing Carmen. The directors Alessandro and Erica just, like, wanted me to like be as silly and have as much fun as I possibly could as the mom, and that was a lot of fun."

The film also stars What We Do in the Shadows actor Matt Berry, Abbott Elementary’s Quinta Brunson, Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness actress Xochitl Gomez, Breaking Bad actor Giancarlo Esposito and Wicked star Bowen Yang.

Hader, who also served as an executive producer on the film and is known for being a former cast member of Saturday Night Live, said the process allowed him to get more "comfortable" with using his own voice.

He said: "Doing my own voice was really interesting because usually I do a voice.

"Using my own voice as an animated character was different, but I actually got very comfortable with it.

"I realised, usually I try to do other voices to hide a thing or something, so I got a little more comfortable with my own voice."

He went on to say that the project has been a "great experience from top to bottom".

"It’s been fun," he added, "it’s been a great experience from top to bottom and everybody’s so pleasant.

"It’s just been a really pleasant experience."

The Cat in the Hat will hit cinemas in the UK and Ireland from Friday December 4.
