Over 314,000 older Toyota and Lexus vehicles recalled for potentially dangerous airbag issue

TheTools · 22.11.2026 18:21 · source · permalink

Affected Corolla, Tundra, Sequoia, 4Runner and Lexus models will get free dealer repairs due to a shrapnel hazard

Over 314,000 older Toyota, Lexus vehicles recalled for faulty airbags

Toyota is recalling 314,029 Toyota and Lexus vehicles in the United States over passenger airbag inflators that could rupture, sending metal fragments toward occupants and risking serious injury or death.

Toyota is recalling more than 314,000 vehicles in the U.S. because front passenger airbag inflators made by Takata could rupture and hurl metal fragments at occupants, increasing the risk of serious injury or death.

The recall affects 314,029 Toyota and Lexus vehicles from the 2002 through 2017 model years, according to a report filed with the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) and reported by Reuters.

Affected models include the Toyota Corolla, Tundra, Sequoia and 4Runner, along with the Lexus SC430, IS250 and GX460.

Toyota reported no ruptures involving the affected inflators, according to the NHTSA recall report.

The problem stems from Takata front passenger airbag inflators that are nearing the end of their estimated safe service life. NHTSA said the inflators could rupture due to propellant degradation after long-term exposure to high humidity, high temperatures and high temperature cycling.

If the front passenger airbag deploys in a crash, the degraded inflator could rupture and send sharp metal fragments into the cabin.

Toyota said it is conducting the recall out of an abundance of caution, according to NHTSA.

Depending on the vehicle, authorized Toyota and Lexus dealers will replace the front passenger airbag inflator or the entire airbag assembly free of charge, the agency said.

Owners can check whether their vehicles are included in the recall by visiting NHTSA's recall database at nhtsa.gov/recalls and entering a 17-digit vehicle identification number or by contacting an authorized Toyota or Lexus dealership.

Toyota plans to mail owner notification letters between Dec. 28 and Jan. 11, according to the NHTSA filing, though owners do not have to wait for those letters to contact a dealer about repairs.
