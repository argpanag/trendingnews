Double the basketball!

This is quite the evening for professional basketball in the A.

The Atlanta Hawks tip off their season at the Orlando Magic. At the same time, the Atlanta Dream are hosting their first finals game in 13 years having split 1-1 two games at the Golden State Valkyries.

So, with such a special occasion, let’s do a combined game thread!
