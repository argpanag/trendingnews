Nets' Moritz Wagner: Shooting woes in loss

TheTools · 25.10.2026 23:11 · source · permalink

Wagner finished with six points (1-6 FG, 1-2 3Pt, 3-4 FT), four rebounds and one assist across 14 minutes during Saturday's 125-121 loss to the Cavaliers.

Wagner struggled mightily with efficiency from inside the arc Saturday and is now shooting 22.2 percent from the field and 50.0 percent from deep through two regular-season appearances. The big man will likely continue operating as Day'Ron Sharpe's primary backup.
