An Update From AD Jon Gilbert

September 24, 2026 | General

  • Tailgate Plaza opens at 10 a.m., with North Carolina singer-songwriter Zack Lee taking the stage at 1:30 p.m.
  • The Pirate Walk will begin at 1:45 p.m. along the Howard & Lyn Swain Pirate Promenade. Come out and welcome the team as the players step off the buses.
  • The All Veteran Group will parachute into Dowdy-Ficklen Stadium approximately 20 minutes before kickoff. Thank you to Pepsi–Minges Bottling Group for making this possible.
  • Junior Pirate Club members will take a halftime lap around the perimeter of Bagwell Field. There is still time to join the Junior Pirate Club and register for the halftime lap.  Every child registered for the lap will be entered for a chance to raise the No Quarter Flag at the start of the fourth quarter.
  • Thanks to Pepsi - Minges Bottling Group, fans can once again enjoy a full array of fireworks throughout the game.
  • At halftime, please remain in your seats for a special performance by the Jr. Pirate Dancers followed by a performance from our very own Marching Pirates.
