No sophomore slump for Cooper Flagg, who leads Mavs vs. Clippers

The Los Angeles Clippers look for their first road win of the season when they continue a four-game road trip Saturday night against the Dallas Mavericks.

While the Clippers have struggled on the road, the Mavericks are 6-2 at home and halted a three-game losing streak with an impressive 116-94 win over Golden State Wednesday.

“It’s pretty obvious for everyone, the timing, the schedule and the health, all those factors coming in,” said first-year Mavericks coach Dusty May about the advantage of playing at home. “As I get to know this league better, I know availability is paramount but (at home) you sleep in your own bed and you have a lot of support behind you.”

Mavs second-year star Cooper Flagg ranks in the top 20 in the NBA in scoring (24.2 points) and assists (6.4). Kyrie Irving has returned and is averaging 22.3 points a game. The Mavs have also been getting good minutes from PJ Washington (14.0 ppg) and Naji Marshall (11.6 ppg).

May said after 15 games he was starting to get a feel for his team and what it was capable of.

“We’re at 15 (games),” May said. “We will look at our line-up combinations, two-man groups, three-man groups, five-man groups, but part of it is also the eye test. Do we feel good about the way we’re playing because sometimes you play great and the ball doesn’t go in, sometimes you play terrible and the ball does go in. You try to combine the analytics with the data and your experience to make the best overall decision for the group.”

Los Angeles is 0-6 on the road this season and coming off a 124-112 loss in San Antonio Thursday night. Los Angeles stayed close deep into the fourth quarter with Brook Lopez and Darius Garland each pouring in 23 points.

The Clippers committed 22 turnovers in the loss.

“Turnovers led to easy baskets for them in transition,” Clippers coach Tyronn Lue said afterward. “We knew coming into the game that we had to take care of the basketball… You shoot 49% from the field, 40% from three, but then you have 22 turnovers that lead to 28 points for them. It makes it hard to come back, especially against a team like this.”

The Clippers rank second in the NBA in shooting threes at 39% but are last in total rebounding in the league. They are allowing 118.1 points a game, which is 24th in the NBA.

The Clippers are expected to get Brandon Ingram back as early as Saturday. Ingram, who was acquired in the Kawhi Leonard trade in September has yet to play for LA while dealing with a heel/Achilles injury, but he did make the trip with the club.

Los Angeles did get second-year center Yanic Konan Niederhauser back from his injury against the Spurs. He played 11 minutes and scored two points.

The Clippers won three of the four meetings last season. Dallas leads the all-time series 102-79.
