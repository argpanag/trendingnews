Sandpoint chamber welcomes Rest in Peace RV Park

TheTools · 12.11.2026 01:00 · source · permalink

Greater Sandpoint Chamber officials welcome Rest in Peace RV Park to the organization at a recent ribbon-cutting event. Pictured, from left, are chamber board member Danielle Resso, RIP RV Park owners Kristina and Sean Gavin, and Greater Sandpoint Chamber Executive Director Joan Urbaniak.
Courtesy photo / GREATER SANDPOINT CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

SANDPOINT — The Greater Sandpoint Chamber of Commerce officially welcomed Rest in Peace RV Park with a ribbon-cutting ceremony.

Officials with Rest in Peace (R.I.P.) RV Park, a locally owned and operated RV park south of Sagle, said they are excited to celebrate the park's grand opening with chamber officials and local business representatives. Owned by Sean and Kristina Gavin, the park offers a welcoming place for travelers to stay while exploring the beauty and recreation of North Idaho.

Rest in Peace RV Park offers easy access to Sandpoint, Lake Pend Oreille, Silverwood Theme Park, and surrounding attractions, serving as a base for traveling in the region. The park features 17 spacious RV sites, each offering full hookups and complimentary Wi-Fi. Guests can also enjoy private bathrooms with showers, an on-site laundry facility, a designated pet area, and convenient self-check-in.

The park's unique name is a playful nod to its location near Westmond Cemetery, adding a little personality to a business built around comfort, convenience, and hospitality. Whether guests are passing through for a night, visiting family, attending a sporting event, or spending an extended stay exploring the area, Rest in Peace RV Park strives to provide a comfortable home base.

As longtime members of the North Idaho community, the Gavins bring their own unique backgrounds to the business. Sean Gavin also owns Mountain View Construction and North Idaho Flood and Fire, while Kristina Gavin serves as a school nurse with the Lake Pend Oreille School District. Together, they take pride in their community roots and look forward to welcoming visitors while supporting the local businesses and attractions that make North Idaho such a special place.

For more information visit their website riprvpark.com.
