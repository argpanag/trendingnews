Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie settle Château Miraval lawsuit out of court; Pitt's case against Stoli's Shefler continues

TheTools · 26.11.2026 10:35 · source · permalink

Culture, Lifestyle, Society, World / United States of America, World Culture, World Society

Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie moved Wednesday to dismiss their claims against each other over Château Miraval, saying they settled "in the best interests of our family." The terms are confidential, and Pitt's separate lawsuit against Stoli owner Yuri Shefler is still active

Former Hollywood spouses Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have agreed to an out-of-court settlement bringing an end to their multi-year legal fight over Château Miraval, their jointly owned French winery.

Both parties have moved to dismiss their respective claims under confidential terms, announcing the resolution in a joint statement issued through their representatives. “We have settled out of court in the best interests of our family and will not be commenting further,” the statement read, adding, “Any comments in media stories attributed to ‘sources’ are in fact not coming from either of us, or anyone close to us.”

Pitt and Jolie originally purchased a controlling stake in the French estate in 2008 and later held their 2014 wedding on the property, before Jolie filed for divorce in 2016. Although the pair were declared legally single in 2019, Jolie received court clearance in 2021 to sell her winery shares for $164 million to Stoli Group, a spirits company controlled by Russian oligarch Yuri Shefler.

Pitt sued Jolie in February 2022 over that transaction, alleging she violated a mutual pact barring either owner from selling their stake without the other’s approval.

The litigation escalated sharply in October 2022 when Jolie lodged a countersuit detailing allegations of abusive conduct prior to their split, which Pitt has denied. In that filing, Jolie’s legal team asserted that earlier talks to sell her half of the vineyard directly to Pitt collapsed after he insisted on “a nondisclosure agreement that would have contractually prohibited her from speaking outside of court about Pitt’s physical and emotional abuse of her and their children.”

With this week’s settlement, both sides have formally closed the long-running dispute over the estate.
