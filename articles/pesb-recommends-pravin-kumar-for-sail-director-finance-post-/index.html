PESB Recommends Pravin Kumar for SAIL Director (Finance) Post After Selection Process  - https://indianmasterminds.com

TheTools · 21.09.2026 21:00 · source · permalink

New Delhi: The Public Enterprises Selection Board (PESB) has recommended Pravin Kumar for the post of Director (Finance) at Steel Authority of India Limited (SAIL).

The recommendation was made during PESB Meeting, held on September 21, 2026, following the interview process for the senior finance leadership position in the Schedule ‘A’ Maharatna company.

The recommendation is an important step in the appointment process, while the final appointment will be subject to approval by the competent authority.

Who is Pravin Kumar?

Pravin Kumar is currently serving as General Manager at SAIL. He is associated with the SAIL ERP Center, Corporate Office, Bhilai Location, where he handles key responsibilities across finance and management functions.

His current areas of responsibility include:

  • Finance
  • Costing
  • Funds Management
  • Real Estate Management

Kumar was among the 10 candidates interviewed by PESB for the post of Director (Finance). Following the selection process, PESB recommended his name for the senior finance leadership position at SAIL.

10 Candidates Interviewed for SAIL Director (Finance) Post

The PESB interviewed 10 candidates for the position, comprising senior executives from SAIL as well as finance professionals from other public sector organisations.

The candidates who appeared for the selection process were:

  • Arup Mukherjee – Executive Director (Internal Audit), SAIL
  • Prajesh Chandra Mishra – Chief General Manager (F&A), SAIL
  • Pramod Kumar Chokhani – Chief General Manager (Internal Audit), Bhilai Steel Plant, SAIL
  • Sudershan Kalra – Chief General Manager (Finance), SAIL
  • Varadarajan Iyengar – Chief General Manager, SAIL
  • Hafik Kumar Say – CGM (Internal Audit), SAIL
  • Vikas Shounak – General Manager (F&A), SAIL
  • Pravin Kumar – General Manager, SAIL
  • Sunil Kumar Goel – Executive Director (Finance), Indian Railway Finance Corporation Limited
  • Shubhadeep Sen – CFO & General Manager (F&A), Gujarat State Electricity Corporation Limited

The interview process was conducted to select a suitable candidate for the senior financial leadership position at SAIL.

PESB Recommendation for Director (Finance)

The Public Enterprises Selection Board is responsible for making recommendations for senior management positions in Central Public Sector Enterprises.

In the present case, PESB recommended Pravin Kumar for the post of Director (Finance) after considering the candidates interviewed for the position.

The post carries significant responsibilities related to the financial management and strategic planning of the company.

Key Details

  • Company: Steel Authority of India Limited (SAIL)
  • Post: Director (Finance)
  • Recommended Candidate: Pravin Kumar
  • Current Designation: General Manager, SAIL
  • Selection Authority: Public Enterprises Selection Board (PESB)
  • Meeting Date: September 21, 2026
  • Total Candidates Interviewed: 10
  • Final Appointment: Subject to approval by the competent authority

What Happens Next?

The PESB recommendation is part of the process for appointment to senior management positions in Central Public Sector Enterprises. The recommendation will now go through the required approval process.

The final appointment of Pravin Kumar as Director (Finance) of SAIL will be subject to approval by the competent authority.

If approved, the Director (Finance) will have an important role in areas such as financial planning, resource management, financial strategy and other key finance-related functions of the company.

About SAIL

Steel Authority of India Limited (SAIL) is one of India’s major steel producers and a Schedule ‘A’ Maharatna Central Public Sector Enterprise under the Ministry of Steel. The company operates integrated steel plants and produces a wide range of steel products used across infrastructure, construction, engineering and other industrial sectors.
