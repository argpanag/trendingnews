Reis Targets Conference League Top Eight With Two Wins

TheTools · 26.11.2026 20:26 · source · permalink

Bsinews.id, Jakarta, Trabzonspor coach Thomas Reis has identified two victories from their remaining three UEFA Conference League matches as the target for securing a top-eight finish. He outlined the qualification objective ahead of Thursday's fourth-round encounter against CSKA Sofia in Sofia, Bulgaria.

According to Reis, the Turkish club still have a realistic opportunity to achieve their European ambitions despite losing their previous match in the competition. The upcoming fixture also presents an important challenge against a side competing for points in the standings.

Reis explained that Trabzonspor deserved more from their previous European outing, particularly because of their performance after the interval. He believes the team's remaining schedule offers an opportunity to reach the desired position.

"Looking at the Conference League standings, if we win two of our next three matches, we will achieve our goal of finishing in the top eight." Reis said.

The Trabzonspor manager also addressed the absence of his team's supporters for the match in Bulgaria. He acknowledged that the circumstances could not be changed but expected an enjoyable contest at the stadium.

"Of course, we will be without our supporters tomorrow. Unfortunately, that is the situation, and we cannot change it. This is probably my first European match here.

It is an important and beautiful stadium. There should be a good atmosphere. Given the opposing coach and the teams involved, it should be a good match.

I know their coach, and he knows me as well. It will be a good and entertaining game." Reis said.

Reis also discussed his familiarity with Bulgarian football and explained why domestic competitions do not necessarily reflect the demands of European matches. He noted that European playoff encounters can involve a different tactical approach.

"I am happy to be here. Knowing Bulgarian football is not necessarily a major advantage. Domestic league matches and European matches are different.

Playoff matches can be more tactical. There is a difference between domestic leagues and European competitions. I have not experienced a magical atmosphere in the stadiums during my time here, but I believe we will play in a good atmosphere tomorrow."

Reis said.

The coach highlighted his previous familiarity with Markus Gisdol, who manages CSKA Sofia. Gisdol previously worked at Samsunspor before Reis arrived, giving the Trabzonspor manager an understanding of his opponent's coaching philosophy.

"Markus Gisdol and I know each other very well. He was at Samsunspor before me. I know his style and personality very well.

He tries to build a strong team spirit and win every individual battle. It will be a difficult contest. I also met Markus during the summer holiday."

Reis said.

Reis believes CSKA Sofia have considerable motivation because of their position in the competition. He suggested that the Bulgarian side's need for points could influence the encounter.

"Our opponents need points just as much as we do. Looking at the Conference League standings, they need points more than we do. Perhaps that situation could be an advantage for us.

When I left Ludogorets, they were close to competing for the championship alongside CSKA Sofia." Reis said.
