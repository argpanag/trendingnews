He might have just retired from playing 73 days ago, but Steele Sidebottom has found himself in the AFL coaching space.

Following his time as a panel member of North Melbourne’s search for their next coach after parting ways with Alastair Clarkson, the two-time Premiership player is joining Melbourne as part of Steven King’s coaching staff for the 2027 season.

The recently retired Pie will work as a development coach with the club’s Chief of Football and Innovation, Ned Guy, thrilled to have Sidebottom on board.

"We are thrilled to have someone of Steele's experience and character joining our football program to help develop our players and take their game to another level," Guy said.

"He has been part of successful teams, understands what it takes to perform at the highest level and has a really good understanding of the game.

"Steel is a terrific person who will bring a lot to our group, and we are really looking forward to having him around the club and joining our coaching group."

The Demons begin their 2027 campaign against Essendon on March 21 at the MCG.
