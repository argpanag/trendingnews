Milwaukee Bucks vs Washington Wizards Preview

The Milwaukee Bucks take the court against the Washington Wizards in this regular season debut NBA contest.

Washington enters the new season with a revamped roster featuring Trae Young, Anthony Davis and No. 1 overall draft pick AJ Dybantsa. The Wizards also added Deandre Ayton, Khris Middleton and Tre Mann. Milwaukee begins a new era following the blockbuster trade that sent Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bobby Portis to Miami. In return, the Bucks acquired Tyler Herro, Jaime Jaquez Jr., Kel'el Ware and Kasparas Jakucionis, while also bringing in Caris LeVert from Detroit.

Milwaukee missed the playoffs for the first time in ten years, ending its 2025-26 regular season with a 126-106 road loss to the Philadelphia 76ers at Xfinity Mobile Arena. Cormac Ryan led the Bucks with a double-double of 22 points and ten rebounds, while AJ Green contributed 19 points and Jericho Sims added 15. Green also made five 3-pointers, breaking Ray Allen's franchise record for most 3-pointers in a season.

Washington finished last season with a league-worst 17-65 record, missing the playoffs after closing out its regular season with a 130-117 road defeat to the Cleveland Cavaliers at Rocket Arena. Jamir Watkins scored a career-high 24 points, while Bub Carrington contributed 20 points and nine assists. Sharife Cooper added 20 points and six assists off the bench.

H2H: Wizards Have Won Three Straight Against Bucks

Washington came out on top in the latest meeting with Milwaukee, recording a 109-99 home victory at Capital One Arena. The Wizards have now beaten the Bucks three times in a row, although Milwaukee still holds the edge with six wins in the last ten head-to-head encounters.
