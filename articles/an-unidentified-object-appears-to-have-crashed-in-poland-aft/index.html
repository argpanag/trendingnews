An unidentified object appears to have crashed in Poland after Russian attacks in Ukraine

TheTools · 20.11.2026 11:05 · source · permalink

NOWY LIPOWIEC, Poland (AP) — The Polish military is investigating a "suspected violation" of the country's airspace near its border with Ukraine after officials sought to retrieve an unidentified object that had apparently crash-landed in Poland on Friday morning.

The incident came against the backdrop of Russian attacks in Ukraine.

Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk said he would convene a meeting with government ministers, military commanders and the head of security services in response. Officials said it was too early to tell what the object was.

The potential crash site is near the village of Nowy Lipowiec, roughly 60 kilometers (37 miles) from the Poland-Ukraine border. Nearby schools suspended classes for the day, according to Polish news agency PAP.

Images from closed-circuit cameras and a mobile phone in the region collected by PAP showed a large, loud explosion in a bright flash that appeared to take place in a wooded area.

Military helicopters and rescue vehicles were spotted in the vicinity, and police set up temporary roadblocks as an investigation was underway.

The location is also about 75 kilometers (47 miles) from where thousands of European troops were taking part on Friday in an unprecedented military exercise in southeastern Poland to prepare for a potential postwar mission in Ukraine aimed at helping to stabilize the country and rebuild its military once the war ends.
