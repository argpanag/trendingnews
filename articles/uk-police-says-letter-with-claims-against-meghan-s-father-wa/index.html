UK police says letter with claims against Meghan’s father was not shared with other law enforcement

TheTools · 24.10.2026 17:59 · source · permalink

LONDON (AP) — British police confirmed Saturday that they passed a letter about alleged offending by the father of Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, to her in 2019, but said it did not share details with other law enforcement agencies.
