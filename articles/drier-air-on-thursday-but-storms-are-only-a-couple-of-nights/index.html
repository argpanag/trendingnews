Drier air on Thursday, but storms are only a couple of nights away

  • SHORT TERM: A couple of nice days
  • LONG TERM: Stormier at times

CEDAR RAPIDS, Iowa (KCRG) - It’ll feel much more comfortable for a couple of days in eastern Iowa.

Drier air is flowing into eastern Iowa. If your dew point hasn’t dropped yet, it will overnight. By Thursday morning, those readings will be down in the 50s. With clear skies and light winds tonight, our temperatures will follow down the same path. They’ll hit the low to mid 50s north and upper 50s to low 60s south.

Thursday truly looks like an excellent day in our weather, with comfortable humidity levels, pleasantly warm temperatures, and lots of sunshine. Winds remain light and northerly throughout the day, too. Highs will reach the upper 70s to low 80s. If you’re looking for a day to get outside and enjoy, this is the one.

Another cool night is likely for Thursday night as winds remain light and skies generally clear. Lows head again for the low to mid 50s in many places. A subtle shift in our winds by Friday, however, will start a process of change that puts us back toward active weather.

Fortunately, that change really doesn’t kick in until later Friday night, so the last day of the work and school week should be in good shape. Dew points will be a touch higher in the low to mid 60s, adding a bit of mugginess to the air. Still, with highs in the low 80s, this won’t make for extreme heat for week 3 of high school football in Iowa.

By later Friday night, however, a cold front and associated storm system will act upon that moisture and produce some showers and storms. This chance likely holds off until later at night, and the storms will likely be in a weakening phase compared to when they develop to our northwest. Still, some heavier rainfall could occur as they move through.

This activity hangs around very early on Sunday, but most of the daytime will be dry. That includes Saturday evening, when the big Iowa-Iowa State Cy-Hawk game takes place in Iowa City. Temperatures will stay modest, too, with highs in the upper 70s to low 80s. Gametime temperature could be in the upper 60s by the end of the game.

By Sunday, some scattered showers and storms are possible again. This will be mainly focused on the afternoon and evening hours. The chances will continue for the start of the following week, with Monday afternoon into Monday night another potentially wet time frame.

For fans of cooler weather, the good news is that temperatures will mostly hang closer to seasonal averages next week. Highs will be in the 70s, with lows in the mid 50s to mid 60s. A few storm chances return next Wednesday and Thursday.
