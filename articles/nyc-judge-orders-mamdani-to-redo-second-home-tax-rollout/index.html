NYC judge orders Mamdani to redo second-home tax rollout

TheTools · 30.09.2026 00:09 · source · permalink

Judge ruled Mamdani's administration must void previously mailed second-home tax notices and redo the rollout after finding due process violations

Mamdani administration files appeal against pied-à-terre tax halt

Constitutional law attorney Jonathan Turley joins ‘America’s Newsroom’ to break down the legal battle over New York City’s controversial pied-à-terre tax after a judge blocked its implementation.

A New York judge on Tuesday canceled previously mailed notices for Mayor Zohran Mamdani’s second-home tax, ruling that the city failed to follow required procedures before mailing the notices.

Judge Wayne Ozzi found that the notices were "arbitrary and capricious, affected by errors of law, and in violation of the recipients’ due process rights," according to the ruling.

However, Ozzi added that the city can still apply the tax by removing the current list of taxed properties from its website and posting a corrected list after following the proper procedures.

New notices must then be mailed in compliance with the law.
