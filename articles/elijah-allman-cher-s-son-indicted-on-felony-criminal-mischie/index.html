Elijah Allman, Cher's son, indicted on felony criminal mischief charge

TheTools · 15.09.2026 20:18 · source · permalink

Elijah Blue Allman, Cher's son, has been indicted on a felony criminal mischief charge in connection with an alleged break-in at a Windham home earlier this year.

Allman was arrested on March 1 after investigators say he broke into a home in Windham.

Police say that Allman broke into the home by smashing a glass panel in a back door. According to an affidavit, a woman inside the home called 911 while hiding in a closet.

Officers said they found Allman sitting on a couch inside the home and smoking a cigarette. He has pleaded not guilty to charges including criminal mischief, burglary and breach of bail.

The grand jury's indictment on the criminal mischief charge states that Allman "purposely damaged the property of (the alleged victim) by breaking a glass window."

News 9 previously reported that proceedings in the Windham case would remain on hold while issues surrounding Allman's legal representation were resolved and while he remained hospitalized. A judge ruled on July 13 that hearings would be paused until Allman is released from the New Hampshire State Hospital.

An arraignment date on the felony criminal mischief charge has been set for next month.

The alleged break-in in Windham came two days after an incident at a school in Concord, where Allman was accused of posing as the parent of a prospective student, acting "belligerently" and poking one student with his cane.

Allman faces charges of simple assault, criminal trespass, criminal threatening and disorderly conduct in connection with the Concord arrest.

A competency hearing is scheduled for later this fall in the Concord case.
