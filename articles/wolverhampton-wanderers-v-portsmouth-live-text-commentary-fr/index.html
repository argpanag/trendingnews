  16. Pick of the statspublished at 19:42 BST
    19:42 BST

    Wolves v Portsmouth (19:45 BST)

    • Wolverhampton Wanderers and Portsmouth last faced one another in the 2009-10 Premier League season, when Pompey completed a league double over Wolves.
    • Portsmouth are unbeaten in six league meetings with Wolves (W3 D3) since a 3-2 loss in January 2002.
    • Wolves lost their last home league game 1-3 to Bolton, but haven't suffered successive home defeats in the Championship since February 2017 (3 in a row).
    • Portsmouth are winless in seven away league games against newly relegated opponents (D3 L4) since a 3-2 victory at Reading in League One in October 2023.
    • Portsmouth have won just one of their past 15 midweek (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday) away league games (D2 L12), beating Charlton 3-1 in February.

  19. A potential statement win for Pompey on offerpublished at 19:38 BST
    19:38 BST

    Wolves v Portsmouth (19:45 BST)

    Things are going a little better for Portsmouth as of late.

    After starting their campaign with three losses in four matches, they have only endured one defeat in their following six (W3 D2) to rise up the table.

    However, of those six matches, only one opponent is currently in the top eight with today's hosts Wolves and that's Southampton - Pompey's South Coast rivals who beat them 1-0 in a dramatic derby earlier this month.

    Plus only one of their wins was against a team above them on the table, Sheffield United. A win helped along by a 44th minute red card for their South Yorkshire opponents.

    Victory in the West Midlands tonight would prove Portsmouth are a real force to be reckoned with - and might just fill the travelling support's long journey home with dreams of a better campaign than the past couple.
