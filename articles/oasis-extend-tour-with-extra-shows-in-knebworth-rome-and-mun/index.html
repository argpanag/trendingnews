Oasis extend tour with extra shows in Knebworth, Rome and Munich

TheTools · 26.09.2026 12:47 · source · permalink

Brothers Liam (left) and Noel Gallagher reformed the band for a tour in 2025, having split up in 2009

  • Published
    23 September 2026

Oasis have announced more dates for the second leg of their reunion tour in 2027.

The band will now play six nights at Knebworth House in Hertfordshire, the 125,000-capacity venue where they staged two now legendary concerts in 1996.

They have also added shows in Munich and Rome, while their Paris dates have been rescheduled because of planned railway engineering works.

Tickets will not go on general sale. Instead, purchase links will be sent to fans who have already entered an online ballot.

Oasis will now play 42 shows in 2027, one more than during the first leg of their reunion tour in 2025.

The band had been due to play at the Stade de France in Paris on 23 and 24 July, but the first concert has been brought forward to 20 July.

They have also added dates in Munich's Allianz Arena and at Rome's Stadio Olimpico, taking the total number of shows at each venue from two to three.

Liam Gallagher played Knebworth with Oasis in 1996 and then returned as a solo artist in 2022

The band said standard ticket prices across the tour would be fixed at between £71.50 and £266.60.

For the Knebworth shows, general admission tickets start at £170.50, while front-standing tickets cost from £203.50.

If all six concerts sell out at full capacity, Oasis could sell 750,000 tickets at Knebworth, making it the world's fourth most-attended concert series at a single venue, behind Taylor Swift, Shakira and Coldplay.

Announcing the new dates online, the band said the extra dates were added due to "phenomenal demand".

Knebworth has become known for heavy traffic before and after concerts, as a result efforts have been made to reduce congestion during the Oasis shows.

Stevenage Borough Council said it was working with Hertfordshire County Council and highways officials on a "masterplan" to tackle potential problems.

Ticketmaster has advised Oasis fans, external not to drive, and has arranged return coaches from 124 locations across England and Wales.

Extra trains will run from Stevenage to London after the concerts, while stewards and lighting will be provided along the two-mile (3.2km) route between the station and Knebworth House.

Saturday ticket holders will also have the option to pay extra to camp at the venue on the Friday and Saturday nights.
