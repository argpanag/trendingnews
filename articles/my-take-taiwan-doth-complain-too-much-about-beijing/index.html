My Take | Taiwan doth complain too much about Beijing

TheTools · 08.11.2026 10:16 · source · permalink

Despite being invited to join the Apec leaders’ summit in Shenzhen, Taipei says the mainland has breached protocol

Give Taiwanese president William Lai Ching-te and his Democratic Progressive Party-led government an inch and they’ll take a mile. For more than a year, they demanded to take part as an “equal” in the Apec leaders’ summit in Shenzhen later this month. Now they have been invited. Yet they complain that the invitation is not polite enough and breaches diplomatic protocol.

“Whenever they breach the established rules or diplomatic conventions, we will certainly lodge a protest at the earliest opportunity,” Taiwanese foreign minister Lin Chia-lung told reporters.

What protocol? The island is not a nation; it is part of China. How can Beijing break protocol in whatever way it sees fit to communicate with one of its provinces?
