A shootout to finish top of the group and secure automatic promotion to League A sees Hungary take on Northern Ireland.

Kick-off date/time: 17 November 2026 at 7:45 pm

How to watch on TV: UK – BBC One NI, BBC Three / USA – FOX Sports

How to watch online: UK – BBC iPlayer, BBC Sport website / USA – FOX Sports streaming platforms

Key stats

  • The sides drew 0-0 in Belfast on Matchday 2 in their first UEFA Nations League meeting. Northern Ireland's only win in the eight meetings between the sides (D2 L5) came in Budapest, 2-1 in EURO 2016 qualifying in September 2014.
  • Northern Ireland need a draw to finish top of the group. Hungary must win to overtake them.
  • Hungary have won their last three UEFA Nations League matches, equalling their longest winning run in the competition.
  • Hungary's 1-0 win against Georgia in October was their first UEFA Nations League home win since a 1-0 victory against England in June 2022, ending a run of seven home matches without a win (D4 L3).
  • A win would give Hungary 13 points, their best total in a UEFA Nations League group. Their previous best was 11, in 2020/21.
  • Northern Ireland are unbeaten in their last nine UEFA Nations League matches (W5 D4), their longest unbeaten run in the competition.
  • Northern Ireland have kept clean sheets in all five of their matches in this edition. Their previous longest run of clean sheets in the competition was three.
  • Northern Ireland's 1-0 win in Georgia on Matchday 1 was their first in League B of the UEFA Nations League, at the 11th attempt.
  • Northern Ireland have won both of their away matches in this edition without conceding.
  • Northern Ireland's 11 points equal their best total in a UEFA Nations League group, from 2024/25.

Hungary vs Northern Ireland prediction

Northern Ireland have been one of the stories of this season's Nations League. If they can keep their unbeaten record in Group 2 of League B intact, they will seal a historic promotion to League A ahead of opponents Hungary. To do that, though, they'll have to get the job done in enemy territory, with a ferocious atmosphere awaiting them at the Puskas Arena.

Hungary have arguably underwhelmed in a group many expected them to win, but they have still racked up ten points. Victory here would see them achieve their goal of winning the group and sealing promotion back to League A.

Breaking down this Northern Ireland side is notoriously hard, however. Michael O'Neill's men kept yet another clean sheet this past weekend, beating Ukraine 1-0 at Windsor Park, and they are the only team in League B yet to concede a goal. Hungary will need to find a way past the green and white wall. As has been the case in most fixtures across this particular group, goals are likely to be scarce, and backing under 2.5 of them would be the sensible move in this one.

Hungary vs Northern Ireland tip: Under 2.5 goals
