  3. A first-time men's champion guaranteedpublished at 00:11 BST 13 September
    00:11 BST 13 September

    Men's final: Zverev v Shelton (Sunday, 19:00 BST)

    On Sunday, the men's final between Alexander Zverev and Ben Shelton will bring the curtain down on this year's US Open.

    Zverev ended his long wait for a maiden Grand Slam title by winning the French Open in June and then lost to Jannik Sinner in the Wimbledon final a few weeks later.

    The German is now into a third successive major final and will play in the New York showpiece for the second time in his career.

    In 2020, Zverev won the first two sets but eventually lost to Dominic Thiem in a fifth-set tie-break.

    Big-serving Shelton will attempt to end a 23-year wait for an American men's singles champion at the US Open, with Andy Roddick the most recent home winner of the men's event in 2003.

    Shelton, who beat fellow American Frances Tiafoe in his semi-final, will be appearing in a Grand Slam final for the first time in his career.

    There will be live text coverage of Sunday's final on the BBC Sport website and app, with radio coverage on BBC Radio 5 Live and BBC Radio 5 Sports Extra.

    "Lastly, I want to say thank you to all people who made this tournament possible. It's an amazing event and I can't find the words. This atmosphere is just something unbelievable and it doesn't feel real for now. Thank you so much. I'm looking forward to coming back next year."
