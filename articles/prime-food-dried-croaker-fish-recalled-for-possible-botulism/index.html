Prime Food Dried Croaker Fish Recalled For Possible Botulism

TheTools · 24.11.2026 05:21 · source · permalink

Prime Food Dried Croaker Fish is being recalled for possible botulism contamination. This fish was not properly eviscerated before being packaged for sale. The sale of uneviscerated fish is banned because Clostridium botulinum spores are more likely to be concentrated in that area of the fish than any other portion. No illnesses have been reported to the company to date in connection with the consumption of this product. The recalling firm is Prime Food Processing of Brooklyn, New York.

Both recalled products are Prime Food Dried Croaker Fish sold in 7 oz. (198 g) clear plastic packages. You can see more pictures of the recalled products on the FDA website.

Item AF1340, lot 24302, expires 06/24/27 and has orange and green trim. It was distributed to Asian grocery stores in California, Connecticut, Florida, Georgia, Hawaii, Illinois, Kansas, Massachusetts, Michigan, Maryland, Missouri, North Carolina, New Jersey, Nevada, New York, Oklahoma, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Tennessee, Texas, and Washington.

Item AF1420, lot 24215, expires 02/03/27 and has blue and green trim. It was distributed to Asian grocery stores in Arizona, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Indiana, Massachusetts, Maryland, Michigan, Missouri, North Carolina, New Jersey, New York, Ohio, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Tennessee, Texas, and Washington.

The issue was identified and the recall triggered during routine testing that was conducted by the New York Department of Agriculture and Markets. The problem was with the fish itself, which was processed in Vietnam.

If you bought this product, do not eat it. You can throw it away in a secure garbage can after first double bagging it so other people can’t see it, or you can take it back to the store where you purchased it for a full refund.

If you ate this product, monitor your health for the symptoms of botulism poisoning for the next 18 to 36 hours. If you do experience these symptoms, get to an emergency room because this is a medical emergency.
