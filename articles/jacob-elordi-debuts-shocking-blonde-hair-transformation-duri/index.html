Jacob Elordi Debuts Shocking Blonde Hair Transformation During Intimate Date Night With Kendall Jenner

TheTools · 08.11.2026 17:42 · source · permalink

Jacob Elordi ditched his signature brunette locks for a blonde hair transformation, debuting his new look while out for a casual date night with Kendall Jenner Nov. 7.

Keeping up with Jacob Elordi might prove difficult after his latest transformation. 

The Wuthering Heights star enjoyed a date night with Kendall Jenner in New York City Nov. 7, where he showed off a new blonde hairdo, as seen in photos obtained by DeuxMoi.  

In one snap, Elordi, 29, sat across from Jenner, 31, as the pair enjoyed a few drinks before attending Broadway’s Paranormal Activity. Keeping it low key, the Euphoria alum donned a black long sleeve top and matching beanie.

But it was the tuft of blonde hair peeking out of the accessory that turned heads.

During the outing, Elordi appeared to have ditched his signature brunette locks for a much lighter color, with the dye job also appearing to take up residence on his eyebrows. 

Meanwhile, Jenner kept it classy in a black trench coat. 

Yet, this wouldn’t be the first time the Kissing Booth alum has rocked a new look in the reality star’s presence. After all, Elordi proved during a Los Angeles lunch outing with Jenner in September that when it comes to growing facial hair, he does amazing, sweetie.

In one snap of the pair leaving a Los Angeles hotspot, Elordi—dressed in an all-black ensemble paired with sunglasses and a backwards hat—showed off an unexpected goatee, which he has since swapped for a clean-shaven look. 

And while it remains to be seen whether Elordi’s makeover is tied to a new role, undergoing a dramatic transformation for the big screen is nothing new for the Oscar nominee.

In fact, Guillermo del Toro told E! News last year that Elordi “took around 10 hours every day” to become the Creature in the filmmaker’s 2025 adaptation of Frankenstein, adding that he had “about 42 pieces of makeup, prosthetics. And then he would have to work.”

And it’s safe to say that all the hours Elordi put into the transformation didn’t go unnoticed, with del Toro noting how “he did it all with grace and patience and love.”

But that doesn’t mean the process was an easy one. 

“Your skin gets washed out and covered with something new,” Elordi told Netflix’s Tudum last November. “Your face, and its structure, change. Then you put the costume on and there’s leather and furs, and the weight—it’s all real.”

However, it was during those long hours spent in hair and makeup that Elordi underwent an equally significant internal transformation.

“From the moment I got into the makeup trailer [the performance] began, and it was alive,” he explained. “Guillermo said this would be not just a meditation, but a metamorphosis.”
