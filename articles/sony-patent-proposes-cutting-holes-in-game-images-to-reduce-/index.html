Sony patent proposes cutting holes in game images to reduce streaming data

TheTools · 30.10.2026 17:29 · source · permalink

Sony Interactive Entertainment Europe has filed a patent application for removing parts of game images before transmission and reconstructing them with machine learning.

The application, published October 29 and spotted by Patentlyze, describes cloud gaming as one use. It is not an announced PlayStation feature.

Sony already offers cloud streaming on PlayStation Portal for eligible PlayStation Plus Premium members, including select digital PS5 games from their libraries. That service lets players stream without connecting to their own PS5, but the filing does not establish that this technique runs on Portal.

Figures 3 and 4: grids and importance maps. Image: Sony Interactive Entertainment / USPTO

Two models would remove and reconstruct image detail

The sender’s model chooses image regions to remove before compression and transmission. It inserts small pieces of representative detail, called anchor elements, into some gaps. A reconstruction model at the receiving device uses those anchors and the remaining image to reconstruct missing areas.

The anchors are intended to reduce invented detail and preserve fidelity. Sony also describes VR or mixed reality devices as possible receivers.

The proposal offers no measured bandwidth saving or guaranteed reduction in input lag. It does not confirm a product launch or deployment on a future console.
