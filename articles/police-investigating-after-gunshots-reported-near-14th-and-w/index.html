Police investigating after gunshots reported near 14th and Western late Wednesday

TheTools · 29.10.2026 16:08 · source · permalink

SIOUX FALLS, S.D. (Dakota News Now) - The Sioux Falls Police Department is investigating after gunshots were reported and a house was hit by gunfire in central Sioux Falls late Wednesday, Oct. 28.

The Sioux Falls Police Department received multiple calls reporting gunshots in the area of 14th Street and Western Avenue around 9:15 pm on Wednesday.

Officers on the scene found shell casings in the street, and one home had damage to the siding. Multiple witnesses also reported seeing some vehicles leave the area after the shots were fired.

There have been no arrests made in this case as police work to identify the suspects.

Anyone with information should contact the Sioux Falls Police Department at 605-367-7000.

You can also submit a tip to Crime Stoppers or call Crime Stoppers at 605-367-7007.
