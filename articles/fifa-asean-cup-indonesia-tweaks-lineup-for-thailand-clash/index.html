FIFA ASEAN Cup: Indonesia Tweaks Lineup for Thailand Clash

TheTools · 05.10.2026 13:12 · source · permalink

Reporter

Antara

October 5, 2026 | 08:12 pm

TEMPO.CO, Jakarta - Indonesia made slight lineup adjustments when facing Thailand in the 2026 FIFA ASEAN Cup final at Gelora Bung Karno Stadium, Jakarta, on Monday night.

On this occasion, Indonesia national team coach John Herdman adjusted the lineup compared to the match against Bangladesh.

As reported by ANTARA, the English coach once again fielded senior midfielder Thom Haye, who had previously been sidelined due to a red card suspension.

Additionally, Luke Vickery was named to the starting lineup once again, while center-back Rizky Ridho started on the bench.

John Herdman opted for a 3-4-3 formation, and the goalkeeping duties were once again entrusted to Emil Audero, the goalkeeper for the Spanish club Rayo Vallecano.

Meanwhile, Kevin Diks, Elkan Baggott, and Justin Hubner were entrusted with Indonesia’s three-man central defense.

In midfield, Calvin Verdonk and Luke Vickery occupy the two wing-back positions, and Joey Pelupessy and Thom Haye are in the central midfield.

The two winger positions in the attacking trio are entrusted to Ragnar Oratmangoen and Ole Romeny, who support the towering forward, Mitchell Lee Baker.

Indonesia vs Thailand Lineups

Indonesia (3-4-3): Emil Audero; Kevin Diks, Elkan Baggott, Justin Hubner; Calvin Verdonk, Joey Pelupessy, Thom Haye, Luke Vickery; Ragnar Oratmangoen, Mitchell Baker, Ole Romeny.

Coach: John Herdman (England)

Thailand (4-2-3-1): Saranon Anuin; Nattapong Sayriya, Manuel Bihr, Nicholas Mickelson, Suphanan Bureerat; Sarch Yooyen, Kritsada Kaman; Kakana Khamyok, Supachai Chaided, Chanathip Songkrasin; Teerasak Poeiphimai.

Coach: Anthony Hudson (England)

Referee: Akhrol Risqullaev (Uzbekistan)
