Orlando Magic's Sean Sweeney Challenges Jalen Suggs For Improvement

TheTools · 23.10.2026 12:00 · source · permalink

After a shaky season opener, Coach Sweeney demands defensive discipline and offensive refinement as the Magic prepare to battle a star-studded Timberwolves lineup in their home debut.

Following the Orlando Magic dropping its season opener to the Atlanta Hawks, they have a chance to get into the win column for the first time this season with a home game versus the Minnesota Timberwolves.

Now with the superstar duo of Anthony Edwards and LaMelo Ball, the Timberwolves will be a tough game to win.

Where the Magic could take the first step for improvement is at guard with the play of Jalen Suggs, which head coach Sean Sweeney called out, saying Suggs knows he has to improve on a few things for the next game.

As the Magic look forward to the home matchup, here’s what Sweeney had to say after practice:

Reviewing the film from the season opener against Atlanta: Sweeney acknowledged that the Magic showed positive stretches on both ends of the floor but identified several areas needing improvement. He emphasized the importance of correcting defensive principles and schemes while building on the offensive execution the team displayed at times throughout the game.

Defensive breakdowns and fouling shooters: Sweeney identified transition defense, individual positioning and communication as key areas needing improvement. He emphasized the importance of getting set defensively, maintaining proper positioning and contesting shots vertically to avoid unnecessary fouls. He added that the team must improve its awareness and discipline when closing out on shooters.

The team's offensive turnovers: Sweeney attributed some of Orlando's turnovers to poor spacing, positioning and decision-making when attacking. While he believed the team's intentions were positive and players were attempting to make the right plays, he stressed the need for better execution to limit mistakes.

Addressing early-season mistakes and adjusting to regular-season intensity: Sweeney emphasized the importance of correcting mistakes early while acknowledging that adjustments are part of the learning process. He noted that the increased speed, intensity and playing time of regular-season basketball can lead to fatigue and distractions. He stressed that players must remain focused on each possession rather than allowing the scoreboard, crowd or fatigue to influence their approach.

Anthony Black's participation in practice: Sweeney confirmed that Black fully participated in practice and was working hard. He also praised Black's shooting during the session, noting that he looked comfortable and was consistently making jumpers.

Jalen Suggs' shooting struggles and offensive importance: Sweeney emphasized Suggs' importance to the team's success, acknowledging that he contributed positively in several areas despite some struggles. He said Suggs understands the adjustments he needs to make and stressed that his development and execution remain important to Orlando's overall improvement.

The team's receptiveness to film review and corrections: Sweeney praised the players' willingness to learn from their mistakes and make adjustments following the season opener. He highlighted the team's positive approach to preparation, emphasizing that players have consistently shown a desire to improve while reinforcing the habits and principles that have worked well.

Playing distracting noises during free-throw practice: Sweeney joked that he could not take credit for the unusual sounds played during free-throw practice, suggesting that assistant coach Mo may have been responsible for playing them through his Apple Watch. He added that the intention may have been to challenge the players and motivate them to shoot free throws with greater focus.

His early impressions of the Minnesota Timberwolves: Sweeney praised Minnesota's offensive performance in its previous game, particularly its perimeter shooting and willingness to attempt 3-pointers. He highlighted the offensive aggressiveness of Anthony Edwards and LaMelo Ball while acknowledging the Timberwolves' playoff experience and the challenge they present for Orlando.

Preparing for Minnesota's new-look roster featuring Anthony Edwards and LaMelo Ball: Sweeney emphasized the importance of studying Minnesota's offensive tendencies and defensive schemes to identify areas Orlando can exploit. He highlighted Rudy Gobert's presence as a dominant rim protector who presents a unique challenge offensively. Sweeney stressed that the Magic must focus on limiting Minnesota's strengths while consistently executing their own game plan.
