Juninho (35, Ketch SC) vowed to win away to Gangwon FC. Juninho is a Brazilian striker who acquired his Hong Kong passport in 2023 and is also playing for the Hong Kong national team.

Kitch will face Gangwon in Group H of the 2026-27 Asian Football Confederation (AFC) Champions League Two (ACL2) group stage at 7 p.m. on November 26 at Gangneung High1 Arena in Gangwon Province.

Kitch has one win, two draws and one loss (five points) in four ACL2 group matches this season. Kitch is second in Group H, one point ahead of Gangwon.

Juninho attended a press conference held at Gangneung High1 Arena on behalf of the Kitch squad on the 25th.

Juninho said, "It won't be easy as it's an away game," but added, "Our goal is three points."

"We thoroughly prepared for the match against Gangwon before heading to Korea. He added, "I feel like I'm well prepared."

Juninho is also a player with deep ties to Korean soccer.

Juninho played 83 minutes as a Hong Kong national striker in the 2025 East Asian Football Federation (EAFF) E-1 Championship Korea match held at Yongin Mir Stadium on July 11, 2025. At that time, South Korea beat Hong Kong 2-0.

Juninho is also deeply connected with coach Kim Dong-jin, who was the coach of the Korean national team at the time. Coach Kim received guidance for the 2023-24 season, when he served as a coach and acting coach at Kitch.

Juninho also shared a team-up with striker Kim Shin-wook, a former member of the Korean national team, during this season.

Juninho said, "I've been with coach Kim Dong-jin for a year," adding, "When the coach comes to Hong Kong, we meet separately and talk."

"When I was with coach Kim Dong-jin, the team situation was not good. It was a difficult time. I'm sorry that my grades weren't good, but I remember getting help in a difficult situation," he said.

Juninho also said of Kim Shin-wook, "Of course, I remember him. However, he couldn't play many games with Kim Shin-wook. The time together was not long due to Kim Shin-wook's injury. Still, I am well aware of his career and ability. I remember him as a good player."

[Gangneung = Lee Geun-seung, MK Sports Reporter]
