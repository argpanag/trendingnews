Dellilo Rises to Director of Marketing and Promotions for iHeartMedia New York

TheTools · 11.11.2026 16:16 · source · permalink

iHeartMedia New York ups Danielle Dellilo to director of marketing and promotions. New York EVP of programming Thea Mitchem says, “We’re incredibly excited to have Danielle step into this role. Her leadership, infectious enthusiasm and ability to make things happen have made her a go-to for our teams, talent and sales partners. I’m incredibly proud of the leader she has become over the past 10 years. Danielle has earned this opportunity, and we’re thrilled to have her leading marketing and promotions for our legendary New York brands and talent.” The cluster includes news/talk WOR and The Black Information Network WWRL in addition to five music brands.
