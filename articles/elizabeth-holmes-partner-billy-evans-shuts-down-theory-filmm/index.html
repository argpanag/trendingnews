Billy Evans is weighing in on a viral You Can See Everything take about his partner Elizabeth Holmes.

Warning: spoilers for You Can See Everything follow.

"Do you think that Nathan Fielder is infatuated with your wife?" Matt Gutman of CBS News Sunday Morning asked Evans, 33, in a clip from their upcoming interview, shared on Instagram on Friday, Nov. 13.

Fielder, 43, co-directed the A24 documentary (in theaters now) about Holmes, sharing conversations with the disgraced Theranos founder on camera in the days before she began her prison sentence on May 30, 2023.

"I think a lot of people are infatuated with Elizabeth Holmes," answered Evans in the clip. "I think you're taking things maybe too literally."

Nathan Fielder and Elizabeth Holmes in 'You Can See Everything'
Credit: Courtesy of A24

When Gutman, 48, insisted that Fielder's behavior "seemed romantically leaning" toward Holmes, 42, and that "everybody came away from that film thinking that he's either in love with her or has extraordinarily strong feelings that seem to be romantic," Evans disagreed.

"I think Nathan cares a lot about Elizabeth," said the You Can See Everything subject. "I didn't get the impression for a minute that it was romantic."

"Come on," said Gutman, referring to a scene at the end of the documentary that shows Fielder and Holmes, as he put it, "frolicking in the water."

"I was five feet to the right of them, also in the water," said Evans.

"In some ways, this is a love poem to her," Gutman posited.

Evans replied, "That's an interesting idea. I haven't thought about that."

Elizabeth Holmes and Billy Evans in 'You Can See Everything'
Credit: Lance Oppenheim/A24

You Can See Everything, co-directed by Fielder and Lance Oppenheim, offers a revealing glimpse into Holmes' home life in the time before she began her sentence at a federal prison in Texas.

Later in the film, Amanda Seyfried, who played Holmes in the 2022 Hulu series The Dropout, joins Fielder on camera to stage reenactments, untangle the businesswoman's claims and even meet Evans and the two kids he shares with Holmes.

It's Seyfried, 40, who suggests Fielder has feelings for Holmes during a conversation at the end of the film. When the Nathan For You creator expresses uncertainty, the actress acknowledges, "It's very complicated."

Holmes gained notoriety for the founding and collapse of Theranos, a billion-dollar tech start-up that promised to revolutionize the health care industry with cheap diagnostic testing and devices able to screen patients for hundreds of diseases with just a few drops of blood. But the technology didn't work.

The former Silicon Valley star was ultimately convicted for multiple counts of fraud and conspiracy and is now serving an 11-year sentence (reduced to nine years for good behavior) in prison.

Her sentence was previously reduced by two years for good behavior, and her release date has been rescheduled to February 22, 2030, per Bureau of Prisons records. She will soon move to a halfway house, the Austin Transitional Center in Del Valle, Texas, in August 2027.
