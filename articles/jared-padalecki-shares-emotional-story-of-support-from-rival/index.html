Twenty-five years ago, Jared Padalecki and Milo Ventimiglia played rivals on Gilmore Girls, as their characters fought for the heart of Rory Gilmore (Alexis Bledel). But behind the scenes, the two actors hit it off from the jump.

In the new documentary Drink Coffee, Talk Fast: A Documentary About Gilmore Girls, Padalecki, who played Dean Forester on the long-running drama series, revealed that he formed a friendship with Ventimiglia as soon as the actor joined the cast as Jess Mariano during the show's second season in 2001.

"When Milo Ventimiglia came and played Jess on the show, our characters were very obviously set up to be rivals," Padalecki said, per Entertainment Weekly. "He was kinda set up to be the guy replacing me, you know. And he didn't come in like, Hey, I'm the boyfriend now.' He came in like, 'Hey, dude. You wanna go…there's a gym…want to go work out? Or want to sit and chat? Or go for a walk?"

John Shearer / Getty Images

Padalecki revealed that before Ventimiglia, who is five years older than him, joined the Gilmore Girls cast, he would retreat to his trailer between scenes and just wait to be called to film.

"Milo took me under his wing and was kind to me every step of the way. Makes me kind of emotional," Padalecki said as he wiped tears from his eyes. "I was still concerned about being able to make a career and this and that, and he was just there and supportive and like, 'I gotcha buddy.' It meant a lot."

Over the years, Gilmore Girls fans have been passionate about which "team" they are on regarding Rory's love life.

In a February 2026 interview with People magazine, Ventimiglia, 49, confirmed he'd be fine if his own daughter Ke'ala grew up to be "Team Dean."

"I'd be way okay if my daughter eventually watched the show and she was team Dean," Ventimiglia said. "I get it because I'm team Jared, so I get it."

He also described Padalecki and Matt Czuchry (who played Rory's later college boyfriend, Logan Huntzberger) as "the best guys."

Last year, Padalecki told People that he would root for Ventimiglia's character.

"I'm team Jess, funny enough," he said, explaining that "knowing the show and having some idea of where Rory was in her life," he understood why she left his character for the Stars Hollow bad boy played by Ventimiglia.

"They all were flawed, let's be clear," the Gilmore Girls alum noted. "Well, I'll say Dean was flawed. They all had their issues, but today I'm team Jess."

Drink Coffee, Talk Fast can be streamed now on Apple, Amazon Prime Video and Google/YouTube.
