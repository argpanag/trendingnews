'GTA VI' Lucia Actor Manni L. Perez Shares Thanksgiving Message to Fans: 'I Am Beyond Grateful'

TheTools · 28.11.2026 01:17 · source · permalink

She added that she spent "almost a decade bringing Lucia to life."

In a post shared on her Instagram, Manni L. Perez reflected on bringing Grand Theft Auto VI protagonist Lucia Caminos to life and thanked all the fans for showing love.

On Thursday (Nov. 26), Perez celebrated Thanksgiving with a post showing her appreciation for the positive reception to her performance in the game and the “whirlwind” she’s been through ever since the game dropped this month.

“Life has been such a whirlwind these past couple of days. I just want to take a moment to say thank you,” she wrote on her Instagram Stories.

“Thank you to all the new followers, to all the beautiful messages, and the positive reception the game and my character have received,” she continued. “I’ve dedicated almost a decade bringing Lucia to life. She’s been such a significant part of mine and I am beyond grateful she now gets to be a small part of yours. Happy holidays y’all.”

When the game dropped last week, the actors behind the two leads in the game, which is undoubtedly one of the biggest pop culture events of all time, celebrated all the craft that went into the project.

“We are truly honored to collaborate on this worldwide testament to human creativity, ingenuity, and sheer willpower,” wrote Perez and Dylan Rourke, who provided the voice and motion capture for Lucia and Jason, respectively. “So much learned and so much to learn. Keep fighting for what you love. Stay humble. See y’all in Leonida.”

The long-awaited arrival of GTA VI also revealed that Victoria Lee served as the main stunt double for Lucia.

“After keeping my mouth shut for years (and what feels like forever), I can finally share that I've been lucky enough to help bring Lucia to life as her main stunt double in Grand Theft Auto VI, along with a whole bunch of other characters’ stunts along the way!” Lee captioned a post sharing the news on Instagram.
