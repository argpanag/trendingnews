AKPS confirms arrest of officer following whistle-blowing by subordinate

TheTools · 23.11.2026 13:47 · source · permalink
    bookmark

KUALA LUMPUR: The Malaysian Border Control and Protection Agency (AKPS) has confirmed the arrest of one of its officers following a female officer's move to expose misconduct by her superior.

AKPS director-general Comm Datuk Seri Mohd Shuhaily Mohd Zain said the agency would not shield any personnel involved in corruption or misconduct and was ready to assist police in uncovering a wider network of alleged “counter setting” activities at the country's entry points.

He said AKPS welcomed enforcement action against any personnel involved, stressing that Malaysians had grown tired of such activities.

“Arrest how many you want to arrest. I think we have all had enough. Malaysians have had enough of these kinds of activities.

“I am more than willing to cooperate with the police on this issue,” he said at a press conference during the Sang Perkasa Initiative sponsorship ceremony at Zoo Negara here on Monday (Nov 23).

On the arrest, Comm Mohd Shuhaily said the case was uncovered after a female AKPS officer came forward to report misconduct by her superior.

He said the officer deserved recognition for speaking up despite the possibility of facing pressure within her own organisation.

“I have great respect for her and will meet her soon to personally thank her.

“It only takes one person to change what is happening on the ground. Such courage is the starting point for the changes we want to bring about,” he said.

He also pledged his continued support for the officer and hoped her actions would inspire more AKPS personnel to come forward and report any wrongdoing.

“She went against the tide and I will always stand behind her. That is my promise to her. This was an arrest of our own personnel by our own people,” he said.

However, Comm Mohd Shuhaily declined to elaborate on the modus operandi of the alleged activity, including the possibility of a wider “counter setting” network, as investigations were ongoing.

He said the officer was currently in police custody and expressed hope that the investigation would uncover a wider network behind the alleged activities.

Earlier, a civil servant in his 40s was arrested for allegedly facilitating the entry of a 38-year-old foreign woman without valid travel documents through an Immigration counter at KL International Airport Terminal 2 (KLIA T2).

KLIA OCPD Asst Comm M. Ravi said the case was detected by AKPS before a police report was lodged on Nov 17.

The officer is being investigated under Section 26B(d) of the Anti-Trafficking in Persons and Anti-Smuggling of Migrants Act 2007 (ATIPSOM).

The foreign woman was detained under Section 6(1)(c) of the Immigration Act 1959/63 for entering the country without valid travel documents.
