Leeds United's Dominic Calvert-Lewin given new England reality check after Harry Kane injury

Leeds United's Dominic Calvert-Lewin may selfishly be hoping that Harry Kane is still sidelined when England duty returns.

England's most-capped man suffered an ankle injury in Bayern Munich's 1-1 draw with Koln on Friday

Kane is expected to be out for three months, which would see him return in February if all goes well with his recovery.

The Three Lions are next in action at the end of March, when they face Norway in the UEFA Nations League quarter-finals.

Calvert-Lewin could lead the line if his rival at international level has a setback in his recovery.

The Whites hitman has been firmly Thomas Tuchel's number two option, playing seven minutes in the most recent break.

Tony Cascarino believes that he is not ready to take over a starting role from Kane if the 33-year-old is still missing.

Speaking on the Weekend Sports Breakfast show on talkSPORT on Saturday morning, the host said: “I like Calvert-Lewin, I think he has achieved a hell of a lot with Leeds in the last two seasons.

“To say that he could step in and be like Harry Kane, I think even Calvert-Lewin would admit that is just not possible.”

That is a brutal reality check for the 29-year-old Elland Road ace.

Cascarino's verdict is very harsh, Calvert-Lewin has proved himself to be very capable.

In the Premier League this season, the Peacocks ace has notched four goals and is averaging 1.97 shots, per FotMob data.

Kane is known for his aerial dominance, and Calvert-Lewin is winning 50.6 per cent of his duels in that sphere at moment.

While he does not offer what the former Tottenham man does, the ex-Everton ace is still a very good option for Tuchel to rely on, if necessary.

It is not fair to judge him off three cameos, two against Spain and one against Czechia, which has seen him on the pitch for 22 minutes, and he must be given more time before a verdict like that is handed out.
