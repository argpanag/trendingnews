Full Main Stage Schedule for Evo France 2026 Revealed! Top 8 and Finals for 8 Titles Held at the Same Venue - Saiga NAK

TheTools · 11.10.2026 20:02 · source · permalink

Taking place from Friday, October 9 to Sunday, October 11, 2026 in Nice, France, the full main stage schedule for the fighting game festival, Evo France 2026, has been revealed.
Over the course of three days, the Top 8 and finals for eight titles are all scheduled to take place at the same venue.

Main Stage Schedule for Evo France 2026 Revealed!

On the main stage, the Top 8 for eight titles and the finals will be held over the course of three days from Friday, October 9 to Sunday, October 11, 2026.

In addition, an online stream is also scheduled to take place. The following times are listed in local time (UTC+2).

The main stage schedule for each day is as follows:

Friday, October 9

  • 12:00 PM – 12:45 PM: COSPLAY
  • 1:00 PM – 2:30 PM: VISA FRIDAY SHOWDOWN
  • 3:00 PM – 7:00 PM: Granblue Fantasy Versus: Rising Top 8
  • 7:15 PM – 10:30 PM: Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves Top 8

Saturday, October 10

  • 10:30 AM – 1:45 PM: GUILTY GEAR -STRIVE- Top 8
  • 2:00 PM – 5:30 PM: Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game Top 8
  • 5:45 PM – 9:15 PM: 2XKO Top 8

Sunday, October 11

  • 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM: MARVEL Tōkon: Fighting Souls Top 8
  • 2:15 PM – 6:15 PM: Tekken 8 Top 8
  • 6:30 PM – 10:30 PM: Street Fighter 6 Top 8

For the latest tournament information and details, please check the official Evo France X account (@Evo_France).
