A Buenos Aires embarrassment

TheTools · 04.10.2026 06:36 · source · permalink

On Saturday, Argentina thrashed a Burkina Faso that arrived with under-23 players and reached Buenos Aires just hours before kickoff.

It was an absolute embarrassment, from everything that happened in the buildup to, as expected, the game itself, as Argentina demolished a Burkina Faso side made up of under-23 players recruited at the last minute. The senior team had refused to travel amid disagreements over the match contract, forcing the hastily assembled squad to fly from Casablanca to Buenos Aires and arrive just six hours before kickoff, after an 11-hour journey.

The African side’s starting lineup featured players from Burkina Faso’s domestic league with no international experience and virtually no presence in the major professional soccer databases. The inevitable consequence was three Argentina goals in the opening 11 minutes. Lautaro Martínez converted a penalty, Burkina Faso scored an own goal and then gifted Gianluca Prestianni another.

Lionel Scaloni’s men eased off after building such a commanding early lead, but they still created chances at will. Visiting goalkeeper Abdoul Guira emerged as his team’s standout performer, producing a string of excellent saves to deny Argentina’s biggest stars. Even so, he could not prevent Nico Paz and Julián Alvarez from adding further goals before halftime.

Pace drops in pedestrian second half

The Argentine coach made five changes at the break, giving players such as Franco Mastantuono, Leonardo Balerdi and Leonel Flores their opportunity. The World Cup runners-up remained in complete control. The first of those three added the sixth goal with another comfortable finish from inside the box.

By the 69th minute, Scaloni had replaced all 10 of his outfield starters and the game’s intensity dropped dramatically. The flow of scoring chances also dried up, with the contest settling into a pace far too pedestrian given the attacking talent on the field. Even so, Giuliano Simeone completed the rout right at the death with a back-post header. Despite that late goal, it was a dismal second-half showing from Argentina to bring a forgettable occasion to a close.
