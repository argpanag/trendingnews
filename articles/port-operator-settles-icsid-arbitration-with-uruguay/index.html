Port operator settles ICSID arbitration with Uruguay

TheTools · 09.09.2026 20:45 · source · permalink

A Chilean port operator has agreed to withdraw its US$600 million International Centre for Settlement of Investment Disputes (ICSID) claim against Uruguay over the state’s decision to extend the concession of a rival terminal operator.
