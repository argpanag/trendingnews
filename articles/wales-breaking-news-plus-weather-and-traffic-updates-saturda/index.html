Road remains shut due to crash

Barry Road in Dinas Powys remains closed following a crash earlier on Saturday morning.

According to Inrix: "A4055 Barry Road in both directions closed due to accident between A4231 Barry Docks Link Road (Dinas Powys) and Cross Common Road (Murch). Traffic is coping well. Note change of direction. Sensors show that the carriageway has fully closed again in both directions."

Road in Powys reopens

The A4077 in Powys has reopened after being closed earlier on Saturday morning due to a fallen tree.

According to Inrix: "A4077 Crickhowell Road in both directions re-opened, fallen tree removed from Hillside Road (Llangattock) to Back Road (Gilwern)".

Emotional Children in Need 2026 scenes as Alex Jones reveals staggering total

BBC Children in Need's 2026 Appeal has raised almost £40m with Welsh stars once again playing a vital role in the fundraising.

Viewers tuned in to BBC One on Friday night to see the conclusion of this year's fundraising campaign with a show presented by Alex Jones, Rylan, Sara Cox, Mel Giedroyc, and Tyler West.

The highlight of this year's fundraising for many was the achievement of Ammanford-born TV and radio presenter Owain Wyn Evans. Owain alone raised millions on the Four Nations Cycle.

In total, Children in Need 2026 raised £38.7m.

It was an emotional show(Image: BBC)

Met Office weather maps show heavy snow forecast for Wales

Wales could be in for its first taste of winter snowfall next week, with the latest Met Office forecast maps showing wintry weather affecting areas across the country.

Forecasters are predicting a sharp drop in temperatures this weekend, with patches of snow possible from Monday.

A new cold snap is expected to bring below freezing temperatures this weekend, with overnight lows hovering around -5C in many areas.

The latest predictions suggest the colder conditions could also even bring snow from Monday.

Road shut after crash

Police are at the scene of a crash in Dinas Powys.

A spokesperson for South Wales Police said:

We're at the scene of a collision on Cardiff Road in Dinas Powys, near the junction with the Barry Docks Link Road. The road is currently closed in both directions. It is expected to remain closed for some time; please avoid the area and use alternative routes where possible. We're grateful for your patience.

Main road shut due to fallen tree

A section of the A4077 in Powys is shut in both directions due to a fallen tree.

According to traffic monitoring service Inrix: "A4077 Crickhowell Road in both directions closed due to fallen tree from Hillside Road (Llangattock) to Back Road (Gilwern)."
