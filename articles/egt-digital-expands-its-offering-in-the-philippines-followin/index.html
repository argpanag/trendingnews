EGT Digital expands its offering in the Philippines following new PAGCOR accreditations | SoloAzar International

TheTools · 10.09.2026 12:00 · source · permalink

Thursday 10 de September 2026 / 12:00

EGT Digital

⏱ 2 min read

(Sofia).- EGT Digital is set to significantly expand its offering in the Philippines following new accreditations obtained by EGT Philippines from the Philippine Amusement and Gaming Corporation (PAGCOR), opening the way for a broader range of the company’s products and technology to reach PAGCOR-licensed operators.

The accreditations cover Game Content Provision, including casino games and sports betting, as well as Game Aggregation. They provide the regulatory framework for EGT Digital to bring both its proprietary content and technology solutions to partners in the Philippine market. PAGCOR’s official list of accredited gaming affiliates confirms EGT Philippines as a Game Content Provider for both Electronic Casino Games and Sports Betting and as an accredited Game Aggregator. 

Mariana Manchina, Director of EGT Philippines, commented: “These accreditations are an important milestone for EGT Philippines and reflect our long-term commitment to the local market. They create new opportunities for us to grow our presence, deepen our partnerships with local operators and contribute to the continued development of the regulated gaming sector in the country.”

As an accredited Game Content Provider, EGT Philippines can bring EGT Digital’s in-house developed casino games and Sportsbook solution to PAGCOR-licensed operators. This gives EGT Digital the opportunity to establish a much broader proposition in the country, combining its established gaming content with its advanced sports betting technology. 

The Game Aggregator accreditation further expands EGT Digital’s opportunities in the market, paving the way for the introduction of its aggregation solution. Through a single integration, operators will be able to access a broad portfolio of gaming content from multiple providers, extending EGT Digital’s proposition beyond its proprietary products and enabling partners to diversify their casino offering. 

With the new accreditations in place, EGT Digital can address a significantly wider range of operator needs in the Philippines, bringing together proprietary casino content, Sportsbook technology and game aggregation as part of its growing offering for the regulated market.
