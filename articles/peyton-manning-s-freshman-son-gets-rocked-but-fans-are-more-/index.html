Although, to be fair, Peyton would've spent 40 seconds audibling to the correct protection so he avoided that sort of hit. Marshall will learn. High school football play clocks are only so long.

Marshall Manning (16) watches Baylor set up to face Brentwood Academy during the first half in Chattanooga, Tenn., on Thursday, Aug. 20, 2026. (USA TODAY Network via Reuters Connect)

All in all, it was a fine night for Manning. Baylor cruised to a win, he completed some passes along the way, and finally saw some real playing time behind Ole Miss commit, Keegan Croucher.

See? Nothing special, but still, some experience. That's all you want as a freshman, right? Well, that and a few scholarship offers would be nice ...

Don't worry! Marshall has nothing to worry about on that front.

Believe it or not, the freshman QB already has four Division I offers despite barely playing a down this season. Four!

According to 247 Sports — which tracks these sort of things — Manning has already received offers from Miami, Ohio State, Purdue and Missouri State. Not bad for a 15-year-old.

Not bad for a kid who hasn't even thrown his first high school touchdown yet!
