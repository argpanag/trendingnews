Bayern Munich could lose decades long partner Audi

TheTools · 08.09.2026 22:00 · source · permalink
  • Bavarian Financial Works

Audi currently owns a minority share in FC Bayern Munich.

Bayern Munich could be facing a major restructure in its corporate ownership soon, as its long-term partner Volkswagen AG evaluates a complete exit from its investments in professional football.

Per a report captured by @iMiaSanMia, the Volkswagen Group has agreed on a strategic structural shift that could result in a complete withdrawal from its extensive football investments, potentially ending VW subsidiary Audi’s equity and sponsorship partnership with Bayern. Volkswagen’s plan, approved by their board, includes resolution point 10.9 which explicitly states that equity stakes in professional football clubs no longer align with the carmaker’s core target model. Under this directive, the group is evaluating its entire football portfolio, which includes Audi’s 8.33% equity share in Bayern, Porsche’s 10.4% stake in VfB Stuttgart, Audi’s 19.9% stake in FC Ingolstadt, and full ownership of VfL Wolfsburg. Bayern’s lucrative vehicle sponsorship agreement with Audi runs through 2029, but a preliminary decision regarding future sports marketing commitments is expected next summer, with an extension currently seeming unlikely.

FC Bayern issued a statement reassuring fans that they are extremely satisfied with their long-term alliance with Audi. Even then, the prospect of Volkswagen offloading its minority shares will probably force the Bavarian giants and other affected German clubs to prepare for potential shifts in their corporate shareholder landscapes before the end of the contract.
