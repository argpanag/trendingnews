  1. Join us for day four...published at 16:18 GMT
    16:18 GMT

    An enthralling Test match, in a gripping series, rolls into day four with plenty still to play for...although Australia will be the happier of the two sides.

    The dismissal of South Africa captain Temba Bavuma - bowled by Nathan Lyon after 35 from 115 balls - shortly before the close appears to have swung the momentum the way of the tourists.

    South Africa will resume their second innings on 140-4 on day four - a lead of 40 runs - with Matthew Breetzke and Ryan Rickleton unbeaten on 19 and eight respectively.

    Earlier, Josh Inglis' second Test century had helped Australia post 429 in their first innings while Alex Carey chipped in with 80.

    Simon Harmer finished the pick of the Proteas attack with 4-104 supported by Marco Jansen's 3-60.
