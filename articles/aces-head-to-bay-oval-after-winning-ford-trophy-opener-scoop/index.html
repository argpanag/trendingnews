ACES Head To Bay Oval After Winning Ford Trophy Opener | Scoop News

30 October, 2026

The Auckland ACES travel to Bay Oval to face Northern Districts on Saturday 31 October, following a winning start to their Ford Trophy campaign against Canterbury.

Centuries from Dale Phillips and captain Sean Solia led the way at Eden Park’s Kennards Hire Community Oval on Wednesday 28 October.

Phillips struck 112 from just 95 balls, including 11 fours and six sixes. Solia scored 120 from 119 deliveries, hitting 14 fours and three sixes as both openers reached three figures.

Cam Fletcher added 58 from 43 balls, while Ryan Harrison provided a late burst with an unbeaten 26 from 11.

The ACES then bowled Canterbury out for 311 in 44.1 overs. Angus Olliver and Adi Ashok claimed three wickets apiece, while Solia completed a strong all-round performance with 2/50.

Northern Districts also come into Saturday’s match off a high-scoring win over Central Districts. With both sides finding form with the bat in the opening round, an intriguing contest awaits at Bay Oval as Auckland look to make it two wins from two.
