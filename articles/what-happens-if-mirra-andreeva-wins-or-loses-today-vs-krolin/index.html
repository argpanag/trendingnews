What Happens If Mirra Andreeva Wins or Loses Today vs. Krolina Muchova? WTA Finals Semis Qualification Scenarios Explained

TheTools · 13.11.2026 20:39 · source · permalink

Mirra Andreeva has had an  impressive run in what is her debut in the tournament having secured straight-sets wins over Elina Svitolina and Aryna Sabalenka, booking.

Her 6-4, 6-0 victory over Sabalenka ensured she finished as the Group 2 winner, regardless of the result against Muchova.

The Russian has thus already secured her place in the 2026 WTA Finals semifinals, but she still has one round-robin match remaining against Karolina Muchova today.

Muchova, meanwhile, lost both her matches and is eliminated from semifinal contention. 

She will be looking to end her campaign with a victory after defeats against Sabalenka and Svitolina.

If Andreeva defeats Muchova, she will complete the group stage with a perfect 3-0 record without changing anything else.

The teenager has yet to drop a set in the tournament and will be looking to maintain that record.

Even if the 19-year-old loses to Muchova, she will still advance to the semifinals as the Group 2 winner. Her victories over Sabalenka and Svitolina have already ensured that the top spot belongs to her. 

A defeat would leave Andreeva with a 2-1 record, but neither Sabalenka nor Svitolina can overtake her. Both players are currently 1-1 and will face each other in the final Group 2 match, meaning only one can finish with two victories.

Muchova, meanwhile, will finish the tournament with a 1-2 record if she defeats Andreeva.
