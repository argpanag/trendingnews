Daily Navigator- 4/9/2026 – Market Outlook

TheTools · 04.09.2026 13:20 · source · permalink

4 September 2026

Key highlights

Dovish Fed remarks weigh on USD; payrolls in focus: The US Dollar Index (DXY) fell 0.7% to 98.91, breaking below its 200-day moving average at 99.16. Fed Governor Christopher Waller said he was inclined to support keeping rates unchanged at the September Fed policy meeting if disinflation continues. Markets subsequently lowered the probability of a September hike to around 50%. US ISM Services PMI rose to 55.4 in August from 54.1, but its employment sub-index remained in contraction, pointing to softer labour demand. US government bond yields declined 2–5bps across the curve, with the 10-year yield easing to 4.75%. Focus now shifts to Friday’s US nonfarm payrolls report for August (consensus estimate: 55,000 net new jobs).

USD/JPY to consolidate after drop amid growing BoJ tightening expectations: USD/JPY fell over 2% to 155.47, a six-month low, as markets priced around a 75% probability of a September BoJ hike. Investors also considered the possibility of a larger-than-25bps hike, followed by further tightening, after BoJ board member Hajime Takata said the size and frequency of rate hikes could change as conditions evolve. US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent also backed more decisive monetary policy action from Japan. Near-term risks remain skewed towards further JPY appreciation. We expect USD/JPY to consolidate around 155, with next support at 153.90.

CNH to maintain gradual appreciation path as PBoC slows the pace: CNH strengthened to its strongest since February 2023, with USD/CNH reaching an intraday low of 6.7179 amid broad USD weakness. The PBoC set the daily fixing at 6.7807, compared with 6.7829 previously and a market estimate of 6.7167, indicating a preference for a more gradual pace of yuan appreciation. We expect CNH to maintain a gradual appreciation path, with USD/CNH likely to consolidate around 6.71 in the near term.
