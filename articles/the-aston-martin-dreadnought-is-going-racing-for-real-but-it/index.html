The Aston Martin Dreadnought Is Going Racing for Real, but It Was Born From Call of Duty

TheTools · 17.11.2026 21:38 · source · permalink

Aston Martin is venturing into the exciting world of off-road rally racing, and it’s doing so with a sweet-looking truck: the Aston Martin Dreadnought. Originally conceived as a digital creation for Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4, the Dreadnought is jumping into the mortal realm to do some serious racing at the 2027 Dakar rally.

This explains why when I first saw these photos on social media, I thought they were merely digital renderings or, as automakers like to call them nowadays, “creative exercises.” The off-roader was legit born as a video game war machine, yet it will go racing in the real world in a few months. Even better yet, Aston kept that steampunk-ness that makes it look like it doesn’t belong in reality. The stance, the bodywork, the livery—it’s all very cool and somewhat resembles a rally-fied Valiant.

No performance specs have been released yet, though it’s safe to assume the Dreadnought will not compete against its cross-town rival, the Defender, which races in the Stock class. It’ll most likely be powered by a V8 from the automaker’s stable, but that’s yet to be seen. Furthermore, Aston has chosen the racing experts at Prodrive as its engineering partner, greatly improving the brand’s odds of surviving its first-ever Dakar.

Last but not least, Aston has hired some serious stars to get behind the wheel of its three machines. Performing driving duty will be two-time Formula 1 champion Fernando Alonso, nine-time World Rally Champion Sébastien Loeb, and reigning World Rally Raid Champion Lucas Moraes.
