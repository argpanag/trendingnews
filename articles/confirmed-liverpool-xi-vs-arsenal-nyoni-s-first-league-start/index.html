Confirmed Liverpool XI vs. Arsenal: Nyoni's first league start, Szoboszlai in squad

TheTools · 01.11.2026 15:17 · source · permalink

Andoni Iraola has made seven changes to his lineup for the visit of Arsenal, with Dominik Szoboszlai and Victor Munoz named in the squad.

Liverpool are back at Anfield again and have the opportunity to move within three points of the current league leaders with a victory.

Alisson will be seeking his second successive clean sheet behind a back four of Ronald Araujo, Jeremy Jacquet, Virgil van Dijk and Milos Kerkez.

In midfield, Alexis Mac Allister is partnered by Trey Nyoni, marking his first Premier League start, with Florian Wirtz back in the No. 10 role from the start.

Szoboszlai, meanwhile, has been declared fit enough for a place on the bench.

Up top, Alexander Isak returns to the XI to lead the attack, with Bradley Barcola and Cody Gakpo named to start on the wings.

Munoz is among the substitutes after three games sidelined with a knee injury, as is Rio Ngumoha after Iraola named Gakpo from the start for the fourth game in a row.

Liverpool: Alisson; Araujo, Jacquet, Van Dijk, Kerkez; Nyoni, Mac Allister; Gakpo, Wirtz, Barcola; Isak

Substitutes: Mamardashvili, Gomez, Tsimikas, Endo, Gravenberch, Szoboszlai, Munoz, Koumas, Ngumoha

Arsenal: Raya; Timber, Konsa, Gabriel, Calafiori; Rice, Guimaraes, Odegaard; Madueke, Tzolis, Havertz

Substitutes: Arrizabalaga, White, Mosquera, Hincapie, Lewis-Skelly, Zubimendi, Eze, Gyokeres, Merino
