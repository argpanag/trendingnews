Thousands booked tropical Bahamas cruise and ended up in freezing Canada instead

More than 5,000 passengers expecting sun and sand ended up in Nova Scotia with little time to adjust

A cruise scheduled to sail to the sunny Bahamas this week took a northward turn for the worse when it got rerouted to chilly Canada instead.

More than 5,000 passengers boarded Royal Caribbean’s Oasis of the Seas in New Jersey on Oct. 18, expecting a seven-day cruise to Florida and the Bahamas. But forecasts of stormy weather off the East Coast prompted the cruise line to change the itinerary, sending the ship north to Halifax, Nova Scotia, according to reports.

The ship stopped in Halifax for about 24 hours, giving passengers time to explore ashore, shop and take harbor rides. It departed Wednesday evening and is scheduled to return to New Jersey on Sunday after three days at sea.

The line announced the changed itinerary the evening before the ship sailed, according to reports, leaving passengers with little time to adjust.

Royal Caribbean replaced the Oasis of the Seas’ planned Florida and Bahamas stops with a call in Halifax after forecasts of stormy weather off the East Coast. (iStock)

"My husband said to me, 'This is a joke, right?'" New Jersey resident Marie Durning told CTV News. "I have my winter hat and earmuffs, but I forgot my scarf," she said, adding that she was left scrambling to repack.

Daytime temperatures hovered in the upper 40s to low 50s this week in Halifax, with overnight lows in the 30s, according to weather reports.

For some, the surprise destination was a chance to make the most of an unfamiliar city.

"I’m grateful and I’m going to make the best of it," Jim Matthews, who lives near Paterson, New Jersey, told the outlet. "And I have found out that Halifax is a beautiful city."

Passengers (not pictured) had only hours’ notice before their Bahamas-bound cruise was redirected to Nova Scotia. (iStock)

But not everyone was onboard.
