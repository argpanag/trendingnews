Pocognoli on 'good' first camp, knowing starting line-up & 'fighting spirit'

TheTools · 26.09.2026 17:03 · source · permalink

Sebastien Pocognoli has been talking to the media before his debut as Scotland head coach in Saturday's Nations League opener away to Slovenia.

Here's the key points from the Belgian:

  • "We had a good camp. Exciting because we want to see what we worked on on the pitch."

  • "First impression was good. We need to know each other in this camp. Mixture of focus and hard work."

  • Has picked his team for tomorrow.

  • Will depend on the performance how much of the new squad will play over the four Nations League games.

  • Rangers winger Findlay Curtis and Hearts striker James Wilson could come back to the senior squad for the other games after going to under-21s.

  • What does he most want to see? "To have a good attitude, fighting spirit, desire to apply the work done in the camp."

  • No new injury updates.
