The Sims Project Rene IS PAUSED - The Sims Project Evergreen / Project X IS NEXT!

TheTools · 21.10.2026 17:31 · source · permalink

Andrew Wilson confirms the state of Project Rene and Project X - also known as Project Evergreen.

ARTICLE UPDATE: The official quote has now appeared on the EA website. Previous references from Variety stated that "Project Lotus is cancelled" however that doesn't reflect the internal memo shared publicly by EA. In the official memo the word "paused" was used.

An Entire Studio Closes

The video gaming industry has been hit hard today with the latest announcement of the Bioware studio closure. The makers of Mass Effect and Dragon Age are being shut down entirely, and Sims players are probably wondering what this means for the future of their beloved franchise.

And The First News of The Sims Project Evergreen (X) Arrive

We've been reporting tirelessly on the developments of The Sims Project X - and now it appears that EA's executives Cam Weber and David Tinson have confirmed its existence in a mail to staff sent out early today. In their own words (obtained by Variety), The Sims Project Lotus development has been put under Pause. Project Lotus is also a different, possibly internal name for The Sims Project Rene, which is a multiplayer spinoff game made for mobile-first platforms.

Instead, we're getting full focus on The Sims Project Evergreen. Which sounds very much like all The Sims Project X news we've been reporting for more than a year now!

Read the full quote shared on Variety today, later confirmed on the official EA Portal:

Our Portfolio Choices

Across our portfolio, we're making deliberate choices to align talent and investments where we see the greatest opportunities for players and long-term growth.

In Lifestyle, we’re focusing on the long-term potential of The Sims and skate. We’re going to prioritize our efforts on “Project Evergreen” for The Sims to give the team more time to reach the full potential and vision for the game, and to help make this possible, we’re pausing development on “Project Lotus.” For skate., we’ve had a lot of success in bringing players into the game, and we need to create an experience that keeps them engaged over the long-term. To do that, we are making changes to the studio to have a more focused, smaller team to deliver on a very clear roadmap for our community that we believe will build a healthier and more sustainable business.

Building the EA We Believe In

The decisions we’re making in our priorities, how we work, and with our portfolio reflect our belief in EA’s future. Some open exciting new possibilities. Others are difficult, especially for the people and teams across the company who are directly affected. In those cases, we will support impacted employees in exploring other opportunities within EA, and we're committed to treating impacted colleagues with care and respect.

We’re sure there will be questions about what these changes mean for individual teams and for EA more broadly. You can speak directly with your leaders to hear more on that, and we’ll make sure to spend time discussing our direction and the work ahead at our next Global Town Hall in November.

The past several months have strengthened our belief in what EA can become. We have extraordinary people, franchises that matter to millions of players, and opportunities to build experiences we've only begun to imagine.

It appears this is the first go-ahead that EA is giving the Maxis team, which is already frightened by their sister studio closing, to go ahead and impress the judges with a new Sims base game.

Project X Has An Internal Deadline

The Sims insider that's been sharing all The Sims Project Evergreen (or Project X as we called it) gossip had already foretold the shelving of Project Rene and full focus of this single player entry.

According to this same Sims insider, EA has already given the Maxis studio a deadline to deliver The Sims Project Evergreen by September 2027.

Here's all the latest gossip they delivered on this new Sims singleplayer game just last month:

  • Project X is has been given a deadline of Sep 1st, 2027 to go Gold. Maxis believes that it can make this deadline.
  • The game is going to have a big focus on support for portable devices such as the Steam Deck and Rog Ally.
  • The games DLC will be sold exclusively through a Marketplace, with various encryptions and measures to make piracy near impossible.
  • There's no official classification for Project X, however internally the project has been referred to as TS42 as a bit of a cheeky nod to many feeling this game is a sequel of The Sims 4 itself, rather than a sequel in the franchise.
  • Project Rene is ready to go but has been put on hold, EA aren't sure if the game leans enough into the Sims 4's brand to make sense enough to put out, but can give the greenlight whenever. Last name I heard was "The Sims Together" or some variation of.
  • Freeplay's GamePass has worked well and there may be options to incorporate this into X.
  • They're experimenting with AI beyond development, where the game may be able to intelligently identify a mod conflict or issue, but also potentially block a mod that could cause an issue or cause the mod to break, and alert the user which mod is broken and ask them if they wish to delete. This may be able to extend to being able to determine if certain files the game pulls are incorrect or modified that cause additional issues.
