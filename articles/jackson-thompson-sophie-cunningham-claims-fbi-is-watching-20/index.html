JACKSON THOMPSON: Sophie Cunningham claims FBI is watching 20 people for her, on WNBA anthem controversy

TheTools · 29.10.2026 16:46 · source · permalink

The Indiana Fever guard discussed stalking and player security on Theo Von's podcast as the WNBA's popularity grows

Kat Timpf, Kennedy talk Sophie Cunningham, new 'the Girls' podcast episode

WNBA player Sophie Cunningham faces intense backlash after defending women's sports, prompting a columnist to urge the Indiana Fever to drop her. Fox News contributor Kat Timpf and podcast host Kennedy join Ainsley Earhardt to discuss the controversy, noting that 80% of the country agrees with her position. They also preview a new episode of 'the Girls' podcast.

Indiana Fever guard Sophie Cunningham says the FBI is watching approximately 20 people over concerns involving her, describing the safety challenges that have come with her and the WNBA’s growing popularity.

Cunningham also weighed in on the pattern of certain WNBA peers choosing not to stand on the court for the national anthem, which has received heightened scrutiny throughout the WNBA Finals.

Cunningham's statements came during an appearance on Theo Von’s "This Past Weekend" podcast while discussing stalking, unwanted attention and the importance of player security.
